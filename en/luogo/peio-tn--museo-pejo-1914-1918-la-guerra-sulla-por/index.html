Museo «Pejo 1914-1918. La guerra sulla porta»

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 2003, it collects objects, documents and photographs of the First World War on the Ortles-Cevedale front, recovered at altitude or donated by private: weapons, uniforms, a kitchen, a first aid place, a trenches tabernacle with portable altar and the cart of the cable car recovered in Punta Linke; with the Stelvio Park organizes evenings and excursions on the places of war.

Why go. The White War told from his finds.

  • Where Salita San Rocco 1, Peio Paese
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
