Domus de janas di Chirisconis

History and architecture · Archaeology

Necropolis of the final Neolithic dug into the rock on the valley of the Riu Mannu: twelve domus with 21 rooms, vestibules and traces of paintings in ochre.

Why go. Tombs painted five thousand years ago.
