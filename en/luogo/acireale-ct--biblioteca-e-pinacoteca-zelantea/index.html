Biblioteca e Pinacoteca Zelantea

Museums · Old masters

The Accademia degli Zelanti, founded in 1671, is housed in a neoclassical palace by Mariano Panebianco; the library preserves hundreds of thousands of volumes, with incunabula, and the art gallery painted from the seventeenth to the twentieth century (Pietro Paolo and Alessandro Vasta, Vito D'Anna, Giuseppe Sciuti, Saru Spina). Also displayed are the eighteenth-century carriage of the Senate of Acaese, the Greek-Roman bust found in Capomulini in 1675, weapons, minerals, coins and fossils.

Why go. The oldest Sicilian academy.
