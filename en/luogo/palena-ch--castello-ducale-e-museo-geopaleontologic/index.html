Castello ducale e Museo geopaleontologico Alto Aventino

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

The castle, built around 1100 on a rocky spur in the upper part of the village, is on three levels and houses a museum pole: the Geopaleontological Museum with Abruzzo fossils, including the Prolagus of the collection Di Carlo, many from the geosite of Capo di Fiume; the Pinacoteca Casa degli Artisti e degli Men Illustri di Palena and the room dedicated to the critic and screenwriter Ettore Maria Margadonna.

Why go. Fossils and history in the castle that dominates the village.
