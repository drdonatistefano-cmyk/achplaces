Museo multimediale della transumanza

Museums · Themed

Dedicated to transhumance along the tratturous royal route from Abruzzo to Puglia, it traces the history of the island in Irpinia with a wide cultural and anthropological heritage, in a modern building that plays with light and water effects on the surrounding landscape. Totally accessible.

Why go. The way of the shepherds that crosses the country.
