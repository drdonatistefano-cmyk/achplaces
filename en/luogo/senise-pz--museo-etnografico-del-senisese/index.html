Museo etnografico del senisese

Museums · Themed

Opened in the summer of 2003, it collects materials gathered in about twenty years by the restorer Antonio Guerriero on the peasant life of the Senish: agricultural tools, weaving tools, vintage clothes and household objects. It occupies rooms of a noble palace of the fifteenth century in the historical center, next to the Mother Church; other rooms house sacred furnishings, a permanent nativity scene set in Senise and a projection room with vintage photos, and nearby are rebuilt a peasant house and a shoemaker's workshop.

Why go. The peasant life of the Pollino in a fifteenth-century palace.
