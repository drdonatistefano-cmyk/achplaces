Cattedrale di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Started at the beginning of the eighteenth century and completed around 1776, with the large façade between two bell towers at the top of a staircase; partly collapsed in 1996, it was rebuilt by hand after brick; in the naves the Stations of the Via Crucis by Roberto Ferri.

Why go. The symbol of the Baroque of Noto.

  • Where Piazza del Municipio, Noto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
