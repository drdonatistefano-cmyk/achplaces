Museo Archeologico «Eno Bellis»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Opened in 1881 and today in the Barchessa of Palazzo Foscolo: Venetian inscriptions, votive bronzes, the three-dimensional reconstruction of Opitergium, monuments of the Roman necropolis and late antique polychrome mosaics of a rich urban house.

Why go. The Roman Oderzo in a museum.

  • Where Via Garibaldi 63, Oderzo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
