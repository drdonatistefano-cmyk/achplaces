Museo archeologico e della Via Flaminia «G. C. Corsi»

Museums · Archaeological

In the former Augustinian monastery he tells of the human presence in the territory from prehistory to the Middle Ages: prehistoric finds linked to an ancient track prior to Flaminia, Roman materials including a millionaire of 305 A.D. once exhibited in town hall, and a medieval section on castles and the first Christianization. Most of the pieces come from the collection of Giulio Cesare Corsi.

Why go. The Via Flaminia told from his finds.
