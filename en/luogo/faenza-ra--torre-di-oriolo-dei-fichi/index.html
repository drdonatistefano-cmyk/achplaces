Torre di Oriolo dei Fichi

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Hesagonal tower of 1476 among the vineyards of the Faenza hills, with panoramic terrace at 360 degrees.

Why go. One of the most beautiful panoramic points of the Terre di Faenza.
