Chiesa di San Jacopo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Medieval church in Romanesque style restored in 1838. Above the main altar there is a Holy Trinity of the Camaiorese Francesco Pellegrinetti and under the apse a Madonna with Child in wood carved by Andrea Pisano.

Why go. A wooden sculpture by Andrea Pisano in a village church.
