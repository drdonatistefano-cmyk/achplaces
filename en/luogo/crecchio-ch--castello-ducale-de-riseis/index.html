Castello Ducale De Riseis

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The 12th century castle overlooking the medieval village, with four factory buildings and corner towers around a quadrangular courtyard open on a loggia; lost the defensive role became residence of royals and nobles, and on September 9, 1943 the royal family found hospitality in escape from Rome occupied. Restored in the 1970s, it is linked to the legend of the ...Torre dell'ulivo" (Torre dell'ulivo) where the new owners De Riseis planted an olive tree in peace to drive out ghosts.

Why go. The King's escape stage in 1943.
