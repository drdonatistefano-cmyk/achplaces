Basilica di Santa Restituta

Places of worship · Basilicas

Papal Basilica on the square of the same name: works on the foundations since 1036, enlargement in 1470, nineteenth-century renovations with inauguration in 1886. In 1635 the Turkish corsairs profaned the statue and stole the bells; the saint is celebrated on 16, 17 and 18 May, in memory of his arrival in the bay of San Montano. Under the church is the archaeological area with the museum of Santa Restituta.

Why go. The religious heart of the country, above the excavations.
