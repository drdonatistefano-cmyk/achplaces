Piazza Bellini e mura greche

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The square at the beginning of the major decuman, near Port'Alba, where the first Greek walls of the historic center emerge outside; meeting point between university, Academy and Conservatory.

Why go. Two thousand years under the tables.

  • Where Piazza Bellini, Napoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
