Museo del Territorio dell'Alta Valle Aulella

Museums · Archaeological

It is located in the old town hall of the village and preserves evidence of human settlements from the lower Paleolithic. There is also the baptismal font of 1452 coming from the Pieve di San Pietro di Offiano.

Why go. The oldest history of the valley, from the Paleolithic onwards.
