Museo civico «Floriana Svizzeretto» e Area del Gusto

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated on July 28, 2023 next to the municipal park Giovanni Minozzi, in the Brignano Room, as the provisional seat of the civic museum: exhibits more than fifty works including paintings, sculptures, furnishings and sacred objects recovered and restored after the earthquake. In the connected area of Taste you can see the pipe organ of 1777 by Adriano Fedri Fedeli from the church of Santa Maria del Suffragio, a Renaissance wooden pulpit from the church of San Francesco and the canvas of the Eternal Benedict already at Cola Filotesio, restored with the Art Bonus for the Earthquake.

Why go. The works saved from the rubble, again on display.
