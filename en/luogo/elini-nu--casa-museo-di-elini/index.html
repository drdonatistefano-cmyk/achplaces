Casa museo di Elini

Museums · House museums

Ancient house in the historic center on three floors, restored preserving table floors and ceilings of beams and reeds, with internal courtyard, porch and oven for bread: kitchen with fireplace, rooms with wrought iron beds, cellar with the corner of the bakery and pantry, peasant tools, costumes and embroidery of the local tradition. Guided tours by appointment with the City's cultural office; admission fee; on the day of the visit you can attend the preparation of the culurgiones and taste them.

Why go. An ogliastrina house as it was, with the culurgiones made before you.
