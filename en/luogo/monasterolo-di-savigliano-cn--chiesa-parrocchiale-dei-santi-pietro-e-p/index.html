Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Wanted by the parish priest Don Antonio Cavatorta, it was built between 1899 and 1904 on a project by Giuseppe Gallo from Turin, in modern Baroque style with Corinthian capitals, and inaugurated on 1 October 1904 in the presence of Cardinal Richelmy; the bell tower is 1924. Four side altars dedicated to the Souls of Purgatory, to the Immaculate Heart of Mary, to the Madonna del Rosario and to the Sacred Heart; next to the canonical house of 1825.

Why go. The solemn parish church of the early twentieth century in modern Baroque style.
