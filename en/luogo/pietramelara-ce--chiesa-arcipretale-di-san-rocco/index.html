Chiesa arcipretale di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises on a chapel of San Leonardo documented since 1308; it dates back to the sixteenth century and was entirely rebuilt after the collapse of 1889. It is known for the wooden portal entirely carved by hand.

Why go. The carved wooden portal.
