Santa Maria delle Anime del Purgatorio ad Arco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The seventeenth-century complex on Via dei Tribunali with the upper church, with free entrance, with works of Stanction, Jordan and Vaccaro and the winged skull of Dionisio Lazzari, and the hypogeum of the cult of the souls of Purgatory with the museum, which can be visited with a ticket.

Why go. The cult of the "capuzzelle."
