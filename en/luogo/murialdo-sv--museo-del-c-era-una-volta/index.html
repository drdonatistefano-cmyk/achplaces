Museo del C'era una volta

Museums · Themed

Established by the Cultural Association Riofreddo Together to preserve the memory of a hard and modest life, it collects about 2000 objects from the eighteenth to the first half of the twentieth century: documents of a Ferriera, a weight of Elban origin, Albisola ceramics, nineteenth century furnishings and early twentieth century clothes, sacred objects, banners and clothes of fraternities, materials on crafts, domestic life and leisure. Open on Sunday 14-18, without reservation.

Why go. The daily life of Val Bormida in two thousand objects.
