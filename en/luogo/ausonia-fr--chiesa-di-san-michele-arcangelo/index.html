Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

On the highest point of the village, in white stone on a pre-existing temple of Hercules, is the oldest monument of Ausonia; at the end of September there is celebrated the feast of the patron.

Why go. The oldest church, at the top of the village.
