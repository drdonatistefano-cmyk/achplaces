Santa Maria extra Moenia e Battistero di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Medieval church, national monument, built on the remains of a pagan temple and mentioned already in the Dialogues of St. Gregory the Great; in the apse the Blessing Redeemer, in the hexagonal baptistery frescoes of the late fourteenth century with stories of the Baptist and the Universal Judgment; on June 21 a ray of sun from a window marks the summer solstice.

Why go. The sundial baptistery.
