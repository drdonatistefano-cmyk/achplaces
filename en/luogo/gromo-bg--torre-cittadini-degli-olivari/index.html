Torre Cittadini (degli Olivari)

History and architecture · Towers

11th-XII century tower in large square blocks of local stone, which guarded access to the mineral deposits and to the passages at altitude. The 14th century castle Priacini remains the tower of the Lavanderio, perhaps even older.

Why go. The oldest towers in the mining village.
