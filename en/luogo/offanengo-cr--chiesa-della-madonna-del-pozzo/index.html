Chiesa della Madonna del Pozzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built at the end of the eighteenth century instead of a small oratory, it has simple lines and a very decorated internal altar. The name comes from the well of the little house of the romit, today the housekeeper's residence; on the square there was a fair on September 8th, still remembered by the Fair of the Madonna del Pozzo.

Why go. A simple eighteenth-century church outside, with a richly decorated altar.
