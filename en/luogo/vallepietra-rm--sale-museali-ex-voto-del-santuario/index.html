Sale museali Ex Voto del Santuario

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 2003 in the halls under the square of the Sanctuary, with two entrances: it exhibits the former most significant votes donated by pilgrims and a room dedicated to the Pope's visit to the Sanctuary.

Why go. The testimonies of pilgrims' devotion.
