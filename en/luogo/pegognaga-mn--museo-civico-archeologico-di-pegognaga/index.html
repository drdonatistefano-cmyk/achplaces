Museo civico archeologico di Pegognaga

Museums · Archaeological

Small museum with numerous Roman finds emerged from the excavations of the Roman archaeological area of the municipal territory, housed in the building built in 1927 that also houses the Anselmi Theatre and the library.

Why go. The Roman finds of the Pegognaga plain.
