Castello di Buttrio (Villa de Portis Morpurgo)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The castle of Buttrio was destroyed in 1306 and rebuilt several times; in the seventeenth century the de Portis acquired the property and started the villa, then passed to Varmo, Morpurgo, Vidoni and Felluga, who restored it. The neoclassical dominical body with pentapartite façade flanks an older building with towers and the small church of Santi Gervasio and Protasio, between park and vineyards; today it is home, winery and restaurant.

Why go. A castle among the vineyards of the Eastern Hills.
