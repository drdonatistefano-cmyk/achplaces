Oratori di San Rocco e San Carlo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Oratory of San Rocco (1626-1631), seat of the Brotherhood of Mercy, has the portico of 1725 and paintings attributed to Gherardini and Badaracco; the Oratory of San Carlo (1623-1625) preserves a table of Scipione Crespi and a wooden statue of the Assumption of Montecucco (1852).

Why go. Two seventeenth-century speakers of fraternities.
