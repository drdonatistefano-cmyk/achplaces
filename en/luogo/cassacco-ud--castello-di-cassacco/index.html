Castello di Cassacco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortress with a symmetrical rectangular structure with two high towers at the ends, stone and brick with ceilings and wooden roofs; an internal inscription recalls the restoration of the brothers Iacopo and Sebastiano di Montegnacco in 1480. Consolidated after the earthquake, today it is home with exhibition function: check the possibility of visiting.

Why go. The castle with two towers overlooking the village.
