Chiesa parrocchiale di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Started in 1766 on a project by Carlo Francesco Rangone di Montelupo, instead of the old church and on the site of the ancient castle of Falletti; bell tower of 1788. Inside the altar of the Rosary of the stuccoer Capia (1786), the altar of Sant'Antonio di Padova (1787), the altarpiece of the presbytery of Sante Conti (1891) and the decoration of 1931-1932, with friezes by Carlo Frascaroli and figures by Lorenzo Laiolo.

Why go. The 18th century parish church on the site of the castle.
