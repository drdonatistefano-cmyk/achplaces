Castello della Fava

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Stone fortress attributed to the judges of Gallura and already standing in the thirteenth century, disputed between the judges of Gallura and Arborea, passed to the Aragonese in 1380 and then returned to the Arborens; perhaps residence of the judges, also of Eleonora of Arborea. The square crenellated tower, the ruins of the walls and some cisterns remain. The name comes from the legend of the pigeon stuffed with the last broad beans that convinced the besieging Saracens to retire.

Why go. Panorama on the valley of the river Posada and on the beaches of San Giovanni.
