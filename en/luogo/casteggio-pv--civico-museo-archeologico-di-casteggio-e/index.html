Civico Museo Archeologico di Casteggio e dell'Oltrepò Pavese

Museums · Archaeological

Born in 1974 after the discovery of two tombs of Roman age, thanks to Municipality, Archaeological Superintendence, enthusiasts and private donations; it is recognized by the Lombardy Region and is part of the Oltrepò Pavese Museum System. Four sections: geology and paleontology, prehistory and protohistory, Roman and medieval age, private collectionism; among the important pieces the Roman statues of Mercury and Jupiter and the medieval ceramics found in Stradella. Open Monday-Thursday in the morning (Thursday also afternoon) and Sunday afternoon, closed Friday and Saturday; elevator for the first floor.

Why go. The history of the Oltreppo from fossils to the Roman age, in the Certosa.
