Parrocchiale di San Martino Vescovo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A 17th century church that preserves a black stone baptismal basin and the remains of an anonymous polyptych from the 16th century. In the village also the frescoed chapels of San Bernardo and Santa Croce dell'Ospedale, with works attributed to Pietro Guido da Ranzo.

Why go. Black stone and frescoes in the churches of the village.
