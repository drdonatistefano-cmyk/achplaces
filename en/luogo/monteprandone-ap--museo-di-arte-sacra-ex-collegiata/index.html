Museo di Arte Sacra (ex Collegiata)

Museums · Old masters

Founded in 2004 on the initiative of the parish priest Fr Francesco Ciabattoni, it is set up in the nave of the former Collegiate with frescoes from the 14th to the 16th century; it collects paintings, relics and works of the parish of San Nicolò di Bari, including a wooden crucifix of the 13th century, the 16th century statue of the Madonna of Loreto and a section on St. James of the Marca.

Why go. Frescoes and the crucifix of the thirteenth century.
