Casa del tè (Museo nazionale italiano del tè)

Museums · Themed

Cultural association dedicated to the cultivation, processing, promotion and enhancement of tea and its traditions, in an environment built according to the principles of Feng-shui. It presents itself as the only tea museum in Italy and has twice entered the Guinness of Records, for the collection of over 600 varieties of tea and for the largest teapot and teacup in the world; the Metropolitan City cites its tea room as one of the largest in Italy.

Why go. A Guinness tea museum in the heart of wheat Sicily.
