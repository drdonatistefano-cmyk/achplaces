Chiese di San Rocco, San Pietro, Sant'Angelo e San Nicola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

San Rocco holds two paintings by Francesco Fracanzano with St. Peter and St. Anthony Abbot; St. Peter's a Holy Family in terracotta of Amalia Dupré and an eighteenth-century wooden statue of the Immaculate of Paolo Di Zinno. Sant'Angelo preserves canvases of the Six and eighteenth centuries and San Nicola a remarkable wooden choir.

Why go. Fracanzano and Dupré on a short ride.
