Villa Maggi Corvini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Properties of the Maggi since 1609, which in 1727 obtained from Pope Benedict XIII the private oratory in the hall then called "the columns" (the columns), in 1899 the landscape painter Giovanni Corvini bought it, who rebuilt a wing and decorated it with classical themes. Since 1975 it is part of the Municipality, restored in 1997-1999 and bound since 1973; the Salone delle colonne is intended for art gallery, with the park of the villa.

Why go. The villa of the painter Corvini, today of the city.
