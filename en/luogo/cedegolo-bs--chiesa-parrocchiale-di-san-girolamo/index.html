Chiesa parrocchiale di San Girolamo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of the eighteenth century appreciated for the portal, with frescoes by A. Capello. The 17th-century high altar has a monumental tabernacle and an ancon attributed to Pietro Ramus; the palace is the work of Giovanni Giuseppe Piccini.

Why go. The Baroque altar with the ancon of Pietro Ramus.
