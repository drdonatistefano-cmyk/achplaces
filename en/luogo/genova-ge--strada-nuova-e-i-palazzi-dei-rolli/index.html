Strada Nuova e i Palazzi dei Rolli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The noble residences that the aristocracy put at the disposal of the Republic to host ambassadors and dignitaries; registered by UNESCO in 2006 as "Genoa": the new roads and the system of the Palaces of the Rolls, today there are 42.

Why go. The UNESCO World Heritage Buildings.

  • Where Via Garibaldi, Genova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visitgenoa.it
