Museo archeologico Lino Vanuzzo (Antiquarium di Sambruson)

Museums · Archaeological

It preserves mainly Roman finds found in 1950 in the excavations of prof. Lino Vanuzzo: domestic ceramics, fragments of frescoes and mosaics and building elements, which suggest a settlement of some importance. The ancient sources here recall a mansio called Maio Medauco, near a branch of the Brenta.

Why go. The Roman traces of the Brenta Riviera.
