Grotta dello Smeraldo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · UNESCO World Heritage

Marine cave discovered in 1932 by the fisherman Luigi Buonocore: the light enters from an underwater crack and tints the water of emerald green, while stalactites and stalagmites form columns under the vault. Since 1956 there is a ceramic nativity scene on the bottom. You arrive there from the state with elevator or staircase, or from the sea; you enter on a raft.

Why go. The emerald water of the coast.
