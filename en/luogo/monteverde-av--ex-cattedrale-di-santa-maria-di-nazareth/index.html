Ex cattedrale di Santa Maria di Nazareth

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Under the castle, it has a Baroque shape from the reconstruction of 1728 on a 14th century plant expanded in the 16th century; the restoration after the earthquake of 1980 brought to light Romanesque structures. It preserves paintings of the Neapolitan school of the seventeenth century, wooden statues, altars between the fifteenth and eighteenth centuries and a crucifix dated to the thirteenth century.

Why go. Art and spirituality at the foot of the castle.
