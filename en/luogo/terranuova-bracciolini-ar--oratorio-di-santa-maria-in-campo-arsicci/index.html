Oratorio di Santa Maria in Campo Arsiccio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Oratory in the locality of Campogialli, decorated with cycles of frescoes of the fourteenth century, probably of the workshop of Andrea di Nerio and the Master of Barberino; in the area there was also a manor of the Pazzi del Valdarno.

Why go. Trecentesque frescoes in a country oratory.
