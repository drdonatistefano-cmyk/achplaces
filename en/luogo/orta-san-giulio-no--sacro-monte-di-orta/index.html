Sacro Monte di Orta

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The devotional path started in 1590, with 20 frescoed chapels and life-size terracotta groups on the life of St. Francis; from the churchyard of St. Nicholas one of the most beautiful views of the lake. UNESCO World Heritage.

Why go. The mountain of St. Francis.
