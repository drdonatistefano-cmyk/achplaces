Oratorio di Santa Caterina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque chapel built around 1210 by Provençal workers next to the domus hospitalis founded around 1165 by the Knights Hospitallers for pilgrims and travelers direct to the Monginevro; dedicated first to St. John the Baptist, around 1250 passed to St. Catherine of Alexandria. Restored, it preserves fourteenth-century frescoes and the baroque altar wanted at the end of the seventeenth century by the prevosto Fantin. Visits by reservation (tel. This appropriation is intended to cover the expenditure of the European Regional Development Fund (ERDF).

Why go. Trecento frescoes on the Via del Monginevro.
