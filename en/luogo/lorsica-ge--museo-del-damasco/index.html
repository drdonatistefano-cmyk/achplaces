Museo del damasco

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

On four floors of an ancient building tells the story of the village and of the weaving: Lorsica was among the main centers of the Genoese manufacture, whose fabrics in the sixteenth-sixth century were known in the world as "Velours de Gênes" . It exhibits frames, tools for spinning, canning and dyeing and antique fabrics (damasks, lamps, linen macramè, an embroidered planet of the parish). According to the Metropolitan City opens Saturday and Sunday 16-18 from April to October and 15-17 from November to March, and by reservation on other days (tel. 0185 95019).

Why go. Five centuries of Genoese damask, from the frame to the fabric.

  • Where Via Casali 109, Lorsica
  • Entry €3, reduced (8-14 years) €1,50, free under 8 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
