Museo della civiltà contadina e artigiana

Museums · Themed

About a thousand artifacts on ancient crafts and peasant life: the first room exhibits archaeological finds of the territory, the others reconstruct domestic and working environments (plows and harrows, vinification, killing of the pig, corners of the blacksmith, carpenter, shoemaker, chaplain and knitwear).

Why go. The life of the High Irpinia peasanta, craft by trade.

The MiC card indicates the museum closed every day: check the opening with the Municipality (tel. 0827 32009).
