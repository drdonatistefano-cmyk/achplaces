Palazzo Pitti

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The Palatine Gallery with Raffaello and Tiziano, Galleria d'arte moderna, Museo della moda e del costume, Treasure of the Grand Dukes.

Why go. With a ticket you enter five museums.

  • Where Piazza Pitti 1, Firenze
  • Entry €16 full day, €19 with reservation; €22 with Boboli and Bardini; €2 from 18 to 25 years EU; free under 18
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.uffizi.it
  • Opening hours official page
