Museo di Palazzo Pistilli

Museums · Old masters

Museum in the historic center that exhibits the art collection of Giuseppe Ottavio Eliseo and Michele Praitano destined to Campobasso and Molise; the visit is by reservation.

Why go. The Eliseo-Praitano collection.
