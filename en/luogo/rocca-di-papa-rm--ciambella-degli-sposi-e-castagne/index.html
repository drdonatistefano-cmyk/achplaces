Ciambella degli Sposi e castagne

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The Donut degli Sposi, a sweet handmade ring and Traditional Agro-food Product of Lazio, has its festival usually in September; the "rocchicianella," a small sweet chestnut, is celebrated on the third Sunday of October. On the table polenta with sausage, cheese or mushrooms, borlotti soup, cod in stew and lamb shell with broad beans.

Why go. Sweets and chestnuts of the castle tradition.
