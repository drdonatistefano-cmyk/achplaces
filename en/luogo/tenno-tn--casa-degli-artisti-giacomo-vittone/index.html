Casa degli Artisti «Giacomo Vittone»

Museums · Modern art

Dedicated to the Turinese painter Giacomo Vittone, who was its promoter, purchased and restored in 1965 and managed by the municipalities of Riva del Garda, Arco and Tenno: he holds a collection of works, hosts exhibitions and events and is a residence for artists visiting from all over Europe.

Why go. The village that inspired the painters.
