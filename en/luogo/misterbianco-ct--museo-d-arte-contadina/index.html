Museo d'arte contadina

Museums · Themed

Museum that exhibits testimonies of rural life, near the parking lot Milicia: the Milicia district is the one where in 1669 the 3,656 refugees of the country destroyed by lava stopped.

Why go. The rural memory of the Ethnian territory.
