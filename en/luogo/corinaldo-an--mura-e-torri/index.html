Mura e torri

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

A 912-metre-long road that remained almost intact from the fifteenth century, with the two circles of 1367 and 1480: gates, bastions, Ghibelline blackbirds with swallow tails and round walkways. The Torre dello Sperone, pentagonal and 18 meters high, attributed to Francesco di Giorgio Martini, the Torre dello Scorticatore, where the walls reach 15 meters, the Round of enlargement completed in 1490, the Landroni porticoati and Porta San Giovanni; from the terrace near the Bargello the view arrives at the Conero. You can also visit them with a guide.

Why go. Among the best preserved walls of the Marche.
