Museo della civiltà contadina Casa Dei

Museums · Themed

In a farmhouse in the Padule di Fucecchio, founded by the Dei family and in the regional museum network since 2012: agricultural machines and tools, everyday objects, books, photographs, documents and postcards to tell the link between landscape, field work and domestic life, with educational activities for adults and children.

Why go. The peasant life of the Padule told by objects.
