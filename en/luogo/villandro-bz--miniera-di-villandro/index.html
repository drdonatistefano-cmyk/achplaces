Miniera di Villandro

Museums · Themed

Among the most important mining areas of Tyrol in the Middle Ages, closed at the beginning of the twentieth century and recovered by local associations: 16 galleries for about 20 km, for two thirds excavated by hand, from which were extracted silver, galena, copper and blend, advancing 8-12 meters per year. Guided tour of the Elisabeth gallery (about 1 and a half hours) and, on Wednesdays in July and August, of the Elisabeth and Lorenz galleries (about 2 and a half hours).

Why go. Inside the galleries of medieval miners.

only guided tours, season from 6 April to 8 November 2026 (Tuesday and Thursday 10.30 and 14.30, Sunday 10.30); off-season by reservation
