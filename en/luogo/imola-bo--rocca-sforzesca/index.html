Rocca sforzesca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Medieval fortress reinforced by Sforza and Caterina Sforza, with collection of weapons and ceramics.

Why go. One of the best preserved castles in the region.

Closed for restoration works since March 2024; reopening not yet confirmed (some sites still indicate dates already exceeded)

  • Where Piazzale Giovanni dalle Bande Nere, Imola
  • Entry Civic museums from 2026: €5 whole, €4 reduced
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
