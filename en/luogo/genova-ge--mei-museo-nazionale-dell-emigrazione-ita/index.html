MEI – Museo Nazionale dell'Emigrazione Italiana

Museums · City history

In the Commenda di San Giovanni di Prè, 12th-century building for centuries a place of welcome for pilgrims and travellers: three floors and 16 areas dedicated to Italian migrations from Unity to today.
