Chiesa di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the upper part of the village, it is indicated among the oldest sacred buildings of Varesotto. Leggiuno is the first place to know a document with the term "plebs": at least since 846 it was the seat of the Pieve di Santo Stefano, a role maintained until 1965.

Why go. The ancient plebanic church, religious heart of the territory for over a thousand years.
