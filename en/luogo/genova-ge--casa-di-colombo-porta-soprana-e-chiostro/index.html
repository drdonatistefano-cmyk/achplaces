Casa di Colombo, Porta Soprana e Chiostro di Sant'Andrea

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Porta Soprana, from the 12th century, was the entrance to the city for those who came from the east; just beyond the house where Colombo lived between 1455 and 1470 and the cloister of Sant'Andrea, reassembled here after the demolition of the monastery in 1904.

Why go. Columbus' youth.
