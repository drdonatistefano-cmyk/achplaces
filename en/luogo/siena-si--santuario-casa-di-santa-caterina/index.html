Santuario Casa di Santa Caterina

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

The birthplace of Santa Caterina, in the area of Fontebranda, transformed over the centuries into a sanctuary; it is accessed by the Portico of the Municipalities of Italy (1939) and the heart is the Church of the Crucifix, with the wooden crucifix from which the Saint received the stigmata in 1375.

Why go. In the footsteps of the Patroness of Italy.
