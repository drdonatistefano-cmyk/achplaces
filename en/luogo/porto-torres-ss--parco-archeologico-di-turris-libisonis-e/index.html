Parco archeologico di Turris Libisonis e ponte romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The Roman colony, perhaps founded by Caesar or Ottaviano and at its apogee at the beginning of the III century AD, was the main port of call of the Roman Sardinia: you can visit the central baths called the Palazzo di Re Barbaro, the Maetzke and Pallottino thermal baths with four columns of the peristyle, the Domus of Orpheus with the mosaic of the poet playing the lira and the Domus of the mosaics, paved streets and workshops. On the Riu Mannu the bridge of the first century A.D., seven arches for 135 meters, is the largest Roman bridge on the island and was used until the mid-Twentieth century; remains traces of the aqueduct and the necropolis of Tanca Borgona, with 32 frescoed and mosaic burials. Guided tours from Tuesday to Saturday at 9:30, 10:30, 12:15, optional booking.

Why go. An entire Roman city, with its bridge still standing.
