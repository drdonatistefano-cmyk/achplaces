Spiagge della Riviera di Levante e di Ponente

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches

Wide beaches on both sides of the city, awarded in 2024 with Blue Flag and Green Flag for the reception of children. On the beach of the American landing you can see the remains of a means of landing sunk on January 22, 1944.

Why go. Sea and memory of the landing.
