Abbazia di Santa Maria del Casale

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

According to the tradition built around 1087 by Rodolfo Maccabeo and Emma d'Altavilla on the remains of a Greek-Byzantine monastery; a Romanesque church in Apulian style that preserves a wooden statue of the Virgin of the twelfth century.

Why go. The Norman Abbey of Pisticci.
