Villa Caruso Bellosguardo e Museo Enrico Caruso

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The sixteenth-century villa bought in 1906 by Enrico Caruso, with the park of the Tribolo and the museum with its room and its caricatures.

Why go. The voice of the first Italian divo in the music room.

  • Where Via Bellosguardo 54, Lastra a Signa
  • Entry Museum €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
