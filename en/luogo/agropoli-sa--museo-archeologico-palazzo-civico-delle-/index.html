Museo Archeologico - Palazzo Civico delle Arti

Museums · Archaeological

Born as an archaeological section of the Municipality in the Palazzo Civico delle Arti, it was established as a stable museum with resolution of February 2026, with statute and technical-scientific committee coordinated by Fabio Astone, in collaboration with the Superintendence. It exhibits finds from the municipal territory recovered over decades, in a collection started in the seventies; in the same palace the Spazio Mare and the Civic Library " Costabile Cilento" . Timetable indicated by the Municipality: Tuesday and Thursday 9-13, Saturday 9-13 and 15-19, other days by reservation (0974 277823).

Why go. The archaeology of Agropoli in the new civic museum.
