Fondazione Bandera per l'arte

Museums · Modern art

About one hundred works by Italian and foreign artists, including sculptures by Giorgio De Chirico and Francesco Messina and stained glass windows by Aligi Sassu and Remo Brindisi; it hosts exhibitions, presentations and conferences.

Why go. Art of the twentieth century in a private foundation.
