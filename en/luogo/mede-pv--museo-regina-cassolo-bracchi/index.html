Museo Regina Cassolo Bracchi

Museums · Modern art

He collected the works of the futurist sculptor Regina Cassolo, donated to the city by her husband Luigi Bracchi: 52 sculptures and about 500 drawings, temperas and collages. The route goes from the Futurism of the thirties to the Movement concrete art.

Why go. Regina's female futurism.
