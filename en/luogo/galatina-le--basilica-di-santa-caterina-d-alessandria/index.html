Basilica di Santa Caterina d'Alessandria

Places of worship · Basilicas

Franciscan Basilica in Romanesque-Apulian style built in 1384 by Raimondello Orsini del Balzo, a national monument since 1886. The three aisles are entirely frescoed, almost all in the fifteenth century on the commission of Mary of Enghien: Apocalypse, Seven Sacraments, Genesis, Life of Christ, Stories of the Virgin and, in the presbytery, Stories of Saint Catherine; there is also an octagonal chapel of the fifteenth century. A recent restoration has involved roof, windows, lighting, cloister and bell tower, and in the former refectory there is a museum.

Why go. One of the largest cycles of late-Gothic frescoes in the South.
