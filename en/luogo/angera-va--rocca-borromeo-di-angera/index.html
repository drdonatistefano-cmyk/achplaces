Rocca Borromeo di Angera

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built in the 12th century by the archbishops of Milan on a limestone spur above the southern shore of Lake Maggiore, it was contested between Torriani and Visconti, enlarged by the Visconti and in 1449 entrusted to the Borromeos, who still own it. In the Hall of Justice an exceptional cycle of frescoes; you can also visit the Historical Hall, the Hall of the Maiolicas (300 rare pieces), the Castellana Tower with views of the lake and the Medieval Garden; the Ala Scaligera houses exhibitions. Season 2026 from March 12 to November 8, every day.

Why go. The medieval castle overlooking Lake Maggiore, with frescoes and views from the tower.
