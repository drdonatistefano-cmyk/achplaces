Castello Cavour e Memoriale Cavour

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

An ancient residence renovated several times, today of eighteenth-century appearance on a project by the architect of court Francesco Gallo, country house of the Benso di Cavour where the statesman was born, formed and worked. It is a museum house dedicated to Camillo Benso and the lifestyle of the Piedmontese ruling classes under the Savoy: the Memorial, reopened on March 17, 2021 after a long restoration, includes the tomb of Cavour in the crypt of the chapel, declared a national monument. Reservation required; free with Torino+Piemonte Card and for children under 6 years.

Why go. The house and the tomb of the architect of the Unity of Italy.
