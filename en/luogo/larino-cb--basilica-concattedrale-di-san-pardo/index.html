Basilica concattedrale di San Pardo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

The Cathedral dedicated to the Assumption and to St. Pardo, bishop of Greek origin, consecrated in 1319: the Gothic façade is the work of Francesco Petrini of Lanciano, with sculptural compositions of biblical inspiration and thirteen columns instead of twelve, the thirteenth to represent Judas; the back of the building is instead crooked and irregular. Inside, frescoes, paintings, tabernacles and altars of different eras. At the end of May (25-27) the feast of San Pardo brings to the city over one hundred carts drawn by oxen and decorated with paper flowers.

Why go. The most famous Gothic façade of Molise.
