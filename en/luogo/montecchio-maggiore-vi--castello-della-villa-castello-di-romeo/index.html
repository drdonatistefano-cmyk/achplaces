Castello della Villa (Castello di Romeo)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Erected by Cangrande II della Scala in 1354 and dismantled by the Venetians in 1514, during the war of the League of Cambrai; the walls, the mast and a tower remain. In summer it hosts theatre shows and events, and every May 1st, with the other castle, the re-enactment of Montecchio Medievale. Visits indicated on Saturday 15-18.30 and Sunday 10-12.30 and 15-18.30, in the summer.

Why go. The walls of the castle of Romeo, with a view of the plain.
