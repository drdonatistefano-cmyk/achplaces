Chiesa di Maria Santissima del Carmelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was originally built in 1185 and rebuilt in 1934-36 and has a small porch with inserts from the mosque of Rabato; inside the Madonna del Soccorso in marble carved in 1503 by Bartolomeo Berrettaro of Carrara for the Salamone family, whose tombs are in the chapel on the right of the choir.

Why go. The Madonna of the Rescue of Berrettaro.
