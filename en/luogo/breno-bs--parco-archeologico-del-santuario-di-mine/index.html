Parco archeologico del Santuario di Minerva

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the Spinera plain, along the Oglio at the foot of a rocky spur full of caves, it was a sacred place from the 7th century BC at the end of the 4th A.D.: large parts of a building of Roman worship remain, built on a prehistoric cult of the waters, with mosaics and frescoed plasters. The statue of Minerva Hygeia, one of the three known copies, is now at the National Archaeological Museum of Cividate Camuno. It can also be reached with the cycle path along the Oglio da Breno and Cividate.

Why go. A Roman sanctuary on the water, between caves and river.
