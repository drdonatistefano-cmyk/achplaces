Museo civico di scienze naturali «Alessio Amighetti»

Museums · Science and university

Established by the Municipality in 1996 and dedicated to the nature of the Camuno-Sebino territory, it bears the name of the naturalist priest who in the nineteenth century introduced the lake of Iseo; it exhibits birds, mammals, shells, butterflies and minerals. Open from Tuesday to Sunday 15-18.

Why go. The nature of Sebino in a small museum.
