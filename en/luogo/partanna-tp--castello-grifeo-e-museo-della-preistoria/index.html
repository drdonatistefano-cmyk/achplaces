Castello Grifeo e Museo della preistoria del Belìce

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The castle dominates the historic centre and is one of the best preserved medieval fortresses in western Sicily. The museum exhibits above all the excavations of the excavations started in the eighties in the Strait district, known for the Neolithic ditches, and of the necropolis of Belice with grotticella tombs and chamber of the middle and late Bronze, including ceramics of the Naro-Partanna style and bell towers; didactic panels tell the peoples of Belice before the Greeks. It also houses an archive of parchments from the fourteenth century donated by the Turrisi-Griffeo family and, in the armory, a gallery with a fourteenth-century altarpiece and a San Tommaso da Villanova attributed to Silvestre Ratto da Mazara.

Why go. The prehistory of Belice in a medieval castle.
