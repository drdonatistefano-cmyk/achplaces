Museo archeominerario di Masso (MuCast)

Museums · Archaeological

The centre tells about 5,000 years of man's work in Val Petronio: an interactive museum in the former school of Masso, an excavation area that brings back to the Age of Copper (about 3500 BC) and the Galleria XX Settembre, a copper mine active in the nineteenth century and indicated as the only mine that can be visited in Liguria with an underground route. Excavations indicate that Mass is the oldest copper mine in Western Europe. Guided tours: from 15 June to 14 September Wednesday and Friday at 18, Saturday and Sunday at 10.30 and 18; from 15 September to 14 June on Sunday at 10.30, 14 and 16. Tel. 0185 469139.

Why go. In the oldest copper mine in Western Europe.
