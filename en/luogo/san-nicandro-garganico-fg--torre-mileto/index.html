Torre Mileto

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

On a small rock spur between the lagoons of Lesina and Varano, it is already mentioned in acts of Charles II of Anjou; the current structure, with a quadrangular base with a crown of five caditoias, dates back to the middle of the sixteenth century by mandate of the Viceroy Don Pedro of Toledo. It was garrison, telegraphic base for the Tremiti and barracks of the Guardia di Finanza; owned by the municipality, it was restored and opened in 2005 and houses a visitor center of the Park. From the terrace you can see the other coastal towers up to Molise.

Why go. View of the coast between two lagoons from a 16th century tower.
