Villa Spineda Gasparini Loredan

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Designed by Francesco Maria Preti (1701-1774) and completed by Miazzi, who reworked the project, it is indicated by the Municipality as one of the most successful examples of eighteenth-century Palladianism: a dominical body with central pronao and two ariosian side boats. In front of the façade the garden is surrounded by a fish pond, on the back stretches up to the slopes of Montello. The Benetton Group has been renovated on a project by Afra and Tobia Scarpa and by Prof. Mora.

Why go. A masterpiece of 18th century Palladianism at the foot of Montello.
