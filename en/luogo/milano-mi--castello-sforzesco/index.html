Castello Sforzesco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Among the largest castles in Europe, built between the Three and fifteenth centuries: Piazza d'Armi, Corte della Rocchetta and Corte Ducale; civic museums with the Pietà Rondanini by Michelangelo and the Sala delle Asse painted by Leonardo.

Why go. Michelangelo and Leonardo in the same castle.
