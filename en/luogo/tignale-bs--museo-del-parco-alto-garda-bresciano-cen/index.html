Museo del Parco Alto Garda bresciano - Centro visitatori

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Information and environmental education center dedicated to the hinterland of Garda, born to unite the coastal and the mountain belt: the route combines images, sounds, reconstructions of environments, naturalistic collections, old working tools, videos and interactive stations, with paths marked to the outside.

Why go. Understand the park between lake and mountain.

seasonal opening (from 30 August to 10 October, Mon and Gio-dom 15-19) according to the MiC card updated to 2022

  • Where Località Prabione, Tignale
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.parcoaltogarda.net
