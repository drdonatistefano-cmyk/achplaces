Mausoleo Gambarini-Cagnola-Abbiati

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Wanted in 1823 by Carlo Maria Gambarini for himself and the family, designed by Giovanni Battista Capitanio and built under the direction of Giuseppe Catto; sculptures by Antonio Gelpi il Giovane, including two painful winged figures and the great statue of Time on the pediment, and Masonic and esoteric symbols. Inside there is an octagonal chapel with the tombs of the Gambarini by Luigi Cocchi and a bust by Rosa Tarsis Cagnola by Vincenzo Vela; in the crypt is buried the music critic and composer Franco Abbiati.

Why go. A neoclassical temple full of symbols.

not open freely: open only on special occasions announced on the website of the Municipality and on the social media of the library
