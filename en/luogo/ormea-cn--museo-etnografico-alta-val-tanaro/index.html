Museo etnografico Alta Val Tanaro

Museums · Themed

In the house next to the church of the Madonna degli Angeli, it offers a path of memory with the faithful reconstruction of domestic environments of the past: a house from the beginning of the twentieth century. Open on Sundays from 3 pm to 5 pm or on reservation (tel. +39 0174 391101).

Why go. The omerica house of the early twentieth century, as it was.
