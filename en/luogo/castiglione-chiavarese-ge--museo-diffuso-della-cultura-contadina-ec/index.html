Museo diffuso della cultura contadina - Ecomuseo dell'Alta Val Petronio

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Museum spread in the historical center of Velva, with its headquarters in the ancient Oratorio dei Bianchi (used to worship until the early twentieth century) and in cellars, funds and domestic environments where the objects remain in their context. The sections illustrate agricultural and domestic activities, handicrafts, transport and commercial: workshops of the carpenter-boatmaker, the carter-mulker and shoemaker, beating of chestnuts, threshing, mills and mills. From April to the end of October open on Saturday 14-18. Tel. 0185 408016.

Why go. The rural life of the hinterland in the houses where it took place.

outside the period April-October only by appointment; the MiC card (updated to December 2020) reports "closed" in every day
