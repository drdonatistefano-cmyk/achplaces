Chiesa di Santa Barbara a Bondo Petello

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church of the village of Bondo Petello: on the high altar the altarpiece of Moroni with the Madonna and Child in glory and the saints Catherine of Alexandria and Barbara (1562-1564).

Why go. A altarpiece of Moroni still on its altar.
