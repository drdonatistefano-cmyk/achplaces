Rocca di Rivoli

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the fortress there was a fortification around 1100, linked to the legend of Countess Emma who resisted the siege of Prince Marcualdo and saved himself disguised as a monk; there are traces of Neolithic settlements of the Vase Culture in Bocca Quadrata. The excavations of the late 1970s, promoted by Pro Loco with archaeologists from the universities of Birmingham and Cambridge, have returned weapons, furnishings and perimeter walls and part of the floor of a small church.

Why go. A fortress between medieval legend and English excavations.
