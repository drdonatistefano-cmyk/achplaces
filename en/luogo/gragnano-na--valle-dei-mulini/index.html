Valle dei Mulini

Nature and landscape · Landscapes

13th century hydraulic complex, important technological innovation: in the Middle Ages the mills grind the wheat for the population, exploiting the spring water of the Lattari Mountains which fell by gravity from one mill to another at different altitudes, with a domino effect. The valley is part of an ancient route that connected Castellammare di Stabia to Amalfi and in the Seven and nineteenth centuries was painted by the artists of the Grand Tour.

Why go. The medieval roots of the city of pasta, between water and mills.
