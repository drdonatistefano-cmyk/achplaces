Casa militare Umberto I

Museums · City history

A valuable structure of late nineteenth century, huge, with large lounges and a large park, the only green area of Turate according to the FAI; the activity began in 1898 hosting some garibaldini. Today it houses a museum with relics of the Risorgimento, including objects related to Giuseppe Garibaldi, and occasionally hosts events such as classical music concerts. Reported in the FAI census in 2003 , 2014, 2016, 2018, 2020 and 2022.

Why go. Garibaldi relics in the ancient house of veterans.

according to the FAI the structure is almost unused for about a decade and opens only occasionally for events
