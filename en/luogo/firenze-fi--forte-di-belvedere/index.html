Forte di Belvedere

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The Medici fortress of Buontalenti (1590) connected to Boboli, with one of the best views of Florence and contemporary art exhibitions.

Why go. At sunset on the terraces.

  • Where Via di San Leonardo 1, Firenze
  • Entry €8 full; €5 reduced; free for residents of the Metro City; terraces and free coffee
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com
