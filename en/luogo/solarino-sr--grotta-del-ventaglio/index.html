Grotta del Ventaglio

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Karst cave under the village, with stalactites and stalagmites, discovered by chance on October 27, 1987 during the excavations for the sewer in via Dante Alighieri. It takes its name from a large thin and translucent stalactite in the shape of a fan in the center of the cavity; the tunnels suggest that it extends for tens of meters and is connected to other caves of the area.

Why go. A karst cave hidden under the village.

closed to the public for its position, which can only be visited by qualified researchers; in 2023, the Municipality placed inspections to assess the reopening of the project.
