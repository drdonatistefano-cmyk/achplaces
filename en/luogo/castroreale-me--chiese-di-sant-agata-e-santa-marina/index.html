Chiese di Sant'Agata e Santa Marina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Complex in the lower part of via Siracusa: Santa Marina combines Norman elements, an Aragonese fortification and Baroque decorations; St. Agatha, largely rebuilt in 1857, preserves an Annunciation by Antonello Gagini (1519), a Sant'Agata del Montorsoli (1554), a Madonna by Michelangelo Naccherino (1601) and the Christ Long, carried in procession on 23 and 25 August and in Holy Week.

Why go. Long Christ and Renaissance marbles.
