Museo civico «Umberto Mastroianni» (ex chiesa di Santa Lucia)

Museums · Archaeological

Civic museum inaugurated in 2000 in the former Gothic church of Saint Lucia, the Gothic Temple, built on a Roman cistern of imperial age then transformed into a Christian oratory: the first cult building of 1102, desecrated in 1669 for the construction of the Collegiate Church of San Barnaba, damaged by the bombings of 1944 and purchased by the Municipality in 1974. Exhibits of the territory: classical statuary, sepulchral cippes, sarcophagi, inscriptions, protohistoric depositions.

Why go. The Roman cistern under a Gothic church.

MiC card indicates the museum closed every day, with opening at exhibitions
