Chiesa di San Vito e Pietra di San Vito

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Cappella campestre not far from the centre, dedicated to the patron saint of animals and cited as one of the most important architectures of Grecìa Salentina; a fifteenth-century document describes it with three doors, three altars and a frescoed dome. In the only chapel, a large pre-Christian rock pierced (about 30 cm hole) emerges from the floor: in Pasquetta, by tradition, you pass through to purify yourself or as a wish for fertility, in a feast among olive trees with music, dances and cronelli in griko.

Why go. An ancient rite that repeats every Easter Monday.
