Gipsoteca Giulio Monteverde

Museums · Modern art

Civic museum with the original plaster models of the sculptor Giulio Monteverde, born in Bistagno in 1837, which cover his production from 1870 to 1917; part of the collection was donated by his daughters in Genoa in 1919 and partly returned to Bistagno with donations and loans. Open Saturday and Sunday from spring to autumn, in other periods by reservation (tel. +39 0144 79301, +39 366 5442354); adheres to Castelli Aperti.

Why go. The original plasters of one of the great sculptors of the nineteenth century.
