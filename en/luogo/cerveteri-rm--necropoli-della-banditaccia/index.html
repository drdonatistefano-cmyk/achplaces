Necropoli della Banditaccia

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Etruscan necropolis excavated in tuff (IX-III century BC), along the Via Sepolcrale that descends into the valley as Via degli Inferi: mounds and graves like those of the Capanna, the Cornice, the Capitelli, the Greek vases and the Reliefs, with 13 niches in painted stucco.

Why go. The city of the Etruscan dead.
