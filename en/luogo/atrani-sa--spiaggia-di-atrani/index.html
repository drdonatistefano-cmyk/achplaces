Spiaggia di Atrani

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small sandy beach and fine gravel, divided by the Dragon, partly free and partly equipped, with clear and calm sea; in the evening the fishermen's boats leave for fishing with lamps at night.

Why go. A bathroom just a stone's throw from the square.
