Chiesa di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the 12th century, it was the first parish of Montaldeo; it has a unique nave and contains frescoes dating back to the end of the 15th century. Shortly before, for those arriving from Mornese, we meet the chapel of San Gottardo.

Why go. The oldest church in the village, with fifteenth century frescoes.
