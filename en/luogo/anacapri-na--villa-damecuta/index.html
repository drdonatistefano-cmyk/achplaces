Villa Damecuta

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Remains of an elegant imperial residence on the north-western promontory, with a panoramic ambulacrum overlooking the sea, a loggia and a small late medieval watchtower.

Why go. A Roman villa overlooking the sea.
