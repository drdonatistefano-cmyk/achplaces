Chiesa dei Santi Giovanni e Andrea e Museo parrocchiale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Archpriest's church of the fifteenth century near the fortress, with the Madonna of the Public Vote in terracotta, a fifteenth-century tabernacle and an organ of the eighteenth century; in the museum a altarpiece of Innocent Imola and a crucifix of Donatellian school.

Why go. A stone's throw from the fortress.
