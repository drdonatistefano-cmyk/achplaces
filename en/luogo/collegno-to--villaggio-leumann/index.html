Villaggio Leumann

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

Worker's district built in the late nineteenth century by the Swiss entrepreneur Napoleone Leumann and designed to be self-sufficient: houses, boarding house for workers, public baths, theater, surgery, post office, hotel, kindergarten, schools, church, club and dealership. It preserves the historic station of the Turin-Rivoli line, the old primary school and several restored Art Nouveau buildings, and is still inhabited by a hundred families. Guided tours on scheduled dates (with entrance to the Village Museum): €17, reduced €8.

Why go. The most complete village-factory liberty at the gates of Turin.
