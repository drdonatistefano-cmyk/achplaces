Palazzo Petrignani

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

Palazzo nobiliare in the historic center, which can be visited with the circuit of the civic museums: guided tours on weekends in spring and autumn, daily opening (except Wednesday) from June to September.

Why go. A noble palace with guided tour.
