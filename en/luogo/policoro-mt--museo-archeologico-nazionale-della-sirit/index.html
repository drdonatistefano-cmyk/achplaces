Museo archeologico nazionale della Siritide

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 1969 next to the archaeological park, it exhibits the finds of the Greek cities of Siris and Herakleia and of the indigenous centers of the interior: votive statues and accessories of Siris with some of the oldest figurative ceramics of Magna Grecia (VII-VI century B.C.), matrixes, votive objects and accessories of Herakleia with red-figure ceramics and gold jewels in filigree, bronze armour, silver jewels, gold and amber. Closed on Tuesdays.

Why go. Siris and Herakleia, the Greek colonies of the Ionian.
