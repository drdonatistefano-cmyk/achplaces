Duomo di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage

At the highest point of the historic centre, it is the symbol of the ceramics of the Vietrese: the seventeenth-century church has the dome covered with blue and yellow majolica.

Why go. The majolica dome symbol of Vietri.
