Stufato alla sangiovannese

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Plate mentioned in a document of the fifteenth century, of veal cooked slowly with a set of spices, celebrated every year by the Palio of the stew among the local cooks called "stofatari" (stofatari), other typical product is tarese, traditional salami recognized by Slow Food.

Why go. The dish of the city, with its stake.
