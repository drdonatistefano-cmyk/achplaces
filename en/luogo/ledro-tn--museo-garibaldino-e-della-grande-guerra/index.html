Museo garibaldino e della Grande Guerra

Museums · City history

In the former wash-house of Bezzecca, it recalls the battle of 1866, fought here by the Garibaldini against the Austrians in the Third War of Independence (that of the "Obedisco"). On the ground floor of the battle, the bust of Garibaldi, documents, rifles and uniforms; on the first floor heirlooms of the two world wars, including a machine gun Schwarzlose and a white folding bicycle of cyclist targets. Open from 1st June to 30th September, 10-18, closed Monday and Tuesday.

Why go. The place of the "Obedisco."
