Area faunistica dell'orso

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 2015 in the locality Colleveduta, about 800 m of altitude on 10.770 m2 of fir forest, bushes, fruit trees and pastures; you can visit with a guide along a dirt track and hosts three brown bears born in captivity. Visits by reservation from March to November (season hours), closed in December and January for hibernation.

Why go. See bears closely in semi-freedom.

  • Where Località Colleveduta, Palena (ritrovo al Museo dell'orso)
  • Entry €10, reduced €5, free up to 5 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
