Museo del giocattolo tradizionale della Sardegna

Museums · Themed

Born from a research of the students of the middle school of Ales (1993-96) with the support of the University of Cagliari, opened in 2002 in the former elementary school of Zeppara and reopened in 2013 after the restoration: handcrafted toys of the Sardinian agropastoral world made with local materials, from the dolls of rags and pumpkin to the carts of asphodelo, trottole and sound instruments, with a carpentry workshop to build them.

Why go. Reed and asphodel toys to try with your hands.
