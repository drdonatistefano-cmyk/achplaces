Museo comunale Trappeto Maratea

Museums · Themed

The main exhibition space of Vico, in a mill of the fourteenth century in the medieval village about 32 meters long: wooden presses, millstone and tools for oil and agricultural work, oil tank, warehouse, stable and a kitchen where the workers fed.

Why go. The underground mill where Vico's oil was born.
