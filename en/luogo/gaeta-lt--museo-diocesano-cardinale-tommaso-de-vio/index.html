Museo diocesano «Cardinale Tommaso de Vio»

Museums · Old masters

Next to the Duomo, it preserves three exultets, scrolls illustrated in parchment of Benedictine production, around the thousand, used in medieval religious functions.

Why go. The rare medieval exultets of Gaeta.
