Parco archeologico della Villa romana di San Vincenzino

History and architecture · Archaeology

Urban residential villa built from the second half of the 1st century BC, with gardens, arcades and a water system that collected water in a large underground cistern, now entirely accessible; in the 2nd-III century A.D. were added thermal baths and a summer triclinium with nymph, from the 5th century was abandoned. In the nineteenth-century building of the park the exhibition "Privata Luxuria" is shown.

Why go. Walk in a Roman cistern.
