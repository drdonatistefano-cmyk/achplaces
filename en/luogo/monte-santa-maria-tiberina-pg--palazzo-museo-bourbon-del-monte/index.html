Palazzo Museo Bourbon del Monte

Museums · City history

The 16th-century castle, the heart of the village, houses the museum that tells the Bourbon del Monte family and its marquisate, imperial fief from 1250 to 1815, and the connection with the city and territory of the Tiber Valley. Recently the route has been enriched with an archaeological section with the findings of the territory.

Why go. The story of a small imperial state between Umbria and Tuscany.
