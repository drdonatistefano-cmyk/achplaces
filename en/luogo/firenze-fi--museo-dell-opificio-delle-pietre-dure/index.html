Museo dell'Opificio delle Pietre Dure

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The court manufacture founded by Ferdinand I in 1588 for the clerk in hard stones: work benches, instruments and masterpieces of inlay.

Why go. The institute that still today restores the masterpieces of Italy.

  • Where Via degli Alfani 78, Firenze
  • Entry €6 in full; €2 reduced
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website opificiodellepietredure.cultura.gov.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
