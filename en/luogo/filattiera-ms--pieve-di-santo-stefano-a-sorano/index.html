Pieve di Santo Stefano a Sorano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Jewel of the 11th century Lunigian Romanesque, with stone façade and walls of river pebbles, built along the Via Francigena probably on a medieval building (VIII-IX century); it is mentioned by Sigerico di Canterbury at the end of the 10th century and in a papal bull of 1148. Nearby were found statues-stele of the Bronze Age and the remains of a Roman village.

Why go. The parish church of the pilgrims of Francigena.
