Piazza Navona e Fontana dei Quattro Fiumi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The symbol square of the Baroque Rome on the path of the Stadio di Domitiano, with the Fountain of the Four Rivers and the church of Sant'Agnese in Agone.

Why go. The most iconic Baroque square.
