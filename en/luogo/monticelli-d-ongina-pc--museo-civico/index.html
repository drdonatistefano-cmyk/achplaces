Museo civico

Museums · City history

Documents on the political and cultural history of the country, relics of the composer Amilcare Zanella and other illustrious mountains, paintings of local contemporary artists and a showcase dedicated to music; during the fairs it hosts temporary exhibitions. Open on Sunday 15.30-17.30.

Why go. The memory of the country and composer Zanella.
