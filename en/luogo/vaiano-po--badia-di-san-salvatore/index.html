Badia di San Salvatore

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Monastic complex founded in 1073 by the Benedictines of Cluniac and passed to the Vallombrosans in 1090, around which the medieval village developed. The Romanesque church and the bell tower are from the 12th to the 11th century, the cloister is Renaissance and the complex also has baroque elements. Agnolo Firenzuola was abbot for a few years.

Why go. The monastery from which the country was born.
