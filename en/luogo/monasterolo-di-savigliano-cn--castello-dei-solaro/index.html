Castello dei Solaro

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The imposing manor dates back to the first half of the thirteenth century (Visit Cuneese indicates 1241, at the behest of Thomas I of Saluzzo); destroyed in the wars of 1357-1363, it was rebuilt by the Marquises of Saluzzo between 1363 and 1378. In 1604 he passed to the Solaro di Moretta, who took the title of Counts of Monasterolo, and in the seventeenth century the salons were painted and decorated. Massif building adorned with turrets and interrupted by a large tower, it is of the Municipality since 1928 and hosts the municipal offices; it is visited by reservation.

Why go. The fortress of the Marquises of Saluzzo with the decorated salons of the Solaro accounts.
