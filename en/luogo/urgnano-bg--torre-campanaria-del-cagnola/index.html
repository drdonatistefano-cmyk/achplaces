Torre campanaria del Cagnola

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Campanile designed by Luigi Cagnola, the architect of the Arch of Peace in Milan, built between 1824 and 1829 in a gentle strain of Brembate: 54 meters high, circular plan with three cylinders degrading in the Doric, Ionic and Corinthian orders and a balcony of caryatids under the dome; inside, a spiral staircase of 235 steps. The tourist portal indicates it as the highest stone tower in Italy; today it has 12 bells.

Why go. A unique neoclassical bell tower-monument.
