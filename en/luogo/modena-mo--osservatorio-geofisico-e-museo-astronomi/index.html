Osservatorio Geofisico e Museo astronomico e geofisico

Museums · Science and university

Founded in 1826 by Francesco IV d'Este in the east tower of the Ducal Palace, where it still is, the University Observatory hosts the museum with historical instruments; guided tours by reservation, with a climb of about 150 steps.

Why go. Two centuries of observations of the sky and climate.

  • Where Piazza Roma 22, Modena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility About 150 steps, no elevator
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
