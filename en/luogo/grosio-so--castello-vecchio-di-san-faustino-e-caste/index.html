Castello Vecchio di San Faustino e Castello Nuovo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the hill above the Rupe Magna: the Old Castle (X-XI century), with the little church from the Romanesque bell tower and two medieval tombs carved into the rock, and the New Castle wanted by the Visconti between 1350 and 1375, with double belt and fortified tower, dismantled in 1526 by order of the Three Leagues. The excavations in the area revealed a fortified settlement from the sixteenth century BC to the Iron Age; around dry terraces for the vine.

Why go. Two castles and three thousand years of history on the same back.
