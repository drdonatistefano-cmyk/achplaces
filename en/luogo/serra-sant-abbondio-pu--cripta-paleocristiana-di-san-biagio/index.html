Cripta paleocristiana di San Biagio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Crypt of the IV-V century, just outside the village on the left bank of the Cesano, built with materials of Roman era coming from a pagan temple.

Why go. A rare early Christian witness along the Cesano.
