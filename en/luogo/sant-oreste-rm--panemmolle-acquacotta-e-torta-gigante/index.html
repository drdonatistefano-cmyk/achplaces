Panemmolle, acquacotta e torta gigante

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Among the typical dishes are the acquacotta, the frascarelli with ricotta cheese, the gnocchi with beans and the breadcrumbs, pasta with chickpeas, rosemary and tomato served on breadcrumbs; the giant cake is enjoyed at the feast of the patron Saint'Edisto, in October, with historical procession. On the last Sunday of May the Madonna of May brings the torchlight with bundles of reeds lit on the side of the mountain.

Why go. The poor cuisine of the Soratte.
