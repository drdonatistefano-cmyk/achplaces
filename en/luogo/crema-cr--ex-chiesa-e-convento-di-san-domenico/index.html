Ex chiesa e convento di San Domenico

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Church and convent were built between 1463 and 1471 and suppressed, like the other convents of the city, at the beginning of the nineteenth century; today they are the seat of the theatre.

Why go. A fifteenth-century convent became a theatre.
