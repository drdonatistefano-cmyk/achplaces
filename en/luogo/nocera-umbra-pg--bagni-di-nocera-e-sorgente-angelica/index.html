Bagni di Nocera e Sorgente Angelica

Local life and traditions · Spas

The Angelica Spring, in Bagni di Nocera, was drunk for therapeutic purposes as early as 1500 and was among the most popular spas in central Italy, with guests such as Vincenzo Monti, Pirandello and Leopardi; in September 1805 Humboldt and Gay-Lussac came to analyze the water, then exported from Felice Bisleri to the Americas and India.

Why go. The historical source of the waters of Nocera.
