Basilica della Beata Vergine della Ghiara

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

The baroque sanctuary of the seventeenth century with the Crucifixion of Guercino and the cycle of frescoes dedicated to the heroines of the Old Testament; next to the museum of the sanctuary.

Why go. The Guercino that leaves breathless.
