Centro storico e botteghe della ceramica

Local life and traditions · Crafts · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Viuzze, shops and colorful houses with the workshops of the master ceramicists who paint by hand; the tradition dates back at least to the Middle Ages and was renewed in the twenties of the twentieth century by German craftsmen with a new style inspired by daily gestures. Among the buildings symbolizes the Fabbrica Solimene, designed in the fifties by the architect Soleri.

Why go. The shops where Vietri ceramic is born.
