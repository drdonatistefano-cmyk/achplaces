Villa di Tiberio e Grotta

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Maritime villa of the 1st century BC, remodeled by Tiberius that included the natural cave as a nymph; excavated at the end of the fifties, it also includes a fish pond and a spa and extended for over 300 meters along the east beach. The area closes in case of bad weather and is not accessible to people with reduced mobility.

Why go. The emperor's water cave on the sea.
