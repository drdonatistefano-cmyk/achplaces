Museo Cervi

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Established in 1970 and re-arranged in 2002 in the Reggiana peasant house where the Cervi family lived since 1934; the seven brothers were shot by the fascists on December 28, 1943 with Quarto Camurri, and during the war the house helped antifascists, renitents and opponents. The itinerary has three sections (rural work, antifascism and resistance, a family in memory), with agricultural tools, works of art, photographs and the multimedia room Quadrisfera on the twentieth century; on the whole also the Library Archivio Emilio Sereni, a bookshop and an inn.

Why go. The symbolic place of the peasant resistance.

  • Where Via Fratelli Cervi 9, Gattatico
  • Entry €5
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.istitutocervi.it
