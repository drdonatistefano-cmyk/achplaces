Acropoli di Molino a Vento

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

The acropolis of the ancient city, with traces of prehistoric frequentation and ancient protocorinthian ceramics from the end of the 8th century BC. Open from Monday to Friday 9-13; Saturday and Sunday only by booking by email.

Why go. The sacred heart of the Greek city.
