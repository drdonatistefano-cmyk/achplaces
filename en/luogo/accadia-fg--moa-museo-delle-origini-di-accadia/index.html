MOA - Museo delle Origini di Accadia

Museums · Archaeological

Archaeological and civic museum inaugurated on December 6, 2023 in the Palazzo Di Stefano, former prison, at the end of Via Borgo in front of Piazza della Torre dell'Orologio, door of Rione Fossi. It collects sculptures and finds first scattered in the municipal buildings, including the archaeological collection of the ground floor of Palazzo Vassalli and the statue of the Goddess Mother, with immersive installations.

Why go. The origins of Accadia, at the entrance of the River Fossi.
