Fondazione Lazzareschi

Museums · Themed

Founded in 2003, it organizes exhibitions, fairs, conferences and concerts every year; it is located in the center, next to the Town Hall, in a bright and transparent contemporary building that creates a light room around the exhibition spaces, dedicated to the memory of Giuseppe Lazzareschi.

Why go. Exhibitions and events in an art building.
