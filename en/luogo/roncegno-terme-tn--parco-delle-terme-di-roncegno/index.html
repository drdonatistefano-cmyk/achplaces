Parco delle Terme di Roncegno

Nature and landscape · City parks

Spa park of more than four hectares, between clearings and denser areas connected by avenues, with exotic species such as calocedar, sequoia and cryptomeria next to beech trees, hippocastans, plane trees and lindens; it preserves a chapel, an artificial hill, a circular kiosk belvedere and a fountain with spout. The first initiatives to exploit the source date back to 1860, the plant in the green is documented from 1875 and the complex was completed in the early twentieth century with Grand Hotel and Palace Hotel; since 1972 it is of the Autonomous Province of Trento and has been recently recovered.

Why go. A walk through centuries-old trees in the heart of the spa.
