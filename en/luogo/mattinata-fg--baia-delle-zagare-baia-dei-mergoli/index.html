Baia delle Zagare (Baia dei Mergoli)

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Bay between white cliffs and blue-green water, with two calcareous faraglioni a few meters from the shore, the Arch of Diomede and ...The Scissors are about 1 km of beach divided by a spur in the Bay of Faraglioni (sand and gravel) and Zagare Bay (white pebbles and sand), plus the Cala dei Morti reachable only from the sea. Most of the beach is run by hotel establishments: access for guests and with a limited number of daily passes issued by the municipality; you will also arrive by boat from Vieste or Mattinata. Better in the morning, in the afternoon it goes in the shade.

Why go. The Gargano symbol faraglioni.
