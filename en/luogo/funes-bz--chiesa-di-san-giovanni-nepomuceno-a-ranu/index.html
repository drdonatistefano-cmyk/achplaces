Chiesa di San Giovanni Nepomuceno a Ranui

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Church founded in 1744 by the mining owner Michael von Jenner, in an extraordinary landscape; to see the altarpieces of Franz Sebald Unterberger, painter of Val di Fiemme. It is privately owned and the entrance fee is charged; you ask not to enter the fenced meadows. From the bus stop Ranui 5 minutes walk; parking fee.

Why go. The little church of the meadows of Ranui with the Odle behind.
