Palazzo Fiamingo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace of 1718, date carved on the portal, built by the Cutrullà family, then hegemony in Zungri; it was the seat of the town hall and theatre of the revolt of 1901, when hundreds of citizens broke into the municipal council against the appropriation of the former state property lands assigned to the community in 1806. Today it is home to Pro Loco (prolocozungri@gmail.com).

Why go. The palace of the peasant revolt of 1901.
