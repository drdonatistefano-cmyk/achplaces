Villa della Mensa

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

A polygonal villa with a cloistered court, built between 1474 and 1495 for Bartolomeo della Rovere, residence of the bishops of Ferrara until 1797.

Why go. A monumental complex not known of the extensive countryside.
