Galata Museo del Mare e sottomarino Nazario Sauro

Museums · Themed

Six centuries of maritime history in 31 rooms and about 4,300 original objects, with the section on Colombo and that on emigration; moored next to the submarine Nazario Sauro, built in 1976 and abandoned in 2002, visitable inside.

Why go. The museum of the sea of Genoa.
