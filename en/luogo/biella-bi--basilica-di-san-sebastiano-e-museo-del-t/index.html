Basilica di San Sebastiano e Museo del Territorio

Places of worship · Basilicas

Renaissance church of elegant frescoes by Gerolamo Tornielli in 1540, the most beautiful in the city; in the cloister the Museo del Territorio biellase with Roman finds.

Why go. The Renaissance in Biella.
