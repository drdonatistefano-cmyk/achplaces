Palazzo Pigozzi o Pretorio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Ancient building with austere appearance six-seventh-century, just outside the village in the direction of Redondesco: although with the characteristic elements of the villa, it was born as a public palace. The double nature is explained by the history of Gazoldo, the true lordship of the Ippoliti with a special area of autonomy even during the Gonzaga domination and the right to beat its own currency.

Why go. The public palace of a small autonomous lordship.
