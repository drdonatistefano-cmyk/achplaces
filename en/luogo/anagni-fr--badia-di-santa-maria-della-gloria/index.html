Badia di Santa Maria della Gloria

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Built between 1226 and 1231 at the behest of Cardinal Ugolino Conti, future Gregory IX: fortified monastic complex in yellowish tufa.

Why go. A monastery-fortress of the thirteenth century.
