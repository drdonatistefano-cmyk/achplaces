Eremo di Santa Caterina del Sasso

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

According to tradition, the merchant Alberto Besozzi, escaped a storm on the lake, in 1195 had a small church built in thanksgiving to the saint; the complex then became a monastery, inhabited over the centuries by various religious communities until its closure in 1770. It has been the property of the Province of Varese, it has been restored since 1970 and in 1992 frescoes from the fourteenth century have been re-emerged. It comprises three buildings: the southern convent with a long arched corridor on granite columns and the frescoed capitular hall, the convent with a portico with acute arches, refectory and cells, and the church with fourteenth-century bell tower and mural paintings around the altar. You go down from the square with a panoramic staircase of 268 steps or with the elevator; from the lake, arriving by boat, the steps are about 80.

Why go. A monastery clinging to the rock overlooking Lake Maggiore.
