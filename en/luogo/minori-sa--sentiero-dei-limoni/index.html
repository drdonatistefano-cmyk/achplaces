Sentiero dei Limoni

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Walking path of medium-low difficulty, with about 400 steps and about an hour of walk, which connects Minori and Maiori passing from the village of Torre to the church of San Michele, between lemon gardens (including the Sfusato Amalfi, lemon IGP Costa d'Amalfi) and views of the promontory between the two countries.

Why go. Lemonets terraced between Minors and Maiori.
