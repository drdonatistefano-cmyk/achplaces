Museo storico-etnografico provinciale Augusto Doro

Museums · Themed

Born from the collection of the Turin-based researcher Augusto Doro and the Society of Historical Studies in Cuneo, it has been a museum since 1996 and in 2011 it has been re-equipped with a multimedia path. It tells the story of agriculture from its origins to the twentieth century: maps and models about the birth of the rural landscape, the cultivation of cereals, frescoes and scenes of palaces such as Lagnasco and Stupinigi, the photographs of Paul Scheuermeier and Clemens Kalisher. Tour guide is enabled, included in the ticket.

Why go. The peasant civilization of Cuneese inside the castle.
