Torre Gerosolimitana

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Civic tower of the 14th century about 28 meters high in the highest point of the historic center, with views from the Adriatic to the Sibillini Mountains; it had no defensive function and in the 19th century there was placed an altar to the Unity of Italy. Opening on request to the Tourist Office.

Why go. From the sea to the Sibillini.
