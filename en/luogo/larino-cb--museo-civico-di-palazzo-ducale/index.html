Museo civico di Palazzo Ducale

Museums · Archaeological

In the medieval Palazzo Ducale, also seat of the Town Hall, hosts the permanent archaeological exhibition "Ars et Ingenium" with objects of the territory from the 10th century BC to the 4th A.D.: vases, amphorae, ointments, spears, helmets, lamps and statues related to the cult of Mars, protector of the ancient Larinum, a group with Hercules and Priapo and polychrome mosaics of the patrician villas of the municipality. The most famous piece is the mosaic of the third century A.D. with the wolf that feeds Romolo and Remo. The MiC card does not contain usable times: check before the visit.

Why go. The Roman mosaic of the wolf, known all over the world.
