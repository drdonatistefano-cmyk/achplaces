Museo parrocchiale di arte sacra e contadina

Museums · Themed

Two sections: sacred art of the XVIII-XIX century with a statue of the Addolorata of the 1700, vestments, a noble 19th century dress with golden embroidery, sacred images, plates for hosts, silver and a front of a tabernacle in inlaid marble; local life with peasant tools and artisans, household objects, traditional customs of Gizzeria (for maiden, married and widow), a stone millstone, clay bricks and straw and covered with broom.

Why go. Traditional customs and sacred art of the parish.

the MiC card indicates "Closed" every day of the week; contact the parish (0968 403720) before the visit
