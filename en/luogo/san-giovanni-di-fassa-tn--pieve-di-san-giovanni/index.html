Pieve di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Late-Gothic church consecrated in 1489 on an earlier Romanesque church, symbol of the village, with the 67-metre-long bell tower covered with larch stairs; in the apse frescoes by Ruprecht Potsch (1498) and paintings by David Solbach (1578). Nearby stands the Ciasa Soldà, national monument of the sixteenth century.

Why go. The parish church of the valley and its very high bell tower.
