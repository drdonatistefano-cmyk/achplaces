Chiesa della Beata Vergine Maria del Soccorso e Piazza Duca degli Abruzzi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

With Piazza Duca degli Abruzzi it forms the main complex representative of the historic center of Picciano: the monumental brick façade dominates the square, about 130 cm lower than the building, and is divided into three parts by binated pilasters, with central portal between two composite columns under a triangular tympanum and side wings with broken tympanums. The interior, of baroque taste, has an inscribed cross plan, dome on the central compartment and semicircular apse. It is the parish church (fornìa di Penne).

Why go. The heart of the historic centre, with a spectacular brick façade.
