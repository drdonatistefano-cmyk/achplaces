Collegiata di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The main church of the city, overlooking Piazza Duomo, with walls entirely covered by frescoes from the fourteenth century.

Why go. One of the most complete frescoed cycles in Tuscany.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, San Gimignano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
