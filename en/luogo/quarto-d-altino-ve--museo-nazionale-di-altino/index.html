Museo nazionale di Altino

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

It rises in the vast archaeological area of the Roman city of Altinum, along the Via Annia on the edge of the lagoon, in an area that is part of the UNESCO site "Venice and its lagoon"; it exhibits over 40,000 pieces that document the frequentation of the territory in prehistoric, Venetian and Roman times. Open from Tuesday to Saturday 8-19 and Sundays and holidays 14-19 (ticket office until 18.30), closed on Mondays; cumulative ticket with Portogruaro and Concordia Sagittaria €10 and annual subscription - Altino 365 Â€15.

Why go. Forty thousand finds of the city that begat Venice.
