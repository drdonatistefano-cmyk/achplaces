Chiesa rotonda di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the XII-XIII century as a round church, it was the chapel of the castle of San Giorgio (Alt-Schenna), of which only the mast remains, today "Uhlentower"; in 1591 a lightning strike destroyed almost half and the central part was reinforced with a pillar. The frescoes of the fourteenth century, well restored, show the martyrdom of St. George, the universal Judgment and the legend of St. Nicholas; there is an altar with Gothic doors with St. George on horseback and a rare seventeenth-century image of St. Kümmernis. Open from the end of March to the beginning of November 11.15-15.30, closed in winter; audio guide available. From Scena about 30 minutes walk or by bus 233 and 234.

Why go. A rare round church with Gothic frescoes.
