Casa Buonarroti

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The houses purchased by Michelangelo, with two of his youthful reliefs, the Madonna della Scala and the Battle of the Centauri.

Why go. The teenage Michelangelo.

  • Entry €8 in full
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
