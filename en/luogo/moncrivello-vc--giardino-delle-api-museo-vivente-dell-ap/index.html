Giardino delle Api, museo vivente dell'apicoltura

Museums · Themed

Educational garden on the history of beekeeping, biodiversity and sustainability, which recovers ancient practices of nourishment of bees and hosts over one hundred species of herbs, shrubs and honeycombs. It offers sensory activities: recognizing the fragrances of plants, tasting different honeys, discovering how bees communicate with smells; designed for schools, enthusiasts and families.

Why go. A hundred honeys and honeys to taste.
