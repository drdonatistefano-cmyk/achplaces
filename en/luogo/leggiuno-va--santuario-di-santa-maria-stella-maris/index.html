Santuario di Santa Maria Stella Maris

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Modern shrine, built in the second half of the twentieth century on the initiative of the local community in the hamlet of Reno, directly overlooking Lake Maggiore. It has a simple façade with a sailing bell tower; inside a statue of the Madonna and Child facing the lake, to protect fishermen and sailors. It is a destination of pilgrimages especially in May.

Why go. A small church on the water dedicated to the fishermen of the lake.
