Villa Corbella-Martinelli-Sioli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

It belonged to the Corbella family (in the cadastral maps of the 1820s it was found by Ludovico Corbella), passed to the Martinelli family, who gave it to the archbishops of Milan: since 1820 it was their summer residence. After the laws of 1866 it was sold at auction to Angelo Sioli; acquired and restored by the municipality, it houses municipal offices, a conference room and an exhibition room.

Why go. The summer villa of the archbishops of Milan, today a civic and exhibition space.
