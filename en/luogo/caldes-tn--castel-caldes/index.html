Castel Caldes

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Gothic castle was built after 1230, when Bishop Gerardo allowed Arnold of Cagnò to build a fortified house outside the village; in 1464 he passed to the Thun, who expanded it, and in 1880 he was sold to the Malanotti, before the restoration of the Province. It contains frescoed ceilings, a stone spiral staircase, the stube of the restored count, the painted room called Olinda's prison, linked to the legend of the daughter of the Count in love with the minstrello, and the chapel of the Nativity of Mary frescoed by Elia Naurizio in 1620. It is part of the Castles of Buonconsiglio, it is open in summer with exhibitions, concerts and guided tours and is a stop of the Trenino dei Castelli.

Why go. The castle of Olinda's legend.
