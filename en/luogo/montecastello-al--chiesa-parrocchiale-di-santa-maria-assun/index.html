Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta di Ponzano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish Church of the village, indicated by the Diocese of Alexandria as "Santa Maria di Ponzano in Montecastello" and cited by the FAI among the places of the village. Above the entrance door there is a wall organ of the Lombard school of organaria dated 1827 (inscription "Ottava basso - Monte Castello 1827"), with grey case with double pilasters, 27 canes in façade arranged with cusp, keyboard of 56 keys and 18 registers; it was reformed in 1931 by the organaro Edward Negri of Genoa Bolzano.

Why go. A Lombard organ of 1827 in the parish of the village.
