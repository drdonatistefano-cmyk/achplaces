Campanile di San Mercuriale

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The Romanesque bell tower of Lombard type over 70 meters high, erected in 1178-1180 by master Aliotto next to the basilica-abbey.

Why go. He was pierced from above on opening days.

It only goes up on special occasions: Feast of Our Lady of Fire (February 4), St. Mercury and other events
