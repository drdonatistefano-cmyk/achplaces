Castello ducale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortified building with thick walls and a 12-metre tower; the paved courtyard ends in a Renaissance loggia with a wide view of the countryside. Perhaps of the ninth century, it was used by Frederick II as a hunting lodge and meeting place of the poets of the Sicilian School; in the sixteenth century there held dinners the humanist Giovan Battista Manso, with Torquato Tasso host, and in the eighteenth century became residence of the Duke Ascanio Pignatelli.

Why go. The Renaissance loggia overlooking Irpinia.
