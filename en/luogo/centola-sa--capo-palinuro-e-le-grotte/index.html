Capo Palinuro e le grotte

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Promontory between the Gulf of Velia and the Gulf of Policastro, with walls peaking up to 50 meters and 32 caves above and below sea level. The Blue Grotto, the largest, has a triangular entrance of about 6 by 10 meters and lights up blue for an underwater siphon, with sulphurous springs, stalactites and stalagmites; then the Blood Grotto with reddish walls, the Monks Grotto, the Silver Grotto and the Fetente Cala with the smell of sulphur. The Bay of Buondormire can only be reached by boat.

Why go. The Blue Grotto by boat.
