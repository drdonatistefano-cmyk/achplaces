Museo archeologico territoriale di Terzigno (MATT)

Museums · Archaeological

In the former renovated municipal slaughterhouse, since 2019 the exhibition "Pompei oltre le mura" shows frescoed walls and objects of the Roman villas of Cava Ranieri (Ville 1, 2 and 6) buried by the eruption of 79 A.D.: second style paintings with architectural perspectives, restored by the archaeological park of Pompeii, and the larario of the kitchen of Villa 6 with two Lari, the genius who sacrifices and two snakes. Since 2025, a new room has been showing the Terzigno Treasure of the Villa 2: silver (a situla with swan-head, a cup, a mirror, 21 coins) and three gold necklaces, one with emeralds. Free admission with a valid Pompeii ticket.

Why go. The rustic villas of Vesuvius and their treasure.
