Monastero di San Francesco delle Monache

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Monastery founded between 1230 and 1235, with Baroque interiors and paintings by Jusepe de Ribera, Pietro da Cortona and Francesco de Mura.

Why go. Ribera and Pietro da Cortona in a thirteenth-century monastery.
