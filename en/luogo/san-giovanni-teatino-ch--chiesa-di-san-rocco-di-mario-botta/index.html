Chiesa di San Rocco di Mario Botta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish complex designed by Mario Botta (project of 2009) for an urban area growing, with church, working chapel, parish center, colonnade and parish church. Outside it is a compact volume that slims upwards: the plant is a large square with a central apse and two lateral ones, but tilting up the building takes on a cross shape recognizable in the landscape, and inside the light descends from a Greek cross skylight.

Why go. Contemporary sacred architecture signed by Botta.
