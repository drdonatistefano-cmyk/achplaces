Arche Scaligere

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage

The funeral complex of the La Scala family next to the church of Santa Maria Antica, one of the best examples of European Gothic art; in history is the place where Juliet pretended to die.

Why go. The Gothic tombs of the lords of Verona.
