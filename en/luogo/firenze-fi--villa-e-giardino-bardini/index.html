Villa e Giardino Bardini

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Terraced garden on the hill of Costa San Giorgio, with a pergola of wisteria, a baroque staircase and one of the most beautiful views of Florence.

Why go. In April, the gallery of wisteria in bloom.

  • Where Costa San Giorgio 2, Firenze
  • Entry €10 whole (Feel Florence, April 2026); with Boboli €15
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.destinationflorence.com
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
