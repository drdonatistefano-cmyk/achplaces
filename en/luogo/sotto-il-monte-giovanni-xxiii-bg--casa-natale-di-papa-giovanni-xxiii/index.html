Casa natale di Papa Giovanni XXIII

Museums · House museums

In the farmhouse called Cascina Palazzo was born on 25 November 1881 Angelo Giuseppe Roncalli, pope from 1958 to 1963 and saint from 2014. You can visit the arched porch, the wooden staircase that goes up to the simple rooms, the bedroom of birth with a double bed, drawer-desk and a painting of the Virgin, and a small museum of memories. Not accessible to people with motor disabilities.

Why go. Rooms where the good pope grew.
