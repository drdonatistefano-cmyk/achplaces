Museo diocesano d'arte sacra Santa Giusta

Museums · Old masters

Housed in the oratory of Our Lady of the Rosary, next to the parish, exhibits pieces from the XVI-XVIII centuries, especially liturgical objects.

Why go. Silver and sacred furnishings next to Santa Giusta.
