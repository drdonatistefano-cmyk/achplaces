Antiquarium di Torre Cimalonga

Museums · Archaeological · Viewpoint

Museum in a 16th-century defensive tower in one of the most panoramic squares in the historic centre, founded in 1987 and re-arranged in 2004: the exhibits, in chronological order and in windows obtained in the ancient fire vents, range from the stone instruments of the Middle Paleolithic of Torre Talao to the community of the hill of Petrosa (VII-VI century BC) to Ponticello in the Roman age; there are also exhibits of the archaeological Park of Laos. Open Monday-Friday 8.30-15, closed Saturday and Sunday.

Why go. From the Paleolithic to the late antiquity in a tower.
