Santuario di Nostra Signora del Santo Sepolcro e di Maria Bambina

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

15th century shrine in the chestnut wood accessible from the village. The façade has a rose window made from a single block of stone; inside a wall frescoed by Pietro Guido da Ranzo with Stories of the Passion and a Madonna with Child in Carrara marble by sculptor Filippo Parodi, pupil of Bernini.

Why go. 15th-century frescoes and a baroque marble in chestnut wood.
