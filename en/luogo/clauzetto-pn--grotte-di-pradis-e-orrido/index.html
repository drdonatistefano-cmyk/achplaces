Grotte di Pradis e Orrido

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the Pradis plateau, more than 500 meters in the heart of the Carnic Alps, hundreds of karst cavities; the visit includes the Cave of the Madonna, the Orrido with waterfalls, natural arches and caves, and a ring walk over the gorge. Free for children under 6 years old, people with disabilities and residents; guided tours for groups by reservation (€10-12).

Why go. A karst gorge between waterfalls and rock arches.

seasonal opening from the end of March to the beginning of November, in low season only some weekends (common calendar)
