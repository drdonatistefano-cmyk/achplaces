Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta antica (chiesa del cimitero)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church, already parish, that the catalogue of the Ministry dates back to the first half of the 11th century; bound since 1908, is owned by the Municipality and was extensively restored in 1985. The façade in pebbles with fishbone preserves the primitive forms, simple and harmonious, despite repeated interventions; inside a cycle of frescoes of the first fifteenth century attributed to Pseudo Domenico della Marca d'Ancona.

Why go. An 11th century Romanesque with frescoes from the early 15th century.
