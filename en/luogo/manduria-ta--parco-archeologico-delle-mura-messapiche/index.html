Parco archeologico delle Mura Messapiche

History and architecture · Archaeology

Three megalithic belts of the V, IV and III century B.C.: the inner one about 2 km long, the outer one about 5 meters thick, 6-7 high and 3 km long. It includes a necropolis of more than 1200 graves, the Plinian Fountain, perennial source in a large karst cavity perhaps cited by Pliny in Naturalis Historia, and the small church of St. Peter Mandurino with a frescoed underground crypt.

Why go. The walls that resisted Taranto and Archidamo.
