Villa romana del Randaccio

History and architecture · Archaeology

Large maritime villa on three levels, excavated in 1988-1991: about 1,300 square meters and 40 rooms between residence with mosaics and heating and productive spaces, in use from the 1st century BC to the 4th A.D., perhaps also station between Aquileia and Tergeste (Fons Timavi) Visits by reservation in the aqueduct park.

Why go. The Roman villa at the Timavo sources.
