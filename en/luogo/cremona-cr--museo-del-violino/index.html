Museo del Violino

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the Palazzo dell'Arte designed by Carlo Cocchia and inaugurated in 1947, five centuries of Cremonese violin with the violin "Il Cremonese" by Stradivari of 1715 in the room "Lo casket of treasures"; next, the Auditorium Giovanni Arvedi of 460 seats.

Why go. Listen to and see Stradivari's tools.

  • Where Piazza Marconi 5, Cremona
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website museodelviolino.org
