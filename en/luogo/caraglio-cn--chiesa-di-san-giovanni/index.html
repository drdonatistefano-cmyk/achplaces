Chiesa di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Church of Gothic plant with a painting cycle of the fifteenth century; it dominates the village from above and is connected to the center of the village by a steep staircase.

Why go. 15th century frescoes above the roofs.
