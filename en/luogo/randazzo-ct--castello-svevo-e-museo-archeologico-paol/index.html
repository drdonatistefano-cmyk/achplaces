Castello svevo e Museo archeologico Paolo Vagliasindi

Museums · Archaeological

The museum is housed in the Swabian Castle, the last of the eight towers of the walls, called the Man for its key role in the defence of the city; in the thirteenth century its secrets were used as torture chambers for prisoners and condemned. It collects finds from the VI-IV century BC, largely from the nineteenth-century collection of Paolo Vagliasindi and mainly from the area of Sant'Anastasia, in the territory of Randazzo.

Why go. Greek finds in a medieval tower.
