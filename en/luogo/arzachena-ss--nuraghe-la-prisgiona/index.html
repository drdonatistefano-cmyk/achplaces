Nuraghe La Prisgiona

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On a granite hill above the valley of Capichera, used from the 14th to the 8th century BC: mast with two side towers joined by a curved bastion, entrance architraves of about 3 meters and 7 tons, tholos room almost 7 meters high and a well in the courtyard about 7 meters deep. Around a village of almost one hundred huts on about 5 hectares, with a meeting hut; traces of wine were found in the jugs. Open every day 9-19.

Why go. A nuragic village of a hundred huts.
