Castello di Lanciano e Museo Maria Sofia Giustiniani Bandini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Castle surrounded by an ancient park, built by Giovanni da Varano to guard the north-eastern border of Camerino and transformed into a noble residence by Giovanna Malatesta in 1489; in 1754 the Bandini bought it and the Marquis Alessandro had it enlarged and decorated in the gallery on the first floor by Giovanni Antinori. In 1977 Maria Sofia Giustiniani Bandini donated it with his goods to the Bishopric of Camerino: the museum preserves furnishings, paintings and curiosity of the family, the gallery and the eighteenth-century staircase. Castle and park can be visited; according to the regional portal opens Sunday and holidays 11-13 and 14-18.30.

Why go. A noble residence intact with its park.

italia.it indicates the closed museum for earthquake damage 2016, while the regional portal reports timetables and tickets; the two sources also give different construction dates (1382-1385 and 1350-1418)
