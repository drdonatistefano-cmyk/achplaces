Rocca Pallavicino Casali e Cappella Bembo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Made by Rolando Pallavicino from 1420, a rectangular plan with moat, cylindrical towers, mast and round paths; it contains a chapel with a cycle of Renaissance frescoes commissioned by Carlo Pallavicino, bishop of Lodi, attributed to Bonifacio Bembo, perhaps with his brother Benedetto (mid-15th century).

Why go. The Bembo frescoes in a lowland fortress.
