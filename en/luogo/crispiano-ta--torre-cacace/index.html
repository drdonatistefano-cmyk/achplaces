Torre Cacace

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Symbol of Crispiano, built at the end of the nineteenth century by the wealthy engineer Carlo Cacace as a family funeral mausoleum. From the top, the view extends over the ravines to the Ionian Sea.

Why go. The view of the ravines to the Ionian Sea.
