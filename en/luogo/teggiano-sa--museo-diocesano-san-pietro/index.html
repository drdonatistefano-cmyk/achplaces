Museo diocesano «San Pietro»

Museums · Themed

In the former church of St. Peter, built on a classical temple: stone sculptures of the ancient and medieval city, liturgical furnishings, statues and paintings from the Middle Ages to the contemporary age, and the fresco of the Madonna del Latte. Closed on Monday.

Why go. The history of Diano in stone and sacred art.
