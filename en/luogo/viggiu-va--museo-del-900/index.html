Museo del '900

Museums · Modern art

Pavilion built in the 1960s in the Butti Park at the behest of the widow of the sculptor Giacomo Buzzi Reschini and reopened in autumn 2006 after the restoration, it presents an anthology of the Viggiuto sculpture of the twentieth century. The central halls are dedicated to Buzzi Reschini, with the monumental group "The good that lands the evil" and sketches of monuments; one room is dedicated to Nando Conti and another to Bottinelli, Cattò, Cedraschi and Freschetti, with the plaster model of the head of the Baptist whose marble is in the parish church. Admission ticket, accessible to people with motor disabilities; opening hours: Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 14-18.30, Wednesday and Saturday 10-12 and 14-18.30, Sunday 16-19, Monday closed.

Why go. The school of sculptors of Viggiu in the twentieth century.
