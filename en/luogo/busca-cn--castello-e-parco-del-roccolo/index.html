Castello e Parco del Roccolo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

A neo-Gothic castle with three towers built since 1831 by the Marquis Roberto Tapparelli d'Azeglio, Massimo's brother, as a summer residence; Queen Margherita stayed there for many summers. Lounges with frescoes and stuccoes and part of the furniture of the family of Azeglio; around a romantic park of about 50 hectares attributed to Xavier Kurten, with panoramic paths, cave, lake, fountains and monumental greenhouses for citrus. Open on the first and third Sundays of the month from May to October, 14:30-19:00, with guided tours.

Why go. The neo-Gothic Sabaudo overlooking the plain.
