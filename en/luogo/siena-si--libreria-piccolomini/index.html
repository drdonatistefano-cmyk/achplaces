Libreria Piccolomini

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The hall built in 1492 for the library of Pius II, frescoed by Pinturicchio, with the choral illuminated by Girolamo da Cremona and Liberale da Verona.

Why go. Colors intact after five centuries.

  • Where Duomo di Siena, Piazza del Duomo
  • Entry paid
