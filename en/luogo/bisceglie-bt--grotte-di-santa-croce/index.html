Grotte di Santa Croce

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Karst complex excavated from an underground stream, discovered in 1934 by Francesco Saverio Majellaro, with remains of Neanderthal, animals, stone instruments and paintings; reopened to the public in 1990.

Why go. Prehistory in the cave.
