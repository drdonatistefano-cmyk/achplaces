Villa Monastero, casa museo

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

Born from a Cistercian female monastery of the late 12th century, it passed to the Mornics, Carolina Maumari, Walter Kees and De Marchi, who gave it the eclectic aspect and in 1939 donated it to the State. The route of the museum house touches 14 rooms with original decorations and furnishings, including the Neapolitan Black Room, the Music Room, the Mornic Room, the Neorococo Red Room and the Fermi Room, where Enrico Fermi held his last lessons in 1954. Since 1953, the convention center hosts the summer courses of the Italian School of Physics.

Why go. Four centuries of life of a villa on the lake, to Enrico Fermi.
