Lago Foppa

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

Lake Moncenisio open to the public and members; the fishing is reserved for holders of the card of the US Susa, which can be purchased on site in the wooden house near the lake, where also the good fishing is released.

Why go. An alpine lake for a stopover or a day of fishing.
