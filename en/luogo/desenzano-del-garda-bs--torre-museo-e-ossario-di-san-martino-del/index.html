Torre, Museo e Ossario di San Martino della Battaglia

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Monument to the battle of 24 June 1859: the 64-metre-high tower, with statues of the protagonists of the Risorgimento, frescoed ramp by Vittorio Emanuele Bressanin and panoramic terrace on the lower Garda, the morainic hills and the plain; behind, the museum with weapons, relics, documents and objects of the soldiers; along the avenue the Ossario Church with 1,274 skulls in the apse and the remains of 2,619 fallen in the crypt. Open all year round.

Why go. The Risorgimento seen from the top of the tower.
