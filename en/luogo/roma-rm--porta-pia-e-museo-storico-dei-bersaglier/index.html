Porta Pia e Museo Storico dei Bersaglieri

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

The door built in 1561-1565 in place of Porta Nomentana, where on 20 September 1870 the breach marked the annexation of Rome to the Kingdom of Italy; it houses the museum of the Bersaglieri.

Why go. The breach of 1870.
