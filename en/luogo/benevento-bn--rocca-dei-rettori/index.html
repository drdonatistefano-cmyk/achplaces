Rocca dei Rettori

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The main place of power of the city, begun in the fourteenth century to protect the papal governor at the top of Corso Garibaldi; it houses the provincial administration and museum collections, with a panoramic terrace.

Why go. The panorama from the top of the city.
