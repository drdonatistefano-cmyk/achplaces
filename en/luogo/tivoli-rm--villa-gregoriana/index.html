Villa Gregoriana

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Natural park wanted by Pope Gregory XVI around 1834, with natural and artificial waterfalls, caves, terraces and archaeological remains; it is managed by FAI and is visited from March to December in about an hour and a half.

Why go. Waterfalls and caves in the Aniene gorge.
