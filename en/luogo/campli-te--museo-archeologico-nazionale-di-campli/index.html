Museo Archeologico Nazionale di Campli

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the thirteenth century convent of San Francesco, inaugurated in 1989, exhibits the accessories of the necropolis of Campovalano (scavations since 1967), with the rich tombs of the VII-VI century BC: jewelry, a necklace in magnagreca gold foil, weapons, bucchero and a Phoenician head in vitreous paste.

Why go. The treasures of Campovalano.

  • Where Piazza San Francesco 6, Campli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
