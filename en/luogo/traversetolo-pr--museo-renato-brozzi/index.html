Museo Renato Brozzi

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

Dedicated to the sculptor and sculptor Renato Brozzi (Traversetolo, 1885-1963), who donated his works to the country: over 8,000 drawings, 400 photographs, about 80 plasters and about fifty sculptures and works of applied art, with the artist's library. A section tells of the connection with Gabriele d'Annunzio, who called him the greatest Italian animalist after Pisanello, with a hundred letters signed (1920-1936). Closed Monday and all August; the ticket gives a discount to Magnani-Rocca.

Why go. The animalier loved by D'Annunzio, in his country.

  • Where Centro civico La Corte-Bruno Agresti, Via Fratelli Cantini 8, Traversetolo
  • Entry €5, reduced €3, students €2; free on the first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
