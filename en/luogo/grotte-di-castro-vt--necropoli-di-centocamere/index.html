Necropoli di Centocamere

History and architecture · Archaeology

In front of the plateau of Civita, the oldest necropolis (tombs of the VII century BC), with rooms dug into the tuff connected by a labyrinth of corridors, some opened by the tombbarols; the largest tomb, the CC4, is about 16 meters long with five rooms in axis and three lateral and it welcomed 19 burials. You can visit with the ticket of the Museo Civita.

Why go. The maze of rooms in the tuff.
