Diga del Vajont e visita al coronamento

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The arched dam, 261 meters high according to Italia.it, was built by Sade since 1957; on the evening of October 9, 1963 a landslide of about 270 million cubic meters from Mount Toc fell into the basin and the wave destroyed Longarone and part of Erto and Casso, with almost 2,000 victims. Today it is a memorial: the Park organizes a guided tour of 50 minutes on the crown (groups of maximum 40, meeting at the square of the memorial church, tickets on VivaTicket or at the information point) and visits of 2-3 hours to the places of memory. Path not suitable for those suffering from vertigo.

Why go. Walk on the dam, in front of the landslide of the Knock.

Crowning visits suspended from November 2 for the winter break (Park Calendar 2026)
