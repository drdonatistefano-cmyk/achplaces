Basilica di San Francesco e Cappella Bacci

Places of worship · Basilicas · Paid entry

Gothic church of the thirteenth century in bricks: in the Bacci chapel the cycle of the Legend of the True Cross by Piero della Francesca (1452-1466), one of the masterpieces of Renaissance painting; also an Annunciation attributed to Signorelli.

Why go. Visit by reservation, with shifts of about half an hour.

  • Where Piazza San Francesco 1, Arezzo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
