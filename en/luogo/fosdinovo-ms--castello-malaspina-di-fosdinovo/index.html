Castello Malaspina di Fosdinovo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The best preserved castle of Lunigiana, built from 1340 on towers prior to 1200: antique furnishings, paintings, legend of Bianca Maria Aloisia walled alive and the tradition of Dante's stay in 1306.

Why go. From walkways seen to the sea.

  • Where Via Papiriana 2, Fosdinovo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
