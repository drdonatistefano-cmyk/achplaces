Abbazia di San Giacomo e Museo del Monastero

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded in 1079 by Saint Albert of Prezzate, who returned from the pilgrimage to Santiago donated some of his assets to Cluny and placed the monastery under Abbot Ugo, expanding a church of Saints James, Bassano and Nicola. Here, on April 7, 1167, tradition places the oath of the Lombard League against Federico Barbarossa. The visit includes the fourteenth-century basilica, of Gothic style inside, with Romanesque capitals, frescoes in part of the Marinoni family and medieval Lombard sculptures, including bas-reliefs of the ark of Saint Albert; bell tower and statue of the saint are Renaissance. The complex is completed by the two cloisters, the Renaissance sacristy, the capitular hall and the museum, with paintings and ancient objects used by monks.

Why go. Almost a thousand years of history, from Pontida's oath to the cloisters.
