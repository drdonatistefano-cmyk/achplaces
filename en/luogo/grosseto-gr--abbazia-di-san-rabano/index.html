Abbazia di San Rabano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The ruins of the monastery of Santa Maria Alborense in the woods of Uccellina, documented by the Middle Ages and recently restored; it is reached with a walking itinerary of about 8 or 17 km.

Why go. A historical hike in the park.
