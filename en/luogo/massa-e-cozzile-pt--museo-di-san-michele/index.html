Museo di San Michele

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated in 2004 in the chapel and in two rooms of the former monastery, it exhibits liturgical furnishings and documents of the church of Santa Maria Assunta and of the Municipal Historic Archives: the "Presentation of Mary at the Temple" probably attributed to Luigi Crespi, the wooden choir of the late eighteenth century, silk and gold vestments, the silver monsory of 1663 by Paolo Laurentini, the processional cross of 1664 and the reliquary of the Holy Cross of 1722. The chapel has paintings of 1714-1718.

Why go. Florentine silvers of the seventeenth century.
