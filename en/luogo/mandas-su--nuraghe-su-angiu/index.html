Nuraghe Su Angiu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

A nuragic five-tower complex, one of the almost 50 Nuragic sites in the territory; there was found a bronze ship today exhibited at the National Archaeological Museum of Cagliari.

Why go. The nuraghe with five towers of the Trexenta.
