Cappella gentilizia del SS. Crocifisso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Chapel of the castle, stage of the Sentieri dei Frescanti del Roero: ceilings in trompe-l'oeil with angels, attributed to the Operts and in baroque style, including an angel showing the Holy Shroud.

Why go. An illusionistic baroque with the Shroud among angels.
