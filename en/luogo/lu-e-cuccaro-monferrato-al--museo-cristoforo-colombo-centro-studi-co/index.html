Museo Cristoforo Colombo (Centro Studi Colombiani)

Museums · Themed

In the center of Cuccaro, the museum of the Colombian Study Centre is dedicated to Christopher Columbus and to the kinship, supported by studies and documents, between the Colombo family of Cuccaro and the navigator. Visit by reservation by phone to the Municipality (0131 771928, 9-13 hours, Saturday included).

Why go. On the monferrine traces of Christopher Columbus.
