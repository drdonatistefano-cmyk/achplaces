Abbazia dei Santi Nazario e Celso

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded in 1040 by the bishop of Novara Riprando with the accounts of Biandrate and entrusted to the Benedictines, it was fortified in the 13th century near a ford of Sesia: there remain sections of moat and walls and three of the four corner towers. The Romanesque church has a two-storey atrium and a pebble bell tower with fishbone; the 15th-century Gothic-Lombard church has a hut façade with rosette and three naves, and the fifteenth-century cloister preserves a cycle of frescoes on the life of St. Benedict. Among the frescoes of the church, Santa Caterina and San Rocco of 1480 attributed to Giovanni Antonio Merli; open every day from 8:30 to sunset.

Why go. A Romanesque-Gothic fortified abbey, several times among the Places of the FAI Heart.
