Parco archeologico di Castiglione di Paludi

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On a height at 334 meters, about 40 hectares in two areas joined by a saddle, dominating the Coseries stream: the site was inhabited by the Enotri in the IX-VIII century B.C. (necropolis in the nearby Piano Agritto) and in the IV-III century B.C. by the Brettii, who built a fortified settlement of strong Greek influence, recognized with excavations of the 1950s. The walls with towers, gates and walkways remain and the monumental East Gate, among the best examples of Hellenistic architecture of Magna Grecia, the theater is dug in the rock, the long wall is , houses and the urban system with plateau and stenopoi.

Why go. A fortified city of the Brettii in the spot.
