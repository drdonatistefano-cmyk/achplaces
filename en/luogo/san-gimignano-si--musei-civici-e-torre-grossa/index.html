Musei Civici e Torre Grossa

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In the Town Hall the Dante Room with the Majesty of Lippo Memmi, the Podestà Room with the frescoes of Azzo di Masetto (1290) and the Pinacoteca with Filippino Lippi, Benozzo Gozzoli and Pinturicchio; from the same entrance you climb on the Grossa Tower, 54 meters, built in 1311.

Why go. The highest tower in the city can be visited, 218 steps.

  • Where Piazza Duomo 2, San Gimignano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
