Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Begin in 1063 by Buscheto, masterpiece of the Romanesque Pisan with classical, Byzantine and Islamic influences: elliptical dome, white and black marbles, the pulpit of Giovanni Pisano and the apse mosaic with San Giovanni di Cimabue (1302).

Why go. Here Galileo was baptized.

  • Where Piazza Arcivescovado 7, Pisa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
