Torre Saracena

History and architecture · Towers

The fifteenth-century tower, perhaps on a defensive structure of Arabic age, linked to the Rocca family that named the village; in the eighteenth century it was a stop of the Grand Tour, after 1830 it had two high-pitched windows and battlements. Restored in 2000 and managed by the volunteers of the Literary Park, since 2023 it hosts a permanent exhibition on Quasimodo, which dedicated the poem "Near a Saracen tower, for his dead brother" (1956), and during the year contemporary art exhibitions.

Why go. The tower of Quasimodo's poetry on his brother Enzo.

visits only by reservation required through the Literary Park
