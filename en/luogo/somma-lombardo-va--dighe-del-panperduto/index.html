Dighe del Panperduto

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Paid entry

Complex of industrial hydraulics on the banks of Ticino, along the European path E1 and the river and cycle path of the waterways, with the former tollgate of hydraulic guardianship. Guided tours every Sunday at 10.30 am by reservation (limited places; other days for groups of at least 15 people); reduced for children 6-10 years, free up to 5 years and for people with disabilities; dogs allowed on leash.

Why go. Where the Ticino feeds the Villoresi Canal, between walks and bike paths.

  • Where Via Lungo Canale Villoresi 4, Somma Lombardo
  • Entry Guided tour €10, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it
