Fornace Alba Docilia e Muro Raku

Museums · Themed

Fornace active from the early seventeenth century to 1982, today exhibition space with the permanent exhibition of Macachi, statues from the albisolesi nativity scenes; in front, the Wall Raku of 1200 tiles of 77 artists.

Why go. The Macachi furnace.
