Museo archeologico romano Albino Umiltà

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Brescello, founded in the 4th century BC by the Galli Cenomani and then Roman city that controlled the traffics on the Po between the 1st century BC and II AD: it starts from the necropolis of Brixellum with original stele, then the copy of the funerary monument of the Concordi found in Boretto in 1929, the reconstruction of a domus with mosaic floors and the history of the excavations.

Why go. Brixellum, the Roman Brescello on the Po.
