Santuario di Diana Nemorense

History and architecture · Archaeology

Ancient pilgrimage site dating back to the 6th century BC, near the lake, on the descent to the water; the remains of the temple are located in the woods on the banks.

Why go. The sacred wood of Diana on the lake.
