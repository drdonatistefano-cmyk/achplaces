Museo delle bande musicali di Toffia - Museo Maria Petrucci

Museums · House museums

In a 17th-century house in the historic centre, founded in 1991 by the artist Maria Petrucci in honor of his father Raniero: four rooms on several floors with wooden sculptures, oil paintings, the old fireplace, the orcs for oil and legumes and the studio of the artist; a fifth room collects objects of peasant life. Reservation not required according to the MiC card; tel. 0765 326248.

Why go. The studio of an artist.
