Museo della civiltà contadina friulana di Colmello di Grotta

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

At the end of the eighteenth century, the Strassoldo family built the village of Colmello di Grotta as a residence and a place of work for about ten families of settlers, who lived there until the early sixties of the twentieth century; restored by the municipality, the complex has been a museum since 1993. It reconstructs kitchen, bedroom, cellar and stable, blacksmith shops, carpenters, casket, carraio and shoemaker, the cycle of vine and corn, the breeding of silkworms, the birth of rural coffers and popular religiosity. Open on request.

Why go. An entire eighteenth-century farmhouse transformed into a museum.
