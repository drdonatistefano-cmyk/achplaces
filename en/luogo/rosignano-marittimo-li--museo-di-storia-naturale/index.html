Museo di Storia naturale

Museums · Science and university

Collections of shells, insects, reptiles, minerals and fossils, a large ornithological collection, a permanent exhibition of paleoanthropology and a herbarium with sections on the Mediterranean scrub, aromatic herbs and aquatic plants.

Why go. From the Mediterranean maquis to paleoanthropology.
