Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of the diocese of Treviso. The census of the churches of the CEI records a pre-existing structure as early as 1480 and the construction of the building between 1838 and 1841; destroyed in 1918, the church was rebuilt between 1919 and 1923, with the bell tower rebuilt in 1921-1922. The altars were donated in 1927 and 1934 and the presbytery had a structural intervention in 1970.

Why go. The city's mother church, reborn after the destruction of 1918.
