Museo Etnografico di Premana

Museums · Themed

It collects traces of the past agricultural and pre-artisan activity of the country and documents uses, customs, traditions and local culture; the regional portal indicates it as one of the most valid initiatives realized in the Lombard Prealps. It tells a country whose history is linked to iron and to the processing of scissors and knives, still flourishing crafts at an international level.

Why go. The culture of the country of scissors.
