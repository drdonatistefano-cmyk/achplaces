Chiesa di Santa Maria Carcaxia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Perhaps of the year 1000 and repeatedly rebuilt, the last in 1921, in white stone and basalt; it rises in the site of a medieval village destroyed by Saracen raids in the fifteenth century, among vineyards and olive groves.

Why go. A country church among vineyards and olive groves.
