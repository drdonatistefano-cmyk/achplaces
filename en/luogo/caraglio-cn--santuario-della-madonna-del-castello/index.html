Santuario della Madonna del Castello

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Just outside the center, it rises on the site of the ancient castle, already in ruins and abandoned in the seventeenth century, of which are visible ruins of walls and towers.

Why go. The sanctuary in the ruins of the castle.
