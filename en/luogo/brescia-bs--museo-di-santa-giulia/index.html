Museo di Santa Giulia

Museums · City history · Paid entry

The main museum of the city, in the monastery of St. Saviour Santa Giulia: the Cross of Desire of the ninth century, the frescoes of the Chorus of the nuns, the remains of the Roman domus and the basilica of St. Saviour; part of the UNESCO site and seat of large exhibitions.

Why go. The city museum in a Longobard monastery.

  • Where Via dei Musei 81/b, Brescia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.bresciamusei.com
