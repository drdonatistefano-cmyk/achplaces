Santuario della Madonna del Monte Carmelo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

A 17th-century shrine inside the Carmine Park; in July the village has its most heartfelt feast, with three days of celebrations and processions.

Why go. The seventeenth-century sanctuary in the woods above the village.
