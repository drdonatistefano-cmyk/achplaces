Malga Marinzen e Sedie delle streghe

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Viewpoint

The Marinzen mountain hut, about 1,500 meters above the village, is reached by cable car: panorama, playground and trails like that towards the Schafstall hut; in winter skating rink with views of the Sciliar. Nearby, the Chairs of Witches (Hexenstühle) are a viewpoint linked to local legends.

Why go. The panoramic balcony above Castelrotto.
