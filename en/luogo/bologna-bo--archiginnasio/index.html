Archiginnasio e Teatro Anatomico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Ancient university seat, with courtyard and loggias covered with coats of arms. On the first floor the Anatomical Theatre in carved wood (1637) and the Sala dello Stabat Mater.

Why go. One of the most amazing interiors in Italy, in a few minutes of visit.

  • Where Piazza Luigi Galvani 1, 40124 Bologna
  • Entry Courtyard free of charge. Anatomical Theatre: guided tour €12 / €10 reduced, or audioguide €10 / €8 reduced
  • Suggested time 45 min
  • Accessibility Highlights accessible without barriers
  • Official website www.bolognawelcome.com
