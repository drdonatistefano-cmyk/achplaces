Museo della Basilica di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Museums · Old masters

Small museum of sacred art in the premises next to the basilica, with paintings, sculptures, furnishings and vestments from the late fourteenth to the eighteenth century coming from churches and convents of the territory. The masterpiece is the Annunciation of Blessed Angelico (1432-1440); there are works of Scheggia, brother of Masaccio, already in San Lorenzo, Mariotto di Cristofano, Jacopo del Sellaio, Domenico di Michelino, Giovanni da San Giovanni and the triptych of the Trinity between Saints of Mariotto of Nardo from the Pieve.

Why go. The Annunciation of the Angelic Blessed.
