Museo civico del gioco e del giocattolo «Giovanni Peyrot»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Also known as "The village of the balocchi" (the "land of the balocchi") collects ancient and modern toys, prestigious games and optical illusions, a puppet theater, slide projections and ancient fairy tales.

Why go. Toys of yesterday and today, for adults and children.

  • Where Via Generale A. Diaz 21, Luserna San Giovanni
  • Entry €3,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org
