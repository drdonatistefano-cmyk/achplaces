Parco archeologico di Santa Maria di Agnano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The park around the prehistoric cave used as a place of burial and worship since the Paleolithic, where the Woman of Ostuni was found; it dominates centuries-old olive groves and the Adriatic sea. In summer it is open with guided tours; paths only partially accessible.

Why go. The Ostuni Woman's cave among the olive trees.
