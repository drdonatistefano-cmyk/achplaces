Napoli Sotterranea

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Galleries, cisterns and tunnels dug into the tuff, up to 40 meters below the road: a journey of 2,400 years from the Greek era to today, recovered by volunteers.

Why go. The city under the city.

  • Where Piazza San Gaetano 68, Napoli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Opening hours official page
