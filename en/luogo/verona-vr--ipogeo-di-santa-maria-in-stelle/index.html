Ipogeo di Santa Maria in Stelle

History and architecture · Archaeology

Underground structure of the III century AD, called "Pantheon" about four meters deep under the parish of the Assunta, in the village of Santa Maria in Stars in Valpantena, about 10 km from the center; visits only by reservation.

Why go. A rare place of underground worship.
