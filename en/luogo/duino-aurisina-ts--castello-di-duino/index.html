Castello di Duino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Historical residence of the princes of the Tower and Tasso, opened in part since 2003, on high cliffs overlooking the sea: lounges with stuccoes, furnishings and art collections, the Palladian school helicoidal staircase, the park on several levels with an air-raid bunker dug into the rock and, not far away, the ruins of Castel Vecchio (XI century) and the rock of the Lady White. Open Saturday and Sunday.

Why go. Rilke's Elegie Castle.
