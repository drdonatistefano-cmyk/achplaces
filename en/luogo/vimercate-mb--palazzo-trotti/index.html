Palazzo Trotti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The seat of the Town Hall since 1862, takes its name from the Trotti, who took over in 1739 from the Secco Borella who gave it the present form; towards the park it has the U pattern of the villas of Lombard delight. The interior has three cycles of mythological frescoes: the rooms of Cleopatra, Semiramide, Hercules and Minerva (1705-1706), the room of Bacchus attributed to Carlo Donelli called the Vimercati and the rooms painted around 1750 by the Ticino Giuseppe Antonio Orelli.

Why go. The frescoed lounges of the Lombard baroque in the town hall.

entrance by reservation for organized groups and school groups, with extraordinary openings during the year (tel. 039 6659488)
