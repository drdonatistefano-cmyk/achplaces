Ruderi di Cirella

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Viewpoint

Archaeological area identified with the ancient coastal settlement of Cirellae, probably founded by the Greeks and grown as a commercial center thanks to the port; remain the Carafa Castle, the Mother Church and the Convent of St. Francis of Paola. Not far away is the modern Teatro dei Ruderi, home of summer festivals.

Why go. The medieval ruins overlooking the island.
