Grotta del Monaco e Grotta delle Rondini

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

To the north of the village, the slopes of Aspromonte plunge into the sea forming natural caves, which can be visited or used for underwater fishing.

Why go. The sea caves of the Costa Viola.
