Palazzo della Dogana dei Grani e museo archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

Palace in the present form of 1883, center of trade of the grains in the Bourbon age; since 1993 the ground floor houses the archaeological museum with the finds of the necropolis of Capo La Torre and Abellinum. The permanent exhibition "La Dogana nella Dogana" is also held, with statues that decorated the façade of the Dogana di Avellino. Open Monday to Friday.

Why go. The artifacts of Abellinum in the heart of the square.
