Teatro Farnese

History and architecture · Historic theatres · Paid entry

The wooden theater built in 1618 in the Armored Room of the Pilot for Ranuccio I Farnese, with the large step cavea and the monumental proscenium.

Why go. Getting in there is a twist.

  • Where Piazza della Pilotta, Parma
  • Entry Included in the Pilot ticket: €18 full, €2 from 18 to 25 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website complessopilotta.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
