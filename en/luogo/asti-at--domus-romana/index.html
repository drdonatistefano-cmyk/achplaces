Domus Romana

History and architecture · Archaeology

Small excavation with the remains of a patrician residence of the first century A.D. of the Roman Hasta, with the rare mosaic carpet of the triclinium with fishes and ivy branches within white and black frames.

Why go. The Roman Asti.
