Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 2002 on the ground floor of the seventeenth-century Villa Adele (Villa Pamphilj), in nine rooms it tells the ancient Antium from its origins to the imperial age: protohistoric funerary furnishings of the IX-VIII century BC, votive deposits of the Republican age, underwater finds, mosaics and frescoes of the Imperial Villa and a cycle of plaster paintings of the second half of the II century A.D.; on loan from the National Roman Museum the Nero's music niche with Ebbro Hercules. Closed on Mondays; reservation not required.

Why go. The ancient Antium, from the Latins to Nero.
