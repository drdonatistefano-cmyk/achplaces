Museo storico del Parmigiano Reggiano e della civiltà contadina della Val d'Enza

Museums · Themed

Born from the ethnographic group La Barchessa founded in 1977, it tells the history and production of Parmigiano Reggiano and the peasant life of the valley: about 40 carriages, carriages and agricultural wagons inlaid between the late nineteenth and mid twentieth centuries and about 3,000 work tools. Access to be agreed with the Association La Barchessa.

Why go. Parmigiano Reggiano and its peasant civilization.
