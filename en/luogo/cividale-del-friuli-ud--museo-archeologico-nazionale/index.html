Museo Archeologico Nazionale

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

In the Palazzo dei Provveditori Veneti designed by Andrea Palladio: Roman collections, early Christians, early medievals, Romanesques and Gothics, with lapidary, epigraphic and music sections and a collection of 56 Lombard gold coins.

Why go. The daily life of the Longobards.
