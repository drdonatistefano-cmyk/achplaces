Osservatorio astronomico di Coloti

Museums · Science and university

In the hamlet of Coloti, about 8 km from the center, in a village today uninhabited: opened since 2000, it has a telescope under a dome of 7 meters, twelve rooms, the former church of the village used as meeting room and the Sala Sirio for events.

Why go. The stars from an uninhabited village.
