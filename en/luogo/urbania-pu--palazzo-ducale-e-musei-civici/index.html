Palazzo Ducale e Musei civici

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built by Francesco di Giorgio Martini from the end of the fifteenth century for the Montefeltro-Della Rovere, it was built above the Brancaleoni fortress and enlarged in the sixteenth century by Girolamo Genga, it was the last seat of the Roveresche court. The route of more than 20 rooms exhibits paintings, drawings, prints and cartographic material left by the last Duke Francesco Maria II, with works by Raffaellino del Colle and Barocci, Renaissance ceramic fragments and a ceramic room used by all Italy; the library has about 40,000 volumes, including a Commedia di Dante of 1491, and in the underground there is the Museum of the history of agriculture and craftsmanship. From the loggia of the tower you can see the center and the river; accept the Map Musei Marche.

Why go. The last court of the Della Rovere and its graphic collection.
