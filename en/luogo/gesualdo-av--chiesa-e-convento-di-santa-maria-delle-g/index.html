Chiesa e Convento di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded by Carlo Gesualdo in 1592, as the plaque on the façade recalls: a single nave church with the prince's coat of arms on the entrance arch and the altarpiece of the pardon of Carlo Gesualdo by Giovanni Balducci (1609), with the prince in prayer next to Leonora d'Este. The Capuchin monastery, enlarged in 1629 and restored after 1980, has cloister and garden and a museum dedicated to Saint Pio of Pietrelcina, guest between November and December 1909, with his cell. Visits in the morning by reservation.

Why go. The altarpiece with the Prince's portrait.
