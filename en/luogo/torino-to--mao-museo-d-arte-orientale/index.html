MAO – Museo d'Arte Orientale

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated in 2008 in the 18th century Palazzo Mazzonis, in the heart of the Quadrilatero, with galleries dedicated to China, Japan, South and South-East Asia, Himalayan region and Islamic countries of Asia.
