Forti Mero e Zaccarana

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Austro-Hungarian fortifications of the Tonale system built until 1914, reachable by a medium difficulty walk from the Tonale Pass; guided tours on request organized by the White War Museum.

Why go. Trekking in the ruins of the Great War, under the Presanella.
