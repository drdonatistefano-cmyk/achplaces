Area archeologica di San Giorgio

History and architecture · Archaeology

Reachable from the cloister, behind the eastern apse of the parish church, it preserves remains of buildings of the Iron Age and a water tank used in metal processing.

Why go. The village of the Iron Age under the parish church.
