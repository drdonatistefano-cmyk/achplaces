Museo della ceramica e della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Museum workshop hosted in the State Middle School Perlasca, exhibits the terracotta objects used by the Fermano families until the 1960s and the potter's instruments; next to there is a laboratory to experiment with the techniques of processing. During the school year, the students visit it, during the summer months visitors are accompanied by a tour operator.

Why go. The tradition of the potters of Montottone, with the possibility to try the lathe.
