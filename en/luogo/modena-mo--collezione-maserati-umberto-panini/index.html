Collezione Maserati Umberto Panini

Local life and traditions · Motors · Paid entry

One of the most complete Maserati collections open to the public, born from the passion of the entrepreneur Umberto Panini; from June 2025 in the new headquarters of Marzaglia.

Why go. The Trident in one room.

  • Entry paid

  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
