Parrocchiale dei Santi Giacomo e Donato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1931-32 with the work of the inhabitants, who brought sand and gravel from the Rio Verde; in the apse the triptych of Jacopino Longo with the Madonna and Child and the Saints Giacomo and Donato (1546), stolen in 1997 and returned by the Carabinieri in 2000. The organ is by Antonio Mola (1898); on the same square remains the old parish church of the late seventeenth-early eighteenth century.

Why go. The 16th-century triptych found.
