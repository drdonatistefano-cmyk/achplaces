Complesso religioso della Madonna del Monte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

It rises in the area of the ancient feud of Lupara, strategic node of the Tratturo Ateleta-Biferno, one of the routes that led the shepherds from Abruzzo to Puglia through Molise.

Why go. A sanctuary on the way to transhumance.
