Chiesa di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Started in 1748 on a project by Pietro Bernasconi, with the works entrusted to Carlo Patturelli, and finished in 1791 after several interruptions; the tufa of the parts above ground comes from the local quarries used also for the Palace of Caserta. It develops on two levels: at the top the cult hall, which is accessed by a large churchyard fenced above the road; under the moat, ancient cemetery and ossuary dug into the tuff, with two rooms sometimes on rows of pillars. The façade, about 30 meters by 32, is marked by semi-colons and pilasters with ionic capitals and Corinthians.

Why go. An eighteenth-century church with an ossuary dug into the tuff.
