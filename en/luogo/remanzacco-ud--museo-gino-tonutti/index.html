Museo "Gino Tonutti"

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Housed in the sheds of the old factory (the 'farie') of Remanzacco, museum after in 1965 the production Tonutti moved to a new place, preserves machinery and atmospheres of that time and documents a century of production of agricultural tools and machines. Four sections: industrial archaeology (machines and tools, some still working), agricultural (with a steam engine operating a threshing machine), about eighty vintage cars from 1915 to today (including a Ford Model T, Rolls Royce, a DeLorean, a Ferrari F40 and Lamborghini) and about 75 motorcycles from the early twentieth century to the eighties. Open every day 9-17, without reservation; guided tours.

Why go. A century of Friulian mechanics with cars and collectible motorcycles.

  • Where Via Udine, Remanzacco
  • Entry €8, reduced €6
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.tonutti.it
