Casa natale di Vincenzo Cuoco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The birthplace of the writer Vincenzo Cuoco, owned by the Municipality; according to Italia.it has been restored as a space for events and reception, while Visit Molise indicates it in renovation to become a Museum of Contemporary Art.

Why go. On the steps of one of the protagonists of the Neapolitan culture of the late eighteenth century.
