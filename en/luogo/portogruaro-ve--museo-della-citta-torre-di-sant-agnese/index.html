Museo della Città - Torre di Sant'Agnese

Museums · City history

Opened on May 16, 1999 in the medieval tower of Sant'Agnese and in a small adjacent building: stone and metal materials coming largely from the Museo concordiese, and on the top floor manuscripts, prints and photographs of the Portogruaro between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries; it also exhibits two tombstones of the Jewish cemetery. Open Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 10-12; not accessible to people with motor disabilities.

Why go. The history of the city inside a medieval tower.
