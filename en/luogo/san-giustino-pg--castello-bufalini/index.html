Castello Bufalini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortilizio dei Dotti destroyed by the Florentines and rebuilt after 1487 for Niccolò Bufalini, transformed into a noble dwelling in the 1930s; rooms decorated by Cristofano Gherardi, the Galleria delle Donne Forti, the moat and the Italian garden with rose garden, green gallery, lemon house and one of the rare historical bosso labyrinths. Open Friday, Saturday, Sunday and holidays.

Why go. The boss maze.
