Museo Casa natale «Filippo Corridoni»

Museums · House museums

Dedicated to the trade unionist Filippo Corridoni, born here (then Pausula) on 18 August 1887 and died at the Trencea delle Frasche on 24 October 1915: the path reconstructs its figure as a revolutionary interventionist, then taken as a symbol by fascism, to relocate it to its historical dimension. Open Monday to Saturday 9-13.30, by reservation (0733 439901).

Why go. The trade unionist who named the city.
