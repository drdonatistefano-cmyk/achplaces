Basilica di San Salvatore

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Basilica outside the walls, beyond the Tessino stream, of the VII-VIII centuries: rare example of Lombard architecture with Byzantine and classical influences, part of the UNESCO site of the Longobards in Italy.

Why go. The UNESCO monument of the Longobards.

  • Where Spoleto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
