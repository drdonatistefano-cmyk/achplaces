Basilica e Cupola di San Gaudenzio

Places of worship · Basilicas · Viewpoint

The 121-metre-high brick dome of Alessandro Antonelli, among the most daring brick buildings in the world, climbs with a guided tour up to 100 meters, and up to the Hall of the Compasso also in a wheelchair.
