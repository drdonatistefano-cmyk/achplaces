Casa Carsica (Kraška hiša)

Museums · Themed

On the edge of Rupingrande, a typical rural house of the Karst, perhaps two to three hundred years old, with the current appearance of 1831 and restored in 1968: roof in stone slabs, kitchen and cellar on the ground floor, bedroom and barn on the first floor, furnishings, tools and costumes of the nineteenth century donated by the inhabitants. In the summer barn are held contemporary art exhibitions and events; every two years, at the end of August of the odd years, it hosts the karst weddings, re-enactment of a Slovenian wedding rite of the late nineteenth century. From April to November open Sunday and holidays 11-12.30 and 15-17. It is also an information point of the reserve of Monte Orsario.

Why go. The peasant life of the Karst in a stone house remained intact.
