Museo archeologico nazionale della Lomellina

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the third stable of the castle, opened in 1998 and expanded in 2006 and 2018: finds of the Lomellina from prehistory to the seventh century, including Roman glass, the tomb of a warrior of Valeggio and a treasure of about 1,400 coins.

Why go. The ancient Lomellina in the castle.

  • Where Piazza Ducale 20, Vigevano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
