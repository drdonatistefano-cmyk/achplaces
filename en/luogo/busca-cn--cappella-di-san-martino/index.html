Cappella di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

On a sunny hill, built before the year 1000, it has one of the oldest facades of Cuneo and is frescoed by the brothers Biazaci, painters of Buschesi who in the fifteenth century realized more than thirty fresco works and on table in the territory.

Why go. One of the oldest facades in Cuneo.
