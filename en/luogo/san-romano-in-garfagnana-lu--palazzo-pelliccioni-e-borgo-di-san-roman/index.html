Palazzo Pelliccioni e borgo di San Romano

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The village preserves short streets and medieval steps that go down to the provincial. The Palazzo Pelliccioni-Marazzini, known for its arched terraces, houses the municipal library and houses finds from the Bronze Age attributed to the Ligurians of the upper Serchio valley, with Roman and medieval relics.

Why go. Ligurian finds in the palace of the terraces.
