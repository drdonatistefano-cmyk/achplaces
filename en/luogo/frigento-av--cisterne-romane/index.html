Cisterne romane

History and architecture · Archaeology

Large water complex of Republican age (1st century BC) in uncertain work, at 911 meters at the highest point of the municipal territory: the galleries collected rain, loose snow and water from a nearby spring, then purified and distributed to the village and its surroundings. Of the eleven rooms, perhaps, there are four, three rooms that can be visited, reached by a non-original entrance.

Why go. Hydraulic engineering over 2,000 years ago.
