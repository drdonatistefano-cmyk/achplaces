Modern Automata Museum

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Museum of small mechanical sculptures in paper, wood and metal that visitors themselves put in motion, between irony and invention: the collection has about 200 automatons of artists from Japan, United States, Germany, United Kingdom, France, Canada, South America and Italy, half exposed permanently and half on exhibition itinerant. It is located in part of the small castle of Vezzano, of early medieval origin (IX century); it organizes guided tours and construction courses for children and adults. Open Monday-Friday 9-17.

Why go. Automes to operate with your own hands in a 9th century castle.

  • Where Via Case Nuove 7, Montopoli di Sabina
  • Entry €3, reduced children up to 18 years €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
