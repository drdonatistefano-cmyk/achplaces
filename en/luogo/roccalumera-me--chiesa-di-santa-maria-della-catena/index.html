Chiesa di Santa Maria della Catena

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Completed in 1893 by the inhabitants of the districts Baglio and Ficarra and enlarged in 1937, it has Latin cross plan, coffered ceiling with rosettes and artistic windows; on the main altar dominates the nineteenth century effigy of the Madonna della Catena, carried in procession on the first Sunday of September.

Why go. The September festival that recalls the whole Ionian Riviera.
