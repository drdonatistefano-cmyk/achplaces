Palazzo Colonna

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between 1532 and 1622 on the previous Rocca degli Orsini on a project by Antonio da Sangallo the Younger, from the early twentieth century it is the seat of the Town Hall; it can be visited underground caves.

Why go. The palace of the Column signed by Sangallo.
