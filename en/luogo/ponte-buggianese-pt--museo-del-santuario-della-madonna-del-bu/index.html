Museo del Santuario della Madonna del Buon Consiglio

Museums · Themed

Small museum in the premises next to the sacristy, born as an oratory. It has sacred vestments of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, sacred statues, reliquaries, candelabras, sacred vases, coins, prayer books, crosses and liturgical objects, as well as ancient texts including an edition of 1832 of the "Promises Sposi" (Promises).

Why go. The sacred treasures of the sanctuary, a stone's throw from the frescoes of Annigoni.
