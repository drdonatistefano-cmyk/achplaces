Porta San Donnino e Antiquarium

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Ancient medieval entrance with ogival arch next to the Duomo; in the door and in the Cremonini house an Antiquarium with sculptures, mosaics and an epigraph in bronze, and under the door the remains of a Roman bridge of Augustian age on the Stirone. Along Via Frate Gherardo run the Visconti walls.

Why go. From the medieval gate to the Roman bridge.
