Grotta di Monte Majore

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Cave with large entrance on the eastern side of the mountain and short and easy descent: it develops for 300 meters, with concretions and a dome room, and houses a colony of about 5.000 bats.

Why go. An easy cave with thousands of bats.
