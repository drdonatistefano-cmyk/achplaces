Marina di Arborea

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches

Large sandy beach surrounded by an extensive coastal pine forest, near the lagoon of S'Ena Arrubia; between pine forest and beach you can camp.

Why go. Long and quiet beach with the shade of the pine forest.
