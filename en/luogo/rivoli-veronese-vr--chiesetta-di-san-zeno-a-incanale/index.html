Chiesetta di San Zeno a Incanale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church built around the thousand with Roman materials of the place, among the oldest in the Val d'Adige; in 1796 it was largely damaged by Napoleonic soldiers, but the perimeter walls, the entrance and the 18-metre-high square bell tower remain intact, and the frescoes of the apse arch have been recently restored.

Why go. One of the oldest churches in the Val d'Adige.
