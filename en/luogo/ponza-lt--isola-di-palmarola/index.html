Isola di Palmarola

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Uninhabited, with only one landing place in Cala di San Silverio, cliffs and clear waters; nature reserve and marine protected area, with houses dug in the rock and a restaurant on the beach.

Why go. The wild island, among the most beautiful in the Mediterranean.
