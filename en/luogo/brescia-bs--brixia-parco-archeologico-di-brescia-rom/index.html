Brixia – Parco archeologico di Brescia romana

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Enrolled in the world heritage in 2011: Piazza del Foro, the Capitolium with the Alata Victory, the Republican shrine of the first century BC, the Roman theatre next to Palazzo Maggi Gambara and the Roman basilica.

Why go. The best preserved Roman city in the North.
