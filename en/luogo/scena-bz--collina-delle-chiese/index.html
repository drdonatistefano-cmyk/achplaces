Collina delle chiese

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the center of the village, the hill brings together three churches: St. Martin, from the twelfth century, the oldest monument of Scena, with two naves and on carolingia measures; the old parish church, rebuilt in Romanesque style between the twelfth and thirteenth centuries and redesigned in the sixteenth century; the new neo-Gothic parish church, built between 1914 and 1931 because the old one had become too small. In the center the large heart of flowers with views of the church is among the most photographed points of the village.

Why go. Eight centuries of sacred architecture in a few steps.
