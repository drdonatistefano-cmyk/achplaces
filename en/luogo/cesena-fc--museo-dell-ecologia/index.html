Museo dell'Ecologia

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

In the loggia and tower of the Palazzo Comunale, the main species of fauna of the Savio valley in diorami and a garden with many botanical species.

Why go. The nature of the valley in the center.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Cesena
  • Entry Discover Cesena Card €13 (with Malatestiana, Rocca and Villa Silvia)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
