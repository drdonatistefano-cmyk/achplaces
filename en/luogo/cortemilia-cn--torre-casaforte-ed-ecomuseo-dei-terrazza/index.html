Torre-casaforte ed Ecomuseo dei Terrazzamenti e della Vite

Museums · Themed

The 15th-century tower-house dominates Piazza Oscar Molinari; once the seat of the magistrate's courthouse, it now houses the Ecomuseum of Terraces and Lives.

Why go. Understand the stone terraces of Alta Langa.
