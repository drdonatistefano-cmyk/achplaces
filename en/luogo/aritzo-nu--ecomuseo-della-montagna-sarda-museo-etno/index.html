Ecomuseo della montagna sarda, Museo etnografico

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Centre of the Ecomuseum, in the Pastissu park: eight rooms on the agropastoral culture barbaric, from the shepherds with medallions of "su Giuramentu" used to solve disputes on pastures and livestock, to the products of the wood and to the merchants wandering (torrono, wood, carapigna), from wool to traditional clothes, from the wax workshop to the traditional house, up to the carved cases of Aritzo of the wedding set and to the salt on wood, agriculture, blacksmiths and maniscalci.

Why go. The life of the barbaric mountain in eight rooms.
