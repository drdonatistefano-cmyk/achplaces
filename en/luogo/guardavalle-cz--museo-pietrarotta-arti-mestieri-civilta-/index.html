Museo Pietrarotta. Arti mestieri civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

In the farmhouse Fassi of the hamlet Pietrarotta, exhibits a collection of objects of peasant civilization and a section dedicated to the history of art and crafts of the Calabrian territory.

Why go. Arts and crafts of rural Calabria.
