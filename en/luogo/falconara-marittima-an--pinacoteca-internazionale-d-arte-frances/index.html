Pinacoteca internazionale d'arte francescana contemporanea «In nome di Francesco»

Museums · Modern art

On the ground floor of the convent complex of Sant'Antonio dei Frati Minori, about 800 m2 of works by well-known Italian, European and African artists; next to it are a library of Franciscan and Picena history, the provincial historical archive of the Friars Minor of the Marche and the conference room. Guided tours by reservation.

Why go. Contemporary art inspired by Saint Francis.
