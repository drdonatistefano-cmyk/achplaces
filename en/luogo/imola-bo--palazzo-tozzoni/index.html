Palazzo Tozzoni

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

House museum of a noble family, with furnishings, paintings and kitchens intact eighteenth century.

Why go. You enter a living dwelling, not a gallery.

  • Where Via Garibaldi 18, Imola
  • Entry €5 in full, €4 reduced (from 2026); 2 museums €8, 3 museums €10
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website imolamusei.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
