Corso Ercole I d'Este e Quadrivio degli Angeli

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The addition of Biagio Rossetti's Erculea, considered the first example of modern urbanism: wide avenues, gardens and palaces up to the Quadrivio degli Angeli.

Why go. One of the most beautiful roads in Europe, to be done on foot or by bike.
