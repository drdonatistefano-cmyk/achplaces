Museo Archeologico «Oreste Nardini»

Museums · Archaeological

In the sixteenth century Town Hall, founded in 1920 by Oreste Nardini and reopened permanently in 1992: about 400 exhibits from the protohistory to the Middle Ages. The strong piece is the Sarcophagus of Velletri (II century A.D., found in 1955) with 184 figures between the labors of Hercules and scenes of the underworld; then the Lastra dell'Orante (IV century), the Volsche terracotta of the VI century B.C. and a copy of the Pallade of Velletri, whose original is at the Louvre.

Why go. The sarcophagus of the labors of Hercules, a unique of Roman art.
