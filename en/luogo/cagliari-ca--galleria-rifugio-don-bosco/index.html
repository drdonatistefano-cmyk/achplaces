Galleria rifugio Don Bosco

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Anti-aircraft shelter in a tunnel about 180 meters long between Viale Merello and Viale Fra Ignazio; walled at the end of the war, it has arrived almost intact until today.

Why go. Cagliari under the bombs of 1943.
