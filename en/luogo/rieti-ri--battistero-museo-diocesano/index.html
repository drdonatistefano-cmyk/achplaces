Battistero – Museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters

The fourteenth-century baptistery next to the Cathedral is now the seat of the Diocesan Museum, which exhibits its gallery in the Hall of Audiences of the Papal Palace.

Why go. The sacred art of the diocese of crime.
