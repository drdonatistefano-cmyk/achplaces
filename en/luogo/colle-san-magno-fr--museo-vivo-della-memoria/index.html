Museo Vivo della Memoria

Museums · City history

Five rooms in a historic building tell the Second World War from the point of view of civilians, crushed between Germans and allies during the battles of Cassino, with video testimonies, objects donated by families, documents, war finds and aerial photos of the RAF on the area of Mount Cairo granted by the National Aerophototheque. According to the MiC card is open Friday 15-18, Saturday 10-18 and Sunday 10-13, with reservation for schools and groups; the same card indicates both a ticket of 2 euros (free for residents) and a free offer of 10-13, while VisitLazio gives it to visit only by reservation.

Why go. Cassino's war seen by those who suffered it.
