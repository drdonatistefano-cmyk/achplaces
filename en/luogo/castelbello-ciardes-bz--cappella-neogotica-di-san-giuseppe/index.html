Cappella neogotica di San Giuseppe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Chapel founded in 1924 by Andreas Kuppelwieser as a thank you for coming back with the brothers unharmed from World War I; his initials are carved at the base of the wooden stall.

Why go. A former vote of the Great War in neo-Gothic style.
