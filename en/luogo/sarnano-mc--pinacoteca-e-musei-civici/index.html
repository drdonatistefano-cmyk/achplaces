Pinacoteca e Musei civici

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the former convent of the Clarisse di Santa Chiara, restored after the earthquake of 1997-98: on the ground floor the gallery around the courtyard, with works by Vittore Crivelli (Madonna with Child) and Simone De Magistris; on the upper floor the collections of weapons, avifauna and hammers and the collection of modern art by Mariano Gavasci.

Why go. The Crivelli and the curious collections of weapons and hammers.

  • Where Via Giacomo Leopardi, Sarnano
  • Entry €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
