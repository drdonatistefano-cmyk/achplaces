Cappella di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It preserves the frescoes above the altar of Giovanni de Gregorio known as the Pietrafesa, painter born in Satriano and active in the first half of the seventeenth century, among the most famous of Basilicata.

Why go. The frescoes of Pietrafesa in his country.
