Piazza del Popolo e Santa Maria del Popolo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The large square at the north entrance of the city and the church of Santa Maria del Popolo, at the beginning of Via del Corso, Via del Babuino and Via di Ripetta.

Why go. The trident of the Roman streets.
