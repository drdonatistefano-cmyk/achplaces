Giardino Garzoni

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Baroque garden among the greats of Europe, with stairs, statues, labyrinth, theater of verzura, the water staircase and the statue of Fama; the villa "of the hundred windows" is not open to visitors.

Why go. The maze brings luck to lovers.

  • Where Piazza della Vittoria 1, Collodi, Pescia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
