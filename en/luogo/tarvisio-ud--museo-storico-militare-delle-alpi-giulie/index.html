Museo storico militare delle Alpi Giulie

Museums · City history · Paid entry

It tells the military history of the Julian Alps between Veneto, Carinthia and Slovenia: an introductory multimedia room and it rises on the Napoleonic Wars, the First and the Second World War. Outside there is an open-air museum with the strong Carinthias and the works of Vallo Littorio, partly in recovery. A cumulative ticket with the Geominerary Park €10, reduced €8.

Why go. Three wars on a strategic border.
