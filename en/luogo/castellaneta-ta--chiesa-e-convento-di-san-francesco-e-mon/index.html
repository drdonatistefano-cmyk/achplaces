Chiesa e convento di San Francesco e Monte Santa Trinità

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

A fifteenth-century complex renovated in the seventeenth century, with frescoes from the early eighteenth century in the nave and the seventeenth century in the cloister. It is located to the south-west of the village, on the road to Mount Santa Trinità (411 m), from which you can enjoy a beautiful view.

Why go. Frescoed cloister and panoramic view from Mount Santa Trinità.
