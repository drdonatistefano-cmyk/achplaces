Parco delle Muse e delle Ninfe e mostra su Giovanni Santi

Museums · Themed

Former cultural center Giovanni Santi, born in 1994 for the sixteenth century anniversary of the death of the painter and became in 2019 a path of reading the landscape, with a permanent exhibition on Giovanni Santi, painter, writer and stage designer of the court of Urbino: reproductions of works such as the Visitation of Fano and St. Jerome of the Vatican Art Gallery and the poem in 23,000 verses about the life of Federico da Montefeltro.

Why go. The Montefeltro court painter in his country.
