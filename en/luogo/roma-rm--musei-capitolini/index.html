Musei Capitolini

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The oldest public museums in the world, born in 1471 from the donation of Sisto IV bronzes: the original Marco Aurelio, the Wolf, the dying Galata, the Capitolina Venus; from the Terrazza Caffarelli one of the most beautiful views.

Why go. The original Wolf and Marco Aurelio.

  • Where Piazza del Campidoglio 1, Roma
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
