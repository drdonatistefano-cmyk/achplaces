Collegiata di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque-Gothic church of the XII century, renovated in the Two-Three-th century, with bell tower with cross-windows and portal fourteenth-century flanked by two lions; in the bezel an Annunciation frescoed by Paolo da Visso.

Why go. The lions' portal on the square.

damaged by the earthquake 2016: technical insights in progress and provisional works installed (Region Marche, September 2025)
