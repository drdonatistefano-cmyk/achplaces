Museo del Tesoro e area archeologica di Sant'Eulalia (MuTSEu)

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Next to the parish church of Sant'Eulalia, in the Marina, the museum preserves the artistic heritage of the churches of Sant'Eulalia, Santa Lucia and Santo Sepolcro; under the church extends for about 900 square meters the archaeological area, from the Punic shrine to the Roman city until the modern age, with 13 meters of paved road.

Why go. Over 2,000 years of Navy history underneath.

  • Where Vico del Collegio 2, Cagliari
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
