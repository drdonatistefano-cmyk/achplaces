Museo naturalistico del Centro visitatori dell'Orecchiella

Museums · Themed

In the Visitor Centre of the park, in the territory of San Romano, the Natural Museum is located together with the Museum of raptors (or predator); next to the garden of mountain flowers.

Why go. To get to know the fauna of the park before leaving.
