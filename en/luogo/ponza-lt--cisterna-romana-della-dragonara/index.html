Cisterna romana della Dragonara

History and architecture · Archaeology

One of the three large cisterns built by the Romans against the scarcity of water, preserved almost intact, with underground corridors that cross the aisles and floors and walls in stone. Guided tours only: June-August at 11:00, 12:15, 18:00 and 19:15, September at 11:00 and 17:00, other times by reservation 24 hours in advance (Pro Loco, tel. 0771 80031).

Why go. Roman hydraulic engineering under the country.
