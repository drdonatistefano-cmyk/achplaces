Museo biografico di Padre Pio

Museums · Themed

The wax museum was set up in 2001 at Palazzo Morcaldi, home of Mayor Francesco Morcaldi, friend of Padre Pio, who in the 1920s opposed his transfer: ten scenes tell of the main moments of the life of the saint, chosen by a committee of historians with the advice of the Capuchins.

Why go. The life of Padre Pio in ten scenes.

MiC card (updated to February 2021) is closed every day
