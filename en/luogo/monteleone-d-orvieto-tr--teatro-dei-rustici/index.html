Teatro dei Rustici

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Among the smallest theatres in Italy and Europe, in the Public Palace: an inscription in the hall recalls that in 1732 the community transformed the barn of the palace into a theatre, when the young people of the country got to recite comedies at Carnival; it takes its name from the Philo-dramatic Academy of Rustici and continued its activity despite the hostility of the papal authorities. It has 96 seats; on the ground floor of the building a barn and a mill.

Why go. One of the smallest theatres in Europe.
