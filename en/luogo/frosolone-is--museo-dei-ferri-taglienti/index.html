Museo dei ferri taglienti

Museums · Themed

In the historic center, towards the end of via Garibaldi, it collects hundreds of objects of historical value donated by enthusiasts and heirs of scissors and knives of the twentieth century, together with military blades delivered by various Superintendences, including those of Marche and Lazio. A part of the museum is an active workshop where you can see master craftsmen forging the blades; visits are on reservation, at least 24 hours in advance, through the association Molise to Discover.

Why go. The blades of the Italian Toledo, forged before you.
