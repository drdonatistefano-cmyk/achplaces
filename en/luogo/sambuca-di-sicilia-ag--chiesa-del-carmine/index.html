Chiesa del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Façade with two overlapping orders; it houses burials of noble families, wooden statues and marbles of St. Anne of the mid seventeenth century and the Madonna of the Audience of the mid sixteenth century attributed to the Gagini.

Why go. The Madonna of the Gagines.
