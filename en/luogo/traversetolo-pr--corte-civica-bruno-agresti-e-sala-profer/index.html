Corte civica Bruno Agresti e Sala Proferio Grossi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Rural court of the seventeenth century that houses the Brozzi Museum, the municipal library and the Hall of Columns for the exhibitions; in the Council Hall 28 paintings of Proferio Grossi (born in Guardasone in 1923) are permanently exhibited inspired by the works of Verdi. On the façade of the town hall, in via Vittorio Veneto, the 'Vittoria angulare' of Brozzi (1923).

Why go. Green in 28 paintings inside a seventeenth-century court.
