Museo della guerra e Resistenza

Museums · City history

In the municipality, set up in 2008 and rebuilt in 2023: panels, photographs and objects of epoch on fascism and resistance in Stura Valley, in four rooms (Italian army, German armies and ally, Resistance in Stura Valley, Resistance in Valloriate). Open Monday to Friday from 8 am to 1 pm; afternoons and weekends by reservation.

Why go. The partisan war told in the valley.
