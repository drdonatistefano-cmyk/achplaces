Museo della Città

Museums · City history

In four restored rooms of the seventeenth-century nucleus of the Duomo, finds from Tusculum and Tuscolane villas (an iron age anforet, a polychrome mosaic, frescoes, statues and coins), a medieval papal bull, majolica, seventeenth-century paintings and original documents.

Why go. The artifacts of Tusculum.
