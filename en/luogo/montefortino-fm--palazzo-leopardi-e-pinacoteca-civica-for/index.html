Palazzo Leopardi e Pinacoteca civica Fortunato Duranti

Museums · Old masters

Renaissance noble palace of the 16th century with Guelfe windows and rustic portal, houses the Civic Art Gallery dedicated to Fortunato Duranti (1797-1863).

Why go. The picture gallery of the village in a sixteenth-century building.
