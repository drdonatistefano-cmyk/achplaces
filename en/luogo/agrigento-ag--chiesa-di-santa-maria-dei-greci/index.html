Chiesa di Santa Maria dei Greci

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the 12th century on the foundations of a doric temple of the 5th century BC, with a Norman-Arabic portal of the 13th century and a small courtyard.

Why go. A church over a Greek temple.
