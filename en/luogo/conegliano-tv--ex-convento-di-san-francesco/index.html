Ex Convento di San Francesco

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Franciscan complex built between 1371 and 1411 with two cloisters, a single nave church, a wing of the novitiate of 1708 and a library of 127 codes. Today it remains the main cloister of Renaissance imprint, with the seventeenth-century well surrounded by arches on columns; the cloister is open every day 9-12,30 and 15-17,30.

Why go. A silent Renaissance cloister under the castle.
