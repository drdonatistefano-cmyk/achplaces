Collezione Righini al Castello di Panzano

Local life and traditions · Motors

Private museum of vintage cars founded by Mario Righini, with about 350 vehicles: among them the Auto Avio Costruzioni 815 of 1940, the first car built by Enzo Ferrari before his factory, and the Alfa Romeo 2300 8C belonged to Tazio Nuvolari, as well as Ferrari models, Mercedes-Benz, Isotta Fraschini and Rolls-Royce.

Why go. Enzo Ferrari's first car.
