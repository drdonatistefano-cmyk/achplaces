Grotte di Camerano

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

A network of tunnels and salt excavated by man in the sandstone under the old town, between Piazza Roma and the streets Maratti and San Francesco (the ...Rupe del Sassone) about 2-3 km, of which about 1 km can be visited, with vaulted dome, sail and barrel, circular rooms, columns, bas-reliefs and religious and esoteric symbols. Used in time as a shelter, a food and wine warehouse and a meeting place, they returned to World War II. They visit only accompanied, all year round, in about an hour; inside the temperature is around 14 degrees.

Why go. The most famous underground city in the Marche region.
