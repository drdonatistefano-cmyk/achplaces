Catuvi e vino di Verbicaro

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Verbicaro was considered the capital of Calabrian winemaking and its wine has been Doc since 1995; it survives the crushing in the palm and in the casts, the cellars where the barrels are preserved and you find yourself eating and singing. On December 8th, the feast of the "perciavutt" opens the castuvi with tastings of cold cuts and wine.

Why go. The wine in the traditional cellars.
