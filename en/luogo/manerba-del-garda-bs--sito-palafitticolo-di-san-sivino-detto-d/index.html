Sito palafitticolo di San Sivino detto del Gabbiano

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage

Village on stilts of the Bronze Age (2200-1300 BC), today submerged near the shore, which extended for about 150 meters along the coast; discovered in 1971 by the Triton Sub Club of Desenzano, oak poles reveal two construction phases at least 33 years away. It is one of the components of the UNESCO serial site of prehistoric palafittic sites of the Alps.

Why go. A prehistoric UNESCO village under the waters of Garda.
