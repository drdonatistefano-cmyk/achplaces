Museo Civico d'Arte Antica

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the Palazzo Comunale (or of the Elders) more than 300 works including paintings, sculptures, ceramics and jewels, from the thirteenth to the nineteenth century.

Why go. Seven hundred years of art in Pistoia.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo 7, Pistoia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
