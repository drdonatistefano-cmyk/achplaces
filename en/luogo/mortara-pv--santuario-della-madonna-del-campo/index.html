Santuario della Madonna del Campo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Church already existing in 1100, preserves pictorial evidence of different eras, including a Mercy, and two statues of San Domenico and San Giovanni Battista attributed to Giovan Battista Crespi called Cerano, a painter from Lombardy of the seventeenth century.

Why go. A medieval sanctuary with statues attributed to the Cerano.
