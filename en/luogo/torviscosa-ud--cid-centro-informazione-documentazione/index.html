CID Centro Informazione Documentazione

Museums · City history

In the brick building with its large oculus overlooking the square, it preserves the historical, documentary and artistic heritage of the city-factory: historical and photographic archives, plastic works SNIA Viscosa, the technical library and the collections of ceramics and paintings of the Marinotti family, as well as temporary exhibitions; it tells the transformation of a marshland into an agricultural-industrial reality.
