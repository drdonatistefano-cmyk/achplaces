Castrum di Castelseprio

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified settlement built between IV and V century reusing Roman military structures, surrounded by walls with towers; in the Lombard age it was home to a gastaldate. You can see the remains of the Basilica of St. John the Evangelist with cistern and bell tower, the octagonal baptistery with two baptismal sources, the church of St. Paul and a late medieval convent. The excavations, begun in the fifties, are still in progress.

Why go. To walk into a UNESCO heritage Longobard fortress.
