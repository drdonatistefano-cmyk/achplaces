Piazza delle Erbe

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The oldest and most beloved square of Verona, built on the Roman forum area and the heart of political and economic life, surrounded by the palaces of historical families and dominated by Palazzo Maffei.

Why go. The Roman forum of today.

  • Where Piazza delle Erbe, Verona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
