Gole di Tiberio

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Canyon of about 300 meters excavated by the Pollina River in rocks of the upper Triassic (about 200 million years ago), site recognized by the network of UNESCO Geoparks; a 400 steps path descends to the river bed and nearby there is an equipped area. Visit Sicily offers boat excursions in the gorges.

Why go. The Pollina canyon in the Madonie Geopark.
