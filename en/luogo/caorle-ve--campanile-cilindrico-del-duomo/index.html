Campanile cilindrico del Duomo

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

One of the few original cylindrical bell towers left, of the 11th century, 48 meters high and slightly inclined to the east; it recalls Ravenna models of Byzantine tradition with Romanesque elements and for centuries has been a watchtower over the sea. Recently restored, you can visit by reservation; from the top you can see the sea and the lagoon and in summer you can also climb in the evening, at sunset.

Why go. The view over the sea and lagoon from an 11th century tower.
