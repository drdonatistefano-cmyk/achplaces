Abbazia di San Donato

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Three-nave church built in the town of Scozola, where the bishop of Pavia Liutardo (841-864) established a monastic community; in 1535 monastery and lands passed to the Major Hospital of Milan. The present building is a Romanesque reconstruction with apses and bell tower; inside the Disputa di santa Caterina d'Alessandria (1503) by Bernardino Zenale, the Last Supper of Tarillo da Curia (1581), eighteenth-century frescoes by Bellotti and a wooden crucifix.

Why go. A Romanesque church among the farmhouses, with frescoes from the sixteenth to the eighteenth century.
