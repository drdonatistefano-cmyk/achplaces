Parco Archeologico Città del Tufo e necropoli di Sovana

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The park with the monumental Tomb Ildebranda, the Tomb of the winged demon, the rocky settlement of San Rocco and the hollow streets; in Sovana also the Cathedral of San Pietro.

Why go. The most spectacular Etruscan necropolis in the Maremma.

  • Where Località La Fratta 2, Sorano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
