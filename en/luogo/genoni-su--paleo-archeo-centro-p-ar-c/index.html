Paleo Archeo Centro (P.Ar.C.)

Museums · Archaeological

Museum with sections of paleontology, archaeology and didactics: local fossils, including two sandstone blocks covered with sea urchins from the Duidduru geosite, and Nuragic, Punic and Roman finds of the territory. A model reconstructs the hill of Santu Antine with Punic fortifications, nuraghe, well and church, and a diorama shows the bottom of the well with reproductions of the finds; workshop for schools and families with excavation simulation. Paid admission, total motor accessibility.

Why go. Understand fossils and excavations of Santu Antine before climbing the hill.
