Museo della Venere e dell'Elefante

Museums · Archaeological

Two sections: the Elephant Museum with the skeleton of a female of Mammuthus meridionalis found in 1980 by a fisherman on the gret of the Panaro, in locality Bocchirol, with dioramas on the environment, fossilization and evolution of the trunks; the Museum of Venus with a copy of the statue of about 22 cm carved in soft stone about 25,000 years ago and found in 1925 (the original is at the Pigorini Museum of Rome), compared with the reproductions of the Venus of Lespugue, Willendorf, Chiozza and Marmot. The museum's website indicates opening on Sundays and holidays 14.30-18.30, weekdays by reservation.

Why go. The Paleolithic Venus and the Panaro mammoth.
