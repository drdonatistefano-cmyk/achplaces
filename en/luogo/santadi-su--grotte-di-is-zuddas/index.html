Grotte di Is Zuddas

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Karst cavities in the Cambrian limestone of Mount Meana, about 6 km from the village on SP 70 towards Teulada: discovered in the sixties, when they were marble quarry, and explored in the seventies for 1,650 meters, are open to the public since 1985 with a path of about half a kilometer and a visit of about an hour, at 16 degrees constant. Cross the Theatre Hall, the Room of the eccentric with the vault covered with aragonites that grow in every direction and the Sala dell'organ, where a crib is set up at Christmas; at the entrance there are traces of the Sardinian Prolagus, an extinct rodent.

Why go. The eccentric aragonites, world rarity.
