Museo e archivio civico Sebastiano Guzzone

Museums · City history

It preserves pottery of Magna Grecia, a high relief of the Ninfa Zizza and a collection of bans from the seventeenth century to the Anglo-American occupation; it also exhibits paintings of the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, including a work by Sciuti and works by the local Sebastiano Guzzone, Giuseppe Barone and Santo Marino.

Why go. History and painting of the village in a single museum.
