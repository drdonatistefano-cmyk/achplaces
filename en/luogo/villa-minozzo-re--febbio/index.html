Febbio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Last village of the Val d'Asta, 1053 meters at the foot of Mount Cusna: in winter skiing facilities, in summer base for hiking, mountain biking and horse riding; the chairlift Febbio 2000 works in both seasons.

Why go. Winter skiing and summer trails under the Cusna.
