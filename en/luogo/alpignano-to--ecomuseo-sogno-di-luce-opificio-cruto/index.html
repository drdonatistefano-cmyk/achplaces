Ecomuseo «Sogno di luce» – Opificio Cruto

Museums · Themed

The factory was built in 1885 by Gerolamo Taddei for Alessandro Cruto, to produce on an international scale its carbon filament bulbs; the building remained almost intact, with the chimney of the high factory. Since 1996 it houses a museum in three sections: the life of Cruto and the impact of the factory on Alpignano, the history of lighting with instruments and lamps, the invention in its historical context.

Why go. The factory of the first light bulb of Cruto.
