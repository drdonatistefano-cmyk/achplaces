Castello feudale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It rises on a high sandstone cliff, reached by a stone bridge with several arches, and dominates the Camastra Valley. On the site of a Byzantine hermitage, it was rebuilt in stone by the Normans in the 11th century, renovated by the Angioini around 1250, transformed by the Orsini Del Balzo in the Renaissance and then into a noble residence, with the last major intervention in the 18th century. It has two courtyards, the one representing the mast, drawbridge, shoe walls, portals and Renaissance balustrade; the cliff is recognized as a geosite. Visits by reservation at the municipal offices.

Why go. A fortress suspended on the rock.
