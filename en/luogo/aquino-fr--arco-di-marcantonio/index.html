Arco di Marcantonio

History and architecture · Archaeology

Roman arch of the 1st century A.D. along the Via Latina, in the garden of Santa Maria della Libera, indicated by VisitLazio as one of the oldest arches in Italy.

Why go. One of the oldest arches in Italy.
