Museo della Città e del Territorio

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 2000 in the former Augustinian convent of Sant'Oliva, it exhibits archaeological finds and testimonies of the history of Cori, including the architectural and sculptural fragments of the Temple of the Dioscuri, a 1:75 scale model and a copy of the statue of Minerva donated by the city of Rome in 1999.

Why go. The history of Cora and its temples in a single path.
