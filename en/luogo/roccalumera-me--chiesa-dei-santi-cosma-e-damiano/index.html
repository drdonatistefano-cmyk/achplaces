Chiesa dei Santi Cosma e Damiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Baroque church with a single nave, probably from the seventeenth century (attested in 1674), in a dominant position in the center of the ancient village of Sciglio on the homonymous stream; originally dedicated to the Most Holy Savior, from the eighteenth century venerates the medical saints Cosma and Damiano. Portale in pietra rosacea del 1758, bell tower of the twentieth century, monumental altar of the sixties.

Why go. The eighteenth-century portal in the village of Sciglio.
