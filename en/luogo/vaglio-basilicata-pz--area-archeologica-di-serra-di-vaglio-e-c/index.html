Area archeologica di Serra di Vaglio e Casa dei Pithoi

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

At about 1,000 meters, town of the Peuketiantes inhabited by the 8th century BC, fortified in the 4th century BC with a double wall (some stones bear Greek letters) and divided into districts by a main road; it was abandoned in the 3rd century BC after a great fire. The House of the Pithoi reconstructs a house of the fifth century B.C. with the four large jars. Open on weekends and holidays from June to mid-November, every day in August, by reservation on other days.

Why go. The Lucanian city on the mountain.
