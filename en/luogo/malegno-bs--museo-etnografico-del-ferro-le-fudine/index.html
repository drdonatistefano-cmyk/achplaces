Museo etnografico del ferro «Le Fudine»

Museums · Themed

It occupies the ancient forges of Serini and of Dwarfs, from the name of the last blacksmiths who worked there: one of the oldest forges in Europe, with late dogmatic structure that raises the origin between the mid-300 and the fifteenth century, remained active until a few decades ago for grater, ladles, palettes and picks.
