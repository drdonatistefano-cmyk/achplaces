Piccolo Museo del Diario

Museums · Themed

Hosted since 2013 in the sixteenth century Palazzo Pretorio, it tells the archive that since 1984 collects diaries, letters and memories of ordinary people. Four rooms: an installation of cupboards and drawers to be opened to listen to and see selected stories, the corner dedicated to Saverio Tutino, the Room Rabito on the autobiography of the street Ragusano Vincenzo Rabito beat by car, and the room of the sheet on which the peasant mantua Clelia Marchi wrote her own life.

Why go. A unique museum in Italy, made of memories of the common people.
