Necropoli di Puttu Codinu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Nine domus de janas excavated on two rocky outings near the rivers Temo and Curos, used by the Neolithic end at the ancient age of Bronze (cultures of Ozieri, Campaniforme and Bonnanaro); known since 1903 and excavated in 1987-88. The tomb VIII stands out, with beams and roof of carved hut, where a statue of goddess mother was found, and the tomb IX with ritual fireplace and cups in the floor; on the walls carved taurine horns. Open Tuesday-Sunday 10-13 and 15-18, with guided tours.

Why go. Neolithic tombs that imitate the homes of the living.
