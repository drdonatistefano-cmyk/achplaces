Oasi LIPU di Massaciuccoli

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Protected area of 47 hectares on the lake, between reeds and water lilies, ideal for birdwatching: tarabusini, diving ducks, divers, cormorants, folagues, herons and birds of prey like the royal dawn. Overelevated passerelle on the water lead to observation huts; the tour centre hosts the Museum of Pallustrated Ecology and offers guided tours, boat trips, canoeing, kayak or rowing boat, workshops and summer camps.

Why go. The catwalks on the water between the herons.
