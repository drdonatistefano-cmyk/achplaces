Museo paleontologico «Giulio Maini»

Museums · Science and university

Housed in the fourteenth-century church of Sant'Antonio, it collects mainly local collections, including the Calappilia mani, species of crab present only in this area.

Why go. Fossils of the territory in a church of the fourteenth century.
