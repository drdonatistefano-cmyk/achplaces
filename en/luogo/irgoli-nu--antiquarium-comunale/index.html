Antiquarium comunale

Museums · Archaeological

In the former town hall, on two floors, Irgoli tells from Neolithic to the Middle Ages with almost all finds of the territory, especially from the shrine of Janna 'e Pruna and Su Notante: axes, millstones, melting pots, ceramics, a pintadera to decorate the bread, milk pots, necklaces in glass paste, broochs and bronze bracelets, votive offerings, betiles and a model of nuraghe in basalt. Workshops for children with excavation simulation.

Why go. See the exhibits of the sanctuary before or after the visit to Mount Senes.
