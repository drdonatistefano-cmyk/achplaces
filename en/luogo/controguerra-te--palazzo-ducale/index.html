Palazzo Ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Connected to the tower, it was built in 1279 on the remains of Roman buildings and testifies to the dominion of Acquaviva, dukes of Atri, from the mid-15th century. In a hall of the Town Hall are exhibited some archaeological finds of the territory, others are preserved at the Museo Pigorini of Rome and the Florentine Museum of Prehistory.

Why go. Seven centuries of history around the tower.
