Civico Museo Archeologico Su Mulinu

Museums · Archaeological

In the former Granatic Mountain of the mid-nineteenth century, exhibits ceramic, metal, vitreous and lithic finds of the territory from the fourth millennium BC to the third century AD, especially from the excavations of Su Mulinu, and illustrates the evolution of nuragic architecture. The strong piece is the life-size cast of the nuragic altar of the IX-VIII century B.C. in a scenic reconstruction; there is a section for the blind with captions in Braille and finds to touch. Admission fee, free for residents and children up to 6 years.

Why go. The cast of the altar of Su Mulinu and the history of the site.
