Cripta e Menhir di San Paolo

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the countryside, among the most interesting menhirs: at the base there is a small Byzantine newsstand from which you can see the remains of the interior frescoes, with references to St. Paul and Tarantism.

Why go. Where the prehistoric stone meets the cult of St. Paul and Tarantism.
