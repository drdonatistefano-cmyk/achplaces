Spiaggia delle Due Sorelle

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

It takes its name from the two twin faraglioni that close one end of the beach. In summer you can reach by boat from the port of Numana; Legambiente counts it among the ten most beautiful beaches in Italy and there are released sea turtles. A little north is the beach of the works, pebbles and stones; the faraglioni are also seen from the top of the cliff along a path of 5.4 km of the Park.

Why go. The beach symbol of the Conero, reachable only from the sea.
