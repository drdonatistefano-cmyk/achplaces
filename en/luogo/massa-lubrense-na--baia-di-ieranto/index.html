Baia di Ieranto

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Insect in front of the Faraglioni di Capri, site of community interest in the Marine Protected Area of Punta Campanella, donated to the FAI by Italsider in 1987; once limestone quarry abandoned in the 1950s, today among Mediterranean maquis, olive groves and terraced citrus groves. You can reach it on foot from Nerano (about 40 minutes downhill, 1 hour uphill, medium difficulty) passing next to the sixteenth-century tower of Montalto, or by sea with authorized boats; the FAI offers guided tours by reservation.

Why go. The FAI Bay in front of the Faraglioni.
