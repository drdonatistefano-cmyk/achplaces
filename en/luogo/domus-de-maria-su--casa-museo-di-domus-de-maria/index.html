Casa museo di Domus de Maria

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

It exhibits Phoenician, Punic and Roman finds from the municipal territory, in the area of ancient Bithia: everyday objects, funerary and votive furnishings such as lamps, plates and amphorae; the strong piece is the reconstruction of a Phoenician-Punic tomb with its furnishings. Open every day in summer, in the other months by reservation; guided tours.

Why go. The exhibits of Bithia, a stone's throw from the site.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele, Domus de Maria
  • Entry €1,60 reduced €1
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
