Museo della Cattedrale e Labirinto di Porsenna

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

From the Cathedral Museum you descend into the tunnels of an Etruscan aqueduct, believed for centuries the tomb of King Porsenna: 130 meters up to the Etruscan-Roman cistern of the first century BC and then up to the tower of the twelfth century.

Why go. From the tower you can see the lakes of Chiusi and Montepulciano.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Chiusi
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
