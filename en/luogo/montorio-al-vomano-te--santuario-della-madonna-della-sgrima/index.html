Santuario della Madonna della Sgrima

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Marian Sanctuary attested from 1324, with origins related to an event similar to the miracle of Loreto; it was an obligatory stop for transhumant shepherds and the current appearance derives from a restoration of the sixteenth century.

Why go. The sanctuary of transhumant shepherds.
