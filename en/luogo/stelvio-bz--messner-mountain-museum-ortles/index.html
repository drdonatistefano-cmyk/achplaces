Messner Mountain Museum Ortles

Museums · Themed

In Solda, about 1,900 meters at the foot of the Ortles, an underground museum dedicated to ice and glaciers: mountains of ice, Arctic and Antarctica, avalanches, skis, ice climbing and polar expeditions, with the largest collection in the world of Ortles paintings and ice tools of two centuries. Designed by Reinhold Messner with the Venostano architect Arnold Gapp, it is placed in a hillside: concrete walls bent like ice crystals, a band of windows like a crevasse and a window that frames the top of the Ortles.

Why go. The ice told by Messner.
