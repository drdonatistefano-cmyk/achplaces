Museo naturalistico antropologico

Museums · Science and university

Founded in 2002 and reopened in 2005 in the Art Nouveau Palace Arena, it is dedicated to geology, flora and marine and coastal fauna of the Strait and related traditions: 13 collections from about 150 to 2,000 specimens, including abyssal fish of the Strait, paleontological species studied by Giuseppe Seguenza and a collection of shells; the section Traditions folk tells about the hunting of the swordfish with documents, illustrations of the period and ancient tools.
