Museo naturalistico Libero Gatti

Museums · Science and university

The route is divided into seven sections, including a marina with more than 1000 shells, one dedicated to flowers and a diorama of the coral reef; it is part of a garden-botanical garden of about 60,000 m2 on the Ionian Sea. According to the MiC card it is open every day 16-19, in July and August 16-23.

Why go. Shells, flowers and a large botanical garden on the sea.
