Castello di Carlo V

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the ancient acropolis of Kroton, at the highest point of the city, between the sea and the countryside: rebuilt in the current forms since 1541 by the Viceroy Pedro de Toledo on a project by Giangiacomo D'Acaja and Giovanni Maria Buzzacarino, a polygonal plan with the bastions of San Giacomo and Santa Caterina; it houses the Civic Museum and the Municipal Library.

Why go. The fortress on the acropolis.
