Giardino botanico alpino Valderia

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

More than 500 species of the Maritime Alps in 14 environments, at the confluence of the Gesso della Valletta and Valasco streams. It takes its name from the Valderia Viola, endemic, discovered here by Carlo Allioni in 1780: created by the park in 1990, it is the only alpine garden that contains the place of discovery of the species; the nineteenth-century paths are those of the "Giardino degli Inglesi" of the guests of the spa. Open in summer (in 2022 from 11 June to 12 September, 5 €).

Why go. The violet of Valdieri where it was discovered.
