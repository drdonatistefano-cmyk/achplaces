Museo Irpino – Complesso Monumentale Carcere Borbonico

Museums · Archaeological

The Provincial Museum houses the archaeological finds of the history of the city; it is also located in the Bourbon Prison, wanted by Ferdinando I and designed in 1826 by engineer Giuliano De Fazio.

Why go. The story of Irpinia in a Bourbon prison.
