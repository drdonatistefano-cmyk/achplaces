Castello di Udine e Civici Musei

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

16th-century castle seat of the city museums: Archaeological Museum with materials from Aquileia, Galleria d'Arte Antica from the fourteenth to the nineteenth century with the Consilium in arena of Giambattista and Giandomenico Tiepolo, Museo del Risorgimento from 1797 to 1866 and Museo della Fotografia with the first photo taken in Udine (1856). From the back staircase the panorama of the Carnic and Julian Alps.

Why go. Tiepolo and the Alps from the hill.
