Museo dell'orso marsicano MOM

Museums · Themed

Managed by the Majella Park in a wing of the former convent of Sant'Antonio, active since 2005: five sections with panels, dioramas and a night forest rebuilt on biology, habits, myth and conservation of the bear. From June to August open every day 10-13 and 17-20; from September to May Saturday 16-19 and Sunday 10-13.
