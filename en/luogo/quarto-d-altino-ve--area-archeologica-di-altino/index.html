Area archeologica di Altino

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The Roman city of Altinum extended for about a square kilometre, with hole, basilica, theatre, amphitheatre and spa; after the invasions of the seventh century the inhabitants took refuge in the islands of the lagoon, especially in Torcello. Today in Altino you can visit two open-air archaeological areas, one just a few steps from the museum; the ticket is that of the Archaeological Park (museum and area).

Why go. Walk through the ruins of Altinum, mother of Torcello.

the archaeological area is open from April to October and cannot be visited in case of bad weather
