Biscotto castricianu

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

"U biscottu castricianu" or "da Badissa" (a long secret recipe of the Clarisses of the monastery of Santa Maria degli Angeli), celebrated with a festival in the second half of August; to try also the homemade macaroni and the black Christmas rice.

Why go. The nuns cookie.
