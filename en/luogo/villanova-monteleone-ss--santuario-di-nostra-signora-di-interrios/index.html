Santuario di Nostra Signora di Interrios

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

A rural church erected in the 16th century about 3 km from the village among secular trees, with a modified facade in the 18th century; there are the Nativity of the Virgin on 8 September and St. John the Baptist at the end of August.

Why go. The shrine among the centuries-old trees.
