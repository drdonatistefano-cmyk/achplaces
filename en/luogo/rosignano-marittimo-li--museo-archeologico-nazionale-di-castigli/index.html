Museo archeologico nazionale di Castiglioncello

Museums · Archaeological

Built in 1914 in the form of a Hellenistic temple designed by Luigi Milani, in the pine forest of the Poggetto: furnishings of over 300 Etruscan and Roman tombs of the promontory (end IV - beginning I century BC), with the urn in alabaster of the II century BC that contained the ashes of the Etruscan noblewoman Velia Cerinei.

Why go. Velia Cerinei's urn.
