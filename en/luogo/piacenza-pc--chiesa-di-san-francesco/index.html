Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Gothic-Lombard church in terracotta built between 1278 and 1363 for the Ghibelline Umbertino Landi, with rosettes, spires and rampant arches.

Why go. The Lombard Gothic overlooking Piazza Cavalli.
