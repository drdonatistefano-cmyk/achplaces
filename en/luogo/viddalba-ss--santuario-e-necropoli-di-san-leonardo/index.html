Santuario e necropoli di San Leonardo

History and architecture · Archaeology

The sanctuary is located near the remains of a nuragic village and the necropolis of the end of the III-IV century AD, which has returned funerary furnishings and stems decorated today at the Archaeological Museum; in the church of San Leonardo the simulacrum of San Giovanni Evangelista is kept.

Why go. The place where the stele of the museum comes from.
