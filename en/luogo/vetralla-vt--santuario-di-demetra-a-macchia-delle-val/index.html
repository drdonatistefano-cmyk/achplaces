Santuario di Demetra a Macchia delle Valli

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Etruscan-Roman shrine at a spring, recently discovered and frequented from the end of the III century BC at the beginning of the II AD: the small cell in pepper kept the terracotta statue of the goddess seated on the throne, with the counter, a ritual table, the altar, the bathtub and a natural cave connected to the cell.

Why go. The goddess of the woods.
