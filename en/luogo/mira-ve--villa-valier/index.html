Villa Valier

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Immersed in greenery, with a barchess and a 17th century church; a canvas by Alessandro Maganza with a guitar player, today at the Academy of Venice, gave the name to the locality "Guitarra" . Visits only by appointment.

Why go. The villa that gave its name to the locality Guitar.
