Porta del Cielo, i tetti del Duomo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

A path in the rooms under the roofs of the Cathedral, closed for centuries, overlooking the interior of the Duomo; open from 1 March to 6 January.

Why go. The Cathedral seen from above.
