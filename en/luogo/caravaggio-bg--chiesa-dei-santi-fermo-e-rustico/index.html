Chiesa dei Santi Fermo e Rustico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the 13th century, probably on a pre-existing building, it is considered the oldest cult building in the village; the façade follows the canons of the Lombard Gothic.

Why go. The Lombard Gothic in the heart of the village.
