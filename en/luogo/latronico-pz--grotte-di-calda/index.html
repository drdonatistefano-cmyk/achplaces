Grotte di Calda

History and architecture · Archaeology

At the spa, five caves with stalactitic formations and springs have returned stone and bone tools and ceramics from the Neolithic to the Bronze Age (8000-1300 BC); sacred deposits and votive enclosures suggest an ancient cult of healthy waters.

Why go. The caves of water worship.
