Bottega del Vino

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

In an 18th-century brick building, with the typical crutin excavated in the sandstone. The wine of the village is the Cisterna DOC, red from croatine grapes (traditionally called Bonarda); among the local specialties Meliga biscuits, apple pie, honey and grape mustard.

Why go. The Cisterna DOC in a cellar dug into the rock.
