Museo del Rubinetto e della sua Tecnologia

Museums · Themed

Museo dell'Ecomuseo del Lago d'Orta e Mottarone dedicated to the relationship between man and water: it was born from the permanent exhibition "L'uomo e l'acqua" opened on April 15, 1995. About three hundred finds of industrial archaeology tell the evolution of the tap and craft techniques, with the copy of a faucet of about 25 AD from the villa of Tiberius in Capri; it also explains the birth of the industrial district of the tap and valve in the Cusio. Upstairs totem on the latest innovations; accessible with elevator.

Why go. A unique museum in the world, in the heart of the tap district.
