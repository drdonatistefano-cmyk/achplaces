Casa Museo degli Spaventapasseri

Museums · Themed

Museum in the former mill Angeli, an ancient mill where wheat and other cereals were ground, in the hamlet of Marter. The permanent collection is dedicated to scarecrow, the result of researches by photographer Flavio Faganello in Trentino on these figures of straw, with legends and curiosities; it offers educational activities for schools and families.

Why go. The scarecrow told in an old mill, suitable for children.
