Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the upper part of the village, Romanesque church of 1099 (XII century for the Touring) with Byzantine elements, built on Roman remains and renovated in the Baroque period; remarkable the baptismal font.

Why go. The Romanesque in the upper part of the village.
