Museo Ovoteca di Civitella del Lago

Museums · Themed

In the medieval village of Civitella del Lago, overlooking the lake of Corbara, it collects the painted eggs or carved by artists, amateurs and students for the National Competition Exhibition "Ovo Pinto" which is held every Easter since 1982, tradition of Roman origin still alive in central Italy.

Why go. Ovo Pinto's artist's eggs.
