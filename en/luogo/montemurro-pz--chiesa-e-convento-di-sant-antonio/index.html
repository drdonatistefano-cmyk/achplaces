Chiesa e convento di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Franciscan complex of 1635 in the upper part of the village: in the monastery frescoes with episodes of the life of St. Anthony and St. Francis; in the church a wooden frame with canvas dated 1666, a Nativity of the eighteenth century and a Pietà of the nineteenth century.

Why go. The country's richest art complex.
