Santuario di Nostra Signora di Monte Spineto

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

On the top of Mount Spineto (459 m), where the inhabitants took refuge in 1155 when a department of Federico Barbarossa sacked and burned Stazzano: in thanksgiving they built a chapel to the Madonna. The sanctuary was wanted by Monsignor Paolo Arese, Bishop of Tortona from 1620 to 1644; today it has three naves and three altars. According to the municipality, the view that you can enjoy on clear days is enough alone to justify the climb.

Why go. The hill of the refuge from Barbarossa, with a wide view on clear days.
