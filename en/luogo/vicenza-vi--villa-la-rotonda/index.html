Villa La Rotonda

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Villa Almerico Capra Valmarana, inspired by the Pantheon with four identical facades and the central circular hall under the dome, model for the White House; open all year round, in winter only with guided tours by reservation on Saturday morning.

Why go. The most famous villa in the world.
