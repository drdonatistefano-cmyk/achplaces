Oasi del Bolgià: mulino e maglio

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Along the CAI 104 mule track, beyond the Stabina stream on a stone donkey-back bridge, the corn, rye and barley mill, active until the eve of World War II, and the hammer with forge, mole and the wind trumpet blowing air into the fire; the wheels are moved by the water of the Grobbia Valley.

Why go. The fire of the forge lit by water.
