Museo dell'arte contadina

Museums · Themed

Ten sections on the economy of the territory: the peasant house with the kitchen, the tools of the shoemaker, the blacksmith and the shepherd, the domestic utensils and the relationship between man and environment.

Why go. The peasant civilization of the Sibaritide.
