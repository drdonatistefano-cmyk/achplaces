Museo civico Antiquarium

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in June 2007, it presents archaeological research on the territory: at the center is the necropolis of Piano (Campo dei) Cerasuoli, with tombs of the 8th-VII century BC of the culture of Oliveto Citra-Cairano. Female sets of rank with fibula and bronze bracelets, male accessories with weapons, a ritual axe and vases with wolf protomes, totemic animal of the Irpini, a Sannitic belt, attic ceramics and materials of the inhabitants of Piano dei Tivoli, excavated between 1979 and 1985. Irpinia system indicates opening Monday-Friday 9-12 (Tuesday and Thursday 16-18 also), Saturday and Sunday 9-13.

Why go. Tombs of the princes with totemic wolf.
