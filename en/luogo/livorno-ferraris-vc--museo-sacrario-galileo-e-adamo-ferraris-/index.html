Museo sacrario «Galileo e Adamo Ferraris» (Palazzo Ferraris)

Museums · House museums

The museum is housed in Palazzo Ferraris, an ancient residence of the family (16th-XX centuries) overlooking the main square, and is dedicated to Galileo Ferraris and his brother Adam. Galileo, born in the country, demonstrated in 1885 the existence of the rotating magnetic field and in 1891 was recognized in Frankfurt . A cumulative ticket with the MAVO €5.

Why go. The birthplace of the father of the three-phase current.
