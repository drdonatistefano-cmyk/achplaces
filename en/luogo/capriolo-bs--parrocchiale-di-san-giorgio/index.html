Parrocchiale di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

17th century church in the center of the village, on the slopes of the mountain, with two registers façade and portal in stone of Sarnico; the interior with three naves with transept and central dome, restored at the beginning of the twentieth century, preserves the Resurrection of Jerome Romanino, the Martyrdom of Saints Gervasio and Protasio of Callisto Piazza and the fifteenth century wooden statue of the Virgin called "Madonna Vecchia" .

Why go. Romanino and Callisto Piazza in the parish of the village.
