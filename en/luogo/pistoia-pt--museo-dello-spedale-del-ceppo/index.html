Museo dello Spedale del Ceppo

Museums · City history · Paid entry

The hospital founded according to tradition in 1277 and active until 2013, with the glazed terracotta frieze of the Works of Mercy of Santi Buglioni and the history of Pistoiese health care.

Why go. The frieze shines in the sun.

  • Where Piazza Papa Giovanni XXIII, Pistoia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
