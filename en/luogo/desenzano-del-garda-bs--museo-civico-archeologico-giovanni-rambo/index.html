Museo Civico Archeologico Giovanni Rambotti

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the former Carmelite convent of Santa Maria de Senioribus, opened in 1990 and re-arranged in 2011, documents the palafittic villages of the Bronze Age of Garda and the Moorish amphitheatre: homes, agriculture, ceramics, metals, ornaments. The most famous piece is the Lavagnone plough, found in 1978, oak with beech yoke, of about 2000 BC, with a dedicated room. Free on the first Sunday of the month and with the Museums Lombardy Subscription.

Why go. The oldest plow in the world.

  • Where Via Anelli 42, Desenzano del Garda
  • Entry €7, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
