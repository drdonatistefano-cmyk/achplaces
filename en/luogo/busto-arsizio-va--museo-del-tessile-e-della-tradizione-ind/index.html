Museo del Tessile e della Tradizione Industriale

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1997 in the former spinning department of the Cottonificio Carlo Ottolini (later Bustese), neo-Gothic brick building probably from 1891-1896: machines, products, documents and objects of the textile industry and other industrial traditions of Busto.

Why go. The city of cotton told by his cars.

From 7 January 2026 closed for renovation works (the conference room, the twin rooms and the embroidery room remain open)
