Villa Cusani Tittoni Traversi e parco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Monumental complex of villas and park among the most representative 18th-century villas of Lombardy: the current aspect is due to the architect Giuseppe Piermarini, in a style close to the Reggia of Monza; in the nineteenth century it was restored Pelagio Palagi and at the beginning of the twentieth century Luca Beltrami performed minor interventions. There were guests Ferdinand IV of Naples and Umberto I. On the first floor sculptures and drawings by Giuseppe Scalvini; in summer, since 2012, the park hosts the festival Â"Parco TittoniÂ" . The park has been a candidate for the FAI census on several occasions.

Why go. The delightful villa of Piermarini and its park.
