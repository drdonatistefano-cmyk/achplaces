Torre Normanna

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Of the four towers of the Norman castle, demolished around 1600, there are two remaining: the Norman Tower, almost intact, dominates the ancient village and offers a beautiful view over all Rutigliano. Guided tours during the year, access by reservation.

Why go. The millennial tower above the village.
