Museo del fischietto in terracotta «Domenico Divella»

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Established in 2004 in the Palazzo San Domenico, a former Dominican convent of the end of the sixteenth century with the church of Santa Maria del Rosario, it preserves hundreds of whistles made by Italian artists for the National Competition linked to the whistle fair in January, for the feast of Saint Anthony Abate. Visit only by reservation (Pro Loco).

Why go. Hundreds of whistle-blowers, symbol of the country.
