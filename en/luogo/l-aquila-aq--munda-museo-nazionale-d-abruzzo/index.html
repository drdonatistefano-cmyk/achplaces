MuNDA – Museo Nazionale d'Abruzzo

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The most important museum in the region, now in the former municipal slaughterhouse near the Fontana delle 99 Cannelle: the Abruzzo Madonnas painted on a 14th and 15th century table, works by Andrea de Lithium and Giacinto Brandi and portraits of San Bernardino.

Why go. The art of Abruzzo in one museum.
