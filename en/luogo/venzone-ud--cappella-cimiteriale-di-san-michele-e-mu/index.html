Cappella cimiteriale di San Michele e mummie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

13th century chapel (built around 1200) in the cathedral churchyard, which houses the Venzone mummies, dated between 1348 and 1881. From the tombs of the cathedral were extracted about forty, starting from the mummy of the hunchback found in 1647; the natural mummification is attributed to a mold, Hypha bombicina Pers., which dehydrates the tissues.

Why go. The famous natural mummy of Venzone.
