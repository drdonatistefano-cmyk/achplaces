Museo archeologico regionale «Paolo Orsi»

Museums · Archaeological

In the park of Villa Landolina, in a building of Franco Minissi (1967-86): about 9,000 m2 and over 18,000 finds from prehistory to early Christian, including Venus Anadiomene and the sarcophagus of Adelphia.
