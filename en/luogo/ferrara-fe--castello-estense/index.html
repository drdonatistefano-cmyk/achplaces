Castello Estense

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Fortress built in 1385 by Nicolò II d'Este on a project by Bartolino da Novara, then ducal residence: prisons, kitchens, Garden of Oranges and frescoed halls, in the path set up by Gae Aulenti.

Why go. The symbol of the city, surrounded by the moat still full of water.

Large restoration and seismic adjustment works are under way: the visit route can change or close in part, check before leaving.
