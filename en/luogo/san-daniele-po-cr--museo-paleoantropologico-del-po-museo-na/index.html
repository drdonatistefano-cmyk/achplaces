Museo Paleoantropologico del Po (Museo naturalistico paleontologico)

Museums · Science and university

Founded in 1998 by the Naturalistic Group Paleontophile (GNP) and the Municipality, it collects hundreds of fossils of the Quaternary recovered along the banks of the Po: remains of about thirty species, including mammoth, megacero deer, bison and rhinoceros, and rare finds of leopard and spelean hyena. The most important piece is a Neanderthal Man's frontal bone nicknamed "Pàus" which is presented as an exceptional testimony of the species in the Po Valley. It is divided into four sections (paleontology, anthropology, mineralogy and didactics), with a wing dedicated to the findings of the Po beaches. Open Tuesday-Friday 8-13 only by reservation with one day in advance and Sunday 14-19; closed Monday, Saturday, August and on the main holidays; accessible to those who have difficulty in walking.

Why go. The Neanderthal of the Po and the great fauna of the glacial era of the plain.
