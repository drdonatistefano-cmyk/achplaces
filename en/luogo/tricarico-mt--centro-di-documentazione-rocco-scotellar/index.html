Centro di documentazione Rocco Scotellaro

Museums · Themed

In the former convent of San Francesco, remodelled several times, it houses a specialized library on southern Italy and a photographic archive with works by Henri Cartier-Bresson, Arturo Zavattini, Mario Carbone, Mario Cresci and Antonio Pagnotta.

Why go. The South of Scotellaro in the shots of the great photographers.
