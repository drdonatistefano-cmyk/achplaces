Castello di Châtel-Argent

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The thirteenth century, of great strategic importance, takes its name from the coins coined there; the circular tower, the belt and the ruins of the chapel, 15 minutes walk from the village.

Why go. The castle of coins.
