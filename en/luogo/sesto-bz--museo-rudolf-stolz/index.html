Museo Rudolf Stolz

Museums · Modern art

Two rooms with more than 160 works by the painter Rudolf Stolz (1874-1960), largely self-taught and considered, after Egger-Lienz, among the most important Tyrolean artists of the first half of the twentieth century: sketches, sketches for frescoes, watercolors and studies, with paintings by Albert Stolz. Every summer a temporary themed exhibition.

Why go. The painter of the Madonnas in his country.
