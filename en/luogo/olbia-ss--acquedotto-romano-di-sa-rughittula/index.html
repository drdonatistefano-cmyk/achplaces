Acquedotto romano di sa Rughittula

History and architecture · Archaeology

Considered the most intact aqueduct of Sardinia, dated between the 1st and 3rd century AD and restored or rebuilt between III and IV: it brought water from the sources of Cabu Abbas to the thermal baths for about 3.5 km. You can see a section of about 100 meters with a rectangular cistern; other remains in Via Canova and Via Nanni.

Why go. The arches of the best Roman aqueduct on the island.
