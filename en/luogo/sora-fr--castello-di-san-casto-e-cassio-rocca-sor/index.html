Castello di San Casto e Cassio (Rocca Sorella)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Ruins on the San Casto hill: born as a fortress Volsca, then Roman and Longobard, it was transformed by the Della Rovere in 1520 in the fortress reached until today; remains of the Roman castle are partly visible in the underground. It became a noble residence with Cantelmo, Della Rovere, Boncompagni and Borgia. It is a natural monument and can be reached on foot with CAI paths, with wide views of the city.

Why go. The panoramic terrace on Sora and the valley of Liri.
