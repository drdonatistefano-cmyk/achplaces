Maso Spilzi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Maso built in the second half of the eighteenth century by the rich family Spilzi, unique example of alpine settlement between noble residence and fortified complex: buildings around a deep court, west side protected by high walls with walkway, barn gate with wounds for the ropes of a drawbridge. The barn hosts temporary exhibitions and cultural events. It can also be reached on foot through the Ecken Biotope.

Why go. A farm-fortress of the eighteenth century.
