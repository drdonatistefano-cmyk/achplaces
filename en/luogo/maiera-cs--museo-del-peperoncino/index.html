Museo del peperoncino

Museums · Themed

In the Palazzo Ducale, managed by the Accademia Italiana del Peperoncino di Diamante: four rooms with objects and utensils of all origins, the routes of diffusion of the plant, seeds, spices and sauces, and a video room with advertising posters and satirical cartoons on the chili. Between the pieces a lithography by Renato Guttuso and the image of chili taken from a recipe book of the sixteenth century found in Frankfurt.

Why go. The museum dedicated to the symbol of Calabrian cuisine.

MiC card (updated 2022) is closed every day; times to be checked with the Accademia del Chiperoncino
