Orto botanico dell'Università

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Born from a part of the courtyard dedicated to medicinal plants under the guidance of the doctor Gaetano Rossi; in 1772 he passed to the University, which created the flowerbeds of the "Semplici" in 1772.

Why go. A scientific garden next to the Ducal Garden.
