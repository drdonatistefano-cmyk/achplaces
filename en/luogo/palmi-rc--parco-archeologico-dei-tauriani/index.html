Parco Archeologico dei Tauriani

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On a plateau 70 meters above sea level, frequented by the Neolithic: villages of the Bronze Age, the Italian and then Roman city of Tauriana with the mosaic house, the sanctuary, the building for shows, a basal road and the crypt of San Fantino, late antique nymph with medieval frescoes.

Why go. Three thousand years of history on the sea.

  • Where Località Taureana, Palmi
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
