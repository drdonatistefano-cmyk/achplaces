Cattedrale di Santa Maria del Fiore

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The Duomo, with its facade in white, green and pink marble and the interior under the frescoed vault of the Dome. The entrance to the Cathedral is free, without ticket or reservation.

Why go. The heart of Florence, free.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Firenze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website duomo.firenze.it
