Museo dei fossili e minerali del Monte Nerone

Museums · Science and university

Born in 1977 in the basement of Palazzo Ubaldini (former stables, prisons and snowfields), it exhibits over 2,000 finds: mainly fossils of Mount Nerone of about 200 million years ago such as the ammonites, tools in silce, remains of the cave bear and of prehistoric elephants and minerals from various parts of Italy; a section on dinosaurs with skulls, eggs and claws from Mongolia and the United States and a room on human evolution. LIS video guides, interactive panels and audio content.

Why go. Two hundred million years under a Renaissance palace.
