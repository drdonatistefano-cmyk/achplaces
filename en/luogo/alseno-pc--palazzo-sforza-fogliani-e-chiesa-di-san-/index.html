Palazzo Sforza Fogliani e chiesa di San Biagio, Castelnuovo Fogliani

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The medieval village of Castelnuovo Fogliani has grown around a fortress with a tower dated 1377, transformed into a villa in the mid-18th century designed by Luigi Vanvitelli, to which the nearby church of San Biagio is attributed. Neoclassical facade, a hall frescoed by Giuseppe Natali, an oval salon richly decorated with stucco and an Italian garden with geometric parts; in 1925 the last Duchess Sforza Fogliani donated the complex to the Holy See. The Municipality organizes guided tours of about 80 minutes (church, frescoed rooms and garden) on some Sundays from April to October, at 3.30 pm and at 5 pm, with reservation required at 0523 945500.

Why go. The rooms of the Vanvitelli in a hamlet of the Bassa Piantina.
