Local life and traditions · Spas · Viewpoint

The village is surrounded by forests and springs of water with low mineral content (in Santo Stefano the water is bottled). The spa complex, among the main attractions of the village, offers well-equipped paths among the trees and wide views of the mountains of Basilicata.

Why go. Sources and woods overlooking the Basilicata.
