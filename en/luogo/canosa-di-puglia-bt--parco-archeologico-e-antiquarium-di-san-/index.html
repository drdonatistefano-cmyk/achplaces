Parco archeologico e Antiquarium di San Leucio

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On a south-east height of the city, the italian temple of Minerva destroyed at the end of the 5th century AD and above it a central-planted early Christian basilica, the most imposing of its kind in Puglia, with polychrome mosaics such as the 'mosaico del pavone', the Antiquarium, opened in 2008, exhibits its findings.

Why go. A pagan temple under a basilica.

  • Where Via Santa Lucia 100, Canosa di Puglia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
