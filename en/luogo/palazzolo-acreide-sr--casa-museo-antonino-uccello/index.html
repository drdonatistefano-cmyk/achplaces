Casa museo Antonino Uccello

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

On the ground floor of the eighteenth-century Palazzo Ferla-Bonelli, in the rooms once inhabited by the massarium, collects the objects of the peasant civilization saved by the poet and ethnologist Antonino Uccello (Canicattini 1922), established at Palazzolo in 1944: wooden spoons, orange wood nativity scenes, cart keys and iron sculptures, which he also exhibited in the galleries of the North.
