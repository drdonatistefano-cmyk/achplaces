Tempio della Fraternità dei Popoli

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Sanctuary and shrine of peace at about 700 meters, on the remains of the church of San Martiano a Cella, wanted by Don Adamo Accosa, chaplain of the Alps, and inaugurated in 1958: it is built with stones from about one hundred places affected by the Second World War (from Coutances, Montecassino and El Alamein to Hiroshima and Nagasaki), spires of the Duomo of Milan fallen in the bombings of 1943, a baptismal source by the shutter of a cannon of the battleship Andrea Doria and a crucifix made of weapons. Open every day 9-18:30 from April to October; from November to March Saturday and Sunday 9-17, other days by reservation.

Why go. A shrine of peace made with the stones of war.
