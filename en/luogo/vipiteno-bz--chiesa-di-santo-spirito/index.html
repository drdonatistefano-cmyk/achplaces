Chiesa di Santo Spirito

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Gothic church of 1399 in Piazza Città, born with the hospital: the oldest preserved church of Vipiteno, sober outside and frescoed inside by Giovanni da Brunico.

Why go. The frescoes of Giovanni da Brunico.
