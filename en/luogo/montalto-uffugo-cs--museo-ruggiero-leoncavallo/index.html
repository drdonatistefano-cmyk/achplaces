Museo Ruggiero Leoncavallo

Museums · House museums

It is indicated by the Municipality as the only museum in Italy dedicated entirely to the composer, who lived in Montalto from the age of five and set Pagliacci, inspired by a tragic fact he had witnessed as a boy. It contains documents, scores and testimonies related to Mattinata, Zazà and I Medici; it is located in the Dominican complex of the fifteenth century, today the Town Hall. Open Wednesday-Saturday 10-12 and 16-18 (Touring Club); tel. 368 7368634. Every year the country dedicates a music festival to him.

Why go. The places and cards of the author of Pagliacci.
