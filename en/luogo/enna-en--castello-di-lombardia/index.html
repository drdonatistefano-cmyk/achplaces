Castello di Lombardia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Medieval fortress on the highest point of the city, in the ancient Acropolis: Frederick II expanded it from an Arab fortification renovated by the Normans; from the Pisan Tower, of the thirteenth century, the 360-degree panorama from almost a thousand meters.

Why go. The viewpoint on central Sicily.
