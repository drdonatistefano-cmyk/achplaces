Palazzo Barberini e Museo civico naturalistico dei Monti Prenestini

Museums · Themed

The palace, built on the remains of a previous structure, preserves the architecture of the sixteenth century and was restored in the mid-18th century; it houses the Town Hall and the naturalistic museum, active for over twenty years, with digital dioramas on local habitats, environmental education and guided tours also to the educational garden in the municipal park. Accessible to people with disabilities.

Why go. The biodiversity of Prenestini in a sixteenth-century building.
