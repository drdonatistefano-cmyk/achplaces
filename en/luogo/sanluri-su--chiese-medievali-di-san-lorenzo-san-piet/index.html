Chiese medievali di San Lorenzo, San Pietro e San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

San Lorenzo was the first parish church in the village and has a wooden ceiling decorated with heads of lions and dragons; also San Pietro and San Rocco are medieval churches.

Why go. Small ancient churches, with a curious wooden ceiling.
