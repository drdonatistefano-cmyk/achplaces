Chiesa dei Santi Quirico e Giulitta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Isolated outside the village of Veglio, it is a seventeenth-century building with stuccoes and scagliola that preserves as a side chapel the Romanesque apse of the 11th-XII century, with medieval frescoes; of value the chapel of the Madonna delle Grazie. It meets along the 11 km route between Dizzasco, Cerano and Casasco d'Intelvi.

Why go. Romanesque and Baroque intelvese in the same church.
