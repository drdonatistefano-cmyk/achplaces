Castello del Catajo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Started in 1570 by the Obizzi as the seat of representation, it was enlarged in the nineteenth century for the court extended in exile and became hunting residence of the Habsburgs; on the noble floor the forty squares frescoed by Giovanni Battista Zelotti with the history of the family. Park with free deer, gardens with fountains and fish ponds and panoramic terrace on the hills.

Why go. The strangest of the Venetian villas.
