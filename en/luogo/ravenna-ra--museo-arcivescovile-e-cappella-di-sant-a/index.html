Museo Arcivescovile e Cappella di Sant'Andrea

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The Diocese Museum houses the ivory chair of Maximian and the archbishop's chapel, the only preserved early Christian Orthodox chapel.

Why go. It combines a UNESCO oratory and one of the masterpieces of Byzantine ivory.

  • Where Piazza Arcivescovado 1, Ravenna
  • Entry In the cumulative Diocese (5 monuments)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.ravennamosaici.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
