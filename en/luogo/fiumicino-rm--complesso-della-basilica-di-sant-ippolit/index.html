Complesso della Basilica di Sant'Ippolito

History and architecture · Archaeology

Early Christian Basilica with three naves between the end of the IV and early V century, on a Roman thermal building, brought to light in the seventies along the Fiumicino channel; in the Antiquarium the Carolingian ciborium of the time of Leo III.

Why go. The first Christianity of the harbor.

Open only on a few Sundays, check the calendar
