Santuario dell'Assunta (San Lorenzo)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Sanctuary on the edge of the town, begun in 1418 and consecrated in 1506, with frescoes and a grandiose golden wooden ancona on the main altar.

Why go. The great golden anchor of the sanctuary.
