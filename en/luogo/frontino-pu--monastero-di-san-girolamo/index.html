Monastero di San Girolamo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Built in 1500 and surrounded by secular oaks, it includes a restored church and convent, now a period residence; in the church a altarpiece by Bernardino da Longiano and in the refectory a fresco of the Last Supper.

Why go. A hermitage in the oaks.
