Chiesa e area di Santo Stefano di Sessano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Romanesque church of the year about 1000 on a rocky spur overlooking the Morenic Amphitheatre: it is the only survivor of the village of Sessano, destroyed by a landslide in the 13th century. It preserves frescoes from the middle of the 11th century with the twelve apostles and St. Stephen, restored in 1999; reopened to the public in 1996 after the restorations of the seventies. Next to it, a medieval garden with six parcels of ancient plants, a pergola of Canavesian vines and a collection of rosemary trees, recognized by the Piedmont Region as Public Botanical Garden since 2009.

Why go. Romanesque, 11th century frescoes and views of the Moorish amphitheatre.
