Necropoli di Campu Maiore

History and architecture · Archaeology

24 domus de Janas excavated in a rocky ridge, with traces of red ochre on the walls; the tomb IV has seven cells. In the territory other necropolis (Maniere, Cronta, on Cantaru, Grigos) bring the total to about 50 domus de Janas.

Why go. Prehistoric tombs still marked by the red ochre.
