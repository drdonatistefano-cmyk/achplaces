Villa Carlotti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The main house is probably sixteenth-century of the Horses, bought in 1680 by the Marquises Giulio and Bonaventura Carlotti and transformed between 1670 and 1711; the loggia has date 1704 and the octagonal chapel of San Biagio, of 1695, has an altarpiece of 1731. The original frescoes are no longer there and the rustic court is still in agricultural use.

Why go. A baroque Venetian villa in the countryside of Prova.
