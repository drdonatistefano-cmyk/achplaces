Per Via, Museo Tesino delle Stampe e dell'Ambulantato

Museums · Themed

In Casa Buffa Giacantoni, inhabited by generations of print sellers and empty since the 1960s, tells the street papers that sold art prints in the world, opening shops and becoming publishers, and the families remained in the country. It is run by the Trentina Alcide De Gasperi Foundation.

Why go. The story of the streetwalkers of prints around the world.
