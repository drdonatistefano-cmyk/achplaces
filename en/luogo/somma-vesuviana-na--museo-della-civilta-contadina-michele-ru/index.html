Museo della civiltà contadina «Michele Russo»

Museums · Themed

Ethno-anthropological museum in the garden and cellar of the former convent of Santa Maria del Pozzo, run by an association and recognized museum of regional interest: over 3,000 objects of rural life before mechanization and about 3,000 photographs, in 20 sectors (transport with soma animals, wine room, crafts, peasant food, etnomusicology), plus a nativity scene with figures in eighteenth-century clothes, stables with animals and educational garden. It is educational farm with laboratories (bread, cheese, beekeeping).

Why go. The Vesuvian countryside before the cars, with the real animals.

MiC card (updated 2020) indicates closed every day: check with the museum (tel. 081 5318496)
