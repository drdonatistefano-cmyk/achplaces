San Vito, abbazia e torre

History and architecture · Villages · Viewpoint

Borgo about 40 minutes walk to Mola, known for the remains of the 10th century abbey of the Basilian monks and a quadrangular tower; near the abbey is cultivated the carrot of Polignano, Slow Food garrison. With clubs and restaurants, it is where you move the nightlife.

Why go. The abbey on the sea and the carrot garrison Slow Food.
