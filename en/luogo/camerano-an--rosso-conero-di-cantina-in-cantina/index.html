Rosso Conero di cantina in cantina

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Hills and vineyards of Montepulciano give Rosso Conero, to be tasted from cellar to cellar; the Festa del Rosso Conero is held the first week of September with refreshment points, stalls, street games and tastings, and in summer they organize tastings also in the caves.

Why go. Conero's wine in his land.
