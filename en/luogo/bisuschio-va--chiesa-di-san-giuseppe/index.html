Chiesa di San Giuseppe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Dedicated to San Dionigi until 1721, it is considered the oldest building still existing in the village, probably of the eleventh century. The restorations brought to light the foundations of a second apse and remains of graves, clues of a cemetery area of the VI-VII century.

Why go. The oldest church of Bisuschio, with traces of the early Middle Ages.
