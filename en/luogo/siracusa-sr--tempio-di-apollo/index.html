Tempio di Apollo

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The oldest Doric stone temple in Sicily, then early Christian church, mosque, Norman church and barracks under Charles V.

Why go. The oldest Doric stone temple in Sicily.

  • Entry Free
