Cala d'Oliva

History and architecture · Villages · Viewpoint

Village of low and white houses in the north-east of Asinara, at the end of the 25 km road, with an Aragonese tower of the seventeenth century: housed from 1885 to 1997 the penal colony of maximum security, of which remain in the upper part the buildings and bunkers, and today the seat of the park with the Botanical Observatory and a hostel; near, two white sandy beaches, including Sa Murighessa called the beach of the Detainees. You arrive only with the boats authorized by Porto Torres or Stintino.

Why go. The village of the Asinara prison.
