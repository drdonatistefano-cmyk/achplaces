Ducea di Nelson e Castello Nelson

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

The complex was founded in the 12th century as a Benedictine abbey of Santa Maria di Maniace, partly destroyed by the earthquake of 1693 and then donated to Admiral Horatio Nelson. It includes the residence of the Dukes Nelson-Bridport (called the Castle) with the Nelson Museum among furnished apartments, family portraits, documents and relics, the church of Santa Maria in Arab-Norman style, the granaries transformed into congress halls, the Celtic cross in honor of the admiral in the courtyard, an English garden and a park with lava stone sculptures and the English cemetery of the ducal family.

Why go. The Sicilian residence of Admiral Nelson.
