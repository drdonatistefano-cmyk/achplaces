Biblioteca Guarneriana

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1466 to accommodate 173 codes donated by the humanist Guarniero d'Artegna, it is the oldest public library in Friuli: more than 600 manuscripts, including a Byzantine Bible of the late 12th century and a Divine Comedy of the late 14th century, about 80 incunabula and 12,000 ancient volumes. The ancient section is visited by reservation and is closed in August.

Why go. The illuminated codes of the oldest library in Friuli.
