Museo delle Sinopie

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

It collects the preparatory drawings of the artists of the XII and XIII centuries who frescoed the Camposanto.

Why go. The backstage of the frescoes.

  • Where Piazza dei Miracoli, Pisa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
