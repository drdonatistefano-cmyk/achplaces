Cappella gentilizia di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On the central square, dedicated to the patron saint: built in the early seventeenth century in Baroque style by the noble Vincenzo Rossi, with wooden decorations, stuccoes and polychrome marbles reappeared with recent restorations.

Why go. The noble baroque of the village.
