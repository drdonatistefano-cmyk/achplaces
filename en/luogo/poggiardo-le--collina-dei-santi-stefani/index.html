Collina dei Santi Stefani

History and architecture · Archaeology

To the north of Vaste, the excavations brought to light a early Christian basilica built on a martyrion of the fifth century, surrounded by a vast necropolis with tombs of various eras; near is the crypt of Saints Stefani.

Why go. From the early Christian Martyrion to the medieval tombs.
