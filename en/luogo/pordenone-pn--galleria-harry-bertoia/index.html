Galleria Harry Bertoia

Museums · Modern art

Municipal exhibition venue for temporary exhibitions, dedicated to the designer Harry Bertoia, in the complex of Palazzo Spelladi, whose original structure dates back at least to the beginning of the fourteenth century.

Why go. Exhibitions in the 14th century palace.
