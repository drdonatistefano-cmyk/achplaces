Galleria d'arte moderna Pino Casarini

Museums · Modern art

He exhibited the works of the Veronese painter Pino Casarini, who donated his collection to the parish of Sacile in two films (1982 and 1988): paintings, drawings and sketches for the settings of the Verona Arena, covering about fifty years of activity, from 1920 to 1970. Open on the first and third Sundays of the month 10-12; on the other days on request, with mandatory booking (tel. 0434 780702).

Why go. The sketches for the Verona Arena by the author of the frescoes of the Duomo.
