Chiesa di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Outside the walls on the Cacultian hill, elevated to cathedrals as the second seat of Sabina after the Saracen destruction of Foronovo. In the material façade of Roman tombs, two Lusoriae tabulae and a Roman bas-relief with a seated emperor; inside the chapels of San Sisinio (1656) and San Saturnino (rebuilt in 1782) and at the main altar the Martyrdom of San Lorenzo di Vincenzo Manenti.

Why go. Roman stones on the facade.
