Museo della Memoria di Ferramonti

Museums · Themed

The camp, built in 1940 on about 160,000 m2 with white barracks arranged in horseshoe, was the largest fascist internment camp: there were foreign Jewish prisoners, then Italian Jews, opponents of the regime, political refugees and Chinese citizens. The museum, inaugurated on 25 April 2004 in the restored administrative shacks, exhibits documents, photographs, letters, personal objects and video testimonies, with a plastic room that reproduces the field in scale and, since 2009, a section on the Shoah.

Why go. The place of the memory of the internship in Calabria.

visits by appointment, also on Sundays (Common); tel. 0984 32377
