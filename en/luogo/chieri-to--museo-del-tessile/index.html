Museo del Tessile

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Ancient machinery and original instruments still working, with weaving courses; tells the city of textiles, already main activity in the fifteenth century.

Why go. Frames that still work.

  • Where Via Santa Chiara 11A, Chieri
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org
