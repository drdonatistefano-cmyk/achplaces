Chiese della SS. Trinità, di San Giuseppe e di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Church of the SS. Trinity is known for its medieval frescoes, that of St. Joseph for the late Baroque façade; the Church of St. Anthony, at the northern entrance of the village, has a portal coming from the cathedral.

Why go. Medieval and Baroque frescoes in the village.
