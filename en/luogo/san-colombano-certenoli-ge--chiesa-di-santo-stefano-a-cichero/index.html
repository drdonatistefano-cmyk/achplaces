Chiesa di Santo Stefano a Cichero

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish already existed in the sixteenth century; during the Second World War the church was severely damaged and the parish registers were destroyed. It preserves a painting of the Madonna dell'Orto, very venerated, escaped from the fire of 1944.

Why go. The Madonna survived the 1944 fire.
