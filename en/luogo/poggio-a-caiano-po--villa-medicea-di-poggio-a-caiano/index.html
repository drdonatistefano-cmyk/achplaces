Villa Medicea di Poggio a Caiano

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Wanted by Lorenzo il Magnifico and built by Giuliano da Sangallo between 1484 and 1520, prototype of the Renaissance villa: frescoes of Pontormo (Vertumno and Pomona), Andrea del Sarto, Filippino Lippi and Allori, the garden and the lemon house.

Why go. The most famous Medici villa.
