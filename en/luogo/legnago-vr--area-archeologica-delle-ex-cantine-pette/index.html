Area archeologica delle ex Cantine Petternella

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Remains of the hexagonal fortress designed in the sixteenth century by Michele Sanmicheli, with thick walls, terrapies, moat and esplanade; the works began in 1525 and lasted about seventy years. After the Unity, the walls were largely demolished from 1887 onwards: only a few stretches remain here and along Via Leopardi.

Why go. The last walls of the fortress of the Quadrilateral.
