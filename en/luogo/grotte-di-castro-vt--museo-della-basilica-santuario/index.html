Museo della Basilica Santuario

Museums · Themed

Small museum in the basement of the Basilica of Maria Santissima del Suffragio with sacred furnishings from the 15th to the 18th century and prehistoric, protohistoric and Etruscan archaic finds of the territory; among the main pieces a fifteenth century reliquary and sacred vestments of the early seventeenth and eighteenth centuries.

Why go. Sacred and Etruscan furnishings under the basilica.
