Museo delle macchine agricole Orsi

Museums · Themed

In the former Orsi tractor factory, example of industrial archaeology: 1800 m2 with the permanent exhibition in the former tournament of 22 machines of the Orsi and Berri collections (tractors, locomobiles, presses, threshing machines and a harvester) documenting production from the early twentieth century to the late 1950s.

Why go. The historic tractors of a factory in Dolonese.

no regular time: visits by reservation or during events of the Municipality
