Castello di Peschici

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It was built by the Normans between the 10th and 11th centuries against Saracens and pirates and was also the residence of the monks of the Abbey of Tremiti, and Frederick II added the tower called imperial rock and baronial enclosure, and in the eighteenth century it was rebuilt in its present form. It is privately owned, but you can visit the dungeons, once prisons and weapons depots, with instruments of torture from the Middle Ages to the Renaissance, and a small terraced garden on the sea.

Why go. The dungeons and the terrace on the sea.
