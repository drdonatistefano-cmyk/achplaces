Chiese di Nostra Signora del Rosario e di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The eighteenth-century parish church of Our Lady of the Rosary, in the center of the village on a large churchyard, houses three paintings (the Virgin of the Rosary of the seventeenth century, the Pentecost of 1874 and the Purging Souls of 1927) and two marble lions. In front of it stands Santa Croce, in 1677, the seat of the fraternity that takes care of the rites of Holy Week. In the village also St. Peter (XVII century) and the Purgatory (XVIII century).

Why go. The religious heart of the country and the Easter rites.
