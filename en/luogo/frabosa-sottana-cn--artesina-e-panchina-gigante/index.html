Artesina e Panchina Gigante

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

The ski resort of Mondolè offers summer tubing, bike park and a bob descent on a monorail of 1.3 km; in the area the giant red bench of Chris Bangle, reachable all year round, from which on clear days you can see the sea from 1,500 meters. The network of trails Mondolè Ring you can walk, bike or horseback.

Why go. The sea seen in the Alps.
