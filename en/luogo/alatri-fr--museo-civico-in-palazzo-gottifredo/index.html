Museo civico in Palazzo Gottifredo

Museums · Archaeological

In the thirteenth century Palazzo Gottifredo, commissioned by Cardinal Gottifredo of Raynaldo; museum founded in 1932 and reopened in 1996. Archaeological sections on the 19th century scholars of the polygonal walls and on the ancient Aletrium (inscription by Lucio Betilieno Varo, model of Etruscan-Italian temple, bronze perhaps of Hercules, paintings of the Domus of Betilienus), vases recovered by the Guardia di Finanza and demoetnoanthropological collection. Open Tuesday-Sunday 10-13, Friday-Sunday also 15.30-18.30.

Why go. The story of Aletrium in a medieval palace.
