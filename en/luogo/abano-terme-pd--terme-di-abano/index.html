Terme di Abano

Local life and traditions · Spas

With Montegrotto, it forms the largest thermal resort in Europe: more than one hundred establishments with mud and healing baths, in waters rich in beneficial minerals.

Why go. The Euganean thermal mud, in one of the oldest spas in Europe.
