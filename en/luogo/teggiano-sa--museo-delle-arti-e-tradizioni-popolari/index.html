Museo delle arti e tradizioni popolari

Museums · Themed

Objects of everyday life, work and religiosity popular between the late nineteenth and mid twentieth centuries: tools of peasants, spinners, carpenters and blacksmiths, and photographic plates of the historic center. Open Monday and Sunday.

Why go. Crafts and faces of the Teggiano of the past.
