Museo etnografico Galluras - Museo della femina agabbadora

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

First ethnographic museum of Gallura, in a granite building of the late eighteenth century in the historic center: on three floors it reconstructs the rooms of the Gallura house, with the vinification on the ground floor, kitchen, dining room and bedroom on the first floor and the processing of wool and cork at the last. The symbol piece is the "martello" of the feminine agabbadora, a branch of olive grove of about 40 cm found in an abandoned building near the village, linked to the figure that according to tradition ended the agony of the dying.

Why go. The peasant Gallura and the legend of the agabbas.

  • Where Via Nazionale 35, Luras
  • Entry €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
