Villa di Montefalcone

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Villa on the Cerbaie built on the basis of a castle of the Cadolings of the 11th century, then the Albizi, who erected the current fifteenth-century church; ample view of the Valdarno.

Why go. The view of the Valdarno from the Cerbaie.
