Chiesa Madre di Maria SS. Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The original nucleus, dedicated to St. Christopher, dates back to 1552-1570; the naves are from 1639-1642, the bell tower was completed in 1783 and the facade rebuilt in 1960 by Antonio Valenti. It preserves the Assumption of Antonino Spatafora called the Panormita, dated 1579, and four paintings of the Lombard school of the seventeenth century with Santa Maddalena, Santa Rosalia, Sant'Agata and Santa Caterina.

Why go. The Panormite Assumption in the main church.
