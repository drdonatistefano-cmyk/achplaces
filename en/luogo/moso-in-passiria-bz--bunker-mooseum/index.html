Bunker Mooseum

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Museum opened in 2009 in a former bunker of the Second World War: it tells the history and nature of Val Passiria with the visitor center of the Gruppo di Tessa Nature Park, sections on the high Passiria, the mine of Monteneve and on geology and local nature, and the life of Moso and its inhabitants during the war. Outside a fence of the ibex and a climbing wall; guided tours and workshops on request.

Why go. Valley, park and war in a bunker.
