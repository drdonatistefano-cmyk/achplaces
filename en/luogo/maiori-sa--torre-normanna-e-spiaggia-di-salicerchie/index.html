Torre Normanna e spiaggia di Salicerchie

History and architecture · Towers · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Saracen Tower, an ancient defensive spot, connected to beaches and coves reachable also by sea; beyond the tower you go down with 160 steps to Salicerchie beach. In the hamlet of Erchie stands another tower, the Cerniola; later on the beach of the Dead Horse is not accessible due to the risk of landslides and you can only admire from above.

Why go. Sighting towers and coves outside the center.
