Museo del Mare

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 2006 by the park with Italia Nostra in Caprera, it follows in chronological order, from prehistory to today, the history of the sea and the routes of the archipelago with photos, documents, models, remnants of boats, navigation and fishing instruments, a copy of the Peutingerian Tabula and a Granite Mother Goddess.

Why go. The marine history of the archipelago.
