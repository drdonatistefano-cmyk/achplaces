Museo dell'identità dell'alta Garfagnana «Olimpio Cammelli»

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated on May 16, 2009 on the Colle di Casa di Gorfigliano, where the castle stood, it was born from the collection of over 1300 pieces donated by Emma Orsi, widow of Olimpio Cammelli; today it exhibits more than 3000 objects. The route reconstructs Gorfigliano before and after 1900, when the extraction of marble began, tells the volunteers who have recovered the village and the crafts of quarry, blacksmith, farmer, shepherd, shoemaker and woodmaker; on the upper floor the world of carpenter and a library of over 500 volumes. Free guided tours by reservation for schools, groups and tourists.

Why go. The life of caves and mountain crafts.
