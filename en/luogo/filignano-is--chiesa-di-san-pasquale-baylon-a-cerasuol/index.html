Chiesa di San Pasquale Baylon a Cerasuolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church wanted by Duke Pasquale Marotta, the last feudal man of Cerasuolo (dead in 1884), started at the end of the nineteenth century and completed at the beginning of the twentieth century. Destroyed during the Second World War, it was rebuilt thanks to the abbot of Montecassino Gregorio Diamare; damaged by the earthquake of 1984, it had cover and facade restored in 2005. It has three aisles and a stone facade, raised on the road and reached by an external staircase, marked by parasts with doric capitals.

Why go. The church of the village on the Via Francigena del Sud.
