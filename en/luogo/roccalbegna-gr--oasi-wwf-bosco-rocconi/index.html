Oasi WWF Bosco Rocconi

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Reserve overlooking the river Albegna, important for birdlife: it hosts rare birds of prey such as biancon, lodolaio, sparrow and lanario.

Why go. The rare birds of prey of the Albegna gorges.
