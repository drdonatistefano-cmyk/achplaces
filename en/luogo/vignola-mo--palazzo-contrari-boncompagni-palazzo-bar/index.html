Palazzo Contrari-Boncompagni (Palazzo Barozzi) e la scala a chiocciola

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Renaissance Palace of the XVI-XVII centuries in front of the Fortress, perhaps on a project by Jacopo Barozzi called the Vignola. Celebrate the spiral staircase with oval plan, self-supporting, 106 steps, attributed to Barozzi and built by Bartolomeo Tristano.

Why go. The Vignola ladder, suspended in the void.
