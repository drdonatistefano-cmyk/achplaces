MARCA - Museo delle Arti

Museums · Modern art

In an ancient building of the center on three levels: Pinacoteca and provincial gipsoteca with about 120 works from the sixteenth to the twentieth century, the permanent collection of the Rotella Foundation dedicated to the Catanzarese Mimmo Rotella and a center for contemporary culture.

Why go. Mimmo Rotella and the art gallery.
