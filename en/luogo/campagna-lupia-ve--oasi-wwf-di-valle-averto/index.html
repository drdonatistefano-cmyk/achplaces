Oasi WWF di Valle Averto

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

State nature reserve of about 200 hectares in the lagoon of Venice, managed by the WWF and wetland of international importance (Ramsar) since 1989: brackish water basins, reeds, humid meadows, riparian woods and canals, with about 400 plant species and two European priority habitats (falasco limestone bogs and black alder and ash woods). There are about 250 species of birds passing through; they nest swamp falcon, avocetta, common stern, tarabusino, nittochora and have it small, and there is a good population of marsh turtles. You can visit only with a guide and by reservation: 3.5 km, about two hours. It can be reached from Romea (SS 309) at km 112,800, at Lugo.

Why go. Two hours on foot between reeds and herons in the south lagoon.
