Antico complesso parrocchiale – Museo civico alpino «Arnaldo Tazzetti»

Museums · Themed

In the complex of the medieval church with ancient cemetery, chapel of 1547 and Romanesque bell tower, the museum preserves pre-Romanesque and Romanesque finds and collections of local flora, fauna and minerals, with temporary exhibitions.

Why go. The museum of the alpine world.
