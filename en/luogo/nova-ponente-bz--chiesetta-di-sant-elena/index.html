Chiesetta di Sant'Elena

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church probably of the twelfth century, entirely frescoed around 1400 by masters of the school of Bolzano (reconsecrated in 1410), with paintings similar to those of the parish church of Terlano and of Saint Catherine in Aica di Fiè, perhaps commissioned by the nobles Niederthor, whose coat of arms re-emerged in 1885. Outside St. Helena, St. Christopher and the Crucifixion; inside the evangelists in the vault, the Christ judge in the apse, Cain and Abel and Creation on the triumphal arch, the stories of Jesus' childhood on the walls; altarpiece signed J. G. Plazer 1723. About 30 minutes walk from the Pfösl stop; the key is asked at the farm in front of the church.

Why go. A Gothic jewel of frescoes to see with the key of the farm.
