Raccolta storica nel Loggiato Mediceo

Museums · Archaeological

Housed in the Medici Loggia (1472-75), which dominates Piazza Garibaldi, ancient market square, and still bears the Medici coat of arms on a column, documents the history of Altopascio. It exhibits mainly Renaissance ceramics and stone fragments of medieval buildings from the excavations of the eighties and nineties, from the large fifteenth-century orcs for wheat to daily dishes; born in the municipal library, it is an exhibition ordered since 1990 and documentation center since 1991.

Why go. Excavations from the ancient hospital under a 15th century loggia.
