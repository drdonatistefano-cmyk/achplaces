Coulege dei Barba

Museums · City history

In Pradeltorno, small ancient buildings in dry stone, probably school of biblical preparation of the itinerant medieval Waldensian preachers called "barba" , visits by reservation with the museum system of the Valdese Foundation.

Why go. The secret school of preachers.
