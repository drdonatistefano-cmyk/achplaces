Torre di Castelvecchio - Museo Corrado Bocchi

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Born in the 13th century as the gate of Castelvecchio, the first nucleus of the city, it became a tower in 1438 under the Gonzaga with the first clock and was then raised to the present 27 meters: it is the tallest building of Castel Goffredo. Seven floors and 117 steps, with a museum path on walls, doors, moat and vellum of the two defensive belts; a spiral staircase crosses the bell cell to the Belvedere, overlooking the Po Valley, the Prealps and, on clear days, the Apennines. Open from Thursday to Sunday, last ascent at 16:45; hosts the Infopoint.

Why go. The ascent to the viewpoint above the Renaissance square.
