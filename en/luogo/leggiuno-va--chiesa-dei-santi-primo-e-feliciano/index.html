Chiesa dei Santi Primo e Feliciano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church in the center of the village, with Romanesque bell tower, founded before the ninth century by the family of the noble Eremberto, linked to the Emperor Lotario.

Why go. A high medieval church with Romanesque bell tower in the heart of the village.
