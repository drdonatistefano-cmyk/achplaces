Cascate Campanaro

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Paid entry

Waterfall of the river Campanaro about 20 meters high, in a forest of the Sila National Park along the road between Sersale and Zagarise. From the road edge, with few parking spaces, a marked path of a few hundred meters descends for about 70 meters of difference in altitude, with a small parking area; it is not accessible to the disabled and we recommend trekking shoes.

Why go. A waterfall in the woods just minutes from the road.

  • Where Strada Statale 109, tra Sersale e Zagarise
  • Entry €3 in certain periods of the year
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
