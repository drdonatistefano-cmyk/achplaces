Museo «Manduria, Terra di Messapi» nell'ex Convento degli Agostiniani

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in September 2017 in the former Convent of the Augustinians, under the protection of the National Superintendence for the Underwater Cultural Heritage, it holds many finds emerged from the excavations of the Messapian necropolises. According to the MiC card the visit of the cloister is on reservation.

Why go. The messapic supplies of the necropolis of Manduria.
