Museo civico Goffredo Bellini

Museums · City history

Founded in the early twenties of the twentieth century by the collector Goffredo Bellini in the Palazzo Monte dei Pegni. Archaeology from the Bronze Age to the Roman Age, with Celtic furnishings of the necropolis of Carzaghetto, Egyptian stele of the Middle Kingdom and Attic Kylix; incunabula and aldine, the Atlas Veneto of Coronelli (1690), sections on popular traditions, Risorgimento and Great War, painters of the nineteenth century and the life-size copy of the Assedio di Asola is attributed to Tintoretto (victory of 1516). Section of about 600 minerals visible on request.

Why go. The whole Asola thing in one building.
