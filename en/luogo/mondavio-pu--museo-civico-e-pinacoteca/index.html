Museo civico e Pinacoteca

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the cloister of the former convent of San Francesco: paintings of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, paintings of the Barocci school and one attributed to Ridolfi from the convent of the Capuchins, ancient books from the fifteenth to the nineteenth century, ceramics, wooden tabernacles including one of 1620 and a ciborium of Friar Liberale da Macerata, and the table of Olivuccio di Ciccarello da Camerino (1390-1400 circa).

Why go. Holy art and ancient books in the cloister.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Mondavio
  • Entry €4, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
