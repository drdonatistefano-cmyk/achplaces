Chiesa parrocchiale di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The fifteenth-century church in the old village, the first stage of the itinerary of the historic centre; it preserves a seventeenth-century wooden altar by Giovanni Battista Polana di Roncone, purchased by the parish in the second half of the eighteenth century.

Why go. The heart of the old country and its wooden altar.
