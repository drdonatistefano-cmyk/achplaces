Teatro Faraggiana

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built in 1905 for the Marquis Raffaello Faraggiana on a project by Giuseppe Oliverio, donated to the city in 1911, partly destroyed by a fire in 1940 and reopened in 2016.

Why go. The Marquis' theater.
