Chiesa e museo d'arte sacra di San Giacomo

Museums · Old masters

The church of San Giacomo, rebuilt in the sixteenth century, houses with the sacristy and the House of the Regency a small museum of sacred art: paintings of Moncalvo and his daughter Orsola Caccia, goldsmiths and antique fabrics and the work "I Canonici di Lu" by Pietro Francesco Guala.

Why go. The Moncalvo and Orsola Hunts in a museum collected.
