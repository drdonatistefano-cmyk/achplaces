Tomba di Ciro Menotti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Renaissance style chapel covered with polychrome marbles, with the coats of arms of Carpi, Modena and Fiorano, in the church of Spezzano, where in 1929 the remains of the Risorgimento patriot were transferred condemned to hanging in 1831.

Why go. The patriot of 1831.
