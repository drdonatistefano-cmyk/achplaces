Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giorgio Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of Schianno, is among the four churches of the municipality indicated as worthy of particular attention. It contains frescoes by Isidoro Bianchi da Campione and a fine 15th century milk Madonna.

Why go. The Madonna of the fifteenth-century milk.
