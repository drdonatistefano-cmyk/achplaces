Antico monastero di Santa Cristina

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The village appears as "Sce Cristine" (Sce Cristine), stage XL of the itinerary of Sigerico (X century), the oldest travel report on the Via Francigena; its spiritual and logistical importance derives from the monastery, founded in the Lombard period, where Corradino of Swabia also stayed. The medieval system and the 17th century renovations remain recognizable; inside a Gothic bifora there are four columns with capitals of the 8th century coming from the royal palace of Corteolona.

Why go. A stop of Sigerico with Longobard capitals.
