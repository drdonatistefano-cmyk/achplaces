Grotte di Frasassi

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry

Karst complex discovered in 1971, among the largest underground routes; the largest cavity seems to be able to contain the Duomo of Milan. The guided tour (Italian and English) lasts about an hour and touches the Sala Ancona and the Sala delle Candle; there are also speleological routes for about 2 and 3 hours.

Why go. A rock cathedral under the Apennines.
