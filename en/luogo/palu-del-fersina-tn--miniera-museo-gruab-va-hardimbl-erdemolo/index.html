Miniera-museo Gruab va Hardimbl (Erdemolo)

Museums · Themed

Ancient copper mine along the path to Lake Erdèmolo, exploited by the sixteenth century for four centuries: at the beginning of the sixteenth century German miners (Bergknappen) dug a hundred galleries. The underground path reconstructs the original environment with thematic settings; guided tour only, groups of up to 12 people, helmet and cape provided, temperature about 8 °C. Open 10-18 on the weekends of June and September, from Tuesday to Sunday to July and August, on Sunday in October; inside you do not pay by card.

Why go. Enter the galleries of the miners of the sixteenth century.
