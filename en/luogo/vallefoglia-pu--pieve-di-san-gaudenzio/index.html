Pieve di San Gaudenzio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of perhaps the VII-VIII century dedicated to the first bishop of Rimini, with crypt of the XII century that preserves the relics of Santa Marcellina, arrived from Rome in 1666, and fifteenth-century bell tower of 25 meters. Inside works in white and black scagliola of the late seventeenth century, including the altar post of 1687 with St. Francis of Paola, a marble balustrade of the seventeenth century and the baptismal font obtained from a Roman cyp.

Why go. The Romanesque crypt and the seventeenth-century scagliole.
