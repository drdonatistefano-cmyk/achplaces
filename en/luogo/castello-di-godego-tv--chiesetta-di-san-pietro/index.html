Chiesetta di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Sacello of early Christian origin (IV century A.D.), ancient multi-pageng parish church on the eastern bank of the Muson, at the edge of the Prai. It contains a fragment of white stone sarcophagus cover with head of Medusa, of the second century AD, found in 1975 and two fragments of bas-reliefs with Longobard crosses (VII century) inserted in the walls of the apse and the facade; in the surroundings were found Longobard burials.

Why go. A early Christian church with longobard stones.
