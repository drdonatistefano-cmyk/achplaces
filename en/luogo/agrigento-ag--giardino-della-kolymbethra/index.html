Giardino della Kolymbethra

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

In the Valley of the Temples at the Temple of the Dioscuri: about 2,500 years ago the tyrant Terone had it dug tunnels and hypogeans for water, creating a large tank; it has been entrusted to the FAI since 1999.

Why go. A green oasis among the temples.
