Duomo di Sant'Andrea Apostolo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Romanesque-Gothic T-shaped building with three priests and two towers, consecrated in 1338 by the patriarch of Aquileia Bertrando. It preserves an organ of 1792 by G. Callido and is considered an exemplary monument of post-earthquake restoration.

Why go. The symbol of Friuli's reconstruction.
