Colle di Santo Stefano e ossario

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

After the Great War, the church on the hill welcomed the fallen of 1866 and the First World War, becoming a monument to the Ossuary; around the historical park with Italian walks and trenches, which can be walked only on foot, and a path to the Cross. From the parking lot about 138 meters of dirt; ossuary open from May 1 to September 30, 10-13 and 15-18, closed on Monday. Every July 21st commemoration of the battle of 1866.

Why go. Memory of the Risorgimento and the Great War.
