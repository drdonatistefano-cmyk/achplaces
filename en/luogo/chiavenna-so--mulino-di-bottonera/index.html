Mulino di Bottonera

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1867 in the artisan district of Bottonera, next to the bakery of Carlo Moro: on three floors, with pulleys, belts and millstones, he worked day and night until the sixties; today the museum of the cereal cycle, from cleaning to flour.

Why go. Industrial archaeology almost intact.
