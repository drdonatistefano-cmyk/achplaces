Rifugi antiaerei

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

World War II shelters: one, from 1941, could accommodate from 61 to 82 people; the one in Via Roma, from 1943-44, had a capacity of 344 people and a fireplace of 17 meters. The tour, guided only and with sound effects, is held in Italian, English, French and German for groups up to 25 people, by reservation at Discoverminiera; the refuges of Via Roma are fully accessible by wheelchair.

Why go. Get off at the shelters built for the workers during the bombings.
