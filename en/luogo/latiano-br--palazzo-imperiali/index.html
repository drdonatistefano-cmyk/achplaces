Palazzo Imperiali

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The 12th-century Baronial Palace of the Imperial family: it houses the family square, part of the Marseille collection, the Museum of Photography and Messapian collections; the most valuable room is the Sala Flore on the first floor.

Why go. Different collections in a baronial palace.
