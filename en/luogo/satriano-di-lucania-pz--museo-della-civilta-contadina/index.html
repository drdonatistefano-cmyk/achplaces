Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Set up in 1997 in two rooms of the eighteenth-century Palazzo Loreti, seat of the town hall: one reconstructs the peasant house with kitchen, room and shelter of the animals, the other exposes the agricultural tools donated by the elderly, each with its dialect name.

Why go. The peasant tools with the name in dialect.
