Teatro del Paesaggio

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Exhibitive route in the castle, led by Antonio Adriano, which reconstructs the long-lasting history of the landscape of Langhe and Roero through documents and material and intangible knowledge, putting the peasants at the centre as protagonists.

Why go. How farmers built the landscape of the Langhe.

  • Where Castello, Via Alfieri 4, Magliano Alfieri
  • Entry €5, both museums €7, families €10
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.castellodimaglianoalfieri.com
