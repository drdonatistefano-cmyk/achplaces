MUNDI Museo Nazionale dell'Italiano

Museums · Themed

The museum of the Italian language in the former monastery of the Holy Conception, in the complex of Santa Maria Novella, promoted with Crusca, Lincei, Treccani and Società Dante Alighieri; mandatory booking.

Why go. Dante's language told.
