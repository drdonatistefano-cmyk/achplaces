Parrocchiale di San Giovanni del Groppo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

15th century church that houses a polyptych by Agostino Casanova from 1547. St. John the Baptist is the patron saint of the municipality, celebrated on 24 June.

Why go. The second polyptych of Casanova in the municipality.
