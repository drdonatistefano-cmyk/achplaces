Riserva naturale orientata Capo Rama

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Reserve of about 57 hectares established in 2000 and managed by WWF Italy: limestone rocks rich in fossils of about 200 million years ago, with three geosites (one of national importance), a cliff of about 35 meters with alofil vegetation and a plateau covered with dwarf palm, the only spontaneous palm of Europe. On the promontory the watchtower, one of the first built on the Sicilian coast, which gives its name to the head and signaled the pirates with fire and smoke; it is the only circular tower of the coast. Suitable for mountain bikes and sunsets.

Why go. Fossil, dwarf palms and the tower on the head at sunset.
