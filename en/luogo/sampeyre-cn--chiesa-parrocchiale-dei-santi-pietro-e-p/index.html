Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the 15th century on a Romanesque building and renovated in the 19th century, it has only one nave with two side hats. The main portal in green stone, with vegetable frieze, mozzarella heads and a two-faced gianus, is among the best examples of the Romanesque Saluzzo; a lateral portal bears the date 1462. On the right wall the cycle of the brothers Tommaso and Matteo Biazaci (1460-1470) and the octagonal baptismal font in white Zabreri marble, dated 1482.

Why go. A Romanesque portal and fifteenth-century frescoes in the square.
