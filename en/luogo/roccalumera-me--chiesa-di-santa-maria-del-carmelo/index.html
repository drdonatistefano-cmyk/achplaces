Chiesa di Santa Maria del Carmelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1938 in the Botteghelle district, behind a small church documented in 1725 and now disappeared, where Salvatore Quasimodo was baptized by Francesco Maria di Francia; he preserves an ancient statue of the Madonna del Carmelo, a Saint Joseph and a canvas of the Madonna with the souls of Purgatory. On July 16th the procession, and the following Sunday the statue goes by boat on the sea of Roccalumera and Nice.

Why go. On the trail of Quasimodo's baptism.
