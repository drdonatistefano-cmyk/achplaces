Reggia di Persano

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Bourbon hunting pavilion in the hamlet of Persano, today inside a vast military area: baroque palace with porch courtyard and four octagonal turrets with spiral staircases, and in the atrium a large staircase of honor with the marble statue of a guard dog attributed to Canova. In the eighteenth-century estate was selected the breed of horses of Persano, from which it is said to derive the palace of Ferrari.

Why go. The hunting palace of the Bourbons and the horses of Persano.

Inside a military area: visit only on the occasion of events organized by the Italian Army
