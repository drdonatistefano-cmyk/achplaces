Museo demologico dell'economia, del lavoro e della storia sociale silana

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 1984 on the ground floor and first floor of the east wing of the Florense Abbey, it has a thematic itinerary on the folk traditions of Sila: agricultural and pastoral tools, artisan crafts, domestic furnishings, religious and musical culture, iconography, with panels in Italian and dialect. It contains photographic, audiovisual and documentary archives, including the Saverio Marra Photographic Fund (1894-1978). According to the MiC card (updated 2024) open 9-19 from mid-September to mid-June and 10-20 in summer.

Why go. The work and life of the Sila of the past.
