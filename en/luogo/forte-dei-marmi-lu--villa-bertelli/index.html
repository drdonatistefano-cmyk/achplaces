Villa Bertelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Nineteenth-century residence in the district of Vittoria Apuana, with a theatre where evening concerts and spaces for exhibitions and events are held. The rooms tell the story of the landscape and art in Forte dei Marmi, with works by the sculptor Arturo Dazzi (1881-1966) and the masters of the twentieth century related to Versilia, including Carrà, Soffici, Rosai, de Chirico, Campigli and Guttuso, and a drawing by Henry Moore.

Why go. The artistic twentieth century of Versilia in a historic villa.
