Sant'Apollinare ed eremo di San Paolo, Prabi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Prabi the church of Sant'Apollinare and the hermitage of São Paulo both preserve cycles of medieval frescoes.

Why go. Medieval frescoes under the cliffs.
