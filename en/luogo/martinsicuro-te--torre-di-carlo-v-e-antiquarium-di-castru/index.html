Torre di Carlo V e Antiquarium di Castrum Truentinum

History and architecture · Towers

A sighting tower wanted by Carlo V in the middle of the sixteenth century against the Saracen raids, one of the Spanish coastal towers between Trigno and Tronto; around it grew the first inhabited. It houses the Antiquarium of Castrum Truentinum, an archaeological museum with exhibits from prehistory to the Lombard age excavated in the area of Colle Marzio.

Why go. The antique tower and the archaeology of the mouth of the Tronto.
