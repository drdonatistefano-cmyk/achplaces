Santuario della Madonna della Fiducia

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

It rises in Isola Pescaroli, a hamlet of San Daniele Po, along the margins of the Po; the church was designed by Rastelli with the engineer Filippini. It was born from the devotion of Don Martino Aletti for Our Lady of Trust: in 1951 a disastrous flood struck the neighbouring countries saving Isola Pescaroli, and the sanctuary, obtained from the remains of the previous church of San Biagio, was inaugurated in 1952. Archbishop Giovanni Cazzani proclaimed Our Lady Queen of the Po and Pius XII blessed her crowns on May 8, 1952. Each year around May 8th the festival includes mass, procession and the blessing of the waters by boat on the river. Without architectural barriers.

Why go. The sanctuary of the Queen of the Po, linked to the memory of the flood of the river.
