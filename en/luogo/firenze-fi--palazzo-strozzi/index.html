Palazzo Strozzi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

The Renaissance palace of the Strozzi, now home to the great international exhibitions. The courtyard and the loggia are free entrance.

Why go. World-class exhibitions, open until 11pm on Thursday.

  • Where Piazza Strozzi, Firenze
  • Entry Free courtyard; exhibitions for a fee, price according to the exhibition (e.g. Rothko 2026 €21)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.palazzostrozzi.org
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
