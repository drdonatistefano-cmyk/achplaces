Gravina e villaggio rupestre della Madonna della Scala

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Gravine almost 4 km long, about 40 m deep, with about 600 plant species, many rare and medicinal. It houses a rock village of neolithic origin, inhabited until the twelfth century, with more than 200 houses dug into the limestone: among these the Cave of the Cyclops, with blades of flint and fragments of prehistoric ceramics, and the "Farmacia of the Magic Greguro" , twelve communicating environments where according to legend the magician and his daughter Margheritella preserved medicinal herbs. Guided tours of about 3 hours, from Tuesday to Sunday, with reservation required two days before and minimum 10 participants; variable cost; do not recommend to people with motor disabilities.

Why go. A karst canyon inhabited from prehistory to the Middle Ages.
