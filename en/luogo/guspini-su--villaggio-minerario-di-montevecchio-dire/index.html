Villaggio minerario di Montevecchio: Direzione, Officine e Piccalinna

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Viewpoint

It was granted by Carlo Alberto to Giovanni Antonio Sanna in 1848, in 1865 with 1,100 workers it was the most important mine in the Kingdom of Italy; it closed in 1991 and is part of the Park Geominerario of Sardinia. In addition to the well Sant'Antonio you can visit the Palazzina of the direction (1870-1877), with the restored rooms of the first floor including the blue room, the collection of Castoldi director and the chapel of Santa Barbara; the workshops with the foundry of 1885 and the room of the wooden models; the yard of Piccalinna with the machine of extraction from 120 horses of the late nineteenth century, still working, and the villas of the chiefs and the working houses.

Why go. An entire mining village 4 km from the dunes of Piscinas.
