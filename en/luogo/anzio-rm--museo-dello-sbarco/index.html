Museo dello sbarco

Museums · Themed

Opened on 22 January 1994 for the fiftieth anniversary, in a hall of Villa Adele, it recalls Operation Shingle of 22 January 1944, when the Allies landed between Tor San Lorenzo and Torre Astura. Four sections (English, American, German and Italian) with medals, uniforms, flags, weapons, battle plans, motor vehicles and vintage photos, more photo library, tape library, library and hemeroteca; some finds come from the backdrops of Anzio, the wrecks of British ships Janus and Spartan and the hospital ship St. David. Closed on Monday, Wednesday only morning.

Why go. The memory of the 1944 landing.
