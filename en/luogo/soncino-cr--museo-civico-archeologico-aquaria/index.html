Museo civico archeologico Aquaria

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the Rocca's rooms, it enhances the archaeological heritage of the north-east of Cremona with the findings of about thirty years of research by the Archaeological Group and the discoveries of the Superintendence, in a path from prehistory to the seventeenth century. Closed on Monday, open Tuesday-Friday in the morning and on weekends also in the afternoon.

Why go. The history of the territory inside the Fortress.

  • Where Via Ferrari Enea 1, Soncino
  • Entry Cumulation €7, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
