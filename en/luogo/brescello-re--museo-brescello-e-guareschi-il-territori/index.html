Museo Brescello e Guareschi, il territorio e il cinema

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Opened on 21 June 2009 and managed by the Fondazione Paese di Don Camillo and Peppone, it tells the story of the peasant life of the 1950s and the flood of 1951, with the reconstruction of a set and an exhibition on film making.

Why go. The world of Guareschi and behind the scenes of the films.

  • Where Via Felice Cavallotti 24, Brescello
  • Entry €6, reduced €4 (one ticket three museums)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com
