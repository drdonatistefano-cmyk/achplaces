Museo del Sale

Museums · Themed

In the district of Nubia, in a seventeenth-century lamp where salt was worked (the Casa Salaria), it tells centuries of salt culture of western Sicily; next to it there is a large windmill that raised sea water and ground the raw salt. It exhibits the original tools of the salt-salters, a vine of Archimedes, sacks of jute, millstones, mill blades and photographs in black and white; it was born on the initiative of Alberto Culcasi with the provincial body for tourism, the Municipality and the faculties of Letters and Architecture of the University of Palermo, and is part of the project Via del Sale.
