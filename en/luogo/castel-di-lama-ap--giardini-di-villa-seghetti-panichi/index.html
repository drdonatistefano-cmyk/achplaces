Giardini di Villa Seghetti Panichi

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

The villa was built in the eighteenth century by Odoardo Odoardi on the remains of a medieval fortification; the garden was commissioned by Vincenzo Carfratelli Seghetti and built between 1875 and 1890 by the German landscaper Ludwig Winter. On the ridge that dominates the Tronto and the Salaria grow a rare Jubaea spectabilis, palms of the Canary Islands and dates, monumental Washingtonia, red beech and oaks; in the pond, on an island, an eighteenth-century statue in travertine of Venus and Cupid, and there is also the "romitory" typical of the gardens of Winter.

Why go. A historic garden of palm trees overlooking the Tronto valley.
