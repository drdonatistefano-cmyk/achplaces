Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore a Dierico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Bell tower with cusp of 1577, 16th century frescoes with stories of the Old and New Testament by Giulio Urbanis, pupil of Pomponio Amalteo, and in the apse a wooden altar painted and gilded by Antonio Tirone of 1522.

Why go. Frescoes and wooden altar of the sixteenth century.
