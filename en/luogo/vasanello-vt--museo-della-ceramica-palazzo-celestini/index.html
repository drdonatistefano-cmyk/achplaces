Museo della Ceramica (Palazzo Celestini)

Museums · Themed

In the Palazzo Celestini, in the heart of the historic center, tells the local ceramic manufacture favored by clay deposits, from the falisco period to the modern age: excavations from 1984-1985, pieces of the factory "Ceramica Bassanello" (active until 1978) donated by the Misciatelli family and statues of Giulio Francesconi. Vasanello is with Civita Castellana the most famous town of Tuscia for ceramics, and its potters are called "cocciari" (cocciari).

Why go. The history of the cockpits, from the Falisci to the twentieth century.
