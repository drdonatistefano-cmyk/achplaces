Monoliti di Petra Aggìallu e Misorbo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Two routes of the reserve between canyons, gorges and monoliths: Petra Aggiallu (1.6 km, about 2 hours and a half, difficulty E, with the source of the Three Fountains) and Misorbo with the Campanaro stream (1.4 km, about 2 hours, difficulty E).

Why go. Monumental rocks in the canyon.
