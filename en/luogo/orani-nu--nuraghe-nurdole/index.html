Nuraghe Nurdole

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The most imposing complex among the thirty Nuragic sites of the territory: a central tower enclosed by a bastion with four towers, with a sacred source nearby. In the territory also the necropolis at domus de Janas of Sas Fossas and Badde Roma, two dolmens, six menhirs and the tombs of Giants of Istelenneru.

Why go. The nuraghe-fortress of Orani.
