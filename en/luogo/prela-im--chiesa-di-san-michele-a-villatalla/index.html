Chiesa di San Michele a Villatalla

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish built in the 15th century on a previous Romanesque church and renovated in the Baroque period. On the wall opposite the side door, above an architrave dated 1470 with the Agnus Dei, opens a Gothic window with sharp arch; inside the polyptych of Agostino Casanova painted around 1540.

Why go. A Renaissance polyptych in a hamlet church.
