Castello dei Principi Capano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

In 1290 he passed to Guido d'Alment, who arrived in Italy with Carlo I d'Angiò, then to the Capanos; the present aspect dates back to the restoration of 1610 wanted by Vincenzo Capano, XV prince of Pollica, and since 1997 he is of the Municipality. It has a massive three-storey square tower and preserves intact ugliaro, oil mill, guard posts and stables; the tradition wants St Alfonso de' Liguori to stay there. It hosts the Centre for Studies on the Mediterranean Diet "Angelo Vassallo" opened in 2010.

Why go. The castle that dominates the village.
