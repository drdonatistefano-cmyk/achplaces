Chiesa di Santa Maria ad Nives

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

In Chapel, about 5 km from the center: probably a Benedictine cenobium, with abbey title under the Chapter of Santa Sofia of Benevento.

Why go. An ancient abbey in the country.
