Museo Utensilia

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Museum of the middle-class culture in the basement of the medieval castle: in eight rooms a reasoned collection of traditional work tools, such as ploughs, frames, wagons and presses, that peasant families built in winter turning to the blacksmith only for metal parts.

Why go. The Marche peasant world in eight halls.
