Chiesa di San Lorenzo e Oratorio di Sant'Agostino

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

At the bridge that connected the castle to the inhabited, the new parish church of San Lorenzo was started in 1440 and ended in 1445, as the epigraph of the portal says, by the master Francesco Garone, with local masters similar to those of San Lorenzo di Genova; in 1676-1681 the Baroque reconstruction incorporated the Gothic columns into the pillars, but remain the portal, the rosette in terracotta, the gate On a column re-emerged in the restorations is painted Santa Caterina d'Alessandria with date 1459. The nearby Oratorio di Sant'Agostino was born from a fourteenth-century fortification, adapted to oratory at least from 1573, and preserves a late-fourth-century cycle of the Passion on all walls, now in rediscovery.

Why go. The Ligurian Gothic in Val Bormida, with frescoes from the fifteenth century.
