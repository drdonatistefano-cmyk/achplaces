Chiesa dei Cappuccini (San Germano)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient parish church mentioned as early as 702, the current building was begun at the end of the XII century and completed in the façade at the beginning of the fourteenth century, and documents the passage from the Romanesque to the Gothic: façade in stone with pale and dark bands and brick with frieze of interwoven arches, crochet capitals, triumphal arch with acute sixth. The cappuccinos settled there in 1623; the restoration of 1971-1996 brought back to light truss and semicircular apse. Inside is a statue of the Redeemer Christ of the 13th century and a fragment of the Annunciation of the Boxilius brothers (about 1484).

Why go. The Romanesque-Gothic parish at the end of the greenway.
