Museo delle macchine da caffè Cagliari

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Over 100 espresso coffee machines, from the first column and steam machines of 1901-1940 to the post-war lever models and the first automatic machines of the 1960s and 1970s.

Why go. The history of Italian espresso.
