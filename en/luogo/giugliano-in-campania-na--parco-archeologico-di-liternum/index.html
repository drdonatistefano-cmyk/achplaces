Parco archeologico di Liternum

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Roman maritime colony founded in 194 BC on the left bank of Lake Patria and assigned to the veterans of the second Punic war; flourished in Augustian age and between the end I and II century A.D., then decayed with the impaludation of the area. The hole with Capitolium, basilica and theatre was discovered in 1932; in the urban park of over 67,000 m2, recently redeveloped, you can see a spa complex with a bath still fed by natural springs, vaulted substruttuzioni, a wall in cross-linked work and over 80 meters of basolato of the Via Domitiana. Part of the artifacts is exhibited at the Museo Archeologico dei Campi Flegrei in Baia.

Why go. The forum of a Roman colony of 194 BC on the shore of Lake Patria.
