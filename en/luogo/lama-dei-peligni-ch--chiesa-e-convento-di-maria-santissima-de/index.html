Chiesa e convento di Maria Santissima della Misericordia

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

In the upper part of the village, also known as the convent of Sant'Antonio: founded in 1327 by Roberto da Salle, follower of Celestino V, and remodelled between 1530 and 1600. It contains manuscripts of Saint Alphonsus Maria de' Liguori, a letter from Saint Joseph Calasanzio, an Abruzzo copper cross of the fourteenth century and a thirteenth-century statue of Our Lady of Mercy.

Why go. A 14th century Celestinian monastery.
