Laboratorio Rinascita

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Restoration center on the large square where the works saved from churches and collapsed buildings are recovered: here the organ of the church of Suffragio and several large canvases now at the museum Floriana Switzerland; the restorer Stefania Zucconi welcomes visitors.

Why go. See the restoration of the wounded heritage closely.
