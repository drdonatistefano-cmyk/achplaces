Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giovanni Battista e Museo dei Tesori

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

Cited in 1057 and almost entirely rebuilt in the sixteenth century, example of a late-Gothic alpine church: monumental protire of 1536 by Matteo Rode of Melezet, façade with the procession of Vizi and Virtues, frescoes from the fourteenth to the sixteenth century, the ancon of the main altar of Jean Faure of Thures (1677) and, in the sacristy, the museum of vestments.

Why go. The richest Alpine Gothic in the valley.

  • Where Piazza Martiri della Libertà, Salbertrand
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
