Homo et Ibex, centro visitatori nell'ex Grand Hotel

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Visitor center of the Park dedicated to the relationship between man and ibex in history and art: multimedia materials, reconstructions including a cave with rock paintings of the first ibex and prehistoric hunting scenes, objects, photographs, paintings, myths and legends. It is located in the historic complex of the Grand Hotel, witness of the elite tourism of the late nineteenth century, partially restored by the Park Authority which has its operational headquarters and a multipurpose hall in the ancient festival hall. Free under 6 years and over 65 years of age; compulsory booking for groups and schools (tel. +39 0124 953321).

Why go. The ibex, symbol of the Park, in a great historic hotel.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
