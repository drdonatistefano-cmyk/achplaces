Museo archeologico di Scansano

Museums · Archaeological

In the Palazzo Pretorio of the fifteenth century, restored in 1998, it tells of the human presence in the Valle dell'Albegna from prehistory to the Etruscans and the Romans. It exhibits the Hellenistic finds of Ice Strong (ceramics, bronze statuettes, objects related to fertility rites) and reconstructions of the Roman villa at the farm Aia Nova, with parts of the floors in cottage.

Why go. The treasures of Strong Ice.
