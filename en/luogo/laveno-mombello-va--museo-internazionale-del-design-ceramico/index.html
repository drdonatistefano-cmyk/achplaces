Museo Internazionale del Design Ceramico (MIDeC)

Museums · Themed

In the sixteenth century Palazzo Perabò, built at the end of the sixteenth century for the Counts Guilizzoni on the lakefront of Cerro and purchased by the Municipality in 1968; the museum opened on May 9, 1971 as Civic Collection of Terraglie. It tells the Lombard ceramic production from the mid-nineteenth century (Laveno hosts ceramists from 1856) to today, with pieces from the Richard Ginori collection, Art Nouveau objects, rare Italian and foreign sanitary items, sculptures and panels by Biancini, Campi, Andlovitz, Melandri and Gariboldi and works by contemporary artists.

Why go. The history of the ceramics of Laveno in a palace on the lake.
