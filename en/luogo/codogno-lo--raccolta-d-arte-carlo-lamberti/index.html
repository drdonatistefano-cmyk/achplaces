Raccolta d'arte Carlo Lamberti

Museums · Modern art

Opened in 1973 after the death of the collector and painter Carlo Lamberti and enriched in 1988 by the collection of Giuseppe Novello, it occupies eight rooms on the first floor of an eighteenth-century palace (with stairs). It has 122 works from the sixteenth century to today: watercolors by Tranquillo Cremona,

Why go. The 19th century Lombard, from Cremona to Belloni.
