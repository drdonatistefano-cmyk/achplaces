Selinunte, Acropoli e Porta Nord

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The only fortified part of the city, seat of political, religious and military functions: there rise the temple C, begun around 560 BC and attributed to Apollo, among the oldest known Doric temples, the temple D dedicated to Athena and the temples A and O. At temple D the North Gate opens, with sections of the Hellenistic walls reinforced after 409 BC under Hermocrates.

Why go. The sacred and fortified heart of the colony.
