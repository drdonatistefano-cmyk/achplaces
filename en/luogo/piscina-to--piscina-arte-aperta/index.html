Piscina Arte Aperta

Museums · Modern art

Museo d'arte contemporanea a venta tra piazzas e vie del paese, started in 1991 by the Piscinese painter Antonio Panino to represent the currents of Piedmontese art at the end of the millennium: about seventy works by artists of different generations and schools, along a city itinerary.

Why go. A walk between seventy works en plein air.
