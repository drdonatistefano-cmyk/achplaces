Museo archeologico di Scolacium

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In a 19th-century rural building on two floors of the Barons Mazza complex, renovated as a museum, exhibits the excavations from prehistoric to Middle Ages: everyday objects, valuable artifacts, statues and monumental portraits. Unique ticket with the park.

Why go. The statues and portraits of the Roman city.

  • Where Via Scylletion 1, Roccelletta, Borgia
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
