Mosaici del Volo, ex Collegio Aeronautico

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

102 linear metres of mosaics dedicated to the history of flight on the walls of the four-portico of the former Aeronautic College, along the Rationalist route of the White Miglio.

Why go. A unique work in the world, little known.
