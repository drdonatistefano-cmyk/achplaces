Museo della pietra

Museums · Themed

Anthropological demoetanological museum opened on June 2, 2004 in the restored medieval castle, in the Clock Tower: without permanent collections, it tells the cultural uses of stone from the Middle Ages to today, with monitors, panels, audio and showcases on geology from the plain to the mountain and a section on the processing; the castle itself, all in stone, is the main object exposed. MiC timetables: Monday and Sunday 10-17, Friday and Saturday closed; booking not required.

Why go. The culture of the stone of Ausonia.
