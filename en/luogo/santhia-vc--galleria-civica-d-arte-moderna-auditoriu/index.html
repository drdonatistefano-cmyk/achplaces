Galleria civica d'arte moderna – Auditorium San Francesco

Museums · Modern art

In the former church of San Francesco, part of the ancient hospital, which since the eighties hosted the National Exhibition of Contemporary Painting "Santhià" promoted by the Pro Loco founded in 1962: the paintings awarded in various editions form the Civic Gallery of Modern Art City of Santhià, with over 350 works exhibited on the walls of the Auditorium. Rotating art exhibitions are also held at the Jacopo Durandi Cultural Centre.

Why go. The collection was born from half a century of painting awards.
