Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta di Cerqueto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church before 1163, with a bell of 1394. On the right wall of the presbytery the San Sebastiano of 1478, indicated as the oldest authentic work of Perugino, coming from the chapel of Mary Magdalene destroyed in 1779; there is also a Crucifixion of Tiberius of Assisi.

Why go. The oldest authentic work of Perugino.
