Zona archeologica di Poliporto

History and architecture · Archaeology

In San Nicola, north of the town, graves with incineration attributed to the Siculi (the caves that are dug in the rock) and the remains of a Greek-Roman settlement, perhaps a commercial landing place, with warehouses, millstone quarries and a large dolium; part of the structures is submerged and resurfaces with the winter marshes.

Why go. Sicular necropolis and ancient port with water.
