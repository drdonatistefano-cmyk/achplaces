Cappella di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

An ancient church porched on the road to Niella Belbo, once a stop for travellers and lazzaretto during the epidemics. The cycle of frescoes shows the rare "Cavalcata dei Vizi" (Ride of the Vizi), with the seven deadly sins in vintage figures on horseback of beasts, chained and headed to hell; on the back wall of San Rocco and San Sebastiano, on the right the temptations of Sant'Antonio Abate.
