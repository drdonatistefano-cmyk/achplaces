Villa Filiani e Museo Etnomusicologico d'Abruzzo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

An ancient residence of the Filiani family wanted by Giacinto Filiani, in rural style with double central staircase and internal altar frescoed; until the fifties some rooms served to the bachiculture, from which the mulberry trees of the garden. Restored by the Municipality in 2005, it is the seat of the Museo Etnomusicologico d'Abruzzo and hosts exhibitions, conferences and concerts; accessible with monastery. Information to the IAT Office (055 9491745).

Why go. The villa of the founders of Pineto.
