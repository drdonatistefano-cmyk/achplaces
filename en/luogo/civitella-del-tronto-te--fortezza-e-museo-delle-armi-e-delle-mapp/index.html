Fortezza e Museo delle armi e delle mappe antiche

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Among the largest military works in Europe (over 500 m long, about 25,000 sqm), commissioned in the 16th century by Philip II of Spain in place of an Aragonese fortress; inside galleries, gun squares, cisterns, barracks, the church of St. James and the remains of the Governor's Palace. It was the last Bourbon stronghold to surrender on March 20, 1861. The museum, opened in 1988 in the former artillery stores, exhibits weapons from the Renaissance to the battles of 1806 and 1860-61 and historical maps.

Why go. The Bourbon fortress with views from Gran Sasso to the Adriatic.
