Museo della vite e del vino di Ciggiano

Museums · Themed

Opened in May 2009 in the old mill in the center of the country and managed by Pro Loco in agreement with the Municipality, it illustrates the phases of the vinification, from the grape harvest to the fermentation to the bottling, and exhibits traditional agricultural tools.

Why go. The culture of wine in an old country mill.
