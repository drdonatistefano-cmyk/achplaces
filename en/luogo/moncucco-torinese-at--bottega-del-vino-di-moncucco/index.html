Bottega del Vino di Moncucco

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Born in the late 1970s, in a period of crisis in the wine market, and inaugurated in 1981 by local producers and municipalities to promote wines in the area, in particular Freisa; the building preserves original plaster floors that can be visited with the path of the Museum of plaster.

Why go. Taste the Freisa under historic chalk ceilings.
