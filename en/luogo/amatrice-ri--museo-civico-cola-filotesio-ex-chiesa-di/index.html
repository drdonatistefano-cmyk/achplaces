Museo civico «Cola Filotesio» (ex chiesa di Sant'Emidio)

Museums · Old masters

Civic Museum of Religious Art established on September 7, 2002 in the fourteenth-century church of Sant'Emidio and named after Nicola Filotesio, called Cola dell'Amatrice: more than 80 works from the churches of Amatrice and the hamlets, including the Madonna with Child and San Giovannino di Cola (1527), the fifteenth-century processional crosses of Pietro Vannini (from Pinaco and Preta) and a Madonna with Child from the second half of the thirteenth century by Cossito.

Why go. The house of the works of Cola dell'Amatrice, waiting to reopen.

after the earthquake of 2016 the works are exhibited in the temporary museum . Floriana Svizzeretto , temporary solution until the reopening of the Cola Filotesio (VisitLazio, 2023)
