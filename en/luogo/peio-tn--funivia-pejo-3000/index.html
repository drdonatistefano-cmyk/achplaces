Funivia Pejo 3000

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Opened in 2011, it leads to an altitude of 3,000 meters with views over most of the Eastern Alps; from the top a track of almost 8 km goes down to Peio Fonti. Around the Cevedale (3,764 m) and the Vioz (3,644 m).

Why go. The eastern Alps are 3,000.
