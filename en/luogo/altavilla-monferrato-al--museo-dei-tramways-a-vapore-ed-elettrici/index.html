Museo dei tramways a vapore ed elettrici

Museums · Themed

Documents, historical images and objects of the Monferrato trams: two Lanzo's Henschel & Sohn locomotives, a late nineteenth-century freight wagon, an electric accumulator locomotive and an American Davenport locomotive, as well as telegraphy and telephone equipment, signalling lamps, timetables and track and sleeper samples with the original brands.

Why go. Locomotives and heirlooms of steam trams.

the MiC card reports the museum closed every day; check on tel. 0131 225810
