Castello di Santa Severina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Norman fortress attributed to Robert Guiscard, on about 10,000 square meters: square mast with four cylindrical towers and bastions, moat, underground labyrinths, stables with medieval frescoes and Baroque paintings by Francesco Giordano. The restorations 1994-1998 brought to light Greek materials, a church and a Byzantine necropolis.

Why go. One of the best preserved castles in the South.

  • Where Piazza Campo, Santa Severina
