Museo comunale della ceramica

Museums · Themed

Established in 1985 as an exhibition of traditional terracotta, it has been enriched with donations and purchases of majolica and documents the country's long ceramic tradition with collections from prehistory to contemporary age; since 2010 it hosts the entire collection of terracotta whistles donated by the Milanese collector Mario Briosi. It shares the premises with the Municipal Library, which has a section of studies on ceramics. Hours indicated by MiC: Monday-Saturday 8:30-13:30 and 15:00-20:00, Sunday closed.

Why go. Cutrofiano ceramic, from terracotta to whistle.
