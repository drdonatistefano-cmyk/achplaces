Museo Ducati (Borgo Panigale)

Local life and traditions · Motors · Paid entry

Inside the historical factory of 1936: the history Ducati and the bikes of the victories. Upon reservation you can add the factory tour.

Why go. Motor Valley Emilia stop, inside the municipal boundaries.

  • Where Via Antonio Cavalieri Ducati 3, 40132 Bologna
  • Entry €18 whole. Factory tour separately, upon reservation
  • Suggested time 1-2 h
  • Accessibility Required closed shoes for the factory
  • Official website www.ducati.com
