Teatro ellenistico-romano di Pareti

History and architecture · Archaeology

Built in the second half of the second century BC on the attack of the southern walls, restored after the earthquake of 62 and the eruption of 79 and used until the fourth century: scene front with three niches, pulpitum in cross-linked work and orchestra paved in marble of almost 24 meters of diameter, the largest documented example in Campania according to the Municipality.

Why go. The largest ancient theatre in Campania.
