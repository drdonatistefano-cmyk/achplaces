Museo di cultura popolare

Museums · Themed

In a historic building under the arcades, born from the private collections of Anna Grandi Müller: over 3,000 objects tell the life of a bourgeois family from 1815 to 1950, with rebuilt rooms including playroom, kitchen with pantry, living room and bedroom.

Why go. Enter the house of a family of the nineteenth century.
