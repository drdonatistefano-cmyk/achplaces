Prosciutto di Bassiano

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The ham of Bassiano is the king of the cold cuts of Monti Lepini, celebrated by the Festival of Ham on the last Sunday of July; among the typical products also oil, sausages with fennel and truffles.

Why go. The symbolic flavor of the Lepini.
