Padule di Fucecchio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

With 2081 hectares between the provinces of Florence and Pistoia it is the largest inland marsh in Italy, protected since 1996 by the Natural Reserve "Padule di Fucecchio" (Fucecchio Padles), it hosts the most important gauze in central-southern Italy for number of herons, as well as ibis, ducks, raptors and, for some years, pink flamingos. A dense network of paths, which can be walked, by bike and on horseback, crosses the Crater and can flood in part after intense rains; in the municipal territory there are the Capannone, an ancient port with an old customs, and the tobacco dryer of Pratogrande, where tombstones remind the victims of the Nazi massacre of 1944.

Why go. The birdwatching paradise in Tuscany, just a stone's throw from the village.
