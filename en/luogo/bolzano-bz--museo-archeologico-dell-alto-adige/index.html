Museo Archeologico dell'Alto Adige

Museums · Archaeological

Ötzi's house, the ice-clad man who lived over 5000 years ago and was found in 1991 on the Similaun glacier, preserved with clothes and tools, and the history of the region up to the high Middle Ages.
