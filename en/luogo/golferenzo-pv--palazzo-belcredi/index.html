Palazzo Belcredi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Next to the parish church, in the most panoramic point of the village, rises on the foundations of the castle with local stone masonry; next to barn and stable the municipality has created a multifunctional space that also enhances the typical products. Opposite, on the other side of the street, the noble Quarone Palace.

Why go. The residence of the Marquis on the foundations of the castle.
