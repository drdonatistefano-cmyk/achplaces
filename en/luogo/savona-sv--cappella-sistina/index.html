Cappella Sistina

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Next to the cathedral, the chapel wanted by Pope Sixtus IV, not to be confused with the Roman homonym; there is preserved the funeral monument of the pope's parents, with the Madonna enthroned between Sixtus IV and Saints Francis and Antonio da Padua.

Why go. The other Sistine.
