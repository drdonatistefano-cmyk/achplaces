Chiesa prepositurale di San Siro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The first stone was laid in 1582 by the bishop of Cremona Nicolò Sfondati; the current façade is in 1941, the work of the Soresian architect Giulio Carotti. He preserves works by Luigi Miradori called the Genovesino, Giacomo Miradori, Giovanni Battista Trotti called the Malosso, Giovanni Giacomo Pasini and Ermenegildo Lodi, and an organ Serassi-Balbiani (1810-1887); the nineteenth-century bell tower, designed by L. Voghera and completed by C. Visioli, hosts a concert of 12 bells.

Why go. The Genoese and the Malosso in the mother church of the country.
