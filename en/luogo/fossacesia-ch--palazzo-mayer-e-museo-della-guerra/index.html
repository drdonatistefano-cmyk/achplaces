Palazzo Mayer e Museo della guerra

Museums · Themed

The noble palace of Piazza del Popolo houses the fifteenth-century church of Sant'Antonio Abate, an exhibition on peasant life and a war museum.

Why go. Rural life and memories of war in a noble palace.
