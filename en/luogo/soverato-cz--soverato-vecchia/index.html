Soverato Vecchia

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Viewpoint

Ancient fortified village on a hill, mentioned as Suberatum in a text dated 1096, with walls and two towers, the main church, the church of Santa Caterina d'Alessandria and a hermitica laura; destroyed by the earthquake of 1783, remains the ruins of tower-houses, the Baronial Palace and the ancient parish.

Why go. The ruins of the medieval village erased from the earthquake.
