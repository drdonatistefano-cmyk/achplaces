Museo del Balsamico Tradizionale

Museums · Themed

In Villa Fabriani, decorated with frescoes from the early twentieth century and seat of the Consorteria dell'Aceto Balsamico Tradizionale, tells history and production of traditional balsamic vinegar of Modena. The Touring indicates it as a provisional location pending transfer to the Rock.

Why go. The secret of the traditional balsamic.
