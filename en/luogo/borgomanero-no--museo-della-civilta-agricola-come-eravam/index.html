Museo della civiltà agricola «Come eravamo» (La Manera)

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Established in 1992 in the former Palazzo Bono in 1605, it tells with photographs and tools the peasant life and the ancient trades of the fields: reconstructions of kitchen, bedroom and classroom of the late nineteenth century, a small watermill, the 1:40 scale model of Cascina Colombera and documents from the sixteenth to the twentieth century; from the museum you can see the Monte Rosa.

Why go. The rural life of Novara, with a view of the rose.
