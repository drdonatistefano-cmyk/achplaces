Museo della Barca Lariana

Museums · Themed

Born from the association "La Raccolta della Barca Lariana," founded in 1978 to save old boats destined for demolition, the museum opened in 1982 in a nineteenth-century mill. In fourteen halls exhibits about 160 of the more than 350 boats owned (fishing boats, motorboats for racing and tourism, sailing boats, smuggling, military and work) with nets, engines, sails, tools and documents on the Larian nautical, even with increased reality. The most important piece is a gondola built in 1860 on Lake Como, with asymmetrical shapes and the felze of the Venetian tradition. Admission fee, reduced for TCI members and a companion.

Why go. A century and a half of Lario boats in an old spinning mill.
