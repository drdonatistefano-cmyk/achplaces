Peregrinatio Fidei

Museums · Old masters

Museum of sacred art in the 17th-century church deconsecrated on the southern outskirts, dedicated first to the Virgin of the Rosary and then to St. Christopher, a single nave with three arches and wooden cover; it recalls the Way of Santiago for devotion to St. James, patron saint of the village. It exhibits polychrome statues from the 16th and 17th centuries and sacred furnishings recovered from St. James and the campestri churches, including works by the Genoese silversmith Luigi Montaldo and the Virgin sleeping on a canopy of carved and painted wood.

Why go. Statues and silver sacred saved from abandonment.
