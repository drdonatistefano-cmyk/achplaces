Castello Episcopio e Museo della Ceramica

Museums · Themed · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Bishop's fortress built in the second half of the fourteenth century by Archbishop Giacomo D'Atri on a structure of the thirteenth century, on the edge of a gravina, with a tower greater than twenty blackbirds, ground floor dug into tuff and sixteenth-century garden. Since 1999 its 11 rooms house the Ceramic Museum, about 500 works from the 8th century BC to today. Opening hours from the source italia.it: weekdays 10-13 and 15:30-19:30, Sunday 10:30-13 and 16:30-21.

Why go. The history of the grotto majolica in a castle.
