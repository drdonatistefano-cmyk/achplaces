Museum Ladin Ursus ladinicus

Museums · Science and university

Section of the Museum Ladin, the provincial museum of South Tyrol, opened in 2011 on three floors: at the top the formation of the Dolomites through the fossils typical of the surroundings of San Cassiano, at the center the discovery and excavations of the Conturines cave with the remains of the Ursus Ladinicus, the cave bear lived over 50,000 years ago, and on the ground floor the reconstruction of the cave with the bear hibernating and the puppy. Fully wheelchair accessible; open 10-18 hours from 26.12.2026 to 15.03.2027 and from 02.05.2027 to 05.11.2027.

Why go. The Dolomites cave bear, family-friendly.
