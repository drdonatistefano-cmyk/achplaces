Museo Stefano Bardini

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The collection of the greatest antiques of the nineteenth century: sculptures, paintings, weapons, carpets in the blue rooms of his palace.

Why go. The "Bardini blue" that highlights every work.

  • Where Via dei Renai 37, Firenze
  • Entry €10 whole; €7 from 18 to 25 years and students; free under 18
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website musefirenze.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
