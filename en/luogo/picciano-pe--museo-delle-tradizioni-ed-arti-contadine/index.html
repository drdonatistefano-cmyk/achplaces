Museo delle tradizioni ed arti contadine

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Museum born from a private initiative that collects objects of everyday rural life, with reconstructions of ancient environments and a late medieval mill recovered in Pacentro; organizes exhibitions on local traditions. Contacts: 085 8285452, info@mutac.org.

Why go. The Abruzzo peasant world and a medieval mill.
