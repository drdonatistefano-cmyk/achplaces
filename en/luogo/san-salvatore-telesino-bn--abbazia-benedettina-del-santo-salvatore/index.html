Abbazia Benedettina del Santo Salvatore

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Monastery complex founded in the ninth century in the Calore valley, in the Middle Ages was one of the most important religious centers in the area and played a role in the spread of Benedictine monasticism in the South; destroyed and rebuilt several times after invasions, earthquakes and lootings. There are still parts of the ancient church, sections of mosaic floor, fragments of frescoes and other medieval structures, in an archaeological area that documents the monastic life. Open on Sunday 10:30-12:30, Monday and Saturday by reservation (tel. 0824 948144).

Why go. The ruins of a large medieval Benedictine monastery.
