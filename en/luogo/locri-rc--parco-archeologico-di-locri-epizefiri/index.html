Parco archeologico di Locri Epizefiri

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

More than 230 hectares along the state road 106, about 5 km from the city: the colony founded at the end of the 8th century BC, with sandstone walls, the Greek model theatre, the sanctuaries of Aphrodite, Persephone and Demeter, the sacred area of Marasà and the artisan district of Centocamere; Roman from 205 BC, abandoned in the 7th to 8th century A.D.

Why go. A city of Magna Grecia to travel.
