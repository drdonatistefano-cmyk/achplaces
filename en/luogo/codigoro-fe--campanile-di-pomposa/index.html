Campanile di Pomposa

History and architecture · Towers · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The Romanesque bell tower erected in 1063 by Magister Deusdedit, in red and yellow bricks with ceramic basins imported from Egypt, Tunisia and Sicily.

Why go. It's seen from miles on the plain.
