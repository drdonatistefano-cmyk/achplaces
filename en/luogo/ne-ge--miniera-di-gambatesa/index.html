Miniera di Gambatesa

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the upper Graveglia Valley, less than 20 km from Lavagna, the manganese deposit in the Monte Alpe diaspers was the most important in Europe; it has given numerous rare and new minerals. Museum since 2000, owned by the Aveto Park Authority since 2009: more than 8 of the more than 25 km of galleries are equipped for the visit, reached with the mining train, with routes of different lengths and difficulties and guided tours. Visitor center with bar, multimedia room, mineral collections, picnic area, guesthouse and self-guided paths. Tel. 0185 338876.

Why go. By train in the tunnels of the largest manganese mine in Europe.

the MiC card is closed every day during the hours; check opening on the mine site
