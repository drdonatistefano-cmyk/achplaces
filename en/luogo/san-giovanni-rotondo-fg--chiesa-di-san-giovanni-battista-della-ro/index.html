Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista «della Rotonda»

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It combines a high medieval baptistery with circular plan, the Round, and a rectangular nave probably added from the twelfth century; the baptismal font of the V-VI century, brought to light by the excavations of 2014, is one of the rare early Christian baptisteries of southern Italy. Inside frescoes from the 13th to the 1st 15th century, with the most important phase in the 14th century. According to the municipality the name of the city could derive from this building.

Why go. The early Christian baptistery that named the city.
