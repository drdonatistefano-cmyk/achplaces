Duomo Collegiata di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Started in 1415, it has a precious façade in latedogotic terracotta of 1420 with a doorway, ghimberg, rosette and terracotta friezes. Inside is a polychrome terracotta group of the 15th century Pietà with eight figures and a Deposition from the 16th century Cross of Defendant Ferrari, born in Chivasso. Next to it, the octagonal tower is the only remnant of the castle built in the 12th century by the Marquises of Monferrato.

Why go. The late-dogotic and Defendant Ferrari at his house.
