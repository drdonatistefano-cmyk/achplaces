Abbazia di Santa Maria di Cadossa

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

To the south of the village, in a place rich in water and vegetation: documented from the thirteenth century, passed between 1294 and 1306 to the hospitable order of the Holy Trinity of Venosa and, by tradition, lived there San Cono di Teggiano. In 1514 Pope Leo X joined her to the Charterhouse of Padula, of which she became a grange; the new church of 1578 has baroque stuccoes and the Assumption on the high altar. Succeeded in 1866, in 1869 it was bought by the Barons Gerbasio and used as a residence of the settlers, storehouse and stable. On the first Sunday of August there is the pilgrimage in honor of San Cono.

Why go. The monastery of San Cono and the pilgrimage in August.
