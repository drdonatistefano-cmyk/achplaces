Area archeologica di Aequum Tuticum

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Nodo viario romano in the locality of Sant'Eleuterio, where the Via Aemilia, Via Traiana (109 A.D.) and the Herculia passed: first century A.D. baths with black and white mosaics, rooms of the second century, a large room with polychrome mosaic and medieval blocks around a courtyard with well; an earthquake damaged it in 346 A.D.

Why go. At the intersection of the Roman roads.
