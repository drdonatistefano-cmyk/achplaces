Museo della maschera del Carnevale

Museums · Themed

The museum mainly documents the tradition of the Castlein Carnival and the history and manufacture of the mask: the nucleus is a collection of 222 pieces among masks, models in canvas and plaster, metal moulds and printing materials, acquired by the Municipality in 1997 and enriched in 2002 by masks donated by the collector Eugenio Gabrielli. Some rooms are dedicated to the mask factory of the Guatteri family, founded in 1810 by Prospero Guatteri and active until 1933.

Why go. Castlein Carnival masks.
