Chiesa Parrocchiale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A neoclassical church built between 1853 and 1858 on a project by Alessandro Antonelli, with four granite columns pronao and dome on the main altar; decorations by Luigi Morgari (dome and apse, 1898) and polychrome windows by Giovanni Da Re (1927-1943), including the It preserves an Immaculate Morazzone and a altarpiece of the Madonna enthroned attributed to Bernardino Lanino.

["Il cuore civico di Bologna dal Duecento: San Petronio, Palazzo d'Accursio, Palazzo del Podestà e, a fianco, il Nettuno del Giambologna.", 'Piazza Maggiore / Piazza del Nettuno, 40124 Bologna']

Why go. The church of Antonelli and its windows.
