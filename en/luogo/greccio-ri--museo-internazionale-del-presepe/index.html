Museo Internazionale del Presepe

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Opened in 2016 in the former church of Santa Maria, mentioned already in 1398, and in an adjacent building purchased and restored by the Municipality; it exhibits dozens of nativity scenes from all over the world, including the porcelain Triptych, the Ceramic Book of Life and great scenery such as "Rome that is no longer there," with views of the valley. It hosts exhibitions, conferences and workshops.

Why go. Nativity scenes from all over the world in the village of the crib.
