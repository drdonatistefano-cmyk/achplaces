Museo agricolo e del vino Ricci Curbastro

Museums · Themed

Set up in the rural buildings of the Ricci Curbastro estate, unique in Franciacorta, it collects thousands of tools and objects of rural life with particular attention to viticulture. Opened in 1986 after more than thirty years of research by Gualberto Ricci Curbastro and expanded by his children, it hosts temporary exhibitions, cultural events and guided tastings, and a library of over 2,000 volumes of agricultural history and local traditions. Admission fee (tel. 030 736094).

Why go. The peasant civilization and wine in a cellar of Franciacorta.
