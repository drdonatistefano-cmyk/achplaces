Miniera di Malfidano e Museo del minatore

Museums · City history

In 1864 engineer Giovanni Eyquem discovered a rich zinc deposit and founded the company of Malfidano; between 1870 and 1890 the laverie arose and since 1866 more than one million tons of zinc and 200,000 of lead were extracted. On September 4, 1904, the miners' strike ended in the Buggerru massacre, with three workers killed. The visit crosses the miners' workplaces with a view of the sea and cliffs; the museum of local history is in a former workshop in front of the port.

Why go. The working history of the mine and the first major strike.
