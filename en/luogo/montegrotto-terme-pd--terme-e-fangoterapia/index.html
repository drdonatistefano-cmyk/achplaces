Terme e fangoterapia

Local life and traditions · Spas

Montegrotto is known throughout Europe for its mud therapy and its waters were exploited already in Roman times; today it has over 240 thermal pools and numerous activities related to wellness.

Why go. The mud spa that made the resort famous.
