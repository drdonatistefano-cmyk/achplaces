Museo della Repubblica partigiana e della Resistenza italiana

Museums · City history

Inside the Fortress, the Republic of Montefiorino tells (18 June - 2 August 1944), so the country had the gold medal for military valor; re-established in 2015, it develops in nine rooms with weapons, uniforms, documents, daily objects, testimonies and multimedia projections. Open on weekends and holidays, 10-13 and 15-18.

Why go. The history of the partisan republic in the place where it was born.
