Galleria civica G. Segantini

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Opened in 2012 by the MAG Museo Alto Garda in the seventeenth-century Palazzo dei Panni, it is dedicated to Giovanni Segantini (Arco 1858-1899), one of the major exponents of divisionism: paintings, sculptures and works on his own paper and contemporary Trentino and Veneto such as Eugenio Prati, Vittore Grubicy and Alessandro Milesi, works by Leonardo Bistolfi, a documentary section and interactive posts. Since November 2024 he has exhibited "Autumn sun," not visible to the public since 1954. Free entrance with Garda Trentino Guest Card and Museum Pass.

Why go. The painter of the Alps in his hometown.

  • Where Via G. Segantini 9, Arco
  • Entry €4, reduced €2; during the exhibitions €7, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info
