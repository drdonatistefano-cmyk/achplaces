Chiesa di San Domenico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Testimony of the Dominican presence, it combines Renaissance and Baroque elements: facade with stuccoes and a refined portal, interior with a single nave with frescoes on St. Dominic, the apostles and the Dominican saints, side altars with marble decorations and statues.

Why go. Dominican frescoes in a single nave.
