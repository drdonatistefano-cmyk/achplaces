Sentiero etnografico del Vanoi

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Main element of the Ecomuseum, built and managed by the Paneveggio Park, salt from Caoria (850 m) to Malga Vesnota di Sopra (1850 m) with thematic links between rural buildings of the subsistence economy, in four areas: village, meadows, forest and pastures. It starts from the Casa del Sentiero Etnographico, open from 28 June to 13 September 9-12 and 15-18:30; guided tours by reservation.

Why go. The peasant life from the country to the pasture.

  • Where Casa del Sentiero Etnografico, Caoria, Canal San Bovo
  • Entry €3 (Hiking house, unique Ecomuseum ticket)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
