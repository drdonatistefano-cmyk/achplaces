Roseto botanico «Carla Fineschi»

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

Defined the world's largest private rose garden, born in 1967 from the collections of Professor Gianfranco Fineschi: thousands of varieties (over 6,500 according to the card) from all continents, even rare, ordered in 21 sectors according to the classification, with plates indicating the name, age and creator of each rose. The best period is the main flowering, May-June.

Why go. The largest rose collection in the world in private hands.
