Villaggio-santuario nuragico di Romanzesu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the plateau of Poddi Arvu, in a forest near the Tirso springs about 13 km from the village: about seven hectares entirely granite with a hundred huts, a well temple with a corridor of over 40 meters leading to a basin surrounded by six steps amphitheatre, two megaron temples, a rectangular temple and a spiral labyrinth. Well and labyrinth date back to the 13th-IXth century BC; the name comes from a Roman settlement of the II-III century A.D. Admission fee.

Why go. One of the most important centers of Nuragic Sardinia.
