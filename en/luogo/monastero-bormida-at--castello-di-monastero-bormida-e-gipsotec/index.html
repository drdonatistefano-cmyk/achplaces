Castello di Monastero Bormida e Gipsoteca Rubino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Founded in 1050 as a Benedictine monastery by monks of the abbey of Fruttuaria, from the end of the fourteenth century it was fortified by Del Carretto, then six-seventh-century residence of Della Rovere and Polleri of Genoa, which in 1876 gave it to the Municipality, today owner and with the town hall on the noble floor. The noble floor is home to the Gipsoteca "Edoardo Rubino" with the plasters of the Piedmontese sculptor, the attic temporary exhibitions, the mastic the "Torre dei Cavalieri" on weapons and medieval costumes; in the cellars are aged wines of the Langa Astigiana. In 2026 Sunday openings from May to November: guided tours at 15 and 16 (about 1 hour), theatricalized at 11 and 17 (about 1.5 hours); reduced with subscription Musei Piemonte.

Why go. From the Benedictine monastery to the castle of Del Carretto, with the plasters of Rubino.
