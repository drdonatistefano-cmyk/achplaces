Camillo Sirianni

Local life and traditions · Crafts

A company founded in 1909 by Angelo Sirianni in a small warehouse in the historic center transformed into a joinery; today an industry that combines artisan care and processing of wood and steel, with over a thousand items exported in more than 35 countries. Open on the occasion of the 2018 FAI Fall Day.

Why go. A century of carpentry became industry.
