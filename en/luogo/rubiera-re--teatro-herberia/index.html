Teatro Herberia

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Opened on 14 January 1926 with the Bohème di Puccini, designed by engineer Antonio Panizzi and architect Italo Costa; animated until the 1950s, it became a cinema. Purchased by the Municipality, restored since 1988 preserving its late liberty characters and reopened in 1998, it hosts up to 420 spectators with stage to mobile platforms.

Why go. A late Liberty theater reborn.
