Museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters

Born in 1993 in the monumental 18th-century seminary on Piazza della Repubblica, with porticos and loggias of the architect Giovan Biagio Amico Trapani and eleven arches on two floors; exhibits works of art in the rooms on the ground floor.

Why go. Sacred art at the Seminary of Giovan Biagio Amico.

The MiC card (updated 2021) indicates closure in November and December due to lack of staff; in the other months Tuesday-Saturday 10-12.30 plus some afternoons; tel. 0923 909431.
