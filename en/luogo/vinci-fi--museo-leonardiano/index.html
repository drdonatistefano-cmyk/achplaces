Museo Leonardiano

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

The oldest collection of machines and models dedicated to Leonardo engineer and scientist, between Palazzina Uzielli and Castello dei Conti Guidi.

Why go. From the castle tower you can see the whole valley.

  • Where Piazza dei Guidi, Vinci
  • Entry €11 whole, €9 reduced (with the "Leonardo and Painting" section); cumulative with the whole Christmas house €14, €11 reduced; family €40
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website museoleonardiano.it
  • Opening hours official page
