Ipogeo ebraico e insediamento rupestre di Cugno Case Vecchie

History and architecture · Archaeology

Rock site around the country, along the European Path E1. At Case Vecchie there are tombs of the Bronze Age and a medieval rock settlement known for Jewish and early Christian frescoes and symbols; in the Jewish hypogeum the Aditus group in Rupe has identified engravings of menorah, documented with a 3D model.

Why go. The menorahs carved into the rock of the Ibleans.
