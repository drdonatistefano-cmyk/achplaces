Castello di Prasco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Castle of the Counts Gallesio-Piuma dating back to the 12th century, with a quadrangular body and three semicircular towers. It rises on a terrace with three gardens inside the walls, surrounded by a park in which there is a seventeenth-century snowfall; it houses the Welsh Study Centre, dedicated to Giorgio Gallesio, and a small museum of material culture. Visits by reservation (tel. 0144 375628); adheres to open castles with opening on the first and third Sundays of the summer month.

Why go. Castle, gardens and snowfall of the family of Giorgio Gallesio.
