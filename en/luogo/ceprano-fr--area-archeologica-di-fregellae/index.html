Area archeologica di Fregellae

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Colonia diritto Latino founded by the Romans in 328 BC on the left bank of Liri, in the valley between Mount Cairo and the Ausoni and Aurunci mountains; celebrated by Tito Livio, it was destroyed in 124 BC and rebuilt a little further south as Fabrateria Nova. The excavations brought to light an urban stretch of the Via Latina, the road that after the refoundation was prolonged to Capua. The site is part of the Regional Nature Reserve.

Why go. The place from which the museum's artifacts come along the Via Latina.
