Chiesa della Madonna della Neve

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Three-nave Gothic church built around the year 1000 on the remains of a Roman temple dedicated to Ceres (or Bona), of which remains the fishbone floor; it preserves statues and ancient frescoes, a plaque dedicated to Helvia, priestess of Ceres, and a bas-relief that portrays it during a rite.

Why go. The church on Ceres temple.
