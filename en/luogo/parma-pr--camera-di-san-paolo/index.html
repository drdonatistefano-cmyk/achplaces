Camera di San Paolo

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The apartment of the abbess Giovanna da Piacenza with a pergola vault frescoed by Correggio in 1519 and the Room of the Araldi.

Why go. The first masterpiece of Correggio in Parma.

Closed for works from 31 August 2026 to spring 2027

  • Where Via Melloni, Parma
  • Entry Ticket single Civic Museums €15 full, €12 reduced
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website emiliaromagnaturismo.it
  • Opening hours official page
