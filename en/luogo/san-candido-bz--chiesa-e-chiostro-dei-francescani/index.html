Chiesa e chiostro dei Francescani

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Church deliberately stripped, consecrated in 1697 on the project of the friar Vitus Rastbichler; in the cloister the cycle of St. Francis, 31 large tables with 72 scenes painted by Lukas Platzer between 1706 and 1709. The main altarpiece of Christoph Unterberger, statues of Johann Pater and Andreas Fasching, was restored in 1992 and received the Europa Nostra Prize in 1994.

Why go. The Franciscan cycle of the cloister.
