Pieve di Santa Giustina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises where until the thirteenth century there was the original nucleus of the town, today inside the cemetery of Palazzolo; according to tradition on this hill San Zeno, bishop of Verona in the fourth century, celebrated Mass for the population. Built probably around the Mille in Romanesque style, with Moorish fishbone stones, and damaged by the earthquake of 1117, it has only one nave and two apses, one of which housed the ancient baptismal font; inside frescoes partly dated around the Mille. Reopened after more than two years of restoration.

Why go. A Romanesque parish church of the year 1000 in the hills of Garda.

normally not accessible; open with guided tours for the Spring FAI Days (21-22 March 2026)
