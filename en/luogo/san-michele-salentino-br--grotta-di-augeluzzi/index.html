Grotta di Augeluzzi

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

In the district of Augeluzzi, between olive groves and trulli, the cave dates back to the Middle Paleolithic and testifies to the presence of Neanderthal man in the territory.

Why go. Neanderthal tracks in the trulli campaign.
