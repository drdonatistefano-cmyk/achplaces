Museo Alta Val Venosta

Museums · City history

Dedicated to the submersion of 1950 and to the fate of Curon Vecchia and Resia: it tells the trauma of the loss of homes and land and reconstruction with historical photographs, models, sacred objects and statues of the ancient churches and testimonies of peasant life; some rooms reproduce a traditional stube. In summer guided tours by reservation.

Why go. Understand the history of the submerged country before seeing the bell tower.
