Torre e Ricetto di San Mauro

History and architecture · Villages · Viewpoint

In 1029 there was already a church with a bell tower on an outlying rock, around which the Benedictine monks built the center of their curtis; about 250 years later the defenses were strengthened. The tower is visited with the guides of the FIE and some rooms of the shelter host exhibitions and events.

Why go. The fortified village of the Benedictines.
