Museo dei Brettii e del Mare

Museums · Archaeological

In the Palazzo del Trono, overlooking Piazza del Popolo, exhibits archaeological finds from Bretty necropolis and underwater finds.

Why go. The ancient roots of the coast, from the land and the sea.
