Chiesa di Nostra Signora d'Itria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of late-Gothic forms whose façade is decorated by the twentieth-century graffiti by Costantino Nivola. In the village also the seventeenth-century St. John the Baptist, with a wooden pulpit carved with a Franciscan symbol, and the church of the Rosary, with frescoes.

Why go. The facade signed by Nivola.
