Museo delle ceramiche di Castelli

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the Franciscan monastery enlarged at the end of the seventeenth century, with the cloister frescoed in 1712 with stories of the Virgin; it tells the tradition of the capital of the ceramics of Abruzzo.

Why go. The history of the castle majolica.
