Teatro La Vittoria

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Embedded in the Palazzo Comunale, the current theatre of 1863, fourth in the history of Ostra, is the work of the architect Francesco Fellini of Barbara: horseshoe plant, 29 stages on two orders and loggia, decorations with symbols of the Theatre and Music, curtain with the Town Hall and ancient scenic machinery still working. Closed in 1980, it was reopened on 6 January 1998 and hosts prose, theatre for children and concerts.

Why go. An Italian theater with original stage cars.
