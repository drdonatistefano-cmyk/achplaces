Castello Bonoris

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the hill of San Pancrazio, where a fortified building has been documented since 1107, the owner Gaetano Bonoris rebuilt his residence in neo-Gothic style since 1890, on the model of the medieval village of Turin of 1884 (inspired by the castles of Fénis and Issogne): project by Antonio Tagliaferri, completed with Carlo Melchiotti; frescoes by Giuseppe Rollini (1898-1900) in the chapel and baronale salon, furnishings by the Arboletti brothers, park redesigned in 1901 by Giuseppe Roda. Municipal since 1996. Open from April to October with guided tours on weekends; admission fee.

Why go. A fairytale castle built in the nineteenth century.
