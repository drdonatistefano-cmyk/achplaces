Castello Risolo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortified building of the sixteenth century, originally isolated, with two high square towers at the corners, the eighteenth-century court on two levels towards Piazza del Popolo and the rustic portal surmounted by the coat of arms and two statues; the Protonobilissimi, marquis in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries, transformed it into a marquisal palace. Today it hosts exhibitions, shows and conferences.

Why go. The palace-fortress on the square.
