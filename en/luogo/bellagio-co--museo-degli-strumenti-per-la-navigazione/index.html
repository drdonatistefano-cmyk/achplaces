Museo degli strumenti per la navigazione

Museums · Themed

Private collection of more than 200 nautical objects, in an ancient tower house of the village of San Giovanni: Venetian telescopes of the eighteenth century, compasses, sundials, astrolabi, sea chronometers, an armillary sphere and a brass planetarium, with a story of life on ships of the nineteenth century.

Why go. Explores and on-board instruments on the lake shore.

seasonal opening (from Easter/April to September-October, morning 10-13; afternoon only by reservation), the two sources Promobellagio give different periods
