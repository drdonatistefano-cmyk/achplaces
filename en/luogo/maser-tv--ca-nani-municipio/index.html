Cà Nani, Municipio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa belonged to the Nani, then to the Trieste and the Fanzago, sold to the Municipality in 1927 and restored in the eighties of the twentieth century; it has an eighteenth-century sundial and the oratory of San Giovanni Battista, documented since 1686. Visit during the opening hours of the municipal offices.

Why go. A Venetian villa became a town hall.
