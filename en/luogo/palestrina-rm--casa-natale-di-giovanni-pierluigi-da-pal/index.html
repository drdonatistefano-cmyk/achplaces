Casa natale di Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina

Museums · House museums

The sixteenth-century house where the composer was born, restored in 1994 on three levels, is home to the Fondazione Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina, research center with a library of polyphonic music; the biographical and iconographic exhibition, curated by Lino Bianchi, retraces life and works from 1525 to 1594. Open Tuesday-Sunday (variable hours, closed Monday).

Why go. In the house of the prince of polyphony.
