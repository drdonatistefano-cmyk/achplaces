Palazzo Rossi, Museo di storia naturale della Calabria (Sezione Diorami)

Museums · Themed

On the ground floor of the historic Palazzo Rossi, in 120 m2, four dioramas reconstruct the environments of the National Parks of Pollino, Sila and Aspromonte and the Regional Reserves of Lake Tarsia and the Foce del Crati. It is one of the four sections of the Natural History Museum of Calabria, created to spread the natural sciences especially among young people.

Why go. The Calabrian parks in a hall.
