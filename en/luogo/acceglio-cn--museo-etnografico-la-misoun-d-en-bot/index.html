Museo etnografico La Misoun d'en bot

Museums · Themed

Born in 1992, it reconstructs the interior of a traditional house with about 1,500 objects collected in the abandoned villages of the valley of Unerzio: on the first floor work tools and objects of daily mountain life. Opening on request (tel. +39 0171 99017).

Why go. The domestic life of the former hamlets.
