Museo di arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

Paintings, statues, furnishings, vestments and silvers from the 16th to the 20th century, linked to devotion to the patron and founder Costabile Gentilcore: cups, pissides and patenes dated from 1586 to 1895, an ostensory and a crown of the eighteenth century, parchments of the sixteenth century, portraits of canons, Neapolitan nativity figures from the seventeenth to the nineteenth century, a pivial of about 1700 and statues including a wooden Madonna of the sixteenth century.

Why go. The devotional treasure of the village of the abbot.
