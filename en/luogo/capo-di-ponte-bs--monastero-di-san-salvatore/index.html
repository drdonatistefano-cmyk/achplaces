Monastero di San Salvatore

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The only surviving building of a monastic complex from the 11th to 11th centuries, hidden among the trees at the slopes of the mountain: three naves, transept, dome on plumes and three apses; portal and capitals carved with raptors, hippogriffi, sirens and racemes of French taste.

Why go. The Romanesque Cluniac in the woods.
