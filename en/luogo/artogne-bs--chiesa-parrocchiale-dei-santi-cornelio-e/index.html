Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Cornelio e Cipriano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Rebuilt in late Baroque style in the mid-18th century on a fifteenth-century building, it has a facade with eight niches with statues and an eighteenth-century portal in stone of Sarnico. Inside the frescoes of the eighties of the eighteenth century by Ludovico Gallina, the main Baroque wooden altar by Andrea Fantoni, the altarpiece of the Madonna del Rosario by Giacomo Ceruti called the Pitocchetto (coming from the church of Santa Maria) and paintings by Gaspare Diziani in the presbytery, including an Adoration of the Magi.

Why go. Fantoni, Ceruti and Diziani in a single church in the valley.
