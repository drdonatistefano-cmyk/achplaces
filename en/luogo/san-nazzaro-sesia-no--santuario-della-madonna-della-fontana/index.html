Santuario della Madonna della Fontana

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Country shrine between San Nazzaro Sesia and Villata, with devotion attested from 1591 and pilgrimages from the seventeenth century; the church was rebuilt in 1752-68 on a previous structure. Under the marble altar, with a palotto made of hard stones and a rococo eraser made of wrought iron, a spring gushes out; it preserves frescoes of Peracino (1767) and G. Avondo (1817) and a small 16th century painting of the Miracle.

Why go. The spring under the altar, in the middle of the rice fields.
