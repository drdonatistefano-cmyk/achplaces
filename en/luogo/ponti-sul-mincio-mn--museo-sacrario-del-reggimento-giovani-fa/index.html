Museo sacrario del Reggimento «Giovani Fascisti» (La Piccola Caprera)

Museums · Themed

Museum founded in 1960 with relics of the regiment and the countryside in North Africa 1940-1943, in three rooms dedicated to the fallen and the commander, the countryside and the Afrika Korps and the opposing forces; a historical office keeps library, photos and documents. Outside, beyond the tomb of the commander, the alert of remembrance with cippi for forgotten departments. Recognized by Regione Lombardia as a museum regiment.

Why go. Military memory of the Africa campaign.

visits by telephone (tourist site of the Municipality); the MiC card indicates every day closed
