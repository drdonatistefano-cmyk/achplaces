Museo delle maschere mediterranee

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

It connects the Mamuthones and Issohadores with the carnivals of other Mediterranean lands: at the entrance images, texts and sounds on the origins of the Mamuthones and the oldest Mamuthone mask, perhaps early nineteenth century; then the hall of the barbaric carnival, with Boes, Merdules and Filonzana of Ottana and Thurpos of Orotelli, and the Mediterranean hall, with the carnivals of the Alpine arch and the Iberian and Balkan peninsulas. The Mamuthones carry the black face of wood, sheepskins and about 30 kilos of bells. A cumulative ticket with the Museum of Culture and Work €6, reduced €4.

Why go. The barbaric carnival explained by his land symbol.

  • Where Piazza Europa, Mamoiada
  • Entry €4, reduced €2,60
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
