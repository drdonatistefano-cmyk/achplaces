Chiesa parrocchiale dell'Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1777, it has a stone façade with a double ramp staircase and a statue of the Assumption. Inside he preserves paintings by Giacinto Diana from the late eighteenth century: a Madonna in glory, a Madonna del Carmelo and a San Vincenzo Ferreri.

Why go. The eighteenth-century paintings of Giacinto Diana.
