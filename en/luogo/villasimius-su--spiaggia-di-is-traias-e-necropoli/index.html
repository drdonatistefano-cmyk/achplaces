Spiaggia di Is Traias e necropoli

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches

A half-moon of white and very fine sand about 150 meters next to Simius, protected by the mistral by two promontors and in front of the island of Serpentara, with cliffs for snorkeling; not far from the Roman necropolis of Accu is Traias (1st century BC-III century AD), about thirty burials in the pit, urns and amphorae.

Why go. A white sandy cove with a view of Serpentara.
