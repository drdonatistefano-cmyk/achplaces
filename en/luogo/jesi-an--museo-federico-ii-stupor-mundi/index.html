Museo Federico II Stupor Mundi

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Presented as the first museum in the world dedicated to the life and work of the emperor, it is a virtual route of projections and installations in 16 rooms, in the Palazzo Ghislieri where Frederick II was born in 1194. From October to May open Thursday and Friday 15-20, Saturday and Sunday 10-20.

Why go. Federico II told in sixteen multimedia rooms.

  • Where Piazza Federico II 3, Jesi
  • Entry €7, reduced €5, family €19
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
