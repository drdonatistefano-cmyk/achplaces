Abbazia di Santa Maria de Olearia

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

One of the most famous monastic settlements of the ancient Duchy of Amalfi, leaning on a rocky wall between Capo d'Orso and the centre of Maiori; its value lies in the medieval pictorial cycles preserved inside. The tours are guided and by reservation at the tourist office of the Municipality; the Touring indicates opening Wednesday and Saturday 15:30-18:30 and Sunday 10-13, on request the other days, closed from November to March, free admission.

Why go. Medieval frescoes in the rock above the sea.

  • Where SS Amalfitana 43, Maiori
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
