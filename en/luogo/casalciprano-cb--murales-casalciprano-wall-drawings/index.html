Murales «Casalciprano wall drawings»

Museums · Modern art

Project realized in 2010 and among the first paintings in Molise: 8 large murals, 2 collectives and 6 individuals, of internationally renowned artists under the artistic direction of Lorenzo Canova. In 2011 two murals by Alessia Cerio were added dedicated to the grape harvest and pressing, in a corner of the village with arches and stone steps.

Why go. History and traditions of the village told in a contemporary key on the walls of the village.
