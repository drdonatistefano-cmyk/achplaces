Chiesa parrocchiale di San Lorenzo Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient parish church mentioned in 1186, rebuilt several times: the current church, designed in 1728 by Giuseppe Castelli and completed in 1778 by his son Filippo, has a single nave with side chapels and a pronao of four Corinthian columns on six steps. It preserves paintings attributed to Giorgio Alberini (1620 about), palaces by Bernardino Galliari (1776), a wooden crucifix of the sixteenth century and the silver reliquary of San Lorenzo, of the Roman school of the eighteenth century.

Why go. The eighteenth century Piedmontese on the main square.
