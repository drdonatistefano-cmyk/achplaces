Castello di Castiglione Falletto

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Quadrangular fortress that dominates the village, built by Bertoldo Falletti of Alba after having received the feud in 1225 by the Marquises of Saluzzo; the Falletti, who gave the name to the village, held it until the end of the seventeenth century, then passed to the Caramelli, the Clarotto, the arcade poet Giuseppe Cerutti and the Vassallo of Dogliani, who in the mid nineteenth century adapted it to a noble residence. It preserves the medieval system with two lateral towers and the central male; in its walls was found a Roman plaque with the figure of a "sutor" (sculpture).

Why go. The fortress of the Falletti that gives the name to the country.
