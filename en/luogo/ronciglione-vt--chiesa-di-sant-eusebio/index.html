Chiesa di Sant'Eusebio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Early Christian church with three remarkable frescoes: the oldest Visitatio Virginis, from the 8th century, a Last Supper from 1100 and an 11th century Tree of Jesse.

Why go. One of the oldest frescoes in Italy.
