Oasi di Manzolino-Tivoli

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Protected wetland between Castelfranco Emilia and San Giovanni in Persiceto, with water mirrors and reeds where you can observe herons, wild ducks and swamp hawks from huts and paths.

Why go. Birdwatching in the plain.
