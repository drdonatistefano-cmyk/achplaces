Valli di Comacchio in barca da Stazione Foce

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The large brackish lagoons of the Po Delta Park, with fishing houses and flamingos, to visit by boat or by bike on the banks.

Why go. At sunset the most beautiful landscape of the delta.

  • Where Stazione Foce, Valli di Comacchio
  • Entry Excursions for a fee or with the Comacchio Museum Pass
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
