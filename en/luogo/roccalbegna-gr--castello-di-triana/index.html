Castello di Triana

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Castle near Roccalbegna passed from the Aldobrandeschi to the Piccolomini of Siena in 1388; it consists of two large buildings and a mast, mostly covered with ivy.

Why go. A Piccolomini castle wrapped in ivy.
