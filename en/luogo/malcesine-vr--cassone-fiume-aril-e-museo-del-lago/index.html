Cassone, fiume Aril e Museo del Lago

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

Small village a few kilometers south with a fishing boat port and the typical bisse; there flows the Aril, referred to as the shortest river in the world (175 meters), which passes under three bridges, forms a waterfall and flows into the lake. On the port the Museum of the Lake exhibits fishing gear, objects of use and photos of ancient sailing boats.

Why go. The shortest river in the world.
