Big Bench di Pomaretto

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

Blue giant bench, more than two meters high, of the network created by American designer Chris Bangle (the first was installed in Clavesana in 2010); placed in a panoramic point, allows you to look at the landscape from a different perspective. With the Big Bench passport you collect the stamps of the benches.

Why go. A giant sitting with a view of the valley.
