Santuario di Nostra Signora del Becetto

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Built in 1201, it has enjoyed since the early times of great fame and devotion among the populations of the valley and the plain; it preserves an unusual Black Madonna and is a stop of the Varaita Trekking Valley that climbs from Verzuolo.

Why go. The Black Madonna of the Varaita Valley.
