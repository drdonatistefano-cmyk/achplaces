Castello e Piazzale di Corte

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortified nucleus of the village with Renaissance palace, two towers (one pentagonal) and the cell in which the brothers held St. Thomas Aquinas in 1244-45, then transformed into a chapel; the Doge's Palace had five floors, the last three demolished after the earthquakes of 1703 and 1915. The Piazzale di Corte with the male tower, which overlooks the Palazzo del Bargello, is the highest point in the centre, with views from the Ernic Mountains to the Marsicans, the Aurunci and the Lepini.

Why go. The cell of St. Thomas and the wider panorama of the Ciociaria.
