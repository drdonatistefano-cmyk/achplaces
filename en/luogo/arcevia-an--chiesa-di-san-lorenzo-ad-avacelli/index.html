Chiesa di San Lorenzo ad Avacelli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the castle of Avacelli, it preserves a Crucifix and a terracotta altar painted by the Madonna del Rosario and Mercy, the work of a workshop influenced by the Marches Della Robbia.

Why go. Terrecottas of taste robbiano inside one of the castles of Arcevia.
