Parco del castello e Messer Tulipano

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

The English park was designed in the nineteenth century by Xavier Kurten, architect of the Savoy court; at the end of the century there were built the Orangerie and a glass and iron greenhouse of the Lefevre brothers of Paris. Every spring, between April and May, since 2000 there is held "Messer Tulipano" with about 100,000 blooms of tulips and daffodils, including a collection of black tulips; in the park there are a coffee, a picnic area and a shop of flowers and local products.

Why go. The flowering of 100,000 tulips in the park of Kurten.
