Centro Culturale Aldo Rossi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The civic centre of Borgoricco is a multifunctional space designed by Aldo Rossi, one of the most revolutionary architects of the twentieth century: the project takes up the multifunctional role of the Venetian villa and its geometric shapes as well. It houses a large theatre and the Museum of the Roman Centuration and is barrier-free. In 2026 the project Visit Camposampierese proposed free evening openings on reservation with guided tour of the architecture.

Why go. A work by Aldo Rossi that reinterprets the Venetian villa.
