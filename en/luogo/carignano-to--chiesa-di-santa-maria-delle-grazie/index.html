Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Italia.it indicates it among the three main testimonies of the past of Carignano, together with the cathedral and the sanctuary of Valinotto. It is officiated church, with weekday and festive masses.

Why go. One of the three symbol churches of the city.
