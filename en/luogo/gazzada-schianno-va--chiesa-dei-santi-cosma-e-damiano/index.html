Chiesa dei Santi Cosma e Damiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Cited in the documents from 1288 but probably before the Mille, it has a massive tower with the entrance to the base: it is the only example in Lombardy of clocher-porche, the bell tower that acts as an entrance porch. Inside there are frescoes from the XV-XVI century.

Why go. A bell tower-portico unique in Lombardy.
