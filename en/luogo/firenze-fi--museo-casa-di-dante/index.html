Museo Casa di Dante

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The house built by the Municipality at the beginning of the twentieth century on the place where tradition wants to be born Dante: three floors between the Florence of the thirteenth century, the public life of the poet and his fortune over the centuries.

Why go. On the trail of the Supreme Poet.

  • Where Via Santa Margherita 1, Firenze
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.destinationflorence.com
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
