Villa Cordellina Lombardi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between 1735 and 1742 on a project by Giorgio Massari for the jurist Carlo Cordellina Molin, with an ionic pronao with four columns in homage to Palladio. In 1743 Giambattista Tiepolo frescoed the living room: on the ceiling the Triumph of Intelligence on the Ignorance, on the walls the Family of Dario in front of Alessandro and the Continence of Scipione. Restored by Vittorio Lombardi between 1953 and 1956, he has been from the Province of Vicenza since 1970.

Why go. The salon frescoed by Tiepolo in a villa in Massari.
