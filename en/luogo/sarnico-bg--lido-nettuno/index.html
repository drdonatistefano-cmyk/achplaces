Lido Nettuno

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

Lido on the way to Predore with more than 40,000 m2 of greenery, sports fields and a walkway-solarium on the water; in the village also the Lido Fosio, free area of more than 10,000 m2.

Why go. A bathroom in Lake Iseo.
