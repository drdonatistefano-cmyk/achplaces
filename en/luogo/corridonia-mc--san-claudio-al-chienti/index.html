San Claudio al Chienti

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

One of the most important examples of Romanesque of the Marches, on the site of the Roman city of Pausulae: documented from the 11th century, it was a parish church and not an abbey. Square plan with semicircular apses, two cylindrical towers on the sides of the façade of Ravenna taste and above all two overlapping churches; the Gothic portal in Istria stone of the upper church is an addition. It preserves almost intact the original appearance.

Why go. Two Romanesque churches on top of each other.
