Parco della Pace (ex campo di prigionia)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Park at the entrance of the country in the area of the former camp, used for prisoners of the First World War and then as a refugee camp; since 1970 it is play and sports area and in 2001 it took the name of Park of Peace. The Region has approved two 600,000 euro projects to recover two buildings of the field damaged by the earthquake 2016-2017, destined to ticket office, bookshop and information point and laboratory space.

Why go. The symbolic place of the city of memory.
