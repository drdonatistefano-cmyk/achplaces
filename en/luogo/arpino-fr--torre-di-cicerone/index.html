Torre di Cicerone

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Medieval tower of the XI-XII centuries, 30 meters high, with square plan of about 16 meters on the side, built directly on the blocks of the cyclopean walls; from the top the valley of Liri and, on clear days, the Apennines.

Why go. The view of the Liri Valley.
