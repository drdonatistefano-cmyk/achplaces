Chiesa di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the thirteenth century mentioned in the bull of Boniface VIII of 1302; it preserves the portal of the sixteenth century, some terracotta statues and a cross. On August 24th, the historical festival takes in procession ladies, knights and bishops in medieval dresses.

Why go. The oldest church in the country and its medieval festival.
