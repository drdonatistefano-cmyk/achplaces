Museo etnografico della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

It tells the rural life of the Dauni Mountains: the peasant house, the work in the fields, the handicrafts and the popular religiosity. Born from the initiative of senior members of Pro Loco, it is located on the second floor of the Byzantine Tower.

Why go. The peasant life of the Dauni Mountains inside the tower.
