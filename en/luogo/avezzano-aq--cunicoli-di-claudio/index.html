Cunicoli di Claudio

History and architecture · Archaeology

Roman galleries dug by Emperor Claudio from 41 A.D. to regulate the waters of Lake Fucino and protect the houses from the floods; you can visit them with guided tours.

Why go. The hydraulic work of the Emperor Claudio.
