Pozzo d'Antullo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Large karst chasm born from the collapse of a cave, about 60 m deep and about 300 m broad, about 1 km from the village on the slopes of the mountains La Monna and Rotonaria; on the bottom grows a vegetation with trees up to 20 m tall. In the past, sheep with ropes for seasonal grazing were dropped.

Why go. A giant sinkhole, unique in Europe in size.
