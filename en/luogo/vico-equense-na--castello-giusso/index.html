Castello Giusso

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built between 1284 and 1289, traditionally by Charles II of Anjou, it was a fortress and a residence; in the seventeenth century it became a noble residence with gardens, caves and water games, and in the nineteenth century the Giusso, from which it takes its name, chose the salmon pink colour and frescoed the Hall of Arms and the Hall of Ventagli. Part of the city walls and a terrace overlooking the sea remain.

Why go. The pink Angevin castle on the cliff.
