Pinacoteca comunale «F. Galante Civera» nel Torrione

Museums · Modern art

In the Torrione, a sixteenth-century tower of sighting and defense against Turkish incursions about 12,67 metres high, built after the restoration of the castle of Barletta by the military architect Evangelista Menga (1540 approximately): over 150 paintings, sculptures and drawings by modern artists, including the Pastorello di Francesco Galante, painter of the Neapolitan school of the late nineteenth century, and reproductions of works by Raffaello, Masaccio and Guido Reni.

Why go. Neapolitan art inside the anti-Turkish tower.
