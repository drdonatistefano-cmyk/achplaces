Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built in local stone on the Lombard tower and enlarged by the Aragonese in the 15th century; in 1744 the Sangermano barons transformed it into a residence adding the right wing. Completely restored in 2006, at the base exhibition remains of the pelasgic-sannitic walls (IV-III century BC).

Why go. The manor around which the village grew.
