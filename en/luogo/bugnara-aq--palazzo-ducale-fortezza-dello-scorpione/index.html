Palazzo Ducale (Fortezza dello Scorpione)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built around the year 1000 with defensive structure then adapted to residence; its remains, symbol of the noble past of the village, still seem to control the whole territory. A restoration project is planned for the building.

Why go. The castle that dominates the valley.
