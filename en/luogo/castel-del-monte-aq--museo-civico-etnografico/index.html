Museo civico etnografico

Museums · Themed

Museum spread in the historic center, in disused environments that reconstruct the traditional interiors with the objects of pastoral life; the route has six stages: the mill, the oven of the dance, the handicrafts of wool, the ancient house, the shepherdhood and the popular religiosity.

Why go. The life of the shepherds told in the rooms of the village.
