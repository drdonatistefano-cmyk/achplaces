Museo San Paolo

Museums · City history

The monumental complex of São Paulo, built on the remains of a medieval church, houses the Civic Museum that tells the history of the city from the Roman era to the twentieth century; the early Christian crypt preserves a fresco of St. Francis, indicated as the oldest image of the saint in Veneto.

Why go. The history of Monselice and the oldest San Francesco of Veneto.
