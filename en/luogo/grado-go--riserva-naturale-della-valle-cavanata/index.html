Riserva naturale della Valle Cavanata

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Ancient fishing valley in the easternmost part of the lagoon, an area of international value for birdwatching, reachable from Grado by bike: four routes (Five Woods, Visitor Center, Sea, Valley), interactive tour center, bike and binocular rental; the Pagoda and the Sabbia observatory are free access.

Why go. Birdwatching in the fishing valley.
