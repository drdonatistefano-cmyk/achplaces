Cassero di Manciano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress of the 12th century in the center of the village, municipal seat from the Grand Duchy, with a museum of history and art and the panoramic tower.

Why go. Seen from the Amiata to the Argentario.
