Spiaggia di Punta Molentis

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Small beach at the crescent at the mouth of the river Trottu, sheltered from the winds, closed by a promontory that degrades to the cliffs and from rocks and Mediterranean maquis; there is a abandoned granite quarry and the name comes from "su molentu" (the donkey carrying the extracted material).

Why go. A crescent sheltered between granite and stain.
