Santuario di Santa Maria e borgata Morinesio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

The sanctuary stands on the remains of a fourteenth-century chapel dedicated to Saint Margherita: sanctuary from 1511, enlarged in the eighteenth century for pilgrimages, is surrounded by double arched arcades; the frescoes of the dome and the exterior are by Francesco Agnesotti of Sampeyre, late nineteenth century. You can reach it on foot along the ridge east of the village, one of the most orderly of Stroppo, with megalithic portals, covered passages, round columns under the roofs, a sink-oven in the square and a small fresco of Giors Boneto in an alley. In clear weather you can see the whole valley and the plain of Cuneo. Visits: tel. 335 6020527 or 335 6820158.

Why go. Unspoilt alpine architecture and a view of the plain.
