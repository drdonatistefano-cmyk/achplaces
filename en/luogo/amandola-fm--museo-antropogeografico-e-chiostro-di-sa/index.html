Museo antropogeografico e Chiostro di San Francesco

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Visitor centre of the Park on the first floor of the cloister of San Francesco, rebuilt in the early seventeenth century with frescoed lunettes in 1635: presents the Sibillini Mountains with paths for images and texts on environments, flora, fauna, legends, art, history and popular culture, with attention to how man shaped the landscape. Reduced €3 for children 7-16 years and groups, free of charge up to 6 years.

Why go. The whole Sibillini Park in a museum.

the MiC card (updated to January 2021) indicates closed every day; the Park warns that after the earthquake 2016 some areas are forbidden
