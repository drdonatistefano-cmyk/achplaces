Sacro Tugurio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Small cave in Palazzo Majolica-Landrini where St. Francis, restored by the Landrini for the seventh centenary of his death in 1926; today the seat of the Regional Council of the Secular Franciscan Order and of the OFS Fraternity of Cannara.

Why go. The refuge of St. Francis in the village.
