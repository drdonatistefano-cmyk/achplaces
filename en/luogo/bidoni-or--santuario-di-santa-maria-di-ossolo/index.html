Santuario di Santa Maria di Ossolo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

A novenary and country church of the 13th century, rebuilt in 1632. At the beginning of September there is one of the most important festivals in the country, with the procession from the church to the parish.

Why go. The country shrine of the September festival.
