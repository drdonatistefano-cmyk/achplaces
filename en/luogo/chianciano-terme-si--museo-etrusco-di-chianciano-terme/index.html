Museo Etrusco di Chianciano Terme

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The Etruscan Museum of the city, with canopies, ceramics, funeral sets and terracotta pediment of the Temple of the Fucoli; two sections are dedicated to the Etruscan woman and to the banquet.

Why go. The pediment of the temple is the strong piece.
