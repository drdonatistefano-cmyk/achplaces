Forte Belvedere-Gschwent

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Austro-Hungarian fortress built between 1908 and 1914 at 1,177 m above the upper Valle dell'Astico, with concrete roof of about 2,10 m, three 10 cm obici in armored domes, 22 machine gun stations and four outposts connected by galleries; the motto was "Per Trento" is enough for me. Buyed by the Municipality in 1997 and restored with the Province, it is a museum on the three floors of the block casematte: system of the forts, war at altitude, medicine of war, rebuilt trench and the multisensory .

Why go. The best preserved fortress of the Great War.
