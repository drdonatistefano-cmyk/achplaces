Parrocchiale dei Santi Gervasio e Protasio e Oratorio dei Disciplini

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church of Ossimo Superiore is located on the presumed site of a fifteenth-century building, perhaps a castle; it has facade of the seventeenth century and inside seventeenth-century stuccoes and a altarpiece of 1843 by Antonio Guadagnini. Next to it, the Oratory of the Disciplines, home of the confraternity, dated 1658, with portal in stone of Sarnico and closed arched window.

Why go. Sixteenth century plasters and Guadagnini's shovel.
