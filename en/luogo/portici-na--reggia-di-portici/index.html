Reggia di Portici

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built from 1738 to 1742 at the behest of Carlo di Borbone as a summer residence, attracted by the excavations of Ercolano, on a project by Giovanni Antonio Medrano and Antonio Canevari, with interventions by Vanvitelli and Fuga; the main staircase was frescoed between 1743 and 1746. Sold in 1871 to the Province of Naples, since 1872 it hosts the Scuola Superiore di Agricoltura, today the Department of Agriculture of Federico II.

Why go. The Bourbon palace between Vesuvius and the sea.
