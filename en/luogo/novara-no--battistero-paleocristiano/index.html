Battistero paleocristiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

From the fourth-fifth century, in the quadriportico of the cathedral, among the major early Christian buildings of northern Italy, with frescoes of the Apocalypse of the late tenth century and a Last Judgment of the fifteenth century.

Why go. A baptistery of a thousand and five hundred years.
