Isola di Dino e Grotta Azzurra

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

An island of about 50 hectares, at most 100 meters high, in front of Cape of the Arena; the erosion dug the caves of Monaco, the Sardines (with stalagmites), the Waterfalls, the Lion and the Blue Grotto, the largest, with intensely coloured water and ancient marine fossils on the walls. You reach it by boat, canoe or pedal boat, and the seabed are rich in flora and fauna of natural interest.

Why go. The sea caves and faraglioni of the symbol island.
