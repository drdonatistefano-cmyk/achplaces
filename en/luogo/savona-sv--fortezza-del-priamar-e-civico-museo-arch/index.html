Fortezza del Priamàr e Civico Museo Archeologico e della Città

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Great sixteenth-century fortress on the sea built by the Genoese to assert the dominion over the city; in the fifteenth-century Loggia of the Castle Nuovo the Civic Archaeological Museum and the City, inaugurated on April 7, 1990 at the reopening of part of the complex.

Why go. The fortress on the harbor.
