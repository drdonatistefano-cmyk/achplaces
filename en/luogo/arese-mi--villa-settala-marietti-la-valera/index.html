Villa Settala Marietti «La Valera»

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

18th century residence with ancient rural village in three courts along the east-west axis, entrance garden, main garden and park; passed to Marietti in 1811, then to Tosi and Ricotti. In the garden a neo-Moresca Coffee House with pebble mosaics and, next to the Italian garden, the eighteenth-century lemon house with high wooden ceilings.

Why go. A villa of Lombard delight with its village.
