Teatro Sociale «Valentino Garavani»

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Designed in 1836 by Giacomo Moraglia and built in 1842-1845, it was inaugurated on 18 April 1845 with I Lombardi at the first Verdi crusade; Angelo Moia painted 18 scenes, the Morgari brothers the curtain, and Enrico Cassi realized the bronze bas-relief of Verdi. Over 600 seats with 62 stages on three orders; Arturo Toscanini also performed there. Today it is named after the designer Valentino Garavani.

Why go. The 19th century theatre where Toscanini played.
