Villaggio-santuario di S'Arcu 'e is Forros

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Nuragic village-sanctuary between Barbagia and Ogliastra, from the recent-final Bronze to the early Iron Age: the main megaron temple, about 17 meters long, in granite and shale with temenos and seats, a second temple with two-tone altar today largely at the Nuoro museum, isolated houses and workshops with lead ovens, and not far away a trilobed nuraghe. It is described as the most important metallurgical center of Nuragic Sardinia, with objects from Etruria, Greece and Near East; excavated since 1984 by Maria Ausilia Fadda.

Why go. The great sanctuary of Nuragic metallurghi.
