Chiese di Montanaro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church of San Nicolao and of the Assumption, built between 1644 and 1649, with a Latin cross and a single nave; the church of the Confraternity of Santa Marta, with a smooth and harmonious façade; the Sanctuary of the Madonna of Loreto, of 1681; the church of Santa Maria dell'Isola, dating back to about the year 1000.

Why go. From 1000 to Baroque in a few churches.
