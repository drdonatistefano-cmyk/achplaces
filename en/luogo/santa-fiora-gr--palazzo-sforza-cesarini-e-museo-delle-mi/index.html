Palazzo Sforza Cesarini e Museo delle Miniere di Mercurio

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The Count's palace, now the town hall, with the museum dedicated to the extraction of mercury on the Amiata between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries.

Why go. The mining history of Amiata.
