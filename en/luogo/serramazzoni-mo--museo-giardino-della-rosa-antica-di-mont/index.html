Museo Giardino della Rosa Antica di Montagnana

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Park of 43 hectares at 420 meters, on a panoramic ridge about 25 km from Modena, of which three are dedicated to over 800 varieties of roses: ancient and classic rose garden, "Herbario dei sensi" (Herbary of the senses), "Le roses rediscovered" (Roses found) and a nursery of roses in pot; the shop sells roses products and can be ordered with local specialties. The cynorrhoids are admired from July to January.

Why go. Eight hundred varieties of roses on a ridge.

open every day from sunrise to sunset only from April 15th to June 30th; in the other months visits only by reservation
