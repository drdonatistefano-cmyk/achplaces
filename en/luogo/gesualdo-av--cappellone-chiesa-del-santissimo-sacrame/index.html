Cappellone (Chiesa del Santissimo Sacramento)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was built by Domenico Ludovisi and his son Nicolò, who completed it in 1736; in 1842 the Pesiri stonemasons built the staircase of nine steps in travertine. Inside, the painting of the Palio on the reconciliation between Charles and Emanuele Gesualdo. From here on Easter the representation of the Passion towards the castle. Visits by reservation.

Why go. The large dome of the square.
