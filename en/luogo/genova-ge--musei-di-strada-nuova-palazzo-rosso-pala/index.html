Musei di Strada Nuova – Palazzo Rosso, Palazzo Bianco e Palazzo Tursi

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Along Strada Nuova (via Garibaldi) a single path combines Palazzo Rosso, Palazzo Bianco and Palazzo Doria Tursi, three sixteenth and seventeenth century residences among the most prestigious Rolli, home to civic art collections and Town Hall.

Why go. The heart of the UNESCO Rolli site.
