Castello Scaligero

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built between 1201 and 1202 after Verona had made the village a Free Village (1185), it was set on fire by the army of Ezzelino da Romano in 1232 and rebuilt in 1243, when the Podestà Enrico III da Egna built the main tower; it was part of Serraglio Scaligero along the Tione. With Venice (1405) it lost importance and was used as garrison and customs; the City bought mast and prisons in 1882 and in 1890 the tower was restored and equipped with clock. After the collapse of a corner tower the restorations of 1926-1935 created the public gardens; the great restoration of 2023 opened the interior halls and a new walkway with installations on medieval civil and military life. Opening hours April-October: Tuesday-Friday 10-13 and 14.30-17.30, Saturday 10-13 and 16-19, Sunday 10-12 and 15-19 (in August only Friday-Sunday); November-March: Thursday 10-13, Friday-Sunday 10-13 and 14.30-17.30.

Why go. The medieval castle around which the city was born, with walk on the walls.
