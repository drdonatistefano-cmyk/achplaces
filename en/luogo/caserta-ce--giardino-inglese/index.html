Giardino Inglese

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Wanted by Maria Carolina of Habsburg, in apparent natural disorder between watercourses and fake ruins: the Cryptoporic in the Bathroom of Venus with statues from Pompeii and from the Farnese collection, the Doric Temple and the greenhouses of Graefer.

Why go. The queen's romantic garden.

  • Where Piazza Carlo di Borbone, Caserta
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
