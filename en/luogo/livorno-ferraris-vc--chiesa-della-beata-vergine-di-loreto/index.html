Chiesa della Beata Vergine di Loreto

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Chapel authorized in 1625 and built in the manner of the Loreto house, flanked in 1720 by a building for spiritual exercises and then by a porch. Since 1896 it was the mother house of the Sisters of Santa Maria di Loreto, who bought it back in 1988-1989, restored and transformed into a center of spirituality. Inside a wooden statue of the Virgin in a glass niche.

Why go. A small Santa Casa di Loreto in the plain.
