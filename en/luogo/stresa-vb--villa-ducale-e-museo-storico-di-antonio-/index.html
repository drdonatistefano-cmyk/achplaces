Villa Ducale e Museo storico di Antonio Rosmini

Museums · House museums

The oldest villa in Stresa, built by the Bolongaro family around 1770, was the residence of the philosopher Antonio Rosmini from 1850 to 1855; the Study Centre can be visited includes the Rosminian Museum, Rosmini's room and the park. In via Manzoni there is the monument to friendship between Manzoni and Rosmini (1932), with crucifix and bas-relief by Pietro Canonica.

Why go. Rosmini's house on the lake.
