Teatro dell'Unione e piazza Giuseppe Verdi

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Inaugurated in 1868 on a project by Virginio Vespignani, who transformed the old Vico Squarano into a boulevard portico on the model of the Parisian passages; since 1901 the square is named after Verdi.
