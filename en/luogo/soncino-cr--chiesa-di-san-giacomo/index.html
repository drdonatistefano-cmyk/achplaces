Chiesa di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

In the fourteenth century, modified at the end of the fifteenth century, with a fourteenth-century bell tower and cloister of 1456-68; it preserves the Complaint on the dead Christ of Agostino de' Fondulis, a wooden choir of 1508 and two windows of 1495. Open every day 9-18.

Why go. The Terracotta Complaint of the Fondulis.

  • Entry Cumulation €7, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
