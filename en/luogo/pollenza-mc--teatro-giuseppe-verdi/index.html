Teatro «Giuseppe Verdi»

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Municipal theatre inaugurated in 1883 on a project by Ireneo Aleandri, with a horseshoe room, two orders of stages and a loggia, up to 250 seats.

Why go. An Italian theatre of the nineteenth century in miniature.
