S Pèrkmandlhaus

Museums · Themed

The house of the Pèrkmandl, the imaginary gnome of the mines: a museum near the town hall that tells in an innovative way mines and miners of the valley of the Fersina in the late Middle Ages and their connection with the Mòchena community, designed as an introduction to the mine of Erdemolo. Information and bookings at the numbers of the Mòchen Cultural Institute.

Why go. The history of the mines before going down to the tunnel.
