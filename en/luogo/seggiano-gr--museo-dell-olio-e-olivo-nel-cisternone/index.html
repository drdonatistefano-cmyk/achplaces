Museo dell'Olio e Olivo nel Cisternone

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Museum diffused in the center of the village starting from the suspended olive tree: a specimen of Seggian olive tree with its roots hung in the ancient water tank, nourished by water vapor and nebulized nutrients, presented as the largest plant in the world supported with aeroponic technique, a project born with the neurobiologist Stefano Mancuso. The heart is the ancient oil mill and orciaia, with tasting of the oil Seggiano DOP.

Why go. The olive tree that lives suspended in a cistern.
