Chiesa di San Pietro Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises in the highest part of the village, on the ancient fortification of the village, inside the walls. Attested as early as the 14th century, it owes its current appearance to an important restoration of the 16th century: two-side roof, bell tower with square base next to the facade and portal in Renaissance stone with flat architrave, surmounted by a bezel decorated with rosettes and the ray monogram of San Bernardino da Siena. In 2025 the project to repair earthquake damage 2016 was approved.

Why go. The church in the fortified heart of the village, with the Renaissance portal.
