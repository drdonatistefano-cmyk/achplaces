Museo Farmacia (Farmacia cinquecentesca)

Museums · Themed

Spezieria cinquecentesca with antique tools for the preparation of medicinal products and 238 vases modeled in 1580 in the Urbino workshop of Mastro Antonio Patanazzi, decorated with biblical, mythological and historical scenes and with the coat of arms of Cesaro Candia. The kit was purchased at the auction in Messina in 1628 by Don Gregorio Bottaro for 400 onze and donated to the Brotherhood of the SS. Sacramento, who rented it to various herbalists until 1852, when the spices store ceased its activity.

Why go. A pharmacy from the 16th century with its Urbinated majolica vases.
