Cantine del Morellino di Scansano

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The Morellino di Scansano DOCG, made from Sangiovese grapes, is one of the most famous red wines in Tuscany; wineries in the village and surroundings offer tastings, and Scansano is a stop of one of the wine roads of the Maremma. At the end of September the Grape Festival closes a month of tastings and open wineries.

Why go. Tasting the Morellino where it is born.
