Museo Archeologico Regionale Antonino Salinas

Museums · Archaeological

Regional archaeological museum dedicated to archaeologist and numismatic Antonino Salinas (1841-1914); it was born from the Museum of the Real University (1814), which became a National Museum in 1860, and collects the excavation materials of all Sicily, from Selinunte to Solunto.

Why go. The great archaeological museum of western Sicily.
