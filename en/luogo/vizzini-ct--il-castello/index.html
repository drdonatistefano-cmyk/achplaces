Il Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Probably built in Norman times on the remains of a Byzantine fortress, it was used as a prison in Bourbon times. In the Middle Ages the village was limited to the top of the Castle Hill, surrounded by walls of which today remains trace in one place, with one of the defence towers.

Why go. The oldest nucleus of Vizzini, at the top of the hill of the same name.
