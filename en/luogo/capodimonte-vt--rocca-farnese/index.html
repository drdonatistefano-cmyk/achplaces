Rocca Farnese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Octagonal fortress on a volcanic cliff, restored in the sixteenth century by Antonio da Sangallo the Younger, overlooking the historic centre; it is surrounded by an Italian garden and it is accessed by an inclined bridge designed by Sangallo himself. It is privately owned and can be visited on request.

Why go. The fortress of the Farnese peaks on the lake.
