Piazza Vecchietta e chiese del borgo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The square dedicated to the painter Lorenzo di Pietro called the Vecchietta, with the Town Hall, the Romanesque Santa Maria Maddalena and the church of Saints Stefano and Degna.

Why go. The medieval heart of the village.
