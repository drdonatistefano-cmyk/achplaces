Chiesa di Santa Maria di Brusicco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the church of Brusicco Angelo Roncalli received the baptism and the first communion and celebrated his first Mass as a priest; it is part of the common shrine of the country. Open every day 8-18.

Why go. The source of Pope John's baptism.
