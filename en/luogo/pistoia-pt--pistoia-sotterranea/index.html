Pistoia Sotterranea

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

More than 650 meters below the Hospital of Ceppo, where the river Brana flowed: the longest underground path in Tuscany, accessible also to those with disabilities.

Why go. The city seen from the foundations.

  • Where Ospedale del Ceppo, Pistoia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility Path also accessible to people with motor and visual disabilities
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
