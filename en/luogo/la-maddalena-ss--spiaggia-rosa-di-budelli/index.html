Spiaggia Rosa di Budelli

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Famous yield from Antonioni's red desert (1964), it must be the color of fragments of a pink micro-organism living in posidonia. Budelli, an oasis of about 25 hectares, passed to the park in 2016.

Why go. The most famous rose in the Mediterranean, from afar.

Since 1998 full protection: forbidden landing, bathing and anchor; it is observed from the Beach of the Knight with a guide of the park (SardiniaTurismo)
