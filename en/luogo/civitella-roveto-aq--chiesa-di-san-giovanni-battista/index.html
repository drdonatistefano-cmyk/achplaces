Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church dating back to the fifteenth century, in Baroque forms, which preserves an ancient organ and in the apse frescoes on the life of St. John the Baptist. On June 24, the feast of the saint provides a ritual bath in the Liri and a procession with fireworks.

Why go. The church of the patron saint and the feast of St. John.
