Museo Glauco Lombardi

Museums · City history · Paid entry

The collections dedicated to Maria Luigia d'Hapsburg, Duchess of Parma, and to the ducal court between the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, in the Palazzo di Riserva.

Why go. The Parma of Maria Luigia, wife of Napoleon.

  • Where Strada Garibaldi 15, Parma
  • Entry €7 whole; €5 reduced (15-17 years, over 65); free up to 14 years
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website emiliaromagnaturismo.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
