Museo archeologico di Varallo Pombia

Museums · Archaeological

Born from the initiative of a group of young people with the support of the Superintendence of the Antiquities of Turin and the Municipality, it exhibits in chronological order finds of the area: from the culture of Golasecca of the Iron Age (cinerary urns, bronze fibulas, funeral kits; the first showcase shows the evolution of the urn from biconic to ovoid) to the first Roman finds. It is located on the first floor of Villa Soranzo, with entrance from the municipal seat. Not indicated hours: information to the Municipality, tel. 0321 95355.

Why go. The urns and bronzes of the civilization of Golasecca, found in the surroundings.
