Santuario della Madonna del Rosario

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Also known as "Madonna della Belta" (Madonna of Belt), it is a seventeenth-century reconstruction of the previous Romanesque church, close to the parish, which has been a popular destination for centuries. We venerate an image considered miraculous of the Madonna and Child enthroned between four angels; on the sides of the main altar two great paintings of the twentieth century of an artist of the village, and on the altar of Santa Cristina a precious altarpiece with the martyrdom of the saint. Open during Sunday Mass at 18.15.

Why go. The most expensive sanctuary to the Varallesi, next to the parish.
