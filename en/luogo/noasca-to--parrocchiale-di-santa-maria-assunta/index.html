Parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church rises just above the village, almost close to the waterfall of Noaschetta; you arrive there with a staircase and a pedestrian ramp, and it is the starting point of the path to the waterfall.

Why go. The church at the foot of the waterfall, to be combined with the walk.
