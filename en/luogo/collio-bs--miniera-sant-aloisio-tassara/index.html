Miniera Sant'Aloisio - Tassara

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Iron mine mentioned already in 1819, gathered in 1870 in the Società S. Aloisio, passed in 1886 to Terni and then to the Tassara Group, which since the 1930s transformed it into a modern mining site; closed in 1984, it is reopened to the public in 2003. You enter with guide, helmet and torch for about 2.5 km of tunnels (8-10 °C, very high humidity: warm clothes and hiking shoes) between roasting ovens and miners' tools; there is also an adventure park suspended in the gallery, 85% indoor. Open from the end of April to October (Sundays; in summer Saturdays and Sundays; every day from 8 to 23 August), 10-12.30 and 14-17.30, mandatory booking; not accessible to disabled people.

Why go. A journey into the bowels of the iron mountain.

  • Where Via Castiglione, Collio
  • Entry Mine €12, reduced 5-13 years €10; adventure park €15, reduced €12; mine + park €24, reduced €20
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.minierasantaloisio.it
