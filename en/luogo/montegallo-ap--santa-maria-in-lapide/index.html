Santa Maria in Lapide

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Church probably of 1491, dependent on the monks of Farfa, along the Rio, the main tributary of the Fluvion; it belonged to a monastic settlement of which only partially recognizes the complex. There remains a 9th century crypt and a 15th century bronze bell with Gothic inscriptions.

Why go. A medieval crypt along the stream.

Park warns that descriptions are prior to the 2016 earthquake and some areas may be banned
