Ville di delizia e Passeggiata tra le ville

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The villas in the center were built by the noble families of Milan as holiday residences, with the façade facing the village and the park towards the countryside; the owners called artists and architects known for monumental staircases with balustrades and painted or stucco ceilings. Among the other Villa Frisiani Olivares Ferrario, Villa Borri Manzoli, Villa Massari, Villa Mereghetti and Villa Pagani della Torre. On the first Sunday of May the Pro Loco organizes the walk among the villas, when gardens, historic buildings and churches open to the public with guides.

Why go. One day a year private villas open the gates.
