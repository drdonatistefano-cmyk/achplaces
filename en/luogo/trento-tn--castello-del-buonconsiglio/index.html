Castello del Buonconsiglio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

The most important monumental complex in the region, residence of the bishops until 1803: Castelvecchio on the Tower of Augustus, the Magno Palazzo di Bernardo Clesio with frescoes by Dosso Dosso and Romanino, the courtyard of the Leoni, the cells of Cesare Battisti and the Italian garden.

Why go. The castle of bishop princes.
