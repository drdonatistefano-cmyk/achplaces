Ecomuseo della coltura del riso

Museums · Themed

In the premises of Tenuta Torrione della Colombara, it collects objects donated and restored by the citizens: kitchen and bedroom furnishings, the workshops of the blacksmith, the masquerade and wicker braider, a school hall of epoch, cars and agricultural wagons. In the same estate a modern rice farm shows the rice supply chain, from cultivation to packaging.

Why go. Life and work in Vercelli's rice fields.
