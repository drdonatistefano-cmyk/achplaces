Castello e Cappella di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The castle, of thirteenth-century origin, rebuilt in the fifteenth century and adapted to dwelling in the eighteenth century, dominates the village. The chapel, already documented in 1349 and once connected to the castle, houses on two registers a cycle of fourteenth-century frescoes on the life of Christ, from the Annunciation to the "Noli me tangere" (the Annunciation to the "Noli me tangere"), defined by the Municipality as the major of Piedmont and attributed to a painter of the Lombard school called Maestro di Montiglio; covered with plaster during a pestilence, were rediscovered in 1931 and restored since 1984.

Why go. The great fourteenth-century cycle of the Master of Montiglio.
