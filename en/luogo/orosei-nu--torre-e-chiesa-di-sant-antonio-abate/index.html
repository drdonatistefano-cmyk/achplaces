Torre e chiesa di Sant'Antonio Abate

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The medieval square tower, much renovated over the centuries, is the rest of the restored castle and dates back to the period when Orosei was the main center of its curatory. In the same enclosure stands the church of Sant'Antonio Abate, in volcanic stone, with a single nave with wooden roof and a cycle of frescoes of the fourteenth century; next, on 16 January, the fires of Saint Anthony light up.

Why go. The tower of the castle and the frescoes of the fourteenth century.
