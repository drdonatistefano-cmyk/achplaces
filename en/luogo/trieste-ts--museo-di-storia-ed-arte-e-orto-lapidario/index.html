Museo di Storia ed Arte e Orto Lapidario

Museums · Archaeological

Born around the monument to Winckelmann, killed in Trieste in 1768, with finds from Egypt, Cyprus, Greece, Magna Grecia and Etruria and a small collection of Mayan ceramics.

Why go. The Winckelmann Museum.
