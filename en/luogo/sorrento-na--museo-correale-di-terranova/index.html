Museo Correale di Terranova

Museums · Old masters

Opened in 1924 in the eighteenth-century villa of Correale, with citrus garden and terrace on the sea: over 10,000 pieces in 24 rooms on three floors, from the Greek, Roman and medieval finds of the peninsula to the Neapolitan and Flemish painting, the Sorrentine tarsia, the landscapes of the School of Posillipo (Pitloo, Gigante), porcelain, watches, shepherds of the nativity scene and fans; the library contains original writings of Torquato Tasso. The garden with the ancient araucaria and the avenue of the plane trees leads to the Belvedere on Vesuvius and Gulf. Amedeo Maiuri called him the most beautiful Museum of Italian province.

Why go. The house-museum of Sorrento with the view on the gulf.

the museum remains closed to the public from 6 January to 2 April, open from Tuesday to Sunday (source: museumcorreale.it); tickets also online
