Battistero e Museo Diocesano

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Paid entry

The octagonal baptistery in Verona pink marble started by Benedetto Antelami in 1196, with statues of the months and sculpted portals; next, the cathedral museum.

Why go. One of the masterpieces of the European Romanesque.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Parma
  • Entry €12 whole; €10 reduced (over 65, students, groups); family €25; free up to 12 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.piazzaduomoparma.com
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
