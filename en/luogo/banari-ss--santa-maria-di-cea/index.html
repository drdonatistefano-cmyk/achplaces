Santa Maria di Cea

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Country church with a former monastic complex in a valley about 6 km from Banari: Romanesque, in limestone cones, with east apse, facade divided by a frame and sailing bell tower; a date engraved on the facade reads 1250, 1260 or 1261. Next to the remains of the 'romitorio', with oven, central well and cobbled in the yard. The festival is celebrated around September 8.

Why go. Romanesque isolated in the Meilogu countryside.
