Rocca Colonna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built by the Colonna on the top of Mount Ginestro, it was destroyed in 1298, rebuilt and damaged again in 1436-37; with the restoration of Stefano Colonna in 1482 it lost its military function and became a food store of the community. Abandoned in the nineteenth century, it was recovered for public use at the beginning of the twentieth century; Jacopone was held by Todi and Corradino of Swabia. In winter it hosts an artistic nativity scene with life-size characters.

Why go. The Colonna fortress at the top of the acropolis.
