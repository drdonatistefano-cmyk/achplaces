Palazzo e giardino Borelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built from 1606 by the will of the Duke Charles Emanuele I of Savoy after the destruction of the Angevin castle, it preserves frescoes and wooden ceilings of the eighteenth-eighteenth-century; King Charles Albert lived several times its noble floors in the first half of the nineteenth century. A gallery dedicated to Carlo Alberto, built by Count Borelli in the second half of the nineteenth century, connects him to the nineteenth-century park with four terraces with fountains, caves and greenhouse. It houses the Documentation Centre Lalla Romano, the Porcelain Museum and the Civic Library; guided tours on pre-established dates, by reservation (tel.
