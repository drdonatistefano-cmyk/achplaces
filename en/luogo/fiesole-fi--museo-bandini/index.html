Museo Bandini

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The collection of the canon Angelo Maria Bandini: gold-bottomed plates and Robbian terracotta.

Why go. A small museum of primitive Tuscans.

  • Where Via Dupré 1, Fiesole
  • Entry In the unique ticket of the Museums of Fiesole (€12 with area and archaeological museum)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
