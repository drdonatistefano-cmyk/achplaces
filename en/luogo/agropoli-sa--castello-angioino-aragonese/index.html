Castello angioino-aragonese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The origins date back to the Byzantines, who during the Greek-Gothic war (535-553) fortified the promontory to defend the landing places; the current structure is Aragonese, from the end of the fifteenth century, with the entrance elevated on high arches and drawbridge and the monumental portal in tuff beating. It has a triangular plan with three circular towers, a large dry moat and walls with a walk of covered rounds, blackbirds and wounds; inside the square of arms, today garden and theatre, the hall of the French to the north and the baronial building to the east. In summer, the municipality indicates opening every day 10-13 and 16-19, in winter reduced hours; guided tours by reservation (Tourist Office 0974 827480).

Why go. The fortress at the top of the promontory, with the Gulf of Salerno at the feet.
