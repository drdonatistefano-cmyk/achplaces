Chiesa di Santa Maria Bambina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded around the middle of the fourteenth century, it became parish church in 1443 and archipretura in 1737; the present appearance derives from an eighteenth-century restoration. It preserves paintings from the eighteenth century, a Pietà by Giovanni della Robbia and an urn with the relics of Saint Tito.

Why go. La Pietà robbiana in the parish church of the village.
