Museo del tesoro del Duomo

Museums · Themed

In the chapel of Sant'Apollonia del Duomo: reliquaries, crosses, glasses and vestments, including the green pivial of San Moderano (perhaps byzantine from the 8th to the 9th century or Lucca from the 12th), a glass of 1517 and a rare glass glass, probably of the 8th century, found in 1971 in a tomb under the presbytery.

Why go. The glass glass from a thousand years ago.
