La Casa dei Racconti

Museums · Themed

Opened from 2005 near Santa Maria Assunta, it is an antenna of the Ecomuseo della Dorsale Apenninea Umbra dedicated to the oral tradition of Valnerina: myths, legends, fairy tales and satires like the stories .Vallinates, books, audio and video, the wardrobe of memory in wood and a copper sculpture-light by Franco Pappalardo, and the photographic exhibition ...Vallo dei Racconti. Contact: tel. 0743 616143.

Why go. The stories of the mountain told by voice.
