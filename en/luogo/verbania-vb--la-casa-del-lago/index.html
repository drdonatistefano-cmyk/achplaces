La Casa del Lago

Museums · Themed

Museum of the lake and water culture, in the villa of the patriot Francesco Simonetta where Garibaldi was guest in 1862: multimedia rooms, exhibitions and workshops where samples of the lake water are analysed. Visits and workshops also on request.

Why go. The lake seen up close.
