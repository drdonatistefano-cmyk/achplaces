Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Nazzaro e Celso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Late medieval church (XV-XVI century) that houses the relics of the saints owners, remodelled in baroque forms in the Six and eighteenth centuries and brought back in 1933 to the original appearance by eliminating the baroque additions. The hut façade has horizontal bands white and black, rosette and pinnacles in terracotta; inside marble panels, golden mosaics and frescoes in Gothic style between neo-Gothic and neo-Romanesque. The baroque altar of purging souls remains, two fifteenth-century wooden statues of the patrons on the sides of a great Crucifix co-eval and the high altar surmounted by silver saints and bishops.

Why go. The neo-Gothic face with white and black bands of the mother church.
