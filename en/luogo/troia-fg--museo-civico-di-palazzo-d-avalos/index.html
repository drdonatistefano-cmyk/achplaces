Museo civico di Palazzo D'Avalos

Museums · City history

Set up since 1971 on the ground floor and in the underground of the sixteenth century Palazzo D'Avalos and opened in 1981, it is divided into five sections: on the ground floor the finds of contemporary times, in the underground those of modern, medieval, Roman and pre-Roman age.

Why go. The story of Troy from its origins to today.
