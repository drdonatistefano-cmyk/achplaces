Museo parrocchiale in Santa Maria degli Angeli

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

From 2005 the church of Santa Maria degli Angeli houses the Parish Museum of Sacred Furniture, with sacred paintings from the fifteenth century to the beginning of the seventeenth century: five small tables of the late fourteenth century with stories of the Virgin, a Flemish triptych of the sixteenth century and the Madonna dell'Accomandata by Antonello Freri (1510).

Why go. Tables of the fourteenth century and the Flemish triptych.

  • Where Via Guglielmo Siracusa 31, Castroreale
  • Entry €2
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
