Museo territoriale di Nova Ponente (Castel Thurn)

Museums · Old masters

In the 13th-century Romanesque tower of Castel Thurn, annexed to the current town hall, former residence and seat of judicial hearings around 1341, passed to the Municipality in 1911 that restored and opened it to the public. It exhibits sacred art: ancient altarpieces and consecrated tables of the sixteenth century, statues of saints of the first Baroque and religious paintings that tell the history of the Val d'Ega.

Why go. Sacred art of the valley inside a medieval tower.
