Sagra del Marrone e Cristo Morto

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

During the last two weekends of October the Festival of the Brown of Latera is held; on Good Friday the night procession of the Dead Christ runs along the streets of the country with liturgical songs of oral tradition.

Why go. Autumn browns and ancient songs.
