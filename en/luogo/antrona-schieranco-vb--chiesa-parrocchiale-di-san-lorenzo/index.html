Chiesa parrocchiale di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The ancient parish church of 1200 was buried by the landslide of 1642; the new, expanding the oratory of San Rocco, was entrusted to the foreman Bartolomeo Tami and completed in 1685, with bell tower of 1656-1660. It preserves golden wooden altars of the Carvers Gualio (Guaglio), among which the ciborium of the high altar of 1686, considered among the most beautiful works of the Piedmontese Baroque, the baptismal font recovered from the rubble, the pulpit of sculptors of the Vigezzo (1720-21) and decorations of 1887 of painters of the Vigezzo Valley, including Bernardino Peretti.

Why go. The wooden baroque reborn after the landslide.
