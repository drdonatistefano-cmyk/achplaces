Castello Sottano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the monolith in the center of the village, on the right bank of the river: of the medieval complex are perhaps only the walls of the belt; renovated by the Bourbons around 1845, it was prison until 1968 and today houses a hermitage of the Friars Minor Renovated. In the Middle Ages it was joined to the Castle Soprano by a city wall.

Why go. The second fortress, from prison to hermitage.
