Museo archeologico nazionale della Valle Camonica

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Opened in 1981 and since 11 June 2021 in a new location in the historic center, it tells in eight sections the Roman Camonica Valley. Among the main pieces the Roman marble copy of the Greek Minerva of the 5th century BC from the sanctuary of Breno, two mosaics of the thermal baths (I-II century A.D.), a larch door from Pescarzo di Capo di Ponte (end II-early I century BC), among the best preserved of the alpine arch, and frescoes with water birds from the domus of Via Palazzo. Closed Monday and Tuesday; only electronic payments.

Why go. The Minerva di Breno and the Valle Camonica Romana.

  • Where Piazzale Giacomini 2, Cividate Camuno
  • Entry €6, reduced €2 (18-25 years EU); cumulative 7 days with Naquane and MUTRE €12
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website museilombardia.cultura.gov.it
