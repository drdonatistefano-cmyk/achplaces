Belvedere «I Granati»

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

Panoramic view of the rocky slope at Stulles with views of Moso and the bottom of the Val Passiria: two structures in the shape of garnet, inspired by the rocks of the area, are an exhibition hall and a panoramic platform and tell the highest waterfall in South Tyrol, the country with the highest birth rate in Europe and the most haystack. Managed by the MuseumHinterPasseier.

Why go. The bottom of the Passiria seen from a grenade.
