Duomo di San Pietro

Places of worship · Basilicas

Neoclassical church dedicated to the patron saint, inspired by the Roman basilica of St. John Lateran: started in 1808, open to worship in 1818, completed in 1865 and parish since 1869, in 1967 received the title of Minor Papal Basilica.

Why go. The temple of the patron, with the facade looking to the Lateran.
