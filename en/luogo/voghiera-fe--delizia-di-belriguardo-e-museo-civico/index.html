Delizia di Belriguardo e Museo civico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The first of the extensive delights, begun for Niccolò III around 1435 and called the "Versailles degli Estensi"; the museum has archaeological, Renaissance sections in the Vigna Room, modern art and industrial archaeology.

Why go. One of the most loved residences by Lucrezia Borgia.
