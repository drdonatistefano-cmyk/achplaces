Covolo di Camposilvano

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Ancient large cave in the Ammonitic Red whose vault collapsed, forming an amphitheatre basin over 80 meters deep, almost entirely covered with vegetation; a steps path about 500 meters from the museum leads to a panoramic balcony on the entrance. Man's shelter for at least 50-70,000 years, used in Roman times as a natural dispensation and in the Middle Ages; many historians consider it a possible source of inspiration for Hell and Dante's Cocito. Access only from the museum, at the same times.

Why go. The chasm that would inspire Dante.
