Stazione idroterapica di Monte Grimano

Local life and traditions · Spas

The alkaline, sulphurous and sauce-bromo-iodic waters flowing from the slopes of nearby Mount San Paolo feed the hydrotherapy station, with herbal and mud treatments and a wellness centre for medical and aesthetic treatments.

Why go. The thermal waters that give the name to the country.
