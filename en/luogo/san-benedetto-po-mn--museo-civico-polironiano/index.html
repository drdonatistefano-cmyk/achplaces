Museo civico Polironiano

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Founded in 1977 in the former dormitories, library and scriptorium of the monastery, it is one of the major ethnographic museums in Italy: about 12,000 objects of peasant life, rural crafts and devotion, graphic and painting works by local artists of the twentieth century, a collection of puppets and ancient puppets, an archaeological section and a collection of Padan farm wagons.

Why go. The peasant civilization of Padana in a monastery.

  • Where Piazza Teofilo Folengo 22, San Benedetto Po
  • Entry €5, reduced €4; with Basilica €8, reduced €7
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
