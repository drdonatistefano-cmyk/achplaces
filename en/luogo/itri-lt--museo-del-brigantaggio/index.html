Museo del brigantaggio

Museums · Themed

Anthropological demoethanological museum inaugurated in 2003 in a former kindergarten converted with transparent facades, along the Appia route; dedicated to Michele Pezza called Fra' Diavolo, born in Itri in 1771. The path, curated by Vincenzo Pavilione, has three sections: "Ragioni della Storia" (a century of repression), "Ragioni del Mito" (flying leaves, operas, novels, films) and "Ragioni del Luogo" (the "Terra dei briganti" (the "Terra dei briganti") as heritage. MiC timetables: closed on Mondays; morning Thu, Sat and Sun 10-13, afternoon Mar-Dom 16.30-19 (winter) or 17.30-20 (summer).

Why go. The brigandage between history, myth and territory in the country of Fra' Devil.
