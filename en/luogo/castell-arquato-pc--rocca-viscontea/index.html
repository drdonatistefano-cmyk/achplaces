Rocca Viscontea

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The fourteenth-century fortress in the upper part of the village, set of some scenes of Ladyhawke, with views of the Arda Valley.

Why go. From the keep, hills and vineyards.

  • Where Piazza del Municipio, Castell'Arquato
  • Entry €8 with the guided tour of the village (Sundays and holidays); single entry to check
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
