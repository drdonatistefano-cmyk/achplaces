MuSaBa, Parco Museo Laboratorio Santa Barbara

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Outdoor museum park of about 7 hectares in the valley of the Torbido, created since 1969 by artists Nik Spatari and Hiske Maas in the ancient monastic complex of Santa Barbara and managed by the Foundation Spatari/Maas; it is also workshop where new works are born. Among olive and cypresses monumental sculptures, mosaics and installations; in the church restored the great fresco Â The dream of Jacob (240 sq m), called the Sistine Chapel Calabria, and in the cloister of the Foresteria a mosaic of 1.400 sq m. According to the official website is open every day from 10 am to 2 pm (last entrance 13), closed December 25, January 1 and Easter; guided tours of about 1 hour by booking for groups.

Why go. An entire park turned into a work of art.

  • Entry €10
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
