Castello della Contessa Adelaide – Museo civico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

11th-century castle, residence of Countess Adelaide who married Oddone di Savoia; now home of the Civic Museum. In the area also Roman remains with a monumental entrance of the first century AD.

Why go. The Countess' castle.
