Museo Diotti

Museums · Modern art

Opened in 2007 in the house-study of the neoclassical painter Giuseppe Diotti (1779-1846), director and professor of the Carrara Academy of Bergamo, is the main headquarters of the Civic Collections of Art, with works from the late eighteenth century to today of the main local artists and temporary exhibitions.

Why go. Home painting in Diotti's house.
