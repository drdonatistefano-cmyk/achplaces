Museo di pianoforti antichi

Museums · Themed

In Villa Centanin, about forty instruments (fortepiani, grand pianos, table and vertical) mainly of the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, of German, English, French and Italian builders, plus about 120 ancient music-themed prints from the sixteenth to the nineteenth century. The instruments are restored to play as originally used in concerts of the Fondazione Musicale Masiero and Centanin.

Why go. Fortepiani d'epoca restored and still played.
