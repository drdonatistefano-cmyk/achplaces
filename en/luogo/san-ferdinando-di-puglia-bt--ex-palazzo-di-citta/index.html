Ex Palazzo di Città

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

19th-century palace dated 1847, work of Apulian workers, of public property, overlooking Piazza Trieste with a second access from Piazza Giovanni Paolo II. The main body is now used as a museum.

Why go. The old nineteenth-century town hall in the heart of the country.
