Antico frantoio dell'olio

Museums · Themed

In an old renovated restaurant in the historic centre, it illustrates the phases of the processing of olives, from milling to pressing and oil harvesting. It probably dates back to the XVII-XVIII century, it was active until a few years ago (propriety of Carlo Bartoletti) and from the origins it was called "Il Montano" (The Montano), described as the largest and oldest oil mill in the area, preserves the mill, the oak beam with counterweight in stone and an old block of notes.

Why go. The historic mill of the village, millstone and beams intact.
