Palazzo Comunale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Neo-Gothic palace built in the nineteenth century by Guglielmo Rossi on medieval buildings; the council hall has frescoes by Gerardo Doctori from 1948-1949.

Why go. Another cycle of the Futurist painter.
