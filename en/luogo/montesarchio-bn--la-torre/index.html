La Torre

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Born as a watchtower and then bastion of the defensive walls of the village, it has the current appearance of the late fifteenth century, designed by Francesco di Giorgio Martini, with elements of the almond style attributed to the same architect.

Why go. The symbol of the village, above the valley.
