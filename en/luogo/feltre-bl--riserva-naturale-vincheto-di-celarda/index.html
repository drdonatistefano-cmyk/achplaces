Riserva Naturale Vincheto di Celarda

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Protected oasis of international importance on the right bank of the Piave, established in 1971, owned by the State property and managed by the Carabinieri Biodiversity of Belluno: 92 hectares of wetlands, riparian forests, meadows and shrubs with 45 species of nesting birds, eight of amphibians, hares, wild boars, deer and deer, and rare orchids in the meadows. Open 8-18.

Why go. Birdwatching and water forests along the Piave.
