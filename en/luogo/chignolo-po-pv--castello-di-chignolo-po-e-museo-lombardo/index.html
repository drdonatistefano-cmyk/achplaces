Castello di Chignolo Po e Museo lombardo del vino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Born from a medieval fortified nucleus, of which the central tower remains, it passed in 1441 from the Visconti to the Federici and then to the Cusani, owners until 1936, who between 1673 and 1730 transformed it into a villa of delight: Giovanni Ruggeri built the shelter to the north (1695-1703) and Cardinal Agostino Cusani added the four corner towers; the salon was frescoed by 1760 by Pietro Cremascoli and Bianchini, with the ceiling of the four seasons. Pope Clement XI, Napoleon and Francesco I d'Hapsburg-Lorena stayed there; around there is the French garden on terraces with the "Teatro delle Uccelliere" and in the basement the Museum of History and agricultural civilization in Lombardy - Lombard Wine Museum.

Why go. A baroque villa with frescoes and a museum of peasant civilization.

private residence, only guided tours of 60 minutes: without reservation Saturday, Sunday and public holidays, in the weekdays only groups of at least 25 people with reservation 48 hours before; forbidden to photograph inside
