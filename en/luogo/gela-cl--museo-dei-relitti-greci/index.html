Museo dei Relitti Greci

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Inaugurated in February 2026 in the Littorio Forest, where at the mouth of the river Gela stood an archaic emporium, probably also port of the Greek city, exhibits the remains of three Greek ships with their cargo: at the center the wreck The 4000 m2 room, designed by Ettore Di Mauro, has a reversed wooden roof and a room with 3D viewers.

Why go. A Greek ship of 2500 years with its cargo.

  • Where Area archeologica Bosco Littorio, Gela
  • Entry €6, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
