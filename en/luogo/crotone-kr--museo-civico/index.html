Museo civico

Museums · City history

In the Castle of Charles V, in the barracks Undercampana and in the Tower Helping: tiles and ceramics of the historic center, coins, hunting daggers and swords of the eighteenth-nineteenth century, ceramics of Gerace, Seminara and Squillace, three colubs recovered at sea and the coats of arms of noble families.

Why go. The colubs from the sea.
