Villa Mazzucchelli – Casa Museo Gianmaria Mazzucchelli

Museums · House museums

Villa of the eighteenth century with a U-shaped plan with palladian pronaos, enlarged by Federico Mazzucchelli and his son Gianmaria until 1753: the museum house preserves period furnishings and frescoes by Francesco Savani.

Why go. An 18th century villa inhabited by the collections.
