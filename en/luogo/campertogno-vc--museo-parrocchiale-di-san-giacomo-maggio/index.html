Museo parrocchiale di San Giacomo Maggiore

Museums · Old masters

Founded by Don Pier Cesare De Vecchi, it preserves about 500 items listed: paintings, golden and painted wooden sculptures, furnishings, liturgical objects and sacred goldsmithery, mostly from the oldest parish, including a wooden pyramid altar of 1660. A painting by Melchiorre D'Enrico with the votive procession of the Confraternities of Campertogno at the Sacred Mount of Varallo stands out, after the plague that killed 28 people. Visits only by reservation (tel. 366 3719002).

Why go. The sacred art treasure of Valsesian craftsmen.
