Castello Visconteo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Erected around 1355 by Bernabò Visconti, lord of Milan, and Queen of the Scala as a hunting residence and convivial encounters, she is among the best examples of fortified Visconti architecture of the fourteenth century. Square plan with four corner towers around a large courtyard, arcades on the ground floor and loggias on the upper floor; a recent restoration brought to light the original painted decorations with floral, geometric and polychrome motifs. Today it is the municipality and hosts the library in the ancient stables, exhibitions and events; open Friday morning, Saturday and Sunday morning and afternoon.

Why go. One of the best preserved Visconti castles.
