Frantoio ipogeo

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Crushed mill in the rock, where oil was once produced, part of the cave system of the carpignan basin; Annibale de Capua built the abbey of Santa Maria della Grotta on the crypt of San Giovanni and a nearby underground mill.

Why go. Where the oil was made in the ground.
