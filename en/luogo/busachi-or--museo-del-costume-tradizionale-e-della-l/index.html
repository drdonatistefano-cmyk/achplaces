Museo del costume tradizionale e della lavorazione del lino

Museums · Themed

In the deconsecrated church of San Domenico (1571), part of a Dominican monastery known as "su cunventu" , tells the linen from the cultivation to the fabric: ceremonial male and female clothes, festivities, daily newspapers, for mourning and for children, and in the side chapel and in plowing sacristy, gramole, combs, spindles, warps and frames; the path closes with local linens and embroidery. Organizes laboratories to pass on the skills related to linen.

Why go. The costume of Busachi and linen, from flower to embroidery.
