Antiquarium di Castelseprio

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Opened in 2009 next to the archaeological area, it exhibits the material culture of the site from the protohistoric settlement to the abandonment, with the main findings of the excavations.

Why go. To see closely the objects emerged from the castrum.

  • Where Via Castelvecchio 1513, Castelseprio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
