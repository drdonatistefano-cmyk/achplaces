Gallerie d'Italia - Palazzo Leoni Montanari

Museums · Old masters

The baroque palace transformed in 1999 by Intesa Sanpaolo in the Gallerie d'Italia: three art collections including paintings of the eighteenth century Venetian; free entry on the first Sunday of the month.

Why go. The eighteenth century Venetian in a baroque building.
