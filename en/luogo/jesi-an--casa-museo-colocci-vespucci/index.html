Casa museo Colocci-Vespucci

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

Residence of the Marquis Colocci Vespucci of the second half of the sixteenth century, at the highest point of the city, with furnishings, objects and paintings of one of the main aristocratic families of Jesine, related to Amerigo Vespucci; reopened in 2013. Guided tours only by reservation on the first Thursday of the month and the third weekend, at 16, 17 and 18, for groups of up to 10 people; the museum is on the fourth floor, reachable only on foot.

Why go. Amerigo Vespucci's family home.

  • Where Piazza Angelo Colocci, Jesi
  • Entry €6, reduced 6-25 years €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
