Museo Civico di Bari

Museums · City history

Born in 1913 for the centenary of the Muratian village and inaugurated in 1919, since 1977 it has been in a medieval palace house with a three-storey tower connected to the Petroni Arch: documents, weapons, archive and paintings of the Tanzi family.

Why go. The story of Bari in a house-tower.
