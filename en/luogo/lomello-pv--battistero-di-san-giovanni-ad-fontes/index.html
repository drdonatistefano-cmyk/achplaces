Battistero di San Giovanni ad Fontes

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Paid entry

To the south of the basilica, it is one of the most important buildings in Lombardy and, for Italia.it, among the masterpieces of early Christian art in Lombardy: the lower part is dated to the V-VI centuries, the upper one to the VII. Small brick building with octagonal plan with alternate circular and square niches, illuminated by eight windows and a lantern at the top of the dome; the hexagonal baptismal font with painted cross is unusual in Lombardy and typical of the area of Aquileia and Istria. Rediscovered under the road plan in 1935 and recently restored, it preserves high medieval frescoes.

Why go. One of the oldest baptisteries in Italy, almost intact.

  • Where Via Castrovecchio, Lomello
  • Entry €3 (entrance to the religious complex)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
