Casa museo Pietro Malossi

Museums · House museums

In the Borgo del Maglio, on two floors, exhibits the private collection of the antiques Pietro Malossi and works by the engraver Francesco Medici: weapons, furniture, prints, paintings, ancient books, coins and sculptures, with information panels and audioguides. In the village also parking, bar and hostel.

Why go. The collection of an antique next to the forge.
