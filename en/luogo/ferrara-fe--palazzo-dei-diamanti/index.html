Palazzo dei Diamanti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The façade of about 8,500 diamond-pointed marble bugs creates unique light games; inside the great art exhibitions.

Why go. Stop at the Angel Quadrivio to see it in a glimpse.

  • Where Corso Ercole I d'Este 21, Ferrara
  • Entry Exhibitions for a fee, price according to the exhibition (eg. €16 full, €14 reduced to beginning 2026)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.palazzodiamanti.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
