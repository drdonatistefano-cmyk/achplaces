Museo paleontologico «Luigi Boldrini»

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

In the palaeontological site of Pietrafitta, one of the most important in Umbria: fossils, about 1.5 million years ago, come from the lignite excavations for the central city of Rome in the deposits of the ancient lake Tiberino. Plants, mollusks, insects and especially vertebrates: a large mammoth (Mammuthus meridionalis), skeletons of rhinoceros, deer, archaic bisons, beavers and birds. The recovery began with Luigi Boldrini and continues with the University of Perugia. Open Mar, Mer, Thu, Sat and Sun 9-13 and 15-18.30.

Why go. A mammoth born from lignite mines.

  • Where Via Papa Giovanni XXIII, Pietrafitta, Piegaro
  • Entry €4, reduced €2, free EU under 18
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
