Cripta di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church-Crypt of Greek rite rose around the thousand under the current Via Don Minzoni, abandoned in the fifteenth century, found by chance in 1929; three apsed aisles with stone iconostasis partly collapsed. The detached frescoes are in the museum, while since 1999 the crypt houses copies of fireproof polystyrene in the original positions. You go down with a metal staircase from the side of the Doge's Palace.

Why go. A Byzantine church under the road.
