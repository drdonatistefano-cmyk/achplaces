Su Nuraxi

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1997: nuraghe and village inhabited by the ancient Bronze Age to the end of the Iron; the 12-metre-diameter mast on three floors with terrace, a four-tower bastion and a well of over 20 meters in the courtyard.

Why go. The emblem of Nuragic civilization.
