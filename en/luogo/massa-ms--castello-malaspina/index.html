Castello Malaspina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Medieval fortress transformed into a noble residence and then in prison until 1946, with internal and external frescoes and a view from the plain to the Apuans and the sea.

Why go. The most beautiful view of the city.

  • Where Via della Rocca, Massa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
