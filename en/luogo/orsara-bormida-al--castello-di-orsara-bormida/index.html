Castello di Orsara Bormida

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Guard tower mentioned around the thousand in a donation of San Guido to the Church of Acqui, enlarged after the Saracen incursions and transformed in the fourteenth century into a castle with octagonal tower; in the eighteenth century a second body with cylindrical tower made it a noble residence. Floors in majolica, furnishings and frescoes with coats of arms of the XVI-XVII centuries, cellars with brick vaults. From 1598 to 1922, from 1951 of the Remondini family, which produces wine. Visits according to the official website of the castle.

Why go. From the tower of the Thousand to the dwelling of wine.
