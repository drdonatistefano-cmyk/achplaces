Chiesa di Sant'Antimo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church just outside the village with a cycle of frescoes in the apse attributed to Antoniazzo Romano; in May the feast of Sant'Antimo is held.

Why go. The frescoes of Antoniazzo Romano.
