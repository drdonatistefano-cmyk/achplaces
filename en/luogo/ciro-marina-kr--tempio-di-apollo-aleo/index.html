Tempio di Apollo Aleo

History and architecture · Archaeology

At Punta Alice, at the mouth of Lipuda, the remains of the sanctuary that tradition binds to Filottote, founder of Krimisa. The archaic temple of the end of the sixth century BC (cell of about 27 x 7.90 m) was expanded at the end of the fourth century BC, after the conquest of Bretty, in a stone doric peripter temple of about 46 x 19 m; Paul Orsi identified it in the excavations of 1924. From here comes the acrolith of Apollo, a Greek work of 440-430 B.C.

Why go. The stones of the great sanctuary of Krimisa.
