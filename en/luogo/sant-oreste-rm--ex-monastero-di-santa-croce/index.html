Ex Monastero di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Next to Palazzo Caccia-Canali, it contains the ancient Palazzo Abbaziale; in 1598 the Augustinian cloistered nuns moved there. The inner church preserves an altarpiece with the Saints Elena, Augustine and Nicola and a wooden choir.

Why go. The Augustinian monastery next to the palace.
