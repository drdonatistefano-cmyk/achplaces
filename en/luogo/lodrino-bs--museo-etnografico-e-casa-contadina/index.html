Museo etnografico e Casa contadina

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the premises of the old primary schools of Invico, documents the daily life in Lodrino and Valle Trompia before industrialization: over a thousand objects in about 400 m2 divided into rooms on hunting and territory, haymaking and breeding, wood processing, blacksmith's forge and a collection of medical instruments of the nineteenth century.
