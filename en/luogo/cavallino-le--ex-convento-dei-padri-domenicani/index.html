Ex Convento dei Padri Domenicani

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Convent and adjacent church erected between 1625 and 1635 at the behest of the Marquis Francesco Castromediano and his wife Beatrice Acquaviva, who then donated them to the friars. Today it houses the school of excellence ISUFI of the University of Salento and the direction of the Museo Diffuso.

Why go. A seventeenth-century convent of the Castromedianos today home of the university.
