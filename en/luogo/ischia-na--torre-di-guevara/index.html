Torre di Guevara

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Also known as Torre di Michelangelo and indicated by the Municipality as a symbol of Ischia, it rises in front of the Aragonese Castle on a large green lawn, a few meters from the Scogli di Sant'Anna.

Why go. The look on the castle from the mainland.
