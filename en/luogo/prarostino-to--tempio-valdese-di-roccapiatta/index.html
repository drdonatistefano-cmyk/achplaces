Tempio valdese di Roccapiatta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Probably built in 1592-94 during the occupation of Huguenot commander Lesdiguières, on April 12, 1686 the delegates of the valleys led by Henry Arnaud, who decided to resist the armed decree of Vittorio Amedeo II, destroyed the ducal troops. Rebuilt in 1700 and reopened in 1756, it has a stone floor with inscriptions of foreign Protestant officers at the service of the Savoy.

Why go. The temple where the Waldenses chose to resist in 1686.
