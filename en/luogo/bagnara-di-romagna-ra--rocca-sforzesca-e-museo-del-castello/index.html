Rocca sforzesca e Museo del Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The fortress belonged to Visconti, Alidosi and Riario Sforza; the museum winds through salt, walkways, mast and underground, where in 2005 an archaeological area emerged.

Why go. From prehistoric origins to the castle in a single path.
