Narni sotterranea

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Under the monastery of San Domenico, the underground path of aqueducts, cisterns, tunnels and crypts with the torture hall of the Court of the Inquisition; the excavation dates back to the Etruscan era.

Why go. The city under the city.
