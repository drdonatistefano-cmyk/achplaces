Isolino Virginia, area archeologica palafitticola

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Small triangular atoll of about 9,200 sqm a few meters from the western shore of Lake Varese, separated from the mainland by the narrow called "Ticinello." In 1822 it was bought by the Duke Pompeo Litta who called it "Camilla" (Camilla), and since 1865, with the sale of the lake to Andrea Ponti, it took the name of his wife Virginia, and since 1963 it has been of the Municipality of Varese for the legacy of the Marquis Gian Felice Ponti. The excavations started after the discoveries of Abbot Stoppani have revealed inhabited by the Neolithic (4500 BC) at the Bronze Age and at the early Iron Age: it is the oldest pile-dwelling settlement of the alpine arch, UNESCO World Heritage and protected area, with open-air teaching trail, water lilies and many birds. You can only reach it by boat, with transhipment from Biandronno, locality Strencia; on the island a restaurant-bar open all year round.

Why go. An island on the lake where the first palafittic inhabitants of the Alps lived.
