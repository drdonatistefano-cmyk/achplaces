Palazzo Ducale e Torre Grimaldina

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Ancient residence of the Doges and seat of political power, today the cultural center of the city with exhibitions and events; the Grimaldina Tower was jailed of political prisoners, including Jacopo Ruffini and Niccolò Paganini.

Why go. The House of the Doges.
