Chiesa di San Zeno

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Considered one of the most important Carolingian monuments in Italy, it is a remarkable example of architecture of that time. It is part of the virtual tour of the historical sites of the municipality, with historical notes and audio narration usable as audio guide on site, also in English and German.

Why go. A rare Carolingian building on Garda.
