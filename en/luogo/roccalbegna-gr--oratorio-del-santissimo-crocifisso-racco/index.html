Oratorio del Santissimo Crocifisso (raccolta d'arte)

Museums · Old masters

Behind the church of Saints Peter and Paul, the oratory is now a museum: there is the Crucifix on a shaped table of the Sienese Luca di Tommè (about 1360) and there are often works by Francesco Nasini and Sebastiano Folli.

Why go. The fourteenth-century Crucifix of Luke of Tommè.
