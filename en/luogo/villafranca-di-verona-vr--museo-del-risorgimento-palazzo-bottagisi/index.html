Museo del Risorgimento, Palazzo Bottagisio

Museums · City history

The 18th-century manor villa of the Gandini Morelli Bugna Bottagisio, which in 1859 hosted Francesco Giuseppe and Napoleon III in the days of the Armistice of Villafranca; the Municipality bought it in 1997. The museum, born in 1959 from the exhibition for the centenary of the meeting, is on the first floor noble since 2014 and has been reordered chronologically in 2022/2023: weapons, prints and relics of the armies of the wars of independence, documents and objects of the villafranchesi who fought between 1848 and 1866 and volunteers Garibaldi; on the ground floor three rooms with paintings of the legacy Martinelli, including works from Verona of the early twentieth century. Open Saturday 15.30-18.30 and Sunday 10-12 and 15.30-18.30, closed in August.

Why go. The house where the Armistice of 1859, with the relics of the Risorgimento, was signed.
