Chiesa di Santa Maria dei Battuti

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Next to the Duomo, linked to the hospital of the Battuti founded in 1360, was rebuilt in 1493 with the portal of Pilacorte; the unique hall is decorated with fake marble squares and the apse and dome host the cycle of frescoes by Pomponio Amalteo, with Melchizedek offering bread and wine. Since 1933 it has been home to the shrine of the fallen of the First World War; the old annex house houses exhibitions and conferences.

Why go. The masterpiece of Pomponio Amalteo.
