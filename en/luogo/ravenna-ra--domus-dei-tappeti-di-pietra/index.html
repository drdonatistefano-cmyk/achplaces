Domus dei Tappeti di Pietra

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Under the church of Sant'Eufemia, the mosaic floors of a Byzantine palace, three meters below the road.

Why go. Home mosaics, not religious: another face of Ravenna.

  • Where Via Barbiani (chiesa di Sant'Eufemia), Ravenna
  • Entry €6 whole, €5 reduced, €4 reduced special
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.domusdeitappetidipietra.it
  • Opening hours official page
