Villa Alessandri

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa of the sixteenth century with a representative room, the Barchessa, frescoed in the eighteenth century; on the other side you can see the historic Mira Lanza factory, which for decades produced candles and detergents.

Why go. A frescoed boat in front of the Brenta industrial archaeology.
