Su Stampu de su Turrunu

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Natural monument in the forest of Addolì, on the border between Sadali and Seulo: a threefold karst phenomenon of swallowery, cave and rise, where the river Su Longufresu jumps for about 16 meters in a pond between limestone walls covered with moss. You can reach it with a marked path or with a guide.

Why go. A waterfall inside a cave.
