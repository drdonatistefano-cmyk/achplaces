Palazzo Lembo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Built at the end of the eighteenth century by the Lembo family on the remains of a medieval castle with towers, after the restoration it was recognized in 2011 "Meraviglia d'Italia" for the architecture and above all for the eighteenth-century frescoes of some rooms on the upper floor, combined with those of the Reggia di Caserta. On the back a hanging garden with snow and an arched walk towards a cylindrical tower; below, underground caves of uncertain dating. The municipality indicates mon-jo opening 10-13 and 14-18.

Why go. Frescoes compared to those of the Reggia and a hanging garden with snow.
