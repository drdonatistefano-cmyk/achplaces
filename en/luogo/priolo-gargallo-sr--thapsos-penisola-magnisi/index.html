Thapsos (Penisola Magnisi)

History and architecture · Archaeology

On the Magnisi Peninsula, a site of extraordinary archaeological value, remains of ancient Thapsos, one of the great commercial empori of the Bronze Age (from the end of the 15th century BC), such as Lipari, Cannatello and Ustica. According to the history of the municipality, in the second millennium BC, King Ibleon had chosen his residence there.

Why go. One of the great merchant ports of prehistoric Sicily.
