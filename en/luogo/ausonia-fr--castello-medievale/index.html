Castello medievale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built by the princes of Capua around the year 1000, it preserves imposing remains with square towers; from the strategic position on the hill you can see the Abbey of Montecassino and the Gulf of Gaeta.

Why go. View from Montecassino to the sea.
