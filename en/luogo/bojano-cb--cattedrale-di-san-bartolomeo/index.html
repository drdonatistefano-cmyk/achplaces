Cattedrale di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

The origins date back to the 11th century; it was rebuilt several times after the Middle Ages, often following devastating earthquakes, it preserves the ogival portal and the rose window of the 13th century. Next is the Doge's Palace, transformed in the eighteenth century.

Why go. The medieval cathedral of the city.
