Cappella dell'Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Chapel north of the village, with origins indicated in 1118: it preserves the canvas of the Annunciation (1717) commissioned by Cardinal Orsini to Giuseppe Castellano; restored from 1863 to 1903, in 2008 it had ceiling and six lunettes frescoed by Annunziato De Rubertis with the life of Mary.

Why go. The Annunciation of Castellano and the frescoes of 2008.
