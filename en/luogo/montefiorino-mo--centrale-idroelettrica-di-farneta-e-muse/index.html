Centrale idroelettrica di Farneta e museo dell'energia

Museums · Themed

Central built in 1928 and still in operation, about 3 km from Ponte Dolo, fed by the waters of the basins of the Dolo and the Dragon: you can visit the original plants, one of the four turbine-alternator groups and the control panels.

Why go. A 1928 plant still at work.
