Riserva naturale regionale Cascate del Verde

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The Green River, fed by perennial waters, falls in three jumps of 40, 90 and 30 meters before joining the Sangro: they are indicated as the highest waterfalls of the Apennines and, since 2016, they are in the National Inventory of ISPRA geosites together with the calanchi of Red Blades. The reserve, established in 2001, WWF oasis since 1997 and ZSC . Abetina of Rosello and Rio Verde Falls, combines mountain and Mediterranean species: white holm oaks and firs in the gorges, river prawns (with an incubator of the LIFE CRAInat project), brown trout, blackbird, pilgrim falcons and royal nibs. From the visitor center with bar start the paths; the main one has steps and slopes towards the viewpoint. Open all year until sunset; the ticket is paid when services and facilities are open.

Why go. The highest waterfalls of the Apennines.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
