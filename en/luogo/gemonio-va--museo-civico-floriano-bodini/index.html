Museo civico Floriano Bodini

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 1998 at the initiative of the Municipality of Varese and Comunità Montana della Valcuvia, it occupies an eighteenth-century rural farmhouse known as the "cà della Maria Gill" which was renovated in the nineties by architects Annig Sarian and Gianni Pozzi. He exhibited the works of Floriano Bodini, animator in the post-war era of existential 'realism and author of medals and great public works such as the Paul VI of the Sacred Mount of Varese and the Duomo of Milan and the monument to the quarrymen of Carrara; every year he hosts exhibitions of Italian and foreign artists, workshops, concerts and meetings. Open Saturday and Sunday 10.30-12.30 and 15-18, on other days by appointment.

Why go. The sculptor of Paolo VI of Milan and Varese, in his native country.

  • Where Via Marsala 11, Gemonio
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.museobodini.it
