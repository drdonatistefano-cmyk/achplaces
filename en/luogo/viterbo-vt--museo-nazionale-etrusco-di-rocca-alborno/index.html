Museo nazionale etrusco di Rocca Albornoz

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the fortress built in 1354 by Cardinal Gil Alvarez Carrillo de Albornoz, the museum dedicated to Etruscan archaeology and the oldest origins of the city.

Why go. The Etruscan origins of Viterbo.

  • Where Piazza della Rocca, Viterbo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
