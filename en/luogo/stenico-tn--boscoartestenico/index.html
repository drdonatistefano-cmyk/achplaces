BoscoArteStenico

Museums · Modern art

Museum of art in nature a few kilometers from the Terme di Comano: a ring path signposted in the woods, with works on the theme of the refuge made of natural materials, from traditional wood carvings to avant-garde installations. The path is easy, accessible to all (OPEN mark for accessible tourism), with benches and fountains, and has a children's path, the Path of the little Walkers.

Why go. Contemporary art in the trees, for the whole family.
