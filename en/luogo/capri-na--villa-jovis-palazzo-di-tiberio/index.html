Villa Jovis (Palazzo di Tiberio)

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The largest of the twelve residences of Tiberius, mentioned by Tacitus, 334 meters on Mount Tiberius, at the eastern end of the island: in the center a large square with cisterns, an atrium with columns of chives, the thermal baths with apsed calidary and, on the summit to the north, the private apartments of the emperor with the terrace on the Gulf.

Why go. The emperor's house suspended on the Gulf.

  • Where Viale Amedeo Maiuri, Capri
  • Entry €6 full, €2 EU citizens 18-25 years old, free under 18
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
