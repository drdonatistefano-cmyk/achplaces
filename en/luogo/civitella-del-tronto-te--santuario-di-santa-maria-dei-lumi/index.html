Santuario di Santa Maria dei Lumi

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

On a hill facing the walls: originally dependent on the abbey of Montesanto, it passed to the Franciscans from the 13th century, who around 1466 built the present complex, still inhabited by the friars minor convents. Convent around a 14th century cloister and church with Romanesque portico; at the high altar the Madonna and Child in polychrome wood by Giovanni di Blusuccio (1489).

Why go. The 15th century wooden Madonna in front of the village.
