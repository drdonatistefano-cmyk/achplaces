Museo provinciale degli usi e costumi di Teodone

Museums · Themed

The oldest South Tyrolean regional museum, outdoors, tells of the rural life of the past: the noble residence Mair am Hof of the late seventeenth century with a baroque room, ethnographic collections of historic vehicles and agricultural tools, and a path among traditional farms, gardens, pets and craft shops.

Why go. The South Tyrolean peasant life in an outdoor museum.
