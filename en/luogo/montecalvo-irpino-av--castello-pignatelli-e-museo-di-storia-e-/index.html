Castello Pignatelli e Museo di storia e cultura montecalvese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It was quoted in a document dated 1096, named after the Pignatelli; little remains of its original appearance, because the families that possessed it adapted it to the defense. The current appearance derives from the restorations after the earthquake of 1456 and from the Carafa, feudal for over three hundred years: the arch of access to the courtyard is from 1505, the ramparts of the eighteenth century, and preserves walls and a cylindrical tower. It houses the multimedia museum with vintage photographs and testimonies of local culture; visit by reservation.

Why go. The symbol of the village, between ramparts and local memory.
