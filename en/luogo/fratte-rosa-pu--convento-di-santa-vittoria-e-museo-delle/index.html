Convento di Santa Vittoria e Museo delle terrecotte

Museums · Themed

The convent was founded in 1216 in the wake of the preaching of St. Francis; on the ground floor of the western side it houses the demoethanological Museum of Terracotta, dedicated to the craftsmanship of terracotta for which Fratte Rosa is known.

Why go. The terracotta of Fratte Rosa in a monastery of the thirteenth century.
