Torre Alemanna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The only fortified settlement of the Teutonic Order still existing in the Mediterranean area, born from the command created between 1226 and 1231 on lands donated by Frederick II: the 24-metre tower, of the 13th-XIV century, incorporates the choir of a pre-existing church with remains of a cycle of frescoes of the late thirteenth century (saints, Passion, Lignum vitae) and graffiti in German. The complex includes the Palazzo dell'Abate of 1570, the eighteenth-century loggia and the sixteenth-century church; since 2016 there has been a museum with mainly Renaissance ceramics and archaeological finds.

Why go. A Teutonic commando intact in the countryside, with frescoes from the thirteenth century.
