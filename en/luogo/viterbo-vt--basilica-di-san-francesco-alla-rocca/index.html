Basilica di San Francesco alla Rocca

Places of worship · Basilicas

Basilica minor since 1949, houses the tombs of two popes, Clement IV and Hadrian V; the Romanesque portal has tortile columns and the bell tower sails a bell of 1259.

Why go. The graves of two medieval popes.
