Museo Albino Manca

Museums · Modern art

Dedicated to the sculptor and painter Albino Manca (Tertenia 1898 - New York 1976), who succeeded in Italy and the United States: he hosts his donation of bronze, marble, chalk, medals, paintings, silver and wax, including the Sleeping Fancill and a small bronze copy of the Battery Park eagle in New York. Admission for a fee.

Why go. An artist from Tertenia who arrived in New York.
