Museo Diocesano Tridentino, Palazzo Libera

Museums · Old masters

Section of the Diocesan Museum in an eighteenth-century palace on three floors, with the patrimony of the counts Lodron, patrons of the church of Santa Maria Assunta: on the first floor the hall of the Lodron with five portraits, liturgical vestments and lace; on the second antique fabrics, jewelry and the large archive wardrobe commissioned by Carlo Ferdinando Lodron in 1692; on the third a white satin parade and taffeta embroidered in gold. From 4 July to 31 December 2026 open Friday 15-17.30 and Saturday 9-12; guided tours to museum and church by reservation.

Why go. The treasure of the Lodrons, between thirsts, gold and portraits.
