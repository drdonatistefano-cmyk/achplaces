Raccolta museale comunale

Museums · Archaeological

In the municipal seat, it collects 933 artifacts ordered by origin, from prehistory to the Sannitic and Roman age up to the modern age: among these a protome wolf-head from a public building, probably a temple, the head of a votive statuette, many tiles with stamp of Roman workshops and a stone-stone with an epigraphic corpus useful to reconstruct the history of the country.

Why go. The ancient history of Villamaina in 933 finds.
