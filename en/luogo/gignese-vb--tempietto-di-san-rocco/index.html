Tempietto di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Temple of the XVI century to the east of the village, at the junction between the ancient road Stresa-Orta and the road to Nocco. It was dedicated to the Purification of the Virgin (Madonna of Bretta) and took the name of St. Rocco from a statue of the saint who worshiped there.

Why go. A small 16th century temple on the ancient road between the two lakes.
