Polo culturale San Francesco

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The complex opened on 23 September 2007 in a 13th century convent, with auditoriums in the former Franciscan church frescoed in the 15th century, archaeological museum, library, environmental education center and botanical garden. The museum tells the valley of Aso with a plastic of about 24 meters and preserves piceni finds, including a helmet with ram protomes and accessories of the necropolis of Contrada Crocifisso (IV century BC), as well as about 500 coins.

Why go. The Piceni of the Aso Valley in a medieval convent.

open by reservation (tel. 0734 257396)
