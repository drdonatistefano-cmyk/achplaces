Museo Civico di Scienze Naturali

Museums · Science and university

Municipal museum with collections of mineralogy and natural sciences, which offers an overview of the fauna and minerals of the area. Visiting hours should be requested from the museum.

Why go. Fauna and minerals of the territory in a civic museum.
