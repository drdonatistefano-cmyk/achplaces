Chiesa dell'Immacolata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church with façade tower which preserves a Madonna with Child in white Carrara marble, Renaissance masterpiece by Francesco Laurana (1471-72).

Why go. A Madonna by Francesco Laurana.
