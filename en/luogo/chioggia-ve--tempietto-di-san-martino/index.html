Tempietto di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church with octagonal dome in Venetian Gothic style next to the Cathedral; it conserved a polyptych attributed to Paolo Veneziano, today exhibited in the Diocesan Museum.

Why go. A Venetian Gothic jewel next to the cathedral.
