Marrone di Segni, fregnaquanti e appallocco

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The brown of Segni is celebrated at the end of October with the Festival of Brown and the Copeta procession; on the table the fregnaquanti, very thin egg pasta with meat sauce, the appal of cooked fava beans crushed with garlic, oil and chilli, and the black truffle of Lepini.

Why go. Chestnuts, egg pasta and Lepini truffles.
