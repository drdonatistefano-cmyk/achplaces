Presepe meccanico di Pallerone

Local life and traditions · Crafts

One of the oldest mechanical nativity scenes in Italy; a small museum nearby preserves historical pieces, including the engine of a 1937 representation, and a photo gallery.

Why go. One of the oldest mechanical nativity scenes in Italy.
