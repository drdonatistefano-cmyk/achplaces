Area avifauna e centro recupero rapaci

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Above the country, recovery aviaries and settings with veterinary surgery for injured birds: four cages for specimens no longer liberable and a large aviary where those healed train before release, often public. You can see from close up the royal eagle, buzzard, pilgrim falcon, kestrel, scatter, astore and allocco; they must be approached in silence without touching the nets.

Why go. Eagles and falcons a few meters away, in the village of eagles.
