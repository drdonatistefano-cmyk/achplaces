Abbazia di Santa Croce dei Conti

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Built at the end of the 12th century by the Counts Atti for the Camaldolese monks with stones of Sentinum, it is one of the most important Romanesque buildings of the Marche: an inscribed Greek cross plan, four pillars with semi-colons of granite, capitals of Lombard taste (one with the Crucifixion), narthex with carved portal; it contains frescoes of the Fabrian school, the altarpiece of San Benedetto di Agabiti (1524) and a 17th century carved and gilded palotto.

Why go. The great Romanesque Marche.
