Polo Monumentale Colle del Duomo – Museo Colle del Duomo

Museums · Old masters

The Museum inaugurated in 2000 preserves the historical and artistic heritage of the Diocese in three sections: archaeological, historical-artistic and sacred art, the "Tesoro dei Papi" with vestments belonging to John XXI and Pius IX.

Why go. The treasures of the Viterbo popes.
