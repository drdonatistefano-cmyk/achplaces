Abbazia di San Paolo Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Romanesque church of the 11th century with a polychrome wooden group of the 16th century (Madonna with Child), a canvas of the Baciccia (17th century) with the conversion of Saint Paul and a Baroque crucifix; the bell tower is from 1914. From the back of the church you can enjoy the view of the lake.

Why go. The Baciaccia and the view of the lake.

after the earthquake of 2016 the monuments are partially usable; the card of the Park describes the state prior to the earthquake
