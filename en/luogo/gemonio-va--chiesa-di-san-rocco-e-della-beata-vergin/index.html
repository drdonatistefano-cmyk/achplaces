Chiesa di San Rocco e della Beata Vergine Addolorata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Modesta chapel in 1578 was enlarged from 1643 and became parish in 1669, inheriting the title from St. Peter; the pronao with granite columns of St. Fedelino is from 1764 and the present aspect is due to the enlargement of 1893-98 on a project by Cesare Nava. It preserves wooden furnishings by Bernardino Castelli (pulpit, group of the Addolorata, Crucifix) and decorations of about 1923-33 of Innocent Salvini and frescoes by Giovanni Battista Jemoli.

Why go. Baroque carvings by Bernardino Castelli and paintings by Innocent Salvini.
