Pieve di San Niccolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was mentioned since 1313, it became a parish in the 16th century and a parish church in 1593; transformed in the 17th and 19th centuries, a restoration has recreated nineteenth century decorations and colours. The fourteenth century remains traces in the outer walls and in the stone bell tower; the interior with three naves has the central covered by false domes, marble balustrade and square choir with dome. Around the parish church there is the medieval village.

Why go. The heart of the village among the chestnut trees.
