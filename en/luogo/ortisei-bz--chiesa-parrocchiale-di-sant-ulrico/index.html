Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Ulrico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church in Piazza Sant'Ulrico, with Renaissance and Baroque elements and refined wooden sculptures, including the statue of St. Ulric of Ludwig Moroder and the altarpiece of the Adoration of the Magi by Josef Moroder-Lusenberg. The nineteenth-century neo-Gothic nave was dismantled in 1988 and replaced by an octagonal building.

Why go. The works of the woodcarvers in the village church.
