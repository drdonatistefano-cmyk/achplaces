Duomo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Erected in the fourteenth century and extensively rebuilt, it preserves the Gothic portal, a stone sculpture and the seventeenth-century canvas of the Paradise of Battistello.

Why go. The Gothic portal and the Paradise of Battistello.
