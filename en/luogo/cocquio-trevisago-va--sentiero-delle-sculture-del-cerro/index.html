Sentiero delle sculture del Cerro

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails

Open-air museum in the woods, in an area of the Campo dei Fiori Park, with wooden sculptures made from trunks or branches broken by the local sculptor Sergio Terni: at the entrance the Guardiano del Bosco, then a squirrel with the acorn, the Witch of Cerro, the Dea Berta with his duck, a spider, a gnome, a viper, an owl and a dove, with other strains in processing. The route is flat, no longer than 800 meters long, very easy and suitable for children; from Caldana you climb to Cerro with path 17 and you can continue to Orino.

Why go. An easy walk among the carved creatures of the woods.
