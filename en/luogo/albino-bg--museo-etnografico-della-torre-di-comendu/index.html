Museo etnografico della Torre di Comenduno

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1989 by volunteers in the tower of the former Villa Briolini, where the Briolini silkiers took over the main part of the sharecropping: it tells the first half of the twentieth century on average and low Val Seriana, from the chain of wheat to the rebuilt peasant house, to the cellar and the shops of shoemaker, slut, carpenter, turner, blacksmith and watchmaker. Open on Sunday 15.30-18.30.

Why go. The peasant life of the valley in the twentieth century.
