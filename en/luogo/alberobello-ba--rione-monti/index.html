Rione Monti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest and most famous neighborhood of the trulli, with 1,030 dry stone houses and conical roofs according to the UNESCO site: once the most popular district, today almost only dedicated to tourism. In the evening, illuminated trulli create a crib effect.

Why go. Thousand trulli uphill, UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  • Where Rione Monti, Alberobello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
