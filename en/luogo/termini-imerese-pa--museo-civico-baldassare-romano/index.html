Museo civico «Baldassare Romano»

Museums · City history

Founded in 1873 in the building of one of the oldest hospitals in Sicily, established in 1376 by the will of Gregorio Saglimpi and then entrusted to the Fatebenefratelli. It exhibits prehistoric finds, Greeks from Himera, Romans (a series of marble togates, mosaics, imperial portraits), Arabs and Normans with an inscription in kufic characters; in the artistic section the fifteenth-century polyptych of Gaspare da Pesaro, a Flemish Annunciation of the sixteenth century, a sketch of St. Jerome attributed to Bernini, works by Vincenzo La Barbera and Pietro Novelli, then the nineteenth-century termite, sculptor Filippo Sgarlata and a frescoed chapel of the fifteenth century.

Why go. From Himera lions to Bernini in a medieval hospital.
