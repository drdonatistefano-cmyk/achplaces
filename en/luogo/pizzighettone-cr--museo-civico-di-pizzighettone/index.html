Museo civico di Pizzighettone

Museums · City history

Founded in 1907 with the legacy of the Cremona antiques Vincenzo Favenza, damaged after the war and reopened in 1969 in the Palazzo del Quartier Fino, next to the library. Sections of paleontology, prehistory, Roman finds (vases, pirogues, inscriptions), historical weapons, art objects, Renaissance ceramics and contemporary art.

Why go. From prehistory to modern times in a single palace.
