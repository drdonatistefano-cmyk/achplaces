Giardini di Villa Cimbrone

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Villa built in 1904 by Ernest William Beckett, today a hotel, with gardens open to the public: the Terrace of the Infinity with 18th century marble busts, the rose garden and the Tea Room with Moorish gazebo. A plaque recalls Greta Garbo's stay in the spring of 1938.

Why go. The Infinity Terrace on the Coast.
