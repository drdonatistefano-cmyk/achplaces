Palazzo del Feudatario: Raccolta di grafica contemporanea e Museo degli antichi mestieri itineranti

Museums · Themed

Great palace inside the walls wanted by Count Fabio Landriani, with severe arched portal with rustic frames. It hosts the municipal collection of contemporary, figurative and abstract graphics, often artist's proofs (Adriano Calavalle, Mario Bellagamba, Walter Piacesi, Dino Ciacci, Sandro Tombari, Mario Borghi), a work by Anselmo Bucci, a tablet by Emilio Antonioni and the bicycles of the craftsmen and street vendors from the twenties to the fifties, equipped with barbers, crockery, carpenter, shoemaker, freighter, for bread, milk and fried fish. In summer there is the Market of the Feudatario of handicrafts and antiques.

Why go. Road crafts on two wheels.
