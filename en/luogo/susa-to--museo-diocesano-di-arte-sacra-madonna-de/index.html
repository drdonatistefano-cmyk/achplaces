Museo diocesano di arte sacra (Madonna del Ponte)

Museums · Old masters

Next to the Baroque church of Our Lady of Peace or the Bridge, on the left bank of the Dora, exhibits the Treasure of the Cathedral of San Giusto and religious works of art of the valley.

Why go. The cathedral treasure.
