Villa Corner-Tiepolo Chiminelli, Museo agricolo e dell'arte conciaria

Museums · Themed

16th-century villa Corner-Tiepolo, Chiminelli today: in the western neighbourhoods more than one hundred tools and machines of the Bassanese tanneries, in the barchess more than 2,000 objects on life and work in the Venetian countryside of the past.

Why go. The work of fields and tanneries in a Venetian villa.
