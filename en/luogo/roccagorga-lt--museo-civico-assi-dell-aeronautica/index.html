Museo civico «Assi dell'Aeronautica»

Museums · Themed

Born in 1986 on the initiative of the Association Arma Aeronautica, the year of the twinning of the country with the 9th Stormo . "Francesco Baracca" today has its headquarters in the school building "Giuseppe Restaini." It exhibits a permanent exhibition on General Umberto Nobile, the diary of Francesco Baracca, showcases on the two world wars and on the War of Liberation, the reconstruction of the plane accident of 10 November 1936 in Roccagorga in which the second lieutenant pilot Andrea Fumo (of which the saber is exposed) died and a library of about 300 volumes on aviation. Visits only by appointment with the curator.

Why go. The diary of Baracca in a small town museum.
