Riserva Bosco Pantano e Oasi WWF Policoro-Herakleia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Regional reserve of about 1,200 hectares, Natura 2000 area, with one of the last slices of coastal forest flooded the peninsula: riparian forest, maquis, wetlands and dunes near the sea, over 170 species of birds, otter and nests of sea turtles. The WWF oasis (21 hectares) has a fauna recovery center; guided tours by reservation.

Why go. The last flooded forest of the Ionian Sea.
