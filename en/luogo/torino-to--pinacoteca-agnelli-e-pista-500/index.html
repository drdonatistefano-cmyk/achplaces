Pinacoteca Agnelli e Pista 500

Museums · Modern art · Viewpoint

On the roof of the Lingotto, the first large Fiat factory, the 'scrigno' with the masterpieces of the collection of Giovanni and Marella Agnelli and the track 500 with its garden.

Why go. Art on the roof of the Lingotto.
