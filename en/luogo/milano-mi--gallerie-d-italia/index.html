Gallerie d'Italia

Museums · Modern art

The museum centre of Intesa Sanpaolo between Piazza della Scala and Via Manzoni, with the 19th century Lombardy, the "Cantiere del '900" with Boccioni, de Chirico and Fontana and the garden of Alessandro Manzoni.

Why go. Nineteenth and twentieth centuries in Piazza della Scala.
