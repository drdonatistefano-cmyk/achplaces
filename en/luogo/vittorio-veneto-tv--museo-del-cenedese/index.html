Museo del Cenedese

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the Palazzo della Comunità di Serravalle (1462-1476) in Piazza Flaminio: archaeology from the Neolithic to the Longobards, the Dormitio Virginis dei Moranzone, a Madonna attributed to Jacopo Sansovino, Gemito's Aquarium, a Pinacoteca and the Gipsoteca of the victories.

Why go. Art and archaeology of the Cenedese.

  • Where Piazza Marcantonio Flaminio, Vittorio Veneto
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
