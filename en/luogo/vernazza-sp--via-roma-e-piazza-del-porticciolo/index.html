Via Roma e piazza del porticciolo

History and architecture · Villages · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The central street with shops and a permanent photographic exhibition on the flood of 2011, which ends in the colored square in front of the only marina of the Cinque Terre.

Why go. The village on the marina.

  • Where Via Roma, Vernazza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
