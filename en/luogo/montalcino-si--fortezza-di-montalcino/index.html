Fortezza di Montalcino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The fourteenth-century fortress with pentagonal plan at the highest point of the village, almost unchanged from the Middle Ages, with towers and walkway: the courtyard is free, the climb on the stands is paid.

Why go. From the stands you can see the whole Sienese countryside.

  • Where Montalcino
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
