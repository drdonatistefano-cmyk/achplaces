Palazzina Storica, Museo della Conferenza Interalleata

Museums · City history

Command building of the fortress built in 1854; on November 11, 1917, after Caporetto, King Vittorio Emanuele III brought together the Allied commanders asking not to withdraw. Today it houses the museum that recalls the encounter with documents, photographs and testimonies, and every year exhibitions of classical and contemporary art.

Why go. The place where the resistance was decided on the Piave.
