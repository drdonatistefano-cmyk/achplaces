Museo del Beato Nicola da Gesturi

Museums · Themed

In the convent of the Friars Minor Cappuccini who hosted for thirty-four years Fra Nicola da Gesturi (1882-1958): bronze sculptures dedicated to the friar, the former vows and the treasure of Saint Ignatius of Laconi.

Why go. A place of Cagliari devotion.
