Forte di San Leo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The fortress on the cliff, rebuilt for the Montefeltro, prison and place of death of the Count of Cagliostro.

Why go. The panorama of Valmarecchia is dizzying.

  • Where San Leo
  • Entry €11 whole; €2 from 18 to 25 years; free up to 17 years; with Rocca and Verucchio Museum €14,50
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
