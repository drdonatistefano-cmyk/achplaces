Miniera d'oro del Taglione

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Ancient gold mine recovered and equipped for visits with the Interreg Italia-Switzerland Mineralp project, opened to the public since 2023 with guided tours managed by Geoexplora, to enter the mountain to discover ancient places and crafts. Dates and times on the mine site.
