Museo archeologico nazionale del Melfese «Massimo Pallottino»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the castle: daunie and magnagreche ceramics, Greek and Etruscan bronzes and jewels in gold, amber and silver (VI-III century BC), medieval glass with Islamic inscriptions and Doria rooms; in the Clock Tower the Sarcophagus of Rapolla, Roman marble of the II century A.D. carved in Asia Minor.

Why go. Rapolla's sarcophagus.

  • Where Via Normanni, Melfi
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
