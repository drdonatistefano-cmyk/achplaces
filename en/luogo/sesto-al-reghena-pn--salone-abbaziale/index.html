Salone abbaziale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

It climbs from the fourteenth-century staircase with balustrade; it was the hall where the monks sang the night office and preserves the oldest painting of the complex, of the twelfth century.

Why go. The oldest painting of the abbey.
