Nugola e i vigneti della Costa Etrusca

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

Borgo that appears in 1503 in the Carta della Toscana Marittima di Leonardo da Vinci, among the hilly vineyards of the Strada del Vino della Costa Etrusca (Sangiovese and Trebbiano, with Syrah, Merlot and Vermentino); in September it hosts "September of...wine" with tastings in the cellars.

Why go. The wine of the Livorno hills.
