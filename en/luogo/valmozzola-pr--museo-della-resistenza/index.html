Museo della Resistenza

Museums · Themed

Established in the spring of 2005, in the 60th of the Liberation, and dedicated to the partisan Gian Paolo Larini, who worked as a doctor in the valley; the fruit of thirty years of research, he exhibits in chronological order, from 8 September 1943 to 9 May 1945, about 600 original pieces among objects of war, letters and photographs, over 50 oral testimonies and documents launched by the Allied planes on the Apennines.

Why go. The Resistance of the Taro, Ceno and Mozzola valleys.
