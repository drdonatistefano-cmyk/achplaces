Cisterna della rocca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Of the great fortress designed by Francesco di Giorgio Martini for Duke Federico da Montefeltro there is only the 33 meters deep cistern.

Why go. The last trace of a fortress by Francesco di Giorgio.
