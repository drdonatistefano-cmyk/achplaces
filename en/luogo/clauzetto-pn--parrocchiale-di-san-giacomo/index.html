Parrocchiale di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Built between the 13th and 14th centuries on the Balcony of Friuli, with views of the hamlet Triviât and the plain of Tagliamento; Baroque interior with main altar and lateral altars attributed to the Peschiutta family of Gemona and to local artists. It is a place of pilgrimage with the feasts of Perdon Grant and the Little Perdon.

Why go. A pilgrimage church overlooking the plain.
