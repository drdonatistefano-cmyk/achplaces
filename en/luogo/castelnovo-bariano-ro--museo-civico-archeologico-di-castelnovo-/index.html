Museo Civico Archeologico di Castelnovo Bariano

Museums · Archaeological

Three rooms that cover over three thousand years of history of Polesine: the Bronze Age with ceramics and objects in bone and horn from the palafittic inhabited of Canàr, Canova and Marola along the Tartarus; the Roman era (II century BC-II century AD) with objects of daily use, ceramics and coins; the graffiti and glazed ceramics of the XIV-XVI centuries found along the White Canal, where stood the customs tower called "Croxeta" . Open every day by reservation, 9-12 and 15-19 (tel. 0425 850202).

Why go. The palafittic villages of the Bronze Age of Tartaro, in three rooms.
