Castello di Schisò

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Medieval military fortification that preserves remains of civil and religious buildings of its time; since 2019 it is entrusted to the Archaeological Park of Naxos and Taormina.

Why go. The Middle Ages on the head of the Greek colony.
