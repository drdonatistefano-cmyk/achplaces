Gudon: chiesa di San Martino e Museo locale

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the hamlet of Gudon the local museum, founded in 1972 in the Pfleghaus, exhibits agricultural tools and artisans, a collection of paintings, sacred and profane art and documents of local history (open from Easter to All Saints). The fifteenth-century church of San Martino has the sacristy, called the Koburger chapel, frescoed by Ambrosius Gander; the Castel Summersberg, of the fourteenth century, opens the courtyard only on Monday evening in summer by reservation.

Why go. A village among castles and frescoes.

  • Where Pfleghaus, Gudon, Chiusa
  • Entry €4, 12-18 years €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
