Marina di Vietri e spiagge

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

The Marina has fine sand and shallow and sloping seabed, suitable for families, with free and equipped beaches; near the beach La Crestarella dominated by the tower, the Schiarata and the free beach of Cancelluzzo, pebbles and sand, reachable only from the sea (for example by pedal boat from the Marina).

Why go. The sea of the first pearl of the coast.
