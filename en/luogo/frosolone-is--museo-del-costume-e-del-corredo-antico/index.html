Museo del costume e del corredo antico

Museums · Themed

In Piazza Municipio, in front of the parish, exhibits wedding items, popular costumes and amulets against the evil eye. The ancient costumes come from Frosolone and its villages and many recall the colors of religious and popular festivals, such as that of Sant'Antonio.

Why go. Traditional accessories, costumes and amulets.
