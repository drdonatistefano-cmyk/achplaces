Palazzo Carotenuto e Palazzo Giugliano

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palazzo Carotenuto, from the late nineteenth century, was built by Don Ettore d'Alessio, who administered the goods of the princes of Marinis: it is a solid three-storey building with the facade divided into two symmetrical parts by a door, leading to the internal stairs and a back courtyard overlooking a large garden. Palazzo Giugliano also has a symmetrical façade, with two semicircular wings that seem to embrace the large square in front.

Why go. Two nineteenth-century palaces in the country.
