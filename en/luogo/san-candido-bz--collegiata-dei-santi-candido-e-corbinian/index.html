Collegiata dei Santi Candido e Corbiniano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Begin around 1143, when the Benedictine monastery founded in the eighth century by the Bavarian duke Tassilo III became a college of canons; it reached almost the present form around 1280, the bell tower was erected between 1320 and 1326 and in 1969 the church was restored to the form of 1250. Three naves with transept and dome at the crossroads, it preserves the fresco of the dome with Creation, the Crucifixion group of about 1250, the crypt and, on the southern portal, a fresco by Michael Pacher of about 1450.

Why go. The Romanesque masterpiece of the Eastern Alps.
