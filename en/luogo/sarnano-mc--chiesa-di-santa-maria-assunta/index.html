Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1265 and 1286, with bell tower of a century later and white stone portal with the Assumption in the bezel. He preserves works by Lorenzo d'Alessandro (1483), Niccolò Alunno, Paolo Bontulli (1530) and the Madonna of Mercy by Pietro Alemanno, who also frescoed the crypt in 1494.

Why go. Painting of the Marche of the fifteenth century in Piazza Alta.
