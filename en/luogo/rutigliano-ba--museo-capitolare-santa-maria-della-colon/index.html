Museo capitolare Santa Maria della Colonna e San Nicola

Museums · Old masters

In a 19th-century palace donated by the Settanni family, on the path of the ancient moat and overlooking the course: on the first floor there are rooms with painted ceilings for exhibitions of the capitular heritage, the second didactic workshops and restoration of wood; other paintings are exhibited in the ancient sacristies of the Matrice church.

Why go. The art of the college girl in a painted palace.
