Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1499, it has a white interior, a upper gallery with wrought iron grate and a baroque altar in gold lacquered wood from 1697 by the Palermon sculptor Pietro Bencivinni.

Why go. The golden altar of Bencivinni.
