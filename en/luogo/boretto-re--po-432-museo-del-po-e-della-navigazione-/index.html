Po 432 – Museo del Po e della navigazione interna

Museums · Themed

Museo-cantiere in buildings of the gorge near the river port, dedicated to the river and navigation: industrial archaeology with tools and machinery used especially in the first half of the twentieth century by the Civil Genio. The name recalls the 432 km that separate Boretto from the source of the Po al Monviso.

Why go. The civil genius construction site on the river.
