Castello di Issogne

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Rebuilt by Ibleto di Challant at the end of the fourteenth century and transformed a century later by Prior Giorgio di Challant into a luxurious residence between the late Gothic and early Renaissance; in the courtyard the fountain with the wrought iron pomegranate. At the end of the nineteenth century Vittorio Avondo restored decorations and furnishings.

Why go. The pomegranate fountain.
