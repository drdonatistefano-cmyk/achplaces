Casa Bruseschi

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

A well-preserved bourgeois house of the seventeenth century: the kitchen with the fogolâr, the dining room with the Galvani ceramics of Pordenone, the room, the studio of lawyer Battista Bruseschi and a room with a set. It belongs to the parish of Saints Philip and James; openings on indicated dates, information to the tourist office.

Why go. The carnival house of a bourgeois family.

  • Where Pesariis, presso Casa della Pesa, Prato Carnico
  • Entry Offer of €1 per person
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it
