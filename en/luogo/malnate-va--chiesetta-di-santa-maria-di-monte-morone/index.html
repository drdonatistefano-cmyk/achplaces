Chiesetta di Santa Maria di Monte Morone

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Of ancient Lombard origin, it rises on the homonymous hill and is dedicated to the Madonna: it preserves a sixteenth-century wooden statue of the Madonna della Cintola and remarkable frescoes.

Why go. An ancient little church on the hill of Malnate.
