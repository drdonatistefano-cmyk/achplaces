Piazza Pio II

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The square redesigned by Bernardo Rossellino, closed by the Duomo and three palaces, of the government, of the bishop and of the pope: Pienza is the only Renaissance center of Italy arrived intact until today.

Why go. You can see at a glance the idea of an ideal city.
