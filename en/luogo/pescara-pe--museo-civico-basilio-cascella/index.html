Museo civico Basilio Cascella

Museums · Modern art

In the former chromo-litographic plant founded in 1895 by Basilio Cascella, a civic museum since 1975: it preserves 550 works by a large family of artists from Pescia.

Why go. Five generations of artists in a home laboratory.
