Basilica di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Basilicas · Paid entry

Collegiate defined by Lombardy Cultural Heritage as one of the greatest achievements of the first European Romanesque architecture: built between 1025 and 1040 on the remains of a Longobard building, when the Palatine counts of Pavia settled in the castle. Latin cross plan with three naves with protruding transept and three apses, about 60 metres long and 19 metres wide, with alternating pillars and arches; facade and first spans collapsed in the unknown era and the bell tower was rebuilt in the nineteenth century. Behind the altar a staircase descends to the crypt with remains of a previous building; the tradition preserves relics of the Holy Cross donated by Guglielmo de Grossis, who died in Lomello in 1370.

Why go. One of the first examples of Lombard Romanesque, in a millennial complex.

  • Where Via Castrovecchio, Lomello
  • Entry €3 (entrance to the religious complex)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
