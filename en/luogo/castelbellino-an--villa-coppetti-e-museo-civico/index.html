Villa Coppetti e Museo Civico

Museums · Old masters

Neoclassical villa of the late eighteenth century and early nineteenth century, perhaps of the Meriggiani family, with park of tall trees. The museum exhibits Roman fragments, the Madonna of the Rosary by Benedetto Nucci, two paintings by Ernst van Schayck and a section of contemporary graphics with 260 sheets and drawings in china, from the donations Ginesi, Garibaldi Jallet and Romiti-Mosullani, the latter with material on the Garibaldi family and on Garibalda Canzio, lived in Castelbellino until 1969.

Why go. Painting of the sixteenth century, graphics and memories of Garibaldine in the villa.
