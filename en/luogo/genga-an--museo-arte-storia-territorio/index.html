Museo Arte, Storia, Territorio

Museums · Old masters

In the castle, he exhibits the masterpieces of the ancient church of San Clemente, with ample space to the works of Antonio da Fabriano, the dossal of the Madonna of Mercy by Pietro Paolo Agabiti and the original of the Canovian statue of the Valadier Temple; he also tells Hannibal of the Genga, Pope Leo XII since 1823. Open Mon, Wed, Fri 9-12; Mar and Thu 16-18.

Why go. Antonio da Fabriano and the story of Leo XII.
