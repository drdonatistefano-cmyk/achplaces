Teatro Serra di Parco Raggio

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built between the end of the nineteenth century and the beginning of the twentieth century next to Villa Fortunata, the palace that Armando Raggio had built between 1882 and 1885. It is a hybrid between auditorium and botanical greenhouse: stage in masonry with mouthpiece that preserves traces of floral decorations, iron platea separated from the stage by a curved moulding.

Why go. A theatre born in a greenhouse.
