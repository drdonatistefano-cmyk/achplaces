Sacro Monte di Varallo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The New Jerusalem is designed by the Franciscan Bernardino Caimi at the end of the fifteenth century and expanded until the seventeenth century: chapels with about 800 life-size statues, in the green and then like a city, with a style imprinted by Gaudenzio Ferrari; basilica of the Assumption. UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2003, in a nature reserve; you can also climb by cable car.

Why go. The oldest sacred theatre.
