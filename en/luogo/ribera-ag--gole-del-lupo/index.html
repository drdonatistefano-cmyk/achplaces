Gole del Lupo

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

Gorges excavated from the river Verdura among citrus groves and orchards, about 2.5 km from the city and close to the Castle of Poggidiana; from the area you can see the sea of the stretch of coast of Seccagrande.

Why go. Nature in the original state a stone's throw from the castle.
