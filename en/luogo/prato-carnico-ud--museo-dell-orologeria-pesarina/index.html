Museo dell'Orologeria Pesarina

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Three centuries of watches, from the end of the seventeenth century to today, in the village where watchmaking was for three centuries the main economic activity: eighteenth century pendulums, tower clocks, palette watches and recent production. For ongoing work at the moment it is not accessible to those with motor disabilities.

Why go. The country that built time.

  • Where Frazione Pesariis 124, Prato Carnico
  • Entry €3 whole, €1.50 reduced; free with FVGcard
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it
