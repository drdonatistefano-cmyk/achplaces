Cappella di San Grato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Dedicated to the Taumaturgo Bishop San Grato, considered of very ancient origins, it contains frescoes attributed to Giacomo di Ivrea, painter of the Gothic International, with St Andrew and St Peter; next to the romerium and an equipped area, not far from the glacial muffler

Why go. Gothic frescoes on the hill.
