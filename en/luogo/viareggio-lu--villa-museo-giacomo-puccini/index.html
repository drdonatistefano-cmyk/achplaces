Villa Museo Giacomo Puccini

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The liberty villa on the lake of Massaciuccoli where Puccini lived thirty years composing Tosca, Madama Butterfly and La Fanciulla del West, and where he is buried.

Why go. The composer's house.

  • Where Torre del Lago Puccini, Viareggio
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
