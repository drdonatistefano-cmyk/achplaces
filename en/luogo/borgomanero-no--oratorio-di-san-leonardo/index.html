Oratorio di San Leonardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was the church of the village of San Leonardo, one of the first French villages in Novara, from which the city took its first name; the pilgrims of the Via Francisca stayed there. A unique nave with semicircular apse, a hut facade and walls in square stones and river pebbles; the interior frescoes, perhaps of Romanesque inspiration, have been recently restored.

Why go. The church from which the city was born.

Visitable only on request (tel. 0322 81897)
