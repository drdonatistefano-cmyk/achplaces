Punta Linke

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Austro-Hungarian location at 3,632 meters in the Ortles-Cevedale group, among the highest and most logistically important of the front, with a double cableway system towards the valley floor of Pejo and the glacier of the Forni; the ice has preserved the almost intact preparations, which can be visited outside and inside along an equipped path.

Why go. A White War outpost returned from the ice.

Visit in the company of a guide (Trentino Grande Guerra); the page does not include updated opening periods
