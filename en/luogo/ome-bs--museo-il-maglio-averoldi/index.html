Museo Il Maglio Averoldi

Museums · Themed

A fifteenth-century forge operated by a water wheel still in operation, a rare testimony of the ...bruzafèr... the iron processing once widespread in Franciacorta: a document from 1556 mentions a "mulino della Grotta" and a date of 1430 was visible on an external wall. Between the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries it passed to the Averoldi, who produced agricultural tools there; Andrea Averoldi, among the last blacksmiths in the area, worked with his wife Aldina Barbi until 1984. Purchased by the Municipality in 1997 and open to the public in 1999, it retains its original appearance with hydraulic hammer, stone hydro wind trumpet, pulleys with wooden wheels, wheels and tools, including the 'sappa with horns'; periodically expert craftsmen show live the forging of damask steel blades.

Why go. A fifteenth-century forge still moved by water.
