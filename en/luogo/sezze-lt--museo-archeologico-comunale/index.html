Museo Archeologico Comunale

Museums · Archaeological

From the Neanderthal era to the imperial era: the statue called "Fanciulla di Sezze" is a polychrome mosaic from a domus near Porta Romana, hundreds of ex vows in terracotta from a sanctuary perhaps dedicated to Junone and finds from Forum Appii on the Appia.

Why go. The Roman Silk in a museum.
