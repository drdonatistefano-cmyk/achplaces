Siti archeologici: Spelunca Orre, Paule Luturu, Perda Orrubia

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the territory the Domus de Janas of Spelunca Orre of the Neolithic, the tomb of giants of Paule Luturu and the Nuraghe Perda Orrubia of the Bronze Age, as well as the tomb of Pranu 'e Laccos.

Why go. From Neolithic to nuraghi in a few kilometers.
