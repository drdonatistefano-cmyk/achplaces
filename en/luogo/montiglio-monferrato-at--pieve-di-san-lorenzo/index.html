Pieve di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the twelfth century in the cemetery of the village; at the end of the nineteenth century instead of the three naves were obtained chapels to support once a barrel that replaced the original trusses. Capitals have proto-Christian symbols.

Why go. Romanesque capitals with proto-Christian symbols.
