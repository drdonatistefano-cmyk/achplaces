Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giorgio Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

This is the plan that began on May 7, 1565 at the initiative of Francesco Mozzoni and is parish since November 9, 1605; it reached its final appearance only in the middle of the twentieth century.

Why go. The parish church wanted by the Mozzoni, lords of the villa.
