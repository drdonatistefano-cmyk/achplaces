Castello dei Doria e Museo dell'Intreccio Mediterraneo

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Fortress of the Doria and court residence, probably built in the 13th century (the tradition indicates 1102) with the name of Castelgenovese to control the routes to Genoa; it was for some years residence of Brancaleone Doria and Eleonora d'Arborea. Taken by the Aragonese in 1448, which extended its towers and bastions, it became Aragonese Castle until the eighteenth century. Today, the museum occupies nine rooms on two levels with objects woven into vegetable fibers of Sardinia and the Mediterranean, including the junk boat on fassoi, baskets, sieves and bridles.

Why go. The castle on the gulf and the art of interweaving.
