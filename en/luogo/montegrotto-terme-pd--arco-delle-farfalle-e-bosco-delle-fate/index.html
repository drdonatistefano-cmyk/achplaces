Arco delle Farfalle e Bosco delle Fate

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

Butterfly house where hundreds of tropical butterflies fly free through tropical gardens; the complex also includes the Fairywood and a large insectarius.

Why go. A tropical garden populated with butterflies.
