Eremo di Sant'Erasmo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Complex of sanctuary and monastery of the XI-XII centuries at about 850 meters above sea level, frequented until the end of the sixties and today used as hostel. It is reached by the path of the Leccio.

Why go. A medieval hermitage on the Lepini Mountains.
