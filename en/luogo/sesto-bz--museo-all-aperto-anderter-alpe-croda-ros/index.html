Museo all'aperto Anderter Alpe (Croda Rossa)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

At 2,150 metres on the Croda Rossa are still trenches, stations and barracks of the front of the First World War between Austria-Hungary and Italy, with panels and guided tours of the Bellum Aquilarum association; the exhibition "Unvergessen" and the fort of Mitterberg are linked.

Why go. The Great War in the Dolomites of Sesto.
