Torre civica

History and architecture · Towers

Tower of the Town Hall 27,85 meters high, with an evident out of lead that makes it one of the most hanging towers in Italy; the origins would go back to the 8th century and around the Mille was consolidated outside, taking the current cylindrical shape.

Why go. One of the most pendant towers in Italy.

Damaged with the City Hall by the 2016 earthquake; contribution for the reconstruction work re-established in January 2023
