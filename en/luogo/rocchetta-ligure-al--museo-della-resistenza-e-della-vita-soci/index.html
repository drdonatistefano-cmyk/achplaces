Museo della Resistenza e della vita sociale in Val Borbera

Museums · City history

It occupies some rooms of the lower mezzanine of the seventeenth-century Palazzo Spinola, once used for defensive purposes and as accommodation of the servitude. Since 2003 it has been named after the Partisan commander Gian Battista Lazagna . Carlo , creator of the first Museo della Vallata e dei Partisani (1990). Connected to the Documentation Centre, with a library on the Resistance and interviews, photographs and original documents of the Borbera and Curone valleys. Info: Common, tel. +39 0143 90004.

Why go. The Resistance in Val Borbera told by its protagonists.
