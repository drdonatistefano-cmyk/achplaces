Via Crociferi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The scenic eighteenth-century road introduced by the arch of San Benedetto, an open-air museum of the etneo baroque with churches and convents, including San Francesco Borgia where Bellini was baptized; crossed on February 5 by the procession of Sant'Agata.

Why go. One of the most beautiful streets in Italy.

  • Where Via Crociferi, Catania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
