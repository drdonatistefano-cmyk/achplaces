Nuraghe Riu Mulinu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On a rocky spur at the top of Cabu Abbas, almost 250 meters above the Gulf of Olbia, with views up to Tavolara: circular tower of about 8 meters of diameter of the XIV-XIII century BC, surrounded by a wall of about 220 meters that encloses the rocks. Under the ladder a sacrificial pit with burning bones; in the excavations of 1936 a female bronze with amphora appeared on the head.

Why go. The nuraghe-belvedere on the gulf and on Tavolara.
