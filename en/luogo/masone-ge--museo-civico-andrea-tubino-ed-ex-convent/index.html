Museo civico Andrea Tubino ed ex convento agostiniano

Museums · Themed

Born in the seventies from the collection of the masonous Andrea Tubino, technical Italsider, and named after him in 1993, he has over 4,000 pieces on several floors of the former Augustinian convent of Piazza Castello, built on a slope with two floors towards the square and four towards the valley: ethnographic and archaeological collections, figures of the nativity scene and mechanized crib, lamps, minerals and fossils, the chapel of the convent and the reconstruction of a blacksmith's forge. It tells of the period in which the valley lived of iron processing and hosts an international photographic exhibition with works by Gianni Berengo Gardin, Fulvio Roiter, Franco Fontana and others. Tel. 010 926210, 347 1496802.

Why go. Iron, nativity scenes and great photography in a convent.
