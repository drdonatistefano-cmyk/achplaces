Rocca d'Olgisio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Isolated on a spur of erosa sandstone on the ridge between Val Tidone and Val Chiarone, it is among the most evocative and ancient castles of Piacentino: in the X and XI centuries belonged to the monks of San Savino, then to those of San Sisto until 1296, it was taken by Obizzo Landi in 1314 and sold to the Church in 1322. In the inner courtyard a well 50 meters deep; some rooms house a B&B.

Why go. A fortress on the cliff between two valleys.
