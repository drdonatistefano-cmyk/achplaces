Cripta di San Salvatore e Chiesa di San Vincenzo Ferrer

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Crypt excavated in a tufa counter, dating back to the eighth century; in the eighteenth century above the hypogeum the small church of San Vincenzo Ferrer was built.

Why go. A medieval crypt hidden under a small church.
