Mostra permanente della Grande Guerra sul Lagorai

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Set up in 1991 by the Alpine Group of Caoria, it exhibits Italian and Austro-Hungarian uniforms, weapons, equipment and personal objects of soldiers found on the field or donated by the inhabitants, with vintage photos on the life at the front and in the rear, and also tells the suffering of civilians. Open from 28 June to 13 September every day 15:30-18:30; admission included in the Trentino Guest Card.

Why go. The war on the Lagorai seen from the valley.

  • Where Caoria, Canal San Bovo
  • Entry €3 (single Ecomuseum ticket, valid in day for all sites)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
