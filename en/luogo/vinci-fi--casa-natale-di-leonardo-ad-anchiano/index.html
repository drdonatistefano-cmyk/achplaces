Casa natale di Leonardo ad Anchiano

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The house where Leonardo was born on 15 April 1452, about 3 km from Vinci, with a 3D multimedia story.

Why go. You will reach us on foot to the Green Road, 2 km dirt road between the olive trees.

  • Where Via di Anchiano, Vinci
  • Entry €7 whole, €5 reduced (with the section "Leonardo and Painting")
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website museoleonardiano.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
