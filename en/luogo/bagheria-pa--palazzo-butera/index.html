Palazzo Butera

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The oldest noble residence of Bagheria, built by Giuseppe Branciforti, Prince of Pietraperzia and Count of Raccuja, with the inscription "O Corte a Dio" at the entrance; neoclassical forms, colonnade porch, picture gallery and halls frescoed with crystal chandeliers. From its terraces start the fires of the feast of Santa Rosalia on July 14.
