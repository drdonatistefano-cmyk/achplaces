Confraternita di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built by the Compagnia dei Disciplinati between 1845 and 1854, with decorations from 1907 by Lorenzo and Costantino Mossello and gilded by Bernardo Arduino; since 1910 it houses a cave of the Virgin of Lourdes. It was the church of the Servant of God canon Francis Church.

Why go. Decorations of the early twentieth century and the cave of Lourdes.

visits on request, today used for conferences
