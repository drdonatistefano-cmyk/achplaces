Cappella di San Severo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

Next to the church of San Severo, in the district of Porta Sole, it preserves the only work left in the city of Raffaello: the fresco Trinità and Saints Benedictine and Camaldolese, completed by Perugino.

Why go. Raphael and his teacher on the same wall.

  • Where Piazza Raffaello, Perugia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
