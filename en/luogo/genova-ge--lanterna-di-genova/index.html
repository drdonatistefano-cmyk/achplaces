Lanterna di Genova

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The highest lighthouse in the Mediterranean, the second in Europe and the third oldest among those still active; at the base, in the fortifications, the museum and an urban park, with ascent to the first terrace.

Why go. The symbol of the city.
