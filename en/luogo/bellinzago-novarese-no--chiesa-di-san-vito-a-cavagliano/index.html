Chiesa di San Vito a Cavagliano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the cemetery of Cavagliano, a single nave with semicircular apse decorated outside with terracotta tiles; inside frescoes of the Gaudenzian school of the second half of the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, with the Virgin among the Apostles and a copy of that of Gaudenzio Ferrari in Varallo. Not indicated hours: information to the parish (0321 98027).

Why go. Gaudenzian frescoes in a small country church.
