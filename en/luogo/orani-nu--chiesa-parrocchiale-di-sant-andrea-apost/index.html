Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Andrea Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Among the last neoclassical churches of Sardinia, of Palladian orientation, according to project of the Nurese Giacomo Galfrè (in charge in 1867): pronao tetrastyle with Tuscanic columns on top of a monumental staircase, inside a Greek cross with dome and lantern. It preserves the altarpiece of Mario Delitala with the Gloria of Sant'Andrea, a seventeenth-century pulpit in inlaid marble and a 16th-century retablo with the Madonna enthroned with the Child.

Why go. The neoclassical temple with the altarpiece of Delitala.
