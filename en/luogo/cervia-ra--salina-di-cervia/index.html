Salina di Cervia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Over 800 hectares of salty pools with herons, Italian knights and pink flamingos; Salina Camillone still produces by hand sweet salt, Presidio Slow Food.

Why go. At sunset the salt is dyed pink.

  • Where Centro Visite Salina, Cervia
  • Entry Guided tours for a fee from the Visitor Centre (on foot or by electric boat)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
