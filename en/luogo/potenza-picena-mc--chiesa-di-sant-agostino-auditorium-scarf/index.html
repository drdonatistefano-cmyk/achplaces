Chiesa di Sant'Agostino (Auditorium Scarfiotti)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Augustinian complex occupies a large part of the old town; the Augustinians have been documented since 1250, when they had the church of Santa Maria Maddalena, to which the building has always remained titled. The current layout dates back to the mid-eighteenth century; it houses a terracotta bust of the penitent Magdalene attributed to Ambrogio Della Robbia, the large canvas of the Magdalena by Pietro Tedeschi and an eighteenth-century organ by Giovanni Fedeli. Restored, today it is the Auditorium entitled to the Oscar Ferdinando Scarfiotti award set designer, born in Potenza Picena.

Why go. La Maddalena robbiana in the church of the Augustinians.
