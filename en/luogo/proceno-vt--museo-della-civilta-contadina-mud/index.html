Museo della civiltà contadina (MUD)

Museums · Themed

Born from a collection of the eighties of the association "Guido Ascanio Sforza" and opened by the Municipality in 1993 in four halls of Palazzo Sforza, exhibits about two thousand domestic objects and tools from the 16th to the 20th century. In June 2026 it was re-assembled on the top floor of the palace in the MUD - Urban Museum Diffuso, a journey on sharecropping and agrarian culture in five sections (history, land, hands, community, future) extended to the whole village.

Why go. The peasant memory of the village, just re-arranged.

MiC card (updated 2020) says it is closed every day and run by volunteers; re-arranged in the MUD in June 2026, unpublished times
