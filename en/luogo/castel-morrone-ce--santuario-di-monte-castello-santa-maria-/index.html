Santuario di Monte Castello (Santa Maria della Misericordia)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Founded in the 17th century, it is a small tufa building with a hut façade between the Tuscan parasts and the square bell tower with a dome. The interior with a single nave has stuccoes and frescoes of the eighteenth century, including a Visitation, elliptical apse with three niches and eighteenth-century high altar; in the chapels a Madonna enthroned with Saints Chiara and Francis of the late seventeenth century, an Adoration of the shepherds of the early seventeenth century and a Transfiguration fresco.

Why go. Baroque plasters and frescoes next to the memory of Garibaldi.
