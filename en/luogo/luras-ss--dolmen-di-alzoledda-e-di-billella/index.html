Dolmen di Alzoledda e di Billella

History and architecture · Archaeology

The other two Luras dolmen: Alzoledda, inside the town, is rectangular with trapezoidal chamber, over 2,5 meters long and over 1,5 meters tall, with the background wall formed by a single slab; Billella, between the Vermentino and Nebbiolo vineyards, is about 2,5 meters long and 80 cm high, with the cover slab shaped to fit the supports.

Why go. Megalites of 5,000 years between houses and vineyards.
