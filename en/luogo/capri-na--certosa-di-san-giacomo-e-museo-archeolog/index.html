Certosa di San Giacomo e Museo archeologico

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry

A monastery from the 14th century, which later became a prison, now houses the museum dedicated to the painter Karl Wilhelm Diefenbach, the National Archaeological Museum of Capri and art exhibitions.

Why go. The 14th-century Certosa and the finds of the island.

  • Where Via Certosa, Capri
  • Entry €10 full, €2 EU citizens 18-25 years old, free under 18
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.capritourism.com
  • Opening hours official page
