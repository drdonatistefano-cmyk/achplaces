Museo civico delle ceramiche (Palazzo Trabia)

Museums · Themed

It is located in Palazzo Trabia, one of the most important noble palaces in the area, and exhibits an interesting collection of ceramics: italia.it defines it as a true temple of the traditions of Santo Stefano di Camastra, where it traces the entire ceramic history.

Why go. The history of Stefano ceramics in a noble palace.
