Complesso nuragico di Irru

History and architecture · Archaeology

Nuraghe and well temple in limestone of the X-VII century B.C.: the temple, 16 meters long and 7 meters wide, has tholos, vestibule and two atriums, with the chamber of the well 4.5 meters deep.

Why go. A temple of nuragic water in limestone.
