Giardino botanico di Pietra Corva

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

3-hectare garden founded by Dr. Antonio Ridella, at about 930-950 meters at the foot of Monte Pietra di Corvo, outskirts of dark volcanic rock. Thanks to a particular microclimate it hosts mountain, subalpine and alpine flora of the Alps, with Apennine, Pyrenean and other chains such as Himalayas and Andes; notable communities of Primula, Saxifraga, Gentiana and Iris. It is recommended to visit it with the staff, who informs about the accessibility and conditions of the trails (tel. +39 0382 597788).

Why go. Alpine flowers and distant mountains on the Pavese Apennines.

closed from 15 September 2026 for extraordinary works at the water tanks to protect amphibians, reopened in spring 2027
