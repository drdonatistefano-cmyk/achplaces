Monastero benedettino di San Daniele

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Monastery founded in 1075; the church, built in 1711 by Francesco Muttoni, is open to the public together with a panoramic loggia, from whose terrace you can see the Po Valley.

Why go. A terrace on the plain from a thousand year old monastery.
