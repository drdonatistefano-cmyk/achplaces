Museo del Carnevale «I costumi più belli di Sicilia»

Museums · Themed

It is located in the Munich Plant, a former industrial plant destroyed by a fire in 1922 and completely restored in the early 2000s, which also houses municipal offices, a conference room and the historical archive of the municipality.

Why go. The costumes of the mysterious carnival.
