Museo della ceramica Manlio Trucco

Museums · Modern art

From the avant-garde of the twentieth century to the Middle Ages, with archaeological section, futurist works, ceramic deco, furniture and paintings by Manlio Trucco, Albisole's pharmacy vases and in the garden La Battle of Agenore Fabbri of 1948.

Why go. A thousand years of pottery.
