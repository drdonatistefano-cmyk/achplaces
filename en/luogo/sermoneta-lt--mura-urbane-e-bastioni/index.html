Mura urbane e bastioni

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Renaissance belt commissioned by Onorato III Caetani in 1448 and completed in the first half of the seventeenth century, with the gates of the Well, of the Walnuts, St. Nicholas and Sorda and the bastions of the Torrenuova (at the tail of swallows) and of San Sebastiano with drawbridge. The route of about 1 km between ancient olive groves offers views from the Roman Castles to the Circeo and the Pontine Islands.

Why go. A tour of walls with views to the islands.
