Palazzo Sarcinelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace built in 1518 by a branch of the Sarcinelli Census, which housed Bona Sforza in 1556 and Enrico III of France in 1574: Renaissance façade with three levels with five arches and quadrifora porch on columns of pink marble, interior with stuccoes and monochrome friezes attributed to Conegliano craftsmen. Since 1988, the site of the gallery of modern and contemporary art and then of exhibitions on painting of the sixteenth century; in the attic the Galleria Novecento. Visits to the exhibitions.

Why go. The great exhibitions in the heart of the Contrada.
