Chiostro e percorso archeologico

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the area of the former barracks of the Carabinieri, near Piazza Vescovado, a cloister with an archaeological path around a real Roman well in the center of the garden and the recently recovered wall frescoes of the ancient oratory of San Rocco, protector from the plague.

Why go. Roman finds and frescoes found just a stone's throw from the Duomo.
