Palazzo Santucci

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in the 17th century on the ruins of the medieval fortress: in the courtyard a well dated 1632, year of completion; two stone staircases go up to a loggia with arches and the rooms preserve monumental chimneys.

Why go. The palace-fortress at the top of the village.
