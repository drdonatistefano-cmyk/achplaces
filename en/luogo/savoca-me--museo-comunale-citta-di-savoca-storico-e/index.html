Museo comunale città di Savoca storico-etno-antropologico

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Faithful reconstruction of typical environments of rural houses, renewed and expanded, with tools of peasant work, objects of daily use, the work cycles of the baker and lumberjack set up on original wooden structures and photographic documentation.

Why go. Savoca's peasant life.

  • Where Via San Michele, Savoca
  • Entry €1, reduced €0.50
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
