Chiesa romanica di San Giacomo al Langfenn

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

On the Langfenn, the highest point of the Salto plateau, at 1,525 m and visible from afar, rises on a land frequented already in prehistoric times; next to St. James, patron saint of the wayfarers, the altar with Saints Rocco and Sebastiano. You can reach it by car from the Schermoos car park or by bus 204 and 157.

Why go. A Romanesque church on the roof of the plateau.
