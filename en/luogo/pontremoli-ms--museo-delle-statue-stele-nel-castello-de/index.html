Museo delle Statue Stele nel Castello del Piagnaro

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In the 11th century castle the human figures carved in sandstone between the 4th and 1st millennium BC, among the most important testimonies of European megalithicism.

Why go. The most mysterious sculptures of Tuscany.

  • Where Via Piagnaro, Pontremoli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.statuestele.org
