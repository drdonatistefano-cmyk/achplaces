Villa Mutti Bernardelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa of the fifteenth century, now a municipal cultural centre and seat of the Museum of Arms, with a large park, baroque salons, mozzoni lunettes and, in the private chapel on the ground floor, a bas-relief of the Nativity.

Why go. Baroque salons and park in the center of the village.
