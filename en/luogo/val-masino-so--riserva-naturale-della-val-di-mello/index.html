Riserva naturale della Val di Mello

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Reserve established in 2009, indicated as the largest protected area of Lombardy: an almost flat valley floor between granite walls, suitable for all, with the Qualico pond shortly after the entrance and trails towards the Cascina Piana (1,092 m), the Playa di Rasica and the Alpe Pioda. You enter on foot from San Martino (parking space about 20 minutes; in the parking lot with charge 7 euros per day), with a summer shuttle to Panscèr.

Why go. The valley of granite and clear waters most loved in Lombardy.
