Museo Civico Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the sixteenth century Palazzo Gabrielli the finds of the necropolis of the territory, from the bronze age to the late empire, with canopies, buccheri, fetid stone statues and the real-size reproduction of the Tomb of the Infernal Quadriga.

Why go. You can see the grave even when the original is closed.

  • Where Palazzo Gabrielli, Sarteano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
