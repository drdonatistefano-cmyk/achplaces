Museo della canapa e del lavoro femminile «Fremos, travai e tero»

Museums · Themed

The museum tells of the role of women in the family and society of the past and the cultivation and processing of hemp, a long source of income for the economy of the valley, with numerous tools and artifacts and the reconstruction of period environments. It is part of the Ecomuseum Alta Valle Maira; open on request (tel. 0171 999190 or 347 1032172).

Why go. The history of women and hemp in the high Maira Valley.
