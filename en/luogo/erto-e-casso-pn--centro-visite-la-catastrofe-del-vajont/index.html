Centro visite «La catastrofe del Vajont»

Museums · City history

In the former primary schools of Erto, where the first bodies were collected and recognized in the days after the tragedy, it is one of the most comprehensive documentation centers on Vajont. On the ground floor there is the photographic exhibition "Vajont: images and memories" , the installation "Battito d'ali per la memoria" dedicated to dead children that night and a workshop with 3D models of the valley; on the first floor "A space of memory" in four sections (First, Impact, Emergency, Justice) and the sensory room "9.10.1963 L'Wave" which reconstructs the approximately 240 seconds of the catastrophe. In 2026 it opened 11-18 on weekends, every day from 24 July to 6 September and 9 October.

Why go. The most complete story of the tragedy, where it happened.
