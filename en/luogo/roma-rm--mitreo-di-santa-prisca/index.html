Mitreo di Santa Prisca

History and architecture · Archaeology

Under the church of Santa Prisca all'Aventino, the sanctuary of Mitra discovered in 1934 and destroyed around 400 A.D.; visits by reservation, currently suspended for valorization works.

Why go. Mithra's secret cult.
