Orto botanico «Maria Ansaldi» Pania di Corfino

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

At 1,370 meters, in a clearing of the beech forest of the Orecchiella Park, it gathers about 400 species of the flora of the Lucca Apennines and the Apuan Alps, including rare or endangered plants and species of medicine and popular food, divided for environments: woods, pastures, moors, bogs, gravels, rocks. Among the blooms, from spring to summer, peonies, lilies, orchids, gentian, the Apuan widow and paradise.

Why go. The flora of Apuane and Appennino in one garden.
