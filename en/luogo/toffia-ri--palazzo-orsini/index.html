Palazzo Orsini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The largest building in the historic center, built by the Orsini in the fifteenth century and today seat of the Municipality: the most visible sign of rivalry with the Colonna. Façade with crossed windows in travertine and loggia originally four arches, three of which preserved; on the noble floor, today council room, a large fireplace and two doors surmounted by the coat of arms Orsini in marble.

Why go. The Orsini Loggia.
