Enomuseo Ca' Rugate

Museums · Themed

Inside the cellar of the Tessari family, in a farmhouse of the first post-war period, exhibits more than 150 tools used in a century of vinification and follows the path from the arrival of the grape in the threshing area to the aging and bottling in the caneval; it is recognized of regional interest. The tour with tasting is booked with the wine cellar.

Why go. A century of Val d'Alpone wine in a farmhouse.
