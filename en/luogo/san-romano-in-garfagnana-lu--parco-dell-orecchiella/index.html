Parco dell'Orecchiella

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Nature reserve between Piazza al Serchio, San Romano, Sillano and Villa Collemandina, with woods of beech, chestnut and fir where they live wolf Apennines, deer, mouflon and royal eagle. There are easy paths for families, including the Bear Path of 1,020 meters with views of the Apuan Islands, the garden of mountain flowers and fences with deer and brown bears; it includes the reserve of the Pania di Corfino with its botanical garden.

Why go. Bears, wolves and eagles in the heart of the Apennines.
