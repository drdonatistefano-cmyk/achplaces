Area archeologica del Monte Vendevolo (Buso della Casara)

History and architecture · Archaeology

Roman hydraulic work to supply Atheste (Este): a system of tunnels of more than 100 meters dug in the riolite, with walls covered with large tiles, niches for the lamps and a collection basin, the water caput, from which a duct ran for about 10 km. Freed from debris by the municipality in 1970-71, when a poplar wooden boat was found there, and made it more accessible in 1999-2000.

Why go. Walk in a Roman aqueduct dug into the rock.
