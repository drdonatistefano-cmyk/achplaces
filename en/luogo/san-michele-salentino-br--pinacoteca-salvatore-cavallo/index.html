Pinacoteca «Salvatore Cavallo»

Museums · Modern art

In a large building in the center, it exhibits 260 works donated by Cavallo, artist and local collector, to which is dedicated a monographic section: paintings, sculptures and graphics, from the two small works of Lombard school to a large nucleus of the 1950s and 1960s, with works by Guglielmo Pizzirani, Remo Brindisi, Concept Pozzati, Alberto Sughi, Luigi Filocamo, Salvatore Fiume and Giulio Turcato. A detached seat in the Town Hall hosts the municipal collection in rotation.

Why go. The Novecento italiano in a collection of country.
