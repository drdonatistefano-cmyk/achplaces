Chiesa degli Apostoli e chiesa dell'Ospedale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Gothic church of the Apostles was built in 1467-1470 by master Jörg, with simple portal, pyramid bell tower and five octave choir; the small round church of the Hospital, founded after 1200 by bishop Konrad von Rodank for the hospice of the travelers, is the only part of the complex survived the floods.

Why go. Two medieval churches of the Brenner street.
