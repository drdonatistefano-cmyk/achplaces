Osservatorio astronomico Max Valier e Osservatorio solare Peter Anich

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The only astronomical observatory in South Tyrol, about 1,340 meters to San Valentino in Campo, run by volunteers of the Astrofili Max Valier association, with several telescopes and an observation platform; public tours all year round on Thursday and Friday evening (in summer from 9 pm), cancelled with covered sky. Next, the Peter Anich solar observatory with a special filter telescope to see the surface of the Sun and the sunspots (in summer visit Friday at 2 pm), at the Untereggerhof farm on San Valentino di Sopra; always mandatory booking.

Why go. The stars and the sun from the ... the village of the stars of the Val d'Ega.

  • Where Presso maso Untereggerhof, San Valentino di Sopra, Cornedo all'Isarco
  • Entry Solar Observatory: €8, children €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.sternwarte.it
