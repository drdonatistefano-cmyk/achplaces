Palazzo della Cultura Pasquino Crupi

Museums · Modern art

Opened in 2016 in the former city Brefotropio, it exhibits in a permanent way more than one hundred works of art confiscated from organized crime: the first museum reality entirely dedicated to a cultural heritage confiscated.

Why go. The art returned.
