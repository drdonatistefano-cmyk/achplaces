Museo Civico Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

Set up in the seventeenth-century monastery of Sant'Antonio da Padova, it collects numerous finds; the municipality collaborates in archaeological investigations on Teanum Apulum with its Museum and Archaeological Service.

Why go. The finds of the territory, to be combined with the visit of Teanum Apulum.
