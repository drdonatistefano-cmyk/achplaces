Chiesa di Santa Maria la Vetere

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Church founded by the Normans in 1090 in a valley of landscape and archaeological interest; of the earthquake of 1693 survived only part of the right aisle. The portal of 1506, with probably Antonello Gagini's bezel, has a canopy on lions; next there is a early Christian necropolis. It is managed by the Archaeological Park of Catania, open Saturday and Sunday 10-13.30.

Why go. The Norman church in ruins in the valley of the ancient village.
