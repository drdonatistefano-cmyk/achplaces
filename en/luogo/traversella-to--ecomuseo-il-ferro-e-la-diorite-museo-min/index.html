Ecomuseo «Il ferro e la diorite» – Museo Mineralogico e Geoparco Minerario

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Ecomuseum dedicated to the mining past of the country. The museum, in the Minerary Centre, exhibits a collection of about 300 minerals, a systematic mineralogical collection and the tools of work recovered in the mine; outside didactic paths lead to the Geoparco Minerario, where you can visit some of the galleries, the extraction well and the engine room. Accessible, with podactile and audioguide in Italian, English and LIS; in 2026 open Saturday and Sunday from May 1st to September 27th, 14-18.

Why go. Enter the tunnels of the Traversella iron mines.

  • Where Località Miniere, 10080 Traversella
  • Entry Museum + gallery €10; gallery only €8; museum only €4, reduced €2 (5-12 years)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website comune.traversella.to.it
