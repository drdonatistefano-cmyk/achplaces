Museo delle Suore Benedettine della Provvidenza

Museums · Themed

A small museum with memories of the founder, St. Benedict Cambiagio Frassinello (1791-1858), and the early years of the congregation. Tel. 010 935914.

Why go. On the trail of Saint Benedict Cambiagio.
