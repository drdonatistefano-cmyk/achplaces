Chiesa Collegiata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On the left side of Piazza del Popolo, designed by Pietro Maggi, a Latin plant with three naves. In the transept there is the banner of the miracle of the Virgin that in 1850 would stop the plague; in the crypt a reconstruction of the Cave of Lourdes.

Why go. The main church on the square.
