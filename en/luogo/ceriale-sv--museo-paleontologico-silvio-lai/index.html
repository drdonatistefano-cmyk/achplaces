Museo paleontologico «Silvio Lai»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Founded in 1993 and re-established in 2008 in modern and colorful environments designed especially for children, it is the visitor center of the Rio Torsero Reserve and exhibits fossils of the lower Pliocene collected in the reserve. The path starts from a self-guided paleontological wardrobe on fossilization and local geology, continues with reconstructions of the Pliocene seabed and fossiliferous blocks and ends in a shell-shaped room on geological time; an optical microscope shows rocks and thin sections. Outside a botanical garden on the evolution of flora; under 6 free, over 65 free at the museum.

Why go. Fossil shells millions of years ago, baby-sized.

  • Where Via Nuova di Peagna 209, fraz. Peagna, Ceriale
  • Entry €2, combined museum + guided tour of the reserve €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
