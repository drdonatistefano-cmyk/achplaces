Cripta di San Biagio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church excavated in the rocky bank, part of a monastic settlement of the twelfth century; today it is a single irregular environment with lateral entrance, but the traces indicate an ancient iconostasis. An inscription in Greek given the frescoes in 1196: it is one of the few examples of rock church decorated with Christological scenes (Annunciation, Nativity, Escape into Egypt, Presentation at the Temple, Entrance to Jerusalem), with stories of the saint, episodes of the Gospels apocryphal and saints of the East and West. Guided visits, tel. 0831 955236.

Why go. A cycle of frescoes from 1196 in a cave.
