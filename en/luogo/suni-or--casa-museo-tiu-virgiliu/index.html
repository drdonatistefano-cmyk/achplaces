Casa museo Tiu Virgiliu

Museums · House museums

Historic house near the parish with over a thousand objects, tools and machines of craftsman and farmer work between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, including a shoemaker shop and a corner of weaving.

Why go. The peasant life of the past.
