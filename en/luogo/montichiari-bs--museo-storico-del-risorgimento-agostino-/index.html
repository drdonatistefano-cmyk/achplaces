Museo storico del Risorgimento «Agostino Bianchi» – Museo delle armi

Museums · City history

Inaugurated in 1934 and re-arranged in 1983 in the former choir of the church of Santa Maria del Suffragio: documents, weapons, flags, photographs and relics from the battle of Castiglione (1796) to the Risorgimento and the world wars, including a bolt of the engine of the plane of Francesco Baracca. Open only on Sunday afternoon from April to early October.

Why go. From the Napoleonic wars to Francesco Baracca.
