Osservatorio astronomico Borgo delle Stelle

Museums · Science and university

It rises on a ground taken from organized crime, today a garrison of legality and culture: under a dome of more than three meters a professional telescope for photographs and educational activities, with filters to observe the Sun safely.

Why go. Looking at the stars on a confiscated asset.
