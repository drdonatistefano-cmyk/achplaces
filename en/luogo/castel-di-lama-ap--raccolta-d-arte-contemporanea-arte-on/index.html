Raccolta d'arte contemporanea «Arte On»

Museums · Modern art

Founded in 2001 by the artists Vittorio Amadio and Marisa Marconi, who have recovered part of the former Sacet complex, collects paintings, graphics, sculptures, ceramics and photographs of local, regional, national and international artists; it also hosts exhibitions, concerts, conferences, poetry readings, projections and dance performances.

Why go. Contemporary art in a former industrial complex.

MiC card (updated 2021) marks every day as closed and Let's Marche reports museums only partially accessible
