Chiesa della Madonna della Pieve

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the 11th century, partly rebuilt in the 13th century, reachable from via della Pieve in the village of San Pantaleo.

Why go. The oldest Romanesque in Centemilia.
