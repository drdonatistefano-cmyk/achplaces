Civita di Bagnoregio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Viewpoint

Village perched on a hill of tuff between the valleys of Rio Chiaro and Rio Torbido, almost abandoned due to the fragility of the rock, today one of the most visited in Europe.

Why go. The village suspended in the clouds.
