Museo della Battaglia

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Opened in 1938 in the municipal loggia of Ceneda on the collection of Luigi Marson: the trench, the year of Austrian occupation after Caporetto and the final battle of autumn 1918.

Why go. The end of the Great War.

  • Where Piazza Giovanni Paolo I, Vittorio Veneto
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
