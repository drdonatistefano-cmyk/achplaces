Chiesa di Sant'Eufemia e Museo Diocesano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the 10th century on the site of the residence of the Lombard dukes, with three naves with female galleries; visit from the Diocesan Museum in the bishop's enclosure.

Why go. The Romanesque with the matrons.
