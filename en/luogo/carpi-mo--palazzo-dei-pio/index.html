Palazzo dei Pio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

The castle of the lords Pio, between the fourteenth and seventeenth centuries, with towers, courtyards, frescoed rooms and civic museums, including the Museum Monument to the Deported.

Why go. Overlooking one of the largest squares in Italy.

  • Where Piazza dei Martiri 68, Carpi
  • Entry €5 whole; free on the first Sunday of the month and under 18 years; courtyard of honor free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
