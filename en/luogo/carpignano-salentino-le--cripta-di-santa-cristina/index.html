Cripta di Santa Cristina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Byzantine crypt of the Madonna delle Grazie, known as Santa Cristina (IX-XI century), dug into the rock in a system of rock houses: it is the oldest church in the country and testimony of the Greek rite. It preserves the most ancient frescoes of Puglia with known date and painter, starting from the Pantocrator of 959 attributed to Teofilatto; outside a small Baroque façade.

Why go. The oldest Byzantine frescoes in Puglia.
