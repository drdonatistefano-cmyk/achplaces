Trulli vesuviani

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

These are the typical rural houses of Poggiomarino: cubic plant, often only one room and dome roof, built in compact and waterproof Vesuvius stone. The dome caused the water or lapilli to flow in case of eruption and also served to dry the products of the countryside; the walls, solid and rough on the outside, look like bulwarks of rock.

Why go. The peasant architecture born under Vesuvius.
