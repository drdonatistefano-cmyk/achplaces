Struncatura e sagra sul lungomare

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Stunning is the fresh pasta typical of the city, proposed with anchovies, breadcrumbs and dried or spicy tomatoes with 'nduja di Spilinga; every year in August the promenade hosts the festival dedicated to her. Gioia Tauro is also known for its Clementines of Calabria PGI.

Why go. Taste the symbol dish of the Piana.
