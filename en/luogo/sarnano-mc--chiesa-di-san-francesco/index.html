Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the 14th century rebuilt in neoclassical forms; next to the former Franciscan convent, seat of the Municipality after 1861, and the Library with ancient illuminated manuscripts.

Why go. The Franciscan complex of the village.
