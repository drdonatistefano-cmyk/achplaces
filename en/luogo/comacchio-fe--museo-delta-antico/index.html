Museo Delta Antico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the Hospital of the Infirms of the eighteenth century, the story of the delta from prehistory to the Middle Ages and the entire cargo of a Roman ship found near Comacchio.

Why go. The museum that explains why Spina was here.

  • Where Via Agatopisto 3, Comacchio
  • Entry In the Comacchio Museum Pass (€17 full, €14 reduced, with boat tour in the valleys); free on the first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.museodeltaantico.com
  • Opening hours official page
