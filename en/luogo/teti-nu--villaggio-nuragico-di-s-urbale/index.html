Villaggio nuragico di S'Urbale

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On a hill overlooking the lake of Cucchinadorza, about 12 km from the village: about fifty circular huts in granite blocks with stone floors and central fireplace, once covered with poles and secluded trasches with clay and cork. The complex also includes a nuraghe and a tomb of Giants; the finds of daily life are of the ninth century BC. Admission for a fee.

Why go. Enter a nuragic village almost intact.
