Certosa e Museo di San Martino

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the Vomero from 1325, a baroque jewel with the Cuciniello nativity scene of 800 pieces, the Carrozza degli Eletti, 950 paintings of the nineteenth century and the Cloister Grande with marble skulls.

Why go. The most famous nativity scene in Naples.

  • Where Largo San Martino 5, Napoli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Opening hours official page
