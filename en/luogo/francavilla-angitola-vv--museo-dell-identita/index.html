Museo dell'identità

Museums · City history

Inaugurated in 2010 on the noble floor of Palazzo Mannacio, it has three sections: historical documents, especially of the municipal administrative activity; sacred art linked to local devotion, including a viatic piss in copper and silver from 1598; rural and social life with agricultural and craft tools, instruments of the ancient town band, domestic objects and trunks of emigrants. The route is accompanied by vintage photographs of the twentieth century and images of the territory, such as the medieval ruins of Pendino. Open Monday and Friday 9-13, Wednesday and Thursday 15.30-18.30.

Why go. The history of the country between documents, devotion and trunks of migrants.
