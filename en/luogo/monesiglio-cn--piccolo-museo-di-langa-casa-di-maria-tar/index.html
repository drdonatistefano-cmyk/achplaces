Piccolo Museo di Langa, Casa di Maria Tarditi

Museums · House museums

The house of Maria Tarditi, an elementary teacher who began writing at the age of seventy and is remembered as the last writer of the Langhe, is today a museum open to the public thanks to the publisher Araba Fenice; many of his novels are set in Monesiglio, where it also takes place in part "I Sansôssi" by Augusto Monti.

Why go. The Langhe told in Maria Tarditi's novels.
