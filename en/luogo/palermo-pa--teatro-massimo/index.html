Teatro Massimo

History and architecture · Historic theatres · Paid entry

Opened at the end of the nineteenth century in the form of a temple, the largest opera house in Italy and the third in Europe, with the symbolic Wheel painted by Luigi di Giovanni and guided tours.

Why go. A guided tour or a work.

  • Where Piazza Verdi, Palermo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
