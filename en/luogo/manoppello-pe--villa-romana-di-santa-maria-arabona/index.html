Villa romana di Santa Maria Arabona

History and architecture · Archaeology

A few tens of meters from the abbey, a site destined to productive activities in the late Roman age, with paved mosaics and a thermal system. Private area, available upon reservation (085 859 0003).

Why go. Roman mosaics next to the abbey.
