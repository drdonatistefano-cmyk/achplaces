Grotta di Selvascura (Grotta dei Templari)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In the Selvascura district, under Mount Furchiavecchia, about 3 km from the square along Via della Croce, close to the Sanctuary of the Crucifix; Fraticelli Franciscani and Templari settled there and the walls are frescoed (works dated XIII-XIV century, restored), with a wooden sculpture of 1673 by Fra' Vincenzo Pietrosanti. The last hermit died in the late 1970s; the cave is still open to visitors.
