Basilica di San Martino Vescovo

Places of worship · Basilicas

A first church was attested in 1023; the fifteenth-century building was renovated from 1659 on a design by Gerolamo Quadrio and the façade was completed in the early twentieth century on a design by Virginio Muzio; since 1922 it is a minor basilica. Three naves with eight chapels: the pulpit (1711-1751) by Gian Battista Caniana with Andrea Fantoni has four tarpaulins in Carrara marble, the Chapel of the Octagonal Rosary (1679) houses the canvas "Agar" and "Ismaele" of Piccio, the altars painted by Piazzetta and Cignaroli and on the singing house a Serassi organ of 2242 reeds.

Why go. The baroque Bergamo at its most, just a few steps from Bergamo.
