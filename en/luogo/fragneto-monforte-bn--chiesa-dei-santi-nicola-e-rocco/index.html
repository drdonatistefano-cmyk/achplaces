Chiesa dei Santi Nicola e Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish Church of the diocese of Benevento, built between 1656 and 1688. It has an isolated bell tower: on its south side is walled a marble plaque at the fallen of the two world wars, with a bronze disc depicting Victory as an armed woman in a laurel crown; the upper part recalls the 27 fallen of the Great War, the lower gravestones of the 25 of the Second World War, gathered in 1976.

Why go. The seventeenth-century mother church and the memory of the fallen of the country.
