Museo della Preistoria della Tuscia e Rocca Farnese

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Opened in 1996 in the Rocca Farnese, medieval fortress transformed into a palace for Pier Luigi Farnese, with the Courtyard of Love and the well of Antonio da Sangallo the Younger, the octagonal tower over 25 m high and a Santa Scala, replica of that of the Lateran, with sixteenth-century frescoes of the Passion. The prehistoric section exhibits finds from the Upper Lazio, from the Paleolithic site of Settecannelle to the Neolithic village of Poggio Olivaster, to the necropolis of the Bronze Age of Selvicciola and to the palafittic village of Lake Mezzano, with two golden bronze swords; on the second floor ceramics from the upper Middle Ages to the modern age, including Viterbo majolica of the fifteenth century. In the tower the Etruscan collection D'Ascenzi and the enigmatic tablet with the Caldera di Latera.

Why go. The prehistory of Tuscia in a Farnesian fortress with a panoramic terrace.

  • Where Piazza della Vittoria 11, Valentano
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
