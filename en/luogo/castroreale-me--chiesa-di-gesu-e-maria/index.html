Chiesa di Gesù e Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the fourteenth century, probably the first mother church, reached by a double stone staircase; the wooden altar, called the biscuit of the abbess, was traditionally designed by the nuns of the convent.

Why go. The altar tied to the biscuit of the abbess.
