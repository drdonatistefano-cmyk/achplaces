Civica raccolta di ceramiche rinascimentali

Museums · Archaeological

Set up on two floors in the extense castle, it exhibits ceramics from the beginning of the fifteenth century to the second half of the seventeenth century, largely recovered from the drains emptied during the restorations of the sixties: graffite, faenza majolica (sign of the extensive domination), mugs, bowls and engraved cans, with motifs related to the fertility cycle and animals. On the first floor everyday objects such as spoons and home furnishings.

Why go. Three centuries of pottery found in the wells of the Fortress.
