Palazzo Ducezio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Venue of the Town Hall in front of the Cathedral, built between 1742 and 1761 on a project by Vincenzo Sinatra, with portico of Ionic columns on three sides; inside the Hall of Mirrors, oval with stuccoes in Louis XV style; the upper floor was added in 1951.

Why go. The baroque mirror salon.

  • Where Piazza del Municipio, Noto
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
