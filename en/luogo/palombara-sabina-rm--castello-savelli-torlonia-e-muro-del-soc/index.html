Castello Savelli Torlonia e Muro del Soccorso

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Medieval castle on top of the hill, with more than one hundred rooms on about 10,000 square meters; the tower was born as a bulwark at the end of the ninth century and at the end of the fifteenth century the Savelli transformed it into a Renaissance baronial residence. Inside the Palazzo di Troilo, the Palatium degli Ottaviani (X-XI century), the Palazzo di Giacomo, three courtyards, a chapel and four towers, with sixteenth-century frescoes from the Raphael school wanted by Troilo Savelli. From the fortress starts the Wall of the Rescue, covered walk of 83 meters with 37 wounds, escape route in case of siege, accessible in full. Not indicated times: check on the site of the Municipality.

Why go. A castle of one hundred rooms with frescoes of Raphael's school.
