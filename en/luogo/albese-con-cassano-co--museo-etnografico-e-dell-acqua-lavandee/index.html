Museo etnografico e dell'acqua Lavandée

Museums · Themed

Born in 2004 in the building of 1822 that housed a wash-house porch on the ground floor and the elementary school on the first floor, it tells for themes the use of water in rural communities: tools for washing clothes, drawing, transport and domestic and agricultural use, with customs and popular beliefs; among the pieces a fire machine of the second half of the nineteenth century perfectly working. In 2018 the collections passed to the civic center Fabio Casartelli; the wash-house remains open as a permanent exhibition and hosts the municipal hostel. It offers routes to discover fountains, wells, glaciers and ancient aqueducts of the territory.

Why go. The memory of the water and the washbasins.
