Museo etnografico «La Brunetta»

Museums · Themed

In a building of an ancient rural settlement in the upper part of Torriana, on the slopes of Monte Bracco, exhibits objects from the peasant and artisan world, with exhibitions on the cultivation of chestnut and vine and on the production of wine.

Why go. The peasant civilization of Monte Bracco.
