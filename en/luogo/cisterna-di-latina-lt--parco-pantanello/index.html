Parco Pantanello

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Outside the walls of the Garden of Ninfa, it reconstructs the original environment of the Paludi Pontine and hosts events next to the literary paths of Ninfa; it is open from October to May.

Why go. The pontoon marshes as they were before the reclamation.
