Nuraghe Izzana

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

In the heart of the Valley of the Moon, one of the best preserved nuraghi of Gallura and probably the largest structure of the Bronze Age of the area: mixed type, corridor and tholos, with two entrances and a labyrinth of tunnels and corridors fruit of several construction phases.

Why go. The largest nuraghe in Gallura.
