Palazzo del Principe

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in the second half of the fifteenth century on the remains of a medieval structure, already fortified castle to defend the ancient village; on the north side a moat dug into the rock about four meters deep, on the façade the coat of arms of the Protonobilissimo with the dragon. In the courtyard the Granarie pits and the pavement that follows the medieval walls emerged from the excavations; in the underground large monolithic tanks for oil and a room-prison with the graffiti of the prisoners; on the noble floor the apartments of the princes. Today it is the seat of the municipality and cultural space; free or paid entry depending on the event.
