Area palafitticola e biotopo Fiavé-Carera

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the basin of the ancient lake Carera, formed about 14,000 years ago behind a moraine barrier and then transformed into a peat bog, remain the poles of palafittic villages inhabited between the Neolithic and the Bronze Age. Discovered in the middle of the nineteenth century during the extraction of peat and systematically investigated from 1969 to 1976 and again since 1981, the site has been UNESCO World Heritage since 2011 on the serial site "Siti palafitticoli prehistorici dell'archo alpine" . It is inside the nature reserve Carera, biotope of provincial interest and Site of Community Importance with birds, amphibians and marsh flora; you can visit freely on catwalks with information panels.

Why go. Walk among the poles of a village of the Bronze Age UNESCO heritage.
