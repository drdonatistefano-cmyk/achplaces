Museo Diocesano

Museums · Old masters

Between the Archbishop's Palace and the Cathedral: the Stauroteca, a 12th century gold reliquary cross linked by the tradition to Frederick II and the consecration of the Cathedral in 1222, a polyptych of the Annunciation of 1545, ancient glasses and paintings by Luca Giordano and Andrea Vaccaro.

Why go. The cross of Frederick II.
