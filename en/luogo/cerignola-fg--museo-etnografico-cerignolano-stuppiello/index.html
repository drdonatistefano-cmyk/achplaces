Museo etnografico cerignolano Stuppiello

Museums · Themed

Ethno-anthropological Museum designed and built by Professor Matteo Stuppiello in 1979; after several locations he found a place in a nineteenth-century building, where he exhibits in more rooms about 2,000 objects catalogued in chronological order.

Why go. Daily life and crafts of Cerignola in two thousand objects.
