Chiesa di San Francesco e Pinacoteca comunale

Museums · Old masters

The ancient Santa Maria in Castello, built around the 1000 and passed to the Franciscans in 1225, preserves Romanesque-Gothic remains and a fifteenth-century bell tower; it houses the Pinacoteca municipale. In the nearby Palazzo Persichetti-Ugolini, inside the former convent complex, there is the Pinacoteca civica with the collection of sacred art.

Why go. The Franciscan monastery became a gallery.
