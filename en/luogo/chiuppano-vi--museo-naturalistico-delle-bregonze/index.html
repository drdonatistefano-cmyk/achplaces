Museo naturalistico delle Bregonze

Museums · Science and university

Museum managed by the Naturalistic Group • Anthracotherium , born in 2001 to rediscover and enhance the hills of the Bregonze, and recognized by the Veneto Region as meeting the requirements of the museum; it wants to be a live museum related to the territory, its flora and its fauna. The name of the association recalls the Anthracotherium, whose fossil remains, some 30 million years ago, were found in Chiuppano in 1948-1949; the museum also hosts meetings on lignite mines and the fossil deposits of the Bregonze.

Why go. The fossils of the Anthracotherium and the nature of the Bregonze.
