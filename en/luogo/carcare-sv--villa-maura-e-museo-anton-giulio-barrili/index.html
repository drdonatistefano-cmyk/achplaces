Villa Maura e Museo Anton Giulio Barrili

Museums · House museums

Villa built in 1885 by writer and politician Anton Giulio Barrili, volunteer of Garibaldi, who dedicated it to his mother. On the first floor, already family apartment, four rooms with personal objects and collections of the writer: rare books, five hundred, prints, pamphlets, scores and manuscripts of his lectures at the University of Genoa; the lounges on the front house art exhibitions and the ground floor, since 1981, the civic library. Accessible even in a wheelchair.

Why go. The villa of the writer Garibaldi.
