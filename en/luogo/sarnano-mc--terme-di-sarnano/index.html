Local life and traditions · Spas

The spa uses the waters of the springs of San Giacomo, the Three Saints and others for beauty treatments and treatments; from the new complex of 2017 offers wellness center, indoor thermal pool with whirlpool and outdoor swimming pool with solarium.

Why go. Relax with pure oligomineral waters.
