Biblioteca Gambalunga

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Founded in 1619 by Alessandro Gambalunga, among the first public civic libraries in Italy: the seventeenth-century rooms in walnut, the eighteenth-century room and the Dutch globes.

Why go. One of the most beautiful libraries in the world.
