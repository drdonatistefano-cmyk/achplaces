Museo Ferroviario del Verbano

Museums · Themed

Museum born from the Verbano Express association to restore and preserve the historic railway vehicles that have served on the lines of the Verbano. Open on the second and fourth Saturdays of the month from 14:30 to 18:00, or by appointment.

Why go. Historical trains of the lines of Lake Maggiore.
