Casa del Fante e Museo del giocattolo

Museums · Themed

Building of the fourteenth century with ceilings and wooden beams and facade with two arched windows with stone posts, south of the tower-door of the ancient village of Torre Ratti. Since 30 October 2016, the Toy Museum has hosted 250 unique pieces of Pop Art inspiration made of recycled materials and donated by the painter Enrico Debenedetti. Visit by reservation.

Why go. Recycling toys in a house of the fourteenth century.
