Villa Paolina – Musei civici

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Built in 1822 by Paolina Bonaparte Borghese, it houses the Archaeological and Man Museum, the Museum of musical instruments, the monumental apartments of Paolina and the atelier of the painter Alfredo Catarsini; in front, the monument to the poet Shelley, died in a shipwreck offshore in 1822.

Why go. The villa of Paolina Bonaparte.
