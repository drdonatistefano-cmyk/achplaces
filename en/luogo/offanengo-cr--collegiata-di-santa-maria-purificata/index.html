Collegiata di Santa Maria Purificata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The present church was built in 1896, when the previous one was demolished; the documents remind churches of Mary Purified and St. Lawrence dating back to the IV-V century. The interior has three aisles separated by alternate arches with semi-colons of classical taste; the bell tower exceeds 50 meters.

Why go. The large nineteenth-century parish church with the bell tower of over 50 meters.
