Chiesa di Sant'Anna

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1954 and 1959 by the Italian-Venetian entrepreneur Filippo Gagliardi, in Gothic style; it incorporates the remains of the ancient church of Santa Sofia. Facade rich in statues and rosettes, two high bell towers (one with sundial, the other with clock), interior with three naves with lateral altars, frescoes and sculptures.

Why go. The Venezuelan cathedral of the Vallo di Diano.
