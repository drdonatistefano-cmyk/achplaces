Villa Reale di Marlia

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The villa of Elisa Baciocchi, sister of Napoleon and lady of Lucca, which gave her the present appearance in the nineteenth century, with its large park.

Why go. The park is the highlight.
