Torre di Carlo V

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

A 16th century square-plan sighting tower, also known as Turrazzo or Galilee, built against the incursions of Turkish pirates; privatised in 1863, it was rebuilt in neo-Gothic style and renamed by Charles V. It rises near the Botanical Garden.

Why go. The Pirate Tower on the Gulf.
