Santuario di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Born after the apparitions of the Archangel of 490, 492 and 493, it is a continuous pilgrimage destination: from the octagonal tower of 27 meters, 86 steps descend to the bronze gates merged in Constantinople in 1076 and to the cave with the altar and the statue of the saint; in the Lombard crypts the inscriptions of the dukes of Benevento. Longobard tracks have been a UNESCO heritage since 2011.

Why go. The Archangel Cave.
