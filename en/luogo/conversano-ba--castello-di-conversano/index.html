Castello di Conversano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Norman fortress of about 1054, on megalithic walls of the VI-IV century BC, trapezoidal plan with a tower per corner: the Norman Master Tower about 25 meters high, the cylindrical tower of Giulio Antonio Acquaviva today symbol of the city and the polygonal tower for the artillery; from the XV century residence of the Acquaviva.
