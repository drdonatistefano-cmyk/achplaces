Cattedrale

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Built in 1220, the year of the Late Romanesque bell tower, it was rebuilt in the eighteenth century. It is among the stages not to be missed in the historic center.

Why go. The Romanesque bell tower of the bishop's city.
