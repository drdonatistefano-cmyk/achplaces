Fortezza di Rocca Priora

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

One of the four castles of the municipality, fortress dating back to the time of the birth of Frederick II of Swabia. In front of Rocca Priora a Mare, there is a free beach equipped for dogs.

Why go. A fortress of the Swabian age near the sea.
