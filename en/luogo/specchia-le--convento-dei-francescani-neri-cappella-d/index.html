Convento dei Francescani Neri, cappella di Santa Caterina e cripta

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The convent dates back to 1531; the chapel of Saint Catherine of Alexandria, dating back to 1532, is frescoed with scenes of the life and martyrdom of the saint, and the crypt excavated in the rock rests on 36 slender columns in four rows, with traces of frescoes.

Why go. The crypt of the 36 columns.
