Chiesa rupestre di San Leonardo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Today, in the area of expansion of the city, until the 1950s, it stood among large olive groves. It contains well-readable frescoes, including a deep apse of great expressive power, and an almost intact iconostasis. It is part of the guided tour of the rock churches.

Why go. Byzantine iconostasis almost intact.

  • Where Massafra (ritrovo in piazza Garibaldi)
  • Entry €5 municipal ticket, plus fee of the guide
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
