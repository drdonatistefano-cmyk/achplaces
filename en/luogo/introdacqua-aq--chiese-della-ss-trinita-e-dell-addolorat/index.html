Chiese della SS. Trinità e dell'Addolorata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The SS. Trinity, completed in 1706 on the ruins of St. Panfilus submerged by the flood, preserves the "Madonna" that vèle" that runs to meet the Risen Son at Easter; the Sorrowful keeps the statues of the dead Christ and of the Sorrowful carried in procession on Good Friday.

Why go. The statues of the Easter rites.
