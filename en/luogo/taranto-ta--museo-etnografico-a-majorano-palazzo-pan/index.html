Museo etnografico «A. Majorano» – Palazzo Pantaleo

Museums · Themed

Palace built in 1770-1773 by Baron Francesco Maria Pantaleo at the western end of the Old City, with majolica floors and paintings by Domenico Carella.

Why go. Taranto traditions in a noble palace.
