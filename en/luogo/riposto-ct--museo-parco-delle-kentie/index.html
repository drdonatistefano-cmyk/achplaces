Museo Parco delle Kentie

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

Botanical garden of 9,000 m2 with more than 100 specimens of plants from the early twentieth century, a greenhouse for the thermosensitable species and a museum with fossils and plants; the laboratory preserves a herbarium of about 1,800 specimens of Etna and Sicilian species. Guided tours; times to request (tel. 095 97/02092).

Why go. A garden of hundred-year-old palm trees with etneo herbarium.
