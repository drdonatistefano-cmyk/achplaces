Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

18th century parish church with a single nave with three chapels per side and vaulted barrel; the square bell tower has terracotta decorations. It preserves a group of sixteenth-century statues of the Deposition, the relics of San Novello martyr donated by a Milanese family in the sixteenth century, a fresco of the Madonna with Child, a wooden crucifix of the fifteenth century and two seventeenth-century paintings of Carlo Cane.

Why go. Statues, frescoes and paintings by Carlo Cane.
