Pieve di San Giovanni (Santi Giovanni Battista e Martino)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Documented from the 10th century, in the center of the village of San Giovanni, owes its current appearance especially to the changes of the eighteenth century and preserves a crucifix of the fourteenth century. From the village the view ranges from the Valley of the Beaches and from the peaks of the Alpe di Catenaia to the hills of Chianti.

Why go. A fourteenth-century crucifix and a great view of the valley.
