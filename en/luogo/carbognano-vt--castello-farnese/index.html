Castello Farnese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Residency of Giulia Farnese "La Bella" (Bella Bella), sister of Pope Paul III, who ruled Carbognano from 1506 for 25 years (his name is engraved on the portal): under her the village, reduced to 25 inhabitants by pestilences, came to about 2.000 and hosted personalities like the Zuccari brothers. In August 2021 part of the vaults of the halls of the noble floor and the ground floor collapsed; the Superintendence is treating restoration and consolidation.

Why go. The residence of Giulia Farnese, heart of the village.

Part of the time collapse in August 2021, restoration and consolidation of the current Superintendence (Caput Mundi funds); visitability not indicated
