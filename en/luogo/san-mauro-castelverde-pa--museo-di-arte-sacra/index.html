Museo di Arte Sacra

Museums · Old masters

Next to the former convent of the Dominican cloisters, of which the well remains, it exhibits among other things an ostensory attributed to Cellini; on the upper floor there is the ancient apse of a church with frescoes from 1583 with scenes from the Old and New Testaments.

Why go. Frescoes of the sixteenth century and silver sacred.
