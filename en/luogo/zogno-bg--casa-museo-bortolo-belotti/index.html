Casa museo Bortolo Belotti

Museums · House museums

Villa Liberty, home of the politician, historian and jurist Bortolo Belotti, opened to the public in 2017: the study with original furnishings and the archive, also linked to friendship with D'Annunzio, and 26 paintings from the sixteenth to the twentieth century donated by the family to the Municipality. The park of about 5,000 square meters houses sculptures by Nino Galizzi.

Why go. The house of a historic Brembano between art and D'Annunzio.
