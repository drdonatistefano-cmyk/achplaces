Abbazia di San Galgano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The roofless abbey, one of the first and most notable examples of Romanesque-Gothic architecture in Italy; in summer it hosts the music festival "Summer in Music."

Why go. The sky as a ceiling.

  • Entry paid
