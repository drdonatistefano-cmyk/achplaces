Museo Ferrucciano

Museums · City history

Opened in 1931, in the fourth centenary, in the former Casa Battistini, on whose threshold according to the tradition Francesco Ferrucci was killed by Fabrizio Maramaldo at the Battle of Gavinana on August 3, 1530; re-established in 1957. It has 32 armored loans from the Bargello, 156 coins, documents and relics, a diorama of the battle, two paintings by Massimo d'Azeglio from the Brera Academy and costumes and flags of the historic Florentine Calcio; the Capanna di Ferrucci is part of it. In the square the equestrian statue of Ferrucci by Emilio Gallori; the Feruccian celebrations are held in the first ten days of August.

Why go. Armors and memories of the battle of 1530.
