Battelli sul Sentiero del Fiume

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers

The boats "Airone" and "Martin Pescatore" run from 23 places each along the Tiber between the landing places of Nazzano and Torrita Tiberina in about an hour; a longer itinerary arrives under Sant'Oreste. Along the river you can also go canoeing.

Why go. The Tiber seen from the water.
