Museo archeologico e Antiquarium

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Small exhibition space in the town hall with finds from the early Iron Age. The MiC card (updated to 2020) indicates opening from Monday to Thursday 9-12.30 and 14.30-16.30, Friday 9-12, Sunday 15-18.

Why go. The artifacts of the culture of Golasecca in the country that gives it its name.

  • Where Piazza della Libertà, Golasecca
  • Entry €2, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it
