Lenticchia di Santo Stefano di Sessanio

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Presidio Slow Food cultivated for centuries in clay soils around the village; it does not need soaking. In September, the Lentils Festival is held.

Why go. The legume symbol of the village, to taste on the spot.
