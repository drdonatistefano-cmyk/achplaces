Santo Sepolcro (Außerkirchl)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Wanted by the host Georg Paprion after the pilgrimages to the Holy Land: in 1633 the round chapel on the model of the Chapel of the Graces of Altötting, then the aisle of the Passion and in 1653 the chapel of the Holy Sepulchre, a copy of that of Jerusalem, a polygonal roundabout with dome where the light falls from above. The statues come from the workshop of the local sculptor Matthias Schranzhofer and are among the most important works of local folk art. It is part of the MIK route.

Why go. Jerusalem in miniature in Pusteria.
