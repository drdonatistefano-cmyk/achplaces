Palazzo Ducale e Museo della Città

Museums · City history

Residence of the Torelli and then of the Gonzaga, which hosted Torquato Tasso and Guercino: rebuilt by Francesco Capriani called the Volterra and Tommaso Filippi, with decorations by Bernardino Campi, was completed around 1570. It houses the City Museum with the Maldotti Quadreria, about fifty works including Luca Ferrari, Marcantonio Franceschini, Giuseppe Bazzani, a watercolor of Hayez and an oil of De Nittis, and temporary exhibitions. Open Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday 9.30-12.30 and 15.30-18.30.
