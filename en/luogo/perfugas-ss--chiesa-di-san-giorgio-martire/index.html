Chiesa di San Giorgio martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Church in red volcanite in Gothic-Catalan style of the early sixteenth century, in panoramic position in front of a nuraghe; until the nineteenth century it was called San Giorgio de Ledda and was the original seat of the great retablo. The feast of the patron saint is celebrated in late April.

Why go. The Gothic-Catalan in red stone, with a view.
