Museo nazionale di Casa Giusti

Museums · House museums

Birthplace of the poet Giuseppe Giusti (1809-1850): on the first floor antique furnishings set by Piedmont romance, on the second a large documentary section; in the library intact the best known portrait of the poet, by Giuseppe Bezzuoli; multimedia installations with readings of his verses.

Why go. The house of the satirical poet.
