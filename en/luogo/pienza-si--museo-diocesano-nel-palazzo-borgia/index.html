Museo Diocesano nel Palazzo Borgia

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The palace that Pius II donated to Cardinal Rodrigo Borgia, future Alessandro VI, today seat of the Diocesan Museum with paintings, fabrics and liturgical objects of the territory.

Why go. The Borgia Palace in Pienza.

  • Where Piazza Pio II, Pienza
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
