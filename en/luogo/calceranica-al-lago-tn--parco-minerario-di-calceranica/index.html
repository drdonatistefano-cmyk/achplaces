Parco minerario di Calceranica

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

The pyrite mine, known since the Middle Ages, was an important resource for the country until 1964; the park, inaugurated on July 29, 2008, also in memory of the miners, some of whom lost their lives, is visited along the gallery Leyla, with equipment and materials arranged in a thematic itinerary set up with the Museo Tridentino di Scienze Naturali. The network of tunnels extends for about 40 km until under Bosentino and Vattaro, but most are not accessible. At the Center of mining reception an exhibition of minerals of Trentino and lamps, open from May to August 2026 on Saturday and Sunday 17-19, free admission.

Why go. Inside the pyrite miners' gallery.
