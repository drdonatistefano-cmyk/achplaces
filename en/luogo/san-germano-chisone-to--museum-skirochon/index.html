Museum Skirochon

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated for Turin 2006 in the Villa Widemann, it tells the origin of skiing, the birth of the Sestriere station and the history of the Rochon company, an artisan workshop that between 1930 and 1960 produced wooden skis and supplied the 3rd Pinerolo Alpine Regiment, with finds and documents preserved by the last heir Dina Pons. The hall ... The Factory. Women and work are collected from the former Waldensian Museum on the cotton factory Widemann and the female condition, donated by the Waldensian Consistory in 2009. Visits by appointment (tel. 0121 58601).

Why go. The wooden skis that accompanied the birth of Sestriere.
