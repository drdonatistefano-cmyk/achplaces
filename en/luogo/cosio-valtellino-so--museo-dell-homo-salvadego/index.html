Museo dell'Homo Salvadego

Museums · Themed

In an ancient building near the parish church of Sacco, a room on the first floor is entirely frescoed: decorations, floral motifs and paperwork in Gothic characters with proverbs and moral mottos in Latin and vulgar. Among the figures stands out a man with a hairy body and a thick beard, armed with a knotty stick, who presents himself with the phrase "Ego sonto un homo salvadego per natura," who me offende ge fo paura" (the frescoes are masters Batestinus and Simon and dated 18 May 1464).

Why go. One of the most beautiful and documented images of the Wild Man, figure of Alpine mythology.
