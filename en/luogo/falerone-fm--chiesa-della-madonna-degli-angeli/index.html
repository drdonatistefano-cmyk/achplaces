Chiesa della Madonna degli Angeli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded in 1542 on a hill near the village by the Clare monks who left the convent of the Piagge; above the high altar there is a fresco by Vincenzo Pagani.

Why go. A fresco by Vincenzo Pagani.
