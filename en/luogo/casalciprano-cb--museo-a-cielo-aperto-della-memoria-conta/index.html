Museo a cielo aperto della Memoria Contadina

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Route through streets, stretches and ancient houses of the historic center, with works that combine objects of rural tradition with contemporary sculptures and paintings. Realized by the Municipality and managed by the Cultural Association Arx Prandi, it was set up in 2004 and enriched in 2010 with the painted Muri and in 2011 with the "Sofitta dei Ricordi" (Ricordi's Attic).

Why go. To discover the rural life of Molisana once walking through alleys, stairs and panoramic descents.
