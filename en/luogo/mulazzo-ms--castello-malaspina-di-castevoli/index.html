Castello Malaspina di Castevoli

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Founded around 1000 by the Estensi, it passed to the Malaspinas in 1195 and in 1306 hosted Dante; abandoned after the revolt of 1797, it was rebuilt on the ruins since 1990 by the painter and sculptor Loris Nelson Ricci and his wife Erika H. End is opened in 1998, with sculptures, paintings and architectural projects.

Why go. A reborn castle as an artist's house.
