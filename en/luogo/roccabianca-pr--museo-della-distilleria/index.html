Museo della Distilleria

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

In the ancient cellars of the castle, it tells the story of liqueurs, spirits and balsamic vinegar; it includes the seasoning salt of the culatello.

Why go. Liqueurs and culatello in the cellars of the fortress.
