Chiesa di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A church dating back to the 12th century about a kilometre from the village, built on an ancient Roman pagan temple; according to tradition it was the church of the original village, before the inhabitants moved to the present site.

Why go. The medieval church of the disappeared village.
