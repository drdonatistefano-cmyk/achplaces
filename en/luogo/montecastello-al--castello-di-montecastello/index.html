Castello di Montecastello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built in the 13th century on the remains of the oldest fortifications and renovated in the 18th century, it dominates the village from the top of the hill with a high tower and is visible from kilometers in the Alexandrian plain. Ceded in 1180 by the Bellingers to the Municipality of Alexandria, disputed for centuries, infeudated in 1535 to the Milan Press and Sabaudo since 1707; in 1745, during the Battle of Bassignana, Charles Emanuel III fixed the headquarters there, and Charles Albert stayed there in 1842-1843.

Why go. The shape of the castle above the confluence of Tanaro and Bormida.

private residence, it opens to the public on very rare occasions; the building is seen only from the outside (FAI)
