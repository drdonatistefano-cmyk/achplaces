La Cinta

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Arch of about 5 km of white and thin sand dunes that degrade towards a low and clear sea, without rocks, between juniper, marine lily and helicry; in front there are Punta Coda Cavallo and the island of Tavolara. The belt of dunes separates the sea from the lagoon of San Teodoro. There are parking lots, information points, rentals, sailing school and kitesurfing with a kite zone; you can walk to the northern outskirts of the country.

Why go. The large beach of San Teodoro, also convenient on foot from the center.
