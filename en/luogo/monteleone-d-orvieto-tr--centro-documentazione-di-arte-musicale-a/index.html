Centro documentazione di arte musicale «Attilio Parelli»

Museums · Themed

Museum dedicated to the composer and conductor Attilio Parelli (1874-1944): more than one hundred original manuscripts, including The Lovers, Hermes and Fanfulla of Lodi, also printed scores of colleagues with dedications and autographs, booklets, family photographs, diplomas and documents, donated by the heirs to the municipality.

Why go. The life of a musician in his manuscripts.
