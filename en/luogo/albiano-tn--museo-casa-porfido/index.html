Museo Casa Porfido

Museums · Themed

A small modern museum dedicated to the extraction of porphyry, the resistant stone used in urban furnishings: with multimedia tools and an interactive itinerary, it tells the geology of the territory, the ancient history of porphyry and the socio-economic context of quarries. Visits only by reservation (tel. 0461 689799); for singles and small groups every first Friday of the month, I meet at 13:45.

Why go. Understand the stone that paves squares all over the world.
