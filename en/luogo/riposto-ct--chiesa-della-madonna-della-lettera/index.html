Chiesa della Madonna della Lettera

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest church of Riposto, built on the remains of the so-called "Anticaglia di S. John little, perhaps of proto-Christian origin; the present aspect dates back to 1868 and preserves numerous works of art. The strong piece is the crypt, with artifacts of the Arab-Norman, Byzantine and Roman ages and foundations of 1100.

Why go. A crypt that goes through a thousand years of history.
