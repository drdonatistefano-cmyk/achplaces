Palazzo comunale e Pinacoteca comunale

Museums · Old masters

The Palazzo Comunale, dating back to the XVI-XVII centuries, houses the Pinacoteca Comunale on the noble floor, in the Sala del Consiglio, in the Sala degli Stemmi and in other rooms.

Why go. The noble halls of the town hall.
