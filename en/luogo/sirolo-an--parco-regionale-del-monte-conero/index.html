Parco regionale del Monte Conero

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Regional park established at the end of the 1980s thanks to the committees of citizens and environmental associations. It offers about 20 hiking, horseback and mountain biking trails, including the crossing of the promontory (8 km), the rock engravings (700 m), the Roman caves and a hidden quarry (2.3 km), and the panoramic trail on the Two Sisters (5.4 km). The visitor centre of the park in Sirolo hosts the reconstruction of the Queen's Tomb.

Why go. Walking paths overlooking the sea on one of the most beautiful promontories of the Adriatic.
