Giardini di Villa Centofinestre

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

The eighteenth-century villa was founded as a "Casino di Delizie" by the Marquis Accoretti and transformed in the following century by the Carradori, on the road from San Biagio to Filottrano: Italian gardens, a greenery of symmetrical cypresses, hunting forest with stakeout towers, greenhouses and groves.

Why go. A verzure theater among cypresses.
