Museo della Biodiversità - CITES

Museums · Themed

Inside the Municipal Library, it exhibits in showcases about 150 specimens of animals or their parts protected by the Washington Convention (CITES), in force in Italy since 1980; the route is designed for children, families and schools. Hours from the MiC card: Monday and Wednesday 9-13 and 16-18, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 9-13, Saturday and Sunday closed.

Why go. A small museum on the protection of protected species.
