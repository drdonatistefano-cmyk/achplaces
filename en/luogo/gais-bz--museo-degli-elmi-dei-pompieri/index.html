Museo degli elmi dei pompieri

Museums · Themed

About 200 m2 with more than 700 fire helmets from all over the world: collection pieces in leather, steel and brass, helmets from Egypt, Thailand, Guatemala and New York, and the French metal helmets, particularly elaborate and often decorated with feathers. Visits from 10 am to 6 pm only by telephone (+39 0474 504117).

Why go. A curious and unique collection of its kind.
