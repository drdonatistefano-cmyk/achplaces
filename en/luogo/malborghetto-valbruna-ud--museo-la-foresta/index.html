Museo La Foresta

Museums · Science and university

Naturalistic Museum founded in 1988 by Società Autostrade e Corpo Forestale dello Stato, dedicated to ecology, flora and fauna of the 23,000 hectares of the Tarvisio Forest, with educational exhibition and outdoor botanical garden with path of about 500 meters; trails lead to the sulphurous springs of the Bagni di Lusnizza. Monday to Saturday 8-14.

Why go. To know the Tarvisio Forest.
