Area archeologica di San Marco e Collegiata di Santa Maria

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the area of the Old Cemetery, outside the village, there is a basalt Roman road, a domus in the archaeological area of San Marco and the Collegiate Church of Santa Maria, the most important medieval church of Atina.

Why go. Roman road and medieval church outside door.
