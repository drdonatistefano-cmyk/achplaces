Chiese di San Pietro, Sant'Albertino e Madonna dei Calcinai

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

St. Peter, probably fourteenth-century plant, stands near the stream Scarzito; Sant'Albertino, on the hill north-west of the center, has a classroom, presbytery and bell tower; the Madonna dei Calcinai, on the road to Pioraco, contains frescoes from the sixteenth century.

Why go. Three smaller churches between village and valley.
