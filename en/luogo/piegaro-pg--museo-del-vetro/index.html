Museo del Vetro

Museums · Themed

It occupies the ancient glassworks of the early nineteenth century in the historic center, abandoned in 1968 and restored with the original architecture, example of industrial archaeology and among the few pre-industrial glass plants preserved in Italy. The three-storey route follows the production cycle, from raw materials to hand blowing; on display glasses, bottles, carafes and damsel, some decorated in pure gold, and in the basement a large casting of emerald green glass formed at the closure of the factory. Organize craft workshops.

Why go. Seven centuries of glass inside the old furnace.
