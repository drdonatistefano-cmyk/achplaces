Collina di San Colombano e vino San Colombano DOP

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

An isolated relief of about 75 metres in the Po Valley, between Lambro and Olona, covered with vineyards and protected as the San Colombano Hill Park. On the slopes are cultivated Croatina, Barbera and Uva Rara, base of San Colombano DOP (frizzante, firm and reserve); the first reference to the cultivation of the hill dates back to 1371, with the reclamation started by Galeazzo Visconti, and in the nineteenth century these wines were compared to those of Valtellina. Itinerary of reference: the Strada del Vino San Colombano e dei Sapori Lodigiani.

Why go. The only hill on the Milan plain and its red DOP.
