Castello Lechi (Palazzo Lechi)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The construction was started by the Polini brothers in 1723 with the design of a castle-shaped residence with four corner towers; the building was not completed and passed to the Counts Lechi. The complex in red brick is surrounded by greenery and closed by a long wall of belt with turrets. It is one of the places reported in the FAI census "I Places of the Heart" (2010-2022).

Why go. The eighteenth-century castle house symbol of the village.
