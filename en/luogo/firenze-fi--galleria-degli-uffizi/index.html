Galleria degli Uffizi

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

In the Palace of Vasari (1560-1580), the most important collection of paintings of the Renaissance: Botticelli, Leonardo, Michelangelo, Raffaello, Caravaggio, then Dürer, Rembrandt, Rubens.

Why go. The Birth of Venus and Spring in the same hall.

  • Where Piazzale degli Uffizi 6, Firenze
  • Entry €25 full day, €29 with reservation; €16 for those entering from 16; €2 from 18 to 25 years EU; free under 18 and the first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
