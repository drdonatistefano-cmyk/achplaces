Ex cattedrale di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Domina Piazza Vescovado: built between 1512 and 1536 and renovated in 1760, it preserves in the apse a Madonna delle Grazie by Marco Cardisco (16th century) and in sacristy cupboards and eighteenth-century stuccos. From here starts the walk signposted towards Marina della Lobra.

Why go. The ancient cathedral in the town square.
