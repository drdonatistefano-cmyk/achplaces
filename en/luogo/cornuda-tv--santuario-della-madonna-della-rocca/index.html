Santuario della Madonna della Rocca

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

At 350 metres above sea level, next to the oak-tree site that the legend linked to the Holy Land (which collapsed on 18 June 2010); the nucleus is medieval, with additions of the late seventeenth century, and is entrusted to the Missionary Sisters of the Immaculate. The parishes of Cornuda, Coste, Covolo, Maser, Nogarè and Onigo go on pilgrimage every year.

Why go. The shrine on the hill, the destination of the pilgrimages of six parishes.
