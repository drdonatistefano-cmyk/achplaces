Gemma 1786 – Museo mineralogico e geologico estense

Museums · Science and university

The University Museum of Earth Sciences, heir to the eighteenth-century collections of the Museum of Extense Natural History, with the name "Gemma 1786" since 2005; path without architectural barriers and captions in Braille.

Why go. Minerals and rocks in an accessible museum.
