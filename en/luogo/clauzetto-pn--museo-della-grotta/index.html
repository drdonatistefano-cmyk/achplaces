Museo della Grotta

Museums · Archaeological

It exhibits the prehistoric finds of the excavations of Pradis and testimonies of life on the plateau in the bronze and Roman era; in the territory the Cave of Clusantin and the Cave of Rio Secco document the passage of the last Neanderthal hunters. Open at the same time as the caves.

Why go. The prehistoric plateau next to the caves.
