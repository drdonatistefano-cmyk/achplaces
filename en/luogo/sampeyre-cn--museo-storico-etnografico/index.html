Museo storico etnografico

Museums · Themed

In about ten rooms he collects tools for agricultural work, bakery and wool processing, the reconstruction of an old class with benches and blackboards, the workshops of the blacksmith and blacksmith, traditional costumes, a collection of models with the characters of the Baio and the authentic drapes of the four Sampeyre Bays. Open on weekends in spring and September, from Tuesday to Sunday in July and August, closed in October and November (check first, tel. 0175 977148).

Why go. The Baìo, one of the oldest festivals in the Alps, told by its drapes.
