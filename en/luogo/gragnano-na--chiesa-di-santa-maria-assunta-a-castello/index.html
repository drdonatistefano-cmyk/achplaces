Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta a Castello

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Castello, where the castle of the tenth century rises, the church of Santa Maria Assunta is a national monument since 1927; the dating is discussed, between the 10th and the 5th century according to other sources.

Why go. One of the oldest churches in the Lattari Mountains.
