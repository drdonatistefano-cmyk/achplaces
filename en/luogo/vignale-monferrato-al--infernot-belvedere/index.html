Infernot Belvedere

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · UNESCO World Heritage

Visitable infernot of the country: the infernots are small environments excavated in limestone stone and used for the aging of wine, symbol of the core UNESCO zones "Il Monferrato degli Infernot" . Visits on Sunday 15-18 from April to October, on the other days by reservation (info@monferratodegliinfernot.it); you should call first.

Why go. A cellar dug into the rock, the heart of the UNESCO site.
