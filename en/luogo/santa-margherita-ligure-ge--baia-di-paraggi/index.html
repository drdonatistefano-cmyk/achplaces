Baia di Paraggi

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

The cove of clear waters on the state road to Portofino, with a small sandy beach equipped with lush vegetation, the castle of Paraggi (nineteenth-century adaptation of a Genoese fortress), restaurants and clubs. The seabeds, suitable for the growth of red coral, attract divers, which can also see a ship sunk in 1917; since the twelfth century the hamlet had numerous mills, some still existing in the locality Acqua Viva. The walking path from Santa Margherita to Portofino passes through the bay and Cervara.

Why go. Crystal clear waters on the road to Portofino.
