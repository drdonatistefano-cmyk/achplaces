Oratorio del Santissimo Crocifisso della Grotta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Under the cathedral, four chapels built between the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries; in the Chapel of the Resurrection a Pietà in marble by Giovanni Bandini (1597).

Why go. The cathedral basement.
