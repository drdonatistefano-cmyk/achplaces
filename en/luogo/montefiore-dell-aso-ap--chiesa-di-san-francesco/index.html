Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1247 and 1303, it preserves the Romanesque-Gothic style outside; the portal of 1303 is now in sacristy, while the restorations between the mid-sixteenth and mid-eighteenth centuries gave the Baroque look inside.

Why go. Crivelli's polyptych church.
