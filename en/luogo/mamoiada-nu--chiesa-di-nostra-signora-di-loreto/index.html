Chiesa di Nostra Signora di Loreto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Loreto de bidda is located in the historic centre, perhaps of medieval origin, rebuilt at the end of the 17th century, with the frescoed dome; outside the village there is the little Loret'Attesu, where the feast of Our Lady of the Snow is celebrated.

Why go. The frescoed dome of the village church.
