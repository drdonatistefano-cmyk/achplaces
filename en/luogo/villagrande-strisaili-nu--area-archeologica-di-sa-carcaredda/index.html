Area archeologica di Sa Carcaredda

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

At about 800 meters above sea level in a wood of holm oaks near the village, the remains of an extensive nuragic village with tombs of giants and a small temple with circular plan, rare example of nuragic temple in the antis.

Why go. A rare antis temple in the woods.

  • Where SS 389 var. Buddusò, località Riu 'e Binu, Villagrande Strisaili
  • Entry €5, reduced €3; cumulative €8
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
