Vallo Volsco

History and architecture · Archaeology

The first settlement of Anzio was built on the hill of Santa Teresa in the 8th century BC; here are the first fortifications of the city, called Vallo Volsco: a sandstone wall regularized to the west, stretches of walls in sandstone blocks to the north and deep defensive cuts to the east. The park, managed by the Santa Teresa Committee in agreement with the Municipality, was inaugurated on 9 August 2017.

Why go. The walls of the Antium volsca.
