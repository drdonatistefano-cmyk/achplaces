Pinacoteca della Confraternita del Santissimo Sacramento

Museums · Old masters

Second section of the Museo Don Aldo Rossi, in the former hall of the confraternity near the Town Hall: it contains a fresco of the Crucifixion of 1452, a fresco of Francesco da Città della Pieve and paintings of the XV and XVII century. Entrance from Via della Pitalessa.

Why go. The Crucifixion of 1452.
