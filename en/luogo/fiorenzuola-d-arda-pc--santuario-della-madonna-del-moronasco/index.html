Santuario della Madonna del Moronasco

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In the 4th century there was a small oratory, in 1377 linked to the monastery of Chiaravalle della Colomba; the current Baroque building was started in 1665. It preserves a Nativity of the sixteenth century and, in the last chapel on the right, the oldest part, a fresco probably thirteenth century with the Madonna enthroned with the Child. Visitable on agreement taken in advance with the culture office.

Why go. A medieval fresco rediscovered in the countryside.
