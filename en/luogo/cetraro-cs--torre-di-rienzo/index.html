Torre di Rienzo

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Tower of sighting on a rocky ridge near the sea, of the system of coastal towers built between 1559 and 1567 against Turkish raids; square plan on three floors (deposit, accommodation of soldiers, battery), with entrance at the top reachable by a staircase. It was called Torre d'Acqua Perropata for a nearby waterfall; the tradition binds to San Benedetto a cannon blow that sank two Turkish ships, remembered every year by a procession of boats.

Why go. The anti-corsar tower overlooking the Tyrrhenian Sea.
