Santa Maria di Vescovio

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Also known as Santa Maria della Lode, the ancient cathedral of the diocese of Forum Novum and then of Sabina until 1733: a single nave with raised presbytery and semi-anular crypt of the 11th century, cycle of frescoes of the 2rd-Three hundred with stories of the Old and New Testaments and, in counterfaced, a great universal judgment of the school of Pietro Cavallini, rediscovered under the skilful in 1930; five-order bell tower of double and triple windows. Open every day 8-20 according to the MiC card.

Why go. The lost cathedral of Sabina.
