Mulino della Riviera

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Mill still in operation, where are found flour ground with natural stone. In the village also the bread of the Roofs cooked in the wood-burning oven and the Dranoresi, chocolate praline with rum liqueur cream.

Why go. Stone-ground flour.
