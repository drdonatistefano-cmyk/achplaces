Torre angioina (Castello angioino)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The surviving mast of the Angevin fortress that resisted the sieges of 1361 and 1496 and was destroyed by the earthquake of 1694: originally square plan, four corner towers and moat; the tower recalls the Castel Nuovo of Naples.

Why go. The last tower of the Angevin fortress.
