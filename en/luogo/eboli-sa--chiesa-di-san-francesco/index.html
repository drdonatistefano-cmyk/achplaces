Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church in Gothic style with compact hut façade, updated in the eighteenth century with Baroque decorations; in the former convent is the Archaeological Museum.

Why go. Gothic and Baroque in the same complex as the museum.
