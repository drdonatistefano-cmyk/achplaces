Abbazia di Santa Maria di Staffarda

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded by the Cistercians in 1135 in the Saluzzo plain a few steps from the Po, it is one of the most important medieval monasteries of Piedmont; in 1690 the battle of Staffarda was fought. Owned by the Foundation Ordine Mauriziano.

Why go. The Cistercian monastery.
