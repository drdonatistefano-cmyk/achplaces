Casa della Memoria

Museums · City history

Multimedia teaching hall in the former railway station, with historical archive and materials on the life of those who passed through the camp of Servigliano: built at the beginning of the twentieth century for about 10,000 prisoners, it housed about 10,000 in the First World War and about 20,000 people in the Second (followed, Jews and families helping them), then between 1945 and 1955 between 40,000 and 50,000 refugees, including Julian-Dalmati. Times indicated: July and August Mon-Fri 9-14, Sat 15-20; other months Mon-Fri 9-14 (afternoon by reservation), Sat 15-20 (morning by reservation), Sunday on request.

Why go. The stories of prisoners and refugees from the camp of Servigliano.
