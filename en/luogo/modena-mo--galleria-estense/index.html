Galleria Estense

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The collection of the Dukes of Este on the fourth floor of the Palazzo dei Musei: the bust of Francesco I del Bernini, the portrait of Velázquez, the Triptych of El Greco.

Why go. One of the great national galleries of Italy.

  • Where Largo Porta Sant'Agostino 337, Modena
  • Entry €8 whole; €2 from 18 to 25 years; single ticket €10 with Civic Museum, Gipsoteca and Avia Pervia
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website gallerie-estensi.beniculturali.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide
