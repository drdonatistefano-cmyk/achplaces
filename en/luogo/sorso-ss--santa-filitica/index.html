Santa Filitica

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the 5th century BC, the Carthaginians built a fortress, then occupied by the Romans; there remain a Roman villa with mosaics and a spa complex and a palaeo-Christian cult building.

Why go. From Carthaginians to early Christians.
