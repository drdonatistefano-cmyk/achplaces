Collegiata dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1284 with the support of Paganello II, Bishop of Lucca, in the Tuscan Romanesque style (the brick arch in the façade remains), it was rebuilt and enlarged in 1719 with baroque stuccoes; on the side a sandstone panther recalls the Lucca domination. Inside the fifteenth century baptismal font, a marble Saint Peter and the two polychrome wooden statues of the Annunciation of the Pisan school of the thirteenth century (attributed by Visit Tuscany to Nino Pisano), a crucifix of the fifteenth century very venerated, the Resurrection of Tabita by Cosimo Gamberucci and St. Peter in prison by Alessandro Allori.

Why go. The wooden Annunciation of the Pisan school and the canvas of the Allori.
