Castello di Deliceto

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Symbol of the village, built on the rock and inhabited by Norman lords, Swabians, Angevins and Aragonese, had mainly military function and was declared national monument in 1902. The most imposing part is the Torrione; the towers of the Pier and Parasinno, well preserved, are joined by a walk still accessible. Once surrounded by a moat with a drawbridge, it has a central courtyard with an octagonal cistern for rainwater; legend has it that in the dungeons there was hidden a shaving mill for thieves. Tel. +39 0881 963433

Why go. The castle that dominates the village and the valley.
