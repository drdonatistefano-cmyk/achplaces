Campanile di Giotto, Battistero e Museo dell'Opera del Duomo

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The Baptistery with the gates of Ghiberti, Giotto's Bell Tower and the museum with the originals of the square, including the Paradise Gate and Michelangelo's Pietà Bandini.

Why go. The museum is the most underrated in Florence.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo 9, Firenze
  • Entry Giotto Pass €20 whole, €7 from 7 to 14 years; Ghiberti Pass (without Bell Tower) €15 and €5
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website duomo.firenze.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
