Collezione Delitala

Museums · Modern art

In the Franciscan convent, collection dedicated to the painter and engraver of Oran Mario Delitala (1887-1990): xylographies and some paintings, including portraits, sacred works and scenes of barbaric life.

Why go. The master of Sardinian xylography.
