Museo della Vecchia Officina

Museums · Themed

Set up in the workshops of the Vallone yard of the mines of Calamita, exhibits objects, documents and cartography and reconstructs working environments such as the infirmary, the workshop and the accounting office. It has a small bookshop, a picnic area and a large car park.

Why go. The daily life of miners, before or after visiting galleries.
