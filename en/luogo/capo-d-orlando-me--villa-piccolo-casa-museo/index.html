Villa Piccolo, casa museo

Museums · House museums

Villa of the early twentieth century with park, home of the Piccolo di Calanovella family: Casimiro, photographer and author of ... Magic watercolors, the poet Lucio and the botany Agata Giovanna. The cousin Giuseppe Tomasi of Lampedusa stayed there in the summer and wrote pages of the Gattopardo. The museum, opened in 1978, exhibits paintings, ceramics, ancient weapons, books, family portraits, photographs and cameras, letters of Tomasi di Lampedusa and a fortepiano linked to Lucio Piccolo. Hours: Monday-Friday 10-13 and 16-18, Saturday 10-13, Sunday closed.

Why go. The house of the cousins of the Cat.
