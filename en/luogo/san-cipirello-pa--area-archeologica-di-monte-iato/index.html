Area archeologica di Monte Iato

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On a mountain of 852 meters in control of the valley of Iato, protected on three sides by rock walls, over 200 hectares of the city of Iaitas, founded by indigenous people at the beginning of the 1st millennium BC, Hellenized in the 6th century BC. (Aphrodite temple) and destroyed by Frederick II in 1246. Around 300 B.C. it was renovated with the agorà porticata and the bouleuterion; to the north-west the theatre with 35 rows of steps for about 4400 spectators, then the thermal baths, the sanctuary of Demeter and the House to peristyle 1, of about 1600 square meters on two floors. Excavations of the University of Zurich since 1971.

Why go. A Greek city on a plateau 850 meters away, with the theatre overlooking the valley.
