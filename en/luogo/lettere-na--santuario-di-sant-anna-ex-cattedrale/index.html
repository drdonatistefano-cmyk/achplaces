Santuario di Sant'Anna (ex Cattedrale)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Also known as the former Cathedral of Letters, it stands on a hill of the Lattari Mountains with the fortress behind it and is today the parish of Santa Maria Assunta and San Giovanni Battista. Built at the end of the 16th century, it has a Latin cross plan and a single nave with four chapels on the right and three on the left; the altar of the presbytery is made with the marbles of the ancient balustrade. In the left transept the Coven of the SS. Rosario holds relics and relics; the statue of St. Anne, invoked by pregnant women, is linked to the tradition of the ... dust of the Saint.
