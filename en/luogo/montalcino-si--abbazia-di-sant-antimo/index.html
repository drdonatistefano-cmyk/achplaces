Abbazia di Sant'Antimo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient Benedictine monastery in travertine and alabaster nine kilometers from Montalcino, among the masterpieces of the Italian Romanesque; legend wants it founded by Charlemagne in 781.

Why go. Visit her during the liturgy in Gregorian chant.

  • Where Castelnuovo dell'Abate, Montalcino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
