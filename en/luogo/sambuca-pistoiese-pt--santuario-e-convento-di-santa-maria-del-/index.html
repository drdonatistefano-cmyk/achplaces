Santuario e convento di Santa Maria del Giglio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Sanctuary on a hill near Castello di Sambuca, around which from 1722 the homonymous convent arose.

Why go. The shrine on the hill, stage of the Via Francesca.
