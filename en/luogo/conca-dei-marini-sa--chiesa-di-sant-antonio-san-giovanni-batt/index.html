Chiesa di Sant'Antonio (San Giovanni Battista)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Church of thirteenth-century origin, actually dedicated to St. John the Baptist, with belvedere on the sea, harmonious facade and bell tower with cusp covered with majolica.

Why go. The belvedere on the sea from the churchyard.
