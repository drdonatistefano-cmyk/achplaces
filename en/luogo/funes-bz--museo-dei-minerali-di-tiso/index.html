Museo dei minerali di Tiso

Museums · Science and university

Dedicated to the famous geodes of Tiso, formed for volcanic activity over 290 million years ago, with rock crystals, smoked quartz, garnets and other alpine minerals: it is the collection of a life by Paul Fischnaller. A short film introduces to the formation of the Alps and minerals; on Tuesdays and Thursdays, by reservation, you go with Fischnaller to look for the geodes.

Why go. Tiso's geodes and those who have been looking for them forever.
