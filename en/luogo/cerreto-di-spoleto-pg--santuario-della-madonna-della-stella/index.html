Santuario della Madonna della Stella

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Former hermitage of Santa Croce built in 1308 by Augustinian monks at the oldest Benedictine monastery of San Benedetto in Faucibus; the site dates back to the 8th century, was abandoned in 1652 and restored in the 19th century after events considered miraculous.

Why go. A rock hermitage of high medieval origin.

Currently not accessible according to source
