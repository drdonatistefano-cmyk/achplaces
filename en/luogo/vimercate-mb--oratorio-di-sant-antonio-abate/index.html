Oratorio di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between the 13th and 14th centuries in the Visconti period, it preserves fifteenth-century frescoes of the Passion of Christ in the international Gothic style and the 17th-century canvas "La Gloria di San Carlo" by the Lambugnani brothers, which shows the Vimercate of the time.

Why go. Gothic frescoes and a painting of the 17th century Vimercate.
