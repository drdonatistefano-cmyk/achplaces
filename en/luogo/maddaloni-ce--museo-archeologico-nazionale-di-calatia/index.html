Museo archeologico nazionale di Calatia

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 2003 at the Casino of Starza Penta, Masseria in 1552 and then hunting casino of the Carafa of Stadera, lords of Maddaloni since 1465, of which remain the coat of arms on the entrance vault, original decorations and an eighteenth-century chapel; here on April 9, 1734 the Neapolitan nobility symbolically delivered the keys of the city to Carlo di Borbone. On two floors the remains of the necropolis and the town of Calatia, from the end of the 8th century BC to the late Roman age: a section on the Roman streets with room immersive on the stretch of Calatia-Rome of the Appia and one on the feminine costume with rings, fibulas and scarabs. ICOM Prize

Why go. Calatia and the Appia in a Carafa hunting mess.
