Casa natale di Pio V

Museums · House museums

Home where Antonio Ghislieri was born on 17 January 1504 and lived until 1518, when he entered among the Dominicans of Voghera: four rooms, central staircase, porch with farmhouse, courtyard with well and stable; in 1936 it was furnished in sixteenth-century style and the birth room on the upper floor became chapel. Visitable only by telephone reservation.

Why go. The rooms where the only Piedmontese pope was born.
