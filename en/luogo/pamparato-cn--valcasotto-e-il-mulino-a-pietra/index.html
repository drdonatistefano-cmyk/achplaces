Valcasotto e il mulino a pietra

Local life and traditions · Crafts

In the hamlet of Valcasotto, with the parish church of San Ludovico king of France in 1666, an ancient stone mill from the Napoleonic era is still in operation and mature cheeses are produced in underground premises.

Why go. A Napoleonic mill that still grinds.
