Isola Bisentina

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

Isola del lago di Bolsena quoted by Dante in the Divine Comedy; there are the church of Saints James and Christopher, the work of the Vignola, and an octagonal edict by Antonio da Sangallo the Younger. You can reach it by boat from the marina of Capodimonte, which also touch the island Martana.

Why go. Architecture of the Renaissance in the middle of the lake.
