Oasi WWF Lago Angitola

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Natural reserve on the edge of the country, oasis of Mediterranean biodiversity and stop of migratory birds around one of the most interesting artificial lakes in Calabria; the country is part of the Regional Natural Park of the Serre.

Why go. Birdwatching and walks around the lake.
