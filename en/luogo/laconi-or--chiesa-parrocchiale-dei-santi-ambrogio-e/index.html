Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Ambrogio e Ignazio e Museo parrocchiale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church built in the 15th century and modified several times in the 19th century, also dedicated to St. Ignatius since 1951. The attached museum preserves a silver reliquary with a bone of the saint's hand, its rosary and documents of the cause of canonization, silver sacred of the sixteenth-sixth century, vestments of the sixteenth-century, partly Spanish statues, two 17th-century canvases, Roman finds, coins and objects of missions in Africa and Australia.

Why go. The relics and memories of Saint Ignatius.
