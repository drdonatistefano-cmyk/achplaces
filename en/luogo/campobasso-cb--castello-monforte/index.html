Castello Monforte

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the hill that dominates the city, with square plan with cylindrical towers at the corners; it bears the name of the fifteenth-century leader Nicola Monforte, count of the city after the earthquake of the mid fifteenth century. From the top the view reaches Majella and the Table-table; on the same hill remain ruins of a Sannitic fortification.

Why go. The view to the Majella.
