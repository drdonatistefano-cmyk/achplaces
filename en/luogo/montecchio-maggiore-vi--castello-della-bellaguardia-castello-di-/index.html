Castello della Bellaguardia (Castello di Giulietta)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

A few hundred meters above the castle of Romeo; feud of the Pileo in the thirteenth century, passed to the Scaligeri in 1311 and was bought by the Municipality in 1742. In the restorations of the 1930s there was built a restaurant with a panoramic terrace; the statue of Romeo, located in 2016, welcomes visitors.

Why go. The castle of Juliet and its terrace on the plain of Vicenza.
