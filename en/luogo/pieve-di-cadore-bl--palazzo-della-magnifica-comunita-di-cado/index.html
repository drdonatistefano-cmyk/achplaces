Palazzo della Magnifica Comunità di Cadore

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The seat of the Magnificent Community, built between 1444 and 1492 next to the church of Santa Maria Nascente. In the Council room, the Dedication of the Cadore in Venice by Cesare Vecellio and a Madonna by Marco Vecellio, a pine ceiling with the coat of arms of the Cadore and one by Valentino Paciera Besarel from 1862-64, and a frieze with 34 niches dedicated to illustrious cadorini. Opening to the public on Monday-Saturday 11-12.

Why go. The civic palace of the Cadore since the fifteenth century.
