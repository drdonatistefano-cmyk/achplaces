Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

It hosts, with the Archaeology Superintendence of Calabria, materials coming from the temple of Apollo Aleo and materials of the historical phase of the Brettii. At the inauguration of the museum, on April 1, 2015, they returned to Cirò Marina after 91 years head, feet and hand of the Apollo acrolite.

Why go. The finds of the sanctuary of Apollo and Krimisa Brettia.
