Convento di San Rocco

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Just outside the village, with the church called "Sant'Umano" the seventeenth-century convent and a guesthouse; in the church a wooden choir in walnut carved by two Franciscan friars of French origin.

Why go. The walnut choir of the French friars.
