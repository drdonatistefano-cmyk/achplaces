Museo del Corallo – Collezione Liverino

Museums · Themed

Digged about six meters below ground, it collects over a thousand sculptures and jewels in coral and hard stones from the fifteenth century to today, from the East to Italy; among the pieces a nineteenth-century parure for Paolina Bonaparte and a large cameo on shell engraved in ten years. The collection was born in 1934.

Why go. The art of torred coral.
