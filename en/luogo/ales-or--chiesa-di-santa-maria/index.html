Chiesa di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of 1535 at the entrance of the Riu Mulinu valley, so called for a watermill that stood nearby; from 7 to 10 September there is celebrated the feast of Santa Maria, with the rosary recited in Sardinian, the bonfire, the "goccius" sung and processions with knights in costume and banners embroidered.

Why go. The September party with knights and drowning.
