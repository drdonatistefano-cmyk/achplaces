Torre Ghirlandina

History and architecture · Towers · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The bell tower of the Duomo is almost 90 meters high, symbol of the city, with the Room of the Torresani frescoed and the view on the rooftops of Modena.

Why go. The most beautiful ascent of the city.

  • Where Piazza Torre, Modena
  • Entry €6 whole; €4 reduced; free up to 12 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website emiliaromagnaturismo.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
