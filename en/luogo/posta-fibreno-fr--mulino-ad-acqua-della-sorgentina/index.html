Mulino ad acqua della Sorgentina

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Built in 1810 in the locality of Sorgentina, it was expanded between 1905 and 1908 with a small hydroelectric power plant that, immediately after the Second World War, flooded for a few months the public lighting of the neighboring municipalities. Restored in the early 2000s, with original turbines and generators back in operation, it is the center of the Reserve and has didactic use; next to a picnic area among olive trees, dry walls and terraces.

Why go. A nineteenth-century mill that still spins the turbines.
