Museo etno-antropologico

Museums · Themed

Permanent Museum was founded in 1989 on the initiative of the association "Ribera Verde" (Ribera Verde), which has collected over 4,000 finds of rural civilization, pastoral and handicraft of the territory; tools of ancient peasant art and agricultural machinery have been recovered, catalogued, restored and made accessible to the public.

Why go. The life of the rebellious camps in over 4,000 objects.
