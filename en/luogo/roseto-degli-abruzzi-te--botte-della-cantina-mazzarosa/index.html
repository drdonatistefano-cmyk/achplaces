Botte della Cantina Mazzarosa

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

In the cellar of Villa Mazzarosa, wanted by Senator Giuseppe De Notaris, there is a barrel that could contain 1,000 hectolitres of wine, presented at an exhibition in 1905 and indicated as the largest in Europe.

Why go. The biggest barrel in Europe.
