Galleria Rizzi

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Wanted by lawyer Marcello Rizzi, he exhibited in the palace of Via Cappuccini (1926) the collection of paintings and art objects collected with his father Vittorio: works by Lelio Orsi, Vincenzo Campi, Denis Calvaert, Andrea Ansaldo, Giovanni Andrea De Ferrari, Carlo Dolci, Alessandro Magnasco, Sebastiano Ricci, Corrado Giaquinto and Felice Boselli, with seventeenth-century furniture and two drawers by Giuseppe Maggiolini. Tel. 0185 41300.

Why go. A private picture gallery on Silence Bay.
