Rocca di Dunarobba

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Renaissance fortress with quadrangular plan, with large cylindrical towers on the four sides, in the hamlet of Dunarobba near the Fossil Forest.

Why go. To be combined with the visit of the Fossil Forest.
