Santuario di San Magno

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

At 1760 meters, in an area formerly dedicated to the cult of Mars, it was a hospice for travellers between Valle Grana and Valle Stura; the complex combines an ancient part and an extension between the Seven and the Nineteenth Century. The fifteenth-century frescoes attributed to Pietro da Saluzzo depict evangelists and doctors of the Church, the Passion and the holy martyrs of the Thebes. Open on Saturdays and Sundays until June, every day from June to October (indicatively 9-18); guided tours on Sundays in July and August at 14:30 (5 €). On 19 August, for the patron saint's feast, the Baia di Castelmagno parade.
