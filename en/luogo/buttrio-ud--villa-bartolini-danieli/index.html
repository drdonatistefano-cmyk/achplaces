Villa Bartolini Danieli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in the seventeenth century by the Bartolini family on a rural building, it is reached by a long avenue of cypresses: the central body on three floors is flanked by two square towers connected by a porch and loggia. The lounge has allegorical and landscape frescoes from the mid-eighteenth century by Francesco Chiarottini and Giambattista Canal, restored since 1987; today it is home and restaurant.

Why go. 18th century frescoes of Chiarottini and Canal.
