Castello dei Castriota

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The castle, built by the Angioini after 1231 and renovated in the sixteenth century by Evangelista Menga for Pirro Castriota, was restored in 1911 by architect Adolfo Avena; it dominates the village and has been restored to the sixteenth-century forms.
