Museo dell'Abbazia di Chiaravalle

Museums · Themed

Historic route set up in the ancient dormitory of monks, with illustrated panels and teaching materials: the agricultural economy of the monastery, the converts, the grandeurs and the service buildings, the daily life of the monks and the Cistercian architecture. Open every day 8.30-12 and 14.30-18.30, without reservation for individuals; guided tours of groups and schools by reservation.

Why go. How he lived and worked a Cistercian abbey.
