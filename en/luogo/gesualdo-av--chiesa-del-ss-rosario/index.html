Chiesa del SS. Rosario

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Started by Carlo Gesualdo with the adjoining Dominican monastery and completed by Nicolò Ludovisi in the first half of the seventeenth century, at the same level of the castle: nine baroque altars in polychrome marble plus three others, wooden choir inlaid and the statue of Saint Vincenzo Ferreri, celebrated on the last Sunday of August with the Flight of the Angel. Visits by reservation.

Why go. Baroque altars and the Flight of the Angel.
