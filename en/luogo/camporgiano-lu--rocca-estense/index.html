Rocca Estense

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built in the fifteenth century on a rocky spur overlooking the main square, it is an example of extensive military architecture with trapezoidal walls and truncated corner towers; on top there is a hanging garden with conifers. The earthquake of 1920 left little of its original structure; inside you can see the base of the bell tower of the Romanesque church of San Sisto and stretches of medieval walls emerged from the excavations, and at the feet there is the stone fountain called \"pilon" .

Why go. The Este fortress overlooking the Serchio Valley, opposite the Verrucole Fortress.
