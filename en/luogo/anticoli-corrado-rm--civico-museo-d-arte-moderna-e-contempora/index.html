Civico Museo d'Arte Moderna e Contemporanea

Museums · Modern art

Born in 1935 as a gallery founded by Riccardo Assanti and Pietro Gaudenzi with 20 works created on site and became a museum in 2001, it occupies on three floors the Brancaccio Palace in the area of the ancient castle then a baronial palace. About 700 works including drawings, paintings, sculptures and prints from the late nineteenth century to the seventies, donated by artists related to the country: Kokoschka, Felice Carena, Arturo Martini, Adolfo De Carolis, Giulio Aristide Sartorio, Augusto Corelli, Pietro Gaudenzi, Giuseppe Capogrossi, Fausto Pirandello, Rafael Alberti and the model became painter Pasqualosa Marcelli Bertoletti.

Why go. A century and a half of artists in love with a country.
