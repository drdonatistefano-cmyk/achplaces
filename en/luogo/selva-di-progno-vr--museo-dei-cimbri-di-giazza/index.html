Museo dei Cimbri di Giazza

Museums · Themed

Ethnographic Museum and documentation centre on the cimbrian people, of Bavarian-Tyrolean origin, allocated in Lessinia between the XII and XIV century; Giazza is the closest area to the original language and traditions. The multi-storey itinerary presents language, family life, work, architecture, religiousity and popular beliefs, with art and cimbri crafts; the center organizes demonstrations and courses every year. Open Saturday and Sunday 15-18.

Why go. The heart of the Cimbrian culture, where the language still survives.
