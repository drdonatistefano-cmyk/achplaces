Parco storico di Villa Durazzo Pallavicini

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

In Pegli, among the most original romantic parks, wanted by the Marquis Ignazio Pallavicini and created from 1840 by Michele Canzio, set designer of the Teatro Carlo Felice; in the villa the Museum of Ligurian Archaeology.

Why go. A garden like a play.
