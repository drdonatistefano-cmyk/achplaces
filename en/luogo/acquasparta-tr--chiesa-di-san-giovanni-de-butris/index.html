Chiesa di San Giovanni de Butris

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the thirteenth century, already of the Knights of Malta, built on the remains of a Roman bridge of Augustan age of the Via Flaminia.

Why go. A church over a Flaminia bridge.
