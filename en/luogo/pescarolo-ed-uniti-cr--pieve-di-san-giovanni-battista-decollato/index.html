Pieve di San Giovanni Battista Decollato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Pieve Terzagni, the parish church dates back to the 12th century, when the territory was under the dominion of Matilde di Canossa, and was renovated, restored and enlarged between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. In the presbytery there is a precious Romanesque mosaic floor of the 12th century with figures and symbols.

Why go. The Romanesque mosaic of the 12th century.
