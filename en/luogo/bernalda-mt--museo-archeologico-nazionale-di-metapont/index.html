Museo archeologico nazionale di Metaponto

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Ceramics, sculptures, coins, votive objects and instruments of the Greek colony founded in the 7th century BC, in chronological order from the traces of the Bronze and Iron Ages to the Roman conquest.

Why go. The history of a city of Magna Grecia.

  • Where Via Dinu Adamesteanu 21, Metaponto
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
