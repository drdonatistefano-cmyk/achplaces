Castello di Pontes

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Ruins on a height at the foot of the Tuttavista, above the plain of Cedrino: fortress of the 11th century on a previous Roman fortification, conquered by the Aragonese in the 14th century and abandoned in the 15th century. Still features of the antemural, traces of oven and cistern and a wide panorama up to the Gulf of Orosei; Deledda sets the ghosts of the barons of ... Canne al vento.

Why go. The ruins of Deledda's barons.
