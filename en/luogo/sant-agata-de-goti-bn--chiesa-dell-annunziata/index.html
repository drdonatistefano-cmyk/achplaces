Chiesa dell'Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded in 1237 and rebuilt after the earthquake of 1349, with portal and sixteenth-century bell tower; the Gothic interior houses frescoes from the end of the fourteenth to the beginning of the fifteenth century rediscovered for a few decades (Universal Judith in counter-facade, Crucifixion and Annunciation in the apse, St. Nicholas who endows three girls), a polyptych of the Annunciation of 1483 maybe by Angiolillo Arcuccio and 16 stained glass windows of 1976 designed by Bruno Cassinari.

Why go. The Gothic Last Judgement rediscovered.
