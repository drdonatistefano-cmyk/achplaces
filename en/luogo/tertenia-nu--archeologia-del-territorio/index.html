Archeologia del territorio

History and architecture · Archaeology

Caves at the foot of Mount Julea inhabited by the recent and final Neolithic (IV-III millennium BC), necropolis to domus de janas in Magalàu and Santa Lugia, almost 80 nuraghi, about thirty tombs of giants, about twenty Nuragic villages and a sacred well.

Why go. One of the richest campaigns in prehistory.
