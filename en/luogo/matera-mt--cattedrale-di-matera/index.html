Cattedrale di Matera

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Apulian Romanesque cathedral of the 13th century on the highest point of the Civita, dedicated to the Madonna della Bruna: it preserves a Romanesque rosette, the wooden choir of 1453 and the stone nativity scene carved in 1534 by Altobello Persio and Sannazzaro da Alessano.

Why go. Our Lady of the Brunettes.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Matera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
