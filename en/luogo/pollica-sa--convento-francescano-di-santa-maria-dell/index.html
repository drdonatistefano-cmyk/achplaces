Convento francescano di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded in 1611 by the inhabitants with the patronage of Prince Vincenzo Capano, it has the cloister, a frescoed refectory and a single nave church with four altars on its side, three with the Capano coat of arms; the paintings of the ceiling are attributed to Nicola Malinconico, of the school of Luca Giordano, the wooden chest of drawers is by Giuseppe Marrocco da Celso and the stucco by Pietro Sernicola. On July 2 the statue of Our Lady of Graces comes out in procession.

Why go. Baroque art in a village convent.
