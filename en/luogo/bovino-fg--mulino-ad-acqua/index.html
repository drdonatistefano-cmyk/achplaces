Mulino ad acqua

Museums · Themed

On the Cervaro stream along the ancient Strada Regia delle Puglie, built in 1810 with a system patented by Ferdinando II of Bourbon: the last watermill still working in Puglia, today museum of ancient crafts and grinding of ancient grains such as Senator Cappelli.

Why go. The last watermill that runs in Puglia.
