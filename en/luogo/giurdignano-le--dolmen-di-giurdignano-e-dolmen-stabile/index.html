Dolmen di Giurdignano e Dolmen Stabile

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Giurdignano is considered the largest and most diverse megalithic site in Italy, with seven dolmen still intact. The Dolmen Stable, along the road to Giuggianello between olive trees and dry stone walls on a plateau at 102 meters, dates back to the beginning of the II millennium BC: it has a rectangular plan with a covering plate resting on a series of blocks, and for many scholars it was part of a wider megalithic structure. Other imposing dolmens, perhaps used as altars, are Orfine, Chiancuse, Peschio Gravasce, Santa Barbara, Sferracavalli, Paolo Niuri and Grassi.

Why go. One of the densest concentrations of dolmen in Italy, among the olive trees.
