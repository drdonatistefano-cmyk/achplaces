Chiesa dei Bianchi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The seventeenth-century church of the Confraternity of the Battuti (of the Gonfalone), so called for the white clothes of the confreres, on the north side of the country; the interior has a four-leaf plant.

Why go. The unusual four-leaf plant.
