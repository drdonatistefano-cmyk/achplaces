Museo Moto Laverda

Local life and traditions · Motors

Dedicated to Moto Laverda, whose plant was located south of Villa Malvezzi from the second post-war period to the seventies, exhibits over 100 motorcycles. The city guide indicates this as it was inaugurated in 2022.

Why go. Over 100 bikes of the historic house of Breganze.
