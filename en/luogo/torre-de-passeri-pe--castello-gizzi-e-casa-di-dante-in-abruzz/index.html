Castello Gizzi e Casa di Dante in Abruzzo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The first nucleus of the year Maybe Mille, linked to the defensive towers of San Clemente in Casauria; the present building was born from the works started on 17 December 1719 by the Mazara di Sulmona (Marchesa Smeralda) and lost a floor with the earthquake of 1915. Acquired by the Gizzi family in 1967, since 1979 it hosts the Institute of Studies and Research Casa di Dante in Abruzzo with Dante's Museum, Caldora Library, annual exhibitions on Divine Comedy, the tower and a panoramic park.

Why go. An eighteenth-century castle dedicated entirely to Dante.
