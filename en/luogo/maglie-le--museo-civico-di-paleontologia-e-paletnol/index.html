Museo civico di Paleontologia e Paletnologia «Decio de Lorentiis» (L'Alca)

Museums · Archaeological

Founded in 1960 and since 1999 in the Palazzo Sticchi-Ruberti, noble residence of the early twentieth century that with the Municipal Library forms the cultural complex L'Alca; it is the only museum of the Mezzogiorno dedicated to the reconstruction of the prehistory of Salento, with sections of geology and paleontology, paleolithic and prehistoric art, Neolithic and age of metals, ethnography with African art objects. Touring hours: Mon-Fri 9-13 and 16-18:30, Sat-dom 16-18:30.

Why go. The prehistory of Salento in one museum.
