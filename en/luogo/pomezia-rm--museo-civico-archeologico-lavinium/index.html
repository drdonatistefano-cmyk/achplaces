Museo Civico Archeologico Lavinium

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 2005 next to the Borgo di Pratica, on the ruins of Lavinium, a religious center of the Latins linked to Enea: five thematic rooms from the 10th century BC to the Roman age, with the large statue of Minerva Tritonia (5th century BC), the votive terracotta of the shrine of the goddess and, in the last room, the funeral set of the monument to Enea (sword, spears, sacrificial knife). Multimedia path with holograms and tactile path free of charge upon reservation.

Why go. The Minerva Tritonia and the places of the myth of Enea.

The MiC card reports hours closed every day, but the Superintendence announces events at the museum in October 2026: check opening (tel. 06 91984744)
