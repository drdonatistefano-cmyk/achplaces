Palazzo Ducale (Castello di Giove)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The castle of Jugo is documented since 1191; in 1503, Cesare Borgia had its defenses dismantled and the sixteenth-century palace, initiated by the Farnese, was completed by Mattei, who bought it in 1597 and became dukes by the will of Urban VIII. It has five floors, corner towers, 365 windows and a helical ramp that can be crossed by carriages going up to a loggia on the Tiber Valley; the halls have mythological ceilings attributed to the schools of Domenichino, Veronese and Orazio Alfani.

Why go. The 365-window building.
