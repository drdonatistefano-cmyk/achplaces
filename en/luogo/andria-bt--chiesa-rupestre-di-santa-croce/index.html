Chiesa rupestre di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church excavated in tuff in the 10th century in the Lagnoni district, enlarged between the Three and the fifteenth century: it preserves the frescoes of the Inventio Crucis with the history of Elena in search of the Cross, a rare subject in Puglia.

Why go. The legend of the Cross.
