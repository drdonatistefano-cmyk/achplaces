Megaliti di Nardodipace

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Two granite blocks complexes, called "stones" or "stones," on a plateau near the highest peak of the Serre, reported in 2005: trilite structures with estimated blocks over 200 tons, pillars sometimes engraved with pictograms and possible walls. The investigations have not clarified its origin, perhaps anthropic, and are classified as geosites.

Why go. The mystery of the Stonehenge of the Greenhouses.
