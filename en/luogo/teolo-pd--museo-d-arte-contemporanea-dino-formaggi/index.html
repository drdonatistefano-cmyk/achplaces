Museo d'arte contemporanea «Dino Formaggio» - Palazzetto dei Vicari

Museums · Modern art

In the Palazzetto dei Vicari, 16th century building, the museum opened in October 1993 by the Municipality with Professor Dino Cheese, which donated the collection: about 200 paintings and sculpture from the end of the 19th century to today, including works by Medardo Rosso, Aligi Sassu and Tito Gasparini. Open Saturday and Sunday 9-12 and 16-19, without reservation.

Why go. Medardo Rosso and Sassu in a 16th century building.
