Chiesa della Madonna del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the first half of the sixteenth century and linked to the settlement of the Carmelites, then passed to the Franciscans after the suppressions of 1806; the cult of the Madonna del Carmine was tied to the pasta makers of the city. A single nave with a Latin cross, it is accessed by a staircase in piperno; the ceiling is frescoed by V. Severino (1907) with the Virgin of Carmel in glory, the chapel of the Immaculate has a marble altar of 1763 and a wooden statue of 1759, and the chapel
