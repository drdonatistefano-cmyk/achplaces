Chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo e Museo sistino di arte sacra

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The museum is set up in the fourteenth-century parish church of Saints Peter and Paul, in an elevated position, with terracotta portal and travertine rosette. It houses a large fifteenth-century fresco of the Last Judgement, a wooden statue of St. Peter of the second half of the fourteenth century and the reliquary-ostensory of the Holy Cross in golden silver, chiselled in 1488 by the goldsmith ascolano Pietro Vannini; in the crypt of the Addolorata, perhaps of Farfense era, traces of frescoes attributed to Vincenzo Pagani and Vittore Crivelli. Opening on request outside the scheduled periods: €4 per person with guided tour.

Why go. The Last Judgement and the Ascola goldsmithy of the fifteenth century.

MiC card (updated 2021) marks every day as closed and Let's Marche reports museums only partially open
