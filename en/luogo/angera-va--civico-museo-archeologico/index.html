Civico Museo Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

Since 1982 on the first floor of the fifteenth-century Palazzo del Pretorio, with ogival portal and stone portico of Angera. The Roman vicus with coins and commerce, the Roman necropolis supplies, the organic remains of the Angera Panini and a 5th century Greek funeral stele of a Syrian merchant; stoneware under the porch and children's section (MABA). Closed from 20 December to 31 January.

Why go. The Roman history of Angera, including loaves.
