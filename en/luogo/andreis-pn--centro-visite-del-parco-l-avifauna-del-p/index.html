Centro visite del Parco «L'avifauna del Parco»

Museums · Science and university

The exhibition "L'avifauna del Parco" opened in 2001, exhibits more than 200 naturalized specimens of birds of the park and the region, with teaching room, projection room and conferences and naturalistic laboratory; it is connected to the avifauna area with the recovery center. Seasonal openings, especially on weekends and in August every day.

Why go. Two hundred species of birds in the park up close.

seasonal openings only, autumn closing from September to early December according to the calendar of the Park
