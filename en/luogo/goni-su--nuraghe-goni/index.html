Nuraghe Goni

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

One of Sardinia's best preserved single tower nuraghi, the Bronze Age: blocks of limestone in regular rows, about 10 m in diameter and over 8 m in height, with two construction phases for a restoration of the Nuragic age. Inside the tholos vault is integrated, three cross niches, a triangular window and a staircase interrupted after five steps. It is reached from a dirt road towards the dam of the Mulargia and then on foot on a steep slope (about 15 minutes).

Why go. The untouched tholos and the view of Lake Mulargia.
