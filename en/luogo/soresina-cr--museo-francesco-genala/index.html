Museo Francesco Genala

Museums · City history

He collected documents, manuscripts and objects from the statesman Francesco Genala (Soresina 1843 - Rome 1893), Minister of Public Works in the government Depretis from 1883 to 1887, when he followed the reconstruction after the earthquakes of Casamicciola (1883) and Liguria (1887). Open on weekdays 14.15-18.30, Saturday 14.15-17.30; barrier-free (information: municipal library).

Why go. The story of a post-unitarian minister of Italy born in Soresina.
