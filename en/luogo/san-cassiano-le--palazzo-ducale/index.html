Palazzo ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

With roots in the 16th century, when it had watchtowers and defensive structures, it was renovated in 1720 by the Lubelli family, which eliminated most of the towers: one remains visible. A stone portal between two columns leads into an open square atrium; a three-arched loggia preserves traces of painted decorations. Owned by the municipality since 1982, it is the seat of the Town Hall and has a congress hall.

Why go. A sixteenth-century palace with painted loggia, today city hall.
