Cripta di San Casto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The only remaining part of the Romanesque cathedral, under the presbytery: a 12th-century crypt, rediscovered by chance in 1928, with a cruising vault on sixteen stone columns and medieval and Roman capitals of reuse. It contains frescoes of Byzantine style, three wooden sculptures of the Two-Three Century, Roman epigraphs that evoke a temple of Diana and the funerary stone of Gnesio, priest of the goddess.

Why go. An intact Romanesque setting, with traces of the Roman temple on which it stands.
