Museo civico Città di Cave - Museo Lorenzo Ferri

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Dedicated to the sculptor, restorer and sindonologist Lorenzo Ferri (1902-1975), brought to Cave by the parish priest Mgr. Lorenzo Castellani. Inaugurated in December 2013 in the palace of the former Mattei Hospital, it exhibits on three floors the plasters from the 1920s to 1975: sketches of the statues of Trilussa and Giolitti, Ecce Homo, Christ the Redeemer, reliefs for the competition of the bronze doors of St. Peter, the Alata Victory, the bas-relief of almost three meters ...The legend of Orpheus and the oil of Eva in Eden. Tactile path for people with visual disabilities; open Wednesday-Sunday 10:30-12:30 and 16:30-19:30.

Why go. The gipsoteca of a sculptor of the twentieth century.

  • Where Via Cavour 23, Cave
  • Entry €3, reduced €2,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
