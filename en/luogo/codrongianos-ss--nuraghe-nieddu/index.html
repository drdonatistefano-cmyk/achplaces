Nuraghe Nieddu

History and architecture · Archaeology

The best preserved nuraghe of the area, of the Bronze Age (II millennium BC): a single 11 m high tower with tholos cover, in dark basalt blocks from which the name \"nieddu" is black. In the territory there are about 60 nuraghi.

Why go. A black nuragic tower, almost intact.
