Porti di Claudio e Traiano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The port of Claudio was started in 42 AD north of the mouth of the Tiber to bring to the sea the river traffic of Rome, today under the airport; Trajan enlarged it in 106 AD with a large hexagonal basin more internal and secure, digging the channel that created the Sacred Island.

Why go. The port of Rome imperial.
