Museo civico Ernesto e Teresa Della Torre

Museums · Old masters

The civic museum was born from various donations, it collects paintings, drawings, sculptures and engravings from the 14th century to the present day.

Why go. Seven centuries of art in a museum born from the donations of the Trevigliese.
