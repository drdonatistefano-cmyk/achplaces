Villa Adriana

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Official residence built by the Emperor Hadrian around 126 AD, plundered during the invasions and restored from the nineteenth century: the Pecile with its bath, the baths, the canopy's nymph, the imperial palaces, the accommodation of servants and praetorians and the Maritime Theatre surrounded by a water canal. UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1999.

Why go. The great palace-city of an emperor.
