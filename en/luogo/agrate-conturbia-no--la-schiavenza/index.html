La Schiavenza

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

A noble rural complex that occupies almost the entire west side of the square, with large courtyard, stables and arcades; already shown on the Teresian map of 1725 and reordered in the Baroque age. The palace on the square, with facade to neo-Gothic motifs, was primary school until 1975.

Why go. A rare example of noble courtyard of the Novarese countryside.
