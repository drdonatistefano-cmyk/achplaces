Castello di Pavone

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Castle of the Bishops of Ivrea, born from a walls of the IX-X century around the church of St. Peter, with the mast of the XI century, the round towers of 1390 and the spiral staircase of 1509-1512; in 1363 Bishop Pietro de la Chambre was held prisoner. In 1885 Alfredo d'Andrade bought it for 7,000 lire and restored it in medieval style, creating between 1912 and 1915 the Salone di Re Arduino; since 1991 it is part of the Giodice family and hosts hotels, restaurants and conference center. Private: internal visits should be requested from the property (tel. 0125 672111).

Why go. The castle is rebuilt by the father of the medieval village of Turin.
