Abbazia di Santa Maria La Noara

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

About 5 km from the centre, it dates back to the 12th century, at the time of Ruggero II, on the impulse of Sant'Ugo Abate; it is considered the most significant example of Cistercian architecture in Sicily, with a sober style that reflects poverty and simplicity.

Why go. The most important Cistercian abbey on the island.
