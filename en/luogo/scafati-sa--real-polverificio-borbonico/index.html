Real Polverificio borbonico

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Complex of buildings of different eras in a green area with tree-lined avenues, built since 1851 by Ferdinand II of Bourbon to produce gunpowder instead of that of Torre Annunziata; since 1894 it was intended for tobacco (then experimental tobacco institute) and, damaged by the earthquake, was abandoned in 1980. It is entrusted to the Archaeological Park of Pompeii, which provides for the redevelopment of deposits, laboratories, auditoriums and exhibition spaces.

Why go. Bourbon's industrial archaeology.

The MiC card indicates "Closed" every day (site abandoned, waiting for redevelopment); italia.it indicates instead that since 2010 it hosts the City Museum
