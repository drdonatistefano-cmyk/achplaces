Vino Colli Albani e broccolo attufato

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The white DOC Colli Albani, also superior sparkling wine, the ancient Albano praised by Orazio and loved by the artists of the Grand Tour; in the kitchen the roman broccoli (capoccione) is attuned, cooked in wine.

Why go. The wine praised by Horace.
