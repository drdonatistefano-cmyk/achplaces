Infernot

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The infernot is a small underground chamber excavated in the Stone by Cantons, without light or ventilation, usually reached from a cellar and used to store the wine in bottle at constant temperature and humidity. Ponzano, linked to Alemat Winery, was inaugurated on May 8, 2016 during ... 2016 , and is connected to the Ecomuseo della Pietra da Cantoni.

Why go. The cellars dug in the typical stone of Monferrato.
