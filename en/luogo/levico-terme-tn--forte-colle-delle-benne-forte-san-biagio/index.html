Forte Colle delle Benne (Forte San Biagio)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built by the Austro-Hungarians in 1880-1882, twin of the fort of Tenna, 660 meters on the hill south of the lake: Porphyd and granite housematte with moat, four levels and central corridor, originally armed with four 12 cm cannons and two obici in revolving armoured domes. Disarmed in the spring of 1915, it was only an observatory and a warehouse in war; partly restored for the centenary of the Great War. From the thermal baths you go up via San Biagio and then a dirt road; guided tours in the program

Why go. A strong Habsburg on the lake.
