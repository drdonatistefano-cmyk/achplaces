Villaggio minerario di Ingurtosu

History and architecture · Abandoned villages

Arbus fraction about 10 km from the village, in the valley of Is Animas that descends towards Piscinas, now a ruined village and desert. The silver, lead and zinc mines were active from 1855 to 1968, with the greatest development under British society Pertusola and Lord Thomas Brassey; seven villages with 900 rooms housed up to 2,500 people. The granite direction of the castle remains, called \"castle" (1870, Neo-Medieval style), the church of Santa Barbara (1916) and the villas Wright and Ginestra.

Why go. A village of miners remained deserted.
