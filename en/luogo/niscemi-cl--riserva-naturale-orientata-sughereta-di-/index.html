Riserva naturale orientata Sughereta di Niscemi

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Reserve of 2.939 hectares established in 1997, about 330 meters in the southern part of the plateau: it protects the most important relic of cork mixed with holm oak of central Sicily, which with the Bosco of Santo Pietro di Caltagirone is what remains of the largest cork grove of central-southern Sicily. Between corks, holm oaks, thorny oaks, cysts, heathers, myrtle and arbutus live wild cat and istrice, buzzard, ghetto, eschulapius columbra and leopardin columbra.

Why go. The largest cork forest left in the heart of Sicily.
