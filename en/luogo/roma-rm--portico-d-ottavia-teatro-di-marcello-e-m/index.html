Portico d'Ottavia, Teatro di Marcello e Museo Ebraico

Museums · City history · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

In the Jewish quarter the remains of the Portico d'Ottavia and the Teatro di Marcello, the Temple Major and the Jewish Museum of Rome.

Why go. Two millennia of Jewish history.
