Museo archeologico del Barro (MAB)

Museums · Archaeological

Antiquarium at the Hermitage Park centre, with workshops, which exhibits the main findings of the Gothic settlement (V-VI century) excavated on the mountain.

Why go. The Gothic finds of Monte Barro.
