Cattedrale di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Cathedral Basilica founded in 1094 by the Grand Count Ruggero I d'Altavilla, next to the bishop's palace: it houses the sarcophagus of Queen Adelasia, wife of the Count, and a Adoration of the shepherds attributed to William Borremans; the façade has lava stone and white marble decorations and a portal in Arab-Norman style.

Why go. The tomb of Queen Adelasia.
