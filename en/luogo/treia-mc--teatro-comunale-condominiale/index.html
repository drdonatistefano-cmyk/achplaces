Teatro comunale (Condominiale)

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built in 1801 on a project by the architect Lucatelli and financed by a company of families from Treais, with wooden stages; the curtain painted by Silverio Copparoni in 1865 represents the battle of Vallesacco. Visits are booked through Pro Loco.

Why go. The curtain with the battle.
