Chiesa di San Bernardino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Baroque church with a single nave, linked to a confraternity, with dome above the apse, wooden choir, walls decorated with pilasters and golden stuccoes and bell tower with square plan. It is located about 50 meters from the main square.

Why go. Golden fillings a stone's throw from the square.

the Municipality indicates that currently the interior is not visitable
