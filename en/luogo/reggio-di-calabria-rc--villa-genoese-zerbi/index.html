Villa Genoese Zerbi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa of Venetian neo-Gothic taste, with arches, Ionic columns, medallions and Gothic plumes; the catalog of the MiC the date between 1886 and 1915 and attributed it to Domenico Zerbi.

Why go. Gothic on the waterfront.

Private villa, in the past venue of exhibitions: check if it is visitable inside
