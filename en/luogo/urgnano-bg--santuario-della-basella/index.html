Santuario della Basella

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In memory of an apparition of Our Lady in 1356; around 1460 Bartolomeo Colleoni had the church enlarged and in 1462 the Dominicans of Santo Stefano di Bergamo arrived there, who joined a convent.

Why go. The Marian shrine wanted great by the Colleoni.
