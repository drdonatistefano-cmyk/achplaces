Museo civico Antonio Giacomelli e Mastio di Ezzelino

Museums · City history · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In Castel San Zeno, the oldest part of the fortifications (1242), the museum has archaeological sections from prehistoric to Roman times (with the stele of the gens Vassidia), a medieval and modern section with works by Paduanino and Antonio Zanchi and a map of 1566-75, and a musical section on tenors Giovanni Martinelli and Aureliano Pertile. The mast, about 38 meters high, offers a view of the plain and the Euganean and Berici Hills.

Why go. Climb the mastium and see the walls from above.

  • Where Piazza Trieste 15, Montagnana
  • Entry museum €3,50, reduced €2; mastio €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
