Cappella rupestre di Santa Lucia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small chapel leaning against a vault frequented already in the Bronze Age; inside a rustic altar and two frescoed kiosks of the fifteenth century.

Why go. 15th century frescoes in a cave inhabited by prehistoric times.
