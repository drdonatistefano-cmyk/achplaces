Via Sottoriva

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

One of the most characteristic streets of the center, with the arcades and the ancient taverns of the city, between Ponte Nuovo and Sant'Anastasia.

Why go. The historical Verona taverns.
