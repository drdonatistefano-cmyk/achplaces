Fisarmonica: Palazzo delle Esposizioni e Premio Internazionale

Local life and traditions · Crafts

The Palazzo delle Esposizioni hosts exhibitions and events, including an accordion and an organ that entered the Guinness of Records; every September the International Accordion Award attracts musicians from all over the world and in June the European Music Festival is held.

Why go. The city that made the accordion its industry.
