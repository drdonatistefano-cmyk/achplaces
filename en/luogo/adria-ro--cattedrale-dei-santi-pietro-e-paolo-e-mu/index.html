Cattedrale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo e Museo diocesano

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Italia.it recommends to go down into the crypt, decorated with fine Byzantine frescoes: it is an apse chapel of the ninth century from which starts the path of the Diocesan Museum, opened in March 2025 in the spaces between the ancient cathedral of St. John and the new of Saints Peter and Paul. Eight sections on about 500 square meters with paintings, shovels, silvers, vestments and sculptures, including fifteenth-century fragments in painted and golden terracotta by Michele from Florence. Museum open Friday afternoon, Saturday and Sunday.

Why go. The 9th century crypt with Byzantine frescoes.
