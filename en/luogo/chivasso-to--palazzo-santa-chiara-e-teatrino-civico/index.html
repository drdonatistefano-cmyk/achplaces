Palazzo Santa Chiara e Teatrino Civico

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Building of the late eighteenth century born as a convent of the Clarisses and then became town hall. On the ground floor, obtained from the inner chapel of the former convent, the small horseshoe theatre with the audience and gallery designed in 1834 by Fausto Gozzano, father of the poet Guido; was inaugurated on 16 October 1864 by the Philodramatic Amateurs.

Why go. A 19th-century theater hidden in the town hall.
