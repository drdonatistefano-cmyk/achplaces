Lago di Garda: lungolago, spiaggia e gite in barca

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

From Piazza Betteloni the boats depart for Desenzano, Salò, Lazise and Sirmione, with regular connections on the eastern shore of the lake; on the lake there is the beach of Braccobalco.

Why go. Garda by boat, starting from the port of the fortress.
