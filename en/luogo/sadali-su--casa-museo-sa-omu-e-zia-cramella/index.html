Casa museo Sa omu 'e zia Cramella

Museums · House museums

House of the end of the nineteenth century in the square called "mesu idda" , of which Aunt Carmela was the penultimate owner: four rooms on the rural life with tools for wood, agriculture and breeding, the preservation of food, the attic of supplies and the corner of bread with the grinder moved by the donkey.

Why go. A peasant house the way it was.
