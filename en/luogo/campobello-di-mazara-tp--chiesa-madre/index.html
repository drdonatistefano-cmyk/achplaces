Chiesa Madre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1825 by the architect Sacchetti, with Baroque and neoclassical influences, where in 1587 there was a small church; it houses the wooden crucifix of Fra' Umile da Petralia donated to the city in 1666 and paintings by Luigi Lo Jacono (1843) and Domenico Provenzano (1902).

Why go. Fra' Umile's crucifix from Petralia.
