Basilica polironiana di San Benedetto Abate

Places of worship · Basilicas

Abbey church rebuilt by Giulio Romano between 1540 and 1545 without demolishing the Romanesque and Gothic structures, incorporating the oratory of Santa Maria dell'XI-XII century. It houses 32 statues of terracotta saints by Antonio Begarelli, the carved wardrobes of the sacristy of Giovanni Maria Piantavigna and the alabaster sarcophagus of Matilde di Canossa on four red marble lions (the remains were brought to St. Peter in Rome in 1633).

Why go. Giulio Romano and the tomb of Matilde.
