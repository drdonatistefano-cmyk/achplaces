Basilica di San Lorenzo e Museo della Collegiata

Places of worship · Basilicas

Symbol of the city, is a Romanesque-Gothic building of the fourteenth century in brick, with an elaborate Gothic façade linked to Bartolino da Novara, which painted it at the end of the fourteenth century; the interior with three naves is described by Italia.it as a sort of small museum of sacred art, with paintings especially of the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries. In the chapel of the right aisle there is the large table of Cerano with the Crucifixion with the Magdalene and the Saints Ambrose and Lorenzo; there are then the fifteenth-century Polyptych of St. Joseph of Lorenzo of Mortara, wooden crib with 80 bas-relief figures, the Madonna of the Rosary by Gerolamo Giovenone and works by Bernardino Lanino and Julius Cesare Procaccini.

Why go. Mortara Cathedral, a gallery of Lombard art between the fifteenth and seventeenth centuries.
