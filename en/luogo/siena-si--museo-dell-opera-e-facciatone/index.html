Museo dell'Opera e Facciatone

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In the nave of the "Duomo Nuovo" never completed, one of the oldest private museums in Italy, with the Majesty of Duccio; from the unfinished facade you can enjoy one of the most spectacular views of the city.

Why go. Duccio's Majesty and the view from the Façade.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo 8, Siena
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website operaduomo.siena.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
