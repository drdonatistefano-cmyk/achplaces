Museo Oggetti di civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Objects and photographs chronologically ordered from about 1850 to the end of the fifties: branches, pitchers and jars in terracotta, grinders, irons in coal, plow, shoemaker's counter, pails, cones and spindles, a room that reconstructs a domestic interior, a mantle from mourning of 1900 and clothes in broom fiber. Among the documents the first identity card of 1902, the year of municipal autonomy.

Why go. The rural memory of the Serre in a small civic museum.
