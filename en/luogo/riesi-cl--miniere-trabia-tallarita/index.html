Miniere Trabia-Tallarita

History and architecture · Archaeology

What remains of the plants and sulphur mines Trabia-Tallarita can be visited in the surroundings of the village, in the direction of Sommatino; the complex is documented in the Museo delle Solfare and it is a stop of the "Via delle Zolfare e dei Castelli" in Sicily of Mezzo.

Why go. The remains of the sulphur industry that Riesi made.
