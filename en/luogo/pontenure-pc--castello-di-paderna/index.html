Castello di Paderna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

An unknown castle, with damage and destruction documented in the 13th century; the Marazzani bought it in 1453. Today of the Petrelli family, it is an organic farm with educational farm, orchard and vegetable garden; the visit includes the square tower, the tower in the Water, the courtyards, the church of Santa Maria (cappella of the IX century), the peasant museum and the moat. In May and October it hosts the exhibition "Frutti del Castello" on ancient varieties. It is part of the Castles of the Duchy of Parma, Piacenza and Pontremoli.

Why go. Castle, moat and ancient fruits.
