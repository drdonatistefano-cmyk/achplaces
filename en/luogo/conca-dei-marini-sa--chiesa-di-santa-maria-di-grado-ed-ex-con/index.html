Chiesa di Santa Maria di Grado ed ex convento di Santa Rosa

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Monastery built overhanging the sea in 1681 by Sister Rosa Pandolfi, inhabited by Dominican cloistered nuns: in her kitchens was born the sfogliatella Santa Rosa. After the death of the last nun, in 1912, it became a hotel; the church guards the head of St. Barnabas, among the most important relics of the province.

Why go. Where the Santa Rosa Sfogliatella was born.
