Teatro comunale Giuseppe Verdi

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Elegant theatre with stage, audience and frescoed ceilings; it hosts mainly prose, from comedy to cabaret. Discount with the Touring card on the performances of the Apulian Public Theatre.

Why go. An Italian theater for an evening in the city.
