Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient parish church mentioned already in the 12th century, passed from Romanesque forms to a 16th century Gothic architecture, with 17th century bell tower, valuable wooden altars and a painting by Paolo Naurizio.

Why go. The historic parish church of the plateau.
