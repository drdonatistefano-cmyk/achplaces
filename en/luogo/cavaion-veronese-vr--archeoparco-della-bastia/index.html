Archeoparco della Bastia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

It is a site with medieval finds on Monte San Michele, where there was a fortified settlement on two terraces, probably a destination for pilgrimages.

Why go. Medieval archeology outdoors on the hill of Cavaion.
