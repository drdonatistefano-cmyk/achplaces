Villa Beatrice sul Monte Gemola

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

On the summit of Monte Gemola, the villa is owned by the Province of Padua and is open to the public: inside two educational exhibitions on the flora and fauna of the territory of Euganean, whose visit takes 30-45 minutes. From the Brolo and the meadows you can see Mount Fasolo, Venda, Monte Rusta and Cinto Euganeo.

Why go. Euganean nature and panorama from the top of Gemola.
