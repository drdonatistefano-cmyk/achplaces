Santuario della Beata Vergine del Rosario

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

The sanctuary built by the Dominicans between 1641 and 1660, the destination of pilgrims, with the neo-baroque façade of Lamberto Cusani of 1913.

Why go. One of the most visited Marian shrines in Emilia.
