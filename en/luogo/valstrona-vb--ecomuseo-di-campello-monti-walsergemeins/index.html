Ecomuseo di Campello Monti «Walsergemeinschaft Kampel»

Museums · Themed

Ecomuseum dedicated to the Walser culture of Campello Monti (Kampel), promoted since 1993 by the Walsergemeinschaft Kampel association with research initiatives; on the first Saturday of August it hosts the annual conference "Campello" and the Walser" .

Why go. To understand the Walser community in the valley.
