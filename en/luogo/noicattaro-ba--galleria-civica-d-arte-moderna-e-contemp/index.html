Galleria civica d'arte moderna e contemporanea (Palazzo della Cultura)

Museums · Modern art

In the Palazzo della Cultura, a former Carmelite convent renovated in 2001 and became a cultural centre accessible to all: paintings and sculptures by many local artists, the Nojani Arts Hall, the studio room (Sala Pende) dedicated to Professor Pende, a Nojan scientist and a room with permanent exhibition on the painter Dyalma Stultus. An archaeological section tells of the findings of the sixth century BC. (vases, bronze armor, worked sheets, goldsmiths) of the peucet necropolis of the districts Cipierno, Trisorio and Calcàro, including a bronze belt with a quadriga in running; according to the Municipality and the MiC these finds are kept or exhibited in the archaeological museums of Bari and Taranto. Booking not necessary.

Why go. The art of Nojani painters in a former convent of the center.
