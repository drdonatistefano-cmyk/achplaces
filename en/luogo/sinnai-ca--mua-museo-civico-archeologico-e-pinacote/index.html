MuA - Museo civico archeologico e pinacoteca

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the former House of the Colletta, once the Cavalleggeri barracks: the archaeological section was born from excavations and researches since 1994 and documents the territory from the recent Neolithic to the Middle Ages, with the findings of the nuraghe Santa Itroxia, Bruncu Mogumu and Papalinu. The most important piece is a punic terracotta statue of the god Bes; a fragment of the early medieval epigraphic mentions Saint Saturnino. The art gallery exhibits paintings by artists from the south-eastern Campidano from the 16th century to the present day.

Why go. The history of Sinnai from Neolithic to the Middle Ages, with the god Bes.

  • Where Via Colletta 20, Sinnai
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
