Palazzo Coronini Cronberg

Museums · House museums

A noble residence with original furnishings and collections on the aristocratic life between the Seven and the Nineteenth Century, from 1836 residence in exile of Charles X of Bourbon; in the park trees from all over the world.

Why go. The home of the Bourbons in exile.
