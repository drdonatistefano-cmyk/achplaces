Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish outside the historic centre, in front of the Waldensian Temple. The façade has a central body with tympanum supported by two pairs of pilasters, decorated in Baroque style with a fresco by the Eternal Father; the trilithic portal has a high frame in white stucco with the dedicatory inscription and a niche with the statue of St. John the Baptist.

Why go. A Baroque façade in front of the Waldensian temple.
