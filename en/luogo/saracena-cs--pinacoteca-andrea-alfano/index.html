Pinacoteca «Andrea Alfano»

Museums · Modern art

In Palazzo Mastromarchi, inaugurated on May 1, 1985 and dedicated to the Castrovillarese painter Andrea Alfano, born from a donation of the former members of the association "Sestium" and works donated by artists from all over Italy. There are 236 paintings and drawings and 23 20th century sculptures, with names such as Guttuso, Turcato, Treccani, Fazzini and Emilio Greco.

Why go. Guttuso and the 20th century in a village of Pollino.
