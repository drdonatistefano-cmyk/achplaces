Oratorio di San Faustino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Maybe of origin from the 11th century, as chapel of the hospice of the monks of San Faustino, with the current architecture of the early eighteenth century; it preserves a altarpiece with the patron saint of Giuseppe Tortelli.

Why go. A small oratory of medieval origin in the countryside.
