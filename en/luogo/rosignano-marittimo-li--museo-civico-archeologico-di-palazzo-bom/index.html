Museo civico archeologico di Palazzo Bombardieri

Museums · Archaeological

On three floors of the medieval palace, from prehistory to the Middle Ages: materials from the Etruscan necropolis including the stems of Castiglioncello, amphorae, anchors, coins and vases from the ancient commercial port of San Gaetano a Vada, late-medieval ceramics and majolica of the castle; on the ground floor the reconstruction of a Roman domus of the first century A.D.

Why go. From the Roman port of Vada to the castle.
