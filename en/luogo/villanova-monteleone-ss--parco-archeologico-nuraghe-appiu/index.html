Parco archeologico Nuraghe Appiu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the plateau of Chentu Mannas, about 500 meters at the foot of Mount Cuccu, overlooking the coast from Capo Caccia to Capo Marargiu: quadrilobed nuraghe with 15 meters high mastium and two overlapping rooms, covered courtyard, village of about 200 huts of the beginning of the Iron Age (X-IX century BC), tomb of giants in the cork grove, single tower nuraghe Punta 'e su Crabile, two dolmen, menhir and megalithic enclosure with megagon temple. Entrance fee; located 150 meters from the car park on easy path.

Why go. A nuragic village of 200 huts overlooking the sea.
