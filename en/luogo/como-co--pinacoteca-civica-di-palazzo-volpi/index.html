Pinacoteca civica di Palazzo Volpi

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated in 1989 in the seventeenth-century Palazzo Volpi, one of the four locations of the Civic Museums: the pictorial and sculptural heritage of the city up to the twentieth century, futurism, rationalism and design.

Why go. From ancient art to Como abstraction.
