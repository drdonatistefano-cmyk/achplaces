Palazzo Trimarchi e Bar Vitelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The eighteenth-century Palace which houses the Bar Vitelli, set de "The Godfather" of which it retains the sign; it is the first stage of the film itinerary and houses a collection of ancient objects and a Byzantine painting of St. John Chrysostom.

Why go. The Godfather's bar.
