Agenzia e borgo di Pollenzo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The square, the neo-Gothic church of San Vittore, the castle, the tower and the park made by Carlo Alberto after 1832, with the Agency created to experiment with new agricultural techniques and today seat of the University of Gastronomic Sciences and the Wine Bank. UNESCO World Heritage Site. The castle is a private residence.

Why go. The king's farm.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Pollenzo, Bra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
