Carta Museo d'impresa e Parco d'arte Rubbettino

Museums · Themed

Museum of the publishing house Rubbettino, born in 1972 from an idea of Rosario Rubbettino, who left the post of secretary of middle school: the first book, of philosophy, was printed entirely with lead, and in 1976 the first plant was born. The museum tells the story of the book in Calabria, from the processing of paper pulp to printing and bookbinding, and hosts conferences, seminars and festivals.

Why go. The story of the book told by a village publisher.
