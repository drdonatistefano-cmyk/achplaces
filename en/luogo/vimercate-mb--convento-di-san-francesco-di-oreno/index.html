Convento di San Francesco di Oreno

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded in the thirteenth century, it has a 16th century cloister; the church is an example of provincial architecture of the seventeenth century, with baroque bell tower of the same century.

Why go. 16th century cloister and baroque bell tower in the heart of Oreno.
