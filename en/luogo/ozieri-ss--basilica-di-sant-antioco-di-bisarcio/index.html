Basilica di Sant'Antioco di Bisarcio

Places of worship · Basilicas · Viewpoint

On the plain of Chilivani, outside the village: among the highest expressions of the Romanesque in Sardinia, built in volcanic stone in three phases from the 11th century, with a portico with six spans and decorated apse; it was diocesan seat.

Why go. The Sardinian Romanesque in the open countryside.
