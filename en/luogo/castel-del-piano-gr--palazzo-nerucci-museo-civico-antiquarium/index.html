Palazzo Nerucci: Museo civico, Antiquarium e Raccolta d'arte

Museums · Old masters

In the palace of 1554, recently restored, the civic museum and the art collection of a private collector: above all landscapes and views, with a pastel self-portrait of Rosalba Career, a portrait of Cosimo III de' Medici embroidered in silk and a sketch of Giuseppe Nicola Nasini.

Why go. Rosalba Career on the Amiata.
