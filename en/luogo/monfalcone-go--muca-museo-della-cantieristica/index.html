MuCa – Museo della Cantieristica

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The only museum of this kind in Italy, on the ground floor of the former workers' hotel in Panzano, the company city built in the 1920s by the Cosulichs around the yard, next to the ports where cruise ships are still built. More than a century of history of the yard and the city with large interactive videos, a sensory tunnel, 3D reconstructions and the simulator of a crane of the twenties; the path continues outdoors in the village of Panzano.

Why go. A century of great ships.

  • Where Via del Mercato 3, Panzano, Monfalcone
  • Entry €7 (reduced €5; free with FVGcard)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
