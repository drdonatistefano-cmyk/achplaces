Museo archeologico nazionale di Crotone

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Born from the collections of the Civic Museum of 1909 and opened by the State in 1968, on two floors: on the ground floor the history of Kroton with athletes, doctors and philosophers and the necropolis of Carrara; on the first floor the sanctuaries, with the treasure of Hera Lacinia, the gold diadem and the bronze nuragic boat of the VII century BC, and the askos shaped as a siren of the fifth century BC.

Why go. Hera's treasure.

  • Where Via Risorgimento, Crotone
  • Entry paid
