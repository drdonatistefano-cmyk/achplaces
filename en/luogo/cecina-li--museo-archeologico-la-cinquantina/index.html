Museo archeologico La Cinquantina

Museums · Archaeological

In Villa Guerrazzi, known as "La Cinquantina" (the Fiftyth century building), opened in 1980 and re-assembled in 2003: it tells the Lower Val di Cecina from the Paleolithic to the Roman villas, with the Montescudaio cinerary (VIII-VII century BC), the princely set of Casale Marittimo, the jewelry of Belora and, in the garden, the Etruscan tomb of Casaglia rebuilt.

Why go. The Etruscans of the valley, from Volterra to the sea.
