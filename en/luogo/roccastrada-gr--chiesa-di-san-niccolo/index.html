Chiesa di San Niccolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Cited from 1276, with an inscription of 1283; in the seventeenth century had the Latin cross plan, in 1822 the medieval apse was demolished and in 1953 covered the façade. Inside, frescoes from the middle of the sixteenth century attributed to Giovanni Maria di ser Giovanni Toulouse and a baptismal font from the sixteenth century.

Why go. The 16th century frescoes of the village.
