Biblioteca monumentale di Praglia

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The National Monumental Library of the Abbey preserves about 100,000 volumes; in the upper room 17 paintings by the late 16th century painter G.B. Zelotti decorate the wooden ceiling. You usually visit the second and fourth Saturdays of the month together with the visit to the monastery, with tickets bookable on Eventbrite; to climb there are about 50 steps.

Why go. The ceiling painted by Zelotti over 100,000 volumes.
