Ruderi dell'antico Convento di San Domenico

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Founded in 1510, the Dominican monastery reached its maximum splendor in the 17th century: about 23,000 square meters with five cloisters and a Latin cross church with six side chapels, entirely decorated in marble by mostly Roman and Neapolitan artists. Thomas Campanella stayed there. Destroyed by the earthquake of 1659 and rebuilt by Fr. Bonaventura Presti, it was ruined by the earthquake of February 5, 1783; it remains legible, with the facade of the ancient church declared a national monument.

Why go. The grand ruins of one of the largest convents in the South.
