The Two Towers: Asinelli and Garisenda

History and architecture · Towers · Paid entry · Viewpoint

A symbol of the troubled city. The donkey (1109-1119) is 97.20 m high; the Garisenda, lower and inclined, is not visited inside.

Why go. To see from Piazza di Porta Ravegnana even without climbing.

Donkeys closed from end 2023 for Garisenda's safety work. The reopening does not have a certain date: the local press indicates as hypothesis the 2028. Alternative: Clock Tower.

  • Where Piazza di Porta Ravegnana, 40126 Bologna
  • Entry Ascent normally paid (indicated €5 full / €3 reduced from secondary source). Now suspend.
  • Suggested time 45 min (climb)
  • Accessibility 498 steps, not accessible for disabled motors
  • Official website www.bolognawelcome.com
