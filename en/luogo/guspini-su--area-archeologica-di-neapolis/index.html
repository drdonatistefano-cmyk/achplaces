Area archeologica di Neapolis

History and architecture · Archaeology

A town founded by the Carthaginians at the end of the 5th century BC by the pond of Santa Maria, frequented by flamingos; then occupied by the Romans, who built baths, cisterns and roads.

Why go. Punics and Romans by the flamingo pond.
