Chiesa di Sant'Elia Profeta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the historical center that preserves the polychrome wooden statue of the Madonna of Calena, coming from the abbey of Calena, today reduced to ruins behind the marina.

Why go. The Madonna di Calena venerated by the village.
