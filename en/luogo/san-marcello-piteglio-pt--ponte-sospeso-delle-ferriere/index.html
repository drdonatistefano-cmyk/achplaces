Ponte sospeso delle Ferriere

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Walking pass 227 meters long and 36 meters high on the Lima stream, between Mammiano Basso and Popiglio, inaugurated in 1923 after about two years of work: the scaffolding rests on four steel ropes without intermediate supports. Proposed by engineer Vincenzo Douglas Scotti to shorten the route of the workers of Popiglio to the ironworks on the other bank, it was the longest suspended pedestrian bridge in the world until 2006.

Why go. Walk suspended at 36 meters on the Lima.
