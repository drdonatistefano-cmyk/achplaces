Torre civica (Torre Maestra)

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

55 meters high, started in 1555 under Venice in the center of the Citadel; in the Venetian era it hosted the men's prison. In 1880 the metal dome was replaced by the medieval crenellated crown of Antonio Tagliaferri; it has three bells dedicated to the patrons and the mechanism of the clock is visible inside.

Why go. From the mullioned windows the view of the village and the Fortress.
