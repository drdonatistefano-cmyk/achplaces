Antica Pieve di San Bartolomeo

History and architecture · Archaeology

Ancient parish church of Bornato dating back to the X-XI centuries, now an archaeological site; the Municipality has set up a visitor path with panels and audio guide (Italian and English) that leads from the castle to the parish. Each year it is the destination of the landscape and archaeological walk on the traces of Christendom in Franciacorta, organized by Pro Loco with the association U.S.P.A.A.A. (€5; info pievesanbartolomeobornato@gmail.com).

Why go. The medieval origins of Franciacorta, among vineyards and hills.
