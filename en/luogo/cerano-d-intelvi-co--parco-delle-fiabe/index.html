Parco delle Fiabe

Nature and landscape · City parks

Themed area in the woods designed to be inclusive and accessible, with statues inspired by classical fairy tales (from the Little Prince to the Little Mermaid) and the Sword in the rock, braille panels and events and shows.

Why go. A walk in the woods for families.
