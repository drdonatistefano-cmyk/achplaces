Area archeologica dei Forni fusori di Rondelli

History and architecture · Archaeology

Etruscan site of steel production between the beginning of the 6th and the end of the 5th century BC, linked to Populonia and Vetulonia, where the hematite of Elba was worked: an industrial area with ovens and a housing; the land conserves slag, coal and cookware.

Why go. The Etruscan origins of Follonica iron.
