Palazzo Bisaccioni, sale museali

Museums · Old masters

On Piazza Colocci, home of the Fondazione Cassa di Risparmio di Jesi, with an art collection from ancient to contemporary; it is part of the urban museum network.

Why go. Ancient and contemporary art in a historic building.
