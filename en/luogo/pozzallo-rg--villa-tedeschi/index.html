Villa Tedeschi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

16th century building whose premises and gardens tell the history of the city and of the noble families that succeeded each other; symbol of the family that ruled Pozzallo, today houses the municipal library.

Why go. The historic residence of the lords of Pozzallo.
