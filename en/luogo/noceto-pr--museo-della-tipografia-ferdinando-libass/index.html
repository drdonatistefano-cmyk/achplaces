Museo della tipografia Ferdinando Libassi

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The historic typography . The nocetana graphics, purchased by the Municipality by the family of the master printer Ferdinando Libassi: a press From the Garden of the second half of the nineteenth century, a Monzese pedal, a nineteenth-century plate of the Mordini brothers and a Nebiolo plate, all working, and millions of characters, friezes and reordered fillets. Open Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday 8.30-12.30; Saturday and Sunday by appointment.

Why go. Printing machines still working.

  • Where Via Dalla Chiesa 30, Noceto
  • Entry €3
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
