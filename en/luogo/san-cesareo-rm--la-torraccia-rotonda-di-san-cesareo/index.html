La Torraccia (Rotonda di San Cesareo)

History and architecture · Archaeology

Remains of a large Roman building from the beginning of the fourth century, in the residence of the Emperor Maxentius: a two-level nymph, square outside and circular inside, with a circular exedra in each of the four lower corners, vaulted with octagonal coffers and walls once covered with polychrome marbles.

Why go. The nymph of an emperor's villa.
