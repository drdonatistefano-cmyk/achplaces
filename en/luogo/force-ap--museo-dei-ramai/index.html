Museo dei Ramai

Museums · Themed

Opened at the end of 2005 in the underground rooms of the ancient Palazzo Canestrari, it collects household objects of the copper forcese handicrafts, including the stills called "tamburla" (tamburla) used to distill the mixture from wine. The educators and artisans of the Terravita association organize workshops for children and young people (SpazioLab).

Why go. Five centuries of copper beaten by Force's caldera.
