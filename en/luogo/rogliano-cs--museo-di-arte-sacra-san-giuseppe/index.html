Museo di arte sacra «San Giuseppe»

Museums · Old masters

It is located in the former church of Santa Maria Maggiore in Santo Spirito, an eighteenth-century building wanted by the Congregation of the Noble Ones; it collects local heritage from the 16th to the 18th century, largely from the former convent of the Capuchins: paintings, silver sacred vestments, liturgical vestments and carved wooden works of the local masters, active especially in Rogliano and other areas of Calabria. It has a room for educational activities.
