Museo civico delle produzioni artistiche dell'artigianato popolare

Museums · Themed

In the valley of the village, a few meters from the ancient nucleus along the ancient road of the valley of Fredane, it develops on three levels: municipal library, cart room and sculpture room on the ground floor, and on the first floor a monumental nativity scene of about 35 square meters in stocks of elm and olive trees. The wooden and straw wagon, about 30 meters high, is exposed with facade, 1:5 scale model and dome; more than half of the works are gifts of Italian and foreign artists, and the garden has sculptures, a fountain and a copy of the Nike of Samothrace. Hours from the MiC card: Monday-Friday 9,30-12 and 15-17,30; Saturday and Sunday by reservation (0825 475003, 320 6913966).
