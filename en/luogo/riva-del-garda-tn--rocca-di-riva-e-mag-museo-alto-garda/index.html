Rocca di Riva e MAG Museo Alto Garda

Museums · City history · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Castle built in the twelfth century with moat to defend port and inhabited, then noble residence and barracks; it houses the MAG with the gallery of landscapers of the nineteenth century attracted by Garda and works of Pietro Ricchi, Vincenzo Vela and Francesco Hayez, the archaeological section with the prehistoric statues-stele of Arco, finds of the palafittes of Ledro and San Martino di Campi, and the history of Riva and Bass Sarca between Scaligeri, Visconti and Venice until the years of Thomas Mann and Franz Kafka. From the Mastio view of the city and lake; the garden of the Rocca is also open to closed museum.

Why go. The castle on the water that tells the Upper Garda.

  • Where Piazza C. Battisti 3/A, Riva del Garda
  • Entry €5
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
