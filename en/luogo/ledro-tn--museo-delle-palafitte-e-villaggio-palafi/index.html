Museo delle Palafitte e villaggio palafitticolo

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Museum of the MUSE built around the remains of a village of the Bronze Age, inhabited between 2200 and 1350 BC and re-emerged in 1929 during the lowering of the lake for hydroelectric works; since 2011 it is part of the prehistoric palafittic sites of the Alpine arch, UNESCO World Heritage. Original finds and, on the shore, huts rebuilt and furnished on catwalks. Open every day: March-June and September-December 9-17, July and August 10-18; family ticket €5 or €10.

Why go. A village 4,000 years ago on the lake.

  • Where Via al Lago 1, Molina di Ledro
  • Entry €5, reduced €4, free under 14
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info
