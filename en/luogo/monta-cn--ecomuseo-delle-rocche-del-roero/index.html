Ecomuseo delle Rocche del Roero

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Museo diffuso founded in 2003 on the initiative of the Piedmont Region, dedicated to the dorsal of the Rocks, geological formation of about 250,000 years born from the change of course of the Tanaro and more than 30 km long, and to the eight historical villages arose after the Mille on the ridges. Paths through woods, vineyards and Rocche to walk or mountain bike, with geolocalized audio guides on legends and nature; map and audio guide to the site, free GPS tracks on the site. Office open from Tuesday to Saturday 9.30-12.30.

Why go. The Roero Rocks, on foot or by bike.
