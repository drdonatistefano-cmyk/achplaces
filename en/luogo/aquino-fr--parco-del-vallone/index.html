Parco del Vallone

Nature and landscape · City parks · Viewpoint

Park on the Vallone d'Aquino, reclaimed and renovated, with views of the surrounding nature and the local caves of travertine; it is part of the complex of the House of San Tommaso and is open on request.

Why go. Walk between nature and travertine.
