Museo storico della Resistenza

Museums · City history

A small museum at the end of the second millennium in the former elementary school of St. Anne; it tells the historical context of the massacre of 12 August 1944, in which the SS killed 560 people including many children, and the contribution of Versilia to the Liberation. Outside, a plaque with the epigraph of Calamandrei in Kesselring and a carved reproduction of a detail of Picasso's "Guernica"; the exhibition space is connected to the Ossario Monument on the Col di Cava.

Why go. The place to understand one of the most atrocious Nazi massacres in Italy.
