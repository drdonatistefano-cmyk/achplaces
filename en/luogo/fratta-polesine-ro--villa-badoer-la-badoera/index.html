Villa Badoer (La Badoera)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage

Commissioned by Francesco Badoer to Andrea Palladio: the manor body existed already in 1557 and the villa appears, with some difference, in the Four Books (1570). It stands on a base with scenic staircase and recalls an ancient temple between terraces; pronao and interiors were decorated by Giallo Fiorentino with grotesques and mythological and allegorical themes, rediscovered in the restorations of the Regional Institute Ville Venete. Visit by reservation (tel. 366 324 0619).

Why go. A villa of Palladio UNESCO heritage.
