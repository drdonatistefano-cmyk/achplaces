Oasi WWF Grotte del Bussento

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Canyon covered with vegetation of about 600 hectares in the Cilento National Park: from the village a path along the stream, between springs, waterfalls and an ancient stone mill, descends to the cave where the Bussento re-emerges, with a portal about 20 meters high, a staircase dug in limestone and wooden walkways; it is geosite of the park. Visits by reservation, with seasonal times (last admission two hours before closing time).

Why go. The river that comes from the rock.

visits only by reservation, closed on rainy days
