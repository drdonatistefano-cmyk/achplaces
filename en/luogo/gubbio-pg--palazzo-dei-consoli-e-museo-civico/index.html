Palazzo dei Consoli e Museo Civico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built between 1332 and 1349 on a project by Angelo da Orvieto with the Gattapone, it dominates Piazza Grande; from the ground floor to the last it houses the archaeological museum, where the Eugubine Tables in Umbrian language (III-I century BC) and the art gallery are exhibited.

Why go. The Eugubine Tables in the symbolic palace.

  • Where Piazza Grande, Gubbio
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
