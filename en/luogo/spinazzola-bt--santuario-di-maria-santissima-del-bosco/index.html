Santuario di Maria Santissima del Bosco

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

At the municipal woods, it preserves the image of Our Lady of the Bosco, brought in procession to the Mother Church on Tuesday after Easter and accompanied back to the sanctuary in August.

Why go. The shrine of the patron saint in the woods.
