Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

Baroque church between the late seventeenth and early eighteenth centuries designed by Domenico Antonio Vaccaro, with the majolica floor by Leonardo Chianese designed by Francesco Solimena depicting the expulsion from Paradise.

Why go. The earthly paradise under your feet.
