Museo Civico Archeologico di Remedello

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Set up in the former church of the Disciplini Bianchi, a single large hall with walls entirely frescoed in the sixteenth century with the Life and Passion of Christ, attributed to the local painter Orazio De Rossi. The route goes from Prehistory to the Middle Ages and revolves around the relationship between man and the river Churches, with findings of Remedello and the municipalities between the provinces of Brescia, Mantua and Cremona; there are five bronze swords, perhaps offered to the gods of the waters, found in the rivers Churches and Mella. Open Wednesday-Friday 9-13, Saturday 14-18, Sunday 9-12 and 14-19.

Why go. Prehistoric finds under a cycle of 16th century frescoes.
