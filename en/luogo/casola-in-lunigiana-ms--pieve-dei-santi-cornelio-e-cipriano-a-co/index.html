Pieve dei Santi Cornelio e Cipriano a Codiponte

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Cited from the end of the 8th century and already a garrison of the diocese of Luni, it is considered a symbol of the Romanesque in Lunigiana: in sandstone, with protruding façade, carved reliefs and capitals storied with symbols of medieval beasts. It rises on the right bank of the Aulella near a medieval bridge, beyond which there are the ruins of an 11th century castle, along the Via del Volto Santo. Inside a late medieval triptych with the Virgin enthroned between the Saints Cornelius and Cyprian, with the predella depicting the transport of the Holy Face from Lucca.

Why go. The most beautiful Romanesque of Lunigiana, with medieval beast capitals.
