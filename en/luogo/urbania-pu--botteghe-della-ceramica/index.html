Botteghe della ceramica

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Ceramic city from the Middle Ages, when it was called Castel delle Ripe, worked the clay of the Metauro; under the Montefeltro the shops multiplied and here was born in 1524 the ceramist Cipriano Piccolpasso. Today the majolica workshops organize courses throughout the year.

Why go. The majolica Durantina live.
