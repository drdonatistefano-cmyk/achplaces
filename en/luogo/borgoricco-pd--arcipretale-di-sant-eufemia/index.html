Arcipretale di Sant'Eufemia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient parish church that gave its name to the village, mentioned in the testament of Speronella of 1192; rebuilt in 1946 with a single nave, it preserves a baptismal font in marble dated 1490 and three altarpieces of remarkable artistic value.

Why go. A millenarian parish church with a fifteenth-century baptismal font.
