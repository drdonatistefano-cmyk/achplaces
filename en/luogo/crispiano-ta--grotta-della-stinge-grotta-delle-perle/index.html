Grotta della Stinge (Grotta delle Perle)

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

The most famous cave in the area, at the eastern edge of the Parco Terra delle Gravine where karstism shapes the landscape, with stalactites, stalagmites and characteristic roundish karst formations.

Why go. The forms of the karst of the gravines.
