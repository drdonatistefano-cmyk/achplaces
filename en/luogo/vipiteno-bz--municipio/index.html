History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between 1468 and 1473 in the center of the New City and completed in 1524, with Jörg Kölderer's multi-gonal crenellated erker; the Gothic council hall has wooden walls and ceiling beams, the most beautiful of its kind in all Tyrol, and the palace houses the Sala Vigil Raber, one of the oldest theatres in Tyrol, and an art collection. In the courtyard a marble copy of a Roman relief of Mithras; the Tyrolean Diet met there for the first time in 1502 with Massimiliano.

Why go. The most beautiful wooden Gothic hall in Tyrol.
