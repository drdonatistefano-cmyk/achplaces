Chiesa Madre di San Pietro Apostolo e ruderi del castello

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the historic center the Mother Church rises next to the ruins of the castle, built according to the Municipality by the Beneventan princes and by Ludovico II, then abandoned and demolished by decision of the authorities in the thirties of the twentieth century. The Madonna del Bosco is brought to the Mother Church in procession on Tuesday after Easter.

Why go. The ancient heart of the country.
