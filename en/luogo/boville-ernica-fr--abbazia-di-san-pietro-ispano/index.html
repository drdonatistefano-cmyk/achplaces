Abbazia di San Pietro Ispano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Embedded in the Filonardi complex, it is probably where the Spanish hermit Pietro Ispano lived (X-XI centuries): the crypt of the tenth century is on the cave of the saint. In the Chapel Simoncelli there is the Angel of Giotto, a mosaic fragment of the ancient basilica of St. Peter and the only surviving mosaic of the artist, a marble bas-relief of the Madonna attributed to the Sansovino, a silver bust attributed to Benvenuto Cellini with the relics of the saint, the porphyry cross of the first Jubilee (1300) and a paleochristian sarcophagus (330-350 A.D.), found in 1941, with a Nativity bas-relief considered the oldest carved.

Why go. Giotto's mosaic and the oldest Nativity.
