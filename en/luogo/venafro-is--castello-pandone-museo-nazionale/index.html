Castello Pandone – Museo nazionale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

On Sannitic and Roman walls, Longobard fence of the tenth century with towers, enlarged by the Angioini after the earthquake of 1349 with three cylindrical towers and the moat; in the sixteenth century Count Enrico Pando transformed it into a residence and had the halls decorated with horses of his stable, relief painted in front, each with name, age, race and H of Henricus. 14th century staircase, noble floor, loggia, Salone dei Conti and garden; it is home to the National Museum, which also preserves the Polyptych of the Passion of Christ.

Why go. Count Henry's painted horses.
