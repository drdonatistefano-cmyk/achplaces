Cripta Rasponi e Giardini Pensili

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Viewpoint

Behind the facade of the Palazzo della Provincia, hanging gardens on several levels and a crypt with 6th century mosaics from San Severo di Classe, under a neo-Gothic tower.

Why go. A secret garden above the square.

  • Where Piazza San Francesco 7, Ravenna
  • Entry To be verified: cumulative ticket with TAMO and Domus Stone Mats
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
