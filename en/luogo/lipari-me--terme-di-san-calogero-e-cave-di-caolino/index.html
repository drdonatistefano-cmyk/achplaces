Terme di San Calogero e cave di caolino

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · UNESCO World Heritage

Baths transformed into museums, with a tomb of Mycenaean origin; they can be reached with a challenging trekking from the quarries of the Pleasant, passing through the sulfur fumaroles and a geo-park.

Why go. Trekking among the most ancient spas and fumaroles.
