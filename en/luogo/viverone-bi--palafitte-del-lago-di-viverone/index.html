Palafitte del Lago di Viverone

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

A circular village of 70 metres in diameter of the medium bronze (1450-1400 BC), founded on almost five thousand poles; UNESCO site since 2011 among the prehistoric palafittic sites of the Alps. You don't visit: it is only approached by boat without engine and an installation is on the opposite side, near Viverone.

Why go. The submerged village.
