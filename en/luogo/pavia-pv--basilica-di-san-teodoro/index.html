Basilica di San Teodoro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Romanesque church among medieval alleys, with frescoes in which the 16th century Pavia is mirrored.

Why go. An ancient view of the city painted in church.

  • Where Piazza San Teodoro 9, Pavia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it
