Sentiero ed ex gallerie minerarie del Cornacchino

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

One of the oldest mines in Amiata, reachable with a short detour from the mine route that starts from Castell'Azzara: the narrow galleries, where women and children worked in the 19th century, can be visited safely today.

Why go. The galleries of the oldest mine in Amiata.
