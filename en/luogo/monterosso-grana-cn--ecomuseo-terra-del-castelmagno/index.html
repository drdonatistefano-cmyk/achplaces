Ecomuseo Terra del Castelmagno

Museums · Themed

Ecomuseum spread over the history and traditions of the Grana Valley linked to the cheese Castelmagno, based in San Pietro: the museum has an interactive set up on the processing and history of cheese, and in the country figures of straw in ancient clothes, the "babaciu" , tell the mountain life between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries. The route also includes mills, sawmills and hydroelectric power plants and the Extramuseum, a family ring of 4 km with ten totems (magic folder €10). Open Saturday and Sunday or by reservation.

Why go. The Castelmagno and the life of the valley, between museum and path.
