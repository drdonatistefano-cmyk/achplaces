Museo archeologico nazionale «Vincenzo Laviola»

Museums · Archaeological

Dedicated to the physician and scholar of antiquity Vincenzo Laviola, it traces the population of the High Ionian from the indigenous cultures of Bronze and Iron to Greek colonization. It exhibits the collection Laviola (metal objects and protohistoric ceramics of the XII-VIII century BC) and the findings of the excavations of the Superintendence coordinated by Juliette de la Genière: necropolis of Paladino and Mangosa and archaic settlement of St. Nicholas (late VII-VI century BC, about 20 hectares), from which come over a thousand weights from the frame, some with female names in achean alphabet. Two multimedia stations in four languages.

Why go. Magna Grecia before and after Sibari.
