Chiesa di San Giacomo Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Gothic-Catalan parish built between 1585 and 1605 by the picapedres Gontinio Pinna and Miguel Baldabella; on the portal the coat of arms of the Carroz, lords of the Duchy. Inside the nave with juniper beams, five chapels per side and presbytery with starry vault; it houses a wooden group with Crucifix, Madonna and St. John, polychrome statues of the seventeenth century, wooden altars of the eighteenth century, tabernacle and baptismal font in eighteenth-century marble. At the exit there is sa perda de sa bregungia, ancient medieval gogna.

Why go. The Gothic-Catalan duke of Mandas.
