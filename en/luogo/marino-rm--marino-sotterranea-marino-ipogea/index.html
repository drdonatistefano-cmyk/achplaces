Marino Sotterranea (Marino Ipogea)

History and architecture · Archaeology

Labyrinth of caves and galleries connected, for tens of kilometers, dug from the Middle Ages in the pepper under the historic center to obtain building material; for centuries there were preserved barrels of wine and in war served as anti-aircraft shelters. Some galleries have been cleaned up and transformed into an exhibition path with the finds of the restorations; visible the walls with the eruptive phases of the Lazio volcano. Guided tours all year round on weekends, by reservation at Archeoclub Colli Albani.

Why go. A city under the city, between barrels and shelters of war.
