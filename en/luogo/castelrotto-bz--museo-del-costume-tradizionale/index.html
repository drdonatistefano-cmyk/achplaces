Museo del costume tradizionale

Museums · Themed

In the Laechler residence, next to Kraus Square, exhibits South Tyrolean costumes worn in festivals and popular celebrations. Open all year round, Monday-Saturday 8.30-12 and 14-18; in the same place the museum of nostalgic skiing, on the evolution of skiing in the decades.

Why go. Tyrolean party costumes.
