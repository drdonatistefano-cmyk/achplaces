MuDOP - Museo Diocesano Oppido-Palmi

Museums · Old masters

In a building next to the cathedral designed by Achille Menghi (first stone 1938), eight rooms in chronological order with liturgical objects, paintings and sculptures in wood and stone from churches and convents of the diocese, with ample space to the earthquake of 1783. A St. Sebastiano di Benedetto da Maiano (second half of the 15th century), exhibited in Matera, the Louvre and the Castello Sforzesco between 2019 and 2021, is the main work. Times indicated: Monday, Wednesday and Friday 8-14, Saturday 9-12.

Why go. The San Sebastiano passed by the Louvre.

Reservation required according to the MiC card (updated to January 2022)
