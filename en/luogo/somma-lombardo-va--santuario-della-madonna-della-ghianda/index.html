Santuario della Madonna della Ghianda

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Sanctuary in the hamlet of Mezzana, linked to the legend of a 13th century Marian apparition among the branches of an oak, scene that Michelino da Besozzo frescoed in the vault. Pellegrino de' Pellegrini enlarged the structure by designing the current sanctuary, with a large altar in front of the small original apse that leaves the fresco visible; the facade is decorated with statues.

Why go. A fresco by Michelino da Besozzo kept inside the architecture of Pellegrini.
