Museo del peperoncino

Museums · Themed

In the historic Palazzo Ducale, managed by the Accademia Italiana del Peperoncino: salt on tools and objects ('Fantasia of every part') on the geographical origins of the plant, on seeds, spices and sauces, and a video room with posters and satirical cartoons; among the works a lithography by Renato Guttuso and an image from a recipe book of the sixteenth century.

Why go. The spice museum symbol of Diamond.
