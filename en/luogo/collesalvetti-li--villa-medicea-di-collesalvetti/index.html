Villa Medicea di Collesalvetti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Probably built at the beginning of the 15th century, it was bought by Lorenzo and Giuliano de' Medici in 1476; it was a hunting residence and center of the Grand Duke's Farm, which counted over twenty farms.

Why go. The Medici origin of the country.
