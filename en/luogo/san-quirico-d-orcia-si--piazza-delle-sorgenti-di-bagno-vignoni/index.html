Piazza delle Sorgenti di Bagno Vignoni

Local life and traditions · Spas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The square of Bagno Vignoni is a bathtub of thermal water 49 meters long and 29 meters wide, already known in Roman times; it is not bathing, but at sunset the steam envelops the stone houses.

Why go. A unique water square in Italy.

  • Where Bagno Vignoni, San Quirico d'Orcia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
