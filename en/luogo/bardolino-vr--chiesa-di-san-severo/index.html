Chiesa di San Severo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church linked to archaeological finds of Roman times, which preserves evidence of Verona's Romanesque art. It is included in the virtual tour of the historical sites of the Municipality, with audio guide in Italian, English and German.

Why go. Verona Romanesque art on remains of the Roman age.
