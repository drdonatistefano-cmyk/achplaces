Distilleria di whisky PUNI

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

First distillery of whiskey in Italy, built in 2012 on a project by Werner Tscholl just outside the walls: a 13-metre brick cube inspired by the perforated walls of the barns, with vats and stills in a vaulted basement.

Why go. Contemporary architecture and Alpine whisky.
