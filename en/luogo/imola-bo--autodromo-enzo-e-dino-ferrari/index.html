Autodromo Enzo e Dino Ferrari

Local life and traditions · Motors · Paid entry

The circuit of Formula 1 and the World Superbike, which can be visited in about an hour and a half; at the entrance the Checco Costa Museum.

Why go. The paddock and the curve of the Tamburello told by those who work there.

  • Where Piazza Ayrton Senna da Silva 2, Imola
  • Entry Guided tours for a fee: rates on Bologna Welcome
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
