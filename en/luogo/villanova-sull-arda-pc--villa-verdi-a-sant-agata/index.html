Villa Verdi a Sant'Agata

Museums · House museums

Verdi bought the property in 1848, enlarged it following sketches and indications of his fist and lived there since 1851 with Giuseppina Strepponi. The museum path, in one part of the building, includes the original rooms, the bedroom-study, the pianos used by the Master, portraits, autographs, the music library and the reconstruction of the room of the Grand Hotel in Milan where he died on January 27, 1901. Around, the English park designed by Verdi, with pond, rare plants, sculptures by Giuseppe Torretti and the burials of his dogs. In December 2024 the MiC signed the decree of expropriation that acquires it to the State, with urgent interventions of restoration.

Why go. The house where Verdi lived and composed.

Visit Piacenza indicates it is simultaneously closed and the MiC card closed every day; acquired by the State by decree of expropriation of 16/12/2024 and urgent restoration planned
