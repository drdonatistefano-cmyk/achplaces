Uva fragola e cucina lepina

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The local speciality is the strawberry grape, celebrated in October with a festival; among the typical products also olive oil, goat meat, vegetable soup "rappagacornuti" and "ciammotte" (lumache).

Why go. The flavors of Lepini, strawberry grape on the head.
