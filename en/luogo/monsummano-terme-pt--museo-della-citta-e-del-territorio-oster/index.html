Museo della Città e del Territorio – Osteria dei Pellegrini

Museums · City history

In the Osteria dei Pellegrini built in 1607-1616 by Ferdinando de' Medici, on a project by Mechini, to welcome the pilgrims of the sanctuary: it tells the Valdinievole and the reclamation of the Padule di Fucecchio.

Why go. The Valdinievole and its marshes.
