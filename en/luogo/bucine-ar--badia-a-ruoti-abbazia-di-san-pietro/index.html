Badia a Ruoti (abbazia di San Pietro)

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Abbey dating back to the year 1000, originally belonged to the Camaldolese monks, then besieged and transformed. Inside the frescoes of the sixteenth century and the Coronation of the Virgin of Neri di Bicci (1472); near there is a secular oak tree, one of the "trees" of memory.

Why go. The Coronation of Blacks of Bicci in a millenarian abbey.
