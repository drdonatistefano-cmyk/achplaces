Castello dei Morra Lavriano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built in 1363 by the Roeros and completely rebuilt by the Isnardi in 1647; it was plundered in 1544 by the imperial troops of the Marquis of Vasto and in 1691 the French of Catinat burned a branch of it. It has a round decorated tower, loggia, hall of honor, scenic portal and a large garden; inside frescoes and trompe-l'oeil, a tunnel, a well with wheel for water and prisons.

Why go. A castle of the Roeros among frescoes and prisons.
