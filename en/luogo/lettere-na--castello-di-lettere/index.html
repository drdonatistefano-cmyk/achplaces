Castello di Lettere

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Wanted in the 10th century by the Duke of Amalfi to guard the northern borders, part of the network of fortifications that controlled the two slopes of the Lattari; documents of 1030 and 1033 describe it as a fortified village. The trapezoidal castle preserves four towers, the highest was the mast; within the walls the cathedral, bishop's seat since 987, with 12th century bell tower decorated with tarsies, and the Grano Tower. The museum exhibits ceramics, bronzes and animal remains from excavations started in 2007, from the 10th to the 16th century. Free with the ticket of Pompeii of the same day.

Why go. View from the Gulf to Vesuvius from an Amalfi fortress.

Seasonal hours (Nov-Apr only Thursday-Sunday; May-Oct closed on Tuesday); price €2 on the MiC card against €5 on the Park website
