Museo della Ceramica

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The majolica of Montelupo from the Middle Ages to the nineteenth century, found in the "Pozzo dei Lavatoi," with a large Renaissance collection.

Why go. Then a ride to the ceramists' shops.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Veneto 10, Montelupo Fiorentino
  • Entry €6 whole (with the Archaeological Museum); €4 reduced; free on the first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.museomontelupo.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
