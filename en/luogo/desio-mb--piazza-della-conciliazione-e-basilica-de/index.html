Piazza della Conciliazione e basilica dei Santi Siro e Materno

History and architecture · Squares and old town

The square overlooks the Basilica of Santi Siro and Materno, symbol of the city, with the bell tower of the early fifteenth century, and the monument to Pope Pius XI, born in Desio.

Why go. The heart of the birthplace of Pius XI.
