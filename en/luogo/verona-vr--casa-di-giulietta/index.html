Casa di Giulietta

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage

Casa-torre of the 13th century of the Cappello family, the Capulet of the legend: the hall covered with love cards, the courtyard with balcony and statue of Juliet by Nereo Costantini, the interior with furnishings and costumes of the film by Zeffirelli.

Why go. The most famous balcony in the world.
