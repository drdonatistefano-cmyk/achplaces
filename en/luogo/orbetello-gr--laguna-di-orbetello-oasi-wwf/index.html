Laguna di Orbetello – Oasi WWF

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

The lagoon between two tomboli, historical oasis of the WWF in Tuscany, where you can see numerous species of birds, including flamingos.

Why go. The flamingos are just a stone's throw from the center.
