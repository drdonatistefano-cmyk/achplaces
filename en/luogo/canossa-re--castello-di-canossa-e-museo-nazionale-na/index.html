Castello di Canossa e Museo nazionale Naborre Campanini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The ruins on the white cliff where in January 1077 the emperor Henry IV waited for the forgiveness of Pope Gregory VII; the museum exhibits the finds of the nineteenth-century excavations.

Why go. The place of the humiliation of Canossa.
