Cripta del Crocifisso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Also known as the SS. Crucifix or Santa Costantina, is a natural cavity transformed into a place of worship by the Byzantines in the 11th century, in the open countryside in Manfio district, on the border between Casarano, Ruffano and Taurisano: frescoed walls, paleolithic graffiti and fossils of neolithic communities; near the ruins of a cenobe of the Basilian monks and then of the olivetans. Access with stairs.

Why go. A frescoed cave inhabited since prehistoric times.
