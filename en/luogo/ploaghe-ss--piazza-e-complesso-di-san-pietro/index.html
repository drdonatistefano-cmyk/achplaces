Piazza e complesso di San Pietro

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

On the square are gathered the parish church of St. Peter the Apostle, former cathedral of the fifteenth century several times rebuilt, the oratory of St. Cross of the sixteenth century, the oratory of the Rosary of the eighteenth century and the parish house with the archive of ancient writings and documents; the town hall occupies the former Granatic mountain.

Why go. The religious and civil heart of the country.
