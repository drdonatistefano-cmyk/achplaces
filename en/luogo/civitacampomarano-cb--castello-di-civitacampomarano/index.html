Castello di Civitacampomarano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Castle on a sandstone hill at 520 m, perhaps of Norman origin, rebuilt in the fifteenth century with quadrangular belt and three cylindrical crenellated towers; the Catalan-Aragonese portal bears the coat of arms Carafa della Spina, Di Sangro and Monforte and a dragon with upside down lilies, symbol of the betrayal to the Angioini. You can visit the six-arched loggia, the courtyard with well and cistern, the noble rooms with painting decorations, stables and barn, towers and round paths; national monument since 1979, from 2023 houses a nucleus of contemporary art with works by William Congdon, Gino Marotta and Titina Maselli. Open from Tuesday to Sunday (closed on Mondays), tickets also through the official app.

Why go. One of the most significant monuments of Molise, well preserved, with contemporary art in the rooms.

  • Where Largo Vincenzo Cuoco, 86030 Civitacampomarano (CB)
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
