Ecomuseo del vino e della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Set up in the rooms of the former restored prison, it exhibits objects of vinification and peasant life of a time in a path in four sections: environment, grape, cleft and religious rituals; woman and machines; in the cell of isolation the cholera revolt is linked to the epidemics of the nineteenth and twentieth centuries; the house. A video shows the ancient traditions. Booking not required; MiC (2020) does not report up-to-date times.

Why go. Verbicaro's wine in the former prison.
