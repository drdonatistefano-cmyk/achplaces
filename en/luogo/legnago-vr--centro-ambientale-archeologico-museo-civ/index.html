Centro Ambientale Archeologico - Museo civico Pianura di Legnago

Museums · Archaeological

In an Austrian military building of the early nineteenth century, already barracks of French cavalry and then military hospital, restored in 1999: exhibits the finds of the Verona plain collected in the last thirty years, about 5,000 years of history from the ancient Neolithic (V millennium BC) to the late Iron Age (II-I century BC). Workshops for families and schools; since 2010 it is part of the Legnago Museums network managed by Fondazione Fioroni.

Why go. Five thousand years of prehistory of the Verona plain.
