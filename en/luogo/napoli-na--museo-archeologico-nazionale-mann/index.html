Museo Archeologico Nazionale (MANN)

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

One of the most important archaeological museums in the world, with collections from the Vesuvian cities and the Secret Cabinet.

Why go. The museum that explains Pompeii.
