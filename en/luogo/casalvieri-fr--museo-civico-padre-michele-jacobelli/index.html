Museo civico «Padre Michele Jacobelli»

Museums · Archaeological

Archaeological collection in the church of Santa Maria Assunta, started in 1958 by Father Michele Jacobelli and acquired by the Municipality in 1978: fossils, blocks of the walls of the pre-Roman city of Cominium and former Roman anatomical vote (III-I century BC) from the votive crest of Pescarola; also exhibits musical instruments, agricultural tools and popular costumes from various parts of the world.

Why go. The exhibits of Cominium and the former vote of Pescarola.

opening on request (tel. 0776 639327)
