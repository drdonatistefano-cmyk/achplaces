Museo etnografico dei mestieri del fiume «Roberto Coffani»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Founded in 1998 on the banks of the Mincio, in the heart of the Riserva Valli del Mincio, it is set up in a typical fishing house partly recreated and hosts the Mincio Park Visitor Centre. It exhibits objects of everyday life and work in swamps collected by the inhabitants: tools for the cultivation of the marsh cane and of the carice, fishing tools and hunting, tools and domestic furnishings, with a space on flora and fauna of the protected area. Free of charge up to 5 years; open Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday with different times, other days on request.

Why go. The marsh crafts told in a fishing house on the river.
