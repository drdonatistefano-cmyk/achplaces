Parco Mediceo di Pratolino, Villa Demidoff

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The "garden of wonders" wanted by Francesco I de' Medici, with the Colossus of the Apennines of Giambologna, part of the UNESCO site of the Medici villas and gardens.

Why go. A stone giant in the woods.
