Oratorio della Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small church built in the Lombard period, probably on a Roman cemetery area, already owned by the bishop of Padua in 874. Rectangular plant with semicircular apse and a single nave with wooden trusses; it was parish until 1872. The next tower, of Carolingian origin, became bell tower; the municipal restoration ended in 1984.

Why go. One of the oldest documented churches in the diocese of Padua.
