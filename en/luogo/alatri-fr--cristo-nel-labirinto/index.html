Cristo nel Labirinto

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Fresco discovered in 1996 in the rooms next to the cloister of the former convent of St. Francis: a Christ Pantocrator in the center of a unicursale labyrinth of twelve black circles, dated between the end of the 13th and the beginning of the 14th century. Open Tuesday-Sunday, closed on Mondays.

Why go. An enigmatic medieval maze.
