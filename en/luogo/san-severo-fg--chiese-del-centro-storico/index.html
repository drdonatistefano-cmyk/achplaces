Chiese del centro storico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The churches rebuilt after 1627: the Baroque sanctuary of the Madonna del Soccorso, the Romanesque façade of San Severino, the wooden choir and the four statues of Ambrogio Piazza in San Nicola, the marbles and gildings of the church of Pietà, and San Lorenzo delle Benedettine, stages of the guided tours in the city.

Why go. The Baroque of the Capitannata church after church.
