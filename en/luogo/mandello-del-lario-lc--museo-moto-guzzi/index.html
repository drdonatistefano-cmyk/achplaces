Museo Moto Guzzi

Local life and traditions · Motors · Paid entry

Set up in the historic factory where the brand was born in 1921, with a new wing of 3,000 square meters from which you can see the current assembly lines, exhibits more than 160 series motorcycles, sports, prototypes and restored engines. Among the unique pieces the first bike built by Carlo Guzzi in 1919, the only one signed G.P. (Guzzi-Parodi), the Otto Cylinders 500 of 1957 by Giulio Cesare Carcano, Norge of 1928, the Guzzi 4V of 1924, the 350 World Champion in 1955 and the V7 record of the 19 record speeds in Monza in 1969; sections on cinemas, races, travels and military and police vehicles.

Why go. Enter the Mandello eagle factory and see a century of Italian bikes.

  • Where Via Emanuele Vittorio Parodi 63, Mandello del Lario
  • Entry €12, reduced €10
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
