Giardino Botanico Mediterraneo

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

About 5 hectares between via Paolucci and the northern promenade, recovered in the 2000s from a former parking area: plants and animals of the Mediterranean coast, with marsh turtle and earth turtle.

Why go. The Mediterranean scrub on the sea.
