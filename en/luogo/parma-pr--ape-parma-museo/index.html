APE Parma Museo

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

The exhibition center of the Monteparma Foundation opened in 2018, with temporary exhibitions and museum halls of the painters Amedeo Bocchi and Renato Vernizzi.

Why go. The exhibitions of the moment in the center.

  • Where Strada Farini 32/a, Parma
  • Entry €8 whole; €5 under 35, over 65 and groups; free under 18; combined €12
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
