Parco archeologico del Villaggio neolitico di Sant'Andrea

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

State park of about one hectare, born in 2006 on the west bank of the Trebbia to protect a village of about 6000 years ago discovered in the eighties and excavated since 1995: exposed houses, wells, walls in pebbles and ovens, with three neolithic huts rebuilt in real scale in 2010. Since 2021, a museum area has rebuilt part of the high medieval necropolis of 117 tombs (V-VIII century). It is a Museum of Quality of the Emilia-Romagna Region and is part of the European EXARC network.

Why go. Get into the huts six thousand years ago.

closed from November 1 to March 31; only accessible with guided tour, departures every hour on weekends
