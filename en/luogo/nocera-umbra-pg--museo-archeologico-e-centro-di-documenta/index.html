Museo archeologico e Centro di documentazione siti territoriali

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 2010 in the former municipal palace: tools in flint and limestone attributed to Homo erectus and Homo sapiens, ceramics and objects in bronze and iron; terracotta, ceramic with black paint, marbles, inscriptions and mosaics from the settlements along the Flaminia; finds of the Lombard necropolis of the Portone (165 tombs with gold, silver, precious stones). The conference room has frescoes from the fifteenth century; there is a plaque in Garibaldi already on Porta Nuova. Info: tel. 333 2852940.

Why go. From prehistory to the Longobards of Flaminia.
