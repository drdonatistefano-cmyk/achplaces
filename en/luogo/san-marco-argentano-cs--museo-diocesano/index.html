Museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters

Opened on May 25, 2006 in the church of San Giovanni, no longer open to worship: on the ground floor silver sacred of the seventeenth-seventh century, on the upper floor vestments and paintings from the sixteenth to the eighteenth century. There is a 13th century reliquary cross in embossed silver and the silver bust of St. Nicholas commissioned in 1765 by Bishop Nicola Brescia, perhaps of the workshop of Philip Del Giudice.

Why go. The thirteenth-century reliquary cross.
