Palazzo Comunale e Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

The palace designed by Carlo Sada in 1896 overlooks the square and preserves the slate slab on which the original plan of the city was engraved; it houses a small archaeological museum with the findings of recent finds, while most of those of historical research is at the Archaeological Museum of Syracuse.

Why go. The plan of the city engraved in slate.
