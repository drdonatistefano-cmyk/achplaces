Museo Archeologico Nazionale, Palazzo Costabili

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The finds of the Etruscan city of Spina, Greek and Etruscan port on the delta, in a Renaissance palace called Ludovico il Moro with the garden.

Why go. Ori, Greek vases and the history of Ferrara before the Este.

  • Where Via XX Settembre 122, Ferrara
  • Entry €6 whole; in the MyFE Card; free on the first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website cultura.gov.it
  • Opening hours official page

Sources
