Area archeologica di Palikè

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The seat of the most important sanctuary of the Siculi, dedicated to the Palici brothers, the sons of Zeus and the nymph Talia, excavated since 1995 and opened to the public since 2006. In front of the cave at the foot of the hill of Rocchicella remain the structures of the sanctuary, with a terrace accommodation of the fifth century BC with a building for banquets and two arcades; on the top the remains of a city of the fourth century BC. Open from Monday to Friday 8.30-13.30.

Why go. The shrine of the Siculi, before the Greeks.
