Castello e Cappella Reale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It rises on Byzantine and Arabic structures: rectangular fortress between a square tower and a Swabian pentagonal mast, with Swabian walls considered the best preserved defensive configuration of Sicily and the date 1270 engraved on the cistern. Frederick II of Aragon transformed it into a royal summer residence by opening eighteen large windows; the Byzantine royal chapel would preserve, according to some scholars, the remains of Arnaldo da Villanova. Restored in the eighties, it is municipal and hosts exhibitions and conferences.

Why go. The summer palace of Frederick II of Aragon.
