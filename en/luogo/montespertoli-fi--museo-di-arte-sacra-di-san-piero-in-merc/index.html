Museo di Arte sacra di San Piero in Mercato

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the canonical church of the 11th century, works of the churches of the territory, with a Madonna and Child by Filippo Lippi.

Why go. A Filippo Lippi in the country.

  • Where Via San Piero in Mercato 233, Montespertoli
  • Entry €5 (with the museums Amedeo Bassi and Vite and Wine)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
