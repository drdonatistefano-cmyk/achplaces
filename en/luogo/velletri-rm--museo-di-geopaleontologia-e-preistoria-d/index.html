Museo di Geopaleontologia e Preistoria dei Colli Albani

Museums · Science and university

According to the Civic Museums in the Palazzo Comunale, it tells the formation of the territory from volcanoes to fossils in five sections (geology, paleontology, anthropology, prehistory, protohistory) with fossils, ceramics and environmental reconstructions.

Why go. Like volcanoes did the Alban Hills.
