Chiesa di San Basilio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest church in the village, the center of the rituals of Holy Week that close with the Procession of the Kiss on Easter day; during the restoration of the cathedral there was temporarily placed the Ambone.

Why go. The oldest church of Troy and the Easter rites.
