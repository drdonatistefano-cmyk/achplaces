Museo delle tradizioni musicali della Valle dell'Aniene

Museums · Themed

Opened on 4 November 2006 on the ground floor of the former Granaio Massimo, on the course of Borgo San Bartolomeo, it is dedicated to the musical culture of Arsoli and the Aniene Valley in four sections: pastoral instruments (flauts and clarinets of cane, flutes of bark, horns, bells), artisan instruments (organetto, putipù, triangle, tambourines), instruments of the rite (raganelle, battole, shells, with ample space to the Pilgrimage of the Holy Trinity) and band tradition, with nineteenth century brass and documents of the band of Arsoli, some of the eighteenth century. Many objects are gifts of the inhabitants and of the Maximus family. Opening hours: mon-ven 9.30-12.30, sab-dom 9.30-12.30 and 15.30-18.30, Tuesday closed.

Why go. The popular music of the Aniene Valley.
