Castello di Cisterna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It was mentioned for the first time in a document of 911, it was a fortified fence that in Baroque times became a noble country residence; it took its present form in the eighteenth century. From the medieval phase remain the corner tower of the thirteenth century, used as a lookout and last shelter, and the imposing walled wall; in the inner courtyard the cistern that would give the name to the village. You enter from an ancient arch with the coat of arms of Pope Innocent XII, memory of the lordship of the Church.

Why go. A ridge castle with over a thousand years of history.
