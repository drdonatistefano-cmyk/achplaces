Sinagoga e Museo ebraico

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Paid entry

The nineteenth-century temple in Moorish style, one of the most beautiful buildings of the nineteenth-century Italian, with the community museum.

Why go. The green dome you can see from all over the city.

  • Where Via Luigi Carlo Farini 6, Firenze
  • Entry €9 whole; €5 reduced; free with the Firenze Card
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.firenzebraica.it
