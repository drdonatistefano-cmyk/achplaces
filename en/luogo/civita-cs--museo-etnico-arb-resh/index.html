Museo etnico Arbëresh

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1989, on two floors and four rooms (welcome, frame, totem, costume): Albanian costumes, a photographic exhibition on the countries of Italy, iconography of Byzantine rites and a library of Albanian texts. Among the notable pieces is an icon of Our Lady Odigitria and notes of a storyteller on the history of Skanderbeg.

Why go. Italo-Albanian identity in a live museum.
