Chiesa di Santa Maria del Colledestro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small octagonal temple just outside the village, on the left of the Tiber, near the Colledestro stream, with a unique arch bridge near the facade. It has been mentioned in documents since the 12th century and has the appearance of a restoration of the 16th century.It stands where once there was a pagan temple dedicated to the Tiber.

Why go. An octagonal temple on the river, where the Tiber was worshipped.
