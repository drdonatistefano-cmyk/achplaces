Castello Orsini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

In 1278 Bear Orsini tore Vasanello from the Church and built a first fortification on a falisco-Roman bastion; the fief then returned to the Orsini and in 1489, for the wedding of Orsino Orsini, was commissioned the first cycle of frescoes, probably linked to the workshop of the Pinturicchio, enriched in 1505 with references to the Della Rovere. Passed to the Colonna and in 1700 to the Colonna-Barberini of Sciarra, in 1907 it was purchased by Monsignor Luigi Misciattelli, whose heirs still own it; in 1946 the ancient stables became a ceramic factory, closed in 1978. A spiral staircase leads to a panoramic point.

Why go. The castle of the Orsini with frescoes of Pinturicchiesca school.
