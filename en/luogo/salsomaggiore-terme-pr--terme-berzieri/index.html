Terme Berzieri

Local life and traditions · Spas

Built between 1913 and 1923 on the site of the Old Establishment and still in operation: plant at E, architecture of Ugo Giusti and decorations by Galileo Chini that combine oriental motifs and liberty. Named to the doctor Lorenzo Berzieri, who since 1839-40 studied the properties of the waters; they offer thermal baths, massages and Kneipp routes. In front is the Scotti well in wrought iron and glass.

Why go. The deco temple of Galileo Chini.
