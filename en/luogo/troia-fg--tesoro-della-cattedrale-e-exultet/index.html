Tesoro della Cattedrale e Exultet

Museums · Old masters

Next to the cathedral it houses silver (statue of the seventeenth century, candlesticks of the fifteenth century, a tabernacle of the seventeenth century, a pastoral of the sixteenth century), golden bronzes, vestments, avori from the twelfth to the eighteenth century and parchment miniate, among which three liturgical rolls Exultet of the twelfth and thirteenth century wrapped on a wooden rod called umbilicus; according to Italia.it there are only 32 specimens in the world.

Why go. Three of the rare medieval Exultet rolls.
