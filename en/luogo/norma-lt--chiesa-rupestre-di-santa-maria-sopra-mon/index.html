Chiesa rupestre di Santa Maria sopra Monte Mirteto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Also known as San Michele Arcangelo or Sant'Angelo del Mirteto, it is a karst cave transformed into a church in 1183, in an uninhabited area on the slope that looks at Ninfa; it inspired the abbey of Santa Maria di Monte Mirteto founded by Gregorio IX. On the rock are still frescoes, including a Madonna enthroned between St. Michael and St. Lucia, and a primitive altar; it is curated by volunteers of the association "Opera di Sant'Angelo" in Monte Mirteto.

Why go. A hermitage frescoed in the rock above Ninfa.
