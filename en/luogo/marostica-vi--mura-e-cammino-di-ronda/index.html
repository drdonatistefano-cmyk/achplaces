Mura e Cammino di ronda

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The crenellated walls of about 1700-1800 meters with 24 torrents and the doors Vicentina, Bassanese and Breganzina, built from 1372 under Cansignorio della Scala; the round path, at 8-12 meters above sea level, runs with harness starting from the Tower of the Compagnia delle Mura.

Why go. Walk on the walls scaling above the roofs.

open only Sundays and holidays from March to November, times vary with the weather

  • Where Torre della Compagnia delle Mura, Marostica
  • Entry €5, reduced €3; cumulative with Lower Castle €11
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
