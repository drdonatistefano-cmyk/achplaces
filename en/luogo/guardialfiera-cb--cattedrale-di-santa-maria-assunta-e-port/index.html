Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta e Porta Santa

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Rebuilt around the year 1000 and repeatedly rebuilt, it has external walls rich in bas-reliefs: twelve hanging arches on human heads representing the Apostles, crucifer lambs, hunting scenes on the eastern facade, a Byzantine dolphin, priests in procession and a blessed bishop, under a large walled rose window. The crypt, discovered by chance in 1975 during the restorations, has vaults cruising on tubs columns and archaic capitals, of uncertain dating. On the north-east side, at the top of 14 steps, the Bronze Holy Door (2020-2021) recalls the perpetual plenary indulgence that according to the Leo IX tradition granted in June 1053: it opens on the morning of June 1st and closes on the afternoon of June 2nd, for the patron celebrations.

Why go. Romanesque sculpted stones and a rare Holy Door.
