Museo d'arte moderna Ugo Carà

Museums · Modern art

Permanent collection of the Muggesano sculptor Ugo Carà: more than thirty sculptures, from the first bronzes to mature works, and about thirty prints; it also hosts temporary exhibitions.

Why go. Muggia's sculptor.
