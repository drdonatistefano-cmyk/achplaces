Torre dell'Orologio, mostra Nino Manfredi

Museums · Themed

The tower is today a multifunctional cultural centre with a permanent exhibition dedicated to actor Nino Manfredi, born in Castro dei Volsci in 1921; the visit is on reservation. Near Piazza IV Novembre there is a monument to the actor and a bas-relief of Umberto Cufrini realized for the centenary of the birth.

Why go. On the trail of Nino Manfredi in his country.
