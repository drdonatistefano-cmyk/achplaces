Museo Scalvini

Museums · Modern art

In the Villa Tittoni Traversi, since 1999 he has been donating the Milanese sculptor Giuseppe Scalvini (108-2003): 36 sculptures in plaster, wax and bronze, to which in 2003 16 drawings in charcoal and some engravings were added. Scalvini, author of the eagles of the Central Station and Justice of the Tribunal of Milan, realized in 1937 two angels for the Basilica of Desio and participated in the Venice Biennale in 1948 and 1954. In addition to the permanent collection, temporary exhibitions of artists from the twentieth century. Open Friday, Saturday and Sunday 10:30-12:30 and 15:30-18:00; Thursday by appointment.

Why go. The sculptor of the eagles of the Central Station.
