Mostra museo delle cose dimenticate

Museums · Themed

In the former oratory of Santa Croce, on the square of Valloria, exhibits objects of peasant life, craftsman and maid recovered with the community: agricultural tools, tools and instruments built by Giacomo Pisani (1886-1959), including a "percussive" halfway between piano and organ, a violin and a self-scatting camera. The MiC card indicates opening every day 8-18 (updated to 2020); the Province says open on request. Tel. 0183 282396.

Why go. The objects and inventions of a mountain country.
