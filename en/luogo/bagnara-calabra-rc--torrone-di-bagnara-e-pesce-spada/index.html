Torrone di Bagnara e pesce spada

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Bagnara is considered to be the capital of the swordfish, and its symbol is the "bagnarota" with a basket in the head from which the sword of the fish comes; the Torrone di Bagnara IGP is still handmade with traditional ingredients, and the area produces the Costa Viola IGT wine.

Why go. Taste the IGP nougat and the swordfish.
