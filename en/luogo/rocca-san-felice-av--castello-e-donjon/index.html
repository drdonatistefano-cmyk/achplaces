Castello e Donjon

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

A fortified area that occupies the entire plateau at the top of the fortress, with walls still visible: after the first entrance a space housed the houses of artisans and soldiers, a second fortified entrance leads to the courtyard from which you access the Donjon. The entrance door is at the entrance of the Palazzo De Antonellis-Villani.

Why go. Climb to the mastium that dominates the valley of Ansanto.
