Villa Foscari detta La Malcontenta

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Built in the middle of the sixteenth century by Andrea Palladio, with the noble floor frescoed by G.B. Zelotti in the second half of the century; in 1966 Queen Elizabeth of England was guest. It is a private residence on the UNESCO site of Palladio villas (Villa Foscari, connue sous le nom de La Malcontenta, Gambara di Mira) In the 2026 season it opened from Tuesday to Sunday (Thursday only morning), 9-12 and 14.30-17.30.

Why go. The Palladian villa on the Brenta, a few kilometers from the lagoon.

open from 1 April to 1 November, closed in winter; visit to book at least 10 days before; pedestrian area without parking

  • Entry €15, reduced €10 (18-25 years, FAI and ICOM members, resident in Mira), €8 (11-17 years), family €30, free up to 10 years
