Museo d'Arte Orientale

Museums · Themed

In Palazzetto Leo, four floors of art, weapons, musical instruments and objects especially from China and Japan, with prints ukiya-e, a section on samurai and sculptures of Gandhara.

Why go. The Orient of Merchants.
