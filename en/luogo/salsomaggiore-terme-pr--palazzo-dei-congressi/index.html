Palazzo dei Congressi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The former Grand Hôtel des Thermes, designed in 1898 by Luigi Broggi from Milan and opened in 1901: horse-iron plant, floral decorations by Gottardo Valentini on the facade and deleted by Alessandro Mazzucotelli. After the Great War Ugo Giusti and Galileo Chini added the wing with the Salone Moresco, the Red Tavern and the Loggia, inspired by the East.

Why go. The Grand Hotel Liberty became a congress building.
