Piazza del Campo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The shell-shaped square was created as a space for civic events, closed by the Palazzo Pubblico and the Torre del Mangia, with the Fonte Gaia, a nineteenth-century copy of the original Jacopo della Quercia.

Why go. Here you can run the Palio, July 2nd and August 16th.

  • Where Piazza del Campo, Siena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
