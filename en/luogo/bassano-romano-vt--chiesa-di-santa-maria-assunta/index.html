Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Completed in 1703 next to Palazzo Giustiniani, it has the local stone façade with clock and coat of arms of the Justinians; in the single nave an organ of the eighteenth century, a nineteenth-century choir in painted wood, the Mater Dolorosa, the Assumption of the Virgin and the relic of the head of St. Gratilian.

Why go. The relic of the patron next to the palace.
