Palazzo Orsini e musei

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Aldobrandesco building restored by the Orsini between the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, home to the Museum of Palazzo Orsini and the Civic Archaeological Museum.

Why go. The palace overlooking the entrance of the village.

  • Where Piazza della Fortezza Orsini 25, Pitigliano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
