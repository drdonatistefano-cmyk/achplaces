Terme di Boario

Local life and traditions · Spas

Spa park of about 130,000 square meters with Italian garden and rare botanical species; the sources, already known in the seventeenth century and appreciated by Alessandro Manzoni, are four: Antica Fonte, Fausta, Igea and Boario. At the entrance the colonnade with the Liberty dome of 1913, symbol of the spa. In addition to bathrooms, mud and wellness area with swimming pools, saunas and salt room, the park hosts the Boario Park Adventure with Tibetan bridge and a slittovia (Alpine Coaster) from the Fausta Pavilion to the high park. Spa hours: Mon-Fri 12:30-22:00, Saturday, Sunday and holidays 10:00-22:00, recommended booking.

Why go. The waters and the Liberty dome that made Boario.
