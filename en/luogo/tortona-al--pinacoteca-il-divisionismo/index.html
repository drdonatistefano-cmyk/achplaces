Pinacoteca "Il Divisionismo"

Museums · Modern art

Permanent exhibition inaugurated on May 26, 2012 in the Medieval Palazzetto of the fourteenth century: over 140 works by 50 divisionist artists with Giuseppe Pellizza da Volpedo (more than 20 paintings), together with Segantini, Medardo Rosso, Nomellini and Morbelli; a room is dedicated to the Dolonese Angelo Barabino. Open Saturday and Sunday 15-19, other days by reservation.

Why go. The heart of Italian Divisionism.
