Museo-laboratorio del Parco del Monte Cucco

Museums · Science and university

In the former church of San Marco, in the historic center: explains the geology of the Umbrian-Marche Apennines and the path of rainwater up to springs and slopes. The visit starts from the section of the Cave of Monte Cucco, whose water re-emerges about 1,000 meters below the source of Scirca; follow sections on formation of the mountains, origin of the rocks, extinct fauna, vegetation, a plastic and a documentation center on the karst areas. At about 200 m, in via Galeazzi, the laboratory classrooms \"Energy & Life" on the history of energy. Info: tel. 075 9172723.

Why go. Understand the mountain and its cave before going up there.
