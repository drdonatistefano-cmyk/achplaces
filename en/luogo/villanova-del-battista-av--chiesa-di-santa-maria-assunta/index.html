Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Large church of the historic center with a single nave with three entrances, rebuilt entirely after the earthquakes of 1930 and 1980. It preserves two 17th century paintings attributed to the school of Luca Giordano, the Fall of Jesus and the Deposition. Open during function hours.

Why go. Two Jordanian school canvases.
