Seminario arcivescovile di Venegono

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Viewpoint

A vast complex, with an area of about 40 times the camp of San Siro, whose first stone was laid on February 6, 1928, wanted by Cardinal Schuster to form the Ambrosian priests away from the city. The 64-metre tower stands out, which since the 1930s has hosted the Observatory of Earth Physics for almost half a century; the library has more than 140,000 volumes and the theology chapel is decorated by A. Bergagna. Library and chapel are to be visited on the occasion of the Feast of the Seminary on 1 May.

Why go. The city of the Ambrosian clergy with its tower.
