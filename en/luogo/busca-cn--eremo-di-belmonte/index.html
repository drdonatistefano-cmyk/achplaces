Eremo di Belmonte

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Born in the thirteenth century with a chapel dedicated to the Madonna and a female convent of Carthusians, it passed in the fifteenth century to the Dominicans and the early seventeenth century to the Camaldolese, becoming a pilgrimage destination. In the nineteenth century it was transformed into a villa with mythological paintings by Francesco Gonin; the bombings of the Second World War severely damaged the abbey church, of which the ruins remain. On the slope of the hill the alabaster quarries. Visits by reservation.

Why go. Eight centuries of history on the alabaster hill.
