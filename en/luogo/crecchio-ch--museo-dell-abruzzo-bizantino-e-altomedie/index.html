Museo dell'Abruzzo bizantino e altomedievale

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the Ducal Castle, exhibits the excavations conducted from 1988 to 1991 by the Archeoclub of Crecchio with the Superintendence in the Roman-Byzantine Villa of Vassarella, recovered from a cistern used as a landfill that has exceptionally preserved the wood materials, together with ostrogoth and Longobard finds: they tell the Byzantine life in Abruzzo and the Gothic wars between the end VI and the beginning of the VII century, and the trade with the East and Egypt. The Alberto Carlo Fraracci room hosts an Etruscan collection donated in 1995. Seasonal hours according to the MiC card (from 15 June to 15 September every day 10-12 and 16-20), otherwise by reservation.

Why go. The Byzantines in Abruzzo, inside the castle.
