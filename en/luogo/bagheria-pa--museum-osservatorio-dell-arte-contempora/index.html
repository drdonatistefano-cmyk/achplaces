Museum - Osservatorio dell'Arte Contemporanea in Sicilia

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Space of Ezio Pagano dedicated to contemporary art of Sicilian artists; open 17-20.30, closed on Mondays and on holidays, groups and schools also in the morning by reservation.

Why go. Contemporary Sicilian art.

  • Where Via Cherubini 12, Bagheria
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
