Museo storico aeronautico

Museums · Themed

It is located in Loreto because Our Lady of Loreto, proclaimed in 1920 as the patron saint of aviation, protects aviators. It exhibits a unique collection in Italy of dozens of uniforms complete with the Regia Aeronautica, largely original and in some unique cases, and hundreds of pieces from the birth of the Regia Aeronautica to today.

Why go. Divided and relics of aviators.
