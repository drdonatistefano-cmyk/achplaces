Chiesa di San Francesco e Mostra permanente del vino cotto

Museums · Themed

The church is full of stuccoes and gilding; in the surroundings next to the cloister the permanent exhibition of equipment and tools for cooked wine, the drink for which the village organizes every August the Festival of cooked wine, born in 1948 as a feast of grapes.

Why go. The tools of traditional cooked wine.
