Area marina protetta Isole di Ventotene e Santo Stefano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Posidonia meadows up to about 40 meters with over 350 species of invertebrates and fish, caves with octopus and moray eels and Roman wrecks: one in Cala Rossano in very low water, one in Punta dell'Arco and five over 100 meters between the two islands.

Why go. One of the most beautiful seabeds in the Mediterranean.
