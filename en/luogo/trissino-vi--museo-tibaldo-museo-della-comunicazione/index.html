Museo Tibaldo, museo della comunicazione

Museums · Themed

Born from the collection collected in over 40 years by Giancarlo Tibaldo: more than 10,000 working pieces that tell more than 200 years of audio, video and music communication, science and technology. A laboratory offers experiments on electromagnetism, electrostatics, battery operation and Stirling engine.

Why go. Two centuries of communication equipment, all working.
