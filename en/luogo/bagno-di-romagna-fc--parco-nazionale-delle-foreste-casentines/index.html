Parco Nazionale delle Foreste Casentinesi

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Forests of beech and fir trees on the ridge of Tuscany-Romagna. In the park there is the integral reserve of Sasso Fratino, one of the ancient beech woods UNESCO World Heritage.

Why go. The best trails in autumn, with the foliage.
