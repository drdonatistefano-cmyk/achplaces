Museo di arte sacra (Musei Sistini del Piceno)

Museums · Old masters

In Palazzo Pascali: a temple reliquary, a processional cross of the eighteenth century with a fourteenth-century Christ attributed to the goldsmith Pietro Vannini, a Madonna of the Cincinola of the seventeenth century and a San Liborio by Giuseppe Ghezzi (1634-1721), painter born in Comunanza and active in Rome.

Why go. The Ascolan goldsmith and the painter of the house Ghezzi.

Municipal museums only partially usable (Let's Marche)
