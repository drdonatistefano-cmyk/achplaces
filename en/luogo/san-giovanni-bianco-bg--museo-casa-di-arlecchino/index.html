Museo Casa di Arlecchino

Museums · House museums

The 15th-century Palace of the Grataroli family, enriched in Venice: tradition has it that their Venetian contacts have given rise to Zanni, a rustic character from Bergamo who emigrated to the lagoon, who became the Harlequin of the Commedia dell'Arte in the 16th century. Above the entrance staircase a fresco depicts Homo Selvadego, a hairy man dressed in skins and armed with a stick, with a paper in dialect: "Chi non' de chortesia, non intragi in chasa mia" (who is not de chortesia) Visit by reservation.

Why go. On the trail of Harlequin's mask.
