Giardini di Augusto

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Public gardens with Mediterranean vegetation and views of Marina Piccola, Via Krupp and the Faraglioni.

Why go. The balcony on the Faraglioni.

  • Entry €2.50; free up to 12 years
