Enoteca Regionale delle Marche

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Hosted in the former convent of San Francesco, dating back to the 14th century, it offers guided tastings with cold cuts, cheeses, cakes and bread and a selection of the best labels from Marche, including Offida DOCG.

Why go. Tasting the wines of the Marches.
