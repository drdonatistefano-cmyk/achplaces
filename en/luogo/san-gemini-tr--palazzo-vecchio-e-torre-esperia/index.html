Palazzo Vecchio e Torre Esperia

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The palace of the XII-XIII century was the seat of the Captain of the People; in the central hall frescoes with peasants working in the countryside. Next to the Esperia Tower, with the bell melted in 1318 by an Orvieto master, symbol of the free municipality, who called the citizens at the assemblies. You enter from Porta Burgi, 12th century gate on the path of Flaminia.

Why go. The palace and the bell of the free municipality.
