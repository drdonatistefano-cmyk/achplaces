Museo della civiltà del vino

Museums · Themed

In the area of Villa di Toppo Florio, about 2900 objects document the peasant viticulture from the nineteenth century to the mid-twentieth century: sections on viticulture and wine making, the workshop of the carpenter workshop rebuilt, trade and consumption of wine, a Friulian inn of the thirties and a documentary collection. Open only by reservation (tel. 0432 63611).

Why go. The whole cycle of Friulian wine, from the vineyard to the tavern.
