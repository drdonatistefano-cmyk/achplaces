Marroni di Cave e cucina dei Prenestini

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The chestnuts of Cave, Traditional Agro-food product, are celebrated with the festival between October and November and end up in sweets; on the table dumplings with sheep sauce, tagliatelle ai porcini, polenta with cheese and walnuts, roasts of sheep and snails with Roman.

Why go. The chestnut of the Prenestini Mountains.
