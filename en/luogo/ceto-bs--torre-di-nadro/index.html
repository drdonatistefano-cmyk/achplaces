Torre di Nadro

History and architecture · Towers

13th-century tower in via Voltazzi, in the village of Nadro, to which was later flanked the noble house of the Gaioni family; the village is, with Ceto and Badetto, one of the three localities of the municipality and the gateway to the Engraving Reserve.

Why go. The medieval Nadro, a stone's throw from the engravings.
