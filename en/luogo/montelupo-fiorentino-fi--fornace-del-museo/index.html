Fornace del Museo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The ceramic manufacture Cioni-Alderighi built near the walls in 1859, restored and reopened in 2021 as part of the route of the Ceramic Museum, with workshops on local production.

Why go. You can see where the majolicas used to cook.

  • Where Via Giro delle Mura, Montelupo Fiorentino
  • Entry Included in the ticket of the Ceramic Museum
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website museomontelupo.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
