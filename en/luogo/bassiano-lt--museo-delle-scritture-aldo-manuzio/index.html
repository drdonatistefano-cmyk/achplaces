Museo delle Scritture Aldo Manuzio

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the lower floors of the Baronial Palace of the Caetani, seat of the Municipality, is dedicated to the humanist and publisher Aldo Manuzio (1449-1515), born in Bassiano: three rooms with typographical machines follow the path "Fiume delle Scripture" on the evolution of writing, integrated with the historical traces of the halls and the prison, with space to the marginal writings local like those of the shepherds.

Why go. From writing to the press, in the village of Manuzio.

MiC: visit by reservation required (tel. 0773 355013)
