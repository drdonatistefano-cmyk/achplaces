Chiesa di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of 1585, with facade rebuilt around 1728 with the coat of arms of the Paccaroni, today deconsecrated and used for events as classical music concerts. It has a wooden ceiling painted in tempera in the eighteenth century, attributed to the stop Filippo Ricci and indicated as the most extensive of the genus in the Marches, five wooden altars gilded and marbled, blades attributed to G. Ghezzi and F. Ricci is an organ of the callidian school; the canvas of Simone De Magistris with the Madonna, the Child and Saints is today in the municipal palace.

Why go. The large eighteenth-century painted ceiling.

MiC card indicates closed every day and the church is in MiC database of abandoned cultural assets with mediocre conservation status
