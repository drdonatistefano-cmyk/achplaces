Palazzo Strassoldo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Among the oldest in the city, born between the Five and the seventeenth century joining more buildings, belonged to Count Rizzardo of Strassoldo, commander of the fortress during the wars of Gradiscan (1615-1617); remained almost intact, has a large courtyard and a monumental rectangular portal on Via Ciotti.

Why go. A home of the commanders of the fortress.
