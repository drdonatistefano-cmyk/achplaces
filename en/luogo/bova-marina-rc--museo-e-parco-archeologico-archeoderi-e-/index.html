Museo e Parco archeologico Archeoderi e sinagoga

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

The park surrounds the remains of a synagogue excavated in the 1980s and used between the 4th and 6th centuries AD, the only architectural evidence of the Jewish presence in Calabria at that time and, according to the MiC, the oldest in the West after that of Ostia; the mosaic of the prayer room depicts menorah, palm, cedar and shofar. The museum exhibits the mosaic, a treasure of 3,079 bronze coins in a jug (IV-V century) and a millionaire of 364-367 A.D. Open from Tuesday to Sunday 8.30-14, Monday closed.

Why go. One of the oldest synagogues in the West.
