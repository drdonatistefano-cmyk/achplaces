Basilica di Nostra Signora di Luogosanto

Places of worship · Basilicas

Built around 1218 by the Franciscans, it is considered the first Franciscan church in the north-east of Sardinia; in 1227 Honorius III raised it to a minor basilica, the first in Gallura. In squared granite and Romanesque forms, it retains the three-nave system; it was rebuilt in the eighteenth century, when it obtained the privilege of the Holy Door, and enlarged in 1912. It preserves the wooden Madonna called "Queen of Gallura," frescoes with the Baptism of Jesus and Saints Nicolò and Trano; the bronze door of Luca Luchetti (1970s) opens every seven years for a year, September 8, the feast of the Nativity of Mary.

Why go. The heart of the " Marian city" and one of the rare Saints outside Rome.
