Palazzo Della Rovere e Teatro Mario Tiberini

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Severo sixteenth-century palace wanted by Giulio della Rovere at the entrance to the old castle, home of the civic museums. On the noble floor, perhaps in the ancient dance hall of the Della Rovere, the theatre dedicated to the local tenor Mario Tiberini: completed around 1820 as the Triomphe Theatre, rebuilt in masonry after a fire of 1880, inaugurated with the current name in 1882 and reopened after the restoration in 1983.

Why go. The theatre in the hall of the dukes.

Theater can be visited by reservation or with the guided tour of the archaeological museum
