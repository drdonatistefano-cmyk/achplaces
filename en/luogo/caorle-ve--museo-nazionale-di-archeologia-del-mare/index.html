Museo nazionale di archeologia del mare

Museums · Archaeological

Managed by the Regional Direction National Museums Veneto, it was born from the exhibition Terredacque set up in 2014 by the Superintendence and tells the territory from the protohistoric village of San Gaetano to the Portus Reatinum of Plinio up to the modern Caorle. On the first floor the most significant finds of Caorle and surroundings, from the late Bronze Age (XIII-early XII century BC) to the modern age; on the ground floor the history of the brig Mercury, a warship with two trees of Napoleonic age passed from the French fleet to the Italian one. Number reservations +39 0421 83149 and +39 379 1805649.

Why go. Archaeology of the sea and the history of the brig Mercury.
