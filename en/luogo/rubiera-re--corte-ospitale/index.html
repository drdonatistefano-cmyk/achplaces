Corte Ospitale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The fifteenth-century complex was created to accommodate travellers and pilgrims at the ford of the Secchia, on the streets towards Sassuolo, Frassinoro, Lucca and Rome; it was then a farm. Today, with a porch courtyard and tower, is home to the theatre center La Corte Ospitale; in the church of Santa Maria (16th century) remains fragments of frescoes of the Garofalo (1543).

Why go. The hospice of pilgrims became theater.
