Museo delle tradizioni venatorie

Museums · Themed

Path set up by the vibrant hunting club: the halls exhibit trophies, embalmed specimens, weapons and hunting gear, ancient cartridges, books, prints and calls, each with caption; it welcomes schoolchildren and groups for hunting and naturalistic lessons, including protected species.

Why go. The hunting traditions of Val Vibrata.
