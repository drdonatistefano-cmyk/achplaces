Museo Diocesano «Mons. Corrado Leonardi»

Museums · Old masters

In the former Bishop's Palace of the late sixteenth century, renovated in Renaissance forms with interventions by Francesco di Giorgio Martini, Gerolamo Genga and the stonemasons of Sant'Ippolito. From the lapidary you pass to the rooms of silver, vestments, Madonnas and the metauren mannerists (Federico Zuccari, Giovanni Giacomo Pandolfi, Claudio Ridolfi) and to a section of majolica that documents the production of Castel delle Ripe, Casteldurante and Urbania from the XIII to the XX century. Italia.it indicates opening from Tuesday to Sunday 11-13 and 16-18.

Why go. Seven centuries of Durantine majolica.
