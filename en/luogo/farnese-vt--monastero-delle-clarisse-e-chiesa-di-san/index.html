Monastero delle Clarisse e chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Built in 1560 for the Friars Minor by will of the will of Giulia Acquaviva, wife of Pier Bertoldo Farnese; it includes a garden of about 5.000 m2, the cells of the nuns and a guesthouse. The church contains frescoes from the early sixteenth century, including that of the wedding of Galeazzo Farnese and Isabella degli Anguillara (1514-1518).

Why go. The Farnese-Anguillara wedding fresco.
