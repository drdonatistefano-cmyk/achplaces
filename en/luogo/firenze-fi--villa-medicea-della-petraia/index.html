Villa medicea della Petraia

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The villa on the slopes of Monte Morello, with the Italian garden with three terraces, the courtyard frescoed by Volterrano and the 14 lunettes of Giusto Utens.

Why go. The villa is only visited with guided entrances at fixed times.

  • Where Via della Petraia 40, Firenze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility Only courtyard and ground floor; very difficult garden
  • Official website villemonumentalitoscana.cultura.gov.it
