Spiaggia di Castiglione

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

A stone's throw from the road, with a stairway of a couple of hundred steps, or from the sea; better go in the morning, because in the afternoon the cliffs are shaded over the bay.

Why go. The sea of Ravello under the cliff.

  • Where Castiglione, Ravello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
