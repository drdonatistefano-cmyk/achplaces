Villa romana di Patti e Antiquarium

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Villa of the fourth century A.D. discovered during the works of the Palermo-Messina motorway, with large polychrome mosaics of African taste comparable to those of the Villa del Casale and Tellaro. The small antiquarium, opened in 2001, exhibits ceramics, amphorae, terracotta, goldsmiths, coins, a bronze circus card and marbles of the villa. cumulative ticket with Tindari €11.20 (reduced €5).

Why go. The mosaics of the late antique villa.

  • Where Via Giovanni XXIII 2, Patti Marina
  • Entry €6, reduced €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
