Abbazia di Santa Maria di Rambona

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Built around 891-898 at the behest of Queen Ageltrude, wife of Emperor Guido of Spoleto; in 1443 it was sacked and set on fire by Ciarpellone, captain of Francesco Sforza, and the monastery was destroyed. There are still presbytery and crypt: the three sandstone apses retain the original characteristics, inside the frescoes of Sant'Amico that enshrine the wolf and the Madonna enthroned with the Child.

Why go. One of the oldest and most evocative abbeys in the province.

closed following the earthquake of 2016 according to the regional portal
