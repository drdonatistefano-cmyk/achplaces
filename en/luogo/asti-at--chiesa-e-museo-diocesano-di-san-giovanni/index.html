Chiesa e Museo Diocesano di San Giovanni

Museums · Old masters

In the ancient church of St. John, most likely the first cathedral of Asti, the museum of the works of the episcopal complex; you visit the crypt, the deepest point of the church.

Why go. The first cathedral.
