Villa Mylius Vigoni e Loveno

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The 19th-century villa in the hamlet of Loveno, purchased by Mylius in 1829 and enlarged to house the collections of paintings, statues and furnishings, with an amphitheater-style English park designed by Giuseppe Balzaretto; left by Ignazio Vigoni to the Federal Republic of Germany, is the seat of the Italian-German Centre for European Dialogue. Guided tours by reservation (tel. 0344 361232). In Loveno there are also the villas Bel Faggio, Bolzano, Garovaglio Ricci and Govone and the parish church of Santi Lorenzo and Agnese (1725-1728).

Why go. The romantic park above the lake.
