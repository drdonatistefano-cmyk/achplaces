Chiesa di San Giuseppe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1737-1739 on the old church of San Francesco, with bell tower of 1780: it contains six octagonal paintings by the brothers Antonio and Vincenzo Manno, a wooden statue attributed to Domenico Noffo (1670) and a canvas by D'Antoni from 1857.

Why go. The octagonal canvases of the Manno.
