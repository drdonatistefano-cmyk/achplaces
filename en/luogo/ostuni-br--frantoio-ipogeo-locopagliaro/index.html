Frantoio ipogeo Locopagliaro

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Medieval mill excavated underground, today museum of rural civilization, destination of lovers of rural archaeology: preserves ancient wooden presses, stone millstones, ovens and housing of the workers.

Why go. Like the oil under the ground.
