Piantravigne e Pieve di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

The village is surrounded by the Erose walls of the Balze and connected by bridges over the gorges; the fourteenth-century parish church of San Lorenzo, on a natural cliff, preserves the oldest painting of the Madonna del Valdarno Superiore, recently restored in the 13th century. In the area also the fourteenth-century church of San Pietro in Treggiaia.

Why go. The parish church on the cliff between the Balzes.
