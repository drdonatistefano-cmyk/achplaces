Chiesa di San Salvatore de' Birecto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

X century church with square plan with pronao, three naves and barrel vaults, overlooking the square; facade with clock, staircase and atrium are baroque. At the time of the Republic of Amalfi was the Palatine chapel where the Doges received the ducal berretta. It preserves a 12th century marble slab with two peacocks and the fourteenth-century plaque of Filippa Napolitano; the bronze doors of 1087, donated by Pantaleone Viarecta, are now kept in Santa Maria Maddalena.

Why go. The chapel where the Doges of Amalfi were crowned.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
