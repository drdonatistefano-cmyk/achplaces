Chiesa rupestre della Buona Nuova

History and architecture · Archaeology

The 7th-century rock church, accessible from the churchyard of the sanctuary of the Madonna della Scala. The frescoes include a Virgin of the Good New Odegitria type of the late thirteenth century, a Christ Pantocrator with Deesis and a triptych with Saint Lucia, Saint Vitus and Saint Catherine of Alexandria.

Why go. The Madonna of the Good New, fresco of the thirteenth century in the rock.

Rocky detachment of January 19, 2017, probably due to water infiltration: the municipality inhibited access to the rock church; check if it was reopened
