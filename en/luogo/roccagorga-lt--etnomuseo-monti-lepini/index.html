EtnoMuseo Monti Lepini

Museums · Themed

Anthropological demoetanological museum in the Baronial Palace, dedicated to the identity of the Lepine communities and reference for this discipline in the territorial system of the Lepini Mountains and in the Demos System. The route, based on research on the territory, focuses on sensory involvement with installations, settings and listening stations in seven environments: those of the Rock, Splendor Cinema, Red Easter, Habitus, Grani di storia, Garage-culture and Noi loro. He also remembers the massacre of January 6, 1913, when seven citizens were killed during a strike, with sculptures inspired by the "Fourth State" of Pellizza da Volpedo.

Why go. A museum to listen to and touch on the life of the Lepini.
