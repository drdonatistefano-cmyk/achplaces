Museo storico etnoantropologico di Villa Badessa

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

It documents the origins of the Albanian community (arbëreshë) of Abruzzo, established here in 1743 by Epirus to escape the religious persecutions of the Turks, and preserves its traditions. Open on request (tel. 338 1655479).
