Sentiero Animalborghetto

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Viewpoint

Easy theme path for children, inspired by a fairy tale on the animals of the forest (caprioli, badgers, wild boars, hares, ibex): starts from the square of Palazzo Veneziano at Casa Krahvogl, crosses a forest of pine, hornbeam and ash trees and rises to the clearing of the chapel of Calvary, panoramic, with return along the ancient Via Crucis. At least an hour.

Why go. A fairy tale in the woods for families.
