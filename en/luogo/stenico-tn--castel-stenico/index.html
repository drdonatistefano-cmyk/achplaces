Castel Stenico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Since the twelfth century of the Prince Bishops of Trento, it was the seat of the bishop's captain of the Judicarians and their summer residence: a set of buildings of different eras, from the New Palace of the beginning of the thirteenth century to the Medieval Old Palace, transformed in part from the fifteenth century into a Renaissance residence with loggias, double-fores and triple-fores, enlarged in the sixteenth century by Bernardo Clesio. You can visit the chapel of San Martino with frescoes from the thirteenth century, the Judgment Room, the Bozone Tower called "Torre della Fame" (Torre della Fame), with the dark prison accessible from a trapdoor, and the provincial collections of inlaid furniture, weapons, paintings and applied arts, as well as an archaeological section. The castle also appears in the Cycle of the Months of Torre Aquila in Trento.

Why go. The castle of the Prince Bishops and the Tower of Fame.
