Collegiata di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church founded in the 16th century and rebuilt in monumental Baroque forms, with canvases of Ligari and Petrini. At Christmas hosts the event Christmas Art.

Why go. The monumental baroque with the canvases of the Ligars.
