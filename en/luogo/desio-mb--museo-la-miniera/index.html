Museo «La Miniera»

Museums · Science and university

Hosted in the former Franciscan convent and in the neo-Gothic Torre del Palagi, it exhibits crystals, fossils and minerals from all over the world.

Why go. Minerals and fossils from all over the world in a neo-Gothic tower.
