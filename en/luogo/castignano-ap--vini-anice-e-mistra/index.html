Vini, anice e mistrà

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Private cellars and the Cantina Sociale produce Rosso Piceno, Falerio dei Colli Ascolani, Templaria, Gramelot, brandy of marc, anise, mistrà and sparkling wines. The village is also known for the historic Carnival, which closes with the night procession of the Moccoli with lights off, and for the Templaria festival at the end of August.

Why go. Taste the anise and wines of Piceno.
