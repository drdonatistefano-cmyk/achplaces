Museo della Tecnologia contadina "Maestro Francesco Salis"

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1976 in an 18th-century manor house, home to the UNLA People's Culture Centre, it collects over 2,000 work tools and everyday objects donated by the families of the country, in 11 of the 23 rooms of the building. The halls are arranged for crafts: farmers, shepherds and charcoal makers, artisans' machines, viticulture, cheese-making and mill, spinning and weaving, work and party clothes, transportation and hydraulic machines; panels also illustrate songs and children's games.

Why go. A complete journey into the crafts of rural Sardinia.
