Chiesa di Santa Maria del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient romoratory (foundation indicated in 1243) along the mule track for Campolieto, already the Grancia of the Scalzi Carmelites, then the Doctrinal Fathers until the suppression of 1809 and the Conventuals since 1840; it preserves paintings by the Neapolitan Giuseppe Castellano (1707) and epigraphs recalling Cardinal Orsini.

Why go. Castellano's paintings in an ancient romantic.
