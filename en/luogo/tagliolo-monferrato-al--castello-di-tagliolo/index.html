Castello di Tagliolo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Among the best preserved of the Alto Monferrato, it is still inhabited by the Marquis Pinelli Gentile, who received it in 1745 with the marriage between Teresa Gentile and Costantino Pinelli. The square tower, part of the oldest, is from the 10th century and from the top offers a 360-degree view of the hills; the complex brings together towers, houses, a church and a big garden, with frescoed halls, furnishings and arms collections. Wineries have been producing and preserving local wines for 500 years; it is part of the Castelli Aperti circuit (tel. 0143 89195).

Why go. An inhabited castle, with millennial tower and historic cellars.
