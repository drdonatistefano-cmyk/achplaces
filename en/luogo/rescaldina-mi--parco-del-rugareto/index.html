Parco del Rugareto

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Local park of supra-municipal interest of about 1,400 hectares between Cislago, Marnate, Gorla Minore and Rescaldina, east of the Olona valley up to the pine forest of Tradate. Wood of robinies, silvestri pine trees, oaks, hornbeams, chestnuts and birch trees, with squirrels, peaks, owls and owls; cross it three streams (Fontanile di Tradate, Gradeloso or Bozzentino and Bozzente, the only one with constant flow even in dry periods). The seventeenth stage of the Way of Saint Augustine passes through.

Why go. A large wooded spot in the Upper Milanese, to walk on the Way of Saint Augustine.
