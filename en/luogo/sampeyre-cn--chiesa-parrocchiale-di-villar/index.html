Chiesa parrocchiale di Villar

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It is mentioned already in the list of the cathedral of Turin of 1336, it has two Romanesque portals in green stone of Brossasco (the largest on the side), a single nave with bundles of columns similar to those of the parish church of Sampeyre and a bell tower renovated of Gothic structure; on the square a stone cross.

Why go. The Romanesque green stone of a mountain fraction.
