Civico Museo naturalistico «Ferruccio Lombardi»

Museums · Science and university

Born as the Po Museum, for the finds and fossils returned from the river, it documents today the natural environment of the whole Oltrepò Pavese: geology with minerals, rocks and traces of gold of the Po, a small anthropological section with human and fossil remains of the Paleolithic and Neolithic, a large paleontological section with mammals of the Pleistocene found in the Po, plant and animal species of the territory, collections of butterflies and snails, an area on bees and silk bugs and reconstructions of environments and climates. A cumulative ticket with the Museum of Accordion (€8).

Why go. The fossils and the gold of the Po.
