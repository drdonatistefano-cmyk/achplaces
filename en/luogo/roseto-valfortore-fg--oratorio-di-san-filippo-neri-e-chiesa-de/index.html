Oratorio di San Filippo Neri e chiesa del SS. Corpo di Cristo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In 1623 the archpriest De Santis brought to Roseto the cult of Saint Philip Neri: his house became an oratory that still holds a silver bust of the saint. In the center there is also the church of SS. Body of Christ, an important place of worship between the Seven and the Nineteenth Century, then restored and named after Christ the King, today desecrated; in the village the churches of St. Nicholas and St. Mary Lauretana.

Why go. The silver bust of Saint Philip Neri.
