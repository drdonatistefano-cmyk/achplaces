Museo Internazionale delle Guerre Mondiali (MIGM)

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Civic Museum founded in 2010 at the foot of Monte Marrone, near the places where the Italian Liberation Corps was formed: over 900 square meters in ten rooms with uniforms, accessories and original weapons, a room with more than 200 types of weapons deactivated and external hangars with large military means and artillery, as well as library and rare war periodicals. Seasonal hours from Tuesday to Sunday, 10-13 and 16-20 in summer, 16-19 in winter.

Why go. Means, weapons and relics of world wars on the Mainarde front.

  • Where Via Roma 61/67, Rocchetta a Volturno
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
