Riserva del Padule di Fucecchio e centro visite

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

The largest inland marsh in Italy, about 1,800 hectares between the provinces of Pistoia and Florence, of which about 230 in nature reserve: mirrors of water between reeds, florid and carican ferns, more than 200 species of birds and the most important gauze of central-southern Italy with seven species of herons, as well as black cranes and storks. In Larciano the visitor centre of the reserve of the Padule di Fucecchio and Castelmartini, with exhibitions, guided tours of nature and history and workshops.

Why go. Airons and cranes in Italy's largest inland marsh.
