Italy › Campania › Conca dei Marini

Sentiero delle cinque essenze

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The most panoramic walk of the village, between vine, lemon, olive, carob and pomegranate: it connects the churches of Sant'Antonio and San Michele up to a sacred newsstand overlooking the precipices of Furore.

Why go. The most panoramic path in Conca.
