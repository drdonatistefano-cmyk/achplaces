Museo archeologico nazionale

Museums · Archaeological

In the former stables of Palazzo Rocca, from the prehistory of Tigullio to the furnishings of the necropolis of the Iron Age, up to the Roman and medieval ages; set-up expanded in 2021.

Why go. The necropolis of the Ligurians.
