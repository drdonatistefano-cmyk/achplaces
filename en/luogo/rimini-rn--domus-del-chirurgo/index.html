Domus del Chirurgo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

The house of a doctor of the third century with mosaics and the richest surgical equipment of antiquity ever found, museumized where it was excavated.

Why go. Roman surgical instruments, still in their place.

  • Where Piazza Ferrari, Rimini
  • Entry €7 full, €5 reduced (one ticket with the City Museum); Art Card with the Fellini Museum €14, reduced €10
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.museicomunalirimini.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
