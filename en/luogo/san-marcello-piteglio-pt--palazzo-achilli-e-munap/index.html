Palazzo Achilli e MuNAP

Museums · Science and university

The sixteenth-century palace left in 1845 by Domenico Achilli to the people of Gavinana, today of the Municipality, is home to the center of the Ecomuseum of the Pistoiese Mountain with information point, exhibitions and workshops, and the Natural Archaeological Museum of the Pistoiese Apennines (Munap), renovated in 2018, with animals, rocks, minerals, fossils and the reconstruction of the skeleton of a cave bear; educational garden with hydraulic machines.

Why go. The cave bear and the Apennine nature.
