Palazzo Ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

The palace extended in 1634 on a project by Bartolomeo Avanzani, today seat of the Military Academy, with the Courtyard of Honor and the State apartments. The guided tour includes the Military Academy Museum, with weapons, relics, flags, uniforms and drums.

Why go. You only enter with the guide on weekends.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Modena
  • Entry €10 guided tour
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility Stairways and slopes along the route
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
