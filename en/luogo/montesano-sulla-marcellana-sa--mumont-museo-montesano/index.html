MuMont - Museo Montesano

Museums · Themed

Already Ethno-anthropological Civic Museum, reopened with a new name on November 5, 2021, with the exhibition curated by Alessandra Aufiero. It tells the traditional agricultural and craft world: yokes and finishes, the corner of the blacksmith with zippers and locks, a bedroom rebuilt with rocking iron cradle, the sideboard and the tools for the oven, measures for the wheat and stadera, the corner of the shoemaker, traditional clothes and documents. It also hosts temporary exhibitions and workshops.

Why go. The peasant life of the Vallo di Diano in the household objects.
