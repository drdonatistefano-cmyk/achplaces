Museo Naturalistico Giovanni Zanardo (Museo di Ecologia)

Museums · Science and university

Small naturalistic museum born in the seventies and run by the Carabinieri Forestali, with finds of the plateau, reconstructions of environments and a section on the birdlife. Open from June to October (Wednesday 14-17; Thursday and Friday 9-13 and 14-17; Saturday and Sunday 9-13 and 14-18), in the other months by appointment.
