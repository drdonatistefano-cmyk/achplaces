Museo Nazionale Romano - Crypta Balbi

Museums · Archaeological

Venue of the National Roman Museum dedicated to urban archaeology, a journey of over two thousand years in the history of a block of Rome from ancient times to the Middle Ages; closed for excavations and restorations since 2023, with guided tours "open cantiere."

Why go. The history of Rome in layers.

In progress of re-establishment: check the visits on the site of the National Roman Museum
