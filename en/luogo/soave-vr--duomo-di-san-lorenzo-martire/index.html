Duomo di San Lorenzo Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Pieve documented since 1029, rebuilt in 1303 inside the walls, then demolished and rebuilt between 1744 and 1758 and enlarged in 1884 on a project by Angelo Gottardi. Neoclassical façade with ionic columns and steps in red of Verona; altarpiece of Francesco Morone, paintings of Domenico Morone, Farinati and Cignaroli, statue of the Redeemer of 1553, baptismal font of 1430 and organ of George Trice of 1889.

Why go. A gallery of Verona painters in the parish.
