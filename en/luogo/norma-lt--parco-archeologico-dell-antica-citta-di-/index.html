Parco archeologico dell'antica città di Norba

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Roccaforte founded at the beginning of the fifth century BC against Volsci (Roman Colony from 491 BC), was taken by Silla in 81 BC and, according to Appiano, burned by the inhabitants themselves; never rebuilt, it is called the "Pompei Republicana dei Lepini" . The polygonal drywalls run for about 2.5 km around 44 hectares, with four gates including the monumental Porta Maggiore; inside paved roads, monumental baths, twelve domus, two acropolis with the basements of the temples (including that of Diana) and the sanctuary of Juno Lucina. Why go. A Roman republican city intact in its enclosure of cyclopean walls.
