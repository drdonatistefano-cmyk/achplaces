Piazza del Campidoglio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The square designed by Michelangelo for Paul III, closed by Palazzo Senatorio, Palazzo dei Conservatori and Palazzo Nuovo, with the copy of Marco Aurelio; from here view of the Roman Forum.

Why go. The hill symbol of Rome.

  • Where Piazza del Campidoglio, Roma
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
