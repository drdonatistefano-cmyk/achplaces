Cascata delle Marmore

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

The Velino falls with three jumps for about 165 meters, the highest waterfall in Europe, created by the Romans to drain a swampy area; the paths touch the lower and upper Belvedere, the specola of Pius VI and the "balcony of the lovers."

Why go. A unique water show: check the opening times of the waters.
