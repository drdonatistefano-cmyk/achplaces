Isola Verde e foci del Brenta e dell'Adige

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Small natural and quiet area with beach rich in minerals and vegetation that shades close to the sea, the mouth of the Brenta; the mouths of Brenta and Adige are explored on foot or by bike, with bike paths and horseback riding along the shore.

Why go. Wild beach between two mouths of river.
