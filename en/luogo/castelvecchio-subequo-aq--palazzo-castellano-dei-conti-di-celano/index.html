Palazzo castellano dei Conti di Celano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

It dominates the village and shows construction phases of different eras, with a tower, a body added over time and a loggia; the Park signals the castled palace with Gothic arched door and saracinesca. Castelvecchio was a fief of the Counts of Celano between the 13th and 15th centuries, then from 1484 of the Piccolomini, then of the Colonna, of the Pietropaoli and from 1789 of the Barberini.

Why go. The sign of the Counts of Celano on the village.
