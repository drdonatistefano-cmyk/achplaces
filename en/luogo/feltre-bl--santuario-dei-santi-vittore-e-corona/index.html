Santuario dei Santi Vittore e Corona

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Dedicated to the patrons of the city, it is located in a panoramic position a few kilometers from the center; built in the 11th century by the will of the Crusader Giovanni da Vidor and then expanded, it is a Romanesque complex with Byzantine influences and preserves frescoes of the grotesque school.

Why go. The Romanesque sanctuary of the patrons, overlooking the valley.
