BeGo, Museo Benozzo Gozzoli

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The frescoes by Benozzo Gozzoli for the tabernacles of the Madonna della Cosse (1484) and the Visitation (1490-91), detached and reassembled in the original dimensions. A "museum for all," accessible.

Why go. Welcome up close, out of the way.

  • Where Via Agostino Testaferrata 31, Castelfiorentino
  • Entry €5 whole; €3 reduced
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com
