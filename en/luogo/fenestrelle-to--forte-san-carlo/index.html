Forte San Carlo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the piazza d'armi overlook the governor's palace, the pavilion of the officers and the church; inside three military quarters, the powder keg of St. Ignatius and a royal door. This is where the covered staircase starts.

Why go. The gun square.
