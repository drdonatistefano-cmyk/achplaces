Grotte di Polignano

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Twenty caves dug from the sea in the cliffs, accessible by boat, canoe or SUP and some swimming: the Palazzese, the largest, under a palace today hotel with restaurant; that of the Archbishopric, which is said to be connected by a passage to the bishop's palace; of the nuns, of the Rondinella, Ardito (with a natural column), Stampagnata (without roof) of Sella with a natural bridge and Chiangella.

Why go. The cliffs seen from the sea, cave after cave.
