Chiesa di San Pancrazio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Church documented since 1362, immersed in olive trees with palm trees on the churchyard; legend has it that in the nearby olive grove the janares were gathered. Several times reported to the Places of the FAI Heart.

Why go. A church among olive trees and legends.
