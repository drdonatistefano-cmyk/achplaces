Museo diocesano e parrocchiale di Santa Maria Maddalena

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the parish, he exhibited the Treasure of Santa Maria Maddalena, a former vote of the faithful with Gallura jewels from the 16th to the 18th century, statues, paintings, tapestries and vestments, and the altar furnishing donated by Admiral Nelson: three neoclassical silvers made in 1804 in Barcelona, with autograph letters. Open all year round from Monday to Friday 10-13.30; afternoons and weekends by reservation.

Why go. The silvers donated by Nelson.
