Torri del Castello di Alboino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

The fortress that dominates the historic center dates back to the Longobard domination. The Torre dell'Orologio, the ancient tower-gate of the castle, has a Gothic coat of arms of 1324 and exterior decorations of Lorenzo Luzzo (1518) and houses the "Window of Time" multimedia installation on the evolution of the square since 1510; the Torre del Campanon, about 34 meters high, has the first stages of late-Doromanic construction. The towers, recently restored, are open seasonally and included in the Totem Card Plus (April 1-30 September).

Why go. Climb into the city's symbolic towers.
