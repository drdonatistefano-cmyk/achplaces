Sinagoga

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The synagogue was built in 1672 and rebuilt by Pietro Marchelli in 1858 in the ancient ghetto, today restored by the municipality as a place of culture.

Why go. The memory of Reggio's ghetto.
