Castello di Borgo Fornari

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On a hill at 340 m, it dominates the crossroads between the valley road and the one for Voltaggio. Built by Spinola in the 12th century as a small holiday palace of the feudalist and then enlarged with defensive works, it was long considered the main castle of the upper Scrivia Valley; the semicircular tower could be older. Base in stone and upper floors in brick; the best preserved part is the lower body of the garrison on the walled courtyard, excavated in 2005 by the Institute of Experimental Archaeology of Genoa.

Why go. The main castle of the Upper Scrivia Valley.
