Museo del Miele

Museums · Themed

In Tobia, near Gionghi: hives and instruments from various parts of the world, explanations on the hierarchy of the hive and on the dance of the bees, educational workshops and guided tours, with sale of honey of the Highlands and derived products.

Why go. The world of bees for both adults and children.
