Castello di Amendolara

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

In the Old Rion, with a triangular plan with a single surviving polygonal tower, probably of Norman origin; in the Swabian age was Domus Imperialis and in 1263 hosted Elena of Epirus and the court of Manfredi. Conquered by the Angioins in 1268, it passed among many feudal families until 1808, when the State sold it to the Marquis Gaetano Gallerano. Restored by privates, today it houses a restaurant; from the square with colonnade you can see the woods of Straface and the Dolcedorme Serra.

Why go. A Swabian castle overlooking the Pollino.
