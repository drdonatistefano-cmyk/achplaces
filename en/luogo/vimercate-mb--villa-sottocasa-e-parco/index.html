Villa Sottocasa e parco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Neoclassical villa built at the end of the eighteenth century in the heart of Vimercate, with nineteenth-century frescoes and an English park of over 6 hectares; today it houses the MUST.

Why go. A late 18th century villa with a large English park in the center.
