Palazzo Verga

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace where Giovanni Verga lived, remembered by a plaque (in this house he lived with the creatures of his high imagination Giovanni Verga) the building is presumably between the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries. The upper floors are incomplete for an argument with the nearby Cannizzaro, documented in the municipal historical archive.

Why go. The Vizzinese house of Verga, stage of the Verghian places.
