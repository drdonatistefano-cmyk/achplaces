Il Po a San Pietro e navigazione sul fiume

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

Portalbera rises at the confluence of the Versa nel Po, which until about seventy years ago flowed near the village; here there were a ferry and a port from the thirteenth century and a bridge of boats, still present in 1916, then overwhelmed by a flood. Boat excursions of about an hour and a half with Navigare Fiume Po (€15 per person on October 11, 2026, mandatory booking) start from the pier of the village of San Pietro.

Why go. See the Great River from the water.
