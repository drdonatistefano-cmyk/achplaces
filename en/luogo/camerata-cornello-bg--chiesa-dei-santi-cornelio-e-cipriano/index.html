Chiesa dei Santi Cornelio e Cipriano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On the highest level of the village, of the 12th century and then renovated, with Romanesque bell tower with bifore, rare in Val Brembana; the restorations have brought to light a cycle of frescoes perhaps even of painters of Averara, the Baschenis. The frame of the altarpiece of 1635 with the Crucifixion bears the coat of arms of the Tasso, with the horn of mail and badger.

Why go. The Romanesque bell tower that dominates the village.
