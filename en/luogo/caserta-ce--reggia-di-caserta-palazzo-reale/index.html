Reggia di Caserta – Palazzo Reale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Wanted by Carlo di Borbone and designed by Luigi Vanvitelli: 47,000 m2 on five floors with the Royal Scalone, the Royal Apartments, the Throne Room, the Palatine Library, the Court Nativity Crib, the Palatine Chapel, the eighteenth-century Court Theatre and the contemporary Terrae Motus collection.

Why go. Vanvitelli's masterpiece.

  • Where Piazza Carlo di Borbone, Caserta
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
