Museo Casa Giorgione

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

Opened on May 9, 2009 in the house where Giorgione painted the Fregio delle Arti liberali e meccanici, the only fresco attributed to him, next to the Duomo that houses the Pala; it collects about 100 original pieces of the time, from the civic collection or from the antique market, and architectural reconstructions. Open Mar-Jo 10-13, Friday 10-18; closed Monday, Christmas, January 1st and Easter.

Why go. Giorgione's only fresco in his house.

  • Where Piazza San Liberale, Castelfranco Veneto
  • Entry €7, reduced €5, minor €1, family €12
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.museocasagiorgione.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
