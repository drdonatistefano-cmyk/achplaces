Basilica di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Basilicas

Born from the miracle of Mona Tancia during the plague of 1478, when a grandmother fed her orphan grandson with prayer: the chapel of 1484 became three-nave church in 1523 and the neoclassical façade was built between 1840 and 1856. In the atrium there is the large polychrome terracotta by Giovanni della Robbia (1513), the Assunta that gives the belt to St. Thomas; inside the vaults frescoed at the end of the seventeenth century, the main altar rebuilt after 1596 on a project by Bernardo Buontalenti and the frescoes of the miracle, attributed by Vasari to a pupil of Perugino.

Why go. The Madonna of the Cintola by Giovanni della Robbia.
