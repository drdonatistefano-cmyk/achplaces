Chiesa parrocchiale di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Rebuilt between 1680 and 1715, perhaps by the same workers of the cathedral of Ales, on a Gothic-Aragonese church documented in 1524, of which remain the late dogotic apse with cruising vault and part of the sacristy; restored after the Second World War, it has eighteenth-century bell tower with dome of colored majolica. It preserves the retablo dell'Annunziata, signed and dated 1501 by Lorenzo Cavaro under the mantle of Our Lady, coming from the church of São Paulo di Serzela, and a wooden crucifix of the sixteenth century.

Why go. The retablo of 1501 of the founder of the school of Stampace.
