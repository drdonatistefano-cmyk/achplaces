Chiesa di Sant'Orsola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Started at the end of the sixteenth century and almost completely rebuilt between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, originally "Church of Purgatory" is from 1638 the seat of the Archconfraternity of the Dead. Curved façade with portal in the breach and stone statues of St. Francis and St. Anthony; inside Neapolitan canvases of the seventeenth century, the Purgatory of Nunzio De Nunzi (1707), a Venetian Deposition of the sixteenth century, a sixteenth-century organ and paintings of the Christmas Vault Penati (1938).

Why go. The richest art church in the historic centre.
