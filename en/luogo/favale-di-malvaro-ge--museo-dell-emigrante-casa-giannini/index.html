Museo dell'Emigrante - Casa Giannini

Museums · House museums

Father's house of Amedeo Pietro Giannini, son of Favale's emigrants who, according to legend, left after the earthquake in San Francisco in 1906 with a safe on a cart and founded the Bank of America and Italy. On two floors it preserves original furnishings of the daily life of the nineteenth century, documents, photographs and panels on its life; you can even walk up a mule track. On the last Sunday of June, the country celebrates the "day of the migrant" with the Ligurian communities in the world. Tel. 0185 975195 (Common).

Why go. The family home of the founder of Bank of America.
