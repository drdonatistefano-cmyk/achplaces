Chiesa di Santa Sofia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Founded in the 8th century by a Longobard Duke, it has an original plant that combines two forms, a dome supported by columns and fragments of frescoes of the 8th century; since 2011 it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site on the site "I Longobardi in Italy."

Why go. A Lombard masterpiece.

  • Where Piazza Santa Sofia, Benevento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
