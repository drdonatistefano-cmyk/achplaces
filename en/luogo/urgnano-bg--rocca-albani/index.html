Rocca Albani

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Visconti Castle enlarged in 1354 at the behest of Giovanni Visconti, passed with Venice in a feud to Bartolomeo Colleoni, then to the Longhi and from 1539 to Cardinal Gian Gerolamo Albani; in 1842 Wenceslas Albani restored it, created the hanging garden and rebuilt the Ghibelline Merlature, and since 1953 it is part of the municipality. It preserves moat, mastic with secret passages, fifteenth-century staircase, courtyard with Renaissance porticoes, frescoed rooms (Late 16th century grotesques, Four Seasons), room of the Maps and walkway; guided tours by reservation.

Why go. Seven centuries of history between Visconti, Colleoni and Albani.
