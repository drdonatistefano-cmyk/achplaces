Area faunistica del camoscio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Opened in 1990 for the re-introduction of the Apennine suede on Majella, with educational, scientific and conservation purposes: about five hectares divided into five fences with rocky walls, woods and clearings, and a veterinary center. You can see the chamois from close up; the guided tours are booked at the Visitor Center.

Why go. The Apennine suede up close.
