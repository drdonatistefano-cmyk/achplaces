Museo della Traglia

Museums · Themed

Set up in an ex-church restored with European funds (on the portal dated 1363; inside remains an inscription of consecration of 1696), it collects agricultural tools, wheat-feast branches and the reproduction in wheat and straw of the Holy Door of St. Peter, paraded at the feast of 2000 and blessed that year by the Pope in Rome. The track is a carriage without wheels that slides on two wooden shoes; for the feast the ears, softened in water, are woven in figures such as the ball, the niche, the cone and the hair. Times not indicated by the MiC card: contact the Municipality (0874 710134).

Why go. The Holy Door of wheat and the floats of the feast.
