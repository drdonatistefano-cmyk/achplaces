Castello di Paola

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

A cylindrical tower on a quadrangular bastion, on a rock on a slope overlooking the Tyrrhenian coast; it was the upstream garrison that protected the village and housed the residence of the feudal lord, while the coastal towers controlled the sea. The origins are attributed to the Norman era and then Swabian.

Why go. The view of the Tyrrhenian Sea from above.
