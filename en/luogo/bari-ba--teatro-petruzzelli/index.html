Teatro Petruzzelli

History and architecture · Historic theatres

The fourth theatre of Italy in size, inaugurated in 1903 by the Petruzzelli family: destroyed by arson in 1991 and reopened in 2009, hosts opera seasons, ballet and concerts and offers guided tours.

Why go. The theatre saved from his dome.
