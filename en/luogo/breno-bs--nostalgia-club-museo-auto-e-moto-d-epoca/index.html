Nostalgia Club - Museo auto e moto d'epoca

Museums · Themed

Car on the ground floor, including a FIAT 501 Torpedo of 1925, and motorbike Guzzi, Benelli and Parilla on the upper floor; the most valuable piece is the Paperina Ragnoli of 1948, unique specimen. Library with books, magazines, photos and racing videos. Open from Monday to Friday 8.30-11.30, by reservation evening and weekend.

Why go. For fans of vintage engines.
