Sala Campani (ex convento di Santa Croce)

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The Franciscan monastery of the XIII-XIV centuries, today hotel, preserves the sailing bell tower, frescoes with the life of St. John of Capestrano and a Last Supper of unknown author; the ancient church has become the Sala Campani, exhibition space dedicated to the Campani brothers, opticians and watchmakers of Castel San Felice.

Why go. A Franciscan monastery dedicated to Campani inventors.
