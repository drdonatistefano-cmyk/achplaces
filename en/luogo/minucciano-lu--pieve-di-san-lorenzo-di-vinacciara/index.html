Pieve di San Lorenzo di Vinacciara

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the 12th century, perhaps on a high medieval church, on which depended several other churches of the Val di Magra apuana. Latin cross plan with three naves and semicircular apse; of the medieval wall remains the door in the south-eastern corner, the base of the bell tower is of the fourteenth century, the seventeenth-century portal has the architrave with the Bernardine coat of arms and frames with branches and flowers, and in the bezel a marble bas-relief of San Lorenzo placed in the restoration of 1927.

Why go. The Romanesque on the border with Lunigiana.
