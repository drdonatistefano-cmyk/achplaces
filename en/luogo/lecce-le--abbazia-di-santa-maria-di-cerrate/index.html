Abbazia di Santa Maria di Cerrate

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Well FAI since 2012: the Romanesque church with façade in hut, rosette and portal in Lecce stone houses Byzantine frescoes; around a farm with mill, oven and mill.

Why go. The Byzantine and rural Salento.
