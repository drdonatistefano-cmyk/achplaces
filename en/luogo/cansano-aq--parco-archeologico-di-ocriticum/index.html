Parco archeologico di Ocriticum

History and architecture · Archaeology

An ancient settlement founded by the Peligni in the locality Tavotto-Pantano, depopulated around the ninth century for the Saracen raids that pushed the inhabitants on a cliff further east. It is above all a sacred area with three cult buildings arranged on a two-level plateau at the foot of the Mitra Hill: an Italic temple of Hercules, a sanctuary of Jupiter and a small sacellus of a female deity, of which the perimeters have been carefully reconstructed. It can be reached about 5 km from the village on an asphalted country road and is close to the train stop of the Railway of the Parks, active since summer 2023 on weekends.

Why go. Three Italian sanctuaries at the foot of the Maiella.
