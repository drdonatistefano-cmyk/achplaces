Certosa di Pavia, chiesa e chiostri

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry

Wanted by Gian Galeazzo Visconti, who laid the foundation stone on 27 August 1396, as a dynastic mausoleum to dissolve a vow of his wife Catherine; she was entrusted over time to Carthusians, Cistercians and Benedictines. The marble façade of Carrara, serpentine and porphyry has at the base medallions with heads of ancient characters and, higher up, stories of Christ and the Old Testament. The three-nave church, inspired by the Gothic Cathedral of Milan, has starry sky vaults, fourteen chapels with works by Perugino and Guercino, the funeral monument of Gian Galeazzo and that of Ludovico il Moro and Beatrice d'Este del Solari; next to the small cloister and the large cloister (1472). The €10 ticket replaced the free offer to the monastic community; from Tuesday to Sunday, winter (25/10/2026-25/3/2027) 10-16, summer 10-18, last entry 30 minutes before; online tickets to Museitaliani or on site.

Why go. One of the top of Lombard art between Gothic and Renaissance.

  • Where Viale del Monumento 4, Certosa di Pavia
  • Entry €10, planned reductions; free on the first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
