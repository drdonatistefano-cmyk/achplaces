Museo Archeologico Nazionale e Teatro Romano

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The Roman theatre, brought to light between 1954 and 1960, is part of the museum and still hosts shows; not far away the necropolis of Piazza d'Armi testifies to the aristocracy of the Umbrians of the Iron Age.

Why go. Shows in a Roman theatre.

  • Where Spoleto
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
