Basilica di Sant'Elena imperatrice

Places of worship · Basilicas

Basilica that preserves frescoes, pulpit and baptismal font of the eighteenth century; the statue of the saint is carried in procession on the patronal feast of mid-September.

Why go. The mother church of the city and its feast.
