Villa Manzoni

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The family villa of Alessandro Manzoni, today museum with paintings, manuscripts and first editions, and art gallery.

Why go. The writer's family home.

  • Where Via Don Guanella 1, Lecco
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it
