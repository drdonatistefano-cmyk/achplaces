Castello d'Alagno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortified house built in 1458 by Lucrezia d'Alagno, beloved of King Alfonso I of Aragon, who withdrew there after the death of the king; rises outside the Aragonese walls, just behind the Casamale, in front of Mount Somma. He replaced a more ancient castle near Santa Maria a Castello; among the subsequent owners was Prince De Curtis (Totò), and in 1998 he bought it to make it a museum and library.

Why go. The refuge of Lucrezia d'Alagno, then Totò.
