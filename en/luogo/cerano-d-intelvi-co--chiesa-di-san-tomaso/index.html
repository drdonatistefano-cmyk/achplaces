Chiesa di San Tomaso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Of medieval origins (XII-XIII century) and renovated from the sixteenth century, it is known for its Romanesque bell tower. Inside a six-seventh-century altarpiece with the Unbelief of St. Thomas and stucco statues attributed to the Giani.

Why go. The Romanesque bell tower and the stuccoes of the parish.
