Museo della Badia di San Salvatore «Agnolo Firenzuola»

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated in 1993 and reopened in 1999 after the restoration, he recounts the life of the monks and the role of the ward with finds of excavations, objects of daily life and furnishings of the monastic liturgy: a fifteenth-century ciborium of the circle of Antonio Rossellino, the Commiate of Christ by the Mother attributed to Giacinto Gemignani, a great golden baroque ferculum and, in the Abbot's Chapel, original editions of the works of Agnolo Firenzuola.

Why go. Art and daily life of a Vallombrosan monastery.
