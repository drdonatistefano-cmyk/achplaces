Casa Leopardi

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The palace where Giacomo Leopardi was born (1798-1837), still inhabited by the descendants: the rooms where he grew, the library of rare volumes and the room overlooking the house of Silvia.

Why go. In the poet's house.

  • Where Recanati
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
