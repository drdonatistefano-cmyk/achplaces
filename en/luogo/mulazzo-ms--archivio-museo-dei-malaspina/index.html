Archivio Museo dei Malaspina

Museums · City history

In the Palazzo Malaspina at the top of the village, in front of the Tower of Dante, opened in 2005: over 9,000 books, 600 periodicals and 25,000 manuscript documents collected by Dario Manfredi, with permanent exhibitions on the expedition around the world of the navigator Alessandro Malaspina (born in Mulazzo in 1754), under the command of the Escubierta and Atrevida corvettes from 1789 to 1794. Visitable on request.

Why go. The story of the navigator who went around the world.
