Museo degli usi e costumi delle genti dell'Etna

Museums · Themed

Ethnographic Museum opened in 1995 in Macchia di Giarre to preserve the memory of rural life: five large rooms reconstruct a farmhouse and a peasant house of the last century, with agricultural tools, a spindle and a cone of 1750, a frame, traditional clothes (tailories, wedding dresses, party and Sunday), embroidery and ancient daguerrotypes. Designed as a museum, it hosts activities that revive customs related to popular festivals; guided tours available.

Why go. The peasant life on the slopes of Etna.
