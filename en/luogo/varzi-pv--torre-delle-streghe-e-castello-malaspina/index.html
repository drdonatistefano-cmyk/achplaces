Torre delle Streghe e Castello Malaspina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Square tower of the XII-XIII century, about 29 meters high, fulcrum of the Malaspina fortified complex, documented as a prison by the Statutes of 1320 and used as a prison until the sixties of the twentieth century; the name comes from the tradition according to which in 1460 there were locked up women and men accused of witchcraft before the burning in the square. With 130 steps you can climb to the panoramic terrace overlooking the village, the Staffora and, with the serene, the Castle of Oramala. The adjacent private castle combines an eighteenth-century facade with sundial, a fifteenth-century wing with the coat of arms of Spino Secco and a part of the thirteenth century.

Why go. The view from the tower of the legend of witches.

The interior of the tower is open only on special days, events and guided tours organized by the Municipality or by associations; the castle is privately owned
