Chiesa di Santa Mariedda (Nostra Signora della Neve)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest sanctuary of Senorbì, presumably built in the last quarter of the 13th century as the church of Santa Maria of the village of Segolay, attested from 1215 and disappeared at the end of the 17th century. In square sandstones, it had semicircular apse and unique hall with wooden trusses; the current façade incorporates the Romanesque one with pointed-shaped hanging arches, with a portal and a large sailing bell tower. Party in early August.

Why go. The Romanesque façade of the missing village of Segolay.
