Trullo Sovrano

Museums · House museums · UNESCO World Heritage

The largest trullo of Alberobello and the only two-storey one, with internal staircase in masonry: nucleus to the left of the early seventeenth century, the rest built in the first half of the eighteenth century for the family of the priest Cataldo Perta. The dome of about 14 meters dominates twelve minor cones; among the first trulli built with mortar, national monument since 1923. Vintage furnishings and garden.

Why go. The only two-story trullo.
