Torre civica, Fortilizio di Po e chiese delle confraternite

History and architecture · Towers

The civic tower, the area of the Fortilizio di Po and the churches of Our Lady of Mercy (of the Black Battuti), the Holy Spirit (formerly of the White Battuti, in Via Vittorio Veneto 14) and of St. Joseph, today of the Romanian Orthodox parish, are accessible only with guided tour, to be booked at least two weeks before.
