Santuario di Santa Maria di Macereto

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

On the plateau of Macereto, about 1000 meters below Mount Bove, it was built from 1528-29 by Giovan Battista from Lugano in Bramante's forms around a fourteenth-century chapel: octagonal plan covered with travertine, inside with Greek cross and works by Simone De Magistris (1580-82) in the apse. Next to the Palazzo delle Guaite (1571-83), which with the sanctuary is closed by a wall with a porch for pilgrims and animals.

Why go. Renaissance in high altitude on the Sibillini.
