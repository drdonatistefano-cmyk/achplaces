Riserva della Camosciara

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

The Park's integral reserve between Opi and Civitella Alfedena: limestone amphitheatre and dolomia under Mount Sterpi d'Alto and the Balzo della Chiesa, between beech woods and black pines, where you can spot the chamois. You can visit only on foot and on the paths, also starting from Civitella; an easy path along the creek Scerto leads to the waterfalls, active all year round.

Why go. Chamois and waterfalls in the heart of the Park.
