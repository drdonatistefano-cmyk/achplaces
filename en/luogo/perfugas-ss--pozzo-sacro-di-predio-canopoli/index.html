Pozzo sacro di Predio Canopoli

History and architecture · Archaeology

Nuragic well temple in the center, discovered in 1924 by the owner of the land, dated according to the scholars between the middle and the final Bronze: limestone cones laid dry, vestibule with counters-seats, eight steps staircase and circular chamber with trunk of cone, without the roof to tholos; the style recalls the well of Santa Cristina in Paulilatino. Around, traces of a trachyte fence on a temple in antis and a village; the bull bronze found here is at the Sanna Museum in Sassari.

Why go. A temple of the nuragic water between the houses.
