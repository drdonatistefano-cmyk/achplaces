Complesso di Santa Chiara e San Francesco della Scarpa

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

On the promontory of Bari Vecchia, between the Swabian Castle and the promenade, the former convent of Santa Chiara, born from a church of the thirteenth century of the Teutonic Knights dedicated to Santa Maria degli Alemanni, next to the former convent of San Francesco della Scarpa; today the headquarters of the Superintendence.

Why go. A monumental complex next to the castle.
