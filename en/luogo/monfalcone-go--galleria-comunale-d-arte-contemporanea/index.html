Galleria comunale d'arte contemporanea

Museums · Modern art

In the restored building of the old market in the center, since 2004 member of the AMACI and among the major spaces for contemporary art of the region: a collection of drawings started in 2004 and the bottom of the Montfalcone engraver Tranquillo Marangoni (1912-1992), with over 950 cataloged xylographs.

Why go. Contemporary art in the old market.
