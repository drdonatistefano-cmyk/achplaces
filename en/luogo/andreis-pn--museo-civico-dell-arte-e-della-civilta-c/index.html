Museo civico dell'arte e della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Ethnographic museum opened in July 1981 in the former town hall next to the square: reconstruction of the typical house of Andreis, forge of the blacksmith, combs in bone, Easter ravenelle (cràceles), the working of the shoes and the objects in wood and bone of the "sedoners" , the walking craftsmen of the valley.

Why go. The life and crafts of the Friuli mountain.
