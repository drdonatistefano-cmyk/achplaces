Museo del Carro di Paglia e dei Misteri di cartapesta

Museums · Themed

In the former stables of the Franciscan monastery a path that follows the stages of the Passion with the statues in mache, about one and a half meters tall, that the local artist Antonio Russo (1836-1914) donated to the community in 1875; multimedia rooms explain the technique of mache.

Why go. The Mysteries of Antonio Russo.
