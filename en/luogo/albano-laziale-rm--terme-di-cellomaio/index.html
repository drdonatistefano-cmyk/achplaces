Terme di Cellomaio

History and architecture · Archaeology

Baths built by Caracalla to reconcile with the legionaries of Albano: building on three floors in cement work covered with brick, in the Middle Ages transformed into stronghold and then into houses. Visitable by appointment.

Why go. Imperial thermal baths become fortress.
