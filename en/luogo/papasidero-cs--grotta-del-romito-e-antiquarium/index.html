Grotta del Romito e Antiquarium

History and architecture · Archaeology

Site of the Upper Paleolithic in the locality Nuppolara, discovered in 1961: outside the 1.20 meters long graffito of two bovids (Bos primigenius), among the oldest evidence of prehistoric art in Italy, and burials of about 10,500 years ago (three double burials, including the young hunter The findings cover about 23,000-10,000 years ago; in the Neolithic layer much obsidian suggests a base for trade between Tyrrhenian and Ionian. The site has catwalks, lighting, multilingual audio guides and an antiquarium with some artifacts; the reproduction of the graffito is at the National Museum of Reggio Calabria.

Why go. One of the most important prehistoric sites in Europe.
