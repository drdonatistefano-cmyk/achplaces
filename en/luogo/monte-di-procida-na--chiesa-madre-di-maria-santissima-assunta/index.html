Chiesa Madre di Maria Santissima Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Behind Piazza XXVII January and facing the sea and Procida, it was built in the seventeenth century by Cardinal Ascanio Filomarino and dedicated to the Assumption; enlarged in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, took its present form at the end of the nineteenth century. It preserves the statue of the Assumption of the Neapolitan sculptor Francesco Verzella (1814) and an ancient image of the Madonna considered miraculous, destination of the faithful on 15 August.

Why go. The church of the village and the feast of the Assumption of August.
