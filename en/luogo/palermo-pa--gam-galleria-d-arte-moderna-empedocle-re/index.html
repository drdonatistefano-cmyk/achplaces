GAM – Galleria d'Arte Moderna Empedocle Restivo

Museums · Modern art

Born at the beginning of the twentieth century to collect modern art in Italy, since 2006 it has been located in the monumental complex of Sant'Anna alla Misericordia (a fifteenth-century residence and a seventeenth-century convent), with collections ordered on three floors and 19 sections.

Why go. Eight and twentieth century in the heart of Kalsa.
