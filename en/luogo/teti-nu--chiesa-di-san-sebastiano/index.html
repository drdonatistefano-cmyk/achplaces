Chiesa di San Sebastiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest church of Teti, originally Gothic-Aragonese, rises in a park not far from the village surrounded by the cumbessias, the little houses that house the faithful during the feast of the saint at the end of August.

Why go. A country church with its cumbessias.
