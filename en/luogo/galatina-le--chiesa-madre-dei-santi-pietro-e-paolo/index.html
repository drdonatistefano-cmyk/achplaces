Chiesa Madre dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church built in 1335, rebuilt from 1633 and completed in 1770 in Baroque Lecce, with monumental Rococo façade rich in friezes, niches and statues. Inside, frescoes, altars in polychrome marble and paintings, the Captello del Sacramento of the second half of the seventeenth century, the vault frescoed at the end of the nineteenth century with the life of St. Peter and the stone on which, according to tradition, St. Peter rested, brought here in 1670.

Why go. The most scenic Baroque façade in the centre.
