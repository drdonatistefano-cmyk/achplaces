Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Opened in 2001 in the historic center, it is under construction in a renovated municipal building in Borgo Vaccareccia-Mezzana: two rooms with ancient crafts and crafts, relics of the two world wars and twenty years and the reconstruction of family homes of the early twentieth century with furnishings and utensils.

Why go. The peasant life of the Dauni Mountains.
