Museo del Merletto

Museums · Themed

In the Palazzo delle Opere Pie dell'Isola Maggiore he exhibits embroidery from the early twentieth century of the school founded by Elena Guglielmi, daughter of Senator Giacinto Guglielmi, where the fishermen's daughters learned the "Irish Point" in crochet; the school had international recognitions.

Why go. The Irish spot lace is of the fishermen.
