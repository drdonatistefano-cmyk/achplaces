Chiese di San Giuseppe e della Concezione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

San Giuseppe has a white stone portal richly decorated with a chiaramontana inspiration; the church of the Conception has a palemontano portal with acute arches coming from the church of San Nicolò of the ancient farmhouse of Adragna and sculptures of the eighteenth century.

Why go. Two Gothic portals on the course.
