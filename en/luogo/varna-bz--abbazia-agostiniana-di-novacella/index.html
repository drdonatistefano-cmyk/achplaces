Abbazia agostiniana di Novacella

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded in 1142 by Blessed Bishop Hartmann, it is still an active monastery of Augustinian Canons; in 1742 it was the largest in Tyrol and in the Middle Ages it hosted a school of copyists and choral chanting. The church of the Assumption, Romanesque, was transformed into baroque forms; to see the frescoed Gothic cloister, the courtyard well with the wonders of the world, the roundabout Castel dell'Angelo (a former hospice and defence), the rococo library, the gallery with medieval paintings by Michael and Friedrich Pacher and Marx Reichlich, the wall of the Turks, the mill and the cellar, where the abbey still produces its wines (Sylvaner, Müller-Thurgau, Kerner). Free tours from Monday to Saturday 10-17, closed Sunday and Catholic holidays.

Why go. One of the great monasteries of the Alps, including art, library and wine.
