Mausoleo detto «Pantheon di Alife»

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

From the first century AD, just outside the eastern door: cylindrical body with dome on a square base of about 13 meters, circular interior with eight niches; it was church of St. John Gerosolimitano from the end of the thirteenth century and votive chapel to the fallen since 1924, and today houses exhibitions. Attribution to the Acili Glabrions is not proven.

Why go. A miniature Pantheon.
