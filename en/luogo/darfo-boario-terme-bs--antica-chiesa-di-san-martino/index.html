Antica chiesa di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Of the original church remain the bell tower and a single chapel, entirely frescoed in the fifteenth century; fifteenth century is also the entrance portal of the cemetery.

Why go. A fifteenth-century chapel all painted.
