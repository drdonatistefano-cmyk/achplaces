Museo della Navigazione nelle Acque Interne (MNAI)

Museums · Themed

Born in 2010 after the discovery, in 1989, of a protohistoric piroga in the lake near the island of Bisentina, it tells the navigation in the inland waters from the antiquity to the twentieth century, with finds from the lake of Bolsena and boats from Bracciano, Fondi and Posta Fibreno; the route illustrates the naval architecture of fresh water and the activities of fishing, communication and trade. Timetable indicated by the Municipality: Monday-Friday 8.30-13.30 (tel. 0761 872437).

Why go. The prehistoric piroga of Lake Bolsena.
