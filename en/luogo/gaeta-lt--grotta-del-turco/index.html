Grotta del Turco

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry · Viewpoint

It is the first crack of the Spaccata Mountain, to the left of the entrance of the sanctuary: it descends for about 300 steps, secured, up to the reflections of light on the sea; according to legend it was a refuge of pirates. Opened in 2025 after ten years; times indicated by Visit Lazio: Tuesday-Sunday 9-12 and 14:30-17:30, free admission.

Why go. The descent to the sea into the rock.

  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
