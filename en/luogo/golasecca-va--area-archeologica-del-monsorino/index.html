Area archeologica del Monsorino

History and architecture · Archaeology

State since 2001, it is the most important monumental testimony of the Celtic culture of Golasecca (XII-V century BC) in north-western Italy, identified in the nineteenth century and excavated from the end of the century. Three circular funeral enclosures and two rectangular 8th-VII century BC are visible, delimited by large stones (the cromlech), with tombs grouped in small groups probably familiar; the cinerary urns with ceramic, bronze and iron sets are at the Civic Museum of Sesto Calende and at the Castello Sforzesco in Milan. For the visit the Park of Ticino refers to its authorized naturalistic guides.

Why go. The stone circles of a pre-Roman civilization.
