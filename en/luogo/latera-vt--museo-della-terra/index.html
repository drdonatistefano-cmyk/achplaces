Museo della Terra

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 2001 in the Altomedieval complex of the Grancia di San Pietro and in the adjacent former municipal slaughterhouse, it tells with objects and multimedia materials the story of Latera around the theme of the earth. The core is the Poscia Collection, about 2000 utensils of peasant life, craftsmanship and daily (house and workshop, wood and plain, markets, weights and measures); an audiovisual path on the immaterial heritage includes the Gallery of the processions, the Four Cantons and the Bauli of memory; outdoor local crops and a vegetable garden of aromatic herbs and medicinal. From the museum starts the Trail of the three churches towards abandoned quarries of industrial archaeology; audioguides available.

Why go. The peasant life of Tuscia in two thousand objects.
