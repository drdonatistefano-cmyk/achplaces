Ex Officina Ferroviaria

Museums · Themed

Former steam locomotive shed at the end of the Barge-Bricherasio line (launched in 1883, inaugurated on 6 September 1885): two-storey building with central body and two wings, raised roof for the vent of fumes and steam and pit for ashes and water; on the upper floor the drivers stayed. It is indicated as a railway museum, open Saturday and Sunday 16-22.

Why go. A typical example of railway architecture.
