Museo civico «Mario Morelli»

Museums · Modern art

Born from the free donation to the city of the personal collection of Professor Mario Morelli, who chose the birthplace of his master Aleandro Curatolo: paintings and valuable objects.

Why go. The collection donated by a professor to the city of his teacher.
