Parrocchiale di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between the end of the sixteenth century and the beginning of the seventeenth century at the behest of Francesco Gonzaga, bishop of Mantua; inside stand out the paintings of Andrea Mainardi called the Chiaveghino, of the school of the Camps.

Why go. Chiaveghino's canvases.
