Centro storico e Piazza Umberto I

History and architecture · Villages · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Of Roman origin (Atranum), it retains the medieval structure considered the best preserved of the Coast: alleys, arches, courtyards, squares and stairs that go down to the sea. The little square on the sea was born as a shelter for boats in stormy days; under the arches where fishermen used to repair nets today there are some trattorias. On 22 July, St Mary Magdalene is celebrated.

Why go. The most intact medieval village on the Coast.
