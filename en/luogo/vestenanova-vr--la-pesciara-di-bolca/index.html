La Pesciara di Bolca

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry

A well-known place for over 500 years and famous worldwide for the number and conservation of the fossils of the tertiary era, especially fish and plants, in five layers superimposed of fine limestone; it is a mountain above Bolca crossed by tunnels, which with the Monte Postale forms a paleontological park. Visit the inner gallery and search the land for small fossils.

Why go. Enter the gallery where the fossils of Bolca were born.

  • Where Via San Giovanni Battista 50, Bolca, Vestenanova
  • Entry Guided tour of the gallery €3, reduced €1; fossil research laboratory €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it
