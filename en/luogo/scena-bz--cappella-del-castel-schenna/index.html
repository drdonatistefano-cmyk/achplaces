Cappella del Castel Schenna

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The castle chapel houses small artistic gems: painted from the 17th to the 19th century with religious scenes that give clues to the owners of the castle. During the restoration there were unknown inscriptions that recall dramatic events of the seventeenth century with at the center a woman venerated as a saint. It can be visited only with a guided tour in German (10-10.30, from May to October); it still hosts celebrations of the liturgical year.

Why go. The little secret revealed by the restoration.
