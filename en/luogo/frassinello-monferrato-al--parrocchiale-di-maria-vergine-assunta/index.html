Parrocchiale di Maria Vergine Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It preserves a 12-log organ built in 1864 by the Pavese organaro Luigi Lingiardi and the instruments of the Passion of Christ used in the traditional ceremony of the Entierro.

Why go. The Lingiardi organ and the Entierro tradition.
