Riserva naturale orientata dello Zingaro

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Protected coast to walk only, with five marked trails, coves like Cala Tonnarella, Cala dell'Uzzo, Cala Marinella, Cala Beretta, Cala Disa, Cala Capreria and Cala del Varo, and five museums on traditional activities such as tuna fishing and interweaving. It has two entrances, north from San Vito and south from Scopello; there are no refreshment points.

Why go. The most famous coves on the coast of Trapani, on foot.

Reopened partially from March 14th 2026 after the fire: the north entrance towards San Vito Lo Capo remains closed for works, access only from Scopello (Sicily region); payment only electronic, free under 8 years; italia.it indicates opening 8-16
