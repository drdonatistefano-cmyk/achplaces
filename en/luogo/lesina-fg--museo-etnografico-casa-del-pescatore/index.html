Museo etnografico «Casa del Pescatore»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Set up on the lakefront in the building of the Visitor Centre, it reconstructs a fishing house of the early twentieth century with objects donated by the Lesinesi and an ancient sandal, telling the lagoon culture of the Capitana, between land and water.

Why go. The life of the lagoon fishermen.
