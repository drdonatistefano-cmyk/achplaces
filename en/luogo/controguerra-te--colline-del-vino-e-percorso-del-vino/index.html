Colline del vino e Percorso del Vino

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

The territory is a landscape of hills and olive groves that descend towards the coast; the village is part of the Città dell'Olio and produces DOC and DOCG wines to be tasted in the local farms. The Wine Route, about 30 km between hills and tastings, starts from Controguerra and reaches Torano Nuovo, Ancarano, Corropoli and Colonnella; in summer the Wine Festival and the Wine Bike animate the historic center and vineyards.

Why go. Tastings in the vineyards with sea view.
