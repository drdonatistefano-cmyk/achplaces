Museo civico Vittorino Cazzetta

Museums · Archaeological

Established in 1982 and re-arranged and expanded in 2010 on four floors, it is a geological-paleontological, archaeological and historical museum. The best known piece is the burial complete with a mesolithic hunter lived about 7,500 years ago, found in 1987 in Mondeval de Sora at about 2,150 m; next, the cast of the Pelmetto mass with dinosaur prints, a paleoveneta stele of Monte Pore, copies of the rock inscriptions of the Civetta and sections on the Neolithic site of Mandriz, on the Gothic alpine and on the mines of Fursil. It is part of the UNESCO Dolomites museum network; book-shops, cafés, libraries and educational workshops.

Why go. Meet a hunter from 7,500 years ago and in the footsteps of dinosaurs.
