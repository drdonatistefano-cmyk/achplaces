Ecomuseo Mulino al Pizzon

Museums · Themed

Ecomuseum in an intact natural environment where the canal Scortico flows into the Canalbianco (Tartaro-Po di Levante): an example of industrial archaeology of Polesine that still regulates water for agriculture in droughts and helps to prevent floods. It is part of the Polesine Provincial Museum System and organizes educational visits on bread, ancient cereals and renewable energies.

Why go. Industrial archaeology among the canals of Polesine.
