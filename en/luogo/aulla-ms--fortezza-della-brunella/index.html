Fortezza della Brunella

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On a height at the confluence of Magra and Aulella, it is the most powerful military work of the Lunigiana: Renaissance fortress with square plan attributed to Jacopo Ambrogio Malaspina (end of the fifteenth century) or to Giovanni dalle Bande Nere (1522), perhaps according to design by Antonio da Sangallo the Elder, already efficient in 1553. It was taken by the Spaniards on 24 December 1733 after three weeks of siege, it was the residence of the British Lina Duff Gordon and Aubrey Waterfield since 1920 and since 1977 it has been of the State, entrusted to the Municipality. Guided tours every 45 minutes without reservation; free for under 6 and residents.

Why go. The fortress that dominates the Lunigiana.
