Castello normanno-svevo (Palazzo Marchesale)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Hunting residence of Frederick II, who chose the area as a reserve and raised horses there; it has two square towers, four mullioned windows and a central trifora similar to a loggia. From the mid-th century the fief passed to families like the Ruffos and the Caracciolos.

Why go. The hunting castle of Frederick II.
