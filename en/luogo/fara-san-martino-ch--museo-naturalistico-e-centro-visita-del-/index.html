Museo naturalistico e Centro visita del Parco

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

Attached to the Park Visitor Centre in the main square, it presents the environments and caves of the eastern side of Majella, from high altitudes to rocky walls, to moors, gorges and underground environments, with photos, illustrations, panels and natural finds; it reconstructs a pastoral cave with the habitat of the yellow-bellyed ululon, rare amphibian, and an audiovisual panel makes you listen to the songs of the birds of the Park. It is the starting point for the Valley of Santo Spirito and the green springs.

Why go. To understand Majella before going up there.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Fara San Martino
  • Entry €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
