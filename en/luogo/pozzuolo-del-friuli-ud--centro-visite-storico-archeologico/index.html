Centro visite storico-archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 1998, it documents the ancient history of the territory and especially the Neolithic site of Sammardenchia, one of the largest in Europe (about 5500-4700 BC), which has returned tools in flint, stone and ceramics. Educational path with settings, copies of artifacts to touch and experimental archaeology workshops for children; a section is dedicated to the local protohistory and castle of Pozzuolo.

Why go. The great Neolithic village of Sammardenchia.
