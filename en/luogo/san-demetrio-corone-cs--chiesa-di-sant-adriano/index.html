Chiesa di Sant'Adriano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the Basilian abbey founded in the tenth century by San Nilo di Rossano. With three naves with wooden roof and ancient columns, it has frescoes of saints from the 12th to the 13th century and, in the apse, the Presentation of Mary at the Temple; the Baroque dome shows the Creator with holy monks and Saint Nile in prayer. The high altar (1731) is attributed to Domenico Costa, with the painting of the Martyrdom of Saint Adriano probably by Francesco Saverio Ricci and two wooden busts of the seventeenth century. The masterpiece is the floor, partly in opus sectile and partly in mosaic; the main portal has been closed since 1856 and you enter from the sides, under the bell tower or from the Porta dei Monaci with two stone masks.

Why go. The Byzantine mosaics of the abbey of St. Nile.
