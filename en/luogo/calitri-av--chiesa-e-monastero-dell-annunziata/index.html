Chiesa e monastero dell'Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The sixteenth-century church, with a Renaissance bezel portal, is among the few remaining standing after the earthquake of 1694; next door, the monastery founded in 1557 for the Benedictines and finished in 1586 is now the Town Hall, and its portal on Via Roma was the entrance of the inner garden.

Why go. One of the few churches that survived 1694.
