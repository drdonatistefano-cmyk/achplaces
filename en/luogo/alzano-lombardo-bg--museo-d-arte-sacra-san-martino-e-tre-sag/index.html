Museo d'arte sacra San Martino e Tre Sagrestie

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The three sacristies, designed by Gerolamo Quadrio, were furnished in a little over fifteen years with wardrobes, kneelers and chests carved and inlaid by the shops Fantoni and Caniana, between stuccoes and frescoes, in a passage from baroque to rococo. From the third you enter the museum, opened in 1995 in a seventeenth-century palace purchased by the Pellicioli in 1670: 14 rooms with miniature chorals, vestments, the "San Cristoforo" of Tintoretto, the "Martyrdom of Saint Peter of Verona" of Palma the Old and 44 small wooden sculptures of the eighteenth-century Via Crucis. Closed on Tuesdays; groups by reservation.

Why go. The sacristies inlaid among the richest in Lombardy, with Tintoretto and Palma the Elder.

  • Where Piazza Italia 8, Alzano Lombardo
  • Entry From €6; reduced for families, students and groups; free up to 14 years
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
