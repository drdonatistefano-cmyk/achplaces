Cappella di Castel d'Appiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

An isolated chapel in the courtyard of the castle, consecrated around 1131 and known as the Sistine Chapel of the Alps for the Romanesque frescoes of the 13th century, exceptionally well preserved: stories of Jesus and the Apostles, the foolish Virgins, the Visitation, a hunting scene and the famous menu of canederli. It is the stop of the Romanesque Alpine Road between Chur and Lake Garda; guided tours of the historical and artistic half an hour, on the days of the opening of the castle.

Why go. The most famous Romanesque cycle in South Tyrol, with the first canederlo of art history.
