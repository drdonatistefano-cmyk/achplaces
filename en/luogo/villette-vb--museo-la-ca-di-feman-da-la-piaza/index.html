Museo «La Ca di Feman da la Piaza»

Museums · Themed

Museum of rural art in a 17th century stone house in the oldest part of the country, dedicated to the three single sisters that the inhabitants called \"feman da la piza" . On the ground floor tools for agriculture, milk processing, chestnut harvesting and fishing, a large fireplace with branches and a nineteenth-century bedroom; on the upper floor tools for textile fibres and footwear, the model of a mill, a working frame and ancient school benches. Open in July Friday-Sunday and August every day (16.30-18.30), in the rest of the year by reservation.

Why go. The daily life of a mountain country in a house of the seventeenth century.
