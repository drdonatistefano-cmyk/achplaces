Museo archeologico comunale

Museums · Archaeological

In the thirteenth century Palazzo della Comunità on the main square, tells the ancient Signia from prehistory to the Middle Ages on two floors: necropolis sets, ex-vote in terracotta (tones, statuettes, a head of newborn in bands), figures of Minerva, Hercules and Venus, sacred and funerary Latin inscriptions, ceramics, architectural fragments and reconstructive plastics of the city. It runs the urban archaeological park; the specialist library is visited by appointment.
