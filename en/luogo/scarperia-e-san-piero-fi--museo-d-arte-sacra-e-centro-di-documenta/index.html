Museo d'arte sacra e Centro di documentazione archeologica di Sant'Agata

Museums · Archaeological

Next to the parish church of Sant'Agata: works of the church such as the Spousalizio di Santa Caterina di Bicci di Lorenzo (1430) and Robbian terracotta, and finds of Mugello from the Paleolithic to the age of metals with the reconstruction of a prehistoric village.

Why go. Two small museums in a quiet part.
