Cattedrale di Sant'Agata

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The cathedral on the piazza del Duomo, dedicated to the patron saint: since 1876 it houses the remains of Vincenzo Bellini in a funeral monument near a pillar of the right aisle and is home to the concerts of the Bellini Festival.

Why go. The tomb of the "Swan of Catania."

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Catania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
