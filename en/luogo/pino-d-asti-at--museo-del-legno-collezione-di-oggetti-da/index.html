Museo del Legno – collezione di oggetti da falegname ed ebanista

Museums · Themed

It is part of the Ecomuseum of the Bass Monferrato Astigiano and preserves, in the former Municipal Furnace, a collection of carpenter and cabinetmaker objects. The Romanesque itinerary from Castelnuovo Don Bosco mentions Pino d'Asti among the small villages of Castelnuovo with the Wood Museum.

Why go. The tools of the wood trade.
