Marrone e sagra delle castagne

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The village is known for its brown, roasted on the fire in the traditional pan at the festival of late October; in the table polenta with truffle, sheep sauces, goat and sheep cheese and the Cesanese del Piglio DOCG.

Why go. Chestnuts and mountain cuisine.
