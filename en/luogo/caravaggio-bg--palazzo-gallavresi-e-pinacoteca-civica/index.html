Palazzo Gallavresi e Pinacoteca civica

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

A noble residence from the 16th century, named after the last owner, today a town hall: Giampaolo Sforza, the first Marquis and son of Moro, lived there, witness of the wedding of Michelangelo Merisi's parents. In the façade tombstones to Vittorio Emanuele II, Garibaldi, Cavour and Mazzini next to ancient openings to Gothic arch; on the noble floor the Pinacoteca civica with works by painters of the Five and seventeenth centuries such as Moietta and Procaccini and paintings of the romantic Moriggia. Visits by reservation.

Why go. The palace linked to the wedding of the parents of Caravaggio.
