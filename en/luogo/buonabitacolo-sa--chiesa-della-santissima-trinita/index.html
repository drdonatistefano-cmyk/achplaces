Chiesa della Santissima Trinità

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It preserves a Renaissance canvas by Orazio Iacobotta da Spinazzola dated 1583, with the family of Alfonso Abbatemarco in prayer, rich in details on clothes, objects and environment, which offers a glimpse of the ancient Good cockpit.

Why go. A family portrait of the sixteenth century.
