Museo Casa di Giotto a Vespignano

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The house where tradition wants Giotto born, with materials on his work and the reconstruction of the workshop of a painter of the fourteenth century; near, the bridge of the encounter with Cimabue.

Why go. Vicchio is also the village of Blessed Angelico.

  • Where Vespignano, Vicchio
  • Entry €4
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
