Chiesa dell'Annunziata (ex Abbazia di Sant'Angelo)

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Romanesque church of the 11th century with two Byzantine domes of different sizes, born from a monastery perhaps of the 8th century, Benedictine complex before 1125 and entrusted in the 13th century to the Knights of Calatrava (1228-1294).

Why go. The Byzantine domes of the ancient abbey.
