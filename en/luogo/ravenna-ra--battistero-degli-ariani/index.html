Battistero degli Ariani

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The baptistery of the Aryan cult of Theodoric, octagonal and now partly underground, with a baptism of Christ in the dome to be compared with that of Neonian.

Why go. Seeing the two baptisteries on the same day tells the two rival churches of the Ravenna Gota.

  • Where Piazzetta degli Ariani, Ravenna
  • Entry €3 (no reduced); also included in the state cumulative from €7 with Mausoleum and Palace of Theodoric
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website cultura.gov.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
