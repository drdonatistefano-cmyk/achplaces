Parco archeologico di Monte Luna e Santu Teru

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The settlement of Santu Teru (Teodoro), about 2 km from the village at the confluence of two streams, was founded at the end of the 6th century BC as a Carthaginian outpost and developed as an agricultural center between the 5th and the 3rd century BC, when it was abandoned: the acropolis, a walls, houses outside the walls and perhaps fusion workshops remain. On the slopes of Monte Luna, a few tens of meters, more than 120 tombs have been identified, mostly a well with side rooms, but also a pit, a chest, loculus and enchytrismos for children, with votive trays and niches with traces of geometric paintings in red ochre. In Roman times Monte Luna became the most important center of the area.

Why go. The richest Punic necropolis in Trexenta.
