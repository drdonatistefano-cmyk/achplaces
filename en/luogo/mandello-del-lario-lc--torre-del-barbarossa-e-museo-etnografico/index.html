Torre del Barbarossa e Museo etnografico

History and architecture · Towers · Paid entry · Viewpoint

A square tower in Maggiana, 40 arms high, with ogival windows and slits overlooking the lake: according to tradition in 1158 the Mandelli family hosted Frederick Barbarossa, who from here would observe the naval battles between Milan and Como; a granite plaque with date 1158, found in 1828, was lost. Owned by the Amici di Maggiana Group from 1976, it houses an ethnographic museum with objects and images from the end of the nineteenth century to the middle of the twentieth century on the life and work of the hamlet.

Why go. Climb into the emperor's tower and discover Maggiana's peasant life.
