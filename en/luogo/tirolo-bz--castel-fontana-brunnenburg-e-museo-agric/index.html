Castel Fontana (Brunnenburg) e Museo agricolo

Museums · Themed

Built around 1250, with fortifications of 1241-1253, destroyed and rebuilt several times and restored in a unique way at the beginning of the twentieth century; it rises on an ice conoid between the village and Castel Tyrol and would take its name from a nearby source. Today it is a cultural centre and hosts the South Tyrolean Agricultural Museum. You can walk to the Falkner promenade and the Ezra Pound trail.

Why go. A castle-museum on the path to Castel Tirol.
