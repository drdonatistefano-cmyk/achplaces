Castello Scaligero

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built between 1260 and 1276 in the defensive system of the Scaligeri to control the valley towards Garda, it is a castle fenced with an elongated and polygonal plan with five towers, two of which real buildings closed; lost military function in the eighteenth century. Access from a single staircase, not suitable for wheelchairs and strollers; audioguide in Italian, English and German.

Why go. The Scaliger fence that dominates the country.

closed to the public for restoration works, guided tours only by reservation (tourist site of the Municipality)
