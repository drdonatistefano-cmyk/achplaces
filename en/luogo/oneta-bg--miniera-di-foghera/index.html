Miniera di Foghera

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

The Val del Riso mine is safely and open to the public recently and can be reached by a walk of about 10 minutes from Plassa. From the mountains of the valley were extracted blend, galena and calamine to obtain zinc, lead and sulfuric acid; the mines were opened by the Romans over two thousand years ago and exploited also by the condemned to forced labor.
