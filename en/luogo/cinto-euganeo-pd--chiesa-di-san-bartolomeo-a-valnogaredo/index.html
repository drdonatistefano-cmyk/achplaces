Chiesa di San Bartolomeo a Valnogaredo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Rebuilt in 1758 by the brothers Angelo and Giulio Contarini next to their villa, it has facade with statues and rocaille portal attributed to Antonio Bonazza; inside, with a single nave with rocaille stuccoes, a large fresco on the ceiling of Jacopo Guarana, angels in Bonazza marble on the sides of the main altar and a altarpiece by Domenico Fedeli called the Mayt. Under the altar rests the body of the holy pope Adeodato, donated to the Contarini by Innocent XII.

Why go. Venetian Rococo in the hills, with Guarana and Bonazza.
