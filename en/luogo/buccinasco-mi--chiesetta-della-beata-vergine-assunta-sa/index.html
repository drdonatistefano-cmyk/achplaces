Chiesetta della Beata Vergine Assunta (San Michele)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A few meters from the castle, it is already mentioned in the chronicles of Goffredo da Bussero of the late thirteenth century, when Buccinasco had a church dedicated to San Michele; it depends on the church of Romano Banco and is owned by the counts Brivio Sforza. It preserves a Crucifixion frescoed in 1480, attributed to Zanetto Bugatto, and a subsequent fresco by anonymous author with the saints Agata, Barbara and Apollonia, probably ex-vote to protect the crops.

Why go. A fifteenth century Crucifixion attributed to Zanetto Bugatto.
