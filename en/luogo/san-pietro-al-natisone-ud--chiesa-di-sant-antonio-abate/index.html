Chiesa di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church in the hamlet of Clenia with late-Gothic apse that houses a golden altar of 1601-1602, then modified by Bartolomeo Ortari; on the walls of the aisle there is a cycle of frescoes from the fifteenth century.

Why go. Frescoes from the fifteenth century and a golden altar from the early seventeenth century.
