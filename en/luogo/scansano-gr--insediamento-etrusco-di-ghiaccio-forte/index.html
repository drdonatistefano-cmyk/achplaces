Insediamento etrusco di Ghiaccio Forte

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Etruscan village founded in the fourth century BC near the border of Vulci, on the right of the Albegna, perhaps refuge from the Roman assaults or center of wine producers; it was conquered and devastated in 280 BC. The best preserved part is the 1 km long walls and about 4 meters thick; inside the remains of the small Temple of a farm deity, whose ex-vote are at the Archaeological Museum.

Why go. The most powerful Etruscan walls of the Maremma.
