Monastero di San Benedetto e Museo Civico Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Tenth century complex with Romanesque church, medieval cloister of the XI-XIII and two bell towers, a Norman and a Baroque maiolic; for centuries home of mitrated abbesses, it houses the museum with the artifacts of the city from prehistory to late antiquity.

Why go. A medieval cloister and the history of the city.

  • Where Via San Benedetto, Conversano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
