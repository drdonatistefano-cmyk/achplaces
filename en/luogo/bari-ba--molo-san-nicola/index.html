Molo San Nicola

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

The small pier of "Nderr la lanz," open-air restaurant where you can taste the "fisherman breakfast" with curls, oysters, mussels, octopus and raw shrimps just caught.

Why go. The raw sea of the Bari.
