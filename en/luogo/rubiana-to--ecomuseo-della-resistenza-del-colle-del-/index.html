Ecomuseo della Resistenza del Colle del Lys

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

On the watershed between the Lanzo Valleys and the Susa Valley, it tells the history of the Resistance and the events that took place in this territory with films shot with witnesses, CD-ROMs, touring exhibitions and brochures on the routes; the territory of the Ecomuseum has returned to live through rediscovered paths and offers wide views.

Why go. Partisan memory on the site of events.
