Teatro comunale Pietro Montanari

History and architecture · Historic theatres

It was wanted by the merchant Pietro Montanari, who presented the project in 1910, and was inaugurated on February 9, 1911 with the comedy "Il Cardinale" and hosted also cinemas, dances and parties; the Municipality bought it in 1986 and reopened it in 1993 with the flutist Andrea Griminelli. The small elliptical hall has two orders of galleries in eclectic style, painted by Sardinian Luigi Pillitu with Musici and Comedants that form a fixed scene aimed at the public.

Why go. A painted audience with its imaginary audience.
