Chiesa dei Santi Vittore e Corona

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient Romanesque church near the Fortress, along the Ru Nero, where in the twelfth century the bodies of the two saints were transferred.

Why go. A small Romanesque church linked to the relics of the Saints Vittore and Corona.
