Museo di Archeologia Industriale Antica Miniera Brunetta

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Molco mine at 1,580 m in the valley of Brissout, active from the early twentieth century to 1979, set up and managed by CAI di Lanzo. The galleries, about 3 km on two levels, are preserved as during the activity and are only visited with a guide by reservation; the external mining park (miner's room, electric locomotive shed, engine room) is always open and freely accessible. You can only walk from the village Vrù, in about an hour-one-and-a-half; not accessible in a wheelchair.

Why go. Galleries and machines of a high-altitude mine.
