Museo Francesco Messina - Salvatore Incorpora

Museums · Modern art

Museum dedicated to the Sicilian and Italian twentieth century, in the former Casa del Fascio: it collects artistic nativity scenes, paintings and sculptures of the Calabrian Salvatore Incorpora, whose work is linked to Guttuso, Migneco and Rijeka; it is also named after the sculptor Francesco Messina, baptized in the Mother Church in 1901.

Why go. The Sicilian twentieth century at the foot of Etna.
