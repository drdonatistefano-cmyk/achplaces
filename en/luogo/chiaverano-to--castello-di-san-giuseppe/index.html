Castello di San Giuseppe

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the top of Mount Albagna, in the middle of a morainic arch between small lakes and a park of 11 hectares. In 1640 the hermit Tomaso Barberio built a chapel in San Giuseppe; then became a convent of Teresiani and Carmelitani and sanctuary, was fortified in Napoleonic age and transformed into a noble residence, where Eleonora Duse, Arrigo Boito and Giuseppe Giacosa stayed at the end of the nineteenth century. Restored after long abandonment, today it is a hotel.

Why go. History of hermits, soldiers and artists on a morainic balcony.
