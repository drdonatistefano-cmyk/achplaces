Castello Longhi De Paolis e cella di Celestino V

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Well-preserved medieval fortress of the Marquis Longhi De Paolis since the end of the sixteenth century; there was imprisoned Pope Celestine V in 1296, who according to tradition died there after ten months using the step of the altar as a pillow. You can visit the cell, the small sanctuary of Celestino V built by the Longhi, furnishings and relics mostly nineteenth century and the case with the embalmed body of the little Francesco Longhi Caetani, who died at five years. Visits by reservation required.

Why go. Celestine V Prison, also mentioned by Dante.
