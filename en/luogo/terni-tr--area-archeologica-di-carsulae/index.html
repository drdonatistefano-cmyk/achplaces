Area archeologica di Carsulae

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The remains of the Roman town hall arose along the Via Flaminia (traced around 220 BC): forum, Gemini temples, well-preserved theatre and amphitheatre, spas, necropolis and the monumental arch of San Damiano.

Why go. A Roman city abandoned in the middle of the meadows.

  • Where Strada di Carsoli 8, Terni
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
