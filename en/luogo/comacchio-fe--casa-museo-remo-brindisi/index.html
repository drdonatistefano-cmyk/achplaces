Casa Museo Remo Brindisi

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Artist's villa designed by Nanda Vigo in the early 1970s, with works by Modigliani, Picasso, Fontana, Dalí, De Chirico, De Pisis and Morandi.

Why go. Art of the twentieth century a short walk from the beach.
