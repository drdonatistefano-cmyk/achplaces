Chiesa campestre di San Pasquale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church dedicated to Santa Maria, San Pasquale and Sant'Isidoro, patron saint of the peasants, probably built between the end of the seventeenth and early eighteenth centuries, perhaps on the remains of a more ancient church of Santa Maria: in 1699 it was very ruined, in 1714 it was well provided. From 1831 she was raised next to the cemetery, moved in 1944 to San Vittore. A painting behind the altar shows the original church and the ancient castle of Morsasco. Visitable during religious services.

Why go. A country church with a painting of the ancient castle.
