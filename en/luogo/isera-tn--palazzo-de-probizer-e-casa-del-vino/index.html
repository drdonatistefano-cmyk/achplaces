Palazzo de Probizer e Casa del Vino

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

18th century palace with mythological frescoes, seat of the Wine House; it houses art exhibitions, concerts and cultural events. Around, the hills of vineyards of Marzemino, to be seen at best between the end of September and the beginning of October, in harvest.

Why go. The Marzemino in his house, under frescoes of the eighteenth century.
