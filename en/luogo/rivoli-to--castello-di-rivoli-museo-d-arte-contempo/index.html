Castello di Rivoli – Museo d'Arte Contemporanea

Museums · Modern art · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Fortress, palace remained unfinished on the project of Juvarra, barracks and then museum: since 1984 the first museum of contemporary art of Italy with masterpieces of the Poor Art, the Long Channel and since 2019 the Cerruti Collection (Picasso, De Chirico, Kandinsky, Bacon). From the panoramic sport the view of Turin. UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Why go. The unfinished palace of contemporary art.
