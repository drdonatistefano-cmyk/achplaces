Laboratorio e Museo Foletto

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Laboratory behind the family pharmacy, active since 1900 as a small pharmaceutical workshop: injectable vials, fruit syrups and registered medicinal products. It exhibits alembics, pestles, mortars and antique instruments; pharmacists Foletto still produce syrups and liqueurs, including Picco Rosso, with tastings. Out of season only by appointment; free entry with the Garda Guest Card.

Why go. A pharmacy from the beginning of the 20th century and its syrups.

  • Where Via Vittoria 11, Pieve di Ledro
  • Entry €5, reduced €4, family €10
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info
