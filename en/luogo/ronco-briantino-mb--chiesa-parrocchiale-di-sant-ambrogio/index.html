Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Ambrogio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of the Diocese of Milan (decanated in Vimercate), built between 1924 and 1934, the year in which the frescoes and the interior flooring were executed; the presbytery had a structural intervention in 1974. The CEI card records in 1837 the church of Sant'Ambrogio in Nemus, today deconsecrated, and censisce inside the main altar and lateral altars, side chapels, frescoes, stained glass windows, statues, organ, source, tombstones and inscriptions; outside door, bell tower and parish church.

Why go. The village church, frescoed in the 1930s.
