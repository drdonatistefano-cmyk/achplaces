Torre di San Pietro

History and architecture · Towers

In the region of San Pietro, perhaps rest of a Romanesque bell tower; at the base are faded frescoes with the coat of arms of the De Contesa next to San Sebastiano, dated 1448 and attributed by the scholar Aldo Moretto to Domenico della Marca d'Ancona.

Why go. 15th century frescoes in a country tower.
