Parrocchiale di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the sixteenth century, already mentioned in the pastoral visit of Mgr. Regazzoni of 1577, with a single nave decorated in the last century by the ponzonese painter Ivaldi called "il Muto" . It has a valuable 17th century high altar and side altars with 17th century paintings, including those of San Bovo and the Holy Rosary of Monevi; in the ancient sacristy a seventeenth-century canvas attributed to Beccaria. The outdoor porch was wanted in the eighteenth century by the Delfini family. Visitable during religious services.

Why go. The altars and paintings of the seventeenth century next to the castle.
