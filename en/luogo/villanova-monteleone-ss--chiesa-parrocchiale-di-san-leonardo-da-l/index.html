Chiesa parrocchiale di San Leonardo da Limoges

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A 16th century church in Gothic-Aragonese style, rebuilt in neo-Gothic forms in 1789, which preserves two wooden altars of the eighteenth century; feast on 11 June. In the village also the seventeenth-century church of the Madonna del Rosario and the oratory of Santa Croce of the sixteenth century.

Why go. The Gothic-Aragonese mother church of the country.
