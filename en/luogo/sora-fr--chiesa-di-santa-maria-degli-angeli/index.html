Chiesa di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A single nave church annexed to the monastery of the Passionists in Collacchio district, once the seat of the Capuchins; the altarpiece of the high altar, ...Santa Maria degli Angeli, shows in the background Sora as it appeared at the beginning of the seventeenth century.

Why go. A portrait of Sora in the early seventeenth century.
