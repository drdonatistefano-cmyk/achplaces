Resti di Superaequum

History and architecture · Archaeology

Ruins of the Roman town hall of Superaequum in Macrano and Colle Caprelle: the excavations have returned houses with mosaic floors, paved streets and sacred buildings, including one dedicated to Hercules Winner with bronzes and votive inscriptions. In the territory also the fortified italic center of Colle Cipolla and the necropolis of Le Castagne.

Why go. The town of Peligna at the origin of the village.
