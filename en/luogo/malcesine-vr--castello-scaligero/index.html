Castello Scaligero

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Rocca probably Longobard, destroyed by the Franks in 590 and rebuilt, took its name from the Scaligeri who held it from 1277 to 1387; then passed to Visconti, Venice and Austria and was declared National Monument on August 22, 1902. The pentagonal mast is 31 meters high (70 meters on the lake) and the viewpoint is 24 meters on the water; it houses the Museum of Natural History of Baldo and Garda (multimedia route), the Hall of Galee on the navigation and transport of Venetian ships by land in the fifteenth century, and the Goethe Hall in the former Austrian powder keg, with bust of the poet in the garden. In summer the lawn of the Lacaòr serves as an outdoor theatre.

Why go. The castle on the lake with view from the mastium.
