Chiesa di San Silvestro Papa

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church of the historic centre, whose foundation dates back to 1246 for an inscription on a stone of the bell tower. It was banned in 1851 for its poor condition and was restored for about twenty years and reopened before 1870; damaged by a fire on August 16, 1891, it was reconsecrated on May 14, 1896. It has three naves with vaulted barrels lunetted in the central nave, stone portal with bezel and oculus, and a square bell tower in three orders with cusp.

Why go. The medieval parish church in the heart of the village.
