Museo del Cappello

Museums · Themed

Opened in 1991 to preserve the tradition of straw hat, in the former palace of Piazza Roma, documents all the phases of processing, from the collection to the sale to the pressing that gives the final shape, with photographs, panels and original machinery. The section "Marche," so much of hat "details" has hats reinterpreted by regional excellences in glass, ceramics and wood.

Why go. The history of the country of straw hats.
