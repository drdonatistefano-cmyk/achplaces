Chiesa e convento di San Francesco

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Former convent with a museum in the press and a large collection of ancient books, considered one of the most important archives in Italy; the church, known for its paintings, has wooden ceilings painted by hand with Baroque religious images.

Why go. Baroque ceilings and ancient books.
