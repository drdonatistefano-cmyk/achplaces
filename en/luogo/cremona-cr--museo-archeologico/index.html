Museo Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the former church of San Lorenzo of the 10th century, the finds of the city founded by the Romans in 218 BC and a domus of the 2nd century BC brought to light in the courtyards of Palazzo Zuccari.

Why go. The Roman Cremona.

  • Where Via San Lorenzo 4, Cremona
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
