TRIBU – Museo Ciusa

Museums · Modern art

Next to the cathedral, from a panoramic point you can access the Tribu space and the museum that exhibits the sculptures of Francesco Ciusa, first prize at the Venice Biennale of 1907, including the Mother of the killed.

Why go. The Mother of the Killed.
