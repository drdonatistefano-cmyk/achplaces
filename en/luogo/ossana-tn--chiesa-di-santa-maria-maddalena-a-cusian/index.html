Chiesa di Santa Maria Maddalena a Cusiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Documented from 1368 and rebuilt in the fifteenth century, it contains 16th century frescoes by the Baschenis brothers.

Why go. Baschenis frescoes in hamlet.
