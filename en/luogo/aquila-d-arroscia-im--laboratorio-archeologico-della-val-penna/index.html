Laboratorio archeologico della Val Pennavaire

Museums · Archaeological

The site of the territorial museum "I volte dell'Ubagu" of the Arroscia Valley, tells about 10,000 years of local history, from prehistory to today: from the nearby caves, called pre-Roman Ligurian name "arma" are stone instruments, ceramics of the Iron Age and remains of fireplaces. A plastic exhibition where the caves are accessible, reachable by a signposted path; educational room and guided tours. Tel. 0183 382057.

Why go. The prehistoric valley before climbing to the caves.
