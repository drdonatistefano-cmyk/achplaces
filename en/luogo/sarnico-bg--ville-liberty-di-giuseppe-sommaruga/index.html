Ville Liberty di Giuseppe Sommaruga

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

For the Faccanoni family the Milanese architect Giuseppe Sommaruga signed in Sarnico villa Pietro Faccanoni (1907, former spinning mill, irons by Alessandro Mazzucotelli and wood by Eugenio Quarti), villa Giuseppe Faccanoni (since 1907, with bas-reliefs by Ernesto Bazzaro and terrace garden open on the lake), villa Luigi Faccanoni today Villa Surre with high tower, the childhood nursery of 1912 and the mausoleum Faccanoni in the cemetery, with sculptures by Ambrogio Pirovano.
