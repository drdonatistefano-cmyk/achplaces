Antica città di Bithia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Phoenician-punic centre then Roman, whose remains were brought to light by a tide: a tophet, the ancient road to Nora and a necropolis with the temple of the god Bes, whose statue is at the National Archaeological Museum of Cagliari.

Why go. A Phoenician city on the sea of Chia.
