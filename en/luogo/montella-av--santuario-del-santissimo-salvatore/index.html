Santuario del Santissimo Salvatore

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

On Mount Sovero, at 954 meters, it dominates the valley; a chapel is attested already in the sixteenth century, while the current church was built from 1779 to 1783 after the pilgrimage that, according to tradition, ended the great drought of 1779. Among the customs of pilgrims, drink from the well of miracles and ring the big bell; the feast is celebrated from 1780 on the day of the Most Holy Trinity.

Why go. The sacred mountain of the Montallesi and the view of the valley.
