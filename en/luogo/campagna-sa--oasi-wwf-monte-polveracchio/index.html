Oasi WWF Monte Polveracchio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Established in 1988 on about 200 hectares in the territory of Campagna, managed by the WWF in agreement with the municipalities of Campagna and Senerchia, with observation points and equipment for picnics and excursions.
