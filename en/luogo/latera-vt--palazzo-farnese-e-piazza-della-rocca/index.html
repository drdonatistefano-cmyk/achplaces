Palazzo Farnese e Piazza della Rocca

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palazzo Ducale, residence of the Farnese family, who owned it until the extinction of the family in 1668; is part of the Latium Historical Houses Network and overlooks Piazza della Rocca, indicated among the things not to be missed.

Why go. The residence of the Farnese in the heart of the village.
