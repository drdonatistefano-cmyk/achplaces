Isola dei Cavoli

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches

The island of the protected marine area, in front of Capo Carbonara, which owes its name to the abundance of wild cabbages; in its backdrops the submerged statue of the Madonna del Naufrago (Virgin of the Sea) of Pinuccio Sciola, half every third Sunday of July of a procession of decorated boats.

Why go. Snorkeling on the submerged Madonna.
