Museo Angelo Versace

Museums · Old masters

Museo dell'Arciconfraternita di Maria Santissima del Monte Carmelo, in two sections: sacred art, with liturgical furnishings and vestments of Messinian and Neapolitan workshops of the 18th and 19th centuries, and archaeology, with finds from the Eneolithic to the Byzantine-Norman age.

Why go. Sacred silver and finds from prehistory to Normans.
