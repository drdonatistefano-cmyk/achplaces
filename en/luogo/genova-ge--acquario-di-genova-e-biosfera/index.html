Acquario di Genova e Biosfera

Museums · Science and university

Among Europe's major marine aquariums, opened in 1992 for Colombians: Mediterranean, Caribbean coral reefs, Antarctic waters with penguins and tactile tanks with rays; next to the Biosphere in glass and steel with a tropical forest.

Why go. Around the world under the seas.
