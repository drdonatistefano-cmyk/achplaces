Villa di Maser (Villa Barbaro)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage

Wanted by the brothers Daniele and Marcantonio Barbaro, commissioned between 1554 and 1560 and designed by Andrea Palladio, who inspired himself to the Roman temples for the facade; the central body is decorated by the fresco cycle of Paolo Veronese (1559-1561), while the stuccoes are attributed to Alessandro Vittoria. Behind the building there is the water lilies wanted by Marcantonio Barbaro; the side boats, intended for agricultural activities, end with two dovecotes decorated with sundials. The visit to the frescoes includes the six rooms painted by Veronese; the villa has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1996.

Why go. Palladio and Veronese together, in the most famous Venetian villa of Treviso.
