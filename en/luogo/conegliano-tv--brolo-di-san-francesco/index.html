Brolo di San Francesco

Nature and landscape · City parks · Viewpoint

The garden of the convent of San Francesco sul Colle, between the Calle Madonna della Snow and a stretch of the fourteenth-century walls: for over four centuries vineyard and vegetable garden of the friars, donated to the Municipality in 1986 and re-arranged by Italia Nostra since 1994 with vines, olive trees and fruit trees; from the vines you get a strawberry wine.

Why go. A garden between vineyards and medieval walls.
