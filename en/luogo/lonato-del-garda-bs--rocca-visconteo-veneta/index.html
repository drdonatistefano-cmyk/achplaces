Rocca visconteo-veneta

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress on the morainic hill used by the Visconti and the Venetians, divided into Rocchetta and Main Quarter reached by a drawbridge; in front of its walls Napoleon defeated the Austrians in 1796. National monument since 1912, belongs to the Ugo Da Como Foundation; the ticket includes the immersive room with 360° projections and the Ornithological Museum. Free visit every day 10-18 according to the Municipality.

Why go. From the walks and the mast you can see the low Garda and the plain, on clear days up to Baldo and the Apennines.
