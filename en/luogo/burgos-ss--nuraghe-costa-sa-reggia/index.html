Nuraghe Costa (Sa Reggia)

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The site of the most important Bronze Age of the area, of the fourteenth century BC, on a hill of 800 meters between oaks and holm oaks: pentalobed nuraghe of about 450 m2 with basalt mast of 14 meters of diameter and four towers, a village of about forty huts and an antemural about 70 meters long and tall 3, with walkway, almost intact. Parking is possible at the Scuderie Costa di Foresta Burgos and you can walk up for about 1 km.

Why go. A nuragic fortress with a wall almost intact.
