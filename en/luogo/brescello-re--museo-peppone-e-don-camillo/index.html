Museo Peppone e Don Camillo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Inaugurated on April 16, 1989 by volunteers from the country, it collects posters, anecdotes, photographs and original props of the films, including the Guzzi sidecar, Peppone's bike, Don Camillo's dress and bicycles, with reconstructions of some places.

Why go. The original props of the films.

  • Where Via Cavallotti 24, Brescello
  • Entry €6, reduced €4 (one ticket three museums)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com
