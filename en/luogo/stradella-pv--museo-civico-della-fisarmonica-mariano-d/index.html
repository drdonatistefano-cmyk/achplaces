Museo civico della Fisarmonica «Mariano Dallapè»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Established in 1997 and inaugurated on 20 May 1999 in the presence of Paolo Conte, it tells the accordion industry that made Stradella famous in the world: the origins of the instrument and the 44 factories active in the city since 1876, when Mariano Dallapè opened his first laboratory. Three sections in five halls (historical context, didactic part on the instrument's operation, street models in chronological order) with the first instrument built by Dallapè, the precious Organfisa \liturgica \"the organ sound and the reconstruction of a craft workshop. Free admission on the first Sunday of the month; reservation recommended also to individuals.

Why go. The history of the accordion in the city that made it famous.

  • Where Via Montebello 2 (Centro Culturale, Palazzo Garibaldi), Stradella
  • Entry €5, under 16 €2; with Naturalistic Museum €8
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.comune.stradella.pv.it
