Monticello Amiata e Casa museo

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

The highest center of the municipality, with a historical center to walk, marked paths between ancient chestnut groves (the Via della Castagna) and a panorama from Monte Amiata to the coast. In the old town hall the House museum dedicated to folk traditions, daily life and ancient crafts.

Why go. Amiata crafts in the chestnuts.
