Palazzo di Bonifacio VIII

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

Built at the beginning of the thirteenth century by the Counts and passed to the Caetans in 1297, with large frescoed rooms; here in 1303 the emissaries of Philip IV of France attempted to capture Pope Bonifacio VIII, the episode known as the "slap of Anagni" .

Why go. The palace of Anagni's slap.
