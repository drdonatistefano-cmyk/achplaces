Museo nazionale Jatta

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Rare private collection of the 19th century by the brothers Giovanni senior and Giulio Jatta, remained in the original layout, acquired by the State in 1990-1991: over 2,000 finds in four rooms on the ground floor of Palazzo Jatta, including about 700 vases with red figures and a masked crater of the fourth century BC attributed to the painter of Baltimore. In Room 4, the famous Talos vase (420-400 B.C. about), with the death of the giant guardian of Crete among the Dioscuri. Closed on Monday.

Why go. The Talos vase in an intact nineteenth-century museum.

  • Where Piazza G. Bovio 25, Ruvo di Puglia
  • Entry €6, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
