Palazzo Avogadro

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

In the historic center of Zanano, owned by the municipality, it is formed by a northern building older, with the medieval house-tower, and by a south modified in the seventies of the twentieth century. On the first floor, a fresco between the 14th and 15th centuries tells of a clash between Guelphs and Ghibellines in the Trompia Valley, with black bears armed against white bears; in the same room a Madonna enthroned with the Child and St. Martin bishop of the 15th century, and in another room a St. Jerome of 1474. It usually hosts the City's annual Art Prize.

Why go. The Guelph bears and Ghibellines frescoed in a medieval house-tower.
