Chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of the second half of the sixteenth century: it preserves an Annunciation on canvas of Baroque school, perhaps attributable to Antonio Viviani, and a Madonna with Child, Saint Peter martyr and Saint Sebastian.

Why go. Sacred canvases in the 16th century parish.
