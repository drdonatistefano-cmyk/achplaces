Foresta di Montimannu

Nature and landscape · Mountains

One of the largest state forests in Sardinia, about 4,600 hectares in the heart of the park of Monte Linas, Oridda and Marganai, populated by Sardinian deer and mouflon; it is reached along the lake of Leni and the pine forest equipped with Campus de Monti. The CAI trails start from the former Canale Serci mine (cassiterite, 1866-1946) and the Cantina Ferraris refuge: 113 leads to Piscina Irgas, 109 to the Muru Mannu waterfall, over 70 metres.

Why go. Trekking between Sardinian deer and Linas Falls.
