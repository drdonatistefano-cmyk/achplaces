Casone dei Bardi e Oratorio di San Niccolò

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The eighteenth-century Casone, formerly the residence of the Bardi Counts who moved the seat of power in the seventeenth century, is today the town hall; the adjoining oratory, of the early eighteenth century, has paintings of the eighteenth century, one of the last Traeri organs still working and a permanent exhibition on the Gothic Line.

Why go. The Bardi's palace with its historical organ.
