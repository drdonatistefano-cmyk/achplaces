Villa Durazzo Centurione e parco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Born as a fortress on a hill overlooking the city, it was rebuilt in 1678 by the Durazzo family as a summer residence on the model of the Genoese villas of the seventeenth century; since 1973 it is of the Municipality. Inside there are statues, frescoes, majolica floors and Genoese granite, Murano chandeliers, antique furniture, a painting gallery of Genoese painters from the 17th and 18th centuries (Piola, Borzone, Paggi, De Ferrari) and the reconstruction of the study by writer Vittorio G. Rossi; in front of the entrance a square in white and black stones. The park, of about three hectares overlooking the Gulf, brings together Agrumeto, Giardino all'italiana, romantic English wood (voluted in 1919 by Alfredo Chierichetti) and Secret Garden of the Princes Centurione, and since 2006 is part of the Great Italian Gardens; the entrance to the park is free. Ticket €3 from 6 to 12 years, €4 for groups of 15 people and members FAI and Touring (MiC card updated to 2020). Tel. 0185 293135.

Why go. The villa with the panoramic park on Tigullio.
