Museo degli antichi saperi

Museums · Themed

In the historic rooms of the palazzo baronale Picinni-Leopardi, born from an idea of Maurizio Vignati with the Municipality and the Palazzo Picinni-Leopardi Association thanks to the objects donated by citizens: work tools divided by sections (drawing, leather goods, tailoring, joinery, iron, kitchen) that tell who made and used them.

Why go. The crafts of the past, with objects donated by the country.
