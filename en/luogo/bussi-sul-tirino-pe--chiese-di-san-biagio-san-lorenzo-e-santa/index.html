Chiese di San Biagio, San Lorenzo e Santa Maria di Ponte Marmoreo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the village are the baroque church of San Biagio, of the sixteenth century, the church of San Lorenzo, of the fourteenth century, and the church of Santa Maria di Ponte Marmoreo, with 16th century frescoes inside.

Why go. Three churches for three centuries of country history.
