Castello Reale di Casotto (Valcasotto)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

At 1,090 meters in Val Casotto, the royal residence further south of the Sabaudo circuit, born on the remains of the Carthusian founded in 1172 and transformed by Carlo Alberto into hunting residence. Guided tours by reservation from spring to autumn. UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Why go. The palace in the woods.
