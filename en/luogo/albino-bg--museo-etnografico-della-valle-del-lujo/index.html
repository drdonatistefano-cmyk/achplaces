Museo etnografico della Valle del Lujo

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1995 in Casale, it documents the rural and artisan life of the valley: agricultural tools, pastoral and forestry, miners' trades, coalsmiths, blacksmiths, blacksmiths and shoemakers, the rural house and collections on chestnut and ancient varieties of apples and pears.

Why go. Chestnuts and trades in the Lujo Valley.
