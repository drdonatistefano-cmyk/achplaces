Museo civico etno-antropologico e archivio storico «Mario De Mauro»

Museums · City history

Called in a provocative way to a multifaceted figure lived in Scordia in the first half of the nineteenth century, it presents a material documentation of the history of the place and its inhabitants. Open from Monday to Friday 8.30-12.30, Wednesday also 14.30-19.30; closed Saturday and Sunday (tel. +39 095 7935921).

Why go. The material history of Scordia and his people.
