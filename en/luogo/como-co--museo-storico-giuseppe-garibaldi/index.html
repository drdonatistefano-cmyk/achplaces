Museo storico «Giuseppe Garibaldi»

Museums · City history

In Palazzo Olginati, dating back to the fifteenth century, donated to the Municipality by the last heir of the family, provided that a museum dedicated to Garibaldi, which stayed there in 1866; inaugurated in 1932, currently reported in temporary closing.
