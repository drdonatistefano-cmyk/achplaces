Museo multimediale della 500 «Dante Giacosa»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Born on the initiative of Fiat 500 Club Italia and inaugurated in July 2007, it tells the story of the 500 and its role in bringing Italy to modernity in the sixties. You enter through a magical tent: inside there are the reconstruction of an old workshop, showcases with models, components and accessories, touch-screen stations with documentaries and commercials, two driving simulators with the seats of two real half cars, some working cars, a bar in vintage style, library and themed emerotheque. MiC timetables: mon-ven 9-18, sat 9-12, sun closed; booking for groups. Tel. 0182 582282.

Why go. The myth of the 500 told by his fans.
