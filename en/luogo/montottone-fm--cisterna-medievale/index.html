Cisterna medievale

History and architecture · Archaeology

Built about the 12th century in front of the church of Santa Maria Ausiliatrice, it is about 15 meters deep, divided into two floors and measuring 6 x 6 meters; it collected water for periods of drought or siege. The two rooms can be visited thanks to routes along the edges, with audio guides in Italian and English.

Why go. Get off in a two-storey medieval cistern.
