Museo della civiltà contadina e artigiana della Calabria

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1983 on the initiative of Pro Loco after a collection of over 3,000 objects started in 1978, he received a mention at the European Museum of the Year Award. Six sections (farm art, textile fibers, terracotta, wood, iron, costumes) with a wooden wine press and a working 18th century lathe, a Calabrian frame, a 12th century host mold, a rebuilt forge and traditional costumes from different countries along the steps. It is located in the seventeenth-century Palazzo Aceti-Amoroso; the Parco delle Serre presents it in the Museum Polo La Filanda. Hours according to the municipality: Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday 9-12, Sunday 16-18.

Why go. The award-winning peasant museum in Europe.
