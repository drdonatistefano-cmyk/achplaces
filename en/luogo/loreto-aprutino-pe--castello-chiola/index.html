Castello Chiola

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Comital castle with stone cores among the oldest in the country and parts of the XV-XVI century, rebuilt in neoclassical style by architect Francesco Valentini in the second half of the nineteenth century.

Why go. The neoclassical castle on top of the village.
