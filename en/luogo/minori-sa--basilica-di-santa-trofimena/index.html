Basilica di Santa Trofimena

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage

The most famous of the thirty churches of the village, near the promenade: of origin XI-XII century and rebuilt in the nineteenth century, has a crypt rearranged in the eighteenth century with a coeva urn that guards the remains of the patroness. In the presence of the relics is linked the survival of the rites of Holy Week, with the processions of the "baptants" and the traditional songs.

Why go. The crypt with the relics of the patroness.
