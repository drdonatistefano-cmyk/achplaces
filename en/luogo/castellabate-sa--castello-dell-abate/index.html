Castello dell'Abate

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On top of the village, wanted by St. Costabile Gentilcore, IV abbot of the Badia di Cava, who laid the first stone on October 10, 1123 to defend population and navigation from Saracen raids: four round corner towers and walls that contained houses, warehouses, ovens and cisterns. In the seventeenth century he lost his defensive function and became a noble palace. The visit, about an hour long, includes the Antiquarium Museum, the stables, the Norman Room and the courtyard, the Mastio Tower, the Aragonese walkway and the Municipal Library.

Why go. The castle from which the village was born.
