Villa e parco Trissino Marzotto

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

In the park of the medieval castle of the Trissino, family of Lombard origin remodelled several times over the centuries, survive two villas: the Villa Trissino Paninsacco, with Italian gardens and eighteenth-century statues of the workshops of Marinali and Cassetti, and the Villa Trissino Da Porto, now Marzotto, completed in the eighteenth century on a project by Francesco Antonio Muttoni (with interventions by Dal Pozzo and Calderari). The interiors are visited by reservation; closed in August and during the Christmas period.

Why go. Villas and gardens with baroque statues on the hill of the Trissino.
