Convento e chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

A seventeenth-century account of the minor friars near the museum Peregrinatio Fidei: the cloister contains frescoes with scenes of hagiographics and the Via Crucis and today hosts cultural events.

Why go. A frescoed cloister outside the circuits.
