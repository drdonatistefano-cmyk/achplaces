Castello di Colcavagno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

First traces in the 10th century (the name Curcavagnus is attested in 1164), several times rebuilt; in 1667 the Marquis Flamino Giacinto Balbiano transformed it into a baroque brick villa. From 1902 to 2010 it was the retirement home of the nuns of the Cottolengo; from 2010 it is the seat of the Paolo Ferraris Foundation, a cultural center with exhibitions and events, with the Salone delle Feste and the museum project "The tools of memory" on the tools for the restoration of paper and parchment. In the hamlet there is also the Romanesque church of the Saints Vittore and Corona.

Why go. A baroque villa reborn as a cultural center.
