Centro di documentazione sulla lavorazione del ferro (Officine di Netro)

Museums · Themed

Cell of the Ecomuseum of Biellese opened in 1996 in the buildings of the former Rubino factory, founded in 1896 by Giovan Battista Rubino with his sons Ernesto and Colombo (now Bono Netro). It tells the story of the forge born in the seventeenth century in small workshops, the "boite" along the river Ingagna: tools produced by Rubino and samples, over a thousand drawings, an archive of documents from 1901 to 1964, technical standards for car and motorbike components and an exhibition of historical machinery. Guided tours, workshops and excursions by reservation; collaborate with Fucina Morino in Mongrando. Ecomuseum Contacts: +39 349 3269048.

Why go. Four centuries of wrought iron in a historic factory in the Elvo Valley.
