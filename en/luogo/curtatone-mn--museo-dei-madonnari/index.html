Museo dei Madonnari

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Set up at the Sanctuary, it exhibits reproductions and preparatory cartoons of the works realized in the various editions of the Meeting of Our Lady. Open Friday 15-18, Saturday and Sunday 10:30-12:30 and 15-18; other days by reservation (tel. The Commission shall adopt delegated acts in accordance with Article 3 of this Regulation.

Why go. The art of sacred street painters.
