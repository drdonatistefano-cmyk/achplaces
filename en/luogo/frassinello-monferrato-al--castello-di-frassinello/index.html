Castello di Frassinello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

At the top of the village, it is the most important building in the village: for centuries it was the noble residence of the Nemours family, then Sacchi-Nemors, counts of Frassinello, whose coat of arms is still in the floor of the salon of honor.

Why go. The castle of the counts of Frassinello at the top of the village.

the Municipality lists it among the accommodation facilities (B&B and venue of events): visitable only on special occasions
