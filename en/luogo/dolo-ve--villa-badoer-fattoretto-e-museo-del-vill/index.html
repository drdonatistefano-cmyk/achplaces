Villa Badoer Fattoretto e Museo del Villano

Museums · Themed

An eighteenth-century villa surrounded by a park on the right bank of the Naviglio del Brenta, in Sambruson, where it also produces Venetian wine in bottle. In eight rooms, for about 1,000 square meters, the Museo del Villano collects about 20,000 objects of peasant life (equipments, instruments, documents) gathered by the founder Luigino Fattoretto, of sharecropping origins; the hall Â of edicts Â exhibits customs letters and notices of the Riviera del Brenta, also of Napoleonic era, next to clocks, musical instruments, carriages and calessi.

Why go. The memory of rural civilization in a villa on the Riviera.
