Grotta di Curtomartino

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Karst cavities outside the town, known for the rare plants and orchids growing at the entrance and for stalactites and ancient stalagmites, testimony of the distant geological past of the area.

Why go. Karst nature and orchids a stone's throw from the village.
