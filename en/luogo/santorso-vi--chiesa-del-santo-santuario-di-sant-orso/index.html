Chiesa del Santo (Santuario di Sant'Orso)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Sanctuary dedicated to Sant'Orso, patron saint of the village, and to the Madonna del Monte Summano, is the building of most architectural interest among the churches of Santorso. Designed by Ottone Calderari in 1777 and built in the early nineteenth century in neoclassical style, it houses interior decorations and paintings by Alessandro and Gian Battista Maganza.

Why go. The neoclassical of Calderari and the paintings of the Maganza.
