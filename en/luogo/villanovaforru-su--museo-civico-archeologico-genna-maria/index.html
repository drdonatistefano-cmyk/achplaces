Museo civico archeologico «Genna Maria»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In an elegant 19th-century building already a granite mountain: on the ground floor the finds of the village of Genna Maria (pots, stone and metal instruments) exposed according to the environments of origin, on the upper floor materials of the Marmilla from Neolithic to the Byzantine age; the path arrives at the cult of Demeter and Core. Open Tuesday-Sunday 9:30-13 and 15:30-18 (April-September until 19:00).

Why go. The finds of Genna Maria and Marmilla.

  • Where Piazza Costituzione, Villanovaforru
  • Entry €6, reduced €4; cumulative with park €10, reduced €7
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
