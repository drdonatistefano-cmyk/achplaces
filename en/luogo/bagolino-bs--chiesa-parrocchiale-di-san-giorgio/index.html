Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

This cathedral in the mountains, designed by Giovan Battista Lantana, was the largest monumental church in Bresciano in the 17th century. Frescoes from 1647-1652 with squares by Tommaso Sandrini and Ottavio Viviani and figures by Palma il Giovane, Camillo Rama and Lucchese; seventeenth-century wooden altars by Giacomo Faustini, Baldassarre Vecchi and Pietro Bonomi; paintings attributed to Tiziano, Torbido, Barbelli, Celesti and others.

Why go. A baroque cathedral in a mountain country.
