Museo di storia naturale

Museums · Science and university

Set up by the Oppelide Foundation in the 14th-century palace opposite the Fortress, it exhibits fossils and minerals that illustrate the evolution of life through geological ages, coming from the Elce valley and from various countries even outside Europe; many specimens are rare or unique.

Why go. Fossils and rare minerals in a 14th century palace.
