Cascata Campanaro

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Paid entry · Viewpoint

One of the most famous and accessible waterfalls of the Valli Cupe, with a jump of 22 meters, reached by a path excavated naturally in the rock between red algae and ancient ferns; the route is 300 meters (about 1 hour round trip, tourist difficulty). The name, according to oral sources, would recall the bell tower of the nearby monastery of Santa Maria di Acquaviva.

Why go. The easiest waterfall to reach.

  • Where Valli Cupe, Sersale
  • Entry €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
