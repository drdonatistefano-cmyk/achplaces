Museo Etnoantropologico (ex convento del Carmine)

Museums · Themed

The museum of peasant civilization occupies the small convent of 1664 next to the recently restored Carmelo church; from here also starts the guided tour to Monte San Paolino.

Why go. The peasant life of a village inland.
