Chiesa del Gesiùn (San Pietro di Sugliaco)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ruins of a small Romanesque church, dated between the end of the 10th and the half of the 11th century, rectory of the lost village of Livion cited in 1209: single nave of 4.62 x 3.80 m, three arches on two columns towards the presbytery, a bell tower almost to tibury over the presbytery and a fragment of fresco with a hand holding a book, perhaps a St. Peter. The name means "chiesona" (church), it is located isolated at the intersection of two country roads between the vineyards, along the Via Francigena.

Why go. The solitary Romanesque ruin in the vineyards.
