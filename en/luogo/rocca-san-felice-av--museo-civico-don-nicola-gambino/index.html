Museo civico «Don Nicola Gambino»

Museums · Archaeological

In the middle of the medieval village, with the remains of the Donjon in the background, exhibits archaeological finds: metal objects (knifes, nails, horseshoes, tools), glass, worked bone, fusaiole, pipe, a base of limestone fireplace with anthropomorphic figure, stone bullets, coins and about 150 ceramics, including glazed XII-XIII and protomaiolic of the XIII-XIV. Contacts: tel. 0827 45031.

Why go. Daily life in the medieval castle through the finds.
