Museo della civiltà contadina di Sant'Anna di Boccafossa

Museums · Themed

Founded by a group of volunteers to document the origins of the community, linked to the reclamation, it collects about 2,800 pieces of tools, equipment, machines and tools for earth processing, with a photographic archive; the route also shows how the peasants adapted and invented tools. Every year in July the harvest festival with agricultural machinery exposed at the entrance. Visits on request, with audio guides and guided tours.

Why go. The story of the reclamation told by the tools of those who made it.
