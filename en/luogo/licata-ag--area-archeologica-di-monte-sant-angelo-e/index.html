Area archeologica di Monte Sant'Angelo e Castel Sant'Angelo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the large promontory above the city you can visit the excavations of ancient Finziade, with houses of Hellenistic age, and the castle born as a Aragonese signal tower, from which you can enjoy the view of the coast. Open Monday-Saturday 9-13, Sunday and holidays closed.

Why go. The Hellenistic city and the panorama of the coast.

  • Where Monte Sant'Angelo, Licata
  • Entry €6, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
