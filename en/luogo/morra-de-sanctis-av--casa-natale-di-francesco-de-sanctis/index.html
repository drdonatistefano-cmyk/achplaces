Casa natale di Francesco De Sanctis

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

In the historic centre, Palazzo De Sanctis, with its typical stone portal, is the house where Francesco De Sanctis was born on 28 March 1817, a critic and historian of literature. A Museum of Memories was also set up in the context of the Literary Park "Francesco De Sanctis" (1999-2001) with a small collection of its memorabilia.

Why go. On the trail of the father of Italian literary critics.
