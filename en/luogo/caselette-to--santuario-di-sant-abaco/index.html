Santuario di Sant'Abaco

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Originally a hermitic chapel, it was enlarged in the second half of the nineteenth century at the behest of Count Cays, friend of Don Bosco. It rises at the foot of the Musinè, about 30 minutes walk from Cays Square along a paved road with the pylons of the Way of the Cross; every year in January there is a pilgrimage.

Why go. The sanctuary at the foot of the Musinè.
