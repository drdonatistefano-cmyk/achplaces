Ca' Maitino, Casa dei ricordi di Papa Giovanni XXIII

Museums · House museums

15th-century residence built for Maytinus Roncalli, ancestor of the pope, who chose it as summer residence until shortly before the election; on the façade a sundial with the motto "Errantes revocotat" and inside fifteenth-century frescoes with the coat of arms of the Maytini, then became papal coat of arms. Today it is a museum with relics: part of the library, gifts, the typewriter and the world map of the papal study, the bed in which he died and the altar of the private chapel, works of Manzù, Scorzelli, Bodini, Manfredi and German Nani and an Opel Kapitan of 1956. Open on Saturday and Sunday, 9-12 and 14-18.

Why go. The real home of Pope John, with the objects of his study in the Vatican.
