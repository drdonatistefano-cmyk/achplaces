Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded in 1267 and renovated in the eighteenth century, it houses the funeral monument of Ludovico d'Artus (dead in 1370), the majolica floor of the Massa brothers, the ceiling with stories of the Old Testament and an Annunciation (1702) by Tommaso Giaquinto, a fresco of the fifteenth-century Madonna del latte and a reed organ.

Why go. Maiolica dei Massa and Giaquinto's paintings.
