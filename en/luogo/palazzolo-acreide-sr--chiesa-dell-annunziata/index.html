Chiesa dell'Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Rebuilt after 1693, with richly ornate portal, four-column façade, three naves and a precious marble altar; it housed the Annunciation of Antonello da Messina (1474), today at the Museo Bellomo in Syracuse.

Why go. The richest portal in the city.
