Museo dell'ossidiana

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Founded in 1999 at the entrance of the country, it is the only monothematic museum in Europe dedicated to obsidian: multisensory path with cartography, interactive audiovisual and sounds of the workmanship, works by sculptor Karmine Piras and brothers Atzori, minerals and fossils. It tells more than three thousand years of production, from the recent Neolithic to the Bronze Age, and from here start the guided excursions in the Park of Ossidiana; cumulative ticket with guide, excursion and laboratory €16. Open from Wednesday to Sunday.

Why go. The black glass of the prehistoric, from the quarry to the sculpture.

  • Where Via San Giorgio 8, Pau
  • Entry €5, reduced €4; with guide €6
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.museossidiana.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
