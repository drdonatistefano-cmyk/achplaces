MUBA Museo della Bonifica

Museums · City history · Paid entry

It is located in the restored historical mill and tells about the reclamation of the territory, the rural civilization, the electrification of Sardinia and urban history; it preserves the Historical Civic Archives. The historical section presents architecture and architects of the city with vintage films and a touch table with about 800 historical images; the archaeological section exhibits the materials of the nuragic sacred well of Orri and, in the Antiquarium, more than 300 finds of the necropolis of S'Ungroni from the Punic to the Byzantine age. Open Saturday 14:30-19:30, Sunday 8:30-13:30 and Monday 14:00-19:00.

Why go. To understand how a swamp has become a city, from the Punics to the reclamation.
