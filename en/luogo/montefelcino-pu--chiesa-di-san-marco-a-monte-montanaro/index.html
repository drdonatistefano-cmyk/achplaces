Chiesa di San Marco a Monte Montanaro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Monte Montanaro, it preserves a large altarpiece by Gianfrancesco Guerrieri from Fossombrone with the Madonna and Child and Saints Francis, Marco, Biagio and Lorenzo.

Why go. The warrior's altarpiece in a hamlet church.
