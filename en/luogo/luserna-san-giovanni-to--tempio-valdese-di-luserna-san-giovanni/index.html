Tempio valdese di Luserna San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Temple of the Waldensian community, also called the Bellonatti Church: the project was approved by the Napoleonic government on April 9, 1806 and the community built it with voluntary work and own contributions, completing it thanks to the gifts of foreign friends. It rises in the hamlet of San Giovanni, in front of the Catholic church of San Giovanni Battista. Visits by reservation or on Sunday morning during worship.

Why go. The Waldensian temple was born in Napoleonic age.
