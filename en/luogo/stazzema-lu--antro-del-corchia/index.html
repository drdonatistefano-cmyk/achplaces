Antro del Corchia

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry

A karst system of 53 km of tunnels and wells, indicated as the largest in Italy and among the largest in Europe, with stalactites and stalagmites. The guided group tour lasts about two hours on about 2,000 meters of steel footbridge, plus an artificial tunnel of 170 meters; inside the temperature is 7 °C. The ticket includes the shuttle from Levigliani (581 m) at the entrance (850 m).

Why go. The largest karst complex in Italy.

  • Where Via della Chiesa, Levigliani (biglietteria)
  • Entry €18, children 5-14 years €10, reduced €16; combined Corchia Park Experience €22
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.corchiapark.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
