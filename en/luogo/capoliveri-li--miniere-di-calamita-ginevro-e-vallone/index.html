Miniere di Calamita (Ginevro e Vallone)

History and architecture · Archaeology

On Monte Calamita, in the Tuscan Archipelago National Park, the mining activity lasted over 3000 years and was suspended in 1981. The mines of the Ginevro and Vallone, the oldest, are the only underground of the island, with about 7 km of tunnels, and are indicated as the largest magnetite deposit in Europe. They are visited with guided tours.

Why go. A trip to the iron tunnels that made the history of Elba.
