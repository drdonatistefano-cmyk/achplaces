Castello dei De Capua e Torre

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Castle of probable Longobard origin, modified by the De Capua from the thirteenth century and transformed between 1510 and 1515 by Bartolomeo III in fortress with palace: the portal bears the date 1515 and the Latin motto "If guest, login. Flee, if enemy The cylindrical tower is almost 20 meters high, with crowning of stone bushes, three inner rooms, iron staircase and cistern dug into the rock, on a cliff near the stream Succida that dominates the valley of Fortore.

Why go. The tower symbol of Riccia overlooking the valley.
