Forte della Consolata

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Made in 1590 by Carlo Emanuele di Savoia to engineer Gabrio Busco, he was shot down after the armistice of Cherasco in 1796. The area can be visited with guided tours organized by the Tourist Office of Demonte.

Why go. The remains of the Sabauda fortress that barred the Stura Valley.
