Museo archeologico e della Via Flaminia

Museums · Archaeological

Housed in the Palazzo del Podestà, part of the Palazzo Pubblico, it contains numerous archaeological finds of the city and the territory from prehistory to the Middle Ages; the museum is connected to the lapidary in the vestibule of the palace, restored in 2007.

Why go. The story of Cagli and Flaminia from prehistory to the Middle Ages.
