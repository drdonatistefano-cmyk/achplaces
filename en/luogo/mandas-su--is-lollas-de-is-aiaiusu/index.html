Is Lollas de is Aiaiusu

Museums · Themed

Ethnographic house-museum, the rooms of the grandparents, in a manor house of the late eighteenth century a few steps from the medieval complex of Sant'Antonio: it reproduces the peasant house with the loom room, the bedroom, sa lolla (the goddess), the kitchen, the tool room, the wine warehouse and the oven room, and collects weaving, basketry, copper and wrought iron artefacts, still living crafts in the village.

Why go. The farmhouse of Trexenta, \"granary of Rome" .
