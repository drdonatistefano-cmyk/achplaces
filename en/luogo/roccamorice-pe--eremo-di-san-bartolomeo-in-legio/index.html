Eremo di San Bartolomeo in Legio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

At about 610 meters below a large rock roof in the Vallone di Santo Spirito, documented from 1275 and rebuilt by Fr.Pietro da Morrone, inhabited still in 1320: the oratory is almost all carved into the rock, with the Holy Staircase on the terrace, two frescoed squares of the thirteenth century on the façade (Madonna with Child and Christ blessing) and, on the sixteenth century altar, the wooden statue of St. Bartholomew; under a stone flows the water considered miraculous. You can walk from the parking lot of Macchie di Coco with the path

Why go. A rock oratory reached through a tunnel in the rock.
