Chiesa Matrice di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the thirteenth century dedicated to the Assumption, already cathedral, in the heart of the historical center beyond the Arch of Marchesale, on the same square of the Palazzo dell'Orologio.

Why go. The ancient cathedral of the village.
