Castello di San Pelagio - Museo dell'Aria e dello Spazio

Museums · Themed

Castle of medieval origin (around 1340, as shown by the watchtower of Carrara), also called Villa Zaborra from the counts that adapted it to villa in 1775, museum since 1980; during the restorations a Roman spa has emerged. The museum tells the story of the flight from Leonardo's drawings to space exploration with over 300 models of planes, airships and balloons, original uniforms, engines and relics, including the Lindbergh plane for the Atlantic crossing and, in the cellars, the space section with models of the LEM and Space Shuttle Columbia, the Solar Sailing of the University of Padua and the images of the Giotto probe. On the noble floor, the section dedicated to D'Annunzio, which from here took off on 9 August 1918 with the squadron "La Serenissima" for the flight to Vienna: the frescoed rooms where he lived for a year keep original furniture, uniforms and personal objects.

Why go. A unique aeronautical collection in Europe, in the castle of the flight on Vienna.
