Spiaggia di Porto Levante e Sacca Cavallari

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

In front of Porto Levante there is the Sacca Cavallari, separated from the Adriatic by a scano called Porto Levante beach; in summer a ferry connects the port to the beach.

Why go. A scano beach reachable by ferry.
