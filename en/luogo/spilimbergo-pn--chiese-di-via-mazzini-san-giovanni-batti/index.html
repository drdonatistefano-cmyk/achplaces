Chiese di via Mazzini: San Giovanni Battista e San Giuseppe e Pantaleone

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In via Mazzini there are the church of San Giovanni Battista, a 14th century building with a frescoed vault in the 18th century, and the church of San Giuseppe and San Pantaleone, which preserves a wooden choir from the late 15th century.

Why go. Two churches within walking distance, between the fourteenth and eighteenth centuries.
