Casa Museo Cirillo Grott

Museums · House museums

In the ancient church of Guardia, then atelier of the painter, sculptor and poet Cyril Grott, born in the village: collection-exhibition of his paintings and sculptures, with the public section on the ground floor.

Why go. The artist's studio of the painted country.
