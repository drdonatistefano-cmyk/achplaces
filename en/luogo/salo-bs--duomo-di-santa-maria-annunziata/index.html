Duomo di Santa Maria Annunziata

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Late-Gothic, designed by Filippo delle Vacche with first stone laid on October 7, 1453 on the site of a pagan temple and the parish church of Santa Maria. It preserves the polyptych of Paolo Veneziano, two paintings by Romanino, works by Moretto, Zenone Veronese, Palma il Giovane, Andrea Celesti and Angelo Landi, the chapel of the Most Holy decorated by Malosso and an ancient wooden crucifix by Giovanni Teutonico. In July, the concerts of the Summer Musicale are held on the churchyard.

Why go. Moretto, Romanino and Paolo Veneziano by the lake.
