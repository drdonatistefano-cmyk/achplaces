Santuario prenuragico di Monte d'Accoddi

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Sacred pyramid building with ramp, unique in Sardinia and the Mediterranean, with village and menhir: phases from the first half of the fourth millennium BC.

Why go. A five-thousand-year-old high-rise altar.
