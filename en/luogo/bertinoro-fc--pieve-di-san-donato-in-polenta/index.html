Pieve di San Donato in Polenta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

The Romanesque parish church of the 10th century, about 5 km from the village, praised by Giosuè Carducci and linked to the memory of Dante.

Why go. The parish church sang by Carducci.
