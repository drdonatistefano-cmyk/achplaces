Salsiccia di Palazzolo e cucina iblea

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Palazzolo's sausage, of lean minced pork meat or cubes with spices, chilli and red wine in natural gut; the cavated with pork sauce, ricotta ravioli and caponata.

Why go. Red wine sausage.
