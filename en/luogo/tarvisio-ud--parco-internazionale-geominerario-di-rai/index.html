Parco internazionale geominerario di Raibl

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

The lead and zinc mine of Raibl-Cave del Predil, for centuries among the most important in Europe, closed in 1991, had 120 km of tunnels on 19 levels up to 520 m of depth. You can visit only with a guide (about an hour), on foot or with an electric train; the Exhibition of the mining tradition tells the country, the work of miners and the closure. A cumulative ticket with the military historical museum.

Why go. Get down into the galleries of a historic mine.
