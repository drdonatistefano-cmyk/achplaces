Castello Pallotta

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Castle on a hill with origins in the early Middle Ages, gods from Varano from 1240 to 1434, then Maurizi and finally Pallotta, still owners; at the end of the sixteenth century Cardinal Evangelista Pallotta transformed it into summer residence and hosted Clement VIII in 1598 (and then Cristina of Sweden in 1666). You enter from Porta Camerte and a drawbridge; the museum has weapons from the 13th to the 17th century, carriages from the 19th to the 19th century, salt with antique furnishings, ceramics from the Marche and a frieze attributed to Simone De Magistris, and in the park a pine planted in 1598.

Why go. The castle-residence of Cardinals Pallotta.

Temporarily closed to the public for restoration works after the earthquake of 2016
