Palazzo Santa Margherita – FMAV

Museums · Modern art

The building houses the Delfini Library and the Modena Arti Visive Foundation, which manages the Civic Gallery founded in 1959, with over 4,000 works on paper from the twentieth century and the collection of photography started with the Franco Fontana fund.

Why go. Contemporary design and photography in the center.
