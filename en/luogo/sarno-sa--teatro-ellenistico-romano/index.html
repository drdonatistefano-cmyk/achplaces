Teatro ellenistico-romano

History and architecture · Archaeology

Theatre of the first century BC, transformed into Augustian age with a proscenium in masonry decorated with paintings; the remains of the cavea emerged in the excavations of the sixties of the twentieth century. The first row of seats for the authorities is in tuff blocks, flanked by a sphinx and a winged lion.

Why go. The prohedria with sphinx and winged lion.
