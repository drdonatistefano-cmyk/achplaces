Museo archeologico cadorino

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

On the second floor of the Palazzo della Magnifica Comunità, founded in 1880 and set up in 1984: pre-Roman and Roman finds of the sanctuary of Lagole, dedicated to the deity Trumusiate then identified with Apollo, the necropolis of Domegge and the crest of Valle di Cadore. In the atrium a black and white geometric mosaic from the end of the first century A.D. from a Roman domus.

Why go. The ancient origins of Cadore and the Venetian language.
