Chiesa parrocchiale di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the historic centre, one of the most important works is a sixteenth-century altarpiece by Giovan Battista Moroni and works by Fantoni, including the wooden choir and the main altar.

Why go. The Moroni's shovel and the Fantoni's woods.
