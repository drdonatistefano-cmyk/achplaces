Museo Cabriniano

Museums · House museums

In the house where Saint Francesca Cabrini lived (1850-1917), born in the neighboring Sant'Angelo Lodigiano and foundress in Codogno of the Missionaries of the Sacred Heart of Jesus: objects, garments and memories of the saint and of the origins of the Institute, with the cells of the sisters visited. In the church of Tabor the relic of the heart of the saint.

Why go. In the house of the patron saint of immigrants.
