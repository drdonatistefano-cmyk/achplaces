Museo del Giocattolo e del Bambino

Museums · Themed

It offers a historical perspective on the buzzards of the past and the social value of the game in the last three centuries, with two paths: one historical, from handcrafted to romantic toy to golden age, the period between the two wars to toys of the 1950s; one thematic, on toys for girls, games of war, circus and theatre and educational toys of fun science. Open Sundays and holidays 14.30-19; groups and schools by reservation in other days.

Why go. Three centuries of toys, from handcrafted stumps to the 1950s.
