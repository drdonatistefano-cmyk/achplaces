Porto di Solanto e castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Continuing along the coast you reach the ancient port of Solunto, with the fortress of the 12th century and the Gothic castle of the Princes of San Vincenzo, which has performed the function of trap and loader.

Why go. Rock and castle on the sea.
