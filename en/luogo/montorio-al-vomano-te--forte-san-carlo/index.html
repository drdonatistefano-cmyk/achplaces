Forte San Carlo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the top of the hill, it was started in 1686 by the Spanish viceroy of Naples and the troops engaged in the fight against the brigandage were lodged there.

Why go. The Spanish fortress over the country.
