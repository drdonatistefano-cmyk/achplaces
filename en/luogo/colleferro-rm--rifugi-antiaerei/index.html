Rifugi antiaerei

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

About 6 km of galleries 3-4 meters high and 3 wide, with 15 entrances, born as pozzolana quarries to build the first houses of the BPD workers. From November 1943 to June 1944 more than 3,000 people were accommodated, with cellar, infirmary, registry, chapel, common rooms and niches for families; restored since 1985, there is also a living crib.

Why go. The underground city of World War II.

open for the patronal feast of December 4, on the occasion of events or by reservation
