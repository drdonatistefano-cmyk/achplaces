Badia di San Pietro

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Longobard Abbey founded in the VII century, preserves the original Romanesque plant with apse and facade; it is located along the Via Francigena, whose stage 26 arrives from Massa to Camaiore and the 27 continues to Lucca.

Why go. A Longobard Abbey on the Via Francigena.
