Museo civico di Palazzo Elti

Museums · Old masters

In the fifteenth century Palazzo Elti rebuilt after the earthquake: paintings and sculptures by Friulian and Venetian artists, including Cima da Conegliano, Pomponio Amalteo and Pellegrino da San Daniele, works of the seventeenth century and of hegemonic painters; open on Sundays.

Why go. Friulian art in a reconstructed building.
