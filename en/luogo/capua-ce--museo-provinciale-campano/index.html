Museo Provinciale Campano

Museums · Archaeological

Founded in 1870 in the fifteenth-century Palazzo Antignano of Catalan taste: the Matres Matutae, votive statues in tuff from the VII to the first century BC, vases from the Iron Age to the Attic Ceramics, terracotta, bronzes, coins, epigraphs, mosaics from Sant'Angelo in Formis and Nola, the federician room with the remains of the towers of Frederick II and the Bourbon, Liani and Savoy rooms.

Why go. The Mothers of Capua.
