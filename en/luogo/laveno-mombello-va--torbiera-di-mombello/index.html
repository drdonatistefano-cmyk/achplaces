Torbiera di Mombello

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Wet zone in a circle of morainic hills, born in the Quaternary from an ice lake then become swamp and filled by vegetation transformed into peat; at the edges typhus, reeds and horseback rides. On its banks stood a Neolithic village of stilts, whose traces were discovered in the nineteenth century. Managed with the Montana Community of Valcuvia, it has protected observation points and educational panels.

Why go. Nature and prehistory in an ancient glacial peat bog.
