CaMus - Museo Camuno

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated in 1923 thanks to Don Romolo Putelli, it occupies two floors of the fifteenth-century Palazzo Federici (Palazzo della Cultura) with ten rooms and a loggia: it is the only art gallery of Valcamonica, with works by Giacomo Ceruti called the Pytocchetto, Romanino, Callisto Piazza and Franca Ghitti, carvings by the Fantons, coats of arms, furniture and artefacts from Prehistory to Roman times. Open Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday 9-12 and 15-18, Wednesday and Friday 9-12, Sunday 15-18.

Why go. The gallery of the Camonica Valley.
