Castello di Lauria ed Enoteca regionale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

12th century castle on a sandstone spur, main defense of the village, connected by tunnels to the Castidduzzu and the apse of St. Peter; in 1283 was granted in feud to Ruggero di Lauria. Today it hosts the Regional Enoteca for Eastern Sicily.

Why go. The fortress of the village and the wines of Etna.
