Museo di arte sacra di Force (Palazzo Canestrari)

Museums · Old masters

The historic Palazzo Canestrari houses the museum, which preserves artistic artifacts from the churches of the territory, in particular works by De Magistris. It is part of the Sistine Museums of Piceno.

Why go. The sacred art of the churches of Force, with the works of the De Magistris.
