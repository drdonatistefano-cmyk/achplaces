Parco giardino Tenuta di Sant'Apollonio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry

Over 70,000 m2 owned by the Fondazione Senza Frontiere, born in 1973 from the plantations of Anselmo Castelli and designed by Gianfranco Paghera imitating nature: forest of native species of the Po Valley, wetland with fauna, orchard of ancient varieties, rose garden and medicinal garden, with educational trail. The Ethnographic Museum documents the Kanaka del Sepik (Papua New Guinea) and the Krahô del Tocantins (Amazon Brazilian) with masks, musical instruments and tools. Open from April to October, visits by reservation.

Why go. A plain garden park with a museum of distant peoples.
