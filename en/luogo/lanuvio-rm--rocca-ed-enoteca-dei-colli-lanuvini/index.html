Rocca ed Enoteca dei Colli Lanuvini

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

The 14th-century Fortress houses the Enoteca del Consorzio dei Vini Colli Lanuvini, the best place to taste the wines of the area, a small museum of rural traditions and the watchtower, symbol of the village, with panoramic views.

Why go. Tasting the Colli Lanuvini in the fortress.
