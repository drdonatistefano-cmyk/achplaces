Cappella e pozzo di San Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A small single nave chapel in Palazzo Tondi-Vignola, built in 1789 where a well was the destination of those who had been bitten by poisonous animals. Until a few decades ago, at the dawn of June 29, the feast of Saints Peter and Paul, arrived there the "tarantate" to ask for healing and drink the water of the well: a ritual linked to the origins of the pinch. On the altar, after a long restoration, the canvas of Saint Paul by Francesco Saverio Lillo is back. Info: IAT Galatina, tel. +39 0836 569984.

Why go. The symbol of Salento tarantism.
