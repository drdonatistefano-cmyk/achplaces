Casa natale di padre David Maria Turoldo

Museums · House museums

The headquarters of the David Maria Turoldo Study Centre, founded in 2019 by an association founded in 1992, which takes care of its restoration and guided tours; it is an example of a Friulian rural house with courtyard, porch, kitchen on the ground floor, two bedrooms on the first floor and attic with conference room and small library, with walls in cobbles of the Tagliamento and fragments of terracotta. The two rooms of the Turoldo family retain the original structure. Open Saturday and Sunday 9-18, only by reservation by email.

Why go. The peasant house in which the friar poet was born.
