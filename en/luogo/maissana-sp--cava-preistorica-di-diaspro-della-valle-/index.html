Cava preistorica di diaspro della Valle Lagorara

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Open-air jasper mine discovered in 1987 and excavated by the Superintendence between 1988 and 1995, exploited between the 4th and 2nd millennium B.C. (Radiocarbon indicates about 2920-1890 B.C.): the quarryers were made semi-finished for tools in splintered stone, especially arrowheads. The archaeological and naturalistic path shows the extraction surfaces as they were left, two quarry areas and two shelters with splinters, percussors and fragments of ceramic. Tel. 0187 845617.

Why go. A mine from 5,000 years ago.

visit only by appointment with reservation required according to the MiC
