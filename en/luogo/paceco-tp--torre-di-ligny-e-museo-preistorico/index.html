Torre di Ligny e Museo preistorico

Museums · Archaeological

In the Tower of Ligny, built in 1671 at the behest of Viceroy Claudio La Moraldo, Prince of Ligny: on the ground floor prehistoric and protohistoric finds of the province of Trapani, on the first floor over one hundred objects recovered in the Mediterranean sea bottom, between amphorae, anchors and objects of Greek, Roman and Punic use, with a bronze helmet of the IV-III century BC fished off Trapani. From the terrace the view ranges from Capo San Vito to Marsala and Mozia, with Trapani, Erice and the Egadi.

Why go. Marine archaeology and views of the Egadi.

The MiC card (updated to March 2021) is closed every day; tel. 0923 881991
