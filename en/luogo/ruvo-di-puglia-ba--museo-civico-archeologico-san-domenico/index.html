Museo civico archeologico San Domenico

Museums · Archaeological

On the first floor of the former Dominican monastery outside the walls, in front of Porta Noè, built around 1560: left by the Dominicans in 1809, it was then school of the Scolopi since 1819 and home to various schools after 1866.

Why go. The 16th-century convent outside the door.
