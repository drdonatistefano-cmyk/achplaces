Pieve di Santa Maria a Cisano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque parish church in the hamlet of Cisano, well visible from the Gardesana road. It is one of the historical sites included in the virtual tour of the municipality.

Why go. A Romanesque church overlooking the Gardesana.
