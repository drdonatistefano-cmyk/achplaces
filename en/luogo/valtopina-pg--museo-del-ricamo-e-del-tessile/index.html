Museo del Ricamo e del Tessile

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 2007 on the ground floor of the town hall, it was born from donations collected since 2000, stimulated by the School of Embroidery of Pro Loco: objects of historical families of Foligno, Spello and Perugia and, later, Tuscan and Ligurian artifacts, including a collection arrived thanks to contacts with the Museo del Merletto di Rapallo. About 400 pieces between women's fashion of the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, underwear and accessories, household linen with the "learned" ones, the samples on which the girls showed the techniques learned, and child clothing. According to the page of the Municipality (updated in 2020) is open Wednesday and Saturday afternoon (15.30-18 in winter, 16-18.30 in summer) and from Tuesday to Friday 10-12 on request; tel. 0742 74625.

Why go. The tradition of Umbrian embroidery in four hundred pieces.
