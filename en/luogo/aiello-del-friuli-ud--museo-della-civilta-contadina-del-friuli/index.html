Museo della civiltà contadina del Friuli imperiale

Museums · Themed

Opened from 1992, it tells the rural life from 1500 to 1918, when the County of Gorizia was under the Habsburgs, in three buildings: an eighteenth-century structure (cellar and barn), a nineteenth-century one (stalls and barns) and a canopy of the mid-Twentieth century. About 25,000 pieces catalogued: trades such as mason, basket, shoemaker, blacksmith, carpenter and a late 19th century typography; plows from the 16th to the 20th century and tools for sowing, harvest and threshing; weights and measurements before and after the metric system (introduced in the County in 1841); tin boxes of the Monfalcone Passer factory; a vintage school room and a room on women in the rural world. Open Wednesday 10-13 and 15-19, Thursday and Friday 10-13 and 18-19, Saturday and Sunday 9-13 and 15-19; groups with one week notice.

Why go. The largest ethnographic collection of Friuli Venezia Giulia.
