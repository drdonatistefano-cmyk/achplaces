Passo del Maniva e Maniva Ski

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Valico delle Prealpi Brescia between Val Trompia and Val Sabbia, about 50 km from Brescia, reachable from Collio with a winding road well signposted. In winter, the Maniva Ski area offers 40 km of slopes between 1,450 and 2,100 meters (1 black, 6 red, 8 blue) and 9 lifts, with snowshoeing also in the moonlight, ski touring, cross-country skiing, sleddog and ski schools also for disabled; in summer the Bike Park Pezzeda offers 8 slopes for 26 km in the Iron Valley network. The roads from Val Sabbia and Crocedomini are usually closed in winter.

Why go. Snow and high-altitude views an hour from Brescia.
