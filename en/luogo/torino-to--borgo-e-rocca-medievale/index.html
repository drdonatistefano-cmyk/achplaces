Borgo e Rocca Medievale

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Designed for the Italian General Exhibition of 1884, it reproduces fifteenth-century buildings and furnishings of Piedmont and the Aosta Valley.

Why go. The Middle Ages rebuilt on the Po.
