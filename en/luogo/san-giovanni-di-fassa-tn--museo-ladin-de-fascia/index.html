Museo Ladin de Fascia

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Modern Museum with ethnographic collections of the Ladin Cultural Institute, with multimedia supports and tables by the designer Milo Manara; Mobicult audioguide in six languages (including Ladin) and experience in virtual reality Pigui Experience (€2 more, by reservation).

Why go. The Ladin culture of Val di Fassa.

  • Where Strada de Sèn Jan 9, San Giovanni di Fassa
  • Entry €7, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.visittrentino.info
