Chiesa parrocchiale di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1626 in Gagliago, instead of a 14th-century chapel, it preserves Romanesque features in the bell tower. Inside are frescoes by Bernardino Peretti and Antonio Cotti: the twelve apostles around the choir, the Trinity behind the main altar and the Annunciation; the baptistery is precious. On the façade there is the Baptism of Jesus, a Madonna, the coat of arms of Ferrari Ghibellines, the Borromeo coat of arms and several sundials.

Why go. Frescoes and sundials on the main square.
