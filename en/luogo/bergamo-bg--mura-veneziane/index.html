Mura Veneziane

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

16th century fortifications about 6 km long, UNESCO World Heritage Site, with panoramic routes to the plain.

Why go. The most beautiful walk at sunset.

  • Where Città Alta, Bergamo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net
