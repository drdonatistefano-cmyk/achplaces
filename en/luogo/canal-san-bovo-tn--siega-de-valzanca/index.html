Siéga de Valzanca

Local life and traditions · Crafts · Paid entry

Venetian hydraulic sawmill built in 1872 by Crisanto Orsingher and active until 1954, with drawer wheel and long vertical blade: it sawed about 3,000 cubic meters a year of timber from the Redòs and Valzanca valleys. Disassembled after the closure, it was rebuilt in the late 1990s by the Paneveggio Park with the whole hydraulic machine. In the summer demonstrations on Sunday 14:30-17.

Why go. The water sawmill restarted.
