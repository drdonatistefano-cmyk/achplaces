Riserva naturale orientata Saline di Trapani e Paceco

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

One of the most important coastal wetlands, managed by the WWF: the chessboard of the tanks, made from the ancient coastal pantani, is dotted with windmills, the Dutch-style star ones with six wooden blades and the American ones with sheet metal blades, used to move water between the tanks and grind the salt. There are flamingos, spatulae, big white herons, garzettes, swamp falcons and small limits, to be seen especially in autumn (September-November) and in spring (February-May); the Via del Sale, to be followed also by bike, connects Trapani to Marsala.

Why go. Flamingos and mills in the salt tanks.
