Castello di Proceno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress of the twelfth century with square tower on a spur of volcanic rock, border fortress of the Heritage of St. Peter, with walls that go down to overhang; according to the municipality the village was fortified by Pope Gregory V in 997, destroyed by the troops of Henry IV in 1083 and rebuilt by the Counts Bovacciani of Chiusi in 1097, and in 1444 it surrendered after months of siege the castle Bernardo d'Utri. From the blackbirds you can see 360 degrees on Lazio, Umbria and Tuscany, from Monte Amiata and Radicofani to the valley of Paglia; today it is a hotel with restaurant and from here starts the Lazio section of Via Francigena.

Why go. The view over three regions from the border towers.
