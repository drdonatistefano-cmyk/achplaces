Parco di Su Motti

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Archaeological and botanical park above the village, in a basaltic amphitheatre with centuries-old oaks, holm oaks and maquis, inhabited by the Neolithic (3800 BC). The path on the left, easy, leads to the necropolis of about fifteen domus de Janas excavated in the walls of sa Carona Arrubia; the one on the right, challenging, rises to the plateau of Taccu Idda with wide views. There are also the polylobed nuraghe is Allonis, the corridor nuraghe Su Motti and the drywalls of the "tancas" .

Why go. Domus de Janas and views on foot from the village.
