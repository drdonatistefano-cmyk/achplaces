Parco del Grassano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Natural oasis with clear springs and lush vegetation crossed by the river Grassano, suitable for picnics, excursions and canoeing, with areas for families, nature trails, canoe rental, play area, bars and restaurants. Open from Tuesday to Sunday from 9:30 am to 11:30 pm (in August also on Monday).

Why go. Water springs and canoe a stone's throw from the village.
