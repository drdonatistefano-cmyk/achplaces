Fortezza di Radicofani e Museo del Cassero

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The fortress visible from tens of kilometers, linked to the figure of Ghino di Tacco, now home to the Museo del Cassero with the archaeological finds of the territory.

Why go. From the tower you can see Amiata, Appennino and the lakes Trasimeno and Bolsena.
