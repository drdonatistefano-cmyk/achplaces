Zucchi Collection, blocchi antichi per la stampa a mano 1785-1935

Museums · Themed

A collection that traces three centuries of textile culture in Europe: it is indicated as the world's largest collection of hand-printing blocks on fabric, wood and metal, catalogued and sorted according to style criteria.

Why go. The world's largest archive of fabric moulds.
