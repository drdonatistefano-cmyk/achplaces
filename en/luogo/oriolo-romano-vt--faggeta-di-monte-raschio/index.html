Faggeta di Monte Raschio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Beech of about 150 hectares in the Bracciano-Martignano Regional Natural Park, since 2017 part of the UNESCO site of the ancient primordial beech forests of the Carpathians and other regions of Europe, with beech trees next to oaks and pine trees. It crosses the CAI 175C trail, a 8.4 km ring (about 2 hours, 205 m of climb, difficulty T) that starts from the main square under the municipality and is walkable, by bike and horseback, with picnic area and water point in the locality Cerratina.

Why go. A beech forest of humanity just a stone's throw from the country.

  • Where Partenza dalla piazza principale, Oriolo Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
