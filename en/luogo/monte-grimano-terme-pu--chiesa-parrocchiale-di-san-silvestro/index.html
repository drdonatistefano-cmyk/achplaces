Chiesa parrocchiale di San Silvestro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built at the end of the eighteenth century on a project by Cosimo Morelli of Imola, in neoclassical style then renovated; it preserves eight large paintings between the seventeenth and nineteenth centuries, a 16th century Crucifix and an eighteenth-century organ of little less than 500 reeds, inventoried as a cultural heritage of the Marche Region.

Why go. The paintings and the historical organ of the parish.
