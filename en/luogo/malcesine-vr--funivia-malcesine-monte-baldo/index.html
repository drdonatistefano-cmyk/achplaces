Funivia Malcesine-Monte Baldo

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Two trunks, Malcesine-San Michele and San Michele-Monte Baldo; the cabins of the second rotate on themselves offering a 360° view of the lake, the Alps and the plain, and are wheelchair accessible. At altitude hiking and Nordic walking trails, downhill MTB trails and a paragliding takeoff; Baldo hosts almost half of the flora of the Alps.

Why go. From the lake to the ridge of Baldo in a few minutes.
