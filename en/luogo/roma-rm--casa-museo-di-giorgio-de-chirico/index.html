Casa Museo di Giorgio de Chirico

Museums · House museums

On the top floor of the seventeenth-century Palazzetto dei Borgognoni, the house where the painter lived from 1948 to 1978, opened in 1998 with paintings from the end of the twenties to the mid-seventies, the studio and the library.

Why go. "The center of the center of the world."
