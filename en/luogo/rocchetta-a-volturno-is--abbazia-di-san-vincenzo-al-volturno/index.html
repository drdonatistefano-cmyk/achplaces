Abbazia di San Vincenzo al Volturno

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry

Benedictine monastery founded according to tradition at the beginning of the 8th century by three Beneventan nobles, Paldo, Taso and Tato, at the springs of Volturno; placed by Charlemagne under his protection in 787, under the abbots Joshua, Talarico and Epifanio became a monastic city with about ten churches and about 350 monks, until the 881 raid devastated it. The archaeological park preserves the remains of the monastic city of the IX century, San Vincenzo Minore with the Crypt of Epifanio and its high medieval frescoes, in which the abbot appears among saints and angels, and the basilica of San Vincenzo Maggiore, rebuilt in the twentieth century, with the ring crypt of Joshua. The state archaeological complex, on the border with Castel San Vincenzo, is closed on Mondays (ticket reduced to €3 from 13 March 2026 until the completion of the works on the barriers).

Why go. The frescoes of the Crypt of Epifanio, a masterpiece of the Middle Ages.
