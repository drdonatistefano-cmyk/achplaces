Riserva regionale dei Calanchi

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Established in 2011, it is the largest reserve of Basilicata: calanchi and ravines of clay that reach the cultivated fields, among Mediterranean maquis, pine trees and cypresses, with the geosite of Tempa Petrolla, a rocky spur that emerges from the clay. Rich in birds, it is recognized as IBA (Important Bird Area); ancient mule tracks that join the village to the Val d'Agri are now hiking routes. The tours take place as guided trekking of the center of environmental education "I Calanchi" of Legambiente.

Why go. The lunar landscape of the calanchi.
