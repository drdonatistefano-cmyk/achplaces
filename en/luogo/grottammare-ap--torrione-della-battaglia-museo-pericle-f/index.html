Torrione della Battaglia, Museo Pericle Fazzini

Museums · Modern art · Viewpoint

Fortification of the castle's walls, built after the sacking of 1525 by the pirate Dulcigno and transformed into a museum in 2004: about 230 works by the grottammarese sculptor Pericles Fazzini, including bronzes, silver, gold, drawings, pastels, engravings and two portraits of Giuseppe Ungaretti, with the silver sketch of the Resurrection of the Sala Nervi in the Vatican.

Why go. The Resurrection of Fazzini in miniature.
