Museo Beltrami

Museums · House museums

On the upper floor of Palazzo Beltrami, it preserves relics, documents and works by Giacomo Costantino Beltrami, magistrate and traveler, discoverer of the sources of the Mississippi, including a Sioux medicine drum chosen as a symbol of the Winter Olympics of Calgary in 1988.

Why go. The Sioux drum of Calgary 1988.
