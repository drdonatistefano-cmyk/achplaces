Museo della Vite e del Vino

Museums · Themed

In the same Palazzo Pretorio, in five sections, it tells the link between climate, soil and wine, with objects and recipes in dialect, and presents the six DOCs of the territory, including Bianco di Pitigliano and Ansonica. Morellino di Scansano has been DOC since 1978 and DOCG since the 2006 harvest.
