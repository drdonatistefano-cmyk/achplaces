Museo civico «Angelo Bozzola»

Museums · Modern art

Since 1998 it occupies the north-east tower of the castle and part of the round path; it collects sculptures, paintings and graphic works that the Galliate artist Angelo Bozzola has donated to his community.

Why go. The work of Bozzola inside the walls.
