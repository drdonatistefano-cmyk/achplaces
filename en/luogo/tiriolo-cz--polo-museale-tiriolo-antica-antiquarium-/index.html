Polo museale Tiriolo Antica (Antiquarium e Museo del costume)

Museums · Archaeological

It brings together the archaeological museum, with finds of ancient, medieval and modern age from the city and the territory, and the section of the traditional costume, dedicated to the "pacchiana" , the costume richly embroidered that was part of the dowry of the bride. The archaeological park of Gianmartino and the Ecomuseo Fortezza-Monte are also part of the polo; guided tours, also in foreign language, and excursions are offered. Access with ramps.

Why go. Archaeology and traditional costume of Tiriolo.
