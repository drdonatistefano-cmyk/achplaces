Foresta fossile di Dunarobba e Centro di paleontologia vegetale

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Giant evergreen coniferous logs, similar to the present sequoias, grown in the Pliocene on the shore of the ancient Lake Tiberino and buried from the debris of the floods up to the roots; some reach about 1.5 m of diameter and have been preserved thanks to a process of chemical fossilization. Umbriatourism indicates it among the most important paleontological sites in the world, discovered by chance in the seventies during the extraction of clay. The visit starts from the Centre of Plant Paleontology, east of the country, and is suitable for children.

Why go. A forest millions of years ago, unique in Europe to see live.
