Villa Lante

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In Bagnaia, summer residence of the bishops of Viterbo: eighteen hectares between park and formal garden, one of the most spectacular manor gardens of the sixteenth century, attributed to Jacopo Barozzi da Vignola.

Why go. Among the great Italian gardens.

  • Where Via Jacopo Barozzi 71, Bagnaia, Viterbo

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
