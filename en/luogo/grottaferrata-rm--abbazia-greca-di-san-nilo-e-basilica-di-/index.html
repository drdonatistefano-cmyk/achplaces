Abbazia greca di San Nilo e basilica di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded in 1004 by St. Nile of Rossano among the ruins of a Roman villa, where a republican sepulchre closed by iron grates gave the name of "Crypta ferrata" , the basilica was consecrated on 17 December 1024 and Leo XIII gave it the title of basilica. Inside the mosaics with the Pentecost on the triumphal arch, thirteenth-century frescoes attributed to the school of Pietro Cavallini, the Byzantine icon of the Theotokos of the XII-XIII century, the bell tower of the XII-th century with five orders of triphores and the chapel of the Holy Founders, rebuilt in 1608 for Odoardo Farnese and frescoed by the Domenichino; the complex is closed by a belt of the fifteenth century.

Why go. A corner of Byzantium at the Roman Castles.
