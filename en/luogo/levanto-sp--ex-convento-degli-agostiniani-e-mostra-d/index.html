Ex Convento degli Agostiniani e Mostra della cultura materiale

Museums · Themed

A medieval building overlooking the ancient canal port, mentioned in the statues of 1377, with decorations from the XV-XVI century on the first floor; from 1600 Augustinian convent, then hospital and school, was recovered for the Jubilee of 2000 and hosts the Hostalia del Mare hostel, an auditorium and the permanent exhibition of material culture. Opened in 1981, it exhibits about 300 objects of peasant work in six cycles (e.g. tools, vine and wine, mills and mills, breeding, home, games and traditions), with a section on the dialect and reconstruction of the canal port. In the summer open in the evening, 21:30-23:30, on Friday and Saturday (and also on Sunday in the second half of July). Tel. 0187 817776.

Why go. The peasant life of the valley in a convent on the old port.
