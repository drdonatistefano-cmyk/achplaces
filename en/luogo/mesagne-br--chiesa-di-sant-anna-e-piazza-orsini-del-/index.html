Chiesa di Sant'Anna e piazza Orsini del Balzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The church, which the Municipality defines as a masterpiece, overlooks Piazza Orsini del Balzo and has a history linked to that of the castle; it is one of the four Baroque churches of the itinerary "Mesagne," the Baroque heart of Salento.

Why go. A baroque church on the square next to the castle.
