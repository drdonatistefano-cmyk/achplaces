Monte Capanne e funivia

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

With 1019 meters it is the highest peak of Elba and the province of Livorno; you walk up the CAI trails or in a few minutes with the cable car near the village, active in summer.

Why go. The roof of Elba.
