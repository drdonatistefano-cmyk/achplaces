Santuario della Via Crucis («le Capèle»)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Next to the parish church of San Martino, a 14-station Via Crucis with 198 life-size statues in wood and plaster, commissioned in 1752 to Benjamin Simoni by the parish priest Don Andrea Boldini and completed in 1764 by Donato and Grazioso Fantoni (stations VIII, IX and perhaps X); the 14th current station is Giovanni Selleroni (1869), while the original of Simoni is in Breno. The frescoes on the walls are by Scotti and the Corbellini brothers; in the parish there are wooden sculptures of Andrea Fantoni's workshop. Guided tours for groups or individuals organized by parish and municipality, which also include the church of Our Lady of Carmine.

Why go. A Sacred Mount in miniature, with statues of human size.
