Chiesa di San Giusto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Romanesque roundabout of the XI-XII centuries on a hill near the castle of San Maroto, cited as priory in 1240, among the most singular Romanesque works of the Marche: circular plan with four apses decorated with hanging arches and concentric stone ring dome built without centimeters. The bell tower dates back to the first fourteenth century; inside a Madonna enthroned from the second half of the thirteenth century and a table of the Madonna del Rosario di Venanzo da Camerino.

Why go. The rare Romanesque roundabout with four apses.
