Museo della Stampa – Casa degli Stampatori

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the brick building that was the seat of the Jewish printing house, in the Jewish quarter: here Israel Nathan da Spira printed in 1483 the treatise It has manual printing machines from the late 19th and early 20th centuries; it is not included in the cumulative ticket. Closed on Monday.

Why go. Where the first printed Jewish Bible was born.

  • Where Via Lanfranco 6, Soncino
  • Entry €3, family €6, groups €2,50
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website museostampasoncino.it
