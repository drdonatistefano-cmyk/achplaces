Le Priare

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

The underground complex of ancient stone quarries, which can be visited with the guides of the Pro Loco Alte Montecchio; in summer it opens on Sunday afternoon, at Christmas it becomes the real home of Santa Claus and on Halloween it hosts themed events.

Why go. An underground route in the old stone quarries.
