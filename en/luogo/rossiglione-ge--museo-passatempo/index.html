Museo Passatempo

Local life and traditions · Motors · Paid entry

Itinerary in the memory and Italian costume of the twentieth century, the result of more than fifteen years of research by the collectors Guido Minetti and Sylvia Pizzorno: themed rooms with motorcycle workshop, '40s emporio, lounge, appliances, Olivetti machines, Vespa dealership and tribute to Guzzi, bicycles, toys, juke-box and pinball machines, many objects still working.

Why go. Lambrette, Vespa and the daily life of the last century.

No fixed time: openings are communicated month by month or by phone (010 9239921)

  • Where Via Sciutto 3, Rossiglione
  • Entry €4, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.museopassatempo.it
