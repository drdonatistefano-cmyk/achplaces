Parco archeologico e Antiquarium di Canne della Battaglia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The place of the victory of Hannibal over the Romans in 216 BC: the Antiquarium with prehistoric, classical and medieval finds, the remains of the Byzantine castle and the citadel crossed by the decuman.

Why go. The battlefield of Hannibal.
