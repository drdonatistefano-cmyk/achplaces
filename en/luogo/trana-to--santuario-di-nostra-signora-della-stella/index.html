Santuario di Nostra Signora della Stella

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Built in the eighteenth century on the site of an ancient church of Roman times, of which remain the arch of the altar and a niche; the bell tower is from 1885. The interior is rich in decorations and furnishings and preserves an ancient cedar statue. Visitable during religious services.

Why go. An eighteenth-century sanctuary on an ancient church.
