Musei universitari di Modena

Museums · Science and university

The University's historical collections: the Museum of Paleontology, born in 1776 at the behest of Duke Francesco III d'Este as part of the Museum of Natural History; the Museum of Zoology and Comparative Anatomy with about 3,500 naturalized vertebrates; the Museum of Human Anatomy with the anatomical theatre wanted according to the dictates of Antonio Scarpa and the first Italian anthropological collection; the Laboratory of mathematical machines with its geometric instruments.

Why go. 18th century science in the original rooms.
