Palazzo Ducale e Galleria Nazionale delle Marche

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage

"A city in the form of a palace": the main part, with the façade of the Torricini, the Courtyard of Honour and the staircase, was designed by Luciano Laurana (1464-1472) and completed by Francesco di Giorgio Martini; it houses the National Gallery of the Marches with the Flagellation and the Madonna of Senigallia by Piero della Francesca, the Muta di Raffaello and the ideal City, as well as the Studiolo del Duca with the tarsie perspective.

Why go. One of the highest points of the Italian Renaissance.
