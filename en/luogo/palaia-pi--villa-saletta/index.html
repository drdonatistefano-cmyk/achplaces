Villa Saletta

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Viewpoint

Ancient castle walled today ghost village, built as a village-farm between the sixteenth and eighteenth centuries: remains of the castle, a row of abandoned buildings along the main street and a clock tower with the coats of arms of the owners on the large courtyard, which in summer hosts a festival of popular theatre. The night of St. Lawrence, Good Morning Babylon and Flower of the Taviani brothers and Napoleon of Paolo Virzì.

Why go. The ghost village of the Taviani cinema.
