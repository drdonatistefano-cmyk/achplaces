Chiesa di San Jacopo Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In 1988 the remains of a first church of the late XI century emerged; the medieval building, dedicated to the saints Iacopo, Egidio and Cristoforo patrons of the pilgrims, was restored in the fifteenth century and in the sixteenth century had a door to colored marbles on the south side. After the closure of the hospital desired by Pietro Leopoldo, in 1827-1830 the church was rebuilt with different orientation and the medieval part became the transept.

Why go. The church of the pilgrims of Francigena, with the medieval church incorporated into the transept.
