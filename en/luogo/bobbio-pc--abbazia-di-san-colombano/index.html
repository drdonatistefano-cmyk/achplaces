Abbazia di San Colombano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The monastery founded in 614 by the Irish monk Colombano, called the northern Montecassino, with the crypt, the saint's sarcophagus and the 12th century pavement mosaic.

Why go. One of the great cultural centers of the Middle Ages.

  • Where Piazza San Fara, Bobbio
  • Entry To be checked: church; the three museums at extra charge
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
