Duomo di Santa Maria Assunta e San Pantaleone

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Founded in 1086-1087 on the model of the abbey of Montecassino, it has above the three Roman re-utilization portals and in the center the bronze door to 80 tiles of Barisano da Trani (1179). Inside two marble inlays in front of each other and the seventeenth-century chapel with the vial of the blood of Saint Pantaleone, patron, in Ravello since 1112.

Why go. Barisano's door and cosmates' ambos.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Ravello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
