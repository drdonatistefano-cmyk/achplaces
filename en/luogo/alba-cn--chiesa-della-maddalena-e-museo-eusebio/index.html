Chiesa della Maddalena e Museo Eusebio

Museums · Archaeological

In the convent founded in 1441 by Blessed Margherita di Savoia, the baroque church with an elliptical plan by Bernardo Vittone and the Archaeological Civic Museum and Natural Sciences . Federico Eusebio is held in 21 rooms; in the courtyard there is the White Truffle Fair.

Why go. Vittone and the civic museum.
