Castello Reale di Govone

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built by the accounts Solaro from 1675 on a project by Guarino Guarini and completed around 1780 with the staircase adorned with marbles and sculptures of the Fontana d'Ercole della Venaria; of the Savoy since 1795, summer residence of Carlo Felice. You can visit royal apartments, party hall and Chinese halls. UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Why go. The summer residence of Carlo Felice.
