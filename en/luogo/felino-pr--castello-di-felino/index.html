Castello di Felino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Castle built in the 12th century on a hill just outside the village, then enlarged and fortified up to the maximum splendor under Pier Maria dei Rossi; then passed to Pallavicino, Sforza, Farnese and Mensa Vescovile, and was restored by the Alessandrini family. It dominates the valley between Parma and Baganza, is part of the circuit of the Castles of the Duchy and houses a restaurant and the Salame Museum.

Why go. A medieval manor overlooking Parma and Baganza.
