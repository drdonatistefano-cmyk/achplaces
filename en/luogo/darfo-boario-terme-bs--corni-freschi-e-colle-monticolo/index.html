Corni Freschi e colle Monticolo

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the base of the Monticolo hill, on the right bank of the Oglio, a ritual mass of the Copper Age (second half of the III millennium BC) called "Rock of the Alabards" for the nine alabards carved almost life-size; the excavation of 2002 revealed under them a second composition of fifteen daggers. The site is free and always visitable; at the Corni Freschi there is also a climbing wall and on the Monticolo the Giant Bench n. 72.

Why go. The alabards engraved 4,500 years ago, always accessible.
