Castello degli Alfieri

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In the seventeenth century Count Catalano Alfieri, general of the sabauda infantry, had built a baroque residence on the site of the medieval fortress, completed by his son Carlo Emanuele; in 1797 he passed to the Support Alfieri and in 1952 the last descendant left him to the parish, which gave him to the municipality. The door in walnut with carved baroque tiles, the staircase of honor, the fairytale hall and the chapel are outstanding; the tradition that binds you to the inspiration of the Saul by Vittorio Alfieri is judged incorrect by the same source. Open from April to October on weekends, 10.30-18.30; accessible by elevator.

Why go. A baroque residence of the Alfieri overlooking Langhe and Roero.
