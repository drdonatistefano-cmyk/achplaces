Museo storico etnografico dei Padri Cappuccini

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

It is located in the Capuchin monastery built since 1608 on a hill overlooking the village. It exhibits illuminated choir books, papal decrees from the sixteenth century, relics, wooden statues, paintings and liturgical furnishings from other Sardinian convents; an archaeological section with finds from the Neolithic to the early Christian and Byzantine ages; and the reconstruction of the daily life of the convent with the tools of the friars. The guided tour is included in the ticket. Open Saturday and Sunday 10-13 and 15:30-19 (from June to October 16-19:30).

Why go. Sacred art, archaeology and life of the friars in a convent of the seventeenth century.
