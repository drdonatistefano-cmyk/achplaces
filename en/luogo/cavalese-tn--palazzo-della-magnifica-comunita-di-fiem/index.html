Palazzo della Magnifica Comunità di Fiemme e Pinacoteca

Museums · Old masters

Cited from the end of the thirteenth century as the seat of the vicar of the prince bishop of Trento, who went up to the valley twice a year to administer justice; renovated in the sixteenth century by artists of the Buonconsiglio yard, prison since the beginning of the nineteenth century, purchased by the Magnifica Comunità in 1850 and reopened as a museum-pinacoteca on July 5, 2012. He painted paintings of the School of Fiemme (XVII-XIX century) in chronological order; in the nineteenth-century prisons the incisions of the prisoners remain. Guided tours every day, even theatricalized.

Why go. A thousand years of self-government in the valley, between paintings and prisons.
