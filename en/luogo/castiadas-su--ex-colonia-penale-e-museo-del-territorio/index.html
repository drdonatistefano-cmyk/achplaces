Ex colonia penale e Museo del territorio

Museums · City history

In August 1875 thirty prisoners and seven officers landed in Cala Sinzias to clean up a marshy territory; the colony arrived to accommodate more than two thousand people, with workshops, joineries, hospital, pharmacy and post office, and closed definitively in 1956. Today you can visit the building of the management, which houses on two floors the museum of the territory on the history of the colony and the traditions of the Sarrabus, the prison structures, the villa of the director with garden, the church of San Basilide built by the prisoners at the end of the nineteenth century and the historical cemetery.

Why go. An agricultural prison of the nineteenth century became a country.
