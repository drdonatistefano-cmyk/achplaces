Casa-museo Sa Domu Antiga

Museums · Themed

Sulcitan farmhouse of the early 20th century in the center of the village, with bedroom, dining room, kitchen and anteroom furnished, a wooden frame still working and tools for weaving, dyeing of wool and processing of linen; in the courtyard with the well the tools for cultivation, bread, wine and breeding, plus a room on the local handicrafts. On the morning of the Marriage the bride dresses there.

Why go. The Sulcis farmhouse as it was.

  • Where Via Mazzini 45, Santadi
  • Entry To be checked (for a fee, cumulative with other museums)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
