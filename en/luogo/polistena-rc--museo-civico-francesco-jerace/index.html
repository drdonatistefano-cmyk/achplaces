Museo civico "Francesco Jerace"

Museums · City history

Provisionally housed in two halls of the Municipal Library, it contains gravestones, coats of arms and works of the churches, convents and civil buildings of the Polistena destroyed by the earthquake of February 5, 1783, paintings and sculptures of local artists (including eighteen works by Francesco Jerace), a herbarium, fossil and archaeological finds also protohistoric with funerary furnishings, parchments from 1430 to the end of the eighteenth century, photographs of the late nineteenth-early twentieth century and tools of peasant and craftsman work.

Why go. The works of Jerace and the memory of the city before 1783.
