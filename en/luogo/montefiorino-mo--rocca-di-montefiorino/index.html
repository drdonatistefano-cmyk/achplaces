Rocca di Montefiorino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Born around 1170 as a quadrangular tower guarding the lands of the Badia di Frassinoro, then a castle mast erected by the Montecuccoli in the thirteenth century; after 1429 it was the seat of an extensive podestria until 1797. The restored tower can be visited and at the top has a 360-degree panoramic balcony on the valleys of the Dolo and the Dragon and on the peaks of the Cimone and the Cusna.

Why go. The view from the tower over the valleys of the Dolo and the Dragon.
