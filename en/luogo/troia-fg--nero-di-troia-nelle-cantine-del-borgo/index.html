Nero di Troia nelle cantine del borgo

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The most typical wine of the place is the Nero di Troia DOC, served in wine bars, wine cellars and restaurants of the village; the cooperative winery Decanto has invested in the territory since 2004.

Why go. Taste the vine that bears the name of the village.
