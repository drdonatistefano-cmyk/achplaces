Raito e Santa Maria delle Grazie

History and architecture · Villages · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Frazione can be reached only on foot by steps, with the church of Santa Maria delle Grazie in panoramic position on the sea and Villa Guariglia with its terraced park.

Why go. The hamlet-balcony on the gulf.
