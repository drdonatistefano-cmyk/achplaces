Necropoli umbro-etrusca del Vallone di San Lorenzo

History and architecture · Archaeology

Among the largest pre-Roman necropolis in Umbria, in the Tiber River Park between Montecchio and Baschi: chamber tombs excavated in the local limestone ('Matile'), with dromos and side docks, used from the VII to the IV century BC and discovered in 1855 by Domenico Golini. They were sacked in ancient times, they returned weapons and pottery from banquets in the male, jewelry and ointment tombs in the female ones; they showed strong ties with the Etruscan Orvieto.

Why go. The Etruscan tombs in the holm oak forest.
