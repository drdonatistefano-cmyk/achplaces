Museo del grano

Museums · Themed

It is located on the Piano delle Fosse in the complex of the former Opera Pia Monte Fornari: the ground floor was a Dominican monastery of the sixteenth century, to which the first floor was added in the nineteenth century; after being barracks and orphanage, the building with internal cloister was recovered by the municipality with European funds through the LAG Piana del Tavoliere to house the civic museum of wheat.

Why go. The civilization of the table grain, next to the grain pits.
