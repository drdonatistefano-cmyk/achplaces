Palazzo Quadrio Curzio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Former Venosta Palace, a residence of the Cinque-Seicento in front of the parish church of San Martino; in the eighteenth century it had the present entrance with the loggia above and preserves a porch courtyard with stone doors surmounted by stuccoes.

Why go. The porch courtyard of a noble residence.
