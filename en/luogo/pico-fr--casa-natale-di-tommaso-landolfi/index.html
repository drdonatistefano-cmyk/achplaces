Casa natale di Tommaso Landolfi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

House with arched windows and internal garden along via Landolfi, inserted in the Network of the Historical Houses of Lazio; here Montale visited his friend Landolfi in 1937, before writing the Elegia of Pico Farnese. The writer, who died in Ronciglione, is buried in Pico.

Why go. The home of the writer and Montale guest.
