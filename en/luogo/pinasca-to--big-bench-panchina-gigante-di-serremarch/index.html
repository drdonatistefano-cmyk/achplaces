Big Bench – Panchina gigante di Serremarchetto

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Viewpoint

It is reached in about twenty minutes on foot following the old ring of the Serremarchetto cross-country track of the seventies and eighties, a quiet walk among shady beech woods suitable for everyone. From the bench the view spans the lower Val Chisone and the plain of Pinerol and, on clear days, the Alpine arch from the Marittime to the Monviso.

Why go. Easy walk with view to the Monviso.
