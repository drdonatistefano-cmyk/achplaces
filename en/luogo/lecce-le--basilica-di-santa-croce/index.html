Basilica di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Basilicas

The first stone was laid in 1353 and the enlargement lasted almost two centuries; a very rich Baroque façade with rosette, 16 altars and a 16th century fresco of the Madonna of Constantinople; next to the former convent of the Celestines, today Prefecture.

Why go. The symbol of the Lecce baroque.
