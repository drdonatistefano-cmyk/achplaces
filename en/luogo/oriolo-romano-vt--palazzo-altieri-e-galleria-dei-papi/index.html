Palazzo Altieri e Galleria dei Papi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

The manorist palace-villa built between 1578 and 1585 by Santacroce, with a loggia with five arches; the project is traditionally attributed to Vignola, but the Ministry excludes it because it died in 1573. In the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries the Altieri, with Carlo Fontana, added the northern wings with the courtyard, the clock tower, the entrance with basalt bridge and the fountain of the square. The rooms have stuccoes and frescoes with landscapes and fairy tales of Esopo; the Galleria Altieri, initiated by Cardinal Paluzzo Altieri, exhibits about 240 portraits of all the Popes from St. Peter to Francis, the only complete collection of the genre, with copies from Raffaello, Tiziano and Caravaggio. In the garden a rate of over 100 years. Open Tuesday-Sunday 8.30-19.30 (ticket office until 6.30 p.m.), closed on Monday.

Why go. All the Popes of history in one gallery.
