Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo e Madonna del Rosario

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In Piazza Leopardi, the main church of San Michele Arcangelo was rebuilt in 1934 and has two altars; the table of Our Lady of the Rosary by Giulio Vergari (1519) is now exposed in the parish church.

Why go. A table from the Vergari of the early sixteenth century.

after the earthquake of 2016 the monuments are partially usable; the card of the Park describes the state prior to the earthquake
