Porta Borsari

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The door of the Roman walls of the first century A.D., originally Porta Iovia, so called by the "bursarii" who collected duties; a plaque recalls the clash between Tebaldo and Romeo.

Why go. The Roman entrance to the city.

  • Where Corso Porta Borsari, Verona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
