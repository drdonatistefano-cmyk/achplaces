Parco naturale dei Lagoni di Mercurago

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Protected area since 1980 in the hamlet of Mercurago, among peat bogs, pastures and woods; the Lagoni are part of the UNESCO heritage site as a prehistoric palafittic site of the Alps

Why go. The UNESCO stilts.

  • Where Via Gattico 6, Mercurago, Arona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it
