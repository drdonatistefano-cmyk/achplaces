Castello Aragonese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It rises on an islet of trachyte 113 meters high, joined to Ischia Ponte by a masonry bridge of about 220 meters. The first fortifications date back to 474 B.C. with Gerone I of Syracuse; in 1441 Alfonso V of Aragon made it a fortress with walls, towers and ramparts against pirates and dug into the rock a gallery of 400 meters. Inside churches, courtyards, convents and gardens, including the church of the Immaculate Church with the large dome and the Cathedral of the Assumption with frescoes of the sixteenth century; an elevator rises to 60 meters.

Why go. The fortress overlooking the sea with a view of the gulf and Vesuvius.
