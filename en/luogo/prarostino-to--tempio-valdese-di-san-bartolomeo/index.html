Tempio valdese di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A place of Waldensian worship has been documented since 1692, a hut with straw roof replaced by loose in 1724; after long disputes the current temple was built in 1828-29. Inside wooden floor, gallery raised on three sides, pulpit and pipe organ in front of the entrance and semicircle benches.

Why go. A Waldensian temple of the nineteenth century with its wooden gallery.
