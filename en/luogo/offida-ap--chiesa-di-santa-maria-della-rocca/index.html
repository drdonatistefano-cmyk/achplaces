Chiesa di Santa Maria della Rocca

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

Built in 1330 on a Longobard castle donated in 1039 to the Abbey of Farfa, it is the Romanesque-Gothic monument in the most important terracotta of Offida, on a steep cliff. The three-nave crypt contains fourteenth-century frescoes by the Master of Offida with the stories of Saint Catherine of Alexandria and Saint Lucia; in the apse of the upper church prophets, musical angels and virgins of Ugolino di Vanne, and a Madonna with Child attributed to Vincenzo Pagani. Open Saturday and Sunday.

Why go. The Gothic masterpiece of Piceno on the cliff.

  • Where Via Roma 98, Offida
  • Entry €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
