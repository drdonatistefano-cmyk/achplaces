Polo museale di San Francesco

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Opened in October 2006 in the Franciscan monastery of the 13th century. The Sala Carlo Crivelli exhibits the surviving parts (central register and cusps) of the polyptych painted shortly after 1470 for the church of San Francesco; the Museo Adolfo De Carolis collects about five hundred works of the painter born here (1874-1928), including the oil sketches for the frescoes of the Palazzo del Podestà of Bologna and the xilographies for the Fioretti. The itinerary is completed by Giancarlo Basili's Scenographic Documentation Centre, the Museum of Rural Civilization and the collection of engravings by Domenico Cantatore. It adheres to the Marche Museums Charter.

Why go. Crivelli's polyptych and De Carolis' world.

  • Where Piazzale San Francesco 1, Montefiore dell'Aso

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
