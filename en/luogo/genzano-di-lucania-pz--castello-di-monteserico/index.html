Castello di Monteserico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Norman Castle of the 11th century on Monte Serico, 550 meters and about 15 km from the village, on the border between the Byzantine lands of the Bradano and those Lombard and Norman; it was the scene of the defeat of the Byzantines against the Normans and then masseria of the state property of Frederick II, who frequented it for raptors. It has a trunk-conical shape surrounded by a moat, central square tower, low belt with shoe and drawbridge; in the underground there are prehistoric caves. A simple chapel stands on the floor.

Why go. The castle is federicano among the wheat fields.
