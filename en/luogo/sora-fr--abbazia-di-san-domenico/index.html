Abbazia di San Domenico

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

A medieval abbey, today a basilica, which preserves the relics of the saint; it rises next to the remains of the Marmon Bridge and is the twelfth stage of the Way of St. Benedict (Casamari-Arpino).

Why go. The medieval abbey on the Way of Saint Benedict.
