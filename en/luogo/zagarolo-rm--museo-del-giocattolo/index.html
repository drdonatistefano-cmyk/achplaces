Museo del Giocattolo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Opened in 2005 in the east wing of Palazzo Rospigliosi, on about 1,400 m2 it exhibits more than 800 pieces, about one third of the collection, which tell the story of the toy in Italy in the last century, with European and American productions (brands like Lehmann, Märklin and Bing), ordered for themes that bind the game to objects and social life; three rooms host temporary exhibitions. Free under 6 years, reduced from 6 to 12 years and over 65 years; closed on Mondays and from 10 to 24 August, with seasonal times (in winter Tuesdays and Fridays 9-13 and 15-18, Wednesdays and Thursdays 9-13, Saturdays and Sundays 10-13 and 15-18).

Why go. A century of toys in a historic home.

  • Where Piazza della Indipendenza 6, Zagarolo
  • Entry €5, reduced €2,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.museogiocattolo.it
