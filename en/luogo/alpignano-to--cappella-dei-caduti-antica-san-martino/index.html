Cappella dei Caduti (antica San Martino)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The ancient parish of San Martino, a small Romanesque church first mentioned in 1031 in a document from the Abbey of Novalesa, today the Chapel of the Fallen.

Why go. The oldest Romanesque church in the country.
