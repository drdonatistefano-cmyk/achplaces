Museo archeologico nazionale Cerite

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

In the thirteenth-century castle of the Ruspolis, on two floors: urns of the necropolis of the Sorbo, orientalizing kits and the crater of Eufronio (about 515 BC) and the kylix of Onesimos, returned to Italy in 2015.

Why go. Eufronio's crater at home.

  • Where Castello Ruspoli, Piazza Santa Maria, Cerveteri
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com
