Chiesa Vecchia di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Small Romanesque church of the 12th century in a dominant position on a promontory above the village, with Romanesque bell tower and frescoes of the 16th century recently restored; view of the lake and the mountains.

Why go. Romanesque and frescoes with the lake at the foot.
