Area archeologica di Altilia-Saepinum

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Roman city founded at the bottom of the valley after the conquest, in 293 BC, of the Sannita Saipins; archaeological park established by the MiC in 2021. There are still more than 1 km long walls with towers, doors similar to triumphal arches such as Porta Bojano, the basilica, the forum and the theatre of the first century A.D. (up to 3,000 spectators), on which peasant houses arose that follow the curve; cardoon and decuman follow ancient ways of transhumance. Open from Tuesday to Sunday 9-18, Monday closed.

Why go. A whole Roman city, with the inhabited theatre.

  • Where SP82, località Altilia, Sepino
  • Entry €10 (one ticket with the Museum of the city and the territory)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
