Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded around the 5th century and enlarged in Romanesque times, it has the present aspect of the thirteenth century works of Burgundian workers: a hut façade with three portals and a rhyphole rosette, inside three naves. It preserves the Madonna of Constantinople, a wooden group of the thirteenth century, the Triptych of the Redeemer by Antonio da Alatri (first half of the fifteenth century) and a baptismal font of the thirteenth century with three telamons.

Why go. The Romanesque-Gothic masterpiece of the center.
