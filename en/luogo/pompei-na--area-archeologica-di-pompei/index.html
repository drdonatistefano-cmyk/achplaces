Area archeologica di Pompei

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

On 66 hectares, about 50 excavated, the ashes and lapilli of the eruption of 79 AD have preserved houses, shops, the Forum, sacred areas, thermal baths, the theaters district and the amphitheatre; you enter from Porta Marina. Rediscovered in the sixteenth century during the excavation of the channel of the Earl of Sarno, it was excavated by the Bourbons (Tempio di Iside, Casa del Fauno); Giuseppe Fiorelli in 1858 divided it into regions and islandes and introduced the casts of the victims. Closed only on December 25th.

Why go. Walking through the streets of a Roman city stopped at 79 A.D.

  • Where Via Plinio, Pompei
  • Entry €20, reduced €2; free on the first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
