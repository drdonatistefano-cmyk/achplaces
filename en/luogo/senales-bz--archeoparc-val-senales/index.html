archeoParc Val Senales

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Outdoor museum of 5,000 m2 near the Ötzi's discovery site, which reconstructs the daily life of about 5,300 years ago (the mummy and its equipment are at the South Tyrol Archaeological Museum). Inside there is a permanent exhibition with touchable replicas of Ötzi's clothes and tools and a plastic of the basin of the discovery; outside there are nine life-size houses rebuilt by excavations, fields and gardens with plants of the time, clay oven, pond with monoxyl canoe, long archery and daily workshops of fire, bread and flint. Open every day from Easter to All Saints; the complete visit with the outdoor area takes 3-4 hours.

Why go. Live one day in the Ötzi copper age.

  • Where Madonna 163, 39020 Senales
  • Entry €8, reduced €6 (path 1, prices 2024)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
