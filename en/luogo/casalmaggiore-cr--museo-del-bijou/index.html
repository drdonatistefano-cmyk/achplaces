Museo del Bijou

Museums · Themed

Established in 1986 in the basement of the eighteenth century College of Santa Croce, unicum in Italy: over 20,000 objects produced by Casalmaggiore factories between the late nineteenth and seventies, with tools, machines, photos and catalogs of the industrial gold century of the city, plus a didactic workshop of fusion and gilding. Open Tuesday-Saturday 10-12 and 15-18, Sunday and holidays 15-19; free on the first Sunday of the month.

Why go. The unique history of Italian jewelry.
