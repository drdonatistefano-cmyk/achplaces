Grotta Camerona

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Above Ballino, on the slope of Monte Misone, opens with an entrance about 12 meters high and about 20 meters wide; it is reached with a path and in some summer periods hosts events.

Why go. A huge rock portal on the Mison.
