Palazzo di Baldu e villa di Sent Steva

History and architecture · Archaeology

Medieval complex of about 1,379 m2 in the locality of Santu Stevanu, in the ancient curatory of Balaiana: a quadrangular tower about 10 meters high on three floors and about twenty environments around a pentagonal courtyard, among warehouses, kitchens, stables and metalworking workshops. The tradition ties him to Lamberto Visconti or to Judge Giovanni (1238), in memory of his father Ubaldo; for masonry and openings is interpreted as residence or administrative center rather than as a castle. On the western side there is the church of Santo Stefano, from the XVII-XVIII century on a medieval church, and next to a brick furnace emerged from the excavations.

Why go. The ruins of a residence of the judges of Gallura among the granite boulders.
