Giardino della Villa medicea di Castello

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The prototype of the Italian garden, designed by Tribolo in 1538 for Cosimo I, with the Fountain of Hercules and Anteo and the Cave of Animals. The villa is home to the Accademia della Crusca.

Why go. The Cave of Animals, just restored.

  • Where Via di Castello, Firenze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website villemonumentalitoscana.cultura.gov.it
