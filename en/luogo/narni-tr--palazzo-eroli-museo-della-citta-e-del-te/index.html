Palazzo Eroli – Museo della città e del territorio

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the third floor Fraporta, home to the museum and the civic library: it preserves the Coronation of the Virgin by Domenico Ghirlandaio and the Annunciation of Benozzo Gozzoli, commissioned by Cardinal Bernardo Eroli, and an archaeological section.

Why go. Ghirlandaio and Gozzoli in Narni.
