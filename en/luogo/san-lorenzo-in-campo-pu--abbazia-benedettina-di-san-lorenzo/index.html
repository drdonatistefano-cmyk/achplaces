Abbazia benedettina di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Great Romanesque-Gothic building, one of the most beautiful of the Marches according to the Municipality, built by the Benedictine monks in the early Middle Ages reusing materials of the destroyed Roman town hall of Suasa; the monastery, originally San Lorenzo in Silvis, already existed around the IX century and since 1570 it had as Commendatario Giulio della Rovere. The basilica has a crypt.

Why go. An abbey built with stones of Roman Suasa.

Visits by reservation through the I.A.T. Point.
