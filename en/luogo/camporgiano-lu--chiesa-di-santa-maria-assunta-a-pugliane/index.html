Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta a Puglianella

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Puglianella, the church houses a Madonna with a wooden Child by Nino Pisano, son of Andrea.

Why go. To be combined with San Jacopo to see father and son Pisano a few kilometers.
