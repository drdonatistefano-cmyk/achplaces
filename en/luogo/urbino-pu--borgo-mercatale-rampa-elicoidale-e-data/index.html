Borgo Mercatale, Rampa elicoidale e Data

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The square was built at the end of the fifteenth century on a project by Francesco di Giorgio Martini, with powerful containment works; on one side the Data, on the other the ramp of Francesco di Giorgio, above which stands the nineteenth-century theatre, which connects the Mercatale to the historic center.

Why go. The monumental entrance to the city from below.
