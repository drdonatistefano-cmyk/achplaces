Grotta del Farneto

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Grotto in plaster discovered by Francesco Orsoni in the nineteenth century and reopened in 2008, with an important protohistoric settlement; Luigi Fantini found a necropolis of the Copper Age.

Why go. An easy speleological experience, also suitable for children from 4 years.

  • Where Centro visite Casa Fantini, Via Jussi 171, Farneto, San Lazzaro di Savena
  • Entry Guided tours for a fee: fare with reservation
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility You need boots or boots with carved sole; the helmet is supplied
  • Official website enteparchi.bo.it
