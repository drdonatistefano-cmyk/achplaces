Palazzo Pubblico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Venue of the Municipality on Piazza Matteotti, joins the Palazzo Maggiore and the Palazzo del Podestà, built from 1289 perhaps with Eugubine workers; in 1476 it was donated to Federico da Montefeltro, who transformed it into a ducal residence, perhaps with Francesco di Giorgio Martini. On the façade the Madonna and Child in Istria stone (1680) and the clock; inside the vestibule with stone stones and frescoes by Giovanni Dionigi (1563), the Hall of Arms (1889), the Courtyard of the Cosmos with the Cosmic Order of Eliseo Mattiacci and the ancient prisons.

Why go. The Town Hall which was the residence of the Montefeltro.
