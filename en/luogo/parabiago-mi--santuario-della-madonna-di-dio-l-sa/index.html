Santuario della Madonna di Dio 'l Sa

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

It rises on the road to Nerviano on the remains of an ancient oratory of the country, of which there are still frescoes; tradition attributes it, without confirmation, to Bramante or his pupil. He lived the greatest splendor in the seventeenth century and since 1914 he has been a National Monument. At the altar there is a polyptych from the school of Luinesca with the Virgin with the Child and saints, an eight-faced gallery and seventeenth-century statues of saints inside and outside.

Why go. A Renaissance sanctuary suspended between Bramante and legend.
