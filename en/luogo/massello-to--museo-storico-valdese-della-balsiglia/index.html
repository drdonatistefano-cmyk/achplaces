Museo storico valdese della Balsiglia

Museums · City history

On the ground floor of the former school hall of the village, a historic layout of the 1930s, realized for the 250th anniversary, recalls the return of the Valdesi to Piedmont between 1686 and 1690 and the siege of the Balsiglia of May 1690, when about a thousand Waldenses, returned the year before after three years of exile with the "Glorious Repatriation," resisted in this last trench. Audioguide available; management of the Fondazione Centro Culturale Valdese.

Why go. The last trench of Waldensian history, in the place where it happened.
