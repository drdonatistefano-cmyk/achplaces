Museo di Palazzo Lanfranchi

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the palace built between 1668 and 1672 as a seminary, next to the Sasso Caveoso: sections of sacred art, Camillo d'Errico collection and contemporary art with the works of Carlo Levi, including the canvas "Lucania '61" .

Why go. Carlo Levi's canvas.

  • Where Piazza Giovanni Pascoli 1, Matera
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
