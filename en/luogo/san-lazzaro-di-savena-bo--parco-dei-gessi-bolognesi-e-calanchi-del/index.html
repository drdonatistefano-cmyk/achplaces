Parco dei Gessi Bolognesi e Calanchi dell'Abbadessa

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 5,000 hectares of chalk and calanchi at the gates of Bologna, part of the UNESCO site on karst in the evaporites.

Why go. Dolines and caves half an hour from the centre of Bologna.
