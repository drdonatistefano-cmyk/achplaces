Centro di documentazione Stava 1985

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Managed by the Stava Foundation 1985, it tells of the collapse of the decantation basins of the Prestavèl mine of July 19, 1985: causes, responsibility, reconstruction and parallels with the tragedies of Vajont and Cermis. The 1-1,5-hour visit usually begins with the short documentary "Stava 19 July" (in English on request). Open every day from 15 June to 15 September from 15 to 18; in the rest of the year on Saturdays by reservation (minimum 8 people, by 12 on Friday).

Why go. To understand one of the great tragedies of the twentieth century alpine.

  • Where Piazzale Sgorigrad, Stava, 38038 Tesero
  • Entry free (15/6-15/9), off-season €5, families €10
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.stava1985.it
