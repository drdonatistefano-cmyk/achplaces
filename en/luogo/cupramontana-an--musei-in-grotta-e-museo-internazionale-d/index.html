Musei in Grotta e Museo internazionale dell'etichetta del vino

Museums · Themed

In the caves of the Monastery of Santa Caterina, the Museums in Grotta (MIG) space houses the museum with labels from all over the world: a historical collection with rare pieces, some from the late nineteenth century, and a contemporary collection with hundreds of sketches inspired by the wine of Italian and foreign artists.
