Chiesa monumentale della Maddalena

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the fifteenth century, declared a national monument, in an intact environment surrounded by woods; it is reached by an asphalt road about 1 km beyond the crossroads to Vasia, passing next to a pond with a weeping willow.

Why go. A 15th century church isolated in the woods.
