Badia della SS. Trinità

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded in 1011 by St. Alferio Pappa Carbone in the Selano valley: the basilica of 1761 by Giovanni del Gaizo preserves an ambo from the 12th century and sculptures by Tino da Camaino; you can also visit the Romanesque cloister, the chapels of the ancient basilica, the catacombs, the Longobard cemetery, the museum and library with over 80,000 volumes and an archive of parchments since 792.

Why go. A thousand years of Benedictine monasticism.
