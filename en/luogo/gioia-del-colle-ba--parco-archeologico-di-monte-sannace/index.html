Parco archeologico di Monte Sannace

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Inhabited from the Iron Age to the First Imperial Age, with the greatest splendor between the VI and III century BC and defensive walls of the second half of the IV century BC that separate acropolis and inhabited in the plain. From the Masseria Montanaro start an archaeological and naturalistic itinerary: houses, large tombs, stoà, a Hellenistic house, painted tombs, a viewpoint and a trullo in the Murgia landscape. Open every day 8:30-15:00.

Why go. The city peucezia on the Murgia, with a view.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
