Domus de Janas di Fanne Massa e Sa Spelunca de Nonna

History and architecture · Archaeology

Prenuragic tombs dug into the rock, with an atrium, a vaulted cell and two funeral chambers; in the territory there are also 64 nuraghi and twelve tombs of Giants.

Why go. The oldest tombs in Montiferru.
