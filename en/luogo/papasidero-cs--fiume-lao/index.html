Fiume Lao

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

The Lao runs 51 km from the province of Potenza to the Tyrrhenian Sea near Scalea; in the Natural Reserve of the Valle del Rijeka Lao its rapids, between gorges and canyons, are destination for rafting and canoeing.

Why go. Rafting in the gorges of Pollino.
