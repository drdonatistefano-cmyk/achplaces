Palazzo Simi – Centro operativo per l'Archeologia

History and architecture · Archaeology

The headquarters of the Superintendence was founded in 1999 in the heart of Bari Vecchia, a reference point for the archaeology of the territory: from here starts the project "Underground Bari" that crosses the archaeological subsoil to the Museum of Santa Scolastica.

Why go. The city stratified under the alleys.
