Chiesa di Santa Maria del Ponte

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Built on the Dora to protect travelers and pilgrims, it was enlarged in 1855; inside the choir and gallery of 1840 on four stone columns, in façade a mosaic of 1957.

Why go. The church overlooking the Dora.
