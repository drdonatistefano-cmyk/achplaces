Complesso nuragico di Sa Mandra 'e Sa Giua

History and architecture · Archaeology

Village of huts around a nuraghe with tholos complex of which the central tower remains visible, in the Litterai district; among the huts the house of bread is paved circular environment with seats and bathtub, today attributed to a cult of the waters. The excavations have returned bronzes, basins for fusion and a statue of chief tribe.

Why go. A nuragic village inside the village.
