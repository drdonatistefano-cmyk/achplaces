Rocca Pisana

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Also called Rocca Aldobrandesca: built in the Middle Ages for the Aldobrandeschi and then passed to the Pisani as a watchtower on the coast and the plain, on a site inhabited since the late Bronze Age. Irregular pentagonal plan connected to the walls, three corner towers of different shape (round, triangular, quadrangular), restored at the end of the twentieth century; in summer it hosts events.

Why go. The three-tower fortress on the gulf.
