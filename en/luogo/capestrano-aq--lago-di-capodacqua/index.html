Lago di Capodacqua

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

A small artificial lake formed in 1965 by a dam on the Tirino, called the small Atlantis of Abruzzo: the clear waters cover an ancient mill and its silciati and leave in part emerge a paint factory. A diver's destination; those who do not dive see the mill from an electric boat with glass bottom. Balneation prohibited to protect the bird; open Saturday and Sunday 10.30-15.

Why go. The submerged mill seen from the boat with glass bottom.
