Santa Maria della Pietà in Telusiano e Crocifissione di Lorenzo Lotto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the 14th century restored between 1513 and 1529 by Nicolò Bonafede (1464-1534), bishop of Chiusi, as a family mausoleum. On the high altar the Crucifixion of Lorenzo Lotto (1531), on three levels, with the client in prayer on the left; also late-fiveth century frescoes of Durante Nobili, pupil of Lotto, a stone Pietà of the fifteenth century and a Madonna of Trapani attributed to Nino Pisano. Hours: every day 9-12 and 16-19.

Why go. One of the masterpieces of the Marches Lotto.
