Palazzo Brancaccio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

In 1625, built as a noble residence on the remains of a medieval fortress, it is the heart of the country: the arches of its splendid loggia connect it to the Matrix Church, next to which it rises.

Why go. The loggia that unites palace and mother church.
