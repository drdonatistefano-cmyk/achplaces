Vulcano: Fossa, fanghi e spiagge nere

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Wild island known since ancient times for its thermal baths and healing mud, with the sea heated by fumaroles and black sandy beaches; the crater of the Fossa di Vulcano is the stop not to be missed.

Why go. Get on a crater and get wet between the fumaroles.
