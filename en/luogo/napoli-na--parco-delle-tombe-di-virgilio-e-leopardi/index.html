Parco delle Tombe di Virgilio e Leopardi

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The park on the slopes of Posillipo with the Roman doumbarium traditionally called the tomb of Virgil, the mausoleum of Leopardi of 1939, a stretch of the Augustan aqueduct and the entrance of the Crypta Neapolitana.

Why go. Two poets in a garden.
