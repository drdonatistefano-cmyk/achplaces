MUST - Museo del territorio vimercatese

Museums · City history

Civic museum opened in 2010 in Villa Sottocasa, tells the history, art, economy and society of the Brianza est, between Lambro and Adda, in 14 rooms on two floors (about 1000 square meters): on the ground floor a chronological path from the first Roman settlements to the nineteenth century, on the first floor industry, transformation of the territory and local memory. It exhibits archaeological finds, paintings, 3D models, multimedia installations, photographs and historical movies; in 2012 it received the ICOM Museum of the Year Award for the best Italian setup. Opening hours: Wednesdays and Thursdays 10-13; Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays 10-13 and 15-19.

Why go. Two thousand years of Brianza's history told with award-winning multimedia equipment.
