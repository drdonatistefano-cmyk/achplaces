GeoMuseo del Monte Arci "Stefano Incani"

Museums · Science and university

In the former seventeenth-century convent of the Capuchins, next to the church of San Francesco, it tells the geology of Sardinia and Marmilla, from the formation of the Mediterranean to the evolution of Monte Arci, an ancient volcano today regional park. It exhibits thousands of minerals and about 1,800 fossils, including fossil fish, colored diaspris, hydrothermal minerals such as quartz, limedonia, fluorite and large crystals of calcite, dioramas on the activity of the volcano and a room with fluorescent minerals. The collections are born from the cataloguing work of Vincenzo Incani.

Why go. A journey of over 20 million years in the geology of Marmilla.
