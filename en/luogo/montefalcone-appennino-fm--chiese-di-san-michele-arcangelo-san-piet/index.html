Chiese di San Michele Arcangelo, San Pietro in Penne e Santa Maria in Capite Scalorum

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

St. Michael the Archangel is a Romanesque church with a Greek cross; San Pietro in Penne has farfensi origins; Santa Maria in Capite Scalorum was built around 1300 around a much older aedicule.

Why go. Three medieval churches in a small village.
