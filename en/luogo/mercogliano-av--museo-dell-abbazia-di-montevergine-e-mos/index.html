Museo dell'Abbazia di Montevergine e Mostra del Presepe

Museums · Old masters

The museum preserves the abbey chair of the 12th century with inlays of Arabic taste, the Madonna of St. William of the late 12th century, a wooden crucifix of the 13th century and oriental icons; next to the permanent exhibition of Neapolitan and world nativity scenes from the 17th to the present day.

Why go. Medieval treasures and nativity scenes.
