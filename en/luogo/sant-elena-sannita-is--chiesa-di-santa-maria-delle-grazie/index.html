Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Considered coeval at the birth of the village, wanted by the feudal lords next to the Baronial Palace and originally called Santa Maria in Castello. It collapsed in the earthquake of 1805 and rebuilt in 1826, it has three naves with three apses and frescoes by Mario De Tollis in the aisles.

Why go. The oldest church in the village, next to the baronial palace.
