Chiesa dei Santi Cosma e Damiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest building in Grazzano, with its current appearance due to a 17th century redecoration; inside the chapel of Our Lady of Lourdes with numerous ex-vote.

Why go. The ancient nucleus of the village.
