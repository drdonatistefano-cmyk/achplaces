MA·DE Museo Archeologico Dertona

Museums · Archaeological

In the fifteenth century Palazzo Guidobono, rebuilt with a Gothic façade in 1939-42, tells the ancient Dertona: the sarcophagus of Publius Elio Sabino, the tombstone of soldier Aurelius Veterano, a stele of gens Latinia, a togata statue, two polychrome mosaics and three fistulae of the aqueduct. Open Saturday and Sunday 15.30-19, with free guided tours on Saturday.

Why go. The Roman Tortona in a palace in the center.
