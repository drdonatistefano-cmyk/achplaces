Museo civico etrusco-romano «Gregorio Bianchini»

Museums · Archaeological

On the ground floor of the Town Hall, it exhibits about 350 objects from the Etruscan tombs: buccheri, bronze vases, fibulas, gold and amber ornaments of the VII-VI century BC; there are a large bronze flabello and two amphorae painted by the Annesi Piacentini and the Flabelli tombs, found intact in the 1960s in the necropolis of the Olivetello, a tomb of warrior of the VIII century BC and the remains of two chariots.

Why go. The Etruscans of the lake, perhaps the ancient Sabbath.
