Accademia Nazionale di San Luca

Museums · Old masters

In the Palazzo Carpegna near Fontana di Trevi, the academy born from the ancient University of the Arts of Painting, with the gallery of academic collections of paintings, sculptures and drawings.

Why go. Four centuries of academy.
