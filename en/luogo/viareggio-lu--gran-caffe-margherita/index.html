Gran Caffè Margherita

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Caffè storico frequented by Giacomo Puccini and Arturo Toscanini, designed by architect Alfredo Belluomini with ceramics by Galileo Chini, the first stage designer of Turandot.

Why go. Puccini's coffee.
