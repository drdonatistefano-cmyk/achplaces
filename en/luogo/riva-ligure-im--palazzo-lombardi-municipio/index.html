Palazzo Lombardi (Municipio)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Country residence of the family of Lombardi, today municipal seat: in 1714 Elisabetta Farnese was hosted, betrothed to the king of Spain. In the Town Hall there is the multimedia exhibition space SEM, with the virtual visit of the archaeological area of Costa Balenae.

Why go. A historic residence and the basilica of Costa Balenae in a virtual version.
