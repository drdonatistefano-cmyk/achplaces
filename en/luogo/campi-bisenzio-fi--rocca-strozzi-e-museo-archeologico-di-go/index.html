Rocca Strozzi e Museo archeologico di Gonfienti

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

The fortress of the first fifteenth century on the Bisenzio, with the findings of Gonfienti, one of the major villages of the Bronze Age of northern Italy, then Etruscan and Roman.

Why go. An archaeological stop just a stone's throw from Florence.

  • Where Via Roma 6, Campi Bisenzio
  • Entry From €2 to €4
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com
