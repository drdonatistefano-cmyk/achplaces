Palazzo e Giardini Moroni

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Historical residence of Bergamo Alta with frescoed rooms, period furnishings, an art collection on the Moroni family and terraced gardens; exhibitions in collaboration with the FAI.

Why go. A noble residence with a secret garden.
