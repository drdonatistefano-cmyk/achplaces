Museo diocesano di arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

In the Archbishop's Palace, opened in 1998: sacred art from the High Middle Ages to the twentieth century, the Treasure of the Cathedral with an angioin clasp in gold and enamel, the reliquary arm and the silver statue of St. Anastasia in 1792.

Why go. The treasure of the Cathedral.
