Torre di Galatrona

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Rest of one of the most important castles in the area, documented by 963 and perhaps already Etruscan settlement; the castle, which had two towers, survives the watchtower about 27 meters high, about 500 meters above sea level. Inside a multimedia path on the history of Galatrona and the castle; climbing five floors of stairs you have a 360 degree view of the Valdarno and the Chianti Mountains. Open from April to October, usually on Saturday and Sunday.

Why go. The 360 degree panorama of Valdarno and Chianti.
