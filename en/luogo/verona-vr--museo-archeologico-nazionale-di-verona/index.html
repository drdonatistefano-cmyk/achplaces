Museo Archeologico Nazionale di Verona

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 2022 in the former Habsburg barracks of San Tomaso, next to the homonymous church: the evidence of archaeological research in the city and in the territory of Verona, from prehistory onwards, including the "Sciamano" (Sciamano), perhaps the oldest human figure in the world.
