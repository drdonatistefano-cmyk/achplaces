Tempio italico (Santuario sannitico) di Colle Rimontato

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Sacred area of about 740 m2 fenced by large blocks of stone, in panoramic position on the valley of the Fiumarello and close to the tratturo Castel di Sangro-Lucera: a small temple flanked by two colonnaded arcades, of which remains the high podium with molded frames and the cell with floor in cottage and white tiles. The coins under the cell (the most recent one denarius of 104 B.C.) date from the construction; the lamps attest to the use at least until the second century AD. Discovered in the 1990s, it is still the subject of excavations by the University of Leiden and Wisdom with the Superintendence of Molise.

Why go. A Sannitic sanctuary still in the process of excavation.
