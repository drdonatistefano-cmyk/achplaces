Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta e cripta romanica

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque parish church of the XI-XII centuries in the upper part of Pievebovigliana (a plaque probably bears date 1162), of which remain the eastern apse with monofore and the stone wall; facade, bell tower and interior were renovated from the eighteenth century. The coeva crypt, rediscovered in the twentieth century, has five naves on four rows of columns and three apses, altar columns in piperin, a Roman plaque of the second century A.D. and a fresco of Saint Lucia attributed to Archangel of Cola (1420-25 about): it is among the most famous Romanesque monuments of the Marches.

Why go. One of the most famous Romanesque crypts in the Marche region.
