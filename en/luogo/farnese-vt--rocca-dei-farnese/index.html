Rocca dei Farnese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The noble residence of the 13th and 17th centuries; the chapel has a dome and the coats of arms of the Anguillara, Chigi and Farnese families. It is private but open to visitors.

Why go. The residence of the lords of the village.
