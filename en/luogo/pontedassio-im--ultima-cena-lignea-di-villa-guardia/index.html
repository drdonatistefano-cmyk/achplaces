Ultima Cena lignea di Villa Guardia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Villa Guardia there is a group of wooden statues of the Last Supper of the late 16th century, inspired by the iconography of Leonardo's Cenacle in Santa Maria delle Grazie in Milan and almost identical to the original. It is one of only two three-dimensional copies of the painting, along with another in Lombardy.

Why go. The Last Supper of Leonardo translated into wood carvings.
