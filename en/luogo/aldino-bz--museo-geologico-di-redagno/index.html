Museo GEOlogico di Redagno

Museums · Science and university · UNESCO World Heritage

In the House Peter Rosegger in Redagno di Sopra, at the other end of the GEOPARC: popularizing panels and a stratigraphic column that reproduces the sequence of rocks of the gorge, from the porphyry quartzifer of Bolzano to the sandstone of Val Gardena, to the layers in Bellerophon and Werfen up to the Formation of the Contrin. On Sundays in season guided tours in the gorge start from here.

Why go. Understand the throat before going down there.
