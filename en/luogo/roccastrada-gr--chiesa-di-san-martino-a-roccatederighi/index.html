Chiesa di San Martino a Roccatederighi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On a rocky spur at the southern end of the village, with a single nave with a flat apse and wooden roof; a 1489 plaque on the façade recalls great works, other extensions at the end of the eighteenth century. Inside a baptismal font in marble of the fifteenth century and a sixteenth-century table of Sienese school with the Madonna and Child and Saint Augustine who holds the castle of Roccatederighi.

Why go. The table with the picture of the castle.
