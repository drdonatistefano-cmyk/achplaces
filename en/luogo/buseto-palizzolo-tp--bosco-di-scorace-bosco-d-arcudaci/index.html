Bosco di Scorace (Bosco d'Arcudaci)

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

About 750 hectares once almost all corkscrew, cut down largely after the Second World War and reforested by the Forest Corps with conifers and eucalyptuses; today pines, cypresses, holm oaks and aromatic herbs, with rabbits, hares and istrices. It has parking lots, areas equipped with tables, cooking points and games for children and paths up to a hilly lake; from a hill of 642 meters you can see Segesta with theatre and temple, Monte Inici, Monte Sparagio and the Gulf of Castellammare.

Why go. Picnic and views to Segesta.
