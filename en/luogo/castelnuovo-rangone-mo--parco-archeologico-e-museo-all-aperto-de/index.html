Parco archeologico e Museo all'aperto della Terramara di Montale

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Park of 23,000 square meters dedicated to the terracing, villages of the Bronze Age of the Po Valley arose towards the middle of the II millennium BC and disappeared around 1200 BC. Under a cover are observed the excavations with over four centuries of life of the village; the open-air museum reconstructs life-size moat, embankment with palisade, door and two large houses furnished with copies of pottery, tools and weapons. On opening days 9.30-13.30 and 14.30-18.30, guided tours every 45 minutes, recommended booking.

Why go. Entering a village 3,500 years ago.

  • Where SS 12 Nuova Estense, Montale, 41050 Castelnuovo Rangone (MO)
  • Entry €7, reduced €5 (6-13 years), free up to 5 years and 65 years
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
