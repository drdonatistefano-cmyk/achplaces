Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta a Pisogneto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church built in 1425 and enlarged in the eighteenth century, visited by Saint Charles Borromeo in 1580 and Francesco Morosini in 1594. It preserves a wooden pulpit of 1746, an organ of 1805 and a Madonna with Child carved by the Del Maino brothers.

Why go. Wooden furnishings and a Madonna dei Del Maino.
