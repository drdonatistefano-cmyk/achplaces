Palazzo Cesi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between 1561 and 1579 on a previous fortress, on a project by Guidetto Guidetti and then by Giovan Domenico Bianchi; here lived Federico Cesi, founder of the Accademia dei Lincei (1603), who hosted Galileo in 1624. On the ground floor mythological vaults inspired by the Metamorphosis of Ovid, on the noble floor wooden coffered ceilings (Throne Room, Emperors' Room, Muse); since 1964 it is of the University of Perugia and hosts guided tours, exhibitions and the Museum of Bees and Honey.

Why go. The Lincei Palace where Galileo stayed.

In 2025 began the restoration of the rooms of the noble floor; schedules and tickets on the site of the palace
