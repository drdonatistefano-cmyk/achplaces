Museo Don Nanni Guiso

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Opened in 2000, when Don Nanni (Giovanni) Guiso donated to Orosei the ancient residence of the noble Guiso, then barracks of the Royal Carabinieri, along with his collection: vintage theatres from all over Europe and theatre production from the eighteenth century to today, diorami and graphics of international artists including drawings of the Roman School of 1930-1945, ancient and rare books on Sardinia, dresses and costumes from 1880 to today and works of art from Sienese. From May to September open Monday, Tuesday and Friday 9-13 and Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 17-21; from October to April only by reservation.

Why go. The miniature theatres of half of Europe in a noble palace.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Musio 22, Orosei
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it
