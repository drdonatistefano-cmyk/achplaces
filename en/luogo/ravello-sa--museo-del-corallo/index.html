Museo del corallo

Museums · Themed · UNESCO World Heritage

Born in 1986 on the initiative of Giorgio Filocamo, a coral craftsman of Sicilian-Campana origin, exhibits ancient objects from the family collection worked according to the coral tradition.

Why go. The art of coral in Piazza Duomo.
