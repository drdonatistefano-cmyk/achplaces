Museo-laboratorio dell'Appennino Umbro «Sotto-Sopra»

Museums · Science and university

Set up inside the fortress, in the center of Polino, it tells with interactive multimedia models and systems the evolutionary history of Umbria, from geology and paleontology to natural sciences: carbonate rocks, fossils of ammonites and formation of the Umbrian mountains. It has an educational workshop on the ammonites and the information point • The window on the Black • • with fossil extraction activities in a small nearby quarry.

Why go. Touch the fossils of the Apennines with hands.
