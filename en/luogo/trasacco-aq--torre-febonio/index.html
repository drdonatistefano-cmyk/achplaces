Torre Febonio

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

27.50 meters high, square for two thirds and cylindrical at the top, already remembered in 999 in the Chronica Farfense; it was seen on Lake Fucino and night lighthouse for fishermen, and takes its name from the Febonio family that restored it. Damaged by the earthquake of 1915, restored in the seventies and in 2003; an internal wooden staircase leads to the summit with a view of the Fucino.

Why go. From the beacon tower of the lake to the panorama of the plain.
