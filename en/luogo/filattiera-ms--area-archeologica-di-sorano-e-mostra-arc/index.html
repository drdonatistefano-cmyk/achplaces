Area archeologica di Sorano e mostra «Archeologia a Filattiera»

History and architecture · Archaeology

The site tells of the human presence in Val di Magra, from the Ligurians Apuani to the Romans, from the Byzantines and Longobards to the medieval pilgrims, thanks to excavations initiated by Tiziano Mannoni with the Institute of Material Culture of Genoa and the Superintendence. Panels and casts of statues-stele accompany the visit; the exhibition curated by Enrico Giannicedda (inaugurated in October 2023) exhibits finds of almost six thousand years, including statues-stele, urns, Roman crockery, medieval weapons, coins and keys. Show available by reservation.

Why go. Six thousand years of Lunigiana in a hall.
