Parco archeologico di Pezza Petrosa

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Just over 2 km from the village, it occupies about 5,000 m2, of which at least 1,000 are a necropolis with 33 intact tombs, different in shape, covering, covering and orientation. The remains of a city walls and an inhabited area have also emerged; the furnishings indicate settlements since the Neolithic period. The site began to depopulate with the Roman conquest and was destroyed in the 5th century AD during the wars between Goths and Byzantines.

Why go. To walk through the tombs of an ancient town of the Murgia.
