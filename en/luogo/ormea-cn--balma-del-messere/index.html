Balma del Messere

History and architecture · Archaeology

Also known as the Saracen Cave, it is a natural cave at the foot of a rocky wall closed by a wall in stone and mortar of the tenth century, often about 70 cm at the base, tied to Saracen raids; the excavations of 1979-1982 found traces of human presence in the Neolithic. Visit Cuneese indicates it as the largest fortified cave in the Western Alps.

Why go. A walled cave of a thousand years ago.
