Convento di Montefiorentino e Cappella Oliva

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Franciscan monastery founded in 1248, one of the largest in the Marche region. The Chapel of Counts Oliva, with inscription of 1484 and probably designed by Simone Ferrucci, houses the tombs of Gianfrancesco Oliva and Marsibilia Trinci by Francesco di Simone Ferrucci, the altarpiece with Madonna and Child and saints by Giovanni Santi, father of Raffaello (1489), and the kneelings inlaid by Zocchini (1493).

Why go. A Renaissance jewel signed by Raffaello's father.
