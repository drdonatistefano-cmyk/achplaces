Torre medievale e museo della parrocchiale

History and architecture · Towers

A circular tower probably in the mid-15th century, restored in the nineties, which houses the museum of the old parish church of Santa Maria Assunta destroyed by the earthquake of 1915: furnishings of the church, sculptures and sacred vestments of over three centuries.

Why go. The tower and the treasures of the lost church.

open only in summer (July Saturday and Sunday, August every day, 16:30-19:30)
