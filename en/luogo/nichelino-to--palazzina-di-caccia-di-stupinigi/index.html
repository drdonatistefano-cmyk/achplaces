Palazzina di Caccia di Stupinigi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Hunting and party residence designed by Filippo Juvarra since 1729 for Vittorio Amedeo II, crowned by the deer on the dome; since 1919 it houses the Museum of Art and Furniture, today of the Foundation Ordine Mauriziano. UNESCO World Heritage.

Why go. Juvarra's masterpiece.
