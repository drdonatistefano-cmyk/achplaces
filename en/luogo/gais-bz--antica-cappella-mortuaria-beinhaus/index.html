Antica cappella mortuaria (Beinhaus)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The ancient ossuary has been transformed into a small museum: at the center a cycle of late-Gothic frescoes of the Last Judgment, with other paintings on the redemption of souls who were a catechism for the faithful who could not read. In the showcase are the precious vestments called Sonnenburg and, in a reliquary, the entire skeleton of St. Constantine II. Opening on request.

Why go. Late-gothic frescoes in a collected chest.
