Museo degli scavi di Piuro

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the sacristies of the church of Sant'Abbondio tells the Pirus before and after the landslide: a bell melted in 1398, a seat and a pool in ollar stone, a silver head of Galeazzo Maria Sforza, pins, buckles and buttons in copper, swords, rifles and iron lights, and a room with the excavations of 1988 in wood, iron, leather and copper. Open from June to September on Saturday and Sunday 15-17; free admission with the ticket of Palazzo Vertemate Franchi or the Waterfalls.

Why go. The objects of the ancient buried Pirus.
