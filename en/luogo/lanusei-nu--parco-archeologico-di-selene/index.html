Parco archeologico di Selene

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the granite plateau of Selene, almost 1,000 meters in a wood of holm oaks, chestnuts and oaks about 6 km from the village: remains the base of the nuraghe Gennacili, surrounded by about 200 huts and defensive walls, with two tombs of giants about 80 meters from each other and sacred sources. The smaller Tomb I has central stele and exedra with seat and chamber of about 7 meters of the fifteenth century BC; the Tomb II, about 12 by 5 meters, had a crowning pyramid trunk with three holes for the betiles (XIV-XII century BC). Picnic area; time indicated by MiC: summer 10-13 and 16-20, winter 10-13 and 15-sunset, closed on Mondays.

Why go. Nuraghe, village and tombs of giants in a mountain forest.
