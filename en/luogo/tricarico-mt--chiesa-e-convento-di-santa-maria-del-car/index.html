Chiesa e convento di Santa Maria del Carmine

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Convent of the barefoot Carmelites founded in 1605 in an area rich in water and green. In 1612 Pietro Antonio Ferro, an exponent of Mannerism and one of the most representative artists of the seventeenth-century Lucan, frescoed the vault of the apse with stories of Christ and Our Lady, saints, prophets and virtues; the cloister is frescoed by his sons Carlo and Giovan Battista.

Why go. The great cycle of Pietro Antonio Ferro.
