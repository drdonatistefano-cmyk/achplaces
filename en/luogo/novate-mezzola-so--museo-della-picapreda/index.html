Museo della picapreda

Museums · Themed

Images, tools and testimonials of the granite stonemasons Sanfedelino, which takes its name from the first open quarry next to the Sanctuary of San Fedele and paved streets and squares of Milan, Como, Pavia and Bologna: in the five years before the Great War, more than 82,000 tons of stone left the station of Novate, and between the twenties and thirty the quarries were twenty; today one remains active. Open a few days a week; guided tours by reservation.

Why go. The stone that paved the cities of the North.
