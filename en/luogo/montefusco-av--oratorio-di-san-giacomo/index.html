Oratorio di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient crypt of the church of Santa Maria della Piazza, one of the oldest religious buildings in the country; in 1652 it was donated to the Confraternity of San Giacomo, which had it restored. The 17th-century frescoes depict scenes from the Old and New Testaments, the glory of Mary on the vault and Saint Catherine of Alexandria on the bottom; the trapdoor 'pro confratibus' towards the ancient sepulchre is closed after the 1980 earthquake. Visit by reservation.

Why go. A small chest of frescoes behind a wooden door.
