Acropoli e mura ciclopiche di Circei

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the cliff overlooking the village, the acropolis was the last stronghold of the city of Circei; polygonal walls are preserved on top and in the historic center, dated from the sources between the VI-IV century BC. (VisitLatium) and the beginning of the third century B.C. (Circle Park).

Why go. The megalithic walls of Circe.
