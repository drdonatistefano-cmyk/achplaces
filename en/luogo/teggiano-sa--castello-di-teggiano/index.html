Castello di Teggiano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress of probably Norman origin of the Sanseverino, strengthened by Ferrante d'Aragona after the Conspiracy of the Barons: pentagonal belt with angular towers, the mast and the Tower of the Cimmaruca; from the terrace you can see the whole Vallo di Diano. Private property, partly exhibition space; in August the re-enactment of the siege of 1497.

Why go. The castle of the Baron Conspiracy.
