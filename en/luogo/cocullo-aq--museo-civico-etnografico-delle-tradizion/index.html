Museo civico etnografico delle tradizioni popolari «Alfonso Maria di Nola»

Museums · Themed

The museum and documentation centre, housed in the premises adjacent to the Town Hall, contains testimonies and images of the feast of San Domenico and the Serpari. The Study Centre has a large library dedicated to the most popular events in Abruzzo and organizes educational activities for schools and visitors. Times not indicated by sources: contact the Municipality (tel. This appropriation is intended to cover the costs of the implementation of the programme for the period from 1 January 2014 to 31 December 2014.

Why go. To understand the rite of snakes all year round, not only on May 1.
