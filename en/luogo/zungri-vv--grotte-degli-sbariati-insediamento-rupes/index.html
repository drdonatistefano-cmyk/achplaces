Grotte degli Sbariati - Insediamento rupestre

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Rock village of about 3,000 square meters formed by dozens of units partly dug into the rock and partly built, used as houses, shelters for animals, for the production of wine and lime and as granaries; the oldest traces seem to date back to the VIII-XII centuries. The hypothesis about origin are two: a colony of oriental populations or a productive outpost or deposit of the nearby Kastron of Mesiano. Guided tours by reservation (info@grottezungri.it, 377 441 9886); not recommended to those who have difficulty in getting rid of steps and uneven and steep terrain.

Why go. The small Calabrian Petra, an entire village dug into the rock.

  • Where Via Indipendenza, Zungri
  • Entry €4, reduced €3 (groups over 20 people)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
