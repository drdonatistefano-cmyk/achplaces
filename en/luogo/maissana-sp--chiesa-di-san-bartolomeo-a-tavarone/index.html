Chiesa di San Bartolomeo a Tavarone

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish, then dedicated to St. Sirus and belonging to the diocese of Brugnato, is already attested in 1143 and appears in the Eximum of the churches of the bishopric of Brugnato in 1452. Joined in San Pietro in Frascati in 1498, it passed to the diocese of Genoa in 1519 and was reconstituted parish in 1715 by Cardinal Lorenzo Fieschi; since 1892 it was of the diocese of Chiavari and since 1959 of Spezia. N. S. of the Belt on the second Sunday of July; festive Mass at 11.

Why go. A parish documented for almost nine centuries.
