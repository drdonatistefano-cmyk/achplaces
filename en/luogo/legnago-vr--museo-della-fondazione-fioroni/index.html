Museo della Fondazione Fioroni

Museums · House museums

House-museum in the Floroni-Accordi palace, built on the new avenues after the demolition of the walls: the collection of Risorgimento relics collected by the Fioroni family from the last decades of the nineteenth century was exhibited in the early twenties of the twentieth century in rooms furnished with furniture, tapestries and chandeliers of epoch. Eight rooms retracing the Unity from the Napoleonic epic to the myth of Garibaldi; the ground floor tells the Medieval and Renaissance Legnago with weapons and ceramics, and in the courtyard there are the lapidary, one of the marching lions and the portal of the birthplace of Salieri. Open Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 9-12, Wednesday and Sunday 15-19.

Why go. The Risorgimento in an atmosphere house-museum.
