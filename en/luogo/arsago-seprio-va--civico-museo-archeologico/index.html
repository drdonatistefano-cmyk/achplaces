Civico Museo Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

Born in 1983 in the middle school, after the discovery in 1972 of a Lombard necropolis during the expansion of the building; closed for flooding in 1991, it reopened definitively in 1998. Three rooms with finds from the Bronze Age to the Renaissance (also from the Roman necropolis of the vicus), a paleontological room with the collection G. C. Politi (more than 400 pieces, including fossils of Solnhofen and Bolca), a stone-stone under the porch and in the garden the Longobard graves, indicated as the only Lombard necropolis preserved on sight in Italy. Open Saturday 14.30-18.30 and Sunday 10-12 and 14.30-18.30; closed in August, from 20 to 31 December, from 1 to 6 January and at Easter. Accessible.

Why go. Longobard graves in open air, next to Roman finds.
