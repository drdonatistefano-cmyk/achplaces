Ex impianto idrovoro di Marozzo e Casa della Memoria

Museums · Themed

The ancient reclamation plant, which was abandoned in 1986, houses the herbarium of the territory and a documentation centre accessible to people with motor and visual disabilities.

Why go. To understand how the land of the delta was born.
