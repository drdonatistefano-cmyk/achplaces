Casa Museo Nena

Museums · House museums

The Palazzo Boschi-Rivani-Garagnani in Ceretolo, documented from the late seventeenth century and opened as a museum house since 2020: three floors of furniture, paintings, ceramics and family memories, with kitchens, loggias, gardens and chapel.

Why go. A home lived, not a cold museum.
