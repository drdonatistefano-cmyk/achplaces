Santa Maria in Pantano (Santa Maria delle Sibille)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church whose foundation dates back to the eighth century, with origins still wrapped in legend; it preserves frescoes from the second decade of the seventeenth century by Martino Bonfini da Patrignone depicting five Sibyls: Cumana, Ellespontica, Agrippina, Frigia and Delfica.

Why go. The Sibyls painted in the land of the Sibyl.

Park warns that descriptions are prior to the 2016 earthquake and some areas may be banned
