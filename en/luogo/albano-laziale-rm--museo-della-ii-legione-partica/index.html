Museo della II Legione Partica

Museums · Archaeological

Set up in the spa area built by Caracalla for legionaries, it exhibits Roman finds and reproductions of the military equipment of the legion dedicated to Albano from 202 A.D. Sunday opens the 2nd and 4th of the month, alternating with the Civic Museum.

Why go. The life of the Roman legionnaire a stone's throw from the camp.
