Museo della Focarazza di Santa Caterina

Museums · Themed

In the former shop of the smith ferraio of the hamlet, documents the work and rites of the fire and the tree of the Amiata in two sections: tools for forging and objects of domestic fire, and the Focarazza, the rite for Saint Catherine of Alexandria of the evening of November 24, when the men of the districts contend between smoke and heat the "stollo," the pole of cerro in the center of the cadasta. Photos, folk art, books and a collection of traditional children's games.

Why go. The rite of the most felt fire of the Amiata.
