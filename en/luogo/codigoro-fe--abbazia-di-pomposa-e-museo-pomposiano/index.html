Abbazia di Pomposa e Museo pomposiano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Benedictine Abbey was born around the sixth century on the ancient Pomposia Insula: the church of Santa Maria with frescoes, the bell tower visible from afar and the museum. Here the monk Guido invented the modern musical notation.

Why go. A masterpiece of the Romanesque between Byzantine and Padano.

  • Where Via Pomposa Centro 12, Codigoro
  • Entry €5 full, €2 reduced; free on the first Sunday of the month; only electronic payments
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website cultura.gov.it
