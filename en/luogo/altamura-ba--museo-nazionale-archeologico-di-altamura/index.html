Museo nazionale archeologico di Altamura

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Opened in 1993, on two floors and six sections from the Paleolithic to the Middle Ages: the civilizations of the Upper Murgia, the origins of bread and a plan dedicated to the European Paleolithic, with the life-size reconstruction of the Man of Altamura presented in 2017.

Why go. The face of the man of Neanderthal of Lamalunga.

  • Where Via Santeramo 88, Altamura
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
