Pasticciotti di carne e prodotti tipici

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

In the main bakeries you will find meat, round and veal pastries and almonds; the territory produces Mamertino DOC wine and Valdemone DOP oil.

Why go. Patti's typical sweet-salate.
