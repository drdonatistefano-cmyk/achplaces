Museo storico della Resistenza di Sasso

Museums · City history · Paid entry

It reconstructs with panels, maps and films the facts from 30 June to 7 July 1944, the German rake with killings of civilians to the east of Cisa: the partisan band, the mountain population that suffered reprisals and requisitions, the context of the massacres of summer 1944. Open from April to November by reservation.

Why go. The summer of 1944 in Apennines.
