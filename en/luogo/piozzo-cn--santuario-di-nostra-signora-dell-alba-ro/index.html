Santuario di Nostra Signora dell'Alba Rosa

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Small temple behind the Orange Giant bench, with a large view of the Tanaro valley and the nearby hills of the Langhe.

Why go. The view over the Tanaro and the Langhe.
