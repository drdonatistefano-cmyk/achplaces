Museo civico Luigi Mallé

Museums · Old masters

In the family house left as a legacy by art historian Luigi Mallé, who died in 1979, with his collections ordered by historical period: Flemish paintings, eighteenth century pastels, ceramics, vases and vintage photographs; on the ground floor the Civic Library. Open Saturday, Sunday and public holidays from 15 to 19.

Why go. The private collection of an art historian.
