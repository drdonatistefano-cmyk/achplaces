Museo d'Arte Carlin Bergoglio (Galleria d'arte moderna e contemporanea del Canavese)

Museums · Modern art

Inaugurated in 2014 in the former Manufacture, it is dedicated to the painter, journalist and caricaturist Carlo Bergoglio named Carlin (1895-1959): about 200 paintings, caricatures and personal objects, with many subjects of Canaves' rural life and sports caricatures for Guerin Sportivo, Gazzetta del Popolo and Tuttosport. It also includes the Clizia Collection, 100 works by Clizia ceramist. Open Monday to Friday 9-17.

Why go. Carlin's sports cartoons, the signature of Turin's journalism.
