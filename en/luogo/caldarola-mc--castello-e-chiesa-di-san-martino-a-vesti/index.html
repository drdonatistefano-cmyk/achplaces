Castello e chiesa di San Martino a Vestignano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Castle donated by Ludovico II to the monastery of Casauria and then passed to the Varano; the annexed church, of thirteenth-century origin, has frescoes of the first fifteenth-century of the Master of Rasiglia and paintings of Nobile da Lucca, Giovanni and Simone De Magistris of the first half of the sixteenth century.

Why go. A cycle of frescoes in a castle in the woods.
