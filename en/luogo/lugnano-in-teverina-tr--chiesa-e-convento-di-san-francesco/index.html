Chiesa e convento di San Francesco

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Built in 1229 where in 1212 St. Francis preached and performed the miracle of the child freed from the wolf: the fresco of the miracle, repainted in the seventeenth century, revealed under a fresco fourteenth century intact. The cloister of 1608 has travertine columns and frescoes on the life of the saint; the baroque bell tower dates back to 1680. In the convent, just outside the old town, Blessed Gonella da Norcia died in 1540.

Why go. The miracle of the frescoed wolf twice.
