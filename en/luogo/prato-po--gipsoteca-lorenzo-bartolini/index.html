Gipsoteca Lorenzo Bartolini

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Inside the Museum of Palazzo Pretorio the gipsoteca of the plaster models of the sculptor Lorenzo Bartolini.

Why go. The laboratory of a sculptor from the 19th century.

  • Where Piazza del Comune, Prato
  • Entry Included in the ticket of the Museum of Palazzo Pretorio
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
