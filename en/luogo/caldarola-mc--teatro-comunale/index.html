Teatro comunale

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built in wood in 1823 inside the then municipal palace and opened in Carnival 1830: 22 stages for the families of the village and two public, the pontifical governor and the magistrate.

Why go. A small 19th-century wooden theatre.
