Fortezza di Mont'Alfonso

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built between 1579 and 1586 for Alfonso II d'Este on a medieval village, the last bulwark of the Duchy of Ferrara on the border with Lucca; in the Napoleonic age passed to the Principality of Lucca and Piombino and in the twentieth century was summer residence of the Italian-Scotland family Bechelli, who transformed a building into a liberty villa. Since 1980 it has been of the Province of Lucca and houses provincial offices and the Park of the Apuan Alps: about 1,150 meters of wall with 7 bastions enclose the house of the captain and the quarters of the troops of the sixteenth century, on about 60,000 m2. Exhibitions and events, including the festival "Mont'Alfonso" under the stars "Stars," and 360 degree views of the Tuscan-Emilian Apennines, Pania and Apuane.

Why go. The last expansive fortress, with views of Apuane and Apennines.
