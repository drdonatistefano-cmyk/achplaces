Bottega dei vasai della famiglia Bozzi

Local life and traditions · Crafts

The art of potters is documented in Montottone already between 1200 and 1300; the emigration of the Sixties and Seventies drastically reduced the number and today only one workshop, that of the family Bozzi. The clay is handmade, the objects are modeled on the footwork and baked in a wood-burning oven with traditional paints.

Why go. The last workshop in the country of potters.
