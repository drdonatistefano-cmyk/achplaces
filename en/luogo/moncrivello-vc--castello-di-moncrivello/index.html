Castello di Moncrivello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built in a dominant position with the horizon up to Novara and Canavese, with a double circle of walls; the origins date back to around the Mille and in 1152 Frederick Barbarossa assigned it to the bishop of Vercelli. After the Fieschis (1394) and the Savoys, at the end of the fifteenth century the Duchess Jolanda transformed it from a Romanesque fortress into a Renaissance residence and died there in 1478; in 1650 she rejected a Spanish attack. Damaged by a fire at the beginning of the 19th century and abandoned, it has been restored since 1972 and opened to the public in 1996.

Why go. The residence of Jolanda di Savoia with view from the Canavese to Novara.
