Palazzo Taffini d'Acceglio e Múses - Accademia Europea delle Essenze

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

Baroque palace built around 1630 for the Marquis Taffini d'Acceglio: the Aula Regia is frescoed with the military campaigns of Vittorio Amedeo I of Savoia by artists of the circle of Giovanni Antonio Molineri, including Jean Claret, and the Rococo salons were wanted by Isabella of Savoia-Carignano. Host Múses, a techno-sensory museum on Piedmontese aromatic herbs and the history of perfumery, with multisensory installations and works by contemporary artists. Open Thursday-Saturday 14-18, Sunday 11-13 and 14-18.

Why go. Baroque frescoes and a perfume museum.

  • Where Via Sant'Andrea 53, Savigliano
  • Entry €12, reduced €8
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
