Riserva regionale Abetina di Laurenzana

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Reserve in the municipal territory, inside the Lucano Apennines National Park, dominated by the rare white fir with beech and oaks; it hosts rare fauna, it is open all year round, it is a frequent destination for schoolchildren and inside it is home to the ecomuseum center of the white fir.

Why go. The woods of the white fir.
