Basilica di San Martino

Places of worship · Basilicas

Rococo church started on May 5, 1747 on a project by Giovanni Mariani, then revised by Giuseppe Morgese, and consecrated on October 22, 1775; the façade in 37 meters high limestone has the high relief of St. Martin who divides the cloak with the poor. Inside the main altar of Sanmartino and Varriale (1773) with the Saint Martin of Stephen of Putignano (1518) and the statues of Abundance and Charity, the Christ to the wooden Column of 1622 and the Hat of the Sacrament with paintings by Domenico Carella; Minor Basilica since 1998.

Why go. The masterpiece of the martinese baroque, inside and outside.
