Ponte: borgo e chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

History and architecture · Villages

The church of Santa Maria Assunta was probably built in 1201 on a previous Lombard church; the castle remains a gateway and some defensive structures, including the fortress on top of the hill, and the old town hall has the coat of arms of Bridge on the portal.

Why go. Romanesque and castle remains in a hamlet of Valnerina.
