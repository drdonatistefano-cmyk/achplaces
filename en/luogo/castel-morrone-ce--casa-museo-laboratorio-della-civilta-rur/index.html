Casa museo laboratorio della civiltà rurale

Museums · Themed

The museum recovers, preserves and enhances the testimonies of the rural life of Campania, with attention to the Morronese territory, with the aim of an ethnographic museum in part outdoors and for didactic purposes; the research study center and demonstrates with tools of traditional vintage techniques, including food, and promotes the regional craftsmanship of wood, ceramics, iron, goldsmiths, furniture and fabrics.

Why go. The peasant memory of the Morronese territory.
