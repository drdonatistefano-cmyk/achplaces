Museo dei Misteri

Museums · Themed

It exhibits the thirteen "ingenious processionals" created by Paolo Saverio Di Zinno (1718-1781), who parade through the city on the day of Corpus Domini, with costumes of the fifties, vintage films and a screening room; edited by the Association Mysteries and Traditions.

Why go. The Corpus Domini cars.
