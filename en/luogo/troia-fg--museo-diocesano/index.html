Museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters

It is located in the sixteenth-century monastery of San Benedetto and exhibits architectural elements and statues of the ancient marble furnishings of the cathedral, partly rebuilt, bronzes, paintings of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries and sculptural groups from various town churches. Guided tours available.

Why go. The lost marbles of the cathedral.
