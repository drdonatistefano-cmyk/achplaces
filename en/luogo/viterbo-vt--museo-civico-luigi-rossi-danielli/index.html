Museo Civico «Luigi Rossi Danielli»

Museums · City history

In a former monastery of the 12th century next to Santa Maria della Verità, inaugurated in 1912: three floors from the Etruscan period to the 19th century, with Etruscan finds, memories of the city and nineteenth-century projects of the Santa Rosa Machine.

Why go. From the Etruscans to the modern Viterbo.
