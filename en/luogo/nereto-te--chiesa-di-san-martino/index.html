Chiesa di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Benedictine building a little outside the center, indicated as one of the oldest churches in Abruzzo, with simple stone façade; above the portal two small bas-reliefs, the patron who cuts the cloak to give it to a poor and an Agnus Dei.

Why go. One of the oldest churches in Abruzzo.
