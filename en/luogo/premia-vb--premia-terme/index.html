Premia Terme

Local life and traditions · Spas

The spa opened in 2008 in Cadarese, indicated as the most important in northern Piedmont: the spring water, classified hyperthermal sulphate-calcic, flows at 44 °C. It offers thermal baths, spa, beauty treatments and medical treatment for specific pathologies.

Why go. Natural hot water at the foot of the mountains.
