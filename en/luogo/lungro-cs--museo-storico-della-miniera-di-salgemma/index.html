Museo storico della Miniera di Salgemma

Museums · Themed

The mine, already mentioned by Pliny the Elder and exploited by Greeks, Romans and Normans, then became Royal Mine of Salgemma and closed in 1976; today of the Municipality, it has been restored and since 2010 it houses the museum with documents and finds.

Why go. One of the oldest salt mines in Europe.
