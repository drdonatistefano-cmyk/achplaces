Chiesa di Santa Maria dei Monti

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

On a hill just outside the village, reached by a characteristic staircase; the legend wants it erected in the fifteenth century after the Virgin expelled the evil spirits that tormented the shepherds. It preserves frescoes and since 1479 it is the destination of a solemn procession every year.

Why go. Frescoes and a procession since 1479.
