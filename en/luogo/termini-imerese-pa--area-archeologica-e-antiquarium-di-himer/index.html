Area archeologica e Antiquarium di Himera

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Greek colony founded in 648 B.C. by Greeks of calcidese and Doric origin at the mouth of the Northern Impera, the most western of the north coast of Sicily, destroyed by the Carthaginians in 409 B.C.: remain the high and low city, archaic shrines, the necropolis and the Temple of Victory, erected after the battle of 480 B.C. The Antiquarium of Franco Minissi (opened in 1984) exhibits on four levels the excavations, with the gold foil with Gorgone from the Shrine of Athena and the Golden Phial of Caltavuturo (IV-III century BC); the Museum Pirro Marconi (2016) is dedicated to the Western necropolis and the Temple of Victory.

Why go. The Greek colony of Himera and its temple of victory.
