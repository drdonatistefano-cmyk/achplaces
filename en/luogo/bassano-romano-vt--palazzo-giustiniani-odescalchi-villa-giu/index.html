Palazzo Giustiniani Odescalchi (Villa Giustiniani)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Born from the reconstruction of the castle of the Anguillara, of which remain basement and ground floor, was purchased by the Justinians in 1595: the Marquis Vincenzo added the noble floor connected to the Italian garden with fountains, avenues and water games; the Odescalchi gave it to the State in 2003. On the sides of the portal four marble heads of the II century A.D.; in the halls the Fall of Fetonte by Francesco Albani (1609), the Hall of Love and Psyche by Bernardo Castello (1605), the Hall of Eternal Happiness by Paolo Guidotti (1610), the Camerino di Diana di Domenichino (1609) and decorations by Antonio Tempesta; on the ground floor a private theater with wooden boxes, under renovation. Open Mar-dom 8.30-12.30, Monday closed; booking not required.

Why go. The frescoes of Albani and Domenichino.
