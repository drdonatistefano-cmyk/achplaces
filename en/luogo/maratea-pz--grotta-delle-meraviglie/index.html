Grotta delle Meraviglie

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Discovered in 1929 in Marina di Maratea, it has a room of 70 meters for 20 with stalactites and stalagmites and is considered one of the smallest and best preserved tourist caves in Italy.

Why go. A miniature tourist cave.
