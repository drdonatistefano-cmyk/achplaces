Castel del Monte

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built by Federico II from 1240 on a hill 540 meters on the western Murge: octagonal plan with eight towers on the edges and eight rooms per floor, monofore windows on the ground floor and bifore on the upper floor, except the trifora facing Andria; three spiral staircases of 44 steps, facades in limestone and white marble and an arc portal with classic tympanum. UNESCO heritage since 1996.

Why go. Federico II's octagon.
