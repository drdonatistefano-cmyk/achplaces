Dolmen di San Silvestro

History and architecture · Archaeology

Among the olive groves of the coastal Murgia, about 6 km from the center, was discovered in 1961 demolishing a pile of stones. Originally at mound, with a diameter of 40 meters, it is built dry and crossed by a long and narrow corridor; on the original deposition plane were found the remains of 13 individuals with fragments of the set, testifying to the human presence in the area since the Bronze Age. Tel. 080 2254581.

Why go. A megalithic tomb of the Bronze Age in the middle of olive trees.
