Riserva naturale Grotta di Sant'Angelo Muxaro

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Reserve of 21 hectares established in 2000 and managed by Legambiente Sicilia: it protects the Grotto of Sant'Angelo Muxaro, also called Cave Ciavuli, in total 1,760 meters long on two branches and reached by the entrance hall with an almost flat path, and the Infantino Hooker, with a vertical well of about 10 meters. Host amphibians, reptiles and bats.

Why go. A karst cave about two kilometers long.
