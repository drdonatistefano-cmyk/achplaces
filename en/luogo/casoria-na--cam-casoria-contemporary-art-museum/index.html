CAM Casoria Contemporary Art Museum

Museums · Modern art

Founded in 2005 and directed by Antonio Manfredi, it is a center of research, production and exhibition of contemporary art with painting, sculpture, photography, video, multimedia art and installations, a section on Neapolitan art from the second post-war to today and one on contemporary African art. After more than a year of closure, it reopened on 17 January 2025 in completely renovated spaces: 2,000 sqm exhibition, a permanent collection of more than 2,200 works, concert area, theatre space, art library and area for temporary exhibitions.

Why go. One of Napoletano's greatest contemporary art collections.
