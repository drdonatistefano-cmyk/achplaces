Villa Baldironi Reati

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa probably from the early sixteenth century, originally with family chapel and a large park, transformed several times over the centuries. The ground floor was restored and reopened in 2001, the first floor in 2007; today civil weddings are celebrated.

Why go. A historic villa in the center, to be seen on the opening days.

open to the public only on special occasions (FAI)
