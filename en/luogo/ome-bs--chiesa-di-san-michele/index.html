Chiesa di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

It rises on the homonymous hill in Goiane and preserves most of the original characters: it was born from a primitive chapel with a single nave, probably Romanesque, with a nave in three bays and a quadrangular presbytery turned into a barrel; between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries were added bell tower, altar, sacristy and guardian's house. It preserves frescoes from the XV and XVI centuries and votive decorations of local artisans; behind the church a park with tables and benches, and on clear days the view reaches up to the Apennines.

Why go. Four-five-century frescoes and views of the Po Valley.
