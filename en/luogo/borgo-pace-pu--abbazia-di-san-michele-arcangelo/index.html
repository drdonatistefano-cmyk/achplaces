Abbazia di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

An ancient Benedictine complex not far from the town of Lamoli, where the monks kept the knowledge about dye and medicinal plants; in the cloister is the Museum of natural colors.

Why go. The monastery of colors.
