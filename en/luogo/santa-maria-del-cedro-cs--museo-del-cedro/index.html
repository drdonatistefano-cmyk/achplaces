Museo del Cedro

Museums · Themed

In the Palazzo Gabriele Marino, he tells with educational panels and multimedia stations the history of the Cedar of Calabria DOP, the collection, the processing and the ritual use in Judaism; from the museum start an art journey in the historic center and an archaeological towards Laos.

Why go. The fruit that gives the name to the country.
