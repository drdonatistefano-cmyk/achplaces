Castello di Massafra

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The castle is mentioned for the first time in a Longobard document of the tenth century and was certainly a fortified building in Norman age. Today it houses the Historical and Archaeological Museum of the Civilization of Oil and Wine.

Why go. The medieval castle overlooking the ancient center.
