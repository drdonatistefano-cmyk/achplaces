Palazzo Milzetti, Museo nazionale dell'età neoclassica

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

One of the most important neoclassical residences in Italy, with rooms decorated by Felice Giani for the Milzetti family in the Napoleonic age.

Why go. It is part of a neoclassical itinerary that includes the Masini Theatre and the Laderchi Palace.

  • Where Via Tonducci 15, Faenza
  • Entry €5 full, €7 with exhibition, €2 from 18 to 25 years, free under 18 and the first Sunday of the month (afternoon); FaenzaMuseiCard
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website palazzomilzetti.cultura.gov.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
