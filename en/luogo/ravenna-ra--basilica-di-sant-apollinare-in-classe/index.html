Basilica di Sant'Apollinare in Classe

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Consecrated in 549 by Archbishop Maximiano, in Classe: the green apse with Saint Apollinare among the lambs is one of the sereneest visions of the Ravenna mosaics.

Why go. It combines with the Classis Museum and the area of the ancient port, a few minutes away.

  • Where Via Romea Sud 224, Classe (Ravenna)
  • Entry In the state cumulative of €14 (with National Museum and state monuments); free on the first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website cultura.gov.it
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets
