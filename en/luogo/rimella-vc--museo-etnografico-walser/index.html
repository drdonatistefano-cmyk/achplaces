Museo etnografico walser

Museums · Themed

Set up in the 17th century Casa Eredi Vasina, it tells of the Walser colonization and the daily life of a time with reconstructed environments, original objects and panels: crafts, mountain agriculture, religious and ritual customs, family structure and the Walser language, the "titsch" . Visits by reservation (tel. 0163 55203).

Why go. The language and life of the Walser of Rimella.
