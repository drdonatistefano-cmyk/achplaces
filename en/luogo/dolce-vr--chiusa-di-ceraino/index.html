Chiusa di Ceraino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Remains of an Austrian fortress on the Adige, where the valley is narrowed over the river, used for the control of goods and the payment of duties; near stands the Fort Ceraino, part of the network of Austrian and Italian forts that controlled the valley.

Why go. The narrow Adige, a disputed passage for centuries.
