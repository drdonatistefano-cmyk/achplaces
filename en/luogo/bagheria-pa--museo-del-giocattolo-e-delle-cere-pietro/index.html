Museo del giocattolo e delle cere Pietro Piraino

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Porcelain dolls, automata and ancient mechanical toys, exhibited in the eighteenth century Villa Cutò (Aragona Cutò), recognizable for the elegant five-arched loggia. Times indicated by the source: Tuesday-Friday 9-13 and 15.30-18.30, Saturday and Sunday 9-13, Monday closed.

Why go. Toys and vintage automatons in a 18th century villa.

  • Where Via Consolare, Villa Aragona Cutò, Bagheria
  • Entry €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
