Santuario di Giunone Sospita

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the park next to Villa Sforza Cesarini, co-eval of the walls, it was dedicated to the Juno Sospita cited by Cicero. The excavations began in 1884-1892 with Lord Savile Lumley, who found there the Donary of Licinius Murena, marble group of knights and warriors of the first century BC offered by the consul after the victory over Mithridates and brought to England; the subsequent finds are in the civic museum.

Why go. The shrine of the patron goddess of Lanuvium.
