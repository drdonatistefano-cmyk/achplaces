Villa Barattolo, sede del Parco

Nature and landscape · City parks

Villa built in the early 20th century by Achille Franzosini and sold in 1917 to the film producer Giuseppe Barattolo; since 1989 it is the seat of the Regional Park of the Roman Castles. The 7,500 m2 park has a nature trail with information panels and a pond.

Why go. The villa of a silent cinema producer, today home of the Park.
