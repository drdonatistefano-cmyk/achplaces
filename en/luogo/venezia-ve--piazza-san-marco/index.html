Piazza San Marco

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The only "piazza" of Venice, trapezoid, 175 for 72 meters, defined by Napoleon the most beautiful living room in Europe; it is the lowest area of the city and flooded with high water. Under the New Procuratie the Florian Coffee, the oldest in Italy.

Why go. The heart of Venice.

  • Where Piazza San Marco, Venezia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
