Villa Necchi Campiglio

Museums · House museums

The industrial villa Necchi Campiglio, today of the FAI, which tells the high-bourgeois Milan of the first half of the twentieth century, part of the Case Museo circuit.

Why go. The elegant twentieth century with swimming pool.
