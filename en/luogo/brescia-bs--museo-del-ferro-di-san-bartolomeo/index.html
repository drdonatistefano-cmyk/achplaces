Museo del Ferro di San Bartolomeo

Museums · Themed

In the forge of San Bartolomeo, the center of the Museum of Industry and Work: the hammer put back into operation, a hydraulic wheel and the entire system of exploitation of the energy of the water visible outside, with the plastic of the neighborhood.

Why go. Industrial archaeology in motion.
