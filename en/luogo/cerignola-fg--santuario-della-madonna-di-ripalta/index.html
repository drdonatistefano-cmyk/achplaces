Santuario della Madonna di Ripalta

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

It rises in the open countryside about 9 km from Cerignola, on a spur overlooking the river Ofanto from which it derives its name (the high ripa) . The church of the patron saint holds for six months a year the Greek-Byzantine icon of Our Lady, probably of Basilian origin, found in 1172; in the area was found a stone with inscription to the goddess Bona. Diocesan shrine since 1992, has been several times among the Places of the FAI Heart.

Why go. The sanctuary of the patroness overlooking the valley of Ofanto.
