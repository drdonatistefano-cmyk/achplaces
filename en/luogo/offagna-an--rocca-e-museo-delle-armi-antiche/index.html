Rocca e Museo delle armi antiche

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built between 1454 and 1455 by the Anconetans on the remains of a previous castle, it has quadrangular plan, towers, fifty stations for bombing and the five-storey mast still intact, seat of the Museum of the ancient weapons; along the walls runs the way of patrol with blackbirds tail of swallows. At the top of the mast there is a bell dating back to 1476 and a panorama of the countryside.

Why go. Bombs, weapons and a view from the mastium.
