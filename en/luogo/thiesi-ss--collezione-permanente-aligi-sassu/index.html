Collezione permanente Aligi Sassu

Museums · Modern art

Museum inaugurated in 2010 by the Municipality and the Sassu Foundation in the country where the painter (Milano 1912-Pollença 2000) spent part of his childhood: 120 graphic works from 1929-1995 donated by the heirs, among aqueforts, aquatinte, pricks and lithographs, with the folders of the Orlando Furioso (1974), of the Fallen Horses (1973), the engravings on the Apocalypse and the five lithographs Homage to Sardinia with verses by Sebastiano Satta. In the nucleus also the two mural cycles of the sixties, the Angevin Moti , on the antifeudal motions of the late eighteenth century of which Thiesi was the center promoter, and ... The Life and Nature.

Why go. The great Sassu and his horses in the country of childhood.

Closed for renovation and installation, undefined reopening (SardiniaCultura)
