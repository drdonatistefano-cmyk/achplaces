Mulino di Fiume

Museums · Themed

Medieval water mill in the hamlet of Rijeka, the detached seat of the Museum of Our Earth, still working with pond, closed and original waterfalls fed by the stream Sant'Angelo. For centuries it was one of the five mills of the territory, with a capacity of over 700 quintals of wheat per year; the route reconstructs the life and work of the miller.

Why go. A medieval mill that still grinds, at the end of the Water Trail.
