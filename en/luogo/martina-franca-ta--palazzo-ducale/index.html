Palazzo Ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Wanted in the second half of the seventeenth century by Petracone V Caracciolo on the site of the castle of the Orsini, it has a baroque façade with pilasters on two levels divided by a balcony with wrought iron railing; inside the Chapel of the Dukes and the halls of the Arcadia, the Myth and the Bible, partly decorated by Domenico Carella in 1771-76. It houses the Town Hall and, in the atrium, works and concerts of the Festival of the Itria Valley.

Why go. The painted rooms of the Dukes Caracciolo.
