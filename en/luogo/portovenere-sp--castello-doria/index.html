Castello Doria

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Pentagonal fortress that dominates the village, with the main part of the sixteenth century, a 17th century belt to defend the oldest buildings and bastions overlooking the sea; it preserves traces of a medieval fortress previous.

Why go. The fortress on the village.
