Museo del pino loricato

Museums · Science and university

In the historic center, dedicated to the pine loricato symbol of the Pollino Park, which grows on the peaks of Serra Dolcedorme and Serra delle Ciavole: finds, photos of the tree in its environment and panels on the types, diffusion and scientific characteristics. From June to September 9-13 and 15.30-19.30, in winter by reservation.

Why go. The armored pine of Pollino.
