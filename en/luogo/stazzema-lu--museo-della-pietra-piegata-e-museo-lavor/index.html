Museo della Pietra Piegata e Museo «Lavorare liberi»

Museums · Themed

In Levigliani, next to the ticket office of Corchia, the museum tells the uses of stone and Apuan marble: sacred art in marble, colored marbles now little hollowed, baroque engravings, kitchen mortars and for medicinal herbs, a section dedicated to the biologist Emilio Simi, discoverer of the Antro del Corchia, and a tomb in box of the Ligurian Apuans from the nearby necropolis (III-I century BC). The Museo di comunità "Lavorare liberi" tells the history of the Comunello and the Cooperative Condomini Levigliani, which has been riding Monte Corchia for over 50 years, with tools and the reconstruction of a quarry with wire cutting.

Why go. Apuan marble from prehistory to the quarry.
