Castello di Formigine

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortified by the Municipality of Modena in 1201 after a defeat against Reggio, from 1405 he passed to the Pio di Carpi who gave him the current layout with the rock, the brand building and the clock tower; in 1599 he returned to the Estensi, who in 1648 infeuded him to the Calcagnini. Seriously damaged by the bombings of the Second World War, it was a municipal seat and then restored and opened to the public: the Marchan Palace houses the reception and wall drawings of David Tremlett, and the archaeological park of over 4,000 m2 preserves the remains of the parish church of San Bartolomeo with bell tower and burials.

Why go. The historic heart of the city, among towers and excavations.
