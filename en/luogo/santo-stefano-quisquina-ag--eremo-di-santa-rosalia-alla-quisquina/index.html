Eremo di Santa Rosalia alla Quisquina

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Monastery of the eighteenth century in a natural wood of oaks on Monte Cammarata, next to the cave where Santa Rosalia took refuge for twelve years after refusing the wedding in Palermo; it preserves an epigraph in Latin of the saint. It is the starting point of the Itinerarium Rosaliae, the path to the Sanctuary of Monte Pellegrino.

Why go. The Santa Rosalia cave in the woods.
