Parco naturale Molentargius-Saline

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Park established in 1999, over 1,600 hectares of ponds with different salinity (Bellarosa Minore and Perdalonga of fresh water, Bellarosa Maggiore and the pond of Quartu first basins of the former state salt pans), with pink flamingos, swamp falcons, garzettes, avocette and sultan chicken; you visit on foot, by bike or with guided birdwatching trails, better in spring and autumn.

Why go. The pink flamingos at the gates of the city.
