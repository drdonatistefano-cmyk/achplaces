Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On a hill in the historic centre, probably the first cathedral of the city: Byzantine church with Greek cross with dome, three circular apses, eight columns and Byzantine frescoes that tell the story.

Why go. The Byzantine jewel of the village.
