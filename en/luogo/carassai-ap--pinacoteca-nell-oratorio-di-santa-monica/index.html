Pinacoteca nell'Oratorio di Santa Monica

Museums · Old masters

Next to the Palazzo Comunale, the oratory of 1699 has a late Baroque Rococo façade; restored in 2003-2004, it houses valuable paintings and the Credenza with the statue of Saint Monica.

Why go. A small baroque treasure chest of sacred art.
