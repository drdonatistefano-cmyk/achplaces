Spiaggia di Sottomarina

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

The sea of Chioggia, Blue Flag, meets the Venetian lagoon; the sand is very fine, clear and rich in minerals, associated with psamotherapy. Along the dam you can see the fishing scales.

Why go. A fine sand and a walk on the dam.
