Parrocchiale di San Giacomo Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church of the village took the place of the church of San Pietro, demolished in 1770; its bell tower, with that of the deconsecrated church of the Confraternity of San Michele, stands out on the roofs of the center.

Why go. The main church of the medieval village.
