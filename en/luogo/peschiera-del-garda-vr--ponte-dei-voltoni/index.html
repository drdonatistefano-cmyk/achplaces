Ponte dei Voltoni

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Five-arched stone bridge built in 1556 by the Republic of Venice, immediately after the completion of the fortifications, under the Provveditore Iacobo Gauro remembered by a plaque in Via Dante; it is considered the architectural symbol of the city. Under the arch towards Piazza Ferdinando di Savoia there was Pescarezzo, a fixed fish pond; the only structure of this type still visible on Garda, recently restored, can be seen from the Longolago Bonomi.

Why go. The symbol bridge of Peschiera, gateway to the Habsburg quarter.
