Museo Tiere Motus

Museums · City history

Show permanent "Tiere Motus." History of an earthquake and its people are on the first floor of the sixteenth-century Palazzo Orgnani-Martina: photos, videos and documents on the earthquake of May 6, 1976, from the day of the shock to the reconstruction, with a 3D simulation of the collapse of the cathedral.

Why go. Understand the 1976 earthquake and reconstruction.
