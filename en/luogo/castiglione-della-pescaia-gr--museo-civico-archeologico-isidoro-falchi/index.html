Museo civico archeologico Isidoro Falchi

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Born as Antiquarium of Vetulonia and reopened expanded in June 2000, it exhibits in seven rooms on two floors the artifacts of the city: urns Villanovian Poggio alla Guardia, gold and silver sets of the circles of the Silver Leoncini and the Golden Fibula, the stele of the warrior Auvele Feluske and an alphabet engraved of Hellenistic age.

Why go. The Etruscan princes of Vetulonia.

  • Where Piazza Vetluna, Vetulonia, Castiglione della Pescaia
  • Entry €5, reduced €2.50 (museum and exhibition €7, reduced €5)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
