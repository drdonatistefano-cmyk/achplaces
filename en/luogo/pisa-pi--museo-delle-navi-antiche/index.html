Museo delle Navi Antiche

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the Medici Arsenals seven Roman ships from the third century BC to the VII AD, found since 1998 near San Rossore, with about 800 exhibits, visits and workshops for families.

Why go. One of the most amazing museums in Tuscany.

  • Where Arsenali Medicei, Lungarno Ranieri Simonelli, Pisa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
