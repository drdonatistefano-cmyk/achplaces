Villa Frisiani Mereghetti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built around the middle of the seventeenth century for Gotthard Frisiani, on a project attributed to Francesco Maria Richini, incorporating as wing a fifteenth-century body of the previous castle; the interior frescoes with a profane subject are Montalto brothers. U-shaped plant with central body, two wings and ample park: the left wing, the oldest, has ogival windows, wooden floors and fifteenth-century frescoes; to the north a portico with three fornics on granite binated columns of Baveno, inside a staircase of honour.

Why go. A seventeenth-century villa of the Richini that incorporates the old castle.

Private property for residential use (SIRBEC card); the villas open thanks to the owners during the walk between the villas (Common)
