Chiesa della Santissima Trinità («piccola Sistina»)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small fifteenth-century church outside the ancient walls, at the crossroads between Francesca and Cremasca, restored in 1979 by the Alps. In the presbytery the Trinity with the Apostles and the Assumption; on the walls a cycle inspired by the Sistine Chapel, from Creation to the golden calf, and in counterfaced the Last Judgment, of a painter perhaps near Callisto da Lodi.

Why go. A miniature country "Sistina"
