Complesso di Orsanmichele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

The ancient barn became the church of the Arts, with the external niches and, on the upper floor, the original statues of Donatello, Ghiberti and Verrocchio.

Why go. Masterpiece statues, few tourists.

  • Where Via dell'Arte della Lana, Firenze
  • Entry €10 whole for the museum (since 1 February 2026)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
