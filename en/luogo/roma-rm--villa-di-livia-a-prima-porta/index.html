Villa di Livia a Prima Porta

History and architecture · Archaeology

The suburban residence of Livia, wife of Augustus, along the Via Flaminia, with gardens, paintings and mosaic floors; here was found the Augustus of Prima Porta, today at the Vatican Museums. Visits suspended since August 2025 for the PNRR yard.

Why go. The villa of the first Empress.
