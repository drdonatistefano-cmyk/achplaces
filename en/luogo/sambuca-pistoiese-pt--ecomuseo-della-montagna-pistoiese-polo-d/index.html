Ecomuseo della Montagna Pistoiese, Polo didattico della Pietra

Museums · Themed

Centre of the Itinerary of the Stone of the Ecomuseum, which documents the use of local sandstone in houses, mills, dryer houses and walls; it is located in the middle school of Pavana. Visits by reservation.

Why go. The starting point to understand the stone of the mountain.
