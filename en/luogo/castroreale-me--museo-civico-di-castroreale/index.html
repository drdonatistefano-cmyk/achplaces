Museo civico di Castroreale

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Founded in 1984 and opened in 1989 in the former oratory of the Filippini Fathers (1632) and in the church of San Filippo Neri, on two floors of works Messinese from the fifteenth to the eighteenth century: a Salvator Mundi attributed to Polidoro da Caravaggio, a painted cross of the fourteenth century, a wooden crucifix of the fifteenth century, the funeral monument of Geronimo Rosso by Antonello Gagini, a polyptych of the late fifteenth century and a table of Antonello de Saliba.

Why go. The Messinese Renaissance in a village.

  • Where Via Guglielmo Siracusa 35, Castroreale
  • Entry €3, reduced €1,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
