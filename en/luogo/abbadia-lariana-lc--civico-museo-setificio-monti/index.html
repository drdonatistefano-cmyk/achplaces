Civico Museo Setificio Monti

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Around 1818, the silksmith Pietro Monti transformed an ancient cloth crowd, moved by a hydraulic wheel on the stream Val Zerbo, into a silk spinning mill; in 1869 the spinning mill was added, closed in 1903, and the spinning mill worked until 1934. Purchased by the Municipality in 1978 and restored since 1981, the museum opened in 1998: the heart is the large circular torch-piece of 1818 (5 m in diameter, 11 m in height, 972 casts), put into operation, with three rectangular torcuits and, since 2021, an tapestry embroidered in silk by Filippo Biagioli on the legend of Grigna.

Why go. See one of Europe's largest silk torciotes turn.
