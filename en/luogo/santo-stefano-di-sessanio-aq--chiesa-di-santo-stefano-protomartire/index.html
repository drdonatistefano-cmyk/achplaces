Chiesa di Santo Stefano Protomartire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries, it has only one nave in five spans, an unusual presbytery open on chapels and a semicircular apse. The patron saints Stefano protomartyre and Saint Isidoro are celebrated on 2-3 August.

Why go. The mother church of the Medici village.
