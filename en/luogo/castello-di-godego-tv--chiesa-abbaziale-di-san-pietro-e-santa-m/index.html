Chiesa abbaziale di San Pietro e Santa Maria Nascente

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church of Godego is one of the oldest in the diocese of Treviso; in 1754 Pope Benedict XIV raised it to abbey church on the initiative of Archpriest Nuzio Quirini. Next to the eighteenth-century church was built a larger one in the mid-Twentieth century, designed by architect L. Candiani.

Why go. The ancient parish church of the village, abbey church since 1754.
