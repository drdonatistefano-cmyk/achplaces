Villa rustica di s'Imbalconadu

History and architecture · Archaeology

Villa-farm of the II-I century B.C. about 2 km from the city: about twenty rooms around a central court, with tanks for winemaking (calcatory).

Why go. A farm of the Roman Republican wine.
