Casa Museo di Cerveno

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The rural house of the end of the sixteenth century of the Mondoni-Bazzoni families, purchased by the municipality and opened in 2006 as a museum collection recognized by the Lombardy Region: original vaults and limestone, tools for fields, viticulture and winemaking, breeding and crafts, and clothes, weapons, helmets and armour of the Santa Crus. Outside there is a horizontal lever press. Open Tuesday, Friday (14.30-16.30), Thursday (9-12), Saturday and Sunday (14.30-17.30); groups by reservation.

Why go. The peasant life and the customs of Santa Crus.

  • Where Vicolo Sonvico, Cerveno
  • Entry €2 (free residents and children up to 5 years)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
