Filatoio Rosso – Museo del Setificio Piemontese

Museums · Themed

Built between 1676 and 1678 by the Serico entrepreneur Giò Gerolamo Galleani and reached almost intact until today, it is one of the oldest examples of industrial archaeology in Europe: a building with the appearance of an almost fortified noble residence, with two courtyards, cylindrical turrets at the corners and decorations in stucco and terracotta. The guided tours show the torccioi rebuilt in operation, the spinner, the spinning mill and some rooms; it hosts temporary exhibitions and the info point of the Gate of Valle.

Why go. The silk of the seventeenth century with the cars in motion.
