Mostra permanente di tessuti artigianali

Local life and traditions · Crafts

In the historic centre, it exhibits the tradition of carpets, blankets and embroidery of the village: Longobucco is one of the few places in Calabria where weaves again on handlooms, with the design woven into the weft and not embroidered; its fabrics were exhibited in Rome between 1921 and 1925 and awarded with the silver medal in Barcelona (1929) and Milan (1930).

Why go. The weaving by hand that won medals in Europe.
