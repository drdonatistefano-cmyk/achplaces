Villa Della Porta Bozzolo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Country residence of the sixteenth century transformed in the eighteenth century into a noble villa of delight, with U-shaped structure around the courtyard of honour, Rococo and Baroque decorations, trompe-l'oeil and frescoed vaults; in the salon and gallery of the noble piano paintings of the Milanese Salvione and Bosso. The Italian garden rises on four terraces connected by stairs with balustrades, statues and fountains, up to the lawn of the theater closed by an eighteenth-century fish pond; in the park also the Filanda. Bene del FAI, open from March to November with variable times (in autumn from Wednesday to Sunday 10-17).

Why go. A delightful villa with a scenic garden on the Valcuvia.
