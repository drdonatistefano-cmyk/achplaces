Castello e borgo di Vettignè

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The castle dates back to about 1460, with a quadrangular plan around a large courtyard, with a cylindrical tower, a square tower and a tower where you can still see the entrance with drawbridge; transformed between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, passed to the Savoys in 1867 with the wedding of Maria Vittoria From the Well of the Cisterna and Amedeo of Savoy, who built the royal apartments there, and was sold to private individuals after the Second World War. The village, which had more than 500 inhabitants at the beginning of the 20th century, is now almost depopulated and in precarious state, but offers views of the rice fields with the Alps in the background.

Why go. A sabaudo castle in the sea in squares of the rice fields.
