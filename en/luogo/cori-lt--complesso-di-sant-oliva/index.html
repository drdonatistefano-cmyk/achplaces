Complesso di Sant'Oliva

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

It rises on the remains of a temple of the III-I century BC: the church and the bell tower are of the first half of the XII century, the chapel of the Holy Crucifix (second half of the XV century) was commissioned by Cardinal Guillaume d'Estouteville and by Ambrogio Massari, with frescoes of biblical scenes of the late fifteenth century. The cloister of the former Augustinian convent, by Antonio da Como, has 27 marble capitals all different. Convent and cloister are visited by appointment.

Why go. Church and cloister, the "da non persare" of Cori.
