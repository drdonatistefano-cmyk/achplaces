Rocca Estense e Pinacoteca Coppelli

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Castello di origine matildica, poi dei Roberti e dal Quattrocento di un ramo marchanale degli Este: quadrangular plant and an unspoilt corner tower with ghibelline blackbirds and caditoias. Inside the chapel of St. John with a sharp sixth and the noble floor with painted coffered ceilings, frescoes and stuccoes from the fifteenth to the eighteenth centuries; it houses the Pinacoteca Coppelli, the library and the open archive dedicated to Henghel Gualdi. Open Sundays and holidays, 10-12.30 and 15-18.30, with guided tours; closed in August.

Why go. The fortress of the Este in the Lower.
