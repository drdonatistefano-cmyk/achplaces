Santuario della Madonna della Coltura

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Modern building with Romanesque-Gothic lines, designed by Napoleon Pagliarulo after the demolition in 1913 of the previous Baroque church and inaugurated in 1920. Under the canopy of the main altar the monolith with the Madonna Panagia, Byzantine fresco perhaps of the twelfth century repainted in the seventeenth century, that tradition wants found by a farmer in the district Bread; a large fresco of the counter-facade tells the discovery. Patron Festival on the last weekend of May with the race "currature" .

Why go. The Byzantine monolith, heart of Salento devotion.
