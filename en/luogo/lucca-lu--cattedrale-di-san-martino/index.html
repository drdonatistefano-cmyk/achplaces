Cattedrale di San Martino

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Paid entry

Started in 1060, with the asymmetrical Romanesque façade and the months bas-reliefs under the porch; inside the small temple of Matteo Civitali (1482) with the Holy Face and the monument to Ilaria del Carretto by Jacopo della Quercia.

Why go. The Holy Face and Ilaria, two masterpieces.

  • Where Piazza San Martino, Lucca
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
