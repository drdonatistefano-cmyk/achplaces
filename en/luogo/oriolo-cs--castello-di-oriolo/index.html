Castello di Oriolo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

15th century castle, the only surviving part of the ancient defenses of the village, with square mast and angular cylindrical towers of the Aragonese era. It belongs to the Sanseverino and recently restored, it preserves the original structure with two watchtowers and the guard body; inside a museum with interactive classrooms, educational and multimedia and a conference room.

Why go. The symbol of the village, with the multimedia museum inside the Aragonese walls.
