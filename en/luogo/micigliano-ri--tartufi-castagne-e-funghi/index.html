Tartufi, castagne e funghi

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Typical products of the village are truffles, chestnuts, mushrooms and cheeses, celebrated by the Truffle Festival in July and that of the chestnut on the last Sunday of October.

Why go. The flavors of the woods of Terminillo.
