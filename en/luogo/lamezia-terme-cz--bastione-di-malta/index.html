Bastione di Malta

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Coast tower built around 1550 by order of Viceroy Don Pedro of Toledo against the Saracens and entrusted to the Knights of Malta: square plant, pyramidal trunk, four rooms and terrace on top; appears in the city coat of arms.

Why go. The tower of the coat of arms of Lamezia.
