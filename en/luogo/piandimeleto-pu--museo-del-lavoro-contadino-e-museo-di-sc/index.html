Museo del lavoro contadino e Museo di scienze della terra

Museums · Themed

The Museo del Lavoro Contadino was founded in 1982 on the initiative of the Municipality with the Institute of History of the University of Urbino: on the ground floor of the castle, sections on the production of wheat, the farmhouse and crafts. On the first floor the museum of earth sciences, born from the donation of prof. Renzo Pieruccini, exhibits about 4,000 pieces on Earth, minerals and rocks.

Why go. The peasant world and 4,000 minerals and rocks in the castle.
