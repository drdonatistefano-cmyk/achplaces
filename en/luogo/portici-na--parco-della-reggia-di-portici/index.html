Parco della Reggia di Portici

Nature and landscape · City parks

The garden, designed by Francesco Geri, includes the Lower Bosco and the Upper Bosco, with the Boschetto, the Pallone Game, the Castle with observatory tower, the Real Pagliaia and the Vaccheria; in 1814-1815 an English garden with artificial pond was created. The Gallopatoio, a rare covered riding stable, was restored in 2021.

Why go. The royal forest of the Bourbons.
