Museo Lapidario

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage

Epigraphic Museum of the eighteenth century, born from the collection of Cardinal Giovan Francesco Stoppani, on the ground floor of the Doge's Palace.

Why go. To be combined with the visit of the Palace.
