Eremo di Minucciano e Parco archeologico delle stele

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

The Hermitage, of medieval origin, with the sanctuary of the Blessed Virgin of the Rescue, is flanked by an archaeological park where copies of the anthropomorphic stele, menhir of the Bronze Age are displayed.

Why go. The prehistoric statues-stele next to the hermitage.
