Museo diocesano di Caserta

Museums · Old masters

Next to the church of the Blessed Redeemer, a short distance from the Cathedral and the Reggia: liturgical furnishings, paintings, ex-vote and sculptures from the churches of the diocese.

Why go. The sacred art of working land.
