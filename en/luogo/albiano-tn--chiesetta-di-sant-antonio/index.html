Chiesetta di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Lonely chapel of the seventeenth century at the entrance of the village, with paintings and frescoes dedicated to the saint, made as thanksgiving for the received.

Why go. A small ex-sixteenth-century vote at the entrance of the country.
