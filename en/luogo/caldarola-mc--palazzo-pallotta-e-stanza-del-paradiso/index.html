Palazzo Pallotta e Stanza del Paradiso

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Former cardinal residence, today the town hall: on two sides surrounds the square Vittorio Emanuele II wanted in the sixteenth century by Cardinal Pallotta and forms a single complex with the Collegiate. Inside the Hall of Paradise, frescoed by Simone De Magistris.

Why go. The frescoes of De Magistris in the town hall.
