Museo provinciale delle miniere - Miniera di Predoi

Museums · Themed

Along the Rötbach copper was extracted for over 500 years: first citation in 1426, definitive abandonment in 1971, museum opened in 1996. A mine train takes more than a kilometer into the tunnel of Sant'Ignazio, excavated from 1761 and arrived at the deposit after 43 years; two ring routes with reconstructions show tools, machinery and life of the miners, and a preserved copper vein can be touched. Even today, cementation copper is obtained with the help of bacteria. Visits only by reservation, last entry at 15, closed on Mondays not festive and in winter until Easter.

Why go. Get in the train in the heart of a copper mine.
