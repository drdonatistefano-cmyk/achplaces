Museo etnografico Sa Domo 'e sos Marras

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the 18th century manor house of the Marras family, restored in 1995: courtyard and ground floor with agropastoral tools, on the first floor the domestic environments, and closing traditional clothes including a wedding dress of the early twentieth century; about 1800 objects exposed. In May he organizes excursions in the Deleddian literary park.

Why go. The life of the Baronies in a noble house.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Garibaldi, Galtellì
  • Entry €3, reduced €2,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
