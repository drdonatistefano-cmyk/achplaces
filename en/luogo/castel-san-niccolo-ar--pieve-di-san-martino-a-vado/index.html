Pieve di San Martino a Vado

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built at the beginning of the 11th century by the counts Guidi, according to the tradition of Matilde di Canossa, it is one of the most important Romanesque churches in Casentino. Three aisles divided by six monolithic columns in serene stone with all different capitals, work of Lombard workers of the twelfth century; the frescoes walls coming from the parish church of San Niccolò (Crucifixion and St. Nicholas with Saints) and a seventeenth-century canvas with St. Simon Stock receiving the scapular from Our Lady.

Why go. Romanesque capitals in serene stone, one different from the other.
