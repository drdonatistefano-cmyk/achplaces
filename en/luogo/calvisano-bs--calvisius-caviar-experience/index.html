Calvisius Caviar Experience

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Paid entry

Guided tour by reservation at the Calvisius plant in Agroittica Lombarda, a caviar producer with a genetic reserve of sturgeons: the route illustrates the life cycles of sturgeons, the fountains and an educational observatory; in the pools flows resurgence water that flows into the property. Six packages combine the visit to the tasting in restaurants in the area. Visits from Monday to Friday at 11 or 5.30 p.m., on Saturday at 11 am only for large groups.

Why go. See closely how Calvisano's caviar is born.

  • Where Via Kennedy, 25012 Calvisano
  • Entry Packages from €55
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
