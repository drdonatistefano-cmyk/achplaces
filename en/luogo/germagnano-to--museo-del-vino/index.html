Museo del vino

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

It tells the life of the population in the twentieth century through the ancient tools to make wine of the Society Worker and the area, with attention to how we supported each other during the war and to the network of mutual help. Visit by reservation; accessible.

Why go. The wine and mutual aid of a valley country.

  • Where Via Roberto Miglietti 26, Germagnano
  • Entry €2, free school and under 14
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.germagnano.to.it
