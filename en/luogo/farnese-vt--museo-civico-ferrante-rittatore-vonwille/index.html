Museo civico Ferrante Rittatore Vonwiller (MuRV)

Museums · Archaeological

In the former municipal warehouse of wheat ('Ammosso'), behind Palazzo Ceccarini-Chigi; born in 1980, museum since 1990, re-assembled in March 2003, it bears the name of the archaeologist of the University of Milan (1919-1976) who studied these places. It exhibits materials from prehistory to the modern age: Paleolithic, Neolithic and Eneolithic finds, the village of the final Bronze of Sorgenti della Nova with reconstructions, an Etruscan section (Naviglione, Rofalco, tombs of Palombaro) and the ceramics of medieval throws, which tell the daily life from the 14th to the beginning of the 17th century. It is part of the Bolsena Lake Museum System.

Why go. From the prehistory of the Selva to the Farnese table.
