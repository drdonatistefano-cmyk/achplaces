Forte Werk Lusérn

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built between 1908 and 1912 at the top of the Cima Campo, at 1,549 meters, called "Padreterno": with the fort Busa Verle was guarding the Vezzena Pass, armed with four bikes in armored domes, two guns and stations for 19 machine guns; in the first days of the Great War it was heavily bombarded and the garrison was close to surrender. You can reach it on foot from the village (path of the Baites, path Cimbri imaginary, path ... From stories to history) or from Malga Millegrobbe; in summer guided tours inside, passing through the Oberwiesen outpost.

Why go. The Father of the Great War.
