Miniera di Libiola

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

One of the major Italian deposits of copper and iron sulphides, active from the middle of the nineteenth century to 1962 and already known in the Bronze Age (a handle of pickaxes of 1876 is dated to the age of Copper). The rocks are ancient oceanic crust (serpentinite, gabricus, basalt pillow); the acidic mineral waters color the landscape of reddish deposits. You can reach it on foot or by car on the road that climbs from Santa Vittoria di Libiola to Tassani; management Mediterranean Services (0185 478603).

Why go. An unusual mining landscape.
