Palazzo Marchesi Campanari

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built by Campanari at the beginning of the fourteenth century in via Umberto I and expanded from the seventeenth century, with the representation hall and a glass chandelier of Murano; municipal since 2019 and open to the public.

Why go. A noble palace to visit.
