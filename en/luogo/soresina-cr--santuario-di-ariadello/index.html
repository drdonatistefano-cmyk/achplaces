Santuario di Ariadello

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Sanctuary in the countryside, about 2.5 km north of the center, built by the feudal lord G.B. Barbò with the contribution of the population to remember a miracle: the deaf-domed daughter regained the word while offering flowers of field to a painted Madonna, image still very venerated by the Soresinesi.

Why go. The sanctuary of the miracle, in the Cremona countryside.
