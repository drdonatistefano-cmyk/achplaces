Museo del Cavallino della Giara

Museums · Themed

In a typical campidano house, it tells the wild horses of the Giara, the last survivors of a species once diffused throughout the island and considered introduced by the Phoenicians: photo gallery on the life cycle, the environment of the plateau, the tools for tamination, ironing and marking (now replaced by microchip). An ethnographic section, with objects donated by the inhabitants and video-interviews to the elderly, documents the agropastoral culture; educational space for schools and families.

Why go. Know the horses before you look for them on the Giara.
