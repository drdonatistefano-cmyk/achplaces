Chiesa di Santa Maria dell'Olmo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Outside the walls, enlarged in 1521 incorporating an ancient Gothic newsstand frescoed by Vincenzo Pagani with the Crucifixion, which separates the church individually into two; it has two altars. On the last Sunday of April there is a day out with lunch on the lawn.

Why go. Pagani's newsstand that divides the church.
