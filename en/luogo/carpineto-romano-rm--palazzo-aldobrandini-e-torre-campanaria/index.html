Palazzo Aldobrandini e Torre campanaria

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

On the cliff under the tower, preceded by a large garden with centuries-old plants, it was the seat of the baronial power of the Duchy of Carpineto and in the seventeenth century it was bought by the Municipality; there was transcribed the act of peace of 1477 between Carpineto and the neighboring communities. Today it hosts the museum "Reggia dei Volsci" (Flights Reggia). The tower, part of the medieval castrum, has a sixteenth-century clock by master Caesar from Subiaco and a 17th-century majolica exhibition.

Why go. The palace of the dukes with the garden on the cliff.
