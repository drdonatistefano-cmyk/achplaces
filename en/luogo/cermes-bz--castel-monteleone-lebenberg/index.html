Castel Monteleone (Lebenberg)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built in the 13th century by the lords of Marlengo, also known as Lebenberg, on a morainic hill at 505 meters above the village; it was counts Fuchs from 1426 to 1828 and since 1925 it belongs to the family van Rossem, who lives there. You can visit the courtyards, the late-Gothic chapel with frescoes in part fourteenth-century recently rediscovered, the Rococo room of mirrors, the hall of the knights with the large family tree of Fuchs, the armory, the peasant stube with Gothic furniture, the staircase of the coats of arms (from 1216) and the French garden with a mulberry of over 200 years. Open from the end of April to the end of October with visits 10:30-12:30 and 14:00-16:30; Saturday only on request.

Why go. An inhabited castle, with vintage rooms and views of the Adige valley.
