Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Built between the 12th and 15th centuries next to a view on the Biellese, it has a facade with buttresses, rosette and terracotta decorations around the portal. Inside, two paintings from the end of the sixteenth century attributed to the Vercellese school and the pulpit carved in 1712 by Carlo Francesco Auregio Termine, among the most important examples of Biella's wooden sculpture.

Why go. The Baroque pulpit of Auregio Termine and the view from the viewpoint.
