Villa Selvatico e giardino termale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Villa built from 1593 to 1647 for the Selvatico on the hill of Sant'Elena, with the sudoriferous cave frequented already in the Middle Ages; in the hall the frescoes of the Stories of Antenor of Luca Ferrari (1650) and in the dome works of Lorenzo Bedogni and of the Paduanino. It climbs with 144 steps to the romantic park of about 11 hectares redesigned by Giuseppe Jappelli, with thermal lakes.

Why go. Villa and romantic park fed by thermal waters.
