Museo Civico

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the former late 19th century brick school on Piazza Gramsci, inaugurated in 1994, it brings together the Giulio Superti Furga Toy Collection (hundreds of dolls and toys, largely of local production: Furga, founded by Luigi Furga Gornini around 1880, was the first factory of dolls in Italy), the Oglio-Church Ecomuseum with fossils, archaeological finds, peasant tools, dioramas and the Visitor Centre of the Oglio Sud Park, the donation Bellani and the Mortara Collection of Lombard painting between Scapigliatura and Divisionismo (Tranquillo Cremona, Previati, Longoni, Tosi). Open Saturday afternoon, Sunday and holidays.

Why go. The dolls of the first Italian factory and the Lombard painting of the nineteenth century.

Closed approximately from late December to end of February, Easter and Easter, in July and August (Common)
