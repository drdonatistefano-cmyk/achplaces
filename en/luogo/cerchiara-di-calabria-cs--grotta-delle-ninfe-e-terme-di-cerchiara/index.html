Grotta delle Ninfe e Terme di Cerchiara

Local life and traditions · Spas

Cavità limestone on the south-eastern slopes of Mount Sellaro where sulfurous hot water flows that form therapeutic muds, indicated for rheumatic and dermatological diseases; you can bathe in the tub at about 30 °C. The legend hides the nymph Calipso. Time: every day 8-19.

Why go. A hot bath in the cave of Calipso.
