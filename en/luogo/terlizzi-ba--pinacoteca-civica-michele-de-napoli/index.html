Pinacoteca civica «Michele De Napoli»

Museums · Modern art

Paintings, oil sketches, portraits, studies, cartoons and drawings donated to the Municipality by the neoclassicalist painter Michele De Napoli (1808-1892), author of the design of the triumphal chariot of the Madonna di Sovereto; complete the collection of architectural fragments of the Seven-nineteenth century and a carriage of the late eighteenth century restored.

Why go. The neoclassicism of a painter from Terlizzo.
