Villa Falconieri

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

The first villa built in Frascati, by Bishop Alessandro Rufini, called "La Rufina" (La Rufina), now home to the Vivarium Novum Institute, guided tours on Sundays.

Why go. The first of the Tuscan villas.
