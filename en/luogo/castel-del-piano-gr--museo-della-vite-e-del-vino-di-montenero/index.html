Museo della vite e del vino di Montenero d'Orcia

Museums · Themed

In the outbuilding of the large farmhouse of the Avanzati family, used until the 1950s: panels on wine culture, tools for viticulture and winemaking, chestnuts and oil of the Amiata and pijauva, 18th century press used until 1910.

Why go. The 18th century press.
