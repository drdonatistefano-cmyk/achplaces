Osservatorio astronomico «Leopoldo Del Re»

Museums · Science and university

To the north of the municipal territory, named after the astronomer of Cantalupo in Sannio, it allows astrologers to observe the sky and has a planetarium that reproduces the celestial vault with 20,000 sidereal bodies.

Why go. The sky of Alto Molise, with planetarium.
