Parco di Villa Serbelloni

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Park on the steep promontory between the two branches of the lake, wanted by Alessandro Serbelloni, with 18 km of avenues, paths and terraces and the remains of the ancient castle of Bellagio; the villa, of 1854 according to Italia.it, is of the Rockefeller Foundation and hosts conferences and study stays. Not to be confused with the Grand Hotel Villa Serbelloni.

Why go. The viewpoint on the spot where the lake divides.

only guided tours (about 90 minutes, departures 11 and 15.30, closed on Monday, groups minimum 6 people) from mid-March to early November, I meet at the office Promobellagio in the tower of Piazza della Chiesa
