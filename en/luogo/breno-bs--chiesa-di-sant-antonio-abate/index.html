Chiesa di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the first half of the 14th century, with curved corner façade: it preserves frescoes from the late 15th century, the 16th century frescoes by Girolamo Romanino in the presbytery and a altarpiece by Callisto Piazza.

Why go. Romanino in the heart of Breno.
