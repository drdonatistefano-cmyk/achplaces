Chiese delle frazioni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The hamlets preserve numerous churches with works of art and glazed terracottas of the school of Della Robbia: San Lorenzo a Baldignano with crypt of the eleventh century, Sant'Andrea della Torre a Mignano, documented since 1085 and linked to a small fortified village on the road to Verna, the Hermitage of the Madonna del Faggio in Cercetole, of the first fifteenth century, and the churches of Saints James and Christopher in Montalone and Saints Peter and Paul in Valsavignon, with works of the sixteenth century.

Why go. A tour of the parish churches and terracottas of the Valtiberina.
