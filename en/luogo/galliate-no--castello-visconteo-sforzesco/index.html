Castello Visconteo Sforzesco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The Duke of Milan Galeazzo Sforza had him built in 1476 on a previous Visconti fortress, entrusting the works to Ambrogio Ferrari and Danesio Mainerio, as hunting residence of the Milanese court, continued use with Ludovico il Moro. Rectangular plant of 80 x 108 meters, walls about 3,5 meters thick and 7.5 meters high up to the blackbirds, moat fed by the Codimonte Roggia, four corner towers connected by underground passages and two entrances with drawbridge and poster it still visible. In the sleeve and in the south-west tower there is the municipal library, with over 10,000 rare pieces between incunaboli and five hundred.

Why go. One of the hunting residences of the Sforza, with moat and towers.
