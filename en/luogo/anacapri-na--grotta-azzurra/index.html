Grotta Azzurra

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Ancient water lilies of the imperial villa of Gradola, which placed statues of Tritoni and Poseidon there; destination of travelers and artists since 1826, can be reached only by boat and is not visitable by the rough sea.

Why go. The most famous blue light in Italy.

Access only by boat, suspended with rough sea
