Casa Cavazzini - Museo d'Arte Moderna e Contemporanea

Museums · Modern art

Renovated on the project of Gae Aulenti: on the first floor the brothers Dino, Mirko and Afro Basaldella with a cycle of frescoes by Afro, on the second the Astaldi Collection with Morandi, Martini, Mafai, Guttuso, De Chirico and Savinio, and the Friam Collection with De Kooning, Lichtenstein and Frank Stella; temporary exhibitions on the ground floor.

Why go. Afro to Lichtenstein.
