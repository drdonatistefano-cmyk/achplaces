Chiesa parrocchiale di San Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The country dome, dedicated to the Conversion of St. Paul, is one of the most important sacred buildings in South Tyrol: the parish, one of the richest in the province since about 1147, built a church oversized in over 250 years, German late dogotics in the base and in the central and baroque Italian body in the onion dome. The bell tower 86 meters high has nine bells, including the second largest in South Tyrol (3.8 tons, merged from Grassmayr to Innsbruck); the interior is a three-nave hall church with a major altar and two side of Michael Stolz, noble coats of arms, busts of the apostles and a Madonna with the Child of the fifteenth century. Guided tour once a week and audio guide.

Why go. A bell tower of 86 meters that dominates the whole Wine Road.
