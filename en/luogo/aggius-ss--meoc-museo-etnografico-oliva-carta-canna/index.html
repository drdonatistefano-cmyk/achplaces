MEOC - Museo etnografico Oliva Carta Cannas

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

About fifteen rooms on two floors reconstruct Gallura's life: shops of the shoemaker, the blacksmith and the carpenter, cellar, kitchen, loom room where women of the village show live the weaving of the carpet, and a room dedicated to the Chorus of Aggius, founded in 1928 with a letter from D'Annunzio. The MiC indicates it as a permanent exhibition of the carpet from 1600 to today.

Why go. The Aggius rug fabric in front of your eyes.

  • Where Via Monti di Lizu 6, Aggius
  • Entry €4, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
