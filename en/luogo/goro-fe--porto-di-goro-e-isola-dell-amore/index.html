Porto di Goro e Isola dell'Amore

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Paid entry · Viewpoint

From the fishing port, the capital of clams, the boats depart to the Island of Love, a tongue of sand with dunes that change with winds and tides.

Why go. One of the wildest beaches in Italy.
