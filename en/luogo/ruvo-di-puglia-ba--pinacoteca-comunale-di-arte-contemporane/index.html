Pinacoteca comunale di arte contemporanea «Cantatore»

Museums · Modern art

On the ground floor of the former convent of San Domenico: works from the early twentieth century to the seventies by artists such as Grumo, Chieco, Di Terlizzi, Sacco, Brandonisio and van Westerhout, and the graphic collection by Domenico Singer with aquariums, etchings and lithographs; space for exhibitions and cloisters for events.

Why go. The Twentieth Century Puglia and the graphics of Singer.
