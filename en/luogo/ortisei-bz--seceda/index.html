Seceda

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Panoramic view of the sunny side of the Val Gardena, in the Puez-Odle Nature Park, reachable with the facilities from Ortisei: from the top the view embraces the Dolomites and the mountains of South Tyrol. Several cabins serve local cuisine with sun terraces and stube; destination of skiers, hikers, paragliders and families, open all year round.

Why go. One of the great views of the Dolomites, within reach of cable car.
