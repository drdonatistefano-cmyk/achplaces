Castello della Lucertola e Museo della storia di Apricale

Museums · City history · Viewpoint

Castle built on the fortress since the tenth century, destroyed in 1523 by order of Bishop Agostino Grimaldi; today of the municipality, houses the museum of the history of the village, art exhibitions and the Gallery of the Theatre with the scenery of Emanuele Luzzati. From the castle lies the bike of Sergio Bianco.

Why go. The castle of the village.
