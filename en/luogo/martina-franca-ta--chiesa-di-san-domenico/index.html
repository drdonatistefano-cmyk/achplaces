Chiesa di San Domenico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1745 and 1750 on a project by the Dominican Fra' Antonio Cantalupi, instead of the medieval San Pietro Martire; the façade, considered the most emblematic example of the martinese baroque, has capitals with mermaids between acanto leaves, musical putti, a window framed by a cherub and the tympanum broken at will.

Why go. The most emblematic Baroque façade in the city.
