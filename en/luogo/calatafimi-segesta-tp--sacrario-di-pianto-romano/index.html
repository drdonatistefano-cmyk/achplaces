Sacrario di Pianto Romano

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

On the top of the hill the theatre of the battle between the troops of Garibaldi and the Bourbon army, reachable along the provincial road: an obelisk 30 meters high, in neoclassical style with Greek elements, guards the bones of the fallen of the battle of Calatafimi of 5 May 1860. There is a ticket combined with the Archaeological Park of Segesta.

Why go. The place of the first victory of the Thousands.
