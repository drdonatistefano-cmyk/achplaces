Santuario della Madonna del Lago

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

On the shore of a small lake about three kilometers from the village: the first church, seventeenth-century and dedicated to the Visitation, was enlarged in 1842 and today houses an inn, while the current sanctuary is of the late nineteenth century. On the first Sunday of July there is the Procession of the Christs, with the Ligurian and Piedmontese confraternities that carry great crucifixes around the lake; from here start trails towards the High Via dei Monti Liguri and the Armetta, Dubasso and Galero mountains.

Why go. The shrine on the lake and the Procession of Christs.
