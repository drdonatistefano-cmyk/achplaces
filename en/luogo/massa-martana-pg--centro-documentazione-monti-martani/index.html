Centro documentazione Monti Martani

Museums · City history

Inaugurated in 2006, it tells in chronological order the history of Massa Martana and its territory, with objects of productive activities, especially agricultural, and Roman finds from the excavations of the seventies and nineties of the twentieth century. Information: Tel. 075 889371.

Why go. The Martani territory in a museum.
