Museo multimediale Turcus e Morus

Museums · Themed

In the former restored Granatic Mountain, it tells of a thousand years of encounters and especially clashes between two worlds, from the 8th to the 18th century, with the incursions of the barbarian pirates at the center; it takes its cue from the inscription in the country church of São Paulo that recalls the destruction of the nearby village of Uras by the Barbarossa in 1515. Multimedia panels, interactive video portraits and life-size holograms, reconstructions of coastal towers and bridges of ships, dioramas, models and vintage weapons, digital reproduction of the retablo of Lorenzo Cavaro and a section on the disappeared village of Serzela. Accessible to the blind and disabled, with educational workshops.

Why go. The barbaric pirates told by the protagonists in hologram.
