Chiesa di Sant'Ambrogio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Mother church on Piazza Beltrami, built around the year 1000 and renovated several times: simple Romanesque facade, baroque interior with three naves with side chapels; of the Middle Ages remain south side, lantern of the dome, bell tower and facade. It houses a altarpiece painted in 1547 by Fermo Stella da Caravaggio and the tomb of San Vito martyr, patron saint of Omegna.

Why go. The millennial mother church of the city.
