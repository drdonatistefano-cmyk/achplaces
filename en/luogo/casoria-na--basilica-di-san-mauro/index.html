Basilica di San Mauro

Places of worship · Basilicas

It is the main and oldest church of Casoria, attested by 1092; the present building, Baroque, was built between 1606 and 1621 and completed in the seventeenth century, and the Municipality is patron and owner of it. It houses works by Pietro di Martino and Giovan Vincenzo d'Onofrio called Forlì, including the altarpiece of the Madonna di Monserrato (1599), the last paintings by Domenico Antonio Vaccaro, a pipe organ of the eighteenth century and a ceiling with golden drawers of local carvers.

Why go. Neapolitan Baroque and Vaccaro's last paintings.
