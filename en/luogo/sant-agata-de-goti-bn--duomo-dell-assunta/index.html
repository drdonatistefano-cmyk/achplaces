Duomo dell'Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Founded in 970 on a pagan temple, rebuilt in the 12th century and renovated in the 18th century mainly by Neapolitan artists; ceiling of 1878. The 12-column crypt contains 14th-century frescoes and remains of mosaic flooring.

Why go. The millenarian cathedral with the frescoed crypt.
