Abbazia di Santa Maria di Vezzolano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Considered the most important Romanesque monument of Piedmont, it has been documented since 1095 (the foundation by Charlemagne in 773 is only legend) and combines Romanesque and Gothic forms. The façade alternates bricks and sandstone bands with statues of the archangels Michael and Raphael and of Christ; inside the pier of the late 12th century brings the bas-reliefs of the 35 patriarchs and stories of Mary. The cloister, one of the best preserved in Piedmont, has frescoes from the 13th and 15th centuries; since 2015 the complex has been entrusted to the Museum Pole of Piedmont.

Why go. The masterpiece of the Piedmontese Romanesque.
