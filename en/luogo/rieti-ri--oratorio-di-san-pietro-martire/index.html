Oratorio di San Pietro Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It preserves the Last Judgment painted by the brothers Torresani between 1552 and 1554, extended on three walls until the time and restored in 2026.

Why go. A sixteenth-century Last Judgement.
