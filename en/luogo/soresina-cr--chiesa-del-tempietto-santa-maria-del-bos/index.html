Chiesa del Tempietto (Santa Maria del Boschetto)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The construction was promoted in 1606 by the Confraternity of the Holy Trinity; it has a single nave, six side chapels and a choir-oratory, with wooden sculptures of the sixteenth century. It was reopened to worship in 2001 after extensive restoration.

Why go. 16th century wooden sculptures in a church restored in 2001.
