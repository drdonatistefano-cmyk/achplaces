Castello di Brolio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Castello di origine Longobarda dei Ricasoli since 1141, considered the oldest winery in Italy: you can visit the garden, the museum and the ramparts, with tastings and guided tours; the residence of the baron remains private.

Why go. From the bastions you can see the whole Chianti.
