Museo di Arte sacra di San Francesco

Museums · Old masters

In the former Franciscan oratory, works of the churches of the territory, including the Terracotta Complaint of the workshop of Baccio da Montelupo and an archaeological section.

Why go. A small museum in the heart of Chianti.
