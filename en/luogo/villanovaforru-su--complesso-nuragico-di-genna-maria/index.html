Complesso nuragico di Genna Maria

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the hill of Genna Maria, about 400 meters, the original tower of the fifteenth century BC was surrounded in the thirteenth century by a four-tower bastion with courtyard and well partly dug into the rock, then by an ante-mural with six towers; between bastion and ante-mural rose in the early Iron Age a village abandoned in the eighth century BC. From the 4th century BC, mast and courtyard became a place of worship, with about 600 votive lamps, until the 7th century A.D. From the top the view ranges from the Gulf of Cagliari to the Gulf of Oristano to the Gennargentu.

Why go. A thousand years of Nuragic history on a panoramic hill.
