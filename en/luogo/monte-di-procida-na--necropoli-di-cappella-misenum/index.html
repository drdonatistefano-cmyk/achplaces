Necropoli di Cappella (Misenum)

History and architecture · Archaeology

Hypogeal tombs along the ancient road that connected the municipality of Misenum to Cuma: the excavated section includes at least seven quadrangular funerary buildings and barrel-shaped vaults, aligned in a single fifth, with a pediment kiosk on the back wall and niches on the walls, plus a multi-storey underground mausoleum behind. The oldest monument is late Republican, the others of the first century A.D.; the Municipality recalls the doumbars of officers of the Imperial fleet of Miseno, with portraits and birds frescoed and an inscription citing a military of the ship Virtus.

Why go. The tombs of the sailors of the Roman fleet of Miseno.

opening only on request with reservation required (pa-fleg.communication@cultura.gov.it, 393 31188993)
