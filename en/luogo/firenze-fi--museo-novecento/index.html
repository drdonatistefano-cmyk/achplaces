Museo Novecento

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

The Italian art of the XX and XXI century in the ancient Hospital of Leopoldine, with exhibitions and temporary installations.

Why go. The modern counterpoint to Santa Maria Novella.

  • Where Piazza Santa Maria Novella 10, Firenze
  • Entry €13 whole; €9 from 18 to 25 years; free under 18
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website musefirenze.it
  • Opening hours official page
