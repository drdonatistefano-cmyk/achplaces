Laghi di Monticolo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers

Two emerald green lakes, large and small, in the thick woods of Monticolo near San Michele, in a protected landscape area of 100 hectares frequented by hikers, knights and bathers; from the large lake stands among the reeds the "Seeschlössl" . A signposted path runs around both lakes starting from the parking lot.

Why go. Baths and walks in the woods just a stone's throw from the vineyards.
