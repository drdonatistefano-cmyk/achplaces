Museo del Nastro Azzurro

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1949 and unique in Italy, it is located in the Santa Giustina complex, in the basement of the MuSa: military valor decorations, Risorgimento relics and materials of the two world wars to tell the decorated from 1793 to today, with uniforms, flags, weapons and coins.

Why go. Two centuries of stories of soldiers and decorated.

in the MuSa complex, closed from 5 October 2026 for works, reopened scheduled in spring 2027
