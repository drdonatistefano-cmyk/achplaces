Parco archeologico di Suasa e Domus dei Coiedii

History and architecture · Archaeology

Remains of the Roman city of Suasa, abandoned in the 6th century A.D., excavated by the University of Bologna with the Superintendence: base road, commercial forum, two necropolis, houses and the amphitheatre (end of the 1st century BC, discovered in 1990), where performances are held in summer. The heart of the park is the Domus dei Coiedii, from the 1st century A.D., at the most splendid in the 2nd century: under a modern roof, with raised walkways, mythological, floral and geometric mosaics and a floor with more than fifteen types of marble form the most important unitary complex of the Marches. Opening hours: 24/4-30/6 and 1/9-8/12 Saturday, Sunday and holidays 15:30-19:30; July-August Tuesday-Saturday 16-20, Sunday 10:30-12:30 and 16-20.

Why go. The most important Roman mosaics in the Marche region.
