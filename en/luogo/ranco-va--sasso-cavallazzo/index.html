Sasso Cavallazzo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

The "Sass Cavalasc" is a colossal erratic rock on the shore of the Verbano, in the territory of Ranco near the border with Ispra, which emerges from the water next to a beach. Like the other erratic boulders in the country, it was transported by glaciers that covered the entire Lake Maggiore during the glaciations.

Why go. A giant of rock carried by the glaciers, straight into the lake.
