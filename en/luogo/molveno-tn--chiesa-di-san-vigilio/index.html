Chiesa di San Vigilio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the XIII century at the cemetery, Romanesque plant with Gothic elements added later: a frescoed bezel with Our Lady, St. John the Baptist and Jesus and, in the apse, a cycle of frescoes with a Last Supper.

Why go. Medieval frescoes in the oldest church in the country.
