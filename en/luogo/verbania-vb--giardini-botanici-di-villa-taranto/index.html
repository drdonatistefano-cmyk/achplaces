Giardini Botanici di Villa Taranto

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Between Intra and Pallanza, the garden created by the Scottish captain Neil McEacharn, which in the 1930s bought about 20 hectares of land; opened to the public since 1952, preserves about 1,000 non-native species and 20,000 among species and varieties of botanical interest. They boarded by the entrance. Opening season and tickets on the official website.

Why go. One of the most important botanical gardens in Europe.
