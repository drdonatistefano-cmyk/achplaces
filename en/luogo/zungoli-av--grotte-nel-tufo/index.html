Grotte nel tufo

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Under the old town there are caves dug into the tuff, on several levels and connected to each other, dating back to the Byzantine era and used in time as a refuge and cellar; today there matures the podolic caciocavallo, aged in cave for at least two years.

Why go. The podolic caciocavallo that matures in the tuff.
