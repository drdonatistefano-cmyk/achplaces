Museo di arte religiosa alpina

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Founded in 1994 in the premises next to the parish church of San Vincenzo Martire, on an ancient place of worship overlooking the Valley of Susa, it was born from the exhibition "Valle di Susa": art and history from the 11th to the 18th century in 1977; since 2001 it is part of the diocesan museum system. It collects works from the numerous chapels of the territory: above all wooden statuary from the 15th to the 20th century, with seventeenth-century pieces of the Moriana carving schools (Clappier of Bessan, Claude Simon of Bramans), goldsmiths and sacred vestments. The visit also includes the church; openings on dates indicated by the Diocesan Cultural Centre.

Why go. The wooden statues of the Alpine carving workshops between Piedmont and Savoy.
