Basilica paleocristiana di Col di Zuca

History and architecture · Archaeology

On the small hill of Col di Zuca (cuel di Cjucie), west of the village, stood a place of early Christian worship used between the V and VII century AD; to the oldest phase belongs a basilica with a Mosaic presbyterial bench of Aquileian type, and remains visible part of the elegant mosaic floor with geometric and floral motifs.

Why go. Early Christian mosaics of the Aquileian type in Carnia.
