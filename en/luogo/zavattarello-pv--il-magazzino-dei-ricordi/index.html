Il Magazzino dei Ricordi

Museums · Themed

Museum of art, culture and agriculture curated by an association founded in 2004, which reconstructs the daily peasant and artisan life of the Oltrepò between the Eight and twentieth century: peasant cuisine, tailoring, barberia, school and shoemaker shops, blacksmith and blacksmith.

Why go. A century ago in the Oltrepò, room by room.

open only on Saturdays and holidays 15:30-18; different address between MiC (Via Vittorio Emanuele) and in-Lombardy (Cascina Mirani)
