Museo civico della Media Valle del Liri

Museums · City history

In the former convent of Minor Conventuals next to St. Francis (XIV century, rebuilt between 1683 and 1727 after the earthquake of 1654), built on the foundations of a Roman villa; established in 1979, opened in 2005 and completed in 2010, it develops on three floors from prehistory to the twentieth century. The materials of the sacred area underneath the Cathedral (the bronze thesaurus, headphone dedicated to Minerva, altar of Mars, architectural terracotta), statues of military tribes, medieval capitals, the copy of the diploma of 1292 of Charles II of Anjou and a section on the Duchy Boncompagni and the first paper mills. Open Monday and Wednesday-Friday 9-13, Saturday 9-13 and 16-20, Sunday 15-19; closed on Tuesday.
