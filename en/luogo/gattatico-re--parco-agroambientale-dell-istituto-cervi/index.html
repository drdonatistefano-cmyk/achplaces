Parco agroambientale dell'Istituto Cervi

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

Next to the museum, an outdoor eco-museum that promotes agricultural practices to protect the historical agricultural landscape, such as the "Piantata Reggiana" (Piantata Reggiana), in 2011 there was inaugurated a row of ancient fruits of Emilia-Romagna.

Why go. The old Reggian countryside, outdoors.
