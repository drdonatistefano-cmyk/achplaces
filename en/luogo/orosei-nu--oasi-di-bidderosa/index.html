Oasi di Bidderosa

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Park of 860 hectares entrusted to the Municipality of Orosei, 13 kilometers north of the village to the beach of Berchida: five white sand coves, about one kilometer long each, between granite reliefs, age-old junipers, pine trees, holm oaks and cork. The Curcurica sa lagoon hosts sedentary and migratory birds that can be observed from birdwatching stations. You enter by car from the SS 125 stopping at the command of the Forest Office; on foot or by bike no seasonal limit, in compliance with the park regulations.

Why go. Five white coves inside a protected park.
