Chiese di Torricella e di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Two churches outside the historic center: that of Torricella, a small country chapel, dates back to the twelfth century; that of San Rocco in the fifteenth century.

Why go. The oldest churches in the territory.
