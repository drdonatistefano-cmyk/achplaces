Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals

It dates back to the end of the 10th century, remodeled in Romanesque forms in the 12th century; the façade was built in 1231 by master Zano. Inside fragments of Byzantine sculpture, including Daniel in the lions' pit on Roman columns in the presbytery, the wooden choir of the seventeenth century and the funerary inscriptions of the lords of Bovino.

Why go. The facade of 1231 and Byzantine sculptures.
