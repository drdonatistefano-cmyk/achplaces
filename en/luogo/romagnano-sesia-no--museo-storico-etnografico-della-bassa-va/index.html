Museo storico etnografico della Bassa Valsesia

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Promoted by Maria Adriana Prolo, founder of the Museo del Cinema in Turin, and built in 1973 in the halls of Villa Caccia. It gathers, largely for the gift of citizens, peasants and artisans, the testimonies of the local agricultural and wine civilization: tools and agricultural machinery with a press about 13 meters long (with 3D reconstruction), the phases of the wine production and the cellar of the villa, the crafts with historical photos, and reconstructions of kitchen, room, school and tavern. Open from 15 March to 30 November, Saturday and Sunday 15-18.30; groups and schools by reservation (tel. 342 1631245).

Why go. Country life and wine of the Lower Valsesia in an antonellian villa.

  • Where Villa Caccia, Viale Antonelli 1, Romagnano Sesia
  • Entry €7.50, reduced €5.50, family €15
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
