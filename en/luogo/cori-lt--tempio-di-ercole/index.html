Tempio di Ercole

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the acropolis of Cori, built between '89 and '80 B.C. by the magistrates Marco Maglio and Lucio Turpilio, whose names are engraved on the architrave of the door; it is a Doric temple reinterpreted in an italic key, with the pronao of eight columns in travertine about 7 meters high, trigliph frieze and metope. National monument since 1898, dominates the Pontina Plain.

Why go. The Roman temple overlooking the Pontina plain, beautiful at sunset.
