Mostra permanente dell'Associazione Scarpèti

Local life and traditions · Crafts

The association preserves the history of the valley with meetings, events and a permanent exhibition of local handicrafts: wooden objects, shoes, embroidery and sewing.

Why go. The shoes and the ability to make Val Colvera.
