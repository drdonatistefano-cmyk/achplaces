Museo archeologico nazionale di Adria

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In a large tree-lined park, it tells the city from the Bronze Age to the end of the Roman era, starting from the collection of the Bocchi family, which collected more than 6,000 artifacts between the Seven and the nineteenth century. Among the strong pieces the Tomb of the Biga of the III century B.C., with chariot and three horses recovered with its excavation context in 1938, attic ceramics with black and red figures, Etruscan ores, ambre, the milestone of Popilio Lenate on the Via Popillia and the famous showcase of Roman glass, with blown cups in the mould of the Syro-Palestinian glassmaker Ennione (I century A.D.). Closed on Mondays; in the garden an archaeological theme playground.

Why go. The Tomb of the Biga and the colored Roman glass.
