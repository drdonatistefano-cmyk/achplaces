Chiostro di Voltorre

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

On the site of a first monastic complex of the 5th century a Romanesque church arose in the 11th century; the cloister, behind the church of San Michele, and the bell tower dates back to the 12th century, with the cloister of Lanfranco da Ligurno. The colonnade has capitals of different styles; national monument since 1911, today houses modern art exhibitions.

Why go. A Lombard Romanesque cloister on the lake.
