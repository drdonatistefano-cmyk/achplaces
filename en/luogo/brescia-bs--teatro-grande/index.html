Teatro Grande

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built in 1810 instead of the Accademia degli Erranti, neoclassical, with five orders of stages and the Ridotto frescoed with golden stuccoes and mirrors; national monument, with guided tours in summer.

Why go. The Brescian music salon.
