Aule didattiche archeologiche e mostra permanente di Fossa Nera

Museums · Archaeological

On the first floor of the former Collegio Cavanis, seat of the municipal library, the classrooms inaugurated in 2014 host workshops on the archaeology of the Piana di Lucca with diorami and a small permanent exhibition of finds, in copy and original; the originals come from the excavations of Fossa Nera.

Why go. Excavation exhibits seen from close up.
