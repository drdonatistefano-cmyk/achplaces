Castello Cantelmo e Museo del Territorio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Medieval castle, once among the most important garrisons of the area: the imposing remains are restored and the pentagonal tower is the core of the defensive system. It houses art exhibitions and the route of the Museo del Territorio della Riserva, in five rooms with plastic and reconstructions, from the history of the Peligni to the Middle Ages to the popular traditions.

Why go. The Cantelmo Tower and the history of the valley.
