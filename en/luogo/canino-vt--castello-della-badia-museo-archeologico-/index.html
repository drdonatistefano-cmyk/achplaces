Castello della Badia – Museo archeologico di Vulci

Museums · Archaeological

A castle in lava stone documented from the IX century, already Benedictine abbey, Farnese, Luciano Bonaparte and Torlonia; the museum tells the Etruscan-Roman city with the findings of the necropolis.
