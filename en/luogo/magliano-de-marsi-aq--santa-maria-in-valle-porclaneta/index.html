Santa Maria in Valle Porclaneta

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

What remains of a Benedictine ward isolated between secular oaks at the slopes of Velino, in the Sirente-Velino Regional Park: the first news dates back to the middle of the 11th century, when the monastery passed under Montecassino. It is one of the most important examples of Abruzzi Romanesque, with Arabic-Hispanic, Byzantine and Longobard influences; it preserves the ambo and ciborium of the 12th century (1150) of Nicodemus and Roberto masters, the iconostasis with plutei decorated with griffins, eagles and lions and a very rare wooden architrave, frescoes of the fifteenth century and the external apse rebuilt in polygonal form in the thirteenth century. Visit by telephone appointment according to the MiC card.

Why go. Ambones and Romanesque iconostasis among the most beautiful in Abruzzo.

the MiC card is closed every day: visit only by booking by phone
