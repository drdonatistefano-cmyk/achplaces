Roseto della Sorpresa

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

Garden in Serra Perno with a collection of over 500 specimens of botanical and ancient roses collected by Professor Piero Amerio; it is part of the historical gardens of Piedmont.

Why go. Over 500 ancient roses in the hill.
