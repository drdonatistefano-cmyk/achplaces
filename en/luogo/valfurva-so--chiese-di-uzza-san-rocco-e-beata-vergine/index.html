Chiese di Uzza: San Rocco e Beata Vergine della Misericordia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Uzza rise the church of San Rocco, probably of 777 and rebuilt in 1491, and the octagonal church of the Blessed Virgin of Mercy, of the early eighteenth century.

Why go. Two churches of distant eras in the same village.
