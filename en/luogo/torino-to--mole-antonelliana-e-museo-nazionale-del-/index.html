Mole Antonelliana e Museo Nazionale del Cinema

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

167 meters high, born as a synagogue and symbol of the city, hosts the National Cinema Museum from its origins to today; the dome can be reached on foot or with the panoramic elevator.

Why go. The symbol of Turin and the view of the Alps.
