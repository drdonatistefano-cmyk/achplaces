Palazzo della Cancelleria

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palazzo cinquecentesco traditionally attributed to the Sansovino and probably the residence of the Di Monte, whose coat of arms it bears; since 1761 the headquarters of the Community Chancellery, today it houses the Municipal Historical Archives with over 4000 pre-unitary units. On the façade two orders of five windows and a niche with Madonna and Child; on the side a portal with the coat of arms Baldi and the motto

Why go. A curious motto about a palace of Sansovino.
