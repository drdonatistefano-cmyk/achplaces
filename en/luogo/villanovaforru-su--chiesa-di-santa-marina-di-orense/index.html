Chiesa di Santa Marina di Orense

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Dedicated to a Spanish martyr, it is the centerpiece of the most heartfelt festivals in the country, celebrated twice a year, Monday and Tuesday after Easter and in mid-July, with processions, singing in Sardinian and distribution of the blessed basil.

Why go. The church of basil festivals.
