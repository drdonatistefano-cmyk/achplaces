Castello di Taormina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Ancient defensive fortress at the top of the city, which according to Italia.it hosts the Museum of the Sicilian Middle Ages; it is managed by the Naxos Taormina Archaeological Park.

Why go. The fortress above Taormina.

  • Where Monte Tauro, Taormina
  • Entry €10, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
