Teatro Vincenzo Bellini

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Heir of an eighteenth-century room, was renovated by the architect Vincenzo Costa and inaugurated in 1846; the restoration of 1882 (Carlo Sada and Antonino Grasso) gave him the present appearance, with a horseshoe room, 44 loggias on three orders and golden stuccoes, and curtain of Giuseppe Rapisarda with Timoleonte in the temple of Adrano. The Liberty façade, completed in the early twentieth century by Gaspare Silvestri Amari, is crowned by the group of Mario Moschetti with Tragedy, Music and Comedy. Since about 1920 it bears the name of Bellini; it appears in the Italian-style Divorce of Pietro Germi; it was recovered in 2004 after more than twenty years of closure.

Why go. Gold fillings and a set by Pietro Germi.
