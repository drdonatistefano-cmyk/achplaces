Laghi di Fusine

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

Two basins of glacial origin, Lower and Upper, connected by an underground water system, on the border with Slovenia, where the chain of Ponze, Mangart and Masso Pirona are mirrored. Spruce, spruce and beech woods host deer, deer, chamois and migratory birds; a path climbs to the Zacchi Refuge and to Capanna Ponza.

Why go. The alpine postcard lake under the Mangart.
