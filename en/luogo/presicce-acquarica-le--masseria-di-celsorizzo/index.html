Masseria di Celsorizzo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

At the north-western edge of the town of Acquarica del Capo, it preserves the remains of a Norman tower, an ancient crypt and the little church of St. Mary of Oils, very remodeled.

Why go. Norman tower and crypt in the Salento countryside.
