Museo etnografico della Piana del Dragone

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 1999 by Antonio Marra, it tells the peasant and artisan civilization between the late nineteenth and the sixties of the twentieth century: it rises on the daily life marked by the seasons, religious festivals and work in the fields, with attention to the patriarchal family and the agricultural economy of the Plain. Telephone 0828 982073.

Why go. The peasant life of the Dragon Plain.
