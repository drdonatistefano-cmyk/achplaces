Castello di Montecuccolo e Centro museale (CEM)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On a rock spur above the valley of Scoltenna-Panaro, about three kilometers from the center, it was a residence and military center of the Montecuccoli, which controlled dozens of towers guarding the streets for Tuscany; the mast is on the highest point, surrounded by three circles of walls. A Gothic portal introduces to the medieval village with the Palazzo del Podestà and the fifteenth-century church of San Lorenzo. The castle, completely restored in 2009, hosts the Montecuccolo Museum Centre with the Donation Raffaele Biolchini, the country rediscovered by Gino Covili and the Naturalistic Museum of Frignano.

Why go. Castle and village intact above the valley.
