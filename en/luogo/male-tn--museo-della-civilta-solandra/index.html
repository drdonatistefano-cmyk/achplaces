Museo della Civiltà Solandra

Museums · Themed

Ethnographic Museum on the ground floor of the former Palazzo Pretorile, once home of the Austrian barracks: it tells the life in Val di Sole in the last century with original tools such as the loom and the kangaroo, the reconstructions of the stua and the cuisine and the traditional solandra house, the carpenter, blacksmith, breeder, weaver and calderaio and miniature models of the Venetian sawmills and farms. A section is dedicated to the mycologist Don Giacomo Bresadola.

Why go. The peasant life of Val di Sole, room by room.
