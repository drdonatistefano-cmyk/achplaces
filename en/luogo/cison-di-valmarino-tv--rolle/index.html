Rolle

History and architecture · Villages · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fraction between vineyards and wooded hills, with a small waterfall that descends from a rock wall, in the area of the Prosecco hills of Conegliano and Valdobbiadene UNESCO World Heritage.

Why go. The village among the UNESCO vineyards.
