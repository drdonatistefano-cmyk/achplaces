Tempio del Valadier ed Eremo di Santa Maria Infra Saxa

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Octagonal temple in white travertine with a lead-coated dome, desired in 1828 by Pope Leo XII and designed by Giuseppe Valadier in a cave of the Throat of Frasassi; behind the hermitage, quoted in 1029, partly dug into the rock and once Benedictine cloistered monastery. It is reached with about 700 meters of paved path.

Why go. A neoclassical temple set in the rock.
