Eremo di San Salvatore

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Natural cave on a side of Mount Antuni, overlooking the lake, frequented from about 1300; it preserves two frescoes painted in 1640.

Why go. A hermitage in the cave above the water.

visits only with guided tour and by reservation
