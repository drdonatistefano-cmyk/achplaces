Chiesa parrocchiale di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The mother church of all the others of the Valle Antigorio: originally Romanesque, lower and narrower, with the bell tower dated 1026; enlarged in 1616 and 1777.

Why go. The mother church of the valley, with the bell tower of 1026.
