Ecomuseo della pietra - Cava del Tupinet e fornaci

Museums · Themed

Historical cable in Tupinet, on the road to the Montano Park, abandoned after the Second World War: the route shows with wooden figures the manual phases of stone processing in the nineteenth century, with original elements of the quarry and panels. Along the main road there is a rebuilt lime furnace. Visits only in small groups, with authorized guide and favorable time (information Office Il Barba).

Why go. How the Stone of Luserna was hand-crushed.
