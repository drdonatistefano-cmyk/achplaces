Castello di Bornato e Villa Orlando

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

13th century castle built on the remains of an older building, with crenellated walls, towers, buttresses, moat, drawbridge and underground; inside the Gandini family had built in 1564 a Renaissance villa with frescoed halls, rare example of a Renaissance villa inside a medieval castle, since the 1930s of the Orlando family. The park has an Italian garden and an English garden with centuries-old trees, including a sophora japonica of about 200 years old; you can also visit the nineteenth-century secret cave, the ancient cellars under the main tower and the sacristy of the private chapel of St. Francis with sacred books of the eighteenth century and a Biblia of 1536. Guided tours and tastings for a fee (tel. 030 725006).

Why go. A Renaissance villa frescoed inside the walls of a castle, among the vineyards.
