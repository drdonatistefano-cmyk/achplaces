Grotta del Trullo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

In the countryside around the village, imposing karst cavities embellished with crystal-clear formations and a forest of stalactites and alabaster stalagmites.

Why go. An underground world of alabaster just a stone's throw from the village.
