Centro di documentazione territoriale (Museo del Territorio)

Museums · Themed

Small exhibition space at the entrance of the village, on the ground floor of the primary school Sante De Sanctis, with sections on geology and formation of the gorge of the Tane, excavations and finds from the ancient Paleolithic to the late Bronze Age, the Etruscan tomb of Soriano with vases of the Orvieto Group (VI century BC) and the history of the castrum of Parrano, with copies of the Statute of 1559. It can be combined with the excursion to the Tane.

Why go. To figure out the tanes before you go down the throat.
