Funivia e Piani di Bobbio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

From Barzio climbs the historic cable car to the Piani di Bobbio, renowned for winter sports; the name comes from the abbey of Bobbio (Piacenza), of which they were alpine pastures. In summer hiking rings between 1,800 and 2,000 meters around the Campelli and towards Artavaggio, easy (2h30-3h30) or challenging as the Path of the Roads with equipped stretches.

Why go. Winter snow and hiking trails in summer.
