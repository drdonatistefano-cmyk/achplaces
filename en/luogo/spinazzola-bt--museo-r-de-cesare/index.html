Museo «R. De Cesare»

Museums · City history

Dedicated to politician and writer Raffaele De Cesare (1845-1918), he collects ceramics, coins and instruments of the Roman age, an ethnographic section with tools from the nineteenth century to the mid-Twentieth century and a geological section with plio-pleistocene and clay materials of the territory.

Why go. From the Roman Spinazzola to the geology of the Murgia.
