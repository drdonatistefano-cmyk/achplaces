Museo Civico Paleontologico del Fortore

Museums · Themed

Born in 1996 on the initiative of the Archeoclub d'Italia di Baselice and since 2013 in convenient to the Municipality, on the ground floor of Palazzo Lembo exhibits fossils of 20-100 million years ago, especially molluscs and lamellibranches, a section of minerals and rocks and a small part dedicated to peasant civilization; other sections are planned on the first floor after the restoration of the premises.

Why go. Fossils of a hundred million years in the Fortore.
