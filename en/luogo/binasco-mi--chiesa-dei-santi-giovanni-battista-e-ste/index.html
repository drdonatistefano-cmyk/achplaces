Chiesa dei Santi Giovanni Battista e Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded in 1750 by architect Giulio Galliori, the works stopped in 1768 due to lack of funds and ended in 1783, date engraved on the choir; the façade, which had to be covered with stuccoes and marbles, remained unfinished. A unique nave with three chapels on each side, dome at the crossroads and interior entirely frescoed in the style of Lombard Rococo illusionism; in the apse a neoclassical circular marble temple with six Corinthian columns above the main altar.

Why go. An 18th-century interior entirely frescoed.
