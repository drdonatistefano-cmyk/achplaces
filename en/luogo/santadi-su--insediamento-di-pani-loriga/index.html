Insediamento di Pani Loriga

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The most important site of Santadi, frequented from the 4th millennium BC to the 8th century A.D.: domus de Janas on the southern slopes, a nuragic complex on the summit with the nuraghe Diana incorporated in the double walls, and the Phoenician-punic settlement founded in the second half of the 7th century BC, of which remain the foundations of acropolis, residential district and sanctuary; at the sides the Phoenician necropolis with fossa and the Punic one in room. Partial motor accessibility, parking, recommended hiking shoes.

Why go. Six thousand years of history on one hill.
