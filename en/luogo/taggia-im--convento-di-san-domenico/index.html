Convento di San Domenico

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Dominican monastery of the fifteenth century with cloister with black stone columns; the gallery in the former dormitory preserves polyptychs and frescoes by Ludovico Brea and Canavesio and the Adoration of the Magi del Parmigianino (1528-29).
