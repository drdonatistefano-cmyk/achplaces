Casa Barezzi

Museums · House museums

The house of the patron and father-in-law of Verdi: in the hall, seat of the Philharmonic, the young composer performed for the first time in 1830; piano, portraits and letters autographs.

Why go. Where the Verdi musician was born.

  • Where Via Roma 119, Busseto

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
