Teatro comunale

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Small Italian theatre of the second half of the nineteenth century, with a porticoed façade with brick columns and richly decorated interior; still used for shows and concerts.

Why go. A miniature 19th century theatre.

Accessible only on request, to do a few days before (Italia.it)
