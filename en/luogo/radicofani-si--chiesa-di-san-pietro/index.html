Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Romanesque church of the thirteenth century, damaged in war and restored in 1946, with a splendid collection of Robbian terracotta and wooden statues, including the Madonna and Child of Francesco di Valdambrino.

Why go. Behind the church there is a panoramic square.

  • Where Piazza San Pietro, Radicofani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
