Oasi WWF di Macchiagrande

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Around 280 hectares of maquis on the dunes and Mediterranean holm oak in the Statale Reserve of the Roman coast, with deer, hertrics and turtles of Hermann; together with Foce dell'Arrone and the Vasche di Maccarese forms the system of oases managed by the WWF.

Why go. Wild nature a stone's throw from Rome.
