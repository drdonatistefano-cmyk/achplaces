Villa Arbusto e giardino, Museo Angelo Rizzoli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

The eighteenth-century villa, formerly the residence of the Duke of Atri, on a hill above Piazza Santa Restituta in front of Monte di Vico; in 1952 the publisher and producer Angelo Rizzoli, who collected rare plants from the five continents, from the domestic pines symbol of Ischia to jacaranda, ginkgo and eucalyptus. The garden is visited with the museum; in the villa the Museo Angelo Rizzoli exhibits more than 500 photographs of the years when Ischia became the destination of politicians, divines and journalists.

Why go. Rizzoli botanical park and the sweet life of Ischia.
