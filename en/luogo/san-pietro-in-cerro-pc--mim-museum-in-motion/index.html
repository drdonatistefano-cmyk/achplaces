MIM - Museum in Motion

Museums · Modern art

Museum of contemporary art set up after the restorations of 2001 in the attic and along the ancient walkway between the two towers of the castle: works especially of contemporary Italian and foreign artists, also Piacenza, between painting, sculpture and photography, exposed in rotation.

Why go. Contemporary art in the walkway of the round.
