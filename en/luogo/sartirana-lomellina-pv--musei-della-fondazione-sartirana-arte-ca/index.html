Musei della Fondazione Sartirana Arte (castello e Pila)

Museums · Modern art

Since 1993 the Fondazione Sartirana Arte has been managing the interior of the castle with one of the most complete museum routes of Lomellina: contemporary silver and artist jewels, and cataloged works including sculptures by Fausto Melotti, Arnaldo Pomodoro, Alik Cavaliere, Marcello Pirro and Paolo Cristiani; exhibitions of painting, sculpture and handicraft are held in the castle. La Pila, an eighteenth-century building for the processing of rice restored as an exhibition space between 1990 and 1996, hosts antiques and vintage design exhibitions and a permanent exhibition dedicated to designer Ken Scott. Italia.it advises you to check the timetables before the visit.

Why go. Silvers, artist jewelry, design and fashion inside a Visconti castle.
