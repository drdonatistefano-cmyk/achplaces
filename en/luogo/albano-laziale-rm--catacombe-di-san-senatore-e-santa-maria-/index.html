Catacombe di San Senatore e Santa Maria della Stella

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Paid entry

Catacombs along the Appian Way to the fifteenth mile, under church and convent of Santa Maria della Stella (mid XVI century, current appearance since 1687), used for burials from the end of the third-beginning IV century; the central crypt was frescoed in successive epochs. Next to the cemetery of cholera, where in 1867 more than 500 citizens were buried. Free admission with voluntary contributions.

Why go. The early Christians of the Albani Hills underground.

  • Where Via della Stella 5, Albano Laziale
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
