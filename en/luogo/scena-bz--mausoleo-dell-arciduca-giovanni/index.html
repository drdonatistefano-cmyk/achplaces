Mausoleo dell'arciduca Giovanni

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Neo-Gothic burial church built between 1860 and 1869 on the design of the Viennese Moritz Wappler, considered one of the first and most successful sacred architectures of this style; in red sandstone and granite of the area of Mount Ivigna, rises on an artificial terrace on the west side of the hill. In the crypt the marble sarcophagus of Archduke John of Austria and his wife. Only with guided tour, in the morning, from June to October; closed in winter.

Why go. The neo-Gothic tomb of Archduke John of Austria.
