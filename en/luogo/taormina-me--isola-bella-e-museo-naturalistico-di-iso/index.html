Isola Bella e Museo naturalistico di Isolabella

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Isolotto in the middle of the cove between Capo Sant'Andrea and Capo Taormina, protected since 1984 and regional naturalistic museum since 2011, combined with the mainland by a thin tongue of pebbles that with the high tide can end under water. Lady Florence Trevelyan bought it in 1890 and built a house there: the red villa, called Villa Caronia by the last owner, is surrounded by about 1.6 hectares of terraces and Mediterranean maquis, marked in 1970 by De Agostini as a botanical garden.

Why go. The symbol of Taormina, between pebble beach and garden.

  • Where Via Nazionale, Taormina (sentiero dal belvedere di Isola Bella)
  • Entry €5, reduced €2,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
