Bunker Soratte

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

About 4.5 km of concrete tunnels excavated since 1937 by Mussolini as an air raid shelter for the military leaders, disguised as a Breda weapons factory; since September 1943 for about ten months there was the headquarters of the South Command of Field Marshal Kesselring, and in 1967, in NATO, about 1.3 km became an anti-atomic bunker for government and President of the Republic. Recovered by the municipality, it has been a museum since 2003 and is visited as a "Memory Path" with guided tours of about two hours, including military means, radio and maps.

Why go. World War II and Cold War inside a mountain.
