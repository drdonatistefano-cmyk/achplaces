Museo Internazionale delle Ceramiche (MIC)

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Founded in 1908, according to the regional catalogue, the largest collection of ceramics in the world: from the Faenza majolica to the works of Picasso, Matisse, Fontana and Burri.

Why go. Faenza gave the name to "faïence": here you can understand why.

  • Where Viale Baccarini 19, Faenza
  • Entry For a fee: €12 full (secondary source); with the FaenzaMuseiCard discount of €2 on the second and third museum; does not adhere to the Sunday museum
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.micfaenza.org
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
