Chiesa di San Gregorio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the thirteenth century along the Sambro, with sailing bell tower and porch on the façade; in the Middle Ages it was used as a lazzaretto.

Why go. A former medieval lazzaretto along the stream.
