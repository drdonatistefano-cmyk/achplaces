Ponte romano e Torre medievale

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The three-arched bridge over the Metauro, which the Roman tradition wants, is built in rows of stone blocks with brick parts of subsequent restorations; with the next tower, 24 m high and perhaps on Roman foundations, it forms a single complex that controlled an important ford, served as customs and defended the country. At the foot of the tower there is the public fountain of the "Mascherone" (1886). The tower is visited by reservation through the IAT office (0722.330523).

Why go. The symbol of the country, suspended on the Metauro.
