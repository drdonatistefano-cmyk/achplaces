Villa Sora

History and architecture · Archaeology

Maritime villa of the middle of the first century BC, with a seafront of about 150 meters and a large apse hall; it was in restoration at the time of the eruption of 79 AD and preserves the traces of the yard interrupted.

Why go. A Roman villa surprised by the eruption.

Visits only on Sunday mornings by reservation
