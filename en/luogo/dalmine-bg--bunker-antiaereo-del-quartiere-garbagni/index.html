Bunker antiaereo del Quartiere Garbagni

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

It is one of the two anti-aircraft shelters built in Dalmine in 1943: excavated at 20 meters deep, it was reopened to the public recently. You can visit by day, night or in a route combined with the company town (tel. This appropriation is intended to cover the expenditure of the European Regional Development Fund (ERDF) for the period from 1 January 2014 to 31 December 2020. The city was bombed on July 6, 1944, date remembered by the Square of the Fallen 6 July 1944.

Why go. Get off at 20 meters below the industrial city of war.
