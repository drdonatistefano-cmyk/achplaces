Villa Grock – Museo del Clown

Museums · House museums · Viewpoint

The house of Charles Adrien Wettach, in Grock art, born as Villa Bianca and transformed with the garden; after the death of the clown in 1954 decayed, it was acquired by the Province and restored. The park has columns, exotic trees and Masonic symbols; inside the Clown Museum with interactive settings.

Why go. The clown's house.
