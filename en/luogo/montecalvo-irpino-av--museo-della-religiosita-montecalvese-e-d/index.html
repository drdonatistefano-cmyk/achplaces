Museo della religiosità montecalvese e della memoria pompiliana

Museums · Themed

Founded on 29 December 1898 thanks to the generosity of the faithful, it is one of the oldest museums in Campania and occupies the birthplace of San Pompilio Maria Pirrotti (1710-1766). In five rooms he exhibits objects from the house of the saint and works of art from other churches of Montecalvo, including the statue of the Mother Bella dell'Abbondanza (XVI-XVII century) and a seventeenth-century portrait of the saint. Visit by reservation.

Why go. The patron's birthplace and the devotion of the country.
