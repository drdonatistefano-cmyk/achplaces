Museo civico Carlo Verri

Museums · City history

Founded in 1977 mainly for archaeological finds of Biassono of the Group of Archaeistorical Research of Lambro, next to Villa Verri. On the ground floor there is the ethnological section with ploughs, sowers, wine presses, a leaf cutter for mulberry, a rare incubator for silk baco eggs and the reconstruction of kitchen and bedroom; on the upper floor there are local finds and the numismatic collection, with the storage room of 2235 Roman bronze and silver coins found at the Roman villa of Cascina Sant'Andrea. Detachable sections: since 2006 ©Segno Scrittura Stampa , on the history of communication, next to the library, and since 2009 the peasant world under the arcades of Cascina Ca' Nova.

Why go. Roman archaeology and peasant life of Brianza in a single museum.
