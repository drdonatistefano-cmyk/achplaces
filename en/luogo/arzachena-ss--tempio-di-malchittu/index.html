Tempio di Malchittu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Just over two kilometers from Arzachena, it is the only nuragic religious building that has so far been investigated in the territory: a megaron temple of the Middle Bronze (XVI-XIV century BC), more ancient than the other temples of this type in Sardinia. Dress and room of about 8 meters with seats, counter for the offers, two niches and fireplace ritual in the center; around a collapsed nuraghe, a circular hut and burials in the tafoni. Open every day 9-19.

Why go. The oldest megaron temple on the island.
