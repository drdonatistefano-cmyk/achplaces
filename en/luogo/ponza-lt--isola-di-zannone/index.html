Isola di Zannone

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Uninhabited, since 1979 in the National Park of the Circeo, with landing at Punta del Varo; it is a stop of migratory birds and preserves the remains of the convent of Santo Spirito di Zannone.

Why go. Nature intact in the Circeo Park.
