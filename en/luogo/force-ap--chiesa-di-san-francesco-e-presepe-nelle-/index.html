Chiesa di San Francesco e presepe nelle grotte

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The nineteenth-century church was restored by the architect Sacconi; in the caves below there is a mechanical nativity scene of about 250 square meters, designed in 1992 by Fernando and Ilario Testa and Massimo Benedetti.

Why go. The large mechanized crib in the caves at Christmas.
