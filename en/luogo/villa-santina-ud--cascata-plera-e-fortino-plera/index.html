Cascata Plera e Fortino Plera

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Next to the waterfall, surrounded by vegetation, on the right bank of Tagliamento there is a cave work of the Vallo Alpino del Littorio of the 1940s; the fort has been restored and you can visit with guided tours on weekends.

Why go. A fortification of the Alpine Valley next to a waterfall.
