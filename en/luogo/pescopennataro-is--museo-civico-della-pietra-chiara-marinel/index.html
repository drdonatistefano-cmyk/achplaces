Museo civico della Pietra «Chiara Marinelli»

Museums · Themed

Founded in 2006, it tells of the tradition of stone processing in the country, where in the eighteenth century a school of stonemasons was born and where the stonemasons still work. It exhibits prehistoric finds of Alto Molise, inhabited already in the lower Paleolithic and Neolithic, including jewels and idols collected by the spouses Pietro Patriarch and Fortuna Ciavolino, who dedicated it to his nephew Chiara Marinelli, who disappeared young, and a handcrafted crib of polished stones.

Why go. The stone and its chisels, from prehistory to today.
