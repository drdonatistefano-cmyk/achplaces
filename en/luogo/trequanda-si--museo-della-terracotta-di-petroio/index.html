Museo della Terracotta di Petroio

Museums · Themed

It tells the production of terracotta that gave work to the whole country, with the "Little Tuscany" of the potter Rodolfo, which reproduced in terracotta the villages of the area.

Why go. The Tuscan villages in miniature.
