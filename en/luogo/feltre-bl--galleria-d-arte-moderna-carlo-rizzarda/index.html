Galleria d'arte moderna Carlo Rizzarda

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Born at the will of the blacksmith Carlo Rizzarda and opened in 1938, it preserves over 400 wrought iron works made between 1910 and 1930, as well as ceramics, glass (also by Carlo Scarpa), painting and sculpture of the nineteenth century; in December 2018 it received as a gift the collection of glasses Nasci-Franzoia (738 pieces).

Why go. The wrought iron author in a unique gallery in Italy.

  • Where Via Paradiso 8, Feltre
  • Entry €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
