Area archeologica e Museo archeologico di Fiesole

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The Roman theatre, the thermal baths and the Etruscan-Roman temple of the Etruscan city that controlled the streets of the Apennines, with the museum of artifacts.

Why go. The Roman theatre with views of the hills.

  • Where Largo Fernando Farulli 1, Fiesole
  • Entry €7 archaeological area; €10 with the Archaeological Museum; €12 also with the Bandini Museum
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.museidifiesole.it
  • Opening hours official page
