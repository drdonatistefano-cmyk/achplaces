Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded shortly after the year 1000 and remodeled in the following centuries, it houses a small canvas of the Assumption of the Virgin by Giovan Battista Moroni and a fresco of the fifteenth century Pietà.

Why go. Little Assumption of the Moroni.
