Villa del Principe

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The largest and most sumptuous noble residence in the city, wanted by Andrea Doria from the 1920s and decorated by Perin del Vaga; in 1533 the emperor Charles V stayed there.

Why go. Andrea Doria's court.
