Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated on 7 August 1982 in the village of Pozzo, it was born from the collection of objects of rural life made for twenty years by Gelindo Lenarduzzi; the Municipality bought and restored a building to accommodate it provided that it remained in Pozzo, and the collection then grew with donations from citizens and companies. Among the pieces three handmade pendulum clocks, wagons and agricultural tools of the twentieth century and, the oldest, gramola and combs for hemp of 1810. Open on Sunday 10:30-12:30 (MiC card updated to 2020); audioguide.

Why go. The collection of a life on the peasant world of Friuli.
