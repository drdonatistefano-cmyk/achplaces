Cenacolo di Sant'Apollonia

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the refectory of the monastery, the Last Supper of Andrea del Castagno with the Resurrection, the Crucifixion and the Deposition of 1447.

Why go. A very powerful Last Supper, almost always without a tail.

  • Where Via XXVII Aprile 1, Firenze
  • Entry €5 whole; €15 for all Florentine dinners (7 days)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility 7 cm step at the entrance, no ramp
  • Official website www.destinationflorence.com
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
