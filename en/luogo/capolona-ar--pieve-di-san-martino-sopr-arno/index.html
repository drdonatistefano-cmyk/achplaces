Pieve di San Martino Sopr'Arno

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Cited in 1017 among the goods of the canons of the Arezzo Church, it was transformed in the nineteenth century; it remains the semicircular apse and some original masonry.

Why go. One of the oldest documented parishes in the territory.
