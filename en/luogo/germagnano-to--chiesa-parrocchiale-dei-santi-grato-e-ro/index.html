Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Grato e Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises on the site of a church already existing in the fourteenth century (Saint Peter in Pincoli, then dedicated to the Saints Grateful and Rocco) and became parish in 1765, separating from Lanzo. At the main altar eighteenth-century altar with the Virgin and the patrons; at the altar of the Rosary a crucifix of the sixteenth century, with paintings of the Madonna del Rosario and purgant souls surrounded by Mysteries.

Why go. Six-seventh-century sacred art in the village church.
