Chiesa di San Germano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Medieval church erected in 1480 on an ancient Roman temple and renovated several times: it preserves a pulpit of the fourteenth century with trilobed arch and figures of saints, two saints on Romanesque lions coming from the destroyed church of St. John the Baptist, a canvas of Neapolitan school with the Baptist and, in the apse, the Creation of the world frescoed in 1949 by Amedeo Trivisonno.

Why go. The fourteenth-century pulpit and Trivisonno's apse.
