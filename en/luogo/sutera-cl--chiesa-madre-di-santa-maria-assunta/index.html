Chiesa Madre di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the Rabato, built in 1370 by Baron Giovanni Chiaramonte on the site of a mosque of about 875, of which it incorporates elements; renovated in 1585, it has a Renaissance portal and a marble baptismal font of 1495.

Why go. A church born on a mosque.
