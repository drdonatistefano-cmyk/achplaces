Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta e statua di Sant'Eufemia

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Built in the 13th century and renovated in 1777, it has a Baroque façade, a Gothic bell tower with double-fores and crypt, and preserves a relic of St. Euphemia's arm. The most famous work is the polychrome statue of the stone saint of Vicenza, 172 cm high, dated around 1450 and brought here in 1454 by Roberto De Mabilia from the Padua workshop of Mantegna: the saint holds one hand in the mouths of a lion and with the other holds the three-top mountain with the castle, symbol of Irsina. Attribution to Mantegna is now discussed by scholars.

Why go. The Lucan Mantegna.
