Museo Casa Carducci

Museums · House museums

The house where Giosuè Carducci spent his youth with his family and his father Michele, a doctor; he exhibits works by the artist Antonio Possenti illustrating the poet's most famous poems. The gravestones of his father and brother Dante are still visible in the village cemetery.

Why go. The house of Carducci's youth.
