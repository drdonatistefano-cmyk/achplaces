Percorso dei menhir: San Vincenzo, Menhir Gemelli e Vicinanze

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the town there are the menhirs of Vico Nuovo (the "Menhir Gemelli") Cross of Fausa, Santa Maria di Costantinopoli and San Vincenzo, the latter in a small square of Via Menhir, probably of the Bronze Age and starting point of the megalithic route. In the countryside, beyond the underground mill, the Menhir Vicinanze 1 door on the eastern face two crosses engraved after the edict of Thessalonica, and about 200 meters the Menhir Vicinanze 2 rises on a rocky spur; other menhirs are Mount Tongolo, Palanzano and Madonna del Rosario.

Why go. A walking itinerary between stones erected from the village to the countryside.
