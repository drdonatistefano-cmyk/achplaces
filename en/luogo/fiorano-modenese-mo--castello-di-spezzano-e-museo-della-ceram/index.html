Castello di Spezzano e Museo della Ceramica

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Of medieval origin, transformed into a palace by the Pio di Savoia since 1529: courtyard portico Renaissance, Sala delle Vedute frescoed around 1596 by Cesare Baglione with 56 views of the State of Sassuolo, Galleria delle Battaglie and chapel with stucco altar from 1699. On the first floor and in the basement, the Museum of Ceramics, from prehistory to industrial and contemporary production, with the multimedia section on work; in the pentagonal tower the municipal vinegar mill. Around a nineteenth century romantic park.

Why go. The 56 views of a small state.
