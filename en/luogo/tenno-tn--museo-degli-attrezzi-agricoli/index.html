Museo degli attrezzi agricoli

Museums · Themed

In the Centre \"Aldo Gorfer" , in rooms with stone vaults: tools for fields, viticulture, breeding and processing of milk , lumberjack , carpenter and barrel , with the reconstruction of stable , cave and open kitchen of a farmhouse .

Why go. The peasant fatigue from haymaking to harvest.
