Giardino storico dell'Abbazia di Novacella

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

At the entrance of the complex, reopened in summer 2004 after a large restoration: a centuries-old sequoia dominates the bottom of the garden, in front of old ginkgo; in the middle a baroque garden with flowers, medicinal herbs and a fountain. Open Monday to Saturday 10-17.

Why go. The garden of the monks, between redwood and ginkgo.
