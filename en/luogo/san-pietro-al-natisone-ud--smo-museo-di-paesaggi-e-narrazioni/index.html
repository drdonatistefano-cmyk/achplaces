SMO, Museo di paesaggi e narrazioni

Museums · Themed

Museum of narration and not collections, multimedia and interactive, dedicated to the cultural landscape from the Julian Alps to the sea and to the history and currentity of the area in which they historically live the Slovenian provinces of Udine, Gorizia and Trieste, with interactive paths and multimedia frescoes; it is also center of documentation, archive and research. Admission fee according to the Touring; open daily, 10-13 and 14-16, with times that can change.

Why go. The Natisone Valleys and the Slovenian culture of Friuli are told in an interactive way.
