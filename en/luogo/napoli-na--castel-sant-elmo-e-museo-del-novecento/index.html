Castel Sant'Elmo e Museo del Novecento

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The largest medieval castle of Naples, of the thirteenth century, on the Vomero, with the Museum of the twentieth century in Naples (over 170 works) and the view on the sea, Vesuvius, islands and Campi Flegrei.

Why go. The largest view of the city.

  • Where Via Tito Angelini 20/A, Napoli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
