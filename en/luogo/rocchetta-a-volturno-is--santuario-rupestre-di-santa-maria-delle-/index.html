Santuario rupestre di Santa Maria delle Grotte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

A solitary church carved into the rock and decorated with frescoes, a precious treasure chest nestled among the rocks, reachable with a short detour from the abbey of San Vincenzo al Volturno.

Why go. A shrine frescoed inside the rock.
