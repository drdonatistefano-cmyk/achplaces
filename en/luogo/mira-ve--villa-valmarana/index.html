Villa Valmarana

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Of the original complex, the Dolfin and purchased in 1734 for Leonardo Valmarana, the two boats and the small church, where the first Valmarana owners are buried: the villa was demolished in 1908 by the owner Antonio Baldan. In 1960 the sculptor Luciano Minguzzi bought the guesthouse and restored the frescoes; today the guest house hosts events and ceremonies. Private property open to the public.

Why go. The frescoed boats of a missing mansion.
