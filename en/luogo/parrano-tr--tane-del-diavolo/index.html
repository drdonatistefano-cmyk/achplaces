Tane del Diavolo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Complex of karst caves that opens onto a limestone wall overlooking the Fosso del Bagno, under the village; there were found finds from the Paleolithic to the late Bronze Age. At the beginning of the gorge there is a spring at about 28 °C; guided tours of the gorge and caves are possible.

Why go. Prehistoric caves in a wild gorge.
