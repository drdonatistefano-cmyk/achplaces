Castello Del Carretto

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built by Enrico II to defend the village, it formed with Cengio, Cosseria and Roccavignale a defensive quadrilateral on the road between Piedmont and Liguria; stone tower and eastern walls are the oldest parts.

Why go. The quadrilateral castle.
