Museo virtuale garibaldino in Aspromonte

Museums · City history

Born within the framework of the project that has restored the Mausoleum of Garibaldi in Aspromonte, it re-reads with multimedia technologies the iconographic material of the Fondazione Spadolini-Nuova Antologia, the Central Museum of the Risorgimento and the DIGIMA project to tell the campaign of Aspromonte of 1862, designed especially for young people.
