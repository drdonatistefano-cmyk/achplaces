Museo civico d'arte - Palazzo Ricchieri

Museums · Old masters

Born in the thirteenth century as a fortified tower of the Ricchieri and transformed into a residence between the Three and the sixteenth century, donated to the city in 1949: wooden ceilings, fifteenth-century frescoes with the history of Tristan and Isotta and works from the thirteenth to the nineteenth century with the center of Friulian art and Pordenone; the starting nucleus were the paintings left by Michelangelo Grigoletti in 1870.

Why go. Tristan and Isotta and Pordenone.
