Parco Serenissima

Nature and landscape · City parks

Ample park with innovative features for its division into thematic areas and with attention to the protection of biodiversity; the Municipality presents it as a space for relaxation, the play of the little ones and the outdoor sports activity, as well as as as a meeting place and aggregation.

Why go. An outdoor break between thematic areas dedicated to biodiversity.
