Casa del Pittore

Museums · Modern art · Viewpoint

Built in 1956-1957 to host the artists involved in the frescoes of Arcumeggia and home to the school of frescoes (summer courses since 1961), it preserves sketches, cardboards, tiles, sketches and ceramics that document the birth of the works; it has a large panoramic terrace on the valley and Lake Maggiore. Owned by the Province of Varese, it hosts exhibitions and guided tours.

Why go. Behind the scenes of the frescoes, with lake view.
