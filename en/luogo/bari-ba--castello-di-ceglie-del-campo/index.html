Castello di Ceglie del Campo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

In the district of Ceglie del Campo, a castle dominated by a square tower about 35 meters high, probably Norman foundation, set on structures of Roman age of ancient Caelia; modified by the Arcamone between the fifteenth and sixteenth century.

Why go. A Norman tower in the southern quarters.
