Area archeologica: foro, porto fluviale e mercati

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Roman open-air city near the centre: the remains of the forum with the civil basilica, the river port with the long row of warehouses and docks embedded in the walls of the fourth century and the late antique markets.

Why go. Walking in the Roman city.
