Parco archeologico dell'antica Abellinum

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Remains of the Roman colony of Abellinum, founded at the end of the II century BC on a Sannite settlement, on the plateau of the Civita along the left bank of Saturday: a stretch of wall in cross-linked work, a public area with spas and hole and a domus of Hellenistic-Pumpian type of about 2,500 m2, perhaps of a freedom of Agrippa. The colony fell after the earthquake of 346 AD and was abandoned during the Greek-Gothic War.

Why go. The Roman city that gave rise to Atripalda and Avellino.
