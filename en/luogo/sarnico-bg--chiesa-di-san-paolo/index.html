Chiesa di San Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Medieval church built in 1428 on the remains of the castle tower, next to Palazzo Gervasoni; its clock bell tower, rebuilt in the mid-nineteenth century, can be seen coming from the lake.

Why go. The bell tower symbol of the village.
