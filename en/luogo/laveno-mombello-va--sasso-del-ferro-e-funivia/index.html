Sasso del Ferro e funivia

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Mount of about 1,062 meters above Laveno, reachable in about 15 minutes by cable car with two cabins from the center to Poggio Sant'Elsa, or by car to Casere (via Cittiglio) and then walk in the woods. From the top you can see the lake, the Campo dei Fiori, the lakes of the province, the Val Grande and the Alps up to Monte Rosa; it is also a takeoff point for hang gliding and paragliding. On arrival bar, restaurant, hotel and shop of local products.

Why go. One of the most spectacular viewpoints on Lake Maggiore.
