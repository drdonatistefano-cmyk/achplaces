Museo Archeologico Nazionale e città antica di Cosa

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Latin colony founded by the Romans in 273 BC on the headland of Ansedonia: walls of over a kilometer with nineteen towers and three gates, the forum and, on the acropolis, the Capitolium and the temple of Mater Matuta; the museum, born with the American Academy in Rome, exhibits the finds of the colony, the port and the necropolis.

Why go. A Roman city overlooking Argentario and Giglio.
