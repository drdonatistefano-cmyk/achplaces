Museo della seta, della canapa e del lino Marta Pozzobon in Girotto

Museums · Themed

Housed in the ancient granaries of the convent of the Carmelite Fathers Scalzi, it collects tools and tools for spinning and weaving hemp, linen and silk, personal and domestic linens, sacred vestments and naturalistic collections of silkworms, chrysalis and butterflies. The path follows the production cycles: for silk from the baco to the finished product, for hemp and flax from plant collection to fabric.

Why go. From the silkworm to the fabric, in the granaries of the convent.

can be visited only by appointment (tel. 030 7356623) according to ISPRA
