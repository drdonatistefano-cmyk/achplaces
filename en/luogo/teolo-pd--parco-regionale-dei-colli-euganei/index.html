Parco Regionale dei Colli Euganei

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Protected area of about 18,694 hectares in the province of Padua, based in Teolo and the first Veneto Regional Park (since 1989): about one hundred volcanic reliefs, none more than 600 meters. A network of walking, cycling and horseback riding trails includes the 78 km cycle ring of Euganean Hills and the 41 km trekking Alta Via dei Colli.

Why go. The volcanic cones of the Colli, to be turned on foot or by bike.
