Chiesa e convento di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built at the beginning of the fourteenth century in memory of the passage of Saint Francis to Sora in 1219, headed for Egypt; the complex is annexed to a late Baroque palace and the former convent houses the Civic Museum.

Why go. The Franciscan memory in the heart of the city.
