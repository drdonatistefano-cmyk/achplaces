Mausoleo delle Carceri Vecchie

History and architecture · Archaeology

Roman funerary mausoleum with circular plan, built along the ancient Appian street in the first imperial age, which preserves remains of the decoration in stucco; not far is the mausoleum called Conocchia. It is entrusted to the Regional Direction National Museums Campania, which organizes extraordinary openings with guided tours, as for European Heritage Days.

Why go. A monumental tomb on the ancient Appia.

Visitable only on request with reservation required (tel. 0823 844206, drm-cam@cultura.gov.it)
