Museo Civico di Palazzo Cuttica

Museums · Old masters

In the eighteenth-century Cuttica palace of Cassine, part of the civic collections with the archaeological finds of Dertona, works such as the polyptych of the Coronation of the Virgin of Gandolfino from Roreto and a section on Napoleon and Marengo.

Why go. The city's collections.
