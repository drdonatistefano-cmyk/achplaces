Esposizione «Preone 200 milioni di anni»

Museums · Science and university

In Palazzo Lupieri, a six-seventh-century Carnic style residence, collects the fossils of the Preone valley and illustrates the geological and biological history of the Carnic Prealps: the first room reconstructs with dioramas environments and animals of the territory, the second tells the area from 200 million years ago with some important fossils. It is part of the CarniaMusei network. Summer time indicated by MiC: June-September 16-18 (data 2020, to be verified).

Why go. The fossils of one of the oldest flying reptiles.
