Museo della tarsia

Museums · Themed

Born to document the tradition of the wooden tarsia of the Rolese, bloomed between the eighteenth and twentieth centuries with the round tables called "Rolini" , requested in the seventeenth century throughout the Duchy of Modena and Reggio and then sold abroad. It exhibits ancient carpenter tools, original documents and some furniture; it is also documentation center with library specialized in wood art and restoration and a photographic archive on the inlaid furniture of Rolo. According to the page of the City is visitable by reservation.

Why go. The history of the "rolini" , the inlaid furniture of the country.
