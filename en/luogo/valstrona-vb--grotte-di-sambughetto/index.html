Grotte di Sambughetto

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Beyond Piana di Fornero, the most interesting underground complex in the valley, with winding tunnels, small waterfalls and holes that tell the geological history of the area; the main cave (Cave of the Witches, old mine) is 707 meters long.

Why go. An underground world in the Sambughetto quarry.
