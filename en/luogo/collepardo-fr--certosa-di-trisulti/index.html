Certosa di Trisulti

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

A 13th century monastic complex renovated in the Baroque age, about 800 m above sea level, a national monument from 1873 and dedicated to the Virgin Assumption, St. Bartholomew and St. Bruno. The single nave church has paintings by Filippo Balbi, the chorus of the chorus of the choristers of the late sixteenth century carved by Jacobò and on the vault the "Gloria" of Blesseds and Saints of Caci (1683); on the square the Palazzo di Innocenzo III houses an important library. The visit is completed by the Italian gardens and the bell tower with three quadrants that mark only the minutes.

Why go. A medieval Carthusian immersed in the Ernic woods.
