Museo Navale di Imperia e Planetario

Museums · Themed

In Borgo Marina, the history of the sea from the seventeenth century to today with archaeological finds, nautical instruments, multimedia installations and simulators; under a green copper dome the planetarium projects over 6,000 stars.

Why go. The sea and the stars.
