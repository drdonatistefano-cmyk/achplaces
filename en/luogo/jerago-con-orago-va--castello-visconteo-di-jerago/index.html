Castello visconteo di Jerago

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Rocca built in the thirteenth century on a small hill by Archbishop Ottone Visconti, perhaps on a previous early medieval settlement, to defend the Contado del Seprio disputed between Torriani and Visconti; the fief was given to a branch of the Visconti, extinct in 1751. Passed to the Bossi, at the end of the eighteenth century it became a noble residence with two terraces with Italian garden and an underground water lily with fake caves, mosaics and shells; in 1960 the restoration was entrusted to the Milanese studio BBPR. It is the stop of the regional route Â"Tour dei castelli viscontei" with Caidate through the valley of the Boya; signposted in the Places of the Heart FAI from 2003 to 2022 and opened by the FAI in 2021 and on the Day FAI of Autumn 2024.

Why go. A castle of Seprio on the route of the Visconti castles.
