Museo del Territorio

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the seventeenth-century cloister of the ancient hospital, already a Dominican convent: sections of archaeology (covered from the 11th-VIII century BC to the Middle Ages), sacred art (two wooden altars from the churches of Sant'Antonio Abate and Santa Maria della Fratta, fragments of frescoes of the Tre-Quattrocento) and ethnography. Open Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday 8.30-12.30.

Why go. From prehistory to peasant civilization.

  • Where Via Udine 4, San Daniele del Friuli
  • Entry €3.50 with guided tour, €2.50 without, students up to 26 years €2; free with FVG Card
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it
