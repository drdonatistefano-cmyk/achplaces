Museo del Paesaggio

Museums · Modern art

In Palazzo Viani Dugnani the Gipsoteca by Paolo Troubetzkoy and the Pinacoteca: painting, sculpture, photography and archaeology, collected since 1909 by Antonio Massara, with sculptures by Arturo Martini. In summer open 10-18, closed on Tuesdays; in other periods by appointment.

Why go. The art of Lake Maggiore.
