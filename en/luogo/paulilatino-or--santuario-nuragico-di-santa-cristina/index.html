Santuario nuragico di Santa Cristina

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Well temple of the final Bronze (XII century B.C.) in basalt cones, with fence to ... serratura..., staircase of 25 steps and chamber to tholos; around the hut of the meetings and, at 200 meters, the nuraghe Santa Cristina of the XV century B.C.

Why go. The most beautiful temple to nuragic well.

  • Where Altopiano di Abbasanta, Paulilatino
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
