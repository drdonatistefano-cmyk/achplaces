Castello ducale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built by the Normans on the remains of a Roman fortress, with the cylindrical Norman tower of the 11th century attributed to Count Drogo; expanded by Frederick II and transformed into a residence in the 17th century by the dukes of Guevara, whose descendants lived there until 1961. Coffered ceilings, courtyard with clock tower and hanging garden; Torquato Tasso and Pope Benedict XIII stayed there.

Why go. The Norman tower and the Guevara wall garden.
