Chiesa di San Domenico (del Rosario) e convento dei Domenicani

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Church and convent of the Dominican friars, founded in 1561; the church, work of Giovanni Tarantino dated 1583, rises at the northern edge of the village.

Why go. The 16th century Dominican complex.
