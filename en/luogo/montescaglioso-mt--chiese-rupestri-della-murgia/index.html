Chiese rupestri della Murgia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

In the Park of Murgia there are rock churches of the X-XI century; the abbey had rock churches in the Murgia of Sant'Andrea and near the village there is the crypt of Cozzo Sant'Angelo (XI-XII century).

Why go. The rock face of the territory.
