Teatro Serpente Aureo

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Designed by Pietro Maggi, built in 1820 and modernized in 1862, it is reached from the portico of the Town Hall: horse-iron plant with 50 stages on three orders, gallery and audience, with stuccoes and golden decorations.

Why go. A small 19th-century theater all gold and stucco.
