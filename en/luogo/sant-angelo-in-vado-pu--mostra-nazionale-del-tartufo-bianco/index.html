Mostra nazionale del tartufo bianco

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Sant'Angelo in Vado is known as the capital of the white truffle, celebrated with the National Exhibition in the last three weeks of October and in the first of November; in the village the Experimental Center for truffle cultivation is also located.

Why go. The white truffle in autumn.
