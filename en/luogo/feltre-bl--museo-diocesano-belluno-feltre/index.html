Museo Diocesano Belluno-Feltre

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the ancient bishop's palace, born as a fortress and residence of the bishops from the second half of the thirteenth century, restored in 2018: sacred art from the early Middle Ages to the contemporary of the dioceses of Feltre and Belluno, about 250 works including the cup of the deacon Bear (VI century), considered the oldest Eucharistic chalice of the West, a post-Byzantine cross of 1542, a Madonna in alabaster of the fifteenth century, the reliquary bust of San Silvestro by the Florentine goldsmith Antonio di Salvi and works of Tintoretto, Sebastiano Ricci, Luca Giordano, Andrea Brustolon, Gaspare Diziani and Domenico Corvi. Open from Friday to Sunday (June-September 9.30-13 and 14.30-19; October-May also holidays, 9-13 and 14-18).

Why go. The oldest glass in the West and masterpieces from Tintoretto to Brustolon.
