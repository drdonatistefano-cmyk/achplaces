Museo civico di Asolo

Museums · City history · Paid entry

It is located in the Palazzo del Vescovado and in the Loggia della Ragione, frescoed around the middle of the sixteenth century; born from the legacy of Andrea Manera and Pacifico Scomazzetto, it has an archaeological section, a gallery from the fifteenth century to today with works by Luca Giordano, Bernardo Strozzi and Bernardo Bellotto, the treasure of the Duomo and the sections dedicated to Caterina Cornaro, Eleonora Duse and Freya Stark.

Why go. The three women who made the story of Asolo in one museum.
