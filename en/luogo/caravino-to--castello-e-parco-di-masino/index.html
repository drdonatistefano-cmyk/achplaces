Castello e Parco di Masino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Millennial castle on a hill of the Serra morainica d'Ivrea, held for over nine centuries by the Valperga, which transformed it from a fortress into a noble residence and summer villa; the FAI bought it in 1988. Frescoed halls, ambassadors' apartments, library of over 25,000 volumes and Savoy Hall with views of 22 Sabaude cities, reopened in 2022. Outside, Italian and French gardens, romantic English park and an eighteenth-century labyrinth of about a thousand hornbeams; café on the Oleandri Terrace. In 1578 there was guest San Carlo Borromeo. Tickets online on ticket.fondoambiente.it.

Why go. One of the great castles-residence of Piedmont, overlooking the plain.
