Oratorio di San Bernardino e Museo Diocesano di Arte Sacra

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Complex on two levels: the lower oratory frescoed by Siena painters of the seventeenth century and the upper oratory entirely frescoed; in the museum the Madonna del latte di Lorenzetti, the Annunciation of Matthew of John and the Christ portacroce of Beccafumi.

Why go. Sienese painting away from the Duomo crowd.

  • Where Piazza San Francesco, Siena
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.terredisiena.it
