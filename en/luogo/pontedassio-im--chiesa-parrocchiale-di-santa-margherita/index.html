Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Margherita

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of medieval origins rebuilt in monumental forms in the nineteenth century; the Province recalls the reconstruction of the parish in 1880 and indicates a triptych of 1503 by Luca Baudo da Novara.

Why go. A triptych of the early sixteenth century in a large nineteenth-century church.
