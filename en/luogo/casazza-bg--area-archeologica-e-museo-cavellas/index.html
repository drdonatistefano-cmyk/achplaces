Area archeologica e Museo Cavellas

History and architecture · Archaeology

Roman rural village (I-VI century A.D.), one of the largest in the region, discovered during construction works at the state road 42 with excavations between 1986 and 1996 and preserved under a supermarket: over 1,000 m2 buried by about four meters of Drion deposits, with stone walls and lime, rooms, floors and fireplaces preserved for about one meter and three construction phases. Farming, cereals and weaving were lived; a walkway above the excavation, interactive totems and panels guide the visit, with exhibits. Open Wednesday 9-12,30, Saturday and Sunday 15-18, other days by reservation.

Why go. Walk over a whole Roman village.
