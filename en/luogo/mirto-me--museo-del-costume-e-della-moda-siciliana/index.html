Museo del Costume e della Moda Siciliana

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the Palazzo Cupane, acquired and restored by the Municipality in 1990, the museum was created from the private collection of clothes and accessories by the architect Giuseppe Miraudo and was inaugurated in the years 1993-97. It preserves over a thousand pieces (1,500 according to the card) including dresses, even evening and wedding dresses, accessories, costumes and underwear of Sicilian fashion from the eighteenth to the early twentieth century; some pieces were exhibited in Brazil and the USA. Open Monday-Friday 9.30-12.30, Saturday and Sunday 16-19.

Why go. Two centuries of Sicilian fashion in a noble palace.

  • Where Via Ugo Bassi (ingresso da Via Cupane), Mirto
  • Entry €3,50, reduced €2,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
