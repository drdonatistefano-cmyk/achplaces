MAGMA, Museo delle Arti in Ghisa della Maremma

Museums · Themed

Born in 1970 and since 1998 in the Forno di San Ferdinando, the oldest building in the city, tells Follonica, the main iron centre of the Grand Duchy since 1836, on three floors with interactive installations, wooden models and nineteenth-century castings; the Etruscan section exhibits the finds of Rondelli, with a 6th century BC oven. It is a gateway to the Park of the Metalliferous Hills.

Why go. The history of cast iron in an old foundry.
