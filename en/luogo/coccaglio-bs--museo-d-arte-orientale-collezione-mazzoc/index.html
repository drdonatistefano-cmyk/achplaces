Museo d'Arte Orientale - Collezione Mazzocchi

Museums · Themed

It gathers about 500 items, mostly Japanese, brought by Pompeo Mazzocchi (1829-1915), merchant and entrepreneur of Coccaglio who traveled to Europe, Africa and Asia, especially in Japan, on the trail of the silkworm to save the Lombard silk-growing. Weapons, ivory, xilographies, lacquers, jewelry, paintings, ceramics, fabrics, photographs and bronzes are ordered in four rooms: Gelso (Mazzocchi e la bachicoltura), Cherry (history of Japan), Bamboo (daily objects) and Acero (prints ukiyo-e). The Municipality indicates opening on Wednesdays from 15 to 18; guided tours and workshops on request; ticket at variable cost.

Why go. A piece of 19th century Japan in Franciacorta, collected by a silk pioneer.
