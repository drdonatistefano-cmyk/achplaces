Parco e giardini del castello

Nature and landscape · City parks · Viewpoint

The Italian garden, the park with large trees, bosso hedges, statues, fountains and greenhouses, with the entrance to the large eighteenth-century fountain with horseshoe; open from May to October.

Why go. The park of the dukes.
