Villa Medicea La Magia

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Casa-torre dei Panciatichi passed to the Medici in 1583 and renovated by Buontalenti, base for hunting in the Royal Barco; Italian garden, lemon and romantic park; in the halls the Museo del Filet.

Why go. An open and uncrowded Medici villa.
