Castello Normanno Svevo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

The castle is located in the heart of the historic center and is considered by the Municipality a monument of high architectural value, witness to the lordship that have succeeded one another in the territory. With that of Oria it marked the ridge of the Via Appia, as intermediate stage between Taranto and Brindisi. It hosts the MATER Museum and has the same times: in summer (from 1st June 2025) Monday 17-22, Tuesday-Friday 9-13 and 17-22, Saturday and Sunday 9-13 and 17-23, last entry half an hour before closing.

Why go. The castle on the Appia street, open until the evening of summer.

  • Where Via Castello, Mesagne
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
