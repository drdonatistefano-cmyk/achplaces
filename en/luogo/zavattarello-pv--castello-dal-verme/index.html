Castello Dal Verme

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Born as a military outpost and for six centuries of the Dal Verme, it is a large quadrilateral building above the village and the valley, with stone walls thick up to more than four meters, about forty rooms connected by a system of internal stairs and a view from the terraces and the tower up to the plain of Padana. It rises in a nature reserve of over 80 hectares.

Why go. The view over the Oltrepò from the castle towers.

open from April to October, entrance only with guided tour fee (in-Lombardy)
