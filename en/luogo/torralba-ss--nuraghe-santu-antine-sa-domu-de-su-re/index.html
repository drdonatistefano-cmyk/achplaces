Nuraghe Santu Antine – Sa Domu de su Re

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The most imposing complex after Su Nuraxi: today's 17-and-a-half-metre mast, which is estimated to have reached the 24, triangular bastion with three towers, the largest known courtyard and a village of 14 huts.

Why go. The king's house.
