Galleria anglosarda - Pozzo Sant'Antonio

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Underground gallery of about one kilometer excavated in the galena line of the Arburese and Guspinese, up to the well Sant'Antonio, begun in 1852 by the Royal Anglo-Sardinian Company from which it takes its name. Guided tour of about 40 minutes; constant internal temperature of 15-16 °C, recommended closed and low shoes. From November to March Saturday and Sunday a departure at 13:15, on the other days by reservation; ticket office in Montevecchio, 15 minutes before.

Why go. One kilometer underground in the life of miners.

  • Where SP 66, località Miniera di Montevecchio, Guspini
  • Entry €12, free 0-6 years
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.minieradimontevecchio.it
