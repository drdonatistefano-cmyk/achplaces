Palazzo Doria

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between 1574 and 1578, perhaps on a project by Galeazzo Alessi, today it is the seat of the Town Hall and preserves a mosaic floor of the third century; in 1685-1687 Taddeo Carlone built two fountains for the garden. It is connected by a balcony to the Pentagonal Tower.

Why go. The Doria Palace with a Roman mosaic.
