Palazzo Municipale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

C-shaped building with back courtyard and Venetian loggia of 1610; inside the Salone del Consiglio Maggiore and the Salone del Consiglio dei Ten, decorated with stuccoes and medallions of the Doges, and on the staircase the original fountain of Hercules and Hydra.

Why go. The halls with the Dogi medallions.
