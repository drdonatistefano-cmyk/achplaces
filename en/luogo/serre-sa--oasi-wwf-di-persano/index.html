Oasi WWF di Persano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Large green area crossed by the Sele, within the Regional Nature Reserve Foce Sele-Tanagro: 110 hectares, about 70 occupied by the dam basin completed in 1934, wetland of international importance for the Ramsar Convention. It hosts one of the most important populations of otter, as well as foxes, wild boars, fairies and weasels, and is stop and wintering for migratory birds: 184 species, 47 nesting.

Why go. Otters and migratory birds on the Sele.
