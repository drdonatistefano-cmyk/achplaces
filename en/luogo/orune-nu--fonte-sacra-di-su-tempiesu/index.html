Fonte sacra di Su Tempiesu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Well temple dedicated to the cult of the waters, of the recent Bronze (XIII century B.C.) and frequented until the early Iron Age, discovered in 1953: about seven meters high, in cones of trachite and basalt leaning on a wall of shale from which flows the spring. A vestibule with seat counters and four steps lead to the small chamber with paved tholos; the double sloping cover has a double sculpted eaves and a triangular pediment, with an acroterium that held twenty votive bronze swords; in the cockpit bronze of tenderers and warriors have emerged. From the village follow the signs, then an asphalted road at the cemetery to the archaeological area and a walking path of several hundred meters.

Why go. The most scenic water temple in Nuragic Sardinia.
