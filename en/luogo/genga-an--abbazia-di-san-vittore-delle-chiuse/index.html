Abbazia di San Vittore delle Chiuse

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Among the most important Romanesque churches in the Marche region, with Byzantine influences, probably of the 11th century on Roman remains and restored between 1925 and 1932: facade with bell tower and cylindrical climbing tower, almost square interior with three naves with four cylindrical pillars that hold the dome.

Why go. The Romanesque Marche in the background of the gorge.
