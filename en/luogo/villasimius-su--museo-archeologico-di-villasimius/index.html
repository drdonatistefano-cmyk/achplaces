Museo archeologico di Villasimius

Museums · Archaeological

In a rural house in the courtyard of the nineteenth century on two floors and four rooms, with finds from the Nuragic Age to the Spanish domination, many from the backdrops: punic amphorae of the fifth century BC recovered in front of the island of the Cavoli, ex vow and objects of the Phoenician-punic sanctuary of Cuccureddus, Roman materials from the necropolis of Cruccuris and Accu is Traias, and in the last room cannons and azulejos of the Spanish wreck sunk in the fifteenth century near the island of the Cavoli. Open Tuesday-Sunday 9-13 and 16-18.

Why go. The submerged treasures of the sea of Villasimius.
