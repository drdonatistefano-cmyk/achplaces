Caffè Pedrocchi

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The great coffee designed by Giuseppe Jappelli and inaugurated in 1831, called "without doors" because it remained open day and night until 1916, meeting of intellectuals: to try the "Pedrocchino" with coffee, mint, cream and cocoa.

Why go. The good living room of the city.
