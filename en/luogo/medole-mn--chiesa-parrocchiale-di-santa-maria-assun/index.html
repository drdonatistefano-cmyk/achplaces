Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church overlooks the large square of the center, built around the ancient castle. It preserves the altarpiece of Titian with the risen Christ that appears to the Mother.

Why go. To see a Titian in a small village in Mantovano.
