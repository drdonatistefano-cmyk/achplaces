Museo delle armi e della tradizione armiera

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Collection of historical weapons, from white to firearms, from defence and hunting, chronologically ordered from the fifteenth century, with the history of families and local armoring tradition; a didactic section shows with videos and technical models and manufacturing processes. Free up to 13 years old and for residents of the municipalities of the Trompia Valley Museum System. Sunday only guided tours for groups of at least 25 people, booking 15 days before.

Why go. The history of the industry that made Val Trompia.

  • Where Via XX Settembre 31, Gardone Val Trompia
  • Entry €5, reduced €3 (13-18 years)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page comune.gardonevaltrompia.bs.it
