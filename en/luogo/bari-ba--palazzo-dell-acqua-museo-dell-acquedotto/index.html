Palazzo dell'Acqua – Museo dell'Acquedotto Pugliese

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The historic seat of the Apulian Aqueduct in the Muratian village, designed in every detail by Duilio Cambellotti: frescoed rooms and furnishings inspired by the world of water, documents, photos and a chemical laboratory of the thirties with original equipment; guided tours on weekends.

["Il cuore civico di Bologna dal Duecento: San Petronio, Palazzo d'Accursio, Palazzo del Podestà e, a fianco, il Nettuno del Giambologna.", 'Piazza Maggiore / Piazza del Nettuno, 40124 Bologna']

Why go. The secular cathedral of the water.
