Museo Archeologico Nazionale

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Since 1871 in a neoclassical building the Etruscan, Roman and Longobard finds of the territory: ceramics, sculptures, the famous canopies closed, painted urns, jewels and reconstructions of the main tombs.

Why go. The ticket can include some Etruscan tombs, upon reservation.

  • Where Via Porsenna 93, Chiusi
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
