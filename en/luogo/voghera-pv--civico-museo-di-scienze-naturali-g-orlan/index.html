Civico museo di scienze naturali G. Orlandi

Museums · Science and university

Born in the seventies from the collections of fossils and minerals donated by enthusiasts (Geo-Paleontological Group Vogherese), it has sections of zoology (birds including flamingos, herons and raptors), paleontology with a humerus of plesiosaur found at Zavattarello and a skull of Megaceros, mineralogy with 221 samples on display and a botanical section in preparation. It is located in the former Cavalry.

Why go. Fossils and fauna of the Oltrepò, including plesiosaur.
