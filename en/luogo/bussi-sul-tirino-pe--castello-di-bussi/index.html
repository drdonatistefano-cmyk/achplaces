Castello di Bussi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The castle is documented from 1092, when it was part of the monastery of San Benedetto in Perillis; purchased in 1377 by the Cantelmo, who held it until 1579, then passed to Pietro Pietropaoli and the Grand Duke of Tuscany Cosimo de' Medici. The current Medici castle-palace of the 16th century has a rectangular plan around a courtyard, a quadrangular tower to the north, imposing walls, crenellated tower and medieval and Renaissance windows; it dominates the village and is now a private residence of the De Sanctis family.

Why go. The symbolic monument that dominates the country.
