Rocca dei Borgia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built between 1494 and 1500 and known as "dei Borgia" in honor of Pope Alexander VI, it was the last fortification of a system of border disputed between Spoleto, Todi, Foligno and Perugia. Triangular plant with three round towers, conical, connected by underground walkways; the highest, 20 meters on 5 floors, has a base of 80 meters of circumference. In 1624 Galileo Galilei stayed there, who called it a small compendium of the universe.

Why go. The most important monument of the village.
