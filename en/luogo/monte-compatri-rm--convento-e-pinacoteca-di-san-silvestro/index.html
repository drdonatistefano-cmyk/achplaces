Convento e Pinacoteca di San Silvestro

Museums · Old masters · Viewpoint

In the highest part of the village, the convent has a first settlement of the mid-15th century but it is largely late seventeenth century. The gallery contains paintings from the Mannerist and Caravaggio school: the Trasverberazione di Santa Teresa di scuola bolognese, paintings from Luca Fiammingo, Maddalena penitente and Christ at the Mount of Olives of Cerrini, Christ crowned with thorns and Emmaus dinner of the Caravaggio school, works by Lucas de la Haye and Giovanni Della Miseria; the San Giuseppe carpenter of Gerrit van Honthorst was stolen.
