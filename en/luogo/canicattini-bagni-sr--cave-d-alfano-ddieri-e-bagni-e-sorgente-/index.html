Cave d'Alfano, Ddieri e Bagni e sorgente del Cardinale

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

Canyon limestones (cave) of the territory: Cava d'Alfano, Cava Ddieri and Cava Bagni; in Alfano, Passo Ladro and Cardinale there are prehistoric necropolis with monumental tombs of the early Bronze Age. The source of the Cardinal feeds the river that supplies the country with drinking water; in 1898 about 200 liters per second were channeled for irrigation and mills, and Greek hydraulic works from the end of the 8th century B.C. carried its waters towards Syracuse.

Why go. The Iblean quarries between prehistoric necropolis and ancient waters.
