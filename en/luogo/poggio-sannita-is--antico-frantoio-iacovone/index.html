Antico frantoio Iacovone

Museums · Themed

An underground mill from medieval times entirely restored in the historic Palazzo Iacovone, registered with the Associazione Dimore Storiche Italiane, testimony of the oldest peasant culture of Alto Molise. In the palace was born in 1850 Cosmo Maria de Horatiis, considered the father of Italian homeopathy and personal physician of Francesco I di Borbone: guided tours include oil mill and hometown, by telephone or by mail.

Why go. A medieval underground mill in a historic house.
