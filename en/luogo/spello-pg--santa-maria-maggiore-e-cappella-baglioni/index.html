Santa Maria Maggiore e Cappella Baglioni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The collegiate church of the XII-XIII centuries houses, on the left side of the nave, the Baglioni Chapel frescoed in 1501 by Bernardino di Betto called the Pinturicchio.

Why go. A masterpiece of the Umbrian Renaissance.
