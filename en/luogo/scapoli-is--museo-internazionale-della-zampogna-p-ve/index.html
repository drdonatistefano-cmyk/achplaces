Museo internazionale della zampogna «P. Vecchione»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 2002 in the historic Palazzo Mancini, it is spread over three floors with zampogne from all over the world and different eras, wind instruments, iconographic and literary documentation, historical photographs and the reconstruction of the workshop of the artisans of Scapolesi; there are also an acoustic listening room, a film library and a permanent nativity scene of the Neapolitan school of the Capuan masters of San Gregorio Armeno. A multimedia app allows you to listen to the sounds of the instruments. Timetable indicated by italia.it: Friday-Sunday 10-12 and 16-18.

Why go. The paws of the world in the country that still builds them.

  • Where Via Santa Maria 1, Scapoli
  • Entry €1, reduced €0.50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
