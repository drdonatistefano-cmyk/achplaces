Museo etnografico dei maestri artigiani e della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Also known as the Ethnographic Museum of Farming and Craftsmanship, it is housed in the Professional Institute for Agriculture and Environment and was set up in 2000 with the pupils: agricultural tools, domestic tools, shoemakers, bottai and blacksmiths and three permanent photographic exhibitions.

Why go. The ancient crafts of the country.

MiC card without opening hours, updated to 30/04/2020; contact the institute (tel. 0971 774021)
