Antiquarium statale di Numana

Museums · Archaeological

It tells the development of ancient Numana and the Riviera del Conero from prehistory to the Roman age, with attention to the trade of the Numana store in the Mediterranean. On the first floor the rich burial of the Queen of Sirolo (VI century BC), with attic and picene ceramics, bronzes, avori, amber jewelry and the remains of a horse and a war chariot, flanked by the picena tomb of a warrior; on the second floor the chronological section, and in closing the collection of Girolamo Rilli. Among the pieces, a Roman tile with a ship engraved in the fresh clay. Times indicated: Tuesday to Saturday 8:30-19:45, Sunday 14-19:45, Monday closed.

Why go. The picnic treasures of the Queen of Sirolo.
