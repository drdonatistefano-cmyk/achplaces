Vigneti e Festa del vino

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Castelvenere is considered the most lively municipality in the South, with Falanghina, Aglianico and Barbera del Sannio (Camaiola); in 2019 it was European Wine City. The underground cellars in the historic centre host the Wine Festival on the last weekend of August.

Why go. The Sannio vineyards.
