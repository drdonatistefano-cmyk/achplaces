Parco dei Fossili e Museo malacologico delle argille

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Former clay quarry closed at the end of the seventies, with geological layers of marine origin partially rich in fossils; after the abandonment it was reclaimed in the nineties by Colacem with the planting of 8,000 trees. In 1999 the Fossil Park and the Malacological Museum of Clays were born, set up in a farmhouse of the seventeenth century: one of the very few examples in Italy and in Europe of the recovery of a quarry abandoned for scientific and educational purposes.

Why go. Marine fossils and shells in a quarry returned to nature.
