Palazzo Locatelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Elegant 18th-century Palladian residence built by the Bergamo family Locatelli, who lived there since the mid-eighteenth century; the family coat of arms dominates the portal. Beyond the hall there is an Italian garden; the noble floor conserves frescoes from the second half of the nineteenth century by Antonio Picco. Today it is the seat of the municipality, the town wine shop and the Civic Museum of the territory.

Why go. The Palladian Palace, the heart of the city.
