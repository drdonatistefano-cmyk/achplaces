MUVAT - Museo vivente dell'arte tessile

Museums · Themed

It preserves carpets, tapestries and saddlebags woven on ancient horizontal frames, with live demonstrations of the women of the country; in August the exhibition of the tapestry and carpet is held.

Why go. Weaving machines in front of the visitors.
