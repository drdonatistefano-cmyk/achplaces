Basilica di San Simplicio

Places of worship · Basilicas

Wanted by the judges of Gallura in three phases between the second half of the 11th and the middle of the 12th century, it is the most important religious monument of north-eastern Sardinia: three naves in local granite, about 33 for 13 meters and 12 high, on a hill once outside the walls. It is dedicated to Bishop Simplicius, martyr on May 15, 304; in 1614 under the high altar were found the relics of the Saints Simplicius, Rosula, Diocletian and Fiorenzio. In the area a Roman necropolis of about 450 tombs; in mid-May the feast of the patron with costume procession and Palio della Stella.

Why go. The Romanesque granite of the Gallura Giudicale.
