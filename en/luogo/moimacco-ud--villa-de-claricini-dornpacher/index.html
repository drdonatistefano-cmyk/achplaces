Villa de Claricini Dornpacher

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Venetian villa of the end of the 17th century of the Counts of Claricini, arrived from Bologna to Cividale in the thirteenth century: central body with pediment, two wings, rustic, lemon and chapel of Santa Croce (about 1700) with paintings by Lucilius Candido. Since 1971 it has been home to a foundation: the museum preserves paintings by Giacomo and Antonio Carneo, Sebastiano Bombelli and works attributed to Palma il Giovane, Pomponio Amalteo and Pordenone, furniture, fabrics, weapons and prints. Around the Italian garden and a park with trees from the late eighteenth century. Open to the public on request.

Why go. A Venetian villa with its family collection.
