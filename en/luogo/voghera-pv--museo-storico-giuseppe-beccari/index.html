Museo storico Giuseppe Beccari

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Dedicated to the period between the Napoleonic age and the twentieth century and wanted by the military rowing Giuseppe Beccari: relics, documents, medals and Italian and foreign uniforms, hundreds of firearms and whites, a library of military history with thousands of volumes and an archive of letters and diaries of fighters and postcards. It is located in the former Caserma di Cavalleria (1857-1864), barrier-free; times indicated by the City: Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday 15-17.30, groups by reservation.

Why go. Two centuries of military history in a 19th century barracks.

  • Where Via Gramsci 1, Voghera
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it
