Teatro Massimo Bellini

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Inaugurated in 1890 in Piazza Bellini, about 1200 seats and a perfect acoustic: ceiling frescoed by Ernesto Bellandi with the Apotheosis of Bellini and the historical curtain of Giuseppe Sciuti, which can also be visited with guided tours.

Why go. Opera season in a golden room.
