Chiesa di San Biagio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

At the foot of the staircase of the Mother Church, with Gothic arch; on the door of the sacristy a plaque with Gothic inscription of 1375, the oldest text of the country.

Why go. The oldest inscription of the village.
