Siti fossiliferi del Monte San Giorgio

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Porto Ceresio is one of the three municipalities (with Besano and Viggiù) of the Italian extension of the UNESCO site of Monte San Giorgio, 240,34 hectares evaluated by IUCN in 2010 for the succession of about 1,000 meters of limestone, dolomies and bituminous schists of the Triassico; on the Italian side, studied since 1863, are known about 35 species of reptiles and almost 100 fish. From the lakefront starts a bike itinerary (about 35 km, 5 hours, 1,100 m of difference in altitude, difficult, recommended e-bike or mountain bike) that goes up to the fossil sites, touches trenches of the Cadorna Line, Sasso Caldo where was found the Besanosaurus, and the fossil museums of Meride (Switzerland) and Besano. The Cadorna Line defended the border during the First World War from a possible Austrian break through neutral Switzerland.

Why go. The deposits of marine reptiles of the Triassico, UNESCO World Heritage, between Italy and Switzerland.
