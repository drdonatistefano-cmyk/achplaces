Castello Ducale di Agliè

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage

Royal residence born in 1141 and rebuilt several times, today with about 300 furnished rooms, including the large ballroom with stuccoes and frescoes of the "Fasti di re Arduino" by Giovanni Paolo Recchi. UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Why go. The palace of the Canavese.
