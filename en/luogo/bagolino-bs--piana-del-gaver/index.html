Piana del Gaver

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

A few kilometers from Bagolino, near the Adamello Park: ski area with five slopes of medium difficulty, the cross-country ring Gaverland 5 km and the snowpark.

Why go. Skiing and down at the foot of Adamello.
