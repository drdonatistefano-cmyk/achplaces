Museo diocesano di Ischia

Museums · Old masters

Founded on 5 March 1997 by Bishop Antonio Pagano, the section of sacred art is divided into five nuclei (marbles, sculptures, paintings, silvers, various objects) and preserves a cover of early Christian sarcophagus from the IV-V century. The archaeological section, of about 1,000 m2 with the furnaces and necropolis of Pithecusae, is located instead at the complex of Santa Restituta in Lacco Ameno.

Why go. The sacred art of the island in one seat.
