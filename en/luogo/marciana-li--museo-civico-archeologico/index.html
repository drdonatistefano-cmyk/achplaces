Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

In the seventeenth-century Palazzo del Pretorio, it preserves Elbani finds in store by the Superintendence: prehistoric, protohistoric sections (with Villanovian materials from Monte Giove and Madonna del Monte) and Etruscan, documentation on granite and materials of the Roman wreck of Procchio.

Why go. The oldest story in Elba.
