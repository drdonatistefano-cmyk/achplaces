Museo mineralogico Parma Gemma

Museums · Science and university

Born from a private collection of Apennine minerals, it exhibits thousands of pieces, some visible only under the microscope, in three rooms with three hundred and fifty windows: copper, manganese, sulphur and plaster minerals of the Ligurian-Tuscan-Emilian Apennines, as well as specimens from other regions and from abroad. Outside a litological museum gathers hundreds of large mineralized rocks along a route; the museum also organizes free guided tours to the mines in the area. Tel. 0185 46229.

Why go. The Apennine mines in three hundred and fifty windows.
