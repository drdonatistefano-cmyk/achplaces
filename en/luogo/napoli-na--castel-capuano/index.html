Castel Capuano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The oldest castle in Naples after Castel dell'Ovo, of Norman origin, at the entrance of Via dei Tribunali; for over five hundred years the seat of the Court, which can be visited on organized occasions.

Why go. Five centuries of justice.
