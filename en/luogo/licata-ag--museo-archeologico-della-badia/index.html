Museo archeologico della Badia

Museums · Archaeological

In the sixteenth-century convent of Santa Maria del Soccorso, called "Badia" (Badia), enlarged between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, exhibits around the cloister porched finds from the ancient Neolithic (VI millennium BC) to the late antiquity: the sanctuaries of Demeter and Kore of Mollarella and Casalicchio, the settlements of Poggio Marcato d'Agnone and Monte Sole and an entire hall on Finziade, founded in 282 BC by the tyrant agrigentino Finzia, with the House 1 and the treasure found inside. Since 2025 the medieval and modern section exhibits the fifteenth-century triptych attributed to the Master of San Martino, the Madonna del Soccorso attributed to Domenico Gagini (1470) and a sixteenth-century sarcophagus with knight. Open Monday-Friday 9-13.

Why go. From Neolithic to late antiquity in a convent.
