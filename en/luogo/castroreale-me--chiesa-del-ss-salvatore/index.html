Chiesa del SS. Salvatore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built towards the end of the fifteenth century in the ancient Giudecca, then enriched with baroque stuccoes, with Gothic portal, marble altar and bell tower of 1560; today it is used as auditorium.

Why go. The Jewish Quarter Church.
