Basilica di San Paolo Maggiore e Piazza San Gaetano

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage

On the major decuman, the basilica rises on the temple of the Dioscuri, of which remain the Corinthian columns on the façade, overlooking Piazza San Gaetano, already Greek agorà and Roman forum.

Why go. The heart of ancient Neapolis.
