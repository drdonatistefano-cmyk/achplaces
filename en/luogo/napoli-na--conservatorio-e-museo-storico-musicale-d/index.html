Conservatorio e Museo storico musicale di San Pietro a Majella

Museums · Themed

In the former convent of the Celestines, home to the Conservatory since 1826, the museum with musical instruments, portraits and relics of the great musicians of the Neapolitan school.

Why go. The house of Neapolitan music.
