Museo civico di Palazzo Cento

Museums · City history

In the sixteenth-century noble palace renovated in the seventeenth-century, which housed the family of Giacomo Leopardi: archaeological collection with mosaics and Roman statues from Montefrancolo, picture gallery of Pollento painters, ceramics produced in Pollenza between 1810 and 1905, antique furniture, a collection of crucifixes and the Vespa Museum curated by Marco Romiti. In the same building library and historical municipal archive.

Why go. The history of the village, from Roman statues to ceramics and Vespa.

closed due to the earthquake of 2016 according to the regional portal
