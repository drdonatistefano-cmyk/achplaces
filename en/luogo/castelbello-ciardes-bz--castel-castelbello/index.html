Castel Castelbello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It was first mentioned in 1238 and built by the lords of Montalban on a cliff on the left bank of the Adige, from 1531 it was a fief in pledge of the Hendl, which gave it its present appearance; the fires of 1813 and 1824 devastated it and in 1956 the State bought the complex, restored under the Superintendence until 1995 and managed since 1999 by a cooperative. The chapel, at the end of the thirteenth century or early fourteenth century, has a frieze with the twelve Apostles in pairs and the texts of the Creed, with alternating normal and mirrorful inscriptions. Today it is the cultural center of the country, with exhibitions in spring and autumn; it can be visited only with a guide, from April to October.

Why go. Castle and chapel frescoed on the cliff above the Adige.
