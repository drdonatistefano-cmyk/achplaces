Teatro Sociale

History and architecture · Historic theatres

It was built around 1813 from a rustic building used as a barn and inaugurated in 1852 with I Capuleti and Montecchi di Bellini: up to 400 seats, 47 stages on three orders and a curtain painted by hand with the Fiera di Luzzara in the presence of the Gonzaga princes.

Why go. A 19th-century theatre born of a barn.
