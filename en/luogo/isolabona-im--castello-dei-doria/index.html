Castello dei Doria

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the hill north of the village, above the road to Apricale, it has hexagonal plant and square mast in local sandstone. It was mentioned in the sources since 1287, linked to the arrival of the Doria at the end of the thirteenth century, and was born as a fortress (1330) and then took residential characters; around 1520 it was briefly held by the Grimaldi of Monaco. Damaged by the earthquake of 1887, it was restored and consolidated in 1989 and in summer it hosts evenings of theatre and music and the Festival of harps.

Why go. The Doria castle that acts as a theatre at the Festival of Harps.
