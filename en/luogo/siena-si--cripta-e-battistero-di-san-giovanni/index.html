Cripta e Battistero di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The "Crypta" under the floor of the Duomo and the Baptistery with the baptismal font, both included in the unique ticket of the Duomo complex (Opa Si Pass).

Why go. The tour of the Duomo from below is completed.

  • Where Piazza San Giovanni, Siena
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website operaduomo.siena.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
