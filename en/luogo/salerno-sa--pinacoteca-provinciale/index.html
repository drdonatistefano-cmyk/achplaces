Pinacoteca provinciale

Museums · Old masters

Six small galleries with works from the Renaissance to the first half of the twentieth century, including Andrea Sabatini's paintings from Salerno and works by foreign artists such as Irene Kowaliska.

Why go. Andrea Sabatini and the painting Salerno.
