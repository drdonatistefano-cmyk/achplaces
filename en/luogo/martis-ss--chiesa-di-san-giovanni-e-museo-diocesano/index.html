Chiesa di San Giovanni e museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters

17th century church of Gothic architecture that houses the diocesan museum, with liturgical vestments, sacred silvers and a 17th century crucifix used for Holy Week.

Why go. The silvers and vestments of the Anglona religious tradition.
