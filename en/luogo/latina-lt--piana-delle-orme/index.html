Piana delle Orme

Museums · Themed

Historical and Ethnological Museum of over 30,000 m2 with 15 pavilions on bridge reclamation, landing of Anzio and battle of Cassino, in two routes (War and Agriculture) with a pavilion of vintage toys.

Why go. A century of history in 15 halls.
