Museo del Tempo e delle Meridiane

Museums · Themed

It tells the sundials preserved among the old houses of Bellino, painted on private houses and chapels and along the paths, recovered since 1999 on impulse of the Municipality with European funds of the LAG Valli del Viso. Open in June, July and September Saturday and Sunday 15-18, in August every 15-18; in winter on call for groups (tel. +39 0175 689 629).

Why go. The key to reading the country's solar dials.
