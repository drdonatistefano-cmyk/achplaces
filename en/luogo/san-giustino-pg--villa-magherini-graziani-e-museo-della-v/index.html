Villa Magherini Graziani e Museo della villa di Plinio

Museums · Archaeological

Ancient castle transformed in 1616 into a late Renaissance villa designed by Antonio Cantagallina, with loggia and Italian gardens; it houses the archaeological museum with the excavations of the villa in Tuscis of Plinio il Giovane.

Why go. The finds of the villa of Pliny.
