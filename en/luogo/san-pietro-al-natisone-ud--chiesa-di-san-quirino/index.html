Chiesa di San Quirino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Late-Gothic church of Škofja Loka school: on the apse an inscription shows the date 1493 and the name of the builder, Martin Petric. Under its lindens were gathered the assemblies of the valleys, including the Great Arengo of Schiavonia, convened for the last time in 1804; in the surroundings emerged a necropolis of the Iron Age and Roman and Longobard finds.

Why go. The place of the ancient assemblies of the Friulian Slavia.
