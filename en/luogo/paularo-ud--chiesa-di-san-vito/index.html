Chiesa di San Vito

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1772 on a project by Domenico Schiavi and completed in the following century, with frescoes by Antonio Schiavi and paintings by Francesco Pillizzotti, follower of Nicola Grassi; the main altar in white marble was donated by Jacopo Linussio in 1747.

Why go. The eighteenth-century Carnic church.
