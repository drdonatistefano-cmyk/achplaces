Museo l'escolo de mountanho

Museums · Themed

It tells the mountain school from 1900 to 1970 reconstructing a classroom with benches, chair, blackboard, registers and original reports of the early twentieth century, accompanied by many vintage photographs. Open from Tuesday to Friday in the morning (Wednesday and Friday also in the afternoon) and on the first Saturday of the month; guided tours on request (tel. 0171 999112).

Why go. How you went to school in the mountains a century ago.
