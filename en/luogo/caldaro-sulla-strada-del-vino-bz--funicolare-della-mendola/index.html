Funicolare della Mendola

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Designed by Emil Strub and in service since 1903, when it was the longest and steepest funicular, it is a symbol of the country: it climbs in about twelve minutes (845 meters according to Italia.it). From the mountain station you climb in about 3 and a half hours to Monte Roen (2116 m), with views of the Dolomites and Ortles and alpine blooms at the end of June.

Why go. The historical funicular of 1903.
