Spiaggia di Porto Sa Ruxi

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches

Between cliffs in Mediterranean scenery, with blue sea and white dunes fixed by centuries-old junipers, priority habitat of the Habitat Directive subject to conservation actions.

Why go. White dunes and secular junipers.
