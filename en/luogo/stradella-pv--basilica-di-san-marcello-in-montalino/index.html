Basilica di San Marcello in Montalino

Places of worship · Basilicas · Viewpoint

Romanesque monument of great importance built at the end of the 12th century on the hill of Montalino, where there is also the homonymous fortress (today private residence not visitable, with sixteenth-century loggia closed by a veranda).

Why go. The Romanesque on the hill above the city.
