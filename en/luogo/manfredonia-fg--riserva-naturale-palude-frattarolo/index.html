Riserva naturale Palude Frattarolo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

About 260 hectares at the mouth of the Candelaro, the last strip of the great marshes of the Capitana and SPA in the Gargano Park: freshwater ponds and brackish with Italian knights, fighters, pittims, chiurli and beccaccini. Information on visits to the Park Visitor Centres.

Why go. Birdwatching in the last swamps of the Tablekeeper.
