Rocca di Asolo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress built between the end of the XII and the beginning of the XIII century on the top of Mount Rich, 310 meters, as military garrison of the city; you reach from Porta Colmarion with 276 steps and from the stands the look goes from the Dolomites to the Venetian plain. It often hosts cultural events and costume reenactments.

Why go. The symbol of Asolo and the wider view of the village.
