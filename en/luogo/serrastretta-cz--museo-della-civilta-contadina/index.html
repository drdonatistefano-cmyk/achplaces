Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Permanent set-up of work tools, handicrafts and everyday objects, with reconstructions of domestic and working environments. It exhibits the tools for wood processing and the production of the typical stuffed chairs of Serrastretta, woven with the ... vuda, plant of the wetlands, and a clock of the late nineteenth century still working; in the courtyard an ancient nineteenth century mill and a water mill.

Why go. The art of Serrastretta's stuffed chairs.
