Classis Ravenna, Museo della Città e del Territorio

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the former Classe sugar factory, the history of Ravenna from prehistory to the Middle Ages; part of the Archaeological Park with the Old Port.

Why go. The best way to understand why Ravenna became capital.

  • Where Via Classense 29, Classe (Ravenna)
  • Entry €8 full, €6 reduced; free on Wednesdays under 26 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
