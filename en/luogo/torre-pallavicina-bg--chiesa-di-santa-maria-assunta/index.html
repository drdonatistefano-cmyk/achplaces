Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the hamlet Santa Maria, dating back to the XVI century and restored in the XX. Inside it preserves paintings from the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries and a Solaro's altarpiece depicting the Assumption. The municipality is included in the Park of Oglio Nord, established in 1988.

Why go. A sixteenth century country church with the Assunta di Solaro altarpiece.
