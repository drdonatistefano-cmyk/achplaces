Castello Baronale e Museo civico del paesaggio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Local stone castle, 360 m above sea level, overlooking the village and the valley of mid-August: developed from the thousand in forms from Romanesque to Baroque, belonged to Da Ceccano, De Cabannis and Caetani; purchased from the Province of Latina in the mid-1960s, was restored until 2001. Almost quadrangular plant on four levels, three quadrangular towers and a semicircular one, Guelph merlons, Gothic portal and Renaissance fireplaces; the restoration has recovered ten panels of grotesque frescoes, and on the third level there is the Sala di San Tommaso, which stopped sick there in 1274. It is the seat of the Museum of the landscape.

Why go. The castle of the herring miracle of St. Thomas Aquinas.
