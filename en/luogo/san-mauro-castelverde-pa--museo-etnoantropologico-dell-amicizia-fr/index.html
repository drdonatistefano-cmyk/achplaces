Museo etnoantropologico dell'Amicizia fra i popoli

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1981 by the municipality with objects donated by the inhabitants: tools related to the cultivation of wheat, olive and flax, looms, ancient shoes for the harvest and tools of blacksmiths and craftsmen, as well as costumes and furnishings donated by the embassies of the Community countries in memory of the twinning.

Why go. The Madonie peasant life.
