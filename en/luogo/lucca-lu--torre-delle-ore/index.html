Torre delle Ore

History and architecture · Towers · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The highest tower in the city, 50 meters, with 207 wooden steps and the historic clock mechanism, still working, that you can see going up.

Why go. You go up into the clock.

  • Where Via Fillungo, Lucca
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
