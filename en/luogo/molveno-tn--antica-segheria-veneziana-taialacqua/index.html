Antica segheria veneziana Taialacqua

Museums · Themed

Built in the 16th century as a cooperative by the inhabitants on the push of the parish priest Don Taialacqua, it still works with water: the wheel moves a rod that transforms the rotation in the motion of the blade; it produced the axes called Molvene, sold in the area of Riva del Garda. A visitor centre explains the operation and role of wood in the Trentino economy; the valley is still called Val delle Seghe.

Why go. A 16th century sawmill still in operation.
