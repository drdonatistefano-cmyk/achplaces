Torre di Mels

History and architecture · Towers

Last rest of Mels Castle, built between 1026 and 1045 and granted by the Tirol counts in fiefdom to the Waldsees; burned in 1262 and partly destroyed in 1352 by the patriarch Nicolò of Luxembourg, stood on the hill north of the town with quadrangular plan, crenellated wall and three terraces. The ancient square tower remains, partly rebuilt after the 1976 earthquake, with fourteenth-century houses along the belt; the church annexed to St. Andrew was consecrated in 1056.

Why go. The surviving tower of the oldest castle in the town, dating back to the 11th century.
