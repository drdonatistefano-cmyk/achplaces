Grotte di Equi e Museo archeologico

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

In the regional park of the Apuan Alps, UNESCO World Park: tourist caves with stalactites, stalagmites and hanging lakes (about 50 minutes); archaeological museum and prehistoric Tecchia, frequented about 40,000 years ago, with remains of the cave bear (about 40 minutes); and then zipline of 200 meters on the stream, cave-adventure, rock gym and center visits of the Park with the museum of geology ApuanGeoLab. Managed by the AlterEco Community Cooperative for the Municipality.

Why go. Caves, cave bears and a prehistoric technique.
