Stadio San Siro e Museo

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Paid entry

The Giuseppe Meazza stadium, the largest in Italy and "Scala del calcio" in Milan and Inter, with the museum born in 1996, the first set up in a stadium in Italy, between historical links and cups.

Why go. The Football Stairway.
