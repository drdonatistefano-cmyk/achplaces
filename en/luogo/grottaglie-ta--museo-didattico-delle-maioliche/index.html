Museo didattico delle maioliche

Museums · Themed

Established in the school year 1937-38 at the State Institute of Art, on the edge of the Ceramic Quarter, it preserves about 300 artifacts from 1887 to today: grottaglian majolica of the XVI-XVII centuries, works of the Institute of the early twentieth century and pieces made for national and international competitions.

Why go. The art school majolicas.
