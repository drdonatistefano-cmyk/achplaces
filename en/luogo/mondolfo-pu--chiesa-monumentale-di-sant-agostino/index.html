Chiesa monumentale di Sant'Agostino

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Part of the Augustinian convent complex, documented by the end of the 13th century and today in the 16th century reconstruction with 18th century renovations: unique hall in red brick with barrel vault and twelve altars carved, carved and golden. It is a real art gallery, with paintings by Claudio Ridolfi, Guerrieri, Presutti, Tiarini and the workshop of Barocci, a polychrome fifteenth-century sandstone Pietà, a bar of the 16th-century Magistrate and a 17th-century cloister next door.

Why go. Twelve golden altars and a picture gallery in church.
