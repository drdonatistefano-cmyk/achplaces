Museo mineralogico «Don Giovanni Bonomo»

Museums · Science and university

Opened on August 3, 2002 in the building that housed the town hall and elementary schools, in front of the square, it was born from the collection donated by the parish priest Don Giovanni Bonomo, then enriched by other collectors. It preserves 3,333 minerals found almost all in Valle Antigorio and Val Formazza, including cervandonite, gasparite, fetiasitis and asbecaite. Open from July 15 to August 30 every day and from December 27 to January 4 and Easter, 16-18; other days by contacting the Municipality (tel. 0324 62021).

Why go. The rare minerals of the Ossola in one room.
