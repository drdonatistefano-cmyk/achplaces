Torrione e Antiquarium

Museums · Archaeological

Medieval tower at the entrance of the village, under the fourteenth-century belt. It houses the Antiquarium, in three sections from prehistory to the Middle Ages with instruments, funerary equipment and botanical and animal remains, the headquarters of the Order of the Nocino Modenese and the Cell of Messer Filippo, secret prison found in 1947.

Why go. The secret cell discovered in 1947.
