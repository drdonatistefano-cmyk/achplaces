Cappella Brancacci

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The frescoes of Masaccio, Masolino and Filippino Lippi in Santa Maria del Carmine, the painting school of the whole Renaissance.

Why go. The Tribute and the Hunted by Masaccio's Paradise.

  • Where Piazza del Carmine 14, Firenze
  • Entry €15 whole; €10 from 18 to 25 years; free under 18
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
