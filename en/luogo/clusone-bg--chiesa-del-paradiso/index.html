Chiesa del Paradiso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1495, with a simple and interior façade rich in paintings and chapels; a great restoration in 2016 brought to light a cycle of frescoes in the chapel of the Sorrows.

Why go. Frescoes recently rediscovered.
