Necropoli etrusca del Palazzino

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The most important archaeological area of the municipality: almost fifty late-Etruscan burials arranged on terraces overlooking the village, with an ancient ritual quarry road that connected the sources of Bacchus and the stream to the upper part, where probably stood a natural sanctuary.

Why go. The Etruscan necropolis overlooking the village.

the MiC card indicates "closed" every day without hours; summer guided tours reported by the MiC for the area: contact the Accademia degli Etruschi (tel. 348 5106404)
