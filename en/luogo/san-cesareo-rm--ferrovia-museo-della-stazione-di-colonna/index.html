Ferrovia-Museo della Stazione di Colonna

Museums · Themed

Born to preserve the last section, about 500 meters, of tracks of the line Rome-Fiuggi-Frosinone (designed from 1907, built from 1913 and opened in full in 1917, then abandoned in the post-war period) together with the buildings of the centenary station of Colonna, today in the territory of San Cesareo. After more than twenty years of abandonment, a group of individuals restored the tracks, which house a collection of historical rotabili; in the traveler building a faithful reconstruction of the office of the station manager, instruments, objects, documents and vintage photographs. Open Friday, Saturday and Sunday 9-13 and 14.30-18.30, with reservation required.

Why go. Historical trains on the last section of the Rome-Fiuggi.
