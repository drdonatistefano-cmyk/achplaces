Castello Aragonese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortification of Byzantine origin (IX-XI century), with round towers wanted by Ferdinand I of Aragon; in 1911 the Ministry of War allowed its demolition for building purposes and the oldest part was demolished.

Why go. The symbol of the city.
