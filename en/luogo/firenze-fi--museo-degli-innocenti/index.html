Museo degli Innocenti

Museums · City history · Paid entry

The museum of the ancient hospital of abandoned children overlooking Piazza Santissima Annunziata, with the fourteenth-century terrace of Caffè del Verone, accessible without ticket.

Why go. Six centuries of childhood.

  • Where Piazza della Santissima Annunziata 12, Firenze
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com
