Chiesa Matrice di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small church of the historic centre built between 1876 and 1882, with a modest façade on Piazza Dante; the sober interior with a single nave houses in the presbytery a wooden altar, a baptismal stone font and statues of St. Michael the Archangel and the Risen Christ, as well as a Pietà by Michele Pomes di Grottaglie on a sketch by the local sculptor Cosimo Carlucci.

Why go. The church of the origins of the village.
