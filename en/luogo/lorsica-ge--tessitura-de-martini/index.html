Tessitura De Martini

Local life and traditions · Crafts

A family workshop with a tradition dating back to the 16th century and over 300 exclusive designs handed down from generation to generation: silk damasks, lampshades, voiles, shantungs, satin and linen, and recently cashmere and silk cashmere; Giuseppe De Martini has built and patented a frame that reproduces the fabrics once handmade. Open Monday to Saturday 9-18 (tel. 0185 977302).

Why go. The last shop that still weaves Lorsica's damask.
