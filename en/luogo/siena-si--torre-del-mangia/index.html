Torre del Mangia

History and architecture · Towers · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The fourteenth-century tower of the Palazzo Pubblico, 88 meters high according to Terre di Siena: you climb on foot, to contingent groups, up to the view over the whole city.

Why go. The highest view of Siena.

  • Where Piazza del Campo 1, Siena
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website museocivico.comune.siena.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
