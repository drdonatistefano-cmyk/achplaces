Museum Ladin Ciastel de Tor

Museums · Themed

Museum opened in 2001 in a medieval castle of the 13th century, the main site of the Ladin provincial museum: it tells history, language, culture, archaeology, geology, legends, crafts and tourism development of the Ladin valleys of the Dolomites; halls and courtyard host exhibitions, concerts and seminars. Open from 1 May to 1 November, from Tuesday to Sunday at 10-18; only partially wheelchair accessible.

Why go. The castle that tells the Ladin world.
