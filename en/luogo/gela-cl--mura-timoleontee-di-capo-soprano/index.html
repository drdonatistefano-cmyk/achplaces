Mura Timoleontee di Capo Soprano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

The walls of the Greek city in Capo Soprano, a remarkable example of mixed construction technique, with the stretch discovered in excellent condition: Italia.it indicates them as the best preserved example of Greek military architecture.

Why go. The Greek walls best preserved.

Open only on days indicated by the Park (in October 9-13) and with obligatory booking by email to park.archeo.gela@region.sicilia.it

  • Where Capo Soprano, Gela
  • Entry €6, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
