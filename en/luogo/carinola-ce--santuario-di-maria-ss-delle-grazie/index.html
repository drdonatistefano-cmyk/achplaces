Santuario di Maria SS. delle Grazie

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

The church rises where, around 1600, the Madonna delle Grazie would appear to a young woman of the place; however, the structure could be older, due to the presence of previous frescoes and an icon of the Virgin of the fifteenth century, still object of lively devotion. It preserves a crypt with drainholes for the burial of the dead.

Why go. The fifteenth-century icon and the crypt with the scrollers.
