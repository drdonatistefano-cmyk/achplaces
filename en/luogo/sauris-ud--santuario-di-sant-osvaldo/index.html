Santuario di Sant'Osvaldo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In Sauris di Sotto, it conserves an altar with doors in carved wood, golden and painted by Michael Parth by Brunico in 1524, numerous ex-vote and a relic of Saint Osvaldo, venerated for centuries by pilgrims.

Why go. Michael Part's 16th century altar.
