Museo Nazionale della Scienza e della Tecnologia Leonardo da Vinci

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

In an olive grove of the sixteenth century, the Leonardo Galleries, a library of 40,000 volumes, the launcher Vega, the only fragment of the Moon exposed in an Italian museum and the submarine Enrico Toti.

Why go. You get on a real submarine.

  • Where Via San Vittore, Milano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources

Italia.it, Museo della Scienza e della Tecnologia · In Lombardia, Milano
