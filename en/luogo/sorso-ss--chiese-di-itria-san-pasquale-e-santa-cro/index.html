Chiese di Itria, San Pasquale e Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The church of the Blessed Virgin of Itria (XVII century), around which at the beginning of July the ardy of St. Constantine runs; the Baroque St. Pasquale Baylon, seat of the Jesuits; the late Dogotic oratory of St. Cross (XVI century).

Why go. Three centuries of churches in the center.
