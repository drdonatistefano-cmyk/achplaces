Chiesa di Sant'Ignazio di Loyola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church next to the seventeenth-century Jesuit College: it preserves wooden statues, including the Christ de s'Iscravamentu, and a sixteenth-century retablo called St. Christopher.

Why go. The retablo of St. Christopher.
