Giardini pensili del castello

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Made by the Longhi family in the 17th century on the vaults of the walkways, in the moat and between the towers: 3,500 square meters with secular trees about 800 meters above sea level, presented as the tallest hanging gardens in Europe, with a multisensory path (olfactory, tactile, audio and LIS). The view extends from the Colli Albani to the Itri valley.

Why go. An Italian garden with 360 degree views of the Ciociaria.
