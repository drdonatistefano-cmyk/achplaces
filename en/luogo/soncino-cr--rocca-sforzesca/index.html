Rocca Sforzesca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Wanted by the Duke Galeazzo Maria Sforza and started in 1473 by the Cremonese Bartolomeo Gadio, entirely made of bricks, with three square towers and a cylindrical double apparatus to protrude, an underground room used as a prison and the Tower of the Captain able to defend itself. Private residence of the Print since 1536, was restored by Luca Beltrami in 1886-1895; you enter from a bridge on the rivet.

Why go. One of the best examples of late fifteenth century fortress.

  • Where Via Ferrari Enea, Soncino
  • Entry Cumulation with Aquaria, Museum of Fighters and churches €7, reduced €5, family €16
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
