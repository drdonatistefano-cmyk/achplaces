Nuraghe Losa

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Entirely in large basalt blocks: trilobed nuraghe of the Middle Bronze (XV-XIV century B.C.), antemural with towers and remains of a village of huts until the Iron Age.

Why go. A masterpiece of the Nuragic age.
