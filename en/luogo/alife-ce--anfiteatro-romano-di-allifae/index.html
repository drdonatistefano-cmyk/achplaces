Anfiteatro romano di Allifae

History and architecture · Archaeology

Built in the first decades of the first century AD and discovered in 1976 thanks to aerial photos: 107 meters by 84, for 12,000-14,000 spectators according to estimates, with the arena later lowered and a forum for notables; in part it is still under the modern houses and today it hosts shows and recreational activities.

Why go. A small Colosseum in the Volturno valley.
