Giardino dei Tarocchi

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The park opened in 1998 from Niki de Saint Phalle to Garavicchio: 22 monumental sculptures 12-15 meters high covered with mirrors, Murano glass and ceramics, with the ticket office of Mario Botta.

Why go. A fantastic world that enchants children.

  • Where Garavicchio, Pescia Fiorentina, Capalbio
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website ilgiardinodeitarocchi.it
