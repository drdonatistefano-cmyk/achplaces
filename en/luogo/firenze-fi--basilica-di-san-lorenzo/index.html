Basilica di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The church of the Medici rebuilt by Brunelleschi since 1418 for Giovanni de' Medici, with the Old Sacristy, the cloister and the Treasury Museum.

Why go. The pure Renaissance, next to the market.

  • Where Piazza di San Lorenzo 9, Firenze
  • Entry €9 whole (church and museum)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility Fully accessible with ramps
  • Official website www.operamedicealaurenziana.org
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
