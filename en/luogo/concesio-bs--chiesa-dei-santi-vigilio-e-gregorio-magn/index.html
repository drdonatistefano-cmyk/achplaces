Chiesa dei Santi Vigilio e Gregorio Magno

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was cited as a new ecclesia sancti Gregori, built in 1237, born as a small chapel and became a parish in 1632, transformed in the eighteenth century and for the last time in 1932. It has four chapels on the side with barrel vaults and paintings from the 16th to the 18th century: a Pietà, perhaps from the Richino, the altarpiece with the Madonna and Child dated 1569, a painting by Pietro Marone, frescoes detached from the ancient church of San Velgio and paintings by Giuseppe Remida; in sacristy angels reggigandelabro baroque.

Why go. Brescian paintings from the Five to the eighteenth century in a hamlet.
