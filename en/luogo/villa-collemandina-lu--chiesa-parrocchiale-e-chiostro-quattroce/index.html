Chiesa parrocchiale e chiostro quattrocentesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The 15th century cloister, with trapezoidal plan with sixteen arches on thin columns in serene stone and carved capitals, is the only structure surviving the earthquake of 1920 that destroyed the village, then rebuilt.

Why go. The only survivor of the 1920 earthquake.
