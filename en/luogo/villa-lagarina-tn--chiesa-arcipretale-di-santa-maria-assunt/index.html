Chiesa arcipretale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Considered one of the most beautiful and important Baroque monuments of Trentino, imposing church of the center of which the Counts Lodron had the patronage, symbol of their political and religious power; its furnishings are now exhibited in the nearby Diocesan Museum, which organizes guided tours also to the church.

Why go. The Baroque heart of the Lodron village.
