Serrai di Sottoguda

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Natural park formed by a gorge of 2 km excavated by the stream Pettorina, with walls over 150 meters and 13 bridges, walkable in about an hour from Sottoguda towards Malga Ciapela. Entrance only on foot and with closed number (maximum 400 people), with helmet included in the ticket; last entrance at 17.

Why go. Two kilometers of canyon at the foot of the Marmolada.
