Museo «Strade di pietra»

Museums · Themed

Museo-Laboratorio sulle scalpellini (lapicidi) of the valley of Giara, whose school of Cènova produced decorative elements for the architecture of the whole western Liguria. Set up by the Municipality with the Montana Community, it has two sections: the stone museum in Cènova, about 140 m2 on the stone cycle from extraction to laying in operation, with videos, maps and a teaching room where to touch tips, chisels and compass; and three paths in the historical centers of Rezzo, Cènova and Lavina, where each piece is marked on the map with letter and number and described by a plaque. Tel. Common 0183 34015.

Why go. Understand how stone has become art in the portals of the valley.

the Municipality and the Park indicate the visit to the museum by reservation
