Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista e Museo Sistino

Museums · Old masters

The present church, built in 1802 by Pietro Maggi, has a single nave decorated in the early twentieth century by Giuseppe Pauri and an organ by Carlo Carlo Carletti installed in 1884; reopened after the restoration concluded in 2002, it houses the Museo Sistino with the cup donated by Sisto V to his birthplace, a medal with the effigy of his sister Camilla Peretti, the Sacred Conversation of Vincenzo Pagani and two tables by Vittore Crivelli with San Rocco and San Sebastiano.

Why go. The gifts of Sixtus V and two tables by Vittore Crivelli.
