Museo degli alpini

Museums · Themed

Managed by the section of Genoa of the National Alpine Association, documents the history of the Alpine troops and the association from the foundation to today with photographs, documents, uniforms, badges and relics, with attention to the Alpine traditions of the Ligurian valleys. It has a library, two exhibitions (on the War of Liberation and on the history of the Alpine troops) and two audiovisuals, distributed between the Tricolor Hall, the Hall of Fighters, the Hall of Father Basil, the Hall of Delfo Pieramati and the Hall of Cuneense Division; guided tours available. The MiC card (updated to 2020) does not show usable times: contact the museum (tel. 010 936930, museumeglialpini@libero.it).

Why go. Memories and memories of the Ligurian Alps.
