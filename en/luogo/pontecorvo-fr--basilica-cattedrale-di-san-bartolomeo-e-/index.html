Basilica Cattedrale di San Bartolomeo e Torre di Rodoaldo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Built in the 11th century on the ruins of the castle, next to the Tower of Rodoaldo then transformed into a bell tower; it conserved frescoes attributed to the Cavalier d'Arpino and the Baciccia, but was almost completely destroyed in the Second World War and rebuilt in Romanesque style.

Why go. The heart of the city, with the Lombard tower.
