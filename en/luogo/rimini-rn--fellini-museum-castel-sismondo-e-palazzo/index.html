Fellini Museum: Castel Sismondo e Palazzo del Fulgor

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The museum dedicated to Federico Fellini, between the fortress of Sigismondo Malatesta, Piazza Malatesta and the Palazzo del Fulgor with the cinema of "Amarcord."

Why go. The world of Fellini in his city.

  • Where Piazza Malatesta, Rimini
  • Entry €10 whole; €8 reduced (on Wednesday for all); only Palazzo del Fulgor €2; Art Card €14
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website fellinimuseum.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
