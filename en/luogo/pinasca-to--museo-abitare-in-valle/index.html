Museo «Abitare in Valle»

Museums · Themed

Museum of furniture and culture in Valligiana: documentation centre on the furnishings and culture of the valleys, with objects and furniture that show how the valley populations conceived their home. It also hosts temporary themed exhibitions. Opening hours on request (tel. 0121 800716).

Why go. The mountain house told by his furniture.
