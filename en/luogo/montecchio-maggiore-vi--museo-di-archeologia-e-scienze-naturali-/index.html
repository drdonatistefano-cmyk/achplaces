Museo di archeologia e scienze naturali G. Zannato

Museums · Archaeological

In the nineteenth-century Villino Lorenzoni, it is also the center of the Agno-Chiampo Museum System; twelve rooms, five of archaeology, from prehistory to the early Middle Ages with mainly ceramics of the Bronze and Roman Ages, and seven of natural sciences, mainly paleontology and mineralogy. Times indicated: Saturday 15-18.30, Sunday 9.30-12.30 and 15-18.30.

Why go. Finds and fossils of the territory between Agno and Chiampo.
