Chiesa di San Marco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Consecrated on 18 May 1197 according to an epigraph of 1703; the Byzantine bezel above the portal depicts St Mark between two deacons. It preserves monumental tombs of bishops of the ancient diocese.

Why go. The Byzantine 12th century bezel.
