Villa Pallavicino e Museo nazionale Giuseppe Verdi

Museums · Themed

The sixteenth-century villa with five chessboard bodies by the lords of Busseto, which houses a museum with the settings and atmospheres of Verdi's works.

Why go. The works of Verdi room by room.
