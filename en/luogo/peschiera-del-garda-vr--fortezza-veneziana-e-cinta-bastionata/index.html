Italy › Veneto › Peschiera del Garda

Fortezza veneziana e cinta bastionata

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Peschiera rises on one of the three fortified islands that connect Garda to the Mincio; the fortress, enlarged by the Scaligeri at the end of the thirteenth century, owes its pentagonal plan to the walls built by the Republic of Venice, which renewed the fortifications with terrapies and bastions at the five corners of the medieval belt. It was a link between Venice and its most western territories beyond the Mincio, played a role in the Napoleonic countryside and in the wars between the Austrian Empire and the Unity of Italy, and the Venetian defence works have been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2017. Walls, gates, canals and water ditches surround a historic centre of cobbled streets.

Why go. A UNESCO Venetian fortress entirely surrounded by water.
