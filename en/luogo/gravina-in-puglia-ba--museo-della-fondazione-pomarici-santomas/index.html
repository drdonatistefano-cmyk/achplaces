Museo della Fondazione Pomarici Santomasi

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the seventeenth-century palace left in 1917 by Ettore Pomarici Santomasi: museum, gallery, library and historical archive, with the frescoes of the crypt of San Vito Vecchio. Tuesday-Sunday 9-13 and 16-20, guided tours every hour; whole 4 €, reduced 3 €.

Why go. The frescoes of San Vito Vecchio.

  • Where Via Museo 23, Gravina in Puglia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
