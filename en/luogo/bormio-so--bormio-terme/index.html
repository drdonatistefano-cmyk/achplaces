Bormio Terme

Local life and traditions · Spas

Spa and sports center near the old town, fed by the Cinglaccia spring: indoor and outdoor pools, 60 meters slide, thermorium with saunas, Turkish bath and whirlpool, treatments with mud and inhalations and wellness treatments.

Why go. Thermal water in the village, also for families.
