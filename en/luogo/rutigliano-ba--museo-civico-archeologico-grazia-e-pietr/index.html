Museo civico archeologico «Grazia e Pietro Di Donna»

Museums · Archaeological

It collects the finds of the ancient Azetium peucezia from the Neolithic to the Early Middle Ages, with the collections donated Colamusi, Diogguardi, Didonna and Catamo: accessories of the necropolis of Azezius and Purgatory (over 400 tombs between VII century BC and XI AD), a dolmenic tomb of about 3500 BC and traces of the Byzantine and Longobard settlement of Sant'Apollinare.

Why go. The tombs of Azetium.
