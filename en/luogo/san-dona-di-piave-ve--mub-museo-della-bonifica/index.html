MUB - Museo della Bonifica

Museums · City history

In a building designed in 1967 as a convent of the Clarisses, adapted to exhibition space in 1983 and expanded in 1998 with European funds; in 2018 the exhibition was renovated according to the formula of the ecomuseum and in 2021, with the European project WALKofPEACE, a space dedicated to the Great War and the reclamation was inaugurated. The route tells the relationship between man and environment in the Lower Piave from the Roman and late Roman times to the contemporary age: the peasant life before the Bonifiche, the hydraulic transformations of the eastern Veneto, the First World War and the reconstruction of the landscape. Open Tuesday 9-12.30, Wednesday-Friday 10-12.30 (Thursday also 15-18), Saturday and Sunday 15-18; closed on Monday.

Why go. Understand how the Bonifiche and the Great War designed the Lower Piave.
