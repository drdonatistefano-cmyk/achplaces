Museo Diocesano

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the Palazzino dei Cadetti at the end of the 16th century: the triptych by Bernardino del Castelletto, a San Leonardo by Jacopo della Quercia and a crucifix attributed to Gian Lorenzo Bernini.

Why go. A small treasure chest of sacred art.

  • Where Via Alberica, Massa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
