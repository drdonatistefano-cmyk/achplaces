Fortezza Vecchia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On a promontory overlooking the sea to the west of Capo Carbonara, about 3 km south of the village: to the first polygonal nucleus, probably Aragonese, the Spanish added a complex with four towers, three of which pointed, with walls with two entrances and internal courtyard, in defence of the barbaric incursions of the port of Capo Carbonara and the mouth of the river; restored at the end of the eighteenth century, was abandoned in 1847. Outside, the permanent exhibition "Enemigos de la fe," pirates and coastal defenders, on the assaults on the Sardinian coast between the sixteenth and nineteenth centuries; from the wounds you can see the towers of Capo Boi and Porto Giunco and the lighthouse of Capo Carbonara.

Why go. The pirate fortress with sunset on the harbor.

Open only from June to October (Tuesday-Sunday 9-13 and 16-19), closed from November to May; the source advises you to contact the site before visiting

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
