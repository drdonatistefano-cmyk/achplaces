Riserva naturale del Torrente Orba

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Reserve of about 257 hectares established in 1987 between Predosa, Bosco Marengo, Capriata d'Orba and Casal Cermelli, also ZSC and SPA: riparian woods of willows and poplars, oaks, small grets and arid prairies on the first terraces, between fields and poplars. There are reported more than 200 species of birds, blooms protected as snowshoe and orchids, the dragonfly Oxygastra curtisii and the return of the wolf; the visitor center with ornithological museum is in the municipal mill of Bosco Marengo. Dogs only on a leash.

Why go. River forests and over 200 species of birds along the Orba.
