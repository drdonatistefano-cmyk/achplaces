Museo Nivola

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Founded in 1995 and run by the Nivola Foundation, it is located in the former public wash-house on the hill of Su Cantaru, overlooking the village, with pavilions designed by Peter Chermayeff and Umberto Floris and a park that contains the historical source. The permanent collection contains hundreds of sculptures, paintings and graphics by Costantino Nivola (1911-1988), a figure of modernism of the twentieth century; it also houses temporary exhibitions and artist residences on art, architecture, landscape and public art. The MiC indicates opening 10:30-19:30, closed on Wednesdays.

Why go. The great Sardinian-American sculptor in his home country.
