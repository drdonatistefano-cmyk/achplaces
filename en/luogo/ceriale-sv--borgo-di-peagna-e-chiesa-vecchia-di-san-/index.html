Borgo di Peagna e Chiesa vecchia di San Giovanni Battista

History and architecture · Villages

Medieval inland fraction, historically inhabited by noble families such as Girardenghi, Genta and Arduini. The old church of San Giovanni Battista, of fourteenth-century origin, contains frescoes from the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries and a Baroque bell tower; the parish church is eighteenth-century, in Baroque style. At the edge of the stream the source Anthia, which according to the legend of Anthia and Abrocome gives the name to the hamlet; at the end of August the library of Ligurian books hosts the exhibition "Books of Liguria" .

Why go. 15th century frescoes just a stone's throw from fossils.
