Duomo di Santa Maria Assunta e campanile

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Of thirteenth-century origins, it was rebuilt in 1840 after the collapse of the old bell tower (1838) and completed in the present form in 1930; inside seven altars dedicated to the saints and one to the Madonna, a relic of Blessed Bartholomew, an eighteenth-century altarpiece and the frescoes of Mattielli (1929-1939) on the ceiling and presbytery. The bell tower, begun in 1842 on a project by the Venetian Antonio Diedo and completed in 1893, is 90 meters high, the third of Veneto, and hosts 16 bells (the largest weighs 2.830 kg). The summit, with 360 degrees view, is accessible during the Prima del Torcolato.

Why go. Climb on the third bell tower of Veneto during the Premiere del Torcolato.
