Palazzo Pianetti, Pinacoteca civica e Galleria d'arte contemporanea

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The eighteenth-century palace has been built since 1748 for the Marquis Pianetti and completed around 1781, with an Italian garden; on the noble floor there is the rococo gallery of stuccoes and paintings, more than 70 metres long. The Pinacoteca has as reference Lorenzo Lotto, with works performed between 1512 and 1535 (Deposition, Annunciation, Madonna delle Rose, Pala di Santa Lucia, Visitation), and over two hundred 18th-century pharmacy vases of the ancient hospital; in the palace also the Archaeological Museum and the contemporary Art Gallery.

Why go. Five Lots and a 70-meter rococo gallery.

  • Where Via XV Settembre 10, Jesi
  • Entry €5 one museum, €6 two, €8 three
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
