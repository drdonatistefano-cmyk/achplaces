Museo delle Navi

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

It exhibits five Roman wrecks found between 1958 and 1961 during the work of the airport, in the ancient harbour basin: three river vessels for the transport on the Tiber, a sea cargo ship and a fishing boat with central tub for the live catch. Reopened after a long restoration.

Why go. The Roman ships of the harbor, up close.

  • Where Fiumicino
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
