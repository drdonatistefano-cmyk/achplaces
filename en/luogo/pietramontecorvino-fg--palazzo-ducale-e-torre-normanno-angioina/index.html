Palazzo Ducale e Torre normanno-angioina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The quadrangular crenellated tower of the 13th century, almost 40 meters high and recently restored, is connected by a high wall to the Doge's Palace, of supposed Angevin origin, on three floors and about 2,500 sqm: you can visit the Hall of representation with barrel vault, two courtyards and the hanging garden; on the vault the coat of arms of the Montalto di Tocco, last feudal.

Why go. The tower that dominates the Dauni Mountains.
