Fonte Aretusa

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fresh water source that forms a small lake with papyrus, on the seafront of Ortigia; next to the Old 18th century wash-house, open for free.

Why go. The papyrus by the sea.

  • Where Lungomare di Ortigia, Siracusa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info
