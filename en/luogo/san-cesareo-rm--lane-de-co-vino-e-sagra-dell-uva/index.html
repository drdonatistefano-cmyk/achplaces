Lane De.Co., vino e Sagra dell'Uva

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Lana is a fresh pasta born at the end of the nineteenth century, when peasants from neighbouring countries built a village of huts on the Colle dei Marcelli: soft wheat flour ground with stone, eggs, water and salt, spread, dried on wooden sticks and cut into triangles; since 2019 it is produced De.Co. and served with cod. The Castelli Romani DOC wine and the polenta pizzas with herbs are also produced; at the end of September the Festival of Grapes and the Typical Products carries allegorical floats decorated with grapes.
