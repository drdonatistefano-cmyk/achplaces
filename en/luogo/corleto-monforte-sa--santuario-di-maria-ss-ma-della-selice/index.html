Santuario di Maria SS.ma della Selice

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

At the slopes of the Monticelli hill, just over a kilometer from the village, look over the valley of Fasanella and of the heat up to the Gulf of Naples on clear days. The origins of the cult are dated back to the sixth century; the name Selice comes from the mule track that led to the first sacellus. The present building, rebuilt in 1960 after a landslide on the original plan, has a single nave covered in travertine, mosaic rosette, stained glass windows with the seven sacraments and a stone bust of the Madonna in Byzantine style; next, the ancient stone fountain called "Moccio" . It has been a diocesan shrine since 2016 and the festival falls on Tuesday after Pentecost.

Why go. An ancient sanctuary with views of the valleys of the Alburni.
