Gola del Bletterbach (GEOPARC)

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

The smallest system of the UNESCO Dolomites (271 hectares, since 2009): a deep gorge up to 400 meters long and about 8 km long, with at least four waterfalls, which bares the rocks from the late Permian to the Anisic, with traces of the greatest mass extinction of the Earth's history and fossils of footprints of saurians, shells, plants and fishes of about 260 million years ago. The Municipality established the GEOPARC in 2005; from the Visitor Centre of Aldino start the Butterloch geopaths (about 4.6 km, 2h30, to the waterfall) and Gorz (about 6.3 km, 3h20), and daily guided tours in season; compulsory helmet, rentable at the center.

Why go. The Grand Canyon of South Tyrol, open book of Dolomite geology.
