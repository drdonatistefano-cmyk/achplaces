Museo delle tappatrici d'epoca

Museums · Themed

Private collection of ancient corking machines, dated between 1600 and 1950 (over 200 according to the MiC, almost 300 according to Emilia-Romagna Turismo), with tools and objects related to the bottling of wine.

Why go. Three and a half centuries of corks and bottles.
