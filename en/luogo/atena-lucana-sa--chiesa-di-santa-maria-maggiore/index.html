Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Documented from 967 and collegiate since 1541, it rises on the remains of the Roman forum; rebuilt from 1741 in Baroque forms and decorated with stucco after 1857, it is called the church of the cherubs for its 108 angel heads. He preserves a cycle on the life of Our Lady and paintings by Nicola Peccheneda di Polla, including eight apostles of 1751.

Why go. The 108 angel heads and the canvases of Peccheneda.
