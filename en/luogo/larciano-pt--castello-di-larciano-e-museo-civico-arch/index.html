Castello di Larciano e Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Viewpoint

Ancient stronghold at the highest point of Larciano, the last refuge of the population in wartime, with irregular plan following the hill; inside the walls a church, a palace with tower, the mast, an ancient cistern and the foundations of the Captain's house. The 36-metre tower-cassero offers a wide view and since 1975 hosts the Civic Museum: kitchen ceramics, metals, polychrome majolica, brickwork, burials and everyday objects, with a didactic section and reconstruction of a tomb to the cappuccino of the Roman age. The sources date the castle between the XI-XII and the XIV century.

Why go. Climb the tower and read the history of the castle in the finds.
