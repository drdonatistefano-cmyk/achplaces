Convento di San Francesco a Casanova

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The 13th century complex consists of a church and a convent with cloister, which some sources attribute to Saint Francis himself. Tradition indicates the rock anfratto where the saint would rest; in sacristy an inscription contains the Gospel verse "Faciem tuam lava" (Matthew 6:16-18).

Why go. A thirteenth-century convent linked to the passage of Saint Francis.
