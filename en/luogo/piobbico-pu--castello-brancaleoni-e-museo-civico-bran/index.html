Castello Brancaleoni e Museo civico Brancaleoni

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortress of the thirteenth century on the rock ridge that dominates the village, transformed into a Renaissance residence between 1573 and 1587 by the counts Brancaleoni, lords of the place from 1213 to 1816. The courtyard of honour is Baccio Pontelli, the staircase is inspired by the Doge's Palace of Urbino; the Greek Chamber was frescoed in 1585 by Giorgio Picchi with stuccoes by Federico Brandani, author of the decorations of the Sala del Leon d'Oro; the clock tower turns counterclockwise. The Civic Museum has sections of uses and customs, speleology and geo-paleontology (more than 5000 fossils and the skeleton of a cave bear), ornithology, archaeology and numismatics. Open Saturday and Sunday 9-12 and 15-18.

Why go. A Renaissance Montefeltro court with a museum ranging from fossils to coins.
