Museo civico Fortunato Gaccetta

Museums · Modern art

Founded by the Municipality in honor of the sculptor Fortunato Gacce, born in Polia in 1920: trained in a school of Calabrian art, after the war he settled in Vipiteno and perfected himself in the workshop of Antonio Benetton; gold medal in Stockholm in 1964, awarded in Stuttgart in 1967 and honored in 1990 by the king of Spain for "I guerrieri di Spagna" he died in Treviso in 2006. The cultural center also has a library of about 2,000 volumes and a conference room.

Why go. Polia's sculptor came home.

The MiC card (updated to May 2020) is closed every day; Common tel. 0963 321091
