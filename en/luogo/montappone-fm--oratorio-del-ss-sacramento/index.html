Oratorio del SS. Sacramento

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the square built on the site of the ancient castle, it has a fourteenth-century terracotta portal surmounted by the coat of arms of the confraternity and frescoes of the sixteenth century, including a Madonna and Child of the Pagani.

Why go. The fourteenth-century portal in the heart of the village.
