Musei del Castello di Alviano

Museums · City history

In the fortress are located the historical and multimedia Museum of Bartolomeo d'Alviano and the captains of Ventura Umbria, the Museum of peasant civilization and the Centre of Faunistic Documentation of the Oasis of Alviano.

Why go. Leaders, rural life and nature under the same roof.
