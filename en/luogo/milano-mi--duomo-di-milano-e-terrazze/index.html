Duomo di Milano e Terrazze

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The largest church in Italy, Gothic, with its golden spires and Madonnina since 1774; inside capitals with statues, biblical windows and the sundial of 1786; in the elevator you go up to the terraces between the spires.

Why go. The city seen from the spires.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Milano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
