Chiesa e Chiostro del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the Carmelites rebuilt from 1727 and completed around 1758, with a major altar consecrated in 1759 and canvas by Pietro Cataldo Mauro (1760); it preserves the Madonna della Libera by Giovanni Stefano Caramia (1660), the wooden Crucifix by Giacomo Genovini (1630), the carved pulpit by Domenico Semeraro (1859) and the inlaid wardrobes of the sacristy. From the church you enter the cloister of the former convent. Open 7:30-12:30 and 16:30-20:30.

Why go. A very rich interior of Apulian baroque signatures.
