Fortezza Pisana e Casa del Parco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built by the Pisans in the 12th century in the highest part of the village and enlarged in the 15th century by the Appians with four bastions, it was the refuge of the inhabitants during the pirate raids; from the walk the view goes from Monte Capanne to the coast. It houses the Park's Environmental Education Centre, with free admission, on the geology of the Archipelago.

Why go. The village and the western Elba from above.
