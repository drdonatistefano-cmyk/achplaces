Filatoio di Piazzo

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Water-based silk yarn with the rotating shaft still visible, rare testimony in Europe: at the beginning of the nineteenth century there were three torcoils on five floors, moved by a hydraulic wheel, and a canning machine.

Why go. A silk machine remained almost unique in Europe.
