Funivia della Marmolada a Punta Rocca

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

From Malga Ciapela the cable car climbs to Punta Rocca, 3265 meters, with panoramic terrace; in winter from here descends La Bellunese, a 12-km track to Malga Ciapela in the Dolomiti Superski.

Why go. Top of the Dolomites Queen.
