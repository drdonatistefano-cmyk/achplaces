Chiesa di San Rocco a Piccianello

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church built on a project by Aniello Francia and completed in 1838, on a hill in the middle of the hamlet, in the hilly area of Val del Fino. It has a single rectangular nave marked by Ionian binated pilasters on three spans, with the central covered by a lower dome on plumes; the neoclassical brick façade is crowned by a large triangular tympanum with denticles and has triglyphs in the stripe marchepiano.

Why go. A nineteenth-century neoclassical church overlooking the Val del Fino.
