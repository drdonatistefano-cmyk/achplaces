Villa Rosa, Museo dell'Arte Vetraria Altarese

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Villa Liberty designed by Nicolò Campora at the beginning of the twentieth century, purchased by the Ministry in 1992 and restored, houses the museum of the history of the glass altar: works from the eighteenth century to today, tools for craftsmanship and multimedia stations on the processing phases. In the garden a demonstration furnace of about 70 m2, with two melting furnaces and a tempera oven, active since 2000 and often lit during the year. Open Tuesday-Sunday 14-18, closed on Mondays.

Why go. A thousand years of glass in a Liberty villa, with the furnace on.

  • Where Piazza del Consolato, Altare
  • Entry €7, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.museodelvetro.org
