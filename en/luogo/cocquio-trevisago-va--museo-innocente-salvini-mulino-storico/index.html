Museo Innocente Salvini - Mulino storico

Museums · House museums

The birthplace of the painter Innocent Salvini (1889-1979), who lived and worked all his life here, is a rare example of industrial archaeology of the seventeenth century with remains of an ancient medieval tower: two wheels and working gears moved by the stream Viganella, millstones, sieves and systems of wheat transport designed and automated by Salvini himself. Since 1983 it is a gallery: three rooms with rotating paintings with warm and bright colors on rural life, family and landscapes of the village, and frescoes en plein air in the courtyard; it houses exhibitions and events. Open from 1 April to 30 October Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday 15-18.

Why go. A watermill still working and painting of a lifetime.
