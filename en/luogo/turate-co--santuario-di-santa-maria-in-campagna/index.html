Santuario di Santa Maria in Campagna

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

A first nucleus is remembered as the church of San Maurizio da Goffredo da Bussero around 1270; in the middle of the fourteenth century the two churches were demolished to make room for the new church, saving the fresco of the Madonna today venerated on the altar of a side chapel, with baroque decorations and polychrome stuccoes of saints, angels and prophets. The presbytery has a cycle of frescoes on the childhood of the Virgin and a large cycle of sixteenth-century Stories of the Virgin. Cardinal Schuster raised him to the sanctuary in 1945; reported in the FAI census "I Luoghi del Cuore" in 2016, 2018, 2020 and 2022.

Why go. Trecentesque and sixteenth century frescoes dedicated to the Virgin.
