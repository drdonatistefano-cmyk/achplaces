Villino Campi

Museums · Science and university

Inaugurated in 1998, it is the main provincial centre dedicated to the lake environments, the water cycle and the ecosystem of Garda, with activities of environmental education and attention to sustainable tourism; it is open on request for educational activities.

Why go. Understand the ecosystem of the lake.
