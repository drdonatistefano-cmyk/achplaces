Chiesa di Sant'Agata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church with two naves in Moltrasino stone, in the locality of Vignola along the ancient Via Regia: dates back to the 11th century, a document of 1197 attests to a charity dedicated to the saint and in the 17th century was used as a lazzaretto. Outside the images of St. Agatha and St. Christopher, protectors of the travelers; inside sixteenth-century frescoes including the Christ Pantocrator between the Saints Rocco and Antonio Abate (restored in 2006) and a Renaissance wooden crucifix. Next to the churchyard is the nineteenth-century chapel Lucini Passalacqua.

Why go. A Romanesque gem in local stone on the way of the travelers.
