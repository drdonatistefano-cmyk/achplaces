Polo museale - Museo civico nell'ex Collegio dei Gesuiti

Museums · Old masters

In the seventeenth century Collegio dei Jesuiti, with works from the Renaissance sculpture to Mannerist and Baroque painting: the section of sacred art exhibits works saved from the churches damaged by the earthquake of Belice of 1968, including pieces attributed to Domenico Gagini and Francesco Laurana; the archaeological section finds from the excavations of the territory. A wing reconstructs the Holy House of Loreto with frescoed vaults.

Why go. The works saved from the earthquake of Belice.
