Duomo di Modena

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The Romanesque cathedral of Lanfranco and Wiligelmo consecrated in 1106, with the reliefs of Genesis on the facade, the Porta della Pescheria and the crypt of St Geminiano.

Why go. The most beautiful Romanesque in Europe, free.

  • Where Corso Duomo, Modena
  • Entry Presbytery €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
