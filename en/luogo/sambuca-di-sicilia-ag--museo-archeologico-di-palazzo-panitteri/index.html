Museo archeologico di Palazzo Panitteri

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The noble floor of the palace, born from the tower of the castle of the end of the sixteenth century, houses the archaeological museum of the Park of the Valley of the Temples, with Greek vases and the complement of the CLXX tomb of the middle of the fifth century BC. Open Mon-Fri 9-13, Sat-dom 9-18.

Why go. The artifacts of Monte Adranone in the heart of the village.

  • Where Via Panitteri 1, Sambuca di Sicilia
  • Entry €6, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
