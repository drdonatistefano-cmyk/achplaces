Convento e chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The seventeenth-century convent, now home to the GeoMuseum; the adjoining church houses wooden statues and sculptures from the fifteenth to the nineteenth century.

Why go. To be seen together with the GeoMuseum, in the same complex.
