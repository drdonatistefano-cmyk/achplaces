Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grotte

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church built in the second half of the thirteenth century in Gothic-Cistercian style, with frescoes of French and Tuscan taste late-Giotesque.

Why go. Unknown Gothic frescoes a stone's throw from the abbey.
