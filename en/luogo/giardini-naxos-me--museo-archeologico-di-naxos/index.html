Museo archeologico di Naxos

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

It rises on Capo Schisò and tells about the Greek colony and the prehistory of the site with materials coming mainly from the excavations begun in 1953. On the ground floor there is the neolithic cup of Stentinello, the Corinthian and Euboic ceramics of the first colony and the masquerade antefixes of silenum, typical production of the city (late VI-V century BC); on the upper floor are the sacred areas, the archaic and classical city, the necropolis and a numismatic toilet. To see the arula with sphinxes faced (530 BC) called "Heidelberg-Naxos" rebuilt in 1997; in the sixteenth-century tower adjacent to the stone and lead anchors recovered in the bay. Open every day 9-19.45.

Why go. The finds of the Greek colony, from prehistory to the sea.
