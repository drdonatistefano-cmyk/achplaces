Museo etnografico dell'Alta Brianza (MEAB)

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 2003 in the closed courtyard, partly still inhabited, of the fourteenth-century agricultural village of Camporeso, among the terraces: it tells the life and work of the folk classes of the Brianza hills and of the Lecchese between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries. It is one of the few demo-ethno-anthropological museums recognized by the Lombardy Region and leader of the REBEL network.

Why go. The Brianza peasant life in a medieval village.
