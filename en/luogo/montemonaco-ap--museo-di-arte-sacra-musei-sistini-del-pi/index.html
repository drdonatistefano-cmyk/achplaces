Museo di arte sacra (Musei Sistini del Piceno)

Museums · Old masters

It collects many wooden sculptures of the territory; among the paintings the Madonna del Soccorso di Giulio Vergari of 1521, a Madonna del Rosario of the late sixteenth century and a altarpiece of Saints Biagio and Antonio Abate, recently found and attributed to Pier Leone Ghezzi (1674-1755).

Why go. Wooden sculptures and the altarpiece attributed to Ghezzi.
