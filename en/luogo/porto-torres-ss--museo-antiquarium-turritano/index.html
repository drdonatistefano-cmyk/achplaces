Museo Antiquarium Turritano

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Built in the early 1970s and opened in 1984 in the area of Turris Libisonis, it exhibits in two sections the statuary and architectural sculpture of the cult buildings and the objects of daily life from spas, tabernacles, houses and necropolis, mostly of the I-III century AD, including the Ara di Bubastis in marble of the first century AD and the marble mask of the Domus del Satiro; it preserves a heated environment of a spa and from the windows of the upper floor you can see the excavation. Open from Tuesday to Saturday 9-16 (ticket office until 3 pm), closed on Sunday and Monday.

Why go. The findings of the Roman colony on the site of the excavation.

  • Where Via Ponte Romano 99, Porto Torres
  • Entry €5, reduced €2; cumulative with the archaeological area €8
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website antiquariumportotorres.cultura.gov.it
