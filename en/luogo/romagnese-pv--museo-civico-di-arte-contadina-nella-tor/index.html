Museo civico di arte contadina nella torre del castello

Museums · Themed

Museum of the Lombard Apennines of the Four Provinces set up in the castle tower: it preserves tools, tools and machinery of the local agricultural and craft life.

Why go. The tools of peasant work inside the medieval tower.
