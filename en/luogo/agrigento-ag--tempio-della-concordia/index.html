Tempio della Concordia

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

One of the best preserved Doric temples in the world, in the heart of the Valley of the Temples; there the tripod of the Festival of the Almond in bloom is lit.

Why go. The Doric temple is almost intact.
