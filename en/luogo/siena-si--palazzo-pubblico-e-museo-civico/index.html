Palazzo Pubblico e Museo Civico

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The Palazzo della Signoria built between 1297 and 1310, now seat of the Municipality, with the Civic Museum: the Majesty and the Guidoriccio of Simone Martini and the cycle of the Good and Bad Government of Ambrogio Lorenzetti (1338-1339).

Why go. The most famous political fresco in the Middle Ages.

  • Where Piazza del Campo 1, Siena
  • Entry paid
