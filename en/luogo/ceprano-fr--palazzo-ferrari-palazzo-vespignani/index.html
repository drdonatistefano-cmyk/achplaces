Palazzo Ferrari (Palazzo Vespignani)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

In front of the bridge over Liri, it was commissioned in 1834 by Monsignor Ferrari to the architect Vespignani, from which the second name; the facade has Doric columns, pilasters and floral festoons. It is one of the historic residences of Lazio and on the lower floors is the definitive seat of the Archaeological Museum of Fregellae. You visit only by reservation.

Why go. A nineteenth-century residence overlooking the Liri.
