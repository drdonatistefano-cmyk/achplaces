Villa Bertarelli e giardini storici

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Attested in the Teresian cadastral of 1721, it hosted several times Carlo Porta between 1801 and 1815; the Ballabio created brolo and formal gardens with fountains, nymphs and stone mosaics, Giuseppe Bovara designed the gate in 1816 and Piero Portaluppi restored it in neo-baroque style in 1911-1913. Since 2003 it has been a municipality and park, which are located in the center of Flora Autoctona; the historical gardens are visited on Saturday morning not festive, with views of the Briantian lakes from the entrance.

Why go. Nineteenth-century gardens overlooking the lakes.
