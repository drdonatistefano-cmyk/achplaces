Museo archeologico «La Maddalena»

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in June 2004, it documents the site of the Magdalene from the recent Neolithic period (end of the fifth millennium BC) to the second Iron Age (IV century BC), with the findings of the excavations; it also exhibits paintings of the landscape landscaper G.A. Levis (1873-1926), pupil of Delleani and mayor of the village at the beginning of the twentieth century. Guided visits and workshops with archaeologists upon reservation.

Why go. The prehistory of the Valley of Susa.

The MiC card indicates "Closed" every day; the city reports guided tours by reservation (tel. 0122 54104)
