Museo della cultura contadina Alta Val Trebbia

Museums · Themed

Opened in 1990 in the former convent of the Augustinians next to the parish of Santa Maria Assunta. The ethnographic section collects tools of arts and crafts, agricultural machinery, chariots and calexs, an ancient still working mill and rebuilt peasant environments (stalla, cucina, camera); the section of sacred art exhibits objects of worship, banners and pictures, while the cloister houses photographic documentation on the churches of the valley. Tel. 010 95033.

Why go. The peasant world of Val Trebbia in a 15th century convent.
