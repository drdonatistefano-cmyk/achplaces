Torre del Castello di Orezia

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Tower on a rock spur overlooking the plain of Dervio; more exactly a mastic, whose fortification consisted of the walls of the surrounding buildings. The municipality indicates it with access for a fee, without amount or times.

Why go. The mastium that guards the Varrone plain.
