Chiese del centro storico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In addition to the Duomo, the center preserves the Gothic church of St. James (13th century), restored with the former convent of the Augustinians; St. Francis, with oval portal of the fourteenth century; Santa Maria Assunta, the church of the Kings Magi and Saint Andrew, with Baroque interiors; and the eighteenth-century church of the Tintes, with Greek cross with octagonal dome.

Why go. An itinerary from Gothic to Baroque in a few streets.
