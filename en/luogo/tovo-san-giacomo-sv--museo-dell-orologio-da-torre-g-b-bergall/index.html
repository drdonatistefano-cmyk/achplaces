Museo dell'orologio da torre G.B. Bergallo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Opened in 1997, the first museum of its kind in Italy, was born from the collection donated to the village by Giovanni Bergallo, the last watchmaker of the family who built tower clocks in the workshop house of Val Maremola between 1861 and 1980. In 260 square meters of an old town hall exhibits about thirty authentic tower clocks (the oldest of the 16th century), machinery and tools, dials, hands, pulleys and objects from bell towers, documents and a video; the visit may include the Bergallo workshop house. For the twenty-year anniversary, the documentary "Time Beyond the Ocean" was shot on the clock sold in 1934 to the Salesian mission of Puerto Santa Cruz in Patagonia. Open Friday, Saturday and Sunday: October-May 15-18, June-August 17-21, September 16-20; schools €3, free for residents; guided tour of about 45 minutes.

Why go. Five centuries of bell tower watches.
