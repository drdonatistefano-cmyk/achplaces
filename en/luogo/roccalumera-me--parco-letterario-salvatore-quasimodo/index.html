Parco Letterario Salvatore Quasimodo

Museums · House museums

It is located in the former FS station and in vintage freight wagons, with the Quasimodiano Museum, the Train-Museum and the Garden-Museum-A. Quasimodo: he holds documents, relics, writings, audiovisual materials and the Milanese study of the poet, Nobel Prize in literature in 1959.

Why go. The Milanese Nobel study rebuilt in a station.

visits only by reservation required (tel. 347 4086661)
