Complesso monumentale di Santa Caterina

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

The Dominican church and convent arose around 1360 at the behest of the widow of the Marquis Giorgio I Del Carretto; the convent was imprisoned from 1863 to 1964.

Why go. From the convent to the prison to the museum.
