Ex cattedrale di San Pietro

Places of worship · Cathedrals

In the enclosure of the cemetery, at the east exit of the village: seat of the small medieval diocese (transferred to Nuoro in 1496), includes the Romanesque cathedral started around 1090 and remained unfinished, the sixteenth-century bell tower and the church of San Pietro with frescoes from the first thirteenth century of Umbrian-Latial painters on stories of the Old and New Testaments.

Why go. Two hundred frescoes in an unfinished cathedral.
