Museo dinamico del laterizio e delle terrecotte

Museums · Themed

Museum on the territory dedicated to rural civilization, crafts and the brick industry, of which Marsciano is the largest Umbrian center. The main pole is Palazzo Pietromarchi, 14th century noble residence of the Counts Bulgarelli, with the section on the brick and a fresco of the first sixteenth century school of Pinturicchio (Madonna on throne with Child and Angels, San Silvestro and San Rocco).

Why go. The history of the brick in its Umbrian capital.
