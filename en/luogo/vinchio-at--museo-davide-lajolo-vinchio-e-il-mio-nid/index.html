Museo Davide Lajolo «Vinchio è il mio nido»

Museums · House museums

Multimedia Museum dedicated to Davide Lajolo (1912-1984), born in Vinchio, partisan with the name of battle "Ulisse" since 1943, director of "L'Unità" (1948-1958), deputy and Viareggio Prize 1977 with "Veder l'erba" on the side of the roots ""; retraces his life as partisan, journalist and writer. Individual and guided tours by reservation (groups up to 30 people), audioguide in Italian and English, accessible to disabled people. In the village there are also the birthplace with plaque (Via Ramaudio 4) and the bronze bust of Floriano Bodini on the stairway between Piazza Vercelli and Piazza San Marco.
