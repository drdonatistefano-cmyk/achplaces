Sorgente di Su Gologone

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

Karst resurgence at the foot of Mount Uddè, the most important Sardinian spring and natural monument since 1998: the water flows from a vertical rift with an average flow rate of about 500 liters per second, in shades from emerald green to blue; in 2010 it was explored up to 135 meters. It is observed from the terrace of the little church of Our Lady of the Pietà; around a forest of eucalyptuses.

Why go. The most spectacular spring in Sardinia.
