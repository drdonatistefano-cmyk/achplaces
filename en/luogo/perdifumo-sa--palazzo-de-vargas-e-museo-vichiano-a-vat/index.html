Palazzo De Vargas e Museo vichiano a Vatolla

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The philosopher Giambattista Vico lived in Palazzo Vargas in Vatolla from 1686 to 1695, as tutor of the grandchildren of Monsignor Rocca, and spent the study days there. The palace is home to the Giambattista Vico Foundation, which houses the Library of the Park and a museum dedicated to the philosopher in the place where he lived for nine years; in the courtyard of the castle is held every summer the Festa della Cipolla.

Why go. The house where Vico studied for nine years.
