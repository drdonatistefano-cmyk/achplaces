Riserva Naturale delle Torbiere del Sebino

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Protected wetland of about 360 hectares at the southern edge of Lake Iseo, from which separates a thin morainic cordon, between the municipalities of Iseo, Provaglio d'Iseo and Corte Franca: swampy soils, reeds and water mirrors born where in the nineteenth century peat was extracted for the ironworks of Brescia. Known as a wetland of international importance, ZSC and ZPS, it hosts red herons, swamp hawks, brown and tarabous nibble, and fishes such as tinca, pike and royal perch. You can visit wood paths and walkways between the blades; three main entrances, one at Corte Franca, each with an automatic ticket; open daily. No bikes inside.

Why go. Walks on the water between reeds and herons, a stone's throw from the lake.
