Palazzo Caetani

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built by Duke Bonifacio Caetani in 1560 incorporating the medieval fortress of the Frangipane, on a project by Francesco da Volterra, he lost in the Second World War the oldest part with the rooms painted by Girolamo Siciolante, Stefano Dupérac and perhaps the Zuccari brothers. The Zuccari Room remains with the large fresco of the Caetani's pontoons in the eighteenth century, the trompe-l'oeil of the Loggia Room, the court porticoed with a Roman well and access to the Torre Frangipane, and under the building a network of caves about fifteen meters deep where at Christmas the crib is set up. It hosts exhibitions, concerts, the Cisterna Film Festival and the municipal library.

Why go. The rooms and caves of the Caetani dukes.
