Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Longobard stronghold of the tenth century, passed to the Sanseverino, Ferrillo and Orsini of Gravina; it is remembered for the imprisonment and killing of Queen Joan I of Anjou. It collapsed with the earthquake of 1694, it was rebuilt and expanded.

Why go. The castle of Queen Joan I.
