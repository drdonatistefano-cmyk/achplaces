Chiesa dei Santi Gallo e Desiderio e Via Crucis

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Outside the town of Ponna Inferiore, it has Romanesque origins (XI century hypothesised), was enlarged in the fifteenth century and preserves a fresco of Ambrogio da Valsolda of 1504. Faces and walls are frescoed with squares of the Barelli, stories of St. Gallen and St. Desire and paintings of Felice Trivella; altarpieces of Christopher and Carpoforo Tencalla (1745) and high altar at temple of Pietro Giacomo Barelli (1756). The access road is marked by the fourteen chapels of the Via Crucis designed by Gallo Barelli and decorated by Carlo Scotti in 1771; on the south side there is an atrium with a trompe-l'oeil painted façade.

Why go. The work of the intelvese masters in the church of their country.
