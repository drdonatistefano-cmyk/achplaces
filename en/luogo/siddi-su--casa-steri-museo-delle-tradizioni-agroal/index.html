Casa Steri - Museo delle tradizioni agroalimentari della Sardegna

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In a farmhouse in the courtyard of the seventeenth century in the historic center, it tells the food history of the island from the Nuragic age: it rises on milk and oil, a mill with a rare eighteenth-century animal traction mill, the kitchen, the stables with the tools for vinification, the barn and a room with murals liberty. Periodic workshops on pasta, bread and cakes.

Why go. The taste of the Marmilla in a house at court.

  • Where Via Roma 2, Siddi
  • Entry €10.50, reduced €5, family €20
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
