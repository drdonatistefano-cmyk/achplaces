Area archeologica di Fossa Nera

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the "Piana delle centeno farme" it preserves traces of a village of the Bronze Age (about 1200 BC, with a decorated fibula, a torques and Baltic amber beads), a nucleus of Etruscan houses around 500 BC with Etruscan and Greek vinarium amphorae, and two Roman farms: Black Fossa A, of the II century BC, with a calcatorium for wine, and Black Fossa B, dedicated to weaving, cheese and wine. The site has dedicated tours.

Why go. Three thousand years of peasant life in the plain.
