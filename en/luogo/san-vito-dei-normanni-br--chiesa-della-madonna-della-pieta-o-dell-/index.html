Chiesa della Madonna della Pietà o dell'Ospedale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1559 and profoundly remodelled in the last decade of the eighteenth century, it was annexed to a shelter for the poor and travellers then demolished, hence the name. Small temple with square plan with table on two levels marked by pilasters; today it is in convenient to the Municipality. Tel. 0831 955236.

Why go. A small sixteenth-century temple in the center.
