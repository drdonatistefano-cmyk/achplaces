Teatro Comunale

History and architecture · Historic theatres

From the Austrian period, after Casalmaggiore had the title of city in 1754, designed by architect Mones with the approval of Giuseppe Piermarini: it is a small version of the Scala of Milan.

Why go. A little Scala on the Po.
