Anfiteatro romano del Verlasce

History and architecture · Archaeology

The ancient Roman amphitheatre beyond the area of the springs, incorporated into the urban fabric; part, with theater, nymph, ancient roads and an aqueduct of about thirty kilometers, of the Roman Venafro.

Why go. The arena inside the city.
