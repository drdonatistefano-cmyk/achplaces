Muvita Science Center

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

Centro di animazione scientifica, the first in Italy dedicated to the relationship between man, energy and climate, with educational paths and workshops for young and adult people on climate machine, energy sources, saving and energy efficiency. According to the Mic card (updated to 2020) opens Monday to Friday 8-18.

Why go. Energy and climate explained with laboratories.

  • Where Via Marconi, Arenzano
  • Entry €7, reduced €6
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.muvita.it
