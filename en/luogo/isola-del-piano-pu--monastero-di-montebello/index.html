Monastero di Montebello

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

On the hills between Isola del Piano and Montefeltro, in 1377 Blessed Pietro Gambacorta arrived there; today it is the headquarters of the Agricultural Cooperative Girolomoni, one of the first organic agricultural cooperatives in Italy, and hosts meetings, educational and cultural activities, including usually a stage of the summer festival Â"Ville and Castella" .

Why go. Where Italian organic farming was born.
