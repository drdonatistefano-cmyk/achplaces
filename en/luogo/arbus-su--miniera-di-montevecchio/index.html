Miniera di Montevecchio

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Mining complex between Arbus and Guspini, part of the Park Geominerario of Sardinia: the first concession was given in 1848 to Giovanni Antonio Sanna and the activity lasted until the final closure of 1991; in 1865 it was the most important mine of the Kingdom with 1,100 workers. Four itineraries of visit: the Palazzina of the direction (1870-77) with the blue room and the church of Santa Barbara, the well Sant'Antonio, the workshops with the foundry of 1885 and the Piccalinna route with the winch of the late nineteenth century.

Why go. The heart of mining Sardinia.
