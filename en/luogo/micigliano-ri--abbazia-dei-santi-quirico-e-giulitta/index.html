Abbazia dei Santi Quirico e Giulitta

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Founded by the Benedictines in the first half of the 10th century (first definite news in 984) between the Via Salaria and Velino, it depended directly on the Holy See; burned during the Norman invasion, it was rebuilt by the abbot Sinibaldo and consecrated in 1179, and was abandoned at the end of the 18th century. It preserves the fortified structure with quadrangular wall and Romanesque elements, and in the center an imposing bell tower used as a sighting tower, with sculptures of a human head, an abbot with miter and pastoral and a lion head.

Why go. An abbey-fortress on the Salaria.
