Museo civico Angelo Comolli

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

Dedicated to the painter and fresco artist Liberty Angelo Comolli, active in Milan between the eight and twentieth centuries: he exhibits the great preparatory cartoons to charcoal of sacred subject, found in the abbey after 1982, with sketches, paintings, furniture and photographs. Visit accompanied by about half an hour, by reservation or in the free openings scheduled.

Why go. The cartoons of a Milanese liberty decorator.

  • Where Piazza Municipio 1 (primo piano del Palazzo Comunale), Morimondo
  • Entry €3,50
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
