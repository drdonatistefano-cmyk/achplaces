Area archeologica, Teatro e Antiquarium di Eraclea Minoa

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

City founded by the Selinuntini in the 6th century BC, then under Akragas, punic after 409-406 BC and re-colonized by Publius Rupilius in 132 BC; a walls of about 6 km encloses the plateau. The excavations, begun in the fifties, have exposed walls, necropolis, houses and the theatre of the fourth century BC in blocks of marna, facing the sea and protected by a temporary cover. The small Antiquarium at the entrance exhibits figurative ceramics, terracotta statuettes and accessories.

Why go. A Greek theatre suspended on the sea.
