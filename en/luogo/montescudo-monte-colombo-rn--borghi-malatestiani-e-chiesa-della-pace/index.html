Borghi malatestiani e Chiesa della Pace

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Two walled villages on the Valconca; in Trarivi the ancient parish church has become Museum of the Eastern Gothic Line - Church of Peace, and in Valliano there is a small ethnographic museum.

Why go. In August the IGP potato festival of Montescudo.
