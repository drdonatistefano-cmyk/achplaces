Museo Hendrik Christian Andersen

Museums · House museums

In the Flaminio district the house-study built between 1922 and 1925 by the Norwegian-American sculptor, lived in Rome from 1898 to 1940, with the monumental statues of the utopian project of the "World City."

Why go. The utopia of a sculptor.
