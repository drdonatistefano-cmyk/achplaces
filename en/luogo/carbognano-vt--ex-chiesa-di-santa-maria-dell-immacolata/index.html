Ex Chiesa di Santa Maria dell'Immacolata Concezione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

Wanted by Giulia Farnese to venerate the Immaculate Conception and built in 1522 (inscription on the portal) instead of a smaller chapel, of which Gothic mullioned windows remain. Façade with a bezel portal, rose window and a small asymmetrical bell tower; inside the single nave with a trussed roof, three Renaissance windows on the side and an apse with paintings dated 1581, attributed to students of the brothers Zuccari; the restoration started in 2006 brought to light frescoes waiting for restoration. Today it is the municipality and hosts exhibitions and conferences; the tradition wants the remains of Giulia Farnese to be hidden.

Why go. The church of Giulia Farnese with paintings from the Zuccari school.
