L Molin, il mulino del Museo Ladin

Museums · Themed

Working mill in the area of Pezol, of the Rizzi family (Salin) from the nineteenth century: two millstones for barley, an oil mill, a pestle with vertical motion and a windmill. Restored by Giuseppe Longo and opened in 1983, it was the first section on the territory of the Ladin Museum.

Why go. A Ladin mill still running.
