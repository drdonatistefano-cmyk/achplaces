Museo Deleddiano – Casa natale di Grazia Deledda

Museums · House museums

The birthplace of the Nobel Prize-winning writer, in the district of Santu Pedru, which preserves the memory of an artist who opened Sardinia to the world.

Why go. In the house of the Nobel laureate.
