Cisterna romana

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Built in the first century B.C. under the hole, it is about 57 meters long: ten parallel rooms with barrel vaults carved into the rock; in the last room the lead fistulae that fed it.

Why go. Ten rooms under the square.

  • Where Piazza Giacomo Matteotti 75, Amelia
  • Entry In the single ticket (€7 full, €5 reduced)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
