Monte Nieddu e rio Pitrisconi

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Granite massif between Padru and San Teodoro, at the southern end of Gallura; from the peaks of Punta Majore and Punta Coloredda you can see the Gennargentu, the beaches of San Teodoro and Tavolara. In the woods of holm oaks, junipers and oaks flows all year round the Pitrisconi river, with waterfalls, natural pools (such as that of Pitriolu), the waterfall of Scala Taddata and a gorge in granite; a ring itinerary follows the course and there are picnic areas. There live mouflons and nest the royal eagle.

Why go. Trekking between waterfalls and natural pools, with views of Tavolara.
