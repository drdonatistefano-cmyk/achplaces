Museo civico archeologico «I luoghi del tempo»

Museums · Archaeological

In the basement of the Town Hall: a Roman cryptoportico, inscriptions, capitals and sections of polygonal walls of the fourth century BC, with a reproduction of the Verolani Fasti.

Why go. The cryptoporic under the municipality.
