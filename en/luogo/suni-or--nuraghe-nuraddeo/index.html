Nuraghe Nuraddeo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The only complex nuraghe of Suni, on the plateau of Pedrasenta at 350 meters, of the Bronze Age (XIV-X century BC): the central tower exceeds 14 meters, with two rooms in tholos, and is surrounded by a bastion with three towers around a pentagonal courtyard.

Why go. The most impressive nuraghe in the park.
