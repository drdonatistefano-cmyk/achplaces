Museo del Mare

Museums · Themed

Opened in 2014 in the center of Capoliveri, it is dedicated to the treasure of the ship Polluce, departed from Naples to Marseille and sunk off Capoliveri on the night of June 17, 1841 with a large load of coins; the wreck was found, looted and then recovered by the State. Organizes workshops, guided tours, conferences and walks

Why go. The true story of a hidden treasure off the Elbe.
