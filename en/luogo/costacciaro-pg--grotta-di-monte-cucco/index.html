Grotta di Monte Cucco

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

The most important element of the park is one of the major karst formations in Italy, with about 30 km of galleries. In Costacciaro are located the School of Speleology and the National Center of Speleology, in the former Clarisse monastery, and the Didactic Village linked to the speleological Hiking Centre; among the experiences proposed the crossing of the cave.

Why go. In the belly of the mountain, with speleological guides.
