Museo del castagno

Museums · Themed

In the building in front of the town hall, it illustrates with panels and reproductions the diseases and parasites of chestnut and its fruit, with numerous original tools for the working of the wood and its products. Open only by reservation.

Why go. The culture of mountain chestnuts.
