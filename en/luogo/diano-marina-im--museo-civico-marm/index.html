Museo Civico MARM

Museums · Archaeological

Managed by the Istituto Internazionale di Studi Liguri, it occupies ten rooms (a multimedia one) on the second floor of the Palazzo del Parco and traces in chronological order the human presence between Capo Berta and Capo Cervo, from the bronze age (XVIII century B.C.) to the late antiquity and the first barbaric invasions that led to the birth of Diano Castello on the hill. The fifth hall is dedicated to ancient routes, goods and means of transport; the wreck of a ship Â"a doliaÂ" sunk in the middle of the first century AD in the Gulf of Dianese stands out. There are also the archaeological sections "Lucus Bormani" , Risorgimento "Andrea Rossi" and mineralogical "De Cavero" . Winter time: Mar 9-14; mer-ven 9-12 and 15-17; Sat 10-12 and 15-17; in summer evening openings Mer and Fri 21-23; closed Sun and Mon. Tel. 0183 497621.

Why go. The cargo of a Roman ship found at the beach.
