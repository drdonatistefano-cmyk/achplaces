Museo eoliano dell'emigrazione

Museums · Themed

Founded by the International Centre for Research on Aeolian History and Culture (C.I.R.C.E.), it tells the stories of those who emigrated from the Aeolian Islands to the United States, Australia, Argentina and other destinations since the end of the 19th century. Open every day 9-13, on request also at other times.

Why go. The stories of the Eolian immigrants in the world.
