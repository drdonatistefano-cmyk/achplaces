Parco avventura Selva del Buffardello

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Near the Orecchiella and Fortezza delle Verrucole, acrobatic paths on trees for all ages with catwalks, Tibetan and Tyrolean bridges up to 15 meters from the ground, as well as excursions, trail running and e-bike; picnic areas with barbecue, bar and playground for children.

Why go. One day in the trees for the whole family.
