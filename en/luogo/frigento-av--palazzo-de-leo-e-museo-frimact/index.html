Palazzo De Leo e Museo FriMact

Museums · Archaeological

Palazzo Gentilizio 19th century, house of the literary Marciano De Leo, today House of Culture with the Civic Library and the FriMact Museum, which combines an archaeological section on the history of the village with a collection of contemporary art among the three most important in Southern Italy.

Why go. Archaeology and contemporary art in the same building.
