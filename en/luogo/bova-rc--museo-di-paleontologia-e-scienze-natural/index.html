Museo di Paleontologia e Scienze Naturali dell'Aspromonte

Museums · Themed

In the former Pretura, on about 300 m2, it traces the geological eras and the environmental evolution of Calabria with about 15,000 findings from the Paleozoic to the Quaternary collected by Demetrio Lo Giudice and acquired by the Municipality: a deer jaw of about 100,000 years old, skull bones of Pliocene whale found at 750 meters on the Aspromonte, a rib of sirenide from the Lazarus Stone. Open Monday-Friday 8-14, closed Saturday and Sunday.

Why go. The natural history of Calabria in a mountain village.
