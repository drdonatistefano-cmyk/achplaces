Piazza Grande e Palazzo Comunale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Viewpoint

The heart of the city, with the well of Griffins and Lions designed by Antonio da Sangallo and the Gothic Town Hall in travertine, whose crenellated tower rises with an internal staircase up to the panorama of Valdichiana and Val d'Orcia.

Why go. From the tower you can see two valleys.
