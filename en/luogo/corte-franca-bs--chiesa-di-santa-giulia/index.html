Chiesa di Santa Giulia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Chapel founded in the Lombard age and named after the Brescia monastery of San Salvatore-Santa Giulia, transformed around the XI-XII century into a single nave church; the mural paintings, attributed to the fourteenth century, remain only in fragments.

Why go. An ancient dedication to the monastery of Santa Giulia.
