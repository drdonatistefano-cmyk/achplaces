Chiese di Santa Croce, dell'Assunta e di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Next to the Crucifix stands the medieval church of Santa Croce, with porch, sailing bell tower and frescoes inside; less than 100 meters from the seventeenth-century churches of the Blessed Virgin Assumption and St. Francis. Galtellì counts about five churches in a square kilometre.

Why go. Five churches in a mile.
