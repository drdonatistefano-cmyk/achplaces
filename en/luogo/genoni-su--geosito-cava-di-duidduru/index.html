Geosito Cava di Duidduru

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Geopaleosito visitable, described as unique in Sardinia and with few comparisons in Italy; part of the recovered fossils is exposed at the Paleo Archeo Centro at the foot of the hill.

Why go. A rare paleontological site, to be combined with the museum.
