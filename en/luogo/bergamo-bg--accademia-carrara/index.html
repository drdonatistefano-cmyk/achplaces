Accademia Carrara

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Founded in 1796 on the initiative of Count Giacomo Carrara, it houses more than 600 masterpieces by Botticelli, Raffaello, Tiziano, Lotto, Moroni and Canaletto, in a five-century itinerary of Italian painting.

Why go. One of the great art galleries in Italy.

  • Where Piazza Giacomo Carrara 82, Bergamo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.lacarrara.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
