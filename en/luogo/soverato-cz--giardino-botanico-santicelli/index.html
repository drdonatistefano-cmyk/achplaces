Giardino botanico Santicelli

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Mediterranean maquis garden with over a thousand species of Mediterranean and exotic plants, some at risk of extinction, and view over the entire gulf. On the plateau called "Chianu d'a Batteria" there are four artillery stations.

Why go. A thousand species of plants with the gulf at their feet.
