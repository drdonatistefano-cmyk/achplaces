Museo Nazionale di Villa Guinigi

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the fifteenth-century villa of the Guinigi the history of Lucca among Etruscan finds, medieval sculptures and Renaissance paintings.

Why go. The history of the city in a museum only.
