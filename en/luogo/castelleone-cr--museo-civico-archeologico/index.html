Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

It is located in via Roma in the eighteenth century Palazzo Brunenghi-Salvago, together with the civic library, and documents the human presence in the territory from the Mesolithic to the Early Middle Ages.

Why go. For the prehistory and archaeology of Cremasco.
