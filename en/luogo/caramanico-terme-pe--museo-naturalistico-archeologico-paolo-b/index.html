Museo naturalistico archeologico "Paolo Barrasso" e area della lontra

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Founded in 1986 by the State Forestry Corps, it reconstructs the habitats of Majella (beech, rocky walls, mugheta), with sections of geology and fossils, the path "Tana della Lontra" and an archaeological section with finds from the Paleolithic below the Roman age, accessible to blind and visually impaired. Next to it is the fauna area of the Eurasian otter; it is also information center of the Park.

Why go. The center visits to understand the Majella before walking there.

  • Where Via del Vivaio, frazione Santa Croce, Caramanico Terme
  • Entry €2,70, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
