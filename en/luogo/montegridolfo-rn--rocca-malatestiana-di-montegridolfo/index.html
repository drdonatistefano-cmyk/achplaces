Rocca malatestiana di Montegridolfo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The fourteenth-century fortress wanted by Galeotto Malatesta, the heart of the walled village in a panoramic position between the valleys of the Foglia and the Conca.

Why go. One of the most picturesque villages in Italy.
