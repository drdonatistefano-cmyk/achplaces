Tonnara di Su Pranu e chiesa di Sant'Antonio da Padova

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Tennara was built in the middle of the 17th century next to the tower, still active every year between May and June for the mornings; inside is the church of Sant'Antonio da Padova.

Why go. One of the historic traps still in operation in Sardinia.
