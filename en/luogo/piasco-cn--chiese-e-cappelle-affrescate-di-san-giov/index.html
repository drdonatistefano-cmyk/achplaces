Chiese e cappelle affrescate di San Giovanni, Sant'Anna e San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

During the golden age of the marquisate of Saluzzo, the painter Pietro da Saluzzo, called Maestro del Villar, painted the apse of the church of San Giovanni and the ancient pylon in front of it (frescoes of the pylon of the XV century) and also worked in the chapel of Sant'Anna. The chapel of San Rocco conserves frescoes from 1534; the chapel of Santa Brigida, with Romanesque apse, was frescoed in Saluzzo by unknown artists.

Why go. Late frescoes by the Maestro del Villar in the chapels of the village.
