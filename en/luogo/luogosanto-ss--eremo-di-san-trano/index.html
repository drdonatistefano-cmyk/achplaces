Eremo di San Trano

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Small chapel of the 13th century built on a large granite mass, where according to tradition in 1227 two Franciscan friars found the bodies of the hermits Nicola and Trano. The cave in which the saints lived is embedded in the wall and acts as a natural apse above the altar, made from a single block of granite; a single nave, granite floor and a roof with exposed beams. On clear days you can see the mouths of Bonifacio and Corsica; at the beginning of June the saints are celebrated with an evening procession.

Why go. A hermitage dug into the rock, with a view to Corsica.
