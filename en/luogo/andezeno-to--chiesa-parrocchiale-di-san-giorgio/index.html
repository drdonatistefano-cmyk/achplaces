Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Designed by Giovan Battista Casasopra, pupil of Vittone, built in 1753-1759 and consecrated in 1764: baroque, with a single nave with three chapels connecting on the side and brick facade marked by pilasters and columns. Inside the relics of Santa Giustina on the main altar, frescoes of the vault of Luigi and Rodolfo Morgari (1853 and 1858), organ of 1866 and altarpieces attributed to Antonio Sariga and workshop.

Why go. The Piedmontese baroque from Vittone's school.
