Boschi del Monte Catria

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Monte Catria (1,701 m) overlooks the hermitage of Fonte Avellana, surrounded by holm oaks, oaks and hazelnuts; the monastery is the starting point for walks in the woods of Catria between beech and chestnut trees, on panoramic paths.

Why go. Walk in the woods around the hermitage with a view of the Apennines.
