Cappella di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the center of the village, built in 1300 with the offers of the population and decorated in the eighteenth century with shovels of the Turinese painter Rebaudengo; today it houses a library.

Why go. A 14th-century chapel became a library.
