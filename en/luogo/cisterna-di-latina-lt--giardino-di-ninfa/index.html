Giardino di Ninfa

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

Eight hectares with about 1,300 plant species, a pond and streams among the ruins of the medieval town of Ninfa: castle, walls, churches and a restored town hall. Ninfa, bought by the Caetans in 1298, was destroyed under Barbarossa and again in 1382 and then abandoned; since 1921 Gelasio, Roffredo Caetani and Marguerite Chapin transformed it into a romantic garden, to which Lelia Caetani gave the present form. Regional natural monument since 2000, is managed by the Caetani Foundation; you can visit with guided tours from March to November according to annual calendar, with mandatory booking on gardendininfa.eu (save 10% for FAI subscribers).

Why go. One of the most romantic gardens in the world, at most in April and May.
