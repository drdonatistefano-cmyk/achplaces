Castello-Convento e chiesa di Santa Maria degli Angeli

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the top of the hill that dominates the village, it was the defensive center of the Brescian bank of Oglio, disputed between Brescians and Bergamoschi and then between Visconti and Venice; in 1265 it was besieged and conquered by Roberto di Flanders on behalf of Carlo d'Angiò. In 1610, in 1692 it became a female convent by initiative of Chiara Foscarini Duolo and the parish priest Francesco Lantieri de Paratico, with the church of Santa Maria degli Angeli consecrated two years later and reopened in 1835 with the arrival of the Orsolines. From its remains you can enjoy the view of Franciacorta and the Oglio valley.

Why go. The hill of the castle, between medieval history and panorama of Franciacorta.
