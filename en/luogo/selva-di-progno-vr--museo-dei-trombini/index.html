Museo dei Trombini

Museums · Themed

In the old church of San Bortolo is exhibited the collection of trombini, heavy firearms derived from the archibugi of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, born as weapons of defense and become a festive instrument for celebrations, commemorations and festivals; St. Bortolo keeps alive the tradition of shooting. Ancient specimens, decorated and carved, with films and projections. Open from 1 April to 30 September on Sundays and holidays 10-12 and 15-18.30; from October to March and in weekdays only by appointment for groups of at least 10 people.

Why go. A unique tradition of festive shots fired in Lessinia.
