Mulino di Pontevecchio e Museo della Mezzadria

Museums · Themed

Mill on the Foglia known since the sixteenth century, belonged to the Dukes of Urbino and then to the counts Paciotti, who for five centuries macinò wheat and from the twentieth century produced electricity; there were wounded in 1862 two brigands of the Banda Grossi. Recovered since 2002, it hosts a new hydroelectric power station and the Mezzadria Museum since 2018: thirteen rooms of objects of rural culture donated by Adriano Campanari, from the kitchen to the cellar with 18th century press, a 1888 birock and historical turbines. Open Tuesday 15-18 and Saturday 9-12.

Why go. Five centuries of mill and the life of sharecroppers.
