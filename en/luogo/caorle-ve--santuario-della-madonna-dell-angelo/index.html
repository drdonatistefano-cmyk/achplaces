Santuario della Madonna dell'Angelo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

On the promontory at the bottom of the cliff, at the beginning of the beach of Levante, it is probably the oldest religious building in Caorle, built on remains of the 6th - 7th century where there was a wooden church with three naves dedicated to St. Michael the Archangel. The current building, with a single nave with a palladian pronao style, is from 1751, wanted by Bishop Francesco Trevisan Suarez; the legend tells of fishermen who found a wooden statue of the Madonna and Child in the sea on a marble pedestal.

Why go. The fishermen's church overlooking the Adriatic.
