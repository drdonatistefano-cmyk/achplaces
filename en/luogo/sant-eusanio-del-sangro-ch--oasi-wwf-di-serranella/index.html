Oasi WWF di Serranella

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Artificial lake born at the confluence of the rivers Sangro and Aventino, important rest area for migratory birds; the oasis is managed together with the municipalities of Altino and Casoli and can be reached from the district Brecciaio towards Casoli, near the district Castellata-Tori.

Why go. Birdwatching on the lake at the confluence of Sangro and Aventino.
