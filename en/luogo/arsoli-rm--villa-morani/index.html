Villa Morani

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Among the historic residences of Lazio, built around 1880 near the station and purchased in 1902 by the painter Alessandro Morani and his wife Lili Helbig; from the window of the studio you can see the landscape that the artist painted. He hosted D'Annunzio, Pascarella and the artists of "In Arte Libertas" .

Why go. The artist's house of Alessandro Morani.
