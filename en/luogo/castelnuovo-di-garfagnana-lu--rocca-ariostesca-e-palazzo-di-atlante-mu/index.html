Rocca Ariostesca e Palazzo di Atlante (Museo Furioso)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Of trapezoidal plan, it dominates Piazza Umberto I: on a small fortress of the tenth century, the structure of the twelfth century was enlarged in the thirteenth century, the central tower with the civic clock is owed to Paolo Guinigi and in 1675 the terrace on the square and the monumental arch were added. Ludovico Ariosto resided there from 1522 to 1525 as governor of the extended province; municipal seat until the war, it was severely bombed in 1944 and then restored. It houses the Palazzo di Atlante, a museum dedicated to Ariosto that combines contemporary art and traditions of Garfagnana, with a multimedia installation in the bell tower on the first two episodes of the Orlando Furioso, Follia d'Orlando and Â"Astolfo sulla Luna" .

Why go. The Fortress of Ariosto and the Orlando Furious multimedia.
