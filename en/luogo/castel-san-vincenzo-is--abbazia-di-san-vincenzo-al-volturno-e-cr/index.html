Abbazia di San Vincenzo al Volturno e Cripta di Epifanio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Benedictine Abbey founded in 703 by three Beneventan princes in a rustic villa abandoned at the springs of the Volturno; supported by Charlemagne, it came to drive a network of more than 130 monasteries. The excavations have returned an archaeological park of 11,000 m2 with evidences of the monastic life and the Crypt of Epifanius, rich in frescoes, discovered by a farmer while working the camp; today the monastic house was reopened by a community of nuns from the United States.

Why go. One of the most important archaeological finds in Europe, with the crypt frescoed in the early Middle Ages.
