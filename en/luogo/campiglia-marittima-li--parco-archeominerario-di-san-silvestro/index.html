Parco archeominerario di San Silvestro

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the hills north of the village, 450 hectares of the mining district exploited from the Etruscan era until 1976: the galleries of the Temperino Mine, the Lanzi-Temperino tunnel by mining train and the medieval village of Rocca San Silvestro, a village of miners and founders of the Della Gherardesca.

Why go. A trip into the mine.
