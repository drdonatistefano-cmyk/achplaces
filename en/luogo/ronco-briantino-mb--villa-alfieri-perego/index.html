Villa Alfieri Perego

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Late Baroque residence commissioned by the canon Giovanni Perego, humanist and art lover, as a place of holiday and family representation. It is located along the cycle path that crosses the square of Ronco Briantino and continues in the Park of Rio Molgora towards Carnate.

Why go. A late baroque villa of delight along the bike path of the Brianza parks.
