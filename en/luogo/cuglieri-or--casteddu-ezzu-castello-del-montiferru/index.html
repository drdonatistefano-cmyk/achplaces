Casteddu Ezzu (Castello del Montiferru)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On a basaltic hill just over 3 km from the village, along the road to Macomer: a court castle built by Ittcorra, brother of Judge Barison II, attested in 1196. In 1668 Countess Francesca Zatrillas took refuge before fleeing from Sardinia; shortly afterwards he was abandoned. There are still traces of the perimeter wall, watchtowers and underground, and from the top the view ranges to 360 degrees to the coast.

Why go. Ruins with legend and panorama of Montiferru.
