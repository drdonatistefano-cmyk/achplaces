Palazzo Pretorio e Museo del Minerale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The highest building in the centre, the ancient seat of political and military power, with the clock tower, houses the historical archive, the Carlo Guarnieri Museum and the Museum of the Mineral of the Toscano Mineral Circle.

Why go. The minerals of the hills in a medieval palace.
