Torre Civica

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

64 meters high, it is one of the symbols of the city; from the terrace the panorama goes from the Sibillini Mountains to the Adriatic Sea.

Why go. From the Sibillini to the sea at a glance.
