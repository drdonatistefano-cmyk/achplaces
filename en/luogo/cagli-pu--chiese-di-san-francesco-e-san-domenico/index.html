Chiese di San Francesco e San Domenico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Two churches of medieval origin with internal hall enriched with paintings and frescoes; San Domenico is rich in frescoes. In Piazza San Francesco the bronze bust of Angelo Celli by the sculptor Biancini, located in 1959.

Why go. Paintings and frescoes in two medieval churches.
