Giardino Botanico Alpino

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Designed in 1972, it gathers about 500 species of plants of the group Cansiglio-Cavallo ordered for environments on about one and a half hectare, with a collection of medicinal plants.

Why go. The flora of the Cansiglio reunited in one path.
