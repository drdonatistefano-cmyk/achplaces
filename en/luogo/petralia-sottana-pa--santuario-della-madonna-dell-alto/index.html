Santuario della Madonna dell'Alto

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

On the summit of the same name, over 1,000 meters, it houses an effigy of the Madonna considered miraculous, carved by Domenico Gagini in 1471.

Why go. A shrine at the top with the Madonna dei Gagini.
