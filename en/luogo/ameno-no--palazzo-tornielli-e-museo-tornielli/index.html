Palazzo Tornielli e Museo Tornielli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The palace of the noble family Tornielli, which held receptions, concerts and dances, combines parts of different eras with two internal courtyards, a staircase of honour and mythological frescoes. Today it houses the Town Hall and the Museum Tornielli, opened in 2012, with a permanent collection of site-specific contemporary art, temporary exhibitions, conferences and cultural events.

Why go. A frescoed noble palace that houses contemporary art.
