Santuario di Santa Maria della Ginestra

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Place of worship very well known, reached by the Via della Ginestra, route between country roads, vineyards, cypresses and villas (about 7.5 km, 2 hours and a half). Next to the sanctuary was found an ancient Etruscan well.

Why go. A destination for a walk between vineyards and cypresses.
