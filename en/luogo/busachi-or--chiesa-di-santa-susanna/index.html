Chiesa di Santa Susanna

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Built in 1349 in Gothic style on the remains of a pagan temple, it preserves a Byzantine holy waterworks; in April and August, during the novenas, the faithful inhabit the ancient cumbessias around the church.

Why go. 14th-century gothic and novena cumbessias.
