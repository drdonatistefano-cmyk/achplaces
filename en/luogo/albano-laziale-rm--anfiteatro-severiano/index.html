Anfiteatro Severiano

History and architecture · Archaeology

Built by the workers of the legion in the early third century, with an axis greater than 113 m and an estimated capacity between 15,000 and 16,000 spectators; in the Middle Ages it was used as a quarry and Christian cemetery. It is located behind the church of São Paulo.

Why go. The arena of the legionaries of Septimius Severus.
