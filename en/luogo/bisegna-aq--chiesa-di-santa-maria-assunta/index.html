Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Recently restored parish church, with elements of seventeenth-century and eighteenth-century character; the feast of Saint Mary Assunta is celebrated on 15 August.

Why go. The mother church of the village.
