Museo Parrocchiale di Archeologia e Arte sacra «Don Fiore Borrelli»

Museums · Themed

Wanted by the parish priest Don Fiore Borrelli, who designed it since 1952, opened in 1997 in two rooms of the Mother Church of Santa Maria della Motta and, after a restoration, reopened in summer 2007. The collections cover more than 4,000 years, from the Bronze Age to today: archaeological section, the rebuilt chapel of the old Mother Church, a picture gallery with the Madonna del Carmelo by Nicola Troisi (1690), sculptures of the seventeenth-seventeenth century, vestments and liturgical furnishings of the confraternities (XVI-XIX century) and the fourteenth-century coat of arms of Filippo Sangineto.

Why go. Four millennia of history of the Esaro Valley in a village museum.

open only by reservation (tel. +39 340 665 9975)
