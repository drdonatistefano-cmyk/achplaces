Abbazia di Sant'Angelo in Piano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Romanesque church about 2 km from the Fortress of Montevarmine, next to which in the first half of the 11th century arose a Benedictine monastery today disappeared; from the 14th century it was a destination of pilgrimages for the indulgences granted by the bishops of Fermo and strategic point on the road between Ascoli, Fermo and the Aso valley.

Why go. Romanesque countryside along an ancient pilgrim's street.
