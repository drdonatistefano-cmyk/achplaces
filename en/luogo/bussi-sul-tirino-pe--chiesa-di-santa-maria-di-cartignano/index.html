Chiesa di Santa Maria di Cartignano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Benedictine church with three naves with semicircular apse and sailing bell tower, documented since 1021: it was a cell of Montecassino, grange of San Liberatore at Maiella and then of the Celestines. It is known for its shape, without the central roof, with the ruins restored; the thirteenth-century frescoes of Armanino da Modena, detached in the sixties, are at the National Museum of Eagle. Church is tratural along the paths of transhumance, it is a national monument since 1902. Outside the town, visible from the state; the MiC card does not record usable times.

Why go. An open-air medieval church on the route of the tracts.
