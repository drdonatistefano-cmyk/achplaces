Museo della Grande Guerra di Ragogna

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 2007 and curated by historian Marco Pascoli, with bilingual Italian-German imprint, it is dedicated to the battle of Ragogna after Caporetto: panels and vintage images, a large plastic of the battlefield with trenches and sides at 31 October 1917, objects recovered on battlefields, a section on Risorgimento and Second World War and a multimedia room. Open Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 15.30-18 according to the Municipality.

Why go. Understand the battle of the Tagliamento of 1917.
