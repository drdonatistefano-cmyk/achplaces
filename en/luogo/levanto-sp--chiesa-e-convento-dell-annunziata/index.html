Chiesa e Convento dell'Annunziata

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Franciscan complex of the fifteenth century isolated on a hill, with works by Pier Francesco Sacchi and Bernardo Strozzi and the former refectory with paintings by Gian Battista Casoni.

Why go. Strozzi and Sacchi in the Franciscan monastery.
