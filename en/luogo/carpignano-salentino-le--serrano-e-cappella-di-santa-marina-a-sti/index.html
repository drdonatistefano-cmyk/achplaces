Serrano e cappella di Santa Marina a Stigliano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Serrano the parish church of San Giorgio Martire, probably fourteenth century and rebuilt in the second nineteenth century, the monument to the fallen with San Giorgio on horseback by Francesco Oronzo De Vitis and Palazzo Lubelli, residence of the barons; in Stigliano the chapel with cross of Santa Marina, of 1762, with below frescoed rock crypt.

Why go. The fractions and a hidden frescoed crypt.
