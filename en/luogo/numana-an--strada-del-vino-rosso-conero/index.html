Strada del vino Rosso Conero

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Itinerary that connects Ancona and Osimo passing through Numana, Sirolo, Camerano, Offagna and Castelfidardo; about twenty wineries produce Rosso Conero DOC and Rosso Conero Riserva DOCG from Montepulciano and Sangiovese grapes.

Why go. The cellars of Rosso Conero a stone's throw from the sea.
