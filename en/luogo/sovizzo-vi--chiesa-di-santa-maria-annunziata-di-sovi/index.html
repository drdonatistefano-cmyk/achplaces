Chiesa di Santa Maria Annunziata di Sovizzo Colle

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Archpriest's church also called Santa Maria del Colle, which tradition connects to an ancient temple dedicated to Diana; a Roman inscription dedicated to the goddess is walled in the building. It preserves works of the seventeenth century, including sculptures by Orazio Marinali and 15 copper tiles by Francesco Maffei.

Why go. Quality Vicenza baroque in a hill church.
