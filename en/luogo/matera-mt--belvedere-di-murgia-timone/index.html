Belvedere di Murgia Timone

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Panoramic point on the Murgia, beyond the Gravina, from which you can see the Sassi in front.

Why go. The rocks on the other side.
