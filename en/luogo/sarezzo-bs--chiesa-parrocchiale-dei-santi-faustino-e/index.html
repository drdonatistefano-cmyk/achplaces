Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Faustino e Giovita

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1633 on medieval structures and consecrated in 1652, on a project by Giovanni Battista Lantana, author in 1604 of the Duomo Nuovo di Brescia. Classic façade with four columns and tympanum, and statues of the saints holding in two niches. Inside the altarpiece of the Madonna with Child and saints of the Morettian school in a frame of Carlo Dossena, carvings of the Pialorsi called Boscaì di Levrange in the presbytery, altarpieces by Antonio Gandino and Francesco Paglia on the side altars and a sixteenth-century wooden crucifix by Maffeo Olivieri on the holy arch.

Why go. Six hundred Brescia, with the crucifix by Maffeo Olivieri.
