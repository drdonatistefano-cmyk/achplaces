Abbazia dei Santi Fidenzio e Terenzio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Abbey of the IX-X century, outside the historic center, with crypt; it was run by the Benedictines until the end of the fourteenth century.

Why go. A medieval abbey with crypt.
