Museo diocesano d'arte sacra

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Opened in 2009 at the initiative of Mgr. Antonino Orrù on the second and third floors of the former Tridentino Seminary, 19th century building: works from the cathedral and other parishes of the diocese, including four bells from the 16th to the 18th century (the oldest cast in 1573 by Pietro Tolo) and two stone lions from the 17th century. Open on midweek holidays; from Monday to Thursday by reservation.

Why go. The treasure of the cathedral and the ancient bells.

  • Where Piazza Cattedrale 1, Ales
  • Entry €3, reduced €1,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.sardegnaturismo.it
