Museo della civiltà rupestre e contadina

Museums · Themed

Opened in 2003 in a former mill near the rocky settlement, it gathers about 3,000 objects of the peasant and rocky life of Zungri, in sections dedicated to the home, to the work of the fields, to traditional crafts, to the weaving and production of wine; a room is furnished as a vintage bedroom, with devotional images, daily men's and women's clothes and wedding costumes.

Why go. The daily life of those who lived in caves and fields.
