Santuario della Madonna del Pozzo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

On the hill Serralonga, about three kilometers from the village: according to tradition in 1709 Our Lady appeared to the farmer Giuseppe Mastrostefano and caused a spring to spring. On August 15th the eighteenth-century statue is taken in procession to the village; the main feasts are August 24th and August 25th and on 26th the statue returns to the sanctuary.

Why go. The shrine of the August feast.
