Chiesa di Santa Maria dei Raccomandati

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church at the beginning of the village, completed in 1820 with baroque shapes, facade in white stone with central window and unique nave. Inside two canvases by Teofilo Patini, the ...Miracle of the Cross and ...Carlo Borromeo with a moribund.

Why go. Two canvases by Teofilo Patini.
