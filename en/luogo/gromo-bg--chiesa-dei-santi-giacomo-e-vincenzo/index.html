Chiesa dei Santi Giacomo e Vincenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The most important church of the village, outside the historic center: austere exterior with local stone porch and classic portal in white marble, inside with 16th century frescoes, paintings, wooden and marble statues, baptismal font of 1511, golden altar and seventeenth century reliquaries behind doors of golden copper.

Why go. The true jewel of Gromo.
