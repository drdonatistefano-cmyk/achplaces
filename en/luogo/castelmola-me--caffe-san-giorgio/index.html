Caffè San Giorgio

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Historic bar-tavern founded by the monks in the eighteenth century, considered the place of origin of the almond wine, probably invented by the owner Don Vincenzo Blandano; it preserves an album of signatures of illustrious visitors since 1907.

Why go. The almond wine where he was born.
