Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

On the ground floor of the ducal castle, in three rooms, exhibits more than 800 state-owned finds from 50 tombs of the first and second Iron Age (late IX-VII century BC) carved on the hill of Cemetery Old, especially ceramics and personal ornaments; a 3D video reconstructs the protohistoric settlement. Open from Tuesday to Sunday, 11-13 and 17-19.
