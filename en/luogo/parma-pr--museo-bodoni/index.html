Museo Bodoni

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Punches, plates and books by Giambattista Bodoni, the court printer who invented one of the most used characters in the world.

Why go. The Bodoni character where he was born.

  • Where Piazza della Pilotta, Parma
  • Entry Included in the Pilot's ticket: €18 whole
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website complessopilotta.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
