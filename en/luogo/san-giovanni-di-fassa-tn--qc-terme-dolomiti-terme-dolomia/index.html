QC Terme Dolomiti (Terme Dolomia)

Local life and traditions · Spas

Powered by the sulfur source of Alloch, known since the late fifteenth century and the only of this type in Trentino, which flows about 1,320 meters in the Monzoni group at 9.5 °C; the modern spa opened in 2003, with conventional care. The wellness area on more than 4,000 m2 and three levels has Japanese bathrooms, saunas, rooms of the rooms, outdoor dining rooms, Kneipp path and swimming pools.

Why go. The only sulphur water in Trentino.
