Museo delle Genti delle Valli di Lanzo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

It rediscovers traditions and ancient crafts with everyday objects and work tools, rebuilding scenes of life disappeared with old maps and vintage photographs. The route is divided into themes: the Lanzo Valleys, the people, agriculture and sheep farming, handicrafts, flora, fauna and mineralogy; curious the collection of nails, once forged in the valley and exported throughout Italy. From May to October the first and third Sundays of the month, 15-18; in the other periods by telephone reservation (333 4825771). Accessible to the disabled.

Why go. The museum that tells the life of the Lanzo Valleys.

  • Where Piazza Municipio 12, Ceres
  • Entry €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.ceres.to.it
