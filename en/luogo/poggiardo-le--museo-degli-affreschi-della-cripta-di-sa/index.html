Museo degli affreschi della Cripta di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated on June 12, 1975 with Aldo Moro, in the Villa municipale exhibits the original frescoes of the crypt, detached and restored at the Central Institute of Restoration in Rome and mounted in the original layout, with the reconstruction of the plant: among these the Madonna and Child between the archangels Gabriel and Michael (first half of the XII century), St George on horseback, Christ blessing on throne, the saints Cosma and Damiano and the Archangel Michael.

Why go. Byzantine frescoes in Salento to see up close.
