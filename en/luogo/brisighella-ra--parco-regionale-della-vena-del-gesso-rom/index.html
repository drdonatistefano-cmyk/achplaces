Parco regionale della Vena del Gesso Romagnola

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

A 25 km long chalk dorsal with caves and dolines, part of the UNESCO site on karst in the evaporites.

Why go. Panoramic trails, including the ascent to Monte Spugi with the giant bench.

  • Where Tra Brisighella, Riolo Terme, Casola Valsenio e Borgo Tossignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
