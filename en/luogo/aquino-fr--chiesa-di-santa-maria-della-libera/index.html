Chiesa di Santa Maria della Libera

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church, national monument and among the historic residences of Lazio, built on the ruins of the Temple of Hercules Liberatore; for the municipality is the most remarkable of the buildings of the post-Roman age and example of medieval art. In its garden there is the Arc de Marcantonio.

Why go. Romanesque on the ruins of a temple.
