Teatro Giovan Battista Pergolesi

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built between 1790 and 1798 by Francesco Maria Ciaraffoni, modified by Cosimo Morelli and inaugurated in 1798; known for acoustics and rococo decorations, it has the historical curtain of Luigi Mancini (1850) and hosts every year the Festival Pergolesi Spontini.

Why go. The theatre dedicated to the composer Jesino.
