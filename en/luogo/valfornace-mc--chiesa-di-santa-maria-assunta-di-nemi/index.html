Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta di Nemi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church isolated on the hill of Nemi, mentioned already in 1218 among the appliances of the Pieve di Bovigliano; it preserves a series of images of Paolo da Visso of 1447 and a painting of the Boccati with the Madonna crowned with the Child between the Saints Matteo and Macario.

Why go. Paolo da Visso and Boccati in a country church.
