Chiesa e Monastero di San Nicola

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The church is located on the site of a more ancient, of which remains a walled inscription at the entrance of the convent; the current building bears witness to the passage from the Byzantine to the Norman dominion. Inside stand out the apse, very well preserved, and the work of local chisels, with columns, pilasters and blind arches made from unique blocks.

Why go. The stone worked between Byzantines and Normans.
