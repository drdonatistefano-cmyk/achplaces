Museo Regionale della Ceramica

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 1965, the Sicilian ceramics from prehistory to the early twentieth century, from Greek and Roman artifacts to medieval, Baroque and nineteenth-century works; among the main pieces a crater of the fifth century BC with a workshop of ceramist and Athena Ergane.

Why go. The history of Sicilian ceramics.

  • Where Via Giardini Pubblici, Caltagirone
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
