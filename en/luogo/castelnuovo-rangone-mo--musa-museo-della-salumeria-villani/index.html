MuSa - Museo della Salumeria Villani

Museums · Themed

The first Italian museum dedicated to salami, with a multimedia exhibition next to the historic headquarters of Villani, founded in 1886 and considered the oldest salami factory in Emilia-Romagna.

Why go. The story of salami where they have been for 140 years.
