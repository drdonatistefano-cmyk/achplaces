Chiesa di San Gennaro e Museo di arte sacra

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Baroque church with elliptical dome covered with majolica hembrics; it houses the sacred art section of the Ceramic Museum, with a calendar of reliquary cesellato that houses relics of various saints and a fragment of the Cross.

Why go. Majolica dome and sacred art.
