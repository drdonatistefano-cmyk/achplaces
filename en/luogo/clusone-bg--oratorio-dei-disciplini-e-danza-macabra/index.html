Oratorio dei Disciplini e Danza Macabra

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the fourteenth century by the Confraternita dei Disciplini Bianchi, enlarged in the fifteenth century and dedicated to Saint Bernardino in 1452. On the east façade the frescoes of 1485 by Giacomo Borlone de Buschis: at the top the Triumph of Death with the encounter of the three living and the three dead, at the center the Dance Macabra with people of every class led by skeletons. Inside the Stories of Jesus in 42 squares on three bands (31 visible) and a Crucifixion over the arch of the presbytery.

Why go. Triumph and Dance of Death, unique in Europe.
