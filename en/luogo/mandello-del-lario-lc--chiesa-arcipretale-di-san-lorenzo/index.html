Chiesa arcipretale di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The present building dates back to the 17th century and replaced a previous church; the Romanesque bell tower, of the 12th century, was one of the defensive towers of the castrum. Inside the 17th century baroque decoration with carved ciborium and canopy altar in polychrome wood, and an organ Serassi of 1764 sculpted and gilded; in front stands the ancient Chapel of the Octagonal Dead.

Why go. A baroque interior with the organ Serassi a few steps from the lake.
