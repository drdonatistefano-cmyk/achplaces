Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Cited from 1208, rebuilt in late dogotic style by master Benedikt Weibhauser of Bressanone and consecrated in 1494; inside a folding illustrates its history and works.

Why go. The Gothic in the heart of the city.
