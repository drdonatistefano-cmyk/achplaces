Museo di arte sacra

Museums · Themed

Parish Museum in the hamlet of Alvignanello that preserves statues and votive gold, ex-vote, liturgical objects, sacred vestments presented in their historical evolution, paintings linked to religious tradition and ceramic fragments from local excavations, together with findings donated already studied by archaeologists. It addresses educational activities in particular to young people; contacts: 0823 863388, 339 3469741.

Why go. Alvignanello's devotional treasure, from ex-vote to vestments.
