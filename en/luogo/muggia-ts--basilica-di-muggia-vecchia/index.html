Basilica di Muggia Vecchia

Places of worship · Basilicas · Viewpoint

Romanesque basilica of early medieval origin on the promontory above the port, with the remains of the walls and the protohistoric casteliers of Muggia Vecchia and Santa Barbara; wide panorama of the Gulf of Trieste.

Why go. The basilica on the gulf.
