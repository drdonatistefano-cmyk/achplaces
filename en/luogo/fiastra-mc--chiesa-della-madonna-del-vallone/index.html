Chiesa della Madonna del Vallone

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the locality Campicino, it has seven monumental baroque altars and paintings including a 17th century Deposition of the Roman Caravaggio school and a Madonna with Child of the 16th century.

Why go. Baroque altars and a Caravaggio deposition.

after the earthquake of 2016 the monuments are partially usable; the card of the Park describes the state prior to the earthquake
