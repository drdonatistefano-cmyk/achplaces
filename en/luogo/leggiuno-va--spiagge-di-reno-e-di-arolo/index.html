Spiagge di Reno e di Arolo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

The beach of Reno, sand and gravel, looks at the Borromeo Gulf and the Borromee Islands and has showers and picnic areas. Arolo's is a meadow with trees that give natural shade, clear water and panoramic view, with bars, showers and toilets and parking spaces along the lake and in the village; you swim there and dive from the nearby cliffs.

Why go. Bathrooms in Lake Maggiore with views of the Borromean Islands.
