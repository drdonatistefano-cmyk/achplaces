Museo archeologico di Santa Scolastica

Museums · Archaeological

In the ancient monastery of Santa Scolastica, in the historical and archaeological core of the old town, the archaeological museum with more than 30,000 finds and a medieval church with necropolis.

Why go. Three thousand years of Bari history.
