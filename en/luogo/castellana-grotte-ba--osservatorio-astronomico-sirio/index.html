Osservatorio astronomico Sirio

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Born in 2000 on proposal of prof. Nicola Rizzi, is located in the upper part of the building of the Museo Anelli and is managed by the Astronomical Cultural Association Sirio. The evenings Â One night with Sirio Â Â , in summer, combine an introduction to astronomy with minerals and meteorites in the museum's teaching room and guided observation of the sky to the telescope from the terrace; there are proposals for families (€10 a child with an adult) and special events for full moons and eclipses.

Why go. An evening under the stars above the caves.
