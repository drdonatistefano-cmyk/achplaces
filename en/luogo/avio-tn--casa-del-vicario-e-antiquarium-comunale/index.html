Casa del Vicario e Antiquarium comunale

Museums · Archaeological

Building overlooking the medieval route that connects the Pieve to the Castle; it houses the Antiquarium municipal with Roman finds, including the cippi that marked the Via Claudia Augusta.

Why go. The cippi of the Claudia Augusta street.
