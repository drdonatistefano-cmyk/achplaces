Casa degli Orsini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Residence of the fourteenth century, the smallest of the three Orsini properties in the village, with an internal cloister in common with the Oratory of the Most Holy Stimmates; above the entrance the rose of the Orsini, inside a more elaborate coat of arms above the door of the loggia, travertine windows with stone seats and, in the basement, a preserved stone mill.

Why go. The mill under the house Orsini.
