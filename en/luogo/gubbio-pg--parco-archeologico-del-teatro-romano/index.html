Parco archeologico del Teatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Theatre completed around 20 BC by the magistrate Gneo Satrio Rufo, capable of about 6,000 spectators, in the late-republish district of Guastuglia; the lower order of arches and some arches of the upper gallery remain standing, with the Antiquarium.

Why go. The Roman Gubbio of Iguvium.

  • Where Via del Teatro Romano 1, Gubbio
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
