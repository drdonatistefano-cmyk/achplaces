Grotte di Villanova

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

The karst complex of four large cavities with more than 20 km of development; the Cave Nuova, discovered in 1925 by inhabitants who noticed a slit from which came wet air, has almost 9 km of development and is among the most extensive in the world because formed to the contact between two types of rock. The guided tourist route, illuminated and with walkways, crosses galleries, halls and canyons with stalactites and stalagmites to the Corner of the Crystals and the Hall of the Gran Portone with its natural arch (about 1 hour and 15 minutes, 11 °C constant); on reservation easy speleological excursions of 3 or 5 hours. It is on the regional list of tourist caves.

Why go. The longest cave in Friuli, with an underground natural arch.
