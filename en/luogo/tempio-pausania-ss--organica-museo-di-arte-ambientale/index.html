Organica, museo di arte ambientale

Museums · Modern art

In the woods of Curadureddu sul Limbara, between holm oaks and cork trees about 7 km from Tempio along the SS 392: works installed outdoors and the CEDAP Space, a former troticulture transformed by the architect Antonello Menicucci in two exhibition rooms, near the pools of the Rio Pisciaroni.

Why go. Contemporary art in the woods.

exhibitions from May to December (edition 2026 from 24 May to 31 December)
