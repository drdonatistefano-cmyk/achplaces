Palazzo della Signoria e Biblioteca Planettiana

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

Renaissance brick palace built between 1486 and 1498 on a project by Francesco di Giorgio Martini, with decorations by Michele di Giovanni da Milano and his son Alvise; the tower, designed by Andrea Sansovino, collapsed with the earthquake of 1657. It houses the Planetian Library, founded in 1859, with about 110,000 volumes, manuscripts and incunabula, including an autograph letter by Giacomo Leopardi from 1825; guided tours to the Antique Funds Rooms on Thursday at 11 am and at 12 noon.

Why go. The palace of Francesco di Giorgio Martini.

  • Where Piazza Angelo Colocci, Jesi
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
