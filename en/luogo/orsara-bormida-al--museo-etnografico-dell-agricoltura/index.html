Museo etnografico dell'agricoltura

Museums · Themed

Agricultural tools, furnishings and utensils of a 19th century peasant house, linens, alarm clocks collections, purses, mantillas and Borsalino hats, works by local artists, a rebuilt school classroom and documents on emigration and the Great War. Curated by the association Ursaria Amici of the Museum.

Why go. The peasant civilization of Monferrato.
