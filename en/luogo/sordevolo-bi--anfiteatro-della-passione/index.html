Anfiteatro della Passione

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Outdoor amphitheatre of about 4,000 square meters whose setting reconstructs Palestine: Cenacle, Sanhedrin, Gethsemane, Herod's Palace, Pretorio di Pilate and Calvary. Here more than 400 inhabitants stage the Passion, documented since 1816 and interrupted only by world wars; the 29th edition runs from 19 June to 26 September 2027, with up to 3,000 spectators per replica. You can also visit behind the scenes.

Why go. The stage of the Passion, in the year of the new edition.
