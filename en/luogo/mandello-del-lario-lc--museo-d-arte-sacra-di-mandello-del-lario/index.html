Museo d'Arte Sacra di Mandello del Lario

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated on December 7, 2019 next to San Lorenzo, in the ancient headquarters of the confraternity of the regulations of Saint Martha, it makes the heritage of the archpriest accessible. It exhibits a fresco of the Crucifixion attributed to a Lombard painter between the late fifteenth and early sixteenth centuries, the fifteenth-century cross astile (the most representative work), a Madonna with Child sculpted from the fifteenth century, the canvas of the Circumcision by Francesco Zucco, liturgical wallpapers and ex-vote painted by the church of Santa Maria Nascente di Debbio.

Why go. The 15th century cross and the former vows of the lake.
