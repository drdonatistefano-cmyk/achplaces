Chiesa matrice di San Brizio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The church of the patron saint, of Greek imprint, preserves altars and Renaissance and Baroque paintings, including a Madonna of Mercy with an unprecedented Madonna pregnant. Originally two naves, it was rebuilt with a single nave at the end of the seventeenth century; hatches give access to underground burials.

Why go. Baroque altars and a rare pregnant Madonna.
