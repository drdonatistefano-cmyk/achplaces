Museo Enzo Ferrari

Local life and traditions · Motors · Paid entry

The museum in Enzo Ferrari's birthplace, with the yellow hooded gallery showing racing and road cars and telling the life of the founder.

Why go. The Ferrari myth where it started.

  • Where Via Paolo Ferrari 85, Modena
  • Entry €27 in low season, €32 in high season; €38-42 with the Ferrari Museum in Maranello
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
