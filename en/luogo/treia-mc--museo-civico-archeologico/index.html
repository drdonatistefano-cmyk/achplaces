Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

In the former convent of St. Francis, it collects fragments of sculptures and inscriptions from the Picena to the Roman age, with strong ties with Egypt: two Egyptian sculptures (a female deity and a head of Serapide) found at the Sanctuary of the SS. Crucifix, fragments from the Serapean and Roman statuary from the age of Augustus to that of Hadrian and Antoninus.

Why go. Egyptian artifacts in the Marches.
