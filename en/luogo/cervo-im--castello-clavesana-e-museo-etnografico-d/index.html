Castello Clavesana e Museo etnografico del Ponente ligure

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Castle of the 12th century strengthened by three circular towers; it houses the museum dedicated to work, crafts and sea life of the 19th century and the permanent exhibition on Ligurian woman 1850-1950.

Why go. The castle and the memory of the village.
