Museo paleontologico e preistorico

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 1998 and titled Don A. Blessed, he tells the story of Western Lessinia in two rooms. The prehistory section exhibits instruments from the Paleolithic lower than the age of metals, many from the Cave of Fumane and from the Riparo Tagliente of Stallavena: flints, engraved bones, painted stones, the cast of a burial of the upper Paleolithic, the "Sciamano" and a skeleton of bison. The section of paleontology shows marine fossils of the Stone quarries of Prun, including curls, ammonites and large sharks, in particular a cretodus of about 2 meters preserved with a sea turtle, perhaps his last meal: it is the most complete fossil known of this shark. Ground floor accessible by wheelchair; open from July to October Friday, Saturday, Sunday and holidays 9.30-18, the rest of the year by reservation.

Why go. The most complete fossil shark of its kind and the prehistory of Lessinia.
