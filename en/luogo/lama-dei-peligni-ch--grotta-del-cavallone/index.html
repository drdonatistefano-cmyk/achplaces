Grotta del Cavallone

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

karst cave over 2 km long, with 15 meters high entrance at 1,475 meters above sea level: among the highest visitable caves in Europe, explored since 1704 and open to tourists since 1893; 1,360 meters, with salt such as the enchanted Forest, the Hall of the Elephants, the lakes of Splendore and Ornella and the Pantheon. In 1904 Francesco Paolo Michetti took it as a model for the scene of the second act of the "Daughter of Iorio" by D'Annunzio. It is one of the geosites of the UNESCO Geopark of Majella; climbing with the Colle Rotondo-Cavallone cable car, reopened in 2022, or on foot in about two hours; guided tours in the summer months.

Why go. D'Annunzio underground stage.
