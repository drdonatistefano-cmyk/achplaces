Museo di storia naturale «Antonio Stoppani»

Museums · Science and university

Set up in the Seminary in 1930 with fossils collected by Antonio Stoppani, geological collections of Giuseppe Mercalli and shells, titled to Stoppani in 1952: 104 windows with almost 6,000 finds among zoology (about 1,400 naturalized animals, including a great crocodile of the Nile), paleontology (about 2,500 fossils for geological era, total collection over 12,000 pieces centered on the Lombard Triassic), geology and mineralogy and archaeology. It offers educational routes for schools.

Why go. Fossils, minerals and a Nile crocodile in seminary.
