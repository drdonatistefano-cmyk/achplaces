Palazzo Mazziotti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palazzo dei Mazziotti, barons of Celso and opponents of Bourbon absolutism, with fifteenth century plant and current appearance of the late eighteenth century; declared national monument, it preserves frescoes by Matteo Cilento and surrounds three sides of the main square of Celso with a facade with twelve balconies and two watchtowers. The chapel of St. Nicholas is placed on it; in 2010 it appears in the film "We believed" by Mario Martone.

Why go. The Renaissance palace of Celso.
