Chiesa di Maria Santissima del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the XIV century in the homonymous square, which preserves frescoes of the eighteenth century; on the same square stands Palazzo Costantini, with tower and three portals with sharp arch.

Why go. The oldest church in the village, with 18th century frescoes.
