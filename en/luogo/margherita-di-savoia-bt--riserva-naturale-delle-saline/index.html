Riserva naturale delle Saline

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

4,500 hectares, of which about 4,000 are evaporation tanks: the largest salt pans in Europe, born where the sea flooded the low parts of the ancient Lake Salpi and developed especially in the nineteenth century under Ferdinand I of the Two Sicilies; protected since 1977, they host many species of aquatic birds such as herons ashes, gauzes, nitticolar and sgarze tuft. Guided tours only by reservation, every day from sunrise to sunset, for a fee.

Why go. Europe's largest salt pans and their water birds.
