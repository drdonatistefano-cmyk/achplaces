Castello di Serralunga d'Alba

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Among the best examples of the 14th century noble castle of Piedmont, remained almost unchanged: the palacium elongated with cylindrical tower, hanging watchtower and square tower, with large overlapping rooms. Guided tour by reservation.

Why go. The Langhe Donjon.
