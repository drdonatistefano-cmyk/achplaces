Museo di storia naturale di Melilli

Museums · Science and university

Multidisciplinary Museum in three sections: archaeological, with marine and terrestrial finds from excavations in the territory; ethno-anthropological, with carts, models of boats and objects of material culture; naturalistic, with a geopaleontological sector and paleontological, malacological, entomological, ornithological and mammal collections. The naturalistic and ethnographic collections belong to the Regional State. Open 9-13.30 and 14-18.45; tel. 0931 552193.

Why go. Fossils, fauna and peasant life of the Iblei in one museum.
