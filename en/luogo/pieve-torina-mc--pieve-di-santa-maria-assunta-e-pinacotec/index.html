Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta e Pinacoteca

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Pieve dell'XI secolo, attested by a document of the bishop of Spoleto of 1067, with bell tower and polygonal apse on the main square. It houses the art gallery with works already in the church of San Giovanni, palaces from the destroyed churches of Pomarolo and San Teodora, a Madonna with Child, angels and saints of Giovanni Andrea De Magistris and a cycle of frescoes from the second half of the fourteenth century detached from the parish church, important for the origins of the chamber school.

Why go. The fourteenth-century frescoes of the Camerino school.
