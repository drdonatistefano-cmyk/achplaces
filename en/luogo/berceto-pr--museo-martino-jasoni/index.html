Museo Martino Jasoni

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

In Casa Corchia, the paintings by Martino Jasoni, a painter born in Corchia, emigrated to the United States in the early 20th century, returned in 1924 and exhibited at the Venice Biennale in 1936: visionary works on emigration and alienation, sacred, dreamlike and rural subjects. Sunday 15-18 (July-August also Saturday and Sunday morning), other days by reservation.

Why go. The emigrated painter who returned to his village.

  • Where Casa Corchia, Corchia, Berceto
  • Entry 2 € (over 65 0.50 €, free under 18 years)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
