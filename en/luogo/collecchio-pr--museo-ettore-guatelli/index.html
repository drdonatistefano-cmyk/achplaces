Museo Ettore Guatelli

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Born in the granary of the farm Bella Foglia and then extended to the farmhouse, it is the work of the elementary master and collector Ettore Guatelli (1921-2000): about 60,000 objects among agricultural tools and craftsmen, furniture, toys, musical instruments, clothes and photographs, arranged on walls and ceilings in geometric compositions, in rooms dedicated to glass, ceramics, tin, shoes, suitcases, watches. Sections on emigration from Parmense to England and on the wanderings of the show. Open Tuesday-Friday 10-13, Saturday and holidays even 15-18, with guided tours.

Why go. The daily museum, objects like works of art.

Closed from 14 December 2026 to all January 2027, reopened scheduled to February 2027 with date to be defined

  • Where Via Nazionale 130, Ozzano Taro, Collecchio
  • Entry €8, reduced €6
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
