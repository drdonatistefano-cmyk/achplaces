MUMAT - Museo delle macchine tessili

Museums · Themed

In the former Meucci factory, founded in 1813 as a calificio and flanked in 1897 by a charcoal, active until 1988 and then purchased and restored by the municipality, it collects machines and textile tools from the end of the nineteenth century to the mid-nineteenth century, including a turbine still working and a rare wooden frame restored; it tells the industrial past of the Val di Bisenzio and the recycling of the typical rags of Prato. It is part of the Prato Museum Network; guided tours and workshops for schools.

Why go. The history of Prato textiles between frames and turbines.
