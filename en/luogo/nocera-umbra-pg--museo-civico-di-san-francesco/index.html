Museo civico di San Francesco

Museums · Old masters

In the former church of San Francesco (1368), enlarged and frescoed in the Quattro e prima Cinquecento, reopened in 2003 after the earthquake of 1997. It has a shaped cross from the second half of the thirteenth century, the great polyptych by Niccolò di Liberatore, known as the pupil, painted in 1483 for the main altar of the Duomo, the Encounter of Gioacchino and Anna at the Aurea Gate of Matteo da Gualdo with his frescoes and his circle, and two Longobard furnishings from the necropolis of the Portone, in storage by the Museum of the High Middle Ages in Rome. Info: tel. 0742 818640.

Why go. The pupil's polyptych and the Longobard gold.
