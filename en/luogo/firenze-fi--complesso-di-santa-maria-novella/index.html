Complesso di Santa Maria Novella

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The Dominican basilica with the façade of Leon Battista Alberti, the Masaccio Trinity, the Giotto Crucifix, the Ghirlandaio Tornabuoni Chapel and the Green Cloister.

Why go. The Masaccio Trinity, the first perfect perspective.

  • Where Piazza della Stazione 4, Firenze
  • Entry €7,50 whole; €5 from 11 to 18 years; free under 11 years
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
