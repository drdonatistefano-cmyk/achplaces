Chiesa di Sant'Ippolisto Martire e Specus Martyrum

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of late early Christian origins, known for the crypt called Specus Martyrum, a catacomb with the remains of Christians killed during the persecutions; there are traditionally preserved the graves of Romulus and Bishop Sabino (VI century).

Why go. The crypt of martyrs under the Collegiate.
