Abbazia di Fontigliano e Antiquarium

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The Abbey was founded in the 12th century and rebuilt in 1950; it houses an Antiquarium with augustian epigraphs, admired by Theodor Mommsen.

Why go. Roman epigraphs studied by Mommsen.
