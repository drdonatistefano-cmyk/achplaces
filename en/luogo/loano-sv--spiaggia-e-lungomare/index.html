Spiaggia e lungomare

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

The old town is bordered by the long sandy beach that runs from the station to the port; beaches and sea have obtained the Blue Flag for the twelfth consecutive time in 2021. To protect the marine environment the city has built an artificial barrier that favors the return of flora and fauna. IAT office in Lungomare Nazario Sauro 12.

Why go. Sand, sunsets on the sea and Blue Flag.
