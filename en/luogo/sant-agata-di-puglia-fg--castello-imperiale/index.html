Castello imperiale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Impressive castle in Romanesque style, between the residences of Frederick II, on a strategic hill overlooking the Calaggio valley and the borders of Irpinia, Lucania and Daunia; in the Middle Ages it was military-administrative district and last outpost of the Longobard Duchy of Benevento, and around the Mille belonged to the Landolfo and Pandolfo princes. From the top opens the view that is worth the name of the "Loggia delle Puglie" (Puglia Loggia).

Why go. The panoramic loggia on the Apulia.

MiC card indicates the castle closed every day (tel. Common 0881 984007)
