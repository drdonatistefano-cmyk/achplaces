Chiesa di Santa Marta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

16th century church in via Calvi: on the façade the Roman plaque of Minicio Esorato, imperial official of the first century, found in the lake and located here by the humanist Francesco Calvi, and a key of fifteenth century arch with the castle of Menaggio, today municipal coat of arms; inside seventeenth century paintings and a medieval source.

Why go. A Roman plaque and the coat of arms of the village.
