Museobottega della Tarsialignea

Museums · Themed

Private museum opened in 1999 in the eighteenth-century Palazzo Pomarici Santomasi, where the restoration brought to light frescoes and ceilings in hand painted paper. On four floors it tells the Sorrento wooden inlay: masters from the 15th to the 18th century, the nineteenth-century schools, techniques and materials, the local School of Art, with a layout of the city; on the ground floor a modern collection. Open every day 10-18, with audioguide.

Why go. The tradition of the Sorrento tarsia.
