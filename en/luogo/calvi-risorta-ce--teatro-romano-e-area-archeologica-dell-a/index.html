Teatro romano e area archeologica dell'antica Cales

History and architecture · Archaeology

City founded by the Ausoni and Roman colony from 334 BC, crossed today by the Autostrada del Sole: there are still walls with six doors (some sections of the 5th century BC), the passage in rock of the Ponte delle Monache, the amphitheatre of the 1st century BC, the central thermal baths with stuccoes and the northern ones on three levels along the cardo that follows the Via Latina, a temple on podium, probably Capitolium. The theatre, perhaps from the 2nd century B.C. and enlarged in the Augustan age, has the cavea on double arc galleries in cross-linked work, without more steps.

Why go. An entire Roman city, from the theatre to the spa.

Visitable only on request with reservation required at the Office for the Archaeological Heritage of Calvi Risorta (tel. 0823 652533, drm-cam.calvirisorta@cultura.gov.it)
