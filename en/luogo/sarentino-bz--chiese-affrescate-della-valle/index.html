Chiese affrescate della valle

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The church of San Nicolò in Valdurna contains frescoes from the 15th century; sacred art also in the churches of Maria Assunta and San Cipriano in Sarentino, San Valentino al Gentersberg and San Giovanni nel Bosco near Pennes.

Why go. Gothic frescoes in the mountain churches.
