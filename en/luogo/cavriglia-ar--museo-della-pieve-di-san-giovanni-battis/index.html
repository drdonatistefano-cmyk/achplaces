Museo della Pieve di San Giovanni Battista

Museums · Old masters

Small museum of sacred art in the former oratory of the Society of the Name of Jesus, leaning on the left aisle of the parish. It keeps busts in glazed terracotta by Benedetto Buglioni (San Giovannino, Santo Stefano and probably San Romolo) and an angel polychrome regiment, a thirteenth-century processional cross in chiselled and gilded copper with Christ in golden bronze, a plate in copper painted with Nativity of the sixteenth century perhaps of workshop of Limoges, the Circumcision attributed to Benedetto Veli and eighteenth-century furnishings with figures of nativity scenes of Neapolitan taste.

Why go. The glazed terracotta of Benedetto Buglioni.
