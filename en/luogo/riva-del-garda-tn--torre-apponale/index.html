Torre Apponale

History and architecture · Towers · Paid entry · Viewpoint

34-metre tower on Piazza III Novembre, documented in the thirteenth century to guard and defend the city; on top the golden angel-signavento "Anzolim de la Tor" is one of the symbols of Riva. 165 steps up to the panoramic terrace on the center and lake; free under 15 years.

Why go. 165 steps for the best glance at the center.
