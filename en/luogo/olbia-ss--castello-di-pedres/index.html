Castello di Pedres

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

On a granite rock about 140 meters above sea level, about 5 km south of the center: probably built in the 13th century under the Pisan Visconti, in the mid-300s passed to the Hospitallers of St. John of Jerusalem and then to the Aragonese, and was abandoned from the following century. A recent staircase rises to the top, where the quadrangular mast is about 10 meters high (two of the four original floors) and an underground cistern; halfway up the remains of a small watchtower.

Why go. The pisan mast on the granite cliff.
