Castagneti secolari e parco di sculture Drena 3000

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails

The village is surrounded by centuries-old chestnut groves that give the chestnuts of Drena, symbol of local gastronomy; in the northern part of the village, among chestnuts and trekking trails, is located Drena 3000, park of outdoor sculptures.

Why go. Outdoor art in the chestnut trees.
