Museo naturalistico del Monte Soratte e Palazzo Caccia-Canali

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The museum occupies the Palazzo Caccia-Canali, a project attributed to Jacopo Barozzi called the Vignola, completed in 1589, with frescoes, friezes and large sixteenth-century fireplaces; since 2020 it is part of the network of historic residences of Lazio and also houses the Pinacoteca. The geological, anthropological, botanical and fauna routes tell the Riserva del Soratte: traces of the animals of the woods, typical plants, the formation of the caves and the prehistoric tools of the first populations.

Why go. The Soratte explained in a Vignola palace.
