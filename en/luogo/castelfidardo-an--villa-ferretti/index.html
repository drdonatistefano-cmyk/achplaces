Villa Ferretti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Open to visitors on the summer weekends, it preserves the works of art collected by Duke Roberto Ferretti, including pieces from Palazzo Ferretti in Ancona.

Why go. A collection of noble art in the villa.
