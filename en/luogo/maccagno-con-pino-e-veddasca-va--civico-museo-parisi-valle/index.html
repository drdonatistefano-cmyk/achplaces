Civico Museo Parisi Valle

Museums · Modern art

At the centre of the lake promenade, the building was designed in 1980-86 by the architect Maurizio Sacripanti and since 1998 hosts a permanent collection with works by Picasso, Balla, De Chirico, Cassinari, Rijeka and Levi, as well as temporary exhibitions. The collection has grown with an acquisition project started in 2006 and has over 200 paintings and sculptures.

Why go. Picasso and de Chirico by Lake Maggiore.
