Mausoleo di Teodorico

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The tomb of the king of Istria (520 approximately) in blocks of Istria, covered by a single monolith of about 300 tons.

Why go. The monolithic dome is an engineering enigma that can only be understood by seeing it live.

  • Where Via delle Industrie 14, Ravenna
  • Entry €6 full, €5 reduced, €2 from 18 to 25 years; free on the first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website cultura.gov.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
