Museo Civico Aufidenate

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the Franciscan monastery of Maddalena, built in 1487 on a pilgrim's hospice along the Via degli Abruzzi and then home to the Customs of Foggia, school and prison. The collection, opened in 1898 with 335 exhibits and 130 coins and largely dispersed in the war, has fossils and paleolithic finds, statues of the Iron Age and osque ceramics, inscriptions and Roman furnishings, votive terracotta and a Sannite bull in golden bronze, as well as medieval and modern statues and ceramics. In the same complex the botanical garden, a nativity scene of the Neapolitan school, the War Museum and the International Museum of Fly Fishing "Stanisao Kuckiewicz" . Wednesday-Sunday 10.30-18.30.

Why go. Sanniti and Romans of the Val di Sangro in a monastery on the river.
