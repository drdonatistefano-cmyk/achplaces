Spiaggia della Scialara

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

About 3 km of fine white sand with shallow and crystal clear waters, in the Gargano National Park, with the historic centre on the limestone massif behind. At the beginning there is the Pizzomunno and the castle swabble dominates it from the cliff, from which the nickname of "Beach of the Castle." It has facilities with sun loungers, umbrellas, showers and restaurants, but also quiet stretches; you can walk to the promenade Mattei.

Why go. Easy beach suitable for children, just a few steps from the center.
