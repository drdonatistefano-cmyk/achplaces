Chiesa e Convento di San Francesco

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

The complex was founded in 1288 and built outside the walls by the Counts of Celano; in 1294 it was hosted by Pietro del Morrone, future Celestine V, on his way to L'Aquila for the coronation. The church preserves cycles of frescoes on the life of the saint (XIV-XV century) and baroque altars in wood, stone and stucco of the mid-Sixteenth century; the convent houses the Museum of Sacred Art, with reliquaries, an altar cross of 1403 and the "Pasquarella" , Virgin with the Child and two silver angels donated by the Counts of Celano, and next to the Archaeological Museum with the finds of Superaequum. The MiC tab indicates opening every day 9-17.

Why go. Franciscan frescoes and the silver of the Counts of Celano.
