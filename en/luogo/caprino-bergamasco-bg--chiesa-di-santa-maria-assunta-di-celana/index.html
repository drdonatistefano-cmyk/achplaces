Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta di Celana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the hamlet of Celana, next to the Borromaic College, which houses an important altarpiece of the Assumption of Lorenzo Lotto; the complex was opened on the FAI Spring Days of 2021.

Why go. A altarpiece of Lorenzo Lotto in a church in the valley.
