Ferrata Falconera

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Made by the Alpine Rescue of Varallo with the Alpine Guides of Alagna, it has two routes: the Partusacc, ring to Mount Tovo, and the most challenging Falconera, signposted by Civiasco with path 636. At the opening of the night lighting in 2020, it was presented as the first European ferrata equipped with a lighting system.

Why go. View of the valley and illuminated ferrata also at night.
