MuMi - Museo Michetti

Museums · Modern art

In Palazzo San Domenico, seat of the Fondazione Francesco Paolo Michetti established in 1952, a few years after the National Prize of painting born in 1947. He has exhibited great works by Michetti, including "The Numbs" and "The Snakes" and the paintings of the artists of the Prize; since 2006 the foundation has collected over 260 works, including painting, photography, video, sculpture and installations, and hosts monographic exhibitions on Michetti and contemporary art.

Why go. The great paintings of Michetti and a century of Prize.
