Scavi archeologici della Civitella

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the hill of Civitella, in the shadow of a chestnut grove, the excavations have returned terrace walls, tiles, vases, an arched door and foundations of the fourth century BC, remains of a fortified center that controlled the valleys towards the sea of Velia. On the south side there is a well preserved door with narrow corridor and stone hinges of the wooden door; the central plateau, excavated only in part, has a paved space and a building perhaps of worship, as suggested by a votive deposit.

Why go. A fortress of the fourth century BC among chestnut trees.
