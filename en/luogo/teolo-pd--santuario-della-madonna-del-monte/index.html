Santuario della Madonna del Monte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

On the summit of the Mount of the Madonna, second relief of the Euganean Hills, a church is already attested by a testament of 1253; in 1508 Pope Julius II entrusted it to the Abbey of Praglia. Rebuilt largely in 1940, it has next to a small monastery with cloister built between 1955 and 1965; the restoration brought to light the walls of the sixteenth century chapel and in the presbytery there is a Madonna with the fourteenth-century Child in polychrome stone attributed to Andriolo de' Santi. On Sundays and holidays, the monks of Prague celebrate there.

Why go. The sanctuary at the top of the hills, linked to Praglia for five centuries.
