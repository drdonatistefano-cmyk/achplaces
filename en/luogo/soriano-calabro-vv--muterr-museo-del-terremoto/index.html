MuTerr - Museo del Terremoto

Museums · Themed

In the underground spaces of the ancient Convent of San Domenico, it tells the seismic phenomena with attention to Calabria and Italy, from historical, anthropological and architectural perspectives. Designed by Schiavello Architects Office with architects Ciconte and Prestanicola, it has a room with corrugated wooden ceiling of more than a thousand pieces and a second environment, Scuri, with barrel vaults; it also hosts exhibitions and events.

Why go. The earthquake of 1783 told inside its ruins.
