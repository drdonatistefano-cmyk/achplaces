Museo storico etnografico e di tradizione

Museums · Themed

Born from the generosity of the country and the work of a group of volunteers, it occupies ten rooms of the ancient Town Hall: wine production, agricultural work, the house, the sports of tradition, history and local characters. From April to October open on Sundays from 15 to 18 and by reservation; in winter and August only by reservation (tel. 0173 790103).

Why go. The peasant life of Langa told by the country itself.
