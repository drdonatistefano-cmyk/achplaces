Museo del Mare (Meraviglie marine)

Museums · Themed

Collection ranging from the great cetaceans of the Mediterranean, such as sperm whale, whale and dolphin, to sea turtles, shells, fossils and fishing instruments; on the upper floor an area for the care and recovery of sea turtles.

Why go. The Adriatic Sea explained to the little ones.
