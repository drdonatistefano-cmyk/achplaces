Museo civico archeologico della Valle Sabbia

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Founded in 1956 by Gruppo Grotte Gavardo and then sold to the Municipality, it occupies a fifteenth-century building in the historic center and tells about the Valley Sand and the western Garda area from the Middle Paleolithic to the post-Renaissance age, with fossils and a section of paleontology. Among the finds are the skeleton of a bear of the caves of the Hollow of the Friar, materials of the pile of the Lucon of Polpenazze (part of a UNESCO site) and the Roman necropolis of the Lugon of Salò. The exhibition itinerary is being reviewed.

Why go. The cave bear and the stilts of Garda.

  • Where Piazzetta San Bernardino 5, Gavardo
  • Entry €4, reduced €2 (4-17 years and over 65), Sunday €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.museoarcheologicogavardo.it
