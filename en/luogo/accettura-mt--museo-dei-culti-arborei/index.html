Museo dei culti arborei

Museums · Themed

Ethnoanthropological Museum inaugurated in 2005 and dedicated to the Lucanian arboreal cults, linked to the peasant world; it also serves as a visitor center of the Park of Gallipoli Cognato and has a library on the arboreal cults and a video library on the feasts of May. May combines the top of a holly with a large oak, symbol of fertility and good harvest.

Why go. To understand May, the most famous ritual of Acceptura.
