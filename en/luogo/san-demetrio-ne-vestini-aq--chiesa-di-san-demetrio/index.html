Chiesa di San Demetrio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church at the foot of the village, seventeenth century but of medieval origin: the act for its construction was written by the notary Joseph Cappa on March 28, 1600 and the relics under the altars were consecrated by Bishop Domenico Tagliatatela on October 16, 1736, as recalls a plaque in the right aisle. On the north wall the plaster hides a Roman tombstone and a tile with a stretched thumb; two sundials are on the side and on the back.

Why go. The mother church of the village, with its sundials.
