Riserva naturale Gole del Sagittario

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Deep canyon dug in thousands of years by the Sagittarius river, WWF oasis since 1991 and regional nature reserve since 1997; English travellers of the late nineteenth century called it "fearful and beautiful." A panoramic view of the gorges can be reached from via Duca degli Abruzzi.

Why go. The canyon of Sagittarius under the village.
