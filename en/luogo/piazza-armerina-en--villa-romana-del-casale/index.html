Villa Romana del Casale

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Villa of the 4th century A.D., probably Lucio Aradio Valerio Proculo, governor of Sicily: about 3,500 square meters of mosaics, including the Corridor of the Great Hunt with the capture of animals in Africa and the ten girls in bikini are engaged in sports exercises.

Why go. The most famous Roman mosaics in Sicily.
