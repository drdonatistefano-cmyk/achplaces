Castello di Roccapelago e museo «Sulle orme di Obizzo da Montegarullo»

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On a spur over 1000 m on the Pelago basin, it was of the Longobards and Matilde of Canossa and from 1240 of the Montegarullo; Obizzo made it the Guelph fortress of Frignano until he was defeated in 1408. The arms room became parish church in 1586: in its crypt in 2011 more than 300 bodies were discovered buried between the Five and the eighteenth century, almost one hundred mummified, exhibited with the accessories in the museum of the fortress. In the parish of São Paulo a golden tabernacle of 1603 and a Madonna of the Rosary of G.B. Bertusio (1627); at the foot of the Captain's House.

Why go. The mummies of Roccapelago in the castle of Obizzo.
