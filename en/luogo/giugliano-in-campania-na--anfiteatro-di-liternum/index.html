Anfiteatro di Liternum

History and architecture · Archaeology

Immediately south of the ancient city; a limited excavation identified its limits and at least two phases: probably built in the second century BC with at least two entrances (north and south), it was expanded and renovated between I and II century A.D. Like Pompeii's, it looks like it's got no basement. You can reach it from the exit Lake Tangenziale or by bus EAV 914 from Fuorigrotta.

Why go. A Roman amphitheatre born in the second century BC
