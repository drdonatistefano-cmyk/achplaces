Museo del Latte

Museums · Themed

Set up in the ancient dairy farm of Cerano, it collects the historical tools of milk processing (churches, conga, wrap and a culdera) and offers a film of the eighties on traditional crafts.

Why go. The mountain dairy life, in the dairy where it took place.
