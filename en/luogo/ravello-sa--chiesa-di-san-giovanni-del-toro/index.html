Chiesa di San Giovanni del Toro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Built between 975 and 1018 by the Rogadeo, Pironti and Muscettola families and consecrated in 1276, in the district of the oldest palaces. It preserves an ambo of the twelfth century with mosaics (Jonas and the sea monster) and twenty-one majolica basins of Arab origin, a sarcophagus of the third century AD, frescoes fourteenth-century in the crypt with the Christ Pantocrator and in sacristy a risen Christ attributed to Roberto d'Oderisio or to his circle.

Why go. The ambo with Jonah and the Arab majolicas.

  • Where Via San Giovanni del Toro 4, Ravello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
