Palazzo Mocenigo – Museo del Tessuto, del Costume e del Profumo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Museum of fabric and costume with paths dedicated to perfume; unique ticket with Ca' Rezzonico and Casa di Carlo Goldoni.

Why go. Fashion and perfume in Venetian history.

  • Where Santa Croce, Venezia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
