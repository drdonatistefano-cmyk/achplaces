Villa d'Este

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Wanted by Cardinal Ippolito II d'Este, governor of Tivoli in the middle of the sixteenth century: frescoed rooms by Mannerist painters, including the Salone della Fontana by Girolamo Muziano, and the Italian garden with fountains fed only by gravity by Pirro Ligorio, such as the Bicchierone, the ovate, the Dragons, the Cento Fontanelle and the Organ. UNESCO heritage since 2001.

Why go. The garden of the fountains that has been schooled in Europe.
