Pinacoteca Nazionale di Ferrara

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

On the noble floor of Palazzo dei Diamanti, the great Ferrara painting of the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries.

Why go. Cosmè Tura, Dosso Dossi and Garofalo in the same building as the exhibitions.

  • Where Corso Ercole I d'Este 21, Ferrara
  • Entry Indicated €8 whole, €2 reduced (Ministry, 2024); in MyFE Card
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website emiliaromagnaturismo.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
