Museo della Viticoltura

Museums · Themed

A small museum that with objects, tools, photographs and drawings tells the life of the winemakers of the foothills: the path follows the cycle of the vine, from undoing to grafting, pruning and harvesting; a second section is dedicated to the vinification and preservation of wine.

Why go. The work of the vineyard on the hill, tool for tool.
