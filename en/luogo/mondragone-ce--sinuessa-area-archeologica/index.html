Sinuessa, area archeologica

History and architecture · Archaeology

Cologne founded by the Romans in 296 BC and then important stop of the Via Appia, famous for the thermal baths frequented by Roman matrons and patricians; in 375 it suffered enormous damage due to a catastrophic earthquake. The remains of the submerged city are located in the hamlet Le Vagnole. On the occasion of the Appia Day 2026 the Superintendence opened the Regina Viarum Archaeological Park in Mondragone with free guided tours.

Why go. The Roman town of the Appia overlooking the Tyrrhenian Sea.
