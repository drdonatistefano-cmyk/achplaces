Parco dell'ossidiana e sentiero di sa Perda Crobina

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

On Mount Arci, in the territory of Pau, nodules and obsidian waste cover over 20 hectares between the deposits of Perdas Urias, Sennixeddu and on Forru de is Sintzurreddus. The trail of Perda Crobina (the Corvina stone), steep and with large heaps of obsidian, crosses in about an hour and a half one of the largest prehistoric workshops, among thousands of shrapnel; you can walk with the guides of the museum. Marmora wrote that it seems to walk on the pieces of an old black bottle factory.

Why go. Walk on a carpet of prehistoric obsidian.
