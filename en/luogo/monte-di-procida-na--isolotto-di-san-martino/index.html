Isolotto di San Martino

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Isolotto of about 1600 sqm, accessible for a fee through a tunnel and a jetty; probably detached from the coast with a tidal wave in 1488. In the 5th and 17th centuries it was a base for tuna fishing, in the 1930s for testing the torpedoes of the torpedo factory of Baia and in the post-war meeting of the "sweet life" Neapolitan; today it is a seaside destination and for diving, with deep waters rich in fish and ancient finds.

Why go. Baths and diving on an island with curious history.
