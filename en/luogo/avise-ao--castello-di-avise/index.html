Castello di Avise

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built towards the end of the fifteenth century by Bonifacio or Rodolfo d'Avise: three-storey building with square tower, the first on the left entering the village.

Why go. The castle of the gentlemen.
