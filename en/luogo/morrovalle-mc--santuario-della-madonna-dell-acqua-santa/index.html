Santuario della Madonna dell'Acqua Santa

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Originally a small newsstand dedicated to the Madonna della Snow, after a snowfall of August 5th; it preserves the remains of Pier Francesco Grisei.

Why go. A sanctuary born of a snowfall in August.
