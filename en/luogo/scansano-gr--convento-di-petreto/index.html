Convento di Petreto

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

On the hill that dominates the village, among oak woods, holm oaks, cypresses and pine trees, it preserves traces of the Aldobrandesque walls and a church with Romanesque foundations.

Why go. The convent in the woods above the village.
