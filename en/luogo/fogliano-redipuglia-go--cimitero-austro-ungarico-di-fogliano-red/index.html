Cimitero austro-ungarico di Fogliano Redipuglia

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Military cemetery of the First World War that guards the remains of 14,550 Austro-Hungarian soldiers who fell in this area.

Why go. The other face of the Isonzo front.
