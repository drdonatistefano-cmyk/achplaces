Teatro Civico

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Designed by engineer Pietro Pernigotti and inaugurated on May 2, 1838, it has three orders of stages, the curtain of Luigi Vacca with Orpheus and Eurydice and the medallion of the Muse on the ceiling; closed in 1952, was reopened after the restoration on May 2, 1990.

Why go. A 19th-century stage theatre.
