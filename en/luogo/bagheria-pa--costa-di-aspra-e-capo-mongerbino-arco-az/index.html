Costa di Aspra e Capo Mongerbino (Arco Azzurro)

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Along the coast sandy beaches alternate with cliffs and secluded coves; between Mongerbino and Aspra there is the geosite of the Blue Arch, in Capo Mongerbino.

Why go. Sea, cliffs and Mongerbino's natural arch.
