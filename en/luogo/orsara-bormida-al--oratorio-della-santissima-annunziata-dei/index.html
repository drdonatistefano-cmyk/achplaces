Oratorio della Santissima Annunziata (dei Disciplinanti)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish until 1676, in Lombard Romanesque style with a single nave, with traces of the building of the Mille at the base of the bell tower; until 1810 the Orsaresi buried there. Inside stuccoes and frescoes of the eighteenth century recently restored. It rises in the castle's shelter.

Why go. Romanesque oratory in the shelter.
