Rocca dei Papi e giardino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The residence of the popes fleeing the sieges of Rome and the pontifical rectors of Tuscia; the garden, created in 1736 by Cardinal Aldrovandi paving a hill, looks together at the lake, the Tyrrhenian and the Apennines.

Why go. The balcony of high Tuscia.
