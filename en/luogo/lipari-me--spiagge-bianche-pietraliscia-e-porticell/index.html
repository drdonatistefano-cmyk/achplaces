Spiagge bianche, Pietraliscia e Porticello

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The north-eastern coast has white sand: the White Beach can be reached with a steep staircase, another only from the sea; then Pietraliscia, Porticello and the Secca della Scis in Cappero area.

Why go. The clear sea between pumice and lard.

  • Where Costa nord-orientale, Lipari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
