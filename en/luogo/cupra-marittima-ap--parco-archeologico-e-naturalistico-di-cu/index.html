Parco archeologico e naturalistico di Cupra Maritima

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

About 1.5 km north of the center, on the height of the Civita, the Forum of the Roman city with the remains of a rectangular temple of the Ariane era and of two arches; in the Picena age the place was known for the sanctuary of the goddess Cupra. Visits by reservation (375 739 0077).

Why go. The Roman city of the goddess Cupra.
