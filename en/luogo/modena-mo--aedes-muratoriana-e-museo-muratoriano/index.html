Aedes Muratoriana e Museo Muratoriano

Museums · House museums

In Piazza della Pomposa, the church of Santa Maria della Pomposa rebuilt by Lodovico Antonio Muratori, parish priest since 1716, with works by Bernardino Cervi and Jean Boulanger; in the canon where he lived, the museum opened in 1931 with manuscripts, the writing desk, the librarian chair and numerous portraits.

Why go. The house of the great Modena historian.
