Castello d'Albertis – Museo delle Culture del Mondo

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Neo-Gothic castle overlooking the city, furnished by Captain Enrico Alberto d'Albertis with objects from five continents; today it houses the Museum of Cultures of the World. You will also arrive with the elevator of Montegalletto from via Balbi.

Why go. The navigator's castle.
