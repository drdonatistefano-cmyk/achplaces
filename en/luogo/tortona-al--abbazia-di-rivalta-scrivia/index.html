Abbazia di Rivalta Scrivia

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The 12th century Abbey where the Cistercians settled in 1180; the restorations of 1941-42 brought to light frescoes of the second fifteenth century, including a San Cristoforo signed by Franceschino Boxilio in 1490. Next to it stands a noble residence with loggias from the middle of the seventeenth century.

Why go. A Cistercian abbey with 15th century frescoes.
