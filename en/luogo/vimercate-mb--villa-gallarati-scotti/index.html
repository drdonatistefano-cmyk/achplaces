Villa Gallarati Scotti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The baroque villa was born and then remodeled in neoclassical style; in the park there are traces of the Baroque garden and a nymphaeum of Neptune.

Why go. A villa of brianzola delight with the nymphaeum of Neptune in the park.
