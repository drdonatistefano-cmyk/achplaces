Museo del Parco Pianelle

Museums · Themed

Opened in 1999 on the second floor of the Doge's Palace as a scientific and didactic space that prepares for the visit of the reserve: it presents the natural heritage, landscape and culture of the Pianelle Park, Mediterranean ecosystem of the Murge between Massafra, Crispiano and Martina Franca.
