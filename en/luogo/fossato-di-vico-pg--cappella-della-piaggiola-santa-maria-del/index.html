Cappella della Piaggiola (Santa Maria della Piaggiola)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Inside the thirteenth century with a barrel vault and frescoes of the fifteenth century well preserved by Ottaviano Nelli da Gubbio; it was the seat of the Monte di Pietà.

Why go. The frescoes of Ottaviano Nelli.
