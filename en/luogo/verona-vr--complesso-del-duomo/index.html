Complesso del Duomo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage

Around the cathedral of Santa Maria Mastracolare, with three aisles of the twelfth century, the Romanesque baptistery of San Giovanni in Fonte with the octagonal tub, the church of Sant'Elena, where Dante held the Quaestio de aqua et terra in 1320, and the canonical cloister; under the cloister and Sant'Elena the remains of the two early Christian basilicas with mosaic floors.

Why go. From the first cathedral of the fourth century to the Romanesque.
