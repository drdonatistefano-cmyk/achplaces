Museo archeologico e multimediale

Museums · Archaeological

It tells the history of the territory from Prehistory to the seventeenth century with over 4,000 archaeological finds, plastic, reconstructions of environments, holograms, video and virtual reality. The route is divided into two locations: the section of Roman archaeology on the ground floor of the Municipal Library "P." Angelani and that of pre-Roman archaeology, with frescoed rooms, on the first floor of Palazzo Orsini. Accessible even to disabled people.

Why go. Four thousand exhibits and an immersive set-up in two locations.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Serrecchia (Biblioteca Angelani) e Piazza Angelo Frammartino 4, Monterotondo

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
