Castello dei Burattini, Museo Giordano Ferrari

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The collection of puppets, puppets, scenes and scripts of the family of puppet makers Ferrari and other Italian and European companies.

Why go. Perfect with the kids.

  • Where Via Melloni, Parma
  • Entry €4 full, €3 reduced; included in the single ticket of the Civic Museums (€15)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website emiliaromagnaturismo.it
