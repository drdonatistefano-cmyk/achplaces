Museo di Collepietra

Museums · Themed

Museum of Tyrolean history and rural life, with its religious roots: almost forgotten crafts, war memories, rooms and workshops rebuilt in original style, an exhibition on local fauna and video interviews with witnesses of the time. Open from Palm Sunday at the end of October, only with guided tour in German or Italian (mar-ven 10:10 and 11:10; Saturday, Sunday and holidays 17:00), by reservation; not without barriers.

Why go. Tyrolean peasant life told room by room.
