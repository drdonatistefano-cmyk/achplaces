Castello di Aymavilles

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

14th-century castle of Amedeo di Challant, with four towers and white facades between the Middle Ages and Baroque; exhibits the history of the Challant and the collection of the Académie Saint-Anselme.

Why go. The four tower castle.
