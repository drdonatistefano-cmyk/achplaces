Santuario della Beata Vergine della Spina

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

According to tradition, it was born from an image of the Virgin painted on a pylon that, wounded to one eye by a thorn, began to bleed; already in 1585 the bishop of Asti described there a great turnout of faithful. Next to it stands the monastery of the middle seventeenth century, inhabited by Trinitarians, Cistercians and other orders, since 1991 of the Adoring nuns of the Cottolengo; in 1877 the painter Felice Barucco painted the vaults. Feast on August 15th.

Why go. A country sanctuary linked to a popular legend.
