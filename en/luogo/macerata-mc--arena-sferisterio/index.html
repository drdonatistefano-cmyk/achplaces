Arena Sferisterio

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Neoclassical arena designed by Ireneo Aleandri and built in the twenties of the nineteenth century with a private subscription; every summer it hosts the opera festival outdoors.

Why go. The symbol of the city and its opera season.
