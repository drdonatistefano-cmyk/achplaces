Oasi di Trepalade

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Small natural environment along the Sile between Quarto d'Altino and Portegrandi, reachable by bike Girasile and equipped with a service center; can be visited all year round by reservation with the Ornithological Association Basso Piave.

Why go. Birdwatching on the banks of the Sile.
