Castello dei Principi Biondi Morra

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Longobard fortification of the IX-X century, cited in 1137 when the Norman Roberto Morra pursued the abbot Rainaldo of Montecassino; he passed to the Giamvilla and the Caracciolo, who at the beginning of the seventeenth century made it a residence, and then returned to the Morra. Damaged by the earthquake of 1695, by the French looting of 1799, by the fire of 1911 that destroyed the library and by the earthquake of 1980, in the eighties it was donated to the municipality. The stone façade remains and part of the two circular towers at the entrance; in the atrium there is a Roman milestone of the I-III century.

Why go. The Morra Castle above the Ofanto Valley.
