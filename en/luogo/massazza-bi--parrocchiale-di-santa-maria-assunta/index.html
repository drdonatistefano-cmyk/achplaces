Parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Rebuilt in the 17th century on the site of a sixteenth-century church, it has a rectangular plan with two side chapels adorned with eighteenth-century canvases and a large porch facing the facade. A marble tomb with coat of arms recalls the burial of the Avogadro, feudal lords of the place; the organ is of 1814, the work of Giacinto Bruna of Migliano. The bell tower, restored in the early eighteenth century, is made of stone in the lower part and brick with two orders of mullioned windows at the top.

Why go. The memories of the Avogadro and a nineteenth century organ.
