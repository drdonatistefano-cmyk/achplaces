Collegiata di Santa Maria d'Itria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1121 and 1198 by the counts of Loritello, it had originally three naves; damaged by the earthquake of 1744, it was rebuilt to a single nave preserving the portal in limestone with floral motifs. Inside, the niches of the lateral altars with traces of colour and baroque stuccos remain; behind, the bell tower shows wall stratifications from the Norman foundation to the twentieth century. Facade and roof collapsed with the earthquake of 1905, after which it was abandoned in favor of the nearby church of Santa Maria Assunta.

Why go. The Norman portal of a church that was in ruins.
