Museo civico di storia naturale

Museums · Science and university

In the Villa Gardella, donated to the Municipality in 1964, it is managed by the Naturalisti Stazzano Group (founded in 1974) and opened to the public around 1980; the fifth room, dedicated to botany, is 1996. It exhibits mainly material of the Ligurian-Piedmontese area: rocks, minerals, fossils, insects, fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds and mammals; it preserves over 5,000 leaves of herbarium, including an eighteenth-century herbarium, and more than 10,000 insects. Open on Saturday 15-18, otherwise by appointment (tel. 0143 65303.

Why go. Five natural rooms of the Apennines, with an 18th century herbarium.
