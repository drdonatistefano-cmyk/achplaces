Museo della Stampa «Lodovico Pavoni»

Museums · Themed

Founded at the beginning of the 2000s by Simone Quetti, a former pupil of the Pavoni printing school in Brescia, he tells the evolution of printing with machines still working that can be tried: presses of the nineteenth century, a linotype, flat-cylinder machines and a plate "Heidelberg"; with the guide you learn the traditional typographical techniques. Guaranteed opening by reservation (tel. 346 8315120, museodellastampa.artogne@gmail.com).

Why go. Compose and print with the machines of a real printer.
