Museo di Sant'Alberico Crescitelli

Museums · House museums

Museum house dedicated to the missionary of PIME Alberico Crescitelli, born in Altavilla and martyred in China in 1900 during the Boxer revolt.

Why go. On the trail of the holy missionary of the country.
