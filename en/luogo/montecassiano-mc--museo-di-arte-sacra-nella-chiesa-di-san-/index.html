Museo di arte sacra nella chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Museums · Themed

In the sixteenth-century church with an octagonal Baroque plan, reachable from the steps of Via Roma after Porta San Giovanni: paintings of the ancient altars and oratory, thrones, processional machines, candelabra and restored crucifixes.

Why go. The restored processional machines.
