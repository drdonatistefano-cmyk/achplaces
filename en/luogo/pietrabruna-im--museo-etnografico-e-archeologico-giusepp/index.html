Museo etnografico e archeologico «Giuseppina Guasco»

Museums · Themed

Museum that collects and exhibits testimonies of the peasant civilization and archaeological finds found in the municipal territory, related to the native populations. Tel. 0183 90200.

Why go. Rural civilization and archaeology of the valley.
