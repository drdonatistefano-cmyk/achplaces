Museo Archeologico Civico

Museums · Archaeological

Housed in the town hall, it exhibits prehistoric finds of Cavaion, of the pond of Cà Nove and the nearby municipalities: ceramics, bronzes, dagger blades, bone and stone artifacts, amber, tablets and frame weights. It also preserves the remains of a necropolis and a Roman villa.

Why go. The prehistory of the lower Garda in a small civic museum.
