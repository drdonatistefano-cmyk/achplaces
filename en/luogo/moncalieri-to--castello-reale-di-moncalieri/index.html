Castello Reale di Moncalieri

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Medieval fortification expanded in the seventeenth century in royal residence, UNESCO heritage since 1997: here on 20 November 1849 Vittorio Emanuele II signed the Proclamation of Moncalieri. You can visit the Royal Apartments with the House of Queen Maria Adelaide, the Chinese Cabinet, the Proclamation Hall and the Royal Chapel, reopened in 2017 after the fire of 2008. Visit by reservation.

Why go. The palace on the hill.
