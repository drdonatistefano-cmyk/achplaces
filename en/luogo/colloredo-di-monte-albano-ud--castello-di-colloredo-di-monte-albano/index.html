Castello di Colloredo di Monte Albano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Erected from 1302 by William of Waldsee and completed by his sons, the first Colloredo Mels Waldsee; destroyed in 1315 by the Gorizians and rebuilt, passed to Venice in 1420, was burned by the peasants and damaged by the earthquake in 1511 and rebuilt in the sixteenth century, with the monumental staircase of 1626. On a ridge of the morainic hills includes mastium, west and east wings, guard body, tower door, red house and three towers; Ippolito Nievo, related to the Colloredos, wrote the "Confessions of an Italian" there. After the long post-sysm recovery, on 19-20 September 2026 the Region returned it to the community reopening the Nievo Wing; the West Wing is the seat of the Friuli Hill Community and cultural center.

Why go. The castle of the "Confessions" of Nievo, reborn after the earthquake.
