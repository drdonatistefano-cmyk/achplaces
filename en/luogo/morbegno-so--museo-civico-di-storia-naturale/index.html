Museo civico di storia naturale

Museums · Science and university

Founded in 1974 with the donation of 500 minerals by Giacomo Perego, since 1983 it is located in the eighteenth-century Palazzo Gualteroni. It preserves about 1,500 minerals and fossils, including the Cassinisia orobica symbol of the museum, over 35,000 insects, 1,000 birds and 1,300 mammals, with reconstructions of alpine environments, showcases on wolf and bear and skull of a knocked bear in Val Gerola in 1887. Open Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 9-12 and 14:30-17:30.

Why go. The nature of the central Alps in an eighteenth-century palace.
