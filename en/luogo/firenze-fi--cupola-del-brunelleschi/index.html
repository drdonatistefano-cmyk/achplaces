Cupola del Brunelleschi

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The double-cap dome built without centimeters between 1420 and 1436: you climb between the two caps, next to the Last Judgment of Vasari and Zuccari, up to the lantern.

Why go. The view from the lantern pays off all the steps.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Firenze
  • Entry Only with the Brunelleschi Pass: €30 whole, €12 from 7 to 14 years, free up to 6 years (3 days, includes all the monuments of the square)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility Walk up narrow stairs, not accessible
  • Official website duomo.firenze.it
  • Opening hours official page
