Cattedrale Vegetale

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

The work designed by the Lodigian artist Giuliano Mauri, stands on a sheltered hill along the ascent to Pizzo Arera: over 20 metres high, it has a gothic five-nave plant with 42 beech columns, supported by about 1,800 spruce poles, 600 chestnut branches and 6,000 metres of hazel branches taken from the nearby woods and tied with ropes, pins and nails. In time the beech trees will grow until they no longer need scaffolding, destined to deteriorate.

Why go. A Gothic cathedral made of live trees at the height.
