Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Just outside the village, next to the Capuchin monastery of the sixteenth century then suppressed. The main altar is a retablo inlaid, carved and painted, with nine tables of Neapolitan Mannerist school of the seventeenth century.

Why go. A 17th-century retablo at nine tables.
