Museo La Specola

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

The University's museum of zoology and mineralogy, with the famous anatomical waxes of the eighteenth century.

Why go. Anatomical waxes, unique in the world.

  • Where Via Romana 17, Firenze
  • Entry €10 whole; €5 reduced (6-19 years, over 65, residents of the Metropolitan City)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.sma.unifi.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
