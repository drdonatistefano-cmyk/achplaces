Castello Litta-Beccaria

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

A fortified nucleus (castrum) built around the Mille, it assumed in the last quarter of the fifteenth century the current quadrilateral plant with angular towers in Visconti-forzesco style; from 1412 the fief was of the Beccaria, and on January 30, 1573 the Marquis Agostino Litta bought it to transform it into a country villa. In 1680 the tower of the drawbridge became the entrance gate, where you can still see the seats of the locks; in the early eighteenth century the Long Channel was started, or Loggia delle Dame, which ends in the Mirabella tower with a view. Donato al Comune dai Robecchi, heirs of Litta, today houses a school, municipal services and the archaeological museum.

Why go. The castle Visconteo-forzesco became villa dei Litta, heart of the village.
