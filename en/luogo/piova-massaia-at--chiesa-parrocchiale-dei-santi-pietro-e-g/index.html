Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Pietro e Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Attributed to Benedetto Alfieri, it was built between 1749 and 1774 on the initiative of Count Francesco Antonio Ricci, with a baroque plan with an elongated Greek cross and an octagonal tiburian dome; the bell tower (1779-1781) has five bells merged by De Giorgis in 1781. Inside there are three marble altars, twelve paintings with the Apostles of Pietro Montaldo (one dated 1824), the altarpiece of Sebastiano Taricco's Assumption and wooden stands perhaps from Sant'Andrea di Chieri. Open on Saturday 15-18 and Sunday 9.30-12.30, other days by reservation (tel. 0131 996603).

Why go. The baroque Alfierian in a hill country.
