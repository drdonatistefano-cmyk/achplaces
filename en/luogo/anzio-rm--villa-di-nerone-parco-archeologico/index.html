Villa di Nerone - Parco archeologico

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The imperial residence extends on the coast between the root of Cape of Anzio and the promontory of the Muto Arch, with phases from the II century BC to the III century AD: late-Republican aristocratic domus, then a long curved portico of Augustian age and, at the end of the II century A.D., a building with double portico on the sea over 700 meters long, a spa complex on four levels and the so-called library. The excavations of 1889 and 1929-1933 restored figurative mosaics, including Hercules and Acheloo; Nero was born in Anzio on December 15, 37 A.D.

Why go. The remains of the imperial palace on the sea.
