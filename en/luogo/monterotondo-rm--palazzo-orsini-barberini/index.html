Palazzo Orsini Barberini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built by the Orsini in the 13th century as a fortress to control the territory between Salaria and Nomentana, it was purchased in 1626 by Barberini who, at the impulse of Pope Urban VIII, transformed it into a ducal residence; then passed to the Grillo and Boncompagni-Ludovisi and today it is the seat of the Municipality. The rooms of the noble floor, decorated at the behest of Cardinal Franciotto Orsini, have the myth of Adone attributed to Girolamo Siciolante da Sermoneta (about 1553), the Stories of Joseph the Jew, frescoes by Giacinto Calandrucci and a view of the city painted by Paul Brill in 1581. The tower was rebuilt after the earthquake of Avezzano in 1915; in the courtyard a marble cistern of the sixteenth century, the Swan Garden and the Cippo-Ossario garibaldino.

Why go. Rooms frescoed by Siciolante and Paul Brill in the palace that designed the city.
