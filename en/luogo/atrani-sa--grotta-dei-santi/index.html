Grotta dei Santi

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small natural cave overlooking a lemon terrace, under the Ziro Tower: this is what remains of the Benedictine monastery of Saints Quirico and Julitta, founded in 986 by Archbishop Leo I. The walls preserve Byzantine frescoes of the 12th century with the four Evangelists.

Why go. Byzantine frescoes among lemons.

  • Where Sotto la torre dello Ziro, Atrani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
