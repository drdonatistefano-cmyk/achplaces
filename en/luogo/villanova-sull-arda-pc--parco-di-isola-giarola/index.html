Parco di Isola Giarola

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

River park linked to the Po ecosystem, with a lake born from a former quarry: after the end of the excavations the Bonifiche have reported native species in rivers and wetlands. Birdwatching, sport fishing, life and cycling trails, eco-friendly guided tours.

Why go. Birdwatching near the Po.
