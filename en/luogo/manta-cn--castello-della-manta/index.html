Castello della Manta

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

House of the Counts Saluzzo della Manta, well FAI: in the Baronial Hall the Nine Prodi and the Nine Heroines in fifteenth-century dresses and the legendary Fountain of Youth, inspired by the novel "Le chevalier errant" by Thomas III.

Why go. The heroes and the fountain of youth.
