Chiesa di Santa Maria Ausiliatrice e tela del Pagani

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It preserves the canvas of Vincenzo Pagani "Madonna enthroned with Child and Saints Giovanni Battista, Giovanni Evangelista, Maddalena and Caterina" (mystical bride of Saint Catherine), of 267 x 215 cm, which after a restoration carried out in Urbino was relocated on the right wall of the left arm of the transept. The municipal source indicates the influences of Raphael, in particular the Madonna del Pesce and Madonna di Foligno.

Why go. A great altarpiece of the Pagani of Raffaellesco taste.
