Complesso degli Agostiniani e Raccolta d'arte sacra

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

The Augustinian complex includes a library with collection from the adjacent church and a hospice; outside the bronze monument to Pope Niccolò V, linked to the origins of Fivizzano.

Why go. The convent and the pope of Fivizzano.
