Museo civico Domenico Dal Lago

Museums · Science and university

Born from the geological collection that the doctor conducted Domenico Dal Lago collected by visiting patients and donated to the City in 1929; catalogued in 1975 by Paolo Mietto of the University of Padua, it was established as a museum in 1982, opened in 1995 in Villa Valle and transferred in 1999 to Palazzo Festari. Fossils and finds of the Valle dell'Agno retrace almost 300 million years; in the garden, wooden reconstructions of a velociraptor and a pteranodon, steel silhouettes on the evolution of life and a stratigraphic pyramid. Saturday with timetables indicated by the museum, the other days upon reservation.

Why go. The geological history of the valley, with dinosaurs in the garden.
