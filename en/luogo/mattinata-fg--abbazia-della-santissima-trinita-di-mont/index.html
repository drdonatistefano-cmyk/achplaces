Abbazia della Santissima Trinità di Monte Sacro

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

On Mount Sacro (872 m), between holm oaks and mixed woods, the ruins of the abbey born from an 11th-century hermitage dependent on Santa Maria di Calena, a Benedictine priory from 1138, influential up to Bari, in decline from the fifteenth century and abandoned in the seventeenth century; remain the pronao with arches and pillars, part of the apse and the Romanesque portal. On clear days the view extends to the coast, the Umbra Forest and Monte Spigno.

Why go. Romanesque ruins in the woods with views of the Gargano.
