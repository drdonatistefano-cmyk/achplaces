Faro di Capo Spartivento

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Coastal lighthouse, visible from the outside; archaeological investigations of the last twenty years have found traces of human attendance from the age of Copper to the Greek era in the area. The MiC indicates opening every day 8-18, without reservation.

Why go. The lighthouse on the head at the extreme south of Ionic Calabria.
