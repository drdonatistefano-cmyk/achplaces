Cisternone Romano

History and architecture · Archaeology

Built in the first century BC to supply Formiae, the fish ponds and villas of Cicero and Mamurra, more than 10 meters below the village of Castellone: about 1,200 m2 and 7,000 m3 of capacity, four naves divided by 60 pillars about 6.5 meters high with pseudo-cruise vaults, from which the nickname of underground Basilica was identified with precision in 1860 by Pasquale Mattej, it was probably model for the Mirable Pool of Bacoli.

Why go. The underground basilica of Formia.
