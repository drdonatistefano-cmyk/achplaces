Villa Filangeri e Basilica Soluntina

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa built around 1600 by the family Fillangeri, now home of the Town Hall: it preserves liberty frescoes, has a staircase to two ramps and a large park with many species of Mediterranean flora. Next is the Basilica Soluntina, an example of the late Sicilian Baroque.

Why go. Villa noble with Mediterranean park.
