Museo archeologico nazionale «Matteo Sansone»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the former civic museum, he exhibits the collection of the pharmacist Matteo Sansone (1916-1992), one of the most important private collections of Puglia, donated to the state by the heirs in 2022: about 2,500 ceramic objects, metal, stone and coins especially of Daunia. Six sections: Monte Saraceno with the ... scudi" funerari in stone, the daunie stele with the schematic figures of the deceased in funeral dress, the contacts with Dalmatia and the Balkans with the ambers of Monte Tabor and Monte Saraceno, the geometric ceramics and red figures, the Hellenistic age and the finds of the Roman villa of Agnuli. Monday closed; Tuesday-Thursday 18-21, Friday-Sunday 9-13 and 18-21.

Why go. The stele daunie, unique art of ancient Apulia.
