Museo Archeologico Nazionale

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Opened in 1882 in the neoclassical villa Cassis Faraone, re-assembled in 2018 on the Roman city: mosaics such as the unsaved floor, the Pudica Venus, portraits and the Giulio-Claudio statuary cycle, with stone galleries, historical garden and deposits open to visitors with over 5,000 artifacts.

Why go. The daily life of the Roman metropolis.
