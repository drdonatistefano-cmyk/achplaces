MACUA - Museo civico d'arte contemporanea Umbro Apollonio

Museums · Modern art

Named after Umbro Apollonio, to which refer the catalogues published between 1983 and 1990, it contains about a hundred works made between 1970 and 1980, divided into three sections: cinetism and perception, module and series, color. It is dedicated to optical art, neo-constructivism, kinetic and programmed art and the collectives of the sixties and seventies; the works are exhibited in the council hall of the City Hall, with opening inaugurated on 30 November 2024. Photographic reproductions of the works are not allowed.

Why go. A collection of kinetic art and programmed inside the town hall.
