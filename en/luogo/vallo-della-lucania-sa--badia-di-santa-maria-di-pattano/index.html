Badia di Santa Maria di Pattano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Ancient monastery of Byzantine rite in the hamlet Pattano, probably founded between the 8th and the middle of the 10th century: a document from 993 already shows it owner of vast lands up to Ceraso. It flourished between the 11th and 14th centuries under Longobards, Normans and Angioini thanks to the Greek monks, then decayed and after the papal investigation of 1458 was suppressed and entrusted to commendatory abbots.

Why go. One of the Italian-Greek monasteries of Cilento.
