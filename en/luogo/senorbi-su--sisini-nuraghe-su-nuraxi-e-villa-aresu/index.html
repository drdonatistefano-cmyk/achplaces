Sisini: Nuraghe su Nuraxi e Villa Aresu

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the hamlet of Sisini the nuraghe on Nuraxi has an unusual plant, similar to that of a well temple; its Nuragic finds are exhibited at the museum sa Domu Nosta. In the hamlet also Villa Aresu, a nineteenth-century residence, and the arrival of the trot races is Cuaddus de santa Vida for the feast of Santa Vitalia.

Why go. A nuraghe with an unusual plant.
