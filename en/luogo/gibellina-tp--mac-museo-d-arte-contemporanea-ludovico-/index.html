MAC, Museo d'arte contemporanea Ludovico Corrao

Museums · Modern art

Founded in 1980 in a wing of the ground floor of the Institute including . Pope John XXIII , preserves about 1,900 pieces among paintings, graphics, sculptures and sketches; the main work is the great cycle .......... The rooms are dedicated to Realism and Abstraction of the twentieth century, Transvanguardia and Informale, photography and to the group Forma 1 (Sanfilippo, Consagra), with works among others by Arnaldo Pomodoro, Guttuso, Fontana, Accardi, Paladino, Rotella and Beuys.

Why go. The collection born from reconstruction.
