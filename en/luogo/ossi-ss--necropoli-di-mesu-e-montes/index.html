Necropoli di Mesu 'e Montes

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Eighteen domus de Janas multicellular excavated in the limestone of the southern slope of Monte Mamas, divided into two groups by an ancient tracturo and used by the Neolithic final to the middle Bronze; the tombs I and II have twelve rooms each and the decorations reproduce elements of the neolithic huts. From 12 July 2025, he was one of the 17 members of the UNESCO Domus de Janas website.

Why go. The houses of the fairies of humanity's heritage.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
