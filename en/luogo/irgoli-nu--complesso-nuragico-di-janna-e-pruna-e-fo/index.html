Complesso nuragico di Janna 'e Pruna e fonte sacra di Su Notante

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Sacred area linked to the cult of the waters on the granite ridge of Mount Senes, about 600 meters overlooking Gennargentu and Supramonte. The temple, in granite blocks well squared and dated to the Bronze final-first Iron (XII-IX century BC), has a rectangular atrium with seat and a circular cell with fireplace, inside a trapezoid temenos where the offerings were laid. The source of Su Notante (XII-X century B.C.) is built in squared basalt, non-local stone brought to the place, and the water gushes still. You can reach from Irgoli following for about 12 km the road to Mount Senes.

Why go. A water sanctuary of three thousand years in a spot landscape.
