Vigneti del Montecucco

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

The territory is part of the Montecucco denomination (red, Sangiovese, white and Vermentino); wines and oils are tasted in companies and cellars along the Montecucco Wine and Flavour Trail, and at the end of September-early October the Grape Festival brings the parade of the floats of the three districts and the open cellars.

Why go. The young wine of Maremma Amiatina.
