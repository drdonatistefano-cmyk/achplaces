Civica raccolta d'arte «Claudio Ridolfi»

Museums · Old masters

Since 2012 in the former convent of the Hermits of St. Augustine, it collects sacred art from demolished churches and suppressed orders: works of Hercules Ramazzani, Domenico Peruzzini and two of Claudio Ridolfi, a Veronese painter who died in Corinaldo in 1644, eighteen Sicilian bust reliquaries of the early seventeenth century, a silver processional cross of 1615 and a section on the Prince's Tomb, a Piceno necropolis of the VII century BC, with a princely helmet. It is part of the Cultural Places card for sale at IAT.

Why go. Corinaldo's painter and the prince's helmet.
