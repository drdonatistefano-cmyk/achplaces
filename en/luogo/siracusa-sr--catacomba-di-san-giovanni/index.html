Catacomba di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

The great early Christian cemetery; the nearby funerary area of Vigna Cassia, with a community cemetery and five private hypogeans of the III-V centuries, is visited on request to the Pontifical Commission for Sacred Archaeology.

Why go. The Syracuse of the early Christians.
