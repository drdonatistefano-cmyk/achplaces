Castello Aragonese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Designed by Ciro Ciri with Francesco di Giorgio Martini and built between 1485 and 1498 for Ferdinando I d'Aragona on the Swabian fortifications: irregular pentagonal plant surrounded by the moat, three cylindrical towers and a rampart to launch towards the sea with the 1578 ramps, the coats of arms of Antonio de Mendoza and Pedro de Toledo and the great coat of arms of Carlo V on the portal.

Why go. The fortress on the moat after the Turkish siege.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Otranto
  • Entry €12, reduced €10 (adults) and €8 (under 18)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
