Museo etnografico «Ossimo Ieri»

Museums · Themed

Set up in the old parish house of Ossimo Superiore, it reconstructs the life of the early twentieth century and restores the value of the tools that accompanied the life of mountain people, in relation to the land and the surrounding nature. It is part of the Museum System of Valle Camonica.

Why go. The mountain life of the early twentieth century.

visits only by reservation (Comune di Ossimo, tel. 0364 41100)
