Cripta di San Pietro e Alba sotterranea

History and architecture · Archaeology

The Diocesan Museum in the crypt with the lapidary from the Roman to the late Middle Ages, from which you reach the underground environments with the baptismal font of the sixth century; guided tours to the remains of Alba Pompeia.

Why go. The city under the city.
