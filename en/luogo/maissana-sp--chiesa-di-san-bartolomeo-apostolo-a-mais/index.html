Chiesa di San Bartolomeo Apostolo a Maissana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish already existing before 1225, which became a prevostura in 1823, vicariate in 1838 and archpretura in 1923; on October 11, 1959 it passed from the diocese of Chiavari to that of Spezia. St. Bartholomew is celebrated on 24 August and Our Lady of Carmine is celebrated on 16 July.

Why go. The mother church of the village, with thirteenth-century roots.
