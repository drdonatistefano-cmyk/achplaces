Santuario della Fortuna Primigenia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built towards the end of the second century BC, it was famous throughout the Roman world for the oracle of the lotes: it climbs on the slopes of Mount Ginestro in terraces, ramps and symmetrical staircases on Hellenistic models, up to the round temple with the statue of the goddess; in the center the Well of the Sorti. It is considered the largest late-republican complex in ancient Italy and its remains envelop the medieval village; the upper part is incorporated in Palazzo Barberini and you can visit with the museum.

Why go. The terraced sanctuary that goes up with the country.
