Chiesa Madre di Tutti i Santi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Considered one of the most original works of Baroque Lecce, it had the present form by the Mesagnese priest-architect Francesco Capodieci; the origins, uncertain, would date back to the fourteenth or fifteenth century. The three-order façade has niches with the apostles, a bas-relief of the Madonna del Carmine and, above the portal, statues of Saint Eleutery, Anthia and Corebo. Inside, with a Latin cross with chapels and altars in finely carved stone, the Adoration of the Shepherds began by Gian Pietro Zullo and completed by Andrea Cunavi and Domenico Pinca, and a Madonna of Carmine by the Neapolitan Giuseppe Bonito.

Why go. The Baroque masterpiece of Capodieci, with a canvas by Giuseppe Bonito.
