Ex convento dei Cappuccini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The Capuchin friars settled in Caraglio in 1611, restored the churches in ruins and distinguished themselves during the plague of 1630. The former convent, renovated, is the seat of the Cultural Association Marcovaldo; next to the former Romanesque church of Saints Peter and Paul with wooden altar, under restoration.

Why go. The monastery of the friars of the plague, today a place of culture.

The MiC card indicates closed every day; former church of the SS. Pietro e Paolo in phase of restoration according to the Municipality
