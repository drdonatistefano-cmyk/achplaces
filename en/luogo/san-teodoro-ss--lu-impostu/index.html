Lu Impostu

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches

Beach about one kilometer long on the promontory of Capo Coda Cavallo, near Cala Brandinchi, with white and fine sand, dunes with juniper, mimosa and marine lilies and a low bottom that gently descends, suitable for children. It is part of the marine protected area of Tavolara and Capo Coda Cavallo; behind is the second lagoon of San Teodoro, connected to the sea by a canal. It has access for people with disabilities, ample parking also for campers, refreshment points and rentals.

Why go. Low water and comfortable facilities, ideal with children.
