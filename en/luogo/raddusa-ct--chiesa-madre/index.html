Chiesa Madre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1850 on a project by the architect Giuseppe Maggiore, in the town. It is appreciated for its stuccoes and decorations in Corinthian style.

Why go. The nineteenth-century stuccoes of the main church of the village.
