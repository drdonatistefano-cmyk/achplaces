Museo della ceramica del Borgo Castello

Museums · Themed

Established on 29 September 2008 in the restored buildings of Borgo Castello, the castle complex destroyed by the earthquake of 1694, then inhabited village and municipal property after the earthquake of 1980. The route goes from the finds of the Fossakultur of Oliveto-Cairano and of Roman age to the half medieval majolica and the Renaissance majolica, up to the production of the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, to the historical bricks, industrial ceramics and the works of contemporary craftsmen.

Why go. The ceramics of the High Ofanto from prehistory to today.
