Palazzo Ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage

The first building built by Vespasiano Gonzaga, overlooking Piazza Ducale, was built in two phases (1556 and 1590) on four levels and rebuilt in 1561 after a fire; it was the centre of public life of the small State. You can visit the Sala del Duca d'Alba, rich in wood and gold with the fireplace supported by pink marble lions, and the Sala delle Aquile with the four surviving wooden equestrian statues of the Riding (1587).

Why go. The equestrian statues of the Gonzaga in the palace of the duke.
