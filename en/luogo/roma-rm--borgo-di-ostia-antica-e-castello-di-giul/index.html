Borgo di Ostia Antica e Castello di Giulio II

History and architecture · Villages

The medieval fortified village, Gregoriopoli from the IX century, with the terraced houses of Cardinal d'Estouteville and the castle of Baccio Pontelli (1483-1487) for Cardinal Giuliano della Rovere.

Why go. A Renaissance castle at the sea.
