Chiesa e Chiostro di San Domenico

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Church and convent were started in 1456 and are among the oldest buildings in the historic center; from the seventeenth century they had various modifications, including the Baroque facade, while the ancient bell tower remained unchanged. The former Dominican monastery now houses the Town House and the Leoncavallo Museum. In the church an altarpiece with Dominican saints of Neapolitan workshop (XVII century) and an eighteenth-century painting of San Domenico resurrecting Napoleon Orsini.

Why go. The fifteenth-century complex in the heart of the country.
