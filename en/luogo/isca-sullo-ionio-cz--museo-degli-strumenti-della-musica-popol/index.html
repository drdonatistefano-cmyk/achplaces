Museo degli strumenti della musica popolare calabrese

Museums · Themed

It is based at the Conservatory of Popular Music in Calabria .Antonio Procopio and is managed by the ARPA, a consortium that studies the folk traditions of Calabria. It exhibits over 60 traditional instruments all working, including aerophones, cords, members and idiophones; library and media library with about 200 hours of audio, 100 of video and over 120,000 photos. Phone 0967 45357.

Why go. The sounds of the Calabrian tradition.

MiC card (updated in 2020) indicates "Closed" every day of the week; call before the visit
