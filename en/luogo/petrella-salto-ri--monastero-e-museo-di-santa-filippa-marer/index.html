Monastero e Museo di Santa Filippa Mareri

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

St. Philipp, who met St. Francis between 1221 and 1225, founded in 1228 the monastery of St. Peter de Molito, submerged in 1940 by the lake together with the old Borgo San Pietro: church and monastery were dismantled and rebuilt stone for higher stone, and the chapel preserves frescoes from the sixteenth century and the reliquary with the heart of the saint. The museum, opened in 1977 and re-assembled in 2000, exhibits capitals, coats of arms and bells from the 12th to the 18th century, the wooden portal of 1511 by Maestro Giacomo di Bernardino, a wooden Madonna of the 14th to the 15th century, the statue of Saint Filippa of the 15th century, tools of pharmacy and leather trunks of the Clarisse, as well as paintings by Giorgio De Chirico, including a self-portrait of 1960. Open every day 8.30-12.30 and 16-18; tel. 0746 558134.

Why go. A monastery saved from the waters, with a De Chirico.
