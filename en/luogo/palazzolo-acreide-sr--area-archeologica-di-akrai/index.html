Area archeologica di Akrai

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Akrai, the oldest sub-colony of Syracuse, was founded in 664 B.C. on the hill of the Chromon. Theater (between III and II century BC according to sources) and the bouleuterion, the decuman with lava flooring, a circular spa building, the foundations of a temple of the second half of the 6th century BC considered of Aphrodite, the side-shaped of the Carved and the Carnival with catacombs and early Christian hypogeans and the Feral Temples are visited.

Why go. A Greek theatre in a solemn landscape.
