La Mozartina

Museums · House museums

In an eighteenth-century building, the house of the composer master Canciani houses on three floors a collection of musical instruments that illustrates the history of organ, harpsichord and piano, with eighteenth-century manuscripts of unpublished music and rare ancient books; the tour is guided by his son Daniel Canciani. It is a section of the Ecomuseum Mistirs; visits by reservation (0433 70162).

Why go. Three centuries of keyboards in a private house.
