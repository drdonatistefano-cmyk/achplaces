Grancia di Tecchiena

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The Protocenobio di San Sebastiano, a large barn of the Carthusians of Tsulti, considered the best example of 18th century architecture of Alatri; it contains the small church of San Bartolomeo apostolo and is on the Via Francigena del Sud.

Why go. The Carthusian barn on the Francigena.
