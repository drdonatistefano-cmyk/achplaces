Museo del grano

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the ancient courthouse of the Serra family, on the main road to Guasila: objects on the cultivation and use of wheat, many donated by the inhabitants, with rooms divided between the male work in the fields and the female work of the bakery and weaving; stands out a rare wooden wheel instead of stone. A room features the film of the early 1960s by Danish scholar Weis Bentzon on sowing, working and collecting, and a section recalls the launeddas player Dionigi Burranca, who died in Ortacesus in 1995. Workshops and the route "The way of bread" with tasting; open all year round, from Tuesday to Sunday 9-13 and 16-19, free for over 65 and under 5.

Why go. The cycle of wheat and bread in the house of a peasant family.

  • Where Casa Serra, Ortacesus
  • Entry €2, reduced €1,50 (groups over 10) and €1 (schools)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
