Museo dell'Infiorata

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated on April 26, 2003 for the feast of Our Lady of the Heart: the central room exhibits historical documents and ancient tools of the master inflorers, with the techniques and flowers used over time; another room collects the photographic archive of inflorate of Gerano, Italy and abroad, and a section is dedicated to traditional crafts and archaeological finds.

Why go. The behind the scenes of the oldest infiorata in Italy.
