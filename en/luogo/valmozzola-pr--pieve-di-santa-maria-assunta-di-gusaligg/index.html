Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta di Gusaliggio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the 11th century, rebuilt in the Baroque period, with interior renovated in neo-Gothic style and neo-Romanesque façade of 1965; it contains frescoes, a altarpiece of the Assumption and eighteenth-century statues.

Why go. The ancient parish church under the fortress.
