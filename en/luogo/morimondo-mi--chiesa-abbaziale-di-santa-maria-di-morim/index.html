Chiesa abbaziale di Santa Maria di Morimondo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

First Cistercian Abbey of Lombardy, founded by monks who came from Morimond in 1134 and built between 1182 and 1296 in Cistercian style already evolved towards the Gothic: exposed bricks, no unnecessary decoration, facade with portal, rose window and double window and some deliberately asymmetric elements. Inside a fresco by Bernardino Luini, a 15th century crucifix, a stack of 14th century holy water and the wooden choir by Francesco Giramo (1522).

Why go. The simplicity of Cistercian brick in the Milanese countryside.
