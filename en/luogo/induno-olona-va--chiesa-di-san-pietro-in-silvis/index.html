Chiesa di San Pietro in Silvis

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque building probably of the 11th century, restored and enlarged in Renaissance age, when the bell tower was erected; the sacristy is of the late 16th century. It was the parish church of Induno until the construction of St. John the Baptist. The hut façade preserves in part the original plaster frescoed with the Virgin, Child and saints; the portal with bezel is made of Viggiu stone and inside remain precious frescoes, marked by time. Open for Fall 2020 and Spring 2022 FAI Days.

Why go. The oldest church in the village, with frescoes stratified over the centuries.
