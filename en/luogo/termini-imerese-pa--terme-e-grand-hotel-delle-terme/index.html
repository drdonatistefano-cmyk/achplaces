Terme e Grand Hotel delle Terme

Local life and traditions · Spas

The spa of the seventeenth century rises on the ruins of an older one and uses hyperthermal volcanic waters at almost 43 °C for baths and mud; in the nineteenth century there was added the Grand Hotel delle Terme, neoclassic, designed by Damiani Almejda, loved by the Palermo nobility and base of Ignazio Florio during the Targa Florio.

Why go. The warm waters that gave the city its name.
