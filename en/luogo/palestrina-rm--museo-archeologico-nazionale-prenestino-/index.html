Museo archeologico nazionale prenestino e Mosaico del Nilo

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Since 1956 in Palazzo Barberini, built on the cavea of the sanctuary: on the ground floor the cryptoporic with sculptures and inscriptions, on the first floor the materials of the cult of Fortuna (the colossal statue of Iside-Fortuna, the relief Grimani of Augustan age), the second mirrors and bronze cysts and votive terracotta, at the last the great Mosaic of the polychrome Nile, view of an Egyptian landscape during the flood, among the major Hellenistic mosaics preserved. At the entrance, a 1:50 scale of the shrine. Touring hours: every day 9-20.

Why go. The Nile Mosaic.

  • Where Palazzo Barberini, Piazza della Cortina, Palestrina
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
