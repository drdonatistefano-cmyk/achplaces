Grotte gialle

History and architecture · Archaeology

Etruscan tombs in the countryside, excavated in limestone, an ancient seabed of about one million years old, with an ear of rabbit and two V-shaped chambers divided by a false column; remodelled in more recent times. Frequent guided tours, especially in summer, with the Proloco di Bibbona.

Why go. Etruscan tombs carved in a fossil bottom.
