MAR, Museo d'Arte della città

Museums · Modern art

Pinacoteca from the fourteenth to the twentieth century and exhibitions in the Loggetta Lombardesca, the 16th century cloister of Santa Maria in Porto, with a section dedicated to contemporary mosaic.

Why go. To see the mosaic as today's art language.

  • Entry To be checked on the website of the museum
