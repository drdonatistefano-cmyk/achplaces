Chiesa dei Santi Nicola e Clemente e piazza Umberto I

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Overlooking the panoramic Piazza Umberto I, built in 1589 according to the date on the bell tower: it is one of the few Renaissance churches of Abruzzo with a basilica plan, with a six-arched porch and portal and rose window decorated with angel heads. Inside a wooden pulpit with statues of 17th century saints attributed to Bartholomew Balcony and, in the sacristy, a silver Madonna of Nicola da Guardiagrele; behind the high altar the urn of the Holy Child, arrived from Jerusalem in 1760, celebrated on the third Sunday of September. Since 2015 it has been dedicated to the Child Jesus.

Why go. Abruzzo renaissance on the panoramic square.
