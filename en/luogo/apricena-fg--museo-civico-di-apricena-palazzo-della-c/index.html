Museo civico di Apricena (Palazzo della cultura)

Museums · City history

Born from the collaboration between Archeoclub d'Italia, Municipality and Ente Parco Nazionale del Gargano, it is located in the eighteenth-century Palazzo Paolicelli, public building of the historical center restored with funds of the Park and renamed "Palazzo della cultura" (Palace of Culture), opened in March 2010. It exhibits palaeontological and medieval finds; on the whole also the municipal library, the historical archive, a media library on Frederick II and a thematic museum on the Middle Ages and the Emperor.

Why go. Fossil, Middle Ages and Frederick II in an eighteenth-century palace.
