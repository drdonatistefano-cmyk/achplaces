Selinunte, Collina orientale e templi E, F, G

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the eastern hill rise the E temple, dedicated to Hera, of the middle of the fifth century BC and largely reassembled in the 1950s and 1960s; the F temple of the middle of the sixth century BC, perhaps dedicated to Dionysus; and the G temple, begun around 530 BC and never ended up destroying the city, among the greatest in the Greek world. The park, which the Region presents as the largest in Europe with 270 hectares, includes acropolis, necropolis and three hills.

Why go. The most imposing Doric temples of Selinunte.
