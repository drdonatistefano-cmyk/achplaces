Casa museo Omu Axiu

Museums · Themed

A manor house of more than 1,200 square meters, also called Casa Vargiu, of the Vargiu family since the sixteenth century, today also a hotel spread: on the ground floor the environments of everyday life and the peasant trades of Sarcidano (tool room, bread and sausage storage, bedroom), on the upper floor the embroidering museum with clothes and family accessories. Paid access with guided tour included, at reception and restaurant times.

Why go. The life of a manor house in Sarcidano.
