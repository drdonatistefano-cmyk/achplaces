Golf Club Garlenda

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

18-hole par 72 course founded in 1964 on the idea of John Morrison and John Harris, in the valley of the Lerrone near Albenga and Alassio: the trail alternates views, pines, farmhouses and centuries-old olive trees.

Why go. Golf among centuries-old olive trees a few kilometers from the sea.
