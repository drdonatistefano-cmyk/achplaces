Palazzo Salmatoris

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Frescoed by Sebastiano Taricco, the Sabauda residence where the peace of 1631 was signed, the Shroud was guarded in 1706 and Napoleon dictated the surrender to Vittorio Amedeo III; today it is the seat of art exhibitions.

Why go. The Peace Palace.
