Museo della Centuriazione Romana

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the civic center designed by Aldo Rossi, it tells the Roman centuriation of the territory between Villa del Conte and Camposampiero, bordered by the Muson Vecchio, the territory of Mirano and the Brenta: from the imposition of the agricultural grid to agriculture, breeding, trade and domestic life, to the transformations followed by barbaric invasions. All the finds come from the centuriate area, including those of the recent excavations in Borgoricco with the Superintendence; among the most valuable pieces the bronze strigili and the architectural slabs in terracotta decorated with vegetable bas-relief, considered unique. Guided visits, workshops for schools, temporary exhibitions and activities with the University of Padua. Closed on Mondays; reduced for schools, 7-14 year olds and over 65 years olds.

Why go. Understand how Rome designed the Padua countryside.
