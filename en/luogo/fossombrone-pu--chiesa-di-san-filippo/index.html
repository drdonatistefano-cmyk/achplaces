Chiesa di San Filippo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

17th century church with a single nave with unfinished façade and baroque interior of white stuccoes, gilded and frescoes; altarpiece by Giovanni Francesco Guerrieri, works by Ridolfi and Diamantini and stuccoes by Tommaso Amantini. It hosts concerts and events.

Why go. A baroque box.
