Fortezza Medicea

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built in 1261 by the Republic of Siena, several times rebuilt, the last time by Antonio da Sangallo the Elder, from which the name Medici; restored in 1885.

Why go. Close to Piazza Grande.
