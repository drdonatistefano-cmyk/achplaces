Cantina Val San Martino

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Founded in 1957, it is indicated as the first winery born in the whole province of Bergamo; it has vineyards in Almenno, Pontida, Palazzago, Mapello and Scanzo, cultivated mainly in Merlot, Cabernet, Manzoni and Chardonnay. It produces red Valcalepio and IGT San Martino, a Blanc de Blancs of Chardonnay only with charmat method in autoclave and, since the end of 2021, a red from Merlot vineyards with more than 25 years. For visits and tastings contact the cellar (tel. 035 795035).

Why go. The wines of the San Martino Valley at the oldest winery in Bergamasca.
