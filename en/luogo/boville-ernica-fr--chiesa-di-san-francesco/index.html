Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Today's exhibition room, it contains frescoes from the 13th to the 15th century, some of Byzantine style, and a fourteenth-century cycle attributed to a student of Giotto; next to the monastery of San Francesco and the Torre Bulgarini.

Why go. Medieval frescoes of the Giottesca school.
