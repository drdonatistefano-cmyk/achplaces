Chiesa del Santissimo Sacramento

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Wanted in 1684 by the Venerable Company of the Blessed Sacrament and today museum space; the restoration of 1994 brought to light a fragment of Roman mosaic of the first century believed to come from Carsulae and recovered a Last Supper of the eighteenth century.

Why go. A Roman mosaic inside a baroque church.
