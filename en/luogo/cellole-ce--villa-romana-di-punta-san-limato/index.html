Villa romana di Punta San Limato

History and architecture · Archaeology

Maritime villa of Julian-Claudia age (1st century A.D.), with reconstructions in brick from the 2nd century, partly incorporated in the eighteenth century farmhouse of San Limato. At the lower level a cryptoportico in mixed work; to the north the spa district with frigidarium in black and white mosaic with marine animals, tepidarium and calidarium; to the south three residential areas with niches, apses and mosaic and marble floors.

Why go. Roman mosaics on the sea of Sinuessa.

opens only in extraordinary openings scheduled with the Municipality (in 2025 on Saturdays of July and August, 18:30-20:30); visits on request via PEC to the Superintendence
