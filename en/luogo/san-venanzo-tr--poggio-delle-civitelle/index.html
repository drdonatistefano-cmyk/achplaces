Poggio delle Civitelle

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The excavations have returned Etruscan materials: the site seems to have been a defensive outpost of Orvieto and a weapons production center, judging by the large amount of iron found.

Why go. An Etruscan outpost on the Peglia.
