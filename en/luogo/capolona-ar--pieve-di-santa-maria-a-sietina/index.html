Pieve di Santa Maria a Sietina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Near the shore of the Arno, along an ancient Roman road, it is the most important sacred building of Capolona; the name would call Satres, the Etruscan Saturn, to which a temple was dedicated here. After an early Christian church, the present Romanesque parish church with three aisles and three apses dates back to the 13th century. It preserves two cycles of frescoes: one of about 1370-1380 attributed to the Master of Pieve in Sietina and one of the nineties of the fifteenth century, of followers of Lorentino d'Andrea influenced by Piero della Francesca, commissioned by the family of merchants Bacci Arezzo.

Why go. A Romanesque church on the Arno with Gothic and Renaissance frescoes from Piero's school.
