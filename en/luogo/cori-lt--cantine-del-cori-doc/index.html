Cantine del Cori DOC

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Along the route "Wine," Oil and Flavours Trail you can visit the wineries Marco Carpineti, Cincinnato and Pietrapinta; among the local specialties the pizzerias and Cori DOC wines.

Why go. Taste the wine of the Lepini where it is born.
