Chiesa Madre Natività di Maria SS.

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises next to the central square of the village, founded in the early eighteenth century with the licentia populandi granted in 1688 by Charles II; the building was born from a warehouse of an ancient farm together with the pre-existing sixteenth-century church of the SS. Crucified. The façade is marked by two large sandstone pilasters, with a tympanum portal and a small rose window; the 17th century square bell tower in carved stone, originally in two orders, was raised with the civic clock. Inside, with a single nave, the ancient main altar with a tabernacle in polychrome marble and, in the niche above, the eighteenth-century wooden statue of the Madonna delle Grazie; altar and modern marble ambos are brothers Giacomo and Lucio Alberghina of Caltagirone.

Why go. The mother church was born from a farm, with the seventeenth-century bell tower on the square.
