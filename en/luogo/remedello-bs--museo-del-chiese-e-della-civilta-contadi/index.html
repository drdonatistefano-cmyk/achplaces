Museo del Chiese e della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Collection of tools, furnishings and objects of the peasant life belonged to Giovanni Filippini, expert in the agricultural traditions of the low Brescia, today owned by the Municipality; among the pieces stands out a nineteenth century sieve to separate the seeds. The museum is dedicated to Giovanni Bonsignori, priest, educator and agronomist. Visits by reservation.

Why go. The life of the fields of the low Brescia.
