Civico Museo Archeologico (CMAC)

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In Palazzo Tori Massoni, exhibits artifacts from prehistory to the Middle Ages, with interactive and tactile places including prehistoric instruments and the cast of a Neanderthal femur; the museum project connects it to the Grotto to the Wave, to the Roman villa of Via Acquarella and to the medieval settlement of Montecastrense. Open from Tuesday to Friday in the morning, Saturday and Sunday morning and afternoon; closed on Monday. Also reduced with Carnet Versilia Museums or Edumusei Card.

Why go. From Neanderthals to the Middle Ages, with artifacts to touch.

  • Where Via Vittorio Emanuele, Camaiore
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
