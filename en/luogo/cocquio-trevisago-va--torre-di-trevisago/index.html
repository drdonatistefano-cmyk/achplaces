Torre di Trevisago

History and architecture · Towers

Remains of a tower originally over fifteen meters high, probably of the 11th century, which gives the name to the hamlet Tower: military outpost to control people and goods in transit from Seprio to the subalpine localities, signaled the dangers to the other garrisons with fires visible from afar. It was destroyed and rebuilt several times, it was further damaged during the Italian Wars (1494-1559); not far away stood the church of San Giorgio with a small cemetery, last documented in 1758. Reported in the FAI census

Why go. A medieval ruin of surveillance on the path of the Seprio.
