Villa Alari Visconti di Saliceto

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa of U delight designed by Giovanni Ruggeri for Giacinto Alari, with works from 1702 to about 1721 and chapel of St. Teresa blessed in 1725; garden towards the Naviglio. In 1772-1776 the governor of Lombardy resided there, Archduke Ferdinand of Habsburg, with Beatrice d'Este; since 1831 of the Visconti of Saliceto, in 1948 he passed to the Fatebenefratelli and has been public since 2007. Hall of music, gallery and ballroom have frescoes and golden stuccoes by Giovan Angelo Borroni, Francesco Fabbrica and (attributes) Pietro Maggi; in 2014 it was the most voted place in Lombardy at the ...
