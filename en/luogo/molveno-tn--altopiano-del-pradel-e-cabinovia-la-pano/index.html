Altopiano del Pradel e cabinovia La Panoramica

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

The cable car ...La Panoramica is usually open from May to October and climbs to the Pradel plateau at 1,367 metres, with views of the lake and the village; from here a 20-minute path (or a chairlift) leads to the La Montanara mountain hut, and starts the trails towards Cima Paganella.

Why go. The lake seen from above.
