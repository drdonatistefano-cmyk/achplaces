Nuraghe Diana

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The most beautiful of the 38 Nuragic settlements in the territory, on a promontory on the Gulf of Cagliari above the beach of Capitana and Is Mortorius, guarding the Nuragic Harbour: central tower with false tholos dome, two minor towers connected and courtyard closed by a wall, with entrance to the Dolmenic system; dated to the middle of the II millennium BC, dug from the fifties, in the second world war the tower was reinforced as a viewing post.

Why go. A nuraghe overlooking the sea.
