Chiesa di Sant'Urbano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was born from the Benedictine monastery of Santa Maria Maggiore built in 1034 by the abbot of San Liberatore in Maiella and built by Saint Aldemario da Capua, in a dominant position towards the north-east; the relics of Aldemario are venerated in the medieval crypt. Tradition has it that in the 14th century a dream of St Urban has solved without blood a siege of the Teatini: from here the Feast of the Bandersi, in May, re-enactment that lasts for about seven centuries.

Why go. The church of the legend of the Bandersi.
