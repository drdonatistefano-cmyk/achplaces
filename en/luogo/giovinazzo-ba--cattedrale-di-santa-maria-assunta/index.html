Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals

12th century cathedral in Piazza Duomo, in the area of the old port, which can be reached by passing under the arches of Via Cathedral. Only the apse retains the original Romanesque system: the rest was largely renovated in Baroque style in the eighteenth century. The best preserved part is the crypt, remained as it was, supported by 10 columns with unusual Roman capitals and 12 pillars that protrude from the perimeter walls; the church preserves the Byzantine icon of the Madonna of Corsignano.

Why go. The Romanesque silhouette that stands on the marina, with an intact crypt.
