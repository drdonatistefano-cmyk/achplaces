Chiesetta dei Santi Gervasio e Protasio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

On a rocky promontory at 956 meters above the village, mentioned in documents since 1598; there lived the "Romèt" - a hermit who watched the fires and sounded the alarm bell.

Why go. The country seen from above.
