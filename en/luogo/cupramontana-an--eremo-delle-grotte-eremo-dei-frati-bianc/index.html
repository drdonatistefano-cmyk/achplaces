Eremo delle Grotte (Eremo dei Frati Bianchi)

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

It takes its name from the white saio of the Camaldolese, who lived for over four centuries, and is considered one of the most significant testimonies of religious settlement of the origins in the Marches. Here were laid the foundations of the Congregation of Monte Corona and in the 16th century there found refuge two minor friars who founded the Order of the Capuchins; the caves also served as a hiding place for the friars persecuted by the Inquisition. The cellars can only be visited in a group.

Why go. A hermitage dug into the rock where the Capuchins were born.
