Chiesa di Santa Giuliana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Documented in 1237, rebuilt and consecrated in 1452, it preserves the triptych of the Trinity made of polychrome and golden wood by Georg Artzt (1517), among the most precious wooden works of Trentino, although some statues are copies.

Why go. One of the wooden masterpieces of Trentino.
