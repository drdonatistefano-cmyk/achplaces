Chiesa di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A place of worship already in the 12th century, rebuilt in the 16th century and rebuilt in Baroque forms in the 17th century with frescoes; it has a marble portal of Vezza of 1748 and a side in white marble of 1902, and was consecrated to Saint Martin of Tours in 1921.

Why go. Eight centuries of history in a baroque mountain church.
