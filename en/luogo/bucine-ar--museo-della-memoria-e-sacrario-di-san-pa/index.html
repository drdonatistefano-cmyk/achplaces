Museo della memoria e Sacrario di San Pancrazio

Museums · City history

Opened on 29 June 2007 in the cellars of the former Palazzo del Podestà, where on 29 June 1944 German soldiers of the armored division Hermann Göring gathered and killed the inhabitants of San Pancrazio before setting the building on fire. It documents the massacre with photos of the Canadian Military Archive and a historical map of the massacres of Nazis between Valdichiana and Valdambra.
