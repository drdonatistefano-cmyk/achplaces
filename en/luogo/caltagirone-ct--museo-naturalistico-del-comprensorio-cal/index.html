Museo naturalistico del Comprensorio Calatino – Bosco di Santo Pietro

Museums · Science and university

Ecomuseum and center visit in the hamlet of Santo Pietro dedicated to the geological history of Sicily and the ecosystem of the forest, protected by the nature reserve oriented Bosco di Santo Pietro.
