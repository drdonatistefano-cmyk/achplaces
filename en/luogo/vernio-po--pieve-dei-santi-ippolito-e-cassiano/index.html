Pieve dei Santi Ippolito e Cassiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church, the most important church of the feud of Vernio, documented since 998 and in the current forms probably of the XII century, with medieval facade and porch on columns of the XVI-XVII century. In the single aisle baroque altars in sandstone, a Deposition of 1579 by Giovanni Bizzelli with the coat of arms of Cardinal Girolamo de' Bardi and a Madonna of the seventeenth-century Rosary; in canonical a Adoration of the Shepherds of Girolamo Ristori.

Why go. The Romanesque parish church of the Bardi's manor.
