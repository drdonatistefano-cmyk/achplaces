Protoconvento Francescano

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Founded in 1220 by Pietro Cathin, today a multipurpose centre of culture and art with the Archaeological Museum and the Sybaris Theatre, home to the festival "Primavera dei Teatri" .

Why go. Culture and theatre in a medieval convent.
