Chiesa di San Giusto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

The main church of the village, in white marble and in a raised position, was built from the 15th century where there was a church dedicated to St. John; the current structure with three naves was completed in 1745, the neo-medieval façade dates back to 1884 and the frescoes by Michele Marcucci in the early twentieth century. Next to the bell tower and the square, from which you can see the Piana di Lucca; the patron festival falls on Monday after Pentecost.

Why go. The view of the plain from the churchyard, especially at sunset.
