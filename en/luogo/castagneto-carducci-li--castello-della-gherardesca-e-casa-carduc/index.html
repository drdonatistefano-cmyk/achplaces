Castello della Gherardesca e Casa Carducci

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The castle built around the year 1000 with the church of San Lorenzo and the terrace belvedere; in the village the house where Carducci lived, today museum.

Why go. The viewpoint of Piazza del Popolo.
