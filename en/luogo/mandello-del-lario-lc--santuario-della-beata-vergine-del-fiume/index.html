Santuario della Beata Vergine del Fiume

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In the lower part of the village, it was born from a miracle of the seventeenth century: after a flood of Meria the waters deposited a lot of wheat, carried away by a mill, in front of an image of the Madonna with the Child painted on the wall of a vineyard. To welcome the image the archpriest Giovanni Battista Sambuca promoted the construction with the offerings of the community; the interior has a rich Baroque decorative apparatus.

Why go. The baroque sanctuary was born from a flood of the stream.
