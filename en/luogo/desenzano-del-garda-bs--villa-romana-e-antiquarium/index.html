Villa Romana e Antiquarium

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Villa built in several phases between the 1st century BC and the 4th AD, one of the most important late ancient villas in northern Italy, famous for the polychrome mosaic floors with pagan scenes; the client of the last phase could be Flavio Magno Decenzio, brother of the emperor Magnenzio, perhaps at the origin of the name of the city. The Antiquarium exhibits a second century marble Hercules, a late 4th century glass cup with Christ and the rooster and a teenage bust. Tuesday-Saturday 9-19.30, Sunday 14.10-19.30, Monday closed; cumulative ticket with Caves of Catullo and Castle of Sirmione €14 and annual pass Garda Heritage €21.

Why go. The mosaics of the villa that perhaps gave the name to the city.

  • Where Via Crocefisso 22, Desenzano del Garda
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
