Collegiata di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The main church of Caldarola, inaugurated in 1590 with the bubble of Sixto V and then elevated to Collegiata distinguished; overlooking the square next to Pallotta Palace.

Why go. The mother church of the little Urbe.
