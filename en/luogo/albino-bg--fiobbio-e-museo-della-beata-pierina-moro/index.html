Fiobbio e Museo della beata Pierina Morosini

Museums · Themed

Fraction on the slopes of Mount Misma with origins in the twelfth century and the church of the Holy Trinity of the fifteenth century. The young Pierina Morosini, beatified in 1987, rests in the parish of Sant'Antonio da Padova; a small exhibition space is dedicated to her life.

Why go. The village and the memory of Blessed Pierina.
