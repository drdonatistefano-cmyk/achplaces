Museo della Città

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the former Jesuit college, the history of Rimini from prehistory to the twentieth century, with the painting of the fourteenth century.

Why go. The richest museum in the province.

  • Where Via Tonini 1, Rimini
  • Entry €7 full, €5 reduced, €2 from 18 to 25 years (with the Surgeon's Domus)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.museicomunalirimini.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
