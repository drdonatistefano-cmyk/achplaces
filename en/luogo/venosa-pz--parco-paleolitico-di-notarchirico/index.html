Parco paleolitico di Notarchirico

History and architecture · Archaeology

Site of 670-695,000 years ago with nine levels of excavation: the skull with tusks of an ancient elephant, a fragment of femur of Homo heidelbergensis, the oldest human presence in Italy, and the most ancient Acheulean bifacials of Western Europe.

Why go. The first men of Italy.

Open only on educational occasions or events, by reservation, waiting for the end of the work
