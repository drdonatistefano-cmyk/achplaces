Museo civico di scienze naturali

Museums · Science and university

Established in 1983 and opened in 1989, it has its core in the ornithological collection begun by Angelo Priolo in 1939: about 2,000 specimens of about 400 species, precious for the study of Sicilian birdfauna, with extinct species on the island such as the Francoline and the latea. The geological collection of Luigi Lino includes fossils, minerals, rocks and shells especially Sicilian, pleistocene fauna, volcanic products of Etna and fossil fish; the diorama of the valley of the Rosemary stream stands out, from which the griffins disappeared in 1965 by poisoning and where they were re-introduced with LIPU and Nebrodi Park.

Why go. The birds of Sicily, even those disappeared.

The MiC card (updated to March 2021) is closed every day; tel. 095 921615
