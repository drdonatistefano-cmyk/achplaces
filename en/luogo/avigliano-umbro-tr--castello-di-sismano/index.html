Castello di Sismano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Medieval castle recognizable for the two imposing side towers; in the village the church of Sant'Andrea Corsini preserves paintings attributed to the Polinori.

Why go. A castle towered in the countryside between Todi and Amelia.
