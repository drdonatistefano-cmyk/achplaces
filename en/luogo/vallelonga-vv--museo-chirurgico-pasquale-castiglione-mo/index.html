Museo chirurgico Pasquale Castiglione Morelli

Museums · Science and university

The museum is a first attempt to reconstruct a part of the health history of South Italy at the beginning of the twentieth century through the story of a hospital in a small town of the Calabrian Serre. Booking not required; MiC (2020) does not report up-to-date times.

Why go. The story of a village hospital.
