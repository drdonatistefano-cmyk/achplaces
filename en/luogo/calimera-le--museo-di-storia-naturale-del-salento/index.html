Museo di Storia Naturale del Salento

Museums · Science and university

Founded in 1982 and managed by the Naturalia Cooperative, it has sections of paleontology and paleoanthropology (vegetable and animal fossils even millions of years), entomology, herpetology and ornithology (with live animals), mineralogy and malacology, mammology, embryology and teratology, as well as aquariums and terrariums. Inside the Provincial Fauna Observatory recovers and rehabilitates injured wild animals, including sea turtles and raptors. Hours: Tuesday-Friday 9-13 and 15-17, Saturday and Sunday 10-17, Monday closed.

Why go. Fossils, live animals and a fauna recovery center in a single museum.
