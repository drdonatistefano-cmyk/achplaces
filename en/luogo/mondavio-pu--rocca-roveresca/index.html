Rocca Roveresca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Designed by the Sienese Francesco di Giorgio Martini for Giovanni della Rovere and built between 1482 and 1492, it was never attached: inclined walls to divert the blows, ten-sided mast with entrance to the third floor; seen from above a part resembles a crossbow. In the moat are exposed rebuilt siege machines in real size (catapults, overflows, bombs).

Why go. The Renaissance war machine never used.
