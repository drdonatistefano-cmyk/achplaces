Museo dell'agricoltura del Piemonte

Museums · Themed

Supported by the homonymous association founded in 1977 by teachers of the Faculty of Agriculture of the University of Turin, it preserves about 1,600 objects, from simple tools to agricultural machines of historical interest, all inventoryed and catalogued. Times to check with the museum.

Why go. The tools and machines of the Piedmontese peasant civilization.
