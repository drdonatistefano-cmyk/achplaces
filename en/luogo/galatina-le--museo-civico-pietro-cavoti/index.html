Museo civico Pietro Cavoti

Museums · City history

Born in the thirties of the twentieth century on the initiative of Francesco Bardoscia and reopened in 1998 in the former convent of the Dominicans, today Palace of Culture. It exhibits artistic, archaeological, epigraphic and documentary material, medieval finds, a vast collection of drawings, notebooks, prints and photographs by Pietro Cavoti and spaces dedicated to the sculptor Gaetano Martinez and the Galatine artists.

Why go. Galatina's art and memory in a former convent.
