Chiesa rupestre di San Michele delle Grotte

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Among the oldest churches in Gravina and its first cathedral, dug into a single block of tuff between the 8th and 10th centuries at the end of the Fondovito district.

Why go. The first cathedral in the rock.
