Miniera di Monteneve (Museo provinciale Miniere)

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The mining district of San Martino Monteneve, reachable only on foot from Val Passiria, includes a mining village at 2,355 m and the extraction area, inhabited and worked in very harsh conditions from the Middle Ages to 1967: it was the most important deposit of lead and zinc in Tyrol and between 2,000 and 2,500 m is presented as the highest mine in Europe, with over 150 km of tunnels. Today there are refuge, exhibition and mine adventure, with half day or full day excursions with the train of the mine; guided tours (Tuesday and Thursday 9:15, Saturday and Sunday 8:30) only by reservation before 16 of the day before, from the car park of Monteneve Bridge.

Why go. The highest mine in Europe, between tunnels and mining village.

  • Where Corvara 52/53, Moso in Passiria
  • Entry Small tour €9, reduced €8; big tour €26, reduced €23
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.museominiere.it
