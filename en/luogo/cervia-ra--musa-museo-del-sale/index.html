MUSA, Museo del Sale

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the Warehouse of the Sale Torre on the canal port, tools, documents and photos of the work of the saltworks; the Salina Camillone is its open-air section.

Why go. Recognized quality museum by the Region: prepares for the visit of the salt.

  • Where Viale Nazario Sauro 24, Cervia
  • Entry For a fee; free on the first Sunday of the month and always up to 12 years (secondary source)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.turismo.comunecervia.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
