Castello di Morsasco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Manor in dominant position over the Bormida valley, born smaller and enlarged by the Malaspina in the fifteenth century, by the counts of Lodron in the sixteenth and by the Genoese princes Centurione in the early eighteenth century; drawbridge and moat were replaced in 1740 by a stone bridge. Inside a spacious living room once used for the ballcord, the castle apartments, two frescoed rooms with ancient fireplaces and prisons with graffiti and inscriptions; on the southeast tower the coat of arms of the Scotto Centurion, on the corners of the tower the heads of lion of the Lodrone. It is privately owned: the tours are organized and announced on the site of the castle, which also offers visits on request to the contemporary collection (tel. +39 334 376 9833).

Why go. A private castle with frescoed rooms, graffiti prisons and views from Monte Rosa to the Apennines.
