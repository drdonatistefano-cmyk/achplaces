Museo di Palazzo Pretorio

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the thirteenth-century palace about three thousand works on the history of Prato: Bernardo Daddi, Giovanni da Milano, Donatello, Filippo and Filippino Lippi, Santi di Tito, Allori and the gipsoteca by Lorenzo Bartolini.

Why go. The city's art gallery.

  • Where Piazza del Comune, Prato
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
