Monastero di San Bernardino

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry

Dedicated to Saint Bernardino da Siena, it includes the church of San Bernardino and the convent of Santa Maria degli Angeli. The church has a unique nave with three small polygonal chapels on the left: in the first, dedicated to the Madonna, frescoes attributed to the Treviso Bernardo Zenale and Bernardino Butinone; on the tramezzo the cycle of the Passion of Fermo Stella, recently restored. Open Monday to Friday 9-17; guided tours by reservation.

Why go. The cycle of the Passion of Fermo Stella on the tramezzo.

  • Where Viale Giovanni XXIII, Caravaggio
  • Entry €5, free under 18 and people with disabilities
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
