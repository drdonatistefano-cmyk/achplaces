Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the thirteenth century, enlarged and partly rebuilt around 1575 (data engraved on the facade), with rose window and double-fore bell tower; the frescoes are by Jacopo Siculo, including the Dormitio Virginis of 1536 in the apse basin.

Why go. The cycle of Jacopo Siculo.
