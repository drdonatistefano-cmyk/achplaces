MUNAF - Museo Nazionale di Fotografia

Museums · Modern art

Born as Museum of Contemporary Photography (MuFoCo), opened in April 2004 as the first Italian public museum dedicated to photography and technological image, and today MUNAF - National Museum of Photography. It occupies the southern wing of Villa Ghirlanda and preserves more than 2 million photos from the post-war period to the present day of more than 1,000 Italian and foreign authors, with funds on landscape, portrait, social photography and artistic research, and a specialized library; some exhibitions are held at the Triennale in Milan.

Why go. The largest Italian public collection of contemporary photography.
