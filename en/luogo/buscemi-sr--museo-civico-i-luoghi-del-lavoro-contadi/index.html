Museo civico «I luoghi del lavoro contadino»

Museums · Themed

Ten museum units reconstruct life and peasant work: the house of the massarium, the palment, the shop of the blacksmith, the house of the laborer, the shops of the calderaium, the carpenter, the shoemaker and the tannerbrocche and the mill ("u trappitu") in a special building the didactic workshop and sections on the wheat cycle and stone processing in the Val di Noto, as well as a water mill and a documentation centre of the iblea popular life.

Why go. An entire country that tells the work of the fields.
