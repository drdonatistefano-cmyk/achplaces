Borgata Museo Balma Boves

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Settling built entirely under a huge mass of gneiss on Mount Bracco, about 650 meters, in the chestnut woods; settled until the 1950s-sixties, today it is an ecomuseum. You can visit stone houses with flat roofs (used to repair hay and charms), stables, barns, tool store, dryer, sink and bread oven, some houses with original furnishings. It is reached on foot in about half an hour from the hamlet Rocchetta, for mule tracks between secular chestnut trees; guided tours from June to October.

Why go. An entire village under a rock, witness to the civilization of the chestnut tree.
