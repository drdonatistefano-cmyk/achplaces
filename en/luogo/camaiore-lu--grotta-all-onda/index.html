Grotta all'Onda

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Karst cave in the Apuan Alps Park, near Casoli, inhabited by Neanderthal hunters and then used for agriculture and breeding in the Neolithic and Copper Age; waterfalls feed a stream at its base.

Why go. A prehistoric site among waterfalls in the Apuan Islands.
