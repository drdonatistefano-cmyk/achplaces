Museo diocesano «Mons. Giovanni Andrea Tria»

Museums · Old masters

In the Bishop's Palace, part of a path that includes Duomo, churches of St. Francis and St. Stephen and Ducal Palace; the first section is in the sacristy of the Cathedral, with altar, bishop's throne, choir stalls, sacristy furnishings, liturgical silvers from the seventeenth to the nineteenth century and two wooden panels from the fifteenth century. On the first floor the paintings of the Molisano Paolo Gamba, a pupil of Francesco Solimena; among the notable pieces the homeral veil of Bishop Belisario Baldevino (1555-1591), who in 1564 founded the first seminary of Christendom. Hours indicated by italia.it: every day 9-12.30 and 17-19.30.

Why go. The silvers and furnishings of the Duomo and the memory of the first seminar.
