Museo archeologico di Pithecusae

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Opened in 1999 on the first floor of Villa Arbusto, Ischia tells from prehistory to the Roman age through the artifacts of Pithecusae, founded in the eighth century BC by Greci dell'Eubea and dug by Giorgio Buchner since 1952: accessories of the necropolis of San Montano, the crater of the shipwreck of local production and the Cup of Nestor, with an epigram in Euboic alphabet that recalls the Iliad Cup. A section illustrates businesses with Near East, Carthage, Etruria and Sardinia.

Why go. To see the Nestor Cup and the first Greek colony of the West.

  • Where Corso Angelo Rizzoli 210, Lacco Ameno
  • Entry €8, reduced €6, 6-13 years and residents €3
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
