Cattedrale di Gerace

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Romanesque-Norman, consecrated in 1045 and again in 1222, partly built on the rock, with a Greek crypt perhaps of the eighth century and the chapel of Our Lady of Itria, obtained in 1261 from a rock church.

Why go. The largest church in Calabria.
