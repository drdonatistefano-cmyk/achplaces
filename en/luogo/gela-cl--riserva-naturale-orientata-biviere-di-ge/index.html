Riserva naturale orientata Biviere di Gela

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Reserve established in 1997 and entrusted to the LIPU, with marsh vegetation and riparian: it is important for the wintering, the nidification and the stop of migratory birds, with mothsmen, spatulae, a colony of pendolini and the only Sicilian site of nesting of the partridge of sea; there live also the discoglosso painting and the endemic Leopoldia gussonei. Hiking is free, without leaving the marked trails in zone A.

Why go. Birdwatching on the coastal lake.
