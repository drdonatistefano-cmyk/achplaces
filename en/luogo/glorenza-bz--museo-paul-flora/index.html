Museo Paul Flora

Museums · Modern art

On the second floor of the Tower of Tubre (door of the church), since 2011 a permanent exhibition on the life and works of Paul Flora, graphic designer and illustrator born in Glorenza who for about 25 years designed cartoons for "Die Zeit"; he lived mainly in Innsbruck, but Glorenza remained his point of reference.

Why go. The designer of ... Die Zeit in his town.
