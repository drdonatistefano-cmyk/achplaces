Borgo e Arboreto di Mondaino

History and architecture · Villages · Viewpoint

The village of the Malatesta peace treaties, with the Palaeontological Museum, the majolica and the botanical garden of the Arboreto of nine hectares.

Why go. The semicircle square and the Val Mala.
