Centro etnografico S'Haus van der Zahre

Museums · Themed

In a rural building of stone and wood (former barn with nineteenth-century barn), typical example of Alpine architecture, exhibits work tools and objects of the local families and hosts temporary thematic exhibitions. It is part of the CarniaMuseum network; times to ask at the museum (0433 86262) or at the tourist office.

Why go. The daily life of the Saura linguistic island.
