Chiesa dei Rossi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1636 by the Confraternity of the Most Holy Trinity, it owes its name to the red capes of the confreres; next stood a hospice for pilgrims. It preserves elaborate balustrade and altar, furnishings and carved walnut choir, a chair dated 1785, an angel in golden wood of 1684 and a painting with the Blessed Panacea.

Why go. The wooden furniture of the fraternity.
