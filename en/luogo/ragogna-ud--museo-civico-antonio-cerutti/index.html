Museo civico «Antonio Cerutti»

Museums · Archaeological

Born in 1981 by the Archaeological Group Reunia led by Antonio Cerutti, on the ground floor of the Cultural Center: naturalistic section on the morainic amphitheatre of Tagliamento and on the ecosystems of the river and lake of Ragogna, with dioramas; archaeological section from the Mesolithic to the nineteenth century, with lithic instruments of the lake, an axe in serpentine, a fibula with bronze knight (II-III century), Lombard reliefs, the holy water-capitello of San Remigio (XI-XII century) and coins from the Roman age in Venice. Different times between MiC and Municipality: to be checked.

Why go. From prehistory to the Longobards on the Tagliamento.
