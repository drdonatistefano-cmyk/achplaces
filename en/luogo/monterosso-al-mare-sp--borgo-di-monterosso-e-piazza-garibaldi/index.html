Borgo di Monterosso e piazza Garibaldi

History and architecture · Villages · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The ancient center at the mouth of the Buranco stream, with parallel streets Rome and Vittorio Emanuele and the tree-lined Piazza Garibaldi of the Two-Three hundred; one of the five villages of the UNESCO site.

Why go. The ancient village.
