Casa del Podestà – Fondazione Ugo Da Como

Museums · House museums

fifteenth-century house of the Venetian podestà, purchased at auction in 1906 by Senator Ugo Da Como from Bresciano and restored by architect Antonio Tagliaferri. It preserves antique furniture, pharmacy vases, ceramics, paintings, private studio and the Red Room, and a library of over 50,000 volumes, among the largest private collections in Northern Italy, with the smallest book in the world (pages 15 x 9 mm). Admission fee.

Why go. A senator's library and the smallest book in the world.
