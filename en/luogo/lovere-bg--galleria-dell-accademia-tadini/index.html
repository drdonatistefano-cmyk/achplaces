Galleria dell'Accademia Tadini

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the neoclassical palace wanted by Count Luigi Tadini and built between 1821 and 1824 on a project by Sebastiano Salimbeni, with the windows on the lake and the ceilings painted by Luigi Dell'Era; the Academy, established in 1829, is one of the oldest museums in Lombardy. In 23 rooms: the Religion and the Tadini Stele by Antonio Canova, paintings by Jacopo Bellini, Antonio and Bartolomeo Vivarini, Paris Bordon, Giacomo Ceruti and three paintings by Francesco Hayez, as well as porcelain by Meissen, Sèvres and Naples.

Why go. Canova and the Lombard and Venetian painting by the lake.

  • Where Via Tadini, Lovere
  • Entry €7
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
