Museo dell'arte orafa

Museums · Themed

Set up by the Municipality in the premises of the former Pretura, it tells a tradition that in the early decades of the twentieth century counted about thirty goldsmith shops. It exhibits machinery, moulds and tools of the various phases of processing and jewelry produced in Cervaro between the end of the seventeenth and twentieth centuries, largely donated by families, with filigree works, mold and tunnel, caston rings and the typical rings with two braided hands; in a workshop rebuilt a craftsman shows the phases of the work. Not indicated hours: Inquire at the Municipality.

Why go. The gold of the Cervarese tradition and the goldsmith's shop.
