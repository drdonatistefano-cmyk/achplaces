Museo della Val d'Ultimo

Museums · Themed

The San Nicolò Museum offers a wide view of the daily life of the peasants of the valley. Open approximately from mid-May to late October in the afternoon (in 2026 from 17 May to 25 October, 14.30-17.30); guided tours on request.
