Chiesetta rurale di Sant'Alessandro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church in a hut along the road between Vione and Lecanù, with fourteenth-century bell tower on the north side, restored after a period of degradation lasted until the 1930s; tradition binds it to St. Charles Borromeo, with a stone object of veneration.

Why go. A little church of the fourteenth century linked to St. Charles.
