Museo archeologico della Val Tidone

Museums · Archaeological

In three rooms in the basement of the Rocca Dal Verme, it collects prehistoric and protohistoric finds (from the sixth millennium to the fifth century BC), Roman and medieval found in Val Tidone.

Why go. Seven thousand years of valley history.
