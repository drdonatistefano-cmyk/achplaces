Tempio di Hera – Tavole Palatine

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Doric Temple dedicated to Hera, on the right bank of the Bradano about three kilometers from the ancient city: there are 15 of the 32 original columns, 10 on the north side and 5 on the south side; dug in 1926 and restored in 1961, in the Middle Ages it was called "Mensae Imperatoris."

Why go. The Doric columns of Metaponto.
