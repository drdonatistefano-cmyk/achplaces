Museo Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

On the ground floor of the Franciscan complex, in front of the former refectory: finds from the prehistory to the Renaissance, with sections on Romanity and the Middle Ages, coins and funerary inscriptions.

Why go. We visit the Malatestiana together.
