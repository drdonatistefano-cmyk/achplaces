Forte di Gavi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Podere fortress built between the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries with a starry plan with six bastions; abandoned in the nineteenth century, it was prison and prison camp in the wars. You visit with guided tours.

Why go. The star fortress.
