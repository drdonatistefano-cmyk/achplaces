Presepe meccanico di Dubbione

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Born in 2002 with the event "There was once Christmas" and since 2014 set up in the Oratory S. Paolo, is entirely self-built, set in the Waldensian valleys with a section dedicated to the Germanasca Valley, and is registered with the Italian Association of Friends of the Nativity scene. According to the Town Hall page ( 2019 poster) it is open from 8 December to 6 January on Saturday and Sunday from 15 to 18, with visits for schools and groups by reservation.

Why go. A handmade crib in the Waldensian valleys.
