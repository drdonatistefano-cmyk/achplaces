Abbazia di Santa Maria Arabona

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Cistercian Abbey probably born from a donation of 1197, with works started by 1208 and interrupted after choir, transept and capitular hall; in the chorus sculptures thirteenth century and frescoes attributed to Antonio da Atri. From the end of the sixteenth century to the eighteenth century the Franciscans stayed there, then passed to the Zambra family until 1968 and then to the diocese. The Superintendence indicates it as an example of the Cistercian Gothic in Italy.

Why go. Cistercian Gothic in the middle of the countryside.
