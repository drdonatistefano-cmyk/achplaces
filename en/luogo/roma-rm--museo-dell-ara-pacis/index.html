Museo dell'Ara Pacis

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The museum that houses the Ara Pacis, one of the greatest works of the ancients, the first architecture built in the center of Rome since the post-war period.

Why go. The altar of peace of Augustus.
