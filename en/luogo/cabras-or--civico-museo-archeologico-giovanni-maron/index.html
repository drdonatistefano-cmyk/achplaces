Civico museo archeologico Giovanni Marongiu

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

It tells the Sinis from the Neolithic to the Roman age: the Neolithic village of Cuccuru is Arrius, the Nuragic finds, Tharros and the lead bars of the Roman wreck of Mal di Ventre. From April 2026 he exhibited the entire statuary complex of Mont'e Prama, nuragic sculptures of 900-700 BC. (Paintings, archers, warriors and models of nuraghe) discovered in 1974, among the oldest stone statues in the Mediterranean.

Why go. The Giants of Mont'e Prama.

  • Where Via Tharros, Cabras
  • Entry €4, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
