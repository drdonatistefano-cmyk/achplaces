Bosco di San Silvestro – Oasi WWF

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

The Royal Tenuta di San Silvestro was part of the Royal Delights of the Reggia together with San Leucio, the Royal Park and the English Garden; today it is an Oasis of the WWF that organizes visits and initiatives.

Why go. The Bourbon Hunting Reserve.
