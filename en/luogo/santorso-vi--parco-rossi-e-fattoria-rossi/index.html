Parco Rossi e Fattoria Rossi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

A set of historical environments designed and inhabited by Alessandro Rossi: a nineteenth-century villa, the park and a model farm. The Rossi Park is a romantic garden at the foot of Mount Summano.

Why go. The romantic garden and the model farm of Alessandro Rossi.
