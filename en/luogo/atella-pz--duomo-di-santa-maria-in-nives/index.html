Duomo di Santa Maria in Nives

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Mother church with fourteenth-century façade crowned with hanging arches and portal without comparison in the region, with a small bezel between sun and moon; a single nave interior of the eighteenth century, rebuilt after the earthquake of 1694.

Why go. The sun and moon portal.
