Kosmos – Museo di Storia Naturale

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

Born in 1771 with the reform of the university wanted by Maria Teresa of Austria, since 2019 has a new path to Palazzo Botta that tells the natural world and the history of natural sciences.

Why go. Animals, fossils and history of science.

  • Where Piazza Botta 9, Pavia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it
