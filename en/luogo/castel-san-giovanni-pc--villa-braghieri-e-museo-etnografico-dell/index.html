Villa Braghieri e Museo etnografico della Val Tidone

Museums · Themed

Villa at the eastern entrance of the city, which hosted the Duchess Maria Amalia wife of Ferdinand of Bourbon, with furnishings and frescoes of the Seven and Nineteenth Century: today it is home to the municipal library, the ethnographic museum of Val Tidone, with materials on the peasant life, fossils and fragments of Roman objects, and the museum dedicated to Cardinal Agostino Casaroli.

Why go. The peasant life of the Tidone Valley in a ducal villa.
