Abbazia di Santa Maria di Rio Sacro

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Benedictine Romanesque Abbey in the valley of the Rio Sacro, in Acquacanina, with a Romanesque crypt with three naves, a crucifix of the Lazio school of the twelfth century, a wooden group of the Madonna with the Child of the sixteenth century, the small canvas of the Madonna of the Suffragio of Carlo Maratta and a fresco of the Martyrdom of San Sebastiano attributed to Jerome of John.

Why go. A Romanesque abbey hidden in a valley of the Sibillini.

after the earthquake of 2016 the monuments are partially usable; the card of the Park describes the state prior to the earthquake
