Palazzo Bonafede

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace-fortress built at the beginning of the XVI century by Nicolò Bonafede, born in the village and bishop of Chiusi, today seat of the town hall: the central body is inspired by Palazzo Venezia in Rome, the courtyard to that of the Doge's Palace of Urbino.

Why go. An Urbino courtyard in a Macerata village.
