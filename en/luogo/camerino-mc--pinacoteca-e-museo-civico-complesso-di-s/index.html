Pinacoteca e Museo civico – Complesso di San Domenico

Museums · Old masters

In the 13th century convent of San Domenico: paintings from the 14th to the 16th century of the chamber school (John Angel of Antonio, Jerome of Giovanni, Giovanni Boccati), fossils of hippopotamuses, elephants and rhinoceros of the plain of Colfiorito, contemporary art and pharmacy vases.

Why go. Camerino's painting school.
