Ex ospedale psichiatrico e Archivio storico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Former provincial asylum founded in 1881 in a building that had been a seventeenth-century convent of the Friars Minor; with its historical archive it was opened for the FAI Fall Day 2023 and has been repeatedly reported to the Places of the Heart.

Why go. A page of social history inside an ancient convent.
