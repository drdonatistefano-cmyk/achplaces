Palazzo Madama – Museo Civico d'Arte Antica

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage

In Piazza Castello, from Roman gate to medieval fortress and castle of the Acajas, then the first Senate of Italy; over seventy thousand works, from the Portrait of Man by Antonello da Messina to the Très belles Heures by Jean de Berry, and the staircase of Juvarra.

Why go. Two thousand years in a palace.
