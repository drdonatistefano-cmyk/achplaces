Chiesa di Sant'Anatolia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church dedicated to a Roman martyr of the third century, with painting decorations of the XIV-XV centuries, a marble palace inlaid with a 17th century Neapolitan school at the main altar and paintings of the same period.

Why go. Frescoes of the fourteenth century in the parish.

closed for restoration work according to Umbriatourism
