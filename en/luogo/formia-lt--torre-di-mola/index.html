Torre di Mola

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

A cylindrical tower up to 27 meters high built in 1289 by Charles II of Anjou as an outpost to defend Gaeta, with belt and two doors to land and to the sea; it rises on the remains of Roman baths, visible from a glass floor with calidarium and frigidarium. It was long of the Caetans, damaged in war and then restored with a Renaissance portal brought by Gaeta.

Why go. Middle Ages and Roman baths together.
