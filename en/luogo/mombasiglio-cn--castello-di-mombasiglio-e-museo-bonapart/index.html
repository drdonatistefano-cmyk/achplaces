Castello di Mombasiglio e Museo Bonaparte

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Built around the year 1000 in a panoramic position above the village, it is one of the best preserved buildings of the ancient Marquisate of Ceva, with a high tower that dominates the shelter; in 1796 there were aquartiered the Napoleonic armies. On the first floor the Bonaparte Museum preserves the largest collection of original prints and etchings by Giuseppe Pietro Bagetti on the first Italian countryside of Napoleon; in the tower a museum of marbles and local stones, with a wide view of mountains and valleys. From 21 March to October 2026 open on Saturday 14-18, Sunday and holidays 10-18; schools by reservation with CoopCulture.

Why go. The tables of the Bagetti on the Napoleonic countryside of 1796, in a castle with views.
