CSAC, Centro Studi e Archivio della Comunicazione

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

In the Cistercian Abbey of Valserena, the museum-archive of the University with millions of drawings, photographs, design projects, architecture and fashion of the twentieth century.

Why go. Italian design inside a Gothic abbey.

  • Where Abbazia di Valserena, Strada Viazza di Paradigna 1, Parma
  • Entry €10 whole; €8 groups; €5 from 13 to 18 years old and students
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
