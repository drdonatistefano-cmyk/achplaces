Anfiteatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

It occupies the south half of Piazza Sant'Oronzo: of Augustan age, excavated from 1900 by Cosimo De Giorgi for about a third, national monument since 1906; the rest is still under the square.

Why go. Roman Lupiae in the center.
