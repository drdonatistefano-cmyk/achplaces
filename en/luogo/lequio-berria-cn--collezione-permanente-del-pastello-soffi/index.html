Collezione permanente del Pastello Soffice

Museums · Modern art

Collection of 49 pastels donated to the Municipality by Italian and Russian artists, made between 1996 and 2001 mainly on velvety paper: views of St. Petersburg, Jaroslavl and Rostov alongside Italian subjects such as Sanremo, Monviso with the hills of the Langhe and Lequio Berria, with works by Renzo Magnanini, Vladimir Emelianov, Luigi Carbone, Peter Pavlov and others. The City page does not indicate exhibition center or timetable: information in the Municipality, Via Roma 36, tel. 0173 52402.
