Villa Priuli e Museo civico

Museums · Archaeological

Villa of the XVII century wanted by the Priuli family, with a large cycle of frescoes by the seventeenth-century painter Paolo Piazza di Castelfranco; today owned by the municipality, it houses cultural center, library and the Civic Museum. The archaeological collection includes ceramics from the Middle-Recent Bronze Age from the Motte di Castello di Godego, bowls, olles, millstones and millstones, recovered since 1975 by the Historical Archaeological Group of Castelfranco Veneto and Godego Castle, and the Longobard sets with lunula earrings found in San Pietro in 1952. Free access or upon reservation (tel. 0423 761185).

Why go. Frescoes by Paolo Piazza and the Bronze Age of the plain.
