Villa Cahen e Selva di Meana

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Villa Liberty built in 1880 for the financier of Antwerp Edouard Cahen, made marquis by Vittorio Emanuele II, on a large estate between two ridges of the Paglia; it is surrounded by a largely Italian garden with rare plants, a Japanese garden restored, greenhouses and lemon trees, in the protected area of Allerona-Meana.

Why go. The only Liberty building in Umbria with an Italian garden.
