Siti nuragici: Nuraghe Don Micheli, tomba di giganti di Fiorosu, fonte sacra di Frades Mareos

History and architecture · Archaeology

The territory has more than 70 Nuragic settlements: the nuraghe Don Micheli, a 12-metre-diameter tower with finds from the 15th to the 9th century BC; the tomb of giants of Fiorosu with a 16-metre corridor covered by slabs; the sacred source of Frades Mareos, in blocks of trachyte, with paved and circular chamber in tholos on a vein of water.

Why go. Nuraghe, grave and sacred fountain in one territory.
