Rocca Colonna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

At the highest point of the country; the first fortification, before the Thousand, was of the Roman prince and senator Alberico. The columns, lords from the fifteenth century, the walls with circular towers and Alessandro and Sciarra Colonna transformed it into a Renaissance palace. It preserves frescoed rooms, ancient prisons, a small medieval church and the Sala Pinta, frescoed by Federico Zuccari with the foundation of Rome, Roman legends, battles and the possessions of the Colonna.

Why go. The Zuccari Pint Hall.
