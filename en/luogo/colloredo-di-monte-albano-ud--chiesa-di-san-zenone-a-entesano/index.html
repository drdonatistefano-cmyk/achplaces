Chiesa di San Zenone a Entesano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The church is isolated to the south of Entesano, within the stone wall of the ancient cemetery: a single nave, a hut façade with stone and oculus portal, brick bell tower of 1907. From the first building, from the late fifteenth or early sixteenth century, remains the presbytery, decorated with a cycle of frescoes probably seventeenth century with stories of Mary and Jesus, saints, prophets and angels, attributed by tradition to Matteo Leban from Tolmino. Damaged by the 1976 earthquake and long abandoned, it was consolidated and restored from 2013 to 2016.

Why go. A cycle of 17th century frescoes in a restored country church.
