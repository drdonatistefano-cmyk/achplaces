Chiesa di San Bartolomeo delle Montagne

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

San Bortolo has been inhabited by the Cimbri since 1287; the first church of San Bartolomeo dates back to the beginning of the fifteenth century, and the new, larger one, was built between 1894 and 1904 with the parish priest Don Giobatta Valentini, with facade completed in 1960. Neoclassical façade with four semi-colons Corinthians, tympanum and rose window; single nave interior with four side chapels and dome on the presbytery. Next to the old church the stone bell tower of Lessinia.

Why go. Nineteenth-century church of the cimbrian village, next to the Trombone Museum.
