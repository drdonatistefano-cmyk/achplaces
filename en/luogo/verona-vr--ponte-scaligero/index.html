Ponte Scaligero

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The fourteenth-century pedestrian bridge that starts from the tower of the Mastio di Castelvecchio, a superb example of 14th century engineering, blown up in 1945 and rebuilt after the war.

Why go. Walking on Scaliger blackbirds.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
