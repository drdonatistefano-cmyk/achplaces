Certosa del Galluzzo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The large monastic complex on the hill of Galluzzo with the cycle of Passion frescoed by Pontormo, which can be visited with guided tours of about an hour.

Why go. The Pontormo in the hill.

  • Where Via della Certosa 1, Firenze
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com
