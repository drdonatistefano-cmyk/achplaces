Teatro Cavallino Bianco

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Theatre built between 1947 and 1949 by Antonio De Gioia on a former cellar near the station, with hotel and restaurant attached and 1,062 seats; the name comes from a operetta by Ralph Benatzky. Inaugurated on February 3, 1949 with the Rigoletto di Verdi, in 1980 he hosted the Living Theatre. Place: https://www.cavallinobiancogalatina.it, tel. +39 0836 633424.

Why go. A great post-war theatre with a lively history.
