Santuario della Madonna del Pericolo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Small sanctuary next to a cave used by the first Christian communities for secret worship; the entrance of the cave is still visible, closed by a grate next to the facade.

Why go. A place of worship born around a early Christian cave.
