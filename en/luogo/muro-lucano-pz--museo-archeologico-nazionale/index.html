Museo archeologico nazionale

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Opened in 2003 at the Bishops' Seminary of the 16th century, on three floors: the five-section route goes from the archaeologist's craft to life in an imperial Roman villa. Among the main pieces the mosaic with the Seasons and Aion by Masseria Ciccotti of Oppido Lucano; cumulative with the National Museum of Power €8.

Why go. The Roman mosaic of the Seasons.

  • Where Via Seminario 6, Muro Lucano
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.museiitaliani.it
