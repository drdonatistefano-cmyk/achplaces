Museo regionale dell'emigrazione «Pietro Conti»

Museums · Themed

In the Palazzo del Podestà, entitled to Pietro Conti, the first president of the Umbria Region, is indicated as the first museum in Italy entirely dedicated to emigration: it tells with voices, sounds, images, documents and objects the Italian emigration from the late nineteenth century to the sixties, with a path backwards from the arrival to the journey to the departure. It is also a study and documentation centre with video library and library. It is part of the unique ticket of the museum (€6). Info: tel. 075 9142445.

Why go. The stories of Italians left for the world.
