Museo comunale di Praia a Mare

Museums · Modern art

Housed in an old military structure, it has archaeological sections, contemporary art, postcards of artist, ceramics and photography; the permanent collection of modern and contemporary art has over 200 works donated by Italian and foreign artists.

Why go. Over two hundred contemporary works of art donated by artists.
