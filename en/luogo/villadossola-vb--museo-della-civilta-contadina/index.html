Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

It collects tools, customs and customs of the agricultural works of the territory, with captions and cards edited by Villarte and written in dialect to protect the local speech. Open from April to October on the first and third Sundays of the month, 14-17.

Why go. The tools of the peasant world, told in dialect.
