Galleria civica

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

In the civic palazzo "S'omu comuni" of the late nineteenth century, already school and pretura: a hall for temporary exhibitions, a hundred works of contemporary painters, many born from the annual competition of painting and sculpture, and in the council hall three seventeenth-century paintings of the Caravaggio school; an external path touches the modern sculptures in the streets of the village. Tuesday-Sunday, seasonal hours.

Why go. Three caravaggio canvases in the council room.

  • Where Via della Sapienza 38, Seui
  • Entry €5 unique ticket of the museum route; reduced €4, €3,50, €1
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website museiseui.altervista.org
