Grotta del Lauro

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

karst cave at about 1,068 meters in the limestone-dolomitic massif of Crasto, the main one of the western side of the Fortress, partly still unexplored: stalactites, stalagmites, columns and mammellonary concretions of calcite; there live two endemic troglobular invertebrates and there were found materials of the Neolithic and early metals. Not accessible to people with limited mobility.

Why go. A cave of concretions with species that live only here.

the section closed by gate is visited only on request (tel. Common 0941 793010)
