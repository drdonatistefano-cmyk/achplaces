Parco della Fantasia e Museo Rodari

Museums · Themed

Cultural center for children that enhances the work of Gianni Rodari, born in Omegna in 1920; it is divided into a ludotheque, educational museum (the Rodari Museum, in the historic center) and gardens on Monte Zuoli, outside the city.

Why go. In the hometown of Gianni Rodari.
