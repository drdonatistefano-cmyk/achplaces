Archeoparc Villandro

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Archaeological park at the Plunacker, inhabited without interruption by the Mesolithic, with remains of the Stone, Copper, Bronze and Roman era; typical buildings rebuilt with their furnishings revive the first settlements.

Why go. The prehistory of the Isarco Valley rebuilt outdoors.

can be visited with the guided tour of the country on Wednesday at 16.30 from May to October; groups on request

  • Where Plunacker, Villandro
  • Entry €3, reduced €1 (6-14 years), free under 6 and with KlausenCard
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.klausen.it
