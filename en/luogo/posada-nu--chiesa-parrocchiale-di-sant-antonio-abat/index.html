Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Antonio abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church at the foot of the castle, in the center of the village, rebuilt in the seventeenth century on a plant of 1324 and several times renovated; the patron saint is celebrated in mid-January with bonfires, processions and typical sweets.

Why go. The church of the fire rite of January.
