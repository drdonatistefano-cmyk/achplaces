Collegiata di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In front of Palazzo Chigi on Piazza di Corte, with its large dome, it is one of the last masterpieces of Gian Lorenzo Bernini, who drew every detail, including furnishings.

Why go. Bernini in every detail.
