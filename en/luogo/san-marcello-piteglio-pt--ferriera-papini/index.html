Ferriera Papini

Museums · Themed

Considered the oldest ironworks in Tuscany, operating from the fourteenth century until the eighties of the twentieth century, it is an example of industrial archaeology: the mountain steelmaking used water and local woods to work the Elba mineral, enhanced by Cosimo I in the sixteenth century and by the Grand Ducal road of 1778. You can see sweaters, wheels and forges and in the workshops you can try the techniques of the blacksmith; it is the main stage of the itinerary of the Iron of the Ecomuseo.

Why go. Sweats and forges of the oldest ironworks in Tuscany.
