Parco archeologico di Laos

History and architecture · Archaeology

On the hill of San Bartolo, in the hamlet of Marcellina, about 60 hectares preserve the remains of Laos, achea colony later became autonomous (italia.it date 510 BC): established in 1994 after the Italian-French excavations, it shows the main north-south road originally 12 meters wide, crossed every 96 meters from east-west roads, and large houses around uncovered courtyards, almost all with facilities for rain water. A wooden fence follows the ancient streets; the finds are exposed to the Antiquarium of Scalea.

Why go. The most important Greek city of the High Tyrrhenian Cosentine.

visits only by reservation, with seasonal timetable (MiC): tel. 0985 9969, Sabap-cal.scalea@cultura.gov.it
