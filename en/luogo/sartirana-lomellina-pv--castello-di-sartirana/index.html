Castello di Sartirana

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built by the Visconti around 1370, after Galeazzo Visconti took Sartirana to the Counts of Lomello, it is the largest castle of Lomellina: quadrangular plan in red bricks with moat, inner courtyard covered with ivy and four angular towers, dominated by the cylindrical tower of north-east. About 1462-1463, under the Sforza, Aristotile Fioravanti strengthened him by adding to the tower the dodecagonal base and the terracotta bells; restored in the nineteenth century with eclectic decorations, since 1975 he is of the Foundation of Studies of Lomellina. Beyond the moat, to the north-west, a garden connected by a crenellated catwalk; next to the parish church and the gun square rise.

Why go. The largest Visconti castle in Lomellina, with a tower reinforced by Fioravanti.
