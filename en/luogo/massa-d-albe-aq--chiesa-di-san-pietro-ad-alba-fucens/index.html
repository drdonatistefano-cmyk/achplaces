Chiesa di San Pietro ad Alba Fucens

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

On the hill of San Pietro, obtained from the temple of Apollo of the third century BC, converted to worship in the sixth century and expanded in the twelfth with three aisles, apse and crypt. It preserves an ambo and an iconostasis cosmates of the 13th century with inlays in porphyry and numerous graffiti; damaged by the earthquake of 1915, it was restored in 1955-57. Visit by reservation required (340 6255973).

Why go. The Roman temple became Cosmatesque church.
