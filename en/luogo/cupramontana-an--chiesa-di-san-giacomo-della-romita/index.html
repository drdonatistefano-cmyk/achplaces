Chiesa di San Giacomo della Romita

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Priory of Camaldolese already before the thirteenth century, called "Romitella delle Mandriole" passed to the Franciscans in 1452; the present building was rebuilt between 1782 and 1793 on a design by D. Apollonio Tucchi. Blessed John Righi is buried there by Fabriano.

Why go. An ancient priory between Camaldolese and Franciscans.
