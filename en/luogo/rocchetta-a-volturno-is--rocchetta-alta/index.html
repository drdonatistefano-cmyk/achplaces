Rocchetta Alta

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Viewpoint

The original nucleus of the village, built along a rocky ridge with rock spurs that emerge between the houses and the castle on the highest summit, was abandoned for the damage of war, the difficult conditions of life and the high hydrogeological risk; the new center, Rocchetta Nuova, was born at the end of the nineteenth century on the plateau between the Mainarde and Volturno. Today it is a suggestive ruin among whose alleys you can walk.

Why go. The ghost country on the ridge.
