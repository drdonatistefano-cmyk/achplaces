Castagno dei Cento Cavalli

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

Castagno with three stems of an estimated age over 3000 years, 19.42 m tall and with a circumference of 10.51 m, considered the oldest known; according to tradition, Queen Joan of Aragon recovered with her knights during a storm, and Goethe and other travelers described it.
