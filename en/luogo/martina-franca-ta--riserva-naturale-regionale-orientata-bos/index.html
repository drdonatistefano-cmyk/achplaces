Riserva naturale regionale orientata Bosco delle Pianelle

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

About 600 hectares towards Crispiano and Massafra, reached crossing south of Martina a landscape of red earth, with the richest evergreen formations of the Murge and numerous wild orchids; in the nearby district Specchia Tarantina the Cave of the Nine Housedde, 42 meters deep with limestone concretions, and Masseria Russoli a conservation center of the Donkey of Martina Franca.

Why go. The richest forest in the Murge, a few kilometers from the Baroque.
