Chiesa di San Vincenzo al Furlo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Remains of an 8th century abbey where St Romualdo (1011) and St. Pier Damiani (1042) stayed, remained autonomous until the 11th century, then passed under the influence of Fonte Avellana. Nearby is a small Roman bridge.

Why go. A millenarian abbey at the entrance of the throat.
