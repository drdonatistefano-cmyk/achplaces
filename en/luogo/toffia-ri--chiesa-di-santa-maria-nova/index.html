Chiesa di Santa Maria Nova

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

At the highest point of Toffia, built in the seventeenth century on the site of the Palace-Castle of the Columns: the bell tower reuses the male of the castle and the apse one of its towers. Originally a single nave, then divided into three naves; it preserves a Virgin with the Child of Carlo Maratta school and a round with the Virgin and Child of the Florentine school of the fifteenth century.

Why go. The church on the fortress of the Colonna.
