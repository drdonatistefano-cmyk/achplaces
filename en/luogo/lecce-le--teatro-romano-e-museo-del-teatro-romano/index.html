Teatro romano e Museo del teatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology

Re-emerged in 1928 during works in a private residence and excavated in 1938: an auditorium of about 75 meters for at least 6,000 spectators; the nearby museum in Via degli Ammirati tells the ancient Lupiae.

Why go. The theater hidden between the buildings.
