Civiche raccolte d'arte di Palazzo Marliani Cicogna

Museums · Old masters

The Palazzo dei Conti Marliani, feudal lords of Busto between the Six and the eighteenth century, then of the Cicogna and municipal since 1820, has been a museum since 1990; after the restoration of 2002 the spaces have doubled. Paintings, drawings, documents and sculptures, more temporary exhibitions.

Why go. The feudal palace became a civic museum.

From 7 January 2026 closed for renovation works, except the halls of temporary exhibitions
