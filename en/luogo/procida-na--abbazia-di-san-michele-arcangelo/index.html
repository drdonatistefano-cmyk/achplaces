Abbazia di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The first nucleus dates back to the 11th century; it preserves works of art, a library of the 16th century, a permanent nativity scene and a necropolis ossuary. Under the abbey a museum complex is spread over three floors.

Why go. The millennial abbey in the heart of Terra Murata.
