Museo Nazionale Atestino

Museums · Archaeological

In the sixteenth century Palazzo Mocenigo, which includes part of the fourteenth-century walls of the Carrara castle, exhibits protohistoric finds of the Iron Age and Romans from the excavations begun in the second half of the nineteenth century in Este, Lozzo, Arquà and in Lower Padua, in a chronological and thematic path (daily life, cults, funeral rites) that closes with a small medieval and modern section. Among the main pieces the Situla Welcome, symbol of ancient Venetians, the funeral set of the noblewoman Nerka Trostiaia, a gold medallion coined under Augustus in 2 BC and the Madonna with Child of Cima da Conegliano (1504). Hours: Monday 8.30-13.30, Tuesday to Saturday 8.30-19.30, Sunday 14.30-19.30.

Why go. One of the most important archaeological museums in Italy, with the Situla Welcome to the ancient Venetians.
