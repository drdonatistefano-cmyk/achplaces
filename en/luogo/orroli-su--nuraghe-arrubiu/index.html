Nuraghe Arrubiu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Nuragic complex of about 5,000 square meters and 21 towers on the relief of Su Pranu, above the medium valley of the Flumendosa, inhabited between the XIV and IX century BC: the mast, 15 meters high (about half of the original), preserves the dome in tholos and is surrounded by a bastion of five towers and a wall of seven, with courtyards equipped with cisterns, fireplaces and huts. In the 2nd century BC the Romans transformed it into a wine plant, of which two tanks remain; ceramics from the Peloponnese attest contacts with the Mycenaean world. The name, "Gigante rosso" comes from the reddish lichens that cover it. Admission for a fee.

Why go. One of the largest nuraghi in Sardinia, with the dome intact.
