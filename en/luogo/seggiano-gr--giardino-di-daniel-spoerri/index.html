Giardino di Daniel Spoerri

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Open-air art park of 16 hectares on the slopes of the Amiata, in the place called "the Paradise" for the mild climate: the Swiss artist Daniel Spoerri bought the estate in 1991 and exhibited his bronzes from the 1980s; monograph until 1996, open to the public in 1997, today has 113 installations of 55 international artists among trees, meadows and paths, curated by the Hic Terminus Haeret Foundation. The route takes about three hours; restaurant and accommodation on site. Seasonal timetables, to be checked on the site.

Why go. Three hours of contemporary art in the woods.

  • Where Strada Provinciale Pescina, Seggiano
  • Entry €12, students €9, free under 8 years
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.danielspoerri.org
