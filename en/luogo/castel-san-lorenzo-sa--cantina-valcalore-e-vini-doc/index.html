Cantina Valcalore e vini DOC

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The plants of the social cellar, largely industrial archaeology, have been hosting since the 1960s an experience of wine cooperation; in 2018 the new company of partners bought the spaces of the former winery of 1960 and restarted the production of wine and extra virgin olive oil. Every year from 12 to 19 August, the Festa dei Sette Vini DOC is held.

Why go. Co-operative Wine of the Heat.
