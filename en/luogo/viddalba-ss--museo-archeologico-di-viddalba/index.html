Museo archeologico di Viddalba

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

It collects the testimonies of the lower valley of Coghinas from the Neolithic to the Middle Ages: ceramics of the culture of Ozieri, Bonnanaro finds from Monte San Giovanni, a bronze axe from the nuragic village of San Leonardo, jewels in gold, silver and vitreous paste from the Roman necropolis and Punic, Roman and Renaissance coins. The strong piece are about 80 stone funerary stems, from the end of the first century BC and from the first imperial age, with an anthropomorphic figure enclosed by a palm branch, exposed in the open-air moat together with millstones, large vases and reconstructions of graves. April-September Tuesday-Sunday 10-13 and 16-19; October-March 9.30-12.30 and 15-18.

Why go. One of the largest collections of Roman stele of Sardinia.

  • Where Piazza Giovanni Maria Angioy, Viddalba
  • Entry €3, reduced €2 (10-18 years); free up to 9 years and over 65
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.mav-viddalba.it
