Forte dell'Annunziata e Museo Girolamo Rossi

Museums · Archaeological · Viewpoint

Strong overlooking the sea that houses the archaeological civic museum dedicated to the discoverer of Albintimilium, with over 700 finds.

Why go. The finds of Albintimilium.
