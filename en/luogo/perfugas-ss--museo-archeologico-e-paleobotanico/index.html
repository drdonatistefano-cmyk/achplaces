Museo archeologico e paleobotanico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the former premises of the livestock market, it collects the finds of the territory from the Paleolithic to the Middle Ages: the vegetable fossils of the petrified forests of the Anglona, the oldest stone instruments found so far in Sardinia, the statue of the mother Reduced for groups of at least 20 people, schools and over 60.

Why go. Half a million years of Anglona in a few rooms.

  • Where Via Nazario Sauro, Perfugas
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
