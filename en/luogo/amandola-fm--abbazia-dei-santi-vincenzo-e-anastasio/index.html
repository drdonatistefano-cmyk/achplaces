Abbazia dei Santi Vincenzo e Anastasio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Benedictine abbey about 850 meters at the foot of the Amandola mountain, 5 km from the village: documented from 1044, deeply modified between 1295 and the fifteenth century, with bell tower rebuilt in 1461. The original structure remains the right wing, some arches of the cloister and fragments of the apse; single nave interior with trussed ceiling and single nave crypt on a single central pillar. For the timetables, contact the Municipality.

Why go. A thousand-year-old abbey among the Sibillini.
