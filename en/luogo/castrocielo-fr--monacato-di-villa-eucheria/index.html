Monacato di Villa Eucheria

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Former Benedictine monastery in the hamlet of Villa Eucheria, with a still intact cryptoportico; it is part of the network of the Historical Residences of Lazio.

Why go. An intact cryptoporic in a former convent.
