Galleria Nazionale di Palazzo Spinola

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

In Piazza di Pellicceria, donated to the State by Spinola in 1958; it preserves among other things the Ecce Homo by Antonello da Messina, with marbles painted in trompe-l'œil on the first floor.

Why go. A Rolli palace with its furnishings.
