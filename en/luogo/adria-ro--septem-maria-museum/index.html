Sèptem Mària Museum

Museums · Themed

Opened on November 21, 1998 in the engine room of the nineteenth-century hydrovora of Amolara, on the outskirts of Adria, in the area of one of the richest Etruscan necropolis beyond Po: it tells the management of the waters in Polesine from prehistory to today and preserves the two centrifugal pumps that pushed towards the sea the excess waters of the Lower Old Country. The name comes from an ancient incision at the entrance. Next to it, the Hostel Amolara organizes excursions by bike and on foot.

Why go. Understand how Polesine was torn from the waters.
