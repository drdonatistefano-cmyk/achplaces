Monte Santo di Lussari e santuario

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

At about 1,800 meters, on the meeting point between Carnic and Julian Alps, with views of Jof di Montasio, Jof Fuart and Mangart; the village is the highest in Friuli Venezia Giulia. The sanctuary, linked to an apparition of the Virgin, dates back to 1360, rebuilt and restored several times last in 2000; on foot you climb from the valley floor to the Pilgrim's Path.

Why go. The panoramic sanctuary between three cultures.
