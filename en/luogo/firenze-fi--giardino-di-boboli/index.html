Giardino di Boboli

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The Italian garden of the Medici behind Palazzo Pitti, with caves, fountains, statues and uphill avenues: model for the gardens of the European courts.

Why go. From Kaffeehaus to the city at your feet.

  • Where Piazza Pitti, Firenze
  • Entry €10 full day, €13 with reservation; reduced €3; €15 with the Garden Bardini (only in ticket office)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
