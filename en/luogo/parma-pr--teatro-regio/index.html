Teatro Regio

History and architecture · Historic theatres · Paid entry

The theatre wanted by Maria Luigia and inaugurated in 1829, temple of the Verdi opera, with the foyer, the audience and the Parisian chandelier from a ton.

Why go. Verdi's theater, backstage.

  • Where Strada Garibaldi 16, Parma
  • Entry €12 whole; €8 under 25, over 65 and groups; €3 under 18; free up to 6 years
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website emiliaromagnaturismo.it
  • Opening hours official page
