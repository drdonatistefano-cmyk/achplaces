Teatro Belluscio

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Inaugurated in 1988 with the first edition of the Festival of the Two Seas, it is decorated with mosaics with scenes of Greek theatre; it hosts events such as the Euro-Mediterranean Festival.

Why go. The outdoor theatre of summer festivals.
