Centro visite della Grotta di Lamalunga

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

At the karst cave where, over a swallower and about sixty meters of tunnels, lies the intact skeleton of the Man of Altamura, an adult male lived about 250,000 years ago, in a phase of passage towards the Neanderthals.

Why go. Where the man of Altamura rests.

  • Where Strada Provinciale 157, Altamura
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
