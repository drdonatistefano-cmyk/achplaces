Casa natale di Santa Maria Bertilla Boscardin

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

A peasant house where the saint was born in Brendola in 1888 and died in Treviso in 1922, she spent her first youth; next door a small church was built where many faithful come to pray. The house is preserved almost intact, with the small doors, beds and utensils of the peasant life of the time and some civil and religious clothes of the saint; numerous ex-votes, especially for intercessions in favour of children, which the saint assisted as a nurse. Beatified by Pius XII in 1952 and canonized by John XXIII on May 11, 1961, the place is cared for by the Dorotean Sisters.

Why go. A peasant house as it was, between popular devotion and ex-vote.
