Castello Incantato

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Viewpoint

In an olive grove, hundreds of faces carved in the rock and trees by Filippo Bentivegna (1888-1967), who lived there in hermitage for about half a century; it is among the most visited sites in the province after the Valley of the Temples.

Why go. The stone heads of a lonely artist.
