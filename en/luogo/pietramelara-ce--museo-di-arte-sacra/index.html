Museo di arte sacra

Museums · Themed

A small museum between the church and the municipality (former convent of the Augustinians next to the parish church of Sant'Agostino, renovated in the eighteenth century): it exhibits paintings, sacred objects and vestments of the church's treasure.

Why go. The treasure of the church of the village.
