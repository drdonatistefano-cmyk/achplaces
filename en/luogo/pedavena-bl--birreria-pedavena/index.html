Birreria Pedavena

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The brewery, founded in 1897, has rooms with painted walls, staircases, columns and copper mescita counters, and a large park with tennis courts and bowls; it houses exhibitions, tournaments, concerts and outdoor shows. A cycle path along the Colmeda stream takes about 10 minutes to the center.

Why go. The historic brewery in the painted rooms and in the park.
