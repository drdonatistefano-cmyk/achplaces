MA*GA Museo Arte Gallarate

Museums · Modern art

Founded in 1966 around the works acquired with the National Visual Arts Award of the city, established in 1950; since 2009 it is managed by a Foundation with founding members the Ministry of Culture and the Municipality, and since 19 March 2010 it has new name and headquarters in Via De Magri, with permanent collection, exhibitions and educational activities. Tuesday-Friday 10-18, Saturday and Sunday 11-19, Monday closed.

Why go. One of the great Italian contemporary art museums.
