Museo Diocesano d'Arte Sacra

Museums · Old masters

Next to the Cathedral, built at the end of the nineties and dedicated to the patrons Felice and Fortunato: historical section with part of the relics transferred from Malamocco in 1110, iconographic section with works by Paolo Veneziano, Cima da Conegliano, Giovanni Bellini, Palma il Vecchio, Andrea Vicentino and Andrea Brustolon, silverware of the cathedral and objects of popular devotion.

Why go. Paolo Veneziano, Cima and Bellini are just a few steps from the cathedral.
