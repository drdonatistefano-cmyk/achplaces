Palazzo Simoncelli e chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

17th century palace on the Corso, now home to the Town Hall and a Benedictine cloistered monastery, whose nuns carry on the tradition of embroidery. It includes the small church of St. John the Baptist (open to worship in 1633, today deconsecrated), with a fresco of the Baptism of Christ of the Domenichino.

Why go. A fresco by Domenichino in the town hall.
