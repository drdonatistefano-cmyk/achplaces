Villa Sforzesca

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in 1576 (1580 according to another card of the same portal) for Cardinal Alessandro Sforza, as a summer residence and base for the repression of the brigandage; after the death of the cardinal he fell for a long time. Recently restored, it houses gates, coats of arms, remains of fortifications and frescoes, and houses works by Carlo Alberto Torlai and architectural drawings by Fabio Rossi.

Why go. The residence of the Sforza on the Amiata.
