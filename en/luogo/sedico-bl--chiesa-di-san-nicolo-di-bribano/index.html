Chiesa di San Nicolò di Bribano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1502 by the brothers Buzzatti and today a national monument, it houses a wooden altar and a triptych attributed to Augustine by Lodi.

Why go. A Renaissance triptych in a country oratory.
