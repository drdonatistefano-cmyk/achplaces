Busto di Shakespeare

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The bust of William Shakespeare sculpted by Sergio Pasetto on the wall of Piazza Bra facing the Arena, with a quote from Romeo and Juliet.

Why go. Tribute to the bard.

  • Where Piazza Bra, Verona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
