Museo della Bambola e del Giocattolo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Inside the Fortress, born from the collection of Princess Bona Borromeo Arese; it is indicated as the most important in Europe of its kind. It has porcelain, wood and plastic dolls, handmade and standard, from Germany, Spain, Japan and other countries, with a core of French school, as well as wooden and tin toys and books and newspapers for boys from the eighteenth century to today.

Why go. The largest collection of dolls in Europe, inside a castle.

  • Where Via Rocca Castello 2, Angera
  • Entry Included in the Rocca ticket (€16.50, boys 6-15 years €11)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it
