Eremo di Monte Corona

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Built about 700 metres in a wood of beech and chestnut trees above the abbey, it preserves a 11th century crypt with Romanesque capitals and a 14th century fresco of the Annunciation.

Why go. We'll weave it in the woods above the abbey.
