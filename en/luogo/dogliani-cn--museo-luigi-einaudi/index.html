Museo Luigi Einaudi

Museums · City history

Dedicated to the most illustrious citizen of Dogliani, President Luigi Einaudi, was realized by the Municipality with the Luigi Einaudi Foundations of Rome and Turin and inaugurated on June 25, 2011. It is located in the Town Hall.

Why go. On the trail of the first President of the Republic.
