Palazzo Ferrero e Palazzo Gromo Losa

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

With Palazzo La Marmora they form the Cultural Polo del Piazzo, seat of exhibitions; the octagonal tower of Palazzo Ferrero is one of the symbols of the city and from its gardens you can admire Biella.

Why go. The noble residences.
