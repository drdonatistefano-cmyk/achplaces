Torre dei Lambardi

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

A tower of about 30 meters with a quadrangular plan on a hill just above the village, with views of Perugia and Trasimeno; built between XII and XIII century by the Knights Hospitallers, controlled supplies for Perugia, especially the fish of the north shore of the lake. Restored by the Municipality in the eighties, it hosts exhibitions on four floors and has a panoramic terrace; around a park with life path. Hours: July-August Mar-dom 10-13 and 16-19.30 (sab-dom also 21.30-23.30); April-June and September-Jo-dom 10-13 and 15-18; October-March by reservation. Info: tel. 075 8473078.

Why go. The terrace on the Trasimeno.
