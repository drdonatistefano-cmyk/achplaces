Museo storico della bicicletta «Toni Bevilacqua»

Museums · Themed

Born from the collection of Sergio Sanvido (1928-2015), restorer and collector of bicycles, donated in part to the Municipality in 2007: from the precursors, like a celerifer in wood of late eighteenth century, two Michaux and the bike Grand-bi, to the bikes from work, from city, military and as a child, to the bikes from race tied to Bottecchia, Coppi, Bartali, Saronni, Moser and Pantani, with accessories, shirts and covers of the Sunday of Corriere. Autonomous visit with tablet or app; open 10-13 and 15-18 on weekends from April to October (also on Fridays in July and August).

Why go. Two centuries of bikes, from the celeripher to Pantani.

seasonal opening, closed from November to March
