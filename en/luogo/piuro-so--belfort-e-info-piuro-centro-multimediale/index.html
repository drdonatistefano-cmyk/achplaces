Belfòrt e Info Piuro Centro Multimediale

History and architecture · Archaeology

In Borgonuovo the archaeological site of Belfòrt preserves the remains of the Palazzo dei Piaceri buried by the landslide of 1618, with large rooms, cellars and crotti built with care. In the nearby rural building the multimedia center has panels, video totems, a holographic projection on the landslide and a touch-screen; from here starts a path with audioguide downloadable via QR code.

Why go. Walk over the village erased from the landslide.
