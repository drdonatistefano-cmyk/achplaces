Palazzo d'Avalos – Musei civici

Museums · City history · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Founded perhaps by Giacomo Caldora at the beginning of the fifteenth century, passed to Guevara in 1496 and to d'Avalos in 1573, recovered since 1970: it houses the art gallery (Michetti, Palizzi), the archaeological collection on the Frentani and Histonium and a Neapolitan garden of eighteenth-century plant with terrace on the sea.

Why go. Art, archaeology and terrace on the sea.
