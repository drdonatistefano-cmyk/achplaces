Prato Nevoso

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

1,500 m station of the Mondolè Ski area (over 100 km of slopes and 30 lifts with Artesina and Frabosa Soprana): more than 36 km of slopes with views up to Monviso and Cervino, the panoramic cable car "La Rossa" can also be used by pedestrians, snowparks, snowtubing and night skiing.

Why go. Snow panoramic from Monviso to Matterhorn.
