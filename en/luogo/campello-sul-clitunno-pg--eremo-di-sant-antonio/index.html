Eremo di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

It was built at the beginning of the 10th century on a cave, where according to tradition St. Francis stayed in the 13th century. Succeeded in 1860, since 1926 it is a female Franciscan hermitage, the one of the "Allodole" of St. Francis.

Why go. Franciscan spirituality on the mountain.
