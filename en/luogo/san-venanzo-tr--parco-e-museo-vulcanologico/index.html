Parco e museo vulcanologico

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Opened in 1999 in a building dating back to the early nineteenth century next to Villa Faina, the museum has four rooms on the geology of local eruptions: Venanzite, lava rock with almost unique composition, a dinosaur egg from China and the skull of Elephas southernis found in the surroundings. The open-air park is about 2 km long on the large venanzite casting, partly emptied in the 1970s, between cones, craters, casts, rare minerals and the remains of a millstone quarry.

Why go. Walk on a lava flow of a volcano \"bonsai" .

the MiC card reports the museum closed every day, Umbriatourism indicates numbers for the reservation: check the opening before the visit
