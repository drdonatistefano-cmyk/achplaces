Fondazione Magnani-Rocca

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The Villa of Masterpieces, opened to the public on April 17, 1990, houses the collection of Luigi Magnani (1906-1984) with works by Gentile da Fabriano, Tiziano, Van Dyck, Rembrandt, Goya, Canova, Monet and Morandi; twice a year it hosts temporary exhibitions. Around the villa there is a large romantic park that can be visited by itself. Open from Tuesday to Sunday, closed on Mondays.

Why go. Masterpieces from Tiziano to Morandi in a villa in the green.

  • Where Via Fondazione Magnani Rocca 4, Mamiano di Traversetolo
  • Entry €9 whole (shows and collection), reduced €5 or €7; only park €3
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.magnanirocca.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
