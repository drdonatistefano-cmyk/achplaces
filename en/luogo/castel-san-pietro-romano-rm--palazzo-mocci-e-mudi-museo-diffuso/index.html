Palazzo Mocci e MUDI - Museo Diffuso

Museums · City history

Elegant palace of 1732 overlooking St. Peter's Square, home of the exhibitions of the MUDI (Museo Diffuso), including a section dedicated to Adolfo Porry Pastorel, mayor from 1952 to 1960.

Why go. The popular museum of the village.
