Villa La Bellotta

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Neoclassical villa of the late eighteenth-early nineteenth century about 1 km from Valconasso, purchased by the Rays in 1899 and renovated in 1914-15, when it received the façade in Venetian style with graffiti decorations and stables where Edilio Raggio bred the breed of thoroughbreds "Bellotta" (Bellotta); you arrive there from a avenue of lindens. Today it is the pastoral and holiday centre of the Diocese of Piacenza-Bobbio.

Why go. The linden avenue and the Venetian façade.
