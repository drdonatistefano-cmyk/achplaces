Torrione e Centro per la Scultura Contemporanea

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

In 1481 Francesco di Giorgio Martini built for Federico da Montefeltro the fortress on the Cappuccini hill, demolished in 1502 by Guidobaldo so that it would not fall into the hands of Cesare Borgia. It remains intact, downstream, the elliptical mast, connected to the fortress by the tunnel of the "Socrso Coverto" recently restored; today it houses the Center for Contemporary Sculpture linked to Eliseo Mattiacci.

Why go. Francesco di Giorgio Martini and contemporary sculpture.
