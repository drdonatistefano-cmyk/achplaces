Teatro Salieri

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Designed in 1911 by the Anonymous Theatre Society, with the works interrupted by the First World War, opened in 1933 and in 1934 it was named after Antonio Salieri; it replaced the ancient theatre of the fortress, abandoned in 1903 and destroyed by the bombs of the Second World War.

Why go. The theatre dedicated to the musician from Legnaghe.
