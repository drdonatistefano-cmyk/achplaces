Cattedrale di San Pietro Apostolo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Cathedral of the diocese of Tempio-Ampurias, behind the Town Hall: the first church is of the first thirteenth century, rebuilt between 1832 and 1839 and then reconsecrated. In granite, with a single nave with four chapels on each side and barrel vault; nineteenth-century marble furnishings with a genoese Baroque taste, fresco of St. Peter in the apse and organ Cyresa of 1980 with 1726 reeds.

Why go. The granite cathedral.
