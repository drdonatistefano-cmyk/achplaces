Castello Eurialo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Fortress of the beginning of the fourth century BC wanted by Dionysius I on the Epipolis plateau, about 8 km from the center: defensive moats and ruins on 1.5 hectares overlooking the city.

Why go. Dionysius' defenses on Epipolis.
