Burano e Museo del Merletto

History and architecture · Villages · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The island of colorful houses and lace, with the Museum of Lace in Galuppi Square and the church of San Martino with a canvas of Tiepolo and the bell tower of 56 meters.

Why go. The best view from the bridge called Tre Ponti.

  • Where Burano, Venezia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
