Miniere dell'Argento Vivo di Levigliani

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Indicated as the oldest mining site in Upper Tuscany, cited in a Pisan document of 1153: for centuries they gave the cinnabar used by Florentine workshops for red pigment, then mercury to work precious metals and tan the skins of felt hats. Abandoned in the seventies, they were recovered in the years 2000; shuttle from Levigliani included in the ticket.

Why go. The red of the cinnabar inside the mountain.

  • Where Levigliani, Stazzema
  • Entry €8, reduced €6
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.corchiapark.it
