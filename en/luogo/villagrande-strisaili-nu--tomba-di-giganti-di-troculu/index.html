Tomba di Giganti di Troculu

History and architecture · Archaeology

Main rest of a nuragic complex with two nuraghes and a village of the Bronze Age: the tomb, in granite blocks on irregular rows with the body still intact, was disassembled and rebuilt not far from the end of the twentieth century for the construction of the Nuoro-Lanusei highway.

Why go. A tomb of giants saved from the freeway.
