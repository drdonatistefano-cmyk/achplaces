Cascata del Varone

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Waterfall in a narrow gorge excavated by the Magnone stream, fed by Lake Tenno, with walkways and panoramic points; near there is a botanical garden.

Why go. A waterfall in the heart of the rock.
