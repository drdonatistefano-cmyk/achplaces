Chiesa matrice dei Santi Martino e Lucia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The mother church of Apricena, dedicated to Saint Martin and Saint Lucia, was entirely rebuilt in two or three years after the earthquake of 1627.

Why go. The mother church reborn after the earthquake of the seventeenth century.
