Tempio valdese di Villar Pellice

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises on the site of the ancient Catholic church of the village, often destroyed and rebuilt; the current building was built in 1706 thanks to the charity of the French Huguenot marquis Pierre de Belcastel, and its pyramid bell tower, built together with the temple, is unique in the Waldensian valleys. The interior with three naves, with barrel vault without decorations, is illuminated by seven windows and a rose window; the door and its key are from the beginning of the eighteenth century, while the wrought iron chandeliers were donated by the Waldensian Church of Milan after the demolition of the Romanesque church of San Giovanni in Conca. On the frieze of the portal the verse "Come, let us worship the Lord" from Psalm 95.

Why go. A sober early 18th century Waldensian temple with a unique bell tower in the valleys.
