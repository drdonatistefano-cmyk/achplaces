Orto Botanico di Napoli

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

The great botanical garden of the University Federico II at the foot of the hill of Capodimonte, next to the Real Hotel of the Poor, with thematic areas such as desert, peat bog, citrus grove and greenhouses; directed by Michele Tenore from 1810 to 1860.

Why go. A green oasis on Via Foria.
