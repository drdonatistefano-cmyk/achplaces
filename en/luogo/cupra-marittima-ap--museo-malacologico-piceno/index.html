Museo malacologico piceno

Museums · Science and university

Museum of shells with over 900,000 specimens, indicated as one of the most important malacological collections in the world, with also some specimens of shark; educational services, guided tours and book shop. Afternoon and seasonal opening: better call first (0735 777550).

Why go. 200,000 shells.
