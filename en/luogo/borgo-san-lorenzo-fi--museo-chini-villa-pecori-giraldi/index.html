Museo Chini, Villa Pecori Giraldi

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Ceramics, stained glass and Liberty decorations of the Chini family, born in Borgo San Lorenzo in 1906.

Why go. Then the Liberty route through the streets of the village.
