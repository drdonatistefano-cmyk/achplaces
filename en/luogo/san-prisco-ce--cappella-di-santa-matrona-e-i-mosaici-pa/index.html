Cappella di Santa Matrona e i mosaici paleocristiani

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A small funerary sacellus, perhaps annexed to the primitive early Christian basilica, with square plan with bare columns that hold four arches; in the apse a marble pool, perhaps a Capuan fountain of imperial age, is an altar and traditionally welcomes the remains of the saint. The mosaics, dated between the beginning of the fifth and sixth centuries, cover the vault with branches of vine, birds and a golden crater on a blue background and the lunettes with the blessing Christ, the divine throne with the roll of the seven seals and the symbols of the Evangelists.

Why go. Mosaics of the first Christianity among the oldest in Campania.
