Chiesa di Sant'Antonio Abate a Murle

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Started around 1470 and consecrated in 1621, it preserves a sixteenth-century altarpiece by the painter Pietro De Marascalchi.

Why go. A altarpiece of Marescalci in a hamlet church.
