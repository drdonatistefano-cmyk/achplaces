Parco Chigi

Nature and landscape · City parks

Park of 28 hectares annexed to the palace, planted by Savelli and modified by the Chigi, which owned it from 1661 to 1988: romantic garden admired by travellers of the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, with oaks, holm oaks and ancient laurels and ruins among the trees. In October 2023 only a small area was opened next to the palace, waiting for a network of paths; VisitLazio indicates the opening Saturday, Sunday and holidays 10-13 and 15.30-19.

Why go. Secular forest and ruins in the garden of the Chigi.
