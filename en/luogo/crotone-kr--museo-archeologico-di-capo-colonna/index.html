Museo archeologico di Capo Colonna

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 2002 inside the park, on a floor with three rooms (the Earth, the Sacred, the Sea): a corinzio helmet in bronze of the fifth century, a stone with the dedication to the athlete Phayllos, a statue of Zeus Meilichios and a small group in marble of Love and Psyche recovered at sea; around the Garden of Hera.

Why go. The Sacred and the Sea.
