Chiesa di San Floriano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Dedicated to the patron since the 12th century, in 1439 he passed to the Friars Minor Conventuals; for it Lorenzo Lotto painted the Deposition, the Annunciation and the Pala di Santa Lucia, today in Pinacoteca. Renovated in 1743 by Francesco Maria Ciaraffoni, deconsecrated in 1860, it has the dome frescoed by Francesco Mancini and today houses the Teatro Studio Valeria Moriconi.

Why go. The Lotto Church, today theater.
