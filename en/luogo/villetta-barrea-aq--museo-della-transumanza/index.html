Museo della transumanza

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the "Baraccone" , rural building nineteenth century bought by the Municipality in 1996, panels and an audiovisual tell the transhumance: life and work of the shepherds, wool and cheese, temporary equipment and shelters; educational area and workshop of products related to transhumance, laboratory by reservation.

Why go. The lives of transhumant shepherds.

discordant times: MiC every day 17-20, Municipality only July and August

  • Where Via Roma, Villetta Barrea
  • Entry €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
