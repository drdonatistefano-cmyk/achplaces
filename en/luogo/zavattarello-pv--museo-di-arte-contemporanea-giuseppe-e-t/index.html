Museo di arte contemporanea Giuseppe e Titina Dal Verme

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

From 2003 to the last floor of the castle, he exhibited Italian art from the second half of the twentieth century to today, born from the donations of the artists who exhibited there, and paintings of Count Giuseppe Dal Verme, to whom he is entitled with his wife Titina Gavazzi.

Why go. Contemporary art inside a medieval castle.

  • Where Via Carlo Dal Verme, Zavattarello
  • Entry €2, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
