Casa natale di Paolo VI

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

15th century country building on the road to the Trompia Valley, built by the Counts Lodron and purchased by the Montini family in 1863: here on September 26, 1897 Pope Paul VI was born. It preserves a sundial dated 1658, a seventeenth-century balcony in wrought iron above the entrance and a gallery of the seventeenth century with twelve medallions of landscapes of the months and signs of the zodiac. Left in 1997 by Eng. Vittorio Montini at the Opera for Christian Education in Brescia, is a pilgrimage destination and has become a biographical journey on the stages of the Pope's life and pontificate; next to it lives a community of Daughters of Mary Help of Christians who welcomes visitors. Open from Tuesday to Saturday 10-12 and 14.30-17, Sunday 14.30-17.

Why go. Enter the house where Paul VI was born.
