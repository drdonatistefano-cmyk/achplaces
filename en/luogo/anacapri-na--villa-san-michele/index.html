Villa San Michele

Museums · House museums · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built by the Swedish doctor Axel Munthe, it preserves the richest collection of ancient finds and works of art on the island and is the only nineteenth-century residence of Capri as it was.

Why go. Axel Munthe's house above the sea.

  • Where Via Capodimonte 34, Anacapri
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
