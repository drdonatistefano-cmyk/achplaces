Museo archeologico nazionale di Locri

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Born in 1971 as Antiquarium and National Museum since 1998, it recounts the Greek Locri with hundreds of finds, including the pinakes, 5th century BC terracotta votive tablets, and necropolis supplies.

Why go. Locri's pinakes.
