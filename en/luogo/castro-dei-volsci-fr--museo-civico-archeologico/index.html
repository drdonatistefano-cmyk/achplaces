Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Founded in 1994 in the former middle school of Madonna del Piano, it exhibits about 700 exhibits from prehistory to the early Middle Ages in eight sections plus a lapidary, especially from the excavations of the villa of Casale di Madonna del Piano; among the main pieces the funeral inscription of the child Artemisio (III century A.D.) and a plastic of the settlement on a 1:50 scale. Closed on Monday.

Why go. The history of the territory from Volsci to Longobards.

  • Where Via Antonio Carnevale 24, Madonna del Piano, Castro dei Volsci
  • Entry €2.60, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
