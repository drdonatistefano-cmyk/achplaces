Museo archeologico nazionale dell'antica Allifae

Museums · Archaeological

The history of the territory from prehistory to the Roman era: weapons, stone instruments, ceramic and glass vases and metal objects from Monte Cila, Roccavecchia di Pratella and from the necropolis, a part of fresco of IV style from a domus on the decuman and fragments of Roman mosaics; there are also coins, jewels and skylights from the excavations of the cryptoportico.

Why go. Allifae before and after Rome.
