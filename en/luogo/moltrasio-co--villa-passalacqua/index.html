Villa Passalacqua

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built at the end of the eighteenth century by the will of the Odescalchi on the ruins of a monastery of the Humiliates, it passed in 1787 to Count Andrea Passalacqua who transformed it into a sumptuous dwelling, with decorations by Giocondo Albertolli and frescoes by Andrea Appiani. Vincenzo Bellini stayed there between 1829 and 1833, finding inspiration for Norma, La strangere and La sleepambula.

Why go. The villa where Bellini composed his works.
