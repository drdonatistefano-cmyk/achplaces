Terme Regina Isabella

Local life and traditions · Spas

Spa complex of late nineteenth century engineer Nicola Cianelli, rethought between 1950 and 1954 by Ignazio Gardella: the Ionic colonnade becomes an abstract diaphragm, a rose body connects to the historical facade and an air bridge combines thermal baths and hotels; inside remain the double-height atrium and a Neapolitan majolica floor.

Why go. A piece of twentieth century architecture signed Gardella.
