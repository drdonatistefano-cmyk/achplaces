Municipio neoclassico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Neoclassical municipal palace, formerly the prison palace, built in the second half of the nineteenth century with the contribution of the Turin architect Angelo Santoné; today it houses the Art Gallery of the Municipality.

Why go. 19th century architecture in the main square.
