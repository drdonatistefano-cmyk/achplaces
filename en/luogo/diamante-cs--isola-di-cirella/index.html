Isola di Cirella

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

The second island of Calabria by extension, in the Regional Marine Park of the Cedars: limestone outcrop covered with Mediterranean scrub, with caves, coves and cliffs, seabeds with Posidonia oceanica and Pinna nobilis. At the top of the remains of the sighting tower, dated to the ninth century. You can catch up by boat.

Why go. An island of spot and crystal clear sea to be turned by boat.
