Museo etnografico dell'attrezzo agricolo «'L Çivel»

Museums · Themed

Housed in the ancient Cascinale dei Nobili, a large courtyard with rural houses that opens under an arched porch and is bordered by five churches of decreasing size, called churches "matrioska" , some used as exhibition halls. A multimedia path (consultable with tablets or smartphones) tells about life in rice fields, the work of peasants and worldly people, customs and songs; the second section is a gallery of wagons, tools and machines for processing rice. Open only by reservation; accessible to disabled people.

Why go. The civilization of rice in a rural court with five churches.
