Dolmen di Ladas e di Ciuledda

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The recent Neolithic "sepulturas de zigantes" (3500-2700 B.C.). Ladas, the most imposing of the four dolmens of Luras, is a tunnel about 6 meters long and over 2 meters high, covered by two large slabs and closed by an apse; the back cover plate measures about 15 m2. Next to it is Ciuledda, smaller and semicircular, on a granite bench overlooking the Limbara; from both come ceramics of the third millennium BC. Ladas can be reached with a walk of about 15 minutes from the village.

Why go. The most imposing dolmen of Sardinia, a few steps from the village.
