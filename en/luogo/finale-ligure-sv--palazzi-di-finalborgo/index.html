Palazzi di Finalborgo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The Town Hall Palace, renaissance of the fifteenth century modified in Spanish age; Palazzo Cavassola, which housed Pope Pius VII; the Palaces Gallesio, Brunengo and the Tribunal.

Why go. The palaces of the marquisate.
