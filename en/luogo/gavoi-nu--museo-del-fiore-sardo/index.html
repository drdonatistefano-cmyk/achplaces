Museo del Fiore Sardo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the noble house of the Lai family, it is dedicated to the cheese Fiore Sardo DOP: stone rooms with barrel vaults for sensory paths and tastings, traditional and modern tools of the cheesemaking with photographs of the Slovenian Žiga Koritnik, the documentary "Fiore Sardo" by Fabio Olmi and a plan for temporary exhibitions. A cumulative ticket with the Porcu Satta Museum House €7, reduced €5.

Why go. The DOP cheese of Barbagia, from pasture to table.

  • Where Via Margherita 9, Gavoi
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
