Chiesa ipogea di Sotterra

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In Gaudimare district, about 3 km from the village: church of Byzantine origin, remodelled in Norman age, finished under the ground level for landslides or floods and discovered in 1874 during the works of the above church of Madonna del Carmine. The frescoes, dating back to the seventh century, include the Annunciation on the sides of the apse, a group of saints with perhaps Mary Magdalene in the center, the Virgin Galaktotrefouse who feeds the Child with a pomegranate, St. Anthony Abbot and fragments of the Christ Pantocrator.

Why go. Byzantine frescoes in the ground.

FAI reports a highly endangered state of conservation; not indicated times
