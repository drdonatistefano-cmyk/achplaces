Italy › Abruzzo › Lama dei Peligni

Museo naturalistico archeologico «Maurizio Locati» e giardino botanico

Museums · Science and university

Museo-Laboratorio del Centro visita del Parco, dedicated to the Milanese zoologist who re-introduced suede here: two large halls with panels, dioramas, multimedia and finds. At the centre is the Abruzzo suede; showcases on traditional industries (wool, pasta) and the replica of a Majella cave with rock paintings; the archaeological section dedicated to F. Verlegia goes from prehistory to the Middle Ages, with Roman funeral epigraphs of imperial age and the cast of the Majella man, over 7,000 years old, from the Neolithic village of Fonterossi. Next to it, the botanical garden "Michele Tenor" with about 500 species, many endemic.

Why go. Chamois, prehistory and flora of Majella on one visit.
