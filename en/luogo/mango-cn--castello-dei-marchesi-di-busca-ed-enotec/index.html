Castello dei marchesi di Busca ed Enoteca Regionale Colline del Moscato

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It rises on the remains of a fortress of the twelfth century; the current building was built in 1630 by the Marquises of Busca, renovated in Baroque style and recently restored. On the ground floor, the Enoteca Regionale Colline del Moscato collects the products of over 80 wine producers and 20 food and wine producers of 52 municipalities between Cuneo, Asti and Alessandria, with tastings dedicated to Moscato d'Asti DOCG, Asti Spumante, Canelli DOCG and Strevi DOC; the castle also houses the Pinacoteca delle Langhe, concerts, exhibitions and events. Dominates the old village with views of the hills.

Why go. Moscato told in the castle that dominates the Langhe.

Wine shop closed in January and from Tuesday to Friday according to Visit MRL
