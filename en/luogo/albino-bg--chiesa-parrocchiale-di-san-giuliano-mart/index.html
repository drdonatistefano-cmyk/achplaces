Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giuliano Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the historic centre, it houses paintings by Moroni, Salmeggia, Cavagna, Zucco, Cignaroli, Barbello and Paduanino and frescoes by the recently discovered Marinoni workshop. Di Moroni the Crucifix between the Saints Bernardino and Antonio da Padova (about 1553-1575), the Trinity (1555), destined to the church of Fiobbio, and a standard painted on both sides in the sacristy.

Why go. The most important Moroni in his hometown.
