Museo diocesano d'arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

In the diocesan centre of pastoral activities: sculptures, paintings, frescoes, fabrics and sacred furnishings from the VII to the XX century by the churches of the diocese, with works by Pordenone and Pomponio Amalteo.

Why go. The Pordenone of the churches.
