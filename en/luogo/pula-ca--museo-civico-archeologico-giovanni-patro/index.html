Museo civico archeologico «Giovanni Patroni»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In a period building in the historic center, the finds of Nora and the territory: objects of daily use and worship, stele of the Punic tofet (VIII century BC), amphorae and anchors from the backdrops and the golden lamina with gorgoneion, symbol of the museum. Summer 9-20, winter 9-17.30, closed on Mondays; entire 2.50 €, reduced 2 €, guided tour included.

Why go. Nora's gold foil.

  • Where Corso Vittorio Emanuele 67, Pula
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
