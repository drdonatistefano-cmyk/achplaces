Espaci Occitan – Museo Sòn de Lenga

Museums · Themed

In the former Beltricco barracks, also home to the Institute of Occitan Studies, a multimedia sound museum: places on literary tradition, music, history and material life, folkloric and social valleys of the òc language, consultable in Italian, Occitan, English and French. In summer open Thursday and Friday 10-12 and 15-18 and Saturday 10-12; in winter only by booking for groups over 15 people.
