Ex Ospedale San Rocco

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The seat of the National Museums of Matera with the adjoining church of Cristo Flagellato and its frescoes, houses the restored ethnographic collection.

Why go. The ethnographic collection.

  • Where Piazza San Giovanni, Matera
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
