Chiesetta di San Martino Franco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Among the oldest churches in Valcamonica, dating back to the time of Charlemagne; in the sixteenth century it was used as a lazzaretto. It contains frescoes, including St. Martin on horseback that divides the mantle with the poor.

Why go. One of the oldest churches in the valley.
