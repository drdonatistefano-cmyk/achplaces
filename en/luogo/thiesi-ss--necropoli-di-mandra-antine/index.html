Necropoli di Mandra Antine

History and architecture · Archaeology

About 10 km from the village, four domus de Janas excavated in a trachytic ridge; the tomb III, called "painted" preserves colorful frescoes of the Neolithic (3rd millennium BC).

Why go. One of the rare prehistoric tombs painted in Sardinia.
