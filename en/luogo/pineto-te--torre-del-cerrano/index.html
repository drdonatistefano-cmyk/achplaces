Torre del Cerrano

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The coastal tower is made of square brick on a small height near the beach, built in 1568 in the sighting line desired by Carlo V against the incursions from the sea; the upper and lateral parts were added in the last century by the families who lived in it. Since 1981 it has been from the Province of Teramo; the historians link the archaeological remains in front of the tower to the port of ancient Hatria (Atri). Today it houses a research and veterinary training center, a library, the information point of the Marine Protected Area and a Museum of the Sea in preparation; guided tours.

Why go. The tower of Carlo V on the sea of Pineto.
