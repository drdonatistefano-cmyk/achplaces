Parco Archeologico di Cuma

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The first Greek colony of the West, founded in the second half of the 8th century BC by Euboic settlers of Pithekoussai: on the acropolis the temples of Apollo and Jupiter united by the Sacred Way and, at the foot of the hill, the Antro della Sibilla dug into the tuff; in the lower town forum, Capitolium and spa.

Why go. The Sibyl and the first Greek city.

  • Where SP 164, Pozzuoli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
