Museo Attilio Mussino - Museo di Pinocchio

Museums · Themed

In the basement of the ancient church of the Confraternita, he collects works and memories left to the village by Margherita Martini, second wife of illustrator Attilio Mussino, who spent the last years in Vernante founding a free art school open to all; at the cemetery a bas-relief shows Pinocchio crying on his grave.

Why go. The works of the illustrator who gave the colors to Pinocchio.
