Chiesa di Santa Margherita

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Late-Medieval church that preserves a cycle of frescoes of the sixteenth century by Pietro Guido da Ranzo; it is the starting point of the ring of the waterfalls of the Arroscia.

Why go. 16th century frescoes in a mountain church.
