Archeoparco del Basileus

History and architecture · Archaeology

Park on an area rich in finds, including the tomb of the Basileus, sovereign of the Peuketiantes buried here about 2500 years ago with weapons and symbols of power; the route illustrates the Basilicata between the 6th and 4th century BC with scenic locations and is visited in summer. It includes the ArcheoLab, an interpretation center with three multimedia rooms and final quiz, the Archeotour with contents via QR code and the Archeogame, treasure hunt in the historic center.

Why go. The grave of a king of Lucan from 2500 years ago.
