Catacombe dei Cappuccini

Places of worship · Monumental cemeteries

The cemetery and the catacombs of the Capuchins, next to the church of Santa Maria della Pace and the convent: one of the most evocative and mysterious places in Sicily, with a deep historical and anthropological value.

Why go. A unique time travel in Europe (not suitable for everyone).

  • Where Piazza Cappuccini 1, Palermo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
