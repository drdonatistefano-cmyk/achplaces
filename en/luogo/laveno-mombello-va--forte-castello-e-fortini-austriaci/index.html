Forte Castello e fortini austriaci

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Between 1850 and 1859 Austria made Laveno a "Imperial Regia Piazzaforte" (Imperial Direction), with a system of forts designed by the Austrian school. On Monte Castello an ancient fortress was transformed into a fort, reached by a steep road; at the tower an ossuary recalls the battles of 1859 of the Hunters of the Alps of Garibaldi. There are also the barracks of San Michele, made of exposed bricks, and the circular fort of Cerro, today a private residence.

Why go. Memories of the Risorgimento on the lake.
