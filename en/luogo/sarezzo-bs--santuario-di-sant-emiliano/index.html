Santuario di Sant'Emiliano

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

It dominates the village of Sarezzo and the middle Valle Trompia; according to an ancient plaque the church dates back to the 13th century and the restorations have emerged the foundations of a Romanesque apse. The 18th century wooden main altar houses the statues of the Saints Emiliano and Tirso and of the Madonna del Sodalto, and the altarpiece of Giovan Battista Botti (1746) depicts the Saints Barnaba, Vincenzo Ferreri and Fermo in veneration of the Virgin. It is reached by four paths from Sarezzo, Lumezzane, Marcheno and Gardone Val Trompia, or by car from Zanano up to about 500 m from the sanctuary.

Why go. The sanctuary-belvedere above the Trompia Valley, on foot from four villages.
