Botteghe dei vetrai

Local life and traditions · Crafts

In the workshop I Vetri di Sandro Bormioli we can see the craftsmanship of the incandescent glass; the Artistic Showcase Amantzio Bormioli produces blown or shaped objects in borosilicate glass (trophys, furnishings, sacred art, animals) and welcomes tourist and school groups on weekdays (tel. 0109 58254).

Why go. Watch the glass blow live.
