Monte Kronio e Stufe di San Calogero

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

On the mountain north of the city, a complex of karst caves from which come hot vapors, the Stufe di San Calogero still exploited; the Antro di Daedalo, described by Diodoro Siculo, is the oldest thermal structure of Sciacca.

Why go. The vaporous caves above the city.
