Colline del Prosecco e sentieri

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Follina is one of the twelve municipalities of the core areas of the Prosecco hills of Conegliano and Valdobbiadene, UNESCO World Heritage, along the Prosecco road; the territory offers hiking, mountain biking, cycling, horse trekking and Nordic walking.

Why go. UNESCO vineyards within walking distance.
