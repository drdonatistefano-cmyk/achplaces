Convento di Sant'Onofrio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

A small medieval monastery built by the Benedictines of Montecassino, according to Visit Lazio in the 11th century, on a hill west of the village; the feast of the saint is held on June 12.

Why go. The Benedictine monastery on the hill.
