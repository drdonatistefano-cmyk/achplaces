Museo per la regione dell'Ortles

Museums · City history

History and culture of the Ortles region: about a hundred years of tourism with shelters, funders and pioneers of Solda, an exhibition on the war of 1915-1918 and a collection of minerals in the area. Mostly set up by volunteers from a local association led by Konrad Knoll, which has funded a large part of it. Open all year round, every day 9-19.

Why go. A century of mountaineering and tourism in Solda.
