Necropoli di Su Crucifissu Mannu

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage

Necropolis of 22 domus de Janas excavated in a limestone bank not far from the town, in the Nurra, built by the recent Neolithic (3200-2800 BC) and used until the ancient Bronze Age (16th century BC), less than six kilometers from the altar of Monte d'Accoddi. Since 2025 it is one of the 17 Sardinian necropolis inscribed in the UNESCO World Heritage.

Why go. The Domus de Janas World Heritage Site at the gates of the city.
