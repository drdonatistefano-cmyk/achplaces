Chiesa e Palazzo San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The church rebuilt since 1638 and the Jesuit college at the bottom of Via Farini, today the Panizzi Library, with the ceiling painted by Sol LeWitt.

Why go. A Sol LeWitt ceiling in the library.
