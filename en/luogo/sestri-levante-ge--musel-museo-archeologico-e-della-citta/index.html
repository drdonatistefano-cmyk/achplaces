MuSel - Museo Archeologico e della Città

Museums · Archaeological

On the third and fourth floor of Palazzo Fascie Rossi, it tells the history of the territory from the Paleolithic to the Bronze Age, at the Roman era and the Middle Ages, until the development of the city in the twentieth century, with the bond with the sea (fishing, yards, tourism). Organizes conferences, presentations, activities for families and exhibitions; building with elevator. Info: IAT 0185 478530, info@musel.it.

Why go. From prehistory to the city of the two seas.
