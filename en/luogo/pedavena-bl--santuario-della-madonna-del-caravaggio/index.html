Santuario della Madonna del Caravaggio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In Travagola, built on the ruins of a medieval castle after, from 1420, Venice ordered its demolition; it preserves the altarpiece of the Belluno painter Francesco Frigimelica

Why go. A altarpiece of Frigimelica where a castle stood.
