Torrente Noce e ciclopedonale

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

At the foot of the village the Noce, indicated by the National Geographic among the ten best rivers in the world for rafting, descends with professional guides; along the stream runs the cycle path of the Val di Sole between meadows and pastures, and the paths in the woods around the village are crossed in every season on foot or by bike.

Why go. Rafting and bike along the Walnut.
