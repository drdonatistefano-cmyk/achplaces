Chiesa di San Giovanni Evangelista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest monument in the village, dated 1329, is located in an alley on the right of the nineteenth-century parish; it preserves a Christological cycle and a universal Judgment painted by Byzantine masters.

Why go. Byzantine 14th century frescoes hidden in an alley.
