Basilica di Santa Maria de Giulia

Places of worship · Basilicas

A sixteenth-century façade with a four-storey bell tower, inside a nave and two aisles: it preserves an anonymous author of San Michele Arcangelo and an altar triptych by Pavanino da Palermo dated 1472.

Why go. A 15th century triptych in the heart of the village.
