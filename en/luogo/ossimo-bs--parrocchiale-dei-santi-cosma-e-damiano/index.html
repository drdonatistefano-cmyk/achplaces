Parrocchiale dei Santi Cosma e Damiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of Ossimo Inferiore rebuilt in 1730 under the direction of the Milanese foreman Domenico Tettamanti; the bell tower was completed in 1780 and in 1905 Fortunato Canevali made important changes.

Why go. The 18th-century parish church of Ossimo Inferiore.
