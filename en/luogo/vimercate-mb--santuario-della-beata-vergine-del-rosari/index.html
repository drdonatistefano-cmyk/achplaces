Santuario della Beata Vergine del Rosario

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Designed by Fabio Mangone and Francesco Maria Richino, Milanese architects of the seventeenth century. The Salvatore's chapel, with stuccoes, frescoes and Baroque paintings, and the chapel of St. Catherine, with a altarpiece by Lattanzio Gambara, stand out.

Why go. The baroque chapels of a sanctuary signed by great Milanese architects.
