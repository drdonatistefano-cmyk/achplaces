Museo diocesano dell'Ogliastra

Museums · Old masters

On the first floor of the Bishop's Seminary, a building dating back to the early 20th century, the four sections of the course range from minerals, fossils and Nuragic, Punic and Roman finds to the history of the diocese from the judicial period, to the sacred art of Ogliastra (silver, vestments, parish registers and rare books) and to documents on the arrival of the Salesians. Among the main pieces a parchment in Hebrew and Latin of the thirteenth century found in the binding of a sixteenth-century book, a Madonna of Monserrato in sandstone of the fourteenth century and a small bronze of Hellenistic Hercules.

Why go. Treasures and history of the diocese of Ogliastra in one path.
