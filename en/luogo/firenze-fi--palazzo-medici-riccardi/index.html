Palazzo Medici Riccardi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The first palace of the Medici, where Cosimo il Vecchio and Lorenzo il Magnifico lived, with the Chapel of the Magi of Benozzo Gozzoli.

Why go. The Magi parade with portraits of the Medici.

  • Where Via de' Ginori 2, Firenze
  • Entry €15 whole; €10 from 18 to 25 years and university students; free up to 17 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website musefirenze.it
  • Opening hours official page
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
