Museo etnografico Coumboscuro della civiltà provenzale

Museums · Themed

In the hamlet of Santa Lucia (Sancto Lucio in Provencale), where since the 1950s the cultural association Coumboscuro has contributed to the rediscovery of Provencal culture in Italy; the museum presents local traditions and is considered one of the most interesting collections of the Alpine valleys. Every first week of September the village hosts the Roumiage de Setembre, preceded by the crossing of the Alps of the French neighbors.

Why go. The culture of the Provençal valleys of Cuneo.

Open only by reservation
