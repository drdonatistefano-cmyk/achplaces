Museo Civico del Territorio «Santa Parasceve»

Museums · Archaeological

The museum recounts the territory through finds of more eras: traces of frequentation of the Middle Paleolithic (developed phase of the Musterian culture), apulian craters, epichysis and kyathos of the peucet culture (VI-II century BC), objects of the Roman municipality of Genusia including a tabula patronatus of 395 A.D., lay majolica from the rock villages of Casale and Revolt and ceramics of the 11th century. It takes its name from the Benedictine monastery of Santa Parasheve, built with the castle after the Norman conquest at the end of the 11th century.

Why go. From prehistory to the Middle Ages, the story of Ginosa in one path.
