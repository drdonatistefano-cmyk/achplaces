Villa Carlotti e Museo Civico

Museums · City history

It was built on a 15th-century rural house in the Vimercati area and passed to the Carlotti family in 1601, who completed it in 1632 with a large garden with statues and enlarged it at the beginning of the 18th century; bought by the Municipality in 1937, since 1952 it is municipal seat. On the ground floor the Civic Museum, born from the finds collected by the master Giovanni Solinas and expanded by the researchers led by Mario Marangoni, exhibits archaeological and paleontological finds, objects of the Great War and the late 14th century by the church of the Holy Sepulchre, attributed to the Master of Santa Anastasia; the most suggestive room is the Hall of Dreams, of the early seventeenth century, decorated with grotesques. Closed on Monday.

Why go. The 14th-century Complaint of the Master of Santa Anastasia.
