Collegiata di Santa Maria della Colonna e San Nicola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Three-nave church with Romanesque bell tower, awarded the Blue Shield for the protection of cultural heritage: it houses the fourteenth-century icon of the Madonna delle Grazie and numerous frescoes.

Why go. A treasure chest of art in the ancient village.
