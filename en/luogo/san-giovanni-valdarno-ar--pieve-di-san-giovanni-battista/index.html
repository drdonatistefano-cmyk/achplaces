Pieve di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The baptismal church of the city, begun in 1312 next to the disappearance Porta Sant'Andrea, in front of the Palazzo d'Arnolfo: classic façade with pediment and three arched porch on Tuscan columns decorated with rounds of Robbiana school, inside with a single nave with wooden cover.

Why go. The fourteenth-century parish church in front of the palace.
