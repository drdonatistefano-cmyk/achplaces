Castello di Grinzane Cavour e Museo delle Langhe

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The 11th century castle on the hill among the vineyards, where he lived and was mayor Camillo Benso of Cavour: it houses the Museum of the Langhe on the peasant civilization and the Cavurian relics. Member of the UNESCO site.
