Ecomuseo di Cascina Moglioni

Museums · Themed

Traditional farmhouse of the Parco delle Capanne di Marcarolo with exhibitions and multimedia facilities on the local life and economy; the roof is made of chestnut wood, around centuries-old chestnut trees, fruit trees and a new orchard of ancient local varieties of apples, pears and plums. It has a theater in nature that in summer hosts shows and meetings.

Why go. The peasant life of the Apennines in a farmhouse in the park.
