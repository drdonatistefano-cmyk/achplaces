Torre del Feudo

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Tower built around the eighteenth century on an outlying rock, with irregular octagonal plan, with still intact walls. It is located about 10 km from the center, at the junction of the Jumenta.

Why go. An eighteenth-century octagonal tower in the wheat countryside.
