Ponti della Valle (Acquedotto Carolino)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The large canal bridge of the Carolino Aqueduct, which Carlo di Borbone entrusted to Luigi Vanvitelli to feed the water games of the Reggia: he worked from 1753 for 17 years, with a cost of 622,424 ducats, for a pipe of about 38 km from the sources of Fizzo at the foot of the Taburno. Here the aqueduct emerges in a structure with three orders of arches 60 meters high and about 500 long, remembered by two epigraphs of 1759 at the level of the road. From the foot of the arches a road climbs along the Longano mountain between the ruins of the church of San Carlo and the mills built by Ferdinand IV in 1795. Free access every day; the upper walkway opens only during the initiatives of the Reggia.

Why go. An eighteenth-century work of engineering on a European scale, a UNESCO heritage.
