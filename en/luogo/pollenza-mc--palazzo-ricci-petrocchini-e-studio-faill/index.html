Palazzo Ricci Petrocchini e Studio Failla

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace of the Ricci Petrocchini family, to which belonged Father Matteo Ricci, protagonist of the mission in China started in 1582. Inside the studio of the self-taught painter Fabio Failla (Lucca 1917 - Roma 1987), descendant of the Ricci.

Why go. Matteo Ricci's family home.
