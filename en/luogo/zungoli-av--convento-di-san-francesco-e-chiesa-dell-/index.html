Convento di San Francesco e chiesa dell'Incoronata

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The convent of the Friars Minor Reformed rises at the foot of the village, outside the walls; it dates back to the second half of the XVI century, on the foundations of the church of San Cataldo collapsed after the earthquake of 1456, and was largely rebuilt after the earthquake of 1930. In the adjacent little church, recently restored, the Madonna dell'Incoronata, patron saint of shepherds and tractures, is kept.

Why go. The Madonna of the shepherds and of the tratturi.
