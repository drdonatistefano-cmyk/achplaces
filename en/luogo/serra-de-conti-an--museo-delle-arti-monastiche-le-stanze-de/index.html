Museo delle arti monastiche «Le Stanze del Tempo Sospeso»

Museums · Themed

Set up in the monastery of Santa Maria Maddalena delle Clarisse, built in 1586 on the ruins of a previous monastery and enlarged in the eighteenth century; the annexed church, of 1603, has elliptical plan and decorations of Borrominian inspiration.
