Museo civico Villa Visconti Venosta

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

Residenza dei Visconti Venosta: left wing rebuilt at the end of the 17th century, right wing rebuilt at the end of the 19th century by the Marquis Emilio, Minister of Foreign Affairs, and public park of 20,000 m2. Donated to the Municipality in 1982 by Marquisa Margherita Pallavicino Mossi, it preserves historical environments, period furniture, the doors of an ancon of Cipriano Valorsa (1597), a Redeemer on the Sepulchre of Scuola Ferrarese of the sixteenth century, the library and the family archive. Only with guided tour at 10.30, 14 and 16, recommended booking.

Why go. An intact noble residence in Valtellina.

  • Where Via Visconti Venosta 2, Grosio
  • Entry €6, boys 11-17 €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
