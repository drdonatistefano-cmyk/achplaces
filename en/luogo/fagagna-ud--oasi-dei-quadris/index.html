Oasi dei Quadris

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Avifaunistic oasis of municipal property in the hills of Fagagna, born with a project to re-introduction of white stork, today visible throughout the country. In 2025 69 storks and 52 ibis hermits were born there, a species of which the oasis hosts about a quarter of the 800 specimens in the world; it also hosts ducks protected as elevator, dairy and tobacconist bites, and hosts about 10,000 visitors a year.

Why go. Storks and ibis hermit at your fingertips.
