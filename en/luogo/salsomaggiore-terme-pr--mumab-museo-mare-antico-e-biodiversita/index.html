MuMAB – Museo Mare Antico e Biodiversità

Museums · Science and university

In the Stirone Park, it brings together the former paleontological and naturalistic museums: the fossils of the Stirone bed, when the Po Valley was sea, with the only known skeleton of the whale Plesiobalaenoptera fortellii, the immersive room of the whale Wednesday and Friday 15-19, Saturday and Sunday 9-13 and 15-19.

Why go. The only whale of his kind.
