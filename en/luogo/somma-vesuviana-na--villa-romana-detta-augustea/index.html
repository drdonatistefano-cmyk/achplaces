Villa romana detta «Augustea»

History and architecture · Archaeology

Discovered in the 1930s and excavated since 2002 by the mission of the University of Tokyo, the villa was buried by the eruption of 472 A.D. (Pollena's) The believed statue of Augustus is a Dionysus with a puppy of panther; monolithic columns in marble of Asia Minor, white mosaics, apses with tritons and nereids and a wine cell with over thirty dolias. On site there are copies: the originals are at the Archaeological Museum of Nola.

Why go. A wine villa buried by Vesuvius in the 5th century.

the site is temporarily closed, visitable only in the extraordinary openings of the Superintendence
