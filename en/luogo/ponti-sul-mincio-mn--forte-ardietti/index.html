Forte Ardietti

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Fort of the Austrian Quadrilateral built after the First War of Independence in two phases (1853-1859 and 1859-1861), designed for 612 men and 25 cannons; passed to the Kingdom of Italy in 1866, it was fortress until 1918 and ammunition storage until 1998. Well preserved and almost entirely original, exhibits artillery pieces from the sixteenth century to the Korean war; audio guide in three languages.

Why go. A strong Austrian still intact.
