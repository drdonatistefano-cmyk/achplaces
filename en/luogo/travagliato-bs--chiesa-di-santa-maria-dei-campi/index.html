Chiesa di Santa Maria dei Campi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church with frescoes by Vincenzo Civerchio da Crema; the regional portal describes it in two pages with different dates: one dates back to the 14th century, the other says it was built in the 16th century, with remarkable 16th century frescoes.

Why go. The frescoes of Civerchio in a country church.
