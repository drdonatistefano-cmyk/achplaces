Tomba di Giganti di Pascareddha

History and architecture · Archaeology

In Badu Mela, on the northern slopes of Mount Deu, in the cork woods, dates back to the Middle-Recent Bronze (1700-1400 BC). In the territory also the sacred source of Li Paladini (II millennium BC) and the nuraghe Agnu flanked by a dolmen.

Why go. Nuragic archaeology in the cork forest.
