Acropoli, chiesa di San Pietro e cisterna romana

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the acropolis the church of St. Peter stands on the podium of the Roman temple of Juno Moneta, of the third century BC, and inside it remain visible the original walls of the cell in square blocks. Below is a large Roman cistern, now used for cultural events.

Why go. A church inside a Roman temple.
