Museo delle Arti e Tradizioni Popolari - Cultura del Gesso

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Documentation centre opened in 1994 in the east wing of the noble floor of the castle: it collects more than one hundred decorative motifs of the plaster ceilings of the Piedmontese peasant houses, the oldest dated 1580, with salt on the extraction of the plaster, its uses in the construction and finds of the peasant life.

Why go. The plaster ceilings of the peasant houses, a unique decoration.

  • Where Castello, Via Alfieri 4, Magliano Alfieri
  • Entry €5, both museums €7, families €10
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Official website www.castellodimaglianoalfieri.com
