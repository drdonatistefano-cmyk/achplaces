Abbazia di San Salvatore e cripta

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The Benedictine abbey of the 8th century, an influential religious center of southern Tuscany: the crypt under the presbytery has columns carved with human and animal faces of over a thousand years ago.

Why go. The crypt is the strong piece.
