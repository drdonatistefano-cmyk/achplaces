Oratorio di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small Romanesque building, probably from the 12th century, already mentioned in the donation of Ludovico II of 864: restored several times, it preserves fragments of frescoes. Just outside the center.

Why go. A Romanesque oratory outside the door.
