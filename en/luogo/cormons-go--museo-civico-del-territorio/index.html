Museo civico del territorio

Museums · City history

In the outbuildings of Palazzo Locatelli, with entrance from the inner garden: on the raised floor a thematic section dedicated to the cormonese sculptor Alfonso Canciani (1863-1955); ground floor and first floor host temporary exhibitions of history, art and local culture during the year.

Why go. The works of Canciani and exhibitions on the territory.

MiC card (updated 2020) indicates "closed" every day; check openings with the Municipality (tel. 0481 637152)
