MAST - Museo della città

Museums · City history

In the historic center near Piazza Mazzini, in a Renaissance building, tells the city from prehistory to today following two threads, water and weaving, documented as early as the 13th century and became in the 20th century textile district. The first open section goes from the Lombard age to the end of the sixteenth century and brings together marble, silver, codes, wooden sculptures and painted fabrics. Open from Wednesday to Sunday and holidays, closed in most of August; fully accessible, with elevator.

Why go. The history of Castel Goffredo between water, Gonzaga and weaving.
