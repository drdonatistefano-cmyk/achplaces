Museo dei fossili e dei minerali (Museo dell'Alamanno)

Museums · Science and university

In the eighteenth century Palazzo Felici: about 500 local fossils of marine fauna of the lower Pliocene (cyrripeds, about sixty species of molluscs, sea urchins, shark teeth, fossil leaves) and finds from the world, including mammoth fangs of the North Sea, an egg of dinosaur from China and an egg of Aepyornis. About 1,200 minerals, including a 26 kg block of stilbite with natrolite, and a fluorescent mineral cave at the Wood lamp; on the first floor about 300 paintings made with ten thousand shells by Elio Ceccotti. In the building also the Environmental Education Center and the Ecomuseo, archive of the popular knowledge of Fermano.

Why go. From the Pliocene sea to the minerals that light up in the dark.
