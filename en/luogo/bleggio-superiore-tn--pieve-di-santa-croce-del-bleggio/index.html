Pieve di Santa Croce del Bleggio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The seat of the ancient Pieve del Bleggio is one of the rare sacred buildings in pure Renaissance style of Trentino, rebuilt in the sixteenth century by the Lombard architect Antonio Gras on the model of Santa Maria Maggiore in Trento; a Romanesque crypt leads to the temple of the XI-XII century. Frescoes by Carlo Donati and, in the chapel, a wooden altar with Pietà and scenes of the Passion that has guarded the Holy Cross considered miraculous since 1624. Open every day 9-17.

Why go. The pure Renaissance among walnuts.
