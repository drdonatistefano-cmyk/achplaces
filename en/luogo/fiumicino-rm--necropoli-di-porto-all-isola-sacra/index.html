Necropoli di Porto all'Isola Sacra

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Over 200 funerary brick buildings from the end of the 1st to the 4th century AD along the Via Flavia Severiana, with Latin inscriptions and scenes of crafts that tell a population of traders, freedom and small entrepreneurs.

Why go. The city of the dead of the imperial port.

  • Where Isola Sacra, Fiumicino
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Accessibility To be checked with the operator
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it
