Frantoio ipogeo Trappitello del Duca

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Built in 1518 under a farm outside the old town and entirely dug into the rocky bank, it is the only remaining of the four underground mills that the village had around 1870. A staircase with a barrel vault goes down to the large room with the base of the millstone pool; around the "slaves" for the olives, the deposits, the rest of the "trappetari" and the mule stable, with presses rebuilt to the Calabrian and the Genoese. He worked for over three and a half centuries until 1940; he was abandoned, he was bought by the municipality in 2000 and equipped with information panels.

Why go. Like oil was made in Salento, going down into the rock.
