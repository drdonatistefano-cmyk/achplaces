Casa museo Giulio Mazzarino

Museums · House museums

Municipal Museum dedicated to Cardinal Giulio Raimondo Mazzarino, Prime Minister of France, born in Pescina on July 14, 1602 and died in France in 1661: documents on his life, reading room, specialized library on Abruzzo and space for contemporary art exhibitions, in a building of 1971-72. Open Tuesday and Thursday 10.30-19, Saturday and Sunday 10.30-12.

Why go. Following the cardinal who ruled France.
