Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta e Museo civico di arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

The church houses the Civic Museum of Sacred Art: 17th century paintings, works by the Crivellesque school and the oil altarpiece on the table . Antonio Abate enthroned, S. Antonio da Padova e S. Job: (2,25 x 1,70 m) by Vincenzo Pagani (1490 circa-1568); a reliquary with remains of many saints and a fragment of cross, probably medieval, considered of the cross of Christ, gift of Niccolò IV.

Why go. Vincenzo Pagani's shovel.

Municipal museums only partially usable (Let's Marche)
