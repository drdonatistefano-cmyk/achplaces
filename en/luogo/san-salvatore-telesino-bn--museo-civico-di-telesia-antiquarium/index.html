Museo civico di Telesia (Antiquarium)

Museums · Archaeological

Set up in rooms of the Benedictine Abbey and opened from June 19, 2010, it collects finds of Sannitica and Roman age, from the fourth century BC to the second century AD, coming from the excavations of the ancient city of Telesia; the route follows the Sannita period, the Roman colony and the destruction of the city, in an environment with frescoes of the fourteenth century and underground spaces. Booking not required; the ministerial card does not record active times, referent to Pro Loco.

Why go. The finds of ancient Telesia inside the abbey.
