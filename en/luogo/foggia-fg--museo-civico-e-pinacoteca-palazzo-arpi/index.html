Museo Civico e Pinacoteca – Palazzo Arpi

Museums · Archaeological

Established in 1931 at Palazzo Arpi: archaeological and ethnographic sections, picture gallery, lapidarium and the reconstruction of two daunie tombs of the IV-III century BC, the Tomb of Medusa and that of the Knights, with the mosaics of the House of lions and the Panthers of Arpi.

Why go. Daunia's painted graves.
