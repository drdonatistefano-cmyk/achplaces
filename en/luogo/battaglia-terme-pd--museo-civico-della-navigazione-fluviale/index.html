Museo civico della navigazione fluviale

Museums · Themed

In the former municipal slaughterhouse of Ortazzo, the old district of the boatmen, it collects hundreds of objects, boats and their parts, models, vintage photos and tools donated by boaters who transported goods and people from Vicenza to the lagoon; it tells over seven centuries of inland navigation. Open Saturday and Sunday.

Why go. A rare museum in Italy on boaters' lives.
