Basilica dei Santi Eusebio e Maccabei

Places of worship · Basilicas

Parish church built in 1940 and completed in the 1940s, in the city center, with a Latin cross plan with three aisles; the facade has three entrances.

Why go. The mother church of the city, in the heart of the center.
