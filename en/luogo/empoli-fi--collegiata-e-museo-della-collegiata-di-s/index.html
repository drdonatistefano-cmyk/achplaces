Collegiata e Museo della Collegiata di Sant'Andrea

Museums · Old masters

The Romanesque façade in white and green marbles and the museum founded in 1859, one of the oldest ecclesiastical museums in Italy, with Christ in Masolino's Pietà and works by Lorenzo Monaco and Filippo Lippi.

Why go. Masolino half an hour by train from Florence.
