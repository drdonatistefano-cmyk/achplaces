Complesso feudale Malingri: Castello e Parco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The Castle-Forte dates back to the 11th century and preserves medieval characteristics, the Castle-Piano (Palace) dates back to the 14th century with frescoes from the 15th century. The park of about two hectares, in the Grandi Giardini Italiani circuit, has 40-50 meters high centuries-old trees, an elliptical stone pool, rhododendrons and azaleas in spring and summer hydrangeas; the avenue leads to the Chapel of Ognissanti. Group visits by reservation (tel. +39 335 5244080), at least 2-3 hours.

Why go. Seven centuries of a family among castle, palace and garden.
