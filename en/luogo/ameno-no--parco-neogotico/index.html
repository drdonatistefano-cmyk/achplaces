Parco Neogotico

Nature and landscape · City parks

The park next to Palazzo Tornielli, once private and accessible only from the palace, summer residence of the family, is open to the public since 1920 and has been restored after a long intervention: neo-Gothic architecture, a historic stone glacier, centuries-old trees and autochthonous aromatic plants.

Why go. A neo-Gothic garden with the old stone icebox.
