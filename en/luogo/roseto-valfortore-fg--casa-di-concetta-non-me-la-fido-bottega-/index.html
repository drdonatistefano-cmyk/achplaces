Casa di «Concetta non me la fido», bottega del fabbro e forno a paglia

Local life and traditions · Crafts

In the historic center you can visit the house of "Concetta" not me the trust, the house of craftsmanship, the shop of the blacksmith-maniscalco and the ancient straw oven, still working and used in events and recurrences for bread with yeast mother and flour ground at the old mill.

Why go. The shop life of an Apennine village.
