Diga e lago di Ridracoli

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

The 1982 dam, 103,5 meters high, forms a lake in the heart of the park; there is the Hydro-Ecomuseum and you sail with an electric boat.

Why go. The lake in the forests seen by the boat.
