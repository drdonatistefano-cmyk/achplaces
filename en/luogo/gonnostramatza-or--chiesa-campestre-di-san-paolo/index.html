Chiesa campestre di San Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient parish church of the deceased village of Serzela, dating back to the 13th century and rebuilt in the 17th century; a stone in the country sanctuary recalls the destruction of the nearby village of Uras by pirates led by Barbarossa.

Why go. The inscription that inspired the pirate museum.
