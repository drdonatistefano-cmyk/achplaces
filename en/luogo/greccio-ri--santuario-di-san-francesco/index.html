Santuario di San Francesco

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Sanctuary of the Holy Valley clinging to the rock in a wood of holm oaks, home to St. Francis since 1217; here in Christmas 1223 he set up the first nativity scene. In the Chapel of the Nativity scene, dug into the rock, a fresco of the fifteenth-century school of Giottesca with the Nativity and the Nativity of Greccio and, on the outer wall, a fresco of the Master of Fossa; you can also visit the dormitory of San Francesco, the church of San Bonaventura (XIII century), the refectory and the cave of Blessed Giovanni da Parma. Open every day from 9 am to 6 pm.

Why go. The place of the first nativity scene in history.
