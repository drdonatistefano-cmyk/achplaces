Torre normanna

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

It rises between the IX and X century as castle fortress and completed in Norman times, it is 27 meters high, with walls more than 5 meters thick on 4 levels. In 1333 the castle was donated to the Clarisses for the monastery of Santa Chiara; the tower maintained military function until 1657 when Duke Ippolito Revertera donated it to the monastery. National monument since 1931.

Why go. The symbol of Tricarico, visible from all over the valley.
