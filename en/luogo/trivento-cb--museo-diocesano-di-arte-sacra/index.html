Museo diocesano di arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

The Museum of Sacred Art of the Diocese of Trivento, inaugurated in 2001, preserves works from the 14th to the 19th century, including wooden statues and sacred vestments.

Why go. The treasure of a diocese that dates back, by tradition, to the end of the first century.
