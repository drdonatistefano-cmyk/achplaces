Palazzo Attems Petzenstein

Museums · Old masters

Palace built in the first half of the eighteenth century for the Attems family, seat of the Pinacoteca dei Musei provinciali: masters of the eighteenth century Veneto, portraits of the nineteenth century including those of Giuseppe Tominz and works of the twentieth century.

Why go. The Gorizia art gallery.
