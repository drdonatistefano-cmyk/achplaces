Castello Orsini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built in the fifteenth century by the Orsini, lords of Fiano: a robust central cylindrical mast, thirty meters high and crowned with blackbirds, with a square side tower. It leans on Porta Capena, the entrance of the village; inside a courtyard with the staircase that goes up to the noble floor.

Why go. The crenellated beast symbol of the country.
