Museo naturalistico degli Alburni - Fondazione I.Ri.Di.A.

Museums · Science and university

Founded in 1997 in a central position with respect to the Alburni Mountains, reference of its activities, it is managed by the I.Ri.Di.A. Foundation. (Institute for Environmental Research and Didactics). The permanent exhibition of European fauna has over 1,300 vertebrates, with a remarkable collection of birds (about 1,200 European species according to the Park), over 60 species of mammals, decapod crustaceans of the Mediterranean and over 20,000 insects; the museum also carries out research, teaching and wildlife monitoring.

Why go. The fauna of Europe collected at the foot of the Alburni.
