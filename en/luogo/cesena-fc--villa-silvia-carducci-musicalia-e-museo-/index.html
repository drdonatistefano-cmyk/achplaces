Villa Silvia-Carducci, Musicalia e Museo Carducciano

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The villa frequented by Giosuè Carducci, with the Carduccian Museum and Musicalia, the museum of mechanical musical instruments.

Why go. Piano and music box playing again.
