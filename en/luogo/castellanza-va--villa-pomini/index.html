Villa Pomini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in 1919 for Ottorino Pomini in a late eclectic style: three floors and a panoramic tower, exposed brick facades and plaster with two-lighted windows, arches, loggias and graffiti, a stone plinth of Moltrasio and a long portico with red brick arches. In 1982 Luciano Pomini offered it to the municipality as a cultural centre: today it houses exhibitions, courses and the library donated by the family, in a park of about 5,300 m2.

Why go. An eclectic villa from the beginning of the twentieth century, today home of culture.
