Museo Salvatore Ferragamo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the medieval Palazzo Spini Feroni, the museum opened in 1995 by the Ferragamo family on the history of the "star shoemaker" and of the made in Italy.

Why go. Diva shoes.
