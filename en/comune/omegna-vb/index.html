What to see in Omegna

The main town of the Cusio, lying on the northern shore of Lake Orta and crossed by the Nigoglia, which brings the waters of the lake northwards; home of Gianni Rodari (1920), with the Fantasy Park, the Forum dedicated to industrial history, the church of Sant'Ambrogio and the Roman Porta.

Chiesa di Sant'Ambrogio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Mother church on Piazza Beltrami, built around the year 1000 and renovated several times: simple Romanesque facade, baroque interior with three naves with side chapels; of the Middle Ages remain south side, lantern of the dome, bell tower and facade. It houses a altarpiece painted in 1547 by Fermo Stella da Caravaggio and the tomb of San Vito martyr, patron saint of Omegna.

  • Where Piazza Beltrami, Omegna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · distrettolaghi.it

Centro storico e Porta Romana

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The medieval Roman Gate is the only surviving of the five gates (Maggiore, Salera, Segnara, Castello, Valle) that in the fifteenth century gave access to the walled village; the Rodari Museum is located in the historic center, reachable with a small ascent.

  • Where Centro storico, Omegna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it · italia.it

Ponte Vecchio sullo Strona

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Built by the Sforza on the Strona stream at the turn of the fifteenth century, it has a sharp sixth arch and a low one, with the central stack resting on a rock in the stream.

  • Where Torrente Strona, Omegna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it · italia.it

Lungolago di Omegna

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lakefront follows the curved bank at the northern end of Lake Orta, a lake of glacial origin, divided in two by the Nigoglia, which flows northwards bringing the waters of the lake into the Strona, the Toce and finally into Lake Maggiore. It is the starting point for boat trips, canoeing or kayaking and mountain biking to Monte Zuoli.

  • Where Lungolago, Omegna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Forum Omegna

Museums · Themed

A multipurpose cultural centre founded in 1998 in a former steel mill, with the contribution of the European Union and its citizens; it is part of the Parco della Fantasia (Fantasia Park) Gianni Rodari. On two floors he exhibits home objects of the local companies (Alessi, Bialetti, Lagostina, Girmi, Calderoni and others) and tells the industrial history from the nineteenth century to the present day, with temporary exhibitions.

  • Where Parco Maulini 1, Omegna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.omegna.vb.it · italia.it · distrettolaghi.it

Parco della Fantasia e Museo Rodari

Museums · Themed

Cultural center for children that enhances the work of Gianni Rodari, born in Omegna in 1920; it is divided into a ludotheque, educational museum (the Rodari Museum, in the historic center) and gardens on Monte Zuoli, outside the city.

  • Where Centro storico, Omegna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it · italia.it

Monte Zuoli e massi coppellati di Cireggio

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On Mount Zuoli, reachable by mountain bike from the lakefront and overlooking the lake, a large boulder carries prehistoric incisions in cups; in the same area two ... slide masses, polished inclined rocks, were used in an ancient fertility rite. From the top the panorama embraces Lake Orta and the mountains.

  • Where Monte Zuoli, Cireggio, Omegna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Gaudenzio a Crusinallo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the twelfth century with two apses; the baptistery was added in the fifteenth century. The façade has hanging arches in cones and a bezel with cross over the portal.

  • Entry To be checked

Chiesa di San Maiolo ad Agrano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the XII-XIII centuries with the altar of the Rosary made by Lombard artists called "i Fiamminghi" (Flemish).It preserves the mummy of a woman of the seventeenth century, the "Morta di Agrano" (Morta of Agrano), for a long time object of popular devotion and numerous ex-vote.
