What to see in Gualdo Cattaneo

Medieval village on the slopes of the Martani Mountains dominated by the Rocca dei Borgia (1494-500), where in 1624 Galileo stayed; the territory is an open-air museum of castles of the 13th-XIV centuries, from Barattano to San Terentiano, Marcellano, Saragano and Grutti.

Rocca dei Borgia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built between 1494 and 1500 and known as "dei Borgia" in honor of Pope Alexander VI, it was the last fortification of a system of border disputed between Spoleto, Todi, Foligno and Perugia. Triangular plant with three round towers, conical, connected by underground walkways; the highest, 20 meters on 5 floors, has a base of 80 meters of circumference. In 1624 Galileo Galilei stayed there, who called it a small compendium of the universe.

Chiesa di Sant'Agostino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest church in the country, founded by the Benedictines in 1136 and passed in 1258 to the Augustinian hermits. The sandstone façade with an ogival portal in pink and white stone and the figure of St. Michael the Archangel, patron saint; the bell tower rests on an ancient defensive tower. He holds a Crucifixion in 1482 of the workshop of the pupil, two seventeenth-century paintings by Andrea Polinori and works by Pier Paolo Sensini and Francesco Providoni.

Castello di Barattano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

A few kilometers from Gualdo, indicated as the most characteristic archetype of the area: large walls, medieval entrance and high towers. The church of San Bartolomeo, dating back to the thirteenth century, has frescoes from the 16th to the 11th century and two wooden angels from the sixteenth century.

Castello di San Terenziano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Fortified village of the fourteenth century in local stone with square plan; the church is formed by two overlapping churches, the lower one of the 11th century with Romanesque crypt and the upper one of the late thirteenth century. There are also the church of Sant'Apollinare (XIII-XIV century) and that of Our Lady of Graces (XV century).
