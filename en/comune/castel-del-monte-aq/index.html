What to see in Castel del Monte

Medieval village about 1,346 meters in the National Park of Gran Sasso and Monti della Laga, symbol of transhumance, with tower houses, covered passages called sports, the tower of San Marco and a widespread ethnographic museum; from here you go up to Campo Imperatore.

6 places to visit in Castel del Monte, in the province of L'Aquila (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo, Ricetto e sporti

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village, built on five hills with a plant that appears to be a star from a distance, grew around the Ricetto, fortified nucleus of the twelfth century; it preserves tower houses, the Governor's Palace of 1559 and the "sports," arc passages dug under the roads on which the houses rest.

  • Where Castel del Monte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: gransassolagapark.it · italia.it

Torre e chiesa di San Marco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish church at the foot of the large square tower, symbol of the village: born as a watchtower, used as such until the mid-nineteenth century and then transformed into a bell tower. On the altar there is the oldest representation of the coat of arms of the country.

  • Where Castel del Monte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: gransassolagapark.it · italia.it

Chiesa della Madonna del Suffragio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the edge of the village, overlooking the Germano Valley, it is known for its golden wooden baroque altar and the procession of July 2nd with which the shepherds were welcomed back.

  • Where Castel del Monte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: gransassolagapark.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Donato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church to the north of the village, outside the walls, dating back to about the year 1000, which replaced an older church in San Donato Vecchio, near Mount Bolzano.

  • Entry To be checked

Museo civico etnografico

Museums · Themed

Museum spread in the historic center, in disused environments that reconstruct the traditional interiors with the objects of pastoral life; the route has six stages: the mill, the oven of the dance, the handicrafts of wool, the ancient house, the shepherdhood and the popular religiosity.

  • Where Via del Municipio, Castel del Monte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Campo Imperatore

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the village you climb to the Capo la Serra pass (about 1,600 m) and then to the Campo Imperatore plateau, about 25 km long and defined the largest of the Apennines, with canyons, seasonal lakes and spring blooms; near the Astronomical Observatory at about 2,100 m and the Gran Sasso cable car.

  • Where Campo Imperatore, Castel del Monte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · gransassolagapark.it
