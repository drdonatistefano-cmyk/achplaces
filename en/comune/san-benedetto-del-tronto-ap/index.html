What to see in San Benedetto del Tronto

The heart of the Riviera delle Palme: a 5 km promenade with thousands of palm trees, one of the largest fishing ports in Italy with the Museum of the Sea Pole, the High Country with the Tower of Gualtieri and the Sentina Reserve at the mouth of the Tronto.

8 places to visit in San Benedetto del Tronto, in the province of Ascoli Piceno (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open San Benedetto del Tronto in the guide with map and filters

Lungomare e Riviera delle Palme

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 5 km from the Rotonda Giorgini in Porto d'Ascoli, over 30 meters wide: inaugurated in 1932 on a project by Luigi Onorati, with thousands of palm trees, oleanders and tamarisks, thematic gardens, the bike path and fine sandy beaches with low seabeds.

  • Where Lungomare, San Benedetto del Tronto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Torre dei Gualtieri

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The hexagonal tower of the High Country, the castle's castle was granted in 1146 to the Gualtieri brothers by the bishop of Fermo; restored by Giuseppe Sacconi in 1901, it has the pendulum clock of 1902 and is the symbol of the city.

  • Where Piazza Sacconi, San Benedetto del Tronto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Polo Museale del Mare

Museums · Themed

In the wholesale fish market on the north pier brings together the Museum of the Maritime Civilization of the Marches, the Museum of the Amphoras, the Museum of the Itticus and the Antiquarium Truentinum.

  • Where Molo Nord, San Benedetto del Tronto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Pinacoteca del Mare

Museums · Modern art

Inaugurated in 2009 at Palazzo Piacentini in the ancient center, it is part of the Museum of the Sea.

  • Where Palazzo Piacentini, San Benedetto del Tronto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · eventi.turismo.marche.it

MAM – Museo d'Arte sul Mare

Museums · Modern art · Free entry · Viewpoint

Permanent open-air museum along the southern pier, with sculptures and murals.

  • Where Molo Sud, San Benedetto del Tronto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · eventi.turismo.marche.it

Riserva Naturale Sentina

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 180 hectares established in 2004 between Port of Ascoli and the mouth of the Tronto: sandy cordons, humid backdunal areas and brackish prairies, two ponds for birdwatching, the Tower on the Port of 1543 and cyclopedonal paths.

  • Where Porto d'Ascoli, San Benedetto del Tronto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Palazzina Azzurra

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Rationalist building of 1935 at the mouth of the river Albula, on the promenade.

  • Where Lungomare, San Benedetto del Tronto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Mercato ittico all'ingrosso

Local life and traditions · Historic markets · Free entry
