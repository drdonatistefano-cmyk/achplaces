What to see in Aosta

The ancient Augusta Praetoria, ... the Rome of the Alps: arch, port and the Roman theatre still standing, the medieval complex of Sant'Orso and the cathedral, among the mountains of the valley.

27 places to visit in Aosta, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 8 of the 10 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Teatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Masterpiece of the Roman provincial architecture of the High Empire: the southern facade 22 meters high, lightened by three orders of arches and windows; the cavea housed almost four thousand spectators.

  • Where Via Porta Praetoria 1, Aosta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it

Arco di Augusto

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Erected to celebrate the victory over the Salassi in 25 BC, almost 12 meters high, at one fornice, at the beginning of Via Sant'Anselmo.

  • Where Via Sant'Anselmo, Aosta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it

Porta Praetoria

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

The eastern gate of the city between two mighty square towers, which has come to us intact: one of the best preserved doors of the Roman world.

  • Where Aosta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
