What to see in Sant'Angelo Muxaro

Borgo on a hill above the river Platani, identified by tradition with the Sicana Camico, with a necropolis of more than two hundred cave tombs and tholos, the Archaeological Museum in Palazzo Arnone and the natural reserve of the Grotto of Sant'Angelo Muxaro.

Necropoli di Sant'Angelo Muxaro

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

On the southern side of the hill, above the Platani (the ancient Halykos), more than two hundred tombs dug into the rock between the 13th and the 6th century BC, cave and tholos: a long corridor leads to a circular chamber with convex vault. The place is linked to the myth of Camico, the king of Sicily Kokalos and Daedalo; from here comes the group of goldsmiths most important of the indigenous Sicily, with a patera today at the British Museum.

Museo archeologico di Palazzo Arnone

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

In the Palazzo Arnone in Piazza Umberto I, the museum preserves a selection of funeral furnishings of the necropolis, with materials from the 12th to the 6th century BC, and finds of the territory.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I 24, Sant'Angelo Muxaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it

Riserva naturale Grotta di Sant'Angelo Muxaro

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Reserve of 21 hectares established in 2000 and managed by Legambiente Sicilia: it protects the Grotto of Sant'Angelo Muxaro, also called Cave Ciavuli, in total 1,760 meters long on two branches and reached by the entrance hall with an almost flat path, and the Infantino Hooker, with a vertical well of about 10 meters. Host amphibians, reptiles and bats.
