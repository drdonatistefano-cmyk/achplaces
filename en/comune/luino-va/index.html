What to see in Luino

A small town on the Lombard shore of Lake Maggiore, with a lakefront of about 2 km shaded by plane trees, the old port and a historic center of shops and palaces; to see the church of Carmine of the fifteenth century, the great international station nineteenth century and, on Wednesday, the historic market.

8 places to visit in Luino, in the province of Varese (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico, porto vecchio e lungolago

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lakefront, about 2 km long, runs between plane trees and palaces; the old port, closed by historical buildings, is crowned on the pier by a column with the Madonna. Under the porch of Caffè Clerici were found the writers Piero Chiara and Vittorio Sereni; Piazza Libertà has been the port of the boats since 1826, while via Felice Cavallotti, the ancient counter of the merchants, now houses ateliers and art workshops. More secluded, on the hill, Piazza San Francesco was the center of the ancient town.

  • Where Piazza Libertà, Luino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Platani di Luino

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

In Piazza Garibaldi, a few steps from the lake, there are several large plane trees, three of which with trunks of more than 4 meters circumference. They are the last survivors of Luino's historic row of plane trees, largely destroyed in recent decades for plant protection reasons.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Viale XV Agosto, Luino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Stazione internazionale di Luino

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Station built from 1881 on the Gottardo line towards the Swiss border, on a project directed by the Livorno engineer Alfredo Giambastiani; the large metal shelter is the work of Giovanni Battista Biadego. After work the railway beam exceeded 1 km in length by 131 m wide, with 14 tracks, 5 of which for travellers under the shelter; the tourist guides of the time often cited it as a monument to progress.

  • Where Luino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Museums · Themed

Mercato del mercoledì

Local life and traditions · Historic markets · Free entry

Every Wednesday, from morning to late afternoon, more than 350 stalls occupy the streets of the center and the lakefront: it is indicated as the most important market in the area of Lake Maggiore. The market privilege was granted to the Rusca accounts by Emperor Charles V on September 5, 1541.

  • Where Centro e lungolago, Luino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
