What to see in Palmoli

The medieval village of the hinterland is 727 meters above the river Treste, born around the castle built in 1095 by Pandolfo di Sangro; today the Castello Marchesale houses the town hall and, in the tower, the Museum of peasant civilization, while in the village you can visit the church of Santa Maria delle Grazie and the sanctuary of the Madonna del Carmine.

Castello Marchesale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the highest part of the village, the ancient Monteverde, guarding the Treste: around a watchtower the oldest part was built in 1095 by Pandolfo di Sangro; between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries the tower was transformed into a fortified noble residence. It dominates the massive tower with a polygonal base with a shoe, cylindrical body and Guelph merli on hanging arches; the complex includes walls, passetto, palazzo marquisale in the 15th century and the chapel of San Carlo, eighteenth century, with stone portal. Restored and owned by the municipality, it hosts the town hall, events and parties.

  • Where Via S. Nicola, Palmoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.palmoli.ch.it

Sources: comune.palmoli.ch.it

Museo dell'arte contadina - Museo dei castelli abbandonati

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Opened in 1978 in the Torre del Castello Marchesale, it extends from the ancient stables to the top of the tower: tools of blacksmiths and weavers, clothes and tools of the peasants, the reconstruction in scale 1:1 of a peasant house and a small section of finds of the Roman age of the territory; documents also the fortified architecture of the Vastese. Managed by the Vastese Foundation. According to the MiC card is open in July, August and the first weekend of September on Saturday and Sunday (9-12 and 16-19), in the other months by reservation 24 hours in advance; the municipality indicates for visits the number 350 1350970.

  • Where Castello Marchesale, Via S. Nicola, Palmoli
  • Entry €2, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.palmoli.ch.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.palmoli.ch.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt in the middle of the eighteenth century on the site of a church considered of 1300, of which remains the fourteenth-century bell tower. He keeps in a bronze urn, in the chapel dedicated to him, the body of St. Valentine, patron saint of Palmoli, an eighteenth-century organ carved and gilded and two water saints carved with a snake and a fish.

  • Entry Free

Santuario di Maria SS. del Carmine

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry
