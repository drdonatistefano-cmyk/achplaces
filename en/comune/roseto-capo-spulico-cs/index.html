What to see in Roseto Capo Spulico

Borgo dell'Alto Ionio Cosentino was born in the 10th century, with a medieval centre surrounded by walls and panoramic views; on the sea the Castello Federiciano, symbol of the village, and along the coast the remains of the tower of Capo Spulico, with the Blue Flag promenade.

5 places to visit in Roseto Capo Spulico, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Federiciano (Castrum Petrae Roseti)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Impressive castle overlooking the Ionian Sea, erected in the thirteenth century, when the village lived its maximum splendor; it is among the marine fortresses that defended the Calabrian coast, perfectly preserved and visitable even inside.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · enit.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Historical center of medieval plant, enclosed by the walls, rich in art and history and with breathtaking views of the sea.

Torre di Capo Spulico (Torre Spaccata)

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Remains of the watchtower along the coast, known as the tower of Capo Spulico or Torre Spaccata.

Lungomare e spiaggia

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The promenade of Roseto is awarded with the Blue Flag; the beach, also the one in front of the castle, is several times nominated for the "Places of the Heart" of the FAI.

  • Where Roseto Capo Spulico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: enit.it · fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Museo etnografico

Museums · Themed

More than two thousand objects reconstruct the past of Roseto, his people and his crafts: agricultural tools and domestic tools, ancient toys, a school hall of epoch, carpenter shops, blacksmith, shoemaker and barber, weaving frames; a photographic section with portraits of rosetans, photo library, classroom and library.
