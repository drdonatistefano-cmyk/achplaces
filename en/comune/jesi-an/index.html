What to see in Jesi

Town on the hill of the lower valley of the Esino, home of Frederick II of Swabia (born here on 26 December 1194) and Giovan Battista Pergolesi, closed by a well-preserved wall: Piazza Federico II with the Cathedral of San Settimio, the Palazzo della Signoria of Francesco di Giorgio Martini, the Lotto della Pinacoteca of Palazzo Pianetti, the Pergolesi Theatre and, just outside, the Riserva Ripa Bianca.

Piazza Federico II

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

It rises on the area of the ancient Roman Forum; here on 26 December 1194 Frederick II of Swabia was born, under a great imperial pavilion. The Cathedral of San Settimio, the Rococo Palazzo Balleani, the convent of San Floriano, dedicated to the patron saint, and Palazzo Ripanti, are just a few steps away.

  • Where Piazza Federico II, Jesi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Cattedrale di San Settimio

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built between the 13th and 14th centuries by Giorgio da Como, it was demolished and rebuilt between 1732 and 1741 on a project by Filippo Barigioni; bell tower by Francesco Matellicani (1782-84), facade by Gaetano Morichini (1889), bronze door by Paolo Annibali (2001-2004) and Christ Pantocrator painted in the apse by Biagio Biagetti in 1939.

  • Where Piazza Federico II, Jesi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Museo Federico II Stupor Mundi

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Presented as the first museum in the world dedicated to the life and work of the emperor, it is a virtual route of projections and installations in 16 rooms, in the Palazzo Ghislieri where Frederick II was born in 1194. From October to May open Thursday and Friday 15-20, Saturday and Sunday 10-20.

  • Where Piazza Federico II 3, Jesi
  • Entry €7, reduced €5, family €19
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.jesi.an.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Opened on November 13, 1966 in the former Palazzo Ripanti, next to the Cathedral: nine rooms with works of art and crafts of religious inspiration and, since 1990, a section of contemporary art. Open Monday to Friday 9.30-13.30 and Sunday 16-19; it does not have a separate ticket but is part of the unique ticket of the museum network.

  • Where Piazza Federico II 7, Jesi
  • Entry Single ticket €15, reduced €10
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.jesi.an.it · italia.it

Palazzo Pianetti, Pinacoteca civica e Galleria d'arte contemporanea

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The eighteenth-century palace has been built since 1748 for the Marquis Pianetti and completed around 1781, with an Italian garden; on the noble floor there is the rococo gallery of stuccoes and paintings, more than 70 metres long. The Pinacoteca has as reference Lorenzo Lotto, with works performed between 1512 and 1535 (Deposition, Annunciation, Madonna delle Rose, Pala di Santa Lucia, Visitation), and over two hundred 18th-century pharmacy vases of the ancient hospital; in the palace also the Archaeological Museum and the contemporary Art Gallery.

  • Where Via XV Settembre 10, Jesi
  • Entry €5 one museum, €6 two, €8 three
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: jesieduca.comune.jesi.an.it · comune.jesi.an.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Palazzo della Signoria e Biblioteca Planettiana

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

Renaissance brick palace built between 1486 and 1498 on a project by Francesco di Giorgio Martini, with decorations by Michele di Giovanni da Milano and his son Alvise; the tower, designed by Andrea Sansovino, collapsed with the earthquake of 1657. It houses the Planetian Library, founded in 1859, with about 110,000 volumes, manuscripts and incunabula, including an autograph letter by Giacomo Leopardi from 1825; guided tours to the Antique Funds Rooms on Thursday at 11 am and at 12 noon.

  • Where Piazza Angelo Colocci, Jesi
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.jesi.an.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Casa museo Colocci-Vespucci

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

Residence of the Marquis Colocci Vespucci of the second half of the sixteenth century, at the highest point of the city, with furnishings, objects and paintings of one of the main aristocratic families of Jesine, related to Amerigo Vespucci; reopened in 2013. Guided tours only by reservation on the first Thursday of the month and the third weekend, at 16, 17 and 18, for groups of up to 10 people; the museum is on the fourth floor, reachable only on foot.

  • Where Piazza Angelo Colocci, Jesi
  • Entry €6, reduced 6-25 years €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.jesi.an.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Teatro Giovan Battista Pergolesi

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built between 1790 and 1798 by Francesco Maria Ciaraffoni, modified by Cosimo Morelli and inaugurated in 1798; known for acoustics and rococo decorations, it has the historical curtain of Luigi Mancini (1850) and hosts every year the Festival Pergolesi Spontini.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica 9, Jesi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Mura e Arco Clementino

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

A wall of about 1.5 km, built between the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries on the Roman route and enlarged in the fifteenth century by Baccio Pontelli, with at least seven doors; on Corso Matteotti the Arch Clementino, erected in 1734 in honor of Pope Clement XII for the abolition of the duty on wheat.

  • Where Corso Matteotti, Jesi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Floriano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Dedicated to the patron since the 12th century, in 1439 he passed to the Friars Minor Conventuals; for it Lorenzo Lotto painted the Deposition, the Annunciation and the Pala di Santa Lucia, today in Pinacoteca. Renovated in 1743 by Francesco Maria Ciaraffoni, deconsecrated in 1860, it has the dome frescoed by Francesco Mancini and today houses the Teatro Studio Valeria Moriconi.

  • Where Piazza Federico II, Jesi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Pietro Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It rises on a Roman building and was rebuilt from the mid-eighteenth century on a project by Gaetano Fammilume; the statues of the apostles of Stefano Montrocchi were destroyed by the Jacobins in 1798. On the high altar the delivery of the keys of G. Curly.

  • Where Piazza Franciolini, Jesi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Abbazia di Santa Maria del Piano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Between the old town and the Esino, founded in the fifth century, it is the only abbey in the area that survived the Middle Ages; the neoclassical aspect with a single nave is the result of the eighteenth century restoration. The crypt preserves a sarcophagus, three tombs of the III-IV century and a fresco with the face of an angel of the X-XI century. Free entrance.

  • Where Via Santa Maria 9, Jesi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.jesi.an.it · italia.it

Museums · Old masters

Riserva naturale regionale Ripa Bianca

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Established in 1977 along the Esino at the east entrance of the city, on an area once occupied by landfill, quarry and fields: it houses the largest gauze of the Marche, with over 100 nests of nitthor, herons and herons ashes, a view on the top of the calanchi and a bridge of rafts floating on the river. The starting point is the Nature Centre; times on the reserve website.

  • Where Via Amos Zanibelli 3/bis, Jesi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.jesi.an.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it
