What to see in Nereto

Centre of the Vibrata Valley with the Benedictine church of San Martino, one of the oldest in Abruzzo, and the mother church with frescoes of the miracle of the Madonna della Consolazione, which according to tradition saved the country from French sack in 1798.

4 places to visit in Nereto, in the province of Teramo (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Benedictine building a little outside the center, indicated as one of the oldest churches in Abruzzo, with simple stone façade; above the portal two small bas-reliefs, the patron who cuts the cloak to give it to a poor and an Agnus Dei.

Chiesa parrocchiale di Maria SS. della Consolazione e Campana del Bicentenario

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A neoclassical mother church, restored several times between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, which preserves the main sacred works of the country, including the cycle of frescoes by the painter Toscani on the miracle of the Madonna della Consolazione: according to tradition in 1798 Our Lady and her angels saved the country from the plundering of French troops. In front, next to the Town Hall, the Bell of the Bicentenary merged in 1998 by the Fonderie Marinelli of Agnone, with the inscription Â"Città della Solidarietà" (City of Solidarity).

Museo delle tradizioni venatorie

Museums · Themed

Path set up by the vibrant hunting club: the halls exhibit trophies, embalmed specimens, weapons and hunting gear, ancient cartridges, books, prints and calls, each with caption; it welcomes schoolchildren and groups for hunting and naturalistic lessons, including protected species.
