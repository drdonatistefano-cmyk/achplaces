What to see in Latiano

City between Brindisi and Taranto with the baronial palace of the Imperials and its museums dedicated to the Apulian peasant traditions and underground.

3 places to visit in Latiano, in the province of Brindisi (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Latiano in the guide with map and filters

Palazzo Imperiali

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The 12th-century Baronial Palace of the Imperial family: it houses the family square, part of the Marseille collection, the Museum of Photography and Messapian collections; the most valuable room is the Sala Flore on the first floor.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Latiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Museo delle arti e tradizioni di Puglia

Museums · Themed

It reconstructs the late 19th century peasant house in three rooms, domestic, handicraft and agricultural, with a detached section dedicated to ceramics.

  • Where Latiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Museo del sottosuolo

Museums · Science and university

Founded in 1977 by naturalist Pietro Parenzan, since 1993 in the premises of the sixteenth century former abbey of Santa Margherita.
