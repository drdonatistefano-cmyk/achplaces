What to see in Prato Carnico

At the top of the Pesarina Valley, under the Pesarine Dolomites: Pesariis, the village of watches with the museum and twelve monumental watches along the streets, Casa Bruseschi and the stables of Orias.

6 places to visit in Prato Carnico, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Prato Carnico in the guide with map and filters

Museo dell'Orologeria Pesarina

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Three centuries of watches, from the end of the seventeenth century to today, in the village where watchmaking was for three centuries the main economic activity: eighteenth century pendulums, tower clocks, palette watches and recent production. For ongoing work at the moment it is not accessible to those with motor disabilities.

  • Where Frazione Pesariis 124, Prato Carnico
  • Entry €3 whole, €1.50 reduced; free with FVGcard
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Percorso dell'orologeria monumentale di Pesariis

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Twelve monumental clocks scattered across the streets of the country, a real open-air museum freely accessible at any time.

  • Where Pesariis, Prato Carnico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Casa Bruseschi

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

A well-preserved bourgeois house of the seventeenth century: the kitchen with the fogolâr, the dining room with the Galvani ceramics of Pordenone, the room, the studio of lawyer Battista Bruseschi and a room with a set. It belongs to the parish of Saints Philip and James; openings on indicated dates, information to the tourist office.

  • Where Pesariis, presso Casa della Pesa, Prato Carnico
  • Entry Offer of €1 per person
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Piccolo museo storico delle macchine per la fotografia e la cinematografia

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In Pieria's former dairy, the collection collected in thirty years by Odi Gonano: dozens of cameras, cameras and amateur projectors, large projectors of the cinemas of Pesariis, Comeglians, Tolmezzo and Udine restored by the owner and 35 mm professional cameras. Visits on request.

  • Where Frazione Pieria, Prato Carnico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Stavoli di Orias

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

About fifteen old stables that housed herds during transhumance, under the Forata Crete and Mount Pleros; you can reach them with a short walk from Truia.

  • Where Orias, Prato Carnico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Rifugio De Gasperi e Giardino botanico del Clap Grant

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Fratelli De Gasperi hut, over 1,700 meters on the Clap Grande, is reached with a path marked by the Centre Pian di Casa and is base for climbs in the Dolomites Pesarine; next, the botanical garden with about 200 species of mountain plants, between orchids and lilies.

  • Where Clap Grande, Prato Carnico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it
