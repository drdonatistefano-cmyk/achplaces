What to see in Montjovet

Ancient toll village on the Roman Gallie road, dominated by the castles of Saint-Germain and Chenal, the municipality with several villages of the region.

4 places to visit in Montjovet, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Saint-Germain

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Castle of the 11th century that has marked the history of the territory; remain a beautiful tower and some stretches of walls, reachable by a short walk.

  • Where Saint-Germain, Montjovet
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it
