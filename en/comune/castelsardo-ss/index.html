What to see in Castelsardo

Fortified village on the Gulf of Asinara, founded by the Dorias as Castelgenovese, then Castel Aragonese and from the eighteenth century Castelsardo: the castle with the Museo dell'Intreccio Mediterraneo, the Cathedral of Sant'Antonio Abate overlooking the sea, the fourteenth-century Santa Maria delle Grazie with the Black Christ and, in the surroundings, the Rock of the Elephant with its domus de janas, UNESCO Heritage since 2025.

Castello dei Doria e Museo dell'Intreccio Mediterraneo

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Fortress of the Doria and court residence, probably built in the 13th century (the tradition indicates 1102) with the name of Castelgenovese to control the routes to Genoa; it was for some years residence of Brancaleone Doria and Eleonora d'Arborea. Taken by the Aragonese in 1448, which extended its towers and bastions, it became Aragonese Castle until the eighteenth century. Today, the museum occupies nine rooms on two levels with objects woven into vegetable fibers of Sardinia and the Mediterranean, including the junk boat on fassoi, baskets, sieves and bridles.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Centro storico (Casteddu)

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

A labyrinth of narrow and winding alleys, steep steps and paved squares within the ramparts, remained almost intact over time. Among the historic buildings the Palazzo La Loggia, seat of the town hall, and the Palazzo di Eleonora d'Arborea; from the viewpoints of the promontory the view runs over the castle, the coast and towards the Asinara.

  • Where Centro storico, Castelsardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Cattedrale di Sant'Antonio Abate e Museo del Maestro di Castelsardo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Viewpoint

Cathedral of the sixteenth century in Catalan Gothic style, overlooking the sea, with bell tower covered with majolica. The retablo is attributed to the Master of Castelsardo, active before 1492; the eighteenth-century altars are carved in juniper wood. In the crypts is the museum dedicated to the Master of Castelsardo.

  • Where Centro storico, Castelsardo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Building of historical worship of the fourteenth century, without facade, with lateral entrance between three arches in trachite and limestone; it was cathedral of the diocese of Anglona until the beginning of the sixteenth century and then oratory of the confraternity of Santa Croce. In the chapel with a cruising vault the Black Christ, fourteenth-century crucifix made of juniper wood among the oldest in Sardinia, carried in the procession of Lunissanti up to Tergu.

  • Where Centro storico, Castelsardo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Roccia dell'Elefante

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Block of rusty-coloured trachyte about 4 meters high, detached from Mount Castellazzu and shaped by the atmospheric agents in the form of a seated elephant, originally called "sa pedra pertunta" (the pertunta pedar). It houses two domus de janas excavated probably in the final Neolithic (3200-2800 BC); in the lower tomb are sculpted two bovine protomes. Since 2025 it is among the prenuragic necropolis Sardinian Heritage of Humanity.

  • Where SS 134 km 19,300, località Multeddu, Castelsardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · comune.castelsardo.ss.it · italia.it

Nuraghe Paddaggiu

History and architecture · Archaeology

In front of the Rock of the Elephant, well preserved and dating back to the last phase of the Nuragic age, with main tower almost 9 meters high and diameter of 11 meters.

  • Where SS 134, località Multeddu, Castelsardo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Spiaggia della Marina di Castelsardo

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Cream-coloured sand crescent mixed with pebbles at the entrance of the village, on the Anglon seafront, dominated by the castle on the promontory: low and sandy bottom suitable for children and two snorkeling fringes; parking, bar and restaurant.

  • Where Lungomare Anglona, Castelsardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Spiaggia di Lu Bagnu e Punta La Capra

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the hamlet of Lu Bagnu, about 2.5 km from the village, a fine sandy beach with creamy colored rocks, reached by a staircase, with clear waters and fish suitable for snorkeling and diving, rare sandy cove in a coast of high red trachyte cliffs; to the west Punta La Capra, loved by windsurfers and sailors.

  • Where Lu Bagnu, Castelsardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
