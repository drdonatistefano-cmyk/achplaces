What to see in San Mauro Castelverde

Borgo delle Madonie about a thousand meters away, with churches rich in Gaginian marbles such as Santa Maria de Francis and the Mother Church of San Giorgio, the ethno-anthropological museum, the ruins of the castle with views from Etna to the Aeolian Islands and, in the territory, the Gorges of Tiberius dug by the river Pollina.

Chiesa di Santa Maria de Francis

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built around 1260 in Angevin age and rebuilt in 1634, with three naves with portal in sandstone dated; bell tower completed in 1960. It preserves the Madonna del Soccorso in marble by Domenico Gagini (1480), the baptismal font by Antonello Gagini (1531), a marble icon of the Gaginian school (1522), the bust of the Most Holy Savior of Hercules Ferrata, a majolica floor of the eighteenth century and a Via Crucis of majolica by Collesano.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, San Mauro Castelverde
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: parcodellemadonie.it · visitsicily.info

Chiesa Madre di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Norman church with three aisles, with marble bas-reliefs under the altars and a marble icon from the Gaginian period of the sixteenth century on the main altar; it preserves a wooden crucifix of the seventeenth century probably attributed to Fra Umile da Petralia, restored in 2006, the chapel of the Blessed Sacrament with the great fresco of the Fall of Manna and, in sacristy, portraits of the archpriests from 1601 to 1892.

  • Where Centro storico, San Mauro Castelverde
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: parcodellemadonie.it · visitsicily.info

Chiesa di San Mauro Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the patron saint with three naves, built at the beginning of the seventeenth century by popular initiative on the site of three previous churches, with sandstone portal of master Luca Murena from Nicosia; inside the base of the Madonna of the Audience dated 1477 and the chapel with the Vara of the saint. The bell tower has a dome of colored stones. Feast on January 15th and the first Tuesday of July.

  • Where Centro storico, San Mauro Castelverde
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: parcodellemadonie.it · visitsicily.info

Museo di Arte Sacra

Museums · Old masters

Next to the former convent of the Dominican cloisters, of which the well remains, it exhibits among other things an ostensory attributed to Cellini; on the upper floor there is the ancient apse of a church with frescoes from 1583 with scenes from the Old and New Testaments.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Museo etnoantropologico dell'Amicizia fra i popoli

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1981 by the municipality with objects donated by the inhabitants: tools related to the cultivation of wheat, olive and flax, looms, ancient shoes for the harvest and tools of blacksmiths and craftsmen, as well as costumes and furnishings donated by the embassies of the Community countries in memory of the twinning.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: parcodellemadonie.it · visitsicily.info

Ruderi del Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Remains of Castrum Sancti Mauri, about 1100 m, perhaps of Byzantine origin: in 1082 it was part of the diocese of Troina, in 1196 Countess Guerrera Creon had it performed works and in 1394 it was lord Fernando Lopez de Luna. The panorama spans 360 degrees from Etna to the Aeolian Islands.

  • Where Sommità del paese, San Mauro Castelverde
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: parcodellemadonie.it · visitsicily.info

Corso Umberto, Piazza Municipio e meridiane

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Via Umberto, the "strata' a fera" of the walk, with sundials on the facades, the arch of Via Archi, portals and balconies in sandstone; Piazza Municipio with the stairway used as amphitheatre and the nineteenth-century Municipal Palace with the clock of 1884; the Piazzetta Badia with sea-themed sculptures. A dozen vertical solar clocks form the sundial path.

  • Where Corso Umberto, San Mauro Castelverde
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: parcodellemadonie.it · visitsicily.info

Gole di Tiberio

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Canyon of about 300 meters excavated by the Pollina River in rocks of the upper Triassic (about 200 million years ago), site recognized by the network of UNESCO Geoparks; a 400 steps path descends to the river bed and nearby there is an equipped area. Visit Sicily offers boat excursions in the gorges.

  • Where Borgo Tiberio, San Mauro Castelverde
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: parcodellemadonie.it · visitsicily.info
