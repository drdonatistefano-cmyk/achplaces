Museo Ferrari

Local life and traditions · Motors · Paid entry

The museum next to the Cavallino factories, with Formula 1, historic road cars and driving simulators.

  • Where Via Alfredo Dino Ferrari 43, Maranello
  • Entry €27 in low season, €32 in high season; €38-42 with the Enzo Ferrari Museum
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
