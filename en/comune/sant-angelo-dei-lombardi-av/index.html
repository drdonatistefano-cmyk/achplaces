What to see in Sant'Angelo dei Lombardi

Centre of Alta Irpinia at 870 meters, founded by the Longobards around the castle and bishop's seat since 1076, was one of the most affected by the earthquake of 23 November 1980; today we visit the Castle of the Imperial with its museums, the Cathedral of Sant'Antonino Martire and, in the district of San Guglielmo, the Abbey of the Goleto founded in 1133.

Abbazia del Goleto (San Guglielmo al Goleto)

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Founded around 1133 by San Guglielmo da Vercelli, who died there in 1142, as a community of monks and nuns led by an abbess; after the earthquake of 1732 Domenico Antonio Vaccaro built the Great Church (1733-40), today in ruins, and with the suppression of 1807 the complex fell until its rebirth in the second twentieth century with Father Lucio Maria De Marino and the Little Brothers of Jesus. Inside the walls, two cloisters, the defensive tower of 1152 built with blocks of a Roman mausoleum, the lower Romanesque church with two naves (XII century) and the upper church of San Luca, begun in 1247 and completed in 1255 under the abbess Marina II, with frescoed medallions. Open from 8am to 7pm.

Castello degli Imperiale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Of Lombard origin (about the 9th century), it was part of the defenses at the border between the principalities of Benevento and Salerno, and in the towers remains a medieval masonry; in the Norman age it grew around a trapezoidal courtyard with three round arches covered with marble, and in the eighteenth century it was enlarged by the Imperial princes of Genoa. Damaged by the 1980 earthquake, it has been restored; visit by reservation (tel. 0827 23094).

Museo dell'Opera e Museo dell'Emigrazione e della Civiltà contadina

Museums · Archaeological

In the castle, the Museo dell'Opera rises on an archaeological area linked to the early cathedral of Sant'Antonino of the 11th century and preserves architectural, sculptural and archaeological remains, including Byzantine finds; the Museum of Emigration and Rural Civilization collects about 200 objects on the emigration and agricultural life of the Irpini. Visits by reservation.

Cattedrale di Sant'Antonino Martire

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Founded in 1076 under Pope Gregory VII, of Norman origin and transformed in the sixteenth century, it has a façade in local stone and travertine and an eighteenth-century portal surmounted by three Norman statues (Christ Risen, St. Michael and St. Anthony). The 1980 earthquake caused the apse to collapse and part of the vaults; reopened in the early 2000s, it preserves a wooden crucifix from the Goleto, a wooden choir of the eighteenth century, the Madonna of Montevergine by Angelo Michele Ricciardi and Christ the Redeemer by Domenico Antonio Vaccaro. Open Mon-Fri 8-19, Sat 8-18, Sun 8-14.

Casa Loreto

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Urban villa of the first half of the eighteenth century, inhabited by the families Galante, De Vito and finally Loreto, then passed to the Municipality; about 1,500 square meters on three levels, with stone facade, decorated portal, rosette, worked balconies and an eighteenth-century balustrade on the Baroque garden with well. Recently restored, you visit by reservation.
