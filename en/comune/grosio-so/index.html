What to see in Grosio

The town of Media Valtellina known for the Park of the Rupestri Engravings, with the Rupe Magna and its more than 5000 figures under the remains of two medieval castles, and for the Villa Visconti Venosta, now a civic museum in a public park; in the historical center narrow streets with palaces, portals and fountains and the birthplace of the painter Cipriano Valorsa.

Parco delle Incisioni Rupestri e Rupe Magna

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Between Grosio and Grosotto, the most important testimony of the ancient populations of Valtellina: the Rupe Magna, discovered in 1966 and among the rocks engraved larger than the Alps, brings more than 5000 figures from the end of the Neolithic to the Iron Age (armed men, animals, cups, geometric shapes) and a double serpentiform figure of about 20 meters. The visit, on barefoot and with the operator of the park, starts from April to October from Tuesday to Sunday at 10.30, 14 and 16; in the park also the Antiquarium Ca' del Cap and a naturalistic path in the chestnut grove.

  • Where Via San Faustino, Grosio
  • Entry €6, boys 11-18 €3
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.parcoincisionigrosio.it

Sources: parcoincisionigrosio.it · valtellina.it · italia.it · in-lombardia.it

Castello Vecchio di San Faustino e Castello Nuovo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the hill above the Rupe Magna: the Old Castle (X-XI century), with the little church from the Romanesque bell tower and two medieval tombs carved into the rock, and the New Castle wanted by the Visconti between 1350 and 1375, with double belt and fortified tower, dismantled in 1526 by order of the Three Leagues. The excavations in the area revealed a fortified settlement from the sixteenth century BC to the Iron Age; around dry terraces for the vine.

  • Where Dosso dei Castelli, Grosio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: valtellina.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Museo civico Villa Visconti Venosta

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

Residenza dei Visconti Venosta: left wing rebuilt at the end of the 17th century, right wing rebuilt at the end of the 19th century by the Marquis Emilio, Minister of Foreign Affairs, and public park of 20,000 m2. Donated to the Municipality in 1982 by Marquisa Margherita Pallavicino Mossi, it preserves historical environments, period furniture, the doors of an ancon of Cipriano Valorsa (1597), a Redeemer on the Sepulchre of Scuola Ferrarese of the sixteenth century, the library and the family archive. Only with guided tour at 10.30, 14 and 16, recommended booking.

  • Where Via Visconti Venosta 2, Grosio
  • Entry €6, boys 11-17 €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.villaviscontivenosta.it

Sources: villaviscontivenosta.it · valtellina.it · in-lombardia.it · comune.grosio.so.it · italia.it
