What to see in Susa

Orange Flag town at the gates of the valley, where the arch of Augustus, the amphitheatre and Porta Savoia tell the Roman follow and the castle of the Countess Adelaide dominates the village.

8 places to visit in Susa, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Susa in the guide with map and filters

Arco di Augusto

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Erected in 9-8 BC to establish the alliance between Augustus and the Alpine king Cozio, it is among the best preserved Roman arches of Italy; the area of the arch and the aqueduct is always accessible.

  • Where Via degli Archi, Susa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · cultura.gov.it · old.turismotorino.org · italia.it

Anfiteatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

The amphitheatre of the Roman Segusio, part of the archaeological park of the city.

  • Where Susa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: old.turismotorino.org · turismotorino.org · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Castello della Contessa Adelaide – Museo civico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

11th-century castle, residence of Countess Adelaide who married Oddone di Savoia; now home of the Civic Museum. In the area also Roman remains with a monumental entrance of the first century AD.

  • Where Via degli Archi, Susa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: old.turismotorino.org · italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Cattedrale di San Giusto

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Founded in 1027, Romanesque with subsequent Gothic and Baroque transformations and Romanesque bell tower over 50 meters high.

  • Where Piazza San Giusto, Susa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · old.turismotorino.org · italia.it
