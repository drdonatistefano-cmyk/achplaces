What to see in Matrice

Borgo del Campobassano of Sannita origin, known for the Romanesque church of Santa Maria della Strada (XII century), already Benedictine monastery and national monument since 1889, with a facade rich in sculptures and materials of re-use from a nearby Roman villa; in the historic center the parish church of San Silvestro Papa, founded according to tradition in 1246.

Santa Maria della Strada e area archeologica

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Romanesque church of the 12th century, then Benedictine monastery mentioned in the Bull of Anastasius IV of 1153; of the monastery, ruined by the earthquake, only the church remains, reopened in 1703 and declared a national monument in 1889. The salient façade has a protire with two dragons that devour and regurgitate two men, a rosette with twelve arches, peacocks, knights, hunting scenes and a northern portal with Alexander the Great kidnapped by the griffons; the bell tower is detached. The interior with three naves, freed from the baroque additions, houses a Madonna with the wooden Child, a wooden Crucifix, a fifteenth century holy waterworks and the fourteenth-century Gothic sarcophagus of Blessed Berardo d'Aquino; part of the materials comes from a Roman villa found near the church. Next to it is a picnic area.

Chiesa di San Silvestro Papa

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church of the historic centre, whose foundation dates back to 1246 for an inscription on a stone of the bell tower. It was banned in 1851 for its poor condition and was restored for about twenty years and reopened before 1870; damaged by a fire on August 16, 1891, it was reconsecrated on May 14, 1896. It has three naves with vaulted barrels lunetted in the central nave, stone portal with bezel and oculus, and a square bell tower in three orders with cusp.
