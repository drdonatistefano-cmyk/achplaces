What to see in Cuneo

The green capital of Piedmont on the plateau wedge between Stura and Gesso, citadel Sabauda: Piazza Galimberti, heart of the Resistance, via Roma porticata, contrada Mondovì and the river park.

14 places to visit in Cuneo, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Cuneo in the guide with map and filters

Piazza Galimberti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The huge piazza porticata, lounge of the city and meeting point between the medieval Cuneo and the nineteenth century, with the statue of Barbaroux and the market on Tuesday.

  • Where Piazza Galimberti, Cuneo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · italia.it

Museo Casa Galimberti

Museums · House museums · Free entry

The house of the hero of the Resistance Duccio Galimberti, which can be visited free of charge: from the terrace on July 26, 1943 he gave the speech that incited the liberation struggle.

  • Where Piazza Galimberti, Cuneo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Via Roma e portici

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient district Maestra, entirely pedestrian and porched, open-air museum with Palazzo della Torre, Casa Basso and Casa Quaglia with frescoed façade.

  • Where Via Roma, Cuneo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · italia.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria del Bosco

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Cathedral with neoclassical façade, born with the foundation of the city in 1198, with the altarpiece of Andrea Pozzo; here is celebrated the patron Saint Michele on September 29.

  • Where Via Roma, Cuneo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · visitpiemonte.com

Torre Civica
