What to see in Aiello del Friuli

In the Friulian plain, Aiello del Friuli preserves the largest ethnographic collection of the region, the Museum of peasant civilization of the Imperial Friuli, and is known for the numerous sundials painted on the walls of houses and public buildings; it is also a stop of the Celestial Way between Aquileia and Cormòns.

Museo della civiltà contadina del Friuli imperiale

Museums · Themed

Opened from 1992, it tells the rural life from 1500 to 1918, when the County of Gorizia was under the Habsburgs, in three buildings: an eighteenth-century structure (cellar and barn), a nineteenth-century one (stalls and barns) and a canopy of the mid-Twentieth century. About 25,000 pieces catalogued: trades such as mason, basket, shoemaker, blacksmith, carpenter and a late 19th century typography; plows from the 16th to the 20th century and tools for sowing, harvest and threshing; weights and measurements before and after the metric system (introduced in the County in 1841); tin boxes of the Monfalcone Passer factory; a vintage school room and a room on women in the rural world. Open Wednesday 10-13 and 15-19, Thursday and Friday 10-13 and 18-19, Saturday and Sunday 9-13 and 15-19; groups with one week notice.

Le meridiane di Aiello

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

Numerous sundials placed by a group of enthusiasts on the walls of houses and public buildings of the village; the last Sunday of May the sundial Festival brings exhibitions, music and shows.

  • Where Centro, Aiello del Friuli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Cammino Celeste, tappe Aquileia-Aiello e Aiello-Cormòns

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Aiello is the point of arrival of the first stage of the Celestial Way, from Aquileia (about 16.6 km, 4 hours and a half, slight difficulty), and departure of the second towards Cormòns (about 18 km, 5 hours, 140 m of difference in altitude).

  • Where Aiello del Friuli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it
