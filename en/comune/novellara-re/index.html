What to see in Novellara

The capital of the small county of Gonzaga from 1371 to 1728: the Fortress with the Gonzaga Museum and the theatre, the sixteenth-century portico square with the Collegiate Church of Lelio Orsi and, outside the village, the Valli and the country residences of the court.

Rocca dei Gonzaga

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Begin at the end of the fourteenth century and transformed in the sixteenth century into a Renaissance residence of the Gonzaga di Novellara; the Municipality bought it in 1754. It hosts town hall, Gonzaga Museum, theatre, library and archive: on the ground floor the Sala del Fico with grotesques of the mid-500s, in the north-west tower the Sala del Consiglio repainted by Cesare Cervi, the Sala Civica with works of Augusto Daolio dei Nomadi and, in the attic, the municipal maple. Visits on Sundays on request at the museum bookshop, 10-12.30 and 15-18.30; closed in August.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Museo Gonzaga

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the rooms of the sixteenth-century apartment of the Counts Gonzaga, on the noble floor of the Rocca: the frescoes of Lelio Orsi detached from the Casino of Sopra, his Annunciation on board, the tapestry of Alfonso I Gonzaga with Jason and Medea (1554), Romanesque-Bizantine frescoes from San Giovanni della Fossa and one of the most important European collections of pharmacy vases, from the Jesuits' spices. Tuesday-Friday 10-13 (call on arrival), Saturday and Sunday 10-13 and 15-19; free on the first Sunday of the month.

  • Where Piazzale Guglielmo Marconi 1, Novellara
  • Entry €4 (reduced €2)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: comune.novellara.re.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Teatro della Rocca «Franco Tagliavini»

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Italian theatre built between 1862 and 1868 on a project by Antonio Tegani, modelled on the theatres of Reggio and Carpi; it replaced the court theatre decorated by Lelio Orsi. Room and reduced are decorated by Cesare Cervi; you enter from the inner courtyard of the Fortress. It is named after the tenor Franco Tagliavini from Novella.

Mostra permanente della Civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In the 15th-century basement of the Fortress, thousands of objects of work in the fields, domestic life and crafts, collected since the 1970s from a research of the students of the middle school on the work of the grandparents. Open only on the first Sunday of the month, 10-12.30 and 15-18.30.

  • Where Piazzale G. Marconi 1, Novellara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: reggioemiliawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Collegiata di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Erected in 1567 on a design by Lelio Orsi, enlarged in the eighteenth century, with 1616 bell tower and stucco by Giuseppe Mazza (1704). Inside the Martyrdom of St. Lawrence (1570) and the Ascension of Pier Maria Bagnadore (1580); in the sacristy two historian plates signed by Nicolò da Urbino and Orazio Fontana (1538).

  • Where Piazza Unità d'Italia, Novellara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Piazza Unità d'Italia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Large piazza porticata and scenic, the ancient Piazza Maggiore of the sixteenth-century village, with the Collegiata, the bell tower of 1616, the monument to the Fallen by Giuseppe Graziosi and, to the east, the entrance to the Rocca. A 1563 regulation fixed the height and colorful decorations of the houses.

  • Where Piazza Unità d'Italia, Novellara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Casino di Sotto

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

16th century villa of the Gonzaga, country residence of the court outside the village towards Bagnolo in Piano, with late-Manierist frescoes and well-preserved 17th century stuccoes.

Valli di Novellara e sentiero CAI 608

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Large areas of ecological rebalancing at the border with Reggiolo and Guastalla, where in spring along ditches and canals bloom the lawn violet and the dread. The CAI 608, open in 2017, starts from Ponte del Pastore: about 10 km, two and a half hours on foot, easy and suitable for everyone.

  • Where Ponte del Pastore, Novellara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
