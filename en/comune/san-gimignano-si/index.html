What to see in San Gimignano

The "Manhattan of the Middle Ages" of the more than seventy medieval towers remain 13-14, in the historic UNESCO heritage center since 1990, on the Via Francigena.

7 places to visit in San Gimignano, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Musei Civici e Torre Grossa

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In the Town Hall the Dante Room with the Majesty of Lippo Memmi, the Podestà Room with the frescoes of Azzo di Masetto (1290) and the Pinacoteca with Filippino Lippi, Benozzo Gozzoli and Pinturicchio; from the same entrance you climb on the Grossa Tower, 54 meters, built in 1311.

  • Where Piazza Duomo 2, San Gimignano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · sangimignano.com

Collegiata di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The main church of the city, overlooking Piazza Duomo, with walls entirely covered by frescoes from the fourteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, San Gimignano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Piazza della Cisterna e Piazza Duomo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The two adjacent squares of the medieval heart: that of the Cisterna, triangular, with the well in the center and the houses-towers around, and Piazza Duomo with the Collegiata and the public buildings.

  • Where Piazza della Cisterna, San Gimignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa di Sant'Agostino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Augustinian Church at the north end of the center, signalled by Visit Tuscany among the stops of the visit to San Gimignano.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Agostino, San Gimignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Torre e Casa Campatelli

Museums · House museums

A medieval house-tower inhabited until recent times, open to the public with the furnishings of the family: you can understand how you lived inside a tower.

  • Where Via San Giovanni, San Gimignano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets

Sources: visittuscany.com

San Gimignano 1300

Museums · Themed · Free entry

A large ceramic plastic that reconstructs the city as it was around 1300, with all its towers; the entrance is free, with free offer.

  • Where San Gimignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The findings of the territory from the VII to the I century BC and a medieval section, reported by the city portal among the civic museums.

  • Where San Gimignano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: sangimignano.com · visittuscany.com
