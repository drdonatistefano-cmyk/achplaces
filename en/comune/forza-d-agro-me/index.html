What to see in Forza d'Agrò

Borgo at 420 meters on the Messinian Ionian coast, with views of the Etna, the Bay of Taormina and the Strait, set de "The Godfather": the Norman castle, the Durazzesco Arch, the Baroque Mother Church with the Annunciation of the Antonello area and the medieval village of the Quartarello.

Castello normanno e Guardiola

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Traditionally attributed to Count Ruggero in the 11th century and restored in 1595, it preserves a bell tower and walls with slits; a short distance is the Guardiola, massive defensive tower also with slits.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa Madre di Santa Maria Annunziata e Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Baroque church of the early eighteenth century built on the site of a 15th century church, restored in 2001. It preserves the Annunciation of the Annunciation of the Antonellian coveted (early XVI century, frame of 1560), the cross painted on the table of the SS. Crucifix, patron, in the middle of the fifteenth century, the eighteenth-century choir in walnut with 24 barns and the marble statue of Saint Catherine of Alexandria coming from St. Francis.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Forza d'Agrò
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa della SS. Trinità ed ex convento agostiniano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The church of the Confraternity of the Trinity was rebuilt in 1576 on a fifteenth-century structure; in 1591 it was granted to the Hermits of Sant'Agostino, whose former convent preserves the cloister and a small crypt for mummification with masonry channels and decorations, restored in 2000.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Arco Durazzesco e Piazza Carullo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Catalan Gothic style arch of the late fifteenth century is reached by a sandstone staircase, next to the smallest Augustinicchiu arch, and leads to a square overlooking the Bay of Taormina; Piazza Pasquale Carullo looks on the Strait of Messina and is a point to observe the meteors of August.

  • Where Forza d'Agrò
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Quartarello, Magghia e Rocca

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Quartarello is a medieval village clinging to the cliff of the castle, with zigzag alleys, stone stairs and a sixteenth-century house with balcony on anthropomorphic shelves; on Christmas it houses a living nativity scene of about one hundred people. La Magghia has perched houses and the church of San Sebastiano with a painting of the Eternal Father; the Rocca has houses on the rock and the circular square "u tunnu" with panoramic bench.

  • Where Quartarello, Forza d'Agrò
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Via SS. Annunziata e palazzi nobiliari

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Street of high and narrow houses overlooking the main palaces: Palazzo Miano-Pizzolo (XVII century) with balcony on sandstone shelves, Palazzo Garufi-Schipilliti renovated in 1771; in the surroundings Palazzo Mauro with arched porch, Palazzo Bondì with domed balcony and elliptical windows, the eighteenth-century houses of Via Roma and Casa Crisafulli-Gussio in Piazza Duomo, and the alley of the prisons with grate cells and underground shelters.

  • Where Via SS. Annunziata, Forza d'Agrò
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
