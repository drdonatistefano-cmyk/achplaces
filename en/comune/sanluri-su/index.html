What to see in Sanluri

Sanluri, in the Middle Campidano, is dominated by the medieval castle with four towers, the only Sardinian castle still habitable, today house-museum with the collection of waxes. The historical centre enclosed in the walls preserves the parish church of Our Lady of Graces with the retablo of Saint Anne, medieval churches, the Museum of the Capuchins and the Bread Museum dedicated to civraxu.

Castello di Sanluri (Castello di Eleonora d'Arborea)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Born at the end of the 12th century as a boundary stronghold between the judges of Cagliari and Arborea, it was fortified in 1355 by the will of Peter IV of Aragon and in 1409 it was conquered by the Spaniards after a decisive clash; it has a square plan of 26 m on the side, walls 12 m high and four crenellated angular towers. In the twentieth century General Nino Villasanta restored it and transformed it into a home museum: today it houses the study of the general with the correspondence with D'Annunzio, the Gondi halls, the Salone delle Milizie with the Tricolore della Vittoria and the Bulletin della Vittoria signed by Diaz, it rises on the world wars and the countryside of Africa, the feudal district and a collection of waxes between the sixteenth and nineteenth century, indicated as the largest in Europe. It is not certain that Eleonora d'Arborea stayed there.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Museo storico etnografico dei Padri Cappuccini

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

It is located in the Capuchin monastery built since 1608 on a hill overlooking the village. It exhibits illuminated choir books, papal decrees from the sixteenth century, relics, wooden statues, paintings and liturgical furnishings from other Sardinian convents; an archaeological section with finds from the Neolithic to the early Christian and Byzantine ages; and the reconstruction of the daily life of the convent with the tools of the friars. The guided tour is included in the ticket. Open Saturday and Sunday 10-13 and 15:30-19 (from June to October 16-19:30).

  • Entry €4, reduced €3, 6-14 years €2

Museo del Pane e panificazione del Civraxu

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Opened in 2017 in the house Villasanta, which housed the oil mill, a few meters from the Town Hall. In five sections it tells the civraxu, the typical durum wheat bread of Sanluri, from the work of the earth to the dough, the leavening and the cooking; on request there are bakery workshops.

  • Where Via Municipio, Sanluri
  • Entry €4, reduced €3, 6-14 years €2
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.sardegnaturismo.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Chiesa di Nostra Signora delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church built at the end of the eighteenth century on a 14th century church; it was once the cathedral of the village. It preserves the medieval retablo of Saint Anne, attributed to the Master of Sanluri.

Chiese medievali di San Lorenzo, San Pietro e San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

San Lorenzo was the first parish church in the village and has a wooden ceiling decorated with heads of lions and dragons; also San Pietro and San Rocco are medieval churches.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The old town is enclosed inside the medieval walls, while outside the walls the streets widen fan-wide. At the end of September the Festa del Borgo recalls its historical atmosphere, together with the festival of civraxu.

  • Where Centro, Sanluri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it
