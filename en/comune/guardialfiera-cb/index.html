What to see in Guardialfiera

Small village perched on a cliff above the lake, birthplace of writer Francesco Jovine, born in the Lombard period around a Roman tower; the historical walled center runs along the ridge to the Cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta, with its bas-reliefs, the crypt discovered in 1975 and the Holy Door linked to the indulgence granted, according to tradition, by Leo IX in 1053.

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta e Porta Santa

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Rebuilt around the year 1000 and repeatedly rebuilt, it has external walls rich in bas-reliefs: twelve hanging arches on human heads representing the Apostles, crucifer lambs, hunting scenes on the eastern facade, a Byzantine dolphin, priests in procession and a blessed bishop, under a large walled rose window. The crypt, discovered by chance in 1975 during the restorations, has vaults cruising on tubs columns and archaic capitals, of uncertain dating. On the north-east side, at the top of 14 steps, the Bronze Holy Door (2020-2021) recalls the perpetual plenary indulgence that according to the Leo IX tradition granted in June 1053: it opens on the morning of June 1st and closes on the afternoon of June 2nd, for the patron celebrations.

Centro storico e mura

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town, the piedicastello, stretches over the ridge of the hill to the church and is closed by a wall with three entrance doors and several towers, overlooking the lake. The village was born in the Lombard age around a Roman tower then abandoned, it was occupied by Ruggero II of Altavilla in 1130 and in the eighteenth century it was named city; here was born the writer Francesco Jovine.
