What to see in Accettura

A hilly village in the south-east of Tricarico, in the heart of the Regional Park Gallipoli Cognato Piccole Dolomiti Lucane, with ancient aristocratic palaces overlooking the alleys; it is known for May, the spring rite of the wedding of the trees, told in the Museum of Arborean Cults.

4 places to visit in Accettura, in the province of Matera (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo dei culti arborei

Museums · Themed

Ethnoanthropological Museum inaugurated in 2005 and dedicated to the Lucanian arboreal cults, linked to the peasant world; it also serves as a visitor center of the Park of Gallipoli Cognato and has a library on the arboreal cults and a video library on the feasts of May. May combines the top of a holly with a large oak, symbol of fertility and good harvest.

the MiC card indicates to contact the Municipality for the visit and closed on the other days

  • Where Via del Maggio, Accettura
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Centro storico e palazzi aristocratici

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the center of ancient aristocratic palaces overlook narrow alleys, among which stands the mother church of St. Nicholas.

  • Where Accettura
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Parco Regionale Gallipoli Cognato Piccole Dolomiti Lucane

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Acceptura is located in the heart of the regional park, at the foot of the rocky peaks of the Little Lucane Dolomites; the territory offers views, walks and the possibility to observe the fauna.

  • Where Accettura
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · basilicataturistica.it

Lucania Outdoor Park

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Adventure park in the forest of Montepiano with trails suspended between walkways, pulleys and lianas, suitable for families.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
