What to see in Trieste

The capital of Friuli Venezia Giulia on the gulf, with Piazza Unità d'Italia open on the sea, the Borgo Teresiano around the Grand Canal, the hill of San Giusto with cathedral, castle and remains of ancient Tergeste, and outside the city the Castle of Miramare and the Risera di San Sabba.

Piazza Unità d'Italia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest centre of the city, with the structure of the late nineteenth century: the Town Hall with eclectic façade, the Palazzo del Governo neo-Renaissance, Palazzo Pitteri of late eighteenth century and the Palazzo del Lloyd, today the presidency of the Region.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
