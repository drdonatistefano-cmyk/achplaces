What to see in Santa Maria Maggiore

Borgo Bandiera Arancione della Valle Vigezzo, land of chimney sweeps, perfumers and painters, where the Cologne Water was born.

3 places to visit in Santa Maria Maggiore, in the province of Verbano-Cusio-Ossola (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo dello Spazzacamino

Museums · Themed

In the park of Villa Antonia, eighteenth-century, unique in Italy, pays tribute to the history of small chimney sweeps.

  • Where Villa Antonia, Santa Maria Maggiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it · magazine.navigazionelaghi.it

Casa del Profumo Feminis-Farina

Museums · Themed

A few meters from Piazza Risorgimento, pays tribute to Giovanni Paolo Feminis, born here and emigrated to Cologne, inventor of the formula of the Cologne Water, and to Giovanni Maria Farina who spread it.

  • Where Santa Maria Maggiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it · magazine.navigazionelaghi.it

Pinacoteca della Scuola di Belle Arti Rossetti Valentini

Museums · Modern art

The art gallery that testifies to the tradition of the painters of Vezzini.

  • Where Santa Maria Maggiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it
