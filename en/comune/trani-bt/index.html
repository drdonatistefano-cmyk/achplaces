What to see in Trani

The white city on the Adriatic: the Romanesque Cathedral overlooking the sea, the Castle of Frederick II, the Giudecca with the medieval synagogues of Scolanova and Sant'Anna and the port with the apses of Ognissanti.

12 places to visit in Trani, in the province of Barletta-Andria-Trani (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

Jewel of the Romanesque Apulian overlooking the sea, in front of the Castle: from the promenade along the castle you can enjoy the best view; on the same square Duomo are located the Diocesan Museum and the Museum of the typewriter.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Trani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello svevo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Quadrilateral with four towers at the edges, bathed by the Adriatic on one side, started by Frederick II in 1233 on the model of the Crusader castles of Terrasanta, part of the defensive system of the Kingdom of Sicily; in 1259 there were celebrated the wedding of Manfredi with Elena Ducas Comneno. Today it is accessed by a stone bridge in place of the ancient drawbridge.

  • Where Piazza Re Manfredi 16, Trani
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Raised along the sea, with palaces that preserve the traces of the Jewish community, rare case in Puglia.

  • Where Centro storico, Trani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Giudecca

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Jewish quarter, where in the middle of the 12th century there lived about two hundred families, expelled in 1541 by edict of Charles V; the streets still remember the past (via La Giudea, via Synagogue, via Mosè da Trani, wide Scolanova).

  • Where Via La Giudea, Trani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitjewishitaly.it

Sinagoga Scolanova

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Romanesque synagogue with cusp façade and sailing bell tower, which has been active for the Jewish community since 2005.

  • Where Largo Scolanova, Trani
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitjewishitaly.it

Museo Sinagoga Sant'Anna

Museums · Themed

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: italia.it · visitjewishitaly.it
