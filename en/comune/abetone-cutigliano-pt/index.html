What to see in Abetone Cutigliano

Municipality of Montagna Pistoiese that joins the Abetone, the largest winter station of the northern Apennines with the Pyramids of 1778 at the pass, and the medieval village of Cutigliano with the Palazzo dei Capitani della Montagna; around the Forest Botanical Garden, the Doganaccia and Lake Scaffaiolo.

12 places to visit in Abetone Cutigliano, in the province of Pistoia (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Orto Botanico Forestale dell'Abetone

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Founded in 1987 at about 1,300 meters in the Upper Sestaione Valley, it preserves the flora of the northern Apennines: the cold microclimate hosts alpine species at the southern limit, such as spruce and rhododendron, and two endemic species, the Globularia incanescens and the carnivorous Pinguicula christinae. The route crosses beech, abetina, rocky garden, blueberry moor, peat bog and a pond; it is part of the Ecomuseum of the Pistoiese Mountain. Seasonal opening.

  • Where Alta Valle del Sestaione, Abetone Cutigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.ecomuseopt.it

Sources: ecomuseopt.it · cultura.gov.it · visittuscany.com · italia.it

Passo dell'Abetone e Piramidi

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 1,388 metres, there are the two stone pyramids built in 1778, starting point for excursions; near customs, the post office station and the church of San Leopoldo.

  • Where Passo dell'Abetone, Abetone Cutigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com

Comprensorio sciistico dell'Abetone

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

The largest winter station of the northern Apennines: about 50 km of slopes served by 17 plants and two cross-country rings for 15 km as a whole; it also includes the Val di Luce.

  • Where Abetone, Abetone Cutigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com

Palazzo dei Capitani della Montagna e borgo di Cutigliano

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village on a spur among the woods near Mount Cuccola. The facade of the Palazzo dei Capitani della Montagna brings 96 coats of arms in stone and polychrome terracotta left by the Captains in memory of the mandate; it is admired from the fifteenth-century loggia in front, near the fountain and the church of the Madonna di Piazza.

  • Where Cutigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com

Lago Scaffaiolo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

High-altitude natural lake at 1,775 meters, among the most evocative of the northern Apennines: from the Doganaccia (1.547 m, reached by cable car) the path no. 66 rooms for the Arcana Cross and the Calanca in about 1 hour and 10 minutes, 8 km round trip.

  • Where Doganaccia, Abetone Cutigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.abetonecutigliano.pt.it · visittuscany.com · italia.it

Torre del Fattucchio

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

The sandstone tower is a few tens of meters high, overlooking the valley of the Sestaione; it is reached by mule track from La Peschiera (Pian degli Ontani), 8 km round trip and 270 m of difference in altitude, passing through the source Polla dell'Isola and the Buca delle Fate.

Museo della Gente dell'Appennino Pistoiese

Museums · Themed

In the hamlet of Rivoreta, it tells the life of the mountain communities of yesterday and today: the processing of chestnuts, the basis of the local economy, the production of coal and the tools for work in the woods, with annual rotational arrangements and a workshop of the toy for children. Visits by reservation.

  • Where Via degli Scoiattoli 14, Rivoreta, Abetone Cutigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · comune.abetonecutigliano.pt.it · italia.it

L'altrolato del Caposaldo e Museo della Linea Gotica

Easy route between the bunkers of the Gothic Line, about 1.5 km or 10 km (on foot or by e-bike), with exhibition of finds of the Second World War and monument to the victims of the Pianosinatic massacre, where the Museum of the Gothic Line is located.
