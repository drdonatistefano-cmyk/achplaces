Tempio sacrario di Cargnacco (Madonna del Conforto)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

National Temple Madonna del Comforto, born on the initiative of Mgr. Carlo Caneva, military chaplain in Russia in the Second World War, in memory of the more than 90,000 fallen and dispersed of the countryside of Russia; he guards the remains of about a thousand soldiers, the sacellus of the unknown soldier and the crypt with the tomb of Mgr. He was a dog. Every September the Italian National Union veterans of Russia celebrates the Day of the Fall and the Missing.

  • Where Piazza don Carlo Caneva, Cargnacco, Pozzuolo del Friuli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: regione.fvg.it · consiglio.regione.fvg.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo storico della Campagna di Russia

Museums · Themed

Next to the temple of Cargnacco, it collects photographs, documents and relics of the Italian army in the Russian countryside (1941-1943); a room is dedicated to the arrival of the divisions deployed on the Don, with uniforms and vintage documents. The secretariat provides information on the fate of the soldiers not returned. Times to ask (tel. 0432 561649).

  • Where Via Leonardo da Vinci, Cargnacco, Pozzuolo del Friuli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Centro visite storico-archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 1998, it documents the ancient history of the territory and especially the Neolithic site of Sammardenchia, one of the largest in Europe (about 5500-4700 BC), which has returned tools in flint, stone and ceramics. Educational path with settings, copies of artifacts to touch and experimental archaeology workshops for children; a section is dedicated to the local protohistory and castle of Pozzuolo.

  • Where Pozzuolo del Friuli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it · cultura.gov.it
