What to see in Brinzio

Small village entirely included in the Regional Park Campo dei Fiori, which houses the headquarters of the agency; there are the Museum of Rural Culture Prealpina, Lake Brinzio with the nearby Cascata del Pesegh and the departure of several paths of the park.

3 places to visit in Brinzio, in the province of Varese (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Cultura Rurale Prealpina

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in September 2008 as part of an ecomuseum, it protects the ethnic-cultural and landscape heritage of the Alps. Eleven sections on two floors: on the ground floor the outdoor activities (forest resources, stable, land works, building, bucchering and beekeeping), on the first floor the domestic life and crafts (fabric, kitchen, carpenter, shoemaker, spinning and weaving). Open Wednesday 14-18, Saturday and Sunday 15-18; groups and schools every day by appointment (tel. 0332 435714).

  • Where Via Trieste 24, Brinzio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.vareseturismo.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · vareseturismo.it

Lago di Brinzio e Cascata del Pesegh

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lake, reserve of the Regional Park Campo dei Fiori, is also seen from the main road and can be reached on foot with a short dirt road; not far away is the Pesegh Falls. From the lake pass the paths 10/310 C and 20/320 of the park.

  • Where Lago di Brinzio, Brinzio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.vareseturismo.it

Sources: vareseturismo.it

Sentiero n. 5 Brinzio - Rasa

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

3 km, about 2 hours, with 35 m of difference in altitude and suitable for all, from Via Piave to Brinzio to the locality Pizelle and the village of Rasa di Varese on the slopes of Monte Chiusarella. Along the way the Cerro Fountain, the Erratico Mass of the Val d'Intrino, recognized Natural Monument, and the woods, clearings and huts of the Valle del Legnone. Easy and free parking.

  • Where Via Piave, Brinzio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.vareseturismo.it

Sources: vareseturismo.it
