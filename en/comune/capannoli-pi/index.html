What to see in Capannoli

Medieval village on the hills of the valley of the Era, among the Pisan Hills, born as a fortified village and then grown as a center of commerce. On the top of the hill stands Villa Baciocchi with the zoological museum, the archaeological museum of Valdera and a botanical park; in the hamlet of Santo Pietro Belvedere there is the layout of a medieval village-castle.

Musei di Villa Baciocchi

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

An eighteenth-century villa built on the remains of the medieval castle of Della Gherardesca, later belonged to the Pucci and the Marquises Baciocchi, who gave it its name. On the ground floor the Zoological Museum exhibits over 1,400 specimens of mammals, birds, reptiles and invertebrates in natural settings; on the noble floor the Archaeological Museum tells the Valdera from prehistory to the Middle Ages, with ceramics, coins, stone finds, Etruscan and Roman sections and reconstructions of houses, including a Neolithic village and Etruscan houses. Open from Thursday to Sunday, 9.30-12.30 and 15.30-18.30 in winter, 9.30-12.30 and 16-19 in summer; visit to the underground €3, free under 6 years.

  • Entry €6, reduced €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museivillabaciocchi.it

Parco botanico di Villa Baciocchi

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

The park of the villa, on the top of the hill called "castle" has 12 monumental trees and 160 species of rare and exotic plants, some centuries old, including a sequoia, a cedar of the Atlas and a rate about 20 meters high, with small tabernacles and caves scattered in the green. It hosts some sculptures of the route "Equilibri in natura" and, on the second Sunday of September, the National Bird Fair, which has been held for over 60 years.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.museivillabaciocchi.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Santo Pietro Belvedere

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The only hamlet of the municipality, preserves the typical urban structure of a medieval village-castle, with its historical centre with circular plan and pedestrian Belvedere Square, where the exhibition of outdoor sculptures is set up. The church of San Pietro is of medieval origin, renovated in the nineteenth century; recent studies suggest an Etruscan origin of the village, and in the historical center and on the hill of San Rocco are ongoing reliefs on ancient hypogeans.

  • Where Piazza Belvedere, Santo Pietro Belvedere, Capannoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Parco della Castellina

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

It occupies the highest point of the hill of Santo Pietro Belvedere and is on two levels: at the bottom the children's games, at the top the view of the Valdera hills.

  • Where Santo Pietro Belvedere, Capannoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
