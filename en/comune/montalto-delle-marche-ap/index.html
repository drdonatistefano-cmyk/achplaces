What to see in Montalto delle Marche

Città d'origine della famiglia di Felice Peretti, pope Sixto V since 1585, 512 meters between the valleys of Aso and Tesino: Piazza Sisto V with the cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta, Palazzo Paradisi with the reliquary donated by the pope in 1587, the civic museums in the Town Hall and, in Valdaso, the fortified mill of 1320. Historical center, monuments and museums are partially usable.

8 places to visit in Montalto delle Marche, in the province of Ascoli Piceno (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza Sisto V e Concattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

To build the Cathedral Sixto V he made a hill flat; on the square the bronze monument of Pericles Fazzini, a rotating sculpture that projects the profile of the pope on always different views.

  • Where Piazza Sisto V, Montalto delle Marche
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Palazzo Paradisi e Reliquiario di Montalto

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace where Felice Peretti lived, today the seat of the Reliquary of Montalto (Imago Pietatis and Scenes of the Passion), masterpiece of Parisian goldsmiths and precious stones donated by Sixto V to the city in 1587, the second year of the pontificate; the Region indicates it currently visitable.

  • Where Montalto delle Marche
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Museo sistino vescovile

Museums · Old masters

The diocesan museum is the usual seat of the reliquary donated by Sixtus V and also preserves a relic of Paul II donated by the pope.

Closed for restoration after the earthquake of 2016 according to the Marche Region; the reliquary is exhibited at Palazzo Paradisi

  • Where Montalto delle Marche
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Musei civici: Pinacoteca, Museo archeologico, Antiche carceri sistine

Museums · City history

The Pinacoteca civica and Archaeological Museum exhibits paintings from the 15th to the 20th century and the rebuilt study of Giuseppe Sacconi, born in Montalto and architect of the Vittoriano; in the Town Hall the Museum of the prisons, with graffiti and drawings of the prisoners, and the museum "L'Acqua, la Terra, la Tela" .

According to the Regione Marche historical center and museums are partially usable; info@visitmontaltomarche.it

  • Where Municipio, Piazza Umberto I 12, Montalto delle Marche
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Mulino Sistino

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Rare fortified mill active since 1320 in the valley of Aso, organized in three sections, sold in 1567 to Felice Peretti and sister Camilla; restored, hosts events and guided tours.

  • Where Valdaso, Montalto delle Marche
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Convento di San Francesco alle Fratte

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

According to the tradition founded by San Francesco in 1215 in a secular wood, it preserves frescoes of the Giotto school; it studied Felice Peretti.

  • Where Montalto delle Marche
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Patrignone e Santa Maria in Viminatu

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Frazione from the well-preserved medieval center, home of the humanist Antonio Bonfini, with the Porta del Borgo which bears an epigraph dedicated to King Manfredi and the Romanesque church of Santa Maria in Viminatu, frescoed between the fourteenth and sixteenth centuries.

  • Where Patrignone, Montalto delle Marche
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Porchia, Torrione e chiesa di Santa Lucia

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Fraction with a tower of the fourteenth century; in the crypt of the church of Santa Lucia a fresco of the Nativity of the fifteenth century, and in the village a table of the Pagani.

  • Where Porchia, Montalto delle Marche
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it
