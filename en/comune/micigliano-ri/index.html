What to see in Micigliano

Small village about 1,000 m on the slopes of Terminillo, overlooking the Velino Gorges 112 km from Rome: along the Salaria, next to the river, stands the Benedictine abbey of Saints Quirico and Julitta, documented since 984; in the village the church of San Lorenzo Martire and the Civic Museum of the Arts and Popular Traditions.

Abbazia dei Santi Quirico e Giulitta

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Founded by the Benedictines in the first half of the 10th century (first definite news in 984) between the Via Salaria and Velino, it depended directly on the Holy See; burned during the Norman invasion, it was rebuilt by the abbot Sinibaldo and consecrated in 1179, and was abandoned at the end of the 18th century. It preserves the fortified structure with quadrangular wall and Romanesque elements, and in the center an imposing bell tower used as a sighting tower, with sculptures of a human head, an abbot with miter and pastoral and a lion head.

  • Where Lungo la via Salaria, presso il fiume Velino, Micigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Museo civico delle arti e tradizioni popolari

Museums · Themed

It collects over 600 tools and furnishings of peasant and craft life, with the reconstructions of the workshops of the lumberjack, the blacksmith and the carpenter, corners of the house and historical photographs of work, family and patron celebrations.

  • Where Micigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Chiesa di San Lorenzo Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A small church with a single nave dedicated to the patron, built where the harvest was saved by a storm; behind the altar a fresco shows the deacon Lorenzo with the grating, symbol of martyrdom.

  • Where Micigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Mulattiera Micigliano - Sella di Leonessa

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient challenging mule track that connects Micigliano to the Sella di Leonessa (1,900 m) passing through the Valloni and Elephant mountains (about 2,000 m), with wide views from the summit of Terminillo to the Velino Gorges.

  • Where Partenza da Micigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Tartufi, castagne e funghi

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Typical products of the village are truffles, chestnuts, mushrooms and cheeses, celebrated by the Truffle Festival in July and that of the chestnut on the last Sunday of October.
