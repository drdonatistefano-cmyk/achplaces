What to see in Romano d'Ezzelino

On the southern slopes of Monte Grappa, among hills that open onto the Veneto plain, Romano d'Ezzelino offers the wild Valle Santa Felicita for trekking, mountain biking and free flight, the villa Ca' Cornaro with its nineteenth-century park, the remains of the castle of the Ezzelini on Colle Bastia and the Museum of the Car Bonfanti-Vimar.

4 places to visit in Romano d'Ezzelino, in the province of Vicenza (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Valle Santa Felicita

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the most important valleys of the Massif del Grappa, deep and wild, suitable for trekking, mountain biking and hiking; it is very close to the take-off and landing areas of the free flight of Semonzo and Roman Ezzelino.

  • Where Valle Santa Felicita, Romano d'Ezzelino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Ca' Cornaro

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa immersed in a park of the nineteenth century, with stuccoes and frescoes eighteenth century inside and, outside, a lemon house of the sixteenth century, balustrades and decorative vases of the twenties of the twentieth century; the chapel, built in 1720, contains frescoes attributed to Giovanni Battista Canal and two paintings of the Tiepolesque school.

  • Where Romano d'Ezzelino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello degli Ezzelini sul Colle Bastia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

A place with a very long history, dating back to the pre-Roman age and experienced the greatest splendor with the powerful Ezzelini dynasty; on the Bastia Hill remains leftovers of the first decades of the twelfth century, where medieval reenactments are held each year.

  • Where Colle Bastia, Romano d'Ezzelino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo dell'Automobile Bonfanti-Vimar

Museums · Themed

Museum dedicated to engines and cars, which renews the exhibition every six months, in spring and autumn, with always different themes related to the car.

  • Where Via Torino 2, Romano d'Ezzelino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: vicenzae.org · italia.it
