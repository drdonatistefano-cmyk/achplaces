What to see in Cavriglia

The town of Valdarno, above the foothills of the Chianti Mountains, marked by the extraction of lignite that emptied the village of Castelnuovo dei Sabbioni, now home to the MINE museum; also worth seeing is the Botanical Roset "Carla Fineschi," the parish church of San Giovanni Battista with its museum and the castles of Montegonzi and Montaio.

MINE - Museo delle miniere e del territorio

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the recovered buildings of the abandoned village of Castelnuovo dei Sabbioni, it preserves finds and documents of the lignite mine of Valdarno, which during the First World War employed up to 5,000 miners and since 1955 passed from the galleries to the open-air mine. A multimedia path tells the mining history, the transformation of the landscape and the life of the miners; the documentation centre has photos and audiovisuals, with workshops for schools. From the terrace you can see the mining village of Santa Barbara. Free under 12.

Visit Tuscany indicates the museum temporarily closed to the public for restoration, while the municipality reports regular times; check on minecavriglia.it

  • Where Via Giuseppe di Vittorio 7, Castelnuovo dei Sabbioni, Cavriglia
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.minecavriglia.it

Sources: minecavriglia.it · visittuscany.com · comune.cavriglia.ar.it

Borgo vecchio di Castelnuovo dei Sabbioni

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Borgo on the hills of Valdarno in the center of the mining basin: the extraction of lignite caused land collapses and the village was permanently abandoned due to lack of security, with the inhabitants transferred to the new mining village of Santa Barbara. Today it houses the MINE museum; near, the artificial lake in the former mining area, now green oasis.

  • Where Castelnuovo dei Sabbioni, Cavriglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Roseto botanico «Carla Fineschi»

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

Defined the world's largest private rose garden, born in 1967 from the collections of Professor Gianfranco Fineschi: thousands of varieties (over 6,500 according to the card) from all continents, even rare, ordered in 21 sectors according to the classification, with plates indicating the name, age and creator of each rose. The best period is the main flowering, May-June.

  • Where Via Casale 16, Castelnuovo dei Sabbioni, Cavriglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.rosetofineschi.it

Sources: rosetofineschi.it · visittuscany.com

Museo della Pieve di San Giovanni Battista

Museums · Old masters

Small museum of sacred art in the former oratory of the Society of the Name of Jesus, leaning on the left aisle of the parish. It keeps busts in glazed terracotta by Benedetto Buglioni (San Giovannino, Santo Stefano and probably San Romolo) and an angel polychrome regiment, a thirteenth-century processional cross in chiselled and gilded copper with Christ in golden bronze, a plate in copper painted with Nativity of the sixteenth century perhaps of workshop of Limoges, the Circumcision attributed to Benedetto Veli and eighteenth-century furnishings with figures of nativity scenes of Neapolitan taste.

not published times; the MiC card indicates closed every day, contact the parish (tel. 0559166528)

  • Where Piazza Umberto I 4, Cavriglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.cavriglia.ar.it · visittuscany.com

Montegonzi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified village documented by 1063, feud of the counts Guidi and the first fourteenth century of the Fridolfi Ricasoli; in the thirteenth century the castle was surrounded by walls with three gates, in 1529 the castle was conquered by the troops of the Prince of Orange and around 1550 it became the seat of the League of Havane. To see the church of San Pietro with the main bell of 1322 and the silver votive lamp of 1571 for the victory of Lepanto, the Porticciola (the only surviving door) and the coat of arms of the Municipality of Montegonzi in via Bartolo.

  • Where Montegonzi, Cavriglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.cavriglia.ar.it · visittuscany.com

Castello di Montaio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Castle on a steep hill above Cavriglia, confirmed at the counts Guidi in 1191; in 1251, it was occupied by ghibellines and German knights and in 1252, after a winter siege, Florence took it and razed the walls to the ground. Today it is a small rural village with the church of San Silvestro, cited since 1299, with neoclassical nineteenth century facade and stretches of medieval walls incorporated in the back; wide view of Cavriglia and the valley.

  • Where Montaio, Cavriglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.cavriglia.ar.it · visittuscany.com
