What to see in Quartu Sant'Elena

The third city of Sardinia, born from the union of three villages and known for malvasia, gold filigree and traditional sweets: the Basilica of Sant'Elena, the medieval churches of Sant'Agata and Santa Maria di Cepola, the museum Sa Dom'e Farra, the Nuraghe Diana on the gulf, the park of Molentargius and a coast that goes from Poetto to Cala Regina, Mari Pintau and Geremeas.

Basilica di Sant'Elena imperatrice

Places of worship · Basilicas

Basilica that preserves frescoes, pulpit and baptismal font of the eighteenth century; the statue of the saint is carried in procession on the patronal feast of mid-September.

Nuraghe Diana

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The most beautiful of the 38 Nuragic settlements in the territory, on a promontory on the Gulf of Cagliari above the beach of Capitana and Is Mortorius, guarding the Nuragic Harbour: central tower with false tholos dome, two minor towers connected and courtyard closed by a wall, with entrance to the Dolmenic system; dated to the middle of the II millennium BC, dug from the fifties, in the second world war the tower was reinforced as a viewing post.

  • Where Is Mortorius, Quartu Sant'Elena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Archeologia industriale: fornaci, mattatoio ed ex cartiera Perra

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

The kilns produced bricks and tiles from 1878 to 1985, the slaughterhouse worked until 1968 and the paper mill Perra, of 1911, was at the time the only factory of wrapping paper.

Parco naturale Molentargius-Saline

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Park established in 1999, over 1,600 hectares of ponds with different salinity (Bellarosa Minore and Perdalonga of fresh water, Bellarosa Maggiore and the pond of Quartu first basins of the former state salt pans), with pink flamingos, swamp falcons, garzettes, avocette and sultan chicken; you visit on foot, by bike or with guided birdwatching trails, better in spring and autumn.

  • Where Via Don Giordi 2, Quartu Sant'Elena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Cala Regina e la sua torre

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Bay on the Gulf of Angels closed by cliffs with eucalyptus and pine trees, rocks and rocks with stretches of amber sand and turquoise water, sheltered from the mistral and suitable for snorkeling and diving; on the western promontory the cylindrical granite tower built in the second half of the XVI century for the Spanish Crown. Parking space, also for campers, at the end of the road.
