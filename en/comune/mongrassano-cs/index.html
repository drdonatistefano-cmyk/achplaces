Centro iconografico Arbëresh

Museums · Themed

Permanent exhibition on the material culture of the Arbëreshe communities: on the ground floor six rooms, each dedicated to a theme, with objects and photographic documents arranged to evoke the context of use, not reconstructed environments. Each object has a caption in arbërisht, in Calabrian dialect, in Italian and, where possible, in English, with a brief description of the function. He tells about domestic and working life as it was until about half a century ago and introduces to the section on traditions. For timetables and visits: Municipality, tel. 0984 527209.

Chiesa dei Carmelitani (Congrega)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Probably born as a church of Basilian rite in the second half of the thirteenth century, in the sixteenth century it passed to the Carmelites, who built next to it the convent today seat of the Town Hall; from 1649 it had the title of Santa Maria del Carmine and from 1767 it housed the lay Congregation of the Most Immaculate, from which the name "Congrega" was given. The wooden door entirely carved is from the 17th century, with a stone frame attributed to Costantino Licursi and Cesare Capparelli. Inside a sixteenth-century canvas of the Annunciation, probably by Pietro Negroni, and the wooden statue of the Immaculate of Carlo Santoro.
