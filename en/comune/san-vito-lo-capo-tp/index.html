What to see in San Vito Lo Capo

Trapanese seaside resort on a sheltered bay dominated by Monte Monaco, born around the sanctuary-fortress of San Vito; from the village you can reach the lighthouse of Capo San Vito, the Secco Tonnara and the Zingaro Reserve, and at the end of September the Cous Cous Fest is held.

Santuario di San Vito

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

At the origin of the village: a small chapel dedicated to St. Vito martyr, built around the fourteenth century, was transformed at the end of the fifteenth century into a fortress and shelter to protect pilgrims from thieves and brigands, and with the increase of pirate raids had also a tower. It looks like a fortified church, more like a bastion, with Arab-Norman architecture; around it in the eighteenth century the houses of the country grew.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Cappella di Santa Crescenzia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Tiny chapel with Arab-Norman influences, in the middle of the countryside along the main road that leaves the village, linked to the devotion to St. Vitus: St. Crescent was, according to tradition, the nurse who accompanied the saint.

Tonnara del Secco

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Tonnara of the fifteenth century in Zarbo di Mare, in the Gulf of Firriato, next to the remains of plants for the processing of fish of the fourth century BC; it preserves the "Palazzotto" and tubs in cottopesto. Acquired in 1930 by Plaja and Foderà, it was active until 1969 and is today an example of industrial archaeology in abandonment, along the path to the Zingaro Reserve; in front lies the wreck of the merchant Kent, sunk in 1978.

The site is not visitable and is in a state of abandonment (Sicily Region): it can be seen only from the outside

  • Where Località Zarbo di Mare (circa 3 km dal centro), San Vito Lo Capo
  • Entry Free

Sources: pti.regione.sicilia.it · italia.it

Riserva naturale orientata dello Zingaro

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Protected coast to walk only, with five marked trails, coves like Cala Tonnarella, Cala dell'Uzzo, Cala Marinella, Cala Beretta, Cala Disa, Cala Capreria and Cala del Varo, and five museums on traditional activities such as tuna fishing and interweaving. It has two entrances, north from San Vito and south from Scopello; there are no refreshment points.

Reopened partially from March 14th 2026 after the fire: the north entrance towards San Vito Lo Capo remains closed for works, access only from Scopello (Sicily region); payment only electronic, free under 8 years; italia.it indicates opening 8-16

  • Where Ingresso nord, San Vito Lo Capo
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.riservazingaro.it

Sources: riservazingaro.it · italia.it · terra.regione.sicilia.it
