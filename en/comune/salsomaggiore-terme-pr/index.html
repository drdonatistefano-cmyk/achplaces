What to see in Salsomaggiore Terme

Salt town since 798 and then the salty waters: the Berzieri Thermal Baths and the former Grand Hôtel des Thermes decorated by Galileo Chini, the fossil museum of the ancient sea in the Stirone Park and the Pallavicino castles on the hills.

7 places to visit in Salsomaggiore Terme, in the province of Parma (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Terme Berzieri

Local life and traditions · Spas

Built between 1913 and 1923 on the site of the Old Establishment and still in operation: plant at E, architecture of Ugo Giusti and decorations by Galileo Chini that combine oriental motifs and liberty. Named to the doctor Lorenzo Berzieri, who since 1839-40 studied the properties of the waters; they offer thermal baths, massages and Kneipp routes. In front is the Scotti well in wrought iron and glass.

Palazzo dei Congressi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The former Grand Hôtel des Thermes, designed in 1898 by Luigi Broggi from Milan and opened in 1901: horse-iron plant, floral decorations by Gottardo Valentini on the facade and deleted by Alessandro Mazzucotelli. After the Great War Ugo Giusti and Galileo Chini added the wing with the Salone Moresco, the Red Tavern and the Loggia, inspired by the East.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

MuMAB – Museo Mare Antico e Biodiversità

Museums · Science and university

In the Stirone Park, it brings together the former paleontological and naturalistic museums: the fossils of the Stirone bed, when the Po Valley was sea, with the only known skeleton of the whale Plesiobalaenoptera fortellii, the immersive room of the whale Wednesday and Friday 15-19, Saturday and Sunday 9-13 and 15-19.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: parchidelducato.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Parco regionale fluviale dello Stirone

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

2,190 hectares along the bed of the Stirone, almost dry in summer, where the entire series of marine clays of the Pliocene and the Lower Pleistocene emerges: rich in fossils, it is an open-air museum.

  • Where Salsomaggiore Terme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Pieve di San Nicomede

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque church of the XII century, enriched in the fourteenth century and with a nineteenth-century portico, built on foundations of the IX century still visible in the lower part of the stone walls, pebbles and bricks.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Castello di Scipione

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

At the top of the medieval village of Scipione, 260 meters: built in the 11th century by Adalberto Pallavicino, transformed in the mid-15th century into a noble palace with cylindrical tower; extinct the Pallavicino di Scipione in 1776, passed to the Fogliani. Private but open to visitors.

  • Where Scipione, Salsomaggiore Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Terme Zoja e Parco Mazzini

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

The former Parco Regina Margherita, today Parco Mazzini, with the Luigi Zoja Thermal Baths built between 1967 and 1969 on a project by Franco Albini and Franca Helg, notable for its architecture as well as its thermal equipment.
