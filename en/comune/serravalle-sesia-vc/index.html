What to see in Serravalle Sesia

Municipality of the lower Valsesia born in 1927 from the union with Vintebbio, Piane and Bornate: the museum Don Florindo Piolo in the Castle of Avondo, the seventeenth-century sanctuary of Sant'Euseo, the Romanesque parish church of Santa Maria di Naula and the remains of the castles of Vintebbio and Bornate.

7 places to visit in Serravalle Sesia, in the province of Vercelli (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo di storia d'arte e d'antichità Don Florindo Piolo

Museums · City history

It is located in the Castle of Avondo (sec. XVIII) and collects local Roman finds, the legacy of the historian Don Florindo Piolo, an ethnographic collection, a former vote and a picture gallery. Times to be agreed with the Municipality.

  • Where Via Avondo 10, Serravalle Sesia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Santuario di Sant'Euseo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

A 17th century shrine built on a rocky spur, incorporating an older temple, reached by a white granite staircase of the quarries of Alzo. In the first chapel on the right there is a fresco of the fifteenth century with the twelve Apostles and Christ in almond, restored by the Superintendence.

  • Where Serravalle Sesia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.serravallesesia.vc.it

Sources: comune.serravallesesia.vc.it

Pieve di Santa Maria di Naula

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque parish church shaded by badgers, cited in Vatican Code 4322, list of the Vercellian parish churches of the 10th century that reflects a situation of the 5th-6th century. Inside there are frescoes from different eras, including a Madonna of Late-Gothic Milk.

  • Where Cantone Naula, frazione Piane, Serravalle Sesia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.serravallesesia.vc.it

Sources: comune.serravallesesia.vc.it

Parrocchiale dei Santi Giovanni Battista e Nicolao

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the seventeenth century with frescoes by Borsetti and Peracino; on the Corso Matteotti rises the stone bell tower and lime with a massive profile.

  • Where Corso Matteotti, Serravalle Sesia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.serravallesesia.vc.it

Sources: comune.serravallesesia.vc.it

Castello e borgo di Vintebbio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Castle wanted by the bishops of Vercelli and built with pebbles of Sesia: the wounds of the west tower remain, some mullioned windows, parts of the entrance and a wolf tooth frieze on the south tower. In the hamlet also the church of St. Joseph, parish until 1667 with fragments of 16th century frescoes, and the church of the Madonna del Rosario, built in 1628 on the saxo of the Madonna.

  • Where Frazione Vintebbio, Serravalle Sesia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.serravallesesia.vc.it

Sources: comune.serravallesesia.vc.it

Piane: parrocchiale di San Giacomo e oratorio di San Carlo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The 17th-century parish church of San Giacomo preserves, behind the present altar, a more ancient altar in carved wood and golden of exceptional workmanship; the oratory of San Carlo holds a polychrome wooden Pietà. In the canton La Sella a fountain and the oratory of the Immaculate Conception with a cruising porch; The Caterra is a small nucleus of dense houses.

  • Where Frazione Piane, Serravalle Sesia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.serravallesesia.vc.it

Sources: comune.serravallesesia.vc.it

Bornate: chiesa del Nome di Maria e rovine del castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The church of the Name of Mary, to the west of the village, is the oldest and most remarkable building of the hamlet, with remains of frescoes in façade dated 1453; next, courtyards with loggia houses. To the south, on a rocky spur, the remains of the castle made of porphyry stone overlooking the valleys of Sesia, Sessera and Strona.

  • Where Frazione Bornate, Serravalle Sesia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.serravallesesia.vc.it

Sources: comune.serravallesesia.vc.it
