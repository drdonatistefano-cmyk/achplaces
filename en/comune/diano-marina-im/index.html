What to see in Diano Marina

Seaside resort of the Dianese Gulf, between Capo Berta and Capo Cervo, with more than 3,500 meters of sandy beach from the bottoms; in the Palazzo del Parco the Museo Civico MARM tells the wreck of a Roman ship to sunk in the first century A.D. in front of the coast.

Museo Civico MARM

Museums · Archaeological

Managed by the Istituto Internazionale di Studi Liguri, it occupies ten rooms (a multimedia one) on the second floor of the Palazzo del Parco and traces in chronological order the human presence between Capo Berta and Capo Cervo, from the bronze age (XVIII century B.C.) to the late antiquity and the first barbaric invasions that led to the birth of Diano Castello on the hill. The fifth hall is dedicated to ancient routes, goods and means of transport; the wreck of a ship Â"a doliaÂ" sunk in the middle of the first century AD in the Gulf of Dianese stands out. There are also the archaeological sections "Lucus Bormani" , Risorgimento "Andrea Rossi" and mineralogical "De Cavero" . Palazzo del Parco e giardino botanico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Palace on three floors built in the second half of the nineteenth century for a wealthy local family, with frescoes from the beginning of the twentieth century on the noble floor and valuable concrete floors; in the cellars are re-emerged tanks for oil and well. The garden is a botanical park in the taste of the time, with flower beds, benches overlooking the promenade and the sea and many exotic plants. Today it is the cultural center of the municipality: Biblioteca Civica (Civic Library) . Novaro, Museo MARM and Sala Mostre Hawks.

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It was elevated to archpretura in 1690, it was demolished and rebuilt larger in 1862-1865 on a project by the Roman Luigi Crescia, consecrated in 1873 and reopened in 1887 after the earthquake that destroyed the pictorial cycle of Lazaro De Maestri. It has three naves with twenty columns with Corinthian capitals; frescoes by Raffaele Resio in the central nave and apse, decorated by Antonio and Francesco Bertolotto, a Deposition by Giovanni Battista Casoni, the main altar by Giuseppe Gaggini (in 1694) and the marble pulpit by Gio Maria Augustallo. The 17th-century churchyard is made of white and black pebbles.

Lucus Bormani e zona archeologica di Prato Fiorito

History and architecture · Archaeology

Area of the Roman settlement of the first century BC on the Via Iulia Augusta, where according to the source under Augustus (13/12 BC) a mansio arose. The fulcrum is the deconsecrated church of Saints Nazarius and Celsus, a single nave with semicircular apse, built on Roman structures; the excavations of the International Institute of Ligurian Studies, since 2005, have brought to light about eighty burials. Few remains are visible because the modern city rises above the site; the finds are at the Museum MARM.

Promontorio di Capo Berta e Torre dell'Alpicella

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Capo Berta closes the Gulf of Dianese to the west (on the east there is Capo Cervo) and is a protected area crossed by the Aurelia and climbed from Milan-Sanremo. On the cliffs grow gariga of rosemary, thyme, broom and dwarf palm, pine forests of Aleppo pine trees and endemic species such as Limonium cordatum and Campanula Sabatia; in the sea there are prairies of Posidonia oceanica. At the highest point, 267 m in the hamlet of Diano Gorleri, stands the Torre dell'Alpicella, circular fortification in masonry of the XIV-XV century, probably a sighting tower; you arrive from Via Diano Calderina following the signs.

Spiagge e lungomare

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

The coast extends for over 3,500 meters with shallow and sandy bottoms suitable for children; the beaches, Blue Flag, have facilities where you can practice beach volleyball, surfing and kitesurfing. The promenade is a long walk that continues towards San Bartolomeo al Mare and Andora; from the port start boat excursions in the Sanctuary of the Cetacei Pelagos to spot whales and dolphins.
