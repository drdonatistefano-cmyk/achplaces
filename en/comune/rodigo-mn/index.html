What to see in Rodigo

The municipality of Alto Mincio, which with the hamlet of Rivalta sul Mincio overlooks the Natural Reserve Valli del Mincio, where a fishing house has been hosting the Ethnographic Museum of the Crafts of the River since 1998. In the capital, the sixteenth-century Villa Balestra houses the museum dedicated to Ippolito Nievo.

Museo etnografico dei mestieri del fiume «Roberto Coffani»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Founded in 1998 on the banks of the Mincio, in the heart of the Riserva Valli del Mincio, it is set up in a typical fishing house partly recreated and hosts the Mincio Park Visitor Centre. It exhibits objects of everyday life and work in swamps collected by the inhabitants: tools for the cultivation of the marsh cane and of the carice, fishing tools and hunting, tools and domestic furnishings, with a space on flora and fauna of the protected area. Free of charge up to 5 years; open Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday with different times, other days on request.

  • Where Via Porto, Rivalta sul Mincio, Rodigo
  • Entry €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website cultura.gov.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.rodigo.mn.it

Riserva naturale Valli del Mincio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

In Rivalta, the Mincio extends into a large valley area divided into canals and water mirrors; the wetland between Casazze and the Angels was declared a natural reserve by the Lombardy Region in 1984. It grows water lilies, nannufer, water chestnut, iris, marsh hybiscus and watermint, grasslands of charici and rare species such as marsh gentian, orchids and marshy garlic; the lotus flower, introduced in the last century, became infesting.

  • Where Rivalta sul Mincio, Rodigo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Parco Fondo Mincio

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Municipal park overlooking the Mincio, dedicated to the Comm. Giancarlo Gauli, with the ethnographic museum, hostel, bar and restaurant; a small landing place allows you to board boats and canoes, with canoes, water bikes and boats. Free pedestrian access, parking on the river.

  • Where Via Porto, Rivalta sul Mincio, Rodigo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: comune.rodigo.mn.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo diffuso di Rivalta sul Mincio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Outdoor route of a dozen photo panels with caption, some with QR code, which tell the story of the village along some streets of the village; you can follow online. Streets without architectural barriers.

  • Where Corte Mincio, via Porto, Rivalta sul Mincio, Rodigo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.rivaltasiracconta.it

Sources: rivaltasiracconta.it · comune.rodigo.mn.it · cultura.gov.it

MuDRI Alto Mincio - Ippolito Nievo a Villa Balestra

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In the sixteenth century Villa Balestra, in a park at the edge of the village, the Museo diffuso del Risorgimento has two permanent exhibitions on Ippolito Nievo (1831-1861): Open during the hours of the municipal library or by appointment; accessible by elevator.

  • Where Villa Balestra, piazza Balestra 1, Rodigo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: comune.rodigo.mn.it · cultura.gov.it
