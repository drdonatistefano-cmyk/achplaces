What to see in Lavagna

Città dei Fieschi on the Gulf of Tigullio, at the mouth of Entella, which takes its name from the slate of its quarries: the "caruggio" of Via Roma with medieval porticoes, the basilica of Santo Stefano, the Casa Carbone del FAI, the Tower of the Village and the Blue Flag of Cavi beach.

5 places to visit in Lavagna, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Via Roma, Basilica di Santo Stefano e Porticato Brignardello

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Via Roma, the ...caruggio is the main street with medieval porticoes, historic shops and restaurants; it ends in Piazza Marconi, dominated by the Basilica of Santo Stefano and the Porticato Brignardello. The old town is made of arcades and colorful houses.

  • Where Via Roma e Piazza Marconi, Lavagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: lamialiguria.it · italia.it

Casa Carbone

Museums · House museums

This nineteenth-century villa with a back garden remains intact: it tells the life, tastes and interests of a bourgeois family from the late nineteenth century, with its central salon, rooms furnished in eclectic styles, tempera ceilings with Ligurian themes, mosaic floors and paintings especially from Genoese from the sixteenth to the eighteenth century. It belonged to the Whites, the Raffas and then to the Carbone brothers, it was left to FAI in 1987 by Emanuele and Syria Carbone. Free entry for FAI members.

winter closing from November 2nd according to the FAI

  • Where Via Riboli 14, Lavagna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: fondoambiente.it · lamialiguria.it · italia.it · ticket.fondoambiente.it

Torre del Borgo

History and architecture · Towers

Ancient Saracen tower, 13 metres high, probably erected against pirates, for centuries the tallest building in Lavagna; restored for the Jubilee of 2000, in a garden in the historic center, hosts cultural events, the slate works of the sculptor Francesco Dallorso and the archaeological collection Rodolfo Alloisio with Tuscan ceramics and Ligurian majolica. In the summer open Thursday-Saturday 20:30-22:30.

  • Where Via XX Settembre, Lavagna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.lavagna.ge.it · italia.it

Spiaggia di Cavi

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Sandy beach about four kilometers long, part of the coast Blue Flag of Lavagna, indicated among the most beautiful seaside destinations of Liguria; also the marina has the Blue Flag.

  • Where Cavi di Lavagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: lamialiguria.it · italia.it

Palazzo Franzoni

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

17th century palace, dated 1696, now seat of the Town Hall.

  • Where Piazza della Libertà 47, Lavagna
