What to see in Vasto

The ancient Histonium overlooking the sea, half a kilometer from the coast: Palazzo d'Avalos with the civic museums, the Caldoresco Castle, Piazza Rossetti and the walks to the sea.

6 places to visit in Vasto, in the province of Chieti (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo d'Avalos – Musei civici

Museums · City history · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Founded perhaps by Giacomo Caldora at the beginning of the fifteenth century, passed to Guevara in 1496 and to d'Avalos in 1573, recovered since 1970: it houses the art gallery (Michetti, Palizzi), the archaeological collection on the Frentani and Histonium and a Neapolitan garden of eighteenth-century plant with terrace on the sea.

  • Where Vasto
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
