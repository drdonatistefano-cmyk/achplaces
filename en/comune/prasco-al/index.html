What to see in Prasco

Small town of the valley of the Visone, dominated by the castle of the Counts Gallesio-Piuma (XII century), seat of the Welsh Studies Center; from the village starts the hiking route "Collinando" towards the Fortress of Visone, between ancient churches and panoramic points.

Castello di Prasco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Castle of the Counts Gallesio-Piuma dating back to the 12th century, with a quadrangular body and three semicircular towers. It rises on a terrace with three gardens inside the walls, surrounded by a park in which there is a seventeenth-century snowfall; it houses the Welsh Study Centre, dedicated to Giorgio Gallesio, and a small museum of material culture. Visits by reservation (tel. 0144 375628); adheres to open castles with opening on the first and third Sundays of the summer month.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Percorso Prasco-Visone «Collinando»

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Itinerary of 8.5 km (about 3 hours and 20 minutes, difference in altitude 246 m, difficulty T) from the swimming pools of Prasco to the Rocca di Visone, on asphalted country roads with two short dirt sections, also suitable for the MTB and accessible all year round. Touch the former parish church of Saints Nazarius and Celsus (XI-XVI century, in the cemetery of Prasco), the country church of the Cappelletta (XV century), the Roman bridge over the river Visone, the oratory of San Rocco and the tower of the castle Malaspina; churches and towers are visited by appointment with the municipalities of Prasco or Visone.

  • Where Partenza: piscine di Prasco, Via Roma, Prasco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.prasco.al.it

Sources: comune.prasco.al.it
