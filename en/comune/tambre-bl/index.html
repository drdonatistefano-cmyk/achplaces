What to see in Tambre

Alpago mountain municipality at 922 meters, at the foot of Monte Cavallo and surrounded by the Cansiglio Forest, ancient dominion of the Serenissima: on the Pian del Cansiglio you will find the Regional Museum of Man, the naturalistic museum of the Forest Carabinieri, the Alpine Botanical Garden and the karst abyss of the Bus de la Lum.

5 places to visit in Tambre, in the province of Belluno (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo dell'Uomo in Cansiglio (MUC)

Museums · City history

Regional Museum of Veneto Agricoltura on two floors, in a building of the early fifties: it tells the human presence in Cansiglio from Prehistory to the present day, including the long phase of the Republic of Venice.

  • Where Pian Osteria, Pian del Cansiglio, Tambre
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.dolomitiprealpi.it

Sources: cansiglio.venetoagricoltura.org · dolomitiprealpi.it

Museo Naturalistico Giovanni Zanardo (Museo di Ecologia)

Museums · Science and university

Small naturalistic museum born in the seventies and run by the Carabinieri Forestali, with finds of the plateau, reconstructions of environments and a section on the birdlife. Open from June to October (Wednesday 14-17; Thursday and Friday 9-13 and 14-17; Saturday and Sunday 9-13 and 14-18), in the other months by appointment.

  • Where Via Cansiglio, Tambre
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.dolomitiprealpi.it

Sources: dolomitiprealpi.it · cansiglio.venetoagricoltura.org

Giardino Botanico Alpino

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Designed in 1972, it gathers about 500 species of plants of the group Cansiglio-Cavallo ordered for environments on about one and a half hectare, with a collection of medicinal plants.

  • Where Via Cansiglio, Pian del Cansiglio, Tambre
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.dolomitiprealpi.it

Sources: cansiglio.venetoagricoltura.org · dolomitiprealpi.it

Bus de la Lum

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Karst swallower about 180 meters deep, formed by a single vertical well of 10-15 meters of diameter, dug by water in the carbonate rock.

  • Where Pian del Cansiglio, Tambre
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.dolomitiprealpi.it

Sources: cansiglio.venetoagricoltura.org · dolomitiprealpi.it

Foresta del Cansiglio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The forest surrounds Tambre and has been an ancient dominion of the Serenissima; its trails run on foot, on horseback and by mountain bike, and in the heart of the forest there is an 18-hole golf course.

  • Where Pian del Cansiglio, Tambre
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.dolomitiprealpi.it

Sources: dolomitiprealpi.it
