What to see in Magasa

Small village in the hinterland of Valvestino, inside the Park Alto Garda Bresciano, Magasa is known for the hamlet of Cima Rest, at 1200 m, with the hay-roofed barns, the ethnographic museum and the astronomical observatory, and for the cheese Tombea, which takes its name from the mountain that dominates the village.

Fienili di Cima Rest

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the plateau of Cima Rest, in the northern part of the Vestino Valley at the foot of the Tombea and Caplone mountains, stand the barns-stallas in stone with the sloping roof in straw: the oldest date back to the seventeenth century and each roof is formed by about 7,500 bundles of wheat straw more than one metre long, bound in such a way as to be waterproof. According to Visit Val Vestino are the only known example in Lombardy of buildings with still existing straw roof; some have been restored as holiday homes. Here again the cheese Tombea, Presidio Slow Food, is produced, and every September since 1979 the Cheese Festival of Cima Rest is held.

Museo etnografico della Valvestino

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Set in a characteristic barn of the village of Cima Rest, it is divided into three rooms: the barn on the upper floor, the barn and the caséra on the lower floor. The exhibits, mostly tools of agricultural, craft and dairy work, are chosen for their symbolic and cultural value and show how the interiors of these buildings once presented themselves.

open by reservation (Consorzio Forestale Terra tra i due Laghi, tel. 0365 745007 or 334 6686327)

Osservatorio astronomico di Cima Rest

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

In Cima Rest, under a sky free from air pollution and light, the Astrofili Association of Salò offers its instruments with an annual calendar of openings to the public, in collaboration with the Municipality of Magasa and the Park Alto Garda Bresciano. The structure accommodates 20-30 people; the evenings, from May to October, begin at the drop of the darkness about 20 minutes walk from the church and last about two hours; with covered sky you follow the theoretical part.

evenings from May to October with reservation required at the Tourist Office Val Vestino (tel. +39 0365 745060)

Monte Tombea e trincee della Grande Guerra

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

A path from Magasa climbs towards the Tombea and Caplone mountains along the ancient border between Italy and Austria (the valley was Austrian until 1915-18): military deposits are still excavated in the rock, trenches and masonry stations; from the Bocca di Cablone a military road leads to the Bocca di Campei. On the summit of Tombea (1950 m) a walled compass indicates the names of 34 visible mountains; here the Saxifraga tombeanensis was discovered and royal eagles, deer, ibex and deer are observed.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
