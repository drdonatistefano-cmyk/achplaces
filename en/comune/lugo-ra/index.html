What to see in Lugo

4 places to visit in Lugo, in the province of Ravenna (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca di Lugo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

The fortress extended in the center, today seat of the municipality, with the hanging garden and the tombstones of memory.

  • Where Piazza dei Martiri, Lugo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo Francesco Baracca

Museums · Themed

The ace museum house was born in Lugo, with its aircraft and relics of the Great War.

  • Entry To be checked on the official website

Pavaglione

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The large eighteenth-century portico was created for the silkworm market.

  • Where Centro storico, Lugo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Pieve di Santa Maria in Fabriago (Pieve di Campanile)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

One of the oldest parishes of Ravennate, with a single nave with polygonal apse rebuilt in the early sixteenth century.

  • Where Fabriago, Lugo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
