What to see in Monteverde

Irpino village gathered around the Aragonese castle, with the former cathedral of Santa Maria di Nazareth, alleys and steps dug in the rock and, under the village, the lake of San Pietro and the valley of Ofanto, where nests the black stork.

5 places to visit in Monteverde, in the province of Avellino (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built in local stone on the Lombard tower and enlarged by the Aragonese in the 15th century; in 1744 the Sangermano barons transformed it into a residence adding the right wing. Completely restored in 2006, at the base exhibition remains of the pelasgic-sannitic walls (IV-III century BC).

Ex cattedrale di Santa Maria di Nazareth

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Under the castle, it has a Baroque shape from the reconstruction of 1728 on a 14th century plant expanded in the 16th century; the restoration after the earthquake of 1980 brought to light Romanesque structures. It preserves paintings of the Neapolitan school of the seventeenth century, wooden statues, altars between the fifteenth and eighteenth centuries and a crucifix dated to the thirteenth century.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

It preserves the original urban layout, with narrow alleys, steps and houses that seem to merge with the rock; in Via Bocchetti the Palaces Pelosi and Spirit have artistic portals and along the alleys a rock in the shape of an elephant, shaped by the wind, is set in a house. From the village the view extends over the wheat fields to the sea of Manfredonia.

  • Where Via Bocchetti, Monteverde
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

An eighteenth-century church annexed to the convent of the Carmelites; the bell tower has Gothic mullioned windows of 1888.

Lago di San Pietro, fiume Ofanto e bosco Zampaglione

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural sites equipped with picnic areas under the village: on summer evenings the lights of the village are reflected on the artificial lake of San Pietro, and the unspoilt environment houses the nesting of the rare black stork.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
