What to see in Cherasco

Città Bandiera Arancione from the chessboard system enclosed between two monumental arches, with noble palaces, the panoramic bastions and the famous antique markets.

7 places to visit in Cherasco, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo Salmatoris

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Frescoed by Sebastiano Taricco, the Sabauda residence where the peace of 1631 was signed, the Shroud was guarded in 1706 and Napoleon dictated the surrender to Vittorio Amedeo III; today it is the seat of art exhibitions.

  • Where Via Vittorio Emanuele II, Cherasco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlmr.it · touringclub.it

Arco del Belvedere

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The seventeenth-century arch erected as a vote for the end of the plague, which closes to the north via Vittorio Emanuele; to the south the eighteenth-century arch of Porta Narzole.

  • Where Via Vittorio Emanuele II, Cherasco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlmr.it · italia.it · touringclub.it
