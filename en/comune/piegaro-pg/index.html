What to see in Piegaro

Borgo dei Monti del Trasimeno overlooking the Nestor, between the lake and Città della Pieve, linked from the end of the thirteenth century to the art of glass brought by Venetian craftsmen, today told in the Glass Museum; in the territory the castles of Castiglion Fosco, Cibotola and Gaiche and, in Pietrafitta, one of the richest collections of fossil vertebrates in Italy.

Museo del Vetro

Museums · Themed

It occupies the ancient glassworks of the early nineteenth century in the historic center, abandoned in 1968 and restored with the original architecture, example of industrial archaeology and among the few pre-industrial glass plants preserved in Italy. The three-storey route follows the production cycle, from raw materials to hand blowing; on display glasses, bottles, carafes and damsel, some decorated in pure gold, and in the basement a large casting of emerald green glass formed at the closure of the factory. Organize craft workshops.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodelvetropiegaro.it

Museo paleontologico «Luigi Boldrini»

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

In the palaeontological site of Pietrafitta, one of the most important in Umbria: fossils, about 1.5 million years ago, come from the lignite excavations for the central city of Rome in the deposits of the ancient lake Tiberino. Plants, mollusks, insects and especially vertebrates: a large mammoth (Mammuthus meridionalis), skeletons of rhinoceros, deer, archaic bisons, beavers and birds. The recovery began with Luigi Boldrini and continues with the University of Perugia. Open Mar, Mer, Thu, Sat and Sun 9-13 and 15-18.30.

  • Where Via Papa Giovanni XXIII, Pietrafitta, Piegaro
  • Entry €4, reduced €2, free EU under 18
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Chiesa di San Silvestro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Documented since 1275, it was almost entirely rebuilt in the nineteenth century and completed in 1864. He preserves a Madonna with the Child among the Saints Sebastiano and Rocco of the Umbrian school of the sixteenth century, a wooden crucifix of the seventeenth century that in 1738 would have 'sudato', a Christ Justifier between Saint Catherine of Siena and Saint Dominic and the scene of Constantine before Pope Silvestro.

Castiglion Fosco

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Medieval village infeudated in the tenth century by Emperor Otto III, with a cylindrical tower of the fifteenth century 25 meters high and the church of Santa Croce, with a wooden cross of the eighteenth century and paintings of the sixteenth century.

Cibottola

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry
