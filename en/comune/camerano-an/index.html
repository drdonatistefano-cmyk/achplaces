What to see in Camerano

A hilly village at the foot of Monte Conero, home of the seventeenth-century painter Carlo Maratti, famous for the Caves, an underground network excavated in the sandstone under the old town; among churches with works of Maratti and vineyards of Rosso Conero, the sea is just a few minutes away.

Grotte di Camerano

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

A network of tunnels and salt excavated by man in the sandstone under the old town, between Piazza Roma and the streets Maratti and San Francesco (the ...Rupe del Sassone) about 2-3 km, of which about 1 km can be visited, with vaulted dome, sail and barrel, circular rooms, columns, bas-reliefs and religious and esoteric symbols. Used in time as a shelter, a food and wine warehouse and a meeting place, they returned to World War II. They visit only accompanied, all year round, in about an hour; inside the temperature is around 14 degrees.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Camerano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.camerano.an.it · italia.it · letsmarche.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Italia.it indicates it as the most beautiful of the ancient churches in the country; it is named after Saint Francis, who according to the local tradition reported by Let's Marche passed from Camerano.

Chiese con opere di Carlo Maratti

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the church of San Nicolò di Bari were transferred from the Roman catacombs the remains of Saint Faustina, transport attributed to Maratti; works of the painter are preserved in the churches of Santa Faustina and the Immaculate Conception.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Rosso Conero di cantina in cantina

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Hills and vineyards of Montepulciano give Rosso Conero, to be tasted from cellar to cellar; the Festa del Rosso Conero is held the first week of September with refreshment points, stalls, street games and tastings, and in summer they organize tastings also in the caves.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Sentieri del Parco del Conero

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Camerano is located in the heart of the Conero Park: from the paths in the woods you walk on foot, by bike or by eBike, between gentle hills, the river Aspio and Mount Conero, with the sea just a few minutes away.

  • Entry Free
