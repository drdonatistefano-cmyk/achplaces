What to see in Torno

Borgo on the eastern shore of Lake Como, already inhabited in pre-Roman and imperial times, with the churches of Santa Tecla and San Giovanni, the sixteenth-century Villa Pliniana anchored to the rock and, on the mountains, the avelli dug in erratic boulders and the Pietra Pendula of Montepiatto along the Strada Regia.

7 places to visit in Torno, in the province of Como (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque-Gothic church of the 12th century (Italy.it indicates the fourteenth century); the façade was restored in 1999 for the 9th centenary of the deposition of the Holy Nail in Torno. It has a fifteenth-century marble portal and paintings from various eras on the walls.

  • Where Torno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Tecla

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Overlooking Piazza Casartelli, it is of Romanesque origin and was renovated in the seventeenth century; the vault contains remains of frescoes by the tornasco painter Bartolomeo De Benzi. It guards the Man of Pain, important fresco of the sixteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Casartelli, Torno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Avelli e Pietra Pendula

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the mountains of Torno you can reach on foot the Avelli, tub graves (V-VI century A.D.) dug in the erratic boulders left by the glaciers, in the localities Maas, Rasina, Piazz and Negrenza. In Montepiatto stands the Stone Pendula, erratic stone shaped like a fungus and natural monument; some erratic boulders were recognized as natural monuments by the Lombardy Region in 1984.

  • Where Montepiatto, Torno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Strada Regia

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

An ancient track of about 32 km, almost entirely pedestrian, that connects Como to Bellagio passing through Torno and its hamlets.

  • Where Torno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it
