What to see in Cortina d'Ampezzo

In the heart of the Venetian Dolomites, between Tofane, Cristallo, Sorapiss and Antelao, Cortina hosted the 1956 Olympics and hosted those of Milan-Cortina 2026; the Natural Park of the Dolomites of Ampezzo, all in the municipality, is part of the UNESCO Dolomites System 5. In the center is the Basilica of Saints Philip and James with the bell tower and the three Museums of the Rules.

Museo d'Arte Moderna Mario Rimoldi

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Born in 1974 from the donation of Mario Rimoldi's collection by the widow Rosa Braun alle Regole, it is located in Ciasa de ra Regoles on the Corso Italia, near the parish church. The collection, declared of cultural interest by the Ministry, includes works by Savinio, Garbari, Depero and Guttuso and important nuclei of de Pisis, Sironi, De Chirico, Semeghini, Tomea, Tosi and Campigli. Family ticket €15; cumulative for the three Museums of the Rules €12, reduced €8.

The museum is currently closed, opening scheduled for December (Museum site)

  • Where Corso Italia 69, Cortina d'Ampezzo
  • Entry €8, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website musei.regole.it

Sources: musei.regole.it · regole.it · italia.it

Museo Paleontologico Rinaldo Zardini

Museums · Science and university

Opened in August 1975, it exhibits the triassic fossils collected in the Cortina area largely by Rinaldo Zardini, one of the largest collections of the genus: megalodontes, shells of marine invertebrates, corals and sponges that tell the geological evolution of the Ampezzo. Open Tuesday-Sunday 15-19, closed on Monday.

  • Where Via Marangoi 1, loc. Pontechiesa, Cortina d'Ampezzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website musei.regole.it

Sources: musei.regole.it · italia.it · regole.it

Museo Etnografico Regole d'Ampezzo

Museums · Themed

In the former regulation sawmill, he tells with objects and images the Rules, the centuries-old collective property of the Cortina valley: traditional heritage, management of woods and pastures and the link between the community and the territory.

  • Where Centro culturale Alexander Girardi Hall, loc. Pontechiesa, Cortina d'Ampezzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website musei.regole.it

Sources: musei.regole.it · regole.it · italia.it

Basilica dei Santi Filippo e Giacomo e campanile

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Built between 1769 and 1775 and restored in 1975, it preserves the late Baroque high altar with a painting by Antonio Zanchi and the altar of the Madonna del Rosario attributed to the school of Andrea Brustolon. The bell tower, 65.80 meters high, was inaugurated in 1858 with bells arrived from Innsbruck.

  • Where Corso Italia, Cortina d'Ampezzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cortina.dolomiti.org · italia.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Documented from 1396, next to the Casa Corazza, it preserves a wooden altar of the eighteenth century and a fresco of the late fourteenth century with three saints, of which it is recognized only St. Bartholomew.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Built in the second half of the fifteenth century and rebuilt in 1743, it preserves a wooden statue of the late fifteenth century and an eighteenth-century painting on the ceiling; the feast is celebrated every January 19th.

Corso Italia e Ciàsa de i Pùpe

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The main shopping street, with cafés and about 250 shops in the village; towards the north the Ciàsa dei Pupe, a house frescoed with the faces of Leonardo da Vinci, Shakespeare and Dante, and near the Basilica, in the former inn Stella d'Oro, the Sibille fresco of the first half of the fifteenth century.

Parco Naturale delle Dolomiti d'Ampezzo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established in 1990, 11,200 hectares entirely in the municipality of Cortina, it is owned and managed by the Regole d'Ampezzo and includes the groups of Crystal, Tofane and Lagazuoi; it is part of the System 5 (northern Dolomites) of the UNESCO site and has as symbol the Sempervivum dolomiticum, plant present only in the Dolomites of Ampezzo. It offers eight via ferrate, six other equipped routes, eleven shelters and in winter seven km of cross-country trails.

  • Where Cortina d'Ampezzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: dolomitiunesco.info · italia.it

Cima Tofana di Mezzo e funivia Freccia nel Cielo

Nature and landscape · Mountains · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

At 3.244 meters is the highest peak of the area of Cortina and the third of the Dolomites; the cable car Areccia nel Cielo climbs up to the top, to the panoramic terrace over the entire basin of Ampezzo, particularly beautiful at sunrise and sunset. The Tofane massif offers about 47 km of slopes in winter and hiking and mountain biking trails in summer.

  • Where Tofane, Cortina d'Ampezzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · dolomitiunesco.info
