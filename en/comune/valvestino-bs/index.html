What to see in Valvestino

Between 680 and 1,900 metres, the scattered municipality of the Alto Garda Bresciano Park with the hamlets of Turano, Armo, Persone and Moerna around the artificial lake fed by Toscolano; in Moerna the Botanical Museum Don Pietro Porta tells the flora of the valley.

Museo botanico Don Pietro Porta

Museums · Science and university · Free entry

Dedicated to the botanist Don Pietro Porta, born in Moerna, who studied the local flora describing new species (some bear his name); he is located in the former turnary cheese factory of the village, in front of his house. Upstairs a multimedia path with objects belonging to Porta and samples of plants collected by him, on the ground floor the original halls of milk processing with kangaroos and sports. It is part of the Alto Garda Museum Network.

the Park indicates opening only by reservation (tel. 0365 745007), the MiC card (221) on Sunday 14.30-17.30

  • Where Via Belvedere 9, Moerna, Valvestino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: parcoaltogarda.eu · cultura.gov.it · in-lombardia.it

Escursione Turano-Armo-Persone-Moerna

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring with departure and arrival in Turano (piazza Madonna Pellegrina, 658 m), which climbs to hairpin bends to the village of Armo, with narrow streets, an ancient fountain and the church, follows the valley of the torrent Armarolo with water jumps, pots and waterfalls and touches the clearing of Messane, highest point with a wide panorama, then cross People and Moerna along the ancient street Turano-Moerna, largely dirt. The parish church of Turano offers a panoramic view of the valley.

  • Where Piazza Madonna Pellegrina, Turano, Valvestino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: gardalombardia.com · in-lombardia.it
