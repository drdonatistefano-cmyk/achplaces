What to see in La Morra

The "balcony" of the Langhe del Barolo: a medieval village with a fan-shaped view of the hills and the Alps.

3 places to visit in La Morra, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Belvedere di piazza Castello

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the square on top of the village a spectacular view of the hills of the Langhe and the Alps.

  • Where Piazza Castello, La Morra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Cappella delle Brunate (del Barolo)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

A chapel among the vineyards transformed into a building with vibrant colorful decoration, inside and outside; the interior is not open to visitors.

  • Where Strada Fontanazza, La Morra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Borgo medievale di La Morra

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village is in a beautiful panoramic position, with a medieval structure reminiscent of a fan.

  • Where La Morra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it
