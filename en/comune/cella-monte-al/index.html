What to see in Cella Monte

The village of the infernot in the heart of the Monferrato degli Infernot UNESCO, among the most beautiful villages in Italy and the Orange Flag, built of stone from cantons.

3 places to visit in Cella Monte, in the province of Alessandria (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Cella Monte in the guide with map and filters

Ecomuseo della Pietra da Cantoni e infernot

Museums · Themed · UNESCO World Heritage

At Palazzo Volta, the origins and uses of the stone cantons and a visitable infernot, one of the underground cells dug by hand in the sandstone to preserve the wine; open on Sundays, the other days by reservation.

  • Where Piazza Vallino, Cella Monte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Centro storico in pietra da cantoni

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village of cobbled streets, manor houses and palaces with stone facades from cantons, hidden courtyards and quiet alleys.

  • Where Via del Borgo, Cella Monte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa romanica di San Quirico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The Romanesque church of the village; in the village also the parish church of Saints Quirico and Julitta with three aisles, the Madonna of Loreto and San Rocco.

  • Where Cella Monte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it
