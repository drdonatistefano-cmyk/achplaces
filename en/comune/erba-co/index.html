What to see in Erba

Centre of Alta Brianza at the foot of the Lariano Triangle, with the Romanesque Sant'Eufemia in Incino, the Hermitage of San Salvatore and the Pessina Cross above Crevenna, the Teatro Licinium of 1928, historic villas such as Villa Amalia and, in Valle Bova, the cave of the Hole of Lead with the remains of the cave bear.

10 places to visit in Erba, in the province of Como (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Buco del Piombo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Cave at 695 meters above sea level in the Bova Valley Nature Reserve: 155 steps lead to a 45 meters high natural arch, on which remain the ruins of a fort of the middle of the 6th century (bound since 1912). The tourist route, easy, is about 280 meters long; at the point called "Banco degli Orsi" (Banco degli Orsi) fossil remains of the cave bear (Ursus spelaeus) were found. Closed for years, it has been reopened thanks to the Association Guides Hole of Lead and the Bova Valley; guided tours on Sundays at 10.30, 12, 14 and 15.30.

Eremo di San Salvatore e Croce Pessina

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Above Crevenna, a 15th-16th century complex enlarged in the 16th century by the Capuchin friars, with a chapel linked to San Carlo Borromeo, frescoes by Michelino da Besozzo and others, a cloister from the late 15th to early 16th century and a avenue of cypresses. Continuing on foot towards the ...Crus Pisina the mule track is steep up to the Sasso Erba, rock gym, then easier; in the wood before the viewpoint there are picnic tables and a barbecue, and next to the cross a small altar and a large bell on iron structure.

  • Where Crevenna, Erba
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · comune.erba.co.it

Chiesa di Sant'Eufemia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient Romanesque church with a single nave overlooking the square of Incino, with square bell tower of the 11th century; it preserves a Romanesque sculpture (X-XI century) in the bezel of the portal, a 1212 holy waterworks, a wooden crucifix and a fresco of the 15th century, while the old canon has three fourteenth-century windows. For centuries it was the reference of the parish, until in 1574 St. Charles moved it to Santa Maria Nascente.

Teatro Licinium e Monumento ai Caduti

History and architecture · Historic theatres · Viewpoint

Open-air stone theatre built in 1928 in style inspired by classical antiquity, with a large circular stage, colonnade on the sides and staircase on the front; it takes its name from the consul who ruled the Roman colony of Herba. Immersed in the green behind the Monument to the Fallen of the First World War (1928-1931), until the mid-fifties it hosted performances of famous producers and stage designers and is still used today, especially in summer.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Villa Amalia e chiesa di Santa Maria degli Angeli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Neoclassical villa of the early nineteenth century, designed by L. Pollak, built on the convent of the Reformed Fathers: their church, partly incorporated into the villa, preserves a marble pulpit of the fifteenth century and frescoes, including a Crucifixion of students by Bernardino Luini. Parini, Stendhal, Monti and Foscolo stayed there; the park has neoclassical statues and architecture.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Villa Majnoni e Parco Majnoni

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

The 18th century Villa Majnoni, much renovated, is the seat of the Town Hall; the park next to it is a public garden, bound since 1960 for its cedars, beech trees, firs and magnolias.

Villa Comunale di Crevenna (Villa San Giuseppe)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Residenza di fine 18th century built by the Ceriani family, then by the Fountains and a religious institute that gave it the name of Villa San Giuseppe; today it is of the Municipality and houses the Municipal Library, the Civic Museum and various associations, surrounded by a romantic garden.

Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

Established in 1961 and opened in 1964 in Villa Majnoni, since 1977 it has been in the Municipal Villa of Crevenna; re-arranged in 1999, it tells the Alta Brianza and the Lariano Triangle in eight halls back in time, from the modern age to the geological eras. Among the pieces a fresco by Andrea Gentilino (1490), the wooden pulpit by Sant'Eufemia, a longobard spatha with silver-armed handle found in Parravicino in 1961, a Neolithic pile pole from the lake of Pusiano, remains of the cave bear from the lead hole and about 40 species of ammonites from the Turati Alps.
