What to see in Acquaviva Picena

Piceno village dominated by the Rocca degli Acquaviva, with a cylindrical mast of 22 meters from which the look ranges from the Adriatic to the Sibillini; in the historical center the Gothic Gate, the Civic Tower and churches like San Rocco, the oldest in the country, and outside the walls the Franciscan monastery.

Rocca degli Acquaviva (Fortezza medievale)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built by Acquaviva d'Atri around 1300, damaged in 1447 and rebuilt in 1474 by Giovan Francesco Azzolino with works directed by Baccio Pontelli; restored by Giuseppe Sacconi in the late nineteenth century. Irregular quadrilateral plan with corner towers, central courtyard with 22 meters high well and cylindrical mast; it is entirely open to visitors and houses the archaeological museum "La Fortezza nel tempo" (inaugurated in 2016) and the Museum of ancient weapons.

Museo della Pagliarola

Museums · Themed

Inside the fortress, dedicated to the local tradition of straw weave: it preserves baskets, kitchen utensils and straw dolls, wicker and natural materials.

Centro storico, Porta gotica e Palazzo municipale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The historical centre is accessed by the Gothic Door of the fifteenth century, built in the walls; there are the Civic Tower, Casa Rossi Delicate and the Municipal Palace, assaulted and set on fire in 1799 by the brigands of Sciabolone, who destroyed the archive.

  • Where Centro storico, Acquaviva Picena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Convento e chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Outside the village, it is considered the oldest Franciscan monastery of the Marche and according to tradition was founded by Saint Francis; returned to worship in 1989 after a complete restoration. The church has a single nave with two stucco medallions on miraculous events of 1673 and a square cloister with well in the center.
