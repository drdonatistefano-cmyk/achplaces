What to see in Carrara

The city of marble at the foot of the Apuan Islands: the Cathedral of St. Andrew with black and white marble bands, the Academy of Fine Arts in the Palace of Cybo-Malaspina, the museums of marble and Michelangelo and, above the city, the basins of Colonnata and Fantiscritti with quarries to visit.

20 places to visit in Carrara, in the province of Massa-Carrara (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The oldest nucleus of the city, Romanesque with Gothic elements: façade in lunar white marble with bands of black marble of Colonnata with rosette, the wheel of the city's coat of arms and the motto "Fortitudo mea in rota"; bell tower of the fourteenth century 33 meters high, frescoes and sculptures of the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries and pulpit in marble.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Carrara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Piazza Duomo e fontana del Gigante

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The heart of the city in front of the Duomo, with the statue of the Neptune called the Giant, left unfinished by Baccio Bandinelli and mounted in 1563 by Alberico I in a fountain with two dolphins; on the corner with Via Finelli a plaque recalls the stays of Michelangelo came to choose the marble.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Carrara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Palazzo Cybo-Malaspina e Accademia di Belle Arti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Castello dei Malaspina from the twelfth century, strengthened in the thirteenth century and transformed by Alberico I into a Renaissance residence with a new body in the sixteenth century; baroque façade of the seventeenth century and, since 1805, seat of the Academy of Fine Arts; in front of the monument to the Carrara sculptor Pietro Tacca.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Carrara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa del Suffragio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Also known as the Purgatory, where once the walls were finished under the Monterosso hill: interior covered with different marbles (white, peach flower, Medici breach, portor) and marble portal of the workshop of Carlo Finelli.

  • Where Centro storico, Carrara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the sixteenth century in front of the ancient porta a mare of the medieval walls, at the end of Via Santa Maria: on the portal the Madonna and Child of Bartolomeo Ordóñez, inside an altar in polychrome marble with a sixteenth-century canvas of the Virgin of Carmel.

  • Where Via Santa Maria, Carrara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Via Santa Maria

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

One of the oldest streets in the medieval city: at number 14 the house of Emanuele Repetti, where according to tradition he was guest Francesco Petrarca in 1343.

  • Entry Free

Piazza Alberica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Born where the cattle market was held, when in 1557 Alberico I Cybo Malaspina enlarged the walls and moved the political center of the city; in the center the monument to Maria Beatrice d'Este, around palaces and fountain adorned with marble.

  • Where Piazza Alberica, Carrara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Museo Civico del Marmo

Museums · Themed

Opened by the Municipality in 1982: finds of the Roman quarries of Fantiscritti and Gioia, a marble library of 310 samples from all over the world, machinery and tools of quarry from Roman times to today, plaster casts and works of contemporary sculpture.

Cava Museo Fantiscritti

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

In the basin of Fantiscritti, an open-air quarry-museum that tells about two thousand years of marble extraction with techniques, life-size sculptures and the life of the quarrymen, with guided tour of the entire cycle of marble.

Cava Galleria Ravaccione

Nature and landscape · Landscapes

In the marble basins between Ravaccione and Fantiscritti, a tunnel cave dug since Roman times: the large rooms of the marble cathedral, the extraction machinery and the Ozmo mural on Michelangelo's Genesis.

Cava romana di Fossacava

A few kilometers from Colonnata, a large quarry dating back to the first century BC in the form of an amphitheater, with cuts, trenches, quarry marks and extraction plans still visible and signs in Italian and English.

Colonnata

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village of quarrymen about 8 km from the city, born in Roman times among the quarries and famous for the lard aged in marble basins: in front of the church of San Bartolomeo the Monument to the Cavatore by Alberto Sparapani; from here start trails with views of the Apuan and the sea.

  • Where Colonnata, Carrara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it
