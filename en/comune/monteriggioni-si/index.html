What to see in Monteriggioni

The castle founded by Siena between 1213 and 1219, closed by 570 meters of walls with 14 towers, mentioned by Dante; stop of the Via Francigena.

Mura e torri del castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The belt of about 570 meters with 14 towers that closes the village, built by Siena to defend against Florence; Dante compares it to the giants in Hell.

  • Where Monteriggioni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page viafrancigena.visittuscany.com

Sources: viafrancigena.visittuscany.com

Abbadia a Isola

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

The ancient Romanesque abbey a few kilometers from the castle, on the route of the Via Francigena, a traditional place for pilgrims to stop.

  • Where Abbadia a Isola, Monteriggioni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page viafrancigena.visittuscany.com

Sources: viafrancigena.visittuscany.com

Via Francigena da San Gimignano a Monteriggioni

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Stage 32 of the Via Francigena in Tuscany, on foot among hills, woods and villages from San Gimignano to the castle of Monteriggioni.

  • Where Monteriggioni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page viafrancigena.visittuscany.com

Sources: viafrancigena.visittuscany.com
