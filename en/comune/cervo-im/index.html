What to see in Cervo

Medieval village on the sea among the most beautiful villages in Italy, enriched in the sixteenth century by coral fishing: the Baroque church of the Corallines on the churchyard of the chamber music festival and the castle Clavesana with the ethnographic museum.

4 places to visit in Cervo, in the province of Imperia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Cervo in the guide with map and filters

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista (dei Corallini)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Baroque church of 1686-1734 on the square of the Corallini; the terrace church hosts in summer the International Chamber Music Festival, born after the violinist Sándor Végh discovered its acoustics in 1964.

  • Where Piazzetta dei Corallini, Cervo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello Clavesana e Museo etnografico del Ponente ligure

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Castle of the 12th century strengthened by three circular towers; it houses the museum dedicated to work, crafts and sea life of the 19th century and the permanent exhibition on Ligurian woman 1850-1950.

  • Where Piazza Santa Caterina, Cervo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Palazzo Viale e Palazzo Balleydier

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Baroque noble Palaces of the Six and eighteenth century frescoed by Francesco Carrega, along the alleys that descend to the sea.

  • Where Cervo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
