What to see in Medesano

Village of hills south of Parma on the Via Francigena, with prehistoric traces in the territory: in Varano Marchesi the Palace of Pallavicino, the ruins of the castle and the Museum of history and peasant civilization, in Sant'Andrea Bagni the thermal baths with eight different mineral waters.

Museo di storia e civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Inaugurated in June 1999 in a restaurant between the church and the canon of San Giorgio, used between the two wars as a small theatre. A section tells the valley of the Recchio from the Roman to the nineteenth century, with coins of Domitian and Maxentius and the cast of the Jubilee stone of Roccalanzona linked to the first Jubilee of 1300; the exhibition of peasant civilization, from the collection donated by Marco Pometti of Roccalanzona, exhibits on ten ancient keys stele, tools of blacksmiths, carpenters and peasants, tools for wine, wool and hemp, a press for spaghetti and tools of the norcino. Open every day by appointment (tel. OJ L 347, 20.12.2013, p. 671.

  • Where Via Valle, complesso parrocchiale di San Giorgio, Varano Marchesi, Medesano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Palazzo e rovine del castello di Varano Marchesi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the village of Varano Marchesi a noble palace of medieval origin and Renaissance appearance, formerly residence of the Pallavicino; in a distant and dominant position remain the ruins of the castle's castle, probably of thirteenth century origin.

  • Where Varano Marchesi, Medesano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Terme di Sant'Andrea Bagni

Local life and traditions · Spas

On the top of a hill between oak and chestnut woods, the thermal baths collect in one basin eight different mineral waters (bicarbonate-alcalino-terrose, salsobromoiodiche, ferrugina-kalic and sulphur-magnesian), picked up in the rock and brought to the plant with an underground path of almost one kilometer; the properties of the waters of Rio Fabbro were perhaps already known to the Romans and the modern center, with the liberty building, dates back to the late nineteenth century. Thermal pool with vascular and hydromassage routes, inhalation, hydropinic and rehabilitation treatments.

  • Where Sant'Andrea Bagni, Medesano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: italia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
