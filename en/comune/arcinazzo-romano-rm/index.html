What to see in Arcinazzo Romano

Small medieval village at the foot of Monte Altuino, called Ponza until 1891; on the highlands of Arcinazzo, at about 850 meters, you can visit the remains of the hunting villa of Emperor Traiano with the civic archaeological museum.

Villa di Traiano e Museo civico archeologico

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Hunting residence of Traiano (98-117 A.D.) on the highlands at 850 meters, spread over about 5 hectares and largely to be dug: plundered from the late antiquity to the seventeenth century, it is systematically investigated by the Sixties and the excavations of 1999. Of the lower terrace, the only excavated, with garden in the center, you can see the south porch with frescoes on dark background and the triclino with niche nymph, mosaic in glass paste and floor in opus sectile. The museum, in a restored farmhouse inside the area, exhibits excavations and a 3D video reconstruction; Tuesday-Sunday 10-16, Monday closed.

  • Where Via Sublacense km 20, Arcinazzo Romano
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · cultura.gov.it

Centro storico e piazza di Santa Maria Assunta

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village on a karst plateau at the foot of Monte Altuino, with arches of the ancient gates, double windows, bell tower and a watchtower. In the square of the church of Santa Maria Assunta are concentrated the medieval remains, with the tower that is believed to mark the oldest nucleus, today reduced to two sides. The village, called Ponza, was renamed Arcinazzo Romano in 1891 so as not to confuse it with the island.

  • Where Piazza Santa Maria Assunta, Arcinazzo Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Altipiani di Arcinazzo

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Karst plateaus about 900 meters between Monte Altuino and Monte Scalambra, in the Simbruini, with a damp and green side and one of bare slopes; fir woods, beech over 800-1000 meters and chestnut woods on the slopes of the Scalambra.

  • Where Altipiani di Arcinazzo, Arcinazzo Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Marrone e sagra delle castagne

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The village is known for its brown, roasted on the fire in the traditional pan at the festival of late October; in the table polenta with truffle, sheep sauces, goat and sheep cheese and the Cesanese del Piglio DOCG.
