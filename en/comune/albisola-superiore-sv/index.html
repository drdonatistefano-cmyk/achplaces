What to see in Albisola Superiore

Country of ceramics that climbs inland from the coast, with the Roman remains at the station, the medieval nucleus on the Riobasco, the Eugenio Montale promenade and the Manlio Trucco museum.

6 places to visit in Albisola Superiore, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Resti romani e chiesa di San Pietro

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Near the station the remains of the first Roman settlement; not far from the Romanesque church of San Pietro, with the bell tower among cypresses.

  • Where Albisola Superiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Ponte sul Riobasco

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Rest of the medieval village built around the church and the castle away from the sea, with a slender arch over the stream.

  • Where Albisola Superiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Passeggiata Eugenio Montale

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the old railway track, a long sinuous bench in colored stoneware made by international artists for the Biennale di Ceramica in 2001.

  • Where Albisola Superiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo della ceramica Manlio Trucco

Museums · Modern art

From the avant-garde of the twentieth century to the Middle Ages, with archaeological section, futurist works, ceramic deco, furniture and paintings by Manlio Trucco, Albisole's pharmacy vases and in the garden La Battle of Agenore Fabbri of 1948.

  • Where Albisola Superiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
