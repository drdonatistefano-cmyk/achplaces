What to see in Villar Pellice

Municipality of Val Pellice where the Ecomuseum Feltricio Crumiere, in Jervis square, tells the chain of felt with large intact machinery, some still functioning; the Waldensian temple of 1706, wanted by the Huguenot marquis Pierre de Belcastel, has a bell tower with unique pyramid in the Waldensian valleys.

Ecomuseo Feltrificio Crumiere

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Ecomuseum dedicated to the production chain of felt, born around the ancient felt factory: exhibits huge and intact machinery, some still working and sometimes in use, next to an active factory that produces every day. All tours are guided; in winter the museum is closed except for group bookings, while the workshop remains open.

  • Where Piazza Jervis, 10060 Villar Pellice
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Tempio valdese di Villar Pellice

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises on the site of the ancient Catholic church of the village, often destroyed and rebuilt; the current building was built in 1706 thanks to the charity of the French Huguenot marquis Pierre de Belcastel, and its pyramid bell tower, built together with the temple, is unique in the Waldensian valleys. The interior with three naves, with barrel vault without decorations, is illuminated by seven windows and a rose window; the door and its key are from the beginning of the eighteenth century, while the wrought iron chandeliers were donated by the Waldensian Church of Milan after the demolition of the Romanesque church of San Giovanni in Conca. On the frieze of the portal the verse "Come, let us worship the Lord" from Psalm 95.

  • Where Villar Pellice
