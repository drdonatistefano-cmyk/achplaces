What to see in Pomarance

Medieval village in the heart of the Val di Cecina, with Larderello, where for the first time in the world electricity from geothermal energy has been produced.

4 places to visit in Pomarance, in the province of Pisa (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Museo della Geotermia di Larderello

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

In the village Enel Green Power, the museum renovated on the history of geothermal energy, from the boric acid of 1777 to the first bulbs of 1904, with 3D room and a visit to the demonstration shower.

  • Where Piazza Leopolda 2, Larderello, Pomarance
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.visitgeotermia.com

Sources: visitgeotermia.com · visittuscany.com

Valle del Diavolo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Showers boraciferi, fumarole, lagoons and geysers around Larderello, a landscape that according to tradition inspired the Inferno di Dante.

  • Where Larderello, Pomarance
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Centro storico e San Giovanni Battista

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center with three doors, Piazza San Dalmatio, the house Mugnaini-Ricci of the twelfth century, the 14th century municipal palace and the church with works of the Pomarancio.

  • Where Pomarance
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Rocca Sillana

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The fortification on a hill near Pomarance from which you could see the whole Val di Cecina to the sea.

  • Where Pomarance
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
