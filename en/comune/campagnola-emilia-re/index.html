What to see in Campagnola Emilia

Agricultural village of the lower Reggiana, belonged to the Canossa, the monastery of Frassinoro and Da Correggio, built from 1621 at the behest of Siro d'Austria and known for its ciccios, vinegars, Lambrusco and Parmigiano-Reggiano: the sixteenth-century square Rome, the Romanesque church of Sant'Andrea, the remains of the abbey of the Holy Trinity and the court of San Bernardino.

Piazza Roma e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Piazza that preserves the regular sixteenth-century plant: to the north the parish church of Santi Protasio and Gervasio, on the sides two rows of similar houses with porticos, to the west the space of Villa Schiattarini then Conti-Cottafavi and in front of the church the Town Hall, building of the seventeenth century several times modified.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Campagnola Emilia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitemilia.com

Chiesa di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church about 1.5 km from the centre, with frescoes from the fifteenth century.

  • Where A 1,5 km dal centro, Campagnola Emilia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitemilia.com

Abbazia della Santissima Trinità

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Abbey founded in the 12th century to the north-east of the village, then one of the most important of the Reggiano territory; it remains a small church obtained from the right aisle of the original building, inserted in a rural courtyard.

  • Where A nord-est del paese, Campagnola Emilia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitemilia.com

Corte di San Bernardino (Casa Folloni)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Court documented already in the twelfth century as Villa dei Reatini, which hosted the Franciscan preacher Saint Bernardino da Siena; the palace of Matildica period, renovated in the fifteenth century, includes the manor house to the west, a dovecote tower to the north and a fifteenth-century oratory dedicated to the saint.

  • Where Frazione San Bernardino, Campagnola Emilia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitemilia.com

Museo dei trattori e macchine d'epoca

Local life and traditions · Motors

Permanent exposure of locomobili and tractors Landini with hot head, motorbikes of the thirties, forty and fifty and four-stroke mopeds, restored according to the original tradition; stands out the series of Slanzi engines. Visits on request (tel. 0522 669858).

  • Where Via Don Milani 10, 42012 Campagnola Emilia (RE)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
