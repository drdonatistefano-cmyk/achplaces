What to see in Buccinasco

Municipality to the south-west of Milan for more than half in the South Milan Agricultural Park, between fountains, farmsteads and lakes of quarry; in the agricultural village of Buccinasco Castello rises the Castle Visconteo, with traces fourteenth-century and loggia of the sixteenth century, and in Viale Lombardia the Foundation of the painter William Congdon.

Castello Visconteo di Buccinasco Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

It gives its name to the agricultural village of Buccinasco Castello, in the countryside of the South Milan Agricultural Park just outside the West Tangenziale. It is a single block with a square plan on two floors, brick exposed with the front entrance plastered; on the opposite side a portico of five spans on the ground floor and a loggia of ten spans on the first floor. There are traces of walls of the fourteenth century and the loggia is of the sixteenth century; it is among the oldest buildings of the municipality and appears in the municipal coat of arms.

Chiesetta della Beata Vergine Assunta (San Michele)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A few meters from the castle, it is already mentioned in the chronicles of Goffredo da Bussero of the late thirteenth century, when Buccinasco had a church dedicated to San Michele; it depends on the church of Romano Banco and is owned by the counts Brivio Sforza. It preserves a Crucifixion frescoed in 1480, attributed to Zanetto Bugatto, and a subsequent fresco by anonymous author with the saints Agata, Barbara and Apollonia, probably ex-vote to protect the crops.

The William G. Congdon Foundation

Museums · Modern art

Established on October 2, 1980 by the will of the painter William Congdon, he protects the corpus of his figurative and literary work, he manages an archive, a library and the William G Collection. Congdon Foundation is promoting research on contemporary art and organizing exhibitions, debates and the regest of the artist's omnia work.

Parco Spina Azzurra

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Park in the north-east of the municipality along three lakes born from former sand and gravel quarries, the Pond of the Salici, the Pioppi and the Fishermen, which the Municipality defines an oasis of high natural value.

Fontanili e campagna del Parco Agricolo Sud Milano

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The South Milan Agricultural Park protects about 6.3 km2, more than half of the municipal territory: the whole area south of the Tangenziale is agricultural, dotted with farmsteads and crossed by rogge. Buccinasco is entirely in the area of the fountains and is among the municipalities of the province with several active fountains (Bazzanella, Carchena, Nosotto, Molinetto, Campociocco, Melzi, Marozzi, Visconti and the wet area of the Castle); south of the Tangenziale there is also Lake Santa Maria, excavated in 1966 for the West Tangenziale and stop of migratory birds.

  • Where Buccinasco Castello e Gudo Gambaredo, Buccinasco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.buccinasco.mi.it

Sources: comune.buccinasco.mi.it
