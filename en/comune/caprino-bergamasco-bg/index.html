What to see in Caprino Bergamasco

Ancient capital of the San Martino Valley, about 20 km from Bergamo, with a historic center of palaces, noble houses and gardens, the eighteenth-century parish church of San Biagio and, in the hamlet of Celana, the church with the altarpiece of the Assumption of Lorenzo Lotto and the Borromaic College with the naturalistic museum of Don Bernardino Gavazzeni.

Museo di storia naturale Don Bernardino Gavazzeni

Museums · Science and university

Naturalistic collection of the Collegio di Celana, constituted at the end of the nineteenth century by Don Bernardino Gavazzeni, a science teacher, who preserves the original nineteenth-century setting with furniture, showcases and handwritten captions and important finds of the valley. The College was wanted by San Carlo Borromeo in 1578 as a seminary and became a college after the suppression of 1798, hosting gymnasium and high school.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta di Celana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the hamlet of Celana, next to the Borromaic College, which houses an important altarpiece of the Assumption of Lorenzo Lotto; the complex was opened on the FAI Spring Days of 2021.

Centro storico, Palazzo Sozzi e torre medievale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center preserves historic buildings, noble houses and gardens: between the eighteenth and nineteenth century nobles and bourgeois built palaces with gardens of delight and decor. The eighteenth-century Palazzo Sozzi (the Counts Sozzi also built the Filatoio spinning mill, second of the province) and a tower of medieval origin; the town recalls the historic Caffè Pagani, the "salotto buono" of the village frequented by Giacomo Puccini and Neera writer.

  • Where Centro storico, Caprino Bergamasco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
