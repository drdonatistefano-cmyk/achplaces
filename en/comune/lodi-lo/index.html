What to see in Lodi

Città d'arte sull'Adda: the great Victoria Square with the Duomo and the Broletto, the Civic Temple of the crowned masterpiece of the Lombard Renaissance, the church of San Francesco and a Liberty path in the center.

14 places to visit in Lodi, in the province of Lodi (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Tempio Civico dell'Incoronata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

An octagonal building rich in Renaissance masterpieces, supervised by sixteen 'testoni', according to tradition it was born after in 1487 the image of the Virgin cried during a fight.

  • Where Via Incoronata 23, Lodi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Museo del Tesoro dell'Incoronata
