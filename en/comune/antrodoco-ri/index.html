What to see in Antrodoco

Village along the Salaria at the entrance of the Velino gorges: the church of Santa Maria extra Moenia with the hexagonal baptistery frescoed, the sanctuary of the Madonna delle Grotte, the military historical museum and the ruins of the castle.

6 places to visit in Antrodoco, in the province of Rieti (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santa Maria extra Moenia e Battistero di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Medieval church, national monument, built on the remains of a pagan temple and mentioned already in the Dialogues of St. Gregory the Great; in the apse the Blessing Redeemer, in the hexagonal baptistery frescoes of the late fourteenth century with stories of the Baptist and the Universal Judgment; on June 21 a ray of sun from a window marks the summer solstice.

  • Where Via della Stazione, Antrodoco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · comune.antrodoco.ri.it

Museo storico militare

Museums · City history

The pride of Antrodoco: over 300 head covers and relics of the world and colonial wars, with weapons, uniforms, bombs and a fully equipped base camp.

  • Where Antrodoco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Museo della città Lin Delija – Carlo Cesi

Museums · Modern art

The civic museum is dedicated to two painters linked to the territory: Carlo Cesi, 17th century engraver of Pietro da Cortona, and Lin Delija, painter of the twentieth century born in Albania and established in Antrodoco; it also hosts cultural events.

  • Where Corso Roma 9, Antrodoco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Santuario della Madonna delle Grotte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the gorges of Antrodoco, built at the beginning of the seventeenth century after the discovery of an image of the Virgin by a shepherd in 1601: baroque portal, covered with wooden trusses and in the crypt the remains of the French soldiers died in the motions of 1799.

  • Where Gole di Antrodoco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Ruderi del castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The castle remains an old ruin that dominates the village from above, panoramic point; Frederick II resumed it in 1226 after a siege, Queen Joan I sold it to the Aquilans in 1382 for 11,000 florins and in 1529 Charles V granted it to Gian Battista Savelli.

  • Where Antrodoco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: comune.antrodoco.ri.it · parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com

Palazzo Pallini e il mistrà

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Palace of 1875 in Piazza del Popolo with residence, distillery and shop with liberty friezes; the distillery produces the mistrà, dry distillate of anise and fennel.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Antrodoco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
