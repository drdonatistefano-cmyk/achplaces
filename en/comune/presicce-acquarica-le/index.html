What to see in Presicce-Acquarica

The town of Capo di Leuca was born from the union of Presicce and Acquarica del Capo: in Presicce a Doge's Palace on Piazza del Popolo, the late Baroque church of Sant'Andrea, noble palaces from the sixteenth to the nineteenth century and an underground network of 23 oil mills; in Acquarica the remains of the medieval castle and the Masseria di Celsorizzo with the Norman tower.

Frantoi ipogei

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry

Prescce is the country with the largest number of cave-trappeti, mills carved in stone: 23 are inserted in the underground network of the country and still store presses and millstones. They open on Piazza del Popolo, where until the beginning of the twentieth century the life of the oil mills took place, so much so that we talk about a village hidden under the square. The Touring indicates opening on request and free entrance.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo / via Gramsci, Presicce
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Palazzo Ducale e piazza del Popolo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The Doge's Palace overlooks Piazza del Popolo: renovated at the beginning of the 20th century, it preserves the chapel of the Annunziata and a hanging garden built with the reconstruction of 1630.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Presicce
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Museums · Themed

Chiesa matrice di Sant'Andrea e colonna votiva

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The church of the late Baroque patron, built between 1778 and 1781, overlooks Piazzetta Villani: a Latin cross plan, a single nave with eight side altars, a major altar in polychrome marble of Neapolitan school and aquasantiere donated by King Francesco I of the Two Sicilies. It preserves the ...Mosè saved from the waters of Oronzo Tiso, the "Martyrdom of S. Andrea's Gian Domenico Catalano and the "Sacrificio d'Isacco" by Liborio Riccio. In front, the votive column with the statue of St. Andrew, placed by the Bartilotti in the early eighteenth century.

  • Where Piazzetta Villani, Presicce
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa della Madonna del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the former convent of the Carmelite Fathers of Via Roma, today seat of the municipal offices; the main altar is carved in Lecce stone in late Baroque style, with three paintings by Saverio Lillo.

  • Where Via Roma, Presicce
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Centro storico e palazzi nobiliari

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

An itinerary between historical residences: Palazzo Valentini and Casa Turrita in via Gramsci (mid 16th century), the Renaissance Palazzo Adamo Izzo with garden in via Vespucci, Palazzo Colella Bisanti on Piazzetta Villani with dovecote of 1563 enlarged in 1770, the Corte Soronzi of the seventeenth century, Palazzo Villani of the eighteenth century, Palazzo Arditi of the late seventeenth century, the sixteenth-century houses Cesi and Rollo (with garden hidden at the bottom of a long androne) and the neo-Gothic castle of Corso Italia, transformed in 1924 by an eighteenth-century structure.

  • Where Piazzetta Villani e vie Gramsci, Vespucci, Corso Italia, Presicce
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Masseria di Celsorizzo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

At the north-western edge of the town of Acquarica del Capo, it preserves the remains of a Norman tower, an ancient crypt and the little church of St. Mary of Oils, very remodeled.
