What to see in Giarre

City of the Ionian coast at the foot of Etna, between Catania and Riposto, with a historic centre of lava stone streets and eighteenth-century buildings around the Neoclassical Cathedral of Sant'Isidoro Agricola; in Macchia the ethnographic museum of the people of Etna and in the city the museum of the crib.

6 places to visit in Giarre, in the province of Catania (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico tra via Callipoli e corso Italia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The intersection between Via Callipoli and Corso Italia is the heart of the historic center, with paved streets in lava stone and 18th century buildings; the center has lively squares, baroque churches and noble palaces.

  • Where Via Callipoli angolo corso Italia, Giarre
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo degli usi e costumi delle genti dell'Etna

Museums · Themed

Ethnographic Museum opened in 1995 in Macchia di Giarre to preserve the memory of rural life: five large rooms reconstruct a farmhouse and a peasant house of the last century, with agricultural tools, a spindle and a cone of 1750, a frame, traditional clothes (tailories, wedding dresses, party and Sunday), embroidery and ancient daguerrotypes. Designed as a museum, it hosts activities that revive customs related to popular festivals; guided tours available.

  • Where Via Emanuele Filiberto, Macchia di Giarre
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo del presepio

Museums · Themed

Born in 1994 on the initiative of the Italian Association of Friends of the Nativity of Giarre, in a noble palace of the nineteenth century, he exhibits about two hundred nativity scenes in Lecce rags, Sicilian and Apulian terracotta, Neapolitan figures and wood of Trentino pine, as well as dioramas on the Sicilian peasant life. Open from 8 December to 6 January every day 16-19.30; in the rest of the year Wednesday, Sunday and holidays 17-19; closed in July and August.

  • Where Via Meli, Giarre
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
