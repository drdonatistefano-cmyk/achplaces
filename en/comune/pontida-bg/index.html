What to see in Pontida

The Bergamo Country of the San Martino Valley, between woods and mountains Treasure and Ocone, linked to the oath of the Lombard League of 1167, which tradition places in the abbey of San Giacomo founded in 1079 by Saint Alberto da Prezzate: basilica, cloisters and museum of the monastery, paths between chestnut trees towards Monte Canto and the first social cellar of the province of Bergamo.

Abbazia di San Giacomo e Museo del Monastero

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded in 1079 by Saint Albert of Prezzate, who returned from the pilgrimage to Santiago donated some of his assets to Cluny and placed the monastery under Abbot Ugo, expanding a church of Saints James, Bassano and Nicola. Here, on April 7, 1167, tradition places the oath of the Lombard League against Federico Barbarossa. The visit includes the fourteenth-century basilica, of Gothic style inside, with Romanesque capitals, frescoes in part of the Marinoni family and medieval Lombard sculptures, including bas-reliefs of the ark of Saint Albert; bell tower and statue of the saint are Renaissance. The complex is completed by the two cloisters, the Renaissance sacristy, the capitular hall and the museum, with paintings and ancient objects used by monks.

Sentiero da Pontida al Monte Canto

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Monte Canto is an orphan mountain, separated from the Bergamo Alps from the valley of San Martino and the first height that is encountered by climbing from the plain on the eastern bank of the Adda. The Pontis stretch of the route to Sotto il Monte (paths 897 and 891, locality Il Canto) starts from the cemetery, runs along the Vallette with their streams and rises among woods of chestnuts, ash trees and wild cherry trees, passing through the sixteenth-century farmhouse of San Bartolomeo with its large bell tower and the church of Santa Barbara, the only surviving building of an ancient village on a plateau. The whole route measures 8.1 km, 2 h 45 min, +340 m, difficulty And with some steep stretches.

Cantina Val San Martino

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Founded in 1957, it is indicated as the first winery born in the whole province of Bergamo; it has vineyards in Almenno, Pontida, Palazzago, Mapello and Scanzo, cultivated mainly in Merlot, Cabernet, Manzoni and Chardonnay. It produces red Valcalepio and IGT San Martino, a Blanc de Blancs of Chardonnay only with charmat method in autoclave and, since the end of 2021, a red from Merlot vineyards with more than 25 years. For visits and tastings contact the cellar (tel. 035 795035).
