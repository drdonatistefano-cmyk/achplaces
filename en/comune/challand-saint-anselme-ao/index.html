What to see in Challand-Saint-Anselme

Ancient crossroads of the Val d'Ayas at the foot of the Corno Bussola, between ancient irrigation canals, a restored mill and the sanctuary of Sant'Anna.

3 places to visit in Challand-Saint-Anselme, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Anselmo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Consecrated in 1762, it preserves an eighteenth-century tabernacle carved and gilded by a Valsesian artist and furnishings of the 16th-XVIII centuries.

  • Where Quinçod, Challand-Saint-Anselme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Mulino di Ruvère

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the river Evançon at the entrance of the valley of Chasten, restored; it ground cereals and produced walnut oil.

  • Where Ruvère, Challand-Saint-Anselme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Santuario di Sant'Anna

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Neogothic, rebuilt at the end of the nineteenth century on the remains of a seventeenth century chapel; feast on July 26.

  • Where Challand-Saint-Anselme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it
