What to see in Aquileia

Roman colony founded in 181 BC, among the major cities of the Empire and Mediterranean gate, destroyed by Attila in 452: UNESCO heritage with the patriarchal Basilica and the largest early Christian mosaic of the West, the river port and national museums.

9 places to visit in Aquileia, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Aquileia in the guide with map and filters

Basilica patriarcale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

It rises on the classrooms founded in 313 by Bishop Teodoro, whose mosaic floor in four bays, with the Good Shepherd, the winged Victory and the history of Jonah, is the largest of the Christian West; the present building is of the 11th century, restored by Patriarch Poppon in 1031 and renovated in the fourteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Capitolo, Aquileia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · whc.unesco.org

Cripta degli scavi

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Ancient hall of the cult complex, with mosaics around the foundations of the bell tower and the panel of the fight between rooster and turtle.

  • Where Piazza Capitolo, Aquileia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website whc.unesco.org
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it · whc.unesco.org · basilicadiaquileia.it

Campanile della Basilica

History and architecture · Towers · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built by Poppone, more than 70 meters high in marble blocks of the ancient amphitheatre; from the top the view of the city and the plain, usually between April and September.

  • Where Piazza Capitolo, Aquileia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it · whc.unesco.org

Battistero, Palazzo episcopale e Südhalle

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

You can visit them with a single ticket; the mosaic floor of the Südhalle, from the IV-V century, is protected by a special structure.

  • Where Piazza Capitolo, Aquileia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website whc.unesco.org
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: italia.it · whc.unesco.org · basilicadiaquileia.it

Area archeologica: foro, porto fluviale e mercati

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Roman open-air city near the centre: the remains of the forum with the civil basilica, the river port with the long row of warehouses and docks embedded in the walls of the fourth century and the late antique markets.

  • Where Aquileia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: italia.it · whc.unesco.org

Domus di Tito Macro

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

In the bottom Cossar, one of the largest Roman mansions discovered in northern Italy.

  • Entry To be checked

Sepolcreto romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Five funerary enclosures along a secondary road south-east of the walls, with decorated balustrades, replicas of the monuments of the Archaeological Museum and limestone sarcophagi raised for the layer.

  • Where Via XXIV Maggio, Aquileia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: italia.it · whc.unesco.org

Museo Archeologico Nazionale

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Opened in 1882 in the neoclassical villa Cassis Faraone, re-assembled in 2018 on the Roman city: mosaics such as the unsaved floor, the Pudica Venus, portraits and the Giulio-Claudio statuary cycle, with stone galleries, historical garden and deposits open to visitors with over 5,000 artifacts.

  • Where Via Roma 1, Aquileia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website whc.unesco.org
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismofvg.it · whc.unesco.org · museoarcheologicoaquileia.beniculturali.it

Museo Paleocristiano

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

On the remains of a early Christian basilica, then church of a female Benedictine monastery: the mosaic floor in situ, mosaics of a second basilica and a collection of funerary stems of the IV-V century, visible also from above.

  • Where Piazza Pirano 1, Aquileia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it · whc.unesco.org
