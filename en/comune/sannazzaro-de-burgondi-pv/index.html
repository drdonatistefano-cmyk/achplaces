What to see in Sannazzaro de' Burgondi

City of Lomellina on the coast overlooking the Po valley, feud of the Malaspinas from 1466 to 1835; to see the gate and the sanctuary of the Madonna della Fontana, erected after the flood of the Po of 1705, the parish church of Saints Nazzaro and Celso and the church of Saints Bernardino and Rocco.

Santuario della Madonna della Fontana

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Started in 1710 and completed in 1786 after the collapse, in the middle of the Po of 1705, of the Marian church downstream, from which only the image of the Virgin was saved; tradition links the place to the water miraculously sprung from the rock. The neoclassical façade has a pediment on Ionic semi-colons and a modern mosaic of the Virgin, preceded by a shaded garden and a fountain; inside, with a single hall, four eighteenth-century altarpieces and a major altar Rococo in polychrome marble with a fresco of the Virgin perhaps of the sixteenth century, very repainted. The Marquis Luigi Malaspina is buried in the church.

Il Portone

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Monumental arch at the entrance of the village erected by the inhabitants in 1724 in memory of the flood of the Po of 1705, which devastated the fields, collapsed farms and houses and moved the river bed; the epigraph recalls the event and the construction of the church of the Blessed Virgin of the Fountain to protect the country. In the same year it was connected to the sanctuary by a tree-lined avenue, the alley, today Viale Italia. The monument has been the subject of a conservative restoration.

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Nazzaro e Celso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Late medieval church (XV-XVI century) that houses the relics of the saints owners, remodelled in baroque forms in the Six and eighteenth centuries and brought back in 1933 to the original appearance by eliminating the baroque additions. The hut façade has horizontal bands white and black, rosette and pinnacles in terracotta; inside marble panels, golden mosaics and frescoes in Gothic style between neo-Gothic and neo-Romanesque. The baroque altar of purging souls remains, two fifteenth-century wooden statues of the patrons on the sides of a great Crucifix co-eval and the high altar surmounted by silver saints and bishops.

Chiesa dei Santi Bernardino e Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Already existing in the seventeenth century and seat of the Confraternita dei Disciplinati, it was renewed in 1782 at the expense of Marquis Luigi Malaspina. The exposed brick façade has a neoclassical pediment and a sundial from 1848 of Stura di Moncalvo, restored, with the motto The interior with a single nave, with pilasters in fake marble and ionic capitals in stucco, is frescoed by the saintly painter Paolo Maggi, author of the altarpieces of the Adoration of the Magi and the Virgin with Saints Rocco and Sebastiano; there is also a Lingiardi organ.

Palazzo Malaspina (il «castello»)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

It was identified with the castle, a fortified residence built overhanging the coast in a defensive position and present in the Torino cadastre of 1759-1760; it is believed that the Marquis Luigi Malaspina has redone it at the end of the eighteenth century giving it its current appearance. The Marquis, who spent the autumn holiday in it, accommodating poets and Lombards, provided it with an English garden with waterfall, temple, pond and a cenotaph dedicated to the poet Sannazzaro, and a private theater. It is private property: it can be seen from the outside.
