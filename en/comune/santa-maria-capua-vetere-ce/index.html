What to see in Santa Maria Capua Vetere

The ancient Capua, one of the most powerful cities of the peninsula from the Osci to the Middle Ages: the Amphitheatre of Campania, second in size to the Colosseum, the Frescoed Mitreus and the museum of the ancient Capua.

4 places to visit in Santa Maria Capua Vetere, in the province of Caserta (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Anfiteatro campano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The amphitheatre of the ancient Capua, of which the giant arches remain; for dimensions second only to the Colosseum. The ticket to the archaeological circuit also includes the Mitreo.

  • Where Santa Maria Capua Vetere
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Mitreo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Underground temple of the end of the first century A.D., among the oldest places of mitraic worship in the West: a great parietal painting with the sacrifice of the bull and the starry vault; discovered in 1922.

Visits only by reservation, to groups of five

  • Where Santa Maria Capua Vetere
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo archeologico nazionale dell'antica Capua

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In a barracks of the mid-nineteenth century that incorporates the tower of Sant'Erasmo, where in 1278 Roberto d'Angiò was born, above the Capitolium del Foro: finds of the excavations of necropolis, sacred areas and amphitheatre and the exhibition "I Segni del Paesaggio: L'Appia e Capua" .

From 2 February 2026 temporarily closed for works

  • Where Via Roberto d'Angiò 44, Santa Maria Capua Vetere
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The cathedral that gave the name to the modern city.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
