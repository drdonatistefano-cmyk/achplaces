What to see in Borgo San Dalmazzo

Città all'incocco delle valli cuneesi, born around the Benedictine abbey of San Dalmazzo di Pedona, today museum with Romanesque crypt and archaeological area; in the center the sixteenth-century Civic Tower and the Arch of San Rocco, next to the station the Memorial of the Deportation with the MEMO4345 route and, on the hill, the seventeenth-century Sanctuary of Monserrato. At the beginning of December, the Cold Fair is dedicated to snails.

Museo dell'Abbazia di San Dalmazzo di Pedona

Museums · Archaeological

The parish church of San Dalmazzo, whose current structure dates back to the 11th century, is an important place of worship of the Maritime Alps; the path, in three sections (museum, archaeological and parish), exhibits excavation finds and panels on the evolution of the building, with remains of Roman times and overlapping churches, and ends in the Romanesque crypt with elements of Lombard taste. Open on Sunday afternoon (June-October 15-19, November-May 14-18) with guided tours at every hour; better check it out first.

  • Where Via Ospedale 2, Borgo San Dalmazzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Memoriale della Deportazione

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Next to the station, the names of the deportees are engraved on slabs on the ground in front of three railway cars, those of the few survivors on vertical plates. In September 1943, some 800 Jewish refugees who had crossed the French border and the Gesso Valley were stopped by the SS and interned in the former barracks of the Alps, then deported to Auschwitz; in 2001 the city received the Gold Medal to the Civil Merit. Free visit.

  • Where Presso la stazione ferroviaria, Borgo San Dalmazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

MEMO4345

Museums · City history

Historical and educational multimedia route on the Shoah in Europe set up in the former church of Sant'Anna, near the Memorial, which tells the story of the Jews interned and deported from Borgo San Dalmazzo.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Santuario della Madonna di Monserrato

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

A 17th-century shrine dedicated to the Black Madonna of Monserrato, the city's companion, surrounded by four wings of porticos and with a fresco of the Crucifixion on the façade; from the churchyard you can see the plain of Cuneo and the Alps. Walk up from the centre in about 2 km along a Via Crucis of thirteen nineteenth-century pylons; in December and January it hosts the exhibition "Presepi di Monserrato" .

  • Where Via Regione Monserrato, Borgo San Dalmazzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Torre Civica e Arco di San Rocco

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the historical centre, the 16th century Civic Tower gives access to Piazza 4 Novembre, where every Thursday the traditional market is held; the Arch of San Rocco, of the second half of the 16th century, is the only survivor of the four medieval gates and carries the original coat of arms of the municipality with San Dalmazzo on horseback as a thebrew soldier.

  • Where Piazza 4 Novembre, Borgo San Dalmazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
