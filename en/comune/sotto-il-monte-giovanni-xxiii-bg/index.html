What to see in Sotto il Monte Giovanni XXIII

Birthplace of Angelo Giuseppe Roncalli, Pope John XXIII, at the foot of Monte Canto on the Bergamasca Island: a "santuario diffuso" that includes the birthplace, Ca' Maitino, the Sanctuary of St. John XXIII and the church of Santa Maria di Brusicco, with in addition the Romanesque abbey of Sant'Egidio a Fontanella.

6 places to visit in Sotto il Monte Giovanni XXIII, in the province of Bergamo (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Casa natale di Papa Giovanni XXIII

Museums · House museums

In the farmhouse called Cascina Palazzo was born on 25 November 1881 Angelo Giuseppe Roncalli, pope from 1958 to 1963 and saint from 2014. You can visit the arched porch, the wooden staircase that goes up to the simple rooms, the bedroom of birth with a double bed, drawer-desk and a painting of the Virgin, and a small museum of memories. Not accessible to people with motor disabilities.

  • Where Via Colombera 5, Sotto il Monte Giovanni XXIII
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: visitbergamo.net · in-lombardia.it

Ca' Maitino, Casa dei ricordi di Papa Giovanni XXIII

Museums · House museums

15th-century residence built for Maytinus Roncalli, ancestor of the pope, who chose it as summer residence until shortly before the election; on the façade a sundial with the motto "Errantes revocotat" and inside fifteenth-century frescoes with the coat of arms of the Maytini, then became papal coat of arms. Today it is a museum with relics: part of the library, gifts, the typewriter and the world map of the papal study, the bed in which he died and the altar of the private chapel, works of Manzù, Scorzelli, Bodini, Manfredi and German Nani and an Opel Kapitan of 1956. Open on Saturday and Sunday, 9-12 and 14-18.

  • Where Via Ca' Maitino 1, Sotto il Monte Giovanni XXIII
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · in-lombardia.it

Santuario San Giovanni XXIII

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Officially recognized as a shrine in October 2014, immediately after the canonization, it brings together the parish church, the Chapel of Peace (Maria Regina della Pace, with an altar coming from the ancient Basilica of St. Peter and a polychrome inlay of the pope), the Garden of Peace and the Crypt "Oboedientia et Pax" under the church, where a large shrine holds the cast of the pope's face and right hand, the one that signed the "Pacem in Terris" (Pacem in Terris) made by Giacomo Manzù. Open every day 8-12.30 and 14.30-18, Saturday and Sunday with continuous hours; the nearby Casa del Pellegrino offers welcome and guided tours for groups.

Chiesa di Santa Maria di Brusicco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the church of Brusicco Angelo Roncalli received the baptism and the first communion and celebrated his first Mass as a priest; it is part of the common shrine of the country. Open every day 8-18.

  • Where Brusicco, Sotto il Monte Giovanni XXIII
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · fondoambiente.it

Abbazia di Sant'Egidio in Fontanella

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Romanesque complex founded in the 11th century by Alberto da Prezzate (in Lombardy it dates to the 10th century) on the slopes of Monte Canto, with a wide view of the plain; it preserves frescoes, partly attributed to the Baschenis, and the sarcophagus of Teoperga. After the suppression the monastery was reborn in the twentieth century thanks to Father David Maria Turoldo. Guided tours by Lombardy Secret, by reservation.

  • Where Via Fontanella, Sotto il Monte Giovanni XXIII
  • Entry Guided tour €13
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: visitbergamo.net · in-lombardia.it

Sentiero da Pontida a Sotto il Monte per il Monte Canto

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

8,1 km (2 hours and 45, 340 m ascent, medium difficulty with steep sections) from Pontida cemetery to Sanctuary St. John XXIII, recommended from spring to autumn. In the territory of Sotto il Monte touches the Abbey of Sant'Egidio, the Torre San Giovanni, a panoramic point on Sotto il Monte and Brianza, the Via degli Alpini, a restored historical staircase that descends to Ca' Maitino, and the pope's birthplace.

  • Where Arrivo al Santuario San Giovanni XXIII, Sotto il Monte Giovanni XXIII
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
