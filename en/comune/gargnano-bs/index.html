What to see in Gargnano

Land of the upper Garda Brescia gathered around the port, with the municipal palace of the end of the sixteenth century that preserves in the lake wall the balls of cannon fired by an Austrian ship in 1866, the church of San Giacomo of the twelfth century, Villa Feltrinelli, Villa Bettoni in Bogliaco and the lemon trees, cultivated here since the thirteenth century.

Porto e palazzo municipale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The center is built around the port, on which elegant buildings overlook, including the municipal palace of the late sixteenth century. On the side towards the lake are walled the balls of cannon fired against the country by an Austrian ship in 1866, during the Third War of Independence.

  • Where Via della Libertà, Gargnano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest church in Gargnano, dating back to the 12th century and recently restored. On an external side wall there are three frescoes from the fourteenth century; inside, in a niche above a terracotta altar, a sixteenth-century wooden statue of San Giacomo.

  • Where Gargnano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Villa Bettoni

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

The eighteenth-century residence of the Counts Bettoni in Bogliaco, called "small Versailles" and registered in the Register of Historic Houses of Excellence; there were guests Puccini, Victor Hugo, Garibaldi and Napoleon. The Italian garden, beyond the state road and connected to the house by two raised walkways, has stairs, three fountains, an artificial cave, remains of a lemon house and sculptures by Gian Battista Locatelli. Guided tours only in spring and summer, by online reservation, conducted by a family member.

  • Where Via della Libertà 77, Bogliaco, Gargnano
  • Entry €13, under 12 €8, 0-4 years free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitbrescia.it · italia.it

Villa Feltrinelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built at the end of the 19th century, during the Republic of Salò, he hosted Mussolini's offices, who chose another villa further north, in San Faustino, as his personal residence.

Limonaie di Gargnano

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

The cultivation of lemons in Gargnano dates back to the 13th century. Some lemonias are open to the public: La Malora, the oldest one, which today produces liqueurs, gelatins and mustards, the Lemonaia Trevisani and Gandossi.

  • Entry To be checked

Lago di Valvestino

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small artificial lake in the mountains, in the Gardesana Western nature reserve and in the Alto Garda Regional Park. When the water level drops, the ruins of the Customs of Lignago emerge, which once separated the Austrian Empire from Italy.

  • Where Lago di Valvestino, Gargnano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
