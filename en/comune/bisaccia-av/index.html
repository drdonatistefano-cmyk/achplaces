What to see in Bisaccia

The centre of Alta Irpinia of Lombard origin, collected around the ducal castle, which was the residence of Giovan Battista Manso, friend of Torquato Tasso, and today houses the Civic Archaeological Museum with the Iron Age equipment of the hill of Cemetery Vecchio.

Castello ducale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortified building with thick walls and a 12-metre tower; the paved courtyard ends in a Renaissance loggia with a wide view of the countryside. Perhaps of the ninth century, it was used by Frederick II as a hunting lodge and meeting place of the poets of the Sicilian School; in the sixteenth century there held dinners the humanist Giovan Battista Manso, with Torquato Tasso host, and in the eighteenth century became residence of the Duke Ascanio Pignatelli.

Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

On the ground floor of the ducal castle, in three rooms, exhibits more than 800 state-owned finds from 50 tombs of the first and second Iron Age (late IX-VII century BC) carved on the hill of Cemetery Old, especially ceramics and personal ornaments; a 3D video reconstructs the protohistoric settlement. Open from Tuesday to Sunday, 11-13 and 17-19.
