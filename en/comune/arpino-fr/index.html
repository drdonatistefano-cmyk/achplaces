What to see in Arpino

The city of Cicero on the hills of the Liri Valley, Orange Flag: the acropolis of Civitavecchia with polygonal walls and the arch with sharp sixth, the Tower of Cicero and the museums of violin, wool and printing.

8 places to visit in Arpino, in the province of Frosinone (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Arpino in the guide with map and filters

Acropoli di Civitavecchia e mura poligonali

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Acropolis Volsca with polygonal walls of the VIII-VII BC centuries, among the best preserved in pre-Roman Italy, and a rare arch with acute sixth in stone.

  • Where Località Civitavecchia, Arpino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Torre di Cicerone

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Medieval tower of the XI-XII centuries, 30 meters high, with square plan of about 16 meters on the side, built directly on the blocks of the cyclopean walls; from the top the valley of Liri and, on clear days, the Apennines.

  • Where Via San Francesco, località Civitavecchia, Arpino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
