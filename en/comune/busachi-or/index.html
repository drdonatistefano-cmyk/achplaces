What to see in Busachi

Barigadu village about 430 meters on the slope overlooking the Tirso valley, divided into the districts Busache and Susu and Busache 'e josso: the seventeenth-century parish church of Sant'Antonio with the majolica dome, the Museum of Costume and linen in the church of San Domenico, the Gothic Santa Susanna and the necropolis of Campu Maiore with 24 domus de Janas.

7 places to visit in Busachi, in the province of Oristano (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo del costume tradizionale e della lavorazione del lino

Museums · Themed

In the deconsecrated church of San Domenico (1571), part of a Dominican monastery known as "su cunventu" , tells the linen from the cultivation to the fabric: ceremonial male and female clothes, festivities, daily newspapers, for mourning and for children, and in the side chapel and in plowing sacristy, gramole, combs, spindles, warps and frames; the path closes with local linens and embroidery. Organizes laboratories to pass on the skills related to linen.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Parrocchiale di Sant'Antonio da Padova

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

17th century church in the historic core of Busache's and Susu, with a 48 meters high bell tower and majolica dome. The patron saint is celebrated in mid-June.

  • Where Busache 'e susu, Busachi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di San Bernardino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It dates back to the 16th century and rises in the district Busache 'e josso, about 80 meters lower than the first nucleus of the village; the festival falls in mid May.

Chiesa di Santa Susanna

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Built in 1349 in Gothic style on the remains of a pagan temple, it preserves a Byzantine holy waterworks; in April and August, during the novenas, the faithful inhabit the ancient cumbessias around the church.

  • Where Santa Susanna, Busachi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Necropoli di Campu Maiore

History and architecture · Archaeology

24 domus de Janas excavated in a rocky ridge, with traces of red ochre on the walls; the tomb IV has seven cells. In the territory other necropolis (Maniere, Cronta, on Cantaru, Grigos) bring the total to about 50 domus de Janas.

  • Where Campu Maiore, Busachi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
