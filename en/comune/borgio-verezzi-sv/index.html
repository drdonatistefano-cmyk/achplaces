What to see in Borgio Verezzi

Municipality in two centers, Borgio on the sea and Verezzi 200 meters on the basin of the river Bottasana, among the most beautiful villages in Italy: stone hamlets, the square of the Festival theater and the caves of the Quaternario.

6 places to visit in Borgio Verezzi, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza Sant'Agostino

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Piazza overlooking the sea in the hamlet of Piazza, with the seventeenth-century church restored after the war; since 1967 it hosts on summer nights the Theatre Festival of Borgio Verezzi, born here.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Agostino, Verezzi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Borgata Crosa

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The oldest hamlet of Verezzi, which seems to be dug into the rock, with caves on the side of the hill inhabited already in the Paleolithic and the main religious buildings.

  • Where Crosa, Verezzi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Borgate Roccaro e Poggio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Roccaro with the eighteenth-century chapel of Our Lady Immaculate from the Baroque altar; Poggio developed around a tower along two perpendicular axes.

  • Where Verezzi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Mulino Fenicio e Croce dei Santi

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

Above Crosa, the 3.5-metre-high mill and stone cross placed in 1664 by Capuchin friars returning from the Holy Land, visible from all over Verezzi.

  • Where Sopra Crosa, Verezzi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Crêuze di Verezzi

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient paths once crossed by mules and chariots, which connect the villages with each other and with the sea.

  • Where Verezzi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Grotte di Borgio Verezzi

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Caves not far from the village and easily reachable, in a paleontological deposit of the Quaternary.
