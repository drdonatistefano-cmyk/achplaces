What to see in Sondrio

The capital of the Valtellina is surrounded by terraced vineyards and mountains: Piazza Campello with the Collegiata and the Palazzo Pretorio, the Via dei Palazzi, the Museo Valtellinese di Storia e Arte, the ancient Scarpatetti and the Castle Masegra with the museum of mountain stories.

9 places to visit in Sondrio, in the province of Sondrio (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza Campello e Palazzo Pretorio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The political and religious center of the city, with the parish church of Saints Gervasio and Protasio and the Palazzo Pretorio, seat of the Grey government since 1552 and today of the Municipality.

  • Where Piazza Campello, Sondrio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sondrioevalmalenco.it

Sources: sondrioevalmalenco.it

Collegiata dei Santi Gervasio e Protasio e Torre Ligariana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church on Piazza Campello and the bell tower called Torre Ligariana, whose first stone was laid on March 29, 1740.

  • Where Piazza Campello, Sondrio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sondrioevalmalenco.it

Sources: sondrioevalmalenco.it

Museo Valtellinese di Storia e Arte (MVSA)

Museums · City history

In the sixteenth century Palazzo Sassi de' Lavizzari, donated to the city in 1922, archaeological finds and works from the Middle Ages to today, the numismatic collection Sertoli and a precious wooden stüa.

  • Where Via Maurizio Quadrio 27, Sondrio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sondrioevalmalenco.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · sondrioevalmalenco.it

Castello Masegra – CAST

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The only one of the three castles of Sondrio still existing, dominates the city for ten centuries and hosts the CAST, a multimedia narrating museum dedicated to stories and mountain culture.

  • Where Sondrio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sondrioevalmalenco.it

Sources: sondrioevalmalenco.it · italia.it · in-lombardia.it
