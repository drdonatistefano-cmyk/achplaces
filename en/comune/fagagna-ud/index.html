What to see in Fagagna

Hill country with views of the morainic hills and the plain, dominated by the remains of the castle with the small church of San Michele Arcangelo; you can visit the museum of peasant life Cjase Cocèl and the Oasis of the Quadris, where white storks have been reintroduced.

Museo della vita contadina «Cjase Cocèl»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the seventeenth-century house of the Chiarvesio family, called Cocèl, since 1994 tells the rural life of Friuli from the nineteenth to the mid-Twentieth century with objects donated by citizens: kitchen with fogolar, rooms of a rich family and a poor, lace workshop (the school was founded in 1892 by Cora Slocomb), granary with grater for silk bugs. Outside, dairy, tavern, age school hall, Bee Museum and a mill from Majano; in the courtyard live donkeys, cows, goats and courtyard animals, and volunteers show crafts such as knife, basket and blacksmith. It is part of the Ecomuseum Il Cavalîr; times to check (MiC card: Monday-Friday 9.30-12.30).

  • Where Via Lisignana 22, Fagagna
  • Entry €3, reduced €2,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it · italia.it

Colle del castello e chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The castle, today largely in ruins, occupies a hill inhabited since Roman times, with views of the hills and the plain. There are still fragments of the walls, the Borgo gate, a clock tower transformed into a bell tower, the ruins of the castle, a house of the fourteenth century and the church of San Michele Arcangelo; in the village stands the Renaissance House of the Community.

  • Where Colle del castello, Fagagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it

Oasi dei Quadris

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Avifaunistic oasis of municipal property in the hills of Fagagna, born with a project to re-introduction of white stork, today visible throughout the country. In 2025 69 storks and 52 ibis hermits were born there, a species of which the oasis hosts about a quarter of the 800 specimens in the world; it also hosts ducks protected as elevator, dairy and tobacconist bites, and hosts about 10,000 visitors a year.

  • Where Località Quadris, Fagagna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · consiglio.regione.fvg.it
