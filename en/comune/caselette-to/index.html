What to see in Caselette

Country at the foot of Mount Musinè, with the Roman rustic villa of Augusta, the nineteenth-century sanctuary of Sant'Abaco, the monumental cross of 1901 on the summit and lakes of the Primavalle area.

4 places to visit in Caselette, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa romana di Caselette

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

The site was chosen at Augustian age, between the last years BC and the first A.D., by colonists of Augusta Taurinorum. It was a farmhouse-residence, used in three phases until the middle of the II century AD: a large but sober building, with a quadrangular plan, with two groups of rooms at different altitudes and a porch overlooking the countryside and lakes. Nearby is the oldest known settlement of Caselette, a village of the end of the ancient Bronze Age (about 1700 BC). You can visit in open days with guided tours of the association Ar.c.A.

  • Where Strada per Val della Torre, località Farchetto, Caselette
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.caselette.to.it

Sources: comune.caselette.to.it · turismotorino.org

Santuario di Sant'Abaco

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Originally a hermitic chapel, it was enlarged in the second half of the nineteenth century at the behest of Count Cays, friend of Don Bosco. It rises at the foot of the Musinè, about 30 minutes walk from Cays Square along a paved road with the pylons of the Way of the Cross; every year in January there is a pilgrimage.

Monte Musinè e Croce monumentale

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Path from Piazza Cays (380 m) to the summit (1150 m) in about an hour and a half: after the 18th century chapel of San Rocco and the sanctuary of Sant'Abaco, a steep ridge path rises to the monumental Cross, 15 meters high, built in concrete reinforced by the cottages in 1901 with materials carried behind the village. From the top the circular panorama embraces the Alps, the Valley of Susa and the plain of Turin.

  • Where Partenza da piazza Cays, Caselette
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.caselette.to.it

Sources: comune.caselette.to.it

Area Primavalle e Laghi di Caselette

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Area on the slopes of the Musinè, one of the richest biodiversity in Piedmont, which includes the Upper and Lower Lakes of Caselette and Lake Borgarino. The markedly xerothermal climate and the iron-rich rocks (peridotites and serpentinites) host rare species such as the Alyssum silvereum. A municipal itinerary from Piazza Cays touches the Roman villa, the plateau with the old quarries of magnesite and a lake with rushes and water lilies.

  • Where Strada Romana, Caselette
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.caselette.to.it
