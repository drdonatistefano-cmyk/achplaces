What to see in San Daniele Po

The village of the low Cremona river on the banks of the Po, known for the Paleoanthropological Museum of the Po, which collects hundreds of fossils of the Quaternary recovered along the river and a frontal bone of Man of Neanderthal; in the hamlet of Isola Pescaroli, on the bank of the river, stands the sanctuary of the Madonna of Trust, Queen of the Po.

Museo Paleoantropologico del Po (Museo naturalistico paleontologico)

Museums · Science and university

Founded in 1998 by the Naturalistic Group Paleontophile (GNP) and the Municipality, it collects hundreds of fossils of the Quaternary recovered along the banks of the Po: remains of about thirty species, including mammoth, megacero deer, bison and rhinoceros, and rare finds of leopard and spelean hyena. The most important piece is a Neanderthal Man's frontal bone nicknamed "Pàus" which is presented as an exceptional testimony of the species in the Po Valley. It is divided into four sections (paleontology, anthropology, mineralogy and didactics), with a wing dedicated to the findings of the Po beaches. Open Tuesday-Friday 8-13 only by reservation with one day in advance and Sunday 14-19; closed Monday, Saturday, August and on the main holidays; accessible to those who have difficulty in walking.

Santuario della Madonna della Fiducia

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

It rises in Isola Pescaroli, a hamlet of San Daniele Po, along the margins of the Po; the church was designed by Rastelli with the engineer Filippini. It was born from the devotion of Don Martino Aletti for Our Lady of Trust: in 1951 a disastrous flood struck the neighbouring countries saving Isola Pescaroli, and the sanctuary, obtained from the remains of the previous church of San Biagio, was inaugurated in 1952. Archbishop Giovanni Cazzani proclaimed Our Lady Queen of the Po and Pius XII blessed her crowns on May 8, 1952. Each year around May 8th the festival includes mass, procession and the blessing of the waters by boat on the river. Without architectural barriers.
