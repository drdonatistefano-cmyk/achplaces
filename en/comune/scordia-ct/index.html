What to see in Scordia

The municipality of Catanese whose territory has returned ceramics, coins and finds of the Greek and Roman ages; the present village was founded in 1626 by Antonio Branciforte, named prince of Scordia by Filippo IV. To see is the ethno-anthropological civic museum and the nineteenth-century church of St. Joseph.

Museo civico etno-antropologico e archivio storico «Mario De Mauro»

Museums · City history

Called in a provocative way to a multifaceted figure lived in Scordia in the first half of the nineteenth century, it presents a material documentation of the history of the place and its inhabitants. Open from Monday to Friday 8.30-12.30, Wednesday also 14.30-19.30; closed Saturday and Sunday (tel. +39 095 7935921).

Chiesa di San Giuseppe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built several times in the 19th century in the center, it has neoclassical façade facing the west. The interior with a single nave has five side altars in marble, wooden sculptures (Holy Family, St. Joseph with Child, Sacred Heart of Jesus), paintings by the Scridian painter Giuseppe Barchitta (1874-1959), including the Madonna of the Lost, two frescoes by Giuseppe Barone on the vault and a large wooden Crucifix in the apse. On March 19, the feast of the saint opens with the parade of barred horses.

  • Where Via Statuto 50, Scordia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.scordia.ct.it

Sources: comune.scordia.ct.it
