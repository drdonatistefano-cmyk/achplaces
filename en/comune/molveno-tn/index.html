What to see in Molveno

Country 864 meters on the north shore of Lake Molveno, Orange Flag of the Touring with the center closed to traffic, in front of the Brenta Dolomites: the thirteenth-century church of San Vigilio, the Venetian water sawmill, the fortinas "napoleonici" on the lake and the Pradel plateau.

6 places to visit in Molveno, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Lago di Molveno e lungolago

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural lake at about 845 meters born about 4,000 years ago from a great landslide that cleared the valley: over 3 km2 and more than 120 meters deep, between the Brenta Dolomites and the massif Gazza-Paganella; on the bottom was found an ancient forest. On the north shore the beach with meadows and fine gravel beaches, boat rental, pedal boats, canoes and SUP; in summer you swim. From the north arm an easy path of about 20 minutes leads to the pond of Bior.

  • Where Lungolago, Molveno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · italia.it

Giro del lago, Fortini napoleonici e Ponte Romano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of about 10-12 km, almost all flat (2-3 hours walk), partly suitable for strollers and bikes. On the west bank are the so-called Napoleonic fortresses, actually fortifications from the eighteenth-nineteenth-century Austria, of which ruins, walls and trenches remain, and the Roman Bridge with a waterfall that falls directly into the lake.

  • Where Sponda occidentale del lago (Monte Corno, Mezzolago), Molveno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Centro storico, Palazzo Saracini e Casa del Dazio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Center closed to traffic around Via Roma, with a direct view of the Brenta Dolomites from the square. Palazzo Saracini is a noble Baroque residence of the seventeenth century, now a parish house, with double stone staircase and quadrilobed windows; the Casa del Dazio preserves medieval elements in the porch.

  • Where Via Roma, Molveno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · italia.it

Chiesa di San Vigilio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the XIII century at the cemetery, Romanesque plant with Gothic elements added later: a frescoed bezel with Our Lady, St. John the Baptist and Jesus and, in the apse, a cycle of frescoes with a Last Supper.

  • Where Presso il cimitero, Molveno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · italia.it

Antica segheria veneziana Taialacqua

Museums · Themed

Built in the 16th century as a cooperative by the inhabitants on the push of the parish priest Don Taialacqua, it still works with water: the wheel moves a rod that transforms the rotation in the motion of the blade; it produced the axes called Molvene, sold in the area of Riva del Garda. A visitor centre explains the operation and role of wood in the Trentino economy; the valley is still called Val delle Seghe.

  • Where Molveno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Altopiano del Pradel e cabinovia La Panoramica

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

The cable car ...La Panoramica is usually open from May to October and climbs to the Pradel plateau at 1,367 metres, with views of the lake and the village; from here a 20-minute path (or a chairlift) leads to the La Montanara mountain hut, and starts the trails towards Cima Paganella.

  • Where Molveno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · italia.it
