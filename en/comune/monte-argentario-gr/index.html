What to see in Monte Argentario

Promontory joined to the coast by two tomboli, with Porto Santo Stefano and Porto Ercole, Spanish fortresses of the sixteenth century and coves.

4 places to visit in Monte Argentario, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 2 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Fortezza Spagnola di Porto Santo Stefano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Tower of 35 meters per side on two levels, built in the second half of the sixteenth century, seat of the exhibitions Submersible Memories and Axe Masters; two terraces overlooking the sea.

  • Where Porto Santo Stefano, Monte Argentario
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Porto Ercole e Forti spagnoli

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the most beautiful villages in Italy since 2004, with the church of Sant'Erasmo, the Clock Tower reminiscent of Caravaggio, who died here in 1610, and the forts Filippo, Stella and the Rocca.

  • Where Porto Ercole, Monte Argentario
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Spiagge della Feniglia e della Giannella

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The two sandy tombs that join the promontory to the coast: the clear sand Giannella and the Feniglia reachable from Porto Ercole.

  • Where Monte Argentario
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Convento dei Padri Passionisti

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

The sanctuary built in 1737 by São Paulo della Croce in the green of the promontory.

  • Where Monte Argentario
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
