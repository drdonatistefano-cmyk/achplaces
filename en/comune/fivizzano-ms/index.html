What to see in Fivizzano

Historical capital of eastern Lunigiana: the Medici square with its fountain inside the walls, the memory of the press of Jacopo da Fivizzano, the castle of the Verrucola dei Malaspina and, in Equi Terme, the caves in the Park of the Apuan Alps.

9 places to visit in Fivizzano, in the province of Massa-Carrara (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza Medicea e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The old walled town is dominated by Piazza Medicea with its imposing fountain, which overlooks many historic buildings.

  • Where Piazza Medicea, Fivizzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo della Stampa «Jacopo da Fivizzano»

Museums · Themed

In Palazzo Fantoni-Bononi, restored by the physician and writer Loris Jacopo Bononi: documents the activity of the printer Jacopo da Fivizzano in the years 1471-72, at the beginning of the printing in mobile characters, the invention of the first typewriter by Agostino Fantoni, the Spanish editions of the publisher Emanuele Maucci, with a historical printing rebuilt.

The Touring Club indicates it is temporarily closed

  • Where Palazzo Fantoni-Bononi, Via Labindo 6, Fivizzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: touringclub.it · visittuscany.com

Teatro degli Imperfetti

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built by the Accademia degli Imperfetti, founded in the sixteenth century and active until the mid-nineteenth century; inaugurated in 1807 with 600 seats.

  • Where Fivizzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Grotte di Equi e Museo archeologico

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

In the regional park of the Apuan Alps, UNESCO World Park: tourist caves with stalactites, stalagmites and hanging lakes (about 50 minutes); archaeological museum and prehistoric Tecchia, frequented about 40,000 years ago, with remains of the cave bear (about 40 minutes); and then zipline of 200 meters on the stream, cave-adventure, rock gym and center visits of the Park with the museum of geology ApuanGeoLab. Managed by the AlterEco Community Cooperative for the Municipality.

  • Where Via Buca 46, Equi Terme, Fivizzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.grottediequi.it

Sources: grottediequi.it · visittuscany.com
