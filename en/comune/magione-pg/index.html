What to see in Magione

Cittadina on the eastern shore of Trasimeno, born around the hospice of the Knights Gerosolimitani, today Castle of the Knights of Malta, theatre in 1502 of the conspiracy against Cesare Borgia; in the village the Lambardi Tower and the frescoes of Gerardo Dottori, on the lake the villages of Monte del Lago and San Feliciano with the Museum of Fishing, around a crown of castles and the nature reserve La Valle.

Castello dei Cavalieri di Malta

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Born between 1150 and 1170 as the hospice of the Knights Hospitallers for the pilgrims going to Rome (documented from 1171), it was fortified in a square manor with corner towers and courtyard with three orders of loggias, in the final fifteenth-century form of the architect Fioravante Fioravanti from Bologna. In 1502 there was held the Diet of Magione, the conspiracy against Cesare Borgia described by Machiavelli in the Prince, re-enacted every summer; Benedict XIV (1746) and Pius VII (1805). In the Romanesque chapel of St. John the Baptist two frescoes by painters of the workshop of Pinturicchio (Nativity; Madonna and Child among Saints John the Baptist and James). Still owned by the Order of Malta, at the center of a farm.

some rooms, including the frescoed chapel, which can be visited only by appointment

Torre dei Lambardi

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

A tower of about 30 meters with a quadrangular plan on a hill just above the village, with views of Perugia and Trasimeno; built between XII and XIII century by the Knights Hospitallers, controlled supplies for Perugia, especially the fish of the north shore of the lake. Restored by the Municipality in the eighties, it hosts exhibitions on four floors and has a panoramic terrace; around a park with life path. Hours: July-August Mar-dom 10-13 and 16-19.30 (sab-dom also 21.30-23.30); April-June and September-Jo-dom 10-13 and 15-18; October-March by reservation. Info: tel. 075 8473078.

Museo della pesca del Lago Trasimeno

Museums · Themed

Since 1997 in the former headquarters of the Consortium for Fisheries and Aquaculture of Trasimeno, in San Feliciano: four rooms named at the moments of the day of the fisherman tell the geological origin of the lake, the fishing from prehistory to today and the fish fauna, with the cast of the "Venus del Trasimeno" , Etruscan-Roman net weights, a rare collection of water birds, aquariums with local species and a multimedia room shaped like a boat. Hours: July-August Mar-dom 10-13 and 16-19.30 (sat-dom also 21.30-23.30); April-June and September-Jo-dom 10-13 and 15-18; October-December and March-Sat-dom 10-13 and 15-18; January-February by reservation. Info: tel. 075 8479261.

Monte del Lago

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Borgo on a promontory on the Trasimeno between Torricella and San Feliciano, surrounded by walls from Perugia from 1312 against Henry VII and in the sixteenth century seat of the Apostolic Chamber, with the Fortress at the eastern entrance and the Porta Trasimena. In the church of Sant'Andrea a great Crucifixion of the late fourteenth century; at the entrance Villa Aganoor Pompilj, where lived the poetess Victoria Aganoor, and under Palazzo Schnabl, living room of artists and home of the musicologist Riccardo Rossi Schnabl, friend of Puccini. In September the Festival of Correspondence.

  • Where Monte del Lago, Magione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Castello di Agello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 411 meters, probably born as a castrum in the war against Hannibal (217 BC); from the twelfth century under Perugia and entrusted to the Vincioli, occupied in 1390 by Michelozzo Michelotti, rebuilt at the end of the fourteenth century and destroyed again in 1642. Palazzo Vincioli (XIII-XIV century) is an important example of medieval civil architecture.

  • Where Agello, Magione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Castello di San Savino ed Emissario di Braccio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Documented since 1006, when Count Pietro Attone of Todi founded a monastery there: walls, ogival gate, well, triangular mast and remains of dwellings, fortifications strengthened by Perugia in 1310 against Henry VII. Nearby the fifteenth-century remains of the emissary built by Braccio Fortebraccio da Montone (Lord of Perugia 1416-1424) to regulate the lake, renovated in 1896.

  • Where San Savino, Magione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Rocca Baglioni Pompilij

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Military architecture of the fourteenth century then rebuilt, between San Feliciano and Monte del Lago: an inscription of 1372 on the tower recalls the expansion of Cola Masci; passed to Baglioni and Pompilij. It has a gate in a sandstone walled with a fish in key, Palazzo Pula with portico and Renaissance loggia, the 14th century mast and remains of medieval houses.

private residence, visible only from outside
