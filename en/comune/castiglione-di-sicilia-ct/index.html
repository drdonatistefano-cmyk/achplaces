What to see in Castiglione di Sicilia

Borgo on a spur of sandstone between Etna and the Alcantara valley, Civitas Animosa for Federico II: the castle of Lauria with the Regional Winery, the tower u Cannizzu, the Church Mother of Saints Peter and Paul with the sundial of 1882, the basilica of the Madonna della Catena and, in the country, the Byzantine Cuba of Santa Domenica.

8 places to visit in Castiglione di Sicilia, in the province of Catania (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Lauria ed Enoteca regionale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

12th century castle on a sandstone spur, main defense of the village, connected by tunnels to the Castidduzzu and the apse of St. Peter; in 1283 was granted in feud to Ruggero di Lauria. Today it hosts the Regional Enoteca for Eastern Sicily.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Piazza Lauria e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Central square paved in lava stone, where stands the Town Hall of the early twentieth century on the site of Peculio, the grain reserve that saved citizens from famines. Nearby, in the Cameni district, the church of Sant'Antonio (began in 1601, Baroque façade of 1796) and the Palaces Camardi, Imbesi, Sardo and Saglimboni.

  • Where Piazza Lauria, Castiglione di Sicilia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info

Chiesa Madre dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It preserves the apse of the first Norman church, dated by tradition to 1105, and the original tower; inside a wooden crucifix of the sixteenth century and the only sundial still working in the Alcantara valley, built in 1882 by the astronomer Temistocle Zona. Next to the 18th century church and the monastery of San Benedetto.

  • Where Via San Pietro, Castiglione di Sicilia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: parcoalcantara.it · visitsicily.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di San Marco e Castidduzzu

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The church of St. Mark, of Norman origin (XII century), is reached from Via Federico II; from here a steep staircase rises to the Castidduzzu, the ruins of a fortification with all probability Byzantine.

  • Where Via Federico II, Castiglione di Sicilia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Basilica di Maria Santissima della Catena

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

The most important church of the village, on the panoramic road exiting from the center, with Baroque façade of Baldassarre Greco and the statue of the Madonna in white marble of Carrara attributed to the school of the Gagines; feast of the patroness on the first Sunday of May.

  • Where Castiglione di Sicilia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · parcoalcantara.it · visitsicily.info

Torre u Cannizzu

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

A cylindrical sandstone tower built between the 12th and 14th centuries, probably part of a larger fortification called "Cittadella" (Cittadella) was perhaps the first cornerstone taken by Frederick III when in 1301 he resumed the feud at Ruggero di Lauria. It is a symbol of Castiglione; next to the church of San Vincenzo Ferreri.

  • Where Via San Vincenzo, Castiglione di Sicilia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Cuba bizantina di Santa Domenica

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Byzantine-style chapel in the open countryside, built by the Basilian monks between the 7th and 9th centuries, unusually large, with a Greek cross on a square base with dome; declared national monument on August 31, 1909 and restored for some years. FAI place, open on the FAI Days of Spring 2025.

  • Where Contrada Santa Domenica, Castiglione di Sicilia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info · parcoalcantara.it · fondoambiente.it

Ponte di San Nicola e Piccole Gole dell'Alcantara

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the remains of the Arab bridge known as St. Nicholas there is a well-equipped path that leads to the Little Gorges of Alcantara, from which you can observe the river closely. Beyond the bridge, on the road to Francavilla, the Norman church of St. Nicholas (XII-XIII century) with frescoes of Byzantine style in the apse.

  • Where Strada per Francavilla di Sicilia, Castiglione di Sicilia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: parcoalcantara.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info
