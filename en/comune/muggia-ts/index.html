What to see in Muggia

The only Istrian-Venetian town on the Italian coast, on the bay at the far east of the Gulf of Trieste: the Mandracchio with fishermen, the Duomo, the 14th century castle and, on the promontory, the Basilica of Muggia Vecchia.

8 places to visit in Muggia, in the province of Trieste (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Basilica di Muggia Vecchia

Places of worship · Basilicas · Viewpoint

Romanesque basilica of early medieval origin on the promontory above the port, with the remains of the walls and the protohistoric casteliers of Muggia Vecchia and Santa Barbara; wide panorama of the Gulf of Trieste.

  • Where Muggia Vecchia, Muggia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
