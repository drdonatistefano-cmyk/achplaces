What to see in Novi Ligure

Cities of painted facades and chocolate in the south of the province of Alexandria, long under the control of Genoese, with the decorated palaces of the historic center, the Salt Fountain and the Museum of the Championships dedicated to Costante Girardengo and Fausto Coppi.

Museo dei Campionissimi

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 2003 in a former industrial warehouse of the early 20th century in the center, on over 3,000 m2, it is indicated as the largest museum of cycling in Europe. A long central track tells the story of the bicycle, from the prototype attributed to Leonardo and from the draisina to the prototypes in titanium, with about forty bicycles; on the sides the history of the components and the Giro d'Italia, two rooms with vintage films, a virtual reality room with computerized bikes and the Sala dei Campionissimi with relics of Fausto Coppi and Costante Girardengo, born in Novi. It also houses the permanent exhibition "Tesori Sacri" from the civic collection, ten paintings from the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. Hours from October to April: Friday 15-19, Saturday, Sunday and holidays 10-13 and 15-19; from May to September: Friday 16-20, Saturday, Sunday and holidays 10-13 and 16-20; closed 25 December and 1 January.

Centro storico e palazzi dipinti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The colorful and painted facades of the center date back to the seventeenth century and testify to the prosperity of the city under the influence of Genoese; they are to be watched looking up as you walk. The route of the Municipality lists 42 stages, including the Collegiate Church, the Castle Tower, the Church of Sant'Andrea, the Oratory of the Disciplined of Santa Maria Maddalena, the Durazzo Palaces, Brignole (of the Customs), Spinola, Balbi and Pallavicini (seat of the Municipality), the Marenco Theatre, Porta Pozzolo and the Pieve of Santa Maria.

  • Where Centro storico, Novi Ligure
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · alexala.it

Palazzo Negroni (oggi Costa)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Palazzo overlooking Piazza Dellepiane, known for the decorations of the facade, including two sundials; it is one of the stages of the historical center of the municipality.

  • Entry Free

Fontana del Sale

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

It recalls a nineteenth-century protest by the nineteenths against the British, who had taken over the entire salt and tobacco store in the city.

  • Entry Free
