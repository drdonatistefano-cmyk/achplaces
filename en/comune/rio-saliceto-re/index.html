What to see in Rio Saliceto

Municipality of the Reggiana plain with a small 1911 municipal theatre decorated by Luigi Pillitu, the neoclassical town hall of 1889 by Giuseppe Aimi and the church of San Giorgio, rebuilt in 1879 with a 36-metre bell tower.

4 places to visit in Rio Saliceto, in the province of Reggio Emilia (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Teatro comunale Pietro Montanari

History and architecture · Historic theatres

It was wanted by the merchant Pietro Montanari, who presented the project in 1910, and was inaugurated on February 9, 1911 with the comedy "Il Cardinale" and hosted also cinemas, dances and parties; the Municipality bought it in 1986 and reopened it in 1993 with the flutist Andrea Griminelli. The small elliptical hall has two orders of galleries in eclectic style, painted by Sardinian Luigi Pillitu with Musici and Comedants that form a fixed scene aimed at the public.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Garibaldi 28, Rio Saliceto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: comune.riosaliceto.re.it · visitemilia.com

Municipio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Symbol of autonomy obtained in 1860, inaugurated on October 23, 1889 on a neoclassical project by Giuseppe Aimi di Correggio: a T-shaped plant, exposed brick tetrastyle pronao and a terracotta frieze, commissioned by the mayor Pier Giacinto Terrachini to his son Bruto, with medallions of the eight founders of the municipality. Accessible during the opening hours of the town hall.

  • Where Piazza Giosuè Carducci 18, Rio Saliceto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: reggioemiliawelcome.it · visitemilia.com

Chiesa di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of ancient origin rebuilt in 1879 in neoclassical style, with three portals facade and circular windows; the bell tower is 36 meters high.

  • Where Rio Saliceto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com
