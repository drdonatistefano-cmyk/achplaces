What to see in Santa Marinella

A seaside resort north of Rome, called "Pearl of the Tyrrhenian Sea" with summer liberty villas, rocky coves and the marina of the ancient Punicum; in the hamlet of Santa Severa the castle on the sea rises on the site of Pyrgi, Etruscan port of Caere, and houses the Museum of the Sea and the Ancient Navigation.

Castello di Santa Severa

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

16th century castle on the sea that contains the oldest Roman fortress and medieval village, on the site of Pyrgi, Etruscan port of Caere founded between the end VII and beginning VI century BC. The oldest part is the Saracen Tower (XI century); in the village the Chapel-Baptistery of the fifteenth century has frescoes attributed to the school of Antoniazzo Romano. It houses the Museum of the Sea, the Antiquarium of Pyrgi, the Museum of the Territory and the Castle Museum, and is open all year round.

Museo del Mare e della Navigazione Antica

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the Castle of Santa Severa, founded in 1993 and renovated in 2023: seven rooms with over 450 finds between original and life-size reconstructions, models of ancient boats, nautical instruments and underwater archaeology, on the maritime history of the Mediterranean.

Area archeologica e Antiquarium di Pyrgi

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The Etruscan shrine of Pyrgi dates back to the 6th century BC and included temples dedicated to Eileithyia-Leukothea, Apollo and Uni; there were found gold sheets in Etruscan and Phoenician- Punic. Above part of the Etruscan city arose after the third century BC a rectangular Roman fortress in polygonal work, used until the V-VI century A.D. The Antiquarium, near the sanctuary, preserves the materials of over forty years of excavation.

Castello e porticciolo di Santa Marinella

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The port was Etruscan port and Carthaginian port (Punicum); the Romans built villas there and the Barberini expanded it in the sixteenth century. The castle, erected by Barberini incorporating the watchtower wanted after the decree of Pius V of 1567, passed in 1773 to the Hospital of Santo Spirito and in 1887 to the Odescalchi princes.

Riserva naturale di Macchiatonda

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Regional reserve of 244 hectares established in 1983, plain between the Ceriti Mountains and the sea with ponds, marshes and coastal woods: wet area of rest for migratory birds, observable from the huts. Paths of the Boschetto, the huts and the beach; in the rainy periods they can be unworkable.
