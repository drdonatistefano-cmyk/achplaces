What to see in Fiano Romano

Center of the Tiber Valley dominated by the fifteenth-century castle of the Orsini, with the parish church of Santo Stefano; in the territory, along Via Tiberina, the Roman villa of the Volusii Saturnini, discovered in 1961 during the work of the Autostrada del Sole.

Villa dei Volusii

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Villa built around the middle of the first century BC by the senatorial family of the Saturnini Volusii: the first owner was Q. Volusio, known to Cicero; his son L. Volusio Saturnino, consul in 12 BC, added new mosaics and a great peristyle with a larary with statues of the ancestors. On a panoramic terrace, it develops on two levels separated by a cryptoportico, with atrium, cubicles, triclino and table and mosaic floors restored under roofs. Discovered by chance in 1961 with the work of the Autostrada del Sole, which cut it in two, it was excavated between 1962 and 1971.

access from the archaeological area of Lucus Feroniae; on Sunday only the first and third of the month, otherwise from the Service Area Feronia West of the A1; the MiC event card indicates mandatory booking and times other than those of the Superintendence

Castello Orsini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built in the fifteenth century by the Orsini, lords of Fiano: a robust central cylindrical mast, thirty meters high and crowned with blackbirds, with a square side tower. It leans on Porta Capena, the entrance of the village; inside a courtyard with the staircase that goes up to the noble floor.

  • Where Centro storico, Fiano Romano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Santo Stefano Protomartire (Santo Stefano Nuovo)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The original church of the XII-XIII century was abandoned for its uncomfortable position and rebuilt in the second half of the fifteenth century; a basilica with three naves with round arches on travertine pillars. It preserves the Madonna with the Saints Giovanni Battista, Stefano, Biagio and Pietro di Antonio del Massaro, the funeral monument of Niccolò Orsini, a table by Salvator Mundi and two paintings by the Umbrian school.

  • Where Centro storico, Fiano Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com
