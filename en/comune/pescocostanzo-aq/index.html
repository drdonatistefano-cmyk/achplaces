What to see in Pescocostanzo

Renaissance village of the Abruzzo Apennines, with the Collegiate Church of Santa Maria del Colle with five aisles, sixteenth-century palaces, the Museum of lace in Palazzo Fanzago and the beech wood of Sant'Antonio in the Majella Park.

7 places to visit in Pescocostanzo, in the province of L'Aquila (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Collegiata di Santa Maria del Colle

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Founded in the late Middle Ages and rebuilt after the earthquake of 1456, it has five naves, a rare case in Abruzzo; the golden coffered ceiling of Carlo Sabatini of 1680, stuccos by Giambattista Gianni, and the Baroque wrought iron gate of Santo and Ilario di Rocco (1699-1705) that closes the Captone del Sacramento. Inside the Museum of the Basilica with goldsmiths, statues and sacred furnishings.

  • Where Via Diomede Falconi, Pescocostanzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Museo del merletto a tombolo

Museums · Themed

In the 17th century Palazzo Fanzago, next to the lace school: phases of workmanship and instruments, ancient lace from private collections and ecclesiastical furnishings, some of the eighteenth century, and recent works such as the Prince's Breakfast. Open every day 11-13 and 17-20 from August 1st to September 10th, from December 24th to January 6th and on Easter week.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Pescocostanzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · parcomajella.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Gesù e Maria e monastero di Santa Scolastica

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church founded in 1611 with precious Baroque altars in marble; the main altar (1626-30) and the portico cloister of the monastery are by Cosimo Fanzago, who also designed the former monastery of Santa Scolastica (1642).

  • Where Pescocostanzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Centro storico e palazzi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Palaces of the Cinque e Seicento with stone portals: Palazzo Colecchi, Palazzo Grilli with four corner towers, Palazzo Mansi, Palazzo Coccopalmeri, Casa D'Amata with the vineyard, the Town Hall with the clock tower in Piazza Municipio and the houses of Corso Roma with loggia and external stairs; filigree, lace, wrought iron and carving remain alive.

  • Where Piazza Municipio e Corso Roma, Pescocostanzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Bosco di Sant'Antonio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient sacred forest in Jupiter, 17 hectares of centuries-old candelabra beech trees with maples, wild pears, hornbeams and badgers, in the Majella Park since 1992; a 2.8 km ring with 70 m of altitude leads to the hermitage of Sant'Antonio (XIV-XV century). Hiking and horseback riding in summer, cross-country skiing in winter.

  • Where Bosco di Sant'Antonio, Pescocostanzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcomajella.it
