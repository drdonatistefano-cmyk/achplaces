What to see in Bressanone

Capital of a bishopric from the 11th to the beginning of the 19th century, called "small Salzburg": the medieval and Baroque center between Isarco and Rienza brings together Duomo, frescoed cloister, Bishop's Palace with the Diocesan Museum, the Portici and the White Tower, with the Plose as a mountain home.

9 places to visit in Bressanone, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di Bressanone

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Cathedral of Saints Cassiano and Maria Assunta, with origins in the 10th century and last Baroque reconstruction in the 18th century: eighteenth-century façade with two bell towers, neoclassical vestibule and baroque interiors that the prince bishop commissioned called the antechamber of paradise.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Bressanone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · brixen.org

Chiostro del Duomo

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

One of the most important art monuments in South Tyrol: of pre-Romanesque origin, rebuilt in the 12th and 14th centuries with gothic cruising vaults. The 20 arches are frescoed between the end of the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries and show the passage from the Gothic to the late Gothic; famous is the presentation of an elephant (3rd arch) and the Adoration of the Magi (13a). It gives access to two hidden churches.

  • Where Vicolo Albuino 2, Bressanone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it

Palazzo Vescovile (Hofburg) e Museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters

The residence of the bishops' princes since the 13th century, rebuilt several times and since the end of the 16th century, with a sumptuous courtyard; it houses the Diocesan Museum, one of the most famous in South Tyrol: paintings, sculptures and arts applied from Romanesque to modern times, with crucifixes and Romanesque Madonnas, medieval manuscripts, tables and Gothic sculptures. Next to the Giardino dei Signori with four flowerbeds and Biedermeier bronze fountain. Open every day 10-17.

  • Where Piazza Palazzo Vescovile 2, Bressanone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.hofburg.it

Sources: hofburg.it · italia.it · brixen.org

Museo della Farmacia

Museums · Themed

Opened in 2002 on the second floor of the building of the Pharmacy Peer, in one of the oldest private residences in the city: in rooms partly covered with historical wood tells about four centuries of history of pharmacy and medicine in Tyrol, starting from the instruments of an ancient family of spiciness in Bressanone.

  • Where Via Ponte Aquila 4, Bressanone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: brixen.org · suedtirol.info · italia.it

Museo della Torre Bianca

Museums · City history · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Museum opened in 2007 in the White Tower, symbol of the city, which was together civic tower and bell tower: models explain its historical and religious role, miniature bells reproduce the sound of the original and you see the interior of the original globe of the bell tower and the work of the guardians of the tower. Particularly suitable for children.

  • Where Piazza della Parrocchia, Bressanone
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Portici Maggiori e Minori

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Porticoed streets built mostly between the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, with colored facades, polygonal bovinds (erker), small squares and courtyards; workshops and artisan workshops are located there from the Middle Ages and are still the center of the city's trade.

  • Where Via Portici Maggiori, Bressanone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: brixen.org · italia.it

Stufles e Ponte Aquila

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Stufles is the oldest neighborhood, with neolithic origins, a time of artisans and poor families and today the artists' neighborhood with small studios. The Aquila Bridge, mentioned since 1233 connects it to the centre: the wooden bridge was destroyed several times by the floods and the iron bridge was built in 1883.

  • Where Ponte Aquila, Bressanone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: brixen.org · italia.it

Confluenza di Isarco e Rienza

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

After the great flood of 1882 the confluence was regulated: today the two rivers meet at the southern end of the Rapp Gardens, home in May of an installation of the Water Light Festival.

  • Where Giardini Rapp, Bressanone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: brixen.org · italia.it

Plose

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

The mountain of the house above the city, a few kilometers and reachable by cable car: in summer panoramic excursions, family paths (Woodywalk and Lorggen), mountain bike and Plosebob; in winter ski area, winter trails and toboggan runs.

  • Where Plose, Bressanone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: brixen.org · italia.it
