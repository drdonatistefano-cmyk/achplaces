What to see in Frisanco

Municipality of Val Colvera, at the foot of Mount Raut and inside the Natural Park of the Friulian Dolomites: the hamlet of Poffabro, among the most beautiful villages in Italy, with stone houses and wooden balconies and the church of San Nicolò; in Frisanco the Piazzatta Pognici and the exhibition "Da li mans di Carlin" .

10 places to visit in Frisanco, in the province of Pordenone (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo di Poffabro

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

It was cited in 1339 and in 1357 as Prafafabrorum or Pratum Fabri. It conserves a 16th-17th century plant: narrow houses and three-four-storey square stone houses, large wooden balconies, internal courtyards reached by narrow arches, alleys and arcades; stone houses resisted the earthquake of 1976 without serious damage. From mid-December to mid-January, Poffabro, Nativity scenes among the Nativity scenes, brings a hundred nativity scenes to unusual corners of the village.

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre, Poffabro, Frisanco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The 17th century parish church of Poffabro, unusually large compared to the houses of the village, with white façade delineated already at the end of the 17th century and several times restored after the earthquakes; it preserves wooden sculptures of Giacomo Marizza and a wooden altar of the 17th century.

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre, Poffabro, Frisanco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Centro visite del Parco di Poffabro

Museums · Themed

In the former cheese factory of the village, it tells about the production of cheese with the Friulian system through the original machinery, with a photographic exhibition on dairy farms and pastures, a section "In mont" on the alpine pastures and the nature of the Park, a space on the Colvera Valley and a multimedia post on the farmhouses of the Park.

  • Where Poffabro, Frisanco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.parcodolomitifriulane.it

Sources: parcodolomitifriulane.it · italia.it
