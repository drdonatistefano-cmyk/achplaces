What to see in San Donà di Piave

San Donà is housed in the MUB - Museum of Reclamation, one of the Sandonese Civic Museums together with the Galleria Civica d'Arte Moderna e Contemporanea and the Space Exhibitions. Battistella is the history of the relationship between man and the environment in the eastern Veneto, from the peasant life before the Bonifiche to the Great War; the parish church of Santa Maria delle Grazie, destroyed in 1918, was rebuilt between 1919 and 1923.

MUB - Museo della Bonifica

Museums · City history

In a building designed in 1967 as a convent of the Clarisses, adapted to exhibition space in 1983 and expanded in 1998 with European funds; in 2018 the exhibition was renovated according to the formula of the ecomuseum and in 2021, with the European project WALKofPEACE, a space dedicated to the Great War and the reclamation was inaugurated. The route tells the relationship between man and environment in the Lower Piave from the Roman and late Roman times to the contemporary age: the peasant life before the Bonifiche, the hydraulic transformations of the eastern Veneto, the First World War and the reconstruction of the landscape. Open Tuesday 9-12.30, Wednesday-Friday 10-12.30 (Thursday also 15-18), Saturday and Sunday 15-18; closed on Monday.

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of the diocese of Treviso. The census of the churches of the CEI records a pre-existing structure as early as 1480 and the construction of the building between 1838 and 1841; destroyed in 1918, the church was rebuilt between 1919 and 1923, with the bell tower rebuilt in 1921-1922. The altars were donated in 1927 and 1934 and the presbytery had a structural intervention in 1970.
