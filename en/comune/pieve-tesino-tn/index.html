What to see in Pieve Tesino

Country of the Tesino basin, at the southern slopes of Mount Silana in Valsugana, among the most beautiful villages in Italy since 2021 and home of Alcide De Gasperi: his birthplace is now a museum, next to the museum Per Via on the sellers of prints thesis and the Arboretum with the Garden of Europe.

7 places to visit in Pieve Tesino, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Casa De Gasperi

Museums · House museums

Opened in 2006 in the home of Alcide De Gasperi, it is a multimedia path of vintage testimonies, sounds and images that goes from the Austro-Hungarian Trentino to the birth of the European Union. Open Saturday and Sunday 10-13 and 14.30-18.

  • Where Via Alcide Degasperi 1, Pieve Tesino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: italia.it · visittrentino.info

Per Via, Museo Tesino delle Stampe e dell'Ambulantato

Museums · Themed

In Casa Buffa Giacantoni, inhabited by generations of print sellers and empty since the 1960s, tells the street papers that sold art prints in the world, opening shops and becoming publishers, and the families remained in the country. It is run by the Trentina Alcide De Gasperi Foundation.

  • Where Via Alcide De Gasperi 6/A, Pieve Tesino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Arboreto del Tesino e Giardino d'Europa

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

One-hour-one-and-a-half ring with about 100 meters of altitude difference, accessible to all, among woods, flowering meadows and peat bogs: mountain woods plants, Japanese maples, Asian and aquatic plants, an orchard of lost fruit and panels with passages of the The 2011 Garden of Europe is a flowered amphitheatre that recalls the European Parliament. We start from the visitors center with parking.

  • Where Accanto al Museo Casa De Gasperi, Pieve Tesino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitvalsugana.it · italia.it · visittrentino.info

Piazza Maggiore e Dama delle fonti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Heart of the village, surrounded by buildings and patronal houses of the Seven and Nineteenth Century, with the monumental eighteenth-century octagonal fountain in red stone called "Dama delle fonte" (Dama of the sources).

  • Where Piazza Maggiore, Pieve Tesino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Chiesa dei Santi Fabiano e Sebastiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church with Romanesque bell tower, where in 1575 Giorgio Castelrotto, captain of Primiero, suspected of bringing the plague, took refuge.

  • Where Pieve Tesino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Forra di Sorgazza-Cengello

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of about three hours without any particular difficulty, between waterfalls, walkways and wooden bridges.

  • Where Sorgazza, Pieve Tesino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Cannoniere di Monte Silana

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A challenging but uncomplicated excursion through a wood of beech and hornbeam, with a view of the Pradellano plain.

  • Where Monte Silana, Pieve Tesino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info
