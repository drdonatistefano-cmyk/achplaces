What to see in Abbateggio

A small white stone village in the Majella National Park, called the village crib, with views from the Gran Sasso to the sea; surroundings with the sanctuary of the Madonna dell'Elcina, the huts in tholos and the Paleolithic site of Valle Gimentina.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient nucleus of white stone houses rises on a rocky spur above the valley of Fosso Fonte Vecchia, between alleys and steep stairs; the most recent part dates back to the early twentieth century. It is so well preserved that it is called the country crib.

  • Where Abbateggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Santuario della Madonna dell'Elcina

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On a rocky hill near the center, rebuilt and expanded in 1927 in local stone; it houses a painted terracotta statue of the Madonna (late XV-early XVI century), a holm trunk under the altar linked to the tradition of the apparition and a canvas probably seventeenth century. From the hill the view embraces Gran Sasso, Majella, Morrone and Adriatico.

  • Where Via Madonna dell'Elcina 12, Abbateggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.abbateggio.pe.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Lorenzo Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Mother church with fifteenth-century portal and quadrangular bell tower with helical stone staircase; Baroque interior with wooden statue of the patron saint and stone stove dated 1600. Patron celebration on August 10.

  • Where Abbateggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · parcomajella.it · italia.it

Ecomuseo del Paleolitico - Valle Giumentina

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 2013 in front of the Paleolithic site of Valle Gimentina, among the most important in Abruzzo for the lower and middle Paleolithic (Radmilli excavations 1954-55, new research since 2012); six shelters in stone rebuilt tell the Neanderthals, the transhumant pastoral care, flora and fauna, with an outdoor workshop of splintering of the flint.

Capanne a tholos di Valle Giumentina

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Pastoral complex made of dry stone: the main building is the largest and only two-storey building between the about 500 of the area, with smaller huts, a small inhabited nucleus and remains of the enclosures for animals; near the Fonte Cugnoli with picnic area.

  • Where Valle Giumentina, Abbateggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · parcomajella.it · italia.it

Macchia di Abbateggio e sorgente della Morgia

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry

Bosco about 960 meters on the north-western side of Majella, with the source of the Morgia, one of the main karst phenomena of the massif.
