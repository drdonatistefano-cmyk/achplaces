What to see in Ostiano

Borgo sul'Oglio entered the Gonzaga domains in 1414, in the Regional Park of Oglio Sud; to see the sixteenth-century castle with the remains of the synagogue, the parish church of San Michele Arcangelo with the paintings of the Chiaveghino and the parish church of San Gaudenzio rebuilt by Vespasiano Gonzaga.

Castello Gonzaga e sinagoga

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Rebuilt in 1511 at the behest of Ludovico Gonzaga on the remains of a previous defensive structure, it was surrounded by a large moat and was part of the defenses of the village with the Door Spinata, still existing, and the Door of the Valley, demolished at the end of the nineteenth century. There remains one wing with one of the four cylindrical towers (the eastern tower), the mast, under which you enter the courtyard, and part of the curtain. Inside a sixteenth-century Gonzaga palace, where the remains of the synagogue of the Jewish community favored by Gonzaga are preserved, and a small restored nineteenth-century theatre. Free access to the ground floor.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Ostiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.ostiano.cr.it

Parrocchiale di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between the end of the sixteenth century and the beginning of the seventeenth century at the behest of Francesco Gonzaga, bishop of Mantua; inside stand out the paintings of Andrea Mainardi called the Chiaveghino, of the school of the Camps.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Ostiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.ostiano.cr.it

Pieve di San Gaudenzio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

An ancient plebanic seat outside the historic centre, it was rebuilt in 1580-81 by Vespasiano Gonzaga demolishing much of the previous building; in the sixteenth century a convent was flanked, today disappeared, and in the nineteenth century it became a cemetery church. Inside there are altars with marble tarsies and a beautiful seventeenth-century wooden cantoria from the parish. Free access to the ground floor.

  • Where Via Pieve 56, Ostiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ostiano.cr.it · italia.it

Chiese di Torricella e di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Two churches outside the historic center: that of Torricella, a small country chapel, dates back to the twelfth century; that of San Rocco in the fifteenth century.

  • Where Fuori dal centro storico, Ostiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.ostiano.cr.it
