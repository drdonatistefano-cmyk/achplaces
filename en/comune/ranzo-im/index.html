What to see in Ranzo

Municipality of the lower valley Arroscia, home of the workshop of the painters Guido da Ranzo, with the frescoed church of San Pantaleo and the ruins of the castle above the gorge.

3 places to visit in Ranzo, in the province of Imperia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di San Pantaleo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Isolated on the left bank of the Arroscia, with the portico frescoed in 1488 (Passion, Scenes of St. Pantaleo, Resurrection of Lazarus) and inside the Blesseds, Hell, the Riding of the Vizi, a great Saint Christopher and the Months of the early sixteenth century.

MiC indicates it as usually closed, open on extraordinary occasions

  • Where SP453, Ranzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

GDR Museo dei Guido da Ranzo

Museums · Old masters

In the House of Friars in Costa Parrocchia, he tells the family of painters Guido da Ranzo and in particular Pietro Guido, active in the Ligurian West between 1490 and 1532.

  • Where Borgata Costa Parrocchia, Ranzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Ruderi del castello di Ranzo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Remains of the castle overlooking the gorge near San Pantaleo, according to the tradition built in the 10th century at the time of Saracen raids.

  • Entry Free
