What to see in Montesarchio

Borgo della Valle Caudina dominated by the Longobard castle, rebuilt at the end of the fifteenth century by Francesco di Giorgio Martini, which houses the National Archaeological Museum of Sannio Caudino with the crater of Assteas; below, the districts Latovetere and Latonuovo, convents and Piazza Umberto I.

Castello e Museo archeologico nazionale del Sannio Caudino (MANSC)

Museums · Archaeological · Viewpoint

The castle, of Longobard character with last great reconstruction at the end of the fifteenth century attributed to Francesco di Giorgio Martini, houses in six rooms the history of the Caudina Valley and the furnishings of the necropolis of Caudium (mid VIII-III century BC), with attic and italian vases and materials of Saticula and Telesia; the most famous piece is the crater of Assteas with the rat of Europe. Open from Tuesday to Sunday 9-19 (last entry 18.30).

  • Where Via Castello, Montesarchio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: comune.montesarchio.bn.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it

La Torre

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Born as a watchtower and then bastion of the defensive walls of the village, it has the current appearance of the late fifteenth century, designed by Francesco di Giorgio Martini, with elements of the almond style attributed to the same architect.

  • Where Via Castello, Montesarchio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Rione Latovetere e Abbazia di San Nicola

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

A fortified area of the Lombard period that preserves the original topography, the eastern and western doors and one of the few churches of the eighth century; there stands the Abbey of St. Nicholas of the thirteenth century, with a altarpiece of Giovan Bernardo Lama and the statue of the saint.

  • Where Rione Latovetere, Montesarchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Rione Latonuovo e Santuario della SS. Trinità

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

A district of Norman times that preserves a gate and a stretch of walls; there is the Sanctuary of the SS. Trinity with the original statue depicting the mystery of the Trinity.

  • Where Rione Latonuovo, Montesarchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Borgo San Francesco

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Historical district gathered around the former Franciscan convent, now Town Hall, with a large cloister and a monumental baroque entrance; near stands the church of San Francesco, founded in the fourteenth century.

  • Where Borgo San Francesco, Montesarchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Piazza Umberto I

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The seventeenth-century Palazzo D'Avalos with the chapel of Our Lady of Purity and St. Leo the Great (today the parish) and the church of the Annunziata of the seventeenth century, with on the main altar a canvas donated by Cardinal Orsini, then Pope Benedict XIII; in the center the monumental fountain "Munt Ercole" built after the Unity.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Montesarchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
