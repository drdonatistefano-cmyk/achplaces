What to see in Ploaghe

The village is more than 400 meters on the slopes of Mount San Matteo, about 25 km from Sassari, linked to the scholar Giovanni Spano: the Spanish Quadreria with paintings from the fourteenth to the nineteenth century, the complex of San Pietro, the first monumental cemetery of the island and, in the countryside, the Romanesque churches of Salvennor and more than 70 nuragic sites.

Quadreria Spano

Museums · Old masters · Free entry

In the Oratory of the Rosary, Dominican building of the eighteenth century, 31 works of the collection of Giovanni Spano (1803-1878), donated to the church of San Pietro in 1873, including a San Domenico of 1340-1350 attributed to Francesco Neri of Volterra, a Madonna with Child and Saints attributed to Domenico of Michelino, the Stimitates of San Francesco attributed to Pietro Cavaro and a Holy Family of the Master of Ozieri; 29 other works are in the Rectoral House. Guided visits with the curator only, by telephone reservation.

  • Where Piazza San Pietro, Ploaghe
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Piazza e complesso di San Pietro

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

On the square are gathered the parish church of St. Peter the Apostle, former cathedral of the fifteenth century several times rebuilt, the oratory of St. Cross of the sixteenth century, the oratory of the Rosary of the eighteenth century and the parish house with the archive of ancient writings and documents; the town hall occupies the former Granatic mountain.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Cimitero monumentale

Places of worship · Monumental cemeteries · Free entry

The first cemetery built in Sardinia after the edict of Saint Cloud, not far from the center, with tombstones in Logudorian.

  • Entry Free

Chiesa di San Michele di Salvennor

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Built at the end of the 11th century at the initiative of the judge of Torres Mariano I in the village of Salvennor, abandoned in the 18th century, passed around 1139 to the Vallombrosan monks. T-shaped plant with three apses, brick masonry of limestone and black volcanic stone and close relation with the nearby Saccargia; remains a stretch of the portico that joined it to the disappeared monastery. The area is now close to industrial plants.

  • Where Località Salvennor, a circa 3 km da Ploaghe (bivio per Florinas dalla SS 131)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio di Salvennor

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Former parish church of the village of Salvennor, Romanesque in the first quarter of the 13th century according to the stylistic dating, only one apse, with bichrome limestone and volcanic stone masonry; two eighteenth-century structures hide its sides. It rises a short distance from San Michele.

  • Where SP 68, località Sant'Antonio, Ploaghe
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Siti nuragici: Nuraghe Don Micheli, tomba di giganti di Fiorosu, fonte sacra di Frades Mareos

History and architecture · Archaeology

The territory has more than 70 Nuragic settlements: the nuraghe Don Micheli, a 12-metre-diameter tower with finds from the 15th to the 9th century BC; the tomb of giants of Fiorosu with a 16-metre corridor covered by slabs; the sacred source of Frades Mareos, in blocks of trachyte, with paved and circular chamber in tholos on a vein of water.

  • Where Territorio di Ploaghe
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
