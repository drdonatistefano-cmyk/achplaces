What to see in Golferenzo

Medieval village of Oltrepò Pavese restored by private and municipality, with the Baroque parish of San Nicola, the palaces of the Belcredi marquises and the surviving tower of the castle destroyed in 1216; from the church square, the highest point of the village, the view reaches the Alps.

Nucleo medievale e piazza della Chiesa

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

A medieval nucleus with a labyrinth typical of the Apennine villages, carefully restored by private individuals and the municipality and considered one of the most beautiful centres of Oltrepò Pavese. From the square of the church, the highest point of the village, opens a wide view that on clear days has the Alps in the background.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa, Golferenzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Nicola da Bari

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Of probable Romanesque origins (XI-XII century), the current building was founded in the sixteenth century as a chapel of the castle and was enlarged in Baroque forms in the following century; neoclassical facade with frescoes of Santa Rita and San Francesco, Baroque interiors with precious marbles, seventeenth-century canvases in the choir and pulpit of the Napoleonic era. The parish is documented as early as 1336. The source indicates it open at the Mass on Saturday afternoon.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa, Golferenzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · in-lombardia.it

Torretta del Castello di Golferenzo

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The only surviving part of the castle destroyed in 1216 by the Piacenza troops, rebuilt and sold in 1691 to Barnaba Belcredi; the tower in local stone blocks, attributed to the 14th-XV century, has a window at the top with the ancient inferriata. Tradition has it that it has long been a prison: inside were found strains of torture. It is private property, visible only from the outside from the highway.

  • Where Via Circonvallazione 14, Golferenzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: comune.golferenzo.pv.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · in-lombardia.it

Palazzo Belcredi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Next to the parish church, in the most panoramic point of the village, rises on the foundations of the castle with local stone masonry; next to barn and stable the municipality has created a multifunctional space that also enhances the typical products. Opposite, on the other side of the street, the noble Quarone Palace.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa, Golferenzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesetta di San Mauro e sentiero botanico Pomorosso-Bosco Baccano

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

A hundred metres from the centre, the little church of San Mauro is the starting point of the 5 km botanical educational trail created with the students of the Istituto Comprensivo Valle Versa: it crosses the centuries-old forest of Baccano and reaches a natural lake fed by a spring near La Cà, near Casa Guastoni, on the border with Emilia-Romagna.

  • Where Via Brevio (strada di Cappo), Golferenzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it
