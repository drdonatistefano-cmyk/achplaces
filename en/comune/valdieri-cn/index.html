What to see in Valdieri

The gateway to the Maritime Alps Park in the Gesso Valley: the sulphurous thermal baths at 1,370 meters wanted by Vittorio Emanuele II, the Valderia botanical garden of the violet of Valdieri, the eccomuseum of rye in Sant'Anna and the necropolis of the Bronze and Iron Age.

6 places to visit in Valdieri, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parco naturale delle Alpi Marittime

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Regional park born in 1980 in the ancient Savoy hunting reserve, bordering on the French National Park of Mercantour: you can see the suede, symbol of the park, the ibex, the marmot, the royal eagle and the gipet. The headquarters is in Valdieri.

  • Where Piazza Regina Elena 30 (sede), Valdieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valdieri.cn.it

Sources: comune.valdieri.cn.it · visitpiemonte.com

Terme di Valdieri

Local life and traditions · Spas

Notes already in ancient times: in 1588 the Municipality built the first factories and on July 10, 1857 Vittorio Emanuele II laid the first stone of the present one, designed by Giuseppe Bollati, which at the end of the nineteenth century housed up to 600 people. Sulfurous waters at about 50 °C with algae used in care, thermal pools. At the foot of the Matto mountain at 1,370 meters, open from the end of May to September.

  • Where Località Terme (strada provinciale 239), Valdieri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valdieri.cn.it

Sources: comune.valdieri.cn.it

Giardino botanico alpino Valderia

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

More than 500 species of the Maritime Alps in 14 environments, at the confluence of the Gesso della Valletta and Valasco streams. It takes its name from the Valderia Viola, endemic, discovered here by Carlo Allioni in 1780: created by the park in 1990, it is the only alpine garden that contains the place of discovery of the species; the nineteenth-century paths are those of the "Giardino degli Inglesi" of the guests of the spa. Open in summer (in 2022 from 11 June to 12 September, 5 €).

  • Where Terme di Valdieri, Valdieri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valdieri.cn.it

Sources: areeprotettealpimarittime.it · comune.valdieri.cn.it

Museo e parco archeologico della necropoli

History and architecture · Archaeology

Necropolis discovered in 1983 by extending the municipal road of Ripe, with burials from the Bronze Age (1350-1200 BC) to the Iron Age (about 625-475 BC); in the urn museum and urns in bronze and bone. Archaeological area open in spring and summer, museum by reservation.

  • Where Via Guardia alla Frontiera (area); Piazza della Resistenza 28 (museo), Valdieri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valdieri.cn.it

Sources: comune.valdieri.cn.it

Museo etnografico degli antichi mestieri

Museums · Themed

Small museum of ancient mountain crafts in the Andonno village, with rooms rebuilt on three levels that tell the daily life of the past centuries. Visits by reservation.
