What to see in Val Masino

Granite Valley of the Retica Alps dominated by Monte Disgrazia (3,678 m) and Pizzo Badile, a climbing and trekking destination: here you will find the Natural Reserve of the Val di Mello, the Sasso Remenno, the ancient thermal baths of the Bagni di Masino and the Sentiero Roma.

6 places to visit in Val Masino, in the province of Sondrio (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Riserva naturale della Val di Mello

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Reserve established in 2009, indicated as the largest protected area of Lombardy: an almost flat valley floor between granite walls, suitable for all, with the Qualico pond shortly after the entrance and trails towards the Cascina Piana (1,092 m), the Playa di Rasica and the Alpe Pioda. You enter on foot from San Martino (parking space about 20 minutes; in the parking lot with charge 7 euros per day), with a summer shuttle to Panscèr.

  • Where San Martino, Val Masino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: valtellina.it · italia.it

Bidet della Contessa (Bùrgùn)

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural pool of transparent waters between granite walls, in the Mello Valley: easy path of about an hour and a half with 150 meters of altitude difference, from spring to autumn.

  • Where Val di Mello, Val Masino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: valtellina.it · italia.it

Sasso Remenno

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Granite mass is the largest erratic rock in Europe, today a climbing and bouldering gym.

  • Where Località Zocca-Filorera, Val Masino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it

Bagni di Masino e Bosco dei Bagni

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails

Ancient thermal baths known since ancient times, with hot, alkaline and radioactive water. From the locality Bregolana a network of trails and old mule tracks crosses the Wood of the Bagni, recommended in spring and autumn, with the sources to be visited all year round and a sensory path realized by ERSAF with the Union blind and visually impaired of Lombardy.

  • Where Bagni di Masino, Val Masino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it

Sentiero Roma e Monte Disgrazia

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The valley, which starts from 787 meters, opens towards the side valleys of the Bagni, Porcellizzo and Sasso Bisolo-Preda Rossa, at the foot of Monte Disgrazia (3.678 m), with the Pizzo Badile classic climbing destination; the Sentiero Roma is indicated as one of the most beautiful trekking in the Alps.

  • Where Val Masino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Museo vallivo «La Scoperta della Val Masino»

Museums · Themed

Born at the end of the seventies in the former primary school of San Martino and renovated in 2018 by the City with volunteers, it has four sections: historical-alpinistica (from the thermal waters of the Renaissance to the Alpine Rescue), naturalistica with diorama, mineralogical and an ethnographic section with the interior of a peasant house of the early twentieth century and agricultural tools and craftsmen. Open in summer and on the main holidays.

  • Where Via Vanoni 45, San Martino, Val Masino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: valmasino.info · italia.it
