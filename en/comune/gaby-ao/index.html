What to see in Gaby

Alpine village at 1047 meters between rascards and strong stone houses, dear to Carducci who wrote to you "The hostess of Gaby" .

4 places to visit in Gaby, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Gaby in the guide with map and filters

Santuario di Vourry

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Dedicated to the Madonna delle Grazie, a place of pilgrimage with the Way of the Cross with pylons arranged in a triangle upstream, a small imitation of the Sacred Mount of Varallo.

  • Where Vourry, Gaby
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Cascata del Niel

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

30 metres of the Niel creek jump, which can be reached on foot from the village.

  • Where Niel, Gaby
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built after the decree of 1786 that gave Gaby a parish autonomous from Issime.

  • Where Gaby
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Rascards e case forti

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Typical architecture with rascards in wood of Franco-Provençal origin and strong houses entirely in stone.

  • Where Gaby
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it
