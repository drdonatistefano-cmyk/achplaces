What to see in Comunanza

A village perched on the Aso valley near the Parco dei Monti Sibillini, known as the village of longevity, due to the number of centenarians: overhanging houses on the river with walls and bridge, the church of Santa Maria a Terme of the IX century on the remains of a pagan temple, St. Francis and the Museum of sacred art in Palazzo Pascali.

Centro storico, ponte e case sull'Aso

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Picturesque houses built to overlook the river Aso, with the walls and an access bridge; the center, perhaps ancient Roman settlement, was repopulated in the fourteenth century. On December 26 there is the living nativity scene and in the village there is the Mazzumaja, a three-day feast around the crazy thrush.

Chiesa di Santa Maria a Terme e Colle Terme

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

9th century church in sandstone with richly decorated facade, built on the remains of a pagan temple (dedicated to Dionysus according to italia.it) of the lost Roman settlement of Interamnia Poletina Piceni. On the nearby Colle Terme archaeological excavations have brought to light thermal plants of Roman age.

Museo di arte sacra (Musei Sistini del Piceno)

Museums · Old masters

In Palazzo Pascali: a temple reliquary, a processional cross of the eighteenth century with a fourteenth-century Christ attributed to the goldsmith Pietro Vannini, a Madonna of the Cincinola of the seventeenth century and a San Liborio by Giuseppe Ghezzi (1634-1721), painter born in Comunanza and active in Rome.
