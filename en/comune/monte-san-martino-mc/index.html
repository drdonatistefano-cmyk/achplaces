What to see in Monte San Martino

Borgo on a spur at 603 meters on the valley of the Tenna, in front of the Sibillini: in the church of San Martino Bishop three polyptychs of the Crivelli brothers.

Chiesa di San Martino Vescovo e polittici dei Crivelli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church founded in 1218 and rebuilt after the earthquake of 1741, with the convent on three sides: it preserves three polyptychs of the Crivelli brothers and works of Jerome of Giovanni da Camerino.

Panorama sui Sibillini e sulla valle del Tenna

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint
