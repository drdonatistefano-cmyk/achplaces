What to see in Maenza

Medieval village of the Lepini Mountains between Mount Calvello and the valley of the Amaseno, about 116 km from Rome, dominated by the Baronial Castle where in 1274 St. Thomas Aquinas stayed and which today houses the Civic Museum of the landscape; around, Piazza Duomo with the monument to the saint, the church of Santa Maria Assunta, Santa Reparata and the birth palace of Pope Leo XIII.

Castello Baronale e Museo civico del paesaggio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Local stone castle, 360 m above sea level, overlooking the village and the valley of mid-August: developed from the thousand in forms from Romanesque to Baroque, belonged to Da Ceccano, De Cabannis and Caetani; purchased from the Province of Latina in the mid-1960s, was restored until 2001. Almost quadrangular plant on four levels, three quadrangular towers and a semicircular one, Guelph merlons, Gothic portal and Renaissance fireplaces; the restoration has recovered ten panels of grotesque frescoes, and on the third level there is the Sala di San Tommaso, which stopped sick there in 1274. It is the seat of the Museum of the landscape.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Maenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it · visitlazio.com

Piazza Duomo e monumento a San Tommaso

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Under the castle, the square hosts the sculpture in travertine and bronze by Gabriele Jagnocco inaugurated in 2011, dedicated to St. Thomas and the miracle of herring, with its symbols: herring, olive branch, books and the profile of the castle.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Maenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta in Cielo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Next to the castle, in a complex wanted by Cristoforo Caetani in 1400, it preserves the fresco of Our Lady of the Cerase.

Centro storico, Palazzo Pecci e Loggia dei Mercanti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Along the streets of the medieval village there are tower houses and the ancient Loggia dei Mercanti; near Porta Maggiore stands the palace of Gioacchino Pecci, home of the future Pope Leo XIII and today seat of the Town Hall.

  • Where Piazza Leone XIII, Maenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
