What to see in Sacile

Sacile has the historic center on two islands of the river Livenza, with Piazza del Popolo and the loggia of the fifteenth-century town hall, Palazzo Ragazzoni and the Cathedral of San Nicolò with the frescoes of Pino Casarini, whose works are exhibited in the nearby gallery dedicated to him.

Centro storico e Piazza del Popolo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town is located on two islands of the Livenza, with buildings mirroring in the water. The main square, Piazza del Popolo, has the loggia of the fifteenth-century town hall and a row of palaces-fondico of the early seventeenth century, of Venetian taste with Treviso influences, where the owner families traded agricultural products.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Sacile
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it

Palazzo Ragazzoni

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The sixteenth-century palace, symbol of Sacile and its link with the Serenissima: it was the home of the Venetian Ragazzoni, owners and bankers, and together the point of collection of the production of their land properties. It is reached through a canal and is decorated with frescoes by a pupil of Paolo Veronese.

  • Where Via Cavour, Sacile
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it

Duomo di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Renaissance cathedral with the bell tower, flanked by Palazzo Carli and Palazzo Ovio Gobbi from the second half of the sixteenth century, built a few decades after the church. It is known for the portal and the great twentieth century frescoes of the presbytery of the Veronese Pino Casarini; among the oldest paintings a Madonna of the Rosary of Palma il Giovane and a Madonna with Saints by Francesco Bassano, as well as works by Andrea Vicentino.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Sacile
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it

Galleria d'arte moderna Pino Casarini

Museums · Modern art

He exhibited the works of the Veronese painter Pino Casarini, who donated his collection to the parish of Sacile in two films (1982 and 1988): paintings, drawings and sketches for the settings of the Verona Arena, covering about fifty years of activity, from 1920 to 1970. Open on the first and third Sundays of the month 10-12; on the other days on request, with mandatory booking (tel. 0434 780702).

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Sacile
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches
