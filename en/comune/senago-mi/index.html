What to see in Senago

The municipality of the Groane Park is known for its delightful villas: Villa San Carlo Borromeo, where Cardinal Federico Borromeo welcomed seminarians and theologians during the plague of 1630, Villa Corbella-Martinelli-Sioli, former summer residence of the archbishops of Milan, and Villa Verzolo-Monzini with its English park.

3 places to visit in Senago, in the province of Milan (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa San Carlo Borromeo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Purchased in the seventeenth century by Cardinal Federico Borromeo, who hosted seminarians and theologians during the plague of 1630 and died in 1631; Gilberto IV Borromeo, then bishop of Novara and cardinal, designed the renovation, initially entrusted to Filippo Cagnola and completed in the twentieth century by architects Fausto and Giuseppe Bagatti Valsecchi. Today it hosts the International University of the Second Renaissance; how to visit the museum not indicated in the reading sources.

Villa Corbella-Martinelli-Sioli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

It belonged to the Corbella family (in the cadastral maps of the 1820s it was found by Ludovico Corbella), passed to the Martinelli family, who gave it to the archbishops of Milan: since 1820 it was their summer residence. After the laws of 1866 it was sold at auction to Angelo Sioli; acquired and restored by the municipality, it houses municipal offices, a conference room and an exhibition room.

  • Where Via San Bernardo 7, Senago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.senago.mi.it

Sources: comune.senago.mi.it

Villa Verzolo-Monzini e parco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Built by the Monzini family in the mid-18th century with agricultural function, it was transformed into a villa with frescoed ceilings around 1850, on the occasion of a family wedding, and enlarged at the beginning of the twentieth century. The municipal property is home to the Italo Calvino Library; the english park that surrounds it is open to the public.

  • Where Senago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.senago.mi.it

Sources: comune.senago.mi.it · in-lombardia.it
