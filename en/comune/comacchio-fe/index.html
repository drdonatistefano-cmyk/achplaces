What to see in Comacchio

The "small Venice" of the Po Delta: canals, Trepponti, the valleys by boat, the eel and seven beaches on the Adriatic.

7 places to visit in Comacchio, in the province of Ferrara (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers 4 of the 7 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Open Comacchio in the guide with map and filters

Centro storico, canali e Trepponti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lagoon city of the canals and bridges, with the symbol Treppanti, the Cathedral of San Cassiano, the Loggia del Grano and the Clock Tower.

  • Where Centro storico, Comacchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Loggiato dei Cappuccini

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The loggia with 143 arches that leads to the sanctuary, indicated as the longest in Italy.

  • Where Comacchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo Delta Antico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the Hospital of the Infirms of the eighteenth century, the story of the delta from prehistory to the Middle Ages and the entire cargo of a Roman ship found near Comacchio.

  • Where Via Agatopisto 3, Comacchio
  • Entry In the Comacchio Museum Pass (€17 full, €14 reduced, with boat tour in the valleys); free on the first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodeltaantico.com
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: museodeltaantico.com · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · podeltatourism.it

Manifattura dei Marinati

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Paid entry

The ancient eel factory, with the Sala dei Fuochi and its 12 chimneys; in autumn we return to cook and marinate the fish.

  • Where Corso Mazzini 200, Comacchio
  • Entry In the Comacchio Museum Pass
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.parcodeltapo.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · podeltatourism.it

Valli di Comacchio in barca da Stazione Foce

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The large brackish lagoons of the Po Delta Park, with fishing houses and flamingos, to visit by boat or by bike on the banks.

  • Where Stazione Foce, Valli di Comacchio
  • Entry Excursions for a fee or with the Comacchio Museum Pass
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website emiliaromagnaturismo.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · podeltatourism.it

Casa Museo Remo Brindisi

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Artist's villa designed by Nanda Vigo in the early 1970s, with works by Modigliani, Picasso, Fontana, Dalí, De Chirico, De Pisis and Morandi.

  • Where Lido di Spina, Comacchio
  • Entry In the Comacchio Museum Pass
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website emiliaromagnaturismo.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · podeltatourism.it

Lidi di Comacchio

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Seven seaside resorts, from Lido di Volano to Lido di Spina, for years with the Blue Flag.

  • Where Lidi di Comacchio
  • Entry Free beaches; establishments with fee
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
