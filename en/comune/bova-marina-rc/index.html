What to see in Bova Marina

The center of the southern Ionian coast, in the Greek area of Aspromonte, with sandy beaches and pebbles between palm trees and bergamots; it preserves in the Archaeological Park the remains of a synagogue of the IV-VI centuries, one of the oldest in the West.

Museo e Parco archeologico Archeoderi e sinagoga

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

The park surrounds the remains of a synagogue excavated in the 1980s and used between the 4th and 6th centuries AD, the only architectural evidence of the Jewish presence in Calabria at that time and, according to the MiC, the oldest in the West after that of Ostia; the mosaic of the prayer room depicts menorah, palm, cedar and shofar. The museum exhibits the mosaic, a treasure of 3,079 bronze coins in a jug (IV-V century) and a millionaire of 364-367 A.D. Open from Tuesday to Sunday 8.30-14, Monday closed.

  • Where Località San Pasquale, Bova Marina
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Spiaggia di Bova Marina

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Very wide beach of sand and pebbles, surrounded by palm trees and bergamot, from which you can see the slopes of Etna.

  • Where Lungomare, Bova Marina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
