What to see in Castrovillari

The largest center of the Pollino National Park, in the King's Conca: the Aragonese castle, the medieval quarter of the Cìvita and the Franciscan protoconvento of 1220, home of museum and theatre.

Protoconvento Francescano

Places of worship · Monumental complexes
