What to see in Bari

Apulian capital and "Porta d'Oriente": the old town with the Basilica of San Nicola and the Norman-Svevo Castle, the seafront among the most beautiful in Italy and the hamlet of the 19th century.

32 places to visit in Bari, in the province of Bari (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Bari Vecchia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The San Nicola district on the peninsula between the two ports, inside the ancient walls: Byzantine plant, buildings from the 11th to the 17th century, 40 churches and 120 votive newsstands; requalified in 2005, today it is also the heart of the Bari movida.

  • Where Bari Vecchia, Bari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it

Basilica di San Nicola

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Masterpiece of the Romanesque Apulian built between 1087 and 1100 on the site of the residence of the Byzantine catapan: in the crypt the relics of St. Nicholas, the chair of the abbot Elijah, the silver altar and the sixteenth-century mausoleum of Bona Sforza; also a destination for Orthodox pilgrims.

  • Where Largo Abate Elia 13, Bari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello Normanno-Svevo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

The fortress surrounded by the moat built by Frederick II in the 13th century on a Norman fortification, then a Renaissance court led by two women; prison and barracks under the Bourbons, is a museum since 2017 with exhibition halls and the collection of ceramics of the "butt."

  • Where Piazza Federico II di Svevia, Bari
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
