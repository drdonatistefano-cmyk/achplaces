What to see in Castelvetrano

Trapanese city in whose territory extends the archaeological park of Selinunte, Greek colony founded by the Megaresi in the VII century BC and destroyed by the Carthaginians in 409 BC, with the Doric temples of the Eastern Hill and the Acropolis; in the city the church of San Domenico, known as the Sistine of Sicily, the Mother Church, the Fountain of the Ninfa, the Civic Museum with the Ephebo and, little outside, the Trinity of Delia.

Selinunte, Collina orientale e templi E, F, G

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the eastern hill rise the E temple, dedicated to Hera, of the middle of the fifth century BC and largely reassembled in the 1950s and 1960s; the F temple of the middle of the sixth century BC, perhaps dedicated to Dionysus; and the G temple, begun around 530 BC and never ended up destroying the city, among the greatest in the Greek world. The park, which the Region presents as the largest in Europe with 270 hectares, includes acropolis, necropolis and three hills.

  • Where Piazzale Iole Bovio Marconi 1, Marinella di Selinunte, Castelvetrano
  • Entry €14, reduced €7
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.visitsicily.info
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Selinunte, Acropoli e Porta Nord

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The only fortified part of the city, seat of political, religious and military functions: there rise the temple C, begun around 560 BC and attributed to Apollo, among the oldest known Doric temples, the temple D dedicated to Athena and the temples A and O. At temple D the North Gate opens, with sections of the Hellenistic walls reinforced after 409 BC under Hermocrates.

  • Where Parco archeologico di Selinunte, Castelvetrano
  • Entry €14, reduced €7
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.visitsicily.info
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Selinunte, Santuario della Malophoros

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

In the western sacred area of the Gaggera, it was one of the first places of worship of the colony, with a first sacellus of the end of the VII century BC and monumental propylenes of the middle of the V; next, the shrines of Zeus Meilichios and Ecate and the sacellus of Hera Matronale. Thousands of votive statues have been found here.

  • Where Contrada Gaggera, Parco archeologico di Selinunte, Castelvetrano
  • Entry €14, reduced €7
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.visitsicily.info
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Chiesa di San Domenico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Mannerist church wanted in 1470 by the Tagliavias as a place of worship and family mausoleum and completed in the late sixteenth century; called "Sistina di Sicilia" for stuccoes, frescoes, statues and funeral monuments, with the decorations of Antonino Ferraro da Giuliano and the Tree of Jesse in stucco over the arch of the choir. Restored for a long time after the earthquake of Belice.

  • Where Piazza Regina Margherita 47, Castelvetrano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Fontana della Ninfa

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Baroque fountain 10 meters high with four orders of tanks, crowned by a nymph that holds a bottle from which the water flows; the Castelvetranesi call it "Ninfuzza di li cannola."
