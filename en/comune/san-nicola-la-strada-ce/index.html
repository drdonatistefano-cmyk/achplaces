What to see in San Nicola la Strada

At the gates of Caserta, along the ancient Appia street, the Bourbon complex of the Real Convitto di Santa Maria delle Grazie houses the Museum of Rural Civilization; not far away is the eighteenth-century church of Santa Maria degli Angeli, built with the tuff of the quarries of the Reggia.

Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Set up in the approximately 500 square meters of the Borbonico Salone del Real Convitto di Santa Maria delle Grazie, next to the Vanvitellian style church on the ancient Via Appia, it tells the rural life from the early twentieth century to the mid-century in four areas: the fields, with a large mangano and the macennole for hemp; the road, with the tools of the carpenters, blacksmith and cantiere shops; the courtyard, with the well, the vinnolo and the catalog; the house, with kitchen and bedroom reconstructed. In the hall there is also a permanent municipal nativity scene of 2003; since 2009 it is a museum of regional interest and is free of architectural barriers.

Chiesa di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Started in 1748 on a project by Pietro Bernasconi, with the works entrusted to Carlo Patturelli, and finished in 1791 after several interruptions; the tufa of the parts above ground comes from the local quarries used also for the Palace of Caserta. It develops on two levels: at the top the cult hall, which is accessed by a large churchyard fenced above the road; under the moat, ancient cemetery and ossuary dug into the tuff, with two rooms sometimes on rows of pillars. The façade, about 30 meters by 32, is marked by semi-colons and pilasters with ionic capitals and Corinthians.
