What to see in Valsinni

Borgo Bandiera Arancione del Touring, once called Favale, dominated by the castle of Morra on the gorge of Sinni, where in the sixteenth century lived the poetess Isabella Morra; to her is dedicated since 1993 one of the first Italian literary parks, while on Mount Coppolo remain the walls of the ancient Lagaria.

Castello dei Morra

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The main monument of the village dominates the Sinni gorge: of Aragonese aspect, existed already in the Lombard period. In the first half of the sixteenth century there lived the poetess Isabella Morra, daughter of the feudalist, reclused by the brothers Decius, Fabio and Caesar; today it hosts the exhibitions of the Summer of Isabella, which in August animates the village with poetic itineraries, theatrical shows and young people in costume.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Centro storico e Parco Letterario Isabella Morra

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The medieval village at the foot of the castle is a network of narrow alleys connected by passages covered in vault called \"gafii" with the bust of Isabella Morra. The Literary Park, inaugurated in 1993 and among the first in Italy, offers itineraries that use poetry to read the territory, with guided tours and exhibitions; the Visitors Center is in Piazza Carmine 20.

  • Where Piazza Carmine 20, Valsinni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it · basilicataturistica.it

Chiesa Madre di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The main church of the ancient village, dedicated to the Assumption and frescoed. It is believed that Isabella Morra is buried in the basement: in 1928 Benedict Cross had excavated there to search for her tomb, without success.

  • Where Valsinni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Monte Coppolo e mura di Lagaria

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the mountain above the village remain the walls of the ancient Lagaria, a city that legend wants founded by Epeo, builder of the Trojan horse; the ruins of the acropolis are arranged in staggered rows, with a source of water. The mountain is part of the places of the Literary Park.

  • Where Monte Coppolo, Valsinni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · basilicataturistica.it · touringclub.it
