What to see in Bocchigliero

Country of the Greek Sila from the historical center of medieval plant, with houses leaning and narrow streets: the church of Santa Maria Assunta in Piazza del Popolo, inaugurated in 1612, the Museum of peasant civilization in the former City Market and the Monument to the emigrants.

4 places to visit in Bocchigliero, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Started in 1598 and inaugurated in 1612, with a Latin plan with three naves on stone pillars; 1818 central altar, tombs with barrel vault and Sacramento Chapel with stucco dome and frescoes in the lunettes.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Bocchigliero
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fondoambiente.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

It preserves, although modified over the centuries, a medieval structure with lean houses and narrow streets designed for defence; Piazza del Popolo was enlarged by demolishing the old Palazzo Gallo. Among the churches of San Rocco and, in the locality of the Reformation, the Madonna de Jesu next to the former Monks Convent.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Bocchigliero
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.bocchigliero.cs.it

Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

In the former Municipal Market, it collects ancient objects and tools, some by now forgotten, which have allowed to make documentaries on the customs and traditions of the community.

  • Where Ex Mercato comunale, Bocchigliero
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page parcosila.it

Sources: parcosila.it

Monumento agli emigrati

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Dedicated in 1983 by the Municipality to migrants, the work of the architect Mario Montalto; the country, more than 4,000 inhabitants in 1960, has today about 2,000 of them after the waves migrating to the Americas and Europe.

  • Where Bocchigliero
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.bocchigliero.cs.it
