What to see in Minturno

The hilly village of Aurorno and Roman origins on the border with Campania, above the sea hamlets of Scauri and Marina di Minturno; on the Garigliano you can visit the Roman colony of Minturnae, crossed by the Appia and part of the UNESCO site Via Appia. Regina Viarum and Ponte Real Ferdinando, the first iron chain bridge in Italy (1832).

Parco archeologico e Antiquarium di Minturnae

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Roman maritime colony founded in 296 BC on the right bank of Liri-Garigliano, with Appia as the maximum decuman. You can visit the republican forum (II century BC) with the Capitolium and the theatre, the imperial forum with remains of curia and basilica, the macellum and the baths with mosaics, in the aspect of the city of Ariane. Since 1984 the Antiquarium, in the ambulances of the theater, exhibits the "gallery of the statues" with Augustus, Livia and the Muse, the lapidarium and a series of togati. Closed Monday and Tuesday; free on the first Sunday of the month; Cassino-Minturno €8 integrated ticket and cumulative of the state sites of the province of Latina €12.

  • Entry €5, reduced €2 (18-25 years)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Ponte Real Ferdinando

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

On the Garigliano, on the border between Lazio and Campania, it was commissioned by Francesco I and completed by Ferdinando II of Bourbon on a project by the engineer Luigi Jura: completed in 1832, it is the first iron chain bridge suspended in Italy and among the first in Europe. Theatre of a clash on October 29, 1860, was blown up by the German spoilers on October 14, 1943; today it is entirely restored.

Visitable only on occasion of extraordinary openings (calendar on the site of the Regional Museums Directorate)

Castello ducale Caracciolo Carafa

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Considered of the IX century and renovated over the centuries, it has a cylindrical tower, a round walkway and an internal portico with ogival arches; a staircase leads to the Sala dei Baroni. Among the guests are St. Thomas Aquinas, Isabella Colonna and Giulia Gonzaga. Visitable by appointment.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Minturno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Centro storico e chiese

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The historical centre of medieval origin preserves the church of San Pietro Apostolo, among the most valuable buildings, the church of San Francesco built by the Caetani in 1363, and the church of the Annunziata of the XIV century. In July the Festival of the Kingdoms, in honor of the Virgin of Graces, recalls the ancient feast of harvest.

  • Where Centro storico, Minturno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Villa di Marco Emilio Scauro e Torre dei Molini

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

In Scauri, which takes its name from the consul Marco Emilio Scauro (162-90 BC), remains of the maritime villa a double arched portal, a corridor with parietal paintings of the third Pompeian style and seven blind arches of the II-I century BC; the villa produced wine for export. The fourteenth-century Torre dei Molini, today space for events, is visited when it is open. The complex is part of the Latium Historical Houses Network.

  • Where Scauri, Minturno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Area di Gianola e Monte di Scauri

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Protected area with paths leading to the remains of Pirae, city of the Ausoni, and to the Roman villa of the consul Marco Emilio Scauro; the seat of the Regional Park Ulysses Riviera is in the former Sieci furnace of Scauri, example of industrial archaeology abandoned in 1982. From 2025 on the Golden Mountain a Great Bench looks at the Gulf of Gaeta.

  • Where Scauri, Minturno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
