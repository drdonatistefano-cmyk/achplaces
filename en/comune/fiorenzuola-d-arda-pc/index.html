What to see in Fiorenzuola d'Arda

The centre of the Piacenza plain was born at the crossroads between Via Emilia and the Arda valley, with the system aligned to Corso Garibaldi on the path of the Roman consular; the main monument is the Gothic Collegiate Church of San Fiorenzo, next to the Teatro Verdi in an ancient abbey and the former convent of San Giovanni.

Collegiata di San Fiorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church in Lombard Gothic style started in the seventies of the thirteenth century on Roman and Lombard structures and completed at the end of the fifteenth century, with chapels added in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries; the place welcomed in the early Middle Ages the cult of the pilgrim Fiorenzo, then bishop of Orange. In the apse cycles of frescoes by Lombard painters between the late 15th and early 16th centuries with Crucifixion and stories of the saint; the main altar is designed by Giovanni Paolo Panini (1741), and on the altars the Miracle of San Fiorenzo by Marco Benefial (1741) and a Madonna with San Bernardo by Giulio Cesare Procaccini. Open every day 7-12 and 15-19, entrance from the side door.

  • Where Piazza Fratelli Molinari 21, Fiorenzuola d'Arda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.fiorenzuola.pc.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Teatro Comunale Giuseppe Verdi

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Theatre housed in an ancient abbey renovated from 1847 on a project by G. Perreau, with decorations by the painter Bertolotti; inaugurated on October 8, 1853 with the Attila di Verdi, was named after the composer in 1901 and reopened in 2006 after decades of closure. It has horseshoe stalls, three orders of stages and loggia, for 330 seats; it is visited during the theater season.

  • Where Via della Liberazione, Fiorenzuola d'Arda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.fiorenzuola.pc.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Ex convento di San Giovanni

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The Reformed Cistercian monks began at the beginning of the seventeenth century the present building, on the site of a church and houses of the Humiliates of the 14th-XV century; cast out the monks from the French in 1805, it was hospital, button factory, prison, barracks and craft workshop. Today it houses the Town Hall, the civic library and, in the cellars, the municipal historical archive.

  • Where Piazzale San Giovanni 2, Fiorenzuola d'Arda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.fiorenzuola.pc.it · italia.it

Chiesa della Beata Vergine di Caravaggio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built between 1722 and 1749 by the Confraternita degli Agonizzazioni (or St. Joseph), with a bell tower of 1804; it was named ducal oratory by Don Ferdinando di Borbone, confirmed by Maria Luigia.

  • Where Via Melchiorre Gioia 7, Fiorenzuola d'Arda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.fiorenzuola.pc.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Santuario della Madonna del Moronasco

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In the 4th century there was a small oratory, in 1377 linked to the monastery of Chiaravalle della Colomba; the current Baroque building was started in 1665. It preserves a Nativity of the sixteenth century and, in the last chapel on the right, the oldest part, a fresco probably thirteenth century with the Madonna enthroned with the Child. Visitable on agreement taken in advance with the culture office.

  • Where Strada del Moronasco, Fiorenzuola d'Arda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.fiorenzuola.pc.it · visitpiacenza.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Baselica Duce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of probable Lombard foundation, originally dedicated to San Salvatore and from the fifteenth century to the Saints Felice and Tranquillino; in the seventeenth century it was renovated and oriented with the façade to the east. Behind the main altar original frescoes from 1652 by the painter Salvatore Porro.

  • Where Via San Felice, Baselica Duce, Fiorenzuola d'Arda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.fiorenzuola.pc.it · italia.it

Centro storico e Palazzo Grossi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center is aligned with Corso Garibaldi, which follows the path of the Roman consular, and perpendicular to the stream Arda. Palazzo Grossi, private and non-visitable, shows a Renaissance frieze in terracotta and two ogival windows; private also Palazzo Bertamini Lucca.

  • Where Corso Garibaldi, Fiorenzuola d'Arda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it
