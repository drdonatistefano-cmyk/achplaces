What to see in Tricarico

Borgo on a limestone spur above the valley of the Basento, recognizable from a distance for the Norman tower 27 meters high; it preserves the ancient districts of Rabatana and Saracena, the cathedral wanted by Robert Guiscardo, the Doge's Palace and the monasteries frescoed by Pietro Antonio Ferro. The writer Rocco Scotellaro was born in 1923.

Torre normanna

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

It rises between the IX and X century as castle fortress and completed in Norman times, it is 27 meters high, with walls more than 5 meters thick on 4 levels. In 1333 the castle was donated to the Clarisses for the monastery of Santa Chiara; the tower maintained military function until 1657 when Duke Ippolito Revertera donated it to the monastery. National monument since 1931.

Convento di Santa Chiara e cappella del Crocifisso

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The convent of the Clarisse rises in the upper part of the village; it is accessed by passing the chapel of the Crucifix, of the twelfth century, entirely frescoed in the seventeenth century by Pietro Antonio Ferro, painter of Ferrandina.

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built in the 11th century at the behest of Robert Guiscardo and enlarged over the centuries, with two large supporting arches added to the façade in the 17th century. The interior with three naves with chapels preserves the relics of Saint Potito martyr and Bishop Raffaello Delle Nocche; the bell tower preserves a bifora of the thirteenth century.

  • Where Via Vittorio Veneto 33, Tricarico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.tricarico.mt.it · italia.it

Palazzo Ducale e museo archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

House of the accounts after the donation of the castle to the Clarisse, it has the layout of the first fourteenth century and appearance of a castle with turrite belt; from 1631 residence of the Reverèra, who in the eighteenth century had a wooden ceiling painted with scenes of the ...Jerusalem liberated. It hosts the state exhibition centre, the Museum of the Territory, on archaeological research in the Basento valley: from prehistoric to classical times, fortified Lucanian settlements and Roman rustic villas.

Porta e Torre Rabatana

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

According to the tradition reported by the municipality, the gate and tower were built by the Saracens who arrived around 980, who closed with walls and fortresses the lower part of the country forcing the inhabitants to work; the tower, very solid and with small doors hidden for the sortites, watched for centuries the neighborhood with a stable military garrison.

  • Where Via Badia 48, Tricarico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.tricarico.mt.it · italia.it

Porta e Torre Saracena

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

It dates back to the tenth century: a round arch, flanked in the following centuries by a smaller opening, gave access to an Arab fort with walls and tower, which continued to defend this part of the city under Byzantines and Normans.

Arco di Re Ladislao

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

It has one of the oldest doors, the Old Door, already marked in a 1605 print; the current forms date back to changes between the second half of the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries, and on the vault of the subjectic there is the coat of arms of Bishop Antonio Caprioli, dated 1557.

Chiesa e convento di Sant'Antonio di Padova

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Monastery of the Friars Minor observing authorized in 1479 by Sixtus IV at the request of Geronimo Sanseverino and completed in 1491; on the facade the oldest known representation of the city coat of arms. The quadriportico was frescoed in 1647-1650 by the painter Giuseppe Sciarra from Tritrica; today it is the mother house of the Disciples of the Eucharistic Jesus, with a small museum dedicated to Bishop Raffaello Delle Nocche.

  • Where Piazza San Pio X 1, Tricarico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa e convento di Santa Maria del Carmine

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Convent of the barefoot Carmelites founded in 1605 in an area rich in water and green. In 1612 Pietro Antonio Ferro, an exponent of Mannerism and one of the most representative artists of the seventeenth-century Lucan, frescoed the vault of the apse with stories of Christ and Our Lady, saints, prophets and virtues; the cloister is frescoed by his sons Carlo and Giovan Battista.

Santuario di Santa Maria di Fonti

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Church of the XII-XIII centuries attributed to the master Sarolo of Muro Lucano, cited in a bull of Innocent III and entrusted in 1444 to the chapter of the cathedral; rebuilt from 1624 by bishop Pier Luigi Carafa, whose coat of arms overlooks the portal in terracotta. At the main altar a mural painting of Byzantine taste with the Madonna and Child; it is among the main Marian places of the region, a destination of pilgrimages especially in May.

  • Where SP38, Tricarico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
