What to see in Elva

Small village of the Val Maira at 1,637 meters, reachable from Stroppo or from the hill of Sampeyre: in the village Serre the parish church of Santa Maria Assunta keeps the frescoes of 1493 by Hans Clemer, the "Maestro d'Elva" and the Casa della Meridiana hosts the museum of the guinea pigs, the hair collectors; just above, the Fremo Cuncunà dominates the valley.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta e affreschi di Hans Clemer

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the 13th century and enlarged in the 15th century, it has a late-Romanesque structure that opens to Gothic influences, especially in sculptures, and closes the panoramic square of hamlet Serre. In 1493 the Flemish painter Hans Clemer, active in the valleys of the Marchesato di Saluzzo, frescoed presbytery and apse with scenes of the life of Mary and the infancy of Christ and a great Crucifixion, which earned him the name of ...Master of Elva. Open all year round; if closed, the keys are at the Locanda San Pancrazio (tel. 335 5736255).

  • Where Borgata Serre 3, Elva
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Museo dei Caviè (Museo di Pels)

Museums · Themed

It is located in the House of Meridiana, an example of Alpine architecture so called for the solar clock painted on the south façade. It reconstructs the phases of hair processing, a trade that was widespread in Elva in the second half of the nineteenth century: the hair, collected in exchange for little money or for fabrics and scarfs, were worked in the country to make wigs sold also in England and in the rest of Europe. Displays testimonials, photographs and commercial documents; opening to be checked with the Ecomuseum (tel. 340 9846508).

  • Where Casa della Meridiana, Borgata Serre, Elva
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Fremo Cuncunà

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Rocky spur overlooking the Vallone di Elva, a must for hikers and a point for spectacular photographs. You can reach in about 45 minutes walk from the square of Elva, or in about 10 minutes from the refuge La Sousto dal Col.

  • Where Vallone di Elva
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
