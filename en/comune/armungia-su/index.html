What to see in Armungia

The village of the museums in the hills of the Gerrei, overlooking the valley of the Flumendosa and born around a nuraghe that rises in the middle of the houses; it is the birthplace of Emilio Lussu, remembered by a historical museum and the murals.

5 places to visit in Armungia, in the province of Sud Sardegna (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Nuraghe Armungia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Monotorre del Bronzo medio (XV-XIV century B.C.) in the old part of the village, between the houses: trunk-conic, 12 m of diameter and about 10 m of residual height, in blocks of shale with false dome; a corridor of 4 m leads to the circular chamber, with a cistern in a cell, and a staircase of 17 steps rises to the terrace. Reused as a burial in Byzantine times, it dominates the valley of the Flumendosa with a view up to Gennargentu. It is accessed from the ethnographic museum.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Armungia
  • Entry €3,50, reduced €2,50 (with Luxury Museum); cumulative museum system €6, reduced €4,50
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · sardegnacultura.it · italia.it

Museo storico Emilio e Joyce Lussu

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

In the House of the Secretary, the nineteenth-century master complex of the Dessì family at the church of the Immaculate, tells with images, documents, videos and interactive laws the life of Emilio Lussu (Brigata Sassari, Party of Sardinian Action, anti-fascism, exile, with a section on "One year on the plateau") and of Joyce Lussu Salvadori, writer and partisan; photo of 1971 by Franco Caruso. In the Cannedu district, the writer's birthplace, in shale, is part of the route. Open from Wednesday to Sunday with seasonal hours.

  • Where Via Emilio Lussu, Armungia
  • Entry €3,50, reduced €2,50 (with Nuraghe); cumulative €6, reduced €4,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · italia.it

Museo etnografico Sa Domu de is Ainas

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the ancient town hall of the mid-nineteenth century, built on the site of the church of St. John, about 600 objects of the population of Armungia collected by a group of girls (first exhibition on December 4, 1982) reconstruct the agropastoral culture of Gerrei, with sections on clothing, female work, agriculture, iron and territory, three small stone houses, a weaving workshop and a multimedia section.

  • Where Piazza Municipio 3, Armungia
  • Entry Cumulation system museum €6, reduced €4,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Bottega del fabbro

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Small stone building on two floors of the early nineteenth century, home of the Vellini family, arrived from Piedmont in the mid-nineteenth century, and since 1928 artisan workshop: subjectic with the frame to iron the animals, courtyard with the sandstone moles, forge with mantice, forge and anvils; on the first floor the work of the blacksmith and the history of the family.

  • Where Vico I Mazzini 14, Armungia
  • Entry Cumulation system museum €6, reduced €4,50
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Murales di Lussu e Gramsci

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

In Piazza della Chiesa the profile of Emilio Lussu painted in 1976 by Pinuccio Sciola and restored in 2017 by Massimo Congiu; in Via Gramsci the mural of 2017 by Congiu and Francesco del Casino with Lussu in the rally, four workers reading

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa e via Gramsci, Armungia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: idese.cultura.gov.it · italia.it
