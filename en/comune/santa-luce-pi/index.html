What to see in Santa Luce

Borgo delle Colline Pisane 42 km from Pisa, with a medieval castle with circular plan and one of the oldest parish churches in the territory of Pisa; around the natural reserve of Lake Santa Luce, the lavender fields of the ...Valle dei Profumi and, in Pomaia, the Buddhist Institute Lama Tzong Khapa.

7 places to visit in Santa Luce, in the province of Pisa (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ecomuseo dell'alabastro, sezione di Santa Luce

Museums · Themed

One of the three poles of the Alabaster Ecomuseum, with Castellina Marittima and Volterra, housed in the former Town Hall. It preserves working tools, iconographic material, alabaster artifacts, archaeological finds of the territory and documentation on the quarries, dug in underground tunnels with explosions sometimes dangerous to extract the 'ovuli' then worked in Volterra.

  • Where Ex Palazzo Comunale, Santa Luce
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.rmvaldera.it

Sources: rmvaldera.it · terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Castello e borgo medievale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village preserves the feudal system collected around the castle, with circular plan with two or three walls still visible; remain a tower and a stretch of the ancient walls. A legend links the castle to the Templars and the Holy Grail.

  • Where Centro storico, Santa Luce
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta e Sant'Angelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

One of the oldest parish churches in the Pisan territory, dating back to the VI-VII centuries, with news from 877; in stone, today only one nave because rebuilt after the violent earthquake of 1846 that hit the Pisan Hills. The original building remains the base of the bell tower, three capitals, some sculptures, a Robbian ceramic and a basin of the baptismal font.

  • Where Pieve di Santa Luce, Santa Luce
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: terredipisa.it

Riserva naturale Lago di Santa Luce (Oasi LIPU)

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Artificial invade born in the sixties to supply the Solvay plant, today regional nature reserve and Special Nature Conservation Zone 2000 managed by LIPU. Census over 180 species of birds: in winter moriglioni, Germani royali e cormorani, in summer nightingale of river, cannaiola and gruccione; symbol is the greater dross. A 2 km nature trail along the southern bank leads to a sighting shed; there is a visitor centre. Obligators closed shoes and long pants, dogs not allowed; access at dawn for photographers on reservation 5 days before.

  • Where Lago di Santa Luce, Santa Luce
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.lipu.it

Sources: lipu.it · terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Campi di lavanda della Valle dei Profumi

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The area is called "Valle dei Profumi" (Valle dei Profumi) by a European project involving 15 companies on 18 hectares between Santa Luce, Orciano Pisano, Castellina Marittima and Lorenzana. The main fields are along the provincial road for Orciano Pisano and on a hill overlooking the village; the flowering is in June and July.

  • Where Strada provinciale per Orciano Pisano, Santa Luce
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Istituto Lama Tzong Khapa

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

In the hamlet of Pomaia, for many years one of the most important Buddhist centers in Europe: center of studies of oriental philosophy that hosts meditation retreats, seminars and meetings.

  • Where Pomaia, Santa Luce
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · terredipisa.it

Cava di Scaglione

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Along the Marmolaio torrent, between Castellina Marittima and Santa Luce, it is the only alabaster tunnel quarry in the area of Scaglione (locally known as "venella") still existing, one of the best qualities of alabaster.
