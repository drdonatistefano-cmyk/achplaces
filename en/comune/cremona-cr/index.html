What to see in Cremona

The city of Stradivari and violin, with one of the most beautiful medieval squares in Italy: the Duomo, the Torrazzo, the octagonal Baptistery and the Palazzo del Comune; then the Violin Museum, the gallery Ala Ponzone and the Renaissance palaces of Corso Matteotti and Corso Garibaldi.

28 places to visit in Cremona, in the province of Cremona (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza del Comune

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The perfect medieval square, the city's greatest artistic pride: the Town Hall in the Broletto, the Torrazzo, the octagonal plan Baptistery and the Duomo.

  • Where Piazza del Comune, Cremona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Museo del Violino

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the Palazzo dell'Arte designed by Carlo Cocchia and inaugurated in 1947, five centuries of Cremonese violin with the violin "Il Cremonese" by Stradivari of 1715 in the room "Lo casket of treasures"; next, the Auditorium Giovanni Arvedi of 460 seats.

  • Where Piazza Marconi 5, Cremona
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website museodelviolino.org
