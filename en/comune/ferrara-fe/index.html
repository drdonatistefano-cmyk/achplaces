What to see in Ferrara

The city of the Este Renaissance, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1995: the Castle, the Cathedral, the Erculean Addition with the Diamonds' Palace, the Schifanoia Show of Months and almost 9 km of walls to be turned by bike.

28 places to visit in Ferrara, in the province of Ferrara (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers 14 of the 26 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Castello Estense

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Fortress built in 1385 by Nicolò II d'Este on a project by Bartolino da Novara, then ducal residence: prisons, kitchens, Garden of Oranges and frescoed halls, in the path set up by Gae Aulenti.

Large restoration and seismic adjustment works are under way: the visit route can change or close in part, check before leaving.

  • Where Largo Castello, Ferrara
  • Entry In the MyFE Card; single ticket to check on the Castle website
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.castelloestense.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: castelloestense.it · cultura.gov.it · ilrestodelcarlino.it · finestresullarte.info · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · ferrarainfo.com

Cattedrale di San Giorgio e Piazza Trento e Trieste

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Romanesque and Gothic cathedral with the façade in white marble and the San Giorgio that kills the dragon in the center of the portal; next to the Loggia dei Merciai and the market square.

  • Where Piazza Cattedrale, Ferrara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
