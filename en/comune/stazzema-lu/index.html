What to see in Stazzema

The only mountain town in the area, with 17 villages on the slopes of the Apuan Alps, is located in Levigliani: the Antro del Corchia and the Piegata Stone Museum, in Sant'Anna the places of memory of the Nazi massacre of 12 August 1944, the Gold Medal at the Military Valor in 1970.

8 places to visit in Stazzema, in the province of Lucca (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo storico della Resistenza

Museums · City history

A small museum at the end of the second millennium in the former elementary school of St. Anne; it tells the historical context of the massacre of 12 August 1944, in which the SS killed 560 people including many children, and the contribution of Versilia to the Liberation. Outside, a plaque with the epigraph of Calamandrei in Kesselring and a carved reproduction of a detail of Picasso's "Guernica"; the exhibition space is connected to the Ossario Monument on the Col di Cava.

  • Where Via Coletti 22, Sant'Anna di Stazzema
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website santannadistazzema.org

Sources: santannadistazzema.org · visittuscany.com · italia.it

Monumento Ossario, Via Crucis e Parco Nazionale della Pace

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The National Park of Peace, established in 2000, includes the hills around the village; a paved path beyond the museum rises to Col di Cava along the stations of the Way of the Cross, which bring the Passion of Christ to the scenes of the massacre. On top of the Ossuario, a stone tower 12 metres high with four arches designed by the architect Tito Salvatori and carved by Vincenzo Gasperetti, with a plaque bearing the names of the identified victims.

  • Where Col di Cava, Sant'Anna di Stazzema
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Anna

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Small church of the sixteenth century on the main square of the village, at over 600 meters above sea level, where on August 12, 1944 the Nazis killed hundreds of people, especially children, women and elderly, and burned the bodies with the benches of the church.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa, Sant'Anna di Stazzema
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Antro del Corchia

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry

A karst system of 53 km of tunnels and wells, indicated as the largest in Italy and among the largest in Europe, with stalactites and stalagmites. The guided group tour lasts about two hours on about 2,000 meters of steel footbridge, plus an artificial tunnel of 170 meters; inside the temperature is 7 °C. The ticket includes the shuttle from Levigliani (581 m) at the entrance (850 m).

  • Where Via della Chiesa, Levigliani (biglietteria)
  • Entry €18, children 5-14 years €10, reduced €16; combined Corchia Park Experience €22
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.corchiapark.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: corchiapark.it · visittuscany.com

Miniere dell'Argento Vivo di Levigliani

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Indicated as the oldest mining site in Upper Tuscany, cited in a Pisan document of 1153: for centuries they gave the cinnabar used by Florentine workshops for red pigment, then mercury to work precious metals and tan the skins of felt hats. Abandoned in the seventies, they were recovered in the years 2000; shuttle from Levigliani included in the ticket.

  • Where Levigliani, Stazzema
  • Entry €8, reduced €6
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.corchiapark.it

Sources: corchiapark.it · visittuscany.com

Museo della Pietra Piegata e Museo «Lavorare liberi»

Museums · Themed

In Levigliani, next to the ticket office of Corchia, the museum tells the uses of stone and Apuan marble: sacred art in marble, colored marbles now little hollowed, baroque engravings, kitchen mortars and for medicinal herbs, a section dedicated to the biologist Emilio Simi, discoverer of the Antro del Corchia, and a tomb in box of the Ligurian Apuans from the nearby necropolis (III-I century BC). The Museo di comunità "Lavorare liberi" tells the history of the Comunello and the Cooperative Condomini Levigliani, which has been riding Monte Corchia for over 50 years, with tools and the reconstruction of a quarry with wire cutting.

  • Where Via 4 Novembre 70, Levigliani
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.corchiapark.it

Sources: corchiapark.it · comune.stazzema.lu.it · visittuscany.com

Stazzema, borgo e chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

In the capital the clock tower of 1739 and the church of Santa Maria Assunta, with white marble facade and three bells merged in the sixteenth century, among the oldest in Versilia. The territory has been inhabited since ancient times, as shown by the tombs of the Ligurian Apuani.

  • Where Stazzema
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Alpe di Puntato

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the last pastures of the Apuan Alps, in the Park, between Pania della Croce, Monte Corchia and Freddone under the Pizzo delle Saette; inhabited by shepherds until the 1940s and abandoned after the war, preserves ruins, terraces, mule tracks, charcoal and metati for chestnut flour. Two shelters, Robbio and Quiete, offer overnight stay.

  • Where Puntato, Stazzema
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
