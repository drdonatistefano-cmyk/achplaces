Santuario di San Donato

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

First certain date on 778, when the Longobard Duke Ildebrando of Spoleto gave the church to the abbey of San Vincenzo al Volturno; in the eighteenth century Gaspare Capricci frescoed it with stories of the saint and on the counterfaced the Hunting of merchants from the temple. Keep a collection of ex-vote and the simulacrum of the saint; you climb there with steep stairs up to Largo San Donato.

  • Where Largo San Donato, San Donato Val di Comino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Duomo di Santa Maria e San Marcello Papa

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Of fourteenth-century origins, enlarged and baroque in the eighteenth century with three naves; choir and organ are the work of Cesare Catarinozzi and under the main altar a urn preserves the body of Saint Constance. In war he gave refuge to Jewish prisoners and internees; the masks of the portal had to keep the iconic cell away.

  • Where Via Pedicata, San Donato Val di Comino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Torre dei Conti d'Aquino e Castello

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Tower built in 1200 by the Aquinas as a defence and refuge of the castrum, without entrance door until the one opened in the eighties; the lower part was a cistern and was oriented on the points of the sun to equinoxes and solstices. The castle dominates the village from above.

  • Where Rione Il Castello, San Donato Val di Comino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · parcoabruzzo.it · italia.it

Centro storico, spuort' e chiavi di volta

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Medieval labyrinth of alleys, stairways and covered passages (sport of the Plague, Providence, Origins, Flood and Fra' Tommaso), with sacred newsstands, baroque portals with keys of vault and masks carved by local stonemasons, the Stone of the scandal of Via Maggiore, where in the sixteenth century the insolvent debtors sat, and the sundial of 1891 in Piazza Coletti. The Park offers themed walking tours in the village.

  • Where Via Maggiore, San Donato Val di Comino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · parcoabruzzo.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Memoriale della Shoah

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Stele with the star of David for the 28 foreign Jews interned in the village from 1940 to 1944, including Grete Bloch, friend of Kafka, and actress Grete Berger: on April 6, 1944 sixteen were deported to Auschwitz and only four survived. The memorial was inaugurated on 27 January 2023.

  • Where San Donato Val di Comino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it
