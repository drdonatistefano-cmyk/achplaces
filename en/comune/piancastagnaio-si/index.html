What to see in Piancastagnaio

Medieval village on the slopes of Monte Amiata, a land of chestnuts, with the Aldobrandesca Rocca and the Palio delle Contrade of 18 August.

2 places to visit in Piancastagnaio, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca Aldobrandesca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortress documented from the 12th century, square with shoe walls and two towers, passed by Aldobrandeschi, Orsini, Siena and Florence; today it can be visited free of charge, with museum and temporary exhibitions.

  • Where Piazza Castello 20, Piancastagnaio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Piatto delle Streghe

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Just outside the walls, in the historic gardens of the Bourbon Palace of the Mount, a stone polished in the shape of a plate where according to legend the witches celebrated their sabba.

  • Where Piancastagnaio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
