What to see in Maglione

Small agricultural village of Canavese, about 20 km from Ivrea, known for the MACAM, museum of contemporary art in the open air born in 1985 with more than one hundred works, especially frescoes, painted on the walls of the houses; on the hill remains the tower of the ancient castle of Valperga, today bell tower of the cemetery, and in the village the Baroque parish church of San Maurizio.

MACAM - Museo d'arte contemporanea all'aperto

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

Designed in 1985 by the director Maurizio Corgnati for his native country, he initially invited twelve frescoes: today he has 115 works, mostly frescoes on the exterior walls of the houses, as well as abstract paintings and sculptural forms, by many artists including Piero Gilardi, Eugenio Comencini, Mauro Chessa, Bert Van Zelm and Klaus Munch. You can visit freely, without timetables or tickets; in 2025 you can visit Sundays from July to October (10-12, 15-18).

  • Where Via Castello 2, Maglione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.macam.org

Sources: macam.org · comune.maglione.to.it · turismotorino.org

Parrocchiale di San Maurizio Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Documented from 1287, it was renovated and enlarged with a major altar and choir from 1737 to 1744; it has a baroque façade and four altars. It is open on Sunday evening for Mass.

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre, Maglione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.maglione.to.it · turismotorino.org

Torre del castello

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Of the castle (Castrum Mali) built by the Valperga di Masino around the thirteenth century on the hill at 343 meters, besieged and destroyed by the Spanish in 1652, there is only a quadrangular tower, today bell tower of the cemetery.

  • Where Colle del cimitero, Maglione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.maglione.to.it · turismotorino.org
