What to see in Concesio

Municipality at the southern entrance of the Trompia Valley, birthplace of Pope Paul VI (John Baptist Montini, born here on 26 September 1897): to visit the birthplace and the Paul VI Collection of contemporary art, with about seven thousand works of twentieth century, in addition to the churches of the village of San Vigilio.

Casa natale di Paolo VI

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

15th century country building on the road to the Trompia Valley, built by the Counts Lodron and purchased by the Montini family in 1863: here on September 26, 1897 Pope Paul VI was born. It preserves a sundial dated 1658, a seventeenth-century balcony in wrought iron above the entrance and a gallery of the seventeenth century with twelve medallions of landscapes of the months and signs of the zodiac. Left in 1997 by Eng. Vittorio Montini at the Opera for Christian Education in Brescia, is a pilgrimage destination and has become a biographical journey on the stages of the Pope's life and pontificate; next to it lives a community of Daughters of Mary Help of Christians who welcomes visitors. Open from Tuesday to Saturday 10-12 and 14.30-17, Sunday 14.30-17.

  • Where Via Rodolfo da Concesio 24, Concesio
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrescia.it

Sources: comune.concesio.brescia.it · visitbrescia.it

Collezione Paolo VI – Arte contemporanea

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Born from several donations of Paul VI, it collects about seven thousand works of the twentieth century (paintings, drawings, prints, medals and sculptures) belonging to Giovanni Battista Montini, with authors such as Matisse, Chagall, Picasso, Dalí, Magritte, Rouault, Severini, Morandi, Fontana, Manzù, Hartung and Guitton, testimony of the relationships between artists, the Pope and his secretary Pasquale Macchi. It has sections dedicated to graphics and medals and an educational center with guided tours and workshops. Open from Tuesday to Friday 9-12 and 15-17, Saturday 14-19; free entry for FAI members and with ticket of the Diocesan Museum of Brescia not more than three days before.

  • Where Via Marconi 15, Concesio
  • Entry €2,5
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrescia.it

Sources: visitbrescia.it

Chiesa dei Santi Vigilio e Gregorio Magno

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was cited as a new ecclesia sancti Gregori, built in 1237, born as a small chapel and became a parish in 1632, transformed in the eighteenth century and for the last time in 1932. It has four chapels on the side with barrel vaults and paintings from the 16th to the 18th century: a Pietà, perhaps from the Richino, the altarpiece with the Madonna and Child dated 1569, a painting by Pietro Marone, frescoes detached from the ancient church of San Velgio and paintings by Giuseppe Remida; in sacristy angels reggigandelabro baroque.

  • Where Via Antonio Cottinelli 20, San Vigilio di Concesio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrescia.it

Chiesa di San Velgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Ancient parish church on a hill overlooking the village of San Vigilio, along the ancient Valeriana street: a single nave, it preserves traces of the Romanesque structure, was enlarged between the XV and XVI century and dedicated also to San Rocco. It houses two frescoes in the building, a sixteenth-century wooden statue of San Rocco, a wooden crucifix and a seventeenth-century altarpiece; it has been recovered by the parish community and the Alpine Group. It is only reached on foot from via Quarone.

the church is open only for the feast of San Vigilio; the area in front is always accessible (the site of the Municipality)
