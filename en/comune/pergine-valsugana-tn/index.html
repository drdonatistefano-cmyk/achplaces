What to see in Pergine Valsugana

Borgo della Valsugana under the medieval castle of the Tegazzo hill, with the Renaissance houses of Via Maier, the beach of San Cristoforo on the lake of Caldonazzo and four small museums curated by associations: school, band, agricultural tools and the hydroelectric power plant of Serso.

Castel Pergine

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the Tegazzo hill, along the ancient Via Claudia Augusta, where there was a Roman settlement: present form of the thirteenth century, with the Ala Clesiana added in the sixteenth century by the prince bishop of Trento; the Prison of the drop, the Hall of the throne and the Chapel of Sant'Andrea. Today it is private property with hotel and restaurant; from April to November it hosts exhibitions, theatre and concerts.

  • Where Via al Castello 10, Pergine Valsugana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.fondazionecastelpergine.eu

Sources: fondazionecastelpergine.eu · visittrentino.info · comune.pergine.tn.it · visitvalsugana.it

Museo della centrale idroelettrica di Serso

Museums · Science and university

Opened in 1998 in a power plant still in operation, it recalls the Serso plant of 1893, the first three-phase alternating current of the Austro-Hungarian Empire: turbine-alternator group, measuring instruments and documents. Guided tours only by reservation.

  • Where Serso, Pergine Valsugana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.visitvalsugana.it

Sources: cultura.trentino.it · visitvalsugana.it

Museo della banda sociale

Museums · Themed

Instruments, scores, posters, photographs and historical uniforms of the band founded in 1900, with a banner of fanfare of 1885. Open Tuesday and Friday evening and on the first Sunday of the month in the morning; guided tours by reservation.

  • Where Via Pontara 1, Pergine Valsugana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitvalsugana.it

Sources: cultura.trentino.it · visitvalsugana.it

Museo della scuola

Museums · Themed

Born in 1997 on the initiative of the teachers of the Don Milani school, it collects objects from the history of the school; since 2013 it is in the basement of the new Municipal Theatre. Openings and visits only by reservation.

  • Where Teatro comunale, Pergine Valsugana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitvalsugana.it

Sources: visitvalsugana.it

Museo degli attrezzi agricoli e artigianali

Museums · Themed

Created by the association of the same name in Canezza-Portolo, exhibits tools of the cheese factory of the village, houses and shops. Open on Sunday afternoon; other visits by appointment.

  • Where Canezza, Pergine Valsugana
