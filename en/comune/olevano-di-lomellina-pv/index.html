What to see in Olevano di Lomellina

Small agricultural municipality of Lomellina, the plain between Ticino, Po and Sesia; around the medieval castle, with the south facade of the eighteenth-century villa, is the Museum of art and peasant tradition.

Museo di arte e tradizione contadina

Museums · Themed

Set up in a restored rural building, once a barn and barn, in the estate of the medieval castle, it collects about 2,500 pieces of the world of Lomellino peasant from the nineteenth century to the sixties of the twentieth century: tools for the breeding of cattle and pigs, wagons, ploughs, furniture and agricultural machinery. The route is divided into five areas: agricultural work cycle, domestic life environments, ancient crafts artisans, stable and courtyard, heavy agricultural machinery; remarkable reconstructions of rural environments.

Castello di Olevano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Romantic complex nestled in a well-kept park, around which the village develops: the north side retains the medieval defensive system, while the southern facade has the most refined appearance of a 18th century country villa.
