What to see in Briatico

Costa degli Dei center overlooking the Gulf of Lamezia and the Aeolian Islands, with a succession of bays, cliffs and beaches like Trainiti, Baia Safò, San Giuseppe and Sant'Irene; on the small port of the Rocchetta remains the ruin of one of the most photographed anti Saracen towers on the coast.

4 places to visit in Briatico, in the province of Vibo Valentia (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torri della Rocchetta e di Sant'Irene

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Two ancient towers built to defend against Saracen incursions, the only survivors of the original five. The ruin of the Rocchetta, among the most famous in Calabria, is located next to the marina of the small fishing village of the Bay of the Rocchetta and is one of the most photographed places on the Costa degli Dei.

  • Where Baia della Rocchetta, Briatico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · calabriastraordinaria.it

Spiagge e baie di Briatico

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

White sandy stretches alternate with cliffs and coves reachable only by boat: the beaches of Trainiti, San Giuseppe and Sant'Irene, the Safò Bay and the Cocca cliff. The promenade looks at the Gulf of Lamezia Terme and the Aeolian archipelago.

  • Where Litorale di Briatico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · calabriastraordinaria.it

Ruderi del castello di Briatico Vecchia

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry

The remains of the medieval castle are what remains of the original nucleus of the village, Briatico Vecchia, damaged by earthquakes between 1638 and 1783.

  • Where Briatico Vecchia, Briatico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa Matrice di San Nicola di Bari

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Founded after the earthquake destroyed the old center of Briatico Vecchia, it preserves remains of other churches lost.

  • Where Briatico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
