What to see in Malegno

Country of the Camonica Valley bathed by the Lanico torrent, whose strength has fed for centuries the processing of iron: the ethnographic iron Museum tells of it "Le Fudine" in the ancient forges of Serini and Dwarfs; in the village the two churches of Sant'Andrea, the "old" with frescoes from the XV-XVI century and the eighteenth century parish church.

Museo etnografico del ferro «Le Fudine»

Chiesa di Sant'Andrea Vecchia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

This old church, to distinguish it from the new parish with the same dedication, is located on a Romanesque building of the thirteenth century, which has been renovated several times. It contains frescoes from the 15th and 16th centuries, including a San Cristoforo and episodes from the life of Simonino da Trento.

  • Entry To be checked

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Andrea Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Begin in 1706 on a back of the historic center, demolishing some houses by resolution of the Neighborhood, and completed in 1760; the expert Antonio Spazzi was probably the designer. A unique nave with episodes of the life of Saint Andrew and Apostles in the veils; a major altar in marble inlaid with mother-of-pearl, statues in stucco by Benjamin Simoni, a altarpiece with the crucifixion of Saint Andrew by Antonio Guadagnini (1883) and four side altars with eighteenth-century altars attributed to Carlo Carloni and Sante Cattaneo.
