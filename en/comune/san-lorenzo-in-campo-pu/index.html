What to see in San Lorenzo in Campo

Borgo della valle del Cesano that preserves the structure of the castle with walls, arches and towers, grown around the Romanesque-Gothic Benedictine Abbey of San Lorenzo, built with materials of the Roman city of Suasa; in the sixteenth century Palazzo Della Rovere are located the Teatro Tiberini and the civic museums, and in the territory there are the castles of Montalfoglio and San Vito sul Cesano.

Abbazia benedettina di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Great Romanesque-Gothic building, one of the most beautiful of the Marches according to the Municipality, built by the Benedictine monks in the early Middle Ages reusing materials of the destroyed Roman town hall of Suasa; the monastery, originally San Lorenzo in Silvis, already existed around the IX century and since 1570 it had as Commendatario Giulio della Rovere. The basilica has a crypt.

Visits by reservation through the I.A.T. Point.

Museo archeologico del territorio di Suasa ed etnografico africano

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Municipal Museum founded in 1983 in Palazzo Della Rovere: the finds, mostly local and partly from Etruria, Daunia and Egypt, include amphorae, hexagonal terracotta tiles, column rocks, capitals and friezes. The African section (Collezione Gello Giorgi), collected by a missionary who lived long in Sierra Leone, exhibits apotropaic statues \"nomoli" , masks of initiatic dance, sculptures, head covers and a Xylophone; there is also a section of minerals and fossils of Monte Catria. The cumulative includes the Stamp Room; the guided tour includes the Teatro Tiberini.

  • Where Via Tiberini (Palazzo Della Rovere, in cima a via Cavour), San Lorenzo in Campo
  • Entry €3, reduced €2; cumulative €5, reduced €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.sanlorenzoincampo.pu.it · letsmarche.it

Museo delle Terre Marchigiane - Collezione Straccini

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Private collection of over 3,000 pieces documenting the rural civilization and artistic culture of Marche between the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, in four paths: the rebuilt rooms of the farmhouse sharecropping, the meeting places, the workshops of the old crafts and the collections of ceramics, copper and iron, with environments animated by images and sounds.

visits only by reservation

  • Where Via Leopardi, San Lorenzo in Campo
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · letsmarche.it · comune.sanlorenzoincampo.pu.it

Palazzo Della Rovere e Teatro Mario Tiberini

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Severo sixteenth-century palace wanted by Giulio della Rovere at the entrance to the old castle, home of the civic museums. On the noble floor, perhaps in the ancient dance hall of the Della Rovere, the theatre dedicated to the local tenor Mario Tiberini: completed around 1820 as the Triomphe Theatre, rebuilt in masonry after a fire of 1880, inaugurated with the current name in 1882 and reopened after the restoration in 1983.

Theater can be visited by reservation or with the guided tour of the archaeological museum

  • Where Via Cavour, San Lorenzo in Campo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: comune.sanlorenzoincampo.pu.it · letsmarche.it

Centro storico, Rocca e Cammino dei Lavatoi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center preserves the original layout of the castle, with alleys, entrance arches and walls with towers today without battlements; on the highest point remain parts of the Fortress, set on fire in 1516 by the Medici troops, above the square "Padella" . Among the historical buildings from the fifteenth to the eighteenth century there are Palazzo Amatori, Brini, Ruspoli and Ripanti. The I.A.T. Point organizes on reservation the tour of the historic center and the "Camino dei Lavatoi" (Whitewashing Way).

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, San Lorenzo in Campo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: comune.sanlorenzoincampo.pu.it · letsmarche.it

Santuario del Santissimo Crocifisso

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

It houses a wooden crucifix carved in the seventeenth century by Fra' Innocenzo da Petralia, venerated as miraculous; the stained glass windows and painted ceilings were destroyed by bombs in July 1944.

  • Where San Lorenzo in Campo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Castelli di Montalfoglio e San Vito sul Cesano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Two castles on the left of the Cesano still surrounded by sturdy walls in shoes: Montalfoglio 393 meters and San Vito sul Cesano 353 meters, autonomous municipality until 1869. The I.A.T. Point includes Montalfoglio on the guided tour by reservation, while the San Vito Giovani Association organizes the San Vito Tour with the Pieve Vecchia.

  • Where Montalfoglio e San Vito sul Cesano, San Lorenzo in Campo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · comune.sanlorenzoincampo.pu.it
