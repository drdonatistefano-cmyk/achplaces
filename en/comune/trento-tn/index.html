What to see in Trento

The capital in the Adige valley among the mountains, the city of the Council with the square of the Cathedral frescoed, the Castle of the Buonconsiglio dei principi bishops and the MUSE of Renzo Piano.

25 places to visit in Trento, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 5 of the 10 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Piazza del Duomo e Fontana del Nettuno

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The political, religious and commercial heart of the city, surrounded by noble frescoed buildings, with the eighteenth-century Fountain of the Neptune in the center that holds the trident, recalls the ancient name Tridentum.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Trento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittrentino.info

Cattedrale di San Vigilio

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The Romanesque cathedral dedicated to the patron saint, built from 1212 at the behest of Bishop Federico Vanga, where the solemn sessions of the Council of Trento were held; it houses the tomb of St. Vigilio.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Trento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittrentino.info

Basilica paleocristiana di San Vigilio

History and architecture · Archaeology

The remains of the early Christian basilica that you visit going down into the crypt under the cathedral.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Trento
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · italia.it

Palazzo Pretorio e Museo Diocesano

Museums · Old masters

The crenellated palace next to the cathedral, with the Civic Tower, seat of the Diocesan Museum Tridentino with sacred works, architecture and Christian finds.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Trento
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittrentino.info

Case Cazuffi-Rella

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The noble palaces of Piazza del Duomo with entirely frescoed facades, among the most beautiful examples of the painted palaces of Trento.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Trento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello del Buonconsiglio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

The most important monumental complex in the region, residence of the bishops until 1803: Castelvecchio on the Tower of Augustus, the Magno Palazzo di Bernardo Clesio with frescoes by Dosso Dosso and Romanino, the courtyard of the Leoni, the cells of Cesare Battisti and the Italian garden.

  • Where Trento
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Torre dell'Aquila

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The most extraordinary hall of the Buonconsiglio, reached by the walkway of the round: the fourteenth-century cycle of the Months, an absolute masterpiece of late dogotic painting.

  • Where Castello del Buonconsiglio, Trento
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittrentino.info

MUSE Museo delle Scienze

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

The Science Museum designed by Renzo Piano is open in 2013, eco-sustainable and with shapes that recall the mountains: from dinosaurs to prehistoric man, interactive games and a tropical greenhouse that reproduces the rain forest.

  • Where Trento
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets

Sources: italia.it · visittrentino.info
