What to see in Caneva

A village nestled between vineyards and mountains at the foot of Cansiglio, heir of the ancient Canipa, with the ruins of the castle built around the Mille; it shares with Polcenigo the UNESCO Palù di Livenza pile-up site and preserves the Museum of cycling • Toni Pessot • and the Museum of the miner dedicated to emigration to mines.

Sito palafitticolo del Palù di Livenza

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage

Between the sources of the Livenza, at the border between Caneva and Polcenigo, it is among the oldest settlements in northern Italy, frequented by the Paleolithic; there were found tools in splintered stone, fragments of ceramics and wooden remains of the huts. It is one of the 19 UNESCO World Heritage sites of prehistoric alpine stilts; many finds are in the Archaeological Museum of Western Friuli.

Ruderi del castello di Caneva

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Picturesque remains of a castle built around the year 1000, probably on Roman foundations, on the hill above the village that was the ancient Canipa, abandoned in the seventeenth century and invaded by the wood; from the top the view ranges over the village and the vineyards. The first Sunday of July is held the Festa del Castello.

  • Where Colle del castello, Caneva
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it

Museo del ciclismo «Toni Pessot»

Museums · Themed

T-shirts, objects and photographs of Italian and foreign champions such as Binda, Girardengo, Guerra, Bartali, Coppi, Gimondi, Merckx and Maspes, with yellow tour jerseys and pink tour jerseys and all Toni Pessot jerseys donated by his daughter to the municipality; also on display are three pairs of boxing gloves, one belonging to Primo Carnera. Visits only by reservation, every day.

  • Where Via Trieste, Caneva
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismofvg.it

Museo del minatore «Arduino Martinuzzo»

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Collection on emigration and work in mines, especially in Belgium but also in Brazil and Italy: tools, helmets, lamps, a track with vagoncini, ventilation tubes of galleries, photos and samples of minerals, with recordings of former miners, documentaries on life in mines and Marcinelle and documents of the Italian Association Ex Minatori. Visits on request.

  • Entry Free

Malga Cercenedo e Malga Fossa di Bena

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the center there are many paths to woods, mountains and alpine pastures; among the recommended are the ring between Malga Cerenedo and Malga Fossa di Bena, 9 km round trip with 300 meters of altitude difference, with shorter or longer variations. In Fossa di Bena you buy goat cheese produced on site.

  • Where Malga Cercenedo, Caneva
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: italia.it · turismofvg.it

Vini e fichi di Caneva

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Caneva is linked to ancient wines, in particular Marzemino, and to its numerous fig trees, with which is prepared the "Figo Moro" , suitable for sweet and savoury dishes; recommended tastings of wine and local products.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
