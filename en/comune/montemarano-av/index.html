What to see in Montemarano

Irpino village dominated by the medieval castle at 820 meters, where Giambattista Basile was governor and sketched Lo Cunto de li Cunti; it preserves the medieval crypt of the ancient cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta with the relics of San Giovanni and two museums, sacred and ethnomusical wallpapers.

Castello di Montemarano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

High fortress of medieval plant, probably of the second half of the eighth century, several times renovated: today it is mainly a late Renaissance building; of the two towers remembered by the sources there is no trace and of the walls survives a stretch in via Sottocastello. On a portal is visible the coat of arms with the lion rampant of the Della Leonessa; Giambattista Basile, governor of Montemarano in 1615-1616, completed the first draft of Lo Cunto de li Cunti. Restored in 2015, it hosts exhibitions, including the MuVIM, Ecomuseum of Wine.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta e cripta medievale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Mother church and cathedral until 1818, already mentioned in a bull of Pope Stephen IX. From the external stairs to the lateral aisles you go down to the crypt, restored in 1984: under the marble altar a urn has been guarding since 1095 the remains of St. John, bishop and patron; there are two frescoes from the twelfth century with St. Nicholas of Bari and St. John of Montemarano, a wooden bust of the saint and a crucifix of the fifteenth century.
