What to see in Chiusi

One of the great Etruscan cities, with the National Archaeological Museum, the underground tunnels of the "Labyrinth of Porsenna" and the lake at 4 km.

5 places to visit in Chiusi, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Museo Archeologico Nazionale

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Since 1871 in a neoclassical building the Etruscan, Roman and Longobard finds of the territory: ceramics, sculptures, the famous canopies closed, painted urns, jewels and reconstructions of the main tombs.

  • Where Via Porsenna 93, Chiusi
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo della Cattedrale e Labirinto di Porsenna

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

From the Cathedral Museum you descend into the tunnels of an Etruscan aqueduct, believed for centuries the tomb of King Porsenna: 130 meters up to the Etruscan-Roman cistern of the first century BC and then up to the tower of the twelfth century.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Chiusi
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo Civico "La città sotterranea"

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Etruscan galleries 140 meters long, with a well that lets you see a lake 30 meters below the road, dedicated entirely to the Etruscan epigraph: about 500 inscriptions on urns and tombstones.

  • Where Chiusi
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Cattedrale di San Secondiano

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The cathedral whose interior recalls the great early Christian basilicas of Rome.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Chiusi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Lago di Chiusi

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lake of 300 hectares and deep at most five meters, 4 km from the center: rest of the ancient swamps of Valdichiana, is an oasis for birdwatching.

  • Where Lago di Chiusi, Chiusi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
