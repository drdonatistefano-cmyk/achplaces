What to see in Licata

Seaside town of the coast of Agrigento, called "Dilectissima" by Federico II: on Mount Sant'Angelo the remains of the Hellenistic city of Finziade and the castle, in the 16th century Badia the archaeological museum, in the center the churches of Santa Maria La Nova and San Domenico and the Palazzo of City of Ernesto Basile; along the coast the lighthouse of San Giacomo and the beaches of Mollarella, Marianello, San Nicola and Torre di Gaffe.

Museo archeologico della Badia

Museums · Archaeological

In the sixteenth-century convent of Santa Maria del Soccorso, called "Badia" (Badia), enlarged between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, exhibits around the cloister porched finds from the ancient Neolithic (VI millennium BC) to the late antiquity: the sanctuaries of Demeter and Kore of Mollarella and Casalicchio, the settlements of Poggio Marcato d'Agnone and Monte Sole and an entire hall on Finziade, founded in 282 BC by the tyrant agrigentino Finzia, with the House 1 and the treasure found inside. Since 2025 the medieval and modern section exhibits the fifteenth-century triptych attributed to the Master of San Martino, the Madonna del Soccorso attributed to Domenico Gagini (1470) and a sixteenth-century sarcophagus with knight. Open Monday-Friday 9-13.

  • Where Via Dante, Licata
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Area archeologica di Monte Sant'Angelo e Castel Sant'Angelo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the large promontory above the city you can visit the excavations of ancient Finziade, with houses of Hellenistic age, and the castle born as a Aragonese signal tower, from which you can enjoy the view of the coast. Open Monday-Saturday 9-13, Sunday and holidays closed.

  • Where Monte Sant'Angelo, Licata
  • Entry €6, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria La Nova e il Cristo Nero

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The Black Christ is kept in the homonymous chapel covered with wooden panels inlaid and decorated in gold; according to tradition, during the looting of the pirate Dragut in 1553 the Turks tried in vain to burn the statue with fiery arrows.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
