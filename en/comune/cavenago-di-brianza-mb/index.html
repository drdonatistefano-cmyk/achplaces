What to see in Cavenago di Brianza

Municipality of Lower Brianza oriental gathered around Palazzo Rasini, villa with two towers and courtyard of honor today seat of the town hall and the miniature Museum of peasant life, and the Romanesque church of Santa Maria in Campo.

Palazzo Rasini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built from the 16th century on a project by Martino Bassi and modified in the following centuries, it has two towers and a courtyard of honor and is now home to the town hall. Inside cycles of frescoes and stuccoes between XVI and XVIII century of active painters in Milan, including Mattia Bortoloni (in the hall of honor on the ground floor), Carlo Antonio Procaccini, Giovanni Stefano Montalto and Giovanni Paolo Cavagna; In Lombardy he also mentions stuccoes by Andrea Appiani. For Open Villas 2024 guided tours (50 minutes) cost €5, €7 cumulative with Santa Maria in Campo.

Museo in miniatura della vita contadina e artigianale della Brianza

Museums · Themed

Set up inside Palazzo Rasini, it is a plastic of about 11 square meters in 1:10 scale that reproduces a typical farmhouse brianzola complete with tools, furniture, woodshed and barn, made with the same materials as the original.

Santa Maria in Campo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque church originally dedicated to Santa Maria Assunta, considered built in the 12th century as a small votive oratory at the edge of the town, in the typical Romanesque style of the Po Valley, with hulled roof, Romanesque door and rose window and bell tower. It preserves a painting cycle of the sixteenth century; some frescoes of the Zavattari, Leonardesca school and Bernardino Luini were detached, restored and exhibited in the eighteenth-century parish church of San Giulio, in Piazza Libertà.

no time: entrance during liturgical celebrations, guided tours on the occasion of FAI Days and Open Villas
