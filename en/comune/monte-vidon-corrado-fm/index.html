What to see in Monte Vidon Corrado

Borgo del Fermano where he was born in 1894 and died in 1958 the painter Osvaldo Licini: on the square dedicated to him the eighteenth-century house where he lived and the Center studies with drawings and writings; in the village the parish church of San Vito Martire, the walls of the Three-Fourteenth century and views from the hills to the sea to the Sibillini.

Casa museo e Centro studi Osvaldo Licini

Museums · House museums

The Centre, founded in 1986, conserves works on paper (leaflets, preprinted forms, typewritten) in which Licini designed Amalasunte, rebel angels, still lifes, marines, hilly landscapes and views of countries, often next to poetic texts; permanent exhibition of photos, letters and 63 sheets of drawings, writings and poems of all the phases of his work. On the square, Casa Licini, an eighteenth-century manor house on three floors where the painter was born on 22 March 1894 and lived with his Swedish wife Nanny; he died in 1958, the year of the Grand Prix of the Biennale.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Vito Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

18th century façade; inside paintings of the eighteenth century of the mountains, an intense crucifix of Guercino school and the 17th century chapel of the saint, entirely covered with stuccoes.

Centro storico, mura e panorami

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Polygonal walls of the 14th-XV century, today partly incorporated into more recent buildings; going up towards the square a fifteenth-century palace with decorations in terracotta and acute arch of Sienese taste; on the square the Town Hall and Casa Licini. The tour of the center offers views of the nearby villages, the hills towards the sea and the Sibillini.
