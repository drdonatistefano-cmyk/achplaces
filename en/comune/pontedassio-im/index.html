What to see in Pontedassio

Country of the Empire Valley was born at an ancient bridge, already the capital of the castle of Bestagno and the place of origin of Pasta Agnesi: on the heights remain the ruins of the castle of Bestagno of the twelfth century, while in the hamlet Villa Guardia there is a wooden group of the Last Supper of the late sixteenth century inspired by Leonardo's Cenacle.

Ruderi del castello di Bestagno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Remains of the twelfth century of what was one of the main points of control of the lower Empire Valley; Pontedassio was the capital of the castle of Bestagno, a feud under the jurisdiction of the bishop of Albenga, then passed to the Doria and the Savoy. The castle rises on an isolated ridge to the north of the village: the Province of the castle, the tower and the walls of belt, privately owned and in a bad state of conservation.

Museo etnografico del castello di Bestagno

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in September 2001, it occupies three rooms in the center of the village of Bestagno: two ancient communal ovens, where bread was cooked throughout the country, and a cellar. One oven is set up as it was originally, the other collects tools for the cultivation of olive and vine with their dialect names; in the cellar more recent objects like an old projector and a turntable. The findings were collected by the parish priest Don Ramoino and given in comodato to the Municipality in 1997; the DMO cites it as a parish museum with ancient sacred art and everyday objects. Tel. 0183 279026.

Ultima Cena lignea di Villa Guardia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Villa Guardia there is a group of wooden statues of the Last Supper of the late 16th century, inspired by the iconography of Leonardo's Cenacle in Santa Maria delle Grazie in Milan and almost identical to the original. It is one of only two three-dimensional copies of the painting, along with another in Lombardy.

Ponte medievale di Pontedassio

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The bridge of the capital, recorded by the Province as medieval, is at the origin of the name of the village, which would come from a bridge of axes near a mill. Next to it stands the mill purchased in 1824 by Paolo Battista Agnesi, from which the brand Pasta Agnesi was born.

Panchina letteraria di Francesco Biamonti

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

The first literary bench in Liguria, dedicated to the writer Francesco Biamonti: it is made of stone, is located in the courtyard of the town hall and has been decorated by two artists with images of landscapes and phrases in Ligurian dialect.
