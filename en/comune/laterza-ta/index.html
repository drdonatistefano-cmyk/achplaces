What to see in Laterza

One of the capitals of the Apulian majolica, overlooking an immense gravina: the MuMa in the Palazzo Marchesale, the galleries of the ceramists in the historic center and the Oasi Lipu on the Gravina di Laterza, 12 km long and up to 500 meters wide.

4 places to visit in Laterza, in the province of Taranto (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

MuMa – Museo della Maiolica di Laterza

Museums · Themed

Opened in 2015 in the Palazzo Marchesale, renovated in the sixteenth century by Giovan Battista I d'Azzia and Giulia di Capua. It exhibits late seventeenth and early eighteenth century majolica in blue monochrome on white, including albarelli, the dish with the Myth of Callisto by Angelo Antonio D'Alessandro and the Amatoria Cup, and the production of the laertine workshops of Seven and Nineteenth century. Open from Tuesday to Sunday 10-12.30 and 16-18.30, closed on Monday.

  • Where Piazza Plebiscito, Laterza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo dei padri e della maiolica

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 2003 in the Institute of Art, it has two sections: prehistory, with finds of the Neolithic settlement of Fragenaro and of the necropolis of Candile, and local majolica, from the magnagrec and medieval ceramics to the 17th and 18th century laertina figulina.

  • Where Via della Conciliazione, Laterza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Gravina di Laterza e Oasi Lipu

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Gorge 12 km long and wide up to 500 meters, covered with vegetation. The Lipu Oasis, about 800 hectares on the southern edge of the village, has a panoramic point, a tour centre with diorama and educational paths; there live vaccaio, cricket, wolf, otter and istrice. From the viewpoint start path no. 2 (about 1 km downhill and uphill) and a challenging excursion of 7 km; in the gravina there are churches and rock crypts. Visits by reservation with the Oasis manager.

  • Where Via Selva S. Vito, Laterza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · lipu.it

Centro storico e gallerie dei ceramisti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Quiet streets with handicraft workshops and galleries made in ancient caves where you buy ceramics; the viewpoint of the Monument to the Bread offers a view that is worth the detour.

  • Where Centro storico, Laterza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
