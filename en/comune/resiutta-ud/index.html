What to see in Resiutta

Country of the Canal del Ferro in the Natural Park of the Julian Prealps, where for over a century the mines of the Rio Resartico at the slopes of Mount Plauris gave work to the community and since 1844 beer was produced with the waters of the river Resia: today they tell the exhibition of the mine and the ice tunnel of the brewery.

3 places to visit in Resiutta, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Mostra della Miniera del Resartico

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Set up by the Park Authority, it tells the mines of the Rio Resartico, at the northern slopes of Mount Plauris, for over a century one of the main sources of income of Resiutta: videos, photographic panels, interactive installations, a gallery rebuilt in life size with the trolleys, samples of bituminous schist, miner tools and a plastic of the valley. Extraordinary openings for schools and groups on written request at the Park (info@parcoprealpigiulie.it, tel. 0433 550241).

closed until spring 2027 according to the site of the Park

  • Where Via Roma 32, Resiutta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.parcoprealpigiulie.it

Sources: parcoprealpigiulie.it

Galleria ghiacciaia di Resiutta

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Paid entry

Digged on the north-eastern side of Mount Gravizze to preserve the ice with which the beer was cooled: the production was started in 1844 by Carinthia entrepreneurs with the waters of the river Resia and passed in 1881 to the factory of Francesco Dormisch. Renovated, it can be visited as a complement to the old factory, at the same times as the Mine Exhibition; on weekends reservation at least one day before.

opens with the Mine Exhibition, closed until spring 2027 according to the site of the Park

  • Where Via Roma, Resiutta
  • Entry €1 (free residents and under 10)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.parcoprealpigiulie.it

Sources: parcoprealpigiulie.it

Anello di Borgo Cros e Rio Serai

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

An easy ring of about 3 hours and 250 m of difference in altitude, proposed by the Park with meeting in Borgo Povici di Sotto: touches Borgo Cros, village abandoned after the earthquake of 1976 with houses then renovated in respect of the original characteristics, and continues on catwalks, mule tracks and grassy hills; on the return you will meet the large gravel beehive of Rio Putto, the Rio Serai and the Resartico stream.

  • Where Borgo Povici di Sotto, Resiutta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.parcoprealpigiulie.it

Sources: parcoprealpigiulie.it
