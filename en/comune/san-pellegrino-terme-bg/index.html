What to see in San Pellegrino Terme

Spa resort of the Val Brembana, symbol of the Belle Époque and Liberty in Lombardy: the Grand Hotel opened in 1904, the Casino, the spas reopened in 2014 and the funicular of 1909 that goes up to the Vetta; in the surroundings the Grotte del Sogno and the cycle path of the Val Brembana.

Grand Hotel San Pellegrino

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Designed by the architect Romolo Squadrelli with the engineer Luigi Mazzocchi on commission of 1902, it was inaugurated in summer 1904 to attract the clients of Vichy and Carlsbad; among the guests of the Book of Guests are Queen Margherita of Savoia, Pietro Mascagni, Eugenio Montale, Salvatore Quasimodo and Federico Fellini. The central body of seven floors is crowned by a dome and the façade is decorated with frescoes, stone sculptures, wrought irons, caryatids, putti and festoons; the 250 rooms already had elevators, electric light and telephone. Closed in 1979 and bound in 1984-85, it was the subject of a structural restoration planned between 2015 and 2019; it is private property. In front, Prince Umberto Bridge in reinforced concrete of 1924 replaced the temporary bridge of the yard.

Casinò di San Pellegrino Terme

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

Built in the early twentieth century in stone and brick in Liberty style, recognizable for the towers on the facade, it worked as a playhouse only until 1917; today, restored, it hosts congresses, weddings and events. Outside friezes, bas-reliefs with flying deer and butterflies, balustrades and wrought irons; inside crystal chandeliers, frescoes and sculptures, an atrium with eight columns of red Verona marble and a monumental staircase. You can visit with guided tours by local operators (from €7; Liberty tour of the city from €15).

  • Where Viale della Vittoria 53, San Pellegrino Terme
  • Entry Guided tours from €7 (local operators)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Terme QC San Pellegrino

Local life and traditions · Spas

The spa Liberty of the Belle Époque, extensively renovated, reopened in December 2014: frescoes, columns and ceilings from the early twentieth century coexist with contemporary design installations. Over 6,000 m2 indoors and 3,200 m2 outdoors in the Fountain Park offer over 30 routes, panoramic and sensory pools, waterfalls, saunas, Turkish baths, salt and ice rooms and a hydrotherapy area with inhalations, mud and balneotherapy. Each evening the "Aperiterme" is by the pool.

Funicolare di San Pellegrino

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built between autumn 1907 and July 1909, it rises for 710 meters with a difference in altitude of about 435 meters and slopes from 31 to 55% from the center to the summer residences of the Vetta; together with the plant was built the Liberty building of the ticket office. Closed in 1989, it was reopened by the Municipality on Saturday 25 April 2026 with a seasonal calendar; the tickets are paid electronically at the departure and arrival stations.

  • Where Viale della Vittoria 45, San Pellegrino Terme
  • Entry €6 round trip, single travel €4
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · comune.sanpellegrinoterme.bg.it · italia.it

Grotte del Sogno

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Discovered in 1931 by the Speleologist Zanchi and made accessible by the group "Grotte San Pellegrino" with an artificial gallery, catwalks, stairs and electric lighting; in 1941 a second tunnel was dug to drain the water into the flood. They are among the first caves discovered and equipped in Italy, even if of small size: a chasm covered with limestone concretions, stalactites, stalagmites, underground lakes and swallowholes.

Museo Brembano di Scienze Naturali

Museums · Themed

Born in 1978 after the recovery of fossils found in Endenna di Zogno, since 1996 it is located in Villa Speranza and since 2007 it is part of the TRIASSICO.IT museum system. It exhibits about 1,500 palaeontological finds on the geology of the Brembana Valley, the most complete documentation of the lepidopterans in the valley (over 700 species, donation Attilio Torriani), the vegetation bands and endemic plants, a room on fauna and a diorama of the Calvi basin of the German artist Grapes Thuernau. Hours: in winter Saturday 10-12, in summer Tuesday 10-12 and Thursday 15-18; guided tours by reservation, audioguide in Italian and English.

Ciclovia della Val Brembana

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

An asphalted track of about 23 km from Zogno to Piazza Brembana along the Brembo and the track of the old abandoned railway, with many tunnels and a maximum altitude difference of 230 meters, suitable for those with little experience. Tocca San Pellegrino Terme, where it runs for short stretches on ordinary road, San Giovanni Bianco and the village of Cornello dei Tasso; on summer Sundays it is very frequented.

  • Where Attraversamento del centro, San Pellegrino Terme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it
