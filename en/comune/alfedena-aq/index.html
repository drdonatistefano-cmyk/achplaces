What to see in Alfedena

Abruzzo village dominated by the ruins of a medieval castle with Norman octagonal tower; the Sannite necropolis of Campo Consolino has returned the equipment exhibited in the Civic Museum . Antonio De Nino , and the paved streets in silce recall the stonework that paved the streets of Rome, including Piazza San Pietro.

Museo civico aufidenate «Antonio De Nino»

Museums · Archaeological

Founded in 1897, it preserves the most important excavations in the Sannite necropolis of Alfedena-Campo Consolino, started by Lucio Mariani in the late nineteenth century: burials with inumation between the sixth and fourth centuries BC with rich kits, including a bronze belt, a Sannitic armor with three discs and two kardiophylakes, as well as collars and rings in bronze, pearls in vitreous paste, amber necklaces, pendants and weapons.

Ruderi del castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The remains of the medieval castle dominate the village: large walls and an octagonal Norman tower that make it look like an ancient acropolis.

Centro storico e monumento allo scalpellino

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The narrow streets paved in silex, the doorposts and the external stairs show the art of the local stonemasons, so skilled that they paved several streets of Rome, including Piazza San Pietro; in the municipal villa there is the monument to the stonemason.

  • Where Villa comunale, Alfedena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
