What to see in Ossana

Borgo dell'alta Val di Sole at about 1,000 meters, at the confluence of Noce and Vermigliana, with the hamlets of Cusiano and Fucine: the Castello San Michele with its 25-metre mast, the church of San Vigilio from 1180, Stuva Nova and, from the end of November to the Epiphany, the Borgo dei Mille Presepi.

Castello San Michele

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On a hill inhabited by the Bronze Age, probably of Lombard origin and documented since 1191: double wall, sixteenth-century bastion and 25 meters high mast. The bishops of Trento, then of Mainardo di Tirolo, were rebuilt in the fifteenth century by the de Federicis, taken by the rebel peasants in 1525 and used as barracks and deposit Austro-Hungarian in the Great War; of the Province since 1992, it has been open to the public since 2014. From summer 2026 free audio guide in Italian, English and German.

  • Where Via Al Castello, Ossana
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Stuva Nova

Museums · House museums

Ancient stùa with carved wooden walls wanted around 1740 by the archpriest Ludovico Rovereti, with tables, chairs, stove and numerous paintings; burned by Italian soldiers in November 1918, was restored in 1926-1928.

  • Where Ossana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitvaldisole.it

Parco della Pace (ex cimitero austro-ungarico)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The Austro-Hungarian military cemetery, opened in 1915 and then dismantled, is today the Park of Peace, memorial of all wars; in the wall remains the statue of the Kaiserschütze that guarded it.

  • Where Ossana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Bosco Derniga

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

Forest garden open in summer, set up as a didactic area on the site of a forest nursery at the beginning of the twentieth century: it gathers 26 species of trees, shrubs and herbs of the woods of Val di Sole and Trentino, with alpaca and activities for families; there comes the naturalistic path of the Raimondo Fox with installations in QR code.

  • Where Derniga, Ossana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitvaldisole.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · visittrentino.info

Valpiana e Malga del Doss

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valpiana is a pasture at 1,211 meters for excursions and picnics; with Malga del Doss is the site for which Astronomitaly gave Ossana the recognition of the most beautiful skies in Italy GOLD level.

  • Where Valpiana, Ossana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
