What to see in Cattolica Eraclea

In the municipal territory, on the promontory of Capo Bianco overlooking the sea, there are the ruins of the Greek city of Eraclea Minoa with the theater of the fourth century BC and the Antiquarium, inside the natural reserve Foce of the river Platani, with the amphitheatre beach under the white cliff.

Area archeologica, Teatro e Antiquarium di Eraclea Minoa

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

City founded by the Selinuntini in the 6th century BC, then under Akragas, punic after 409-406 BC and re-colonized by Publius Rupilius in 132 BC; a walls of about 6 km encloses the plateau. The excavations, begun in the fifties, have exposed walls, necropolis, houses and the theatre of the fourth century BC in blocks of marna, facing the sea and protected by a temporary cover. The small Antiquarium at the entrance exhibits figurative ceramics, terracotta statuettes and accessories.

Riserva naturale Foce del fiume Platani

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Reserve of 206,88 hectares established in 1984 between Cattolica Eraclea and Ribera, at the mouth of the Platani dominated by the cliff of Capo Bianco, with sandy beaches, dunal cordons, promontories and clay gullies; protects the birds, for many migrators from Africa first stop.

  • Where Foce del Platani, Cattolica Eraclea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: orbs.regione.sicilia.it · italia.it

Spiaggia di Eraclea Minoa e Capo Bianco

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Amphitheatre beach under the walls of Capo Bianco, a white limestone cliff about 75 meters high; the mud of the head offers to the bathers natural treatments outdoors. In the village under the archaeological area tourist facilities and a camping in the shade of a forest of eucalyptuses.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
