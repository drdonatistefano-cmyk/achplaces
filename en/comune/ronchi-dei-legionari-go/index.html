What to see in Ronchi dei Legionari

Cittadina della Bisiacaria seat of the regional airport: the neoclassical Villa Vicentini Miniussi with the photo library of the Monfalconese Cultural Consortium, the Roman villa discovered in 1987 with its Antiquarium and, on the Karst, Mount Six Busi of the Great War.

5 places to visit in Ronchi dei Legionari, in the province of Gorizia (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa Vicentini Miniussi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Neoclassical villa built by Giacomo Vicentini after 1829, with frescoes, central colonnade façade and an octagonal cellar under the garden accessible from an underground passage. It is the seat of the Monfalconese Cultural Consortium, with archive of memory, library, magazine "Il Territorio" and the photo library that has been active since 1978 (about 12,000 photographs on the history of Bisiacaria and a collection of cameras); it hosts conferences and exhibitions. Visits by reservation.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Villa romana

History and architecture · Archaeology

Residential and productive rustic villa built in the middle of the 1st century BC and abandoned in the 3rd century AD after a fire, with representative rooms, mosaic floors and suspensurae heating; so far the eastern sector has been dug. A walkway with educational panels crosses the complex and shows some restored mosaics.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it · turismofvg.it

Antiquarium comunale

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 2012 in the requalified barn of an 18th century manor house in the centre, it exhibits on two floors mosaics, coins, ceramics, glass and metal objects of the Roman villa discovered in 1987, with panels and a didactic video.

  • Where Centro di Ronchi dei Legionari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it · turismofvg.it

Monte Sei Busi e Dolina del XV Bersaglieri

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

117 meters high of the isontian Karst, in front of the shrine of Redipuglia, between Fogliano Redipuglia and Ronchi dei Legionari, the scene of bloody fighting in the Great War; a non-binding walk touches the remains of the Dolina of the XV Bersaglieri, open-air museum, and the Mazzoldi trench with inscriptions and engraved drawings. From the top you can see the sea.

  • Where Monte Sei Busi, Ronchi dei Legionari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Lapide di D'Annunzio

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

On the highway towards Monfalcone, a plaque marks the house that hosted Gabriele D'Annunzio and recalls the expedition that occupied Rijeka on September 12, 1919; not far from a column in front of the cemetery.

  • Where Strada statale per Monfalcone, Ronchi dei Legionari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it
