What to see in Pizzo

A small town on a promontory in the middle of the Gulf of Sant'Eufemia, linked to the end of Gioacchino Murat, shot in the Aragonese castle in 1815; on the sea the little church of Piedigrotta dug into the tuff, in the square the famous Tartufo di Pizzo ice cream.

6 places to visit in Pizzo, in the province of Vibo Valentia (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Aragonese (Castello Murat) e Museo Murattiano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built in the second half of the 15th century by Ferdinand I of Aragon on the western side of the village: quadrangular plan with two cylindrical towers, the major (Maestra Tower) of Angevin age (1380). Here Gioacchino Murat, landed at the Navy on October 8, 1815 to regain the Kingdom of Naples, was imprisoned and shot on October 13 ('Mirate to the chest, not to the face'). Inside the Museo Murattiano reconstructs the environment of those days; managed by the Municipality in the Civic Museums of Pizzo.

  • Where Via P. Marincola, Pizzo
  • Entry €5, reduced €3,50; cumulative with Foot €8
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.pizzo.vv.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Chiesa di Piedigrotta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Digged in the sandstone on the coast next to the Regional Marine Park . Costa degli Dei: according to tradition, they obtained it at the end of the seventeenth century Neapolitan shipwrecks, which attributed salvation to a painting of the Madonna found on the beach. From 1880 about the local artist Angelo Barone, then his son Alfonso, expanded it by sculpting statues of sacred scenes and the life of Christ in the tuff. Festival on July 2nd with a procession to the sea; in winter open only Saturday and Sunday.

  • Where Località Madonnella, Via Riviera Prangi (SS522), Pizzo
  • Entry €5, reduced €3,50; cumulative with the Castle €8
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.pizzo.vv.it · italia.it

Piazza della Repubblica e Tartufo di Pizzo

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry · Viewpoint

The small panoramic square just a few steps from the castle is the living room of the historic center, with views of the Costa degli Dei and seat of the main events. In the historic bars you can taste the Truffle of Pizzo, hazelnut ice cream shaped by hand in semisphere, dusted with cocoa and with a melted chocolate heart, whose recipe is guarded by the active ice cream parlors since 1940.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica, Pizzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.pizzo.vv.it

Spiaggia della Marina e passeggiata Pizzapundi

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The most central and famous beach of Pizzo, along the Lungomare Cristoforo Colombo; there ends the walk of Pizzapundi, from which at sunset you can see the Stromboli.

  • Where Lungomare Cristoforo Colombo, Pizzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.pizzo.vv.it

Museo del mare di Pizzo Forno

Museums · Themed

Collecting more than 100,000 shells from all over the world, some 5,000 fossils, hundreds of embalmed and mummified fish, fish and cetacean skeletons, naval and fishing equipment, seashells and handmade corals and 400 marine paintings.

  • Where Via Prangi, Pizzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
