What to see in Cervia

The city of salt: salt salt, salt stores, salt mines, pine forests and the beaches of Milano Marittima, Pinarella and Tagliata.

7 places to visit in Cervia, in the province of Ravenna (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Salina di Cervia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Over 800 hectares of salty pools with herons, Italian knights and pink flamingos; Salina Camillone still produces by hand sweet salt, Presidio Slow Food.

  • Where Centro Visite Salina, Cervia
  • Entry Guided tours for a fee from the Visitor Centre (on foot or by electric boat)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

MUSA, Museo del Sale

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the Warehouse of the Sale Torre on the canal port, tools, documents and photos of the work of the saltworks; the Salina Camillone is its open-air section.

  • Where Viale Nazario Sauro 24, Cervia
  • Entry For a fee; free on the first Sunday of the month and always up to 12 years (secondary source)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.turismo.comunecervia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: turismo.comunecervia.it · cultura.gov.it · familygo.eu · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Quadrilatero di Cervia Nuova e Torre San Michele

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The city of foundation built between 1698 and 1714, with the houses of the saltworks to act as walls, Piazza Pisacane and the tower on the canal port.

  • Where Centro storico, Cervia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Porto canale e Borgomarina

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

The fishing village along the canal, with sailing boats on the third and summer markets.

  • Where Porto canale, Cervia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Pineta di Cervia e Milano Marittima

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

More than 300 hectares of pine forest between the city and the sea, with paths, bike routes and kayaks in the canal.

  • Where Cervia e Milano Marittima
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Casa delle Farfalle

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Paid entry

Tropical greenhouse with hundreds of free butterflies and an insectario, in the pinewood of Milano Marittima.

  • Where Milano Marittima, Cervia
  • Entry For a fee: rates on the official website
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Spiagge di Milano Marittima, Pinarella e Tagliata

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

The beaches of the municipality, from the promenade of Milano Marittima to the Riviera dei Pini.

  • Where Litorale di Cervia
  • Entry Free beaches; establishments with fee
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
