Valli di Ostellato

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Three wrecks of the Mezzano Valley, about 125 hectares of oasis of protection of fauna.

  • Entry Free

Pieve di San Vito

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque church founded according to tradition in 1027, with bell tower of 1228 and a Roman sarcophagus in the crypt.

  • Where Ostellato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
