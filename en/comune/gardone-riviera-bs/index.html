What to see in Gardone Riviera

Locality of the Brescian shore of Garda linked to Gabriele d'Annunzio, who created the Vittoriale degli Italiani between 1921 and 1938; with the Botanical Garden André Heller, the lakefront and the Liberty villas and déco immersed in Mediterranean parks.

5 places to visit in Gardone Riviera, in the province of Brescia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Il Vittoriale degli Italiani

Museums · House museums · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Last great work of D'Annunzio, who lived there since 1921 and donated it to the State in 1930; built with architect G. C. Maroni between 1921 and 1938, includes the Priory (the house, which can be visited only with a guide in about 35 minutes, with a library of 33,000 volumes), museums, park, auditorium, the open air theatre with 1,500 places with summer events, the ship Puglia and on top the mausoleum where the poet is buried.

Giardino botanico André Heller

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

Founded in 1903 by Austrian dentist and botanist Arthur Hruska with plants from the Alps, Himalayas, Japan, Australia, Canada and New Zealand; purchased in 1988 by artist André Heller, it combines about 3,000 species with works by Keith Haring, Roy Lichtenstein, Rodin, Miró and Mimmo Paladino, with a Japanese garden. In spring, tulips, wisteria, magnolias, azaleas and water lilies flourish.

Lungolago

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Walk among oleanders, orange trees and roses, with views of the island of Garda and, in winter, on the snow-covered Monte Baldo.
