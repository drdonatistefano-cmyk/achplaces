What to see in Viù

Country that gives its name to one of the three valleys of Lanzo, in a basin crossed by the Stura di Viù, with the parish church of San Martino, the largest of the valleys, and the Celtic Stone of the Mothers. From here you can climb to the Colle del Lys, towards the Val di Susa, home to a natural park and the Ecomuseum of the Resistance Carlo Mastri.

Ecomuseo della Resistenza «Carlo Mastri»

Museums · Themed · Free entry

The place of memory is located on the watershed between Valli di Lanzo and Val di Susa, in the old cantoniere house of Colle del Lys: on the ground floor the reception, on the first floor the photographic exhibition on the partisans of the 17th Brigade Garibaldi .Felice Cima , which here had its base from 1943 to 1945; the exhibition revolves around the idea of resilience, human and natural. Parco naturale del Colle del Lys e Sentieri Partigiani

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the crossing between Viù and the Val di Susa the park preserves an ancient network of trails and mule tracks that connected the hamlets and that during the Resistance served to the 17th Brigade Garibaldi. The Nazi Fascist rake of July 2, 1944, in which many young partisans died, is remembered by a tower, a memorial garden and a stone.

  • Where Colle del Lys, Viù
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Parrocchiale di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

According to tradition, the parish was mentioned as early as 1011 when Bishop Landolfo confirmed the donation of the church to the monastery of San Solutore; after 1781 it was extended to the present size, with three naves, the largest of the Lanzo Valleys. Inside an eighteenth-century Crucifix by Stefano Maria Clemente and the altar of Our Lady of the Rosary with altarpiece from 1869; next to the chapel of the Brotherhood of the Most Holy Name of Jesus. Information: Tel. 0123 696117.

  • Where Piazza Cibrario, Viù
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: turismotorino.org

Pietra delle Madri

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Masso also known as Masso Falchero, named after the donor, discovered by Marco Cima in 1992 in a mill at the Mills Ninìn, where for centuries it was the basis of a press for nut oil. The three sculpted figures are dated by various scholars at the second Iron Age (III-II century BC) and would represent the Three Mothers of Celtic culture, linked to life, fertility and the cycle of the seasons. Exposed in the market square.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Veneto 2, Viù
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Ciarm del Prete

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

It rises 2,390 meters overlooking a natural amphitheatre visible from the hamlet Tornetti, among the favorite destinations of the hikers of the valley.
