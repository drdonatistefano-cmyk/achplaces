What to see in Nocera Umbra

Walled city on the Flaminia, famous for its waters: on top of the hill the Duomo and the tower of the Bell Tower, in Piazza Caprera the Civic Museum in the church of San Francesco with the polyptych of the pupil, the Archaeological Museum with the Lombard finds; outside the city Bagni di Nocera with the Angelica Source.

Museo civico di San Francesco

Museums · Old masters

In the former church of San Francesco (1368), enlarged and frescoed in the Quattro e prima Cinquecento, reopened in 2003 after the earthquake of 1997. It has a shaped cross from the second half of the thirteenth century, the great polyptych by Niccolò di Liberatore, known as the pupil, painted in 1483 for the main altar of the Duomo, the Encounter of Gioacchino and Anna at the Aurea Gate of Matteo da Gualdo with his frescoes and his circle, and two Longobard furnishings from the necropolis of the Portone, in storage by the Museum of the High Middle Ages in Rome. Info: tel. 0742 818640.

Museo archeologico e Centro di documentazione siti territoriali

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 2010 in the former municipal palace: tools in flint and limestone attributed to Homo erectus and Homo sapiens, ceramics and objects in bronze and iron; terracotta, ceramic with black paint, marbles, inscriptions and mosaics from the settlements along the Flaminia; finds of the Lombard necropolis of the Portone (165 tombs with gold, silver, precious stones). The conference room has frescoes from the fifteenth century; there is a plaque in Garibaldi already on Porta Nuova. Info: tel. 333 2852940.

Duomo di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Romanesque building of the 12th century on the walls at the top of the old town, completely renovated in 1448; in the sacristy a majolica floor, in a seventeenth-century chapel by Julius Caesar Angels. Until 1986 it was a cathedral of the diocese of Nocera and Gualdo; according to the Commune it would rise on a temple dedicated to the goddess Favonia.

Piazza Caprera e Via del Borgo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Piazza Caprera is the medieval square of the Municipality, with three-four-century palaces of the civil and religious authorities, reached by Porta Vecchia. Via del Borgo, of thirteenth century origin, has houses with traces of frescoes and Romanesque and Gothic portals; around the alleys of Via Camilli, Via Fossatello, Via Ortaccio and Via Il Sasso.

Passeggiata delle antiche mura

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Itinerary of the Municipality from viale dei Belgi along bastions and wounds of the municipal park to Porta San Martino, main door before the thirteenth century on the ancient Flaminia, and to the Palombara tower, the oldest defence tower, circular like the Byzantine towers; then Largo Porta Santa Croce, Palazzo Olivieri Benigni, the Water Garden, the Monument to the Fallen of 1925 by Turillo Sindoni, merged with the bronze of the cannons of Vittorio Veneto, and the sixteenth century Porta Nuova, the vault of which was removed in 1929 for the Mille Miglia. One stretch is narrow and steep.

Bagni di Nocera e Sorgente Angelica

Local life and traditions · Spas

The Angelica Spring, in Bagni di Nocera, was drunk for therapeutic purposes as early as 1500 and was among the most popular spas in central Italy, with guests such as Vincenzo Monti, Pirandello and Leopardi; in September 1805 Humboldt and Gay-Lussac came to analyze the water, then exported from Felice Bisleri to the Americas and India.
