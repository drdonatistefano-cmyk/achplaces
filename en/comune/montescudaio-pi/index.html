What to see in Montescudaio

Medieval village of the Val di Cecina, a town of wine and bread, with views from the walls of the Tyrrhenian Sea and the islands.

4 places to visit in Montescudaio, in the province of Pisa (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Montescudaio in the guide with map and filters

Castello e Guardiola

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The walls of the castle up to 15 meters high and the Guardiola, the only watchtower left, with views from the plain of Cecina to the sea.

  • Where Montescudaio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Piazza Matteotti e palazzi nobiliari

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The square with the birthplace of the sculptor Italo Griselli and the Marchionneschi, Ridolfi and Guerrini palaces, today town hall.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Montescudaio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Santa Maria Assunta e Santissima Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the Piazzale del Castello the church of Santa Maria Assunta with panoramic views, and in the village the Santissima Annunziata.

  • Where Montescudaio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Via dei Pellegrini e giardino di Scornabecchi

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

The path in the surroundings with the historic Badia and the garden of Scornabecchi, an oasis with medieval springs.

  • Where Montescudaio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
