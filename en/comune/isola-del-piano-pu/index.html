Monastero di Montebello

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

On the hills between Isola del Piano and Montefeltro, in 1377 Blessed Pietro Gambacorta arrived there; today it is the headquarters of the Agricultural Cooperative Girolomoni, one of the first organic agricultural cooperatives in Italy, and hosts meetings, educational and cultural activities, including usually a stage of the summer festival Â"Ville and Castella" .

Palazzo del feudatario e chiesa parrocchiale dei Girolamini

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the center, on the ruins of the Castle of the Island, the palace of the feudatorio (now Battistoni) has an artistic portal of the sixteenth century; next stood the large convent of the Girolamini, demolished in the early Sixties. The church of the friars, today parish, restored and enlarged, contains three fine paintings including the Madonna del Carmine, already in the first Girolamine church.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Isola del Piano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Castelgagliardo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Small nucleus of Malatesta origin on the borders with the Duchy of Urbino, already seat of Municipality; the church with the canonical and the mighty walls of the 13th century remain.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Foresta demaniale delle Cesane

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Forest plateau between Urbino, Isola del Piano and Fossombrone, up to 648 meters, with about 1500 hectares of wood at high stem born largely from the reforestations started in 1915 with black pines and continued until the 1950s, so much to form a living catalog of conifers; there live deer, wild boars, foxes, badgers and raptors. Sixteen marked trails of non-high difficulty and a mountain bike circuit.

  • Where Le Cesane, Isola del Piano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: turismo.comune.isola-del-piano.ps.it · letsmarche.it
