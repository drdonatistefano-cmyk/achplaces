What to see in Carmagnola

Town of the plain south of Turin linked to hemp and ropes for ships: the thirteenth-century castle today town hall, the palaces of the center, the Cistercian abbey of Casanova and small civic museums, from naval to natural history.

5 places to visit in Carmagnola, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Carmagnola in the guide with map and filters

Centro storico, castello e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the center the castle of the thirteenth century, now seat of the Municipality, the fifteenth-century church of Sant'Agostino and palaces such as Palazzo Lomellini, Casa Cavassa and the House of the Meridians; there is also a synagogue.

  • Where Centro storico, Carmagnola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Abbazia di Casanova e Museo di Arte Sacra

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Cistercian abbey complex; in the great sacristy of the church the museum exhibits important sacred furnishings.

  • Where Piazza Antica Abbazia 3, Carmagnola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Museo Civico Navale

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Founded in 1996 in the former public weight, it recalls the link of the city with the sea through sails and hemp ropes: history of the Navy, naval activity from Unity to today, marine environment and modeling, with scafandron, periscope and compass.

  • Where Piazza Mazzini 1, Carmagnola
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: abbonamentomusei.it · turismotorino.org

Museo Civico di Storia Naturale

Museums · Science and university

Born after the floods of 1973-74, from 1990 at Cascina Vigna in the city park: minerals, plants and animals, with the entomological collection as the most important nucleus; visits by appointment.

  • Where Cascina Vigna, Carmagnola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: storianaturale.org · turismotorino.org

Ecomuseo della Cultura e della Lavorazione della Canapa

Museums · Themed

It preserves and transmits the processing of hemp and the art of rope.

  • Where Carmagnola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org
