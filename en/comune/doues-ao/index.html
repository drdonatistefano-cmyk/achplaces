What to see in Doues

Sunny terrace of Valpelline on a morainic basin at 1176 meters, the ancient Roman Dovia, with views of Mont Emilius.

4 places to visit in Doues, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Biagio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In 1790, with the chapel of St. Nicholas founded by the De La Crête, ancient lords of Doues, who are buried there; the parish is already mentioned in 1176.

  • Where Doues
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · gestlovevda.it

Cappella della Santissima Trinità di Châtelair

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The oldest of the five chapels, at 1430 meters on a height above the village.

  • Where Châtelair, Doues
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Col Champillon e alpeggio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Alpeggio with the chapel of Notre-Dame-des-Neiges at 1680 meters and the refuge suitable for star observation; more on the hill with 360° panorama.

  • Where Champillon, Doues
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it
