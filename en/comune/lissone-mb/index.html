What to see in Lissone

Città della Brianza known for its MAC, the contemporary art museum born from the collection of the Lissone Prize (1946-1967), and for the rationalist Palazzo Terragni; in the center the prepositural of Saints Peter and Paul, with a bell tower of more than 77 meters.

MAC Museo d'Arte Contemporanea

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

Opened in 2000 in the area of the former furniture factory Angelo Meroni (founded in 1840), of which it retains the most significant part, on a project by Marco Terenghi. The permanent collection was largely born from the Lissone Prize (1946-1967), a showcase of the international avant-garde of the 1950s and 1960s, with works by Emilio Vedova, Piero Dorazio, Ennio Morlotti, Karel Appel, Antoni Tàpies, Emilio Scanavino, Renato Birolli and Mario Schifano, plus a section dedicated to the Lissonese Gino Meloni. It hosts temporary exhibitions and, every other year, the Lissone Prize for painting and the Lissone Design Prize; since 2025 it is part of AMACI. Open Wednesday-Friday 16-19, Saturday and Sunday 11-19; guided tours for a fee.

  • Where Viale Elisa Ancona 6, Lissone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.lissone.mb.it

Palazzo Terragni

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Built between 1938 and 1940 as a Fascio House on a project by Giuseppe Terragni and Antonio Carminati, winners of a competition; after the Liberation became the House of the People and the theatre room a cinema. Purchased by the Municipality in 1968 and named after Terragni in 1975, it is now a gallery, conference center and cultural event venue. It has two floors with large windows and a stone tower of Moltrasio with the arengary and monument to the fallen; the north part was never completed.

  • Where Piazza Libertà 2, Lissone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.lissone.mb.it

Sources: segnalazioni.comune.lissone.mb.it · fondoambiente.it · comune.lissone.mb.it

Chiesa prepositurale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The ancient church, mentioned by the second half of the thirteenth century, was expanded several times, also by Simone Cantoni at the end of the eighteenth century; the new church, begun in 1904 and consecrated in 1926, is three naves on a Latin cross plan in brick and stone, in eclectic style between late Romanesque and Gothic, with octagonal apses. The bell tower, 77.45 m high, is among the highest in Italy and leads to the top of the Golden Angel, considered protector of Lissone.

  • Where Piazza Giovanni XXIII, Lissone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.lissone.mb.it

Sources: comune.lissone.mb.it

Villa Baldironi Reati

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa probably from the early sixteenth century, originally with family chapel and a large park, transformed several times over the centuries. The ground floor was restored and reopened in 2001, the first floor in 2007; today civil weddings are celebrated.

open to the public only on special occasions (FAI)

  • Where Via Padre Reginaldo Giuliani 2, Lissone
