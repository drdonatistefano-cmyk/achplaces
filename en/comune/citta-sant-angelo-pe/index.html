What to see in Città Sant'Angelo

Village 325 meters between sea and mountain, 18 km from Pescara, with views of the Adriatic, Maiella and Gran Sasso: course of noble palaces, alleys and churches, dominated by the collegiate church of San Michele Arcangelo.

5 places to visit in Città Sant'Angelo, in the province of Pescara (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Collegiata di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Built in 1200 on a building perhaps of the VIII-IX century and collegiate since 1353: portal of Raimondo di Poggio (1326), fifteenth-century portico, 47-metre bell tower, wooden ceiling with coffers, frescoes fourteenth-century attributed to the Master of Offida, wooden statue of St. Michael of the fourteenth century and seventeenth-century choir.

Chiesa e convento di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The fourteenth-century polychrome portal of Raimondo di Poggio, 15th-century square bell tower and mosaic floor of 1845; rebuilt in Baroque forms in 1741. The cloister is in the former convent, today the town hall; inside also the Clarisse parlour and the wheel of the exhibits.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · abruzzoturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Chiara

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The private chapel of the Clarisse with a trilobed plan, unique in the region, decorated with stuccoes, gilding and mosaic floor; the former monastery is now a cultural center.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Corso e palazzi del centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Along the course the palaces Giampietro (with medieval courtyard), Colamico, Sozj, Ursini and Coppa Zuccari; further on the Baronial Palace, the oldest noble residence of the village. Alleys, churches and views of the sea and mountains.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · abruzzoturismo.it · italia.it

Porte storiche

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Porta Casale e Porta Licinia, probably fourteenth century, and Porta Sant'Egidio, built between the late eighteenth and mid nineteenth century next to Porta Sant'Antonio, today Porta Nuova.
