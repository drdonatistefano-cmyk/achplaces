What to see in Vilminore di Scalve

The capital of Val di Scalve is linked to the dam of Gleno, whose collapse on 1 December 1923 caused more than 500 victims: the ruins are a destination for excursions and in the village an exhibition space preserves its memory. In the surroundings the Alpine Arboretum Gleno, with over 100 species of the valley, and the eighteenth-century water forge of Teveno.

Ruderi della diga del Gleno

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

On 1 December 1923 the central pillar of the dam yielded under the pressure of about 6 million cubic meters of water: the wave overwhelmed Bueggio, the sanctuary of the Madonnina and the hamlet of Dezzo, where 209 people died, and reached Lake Iseo in about 45 minutes, with over 500 total victims. The ruins, in a basin at the foot of Mount Gleno, can be reached by two paths from Piazza and Nona; in summer a shuttle connects Vilminore to Piazza and are possible guided excursions for small groups.

Spazio Espositivo Gleno

Museums · City history

Museum in the center of Vilminore opened by Pro Loco with the support of Regione Lombardia: collects documents, photographs, videos and testimonies on the disaster of the Gleno of 1923. Information: Pro Loco di Vilminore, tel. 0346 51002.

  • Where Centro di Vilminore di Scalve
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: visitbergamo.net

Arboreto Alpino Gleno

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Free entry

Arboreto with over 100 tree species typical of Val di Scalve, planted in the early 2000s, about 500 m from Vilminore on the road to Bueggio, near the Povo stream. It includes the Museum of Woods with 72 types of wood described, a multimedia classroom, a garden with the yellow potato of the valley, a picnic area and an area where coal is produced using the traditional method (poiat).

  • Where Via per Oltrepovo, Vilminore di Scalve
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: visitbergamo.net · in-lombardia.it

Fucina di Teveno

Local life and traditions · Crafts · Free entry

It was built on a large rock, probably in the eighteenth century: the hammer moved by the water of the river Nembo produced the tools of the village. It is the last of about 30 forges of the Val di Scalve and preserves the crankshaft, the grinding wheel, the forge and a hydrohelic horn; in 2016 it was ranked 175th at the FAI census "The Places of the Heart." Visits by reservation with Pro Loco (tel. +39 0346 51002).

  • Where Via San Michele 1, Teveno, Vilminore di Scalve
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: visitbergamo.net
