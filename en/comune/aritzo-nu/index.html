What to see in Aritzo

Country of holiday about 800 meters in the massif of Gennargentu, in the Barbagia of Belvì, among chestnut and hazelnut woods: the parish church of San Michele Arcangelo with Gothic-Aragonese parts, the Spanish prisons of Sa Bovida with the exhibition \ Bruxas Â, the Ecomuseum of the Sardinian mountain and the natural monument of Su Texile.

Sa Bovida, carcere spagnolo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Sixteenth century building in shale, mud and chestnut wood in front of the parish, maximum security prison until the 1940s; in 1793 there were French officers imprisoned during an attempt to disembark Napoleon. The name comes from the vaulted underpass, In the male and female cells restored the permanent exhibition "Bruxas" on witchcraft in Sardinia between the fifteenth and seventeenth centuries, with ritual objects, a section on the Inquisition with instruments of torture and the sanbenito; in the courtyard an ancient sundial. From September to May Tuesday-Sunday 10-13 and 15-18, from June to August 10-13 and 16-19.

  • Where Via G. Marconi, Aritzo
  • Entry €3 (6 years); cumulative 4 museums €8, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Ecomuseo della montagna sarda, Museo etnografico

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Centre of the Ecomuseum, in the Pastissu park: eight rooms on the agropastoral culture barbaric, from the shepherds with medallions of "su Giuramentu" used to solve disputes on pastures and livestock, to the products of the wood and to the merchants wandering (torrono, wood, carapigna), from wool to traditional clothes, from the wax workshop to the traditional house, up to the carved cases of Aritzo of the wedding set and to the salt on wood, agriculture, blacksmiths and maniscalci.

  • Where Parco Pastissu, Aritzo
  • Entry Cumulation 4 museums €8, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · sardegnacultura.it · italia.it

Museo Antonio Mura

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

In the restored building of the late nineteenth century that housed the town hall, a permanent space dedicated to the painter and engraver Aritzese Antonio Mura, with works related to the traditions and landscapes of Sardinia coming from the parish of San Michele Arcangelo and private collections; there is also a collection of coffers (scashes) on display.

  • Where Pratz'e Iscola, Aritzo
  • Entry Cumulation 4 museums €8, reduced €5 (with guided tour)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Casa Devilla

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

A 17th century building in the historic centre, presented by the Ecomuseum as a home to a bourgeois family of the 19th century; it is one of the museum's locations along with the Ethnographic Museum, Sa Bovida and the Mura Museum.

  • Where Centro storico, Aritzo
  • Entry Cumulation 4 museums €8, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · sardegnacultura.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the center of the village; the oldest part dates back to the year 1000 and the restoration of 1917 left intact the Gothic-Aragonese parts of the 14th-XV century. Inside paintings, statues, an eighteenth-century organ and an altar in polychrome marble.

  • Where Centro, Aritzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Su Bastione e sorgenti di Is Alinos e Funtana de Sant'Antoni

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Outside the parish, Su Bastione overlooks the chestnut and hazelnut woods and is the starting point for excursions on foot or on horseback; the springs of Is Alinos and Funtana de Sant'Antoni are the most frequented for their light water.

  • Where Presso la parrocchiale di San Michele, Aritzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Su Texile

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

A "taccu" , cylinder of limestone and mesozoic dolomia on a cone of schists, about 24 meters high and 50-70 wide, which reaches almost 1,000; rest of the Jurassic cover of Gennargentu, is a natural monument since 1989 and symbol of the country. It is located about 2 km from the village along the road towards the cantoniera of Cossatzu, among pastures, holm oaks, chestnuts and hazelnuts.

  • Where Strada per la cantoniera Cossatzu, Aritzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Neviere di Funtana Cungiada

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 1,300 meters, near the church of Santa Maria della Neve, the 'domos de nie', 17th century wells used as snow.

  • Where Funtana Cungiada, Aritzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
