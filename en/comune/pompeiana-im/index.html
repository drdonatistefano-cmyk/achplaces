What to see in Pompeiana

Village among the olive groves of the Argentina-Armea valley, divided into small villages on the hill overlooking the sea, with the watchtower of the Panei as symbol.

4 places to visit in Pompeiana, in the province of Imperia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Pompeiana in the guide with map and filters

Torre dei Panei

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Defense tower and sighting against the Barbareschi, built between the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries and reachable for a mule track; a plaque recalls the resistance of the inhabitants to the attack of Ulugh Ali on 20 August 1563.

  • Where Pompeiana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page rivieradeifiori.travel

Sources: rivieradeifiori.travel

Borgata Cae Süttane

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The largest hamlet, with winding alleys, climbs and covered passages typical of the medieval hillside villages, connected to the others by the ancient creuze.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Oratorio di Santa Maria Maddalena

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Oratory dedicated to the saint who, according to tradition, stayed in Pompeiana on his journey to Provence.
