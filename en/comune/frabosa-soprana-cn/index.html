What to see in Frabosa Soprana

The holiday resort of the Monregalese Valleys since the end of the nineteenth century, at the foot of the Moro and Malanotte mountains: in Val Corsaglia the Grotta di Bossea, the first tourist cave of Italy, in the village the Ecomuseum of Marble with the Verzino used by Guarini for the Chapel of the Shroud, in Fontane the ethnographic museum Cesare Vinaj.

5 places to visit in Frabosa Soprana, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Grotta di Bossea

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry

Explored in 1850 by valleymen led by Domenico Mora and opened to the public on 2 August 1874, the first tourist cave in Italy; protected area managed by the Marguareis Park. The lower part has grandiose environments, the upper one narrow galleries on several floors, separated by the waterfall of Lake Ernestina; in the Hall of the Temple is exposed the reconstructed skeleton of a cave bear. Constant temperature of 9 °C, guided tours all year round with mandatory booking.

  • Where Località Bossea 10, Frabosa Soprana
  • Entry Adults full tour €14, short tour €12; children 3-10 years €9 / €7
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.grottadibossea.com

Sources: grottadibossea.com · visitcuneese.it

Ecomuseo del Marmo

Museums · Themed

Champions of the marble of Frabosa, the Verzino chosen in the seventeenth century for many Turinese churches and Guarino Guarini for the dome of the Chapel of the Shroud, exposed with original tools along the route to the multipurpose hall and the hall of the Town Hall, with the installation \"Marmi to touch" by Daniele Aletti. In Via di Bossea 2 the workshop-museum of sharp irons, restored in 2017, tells of the Frabousan knives documented since 1790 (free visit on request).

  • Where Municipio e via di Bossea 2, Frabosa Soprana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Museo etnografico Cesare Vinaj

Museums · Themed

Curated by the Association E. Kyé', exhibits objects and memories of the mountain life of the past and recalls the easternmost Provençal enclave of Europe present in the valley. Open by reservation (tel. +39 0174 349240, +39 338 3843572).

  • Where Piazza Bersezio 2, località Fontane, Frabosa Soprana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Fontane e la Big Bench

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Frazione di Frabosa Soprana, which houses the ethnographic museum and one of the giant benches of the artist Chris Bangle, a panoramic point.

  • Where Località Fontane, Frabosa Soprana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Mondolè Ski e Falesia dei Quarzini

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Frabosa Soprana is part, with Prato Nevoso and Artesina, of the ski area of Mondolè, with 100 km of slopes and 30 lifts; in summer the Falesia dei Quarzini is a climbing wall.

  • Where Frabosa Soprana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
