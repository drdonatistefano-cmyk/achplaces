What to see in Castelrotto

Country at the foot of the Alpe di Siusi, the largest plateau in Europe, with the Sciliar massif and the Santner peak in the background: in Kraus square the 82-metre bell tower, the parish church of Saints Peter and Paul and the town hall of 1607, around houses with facades painted by Eduard Burgauner and, on the Colle, the Via Crucis among the porphyry rocks.

Piazza Kraus e facciate dipinte

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The square brings together bell tower, church, town hall of 1607 (with wooden council hall and a sixteenth-century room with coffers) and historic houses; on clear days you can see the Sciliar and the tip Santner. Many of the country's facades were painted by Eduard Burgauner (1873-1913), including Mendelhaus, Edenberg, Landhaus and Wolfwirt; Villa Felseck, in liberty and neo-baroque style, represents the months with rites and peasant works. Historic also hotels Goldenes Rössl, of the seventeenth century, and Zum Wolf.

  • Where Piazza Kraus, Castelrotto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Campanile di Castelrotto

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The 82-metre-high Baroque tower, rebuilt after the 1753 fire, is the third tallest bell tower in the province of Bolzano and can be seen from afar; it is visited with guided tours and its 325 wooden steps lead to a view of the Dolomites.

  • Where Piazza Kraus, Castelrotto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · suedtirol.info · seiseralm.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Dean's seat of early medieval origins, the current church in classical style dates back to the mid-19th century and forms with the isolated bell tower, the town hall and the houses of the square a harmonious complex. Open every day from 8 am to 6 pm; Sunday Mass at 10 am in German and, from mid-July to August, at 11.15 am in Italian. At the tourist office there is the guide to the churches of Castelrotto and Siusi (€5).

  • Where Piazza Kraus 1, Castelrotto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: seiseralm.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Colle (Kofel) e Via Crucis

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

The wooded hill of porphyry in the village, with the ruins of the destroyed castle that gave the name to Castelrotto: a path of about 20 minutes from the center climbs between the rocks with seven chapels of the Calvary of the late seventeenth century (mountain of Olives, flagellation, coronation of thorns, burial) to a tower; at the top view on the roofs, the bell tower, the tip of Santner and the Sciliar. There are also a recreation area and a playground.

  • Where Piazza Kraus, Castelrotto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it · seiseralm.it

Museums · Themed

Alpe di Siusi

Nature and landscape · Mountains · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest high altitude alpine pasture in Europe: 56 km2 between 1,630 and 2,350 meters, with about 350 km of trails. On the way to the reserve, private traffic is forbidden from 9 am to 5 pm: you can go up with the cable car from Siusi or by bus from Castelrotto.

  • Where Alpe di Siusi, Castelrotto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · suedtirol.info · seiseralm.it · italia.it

Parco naturale Sciliar-Catinaccio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established in 1974, the first of the seven South Tyrolean natural parks, covers 7,288 hectares between Castelrotto, Tires and Fiè (20% of the territory of Castelrotto): it includes the Sciliar massif, the Mountain of Destiny, with the peaks Santner and Euringer, and the Catinaccio; since 2009 it is part of the UNESCO World Heritage Dolomites. The visitor center is in Siusi, in Piazza Oswald von Wolkenstein 6; nature trails and, according to italia.it, the Path of geologists.

  • Where Piazza Oswald von Wolkenstein 6, Siusi, Castelrotto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parchi-naturali.provincia.bz.it · italia.it

Malga Marinzen e Sedie delle streghe

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Viewpoint

The Marinzen mountain hut, about 1,500 meters above the village, is reached by cable car: panorama, playground and trails like that towards the Schafstall hut; in winter skating rink with views of the Sciliar. Nearby, the Chairs of Witches (Hexenstühle) are a viewpoint linked to local legends.
