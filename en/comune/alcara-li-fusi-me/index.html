What to see in Alcara li Fusi

Borgo dei Nebrodi linked to San Nicolò Politi, who lived hermitage in a grotto of the territory from 1137 to 1167: the Mother Church preserves its body, the hermitage rises about 2.5 km from the village; above the village rise the Rocks of Crasto, limestone massif with royal eagles and griffon, and the Grotto of Lauro.

Rocche del Crasto

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Mesozoic limestone massif, 1,315 metres high, on whose cliffs you can see the royal eagle and the griffon; large areas with little trees, pastures and walls that attract climbers and hikers, with paths between Alcara li Fusi, Longi and San Marco d'Alunzio. The Rocche guard the hermitage of San Nicolò Politi and are crossed by the path dedicated to him, in the Nebrodi Regional Park.

  • Where Contrada Rocche del Crasto, Alcara li Fusi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Grotta del Lauro

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

karst cave at about 1,068 meters in the limestone-dolomitic massif of Crasto, the main one of the western side of the Fortress, partly still unexplored: stalactites, stalagmites, columns and mammellonary concretions of calcite; there live two endemic troglobular invertebrates and there were found materials of the Neolithic and early metals. Not accessible to people with limited mobility.

the section closed by gate is visited only on request (tel. Common 0941 793010)

  • Where Contrada Rocche del Crasto, Alcara li Fusi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.alcaralifusi.me.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Santuario dell'Eremo di San Nicolò Politi

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

It rises at the end of the Middle Ages around the small natural cave where the patron saint of Alcara lived from hermitage from 1137 until his death, on 17 August 1167; it is about 2.5 km from the village and can be reached from Portella Gazzana, the final stop of the path of San Nicolò Politi.

  • Where Contrada Rocche del Crasto, Alcara li Fusi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa Madre di Maria SS. Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Of Byzantine origin, it overlooks the main square dedicated to St. Nicholas and guards the body of the saint with his clothes and vestments.

  • Where Piazza San Nicolò, Alcara li Fusi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
