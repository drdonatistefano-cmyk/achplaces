What to see in Masone

Country of the Stura Valley in the Park of Beigua, once linked to the processing of iron: the historic center perched with the terraced houses of the blacksmiths, the civic museum Andrea Tubino in the former Augustinian convent and the sanctuary of the Madonna della Cappelletta in the woods.

3 places to visit in Masone, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo civico Andrea Tubino ed ex convento agostiniano

Museums · Themed

Born in the seventies from the collection of the masonous Andrea Tubino, technical Italsider, and named after him in 1993, he has over 4,000 pieces on several floors of the former Augustinian convent of Piazza Castello, built on a slope with two floors towards the square and four towards the valley: ethnographic and archaeological collections, figures of the nativity scene and mechanized crib, lamps, minerals and fossils, the chapel of the convent and the reconstruction of a blacksmith's forge. It tells of the period in which the valley lived of iron processing and hosts an international photographic exhibition with works by Gianni Berengo Gardin, Fulvio Roiter, Franco Fontana and others. Tel. 010 926210, 347 1496802.

Centro storico dei fabbri

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town, perched on the hill, preserves the long row of terraced houses where the blacksmiths lived when the main activity of the area was the processing of steel and iron.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Masone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.parcobeigua.it

Sources: parcobeigua.it

Santuario della Madonna della Cappelletta

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Sanctuary surrounded by woods, a pilgrimage destination that the inhabitants make every 2 July to thank the Virgin for having saved them from the plague of 1656; from the Cappelletta also starts a trek on the High Via dei Monti Liguri towards the Passo del Veleno.

  • Where Cappelletta, Masone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.parcobeigua.it

Sources: parcobeigua.it
