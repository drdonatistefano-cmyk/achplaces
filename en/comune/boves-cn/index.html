What to see in Boves

Country at the foot of the Bisalta, gold medal for military and civil valor for the fires and massacres of 1943: the partisan works of Adriana Filippi in the former spinning mill, the mushroom museum, the chestnut eco-museum in the Marquet park and the sanctuary of Madonna dei Boschi with frescoes from the fifteenth to the eighteenth century.

7 places to visit in Boves, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Adriana Filippi

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

In the former fairy tale mill, more than 150 works by the teacher and artist Adriana Filippi, who between 1943 and 1945 portrayed the life of the partisans of Valle Colla: the memory of the resistance in Boves, fired twice by the Germans in 1943. Free admission on the first day of the month from April to October, with guided tours by reservation; Card Memory Museum 7 € with three other museums of the Cuneese Resistance.

  • Where Via Moschetti 15, Boves
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Museo del fungo e di scienze naturali

Museums · Science and university

Set up like a ...wunderkammer: eighteen showcases of reproductions of plaster and resin mushrooms and sections of natural sciences with fossils, naturalized animals, shells, reptiles and over 130 species of butterflies, as well as exotic fibers, silks and ceramics. Open on the first Sunday of the month from April to October, other days by reservation.

  • Where Piazza Borelli 6, Boves
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Parco Marquet – Museo etnografico ed Ecomuseo della castagna

Museums · Themed

Outside the village, in an ancient farmhouse among the chestnut groves, it tells of the role that chestnut has played for decades in the economy of the Bovesan mountain.

  • Where Parco Marquet, Boves
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Santuario di Madonna dei Boschi

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Small church in a hamlet on the sides of the Bisalta, entirely frescoed between the fifteenth and eighteenth centuries: cycles of the life of Our Lady and Passion and a universal Judgment inspired by the Sistine Chapel.

  • Where Frazione Madonna dei Boschi, Boves
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Santuario Regina Pacis

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

A large Marian shrine recently built in the hamlet of Fontanelle, in eclectic style with a large dome; in the garden the chapel of the Addolorata, a cave of Lourdes and the monument to the Fallen.

  • Where Frazione Fontanelle, Boves
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Centro storico e fontane

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

It preserves the traces of the Ricetto, the ancient fortified area then demolished; the ancient town hall houses the civic library and the School of Peace. Stone fountains, including the sixteenth century of Piazza dell'Olmo and the eighteenth century of Piazza Garibaldi.

  • Where Piazza dell'Olmo, Boves
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Bisalta

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The mountain at whose feet Boves stands, with walks in the woods and more challenging routes on foot or by bike, including a bike park for the descent.

  • Where Bisalta, Boves
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
