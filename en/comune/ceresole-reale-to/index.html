What to see in Ceresole Reale

The capital of the Orco Valley in Gran Paradiso National Park, elite tourism centre at the end of the 19th century: in the former Grand Hotel of hamlet, it took the visitor centre Homo et Ibex on the ibex, around alpine lakes such as Dres and Serru, the Nivolet road and the mule track of Colle Sià.

4 places to visit in Ceresole Reale, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Homo et Ibex, centro visitatori nell'ex Grand Hotel

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Visitor center of the Park dedicated to the relationship between man and ibex in history and art: multimedia materials, reconstructions including a cave with rock paintings of the first ibex and prehistoric hunting scenes, objects, photographs, paintings, myths and legends. It is located in the historic complex of the Grand Hotel, witness of the elite tourism of the late nineteenth century, partially restored by the Park Authority which has its operational headquarters and a multipurpose hall in the ancient festival hall. Free under 6 years and over 65 years of age; compulsory booking for groups and schools (tel. +39 0124 953321).

  • Where Frazione Prese, c/o ex Grand Hotel, Ceresole Reale
  • Entry €2.50, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: pngp.it · turismotorino.org

Lago di Dres

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lake at 2,087 m in a glacial basin, reached by Villa Poma (right side of Lake Ceresole) with path 521: about 2 hours, 4 km and 503 m of altitude difference, through one of the most interesting coniferous forests of the Park (laric, red and white firs, pine trees) and pastures in front of the East Levanna. Support point of the Refuge Leonesi; recommended period June-October.

  • Where Partenza da Villa Poma, Ceresole Reale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: pngp.it · turismotorino.org

Lago Serrù e strada del Nivolet

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lake 2,275 m in an ice basin at the highest edge of the valley, on the border with the French National Park of the Vanoise: an almost flat path from the parking lot offers ibex, chamois, marmots, eagles and gipetes, a waterfall and the permanent exhibition on high altitude peat bogs. From the lake starts the road towards the Colle and Piano del Nivolet, plateau of over 6 km at about 2,500 m with lakes and peat bogs; in summer the Park prohibits the transit of cars from Serru to the hill. Recommended period July-September.

  • Where Lago Serrù, Ceresole Reale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: pngp.it · turismotorino.org

Mulattiera del Colle Sià

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

From Ceresole Reale, a mule track rises between larch and fir woods to Colle Sià, which connects the headboard of the valley of the Orco with the Vallone del Roc, with views of the glaciers of the Levanna and the possibility to observe the fauna of the Park.

  • Where Ceresole Reale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: pngp.it · turismotorino.org
