What to see in Ponte in Valtellina

Borgo della media Valtellina, mentioned as "Vico Ponte" in 918 and home of the astronomer Giuseppe Piazzi, discoverer of Ceres: around Piazza Luini the parish church of San Maurizio with the bezel of Bernardino Luini and the Parish Museum, higher up the Jesuit church of Sant'Ignazio with the ethnographic museum and the mulberry centuries-old, and in the locality San Bernardo the observatory entitled to Piazzi.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Maurizio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church of the patron, which unites Romanesque, Gothic and Renaissance: inscriptions on the entrance pillars recall an extension of 1347, the façade and the Gothic-Venetian marble portal are by Jacopo Corti di Valsolda (1460) and the bezel of the portal is frescoed by Bernardino Luini with the Madonna with the Child and St. Maurizio. On the south side there is a large Saint Christopher, a sundial and the coat of arms of the Quadrius; Romanesque-Lombard bell tower of the fourteenth century. Inside, three aisles frescoed and never touched by the Baroque, the bronze ciborium of the Guicciardi brothers (1578) and a golden wooden ancone of the late fifteenth century attributed to Giacomo Del Maino.

Piazza Luini e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Heart of the village and one of the most aristocratic squares of the average Valtellina, closed by historical buildings: there are an octagonal stone fountain, a neoclassical covered wash-house and the monument to Giuseppe Piazzi, the astronomer born in Ponte who discovered Cerere. The center alternates peasant courtyards and noble residences; the narrow Via Sant'Ignazio salt paved with pebbles ("risc") between stone houses and large wooden doors.

  • Where Piazza Bernardino Luini, Ponte in Valtellina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valtellina.it

Sources: valtellina.it · in-lombardia.it

Museo parrocchiale e Oratorio dei Disciplini

Museums · Old masters

On the north side of Piazza Luini, in the complex Casa parrocchiale - Teatro comunale that was of the Scuola Maggiore dei Laici (active from the fifteenth to the eighteenth century), exhibits valuable furnishings from the churches of Ponte; one room was the ancient Oratorio dei Disciplini and preserves frescoes of Fermo Stella.

  • Where Piazza Luini, Ponte in Valtellina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valtellina.it

Sources: sondrioevalmalenco.it · valtellina.it

Museo etnografico della civiltà contadina e chiesa di Sant'Ignazio

Museums · Themed

In the former Jesuit College, overlooking the courtyard of the Prisons next to the church of St. Ignatius (built by the Jesuits at the end of the 16th century), it collects household objects and tools that tell the country's rural past, its socio-economic history, culture and traditions. The complex also houses the library.

  • Where Via Sant'Ignazio, Ponte in Valtellina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valtellina.it

Sources: sondrioevalmalenco.it · valtellina.it

Gelso di Ponte in Valtellina («Muronée»)

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

White mulberry among the most important monumental trees of Lombardy: about 600 years estimated, 8 meters high and a trunk of more than 4.5 meters circumference. It grows next to the Jesuit church of Sant'Ignazio, among rural stone buildings in the historic center, and is freely accessible.

  • Where Via Sant'Ignazio, Ponte in Valtellina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valtellina.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · valtellina.it

Osservatorio astronomico Giuseppe Piazzi

Museums · Science and university

Opened in 2009 in San Bernardo and named after the astronomer born in Ponte, it offers observations of the sky and educational, tourist and scientific activities; it is home to the Astrofili Association, which organizes periodically initiatives.
