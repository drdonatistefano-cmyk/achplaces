What to see in Olevano Romano

Borgo 60 km from Rome on Mount Celeste, overlooking the high valley of Sacco, with pre-Roman polygonal walls and the tower of the castle of the thirteenth century; from the beginning of the nineteenth century it attracted European painters of the landscape, as the Civic Museum of Art of Villa De Pisa tells with the Roman Vedutes of Joseph Anton Koch, and the forest of the Serpentara that inspired Gustave Doré. It is the land of the Cesanese di Olevano Romano DOC.

Museo civico d'Arte, Villa De Pisa

Museums · Modern art

Established in 1989 in Villa De Pisa, built between the twenties and thirties by the Roman Jewish family Bondi, seat of a German command in the Second World War and today in the network of the historic residences of Lazio, in a park of pine, acacia, oak and robinie. It preserves over 2,000 oils, watercolors, drawings, sketches and engravings collected in thirty years by the Association Friends of the Museum of Olevano, witnesses of European artists attracted to Olevano since the nineteenth century; in permanence the Roman Views, twenty engravings of 1810 by Joseph Anton Koch donated by the heirs in 1999, while the works of the twentieth century and contemporary rotate.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Borgo medievale, mura poligonali e torre del castello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

From Monte Celeste the village dominates the high valley of Sacco and preserves pre-Roman polygonal walls and the castle tower of the 13th century. In July, the re-enactment "Dies in Castro Olibani" celebrates the patron saint with medieval crafts and shows.

  • Where Centro storico, Olevano Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Palazzo Colonna Marcucci

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The palace in the castle was first inhabited by the Benedictines, then by the Colonna and the Borghese until the beginning of the twentieth century; around 1970 he bought it and restored the Marcucci family. It is part of the historic residences of Lazio and has frescoed rooms that host conferences and exhibitions.

Chiesa di Santa Maria di Corte

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Small church of the 13th century with a nave and a single altar, with votive paintings and frescoes of the 2nd and 3rd centuries; it was the main church of the village before the title passed to Santa Margherita.

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Dedicated to the patron saint of Santa Margherita d'Antiochia, whose cult was introduced by the Olibria family; it is today the main church of the village.

Villa Serpentara e il suo querceto

Nature and landscape · Landscapes

The ancient oak woods of Serpentara inspired many artists, including Gustave Doré, who designed the dark forest of Dante's Hell; in 1873 the German romantics bought them to save them and in the early twentieth century the sculptor Heinrich Gerhardt built a refuge, donated to the Berlin Academy of Arts. Today it is managed by the Accademia Tedesca Roma Villa Massimo.

  • Where Serpentara, Olevano Romano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Cesanese di Olevano Romano e feste del vino

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry

The Cesanese di Olevano Romano DOC is the pride of the village: at the end of June there is Vinointorno and between August and September the Festa del Cesanese, now in the fiftieth year, with tastings in the cellars along the Strada del vino Cesanese and parade of floats allegorical. At Christmas the panpepato is prepared, the protagonist of a competition.

  • Where Olevano Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
