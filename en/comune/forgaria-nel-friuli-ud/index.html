What to see in Forgaria nel Friuli

The hillside town overlooking the Tagliamento, leads to the plateau of Monte Prat and the site of the natural reserve of Lake Cornino, where the griffons fly free; in the village the archaeological exhibition on the Castelraimondo hill and the house of rural manualism.

5 places to visit in Forgaria nel Friuli, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Riserva naturale del Lago di Cornino e i grifoni

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Regional reserve of about 500 hectares between Forgaria and Trasaghis, between the Tagliamento greto and the walls of the Carnic Prealps, home to almost twenty years of an international project to protect the griffin, which is observed in flight especially in the morning; there are also owl real, allocus of the Urals, vulture monk and head vulture. Tour centre with aviaries and terrariums, trails to the lake and Tagliamento, guided tours by reservation.

  • Where Via Sompcornino 81, Forgaria nel Friuli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.riservacornino.it

Sources: riservacornino.it · turismofvg.it

Lago di Cornino

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lake formed about 10,000 years ago, about 140 meters long and deep 8, without immissaries or emissaries: the underground circulation keeps the green-blue water clear and at about 8 °C all year round. The path around the lake is always open; on the night of December 24 the divers set up an underwater nativity scene.

  • Where Riserva del Lago di Cornino, Forgaria nel Friuli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Altopiano del Monte Prat

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The plateau is about 800 meters away by paths marked between woods and high meadows, including ancient mule tracks of transhumance; from the plateau the view ranges over San Daniele and Friuli hills. The old houses of transhumance host a popular hotel; bike routes with the school of Daniele Pontoni.

  • Where Monte Prat, Forgaria nel Friuli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Mostra permanente Zuc Scjaramont - I segreti del colle

Museums · Archaeological

It exhibits the materials of the excavations of Zuc Scjaramont, the Castelraimondo hill, telling the story in four phases: pre-Roman (the first fortified village), Roman, late medieval and medieval-Renaissance; among the finds coins and pre-Roman ceramics. Open Mon-Sat 9-12, Wednesday until 6pm, closed on Sundays.

  • Where Piazza Tre Martiri, Forgaria nel Friuli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.comune.forgarianelfriuli.ud.it

Sources: comune.forgarianelfriuli.ud.it · cultura.gov.it · turismofvg.it

Geis e Riscjei. Casa della manualità rurale

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Opened in 2005 in the former Cornino dairy, it exhibits wooden rakes, wicker baskets, tools and household items produced in the area before the 1976 earthquake, when wicker and rakes were the main craft; sections on cheese making, spinning wool and cooking, sometimes with demonstrations of craftsmen. Open on Sundays, other days upon reservation.

  • Where Via Muris, loc. Cornino, Forgaria nel Friuli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismofvg.it
