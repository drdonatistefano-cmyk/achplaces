What to see in Jerago con Orago

Comune del Seprio whose strategic position, at the time of the struggles between Torriani and Visconti, led to the construction of small defenses later became castles; today offers the castle of Jerago on its hill, stop of the regional tour of the Visconti castles, and a civic museum of natural history with free entry.

Castello visconteo di Jerago

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Rocca built in the thirteenth century on a small hill by Archbishop Ottone Visconti, perhaps on a previous early medieval settlement, to defend the Contado del Seprio disputed between Torriani and Visconti; the fief was given to a branch of the Visconti, extinct in 1751. Passed to the Bossi, at the end of the eighteenth century it became a noble residence with two terraces with Italian garden and an underground water lily with fake caves, mosaics and shells; in 1960 the restoration was entrusted to the Milanese studio BBPR. Oratorio di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small Romanesque building with a rectangular plan along the ascent to the castle, almost hidden by two tall pine trees; for some scholars it dates back to the 10th century and is already mentioned at the end of the 13th century in the Liber Notitiae Sanctorum Mediolani di Goffredo da Bussero. The dedication to St. James Major suggests a pilgrimage to Santiago. The restorations of the fifties and sixties of the twentieth century brought to light frescoes from different eras: in the apse paintings byzantine with apostles, a fragment of the martyrdom of St. James, a St. John the Baptist and a Madonna with Child who blesses a devotee, probably a Visconti, of 15th century painters.

Civico Museo di Storia Naturale

Museums · Science and university · Free entry

Collection of stuffed animals of the Lombard fauna, ordered in five sections: invertebrates, fish, amphibians and reptiles, birds and mammals. Open Tuesday and Friday 15-18.30 and Saturday 10-12.30, the other days on request; guided tours available, entrance and parking accessible by wheelchair.

  • Where Via Rimembranze 8, Jerago con Orago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
