What to see in Montaione

3 places to visit in Montaione, in the province of Florence (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 2 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Gerusalemme di San Vivaldo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Paid entry

A "New Jerusalem" of the early sixteenth century: 18 chapels in the woods arranged according to the topography of the holy city, with scenes of the Passion in polychrome terracotta of the Buglioni and Della Robbia.

  • Where Via San Vivaldo 1, San Vivaldo, Montaione
  • Entry €5 whole
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com

Borgo di Montaione

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village of Valdelsa among the most beautiful villages in Italy, with the Pretorio Palace, the parish church of San Regolo and the civic museum with a fossil whale of the Pliocene.

  • Where Montaione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: feelflorence.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · destinationflorence.com

Museo civico e pieve di San Regolo

Museums · Archaeological

In the center of Montaione the civic museum, which preserves the fossil of a whale of the Pliocene, the Palazzo Pretorio and the parish church of San Regolo.

  • Where Centro storico, Montaione
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com
