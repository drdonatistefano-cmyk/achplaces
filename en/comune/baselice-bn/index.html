What to see in Baselice

Borgo del Fortore, between Molise, Campania and Puglia, enclosed by three medieval doors: Palazzo Lembo, born on the remains of a castle, with eighteenth-century frescoes, hanging garden and the Paleontological Civic Museum of Fortore, the mother church of San Leonardo and the caves of Ripa di Troia.

Palazzo Lembo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Built at the end of the eighteenth century by the Lembo family on the remains of a medieval castle with towers, after the restoration it was recognized in 2011 "Meraviglia d'Italia" for the architecture and above all for the eighteenth-century frescoes of some rooms on the upper floor, combined with those of the Reggia di Caserta. On the back a hanging garden with snow and an arched walk towards a cylindrical tower; below, underground caves of uncertain dating. The municipality indicates mon-jo opening 10-13 and 14-18.

Museo Civico Paleontologico del Fortore

Museums · Themed

Born in 1996 on the initiative of the Archeoclub d'Italia di Baselice and since 2013 in convenient to the Municipality, on the ground floor of Palazzo Lembo exhibits fossils of 20-100 million years ago, especially molluscs and lamellibranches, a section of minerals and rocks and a small part dedicated to peasant civilization; other sections are planned on the first floor after the restoration of the premises.

Borgo medievale e le tre porte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The medieval village is enclosed by three doors: the Gothic Porta da Capo and the Romanesque Porta da Piedi and Porta della Torre del Capitano. The surrounding districts, green and rich in stories and myths, preserve the fountains of Fraud and Ancino.

  • Where Via Porta da Capo, Baselice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.baselice.bn.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · comune.baselice.bn.it

Chiesa di San Leonardo Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Mother Church of Baselice, dated to the fourteenth century, near the Gate of Feet; it preserves an engraving dedicated to the Virgin of 23 March 1948 and, after the Second Vatican Council, the marble altar was brought to the center of the nave.

  • Where Via Roma 6, Baselice
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.baselice.bn.it

Sources: comune.baselice.bn.it

Grotte di Ripa di Troia

History and architecture · Archaeology

Caves dug into the tuff where the tomb of a Sannite warrior was found, perhaps involved in the battle of the Caudine Forks (321 BC); they are also related to the story of a woman accused of witchcraft.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
