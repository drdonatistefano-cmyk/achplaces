What to see in Sezze

The ancient Setia on the Lepini Mountains, overlooking the Pontina Plain: polygonal walls of the fourth century BC, the cathedral of Santa Maria and the Good Friday of the Holy Representation of the Passion.

3 places to visit in Sezze, in the province of Latina (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Mura poligonali

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Cinta of the Latin colony of Setia (founded in 382 BC), of the fourth century BC, in blocks of local limestone worked mainly in a polygonal third way, as in Norba and Cori; the best preserved traits are on the south-west side of the hill.

  • Where Via Guglietto, Sezze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it

Basilica Concattedrale di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Building of the thirteenth century work of workers of the Fossanova shipyard, dedicated in 1364; in the sixteenth century it was reversed its orientation. Inside the wooden canopy of 1672 the choir of Enrico Brennch (1731), the altar above the tomb of San Lidano and the chapel of San Carlo da Sezze.

  • Where Sezze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Museo Archeologico Comunale

Museums · Archaeological

From the Neanderthal era to the imperial era: the statue called "Fanciulla di Sezze" is a polychrome mosaic from a domus near Porta Romana, hundreds of ex vows in terracotta from a sanctuary perhaps dedicated to Junone and finds from Forum Appii on the Appia.

  • Where Largo B. Buozzi 1, Sezze
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com
