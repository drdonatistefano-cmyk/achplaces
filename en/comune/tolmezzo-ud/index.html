What to see in Tolmezzo

The capital of Carnia in the plain of But and Tagliamento, Tolmezzo has its heart in Piazza XX Settembre with the eighteenth-century Cathedral of San Martino; at Palazzo Campeis is the Carnic Museum of the Arts and Popular Traditions, one of the most important ethnographic museums in Europe, and above the city the fifteenth-century Tower Picotta offers views of the valley.

Museo carnico delle arti e tradizioni popolari "Luigi e Michele Gortani"

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the seventeenth century Palazzo Campeis, about thirty rooms on three floors tell the life and traditions of Carnia from the fourteenth to the nineteenth century: the traditional house with kitchen, rooms and dining room, carpenter's shops, blacksmith, battirama-octanaio and weaver, watches, ceramics, spinning and weaving, the hut, the world of cramârs, portraits, costumes, carnival masks, musical instruments and popular religiosity; outside the courtyard with loggia and garden with agricultural tools. The whole ticket includes the exhibition "Stories of herbs" until December 31, 2026; museum accessible with elevator. From October closed on Monday; it is part of the CarniaMusei network.

  • Where Via della Vittoria 2, Tolmezzo
  • Entry €9, reduced €6, free under 6 and with FVG Card
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website museocarnico.it

Sources: museocarnico.it · cultura.gov.it · comune.tolmezzo.ud.it

Duomo di San Martino

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Built between 1752 and 1764 on a project by Domenico Schiavi, it contains works by the Venetian painters Bassano, Diziani and Novelli, frescoes by Angelo Schiavi, a altarpiece by Fontebasso, paintings by the Evangelists and Apostles by Nicola Grassi and a sixteenth-century aquasantiera attributed to Bernardino da Bissone.

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre, Tolmezzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.tolmezzo.ud.it

Sources: comune.tolmezzo.ud.it

Piazza XX Settembre e i borghi storici

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The heart of the city, Piazza XX Settembre brings together the Town Hall, Palazzo Garzolini (the seat of the court), the Duomo and, to the south, the eighteenth-century Palazzo Frisacco, the venue of art exhibitions. Around you will find the Mua Village with Palazzo Campeis, the Roggia Village (via Del Din, via Linussio) with small mills and artisan workshops, especially textiles, built at the Porta di Sotto, still visible between the three gates of the ancient city walls, and Palazzo Linussio, designed by Domenico Schiavi as a factory and residence of Jacopo Linussio, now a military property.

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre, Tolmezzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.tolmezzo.ud.it

Sources: comune.tolmezzo.ud.it

Chiesa di Santa Caterina e Casa Janesi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In Borgat, the medieval church rebuilt in the eighteenth century houses the Spousalizio di Santa Caterina (1537) attributed to Pomponio Amalteo; almost opposite, Casa Janesi (XV century) shows a façade with two-folded Venetian Gothic windows.

  • Where Piazza Santa Caterina, Tolmezzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.tolmezzo.ud.it

Sources: comune.tolmezzo.ud.it

Torre Picotta

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval tower, built between 1477 and 1479 to defend the village from the Turkish threat and recently restored; it is reached on foot from Via Cascina, in the area of Pra-Castello, and offers views of the city and the surrounding landscape.

  • Where Via Cascina (Pra-Castello), Tolmezzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.tolmezzo.ud.it

Sources: comune.tolmezzo.ud.it
