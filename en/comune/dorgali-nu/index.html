What to see in Dorgali

Country between the Supramonte and the Gulf of Orosei: the nuragic village of Serra Orrios, the way to Tiscali in the valley of Lanaittu and, on the sea, Cala Gonone with the Caves of Bue Marino and Cala Luna.

6 places to visit in Dorgali, in the province of Nuoro (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villaggio nuragico di Serra Orrios

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Among the largest and best preserved Nuragic villages of Sardinia, from the Middle Bronze to the New Year: about a hundred huts mostly circular grouped in blocks with narrow streets, squares and wells, and two sacred areas with megaron temples.

  • Where Strada Provinciale 38, Dorgali
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Grotte del Bue Marino

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry

South of Cala Gonone, among the largest caves in Sardinia, with a development of about 5 km of which 1,200 meters equipped: stalactites and stalagmites reflected in the ponds and an underground salt lake; it was among the last shelters of the monk seal. Guided tour.

  • Where Cala Gonone, Dorgali
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: touringclub.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Cala Gonone

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fraction on the Gulf of Orosei, on a hill that goes down to the sea, with the beaches of Cala Cartoe, Ziu Martine, Cala Osalla, Palmasera and Sos Dorroles and an aquarium in the village.

  • Where Cala Gonone, Dorgali
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Cala Luna

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the border with Baunei, a fine sandy beach in an oleander forest, reachable by sea or by a challenging trekking; behind the Codula di Luna with millenarian junipers.

  • Where Dorgali
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Villaggio di Tiscali

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The village-fortress against the Roman advance, reachable on foot from the valley of Lanaittu.

  • Where Valle di Lanaittu, Dorgali
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Museo archeologico di Dorgali

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Archaeology of the territory from Neolithic to modern age in three rooms, with finds of Serra Orrios, Nuraghe Mannu, Tiscali and caves of Bue Marino and Ispinigoli.

  • Where Via Lamarmora, Dorgali
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: idese.cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it
