What to see in Ripe San Ginesio

Medieval village at 430 meters in the Fiastra valley, with spiral streets, staircases and stone houses connected by small overhangs: the Leonina Tower of the sixteenth century, the church of San Michele Arcangelo and a vocation of "city of contemporary art" with a gallery of more than three hundred works and sculptures scattered in the village.

6 places to visit in Ripe San Ginesio, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico medievale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Located on a hill at 430 meters in the Fiastra valley, it preserves spiral streets, staircases and stone houses connected by small overhangs; in recent years they have reopened workshops and craft workshops.

  • Where Centro storico, Ripe San Ginesio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Torre Leonina

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Ruins of the tower erected in the sixteenth century at the behest of Pope Leo X, with the bull ...Cum iniuncta... to control the often rebellious castle in San Ginesio... the last trace of the fortified castle of the thirteenth century... hosted castle and soldiers until almost the Napoleonic age.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Ripe San Ginesio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismo.comune.ripesanginesio.mc.it · italia.it

Pinacoteca comunale d'arte contemporanea

Museums · Modern art

Established in 1982 with the works donated by the artists of the annual exhibition "Ripearte" has over three hundred works, including Umberto Peschi, Giorgio Bompadre and Renato Barisani; with the route "Diffuse" small groups of works are exhibited in shops, restaurants and reception places.

  • Where Via Dante Alighieri, Ripe San Ginesio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismo.comune.ripesanginesio.mc.it · italia.it

Parco Sculture e Viale degli Artisti

Museums · Modern art · Free entry · Viewpoint

Open-air museum with iron and wood sculptures by twentieth-century artists, including Renato Barisani, Giorgio Bompadre, Sauro Craia and Umberto Peschi, inserted in the streets and landscape; some works have a QR code with presentations. Accessible path.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II 8, Ripe San Ginesio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.ripesanginesio.mc.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

On the main square of the historic center, of Benedictine origin of the fourteenth century and several times restored.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Ripe San Ginesio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.ripesanginesio.mc.it · italia.it

Arena «La Cava»

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Viewpoint

Natural amphitheatre from about 1,200 places obtained in 2013 from a abandoned quarry, near the historic center; in summer it hosts shows, exhibitions and the Borgofuturo festival.

  • Where Contrada Faveto, Ripe San Ginesio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.ripesanginesio.mc.it · italia.it
