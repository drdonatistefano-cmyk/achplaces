What to see in Campagna

A small town at the foot of the Picentini Mountains, with a historic centre of religious buildings and noble palaces, the Museum of Memory and Peace in the former internment camp of San Bartolomeo and two WWF oases; in summer the festival "A Chiena" partly deviates the Tenza stream and flood the roads.

5 places to visit in Campagna, in the province of Salerno (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Campagna in the guide with map and filters

Museo Itinerario della Memoria e della Pace - Centro studi Giovanni Palatucci

Museums · City history

In the monumental complex of St. Bartholomew, one of the two centers of internment for Jews and political prisoners that Campaign hosted in the Second World War: panels and documents on the Shoah and the Jews interned, the reconstruction of a lodging and the place of worship of the camp, a room of pencils, one for each interneth, and the Room of the Fugue, which recalls the escape after September 8, 1943 with the sign "Hebrews transferred," Unknown Destination.

  • Where Via San Bartolomeo, Campagna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · museicampania.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center preserves beautiful religious buildings and noble palaces; during 'A Chiena' the course of the stream Tenza is partly diverted and the streets, literally flooded, become a feast with water games and water.

  • Where Centro storico, Campagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Eremo di San Michele

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A historical route that goes up to the peaks of Monte Nero, in the landscape of woods and panoramic paths of the Picentini Mountains.

  • Where Monte Nero, Campagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Oasi WWF di Persano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Along the left bank of the Sele, in the regional reserve Foce Sele-Tanagro: 110 hectares, about 70 occupied by the invasion of the dam of 1934, wetland of international importance (Ramsar) with reeds. Celebrated for the otter, counts 184 species, 47 nesting, and the paths allow you to observe aquatic plants and fauna.

  • Where Persano, Campagna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parks.it

Oasi WWF Monte Polveracchio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Established in 1988 on about 200 hectares in the territory of Campagna, managed by the WWF in agreement with the municipalities of Campagna and Senerchia, with observation points and equipment for picnics and excursions.

  • Where Monte Polveracchio, Campagna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcoregionalemontipicentini.it
