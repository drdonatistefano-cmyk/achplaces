What to see in Ortisei

The capital of Val Gardena, known for more than two centuries for its wood carving, with the pedestrian zone between the churches of St. Ulrich and St. Anthony, the small church of St. James of the twelfth century, the oldest in the valley, and the Museum Gherdëina; from the village plants and paths go up to the Seceda, in the Puez-Odle Natural Park, and to the Rasciesa.

Museum Gherdëina

Museums · City history

The Val Gardena Museum tells the history, carving and culture of the valley: geology and fossils, with a virtual reality reconstruction of the ichthyosaur of Mount Seceda; wooden toys, with a new exhibition (since December 2025) of about 8,000 pieces of the company Sevi; wooden sculptures of the Seven and nineteenth centuries, a Lenten cloth and the original sculptures of the church of San Giacomo; late medieval drawings to blood of a farm, presented in multimedia; and the relics of the mountaineer and director Luis Trenker. Guided tours in Italian, German and English on request.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museumgherdeina.it

Chiesa di San Giacomo («dlieja da Sacun»)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

It dates back to the 12th century and is considered the oldest church in Val Gardena: Gothic exterior appearance, Gothic and Baroque interiors, with Gothic frescoes from the late 15th century presbytery, wall paintings on the life of St. James next to the pulpit and the carved high altar attributed to the brothers Vinazer-Pescosta (around 1750), with golden figures of apostles and angels. The sculptures on the altar are copies of local artisans, the originals are at the Museum Gherdëina. You can only walk along the ancient Troi Paian street; weekly guided tours in summer and winter by reservation at the Tourist Association of Ortisei.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Ulrico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church in Piazza Sant'Ulrico, with Renaissance and Baroque elements and refined wooden sculptures, including the statue of St. Ulric of Ludwig Moroder and the altarpiece of the Adoration of the Magi by Josef Moroder-Lusenberg. The nineteenth-century neo-Gothic nave was dismantled in 1988 and replaced by an octagonal building.

  • Where Piazza S. Durich, Ortisei
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · valgardena.it · italia.it

Zona pedonale e via Rezia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The pedestrian zone connects the parish church of Sant'Ulrico to the church of Sant'Antonio, between historic hotels, small shops, bars and restaurants with local specialties; the families of carvers of Ortisei make sacred sculptures, statues of saints and altars at least from the seventeenth century. Just outside, the church of Sant'Anna and the cemetery are reminiscent of characters like Luis Trenker and Ludwig Norman-Neruda. Ortisei hosted the 1970 Alpine Ski World Cup.

  • Where Via Rezia, Ortisei
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: valgardena.it · italia.it

Seceda

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Panoramic view of the sunny side of the Val Gardena, in the Puez-Odle Nature Park, reachable with the facilities from Ortisei: from the top the view embraces the Dolomites and the mountains of South Tyrol. Several cabins serve local cuisine with sun terraces and stube; destination of skiers, hikers, paragliders and families, open all year round.

  • Where Streda Val d'Anna 1, Ortisei (stazione a valle)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: valgardena.it · italia.it

Rasciesa (Resciesa)

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the valley station of the Rasciesa funicular, north of the village, the path no. 10 climbs in the woods up to the Rasciesa refuge (2,170 m) and the summit cross (2,125 m): 4.9 km, about 2 hours and 40 and 857 m of altitude difference, medium difficulty; alternatively you climb up by funicular. From the top, the view spans Odle, Sella, Sassolungo, Sciliar and Valle Isarco.

  • Where Via Resciesa, Ortisei (stazione a valle della funicolare)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: valgardena.it · italia.it
