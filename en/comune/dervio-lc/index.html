What to see in Dervio

Country on the eastern shore of Lake Como, on a peninsula shaped like a cone, with lakefront, small beaches and the tower of the castle of Orezia; in the hamlet of Corenno Plinio, among the most beautiful villages of Italy, the castle-quench fourteenth century, the church of San Tommaso di Canterbury and the arches of the counts Andreani.

8 places to visit in Dervio, in the province of Lecco (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo di Corenno Plinio

History and architecture · Villages · Viewpoint

Frazione di Dervio on a rocky promontory at the foot of Mount Legnoncino, always fortified, with medieval houses gathered towards the lake. The stairs, 493 steps in stone, go down from the medieval square to the ancient pier, exposed to the west; the name "Pliny" was added in 1863 because it was believed that Pliny the Younger had a villa there. The Municipality indicates an access fee, without amount.

Castello-recinto degli Andreani

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The fourteenth-century castle on the cliff of Corenno, with walls and two towers where the population took refuge in case of danger; it is considered one of the best examples of fortification of the Lario. Next to it, the natural viewpoint looks at the lake and the villages of the opposite shore.

  • Where Frazione Corenno Plinio, Dervio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it

Chiesa di San Tommaso di Canterbury

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built at the end of the 12th century, in the years immediately following the death of the saint to whom it is dedicated, as a chapel of the feudal family of the Andreans; inside frescoes from the 14th to the 16th century.

  • Where Frazione Corenno Plinio, Dervio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.dervio.lc.it · italia.it

Arche Trecentesche

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Three marble tombs of the counts Andreani made in the fourteenth century, in about fifty years, by the champions: a Romanesque of Iacobo Andreani in white marble of Musso and black stone of Varenna, a Gothic with three statues attributed to the champions masters, and a Gothic flowered commissioned by Stefano Andreani in 1371. They are what is left of the cemetery that until the early nineteenth century occupied the churchyard.

  • Where Sagrato di San Tommaso, Frazione Corenno Plinio, Dervio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.dervio.lc.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Torre del Castello di Orezia

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Tower on a rock spur overlooking the plain of Dervio; more exactly a mastic, whose fortification consisted of the walls of the surrounding buildings. The municipality indicates it with access for a fee, without amount or times.

  • Where Loc. Castello, Dervio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.dervio.lc.it · italia.it

Chiesa dei Santi Quirico e Giulitta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

At the foot of the mountain on the left of the Varrone stream, it is one of the oldest churches in the area: it is mentioned in documents of 814. It is dedicated to two fourth century martyrs.

  • Where Via Duca d'Aosta, Dervio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.dervio.lc.it · italia.it
