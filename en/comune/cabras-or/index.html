What to see in Cabras

Sinis town overlooking one of the largest ponds in Europe, home of the bottarga: the ancient Tharros founded by the Phoenicians, the Giants of Mont'e Prama in the civic museum, the Byzantine church of San Giovanni di Sinis, the village of San Salvatore with its hypogeum and quartz beaches of Is Arutas in the protected marine area.

6 places to visit in Cabras, in the province of Oristano (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Area archeologica di Tharros

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

City founded by the Phoenicians at the end of the 8th century BC on nuragic traces, then Carthaginian, Roman from 238 BC and first capital of the Judicate of Arborea, abandoned in 1070. Between Capo San Marco and the San Giovanni hill: tofet by Su Murru Mannu, walls, temples (including the tetrastyle one with the columns still standing), spas, forum, aqueduct, necropolis and Spanish coastal towers. Visits every day 9-19 with reservation; managed by the Mont'e Prama Foundation.

  • Where SP6, Cabras
  • Entry €11, reduced €9
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.tharros.sardegna.it

Sources: tharros.sardegna.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it · corporate.coopculture.it

Civico museo archeologico Giovanni Marongiu

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

It tells the Sinis from the Neolithic to the Roman age: the Neolithic village of Cuccuru is Arrius, the Nuragic finds, Tharros and the lead bars of the Roman wreck of Mal di Ventre. From April 2026 he exhibited the entire statuary complex of Mont'e Prama, nuragic sculptures of 900-700 BC. (Paintings, archers, warriors and models of nuraghe) discovered in 1974, among the oldest stone statues in the Mediterranean.

  • Where Via Tharros, Cabras
  • Entry €4, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · museinazionalicagliari.cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni di Sinis

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Byzantine church built around the middle of the 6th century on a section of the Phoenician- Punic necropolis of Tharros, cross-shaped inscribed with dome on constant-polite plumes of influence, enlarged to three naves in the 11th century; masonry in sandstone blocks of recovery. Free entrance.

  • Where SP6, San Giovanni di Sinis, Cabras
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

San Salvatore di Sinis e il suo ipogeo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Borgata about 9 km from Cabras, with the cumbessias of the late seventeenth century for pilgrims around the 17th century church, set of spaghetti western between 1967 and 1990. Under the church an hypogeum frequented by the Neolithic, nuragic water worship, then Punic and Roman and early Christian sanctuary from the fourth century, with inscriptions in Punic, Greek, Latin and Arabic. On the first Saturday of September, the Race of the Scalzi arrives at dawn.

  • Where San Salvatore di Sinis, Cabras
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Stagno di Cabras

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Two basins for about 2,200 hectares, one of the largest in Europe and Ramsar site with the nearby wetlands: nidify flamingos and real germans, fish the moss from which the bottarga is born, the Cabras gold, with the traditional junk boats "is fassonis"; birdwatching and fishing at the fish pond of Mar'e Pontis.

  • Where SP6, Cabras
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Spiagge di Is Arutas, Mari Ermi e Maimoni

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Beaches of white, pink and green quartz grains, called "chicchi di riso" in the marine protected area Sinis Peninsula - Island of Mal di Ventre (about 26,000 hectares, established in 1997). The sand must be left on the spot.

  • Where Is Arutas, Cabras
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
