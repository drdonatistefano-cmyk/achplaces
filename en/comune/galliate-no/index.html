What to see in Galliate

Large village of the Novarian plain dominated by the Castello Visconteo Sforzesco, hunting residence wanted in 1476 by Galeazzo Sforza, which today houses the museum of the Galliatian artist Angelo Bozzola and the room dedicated to the pilot Achille Varzi; outside the center the Sanctuary of Varallino, frescoed by Lorenzo Peracino.

Castello Visconteo Sforzesco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The Duke of Milan Galeazzo Sforza had him built in 1476 on a previous Visconti fortress, entrusting the works to Ambrogio Ferrari and Danesio Mainerio, as hunting residence of the Milanese court, continued use with Ludovico il Moro. Rectangular plant of 80 x 108 meters, walls about 3,5 meters thick and 7.5 meters high up to the blackbirds, moat fed by the Codimonte Roggia, four corner towers connected by underground passages and two entrances with drawbridge and poster it still visible. In the sleeve and in the south-west tower there is the municipal library, with over 10,000 rare pieces between incunaboli and five hundred.

Museo civico «Angelo Bozzola»

Museums · Modern art

Since 1998 it occupies the north-east tower of the castle and part of the round path; it collects sculptures, paintings and graphic works that the Galliate artist Angelo Bozzola has donated to his community.

  • Where Castello Visconteo Sforzesco, Piazza Vittorio Veneto, Galliate

Sala Museale Achille Varzi

Museums · Themed

In the south-east wing of the castle, he collects trophies and relics on the life and career of Achille Varzi, born in Galliate. Open on Sunday afternoons from 3:30 pm.

Santuario del Varallino

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Born as a small country church dedicated to St. Peter in Vulpiate, it takes its name from the resemblance to the Sacred Mount of Varallo; already in the sixteenth century it recalled faithful for the image of the Virgin who gives a pear to the Child. A central plan with elliptical compartment and ten chapels, it was frescoed for decades by the Valsesian Lorenzo Peracino (1748-1781), with the large dome of the Coronation of the Virgin and Paradise (1765-1767) considered the masterpiece; sculptures of Dionigi Compassola and neoclassical statues of Giuseppe Argenti (1841-1842). Open every day from 8 am to 6 pm.

  • Where Via Varallino, Galliate
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismonovara.it

Sources: turismonovara.it

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Impressive neo-Gothic church built between 1851 and 1862 on a project and under the direction of the Galliate architect Don Ercole Marietti, in place of the sixteenth-century parish. Three naves with dome on the central and eight side chapels; it preserves a canvas of Cuzzio with Saints Peter and Paul and a Saint Anthony of Padua by Bartolomeo Vandoni. Open every day from 8:00 to 6:00.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Veneto, Galliate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismonovara.it

Sources: turismonovara.it
