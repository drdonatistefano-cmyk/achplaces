What to see in Portovenere

At the western end of the Gulf of Poets, the village of Roman origin dominated by the Doria Castle and the Gothic church of St. Peter on the tip of the promontory, with the islands Palmaria, Tino and Tinetto: part of the UNESCO site since 1997.

7 places to visit in Portovenere, in the province of La Spezia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Portovenere in the guide with map and filters

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the tip of the promontory, in Gothic Genoese style; it is among the churches mentioned in the UNESCO site description.

  • Where Promontorio di San Pietro, Portovenere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: italia.it · whc.unesco.org

Castello Doria

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Pentagonal fortress that dominates the village, with the main part of the sixteenth century, a 17th century belt to defend the oldest buildings and bastions overlooking the sea; it preserves traces of a medieval fortress previous.

  • Where Via dei Mulini, Portovenere
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: whc.unesco.org

Borgo di Portovenere

History and architecture · Villages · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Compact coastal village of Roman origin under the Doria Castle, part of the UNESCO cultural landscape between Punta Mesco and Punta Persico.

  • Where Portovenere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: whc.unesco.org

Isola Palmaria

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest of the three islands of the archipelago in front of Portovenere, on the UNESCO site, with remains of ancient monastic settlements.

  • Where Isola Palmaria, Portovenere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: whc.unesco.org

Isola del Tino e San Venerio

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage

Island of the archipelago on the UNESCO site, with the church of San Venerio mentioned in the description of the good and the archaeological area of the monastery.

  • Where Isola del Tino, Portovenere
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: whc.unesco.org

Isola del Tinetto

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The smallest island of the archipelago, part of the UNESCO site, with remains of ancient monastic settlements.

  • Where Isola del Tinetto, Portovenere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: whc.unesco.org

Villa romana del Varignano

History and architecture · Archaeology

At the bottom of a sheltered cove, alle Grazie, the archaeological area preserves the oldest oil mill in Liguria, with the large oil cell for at least 50 jars; since 2025 there is a new museum.

  • Where Via Varignano Vecchio, Le Grazie, Portovenere
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: cultura.gov.it · whc.unesco.org
