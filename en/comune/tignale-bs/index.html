What to see in Tignale

Village on a plateau of olive trees and orchards overlooking Lake Garda, formed by six small centres of medieval origin; above the lake dominates the Sanctuary of Montecastello, while at the port stands the lemon garden of Prà de la Fam.

9 places to visit in Tignale, in the province of Brescia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario della Madonna di Montecastello

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Sanctuary perched on a spur of limestone rock overlooking the lake, reachable on foot or by car with a steep climb; it is known for the fresco of the Madonna and for the largest ex-vote in Europe, which represents the last moments of the bandit Zanzanu. The first reliable testimony of a church is the date 1458 on a fresco; it is still a pilgrimage destination.

  • Where Montecastello, Tignale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa longobarda di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Lombard church of late VI-early VII century in the area of Gardola, with a fine window and a small museum of high medieval tombs; every summer hosts an exhibition of local artists.

  • Where Gardola, Tignale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Museo del Parco Alto Garda bresciano - Centro visitatori

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Information and environmental education center dedicated to the hinterland of Garda, born to unite the coastal and the mountain belt: the route combines images, sounds, reconstructions of environments, naturalistic collections, old working tools, videos and interactive stations, with paths marked to the outside.

seasonal opening (from 30 August to 10 October, Mon and Gio-dom 15-19) according to the MiC card updated to 2022

  • Where Località Prabione, Tignale
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.parcoaltogarda.net

Sources: parcoaltogarda.net · cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Limonaia Prà de la Fam

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Ancient citrus greenhouse (lemons, mandarin, bergamotti) at the port of Tignale, restored in 1985 and used as a museum, where the phases of the ancient cultivation are observed; since 2012 it is part of the Ecomuseum of the Limonaie del Garda. Guided tours only on request for groups of at least 10 people.

  • Where Via Gardesana, Tignale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · visitgarda.com · italia.it

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

The port can be reached by a daily shuttle or by a path that goes down to the lake; it has an equipped beach, a bar, an area for animals and you practice windsurfing, kayaking and sailing.

Frazioni storiche di Gardola, Piovere e Oldesio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Tignale is formed by six nuclei with ancient houses and alleys of medieval origin: Gardola with the parish church of Santa Maria Assunta, Piovere with the viewpoints on the lake and the church of San Marco, Oldesio with the alleys, the ancient wash-houses and the chapel of San Rocco; between Piovere and Oldesio stretch out the characteristic terraces cultivated with olive trees.

  • Where Gardola, Tignale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it · visitgarda.com
