What to see in Pescara

The most populous city of Abruzzo, on the sea at the mouth of the Pescara river: the promenade and the Bridge of the Sea, the Dannunzian Pinewood, the birthplace of Gabriele d'Annunzio in Pescara Vecchia and a network of museums from ethnography to ceramics of Castelli.

14 places to visit in Pescara, in the province of Pescara (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.
