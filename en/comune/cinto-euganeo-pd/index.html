What to see in Cinto Euganeo

At the foot of Mount Cinto, in the Euganean Hills, the municipality houses the Geopaleontological Museum of Cava Bomba with the fossil fishes of 92 million years ago, the path that goes up to the remains of the medieval castle, and in Valnogaredo the eighteenth-century church of San Bartolomeo and the Roman galleries of Mount Vendevolo.

Museo geopaleontologico di Cava Bomba

Museums · Science and university

Born in 1987 in the complex of the ancient lime furnace, after in the mid-seventies in the nearby quarry were found fossil fishes of the Cretaceous about 92 million years ago. The sections of geology of the Euganean Hills, mineralogy and the nineteenth-century collection of Count Niccolò Da Rio are flanked by an open-air path on the lime production cycle and a geological park with large boulders and models of prehistoric animals of life size.

Sentiero del Monte Cinto n. 11 e rovine del castello

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of 4.9 km (about 1 hour and a half, 267 m of difference in altitude, level of "expert" , starting from Cava Bomba and climbing on the southern side between terraced vineyards, olive trees and chestnut groves, with the natural benches of volcanic stone, the old quarry of amphitheater riolite with rock columns and the Busa dei Briganti. Near the top are the ruins of the medieval castle, destroyed in the thirteenth century, rebuilt and abandoned in the fifteenth century under the Serenissima; view of the mountains Venda and Gemola.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Chiesa di San Bartolomeo a Valnogaredo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Rebuilt in 1758 by the brothers Angelo and Giulio Contarini next to their villa, it has facade with statues and rocaille portal attributed to Antonio Bonazza; inside, with a single nave with rocaille stuccoes, a large fresco on the ceiling of Jacopo Guarana, angels in Bonazza marble on the sides of the main altar and a altarpiece by Domenico Fedeli called the Mayt. Under the altar rests the body of the holy pope Adeodato, donated to the Contarini by Innocent XII.

Area archeologica del Monte Vendevolo (Buso della Casara)

History and architecture · Archaeology

Roman hydraulic work to supply Atheste (Este): a system of tunnels of more than 100 meters dug in the riolite, with walls covered with large tiles, niches for the lamps and a collection basin, the water caput, from which a duct ran for about 10 km. Freed from debris by the municipality in 1970-71, when a poplar wooden boat was found there, and made it more accessible in 1999-2000.
