What to see in Cameri

Municipality of the Novara plain overlooking the valley of Ticino, in whose territory there is Villa Picchetta, seat of the Ticino Park and Lake Maggiore, surrounded by historical farms with medieval and eighteenth-century oratories.

Villa Picchetta

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Cascina Picchetta's manor house, already defined as palatium in 1575, when Lucrezia Cioccara acquired possessions; enlarged between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, passed to the Jesuits and then to the Natta d'Alfiano. Purchased by the Park in 1989, since 2001 it is home: U-shaped plant with towers at the corners and tiburium with lantern, grotesque decorations in the Hall of the Octagon and landscapes in the west porch. From spring to autumn, salt on the ground floor and gardens open on Sunday afternoons according to an annual calendar, with free entrance and guided tours.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.cameri.no.it

Parco naturale del Ticino a Cameri

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Cameri is one of the eleven municipalities of the Piedmontese park, which covers about 6.560 hectares along the banks of the Ticino and with the Lombardy park forms the largest protected river area in Europe, in the UNESCO MAB network and Natura 2000. The landscape alternates with forests (about 60% of the surface), meadows, farmsteads, mills, canals, grets and larch, with herons, royal germans and the rare gloomy pelobate. From Villa Picchetta cycle and pedestrian routes start.

  • Where Partenza da Località Villa Picchetta, Cameri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page comune.cameri.no.it

Sources: parcoticinolagomaggiore.com · comune.cameri.no.it

Cascine storiche e oratori di campagna

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

The municipality offers a walk or bike ride through the farmhouses of the territory. Among the stages: the Cascina San Biagio, with the perhaps oldest oratory of Cameri (walling of the second half of the 11th century, fresco of the Virgin enthroned between St. John the Baptist and St. Biagio); the Cascina Argine, where from 1347 the Lateran Canons settled, with the oratory of the Blessed Virgin of the Snow frescoed in the eighteenth century; the Cascina Bornago, a fortified farmhouse already in 912, overlooking the valley of the Ticino; the Cascina Galdina with the oratory of San Galdino. The farmhouses are private property: they are observed from the outside.

  • Where Campagna di Cameri (cascine tra il Terdoppio e la valle del Ticino)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page comune.cameri.no.it

Sources: comune.cameri.no.it
