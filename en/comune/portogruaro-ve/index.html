What to see in Portogruaro

Cities on the Lemene river with the Gothic Town Hall in Piazza della Repubblica, the Molini and the bridge of Sant'Andrea, the National Museum of Concord with the finds of Iulia Concordia and, in Summaga, the abbey of Santa Maria Maggiore with medieval frescoes.

9 places to visit in Portogruaro, in the province of Venice (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo nazionale concordiese

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Opened on 28 October 1888 in a building shaped as a three-nave early Christian basilica, it gathers almost only finds of the Roman colony of Iulia Concordia: statues, mosaics, funerary stele, portraits, numerous inscriptions, figurative bronzes, ambers, glass including a cup engraved with Daniele among the lions. Open Monday-Saturday 8.30-19.30, Sunday and holidays 8.30-13.30.

  • Where Via Seminario 26, Portogruaro
  • Entry €6 cumulative ticket with the archaeological area of Concordia, reduced €2 (18-25 years); free before Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website museoportogruaro.cultura.gov.it

Sources: museoportogruaro.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it · museiveneto.cultura.gov.it · comune.portogruaro.ve.it

Museo della Città - Torre di Sant'Agnese

Museums · City history

Opened on May 16, 1999 in the medieval tower of Sant'Agnese and in a small adjacent building: stone and metal materials coming largely from the Museo concordiese, and on the top floor manuscripts, prints and photographs of the Portogruaro between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries; it also exhibits two tombstones of the Jewish cemetery. Open Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 10-12; not accessible to people with motor disabilities.

  • Where Via del Rastrello, Portogruaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.portogruaro.ve.it

Sources: comune.portogruaro.ve.it

Piazza della Repubblica e Municipio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The square is dominated by the Gothic town hall in bricks crowned with Ghibelline blackbirds, whose central body dates back to 1265 and its wings probably to 1512; in front, the cockpit of the Pilacorte (1494), symbol of the city, with the two cranes faced sculpted by the Turchetto.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica 1, Portogruaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.portogruaro.ve.it

Sources: comune.portogruaro.ve.it

Molini e Ponte di Sant'Andrea

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Molini sul Lemene have existed since at least 1186; the bridge, documented since 1353 and rebuilt in stone in 1554 under the podestà Giulio Valier, in 1918 was mined by the Austrians in retreat but not destroyed.

  • Where Via Roma, Portogruaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.portogruaro.ve.it

Sources: comune.portogruaro.ve.it

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

Oratorio della Madonna della Pescheria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Small oratory on the shore of the Lemene near the Molini, linked to the devotion of fishermen from the first half of the seventeenth century; plundered in 1917 and restored in 1920 by Giuseppe Bittolo Bon. Every August 15, after Mass, the evening arrives the procession of boats from Caorle and Concordia.

  • Where Via Molini 4, Portogruaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.portogruaro.ve.it

Sources: comune.portogruaro.ve.it

Centro storico porticato

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The main streets have porticoes with shops that frame Gothic and Renaissance palaces; under the porch of the Marconi College was walking Lorenzo Da Ponte, librettist of Mozart, and in Via Seminario stands Palazzo Venanzio, birthplace of the futurist Luigi Russolo.

  • Where Via Seminario, Portogruaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.portogruaro.ve.it

Sources: comune.portogruaro.ve.it

Abbazia di Santa Maria Maggiore a Summaga

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

About 3 km from the city, founded between the 10th and 11th century by the bishops of Concordia; the church remains three naves and three apses, arranged in 1211 by the abbot Richerio, with facade of 1740. The sacellus conserves frescoes from the 11th to the 11th century with stories of the Redemption and the Judgment, the central apse frescoes from the early thirteenth century with the Virgin in almond and Christ with the twelve Apostles.

  • Where Via Richerio 7, Summaga, Portogruaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.portogruaro.ve.it

Sources: comune.portogruaro.ve.it
