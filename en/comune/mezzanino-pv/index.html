What to see in Mezzanino

Oltrepò Pavese country probably arose on a small island of the Po, as the name suggests (from "mezzana") in its territory, shortly after the confluence of the Ticino in the Po, runs the Bridge of Becca, long steel bridge of 1912 that connects Pavia to the Oltrepò, the starting point of a pedestrian ring of the Park of Ticino.

Ponte della Becca

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Road bridge in steel tower on the Po, shortly after the confluence with the Ticino, 1040 meters long in 13 bays. The debate on the new bridge began in 1893 and was chosen Mezzanino because there already existed since 1865 a bridge of boats; it was built in 1912 by the Society Nathan Uboldi of Milan on a project by engineer Jules Röthlisberger of the Società Nazionale di Savigliano, and an inscription on the access arch recalls that it was born for the concordi energies of Municipalities and Provinces. Restored in the fifties after the damage of the war, it was the subject of interventions after the flood of 2011.

  • Where S.S. 617, località Tornello, Mezzanino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · natura.parcoticino.it · in-lombardia.it

Anello di Mezzanino

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Itinerary of the Ticino Park of 7.62 km, accessible all year round in about 3 hours: it starts and arrives south of the Becca Bridge, between the riverbank and the Bronese State Road 617 and follows the banks of the Po and for a stretch the dirt road parallel to the state road, between agricultural landscapes and the center of Mezzanino, with views of the bridge at the confluence of the Ticino in the Po.

  • Where Partenza a sud del Ponte della Becca, incrocio tra strada arginale e S.S. 617, Mezzanino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: natura.parcoticino.it · in-lombardia.it
