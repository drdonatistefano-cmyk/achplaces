What to see in Castell'Arquato

4 places to visit in Castell'Arquato, in the province of Piacenza (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 3 of the 4 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Rocca Viscontea

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The fourteenth-century fortress in the upper part of the village, set of some scenes of Ladyhawke, with views of the Arda Valley.

  • Where Piazza del Municipio, Castell'Arquato
  • Entry €8 with the guided tour of the village (Sundays and holidays); single entry to check
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitemilia.com · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Piazza monumentale e Collegiata

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The square on top of the village with the Palazzo del Podestà, the Fontana del Duca and the Collegiate Church of Santa Maria Assunta, Romanesque with Gothic and Renaissance parts.

  • Where Piazza del Municipio, Castell'Arquato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo geologico Giuseppe Cortesi

Museums · Science and university

In the former hospital of Santo Spirito del Cinquecento, the Pliocene marine fossils of which Castell'Arquato is a typical place, with the reconstruction of a whale.

  • Where Ex ospedale di Santo Spirito, Castell'Arquato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Torrione Farnese

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The brick mastic started around 1530 in the Monteguzzo district and remained unfinished, today home to an arms school.

  • Where Via Fontane del Duca, Castell'Arquato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
