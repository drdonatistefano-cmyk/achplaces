What to see in Capoliveri

Medieval village on the terraced ridges of Monte Calamita, on the island of Elba, with arced alleys called "chiassi" , craft shops and one of the most beautiful views of the island. Around, the Sanctuary of the Madonna delle Grazie, the Fort Focardo, the Romanesque parish church of San Michele, the mines of Calamita with the Museum of the Old Workshop and dozens of beaches, from Lacona to Innamorata.

Centro storico di Capoliveri e Borgo degli Artisti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval village is located on the slopes of Monte Calamita and in its narrow arched alleys, the "chiassi" houses artisan shops, wine and food producers, restaurants and cafes. The streets are spread by works of art: the iron of Kurt Mergenthal on the façade of the Town Hall with the anchor and the dolphin of the coat of arms, the homage of Michelangelo Venturini to Maria and Lorenzo in Piazza Matteotti, the installation of the sun and moon by Italo Bolano at the viewpoint of Via Roma. In the center there is also the amphitheatre La Vantina, dedicated to the legendary Amelia Vantini linked to the exile of Napoleon (1814-1815), home of festivals and small concerts.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the historic centre, it has origins around 1300 and Romanesque plant; it was attacked by the troops of Dragut in 1553 and an epigraph recalls the restoration of 1830 wanted by Leopold II. Inside, 19th century statues in plaster and a canvas of the Madonna del Carmine.

Santuario della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Built in the 16th century with a Latin cross with a trussed roof, it preserves in the middle of the main altar a small oil on a table (45×56 cm) with the Holy Family, a copy of a work by Michelangelo attributed to Marcello Venusti, and frescoes from the early 20th century by Eugenio Allori. During the restorations of the twentieth century a medieval stone altar re-emerged under the plaster; under the sanctuary there is the small beach of the same name.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Forte Focardo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built in 1678 under Spanish rule, to defend the cove of Longone and Mola, it is named after Governor Ferdinando Foxardo, as recalled by an inscription of the same year. It rises on a promontory on the coast near Naregno and was then transformed into a lighthouse.

Pieve di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built at the beginning of the XII century in Romanesque-Pisan style on a Lombard building, in local limestone with rosé tones, it is considered the oldest Romanesque architecture of Elba; in 1376 Pope Gregory XI celebrated Mass there. In the sixteenth century it was destroyed by Barbarossa troops except the apse and was then used as a cemetery until Napoleonic laws: today it is partly a ruin, accessible by easy scenic paths.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Miniere di Calamita (Ginevro e Vallone)

History and architecture · Archaeology

On Monte Calamita, in the Tuscan Archipelago National Park, the mining activity lasted over 3000 years and was suspended in 1981. The mines of the Ginevro and Vallone, the oldest, are the only underground of the island, with about 7 km of tunnels, and are indicated as the largest magnetite deposit in Europe. They are visited with guided tours.

Museo della Vecchia Officina

Museums · Themed

Set up in the workshops of the Vallone yard of the mines of Calamita, exhibits objects, documents and cartography and reconstructs working environments such as the infirmary, the workshop and the accounting office. It has a small bookshop, a picnic area and a large car park.

Museo del Mare

Museums · Themed

Opened in 2014 in the center of Capoliveri, it is dedicated to the treasure of the ship Polluce, departed from Naples to Marseille and sunk off Capoliveri on the night of June 17, 1841 with a large load of coins; the wreck was found, looted and then recovered by the State. Organizes workshops, guided tours, conferences and walks

Spiaggia di Lacona e Laconella

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

About a kilometer of white and fine sand, the second longest beach of Elba after Marina di Campo, preserves remains of dunes; it is very equipped, suitable for children and served by the MareBus. The nearby Laconella, less frequented, can be reached on foot with a short dirt road and is suitable for snorkeling.
