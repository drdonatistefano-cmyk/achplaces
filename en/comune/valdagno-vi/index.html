What to see in Valdagno

Centre of the Alta Valle dell'Agno, between Small Dolomites and High Lessons: in Palazzo Festari the Civic Museum Domenico Dal Lago tells almost 300 million years of geological history of the valley, while Villa Valle Marzotto, of the late seventeenth century, is attributed to Frigimelica Roberti.

Museo civico Domenico Dal Lago

Museums · Science and university

Born from the geological collection that the doctor conducted Domenico Dal Lago collected by visiting patients and donated to the City in 1929; catalogued in 1975 by Paolo Mietto of the University of Padua, it was established as a museum in 1982, opened in 1995 in Villa Valle and transferred in 1999 to Palazzo Festari. Fossils and finds of the Valle dell'Agno retrace almost 300 million years; in the garden, wooden reconstructions of a velociraptor and a pteranodon, steel silhouettes on the evolution of life and a stratigraphic pyramid. Saturday with timetables indicated by the museum, the other days upon reservation.

  • Where Palazzo Festari, Corso Italia 63, Valdagno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museodallago.it

Villa Valle Marzotto

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa of the late seventeenth century attributed to the architect Frigimelica Roberti; it houses the civic library and the interiors are open to visitors. Here the Dal Lago Museum opened to the public in 1995, before the transfer to Palazzo Festari.

  • Where Viale Regina Margherita 1, Valdagno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page museodallago.it
