What to see in Magisano

Municipality of the province of Catanzaro, ancient farmhouse of Taverna and called Vucisano from the grasses of the area; in the hamlet of Vincolise there is the House museum of the Garibaldi patriot Antonino Greco, in San Pietro the Sanctuary of Mary Most Holy of Light.

Casa museo Antonino Greco

Museums · House museums

Casa del jurista e patriota garibaldino Antonino Greco, on two floors and a basement: on the ground floor the kitchen with the original structure, the rustic beliefs and an ancient furnace, on the upper floor rooms with antique furniture. It preserves rare books, Latin dictionaries of the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, the first encyclopedia UET (1863) and a sixteenth-century Petrarch, statues of the nativity scene made of mache, hand-embroidered lines and a cradle painted according to the Venetian techniques of the eighteenth century; the chapel, built around 1850 and consecrated with the bull of Pius IX, houses gold reliquaries, a silver cup and a painting by the Neapolitan Antonio Sarnelli.

MiC card (updated 2020) indicates closed every day; visit to be agreed with the person in charge indicated on the card

Santuario di Maria Santissima della Luce

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Sanctuary of the village of San Pietro dedicated to the Madonna of Light, celebrated on September 8 with the arrival of thousands of pilgrims.
