What to see in Goro

3 places to visit in Goro, in the province of Ferrara (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Porto di Goro e Isola dell'Amore

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Paid entry · Viewpoint

From the fishing port, the capital of clams, the boats depart to the Island of Love, a tongue of sand with dunes that change with winds and tides.

  • Where Porto di Goro
  • Entry Paid fishermen's ferry
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sacca di Goro

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Delta coastal lagoon, among the richest birdwatching areas in the province.

  • Where Goro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Faro di Goro e Gorino

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

The lighthouse at the mouth of the Po and the small port of Gorino, where the river meets the sea, reachable by boat or by bike along the banks.

  • Where Gorino, Goro
  • Entry To be checked: boat excursions
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: ferrarainfo.com · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
