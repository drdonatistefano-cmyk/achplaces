What to see in Scido

A small harsh town whose Greek name, Skydon, means "place" from the dense woods, razed to the ground by the earthquake of 1783, it preserves the parish church of San Biagio and, in the nineteenth century Palazzo Ruffo, the library "Paolo Greco" and the Museum of peasant civilization.

Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

At Palazzo Ruffo (1875): agricultural tools, cauliflowers and iron objects, a stone mill from the early twentieth century with a large hydraulic Pelton wheel still working, furniture of the eighteenth-nineteenth century, clothes and terracotta; a room exhibits over two hundred pipes of Mastro Rocco De Giglio, many shaped animals and human figures.

visits only by reservation (Common, tel. 0966 964048)

  • Where Via Roma 49, Scido
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.scido.rc.it

Sources: comune.scido.rc.it

Biblioteca comunale e Museo «Paolo Greco»

Museums · City history

In the ancient Palazzo Ruffo (1875), acquired and restored by the Municipality: books from the sixteenth century onwards (the complete work of Galluppi, editions of Saint-Non, Barrio and Strabone), manuscripts, parchments, archaeological and numismatic finds and the collection of pipes of the craftsman Rocco De Giglio (1913-2009).

  • Where Via Roma, Scido
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.scido.rc.it

Sources: comune.scido.rc.it · cultura.gov.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Biagio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Already in the 12th century, it houses the marble statues of the Madonna del Soccorso and Santa Caterina d'Alessandria, of the school of Gagini, of the 18th century.

  • Where Scido
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.scido.rc.it

Sources: comune.scido.rc.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria della Catena

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built after the earthquake of 1783 in the hamlet of Santa Giorgia, instead of the church of Santa Maria del Soccorso, it houses the statue of Maria della Catena.

  • Where Frazione Santa Giorgia, Scido
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
