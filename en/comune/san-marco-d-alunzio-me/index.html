What to see in San Marco d'Alunzio

Village of the Nebrodi on Monte Castro, overlooking the sea and the Aeolian Islands, with twenty-two churches in the historic centre and the local red marble everywhere: the temple of Hercules under the church of San Marco, the ruins of the castle of Roberto il Guiscardo (1061), the Badia Piccola with the Byzantine-Norman Museum and the churches with stuccoes of the Serpottian school.

Museo della cultura e delle arti figurative bizantine e normanne

Museums · Old masters

Established in 1997 in the former Benedictine monastery (16th century), on over 700 square meters, in three sections: archaeological, with a sacrificial altar dedicated to Augustus, a Corinthian capital in terracotta, Greek inscriptions and medieval bronze seals and medieval figurative Byzantine-Norman arts, in the former church of the Four Saints Doctors with frescoes of the Eastern Church; The Middle Ages in the Nebrodi's, with about 200 coins from the 5th to the 15th century. Outside there is a path illuminated between Hellenistic cisterns and a Hellenistic-Roman mosaic floor.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Chiesa di San Teodoro (Badia Piccola)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the 16th century on the ruins of a Byzantine chapel and linked to the Benedictine monastery that now houses the museum: a Greek cross plan with octagonal central space on eight large pillars, cupolettes, stuccos in Serpotti style and a top floor from which the cloistered nuns attended Mass without being seen.

Tempio di Ercole e chiesa di San Marco

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

In the lower part of the village the church of San Marco, without roof, with the perimeter walls and a rebuilt portal, rises on the Doric temple of Hercules (IV century BC), of which survive the rectangular cell and some blocks of tuff; the place is known for the views.

Chiesa di San Salvatore (Badia Grande)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Isolated towards the sports field, it was annexed to an important Benedictine monastery, now in ruins, founded by Queen Margherita of Navarre: portal in red marble, stuccoes of the Serpottian school and high altar in golden wood with canopy of Don Corrado Oddo (1704).

Chiesa Madre, Annunziata e San Basilio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Centro storico e Piazza Aluntina

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village rises on Monte Castro, surrounded by Nebrodi, overlooking the sea and the Aeolian Islands; the historic centre has twenty-two churches and preserves traces of Greeks, Romans, Byzantines, Arabs and Normans. In Piazza Aluntina a monumental fountain in local red marble, still worked by artisan workshops.
