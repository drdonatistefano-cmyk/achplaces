What to see in Avio

Borgo della bassa Vallagarina among vineyards and olive groves, known for its wine and oil, dominated by the Castello di Sabbionara, well of the FAI with frescoes from the Veronese school; in the village the Baroque parish church, the district with decorated portals that climb to the Pieve Vecchia and the House of the Vicar with Roman finds.

Castello di Sabbionara d'Avio (FAI)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Probable military garrison already in the Lombard age, passed to the Castelbarco and in 1441 to the Venetians; since 1977 it is of the FAI, that has restored it. You go up among the vineyards on the slopes of Mount Vignola to the tower "Picadora" where the condemned hanged themselves; the frescoes of the Verona school include the "Parata dei combatti" in the House of Guards and the courteous "Room of love" in the mast. In 2026 open from 14 February to 4 October at 10-18 (last entrance 17), then reduced hours until December; free entry with Trentino Guest Card and Museum Pass.

  • Where Via al Castello, Avio
  • Entry €8, reduced €5 (6-18 years and students up to 25), family €22; guided tour €13
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website fondoambiente.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · visitrovereto.it · visittrentino.info

Casa del Vicario e Antiquarium comunale

Museums · Archaeological

Building overlooking the medieval route that connects the Pieve to the Castle; it houses the Antiquarium municipal with Roman finds, including the cippi that marked the Via Claudia Augusta.

  • Where Avio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitrovereto.it · visittrentino.info

Centro storico e Pieve Vecchia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The main district is characterized by decorated portals and continues up to the Pieve Vecchia, at the outlet of the Valle dell'Aviana, mentioned since 1145.
