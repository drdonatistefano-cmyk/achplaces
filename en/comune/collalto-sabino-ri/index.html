What to see in Collalto Sabino

Borgo dominated by the Baronial Castle with the Fortress, remodeled by Baron Alfonso Soderini in the sixteenth century, from whose tower you can see Gran Sasso, Terminillo and Maiella; around fifteenth-century walls, the church of Santa Lucia and Monte San Giovanni with the remains of the hermitage of San Giovanni in Fistola.

Castello Baronale e Rocca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The heart of the village owes the plant to the work of Baron Alfonso Soderini in the second half of the sixteenth century. The fortress retains the military aspect of the 16th-XVII century, with a square central tower, two round corner towers, garrets and bastions, and a large park with an ancient well; from the mastium the view goes from Gran Sasso to Terminillo to Maiella, and at sunset the lights of the 34 villages around.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Centro storico e Piazza della Podesteria

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Inside the 15th century walls, cobbled alleys with stone portals; in Piazza della Podesteria an octagonal fountain, the seventeenth-century Latin Palace, recently restored, and the Villino Latini.

  • Where Piazza della Podesteria, Collalto Sabino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Monte San Giovanni e San Giovanni in Fistola

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A tourist trail climbs to the top of Monte San Giovanni, where the church and hermitage of San Giovanni in Fistola remain (first half of the 11th century), built on a pre-existing Roman temple dedicated to Giove Pollente.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Torrente e vecchio mulino

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

In the valley below San Giovanni in Fistola, an impetuous stream flows between two rock walls, at the edge of the mountains San Giovanni and Cervia; on the shore stands an old abandoned mill.
