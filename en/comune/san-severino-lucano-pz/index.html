What to see in San Severino Lucano

Borgo Bandiera Arancione in the heart of the Pollino National Park, with views of the Serra del Prete, the Serra Dolcedorme and Monte Pollino: the Sanctuary of Madonna del Pollino, the Bosco Magnano and the panoramic Ride Ride ride by Carsten Höller.

Santuario della Madonna del Pollino

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On a rocky spur at over 1,500 meters, in the area of Mezzana, the Marian complex dates back to about 1700 and includes church, clergy and guardians rooms and pilgrim's house; it houses the wooden statue of the Madonna with Child, which in summer rises in procession from the village. Accessible on foot, by bike or by car; open from early June to mid-September.

  • Where Località Mezzana, San Severino Lucano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Bosco Magnano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Naturalistic oasis a few kilometers from the village, reachable also on foot from the center, with large beech trees, oaks, oaks, maples, hornbeams and Neapolitan alders; it is crossed by the river Peschiera, habitat of the otter. It has parking, fountain and the Adventure Park of Pollino, inclusive and suitable for all ages.

  • Where Località Bosco Magnano, San Severino Lucano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

RB Ride

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Viewpoint

Carsten Höller's panoramic tour on the Timpa della Guardia (1125 m), produced for Artepollino and known as the slowest in the world: 12 colored gondolas for 24 people, 16.75 m in diameter, which run one lap every quarter of an hour tilting towards the ground.

  • Where Timpa della Guardia, San Severino Lucano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Chiesa Madre di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A 16th century church overlooking the historic centre, with three naves with a fine portal, nine altars in polychrome marble and a wooden crucifix of the 16th century; in winter it houses the statue of the Madonna del Pollino.

  • Where Centro storico, San Severino Lucano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · basilicataturistica.it

Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Born from the collection of master Tommaso Dattoli, formed in about thirty years: three rooms with agricultural tools, domestic tools and tools for the processing of milk, wool and broom, a room that reconstructs the farmhouse and a group of wooden objects between bread stamps, molds and dolls.

  • Where Corso Garibaldi, San Severino Lucano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo Laboratorio della Fauna Minore

Museums · Science and university

Zoological Museum of the Pollino National Park dedicated to invertebrates, in particular insects and butterflies of the territory, with a nod to the flora of the park.

  • Where Frazione Mezzana, San Severino Lucano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Mulino Iannarelli

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

18th century building at the confluence of the torrents Salice and Frido, which housed a hydraulic sawmill and a wool gulcher; renovated in the early 2000s, today it houses a typical restaurant and accommodations.

  • Where Frazione Mezzana, San Severino Lucano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · basilicataturistica.it

Gola del Frido

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

The canyon is rugged and evocative excavated by the Frido stream; from Mezzana itineraries start on foot, on horseback or by mountain bike towards the Pollino massif.

  • Where Frazione Mezzana, San Severino Lucano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
