Centro storico circolare e cummerse

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

A circular centre enclosed by walls, with concentric streets and low houses whitewashed in lime with sharp roofs called "cummerse" , covered with grey stone with chiancarelle. The village was first mentioned at the end of the 11th century; in the 15th century it was part of the principality of Taranto and two centuries later it passed to the Dukes of Martina.

  • Where Centro storico, Locorotondo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it

Lungomare panoramico di via Nardelli

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The balcony surrounding the houses of the center: from via Nardelli the view of the Itria Valley, with trulli (the housedde) and masserie, is particularly suggestive at sunset.

  • Where Via Nardelli, Locorotondo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Chiesa Madre di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built between 1790 and 1825 in place of three churches dedicated to the same saint, a Greek cross with neoclassical facade. Inside 42 marble tombstones with bas-relief of the late sixteenth century, baroque altars of 1764 and paintings of Gennaro Maldarelli (1838-41); the crypt preserves nineteenth-century burials, silver and urns.

  • Where Centro storico, Locorotondo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it · touringclub.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria la Greca

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

First mentioned in 1520 but with elements suggesting the XII-XIII century, of Romanesque-Gothic inspiration with an elaborate rose window; inside capitals with medieval motifs, the triptych of the main altar and sculptures of St. George (1559).

  • Where Centro storico, Locorotondo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it · touringclub.it

Piazza Vittorio Emanuele, Porta Napoli e Palazzo Morelli

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Two nineteenth-century pillars mark the ancient Porta Napoli, access to the historic center with the information office of the Pro Loco; in the center the Baroque Palazzo Morelli, with elegant portal and wrought iron balconies, and the former 18th-century Town Hall, today the municipal library.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele, Locorotondo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Nicola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches
