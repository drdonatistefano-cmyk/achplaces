What to see in Montemarciano

A hilly town overlooking the sea, overlooking the gulf of Ancona, the Conero and the Apennines; in the historical center the Teatro Alfieri of 1886-87 and the church of San Pietro Apostolo, on the coast the hamlet of Marina di Montemarciano with about 5 km of pebble beach and the fifteenth century Mandracchio.

4 places to visit in Montemarciano, in the province of Ancona (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico, portici e chiesa di San Pietro Apostolo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The route in the center touches the monument to the fallen of 1925, the eighteenth-century church of St. Peter the Apostle, the porticos with frescoes from the late nineteenth century by the painter Diotallevi and the monumental school in Liberty style of the early twentieth century. From the village the view covers the gulf of Ancona, the profile of the Conero and the Apennines.

  • Where Via Umberto I, Montemarciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Teatro Vittorio Alfieri

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Neoclassical theatre built in 1886-87 in the historic center, home to a theatrical season.

Mandracchio

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

15th-century building along State 16, what remains of an ancient mail station for changing horses on the coastal road.

  • Where Strada statale 16, Marina di Montemarciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Spiaggia di Marina di Montemarciano

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 5 km of equipped pebble beach, with free beach stretches and music and festivals in the establishments; according to recent findings the hamlet was a Roman settlement, perhaps a changed on the coastal road.

  • Where Marina di Montemarciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it
