What to see in Alatri

Città degli Ernici closed by polygonal walls of about 2 km with five doors; on the Civita, the Cyclopic acropolis with the Cathedral of São Paulo, while in the medieval center there are Santa Maria Maggiore, Palazzo Gottifredo with the Civic Museum and the fresco of Christ in the Labyrinth.

Acropoli (Civita) e mura poligonali

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The trapezoidal acropolis of about two hectares is surrounded by ciclopic walls of the second century BC on a more ancient fortification; the urban belt of about 2 km, work of the Ernics between IV and II century BC, has five doors. The Porta Maggiore has a monolithic architrave of over five meters, estimated in about 30 tons; from the gardens of the Civita the view extends over the Ciociaria and the Ernici Mountains. Every June 21st we go up to the acropolis to see the first light on the eastern wall.

  • Where Via del Duomo 31, Alatri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Cattedrale di San Paolo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

At the top of the acropolis, next to the episcopio, on a tree-lined square: erected before the thousand, modified in the sixteenth century and with eighteenth-century façade. It preserves the relic of the Incarnate Host, linked to a miracle of 1227 recognized by the Church, and the statue of St. Sixtus.

  • Where Via del Duomo, Alatri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded around the 5th century and enlarged in Romanesque times, it has the present aspect of the thirteenth century works of Burgundian workers: a hut façade with three portals and a rhyphole rosette, inside three naves. It preserves the Madonna of Constantinople, a wooden group of the thirteenth century, the Triptych of the Redeemer by Antonio da Alatri (first half of the fifteenth century) and a baptismal font of the thirteenth century with three telamons.

Museo civico in Palazzo Gottifredo

Museums · Archaeological

In the thirteenth century Palazzo Gottifredo, commissioned by Cardinal Gottifredo of Raynaldo; museum founded in 1932 and reopened in 1996. Archaeological sections on the 19th century scholars of the polygonal walls and on the ancient Aletrium (inscription by Lucio Betilieno Varo, model of Etruscan-Italian temple, bronze perhaps of Hercules, paintings of the Domus of Betilienus), vases recovered by the Guardia di Finanza and demoetnoanthropological collection. Open Tuesday-Sunday 10-13, Friday-Sunday also 15.30-18.30.

  • Where Corso Cavour 5, Alatri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.alatri.fr.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Cristo nel Labirinto

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Fresco discovered in 1996 in the rooms next to the cloister of the former convent of St. Francis: a Christ Pantocrator in the center of a unicursale labyrinth of twelve black circles, dated between the end of the 13th and the beginning of the 14th century. Open Tuesday-Sunday, closed on Mondays.

  • Where Piazza Regina Margherita, Alatri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.alatri.fr.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Piazza Regina Margherita e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

From the Porta San Francesco, on the north side, you enter the historic center; on the square there are three churches: San Francesco (XIV century), Santa Maria Maggiore (XIII century) and Santa Maria degli Scolopi in late Baroque style.

  • Where Piazza Regina Margherita, Alatri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Grancia di Tecchiena

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The Protocenobio di San Sebastiano, a large barn of the Carthusians of Tsulti, considered the best example of 18th century architecture of Alatri; it contains the small church of San Bartolomeo apostolo and is on the Via Francigena del Sud.
