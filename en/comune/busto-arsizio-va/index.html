What to see in Busto Arsizio

City of Lombard textiles: the Renaissance shrine of Santa Maria di Piazza with the polyptych of Gaudenzio Ferrari, the basilicas of San Giovanni and San Michele, Palazzo Marliani Cicogna, the Social Theatre on the model of the Scala and the liberty villas of the industrial age.

8 places to visit in Busto Arsizio, in the province of Varese (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario di Santa Maria di Piazza

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Main town shrine, built between 1515 and 1522 in the historic center instead of an older church: outside a cube with octagonal and lantern tiburium, inside an octagon with eight-piece dome. The portals are attributed to Tommaso Rodari; the dome was frescoed in 1531 by Giovan Pietro Crespi with drawers open on a starry sky, the presbytery in 1542 by Giovan Battista della Cerva. On the main altar the polyptych of the Assumption of Gaudenzio Ferrari and the wooden statue of the Madonna dell'Aiuto (1602) by Fabrizio De Magistris; bell tower of 1584. Closed on Wednesdays.

  • Where Piazza Santa Maria 1, Busto Arsizio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.bustoarsizio.va.it · vareseturismo.it · in-lombardia.it

Basilica di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

One of the main parishes of the city and the minor basilica, at the centre of the city's religious life; it is the first stage of the municipal itinerary "Devotion and sacred art."

  • Where Piazza San Giovanni, Busto Arsizio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.bustoarsizio.va.it · in-lombardia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

One of the most important places of worship in the city, dedicated to Saint Michael, patron saint of Busto Arsizio and his people.

Civiche raccolte d'arte di Palazzo Marliani Cicogna

Museums · Old masters

The Palazzo dei Conti Marliani, feudal lords of Busto between the Six and the eighteenth century, then of the Cicogna and municipal since 1820, has been a museum since 1990; after the restoration of 2002 the spaces have doubled. Paintings, drawings, documents and sculptures, more temporary exhibitions.

From 7 January 2026 closed for renovation works, except the halls of temporary exhibitions

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II 3, Busto Arsizio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.bustoarsizio.va.it · cultura.gov.it · in-lombardia.it

Museo del Tessile e della Tradizione Industriale

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1997 in the former spinning department of the Cottonificio Carlo Ottolini (later Bustese), neo-Gothic brick building probably from 1891-1896: machines, products, documents and objects of the textile industry and other industrial traditions of Busto.

From 7 January 2026 closed for renovation works (the conference room, the twin rooms and the embroidery room remain open)

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.bustoarsizio.va.it · in-lombardia.it

Fondazione Bandera per l'arte

Museums · Modern art

About one hundred works by Italian and foreign artists, including sculptures by Giorgio De Chirico and Francesco Messina and stained glass windows by Aligi Sassu and Remo Brindisi; it hosts exhibitions, presentations and conferences.

  • Where Via Andrea Costa, Busto Arsizio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.fondazionebandera.it

Sources: fondazionebandera.it · cultura.gov.it · in-lombardia.it

Teatro Sociale

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Inaugurated on September 27, 1891, with the "Force of destiny" by Verdi, on a project by Achille Sfondrini that followed the model of La Scala; frescoed in the restoration of 1935, it became cinema in the fifties and after the restoration ended in 2002 it returned theatre with 639 seats.

  • Where Via Dante Alighieri 20, Busto Arsizio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.bustoarsizio.va.it · in-lombardia.it

Itinerario Liberty

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

Municipal route in ten stages between the houses and villas of the industrial age, linked to the architect Silvio Gambini and the master of wrought iron Alessandro Mazzucotelli: Casa Colombo, Molini Marzoli Massari, Villa Leone, Palazzo Frangi, Casa Gambini, Villa Nicora and Convitto d'Annunzio, Casa Castiglioni, Villa Ottolini Tosi and Villa Ottolini Tovaglieri.

  • Where Centro, Busto Arsizio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.bustoarsizio.va.it · in-lombardia.it
