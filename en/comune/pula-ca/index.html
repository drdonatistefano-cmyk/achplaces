What to see in Pula

On the south-west coast of Cagliari, Pula preserves the ancient city of Nora, founded by the Phoenicians and became Roman town hall, with spas, forum, mosaics and amphitheatre on the promontory; in the village the Patron Museum with excavations finds, on the bay the church of Sant'Efisio and along the coast the coves of Santa Margherita.

Area archeologica di Nora

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Among the most important archaeological sites of Sardinia: founded by the Phoenicians in the 8th century BC, then Punic city and Roman town since the 1st century A.D. You can visit the thermal baths, the forum square, a temple, the house of the tetrastyle atrium with mosaics, the amphitheatre and the sanctuary of Esculapio; remains submerged visible with snorkeling. A cumulative ticket with the Patron Museum: entire € 5.50.

  • Where Promontorio di Capo di Pula, via Nora, Pula
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Museo civico archeologico «Giovanni Patroni»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In a period building in the historic center, the finds of Nora and the territory: objects of daily use and worship, stele of the Punic tofet (VIII century BC), amphorae and anchors from the backdrops and the golden lamina with gorgoneion, symbol of the museum. Summer 9-20, winter 9-17.30, closed on Mondays; entire 2.50 €, reduced 2 €, guided tour included.

  • Where Corso Vittorio Emanuele 67, Pula
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di Sant'Efisio di Nora

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Where tradition places the martyrdom of Efisio (303 A.D.): a high-medieval sanctuary rebuilt in Romanesque style by the victorious monks of Marseilles at the end of the XI century with stones of Nora, portico and atrium of the seventeenth century. The destination of the great procession that starts from Cagliari on May 1st, with rites on the beach on May 3.

  • Where Strada per Nora, a circa 3 km da Pula
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

A bay of clear and grainy sand with clear and shallow waters, sheltered from the mistral, closed by the promontory with the tower of Coltellazzo and a cliff rich in marine life.
