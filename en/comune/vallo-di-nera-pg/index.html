What to see in Vallo di Nera

Castle-country of the Valnerina on a hill, with the walls and fortified thirteenth century, houses in clear stone joined by passages and arches, churches frescoed by Cola di Pietro and Jacopo Siculo and the Casa dei Racconti dedicated to oral narration; in June the festival of cheese Fior di Cacio.

Centro storico, mura e porte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The castle was built after 1217, when Spoleto allowed the men of Vallo to fortify the hill: the pedestrian village, closed by walls and towers, you enter the Portella and Portaranne gates, among which stands the main tower of the fifteenth century; narrow streets and light stone houses connected by passages and arches converge on the square of San Giovanni Battista.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church enlarged by the Franciscans in the thirteenth century, with Gothic portal and bell tower obtained from a tower of defense; the interior frescoes are of Giotto school and include the Procession of the Whites painted by Cola di Pietro da Camerino in 1401, which depicts the procession of 1399. Nearby there is a large free parking.

  • Entry To be checked

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the thirteenth century, enlarged and partly rebuilt around 1575 (data engraved on the facade), with rose window and double-fore bell tower; the frescoes are by Jacopo Siculo, including the Dormitio Virginis of 1536 in the apse basin.

La Casa dei Racconti

Museums · Themed

Opened from 2005 near Santa Maria Assunta, it is an antenna of the Ecomuseo della Dorsale Apenninea Umbra dedicated to the oral tradition of Valnerina: myths, legends, fairy tales and satires like the stories .Vallinates, books, audio and video, the wardrobe of memory in wood and a copper sculpture-light by Franco Pappalardo, and the photographic exhibition ...Vallo dei Racconti. Casali: San Rocco e Madonna delle Forche

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the village of Casali, of 16th century origin, the church of San Rocco (XV century) rises just outside the walls; along the road to Castel San Felice there is the newsstand of the Madonna delle Forche, frescoed at the end of the 15th century.

Piedipaterno: Santa Maria dell'Eremita e San Sebastiano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Frazione about 3 km on the river Nera: the church of Santa Maria de Ugonis, called of the Hermit, ancient abbey with crypt founded in the 11th century by Vallombrosan monks on hermit cells and closed with the suppression of the order in 1654; there is also the thirteenth-century church of San Sebastiano.

Meggiano e Fosso di Roccagelli

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Meggiano is a hill castle with entrance door and the church of San Michele Arcangelo in the seventeenth century form; the path along the Fosso di Roccagelli has small waterfalls and lends itself to rafting and canyoning.
