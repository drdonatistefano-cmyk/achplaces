What to see in Artogne

Municipality of the lower Camonica Valley with a series of fifteenth-century churches and the late Baroque parish church of Saints Cornelius and Cipriano, with works by Andrea Fantoni, Giacomo Ceruti and Gaspare Diziani; it houses the Museum of Print "Lodovico Pavoni" and, at altitude, shares with Pian Camuno the station of Montecampione.

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Cornelio e Cipriano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Rebuilt in late Baroque style in the mid-18th century on a fifteenth-century building, it has a facade with eight niches with statues and an eighteenth-century portal in stone of Sarnico. Inside the frescoes of the eighties of the eighteenth century by Ludovico Gallina, the main Baroque wooden altar by Andrea Fantoni, the altarpiece of the Madonna del Rosario by Giacomo Ceruti called the Pitocchetto (coming from the church of Santa Maria) and paintings by Gaspare Diziani in the presbytery, including an Adoration of the Magi.

Chiesa di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

15th century church with high bell tower and portal of the fifteenth century; on the façade an eighteenth-century fresco of Purgant Souls and Annunciation, probably linked to pestilences, when the building was used as a lazzaretto. Inside the fourteenth-century frescoes, among ex-vote, a Saint Christopher with the Child and a Martyrdom of Saint Andrew of the early sixteenth century.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di Santa Maria ad Elisabetta («la Madonnina»)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

15th-century church along the ancient Valeriana street, perhaps finished in 1532 as indicated by the date on the stone portal Simona; bell tower with mullioned windows on each side. Inside a Madonna with Child and St. Anthony of Padua and a marble saintly aquarium of Vezza d'Oglio with the coat of arms of the Federici.

Chiesa di Santa Maria della Neve

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Piazze, it is the eighteenth-century reconstruction of a fifteenth-century building, with granite portal and a single nave interior of two bays with side chapels, decorated with white stuccoes and gold, frescoes in the medallions of the vaults and some canvases.

Chiesa di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The fifteenth-century church in the locality of Acquebone dedicated to San Rocco, invoked against pestilences and epidemics; inside paintings on the life of the saint.

Museo della Stampa «Lodovico Pavoni»

Museums · Themed

Founded at the beginning of the 2000s by Simone Quetti, a former pupil of the Pavoni printing school in Brescia, he tells the evolution of printing with machines still working that can be tried: presses of the nineteenth century, a linotype, flat-cylinder machines and a plate "Heidelberg"; with the guide you learn the traditional typographical techniques. Guaranteed opening by reservation (tel. 346 8315120, museodellastampa.artogne@gmail.com).

  • Where Via Concordia 2, Artogne
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Sources: turismovallecamonica.it

Montecampione

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Tourist station at an altitude between Artogne and Pian Camuno, born between the seventies and eighties with the first plants: the original nucleus of Alpiaz is about 1,200 m, the Plan at 1,800 m, where a statue in Corten steel 6 m high Mattia Trotta (2021) recalls the victory of Marco Pantani at the Giro d'Italia in 1998. In summer walks among the rhododendrons, excursions and bikes, including the Trail n. 721 from Plan to Rifugio Rosello and the Trail of the 3 Valleys; the ski area has about 30 km of slopes between 1,200 and 1,800 m.

the MonteCampion Ski Area card indicates the area in recovery and redevelopment in view of a future reopening

  • Where Via Legazzuolo, Montecampione, Artogne
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it
