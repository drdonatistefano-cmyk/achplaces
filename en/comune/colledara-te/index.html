What to see in Colledara

Teramano municipality of the Sicilian Valley at the foot of the Gran Sasso; in the Park equipped with the River Fiumetto, established in 1990, rises the medieval village of Castiglione della Valle, the ancient Castrum Leonis Vallis Sicilianae that according to tradition hosted Lucrezia Borgia, with the church of San Michele Arcangelo of the twelfth century.

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo a Castiglione della Valle

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

12th century church in the medieval square of Castiglione della Valle, a village known in ancient times as Castrum Leonis Vallis Sicilianae that in 1499 would host Lucrezia Borgia and Alfonso d'Aragona. It has a sailing bell tower with four bells and a two-door façade, the first with frescoed bezel and monolithic architrave; the interior with two naves (the left added in the unknown period) preserves Baroque decorations, eighteenth-century frescoes, a wooden ceiling and an organ of 1797. Opening hours not indicated: reference to the parish priest (tel. 328 1494172) or Pro Loco.

Parco attrezzato del Fiume Fiumetto e borgo di Castiglione della Valle

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

A territorial park established in 1990 and managed by the Municipality of Colledara, extended for over 80 hectares between the stream Sazza and the Fosso San Paolo, with inside the medieval village of Castiglione della Valle, where according to tradition stayed Lucrezia Borgia fleeing from his brother Cesare. Along the river grow willows and poplars, with reeded and raft marshes, small waterfalls on the Sazza and, further away, woods of roverella, cerro, orniello, black hornbeam and a querco-carpinet of glacial origin; there live green woodpecker, barbagianni, badger, faina and skunk. The easy botanical trail of the old mill has four thematic boards and cards; the Visite Center (2005), just before Castiglione, is a didactic workshop with herbarium, insectary and xilothotheque, and there is a refreshment point. Information and guided tours: Municipality of Colledara, tel. 0861 698551.
