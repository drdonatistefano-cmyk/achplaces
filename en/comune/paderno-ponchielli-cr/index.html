What to see in Paderno Ponchielli

Cremonese municipality of Roman origin, documented by findings, which preserves the birthplace of the composer Amilcare Ponchielli, today Ponchielli civic museum, and the church of Santo Stefano with its bell tower of about 54 meters.

Museo civico ponchielliano

Museums · House museums

It is located in the birthplace of Amilcare Ponchielli, the famous composer to whom the town owes its name, transformed into a museum dedicated to him. You can see his pianos, sheet music cases and other original objects.

Chiesa di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church is recognized from afar for the bell tower about 54 meters high, the third highest in the province of Cremona after the Torrazzo and the tower of Casalbuttano.
