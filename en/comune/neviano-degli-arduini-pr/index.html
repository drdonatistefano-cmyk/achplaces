What to see in Neviano degli Arduini

Municipality of the Parma Apennines between the Romanesque churches of Sasso and Bazzano, with three small original museums: the Resistance of July 1944, the first museum in the world dedicated to padlocks and the Museum Man-Environment in a house-tower of the thirteenth century.

6 places to visit in Neviano degli Arduini, in the province of Parma (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta di Sasso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

National monument, built according to the tradition in the time of Matilde di Canossa (1080-1090): Romanesque parish church on a spur of rock in the woods, with views of the Parma Apennines and Reggiano up to the Cimone. Inside the floor in sloping stone slabs and the baptismal font with the grifo, the blessing, the lion of St Mark and the angel of St Matthew.

  • Where Sasso, Neviano degli Arduini
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Pieve di Sant'Ambrogio a Bazzano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque parish church probably of the 11th century, with Renaissance additions and an octagonal baptismal font believed to be prior to the 1st century. At Christmas, the village becomes the village of the nativity scenes, with nativity scenes illuminated in the courtyards.

  • Where Bazzano, Neviano degli Arduini
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo storico della Resistenza di Sasso

Museums · City history · Paid entry

It reconstructs with panels, maps and films the facts from 30 June to 7 July 1944, the German rake with killings of civilians to the east of Cisa: the partisan band, the mountain population that suffered reprisals and requisitions, the context of the massacres of summer 1944. Open from April to November by reservation.

  • Where Sasso, Neviano degli Arduini
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo storico dei lucchetti – Collezione Vittorio Cavalli

Museums · Themed

Presented as the first museum in the world dedicated only to portable padlocks: over 3,000 specimens of different forms and epochs, from Europe, China, Africa and Asia, collected in sixty years by Vittorio Cavalli and set up by the Center Studies of the Valleys of the Termina with the Municipality. Open only by reservation.

  • Where Cedogno, Neviano degli Arduini
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo Uomo-Ambiente

Museums · Themed

In a house-tower of the thirteenth century in the center of Bazzano, opened in 2003 by the cultural group Il Camino: interactive routes on the processing of hemp and on the culture of wine, to see, touch and listen, with activities for schools. Recognized Museum of Quality by the Region.

  • Where Strada Borgo 2, Bazzano, Neviano degli Arduini
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitemilia.com · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Oasi di Monte Fuso

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Faunistic oasis on Mount Fuso, destination for walks and picnics, with a bar-restaurant and an adventure park.
