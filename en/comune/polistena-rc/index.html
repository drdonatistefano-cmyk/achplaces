What to see in Polistena

Cittadina della Piana with an 18th-century old town, rebuilt after the earthquake of 1783: the Mother Church with the marble altarpiece of the 16th century Deposition, the church of the SS. Trinity with the majolica dome, the nineteenth-century Rosary and the Civic Museum with eighteen works by the sculptor Francesco Jerace.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

18th century old town, built after the earthquake of February 5, 1783 that destroyed the ancient Polistena; among the alleys you will find coffee, typical desserts and trattorias with stochus and cutting. In the city are held the feast of the patron saint Marina (17 July), the rites of the Holy Week with the "Seven Words" by Michele Valensise and the "Affruntata," and in July the Night of the Giants Mata and Grifone.

  • Where Polistena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa Madre (Duomo di Santa Marina Vergine)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt in the upper part of the village, it has three naves and houses a marble altarpiece of the 16th century Deposition of Christ, brought back to the school of Michelangelo and defined an authentic masterpiece of which Calabria is proud.

  • Where Polistena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismo.reggiocal.it

Chiesa della SS. Trinità

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built on the previous church of Sant'Anna and aggregated in 1541 to the Lateran Basilica of Rome; it has two orders of granite columns, a stone portal and the dome covered with majolica. Inside a sixteenth-century icon and the wooden statue of Our Lady of Itria by Vincenzo Scrivo (1797).

  • Where Polistena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismo.reggiocal.it

Chiesa del SS. Rosario

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in the mid-nineteenth century, it has three naves in which the Baroque blends with the neoclassical, with Morani stuccos and high reliefs.

  • Entry Free

Chiesa dell'Immacolata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Already church of Minori Observanti, with classical exterior and large dome; inside a monumental marble altar of Varvella (1771), marble statues attributed to Pietro Bernini by F. Negri Arnoldi and an Immaculate woodwork (1833) by Vincenzo Sapphiro di Serra.

  • Entry Free

Chiesa di Sant'Anna

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It is the oldest building in the city to survive the earthquake of 1783, and the ashes of over two thousand victims of the earthquake rest there.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Scalinata di Polistena Vecchia

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built after the earthquake of 1783, it has 10 ramps and 80 steps between colorful houses and the wall of the walls of Palazzo Valensise; it starts from an alley next to the Duomo and goes down to Via Muraglie, where the city center stood before 1783. Nearby, the ruins of the Dominican convent on which stands Palazzo Valensise.

  • Where Via Polistena Vecchia 17, Polistena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Museo civico "Francesco Jerace"

Museums · City history

Provisionally housed in two halls of the Municipal Library, it contains gravestones, coats of arms and works of the churches, convents and civil buildings of the Polistena destroyed by the earthquake of February 5, 1783, paintings and sculptures of local artists (including eighteen works by Francesco Jerace), a herbarium, fossil and archaeological finds also protohistoric with funerary furnishings, parchments from 1430 to the end of the eighteenth century, photographs of the late nineteenth-early twentieth century and tools of peasant and craftsman work.

MiC card reports every day as closed and no time; location indicated as provisional
