What to see in Gazzuolo

Country on the right bank of the Oglio, whose name would come from "guado d'Oglio": of the castle of Gonzaga, demolished in 1705, remains the Preda Pescadura, while the main street is marked by the sixteenth-century portico of 30 arches wanted by Ludovico Gonzaga, under which the Giacometti Theatre and the Center of Audiovisual Communication Oreste Coni are located.

Portici Gonzagheschi e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The 120-metre-long porch has 30 arches on 29 marble columns, each different from the others for architecture and decoration: it was built in the early sixteenth century by Ludovico Gonzaga as a covered road and restored in 1933. Under the arcades there is the Teatro Paolo Giacometti, now the seat of the municipal library "Aldo Placchi"; in the center there are also the Baroque oratory of San Rocco, of the early seventeenth century, and the Preda Pescadura, the only testimony of the Gonzaga fortifications demolished in the eighteenth century (the castle was demolished in 1705).

  • Where Strada Provinciale 58, Gazzuolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it · in-lombardia.it

Centro della comunicazione audiovisiva «Oreste Coni»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Born from the donation to the Municipality of the collection of Oreste Coni, it is located in the cultural center under the porch Gonzaghesco, next to the Giacometti Theatre and the library. Cameras and cameras draw an overview from the 1920s to today: the first section follows with captions the origins of photography and cinema, the second collects projectors and a laboratory that introduces to audiovisual, photographic, direction and assembly techniques. Open Monday, Wednesday and Friday 15.30-18.30 and Saturday 16-18; guided tours by reservation (tel. 0376 924030).

  • Where Via Roma 19, Gazzuolo
  • Entry €2, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Nascente

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church, in Baroque style, dates back to the 17th century. It has only one nave with seven side chapels and a large presbytery.

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises on the road to Belforte and is considered the pantheon of the Gonzaga di Gazzuolo: there rest some descendants of the family's cadet branch.
