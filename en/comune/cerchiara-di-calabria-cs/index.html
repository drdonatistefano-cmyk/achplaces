What to see in Cerchiara di Calabria

Bread country on the Ionian side of Pollino, 650 meters on the slopes of Mount Sellaro and dominated by the remains of a medieval castle; above the village the rock sanctuary of Santa Maria delle Armi, under the Ninfe Cave with its sulphurous waters, and two small museums dedicated to bread and loricate pine.

Santuario di Santa Maria delle Armi

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

At 1,015 meters on Mount Sellaro, built in the fifteenth century on a Basilian cave documented already in the 10th century; the chapel of Our Lady is excavated in the rock, with circular plan, covered with white marbles with polychrome inlays, and preserves the image of Our Lady with Child in a silver reliquary. The wooden door is by Silvestro Schifino from Morano Calabro. Donated in 1517 to the University of Cerchiara, he hosted orphans and destitutes like Pia Casa di Carità until the second half of the nineteenth century; today he is of the Santa Maria Foundation. Feast April 25th.

  • Where Monte Sellaro, Cerchiara di Calabria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Grotta delle Ninfe e Terme di Cerchiara

Local life and traditions · Spas

Cavità limestone on the south-eastern slopes of Mount Sellaro where sulfurous hot water flows that form therapeutic muds, indicated for rheumatic and dermatological diseases; you can bathe in the tub at about 30 °C. The legend hides the nymph Calipso. Time: every day 8-19.

  • Where Grotta delle Ninfe, Cerchiara di Calabria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Centro storico e ruderi del castello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

A village about 650 meters on the slopes of Mount Sellaro, about twenty kilometers from the Ionian, built near the Greek settlement of Arponion and dominated by the remains of a medieval castle; the church of St. Peter holds valuable paintings. It is the City of Bread and Slow Food garrison, with a feast dedicated to bakery.

  • Where Cerchiara di Calabria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo del pane

Museums · Themed

In an ancient mill of a small medieval village just outside the center: original tools, the process of bakery and peasant civilization. The bread of Cerchiara, with the brand of the Pollino Park, is made with yeast mother, flour 0 and 00, bran and mountain water, cooked slowly with wood, and remains fragrant up to 15 days. Summer openings indicated 9-13 and 15.30-19, in winter by reservation.

  • Where Via Montebello 35, Cerchiara di Calabria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo del pino loricato

Museums · Science and university

In the historic center, dedicated to the pine loricato symbol of the Pollino Park, which grows on the peaks of Serra Dolcedorme and Serra delle Ciavole: finds, photos of the tree in its environment and panels on the types, diffusion and scientific characteristics. From June to September 9-13 and 15.30-19.30, in winter by reservation.

  • Where Centro storico, Cerchiara di Calabria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Monte Sellaro

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

One of the most eastern peaks of the Pollino massif (1,439 meters), reachable by a path in the vegetation: about 4 hours of excursion accompanied by the guides of the park.
