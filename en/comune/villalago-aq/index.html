What to see in Villalago

Borgo almost a thousand meters on the Valley of Sagittarius, founded in the 11th century by Benedictine monks, with a historic center with amphitheatre of stairs, arches and carved portals; under the village the lake of San Domenico with the hermitage where the saint lived around the year 1000 and, little beyond, the lake Pio, regional nature reserve.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the piazza Celestino Lupi climb to amphitheatre stairs, alleys and squares on the eastern side of the mountain, among the "suppurtu" arches on which lay stone houses and beams, and portals carved with coats of arms of animals, flowers and faces. The Porticella, the old nineteenth-century Town Hall, the bell tower of St. John the Baptist became a civic tower with clock in 1887, the sixteenth-century Palazzo della Cancelleria with two-folded windows and stone architraves and, at the top of the village, the chapel of the Madonna Addolorata (1575) annexed to Palazzo Lupi; the circular defensive towers are incorporated into the houses.

  • Where Piazza Celestino Lupi, Villalago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa della Madonna di Loreto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish Church in the Lower Borgo, mentioned already at the beginning of the fourteenth century: it contains a Madonna del Rosario dated 1561, a statue of St. Dominic, a tooth of the saint in a 15th century reliquary and a beam of the bed from the hermitage of Prato Cardoso.

  • Where Borgo basso, Villalago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small church of the Upper Borgo, called at the Arapezzzana: above the portal a 11th-XII century bezel depicts Christ with the book of life between two kneeling angels and hooded monks.

  • Where Borgo alto, Villalago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Museo delle tradizioni del lavoro

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

In the old town, in a tower with terrace on the Sagittarius Valley and on the lake of San Domenico: objects of peasant art, models of a mill and a gulchiera built by the miller Domenico Grossi and wool fabrics of the traditional male dress of Villalago and Scanno; slides of landscapes and daily life are projected. According to the MiC is open from Monday to Saturday, 15.30-18.30.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Villalago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Eremo e lago di San Domenico

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Cave excavated in the limestone rock where according to tradition lived around the year Mille san Domenico, Benedictine monk of Sora venerated for the miracles of his pilgrimages; you arrive there crossing a bridge in medieval style. The portal of the church has Byzantine-style designs and a bifora overlooks the lake with clear green waters, populated by germans, geese and folk.

  • Where Lago di San Domenico, Villalago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Riserva naturale Lago di San Domenico e Lago Pio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Regional nature reserve at the border of the Abruzzo National Park, between oak woods and beech: Lake Pio is a natural oasis of water birds, along the Sagittario flanked by poplars.

  • Where Lago Pio, Villalago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it
