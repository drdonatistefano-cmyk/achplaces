What to see in Orsara di Puglia

Borgo Bandiera Arancione on the slopes of the Dauni Mountains, with the historic center that rises along the ridge: the abbey complex with the Cave of San Michele and the church of the Annunziata of the 11th century, the Baronale Palace of the Knights of Calatrava and the bread baked in the straw oven.

8 places to visit in Orsara di Puglia, in the province of Foggia (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Grotta di San Michele e chiesa di San Pellegrino

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Partly natural cave and partly dug into the rock, a pilgrimage destination, reached by the Santa Scala with two openings with an ogival arch; inside a single irregular nave with a rock vault and the statue of the Archangel with a sword. In 1643 it was incorporated into the vestibule of the church of San Pellegrino, rebuilt in neo-Gothic style in 1935 after an earthquake; the portal has six bronze panels by Jorio Vivarelli (1969).

  • Where Largo San Michele, Orsara di Puglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa dell'Annunziata (ex Abbazia di Sant'Angelo)

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Romanesque church of the 11th century with two Byzantine domes of different sizes, born from a monastery perhaps of the 8th century, Benedictine complex before 1125 and entrusted in the 13th century to the Knights of Calatrava (1228-1294).

  • Where Orsara di Puglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Palazzo Baronale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Next to the abbey, with a large courtyard over a rustic portal and stone tower with arched windows; of the thirteenth century, it was home to the Knights of Calatrava, who came from Spain, and from 1563 residence of the Guevara feudataries.

  • Where Piazza Mazzini, Orsara di Puglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Museo diocesano a Palazzo Varo

Museums · Old masters

The sixteenth-century Palazzo Varo, Renaissance, preserves the portal with a noble coat of arms, the internal stone staircase and the cellars; purchased by the Diocese of Troy at the beginning of the twentieth century, on the first floor houses the museum, with archaeological finds, works of art and objects of use from the Neolithic to the twentieth century.

MiC card indicates the museum closed every day (tel. 0881 964064)

  • Where Largo San Michele (via Carlo Alberto), Orsara di Puglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Nicola di Bari

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Already existing in 1127, rebuilt in Baroque forms since 1622 and parish seat since 1590; it houses the seventeenth-century statues of St. Michael of Giacomo Colombo and of the Madonna della Neve by Aniello Stallato, a marble altar of 1777, an organ of 1756 and a two-faced stone crucifix carved and painted between the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries.

  • Where Piazza del Municipio, Orsara di Puglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Centro storico, mura e porte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historic centre rises along the ridge; the medieval walls, which had about twenty towers and four gates, remain Porta dei Greci (or Porta Ecana), Porta Nuova opened in the fifteenth century and part of Porta San Giovanni near San Domenico, with the Fontana Nuova in Piazza Mazzini. On November 1st the village is filled with the carved pumpkins of the festival "Fucacoste e cocce priatorje" .

  • Where Piazza Mazzini, Orsara di Puglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Palazzo di Torre Guevara

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

About 7 km from the village, in the Piana di Giardinetto: built in 1680 as a luxurious hunting estate of the Guevara, on three floors and about 60 for 20 meters with 80 rooms, it was among the royal residences of the Aragonese courts and then Bourbonic of Naples.

  • Where Piana di Giardinetto, Orsara di Puglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Pane e Salute, forno a paglia

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

A workshop known for its goat cheese and homemade durum wheat bread, cooked with mother yeast in a straw oven dating back to 1526.
