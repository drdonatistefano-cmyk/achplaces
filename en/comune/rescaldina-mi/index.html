What to see in Rescaldina

Municipality of Alto Milanese near the Milan-Varese motorway that houses the Zucchi Collection, indicated as the largest collection in the world of blocks for hand printing on fabric, and part of the Park of Rugareto, crossed by the Way of Saint Augustine.

Zucchi Collection, blocchi antichi per la stampa a mano 1785-1935

Museums · Themed

A collection that traces three centuries of textile culture in Europe: it is indicated as the world's largest collection of hand-printing blocks on fabric, wood and metal, catalogued and sorted according to style criteria.

the MiC card is closed every day of the week; check if it can be visited by appointment

Parco del Rugareto

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Local park of supra-municipal interest of about 1,400 hectares between Cislago, Marnate, Gorla Minore and Rescaldina, east of the Olona valley up to the pine forest of Tradate. Wood of robinies, silvestri pine trees, oaks, hornbeams, chestnuts and birch trees, with squirrels, peaks, owls and owls; cross it three streams (Fontanile di Tradate, Gradeloso or Bozzentino and Bozzente, the only one with constant flow even in dry periods). The seventeenth stage of the Way of Saint Augustine passes through.
