What to see in Sambuca Pistoiese

Mountain town on the border between Tuscany and Emilia-Romagna, between woods, valleys, rivers and springs; it crossed the Via Francesca della Sambuca, variant of the Francigena between Pistoia and Bologna, which rises from Pàvana to the fortified village of Castello di Sambuca, while the Itinerary of the Stone of the Ecomuseum connects Torri, the Torraccia and Treppio.

9 places to visit in Sambuca Pistoiese, in the province of Pistoia (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Sambuca

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval fortified village, once closed by walls that made it impregnable, the point of arrival of the Via Francesca. The ruins of the pentagonal tower, one third of the original, remain in the fortress; the church of Saints Christopher and Jacopo is Romanesque, renovated and enlarged in the eighteenth century, with portico of the early nineteenth century. It can be reached with 2.1 km of asphalt road from the SS64 in the locality Bellavalle.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Via Francesca della Sambuca, da Pàvana al Castello

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Variant of the Francigena between Pistoia and Bologna, quoted around 1250 by the abbot Migliore. The didactic route from Pàvana to the Castello di Sambuca (CAI 163 trail) is about 2.4 km long, with 254 m of difference in altitude and average slope of 10.4%, about an hour of walk on a 2.20 m wide pavement; passes through La Capanna, mentioned in the cadastre of 1587. Along the way many houses carry ancient masks carved in stone.

  • Where Partenza da Pàvana, Sambuca Pistoiese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.sambuca.pt.it · ecomuseopt.it · visittuscany.com

Pàvana, Logge e Dogana granducale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The most populous center of the municipality, on the SS64 Porrettana, at 492 meters: the church of the Saints Mary and Frediano of the second half of the eighteenth century, the Logge of the mid nineteenth century with frescoes of 1998 by Paolo Maiani, the dam on the Limentra (1923-1928) and, in the hamlet Valdibura, the Grand-ducal Customs of the mid nineteenth century on the border between the Grand Duchy of Tuscany and the Papal State.

  • Where Pàvana, Sambuca Pistoiese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.sambuca.pt.it · visittuscany.com

Torri, La Torraccia e Cave di Torri

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Torri is mentioned in a document of 982, with the church of Santa Maria Assunta and the nucleus of Ciliegia known for the sculpted architraves; at Torraccia the oratory of San Martino and seven sculpted apotropaic faces, difficult to find. To the south of the village the sandstone quarries, on a front of several hundred meters, used until the early eighties.

  • Where Torri, Sambuca Pistoiese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.sambuca.pt.it · visittuscany.com

Treppio e Valle della Limentrella

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Itinerary of the Stone between the church of San Michele Arcangelo, Villa Gargallo and the hamlets of Treppio, with the water mill of Docciola; then the stone arch bridge and the fountain of Casa Nuova, Campaldaio, the Mill of Casina in Poggiolino, a dryer in Tortello and a large sack wall in Piano.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
