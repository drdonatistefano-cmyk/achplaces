What to see in Matera

City of the Sassi, dug into the rock on the side of Gravina and UNESCO heritage with the Park of the rock churches: the Sasso Barisano and the Sasso Caveoso around the Civita and the Cathedral, the frescoed rock churches, the Palombaro Lungo under Piazza Vittorio Veneto and, beyond the gravina, the Materan Murgia.

30 places to visit in Matera, in the province of Matera (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Sasso Caveoso

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the two districts of the Sassi, to the south, where the presence of the caves is more visible: a maze of alleys, cave houses, terraces and tunnels dug into the rock, inhabited until the 1950s.

  • Where Sasso Caveoso, Matera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Cattedrale di Matera

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Apulian Romanesque cathedral of the 13th century on the highest point of the Civita, dedicated to the Madonna della Bruna: it preserves a Romanesque rosette, the wooden choir of 1453 and the stone nativity scene carved in 1534 by Altobello Persio and Sannazzaro da Alessano.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Matera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: geogebra.org · unesco.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Palombaro Lungo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Parco della Murgia Materana

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Park established in 1990 as a natural archaeological regional park of the rock churches of Materano: caves of the Paleolithic like the Grotto of the Bats, Neolithic villages of Murgecchia, Murgia Timone and Trasanello, masserie, jazzi and about 150 rock churches; symbol of the park is the hawk cricket.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
