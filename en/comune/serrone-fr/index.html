What to see in Serrone

A stone village over 700 metres at the foot of Monte Scalambra, overlooking the Sacco valley, on the Via Francigena del Nord; it houses the permanent exposition of theatrical costumes by Beatrice Minori, the hermitage of St. Michael the Archangel in the rock and the churches of St. Quiricus and St. Peter the Apostle. At Christmas the alleys host the ethnographic nativity scene, from 8 December to 6 January.

Esposizione permanente di costumi teatrali

Museums · Themed

The only permanent exhibition of this kind in Lazio and among the few in Italy: over forty costumes made by Beatrice Minori, costume designer RAI born in Serrone in 1923 and assistant to Eduardo De Filippo, who donated them to the birthplace; many were worn by Eduardo, Gassman and Beppe Barra. The itinerary "Il costume," the actor, the character" is divided into two rooms, from the Renaissance to the eighteenth century and from the nineteenth to the twentieth century, with captions on work, author, interpreter, character and year. Open Saturday 16-19 and Sunday 10-19.

  • Where Piazza Plebiscito, Serrone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Eremo di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

We were blessed set in a rocky ridge of Monte Scalambra at 1,100 meters; according to legend the saint defeated the devil, whose fall left the mark of the devil. The patron's feast is September 29th. The first paragliding flight started in 1978 from Monte Scalambra.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

A stone village of alleys, ramps and steps overlooking the valley of Sacco; the name recalls the "serroni" , the rocky ridges of the Scalambra in the middle of whose slope the first fortified settlement arose in the 6th century. Feud of the Counts of Signs since 1378 and of the Column since 1427.

  • Where Centro storico, Serrone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Parco della Rocca di Torre Colonna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Part of the Latium Historical Houses Network: the fortress, in defence of Anagni, was burned in 1184 and rebuilt on the ruins; since 1427 it was for centuries the Colonna. The Torre Colonna remains with battlements, wounds and walkways and stretches of the walls, particularly suggestive under the snow.
