What to see in Gibellina

Destroyed by the earthquake of Belice in January 1968 and rebuilt on a panoramic height by an intuition by Mayor Ludovico Corrao, with the involvement of numerous artists and architects, Gibellina is an open-air museum and is Italian capital of contemporary art 2026: the Great Cretto of Burri on the ruins of the old city, the Star of Consagra, the Mother Church of Quaroni, the MAC and the Museum of Mediterranean Trames.

Grande Cretto di Burri

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

On the site of Gibellina, a few kilometers from the new city, Alberto Burri covered the ruins with white concrete and lime castings that reproduce the cracks left by the earthquake: it is considered one of the greatest land art works in the world and a monument to the earthquake of 1968. Next to the opera there was only one wall of the house; since the eighties the Cretto also acts as a stage for theatre performances.

Museo delle Trame Mediterranee (Baglio Di Stefano)

Museums · Modern art

Opened in 1996 at the will of Ludovico Corrao and today run by the Orestiadi Foundation, it occupies the former rural lighthouse Di Stefano. It brings together objects of use, costumes, writings, ceramics, fabrics, sculptures and contemporary installations from Sicily, Tunisia, Morocco, Palestine, Egypt, Spain and Albania, with works by Accardi, Dorazio, Turcato, Consagra, Schifano, Boetti, Isgrò and Beuys; outside the 'Montagna di sale' by Mimmo Paladino, created for the Orestiadi of 1990. He received an ICOM prize for cultural mediation. Open from Tuesday to Sunday 9-13 and 15-18, Monday closed.

Stella di Consagra (Porta del Belice)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Great stainless steel star by Pietro Consagra (1980) that welcomes those who arrive at the entrance of the new city, symbol of reconstruction.

Sistema delle piazze e Torre civica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Sequence of large squares connected by long arcades (1987-90), designed by Franco Purini and Laura Thermes around Piazza del Comune, whose portico is by Vittorio Gregotti and Giuseppe Samonà; symbol of the city is the civic tower of the clock of Alessandro Mendini. Through the streets of the village murals and works of Schifano, Cascella, Pomodoro, Paladino, Angels, Accardi and Rotella.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
