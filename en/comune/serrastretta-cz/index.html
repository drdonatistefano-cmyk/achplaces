What to see in Serrastretta

Borgo founded in 1383, which takes its name from the narrow gorge between two mountain ranges, the Serre, where the first settlement arose; it is the village of the stuffed chairs, told in the Museum of peasant civilization. In the surroundings the beech forest of Condrò preserves the Margari Stone, the ancient hideout of the brigands.

Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Permanent set-up of work tools, handicrafts and everyday objects, with reconstructions of domestic and working environments. It exhibits the tools for wood processing and the production of the typical stuffed chairs of Serrastretta, woven with the ... vuda, plant of the wetlands, and a clock of the late nineteenth century still working; in the courtyard an ancient nineteenth century mill and a water mill.

Bosco di Condrò e Pietra dei Margari

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Beech of about 200 hectares, Site of Community Interest, with imposing trees and a fairytale atmosphere; in the woods there is the Margari Stone, large dark boulder once hiding from the brigands.

  • Where Bosco di Condrò, Serrastretta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
