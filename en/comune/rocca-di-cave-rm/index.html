What to see in Rocca di Cave

Small village of the Prenestini Mountains, 63 km from Rome, gathered around the Fortress Colonna that houses the Geopaleontological Museum "Ardito Desio" and an astronomical observatory; in the surroundings the fossil clay cliff, natural monument, and the planetary with the reconstruction of the dinosaur "Tito" .

5 places to visit in Rocca di Cave, in the province of Rome (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo geopaleontologico «Ardito Desio»

Museums · Science and university

In the Rocca Colonna, it is dedicated to the geologist Ardito Desio, who led the first Italian expedition on the K2 in 1954, and tells the geological history of Lazio in five rooms with original finds, globes, plastics, tactile models and sound contents, including fossils of coral reef of about 100 million years ago. Renovated with PNRR funds . Attritività dei borghi , reopened with the new equipments on August 9, 2025. On request, guided tours continue on the geopaleontological routes reported around the country; there are audio guides and activities for schools and groups.

Rocca Colonna e osservatorio astronomico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

A watchtower around the year 1000, owned by the Columns from the 12th to the 19th century, built on a Roman site with irregular hexagonal plan; inside there are traces of the ancient church of St. Peter. At the top of the tower is an astronomical observatory with a new dome and telescope.

  • Where Piazza della Torre, Rocca di Cave
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · cultura.gov.it

Scogliera cretacica fossile (Monumento naturale)

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Layers of an ancient cliff of a tropical sea of about 100 million years ago with remains of molluscs, sea urchins, sponges and corals, one of the rare examples on the Atlantic-Abruzzi carbonate platform; it is a natural monument since 2016 (D.P.R.L. 3 October 2016, n. 193), run by the Municipality. A geological route of about 1.5 km starts from Via Genazzano.

Planetario e Giardino paleobotanico (dinosauro «Tito»)

Museums · Science and university

Planetary with full-dome screen of 10 meters, with performances on constellations, Moon, planets and galaxies and stories about the myths of the night sky. In the Paleobotanic Garden there is the six-metre-long, life-size reconstruction of ... Tito , titanosaur of about 112 million years whose remains were found in the stones of a dry wall near the village, along with the models of a Diplodocus and a Sartriosaurus.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Marroni di Cave e cucina dei Prenestini

Local life and traditions · Food and wine
