What to see in Castelfranco Veneto

Giorgione's birthplace, enclosed in a square of red brick walls erected by Treviso between the 12th and 13th centuries, among the best preserved in Italy: inside the walls the 18th-century Cathedral of Francesco Maria Preti with the Pala di Giorgione, the Giorgione House with the Fregio delle Arti and the Academic Theatre; outside, Piazza Giorgione with the Loggia del Paveion and the large English garden of Villa Revedin Bolasco.

Mura, castello e Torre civica

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The square of red bricks was erected between the end of the XII and the first decades of the XIII century by the Municipality of Treviso on an existing embankment: about 17 meters high and about 230 meters long per side, with towers at the corners and in the midway points, escaped the demolitions of the nineteenth century and among the best preserved in Italy. Under the vault of the Civic Tower the coat of arms with the Carraresi chariot; in the evening the walls are illuminated.

  • Where Torre civica, Castelfranco Veneto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.veneto.eu

Museo Casa Giorgione

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

Opened on May 9, 2009 in the house where Giorgione painted the Fregio delle Arti liberali e meccanici, the only fresco attributed to him, next to the Duomo that houses the Pala; it collects about 100 original pieces of the time, from the civic collection or from the antique market, and architectural reconstructions. Open Mar-Jo 10-13, Friday 10-18; closed Monday, Christmas, January 1st and Easter.

  • Where Piazza San Liberale, Castelfranco Veneto
  • Entry €7, reduced €5, minor €1, family €12
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museocasagiorgione.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: museocasagiorgione.it · cultura.gov.it · veneto.eu

Duomo di Santa Maria Assunta e San Liberale e Pala di Giorgione

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Designed in 1723 by 23-year-old Francesco Maria Preti on the model of the Redeemer of Palladio, open to cult unfinished in 1746, with facade of 1892-93 by Pio Finazzi. In the Costanzo chapel, on the right of the presbytery, the Pala di Giorgione, sacred conversation commissioned by the leader Tuzio Costanzo after the death of his son Matthew (1499). In the Duomo works by Ponchini, Palma il Giovane, Paolo Piazza and Beccaruzzi; in the sacristy seven fragments of frescoes by Veronese from the demolished Villa Soranzo of Treville and paintings by Jacopo Bassano. Open mon-sab morning and afternoon (15-17.45), Sunday only afternoon; sacristy on request.

Piazza Giorgione, Paveion e Corso XXIX Aprile

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Outside the walls, the square was the ancient market: there stands the Paveion, loggia of the grains of 1603 where the negotiations were held; Corso XXIX Aprile is the elegant street of ancient palaces. Inside the walls the Palazzo del Monte di Pietà, which until the twentieth century lent to the poor of the city, today the Municipal Library, and the Palazzetto Preti, the only part built of the eighteenth-century hospital of Preti, seat of exhibitions.

  • Where Piazza Giorgione, Castelfranco Veneto
  • Entry Free

Villa Revedin Bolasco e parco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

New Renaissance villa built between 1852 and 1865 for Count Francesco Revedin on a project by Giambattista Meduna, donated in 1984 to the University of Padua and restored in 2015. The English garden, one of the most important in Veneto, covers more than 8 hectares with more than a thousand trees, a lake with two islands fed by the Avenale, the Cavallerizza with semicircle statues, a Moorish greenhouse and two towers. Access by reservation on weekends and holidays (049 827 3939).

Chiesa di San Giacomo Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Erected in 1420 and renovated between 1728 and 1732 on a project attributed to Giorgio Massari, with his convent; altarpiece of the apse of Pietro Damini (1616-17), altarpiece of Egidio Dall'Oglio (about 1740), six lateral altars in 18th century marble and wooden choir in carved walnut.

Oratorio del Cristo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

At the Duomo, of medieval foundation and already home to the Confraternity of the Battuti: above the door the dates 1300 (construction), 1446 and 1618 (restauri) and 1902; enlarged in 1693, it has eighteenth-century facade. Behind the tower of the dead is the old cemetery.

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
