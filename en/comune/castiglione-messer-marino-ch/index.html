What to see in Castiglione Messer Marino

The village of Alto Vastese is over 1,000 meters away, with its medieval historic centre, the Mother Church of San Michele and, in the territory, the Madonna del Monte complex on the ancient fiefdom of Lupara and the WWF oasis of Abetina di Selva Grande.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The center retains the medieval plant; the name recalls Marino Caracciolo, feudal lord who married Maria de Sangro, who brought the territory into dowry. At Christmas, the "'ndocce" wood charms lit, illuminate alleys and squares transforming the country into a large living nativity scene.

  • Where Castiglione Messer Marino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.abruzzoturismo.it

Chiesa Madre di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church in neoclassical style with a beautiful stone portal, perhaps fifteenth century plant transformed between the sixteenth and eighteenth centuries; the interior, recently restored, contains wall paintings by Federico Spoltore.

Complesso religioso della Madonna del Monte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

It rises in the area of the ancient feud of Lupara, strategic node of the Tratturo Ateleta-Biferno, one of the routes that led the shepherds from Abruzzo to Puglia through Molise.

Abetina di Selva Grande

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

WWF oasis of 800 hectares with picnic areas and paths, among white firs, beech trees, maples, badgers and lindens; deer, roe deer and wolves, symbol of the reserve can be seen.
