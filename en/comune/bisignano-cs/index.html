What to see in Bisignano

Ancient bishopric of Cosentino, known worldwide as the home of St. Umile, dedicated to the Franciscan sanctuary with a museum; it is also a land of luthiers, with shops in the alleys of the historic center and a museum dedicated to the De Bonis brothers.

7 places to visit in Bisignano, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario e convento di Sant'Umile

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

The origins date back to 1222, when Blessed Pietro Cathin, sent by Saint Francis, spread the Franciscan ideal there; the convent was rebuilt in 1380 and in 1441 passed to the Friars Minor. Between the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries lived the mystical Saint Humble. It preserves a crucifix carved by Fra Umile da Petralia, the marble statue of Our Lady of Graces (1537) attributed to the Gaggini, an organ of 1756 by Maurus Gallo and a painting by Saint Daniel martyr.

  • Where Via del Santuario, Bisignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.bisignano.cs.it · italia.it

Museo del Santuario di Sant'Umile

Museums · Themed

Also called the Convent Museum, it is inside the sanctuary, in front of the cell of St. Humble; in the windows objects and sacred books, paintings and reliquaries. It was founded by the impulse of Father Modesto Calabretta and pilgrims who arrived at the birthplace of the saint.

  • Where Via del Santuario, Bisignano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.bisignano.cs.it · italia.it

Museo della liuteria

Museums · Themed

Established by the Municipality in memory of the violins Nicola (died 1978) and Vincenzo De Bonis (died 2013), he exhibits guitars and mandolins of the two brothers and their students. The Biignanese violin garden has its roots between the 12th and 13th centuries and has developed mainly since the 18th century.

  • Where Piazza Collina Castello, Bisignano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.bisignano.cs.it · italia.it

Local life and traditions · Crafts · Free entry

Museo diocesano di arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

Set up in the church of San Giuseppe, it contains sacred silvereries, paintings and liturgical vestments of different eras and workshops from the cathedral and the churches of the territory.
