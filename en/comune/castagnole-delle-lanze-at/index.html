What to see in Castagnole delle Lanze

Borgo Bandiera Arancione between Langhe and Monferrato, with the porticoed street painted by Vincenzo Piccato and the panoramic tower of the Park of the Remembrance.

3 places to visit in Castagnole delle Lanze, in the province of Asti (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parco della Rimembranza e torre panoramica

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Dedicated to the fallen of the First World War, with a panoramic tower that was an astronomical observatory and a museum path.

  • Where Castagnole delle Lanze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Via Ener Bettica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The main road with porticos and paintings by the artist Vincenzo Piccato inspired by nature and wine.

  • Where Via Ener Bettica, Castagnole delle Lanze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it
