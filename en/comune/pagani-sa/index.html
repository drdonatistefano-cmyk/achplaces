What to see in Pagani

The city of the Agro Nocerino linked to St Alphonso Maria de' Liguori, who founded in 1742 the religious house with the basilica where it rests: in the complex the museum with the harpsichord on which it composed you come down from the stars.

Basilica di Sant'Alfonso Maria de' Liguori

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

At the centre of the large complex founded in 1742 by Saint Alphonsus Maria de' Liguori, with the redemptorist religious house and the basilica dedicated to the saint.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Alfonso, Pagani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Alfonsiano

Museums · House museums

Inaugurated by John Paul II in 1990: the harpsichord on which St Alphonsus composed ♪You descend from the stars ♪, a ceramic nativity scene of Capodimonte, vestments, autograph letters, the wax burial mask and his intact cell; the gallery counts a hundred paintings, including Flemish works of the sixteenth century and Neapolitan of the eighteenth century of the school of Solimena.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Alfonso, Pagani
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
