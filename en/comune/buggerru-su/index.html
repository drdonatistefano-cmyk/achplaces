What to see in Buggerru

Mining village born in 1864 on the Costa Verde, among the sites of the Park Geominerario of Sardinia: the Henry Gallery, crossed by train overhanging the sea, the mine of Malfidano, the memory of the slaughter of miners of 4 September 1904 and, to the south, the beach of Cala Domestica with the Spanish tower.

5 places to visit in Buggerru, in the province of Sud Sardegna (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Galleria Henry

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Viewpoint

Digged in the last decades of the nineteenth century and named after a director of the French company of Malfidano, there ran a steam locomotive that brought the mineral to the laverie and the port. Today you will walk on an electric train along the old track and on the return on foot in the pedestrian tunnel of the mules, with dark stretches illuminated by large windows on the sea, up to a viewpoint at about 50 m altitude; guided tours.

Visitable only by reservation

Miniera di Malfidano e Museo del minatore

Museums · City history

In 1864 engineer Giovanni Eyquem discovered a rich zinc deposit and founded the company of Malfidano; between 1870 and 1890 the laverie arose and since 1866 more than one million tons of zinc and 200,000 of lead were extracted. On September 4, 1904, the miners' strike ended in the Buggerru massacre, with three workers killed. The visit crosses the miners' workplaces with a view of the sea and cliffs; the museum of local history is in a former workshop in front of the port.

Cala Domestica

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Deep cove and sheltered about 2 km south of the village, with wide beach from white to amber, dunes with Mediterranean maquis and white limestone cliffs; low bottom, loved by snorkelers. Until 1940 the minerals were on board: there remain warehouses and tunnels, one of which leads to the hidden cove La Caletta. On the promontory on the left a Spanish tower of the 18th century about 10 m high, reachable by a path. Parking, motorhome, dining and camping.

  • Where SP 83, Buggerru
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Spiagge e dune di San Nicolò

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Next to the port the beach of Buggerru, of soft sand; further north, behind a pine forest, the dunes of San Nicolò with the beaches of Is Compingius and Is Compingieddus, also suitable for mountain bike excursions, up to the stretch of coast of Capo Pecora and Portixeddu.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Borgo minerario e sculture di Pinuccio Sciola

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village, with its fan-shaped houses on the sea outlet of the valley of Mount Caitas around the parish church of San Giovanni Battista, preserves the remains of wells, machinery, villages and palaces of the direction; in the village the sculptures of Pinuccio Sciola dedicated to the fallen miners.

  • Where Centro abitato, Buggerru
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
