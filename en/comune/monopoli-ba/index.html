What to see in Monopoli

Seaside town south of Bari with the port that enters the ancient center: the baroque cathedral of Santa Maria della Madia with the Byzantine icon come from the sea, the castle on the promontory of Punta Penna, the late Baroque Palace Palmieri and, on the coast, coves and the castle-abbey of Santo Stefano.

9 places to visit in Monopoli, in the province of Bari (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale e Basilica di Santa Maria della Madia

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Founded at the beginning of the 12th century and rebuilt in the 18th century, it is among the best examples of Baroque Baroque of the Land of Bari: monumental façade, paintings by Palma il Giovane and Francesco De Mura and the Byzantine icon of Our Lady of the Madia (about 1280), which legend wants to come from the sea on beams then used for the roof. Open every day 8-12 and 16-20.30.

  • Where Largo Cattedrale, Monopoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello di Santo Stefano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the rocky spur of the ancient Turris Paola, built in 1086 by Goffredo di Conversano: Benedictine monastery, then hospital of the Giovanniti and feud of the Knights of Malta until the early twentieth century; walls, moat, mast, a Romanesque church and a rock sanctuary. Today it is privately owned (family de Bellis): visits to check.

  • Where Contrada Santo Stefano, Monopoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
