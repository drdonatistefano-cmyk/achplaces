What to see in Bisegna

Borgo was born between the XI and XII century with the castle, on a rocky spur at the foot of the Great Mountain, at the beginning of the Valley of the Giovenco where the homonymous river, in the National Park of Abruzzo, Lazio and Molise: medieval center with pentagonal tower, church of Santa Maria Assunta, a baroque building and the village of San Sebastiano dei Marsi.

Centro medievale, torre pentagonale e torre dell'orologio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval centre, at 1,210 metres, contains a pentagonal tower of the 13th-XIV centuries, an ancient bell tower and the clock tower, with heraldic coats of arms of the Counts of Celano, the Piccolomini. The village formed between the 11th and 12th centuries with the phenomenon of the castle, on a rocky spur at the foot of the Great Mountain. The old town is also the stop of maple in the trails "Men and Trees" tell of the Park.

  • Where Centro storico, Bisegna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: parcoabruzzo.it · abruzzoturismo.it

Palazzetto gentilizio barocco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Baroque Lombard style building: the façade has an entrance portal, a balcony with wrought iron balustrade and, on the second floor, two windows decorated with shell motifs.

  • Where Centro storico, Bisegna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: abruzzoturismo.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Recently restored parish church, with elements of seventeenth-century and eighteenth-century character; the feast of Saint Mary Assunta is celebrated on 15 August.

Monumento e fonte di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Small complex composed of a small church, a cave and a fountain. At the source of St. John's locality, tradition attributes virtues against skin diseases; on the morning of June 24th, the feast of St. John, there takes place the ritual of comparative, in which one appears, and the girls looking for a husband drink the water in the night between 23rd and 24th June.

  • Where Località San Giovanni, Bisegna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: abruzzoturismo.it · parcoabruzzo.it

San Sebastiano dei Marsi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Frazione about 3 km from Bisegna, with ancient houses gathered around the church of San Sebastiano; in the area there are also the churches of the Madonna of Loreto and San Sebastiano to the river.

  • Where San Sebastiano dei Marsi, Bisegna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: parcoabruzzo.it · abruzzoturismo.it

Centro visita del Parco «Uomini e alberi raccontano»

Museums · Themed · Free entry

The Center visit to the north entrance of the Park, completely renovated and managed since 2008 by the association Montagna Grande onlus, illustrates the project "Men and Trees" , which links four species to guided paths in the Valley of Giovenco: the beech at the Valle della Fossa, the maple at the historic center, the apple to the orchards of the valley of the Prata, the pine to the nature path. You book excursions and rent mountain bikes and snowshoes. Opening hours from the Park card: in winter on Sundays 10-13 and 15-18; in summer, holidays and prefectures 10-13 and 15-19; every day from 20 July to 31 August and from 24 December to 6 January.

  • Where SP 17, Bisegna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: parcoabruzzo.it · abruzzoturismo.it

Sentieri della Valle del Giovenco

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Many itineraries start from Bisegna: an easy route of about 1 km to Fonte Cerasa (about 30 minutes), a route of about 10 km to Macchia di Rose-Terraegna (about 4 hours) and a challenging one of about 20 km to Pescasseroli, in the heart of the Park, in over 5 and a half hours.

  • Where Partenza dal paese, Bisegna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: abruzzoturismo.it · parcoabruzzo.it
