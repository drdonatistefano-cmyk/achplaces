What to see in Colle San Magno

Borgo della Ciociaria founded in the 11th century by the inhabitants of Castrum Coeli on Mount Asprano and named after St. Magnus of Anagni; behind the front of Cassino was largely destroyed in the Second World War, memory told by the Museo Vivo della Memoria, and preserves the medieval Civic Tower and the church of San Magno.

Museo Vivo della Memoria

Museums · City history

Five rooms in a historic building tell the Second World War from the point of view of civilians, crushed between Germans and allies during the battles of Cassino, with video testimonies, objects donated by families, documents, war finds and aerial photos of the RAF on the area of Mount Cairo granted by the National Aerophototheque. According to the MiC card is open Friday 15-18, Saturday 10-18 and Sunday 10-13, with reservation for schools and groups; the same card indicates both a ticket of 2 euros (free for residents) and a free offer of 10-13, while VisitLazio gives it to visit only by reservation.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Chiesa di San Magno

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A thousand-year-old parish church in the historic centre, dedicated to the bishop and martyr St. Magnus, patron saint of the country, celebrated on August 19.

  • Where Centro storico, Colle San Magno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Centro storico e Torre Civica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Civic Tower, built between 1200 and 1300, was restored after the 1984 earthquake; around there are arches of the fourteenth century, baronial palaces and sixteenth century buildings. In Piazza Umberto I, an ancient meeting place, there is the artistic fountain in neo-imperial style of 1928.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Colle San Magno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta in Cielo sul monte Asprano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

14th century church on Mount Asprano, where the castle of Castrum Coeli stood abandoned by the inhabitants who founded the village. On Monday in Pasquetta you will encounter the processions of Colle San Magno and Castrocielo for the ... Kiss of the Madonnas, with two identical statues of the Virgin who exchange a kiss.

  • Where Monte Asprano, Colle San Magno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com
