What to see in Frontino

Small village of Montefeltro inside the Park of Sasso Simone and Simoncello, with roads, walls and towers built with stones of the river Mutino, works of Franco Assetto, the mill of Ponte Vecchio and the monastery of Montefiorentino with the Cappella Oliva.

9 places to visit in Frontino, in the province of Pesaro e Urbino (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Convento di Montefiorentino e Cappella Oliva

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Franciscan monastery founded in 1248, one of the largest in the Marche region. The Chapel of Counts Oliva, with inscription of 1484 and probably designed by Simone Ferrucci, houses the tombs of Gianfrancesco Oliva and Marsibilia Trinci by Francesco di Simone Ferrucci, the altarpiece with Madonna and Child and saints by Giovanni Santi, father of Raffaello (1489), and the kneelings inlaid by Zocchini (1493).

  • Where Località Montefiorentino, Frontino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Museo Franco Assetto

Museums · Modern art

In a palace in the historical center renovated in the eighties, it collects the works that the Turinese artist Franco Assetto (1911-1993) donated to the Municipality, in chronological order: from surrealism to pop art to water sculptures; among the works Il Divino Bambino (1952) and Sculpture d'acqua (1971).

  • Where Corso Giovanni XXIII 16, Frontino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Mulino di Ponte Vecchio e Museo del Pane

Museums · Themed

A fourteenth-century water mill on the edge of the village, with a guard and defence tower, which supplied the castle with flour and bread; documented as early as 1658, today it houses the Bread Museum.

  • Where Ponte Vecchio, Frontino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Centro storico, torri e mura

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The stones of the Mutino stream pave the streets and form walls and towers; next to the town hall stands the Civic Tower. From the village you can see the mountains of the Moon, Nero, Catria, Witch and San Vicino.

  • Where Centro storico, Frontino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · parcosimone.it

Fontana di Franco Assetto

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Water sculpture of the Turin artist Franco Assetto, made with the stones of the Mutino stream; another work of his was acquired at the end of the fifties by the Museum of Modern Art in New York.

  • Where Centro storico, Frontino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Monastero di San Girolamo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Built in 1500 and surrounded by secular oaks, it includes a restored church and convent, now a period residence; in the church a altarpiece by Bernardino da Longiano and in the refectory a fresco of the Last Supper.

Parco del Sasso Simone e Simoncello

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The interregional park extends around Frontino, dominated by Monte Carpegna with numerous panoramic views; there is the fauna park of Pian dei Prati, which can be visited during the summer months.

  • Where Frontino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
