What to see in Diamante

Borgo della Riviera dei Cedri, known as the city of murals and chilli: more than 150 mural works in the historic centre, born in 1981, the Museo del peperoncino in the Doge's Palace and, off the coast, the island of Cirella with the ruins of the ancient Cirella on the mainland.

Murales del centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The murals were born in 1981 from an idea of the painter Nani Razzetti, who proposed to the mayor Evasio Pascale the "Operation Murales": 83 Italian and foreign painters painted the walls of the seaside village, and between 1986 and 1997 they added others, until they made Diamante the most painted city in Italy, also called the "country of noses to the top." Italia.it has over 150 works and defines it as the largest open-air museum in Italy.

  • Where Centro storico, Diamante
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Mosaico della Chiesa Madre

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

On the outer wall of the Mother Church, in Via Alfieri, the mosaic of the spouses Michele and Angiolina Sposito of Ferrara tells the history of Calabria from the graffito of the "Toro di Papasidero" to the society of farmers and fishermen; in October 1999 there was added a panel with the "Immaculate."

  • Where Via Alfieri, Diamante
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Museo del peperoncino

Museums · Themed

In the historic Palazzo Ducale, managed by the Accademia Italiana del Peperoncino: salt on tools and objects ('Fantasia of every part') on the geographical origins of the plant, on seeds, spices and sauces, and a video room with posters and satirical cartoons; among the works a lithography by Renato Guttuso and an image from a recipe book of the sixteenth century.

MiC card (updated 2020) indicates the closed museum every day

  • Where Via Duomo, Diamante
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Isola di Cirella

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

The second island of Calabria by extension, in the Regional Marine Park of the Cedars: limestone outcrop covered with Mediterranean scrub, with caves, coves and cliffs, seabeds with Posidonia oceanica and Pinna nobilis. At the top of the remains of the sighting tower, dated to the ninth century. You can catch up by boat.

  • Where Isola di Cirella, Diamante
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Ruderi di Cirella

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Viewpoint

Archaeological area identified with the ancient coastal settlement of Cirellae, probably founded by the Greeks and grown as a commercial center thanks to the port; remain the Carafa Castle, the Mother Church and the Convent of St. Francis of Paola. Not far away is the modern Teatro dei Ruderi, home of summer festivals.

  • Where Cirella, Diamante
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Lungomare e spiagge

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Beaches with coves and volcanic cliffs, awarded with Blue Flag, and a promenade to walk, with the historic center of squares, churches and shops.

  • Where Lungomare, Diamante
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
