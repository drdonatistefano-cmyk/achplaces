What to see in Feltre

A walled town on the Colle delle Capre, in the Bellunesi Dolomites National Park, Feltre was rebuilt after the invasion of 1509 of the troops of Massimiliano I of Habsburg: in Piazza Maggiore, dominated by the towers of the Castello di Alboino, the Teatro de la Sena where Goldoni debuted; around buildings with frescoed facades, the Rizzarda Gallery and the Diocesan Museum, under the churchyard of the Duomo the archaeological area of Feltria Romana and, just outside, the Sanctuary of the Saints Vittore and Corona and the reserve of the Vincheto di Celarda.

Area archeologica di Piazza Duomo

History and architecture · Archaeology

Under the cathedral churchyard, more than 900 m2 of remains from the Roman period (II-IV century A.D.) to the Middle Ages (XI-XII century), excavated from 1970 and opened to the public in 1995: shops along a paved road and a large building seat of professional associations (dendrophors, blacksmiths, centonary), documented by two bases, then a building of the VIII-IX century perhaps linked to the ancient bishop's complex and a circular baptistery of the XI-XII century with apse of horseshoe. Opened on 18 July 2025 with guided tours by the association Il Fondaco per Feltre; disabled access not guaranteed.

Centro storico e Piazza Maggiore

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center, rebuilt after the invasion of 1509, rises on the Colle delle Capre within walls enlarged at the end of the fifteenth century and partly accessible with the Way of the Sentinel, with three doors: the monumental Porta Imperiale, Porta Oria and Porta Pusterla. Piazza Maggiore, reachable also by elevator, is dominated by the Castle of Alboino and surrounded by Lombard fountains, the church of Santi Rocco and Sebastiano, the Palazzo della Ragione and the Gazzi palaces, Bovio-Da Romagno and Guarnieri; many noble palaces have frescoed facades.

  • Where Piazza Maggiore, Feltre
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: dolomitipark.it · infodolomiti.it · italia.it

Torri del Castello di Alboino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

The fortress that dominates the historic center dates back to the Longobard domination. The Torre dell'Orologio, the ancient tower-gate of the castle, has a Gothic coat of arms of 1324 and exterior decorations of Lorenzo Luzzo (1518) and houses the "Window of Time" multimedia installation on the evolution of the square since 1510; the Torre del Campanon, about 34 meters high, has the first stages of late-Doromanic construction. The towers, recently restored, are open seasonally and included in the Totem Card Plus (April 1-30 September).

  • Where Salita Nicolò Ramponi, Feltre
  • Entry €5
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitfeltre.info

Teatro de la Sena

History and architecture · Historic theatres · Paid entry

On the first floor of the sixteenth-century Palazzo della Ragione, from the Palladian porticos on Piazza Maggiore: the grand hall of the Major Council, completed in 1611, became the Social Theatre in 1684; here Carlo Goldoni, in Feltre in 1729-1730, discovered his passion for theatre. After lightning in 1769 and the long closing, in 1802 was redesigned by Giannantonio Selva, the architect of the Phoenix of Venice, with 24 stages; the decorations and curtain, the only surviving painting by Tranquillo Orsi, were restored. That's the little Phoenix. Open Friday, Saturday, Sunday and holidays 10-13 and 15-18 (October-March) or 15-19 (April-September); closed December 25 and January 1.

  • Where Piazzetta delle Biade 5, Feltre
  • Entry €5
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitfeltre.info · infodolomiti.it · italia.it

Galleria d'arte moderna Carlo Rizzarda

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Born at the will of the blacksmith Carlo Rizzarda and opened in 1938, it preserves over 400 wrought iron works made between 1910 and 1930, as well as ceramics, glass (also by Carlo Scarpa), painting and sculpture of the nineteenth century; in December 2018 it received as a gift the collection of glasses Nasci-Franzoia (738 pieces).

  • Where Via Paradiso 8, Feltre
  • Entry €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitfeltre.info · italia.it

Museo Diocesano Belluno-Feltre

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the ancient bishop's palace, born as a fortress and residence of the bishops from the second half of the thirteenth century, restored in 2018: sacred art from the early Middle Ages to the contemporary of the dioceses of Feltre and Belluno, about 250 works including the cup of the deacon Bear (VI century), considered the oldest Eucharistic chalice of the West, a post-Byzantine cross of 1542, a Madonna in alabaster of the fifteenth century, the reliquary bust of San Silvestro by the Florentine goldsmith Antonio di Salvi and works of Tintoretto, Sebastiano Ricci, Luca Giordano, Andrea Brustolon, Gaspare Diziani and Domenico Corvi. Open from Friday to Sunday (June-September 9.30-13 and 14.30-19; October-May also holidays, 9-13 and 14-18).

  • Where Via Paradiso 19, Feltre
  • Entry €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitfeltre.info · italia.it

Museo Civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

In the sixteenth century Palazzo Villabruna, opened in 1928 with the setting up of 1954 by Francesco Valcanover, preserves the mantle of Charles IV and paintings by Gentile Bellini, Quentin Metsys, Vittore Belliniano, Cima da Conegliano and Lorenzo Luzzo; it is in transformation into an archaeological civic museum.

Antiche prigioni di Palazzo Pretorio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

In the basement floors of the palace, now the headquarters of the Municipal Archives, there are still cell doors, numbers and graffiti of prison use until 1965; they can be visited seasonally, with evening visits to small groups; not accessible to disabled people.

Santuario dei Santi Vittore e Corona

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Dedicated to the patrons of the city, it is located in a panoramic position a few kilometers from the center; built in the 11th century by the will of the Crusader Giovanni da Vidor and then expanded, it is a Romanesque complex with Byzantine influences and preserves frescoes of the grotesque school.

  • Where Feltre
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: dolomitipark.it · infodolomiti.it · italia.it

Riserva Naturale Vincheto di Celarda

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Protected oasis of international importance on the right bank of the Piave, established in 1971, owned by the State property and managed by the Carabinieri Biodiversity of Belluno: 92 hectares of wetlands, riparian forests, meadows and shrubs with 45 species of nesting birds, eight of amphibians, hares, wild boars, deer and deer, and rare orchids in the meadows. Open 8-18.

  • Where Viale del Vincheto, Celarda, Feltre
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
