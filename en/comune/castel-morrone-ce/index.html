What to see in Castel Morrone

On the hills north-east of Caserta, Castel Morrone preserves the ruins of the Norman castle on the top of the mountain, the polygonal belt of Monte Castellone-Gagliola and the seventeenth-century Sanctuary of Monte Castello, next to the monument to Pilade Bronzetti and the garibaldini fallen in October 1860.

Santuario di Monte Castello (Santa Maria della Misericordia)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Founded in the 17th century, it is a small tufa building with a hut façade between the Tuscan parasts and the square bell tower with a dome. The interior with a single nave has stuccoes and frescoes of the eighteenth century, including a Visitation, elliptical apse with three niches and eighteenth-century high altar; in the chapels a Madonna enthroned with Saints Chiara and Francis of the late seventeenth century, an Adoration of the shepherds of the early seventeenth century and a Transfiguration fresco.

Monumento a Pilade Bronzetti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Behind the Sanctuary of Monte Castello, a narrow passage leads to the stone pyramid dedicated to the Garibaldi hero Pilade Bronzetti, who is also tomb of the Garibaldini fallen in the battle of October 1860.

  • Where Presso il Santuario di Monte Castello, Via Provinciale Finestre 89, Castel Morrone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · cultura.gov.it

Ruderi del Castello normanno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

The feudal castle stands on the top of the mountain around which the village developed; enlarged in Norman times, probably for the Count of Caserta Roberto di Lauro, had square plan, fortified walls and towers and was made of stone and tuff; it was severely damaged by the earthquake of 1456. The walls, the main square tower and some smaller buildings remain.

  • Where Monte Castello, Castel Morrone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: beniabbandonati.cultura.gov.it · sabapce-bn.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it

Cinta fortificata di Monte Castellone-Gagliola

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

On the sides of the mountain, at 330-340 m, a circuit of polygonal walls encloses about 150 hectares in the upper part of the relief; the walls are made of large limestone blocks arranged without regular order within an artificial cut in the rock.

  • Where Monte Castellone-Gagliola, Castel Morrone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: beniabbandonati.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it

Casa museo laboratorio della civiltà rurale

Museums · Themed

The museum recovers, preserves and enhances the testimonies of the rural life of Campania, with attention to the Morronese territory, with the aim of an ethnographic museum in part outdoors and for didactic purposes; the research study center and demonstrates with tools of traditional vintage techniques, including food, and promotes the regional craftsmanship of wood, ceramics, iron, goldsmiths, furniture and fabrics.
