What to see in Morrovalle

A hilly village on the Chienti from the medieval old town, with Piazza del Comune between Palazzetto del Podestà, Palazzo Lazzarini and Torre Civica, the International Museum of the Nativity scene with about 800 nativity scenes and the former Franciscan monastery linked to the Eucharistic miracle of 1560.

Museo internazionale del presepio

Museums · Themed

Set up in six rooms in the basement of a former Augustinian monastery of the eighteenth century, it preserves about 800 nativity scenes from all over the world and about 150 paintings: among others a French copper nativity scene of the nineteenth century, a Palestinian nativity scene in mother of pearl, ebony nativity scenes from Kenya and one engraved on a coral cameo of Torre del Greco. The last room tells the History of Salvation with biblical dioramas.

  • Where Via Bonarelli, Morrovalle
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo civico pinacoteca Palazzo Lazzarini

Museums · Old masters

In the thirteenth century Palazzo Lazzarini, with its precious stone portal, the museum collects mainly paintings on table and canvas from the sixteenth to the eighteenth century coming from the local churches, including the Madonna del Soccorso attributed to the Tolentinate Marchisiano di Giorgio, a series of portraits of illustrious men from the 16th century collection of Paolo Giovio and objects of the Frumentary Mount founded in 1475. Recently, an archaeological section was added with findings from 700-600 B.C.

Piazza del Comune, Palazzetto del Podestà e Torre civica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The main square brings together the medieval Palazzetto del Podestà, of the fourteenth century, renovated after a fire of 1496 and 1854, Palazzo Lazzarini, the Civic Tower and the nineteenth-century Palazzo Comunale, built on an area purchased by the community in the fourteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Morrovalle
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Ex Convento francescano e Auditorium Borgo Marconi

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Outside Porta Alvaro, the convent arose after 1290; in April 1560 a fire destroyed the church but the consecrated host was found intact and Pius IV recognized the miracle, granting the indulgence of the "Perdoni" (17-27 April). Rebuilt by the Municipality in 1564, the Latin cross church preserves the old bell tower and is now auditorium; in the cloister there is the bust of Luigi Canale of the sculptor Cantalamessa.

  • Where Via Guglielmo Marconi 13, Morrovalle
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Porta Alvaro e Porta delle Fonti

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Porta Alvaro, of the fourteenth century and already mentioned in the statues of 1570, is the main entrance to the historic center; the Porta delle Fonti or San Bernardino is the second historical access, between stone stairs, walls and houses on the hill.

  • Entry Free
