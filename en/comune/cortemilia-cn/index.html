What to see in Cortemilia

Centre of Alta Langa at the confluence of the Uzzone in Bormida, of Roman origin and capital of the hazelnut Tonda Gentile delle Langhe: in the village of San Michele the square porticoed with the fifteenth-century tower that houses the Ecomuseo dei Terrazzementi e della Vite; in the village of San Pantaleo the parish and the Romanesque Madonna della Pieve.

4 places to visit in Cortemilia, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre-casaforte ed Ecomuseo dei Terrazzamenti e della Vite

Museums · Themed

The 15th-century tower-house dominates Piazza Oscar Molinari; once the seat of the magistrate's courthouse, it now houses the Ecomuseum of Terraces and Lives.

  • Where Piazza Oscar Molinari, Cortemilia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Borgo San Michele: piazza Molinari e via Dante

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The heart of the village of San Michele is Piazza Oscar Molinari, in front of the hill crowned by the ruins of the medieval castle; from here start via Dante Alighieri, flanked by porticoes that partly preserve the original wooden ceilings. Centre of medieval origins, capital of the Tonda Tonda Gentile delle Langhe, seat of the National Fair of hazelnuts.

  • Where Piazza Oscar Molinari, Cortemilia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di San Pantaleone

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the village of San Pantaleo, reachable from Via San Francesco: rebuilt in 1579 and enlarged in the seventeenth century, with cusp bell tower. A little further you will find Piazza Savona, the heart of the village.

  • Where Via San Francesco, borgo San Pantaleo, Cortemilia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa della Madonna della Pieve

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the 11th century, partly rebuilt in the 13th century, reachable from via della Pieve in the village of San Pantaleo.

  • Where Via della Pieve, Cortemilia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
