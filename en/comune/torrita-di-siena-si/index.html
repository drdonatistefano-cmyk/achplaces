What to see in Torrita di Siena

Ancient cornerstone of the Republic of Siena in Valdichiana, closed by walls with four doors; the Orange Flag is for the walled hamlet of Montefollonico.

5 places to visit in Torrita di Siena, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa delle Sante Flora e Lucilla

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque church in the historic centre with the marble bezel of the Blood of Christ, attributed to Donatello and dated to the first half of the fifteenth century.

  • Where Centro storico, Torrita di Siena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Mura e porte di Torrita

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The walls with square towers rebuilt in 1528, which close the old town with the four gates Gavina, Pago, Sole and Nuova; the battle of Torrita is painted by Lippo Vanni in the Sala del Mappamondo in Siena.

  • Where Torrita di Siena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Montefollonico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The hamlet is still closed by the historical walls, with its characteristic houses: the hamlet awarded with the Orange Flag of the Touring Club.

  • Where Montefollonico, Torrita di Siena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Sentiero del Vin Santo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The path between the cultivated countryside that from Torrita leads to Montefollonico, telling the traditions and legends of the holy wine.

  • Where Da Torrita di Siena a Montefollonico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Tempio della Madonna delle Nevi e chiese di Torrita

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The temple of 1525, Santa Croce with the Baroque interior, the Blessed Annunciation and the Madonna dell'Ulivo, perhaps the oldest church in the country, with a terracotta Madonna considered miraculous.

  • Where Torrita di Siena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
