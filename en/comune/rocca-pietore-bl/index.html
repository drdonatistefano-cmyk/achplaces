What to see in Rocca Pietore

Ladin municipality at the foot of the Marmolada, in the UNESCO World Heritage Dolomites: in Sottoguda the fifteenth-century church, the tabies and wrought iron shops, then the gorge of the Serrai towards Malga Ciapela, from which the cable car climbs to Punta Rocca and the Museum of the Great War; in Laste the Gothic Santa Maria Maddalena.

11 places to visit in Rocca Pietore, in the province of Belluno (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Serrai di Sottoguda

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Natural park formed by a gorge of 2 km excavated by the stream Pettorina, with walls over 150 meters and 13 bridges, walkable in about an hour from Sottoguda towards Malga Ciapela. Entrance only on foot and with closed number (maximum 400 people), with helmet included in the ticket; last entrance at 17.

  • Where Località Sottoguda, Rocca Pietore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.serraidisottoguda.it

Sources: serraidisottoguda.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Sottoguda e i tabièi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village is one of the most beautiful villages in Italy, with the tabies, wooden barns of the Ladin culture used for hay and tools; the church of Santi Fabiano, Sebastiano and Rocco is the only building that survived the fire in 1881.

  • Where Sottoguda, Rocca Pietore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa dei Santi Fabiano, Sebastiano e Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The oldest building in Sottoguda, consecrated in 1486, with bell tower of 1550 and altar of 1616; only to be saved from the fire of 1881.

  • Where Sottoguda, Rocca Pietore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Funivia della Marmolada a Punta Rocca

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

From Malga Ciapela the cable car climbs to Punta Rocca, 3265 meters, with panoramic terrace; in winter from here descends La Bellunese, a 12-km track to Malga Ciapela in the Dolomiti Superski.

  • Where Malga Ciapela, Rocca Pietore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Val Ombretta

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valley where a totem marks the geographical center of the UNESCO World Heritage Dolomites; the path passes through the mountain hut of the same name and continues to the Falier Refuge (2074 m).

  • Where Val Ombretta, Rocca Pietore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Lèch dei Giai e Val Franzedàz

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Lèch dei Giai is a natural lake at 2205 meters at the foot of the Auta peak, reachable from the Franzei Valley with CAI path and easy forest road; another CAI path leads to the panoramic plateau of Val Franzedàz, about 1980 meters, with barns and houses.

  • Where Val Franzei, Rocca Pietore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
