What to see in Sona

On the morainic hills south-east of Garda, along the ancient road between the lake and Verona, the hamlet of Palazzolo preserves the Romanesque Pieve di Santa Giustina, built around the Mille and reopened after a long restoration, and the fifteenth-century church of Santi Quirico and Giulitta with the Crucifixion attributed to the school of Liberale da Verona.

Pieve di Santa Giustina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises where until the thirteenth century there was the original nucleus of the town, today inside the cemetery of Palazzolo; according to tradition on this hill San Zeno, bishop of Verona in the fourth century, celebrated Mass for the population. Built probably around the Mille in Romanesque style, with Moorish fishbone stones, and damaged by the earthquake of 1117, it has only one nave and two apses, one of which housed the ancient baptismal font; inside frescoes partly dated around the Mille. Reopened after more than two years of restoration.

normally not accessible; open with guided tours for the Spring FAI Days (21-22 March 2026)

Chiesa dei Santi Quirico e Giulitta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small church of fifteenth-century origins with a single nave, with rustic façade, sailing bell tower and a tiny rosette; during the plague of 1630 the Dominicans used it as a lazzaretto and from the windows of the facade the food was passed to the plagued ones. The main work is the Crucifixion on the background wall, attributed to the school of Liberale da Verona and rediscovered at the beginning of the nineteenth century; on the left wall a fresco with Saints Peter, Mary Magdalene, Antonio Abate, Quirico and Giovanni Battista, and under the roof a fragment with lamb and palm perhaps coming from the Lombard church of Santa Giustina.
