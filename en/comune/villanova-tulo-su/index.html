What to see in Villanova Tulo

Country of about a thousand inhabitants of Sarcidano, on the right bank of the Flumendosa and overlooking the lake: the nuragic complex of Adoni on a relief near the town and a center with arch of Roman times, the Gothic-Aragonese parish of San Giuliano and the murals of Pinuccio Sciola.

Nuraghe Adoni

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Complex on a relief near the village, with central tower, multi-tower bastion, a mighty wall and the huts of the village, with stairs, niches and curtains that show more construction phases. The excavations have returned about 40 bronze objects (axes, javelin tips, spear tips, laminae), recent and final Bronze ceramics (1350-1150 BC) and a fragment of oinochoe bronze beak of the Etruscan type of the end of the VI century BC, without comparison in Sardinia; a storeroom of vases on the scale of a tower attests the reuse in the VI century AD.

Centro storico, arco romano e parrocchiale di San Giuliano

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village was born in the middle of the 14th century from the union of the villages of Villanova and Tulo and preserves an arch of Roman era. Among the traditional rural houses are the murals of Pinuccio Sciola inspired by the work of the poet Benvenuto Lobina (1914-93), also remembered by a bronze statue of Piergiorgio Gometz; the parish church of San Giuliano was rebuilt in 1663 in Gothic-Aragonese forms. From the center you can see the lake and the valley of the Flumendosa.
