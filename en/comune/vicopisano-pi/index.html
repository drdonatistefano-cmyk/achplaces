What to see in Vicopisano

Medieval village between the Arno and Monte Pisano, fortified by Filippo Brunelleschi.

3 places to visit in Vicopisano, in the province of Pisa (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Vicopisano in the guide with map and filters

Rocca del Brunelleschi e Palazzo Pretorio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The fortress designed by Brunelleschi after the Florentine siege of 1406, with drawbridges, battlements and walkways; in the Palazzo Pretorio the finds of the excavations of Verruca.

  • Where Vicopisano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Torre dell'Orologio e le tredici torri

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Thirteen towers remain in the center, the most important tower of the 12th century clock, today bell tower.

  • Where Vicopisano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Torre degli Upezzinghi a Caprona

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The tower on a rocky promontory in Caprona, remembered by Dante in the Comedy.

  • Where Caprona, Vicopisano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
