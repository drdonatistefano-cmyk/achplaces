What to see in Sorso

Romangia town between olive groves and vineyards, overlooking the Gulf of Asinara with about 18 km of beaches between Marina di Sorso and Platamona; in the medieval center the Baronial Palace with the museum Biddas, the fountain of the Billellera and the church of San Pantaleo, in the countryside the sites of Santa Filitica and Serra Niedda.

Biddas - Museo dei villaggi abbandonati della Sardegna

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

On the second floor of the Palazzo Baronale, it is presented as the first museum in Italy dedicated to depopulation and the abandonment of the inhabited centers, from the Middle Ages to the contemporary age. A room is dedicated to Geridu, a medieval village abandoned and unique in Sardinia excavated in extension, with reconstructions and interactive activities resulting from over twenty years of research; you can also book the guided tour to the site of Geridu. Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday 15:30-20; Wednesday, Friday and Sunday 9-13; Monday closed.

  • Where Via Convento, Sorso
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Centro storico, Palazzo baronale e Fontana della Billellera

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The historical centre, called Bicocca, has medieval layout and tuff houses; the seventeenth century Baronial Palace is now a cultural centre. The fountain of the Billellera, dating back to the 17th century, takes up the model of the fountain of Rosello in Sassari: according to tradition those who drink its waters go crazy.

  • Where Centro storico, Sorso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Santa Filitica

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the 5th century BC, the Carthaginians built a fortress, then occupied by the Romans; there remain a Roman villa with mosaics and a spa complex and a palaeo-Christian cult building.

Marina di Sorso e Platamona

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The beach is about 7 km long and with Platamona, the beach of the sassaresi, forms a 15 km wide stretch from 10 to 30 meters; dunes sheltered by pines, junipers and dwarf palms, emerald green water and sandy seabed that slopes rapidly, sunsets with views of the Asinara. Suitable for kite and windsurf all year round; pinewood behind, rent and refreshment. Further east are the beaches of Pulchili, near the mouth of Silis, with shallow waters, Maritza and La Tonnara, pebbles.

  • Where SP 60 / SS 200, Marina di Sorso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
