What to see in Borgomanero

Città del Novarese was born in the 12th century as a Franco village, which took the first name of Borgo San Leonardo from the oratory where the pilgrims of the Via Francisca stayed; to see the Oratory of San Leonardo, Villa Marazza with the park and library of the Foundation, and in the hamlet Santa Cristina the museum of peasant civilization "How we were." In September, Festival of Grapes and Palio degli Asini.

Oratorio di San Leonardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was the church of the village of San Leonardo, one of the first French villages in Novara, from which the city took its first name; the pilgrims of the Via Francisca stayed there. A unique nave with semicircular apse, a hut facade and walls in square stones and river pebbles; the interior frescoes, perhaps of Romanesque inspiration, have been recently restored.

Visitable only on request (tel. 0322 81897)

Villa Marazza e biblioteca della Fondazione

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

H-shaped villa in the forms of the mid-nineteenth century, with sections of an eighteenth-century farmhouse, wrought iron balconies, rooms with decorated wooden ceilings, marble fireplaces and precious furniture, in a vast park with centuries-old oaks and chestnut trees. The lawyer and politician Achille Marazza left it to the Municipality; it houses the Achille Marazza Foundation, whose library preserves books, prints, parchments, incunabula, five hundred and ancient medals, with a section of local history from Piedmont-Lombardy from the sixteenth century to today.

  • Where Viale Marazza 5, Borgomanero
  • Entry To be checked

  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismonovara.it · italia.it

Museo della civiltà agricola «Come eravamo» (La Manera)

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Established in 1992 in the former Palazzo Bono in 1605, it tells with photographs and tools the peasant life and the ancient trades of the fields: reconstructions of kitchen, bedroom and classroom of the late nineteenth century, a small watermill, the 1:40 scale model of Cascina Colombera and documents from the sixteenth to the twentieth century; from the museum you can see the Monte Rosa.

  • Where Piazzale della Chiesa 1, frazione Santa Cristina, Borgomanero
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismonovara.it · italia.it
