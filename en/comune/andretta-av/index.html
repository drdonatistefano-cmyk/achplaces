What to see in Andretta

High Irpinia country, 850 metres above the Upper Ofanto valley, fortified in the middle of the 6th century according to a Byzantine scheme, with the Baroque mother church of the Assumption, the museum of peasant civilization and the sanctuary of the Madonna Stella del Mattino alla Mattinella.

4 places to visit in Andretta, in the province of Avellino (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa madre di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Born in the 16th century with a single nave on a terrace overlooking the village, damaged by earthquakes, it was rebuilt in Baroque style and enlarged with two naves thanks to Father Agostino Arace (1718-1764). It has four altars, the largest in polychrome marble with a wooden statue of the Virgin, seven chapels and a wooden choir of the eighteenth century; the bell tower was probably a feudal defensive tower. Open in function times.

  • Where Via Padre Agostino Arace, Andretta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page sistemairpinia.provincia.avellino.it

Sources: sistemairpinia.provincia.avellino.it

Santuario della Vergine Stella del Mattino (Mattinella)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Documented from the second half of the 15th century as a grange of the abbey of San Lorenzo in Tufara, it was expanded in the 19th century, rendered unusable by the earthquake of 1980, reopened in 2001 and erected diocesan sanctuary in 2018. In neoclassical style, it preserves an 18th-century high altar with the painting of the Virgin on the throne with the Child of the 15th century and nineteenth-century canvases. On the last Saturday of May there are the communities of Andretta and Vallata on the Festa della Mattinella. Open at the time of the functions.

  • Where Frazione Mattinella 50A, Andretta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page sistemairpinia.provincia.avellino.it

Sources: sistemairpinia.provincia.avellino.it

Museo della civiltà contadina e artigiana

Museums · Themed

About a thousand artifacts on ancient crafts and peasant life: the first room exhibits archaeological finds of the territory, the others reconstruct domestic and working environments (plows and harrows, vinification, killing of the pig, corners of the blacksmith, carpenter, shoemaker, chaplain and knitwear).

The MiC card indicates the museum closed every day: check the opening with the Municipality (tel. 0827 32009).

  • Where Via Libertà, Andretta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page sistemairpinia.provincia.avellino.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sistemairpinia.provincia.avellino.it

Monte Airola

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hill next to the village with pine forest, picnic area, a cross and religious statues, from which opens a wide panorama of the valley.

  • Where Monte Airola, Andretta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page sistemairpinia.provincia.avellino.it
