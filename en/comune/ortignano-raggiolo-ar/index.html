Borgo di Raggiolo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Chiesa di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Made in the Palazzo dei Conti Guidi, with Gothic portal and two works of the fifteenth century: a Madonna from Donatello's workshop and a Redeemer from Andrea Ferrucci's workshop.
