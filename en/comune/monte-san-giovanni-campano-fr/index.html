What to see in Monte San Giovanni Campano

Borgo Ciociano on a rocky hill, surrounded by walls with thirty-six turrets and three surviving gates, dominated by the castle where St. Thomas Aquinas was held prisoner in 1244-45; the name comes from a Benedictine monastery of the sixth century and the village is on the Via Francigena del Sud and on the Way of St. Benedict.

Castello e Piazzale di Corte

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortified nucleus of the village with Renaissance palace, two towers (one pentagonal) and the cell in which the brothers held St. Thomas Aquinas in 1244-45, then transformed into a chapel; the Doge's Palace had five floors, the last three demolished after the earthquakes of 1703 and 1915. The Piazzale di Corte with the male tower, which overlooks the Palazzo del Bargello, is the highest point in the centre, with views from the Ernic Mountains to the Marsicans, the Aurunci and the Lepini.

  • Where Via Corte, Monte San Giovanni Campano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Collegiata di Santa Maria della Valle

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The main church of the village, destroyed by the artillery of Charles VIII in 1495 and rebuilt in the 16th century with a Latin cross on a design of the Bramantesque school; it has a bronze portal by Tommaso Gismondi and houses the wooden statue of the Madonna del Suffragio, about two meters high, of Flemish school, donated by Pope Urban VIII in 1632. On the same square Marconi stands the Town Hall with the council room.

  • Where Piazza Marconi, Monte San Giovanni Campano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Mura, torrette e porte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town stands on the slope of a rocky hill; on the only side not protected by rocks runs a belt with thirty-six turrets, partly incorporated in the houses or covered by vegetation. Of the original five doors remain Codarda, Rendola and Scrima.

  • Where Centro storico, Monte San Giovanni Campano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Convento dei Cappuccini

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

On a hill about two kilometers from the village, built according to the source in 1260, restored at the behest of Vittoria Colonna and donated in 1531 by Antonella dei Conti d'Aquino to the Friars Minor Capuchins; to see the cloister and the church of Saints John the Baptist and Evangelist. The library preserves incunabula donated by the literary and musician Giovan Thomaso Cimello.

  • Where Circa 2 km dal borgo, Monte San Giovanni Campano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
