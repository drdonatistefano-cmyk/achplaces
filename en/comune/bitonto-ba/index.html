What to see in Bitonto

An oil town at the gates of Bari: the Romanesque cathedral with Nicolaus' ambo and excavations under the floor, the National Gallery of Puglia in the Renaissance Palazzo Sylos Calò, the Angevin Tower on Piazza Cavour and the archaeological museum of the De Palo-Ungaro Foundation.

4 places to visit in Bitonto, in the province of Bari (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Concattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Masterpiece of the Romanesque Apulian built between 1150 and 1200 about on the foundations of a early Christian basilica and of one of the IX-XI centuries: tripartite facade with carved portals, three naves, transept with three apses and crypt. Inside the marble ambo signed by Nicolaus (1229); the excavations of 1991-1999 under the floor brought to light Roman remains, burials and the oldest basilicas. Domina piazza Cathedral.

  • Where Piazza Cattedrale, Bitonto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.beweb.chiesacattolica.it

Sources: beweb.chiesacattolica.it · comune.bitonto.ba.it

Galleria Nazionale della Puglia «Girolamo e Rosaria Devanna»

Museums · Modern art

354 works among paintings, drawings and sketches from the sixteenth to the twentieth century, in five sections mostly chronological, including the ...Nude feminine by Man Ray (1912) and the dynamic rhythms curvilinear snapping by Giacomo Balla. Set up in the Renaissance Palazzo Sylos Calò, restored by Giovanni Alfonso Sylos until 1584, with the loggia on the piazza dismantled in 1929 and rebuilt in 1983.

  • Where Palazzo Sylos Calò, Via Rogadeo 14, Bitonto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page museipuglia.cultura.gov.it

Sources: museipuglia.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it

Museums · Archaeological

Museum in the historic center with the finds of the peucet and Roman necropolis of the territory.

Torrione angioino

History and architecture · Towers

Medieval tower of Angevin age overlooking Piazza Cavour, reopened in July 2023 after a conservative restoration financed by the Metropolitan City: the interior and exterior spaces host exhibitions and events, and the ring terrace on the square has returned public space.

  • Where Piazza Cavour, Bitonto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.bitonto.ba.it
