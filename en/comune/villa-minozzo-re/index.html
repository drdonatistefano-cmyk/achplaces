What to see in Villa Minozzo

The Apennines are located at the foot of the ridge of Monte Cusna, in the heart of the National Park of the Tuscan-Emilian Apennines and the UNESCO MAB Biosphere Reserve. It preserves the tradition of dramatic May in the museum inside the old fortress, the medieval fortress of Minozzo, the springs of Poiano and the mountain towns of Civago and Febbio.

9 places to visit in Villa Minozzo, in the province of Reggio Emilia (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo del Maggio drammatico

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Set up in the recovered remains of the old fortress of Villa Minozzo, it preserves texts, costumes, props, drawings, photos and objects of May, the popular open-air theatre of the Reggiano and Modenese Apennines that stage cavalry and mythical feats. It is managed by local Maggistic companies: timetables and visits must be arranged by telephone.

  • Where Piazza della Pace 6, Villa Minozzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: comune.villa-minozzo.re.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sala dell'affresco

Museums · Old masters

In a room of the municipal library there is a 16th century fresco of the Madonna di Loreto.

  • Where Biblioteca comunale, Villa Minozzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: comune.villa-minozzo.re.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Minozzo, rocca e pieve di Santa Maria Assunta

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Ancient seat of parish church, from 1070 it had a fortress of the bishop of Reggio, of which they remain ruined on an oliolithic outskirts next to the road; the parish church of Santa Maria Assunta, built in 1600, preserves a "Noli me tangere" of the Tiarini.

  • Where Minozzo, Villa Minozzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Monteorsaro e Cervarolo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Two hamlets that preserve ancient architectures, with buildings dating back to the fifteenth century, the seventeenth and the nineteenth centuries.

  • Where Monteorsaro e Cervarolo, Villa Minozzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitemilia.com

Fonti di Poiano

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Under the village of Poiano flowing salt and sulphurous springs, outlet of an underground canal, with a flow rate of 300-600 liters per second: the water of the river Lucola infiltrates underground and melts chalks and salt.

  • Where Poiano, Villa Minozzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Monte Cusna

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

With 2120 meters it is the highest peak of the Reggiano Apennines and the second of the northern Apennines, with wide panoramic prairies on top; from the ridge, which shows some glacial circus, the view extends over Val d'Asta.

  • Where Crinale del Cusna, Villa Minozzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Abetina Reale

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Forest complex of 828 hectares in the National Park of the Tuscan-Emilian Apennines and Site of Community Interest, which preserves wrecked populations of conifers of colder climates; very frequented for excursions, with Mount Cusna as the main destination.

  • Where Abetina Reale, Villa Minozzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Febbio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Last village of the Val d'Asta, 1053 meters at the foot of Mount Cusna: in winter skiing facilities, in summer base for hiking, mountain biking and horse riding; the chairlift Febbio 2000 works in both seasons.

  • Where Febbio, Villa Minozzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitemilia.com

Civago

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry

Country at 1011 meters in a basin surrounded by woods, in the high valley of the Dolo: holiday resort from the fifties, is the last center before the forests and prairies of high altitude and starting point for excursions to the peaks of the ridge.

  • Where Civago, Villa Minozzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
