What to see in San Vito di Leguzzano

San Vito di Leguzzano preserves in the Court Priory-Gandin the Ethnographic Museum on woodworking, dedicated to carpentry and the workshop of bottai, crafts certified in the village since the early fifteenth century; the parish church of Santi Vito, Modesto and Crescenzia, rebuilt in the eighteenth century on a project by Giovanni Miazzi, has a classical facade of 1858.

Museo etnografico sulla lavorazione del legno

Museums · Themed

In the Court Priory-Gandin documents the ancient craftsmanship of the village, in particular the carpentry and the workshop of the bottai, certified in San Vito since the first fifteenth century. There are over 2,700 items from the life of the past: tableware, linen and household utensils, tools for breeding and agriculture, for masons and shoemakers, tools for weaving and spinning; a library keeps documents and photographs on the local crafts and the wine cellar the objects for the production of wine.

Chiesa dei Santi Vito, Modesto e Crescenzia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church was rebuilt in the second half of the eighteenth century on the project of the architect Giovanni Miazzi from Bassano and remained without facade until 1857. The front, designed by the Vicenza engineer Luigi Dalla Vecchia thanks to a legacy of Dr. Bortolo Barbieri and completed in 1858 (as the inscription above the portal recalls), has giant Corinthian pilgrims on high stands and a eardrum with tooth frame, cherubs and festoons; From the Old Age he also extended the church about six meters long and built the stairway. The three statues of the saints held on the pediment were placed in 1912-1913.
