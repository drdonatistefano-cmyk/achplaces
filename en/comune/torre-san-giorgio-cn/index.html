What to see in Torre San Giorgio

Country of the plain at the foot of Monviso, between Po and Varaita, already in the marquisate of Saluzzo and then of the Savoy, known as the cradle of the mixed fried: to see the Baroque parish of San Giorgio (1710), the fourteenth-century chapel of San Rocco, the Pinacoteca civica with 47 works by Carlo Sismonda and the WWF oasis of Bosc d'la Perzon.

5 places to visit in Torre San Giorgio, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pinacoteca civica «Carlo Sismonda»

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

In the Palazzo Comunale, a permanent exhibition of 47 works, mainly of religious theme, by the painter Carlo Sismonda, whose style is inspired by the great artists between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, from impressionists to fauves. Freely accessible from Monday to Friday, 8:30-12:30 (better check opening before).

  • Where Via Maestra Adolfo Sarti 27, Torre San Giorgio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church in Piedmontese Baroque style of 1710, with a bell tower 25 meters high that dominates the roofs of the village.

  • Where Centro di Torre San Giorgio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Cappella di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the center of the village, built in 1300 with the offers of the population and decorated in the eighteenth century with shovels of the Turinese painter Rebaudengo; today it houses a library.

  • Where Centro di Torre San Giorgio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Bosc d'la Perzon (Bosco della Prigione)

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

WWF oasis of 15,000 m2 on the border with the territory of Saluzzo, rich in trees and vegetation and frequented by numerous species of birds.

  • Where Al confine con Saluzzo, Torre San Giorgio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Parco dello Sciu Sal

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Green area just outside the center, for walks and venue of events.

  • Where Appena fuori dal centro, Torre San Giorgio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
