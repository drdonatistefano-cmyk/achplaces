What to see in Montefiore dell'Aso

Piceno village among the most beautiful villages in Italy, with intact historical center closed by walls and six towers of the XV-XVI century; in the ancient Franciscan convent the museum polo of San Francesco houses the polyptych of Carlo Crivelli and the works of Adolfo De Carolis.

Polo museale di San Francesco

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Opened in October 2006 in the Franciscan monastery of the 13th century. The Sala Carlo Crivelli exhibits the surviving parts (central register and cusps) of the polyptych painted shortly after 1470 for the church of San Francesco; the Museo Adolfo De Carolis collects about five hundred works of the painter born here (1874-1928), including the oil sketches for the frescoes of the Palazzo del Podestà of Bologna and the xilographies for the Fioretti. The itinerary is completed by Giancarlo Basili's Scenographic Documentation Centre, the Museum of Rural Civilization and the collection of engravings by Domenico Cantatore. It adheres to the Marche Museums Charter.

  • Where Piazzale San Francesco 1, Montefiore dell'Aso
  • Entry €3,50, reduced €2; cumulative Museums Piceni €10
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · letsmarche.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1247 and 1303, it preserves the Romanesque-Gothic style outside; the portal of 1303 is now in sacristy, while the restorations between the mid-sixteenth and mid-eighteenth centuries gave the Baroque look inside.

  • Where Piazzale San Francesco, Montefiore dell'Aso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Piazza della Repubblica e Collegiata di Santa Lucia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The square is the heart of the village and is dominated by the Collegiate Church of Santa Lucia, of ancient origins and rebuilt in neoclassical style; around the eighteenth-century buildings Egidi, Farsinelli, De Scrilli, Rossi, Ciarrocchi and Simonetti.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica, Montefiore dell'Aso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Mura, torri e Belvedere De Carolis

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historic centre, perfectly preserved, maintains important stretches of walls with doors and six towers of the 15th and 16th centuries; you enter it from the Belvedere De Carolis, from which you can see many buildings of the 17th and 18th centuries.

  • Where Montefiore dell'Aso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it
