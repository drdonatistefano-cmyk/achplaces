What to see in Montaldeo

Borgo dell'Ovadese mentioned as "Mons Alpherius" in the 10th century, disputed between Alexandria and Genoa and since 1566 feud of the Doria: the castle of the Doria, the Romanesque church of San Michele with frescoes of the late fifteenth century and the oratories of the village.

3 places to visit in Montaldeo, in the province of Alessandria (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Montaldeo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built in the 13th century, destroyed and rebuilt in the 15th century and adapted to residence in the 17th century; since 1566 it is Doria, and the current owner, the Marquis Clemente Doria, lives there in summer. Almost cubic building on two floors plus the path of the round, on a fortified basement with remains of towers; on the first floor the hall of the coats of arms with fireplace of the late sixteenth century and the courtroom where the minor justice was administered, in the cellars the prisons. The legend of the Spectre of the Nun, Sister Constance Gentile, is linked to it.

private residence, open from May to October for the occasion of Castles Open

  • Where Via Ambrogio Doria 4, Montaldeo

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Chiesa di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the 12th century, it was the first parish of Montaldeo; it has a unique nave and contains frescoes dating back to the end of the 15th century. Shortly before, for those arriving from Mornese, we meet the chapel of San Gottardo.

  • Where Montaldeo (strada per Mornese)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: alexala.it

Parrocchiale di San Martino, oratori e Santa Caterina

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the village the parish church of San Martino, enlarged between the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, the Oratory of the Battuti and the Oratory of San Sebastiano; near the cemetery the church of Santa Caterina, built in 1653, to thank the saint of protection during an enemy attack.

  • Where Centro storico, Montaldeo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: alexala.it
