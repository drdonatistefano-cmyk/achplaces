What to see in Rabbi

The municipality coincides with the Val di Rabbi, which is about twelve kilometers long on the side of the Val di Sole, for more than half in the Stelvio National Park: the spas of Rabbi Fonti with the ferruginous water of the Antica Fonte, the Saént Falls, the bridge suspended over the Ragaiolo, water mills and sawmills and wooden and stone farms.

Terme di Rabbi

Local life and traditions · Spas

In Rabbi Fonti, about 1,200 m in the Stelvio Park, around the Antica Fonte that flows at about 9 °C: carbonate-alcalino-ferruginous carbon water known in Europe already in the seventeenth century for digestion, circulation and anemia; in the nineteenth century the plant expanded and attracted the Habsburg nobility. Today hydropinic treatments, baths, inhalations, mud, Kneipp route in thermal water, sauna and alpine sauna outdoors, with itineraries of the "Terme e Natura" and forest bathing. Open in summer (2027: from May 28th to September 19th), treatments by reservation.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.termedirabbi.it

Centro visitatori del Parco nazionale dello Stelvio (Punto Info Rabbi)

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Information point of the Park in Rabbi Fonti, at the thermal baths: material and publications of the Park and an operator who advises programs and excursions to be done alone or with the experts of the Park, such as the guided exits at dawn in autumn for the greed of the deer and the snowshoeing among the farms. From here you can also organize visits to the working water Venetian sawmill and the restored Somrabbi cheese factory, with the old tools.

  • Where Località Rabbi Fonti, Rabbi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.parcostelviotrentino.it

Sources: parcostelviotrentino.it · visittrentino.info

Ponte sospeso sulla cascata del Ragaiolo

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Tibetan bridge opened in summer 2016 on the Ragaiolo river: 100 meters of walkway about 60 meters high (1.366 m high) above the waterfalls, crossed in every season, even with snowshoes. About 30 minutes walk from the car park of Plan (Rabbi Fonti), passing through the Venetian sawmill of the Bègoi, or from the parking of the thermal baths; you can continue to the Fratte or Stablasolo hut and the Saént waterfalls. Not recommended for those suffering from dizziness; dogs should be carried in their arms.

  • Where Plan, Rabbi Fonti, Rabbi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitvaldisole.it · visittrentino.info

Cascate del Saènt

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the Val di Saént, a side valley towards the pass of the same name, the ring tour of medium difficulty leads to see two waterfalls and is also suitable for children; the waterfalls are among the most famous destinations of the valley and can be reached also from the suspended bridge passing through the area of the Coler and Stablasolo mountain hut.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Cascate Valorz e percorso Kneipp

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

In Valorz you will find the first open-air Kneipp trail in Trentino; from here a ring path passes through the traditional farms and reaches the Valorz waterfalls.

Mulino Ruatti

Museums · Themed

In Pracorno, at the entrance of the valley: one of the few water mills still working, with the millstones and the family house of the nineteenth century, today museum of mountain crafts of the nineteenth century. Guided tours by the Mill Ruatti Association, seasonal openings and sometimes by reservation.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Segherie veneziane dei Braghje e dei Bègoi

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Water sawmills that document the passage from the force of two or three men to the hydraulic force, with only one sufficient worker; the working sawmill is visited with guides organized by the Park. The Bègoi road is on the forest road from Plan to the Ragaiolo bridge.

Masi della Val di Rabbi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Traditional buildings in wood and stone, once home and family farm, scattered throughout the valley and symbol of its history; walking and winter trails with snowshoes connect them to the huts, even with guided excursions of the Park. Small hamlets like Valorz and Pedergnana preserve the traditional system.
