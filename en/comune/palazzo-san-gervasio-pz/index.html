What to see in Palazzo San Gervasio

The hills between Vulture and Murge, which grew up around the castle that Frederick II used as a hunting lodge; along Corso Manfredi, the Palazzo d'Errico houses the Pinacoteca with almost three hundred paintings collected by the patron Camillo d'Errico.

5 places to visit in Palazzo San Gervasio, in the province of Potenza (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pinacoteca «Camillo d'Errico»

Museums · Old masters

In the Palazzo d'Errico, built between 1829 and 1833 by Agostino d'Errico, father of the patron Camillo, who defined the current Corso Manfredi. The collection of Camillo d'Errico has 298 paintings with works attributed to the Neapolitan school (Salvator Rosa, Luca Giordano, Micco Spadaro), Roman (Guido Reni, Carlo Dolci, Maratta) and Flemish (Van Dyck, Brueghel, Snyders); in the palace also the library of about 6000 volumes.

  • Where Corso Manfredi, Palazzo San Gervasio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.palazzo.pz.it

Castello normanno-svevo (Palazzo Marchesale)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Hunting residence of Frederick II, who chose the area as a reserve and raised horses there; it has two square towers, four mullioned windows and a central trifora similar to a loggia. From the mid-th century the fief passed to families like the Ruffos and the Caracciolos.

  • Where Centro storico, Palazzo San Gervasio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: catalogo.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Centro storico e palazzi gentilizi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The hills have grown around the castle of the federiciano; there are the Mancinelli palaces, Lancellotti (with the family chapel), Pizzuti and Errico, and the fountains of the historic center.

  • Where Corso Manfredi, Palazzo San Gervasio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: catalogo.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it

Bosco comunale e Laghetto Santa Giulia

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Municipal forest of about 750 hectares with an artificial pond where in summer sports fishing is practiced, a camping area with about 60 pitches and a network of paths for walking and cycling.

  • Where Palazzo San Gervasio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it
