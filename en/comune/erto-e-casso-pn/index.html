What to see in Erto e Casso

Two rosé stone villages on the opposite sides of the landslide of Mount Toc, in the Friulan Dolomites Park, marked by the Vajont catastrophe of October 9, 1963: you can visit the crowning of the dam, the Visitor Centre with the exhibitions on tragedy, the Vajont Ecomuseum and trails like that of coal and the Val Zemola.

7 places to visit in Erto e Casso, in the province of Pordenone (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro visite «La catastrofe del Vajont»

Museums · City history

In the former primary schools of Erto, where the first bodies were collected and recognized in the days after the tragedy, it is one of the most comprehensive documentation centers on Vajont. On the ground floor there is the photographic exhibition "Vajont: images and memories" , the installation "Battito d'ali per la memoria" dedicated to dead children that night and a workshop with 3D models of the valley; on the first floor "A space of memory" in four sections (First, Impact, Emergency, Justice) and the sensory room "9.10.1963 L'Wave" which reconstructs the approximately 240 seconds of the catastrophe. In 2026 it opened 11-18 on weekends, every day from 24 July to 6 September and 9 October.

Diga del Vajont e visita al coronamento

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The arched dam, 261 meters high according to Italia.it, was built by Sade since 1957; on the evening of October 9, 1963 a landslide of about 270 million cubic meters from Mount Toc fell into the basin and the wave destroyed Longarone and part of Erto and Casso, with almost 2,000 victims. Today it is a memorial: the Park organizes a guided tour of 50 minutes on the crown (groups of maximum 40, meeting at the square of the memorial church, tickets on VivaTicket or at the information point) and visits of 2-3 hours to the places of memory. Path not suitable for those suffering from vertigo.

Crowning visits suspended from November 2 for the winter break (Park Calendar 2026)

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.parcodolomitifriulane.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Borghi di Erto e Casso

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Two distinct communities that form a single municipality, on opposite sides of the landslide of Mount Toc, with rosé local stone houses and spontaneous architecture; Erto is part of the Authentic Villages of Italy. In Erto for centuries, on Good Friday, it represents the Passion for a vow made after a terrible plague epidemic.

  • Where Erto e Casso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.parcodolomitifriulane.it

Ecomuseo Vajont «Continuità di vita»

Museums · Themed

Ecomuseum dedicated to wood: a section for children with toys, cradles and shoe soles, a path on street vendors, sculptures of the symposium, an exhibition on the ancient trade of the coalmaker and an audio path with sensory headphones on the voice of the trees, with Mauro Corona.

  • Entry To be checked

Palestra di roccia della diga del Vajont

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Born in 1978, it is one of the most famous crags in the world, with about 300 routes of every grade up to 8c; the easiest routes are numbered from 1 to 18 and close there is a very simple route for children.

  • Where Diga del Vajont, Erto e Casso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.parcodolomitifriulane.it

Sources: parcodolomitifriulane.it

Sentiero del carbone e Val Zemola

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The path connects the entrance of the Val Zemola, to Erto, with the village of Casso, along the path where once the vegetable coal of the valley woods was carried on shoulder; it crosses the village of San Martino and tells local legends like that of the castle of Queen Claudia. From Erto also start the spectacular itineraries of the Val Zemola.

  • Where Val Zemola, Erto e Casso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.parcodolomitifriulane.it

Sources: parcodolomitifriulane.it

Laste di San Daniele

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Castate di stroni di Rosso Ammonitico sul Monte Borgà, just below the summit of Monte Piave, collected in a space no larger than a football field; in the past these stones served to cover the roofs of the houses of Casso.

  • Where Monte Borgà, Erto e Casso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.parcodolomitifriulane.it
