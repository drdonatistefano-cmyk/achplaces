What to see in Gattatico

Municipality of the lower Reggiana to the north of Reggio Emilia, with capital in Praticello since 1870, born on land reclaimed in the high Middle Ages and made of inhabited scattered between villas and courtyards; it is known for the peasant house of the seven Cervi brothers, today Cervi Museum with the agri-environmental park.

Museo Cervi

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Established in 1970 and re-arranged in 2002 in the Reggiana peasant house where the Cervi family lived since 1934; the seven brothers were shot by the fascists on December 28, 1943 with Quarto Camurri, and during the war the house helped antifascists, renitents and opponents. The itinerary has three sections (rural work, antifascism and resistance, a family in memory), with agricultural tools, works of art, photographs and the multimedia room Quadrisfera on the twentieth century; on the whole also the Library Archivio Emilio Sereni, a bookshop and an inn.

  • Where Via Fratelli Cervi 9, Gattatico
  • Entry €5
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.istitutocervi.it

Sources: istitutocervi.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Parco agroambientale dell'Istituto Cervi

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

Next to the museum, an outdoor eco-museum that promotes agricultural practices to protect the historical agricultural landscape, such as the "Piantata Reggiana" (Piantata Reggiana), in 2011 there was inaugurated a row of ancient fruits of Emilia-Romagna.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · istitutocervi.it

Chiese di San Matteo a Praticello e di San Tommaso a Gattatico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In Praticello, the town hall, the church of San Matteo was built between 1690 and 1703. In the village of Gattatico the parish church, a modest church dedicated to St. Thomas of Canterbury, is of Romanesque origin and preserves some of its elements.

  • Where Praticello e Gattatico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it
