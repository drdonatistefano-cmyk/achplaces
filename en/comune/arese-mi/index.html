What to see in Arese

In the former Alfa Romeo factory, Arese houses the historic Museum of the brand, reopened in 2015; in the Valera area remain the eighteenth-century villa La Valera and the church of San Bernardino, the oldest sacred building in the country.

4 places to visit in Arese, in the province of Milan (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo storico Alfa Romeo «La macchina del tempo»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Born in 1976 on a collection started in the early 1960s, closed in 2011 and reopened on 30 June 2015 after less than a year of work, it occupies the office building designed in 1968 by Ignazio Gardella. The six-storey route, ordered for Timeline, Beauty and Speed, exhibits 69 models including the A.L.F.A. 24 HP, the 6C 1750 Gran Sport by Tazio Nuvolari, the Juliet and 33 TT 12, with sections on design, cinema, Formula 1 and cars in uniform; closes with virtual reality and a 4D room. Open 10-18, closed on Tuesdays; bookshop, cafeteria and test track.

  • Where Viale Alfa Romeo, Arese
  • Entry €12; over 65 €10; 6-18 years €5
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa di San Bernardino in Valera

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest sacred building in Arese, built in 1558 at the behest of the Lattuada family on a pre-existing perhaps Romanesque structure; the two side aisles were added at the beginning of the twentieth century. In the apse it preserves a baptism of Christ of the late sixteenth century, with references to Gaudenzio Ferrari, and a wooden statue of the Madonna del Rosario of the early eighteenth century.

Villa Settala Marietti «La Valera»

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

18th century residence with ancient rural village in three courts along the east-west axis, entrance garden, main garden and park; passed to Marietti in 1811, then to Tosi and Ricotti. In the garden a neo-Moresca Coffee House with pebble mosaics and, next to the Italian garden, the eighteenth-century lemon house with high wooden ceilings.

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Major altar in polychrome marble of 1913 (Rozzi and Spoluzzi), facade of 1927 by the architect Zanchetta, frescoes of the presbytery of Mario Grandi (1931) and dome decorated in 1938 with the Madonna del Carmelo; the organ Ermolli of 1887 is still used for concerts. Behind the green area Einaudi-Vismara.

  • Where Centro di Arese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
