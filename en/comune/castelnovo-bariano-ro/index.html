What to see in Castelnovo Bariano

Center of the High Polesine near the river Tartaro-Canal Bianco; in the hamlet of San Pietro Polesine, next to the eighteenth-century church of San Pietro with two twin bell towers, the Civic Archaeological Museum, which tells over three thousand years of history of the territory.

Museo Civico Archeologico di Castelnovo Bariano

Museums · Archaeological

Three rooms that cover over three thousand years of history of Polesine: the Bronze Age with ceramics and objects in bone and horn from the palafittic inhabited of Canàr, Canova and Marola along the Tartarus; the Roman era (II century BC-II century AD) with objects of daily use, ceramics and coins; the graffiti and glazed ceramics of the XIV-XVI centuries found along the White Canal, where stood the customs tower called "Croxeta" . Open every day by reservation, 9-12 and 15-19 (tel. 0425 850202).

San Pietro Polesine e la chiesa di San Pietro

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Quiet rural center of the upper Polesine, hamlet of Castelnovo Bariano near the river Tartaro-Canal Bianco. On the square there is the eighteenth-century church of San Pietro, with two twin bell towers; in the hamlet there is also the archaeological museum.

  • Where Piazza San Pietro, San Pietro Polesine, Castelnovo Bariano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page smppolesine.provincia.rovigo.it
