What to see in San Miniato

Medieval village on three hills between Pisa and Florence, stop of the Via Francigena and capital of the white truffle.

5 places to visit in San Miniato, in the province of Pisa (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers 1 of the 9 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Open San Miniato in the guide with map and filters

Rocca e Torre di Federico II

History and architecture · Towers · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The tower built by the emperor between 1217 and 1221 on top of the hill, symbol of the city.

  • Where San Miniato
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Cattedrale

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The Duomo of the thirteenth century, with the ceramic basins on the façade that reproduce the constellations of the Bear Major and Minor.

  • Where San Miniato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Santo Stefano e San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

12th century church with a wooden crucifix attributed to Pietro Cavallini, the Evangelists of Domenico Brogi and frescoes by Dilvo Lotti.

  • Where San Miniato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Convento di San Francesco

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

The convent that according to tradition the nobles dedicated to Saint Francis, guest of the nearby Badia di Santa Gonda.

  • Where San Miniato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Mercato dell'antiquariato e dell'artigianato

Local life and traditions · Historic markets · Free entry

Every first Sunday of the month in the historic center. In November the National White Truffle Market Exhibition.

  • Where Centro storico, San Miniato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
