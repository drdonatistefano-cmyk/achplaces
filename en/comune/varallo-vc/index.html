What to see in Varallo

The capital of Valsesia, the city of Gaudenzio Ferrari, dominated by the Sacred Mount of 1491, the oldest in northern Italy and UNESCO.

6 places to visit in Varallo, in the province of Vercelli (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 3 of the 5 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Sacro Monte di Varallo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The New Jerusalem is designed by the Franciscan Bernardino Caimi at the end of the fifteenth century and expanded until the seventeenth century: chapels with about 800 life-size statues, in the green and then like a city, with a style imprinted by Gaudenzio Ferrari; basilica of the Assumption. UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2003, in a nature reserve; you can also climb by cable car.

  • Where Località Sacro Monte, Varallo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Sources: visitvalsesiavercelli.it · italia.it

Santa Maria delle Grazie – Parete gaudenziana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Erected between 1487 and 1493 at the behest of Caimi, a national monument: on the partition wall the 21 scenes of Christ's life frescoed by Gaudenzio Ferrari in 1513, with the Crucifixion in the center.

  • Where Piazza Gaudenzio Ferrari, Varallo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Sources: visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Collegiata di San Gaudenzio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

On a rocky promontory reached by a staircase and surrounded by a loggia of 28 arches, with the Baroque interior and the polyptych of Gaudenzio Ferrari (Supposition of Saint Catherine, 1516-20).

  • Where Varallo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Sources: visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Palazzo dei Musei – Pinacoteca e Museo Calderini

Museums · Old masters

The nineteenth-century palace with the Pinacoteca, more than 300 works of Piedmontese and Valsesian painting, and the Museum of Natural History Pietro Calderini.

  • Where Via Pio Franzani 2, Varallo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Sources: visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Chiesa della Madonna di Loreto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Entirely frescoed by Gaudenzio Ferrari and his circle, with the cycle of the Life of the Virgin by Julius Cesare Luini (1542).

  • Where Varallo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Sources: visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Centro storico e piazza Gaudenzio Ferrari

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The square with the monument to the painter from 1874 and the house where he lived until 1528, the ancient districts, the bridge over the Mastallon and Palazzo Scarognini d'Adda with the Fisheries Museum.

  • Where Piazza Gaudenzio Ferrari, Varallo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Sources: visitvalsesiavercelli.it
