What to see in Capo di Ponte

Heart of rock art in the Camonica Valley, the first Italian UNESCO site (1979): the Naquane Park, the Cemmo Masses and the maps of Bedolina, the National Museum of Prehistory and two Romanesque masterpieces, the Pieve di San Siro and the monastery of San Salvatore.

8 places to visit in Capo di Ponte, in the province of Brescia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parco nazionale delle incisioni rupestri di Naquane

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The first archaeological park in Italy, in the middle of the first Italian UNESCO site (1979): on 30 hectares at the foot of the Pizzo Badile Camuno 140 rocks engraved from the V to the I millennium BC, from the Neolithic to the Iron Age. The Rock No. 1 ha hundreds of figures, n. 70 what the scholars consider the oldest depiction of the Celtic god Cernunnos. Five signposted routes: the shortest takes about an hour, four of them are needed for the complete visit. Closed on Monday; cumulative ticket with MUTRE and Cividate Camuno Museum (7 days) at 12 €.

  • Where Località Naquane, Capo di Ponte
  • Entry 6 €
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website museilombardia.cultura.gov.it

Sources: museilombardia.cultura.gov.it · italia.it · turismovallecamonica.it

Parco archeologico nazionale dei Massi di Cemmo

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

It takes its name from two large boulders decorated with figures, fallen at the beginning of the Holocaust: from the age of Copper around them grew a megalithic sanctuary. The park occupies about one hectare and is visited in about an hour. Free admission, closed on Mondays.

  • Where Località Cemmo, Capo di Ponte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website museilombardia.cultura.gov.it

Sources: museilombardia.cultura.gov.it · comune.capo-di-ponte.bs.it · turismovallecamonica.it

MUPRE – Museo nazionale della preistoria della Valle Camonica

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 2014 in the ancient Villa Agostani, in the historic center: seven sections on two floors with finds from shrines, burials, inhabited and mining and metallurgical activities that tell the life of the communities of Camune, to complete the rock carvings. It is also the starting point for visiting the archaeological sites of the area. Open from Thursday to Sunday; payment only with card.

  • Where Via San Martino 7, Capo di Ponte
  • Entry 6 €
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museilombardia.cultura.gov.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: museilombardia.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it · turismovallecamonica.it

Parco archeologico comunale di Seradina-Bedolina

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Established in 2005, it brings together the sites of Serabine I, II and III, the Horn of Serabine and Bedolina, two areas at about 100 meters of elevation gain connected by an ancient ridge path. The engravings are in much of the age of the final Bronze and Iron; on the plateau of Bedolina there are the topographic maps, with the famous Map of Bedolina. Admission fee; in summer open every day except on Thursdays, in winter Saturdays and Sundays; for Bedolina the keys are withdrawn at the ticket office.

  • Where Via Pieve di San Siro, Capo di Ponte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Sources: comune.capo-di-ponte.bs.it · italia.it · turismovallecamonica.it

Museo didattico d'arte e vita preistorica

Museums · Themed

Not a traditional museum but a laboratory of experimental archaeology: with an archaeologist, objects are handled and people's gestures of over 10,000 years of prehistory are re-created. Times on request.

  • Where Via Pieve San Siro 4, Capo di Ponte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Sources: isprambiente.gov.it · turismovallecamonica.it

Pieve di San Siro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Considered the first parish church of the valley, of the VI-VII centuries and rebuilt in the XII: it has no facade because it replaces the rock wall on the Oglio, and you enter from the side, by a portal carved with animal and vegetable motifs and the statues of a lion and a lamb. Three naves with apses protruding on the overhang, Lombard crypt with Roman materials of reuse and frescoes of the Tre-Quattrocento.

  • Where Via Pieve, Cemmo, Capo di Ponte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Sources: turismovallecamonica.it

Monastero di San Salvatore

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The only surviving building of a monastic complex from the 11th to 11th centuries, hidden among the trees at the slopes of the mountain: three naves, transept, dome on plumes and three apses; portal and capitals carved with raptors, hippogriffi, sirens and racemes of French taste.

  • Where Via XXV Aprile, Capo di Ponte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Sources: turismovallecamonica.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria ad Elisabetta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
