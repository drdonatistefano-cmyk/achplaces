What to see in Asola

The border town of Alto Mantovano, from 1440 to 1797 part of the Republic of Venice that transformed it into a fortress; around Piazza XX Settembre the Venetian Loggia of 1610, the fountain of Hercules and the Gothic cathedral with the works of Romanino, and in Palazzo Monte dei Pegni the Civic Museum Bellini and the Parish Museum Tosio.

Piazza XX Settembre, Loggia Veneta e fontana di Ercole

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The heart of the center, overlooking the Cathedral and the Municipal Palace with the Venetian Loggia erected in 1610 on a project by the architect Lantana. The fountain with Hercules that crushes the Hydra is a marble copy of the work of Antonio Carra and Cesare Vischietti (1593-1595); the original restored is on the staircase of the Town Hall. In the square also the Grand Caffè Liberty, and in the city numerous Liberty residences.

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre, Asola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it · in-lombardia.it

Palazzo Municipale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

C-shaped building with back courtyard and Venetian loggia of 1610; inside the Salone del Consiglio Maggiore and the Salone del Consiglio dei Ten, decorated with stuccoes and medallions of the Doges, and on the staircase the original fountain of Hercules and Hydra.

Cattedrale di Sant'Andrea Apostolo e Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Designed by Guglielmo Cremonese and built between 1472 and 1514 on a Gothic church of the fourteenth century, of which remains the presbytery; it is home to a nullius prelature. Pulpito and organ case are decorated by Romanino with scenes from the Old Testament and an Ecce Homo; the organ Serassi is from 1825. At the center of the presbytery is the Polyptych of Mercy inlaid polychrome wood, 27 tables by A. della Corna, gift of the Serenissima.

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre, Asola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it · in-lombardia.it

Museo civico Goffredo Bellini

Museums · City history

Founded in the early twenties of the twentieth century by the collector Goffredo Bellini in the Palazzo Monte dei Pegni. Archaeology from the Bronze Age to the Roman Age, with Celtic furnishings of the necropolis of Carzaghetto, Egyptian stele of the Middle Kingdom and Attic Kylix; incunabula and aldine, the Atlas Veneto of Coronelli (1690), sections on popular traditions, Risorgimento and Great War, painters of the nineteenth century and the life-size copy of the Assedio di Asola is attributed to Tintoretto (victory of 1516). Section of about 600 minerals visible on request.

  • Where Via Garibaldi 7, Asola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museobellini.it

Sources: museobellini.it · comune.asola.mn.it · turismo.mantova.it · in-lombardia.it

La Chiusa

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

In the green of the plain at the border with Acquanegra on the Churches, it takes its name from the system of taking water on the Churches started around the 11th century by the monks of the abbey of St. Thomas to irrigate the fields with the Seriola. The building dates back to 1428; for centuries it was disputed between the Venetian Asola and Acquanegra, until in 1911 the Water Court recognized Acquanegra the right to water.

  • Where Via Parma 82, Asola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
