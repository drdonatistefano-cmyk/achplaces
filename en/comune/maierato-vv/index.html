What to see in Maierato

Village of the province of Vibo Valentia in the Valley of the Messa, with stone houses, narrow alleys, panoramic views and the Church Mother; in its territory the ruins of the Norman city of Rocca Angitola.

Centro storico e Chiesa Madre

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Borgo della Valle del Messa made of characteristic streets, stone houses, narrow alleys and panoramic views; among the places of interest the Mother Church and other ancient churches.

  • Where Centro storico, Maierato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Sito archeologico di Rocca Angitola

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the relief of Rocca Angitola remain the ruins of the homonymous Norman city, destroyed by an earthquake and then abandoned; the site is among the cultural heritage of the Kalabria Coast to Coast Way, which rises there and from which you can enjoy a panoramic view of the Serre area.

  • Where Rocca Angitola, Maierato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: regione.calabria.it · italia.it
