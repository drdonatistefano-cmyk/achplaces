What to see in Ottone

An ancient pieve center of the high Trebbia Valley and castle of the Obertenga brand, then passed to Malaspina, Fieschi and Doria, for a long time home of a fair frequented by all the nearby valleys: the parish church of San Bartolomeo with the Romanesque bell tower, the baroque San Martiano, the oratory of San Rocco and the diocesan Museum of sacred art.

Museo diocesano di arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

In some halls of the Town Hall it collects furnishings and liturgical objects of the churches of the territory and private, from the fourteenth to the twentieth century; among the most interesting pieces the vestments with the coat of arms of the Doria and the bronze bell of San Bartolomeo fused in 1355.

Pieve di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Of very ancient origins, born as a monastic cell at the time of San Colombano, rises about 1.3 km north of the village near the cemetery, in a panoramic position over the valley; the Romanesque bell tower preserves a bell of 1365. Along the road, above the Trebbia, a mill with two overlapping wheels.

  • Where Presso il cimitero, Ottone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com
