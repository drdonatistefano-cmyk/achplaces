What to see in Canosa di Puglia

Daunia, Roman and early Christian city on the Ofanto: Hellenistic hypogeas and their furnishings in the National Archaeological Museum, the park of San Leucio with the temple of Minerva and the basilica, and the mausoleum of Boemondo next to the cathedral of San Sabino.

Museo archeologico nazionale

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the nineteenth century Palazzo Sinesi, tells of the society canosine between the sixth and third centuries BC with the furnishings of the archaic tombs and the Hellenistic hypogeas: subgeometric dauni vases, red figured apula ceramics, the polychrome vases \CanosiniÂ and the rooms of the Varrese hypogeum.

  • Where Via J. F. Kennedy 18, Canosa di Puglia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Parco archeologico e Antiquarium di San Leucio

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On a south-east height of the city, the italian temple of Minerva destroyed at the end of the 5th century AD and above it a central-planted early Christian basilica, the most imposing of its kind in Puglia, with polychrome mosaics such as the 'mosaico del pavone', the Antiquarium, opened in 2008, exhibits its findings.

  • Where Via Santa Lucia 100, Canosa di Puglia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Mausoleo del Principe Boemondo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Erected after 1111 for the Norman prince Boemond of Altavilla, on the model of the temple of the Holy Sepulchre of Jerusalem; one enters through a bronze gate of Ruggero of Melfi. It rises next to the cathedral of San Sabino.

  • Where Villa comunale, Canosa di Puglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · cultura.gov.it
