What to see in Sulmona

The town of Ovidio and the confetti in the Peligna Valley, at the foot of Maiella and Morrone: the complex of the Annunziata, Corso Ovidio and, just outside, the Morronese Badia founded by the future Celestine V.

13 places to visit in Sulmona, in the province of L'Aquila (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Complesso della Santissima Annunziata

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

The most representative monument of the city: an eighteenth-century church and a Gothic-Renaissance palace with a fifteenth-century statue of Ovidio, seat of the Civic Museum and the Costume Museum.

  • Where Corso Ovidio, Sulmona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: italia.it · abruzzoturismo.it

Abbazia di Santo Spirito al Morrone

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Founded by Peter of Angelerius, the future Pope Celestine V, and from 1293 Celestine's mother house; rebuilt after the earthquake of 1706, it was imprisoned from 1868 to 1993. You can visit the 18th century church on the Courtyard of the Platani, the stone staircase of the Maiella and the refectory painted in 1717-1719.

  • Where Via Badia, Sulmona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · abruzzoturismo.it

Santuario di Ercole Curino

History and architecture · Archaeology

Terraced shrine on the slopes of the Morrone, in use from the IV-III century BC to the middle of the II century AD: temple on high podium, sacellus with polychrome mosaic and a monumental staircase, overlooking the hair basin.

Public closed area for road access work

  • Where Località Badia, Sulmona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · abruzzoturismo.it
