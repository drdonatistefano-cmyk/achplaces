What to see in Loceri

Small hilly village of Ogliastra, about 10 km from the beaches of Tortolì and less than 20 km from Gennargentu, among cannonau vineyards and age-old olive groves: two museums built in old mills tell the story of peasant life, and many houses are decorated with murals.

6 places to visit in Loceri, in the province of Nuoro (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo etnografico Sa Domu 'e s'Olia

Museums · Themed

Set up in a 1910 mill in the center of the village, it reconstructs the domestic environments and the agricultural and festive life: tools for cheese, oil, bread and wine, care of cattle and weaving, a section on traditional men's and women's clothes for work and celebration and a corner with traditional games; the route ends in the bedrooms. First and second floor with architectural barriers.

  • Where Via Vittorio Emanuele, Loceri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Bachisio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

17th century church in popular baroque style; the saint is celebrated in May and October with a procession in which the faithful cross a river bringing the statue to the sound of the launeddas.

  • Where Loceri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
