What to see in Stilo

Borgo on the slopes of Monte Consolino, home of the philosopher Tommaso Campanella and in the X century main Byzantine center of southern Calabria. Its symbol is the Catholic, five-dome Byzantine church; in the stone village the 14th century Cathedral, the churches of St. Francis and St. John Theresti and, at the top, the remains of the Norman castle.

La Cattolica

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Small Byzantine church built between the end of the X and the beginning of the XI century by oriental monks on a spur of Mount Consolino; the name comes from the Greek "Katholikon" , the main church of a monastic complex. In exposed brick, Greek cross inscribed in a square with three apses and five cupolettes, has inside four bare columns, one with a gemstone cross and another with inscriptions in Arabic. The frescoes, discovered by Paolo Orsi in 1927, preserve the Dormitio Virginis, the Ascension and figures of saints; since 2006 the site has been nominated with other Basilian places of Calabrian Heritage of Humanity.

  • Where Via Cattolica, Stilo
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Centro storico e piazza Carnovale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient village rises on Mount Consolino among stone palaces, squares, steps and alleys of dark stone houses. In Piazza Carnovale the bronze monument to Tommaso Campanella (1923); in the village the Fontana dei Delfini, with sculptures of Arabic-Moorish taste.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Ferdinandea

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The works for a foundry of cannons began in the late eighteenth century and were completed in 1841 under Ferdinand II of Bourbon; the complex, of about 15,000 square meters, was active until 1865 as a steel-armor plant. After the Unity passed to private individuals and was purchased by Achille Fazzari, who also used it as a residence.
