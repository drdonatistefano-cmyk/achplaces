What to see in Trivento

Borgo perched on a hill of Molise, home to one of the oldest dioceses in the region: the Cathedral of Saints Nazarius, Celsus and Vittore, consecrated in 1076, houses the Romanesque crypt of San Casto. The historic centre is reached by climbing the steps of St. Nicholas, between Roman and medieval stones walled in the houses and crochet works of the Yarn Bombing.

Cattedrale dei Santi Nazario, Celso e Vittore

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Of Romanesque origins, it was consecrated in 1076 on an area considered sacred for centuries and rebuilt several times after earthquakes. The Renaissance façade dates back to 1905, with a portal in carved stone from the 13th century; the Baroque interior, rebuilt in the 18th century, measures 50 by 25 metres and has a major altar in polychrome marble of 1740.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Cripta di San Casto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The only remaining part of the Romanesque cathedral, under the presbytery: a 12th-century crypt, rediscovered by chance in 1928, with a cruising vault on sixteen stone columns and medieval and Roman capitals of reuse. It contains frescoes of Byzantine style, three wooden sculptures of the Two-Three Century, Roman epigraphs that evoke a temple of Diana and the funerary stone of Gnesio, priest of the goddess.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitmolise.eu · italia.it

Scalinata di San Nicola

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

About two hundred stone steps go up to the historic center; halfway through there is the small church of St. Nicholas, of the sixteenth century. In 2018 the longest crochet carpet in the world was displayed on the stairway.

  • Where Scalinata di San Nicola, Trivento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu

Centro storico e belvedere di Largo Porta Maggiore

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town, perched on the hill, is a network of alleys, squares and panoramic points; the houses have walled numerous blocks of Roman and medieval stone, and Roman tiles with inscriptions are marked by signs. From the viewpoint and from the balcony of Largo Porta Maggiore you can see, according to the side, Abruzzo or the Adriatic Sea; there is also an alley 34 cm wide. In the squares remain street art and crochet works of the Yarn Bombing.

  • Where Largo Porta Maggiore, Trivento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu
