What to see in Rondanina

The smallest municipality of Liguria, in the upper Val Trebbia halfway along the Bric di Rondanina: the ancient fief of the Fieschi and the stop of the trade of salt and wheat between the Po Valley and the sea, with the church of San Nicola, the center visit the wolf, the lake of Brugneto and the Path of Einstein in the Park of Antola.

4 places to visit in Rondanina, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di San Nicola di Bari

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church, next to the Town Hall, was built at the end of the seventeenth century on the remains of a Romanesque building of the thirteenth century and is considered the oldest in the Val Trebbia.

  • Where Centro, Rondanina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · lamialiguria.it

Centro Visita «Il Lupo in Liguria»

Museums · Themed

Museum of the wolf established by the Municipality and the Ente Parco dell'Antola on the ground floor of the former rectory: an interactive path on the wolf and its relationship with man today.

  • Where Ex canonica, Rondanina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it

Lago del Brugneto

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Large artificial basin under the peaks of Mount Antola and Mount Prelà, on which the village faces; from here starts a panoramic path along the banks, in the first flat stretch and almost at water level. During the periods of flood you can see trees with submerged roots.

  • Where Lago del Brugneto, Rondanina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: lamialiguria.it · italia.it

Sentiero di Einstein e Bric di Rondanina

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

In 1895 16-year-old Albert Einstein walked through Rondanina, Fascia, Montebruno and Propata: the route, about 48 km in the Parco dell'Antola, was restored from the Park with signs and panoramic points. From Rondanina, in an open position on the Cassingheno valley, climb in about two hours to the Bric di Rondanina (1,340 m).

  • Where Rondanina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: lamialiguria.it · italia.it
