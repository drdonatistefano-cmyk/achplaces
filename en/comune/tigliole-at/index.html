Chiesa romanica di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Romanesque church of the Monferrino group, with origins in the 11th-XII century: it served a castrum of the bishop of Pavia, had parish functions and cemetery, appears in the census of 1507, and remained isolated in the open countryside with the displacement of the country. Single rectangular nave with semicircular apse in brick and stone masonry, monolithic peducci, carved capitals and three monofores; under the floor an ossuary turned into a barrel. Restored since 1982, it hosts cultural events and exhibitions; for openings contact the Municipality.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Parrocchiale dei Santi Giovanni e Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Designed by Asti architect Filippo Malabayla and built since 1646; in the early seventeenth century he took the parish functions from San Lorenzo. It was painted by Paolo Gaidano in 1892, a painting of the Moncalvo school on the altar of the Rosary, a great crucifix of 1690 on the high altar and two paliotti of 1682-1683; the bell tower was raised in 1905 up to about 30 m.

Chiesa di San Carlo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A single nave church in the homonymous hamlet, with paintings and decorations by Musso (1912) revived by prof. Cold in 1977, side porch, large churchyard and bell tower of 20.50 m built by the foreman Eugenio Torchio.
