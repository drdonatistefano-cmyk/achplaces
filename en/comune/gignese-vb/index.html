What to see in Gignese

Country on the slopes of the Mottarone above Stresa, an elite holiday destination in the early twentieth century with a Liberty imprint, known for the Museum of the Umbrella and Parasole designed by Igino Ambrosini, the sixteenth-century temple of San Rocco and the church of the Madonna del Sasso on Erno.

3 places to visit in Gignese, in the province of Verbano-Cusio-Ossola (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo dell'Ombrello e del Parasole

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Designed in 1939 by the agronomist Igino Ambrosini (1883-1955), already among the creators of the Alpine Garden, it had its first seat in the school building; since 1976 it has been in the seat built on purpose, promoted by the Association Friends of the Museum under the patronage of the Municipality. It tells of a trade that the craftsmen of Gignese successfully brought around the world: over a thousand umbrellas, parasols and handles, including those belonging to Queen Margherita of Savoia and Giuseppe Mazzini, with photos of the first umbrellas, tools and a work bench. Seasonal opening from Tuesday to Sunday 10-12 and 15-18 (date 2024), in other periods for groups by reservation.

  • Where Viale Golf Panorama 4, Gignese
  • Entry €2.50, reduced €1.50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodellombrello.org

Sources: museodellombrello.org · comune.gignese.vb.it · illagomaggiore.com

Tempietto di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Temple of the XVI century to the east of the village, at the junction between the ancient road Stresa-Orta and the road to Nocco. It was dedicated to the Purification of the Virgin (Madonna of Bretta) and took the name of St. Rocco from a statue of the saint who worshiped there.

  • Where Via per Nocco, Gignese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.gignese.vb.it

Sources: comune.gignese.vb.it

Chiesetta della Madonna del Sasso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Inaugurated in 1939, it preserves the image of the Madonna del Rosario painted on a boulder which, according to tradition, grabbed a traveler during a flood of the river Erno.

  • Where Via delle Due Riviere, località Ponte sull'Erno, Gignese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.gignese.vb.it

Sources: comune.gignese.vb.it
