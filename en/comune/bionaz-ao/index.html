What to see in Bionaz

Villages scattered in the sun in high Valpelline under the Dent d'Hérens, with about twenty glaciers and the large dam of Place Moulin.

3 places to visit in Bionaz, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Diga e lago di Place Moulin

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built between 1961 and 1965, one of the largest in Europe, it collects 105 million cubic metres of glaciers water; it gets by car to the invaded.

  • Where Place Moulin, Bionaz
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Passeggiata lungo il lago di Place Moulin

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The road is closed to traffic, flat, an hour of walk between rocks, waterfalls, meadows and woods along the emerald green waters.

  • Where Bionaz
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it
