What to see in Courmayeur

The oldest Italian Alpine station at the foot of Mont Blanc, between Val Veny and Val Ferret, with the Skyway cable car to Punta Helbronner.

7 places to visit in Courmayeur, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Skyway Monte Bianco

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Cable car from Entrèves (1300 m) to the Pavillon du Mont Fréty (2173 m) and Punta Helbronner (3466 m), to the Mont Blanc glaciers.

  • Where Entrèves, Courmayeur
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Museo Alpino Duca degli Abruzzi

Museums · Themed

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Chiesa dei Santi Pantaleone e Valentino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The bell tower, perhaps from the 11th to the 11th century, has a very rare tiara cusp in Valle d'Aosta, perhaps in memory of the popes in Avignon.

  • Where Courmayeur
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Santuario di Notre-Dame de la Guérison

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint
