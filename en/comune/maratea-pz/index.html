What to see in Maratea

The only hamlet of Basilicata overlooking the Tyrrhenian Sea, the city of the 44 churches has a medieval center of alleys and stairs, the Christ Redeemer of Bruno Innocenti on Monte San Biagio and about 32 km of coast between beaches, coves and caves.

18 places to visit in Maratea, in the province of Potenza (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cristo Redentore e Monte San Biagio

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

22 meters high statue, with 19 meters of opening of the arms, in white concrete and scales of Carrara marble, work of the Florentine sculptor Bruno Innocenti, installed in 1965 on commission of the industrial Rivetti; it is reached on a hairpin road and dominates the village from the rocky top of the mountain.

  • Where Monte San Biagio, Maratea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it · italia.it

Basilica di San Biagio

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the foot of the statue of the Redeemer, on Mount San Biagio, it preserves the relics of the patron saint; from here starts the procession of the feast of San Biagio, four days that close on the second Sunday of May.

  • Where Monte San Biagio, Maratea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it · italia.it

Maratea antica sul Monte San Biagio

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The first settlement, the fortified citadel called "the castle" rose in the early Middle Ages, perhaps on a Roman settlement: Basilian monks founded a monastery around the sixth century, then Longobards and Normans fortified it. Walls and much of the town were destroyed after the French attack of 1806 and few ruins of fortified houses remain.

  • Where Monte San Biagio, Maratea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it · italia.it

Centro storico, Piazza Buraglia e Pietra del Sole

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval village, founded around the XI-XII century as Maratea Inferiore, is a network of alleys, stairs and flowering balconies; the oldest part, via Capocasale, dates back to the thirteenth-th century. Piazza Buraglia is the heart of the village, ...The Piazza della Piazza is the shopping street for the walk and from the viewpoint of the Stone of the Sun you can watch the sunset on the Gulf of Policastro. In front of the Annunziata stands the column of San Biagio (1758), in front of the Addolorata the obelisk of 1788 and in Piazza Vitolo the fountain with the bronze Siren of Alessandro Romano (1996).

  • Where Piazza Buraglia, Maratea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · basilicataturistica.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Palazzo De Lieto - Pinacoteca Angelo Brando

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

A noble palace built in 1734 by Giovanni De Lieto as the first hospital in Maratea, restored and today the Ministry of Culture. On the first floor the picture gallery dedicated to the Marateot painter Angelo Brando, the painter of femininity, in four themed rooms with self-portraits, portraits, domestic scenes, still lifes and sacred subjects; there is then the collection of finds, including Roman anchors, recovered from the backdrops of the islet of Santo Janni. Visit of about 60 minutes, free entrance.

seasonal openings; from January to June visits on request (tel. 0973 877676); building not accessible to persons with disabilities

  • Where Via Sotto l'Ospedale 8, Maratea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website pinacotecabrando.beniculturali.it

Sources: pinacotecabrando.beniculturali.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Vito

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest church in Maratea (XI-XII century), in the historic centre, is the tallest building in the village and contains frescoes from the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries.

Grotta delle Meraviglie

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Discovered in 1929 in Marina di Maratea, it has a room of 70 meters for 20 with stalactites and stalagmites and is considered one of the smallest and best preserved tourist caves in Italy.

  • Where Marina di Maratea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Cala Jannita - Spiaggia Nera

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

A long beach of black sand and pebbles in the locality of San Giuseppe, near Marina di Maratea, several times Blue Flag, with an equipped establishment; you go down a path in the vegetation and a staircase. Next to it you can find the Sciabella Cave, with an internal beach, and the Turtle Cave, which can be explored by canoe (rental on the beach).

  • Where Località San Giuseppe, Marina di Maratea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Spiaggia e area degli Illicini

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

To the north of Cala Jannita, a natural area with Mediterranean maquis (mirt, lentisco, pines and holm oaks) that descends to the sea; the sandy beach and pebbles look at the islet of Santo Janni and where the bottom is low forms a natural swimming pool between the rocks.

  • Where Marina di Maratea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · basilicataturistica.it

Spiagge di Fiumicello, Acquafredda e Castrocucco

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fiumicello goes from the headland of Ogliastro to the tip of Santavenere, with small caves and kayak; Fiumicello and Castrocucco are the largest and easiest to reach beaches, suitable for children. On the coast of Acquafredda there are Anginarra and Luppa, divided by a cliff, Grotta della Scala, Porticello and Marizza; in Cersuta the famous beach of u Nastru and Porto i Vranne, reachable only from the sea.

  • Where Costa di Maratea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it · italia.it

Isolotto di Santo Janni

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

Islet about 200 meters half a kilometer from the coast of Marina di Maratea, frequented by the Roman era: there remain tanks for the production of garum and the seabed have returned Greek and Roman anchors and amphorae; it houses a rare lizard, the Podarcis sicula paulae.

  • Where Al largo di Marina di Maratea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · basilicataturistica.it
