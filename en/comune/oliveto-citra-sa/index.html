What to see in Oliveto Citra

A town of medieval origin, mentioned since 1114, on a hill in the Sele valley, dominated by the remains of the baronial castle that houses the Provincial Archaeological Museum of the Upper Sele Valley, with the cultural equipment of ...Oliveto-Cairano.

Museo archeologico provinciale dell'Alta Valle del Sele

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

Inaugurated in 2004 in the baronial castle, it exhibits the excavations of 1928-29, which brought to light 65 burials between the VIII-VII and the IV-III century B.C., of the culture of "Oliveto-Cairano." The feminine ornaments rich in ornaments (pendants, earrings, pendants, heart-shaped bracelets) are outstanding with the reconstruction of a costume, black paint vases and red figures and bronze belts of the Sannitico costume. The Touring indicates opening on Tuesday-Saturday 9-13 and 14-15, Sunday 9-13.

Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Baronial castle on a high rock formation on top of the village, from which dominates the valley; in 1898 it was indicated as baronial castle of Sele. The complex remains a few walls, damaged by the earthquake of 23 November 1980.

There are few walls damaged by the 1980 earthquake; the source does not indicate how to visit the museum

  • Where Via Chiesa, Oliveto Citra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

An inscription dated 1497 the present form, which replaced an older structure; a stone column with cross bears the date 1096 and the pulpit, work of local craftsmanship, is from 1880. Two aisles, the largest divided by arches to the sixth and vaulted with fragments of frescoes, including a Deposition, putti in stucco and altar major in marble; the most precious works are no longer in the church.

The source indicates frescoes in critical state and sacristy in bad conditions, without visiting times
