What to see in Ponte Gardena

A small village in the Val d'Isarco between Bolzano and Chiusa, dominated by Castel Trostburg, one of the largest fortified complexes in South Tyrol, a residence for over six centuries of Wolkenstein and now home to the Museum of South Tyrol castles.

Castel Trostburg e Museo dei castelli dell'Alto Adige

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Documented at least from the 12th century, it passed from the lords of Velturno to the counts of Tyrol and then to the Wolkensteins, who held it for over six centuries: among them the poet and traveller Oswald (1377-1445). The large halls of representation added between the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries give it the present form: Gothic hall with three spans, loggia with frescoes of coats of arms, painted chapel and the New Hall of the Knights (1618 circa) with wooden ceiling inlaid coffered; decisive restorations of the Südtiroler Burgeninstitut at the end of the twentieth century. The Museum of Castles exhibits about eighty scale models. Guided tour only (German or Italian, 40-50 minutes plus 15 minutes for the exhibition), from Holy Thursday to October 31, closed on Monday; Invalid Museumcard.

  • Where Via Burgfrieden 22, Ponte Gardena
  • Entry €9, reduced €7, children 6-14 years €6
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.burgeninstitut.com

Sources: burgeninstitut.com · italia.it

Sentiero da Laion a Ponte Gardena

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Excursion of about 4.2 km (1 hour and about 20, 629 m of descent) on path no. 35: from Laion to the Vogelweiderhof, birthplace of the Minnesinger Walther von der Vogelweide, cross Novale and descend to Ponte Gardena with a view of Castel Trostburg. Recommended in autumn, when the farms along the route offer the Törggelen.

  • Where Ponte Gardena (arrivo; partenza da Laion)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it
