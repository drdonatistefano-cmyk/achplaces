What to see in Montecchio

Medieval village on the slopes of Monte Croce di Serra, with a castle and a double wall of the 12th century; in the territory the large Umbrian-Etruscan necropolis of the Vallone di San Lorenzo, whose finds are at the Antiquarium of Palazzo Ancajani in Tenaglie, and the castle of Melezzole.

Necropoli umbro-etrusca del Vallone di San Lorenzo

History and architecture · Archaeology

Among the largest pre-Roman necropolis in Umbria, in the Tiber River Park between Montecchio and Baschi: chamber tombs excavated in the local limestone ('Matile'), with dromos and side docks, used from the VII to the IV century BC and discovered in 1855 by Domenico Golini. They were sacked in ancient times, they returned weapons and pottery from banquets in the male, jewelry and ointment tombs in the female ones; they showed strong ties with the Etruscan Orvieto.

  • Where Località Copio, Montecchio (parcheggio su via del Molinello)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · umbriatourism.it

Antiquarium comunale (Palazzo Ancajani)

Museums · Archaeological

In the eighteenth century Palazzo Ancajani di Tenaglie, houses the findings of the Umbrian-Etruscan necropolis of Fosso San Lorenzo, dating from the sixth to the fourth century BC.

  • Where Palazzo Ancajani, Tenaglie (Montecchio)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · cultura.gov.it

Centro storico e castello di Montecchio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village with castle and remains of the double walls, 1154 and 1190; the only access is the Porta, with all sixth arch surmounted by a crenellated tower. In the center is the parish church of Santa Maria, before 1400.

  • Where Centro storico, Montecchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Melezzole: castello e chiesa di San Biagio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry
