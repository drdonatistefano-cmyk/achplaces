What to see in Pau

Country on the western side of Monte Arci, on the largest Sardinian deposit of obsidian, exploited by the 6th millennium BC: the Obsidian Museum, the only monothematic museum in Europe dedicated to this rock, the paths between the ancient workshops of Sennixeddu, the obsidian sculptures in the squares and jewelry shops.

4 places to visit in Pau, in the province of Oristano (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo dell'ossidiana

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Founded in 1999 at the entrance of the country, it is the only monothematic museum in Europe dedicated to obsidian: multisensory path with cartography, interactive audiovisual and sounds of the workmanship, works by sculptor Karmine Piras and brothers Atzori, minerals and fossils. It tells more than three thousand years of production, from the recent Neolithic to the Bronze Age, and from here start the guided excursions in the Park of Ossidiana; cumulative ticket with guide, excursion and laboratory €16. Open from Wednesday to Sunday.

  • Where Via San Giorgio 8, Pau
  • Entry €5, reduced €4; with guide €6
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museossidiana.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: museossidiana.it · sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Parco dell'ossidiana e sentiero di sa Perda Crobina

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

On Mount Arci, in the territory of Pau, nodules and obsidian waste cover over 20 hectares between the deposits of Perdas Urias, Sennixeddu and on Forru de is Sintzurreddus. The trail of Perda Crobina (the Corvina stone), steep and with large heaps of obsidian, crosses in about an hour and a half one of the largest prehistoric workshops, among thousands of shrapnel; you can walk with the guides of the museum. Marmora wrote that it seems to walk on the pieces of an old black bottle factory.

  • Where Sennixeddu, Monte Arci, Pau
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Parco del Monte Arci

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Volcanic massif of 270 km2 between eleven municipalities, with the peaks of Trebina Longa (812 m) and Trebina Lada (795 m), woods of holm oak and cork, Mediterranean maquis, wild boars, foxes and deer and re-introduced deers. Pau rises about 300 metres on its western side. Free entrance.

  • Where Monte Arci, Pau
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Sculture in ossidiana e botteghe del centro

Local life and traditions · Crafts · Free entry

In the Town Hall square and in the squares of the village there are contemporary sculptures made from large blocks of obsidian, of record size; in the old town the handcrafted workshops create jewels in obsidian and precious metals.

  • Where Piazza del Municipio, Pau
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · sardegnacultura.it
