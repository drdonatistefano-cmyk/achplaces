What to see in Ostiglia

Village on the Po in Mantovano, with the medieval towers of the castle, palaces and residences Liberty and an archaeological museum; around, the nature reserves of the Paludi di Ostiglia and the Isola Boschina and the cycle path of the former railway Treviso-Ostiglia.

8 places to visit in Ostiglia, in the province of Mantua (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torri medievali e resti del castello

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The Bell Towers, the Clock and the Prisons are what remains of the castle wanted by Ermanno, Marquis of Verona, and the fortified system of the center; they are dated between 1152 and 1297 and attributed to the Scaligeri.

  • Where Centro storico, Ostiglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismo.mantova.it · in-lombardia.it

Museo civico archeologico (Palazzo Foglia)

Museums · Archaeological

The museum tells the history of the village with finds from the V-IV millennium BC to the 19th century AD. It is located in Palazzo Foglia, built from 1833, which also houses the Fondo Musicale Greggiati, archive of publications, scores and rare period books.

  • Where Palazzo Foglia, Piazza Cornelio, Ostiglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it · italia.it

Riserva naturale Paludi di Ostiglia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

A wet area of 123 hectares with large reeds, where about 175 species of birds have been observed.

  • Where Ostiglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it · italia.it

Riserva naturale Isola Boschina

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

An island of about 37 hectares on the Po between Ostiglia and Revere, with forests and golenal habitats, described as a rare example of hanging marsh; it is reached by boat or on foot when the river is dry.

  • Where Fiume Po, tra Ostiglia e Revere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it · turismo.mantova.it
