What to see in Corfinio

Country of the Peligna Valley built on the ancient Corfinium, capital of the Italian peoples rebels in Rome during the social war, which coined coins with the name "Italy." The Basilica Valvense di San Pelino, one of the most important medieval monuments in Abruzzo, the tower mausoleums known as Morroni along the Tiburtina Valeria and the Archaeological Museum "Antonio De Nino" are worth seeing.

Basilica Valvense di San Pelino e Oratorio di Sant'Alessandro

Places of worship · Basilicas

It takes its name from the ancient diocese of Valva and it was built on the site of the martyrdom of San Pelino, where a early Christian cemetery developed. It combines the Oratory of St. Alexander, rectangular hall with apse which was the transept of a consecrated church in 1092 and remained unfinished, and the basilica completed in 1235, with three naves with raised transept; the central apse is scanned outside by four decorative bands with columns, sculpted slabs and windows.

  • Where Piazza San Pelino, Corfinio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo civico archeologico «Antonio De Nino»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

It collects the finds of the ancient Corfinium, capital of the Italian peoples during the social war, documented at the beginning of the twentieth century by the local scholar Antonio De Nino: stones and materials from the excavations of Piano San Giacomo, Sant'Ippolito, via di Pratola and Impianata, with eighty-six funeral epigraphs from the first century BC to the second century A.D. According to the MiC card is open by reservation, on summer weekends and in August also 10-13 and 16:30-19.

  • Where Via Zambeccario, Corfinio
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Morroni, mausolei romani

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Mausolei tower of ancient age along the Via Tiburtina Valeria, a few steps from the Basilica Valvense di San Pelino.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Centro storico e portali in pietra

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village occupies the site of the ancient Corfinium dei Peligni, chosen for its strategic position as the capital of the Italian rebels in Rome; in the historic center houses and palaces are adorned with stone portals.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
