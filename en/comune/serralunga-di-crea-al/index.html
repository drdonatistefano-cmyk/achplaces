What to see in Serralunga di Crea

On one of the highest heights of the Monferrato, the Sacred Marian Mount of Crea, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, with the spectacular Paradise Chapel.

3 places to visit in Serralunga di Crea, in the province of Alessandria (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Sacro Monte di Crea

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Started in 1589 by the prior Constantine Massino around the Marian sanctuary, at 455 meters: 23 chapels and 5 romans dedicated to the mysteries of the life of Mary, in two phases (Five-Sixth and Nineteenth century), with polychrome terracotta statues, in a forest of oaks and ashes. UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  • Where Località Sacro Monte, Serralunga di Crea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · fondoambiente.it

Cappella del Paradiso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

The 23rd chapel, of the Coronation of Mary, at the top of the hill: with more than three hundred statues it is the most complex of the Sacred Mount and an extraordinary viewpoint on hills and Alps; visit by reservation.

  • Where Sacro Monte, Serralunga di Crea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · fondoambiente.it · turismotorino.org · italia.it

Santuario della Madonna di Crea

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

The diocesan sanctuary dedicated to the Assumption, the heart of the devotional complex.

  • Where Piazza Santuario, Serralunga di Crea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
