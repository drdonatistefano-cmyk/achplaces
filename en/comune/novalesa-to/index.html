What to see in Novalesa

Country of Val Cenischia at the foot of Moncenisio, seat of the Benedictine abbey founded in 726, lighthouse of the Carolingian culture along the Via Francigena.

3 places to visit in Novalesa, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 2 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Abbazia dei Santi Pietro e Andrea

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Founded in 726, one of the lighthouses of culture in the Carolingian age, with abbey church, monastic building, four frescoed chapels and a park; over 15,000 visitors a year.

  • Where Borgata San Pietro 4, Novalesa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.vallesusa-tesori.it

Sources: vallesusa-tesori.it · cr.piemonte.it · turismotorino.org

Cappella di Sant'Eldrado

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It preserves a beautiful cycle of Romanesque frescoes of 1095/96, two cycles among the first of their kind in the West.

  • Where Abbazia di Novalesa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.vallesusa-tesori.it

Sources: vallesusa-tesori.it · cr.piemonte.it

Museo archeologico dell'Abbazia
