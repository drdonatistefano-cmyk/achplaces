Conca del Bertazzolo e Museo Diffuso del Fiume

Museums · Themed

In Governolo you can visit the Chiusa and the Conca di navigazione, designed between the end of the XIV and the beginning of the XVII century, adapted over the centuries and partially renewed in 1886-87: they are considered among the greatest testimonies of hydraulic archaeology of the Po Valley. The Rijeka Museum, inaugurated on October 19, 2013 by Roncoferraro and Bagnolo San Vito with Lombardy Region, tells with panels, videos, models and objects the story of the Bass Mincio, the lakes of Mantua and the engineers and machines that regulated the water; the classrooms propose hydraulic experiments. Open all year round by reservation and according to the calendar of events.

  • Where Via N. Bixio 10, Governolo di Roncoferraro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it

Pieve dei due pozzi (Santuario dei SS. Cosma e Damiano)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Romanesque church that recent studies date shortly after the year 1000, enlarged in the presbytery in the eighteenth century and recently restored to bring it back to the original style: today it is a building collected and simple. The eighteenth-century altarpiece depicts the Madonna del Carmine with two Carmelite saints and the Saints Cosma and Damiano; raised to the Sanctuary on September 26, 2006, each year it hosts a conference on health issues. Visits from Monday to Saturday by reservation, Mass on Sunday at 10:00.

  • Where Via Cavallotti, Barbassolo di Roncoferraro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismo.mantova.it
