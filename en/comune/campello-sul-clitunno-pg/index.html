What to see in Campello sul Clitunno

Common at the foot of the mountains between Spoleto and Trevi, with the castle of Campello Alto founded in 921 and the fortified villages of Pissignano, Acera and Agliano. Along the Flaminia you will find the Fonti del Clitunno, a park sung by Pliny the Younger, Byron and Carducci, and the Temple of the Clitunno, a UNESCO World Heritage Site among the places of the Longobards in Italy.

Tempietto del Clitunno

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage

Small Christian building dedicated to the Saviour, on a rocky spur near the sources, in the form of a small temple tetrastyle in antis with four Corinthian columns and Roman materials of reuse; on the pediment a bas-relief with Latin cross, inside a cell turned to barrels and apse. The dating is discussed (end IV-beginning V century or VII-VIII century, in the Lombard age); the frescoes of the VII-VIII century, with angels around the gemstone cross, Christ in majesty and Saints Peter and Paul, are considered the oldest in Umbria. Since 2011 it is one of the seven UNESCO World Heritage Sites in Italy. Places of power (568-774 A.D.); inspired Francesco di Giorgio Martini, Benozzo Gozzoli and Palladio.

Fonti del Clitunno

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Natural park around the sacred springs to the cult of Jupiter Clitunno, which fed a large navigable river connected to Rome; an earthquake of the fifth century destroyed a part of it. In the second half of the nineteenth century Count Paolo Campello della Spina rearranged the place and created the pond of cold and clear waters, among green islets, weeping willows and cypress poplars, with swans and ducks. He inspired Pliny the Younger, Corot, Byron and Carducci. Open every day from 8.30 to 20.

  • Where Via Flaminia 7, Campello sul Clitunno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.fontidelclitunno.it

Sources: fontidelclitunno.it · italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Castello di Campello Alto

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval castle founded in 921 by the Burgundian Baron Rovero de Champeaux, founder of the counts of Campello, with circular walls, watchtowers and a single well preserved door. On the central square the former town hall and the church of San Donato (XII century, with wooden Baroque altar); just below the convent of the Barnabite Fathers, born in the seventeenth century by the union of two Benedictine monasteries.

Castello di Pissignano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified village on Flaminia, which tradition attributed to Baron Sancio, of Germanic origin, grew around a Benedictine community. There are the church of San Benedetto (around the thousand, today desecrated and private) and that of San Cristoforo, then Santa Maria della Misericordia, with a miraculous image of Our Lady.
