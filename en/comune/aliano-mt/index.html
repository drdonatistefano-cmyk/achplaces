What to see in Aliano

Borgo perched on a clay ridge in the heart of the Lucanian calanchi, Orange Flag of Touring, linked to the border of Carlo Levi in the thirties: his house, the gallery and the literary park.

7 places to visit in Aliano, in the province of Matera (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Casa di confino di Carlo Levi

Museums · House museums

The restored house where the writer lived during the confinity in the 1930s, today multimedia museum.

  • Entry To be checked

Parco letterario Carlo Levi

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Viewpoint

It brings together places, objects and events related to the exile of Levi: guided tours, itineraries in the places described in his writings, shows, exhibitions and the literary prize.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
