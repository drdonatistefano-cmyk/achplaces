What to see in Valloriate

The municipality of the Stura Valley in a narrow valley, linked to partisan history: a popular museum combines the museum of war and resistance, the chestnut museum and the path marked towards Paraloup; on the rocky hill overlooks the parish church of San Michele, of Romanesque-Gothic origin.

4 places to visit in Valloriate, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della guerra e Resistenza

Museums · City history

In the municipality, set up in 2008 and rebuilt in 2023: panels, photographs and objects of epoch on fascism and resistance in Stura Valley, in four rooms (Italian army, German armies and ally, Resistance in Stura Valley, Resistance in Valloriate). Open Monday to Friday from 8 am to 1 pm; afternoons and weekends by reservation.

  • Where Piazza Michelangelo Berardengo 1, Valloriate
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Museo del castagno

Museums · Themed

In the building in front of the town hall, it illustrates with panels and reproductions the diseases and parasites of chestnut and its fruit, with numerous original tools for the working of the wood and its products. Open only by reservation.

  • Where Frazione Airale 24, Valloriate
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · comune.valloriate.cn.it:443

Sentiero Valloriate–Paraloup

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A marked route \"intrigued with partisan history" that connects Valloriate to Paraloup, with totems that tell the story along the way; it touches the villages Sapè and Mutta, which had mountain schools, and isolated buildings such as "chabot" and "ports" with the peaks Tajarè, the Alpe and Beccàs of Midday as panoramic points.

  • Where Valloriate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.valloriate.cn.it:443 · visitcuneese.it

Parrocchiale di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Church of Romanesque-Gothic origin on a rocky hill above the stream, linked to the monks of San Teofredo di Le Puy and then to the priory of Bersezio; remain a Gothic portal, a Romanesque apsidiola and a sailing bell tower incorporated in the tower, while the date April 2, 1680 on the apse indicates the probable reconstruction, on the model of Santa Maria a Cuneo by Giovenale Boetto.

  • Where Valloriate
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: vallestura.cn.it · visitcuneese.it
