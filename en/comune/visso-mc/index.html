What to see in Visso

Mountain village in the heart of the Sibillini Mountains and seat of the National Park, with walls, towers and Renaissance palaces around Piazza dei Martiri Vissani, seriously affected by the earthquake of 2016; in the surroundings the Renaissance sanctuary of Macereto.

4 places to visit in Visso, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Visso in the guide with map and filters

Centro storico e Piazza dei Martiri Vissani

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The centre is surrounded by medieval houses, Renaissance palaces and stone portals with Latin coats of arms and mottos; the square is surrounded by buildings from the 15th and 16th centuries, including Palazzo dei Priori, Palazzo dei Governatori (XIII century) and Palazzo Varano (1516).

center severely damaged by the earthquake 2016, parts in red zone without access to the public and reconstruction started in 2025 (Sibillini Park, Marche Region)

  • Where Piazza dei Martiri Vissani, Visso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · sibillini.net · regione.marche.it

Collegiata di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque-Gothic church of the XII century, renovated in the Two-Three-th century, with bell tower with cross-windows and portal fourteenth-century flanked by two lions; in the bezel an Annunciation frescoed by Paolo da Visso.

damaged by the earthquake 2016: technical insights in progress and provisional works installed (Region Marche, September 2025)

  • Where Piazza dei Martiri Vissani, Visso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · regione.marche.it · italia.it

Santuario di Santa Maria di Macereto

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

On the plateau of Macereto, about 1000 meters below Mount Bove, it was built from 1528-29 by Giovan Battista from Lugano in Bramante's forms around a fourteenth-century chapel: octagonal plan covered with travertine, inside with Greek cross and works by Simone De Magistris (1580-82) in the apse. Next to the Palazzo delle Guaite (1571-83), which with the sanctuary is closed by a wall with a porch for pilgrims and animals.

damaged by the earthquake 2016, some transennate parts (Turismo Marche)

  • Where Altopiano di Macereto, Visso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.marche.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Sentiero CAI 587 da Visso al Lago di Vigi

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Hiking route 7 km from Rasenna (932 m) to Lake Vigi, in the municipality of Sellano, between mule tracks, pastures and underbrush with steep and stony traits: it requires experience, sense of orientation and appropriate shoes.

  • Where Rasenna, Visso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
