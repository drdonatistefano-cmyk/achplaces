What to see in Castelnuovo di Val di Cecina

A hilly village in the shape of a grape cluster, among woods, vineyards and geothermal phenomena of the Val di Cecina.

4 places to visit in Castelnuovo di Val di Cecina, in the province of Pisa (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Fumarole di Sasso Pisano e Biolago

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

At Sasso Pisano fumarole and showerheads that give the landscape an infernal air; near the Biolago, fed by natural hot water and geothermal district heating.

  • Where Sasso Pisano, Castelnuovo di Val di Cecina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Terme del Bagnone

History and architecture · Archaeology

Hellenistic thermal structure of the third century BC, reopened after the excavations, with waters between 40 and 70 °C.

  • Where Sasso Pisano, Castelnuovo di Val di Cecina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Montecastelli Pisano e ipogeo etrusco

History and architecture · Archaeology

The fortified village with the Romanesque parish church of Saints Philip and James and, not far away, an Etruscan hypogeum of the 6th century BC with traces of a copper mine closed in 1941.

  • Where Montecastelli Pisano, Castelnuovo di Val di Cecina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Leccia e Cappella della Madonna del Latte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

The small medieval village of Leccia with the chapel of the Madonna del Latte, celebrated on June 14th.

  • Where Leccia, Castelnuovo di Val di Cecina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
