What to see in Palù del Fersina

The main centre of the Mòcheni Valley, 1396 metres away, is the seat of the Mòcheno Cultural Institute, with the farmhouses with the roofs in the background of the Lagorai; from the village a path rises to the 16th century mine Gruab goes Hardimbl and to the lake of Erdèmolo, while the museum S Pérkmandlhaus tells miners and mines of the valley.

Miniera-museo Gruab va Hardimbl (Erdemolo)

Museums · Themed

Ancient copper mine along the path to Lake Erdèmolo, exploited by the sixteenth century for four centuries: at the beginning of the sixteenth century German miners (Bergknappen) dug a hundred galleries. The underground path reconstructs the original environment with thematic settings; guided tour only, groups of up to 12 people, helmet and cape provided, temperature about 8 °C. Open 10-18 on the weekends of June and September, from Tuesday to Sunday to July and August, on Sunday in October; inside you do not pay by card.

S Pèrkmandlhaus

Museums · Themed

The house of the Pèrkmandl, the imaginary gnome of the mines: a museum near the town hall that tells in an innovative way mines and miners of the valley of the Fersina in the late Middle Ages and their connection with the Mòchena community, designed as an introduction to the mine of Erdemolo. Information and bookings at the numbers of the Mòchen Cultural Institute.

  • Where Località Lenzer-Jorger, Palù del Fersina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.visittrentino.info
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: bersntol.it · visittrentino.info

Lago di Erdèmolo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Alpine lake 1700 meters away on foot from the village, along the path that passes from the mine Gruab goes Hardimbl; the route continues to the refuge Sette Selle, where you can stay overnight.

  • Where Erdemolo, Palù del Fersina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info
