What to see in Tirolo

A village above Merano that named Tyrol: Tyrol Castle, home of Tirol's counts and today the Province's historical and cultural museum, Castel Fontana (Brunnenburg) with the South Tyrolean Agricultural Museum, the bird care centre for birds of prey on the castle's motto, the ground pyramids of the Mutbach gorge and the church of St. Peter with Romanesque frescoes.

Castel Tirolo - Museo storico-culturale della Provincia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The first nucleus dates back to about 1100; the mother house of the counts of Tyrol, which gave the name to the region, passed to the Habsburgs in 1363 and fell from the fifteenth century, when the government moved to Innsbruck; plundered and put to auction by the Bavarian occupants around 1800, it was bought by Merano and donated to the emperor Francesco I, then restored. It contains Romanesque sculptures, marble portals, protogothic frescoes, a copy of the Gothic altar (the original is in Innsbruck) and the oldest Tyrolean windows; it houses the provincial museum of history and culture, with the permanent exhibition in a renewed wing. You can only reach it on foot, 25-30 minutes from the village; closed on Mondays; not suitable for wheelchairs.

Castel Fontana (Brunnenburg) e Museo agricolo

Museums · Themed

Built around 1250, with fortifications of 1241-1253, destroyed and rebuilt several times and restored in a unique way at the beginning of the twentieth century; it rises on an ice conoid between the village and Castel Tyrol and would take its name from a nearby source. Today it is a cultural centre and hosts the South Tyrolean Agricultural Museum. You can walk to the Falkner promenade and the Ezra Pound trail.

Centro di cura per l'avifauna di Castel Tirolo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

From 1998 he took care in aviaries on the motto of Castel Tirol the wounded raptors until release and welcomed those who could no longer fly; twice a day demonstrations of flight with the experienced Willy Campei and Florian Gamper, and didactic paths in the park. Piramidi di terra della gola del Mutbach

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Land towers formed over thousands of years by landslides and rains, where large boulders protect the ground below from erosion; in Tyrol they are located in the Mutbach gorge near Castel Tyrol and in the Fine Valley towards Caines. You can see from the top with the Hochmuth cable car, Herrschaftsweg trail or the route of about 5 km from Tyrol Castle to Farmerkreuz.

  • Where Gola del Mutbach, Tirolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it

Sources: merano-suedtirol.it

Piattaforma panoramica «Gerla» dell'Hochmuth

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the Hochmuth, under Steinegg, a gerla-shaped platform recalls the cultivation of the steep Muthöfe farms, where with winches they pulled on soil and manure; views over Merano, the Val d'Adige and the mountains, with two telescopes and panels on the peaks and ancient crops.

  • Where Hochmuth, Tirolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it

Sources: merano-suedtirol.it

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient parish between the moat to the west of Castel Tirol and the Grabbach, on the hill already inhabited in prehistoric age; the legend wants the church built by a people of dwarves and on the churchyard stood one of the oldest baptisteries in the area.
