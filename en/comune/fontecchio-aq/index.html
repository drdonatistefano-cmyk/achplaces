What to see in Fontecchio

Medieval fortified village in stone of the Aterno valley, rebuilt after the earthquake of 2009, with cobbled alleys, steps, doors, walls and towers. In Piazza del Popolo the Fountain with 14th century Masks, symbol of the village; at the top of the hill the 15th century Clock Tower, with its ancient six-hour dial and permanent photographic exhibition on the memory of the eagle earthquake.

Piazza del Popolo e Fontana con Maschere

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The fourteenth-century limestone fountain, symbol of the village and present in the coat of arms, has a circular tub divided by three-pointed arches and a central column with vegetal and human figures and masks with the face of a lion. Next to it, a votive newsstand preserves a fresco of the 15th century Madonna with Child (Madonna dell'Uccellino) with bright colors, then the communal oven where the families cooked bread and another fountain used to water the animals; from the medieval door on the south side of the square you enter the fortified village.

Torre dell'Orologio e Museo della cultura della memoria

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

A medieval tower from the 15th century at the top of the hill, with a clock considered one of the oldest in Italy: the dial is divided into six hours according to the Italian method and the mechanism, still working, moves the only hand of the hours; every evening it plays 50 chimes in memory of the Spanish siege of 50 days of 1648. Inside, the permanent exhibition "The space of memory" with ancient and current photos of the Eagle before and after the earthquake of 2009 by Roberto Grillo, a stele for victims and a multimedia post on anti-seismic prevention. Not indicated hours: contact the Municipality (0862 85131).

Chiesa di Santa Maria della Pace

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church founded in the 11th century and rebuilt after the earthquake of 1703 that struck Fontecchio and the entire region.
