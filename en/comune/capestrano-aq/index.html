What to see in Capestrano

A country of less than a thousand inhabitants in the valley of the Tirino, linked to the Warrior of Capestrano (VI century BC, original in Chieti) of which the Piccolomini Castle, seat of the municipality, exhibits the copy; in the valley the Romanesque church of San Pietro ad Oratorium, at the gates of the village the convent of San Giovanni da Capestrano and the lake of Capodacqua with the submerged mill.

Castello Piccolomini e copia del Guerriero

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The castle on the main square houses the Town Hall and is always open.It houses the reproduction of the Warrior of Capestrano, a limestone statue of Italian warrior over two meters high of the 6th century BC, found in 1934 and preserved at the National Archaeological Museum of Abruzzo in Chieti. From the view of the courtyard you can see Rocca Calascio.

  • Where Piazza principale, Capestrano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di San Pietro ad Oratorium

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

In the valley of the Tirino, about 6 km from the village: abbey founded by the Longobard king Desiderio (756-774), church rebuilt and consecrated by Pasquale II in 1117. On the façade is the inscription of the foundation and, upside down, the magic square "ROTAS OPERA TENET AREPO SATOR"; inside three naves and three apses, a ciborium of the thirteenth century with a branch that comes out of the mouths of a dragon and green and turquoise majolicas, unique in Abruzzo, and in the apse the fresco of the first twelfth century with Christ enthroned between the Evangelists and the 24 Vegliards of the Apocalypse.

Visitable only by reservation (man-ch@cultura.gov.it, tel. 329 776 5574)

Convento di San Giovanni da Capestrano (San Francesco)

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Monastery founded in the fifteenth century by the will of Giovanni da Capestrano, born in the village: started in 1447 and completed in 1853, with the cloister of 1709, the apsed presbytery of 1735, dormitory and library of 1742 and the monumental staircase of 1750. It houses a small museum dedicated to the saint with his relics.

  • Where Via di San Francesco, Capestrano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Lago di Capodacqua

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

A small artificial lake formed in 1965 by a dam on the Tirino, called the small Atlantis of Abruzzo: the clear waters cover an ancient mill and its silciati and leave in part emerge a paint factory. A diver's destination; those who do not dive see the mill from an electric boat with glass bottom. Balneation prohibited to protect the bird; open Saturday and Sunday 10.30-15.

  • Where Via del Lago, Capestrano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
