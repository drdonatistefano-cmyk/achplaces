What to see in Limone sul Garda

Village of alleys, stairs and stone houses on the Brescia shore of Garda, once the most northern commercial citrus growing area in the world; to see the eighteenth-century Limonaia del Castèl, the marina, the Museo dei Pescatori in the park of Villa Boghi and the cycle path suspended on the lake.

8 places to visit in Limone sul Garda, in the province of Brescia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Limonaia del Castèl

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

Limonaia of the beginning of the eighteenth century on 1,633 m2 of terraces (còle) on the sides of the central casèl, with pillars and white walls exposed to the sun; purchased by the Municipality in 1995, restored and open to the public on 22 July 2004, with lemons, cedars, grapefruits, mandarins, chinotti, kumquats and other citrus fruits, didactic route and lake view. It is reached on foot from the center following the ceramic tiles; open from March to October.

  • Where Via Orti 9, Limone sul Garda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.limonesulgarda.bs.it · italia.it · in-lombardia.it · visitbrescia.it

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

A labyrinth of alleys, steps, squares and stone houses overlooking the lake; in the heart of the center the lakefront and the marina with shops and bar overlooking the lake.

Ciclopedonale del Garda

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The new section, inaugurated on July 14, 2018, runs for 2 km suspended about 50 meters above the lake on a 2.5 meters wide footbridge to the border with Trentino; with the existing track from Nanzell to Capo Reamol the track measures 12 km. Illuminated LED, it is also possible to walk in the evening; limit of 10 km/h for bikes, cars in the car parks of the center.

  • Where Da Limone sul Garda al confine con il Trentino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Museo dei Pescatori e parco di Villa Boghi

Museums · Themed

In the municipal park of the former Villa Boghi, now home to the Town Hall and surrounded by citrus fruits, olive trees and Mediterranean plants, the museum preserves objects, photos and tools of the traditional crafts of the lake donated by the fishing families and the local association.

  • Where Via IV Novembre 25, Limone sul Garda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
