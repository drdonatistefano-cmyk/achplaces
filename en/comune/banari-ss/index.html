What to see in Banari

Meilogu village built in red trachite, with the parish church of San Lorenzo and the Romanesque church of San Michele donated in 1113 to the Camaldolese, the contemporary art museum of the Fondazione Logudoro Meilogu and, in a nearby valley, the Romanesque complex of Santa Maria di Cea.

Museo d'arte contemporanea - Fondazione Logudoro Meilogu

Museums · Modern art

Housed in a noble palace in red trachite of the thirteenth century, it exhibits paintings and sculptures of important Italian artists, in particular of the 1950s of the twentieth century.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Santa Maria di Cea

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Country church with a former monastic complex in a valley about 6 km from Banari: Romanesque, in limestone cones, with east apse, facade divided by a frame and sailing bell tower; a date engraved on the facade reads 1250, 1260 or 1261. Next to the remains of the 'romitorio', with oven, central well and cobbled in the yard. The festival is celebrated around September 8.

  • Where Valle di Cea, Banari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Parrocchiale di San Lorenzo martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The original building dates back to the 12th century; it was rebuilt in the 18th century and has a neoclassical 19th century façade. During an extension a burial stone was found with relics and parchments that attest to the year of construction. Nearby stands the oratory of Santa Croce (XVI-XVII century), with a red stone façade.

  • Where Centro storico, Banari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Michele arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in Romanesque style in the 12th century and restored several times; in 1113 the judge of Torres donated it, with San Lorenzo, to the Camaldolese monks.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Centro storico e Palazzo comunale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Most of the houses and palaces are in red trachyte; the Town Hall, in the early decades of the nineteenth century, overlooks piazza sas Bovedas, the center of social life.

  • Where Piazza sas Bovedas, Banari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Nuraghi e domus de janas

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

In the territory there are about ten nuraghi: Sa Tanchitta and Corona Alta in red trachyte, Su Crapione and Buffulinu in white stone. There are also domus de janas, called coroddos: four attributed to ziu Juanne and the domu de su Crapione, dug into the tuff.

  • Where Territorio di Banari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
