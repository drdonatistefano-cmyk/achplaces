What to see in Roncade

Roncade is dominated by Castello Giustinian, the only Venetian villa still surrounded by crenellated walls, now a wine estate; in front there is the church of All Saints with an eighteenth-century pictorial cycle of the Tiepolesque school, while in Biancade the church of San Giovanni Battista rises on the banks of the Musestre.

Castello di Roncade (Villa Castello Giustinian)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The complex, donated around 900 by Otto II to the counts of Collalto and then destroyed by Cangrande della Scala, was rebuilt by the Venetian Girolamo Giustinian on a project attributed to Mauro Coducci: walls, towers and moat precede the villa by a few years, completed in 1508. It is the only Venetian villa still surrounded by crenellated walls, with loggias of Sansovino taste, external frescoes, an oratory dedicated to Sant'Anna with busts of Girolamo Giustinian and his wife Agnesina Badoer, and an entrance avenue with seventeenth-century statues of a company of footmen. From the early twentieth century it belongs to Ciani Bassetti, who produce wine there; in front of the castle stands the Monument to the fallen of Libero Andreotti (1924).

Chiesa di Tutti i Santi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish church with a majestic and elegant neoclassical façade; inside it preserves one of the most refined eighteenth-century pictorial cycles in Veneto, the work of artists from the Tiepolescan school.

  • Where Via Roma 149, Roncade
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.roncade.tv.it

Sources: comune.roncade.tv.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista a Biancade

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt in the current forms on the banks of the river Musestre in 1492, with the bell tower of 1493; in 1903 it was renovated, raised almost three meters and equipped with a new facade and a frescoed ceiling.

  • Where Via Paris Bordone 3, Biancade, Roncade
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.roncade.tv.it
