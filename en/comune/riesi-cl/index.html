What to see in Riesi

The village of the zolfatari, called Altariva until the eighteenth century by Baron Pietro Altariva: the memory of the mines lives in the Zolfataro Monument, in the remains of the Trabia-Tallarita mines and in the Museum of the Solfare; then there are the prehistoric necropolis of the Caves and the Waldensian complex of Monte degli Ulivi by Leonardo Ricci.

6 places to visit in Riesi, in the province of Caltanissetta (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Miniere Trabia-Tallarita

History and architecture · Archaeology

What remains of the plants and sulphur mines Trabia-Tallarita can be visited in the surroundings of the village, in the direction of Sommatino; the complex is documented in the Museo delle Solfare and it is a stop of the "Via delle Zolfare e dei Castelli" in Sicily of Mezzo.

  • Where Dintorni di Riesi, verso Sommatino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Museo delle Solfare

Museums · Themed

Set up in the former Palladio power plant, it reconstructs the production chain of sulphur in all its phases and the socio-economic aspects of mining, documenting the plants of the Trabia-Tallarita mines.

  • Where Ex centrale elettrica Palladio, Riesi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Monumento allo Zolfataro

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The work of the sculptor Onofrio La Leggia, recalls the miners' craft depicting them pushing wagons loaded with sulfur ore.

  • Where Riesi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Grotte di Riesi

History and architecture · Archaeology

A rocky site that is a typical necropolis with a sicule grotticelle, important evidence of the prehistoric attendance of the territory.

  • Where Riesi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Complesso di Monte degli Ulivi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The complex is owned by the Valdese Church, designed in the sixties by architect Leonardo Ricci and considered one of the most original interventions of contemporary architecture in Sicily.

  • Where Monte degli Ulivi, Riesi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Centro storico, scalinata di San Giuseppe e Madonna della Catena

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the historic centre, a scenographic stone staircase rises to the Church of St. Joseph, near the seventeenth-century Church of the Madonna della Catena, dedicated to the patron saint; in Piazza D'Antona there is a monument to the senator of the same name.

  • Where Centro storico, Riesi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info
