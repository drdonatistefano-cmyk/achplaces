What to see in Biandronno

On the west bank of Lake Varese, Biandronno is part of the UNESCO site of prehistoric alpine palafittic sites: just a few meters from the shore there is Isolino Virginia, the oldest inhabited palafittic building in the Alpine arch, with a small museum. The village is crossed entirely by the cycle track that runs around the lake.

Isolino Virginia, area archeologica palafitticola

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Small triangular atoll of about 9,200 sqm a few meters from the western shore of Lake Varese, separated from the mainland by the narrow called "Ticinello." In 1822 it was bought by the Duke Pompeo Litta who called it "Camilla" (Camilla), and since 1865, with the sale of the lake to Andrea Ponti, it took the name of his wife Virginia, and since 1963 it has been of the Municipality of Varese for the legacy of the Marquis Gian Felice Ponti. The excavations started after the discoveries of Abbot Stoppani have revealed inhabited by the Neolithic (4500 BC) at the Bronze Age and at the early Iron Age: it is the oldest pile-dwelling settlement of the alpine arch, UNESCO World Heritage and protected area, with open-air teaching trail, water lilies and many birds. You can only reach it by boat, with transhipment from Biandronno, locality Strencia; on the island a restaurant-bar open all year round.

Museo preistorico dell'Isolino Virginia

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

A small museum on the island, with a new layout since July 2013: on the ground floor a room with a video on the history of Isolino, then panels, reconstructive drawings and relics in case on the daily life of the first inhabitants, and the life-size reconstruction of a neolithic house with arches, arrows and pottery. Most of the artifacts are at the Musei Civici di Villa Mirabello in Varese. Seasonal opening from April to early November on Saturdays, Sundays and holidays; visits of about 2 hours by reservation all year round for groups up to 25 people.

Pista ciclopedonale del Lago di Varese (tratto di Biandronno)

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

An easy ring of 28 km (about 2 hours by bike) that leaves and arrives in Gavirate and, counterclockwise, touches Biandronno, Bodio Lomnago, Azzate, the Schiranna, Calcinate and Voltorre; it crosses the village of Biandronno in full and in the first stretch passes from the boarding point of the boat to Isolino Virginia. Along the tour the beaches of Cazzago Brabbia and Bodio Lomnago, the church of Our Lady of the Lake and the Cloister of Voltorre.
