What to see in Osimo

Cities on the hill between Ancona and Loreto, ancient Roman town hall and bishop's seat: the Romanesque-Gothic cathedral of San Leopardo with baptistery and diocesan museum, the town hall with the Roman statues acephale, the basilica of San Giuseppe da Copertino, the Madonna del Rosario del Guercino in San Marco and more than nine kilometers of underground caves.

10 places to visit in Osimo, in the province of Ancona (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale di San Leopardo e Battistero

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

At the highest point of the city, next to the ancient Cassero, it is one of the main examples of Romanesque-Gothic of the Marches: nucleus of the eighth century enlarged in the XII-XIII centuries with naves, apse, crypt and protyro; in the crypt the sarcophagus of the Saints Martyrs with relief in lunese marble of the fourth century. In the seventeenth-century baptistery the bronze source of the brothers Jacometti of Recanati.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Osimo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.osimo.an.it · italia.it

Museo Diocesano

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the ancient bishopric residences of the Episcopio, next to cathedral and baptistery, sixteen rooms of paintings, sculptures, furnishings and sacred vestments that tell the story of the Christian community osimana. Entire 3 €, reduced 2 €.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Osimo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.osimo.an.it · italia.it

Grotte del Cantinone e Osimo sotterranea

History and architecture · Archaeology

Under the city a network of galleries of more than nine kilometers on several levels, dug and re-used for centuries for water, food and shelter, with air wells and light; in the guided path of the Cantinone religious figures and symbols and daily life, and the names engraved during the Second World War. Guided tour of less than an hour.

  • Where Ufficio IAT, via Fonte Magna, Osimo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Palazzo Comunale, Lapidario e Torre civica

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

On piazza del Comune, built between the Five and the seventeenth century on a project by Pompeo Floriani: in the atrium and in the courtyard from the middle of the eighteenth century the Lapidary with honorary statues and Roman inscriptions. On the east side the 13th century Civic Tower.

  • Where Piazza del Comune 1, Osimo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.osimo.an.it · italia.it

Palazzo Campana e Museo Civico

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the eighteenth-century palace on Piazza Dante, home of the library and historical archive: artistic section with works by Guercino and Giovan Francesco Guerrieri, the Madonna and Child in stone of the thirteenth century, frescoes by Andrea da Bologna and a polyptych by the Vivarini; archaeological section with finds of the historic center and the territory. Entire 2 €, reduced 1 €.

  • Where Via Campana, Osimo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.osimo.an.it · italia.it

Basilica di San Giuseppe da Copertino

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Sanctuary from 1781 of the patron saint, who died in the monastery annexed in 1663, whose remains are in the crypt under the presbytery; preserves the table of Antonio Solario Vergine in throne and Saints (1503).

  • Where Piazza Gallo, Osimo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.osimo.an.it · italia.it

Museo di San Giuseppe da Copertino

Museums · Themed

In the convent of the Friars Minor Conventuals: private chapel with seventeenth-century canvas of the Nativity of Mary, clothes and clothes of the saint, liturgical furnishings, illuminated images on parchment, documents of the sixteenth century and a small royal organ ad ance.

  • Where Piazza Gallo, Osimo
  • Entry To be checked

Chiesa di San Marco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Restored in the fifteenth century by the Dominicans, who officiated it from 1428 to 1920: fifteenth-century fresco of the Madonna enthroned with the Saints Domenico and Pietro martyr and the Madonna of the Rosary painted by Guercino in 1642, with the fifteen Mysteries in the ovals of the frame.

  • Where Osimo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.osimo.an.it · italia.it

Teatro La Nuova Fenice

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built between 1877 and 1894 on a project by Gaetano Canedi and inaugurated with the Carmen of Bizet, it has three orders of stages and loggia; restored between 1987 and 1999, it hosts the seasons of prose and opera.

  • Where Osimo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.osimo.an.it · italia.it

Santuario dell'Addolorata di Campocavallo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

In the hamlet of Campocavallo, 3 km from Osimo: designed by Costantino Costantini and consecrated in 1905; parties of the Addolorata on the third Sunday of September and of the Covo on the first Sunday of August.

  • Where Campocavallo, Osimo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
