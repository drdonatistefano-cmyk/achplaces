What to see in Scanno

Village of terraced houses, stairs and arches in the National Park of Abruzzo, Lazio and Molise, portrait by Cartier-Bresson and Giacomelli, above the lake in the shape of a heart, the largest natural basin in Abruzzo.

12 places to visit in Scanno, in the province of L'Aquila (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Scanno in the guide with map and filters

Lago di Scanno

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest natural basin in Abruzzo, shaped like a heart, for canoeing, windsurfing, bikes and excursions.

  • Where Scanno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Terraced houses, alleys, stairs, fountains and palaces with portals and arches, photographed by Cartier-Bresson, Giacomelli and Berengo Gardin.

  • Where Scanno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · abruzzoturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria della Valle

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

On the main square, built in 1568 on a medieval church, with Romanesque bell tower with sixteenth century cusp and wooden Baroque furnishings.

  • Where Piazza Santa Maria della Valle, Scanno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · abruzzoturismo.it · italia.it
