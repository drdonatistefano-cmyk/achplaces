What to see in Roscigno

Municipality of Alburni in the Cilento National Park: the abandoned village of Roscigno Vecchia, left between 1902 and 1908 for a landslide and remained like a country still in time, with the museum of peasant civilization and, above, the wine-lucano site of Monte Pruno.

3 places to visit in Roscigno, in the province of Salerno (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Roscigno Vecchia

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Left by the inhabitants between 1902 and 1908 for a landslide, according to the laws on landslides: the stone houses in the chamber around the eighteenth-century church of San Nicola and the large Piazza Giovanni Nicotera with shops, barns, stables, water troughs and fountain; in the houses remain beds, kitchens and a stable.

  • Where Roscigno Vecchia, Roscigno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.cilentoediano.it

Sources: touringclub.it · cilentoediano.it

Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

In the six rooms of the former canonical house of Roscigno Vecchia, restored by the Superintendence of Salerno: social life, tools of work and everyday life of the peasant world; visits with the volunteers of the Pro Loco.

  • Where Roscigno Vecchia, Roscigno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.cilentoediano.it

Sources: touringclub.it · cilentoediano.it

Monte Pruno

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the summit, above Roscigno Vecchia and on the only pass between Vallo di Diano and plain of Paestum, the wine and Lucan settlement of the VII-III century BC; in 1938 there was found the tomb of a prince winery, with silver crown and war chariot, today at the Provincial Archaeological Museum of Salerno. Paths between oak trees and stain to the top.

  • Where Monte Pruno, Roscigno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.cilentoediano.it

Sources: cilentoediano.it · touringclub.it
