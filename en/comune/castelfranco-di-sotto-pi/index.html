What to see in Castelfranco di Sotto

The centre of the lower Valdarno in the leather district, which preserves the castrum plant with two main crossed roads and four doors; to see the Collegiate Church of Saints Peter and Paul, the Bell Tower, the Badia di Santa Maria Maddalena, the archaeological exhibitions of Castelfranco and Orentano and the Natural Reserve of Montefalcone on the Cerbaie.

Collegiata dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1284 with the support of Paganello II, Bishop of Lucca, in the Tuscan Romanesque style (the brick arch in the façade remains), it was rebuilt and enlarged in 1719 with baroque stuccoes; on the side a sandstone panther recalls the Lucca domination. Inside the fifteenth century baptismal font, a marble Saint Peter and the two polychrome wooden statues of the Annunciation of the Pisan school of the thirteenth century (attributed by Visit Tuscany to Nino Pisano), a crucifix of the fifteenth century very venerated, the Resurrection of Tabita by Cosimo Gamberucci and St. Peter in prison by Alessandro Allori.

Torre campanaria e Municipio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The thirteenth-century tower, with a pavilion roof and two-fores, is the only original tower left; since 1790 it is the bell tower of the Collegiate and seat of the public clock, and overlooks Porta San Pietro in Vigesimo, the main entrance of the village. On the main square, the Town Hall, formerly Palazzo della Cancelleria of the fifteenth century, with a loggia with columns in stone and arches in all sixth and a marzocco in sandstone, symbol of the Florentine power; the excavations date street, square and domus Communis in the mid thirteenth century.

  • Where Piazza principale, Castelfranco di Sotto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.castelfranco.pi.it · visittuscany.com

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maddalena (Badia)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1220 on the ground of the bishopric of Lucca, before the castle, and entrusted to the regular Canons of Sant'Agostino. Rectangular plant with exposed trusses, porch and apse, central altar with eighteenth-century ciborium in white marble and two lateral altars in stone of the seventeenth century; today it is votive chapel of the fallen of the First World War.

Museo archeologico di Orentano

Museums · Archaeological

It exhibits the results of the ongoing research on the territory and tells, with original materials, graphic reconstructions and a plastic, the history of the settlements in the ancient beehive of Lake Bientina from the Paleolithic to the Middle Ages: wood, stone, metals, ceramics and coins. The strong pieces are the remains of a Roman bridge and a medieval piroga used to cross the lake. Educational activities for schools and temporary exhibitions.

  • Where Via Martiri della Libertà, Orentano, Castelfranco di Sotto

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Riserva naturale di Montefalcone e Cerbaie

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

The most important natural area of Cerbaie, established by ministerial decrees of 1977 (biogenetic reserve) and 1980 (animal repopulation), between 45 and 114 meters of altitude: sea pine with cerro, oak, oak, oak, farnia and hornbeam; deer, roe deer, deer, wild boars and, in the humid zones, germans, folagues, herons ashes and marzaioles. The municipality runs an area of about 10 hectares near Staffoli open in summer, and at the Poor Town a forest road of 800 m with picnic areas. On Cerbaie a network of CAI paths for foot and bike, with sources of Leone, Sasso and Pierdo and about 2 km of Via Francigena in Galleno.

Il Callone sull'Arno

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

In the hamlet on the right bank of the Arno, a hydraulic complex that from the fourteenth century barred the river between Castelfranco and San Romano, operated the municipal mills and allowed the passage of the boats; a grand-ducal customs collected tolls. In 1833 it was the only 'callone' still standing along the river.
