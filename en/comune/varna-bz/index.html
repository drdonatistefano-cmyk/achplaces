What to see in Varna

Country of vineyards north of Bressanone, sheltered from the north winds and marked by the noble residences of the bishop's officers; in the hamlet of Novacella stands the Augustinian Abbey founded in 1142, one of the major monasteries of Tyrol, in the northernmost wine area of Italy.

4 places to visit in Varna, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Abbazia agostiniana di Novacella

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded in 1142 by Blessed Bishop Hartmann, it is still an active monastery of Augustinian Canons; in 1742 it was the largest in Tyrol and in the Middle Ages it hosted a school of copyists and choral chanting. The church of the Assumption, Romanesque, was transformed into baroque forms; to see the frescoed Gothic cloister, the courtyard well with the wonders of the world, the roundabout Castel dell'Angelo (a former hospice and defence), the rococo library, the gallery with medieval paintings by Michael and Friedrich Pacher and Marx Reichlich, the wall of the Turks, the mill and the cellar, where the abbey still produces its wines (Sylvaner, Müller-Thurgau, Kerner). Free tours from Monday to Saturday 10-17, closed Sunday and Catholic holidays.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.kloster-neustift.it

Giardino storico dell'Abbazia di Novacella

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

At the entrance of the complex, reopened in summer 2004 after a large restoration: a centuries-old sequoia dominates the bottom of the garden, in front of old ginkgo; in the middle a baroque garden with flowers, medicinal herbs and a fountain. Open Monday to Saturday 10-17.

  • Where Via Abbazia 1, Novacella, Varna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.kloster-neustift.it

Sources: kloster-neustift.it · suedtirol.info · brixen.org

Sentiero tematico del rio Scaleres

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

An easy ring of 2 km (about 40 minutes, 141 m of difference in altitude) that starts from the central parking lot of Varna under the parish church of San Giorgio and goes up the river Scaleres along the right bank, with wooden walkways, an ancient chestnut and the first original Kneipp tanks of South Tyrol, with water path, playground and lawn; we return to Salern along paths 6 and 20.

  • Where Parcheggio centrale, Varna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.brixen.org

Sources: suedtirol.info · brixen.org

Laghi Gelati (Schrüttenseen)

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

High altitude lakes with crystal clear water between rhododendrons, cembri and clear boulders, reached with a challenging excursion that starts from the Val di Scaleres, valley of high mountain intact above Varna.

  • Where Val di Scaleres, Varna (partenza)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.brixen.org

Sources: brixen.org
