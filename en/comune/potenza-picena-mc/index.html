What to see in Potenza Picena

A hilly village with a medieval structure gathered around Piazza Matteotti, with the Collegiate Church of Santo Stefano, the Pincio viewpoint open from the Sibillini to the Adriatic and the eighteenth century Villa Buonaaccorsi with its Italian garden; on the sea the hamlet of Porto Potenza Picena, with the seaside village and the Blue Flag promenade.

Villa Buonaccorsi e giardino all'italiana

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

Masterpiece of the 18th century Marche according to the FAI, acquired by the State in 2022; the Ministry has allocated about 10 million euros for a first batch of restorations that includes the historical gardens. The Italian garden has fountains, water games, avenues, niches and statues. In summer 2025 the garden was opened a few Saturday afternoons, free of charge, with visits in groups of up to 30 people accompanied by Pro Loco and mandatory booking.

openings only on established days with mandatory booking (calendar 2025); villa under restoration

  • Where Contrada Giardino 1, Potenza Picena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.potenzapicenaturismo.it

Collegiata di Santo Stefano e Cappella dei Contadini

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Started at the end of the sixteenth century as the church of St. Ignatius of the Jesuit complex on a project by Father Giovanni de Rosis, remained empty after the suppression of the Jesuits (1773), since 1796 it is Collegiate and has welcomed many works of the demolished parish church of St. Stephen; the facade has remained unfinished. It preserves a Martyrdom of Santo Stefano attributed to the school of Pomarancio and an organ Bazzani of 1848; the Chapel of the Peasants has the walls frescoed by Benedetto Biancolini with scenes of life and rural devotion.

  • Where Piazzale Santo Stefano 13, Potenza Picena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.potenzapicenaturismo.it

Sources: potenzapicenaturismo.it

Chiesa di Sant'Agostino (Auditorium Scarfiotti)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Augustinian complex occupies a large part of the old town; the Augustinians have been documented since 1250, when they had the church of Santa Maria Maddalena, to which the building has always remained titled. The current layout dates back to the mid-eighteenth century; it houses a terracotta bust of the penitent Magdalene attributed to Ambrogio Della Robbia, the large canvas of the Magdalena by Pietro Tedeschi and an eighteenth-century organ by Giovanni Fedeli. Restored, today it is the Auditorium entitled to the Oscar Ferdinando Scarfiotti award set designer, born in Potenza Picena.

open normally only during calendar events

Piazza Matteotti, Palazzo Comunale e Torre civica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Monumental and social center of the village, of medieval plant, dominated by the Town Hall with next to the Civic Tower and surrounded by gentle palaces, with a fountain in the center. In Palazzo Comunale the Sala della Giunta contains nineteenth-century frescoes and paintings by artists including Simone De Magistris and Corrado Giaquinto.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti 28, Potenza Picena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.potenzapicenaturismo.it

Sources: potenzapicenaturismo.it

Belvedere del Pincio (Giardino dei Tigli)

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Belvedere Donatori di Sangue, known as the Pincio, is a avenue of about fifty lindens planted in 1901 with a large cedar in the center of Lebanon, among the oldest of the Marches. From the terrace the view goes from the Sibillini to the Adriatic, with the Basilica of Loreto, Recanati, Castelfidardo and Cingoli.

  • Where Potenza Picena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.potenzapicenaturismo.it

Sources: potenzapicenaturismo.it

Piramide de Mayo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Replica of the Pirámide de Mayo of Plaza de Mayo in Buenos Aires, the only one in the world according to the Municipality, donated by the Sociedad Potentina de Mutuo Socorro of La Plata and inaugurated in 1967 to commemorate the powerful emigration to Argentina; it rises in the gardens of the same name.

  • Where Viale Cesare Battisti 11, Potenza Picena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.potenzapicenaturismo.it

Sources: potenzapicenaturismo.it

Borgo marinaro e Torre di Sant'Anna

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The historic district of Porto Potenza Picena has an eighteenth-century grilled system and narrow and colorful houses, inhabited by the "sciapecotti" fishermen linked to the fishing with the sciabica. In the village remain the fifteenth-century watchtower of Sant'Anna, erected against pirate raids, and the homonymous neo-Gothic church.

  • Where Porto Potenza Picena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.potenzapicenaturismo.it

Sources: potenzapicenaturismo.it · letsmarche.it

Lungomare Marinai d'Italia e spiagge

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The promenade of Porto Potenza Picena, Blue Flag and Flag Lilla for accessibility, has a wide pedestrian path and a bike path along the Adriatic. More than 3 km of coastline between Porto Recanati and Civitanova Marche, with sandy beaches and a little gravel, have been made accessible, with underpasses and walkways to the sea in the establishments.

  • Where Lungomare Marinai d'Italia, Porto Potenza Picena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.potenzapicenaturismo.it

Sources: potenzapicenaturismo.it · letsmarche.it
