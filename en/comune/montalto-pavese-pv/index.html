What to see in Montalto Pavese

Borgo on the hills of Oltrepò Pavese dominated by a castle of the sixteenth century, one of the best preserved in the area; just outside, the view of the Costa del Vento offers a 360 degree view of the hills.

Belvedere della Costa del Vento e panchina gigante

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The viewpoint in Madonna del Vento, also called Costa del Vento, houses the giant yellow bench n. 161, with 360 degree views of the Oltrepò hills. From the center take the SP 66 towards Lirio-Broni for about 800 m, then turn right to Belvedere-Madonna del Vento up to the chapel (about 1 km) and finally a few hundred meters of dirt road uphill.

  • Where Località Madonna del Vento, Montalto Pavese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitpavia.com
