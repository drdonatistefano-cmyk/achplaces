Pieve di Santa Maria di Gorto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On the hill between Cella and Agrons, it is already mentioned in 1119 in the act of foundation of the abbey of Moggio and the site could go back to the eighth century; on the same hill stood the castle of Agrons, of which the isolated bell tower would be a tower. After the fire of 1430 it was rebuilt from 1431 by the foreman Stefano di Simone di Mena and enlarged in 1722 by the brothers Zamolo of Portis; it is a basilica with three naves on stone pillars, with frescoes of the XII-XIII centuries with the parable of the ten virgins and a rich set of liturgical objects. Visits on request, every day.

Museo della Pieve di Gorto (Museu da Plêf)

Museums · Old masters

Opened in 1973 in a room next to the parish church with archaeological and lapidary section (the findings of Agrons of the IV-XVI centuries), since 2010 it has a section in the former social dairy Agrons-Cella (1909-1911), where there are also some dairy equipment: textiles, wooden statues, silverware, vestments and finds of San Martino di Ovaro. The main piece is the wooden group of St. Martin who gives the mantle to the poor, late work of Domenico da Tolmezzo (before 1507), who found the original polychrome with the restoration of 1981-1982. It is part of the CarniaMusei network.

Monte Zoncolan

Nature and landscape · Mountains

The Carnic Alps are one of the hardest climbs in Europe and one of the most feared stages of the Giro d'Italia. In summer you can climb by bike or on foot, in winter there are 23 km of slopes served by 11 lifts up to 1,970 meters, snowshoeing and snowshoeing.
