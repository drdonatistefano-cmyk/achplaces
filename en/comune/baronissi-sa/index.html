What to see in Baronissi

Irno Valley City 6 km from Salerno, established as a municipality by Gioacchino Murat in 1810: the Convent of the SS. Trinity with the FRac contemporary art museum, a center made of farmhouses between the church of the SS. Salvatore and Villa Farina, and the WWF oasis of Tenimare.

Museo FRaC – Fondo Regionale d'Arte Contemporanea

Museums · Modern art

Opened on 29 September 2002 in the Convent of the SS. Trinity and recognized Museum of regional interest in 2010, collects painting, sculpture, graphics, photography and multimedia works of Italian art and bell from the second half of the twentieth century. Among the funds: almost all Barisani's graphic work (from the period ...Mac ... 1950-58 to organic abstraction), about fifty drawings and watercolours by Guido Gambone (1945-69) and 22 works by Peter Ruta made in Italy between 1947 and 1963. The price depends on the exhibition in progress; barrier-free.

the MiC card (updated to 2020) indicates closed every day; check openings with the Municipality (089 828210, cultura@comune.baronissi.sa.it)

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Convento della SS. Trinità

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Convent on the Monticello, beyond the Vallone di Sava, founded in the sixteenth century on medieval Franciscan roots: it preserves paintings of the eighteenth century, a Franciscan cloister and wooden sculptures of Nicola Fumo, native of Saragnano. It hosts the FRAC museum.

  • Where Via Convento 2, Baronissi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.baronissi.sa.it · fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Parco naturale Diecimare

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Oasis managed by the WWF that extends over about 440 hectares in the municipal territory, towards Capo Saragnano.

  • Where Saragnano, Baronissi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.baronissi.sa.it

Casali storici, SS. Salvatore e Villa Farina

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historic center is a group of farmhouses (House Smoke, Casa Mari, Casa Napoli, Casal Sinscalci) between the church of the SS. Salvatore, on the hill of Saragnano, and Villa Farina, residence of the Farina family with the coat of arms of Decio Farina.

  • Where Saragnano, Baronissi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.baronissi.sa.it
