What to see in Collelongo

Borgo della Vallelonga, the ancient Transaqua Valley, between woods and mountains on the border with the National Park of Abruzzo, Lazio and Molise, at altitudes between 850 and over 1600 meters. It preserves the historic center with walls, doors, tower and square, the Archaeological Civic Museum in Palazzo Botticelli and the Museum of peasant civilization and work.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village preserves the typical architecture with stone houses, narrow and ups and downs and wooden gates, and the structures of the small medieval centers: walls of surroundings, urban doors, a tower, the square and the main church.

  • Where Collelongo (AQ)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Civico Archeologico (Palazzo Botticelli)

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

The MAC, housed with the municipal library in Palazzo Botticelli, is the place to know the archaeological heritage of the Vallelonga, inhabited for centuries by different populations: evidence of prehistoric, Italian, Roman and medieval settlements, partly destroyed by the earthquake of the Marsica of 1915. Free admission; the municipality has two different times (updating April 2025), to be checked before the visit.

  • Where Palazzo Botticelli, Collelongo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website comune.collelongo.aq.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.collelongo.aq.it

Museo della civiltà contadina e del lavoro

Museums · Themed

Collection of objects of peasant life, listed in a catalogue with detailed captions, designed to revive memories and make young people discover a world that seems very far away in time. Not indicated hours: contact the MiC card number (0863 948678).

  • Where Via Stretta, Collelongo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Vallelonga

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The valley, the ancient Transaquana Valley, stretches between woods and mountains of the Apennines on the border with the National Park of Abruzzo, Lazio and Molise, between 850 and over 1600 meters of altitude, with wide views and variety of flora and fauna.

  • Where Vallelonga, Collelongo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
