What to see in Montelupone

Borgo on top of a hill between Macerata and the coast, with the intact walls, the ancient gates and Piazza del Comune dominated by the crenellated tower of the Palazzo del Podestà; at the foot of the hill the Romanesque abbey of San Firmano.

5 places to visit in Montelupone, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Abbazia di San Firmano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Benedictine abbey founded in the late 10th century (first abbot Sign in 986) and rebuilt in 1256 in bricks with three naves; very elevated presbytery with imposing staircase and crypt with a polychrome terracotta statue of the 15th century Saint attributed to the Della Robbia and Roman materials of reuse.

  • Where San Firmano, Montelupone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Centro storico e mura

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village rises on top of a hill and preserves the original layout, with the walls still intact, the ancient doors and stone paved roads.

  • Where Centro storico, Montelupone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Palazzo del Podestà e Torre civica

History and architecture · Squares and old town

The crenellated tower of the Palazzo del Podestà dominates Piazza del Comune; the palace houses the civic art gallery.

  • Where Piazza del Comune, Montelupone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Museo d'Arti e Mestieri Antichi

Museums · Themed

In the basement of the Palazzo Comunale, eight rooms inaugurated in 1998 with tools of artisan and agricultural work, objects, photographs and clothes of the Marche hills; among the pieces the working mechanism of the clock of the civic tower, two film projectors in use until the Sixties and the reconstruction of a nineteenth century printing.

  • Where Via Ludovico Pochini, Montelupone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
