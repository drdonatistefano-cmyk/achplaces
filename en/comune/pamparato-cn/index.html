What to see in Pamparato

Borgo della Val Casotto in the Monregalese Valleys, with 23 fountains named after women: in the upper part the eighteenth-century castle of the Marquis Cordero, now Town Hall, the parish church of San Biagio and the Baroque oratory of Sant'Antonio; among the chestnuts the chapel of San Bernardo with frescoes from 1482, in Serra the Museum of the Uses of Mountain People and in Valcasotto a stone mill of the Napoleonic era.

8 places to visit in Pamparato, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo degli usi e dei costumi della gente di montagna

Museums · Themed

In the hamlet of Serra, the ecomuseum collects the ethnographic material of Val Casotto: work tools and objects of everyday life of the past.

  • Where Frazione Serra, Pamparato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.pamparato.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Cappella di San Bernardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Hidden among chestnut trees about a kilometer from the centre, it probably dates back to the early 11th century; the frescoes, dated 1482, depict the Christ Pantocrator, the symbols of the four Evangelists and scenes of the life of St Bernard.

  • Where A circa 1 km dal centro, Pamparato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.pamparato.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Parrocchiale di San Biagio e Oratorio di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

San Biagio, begun in 1648 on a project by Giovenale Boetto, has a major altar of Genoese Baroque and frescoes and decorations by Giovanni Borgna made between 1881 and 1894. Next, the oratory of Sant'Antonio in Piedmontese baroque, started by Francesco Gallo in the early eighteenth century and completed between 1737 and 1740 by the Luganese foreman Giuseppe Scala.

  • Where Parte alta del paese, di fronte al castello, Pamparato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.pamparato.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Santuario dell'Assunta

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Dedicated to the Blessed Virgin of the Graces and St. Peter, it was built by the pampers in the first half of the seventeenth century to ask for protection from the plague.

  • Where Frazione Arotte, Pamparato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.pamparato.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Valcasotto e il mulino a pietra

Local life and traditions · Crafts

In the hamlet of Valcasotto, with the parish church of San Ludovico king of France in 1666, an ancient stone mill from the Napoleonic era is still in operation and mature cheeses are produced in underground premises.

  • Where Frazione Valcasotto, Pamparato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · comune.pamparato.cn.it

Area carsica del Monte Savino

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Area protected by the Regional Parks Plan, known for sinkholes and sinkholes; the main caves are the Tana del Forno (1,300 m of development, 204 m of depth), the Conca and the Tana delle Turbiglie (over 800 m of development).

  • Where Monte Savino, Pamparato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.pamparato.cn.it · visitcuneese.it
