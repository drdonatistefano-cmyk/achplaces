Cattedrale di Santa Maria Annunziata e cripta di San Magno

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Paid entry

Built between 1071 and 1105, with Romanesque bell tower and cosmatesque floor; the crypt of San Magno has a cycle of frescoes from the thirteenth century with the creation, the stories of the Ark, the Apocalypse and the life of the patron, and a passage leads to the frescoed oratory of San Tommaso Becket. Here Saint Bernard (1174) and Saint Clare (1255) were canonized.

  • Where Via Papa Leone XIII, Anagni
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Museo della Cattedrale

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

It houses the Treasure, the library, the capitular hall, the sacristies, the lapidary, an archaeological collection and valuable fabrics.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Palazzo di Bonifacio VIII

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

Built at the beginning of the thirteenth century by the Counts and passed to the Caetans in 1297, with large frescoed rooms; here in 1303 the emissaries of Philip IV of France attempted to capture Pope Bonifacio VIII, the episode known as the "slap of Anagni" .

  • Where Anagni
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Palazzo della Ragione (Palazzo d'Iseo)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Built between 1159 and 1163 by Jacopo d'Iseo, it was the seat of the city governor and today houses municipal offices and cultural initiatives.
