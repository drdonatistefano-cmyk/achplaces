What to see in Alseno

Municipality of the Piacenza plain crossed by via Emilia, stretch of the ancient Via Francigena: here stands the Cistercian abbey of Chiaravalle della Colomba, founded by St Bernard in 1135-1136, with one of the best preserved thirteenth-century cloisters and the flowered Corpus Domini; in Castelnuovo Fogliani the residence of the Sforza Fogliani extended on a project by Vanvitelli.

Abbazia di Chiaravalle della Colomba

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded around 1135 by Saint Bernard of Chiaravalle at the request of the bishop of Piacenza Arduino and completed in the following century; the monks, removed in Napoleonic age, returned in 1937. The Basilica of the Assumption, in bricks with three naves, marks the passage from the Romanesque to the Gothic; in the octagonal chapel near the sacristy frescoes of fourteenth-century influence of Giotto. The 13th-century cloister, intact on the entire perimeter and built according to a complex numerical symbolism, is among the best preserved of its time; completes the complex the chapter room. For the Corpus Domini (between May and June) the nave is covered with a carpet of petals that reproduces paintings and sacred images. Open 8.30-12 and 14.30-18.30, on Thursdays only in the morning; free parking; shop with liquors and herbal teas of monks.

Museo dell'Abbazia di Chiaravalle

Museums · Themed

Historic route set up in the ancient dormitory of monks, with illustrated panels and teaching materials: the agricultural economy of the monastery, the converts, the grandeurs and the service buildings, the daily life of the monks and the Cistercian architecture. Open every day 8.30-12 and 14.30-18.30, without reservation for individuals; guided tours of groups and schools by reservation.

Palazzo Sforza Fogliani e chiesa di San Biagio, Castelnuovo Fogliani

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The medieval village of Castelnuovo Fogliani has grown around a fortress with a tower dated 1377, transformed into a villa in the mid-18th century designed by Luigi Vanvitelli, to which the nearby church of San Biagio is attributed. Neoclassical facade, a hall frescoed by Giuseppe Natali, an oval salon richly decorated with stucco and an Italian garden with geometric parts; in 1925 the last Duchess Sforza Fogliani donated the complex to the Holy See. The Municipality organizes guided tours of about 80 minutes (church, frescoed rooms and garden) on some Sundays from April to October, at 3.30 pm and at 5 pm, with reservation required at 0523 945500.

Castello dei Landi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

It rises on a hill in the center of the village; documented since 1186, when they built it Siclerio dell'Andito (Landi) and Savino Vicedomino, it was infeudated to the Visconti in 1405, transformed into a residential palace by the Landi in the eighteenth century and passed to the Sforza Fogliani in the nineteenth century.

today hosts a kindergarten (Emilia-Romagna Tourism)
