What to see in Castro dei Volsci

Medieval village of the Ciociaria on a plateau overlooking the Sacco Valley, called "Balcone della Ciociaria": you enter from the Gate of the Valley between stone alleys, the church of Santa Oliva, the Clock Tower with the exhibition on Nino Manfredi and the viewpoint with the monument to the Mother Ciociara; lower the Archaeological Civic Museum and the Roman villa of Casale di Madonna del Piano.

Centro storico medievale e porte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Well-preserved medieval center to which you can access from the Gate of the Valley; the original walls are seen from the turn of Via Civita, the outer belt is crossed from the gates of the Valley, Iron and Olive and the inner one from the Gate of the Clock. Alleys and stairs in stone with portals, ogival arches and ancient shops.

  • Where Porta della Valle, Castro dei Volsci
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Belvedere e Monumento alla Mamma Ciociara

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the foot of the Fortress, just above the church of Santa Oliva, the monument reminds women victims of violence during the Second World War; from the panoramic point of view the view extends over the Valley of Sacco to the borders with Campania and Abruzzo.

  • Where Piazza San Pietro, Castro dei Volsci
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Oliva e resti della Rocca

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

On top of the village, inside the original walls, next to the remains of the Fortress of San Pietro dei Colonna (today Piazza San Pietro); the altar was consecrated in 1537.

  • Where Piazza San Pietro, Castro dei Volsci
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Inside the walls, of uncertain origin, perhaps the second half of the twelfth century; it preserves a marble carved with lilies and an organ of the eighteenth century.

  • Where Centro storico, Castro dei Volsci
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Torre dell'Orologio, mostra Nino Manfredi

Museums · Themed

The tower is today a multifunctional cultural centre with a permanent exhibition dedicated to actor Nino Manfredi, born in Castro dei Volsci in 1921; the visit is on reservation. Near Piazza IV Novembre there is a monument to the actor and a bas-relief of Umberto Cufrini realized for the centenary of the birth.

  • Where Torre dell'Orologio, Castro dei Volsci
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Chiesa e monastero di San Nicola

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Just outside the center, in the plain of the Casale: the monastery was founded by the Benedictines between 542 and 552, about twenty years after Montecassino; the oldest walls of the church preserve Byzantine frescoes with scenes from the Old and New Testaments.

  • Where Località Casale, Castro dei Volsci
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Founded in 1994 in the former middle school of Madonna del Piano, it exhibits about 700 exhibits from prehistory to the early Middle Ages in eight sections plus a lapidary, especially from the excavations of the villa of Casale di Madonna del Piano; among the main pieces the funeral inscription of the child Artemisio (III century A.D.) and a plastic of the settlement on a 1:50 scale. Closed on Monday.

  • Where Via Antonio Carnevale 24, Madonna del Piano, Castro dei Volsci
  • Entry €2.60, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.castrodeivolsci.fr.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Area archeologica di Casale di Madonna del Piano

History and architecture · Archaeology

Remains of a Roman villa, born as a rustic villa in the Republican age and became between II and III century AD luxury residence, probably of a senatorial family; there are also a spa, a early Christian basilica and a high medieval necropolis of the VI-VII century.

  • Where Madonna del Piano, Castro dei Volsci
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.castrodeivolsci.fr.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Botteghe della Regina Camilla

Local life and traditions · Crafts · Free entry

Circuit of artisan workshops in the historic centre, open on Sundays all year except from 6 January to the first Sunday of March: wood and ceramics, jewelry, lace, watercolors.

  • Where Centro storico, Castro dei Volsci
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
