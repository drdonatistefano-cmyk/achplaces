What to see in Monte Cerignone

Borgo del Montefeltro in the upper valley of the Conca, dominated by the fortress on a ridge of tuff erected in the twelfth century by the first counts of Montefeltro and partly rebuilt by Francesco di Giorgio Martini at the end of the fifteenth century; in the village churches with works attributed to Bartolomeo Vivarini and, on Mount Faggiola, the sanctuary of Santa Maria in Recluso.

Rocca e Museo della Rocca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built in the 12th century for the first counts of Montefeltro on a spur of tuff and partly rebuilt by Francesco di Giorgio Martini at the end of the 15th century; consolidated by the Malatesta, it passed to the Montefeltro after their defeat in 1464. On three floors: basement living room with barrel vault, loggia, conference room and hanging garden with bust of Uguccione della Faggiola, and the noble floor with Renaissance windows overlooking Monte Carpegna and the valley of the Conca. Opening on request (tel. 0722 75350).

  • Where Via Battelli, Monte Cerignone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Centro storico e vie acciottolate

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The paved streets of the village go up towards the fortress on the ridge of tuff; downstream the village stretches at the level of the river, in the upper valley of the Conca. In July the castle hosts the Mons Cerignonis reenactment with procession and egg palio.

  • Where Piazza Clementina, Monte Cerignone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Chiese di Santa Maria del Soccorso, San Biagio e Santa Caterina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Santa Maria del Soccorso preserves a Madonna enthroned and two paintings attributed to Bartolomeo Vivarini; San Biagio an Ecce Homo of the same Vivarini; Saint Catherine was built by the Knights of Malta.

  • Where Monte Cerignone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Santuario di Santa Maria in Recluso

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

It is reached by going up Mount Faggiola; to build it materials of the ancient temple dedicated to Juno were used.

Sentiero nel bosco di lecci verso Villagrande

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint
