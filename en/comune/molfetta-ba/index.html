What to see in Molfetta

A seaside town on the coast north of Bari, with the fishing port and the old elliptical centre on a peninsula: the old Cathedral of San Corrado with pyramidal domes, the Baroque cathedral with the diocesan museum, the Templar Hall and the Pulo, a large karst dolina inhabited in the Neolithic.

7 places to visit in Molfetta, in the province of Bari (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo Vecchio di San Corrado

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

Overlooking the port, built between the middle and the end of the XII century and dedicated to the patron: the largest Romanesque-Apulian church with domes in axis, with two bell towers, one of which signalled the arrival of the ships. Inside with three naves a Saracen saintly waterworks of the 12th century and a stone high relief of the 13th century under the main altar.

  • Where Via Chiesa Vecchia, Molfetta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Centro antico e porto

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The elliptical hamlet on a small peninsula, crossed by narrow alleys with ancient buildings, and next to the port full of fishing boats and shipyards, heart of the city's maritime vocation.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta e Museo diocesano

Places of worship · Cathedrals

The Baroque cathedral, the main church of the city; in the bishop's palace next to the diocesan museum with sacred works of art and Neolithic finds from the Pule, and the library of the seminary in a frescoed hall at the end of the nineteenth century, with over 40,000 volumes and the Red Book of town history (1323-1507).

  • Where Molfetta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
