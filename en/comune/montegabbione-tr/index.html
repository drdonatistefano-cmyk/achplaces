What to see in Montegabbione

Medieval village of Orvieto among olive groves and vineyards, with castle, walls and towers; in the territory the castles of Montegiove and Castel di Fiori and especially the Scarzuola, Franciscan convent transformed by Tomaso Buzzi between 1958 and 1978 in an ideal city of seven theatres.

La Scarzuola e Città Buzziana

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

According to tradition, the name comes from the hut of "scarza" built by St. Francis, who suspended there in 1218; in 1282 Nerio di Bulgaruccio dei Conti di Montegiove had it built church and convent, and in the apse of the church of the Santissima Annunziata a fresco depicts the saint in levitation, among his first images. Abandoned by the friars in the eighteenth century, the complex was purchased in 1956 by the Milanese architect Tomaso Buzzi, who built his ideal city between 1958 and 1978 there, a theatre machine of seven theatres culminating in the Acropolis, with the Tower of Babel, the Musical Scala of the seven octaves and the Scala of Jacob; the opera was never finished. Information and reservations on lascarzuola.com.

Borgo di Montegabbione

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village was born around the castle, built around the year 1000, and preserves the original layout with the medieval walls, the entrance tower of the fifteenth century and two sighting towers on the central square; perched on a hill, it probably served as an observatory towards Orvieto.

  • Where Centro storico, Montegabbione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Chiesa della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1625 on a pre-existing chapel, just outside the walls under the hill; Renaissance outside, with a Latin cross with cylindrical dome, and Baroque inside, where there is a 16th century fresco of the Madonna del Latte.

  • Where Montegabbione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta in Cielo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish church built since 1873 extending an ancient circular stone chapel with bell tower, perhaps part of the castle, partly on the area of the old cemetery; in Byzantine style, it is decorated by the Perugians Francesco Biscarini and Raffaele Angeletti, with three terracotta altars and a pipe organ of Nicomede Agati di Pistoia.

  • Where Montegabbione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Castel di Fiori

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Borgo in the woods, mentioned already in 1350 and recently restored, dominated by the castle with the tower of the early thirteenth century and surrounded by medieval walls. In the square the church of Santa Maria Maddalena, rebuilt between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, and the statue of the Archangel Michele carved in 1937 by Michele Perla.

  • Where Castel di Fiori, Montegabbione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Castello di Montegiove

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

12th century castle born as a fortification on a hill between Orvieto and Perugia, disputed by the captains of Ventura; today it is a private residence, indicated as open to the public all year round.
