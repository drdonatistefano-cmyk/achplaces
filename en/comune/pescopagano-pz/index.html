What to see in Pescopagano

Borgo above the valley of the Ofanto with the ruins of the castle, eighteenth-nineteenth-century noble palaces rebuilt after the earthquake of 1694 and the clock tower on the ancient Porta Sibilla.

3 places to visit in Pescopagano, in the province of Potenza (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre dell'orologio e Porta Sibilla

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The clock tower rises on the ancient Porta Sibilla and houses a sculpture of Giano bifronte, one of the oldest Roman gods.

  • Where Piazza della Sibilla, Pescopagano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it · comune.pescopagano.pz.it

Palazzi nobiliari (Laviano, Scioscia, Pascale)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Palaces noble of the village, many built in the eighteenth-nineteenth century after the earthquake of 1694 that destroyed much of the village; one of the Laviano palaces was designed by Piacentini. In the village there are also neoclassical portals.

  • Where Centro storico, Pescopagano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it · comune.pescopagano.pz.it

Ruderi del castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Remains of a 15th century castle on a cliff overlooking the valley of Ofanto; there is little left. The first fortress was born after the Saracen attacks of the IX-X century (Castrum Petrae Paganae).

  • Where Pescopagano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: comune.pescopagano.pz.it · basilicataturistica.it
