What to see in Arborea

Arborea is a city founded in 1928 on the pond of Sassu just reclaimed, with regular plant and Art Nouveau and Neo-Gothic buildings; the Museum of Reclamation tells the story and preserves the finds of the Roman necropolis of S'Ungroni. On the coast there are the lagoon of S'Ena Arrubia, oasis Ramsar for birdwatching, and the beach with pinewood of the Marina of Arborea.

MUBA Museo della Bonifica

Museums · City history · Paid entry

It is located in the restored historical mill and tells about the reclamation of the territory, the rural civilization, the electrification of Sardinia and urban history; it preserves the Historical Civic Archives. The historical section presents architecture and architects of the city with vintage films and a touch table with about 800 historical images; the archaeological section exhibits the materials of the nuragic sacred well of Orri and, in the Antiquarium, more than 300 finds of the necropolis of S'Ungroni from the Punic to the Byzantine age. Open Saturday 14:30-19:30, Sunday 8:30-13:30 and Monday 14:00-19:00.

Città di fondazione e Chiesa del Cristo Redentore

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Founded in 1928 in the center of the pond of Sassu reclaimed, renamed "Mussolinia di Sardegna" in 1930 and called Arborea since 1944. It has the typical regular plan of the founding cities, with liberty and neo-Gothic buildings and, along the avenues, the offices of the reclamation. The parish church of Christ the Redeemer, in Tyrolean style, overlooks the main square.

  • Where Centro, Arborea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Stagno di S'Ena Arrubia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

A shallow lagoon (from a metre to a metre and a half), the rest of the ancient Sassu pond reclaimed between 1934 and 1937, today an oasis of wildlife protection Ramsar (1977) and a site of community interest. Red heron, Turkish fistion and kingfisher nest there; you can also see sultan chicken, garzette, falcon fisherman and, during the migrations, pink flamingos. It is reached from SP 49 turning on the 30th Street, about 6 km from the center.

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Marina di Arborea

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches

Large sandy beach surrounded by an extensive coastal pine forest, near the lagoon of S'Ena Arrubia; between pine forest and beach you can camp.
