What to see in Terranova di Pollino

The village of the Lucanian side called "Porta del Parco" of Pollino, founded in the 16th century by the Pignatelli as a fief of the State of Noia (now Noepoli); it preserves sixteenth-century churches and noble palaces, and from the village you can reach the lake Duglia, the opholitic storms and the source of the Catusa.

Chiesa di San Francesco di Paola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the XVI century dedicated to the patron saint of Terranova; restored, it preserves the original door made of pine (pine of Heldreich) and a 16th century painting by the Lucan painter Attilio De Laurentis with the Madonna of Grace and the Saints Carlo Borromeo and Francesco d'Assisi.

  • Where Terranova di Pollino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · basilicataturistica.it

Centro storico, cappelle e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village, founded by the Pignatelli in the 16th century to attract settlers, preserves the sixteenth-century chapels of the Madonna della Grazia and Sant'Antonio and the palaces Virgallita and Rusciani, the latter with a beautiful internal staircase.

  • Where Terranova di Pollino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · basilicataturistica.it

Santuario della Madonna della Pietà

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

A few kilometers from the village, on the edge of the forest near Santa Maria della Montagna; dates back to the sixteenth century, it was rebuilt in the twentieth century and then renovated. Behind the altar there are frescoes by a local painter anonymously with Saints Peter and Paul and the Virgin with the Child; she preserves a painting from the sixteenth century with the Madonna delle Grazie.

  • Where Località Madonna della Pietà, Terranova di Pollino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · basilicataturistica.it

Lago Duglia

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Laghetto 1375 meters at the foot of the Serra di Crispo, called "Giardino degli dei di Signori," surrounded by beech woods and abetinas; near the nearby Iannace Plan (1650 m) some trees exceed 30 meters, and from there the Grande Porta del Pollino is reached in about an hour and a half of walk.

  • Where Lago Duglia, Terranova di Pollino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it · italia.it

Timpa delle Murge e Timpa di Pietrasasso

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Otiolithic outings, fragments of oceanic crust brought to the surface by movements that formed the Apennines, trails. The Timpa delle Murge (1441 m), of great scientific interest, has basaltic rocks born from magma of marine seabed, today covered by holly; the Timpa di Pietrasasso (1362 m) is a spur of holiolithic rock more than 50 meters high. On the valley also dominates the large wall of the Timpa Falconara.

  • Where Timpa delle Murge, Terranova di Pollino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · basilicataturistica.it

Sorgente e fiume Catusa

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

The source of the Catusa is considered one of the most beautiful of the Park of Pollino; along the river an evocative landscape of beech trees.

  • Where Catusa, Terranova di Pollino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it · italia.it
