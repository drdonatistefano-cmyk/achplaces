What to see in San Giovanni in Fiore

The largest town in Sila, at 1,050 meters, was founded around the Monasterium Florense founded in 1189 by Gioacchino da Fiore: the Florense Abbey, which houses the demological Museum of the social history of Silana, is the heart of the visit; the village is also known for the processing of wood and wrought iron and for the hand-knotted Armenian carpets.

Abbazia Florense

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Founded in 1189 by Gioacchino da Fiore as Monasterium Florense, approved in 1196 by Celestino III; the 13th-century church, renovated in the 16th-17th century and restored twice in the 20th century, has a large portal of 1220 with palm-shaped capitals and archivolti with oak leaves, which bears traces of the fire of 1799. A unique long nave in bare stone, pronounced transept, baroque altar with the wooden statue of St. John the Baptist and rosettes on the back wall; in the crypt the urn with the remains of Joachim. In the halls is the International Center for Joachimite Studies (1982). Free admission; according to italia.it open every day 9-20.

  • Where Via Monastero 9, San Giovanni in Fiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.sangiovanniinfiore.cs.it

Museo demologico dell'economia, del lavoro e della storia sociale silana

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 1984 on the ground floor and first floor of the east wing of the Florense Abbey, it has a thematic itinerary on the folk traditions of Sila: agricultural and pastoral tools, artisan crafts, domestic furnishings, religious and musical culture, iconography, with panels in Italian and dialect. It contains photographic, audiovisual and documentary archives, including the Saverio Marra Photographic Fund (1894-1978). According to the MiC card (updated 2024) open 9-19 from mid-September to mid-June and 10-20 in summer.

  • Where Via Monastero, c/o Abbazia Florense, San Giovanni in Fiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.sangiovanniinfiore.cs.it · italia.it

Centro storico e botteghe artigiane

Local life and traditions · Crafts · Free entry

The first settlement was built in the first half of the 16th century around the Florentine monastery; the village, the largest in Sila, is known for its wood and wrought iron processing and for its hand-knotted Armenian carpets.

  • Where Centro storico, San Giovanni in Fiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
