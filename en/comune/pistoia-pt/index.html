What to see in Pistoia

The "city of enchanted stone" with one of the most beautiful squares in Italy, the Romanesque churches with white and green stripes and the Robbiano del Ceppo frieze; the "Santiago d'Italia."

25 places to visit in Pistoia, in the province of Pistoia (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers 10 of the 26 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Piazza del Duomo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The political and religious heart of the city, with the Cathedral of San Zeno, the Baptistery, the Palace of Bishops, the Town Hall, the Palace of Pretorio and the Tower of Catilina; in July it hosts Pistoia Blues.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Pistoia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Cattedrale di San Zeno e Altare d'argento

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The Romanesque-Pisan cathedral with the crucifix of Coppo di Marcovaldo, the reliquary of Ghiberti and the silver altar of San Jacopo (1287-1456), masterpiece of the Gothic goldsmithery, with a relic of the Apostle from Santiago.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Pistoia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
