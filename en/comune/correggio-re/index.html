What to see in Correggio

Città dei Signori da Correggio e patria del pittore Antonio Allegri: porticos of corso Mazzini, the Renaissance Palazzo dei Principi with the Redeemer of Mantegna, the Basilica of San Quirino and the Asioli Theatre.

8 places to visit in Correggio, in the province of Reggio Emilia (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo civico «Il Correggio» – Palazzo dei Principi

Museums · Old masters · Free entry

On the noble floor of the Renaissance palace started after 1500 for Francesca di Brandenburg: coffered ceiling of 1508, the Redeemer of Andrea Mantegna (1493), two paintings attributed to Correggio, nine Flemish tapestries of the sixteenth century, coins of the Mint of Correggio and finds of the territory. Free admission; open Saturday afternoon, Sunday and holidays, on other days by reservation; closed in August.

  • Where Corso Cavour 7, Correggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.correggio.re.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Basilica dei Santi Quirino e Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built between 1513 and 1587 on a project attributed to Vignola, with the fourteenth-century bell tower that was part of the first walls; inside a painting by Domenico Fetti and an ivory box of the fifteenth century.

  • Where Correggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Corso Mazzini e i portici

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

A wide porticoed road like a square, very colorful: there are the town hall with the bust of Garibaldi di Vela and the house rebuilt in 1755 where the Correggio was born and died; at the end the monument to the fallen of Bistolfi.

  • Where Corso Mazzini, Correggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Teatro Bonifazio Asioli

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Theatre of 1852 with neoclassical façade by Giuseppe Aimi, interior decoration completed in 1898; still in operation.

Santuario della Madonna della Rosa

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

The real sanctuary of Correggio, built in 1626, with elegant façade adorned with festoons and bucrani; open on Sunday morning, on other days on request.

Chiese di San Giuseppe Calasanzio e Santa Chiara

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

St. Joseph Calasanzio, of the mid-500s, has altars in stucco and marble and paintings from the sixteenth to the nineteenth century; Santa Chiara has a façade of 1666 of Borrominian inspiration.
