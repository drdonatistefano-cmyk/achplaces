What to see in Castelnuovo Berardenga

Fortified village of Siena after Montaperti (1260), between the hills of Chianti and Crete Senesi, in a territory full of castles and Romanesque parishes.

5 places to visit in Castelnuovo Berardenga, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa Chigi Saracini e Torre civica

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The complex was born after the demolition of the walls at the end of the eighteenth century, with Italian garden and a nineteenth-century park of botanical interest; next to the civic tower, the only surviving of the sects of 1526.

  • Where Castelnuovo Berardenga
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo del Paesaggio

Museums · Themed

A unique museum of its kind, in a former public slaughterhouse of the late nineteenth century, dedicated to the transformations of the landscape and the relationship between man and nature.

  • Where Castelnuovo Berardenga
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chianti Sculpture Park

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In Pievasciata installations and contemporary sculptures of artists from all over the world in a forest of oaks and holm oaks, along a ring path.

San Gusmè

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small fortified village of the fourteenth century above the valley towards Siena, with the church of Santi Cosma and Damiano and the playful statue of Luca Cava.

  • Where San Gusmè, Castelnuovo Berardenga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Certosa di Pontignano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

One of the great medieval religious presences of the territory, together with the parish church of San Pietro in Cerreto and the monastery of San Salvatore in Montebona.
