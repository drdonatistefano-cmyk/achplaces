What to see in Partanna

The centre of the Belice Valley is dominated by the Gryfeo Castle, one of the best preserved medieval fortresses in western Sicily, home to the Museum of the prehistory of Belice; in June the historic centre is covered with the Infiorata flower carpets.

Castello Grifeo e Museo della preistoria del Belìce

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The castle dominates the historic centre and is one of the best preserved medieval fortresses in western Sicily. The museum exhibits above all the excavations of the excavations started in the eighties in the Strait district, known for the Neolithic ditches, and of the necropolis of Belice with grotticella tombs and chamber of the middle and late Bronze, including ceramics of the Naro-Partanna style and bell towers; didactic panels tell the peoples of Belice before the Greeks. It also houses an archive of parchments from the fourteenth century donated by the Turrisi-Griffeo family and, in the armory, a gallery with a fourteenth-century altarpiece and a San Tommaso da Villanova attributed to Silvestre Ratto da Mazara.

The MiC card (updated to March 2021) indicates museum and ticket office closed every day; the price is that of the card

  • Where Piazza Benvenuto Graffeo, Partanna
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitsicily.info · parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it

Chiesa Madre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

With Santa Ninfa, Partanna preserves examples of rationalist and religious architecture of reconstruction after the earthquake of Belice, including the Mother Church designed by Paolo Di Stefano.

  • Where Partanna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info
