What to see in Nuoro

The Sardinian Athens by Grazia Deledda, Sebastiano Satta and Francesco Ciusa: the birthplace of the writer, the MAN, the Museo Ciusa al Tribu, the Ethnographic Museum Sardo, the Archaeological Museum Asproni and Mount Ortobene with the statue of the Redeemer.

9 places to visit in Nuoro, in the province of Nuoro (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Nuoro in the guide with map and filters

Museo Deleddiano – Casa natale di Grazia Deledda

Museums · House museums

The birthplace of the Nobel Prize-winning writer, in the district of Santu Pedru, which preserves the memory of an artist who opened Sardinia to the world.

  • Where Via Grazia Deledda 42, Nuoro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

MAN – Museo d'Arte della Provincia di Nuoro

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

International temporary exhibitions and permanent collections of the major Sardinian artists of the twentieth century, including Nivola, Ciusa and Maria Lai.

  • Where Via Satta 27, Nuoro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it · cultura.gov.it

TRIBU – Museo Ciusa

Museums · Modern art

Next to the cathedral, from a panoramic point you can access the Tribu space and the museum that exhibits the sculptures of Francesco Ciusa, first prize at the Venice Biennale of 1907, including the Mother of the killed.

  • Where Nuoro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Museo Etnografico Sardo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

On the hill of Sant'Onofrio, the most complete ethnographic exhibition of the island: early twentieth century clothes, jewelry, fabrics and traditional breads.

  • Where Colle di Sant'Onofrio, Nuoro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Museo archeologico nazionale «Asproni»

Museums · Archaeological

In via Mannu, about 150 meters from the cathedral, preceded by a garden.

  • Where Via Mannu, Nuoro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria della Neve

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The town cathedral in the heart of Nuoro, next to the Tribu and a few hundred meters from Piazza del Poeta.

  • Where Nuoro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Piazza del Poeta

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the district of Santu Pedru, the piazza-monument dedicated to Sebastiano Satta, where his house stands.

  • Where Santu Pedru, Nuoro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa della Solitudine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The little church at the foot of the Ortobene where the remains of Grazia Deledda rest.

  • Where Pendici del Monte Ortobene, Nuoro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Monte Ortobene e Redentore

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Nurse Mountain, 1,600 hectares inhabited by prehistory with domus de janas and parks like Sedda Ortai; 925 meters the bronze statue of the Redeemer of 1901, destination of the procession on the last Sunday of August.

  • Where Monte Ortobene, Nuoro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
