What to see in Cassano all'Ionio

The municipality of the plain of Sibari: the archaeological park where Sibari, Thurii and Copia overlap, the museum of the "Toro cosante" (cooking toro), the prehistoric caves of Sant'Angelo and the historic center between the castle and the Stone of San Marco.

8 places to visit in Cassano all'Ionio, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parco archeologico nazionale di Sibari

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the site of the achaea colony founded in the 7th century BC, one of the most prosperous of Magna Grecia, where Sibari, Thurii and Copy overlapped; the remains of the most recent cities are visible in the area called "Cavallo" .

  • Where Sibari, Cassano all'Ionio
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo archeologico nazionale della Sibaritide

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The remains of the Sibaritide from the protohistory to the Middle Ages, with the "Coscante toro" of the fifth century BC and the bronze armor and helmet of a fourth century BC Bruttia tomb; unique ticket with the park.

MiC card does not show opening times: check before visiting

  • Where Località Casa Bianca, Cassano all'Ionio
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
