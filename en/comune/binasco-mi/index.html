What to see in Binasco

To the south of Milan, Binasco preserves the Castello Visconteo of the fourteenth century, today seat of the Municipality, where Beatrice of Tenda was beheaded; in the village the eighteenth-century church of Saints John Baptist and Stefano and MUMAC, museum of coffee machines of the Cimbali Group.

Castello Visconteo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built in the fourteenth century as a fortification to defend Milan, with a square plan with four corner towers: the north-west one is in ruins and the south-east one is the only one still standing. Terracotta walls and wooden beams roof; the east side, the most visited, shows visconteee mullioned windows, ancient battlements and the Visconteo biscion over the original entrance. Beatrice of Tenda, the second wife of Philip Maria Visconti, was beheaded in her walls, falsely accused of adultery.

seat of municipal offices: the source does not indicate hours of visit of the interiors

Chiesa dei Santi Giovanni Battista e Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded in 1750 by architect Giulio Galliori, the works stopped in 1768 due to lack of funds and ended in 1783, date engraved on the choir; the façade, which had to be covered with stuccoes and marbles, remained unfinished. A unique nave with three chapels on each side, dome at the crossroads and interior entirely frescoed in the style of Lombard Rococo illusionism; in the apse a neoclassical circular marble temple with six Corinthian columns above the main altar.

  • Where Largo Andrea Loriga 3, Binasco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.binasco.mi.it

Sources: comune.binasco.mi.it

MUMAC, Museo della Macchina per Caffè

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Opened in 2012, on the centenary of the company, in the headquarters of the Cimbali Group: it is indicated as the largest contemporary museum in the world of professional coffee machines, with about 300 pieces from the Cimbali and Maltoni collections (over 100 exhibited) from the end of the nineteenth century to today, and one of the most rich documentary collections on coffee. Among the acquisitions the Faema bike by Guido Reybrouck of the 1960s; audio guide app in 10 languages, barrier-free.

visits only by reservation, Tuesday and Thursday 10-12 and 15-17, according to the Municipality

  • Where Via Pablo Neruda 2, Binasco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.mumac.it

Sources: mumac.it · comune.binasco.mi.it · museimpresa.com
