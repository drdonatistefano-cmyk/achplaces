What to see in Mandas

Trexenta Centre, in 1614 elevated by Philip III to Duchy, unique in Sardinia created by a Spanish king, and stop of the Green Train told by D. H. Lawrence in 1921: the Gothic-Catalan parish church of St. James, the ethnographic museum Is Lollas de is Aiaiusu, the museum of sacred art Peregrinatio Fidei, the convent of St. Anthony on the Roman road and the nuraghe Su Angiu.

Is Lollas de is Aiaiusu

Museums · Themed

Ethnographic house-museum, the rooms of the grandparents, in a manor house of the late eighteenth century a few steps from the medieval complex of Sant'Antonio: it reproduces the peasant house with the loom room, the bedroom, sa lolla (the goddess), the kitchen, the tool room, the wine warehouse and the oven room, and collects weaving, basketry, copper and wrought iron artefacts, still living crafts in the village.

Peregrinatio Fidei

Museums · Old masters

Museum of sacred art in the 17th-century church deconsecrated on the southern outskirts, dedicated first to the Virgin of the Rosary and then to St. Christopher, a single nave with three arches and wooden cover; it recalls the Way of Santiago for devotion to St. James, patron saint of the village. It exhibits polychrome statues from the 16th and 17th centuries and sacred furnishings recovered from St. James and the campestri churches, including works by the Genoese silversmith Luigi Montaldo and the Virgin sleeping on a canopy of carved and painted wood.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di San Giacomo Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Gothic-Catalan parish built between 1585 and 1605 by the picapedres Gontinio Pinna and Miguel Baldabella; on the portal the coat of arms of the Carroz, lords of the Duchy. Inside the nave with juniper beams, five chapels per side and presbytery with starry vault; it houses a wooden group with Crucifix, Madonna and St. John, polychrome statues of the seventeenth century, wooden altars of the eighteenth century, tabernacle and baptismal font in eighteenth-century marble. At the exit there is sa perda de sa bregungia, ancient medieval gogna.

Chiesa e convento di Sant'Antonio Abate e strada romana

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Medieval complex with church of the XII-XIII century and convent, overlooking a short visible stretch of the Roman road Karalis-Ulbia; in the courtyard there is also the museum Is Lollas.

Convento e chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

A seventeenth-century account of the minor friars near the museum Peregrinatio Fidei: the cloister contains frescoes with scenes of hagiographics and the Via Crucis and today hosts cultural events.

Nuraghe Su Angiu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

A nuragic five-tower complex, one of the almost 50 Nuragic sites in the territory; there was found a bronze ship today exhibited at the National Archaeological Museum of Cagliari.
