What to see in Busseto

5 places to visit in Busseto, in the province of Parma (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Busseto in the guide with map and filters

Teatro Giuseppe Verdi

History and architecture · Historic theatres · Paid entry

The small 19th century theatre in the Rocca Pallavicino, dedicated to the composer born a few kilometers, with audiences and decorated stages.

  • Where Piazza Verdi 10, Busseto
  • Entry Cumulative Home Christmas, Theatre and Casa Barezzi €11 whole (Ministry of Culture)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: castellidelducato.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Casa natale di Giuseppe Verdi

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The house of Roncole where Verdi was born on October 10, 1813 with a multimedia path told by the voice of the Child Master; near, the church where the organ played.

  • Where Località Roncole Verdi, Busseto
  • Entry €6 according to the Castelli del Ducato circuit; €5 according to the Ministry of Culture
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: castellidelducato.it · cultura.gov.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Casa Barezzi

Museums · House museums

The house of the patron and father-in-law of Verdi: in the hall, seat of the Philharmonic, the young composer performed for the first time in 1830; piano, portraits and letters autographs.

  • Where Via Roma 119, Busseto
  • Entry Cumulative Home Christmas, Theatre and Casa Barezzi €11 whole (Ministry of Culture)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Villa Pallavicino e Museo nazionale Giuseppe Verdi

Museums · Themed

The sixteenth-century villa with five chessboard bodies by the lords of Busseto, which houses a museum with the settings and atmospheres of Verdi's works.

  • Where Villa Pallavicino, Busseto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · parmawelcome.it

Collegiata di San Bartolomeo e Santa Maria degli Angeli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Gothic collegiate of 1436 wanted by Orlando Pallavicino and the Franciscan complex of 1470 with the famous Terracotta Compiantic.

  • Where Busseto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
