What to see in Castiadas

Municipality of Sarrabus, autonomous since 1986, born around the penal agricultural colony founded in 1875 and today museum; 13 kilometers of coast with beaches like Cala Pira, Cala Sinzias, Marina di San Pietro and Costa Rei, and behind the Oasis of the Seven Brothers with the Arch of the Angel.

Ex colonia penale e Museo del territorio

Museums · City history

In August 1875 thirty prisoners and seven officers landed in Cala Sinzias to clean up a marshy territory; the colony arrived to accommodate more than two thousand people, with workshops, joineries, hospital, pharmacy and post office, and closed definitively in 1956. Today you can visit the building of the management, which houses on two floors the museum of the territory on the history of the colony and the traditions of the Sarrabus, the prison structures, the villa of the director with garden, the church of San Basilide built by the prisoners at the end of the nineteenth century and the historical cemetery.

Cala Pira

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Arch of about 400 meters of fine white sand with pink reflections for the fragments of coral, dunes with centuries-old junipers and granite cliffs at the ends; on the north promontory, the Cappuccino rock, stands a sixteenth-century watchtower. Sea usually calm and shallow that slopes gently; refreshment point, charter boats and parking lots. Crowded in the middle of summer: better May, June, September or October.

  • Where SP18, Castiadas
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Marina di San Pietro

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

More than three kilometers of fine golden sand between Cala di Monte Turno and Cala Sinzias, with water from emerald green to turquoise, deep-sea suitable for snorkeling and diving (cernies and gorgonies) and an oasis of Mediterranean maquis behind; on the promontory stands the nuraghe Gibe Truttiri. Much of the coast is free.

  • Where Marina di San Pietro, Castiadas
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Cala Sinzias

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

About two kilometers of white and soft sand and clear water, with camping and camping areas behind; here landed in 1875 the first prisoners of the penal colony.

Costa Rei

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

About ten kilometers of fine golden sand; the stretch towards Muravera ends at the beach of Ginestre - Scoglio di Peppino, on the border between the two municipalities.

Oasi dei Sette Fratelli e Arco dell'Angelo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Behind the coast, mountain park of rocky hills, woods inhabited by deer and mouflons, canyons, gorges, rivers and archaeological sites; inside the Arch of the Angel, natural arch of pink granite with panoramic view over the entire valley of Castiadas.
