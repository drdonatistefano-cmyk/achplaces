What to see in Arcevia

Borgo dell'interra anconetano surrounded by walls with medieval gates and towers, with nine castles scattered throughout the territory. In the centre the Collegiate Church of San Medardo houses the only altar of Giovanni Della Robbia in the Marche region, and the complex of San Francesco houses the State Archaeological Museum.

7 places to visit in Arcevia, in the province of Ancona (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e mura

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center is enclosed by walls with medieval doors and towers, whose round path is still accessible. There are the Baroque collegiate church of San Medardo, the church of Sant'Agata, the cultural center San Francesco and the Misa Theatre, housed inside the Palazzo dei Priori.

  • Where Centro storico, Arcevia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Collegiata di San Medardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Baroque church of the historic center. It preserves the Robbiano altar commissioned in 1510 by two Sienese hermits for the hermitage of St. Jerome and completed in 1513 in the Florentine workshop of Giovanni Della Robbia, its only altar in the Marches, transferred here in 1870; also two painted statues of St. Catherine and of the Magdalena (rare terracotta robbian not glazed), a glazed Crucifix of Mattia Della Robbia and perhaps a nativity scene of the workshop of Hercules Ramazzani.

  • Where Centro storico, Arcevia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: regione.marche.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria del Soccorso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the historic center with the glazed altar of the Annunciation realized in the twenties of the sixteenth century by Mattia Della Robbia; the hermits of San Girolamo built a furnace in the hermitage to cook the terracotta.

Museo archeologico statale

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

Founded in 1996 by Superintendence and Municipality in the restored premises of the San Francesco complex, it collects materials from the territory from prehistory to the Roman age: the lithic industry of Ponte di Pietra (Super Paleolithic, about 20,000 years ago), the finds of the moat of Conelle (IV millennium BC), Cava Giacometti and Monte Croce-Guardia (XII-X century BC), the Gallic necropolis and the sanctuary of Montefortino, as well as three cippes with the gate finger. Times indicated: mon, mar, ven, sab, dom and festivities 8:30-13:30; wed and Thu 13:30-19:30; closed on the second Sunday of the month.

  • Where Corso Giuseppe Mazzini 64, Arcevia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.arcevia.an.it · italia.it

Fossato eneolitico di Conelle

History and architecture · Archaeology

Defensive ditch of the 4th millennium BC (Eneolithic), whose materials are exhibited in the State Archaeological Museum; the site is visitable and equipped with educational panels.

  • Where Località Conelle, Arcevia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
