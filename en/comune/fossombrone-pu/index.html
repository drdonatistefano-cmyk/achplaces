What to see in Fossombrone

Città sul Metauro heir of the Roman Forum Sempronii, with the Corte Alta built by Federico da Montefeltro which houses the Museum and Pinacoteca civica, the Quadreria Cesarini Museum House, the Logge of Corso Garibaldi, the Ponte della Concordia and, just outside, the Marmitte dei Giganti.

Corte Alta (Palazzo Ducale) e Museo civico A. Vernarecci - Pinacoteca civica

Museums · Archaeological

Renaissance residence built by Federico da Montefeltro since 1464, with a project attributed to Francesco di Giorgio Martini: elevated courtyard, lymphaeum of Ludovico Carducci, chapel of Cardinal Giuliano and Sala del Trono (Duke's Theatre); in the eastern loggia Duke Guidobaldo died in 1508. It houses the Civic Museum founded in 1901 by scholar Augusto Vernarecci, with exhibits of prehistory, picena culture and especially of the Roman Forum Sempronii, and the Pinacoteca civica.

  • Where Via del Verziere, Fossombrone (accesso anche da Piazza Mazzini)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Casa Museo Quadreria Cesarini

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

House donated to the Municipality by the notary Giuseppe Cesarini (1895-1977) in the Palazzo Pergamini-Negri, with bourgeois furnishings of the Thirties-Quaranta (red living room, pink living room, dining room). Twenty-one rooms of ancient, modern and contemporary art with works by Giorgio Morandi, Gino Severini, Achille Funi, Marino Marini and Francesco Messina, and more than 60 paintings and graphics by Anselmo Bucci.

discordant times: the MiC tab indicates closed every day with extraordinary openings on request (tel. 0721 723263) and ticket office 8-18

  • Where Via Pergamino, Fossombrone
  • Entry €4, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Parco archeologico di Forum Sempronii

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Roman city along the Via Flaminia founded in the second century BC, from which derives the name Fossombrone: remains of houses, public buildings and a thermal complex of the first century BC with over 20 rooms. The visit starts from the church of San Martino; reservation via the IAT Point.

  • Where Via Flaminia, San Martino del Piano, Fossombrone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Logge di Corso Garibaldi e Corte Bassa

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Double porch born after the 1000 for shops, inns and artisans, with the iconic loggias of the rich in front of churches and palaces and the iconic loggias of the poor; today it hosts more than 80 activities. Nearby, the sixteenth-century Corte Bassa which housed the dukes of Urbino, with porch, sandstone windows and rustic portal, and the Red Court.

  • Where Corso Garibaldi, Fossombrone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Filippo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

17th century church with a single nave with unfinished façade and baroque interior of white stuccoes, gilded and frescoes; altarpiece by Giovanni Francesco Guerrieri, works by Ridolfi and Diamantini and stuccoes by Tommaso Amantini. It hosts concerts and events.

  • Where Corso Garibaldi, Fossombrone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Ponte della Concordia

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Bridge of 1792 in stone of Furlo covered with bricks, with a single round arch; destroyed in 1944 and rebuilt in 1947. The reflection in the Metauro draws a very photographed oval and is the starting point for canoeing and kayaking towards the Giant Marmittes.

  • Where Fiume Metauro, Fossombrone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Colle dei Cappuccini e Santuario del Beato Benedetto Passionei

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the hill you can see the city, the Metauro Valley and on clear days the Adriatic. The sixteenth-century church of St. John the Baptist, today the sanctuary of Blessed Benedict Passionei next to the Capuchin monastery, has wooden altar, walnut choir and an eighteenth-century canvas by Gaetano Bessi; in front, a large iron cross illuminated every night.

  • Where Colle dei Cappuccini, Fossombrone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Marmitte dei Giganti

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Canyon excavated from the Metauro a few minutes from Fossombrone, which tells 140 million years of geological history. You can reach it by canoe or kayak from the Bridge of Concordia, or you can see it from the bridge of Diocletian in San Lazzaro and from a panoramic path.

  • Where San Lazzaro, Fossombrone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Rocca Malatestiana e Cittadella

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortress with pentagonal plan, today preserved only in remains, in the citadel on the hill overlooking the historic center; in the courtyard stands the church of Sant'Aldebrando.

  • Where Cittadella, Fossombrone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
