What to see in Chiaramonte Gulfi

Country of the Iblei mountains refounded after the destruction of Gulfi in 1299: palaces of the agricultural nobility along Corso Umberto, the Mother Church, Palazzo Montesano with five museums and the shrines of Gulfi and Graces.

8 places to visit in Chiaramonte Gulfi, in the province of Ragusa (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.
