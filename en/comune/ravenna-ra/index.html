What to see in Ravenna

The eight UNESCO early Christian monuments, the Dante area, museums and, out of town, pine forests, oasis and 35 km of coast. Tickets and timetables from official sources of the municipality and museums.

Basilica di San Vitale

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The great octagon of the 6th century with the mosaics of Justinian and Theodora in the apse, one of the summits of Byzantine art in the West.

  • Where Via San Vitale 17, Ravenna
  • Entry Cumulation Diocese (5 monuments, 7 days): reduced €13.50; entire on the official website
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.ravennamosaici.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: ravennamosaici.it · turismo.ra.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo Nazionale di Ravenna
