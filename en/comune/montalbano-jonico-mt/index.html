What to see in Montalbano Jonico

Hill country overlooking the lower valley of the Agri and surrounded by clay gullies of the Regional Reserve, the largest of the Basilicata; in the center the Mother Church of Santa Maria d'Episcopio, the Clock Gate and the Terravecchia district.

Riserva regionale dei Calanchi

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Established in 2011, it is the largest reserve of Basilicata: calanchi and ravines of clay that reach the cultivated fields, among Mediterranean maquis, pine trees and cypresses, with the geosite of Tempa Petrolla, a rocky spur that emerges from the clay. Rich in birds, it is recognized as IBA (Important Bird Area); ancient mule tracks that join the village to the Val d'Agri are now hiking routes. The tours take place as guided trekking of the center of environmental education "I Calanchi" of Legambiente.

Chiesa Madre di Santa Maria d'Episcopio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Dedicated in the sixteenth century and rebuilt in the first half of the eighteenth century, it now has an eighteenth-century appearance; above the main altar there is a pipe organ of 1785 and a polychrome marble panel dedicated to the patron.

  • Where Montalbano Jonico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · basilicataturistica.it

Rione Terravecchia e Porta dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the end of the course Carlo Alberto the Clock Gate, or Porta Castello, introduces to Terravecchia, interweaving of alleys, narrow passages and arches of Arab-Norman origin. The old town, built inside the medieval and Renaissance walls, preserves palaces with courtyard and stairway, houses with profferlo staircase, churches and chapels, some private palaces are abandoned.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · patrimonioculturale.regione.basilicata.it

Biblioteca civica "Filippo Rondinelli"

Museums · City history

The civic library preserves important documents on the fight against brigandaggio; it is located in a historic building, referred to as Palazzo Rondinelli da italia.it and as Palazzo Cassano, where the first meetings of the anti-borbonic republicans were held, from the regional portal.
