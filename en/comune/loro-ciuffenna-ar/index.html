Mulino ad acqua e ponte

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

The mill built around 1100 on a cliff along Ciuffenna, the oldest still active in Tuscany, used to grind chestnut flour.

Pieve di San Pietro a Gropina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
