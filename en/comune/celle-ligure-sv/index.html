What to see in Celle Ligure

Marinar village of the Riviera di Ponente born around the year 1000, with caruggi, arches and bridges covered behind the beach, and the pine forest Bottini above the village.

4 places to visit in Celle Ligure, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico di Celle Ligure

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old seaside village preserves its structure intact, with narrow caruggi, arches and bridges covered around Via Aicardi and Via Ghiglino.

  • Where Via Aicardi, Celle Ligure
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di Nostra Signora della Consolazione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

17th century church recognizable for the polychrome façade and the bell tower; inside an Annunciation attributed to Domenico Fiasella.

  • Where Celle Ligure
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Pineta Bottini

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

Panoramic municipal park above the village, reachable on foot by an ancient staircase or with the free panoramic elevator of via Cassisi.

  • Where Via Cassisi, Celle Ligure
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
