What to see in Ostana

Country of villages scattered in the upper Po Valley, cradle of Occitan culture, among the most beautiful villages in Italy, with a wonderful view of the Monviso group.

4 places to visit in Ostana, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Ostana in the guide with map and filters

Borgate di Ostana

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The heart of the village recovered and valued, with houses and small streets always in order; from the hamlet Villa (1234 m) you go up to Serre and Durandini.

  • Where Villa, Ostana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visit.terresmonviso.eu

Belvedere di Pian del Ciarm

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 1634 meters, with an unparalleled view of the Monviso group; easy hike of about 400 meters of altitude difference.

  • Where Ostana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visit.terresmonviso.eu · italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Stage of the summer itinerary towards the high villages.

  • Where Ostana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visit.terresmonviso.eu · italia.it

Museo etnografico «Ostana Alta Valle Po»

Museums · Themed

The civic ethnographic museum, where are preserved in eighteenth-century copy the Statuti di Ostana of 1425.

  • Where Ostana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: regione.piemonte.it · italia.it
