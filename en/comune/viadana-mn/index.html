What to see in Viadana

Città del Mantovano between Po and Oglio, inhabited since the fourth millennium BC and linked to Gonzaga since 1415; in the historical center the Arch of Porta Nuova, the Town Hall of Pietro Antonio Maggi and the parish church of Santa Maria Assunta and San Cristoforo with the polyptych of Bartolomeo Vivarini, and in the cultural hub MuVi the Civic Museum Antonio Parazzi.

6 places to visit in Viadana, in the province of Mantua (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico, Porta Nuova e Palazzo comunale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center is dominated by the Arch of Porta Nuova. The Palazzo Comunale, historically Palazzo della Ragione (1502), has a porch and tower and is the work of Pietro Antonio Maggi, who also designed the nearby Palazzo dell'ex Monte di Pietà.

  • Where Centro storico, Viadana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta e San Cristoforo in castello

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises where from 700 AD there is documented an oratory of St. Christopher; the new church, decided in 1522, was completed in 1567 by the architect Pompeo Pedemonte and deeply modified in the nineteenth century (reconsecrated in 1887), when the archpriest Antonio Parazzi extended it, transformed its facade and made it a picture gallery. It preserves a polyptych by Bartolomeo Vivarini, a group in terracotta of influence and paintings by Tassinari, Borgani and Andreasino, as well as works by Parmesan, Bolognese, Cremonese, Mantua and Verona.

  • Where Via Parazzi, Viadana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it · italia.it

Museo civico Antonio Parazzi

Museums · City history

Born from the collection of Monsignor Antonio Parazzi (1823-1899), it has four sections: archaeological (fossil, bronzes, finds of the Bronze Age and Romans, coins, Egyptian statues and Etruscan-Italic finds), Renaissance (one hundred terracottas of the Padan furnaces and ceramics from the fifteenth to the nineteenth century), art gallery (paintings from the sixteenth to the nineteenth century, torn frescoes, wooden tablets) and ancient fabrics related to the Viadana and saffron. It is located in the MuVi cultural centre.

  • Where MuVi, via Manzoni 4, Viadana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.viadana.mn.it · italia.it

MuVi, Galleria civica e Museo della Città

Museums · Modern art

Cultural centre opened in 2003 in the former primary schools of 1908, about 4,000 square meters in Piazza Manzoni. In addition to the Parazzi Museum, it brings together the Galleria Civica d'arte moderna e contemporanea with the Sala della Permanente .Alfredo Saviola, the Museo della Città (Museum of the City) Adolfo Ghinzelli with musical, cartographic and photographic backgrounds and the Library Luigi Parazzi (1862), whose ancient fund with manuscripts and incunabula is consulted by reservation.

  • Where Via Manzoni 4, Viadana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.viadana.mn.it · turismo.mantova.it · italia.it

Chiesa dei Santi Filippo e Giacomo apostoli a Cogozzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish was documented in 1438, dedicated to Saints Philip and James since 1518; the current three-nave church was built in the early nineteenth century and preserves paintings from the nearby former oratory of San Lorenzo, already home to the Confraternity of San Rocco and San Martino, which on the counterface has a Latin plaque of 1576.

  • Where Cogozzo, Viadana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.viadana.mn.it · italia.it

Impianto idrovoro di San Matteo delle Chiaviche

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

In the hamlet of San Matteo delle Chiaviche, in the golenal area, the plant built between 1920 and 1939 on a project by Piero Portaluppi collects the waters of a basin of about 32,000 hectares and, through the canals Navarolo, Ceriana and Fossola, pours them into the Oglio with a flow rate of about 53,000 liters per second; it is 182 meters long and rests on chestnut poles. It hosts the Centre for interpretation and documentation of the Viadana Ecomuseum, Commessaggio, Dosolo and Gazzuolo.

  • Where San Matteo delle Chiaviche, Viadana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it
