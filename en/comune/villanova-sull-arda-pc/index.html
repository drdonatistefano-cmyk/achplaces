What to see in Villanova sull'Arda

In the Piacenza plain near the Po, Villanova sul'Arda is the land of Giuseppe Verdi: in the village of Sant'Agata there is the villa where the Master lived and worked, today passed to the State and temporarily closed, while in the village the monument to Verdi stands in front of the hospital he wanted; complete the visit of the church of Cignano and the river park of Isola Giarola.

Villa Verdi a Sant'Agata

Museums · House museums

Verdi bought the property in 1848, enlarged it following sketches and indications of his fist and lived there since 1851 with Giuseppina Strepponi. The museum path, in one part of the building, includes the original rooms, the bedroom-study, the pianos used by the Master, portraits, autographs, the music library and the reconstruction of the room of the Grand Hotel in Milan where he died on January 27, 1901. Around, the English park designed by Verdi, with pond, rare plants, sculptures by Giuseppe Torretti and the burials of his dogs. In December 2024 the MiC signed the decree of expropriation that acquires it to the State, with urgent interventions of restoration.

Visit Piacenza indicates it is simultaneously closed and the MiC card closed every day; acquired by the State by decree of expropriation of 16/12/2024 and urgent restoration planned

Monumento a Giuseppe Verdi e ospedale

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The monument to the Master is located at the entrance of the hospital of Villanova, which Verdi wanted to donate to the village.

  • Where Ingresso dell'ospedale, Villanova sull'Arda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page visitpiacenza.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitpiacenza.it

Chiesa dei Santi Andrea e Bernardino a Cignano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish of Romanesque origin, transformed at the beginning of the sixteenth century, mosaic styles: Baroque inside, Romanesque-modern outside, with Romanesque-Gothic bell tower rebuilt in 1900; side chapels of 1928 and 1950-51 and terracotta façade of the 1950s. It contains a Madonna with Child dated 1520, an Ecce Homo attributed to the Sodoma school, a Saint Anthony Abbot of the late sixteenth century and frescoes of angels and friezes re-emerged in the vault. Open by day.

  • Where Piazza Vittoria 13, Cignano, Villanova sull'Arda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page visitpiacenza.it

Sources: comune.villanova.pc.it · visitpiacenza.it

Parco di Isola Giarola

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

River park linked to the Po ecosystem, with a lake born from a former quarry: after the end of the excavations the Bonifiche have reported native species in rivers and wetlands. Birdwatching, sport fishing, life and cycling trails, eco-friendly guided tours.
