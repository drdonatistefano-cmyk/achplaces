What to see in Posada

Borgo della Baronia on a limestone hill, among the most beautiful villages in Italy, dominated by the Castle of the Fava; at its foot the valley of the river Posada, inside the park of Tepilora, and long beaches such as Su Tiriarzu and San Giovanni di Posada.

6 places to visit in Posada, in the province of Nuoro (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello della Fava

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Stone fortress attributed to the judges of Gallura and already standing in the thirteenth century, disputed between the judges of Gallura and Arborea, passed to the Aragonese in 1380 and then returned to the Arborens; perhaps residence of the judges, also of Eleonora of Arborea. The square crenellated tower, the ruins of the walls and some cisterns remain. The name comes from the legend of the pigeon stuffed with the last broad beans that convinced the besieging Saracens to retire.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Centro storico di Posada

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village on the slopes of the castle hill, with medieval buildings and narrow and colorful alleys overlooking the plain and the sea; from the promontory once saw pirates and invaders.

  • Where Centro storico, Posada
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Antonio abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church at the foot of the castle, in the center of the village, rebuilt in the seventeenth century on a plant of 1324 and several times renovated; the patron saint is celebrated in mid-January with bonfires, processions and typical sweets.

  • Where Centro storico, Posada
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Spiaggia di Su Tiriarzu

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the largest beaches in Baronia, a southern stretch of a coast of about four kilometers about three kilometers from the village: clear and fine sand, dunes, pine forest and behind the ponds Tondu and Longu and the mouth of the Posada river, with pink flamingos; in the background the castle and Montalbo. Windy, loved by surfers; from the parking lot a path with wooden bridge crosses the ponds.

  • Where Su Tiriarzu, Posada
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

San Giovanni di Posada e torre aragonese

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Borgata marina just over 3 km from the village, immersed in the pine forest, with a long white beach in the coastal area awarded with the Cinque Vele di Legambiente; there is the Aragonese tower, in the ancient portus Liquidonis of Roman times. Sailing, kite and windsurfing, cycling or horseback riding.

  • Where San Giovanni di Posada, Posada
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Parco di Tepilora e foce del rio Posada

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Posada is one of the four municipalities of the park, recognized by UNESCO as a Biosphere Reserve, with paths between forests, springs, lagoons and dunes for trekking, mountain biking and horse riding; the final stretch of the Posada river, can be traced back in kayak, forms canals and ponds like that of San Giovanni, frequented by Italian knights and pink flamingos.

  • Where Valle del rio Posada, Posada
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it
