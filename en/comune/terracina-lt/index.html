What to see in Terracina

City overlooking the gulf between the Circeo and Gaeta, the Via Appia and the Via Francigena del Sud: high up the sanctuary of Monte Sant'Angelo with the so-called Temple of Jupiter Anxur, in the center the Forum Emiliano, the Roman Theatre reopened in 2023 and the Cathedral of San Cesareo.

9 places to visit in Terracina, in the province of Latina (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario di Monte Sant'Angelo (Tempio di Giove Anxur)

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On Mount Sant'Angelo, the last spur of the Ausoni mountains on the sea, the sanctuary was built mainly between the II and I century BC in cement work: the Temple Major on high podium rests on a terrace with twelve arches, with the rock and the oracular cave, cisterns and the Small Temple linked to the cult of Feronia. Recent excavations attribute the major temple to Venus Obsequens more than to Jupiter Anxur. From the terrace the view goes from the Circeo to Gaeta and the Pontine islands; open every day 9-20.

  • Where Monte Sant'Angelo, Terracina
  • Entry €7, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.terracina.lt.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Foro Emiliano e Via Appia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The main square of Terracina preserves the Roman limestone pavement financed by the magistrate Aulo Emilio between the end of the first century BC and the beginning of the I AD, with the inscription in bronze letters Aemilius A. F. Stravit'; on the north side runs a preserved stretch of the Appia Antica. Around stood the civil basilica, the temple then incorporated into the Cathedral and the theatre. Always accessible.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Terracina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.terracina.lt.it

Teatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology

Built at the end of the republican age and at the most splendor under Augustus, it remained hidden for centuries under the medieval city and re-emerged after the bombings of 1943; inaugurated on November 11, 2023, with the cavea re-composed with the original limestone steps and traces of the orchestra floor in colored marble. The last excavations have returned a portrait of Julius Caesar. Tickets through the Tourist Office.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Terracina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.terracina.lt.it

Cattedrale di San Cesareo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

It rises on the temple of the Emiliano Forum and climbs up a stairway; the mosaic of the portico has a marine theme, perhaps a voyage of crusaders by sea, and inside mosaics with knights, battles and fantastic animals; the bell tower is lightened by numerous arches.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Terracina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Museo civico «Pio Capponi»

Museums · Archaeological

At the first level of the thirteenth-century Torre Frumentaria, in front of the Forum Emiliano: sculptures and architectural marbles including a circular ara Augustea with bucrani, an altar dedicated to Traiano, the Telamone in ancient yellow called Founded in 1894 by Pio Capponi, reordered last time in 1999.

MiC card indicates closed every day and the City does not publish timetables

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.terracina.lt.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Favisse del Tempio Maggiore

Museums · Archaeological

Along the side of the Temple-Cathedral, in front of the so-called Capitolium, two rooms of the gallery of Roman substruction, called "Favisse" (Favisse), exhibit the mosaics of a domus of the second century AD found in Via dei SS. Four; it is the third exhibition venue of the civic museum.

  • Where Corso Anita Garibaldi, Terracina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.terracina.lt.it · italia.it

Centro storico alto

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval nucleus contains towers, ogival windows, underpasses and stone lions on guard; in Piazza Municipio overlook the Cathedral and the Torre Frumentaria. Lower down the eighteenth-century city has straight streets and large squares.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Terracina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Spiaggia e lungomare

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Gulf of Terracina is closed to the north by the Circeo and to the south by the promontory of Gaeta; the beach has the Blue Flag (2024) and is one of the ten Green Flags of Lazio, suitable for families with children.

  • Where Lungomare, Terracina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
