What to see in Militello in Val di Catania

Baroque village rebuilt after the earthquake of 1693, between the late Baroque towns of the Val di Noto UNESCO World Heritage Site and Borgo dei Borghi 2025: the sanctuary of Santa Maria della Stella with the Nativity of Andrea della Robbia, the Mother Church with the museum in the crypts, the former abbey of San Benedetto and the Norman Santa Maria la Vetere.

13 places to visit in Militello in Val di Catania, in the province of Catania (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario di Santa Maria della Stella

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · UNESCO World Heritage

Baroque shrine on a scenic staircase, attributed to Giuseppe Ferrara, started in 1722 after the earthquake of 1693 and opened to worship in 1741; the interior stuccoes are by Onofrio Russo, pupil of Serpotta. It preserves the Nativity in glazed terracotta by Andrea della Robbia, brought by Santa Maria la Vetere, and the sarcophagus by Blasco II Barresi; the feast is September 8.

Museo del Tesoro di Santa Maria della Stella

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage

Museum inside the sanctuary of the Madonna della Stella: it contains a bas-relief by Nicolò Speciale by Francesco Laurana and a triptych attributed to Antonello da Messina.

Chiesa Madre di San Nicolò e Santissimo Salvatore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage

Baroque mother church on the Corso Umberto, rebuilt after 1693; its dome is among the first in reinforced concrete of Sicily. Inside stuccoes of the Serpottian school and a statue of the Bagnasco Palermo; the nineteenth-century statue of the Santissimo Salvatore comes out in procession at the end of August.

Museo d'arte sacra San Nicolò

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage

In the crypts of the Mother Church, set up on a project by Giuseppe Pannano, he exhibits the treasure of the church: a Christ died in limestone of the fifteenth century, silvers of the eighteenth century, vestments, prints and paintings, and a chess book of 1617 considered the first to describe the Sicilian "defense."

Ex abbazia e Chiesa di San Benedetto

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · UNESCO World Heritage

Church wanted in the early seventeenth century by Prince Branciforte and his wife Joan of Austria, nephew of Charles V, next to the Benedictine monastery, indicated as the third for importance of Sicily; has sumptuous altars in polychrome marble and there is buried Francesco Branciforte.

Chiesa di Santa Maria la Vetere

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Church founded by the Normans in 1090 in a valley of landscape and archaeological interest; of the earthquake of 1693 survived only part of the right aisle. The portal of 1506, with probably Antonello Gagini's bezel, has a canopy on lions; next there is a early Christian necropolis. It is managed by the Archaeological Park of Catania, open Saturday and Sunday 10-13.30.

  • Where Militello in Val di Catania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info · parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it

Castello Barresi-Branciforte e Fontana della Ninfa Zizza

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Two cylindrical towers remain of the 14th century castle, the environments of the trappet and the south bastion; in 1473 Aldonza Santapau was strangled. In the courtyard the fountain of the Ninfa Zizza of 1607, which marked the arrival of drinking water in the village and recalls the legend of the girl transformed into a spring.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Museo e archivio civico Sebastiano Guzzone

Museums · City history

It preserves pottery of Magna Grecia, a high relief of the Ninfa Zizza and a collection of bans from the seventeenth century to the Anglo-American occupation; it also exhibits paintings of the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, including a work by Sciuti and works by the local Sebastiano Guzzone, Giuseppe Barone and Santo Marino.

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage

Church in a small square with a resigned entrance and a baroque interior decorated with thirteen statues in stucco and gold of the most venerated virgin saints in Sicily.

Chiesa delle Anime Sante del Purgatorio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Confraternal church of 1613 dedicated to Saints Gregorio and Vito, linked to a rich confraternity that prayed for the dead: the Baroque interior has a golden altar that reproduces the mountain of Purgatory and the church offers views of the surrounding landscape.

Centro storico barocco

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The village, rebuilt in late Baroque style after the earthquake of 1693 following the original plant, has more than twenty churches and noble palaces; along Via Porta della Terra overlook the palaces of the Princes of Bellaprima and Majorana of the Nicchiara, and the church of Santi Angeli Custodi has an eighteenth century majolica floor.

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info
