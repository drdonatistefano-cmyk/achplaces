What to see in Villa Castelli

In Villa Castelli the Doge's Palace houses the Civic Museum with the findings of the Magnogreque necropolis of Pezza Petrosa, whose archaeological park is just over 2 km from the village. In the countryside you can visit the Trappeto del Duca, an ancient underground oil mill overlooking the Ionian-Salentine plain.

Museo civico nel Palazzo Ducale

Museums · Archaeological

It occupies the ground floor of the Doge's Palace, now the seat of the Town Hall, and collects about 200 finds from the excavations of the Magnogrec necropolis of Pezza Petrosa. Opened in 2009 after the restoration of the complex, which also includes a medieval tower and the municipal art gallery. It offers guided tours and educational activities.

Parco archeologico di Pezza Petrosa

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Just over 2 km from the village, it occupies about 5,000 m2, of which at least 1,000 are a necropolis with 33 intact tombs, different in shape, covering, covering and orientation. The remains of a city walls and an inhabited area have also emerged; the furnishings indicate settlements since the Neolithic period. The site began to depopulate with the Roman conquest and was destroyed in the 5th century AD during the wars between Goths and Byzantines.

Trappeto del Duca

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient underground mill where already in the eighteenth century oil was produced throughout the fief. It is located in a karst depression with views of the Ionian-Salentine plain that separates the Murgia from the sea. Access is clear.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
