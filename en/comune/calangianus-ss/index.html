What to see in Calangianus

Gallura's cork centre on the eastern side of Limbara, with stone houses and granite paved streets: the parish church of Santa Giusta with the Assumption of Andrea Lusso, the Diocesan Museum in the oratory of the Rosary, the Museum of Cork in the former Capuchin convent and, in the cork forests, the tomb of Giants of Pascareddha.

8 places to visit in Calangianus, in the province of Sassari (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo del sughero

Museums · Themed

In the former convent of the Capuchins, built in 1705 and suppressed in 1866, the guided tour follows all the stages of the work, from the decoration of corks and fini products (slips, shoes, bags, panels, insulation, even baseballs), with videos and photos of the first machines, benches, turnings and a cork nativity scene. The official production in Calangianus began in 1851; at the exit we receive a cork souvenir. Hours indicated: mon-sab 10-18, sun 10-17.

  • Where Via S. Francesco 3, Calangianus
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Museo diocesano d'arte sacra Santa Giusta

Museums · Old masters

Housed in the oratory of Our Lady of the Rosary, next to the parish, exhibits pieces from the XVI-XVIII centuries, especially liturgical objects.

  • Where Oratorio del Rosario, centro storico, Calangianus
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Parrocchiale di Santa Giusta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The main church of the village has the granite façade of the fourteenth century, frescoes and marble works of the early twentieth century; in the right transept is the Assumption of Andrea Lusso, example of Sardinian Mannerism.

  • Where Centro storico, Calangianus
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Centro storico di granito

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Stone houses and granite paved streets around Corso Niccolò Ferracciu, Carrera Longa and Viale Roma; among the buildings the Palazzu Mannu, example of Gallura's architecture of the Sabauda era, the Fountain Nuova in local granite and the church of Sant'Anna of 1665, in granite, with a nineteenth-century painting of the saint.

  • Where Corso Niccolò Ferracciu, Calangianus
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On Viale Roma, it has a portal with bas-relief depicting a choir of angels; next to it stands the former convent of the Capuchins (1705), today the Museum of Cork.

  • Where Viale Roma, Calangianus
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Tomba di Giganti di Pascareddha

History and architecture · Archaeology

In Badu Mela, on the northern slopes of Mount Deu, in the cork woods, dates back to the Middle-Recent Bronze (1700-1400 BC). In the territory also the sacred source of Li Paladini (II millennium BC) and the nuraghe Agnu flanked by a dolmen.

  • Where Località Badu Mela, Calangianus
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Monte Limbara e valle del rio Santu Paulu

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The territory occupies the eastern side of the Limbara massif, with forests of holm oak, conifers and Mediterranean maquis; in the Limbara park, in the valley of the Santu Paulu river, walking and cycling paths with views. Near the village also the park of Stazzana with secular holm oaks.

  • Where Valle del rio Santu Paulu, Calangianus
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
