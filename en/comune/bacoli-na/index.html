What to see in Bacoli

The Bay Gulf, a place of villas and Roman spas: the city submerged by bradisism, the Castle with the Archaeological Museum of the Flegrei Camps, the Mirabilis pool of the Miseno fleet and the Vanvitellian Casine on Lake Fusaro.

10 places to visit in Bacoli, in the province of Naples (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Bacoli in the guide with map and filters

Parco Archeologico Sommerso di Baia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The ancient coast submerged by bradisism: about 7 meters deep in front of Punta Epitaffio a nymph of the Claudia age, the remains of a villa attributed to the Pisoni and the 'villa with entrance to protiro , with mosaic floors, destination of divers.

  • Where Via Lucullo 94, Bacoli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Archeologico dei Campi Flegrei nel Castello di Baia

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In the Castle on the promontory of Baia, which preserves the remains of a Roman villa of the II-I century BC with mosaic floors, the route tells Cuma, Puteoli and Liternum; from the Piazza d'Armi the view sweeps over the gulf of Naples.

  • Where Via Castello 39, Bacoli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Piscina Mirabilis

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Roman cistern about 70 meters long and 15 meters high, with 48 pillars that divide it into five naves and a capacity of 12,600 cubic meters: it was the point of arrival of the aqueduct wanted by Augustus for the fleet of Miseno.

  • Where Via Piscina Mirabile 27, Bacoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: pafleg.cultura.gov.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Parco Archeologico delle Terme di Baia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Thermal buildings on terraces overlooking the gulf; it includes the so-called Temple of Mercury, a room of Augustan age indicated as the first known case of large dome in cement.

  • Where Baia, Bacoli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Tempio di Diana

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Large dome room of the Baia thermal baths, outside the park, whose vault was already visible before the excavations.

  • Where Baia, Bacoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Tomba di Agrippina

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The ruin known as the Tomb of Agrippina, mother of Nero, is actually not a tomb but part of a maritime villa.

  • Where Bacoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: discovercampania.it · italia.it

Casina Vanvitelliana

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Pavilion on Lake Fusaro commissioned by Ferdinand IV of Bourbon to Carlo Vanvitelli and completed by 1782, reachable by a wooden bridge.

  • Where Piazza Gioacchino Rossini 1, Bacoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Capo Miseno

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The promontory seat of the Roman military port, where they had villa Gaio Mario, Cornelia mother of the Gracchi and Lucullo.

  • Where Miseno, Bacoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: discovercampania.it · italia.it
