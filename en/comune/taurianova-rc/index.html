What to see in Taurianova

Città della Piana was born from the union of the farmhouses of Radicena and Jatrinoli, named "Città del Libro 2024" and known for the late spring Infiorata; among the places to visit italia.it indicates the Romanesque Cathedral with rose window and two bell towers, the baroque Villa Zerbi by Filippo Frangipane, Contestabile Palace and the monumental Cemetery of Radicena.

Palazzo degli Uffici

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Two-storey public building, typical of the reconstruction followed by the earthquake of 1908 in southern Calabria, with two lateral bodies slightly protruding and cantonal smooth rustic. Open Monday to Friday 8-18.

  • Where Via Cavour, Taurianova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
