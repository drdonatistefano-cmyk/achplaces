What to see in Monteleone d'Orvieto

Small village in the far north of the province of Terni, born as a castle built by Orvieto in 1052 to guard the northern border: inside the walls, between the North Gate and the Torrione, the Clock Tower, the churches of the Crucifix and the Saints Peter and Paul and the tiny Teatro dei Rustici, born from the public barn in 1732.

Borgo medievale, mura e Porta Nord

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The castle was built by Orvieto in 1052 to guard the northern border; the imposing walls remain and the North Gate, the ancient entrance to the base of the Mozza Tower, while the South Gate opens the other side of the center. Inside the walls colorful squares, alleys and an ancient medieval well.

Torre dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Il Torrione

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Tower at the end of Corso Vittorio Emanuele II, from which you can enjoy a panoramic view of the surroundings; the terraces of the village look at the landscapes that the guides approach to those painted by Perugino.

Teatro dei Rustici

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Among the smallest theatres in Italy and Europe, in the Public Palace: an inscription in the hall recalls that in 1732 the community transformed the barn of the palace into a theatre, when the young people of the country got to recite comedies at Carnival; it takes its name from the Philo-dramatic Academy of Rustici and continued its activity despite the hostility of the papal authorities. It has 96 seats; on the ground floor of the building a barn and a mill.

Centro documentazione di arte musicale «Attilio Parelli»

Museums · Themed

Museum dedicated to the composer and conductor Attilio Parelli (1874-1944): more than one hundred original manuscripts, including The Lovers, Hermes and Fanfulla of Lodi, also printed scores of colleagues with dedications and autographs, booklets, family photographs, diplomas and documents, donated by the heirs to the municipality.

the MiC card indicates closed every day; times to ask the City (tel. 076383402)

Chiesa dei Santi Apostoli Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It preserves a painting of Our Lady with the Child between Saints Peter and Paul, surmounted by a fifteenth-century Pietà attributed to the school of Perugino; in the crypt the relics of St. Theodorus Martyr, patron saint of the country, whose cult flourished after 1738.

  • Where Monteleone d'Orvieto
  • Entry Free
