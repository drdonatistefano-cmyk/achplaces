What to see in Carro

Borgo dell'alta Val di Vara, the village of origin of the family of Niccolò Paganini, who reminds you of the musician with the Paganinian Festival and with Casa Paganini; also houses a mineral museum with a collection of minerals of the municipal territory.

Museo mineralogico permanente

Museums · Science and university

The museum brings together five collections: one systematic, with the minerals sorted by class from the simplest to the most complex; one didactic, with the minerals from which the most common metals are extracted; one of the minerals found in the territory of Carro; one of hard and semi-precious stones; one of fluorescent minerals. Tel. 0187 861005.

Casa Paganini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Carro is the village of origin of the Paganini family and there is the house of the ancestors of the Genoese composer, the ancient pagan residence that is the background of the Paganini Festival. The regional portal announced its inauguration on 26 June, in the 25th edition of the festival, as a study centre dedicated to listening to and promoting the musician's works.
