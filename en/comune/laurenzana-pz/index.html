What to see in Laurenzana

Borgo on a spur in the National Park of the Apennines Lucano Val d'Agri Lagonegrese, dominated by the feudal castle on the sandstone cliff overlooking the Camastra Valley; it preserves in the Mother Church the body of Blessed Egidio, the frescoed cloister of Santa Maria della Neve and, in the territory, the Abetina Reserve.

Castello feudale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It rises on a high sandstone cliff, reached by a stone bridge with several arches, and dominates the Camastra Valley. On the site of a Byzantine hermitage, it was rebuilt in stone by the Normans in the 11th century, renovated by the Angioini around 1250, transformed by the Orsini Del Balzo in the Renaissance and then into a noble residence, with the last major intervention in the 18th century. It has two courtyards, the one representing the mast, drawbridge, shoe walls, portals and Renaissance balustrade; the cliff is recognized as a geosite. Visits by reservation at the municipal offices.

Chiesa Madre di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Next to the remains of the castle, it has thirteenth-century origins but was remodeled in the Baroque age; originally it was dedicated to Santa Maria de Plateis. Since 1524 it has been guarding the body of Blessed Egidio of Laurenzana, of the minor friars observing, and a canvas of Pietrafesa.

  • Where Laurenzana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it · patrimonioculturale.regione.basilicata.it

Riserva regionale Abetina di Laurenzana

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Reserve in the municipal territory, inside the Lucano Apennines National Park, dominated by the rare white fir with beech and oaks; it hosts rare fauna, it is open all year round, it is a frequent destination for schoolchildren and inside it is home to the ecomuseum center of the white fir.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village stands on a spur and was surrounded by an angioin age by walls with towers; from the castle the look ranges over the Camastra Valley and the mountains of Volturino and Caperrino, and the streets retain patriotic names linked to the insurrectional march of 1860 towards Potenza.

  • Where Laurenzana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it · patrimonioculturale.regione.basilicata.it
