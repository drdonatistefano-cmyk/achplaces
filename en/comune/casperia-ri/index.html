What to see in Casperia

The hilly village of Sabina, called Aspra until 1947 and quoted by Virgilio in the seventh book of the Aeneid; the historic center, entirely pedestrian, preserves walls, medieval towers and walks and rises up to the church of San Giovanni Battista, while just outside the village stands Santa Maria in Legarano.

Centro storico e mura

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village can be reached only on foot through a door and winds through alleys and small steps; it is one of the few historical centers of Italy entirely pedestrian and preserves towers, walkways, rivets, wounds and medieval casematte. An order of 1397 sentenced to death those who opened a gap as wide as a person in the walls. The Porta di Santa Maria (Porta Reatina) has a clock that still marks the hours according to the medieval system.

  • Where Porta Reatina, Casperia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Piazza Umberto I e Fontana del Belvedere

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Along a well-restored cobbled path you arrive at the square and the Belvedere Fountain, overlooking the Sabini Mountains and Mount Soratte.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Casperia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church on top of the village, next to the town hall, renovated several times since 1515; it has a Romanesque bell tower dating from around 1200, several works of art, a large organ and a permanent monumental nativity scene that can be visited in the sacristy.

  • Where Centro storico, Casperia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Santa Maria in Legarano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Just north of the village, with a large porch and attached to a former monastery, rises on the remains of a Roman villa: in the sacristy are visible mosaic floors. It houses terracotta figures of artists from the Abruzzi of the sixteenth century.

  • Where Località Legarano, Casperia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com
