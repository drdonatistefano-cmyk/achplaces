What to see in Lugagnano Val d'Arda

Country of the Val d'Arda mentioned in 844, on a passage of the Arda stream and between the Pliocenic calanques of the "clays of Lugagnano": in its territory the Roman city of Veleia with the forum and the Antiquarium, the church of San Zenone with a canvas by Luigi Crespi and the eighteenth-century Palazzo Gandolfi.

Area archeologica e Antiquarium di Veleia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Roman town hall built after the conquest of the 2nd century BC on the town of Ligurian Veleia, 469 meters on a terrace on the valley of Chero, still frequented in the 5th century under the name of Augusta. It was discovered by chance in 1747 with the discovery of the Tabula alimentaria traianea; Duke Philip of Bourbon started its excavations in 1760. You can see the hole paved in sandstone with portico on three sides and the basilica on the fourth, where there were twelve marble statues of the Julian-Claudia family today in Parma, the baths, the residential district and the cistern long believed an amphitheater; the Antiquarium, in the building wanted by Maria Luigia, exhibits pre-Roman finds, the so-called Ligurian Jupiter and the casts of Tabula and Lex rubria. Closed on Mondays; from April to October Tuesday-Thursday 9-16.30, Friday-Sunday and holidays 11-18.30.

In winter (1 November-30 March) hole and spas are covered and not visible: call the Antiquarium to know if the covers have been removed

  • Where Strada Provinciale 14, località Rustigazzo, Lugagnano Val d'Arda
  • Entry €5, reduced €2 (18-24 years), free under 18 years
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website musei.emiliaromagna.beniculturali.it

Sources: musei.emiliaromagna.beniculturali.it · cultura.gov.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Antonino a Veleia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Heir to the Altomedieval parish church of Macinesso, rises just outside the archaeological enclosure: sixteenth century plant, nineteenth-century decorations and a valuable wooden ciborium carved and gilded from the end of the seventeenth century.

  • Where Veleia, Lugagnano Val d'Arda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Chiesa di San Zenone

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish church built in the sixteenth century instead of a church of 1219, with nineteenth-century façade; it preserves the Dinner in Emmaus by Luigi Crespi, signed and dated 1748.

  • Entry Free

Riserva naturale geologica del Piacenziano

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established in 1995, it protects on 345 hectares, in nine stations distributed between Lugagnano and other municipalities, the sedimentary outskirts typical of Piacenzano, the geological plan of the Pliocene linked to the clays of Lugagnano.
