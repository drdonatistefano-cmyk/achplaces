What to see in Gallese

Medieval village on a plateau of tuff on the valley of the Tiber, between two streams, home of two popes and linked to Orsini, Colonna, Borgia and the Rovere; to see the neoclassical cathedral of Pietro Camporese, the basilica of the patron San Famiano and the castle Altemps, where he walked d'Annunzio.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village of medieval origin on a plateau of tuff overlooking the valley of the Tiber and closed between two streams, with walls, central square, piazza del Comune and numerous noble palaces. Born as a falisco settlement, abandoned in the Roman age and repopulated after the fall of the Empire; for San Famiano the "Pioggia di fuoco" illuminates the crenellated walls.

Concattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Neoclassical building designed by Pietro Camporese in 1796, with 17th century paintings and a collection of objects related to the liturgy of Good Friday.

Basilica di San Famiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Dedicated to the patron Saint Famiano, celebrated on August 8, at the foot of the historic center; it has a raised presbytery of great artistic value. It is a stage of the Way of Light, variant of the Via Amerina.

  • Where Ai piedi del centro storico, Gallese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Castello di Gallese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

On a tufa spur between the Cimini Mountains and the Tiber, it was born as a strategic military fortress for the defense of Rome; at the beginning of the seventeenth century the Duke Pietro Altemps transformed it into a noble residence, with finishes attributed to Carlo Fontana and the double staircase to Giacomo della Porta. In the mid-nineteenth century Altemps passed to Duke Jules Hardouin of Walese, whose daughter Maria, the future wife of Gabriel of Annunzio, walked there with the poet in the park.
