What to see in Isernia

Among the oldest inhabited places in Europe: the National Museum of the Paleolithic on the deposit of Isernia La Pineta, and in the historic center the Cathedral on the Italic temple, the Arch of St. Peter, the museum of Santa Maria delle Nunche and the Fraternal Fountain.

7 places to visit in Isernia, in the province of Isernia (Molise). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo nazionale del Paleolitico di Isernia

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Built on the bottom Paleolithic field of Isernia La Pineta, discovered by chance in 1978 during the works of the highway: the excavation pavilion of about 700 square meters allows to see the excavation and archaeologists at work; exhibits about 6,000 original finds in the location of finding, the milk teeth of a child dated about 586,000 years ago and the reconstructions of life size of bear, bison, megacero, rhinoceros and ancient elephant.

  • Where Via Ramiera Vecchia, Isernia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.fondoambiente.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · fondoambiente.it

Museo archeologico di Santa Maria delle Monache

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In a former Altomedieval Benedictine monastery founded under the Longobard counts, already antiquarium municipal since 1934: the history of Isernia from the archaic age to the Middle Ages and a section on the italic sanctuary of Pietrabbondante, with the armour discs and the bronze lare.

  • Where Corso Marcelli, Isernia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.fondoambiente.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · fondoambiente.it

Fontana Fraterna

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

A public fountain with six jets in the shape of a loggia, symbol of the city: round and octagonal arches on round columns, twelve hanging arches at the top and stones of different eras, including a Roman marble slab with two dolphins; moved after the bombing of 1943 in Piazza Celestino V.

  • Where Piazza Celestino V, Isernia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.fondoambiente.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it

Cattedrale di San Pietro Apostolo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Mother Church of the diocese of Isernia-Venafro, built on the italic temple of the third century BC dedicated to Jupiter, Juno and Minerva, of which the podium in travertine remains almost whole; the present aspect comes from numerous interventions after earthquakes.

  • Where Piazza Andrea d'Isernia, Isernia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.fondoambiente.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it

Arco di San Pietro

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The bell tower leaning against the Cathedral on Corso Marcelli, with the large ogival arch through which passes the road and four Roman togate statues; the current structure dates back to the restorations of 1456 of Bishop Giacomo Montaquila.

  • Where Corso Marcelli, Isernia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.fondoambiente.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient center with churches, noble palaces, the Cathedral, the museum of Santa Maria delle Nunche and the Fraternal Fountain in Piazza Celestino V.

  • Where Centro storico, Isernia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.fondoambiente.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it
