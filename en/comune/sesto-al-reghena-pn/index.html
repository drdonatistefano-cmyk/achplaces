What to see in Sesto al Reghena

Borgo della pordenona plain crossed by the Reghena, born from a Roman military district on the road between Iulia Concordia and Norico; it grew around the Benedictine abbey of Santa Maria in Sylvis, in the Middle Ages among the major religious centers of Friuli, fortified after the incursions of the Hungarians and frescoed in the fourteenth century by the school of Giotto.

Abbazia di Santa Maria in Sylvis

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry

Benedictine monastery founded in the middle of the eighth century by the sons of the Longobard Duke Peter, who in 762 received the donation of the nobles Herphus, Mark and Anto; almost destroyed by the Hungarians, it was rebuilt by the abbot Adalberto II in the appearance of a castle. The three-nave church has the vestibule with Inferno, Paradiso and San Michele (about 1450, attributed to Antonio da Firenze) and the presbytery frescoed in 1324-1336 by artists close to the Padua lesson of Giotto; in the crypt the urn of Sant'Anastasia (VIII century), an Annunciation in marble of the late thirteenth century and a fifteenth-century Vesperbild, in the atrium the lapidary with Roman and high-medieval finds. Monday-Saturday 9-12 and 15-18, Sunday 11.30-12 and 15-18; not visitable during functions.

Corte abbaziale e Torre Grimani

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The courtyard of the abbey, similar to a piazza d'armi, is closed by the entrance tower with the coat of arms of the Grimani, the only surviving of the seven towers of the walls, from the bell tower of the XI-XII century perhaps born as a sighting tower, from the palace of the Chancellery and from the palace of the Abbot, which with the frescoed coat of arms of the five commendatory abbots houses today the Town Hall. Next to the entrance of the church the loggia with chivalrous frescoes of the 13th-XIV century.

Chiesetta di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small medieval church of the XII-XIII century with semicircular apse; the restoration, concluded in 1997, was preceded by excavations that revealed two levels of floor and burial from the XII to the XV century.

Chiesa di Ognissanti a Bagnarola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Of fourteenth-century origin, rebuilt in 1895-1899 with two naves and presbytery dome; it preserves a wooden crucifix of the sixteenth century, a baptismal font in stone of the early sixteenth century and a Pietà by Pomponio Amalteo of about 1540.
