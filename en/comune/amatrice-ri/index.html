What to see in Amatrice

Borgo at the foot of the Laga Mountains, in the National Park of Gran Sasso and Monti della Laga, almost completely destroyed by the earthquake of 24 August 2016: today you can visit the temporary civic museum "Floriana Svizzeretto" with the works saved and restored, the Laboratory Renaissance and the Polo del Gusto, as well as Lake Scandarello, Oasi Orie Terme and Conca Amatriciana, home of the spaghetti to the Amatriciana STG.

10 places to visit in Amatrice, in the province of Rieti (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo civico «Cola Filotesio» (ex chiesa di Sant'Emidio)

Museums · Old masters

Civic Museum of Religious Art established on September 7, 2002 in the fourteenth-century church of Sant'Emidio and named after Nicola Filotesio, called Cola dell'Amatrice: more than 80 works from the churches of Amatrice and the hamlets, including the Madonna with Child and San Giovannino di Cola (1527), the fifteenth-century processional crosses of Pietro Vannini (from Pinaco and Preta) and a Madonna with Child from the second half of the thirteenth century by Cossito.

after the earthquake of 2016 the works are exhibited in the temporary museum . Floriana Svizzeretto , temporary solution until the reopening of the Cola Filotesio (VisitLazio, 2023)

  • Where Ex chiesa di Sant'Emidio, Amatrice
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: gransassolagapark.it · visitlazio.com

Museo civico «Floriana Svizzeretto» e Area del Gusto

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated on July 28, 2023 next to the municipal park Giovanni Minozzi, in the Brignano Room, as the provisional seat of the civic museum: exhibits more than fifty works including paintings, sculptures, furnishings and sacred objects recovered and restored after the earthquake. In the connected area of Taste you can see the pipe organ of 1777 by Adriano Fedri Fedeli from the church of Santa Maria del Suffragio, a Renaissance wooden pulpit from the church of San Francesco and the canvas of the Eternal Benedict already at Cola Filotesio, restored with the Art Bonus for the Earthquake.

  • Where Presso il Parco comunale Giovanni Minozzi, Amatrice
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: uss-sisma2016.cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Laboratorio Rinascita

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Restoration center on the large square where the works saved from churches and collapsed buildings are recovered: here the organ of the church of Suffragio and several large canvases now at the museum Floriana Switzerland; the restorer Stefania Zucconi welcomes visitors.

  • Where Piazza grande, Amatrice
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Polo del Gusto e spaghetti all'amatriciana

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Public space designed by architect Boeri and born immediately after the earthquake to accommodate the local restaurants. The symbol dish, originally with pillow and pecorino cheese and with the tomato added in the eighteenth century, is Traditional Speciality Guaranteed from 6 March 2020; the Festival of Spaghetti at Amatriciana is held the last weekend of August. Among the products also cheese and pillow.

  • Where Polo del Gusto, Amatrice
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Lago di Scandarello

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Artificial basin of 1921 created by a dam on the Scandarello stream, reachable with an easy path; in summer nights the sky not polluted by lights is suitable for falling stars. During World War II, a local girl alerted the mines placed by the Nazis, avoiding the flooding of the Tronto Valley. VisitLazio indicates it as the stage not to be missed.

  • Where Lago di Scandarello, Amatrice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Oasi Orie Terme

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

At the hamlet of Configno, owned by the Betturri family since 1610 and oasis open to the public since 2005: a trail of about 3 km with local guide crosses the Sacred Wood of chestnuts, beech and oaks, climbs 140 steps to the square of the Three Mothers and passes through the lake in the shape of a heart and the Source of the Ories, between 22 deers and 12 deer on 18 hectares.

  • Where Configno, Amatrice
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Cerro di Sant'Angelo

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

In front of the small church of the Madonna di Galloro in the hamlet of Sant'Angelo, an oak over 21 meters high with circumference of 6.8 meters and estimated age over 600 years, considered the largest tree in Italy of its species.

  • Where Sant'Angelo, Amatrice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: gransassolagapark.it · visitlazio.com

Conca Amatriciana e Big Bench 246

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Basin in the heart of the National Park with centuries-old trees, pastures, streams and waterfalls that in winter become ice sculptures, crossed by the Path Italy and the Parks Way. The big bench Big Bench 246, 1000 meters between apples and cherry trees, can be reached from the parking lot with a short easy path and looks at 360° on the village, on the Laga Mountains and on the basin.

  • Where Conca Amatriciana, Amatrice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
