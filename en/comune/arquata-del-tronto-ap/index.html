What to see in Arquata del Tronto

A fortress town of the upper Tronto, the only municipality in Europe whose territory falls into two national parks (Monti Sibillini and Gran Sasso and Monti della Laga), dominated by the thirteenth-century Fortress; in the village the church of San Francesco houses an extract of the Holy Shroud and in Spelonga you can preserve the drape of Lepanto. After the earthquake of 2016 historic center, monuments and museums are only partially usable.

Rocca di Arquata

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

A 13th-century fortress overlooking the valley, with mighty walls, a 24-metre-high mast and a polygonal tower with Guelph merlons. The Park of the Fortress hosts the Night of Mysteries (18 August).

Borgo fortificato, Porta Sant'Agata e torre civica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

An ancient Roman settlement (statio) and example of thirteenth century architecture, described by the Park as a compact fortress city with walls and doors around church, noble palace and square. At the Porta Sant'Agata there were once gabelle; the civic tower of the Town Hall houses a bell of the fifteenth century.

after the earthquake of 2016 the old town is partially accessible and some areas are forbidden (red areas)

Chiesa di San Francesco e Sindone di Arquata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the village, under the castle, church with a small monastery of the 13th century, renovated until the 16th century; in the restoration of 1980 two pre-Romanesque bas-reliefs have emerged now in the upper part of the façade. It preserves an original "Extractum ab" of the Holy Shroud (1645), a polychrome wooden crucifix of 1440, a wooden altar of the seventeenth century and a altarpiece of 1527 with Madonna and Saints.

Tempietto della Madonna del Sole

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet Capodacqua, octagonal temple of the sixteenth century built by Nicola Falesio called Cola d'Amatrice, with frescoes attributed to him; inside a painting of the Annunciation and, above the minor entrance, an inscription dated 1550. In July there is the Feast of the Madonna del Carmelo.

Chiesa di Sant'Agata e drappo di Lepanto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In Spelonga, parish church of the second half of the fifteenth century with a Renaissance sandstone portal and a trussed cover; it houses 17th-century tempera, a 15th-century terracotta group, frescoes attributed to Panfilo da Spoleto and the Turkish flag that, according to tradition, a hundred Spelongans tore from an enemy ship in the battle of Lepanto (1571). Many goods come from St. Maria di Collepiccioni, destroyed in 1933; the "Festa bella" (Beautiful Festival) recalls Lepanto.

Pretare, borgo delle fate

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Frazione that preserves the remains of a furnace for live lime, from which perhaps derives the name, and, along the Pianelle Fosso, an ancient water mill restored and transformed into a dwelling with Liberty details. Legend has it that the fairies, surprised from dawn after dancing with the local youths, left their footprints on the side of Mount Vettore; the village staged the representation "The descent of fairies."

  • Entry Free

Forca di Presta e Forca Canapine

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Forca di Presta, at 1,550 m at the foot of Monte Vettore on the border between Marche and Umbria, offers in winter 25 km of cross-country skiing and ski touring slopes and ski lifts; in Forca Canapine, at 1,541 m, there are other skiing facilities on the border between the two regions.

Sentiero n. 6 e Grande Anello dei Sibillini

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The path no. 6, suitable for all, leads to a panoramic platform on the Laga Mountains and the ancient Via Salaria; the territory is also crossed by the Grande Anello dei Sibillini, a 120 km hiking trail completely signposted along the entire chain.
