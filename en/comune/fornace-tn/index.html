What to see in Fornace

One of the centers of the quadrilateral of the porphyry, grown around the castle of Roccabruna, now town hall; in the surroundings the Gothic church of Santo Stefano between the quarries and the path of Monte Piano on the traces of the medieval silver mines of Calisio.

Castello di Roccabruna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Also known as Castel Fornace, it was the mother house of the Roccabruna: Romanesque fortified residence that became a castle since the fourteenth century, passed to the Gaudenti and the Counts of Giovanelli and was rebuilt more sumptuous in the sixteenth century. Sold to the Municipality in 1851, it was partly demolished since 1854 with the loss of rooms and frescoes; since 1992 it is municipal seat. The tower door remains with two slits, the eastern wing with traces of the oldest battlements, a court corner with porch and loggia and a restored solar clock.

  • Where Piazza Castello 1, Fornace
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: cultura.trentino.it · visittrentino.info

Sentiero di Monte Piano e Pian del Gac'

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

A historical and cultural route by the Municipality that from Pian del Gac', a green area with playground, tennis courts and bowls and refreshment point, crosses acacia woods, silvestre pine, chestnut, larch and fir trees and pastures up to the lake of Santa Colomba, next to the traces of the silver mines of Monte Calisio: wells called "cadini" and galleries called "canope" . The extraction had the greatest splendor under the prince bishop Federico Wanga (1207-1218) and almost completely ceased at the end of the sixteenth century; about 80 dead bodies remain open.

  • Where Località Pian del Gacc, Fornace
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: comune.fornace.tn.it · visittrentino.info

Chiesetta di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Gothic church isolated between the porphyry quarries near Fornace; near the church were found ancient graves and coins of the emperors Domitian, Trajan, Tito and others.

  • Where Santo Stefano, Fornace
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: cultura.trentino.it · visittrentino.info
