What to see in Vaccarizzo Albanese

Arbëreshë community born from Albanian refugees who arrived between the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, which preserves its language and traditions: the Museum of Costume and Arbëresh Ori in the eighteenth century Cumane Palace and the historic center with the two churches of Santa Maria di Costantinopoli, of Greek and Latin rite.

Museo del costume e degli ori arbëresh

Museums · Themed

It is located in Palazzo Cumano, built by the Cumano brothers in 1763. It exhibits traditional gala costumes, half gala and newspapers from different arbëreshe communities (Vaccarizzo, Frascineto, Farneta, San Demetrio Corone, Piana degli Albanesi, Santa Sofia d'Epiro, Spezzano Albanese); on the upper floor there is the collection of gold and reproductions of ancient local jewels, with an exhibition of photographs and lithographies of Albanian costumes. Booking not required; MiC card does not report up-to-date times.

Centro storico e chiese di Santa Maria di Costantinopoli

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Ancient rural village of the principality of Bisignano and Corigliano farmhouse under Sanseverino and Saluzzo, where Albanian refugees came to escape from the Turks between the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries. In the historic hilly centre there are two parish churches dedicated to Santa Maria di Costantinopoli, one of Greek rite and one of Latin rite, the church of the Confraternity, the palace of the town hall and some old fountains.
