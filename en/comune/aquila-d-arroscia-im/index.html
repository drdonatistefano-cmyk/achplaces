What to see in Aquila d'Arroscia

Borgo della Valle Arroscia, the site of the archaeological laboratory of the territorial museum "I volte dell'Ubagu" (The Faces of the Ubagu), from which starts a naturalistic, speleological and archaeological path towards the valleys Pennavaira and Ferraia, between the caves (the arms) inhabited since prehistory and the ruins of the castle.

Laboratorio archeologico della Val Pennavaire

Museums · Archaeological

The site of the territorial museum "I volte dell'Ubagu" of the Arroscia Valley, tells about 10,000 years of local history, from prehistory to today: from the nearby caves, called pre-Roman Ligurian name "arma" are stone instruments, ceramics of the Iron Age and remains of fireplaces. A plastic exhibition where the caves are accessible, reachable by a signposted path; educational room and guided tours. Tel. 0183 382057.

MiC card indicates closed every day: check opening with the Municipality

Sentiero delle grotte e ruderi del castello

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the village a path marked naturalistic, speleological and archaeological climbs towards the valleys Pennavaira and Ferraia, passing next to the ruins of the castle and reaching the caves ('arme') that have returned finds from prehistory to the Iron Age. The castle, on the hill of San Giacomo behind the village, is a bound asset that the Province attributes to the Marquises of Clavesana and indicates in bad state of conservation.
