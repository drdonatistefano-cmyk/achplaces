What to see in Nettuno

Medieval village overlooking the sea, linked to the Allied landing on 22 January 1944: the Fort Sangallo with the Museum of Landing and the Antiquarium, the American Cemetery, the sanctuary of Santa Maria Goretti, the village with Palazzo Doria-Pamphilj and, to the south, the castle of Torre Astura.

10 places to visit in Nettuno, in the province of Rome (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Forte Sangallo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Quadrangular fortress with four bulwarks and 5 meters thick walls, built between 1501 and 1503 by Antonio da Sangallo at the behest of Cesare Borgia, to defend from the sea of Neptune, It was the Colonna until 1594, then the Apostolic Chamber and since 1831 the Borghese; in 1925 the convention between Italy and Yugoslavia on the Italians of Dalmatia was signed. Today it is municipal and hosts the Museum of Landing, the Antiquarium and exhibitions.

  • Where Via Antonio Gramsci 5, Nettuno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.nettuno.roma.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Museo dello Sbarco alleato

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Inaugurated in 1990 in Fort Sangallo, it tells the Allied landing of 22 January 1944 on the coast of Anzio and Neptune with documents, original photographs, maps, newspapers, uniforms, helmets, weapons, diaries and personal objects of soldiers and war material found in the area.

  • Where Via Antonio Gramsci 5, Nettuno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.nettuno.roma.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Antiquarium comunale

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

Small archaeological museum in Fort Sangallo with finds of the territory, especially Romans: a section of paleontology and prehistory from the Pleistocene to the Iron Age and a Roman section with salt on the maritime trade (very from Torre Astura), sacred objects, funerary finds and villas.

  • Where Via Antonio Gramsci 5, Nettuno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.nettuno.roma.it · italia.it

Cimitero americano di Sicilia e Roma

Places of worship · Monumental cemeteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

77 acres American war cemetery, dedicated in 1956: 7.845 buried and 3.095 names of missing engraved on the white marble walls of the chapel; the memorial also includes a peristyle and a map room with a bronze relief and four frescoed maps of operations in Sicily and Italy, among Italian gardens. Visitor centre opened in 2014. Open every day 9-17 (last entry 16.45), closed on 25 December and 1 January.

  • Where Piazzale Kennedy 1, Nettuno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.abmc.gov

Sources: abmc.gov · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Borgo medievale e Palazzo Baronale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village, built towards the end of the 10th century against the Saracen incursions, preserves cylindrical towers of the fourteenth century, Piazza Colonna and alleys; the Baronale Palace, residence of the Colonna (which held Neptune from 1426 to 1594), has the clock tower with the coat of arms and the inscription of the works of Marcantonio Colonna of 1569. The Cavone is the ancient walk along the walls with a view of the port.

  • Where Piazza Marcantonio Colonna 4, Nettuno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.nettuno.roma.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Palazzo Doria-Pamphilj

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Born around 1600 as Casino Cesi, it was bought in 1648 by Camillo Pamphilj who expanded it to the present form; it preserves a cycle of frescoes by Pier Francesco Mola. In 1697 the pontifical court of Innocent XII stayed there, of which the coat of arms remains.

Collegiata di San Giovanni Battista ed Evangelista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Of medieval origin, according to tradition on the temple of the god Neptune (in the subsoil Roman finds have emerged), it was entirely rebuilt between 1738 and 1748 on a project by Carlo Marchionni.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Santuario di Nostra Signora delle Grazie e Santa Maria Goretti

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Neo-Gothic, wanted in 1914 by the Passionist Fathers with the contribution of Pius X where the church of the Annunziata stood; it houses the wooden statue of the Madonna arrived according to the tradition from England in 1550 and, in the crypt, since 1929, the remains of Santa Maria Goretti. In May the statue goes in procession to the Collegiate.

  • Where Piazza San Rocco 1, Nettuno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.nettuno.roma.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Torre Astura

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Castle on an islet at the mouth of the Astura, connected to the mainland by an arched bridge: it was built by the Frangipane in 1193 on the Roman remains, including a fish pond that emerges with water and luxury villas; in 1268 Corradino di Svevia was sheltered. It is often film set (The Adventures of Pinocchio).

within the military range: free beach accessible only from early July to September, from Foceverde (VisitLatium)

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it · italia.it

Riserva naturale Villa Borghese di Nettuno

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Regional reserve of 46 hectares between Neptune and Anzio: the historic villa on a hill overlooking the sea, with Italian garden, orange garden and forest of farnie, holm oaks, corks and pine trees; under the villa galleries used during the Allied landing. D'Annunzio stayed there in the early 20th century.

private property, access methods not indicated (Latium Parks)
