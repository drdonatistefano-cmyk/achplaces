What to see in Lucignano

Walled village of Valdichiana Aretina, extraordinary example of medieval urbanism with concentric rings.

4 places to visit in Lucignano, in the province of Arezzo (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 2 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Museo Comunale e Albero d'Oro

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the Palazzo Comunale Pale, Triptychs and liturgical furnishings; in the center the golden reliquary called Tree of Gold, or Tree of Love, which according to tradition brings luck to lovers.

  • Where Piazza del Tribunale, Lucignano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

12th century church with travertine and sandstone façade, with a polyptych attributed to Luca di Tommè where the Madonna holds the castle of Lucignano.

  • Where Piazza del Tribunale, Lucignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Collegiata di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The collegiate with seventeenth-century paintings attributed to Matteo Rosselli, Giacinto Gimignani and Rutilio Manetti.

  • Where Piazza del Tribunale, Lucignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Santuario della Madonna delle Querce e Gargonza

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Outside the walls the sanctuary attributed to Vasari and the remains of a Medici castle; a little further, among the woods, the fourteenth-century fortified village of Gargonza.

  • Where Lucignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
