Paesaggio vitivinicolo UNESCO – Monferrato degli Infernot

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Frassinello is one of the nine municipalities of the core zone The infernots are ancient deep cellars dug directly into a particular rock similar to tuff.

Infernot Accornero

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · UNESCO World Heritage

Small underground cell excavated in the "Pietra da Cantoni" (Pietra da Cantoni), the sandstone typical of the area, accessible from the cellar: like the other infernots, which exist only in Monferrato and were dug by the inhabitants between the late nineteenth and early twentieth century, it served to preserve the wine thanks to constant temperature and humidity, dark, absence of ventilation and silence. It is part of the UNESCO Award of 2014 and is located at the restaurant Fer.Mo.

  • Where Via XX Settembre 7, Frassinello Monferrato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.frassinellomonferrato.al.it

Sources: alexala.it · comune.frassinellomonferrato.al.it

Castello di Frassinello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

At the top of the village, it is the most important building in the village: for centuries it was the noble residence of the Nemours family, then Sacchi-Nemors, counts of Frassinello, whose coat of arms is still in the floor of the salon of honor.

the Municipality lists it among the accommodation facilities (B&B and venue of events): visitable only on special occasions

Parrocchiale di Maria Vergine Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It preserves a 12-log organ built in 1864 by the Pavese organaro Luigi Lingiardi and the instruments of the Passion of Christ used in the traditional ceremony of the Entierro.

Borgo e chiesa di Sant'Anna

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

From Piazza Regina Margherita a walk follows the streets of the village and the old walks of the medieval walls; along the route the church of Sant'Anna preserves an artistic funerary stele of the prince of Salm Salm.
