What to see in Montalcino

The city of Brunello, closed by walls and dominated by the fourteenth-century Fortress, in the UNESCO landscape of Val d'Orcia.

6 places to visit in Montalcino, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 5 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Fortezza di Montalcino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The fourteenth-century fortress with pentagonal plan at the highest point of the village, almost unchanged from the Middle Ages, with towers and walkway: the courtyard is free, the climb on the stands is paid.

  • Where Montalcino
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Abbazia di Sant'Antimo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient Benedictine monastery in travertine and alabaster nine kilometers from Montalcino, among the masterpieces of the Italian Romanesque; legend wants it founded by Charlemagne in 781.

  • Where Castelnuovo dell'Abate, Montalcino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo Civico e Diocesano d'Arte Sacra

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The museum that brings together the sacred works of art of the territory of Montalcino and an archaeological section, reported by Visit Tuscany between the stages in the village.

  • Where Montalcino
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo della Comunità di Montalcino e del Brunello

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the former convent the immersive route "Tempio del Brunello" dedicated to wine and the history of the territory.

  • Where Montalcino
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Piazza del Popolo e Palazzo dei Priori

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The main square with the Town Hall of the thirteenth century, whose thin tower is the symbol of the village, adorned with the coats of arms of the Podestà.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Montalcino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Cattedrale di San Salvatore e Madonna del Soccorso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The cathedral of the village and the sanctuary of the Madonna del Soccorso, reported by Visit Tuscany among the churches of Montalcino.

  • Where Montalcino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
