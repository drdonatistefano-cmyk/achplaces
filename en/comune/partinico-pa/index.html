What to see in Partinico

Town of the plain between Palermo and the Gulf of Castellammare, with the churches of Corso dei Mille (the Mother Church of the Annunziata, San Leonardo with a canvas by Pietro Novelli, San Giuseppe, the Carmine), the Sanctuary of the Madonna del Ponte, the baroque fountain of 1716, the sixteenth-century towers of the countryside, the Poma Lake and the village of Borgo Parrini, a village of artists from colored houses.

Borgo Parrini

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Frazione was born in the sixteenth century when the Jesuit fathers of the novitiate of Palermo, called "Parrini" (Parrini) bought land in the northeast of Partinico building a mill, houses for peasants, watchtowers, warehouses and a chapel dedicated to Maria SS. del Rosario; then passed to the Duke of Aumale, who produced the Moscatello dello Zucco. Today, recovered by private individuals, it is a village of artists with white houses with blue doors, murals, mosaic sculptures and majolica from the atmosphere that recalls Gaudí. The visit takes less than an hour, with parking at the entrance.

  • Where Borgo Parrini, Partinico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.partinico.pa.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · comune.partinico.pa.it

Chiesa Madre di Maria SS. Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The original nucleus, dedicated to St. Christopher, dates back to 1552-1570; the naves are from 1639-1642, the bell tower was completed in 1783 and the facade rebuilt in 1960 by Antonio Valenti. It preserves the Assumption of Antonino Spatafora called the Panormita, dated 1579, and four paintings of the Lombard school of the seventeenth century with Santa Maddalena, Santa Rosalia, Sant'Agata and Santa Caterina.

  • Where Piazza Duomo (ingresso da Corso dei Mille), Partinico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.partinico.pa.it

Sources: comune.partinico.pa.it

Chiesa di San Leonardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1634 by the SS Company. Sacramento and seat of the Civic Council until 1819, preserves the Ascise of Saint Augustine in the presence of the SS. Trinity of Pietro Novelli called Monrealese (1640 about), an Adoration of the Magi of the eighteenth century and the Transfiguration on the vault; the bell tower has eighteenth century majolica.

  • Where Corso dei Mille angolo Piazza Garibaldi, Partinico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.partinico.pa.it

Sources: comune.partinico.pa.it

Chiesa di San Giuseppe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1737-1739 on the old church of San Francesco, with bell tower of 1780: it contains six octagonal paintings by the brothers Antonio and Vincenzo Manno, a wooden statue attributed to Domenico Noffo (1670) and a canvas by D'Antoni from 1857.

  • Where Corso dei Mille, Partinico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.partinico.pa.it

Sources: comune.partinico.pa.it

Chiesa e Palazzo del Carmine

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Former convent of the Carmelites with the church, restored several times, today seat of the Municipal Library and municipal offices. In the church, parish since 1950, paintings by Vincenzo Manno of 1819 (Madonna del Carmelo, Madonna and San Gaetano, Holy Family), two golden wooden candlesticks and an 18th century chair.

  • Where Corso dei Mille 252, Partinico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.partinico.pa.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It is annexed to the Capuchin monastery (1617-1619), has on the high altar the Queen of the Angels of Salerno (1630), a Blessed Bernard from Corleone by Vincenzo Manno, a Via Crucis of 14 wooden tablets of the eighteenth century and a sundial on the facade.

  • Where Piazza V.E. Orlando, Partinico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.partinico.pa.it

Santuario della Madonna del Ponte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Linked to the donation of Frederick II of Aragon to the Cistercians in 1306, it was renovated to three naves in 1774; it houses a painting by Vincenzo Manno of 1819. The week after Easter the feast is held with pilgrimage to torches and the canvas is taken in procession to Partinico, to return to the sanctuary in November.

  • Where Strada provinciale 63 (vicino allo svincolo di Balestrate della A29), Partinico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.partinico.pa.it

Sources: comune.partinico.pa.it

Antica Fontana barocca

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Built in 1716 by Cardinal Acquaviva, abbot of Santa Maria d'Altofonte, and relocated in 1824 by the Baron Mayor of Leccia: in carved marble, it has a rectangular tank, eight cinnamons, four Latin inscriptions and the cardinal's coat of arms, with fence and liberty cast iron lamps.

  • Where Partinico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.partinico.pa.it

Sources: comune.partinico.pa.it

Torre A Braciara

History and architecture · Towers

Probably sixteenth-century tower with a square plan of about 9 meters on the side and 15 meters high, with three floors and terrace; in the eighteenth century belonged to the Marquis of Villabianca, whose marble coat of arms is on the south facade. The name comes from the ancient production of coal.

  • Where Contrada Braciara (stradella dalla provinciale per Montelepre), Partinico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.partinico.pa.it

Sources: comune.partinico.pa.it

Villa Comunale Regina Margherita

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

The nineteenth-century garden on the Gambacorta Plan, belonged to the Capuchins, with plane trees planted around 1850, large Figus magnolioides, pines and palm trees, the busts of Garibaldi (1883) and Vittorio Emanuele II (1866) of Civiletti and an amphitheatre for the band.

  • Where Partinico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.partinico.pa.it

Sources: comune.partinico.pa.it

Lago Poma

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Invaded built between 1963 and 1968, with 163.6 hectares of surface, which irrigates the plain of Partinico and supplies Palermo; since 1994 it is Oasis of protection and shelter of the fauna on 580 hectares.

  • Where Partinico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.partinico.pa.it

Sources: comune.partinico.pa.it
