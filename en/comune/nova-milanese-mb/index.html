What to see in Nova Milanese

Crossed by the Villoresi Canal, inaugurated in 1881, Nova Milanese houses in Villa Vertua, reopened in 2024 after the restoration, the civic artistic collection with the Collection of Fire Arts and the donation of the painter Vittorio Viviani; in the village the parish church of Sant'Antonino Martire, the seventeenth-century Villa Manfredini Ferrari Ardicini and the Parco Grugnotorto Villoresi.

Villa Vertua e Civica Raccolta Artistica (Arti del Fuoco e Donazione Viviani)

Museums · Modern art

Villa of the early twentieth century in Empire style with garden in English, has been the donation of the painter of chierista Vittorio Viviani (ten paintings and more than fifty drawings); restored in 1998 and again until the reopening of 2024, it houses the civic artistic collection. The Arts of the Fire section brings together contemporary painting and sculpture on porcelain, ceramics, terracotta, glass and glazes, awarded at the National Competition "Arti del fuoco" (Fire Arts), the painting section also includes the works of the Vittorio Viviani Prize, the ...Bice Bugatti-Giovanni Segantini Prize and the Engraving Review (1996-2014).

open only Saturday 15-19 and Sunday 10-12 and 15-19, closed from Monday to Friday, according to the Municipality

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Antonino Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the eighteenth century and then renovated and enlarged, it preserves paintings of Bignami, stained glass windows designed by Luigi Morgari and a bezel by the painter Vittorio Viviani, nineths of adoption.

Parco Grugnotorto Villoresi

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

One of the main green areas north of Milan, over 800 hectares of cultivated fields, tree-lined rows, woods, canals and cycle paths, created to protect the last agricultural and natural spaces after the urbanization of Brianza. The need for protection dates back to the eighties and the park was officially established in 1999 by several municipalities; the name comes from the Catasto Teresiano of 1722.

  • Where Ufficio del parco: Palazzo Municipale, Piazza Gio.I.A., Nova Milanese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page comune.novamilanese.mb.it
