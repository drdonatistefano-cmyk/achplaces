What to see in Cuorgnè

Ancient merchant village of the Upper Canavese on the Orco: the medieval nucleus winds along the portico via Arduino, with the Gothic house called King Arduino and the two towers of discord, the roundabout and the square; in the former Manifattura of via Ivrea are located the Archaeological Museum of the Upper Canavese and the art museum dedicated to caricaturist Carlin Bergoglio.

8 places to visit in Cuorgnè, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Via Arduino e il borgo porticato

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The street collects the most typical elements of the medieval village and is an example of an ancient mercantile village: the shops looked out onto the arcades, born at a later moment as canopies to repair the goods on display, and the traffic passed inside the village. At the entrance stood the main door, called "Piè del Borgo" (Piè del Borgo), almost all the street is flanked by arcades and in some stretches remains the original pavement. The building at number 11, with wooden ceiling, is the oldest in the village.

  • Where Via Arduino, Cuorgnè
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cuorgne.to.it

Casa di re Arduino

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Overlooking the Via Arduino, it is a Gothic building on a three-arched porch, with wooden ceiling and terracotta decorations; according to tradition King Arduino stayed there. It is visible only from the outside.

  • Where Via Arduino 29, Cuorgnè
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cuorgne.to.it

Sources: comune.cuorgne.to.it

Torre rotonda (o del Carlevato)

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The only remains of the ancient castle of Cuorgnè, probably built in the 11th century, according to some scholars on the foundations of a late Roman watchtower. It is built in river pebbles laid with fishbone; the popular name comes from a legend set around the year 1000, on a poor young man who returns rich and is welcomed by an old friend.

  • Where Via Arduino 26, Cuorgnè
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cuorgne.to.it

Sources: comune.cuorgne.to.it

Torre quadrata (o dell'Orologio)

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Made to be built between 1330 and 1380 by the Valperga, higher than the round tower of rivals and a short distance from it: it is one of the towers of discord born from the struggles between the local lords. In 1469 he passed to the municipality, which used it as a watchtower and then placed the civic bell and the clock.

  • Where Via Arduino 37, Cuorgnè
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cuorgne.to.it

Sources: comune.cuorgne.to.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the center of the village, it was the church of the Brotherhood of Mercy (attested in 1487); documents of 1378 already testify to the existence, in another site just outside the walls, while the current building dates back to the early seventeenth century. It has only one nave and two side baroque altars; the altar of the Sorrows is by the sculptor Pietro Antonio Perucca di Valperga, who completed it in 1708.

  • Where Via Arduino 37, Cuorgnè
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cuorgne.to.it

Museo archeologico dell'Alto Canavese

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the former Manifattura di Cuorgnè, the archaeological collection is the result of thirty years of research on the territory and includes exhibits of exceptional importance on the prehistory and local protohistory. Open from Monday to Friday 9-17; Saturday and holidays only groups by reservation.

  • Where Ex Manifattura, Via Ivrea 100, Cuorgnè
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cuorgne.to.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.cuorgne.to.it

Museo d'Arte Carlin Bergoglio (Galleria d'arte moderna e contemporanea del Canavese)

Museums · Modern art

Inaugurated in 2014 in the former Manufacture, it is dedicated to the painter, journalist and caricaturist Carlo Bergoglio named Carlin (1895-1959): about 200 paintings, caricatures and personal objects, with many subjects of Canaves' rural life and sports caricatures for Guerin Sportivo, Gazzetta del Popolo and Tuttosport. It also includes the Clizia Collection, 100 works by Clizia ceramist. Open Monday to Friday 9-17.

  • Where Via Ivrea 100, Cuorgnè
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cuorgne.to.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.cuorgne.to.it

Antico ponte sull'Orco

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the Middle Ages the bridge made Cuorgnè an important shopping centre, a necessary passage for merchants from Ivrea to Avigliana and the Valley of Susa; the oldest document that cites it is of 1439, and in 1469 the construction of a more lasting bridge on the site of the current Ponte Vecchio was entrusted to Giovanni di Piacenza. Many times damaged by the floods (1491, 1780), it had thirteen stone and lime arches; after the floods of 1845-1846 it was decided to rebuild a hundred meters further downstream, with works started in 1850 on a project by engineer Edward Capello.

  • Where Piazzale Pontevecchio, Cuorgnè
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cuorgne.to.it

Sources: comune.cuorgne.to.it
