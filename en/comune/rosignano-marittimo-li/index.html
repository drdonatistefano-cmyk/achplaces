What to see in Rosignano Marittimo

On the Costa degli Etruschi, from the walled village of Rosignano with the museum of Palazzo Bombardieri to the promontory of Castiglioncello, with the castle Pasquini and the museum that houses the Etruscan urns, up to the beaches of Vada.

8 places to visit in Rosignano Marittimo, in the province of Livorno (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Rosignano Marittimo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The oldest nucleus, probably of the 10th century, with the palaces of the main owners, the Archbishop's Farm that housed the town hall and looks at the sea and the hills, the church of Sant'Ilario, the parish with a wooden crucifix of the fourteenth century and a Madonna delle Grazie of the fifteenth century, well preserved towers and the cistern.

  • Where Rosignano Marittimo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Museo civico archeologico di Palazzo Bombardieri

Museums · Archaeological

On three floors of the medieval palace, from prehistory to the Middle Ages: materials from the Etruscan necropolis including the stems of Castiglioncello, amphorae, anchors, coins and vases from the ancient commercial port of San Gaetano a Vada, late-medieval ceramics and majolica of the castle; on the ground floor the reconstruction of a Roman domus of the first century A.D.

  • Where Via del Castello, Rosignano Marittimo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Museo di Storia naturale

Museums · Science and university

Collections of shells, insects, reptiles, minerals and fossils, a large ornithological collection, a permanent exhibition of paleoanthropology and a herbarium with sections on the Mediterranean scrub, aromatic herbs and aquatic plants.

  • Where Via Monte alla Arena, Rosignano Marittimo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Castello Pasquini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Neo-Gothic castle on an artificial platform supported by a large bastion, surrounded by a forest with winding paths, built on the former farm of Diego Martelli: it was wanted since 1889 by Baron Fausto Lazzaro Patrone, then passed to the Birindelli and in 1938 to Count Ugo Pasquini; since 1979 it is of the Municipality and is managed by the Armunia Foundation as a center of culture.

  • Where Piazza della Vittoria, Castiglioncello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.rosignano.livorno.it · visittuscany.com · italia.it

Museo archeologico nazionale di Castiglioncello

Museums · Archaeological

Built in 1914 in the form of a Hellenistic temple designed by Luigi Milani, in the pine forest of the Poggetto: furnishings of over 300 Etruscan and Roman tombs of the promontory (end IV - beginning I century BC), with the urn in alabaster of the II century BC that contained the ashes of the Etruscan noblewoman Velia Cerinei.

  • Where Via del Museo 6, Castiglioncello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Parco dei Poggetti e Fonte dei Poggetti

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Between sea pine trees and Mediterranean maquis, wide views from Castiglioncello to the Gulf of Baratti; paths to the Fonte dei Poggetti with wash basins and windmill.

  • Where Rosignano Marittimo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Costa di Castiglioncello e Pineta Marradi

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Red cliffs, sheltered bays and coves on the small promontory; walks from Punta Righini to the marina through the Pineta Marradi.

  • Where Castiglioncello, Rosignano Marittimo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Spiagge bianche di Vada e Rosignano Solvay

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

About 5 km of sandy beaches with pine forests behind, several times Blue Flag; the white colour of the coast of Rosignano Solvay derives from the calcareous residues of baking soda processing and bathing is not allowed in all stretches.

  • Where Vada e Rosignano Solvay
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it
