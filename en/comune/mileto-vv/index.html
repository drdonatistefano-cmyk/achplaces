What to see in Mileto

The capital of the Norman County of Ruggero I and the birthplace of Ruggero II, destroyed by the earthquake of 1783 and rebuilt not far away; today you can visit it between the medieval archaeological park of ancient Mileto, the cathedral and the museum in the bishop's palace, with views of the valley of the Messima.

Parco archeologico medievale di Mileto antica

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 40 hectares along SP 10, the first medieval archaeological park in Calabria, on the site of the Norman capital abandoned after the earthquake of 1783. On the eastern hill the remains of the abbey of the Most Holy Trinity, founded by Ruggero I and consecrated on December 29, 1080; on the opposite hill the Norman cathedral, the bishop's complex and the Lamie district; in the middle the ancient inhabited, largely still buried. It's a stop on the Norman Way.

  • Where Strada Provinciale 10, Mileto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website sabap-rc.cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sabap-rc.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo nazionale di Mileto

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the bishop's palace completed around 1860, on two floors: Roman marbles re-used by Ruggero I in the abbey of the Most Holy Trinity, the fourteenth-century sarcophagi of Ruggero Sanseverino and Giovanna d'Aquino, sacred furnishings of the cathedral, ceramics, paintings and an ivory crucifix attributed to Alessandro Algardi, who arrived in Mileto in 1851.

MiC card indicates the site temporarily closed for PNRR work

  • Where Via Episcopio, Mileto
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Neo-Romanesque cathedral next to the bishop's palace, minor basilica since 2016; heir to the Norman cathedral of ancient Miletus, seat of the first Latin diocese of southern Italy.

  • Where Via Episcopio, Mileto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Santuario di Santa Maria della Cattolica

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In the old town, it houses a wooden statue of the Madonna and Child of about 1700; heir of a parish church of the same name of Byzantine age, in use until the late 18th century.

  • Where Centro storico, Mileto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.mileto.vv.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Paravati

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In the hamlet of Paravati, large church of 3,000 places linked to Natuzza Evolo, a destination of numerous pilgrimages.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
