What to see in San Giovanni Rotondo

The town of Gargano is linked to San Pio da Pietrelcina, who lived in the Capuchin monastery from 1916 to 1968: the new sanctuary of Renzo Piano with the crypt of the saint, the church of Santa Maria delle Grazie, the early Christian baptistery of the Round and a medieval nucleus with towers of the ancient walls.

Santuario di San Pio da Pietrelcina

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Designed by Renzo Piano and inaugurated on 1 July 2004 after about ten years of work, wanted by the Capuchin Friars Minor and financed almost entirely by the offerings of pilgrims. The spiral plant, inspired by the spiral of Archimedes, places the altar in the middle; walls in stone of Apricena, oxidised copper cover and a window with scenes of the Apocalypse. In the lower church the crypt where St. Pius' body has been resting since 2010.

Convento dei Cappuccini e chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry

The convent, founded in 1540, was the house of Padre Pio from September 1916 to his death in September 1968; here he received the stigmata in 1918. Next to it are the ancient church, originally dedicated to Santa Maria degli Angeli and consecrated in 1629, and the church of Santa Maria delle Grazie built on a project by the architect Giuseppe Gentile of Boiano and consecrated on 1 July 1959.

  • Where Piazzale Santa Maria delle Grazie, San Giovanni Rotondo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista «della Rotonda»

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It combines a high medieval baptistery with circular plan, the Round, and a rectangular nave probably added from the twelfth century; the baptismal font of the V-VI century, brought to light by the excavations of 2014, is one of the rare early Christian baptisteries of southern Italy. Inside frescoes from the 13th to the 1st 15th century, with the most important phase in the 14th century. According to the municipality the name of the city could derive from this building.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di Sant'Orsola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Started at the end of the sixteenth century and almost completely rebuilt between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, originally "Church of Purgatory" is from 1638 the seat of the Archconfraternity of the Dead. Curved façade with portal in the breach and stone statues of St. Francis and St. Anthony; inside Neapolitan canvases of the seventeenth century, the Purgatory of Nunzio De Nunzi (1707), a Venetian Deposition of the sixteenth century, a sixteenth-century organ and paintings of the Christmas Vault Penati (1938).

Museo biografico di Padre Pio

Museums · Themed

The wax museum was set up in 2001 at Palazzo Morcaldi, home of Mayor Francesco Morcaldi, friend of Padre Pio, who in the 1920s opposed his transfer: ten scenes tell of the main moments of the life of the saint, chosen by a committee of historians with the advice of the Capuchins.

Grava di Campolato

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

In the territory of the Gargano National Park that surrounds the city, it is the largest cavity of Gargano and the deepest of Puglia, a destination of speleologists.
