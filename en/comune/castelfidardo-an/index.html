What to see in Castelfidardo

The city of accordion, with the international museum in the Town Hall and the International Prize of September, and the battle place of 1860 that brought Marche and Umbria to the Kingdom of Sardinia, remembered by the National Monument of the Marches, the Ossario-Sacrario and the Museum of the Risorgimento; around, the Selva, ancient wreck wood.

Museo internazionale della fisarmonica

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Inaugurated on May 9, 1981 after a city delegation proposed on TV, at ...Portobello, to collect ancient accordions; it celebrates Paolo Soprani, who in 1863 founded Castelfidardo the first Italian industry of the instrument. It occupies over 500 m2 in a restored seventeenth-century wing of the Palazzo Comunale and exhibits 100 instruments chosen from a collection of about 500; according to Italia.it also houses a collection of Giuseppe Panini, the inventor of figurines.

  • Where Via Mordini 1, Castelfidardo
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museodellafisarmonica.it

Sources: museodellafisarmonica.it · letsmarche.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo del Risorgimento

Museums · City history

Civic museum dedicated to the battle of Castelfidardo, in which the Sabaud troops defeated the papal army opening the way to the annexation of Marche and Umbria to the Kingdom of Sardinia; the collection is set up in the halls of Palazzo Ciriaco Mordini and the visit is completed with the places of battle.

  • Where Palazzo Ciriaco Mordini, Castelfidardo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Monumento Nazionale delle Marche

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Monument in bronze to the "Vittoriosi di Castelfidardo" by the sculptor Vito Pardo, completed in 1911: depicts General Cialdini riding at the head of thirty Piedmontese soldiers against the papal troops.

  • Where Castelfidardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Ossario-Sacrario dei Caduti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

On the site of the Battle of Castelfidardo, the shrine-hospital preserves the remains and honors the fallen of the battle between the Savoy army and the pontifical.

  • Where Luoghi della battaglia, Castelfidardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Centro storico, Palazzo Comunale e mura

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The town stands on a rocky spur still partly surrounded by the medieval walls; the Palazzo Comunale, an ancient palazzo dei priori restored in the eighteenth century, houses the accordion museum. Among other attractions Italy.it indicates Porta Marina, the aqueduct tower and numerous churches.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica, Castelfidardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Fisarmonica: Palazzo delle Esposizioni e Premio Internazionale

Local life and traditions · Crafts

The Palazzo delle Esposizioni hosts exhibitions and events, including an accordion and an organ that entered the Guinness of Records; every September the International Accordion Award attracts musicians from all over the world and in June the European Music Festival is held.
