What to see in Santa Venerina

Borgo on the eastern slopes of Etna, founded at the beginning of the 18th century as Porta in the territory of Acireale, where volcanic herb liqueurs are distilled.

Distilleria Fratelli Russo dell'Etna

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

In the village are distilled liqueurs with volcanic herbs, which can be bought at the distillery of the Russian Brothers, reported by italia.it on the itinerary between Etna and Taormina.

Chiesa di Maria Santissima Immacolata a Dagala del Re

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of the hamlet Dagala del Re, in the Diocese of Acireale. According to the census of the churches of the CEI was built in 1631-1632, enlarged in 1710-1715, rebuilt in 1843 and again enlarged in 1861-1872 and 1904-1909, with the completion of the building by 1945; altar and ambo were the subject of interventions in 2008.

  • Where Piazza Immacolata, Dagala del Re, Santa Venerina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.cittametropolitana.ct.it

Sources: chieseitaliane.chiesacattolica.it · cittametropolitana.ct.it
