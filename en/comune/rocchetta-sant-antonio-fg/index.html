What to see in Rocchetta Sant'Antonio

Borgo dei Monti Dauni, Orange Flag of the Touring, dominated by the Castle of Aquino with the ogival tower "a bow of ship" with the church matrix eighteenth century by the majolica bell tower, numerous fountains and, outside the village, the Sanctuary of the Madonna del Pozzo.

Castello d'Aquino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Triangular fortress with an ogival watchtower similar to the bow of a ship, begun at the end of the fifteenth century by the Orsini and completed in 1507 by Ladislao II d'Aquino, Marquis of Corato, who had purchased the fief in 1501; the project is attributed, without certainty, to Francesco di Giorgio Martini.

Chiesa matrice dell'Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1754 and 1768 on a project by the Barletano Giovanni Mancarelli, originally dedicated to Saint Anthony Abbot; the octagonal bell tower of 1588 has a dome covered with colored majolica. The Baroque interior with three naves has polychrome stuccoes, marble altars by Luigi Cimafonte, the ...Madonna del Cardellino of Giaquinto, choir in walnut and sacristy furnishings by Liberato Villani; in front of the church a seat of the sixteenth century.

  • Where Centro storico, Rocchetta Sant'Antonio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Centro storico, Torre civica e Palazzo Bartimmo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval and Renaissance buildings alternate with small houses; the upper part goes back to the Norman foundation and the streets towards the castle are of medieval plant. Among the noble palaces with portals and carved balconies stands out Palazzo Bartimmo, with eighteenth-century portal and loggia with three arches; at the top of the village, behind the castle, the clock tower. There are also some remains of the fortress of 984, erected by the feudal Roberto del Torpo.

  • Where Centro storico, Rocchetta Sant'Antonio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiese del borgo: San Giuseppe, Vergine delle Grazie, Immacolata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

At the top of the village the chapel of St. Joseph contains a 17th century painting of the Holy Family and statues of St. Lucia, St. Mary of Constantinople, St. Joseph and Our Lady of Incoronata; in the district Pescara the church of the Virgin of Graces has late Baroque portal and numerous paintings of the eighteenth century; in the center the eighteenth-century church of the Immaculate Conception and the church of St. John with stone rosette.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

The village has numerous fountains: the fountain of Pescarella is known for its sweet-tasting water considered beneficial; the fountain of San Nicola was renovated in 1952.

Santuario della Madonna del Pozzo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

On the hill Serralonga, about three kilometers from the village: according to tradition in 1709 Our Lady appeared to the farmer Giuseppe Mastrostefano and caused a spring to spring. On August 15th the eighteenth-century statue is taken in procession to the village; the main feasts are August 24th and August 25th and on 26th the statue returns to the sanctuary.
