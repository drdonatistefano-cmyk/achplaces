What to see in Voghera

Città dell'Oltrepò Pavese gathered around Piazza Duomo, with the collegiate church of San Lorenzo started in 1605, the neoclassical Palazzo Gounela and the Social Theatre of 1845; not far away the Visconti Castle of 1372 and, in the former Caserma of Cavalleria, the historical Museum Giuseppe Beccari and the Museum of Natural Sciences G. Orlandi.

7 places to visit in Voghera, in the province of Pavia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo, collegiata di San Lorenzo Martire

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Begin in May 1605 on the ruins of an older church, designed by the Bolognese Antonio Maria Corbetta; the façade was completed by Carlo Maciachini in 1874-1875. It preserves an organ Serassi of 1833, the relics of the patron saint St Bovo (dead at Voghera in 986) under the high altar, the fresco of the Madonna del Soccorso (1496, attributed to Andrino di Edesia), paintings of Paolo Borroni and, in sacristy, vestments donated by Pope Pius V in 1571; the "Tesoro del Duomo" gathers gold brocades, furnishings and miniatures.

  • Where Piazza Duomo 92, Voghera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Piazza Duomo, Palazzo Gounela e Casa Nava

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

On the square, where stood the ancient praetorium, the neoclassical Palazzo Gounela (project by Giacomo Moraglia of 1829, built in 1844-1847), with pediment on four columns and the council hall with frescoed ceiling by Paolo Emilio Morgari; next to Casa Nava, rebuilt in style in 1903 by Vittorio Storchi with arched porch, and Palazzo Beltrami, restored in 1854, seat of the Salone del Millenario. The route of the center ends in via Emilia, the main shopping street.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Voghera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.voghera.pv.it · in-lombardia.it

Castello Visconteo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Massive brick construction with quadrangular plan with four corner towers, begun in 1372 by the will of Galeazzo II Visconti on a project by Ottorello de Meda and Andrea da Mutina. The north façade is very renovated, the south one is best preserved; in the square room of the east wing there are fragments of frescoes with the Muse attributed to Bramantino. It often hosts public events; regular visiting times are not indicated.

  • Where Via del Pozzo, Voghera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.voghera.pv.it · in-lombardia.it

Teatro Sociale «Valentino Garavani»

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Designed in 1836 by Giacomo Moraglia and built in 1842-1845, it was inaugurated on 18 April 1845 with I Lombardi at the first Verdi crusade; Angelo Moia painted 18 scenes, the Morgari brothers the curtain, and Enrico Cassi realized the bronze bas-relief of Verdi. Over 600 seats with 62 stages on three orders; Arturo Toscanini also performed there. Today it is named after the designer Valentino Garavani.

  • Where Centro storico, Voghera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.voghera.pv.it · in-lombardia.it

Chiesa di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Erected unfinished in 1525-1527 with the offers collected during the plague of 1524 and completed in 1578 by the Brotherhood of the SS. Name of Jesus; preserves relics of St. Rocco, the city's companion, and a painting attributed to Borroni with St. Francis of Sales and St. Joan Francesca of Chantal (second half of the eighteenth century).

  • Where Centro storico, Voghera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.voghera.pv.it · in-lombardia.it

Museo storico Giuseppe Beccari

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Dedicated to the period between the Napoleonic age and the twentieth century and wanted by the military rowing Giuseppe Beccari: relics, documents, medals and Italian and foreign uniforms, hundreds of firearms and whites, a library of military history with thousands of volumes and an archive of letters and diaries of fighters and postcards. It is located in the former Caserma di Cavalleria (1857-1864), barrier-free; times indicated by the City: Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday 15-17.30, groups by reservation.

  • Where Via Gramsci 1, Voghera
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.voghera.pv.it · cultura.gov.it · in-lombardia.it

Civico museo di scienze naturali G. Orlandi

Museums · Science and university

Born in the seventies from the collections of fossils and minerals donated by enthusiasts (Geo-Paleontological Group Vogherese), it has sections of zoology (birds including flamingos, herons and raptors), paleontology with a humerus of plesiosaur found at Zavattarello and a skull of Megaceros, mineralogy with 221 samples on display and a botanical section in preparation. It is located in the former Cavalry.

in-lombardy indicates opening only by reservation, the MiC card (2022) Mar-Sab times; date of foundation 1971 or 1974 according to the source; contact the museum (tel. 0383 336526)

  • Where Via Gramsci 1, Voghera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
