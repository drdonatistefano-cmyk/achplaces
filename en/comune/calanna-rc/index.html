What to see in Calanna

Country of the hinterland the regiment dominated by the hill of the Norman castle, garrison disputed between Swabians, Angioini and Aragonese; in the district Ronzo you can visit by reservation the protohistoric necropolis with grotticella tombs dug in limestone, discovered by chance in 1953.

Necropoli e Centro didattico-museale di Contrada Ronzo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Pre-Hellenic necropolis discovered by chance in 1953 during road work, in an artificial canal made in the shell limestone: artificial grutticella tombs, or "forno" (a furnace), largely closed by squared stone slabs, with overlapping burials and skeletons in fetal position, dated from the MiC to the XIII-VII centuries B.C. (XI-X B.C. according to the Municipality). The equipment is at the National Museum of Magna Grecia in Reggio Calabria. The area, redeveloped in 2009, has playground and picnic area and looks at Calvary on the historic center. Open Monday-Thursday 9-12 only by reservation (tel. 0965 742012).

  • Where Via Bruca (Strada comunale Ronzo), Calanna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Castello di Calanna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built in Norman age, perhaps on a Byzantine structure, on the hill overlooking the village. In 1255 it was defended on behalf of Pietro Ruffo; an angioin garrison since 1275, a document dated 1276 describes its towers, a wall of about 80 meters, three cisterns and the churches of St. Nicholas and St. Catherine. In 1313 he resisted the Aragonese attack for a long time before being punished together with the country.

  • Where Collina del castello, Calanna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: comune.calanna.rc.it · cultura.gov.it
