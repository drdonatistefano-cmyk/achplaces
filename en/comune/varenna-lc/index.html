What to see in Varenna

Village of steep streets that descend on the eastern shore of Lake Como, with the Villa Monastero, former Cistercian monastery today house museum with a botanical garden almost two kilometers long until Fiumelatte, the garden of Villa Cipressi, the church of San Giorgio consecrated in 1313 and the ornithological museum Luigi Scanagatta.

Villa Monastero, casa museo

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

Born from a Cistercian female monastery of the late 12th century, it passed to the Mornics, Carolina Maumari, Walter Kees and De Marchi, who gave it the eclectic aspect and in 1939 donated it to the State. The route of the museum house touches 14 rooms with original decorations and furnishings, including the Neapolitan Black Room, the Music Room, the Mornic Room, the Neorococo Red Room and the Fermi Room, where Enrico Fermi held his last lessons in 1954. Since 1953, the convention center hosts the summer courses of the Italian School of Physics.

Giardino botanico di Villa Monastero

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Straight strip of land along the eastern shore of the lake, about two kilometers long from Varenna to Fiumelatte, with over 900 specimens among rare species, exotic trees and thematic collections: palms, citrus fruits, English roses, rare glycines, oaks, hydrangeas, ferns. Among the ornaments the sculptural group of the Clemenza di Tito by Giovanni Battista Comolli (1830), the last unfinished work of the artist, and a large magnolia stretched over the water.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.villamonastero.eu

Museo ornitologico e di scienze naturali Luigi Scanagatta

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

It preserves a collection on the sedentary and migratory birdlife of the Larian territory, a scientific library of over 1,500 volumes, monographs and brochures of ornithology, zoology, malacology and botany, and the epistolary of scholar Luigi Scanagatta.

the MiC card (updated to 2021) indicates Mar-dom 10-18 times but the ticket office is closed every day; contact the museum (tel. 039 21927037)

Chiesa di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church with three naves consecrated in 1313, on the homonymous square; on the façade a large fresco of St. Christopher, inside the most famous work is the Deposition from the colored stone cross.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

An ancient statue of the Madonna from the Cistercian monastery is on display on the altar, which gave its name to Villa Monastero. In Varenna, Cardinal John Henry Newman, canonized in 2019, wrote the litany of our Lady, published in the book Il pilgrimo (London, 1853).

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Giardino botanico di Villa Cipressi

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Neoclassical villa (formerly Villa Isimbardi, today hotel) with terrace garden connected by a staircase and rare plants. In 1839, the musician Fanny Mendelssohn, Felix's sister, was guest; in 1838 the painter Eduard Gurk portrayed the procession of Emperor Ferdinand I who left the villa on the steamer Lario. From 1 November 2026 to 10 January 2027 the garden houses the Villa Cipressi Christmas Garden.

  • Where Via IV Novembre 22, Varenna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · villamonastero.eu · italia.it

Lungolago e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village descends with steep streets to the lake; in the center the church of San Giovanni Battista, indicated as the oldest of Varenna. Along the lake promenade, between the old town and the boat, a poem by David Maria Turoldo dedicated to Varenna; the Hotel Royal Victoria recalls the unknown stay of Queen Victoria in 1838, and Villa Mapelli was the residence of Luisa Campioni Venini, dedicated to Piccolo mondo antico di Fogazzaro. An itinerary of about 1 km on the ground floor, from Villa Monastero to the lakefront, takes about an hour.

  • Where Lungolago, Varenna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: villamonastero.eu · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Fiumelatte

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Seasonal stream that flows from a cave and flows into Lake Como, flowing only in spring; it is indicated as the shortest river in Italy. The botanical garden of Villa Monastero extends along the shore to this hamlet. In 1887 the painter Vittore Grubicy de Dragone painted "Fiumelatte" from the railway underpass.

  • Entry Free
