What to see in Attimis

Country of the eastern foothills of Friuli at 195 meters, surrounded by the ruins of the upper and lower castles and the fortresses of Partistagno, Zucco and Cucagna; in the former dairy the medieval Archaeological Museum tells the daily life in the feudal castles between the 10th and 15th centuries.

Museo archeologico medioevale

Museums · Archaeological

The first regional museum dedicated to everyday life in the feudal castles (X-XV century) of the eastern pedemontana of Friuli, is located in the former dairy and collects the finds of the nine castles along the via pedemontana, part of the feudal state of the Patriarchate of Aquileia. Ceramics, glass and metals, sections on hunting, fishing, agriculture and breeding, weapons of the 13th-XIV centuries, the "sword in the rock" and the oldest wooden Madonna of Friuli, the Majesty of Partistagno of the mid-Twentieth century; here are also conserved the more than 13,000 finds of the castle of Soffumbergo. Open on Sundays 10-13 and 14-19; guided tours for groups by reservation also during the week.

Castelli di Attimis e Partistagno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Around the village you can reach on foot the medieval ruins of the upper castle, the oldest of the two fortresses of Attimis, and the lower one, which with the castle of Zucco is the most recent of the territory; nearby there are also the fortresses of Partistagno, Zucco and Cucagna. The castle of Partistagno has seasonal openings with guided tours: check the calendar with the museum.

  • Where Attimis
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · museoattimis.it

Chiesa di San Silvestro papa a Racchiuso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Neo-Romanesque church in a slightly raised position on the road, perhaps born as a chapel in the last quarter of the thirteenth century: the fourteenth-century church remains a part, the medieval tower became bell tower presumably in 1448 and the building was rebuilt in 1900-1901 and consecrated in 1911. In the sails of the working chapel is preserved the cycle of frescoes of 1518 by Gian Paolo Thanner, with Father Eternal blessing, Madonna with Child and Saints, Doctors of the Church and Saint Virgins.

  • Where Racchiuso, Attimis
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it · turismofvg.it

Malghe di Porzûs

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

FVG tourism refers to the Porzûs mountain huts as a national monument linked to the massacre of partisans in 1945: according to the Ministry of Culture 18 men of the Osoppo formation were killed by partisans of the Garibaldi Brigades.

  • Where Porzûs, Attimis
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · cultura.gov.it
