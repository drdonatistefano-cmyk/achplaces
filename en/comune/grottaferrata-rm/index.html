What to see in Grottaferrata

Città dei Castelli Romani grew up around the Greek Abbey of San Nilo, founded in 1004 by Italian-Greek monks and still of Byzantine rite: basilica of Santa Maria with mosaics and icon of Theotokos, chapel of the Saints Founders frescoed by Domenichino, archaeological museum under the porch of Giuliano da Sangallo and a library of Greek codes.

Abbazia greca di San Nilo e basilica di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded in 1004 by St. Nile of Rossano among the ruins of a Roman villa, where a republican sepulchre closed by iron grates gave the name of "Crypta ferrata" , the basilica was consecrated on 17 December 1024 and Leo XIII gave it the title of basilica. Inside the mosaics with the Pentecost on the triumphal arch, thirteenth-century frescoes attributed to the school of Pietro Cavallini, the Byzantine icon of the Theotokos of the XII-XIII century, the bell tower of the XII-th century with five orders of triphores and the chapel of the Holy Founders, rebuilt in 1608 for Odoardo Farnese and frescoed by the Domenichino; the complex is closed by a belt of the fifteenth century.

Museo archeologico dell'Abbazia greca di San Nilo

Museums · Archaeological

In the abbey palace and under the sixteenth-century portico of Giuliano da Sangallo, it collects artifacts from the surrounding territory and the remains of the cryptoportico of the Roman villa on which the abbey stands; the most famous piece is an attic stele in pentelic marble of 430-420 BC with a young man reading a scroll. Guided tours of volunteers on Saturday at 11:00 and 4:00.

Open only on Friday and Saturday 10-17 and before and third Sunday of the month 9-13 (VisitLatium)
