What to see in Sala Consilina

Centre of the Diano Valley inhabited by the Neolithic, where the Romans founded Consilium: the remains of the Norman Castle on the hill, the National Archaeological Museum with the accessories of the necropolis and, in the historic center, Baroque chapels such as St. Joseph and the Madonna del Monte.

Museo archeologico nazionale di Sala Consilina

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Re-opened with a new layout in the historical site of Via Cappuccini, it traces through the funerary furnishings the history of the town from the IX to the V-IV century BC, when the Greek and Magnogrec figurative ceramic appears in the tombs. Among the pieces exhibited are the cinerary urn of the tomb 14 (900-850 BC), the model of the hut of the tomb 63 of Sant'Antonio (850-75 BC) and a black figured attic lekythos of the collection Sabia (550 BC). Open Mon-Sat 9-13.

  • Where Via Cappuccini, Sala Consilina
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Castello normanno e chiesa della Madonna di Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Rocca on a hill separated from the center, reachable only on foot along a path; attributed to Robert Guiscardo around the Mille, it was destroyed by order of Frederick II after the conspiracy of the Barons, rebuilt around 1250 and damaged again in 1497. Parts of the towers and walls remain; in the eighteenth century the interior was transformed into the church of the Madonna della Consolazione, called Madonna di Castello, still accessible, with an eighteenth-century canvas of the Madonna and Child. In September the feast of the Madonna di Castello.

  • Where Collina del Castello, Sala Consilina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Cappella di San Giuseppe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1735 by the Bigotti family next to its own palace, it is one of the most important examples of Baroque in the Diano Valley: exterior with Corinthian pilasters and capitals, interior decorated with stuccoes and altar with wooden inlays alternating with polychrome marbles.

the source documents only an extraordinary opening (European Heritage Day 2021); ordinary opening not indicated

Cappella della Madonna del Monte

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Late Baroque chapel founded in 1772, already home to the Confraternity of San Girolamo, marks the entrance to the historic center among the olive trees, with facade and porch overlooking the valley and churchyard with stone cross; it preserves a pipe organ of 1797, today not working.

  • Where Via Mario Pagano 2, Sala Consilina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it
