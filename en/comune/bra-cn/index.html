What to see in Bra

The capital of the Piedmontese baroque and home of Slow Food, with the Zizzola on the hill and, in Pollenzo, the farm of Carlo Alberto above the Roman city of Pollentia.

7 places to visit in Bra, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Agenzia e borgo di Pollenzo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The square, the neo-Gothic church of San Vittore, the castle, the tower and the park made by Carlo Alberto after 1832, with the Agency created to experiment with new agricultural techniques and today seat of the University of Gastronomic Sciences and the Wine Bank. UNESCO World Heritage Site. The castle is a private residence.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Pollenzo, Bra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it · residenzerealisabaude.com

Pollentia romana: anfiteatro e teatro

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

The concentric layout of the houses of Pollenzo follows the amphitheatre of ancient Pollentia; the remains of the theatre can be reached through the Albertino complex.

  • Where Pollenzo, Bra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it · residenzerealisabaude.com

Chiesa di Santa Chiara

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

At the corner of via Craveri and via Barbacana, the greatest monument of Piedmontese baroque, designed by Bernardo Antonio Vittone, with frescoes by Pietro Paolo Operti.

  • Where Via Barbacana, Bra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it · old.visitpiemonte.com

Piazza Caduti per la Libertà e Sant'Andrea

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient market square with the Town Hall of Vittone with wavy lines, the church of Sant'Andrea designed by Gian Lorenzo Bernini, Palazzo Mathis with frescoed rooms, Palazzo Valfrè and Palazzo Garrone.

  • Where Piazza Caduti per la Libertà, Bra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it · old.visitpiemonte.com

Palazzo Traversa – Museo Civico

Museums · Archaeological

Fortified residence of the fifteenth century with ogival windows and terracotta decorations, seat of the Civic Museum of Archaeology, History and Art with the finds of Pollenzo.

  • Where Via Parpera, Bra
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it

La Zizzola – Casa dei Braidesi

Museums · City history · Viewpoint

The curious octagonal building on top of Monteguglielmo, symbol of the city.

  • Where Monteguglielmo, Bra
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it · residenzerealisabaude.com
