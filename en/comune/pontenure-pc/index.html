What to see in Pontenure

Municipality of the Piacenza plain with the church of San Pietro from the medieval bell tower and the romantic park of Villa Raggio, which houses the Serra Theatre; in the territory the Castle of Paderna, visitable, and the fifteenth-century castle of Muradello.

6 places to visit in Pontenure, in the province of Piacenza (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Teatro Serra di Parco Raggio

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built between the end of the nineteenth century and the beginning of the twentieth century next to Villa Fortunata, the palace that Armando Raggio had built between 1882 and 1885. It is a hybrid between auditorium and botanical greenhouse: stage in masonry with mouthpiece that preserves traces of floral decorations, iron platea separated from the stage by a curved moulding.

  • Where Via Montale 27, Pontenure
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Ancient church linked to the tradition of pilgrims, with the central nave flanked by double side naves; the bell tower next to dates back to the Middle Ages.

Castello di Paderna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

An unknown castle, with damage and destruction documented in the 13th century; the Marazzani bought it in 1453. Today of the Petrelli family, it is an organic farm with educational farm, orchard and vegetable garden; the visit includes the square tower, the tower in the Water, the courtyards, the church of Santa Maria (cappella of the IX century), the peasant museum and the moat. In May and October it hosts the exhibition "Frutti del Castello" on ancient varieties. It is part of the Castles of the Duchy of Parma, Piacenza and Pontremoli.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Villa La Bellotta

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Neoclassical villa of the late eighteenth-early nineteenth century about 1 km from Valconasso, purchased by the Rays in 1899 and renovated in 1914-15, when it received the façade in Venetian style with graffiti decorations and stables where Edilio Raggio bred the breed of thoroughbreds "Bellotta" (Bellotta); you arrive there from a avenue of lindens. Today it is the pastoral and holiday centre of the Diocese of Piacenza-Bobbio.

  • Where Valconasso, Pontenure
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
