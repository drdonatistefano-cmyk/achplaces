What to see in Alessandria

City founded in 1168 against Barbarossa between Tanaro and Bormida, capital of the Borsalino hat and bicycle: the great Citadel Sabauda, Piazza della Libertà, Borgo Rovereto and, in Spinetta, the museum of the battle of Marengo.

16 places to visit in Alessandria, in the province of Alessandria (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cittadella di Alessandria

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortress built between 1733 and 1745 on a six-pointed star plant on the shore of Tanaro, enhanced by Napoleon; here in 1821 it waved for the first time the Tricolor. Free access, with Piazza d'Armi and a small museum of uniforms and relics of the Royal Army 1848-1946.

  • Where Cittadella, Alessandria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · old.visitpiemonte.com

Ponte Meier

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The bridge over Tanaro connecting the city to the Citadel, designed by American architect Richard Meier and opened in 2016: a single white span of over 176 meters.

  • Where Ponte Cittadella, Alessandria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Santa Maria di Castello

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Founded in 1470 and consecrated in 1545, it is the oldest place of worship in the city, built on two previous temples (VIII and X-XI century) whose remains are visible in the underground.
