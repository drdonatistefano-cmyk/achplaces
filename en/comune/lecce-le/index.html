What to see in Lecce

The capital of the Salento Baroque: the Basilica of Santa Croce, the scenic Piazza del Duomo, the Roman amphitheatre in Piazza Sant'Oronzo, the Roman theatre, Porta Napoli and the Castle of Carlo V; outside the city the archaeological park of Rudiae and the Abbey of Cerrate.

13 places to visit in Lecce, in the province of Lecce (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Basilica di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Basilicas

The first stone was laid in 1353 and the enlargement lasted almost two centuries; a very rich Baroque façade with rosette, 16 altars and a 16th century fresco of the Madonna of Constantinople; next to the former convent of the Celestines, today Prefecture.

  • Where Via Umberto I, Lecce
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Piazza del Duomo e Cattedrale

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Viewpoint

The spectacular square closed with the Cathedral, the bell tower about 70 meters high, the Bishop's Palace and the Seminary.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Lecce
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · conference.unisalento.it

Piazza Sant'Oronzo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The main square, with the column of Sant'Oronzo completed at the end of the 17th century to thank the saint of protection from the plague of 1656 and the Palazzo del Seggio (Sedile), between the Renaissance and Gothic.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Oronzo, Lecce
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · conference.unisalento.it

Anfiteatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

It occupies the south half of Piazza Sant'Oronzo: of Augustan age, excavated from 1900 by Cosimo De Giorgi for about a third, national monument since 1906; the rest is still under the square.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Oronzo, Lecce
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · museipuglia.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it

Teatro romano e Museo del teatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology

Re-emerged in 1928 during works in a private residence and excavated in 1938: an auditorium of about 75 meters for at least 6,000 spectators; the nearby museum in Via degli Ammirati tells the ancient Lupiae.

  • Where Via degli Ammirati, Lecce
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: museipuglia.cultura.gov.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it
