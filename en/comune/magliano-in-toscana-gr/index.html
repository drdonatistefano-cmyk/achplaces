What to see in Magliano in Toscana

Medieval village of the Maremma enclosed by Sienese walls among the best preserved in Tuscany, with Palazzo Checco il Bello and the church of San Giovanni Battista; outside it brings the ruins of the abbey of San Bruzio, the Olive of the Strega and the fortified villages of Montiano and Pereta, in the land of Morellino.

7 places to visit in Magliano in Toscana, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e mura senesi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval plant closed by the walls built by the Republic of Siena, one of the best preserved in Tuscany, with a renovated pedestrian path that looks at the countryside to the sea. You enter from Porta San Giovanni to the south and follow Corso Garibaldi to the Palazzo dei Priori, with the family coats of arms on the facade, and to Piazza della Libertà with the church of San Martino, cited since the 13th century; Porta San Martino, to the north, offers a wide panorama.

  • Where Corso Garibaldi, Magliano in Toscana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church in Piazza della Repubblica, with ancient origins and visible traces of the restorations of the centuries; inside frescoes of the Sienese school and a fifteenth-century baptismal font.

Rovine di San Bruzio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Remains of the monastery built by the Benedictines around the year 1000, a couple of kilometers from the village outside the walls, called "a small San Galgano" with the sky for roof. In Romanesque style with Gothic elements and original Latin cross plan, they retain the semicircular apse, the transept walls and arches that held the dome, with foliage capitals and anthropomorphic heads similar to those of San Rabano.

  • Where San Bruzio, Magliano in Toscana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry
