Castello imperiale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Impressive castle in Romanesque style, between the residences of Frederick II, on a strategic hill overlooking the Calaggio valley and the borders of Irpinia, Lucania and Daunia; in the Middle Ages it was military-administrative district and last outpost of the Longobard Duchy of Benevento, and around the Mille belonged to the Landolfo and Pandolfo princes. From the top opens the view that is worth the name of the "Loggia delle Puglie" (Puglia Loggia).

MiC card indicates the castle closed every day (tel. Common 0881 984007)

Centro storico e Parco urbano delle opere in pietra

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Born as a defensive Longobard settlement on a rocky summit, the village develops in a pyramid along the hill: the entire historic center, well preserved, is an urban park of stone houses and portals decorated with family coats of arms along the alleys, animated by artisan workshops. At Christmas the torchlight illuminates the village with Roman torches like a living crib.

  • Where Centro storico, Sant'Agata di Puglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it
