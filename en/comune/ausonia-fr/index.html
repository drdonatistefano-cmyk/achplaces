What to see in Ausonia

Country on the hills of the Aurunci Mountains, which owes its name to the ancient Pre-Roman Ausona (in the Middle Ages it was called Fratte): from the medieval castle, home of the Stone Museum, the view ranges from Montecassino to the Gulf of Gaeta; downstream the Sanctuary of Santa Maria del Piano, above the village the pine forest of Selvacava and Mount Fammera.

Museo della pietra

Museums · Themed

Anthropological demoetanological museum opened on June 2, 2004 in the restored medieval castle, in the Clock Tower: without permanent collections, it tells the cultural uses of stone from the Middle Ages to today, with monitors, panels, audio and showcases on geology from the plain to the mountain and a section on the processing; the castle itself, all in stone, is the main object exposed. MiC timetables: Monday and Sunday 10-17, Friday and Saturday closed; booking not required.

  • Where Largario San Michele (Castello medievale), Ausonia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museodellapietra.it

Sources: museodellapietra.it · cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Castello medievale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built by the princes of Capua around the year 1000, it preserves imposing remains with square towers; from the strategic position on the hill you can see the Abbey of Montecassino and the Gulf of Gaeta.

  • Where Largario San Michele, Ausonia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

On the highest point of the village, in white stone on a pre-existing temple of Hercules, is the oldest monument of Ausonia; at the end of September there is celebrated the feast of the patron.

  • Where Largario San Michele, Ausonia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it

Santuario di Santa Maria del Piano

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In the valley below the village, is for VisitLatium the most precious jewel of Ausonia: upper church of the fifteenth century and crypt with a cycle of frescoes dedicated to Saint Remicarda. Seriously damaged in World War II, it has been restored.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Pineta di Selvacava

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Pineta in the Aurunci Mountains Natural Park with picnic area and life path; at Passo Bastia, with tables and benches, starts the path to Mount Fammera.

  • Where Passo Bastia, Selvacava, Ausonia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sentiero CAI 914 Selvacava-Monte Fammera

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Path for experienced hikers (EE) of 4.7 km, about 2 hours and 50 minutes, 725 m of difference in altitude uphill: crosses the forest at the edge of a gravel, goes back to hairpin bends an ancient mule track on the east wall of the Fammera alternating rocks and stones, touches the Sella di Fammera di Spigno and with one last steep stretch reaches the summit. Views on the walls, on the eastern Aurunci and on the Ausente Valley to the sea; no water points, recommended season March-October.

  • Where Passo Bastia, Selvacava, Ausonia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com

Marzolina e laine e ciciri

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Marzolina is a soft and spicy goat cheese with an intense taste, which takes its name from March, when it was produced, celebrated at the end of June; the laine and ciciri are fresh pasta with chickpea sauce. Ausonia is also an important marble machining centre, with the Pearl royal.

  • Where Ausonia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
