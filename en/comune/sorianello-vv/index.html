What to see in Sorianello

Hill country on the western side of the Serre, in the high valley of the Messima, perched on a rock spike and for a long time farmhouse of Soriano with the name of ...Soriano Superiore, three churches with works of art, the Museum of peasant civilization and the Valley of the Mills.

6 places to visit in Sorianello, in the province of Vibo Valentia (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the upper part of the village, recently restored, with the ochre color shaded of white and grey that makes it visible also by Soriano Calabro. The portico with ionic columns and arches leads to a back courtyard with a view of the Upper Valley of the Messa and the Convent of San Domenico di Soriano. Inside a wooden crucifix with a full figure of the end of the sixteenth century, attributed to Müller, active also to the Certosa di Serra San Bruno.

  • Where Parte alta del paese, Sorianello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sorianello.vv.it

Sources: comune.sorianello.vv.it · fondoambiente.it

Chiesa di San Nicola e museo parrocchiale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It is the oldest church in Sorianello and houses a small museum run by the parish of Santa Maria e San Nicola, with oil paintings of the Neapolitan school of the eighteenth century and other works of the three churches of the village. The patron saint of St. Nicholas is celebrated on 6 December.

  • Where Sorianello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sorianello.vv.it

Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Established in 1995 in the Palazzo Imeneo-Florenzano, it describes the peasant civilization of the village; the path continues outdoors in the Valley of the Mills, which deepens its anthropological aspects.

  • Where Palazzo Imeneo-Florenzano, Sorianello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sorianello.vv.it

Centro storico di Sorianello

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village rises on a rocky spike; the old town, dense with nearby houses, narrow alleys and underpasses, is tightened around the stronghold, once protected by thick walls.
