What to see in Garlate

Country on the right bank of the lake of the same name formed by Adda south of Lecco, linked to the Manzoni landscape of "Adio agli monti"; to see the Civic Museum of Silk Abegg, in the nineteenth-century spinning mill on the shore of the lake, and the cycle path that makes the tour of the lakes of Garlate and Olginate.

Civico Museo della Seta Abegg

Museums · Themed

It is located in the mill built in 1841 on the shore of the lake, bought in 1887 by the Abegg of Zurich; the museum was born in 1953 at the will of the family, presented as the first museum of industrial archaeology in Italy, and in 1976 was donated to the municipality. It exhibits basins for the artisan and industrial trattura, tools for the breeding of the baco and for the twisting and a large spinning machine in the round of the first half of the nineteenth century, with working machinery; a section is dedicated to the future of silk in biomedical and cosmetic. Open Saturday 14.30-17.30 and Sunday 9.30-12.30 and 14.30-17.30.

Lago di Garlate e ciclopedonale dei laghi

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lake to the south of the Lario wet by Lecco, Vercurago, Pescate, Garlate and Olginate, background of the pass "Adio ai monti" of the Promisi Sposi; in the reeds live anatids and folklore and are observed herons ashes and larger whites, garzettes, cormorants, martin fisherman, flares and swans royals. The cycle track of about 18 km, easy and accessible even with strollers, follows the entire perimeter and Garlate runs along the bank before flanking the Via Statale.

  • Where Lungolago, Garlate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.parcoaddanord.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it
