What to see in Montella

Centro dei Monti Picentini, the land of IGP chestnut, where the Calore river is born: the complex of San Francesco a Folloni, a national monument, the Sanctuary of Santissimo Salvatore at 954 meters, the Castle of Monte, the waterfalls of Calore and the plateau of Verteglia.

Complesso conventuale di San Francesco a Folloni

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

National monument, born in the 13th century according to the legend from the passage of St. Francis directed to the sanctuary of St. Michael on the Gargano; of the sixteenth century monastery remains the cloister with the cistern, while the current structure, with new cloister and new church, is of the mid eighteenth century. The single nave church, with chapels, transept and stucco, has the Chapel of the Crucified with the relic of the Sacco of St. Francis and in sacristy the cenotaph that Margherita Orsini erected for her husband Diego I Cavaniglia, who died in 1481 after the battle of Otranto. Abandoned with suppressions, it was restored in 1933 with the return of the friars.

Museo di San Francesco a Folloni

Museums · Old masters

Opened on September 18, 1981 around the ancient refectory, it preserves works recovered after the earthquake of 1980: a silver cross of Aragonese age, Neapolitan cups, the fifteenth-century vestments of Diego I Cavaniglia (farsetto and giornea), a St. Francis in ecstasy attributed to the workshop of Solimena and a lunette with Annunciation attributed to Francesco da Tolentino. The remaining works will have to be re-arranged in a new venue.

Santuario del Santissimo Salvatore

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

On Mount Sovero, at 954 meters, it dominates the valley; a chapel is attested already in the sixteenth century, while the current church was built from 1779 to 1783 after the pilgrimage that, according to tradition, ended the great drought of 1779. Among the customs of pilgrims, drink from the well of miracles and ring the big bell; the feast is celebrated from 1780 on the day of the Most Holy Trinity.

Cascate del Calore

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

The Calore rises from Mount Accellica and forms several waterfalls: the most famous is the Lavandaia Waterfall, at the foot of the Sanctuary of the Santissimo Salvatore, under a bridge of the 1st century BC and with the ruins of a mill active from the 15th century to the fifties; the Maronnella Waterfall, hidden in the green and dear to the fishermen of trout; the Fascio Waterfall, towards Acerno, an artificial basin of fascist epoch over eight meters high, with a bridge without parapets.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry
