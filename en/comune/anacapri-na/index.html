What to see in Anacapri

The highest municipality on the island of Capri: the Blue Grotto, the ancient water lily of an imperial villa, the remains of Villa Damecuta, Villa San Michele by Axel Munthe and the church of San Michele with the majolica floor.

6 places to visit in Anacapri, in the province of Naples (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Anacapri in the guide with map and filters

Grotta Azzurra

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Ancient water lilies of the imperial villa of Gradola, which placed statues of Tritoni and Poseidon there; destination of travelers and artists since 1826, can be reached only by boat and is not visitable by the rough sea.

Access only by boat, suspended with rough sea

  • Where Anacapri
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page museicapri.cultura.gov.it

Sources: museicapri.cultura.gov.it · files.caprionline.it

Villa Damecuta

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Remains of an elegant imperial residence on the north-western promontory, with a panoramic ambulacrum overlooking the sea, a loggia and a small late medieval watchtower.

  • Where Anacapri
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page museicapri.cultura.gov.it

Sources: museicapri.cultura.gov.it · files.caprionline.it

Villa San Michele

Museums · House museums · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built by the Swedish doctor Axel Munthe, it preserves the richest collection of ancient finds and works of art on the island and is the only nineteenth-century residence of Capri as it was.

  • Where Via Capodimonte 34, Anacapri
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page museicapri.cultura.gov.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: files.caprionline.it · museicapri.cultura.gov.it

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

Baroque church between the late seventeenth and early eighteenth centuries designed by Domenico Antonio Vaccaro, with the majolica floor by Leonardo Chianese designed by Francesco Solimena depicting the expulsion from Paradise.

  • Where Anacapri
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page museicapri.cultura.gov.it

Sources: italia.it · museicapri.cultura.gov.it

Casa Rossa

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Building that exhibits ancient finds of Capri and the Mediterranean; in its walls are walled fragments of marble coming from the villa of Gradola.

  • Where Anacapri
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page museicapri.cultura.gov.it

Sources: museicapri.cultura.gov.it

Scala Fenicia

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Scale dug into the rock from Marina Grande to Anacapri, about 200 meters of difference in altitude, only connection between Capri and Anacapri until 1874; restored in 1998.

  • Where Anacapri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page museicapri.cultura.gov.it

Sources: files.caprionline.it · museicapri.cultura.gov.it
