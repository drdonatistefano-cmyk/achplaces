Bosco della Panfilia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

The wreck of the ancient humid forest of plain, oasis of protection of the fauna, also known for the truffle; the entrance is on the banks of the Napoleonic Cable.

  • Where Circa 1,5 km da Sant'Agostino, Terre del Reno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
