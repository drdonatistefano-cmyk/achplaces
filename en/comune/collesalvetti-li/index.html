What to see in Collesalvetti

Municipality of the Livorno hills crossed in ancient by the Via Aemilia Scauri, then Medici villa and center of the Grand Duke's Farm; to see the Villa Medicea, the Pinacoteca Carlo Servolini, the Roman Mansio of Torretta Vecchia, the Aquedotto Leopoldino da Colognole and, in nature, the Park of the Livornese Mountains and the Oasis of the Countess.

8 places to visit in Collesalvetti, in the province of Livorno (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pinacoteca Comunale Carlo Servolini

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

Established in 2007, he exhibits the protagonists of Livorno painting between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, such as Renato Natali, Guglielmo Micheli, Giovanni Lomi and Giovanni Marchi, alongside more famous authors such as Renato Guttuso, Domenico Purificato, Remo Gordigiani and Anacleto Margotti; he periodically hosts temporary exhibitions. Admission without reservation, open Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 15:30-18:30.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Garibaldi 77 (Villa Carmignani), Collesalvetti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.collesalvetti.li.it · visittuscany.com

Area archeologica della Mansio romana

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Remains of a building from the end of the 1st century AD, modified until the 4th century, on the ancient Via Aemilia Scauri: probably a large public spa at a resting place not far from Pisa. Discovered in the 1990s and almost reduced to the foundations by the debauchery, it retains part of the heating system and mosaic floors. Visits in periodic openings or by telephone appointment.

  • Where Via Torretta Vecchia, Collesalvetti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.collesalvetti.li.it · visittuscany.com

Villa Medicea di Collesalvetti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Probably built at the beginning of the 15th century, it was bought by Lorenzo and Giuliano de' Medici in 1476; it was a hunting residence and center of the Grand Duke's Farm, which counted over twenty farms.

  • Where Collesalvetti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Acquedotto Leopoldino (di Colognole)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Made by the Grand Dukes of Tuscany to bring water to Livorno, which used it until 1912: it starts from Colognole and runs for about 18 km between the countryside and the woods, with arches also very high, tunnels and tunnels in Parrana, towards Nugola, up to the Cisternino di Pian di Rota and the Cisternone di Livorno. Today it is a route for hiking, cycling or horseback riding to the sea.

  • Where Colognole, Collesalvetti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo a Colognole

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the village where the Leopoldino Aqueduct begins, it preserves perhaps the oldest positive octave organ in Tuscany, at the end of the sixteenth century, with the original decorations.

  • Where Colognole, Collesalvetti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Parco dei Monti Livornesi

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Protected area of over 1,000 hectares among Mediterranean maquis, brooms, oaks and cork, inhabited by wild boars, foxes and weavers; there passes the historical path of the Leopold Aqueduct.

  • Where Colline livornesi, Collesalvetti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Oasi della Contessa

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Regional nature reserve, last coastal marsh of the area and stop for many migratory birds such as herons and marine acorns; it is equipped for birdwatching, with guided tours and workshops for children.

  • Where Collesalvetti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Nugola e i vigneti della Costa Etrusca

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

Borgo that appears in 1503 in the Carta della Toscana Marittima di Leonardo da Vinci, among the hilly vineyards of the Strada del Vino della Costa Etrusca (Sangiovese and Trebbiano, with Syrah, Merlot and Vermentino); in September it hosts "September of...wine" with tastings in the cellars.

  • Where Nugola, Collesalvetti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
