What to see in Castel San Vincenzo

Town of the high Volturno Valley overlooking the lake of the same name and the Mainarde, born from the union in 1928 of the villages of Castellone and San Vincenzo. The Benedictine abbey of San Vincenzo al Volturno rises on its territory, with the archaeological area and the Epifanio Crypt.

Abbazia di San Vincenzo al Volturno e Cripta di Epifanio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Benedictine Abbey founded in 703 by three Beneventan princes in a rustic villa abandoned at the springs of the Volturno; supported by Charlemagne, it came to drive a network of more than 130 monasteries. The excavations have returned an archaeological park of 11,000 m2 with evidences of the monastic life and the Crypt of Epifanius, rich in frescoes, discovered by a farmer while working the camp; today the monastic house was reopened by a community of nuns from the United States.

Lago di Castel San Vincenzo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lake turquoise at the foot of the village, in the valley from which the Volturno is born, with the chain of Mainarde in the background; offers numerous activities for visitors and on its banks there is a restaurant of Molise specialties.

Centro storico, chiese e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village was founded between the 10th and 11th centuries, when the abbey reorganized the territory founding the villages of Castellone, then the capital of the Upper Volturno Valley, and San Vincenzo. The main churches are Santo Stefano and San Martino Vescovo; of interest are also the Town Hall, the former Conti-Regina Palace and the Canone Palace.
