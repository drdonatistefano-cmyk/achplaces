What to see in Cittiglio

Borgo at the outlet of Valcuvia, about 4 km from Lake Maggiore, the birthplace of the champion Alfredo Binda, to which a museum is dedicated; from the village start the paths towards the three waterfalls of the stream San Giulio and the ascent to the Passo del Cuvignone passing through the mountain village of Vararo.

Museo Alfredo Binda

Museums · Themed

A large hall dedicated to Alfredo Binda, winner of three World Championships and five Italian Tours: jerseys and Italian champions, saddles, shoes, goggles and above all the two bikes of the World Championships of 1930 and 1932. The collection is completed by the phone that Binda played in the band of Cittiglio, editions of the Gazzetta dello Sport, diplomas, photographs and materials on his career as technical commissioner of the National.

In Lombardy it signals the museum as temporarily closed, check with the Municipality (0332 601434)

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Cascate di Cittiglio

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Three waterfalls formed by the stream San Giulio in a wooded environment, between 324 and 474 meters above sea level, where the watercourse goes down to curves with sudden jumps before the village. Only the first is reachable, with a path that starts from the ancient part of the village following the signs from Via Pianella and that requires a lot of attention.

Varese Tourism indicates the route currently not open to the public

  • Where Via Pianella, Cittiglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: vareseturismo.it · in-lombardia.it

Vararo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The mountain village of Cittiglio is about 750 meters away from the plains by a valley, with wide views of Lake Maggiore and Lake Varese; in the valley of Vararo (757 m) passes the road of Cuvignon. From here you walk towards the Pizzoni di Laveno and at sunset in the meadows it is easy to spot wild animals.

  • Where Vararo, Cittiglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · in-lombardia.it

Salita al Passo del Cuvignone

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The cycling climb starts from Cittiglio and in 9.26 km, with 793 m of difference in altitude and average slope of 8.56%, leads to Pozzit, the Valley Mill (574 m) and the Vararo basin up to the pass (1,036 m), between the Nude Mount and the Laveno Pizzoni, with views of the Alps and Lake Maggiore. Alfredo Binda finished the preparation there since 1920 and Ivan Basso used it as a gym; on the last ramps are written by the fans of the Giro d'Italia.

last 3 km on narrow road with bottom in bad condition; at the top no parking spaces nor refreshment points

  • Where Partenza dal centro di Cittiglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
