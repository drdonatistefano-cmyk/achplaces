What to see in Isili

Country of Sarcidano on a plateau at over 500 meters, for a long time the main Sardinian center of copper processing, told with the weaving in the Maratè museum; on the edge of the town the nuraghe Is Paras, with one of the highest tholos of Nuragic Sardinia, and not far from the lake of San Sebastiano.

Nuraghe Is Paras

History and architecture · Archaeology

On a limestone hill at the northern edge of the village, it is built in blocks of marl limestone that give it the characteristic white color. Born as a single tower nuraghe in the Middle Bronze (XV-XIV century B.C.), it was expanded in several phases until it became a trilobed fortress with a wall to protect a village of huts (XII-XI century B.C.); there are traces of Roman and Byzantine use. The inner chamber of the central tower, covered with tholos with 37 rows, is almost 12 meters high and is described as the highest in Nuragic Sardinia; you can visit the lower floor. The name comes from the fathers Scolopi ("the friars"), owners of the land since the seventeenth century.

Maratè - Museo per l'arte del rame e del tessuto

Museums · Themed

The only copper museum in Sardinia, in the former convent of the Scolopi fathers: the copper section reconstructs the workshop of the Aramai with the original tools and the cart of the street vendor, and shows objects for the house, the fields and the wedding set worked with a cantilever and hammer. The textile section has an ethnographic part on Sardinian weavers and an artistic one with about thirty tapestries in copper, gold, silver, linen and raffia; sounds and voices accompany the visit, including the secret jargon of the ora, s'arromaniscus. Open from Tuesday to Sunday 10-13 and 16-18, closed on Monday.

Lago di San Sebastiano e chiesa sull'isolotto

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lake-park created by the dam of is Barrocus on the river Mannu; on an islet stands the church of San Sebastiano, of the sixteenth century, which existed before the lake and can be reached only by swimming or boat, once a place of marriages of wealthy families and no longer used for worship since the nineteenth century. The cliffs, about 23 million years old, have more than 300 climbing routes equipped; you practice trekking, canoeing, sport fishing and mountain biking, and along the lake passes the Green Train. In the surrounding area also two arches of a bridge of the II-III century AD.

Chiesa di San Giuseppe Calasanzio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the center of the village, built between 1661 and 1737 by the Scolopi fathers; the source indicates it as a sanctuary dedicated to Saint Efisio, celebrated at the end of August.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The traditional houses have large courtyards and arched portals; the town, provincial capital for a short period in the nineteenth century, preserves the artisan traditions of copper and weaving. In June the feast of St. John the Baptist brings traditional costumes to parade from all over the island.

  • Entry Free
