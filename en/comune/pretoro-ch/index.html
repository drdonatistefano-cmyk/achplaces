What to see in Pretoro

Village at 608 meters on the north side of Majella, with houses dug in the rock and superimposed, developed around the seventeenth century after the destruction of the castle; to see the churches of Sant'Andrea and San Nicola, the hermitage of the Madonna della Mazza, the rock mills of the Foro Valley and the fauna of the Apennine wolf.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The houses, excavated in the rock and superimposed, seem from a distance an extension of the ridge of Majella; Roman origins ('Castrum Praetorium') and medieval plant, with the village grown around 1600 after the destruction of the castle. View of the Foro Valley, the Teat Hills and the Highlands of the Maielletta; the local craftsmen work wood for wool castings.

  • Where Pretoro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcomajella.it

Chiesa di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the highest part of the village, from the 15th to the 16th century, rebuilt in neoclassical style with baroque elements; two naves with the floor following the shape of the rock.

  • Where Parte alta del borgo, Pretoro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Nicola (San Domenico)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It preserves a polychrome earthenware Pietà from the seventeenth century and a wooden portal from 1630; on the side towards the valley two monofores. The festivals of "Lu Lope" (the wolf) and "serpares" are celebrated; the San Domenico Centre tells wolves, snakes and local history.

  • Where Pretoro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Eremo della Madonna della Mazza

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

A single nave church upstream of the village, with portal that refers to the 13th-XIV century; the first certain news is of 1324-1325, when they collected the tenth Cistercian monks from France. The fifteenth-century image of the Madonna enthroned with the Child holds the scepter ('mazza') that gives the name; procession back on the first Sunday of July.

  • Where A monte del paese, Pretoro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · parcomajella.it · italia.it

Area Faunistica del Lupo appenninico

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Area of the Majella National Park activated in August 2003 to make known the wolf, at 750 meters: it hosts a couple of wolves and can accommodate injured or problematic specimens not liberable. The best hours to watch them are the winter afternoon and the summer evening; the guided tour starts from the Environmental Education Center • The Big Beech (via Fontepalombo 36).

  • Where Località Calvario (strada per Passo Lanciano), Pretoro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcomajella.it · italia.it

Valle del Foro e mulini rupestri

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Along the Foro river, between flora and fauna, you can reach the cliffs and the remains of ancient water mills, where the millers worked with ancient methods until a few decades ago.

  • Where Valle del Foro, Pretoro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · parcomajella.it · italia.it

Grotta dell'Eremita

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

At about 8 km from the village and 900 meters above sea level, cited in a document dated 1581 as "Ecclesiam Sancti Angeli" (Ecclesiam Sancti Angeli), a place of worship of St. Michael linked to a nearby spring and to the stillicide in the cave; the excavations returned flints, ceramics of the Bronze Age and traces of hermits and shepherds.

  • Where A circa 8 km da Pretoro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
