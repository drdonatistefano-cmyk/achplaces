What to see in Canosa Sannita

A hilly village in the province of Chieti a few kilometres from the Adriatic, surrounded by vineyards, with the castle mentioned already in the 13th century; in the 16th century Palazzo Martucci is home to the Museum of War for Peace, while the Wine Paths cross vineyards, olive groves and woods.

Museo della guerra per la pace

Museums · Themed

Opened in 2001 in Palazzo Martucci, it was born from the rich material of the long military tradition of the Martucci family: in 16 rooms it traces the main Italian wars, from the wars of Independence to the Second World War. The collection of Italian uniforms stands out, which follows the evolution of the uniforms from the Risorgimento to the grey-green and the battle dress of the army of the South Kingdom.

MiC card (updated 2020) marks the closed museum every day of the week; check the opening with the museum contacts

Palazzo Martucci

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Overlooking the viewpoint of the old town, it was founded in the 16th century as a defensive structure and in the 18th century it was transformed into a fortified palace; it preserves ribbed cruising vaults and wolf windows. It houses the Museum of War for Peace.

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Sentieri del vino

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails

Itineraries marked from ancient paths recovered, to be done on foot, by bike or horseback, among vineyards, olive groves and woods, with stops equipped at ancient estates; along the way you will encounter rural testimonies such as the Casino of Moggio, the Torre di Castelluccio and the Mill De Sanctis. The territory produces red, white and rosé wines, in particular from Montepulciano and Trebbiano d'Abruzzo grapes.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: abruzzoturismo.it

Tratturo Magno

Nature and landscape · Landscapes

Ancient transhumance road, 244 km long, between L'Aquila and Foggia, the longest of the five Regi Tratturi: there passed the flocks that from the mountains of Gran Sasso, Sirente and Maiella descended to the pastures of the Tavoliere delle Puglie. Abruzzo Turismo indicates it among the testimonies of the territory of Canosa Sannita.

  • Where Territorio di Canosa Sannita
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: abruzzoturismo.it
