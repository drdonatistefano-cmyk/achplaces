What to see in Villafranca di Verona

Villafranca di Verona grew up around the Scaliger Castle, started in 1201 and restored in 2023 with insides and walkways to visit; at Palazzo Bottagisio, where in 1859 the two emperors of the Armistice stayed, the Museum of the Risorgimento is located, and along the course there are the Duomo and the Baroque church of the Discipline.

Castello Scaligero

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built between 1201 and 1202 after Verona had made the village a Free Village (1185), it was set on fire by the army of Ezzelino da Romano in 1232 and rebuilt in 1243, when the Podestà Enrico III da Egna built the main tower; it was part of Serraglio Scaligero along the Tione. With Venice (1405) it lost importance and was used as garrison and customs; the City bought mast and prisons in 1882 and in 1890 the tower was restored and equipped with clock. After the collapse of a corner tower the restorations of 1926-1935 created the public gardens; the great restoration of 2023 opened the interior halls and a new walkway with installations on medieval civil and military life. Opening hours April-October: Tuesday-Friday 10-13 and 14.30-17.30, Saturday 10-13 and 16-19, Sunday 10-12 and 15-19 (in August only Friday-Sunday); November-March: Thursday 10-13, Friday-Sunday 10-13 and 14.30-17.30.

Museo del Risorgimento, Palazzo Bottagisio

Museums · City history

The 18th-century manor villa of the Gandini Morelli Bugna Bottagisio, which in 1859 hosted Francesco Giuseppe and Napoleon III in the days of the Armistice of Villafranca; the Municipality bought it in 1997. The museum, born in 1959 from the exhibition for the centenary of the meeting, is on the first floor noble since 2014 and has been reordered chronologically in 2022/2023: weapons, prints and relics of the armies of the wars of independence, documents and objects of the villafranchesi who fought between 1848 and 1866 and volunteers Garibaldi; on the ground floor three rooms with paintings of the legacy Martinelli, including works from Verona of the early twentieth century. Open Saturday 15.30-18.30 and Sunday 10-12 and 15.30-18.30, closed in August.

Chiesa della Disciplina (San Martino)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the fifteenth century by Count Giorgio Maffei next to the family palace and donated in 1499 to the Confraternita dei Disciplinati, it was enlarged and renewed in Baroque forms from the second half of the seventeenth century to 1779. It has a hut façade, a large hall with wooden trusses and, beyond the presbytery, the fifteenth-century vaulted building used as a meeting room, with frescoes restored in 2008. The marble main altar houses the altarpiece of Orazio Farinati with the Visitation of Our Lady to Saint Elizabeth and Saints Joseph and Zechariah.

Mercato dell'antiquariato

Local life and traditions · Historic markets · Free entry

Every second Sunday of the month the antique stalls occupy the main street, from the church of San Rocco to the castle, next to the walls, passing in front of the Duomo and the church of the Discipline.
