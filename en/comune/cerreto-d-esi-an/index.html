What to see in Cerreto d'Esi

Country of the valley of the Esino, stage of the Cappuccini Way, with the civic tower among the most pendant in Italy and, in the woods of the valley of the Acquarella, the hermitage where in 1529 the first general chapter of the Capuchins was held.

Eremo di Santa Maria dell'Acquarella

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Hermitage in the valley of Acquarella, above Albacina, among the woods of Mount San Vicino: inhabited by hermits from the thirteenth century, it has a church of 1441 wanted by the hermit Frandeno, leaning on a tower that controlled the valley of the Esino. In April 1529 it was held the first chapter of the Capuchins, which redacted the constitutions of Albacina, abandoned in 1585, was restored in 1845. You can reach it with a suggestive path in the woods; the festival is May 3.

Torre civica

History and architecture · Towers

Tower of the Town Hall 27,85 meters high, with an evident out of lead that makes it one of the most hanging towers in Italy; the origins would go back to the 8th century and around the Mille was consolidated outside, taking the current cylindrical shape.

Damaged with the City Hall by the 2016 earthquake; contribution for the reconstruction work re-established in January 2023
