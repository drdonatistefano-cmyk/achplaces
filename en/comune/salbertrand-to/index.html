What to see in Salbertrand

Borgo dell'alta Val di Susa was born in the 11th century along the Strada di Francia: the parish church of St. John the Baptist and the Natural Park of the Gran Bosco with the ecomuseum Colombano Romean.

5 places to visit in Salbertrand, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giovanni Battista e Museo dei Tesori

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

Cited in 1057 and almost entirely rebuilt in the sixteenth century, example of a late-Gothic alpine church: monumental protire of 1536 by Matteo Rode of Melezet, façade with the procession of Vizi and Virtues, frescoes from the fourteenth to the sixteenth century, the ancon of the main altar of Jean Faure of Thures (1677) and, in the sacristy, the museum of vestments.

  • Where Piazza Martiri della Libertà, Salbertrand
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · touringclub.it · italia.it

Parco naturale del Gran Bosco di Salbertrand

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 3,800 hectares on the north side of the high Val di Susa, established by the Piedmont Region in 1980: woods of spruce and red, silvestre pine, cembrus and larch, with marmots, chamois, deer and wolves.

  • Where Salbertrand
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Ecomuseo Colombano Romean

Museums · Themed

Ring path in the park on the life of the community: hydraulic mill, former school, municipal oven of the village Oulme, charcoal, lime furnace, nineteenth century ice-cakehouse and smoked sauna.

  • Where Salbertrand
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · italia.it

Cappella dell'Annunciazione dell'Oulme

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
