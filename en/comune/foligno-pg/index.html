What to see in Foligno

The city of Quintana in the Umbra Valley, in plain and straight streets: Palazzo Trinci with the frescoes of Gentile da Fabriano, the Cathedral of San Feliciano and, in the mountains, the plateau and the marsh of Colfiorito and the abbey of Sassovivo.

10 places to visit in Foligno, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo Trinci – Museo della Città

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Residenza dei Trinci, lords of Foligno from 1305 to 1439, then of the papal governors: the loggia and rooms of the liberal arts and planets frescoed by Gentile da Fabriano in 1411, the chapel painted by Ottaviano Nelli in 1424 and the Sala dei Giganti; it houses the art gallery, the archaeological museum and the multimedia museum of the rides with the original wooden statue of Quintana.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica 25, Foligno
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Duomo di San Feliciano

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

It rises on a sacred building of the IX-X centuries, with the portal of the masters Rodolfo and Binello of 1201 richly carved; the interior was rebuilt in neoclassical style between 1772 and 1819 by Giuseppe Piermarini. On the square, the ancient Roman forum.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica, Foligno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Museums · Old masters

Abbazia di Santa Croce di Sassovivo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the slopes of Monte Aguzzo among the oak woods, initiated by the hermit Mainardo in 1082; the cloister, built between 1229 and 1232 and attributed to the Roman master Pietro de Maria, is a rare example of Romanesque architecture and sculpture.

  • Where Sassovivo, Foligno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Parco e Palude di Colfiorito

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the Karst plateaus between Umbria and Marche, the marsh of about 100 hectares with dense aquatic vegetation is recognized of international interest by the Ramsar Convention for peat bog, flora and birds.

  • Where Colfiorito, Foligno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Museo Archeologico di Colfiorito (MAC)

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In Colfiorito, along an ancient junction between Umbria, Sabina and the Adriatic coast, exhibits the excavations carried out on the plateau since the 1960s.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
