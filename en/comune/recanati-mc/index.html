What to see in Recanati

The city of Giacomo Leopardi: the birthplace with the library, the garden of the Infinite reopened by the FAI, the places of poetry and Villa Colloredo Mels with the paintings of Lorenzo Lotto; also the city of tenor Benjamin Gigli.

9 places to visit in Recanati, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Recanati in the guide with map and filters

Casa Leopardi

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The palace where Giacomo Leopardi was born (1798-1837), still inhabited by the descendants: the rooms where he grew, the library of rare volumes and the room overlooking the house of Silvia.

  • Where Recanati
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo civico Villa Colloredo Mels

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Villa purchased by the Municipality in 1998: sections from archaeology (from the sixth millennium BC) to the Middle Ages, with the bull of Frederick II with the gold seal and sandals of St. Francis, the Renaissance section with the works of Lorenzo Lotto, the nineteenth century Leopardian and modern art.

  • Where Via Gregorio XII, Recanati
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets

Sources: italia.it
