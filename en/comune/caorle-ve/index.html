What to see in Caorle

Seaside town with its pedestrian historical centre of calli and Venetian foothills, with pastel-coloured houses and fishing port: the Romanesque Cathedral of 1038 with the 11th century cylindrical bell tower, the Sanctuary of the Madonna dell'Angelo on the cliff, the National Museum of Archaeology of the Sea, the lagoon of the casini and about eighteen kilometers of beach.

Duomo di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Overlooking Piazza Vescovado, it is Caorle's oldest and most important building: it dates back to 1038, it is dedicated to Santo Stefano protomartire and stands on the remains of a early Christian basilica, in Romanesque style with Byzantine elements. There is a Roman funeral altar from the first century A.D., a 13th-14th century gold palace behind the main altar, a 15th-century fresco of St. Christopher, a baptismal font from 1587 and paintings from the Venetian school; Roman artifacts are exhibited at the apse.

  • Where Piazza Vescovado 6, Caorle
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.caorle.ve.it

Campanile cilindrico del Duomo

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

One of the few original cylindrical bell towers left, of the 11th century, 48 meters high and slightly inclined to the east; it recalls Ravenna models of Byzantine tradition with Romanesque elements and for centuries has been a watchtower over the sea. Recently restored, you can visit by reservation; from the top you can see the sea and the lagoon and in summer you can also climb in the evening, at sunset.

  • Where Piazza Vescovado, Caorle
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.caorle.ve.it

Santuario della Madonna dell'Angelo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

On the promontory at the bottom of the cliff, at the beginning of the beach of Levante, it is probably the oldest religious building in Caorle, built on remains of the 6th - 7th century where there was a wooden church with three naves dedicated to St. Michael the Archangel. The current building, with a single nave with a palladian pronao style, is from 1751, wanted by Bishop Francesco Trevisan Suarez; the legend tells of fishermen who found a wooden statue of the Madonna and Child in the sea on a marble pedestal.

  • Where Viale Madonna dell'Angelo, Caorle
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo nazionale di archeologia del mare

Museums · Archaeological

Managed by the Regional Direction National Museums Veneto, it was born from the exhibition Terredacque set up in 2014 by the Superintendence and tells the territory from the protohistoric village of San Gaetano to the Portus Reatinum of Plinio up to the modern Caorle. On the first floor the most significant finds of Caorle and surroundings, from the late Bronze Age (XIII-early XII century BC) to the modern age; on the ground floor the history of the brig Mercury, a warship with two trees of Napoleonic age passed from the French fleet to the Italian one. Number reservations +39 0421 83149 and +39 379 1805649.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museiveneto.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Centro storico, Rio Terrà e porto peschereccio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center, today pedestrian, has calli and campielli reminiscent of Venice and colorful houses around the fishing port; the Rio Terrà is one of the most frequented outdoor living rooms of Veneto, even out of season. In Calle Lunga the Palazzòn has an original fifteenth-century body and owes its name to the many families living there; in Rio Terrà Romiati a facade brings a frescoed sundial of marine inspiration. The centre of the city is Piazza Vescovado, recently renovated, with the Duomo and next to the palace.

  • Where Rio Terrà, Caorle
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.caorle.ve.it · veneto.eu · italia.it

Chiostro e percorso archeologico

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the area of the former barracks of the Carabinieri, near Piazza Vescovado, a cloister with an archaeological path around a real Roman well in the center of the garden and the recently recovered wall frescoes of the ancient oratory of San Rocco, protector from the plague.

  • Where Piazza Vescovado, Caorle
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.caorle.ve.it · italia.it

Laguna di Caorle e i casoni

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

A naturalistic area of great beauty in the North Lagoon, known for its casoni, ancient fishermen's buildings used as a home during fishing periods; you can visit with boat excursions.

  • Where Laguna Nord, Caorle
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · veneto.eu

Spiagge di Caorle

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

About 18 kilometers of sandy coast with equipped bathrooms, health trails and playgrounds; the beach of Levante starts from the cliff of the Madonna dell'Angelo.
