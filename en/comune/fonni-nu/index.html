What to see in Fonni

The highest village in Sardinia, about 1000 meters on the Gennargentu, with the ski resorts of Monte Spada and Bruncu Spina: the Baroque basilica of the Virgin of the Martyrs with convent and oratory of San Michele, the districts with murals and roofs in scandulas, the Museum of Pastoral Culture and, towards Pratobello, the nuragic sanctuary of Gremanu and the tombs of Giganti di Madau.

Basilica della Vergine dei Martiri e convento francescano

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry

The Franciscan monastery was founded in 1610 and completed with the church of the Holy Trinity around 1632-33; between 1702 and 1706 the Milanese Giuseppe Quallio, with Corbellini, Mutoni and Reti, built the basilica of the Virgin of the Martyrs with octagonal dome, painted by Pietro Antonio and Gregorio Are. Below is the underground sanctuary dedicated to Saint Efisio and Saint Gregory the Great, with polychrome stucco statues; next to the oratory of St. Michael (1758-59), with dome decorated by Gregory the Are, and the cumbessias of the pilgrims. Free entrance.

  • Where Piazza Martiri, Fonni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Santuario nuragico di Gremanu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

A nuragic sacred area of about seven hectares in the Pratobello valley, about 1000 meters in a wood of roverelle: a fence of 70 meters with circular temple, a megaron temple of eleven meters and apsed environment, about one hundred huts and the only nuragic aqueduct so far known, with the springs closed by a semicircular curtain and a pool perhaps ritual. Facilities of the recent Bronze (XIII-XII century B.C.), finds up to the final Bronze.

  • Where Vallata di Pratobello, Fonni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Tombe di Giganti di Madau

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

About 1.5 km from Gremanu, perhaps linked to his village: four similar tombs of Giants, arranged amphitheatre and oriented to the east, with taurine protome; next to the first the stele of Madau, granite slab with symbols perhaps referring to the Pleiades. Italia.it indicates it as the most representative site of the area.

  • Where Località Madau, Fonni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Nostra Signora del Monte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

A country church called Madonna della Visitazione, less than five kilometers south of the village and about 1000 meters, among the highest churches on the island; described in the nineteenth century by Alberto Della Marmora, was demolished in 1976 and rebuilt. The Fonnesi families organize novena, procession and folk shows, hosted in the two rows of cumbessias next to the church.

  • Where Strada a sud di Fonni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Rioni storici e murales

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Mountain houses covered with scandulas (wood tiles) and murals with scenes of daily life; in the center the lategothic church of St. John the Baptist. During Autumn in Barbagia exceptionally open loggias and cellars; in early August you run the Palio di Fonni, and the carnival is known for the masks of Urthos and Buttudos.

  • Where Centro storico, Fonni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Monte Spada e Bruncu Spina

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

On the Gennargentu massif, over 1800 meters, the ski lifts of Monte Spada and Bruncu Spina for winter skiing, the only ones of the island according to italia.it; in the summer hiking trails among woods, springs and wildlife reserves.

  • Where Gennargentu, Fonni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
