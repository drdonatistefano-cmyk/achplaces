What to see in Montieri

Ancient copper and silver mining centre at the foot of the Poggio di Montieri (1051 m), in the park of the Metallifere Hills, with a small well-preserved historical centre between walls, tower houses of the fourteenth century and the cassero. In the territory the church of San Giacomo, the archaeological site of the Canonica di San Niccolò, the Castle of Fosini, the Red Roses of Boccheggiano and the Garden of the Sounds of Paul Fuchs.

Centro storico di Montieri

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The small old town, well preserved and overlooking a wide panorama of woods, preserves the defensive walls with three towers still visible and streets marked by towers of the fourteenth century. In the highest part stands the cassero, once connected to the ancient prisons. Between the 11th and 14th centuries, Montieri was an important mining centre.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Buca delle Fate

History and architecture · Archaeology

Palazzo Papi Mattii e Fonti di Sopra

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Also known as Palazzo dei Marchesi, it has a large loggia that houses the Fonti di Sopra, built in 1233, fed by the waters of Poggio di Montieri; a plaque on the façade recalls its construction and a marble coat of arms with the lion rampant the conquest of Sienese country. The foundations date back to 1621, when Vincenzo Salviati bought the marquisato by Cosimo II; in the first decades of the twentieth century it was renovated in neo-Renaissance style.

  • Where Strada Provinciale Galleraie, Montieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Palazzo Comunale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Designed by Lorenzo Porciatti in neo-Gothic style, with pointed arches, double-fores and triple-fores on the facade, a small tower and double-ceiling roof; it stands on the foundations of the previous Palazzo di Giustizia. It hosts the City and the tourist information office.

  • Where Montieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Chiesa di San Giacomo Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

13th century church in late Romanesque style, with a large nave and semicircular apse; outside three Romanesque monofores and an arched portal to the sixth. Next, connected by a tunnel, the environment that was the home of Blessed James Papocchi; on the altar a 1618 painting depicts Jesus speaking with the blessed in his penitential cell.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Canonica di San Niccolò

History and architecture · Archaeology

Archaeological site with the remains of a church with an unusual plant, shaped like a flower with six petals, linked to the mining and metallurgical activities of Montieri, center of copper and silver extraction. It is still subject of historical and archaeological investigations and is reached on foot, before the municipal Park Il Piano.

Castello di Fosini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built in the tenth century on a cliff above the Pavone stream, it belonged to the archbishops of Volterra, the Pannocchieschi, the Municipality of Siena and finally to the Barons Sergardi of Siena until the beginning of the twentieth century. The north and east walls retain their original appearance; they include the church of San Niccolò, renovated in the seventeenth century.

  • Where Fosini, Montieri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Cornate di Gerfalco

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Rocky mountain with silvery rocks, whose appearance recalls that of the Moon, with limestone cliffs that dominate the Castle of Fosini; the area is protected by the Cornate and Fosini Nature Reserve. The Parco delle Colline Metallifere offers a network of very busy trails.

  • Where Gerfalco, Montieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Le Roste di Boccheggiano

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Geosite along the first stretch of the Merse river, with calanchi and spire of red scrub, residues of the copper extraction method used at the end of the nineteenth century. Interesting for geology, mining history and its impact on the environment.

  • Where Boccheggiano, Montieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Il Giardino dei Suoni

Museums · Modern art

A few minutes from Boccheggiano, it houses the installations of the artist Paul Fuchs: very tall structures that move and emit sounds with the wind, a place particularly loved by children.
