What to see in Rovigo

The "city of roses" between Po and Adige: Piazza Vittorio Emanuele with Palazzo Roverella and Palazzo Roncale, the towers of the castle, the octagonal Round with the bell tower of Longhena and the Museum of Great Rivers.

25 places to visit in Rovigo, in the province of Rovigo (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Rovigo in the guide with map and filters

Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Documented as Piazza Grande from 1200 and surrounded by arcades, with the column of the Lion of St Mark of 1519 and the monument to Vittorio Emanuele II of 1881, heart of the city life.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Rovigo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: enit.it · italia.it

Palazzo Roverella

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The palace commissioned in 1474 by Cardinal Bartolomeo Roverella, since 2000 seat of the Pinacoteca dell'Accademia dei Concordi and of the Bishop's Seminary and large temporary exhibitions.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Laurenti 8/10, Rovigo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · enit.it

Palazzo Roncale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palazzo nobile of 1555 designed by Michele Sanmicheli, with the hall of the noble floor, seventeenth-century and eighteenth-century canvases and two large french tapestries; now home of exhibitions.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Rovigo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: enit.it · italia.it

Accademia dei Concordi

Museums · City history

The academy founded in 1580 by Count Gaspare Campo, in the palace completed in 1814: a library of about 300,000 volumes and incunabula and part of the art gallery with Tiepolo, Bellini and Palma il Vecchio.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Rovigo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: enit.it · italia.it

Loggia dei Nodari e Torre Civica

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The seat of the town hall, so called for the notaries who worked there before 1286, flanked by the Civic Tower rebuilt in the eighteenth century with the bell of the castle's mast.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Rovigo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: enit.it · italia.it

Torri del Castello

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The two towers Donà and Grimani (Mozza), the remains of the expansive castle of 1138 integrated into the walls; the Tower Donà is 66 meters high.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Rovigo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: enit.it · italia.it

Tempio della Beata Vergine del Soccorso

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

"La Rotonda," octagonal temple with porch designed by Francesco Zamberlan, pupil of Palladio: double cycle painting of the seventeenth century Venetian, wooden choir, gold leaf altar and 57-metre bell tower of Baldassarre Longhena.

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre, Rovigo

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: enit.it · italia.it

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Probably the oldest church in the city (XI century), rebuilt in the eighteenth century by Girolamo Frigimelica with unfinished facade and works of Palma il Giovane and Garofalo.

Museo dei Grandi Fiumi

Opened in 2001 in the Olivetani Monastery, it exhibits the finds of Polesine from the Bronze Age to the Renaissance with reconstructions of daily life and multimedia installations.

Teatro Sociale

History and architecture · Historic theatres · Free entry

Built in 1819 by Sante Baseggio and rebuilt after the fire of 1902 with Art Nouveau decorations by Giovanni Vianello: Beniamino Gigli debuted there; opera theatre of tradition since 1967 with free guided tours by reservation.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Rovigo

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: enit.it · italia.it
