What to see in Belgirate

Borgo della spiaggia piedmontese del Lago Maggiore on a promontory between the Borromeo Gulf and the low lake, a holiday destination from the nineteenth century: historical villas on the lake, the Romanesque Old Church of Santa Maria above the village, the Conelli Park and the Race Society, founded in 1858.

5 places to visit in Belgirate, in the province of Verbano-Cusio-Ossola (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa Vecchia di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Small Romanesque church of the 12th century in a dominant position on a promontory above the village, with Romanesque bell tower and frescoes of the 16th century recently restored; view of the lake and the mountains.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Ville storiche di Belgirate

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

Villa Fontana (Villa Maria), purchased in 1892 by the publisher Emilio Treves and frequented by Verga, Gozzano, Boito and D'Annunzio, with an English garden, overlooking the lake; Villa Carlotta, nineteenth century, today hotel; Villa Bonghi (1858-1861) by Ruggero Bonghi; Villa Cairoli, which hosted Garibaldi; Villa Conelli with the Italian garden; Villa Dal Pozzo d'Annone, Villa Fontana-Fedeli known as the castle and residence of Princess Matilde, nephew of Napoleon. They are seen from the outside: the sources do not indicate them as visitable.

  • Where Lungolago, Belgirate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: illagomaggiore.com · comune.belgirate.vb.it · distrettolaghi.it

Lungolago e spiaggia pubblica

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Walk along the lake overlooking Lake Maggiore and the Varesotto lakes; from the public beach you can rent sailing and motor boats. In Belgirate, the Regate Company, founded in 1858 and designated as the first yacht club in Italy, has its headquarters, with historical regattas every year. Free parking; you arrive by train, bus or boat.

  • Where Lungolago, Belgirate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it

Sentiero Villa Lesa - Monte alle Croci - Belgirate

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of 11.4 km (about 3 hours and 40, 480 m of difference in altitude, suitable for families, recommended in spring and autumn) between woods and terraces, with panoramic view of the lower lake from Comnago and the beech trees of Monte alle Croci; arrives at the Old Church of Santa Maria sopra Belgirate and returns to the station by skirting villas and historical gardens.

  • Where Partenza da Villa Lesa, arrivo a Belgirate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: illagomaggiore.com · distrettolaghi.it
