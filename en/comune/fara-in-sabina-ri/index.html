What to see in Fara in Sabina

Medieval village of Sabina between the olive groves of DOP Sabina, with the panoramic piazza of the Cathedral, the archaeological museum of Sabina Tiberina in Palazzo Brancaleoni and the Museum of Silence of the Clarisse Hermits; in the hamlet of Farfa the Benedictine abbey, national monument since 1928.

6 places to visit in Fara in Sabina, in the province of Rieti (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Civico Archeologico della Sabina Tiberina

Museums · Archaeological

In the sixteenth century Palazzo Brancaleoni in Piazza del Duomo (formerly MUSAF, renamed in 2024; until 2001 it was at the abbey of Farfa) exhibits artifacts from prehistory to the Roman age: the materials of the ancient Cures, the Sabino Cippo of Cures of the second half of the third century BC, the furnishings of the necropolis of Eretum with the Chariot of the Prince of the Tomb XI, stolen and returned in 2024 by the Ny Carlsberg Glyptotek of Copenhagen, and in the Throne Hall the terracotta throne of the XXXVI tomb of Colle del Forno.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Fara in Sabina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Museo del Silenzio e Monastero delle Clarisse Eremite

Museums · Themed

Opened in 2004 in a room of about 60 m2 obtained from the ancient church of Santa Maria in Castello, inside the monastery on Mount Buzio: 21 windows on seven themes (prayer, silence, discipline, sewing, pharmacy, kitchen, work) light up in sequence in the room without windows; a fresco of the 15th century Madonna della Misericordia remains visible. The visit can continue in the monastery accompanied by nuns.

  • Where Via Santa Maria in Castello 16, Fara in Sabina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Piazza del Duomo e Collegiata di Sant'Antonino

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval square with terrace on the valley from which on clear days you can see the hills of Rome, the cistern built by the Farnese in 1588 and the bell tower detached from the church. The Collegiate Church of Sant'Antonino Martire, built between 1501 and 1506, houses a tabernacle in the alabaster of Volterra del Vignola and a crucifix. Nearby, the seventeenth century church of St. James of the Brotherhood of Mercy.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Fara in Sabina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it

Abbazia e borgo di Farfa

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Abbey of Santa Maria di Farfa, founded in the middle of the sixth century, privileged by Charlemagne in 775 and known for the scriptorium of the IX-XI centuries; national monument since 1928 and stage of the Via di Francesco. The basilica has a fourteenth-century portal with frescoed bezel of 1508, three Renaissance aisles with the Universal Judgment of Henrik van der Broek (1561), grotesque school pumpkin (1576) and a golden coffered ceiling with the Orsini coat of arms. Around the hamlet of the shops was born for fairs, with wooden architraves of 1480 and a 17th century frame still active.

  • Where Via del Fontanone 3, Farfa, Fara in Sabina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · bm.bncrm.beniculturali.it · visitlazio.com

Ulivone di Canneto

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the oldest and largest olive trees in Europe, about 15 meters high, in the hamlet of Canneto along the SP 41; freely accessible with the permission of the owner, in a landscape of olive groves that produce Sabina DOP oil.

  • Where SP 41, Canneto, Fara in Sabina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Forno tardo-rinascimentale del Monte Frumentario

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

On the ground floor of Mount Frumentario an ancient oven of end Renaissance still bakes bread with natural yeast as it used to be.
