What to see in Casazza

Municipality of Val Cavallina where, under the alluvial deposits of the Drione torrent, the Roman village of Cavellas was brought back to light, today museum; in the village Palazzo Bettoni, which hosted Garibaldi and houses the historical environmental museum of Val Cavallina, and in the hamlet of Mologno a 13th century castle with tower.

Area archeologica e Museo Cavellas

History and architecture · Archaeology

Roman rural village (I-VI century A.D.), one of the largest in the region, discovered during construction works at the state road 42 with excavations between 1986 and 1996 and preserved under a supermarket: over 1,000 m2 buried by about four meters of Drion deposits, with stone walls and lime, rooms, floors and fireplaces preserved for about one meter and three construction phases. Farming, cereals and weaving were lived; a walkway above the excavation, interactive totems and panels guide the visit, with exhibits. Open Wednesday 9-12,30, Saturday and Sunday 15-18, other days by reservation.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Parco dei Molini

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Park born in the late nineties on a project by Raffaello Cattaneo, between Via Piave and the river Cherio, to recover the area and protect the reed: a high part equipped with games, picnics, barbecue and a hexagonal tower for birdwatching, and the reed along the river. On the cycle path of Val Cavallina, it is the last stop before Lake Endine; always open, free access.

  • Where Via Piave, Casazza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: comune.casazza.bg.it · visitbergamo.net
