What to see in Serralunga d'Alba

One of the most beautiful and intact villages of the Langhe del Barolo, dominated by the 14th century castle.

Castello di Serralunga d'Alba

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Among the best examples of the 14th century noble castle of Piedmont, remained almost unchanged: the palacium elongated with cylindrical tower, hanging watchtower and square tower, with large overlapping rooms. Guided tour by reservation.

  • Where Via Castello, Serralunga d'Alba
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page visitlmr.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlmr.it · abbonamentomusei.it

Borgo di Serralunga

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the most beautiful and intact villages of the Langhe, surrounded by the hills of great wines.

  • Where Serralunga d'Alba
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page visitlmr.it

Sources: abbonamentomusei.it · visitlmr.it

Fontanafredda

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

The wine estate was born from 54 hectares of Serralunga and is part of the private heritage of Vittorio Emanuele II, now a historic winery among the vineyards of Barolo.

  • Where Via Alba 15, Serralunga d'Alba
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page visitlmr.it

Sources: stradadelbarolo.it · cultura.gov.it · visitlmr.it
