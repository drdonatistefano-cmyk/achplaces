What to see in Muccia

Municipality of the Macerata Apennines linked to the Da Varano di Camerino, with the octagonal church of Santa Maria di Varano, the sanctuary of the Madonna of Col di Venti, the hermitage of Blessed Rizzerio and the villages of Massaprofoglio and Costafiore. Eremo e Santuario del Beato Rizzerio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

At 730 meters above sea level, it rises on the tomb of Blessed Rizzerio, born in Muccia and companion of Saint Francis, who left the noble origins to live as a hermit in a cave of the hills of Muccia. The church of San Giacomo Maggiore that kept its remains was demolished in 1948-49; the surgeon Cesare Lami found cave and tomb and rebuilt the church on the ancient foundations. The tower is a symbol of the vanity of material goods, and today it hosts theatres for events and tourist reception.

  • Where Coda di Muccia, Muccia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · letsmarche.it

Massaprofoglio: castello di Prefoglio e Torre di Massa

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

In the hamlet of Massaprofoglio are visible the ruins of the castle of Prefoglio, medieval fortification of the dukes Da Varano di Camerino; near, at 808 meters of altitude, is the Tower of Massa, called Torraccia.

  • Where Strada Provinciale 69, Massaprofoglio, Muccia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Borgo di Costafiore

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

A perched village, inserted by the regional portal among the historical villages: in the Middle Ages its position in the high hills allowed to alarm and protect Muccia.
