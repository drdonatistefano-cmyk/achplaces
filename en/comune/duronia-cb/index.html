What to see in Duronia

Medieval village perched on the Lucera Castle of Sangro, called Civitavecchia until 1875 and identified by some scholars with the Duronia Sannitica conquered by the Romans in 293 BC. Above the village the Civita massif, with the remains of the Sannitic fortifications and a view of 28 municipalities.

Civita, roccaforte dei Sanniti

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The imposing rocky massif, whose profile is interrupted by four ridges that descend into ravines; the peaks, the "morconia" rise for more than two hundred meters on the valley and once made it inaccessible. On the northern and eastern slopes remain powerful but fragmentary traits of the Sannitic walls of the third century BC; from above they are seen with the naked eye twenty-eight common. Always accessible.

  • Where Località Civita, Duronia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: comune.duronia.cb.it · visitmolise.eu

Siti archeologici del territorio

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

In addition to the Sannitic enclosure of the Civita, with tombstones, the territory preserves the remains of a Roman villa in the locality of Casaglivune, Sannitic and Roman finds in the locality of Vicende, Casale and San Basilio and, in the locality of San Tommaso, a shrine in ruin between rocks and vegetation. On the tracturo remains traces of Sannitic fortifications and the farmhouse of the Duke of Pescolanciano. Always accessible.

  • Where Territorio di Duronia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: comune.duronia.cb.it · visitmolise.eu

Borgo antico e Chiesa di San Nicola

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The oldest nucleus, of medieval plant, rises on the top of a mountain, with the morconi of Lauro rocks to strengthen its slopes and the Morconi of San Tommaso rocks to act as a scenery to the parish church. The Church of St. Nicholas is cited as a testimony to the ancient times of the country.

  • Where Centro storico, Duronia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: visitmolise.eu · comune.duronia.cb.it

Tratturo Lucera–Castel di Sangro

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Duronia rises along the ancient Lucera-Castel di Sangro, via della trashumanza, which can be walked; along the path there are traces of Sannitic fortifications.

  • Where Tratturo Lucera–Castel di Sangro, Duronia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu
