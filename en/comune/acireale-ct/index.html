What to see in Acireale

Baroque city between Etna and Ionio, famous for Carnival with carts in mache: Piazza Duomo with the Cathedral and the Basilica of Saints Peter and Paul, the Basilica of San Sebastiano frescoed by Pietro Paolo Vasta, the Opera dei Pupi, the Zelantea and the lava coast of Timpa with the Chiazzette towards Santa Maria La Scala.

Basilica collegiata di San Sebastiano e museo

Places of worship · Basilicas

National monument built from 1609 to 1644 and rebuilt after the earthquake of 1693 between 1699 and 1705: the façade of Angelo Bellofiore has a frieze with fourteen putti, the balustrade of 1754 bears statues of characters of the Old Testament and the frescoes of Pietro Paolo Vasta (1732-1736) adorn transept, choir and dome; among the canvases the San Sebastiano in glory of Michele Vecchio. The museum includes the rooms of the Treasury with silver and liturgical gold and the eighteenth century crypt with vestments, wooden statues and Sicilian paintings.

  • Where Piazza Leonardo Vigo, Acireale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.acireale.ct.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Cattedrale di Maria Santissima Annunziata

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

XV century cathedral with two bell towers identical to the sides of the façade, center of devotion to Saint Venus; in the floor of the transept runs a marble sundial with twelve zodiac signs, work of the Danish astronomer Peters.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Acireale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it · comune.acireale.ct.it

Piazza Duomo e Basilica dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Museum opened with the Cathedral, the Basilica of Saints Peter and Paul (XVI-XVII century), with white stone façade of Syracuse and high bell tower, the eighteenth-century Municipal Palace and Palazzo Modò, which housed the Eldorado Theatre; the concentric ring flooring of 2009 is by Scaccianoce, Messina and Paolo Portoghesi.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Acireale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.acireale.ct.it · italia.it · visitsicily.info

Teatro dei Pupi Pennisi-Macrì (Collezione di pupi siciliani)

Museums · Themed

Teatro dell'Opera dei Pupi founded in 1887 by puparo don Mariano Pennisi and transferred in 1928 to the current headquarters; the adopted son Emanuele Macrì took him on international tours. Acquired and restored by the Region, it houses the Pennisi-Macrì Theatre Collection with puppets and equipment of the Sicilian theatres, with audioguides.

  • Where Via Galatea, Acireale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Biblioteca e Pinacoteca Zelantea

Museums · Old masters

The Accademia degli Zelanti, founded in 1671, is housed in a neoclassical palace by Mariano Panebianco; the library preserves hundreds of thousands of volumes, with incunabula, and the art gallery painted from the seventeenth to the twentieth century (Pietro Paolo and Alessandro Vasta, Vito D'Anna, Giuseppe Sciuti, Saru Spina). Also displayed are the eighteenth-century carriage of the Senate of Acaese, the Greek-Roman bust found in Capomulini in 1675, weapons, minerals, coins and fossils.

  • Where Via Marchese di San Giuliano, Acireale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · accademiadeglizelanti.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Riserva naturale orientata La Timpa

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

It is the last strip of the Aci Wood, with the Palombe Cave with its columnary basalts, springs such as the Iron Water and raptors as a ghep and buzzard. It is an overhanging coast of lava flows of different eras, up to about 80 meters high, established in 1999 on 225 hectares.

  • Where Acireale (tra Pietra Monaca e Santa Maria La Scala)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: orbs.regione.sicilia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Chiazzette e Santa Maria La Scala

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Terraced path built by the Spaniards in the eighteenth century that descends from the city to the seaside village of Santa Maria La Scala; it is accessed from the bridge on the National Road from Via Romeo.
