What to see in Sora

The city of foundation flies on the shores of the Liri, where you can meet the valleys of Liri, Comino and Rovereto; on the hill San Casto dominates the Fortress Sorella, while in the center you will find the Cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta, San Francesco, the Abbey of San Domenico and the Civic Museum of the Media Valle del Liri. It is the birthplace of Vittorio De Sica.

Castello di San Casto e Cassio (Rocca Sorella)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Ruins on the San Casto hill: born as a fortress Volsca, then Roman and Longobard, it was transformed by the Della Rovere in 1520 in the fortress reached until today; remains of the Roman castle are partly visible in the underground. It became a noble residence with Cantelmo, Della Rovere, Boncompagni and Borgia. It is a natural monument and can be reached on foot with CAI paths, with wide views of the city.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo civico della Media Valle del Liri

Museums · City history

In the former convent of Minor Conventuals next to St. Francis (XIV century, rebuilt between 1683 and 1727 after the earthquake of 1654), built on the foundations of a Roman villa; established in 1979, opened in 2005 and completed in 2010, it develops on three floors from prehistory to the twentieth century. The materials of the sacred area underneath the Cathedral (the bronze thesaurus, headphone dedicated to Minerva, altar of Mars, architectural terracotta), statues of military tribes, medieval capitals, the copy of the diploma of 1292 of Charles II of Anjou and a section on the Duchy Boncompagni and the first paper mills. Open Monday and Wednesday-Friday 9-13, Saturday 9-13 and 16-20, Sunday 15-19; closed on Tuesday.
