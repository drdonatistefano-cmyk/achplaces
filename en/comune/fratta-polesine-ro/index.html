What to see in Fratta Polesine

Small center of the Polesine with the Palladian Villa Badoer, the sixteenth-century Villa Grimani Molin Avezzù and, in the boats of the Badoera, the National Archaeological Museum dedicated to the settlement of the Bronze Age of Frattesina; in the surroundings the Ecomuseum Mulino al Pizzon.

6 places to visit in Fratta Polesine, in the province of Rovigo (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa Badoer (La Badoera)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage

Commissioned by Francesco Badoer to Andrea Palladio: the manor body existed already in 1557 and the villa appears, with some difference, in the Four Books (1570). It stands on a base with scenic staircase and recalls an ancient temple between terraces; pronao and interiors were decorated by Giallo Fiorentino with grotesques and mythological and allegorical themes, rediscovered in the restorations of the Regional Institute Ville Venete. Visit by reservation (tel. 366 324 0619).

Museo archeologico nazionale di Fratta Polesine

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Opened on 21 February 2009 in the northern barchess of Villa Badoer, on two floors it tells of more than forty years of research in the middle Polesine and especially Frattesina, European center of importance in the final Bronze Age (XII-X century BC) for the processing of metals, ivory, amber and glass. On the ground floor the daily life and craftsmanship, with the "Tesoretto" of ivory combs, glass and amber pearls and bronze ornaments; on the upper floor the accessories of the cremation necropolis of Le Narde and Fondo Zanotto. According to the Mic card is open every day 8.30-19.30.

  • Where Via Giovanni Tasso, Fratta Polesine
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Villa Grimani Molin Avezzù

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in 1564 for Vincenzo Grimani, in a style close to the Badoera, perhaps on a project by Palladio or his follower; it rises on a sort of island between the Storcido and a minor canal. Large frescoed rooms with themes of love and fertility, garden with balustrades and niches. Noble floor available on Saturday 15-18 and Sunday 10-12.30 and 15-18.

  • Where Via Zabarella 1, Fratta Polesine
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Ecomuseo Mulino al Pizzon

Museums · Themed

Ecomuseum in an intact natural environment where the canal Scortico flows into the Canalbianco (Tartaro-Po di Levante): an example of industrial archaeology of Polesine that still regulates water for agriculture in droughts and helps to prevent floods. It is part of the Polesine Provincial Museum System and organizes educational visits on bread, ancient cereals and renewable energies.

  • Where Pizzon, Fratta Polesine

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
