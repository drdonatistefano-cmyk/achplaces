What to see in Ossi

Coros village 12 km from Sassari, with the necropolis of Mesu 'e Montes, one of the 17 members of the Domus de Janas registered in the UNESCO World Heritage in 2025, other neolithic necropolis, the nuragic village of Sa Mandra 'e Sa Giua and the ethnographic museum in the Baronial Palace.

Necropoli di Mesu 'e Montes

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Eighteen domus de Janas multicellular excavated in the limestone of the southern slope of Monte Mamas, divided into two groups by an ancient tracturo and used by the Neolithic final to the middle Bronze; the tombs I and II have twelve rooms each and the decorations reproduce elements of the neolithic huts. From 12 July 2025, he was one of the 17 members of the UNESCO Domus de Janas website.

  • Where Strada Provinciale 97, Ossi (strada per Santa Maria di Cea, al km 8,2 sterrata a sinistra e 500 m a piedi)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · cultura.gov.it · sardegna.cultura.gov.it · idese.cultura.gov.it

Necropoli di S'Adde 'e Asile

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Domus de janas neolithic excavated in low calcareous outcrop in an oak wood on the slopes of Mount Corona 'e Teula, identified by Hercules Contu in 1969 and excavated since 1984; the Tomb Major has 21 environments with triple corniform pattern, false doors and double sloping ceiling, the Tomb of the Clessiddre owes its name to the geometric motifs of the antecella.

  • Where SP 97, Ossi (al km 5 a sinistra, poi verso la fonte di Piogosa; anche dal tratturo di Sas Rainanas)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Complesso nuragico di Sa Mandra 'e Sa Giua

History and architecture · Archaeology

Village of huts around a nuraghe with tholos complex of which the central tower remains visible, in the Litterai district; among the huts the house of bread is paved circular environment with seats and bathtub, today attributed to a cult of the waters. The excavations have returned bronzes, basins for fusion and a statue of chief tribe.

  • Where Via Nuraghe, Ossi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: idese.cultura.gov.it · comune.ossi.ss.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Museo Etnografico nel Palazzo baronale

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the seventeenth-century Baronial Palace, three floors reconstruct the rural environments of the last century: agricultural tools and for cheese, wine and oil on the ground floor, kitchen, bedroom, "s'apposentu" and carpenter shops, blacksmith and shoemaker on the first, and an archaeological section with the models of the domus de janas on the second.

  • Where Via G. M. Angioy 2, Ossi
  • Entry €4, residents €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.comune.ossi.ss.it

Sources: comune.ossi.ss.it · sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque country church of the 12th century in local stone, only testimony of the medieval village of Briai, with architravate portal, cruciform window and ceramic basins in the bezel of a lateral entrance; in the area also a Roman necropolis in use until the 3rd century. On 16 and 17 January, the bonfire lit on fogarone.

  • Where Campagna di Ossi, a circa 7 km dal paese (verso Florinas)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Centro storico e chiese del paese

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The parish church of San Bartolomeo, dating back to the early seventeenth century, has a unique nave with a barrel vault and wooden statues from the 16th to the 19th century; the church of Santa Vittoria was born in the 12th century and was restored in the 14th and 17th centuries. The feast of St. Bartholomew, at the end of August, has the procession in traditional clothes.

  • Where Centro, Ossi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
