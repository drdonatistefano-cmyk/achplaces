What to see in Arezzo

Ancient Etruscan Lucumonia and Roman city, home to Vasari, Petrarch and Guido Monaco, with the cycle of the Legend of the True Cross by Piero della Francesca and Piazza Grande de "Life is beautiful."

25 places to visit in Arezzo, in the province of Arezzo (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 8 of the 12 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Piazza Grande e Logge Vasari

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The sloping square dominated by the Palazzo delle Logge designed by Vasari (1570-1593), with the Palazzo della Fraternita dei Laici and the facades covered with coats of arms; scene of "La vita è bella" by Benigni.

  • Where Piazza Grande, Arezzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Fiera Antiquaria di Arezzo

Local life and traditions · Historic markets · Free entry · Viewpoint

Every first Sunday of the month and the previous Saturday over 500 banks fill Piazza Grande and the nearby streets: it is the oldest antique fair in Italy, with up to 30,000 visitors.

  • Where Piazza Grande, Arezzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Torre Faggiolana e case-torri di Piazza Grande

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

In the lower part of the square, in front of the Logge, the medieval stone buildings: the crenellated tower Faggiolana of the thirteenth century, the Palazzo Cofani-Brizzolari and the house-tower of the Lappoli.

  • Where Piazza Grande, Arezzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Basilica di San Francesco e Cappella Bacci

Places of worship · Basilicas · Paid entry

Gothic church of the thirteenth century in bricks: in the Bacci chapel the cycle of the Legend of the True Cross by Piero della Francesca (1452-1466), one of the masterpieces of Renaissance painting; also an Annunciation attributed to Signorelli.

  • Where Piazza San Francesco 1, Arezzo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.visittuscany.com
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: visittuscany.com · discoverarezzo.com

Cattedrale dei Santi Donato e Pietro

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The dome in sandstone with the windows of Guillaume de Marcillat, the Magdalena di Piero della Francesca, terracotta by Andrea della Robbia and a source with a relief of Donatello.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Arezzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Pieve di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

One of the best known examples of Romanesque in Tuscany, in sandstone, with the façade with five arches surmounted by three loggias of columns all different; the apse is seen from Piazza Grande.

  • Where Corso Italia, Arezzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Fortezza Medicea e Passeggio del Prato

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The pentagonal fortress desired by Cosimo I, with high bastions and 360 degree view of the city; below, the park of Prato, shady and suitable for children.

  • Where Viale Bruno Buozzi, Arezzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo Archeologico Nazionale Gaio Cilnio Mecenate e Anfiteatro romano

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The state museum built on the remains of the Roman amphitheatre, with the Aretine ceramics and Etruscan jewels of the necropolis of Poggio del Sole.

  • Where Via Margaritone 10, Arezzo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.visittuscany.com
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: visittuscany.com · discoverarezzo.com

Museo Statale d'Arte Medievale e Moderna

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the fifteenth century Palazzo Bruni-Ciocchi sculptures and paintings from the thirteenth to the sixteenth century (Margaritone, Spinello Aretino, Signorelli, Vasari), with majolica, glass, coins, avori and weapons.

  • Where Via San Lorentino 8, Arezzo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo di Casa Vasari

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The house that Giorgio Vasari, born in Arezzo in 1511, bought and frescoed between 1542 and 1548: a rare example of the late Renaissance artist's house, with about fifty paintings of the Florentine Gallerie.

  • Where Via XX Settembre 55, Arezzo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.visittuscany.com
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: visittuscany.com · discoverarezzo.com

Mudas - Museo Diocesano d'Arte Sacra

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the Bishop's Palace the works of the diocese, the cathedral and the churches of the territory, with paintings from the fifteenth to the nineteenth century.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo 1, Arezzo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Palazzo dei Priori

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

The municipal palace of the first half of the fourteenth century, with frescoes, the Wedding Hall with two paintings of Vasari and the Council Hall frescoed by Parri di Spinello; the route goes up towards the tower.

  • Where Via Montetini 4a, Arezzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Casa Museo Ivan Bruschi

Museums · House museums · Paid entry
