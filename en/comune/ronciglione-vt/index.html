What to see in Ronciglione

Borgo with a double face, medieval on the cliff and Renaissance in the streets of the Farnese, with one of the most beautiful historical Carnivals in Italy and Lake Vico.

3 places to visit in Ronciglione, in the province of Viterbo (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Ronciglione in the guide with map and filters

Chiesa di Sant'Eusebio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Early Christian church with three remarkable frescoes: the oldest Visitatio Virginis, from the 8th century, a Last Supper from 1100 and an 11th century Tree of Jesse.

  • Where Ronciglione
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Riserva naturale del Lago di Vico

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The volcanic lake in the nature reserve between the mountains Venus and Fogliano, with the beech forest of Monte Venere, for canoeing, trekking, birdwatching and mountain biking.

  • Where Lago di Vico, Ronciglione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Borgo di sopra, Palazzo Apostolico e Fontana dell'Unicorno

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The medieval village on the cliff with alleys and houses in tuff, in contrast with the straight Renaissance streets of the Farnese, the Apostolic Palace, now Town Hall, and the Fountain of the Unicorn.

  • Where Ronciglione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
