What to see in Pieve di Teco

Borgo dell'alta valle Arroscia on the road between the sea and Piedmont, among the most beautiful villages in Italy since 2026: the arcade course, the collegiate, the small Salvini theatre and the museum of masks of the Ubagu.

7 places to visit in Pieve di Teco, in the province of Imperia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 3 of the 5 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Portici di corso Ponzoni

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The main street of the historic center, porticoed from the sixteenth century, closed by two small squares at the ends.

  • Where Corso Mario Ponzoni, Pieve di Teco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Collegiata di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

An eighteenth-century church on the site of a Gothic church, with a baptismal font of the fifteenth century.

  • Where Pieve di Teco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Madonna della Ripa, museo d'arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

Former parish church of the fourteenth century on the Arroscia river, today a museum with local sacred art of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries.

  • Where Pieve di Teco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo della Maschera Ubaga

Museums · Themed

In the former Augustinian convent on Piazza Borelli, masks of contemporary artists who reinterpret the ubagu, the impervious places at the border between the solar world and the dark world.

  • Where Piazza Borelli, Pieve di Teco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Teatro Tommaso Salvini

History and architecture · Historic theatres

One of the smallest historical theatres in Italy still active.

  • Where Pieve di Teco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Torre dell'Orologio e Palazzo Manfredi

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The tower symbol of civic identity and the noble palace of the village.

  • Where Pieve di Teco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Sentieri clavesani e Via del Sale

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hiking and cycling trails of the Arroscia Valley towards the Ligurian Alps Park, including the ancient Clavesian paths and the Via del Sale.

  • Where Pieve di Teco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
