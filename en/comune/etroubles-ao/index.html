Borgo medievale di Etroubles

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Cobbled streets, stone houses with roofs in los Angeles and fountains fed by the water of Vélan, along the Via Francigena.

Museo a cielo aperto «À Étroubles, avant toi sont passés…»

Museums · Modern art · Free entry · Viewpoint

Since 2005 the village has been a permanent art gallery with 21 works by internationally renowned artists, in collaboration with the Fondation Gianadda of Martigny, visible all year round.

  • Where Etroubles
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Museo della latteria

Museums · Themed

In the first dairy farm of the Valle d'Aosta, founded on 24 July 1853, with the original equipment and the phases of the production of cheese and butter.

Museums · Themed
