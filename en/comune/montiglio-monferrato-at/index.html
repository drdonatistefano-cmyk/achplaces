What to see in Montiglio Monferrato

Borgo del Monferrato is dominated by the castle with the chapel of Sant'Andrea, which preserves a large cycle of fourteenth-century frescoes on the life of Christ; in the village 57 sundials and the Romanesque parish church of San Lorenzo, in the hamlets the baroque castle of Colcavagno and the frescoed Romanesque church of Scandeluzzo.

5 places to visit in Montiglio Monferrato, in the province of Asti (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello e Cappella di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The castle, of thirteenth-century origin, rebuilt in the fifteenth century and adapted to dwelling in the eighteenth century, dominates the village. The chapel, already documented in 1349 and once connected to the castle, houses on two registers a cycle of fourteenth-century frescoes on the life of Christ, from the Annunciation to the "Noli me tangere" (the Annunciation to the "Noli me tangere"), defined by the Municipality as the major of Piedmont and attributed to a painter of the Lombard school called Maestro di Montiglio; covered with plaster during a pestilence, were rediscovered in 1931 and restored since 1984.

the castle is private property, visits with the Walking Tour in fixed dates (€10, free up to 14 years) according to Visit MRL

Centro storico e meridiane

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village has 57 sundials, each with a Latin motto, the work of Gnomonist Mario Tebenghi, including the one on the bell tower; the route in the village goes from the castle to the parish church of Santa Maria della Pace. Bilingual guided tours (Italian and English) with the Montiglio Monferrato Walking Tour, max 25 people.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I 1, Montiglio Monferrato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it

Pieve di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the twelfth century in the cemetery of the village; at the end of the nineteenth century instead of the three naves were obtained chapels to support once a barrel that replaced the original trusses. Capitals have proto-Christian symbols.

  • Where Cimitero, Montiglio Monferrato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it

Castello di Colcavagno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

First traces in the 10th century (the name Curcavagnus is attested in 1164), several times rebuilt; in 1667 the Marquis Flamino Giacinto Balbiano transformed it into a baroque brick villa. From 1902 to 2010 it was the retirement home of the nuns of the Cottolengo; from 2010 it is the seat of the Paolo Ferraris Foundation, a cultural center with exhibitions and events, with the Salone delle Feste and the museum project "The tools of memory" on the tools for the restoration of paper and parchment. In the hamlet there is also the Romanesque church of the Saints Vittore and Corona.

the MiC card (Add. 2021) indicates closed every day: check opening with the Paolo Ferraris Foundation
