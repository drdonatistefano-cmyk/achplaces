What to see in Livorno

Medici and multicultural city-port, with fortresses, the canals of the Venice district, the historical market and the Terrazza Mascagni on the sea; home of Modigliani, Fattori and Mascagni.

25 places to visit in Livorno, in the province of Livorno (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers 6 of the 10 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Terrazza Mascagni

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The seaside square designed by Enrico Salvais in 1925, with a chessboard of 8,700 m2 of over 34,000 black and white tiles, 4,000 balustrade columns and the gazebo for the rebuilt orchestra.

  • Where Viale Italia 165, Livorno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
