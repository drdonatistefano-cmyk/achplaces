What to see in Conzano

The hilly village of Monferrato Casale linked to the Counts Vidua: on the top of the historic center the eighteenth-century Villa Vidua, with the route dedicated to the ... traveler count Carlo Vidua; in the hamlet of San Maurizio the fifteenth-century church founded by Teodoro II of Monferrato, where Empress Sofia is buried.

Villa Vidua

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

The eighteenth-century residence of the Vidua counts, probably on a medieval structure, in the highest point of the historic center overlooking the ancient piazza d'armi (today piazza Australia); purchased by the Municipality in 1996 and restored. The south façade is marked by loggias; the rooms preserve frescoes, in part by Antonio Ivaldi called the Muto, including the Chinese room with scenes from the East. It hosts the permanent journey of Carlo Vidua, Count of Conzano, traveler and collector, curated with the Academy of Sciences of Turin. Information to the Municipality (tel. 0142 925132).

Chiesa di San Maurizio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Founded in 1418 by Teodoro II Marquis of Monferrato, in late Gothic, consecrated in 1625 and Franciscan monastery until the Napoleonic age; parish since 1828. It is buried there by Empress Sofia, daughter of Theodorus II and wife of John VIII Paleologist, crowned in 1421 by Basilissa. It preserves a large canvas of the Assumption attributed to the Spanzotti, the altarpiece of San Diego at the Counts Vidua, the funeral monument of Carlo Vidua and an organ Mentasti; the cloister has frescoed lunettes among the oldest in the area.

Parrocchiale di Santa Lucia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It comes from the chapel of the castle ...Santa Maria di Conzano, mentioned at the end of the XII century; the building and the Romanesque bell tower are of the fifteenth century, consecration in 1843 and last renovation in 1904. Frescoes by Luigi Faini, four tables of a sixteenth-century polyptych dismembered by the Spanish school (San Maurizio, Santa Caterina, Santo Stefano, Santa Lucia) and the altarpiece of Santa Lucia in 1862.

  • Where Via Mezzavilla 2, Conzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.conzano.al.it

Sources: comune.conzano.al.it
