What to see in Villanova del Battista

Country of Irpinia at 742 meters, overlooking the valley of the Ufita and crossed by the Tratturo Pescasseroli-Candela, destroyed and rebuilt several times after the earthquakes of 1694 and 1930; to visit the multimedia museum of transhumance, the Municipal Museum and the church of Santa Maria Assunta. On August 27, for St. John the Baptist, the Giglio drags along the streets with ropes, obelisk of straw woven about 28 meters high.

Museo multimediale della transumanza

Museums · Themed

Dedicated to transhumance along the tratturous royal route from Abruzzo to Puglia, it traces the history of the island in Irpinia with a wide cultural and anthropological heritage, in a modern building that plays with light and water effects on the surrounding landscape. Totally accessible.

Museo comunale

Museums · Themed

Small museum in several sections: wood, stone and glass sculptures of local artists and craftsmen; modern with the first computers, typewriters, projectors, telephones and a Lambretta; the peasant civilization with a nineteenth century bedroom; local stories and folklore, with an old organ, a crucifix of 1930, war relics and straw panels linked to the Giglio of August; a small picture gallery. No reservation; phone 0825 826015.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Large church of the historic center with a single nave with three entrances, rebuilt entirely after the earthquakes of 1930 and 1980. It preserves two 17th century paintings attributed to the school of Luca Giordano, the Fall of Jesus and the Deposition. Open during function hours.
