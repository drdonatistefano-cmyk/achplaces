What to see in Luzzara

Gonzaghesco country on the Po and home of Cesare Zavattini: the portico centre with 55 metres of Civic Tower, the highest in the province, the Palazzo della Macina di Luca Fancelli, the Teatro Sociale, the National Museum of the Naïves Arts in the Agostinian monastery and, outside, the golena of the great river and the Museo del Truciolo in Villarotta.

9 places to visit in Luzzara, in the province of Reggio Emilia (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo nazionale delle arti naïves «Cesare Zavattini»

Museums · Modern art

The first and only National Museum of the Naïves in Italy was established by the national prize created by Zavattini in 1967; it is located in the fifteenth-century convent of the Augustinians, linked to Rodolfo Gonzaga the first lord of Luzzara, next to the church of the Annunziata, transformed by the Municipality into an exhibition space.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Torre Civica

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

55 metres high, it is the highest tower in the province of Reggio Emilia: it was built after the destruction of the fortress in the battle of Luzzara in 1702, it was started in 1724 with the materials of the old fortifications and thanks to collections of wheat, wine and money, and completed in 1780 with the cross surmounted by the pike, emblem of the country. Eleven floors, four bronze bells, 147 steps to the bell cell; in the twentieth century it was also aqueduct tower. It is admired from the outside: the climbs to the bell cell are rare, on special occasions.

  • Where Piazza Ferrari 3, Luzzara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.reggioemiliawelcome.it

Sources: reggioemiliawelcome.it

Palazzo della Macina

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Designed at the end of the fifteenth century by Luca Fancelli, it occupied almost the entire main square and was frescoed; it was largely destroyed in the battle of 15 August 1702. The name comes from a tax on the grain that was collected there; above the portal remains the coat of arms of the Gonzaga in colored porcelain.

  • Where Piazza principale, Luzzara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.reggioemiliawelcome.it

Sources: reggioemiliawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Teatro Sociale

History and architecture · Historic theatres

It was built around 1813 from a rustic building used as a barn and inaugurated in 1852 with I Capuleti and Montecchi di Bellini: up to 400 seats, 47 stages on three orders and a curtain painted by hand with the Fiera di Luzzara in the presence of the Gonzaga princes.

  • Where Luzzara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.reggioemiliawelcome.it

Centro storico e chiesa di San Giorgio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center, with its ancient porticos, preserves the installation of the Gonzaga era, where you can read squares and civil and religious buildings of the fifteenth century; Petrarca stayed there in 1350. The church of San Giorgio, Baroque, preserves Romanesque elements including the apse.

  • Where Luzzara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.reggioemiliawelcome.it

Villa Paralupi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Fortified rural court, among the most famous residences of the Gonzaga, passed over the centuries to several owners: it has a private oratory and a garden with centuries-old trees and rare botanical specimens.

  • Where Maso, Luzzara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.reggioemiliawelcome.it

Museo del Truciolo

Museums · Themed

In the hamlet of Villarotta, it tells about the traditional wood processing in chips to make straw hats, a unique example of this craftsmanship in northern Italy.

  • Where Villarotta, Luzzara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.reggioemiliawelcome.it

Golena del Po ed ex Cava Luccio

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Po golena, the heart of the territory, with the houses often on stilts between poplars and birch trees, offers walks, birdwatching and views of the river; the former Cava Puccio is an environmental recovery area with educational trails and nature trails.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
