What to see in Buonconvento

Borgo delle Crete Senesi closed by the walls of 1379, with two doors, Senese and Romana, and two museums dedicated to sharecropping and sacred art.

4 places to visit in Buonconvento, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Museo d'Arte Sacra della Val d'Arbia

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Sienese masterpieces from the thirteenth to the seventeenth century collected by the churches of the Val d'Arbia.

  • Where Buonconvento
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo della Mezzadria Senese

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The museum dedicated to the peasant world of the Sienese countryside as it was until the seventies of the twentieth century.

  • Where Buonconvento
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church where, according to sources, Emperor Arrigo VII died in 1313 with a date of 1103 engraved on the façade; inside a Madonna with the Child of Matthew of John and a Coronation of Saint Peter.

  • Where Via Soccini, Buonconvento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Mura e Porta Senese

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The walls of 1379 that enclose the village, crossed by Via Socccini; the Porta Senese preserves the ancient doors, while the Porta Romana was destroyed in 1944.

  • Where Buonconvento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
