What to see in Bobbio

3 places to visit in Bobbio, in the province of Piacenza (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 4 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Abbazia di San Colombano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The monastery founded in 614 by the Irish monk Colombano, called the northern Montecassino, with the crypt, the saint's sarcophagus and the 12th century pavement mosaic.

  • Where Piazza San Fara, Bobbio
  • Entry To be checked: church; the three museums at extra charge
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Ponte Gobbo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The bridge over the Trebbia with 11 unequal arches, documented since 1196, called Devil's Bridge for the legend of Saint Colombano.

  • Where Ponte Gobbo, Bobbio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Castello Malaspina Dal Verme

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The 14th-century Malaspina castle that dominates the village, with the Marine Hall and the Celebration Hall.

  • Where Bobbio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
