What to see in Oristano

The ancient Aristanis, the capital of the judge of Arborea: the tower of Mariano of 1290, the Antiquarium Arborense with the finds of Tharros, the cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta, the Gothic of Santa Chiara and, on the sea, the largest Spanish tower in Sardinia.

10 places to visit in Oristano, in the province of Oristano (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre di Mariano (o di San Cristoforo)

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient northern gate of the city, built in 1290 with sandstone blocks recovered from the ancient Tharros; 28 meters high, now isolated in the center of Piazza Roma, is the stop of the Sartiglia.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Oristano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · sardegnacultura.it

Antiquarium Arborense

Museums · Archaeological

Born in 1938 from the collection of Efisio Pischedda, it has been based since 1992 in the neoclassical Palazzo Parpaglia and tells the history of the city and the territory, with the finds of Tharros.

  • Where Palazzo Parpaglia, Oristano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
