What to see in Gardone Val Trompia

The centre of the Trompia Valley is linked to the arms museum in Villa Mutti Bernardelli; among the places of art are the basilica and the fifteenth-century convent of Santa Maria degli Angeli, Palazzo Chinelli-Rampinelli, the parish church of San Giorgio and the panoramic sanctuaries, and paths to Punta Almana and Santa Maria del Giogo.

Museo delle armi e della tradizione armiera

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Collection of historical weapons, from white to firearms, from defence and hunting, chronologically ordered from the fifteenth century, with the history of families and local armoring tradition; a didactic section shows with videos and technical models and manufacturing processes. Free up to 13 years old and for residents of the municipalities of the Trompia Valley Museum System. Sunday only guided tours for groups of at least 25 people, booking 15 days before.

  • Where Via XX Settembre 31, Gardone Val Trompia
  • Entry €5, reduced €3 (13-18 years)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.gardonevaltrompia.bs.it

Sources: visitbrescia.it · cultura.gov.it · comune.gardonevaltrompia.bs.it

Basilica e convento di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry

Complex founded in the fifteenth century by Saint Bernardino da Siena, well preserved, with the porch of the church and the main cloister intact. The basilica has frescoes, including the Madonna of the Angels of 1502, and a copy of the polyptych of Moretto, whose original tables were removed in the Napoleonic age. The convent is now private, the basilica is open on request.

  • Where Via S. Francesco d'Assisi, Gardone Val Trompia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.gardonevaltrompia.bs.it

Sources: visitbrescia.it · comune.gardonevaltrompia.bs.it

Villa Mutti Bernardelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa of the fifteenth century, now a municipal cultural centre and seat of the Museum of Arms, with a large park, baroque salons, mozzoni lunettes and, in the private chapel on the ground floor, a bas-relief of the Nativity.

Chiesa di San Marco e Loggetta rinascimentale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The sixteenth-century parish church of San Marco, probably built on a previous chapel and expanded several times, preserves valuable paintings. The nearby Loggetta del Cinquecento, inspired by the Palazzo Comunale di Brescia, was the seat of the town hall and is now a parish museum. In the center also the Gothic House, medieval house-tower partly hidden from nearby buildings.

Sentiero Gardone - Punta Almana

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Trail 313 from the locality Piazzetto (via Guarda): passes through the fifteenth-century building of Anveno, Cascina Anveno (775 m) and Passata (960 m) to Punta Almana (1391 m), with iron cross and wide view on clear days. About 2.30 hours.

  • Where Località Piazzetto, via Guarda, Gardone Val Trompia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page comune.gardonevaltrompia.bs.it

Sources: comune.gardonevaltrompia.bs.it

Sentiero Gardone - Santa Maria del Giogo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Trail 311 from the middle school in Via Diaz: it passes through the chapel of the Santel de Pusole, Domaro (804 m, protected fossiliferous site) and Coca (875 m) to the small church of Santa Maria del Giogo, an ancient hospice for travelers and pilgrims overlooking Lake Iseo. About two hours.

  • Where Via Diaz, Gardone Val Trompia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page comune.gardonevaltrompia.bs.it

Sources: comune.gardonevaltrompia.bs.it
