What to see in Ercolano

The Roman city buried by the eruption of Vesuvius of 79 AD, less extensive than Pompeii but better preserved, with upper floors, woods and charred food: it is part of the UNESCO site of the archaeological areas of Pompeii, Herculaneum and Torre Annunziata.

Scavi di Ercolano

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Roman city buried in 79 A.D. by pyroclastic flows: are preserved upper floors, carbonized organic matter and everyday objects such as food and wood furniture; between the buildings the gym, two thermal complexes, the College of Augustals; along the ancient beach were found about 300 skeletons.

Villa dei Papiri

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage

One of the largest Roman villas explored, on terraces with a front of more than 250 meters, excavated between 1750 and 1764 for Charles III of Bourbon: it has returned about 90 sculptures and over 1,800 rolls of papyrus, mostly epicurean texts of Philodemus of Gadara.

Teatro antico

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage

The theater of the ancient Herculaneum, still under the modern city, among the buildings mentioned by UNESCO.
