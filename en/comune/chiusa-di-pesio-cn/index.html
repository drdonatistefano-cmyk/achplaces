What to see in Chiusa di Pesio

Borgo Bandiera Arancione della Valle Pesio, between centuries-old chestnuts and fir forests up to the Marguareis massif, with the great Certosa founded in 1173.

5 places to visit in Chiusa di Pesio, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Certosa di Pesio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

The splendid Carthusian of Santa Maria, founded in 1173: a late Renaissance complex very extensive, behind which the Marguareis Park opens to fan.

  • Where Certosa di Pesio, Chiusa di Pesio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · visitcuneese.it · italia.it

Parco naturale del Marguareis

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established in 1978, it protects 7,900 hectares of alpine environment in the high valleys Pesio and Tanaro, with numerous paths.

  • Where Chiusa di Pesio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · italia.it
