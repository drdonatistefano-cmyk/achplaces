What to see in Leffe

Borgo della Val Gandino has always been linked to the textile manufacture, which the Museum of the Textile "Martinelli Ginetto" tells with working machinery; in the village noble palaces from the sixteenth century, the church of San Michele with the Madonna Addolorata dei Fantoni and, on a panoramic hill, the church of San Martino.

Museo del Tessile «Martinelli Ginetto»

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Founded in 2005 by the volunteers of A.R.T.S. onlus, since 5 May 2013 it has been located in a former factory in the school complex Gianni Roots and is recognized by the Region as Museum Collection since 2014. More than 1,000 m2 of working machinery: one of the few silk torciotes in Italy, with parts designed by Leonardo, a hydraulic wheel of over three meters, looms from the Middle Ages to the mid-Twentieth century, card, gauze machine and follon, an embroidery machine Wuerker of 1926 and a working model of Somet frame, as well as a textile and dyeing garden. Guided tour only (about 60-80 minutes), usually Saturday and Sunday from 15 by reservation; closed in January, July, August and December.

  • Where Via Antonio Locatelli 29, Leffe
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodeltessile.it
