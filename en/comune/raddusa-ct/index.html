What to see in Raddusa

Country of the hinterland of Catania founded in 1810, whose first inhabitants lived in the nearby Fondaco delle Canne along the regia trazzera to Palermo; to see the Museum of the Wheat in the headquarters of the cooperative "La Cerere" , the House of Tea, the only museum of tea in Italy, the Mother Church of 1850 and, in the country, the eighteenth-century Tower of Feudo.

4 places to visit in Raddusa, in the province of Catania (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo del Grano

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In the building of the agricultural cooperative "La Cerere" was founded in 1910, managed by Pro Loco: the living part reconstructs a bedroom from the 1930s with a wash-house, a kitchen, a stone oven and a pantry with barrels and dams; the country side exhibits a Sicilian cart, a small field of wheat, historical agricultural tools such as a sower and a monument in spikes donated by the artisans of Foglianise in 1999; in the center three cases with local varieties of bread and wheat, panels on the cultivation of wheat and a projection room. Visits every day 9-13 and 15-19, by telephone reservation.

  • Where Via Tenente Sollima Giuseppe 41, Raddusa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.cittametropolitana.ct.it

Sources: cittametropolitana.ct.it

Casa del tè (Museo nazionale italiano del tè)

Museums · Themed

Cultural association dedicated to the cultivation, processing, promotion and enhancement of tea and its traditions, in an environment built according to the principles of Feng-shui. It presents itself as the only tea museum in Italy and has twice entered the Guinness of Records, for the collection of over 600 varieties of tea and for the largest teapot and teacup in the world; the Metropolitan City cites its tea room as one of the largest in Italy.

  • Where Raddusa (CT)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info · cmct.cittametropolitana.ct.it

Chiesa Madre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1850 on a project by the architect Giuseppe Maggiore, in the town. It is appreciated for its stuccoes and decorations in Corinthian style.

  • Where Centro abitato, Raddusa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cmct.cittametropolitana.ct.it

Sources: cmct.cittametropolitana.ct.it

Torre del Feudo

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Tower built around the eighteenth century on an outlying rock, with irregular octagonal plan, with still intact walls. It is located about 10 km from the center, at the junction of the Jumenta.

  • Where Bivio della Giumenta, circa 10 km da Raddusa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cmct.cittametropolitana.ct.it

Sources: cmct.cittametropolitana.ct.it
