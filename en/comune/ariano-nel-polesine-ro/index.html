What to see in Ariano nel Polesine

Ancient river centre on the flat and alluvial island that divides the Po into two branches, crossed in Roman times by Via Popilia: in San Basilio the Romanesque church and the archaeological museum renovated in 2023, between San Basilio and Grillara the fossil dunes of Sant'Ariano, in Grillara the workshop of the ocarines and, on the river, the village of Santa Maria in Punta.

Centro turistico culturale San Basilio

Museums · Archaeological

Archaeological museum opened in 1995 and re-arranged in 2023 with the support of the Cariparo Foundation, in a brick building next to the church of San Basilio. The chronological route tells of St. Basil as a center of exchanges from the Etruscan to the Roman age up to the early Middle Ages: the oldest finds are ceramics of a settlement of the VI-V century BC, sign of contacts between Paleovenets, Greeks and Etruscans; the main collection comes from the excavations of a nearby Roman villa, documented up to the fifth century A.D. (architectonic elements, skylights, pottery, blown glass, needles and pins in bone or horn), with a numismatic section dedicated to a treasure of coins. Multimedia supports and a covered courtyard for temporary exhibitions; collaborates with the Regional Park of the Po Delta.

Chiesa di San Basilio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Small Romanesque church of the IX-X century on a dune at the southern end of Sant'Ariano, near the embankment of Po di Goro, built by the Benedictines of the nearby Abbey of Pomposa on the site of a place of early Christian worship of the IV century. In the Middle Ages it was an important point of exchange; near passed the Via Popilia, the Roman coastal road between Adria and Ravenna also crossed by pilgrims headed for Rome.

  • Where San Basilio, Ariano nel Polesine
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcodeltapo.org

Dune fossili di Sant'Ariano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Sand cordons that mark an ancient line of coast: from the Etruscans to today the Po, advancing in the Adriatic, has left seven lines of coast, each with its dunes, largely destroyed by erosion, agriculture and sand extraction; those remaining are protected. You can see the phases from the sandy dune to the gray dune with junipers, holm oaks and sea pine trees, and nesting there beggars and marine acorns; old roads on the dunes, used in the Roman and Middle Ages, report towards the Po di Goro.

  • Where Tra San Basilio e Grillara, Ariano nel Polesine
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcodeltapo.org

Museo laboratorio L'Ocarina di Grillara

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Educational farm and museum dedicated to the ocarines and whistles in terracotta, the result of a trade that the family has been carrying out for about a hundred years; it attracts hundreds of visitors every year, who with guided tour can follow the phases of clay processing and participate in the construction of an ocarina.

  • Where Via Bologna 16, Grillara, Ariano nel Polesine
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.arianonelpolesine.ro.it

Santa Maria in Punta

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

River village about 5 km from the capital, in the golena of the Po, where the Po di Goro, the oldest branch, is detached, born from the Taglio di Porto Viro Venetian of the early sixteenth century: you can visit the church of Santa Maria del Traghetto and the oratory of Sant'Antonio. Around, the remains of the old village and abandoned farmsteads, trails along the ancient coastal dunes, reeds and river birds (colombacci, ducks, herons) and a river beach that changes shape with currents; recommended the bicycle, because few cars pass.

  • Where Santa Maria in Punta, Ariano nel Polesine
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcodeltapo.org
