What to see in Marineo

The village is about 550 meters above the Eleuterio valley, about 30 km from Palermo, dominated by the great rock of the Fortress; the main monument is the Castle Beccadelli Bologna of 1559, home of the Archaeological Museum of the Eleuterio Valley, with the finds of the ancient Makella dug on the Montagnola. The road through the village would be built by the Romans in the second half of the third century BC.

Castello Beccadelli Bologna e Museo archeologico della Valle dell'Eleuterio

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

16th century castle of the Marches Beccadelli Bologna: a stone at the entrance of the noble floor indicates 1559 as a year of construction; acquired by the Sicilian Region, it houses the museum with the finds of Makella, the ancient indigenous city of Montagnola, and settlements along the Eleuterio, linking the Tyrrhenian coast and the interior. Among the pieces on display are a fourth century B.C. syceliot crater, a bowl and an archaic tomb kit, Greek jugs and amphorae, Roman finds, Arab-Norman basins and medieval glazed lamps. From the tower you can see the gorges of the Strait dug by Eleuterio. Open every day 9-19.30 (ticket office up to one hour before), Sunday and holidays 9-14.

Area archeologica della Montagnola (Makella)

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

To the north-west of the country, tiles registered in Greek have identified the site with Makella, also known by the Decree of Entella. The late-archaic walls, reinforced in Hellenistic times on the south-east side, defended the town, destroyed in the middle of the third century BC during the first Punic war and partly re-used by the Norman age; they remain a public building of the fourth century BC on terraces connected by two stairs and a large cistern turned into barrels, partly dug into the rock. At the walls was found a votive deposit of the late VI century BC with bronze skippers and helmets. For the visit we ask the museum's care staff.

La Rocca

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Large cliff that dominates the town, also called "Dente canino della Sicilia" or "Tomba di Polifemo" . At its feet was probably a necropolis of the late VI-early 5th century B.C., today covered by buildings.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa Matrice e Santuario della Madonna della Dajna

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The tourist portal of the Metropolitan City indicates the Matrix Church, the sanctuary of Our Lady of the Dajna and ten other churches of the village. Among the works mentioned: a mosaic of St. Cyrus in polychrome majolica of the eighteenth century attributed to the Palermo school, a saintly waterworks in white marble of the fourteenth century and the picture of the Madonna of the Tenerance of the seventeenth century.
