What to see in Cantoira

Small Franco-Provençal municipality of the Lanzo Valleys, between 750 m of Boschietto and 2,345 m of Monte Bellavarda, with the sanctuary of Santa Cristina on the rocky spur above the village and the ancient mine of talc Brunetta.

3 places to visit in Cantoira, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo di Archeologia Industriale Antica Miniera Brunetta

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Molco mine at 1,580 m in the valley of Brissout, active from the early twentieth century to 1979, set up and managed by CAI di Lanzo. The galleries, about 3 km on two levels, are preserved as during the activity and are only visited with a guide by reservation; the external mining park (miner's room, electric locomotive shed, engine room) is always open and freely accessible. You can only walk from the village Vrù, in about an hour-one-and-a-half; not accessible in a wheelchair.

  • Where Località Brunetta, Cantoira (a piedi da Vrù)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Santuario di Santa Cristina

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

It rises at about 1,350 m on top of a cliff, at the end of the ridge between Val Grande and Val d'Ala, visible from Cantoira and the heights of Ceres. A pylon dedicated to the saint is considered before 1440; the pastoral visit of 1769 says it arose after an apparition to a pastor and recently expanded, with a frescoed apsidal vault; in 1840 there were added shelters and walls that give the complex the appearance of a fortress. Three paths (from Cantoira to Casa Goffo, from Trambié bridge, from Brachiello to Ceres) end with a stone staircase. Tradition requires the rock of Ceres, church and cantoira festival.

  • Where Rupe di Santa Cristina, sopra Cantoira (sentiero da Casa Goffo)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.cantoira.to.it · turismotorino.org

Parrocchiale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo e Oratorio di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church, at the top of a staircase, was built in the early eighteenth century at the behest of Antonio Genta; the interior decorations are from 1875 and the ceiling is made of wooden boards. The Oratory of Santa Croce, the ancient parish church of the 11th century, is attached to it, which preserves on the façade some hanging arches, with a sixteenth-century bell tower in Lombard style.

  • Where Via della Chiesa 40, Cantoira
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.cantoira.to.it · turismotorino.org
