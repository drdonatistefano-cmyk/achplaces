What to see in Loreto Aprutino

Borgo on a hill on the left of the Tavo river, crossed by via del Baio that joins the Chiola Castle to the abbey church of San Pietro, with doors four-fifth-century and the Fonte Grande of 1838. Outside the center, the church of Santa Maria in Piano preserves a Judgment of the late 1920s, one of the most important paintings in Abruzzo.

Chiesa di Santa Maria in Piano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Documented from the 12th century, rebuilt in 1280 with the union of the Cistercian monastery of Casanova and modified in the mid-500s by the abbot Giovanni Battista Umbriani of Capua (apse octagona, portico, raised bell tower); single nave with transversal arches of Cistercian type and golden altar with polychrome statues of the 17th century. The walls are covered by cycles of fifteenth-century frescoes; in contrast to the Judgment of the late 1920s of the fifteenth century, with the "bridge of the hair" that souls cross to enter Paradise, is among the most important paintings of Abruzzo.

closed to visitors and faithful after the earthquake 2016-2017; restoration work started in June 2023 with an expected end in June 2024 (SABAP), reopening not confirmed in the reading sources

Centro storico, porte e Fonte Grande

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village rises on a hill on the left of the Tavo, with via del Baio connecting the castle to the mother church and the Gardens of Palazzo Antico supported by the ancient walls. Of the belt there are the Mordecai gate with an ogival arch and the Hospital gate (XV-XVI century) and the nineteenth-century Porta Castello and Porta Palamolla, near which are walled two stone balls attributed by tradition to the cannons of Iacopo Piccinino (1460); outside door the monumental Fonte Grande of 1838, next to the ruins of a source of the late sixteenth century.

  • Where Via del Baio, Loreto Aprutino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.loretoaprutino.pe.it

Sources: comune.loretoaprutino.pe.it

Castello Chiola

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Comital castle with stone cores among the oldest in the country and parts of the XV-XVI century, rebuilt in neoclassical style by architect Francesco Valentini in the second half of the nineteenth century.

Chiesa abbaziale di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Mother church in the center of the village, built in the fifteenth century and enlarged in the sixteenth and eighteenth. It preserves the silver bust of San Zopito (1753-1759), attributed to Giuseppe Sanmartino or to a close collaborator and probably merged by the goldsmith Filippo del Giudice.

  • Where Via del Baio, Loreto Aprutino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.loretoaprutino.pe.it

Sources: comune.loretoaprutino.pe.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It is of fourteenth-century origin, with inside rebuilt in 1729 and stuccoes by Girolamo Rizza; it preserves a wooden choir, an organ attributed to Adriano Fedri and a wooden crucifix of the fifteenth-sixteenth century.
