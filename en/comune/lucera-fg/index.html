What to see in Lucera

City Swabian on the Table, stop of the Via Francigena: the Swabian-Ange fortress, the Gothic cathedral, the Augustan amphitheatre from 16-18 thousand spectators and the Civic Museum Fiorelli in the center enclosed by the walls.

6 places to visit in Lucera, in the province of Foggia (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Fortezza svevo-angioina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Military complex of the 13th century, of the federician and angioin age: it is the monument that best tells the past of Lucera. The palace inside had no doors and housed one of the ticks of the kingdom; some underground passages have not yet been completely discovered. The MiC has allocated 3 million euros for restoration and enhancement.

  • Where Lucera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · programmazionestrategica.cultura.gov.it

Anfiteatro augusteo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Built between 27 B.C. and 14 A.D. on the eastern edge of the ancient city, gift of the magistrate M. Vecilius Campus; it measures about 130 by 95 meters and hosted 16-18 thousand spectators, with arena, podium and four entrances. Ticket office from Tuesday to Sunday 9-16.30.

MiC card indicates it temporarily closed

  • Where Viale Augusto 93, Lucera
  • Entry €3, reduced €1-2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo civico "Giuseppe Fiorelli"

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Since 1936 in the eighteenth-century palace De Nicastri-Cavalli, in the historic center; dedicated to archaeology and the history of the city, with objects often exposed without cases, specialized library and workshop for restoration of the finds. Closed on Mondays; audioguide available.

  • Where Via Famiglia De Nicastri, Lucera
  • Entry €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo diocesano del Palazzo Vescovile

Museums · Old masters

Museum of Sacred Art of the Diocese of Lucera-Troia in the noble wing of the eighteenth-century Bishop's Palace: the historical bishop's gallery with portraits desired by Mons. Alfonso Maria Freda (1798-1816), relics, relics and much of the Treasury of the Cathedral Chapter.

  • Where Piazza Duomo 13, Lucera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Cattedrale

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Gothic cathedral that, with the Swabian-Ange fortress, testifies to the medieval past of the city.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
