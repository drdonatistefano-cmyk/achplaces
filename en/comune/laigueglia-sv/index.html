What to see in Laigueglia

Marinar village among the most beautiful villages in Italy, grown with coral fishing: squares on the beach, the parish church of San Matteo with two majolica bell towers, the Saracen tower and Capo Mele with the Madonna delle Penne.

8 places to visit in Laigueglia, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Matteo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Started in 1715 at the peak of coral fishing, transformed between 1754 and 1783 and restored after the earthquake of 1887; two bell towers with majolica domes, an Assunta by Bernardo Strozzi and an Ecce Homo by Antonio Margliano.

  • Where Piazza San Matteo, Laigueglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Oratorio di Santa Maria Maddalena

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Oratory of the Disciplinanti of 1616-34, with marble altar of Dionisio Corte (1673), altarpiece of Domenico Piola (1676) and a seventeenth-century crucifix with silver ornaments.

  • Where Laigueglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Capo Mele e Madonna delle Penne

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Promontory with the lighthouse, the point of the mainland closest to Corsica; on the rocks the seventeenth-century chapel of Catalan corals (Ostra Señora de la Peña), reachable by a staircase from Via Aurelia, where on August 5 is celebrated the feast of sailors and fishermen.

  • Where Via Aurelia, Laigueglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it
