What to see in Moconesi

The municipality of the upper Val Fontanabuona along the Lavagna stream; in the hamlet of Terrarossa, renamed Terrarossa Colombo, lived the grandfather and great-grandfather of Christopher Columbus, the woollings, and from here starts the Colombian itinerary of 19 km to Genoa Quinto. In Gattorna, on a hill, stands the sanctuary of Our Lady of the Guard.

3 places to visit in Moconesi, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo di Colombo a Terrarossa

Museums · City history

In an ancient rustic restored at the beginning of the terraced houses where the grandparents of Columbus lived, he exhibits thirty lithographs on the life of the navigator, a wooden model of the Santa Maria Caravella, books, copies of documents of the State Archives and the family tree, with a didactic route for schools. Terrarossa is the starting point of the historic Colombian route of 19 km towards Genoa Quinto, the same route as the ancestors of the navigator; in the hamlet there is also the small chapel of St. Joseph. Tel. 0185 939001.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Santuario di Nostra Signora della Guardia a Gattorna

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

The parish of Gattorna, named after St. James the Apostle the Major, is the seat of the sanctuary of Our Lady of the Guard, built in 1712 on a hill overlooking the country and always symbol and landmark of the community. Accessible to the disabled, with parking.

  • Where Piazza N.S. della Guardia 4, Gattorna, Moconesi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it

Sources: fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it · diocesichiavari.it

Chiesa del Sacro Cuore e di Santa Margherita a Ferrada

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Opened to worship on 12 September 1909 in place of the ancient parish of Moconesi and consecrated on 28 February 1916 by Monsignor Giovanni Gamberoni, of the diocese of Chiavari; the parish also included Moconesi Alto, which broke off on 1 November 1929.

  • Where Frazione Ferrada, Moconesi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it

Sources: fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it
