What to see in Macerata Feltria

Borgo del Montefeltro gathered around the castle of Lombard origin, dominated by the Civic Tower and the thirteenth-century Palazzo del Podestà with the Civic Museum; below the churches of San Francesco and San Michele Arcangelo, the Museum of the Radio of epoch, the Terme Pitinum and, towards Carpegna, the Romanesque parish church of San Cassiano and the excavations of the ancient Pitinum.

9 places to visit in Macerata Feltria, in the province of Pesaro e Urbino (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello e Torre Civica

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The oldest and fortified nucleus of the village, of Lombard age, rises on the hill and is dominated by the Civic Tower; built between the 11th and 14th century with pyramidal plan, it has at the southern entrance the Arch of the Pelasgi. In the castle also the sixteenth century Evangelist Palace, with cycles of civil frescoes of the sixteenth century.

  • Where Via dei Toschi 7, Macerata Feltria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: comune.maceratafeltria.pu.it · letsmarche.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Palazzo del Podestà e Museo civico archeologico e paleontologico

Museums · Archaeological

Next to the Civic Tower, the 13th-XIV century palace houses the Civic Museum with archaeological and paleontological finds of Montefeltro, in particular from the excavations of the ancient city of Pitinum Pisaurense; the paleontological section is set up in the civic tower.

  • Where Via dei Toschi, Macerata Feltria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di San Giuseppe al Castello

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the XIV-XV century in the area of the Castle, with terracotta portal, a golden wooden tabernacle and a painting of the Madonna del Rosario of the late sixteenth century.

Chiesa di San Francesco e Museo della radio d'epoca

Museums · Themed

The church, built in the fourteenth century and renovated in the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, has a Gothic portal and fifteenth-century frescoes; in the former convent is set up the Museum of the radio of epoch, according to the regional portal the second public museum of this type in Italy.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di Santa Chiara e Museo di archeologia industriale

Museums · Themed

The church was part of a convent founded at the end of the sixteenth century; in the basement it houses the Museum of Industrial Archaeology, with working equipment. In the village also the sixteenth-century Mulino delle Nunache, belonged to the Clarisses, and the great nineteenth-century Filanda connected to Palazzo Gentili Belli.

Pieve di San Cassiano e scavi di Pitinum

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Along the road to Carpegna, the 11th century Romanesque parish church preserves a marble statue of San Sebastiano from the 15th century; excavations conducted between 1990 and 1997 brought to light a medieval necropolis and a Roman area of republican age, with remains of an ancient Roman road.

  • Where San Cassiano in Pitino, strada per Carpegna, Macerata Feltria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · letsmarche.it

Teatro Angelo Battelli e Teatro dei Condòmini

History and architecture · Historic theatres

The Teatro Angelo Battelli, from the 1930s, has been completely restored and is still in operation; of the eighteenth century Teatro dei Condòmini remains the facade of the original building. Nearby is also the eighteenth-century Palazzo Antimi Clari with the adjoining chapel.
