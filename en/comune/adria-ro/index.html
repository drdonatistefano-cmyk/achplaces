What to see in Adria

Founded at the beginning of the 6th century BC as a Greek-Etruscan fluvial port, the city that gave its name to the Adriatic is today the gateway to the Po Delta Park: the National Archaeological Museum with the Tomb of the Biga and the Roman glass, the cathedral with the 9th century crypt and the new diocesan museum, the small center crossed by the White Canal and, in the countryside, the museum of reclamation in the Amolara hydrovora.

5 places to visit in Adria, in the province of Rovigo (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo archeologico nazionale di Adria

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In a large tree-lined park, it tells the city from the Bronze Age to the end of the Roman era, starting from the collection of the Bocchi family, which collected more than 6,000 artifacts between the Seven and the nineteenth century. Among the strong pieces the Tomb of the Biga of the III century B.C., with chariot and three horses recovered with its excavation context in 1938, attic ceramics with black and red figures, Etruscan ores, ambre, the milestone of Popilio Lenate on the Via Popillia and the famous showcase of Roman glass, with blown cups in the mould of the Syro-Palestinian glassmaker Ennione (I century A.D.). Closed on Mondays; in the garden an archaeological theme playground.

  • Where Via Badini 59, Adria
  • Entry €6, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website museoadria.cultura.gov.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: museoadria.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Cattedrale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo e Museo diocesano

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Italia.it recommends to go down into the crypt, decorated with fine Byzantine frescoes: it is an apse chapel of the ninth century from which starts the path of the Diocesan Museum, opened in March 2025 in the spaces between the ancient cathedral of St. John and the new of Saints Peter and Paul. Eight sections on about 500 square meters with paintings, shovels, silvers, vestments and sculptures, including fifteenth-century fragments in painted and golden terracotta by Michele from Florence. Museum open Friday afternoon, Saturday and Sunday.

  • Where Piazzetta Campanile, Adria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · smppolesine.provincia.rovigo.it

Basilica di Santa Maria Assunta della Tomba

Places of worship · Basilicas

Also known as the Basilica of the Tomb, there is buried the illustrious Roman Quintus of Sertoriano.

Centro storico e Canal Bianco

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The small old town, which deserves a walk, is crossed by the Canal Bianco, which connects the city to the Po and the sea; in the heart of the city the Castello Bridge climbs over the canal.

  • Where Ponte Castello, Adria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · npt-cms.veneto.eu

Sèptem Mària Museum

Museums · Themed

Opened on November 21, 1998 in the engine room of the nineteenth-century hydrovora of Amolara, on the outskirts of Adria, in the area of one of the richest Etruscan necropolis beyond Po: it tells the management of the waters in Polesine from prehistory to today and preserves the two centrifugal pumps that pushed towards the sea the excess waters of the Lower Old Country. The name comes from an ancient incision at the entrance. Next to it, the Hostel Amolara organizes excursions by bike and on foot.

different times between sources (SMPP: Mar-Fri morning and Sunday afternoon; ISPRA: every day 9-19 by reservation), house 1 or 11

  • Where Via Capitello 1, località Amolara, Adria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
