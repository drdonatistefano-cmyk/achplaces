What to see in Ledro

Municipality of the valley that connects Lake Idro to Garda, around Lake Ledro at 655 meters: on the shore of Molina the village of the Bronze Age, since 2011 UNESCO World Heritage among the alpine arch palafittic sites, in Bezzecca the Garibaldi Museum and the Colle-Ossario of Santo Stefano, in the pine forest of Pur the Ledro Land Art.

11 places to visit in Ledro, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo delle Palafitte e villaggio palafitticolo

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Museum of the MUSE built around the remains of a village of the Bronze Age, inhabited between 2200 and 1350 BC and re-emerged in 1929 during the lowering of the lake for hydroelectric works; since 2011 it is part of the prehistoric palafittic sites of the Alpine arch, UNESCO World Heritage. Original finds and, on the shore, huts rebuilt and furnished on catwalks. Open every day: March-June and September-December 9-17, July and August 10-18; family ticket €5 or €10.

  • Where Via al Lago 1, Molina di Ledro
  • Entry €5, reduced €4, free under 14
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: muse.it · visittrentino.info · touringclub.it

Lago di Ledro e spiagge

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Alpine lake at 655 meters with about 10 km of shore, water from green to turquoise that in summer reaches 20-24 °C. Four free beaches: Pieve (pedalò, minigolf, games), Molina-località Besta (beach volleyball, tennis, canoe), Mezzolago (families friendly) and Pur on the south bank ( dog track, wakeboard). A 9-10 km foot and cycle ring follows almost the entire shore (2-3 hours on foot).

  • Where Pieve, Molina, Mezzolago, Pur, Ledro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Museo garibaldino e della Grande Guerra

Museums · City history

In the former wash-house of Bezzecca, it recalls the battle of 1866, fought here by the Garibaldini against the Austrians in the Third War of Independence (that of the "Obedisco"). On the ground floor of the battle, the bust of Garibaldi, documents, rifles and uniforms; on the first floor heirlooms of the two world wars, including a machine gun Schwarzlose and a white folding bicycle of cyclist targets. Open from 1st June to 30th September, 10-18, closed Monday and Tuesday.

  • Where Via Salvator Greco 14, Bezzecca, Ledro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: cultura.trentino.it · visittrentino.info

Colle di Santo Stefano e ossario

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

After the Great War, the church on the hill welcomed the fallen of 1866 and the First World War, becoming a monument to the Ossuary; around the historical park with Italian walks and trenches, which can be walked only on foot, and a path to the Cross. From the parking lot about 138 meters of dirt; ossuary open from May 1 to September 30, 10-13 and 15-18, closed on Monday. Every July 21st commemoration of the battle of 1866.

  • Where Colle di Santo Stefano, Bezzecca, Ledro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: cultura.trentino.it · touringclub.it · visittrentino.info

Laboratorio e Museo Foletto

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Laboratory behind the family pharmacy, active since 1900 as a small pharmaceutical workshop: injectable vials, fruit syrups and registered medicinal products. It exhibits alembics, pestles, mortars and antique instruments; pharmacists Foletto still produce syrups and liqueurs, including Picco Rosso, with tastings. Out of season only by appointment; free entry with the Garda Guest Card.

  • Where Via Vittoria 11, Pieve di Ledro
  • Entry €5, reduced €4, family €10
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: gardatrentino.it · touringclub.it · visittrentino.info

Ledro Land Art

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Outdoor art path started in 2012 by the Municipality in the pine forest of Pur, along the stream Assat towards Malga Cita: sculptures and installations in stone, iron and wood (trees, stone cows, giant violins, a swing forest), enriched every summer by site-specific works of invited artists.

  • Where Pur, Ledro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: touringclub.it · visittrentino.info

Cascata del Gorg d'Abiss

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Hidden waterfall at the bottom of a canyon excavated by the Massangla stream near Tiarno di Sotto: an easy walk of about 20 minutes from the old mill, in the woods among several waterfalls.

  • Where Tiarno di Sotto, Ledro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Madonnina di Besta

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

High above Molina with the statue of the Madonna and view of the lake, reachable on foot starting from behind the Museo delle Palafitte.

  • Where Molina di Ledro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Biotopo del Lago d'Ampola

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Protected area with reeds and water lilies and nature trail, equipped with visitor center; it is touched by the MTB tour of the three lakes (about 25 km) that from Garda passes through Lake Ledro.

  • Where Lago d'Ampola, Ledro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · touringclub.it

Strada del Ponale

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Former road dug into the rock, with tunnels and parapets, today reserved for pedestrians and cyclists: connects the valley of Ledro with Pregasina and Riva del Garda.

  • Where Strada del Ponale, Ledro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Alpi Ledrensi e Judicaria

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Area between Garda and the Brenta Dolomites declared Biosphere Reserve in 2015, with alpine meadows, woods and moors; from the valley start trekking towards Monte Corno, Tremalzo, Cima d'Oro, Cima Parì and Val Concei, and the Ledro Trek with two rings (the Low measures 19 km).

  • Where Valle di Ledro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info
