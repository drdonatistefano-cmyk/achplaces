Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Medieval village at about 342 meters in the Peligna basin, at the foot of the western slope of Mount Morrone. The name "Pratola" first appeared in 997 in a agricultural contract of the Chronicon Volturnense; fief of Charles II of Anjou, in 1294 was assigned to the abbot of the monastery of Holy Spirit al Morrone, and since then his history is linked to Celestine V and his monks. In the territory there are still local beans (bread and ... Gentile) and red garlic of Sulmona.
