What to see in Mineo

The birthplace of the verista writer Luigi Capuana, to which are dedicated the museum house and monument in Piazza Buglio; in the territory the archaeological area of Palikè, home of the most important sanctuary of the Siculi.

5 places to visit in Mineo, in the province of Catania (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Area archeologica di Palikè

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The seat of the most important sanctuary of the Siculi, dedicated to the Palici brothers, the sons of Zeus and the nymph Talia, excavated since 1995 and opened to the public since 2006. In front of the cave at the foot of the hill of Rocchicella remain the structures of the sanctuary, with a terrace accommodation of the fifth century BC with a building for banquets and two arcades; on the top the remains of a city of the fourth century BC. Open from Monday to Friday 8.30-13.30.

  • Where Strada Provinciale 181, Contrada Rocchicella, Mineo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.comune.mineo.ct.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · comune.mineo.ct.it

Casa museo Luigi Capuana

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

A nineteenth-century noble house in Sicilian neoclassical style, already home to the writer Luigi Capuana, father of Verismo with Verga and De Roberto: the alcove, the studio and the library preserve vintage furnishings, newspaper clippings, prints and youthful photos, two etchings by Telemaco Signorini with the old Florence, a caricature by Ugo Fleres and 1,300 ancient volumes by the library of the Cappuccini.

  • Where Via Romano 16, Mineo
  • Entry €3, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.mineo.ct.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitsicily.info · comune.mineo.ct.it

Piazza Buglio e monumento a Luigi Capuana

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Central square dedicated to the Jesuit missionary in China Ludovico Buglio; in the center the monument to Capuana di Vincenzo Torre da Nicolosi (made in 1934, inaugurated in 1936), in bronze on a travertine basis, with bas-reliefs inspired by Jacinta, Bona gente and Il marquise di Roccaverdina. The eighteenth-century Palazzo Tamburino overlooks it, with a staircase with doric stone columns.

  • Where Piazza Ludovico Buglio, Mineo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.mineo.ct.it

Sources: comune.mineo.ct.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria d'Odigidria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of Byzantine origin was built where in the Middle Ages it was the gate of Santa Maria degli Angeli, next to the former monastery of the Observing Fathers of 1620. It houses three marble sculptures (two of Saint Anne with the Madonna of the Angels and one Madonna of the Help), altarpieces and five altars.

  • Where Via Quattro Canti, Mineo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.mineo.ct.it

Sources: comune.mineo.ct.it

Museo etnoantropologico

Museums · Themed

Opened in December 2005 in the former convent of the Minor Fathers, it exhibits mainly objects of rural culture with captions in Italian and dialect, and a large section dedicated to proverbs.

  • Where Via Carlo Alberto, Mineo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.mineo.ct.it

Sources: comune.mineo.ct.it
