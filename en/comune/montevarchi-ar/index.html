What to see in Montevarchi

Centre of the Arezzo Valley gathered around Piazza Varchi, with the Collegiate Church of San Lorenzo and its Museum of sacred art with the temple of Andrea della Robbia, the Palaeontological Museum of the Accademia Valdarnese del Poggio and the Cassero for sculpture.

7 places to visit in Montevarchi, in the province of Arezzo (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza Varchi e Palazzo del Podestà

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The heart of the city, where the Collegiate and Palazzo del Podestà (or Pretorio) overlook, with the coats of arms on the facade, now the town hall and the venue of exhibitions; there you can play the Game of the Well during the Feasts of Forgiveness, the first weekend of September.

  • Where Piazza Varchi, Montevarchi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Collegiata di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built between the 13th and 14th centuries and decorated in Baroque times on a project by Massimiliano Soldani Benzi; there is the relic of the milk of the Virgin donated by Count Guido Guerra.

  • Where Piazza Varchi, Montevarchi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo d'arte sacra della Collegiata di San Lorenzo

Museums · Old masters

In the former sacristy, the Robbian temple of the late fifteenth century rebuilt as it was in the church, the relief of the Della Robbia with the delivery of the relic, processional crosses of the sixteenth century (one by Piero di Martino Spigliati, one attributed to the workshop of Benvenuto Cellini), the seventeenth-century reliquary of the Holy Milk and illuminated codes.

  • Where Via Isidoro del Lungo 4, Montevarchi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · cultura.gov.it

Museo paleontologico

Museums · Science and university

The Accademia Valdarnese del Poggio, in the Franciscan monastery of San Lodovico, exhibits thousands of fossils of the Upper Valdarno of the Pliocene and Pleistocene: the Mammuthus sudridis, giant hyenas, jaguars and the skull of Canis etruscus, the oldest known testimony of the species; an archaeological section with Etruscan and Roman finds.

  • Where Via Poggio Bracciolini 36/40, Montevarchi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Il Cassero per la scultura

Museums · Modern art

In the 14th century Cassero, a tower on three levels of the walls wanted by the Florentines in 1328, the municipal museum of Italian sculpture of the nineteenth and twentieth centuries collects from donations more than 2,500 works among bronze, marble, plasters, terracotta and drawings, with artists such as Temo Bortolotti, Arturo Stagliano, Valmore Gemignani and the local Ernesto Galeffi.

  • Where Via Trieste, Montevarchi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · cultura.gov.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria al Giglio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It preserves the Madonna del Ponte, the fresco above the altar to which miraculous apparitions have been attributed over the centuries.

  • Where Montevarchi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Colle dei Cappuccini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill remain the remains of the 11th century castle, erected by the Bourbon marquises of Monte Santa Maria to protect a hospital for pilgrims, which would give the city its name; where the monastery of Sant'Angelo stood there today is the church of Santa Croce.

  • Where Colle dei Cappuccini, Montevarchi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
