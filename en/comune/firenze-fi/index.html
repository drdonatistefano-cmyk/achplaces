What to see in Florence

The cradle of the Renaissance, UNESCO's historic heritage center since 1982. With the Firenze Card you enter almost all the museums of the city and the Metropolitan City.

59 places to visit in Florence, in the province of Florence (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.
