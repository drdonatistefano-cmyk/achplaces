What to see in Irgoli

Country of Baronia in the valley of Cedrino, with murals and an international festival of the organnetto since 1986; on Mount Senes the nuragic sanctuary of Janna 'e Pruna and the sacred source of Su Notante, whose findings are in the Antiquarium municipale, and a part of Mount Albo.

Complesso nuragico di Janna 'e Pruna e fonte sacra di Su Notante

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Sacred area linked to the cult of the waters on the granite ridge of Mount Senes, about 600 meters overlooking Gennargentu and Supramonte. The temple, in granite blocks well squared and dated to the Bronze final-first Iron (XII-IX century BC), has a rectangular atrium with seat and a circular cell with fireplace, inside a trapezoid temenos where the offerings were laid. The source of Su Notante (XII-X century B.C.) is built in squared basalt, non-local stone brought to the place, and the water gushes still. You can reach from Irgoli following for about 12 km the road to Mount Senes.

  • Where Monte Senes, Irgoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.sardegnaturismo.it

Antiquarium comunale

Museums · Archaeological

In the former town hall, on two floors, Irgoli tells from Neolithic to the Middle Ages with almost all finds of the territory, especially from the shrine of Janna 'e Pruna and Su Notante: axes, millstones, melting pots, ceramics, a pintadera to decorate the bread, milk pots, necklaces in glass paste, broochs and bronze bracelets, votive offerings, betiles and a model of nuraghe in basalt. Workshops for children with excavation simulation.

  • Where Ex municipio, Irgoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Monte Albo e bosco di Talachè

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Part of Monte Albo belongs to Irgoli: paths through the woods towards the white peak of sos Aspros, habitat of muflon and royal eagle, along the path the pinnettos, circular huts of the shepherds in stone and wood. The wood of ancient oaks of Talache is recommended.

  • Where Monte Albo, Irgoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Paese dei murales

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The streets of the village are decorated with murals on rural life and on the passion for music; since 1986 the international organ festival is held every August and about 120 mantice instruments are exhibited all year round.
