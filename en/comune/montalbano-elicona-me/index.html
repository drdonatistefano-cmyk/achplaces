What to see in Montalbano Elicona

Medieval village of the Nebrodi dominated by the Swabian-Aragonese castle that Frederick II of Aragon transformed into a royal residence, with the Mother Church and the Baroque portals of via Mastropaolo; in the territory the reserve of the Bosco di Malabotta and the megaliths of Argimusco.

Castello e Cappella Reale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It rises on Byzantine and Arabic structures: rectangular fortress between a square tower and a Swabian pentagonal mast, with Swabian walls considered the best preserved defensive configuration of Sicily and the date 1270 engraved on the cistern. Frederick II of Aragon transformed it into a royal summer residence by opening eighteen large windows; the Byzantine royal chapel would preserve, according to some scholars, the remains of Arnaldo da Villanova. Restored in the eighties, it is municipal and hosts exhibitions and conferences.

Chiesa Madre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Of medieval origin, enlarged in 1654 with the side naves and the bell tower; it houses a sculpture of St. Nicholas and a marble ciborium attributed to the school of the Gagini, a wooden crucifix of the fifteenth century, a Last Supper attributed to the workshop of Guido Reni and sacred furnishings.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Centro storico: Santa Caterina, Spirito Santo e via Mastropaolo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

In the medieval village the Church of Santa Caterina has a Romanesque portal and a marble statue of the saint; the fourteenth-century Church of the Holy Spirit is located at Porta Giovan Guerino. In via Mastropaolo you can see the Baroque portals of Casa Messina-Ballarino, carved in the seventeenth century by Isardi from Naples, and Casa Mastropaolo; among the points of interest are the fountain of Gattuso, "u roggiu" (a fountain of Gattuso).

Bosco di Malabotta

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Reserve with beech, maple, holm oak, willow and chestnut trees and an oak that can reach 40 meters; the Path of the Patriarchs is known for the scallops of secular oaks. Walks from 5 to 15 km, with panoramic views of Etna and the Alcantara Valley.

  • Where Bosco di Malabotta, Montalbano Elicona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Megaliti dell'Argimusco

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Group of large quartz sandstone rocks with singular shapes, over 1,000 meters, on the northern edge of the Bosco di Malabotta between Montalbano Elicona, Novara di Sicilia and Tripi; called the Sicilian Stonehenge, perhaps a place of rites or astronomical observation or simply shaped by the wind. An easy path passes through the stones, overlooking the Tyrrhenian Sea and the Alcantara valley.

  • Where Argimusco, Montalbano Elicona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it
