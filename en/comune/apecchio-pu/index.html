What to see in Apecchio

Borgo dell'Appennino Marche at the foot of Monte Nerone, with the fifteenth-century bridge, the Palazzo Ubaldini by Francesco di Giorgio Martini which houses the smallest theatre of the Marche and, in the underground, the Museum of fossils and minerals, and the ancient Jewish quarter.

Museo dei fossili e minerali del Monte Nerone

Museums · Science and university

Born in 1977 in the basement of Palazzo Ubaldini (former stables, prisons and snowfields), it exhibits over 2,000 finds: mainly fossils of Mount Nerone of about 200 million years ago such as the ammonites, tools in silce, remains of the cave bear and of prehistoric elephants and minerals from various parts of Italy; a section on dinosaurs with skulls, eggs and claws from Mongolia and the United States and a room on human evolution. LIS video guides, interactive panels and audio content.

discordant times: Italia.it indicates open every day 10:30-12:30 and 16-19, the MiC card indicates closed every day

Palazzo Ubaldini e Teatro G. Perugini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Renaissance Palace of the XV century designed by Francesco di Giorgio Martini; inside the Teatro G. Perugini, called the smallest theatre in the Marche region.

  • Where Via XX Settembre, Apecchio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Ponte medievale e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The route in the village starts from the medieval bridge of the fifteenth century; next to it is the fourteenth-century arch of the bell tower, and in the center rise the churches of the Madonna della Vita and of Santa Caterina d'Alessandria, the latter of the tenth century.

  • Where Centro storico, Apecchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Quartiere ebraico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient Jewish quarter preserves the synagogue, the oven for unleavened bread and the courtyard for the feast of Sukkot; the 'giro d'aria' dates back to the fifteenth century.

  • Where Centro storico, Apecchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Monte Nerone, sentieri e ciclovie

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Around Monte Nerone there are trekking trails of various difficulty, and dirt and asphalt roads for the bike: Apecchio is a stop of the High Marche Apennines.
