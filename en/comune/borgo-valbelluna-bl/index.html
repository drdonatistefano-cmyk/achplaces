What to see in Borgo Valbelluna

The town of Valbelluna sul Piave at the foot of the Dolomites, with the capital Mel: Venetian square between palaces from the fourteenth to the eighteenth century, archaeological museum and necropolis of ancient Venetians, the castle of Zumelle and, in Lentiai, the church with the ceiling painted by Cesare Vecellio.

8 places to visit in Borgo Valbelluna, in the province of Belluno (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza di Mel

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Venetian lounge at the foot of the Dolomites is now Piazza Papa Luciani, surrounded by buildings of different eras: Palazzo Barbuio, the oldest (XIV century), Casa Fulcis-Zadra, Palazzo delle Contesse, Locanda Cappello del Seicento and Palazzo Pivetta-Stefani, under whose arch the ancient Karèra street passed. At the centre a stone base with the coats of arms of the Magnificent Community and the Zorzi dated 1520 holds the pen, iron since 1949.

  • Where Piazza Papa Luciani, Mel
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: dolomitiprealpi.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Castello di Zumelle

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

A symbolic fortress of the Valbelluna, linked according to the legend to the Gothic Gothic (VI century) and his twin sons, from which the name "castrum zumellarum" reached the maximum extent in the 12th century, was destroyed in 1196 and rebuilt by Rizzardo da Camino in 1311. The ecomuseum of about 15 hectares includes the castle, the medieval walls, a 36-metre tower, the church of San Lorenzo and a forest botanical garden, with guided tours and a tavern.

  • Where Via San Donà 4, Mel, Borgo Valbelluna
  • Entry €8, residents €4, children 2-8 years €3
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: dolomitiprealpi.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · castellodizumelle.it · italia.it

Museo Civico Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the Palazzo delle Contesse, museum since 1996, exhibits finds that cover over 40,000 years, from the Paleolithic to the Middle Ages, many from the accessories of the Paleovenet necropolis of Mel (VIII-V century BC), including a feline-shaped fibula; the noble salon has a Venetian floor with the phoenix of the Papadopolis. Open on weekends, from Tuesday to Sunday from 15 June to 20 September.

  • Where Piazza Papa Luciani 1, Mel, Borgo Valbelluna
  • Entry €5, reduced €4 or €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: dolomitiprealpi.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Necropoli dei Veneti antichi

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Used without interruption by the ancient Venetians from the 8th century BC at least until the V, it was excavated between 1958 and 1964; there are still visible some circles of stone with entrance to the south and chest graves with cinerary urns in ceramic or bronze, whose kits are at the museum. Free entrance, barrier-free.

open only by reservation

  • Where Via Tempietto, Mel, Borgo Valbelluna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.borgovalbelluna.bl.it · italia.it

Palazzo della Magnifica Comunità (Palazzo Zorzi)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Already palace of Counts Zorzi, who ruled Mel for three centuries, started in 1510 and today municipal seat; the room on the first floor has frescoes by Marco da Mel with episodes of the Orlando Furioso (1545).

  • Where Piazza Papa Luciani, Mel
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · dolomitiprealpi.it · italia.it

Chiesa dell'Addolorata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The church "vecchia" of Mel, built between 1480 and 1490 with three naves with ten altars, struck by lightning in 1756 and rebuilt; it houses a Pietà by Giovanni da Mel and dello Schiavone.

  • Where Piazza Papa Luciani, Mel
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: dolomitiprealpi.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa arcipretale di Santa Maria Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The large 18th-century church in Palladian style overlooking the square preserves works by Girolamo Denti, Giovanni da Mel (1531), Cesare Vecellio (1580-90) and Luigi Cima.

  • Where Piazza Papa Luciani, Mel
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · dolomitiprealpi.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta di Lentiai

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Pieve cited in 1204 and national monument since 1880: the wooden coffered ceiling has twenty canvases by Cesare Vecellio (1577-1579) with stories of the Virgin and the Polyptych of the Assumption, with eight saints, is of the workshop of Titian. Three aisles on local stone columns and works by Palma il Giovane, Giovanni da Mel, Pietro Liberi and others.

  • Where Via Giovanni Mazzini 1, Lentiai, Borgo Valbelluna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: dolomitiprealpi.it · comune.borgovalbelluna.bl.it · italia.it
