What to see in Nardodipace

Municipality of the Vibonese Serre, rebuilt on the plateau of Ciano after the floods of 1951 and 1972-73; in the territory the megaliths of the stones 'castedrati', the Old habited and the new church with the majolica of Pietro Cascella.

Megaliti di Nardodipace

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Two granite blocks complexes, called "stones" or "stones," on a plateau near the highest peak of the Serre, reported in 2005: trilite structures with estimated blocks over 200 tons, pillars sometimes engraved with pictograms and possible walls. The investigations have not clarified its origin, perhaps anthropic, and are classified as geosites.

Vecchio Abitato («lu Pajisi»)

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry

The original village, born at the beginning of the eighteenth century as a rural agglomeration of Fabrizia's manor, was struck by the floods of the fifties and then abandoned; today few people live there. In the district of Campanaro, a small church houses wooden statues of Serrese workshop, including the Madonna della Natività, carried in procession on September 8th; the bell tower remains only the monofora.

  • Where Vecchio Abitato, Nardodipace
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.nardodipace.vv.it

Chiesa del Nuovo Abitato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church in high position in the rebuilt village, with severe and majolica interiors by Pietro Cascella on facade, altar, baptismal font, via crucis, paliotti, aquasantiere and chandeliers.

  • Where Nuovo Abitato (Ciano), Nardodipace
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.nardodipace.vv.it

Sources: comune.nardodipace.vv.it

Museo Oggetti di civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Objects and photographs chronologically ordered from about 1850 to the end of the fifties: branches, pitchers and jars in terracotta, grinders, irons in coal, plow, shoemaker's counter, pails, cones and spindles, a room that reconstructs a domestic interior, a mantle from mourning of 1900 and clothes in broom fiber. Among the documents the first identity card of 1902, the year of municipal autonomy.

the MiC card (updated to May 2020) indicates closed every day; check with the Municipality (tel. 0963 313092)
