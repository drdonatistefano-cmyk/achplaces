What to see in Bomarzo

The village of peperino degli Orsini and the Sacro Bosco 16th century populated with stone monsters, which inspired artists like Salvador Dalí.

3 places to visit in Bomarzo, in the province of Viterbo (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Sacro Bosco – Parco dei Mostri

Nature and landscape · City parks · Viewpoint

Grotesque sculptures carved into the rock, including sphinxes, warriors in struggle, the hanging house, the Orco and Cerbero, in a path that closes with a small temple.

  • Where Bomarzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Palazzo Orsini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The imposing sixteenth-century palace that dominates the village, expanded over the centuries by the Lante della Rovere and then by the Borghese.

  • Where Bomarzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italyformovies.com · visitlazio.com

Borgo di Bomarzo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village with its peperino houses, to be visited before the Sacro Bosco, tasting the cookie of Sant'Anselmo.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
