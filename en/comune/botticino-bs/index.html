What to see in Botticino

Municipality to the east of Brescia that gives the name to the precious Botticino marble, extracted from its quarries from the sixteenth century; the Marble Museum tells about its geology, extraction and processing and accompanies the nearby quarry with the itinerary Â Landscapes of extraction Â .

Museo del Marmo

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Inaugurated by the Municipality in December 1998, re-assembled and reopened in December 2011, since 2012 it is coordinated by the Ecomuseo del Botticino; it rises between the Corna and the woods, near the quarries. It tells of an extraction present in the territory for more than two thousand years with thematic rooms on geology, extraction, liatura, transport, processing and historical memory, a litoteca of stones from all over the world, plastic, video and multimedia guide; closes the path the Botticino Visitor Center dedicated to marble, wine and stockings. Open on Sundays and holidays 15-18 (free entrance, by reservation according to the Municipality); closed on 1st January, Easter and Easter Mondays, 25 April, 2 June, 15 August, 8, 25, 26 and 31 December. Without architectural barriers.

  • Where Via Cave 76, Botticino Mattina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodelmarmo.it

Sources: museodelmarmo.it · in-lombardia.it · comune.botticino.bs.it

Cave di marmo, itinerario «Paesaggi estrattivi»

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

Guided tour of about 3 hours proposed by the Marble Museum: after the collection, a panoramic walk to the nearby quarry touches a lime furnace, the old piano charger, the derricks and the oil mill, and the work of Urban Art Groups up to 25 people by reservation (info@museodelmarmo.it); recommended period March-May and September-November, in case of rain, please refer.

  • Where Partenza dal Museo del Marmo, Via Cave 76, Botticino Mattina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: museodelmarmo.it · in-lombardia.it
