What to see in Capo d'Orlando

Seaside resort of the Tyrrhenian coast of Messinese, with the promontory of the sanctuary of Maria Santissima and the ruins of the castle overlooking the Aeolian, the Roman baths of Bagnoli, the seaside village of San Gregorio and Villa Piccolo, house museum of the Barons Small cousins of Tomasi di Lampedusa.

Villa Piccolo, casa museo

Museums · House museums

Villa of the early twentieth century with park, home of the Piccolo di Calanovella family: Casimiro, photographer and author of ... Magic watercolors, the poet Lucio and the botany Agata Giovanna. The cousin Giuseppe Tomasi of Lampedusa stayed there in the summer and wrote pages of the Gattopardo. The museum, opened in 1978, exhibits paintings, ceramics, ancient weapons, books, family portraits, photographs and cameras, letters of Tomasi di Lampedusa and a fortepiano linked to Lucio Piccolo. Hours: Monday-Friday 10-13 and 16-18, Saturday 10-13, Sunday closed.

  • Where S.S. 113 km 109, Capo d'Orlando
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.fondazionepiccolo.it

Sources: fondazionepiccolo.it · italia.it · visitsicily.info

Terme romane di Bagnoli

History and architecture · Archaeology

At about 3 km northeast of the center, remains of the thermal baths of a Roman villa of the III-IV century AD, probably damaged by two earthquakes between IV and V century: of the eight rooms are legible frigidarium, tepidarium and calidarium, with mosaic floors of stone and marble tiles. For timetables, tel. 0941 334796.

  • Where Contrada Bagnoli, Capo d'Orlando
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitsicily.info

Santuario di Maria Santissima di Capo d'Orlando

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

A unique nave with three chapels and wooden ceiling inlaid with an eight-pointed star, a pilgrimage destination every 22nd October.

  • Where Monte della Madonna, Capo d'Orlando
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Monte della Madonna e ruderi del castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the top of the promontory, next to the sanctuary, remain the ruins of the medieval castle; you climb for a few hundred steps between aromatic herbs and the view ranges from Capo Milazzo to Cefalù, with the Aeolians in the background.

  • Where Monte della Madonna, Capo d'Orlando
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

San Gregorio e sentiero Goletta

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fishing village reached by the scenic coast, with the church of San Gregorio Magno and a small square of fishing houses; from the square starts the Goletta trail, a medium difficulty trekking that was once the only connection with Capo d'Orlando.

  • Where San Gregorio, Capo d'Orlando

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
