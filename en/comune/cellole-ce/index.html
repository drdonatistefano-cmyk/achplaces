What to see in Cellole

Municipality of the coast domizio with the beaches of Baia Domizia and Baia Felice, overlooking the Aurunci Mountains and the Gulf of Gaeta; in Punta San Limato the remains of a Roman maritime villa of ancient Sinuessa.

Villa romana di Punta San Limato

History and architecture · Archaeology

Maritime villa of Julian-Claudia age (1st century A.D.), with reconstructions in brick from the 2nd century, partly incorporated in the eighteenth century farmhouse of San Limato. At the lower level a cryptoportico in mixed work; to the north the spa district with frigidarium in black and white mosaic with marine animals, tepidarium and calidarium; to the south three residential areas with niches, apses and mosaic and marble floors.

opens only in extraordinary openings scheduled with the Municipality (in 2025 on Saturdays of July and August, 18:30-20:30); visits on request via PEC to the Superintendence

Baia Domizia e Baia Felice

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 9 km of sandy beach protected by a pine forest planted at the beginning of the twentieth century, when the swampy coast was reclaimed, among Mediterranean maquis, vineyards and olive groves. From the beaches you can see the Aurunci Mountains, the Gulf of Gaeta and the islands of Ischia and Ponza.

  • Where Baia Domizia, Cellole
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page sabapce-bn.cultura.gov.it
