What to see in Argentera

The highest municipality in the Stura Valley (1,650 m), on the border with France: in Ferriere the house-museum of smuggler, in Bersezio the medieval church of San Lorenzo, and in altitude the lakes of Roburent and the Lake of Maddalena on the homonymous hill.

5 places to visit in Argentera, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

La Mizoun dal Countrabandìer, Museo del Contrabbandiere

Museums · Themed

In an old house of the hamlet Ferriere, restored and set up as a house of smuggler, kitchen, bedroom and a large room with wooden loft reconstruct the daily life of a few decades ago; objects and utensils tell the contraband with which the inhabitants integrated the lean winter incomes. Open in summer: the keys retire to the Becchi Rossi Refuge (tel. +39 0171 96715).

  • Where Rifugio Becchi Rossi, borgata Ferriere, Argentera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Borgata Ferriere

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village in a large basin of pastures, indicated as an example of Alpine architecture; it houses the house-museum of smuggler.

  • Where Borgata Ferriere, Argentera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Bersezio e chiesa di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Bersezio is the most populous town and seat of the town hall, with ancient houses with very steep roofs once covered with rye straw. The church of San Lorenzo is of medieval age and on the outside it has a Roman marble slab with the bas-relief of two warriors.

  • Where Frazione Bersezio, Argentera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Colle e Lago della Maddalena

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

International route between the Stura Valley and France, with a hairpin road very loved by motorcyclists; on top, on a prairie, the Lake of Maddalena opens.

  • Where Colle della Maddalena, Argentera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
