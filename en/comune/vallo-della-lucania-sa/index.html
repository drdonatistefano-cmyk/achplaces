What to see in Vallo della Lucania

A small town at the foot of Mount Gelbison, in a valley of olive trees, seat of the Cilento National Park Authority and the main shopping and cultural center of the area; it preserves the eighteenth-century Cathedral of San Pantaleone, the fifteenth-century church of Santa Maria delle Grazie, the diocesan Museum and, in the hamlet of Pattano, the ancient Italian-Greek abbey.

Cattedrale di San Pantaleone

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built between 1700 and 1736 and dedicated to the patron, it preserves many works: two curtains with the effigy of San Pantaleone by the master Vito Formisano, San Pantaleone that heals the blind ... by Giuseppe De Mattia, the conversion of San Pantaleone by the Salerno Stefano Trapanese and an ampolla with the blood of the saint.

  • Where Vallo della Lucania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of 1480 in the center of the village, which preserves the polyptych of Andrea da Salerno.

  • Where Vallo della Lucania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters

Born from the commitment of Bishop Biagio D'Agostino for the neglected works of art, it was established on December 8, 1978 as Diocesan Museum and Art Gallery to guard the works of the parishes of the diocese; expanded with restoration workshop and the San Pantaleone room for recent works, exhibits wooden statues and paintings of local tradition.

The MiC tab (updated 2020) indicates closed every day; check opening (tel. 0974 75794, museum@diocesivallodellalucania.it)

  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it

Badia di Santa Maria di Pattano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Ancient monastery of Byzantine rite in the hamlet Pattano, probably founded between the 8th and the middle of the 10th century: a document from 993 already shows it owner of vast lands up to Ceraso. It flourished between the 11th and 14th centuries under Longobards, Normans and Angioini thanks to the Greek monks, then decayed and after the papal investigation of 1458 was suppressed and entrusted to commendatory abbots.

  • Where Frazione Pattano, Vallo della Lucania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Piazze del centro: Vittorio Emanuele e dei Martiri

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Piazza Vittorio Emanuele rises on the stretch of river, covered in front of the Town Hall, which separated the two original farmhouses of Spio and Cornuti, with the colonnade and the fountain in front of Palazzo Bocchetti; in Piazza dei Martiri the fountain with four stone lions. In the center also the seventeenth-century Chapel of the Crucifix and the former convent of the Clarisse.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele, Vallo della Lucania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it
