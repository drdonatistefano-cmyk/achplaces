Villa Cusani Tittoni Traversi e parco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Monumental complex of villas and park among the most representative 18th-century villas of Lombardy: the current aspect is due to the architect Giuseppe Piermarini, in a style close to the Reggia of Monza; in the nineteenth century it was restored Pelagio Palagi and at the beginning of the twentieth century Luca Beltrami performed minor interventions. There were guests Ferdinand IV of Naples and Umberto I. On the first floor sculptures and drawings by Giuseppe Scalvini; in summer, since 2012, the park hosts the festival Â"Parco TittoniÂ" . The park has been a candidate for the FAI census on several occasions.

Museo Scalvini

Museums · Modern art

In the Villa Tittoni Traversi, since 1999 he has been donating the Milanese sculptor Giuseppe Scalvini (108-2003): 36 sculptures in plaster, wax and bronze, to which in 2003 16 drawings in charcoal and some engravings were added. Scalvini, author of the eagles of the Central Station and Justice of the Tribunal of Milan, realized in 1937 two angels for the Basilica of Desio and participated in the Venice Biennale in 1948 and 1954. In addition to the permanent collection, temporary exhibitions of artists from the twentieth century. Open Friday, Saturday and Sunday 10:30-12:30 and 15:30-18:00; Thursday by appointment.

Piazza della Conciliazione e basilica dei Santi Siro e Materno

History and architecture · Squares and old town

The square overlooks the Basilica of Santi Siro and Materno, symbol of the city, with the bell tower of the early fifteenth century, and the monument to Pope Pius XI, born in Desio.

Casa natale e Museo Pio XI

Museums · House museums

In the house where Achille Ratti was born and spent his early childhood, then a spinning mill Gavazzi, orphanage (1925-1971) and high school (until 2004), the Pius XI Museum, established in 1939, preserves 459 objects declared of exceptional cultural interest by the Superintendence, including a radio device designed by Guglielmo Marconi and donated to the pontiff in 1931. It is open for individual or group visits.

Convention of 2024 for the redevelopment of the building for social and cultural purposes (Comune di Desio)

Museums · Science and university
