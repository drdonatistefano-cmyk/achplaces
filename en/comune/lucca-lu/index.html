What to see in Lucca

The "city of the hundred churches," closed by intact Renaissance walls of more than 4 km walkable and by bike, with the square born from the Roman amphitheatre; city of Puccini.

25 places to visit in Lucca, in the province of Lucca (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers 7 of the 14 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Mura di Lucca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The belt of the Cinque-Seicento, among the few examples in Italy of intact walls: over 4 km with bastions, above which runs a tree-lined avenue to walk or by bike.

  • Where Mura urbane, Lucca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Baluardi e sotterranei delle Mura

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The ramparts hide passages used as armories and ammunition deposits; the Baluardo di San Colombano preserves two lions of the thirteenth century.

History and architecture · Towers · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Cattedrale di San Martino
