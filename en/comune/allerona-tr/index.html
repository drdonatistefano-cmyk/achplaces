What to see in Allerona

Borgo dell'Orvietano was born as a castle of Lerona, outpost of Orvieto in the twelfth century, of which two walls and doors remain; in its territory the Selva di Meana with Villa Cahen, the only Liberty building of Umbria with an Italian garden, the Museum of Geological Cycles with marine fossils and the exhibition of Pugnaloni.

7 places to visit in Allerona, in the province of Terni (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa Cahen e Selva di Meana

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Villa Liberty built in 1880 for the financier of Antwerp Edouard Cahen, made marquis by Vittorio Emanuele II, on a large estate between two ridges of the Paglia; it is surrounded by a largely Italian garden with rare plants, a Japanese garden restored, greenhouses and lemon trees, in the protected area of Allerona-Meana.

  • Where Località Selva di Meana, Allerona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · umbriatourism.it

Parco di Villalba

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

The wooded area of more than 20 hectares is equipped in the Selva di Meana, state property, with tall trees, maples, roses and wild orchids, and fauna such as wild boars, deer and roe deer.

  • Where Selva di Meana, Allerona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Museo dei cicli geologici

Museums · Science and university

Founded in 2003 in the town hall, it tells the geological history of south-western Umbria with marine fossils of the Pleistocene (whale remains, shark teeth, molluscs, sea urchins and corals) and geological and botanical finds of the territory; it also organizes thematic excursions.

  • Where Piazza A. Lupi, Allerona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · umbriatourism.it

Mostra permanente dei Pugnaloni

Museums · Themed

Documents, photos and materials on the Feast of the Daggers, floats surmounted by a poplar tree or cerro with scenes of life and work in the fields, parading the third Sunday of May in honor of Saint Isidoro, patron saint of the farmers.

  • Where Via Roma, Allerona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · umbriatourism.it

Borgo e porte del Sole e della Luna

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The castle of Lerona, bulwark of Orvieto in the 12th century, was then destroyed: parts of the walls and two gates remain, Gate of the Sun and Gate of the Moon. In 1585 the community gave itself its own Statutes and in 1595 freed itself from Orvieto and Monaldeschi.

  • Where Centro storico, Allerona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria della Stella

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

12th century church renovated at the end of the last century, with apse frescoes by the Sienese painter A. Villigiardi (1896).

  • Where Allerona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Chiesa della Madonna dell'Acqua

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Small octagonal temple of the early eighteenth century, attributed to Sangallo, built on a fifteenth-century votive chapel near a source considered miraculous.

  • Where Allerona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it
