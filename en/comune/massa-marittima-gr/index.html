What to see in Massa Marittima

City of Etruscan origin on the Metallifere Hills, closed by intact walls, with one of the most beautiful medieval squares in Tuscany and a suggestive network of alleys: for a complete visit it takes at least three hours.

12 places to visit in Massa Marittima, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers 4 of the 9 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Centro storico e vicoli

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town is not exhausted in the main square, but it also develops through a suggestive network of alleys, which deserve to be explored. A full visit of the city takes at least three hours.

  • Where Centro storico, Massa Marittima
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time At least 3 hours
  • Official website www.turismomassamarittima.it

Sources: turismomassamarittima.it

Cattedrale di San Cerbone e Piazza Garibaldi

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Cathedral at the top of a staircase on the main square, in the Pisan Romanesque style: it was begun in the 11th century and enlarged by Giovanni Pisano at the end of the 13th in Gothic style, intervention visible in the third order of the facade, on the spires and on the apse. Inside, in addition to the Majesty of Duccio di Buoninsegna, the baptismal font of the Comacini Masters, the Ark of San Cerbone, the work of Goro di Gregorio, the Crucifixion of Ambrogio Lorenzetti and the altar of Ivan Theimer.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Massa Marittima
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.turismomassamarittima.it

Sources: turismomassamarittima.it

Complesso museale di San Pietro all'Orto

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The Majesty of Ambrogio Lorenzetti is the main attraction of the museum, whose route is divided into five rooms dedicated to Sacred Art.

  • Entry paid

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Located in the immediate vicinity of the San Pietro all'Orto Museum Complex, the Church and the Cloister are an additional element of interest for those who visit the historic centre.

Museo degli Organi Meccanici Antichi

Museums · Themed

Housed in the museum complex of San Pietro all'Orto, the museum of the Santa Cecilia Foundation houses a private collection of ancient instruments, unique of its kind. In the premises, once used as a church, a fresco attributed to Ambrogio Lorenzetti was found.

  • Where Massa Marittima
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.turismomassamarittima.it

Fonti dell'Abbondanza e Albero della Fecondità

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The medieval source with the fresco of the Tree of Fecundity, saved from a veil of limestone: an image to be interpreted as a propitiatory hymn to life and prosperity.

  • Where Massa Marittima
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.turismomassamarittima.it

Sources: turismomassamarittima.it

Cassero Senese e Torre del Candeliere

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The Candeliere Tower dates back to 1228, when Massa Marittima was a free municipality, and is located in the Terziere di Cittanuova, in the highest part of the city. The Arch of Siena was built by the Senesi after the conquest of Massa Marittima, in 1335, to connect the Tower to the Fortress.

  • Where Massa Marittima
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.turismomassamarittima.it

Sources: turismomassamarittima.it

Museo Archeologico Giovannangelo Camporeale

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The museum traces the history of the population of the territory of Massa Marittima, with particular attention to the findings found in the Archaeological Park of Lake Accesa. Among the main elements of the exhibition are also the Stele of Vado all'Arancio, an anthropomorphic stele of the Neolithic era, and the copy of the cast of the Oreopithecus bambooli, a hominid whose first remains were found in the lignite mines of Montebamboli.

  • Where Via Todini 1, Massa Marittima
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.turismomassamarittima.it

Sources: turismomassamarittima.it

Subterraneo, polo museale della miniera

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The unitary museum centre "Subterraneo" has two locations: one dedicated to the mining history of Massa Marittima, the other with a guided tour in a rebuilt mining gallery. It is not an original mine but a particularly faithful reconstruction, where the guide illustrates the techniques of extraction of the pyrite.

  • Where Massa Marittima
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.turismomassamarittima.it

Sources: turismomassamarittima.it

Lago dell'Accesa e Parco Archeologico

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lake, about 10 km from Massa Marittima, with the Etruscan archaeological park with free entrance, to be combined with the Archaeological Museum.

  • Where Lago dell'Accesa, Massa Marittima
  • Entry Free

  • Official website www.turismomassamarittima.it

Sources: turismomassamarittima.it

Aquarium Mondo Marino ed Explora

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Near Massa Marittima, in Valpiana, is a private reality that stands out for its important scientific and research activity dedicated to the protection of sharks and the protection of the marine environment. In addition to the scientific and popular value, it is a destination particularly appreciated by even the smallest visitors.

  • Where Via della Cava, Valpiana, Massa Marittima
  • Entry €10, children 3-12 years €8
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.turismomassamarittima.it

Sources: turismomassamarittima.it

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

The art garden of the sculptor Maria Dompè dedicated to the partisan Norma Parenti, with spirals of earth, lawn and stone and classical music, near the walls.

  • Where Via Norma Parenti, Massa Marittima
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
