What to see in Massa Marittima

City of Etruscan origin on the Metallifere Hills, closed by intact walls, with one of the most beautiful medieval squares in Tuscany.

8 places to visit in Massa Marittima, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 3 of the 9 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Open Massa Marittima in the guide with map and filters

Duomo di San Cerbone e Piazza Garibaldi

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Cathedral of the thirteenth century Romanesque-Pisan with Sienese arch, on top of a staircase on the star square: Romanesque font, rosette, a Gothic reliquary and a Madonna attributed to Duccio.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Massa Marittima
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo di San Pietro all'Orto e Maestà di Lorenzetti

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The museum that houses the Majesty of Ambrogio Lorenzetti.

  • Where Massa Marittima
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Fonti dell'Abbondanza e Albero della Fecondità

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The medieval source with the fresco of the Tree of Fecundity, saved from a veil of limestone: a tree from which dozens of phallics contended by women hang.

  • Where Massa Marittima
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Cassero Senese e Torre del Candeliere

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In the high town built under Siena the fortress with the Candeliere Tower, with the best view of the area.

  • Where Massa Marittima
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the Palazzo del Podestà the finds of the Etruscan territory of Accesa, with houses and graves excavated.

  • Where Via Todini 1, Massa Marittima
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo di Arte e Storia delle Miniere

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The mine museum, linked to the millennial history of the city's mineral resources.

  • Where Massa Marittima
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Lago dell'Accesa e Parco Archeologico

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lake with the Etruscan archaeological park, with free entrance, to be combined with the Archaeological Museum.

  • Where Lago dell'Accesa, Massa Marittima
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Giardino "Sol omnibus lucet"

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

The art garden of the sculptor Maria Dompè dedicated to the partisan Norma Parenti, with spirals of earth, lawn and stone and classical music, near the walls.

  • Where Via Norma Parenti, Massa Marittima
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
