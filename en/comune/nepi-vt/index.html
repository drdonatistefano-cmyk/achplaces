What to see in Nepi

The city of the water is on a promontory of tuff along the Amerina street, with the waterfall of Cavaterra at the foot of the Rocca dei Borgia.

3 places to visit in Nepi, in the province of Viterbo (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca dei Borgia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The castle on the Amerina street, which owes its name to the family of Pope Alexander VI, author of the first major modifications in 1479.
