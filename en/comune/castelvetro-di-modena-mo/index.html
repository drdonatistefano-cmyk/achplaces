Piazza Roma, la Piazza della Dama

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The white and black checkerboard square of the village of the Rangoni, with the Clock Tower, the Prison Tower and the view of the Lambrusco hills.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Castelvetro di Modena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Castello di Levizzano e Museo Rosso Graspa

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The castle with the Matildica Tower and the Bishops' Rooms, now home to the wine museum and the rural society dedicated to Lambrusco Grasparossa.

  • Where Via Celestino Cavedoni, Levizzano Rangone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it

Palazzo Rangoni e Fili d'Oro a Palazzo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The Palace of the Rangoons with decorated atrium, courtyard, staircase and balcony, which in 1564 hosted Torquato Tasso; near, the permanent exhibition of sixteenth-century dresses Gold threads.

  • Where Via Torquato Tasso, Castelvetro di Modena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
