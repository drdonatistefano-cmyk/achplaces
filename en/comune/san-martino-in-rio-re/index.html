What to see in San Martino in Rio

Country of the Reggian plain gathered around the Fortress Estense, which houses the Coppelli art gallery and the agriculture museum, with a private car museum and the 17th century parish.

4 places to visit in San Martino in Rio, in the province of Reggio Emilia (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca Estense e Pinacoteca Coppelli

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Castello di origine matildica, poi dei Roberti e dal Quattrocento di un ramo marchanale degli Este: quadrangular plant and an unspoilt corner tower with ghibelline blackbirds and caditoias. Inside the chapel of St. John with a sharp sixth and the noble floor with painted coffered ceilings, frescoes and stuccoes from the fifteenth to the eighteenth centuries; it houses the Pinacoteca Coppelli, the library and the open archive dedicated to Henghel Gualdi. Open Sundays and holidays, 10-12.30 and 15-18.30, with guided tours; closed in August.

  • Where Corso Umberto I 22, San Martino in Rio
  • Entry €5 (reduced €3)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: reggioemiliawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo dell'Agricoltura e del Mondo rurale

Museums · Themed

On the ground floor of the Rocca, opened in 1968 and renewed in 1994: work in the fields, hemp, wine, breeding, spinning and weaving, ropes and whips, brooms, mills, dairy and pork processing, until the reconstruction of a peasant house.

  • Where Corso Umberto I 22, San Martino in Rio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo dell'Automobile

Local life and traditions · Motors · Free entry

Private collection started in 1956 by Domenico Gentili: more than forty vintage cars, a dozen motorcycles and vintage bicycles in a 650 m2 shed, including the Zedel of Queen Margherita, the Fiat 527 Ardita of General Kesselring and the Alfa Romeo Arccia d'Oro of 1949. Open by reservation.

  • Where Via Barbieri 12, San Martino in Rio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: reggioemiliawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish of the seventeenth century: in the chapel at the bottom of the right a fresco of the 15th century Madonna and Child, inserted in a painting later than the Holy Trinity.
