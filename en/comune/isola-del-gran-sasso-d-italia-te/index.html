What to see in Isola del Gran Sasso d'Italia

Borgo at the foot of the Teramian side of Gran Sasso, between the Mavone and Ruzzo streams, in the Gran Sasso National Park and Monti della Laga: the Sanctuary of San Gabriele, the Romanesque church of San Giovanni ad Insulam and paths to lakes and hermitages.

Santuario di San Gabriele dell'Addolorata

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

It preserves the relics of the patron saint of Abruzzo and the young: born as a convent in the 16th century and enlarged in the 20th century, in front of the ancient church a new sanctuary was built to welcome pilgrims after the canonization of 1959.

  • Where Isola del Gran Sasso d'Italia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: abruzzoturismo.it · discoverteramo.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni ad Insulam

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Romanesque church of the twelfth century on a hill along the Mavone, with facade in white stone, rose window, double-fores and hanging arches; inside three naves and a cruising crypt, and in the apse a fresco of 1421 with the Redeemer between the Virgin and St. John the Baptist. Next to the ruins of the Benedictine monastery.

  • Where Località San Giovanni, Isola del Gran Sasso d'Italia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · fondoambiente.it · abruzzoturismo.it
