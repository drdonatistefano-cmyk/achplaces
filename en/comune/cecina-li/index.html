Parco archeologico della Villa romana di San Vincenzino

History and architecture · Archaeology

Urban residential villa built from the second half of the 1st century BC, with gardens, arcades and a water system that collected water in a large underground cistern, now entirely accessible; in the 2nd-III century A.D. were added thermal baths and a summer triclinium with nymph, from the 5th century was abandoned. In the nineteenth-century building of the park the exhibition "Privata Luxuria" is shown.

  • Where Via C. Ginori 33, Cecina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo archeologico La Cinquantina

Museums · Archaeological

In Villa Guerrazzi, known as "La Cinquantina" (the Fiftyth century building), opened in 1980 and re-assembled in 2003: it tells the Lower Val di Cecina from the Paleolithic to the Roman villas, with the Montescudaio cinerary (VIII-VII century BC), the princely set of Casale Marittimo, the jewelry of Belora and, in the garden, the Etruscan tomb of Casaglia rebuilt.

  • Where Via F. D. Guerrazzi 6, San Pietro in Palazzi, Cecina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visittuscany.com · italia.it

Museo della vita e del lavoro della Maremma settentrionale

Museums · Themed

Riserva naturale Tomboli di Cecina

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

About 15 km of cordons of dunes covered with dense vegetation, from the beach towards the inside: lilies and marine poppies on the sand, junipers, holm oaks, sea pine trees and domestic; there live foxes, squints, roe deer, badgers, green peaks and upups. The centuries-old pine forest reaches the sea and has picnic areas.

  • Where Marina di Cecina, Cecina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Marina di Cecina e le spiagge

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Soft sandy beach about 14.5 km long, Blue Flag since 2006, between free beach and historic establishments (the oldest of the late nineteenth century); the village was born around the grand-ducal watchtower of the sixteenth century, then joined to Villa Ginori. To the north of the port is the beach of the Gorette.

  • Where Marina di Cecina, Cecina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Parco Gallorose

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

A fauna park of about five hectares with a three-kilometre route, divided between the Farm (domestic animals and ancient breeds such as the Pisa cow and the Amiatino donkey) and the Zoo for geographical areas, with over 150 species and a small museum of vintage photos and agricultural tools.

  • Where Via Aurelia Sud 59, Cecina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it
