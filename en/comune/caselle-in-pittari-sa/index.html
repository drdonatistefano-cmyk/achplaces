What to see in Caselle in Pittari

The hilly village of the Cilento National Park, Vallo di Diano and Alburni, Caselle in Pittari has a high part, "u' Castieddu" with medieval tower and mother church, Mount Pittari with the Cave of San Michele, the swallowery of Bussento and the Lucano settlement of Laurelli.

Centro storico, Torre medievale e chiesa madre

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The upper part of the village, called "u' Castieddu" gathers the medieval tower and the mother church of Santa Maria Assunta, of the second half of the twelfth century; the lower part, outside the ancient walls, overlooks the valley. From the square of the tower you can see Monte Cervati, the highest peak in Campania, the river Bussento and the Sanctuary of Novi Velia. In the village also the churches of San Michele and San Rocco, the chapels of San Giovanni Battista and of Sant'Anna and the Palazzo Marchesale; the houses preserve gardens, common ovens and "stieri" , once stables and today dispensed.

Monte Pittari e Grotta di San Michele

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

From Piazzetta San Michele a dirt track climbs in about twenty minutes to a cross overlooking the Bussento valley and Mount Gelbison, then to a large plateau where there was a Byzantine abbey and to the viewpoint from which, on clear days, you can see the Gulf of Policastro and the Calabrian coast. Two natural caves have been places of worship for centuries: the Cave of San Michele, home of the sanctuary, with concretions, masonry, bas-reliefs and a stone saintly waterworks, where ceramics from the Bronze Age were found, and the Cave of Sant'Angelo. At the beginning of the 11th century, the Longobard Prince Guaimaro III founded the Abbey of St. Angel, whose remains remain.

  • Where Partenza da Piazzetta San Michele, Caselle in Pittari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page promozione.cilentoediano.it

Sources: promozione.cilentoediano.it

Inghiottitoio del Bussento «La Rupe»

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

In a karst territory rich in caves, swallowholes and resurgents, the Bussento, born from Monte Cervati, disappears into the subsoil near the Rupe to emerge after about 4 km in Morigerati, in the WWF Oasis, on the opposite side of the mountain.

  • Where Località La Rupe, Caselle in Pittari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page promozione.cilentoediano.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · promozione.cilentoediano.it

Area archeologica Laurelli

History and architecture · Archaeology

Lucano settlement on a large plateau between two watercourses, along the ancient route between the Vallo di Diano and the coast, with regular road system, large houses around a central courtyard and a small group of burials of the late fourth century BC. The excavations, begun in 1990 by the Superintendence of Salerno, continued by the University of Salerno.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
