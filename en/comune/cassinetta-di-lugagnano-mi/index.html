What to see in Cassinetta di Lugagnano

The village of the noble Milanese villas is located on the opposite banks of the Naviglio Grande, joined by a bridge with donkey back, with a sequence of villas of six-nineteenth-century delight, the church of Santa Maria Nascente, the oratory rococo di San Giuseppe and the Molino dei Biraghi of the fifteenth century.

Ponte sul Naviglio Grande e statua di San Carlo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The back bridge of donkey, of seventeenth-century origin and rebuilt in 1862 to facilitate navigation, combines the two villages of Cassinetta and Lugagnano; next to stands the statue of San Carlo Borromeo (1749), which passed here in 1584. Nearby the Osteria del Ponte, a stopover from the sixteenth century, and the docking of the tourist boats on the Naviglio.

  • Where Ponte sul Naviglio Grande, Cassinetta di Lugagnano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Oratorio di San Giuseppe e Villa Nai Bossi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1742 as a chapel of Villa Castiglioni (now Nai Bossi Poroli, of the eighteenth century), along the Naviglio immediately after the bridge, it is an example of Lombard rococo.

Molino dei Biraghi e Roggia Biraga

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Mill of 1428-30, recently restored, the oldest still in operation in the Naviglio belt; it is fed by the Roggia Biraga, derived from the Naviglio Grande in 1428.

Ville di delizia di Lugagnano

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

On the bank of Lugagnano and along Via Roma follow Villa Mörlin Visconti (neoclassical, 1825-27), Villa Frotta Eusebio (XVIII century, with a scenographic wall masking a blind wall), Villa Trivulzio (neoclassical of the late eighteenth century, with oratory open towards the village), Villa Krentzlin (XVII-XVIII century) and Villa Clari Monzini, built in the second half of the seventeenth century, restored in 2007 and now divided into private houses, with the oratory of Sant'Anna; at the end of Via Roma the sixteenth-century Palazzo Mantegazza.

  • Where Via Roma, Cassinetta di Lugagnano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it
