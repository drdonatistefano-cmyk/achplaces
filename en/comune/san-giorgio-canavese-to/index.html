What to see in San Giorgio Canavese

Borgo del Canavese dominated by the castle of the Counts of Biandrate, with the parish church of Santa Maria Assunta and the lunette of Fermo Stella (1537); in the birthplace of the historian Carlo Botta is located the Nossi Rais Civic Museum, and in the villages there are the churches of the confraternities and the sanctuary of Misobolo.

Museo civico Nossi Rais

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In the birthplace of historian Carlo Botta (1766-1837), born from the passion of Giuseppe Dorma in the eighties and inaugurated in 1997, he tells with more than 1,600 finds the agricultural and artisan life of the country: tools, rebuilt environments, vintage clothes, the curious "raddrizza-corna" and two original specimens of the soundproofing machine by Antonio Michela, of which an updated version is still used in Parliament. Open on public holidays, in weekdays by appointment; it is part of the AMI museum network.

  • Where Via Campeggio 8, San Giorgio Canavese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.sangiorgiocanavese.to.it

Castello dei Conti di Biandrate

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Of a first building of the XII-XIV centuries there are few traces left; the castle was created in the XV-XVI century, was unified by Guido Aldobrandino, who completed the works in 1726 eliminating the central tower and giving the east a façade with three avant-corps crowned with tympanians and balconies. The castle was almost completely demolished at the beginning of the 19th century and replaced by an English garden, where a glories of the early 18th century remains. Privately owned, it is the venue of events: visits by reservation (contact@castellosangiorgio.it).

Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Documented in this place from 1385, with Romanesque bell tower fourteenth century, it was almost rebuilt between 1470 and 1529, enlarged in 1684 and completed with dome and apse in 1927-1929 by engineer Bartolomeo Gallo. It preserves the bezel with eight apostles painted in 1537 by Fermo Stella da Caravaggio, the altarpiece of San Giorgio that kills the school dragon of Defendente Ferrari, a Madonna enthroned by the Spanish school, the main altar with marble balustrade (1752) and the urn of San Vitale donated in 1776 by Cardinal delle Lanze. Free entry during functions.

  • Where Piazza Ippolito Sangiorgio 16, San Giorgio Canavese
  • Entry Free

Chiesa di Santa Marta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built by the Brotherhood of Mercy (established in 1478) in the hamlet Molinatto and renovated between the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries, it is dedicated to Santa Marta and San Giovanni Decollato. Inside six large eighteenth-century paintings of Grassi, including the Madonna del Carmine and the Baptist's Decollation, golden wooden statues and the urn of San Celestino, martyr of the Tebea Legion. Free entry during functions.

  • Where Piazza Matteo Pescatore 5, località Molinatto, San Giorgio Canavese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sangiorgiocanavese.to.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Felice a Ritania

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the Confraternity of the Trinity built at the beginning of the seventeenth century, dedicated first to San Lorenzo and then also to the Exaltation of the Cross and San Felice: simple exterior, interior with rich Baroque decoration. In the nearby hamlet of Piatonia, the church of the Immaculate, erected around 1610 by the homonymous confraternity, has an airy interior with many vintage paintings and a remarkable high altar. Free entry during functions.

  • Where Via Umberto I 1, località Ritania, San Giorgio Canavese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sangiorgiocanavese.to.it · italia.it

Santuario di Misobolo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

About 3 km from the village stood a fortified village, abandoned between the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries for unknown causes (perhaps war or plague); its church became a shrine, long officiated by a hermit and also used as a cemetery, and in the mid fifteenth century begged Blessed George of Biandrate. The pilgrimage, linked to the feast of Our Lady on July 2nd, then joined the pilgrimage of Pasquetta. Free entry during functions.

  • Where Strada per Misobolo 15, San Giorgio Canavese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
