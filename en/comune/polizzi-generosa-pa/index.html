What to see in Polizzi Generosa

Borgo delle Madonie on a rocky ridge at 917 meters, overlooking the mountains and the valley of Impera: the Mother Church with the triptych attributed to Rogier van der Weyden, the Town Hall in the former Jesuit College with the Civic Archaeological Museum and the Museum of Abies Nebrodensis, Santa Maria delle Grazie, the ruins of the Commenda and the Vallone Madonna degli Angeli with the last fir trees of the Madonie.

Chiesa Madre di Santa Maria Maggiore e trittico fiammingo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Of Norman origin (gate and two monofores on the right side), rebuilt in the fourteenth century and restored in Baroque forms in 1690. It preserves the triptych of the Madonna and Child attributed to Rogier van der Weyden, who arrived in Sicily at the end of the fifteenth century after a shipwreck, works of Giuliano de Matta and of the Zoppo di Gangi, a Virgin of Giuliano Mancino of 1508, the sarcophagus of Domenico Gagini in the chapel of San Gandolfo and, in the Treasury, the silver urn of the saint and a monstrory of Niblio Gagini (1586).

  • Where Via Roma, Polizzi Generosa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.polizzi.pa.it

Civico Museo Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the former Jesuit College, set up by Amedeo Tullio, which directed the excavations of the Hellenistic necropolis of St. Peter's district: the stratigraphic section exhibits the 50 burials of the II-IV century BC, with figurative terracotta (including a comic actor), ceramics with black and red figures, lekythoi and an amphora protosoceliate with Heracles and the Nemean lion of the Polizzi painter. Open Tuesday-Friday 9-13, Saturday and Sunday 10-13 by reservation; free on the first Sunday of the month.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Garibaldi 13, Polizzi Generosa
  • Entry €2, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.polizzi.pa.it

Sources: comune.polizzi.pa.it · parcodellemadonie.it

MAN, Museo dell'Abies Nebrodensis

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 2019 in the Town Hall, it is dedicated to the Madonie fir tree, endemic conifer rediscovered by botanists in 1957: five multimedia environments with video projections, sounds and an interactive panel, the last two rooms dedicated to the few natural specimens that grow between 1400 and 1650 meters in the Vallone Madonna degli Angeli.

  • Where Corso Giuseppe Garibaldi 13, Polizzi Generosa
  • Entry €2, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.polizzi.pa.it

Sources: comune.polizzi.pa.it · parcodellemadonie.it

Palazzo comunale e chiesa di San Girolamo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The seventeenth-century Town Hall was born as a Jesuit college: from the windows near the entrance there are Greek ruins of 400 BC and inside there is a life-size reproduction of the triptych. Next to it, the Jesuit church of San Girolamo has an octagonal tower with lantern, a Baroque stone portal and a plaque of 1872 with metric equivalences of the ancient local measures.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Garibaldi / Piazza Gramsci, Polizzi Generosa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.polizzi.pa.it

Sources: comune.polizzi.pa.it

Ruderi della Chiesa della Commenda

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In 1177, commissioned by Ruggero d'Aquila and belonged to the Order of Malta, it was the first large building built outside the walls and one of the richest Commendes in Sicily.

Vallone Madonna degli Angeli e faggete delle Madonie

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Above 1500 meters the beech forests cover 10,000 hectares, in an area with more than half of the Sicilian flora and 70 species of orchids; in the Vallone Madonna degli Angeli live about 30 specimens of Nebrodi fir. Among the destinations: the convent of the Madonna dell'Alto, on a cliff overlooking Mount Etna, the Piano Cervi and Pizzo Carbonara (1979), the highest peak of Sicily after Etna, from Piano Battaglia; the tourist office is free to guide to seven excursions by Carl Gardner.

  • Where Parco delle Madonie, Polizzi Generosa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.polizzi.pa.it

Sources: comune.polizzi.pa.it

Mercato settimanale e prodotti locali

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry

On Wednesday morning the market is held, with a part dedicated to food: cheese, ricotta, meats and sausages, organic seasonal products, sweets and ice cream; among the specialties beans, apricots, black cherry for ice cream, porcini in autumn and almonds in summer.
