What to see in Corte Franca

Franciacorta municipality formed by the hamlets of Borgonato, Colombaro, Nigoline and Timoline, which includes part of the Natural Reserve of the Torbiere del Sebino; to see Palazzo Torri, cultural lounge frequented by Carducci and Fogazzaro, Palazzo Pizzini and churches of medieval origin such as Sant'Eufemia with the frescoes of Floriano Ferramola.

Riserva Naturale delle Torbiere del Sebino

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Protected wetland of about 360 hectares at the southern edge of Lake Iseo, from which separates a thin morainic cordon, between the municipalities of Iseo, Provaglio d'Iseo and Corte Franca: swampy soils, reeds and water mirrors born where in the nineteenth century peat was extracted for the ironworks of Brescia. Known as a wetland of international importance, ZSC and ZPS, it hosts red herons, swamp hawks, brown and tarabous nibble, and fishes such as tinca, pike and royal perch. You can visit wood paths and walkways between the blades; three main entrances, one at Corte Franca, each with an automatic ticket; open daily. No bikes inside.

  • Entry €1
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Palazzo Torri

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The 17th century noble villa of the Federici Della Corte, built on a military outpost of the sixteenth century; in the eighteenth century the Peroni di Brescia transformed it into a villa of delight with Baroque furnishings and decorations, and at the end of the nineteenth century the Towers enlarged it. Paolina Calegari Torri and her husband Alessandro made it a cultural living room attended by Giosuè Carducci, Antonio Fogazzaro, Franz Lenbach, Domenico Trentacoste, Bishop Jeremiah Bonomelli and Minister Giuseppe Zanardelli. It is run by the Municipal Library.

visit only on request to the staff of the Library (tel. 030 9826200), variable prices

Palazzo Pizzini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

It rises on a hill next to the castle, on late medieval walls: two buildings overlooking an Italian garden, the palace to the north of the late seventeenth century and a body to the east of the early nineteenth century; on the sides of the villa, simple but of aristocratic character, the bases of two towers never completed. Purchased by Lana in the early seventeenth century, it passed to the Gandini, the Saints, the Duchess Bevilacqua La Masa and finally to the Barons Pizzini Piomarta of Rovereto.

the gate opens only for guided tours booked by phone with the Library (030 984488)

Chiesa di Sant'Eufemia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Church in high position on one of the hills, near the Nigoline cemetery, enlarged from the early Middle Ages until the fifteenth century. In the apse a cycle of 16th century frescoes by the Brescia painter Floriano Ferramola tells the legend of St. Euphemia.

Chiesa di San Salvatore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Founded in the Lombard age at the castle of Borgonato, it has a complex that recent studies date from the 12th century; it is located on a hill visible from the road and contains fragments of frescoes from the 15th century.

Chiesa di Santa Maria di Zenighe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built from the 11th century and very transformed until the 18th century, it stands on a hill partly hidden from the houses; it preserves frescoes of various styles dated from the 13th to the 16th century.

Chiesa di Santa Giulia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Chapel founded in the Lombard age and named after the Brescia monastery of San Salvatore-Santa Giulia, transformed around the XI-XII century into a single nave church; the mural paintings, attributed to the fourteenth century, remain only in fragments.

Chiesa di Sant'Afra

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church at the intersection between Via Sant'Afra and the road to Nigoline, next to Palazzo Barboglio: traces of a building probably not after the fifteenth century, but was rebuilt in the current forms in 1707 by Giuseppe Barboglio and brothers. The Barboglio donated it to the Municipality in 2009; the altarpiece with Sant'Afra is preserved at Palazzo Barboglio. Managed by the Municipal Library (tel. 030 984488).

  • Where Via Sant'Afra angolo via Nazario Sauro, Colombaro di Corte Franca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cortefranca.bs.it

Sources: comune.cortefranca.bs.it
