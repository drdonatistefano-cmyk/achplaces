What to see in Salussola

Ancient village on a spur of the Serra, which is accessed by a medieval town gate; next to a viewpoint on the Biellese stands the church of Santa Maria Assunta, with the wooden pulpit of 1712, and the village houses the Museo Laboratorio dell'Oro and the Pietra dell'Ecomuseo del Biellese.

Borgo medievale, porta urbica e torre del castello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient village on a spur of the Serra, once home of three parishes. The village is accessed by a well-preserved medieval town gate, part of a defensive system today only partially visible. The square tower of the castle is a reconstruction of the twentieth century that recalls the ancient mast.

  • Where Centro storico, Salussola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page provincia.biella.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Built between the 12th and 15th centuries next to a view on the Biellese, it has a facade with buttresses, rosette and terracotta decorations around the portal. Inside, two paintings from the end of the sixteenth century attributed to the Vercellese school and the pulpit carved in 1712 by Carlo Francesco Auregio Termine, among the most important examples of Biella's wooden sculpture.
