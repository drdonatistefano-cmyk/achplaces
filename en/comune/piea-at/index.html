What to see in Piea

Small village of the Monferrato astigiano between wheat fields and pastures, dominated by the large eighteenth-century castle with park and geometric garden; the pumpkins are the gastronomic specialty of the village.

Castello di Piea e parco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The castrum Plage already appears in a bull of Pope Anastasio IV of 1154; the fief passed from Riva to Roero, who held it until the end of the eighteenth century, then to the Faussone of Clavesana (1829), to the Genoese Raffaele Bombrini (1880) and finally to the Cross of Doiola. The present, huge 18th-century castle dominates the village and is surrounded by a park with a geometric garden; there are regularly held antique bars. Visits by reservation, also guided in Italian, English and French; from March to May it opens for the event "Narciso Incantato" .

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Filippo e Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Erected at the end of the sixteenth century in Renaissance style, without side chapels, it became parish church in 1692 under the title of Saints Philip and James Apostles, to which Mary the Mother of God was added; the ceiling, originally flat and low, was then raised in cask, and around 1790 choir, presbytery and sacristy were added (baptistery of 1901). In the choir the fresco of Our Lady with the two patron apostles signed by Giulio Musso di Asti (1894), who also painted evangelists and saints of the nave with decorations by Giovanni Lamberti; the altarpiece of the seventeenth century with the Madonna del Carmelo and four saints is attributed to the Moncalvo school (restored in 2004), and on the right wall there is a penitent Maddalena of 1889 copied by Doré by the Marquisa Vittoria of Clavesana. Free access during functions.

  • Where Via Cesare Battisti, Piea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
