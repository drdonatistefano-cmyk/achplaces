What to see in Gromo

Medieval village of the upper Seriana Valley in grey stone and slate roofs, called "Piccola Toledo" for the forges of white weapons; on Piazza Dante overlook the castle Ginami, the fifteenth-century Palazzo Milesi with the Museum of White Arms and Scrolls and the church of San Gregorio, while outside the center stands the church of San Giacomo.

Piazza Dante e centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval center of grey stone buildings with slate roofs and a maze of alleys, the "strecie" with views of the mountains; Piazza Dante, scenic heart of the village, brings together Ginami castle, Palazzo Milesi and San Gregorio. In the upper part of the fountains, facades with ancient windows and local stone portals along via Milesi; in the Middle Ages the numerous forges made Gromo comparable to Toledo for white weapons.

  • Where Piazza Dante, Gromo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitbergamo.net · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Palazzo Milesi e Museo delle Armi bianche e delle Pergamene

Museums · City history

15th century palace (1443 according to the most beautiful villages and in-lombardia.it, 1456 second italia.it), residence of the Buccelleni, then of the Ginami and the Milesi, today seat of the Municipality: double loggia, Renaissance halls, 17th century frescoes and facade in grey marble of Ardesio. It hosts the MAP, with punches from the families of swordmen Chessers and Ginami and alabards, swords, knives, daggers and Ronconi from the 15th to the 17th century, and the archive with ancient parchments.

  • Where Piazza Dante 8, Gromo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitbergamo.net · italia.it · in-lombardia.it

Castello Ginami

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Armigera tower built for military purposes by the Buccelleni in 1246 on a rock spur guarding the village, passed to the Ginami in the sixteenth century; overlooking Piazza Dante.

  • Where Piazza Dante, Gromo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di San Gregorio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The fifteenth-century church on the square of the village; the altarpiece painted in 1625 by Enea Salmeggia shows at the lower part the Turrita Gromo of the time.

  • Where Piazza Dante, Gromo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it

Chiesa dei Santi Giacomo e Vincenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The most important church of the village, outside the historic center: austere exterior with local stone porch and classic portal in white marble, inside with 16th century frescoes, paintings, wooden and marble statues, baptismal font of 1511, golden altar and seventeenth century reliquaries behind doors of golden copper.

  • Where Fuori dal centro storico, Gromo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it · visitbergamo.net

Torre Cittadini (degli Olivari)

History and architecture · Towers

11th-XII century tower in large square blocks of local stone, which guarded access to the mineral deposits and to the passages at altitude. The 14th century castle Priacini remains the tower of the Lavanderio, perhaps even older.

  • Where Gromo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa della Trinità a Ripa Alta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

The sixteenth-century church in the hamlet of Ripa Alta, from which starts the old mule track that descends to Gromo.

Spiazzi di Gromo

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint
