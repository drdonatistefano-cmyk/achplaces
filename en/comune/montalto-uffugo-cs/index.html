What to see in Montalto Uffugo

The village where Ruggiero Leoncavallo grew, which here set Pagliacci: in the Dominican complex of the fifteenth century, now Town Hall, there is the museum dedicated to the composer; above the ancient walls, on the hill of the Serrone, the Cathedral of the Madonna della Serra, founded in 1227.

Museo Ruggiero Leoncavallo

Museums · House museums

It is indicated by the Municipality as the only museum in Italy dedicated entirely to the composer, who lived in Montalto from the age of five and set Pagliacci, inspired by a tragic fact he had witnessed as a boy. It contains documents, scores and testimonies related to Mattinata, Zazà and I Medici; it is located in the Dominican complex of the fifteenth century, today the Town Hall. Open Wednesday-Saturday 10-12 and 16-18 (Touring Club); tel. 368 7368634. Every year the country dedicates a music festival to him.

Duomo della Madonna della Serra

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Sanctuary built in 1227 on the hill of the Serrone, outside the ancient walls, and consecrated by Bishop Andrea of San Marco; according to the most likely version reconstructs the old church of the Assumption damaged by earthquakes. Gradinata, balustrade in tuff and portal are by the architect Niccolò Ricciulli of Rogliano; after the earthquake of 12 February 1854 the Greek cross plan was transformed into a neoclassical Latin cross, with Baroque façade and city coat of arms. Inside the Byzantine-style wooden Madonna della Serra (XII-XV century), the Baroque altar of St. Francis, the wooden choir of Giovanbattista Altomare (XVIII century), the Holy Family of Rocco Ferrari and the Sacred Heart in marble by Giulio Barberis.

Chiesa e Chiostro di San Domenico

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Church and convent were started in 1456 and are among the oldest buildings in the historic center; from the seventeenth century they had various modifications, including the Baroque facade, while the ancient bell tower remained unchanged. The former Dominican monastery now houses the Town House and the Leoncavallo Museum. In the church an altarpiece with Dominican saints of Neapolitan workshop (XVII century) and an eighteenth-century painting of San Domenico resurrecting Napoleon Orsini.

Centro storico e palazzi nobiliari

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient town, saturated within the walls, expanded beyond the belt between the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries around two churches and the Palazzo dei Nardi (XVII century). In 1550 a bridge was filled to create Piazza Mercato, today Piazza Enrico Bianco; among the buildings the Canenea (XVII century), Alimena, D'Alessandro and Sacchini (XVI century). Outside the walls stood the Jewish district of Cafarò, closed on two sides for the shops, and the Waldensian one of Borgoltramontano along the road to the fields. In 1516 the San Francesco complex was built.
