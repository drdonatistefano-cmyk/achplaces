What to see in Isola della Scala

Country of the plain of Verona crossed by Tartar, the longest river of resurgence in Italy, Isola della Scala is the capital of rice Vialone Nano, with the Rice Fair born in 1967; to see the Scaligera Tower of Mastino II, the Romanesque Sanctuary of Madonna della Bastia (1126), the church of Santo Stefano and the Archaeological Museum in the former church of the Friars.

Museo archeologico di Isola della Scala

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

Inaugurated in 1978 thanks to the local archaeological association and transferred in 1999 to the former chapel of St. Anthony of the church of Santa Maria Maddalena, the ancient church of the Friars was built in 1511 and restored from 1986 to 2001 as auditorium. The only room follows a chronological path from the copper age to the Lombard age, with the materials of the inhabited middle Bronze of Giarella, the necropolis of the Iron Age of Sorgà and Palazzina and a Roman millionaire of Maxentius.

  • Where Via Roma 35/a, Isola della Scala
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.isoladellascala.vr.it

Sources: archeoveneto.beniculturali.unipd.it · comune.isoladellascala.vr.it

Santuario della Madonna della Bastia

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Romanesque church built in 1126 by Chiebizo, Wariento and Annone, as a façade inscription recalls; born with three naves, in the fifteenth century it was reduced to a single nave and enriched with the stone portal and the rose window. Inside there are frescoes, altars, a saint's waterworks of 1412 and an ancient wooden image of Our Lady; on the third Sunday of Lent there is the secular Festival of Bastia. Open on Monday afternoon for the 17:00 Mass and for events.

  • Where Via Bastia, Isola della Scala
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.isoladellascala.vr.it

Sources: comune.isoladellascala.vr.it

Torre Scaligera

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Medieval fortified building symbol of the village, built around the middle of the fourteenth century by Mastino II della Scala in defense against Mantua, on the shore of Tartaro along the road to Vigasio; it was part of the Serraglio Veronese, the defensive system that went from Lake Garda to Ostiglia.

  • Where Lungo il Tartaro, strada per Vigasio, Isola della Scala
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.isoladellascala.vr.it

Sources: comune.isoladellascala.vr.it

Chiesa di Santo Stefano (complesso abbaziale)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt in 1590 on the design of Brugnoli, Michele Sanmicheli's nephew, instead of a Romanesque church, of which remain the bell tower of 1130; he preserves an octagonal baptistery in marble of 1412 and paintings, including a canvas by Nicolò Giolfino (1476-1555).

  • Where Isola della Scala
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.isoladellascala.vr.it

Sources: comune.isoladellascala.vr.it

Fiume Tartaro e bosco urbano

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Tartaro, 147 km from the Risorgive of Povegliano Veronese to the Adriatic in Porto Levante, is the longest river of Risorgiva of Italy, mentioned by Plinio and Tacito and long border between Venice, Mantova and Ferrara; it crosses the center of the village, where there are two mills and an urban forest of several hectares, partly spontaneous and partly public park.

  • Where Centro, Isola della Scala
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.isoladellascala.vr.it

Sources: comune.isoladellascala.vr.it

Fiera del riso e risotto al Vialone Nano

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The Rice Fair, founded in 1967, hosts between September and October the Festa del risotto italiano, with the prize for the best risotto among restaurants, trattorias and private; you cook the Vialone Nano, IGP rice of the low Veronese, also in the typical risotto at the taste.

  • Where Palariso, Isola della Scala
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.isoladellascala.vr.it

Sources: visitverona.it · comune.isoladellascala.vr.it
