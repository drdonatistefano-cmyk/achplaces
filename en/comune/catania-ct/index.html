What to see in Catania

City of bichrome baroque stone lava and white marble at the foot of Etna, between the late Baroque towns of Val di Noto UNESCO World Heritage, with the Pescheria and the places of Vincenzo Bellini.

25 places to visit in Catania, in the province of Catania (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers 9 of the 18 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Piazza del Duomo e Fontana dell'Elefante

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The monumental living room of the Baroque city, with the Palazzo degli Elefanti seat of the town hall, the Fountain of the Amenano and the "Liotru," the lava stone elephant that holds an obelisk, symbol of Catania.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Catania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Cattedrale di Sant'Agata

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The cathedral on the piazza del Duomo, dedicated to the patron saint: since 1876 it houses the remains of Vincenzo Bellini in a funeral monument near a pillar of the right aisle and is home to the concerts of the Bellini Festival.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Catania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Badia di Sant'Agata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Baroque church next to the cathedral where every 23 September remembers the death of Bellini with a concert and where since April there is a musical season dedicated to the Italian opera tradition.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Catania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello Ursino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The castle founded by Frederick II of Swabia in the 13th century, now home to the rich Civic Museum of the city.

  • Where Catania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Palazzo Biscari

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The largest noble residence in Catania, with sumptuous salons that also host the Bellini Festival and the south façade, baroque triumph of Arabic and puttini.

  • Where Catania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Teatro Massimo Bellini

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Inaugurated in 1890 in Piazza Bellini, about 1200 seats and a perfect acoustic: ceiling frescoed by Ernesto Bellandi with the Apotheosis of Bellini and the historical curtain of Giuseppe Sciuti, which can also be visited with guided tours.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Perrotta 12, Catania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Via Crociferi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The scenic eighteenth-century road introduced by the arch of San Benedetto, an open-air museum of the etneo baroque with churches and convents, including San Francesco Borgia where Bellini was baptized; crossed on February 5 by the procession of Sant'Agata.

  • Where Via Crociferi, Catania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Arco di San Benedetto

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The arch that opens via Crociferi and joins the two sectors of the homonymous female monastery: according to legend it was built in one night.

  • Where Via Crociferi, Catania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Civico Belliniano

Museums · House museums

The museum in the birthplace of Vincenzo Bellini, in Palazzo Gravina Cruyllas, where the composer was born in November 1801: original rooms partly preserved and memories of his life.

  • Where Piazza San Francesco d'Assisi 3, Catania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Santuario di San Francesco all'Immacolata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The baroque church that closes the east side of Saint Francis of Assisi square, erected on a site occupied by an ancient pagan temple and a Franciscan church of the fourteenth century.

  • Where Piazza San Francesco d'Assisi, Catania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Teatro greco-romano e Odeon

History and architecture · Archaeology

First century A.D. lava stone theatre built on a Greek building, up to 7000 spectators, with the waters of the Amenano emerging in the orchestra; next to the Odeon for poetic competitions and the Antiquarium in Casa Pandolfo and Casa Liberti.

  • Where Via Vittorio Emanuele II 266, Catania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Anfiteatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built probably in the second century AD in lava stone covered with marble, elliptic of 125 for 105 meters, it could accommodate 15,000 spectators: today we see a section in Piazza Stesicoro.

  • Where Piazza Stesicoro, Catania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Monumento a Vincenzo Bellini

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The monument to the composer who for almost a century and a half dominates Piazza Stesicoro, enclosed among noble palaces, at the beginning of the walk towards Villa Bellini.

  • Where Piazza Stesicoro, Catania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Villa Bellini

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

One of the two oldest gardens and the four main parks of Catania, green lung at the bottom of Via Etnea with driveways, busts of illustrious men and floral compositions; the people called it "Laberinto."

  • Where Via Etnea, Catania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Via Etnea

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The most important street in the city, about 3 kilometers long, that climbs from Piazza del Duomo towards Etna among palaces, squares and shops.

  • Where Via Etnea, Catania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Sources: italia.it

Monastero dei Benedettini di San Nicolò l'Arena

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

One of the largest monastic complexes in Europe, a university since 1997 restored by Giancarlo De Carlo: Baroque facades by Antonino Amato, neoclassical staircase of honour, two cloisters, Roman domus, ancient kitchens with the Factory Museum and botanical gardens.

  • Where Piazza Dante Alighieri 32, Catania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Biblioteche Riunite Civica e Ursino Recupero

Museums · City history

The libraries founded in 1897 in the Benedictine Monastery, combining the bookstores of Benedictine fathers and other collections: over 270,000 volumes, illuminated codes, manuscripts, parchments and incunabula.

  • Where Piazza Dante Alighieri 32, Catania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: italia.it

Casa museo Giovanni Verga

Museums · House museums

The house of the father of Verism in the heart of the historic center, with original interiors and the rich private library, a stone's throw from the ancient theatre and Via Crociferi.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Cimitero monumentale

Places of worship · Monumental cemeteries · Free entry

The historic cemetery of the city, where Giovanni Verga has been resting for a century under a simple marble slab.

Catania sotterranea

History and architecture · Archaeology

The city is buried by lava flows under the old town centre: underground routes between the Roman thermal baths of the Address, the church of San Gaetano alle Grotte and the bed of the river Amenano, which can be visited with guided tours.

  • Where Catania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
