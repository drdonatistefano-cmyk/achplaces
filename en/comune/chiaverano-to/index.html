What to see in Chiaverano

Borgo Cittaslow on the dioritic hills of the Serra, overlooking the Morenic Amphitheatre of Ivrea; among the places to see the Romanesque Santo Stefano of Sessano with its medieval garden, the Sirius Lake, the parish baroque of San Silvestro and the museum of the Bötega del Frér.

Chiesa e area di Santo Stefano di Sessano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Romanesque church of the year about 1000 on a rocky spur overlooking the Morenic Amphitheatre: it is the only survivor of the village of Sessano, destroyed by a landslide in the 13th century. It preserves frescoes from the middle of the 11th century with the twelve apostles and St. Stephen, restored in 1999; reopened to the public in 1996 after the restorations of the seventies. Next to it, a medieval garden with six parcels of ancient plants, a pergola of Canavesian vines and a collection of rosemary trees, recognized by the Piedmont Region as Public Botanical Garden since 2009.

Lago Sirio

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest of the five glacial lakes of the Morenic Amphitheatre of Ivrea, between Chiaverano and Ivrea, and the only one fed by a source: it has no immissaries on the surface. The Piedmont Region has recognized it as a place of significant environmental interest and is one of the few bathing lakes in the area, with two establishments, free beach, tennis courts and restaurants. Along the banks were the traces of the Roman aqueduct of Ivrea.

  • Where Lago Sirio, Chiaverano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.chiaverano.to.it · turismotorino.org

Museo la Bötega del Frér

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Workshop of blacksmith ferraio of the early eighteenth century, only surviving among the artisan workshops of the country: in the first room the machines still work thanks to a hydraulic wheel. The forging room exhibits tools for iron processing, the last illustrates the construction of weights, sizes and staders; rich sample of locks and agricultural tools. Open on Sundays in the summer season (in 2025 from July 6 to October 19), free admission.

  • Where Via Ivrea 3, Chiaverano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Parrocchiale di San Silvestro, Santa Marta e campanile

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the 13th century next to the castle, the parish church was sacked by French troops and rebuilt in Baroque forms between 1741 and 1744; the portal depicts the patrons of St. Silvestro and St. Theodora and in the bezel of the small portico there is a fresco of the late fourteenth century with the Madonna and Child and saints. The 65-metre-high stone bell tower was completed in 1764. In front, the small church of Santa Marta with porch, built by the brotherhood of the same name in 1585.

  • Where Centro storico, Chiaverano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.chiaverano.to.it · turismotorino.org

Castello di San Giuseppe

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the top of Mount Albagna, in the middle of a morainic arch between small lakes and a park of 11 hectares. In 1640 the hermit Tomaso Barberio built a chapel in San Giuseppe; then became a convent of Teresiani and Carmelitani and sanctuary, was fortified in Napoleonic age and transformed into a noble residence, where Eleonora Duse, Arrigo Boito and Giuseppe Giacosa stayed at the end of the nineteenth century. Restored after long abandonment, today it is a hotel.

  • Where Località Monte Albagna, via Lago Sirio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.chiaverano.to.it · turismotorino.org
