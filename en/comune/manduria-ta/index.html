What to see in Manduria

Home to the Primitivo DOC and the ancient Messapian city: the Messapian Walls Archaeological Park with three belts, more than 1200 tombs and the Plinian Fountain, the Museum "Manduria," the Land of Messapi and a historic center with the Matrice Church and the ancient Jewish ghetto.

Parco archeologico delle Mura Messapiche

History and architecture · Archaeology

Three megalithic belts of the V, IV and III century B.C.: the inner one about 2 km long, the outer one about 5 meters thick, 6-7 high and 3 km long. It includes a necropolis of more than 1200 graves, the Plinian Fountain, perennial source in a large karst cavity perhaps cited by Pliny in Naturalis Historia, and the small church of St. Peter Mandurino with a frescoed underground crypt.

  • Where Piazza Scegnu, Manduria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · patrimoniosubacqueo.cultura.gov.it

Museo «Manduria, Terra di Messapi» nell'ex Convento degli Agostiniani

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in September 2017 in the former Convent of the Augustinians, under the protection of the National Superintendence for the Underwater Cultural Heritage, it holds many finds emerged from the excavations of the Messapian necropolises. According to the MiC card the visit of the cloister is on reservation.

  • Where Corso XX Settembre, Manduria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: patrimoniosubacqueo.cultura.gov.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo della civiltà del vino Primitivo

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

In the 19th century tanks with star vaults of the Winery Producers Wines of Manduria: two paths between tools and work of the fields and ancient machinery for vinification, with guided tours, tastings and events.

  • Where Via Fabio Massimo 19, Manduria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museodelprimitivo.it

Sources: museodelprimitivo.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Chiesa Matrice della SS. Trinità e ghetto ebraico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The mother church combines Romanesque and Gothic elements; in front of it an arch gives access to the ancient Jewish ghetto.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Centro storico e Palazzo Imperiali-Filotico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Alleys and squares with bars and restaurants; the Palazzo Imperiali-Pilotico rises on the remains of a Norman castle, and among the churches there are St. Anthony with the convent, St. Mary of Constantinople, St. Lucia, the Madonna of Carmine and St. Francis of Assisi.

  • Where Centro storico, Manduria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
