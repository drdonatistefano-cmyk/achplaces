What to see in Gradisca d'Isonzo

Fortress founded by the Republic of Venice in the fifteenth century on the right bank of the Isonzo against Turkish incursions, then capital of a Habsburg county: Venetian walls with towers and gates, Loggia dei Mercanti, Palazzo Torriani with the Spazzapan Gallery, historical squares and cafes.

8 places to visit in Gradisca d'Isonzo, in the province of Gorizia (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Mura venete, castello e centro storico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

15th century fortress built by the Venetians on the right bank of the Isonzo against Turkish invasions, to whose walls Leonardo da Vinci would have contributed; along the perimeter you can see six towers and two gates, Porta Nuova and Porta del Soccorso. Inside the walls the castle, whose core is the Palazzo del Capitano, the fifteenth-century Venetian House of Provveditori and streets porticoed with traditional coffee.

  • Where Centro storico, Gradisca d'Isonzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · turismofvg.it

Loggia dei Mercanti e Lapidario civico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Also known as Loggia Torriana, it was built in 1688 by Count Francesco Ulderico della Torre on a previous Venetian loggia of 1488; meeting place of the nobility and merchant seat with the public weight, since 1939 it hosts on the ground floor the civic stone, with the plaque that recalls the foundation of the Venetian fortress and the inscription of 1479 on Giovanni Emo and the citadel "Emopoli."

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Galleria regionale d'arte contemporanea Luigi Spazzapan

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Established in 1977 in Palazzo Torriani (1730, now also the seat of the Town Hall), it houses the most important collection of works by Luigi Spazzapan (Gradisca 1889 - Turin 1958), about 87 works from the collection of the Turinese Eugenio Giletti, displayed in rotation on the first floor; the second floor hosts temporary exhibitions. Free with FVGcard.

  • Where Via Marziano Ciotti 51, Gradisca d'Isonzo
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Museo documentario della città

Museums · City history · Free entry

Educational museum that traces in chronological order the history of Gradisca: fossils and geological evolution, Roman stone fragments, medieval and Renaissance weapons, coins, seventeenth-century paintings and Baroque frescoes from the Cathedral of the SS. Peter and Paul, with a section on the Jewish community present since the sixteenth century.

  • Where Casa Maccari, via della Campagnola 18, Gradisca d'Isonzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.comune.gradiscadisonzo.go.it

Sources: comune.gradiscadisonzo.go.it · turismofvg.it · cultura.gov.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Piazza Unità d'Italia e Leone di San Marco

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Born at the end of the nineteenth century after the partial demolition of the walls, it houses two works by Giovanni Battista Novelli: the Monument to the Redemption (1924), a marble column with the Lion of San Marco in memory of the Venetian foundation, with four bronze medallions, and the monument to Martiano Ciotti (1932). On the square overlook the historic cafes and the facade of the theatre of 1925.

  • Where Piazza Unità d'Italia, Gradisca d'Isonzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghibellifvg.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Teatro comunale

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Born between 1660 and 1670 as a barn, it was transformed into a theatre in 1792; almost destroyed in the Great War, it was rebuilt in 1923 with the porch on the square, reopened in 1929 and, after a long closure, returned to the public on April 21, 2009. Eleonora Duse and Mina performed there.

  • Where Piazza Unità d'Italia, Gradisca d'Isonzo
  • Entry To be checked

Sources: borghibellifvg.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Polveriera veneta

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Cylindrical powder bowl in stone blocks of about 7 meters in diameter, built between the end of the fifteenth and early sixteenth centuries with brick vault, to store dust and ammunition of the fortress; probably connected by tunnels to the towers. Recently restored, it is the only circular powder keg known in Friuli Venezia Giulia.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Palazzo Strassoldo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Among the oldest in the city, born between the Five and the seventeenth century joining more buildings, belonged to Count Rizzardo of Strassoldo, commander of the fortress during the wars of Gradiscan (1615-1617); remained almost intact, has a large courtyard and a monumental rectangular portal on Via Ciotti.

  • Where Calle Corona / via Ciotti, Gradisca d'Isonzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it
