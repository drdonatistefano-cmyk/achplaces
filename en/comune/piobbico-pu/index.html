What to see in Piobbico

Country 339 meters at the confluence of the Biscubio in the Candigliano, under Mount Nerone and Montiego, feud of the Brancaleoni counts that transformed the medieval fortress in the great Castle Brancaleoni, now home of the Civic Museum; surroundings of gorges, canyons and paths.

Castello Brancaleoni e Museo civico Brancaleoni

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortress of the thirteenth century on the rock ridge that dominates the village, transformed into a Renaissance residence between 1573 and 1587 by the counts Brancaleoni, lords of the place from 1213 to 1816. The courtyard of honour is Baccio Pontelli, the staircase is inspired by the Doge's Palace of Urbino; the Greek Chamber was frescoed in 1585 by Giorgio Picchi with stuccoes by Federico Brandani, author of the decorations of the Sala del Leon d'Oro; the clock tower turns counterclockwise. The Civic Museum has sections of uses and customs, speleology and geo-paleontology (more than 5000 fossils and the skeleton of a cave bear), ornithology, archaeology and numismatics. Open Saturday and Sunday 9-12 and 15-18.

Chiesa di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt in Baroque style by the Brancaleoni in 1784 after an earthquake; it preserves the altarpiece of the Rest of the Holy Family during the escape to Egypt by Federico Barocci and statues of prophets and biblical characters attributed to Federico Brandani.

Monte Nerone

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

With 1526 meters it is one of the highest peaks of the Umbrian-Marche Apennines: valleys, forre, natural arches and canyons in limestone, some caves that can be visited (in one were found cave bear bones, today in the civic museum). The summit is occupied by antennas and fenced; on the north-east side, between 1290 and 1430 meters, the ski resort with the Mandrion ski lift and 5 slopes.

  • Where Monte Nerone, Piobbico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it · turismo.comune.piobbico.pu.it

Fiume Candigliano e Fosso dell'Eremo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Candigliano flows north of the village between deep gorges and narrow valleys, with streams, waterfalls and mirrors of water, and is frequented for excursions, fishing and rafting; its tributary Fosso dell'Eremo has rocky walls loved by climbers. Above the valleys rise the Nero and Montiego (975 m), covered with beech, oak and conifers.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Fosso di Presale

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Torrente that descends from Monte Nerone in Candigliano; since 1960 its central stretch is a canyoning destination, with a path equipped with 12 descents, the highest of 25 meters. In the spring you will meet small tritons.

  • Where Monte Nerone, Piobbico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
