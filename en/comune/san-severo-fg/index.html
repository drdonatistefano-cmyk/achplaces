What to see in San Severo

City of art on the northern edge of the Tavoliere, several times the capital of the Capitanatata and rebuilt in Baroque forms after the earthquake of 1627: the historic center with the cathedral and churches of the seventeenth century, the Teatro Verdi and the MAT - Museum of the High Tablekeeper in the ancient convent of San Francesco.

MAT - Museo dell'Alto Tavoliere

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

In the former convent of San Francesco, attested from 1232 and rebuilt after the earthquake of 1627, from 1989 the seat of the Civic Museum: a path from prehistory to the Middle Ages with the daunis of the necropolis of Serpente, Pedincone and Casone (74 tombs excavated in 1970-71), a ceramic room apula with red and polychrome figures, a Roman lapidarium and a room on wheat, wine, oil and transhumance. It also houses the Pinacoteca "Luigi Schingo" and a large archive on the cartoonist Andrea Patienza.

  • Where Piazza San Francesco 48, San Severo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The castle Sancti Severini, mentioned already in 1116, bishop's seat since 1580, was severely damaged by the earthquake of 1627 and rebuilt with new churches and convents, with low buildings usually on two floors and a chessboard expansion beyond the walls. Around Piazza Municipio characteristic streets and gentle palaces with coats of arms, the Palazzo dei Celestini and the Episcopio founded in 1668. Since 2004 the Region has included it among the cities of art.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, San Severo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: catalogo.beniculturali.it · touringclub.it

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Chiese del centro storico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The churches rebuilt after 1627: the Baroque sanctuary of the Madonna del Soccorso, the Romanesque façade of San Severino, the wooden choir and the four statues of Ambrogio Piazza in San Nicola, the marbles and gildings of the church of Pietà, and San Lorenzo delle Benedettine, stages of the guided tours in the city.

Teatro comunale Giuseppe Verdi

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Elegant theatre with stage, audience and frescoed ceilings; it hosts mainly prose, from comedy to cabaret. Discount with the Touring card on the performances of the Apulian Public Theatre.
