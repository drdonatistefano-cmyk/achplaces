What to see in Ruvo di Puglia

City of the Murge on the border with the National Park of the Alta Murgia, ancient municipium on the Via Traiana and famous since ancient times for ceramics: the Romanesque cathedral of the XII-XIII centuries, the Jatta National Museum with the vase of Talos, the cave of San Cleto under the church of Purgatory and the convent of San Domenico with picture gallery and civic museum.

Cattedrale

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

An example of Romanesque Apulian from the XII to XIII centuries with grafts of Gothic taste: façade with very inclined spiovents with three portals, the central decorated with lions, winged griffins, symbols of the evangelists and twelve apostles facing Christ, and a rose window with twelve rays of columns and arches. Interior with three naves with fake matroneo and a fragment of 16th century fresco; under the church a path hypogeum with Roman remains, of the first building and ancient and medieval burials. Bell tower off.

  • Where Largo Cattedrale, Ruvo di Puglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Museo nazionale Jatta

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Rare private collection of the 19th century by the brothers Giovanni senior and Giulio Jatta, remained in the original layout, acquired by the State in 1990-1991: over 2,000 finds in four rooms on the ground floor of Palazzo Jatta, including about 700 vases with red figures and a masked crater of the fourth century BC attributed to the painter of Baltimore. In Room 4, the famous Talos vase (420-400 B.C. about), with the death of the giant guardian of Crete among the Dioscuri. Closed on Monday.

  • Where Piazza G. Bovio 25, Ruvo di Puglia
  • Entry €6, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Chiesa del Purgatorio e Grotta di San Cleto

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

A few meters from the cathedral, in the hypogeum of the church of Purgatory, the cave of San Cleto is an ancient cistern of Roman baths of the I-II century AD. Opening on request.

  • Where Largo S. Cleto, Ruvo di Puglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Centro storico e Torre dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

A dazzle of cobbled alleys with noble palaces of the sixteenth century, the Baroque churches of Sant'Angelo and San Domenico and the Clock Tower of 1604.

  • Where Centro storico, Ruvo di Puglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Pinacoteca comunale di arte contemporanea «Cantatore»

Museums · Modern art

On the ground floor of the former convent of San Domenico: works from the early twentieth century to the seventies by artists such as Grumo, Chieco, Di Terlizzi, Sacco, Brandonisio and van Westerhout, and the graphic collection by Domenico Singer with aquariums, etchings and lithographs; space for exhibitions and cloisters for events.

  • Where Via Madonna delle Grazie 2, Ruvo di Puglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo civico archeologico San Domenico

Museums · Archaeological

On the first floor of the former Dominican monastery outside the walls, in front of Porta Noè, built around 1560: left by the Dominicans in 1809, it was then school of the Scolopi since 1819 and home to various schools after 1866.

  • Where Via Madonna delle Grazie, Ruvo di Puglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
