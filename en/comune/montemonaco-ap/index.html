What to see in Montemonaco

Borgo dei Monti Sibillini overlooking Mount Vettore and Mount Sibilla, linked to the legend of the Sibilla: in the center the fourteenth-century civic tower and the church of San Benedetto Abate, from the hamlet of Foce you go up to Lake Pilate, the only natural glacial lake of the Marche.

6 places to visit in Montemonaco, in the province of Ascoli Piceno (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico, Piazza Roma e mura castellane

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

From Piazza Roma, a panoramic terrace, you can reach the Town Hall with the fourteenth-century civic tower and the church of San Biagio of the fifteenth-century; in the public gardens there are traces of the walls of the castle with three towers, then Porta San Biagio, the Monteguarnieri Park, Piazza Risorgimento and the remains of Porta San Lorenzo embedded in the walls.

the Park warns that the earthquakes of 2016 have damaged many historical buildings and some areas are closed; descriptions refer to before the earthquake

  • Where Piazza Roma, Montemonaco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sibillini.net

Sources: sibillini.net

Chiesa di San Benedetto Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the thirteenth century that preserves a polychrome wooden crucifix of the late fifteenth century, a fresco of Crivellesque school with Christ crucified between the Virgin and Saint Lucia and a silver reliquary of the sixteenth century with relics of the arm of Saint Benedict.

the Park warns that the earthquakes of 2016 have damaged many historical buildings and some areas are closed

  • Where Centro storico, Montemonaco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sibillini.net

Sources: sibillini.net

Museo della Grotta della Sibilla (Villa Curi)

Museums · Themed

Museo di arte sacra (Musei Sistini del Piceno)

Museums · Old masters

It collects many wooden sculptures of the territory; among the paintings the Madonna del Soccorso di Giulio Vergari of 1521, a Madonna del Rosario of the late sixteenth century and a altarpiece of Saints Biagio and Antonio Abate, recently found and attributed to Pier Leone Ghezzi (1674-1755).

  • Entry To be checked

Lago di Pilato

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

It is reached from the hamlet of Foce: at 1.940 meters, it is the only natural basin of glacial origin of the Marche. Medieval legends placed the bones of the Roman prosecutor who condemned Jesus; in his living waters the Cirocephalus marcasonii, a small crustacean that does not exist anywhere else in the world.

  • Where Foce, Montemonaco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sibillini.net

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · sibillini.net

Gola dell'Infernaccio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Gorge of the Sibillini reached with a walk of about 2 hours, 6 km and 250 meters of difference in altitude, also offered in autumn excursions among the centuries-old chestnut groves of Montemonaco.

  • Where Montemonaco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
