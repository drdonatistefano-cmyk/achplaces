What to see in Grottaminarda

The centre of the Ufita valley, built around the Castello D'Aquino, is a medieval fortress that has become a noble residence that now houses the Antiquarium Filippo Buonopane, and the Mother Church of Santa Maria Maggiore, rebuilt in the eighteenth century with a 36-metre bell tower.

Castello D'Aquino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Probably early medieval fortress, enlarged in the first half of the 12th century with the walls of the village documented since 1137; after the earthquakes of 1694 and 1732 it was partially transformed into a noble residence with a hanging garden and a view over the valley of the Palombara. A trapezoidal plan, it preserves a cylindrical tower of about 14 meters, the base of a square tower, two other cylindrical towers, walkways and vaulted underground; it is of the Municipality since 1988.

  • Where Via Castello 1, Grottaminarda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.grottaminarda.av.it

Antiquarium Filippo Buonopane

Museums · Archaeological

In Castello D'Aquino, it collects findings from donations and random finds from prehistory to late Middle Ages: neolithic silci and obsidian, italic materials related to the Irpini and Magna Grecia, Roman objects from nearby Aeclanum and medieval ceramics, plus an ethno-anthropological section with sacred objects in wood and objects of the twentieth century.

The MiC tab indicates closed every day; contact 0825 426061 or antiquarium@virgilio.it

  • Where Piazza Vittoria, Grottaminarda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Chiesa Madre di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Collegiate in 1478, destroyed by the earthquake of 1732 and rebuilt in the second half of the eighteenth century on a project by Ciriaco di Silva, pupil of Vaccaro; the 36-metre bell tower (1752-1766) is attributed to design by Vanvitelli. It preserves a canvas by Antonio Sarnelli (1766), the fresco of the Assumption by Matteo Vigilante (1768), a table of the Madonna del Rosario of 1573 perhaps of Flemish school, the main altar in polychrome marble (1761) and an organ by Michele Vigilante of 1779.

  • Where Via Castello, Grottaminarda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.grottaminarda.av.it

Sources: comune.grottaminarda.av.it
