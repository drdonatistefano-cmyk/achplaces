What to see in Nusco

The balcony of Irpinia is a medieval village on a ridge between the valleys of Ofanto and Calore, with the Cathedral of Sant'Amato, the churches of the centre, the remains of the Longobard castle and the Abbey of Fontigliano.

6 places to visit in Nusco, in the province of Avellino (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale di Sant'Amato

Places of worship · Cathedrals

The original nucleus dates back to the 11th century; of the Romanesque church the crypt remains, with columns holding vaults on cruising, where the bones of the saint are preserved and where frescoes of the Nativity were discovered dating back to around 1500. The three-nave interior dates from the 13th and 16th centuries, with 18th-century frescoes in the choir, a 17th-century wooden pulpit and a 33-metre high 16th-century bell tower; on the square stands the 1760 Episcopal Seminary.

Chiese della SS. Trinità, di San Giuseppe e di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Church of the SS. Trinity is known for its medieval frescoes, that of St. Joseph for the late Baroque façade; the Church of St. Anthony, at the northern entrance of the village, has a portal coming from the cathedral.

  • Where Centro storico, Nusco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Centro storico e resti del castello longobardo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village retains a medieval appearance with paved streets in limestone; in the streets Landone, Scarpitti and Trinità you can see stone portals, wrought iron balconies, cisterns and residences of the Jewish families, De Paulis and Del Giudice. The village was probably born around a fortress: the remains of the Longobard castle are visible near the Upper Gate.

  • Where Centro storico, Nusco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Belvedere di Porta Molino e via Coste

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Nusco rises on a narrow ridge between the valleys of Ofanto and Calore, from which the name of the "balcony of Irpinia" is named; from Porta Molino and via Coste you can enjoy wide views of the surrounding mountains.

  • Where Porta Molino, Nusco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Abbazia di Fontigliano e Antiquarium

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The Abbey was founded in the 12th century and rebuilt in 1950; it houses an Antiquarium with augustian epigraphs, admired by Theodor Mommsen.

  • Where Fontigliano, Nusco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Osservatorio della Biodiversità

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Recent structure in the southern part of the country, established by the Municipality, the Park and the Community Montana Termino-Cervialto to protect the environment and study the animal and plant species of the Regional Park of the Picentini Mountains.
