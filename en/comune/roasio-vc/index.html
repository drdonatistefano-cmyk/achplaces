What to see in Roasio

The village with its suitcase, Roasio tells in the Museum of the Emigrant the departures of its inhabitants from the late eighteenth century, especially towards Africa; on a hill above Roasio San Maurizio remain the ruins of a medieval tower of the XII-XIII century.

2 places to visit in Roasio, in the province of Vercelli (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo dell'emigrante

Museums · Themed

Opened on 22 April 2001 at the initiative of Velia Micheletti and a group of children of emigrants, it collects personal cards, photographs, documents, letters, passports and old suitcases of Roasians from the end of the eighteenth century. The route reconstructs the first migrations to Africa and the figure of D'Alberto, a pioneer who worked on railways and roads and was nicknamed King of the Golden Coast, with traces of the port of Takoradi, the bridge over Zambesi and the Independence House of Lagos. From May to October open Saturday and Sunday 15-18; other days by reservation (tel. 0163 87818 / 333 7232028).

Resti della Torre del Castello

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Ruins a few meters high of a square tower of about 4,50 m of side, with square stones at the corners and pebbles arranged with fish spine, masonry dating back to the XII-XIII century: it is the only material testimony of a fortification in Roasio San Maurizio. It is located on a hill at the edge of the village, a few hundred meters north of the parish of San Maurizio.

The Municipality describes the site as totally in abandonment and ruin in slow unraveling

  • Where Colle a nord della parrocchiale di San Maurizio, Roasio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.roasio.vc.it
