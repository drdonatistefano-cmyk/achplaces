What to see in Ghilarza

The village where Antonio Gramsci spent his childhood and adolescence, today remembered by the House museum on the main street; around, the Aragonese tower of the fifteenth century, the Romanesque San Palmerio in two-tone stones, St. Peter of Zuri rebuilt stone by stone over Lake Omodeo and the nineteenth-century village of San Serafino.

Casa museo di Antonio Gramsci

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The two-storey house of the first nineteenth century, in basalt, where Gramsci lived from 1898 to 1911 before leaving for the University of Turin; national monument with Law 207/2016. On the ground floor there is the good room with the reproduction of the letter to the mother of 1928 and the prison notebooks available on touch screen, the kitchen with ceiling in cannitzada and well, the courtyard with the oven; on the first floor photos, letters, personal objects, toys built for wife and children and the reconstruction of the prison cell of Turi. Book by phone or e-mail.

  • Where Corso Umberto I 57, Ghilarza
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.casamuseogramsci.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: casamuseogramsci.it · sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di San Palmerio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the first quarter of the thirteenth century, mentioned in the Condaghe of Santa Maria di Bonarcado as a place where the audiences were held to settle the disputes. Originally a single nave with wooden roof and bell tower; in the seventeenth century it had transept and square presbytery, losing the semicircular apse. The three-mirror façade alternates rows of dark basalt and reddish volcanic stone; hanging arches in part with acute sixth.

  • Where Periferia di Ghilarza, lungo la SP 15 da Abbasanta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di San Pietro di Zuri

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval church in red-ochite ochre, with a single nave with sail bell tower: the inscription on the façade recalls the consecration in 1291 after the renewal desired by the abbess Sardigna de Lacon, with Gothic changes in 1368. It was located in the old village of Zuri, today submerged by Lake Omodeo and visible in the periods of drought: since 1924, with the construction of the dam, it was dismantled and rebuilt with the bottom, returning to use after 28 days.

  • Where Frazione Zuri, Ghilarza (SP 27 e SP 28 da Abbasanta)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Villaggio di San Serafino

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Sanctuary village with a church perhaps of Byzantine origin, surrounded by a novenary of more than one hundred muristenes, the houses built in the seventeenth century to accommodate the faithful in the nine days of the feast of the saint.

  • Where San Serafino, Ghilarza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Lago Omodeo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

For a long time, the largest artificial invader in Europe, built in 1923 barring the Tirso: today's naturalistic attraction of Barigadu, with a great work of engineering; under its waters is the old village of Zuri, which resurfaces during drought periods.

  • Where Lago Omodeo, Ghilarza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
