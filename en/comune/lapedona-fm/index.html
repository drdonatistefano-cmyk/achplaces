What to see in Lapedona

Medieval hillside village mentioned since 1148 and passed to Fermo in the thirteenth century; you enter from the Porta Marina with pointed arches and Ghibelline blackbirds, towards the sixteenth-century municipal palace with a Roman cippo and the church of San Nicolò from the large wooden ceiling.

5 places to visit in Lapedona, in the province of Fermo (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Porta Marina e centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town was built at the beginning of the fourteenth century with the castle; the castrum, quoted in 1148, was assigned to Fermo in 1214 by Aldobrandino d'Este. It is accessed by the well-preserved Porta Marina, with a pointed arch and ghibelline blackbirds.

  • Where Porta Marina, Lapedona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Palazzo comunale e cippo romano

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Palace of sixteenth-century plant with portico with irregular arches, under which is preserved a Roman funerary cyp with inscriptions and bas-reliefs. According to the MiC, today the altarpiece of Simone De Magistris from San Nicolò is kept there.

  • Where Centro storico, Lapedona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · cultura.gov.it

Chiesa di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of 1585, with facade rebuilt around 1728 with the coat of arms of the Paccaroni, today deconsecrated and used for events as classical music concerts. It has a wooden ceiling painted in tempera in the eighteenth century, attributed to the stop Filippo Ricci and indicated as the most extensive of the genus in the Marches, five wooden altars gilded and marbled, blades attributed to G. Ghezzi and F. Ricci is an organ of the callidian school; the canvas of Simone De Magistris with the Madonna, the Child and Saints is today in the municipal palace.

MiC card indicates closed every day and the church is in MiC database of abandoned cultural assets with mediocre conservation status

  • Where Piazza Leopardi, Lapedona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · beniabbandonati.cultura.gov.it · letsmarche.it

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Giacomo e Quirico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish church with neoclassical portal that houses two valuable wooden sculptures; the church of San Quirico is already mentioned in 1148 inside the castle.

  • Where Centro storico, Lapedona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
