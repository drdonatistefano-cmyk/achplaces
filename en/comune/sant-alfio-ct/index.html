What to see in Sant'Alfio

Country on the eastern side of Etna, born in the early eighteenth century around the Mother Church in lava stone, famous for the Castagno dei Cento Cavalli, estimated over 3000 years, and for the nearby Castagno della Nave.

Castagno dei Cento Cavalli

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

Castagno with three stems of an estimated age over 3000 years, 19.42 m tall and with a circumference of 10.51 m, considered the oldest known; according to tradition, Queen Joan of Aragon recovered with her knights during a storm, and Goethe and other travelers described it. Chiesa Madre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The most interesting monument of the village, around which was born in the early eighteenth century the first inhabited nucleus; it has the façade in lava stone and since 1811 is sacramental church by decision of the Archbishop of Messina. The Metropolitan City dates back to the seventeenth century, italia.it places its construction between the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries.

  • Where Centro, Sant'Alfio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cmct.cittametropolitana.ct.it · italia.it

Chiese del Calvario e di Nucifora

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The Church of Calvary was built in 1885 on a hill next to the village; the Church of Nucifora was built by the inhabitants of the homonymous neighborhood, devoted to Our Lady of Tindari, without aid or subsidies.

  • Where Sant'Alfio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cmct.cittametropolitana.ct.it · italia.it
