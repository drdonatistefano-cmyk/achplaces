What to see in Grandate

Small town of the lower comasca, about 3,000 inhabitants, known for the fifteenth-century Sanctuary of the Madonna del Noce and for the Museum of the Horse toy, set up in the former stables Tornese.

2 places to visit in Grandate, in the province of Como (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo del Cavallo giocattolo

Museums · Themed

Museum presented as unique in the world, dedicated to toy horses and hosted in the former Tornese stables; recommended in particular for children.

  • Entry To be checked

Santuario della Madonna del Noce

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Sanctuary of Santa Maria Bambina, better known as Madonna del Noce, dating back to 1470; it was for a period parish church and was expanded in 1615. The hut façade has a portal surmounted by a bezel with high relief terracotta.

  • Where Via Madonna della Noce, Grandate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
