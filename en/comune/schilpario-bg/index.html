What to see in Schilpario

Country of the Valle di Scalve in the Prealpi Orobie, for centuries linked to iron: the Bonicelli mining park in the galleries of Gaffione, small museums of local history and the ring in the pine forest for cross-country skiing.

6 places to visit in Schilpario, in the province of Bergamo (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parco minerario Andrea Bonicelli – Miniere del Gaffione

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

In the gallery of Gaffione, about 1,244 meters and 4 km from the village, a guided tour of about 3 km in the iron mines closed in 1972: the first 2 km by train, then on foot between rails, wagons and gerle used by the boys; inside there are about 7°C.

  • Where Gaffione, Schilpario
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo etnografico

Museums · Themed

Opened in 1986, it collects work tools, household and emigration objects of the twentieth century, wool and linen looms and a small room on mines and miners.

  • Where Schilpario
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
