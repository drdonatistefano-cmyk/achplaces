What to see in Vittoria

City with a chessboard plan with a Liberty centre around Via Cavour and Piazza del Popolo, where stands the neoclassical theatre named after the founder Vittoria Colonna; home of the Cerasuolo di Vittoria, the only Sicilian DOCG, and the door of the reserve of Pino d'Aleppo.

5 places to visit in Vittoria, in the province of Ragusa (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Teatro Vittoria Colonna e Piazza del Popolo

History and architecture · Historic theatres

The neoclassical theatre on Piazza del Popolo, indicated as a remarkable example of neoclassical art, is named after Victoria Colonna, founder of the city.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Vittoria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Centro Liberty e pianta a scacchiera

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Vittoria, the most recent city in the province, has a chessboard plan and a historic centre with a Liberty area that revolves around Via Cavour and Piazza del Popolo.

  • Where Via Cavour e Piazza del Popolo, Vittoria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Museo civico polivalente Virgilio Lavore

Museums · City history

In the castle Colonna Enriquez: a gallery with 60 works, 49 historiographic panels on the territory, an ornithological collection and examples of theater machinery for special effects; audioguides available.

  • Where Via Cavour, Vittoria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitsicily.info

Riserva naturale Pino d'Aleppo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Reserve of about 3,000 hectares between Vittoria, Comiso and Ragusa, established in 1990 to protect what is considered the last spontaneous settlement of Aleppo pine in Sicily; it includes pine forest, Mediterranean scrub, coastal ponds and the final stretch of the river Ippari, with 32 species of orchids and herons, garzettes and knights of Italy in step.

  • Where Valle del fiume Ippari, Vittoria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: orbs.regione.sicilia.it · visitsicily.info

Cerasuolo di Vittoria

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Vittoria is linked to the only DOCG on the island, the Cerasuolo di Vittoria, the grapes of Nero d'Avola and Frappato; the wineries of the territory are visited to know the vinification.

  • Where Territorio di Vittoria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info
