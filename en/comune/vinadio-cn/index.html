What to see in Vinadio

The main center of the Upper Stura Valley, dominated by the Albertino Fort wanted by Carlo Alberto (1834-1847); from its territory you climb to the Sanctuary of Sant'Anna, at 2,035 meters, starting point of the tour of the alpine lakes, and you reach the thermal baths of Bagni di Vinadio.

Forte Albertino (Forte di Vinadio)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Fortress wanted by King Carlo Alberto, built from 1834 to 1847 with a break between 1837 and 1839: it develops for about 10 km on three levels (Front Superior, Front of Attack, Lower Front). In the Great War it became a prison for Austrian soldiers, then a warehouse; abandoned after 1950, it was restored by the City since the late 1980s. Today it hosts the multimedia route "Mountain in motion" with virtual reality and itineraries for families, guided tours between bastions and galleries; at the gates two large statues of the Scottish David Mach and at the feet an artificial lake with free access. Price of the free routes in 2024, free with Museums Subscription; seasonal opening.

  • Where Vinadio
  • Entry €8, reduced €6
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · fondazioneartea.org

Santuario di Sant'Anna di Vinadio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 2,035 meters, 17 km from the village, it is described as the highest sanctuary in Europe reachable by car. Born as an alpine shelter for pilgrims coming from Provence, it is still a pilgrimage destination: near the rock of the apparition, with the statue of the saint, tradition wants St. Anne to appear to the shepherd Anna Bagnis; next to there are the San Gioachino refuge and the ancient house of the rancher, the guardian of the pilgrims.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · visitpiemonte.com

Giro dei laghi di Sant'Anna

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring that starts from the sanctuary and touches a dozen mountain lakes and four hills, crossing briefly into France. From the nearby Strepeis there is also a trek of about 22 km (difficulty E, about 1,450 m of difference in altitude, in two days) that passes from the Passo di Bravaria, from the Lake of Sant'Anna (2,167 m) and from the Passo di Tesina (2,293 m); season recommended from the end of June to July, when snowmen melt.

  • Where Santuario di Sant'Anna, Vinadio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Terme di Vinadio

Local life and traditions · Spas

Thermal complex at 1,320 meters in the upper Stura Valley, with sulphurous waters indicated among the hottest of the Italian thermal panorama, coming from basins between 2,400 and 2,600 meters above sea level.
