What to see in Villadossola

Middle town of Valle Ossola, starting point of Via Antronesca, grew up with the Ceretti industry since 1796: the Romanesque church of San Bartolomeo, the Napoleonic Bridge on the Ovesca, the Historical Museum of the Resistance and the ancient hamlets of Noga and Dream.

8 places to visit in Villadossola, in the province of Verbano-Cusio-Ossola (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Cited in 1289 as dedicated to the Saints Fabiano and Sebastiano, it takes the name of San Bartolomeo in 1360; some constructive characters would date back to the middle of the 10th century. Romanesque remains are the bell tower (probably from the 11th century), the apse, with fragments of frescoes from the 15th century, and the central aisle; at the beginning of the 16th century it was enlarged with the left aisle and the façade was rebuilt using ancient shelves and capitals. Visit on request to the parish house (Via Don Minzoni 5).

  • Where Via San Bartolomeo, Parco della Rimembranza, Villadossola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Ponte Napoleonico

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The bridge over the Ovesca stream, on the ancient Via Francisca, has a history of collapses and reconstructions: a contract for a bridge with three arches in 1442, the great flood of 1588 that buried the crypt of Santa Maria del Piaggio, the reconstruction with two arches of 1627. In 1800, with the Napoleonic road of the Sempione, it was enlarged with a new arch flanked; until 1930 the road of the Sempione passed only from here.

  • Where Corso Italia, sul torrente Ovesca, Villadossola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villadossola.vb.it

Museo storico della Resistenza

Museums · City history · Free entry

Set up by the former partisans of the ANPI after three years of collection and inaugurated in 1983, it tells the Resistenza in Ossola with hundreds of documents and photographs on the formations Garibaldi, Valtoce and Valdossola, photos of the fallen of the province, didactic paintings and materials on deportation, including a model of the crematorium of Dachau. Open from September to July only by reservation (tel. 340 5314140), closed in August.

  • Where Via XXV Aprile 30, Villadossola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villadossola.vb.it

Frazione Sogno

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The highest hamlet and one of the oldest in Villadossola, mentioned in documents as "Scogno": the position on a fertile shelf protected by overhangs suggests an ancient castle, where in times of danger the inhabitants of the valley bottom brought families, cattle and goods. There are the oratory of St. John the Evangelist and the Museum of peasant civilization. You reach it by mule tracks and steps.

Villa Lena e Villa Lidia

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Two noble villas among high ash trees, a long residence of the Ceretti family, which in 1796 founded the "Pietro Maria Ceretti" (Pietro Maria Ceretti) by starting the Villadossola industry. In November 1943 Villa Lena, German garrison, was conquered by the insurgents and then resumed; it retains the signs of the bombing of the Junkers 88, remembered by a stone. Not available inside: they can be seen from the outside.

  • Where Via San Maurizio 2, Villadossola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villadossola.vb.it

Percorso di pesca e naturalistico dell'Ovesca

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hiking trail (grade E) of about 3 km and 190 m of difference in altitude along the Ovesca stream, from the square of San Bartolomeo to the hamlet Boschetto, among deep waters appreciated for sport fishing. Touch the church of San Bartolomeo, the Napoleonic Bridge, the church of Piaggio, the church of Noga and the hydroelectric power station, with bulletin boards, panoramic points and parking areas.

  • Where Via San Bartolomeo (partenza), Villadossola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villadossola.vb.it
