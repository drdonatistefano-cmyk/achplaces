What to see in Giaglione

Municipality of the Valley of Susa at the entrance of the wild Val Clarea, with the fifteenth-century Canal of Maria Bona dug into the rock, the Barricades of Clarea at the old border between Savoia and Delfinato, the Fort Combe of 1891 and the Museum of Alpine religious art next to the parish church of San Vincenzo.

7 places to visit in Giaglione, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo di arte religiosa alpina

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Founded in 1994 in the premises next to the parish church of San Vincenzo Martire, on an ancient place of worship overlooking the Valley of Susa, it was born from the exhibition "Valle di Susa": art and history from the 11th to the 18th century in 1977; since 2001 it is part of the diocesan museum system. It collects works from the numerous chapels of the territory: above all wooden statuary from the 15th to the 20th century, with seventeenth-century pieces of the Moriana carving schools (Clappier of Bessan, Claude Simon of Bramans), goldsmiths and sacred vestments. The visit also includes the church; openings on dates indicated by the Diocesan Cultural Centre.

  • Where Frazione S. Andrea 1, Giaglione
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.giaglione.to.it

Sources: comune.giaglione.to.it · turismotorino.org

Lou Gran Blalhìe – Canale di Maria Bona

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Channel about 500 meters long excavated in the overhanging walls of the left side of the Clarea Valley, up to about 300 meters high, to bring to the village the waters of the Clarea stream (about 550 liters per second). Designed already in the thirteenth century and completed in 1400, it takes its name from the noblewoman Maria Bona, wife of the feudal Andrea Aschieri, who according to tradition donated a gold emina to build it; the plaque dedicated to her is from 1914. Next to it runs a flat path, with stretches to be followed with caution close to the walls; audioguide in Italian and patois.

Vèrtsaoure – Val Clarea

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Vallone is closed and wild, without outlet on other valleys, enclosed by the Gran Rotsa wall, the Giusalet massif, the Ambin group and the buttresses of the Chiomonte teeth. There are the Alpine villages of Buttigliera and Pietraporchera with huts and grange; from here start the paths to Colle Clapier and Vallone di Toullie. The municipality reports its autumn colors.

  • Where Da Pian delle Ruine verso la Val Clarea, Giaglione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.giaglione.to.it

Barricate di Clarea

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Defensive systems in the lower part of Val Clarea, at the confluence with the Dora Riparia, on the border with Chiomonte: the stream marked the border between Savoia and Delfinato until the Treaty of Utrecht (1713). Cited from 1303, the steps were forced several times: in 1537 by the troops of Francis I, in 1590 by the French of the Lesdiguières; in 1628 Carlo di Castellamonte and Tommaso Stanio planned new barricades and trenches, surpassed on March 6, 1629 by the army of Louis XIII and Richelieu.

  • Where Bassa Val Clarea, al confine con Chiomonte (bivio dalla SS 25 per Pian delle Ruine), Giaglione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.giaglione.to.it

Sources: comune.giaglione.to.it

Forte Combe

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fort built in 1891 at over 700 meters above sea level against possible attacks from the Moncenisio, in control of the Val Cenischia and the Dora valley; perhaps remaking of a fourteenth-century fortification called "Castelluzzo" (Castelluzzo). Of almost triangular plant, it could accommodate 150 men and had a metal sliding caponier with machine gun, a unique in the Valley of Susa. After the Great War, it was a deposit of bullets, then abandoned around the mid-Twentieth century and sold by the State to private individuals.

Big Bench n. 142

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Giant bench n. 142 of the project by Chris Bangle, on the top of one of the two morainic hills south of the village, overlooking the mountains of the Alpine arch of the Valley of Susa. On the other side of the hill, just below, stands the manor house of Menate, quoted from 1345; at the foot of the slope flows the Dora Riparia.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Mulini di Ciareina

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

In the hamlet of Ciareina the Clarea stream fed five mills, still visible and partly preserved, in a wild environment with pools between polished rocks.
