What to see in Castell'Alfero

Country of the Lower Monferrato in the Versa valley, about 10 km north of Asti, dominated by the castle of the Counts Amico, transformed into a Baroque residence by Benedetto Alfieri, with the museum 'L Ciar in the underground; in the territory the Romanesque Madonna della Snow and, in Serra Perno, the Roseto della Surpresa with over 500 botanical roses. Here he was born in 1774 Giovan Battista De Rolandis, creator of the Tricolor.

Castello dei Conti Amico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The oldest part dates back to 1290, when the Municipality of Asti had it built as a fortification; in the seventeenth century the counts Amico transformed it into a residence and at the beginning of the eighteenth century Benedetto Alfieri, uncle of Vittorio, made it a baroque residence, with a large central niche façade similar to those of the palaces Mazzetti of Asti and Ghilini of Alexandria and a terrace on lowered arches. The Salone Verde, with hand-painted Vietri ceramic floor, hosts exhibitions and chamber concerts; the Salone Rosso, an ancient hall of representation, is the seat of the City Council.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
