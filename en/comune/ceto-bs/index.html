What to see in Ceto

Municipality of the Camonica Valley formed by Ceto, Nadro and Badetto; Nadro is the main entrance of the Nature Reserve of the rock engravings of Ceto, Cimbergo and Paspardo, the largest protected archaeological area of the valley, with the area of the Foppe di Nadro, the educational museum and the thirteenth-century tower of the village.

Riserva naturale incisioni rupestri – Foppe di Nadro

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The reserve, established in 1983, covers about 300 hectares between Ceto, Cimbergo and Paspardo and is one of the eight stages of rock art camuna, UNESCO heritage since 1979. In Ceto there is the area of the Foppe di Nadro, with 84 engraved rocks identified, about twenty along the visit: a ring of about 2 km, largely cobbled, between thick surfaces engraved from the 5th millennium BC to the high Middle Ages, with weapons of the Bronze Age and warriors of the medium Iron Age; along the path a Tibetan bridge and, at the entrance, an educational area with simulated excavation and reconstructions of a neolithic hut and a retica house. Simple but not equipped for disabled engines, about 3 hours; starting from the museum of Nadro, with free parking at about 200 m; single ticket - Vale Camonica Pass Engravings is available online.

  • Where Via Piana 29, Nadro di Ceto
  • Entry €5 (book of the Reserve), free up to 5 years
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website arterupestre.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: arterupestre.it · turismovallecamonica.it · italia.it

Museo didattico della Riserva

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

In a manor house of the sixteenth century with loggia on a courtyard closed by a wall, in front of the square of Nadro: educational exhibition on rock art and Camuni culture, with reconstructions and panels and, since 2022, two rooms on neolithic weaving and on ceramics with documentaries and finds reconstructed in experimental archaeology; it houses unique ticket office and information of the Reserve, bookshop, library-archive and refreshment area. Open all year round, closed on Tuesday, December 25 and 26, January 1 and Easter: from April to October 9-17:30, from November to March weekdays 10-14, Saturday and Sunday 9-16; accessible to people with disabilities.

  • Where Via Piana 29, Nadro di Ceto
  • Entry €5 (unique ticket), free up to 5 years
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website arterupestre.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: arterupestre.it · comune.ceto.bs.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Torre di Nadro

History and architecture · Towers

13th-century tower in via Voltazzi, in the village of Nadro, to which was later flanked the noble house of the Gaioni family; the village is, with Ceto and Badetto, one of the three localities of the municipality and the gateway to the Engraving Reserve.

  • Where Via Voltazzi, Nadro di Ceto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismovallecamonica.it · italia.it
