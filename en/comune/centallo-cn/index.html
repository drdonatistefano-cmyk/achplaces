What to see in Centallo

Municipality of the Cuneo plain: in the hamlet Roata Chiusani the small museum house dedicated to Cardinal Michele Pellegrino, born in 1903; in the capital, in the Civic Library, the Sala della Memoria with the permanent exhibition on the Italian Military Interiors.

2 places to visit in Centallo, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Casa museo Pellegrino

Museums · House museums

A small museum and documentation centre dedicated to Cardinal Michele Pellegrino (born in 1903), his literary work and his pastoral activity. Visitable every day of the year according to the activities of the parish of San Bernardo Abate; it is advisable to book at least one day before (tel. +39 0171 719329).

  • Where Piazza Cardinal Pellegrino, Roata Chiusani, Centallo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.centallo.cn.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · comune.centallo.cn.it

Sala della Memoria

Museums · City history

In the Civic Library "Faustino Dalmazzo" is the permanent exhibition "Faustino Dalmazzo" held by the Committee of Memory (born in 2005 in the association "Centallo Viva"), dedicated to the Italian Military Interiors, including the archpriest Don Giuseppe Barbero, a former military chaplain in the Second World War. The Committee shall indicate its opening to the public on Monday 10-12 and 17-19 and Friday 17-19 (tel. OJ L 338, 878, 2475).

  • Where Biblioteca civica, Via Garelli 11, Centallo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.centallo.cn.it

Sources: comune.centallo.cn.it
