Ponte della Maddalena o del Diavolo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval bridge with an asymmetrical profile, with a large central arch and three minor arches, which legend wants built with the help of the devil.

  • Where Ponte della Maddalena, Borgo a Mozzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa di San Jacopo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the XI-XII century, rebuilt in the sixteenth-sixth century, with source of 1590, Robbian terracotta and a group of the Annunciation.

  • Where Borgo a Mozzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
