What to see in Torre de' Passeri

Country of the valley of Pescara that takes its name from the "Turris Passum" (Turris Passum), ford tower in the borders of the abbey of San Clemente in Casauria; today it is known for the Castello Gizzi, seat since 1979 of the House of Dante in Abruzzo, and for the Dante Pinacoteca.

Castello Gizzi e Casa di Dante in Abruzzo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The first nucleus of the year Maybe Mille, linked to the defensive towers of San Clemente in Casauria; the present building was born from the works started on 17 December 1719 by the Mazara di Sulmona (Marchesa Smeralda) and lost a floor with the earthquake of 1915. Acquired by the Gizzi family in 1967, since 1979 it hosts the Institute of Studies and Research Casa di Dante in Abruzzo with Dante's Museum, Caldora Library, annual exhibitions on Divine Comedy, the tower and a panoramic park.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Pinacoteca dantesca "F. Bellonzi"

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Collection of works inspired by the Divine Comedy, born from donations and legacies followed by the exhibitions of the Iconographic Museum Dantesco; among the authors present Sassu, Sughi, Cremonini, Kokocinski, Trubbiani, Tabuso, Tommasi Ferroni, Purificato, Mulas and Crocetti.

  • Where Via della Carrozza, Torre de' Passeri
  • Entry €3, reduced €1,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.torredepasseri.pe.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.torredepasseri.pe.it

Chiesa parrocchiale della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built at the end of the eighteenth century and consecrated in 1815, it is dedicated to Our Lady of Graces and welcomes the worship of the patron saint Sant'Antonino, celebrated from 2 to 4 September.

  • Where Piazza Plebiscito, Torre de' Passeri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.torredepasseri.pe.it

Sources: comune.torredepasseri.pe.it

Casa de Pompeis

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

An eighteenth-century building built on a former pottery factory, home to one of the most important families in the country; it was the night of Gioacchino Murat and the pink room with a canopy bed is preserved.

  • Where Piazza Plebiscito, Torre de' Passeri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.torredepasseri.pe.it

Sources: comune.torredepasseri.pe.it
