What to see in Pienza

The "ideal city" of the Renaissance wanted by Pope Pius II in his native village, Corsignano: UNESCO historical center since 1996, in the UNESCO landscape of Val d'Orcia.

6 places to visit in Pienza, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 4 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Piazza Pio II

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The square redesigned by Bernardo Rossellino, closed by the Duomo and three palaces, of the government, of the bishop and of the pope: Pienza is the only Renaissance center of Italy arrived intact until today.

  • Where Piazza Pio II, Pienza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Cattedrale dell'Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Built on the Romanesque church of Santa Maria, visible in the crypt, with Renaissance façade, octagonal bell tower inspired by those seen by Pius II in Germany and five altarpieces of the Sienese school, including a Madonna of Matthew of John.

  • Where Piazza Pio II, Pienza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Palazzo Piccolomini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The masterpiece of Rossellino, residence of Pius II, with the loggia with three arches on the ground floor facing the Duomo.

  • Where Piazza Pio II, Pienza
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo Diocesano nel Palazzo Borgia

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The palace that Pius II donated to Cardinal Rodrigo Borgia, future Alessandro VI, today seat of the Diocesan Museum with paintings, fabrics and liturgical objects of the territory.

  • Where Piazza Pio II, Pienza
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Pieve di Corsignano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient parish church of Santi Vito e Modesto, just below the village, with its millennial cylindrical bell tower.

  • Where Corsignano, Pienza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Monticchiello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified village about ten kilometers from Pienza, with the castle of the mid-Twentieth century and the church of Santi Leonardo and Cristoforo from the Gothic portal.

  • Where Monticchiello, Pienza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
