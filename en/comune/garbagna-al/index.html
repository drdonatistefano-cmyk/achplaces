What to see in Garbagna

The most beautiful village in Italy, the centre of the Genoese Val Grue, with the Renaissance square of the Doria and the Bella di Garbagna cherries.

5 places to visit in Garbagna, in the province of Alessandria (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Garbagna in the guide with map and filters

Piazza Doria e Oratorio di San Rocco

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Renaissance square dedicated to Prince Doria with the Doria Palace and the oratory erected around 1580 after a pestilence, with frescoed façade by Giovan Battista Carlone; four horse castans from 1853 in the center.

  • Where Piazza Doria, Garbagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Parrocchiale di San Giovanni Battista Decollato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Octagonal church of 1714 with the iconic Baptism of Christ of Moncalvo and a precious organ of the seventeenth century.

  • Where Garbagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it
