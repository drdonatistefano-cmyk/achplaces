What to see in Mosciano Sant'Angelo

The Teramano hilly town with the Acquaviva Tower, the circular church of the SS. Rosario and, in the hamlet of Montone, a fourteenth-century city walls with three towers and the sarcophagus of Bucciarello of 1390; in Colle Leone the astronomical observatory houses a museum of natural sciences.

Museo di scienze naturali (Osservatorio Astronomico Colle Leone)

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

Scientific and didactic exhibition hosted in the Astronomical Observatory Colle Leone, with sections of paleontology, mineralogy and meteorite: the first illustrates the history of life on the planet, the second exhibits good quality samples and the collection of meteorites completes the path between terrestrial and extraterrestrial environment.

Montone, mura e torri

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Only village of the Municipality, five kilometers from the chief town. The walls were erected around 1390 according to Mario Moretti, a date confirmed by Carlo Perogalli for the presence of catchers; there are three towers: the main tower in the upper part, behind Sant'Antonio Abate, remained original and without battlements; a square tower in the center of the south side with stairs; a third in brick with protruding apparatus, between the south and west sides. The village is known for its viewpoint; the main church of Santa Maria Assunta was renovated and expanded on a project by Francesco Patella.

  • Where Montone, Mosciano Sant'Angelo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.mosciano.te.it

Sources: comune.mosciano.te.it

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio Abate e sarcofago di Bucciarello

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small church close to the castle of Montone, where the sarcophagus of Bucciarello Jacopo di Bartolomeo was transferred, defined the most beautiful monument of the municipal territory: the nobleman had it carved in stone from 1390 in the chapel of San Jacopo, outside the walls, and carries two coats of arms, his and that attributed to his wife. Sarcophagus and church were restored by the municipality in 1988.

  • Where Montone, Mosciano Sant'Angelo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.mosciano.te.it

Sources: comune.mosciano.te.it

Torre Acquaviva

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

A 24,40 meters high square-plan tower, with protruding apparatus, built for the sighting and defence of the historic nucleus; unlike the major tower of Montone, it has been transformed into a bell tower.

  • Where Centro storico, Mosciano Sant'Angelo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.mosciano.te.it

Convento dei Santi Sette Fratelli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient monastery in the Convent district, south-east towards Giulianova; the church is dedicated to the Saints Seven Brothers and to the Madonna del Casale and preserves a wooden statue of 1688.
