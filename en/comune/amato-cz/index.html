What to see in Amato

Country of the inland Catanzaro with the mother church of the Immaculate in the central square, the Museum of the village born from the collection of the master Francesco Scerbo and the Route of the Mills along the stream Fosso-Acquafredda, between ruins and waterfalls.

3 places to visit in Amato, in the province of Catanzaro (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa della Santissima Immacolata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Mother Church dedicated to the Immaculate Conception, celebrated on 8 December with a procession; not far from the town hall, overlooks the central square dedicated to Francesco Caligiuri. It houses a half-busted statue of St. Francis of Paola of the seventeenth century, donated by the Marquis Mottola with notary act of 1782. Main entrance to stairway, ramp to the secondary entrance.

  • Where Piazza Indipendenza, Amato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.amato.cz.it

Sources: comune.amato.cz.it

Museo del borgo

Museums · Themed

Born on the initiative of Maestro Francesco Scerbo, collector of ancient objects: at the entrance numerous tools including a press for olives, then tools and objects of common use, small handicraft machines, masonry tools, blacksmith and carpenter, and rare photographic and literary documents.

the MiC card indicates "Closed" every day of the week; contact the Municipality (0961 993033) before visiting

  • Where Via Guglielmo Marconi, Amato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.amato.cz.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.amato.cz.it

Percorso dei Mulini

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Along the river Fosso-Acquafredda ruins of ancient mills, many of the Caligiuri family; the best preserved is the 'mulino di Caligiuri' or 'dei Greci', from the district inhabited in the fifteenth century by Albanian population. Numerous water jumps, including a waterfall the highest detected, between pines and ferns; safe paths and picnic area, with enhancement works started in 2026.

  • Where Via Greci 15, Amato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.amato.cz.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · comune.amato.cz.it
