What to see in Tempio Pausania

The "city of stone" of Gallura, at the foot of Monte Limbara, Orange Flag since 2019 and among the most beautiful villages in Italy: the historic granite center with the cathedral of San Pietro and its oratories, the house of Nino di Gallura, Piazza Faber, the station painted by Giuseppe Biasi and, outside the city, the Nuraghe Majori, Monte Pulchiana and the organic open air museum.

15 places to visit in Tempio Pausania, in the province of Sassari (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico di granito

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Palaces and junk in grey granite and tree-lined avenues in a city that was diocesan and courthouse. In Piazza Gallura the eighteenth-century Palazzo Pes di Villamarina, seat of the Curia; the Palazzo dell'Ex Me, of 1663, was first prison and then market. On Piazza San Pietro you can see the cathedral, the oratories and the Teatro del Carmine.

  • Where Centro storico, Tempio Pausania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · touringclub.it

Cattedrale di San Pietro Apostolo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Cathedral of the diocese of Tempio-Ampurias, behind the Town Hall: the first church is of the first thirteenth century, rebuilt between 1832 and 1839 and then reconsecrated. In granite, with a single nave with four chapels on each side and barrel vault; nineteenth-century marble furnishings with a genoese Baroque taste, fresco of St. Peter in the apse and organ Cyresa of 1980 with 1726 reeds.

  • Where Piazza San Pietro, Tempio Pausania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Oratori del Rosario e di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In front of the façade of the cathedral the Oratory of the Rosary, of medieval origin, houses a sculptural retablo of 1700; next to the bell tower the oratory of Santa Croce, of the thirteenth century, preserves wooden furnishings including a retablo and a Deposition.

Nuraghe Majori

History and architecture · Archaeology

Less than 2 km from the center along the SS 133, granite fortress of the Bronze Age on a granite dome in a cork wood, with characters of both the nuraghe in corridor and that in tholos: a corridor leads to two oval rooms and a semicircular courtyard. From April to October-November there lives a colony of tiny bats, observable without flash. Admission fee.

  • Where SS 133, Tempio Pausania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: idese.cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Monte Pulchiana

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Sardinia's largest granite monolith, a pink and yellowish dome 673 metres high on the Lu Parisi plateau, a natural monument since 1994; on the east face the only climbing route equipped with Gallura. Around Tafoni, cork groves, paths and the shelter in stone of Conca Fraicata, called the house of the smurfs.

  • Where Lu Parisi, Tempio Pausania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Monte Limbara

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The massif above Temple, 1362 meters high, on the border with the Logudoro: CAI paths and bike paths, and on clear days the view arrives at Tavolara, at the lake of Coghinas, at the Maddalena and at the Asinara. In the gorge of the Rio Pisciaroni small waterfalls and granite tanks.

  • Where Monte Limbara, Tempio Pausania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: touringclub.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Organica, museo di arte ambientale

Museums · Modern art

In the woods of Curadureddu sul Limbara, between holm oaks and cork trees about 7 km from Tempio along the SS 392: works installed outdoors and the CEDAP Space, a former troticulture transformed by the architect Antonello Menicucci in two exhibition rooms, near the pools of the Rio Pisciaroni.

exhibitions from May to December (edition 2026 from 24 May to 31 December)
