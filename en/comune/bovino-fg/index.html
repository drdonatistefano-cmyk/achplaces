What to see in Bovino

Borgo dei Monti Dauni on the hill overlooking the valley of Cervaro, the ancient Roman Vibinum, Orange Flag and among the most beautiful villages in Italy: about 800 stone portals, seven churches that are worth the name of "Urbino del Sud" (Urbino del Sud), the ducal castle with the diocesan museum and the cathedral of 1231.

Centro storico e portali in pietra

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Labyrinth of paved alleys with river pebbles, stone houses with barrel vaults and about 800 portals carved by local stonemasons; in the Portella district there are traces of the Roman walls and, in the village, remains of medieval walls and towers.

  • Where Centro storico, Bovino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Castello ducale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built by the Normans on the remains of a Roman fortress, with the cylindrical Norman tower of the 11th century attributed to Count Drogo; expanded by Frederick II and transformed into a residence in the 17th century by the dukes of Guevara, whose descendants lived there until 1961. Coffered ceilings, courtyard with clock tower and hanging garden; Torquato Tasso and Pope Benedict XIII stayed there.

  • Where Castello, Bovino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters

Founded in 1999 by Bishop Giuseppe Casale in the ducal castle: reliquaries, monstrers, sculptures and paintings of the diocese, including a Martyrdom of San Sebastiano attributed to Mattia Preti; in the chapel of the palace a silver reliquary with a fragment of the Sacred Spina. Open Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday 16-19, Sunday 10-12.30 and 15.30-19 (recommended to contact first).

  • Where Castello, Bovino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals

It dates back to the end of the 10th century, remodeled in Romanesque forms in the 12th century; the façade was built in 1231 by master Zano. Inside fragments of Byzantine sculpture, including Daniel in the lions' pit on Roman columns in the presbytery, the wooden choir of the seventeenth century and the funerary inscriptions of the lords of Bovino.

  • Where Centro storico, Bovino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Cisterne romane Cerrato

History and architecture · Archaeology

A few meters from the cathedral, underground complex probably Roman: two rooms dug in the sandstone with walls in stone and pillars up to five meters high, part of the water system linked to the aqueduct that fed the baths of Vibinum.

  • Where Centro storico, Bovino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Museo civico Carlo Gaetano Nicastro

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

Opened in 1925 in Palazzo Pisani from the collection donated by the local physician and historian Carlo Gaetano Nicastro, it collects archaeological finds from the excavations in the territory of Bovino. Free admission according to the Touring; for the MiC open Saturday and Sunday 10.30-12.30 and 16.30-18.30, in August every day except Monday.

  • Where Piazza Marino Boffa, Bovino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the upper part of the village, Romanesque church of 1099 (XII century for the Touring) with Byzantine elements, built on Roman remains and renovated in the Baroque period; remarkable the baptismal font.

  • Where Parte alta, Bovino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Marco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Consecrated on 18 May 1197 according to an epigraph of 1703; the Byzantine bezel above the portal depicts St Mark between two deacons. It preserves monumental tombs of bishops of the ancient diocese.

  • Where Centro storico, Bovino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Mulino ad acqua

Museums · Themed

On the Cervaro stream along the ancient Strada Regia delle Puglie, built in 1810 with a system patented by Ferdinando II of Bourbon: the last watermill still working in Puglia, today museum of ancient crafts and grinding of ancient grains such as Senator Cappelli.

  • Where SP110, Bovino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it
