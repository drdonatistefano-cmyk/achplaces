What to see in Fiorano Modenese

On the first hills of Modena, the capital of ceramics: the Castle of Spezzano dei Pio di Savoia with the Sala delle Vedute and the Museo della Ceramica, the Baroque sanctuary of the Blessed Virgin of the Castle and the mud volcanoes of the reserve of the Salse di Nirano.

5 places to visit in Fiorano Modenese, in the province of Modena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Spezzano e Museo della Ceramica

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Of medieval origin, transformed into a palace by the Pio di Savoia since 1529: courtyard portico Renaissance, Sala delle Vedute frescoed around 1596 by Cesare Baglione with 56 views of the State of Sassuolo, Galleria delle Battaglie and chapel with stucco altar from 1699. On the first floor and in the basement, the Museum of Ceramics, from prehistory to industrial and contemporary production, with the multimedia section on work; in the pentagonal tower the municipal vinegar mill. Around a nineteenth century romantic park.

  • Where Via del Castello 12, Spezzano, Fiorano Modenese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Santuario della Beata Vergine del Castello

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Baroque Basilica of the seventeenth century commissioned by Francesco I d'Este on a project by Bartolomeo Avanzani, a Greek cross with central dome and two bell towers; on the parish church the bronze statue of John Paul II and, next to, the archaeological area with the remains of the ancient castle destroyed in the seventeenth century. Patronage party on September 8th.

  • Where Fiorano Modenese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Riserva naturale delle Salse di Nirano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established in 1982 on 200 hectares of the first hills, it protects the group of sauces, cold mud volcanoes, larger and more singular in the region. Free access, always open subject to exceptional conditions; trails can also be reached by wheelchair. A cycle path connects it to the castle of Spezzano.

  • Where Via Rio Salse II Tronco, Nirano, Fiorano Modenese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: comune.fiorano-modenese.mo.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it

Centro storico «il Sasso» e Vedute perdute

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town called ... the Sasso, to be walked: on the walls thirteen large ceramic panels, the lost Sights, starting from Ciro Menotti square.

  • Where Piazza Ciro Menotti, Fiorano Modenese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Tomba di Ciro Menotti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Renaissance style chapel covered with polychrome marbles, with the coats of arms of Carpi, Modena and Fiorano, in the church of Spezzano, where in 1929 the remains of the Risorgimento patriot were transferred condemned to hanging in 1831.
