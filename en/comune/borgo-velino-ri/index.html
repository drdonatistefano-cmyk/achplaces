What to see in Borgo Velino

Village along the Via Salaria, on the left of the river Velino at the foot of Mount Nuria covered with woods and chestnut groves, moved downstream in the fourteenth century with the foundation of the "Borghetto"; it preserves the Flavi Nympheus of a Roman villa of the first century and an ethnographic and naturalistic civic museum in the Town Hall.

Ninfeo dei Flavi

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Monument of the 1st century AD, from the age of the flavian dynasty, when in Borgo Velino there was a Roman villa; its walls are visible inside the public gardens. It is indicated by Visit Lazio as the place not to be missed in the country.

  • Where Giardini pubblici, Borgo Velino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Museo civico di Borgo Velino

Museums · Themed

Piazza Umberto I e Torre civica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The main square of the village, where in August takes place the animated Tressette, a living game in which 32 girls and 8 boys impersonate the 40 cards; the Civic Tower offers a panoramic view of the village and the surroundings.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Borgo Velino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
