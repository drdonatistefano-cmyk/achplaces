What to see in Sesto Calende

The town of the Ticino Park, where the river exits Lake Maggiore: the Civic Archaeological Museum houses one of the most complete collections on the culture of Golasecca, while the Romanesque abbey of San Donato and the oratory of San Vincenzo have frescoes from the XV to the XVIII century.

Civico Museo Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Set up in the nineteenth century Palazzo Comunale, it exhibits finds of the territory from the end of the third millennium BC to the high Middle Ages; the main nucleus is dedicated to the culture of Golasecca, of which the museum has one of the most complete collections, with materials of the Gallic, Roman and Altomedieval era and fragments of the Benedictine church of San Donato (first half of the IX century). Since 1994 Pliocene fossils of Cheglio di Taino have also been exhibited. The municipality indicates opening from Monday to Thursday 9-12 and 14-17, Friday 9-12, Sunday 15-18 (with guide on Sunday afternoons and holidays), Saturday closed.

  • Where Palazzo Comunale, Piazza Mazzini, Sesto Calende
  • Entry €3, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Abbazia di San Donato

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Three-nave church built in the town of Scozola, where the bishop of Pavia Liutardo (841-864) established a monastic community; in 1535 monastery and lands passed to the Major Hospital of Milan. The present building is a Romanesque reconstruction with apses and bell tower; inside the Disputa di santa Caterina d'Alessandria (1503) by Bernardino Zenale, the Last Supper of Tarillo da Curia (1581), eighteenth-century frescoes by Bellotti and a wooden crucifix.

  • Where Via San Donato 6, Sesto Calende
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sesto-calende.va.it · italia.it

Oratorio di San Vincenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built at the end of the 11th century in the agricultural district of San Vincenzo, it is considered by the municipality the real jewel of the area; it was used as a lazaret during the epidemics, the last of cholera in 1884. Inside frescoes in part of the 15th and 16th centuries with the three Kings Magi; it preserves burials and is the object of archaeological excavations with guided tours. In 2026 the extraordinary free openings were held on the first Sunday of the month from April to October.

Sass da Preja Büja

History and architecture · Archaeology

Large erratic rock transported by the glacier in the last glaciation, engraved with symbolic petroglyphs that suggest an ancient sacrificial altar prehistoric; around there are other erratic boulders engraved. It is located in the area of San Vincenzo, an area that has returned traces of prehistoric settlements, Goolasecchiani and Romans.

  • Where Via Nino Banchio, Sesto Calende
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · comune.sesto-calende.va.it · italia.it

Lungofiume del Ticino e Ciclabile del Ticino

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Sesto Calende overlooks the Ticino and Lake Maggiore, in the Ticino Valley Park: walking on the lift you can see swans, flares, folagues and royal germans nesting on the banks. From here starts the Ticino Cyclable, which follows the river towards Golasecca, the dam of the Panperduto and the Naviglio Grande up to Pavia or Milan.

  • Where Lungofiume, Sesto Calende
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sesto-calende.va.it · italia.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The municipality describes it as one of the most evocative areas of the city, with small restaurants and bars of Lombard cuisine and lakes and evenings often animated by events on the banks of the Ticino; there are the Town Hall and the archaeological museum, a few minutes walk from the station.

  • Where Piazza Cesare da Sesto, Sesto Calende
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sesto-calende.va.it · italia.it
