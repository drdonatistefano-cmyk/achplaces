What to see in Brescello

The village of the films of Don Camillo and Peppone, with Piazza Matteotti, the church of the talking crucifix and three museums between cinema and Brixellum Romana, the colony that controlled traffic on the Po.

6 places to visit in Brescello, in the province of Reggio Emilia (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Brescello in the guide with map and filters

Piazza Matteotti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Main square of the village, closed on two sides by large porticos, with the Town Hall and the church. In the center is the statue of Hercules Benefactor carved by Jacopo Sansovino in honour of Hercules II d'Este, finished in 1553 and here since 1727; on the sides are the bronze statues of Don Camillo and Peppone of the sculptor Andrea Zangani, for the 50th anniversary of the first film.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Brescello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: comune.brescello.re.it · visitemilia.com

Chiesa di Santa Maria Nascente

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church that dominates the square, in 19th century style, rebuilt between 1829 and 1837; in a chapel preserves the talking crucifix of the films.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Brescello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: comune.brescello.re.it · visitemilia.com

Museo Peppone e Don Camillo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Inaugurated on April 16, 1989 by volunteers from the country, it collects posters, anecdotes, photographs and original props of the films, including the Guzzi sidecar, Peppone's bike, Don Camillo's dress and bicycles, with reconstructions of some places.

  • Where Via Cavallotti 24, Brescello
  • Entry €6, reduced €4 (one ticket three museums)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: visitemilia.com · comune.brescello.re.it

Museo Brescello e Guareschi, il territorio e il cinema

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Opened on 21 June 2009 and managed by the Fondazione Paese di Don Camillo and Peppone, it tells the story of the peasant life of the 1950s and the flood of 1951, with the reconstruction of a set and an exhibition on film making.

  • Where Via Felice Cavallotti 24, Brescello
  • Entry €6, reduced €4 (one ticket three museums)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: comune.brescello.re.it · visitemilia.com

Museo archeologico romano Albino Umiltà

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Brescello, founded in the 4th century BC by the Galli Cenomani and then Roman city that controlled the traffics on the Po between the 1st century BC and II AD: it starts from the necropolis of Brixellum with original stele, then the copy of the funerary monument of the Concordi found in Boretto in 1929, the reconstruction of a domus with mosaic floors and the history of the excavations.

  • Where Brescello
  • Entry €6, reduced €4 (one ticket three museums)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: visitemilia.com · comune.brescello.re.it

Piazza Mingori e cimeli dei film

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

In Piazza Mingori, the statue of a Russian soldier linked to the film The comrade Don Camillo and a replacement of Don Camillo's tank and Mr Peppone; the locomotive of the films is in the park dedicated to Guareschi.

  • Where Piazza Mingori, Brescello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: visitemilia.com
