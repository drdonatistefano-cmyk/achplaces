What to see in Pinerolo

The capital of the Acaja princes since 1295 and the French fortress between the Five and the Sixteenth Century, at the foot of the Valdese valleys: the historic porticoed centre, the Cathedral of San Donato, San Maurizio on the hill and the Museum of Cavalry.

7 places to visit in Pinerolo, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Pinerolo in the guide with map and filters

Museo Storico dell'Arma di Cavalleria

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In the former Fenulli Caserm, 5,000 square meters on three floors and 33 rooms: uniforms, decorations, weapons, relics and a rich photographic archive from 1861 to today, with the gallery on the School of Cavalry and the room dedicated to Federigo Caprilli.

  • Where Viale Giolitti 5, Pinerolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org
