What to see in Cantiano

Ancient feud of Montefeltro on a hill on the border between Marche and Umbria, along the Via Flaminia: Piazza Luceoli, the Collegiate Church of San Giovanni Battista, the archaeological museum in the former Augustinian monastery, the Good Friday Turb and the reserve of the Tecchie Wood at the foot of Catria.

Museo archeologico e della Via Flaminia «G. C. Corsi»

Museums · Archaeological

In the former Augustinian monastery he tells of the human presence in the territory from prehistory to the Middle Ages: prehistoric finds linked to an ancient track prior to Flaminia, Roman materials including a millionaire of 305 A.D. once exhibited in town hall, and a medieval section on castles and the first Christianization. Most of the pieces come from the collection of Giulio Cesare Corsi.

  • Where Via IV Novembre 1, Cantiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Piazza Luceoli e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The heart of the village, closed by the Town Hall with the Clock Tower, the church of San Nicolò and via IV Novembre; from the square via Fiorucci offers medieval views. The name recalls Luceoli, an ancient Umbrian city founded in the 7th century BC in the area of the current Pontericcioli.

  • Where Piazza Luceoli, Cantiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · comune.cantiano.pu.it · italia.it

Rocche di Cantiano e Colmatrano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Castles on the heights above the Flaminia: the fortress of Cantiano, on the hill of Sant'Ubaldo, dates back to the IX century and hosted the castle of Gabrielli of Gubbio, which had Cantiano as a fief since 1381; on the top of Colmatrano remain the ruins of a military tower.

  • Where Cantiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.cantiano.pu.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it
