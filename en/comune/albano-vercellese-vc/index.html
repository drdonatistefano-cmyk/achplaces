What to see in Albano Vercellese

Country of the Vercellian rice plain, seat of the Natural Park of the Blades of Sesia, established in 1978 to protect the wetlands left by the river: here you can visit the Ornithological Museum of the park and the surviving tower of the fifteenth-century castle of the Arborium of Gattinara.

Parco naturale delle Lame del Sesia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established in 1978, it protects about 900 hectares long almost 8 km of Sesia between the provinces of Vercelli and Novara: blades (mirrors of water born from abandoned branches of the river), gerbids on gravel and edges of the last woods of the plain, in the middle of the rice mill. In the special reserve of the Isolone di Oldenico nest egrets, herons ashes, cormorants and herons, observed from the perimeter and the huts; self-guided paths on foot and by bike all year round, with mountain bike rental at the Locanda delle Lama.

  • Where Sede: Via XX Settembre 12, Albano Vercellese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Museo ornitologico del Parco delle Lame del Sesia

Museums · Science and university · Free entry

In the park's headquarters, it documents the research of the Agency with about 700 European and non-European birds, of which 140 can be observed in the park as sedentary or pass species; other sections on forest botanical, morphology of the territory and entomology. Visits by reservation (tel. 011 4320011).

  • Where Via XX Settembre 12, Albano Vercellese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Sources: visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Castello di Albano Vercellese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Castle in the form of the fifteenth century, built on a previous building (recipe or castle, according to scholars), originally with regular plan with angular towers, central tower and a moat 12 meters wide attested in the seventeenth century. Renovated from the seventeenth century and transformed into a noble residence by the Arborio di Gattinara, there is now only one tower.
