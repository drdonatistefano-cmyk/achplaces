What to see in San Romano in Garfagnana

The municipality of the high Garfagnana dominated by the Fortress of the Verrucole dei Gherardinghi, today living museum with costume guides; in the territory the Orecchiella Park with the Visitor Center and its naturalistic museum, the village of Naggio and the Baroque church of San Romano Martire.

7 places to visit in San Romano in Garfagnana, in the province of Lucca (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Fortezza delle Verrucole Archeopark

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built between the 11th and 12th centuries by the Gherardinghi, fortified in Renaissance times and part of the extensive defensive system in Garfagnana: it is a symbol of the feat of the diamond of Hercules I d'Este, with a zinnia carved on the capital of the church. Purchased by the Municipality and restored since 1986, it is a living museum: costume guides tell the life of the castle at the end of the thirteenth century, with the kitchen, the rack and armor to wear. You go up for a mule track rather steep from the village or with the rack train on reservation; seasonal openings from May to October.

  • Where Verrucole, San Romano in Garfagnana
  • Entry €5, reduced €3 (6-13 years), free up to 5 years
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.fortezzaverrucolearcheopark.it

Sources: fortezzaverrucolearcheopark.it · turismo.garfagnana.eu · visittuscany.com

Parco dell'Orecchiella

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Nature reserve between Piazza al Serchio, San Romano, Sillano and Villa Collemandina, with woods of beech, chestnut and fir where they live wolf Apennines, deer, mouflon and royal eagle. There are easy paths for families, including the Bear Path of 1,020 meters with views of the Apuan Islands, the garden of mountain flowers and fences with deer and brown bears; it includes the reserve of the Pania di Corfino with its botanical garden.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website orecchiella.com

Sources: orecchiella.com · visittuscany.com

Chiesa di San Romano Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Baroque church with seventeenth-century façade and bell tower in front of 1751, enlarged after a pastoral visit of 1584 judged it too small. It preserves an organ restored in 1762 surmounted by the wooden statue of San Romano del Seicento, the choir benches and an altar of the stoning of Santo Stefano.

  • Where Centro, San Romano in Garfagnana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.san-romano-in-garfagnana.lu.it · visittuscany.com

Palazzo Pelliccioni e borgo di San Romano

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The village preserves short streets and medieval steps that go down to the provincial. The Palazzo Pelliccioni-Marazzini, known for its arched terraces, houses the municipal library and houses finds from the Bronze Age attributed to the Ligurians of the upper Serchio valley, with Roman and medieval relics.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves
