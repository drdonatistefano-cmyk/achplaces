What to see in Bardi

Country of the high valley of Ceno, 625 meters on the Parma Apennines and along the Via Francigena, appreciated for the summer holiday and dominated by the castle on a spur of red jasper, which houses the museums of the village; in church a youthful work of Parmigianino.

Castello di Bardi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Castle of medieval origin on a hill of red jasper, first residence of the Landi and in Bourbon era military garrison, several times renovated. You can visit the old manor with stone beaches, the walk of the ring with the angular towers, the square of arms, the salon, the underground glacier and the Bastion of the artillery wanted in the fifteenth century by Manfredo Landi the Magnificent, recently restored; inside also the Ferrarini-Nicoli collection, with about sixty paintings and sixteen sculptures of blind authors of the early twentieth century.

  • Where Via Castello, 43032 Bardi (PR)
  • Entry €10, reduced €6 (up to 14 years), schools €5
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page parmawelcome.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Addolorata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish church built in 1934 in Ravenna style, with three naves inside; it houses a youthful work of Parmigianino, the Mystical Bride of Saint Catherine, of about 1522.

  • Where Bardi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page parmawelcome.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
