What to see in Valtournenche

The Matterhorn valley, with Breuil-Cervinia at 2000 meters, the glaciers of the Plateau Rosa for skiing all year round and the Gouffre des Busserailles gorge.

5 places to visit in Valtournenche, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Lago Blu (Lac de Layet)

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Quiet walks with the profile of the Matterhorn reflected in clear water.

  • Where Breuil-Cervinia, Valtournenche
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Funivia e ghiacciaio del Plateau Rosa

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

The cable car from the large windows climbs to the glacier of the Plateau Rosa, where at 3500 meters you can also ski in summer; here the Museum of Work tells about the construction of the cable cars.

  • Where Plateau Rosa, Valtournenche
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Capanna Luigi Amedeo di Savoia

Museums · Themed

Ancient high-altitude refuge reassembled in the village as a museum of the history of mountaineering.

  • Where Breuil-Cervinia, Valtournenche
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Gouffre des Busserailles

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Digged gorge in the mountains by the Marmore stream, 3 km from Valtournenche on the road to Breuil-Cervinia; entrance fee.

  • Where Loc. Gouffre des Busserailles, Valtournenche
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: gestlovevda.it · lovevda.it

Grotta di ghiaccio

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

It can be reached on foot or by skiing on the glacier, accompanied by mountain guides.

  • Where Plateau Rosa, Valtournenche
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it
