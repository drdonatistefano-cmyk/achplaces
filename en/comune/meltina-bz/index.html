What to see in Meltina

Country of the plateau of the Salto (Tschögglberg) above Terlano, land of Haflinger horses documented here since 1904, with fractions of traditional farms; on the Langfenn the Romanesque church of St. James and higher up the mysterious stone mantles, the Stoanene Mandln; in the village the fossil exhibition of Fossilia.

3 places to visit in Meltina, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Fossilia, esposizione di fossili

Museums · Science and university · Free entry

Free exhibition open all year under the Tourist Office: fossil tree trunks of about 260 million years ago from the Giogo di Meltina, 11 panels on the geology of the Tschögglberg and 11 windows with minerals, fossils and geological samples, to tell extinct plants and animals, deserts and seas, eruptions, earthquakes, glaciers and streams.

  • Where Möltner Straße 1a (sotto l'Ufficio turistico), Meltina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website fossilia-moelten.it

Sources: fossilia-moelten.it · suedtirol.info

Stoanerne Mandln (Ometti di pietra)

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the Tschögglberg, at about 2,000 m, piles of stones stacked by shepherds since prehistoric times, a place surrounded by myth and considered a meeting point of witches and priests; the view embraces the whole Dolomite chain up to Marmolada and Ortles. The excursion from the village measures 18.4 km, about 5 hours and 45, with 912 m of altitude difference.

  • Where Partenza dal parcheggio davanti all'Ufficio turistico, Meltina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Chiesa romanica di San Giacomo al Langfenn

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

On the Langfenn, the highest point of the Salto plateau, at 1,525 m and visible from afar, rises on a land frequented already in prehistoric times; next to St. James, patron saint of the wayfarers, the altar with Saints Rocco and Sebastiano. You can reach it by car from the Schermoos car park or by bus 204 and 157.

  • Where Vicolo Langfenn 1, Meltina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info
