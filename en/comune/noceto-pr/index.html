What to see in Noceto

Borgo about 15 km from Parma along the Via Francigena, with in the center the Rocca dei Sanvitale of the thirteenth century, now Castello della Musica; in the south of the village the museum of the Voteval Bronze Age and in the village the museum of the printer Ferdinando Libassi.

Museo della Vasca votiva

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

A large wooden tank of the Bronze Age, found in 2005 during works at the southern edge of Noceto: rectangular structure of about 12 x 7 meters, deep at least 3, in which about 3,500 years ago a terramaric community laid ritual offerings. Among the 15th century B.C. finds five wooden ploughs, large baskets, fictile figurines of animals and dozens of vases; the bathtub is rebuilt in the museum with videos and interactive stations. Open from Thursday to Sunday and holidays.

  • Where Via Ignazio Silone 1, Noceto
  • Entry €7, reduced €6, 6-18 years €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website vascavotivadinoceto.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: vascavotivadinoceto.it · parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Castello della Musica – Museo della Liuteria e Museo del Disco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

The Fortress of the Sanvitale, dating back to the 13th century, with almost square plan with four rounded corner towers and the mast as the oldest part, called Castello della Musica on 23 December 2005. It houses the Museum of Lyuteria with arched instruments and a pinch of the luthier Renato Scrollavezza and the Lyuteria Parmense, the Museum of Disco with 78 and 33 laps of the legacy of journalist Bruno Slawitz (especially concerts and opera) and the International School of Lyuteria founded in 1975.

Museum closed until date to be dedicated according to Parma Welcome (info Municipality 0521 622137)

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Noceto
  • Entry €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo della tipografia Ferdinando Libassi

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The historic typography . The nocetana graphics, purchased by the Municipality by the family of the master printer Ferdinando Libassi: a press From the Garden of the second half of the nineteenth century, a Monzese pedal, a nineteenth-century plate of the Mordini brothers and a Nebiolo plate, all working, and millions of characters, friezes and reordered fillets. Open Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday 8.30-12.30; Saturday and Sunday by appointment.

  • Where Via Dalla Chiesa 30, Noceto
  • Entry €3
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
