What to see in Savignano sul Panaro

The town is surrounded by two walls around the castle mentioned since 1026; it is the city of the archaeology of the Paleolithic Venus found in 1925 and the mammoth of the Panaro, and it is the birthplace of the sculptor Giuseppe Graziosi.

4 places to visit in Savignano sul Panaro, in the province of Modena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Venere e dell'Elefante

Museums · Archaeological

Two sections: the Elephant Museum with the skeleton of a female of Mammuthus meridionalis found in 1980 by a fisherman on the gret of the Panaro, in locality Bocchirol, with dioramas on the environment, fossilization and evolution of the trunks; the Museum of Venus with a copy of the statue of about 22 cm carved in soft stone about 25,000 years ago and found in 1925 (the original is at the Pigorini Museum of Rome), compared with the reproductions of the Venus of Lespugue, Willendorf, Chiozza and Marmot. The museum's website indicates opening on Sundays and holidays 14.30-18.30, weekdays by reservation.

Italia.it gives the Museum of Venus closed for renovation and VisitModena both museums temporarily closed, while the site of the museum reports Sunday hours

Borgo medievale e castello di Savignano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The high village, fortified by a double belt, is crossed on a road in pebbles of river: the castle, mentioned for the first time in 1026, has a stone tower and on the tower a 17th century fresco of the Madonna with Child; between the houses stand out the Watchtower and the Tower of the Chaplain, Zanantoni square with the Oratory of San Rocco, the House of Matilde, where according to the tradition stayed Matilde of Canossa, and the House of the Captain (or the Bishop) with the coat of arms frescoed of the Contrari, lords of Savignano since 1409.

  • Where Savignano Alto, Savignano sul Panaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · italia.it

Opere di Giuseppe Graziosi

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The sculptor, born in Savignano in 1879, left in the village the Compianto sul Cristo morto (1924) in the parish church, the bronze of the Redeemer on the family tomb, the monument to the Fallen of the First World War in front of the cemetery; in the village also his birthplace.

  • Where Savignano sul Panaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · italia.it

Via delle Querce e itinerari collinari

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A hilly area to the south-east of the Bazzanese state road with large oaks; there pass itineraries on foot or by bike like that of the Mostino and that from Monticelli to Montebudello and Acqua Fredda.

  • Where Savignano sul Panaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · italia.it
