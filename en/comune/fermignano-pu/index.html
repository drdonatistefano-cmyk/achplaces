What to see in Fermignano

Borgo sul Metauro, home of the architect Donato Bramante, gathered around the bridge with three arches and the 24 m high medieval tower; the territory includes the hill of Montelce with the legendary tomb of Asdrubale and part of the Furlo Gorge.

Ponte romano e Torre medievale

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The three-arched bridge over the Metauro, which the Roman tradition wants, is built in rows of stone blocks with brick parts of subsequent restorations; with the next tower, 24 m high and perhaps on Roman foundations, it forms a single complex that controlled an important ford, served as customs and defended the country. At the foot of the tower there is the public fountain of the "Mascherone" (1886). The tower is visited by reservation through the IAT office (0722.330523).

  • Where Corso Donato Bramante 3, Fermignano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Villa Isola

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Along the Metaurense Provincial Road, it was the country residence of Federico Bonaventura; in 1578 it stayed there Torquato Tasso, which here composed the "Canzone al Metauro" .

Collina di Montelce e sepolcro di Asdrubale

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill of Montelce, according to legend, is the tomb of Asdrubale, the Carthaginian leader defeated by the Romans in the battle of Metauro (207 BC); Montaigne, Giacomo Leopardi and Giovanni Pascoli would pass there.

  • Entry Free
