What to see in Montelupo Fiorentino

3 places to visit in Montelupo Fiorentino, in the province of Florence (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Museo della Ceramica

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The majolica of Montelupo from the Middle Ages to the nineteenth century, found in the "Pozzo dei Lavatoi," with a large Renaissance collection.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Veneto 10, Montelupo Fiorentino
  • Entry €6 whole (with the Archaeological Museum); €4 reduced; free on the first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museomontelupo.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: museomontelupo.it · destinationflorence.com

Villa medicea dell'Ambrogiana

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The Medici villa on the Arno, part of the UNESCO site, seen from outside along the bike path; in the park there is the Archaeological Museum.

  • Where Montelupo Fiorentino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com

Fornace del Museo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The ceramic manufacture Cioni-Alderighi built near the walls in 1859, restored and reopened in 2021 as part of the route of the Ceramic Museum, with workshops on local production.

  • Where Via Giro delle Mura, Montelupo Fiorentino
  • Entry Included in the ticket of the Ceramic Museum
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website museomontelupo.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: feelflorence.it · destinationflorence.com · museomontelupo.it
