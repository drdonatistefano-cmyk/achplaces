What to see in Gressan

Municipality of orchards and apples at the gates of Aosta, with the chapel frescoed by Giacomino d'Ivrea and, at altitude, the skiing basin of Pila.

5 places to visit in Gressan, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Gressan in the guide with map and filters

Chiesetta di Sainte-Marie-Madeleine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Romanesque chapel among the orchards with external frescoes and interiors by Giacomino d'Ivrea of 1463; from here comes a crucifix of 12th century goldsmiths today in the museum of the Cathedral of Aosta.

  • Where La Madeleine, Gressan
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

La Tour de Villa

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Castello dei Signori de La Tour de Villa, just above the church among orchards and vineyards, today a receptive structure.

  • Where Gressan
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Maison Gargantua

Museums · Themed

Ancient house with ethnographic museum on rural culture and exhibition space.

  • Where Gressan
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Riserva naturale Côte de Gargantua

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Dry spurs of glacial origin with flora and fauna of the Steppe environments, reachable on foot or by bike.

  • Where Gressan
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Pila

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Conca 1800 meters above Aosta with ski area and bike park among the most important in the region.

  • Where Pila, Gressan
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it
