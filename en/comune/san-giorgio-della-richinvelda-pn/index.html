What to see in San Giorgio della Richinvelda

Municipality of the plain of Pordenone along the Meduna, with the hamlet of Rauscedo known as the world capital of the Barbatella for the wine-growing life; in the plain of Richinvelda, where in 1350 the patriarch Bertrando was killed, stands the church of San Nicolò, while in Pozzo the Museum of peasant civilization preserves the collection of Gelindo Lenarduzzi.

Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated on 7 August 1982 in the village of Pozzo, it was born from the collection of objects of rural life made for twenty years by Gelindo Lenarduzzi; the Municipality bought and restored a building to accommodate it provided that it remained in Pozzo, and the collection then grew with donations from citizens and companies. Among the pieces three handmade pendulum clocks, wagons and agricultural tools of the twentieth century and, the oldest, gramola and combs for hemp of 1810. Open on Sunday 10:30-12:30 (MiC card updated to 2020); audioguide.

Rauscedo e la chiesa di Santa Maria e San Giuseppe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rauscedo, the largest fraction, is called the world capital of barbatella for the wine-growing viticulture, largely cooperative, and is the birthplace of the painters Angiolo and Jacopo D'Andrea; the landscape is marked by the white stones of Meduna. The neoclassical parish church, built between 1845 and 1850 on a project by the Venetian Francesco Lazzari, houses a seventeenth-century altarpiece of the Coronation of the Virgin (property of the Academy of Fine Arts of Venice, attributed to Matteo Ingoli), the Nativity of Angiolo D'Andrea, a Transit of St. Joseph attributed to Michelangelo Grigoletti or Luigi Nono and a frescoed ceiling of the late nineteenth century with the Assumption.

Chiesetta di San Nicolò alla Richinvelda

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Small church of the first half of the 12th century, created for the spiritual assistance of those condemned to death and then a place of worship for pilgrims and needy. With the memorial stone it is one of the historical symbols of the plain of Richinvelda, where in 1350 the patriarch of Aquileia Bertrando of Saint Geneis was killed by the Friulian nobles; from 3 April 2018 it waves every day the flag of Friuli.
