What to see in Cercivento

Small mountain village of Carnia, in the But valley, known as the open-air Bible for mosaics, photo-ceramics and paintings with biblical theme on the walls of the houses; it houses the parish church of San Martino and a historic center of ancient Carnic houses.

3 places to visit in Cercivento, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Una Bibbia a cielo aperto

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

Three biblical-themed itineraries between squares and streets of the village: ten fundamental moments in the history of salvation, the journey of Mary and the path of Mercy, with mosaics, murals, frescoes and photo-ceramics on private houses and public buildings, accompanied by verses in Friuli and Italian; the Last Judgment, the Good Samaritan and the Transfiguration stand out. The sketches are by Paolo Orlando; there are audio guides, the whole route takes about 2 and a half hours (the single routes 10-40 minutes) and it is easy, walkable and by bike and accessible to people with motor disabilities.

Pieve di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was first mentioned in 1358, it rises between the two villages of the village; the bell tower was started in 1656 and the title of parish church arrived in 1794. Inside the pulpit carved by Eugenio Manzoni and gilded in 1726 by Girolamo Agnese, a red marble stove from the early eighteenth century, a crucifix of Bavarian workshop and the organ of 1753, paid with the funds of the emigrant cramârs in Bavaria and restored by the Zanins.

Case storiche del centro

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center preserves ancient Carnic houses: Casa Citârs of 1577, the oldest, where tiles, frames, waterworks and ceramic statuettes were produced; Casa Tiridin of 1634 with open loggias; 17th century Pitt House, set on fire by Nazi soldiers; Casa Morassi of 1690 with stone portal and 18th century chapel dedicated to the Madonna.

  • Where Centro storico, Cercivento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
