What to see in Milazzo

Port for the Aeolian Islands on a narrow peninsula: the largest fortified citadel of Sicily with the castle of Frederick II, the village of the Spanish City and, at the tip of the head, the pool of Venus.

6 places to visit in Milazzo, in the province of Messina (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Milazzo in the guide with map and filters

Castello di Milazzo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The largest fortified citadel in Sicily, over 7 hectares on the peninsula to guard the radium, with defences built in about ten centuries: the castle of Frederick II dominates the enclosure of the Spanish walls, which also contains the ancient Duomo, begun in 1608.

  • Where Milazzo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Capo Milazzo e Piscina di Venere

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the end of the peninsula, the pool of Venus is a pool on the sea inside a marine reserve.

Palazzo D'Amico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palazzo del Cinquecento in the ancient village; on the noble floor it preserves Risorgimento relics belonging to Giuseppe Garibaldi.

  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
