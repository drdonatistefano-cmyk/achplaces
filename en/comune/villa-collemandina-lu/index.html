What to see in Villa Collemandina

The town of Garfagnana of seven hamlets, rebuilt after the earthquake of 1920 from which only the fifteenth-century cloister was saved; above the capital Corfino, at the foot of the Pania, with the Botanical Garden .

Orto botanico «Maria Ansaldi» Pania di Corfino

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

At 1,370 meters, in a clearing of the beech forest of the Orecchiella Park, it gathers about 400 species of the flora of the Lucca Apennines and the Apuan Alps, including rare or endangered plants and species of medicine and popular food, divided for environments: woods, pastures, moors, bogs, gravels, rocks. Among the blooms, from spring to summer, peonies, lilies, orchids, gentian, the Apuan widow and paradise.

  • Where Via della Covezza, Corfino, Villa Collemandina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Parco dell'Orecchiella e Riserva della Pania di Corfino

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural reserve in the heart of Garfagnana, between Piazza al Serchio, San Romano, Sillano and Villa Collemandina, which includes the Pania di Corfino and Lama Rossa Nature Reserve: woods of beech, chestnut and fir with Apennine wolf, deer, mouflon and royal eagle, walking, mountain biking or horseback riding and with guided tours. The visitor center, with the naturalistic museum, is in San Romano in Garfagnana.

  • Where Corfino, Villa Collemandina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Corfino

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Borgo at the foot of the Pania di Corfino, near the Orecchiella Park, at the intersection of the ancient streets that went up to the pasture of Campaiana, with views from the Apuan Alps to the Apennines; the parish church of San Lorenzo and the Sanctuary of the Madonna del Perpetuo Soccorso, with a sixteenth-century table of the Virgin with the Child.

  • Where Corfino, Villa Collemandina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa parrocchiale e chiostro quattrocentesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The 15th century cloister, with trapezoidal plan with sixteen arches on thin columns in serene stone and carved capitals, is the only structure surviving the earthquake of 1920 that destroyed the village, then rebuilt.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Canigiano e Ponte Attilio Vergai

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

A fraction of narrow streets and brick houses with the church of San Leonardo Hermita, built in 1837; you arrive for the provincial road that crosses the Ponte Attilio Vergai on the gorge of the Corfino stream.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sassorosso

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fraction known for the precious red marble (saxo rubeo) extracted on site, which gives a characteristic shade to the landscape and buildings.
