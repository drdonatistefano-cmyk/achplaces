What to see in Sauze d'Oulx

Alta Val di Susa railway station in the Milky Way ski area, with the sixteenth-century parish church of San Giovanni Battista, the chalet of Carlo Mollino al Lago Nero (1946-47) and access to the Gran Bosco Natural Park of Salbertrand.

Chalet Mollino al Lago Nero

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Designed and built by the architect Carlo Mollino between 1946 and 1947 at 2,286 meters, it housed the arrival station of a slittovia and was designed as a refuge for skiers; until the mid-sixties it served skiers under Mount Triplex. The Municipality defines it as one of the most important works of modern architecture in Italy: after a long abandonment it has been restored inside and outside and today it is again a refreshment point on the slopes, with terrace.

open only in winter and accessible with skis

Parco naturale del Gran Bosco di Salbertrand (accesso da Sauze)

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Park established in 1980 on the right side of the Val di Susa, between about 1,000 and 2,600 meters, to protect forests of white fir, larch and pine cembrian (70% forests, 30% pastures and high altitude prairies); there are recorded more than 600 plant species, about 70 of nesting birds and 21 of mammals, including deer, deer and chamois. From Sauze you go up to Gran Villard and Monfol to the parking lot and picnic area of Serre Blanche (about 1.700 m), or to Clotes and the road of Sportinia to Case Enfer, shortly after the border of the park.

  • Where Parcheggio Serre Blanche (via Gran Villard e Monfol), Sauze d'Oulx; sede: via Fransuà Fontan 1, Salbertrand
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sauzedoulx.to.it · regione.piemonte.it · italia.it

Comprensorio della Via Lattea

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Sauze d'Oulx is part of the Milky Way, an eight-station area with 212 slopes and 69 lifts, suitable for skiers of all levels. In summer flowering meadows, itineraries on the ridges between Val di Susa and Val Chisone, bike park and summer ski lifts.

  • Where Sauze d'Oulx
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismotorino.org
