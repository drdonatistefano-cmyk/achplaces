What to see in Verrès

Village at the entrance of the Val d'Ayas dominated by the cubic castle of the Challant on a spur of rock.

4 places to visit in Verrès, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Verrès in the guide with map and filters

Castello di Verrès

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built by Ibleto di Challant just before 1390 on a cliff above the village, with a cubic plan that combines all functions in a single building; in the sixteenth century Count Renato adapted it to firearms.

  • Where Verrès
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Collegiata di Saint-Gilles

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Monastery and parish complex built between the 11th and 18th centuries in the historic centre, at the entrance of the Val d'Ayas.

  • Where Verrès
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Les Murasses

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Buildings surrounded by walls, once stable of the Challant, now home to the mountain community and library.

  • Where Verrès
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Arboretum La Borna di Laou

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the beginning of the Val d'Ayas, in a panoramic area, plants and shrubs of various species reachable on foot.

  • Entry Free
