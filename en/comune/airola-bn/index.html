What to see in Airola

Centro della Valle Caudina elevated to town by Carlo di Borbone with decree of 30 August 1745, near the sources of Fizzo that feed the Carolino Aqueduct: on top of the hill remain walls and gate of the castle founded in 1033 by Rainulfo, in the village the church of the SS. Annunziata di scuola vanvitellina, la chiesa alcantarina della SS. Concept and the Telephone Museum.

Mura e porta del castello medievale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Castle built in 1033 by Rainulfo and enlarged in the twelfth century, with interventions also by Frederick II; it was surrounded by two walls separated by small towers. Today on the top of the hill remain the wall and the door, with the coat of arms of the Carafa still visible on the stone of the entrance gate, and the remains of a drawbridge at the entrance.

  • Where Sommità della collina, Airola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page sabapce-bn.cultura.gov.it

Sources: sabapce-bn.cultura.gov.it

Chiesa della Santissima Concezione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church in Alcantarian style with a single nave with six side chapels, dedicated to the Sacred Heart, to St. Anthony of Padua, to St. John Joseph of the Cross, to St. Pasquale Baylon, to St. Francis of Assisi and to St. Lucia; on the main altar a statue of the Virgin, inside a piped organ, on the portal the heraldic coat of arms. It is connected to a Franciscan monastery, where at the end of 1911 the province of San Giovanni Giuseppe della Croce established its own college.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page sabapce-bn.cultura.gov.it

Sources: sabapce-bn.cultura.gov.it

Chiesa della Santissima Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church indicated by the Palace of Caserta as a significant expression of the architectural culture of the Vanvitellian school, in the country linked to the legacy of Luigi Vanvitelli and the Carolino Aqueduct.

Museo del Telefono

Museums · Themed · Free entry

It tells of the birth of telecommunications, from the first remote communication systems to the telegraph of Samuel Morse, with 81 exhibits, the oldest a telegraph of 1870 in the rooms of a historical building; the route includes a film on the history of the telegraph and an interactive game to write in Morse code with the button of a telegraphic station reconstructed in 3D printing. Timetable indicated by the Museum Network: Wednesday, Thursday and Friday 10.30-12 and 16-18.

  • Where Biblioteca e Museo del Telefono, Airola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.comune.airola.bn.it
