What to see in Senales

Municipality of Val Senales, lateral of Val Venosta, linked to the Similaun mummy: Ötzi was found on the Tisa Giogo at 3,210 m and the archaeoParc of Madonna di Senales reconstructs the life of 5,300 years ago. In the valley the Certosa, an active monastery from 1326 to 1782, the Marian sanctuary of Madonna di Senales and the artificial lake of Vernago.

5 places to visit in Senales, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

archeoParc Val Senales

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Outdoor museum of 5,000 m2 near the Ötzi's discovery site, which reconstructs the daily life of about 5,300 years ago (the mummy and its equipment are at the South Tyrol Archaeological Museum). Inside there is a permanent exhibition with touchable replicas of Ötzi's clothes and tools and a plastic of the basin of the discovery; outside there are nine life-size houses rebuilt by excavations, fields and gardens with plants of the time, clay oven, pond with monoxyl canoe, long archery and daily workshops of fire, bread and flint. Open every day from Easter to All Saints; the complete visit with the outdoor area takes 3-4 hours.

  • Where Madonna 163, 39020 Senales
  • Entry €8, reduced €6 (path 1, prices 2024)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.archeoparc.it

Sources: archeoparc.it · italia.it

Certosa di Senales

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The Carthusian monastery of the Certosa degli Angeli was active from 1326 to 1782; guided tours in Italian and German lead to the cloisters and rooms.

  • Where Certosa, Senales
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Santuario e via meditativa di Madonna di Senales

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

A pilgrimage route of 1.8 km (about half an hour) crosses the larch groves of Madonna di Senales to the Marian shrine, with stations carved by South Tyrolean craftsmen: the copy of the miraculous image of Alois Rainer, the Annunciation of Josef Tappeiner, Bethlehem, the Pietà of Josef Santer and Mary with the Apostles of Ulrich Santer. In the church, on the altar of Our Lady, the statue of Our Lady is kept in an ostensory.

  • Where Madonna di Senales, Senales
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it

Lago di Vernago

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Artificial lake at about 1,700 m: a circuit path of 6.7 km (almost 2 hours, 206 m of difference in altitude) starts crossing the dam and runs largely in the shade above the lake, with two suspended bridges. Depending on the level of the water, the bell tower of a submerged church emerges, and in the chapel of Vernago are displayed photos of the plain and of the flooded farms. The lake has been more beautiful since mid-July, when the basin is full. Free parking and bus 261.

  • Where Vernago, Senales
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it

Luogo del ritrovamento di Ötzi

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the Giogo di Tisa, at 3,210 m, a thin stone pyramid marks the point of finding the mummy. You will arrive with a challenging mountaineering excursion of 15.2 km and about 8 hours (1,607 m of altitude difference) from Vernago, for the Tisen farm and the Tisa Valley to the Similaun refuge and then along a rocky ridge partly equipped; along the path the archaeological site of the Schneck with a neolithic stone engraved. The archeoParc organizes guided hiking tours to the glacier every Tuesday.

challenging alpine excursion, check weather and opening of the Similaun hut (Südtirol)

  • Where Giogo di Tisa, Senales
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · archeoparc.it · italia.it
