What to see in Poviglio

Municipality of Bassa Reggiana known for the Terramara Santa Rosa di Fodico, a village of the Bronze Age of seven hectares with visitable summer excavations and a museum open all year round; in the village the church of Santo Stefano of the twelfth century and the monumental cemetery.

5 places to visit in Poviglio, in the province of Reggio Emilia (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Terramara Santa Rosa

Museums · Archaeological

It exhibits the most significant materials of more than forty years of excavation at the Terramara Santa Rosa: ceramics (cetole, cups, dolii, votive objects, fusaiole, frame weights), ornaments and bronze instruments, with a teaching apparatus on the village and on the terramare of the western Emilia; also remains of a Roman villa found in Fodico. Open all year round, on the 2nd floor of the building of the Municipal Library.

  • Where Via Parma 1, Poviglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: comune.poviglio.re.it · visitemilia.com

Terramara Santa Rosa di Fodico

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Terramaricol village of the medium Bronze age extended about seven hectares, between the sixteenth and the twelfth century BC; the oldest nucleus, of about one hectare, was founded around 1500 BC. It is the largest and most studied site in the province of Reggio Emilia: during the summer excavation campaigns the area is open with free guided tours that introduce to the culture of terracing.

  • Where Via d'Este, Podere Santa Rosa, località Fodico, Poviglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: reggioemiliawelcome.it · visitemilia.com · comune.poviglio.re.it

Chiesa di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A 12th century church that preserves a painting by Carlo Zatti; the 14th century bell tower has a cell with triphor and flat balustrade cover.
