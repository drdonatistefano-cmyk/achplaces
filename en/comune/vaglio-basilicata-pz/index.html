What to see in Vaglio Basilicata

Medieval village near Potenza, at the gates of the ancient Lucanian people: the fortified town of Serra di Vaglio with the House of the Pithoi, the Federal sanctuary of Mefite in Rossano, the Museum of the ancient people of Lucania, the mother church with the octagonal Norman tower and the snowshoe on Mount Jupiter.

Area archeologica di Serra di Vaglio e Casa dei Pithoi

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

At about 1,000 meters, town of the Peuketiantes inhabited by the 8th century BC, fortified in the 4th century BC with a double wall (some stones bear Greek letters) and divided into districts by a main road; it was abandoned in the 3rd century BC after a great fire. The House of the Pithoi reconstructs a house of the fifth century B.C. with the four large jars. Open on weekends and holidays from June to mid-November, every day in August, by reservation on other days.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Santuario di Mefite a Rossano

History and architecture · Archaeology

Federal Sanctuary of the Lucans dedicated to the goddess Mephyte, deity of the waters, and to the god Mamerte, next to a still active source; renovated in Roman times in the second century BC and abandoned in the first century AD, probably due to landslides that transformed the area into marsh.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Museo delle antiche genti di Lucania

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 2006 in the center of Vaglio, it presents with reconstructions in real and virtual size, original materials and reproductions of the archaeological contexts of Serra and Rossano, as a diffused museum; it also exhibits a painting considered a self-portrait of Leonardo da Vinci. Same periods of opening of the archaeological area.

Centro storico e Porta Vecchia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Borgo documented from the Middle Ages, on three parallel roads (via Buonarroti, via Vergara, via Roma) joined by narrow and steep stairs, with two doors: the Porta Vecchia with round arches and "U Spurt" , incorporated in the Baronale Palace; remain drinking troughs, the wash-house of the Old Fountain and the cellars.

  • Where Via Roma, Vaglio Basilicata
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: comune.vagliobasilicata.pz.it · basilicataturistica.it

Chiesa madre di San Pietro Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A Romanesque church with three naves, with paintings and sculptures from the 16th and 17th centuries, including a Madonna del Rosario by Antonio Stabile, and an octagonal Norman tower older than the church; it houses the body of Saint Faustino, patron saint since the 18th century.

  • Where Via Roma, Vaglio Basilicata
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: comune.vagliobasilicata.pz.it · basilicataturistica.it

Convento di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Just outside the ancient center, a convent with two courtyards of the convent friars, suppressed in the French Decade; the church has golden wooden altars, statues and paintings of the seventeenth century and a 17th century fresco of the Madonna with the Child and Saint Anne.

Chiesa della Madonna di Rossano

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Church of the 16th century about four kilometers from the village, in a natural area with oaks and a nearby spring; a single nave with a Madonna and Child enthroned.

Neviera del monte Giove

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the slopes of Mount Jupiter, near the village, circular buildings used in the 14th and 19th centuries to preserve the snow in layers covered with leaves and obtain ice for food.
