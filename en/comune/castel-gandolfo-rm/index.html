What to see in Castel Gandolfo

Borgo dei Castelli Romani overlooking Lake Albano, the deepest of Lazio, summer residence of the popes for centuries: in Piazza della Libertà the Apostolic Palace, the church of San Tommaso da Villanova and the fountain of Bernini; around the Pontifical Villas, Villa Torlonia and the Roman water lilies on the shores of the lake.

Palazzo Apostolico e Ville Pontificie

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The palace, built since 1628, closes the north side of Piazza della Libertà: the façade is by Carlo Maderno, continued by Bernini, author of the gate of the garden; an inscription recalls the intervention of Alexander VII in 1660. He hosted the Vatican Observatory, which was transferred here from the Roman College. Since 2014, at the behest of Pope Francis, palace and gardens are open to the public as a museum pole. The Pontifical Villas, an extraterritorial area of the Holy See, include 55 hectares with Villa Barberini, Villa Cybo and the Italian gardens.

Piazza della Libertà e fontana del Bernini

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Main square of the village, dominated by the Apostolic Palace, with the Bernini fountain of 1661 and the oldest mailbox, installed in 1820. From the eastern side you reach the viewpoint on the lake; the Corso della Repubblica crosses the medieval village to Piazza Cavalletti, with another view of the lake and Mount Cavo. Outside the village remained practically unchanged from the seventeenth century, ceramic doves of local craftsmen mark the five entrances.

  • Where Piazza della Libertà, Castel Gandolfo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Chiesa pontificia di San Tommaso da Villanova

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Bernini's work of the seventeenth century commissioned by Alessandro VII Chigi, a Greek cross plan with dome on Doric pillars, dedicated to the saint in the year of canonization. Inside stuccoes by Antonio Raggi and paintings by Pietro da Cortona (his altarpiece of the Crucifix), Giacinto Gimignani and Guglielmo Cortese.

  • Where Piazza della Libertà, Castel Gandolfo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Villa Torlonia

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Restored in 1817 by Giuseppe Valadier, it has a portico of six Doric columns that holds a terrace with six Ionic columns and a tympanum carved by Thorvaldsen; near the Horti Torlonia and the Archaeological Park of Ibernesi, which preserves ruins of the Villa of Domitian.

  • Where Via Ercolano, Castel Gandolfo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlazio.com

Lago Albano

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient elliptical volcanic crater among woods and meadows in the Regional Park of the Roman Castles, 170 meters deep, with paths suitable for everyone. On the dark volcanic beach, about 25 minutes walk from the village, rental of kayak and SUP, bar and departure of boat tours, every hour in season. The artificial emissary of the lake is dated to 396 B.C.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Ninfeo Dorico e Ninfeo del Bergantino

History and architecture · Archaeology

The Doric Nympheo, in via Gramsci, dates back to the Republican era and was probably part of the villa of Clodio; the Bergantino Nympheo, on the western coast of the lake along the Via dei Pescatori, is called "Bagni di Diana" for a mosaic with the goddess of hunting.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
