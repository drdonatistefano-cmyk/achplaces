What to see in Teti

Mountain country about 700 meters on the north-western slopes of Gennargentu, with a territory rich in Nuragic archaeology: the village of S'Urbale, the village-sanctuary of Abini and the archaeological museum of the area that preserves its finds, as well as the museum houses Satta and Mereu and the lake of Cucchinadorza.

Museo archeologico comprensoriale

Museums · Archaeological

In the old town, next to the parish, since 1990 he has exhibited ex-vote, rings, bracelets, daggers and pins from the Middle Bronze Age of the Iron, bronzes of tenderers and warriors from the sanctuary of Abini (including a figure with four eyes, four arms and double shield), the neolithic female idol of the plateau of Atzadalai and finds of the Mandrolisai; there is also the life-size reconstruction of a hut of S'Urbale.

Villaggio nuragico di S'Urbale

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On a hill overlooking the lake of Cucchinadorza, about 12 km from the village: about fifty circular huts in granite blocks with stone floors and central fireplace, once covered with poles and secluded trasches with clay and cork. The complex also includes a nuraghe and a tomb of Giants; the finds of daily life are of the ninth century BC. Admission for a fee.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Villaggio-santuario di Abini

History and architecture · Archaeology

A place of worship of the "federal" and a pilgrimage destination of the nuragic populations, known since the mid-nineteenth century: they remain part of the town and a sacred well. The numerous votive bronzes, from the IX-VIII century BC, are today at the National Archaeological Museum of Cagliari and the Museum of Teti.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di San Sebastiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest church of Teti, originally Gothic-Aragonese, rises in a park not far from the village surrounded by the cumbessias, the little houses that house the faithful during the feast of the saint at the end of August.

Casa Satta e Casa Mereu

Museums · House museums

Two ethnographic museums: Casa Satta reconstructs environments as they were at the beginning of the twentieth century, Casa Mereu shows the furnishings of a noble residence.

Lago di Cucchinadorza e boschi di Teti

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lake, formed by the rivers Tirso, Taloro and Tino and used to produce electricity, is dominated by the hill of S'Urbale; around the village springs and woods can be walked, inhabited by deer, deer, mouflons and foxes, under the granite slopes of Sa Marghine.
