What to see in Latina

The ancient Littoria, before the founding cities of the Agro Pontino (1932): the rationalist squares of Oriolo Frezzotti with the Civic Tower and Palazzo M, the museums of the reclamation as Piana delle Orme, and on the coast the Lido di Latina and the lake of Fogliano in the National Park of the Circeo.

14 places to visit in Latina, in the province of Latina (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza del Popolo e Torre Civica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The heart of the city designed by Oriolo Frezzotti: the 32-metre-high Civic Tower, overlooking the city, and in front of the Water Monument, a fountain with a travertine sphere.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Latina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Museo della Terra Pontina
