What to see in Venaria Reale

The hunting palace of the Savoys at the gates of Turin: the Baroque palace with the Gardens, the ancient village designed by Amedeo di Castellamonte and the Park La Mandria with the Castle of Vittorio Emanuele II.

5 places to visit in Venaria Reale, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 4 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Reggia di Venaria

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The great baroque palace of the Savoys, a UNESCO World Heritage Site: the Sala di Diana di Amedeo di Castellamonte, the Galleria Grande and the Chapel of Sant'Uberto, eighteenth-century works by Filippo Juvarra, and the Rondò Alfieriano.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica 4, Venaria Reale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website lavenaria.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: lavenaria.it

Giardini della Reggia

Nature and landscape · City parks · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

The vast gardens of the Reggia, part of the UNESCO complex, between the history of the garden and contemporary interpretations; from here you can rent bikes for the Mandria.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica 4, Venaria Reale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lavenaria.it

Sources: lavenaria.it

Castello della Mandria

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

In Borgo Castello inside the park, the royal apartments of Vittorio Emanuele II, who lived here with his morganatic wife Rosa Vercellana, Bela Rosin. UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  • Where Viale Carlo Emanuele II 256, Venaria Reale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website lavenaria.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: lavenaria.it

Parco La Mandria

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

More than 3,000 hectares of meadows and forests: Europe's largest fenced and protected environment, with Borgo Castello and the curious Bizzarria.

  • Where Viale Carlo Emanuele II, Venaria Reale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lavenaria.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: lavenaria.it

Borgo antico e piazza dell'Annunziata

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village designed by Amedeo di Castellamonte between 1667 and 1690 at the service of the palace, with the square as a focal point.

  • Where Piazza dell'Annunziata, Venaria Reale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lavenaria.it

Sources: lavenaria.it
