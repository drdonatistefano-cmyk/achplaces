What to see in Pianezza

Municipality on the Dora Riparia at the gates of Turin, crossed by a branch of the Via Francigena: the fifteenth-century Madonna della Stella with its frescoes, the Romanesque parish church of San Pietro overlooking the river, the remains of the castle linked to the episode of Maria Bricca of 1706 and the ancient factory of Passamanerie Massia.

5 places to visit in Pianezza, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa della Madonna della Stella

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church on the highest point of Piazza, just over half a kilometer from the ancient village, built in 1472, 1474 or 1476 (the date is uncertain) and today national monument. It preserves fifteenth-century frescoes with stories of the life, marriage and death of Mary. Called Madonna di Lessano until 1660, she is patron saint of Pianezza (feast on 12 September) and is located on a branch of the Via Francigena. Opening on request at the cultural office (tel. 011 9670217).

  • Where Via Madonna della Stella 10, Pianezza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pianezza.to.it

Pieve di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Pieve of medieval origin on a site rich in Roman finds, almost overhanging the Dora along a stretch of the Via Francigena. The Romanesque façade probably dates back to the second half of the twelfth century; the building was later renovated in Gothic forms, with the semicircular apse replaced by a square. Next to it, the chapel of San Biagio preserves traces of frescoes. Opening on request at the cultural office (tel. 011 9670217) or the UNECON association.

  • Where Via Maria Bricca 18, Pianezza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pianezza.to.it

Sources: comune.pianezza.to.it

Resti del castello e Galleria di Maria Bricca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Of the castle, perhaps wanted by a bishop of Turin around the Mille and confirmed to Bishop Charles by Frederick I in 1159, remains parts of the underground and external bastion, capitals, fragments of balustrade and other finds scattered in the park and in the gallery called Maria Bricca. It was declared national good after the Napoleonic occupation and was sold in 1808 and partly dismantled; in 1811 the Marquis Agostino Lascaris of Ventimiglia built the villa there. According to tradition, in 1706 the local Maria Bricca led the Piedmontese troops through the underground passages to the ballroom occupied by French officers.

  • Where Via al Borgo (già via Lascaris 4), Pianezza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pianezza.to.it

Sources: comune.pianezza.to.it

Antica Fabbrica Passamanerie Massia Vittorio 1843 (Museo della passamaneria)

Museums · Themed

The company has created theater, castles and museums of all over the world. The visit, of about 2 hours, takes place in a museum-workshop where ancient machinery testify a centuries-old history; reservation through Tourism Turin (Made in Turin).

  • Where Lungo Dora Maria Bricca 20, Pianezza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pianezza.to.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.pianezza.to.it

Masso Gastaldi e belvedere

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Large boulder in the historic centre: from its top the look ranges from the Maritime Alps to the Gran Paradiso. From the viewpoint of Piazza Primo Maggio, at the entrance from Collegno, you can see the Alpine arch with the Monviso in the center.

  • Where Via Masso Gastaldi, Pianezza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pianezza.to.it

Sources: comune.pianezza.to.it
