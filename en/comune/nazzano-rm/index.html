What to see in Nazzano

Borgo 46 km from Rome above the Tiber valley, with the thirteenth-century Savelli castle and, in its ancient stables, the Rijeka Museum; under the village the Riserva Nazzano Tevere-Farfa, the first regional natural reserve of Italy (1979), with the lake born from the barrier ENEL of the 1950s and the boat excursions, and just outside the church of Sant'Antimo with frescoes attributed to Antoniazzo Romano.

Museo del Fiume

Museums · Themed

Naturalistic Museum dedicated to the Tiber and linked to the Tiber-Farfa Reserve, founded in 2000 in the former granaries and stables of the Savelli Castle. Interactive paths follow the river from the source to the mouth, with aquariums, reconstructions of habitats, micro-organisms and insects; the naturalistic section, didactic, has 3D microscopes and photos, the archaeological fossil finds of Nazzano and the middle valley of the Tiber, and the room of temporary exhibitions overlooks with large windows on Piazza della Rocca.

  • Where Via Mazzini 4, Nazzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Riserva Naturale Regionale Nazzano Tevere-Farfa

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Born as an oasis of protection of fauna in 1968 (ENEL, Municipality and WWF), Ramsar area since 1977 and established in 1979 as the first regional natural reserve of Italy, it covers about 800 hectares between the provinces of Rome and Rieti. The lake of Nazzano, about 300 hectares deep 20-100 cm, formed between 1953 and 1955 after the barrier ENEL on the Tiber downstream of the confluence with the Farfa: reeds, willows and poplars host pilgrims, brown nibbi, herons ashes, folk and pigs, and the site is among the most interesting in central Italy for birdwatching. Paths such as La Fornace (1.5 km on wooden walkways) and the Meana riparian ring (1 km, with observation tower); areas equipped for stopping.

  • Where Sede: Via Tiberina km 28,100, Nazzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.parchilazio.it

Sources: parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com

Battelli sul Sentiero del Fiume

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers

The boats "Airone" and "Martin Pescatore" run from 23 places each along the Tiber between the landing places of Nazzano and Torrita Tiberina in about an hour; a longer itinerary arrives under Sant'Oreste. Along the river you can also go canoeing.

  • Where Approdo di Nazzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it

Castello Savelli

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

13th century castle with two towers at opposite angles: the Mastio, to protect the Fortress Savelli, and a watchtower on the Tiber valley. In its former stables and granaries is the Museum of the River.

  • Where Piazza della Rocca, Nazzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com
