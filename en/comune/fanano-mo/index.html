What to see in Fanano

3 places to visit in Fanano, in the province of Modena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parco urbano di sculture in pietra

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

More than 200 sculptures created in the international sculpture Symposium on stone, scattered in the center of Fanano and in the hamlets.

  • Where Centro storico, Fanano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Chiesa di San Giuseppe e pieve di San Silvestro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The Baroque church founded in 1619 by the Counts Ottonelli with the convent of the Scolopi and the Romanesque parish church of San Silvestro, founded in the 8th century.

  • Where Fanano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Santuario della Madonna del Ponte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

The place of worship surrounded by nature near the village, among the points of interest indicated by the tourist portal of Modena.

  • Where Fanano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
