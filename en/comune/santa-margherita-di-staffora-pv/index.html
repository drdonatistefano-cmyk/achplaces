What to see in Santa Margherita di Staffora

The municipality of the upper Staffora Valley, in the Oltrepò Pavese Apennines, with a Roman furnace among the best preserved in Lombardy in Massinigo, the Salumiere Museum dedicated to the Salame of Varzi and the Brignati Trail between secular chestnuts and mills; it is part of the Ecomuseum "The wheat in grass" .

Fornace romana di Massinigo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Circular plan furnace with local stone foundations and raised in brick, discovered in 1957 during the construction of the primary school: the clay hob, of 4,10 meters of diameter, rests on a vaulted corridor, and the praefurnium is partly preserved. It was mainly used to cook bricks and tiles; the Municipality dates to the third century BC, while the archaeological analysis places the last use of it within the first half of the first century A.D. It is considered one of the best preserved in Lombardy.

for the visit you need to call the Town Hall (0383 551100)

Museo del Salumiere

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Inaugurated on September 7, 2013 by Angelo Dedomenici to preserve the knowledge related to the production of Salame di Varzi, it is divided into three rooms: vintage documents, photographs and special pieces; the shop of a salumier rebuilt as it was in the thirties; a winery of ripening with the tools of processing. It is on the ground floor, barrier-free; free entrance.

  • Where Casanova Destra 8/D, Santa Margherita di Staffora
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.santamargheritadistaffora.pv.it

Sources: comune.santamargheritadistaffora.pv.it

Sentiero dei Brignati

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Excursion of about 5 km from Brallo (950 m) to Fego (550 m): from Bralello a mule track descends into the Bosco dei Giganti, chestnut grove of centuries-old trees, then to the ruins of the Mill of Cognassi, with a narrow hydraulic wheel about ten meters high; following and gaining several times the Rio Montagnola you reach the Grotta dei Briganti, shelter between granite boulders that was the refuge of bandits, and finally Fego, near the confluence in the stream Staffora. No fires, plant collection and exits from the marked path.

  • Where Da Brallo a Fego, Santa Margherita di Staffora
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page comune.santamargheritadistaffora.pv.it
