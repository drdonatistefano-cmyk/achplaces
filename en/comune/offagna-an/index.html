What to see in Offagna

Borgo dominated by the fifteenth-century fortress built by the Anconetans, with the castle that houses the Museum of ancient weapons; in the village the neoclassical architectures of Andrea Vici, the Natural Science Museum Luigi Paolucci and the Liberation Museum of Ancona.

Rocca e Museo delle armi antiche

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built between 1454 and 1455 by the Anconetans on the remains of a previous castle, it has quadrangular plan, towers, fifty stations for bombing and the five-storey mast still intact, seat of the Museum of the ancient weapons; along the walls runs the way of patrol with blackbirds tail of swallows. At the top of the mast there is a bell dating back to 1476 and a panorama of the countryside.

Monastero di Santa Zita e Museo della Liberazione di Ancona

Museums · City history

The eighteenth-century monastery, designed by Andrea Vici, has the appearance of a fortified structure; in the adjoining rooms the Museum of Liberation of Ancona collects photographic material on the passage of the allied front in the Marches and on the battles of the Second World War for the liberation of Ancona.
