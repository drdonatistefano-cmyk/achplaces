What to see in Bagolino

Medieval village of Roman origin in the Sabbia Valley, 778 meters on the road between the lake of Idro, Maniva and the plain of the Gaver, known for the Carnival, transhumance and cheese Bagòss: in the center the parish church of San Giorgio, known as the cathedral between the mountains of San Rocco, with frescoes fifteenth century and the Via dei Portici.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

This cathedral in the mountains, designed by Giovan Battista Lantana, was the largest monumental church in Bresciano in the 17th century. Frescoes from 1647-1652 with squares by Tommaso Sandrini and Ottavio Viviani and figures by Palma il Giovane, Camillo Rama and Lucchese; seventeenth-century wooden altars by Giacomo Faustini, Baldassarre Vecchi and Pietro Bonomi; paintings attributed to Tiziano, Torbido, Barbelli, Celesti and others.

Chiesa di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It preserves the cycle of frescoes of 1483-1486 by Pasoto Da Cemmo and his son Giovanni Pietro, between Gothic international and Renaissance, rare testimony of the Lombard painting of the time: all twelve Sibyls and the history of Lamech appear there. Traditionally, the presbytery is based on a Roman temple.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Via dei Portici e piazze del centro

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Via dei Portici, almost entirely covered, was the medieval commercial axis of the village. Piazza Marconi is a viewpoint over the mountains and the parish; Piazza Mercato, among the oldest, houses the market on Tuesday afternoon and the building of the ancient prisons; Piazza Consiglio, set in 1823, has the only fountain of the village, octagonal in marble of Botticino. In the center also the granite Casa Torre, whose underground saved the archive from the fire of 1779, and the Procuratie, municipal palace for bishops and ambassadors from the decorated façade restored in 1991.

Chiesetta dei Santi Gervasio e Protasio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Chiesa degli Adamino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1614 by Gianfranco Dagani with municipal funds and dedicated to the Madonna della Neve, it takes its name from the Adamino family owner of the land; bell tower, sacristy, vestibule and choir were added between 1670 and 1680.

Cimitero vecchio

Places of worship · Monumental cemeteries · Free entry

Opened in 1810 after the Napoleonic edict, it has a row of family chapels on the street side, wrought iron crosses of local craftsmen and silhouettes of angels in sheet metal, once painted, on the children's graves.

Casa Museo Habitar in sta terra

Museums · Themed

Managed by the Habitar association on the ground, it collects materials and tools from the ethnographic heritage of Bagolino in two locations: a wing of the school in via Lombardi and the historic center in via Madonna di San Luca 47, near Piazza Mercato. Visit by reservation by phone to the Municipal Library (0365 99558) or by writing to info@habitarinstaterra.it.

Romanterra e il Chiodo d'oro

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Geosite recognized in 2005 after twenty years of studies at the University of Zurich, among the sites in the world that mark a geological limit: in 2009 on the bed of Caffaro was placed the golden nail that marks the border between Anisico and Ladinico, in the Triassico.
