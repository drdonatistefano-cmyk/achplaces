What to see in Voltaggio

Borgo dell'alta Val Lemme dominated by the castle purchased by the Genoese in 1121, known for its medieval bridge with two arches, the Capuchin monastery with its genoese school gallery and the path that goes up to Mount Tobbio.

6 places to visit in Voltaggio, in the province of Alessandria (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pinacoteca dei Padri Cappuccini

Museums · Old masters

Constituted at the end of the nineteenth century by Father Pietro Repetto, a definitor of the Ligurian province of the Capuchins, it is a fundamental collection for the Genoese school of the sixteenth and eighteenth centuries, with works by Luca Cambiaso, Bernardo Strozzi, Lazaro Tavarone, Domenico Fiasella and Giovanecchino Assereto; there is also a work by Sinibaldo Scorza, a painter of Savoy court. It is located in the Capuchin monastery, built between 1595 and 1604 to give refuge to the friars on their way between Genoa and Milan and renovated in 1880 with the contribution of the Duchess of Galliera.

renovation works in progress in the convent: only the church can be visited; normally access by reservation; the MiC indicates closed every day

Chiesa del Convento dei Cappuccini (San Michele Arcangelo)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the Capuchin complex dedicated to San Michele Arcangelo, consecrated on 15 October 1662 by the bishop of Albenga; facade with a large semicircular window, reached by a cobbled climb. The interior with a single nave with two side chapels contrasts the white walls with the dark wood of altars and coverings; it preserves the statue of the Immaculate on the altar and a French Via Crucis in sepiolite.

  • Where Via Provinciale 1, Voltaggio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.voltaggio.al.it · italia.it

Ponte Romanico (Ponte dei Paganini)

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

A stone bridge with two asymmetrical arches, with parapet and rostrums of Roman origin; it is not documented, but it is believed to date back to the end of the XII and the beginning of the XIV century. It is one of the most photographed views of the village, with the houses and the high bell tower of the parish in the background; always accessible on foot.

  • Where Via Luca Bottaro, Voltaggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.voltaggio.al.it · italia.it

Oratorio di Nostra Signora del Gonfalone

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On the main street of the historic center, it was built between 1631 and 1665 for the confraternity of the Disciplined of Saint Mary (documented from 1569), which from the early eighteenth century collected funds to redeem the Christian prisoners of the barbarians. Single nave full of stuccoes, furniture and marble; Bernardo Carrosio painted in 1665 the fresco of the facade and several internal canvases, and the right side altar preserves the relics of San Clemente and a view of Voltaggio of 1682. Open on summer weekends and oratory parties.

  • Where Via G.B. De Rossi, Voltaggio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.voltaggio.al.it · italia.it

Museo della Civiltà Contadina (Palazzo Gazzolo)

Museums · Themed

Set up in the Documentation Centre for Local History and Culture, the ecomuseum cell of the park in the Genoese style building linked to the holiday of the noble families of Genoese: original work tools, a room on local cultivars and reconstructions of haymaking, working land and mountain traditions, with multimedia room and library. Visitable by reservation with the park.

  • Where Via C. Anfosso 2, Voltaggio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: areeprotetteappenninopiemontese.it · italia.it

Sentiero Voltaggio-Monte Tobbio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hiking trail (difficulty E) that climbs from Voltaggio (353 m) in about 2 hours and 30 minutes, with about 750 m of altitude difference, to the summit of Monte Tobbio (1,092 m): chestnut wood, clearing of the former Cascina Colletta, ridge of the Costa di Cravara with views of the Devil's pulpit, Passo della Dagliola. On top of a small chapel and a refuge always open; on clear days the view ranges from the lakes of the Gorzente to the Ligurian Sea, the Po Valley and the Alps.

  • Where Partenza dal centro di Voltaggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: areeprotetteappenninopiemontese.it · italia.it
