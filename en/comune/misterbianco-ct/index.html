What to see in Misterbianco

Cities on the southern side of Mount Etna, about 7 km from Catania, rebuilt after the lava of March 29, 1669 destroyed the ancient village; of the buried village remain the bell tower, 'U Campanarazzu', and the excavations of the ancient Mother Church, while in the new center are located the Mother Church with the Sacred Art Museum, the Four Songs and the Carnival Museum.

Scavi archeologici e Parco suburbano di Campanarazzu

History and architecture · Archaeology

Area of the ancient Mother Church buried by lava in March 1669, of which survived the bell tower called by the mysterious "U'U Campanarazzu" bell tower. The park, donated to the parish in the sixties by Vespasiano Trigona, Duke of Misterbianco, includes an 8,000 m2 century-old oak grove escaped the cast and the remains of the buried church.

Terme romane

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Remains of a thermal plant built in the late Imperial period, between the II and III century AD; according to the Municipality are freely visitable.

Chiesa Madre e Museo d'arte sacra

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Rebuilt after 1670 with slow works due to lack of funds, it has three naves on a Latin cross plan, is 44 meters high, 51 meters long and 22 wide; naves and dome have classic forms, while the white façade in stone of Priolo, of Romanesque imitation, was resumed in 1888 and completed in 1905. In the crypt the Museum of Sacred Art keeps the treasures of the community and documents of the country destroyed by the eruption of 1669.

Quattro Canti e Chiesa di San Nicolò

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the village rebuilt after 1669 was opened the opening of the Quattro Canti, at the intersection between Via Giordano Bruno and Via Matteotti, on which the buildings of four important families overlook. The church of San Nicolò was the first of the new municipality: it resembles the ancient Mother Church and its bell tower copies the ancient one, but it is on the right of the facade instead of on the left.

  • Where Via Giordano Bruno / Via Matteotti, Misterbianco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page archivio.comune.misterbianco.ct.it

Museo del Carnevale «I costumi più belli di Sicilia»

Museums · Themed

It is located in the Munich Plant, a former industrial plant destroyed by a fire in 1922 and completely restored in the early 2000s, which also houses municipal offices, a conference room and the historical archive of the municipality.

Museo d'arte contadina

Museums · Themed

Museum that exhibits testimonies of rural life, near the parking lot Milicia: the Milicia district is the one where in 1669 the 3,656 refugees of the country destroyed by lava stopped.
