What to see in Villanovaforru

Small village of Marmilla founded in the seventeenth century under Spanish domination, known for the nuragic complex of Genna Maria, on the hill that dominates from the Gulf of Cagliari to that of Oristano, and for the archaeological museum that exhibits its finds in a nineteenth-century building in the historic center.

Complesso nuragico di Genna Maria

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the hill of Genna Maria, about 400 meters, the original tower of the fifteenth century BC was surrounded in the thirteenth century by a four-tower bastion with courtyard and well partly dug into the rock, then by an ante-mural with six towers; between bastion and ante-mural rose in the early Iron Age a village abandoned in the eighth century BC. From the 4th century BC, mast and courtyard became a place of worship, with about 600 votive lamps, until the 7th century A.D. From the top the view ranges from the Gulf of Cagliari to the Gulf of Oristano to the Gennargentu.

  • Where Località Monte Genna Maria, Villanovaforru
  • Entry €6 (with guide), reduced €4; cumulative with the museum €10, reduced €7
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnacultura.it · cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Museo civico archeologico «Genna Maria»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In an elegant 19th-century building already a granite mountain: on the ground floor the finds of the village of Genna Maria (pots, stone and metal instruments) exposed according to the environments of origin, on the upper floor materials of the Marmilla from Neolithic to the Byzantine age; the path arrives at the cult of Demeter and Core. Open Tuesday-Sunday 9:30-13 and 15:30-18 (April-September until 19:00).

  • Where Piazza Costituzione, Villanovaforru
  • Entry €6, reduced €4; cumulative with park €10, reduced €7
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

It was built in the seventeenth century, when the village was founded under Spanish rule, with houses in the agricultural tradition of the Middle Campidano and ancient portals; in the historical center is the archaeological museum.

  • Where Villanovaforru
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Marina di Orense

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Dedicated to a Spanish martyr, it is the centerpiece of the most heartfelt festivals in the country, celebrated twice a year, Monday and Tuesday after Easter and in mid-July, with processions, singing in Sardinian and distribution of the blessed basil.
