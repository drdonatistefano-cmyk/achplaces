Casa natale del vescovo Johannes Baptist Zwerger

Museums · House museums

In the village of Guggal, at 1,180 meters above sea level inside the Monte Corno Natural Park, the house where Prince Bishop Zwerger was born is today a museum with objects and documents about his life; you can visit only with guide.

  • Where Frazione Guggal, Anterivo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: comune.anterivo.bz.it · suedtirol.info

Parco naturale Monte Corno

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Park of 6,851 hectares shared with Trodena, Mountain, Egna and Salorno, among the foothills of the Val di Fiemme, the Val di Cembra and the valley of the Adige; it takes its name from the Horn of Trodena (1,781 m) and, thanks to the sub-Mediterranean climate, has the flora and fauna richest of species among the natural parks of South Tyrol, with red lilies, martagons and orchids.

  • Where Anterivo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Giro di Anterivo e malga Krabes

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of about 13 km (455 m of difference in altitude, between 1,127 and 1,540 m) that goes down from the center beyond the church to the farm Ebner, goes up forest roads to the saddle of Cisa, passes the farm Pausa and reaches the Krabes mountain hut, recommended stop with panoramic view and local specialties; the return runs along a biotope.

  • Where Centro del paese, Anterivo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Malga Monte Corno

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the town hall square, an itinerary of 14.3 km (568 m of difference in altitude) climbs through forest roads to Passo Cisa (1,489 m) and arrives at the Monte Corno mountain hut at about 1,710 meters, the highest point of the tour, with refreshment and panoramic view of the mountains and valleys around; you return to the same road.

  • Where Piazza del Municipio (partenza), Anterivo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info
