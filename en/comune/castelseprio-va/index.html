What to see in Castelseprio

Castelseprio preserves the ancient Castrum Sibrium, a late ancient fortress and longobard destroyed by Ottone Visconti in 1287, today a state archaeological park. Castrum and village with the church of Santa Maria foris portas have been part of the UNESCO site since 2011. The places of power (568-774 A.D.)

Castrum di Castelseprio

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified settlement built between IV and V century reusing Roman military structures, surrounded by walls with towers; in the Lombard age it was home to a gastaldate. You can see the remains of the Basilica of St. John the Evangelist with cistern and bell tower, the octagonal baptistery with two baptismal sources, the church of St. Paul and a late medieval convent. The excavations, begun in the fifties, are still in progress.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website museilombardia.cultura.gov.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria foris portas

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Small church outside the walls of the castrum, the oldest building in Castelseprio and the only surviving destruction of 1287. The apse preserves a cycle of frescoes with the infancy of Christ also drawn from the apocryphal gospels, dated between the VII and X century, with techniques of Roman ancestry and oriental influences: one of the most important testimonies of the European early medieval mural painting. She's deconsecrated today.

  • Where Via Castelvecchio, Castelseprio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it · museilombardia.cultura.gov.it

Antiquarium di Castelseprio

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Opened in 2009 next to the archaeological area, it exhibits the material culture of the site from the protohistoric settlement to the abandonment, with the main findings of the excavations.
