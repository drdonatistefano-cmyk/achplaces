Centro storico, mura e torre civica

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village has developed within three circles of concentric walls, each with four doors; the best preserved part is the civic tower of the XIV-XV centuries next to the Town Hall. In the centre there is also the church of Sant'Agostino with the adjoining convent and the church of San Gregorio Magno of 1615.
