What to see in Poppi

Borgo del Casentino dominated by the Count Guidi, where Dante found refuge during the exile.

3 places to visit in Poppi, in the province of Arezzo (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Open Poppi in the guide with map and filters

Castello dei Conti Guidi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The castle where Dante took refuge in exile: you enter from the Lion Gate, in the courtyard a scenic staircase leads to the large frescoed hall; from the bell tower overlooking the whole valley.

  • Where Poppi
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Biblioteca Rilliana

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

In the castle the library with miniature incunabula and books of the sixteenth century.

  • Where Castello di Poppi
  • Entry Included in the visit to the castle
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Eremo di Camaldoli

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

The hermitage among the forests of Casentino, where nature, silence and sacred architecture merge.

  • Where Camaldoli, Poppi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
