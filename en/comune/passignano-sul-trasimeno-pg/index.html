What to see in Passignano sul Trasimeno

The village is walled on the northern shore of Trasimeno, dominated by the medieval fortress with a 32-metre tower and the Boat Museum; on the lakefront the boats leave for the islands, and in the hilly hamlet of Castel Rigone stands the Renaissance Sanctuary of the Madonna dei Miracoli.

Rocca di Passignano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress on the height of the village on three levels plus the 32-metre tower: the square walls date back to 820-850, in 1187 passed to the Canons of the Cathedral of Perugia and in the thirteenth century became fortification of the Perugian territory; damaged in 1479, partly demolished in 1778 and 1816-1817 and hit by the bombings of the Second World War, was reopened in 2008 after the restoration. From the tower you can see the lake and the major islands, Minor and Polvese; open every day except Mondays, 10-13 and 15-19.30.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo delle barche (Centro di documentazione delle imbarcazioni tradizionali delle acque interne)

Museums · Themed

In the Fortress, it collects traditional boats of Trasimeno, including 13th century monosili obtained from a single trunk of oak, and boats of Piediluco and of the Padule di Fucecchio, in addition to the "fassoi" in marsh plants of the Cabras pond in Sardinia.

Centro storico, mura e Torre di Ponente

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village is surrounded by medieval walls with gates and towers, including the Tower of Ponente that still bears the coat of arms of the Municipality; in July the Palio delle Boache carries the boats shoulder-to-shoulder up the alleys up to the Rocca.

Lungolago e porto

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

A walk with a view of the islands, animated on weekends and summers by coffee shops, ice cream parlors and restaurants, with beach and port from which boats leave every day for the Isola Maggiore and the Isola Polvese.

Castel Rigone

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village almost intact in the highest point on Trasimeno, founded according to tradition in 543 by the commander of the Ostrogoth Arrigo for the siege of Perugia, remembered in August by the Festa dei Barbari.

Santuario della Madonna dei Miracoli

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Built in 1494 with the support of the Municipality of Perugia as a vote against the plague, entirely in sandstone with façade of Domenico Bertini and a Latin cross plan with a single nave; inside the frescoes of Papacello, the rounds of the prophets and the Epiphany of Domenico Alfani on sketches of Rosso Fiorentino and a coronation of the Virgin of G.B. Corporals.
