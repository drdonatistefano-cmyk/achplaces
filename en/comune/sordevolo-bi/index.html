What to see in Sordevolo

Pre-Alpine village of the Elvo Valley, under the reserve of Monte Mars and the Macrone, already feud of the Avogadro and Laniero center; it is famous for the Passion of Sordevolo, represented every five years from 1816 (next edition 2027), with the museum in the church of Santa Marta, and for the Trappa in the high valley.

La Trappa - Ecomuseo della tradizione costruttiva

Museums · Themed

A large stone building about 1,000 meters in the upper Elvo Valley, built in the mid-eighteenth century by the Ambrosetti family, Lanieri di Sordevolo; it owes its name to the Trappist monks who took refuge there from 1796 to 1802 fleeing from revolutionary France. It houses the cell of the Ecomuseo del Biellese dedicated to rural construction techniques, with guided tours, educational activities and guesthouse; it is reached on foot in about 10 minutes from the SP512 (Tracillino, about 8 km west of Oropa) or in about an hour of mule track from Prera.

Museo della Passione

Museums · Themed

Opened in August 2005 in the choir and sacristy of the church of Santa Marta by the Associazione Teatro Popolare di Sordevolo, he tells with documents, films, photographs, texts and objects the story of the Passion of Sordevolo, represented every five years since 1816.

MiC card (Add. 2021) indicates closed every day: check with the Popular Theatre Association (tel. 015 2562486)

  • Where Via Petiva, Sordevolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Anfiteatro della Passione

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Outdoor amphitheatre of about 4,000 square meters whose setting reconstructs Palestine: Cenacle, Sanhedrin, Gethsemane, Herod's Palace, Pretorio di Pilate and Calvary. Here more than 400 inhabitants stage the Passion, documented since 1816 and interrupted only by world wars; the 29th edition runs from 19 June to 26 September 2027, with up to 3,000 spectators per replica. You can also visit behind the scenes.

  • Where Sordevolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Ambrogio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt in the fifteenth century on the ruins of a medieval building, it was enlarged around 1667 demolishing bell tower and facade to lengthen the three naves, designed by the foreman Stefano De Stefanis of Graglia, and restored in the nineteenth century. Inside the altar in polychrome marble by Francesco Olgiati (1776), late-sixth century altars by Giuseppe Giudice and Francesco Colombera di Viggiù, stuccos and columns painted with fake marble; outside friezes by Paolo Giovanni Crida.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Veneto 3, Sordevolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sordevolo.bi.it · italia.it
