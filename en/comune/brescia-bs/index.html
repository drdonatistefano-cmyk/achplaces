What to see in Brescia

The Roman Brixia and the Lombard UNESCO heritage since 2011, with the Capitolium and the monastery of San Salvatore Santa Giulia; the Castle on the Cidneo hill, the squares of the Loggia, Paolo VI and Vittoria, the two Duomi and the Pinacoteca Tosio Martinengo.

17 places to visit in Brescia, in the province of Brescia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo di Santa Giulia

Museums · City history · Paid entry

The main museum of the city, in the monastery of St. Saviour Santa Giulia: the Cross of Desire of the ninth century, the frescoes of the Chorus of the nuns, the remains of the Roman domus and the basilica of St. Saviour; part of the UNESCO site and seat of large exhibitions.

  • Where Via dei Musei 81/b, Brescia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.bresciamusei.com

Sources: bresciamusei.com · visitbrescia.it

Brixia – Parco archeologico di Brescia romana

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Enrolled in the world heritage in 2011: Piazza del Foro, the Capitolium with the Alata Victory, the Republican shrine of the first century BC, the Roman theatre next to Palazzo Maggi Gambara and the Roman basilica.

  • Where Via dei Musei 55, Brescia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.bresciamusei.com
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: bresciamusei.com · visitbrescia.it

Castello di Brescia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortress on the hill Cidneo built between the fourteenth and sixteenth century, overlooking the city: it houses the first public astronomical observatory of Italy, the railway models, the Museum of Arms "Luigi Marzoli" in the Visconti Mastio and the Museum of the Risorgimento "Leonessa d'Italia" (Leoness of Italy); the park is free, the museums pay.

  • Where Via del Castello 9, Brescia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrescia.it

Sources: visitbrescia.it · bresciamusei.com

Pinacoteca Tosio Martinengo

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In 21 rooms, works by Raffaello, Foppa, Savoldo, Moretto, Romanino, Lotto, Ceruti, Hayez and Canova, from the late Gothic to the early nineteenth century, with sculptures, jewels, glazes and medals.

  • Where Piazza Moretto, Brescia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.bresciamusei.com

Sources: bresciamusei.com · visitbrescia.it

Biblioteca Queriniana

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Founded in 1747 by Cardinal Querini in the former bishop's palace; over 500,000 volumes, 1,158 incunabula, 8,386 five hundred and 10,000 manuscripts.

  • Where Via dei Musei 1, Brescia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrescia.it

Sources: visitbrescia.it

Piazza della Loggia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

On one side the Palazzo della Loggia, built between 1492 and 1570 with influences of Palladio and Sansovino, seat of the Municipality; to the east the Clock Tower with the ancient astronomical clock; on the Mount of Pietà fragments of Roman tombstones.

Teatro Grande

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built in 1810 instead of the Accademia degli Erranti, neoclassical, with five orders of stages and the Ridotto frescoed with golden stuccoes and mirrors; national monument, with guided tours in summer.
