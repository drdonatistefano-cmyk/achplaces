What to see in Castelnuovo di Farfa

Borgo della Sabina on a hill between the Farfa river and the Riana ditch, born around the Castellum Novum of the late thirteenth century: the whole town is the path of the Museum of the Sabina Oil, between Palazzo Perelli, an eighteenth-century mill and the medieval remains of San Donato, with the Farfa Gorges just a stone's throw away.

Museo dell'Olio della Sabina

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Project born in 2001 that makes the whole country a route on extra virgin olive oil DOP Sabina, which Galeno considered the best of ancient times. It starts from the sixteenth-century Palazzo Perelli (sections on the myth of oil with contemporary sculptures, botany of the Sabin olive tree, olive-tree tradition and memory of the peasants) and continues in the eighteenth-century olive-tree-tensiled animal preserved in the ancient municipal oven, in the alleys of the village and up to the Altomedieval site of San Donato with the ...Giardino degli ulivi del mondo. Works by Maria Lai, Alik Cavaliere, Hidetoshi Nagasawa, Gianandrea Gazzola (the installation of the "Oleophona" and "Ille Strazza." Open Friday, Saturday, Sunday and holidays 10-18, other days on request.

  • Where Via Perelli (Palazzo Perelli), Castelnuovo di Farfa
  • Entry €6, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · visitlazio.com

Chiesa di San Donato

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The 6th century high-medieval building around which the Castellum Sancti Donati was born, destroyed by the Saracens at the beginning of the 10th century and rebuilt in the 11th century by the abbots of Farfa; place of worship at least until the 16th century, in the second 20th century the ruins were incorporated into an agricultural building. There are still niches with frescoes; today it is a stage of the Museo dell'Olio, with the Garden of Olives of the world.

  • Where Località San Donato, Castelnuovo di Farfa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Chiesa di San Nicola di Bari

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A single nave church with Baroque interiors, wall paintings and an ancient organ; Italia.it date 1779, an elliptical plant, rebuilt in 1939 by the Salusturi Galli family. First Sunday of December.

Chiesa della Madonna degli Angeli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Erected in the Jubilee year 1600 as a thank you to the Virgin for the protection from the plague, a circular plant with a small bell tower of the late seventeenth century; collapsed in 1933, it was rebuilt by the Salusturi Galli family. Feast on the first Sunday of August.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
