What to see in Formazza

Walser valley at the extreme north of Piedmont, between Blindenhorn, Punta d'Arbola and Basodino: at Frua the Toce Falls with its 143 meters jump, at Ponte the sixteenth-century Casa Forte with the Walser Museum, at the bottom of the Riale valley, the oldest Walser settlement, and the lake of Morasco.

Cascata del Toce

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

It was 143 metres high, from 1,675 m to 1,532 m high, 60 metres wide at the base; Richard Wagner, Gabriele D'Annunzio, Queen Margherita, De Saussure and Stoppani visited it. The path along the coast was part of the ancient merchant route between Milan and Bern, and on the top stands the hotel Decò of 1923 by Piero Portaluppi. From the 1930s the waters feed the central of Ponte, so the waterfall is in full for short periods only; opening from June to September, with the accessible route Â A waterfall for all Â .

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Casa Forte (Steinhaus) - Museo walser

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Masonry building of 1569, born as a warehouse of goods in transit between Ossola and Switzerland and then prison, guard post and seat of the Ammano, the governor of the valley. Today it hosts the ethnographic museum and exhibitions on the life and history of the Walser, with wooden statues from the German school of the XV-XVI centuries. Open in July and August on Tuesday and Saturday 15:30-17:30, groups by reservation; two floors with stone stairs, without elevator.

  • Where Frazione Ponte 51, Formazza
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: comune.formazza.vb.it · distrettolaghi.it

Riale e oratorio di Sant'Anna

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 1,718 m, the northernmost hamlet of Piedmont and the oldest Walser settlement, on the border with the Goms, the land of origin of the Walser: stone and wood huts, the Ski Museum open for the centenary of the Formazza Ski Club and, on a hill, the nineteenth-century oratory of Sant'Anna, erected in memory of the church of Morasco submerged by the lake, with a sign that recalls the snow of 1951.

  • Where Frazione Riale, Formazza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it

Lago di Morasco

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Artificial lake of the dam built between 1936 and 1940, which feeds the central of Ponte. A ring of about 8.5 km (3 hours, 150 m of altitude difference, G00 signpost) starts from the parking lot of the dam at 1,747 m and turns around the lake, recommended from May to October; in winter it becomes a cross-country trail.

  • Where Diga di Morasco, sopra Riale, Formazza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it

Via del Gries (Via dello Sbrinz)

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient commercial route between Milan and Bern through the Gries pass (2,479 m), today a route of about 150 km in seven stages from Lucerne to Domodossola; every summer a folk caravan of mules retraces it and is welcomed by the Walser delegation of Formazza.

  • Where Da Riale verso il passo del Gries, Formazza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it
