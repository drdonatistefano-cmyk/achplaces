What to see in Sant'Anatolia di Narco

Borgo della Valnerina which preserves intact the medieval structure, with walls and doors of the fourteenth century and towers of the fifteenth century, and the tradition of hemp told by the Museum of Hemp; in the territory the Romanesque abbey of Saints Felice and Mauro at Castel San Felice and the fortified villages of Case and Gavelli.

Museo della Canapa

Museums · Themed

Ethnographic Museum of the Ecomuseum of the Umbro Apennines, inaugurated in 2008 in the sixteenth-century Town Hall on the main square: tools collected from the seventies, textile collection, a frame of the late eighteenth century and the installation Spinning Dolls of the British artist Liliane Lijn. In the weaving workshops, manual frames are still being worked; the spaces are finished with hemp and lime plaster.

  • Where Piazza principale, Sant'Anatolia di Narco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodellacanapa.it

Sources: museodellacanapa.it · umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Abbazia dei Santi Felice e Mauro

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Among the most important Romanesque churches in Umbria, rebuilt at the end of the 12th century on the place where the Syrian monk Mauro erected a church and a monastery in memory of his son Felice, who died in 535. The façade with rosette tells the life of the saints and the killing of the dragon; inside, a single nave, presbytery with cosmatesque plutei, crypt with three apses with pink stone sarcophagus, an Adoration of the Magi of the first fifteenth century and the blessing Christ attributed to the Master of Eggi.

  • Where Castel San Felice, Sant'Anatolia di Narco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Castel San Felice

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fraction on a hill above the Black which preserves the fortified centre plant, born after the end of the XII century; inside the walls the church of San Sebastiano, from the western gate the view of the abbey and the bridge over the Black and the departure of the path "La Pia" , about 1.5 km to Sant'Anatolia passing through Santa Maria di Narco. It is the birthplace of the brothers Campani, opticians and watchmakers of the seventeenth century.

  • Where Castel San Felice, Sant'Anatolia di Narco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Borgo medievale e castello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The castle, founded according to sources in 1198 after the destruction of that of Narco, preserves doors, walls, defensive towers and wounds; the walls are of the fourteenth century and the two towers of the fifteenth century. The plain below is called the canapine tree for the cultivation of hemp along the Black.

  • Where Sant'Anatolia di Narco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Outside the walls, built around a newsstand with the Madonna and Child between Saints Giacomo and Antonio Abate, a fresco of the mid-15th century attributed to the Master of Eggi, enlarged in 1578 by a pupil of Spain with the Assumption among the Apostles. Within two Roman funeral inscriptions, a wooden crucifix attributed to Francesco da Sangallo and the wooden group of the Dolenti.

Chiesa di Sant'Anatolia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church dedicated to a Roman martyr of the third century, with painting decorations of the XIV-XV centuries, a marble palace inlaid with a 17th century Neapolitan school at the main altar and paintings of the same period.

Sala Campani (ex convento di Santa Croce)

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The Franciscan monastery of the XIII-XIV centuries, today hotel, preserves the sailing bell tower, frescoes with the life of St. John of Capestrano and a Last Supper of unknown author; the ancient church has become the Sala Campani, exhibition space dedicated to the Campani brothers, opticians and watchmakers of Castel San Felice.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Caso e Gavelli

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified villages on the ancient transhumance of Mount Coscerno, known for the churches with pictorial cycles in part attributed to Giovanni di Pietro known as Spain: Santa Cristina and Santa Maria delle Grazie a Caso, San Michele Arcangelo a Gavelli.

Ciclovia del Fiume Nera

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

28 km from Sant'Anatolia di Narco alle Cascate delle Marmore on the route of the former Spoleto-Norcia railway, between bridges and tunnels, through Scheggino, Ceselli, Arrone and Ferentillo; easy, with a more demanding final stretch.
