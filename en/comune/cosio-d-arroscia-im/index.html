Museo delle erbe «In Herbis Salus»

Museums · Themed

Museum dedicated to the typical medicinal herbs of the Maritime Alps, in the former town hall next to the Oratory of the Assumption and part of the Museum of the Territory "I Volti dell'Ubagu" . On the ground floor about 150 species of aromatic plants, medicinal and edible with Latin and vulgar names, and machinery and stills for dyes, extracts, herbal teas and distillates; on the upper floor a didactic laboratory with hemeroteca. It is named after the agronomist of Cosio Giovanni Alessandri, Provincial Herborist; since 2020 there is also a room of edible flowers.

open July-August 10-12 and 15-17, in the rest of the year only for events and by reservation

Borgo museo a cielo aperto, Oratorio dell'Assunta e parrocchiale di San Pietro

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The center preserves the medieval system, with winding streets between houses connected by archivolti, born to defend against rigid mountain winters; the inhabitants have transformed it into an open-air museum with tools of shepherds and peasants, barber tools, home environments, vintage clothes and games exposed in alleys and balconies. The late Baroque Oratory of the Assunta has for its bell tower a late-Roman defensive tower of the fifth century transformed in the thirteenth century in Romanesque bell tower cuspidato with two-fores in terracotta; the parish church of St. Peter the Apostle is of the first half of the seventeenth century, in late Renaissance forms.

Chiesa di San Pietro del Fossato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

To the south of the village, where the Roman farm villa and the primitive village stood, the church has a rectangular apse and frescoes from the 15th century; in the same place, along the ancient mule tracks, there are other frescoed chapels.
