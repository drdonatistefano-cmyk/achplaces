What to see in Calvi dell'Umbria

Borgo of ancient origins (the Bronze Age), mentioned from the IX century and then long in the Papal State, with a historic center of alleys, arches and remains of walls and towers. It's the "Paese del Presepe": the Monastery of the Orsoline houses a monumental nativity scene in 16th century terracotta and since 1982 Italian and foreign artists paint nativity on the walls of the houses.

Museo del Monastero delle Orsoline

Museums · Old masters

In the Palace of the Ferrini family (completed in 1606), passed to the Municipality in 1715 provided to host a monastery, with monumental facade designed by Ferdinando Fuga since 1739. The museum, opened in 2002 and re-equipped in 2012 with the Chiomenti-Vassalli collection, exhibits among other things the Magdalena penitente by Guido Reni, the Andromeda by Francesco Furini, the Parabola of the blind by Pieter Bruegel the Younger, works by Pietro da Cortona, Pompeo Batoni, Gaspar Van Wittel and Magnasco, and the Immaculate Conception attributed to Masucci. You can also visit the 18th century monastic kitchens with original stone fireplaces.

Presepe monumentale e Oratorio di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

In the apse of the former church of Sant'Antonio, today part of the Museo delle Orsoline, more than thirty polychrome terracotta statues bigger than the real made between 1541 and 1546 by the Abruzzo brothers James and Raffaele da Montereale; St. Joseph, Our Lady and Child are probably the work of the two masters, perhaps the rest of the workshop. St. Anthony and St. Brigid share a single façade on Piazza Mazzini. Visitable all year round.

Centro storico e murales del presepe

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

A combination of medieval alleys, arches and vaults with remains of walls and towers. Since 1982 Italian and foreign artists have been painting on the walls of the houses every year their interpretation of the nativity: today the works are over 50 and form an open-air gallery.

  • Where Centro storico, Calvi dell'Umbria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Chiesa e convento di San Francesco

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

About 500 m from the walls, on the ridge between the valleys of Otricoli and Magliano Sabina, rises with the convent of San Berardo, the friar of Calvi converted by the preaching of St. Francis in 1213 and martyr in Morocco. The church, documented in 1291, is a single nave with a barrel vault and ten side chapels; in 1527 the Lanzichenecchi plundered and burned the oratory. In the cloister, with a well of the thirteenth century, there are 17th-century frescoes with stories of St. Francis.

Eremo di San Pancrazio

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the summit of Monte San Pancrazio, near Calvi, remain the ruins of an ancient shrine-hermitage, reached by a panoramic road between woods and clearings.
