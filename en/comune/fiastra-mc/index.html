What to see in Fiastra

Municipality of Parco Nazionale dei Monti Sibillini (with the former municipality of Acquacanina) overlooking Lake Fiastra, the largest hydroelectric basin of the Marche, from which start the paths for the Red Blades; it preserves Romanesque churches, the Abbey of Santa Maria di Rio Sacro and the ruins of the castle of Da Varano. After the earthquake of 2016 historic center, monuments and museums are partially usable.

Lago di Fiastra

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Artificial basin at 685 m formed by the dam on the Fiastrone (86 m high and 360 m long), at the foot of the Sibillini: the main beach of San Lorenzo al Lago is guarded, there are large stretches of free beach with bathing in the marked areas, two campsites, rental of pedalos, canoes and bikes and the Adventure Park with zipline on the lake. The Nature Path starts from San Lorenzo and follows the right bank for about two hours, with 900 m accessible to people with disabilities.

  • Where San Lorenzo al Lago, Fiastra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · sibillini.net

Lame Rosse

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Pinnacles and pebble towers cemented by clay and clay limits, shaped by erosion, rust-coloured walls. You can reach it on foot from the dam of the lake: about 6-7 km round trip, 200 m of difference in altitude and about 3 hours, first on dirt road and then almost always in the shade of a holm oak; alternatively you start from the viewpoint of the Ruffella passing through Fiegni.

  • Where Diga del Lago di Fiastra, San Lorenzo al Lago, Fiastra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Abbazia di San Paolo Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Romanesque church of the 11th century with a polychrome wooden group of the 16th century (Madonna with Child), a canvas of the Baciccia (17th century) with the conversion of Saint Paul and a Baroque crucifix; the bell tower is from 1914. From the back of the church you can enjoy the view of the lake.

after the earthquake of 2016 the monuments are partially usable; the card of the Park describes the state prior to the earthquake

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Abbazia di Santa Maria di Rio Sacro

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Benedictine Romanesque Abbey in the valley of the Rio Sacro, in Acquacanina, with a Romanesque crypt with three naves, a crucifix of the Lazio school of the twelfth century, a wooden group of the Madonna with the Child of the sixteenth century, the small canvas of the Madonna of the Suffragio of Carlo Maratta and a fresco of the Martyrdom of San Sebastiano attributed to Jerome of John.

after the earthquake of 2016 the monuments are partially usable; the card of the Park describes the state prior to the earthquake

  • Where Rio Sacro, Acquacanina, Fiastra
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.letsmarche.it

Sources: sibillini.net · letsmarche.it
