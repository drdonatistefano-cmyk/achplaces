What to see in Parabita

Salento country dominated by the Castriota Castle, with a historic centre rich in sixteenth-seventh-century palaces, the Sanctuary of the Madonna della Coltura which houses the monolith with the image of the Madonna and the Giannelli Art Gallery in Palazzo Ferrari; in the territory the Grotta delle Veneri, discovered in 1966.

Santuario della Madonna della Coltura

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Modern building with Romanesque-Gothic lines, designed by Napoleon Pagliarulo after the demolition in 1913 of the previous Baroque church and inaugurated in 1920. Under the canopy of the main altar the monolith with the Madonna Panagia, Byzantine fresco perhaps of the twelfth century repainted in the seventeenth century, that tradition wants found by a farmer in the district Bread; a large fresco of the counter-facade tells the discovery. Patron Festival on the last weekend of May with the race "currature" .

Castello dei Castriota

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The castle, built by the Angioini after 1231 and renovated in the sixteenth century by Evangelista Menga for Pirro Castriota, was restored in 1911 by architect Adolfo Avena; it dominates the village and has been restored to the sixteenth-century forms.

Pinacoteca Enrico Giannelli in Palazzo Ferrari

Museums · Modern art

In the nineteenth century Palazzo Ferrari, frescoed by Agesilao Flora and donated to the City by America Ferrari, last of the family, with a walled garden of orange trees and cisterns: the collection donated in 1924 by the painter Enrico Giannelli, about fifty works by Neapolitan painters and sculptors between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, and a section of contemporary art set up in the eighties.

Centro storico e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center preserves architectural remains of the sixteenth century and the gates of the walls (Porta di Lecce, Porta di Gallipoli, Porta Falsa). Among the palaces: Palazzo D'Alfonso in Piazza Umberto I, with two-faced portal and fresco "La Primavera" by Agesilao Flora; Palazzo Ramis with the sixteenth-century high relief of Annunciation and Deposition; the Palazzo dei Veneziani with the icon of San Marco; the Rua Chiesa Madre with the external fresco of the Crucifixion; Palazzo Ardito with baroque masks.
