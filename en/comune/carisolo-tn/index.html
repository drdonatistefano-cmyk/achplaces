What to see in Carisolo

Country of Val Rendena at the entrance of the Val Genova, in the Adamello Brenta Nature Park: on the cliff above the Sarca, the church of Santo Stefano with the Dance Macabra and the Charlemagne dei Baschenis, lower on the Antica Vetreria nineteenth century, and above the village the medieval hermitage of San Martino.

Chiesa di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Cemetery church on a rock spur overlooking the Sarca, above the Antica Vetreria, first documented in 1244; the decorations of the Baschenis began in 1461 and the frescoes of Simone II Baschenis are dated between 1519 and 1534. On the southern outer wall the Macabra Dance with the seven deadly sins, with the characters arranged according to the medieval social hierarchy; inside, on the north wall, the great fresco of 1534 with Charlemagne assisting the baptism of a catechumen by Pope Urban I, linked to the legend of the passage of the emperor.

Antica Vetreria - Museo del Vetro

Museums · Themed

In 1804 the members Bolognini and Pernici opened at the entrance of the Val Genova, in a loop of Sarca, a "Fabrica dei cristali" that exploited water, timber and quartz of the Val di Borzago quarry, with glass masters coming from Bohemia; closed in 1888 and Carlo Pernici made holiday homes. Of the kiln building, partly dismantled in World War I, the perimeter walls and a small track with trolleys remain; today it houses a small historical museum and glass processing courses with internationally renowned masters and artists.

Eremo di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

White church from the red roof on the slopes of Mount Lancia, above the village, documented by 1312; in 1485 the hermit Bergamo Baldessarre de Pluzana rebuilt it to live there, with the permission of the prince bishop John Hinderbach and the support of the communities of Rendena, and was guarded by a hermit until 1850. Inside, prayer benches and a small altar with a painting of St. Martin.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The fifteenth-century church in the old village, the first stage of the itinerary of the historic centre; it preserves a seventeenth-century wooden altar by Giovanni Battista Polana di Roncone, purchased by the parish in the second half of the eighteenth century.

Santuario della Beata Vergine del Potere

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Nineteenth-century shrine in a small clearing between the village and the Antica Vetreria, originally the starting point of the Via Crucis that goes up to Santo Stefano; in a niche on the right of the aisle preserves the Madonna and the Child from an ancient medieval capital.

Castagneto di Carisolo - Biblioteca nel bosco

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Historical chestnut tree restored in 2009, already praised in 1673 by the historian Michelangelo Mariani: the inhabitants planted the trees on the municipal soil with a privilege of Roman origin that gave them the property of fruit and wood, used for timber, tannin and chestnuts. Today we see secular chestnut trees, terraces and circular walls of containment.
