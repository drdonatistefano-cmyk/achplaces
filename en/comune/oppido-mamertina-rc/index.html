What to see in Oppido Mamertina

A bishop's city rebuilt in the district of Tuba after the earthquake of 1783, with the great neoclassical cathedral of 1935 and the diocesan Museum that houses a San Sebastiano di Benedetto da Maiano; in the surroundings the ruins of Oppido Vecchia and the Hellenistic-Roman excavations of Mella.

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Inaugurated in 1935 on a project by Ettore Baldanza after the earthquake of 1908 had destroyed the cathedral of 1841, of which only the Chapel of the Blessed Sacrament remains; neoclassical with three naves, 62 meters long, with wooden ceiling with drawers painted by D. Cricket. It houses a marble Madonna from the 16th century, perhaps Gagini from Oppido Vecchia, the chapel of the Santissima Annunziata with the wooden group of A. Head and the Baroque processional throne of the Annunziata; in the Chapter Room vestments bishops from the seventeenth century and an Ecce Homo wooden donated by San Carlo Borromeo.

  • Where Piazza F. M. Zuco 29, Oppido Mamertina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.oppidomamertina.rc.it

Sources: comune.oppidomamertina.rc.it

MuDOP - Museo Diocesano Oppido-Palmi

Museums · Old masters

In a building next to the cathedral designed by Achille Menghi (first stone 1938), eight rooms in chronological order with liturgical objects, paintings and sculptures in wood and stone from churches and convents of the diocese, with ample space to the earthquake of 1783. A St. Sebastiano di Benedetto da Maiano (second half of the 15th century), exhibited in Matera, the Louvre and the Castello Sforzesco between 2019 and 2021, is the main work. Times indicated: Monday, Wednesday and Friday 8-14, Saturday 9-12.

Reservation required according to the MiC card (updated to January 2022)

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Oppido Mamertina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.oppidomamertina.rc.it

Palazzo Grillo e Museo della Civiltà Artigiana e Contadina

Museums · Themed · Free entry

A noble residence built by Giuseppe Grillo after the earthquake of the eighteenth century, purchased and restored by the Municipality; it houses the workshops of the Craftsmanship and the Mamertini Artists and the Museum of the Crafts and Farms Civilization, open to the public free of charge on 24 May 2026 as a first step towards a permanent opening.

no regular hours: experimental openings during the days of the preparation of workshops

  • Where Oppido Mamertina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.oppidomamertina.rc.it

Sources: comune.oppidomamertina.rc.it

Oppido Vecchia e area archeologica di Mella

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Viewpoint

On the hill of the plateau of the Melle, between the rivers Tricuccio and Cumi, remain parts of the walls and doors, the castle called Aragonese, the cathedral, the cloister of the Minimi and some houses of the medieval city destroyed by the earthquake of 1783. Nearby, in Mella district, the excavations of a Hellenistic-Roman settlement with masonry, a cobbled road and houses with hardwood floors; the finds are in Reggio Calabria.

the Municipality offers guided tours per day (May-June 2026, €50 by shuttle from Gioia Tauro); the excavations are easy from May to July with archaeologists at work

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches
