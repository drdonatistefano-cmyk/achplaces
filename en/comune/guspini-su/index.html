What to see in Guspini

Center of the Middle Campidano with the columnar basalts twenty meters high in the heart of the village and, on the hills, the mining village of Montevecchio, for a century and a half the main mining pole of the south-west and today in the Geominerario Park; in the historical center the court houses of the itinerary Domus Guspini and the Romanesque Santa Maria of Malta.

Galleria anglosarda - Pozzo Sant'Antonio

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Underground gallery of about one kilometer excavated in the galena line of the Arburese and Guspinese, up to the well Sant'Antonio, begun in 1852 by the Royal Anglo-Sardinian Company from which it takes its name. Guided tour of about 40 minutes; constant internal temperature of 15-16 °C, recommended closed and low shoes. From November to March Saturday and Sunday a departure at 13:15, on the other days by reservation; ticket office in Montevecchio, 15 minutes before.

  • Where SP 66, località Miniera di Montevecchio, Guspini
  • Entry €12, free 0-6 years
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.minieradimontevecchio.it

Miniera di Montevecchio, percorso Sant'Antonio

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The well, excavated from 1853 on sixteen levels up to more than 500 meters of depth, is dominated by a Neo-Gothic crenellated tower that hid the extraction structures; the coiled winch, electrified in 1919, carried men and minerals to the surface until closing in 1988. You can visit tower, winch and compressor rooms, workshops, forge, lighthouse, power plant, workers' accommodations with numbered rooms on the long corridor and storage with mineralized rock samples. Only guided tours (about 45 minutes, 90 for two routes), in Italian and English.

  • Where Piazzale Rolandi 1, Montevecchio, Guspini
  • Entry €5, reduced €3,50
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.minieradimontevecchio.it

Sources: minieradimontevecchio.it · sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Villaggio minerario di Montevecchio: Direzione, Officine e Piccalinna

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Viewpoint

It was granted by Carlo Alberto to Giovanni Antonio Sanna in 1848, in 1865 with 1,100 workers it was the most important mine in the Kingdom of Italy; it closed in 1991 and is part of the Park Geominerario of Sardinia. In addition to the well Sant'Antonio you can visit the Palazzina of the direction (1870-1877), with the restored rooms of the first floor including the blue room, the collection of Castoldi director and the chapel of Santa Barbara; the workshops with the foundry of 1885 and the room of the wooden models; the yard of Piccalinna with the machine of extraction from 120 horses of the late nineteenth century, still working, and the villas of the chiefs and the working houses.

  • Where Piazza Rolandi, Montevecchio, Guspini
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.minieradimontevecchio.it

Basalti colonnari di Guspini

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

A wall of vertical basaltic prisms twenty meters high, placed like organ reeds, on Mount Cèpera (Cuccureddu 'e Zéppara), a small volcanic cone of 167 meters: they were formed about three million years ago by the slow cooling of the lava. The hill was raised from the nineteenth century; closed the quarry, it is today a natural monument, with a tour of the village. In Italy similar formations are found only in Veneto and Sicily.

  • Where Monte Cèpera, Guspini
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Casa Murgia, museo del vino e dell'olio

Museums · House museums · Free entry

The courtyard house of Campidano left to the Municipality by the last heir, stop of the itinerary Domus Guspini in the historic center: it tells in parallel the family of landowners of Ignazio Murgia, doctor, and Anniva Garau and the viticulture and olive growing of the community. Dining room, study and kitchen on the ground floor, farm cycles and tools on the first floor, rooms rebuilt in the attic, cellar with tasting and, in the garden, well, water trough and a bunker of the Second World War.
