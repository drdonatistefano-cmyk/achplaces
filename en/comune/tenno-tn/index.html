What to see in Tenno

A few kilometers north of Riva del Garda, between dry terraces of olive trees, vines and chestnut trees: the medieval village of Canale, mentioned since 1211, the castle with the fortified village of Frapporta and Lake Tenno turquoise color.

9 places to visit in Tenno, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo medievale di Canale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Stone village at 428 meters, mentioned since 1211 and remained almost intact, between the most beautiful villages in Italy and Orange Flag; since the post-war period it has attracted artists from all over Europe. Outside the village, the statue of the Villa del Monte reminds us of the ancient autonomous administration; in August there is the rustic Middle Ages, in winter the Christmas markets.

  • Where Canale, Tenno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: comune.tenno.tn.it · visittrentino.info · italia.it

Casa degli Artisti «Giacomo Vittone»

Museums · Modern art

Dedicated to the Turinese painter Giacomo Vittone, who was its promoter, purchased and restored in 1965 and managed by the municipalities of Riva del Garda, Arco and Tenno: he holds a collection of works, hosts exhibitions and events and is a residence for artists visiting from all over Europe.

  • Where Canale, Tenno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: comune.tenno.tn.it · visittrentino.info

Museo degli attrezzi agricoli

Museums · Themed

In the Centre \"Aldo Gorfer" , in rooms with stone vaults: tools for fields, viticulture, breeding and processing of milk , lumberjack , carpenter and barrel , with the reconstruction of stable , cave and open kitchen of a farmhouse .

  • Where Canale, Ville del Monte, Tenno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visittrentino.info · comune.tenno.tn.it

Lago di Tenno

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lake 570 meters between the woods, with turquoise waters to the intense blue, reached by a long staircase and short paths; in summer you swim and there are pedalos, and with the low water appears a small island joined to the shore. From 2024 it is a Local Nature Reserve; from Canale you will arrive on foot in about 30 minutes.

  • Where Lago di Tenno, Tenno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Cascata del Varone

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Sito archeologico di San Martino

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

High ground on the hill of San Martino with traces from the Neolithic to the early Middle Ages and continuous use from the Roman Age to the late antiquity; enclosed areas and panels guide the visit.
