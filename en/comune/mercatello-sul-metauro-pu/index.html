What to see in Mercatello sul Metauro

Medieval village of Massa Trabaria surrounded by thirteenth-century walls along the Metauro, with the Collegiate Church of Saints Peter and Paul, the church-museum of Saint Francis with the Crucifix of John of Rimini and the Romanesque bridge with three arches.

8 places to visit in Mercatello sul Metauro, in the province of Pesaro e Urbino (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa e Museo di San Francesco

Museums · Old masters

Romanesque-Gothic church that since 1926 has collected works of other sacred buildings, with the 2005 exhibition extended to the convent: the 1309 Crucifix by Giovanni da Rimini, works by Giovanni Baronzio and Benedetto da Maiano, paintings by Giorgio Picchi (1582-86), the rounds of Ottaviano Ubaldini and Federico da Montefeltro attributed to Domenico Rosselli and Francesco by Giorgio Martini, a fourteenth-century window and an Etruscan and Italian archaeological section.

  • Where Piazza San Francesco, Mercatello sul Metauro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Collegiata dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises on the ancient parish church of San Pietro d'Ico, around which the village grew since 1235, preserves the Romanesque structure with Gothic windows and internal basilica rebuilt in the eighteenth century; it preserves the icon of the Madonna delle Grazie, the Immaculate of Raffaellino del Colle and a San Carlo Borromeo by Giovan Francesco Guerrieri.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Mercatello sul Metauro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it · letsmarche.it

Museo della Collegiata

Museums · Old masters

Founded in 1973 by Don Sergio Campana in two rooms on the left aisle of the Collegiata: processional crosses from the fourteenth to the seventeenth century, a fourteenth-century turbole, a peace in ivory and ebony with the Pietà (XV century), two golden wooden statues of Saints Peter and Paul of the school of Antonio Bencivenni, silver and vestments.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Mercatello sul Metauro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Centro storico e mura

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Ancient castle of Massa Trabaria surrounded by walls of the thirteenth century, protected by the Metauro and the torrent Sant'Antonio; some houses have the doors of the dead, raised exits linked to a tradition that wanted to keep away the souls of the dead.

  • Where Centro storico, Mercatello sul Metauro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
