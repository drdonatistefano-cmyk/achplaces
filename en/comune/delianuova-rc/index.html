What to see in Delianuova

The village of the chestnuts is located on the Tyrrhenian side of Aspromonte, between 500 and 700 meters, surrounded by chestnut trees, holm oaks and abetinas up to the Piani di Carmelia; in the village the churches of San Nicola and dell'Assunta and the Garibaldi Virtual Museum on the Aspromonte countryside of 1862.

Chiesa di San Nicola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

17th century church in pseudo-Renaissance style, with inlaid coffered ceiling. On the high altar in golden wood, a Madonna with the Renaissance Child in Carrara marble from the Gaginesque school (XV-XVI century); behind the altar a paschal candle made from a column with Byzantine capital of the 12th century. Pulpito di Giuseppe Licastro e Giuseppe Ascrizzi (1886), statues by Saverio Scutellà (1820) and Raffaele Angelo Musitano (1877), a Sorrowful Sculptor Ferdinando Perathoner from the Garden; on the façade statues by Elia and Moses by Domenico Papalia.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele, Delianuova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.delianuova.rc.it

Sources: comune.delianuova.rc.it

Chiesa di Maria Santissima Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt with a single nave after the earthquake of 1895, it has a chestnut door carved in 1912 of the Caminiti family with the Assumption and the Annunciation. It houses a marble Madonna from the Messinese school, paintings and statues by Raffaele Angelo Musitano (San Rocco 1894, San Raffaele with Tobia 1892), Carpente del Lecce Giuseppe Malecore and, on the high altar, an Assumption that is believed to come from the old church of Paracorio, before the earthquake of 1783.

  • Where SP2, Delianuova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.delianuova.rc.it

Sources: comune.delianuova.rc.it

Museo virtuale garibaldino in Aspromonte

Museums · City history

Born within the framework of the project that has restored the Mausoleum of Garibaldi in Aspromonte, it re-reads with multimedia technologies the iconographic material of the Fondazione Spadolini-Nuova Antologia, the Central Museum of the Risorgimento and the DIGIMA project to tell the campaign of Aspromonte of 1862, designed especially for young people.

Piani di Carmelia e boschi dell'Aspromonte

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village is surrounded by chestnut trees; above 700 meters, in Serro Grande and Serro Piccolo, dense woods of holm oak, over 1,000 meters white fir and beech, with strawberry woods between April and July. Around 1,000 meters the plateau of the Piani di Carmelia with the pine forests; at the Madonna della Salute pine trees about 25 meters high.

  • Where Piani di Carmelia, Delianuova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page comune.delianuova.rc.it

Sources: comune.delianuova.rc.it
