What to see in Alberona

The village of the Dauni Mountains, 732 meters, with a medieval old town of temple memory, the Tower of the Grand Prior of the twelfth century, historical fountains such as the Mutant Fountain and, from Muraglione, the view of the Tabletop up to Gargano and Tremiti.

9 places to visit in Alberona, in the province of Foggia (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa Madre della Natività di Maria Vergine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Palazzo e Torre del Gran Priore

Arco Calabrese e Arco dei Mille

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Arch Calabrese of the fourteenth century is the only wooden arch in the area and is perfectly preserved; the Arch of the Thousands, of the fifteenth century, opens onto a wide panorama.

  • Where Alberona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Fontana Muta e Fontanella

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

In the village of the many springs, the monumental nineteenth-century Muta Fountain pours water silently into a large stone pool, while the Fontanella, shaped like a small house with a sloping roof, offers fresh water to drink.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
