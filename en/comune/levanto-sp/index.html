What to see in Levanto

Borgo della Riviera spezzina at the gates of the Cinque Terre, with the ancient village surrounded by medieval walls, the 13th century Town Loggia, the Gothic church of Sant'Andrea with white and green bands, the convent of the Annunziata, a long seafront with beaches loved by surfers and the promontory of Punta Mesco towards Monterosso.

10 places to visit in Levanto, in the province of La Spezia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo antico, Loggia Comunale e porto medievale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Town Loggia in Piazza del Popolo, dating back to the 13th century, is one of the few late medieval loggias that survived in Liguria and preserves a fifteenth-century fresco of the Annunciation. Around, via Guani with the Renaissance palaces (including the Palazzo delle Sirene at no. 37), via Garibaldi with painted facades and nine frescoed caricatures called "Grottesche" (Grottesche), the large medieval arches of Piazza da Passano and, between Via della Compera and the nearby alleys, the remains of the medieval port with a dock over 50 meters long. Cavour Square with arcades is the heart of the city.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Levanto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.levanto.sp.it · italia.it · lamialiguria.it

Chiesa di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built from 1222 and then enlarged, it is a remarkable example of Gothic Ligurian with three naves, with the façade with alternating bands of white Carrara marble and local green serpentine; inside a wooden choir of 1589 and a sixteenth century glass. Evening concerts are held on the churchyard.

  • Where Piazzale di Sant'Andrea (da via Toso), Levanto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · lamialiguria.it · comune.levanto.sp.it

Mura e Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval and sixteenth-century walls preserve three towers and the only surviving door, the Door of Fossato or Water. The castle is a sixteenth-century fortress with circular tower and walls on a hill south of the village, behind Sant'Andrea; it is privately owned and can be seen from the outside. Tradition has it that underground passages to the beach and the Annunziata depart from here.

  • Where Porta dell'Acqua, Levanto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.levanto.sp.it · lamialiguria.it · italia.it

Ex Convento degli Agostiniani e Mostra della cultura materiale

Museums · Themed

A medieval building overlooking the ancient canal port, mentioned in the statues of 1377, with decorations from the XV-XVI century on the first floor; from 1600 Augustinian convent, then hospital and school, was recovered for the Jubilee of 2000 and hosts the Hostalia del Mare hostel, an auditorium and the permanent exhibition of material culture. Opened in 1981, it exhibits about 300 objects of peasant work in six cycles (e.g. tools, vine and wine, mills and mills, breeding, home, games and traditions), with a section on the dialect and reconstruction of the canal port. In the summer open in the evening, 21:30-23:30, on Friday and Saturday (and also on Sunday in the second half of July). Tel. 0187 817776.

  • Where Via San Nicolò, Levanto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.levanto.sp.it · italia.it

Chiesa e Convento dell'Annunziata

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Franciscan complex of the fifteenth century isolated on a hill, with works by Pier Francesco Sacchi and Bernardo Strozzi and the former refectory with paintings by Gian Battista Casoni.

Oratorio di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The seat of the oldest confraternity of Levanto, next to the remains of the church of Our Lady of the Coast, houses a nineteenth-century organ by Giosuè Agati. At the end of July the Feast of St. James, or Feast of the Sea, brings historical processions, flag-wavers and fires.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Lungomare e spiagge

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The long promenade accompanies the main beach; the beach behind the pier is among the best known and is frequented by surfers, that of the Stone has a low and sandy seabed suitable for children, that of Vallesanta is of mixed pebbles, interrupted by the rock of Bernardone that hides a small creek.

  • Where Lungomare, Levanto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · lamialiguria.it

Punta Mesco, Semaforo ed Eremo di Sant'Antonio

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Promontory at the western end of the Cinque Terre that separates the bay of Levanto from that of Monterosso, with sandstone walls at layers overlooking the sea. On top of the Sepaforo del Mesco, used by the military until the Second World War and today abandoned, and the medieval hermitage of St. Anthony of Mesco, documented in 1380. It is reached on foot with two paths in the woods, from Levanto or Monterosso.

  • Where Punta Mesco, Levanto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Montale, Pieve di San Siro e Torre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Fraction at the gates of Levanto where in Roman times the settlement of Celua stood; there are the parish church of San Siro, of the twelfth century and then rebuilt in Baroque forms, the tower of the twelfth century and the oratory of Santa Croce.
