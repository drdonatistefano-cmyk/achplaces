Centro storico e resti del castello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ruins of the castle incorporated in the fabric of the village and the semicircle of the ancient walls, between stone houses and terracotta almost intact.

Necropoli etrusca di Casa Nocera

History and architecture · Archaeology

The funeral complex of the Etruscan warrior princes, near a village of the 7th century BC: here emerged the Warrior Princes, among the oldest statues of European art.
