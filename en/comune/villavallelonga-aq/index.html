What to see in Villavallelonga

Marsicano village at 1005 meters at the foot of Mount Lent, in the National Park of Abruzzo, Lazio and Molise: here are the Visitor Centre of the Bear, the museum dedicated to the botanist Loreto Grande and, in Val Cervara, a beech wood included in the UNESCO site of the ancient primordial beech woods of Europe.

Centro Visita dell'Orso

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Park Visitor Center at 1005 meters dedicated to the Marsican brown bear, symbol of the Park: museum with panels, bulletin boards, dioramas and temporary exhibitions, projection room with naturalistic films, 3D station on fauna, space for children and picnic areas. Along the Nature Path you can see a bear in semi-freedom in a large fenced area.

Museo Loreto Grande

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Opened on 15 August 2021 and dedicated to the history, nature and traditions of Villavallelonga, with furnishings in large part of recycled cardboard designed by the architect Rosa Ferrari. The rooms tell the life of the botanist Loreto Grande, recreate a forest in a sensory room and display the costumes of the ugly iconic masquerade of the feast of St. Anthony Abbot, naturalized animals including a large bear, grasses and pictures of the local flora. Summer hours indicated by the Municipality (2024): 10-12 and 16-18.

  • Where Villavallelonga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.parcoabruzzo.it

Faggeta vetusta di Val Cervara

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Beech of about 20 hectares, about 1600 meters in Val Cervara, convales of the Aceretta between Monte Marcolano and Monte Schiena di Cavallo. The dendrochronological analyses of the University of Tuscia have rebuilt a series from 1523 to today, with a beech of at least 503 rings; it is a forest where the trees are born and die without human intervention, inhabited by deer, wolves and marsican bear. Valle Cervara is among the beech forests of the Park included on the UNESCO site of the ancient primordial beech woods of the Carpathians and other regions of Europe.

Prati d'Angro e sentieri della Vallelonga

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the Prati d'Angro, with the refuge of the same name, start several paths of the Park: towards the Sorgente Puzza (2 hours), the Valico Aceretta (1 hour and 30) and the Vallone Acquaro (45 minutes, easy); from the Madonna della Lanna you go up to the refuge in 1 hour and 15. Other routes lead to the Coppo dell'Orso Refuge, also directly from the village (4 hours).

  • Where Prati d'Angro, Villavallelonga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.parcoabruzzo.it

Sources: parcoabruzzo.it
