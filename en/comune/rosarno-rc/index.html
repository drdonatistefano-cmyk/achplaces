What to see in Rosarno

Cittadina della Piana di Gioia Tauro was built on the site of the ancient Medma, a town founded by the Greeks of Locri: the Archaeological Museum (2014) and the archaeological park of Pian delle Vigne with the famous medmea terracotta; in the village the Clock Tower (1812) and the churches of Purgatory and Rosary.

Museo archeologico di Medma

Museums · Archaeological

Established in 2014, it exhibits finds of the ancient Medma, a Locrian foundation: the large necropolis about one kilometer south of the town (about 600 tombs of the VI-III century BC, with burial and incineration), the sanctuaries along an ideal sacred street with the votive crest of contrada Calderazzo discovered by Paolo Orsi, about 33 meters long and filled with terracotta, ceramic and metal offerings, and one of the most important collections of terracotta medmee statuettes, dedicated mainly to Persephone. In closing, finds from the excavations in the city and part of the Gangemi collection.

  • Where Via Filippo Medma 1, Rosarno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismo.reggiocal.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sabap-rc.cultura.gov.it · turismo.reggiocal.it

Parco archeologico dell'antica Medma

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

About 12 hectares in the north-western part of the terrace of Pian delle Vigne, where the archaeological remains blend with greenery and a centuries-old olive grove; excavations from the nineteenth century and then in 1912-1914 to Piano delle Vigne and on the hill of Nolio Carozzo. The route touches the remains of a large public building, classical houses with wells and furnaces and the sacred building of Calderazzo, excavated between 2014 and 2018 near the place of discovery of Orsi.

  • Where Pian delle Vigne, Rosarno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismo.reggiocal.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismo.reggiocal.it

Torre dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Built in 1812 in Piazza San Giovanni Bosco, at the end of Corso Garibaldi, it survived the earthquakes of 1894 and 1908.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa del Purgatorio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church linked to the cult of the dead, with a bell dated 1649 and the "varetta" with the statues of the Deposed Christ and of the Risen Christ; the statue of Christ is the protagonist of the Affruntata and the processions of Holy Week.

  • Where Rosarno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.reggiocal.it

Sources: turismo.reggiocal.it
