What to see in Bussi sul Tirino

Country 344 meters in the National Park of Gran Sasso and Monti della Laga, dominated by the Medici castle-palace and crossed by the Tirino, among the clearest rivers in Italy, to be walked by canoe or on foot; along the road stands the Benedictine church of Santa Maria di Cartignano, national monument since 1902.

Chiesa di Santa Maria di Cartignano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Benedictine church with three naves with semicircular apse and sailing bell tower, documented since 1021: it was a cell of Montecassino, grange of San Liberatore at Maiella and then of the Celestines. It is known for its shape, without the central roof, with the ruins restored; the thirteenth-century frescoes of Armanino da Modena, detached in the sixties, are at the National Museum of Eagle. Church is tratural along the paths of transhumance, it is a national monument since 1902. Outside the town, visible from the state; the MiC card does not record usable times.

Fiume Tirino e approdo di San Martino

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Tirino is born from the aquifer system of Campo Imperatore and re-emerges at Lake Capodacqua; with a flow rate of about 6,000 l/s and water at 11 °C is considered one of the most clear rivers in Italy. At the foot of the village, in San Martino, starting point for navigation, the water is emerald green for the transparency that lets the submerged vegetation grow; there live water celery and trout, fishable only You can walk, by mountain bike on the slopes of the Park, by canoe or kayak also with a guide, on horseback or in a carriage (excursions by reservation).

Castello di Bussi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The castle is documented from 1092, when it was part of the monastery of San Benedetto in Perillis; purchased in 1377 by the Cantelmo, who held it until 1579, then passed to Pietro Pietropaoli and the Grand Duke of Tuscany Cosimo de' Medici. The current Medici castle-palace of the 16th century has a rectangular plan around a courtyard, a quadrangular tower to the north, imposing walls, crenellated tower and medieval and Renaissance windows; it dominates the village and is now a private residence of the De Sanctis family.

Chiese di San Biagio, San Lorenzo e Santa Maria di Ponte Marmoreo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the village are the baroque church of San Biagio, of the sixteenth century, the church of San Lorenzo, of the fourteenth century, and the church of Santa Maria di Ponte Marmoreo, with 16th century frescoes inside.
