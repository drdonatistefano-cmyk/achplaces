What to see in Finale Ligure

Città della Riviera di Ponente in four parts, Finalmarina, Finalpia, Varigotti and the walled village of Finalborgo, among the most beautiful villages in Italy, with the complex of Santa Caterina, the basilica of San Biagio and the Spanish forts.

11 places to visit in Finale Ligure, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Finalborgo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The oldest part of Finale, a medieval village still surrounded by the walls with towers and doors, now known for its artisan workshops.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Basilica di San Biagio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Baroque Basilica of the seventeenth century built on a medieval church of 1372, of which it retains the apse and the late-Gothic octagonal bell tower of 1463; the stone façade remained unfinished.

  • Where Finalborgo, Finale Ligure
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Complesso monumentale di Santa Caterina

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

The Dominican church and convent arose around 1360 at the behest of the widow of the Marquis Giorgio I Del Carretto; the convent was imprisoned from 1863 to 1964.
