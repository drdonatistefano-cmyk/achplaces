What to see in Follina

Borgo dell'Alta Marca Trevigiana grew up around the Cistercian abbey of Santa Maria in Sanavalle, founded in 1146, with the cloister of 1268; the palaces of the center and the opifici tell centuries of wool and silk processing. The municipality is part of the core areas of the hills of the Prosecco of Conegliano and Valdobbiadene, UNESCO World Heritage.

Abbazia cistercense di Santa Maria in Sanavalle

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded in 1146 by the Cistercians on a previous Benedictine settlement; the Latin cross basilica was built between 1305 and 1335, while the cloister, completed in 1268 according to the inscription on the north side, is older than the church; the Romanesque bell tower is the oldest structure of the complex, and cloister of the Abbot and loggia are of the sixteenth century. It preserves the sandstone statue of Our Lady of the Sacred Chalice (VI-VII century, attributed to the Nubian art) in the neo-Gothic wooden altar of 1921, the fresco "Madonna" with Child and Saints" by Francesco da Milano (1527) and a wooden Baroque crucifix. Open every day 7-12 and 14.30-19; no groups during the functions; guided tours through the abbey's fathers (tel. 0438 970231).

Centro storico e palazzi dei lanaioli

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Small centre rich in historical buildings born with the textile activity started by the Cistercian monks and culminated in the second half of the seventeenth century: Palazzo Barberis-Rusca, at the foot of the abbey, built by Francesco Fadda who from 1666 joined the simple cloths more precious fabrics, and the Bernardi and Tandura palaces.

Ex lanificio Andretta

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Filatoio built in 1820 and later became the College of St. Joseph; the Municipality indicates it as a remarkable nineteenth century example of industrial archaeology linked to the woolly tradition of Follina.

Lanificio Paoletti e Serica della Marca

Local life and traditions · Crafts

The Lanificio Paoletti, founded in 1795 by Gaspare Paoletti, is the only surviving reality of local wool tradition and produces high quality fabrics for fashion; the Serica della Marca, born in the late nineteenth century, produces silks, yarns and fabrics continuing the ancient tradition of monks.

Colline del Prosecco e sentieri

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Follina is one of the twelve municipalities of the core areas of the Prosecco hills of Conegliano and Valdobbiadene, UNESCO World Heritage, along the Prosecco road; the territory offers hiking, mountain biking, cycling, horse trekking and Nordic walking.
