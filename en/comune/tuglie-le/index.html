What to see in Tuglie

Country of Salento at the northern foot of the Serre Salentine, with a historic center of courts, wells, arches and "focaliri" and the seventeenth-century Doge's Palace on the main square, located since 1982 of the Museum of peasant civilization and popular traditions of Salento.

Museo della civiltà contadina e delle tradizioni popolari del Salento nel Palazzo Ducale

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1982 in the Doge's Palace of the seventeenth century, in the historic centre overlooking the main square: 16 rooms with rural objects from the seventeenth century to the second world war, among peasant tools, carpenters, workshop, blacksmith, blacksmith and cartographer, a kitchen with pots and dishes, an eighteenth-century frame, palment, laundry and private chapel, with different rooms furnished as originally. Outside about 10,000 m2 of green with vegetable garden, biopark, a medieval rock settlement, viewpoint and botanical garden.

MiC card (updated to February 2021) is closed every day

Centro storico e Rione Santa Lucia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The historic centre, on the northern slopes of the Serre Salentine, preserves shortcuts, wells, large arches, porticoes and "focaliri," the ancient chimneys, especially in the Santa Lucia district, as well as churches, palaces and museums. Here on 23 March the patroness, Maria Santissima Annunziata, is celebrated.

  • Where Centro storico, Tuglie
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
