What to see in Pieve di Cadore

Homeland of Titian at the foot of the Antelao, with the birthplace of the painter, Titian Square dominated by the Palace of the Magnificent Community (site of the Archaeological Museum) and the Museum of Glass with over 4,000 finds.

8 places to visit in Pieve di Cadore, in the province of Belluno (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Casa natale di Tiziano

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

Solid 15th-century cadorian building, opened as a house-museum in 1932; the restoration of 1928-30 removed an 18th-century addition and brought back to light original doors and windows. On the ground floor reproductions of drawings by Tiziano degli Uffizi and documents on the painter, on the upper floor five rooms where probably spent his childhood; the Magnificent Community signals you exposed the Pala di San Tiziano, painted by Tiziano and workshop in the sixties of the sixteenth century for the altar of the Vecellio.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Palazzo della Magnifica Comunità di Cadore

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The seat of the Magnificent Community, built between 1444 and 1492 next to the church of Santa Maria Nascente. In the Council room, the Dedication of the Cadore in Venice by Cesare Vecellio and a Madonna by Marco Vecellio, a pine ceiling with the coat of arms of the Cadore and one by Valentino Paciera Besarel from 1862-64, and a frieze with 34 niches dedicated to illustrious cadorini. Opening to the public on Monday-Saturday 11-12.

  • Where Piazza Tiziano 2, Pieve di Cadore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: magnificacomunitadicadore.it · veneto.eu · italia.it

Museo archeologico cadorino

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

On the second floor of the Palazzo della Magnifica Comunità, founded in 1880 and set up in 1984: pre-Roman and Roman finds of the sanctuary of Lagole, dedicated to the deity Trumusiate then identified with Apollo, the necropolis of Domegge and the crest of Valle di Cadore. In the atrium a black and white geometric mosaic from the end of the first century A.D. from a Roman domus.

  • Where Piazza Tiziano 2, Pieve di Cadore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: italia.it · magnificacomunitadicadore.it · museicadoredolomiti.it

Museo dell'Occhiale

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

More than 4,000 finds on two floors: history and social use of glasses and optical instruments, and history of glasses in Cadore and Bellunese; a small library conserves treatises from the seventeenth century. Among the collections are the collection of the Belgian optician Georges Bodart. Open every day 9.30-12.30 and 15-18 according to the MiC.

  • Where Via Arsenale 15, Pieve di Cadore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodellocchiale.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint
