What to see in Scena

The village above Merano is dominated by Castel Schenna, from the 14th century, still home to the Counts of Merano; next to the neo-Gothic mausoleum of Archduke John of Austria and a hill with churches of eight different centuries, from the Romanesque Saint Martin to the new parish, while in San Giorgio a rare round church contains frescoes of the fourteenth century.

Castel Schenna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

14th-century castle overlooking the village above Merano, home of the Counts of Merano and still inhabited by the same family; it is partly surrounded by the moat: a bridge leads to the entrance, beyond the prison, to the courtyard from which an open staircase rises to the halls. In the halls and halls a gallery of paintings and portraits, a collection of historical weapons and art objects of Tyrolean history, relics of Archduke John and a majolica stove in the Renaissance Hall of the Knights. You can visit only with guided tour (except the private wing of the family), with online registration; also on the calendar evening visits at 21.

Cappella del Castel Schenna

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The castle chapel houses small artistic gems: painted from the 17th to the 19th century with religious scenes that give clues to the owners of the castle. During the restoration there were unknown inscriptions that recall dramatic events of the seventeenth century with at the center a woman venerated as a saint. It can be visited only with a guided tour in German (10-10.30, from May to October); it still hosts celebrations of the liturgical year.

Mausoleo dell'arciduca Giovanni

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Neo-Gothic burial church built between 1860 and 1869 on the design of the Viennese Moritz Wappler, considered one of the first and most successful sacred architectures of this style; in red sandstone and granite of the area of Mount Ivigna, rises on an artificial terrace on the west side of the hill. In the crypt the marble sarcophagus of Archduke John of Austria and his wife. Only with guided tour, in the morning, from June to October; closed in winter.

Chiesa rotonda di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the XII-XIII century as a round church, it was the chapel of the castle of San Giorgio (Alt-Schenna), of which only the mast remains, today "Uhlentower"; in 1591 a lightning strike destroyed almost half and the central part was reinforced with a pillar. The frescoes of the fourteenth century, well restored, show the martyrdom of St. George, the universal Judgment and the legend of St. Nicholas; there is an altar with Gothic doors with St. George on horseback and a rare seventeenth-century image of St. Kümmernis. Open from the end of March to the beginning of November 11.15-15.30, closed in winter; audio guide available. From Scena about 30 minutes walk or by bus 233 and 234.

Collina delle chiese

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the center of the village, the hill brings together three churches: St. Martin, from the twelfth century, the oldest monument of Scena, with two naves and on carolingia measures; the old parish church, rebuilt in Romanesque style between the twelfth and thirteenth centuries and redesigned in the sixteenth century; the new neo-Gothic parish church, built between 1914 and 1931 because the old one had become too small. In the center the large heart of flowers with views of the church is among the most photographed points of the village.

Roggia di Scena (Waalweg)

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring path that follows an ancient irrigation channel, the Scena Roggia, through chestnut trees and apple orchards above Merano, with a bench overlooking the valley along the path.
