What to see in Germagnano

Country of the Lanzo Valleys on the Stura, attested since 1034, with the parish church of the Santi Grato and Rocco, two small museums of rural life (the objects of daily use and the wine of the Society of Workers) and the panoramic path of Monte Momello towards the Sanctuary of Sant'Ignazio.

4 places to visit in Germagnano, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Etnografico degli Oggetti di Uso Quotidiano

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Born from a temporary exhibition owned by the Mussino family, it exhibits about 150 objects of daily use in three sections: chestnuts, hay and barn, the path from the tree to the lumberjack laboratory. Open on Saturday 15-16 and Sunday 10-11, or on request.

  • Where Località Castagnole, Germagnano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.germagnano.to.it

Sources: comune.germagnano.to.it

Museo del vino

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

It tells the life of the population in the twentieth century through the ancient tools to make wine of the Society Worker and the area, with attention to how we supported each other during the war and to the network of mutual help. Visit by reservation; accessible.

  • Where Via Roberto Miglietti 26, Germagnano
  • Entry €2, free school and under 14
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.germagnano.to.it

Sources: comune.germagnano.to.it

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Grato e Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises on the site of a church already existing in the fourteenth century (Saint Peter in Pincoli, then dedicated to the Saints Grateful and Rocco) and became parish in 1765, separating from Lanzo. At the main altar eighteenth-century altar with the Virgin and the patrons; at the altar of the Rosary a crucifix of the sixteenth century, with paintings of the Madonna del Rosario and purgant souls surrounded by Mysteries.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa 1, Germagnano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.germagnano.to.it

Sources: comune.germagnano.to.it

Monte Momello e Santuario di Sant'Ignazio

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring that starts from the town square, climbs through the hamlet Margaula and the wood (white signs) up to the cross and the bivouac of the Alps on the summit of Monte Momello, then follows the ridge in a panoramic stretch up to the square of the Sanctuary of Sant'Ignazio; you return for a path to hairpin bends on the south-west side.

  • Where Partenza dalla piazza di Germagnano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.germagnano.to.it

Sources: comune.germagnano.to.it
