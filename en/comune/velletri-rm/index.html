What to see in Velletri

Città dei Castelli Romani, the ancient Velitrae Volsca, with the Gothic Torre del Trivio in Piazza Cairoli, the Cathedral of San Clemente with the Diocesan Museum and the Town Hall that houses the Civic Museums with the famous sarcophagus of the labors of Hercules.

Museo Archeologico «Oreste Nardini»

Museums · Archaeological

In the sixteenth century Town Hall, founded in 1920 by Oreste Nardini and reopened permanently in 1992: about 400 exhibits from the protohistory to the Middle Ages. The strong piece is the Sarcophagus of Velletri (II century A.D., found in 1955) with 184 figures between the labors of Hercules and scenes of the underworld; then the Lastra dell'Orante (IV century), the Volsche terracotta of the VI century B.C. and a copy of the Pallade of Velletri, whose original is at the Louvre.

  • Where Via Goffredo Mameli 6, Velletri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo di Geopaleontologia e Preistoria dei Colli Albani

Museums · Science and university

According to the Civic Museums in the Palazzo Comunale, it tells the formation of the territory from volcanoes to fossils in five sections (geology, paleontology, anthropology, prehistory, protohistory) with fossils, ceramics and environmental reconstructions.

  • Where Via Goffredo Mameli 6, Velletri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters

In the cathedral of San Clemente, established in 1927: fragments of Exultet of the twelfth century, the Madonna with Child and angels of Gentile da Fabriano (1426-1427), the Madonna in throne signed by Antoniazzo Romano in 1486, the Madonna of the Rosary of Sebastiano Conca and the Velitern Cross, gold reliquary and precious stones of the eleventh-XII century. Closed on Monday; Tuesday-Sunday 9-13 and 15-19.

  • Where Corso della Repubblica 347, Velletri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Cattedrale di San Clemente

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Founded probably in the fourth century on the remains of a Roman temple and rebuilt at the end of the thirteenth century and in the seventeenth century, it has no façade: the portal of 1512 opens onto a small porch. Inside the ciborium with cosmatesque newsstand of the thirteenth century, remains of fourteenth-century frescoes in the apse, the Madonna delle Grazie of the fourteenth century and a Romanesque crypt with re-use columns.

  • Where Piazza San Clemente 1, Velletri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Torre del Trivio e piazza Cairoli

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Gothic tower of about 50 meters built in 1353 by the Masters Comacini at the crossroads of three streets: born as a bell tower of the church of Santa Maria del Trivio, it was separated in 1622; damaged by the bombings of the Second World War, was rebuilt.

  • Where Piazza Cairoli, Velletri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Porta Napoletana

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The only remains of the medieval walls, flanked by two massive crenellated towers with circular base; from here the road to Terracina and Naples left.

  • Where Porta Napoletana, Velletri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Piazza del Comune e Palazzo Comunale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

In Piazza Ottaviano Augusto the Palace of the Apostolic Delegate and the Town Hall, inspired by Vignola at Palazzo Farnese in Caprarola; in the palace are the civic museums. In Piazza Mazzini the monumental Baroque fountain.

Area archeologica urbana SS. Stimmate

History and architecture · Archaeology

Under the church of the SS. Stimitate (titled in 1602) a path with two faces on the excavation shows the remains of a temple of the VII-VI century B.C. superimposed on those of a hut of the Iron Age, under a modern case; here were found the polychrome terracotta and the veliternal "tabula." Recovery completed in 2016.

  • Where Chiesa delle SS. Stimmate, centro storico, Velletri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.velletri.rm.it · italia.it
