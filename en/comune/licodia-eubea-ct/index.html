What to see in Licodia Eubea

A hill country 600 metres of Greek and Byzantine origins, dominated by the ruins of Castello Santapau, mentioned since 1272 and destroyed by the earthquake of 1693; in the necropolis territory from the age of Copper to the Byzantine era and the rock complex of the Cave of Saints with medieval frescoes.

Castello Santapau

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Ruins in the center of the village on a spur of limestone, where Paolo Orsi recognized the acropolis of the Hellenized indigenous center. The Byzantines built a fortress there in the 8th century; the castle is mentioned in 1272 in the Angevin list of the Sicilian fortresses, in 1393 it passed to Ughetto Santapau and was destroyed by the earthquake of 11 January 1693. From the Arabic name of the hill, to the Kudyah, would be the name Licodia.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Centro storico e Chiesa Madre

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Stone streets, panoramic staircases and historic buildings with the Mother Church and the Town Hall; churches and palaces of the eighteenth century. The patron saint, Santa Margherita, is celebrated on July 20.

  • Where Centro storico, Licodia Eubea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info · cittametropolitana.ct.it

Complesso rupestre Grotta dei Santi

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the Poggio dei Santi, about 600 meters on the Cava dei Volaci, a rock complex of the VII-VIII century made known in 1940 by G. Lamb, frequented for over a millennium and abandoned in the middle of the fifteenth century (the oldest graffiti is from 1445). In the eastern arches, the fresco of the Crucifixion with Our Lady, St. John and Longino, from the 11th to the 11th century. It is reached from the old state road 194 Vizzini-Ragusa for a path between dry walls.

  • Where Contrada Alia, Licodia Eubea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: comune.licodiaeubea.ct.it · visitsicily.info

Colle di San Cono

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hill east of the village with a village of the Copper Age, excavated by the brothers Cafici (1879), which gave its name to the culture of San Cono-Piano Notaro; the hill was frequented until the Byzantine era and preserves a Christian-Byzantine necropolis, of which today there are few tombs.

  • Where SP 38/I verso la stazione di Vizzini-Licodia, Licodia Eubea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: comune.licodiaeubea.ct.it · visitsicily.info

Necropoli della Piazzisa

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

On a terrace of the hill, about 3 km north-west of the village, a Sicilian necropolis of the V-IV century BC and a Christian hypogeum of the 5th century A.D., two bedrooms dug into the rock, with loculi and an arcosolian sarcophagus. Free access.

  • Where Contrada Piazzisa, Licodia Eubea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: comune.licodiaeubea.ct.it · visitsicily.info

Necropoli di Fossa Quadara e lago di Licodia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the plateau overlooking the artificial lake of Licodia, about 5 km from the village, a small late Roman sepulchre (end IV-inizi V century A.D.) of about forty were covered with sandstone, on the right bank of the Dirillo, partly covered by the waters of the dam Ragoleto.

  • Where Altopiano di Fossa Quadara, Licodia Eubea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: comune.licodiaeubea.ct.it · visitsicily.info
