What to see in Galtellì

Borgo della Baronia at the foot of Monte Tuttovista, Orange Flag of Touring, ancient bishop's seat and "Galte" of "Canne al vento" of Grazia Deledda: the sanctuary of the Blessed Crucifix, the former cathedral of St. Peter with frescoes of the thirteenth century, the museum Sa Domo 'e sos Marras and the ruins of the Castle of Pontes.

9 places to visit in Galtellì, in the province of Nuoro (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa del Santissimo Crocifisso

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Parish in the Tuscan Gothic style built on the site of the little church of Santa Maria delle Torri, which became too small for pilgrims; restored in the eighteenth century in Piedmontese baroque, it has three naves and wooden roof. It preserves the wooden Christ who, according to tradition, sweated blood between 1612 and 1667 and attracts pilgrims from all over the island, and a sixteenth-century Trinity; feast from April 30 to May 3.

  • Where Piazza Santa Croce, Galtellì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Ex cattedrale di San Pietro

Places of worship · Cathedrals

In the enclosure of the cemetery, at the east exit of the village: seat of the small medieval diocese (transferred to Nuoro in 1496), includes the Romanesque cathedral started around 1090 and remained unfinished, the sixteenth-century bell tower and the church of San Pietro with frescoes from the first thirteenth century of Umbrian-Latial painters on stories of the Old and New Testaments.

  • Where Cimitero, Galtellì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiese di Santa Croce, dell'Assunta e di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Next to the Crucifix stands the medieval church of Santa Croce, with porch, sailing bell tower and frescoes inside; less than 100 meters from the seventeenth-century churches of the Blessed Virgin Assumption and St. Francis. Galtellì counts about five churches in a square kilometre.

  • Where Piazza Santa Croce, Galtellì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Museo etnografico Sa Domo 'e sos Marras

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the 18th century manor house of the Marras family, restored in 1995: courtyard and ground floor with agropastoral tools, on the first floor the domestic environments, and closing traditional clothes including a wedding dress of the early twentieth century; about 1800 objects exposed. In May he organizes excursions in the Deleddian literary park.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Garibaldi, Galtellì
  • Entry €3, reduced €2,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Castello di Pontes

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Ruins on a height at the foot of the Tuttavista, above the plain of Cedrino: fortress of the 11th century on a previous Roman fortification, conquered by the Aragonese in the 14th century and abandoned in the 15th century. Still features of the antemural, traces of oven and cistern and a wide panorama up to the Gulf of Orosei; Deledda sets the ghosts of the barons of ... Canne al vento.

  • Where Fuori paese, lungo la SS 129, Galtellì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Monte Tuttavista

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Separates the village from the sea and offers views of the Gulf of Orosei, Mount Albo and the Cedrino; on the top a 12 meters bronze statue of Christ, reproduction of the wooden one, destination of pilgrimages. Walls equipped for climbing, hiking and mountain biking trails, 32 varieties of wild orchids and caves with fossil fauna.

  • Where Monte Tuttavista, Galtellì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · comune.galtelli.nu.it · italia.it

Sa Preta Istampata

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural monument at 635 meters along the ascent to Tuttavista: a rocky wall with a large natural hole of about 10 meters in diameter, overlooking the valley and the Supramonte, reachable by a short path from the main road.

  • Where Monte Tuttavista, Galtellì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.galtelli.nu.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Parco Malicas

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Municipal park with trees, shrubs and picnic areas, with a castle of the early twentieth century; there is the necropolis of Malicas, seven domus de janas of which two inside the park, testimony of attendance from the Neolithic.

  • Where Parco Malicas, Galtellì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.galtelli.nu.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
