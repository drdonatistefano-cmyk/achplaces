What to see in Demonte

Borgo della Valle Stura already known in Roman times as a passage to the Gallia, birthplace of Lalla Romano: in the porticoed center you can visit the Palazzo Borelli with its terrace park, the parish church of San Donato and the medieval civic tower, while above the village the ruins of the Fort of the Consolata; between June and July the meadows at the entrance of the village are colored with lavender.

Palazzo e giardino Borelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built from 1606 by the will of the Duke Charles Emanuele I of Savoy after the destruction of the Angevin castle, it preserves frescoes and wooden ceilings of the eighteenth-eighteenth-century; King Charles Albert lived several times its noble floors in the first half of the nineteenth century. A gallery dedicated to Carlo Alberto, built by Count Borelli in the second half of the nineteenth century, connects him to the nineteenth-century park with four terraces with fountains, caves and greenhouse. It houses the Documentation Centre Lalla Romano, the Porcelain Museum and the Civic Library; guided tours on pre-established dates, by reservation (tel. Centro storico, portici e parrocchiale di San Donato

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Walking under the arcades you can see the traces of the medieval past of the village. In the center are the Town Hall, former noble palace, the medieval civic tower, the seventeenth-century church of Mercy, seat of the Black Disciplined, and the parish church of San Donato, with a fresco of the Battle of Lepanto by the painter of Flemish origin Giovanni Claret.

  • Where Centro storico, Demonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Forte della Consolata

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Made in 1590 by Carlo Emanuele di Savoia to engineer Gabrio Busco, he was shot down after the armistice of Cherasco in 1796. The area can be visited with guided tours organized by the Tourist Office of Demonte.

  • Where Demonte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Museo etnografico Lou Feracaval

Museums · Themed

Managed by the mountain community, it reconstructs the workshop of the "feracaval" , the masquerade, an essential figure when horses, mules and donkeys were the engine of society, especially in the most remote mountain areas. The tools on display were used until the 1970s. Open only by reservation.

  • Where Via Parrocchia, Demonte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Prati di lavanda

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Between June and July the lavender meadows flourish at the entrance of the village; in occitan the lavender is called ""izop" and "Demonte" is compared to a corner of Provence.

  • Where Ingresso del paese, Demonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
