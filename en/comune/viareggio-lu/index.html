What to see in Viareggio

The city of Carnival and the Art Nouveau promenade on the sea of Versilia, between the pine forests of Ponente and Levante; in Torre del Lago the villa of Giacomo Puccini on the lake of Massaciuccoli.

13 places to visit in Viareggio, in the province of Lucca (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Viareggio in the guide with map and filters

Passeggiata a mare (Lungomare Regina Margherita)

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The promenade with the Art Nouveau buildings of the 1920s, including the Gran Caffè Margherita designed by Alfredo Belluomini and Galileo Chini, the Warehouse Duilio and the Book Gallery, and the great hotel Prince of Piedmont.

  • Where Lungomare Regina Margherita, Viareggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com

Gran Caffè Margherita

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Caffè storico frequented by Giacomo Puccini and Arturo Toscanini, designed by architect Alfredo Belluomini with ceramics by Galileo Chini, the first stage designer of Turandot.

  • Where Lungomare Regina Margherita, Viareggio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Villa Paolina – Musei civici

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Built in 1822 by Paolina Bonaparte Borghese, it houses the Archaeological and Man Museum, the Museum of musical instruments, the monumental apartments of Paolina and the atelier of the painter Alfredo Catarsini; in front, the monument to the poet Shelley, died in a shipwreck offshore in 1822.

  • Where Viareggio
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com

GAMC – Galleria d'arte moderna e contemporanea, Palazzo delle Muse

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

The modern and contemporary art gallery in the Palazzo delle Muse, on the promenade.

  • Where Lungomare, Viareggio
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Cittadella e Museo del Carnevale

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the workshops of the Citadel, the masters of the mache build figures over twenty meters high; the museum tells more than a century of Carnival with models, historical posters and installations.

  • Where Viareggio
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Villa Museo Giacomo Puccini

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The liberty villa on the lake of Massaciuccoli where Puccini lived thirty years composing Tosca, Madama Butterfly and La Fanciulla del West, and where he is buried.

  • Where Torre del Lago Puccini, Viareggio
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Lago di Massaciuccoli e Parco di Migliarino-San Rossore-Massaciuccoli

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lake Torre del Lago, with guided tours of the marshy flora and birdwatching huts, migratory stop for many birds; on the shores the Gran Teatro al fresco of the Puccini Festival in summer.
