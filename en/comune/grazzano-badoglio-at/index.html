What to see in Grazzano Badoglio

Monferrato Astigiano village grew up around the abbey founded in the 10th century by the Marquis Aleramo, whose tomb is preserved in the former abbey church today parish; birthplace of the marshal Pietro Badoglio, remembered by the panoramic stele of 24 meters wanted by Battista Pininfarina and the historical museum Badogliano.

Chiesa parrocchiale dei SS. Vittore e Corona (ex abbazia aleramica)

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Abbey founded by the Marquis Aleramo of Monferrato about 950-960 and endowed by him in 961 and entrusted to the Benedictines; suppressed in 1802. The Baroque interior with a single nave, enlarged in 1580, has three chapels on its side: in the chapel of the Rosary the tomb of Aleramo with a fragment of pavement mosaic and two frescoes attributed to Guglielmo Caccia, in the chapel of the Jesuits a canvas by Andrea Pozzo with the death of Saint Francis Saverio (1670-1675), an Immaculate of the Hunt, the choir in walnut of 1591 coming from Santa Croce di Casale and the organ Lingiardi of 1860. The bell tower has the lower Romanesque-Lombardy; wooden portal of 1766. Free access during functions.

  • Where Via SS. Vittore e Corona 6, Grazzano Badoglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Stele a Badoglio

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Steel stele 24 meters high on a granite base, designed by the Turin architect Amedeo Albertini (author of the Museo dell'Automobile) at the request of Battista Pininfarina, who presided over the Committee for the Honor of the Marshal and gave it to the Municipality; inaugurated on September 27, 1964. The base carries three bronzes by Ettore Tinto and Roberto Terracini (the Faith, the Value, the Earth) and inscriptions dictated by Martian Bernardi; next, the cyppo at Pininfarina by Virgilio Audagna (1968).

  • Where Viale Pininfarina, località Madonna dei Monti, Grazzano Badoglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.grazzanobadoglio.at.it

Sources: comune.grazzanobadoglio.at.it

Museo storico badogliano

Museums · City history

Museum dedicated to the Marshal Pietro Badoglio, born in Grazzano, managed by the Cultural Centre Pietro Badoglio (a voluntary association established in 1991), who expanded its collections and rearranged it in 2001. Since December 2000 the Centre has also set up, in a hall of the former kindergarten, the permanent exhibition "L'Italia dei governamenti di guerra" (Italy of war governments).
