What to see in Sperlinga

Village in Sicily, more harsh and quiet, dominated by a medieval castle excavated in a large sandstone mass between Nebrodi and Madonie; at its feet a rock village of about fifty caves, open-air ethnographic museum.

7 places to visit in Sperlinga, in the province of Enna (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Sperlinga

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built around the year 1000 on a more ancient rock fortress and partly dug into the sandstone, it has drawbridge and panoramic tower reached by steep stairs cut into the rock. In 1282, during the Vespers, the French garrison resisted a siege for a year: it is reminiscent of a sixteenth-century inscription on the arch of the entrance hall; inside twelve small circular niches dug into the wall and, in the castle, you can see the weaving of the frazzed.

Borgo rupestre delle grotte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the side of the castle there are about fifty artificial caves, carved by hand, connected by stairs and narrow streets in the rock: each was a small house of one or two rooms. It's an open-air ethnographic museum.

  • Where Sperlinga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo della civiltà contadina e Grotte Museo

Museums · Themed

Some caves purchased by the municipality have become ethnographic museum; in one of them the Museum of peasant civilization exhibits tools and objects of common use of rural life.

Contrada Rossa, Santi Quaranta e Peirito

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Rock sites in the surroundings connected by artificial caves, probable places of worship and early Christian burial: Contrada Rossa would have hosted a early Christian community, in Santi Quaranta and Peirito there are early Christian tombs.
