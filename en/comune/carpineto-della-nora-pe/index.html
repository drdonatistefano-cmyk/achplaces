What to see in Carpineto della Nora

Small town on the Pescia side of the Gran Sasso, in the District of Grandi Abbazie of the National Park, known for the church of San Bartolomeo, what remains of a Benedictine abbey founded in 962, from which starts the Path of the Abbeys.

Abbazia di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

On a rocky back in front of a mountain amphitheatre (Monte Pietrarossa), it is the surviving church of an important Benedictine abbey founded in 962 by Bernardo, son of Linduno Count of Penne, and rebuilt in the 12th and 13th centuries; in 1258 it passed to the dependencies of Santa Maria di Casanova adopting the Cistercian rule, and from the 14th century decayed. The portal with girals and animals recalls the Master Acuto (XII century); the interior with three naves with raised transept has a plant similar to San Clemente to Casauria, triabsidata crypt and altar on four columns with capitals of the X century. On the right remain ruins of the monastery; last restorations in the seventies.

visit Tuesday-Sunday 8-16 with reservation required (active phone on Mondays only for bookings); the MiC card shows discordant indications on Monday

Sentiero delle Abbazie

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails

The path of the Gran Sasso and Monti della Laga National Park marked according to the CAI rules, which starts from the square in front of the church of San Bartolomeo and leads to the Abbey of Santa Maria di Casanova, at Villa Celiera.
