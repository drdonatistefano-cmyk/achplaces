What to see in Urbino

The ideal city of the Renaissance, home of Raphael and UNESCO historical center: the Doge's Palace of Federico da Montefeltro with the National Gallery of the Marches, the frescoed oratories and the Fortress Albornoz.

Palazzo Ducale e Galleria Nazionale delle Marche

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage

"A city in the form of a palace": the main part, with the façade of the Torricini, the Courtyard of Honour and the staircase, was designed by Luciano Laurana (1464-1472) and completed by Francesco di Giorgio Martini; it houses the National Gallery of the Marches with the Flagellation and the Madonna of Senigallia by Piero della Francesca, the Muta di Raffaello and the ideal City, as well as the Studiolo del Duca with the tarsie perspective.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Duomo di Urbino

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage

The cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta, founded in 1062 by Bishop Mainardo and rebuilt in the fifteenth century, owes its neoclassical appearance to Giuseppe Valadier, who worked there between 1789 and 1801; on the high altar the Assumption of Christphorus Unterberger.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Museo Diocesano Albani

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage

In the episcopal complex next to the Duomo, sacred furnishings from the thirteenth century to today: the brass reading of the library of Federico da Montefeltro, a bronze candelabra by Francesco di Giorgio Martini, goldsmiths, majolica, vestments and paintings by Giovanni Santi, Federico Barocci and Federico Zuccari.

Chiesa di San Domenico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage

Consecrated in 1365 in front of the Ducal Palace, it has on the Renaissance portal the copy of the glazed terracotta bezel of Luca della Robbia (the original is in the National Gallery); the interior with a single nave was rebuilt in the eighteenth century. It is deconsecrated: you visit only during the exhibitions and events it hosts.

  • Where Piazza Rinascimento, Urbino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage

Built in Romanesque-Gothic style in the second half of the fourteenth century and transformed between 1732 and 1751: the ancient building remains the portal with the friezes of Costantino Trappola (1516-1517) and the bell tower with the stone strips of Furlo. The parents of Raffaello, Timothy Viti and Federico Barocci are buried there.
