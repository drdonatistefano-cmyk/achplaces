What to see in Urbino

The ideal city of the Renaissance, home of Raphael and UNESCO historical center: the Doge's Palace of Federico da Montefeltro with the National Gallery of the Marches, the frescoed oratories and the Fortress Albornoz.

17 places to visit in Urbino, in the province of Pesaro e Urbino (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo Ducale e Galleria Nazionale delle Marche

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage

"A city in the form of a palace": the main part, with the façade of the Torricini, the Courtyard of Honour and the staircase, was designed by Luciano Laurana (1464-1472) and completed by Francesco di Giorgio Martini; it houses the National Gallery of the Marches with the Flagellation and the Madonna of Senigallia by Piero della Francesca, the Muta di Raffaello and the ideal City, as well as the Studiolo del Duca with the tarsie perspective.

  • Where Piazza Duca Federico, Urbino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Borgo Mercatale, Rampa elicoidale e Data
