What to see in San Severino Marche

Cities of art along the Potenza with the elliptical Piazza del Popolo, the Castle al Monte with the tower of the Smeducci and the old Duomo, the Pinacoteca with Pinturicchio and the Salimbeni and, in the mountains, the beech forests of Canfaito.

13 places to visit in San Severino Marche, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza del Popolo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Elliptical square with elongated porticos and palaces: the 18th-century municipal palace, the Rococo church of St. Joseph, the 16th-century Palace of Governors with the Clock Tower and the Feronia Theatre; here we run in June the Race of the towers of the Palio dei Castelli.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, San Severino Marche
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it
