What to see in Vico Equense

It rises on the ancient Aequa, sung by Silio Italico for its wines, on a flat stretch of the tuff cliff: walled village of Angevin with the Gothic Santissima Annunziata overhanging the sea and the Castle Giusso, mineralogical and archaeological museums, the marines of Equa and Seiano, the scrajou with the sulphuric spring and the Monte Comune overlooking Positano and the gulf.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The walled village was founded in the fourteenth century by Charles II of Anjou on the site of the ancient Aequa, whose name is linked to the Latin aequus (the plain) because it rises on a flat stretch of the cliff; you visit on foot, left the car at the margins. From the village a steep path descends at the foot of the tuff cliff, towards the beaches of Marina di Equa and Marina di Seiano.

  • Where Vico Equense
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa della Santissima Annunziata (ex Cattedrale)

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

Probably erected between 1320 and 1330 on a ridge about 90 meters above sea level, it is one of the few examples of Gothic on the Sorrento Coast; Baroque façade rebuilt between 1773 and 1792, bell tower sixteenth century. Three naves on pillars of tuff, pentagonal apse with ridge vaults, the Annunciation of Giuseppe Bonito (1733) on the high altar, a wooden crucifix of Giotto's school and the urns of Gaetano Filangieri and Bishop Cimino. Cathedral until 1818, reopened in 1995 after the earthquake damage of 1980.

  • Where Centro storico, Vico Equense
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Castello Giusso

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built between 1284 and 1289, traditionally by Charles II of Anjou, it was a fortress and a residence; in the seventeenth century it became a noble residence with gardens, caves and water games, and in the nineteenth century the Giusso, from which it takes its name, chose the salmon pink colour and frescoed the Hall of Arms and the Hall of Ventagli. Part of the city walls and a terrace overlooking the sea remain.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Antiquarium Aequano Silio Italico

Museums · Archaeological

In the Town Hall there are more than 200 exhibits from the 7th century B.C. to the Roman age, found in the territory in the sixties and seventies: vases with black and red figures, Etruscan bronzes and bell towers (calderons, silhouettes, the "pizzipapera"), Corinthian and Ionian ceramics, buccheri and the bronzes of the archaic necropolis of Vico Equense.

The MiC card indicates "Closed" every day of the week; check opening on tel. 081 8019220

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo Mineralogico Campano

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 1992 by the collection of over fifty years of ing. Pasquale Disciple: more than 3,500 minerals of about 1,400 species, with a section of Vesuvian minerals, meteorites and gems; since 1997 the paleontological section with dinosaur eggs and an imprint of Scipionyx samniticus .Ciro, since 2003 the anthropological one with paleolithic and neolithic instruments. Open from Tuesday to Saturday 10-13.

  • Entry €2,50

Marina di Vico Equense

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Clear waters, coves and wild landscape, with a small port and establishments; the Scoglio della Margherita houses a chapel to the Madonna built by fishermen, and the beach of Pezzolo, of golden sand and shallow bottom next to the remains of a Roman villa, is suitable for families. Deep and rocky seabeds attract water sports.

  • Where Marina di Vico Equense
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Spiaggia della Tartaruga

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Small pebble beach reachable only from the sea, by boat or with the turtle's taxi; it takes its name from a cliff of five meters in the shape of a turtle and is quiet and surrounded by clear waters.

  • Where Marina di Vico Equense
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Scrajo: spiaggia e terme

Local life and traditions · Spas

Pebble beach from the green sea with a pool of sulphur water from a natural spring, linked to the proximity of Vesuvius; on the state road 145, to the north, the Scraio Thermal Baths founded in 1895.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Monte Comune

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

It is 877 metres high, a large plateau covered with Mediterranean maquis with a 360-degree panorama of Positano and its coves, the Li Galli archipelago, the Sorrentine Peninsula and the Gulf of Naples with Vesuvius.

  • Where Monte Comune, Vico Equense
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Pizza al metro e provolone del Monaco

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Vico's bakeries have developed a more crisp variant of Neapolitan pizza, also served \"to the metro"; from the Lattari Mountains come the Provolone del Monaco DOP, cheese with seasoned spun pasta, the chestnuts of the priest, walnuts for the walnut and the extra virgin olive oil DOP Sorrentine Peninsula.
