What to see in Caldonazzo

The village of Alta Valsugana is 480 metres away, between the lake of the same name, the largest in Trentino, the orchards and the Centa torrent: in the center the magnificent crelata Corte Trapp and in front of the church of San Sisto, on Mount Rive the remains of the castle of Sicconi and towards the plateau the ancient road of Menador.

Lago di Caldonazzo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

With 5.4 km2 it is the largest lake in Trentino and gives its name to the village. You swim from May to the end of September on free beaches and equipped shores, with nautical center for sailing, canoeing, windsurfing, rowing, water skiing and diving, and a walk on the banks.

  • Where Lido di Caldonazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittrentino.info

Magnifica Corte Trapp

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The large crenellated building that dominates the village was the fortified residence of the counts of Caldonazzo: Visit Trentino traces its origins around the Mille, the card of the APT given the building in 1319. Justice was administered there, trials were held and the tithes of the peasants were preserved; the wall towards the church has blackbirds tailed by swallows, ghibellines. Today it hosts events, a riding school and stables.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · visitvalsugana.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Sisto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In front of Corte Trapp, it has been documented since 1345 but the current building is from the eighteenth century; it preserves several works of art and a early Christian tombstone.

  • Where Via Roma, Caldonazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitvalsugana.it · visittrentino.info · italia.it

Centro storico, Via Roma e Piazza Vecchia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Alleys and rustic houses between the lake and the Centa: in Via Roma the houses were built together in a few years and they carry engraved dates on the arch keys, the most recent in 1836 at number 23; in Piazza Vecchia the fountain and the tourist office, in front of the Town Hall a panel on the Tower of the Sicconi and on Federico IV "Tascavuota" in Via Graziadei one on the bachiculture.

  • Where Piazza Vecchia, Caldonazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitvalsugana.it · italia.it

Torre e Giardino dei Sicconi

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Until 1915 on Mount Rive stood the Torre dei Sicconi, last rest of the castle infeudato in 1201 to the lords of Caldonazzo and linked to Siccone II, attacked in 1385 by Vicentini and Veronesi; the tower still appears in the municipal coat of arms. The excavations of 2006-2008 have brought to light the tower, sections of walls and a probable palace, with materials of the 13th-XV century; since 2005 the Municipality has started a thematic garden. Walk up from the Town Hall Square in about 50 minutes.

Antica strada del Menador

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Climbing 14 km without traffic on the ancient Menador road, between alpine pastures and traces of the line of the Great War front.
