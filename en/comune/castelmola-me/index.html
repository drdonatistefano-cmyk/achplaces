What to see in Castelmola

Borgo peroccato sopra Taormina, which takes its name from the Norman castle (castello and Mola): the ruins of the fortress, Piazza Sant'Antonino with the viewpoint on the Ionian coast, the Church Mother of St. Nicholas overlooking Etna, the historic Caffè San Giorgio del vino al al almondla and the path of Monte Veneretta.

8 places to visit in Castelmola, in the province of Messina (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ruderi del castello normanno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Of the castle that gives its name to the village remains the Norman walls. A plaque from the tenth century on the facade of the cathedral recalls that the castle was built under Constantine, patrician and strategist of Sicily; on the entrance arch the inscription "Castle faithful to His Majesty in the year 1578" .

  • Where Castelmola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Piazza Sant'Antonino e arco antico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Square paved with white stone mosaic and lava, with trees and stone benches, whose viewpoint looks on Taormina, the Ionian coast and Calabria. In the corner the ancient arch that marked the entrance to the village, on top of a staircase in limestone; on the square the church of Sant'Antonino, now municipal auditorium. From here you will find via De Gasperi, the main street with lace shops and embroidery.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Antonino, Castelmola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa Madre di San Nicola di Bari

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

In Arab-Norman style, rebuilt in 1934-35 on a previous cathedral of which it retains the lateral entrance, the choir arch, the altars and the eighteenth-century pulpit; inside four marble altars and a wooden statue of the Maddalena of the school of Bagnasco. From the churchyard you can see the Etna and the Gulf of Naxos.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Castelmola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di San Biagio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small church in panoramic position on the shortcut to Taormina, perhaps the oldest in the village, founded after the arrival of San Pancrazio in Taormina; it preserves an eighteenth-century fresco and has been restored.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa dell'Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the neighborhood of the same name, built around 1100 by the Norman king Ruggero to thank the Virgin for the victory over the Saracens.

Caffè San Giorgio

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Historic bar-tavern founded by the monks in the eighteenth century, considered the place of origin of the almond wine, probably invented by the owner Don Vincenzo Blandano; it preserves an album of signatures of illustrious visitors since 1907.

  • Where Castelmola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Monte Veneretta

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The highest peak (884 m) of the reliefs between Giardini Naxos, Taormina and Castelmola, reached by a bare and panoramic path; from the summit you can see Castelmola, the Greek theatre of Taormina, Isola Bella and Etna. It is one of the most frequented ramps by paragliders.

  • Where Monte Veneretta, Castelmola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it
