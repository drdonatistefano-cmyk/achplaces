What to see in Fragneto Monforte

Country of Alto Sannio at the foot of the hill Toppa Barrata, between Tammaro and Calore, with a castle plundered by Ruggero II in 1138 and rebuilt after the earthquake of 1456; it houses the seventeenth-century church of Santi Nicola and Rocco, the Museum of Arts and Popular Traditions and in October the International Balloon Gathering.

Museo delle arti e tradizioni popolari

Museums · Themed

It gathers about 1,300 objects and photographs on the crafts and crafts of the territory and reconstructs ancient domestic environments, in 12 rooms divided into four sections: peasant civilization (tool for oil, wine and wheat), arts and crafts (carpenters, shoemakers, barbers, spinning machines and weavers, masons, watchmakers), living environments (costumes, bedrooms, kitchens) and curiosity of communication (instruments, electrical and computer equipment, video library, prints). Accessible to the disabled.

Chiesa dei Santi Nicola e Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish Church of the diocese of Benevento, built between 1656 and 1688. It has an isolated bell tower: on its south side is walled a marble plaque at the fallen of the two world wars, with a bronze disc depicting Victory as an armed woman in a laurel crown; the upper part recalls the 27 fallen of the Great War, the lower gravestones of the 25 of the Second World War, gathered in 1976.
