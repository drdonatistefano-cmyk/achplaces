What to see in Ardesio

Mountain village of the upper Seriana Valley, in the Orobie Alps, Orange Flag of Touring: the Baroque sanctuary of the Madonna delle Grazie, linked to the apparition of June 23, 1607, the ethnographic Museum of the Upper Seriana Valley and the Alpine village of Valcanale, leads to the Trail of the Orobie.

5 places to visit in Ardesio, in the province of Bergamo (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

According to tradition, the Virgin appeared on 23 June 1607 to the sisters Maria and Caterina Salera during a storm; the first stone was laid on 24 June 1608 and the bell tower started in 1645. Three-nave basilica with 18th-century façade in local stone and red marble, frescoes by Alberto and Cesare Mironi and Antonio Guadagnini and pulpit of the Fantoni workshop; in the presbytery the "Santa dei Santi" room, the Salera room of the apparition. Every 23 June the Feast of the Apparition with solemn procession.

MEtA - Museo etnografico dell'Alta Valle Seriana

Museums · Themed

Established in 1982, it tells of the relationship between the territory and work: spinning and weaving, woodworkers and charcoal miners and the silver mine, with historical cartography, tools and reconstructions of life in the mine; it is part of the fifteenth century rural house of the center with domestic environments, artisan tools and agricultural work of the mountain.

Centro storico e Palazzo Vescovile

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

From Piazza Monte Grappa, with the Palazzo Gadaldini seat of the Town Hall, you pass through Riccardi, already Santa Caterina, and via Duca d'Aosta with the fresco of the Immaculate Church to Piazza Bonvicino, dedicated to the 16th century painter Moretto di Ardesio. Here the 12th century Bishop's Palace, with the hall of the Congregation of the Dead from the 1672 stone portal and the fresco of a macabre dance; just beyond the great parish church of San Giorgio.

  • Where Piazza Monte Grappa, Ardesio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: viviardesio.it · italia.it

Valcanale e Rifugio Alpe Corte

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest hamlet, at 987 meters, among meadows, streams and pine forests, with alpine landscapes and a pond; once had ski facilities. Above the village, the Alpe Corte mountain hut (1410 m), from which the Sentiero delle Orobie starts, the ring at an altitude of about 80 km in stages between the huts (in the area also Branchino and Laghi Gemelli).

  • Where Valcanale, Ardesio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: viviardesio.it · touringclub.it · valseriana.eu · italia.it

Contrade antiche: Ludrigno, Piazzolo e Cacciamali

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Among the twelve hamlets, Ludrigno (560 m) is one of the oldest, with a well preserved architectural facility of interest for the minor architecture of Bergamo; Piazzolo (780 m) preserves rural houses from the 14th and 15th centuries; in Cacciamali (1100 m), today almost uninhabited, a small church of the 16th century with frescoes.

  • Where Frazioni di Ardesio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: viviardesio.it · italia.it
