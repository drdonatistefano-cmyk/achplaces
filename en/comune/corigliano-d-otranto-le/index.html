What to see in Corigliano d'Otranto

Borgo della Grecia Salentina with the Castello de' Monti from the Baroque façade at the entrance of the historic center, the Church Mother of St. Nicholas and the Arc Lucchetti; the monuments have panels in Italian, English and Griko and the village hosts the first stage of the Night of the Taranta.

Castello de' Monti

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Medieval nucleus with crenellated towers enclosed in the sixteenth century by the Marquis Giovan Battista de' Monti in a Renaissance fortress with four towers dedicated each to a saint and a cardinal virtue; in the seventeenth century he received the Baroque façade with allegorical statues and busts of leaders, in the eighteenth century became a mill and pasta factory. Today it hosts guided tours, events, workshops of the project "Il Castello Volante" , restaurant and coffee.

  • Where Piazza Castello 1, Corigliano d'Otranto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Arco Lucchetti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Ancient portal covered with thick carvings of Byzantine, classical and Islamic inspiration: the sources suggest to stop to admire it before passing under it.

  • Where Centro storico, Corigliano d'Otranto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Chiesa Madre di San Nicola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The façade has a large central rose window that illuminates the interior, where there is a suggestive mosaic.

  • Where Centro storico, Corigliano d'Otranto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Centro storico della Grecia Salentina

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center can be walked with a series of churches and military and civil buildings, shops and restaurants; the main monuments have panels in Italian, English and Greek, with a poem in Greek to listen to.

  • Where Centro storico, Corigliano d'Otranto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it
