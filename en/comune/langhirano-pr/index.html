Castello di Torrechiara

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The castle built by Pier Maria Rossi between 1448 and 1460 for the beloved Bianca Pellegrini, with the Golden Room frescoed and the view of the Parma valley.

  • Where Strada Castello 10, Langhirano
  • Entry To be checked: single ticket; cumulative €25 with Pilot
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website complessopilotta.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website
