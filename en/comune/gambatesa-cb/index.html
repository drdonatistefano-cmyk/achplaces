What to see in Gambatesa

A hilly village in the province of Campobasso overlooking the valley of Fortore and Lake Occhito, with alleys, arches and stairs; it is known for the Castle of Capua, whose Renaissance residence preserves the cycle of frescoes painted in 1550 by Donato da Copertino, and for the "Maitunate" (Maitunata) the New Year's songs door-to-door.

Castello di Capua

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Medieval manor tied to Riccardo da Pietravalle, lord of the place in the thirteenth century, became in 1484 of the Capua, who transformed it into a Renaissance noble residence by destroying most of the military structures. In 1550 Vincenzo di Capua commissioned Donato da Copertino (called Decumbertino), formed in the school of Giorgio Vasari, a cycle of celebrated frescoes of the family, considered among the most complete of the Kingdom of Naples in the sixteenth century: mythological and biblical episodes, ancient figures, landscapes and allegories in the Salone delle Virtù and in the rooms of the Masks, the Fireplace, the Pergola, the Canneto and the Fire, as well as the Studiolo. It also houses collections of modern and contemporary art (Romeo Musa, Antonio Pettinicchi, Eliseo collection) and a permanent photographic exhibition on Maitunate; hours indicated by the Regional Direction: Tuesday-Sunday 8-19, closed on Monday.

  • Where Largo Castello, Gambatesa
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.musei.molise.beniculturali.it

Sources: musei.molise.beniculturali.it · visitmolise.eu

Centro storico, San Bartolomeo e San Nicola

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town, on a hill overlooking the Fortore valley and the Lake of Occhito, is made of narrow alleys, arches and steep stairs. Next to the castle stands the parish church of San Bartolomeo Apostolo, consecrated in 1696 by Cardinal Orsini, archbishop of Benevento; the church of San Nicola, of the 14th-XV century, is decorated with remarkable canvases.

  • Where Centro storico, Gambatesa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: visitmolise.eu

Santuario di Maria Santissima della Vittoria

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Built in the 12th century, it houses the statue of Our Lady of Victory, carried in procession during the feasts of the Assumption and St. Joseph.
