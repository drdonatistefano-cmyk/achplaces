What to see in Felitto

Medieval village of Cilento perched on the Calore river, closed by walls with thirteen towers and a medieval bridge, entrance to the Gorges of the Calore, oasis of the otter.

4 places to visit in Felitto, in the province of Salerno (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Felitto in the guide with map and filters

Gole del Calore

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Protected area of about 120 hectares where the heat digs limestone cliffs overhanging: rare ferns and horseback riding, otter, raptors, the giants' pots and the natural bridge of Pietratetta. Panoramic path of about 8 km round trip (4-5 hours, medium difficulty); guided tours on Sundays from the town hall, groups by reservation; also destination for kayaking.

  • Where Località Remolino, Felitto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.felitto.sa.it

Sources: comune.felitto.sa.it · promozione.cilentoediano.it

Mura, torri e ponte medievale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village is closed by a wall with thirteen towers and medieval gates, dominated by the 25-metre-high male; by the Rupe di San Nicola a medieval bridge climbs over the Calore and leads to the village, among the palaces of the feudal men.

  • Where Centro storico, Felitto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.felitto.sa.it

Sources: promozione.cilentoediano.it · comune.felitto.sa.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The most important of the churches of the village, in the historic center together with the church of the Rosary and the mother church.
