What to see in Sutera

Nisseno village at 590 meters at the foot of Monte San Paolino, from which you can see 25 villages, the sea of Agrigento, the Madonie and Etna; to visit the Arabic quarter of the Rabato with the houses in plaster, the churches of Sant'Agata, Carmelo and Assunta, the shrine at the top reached by 183 steps and the museum in the former convent of Carmine.

Quartiere Rabato

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Founded by the Arabs around 860 A.D. on the edge of the village, it retains the Arab village's layout with narrow alleys, steep stairways, terraces and houses with plaster walls, including the "dammuso" , the typical Sicilian house in one compartment; many successive stratifications covered it, but the neighborhood design is well read from the hill. It is the scenery of the living nativity scene.

  • Where Via del Carmine, Sutera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info

Monte San Paolino e Santuario

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

The mountain (about 825 meters) rises from Piazza Carmine with a staircase of 183 steps in four ramps, between stations of the Via Crucis, the prison called Filippo D'Angiò and the bell ringed by pilgrims; on top the sanctuary built by Giovanni Chiaramonte in 1370 on the structures of the castle, with a Madonna enthroned between the Saints Damiano and Cosma by Filippo Tancredi and two reliquary urns (1498, with the bones of San Paolino; 1649, by Francesco Rivelo, with those of Sant'Onofrio), and the former convent of the Filippini Fathers. From the summit you can see 25 villages, the sea of Agrigento, the Madonie and Etna; climb about 30 minutes, visit about an hour.

access only accompanied by local staff upon reservation (Ethnoanthropological Museum 331 6976973 or Tourist Office 0934 954300 int. 6), free during the feasts of San Paolino and Sant'Onofrio; not accessible to people with disabilities

Piazza e Chiesa di Sant'Agata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

At the entrance of the village the square is a viewpoint; the fifteenth-century church with three naves houses a marble statue of the Madonna delle Grazie of Lombard workers, the canvas of the Madonna degli Innocenti by Mariano Rossi (mid-18th century) and the wooden choir carved by the Benedictine nuns, transferred in 1727 to a nearby convent.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Agata, Sutera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Maria Santissima del Carmelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was originally built in 1185 and rebuilt in 1934-36 and has a small porch with inserts from the mosque of Rabato; inside the Madonna del Soccorso in marble carved in 1503 by Bartolomeo Berrettaro of Carrara for the Salamone family, whose tombs are in the chapel on the right of the choir.

  • Where Piazza del Carmine, Sutera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Museo Etnoantropologico (ex convento del Carmine)

Museums · Themed

The museum of peasant civilization occupies the small convent of 1664 next to the recently restored Carmelo church; from here also starts the guided tour to Monte San Paolino.

Chiesa Madre di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the Rabato, built in 1370 by Baron Giovanni Chiaramonte on the site of a mosque of about 875, of which it incorporates elements; renovated in 1585, it has a Renaissance portal and a marble baptismal font of 1495.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Collina di San Marco

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hill reached with an easy path, with frescoes of Byzantine style called "figureddi" depicting the four Evangelists, the Madonna and a saint, probably the work of Basilian monks between the fourth and the sixth centuries.

  • Where Collina di San Marco, Sutera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Rocca spaccata

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small hill near the village with the rock split in two, called "jacca" (crack, in dialect): tradition wants it to be opened when Jesus died on the cross.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
