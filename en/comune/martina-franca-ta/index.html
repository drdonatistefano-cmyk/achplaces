What to see in Martina Franca

The most populous municipality of the Valle d'Itria, known mainly for its Baroque architecture: from the Doge's Palace you enter the center from the Porta di Santo Stefano and walk along Corso Vittorio Emanuele to the Basilica of San Martino and the semicircular Piazza Maria Immaculate; in July it hosts the Festival of the Valle d'Itria.

Basilica di San Martino

Places of worship · Basilicas

Rococo church started on May 5, 1747 on a project by Giovanni Mariani, then revised by Giuseppe Morgese, and consecrated on October 22, 1775; the façade in 37 meters high limestone has the high relief of St. Martin who divides the cloak with the poor. Inside the main altar of Sanmartino and Varriale (1773) with the Saint Martin of Stephen of Putignano (1518) and the statues of Abundance and Charity, the Christ to the wooden Column of 1622 and the Hat of the Sacrament with paintings by Domenico Carella; Minor Basilica since 1998.

  • Where Via Masaniello 1, Martina Franca
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.basilicasanmartino.org

MUBA - Museo della Basilica di San Martino

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the eighteenth century Palazzo Stabile, in the cultural complex next to the Basilica, exhibits liturgical furnishings of very high crafts (calici, ostensori, reliquari, piviali, dalmatici, pianete), liturgical books and Gregorian chant on parchments and documents of about five centuries of history of Martina and his Collegiate. Guided tours and café.

  • Where Piazzetta Stabile, Martina Franca
  • Entry €3, reduced €1,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · basilicasanmartino.org · italia.it

Palazzo Ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Wanted in the second half of the seventeenth century by Petracone V Caracciolo on the site of the castle of the Orsini, it has a baroque façade with pilasters on two levels divided by a balcony with wrought iron railing; inside the Chapel of the Dukes and the halls of the Arcadia, the Myth and the Bible, partly decorated by Domenico Carella in 1771-76. It houses the Town Hall and, in the atrium, works and concerts of the Festival of the Itria Valley.

Museo del Parco Pianelle

Museums · Themed

Opened in 1999 on the second floor of the Doge's Palace as a scientific and didactic space that prepares for the visit of the reserve: it presents the natural heritage, landscape and culture of the Pianelle Park, Mediterranean ecosystem of the Murge between Massafra, Crispiano and Martina Franca.

Piazza Maria Immacolata

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Semicircular square on whose open side stands the Basilica; under the neoclassical porch once stood the stalls of the market. Very white during the day, it lights up in the evening.

Corso Vittorio Emanuele e Piazza XX Settembre

Chiesa e Chiostro del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the Carmelites rebuilt from 1727 and completed around 1758, with a major altar consecrated in 1759 and canvas by Pietro Cataldo Mauro (1760); it preserves the Madonna della Libera by Giovanni Stefano Caramia (1660), the wooden Crucifix by Giacomo Genovini (1630), the carved pulpit by Domenico Semeraro (1859) and the inlaid wardrobes of the sacristy. From the church you enter the cloister of the former convent. Open 7:30-12:30 and 16:30-20:30.

  • Where Via Giovanni Battista Pergolesi, Martina Franca
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.martinafranca.ta.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Domenico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1745 and 1750 on a project by the Dominican Fra' Antonio Cantalupi, instead of the medieval San Pietro Martire; the façade, considered the most emblematic example of the martinese baroque, has capitals with mermaids between acanto leaves, musical putti, a window framed by a cherub and the tympanum broken at will.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.martinafranca.ta.it · italia.it

Riserva naturale regionale orientata Bosco delle Pianelle

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

About 600 hectares towards Crispiano and Massafra, reached crossing south of Martina a landscape of red earth, with the richest evergreen formations of the Murge and numerous wild orchids; in the nearby district Specchia Tarantina the Cave of the Nine Housedde, 42 meters deep with limestone concretions, and Masseria Russoli a conservation center of the Donkey of Martina Franca.
