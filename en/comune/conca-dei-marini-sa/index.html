What to see in Conca dei Marini

A fishing village scattered among cliffs and terraces on the UNESCO site of the Amalfi Coast, famous for the Emerald Cave, discovered in 1932; on the promontory the sixteenth-century Torre del Capo di Conca and, overhanging the sea, the former convent of Santa Rosa where the homonymous leaf was born.

Grotta dello Smeraldo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · UNESCO World Heritage

Marine cave discovered in 1932 by the fisherman Luigi Buonocore: the light enters from an underwater crack and tints the water of emerald green, while stalactites and stalagmites form columns under the vault. Since 1956 there is a ceramic nativity scene on the bottom. You arrive there from the state with elevator or staircase, or from the sea; you enter on a raft.

Torre del Capo di Conca (Torre Bianca)

History and architecture · Towers · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Quadrangular watchtower of the sixteenth century, isolated on the headland of Capo di Conca, built to spot Saracen pirates; until about fifty years ago it was used as a small cemetery. Visitable by reservation.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria di Grado ed ex convento di Santa Rosa

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Monastery built overhanging the sea in 1681 by Sister Rosa Pandolfi, inhabited by Dominican cloistered nuns: in her kitchens was born the sfogliatella Santa Rosa. After the death of the last nun, in 1912, it became a hotel; the church guards the head of St. Barnabas, among the most important relics of the province.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio (San Giovanni Battista)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Church of thirteenth-century origin, actually dedicated to St. John the Baptist, with belvedere on the sea, harmonious facade and bell tower with cusp covered with majolica.

Chiesa di San Pancrazio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Church documented since 1362, immersed in olive trees with palm trees on the churchyard; legend has it that in the nearby olive grove the janares were gathered. Several times reported to the Places of the FAI Heart.

  • Where Conca dei Marini
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Sentiero delle cinque essenze

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The most panoramic walk of the village, between vine, lemon, olive, carob and pomegranate: it connects the churches of Sant'Antonio and San Michele up to a sacred newsstand overlooking the precipices of Furore.

  • Where Conca dei Marini
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
