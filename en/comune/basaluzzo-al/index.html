What to see in Basaluzzo

Country with the Latin name "Bis Lucus" (between two woods) in 1799 theater of the battle of Novi: the medieval castle with the watchtower, the fifteenth-century parish church of Sant'Andrea and a historic center with houses in earth.

3 places to visit in Basaluzzo, in the province of Alessandria (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello medievale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Cobblestone streets, stone houses and traditional architecture; some buildings are still in clay, typical construction technique of the area.
