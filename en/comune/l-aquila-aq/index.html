What to see in L'Aquila

The capital of Abruzzo at the foot of the Gran Sasso, reborn after the earthquake of 2009: the Basilica of Collemaggio with the Holy Door of Forgiveness, the 99 Cannelle Fountain, the Spanish Fort and the MuNDA, up to the Roman remains of Amiternum.

Basilica di Santa Maria di Collemaggio

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Begin at the end of the thirteenth century at the behest of Pietro da Morrone, then Pope Celestino V, who is buried there: facade of the first fifteenth century in pink and white marble with three roses and three portals, frescoes of the fifteenth century and the Holy Door open every year for Forgiveness; around the Park of the Sun.

  • Where Piazzale Collemaggio 5, L'Aquila
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it · italia.it

Fontana delle 99 Cannelle

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the Rivera district, three walls with white and pink marble chess with 99 carved faces from which water descends into two large tanks; traditionally recall the 99 castles that founded the city in the thirteenth century.

  • Where Piazza San Vito, L'Aquila
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it · italia.it

Forte Spagnolo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Wanted in 1534 by Vicero Don Pedro of Toledo at the highest point of the city: square plan, high bastions and a 14 meters deep and 23 m wide moat; in the bastion on the right of the entrance the mammoth skeleton, currently closed for restoration.

  • Where Via Castello, L'Aquila
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it · italia.it

MuNDA – Museo Nazionale d'Abruzzo

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The most important museum in the region, now in the former municipal slaughterhouse near the Fontana delle 99 Cannelle: the Abruzzo Madonnas painted on a 14th and 15th century table, works by Andrea de Lithium and Giacinto Brandi and portraits of San Bernardino.

  • Where Viale Benedetto Croce, L'Aquila
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website turismo.abruzzo.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it · cultura.gov.it · museonazionaledabruzzo.it · coopculture.it

Basilica di San Bernardino da Siena

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

It rose in the middle of the fifteenth century, rebuilt in Baroque forms after the earthquake of the eighteenth century: three-order façade of binated columns and, in the central nave, the golden coffered ceiling of Ferdinando Moscow (1730).

Parco archeologico di Amiternum

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The remains of the ancient city near San Vittorino, with the Roman theatre and amphitheatre; recent excavations have revealed tombs and buildings of the VII century BC near the church of San Vittorino. Managed by the National Museum of Abruzzo.

  • Where Frazione San Vittorino, SS80, L'Aquila
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it
