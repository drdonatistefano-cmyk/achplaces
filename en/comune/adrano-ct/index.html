What to see in Adrano

City of the Etna Park on a lava plateau overlooking the valley of Simeto, gathered around the Norman Castle of Piazza Umberto I, home of the Archaeological Museum; in the surroundings the medieval Bridge of the Saracens on the lava forre of the Simeto, in the city the Bellini Theatre, the Mother Church and the Monastery of Saint Lucia.

Castello normanno e Museo archeologico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Symbol of the city in the center of the main square: the current castle, of Catalan character of the early fourteenth century, incorporates an earlier Norman castle tower built on a Saracen tower; Count Giovanni Tommaso Moncada (1461-1501) surrounded it with a bastion with four corner towers. Damaged by the earthquake of 1693 and used for a long time as a prison, it was restored from the fifties of the twentieth century and destined for a museum. The museum, entitled to Luigi Bernabò Brea and Saro Franco, tells about 5,000 years of history on five floors, from the Neolithic (6500-3500 BC) to the Middle Ages: prehistoric materials of the Ethnea area, the capitals of lava stone and ceramics of the Sicilian town of Mendolito, the Greek city of Adranon, Roman finds, ethno-anthropological collections and the "Fondo Imbarrato" of ancient books from the 16th to the 19th century. Open Monday-Saturday 9-18, Sunday 9-13.

  • Entry €5, reduced €2,50
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Ponte dei Saraceni

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Considered one of the most beautiful civil works of the Sicilian Middle Ages, it crosses the Simeto in the district of "Salto del Pecoraio." Probably of Roman origin, along one of the roads of the wheat towards the Ionic ports, was rebuilt in Arabic era with sharp sixth arches and alternation of light and dark stones; in Norman age it connected Troina to Catania. Damaged by the earthquake of 1693 and the flood of 1948, it preserves the main central arch in Gothic style. From 2000 it falls on the site of community interest "Forre lavache del Simeto" with gorges 5 to 15 meters high excavated in basalt; in the nearby Mendolito district the walls and the South Gate of a Sicilian city of the IX-V century BC have been identified.

Teatro Vincenzo Bellini

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Heir of an eighteenth-century room, was renovated by the architect Vincenzo Costa and inaugurated in 1846; the restoration of 1882 (Carlo Sada and Antonino Grasso) gave him the present appearance, with a horseshoe room, 44 loggias on three orders and golden stuccoes, and curtain of Giuseppe Rapisarda with Timoleonte in the temple of Adrano. The Liberty façade, completed in the early twentieth century by Gaspare Silvestri Amari, is crowned by the group of Mario Moschetti with Tragedy, Music and Comedy. Since about 1920 it bears the name of Bellini; it appears in the Italian-style Divorce of Pietro Germi; it was recovered in 2004 after more than twenty years of closure.

Access allowed only at organised events
