What to see in Bassiano

Borgo dei Monti Lepini on a hill among the woods, Flag Arancione, home of Aldo Manuzio: the walls of the Caetani, the Museum of Scriptures in the Palazzo Caetani, the Collegiate Church of Sant'Erasmo and the Cave of Selvascura.

Museo delle Scritture Aldo Manuzio

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the lower floors of the Baronial Palace of the Caetani, seat of the Municipality, is dedicated to the humanist and publisher Aldo Manuzio (1449-1515), born in Bassiano: three rooms with typographical machines follow the path "Fiume delle Scripture" on the evolution of writing, integrated with the historical traces of the halls and the prison, with space to the marginal writings local like those of the shepherds.

MiC: visit by reservation required (tel. 0773 355013)

  • Where Via Aldo Manuzio, Bassiano
  • Entry €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Mura e borgo medievale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The fortified walls were built by the Caetani at the end of the 13th century, with nine towers and three gates; inside, winding alleys and narrow stairs, on a hill surrounded by the woods of the Lepini.

  • Where Centro storico, Bassiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Grotta di Selvascura (Grotta dei Templari)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In the Selvascura district, under Mount Furchiavecchia, about 3 km from the square along Via della Croce, close to the Sanctuary of the Crucifix; Fraticelli Franciscani and Templari settled there and the walls are frescoed (works dated XIII-XIV century, restored), with a wooden sculpture of 1673 by Fra' Vincenzo Pietrosanti. The last hermit died in the late 1970s; the cave is still open to visitors.

  • Where Contrada Selvascura, Bassiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Prosciutto di Bassiano

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The ham of Bassiano is the king of the cold cuts of Monti Lepini, celebrated by the Festival of Ham on the last Sunday of July; among the typical products also oil, sausages with fennel and truffles.
