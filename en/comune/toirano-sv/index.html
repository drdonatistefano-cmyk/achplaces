What to see in Toirano

Borgo della val Varatella, Orange Flag, known for the Toirano Caves with prehistoric traces of Bàsura and the ethnographic museum in the historic centre.

5 places to visit in Toirano, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Toirano in the guide with map and filters

Grotte di Toirano

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

A kilometer from the village among white limestone rocks: the cave of Bàsura, with remains of the lower Paleolithic, human footprints, traces of torches and bear nails, then the cave of Santa Lucia inferiore with stalactites and stalagmites, and the exit near Santa Lucia superiore overlooking the valley.

  • Where Piazzale Dario, Toirano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets

Sources: italia.it

Grotta del Colombo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites
