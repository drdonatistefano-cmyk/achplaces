What to see in Tarvisio

A historic mountain village in the Val Canale valley, where you will find Julian and Carnic Alps and Latin, Slavic and Germanic cultures: the Tarvisio Forest, the Fusine and Predil Lakes, the sanctuary of Mount Lussari, the Cave del Predil mine and the military historical museum.

9 places to visit in Tarvisio, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Monte Santo di Lussari e santuario

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

At about 1,800 meters, on the meeting point between Carnic and Julian Alps, with views of Jof di Montasio, Jof Fuart and Mangart; the village is the highest in Friuli Venezia Giulia. The sanctuary, linked to an apparition of the Virgin, dates back to 1360, rebuilt and restored several times last in 2000; on foot you climb from the valley floor to the Pilgrim's Path.

  • Where Monte Lussari, Tarvisio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismofvg.it

Laghi di Fusine

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

Two basins of glacial origin, Lower and Upper, connected by an underground water system, on the border with Slovenia, where the chain of Ponze, Mangart and Masso Pirona are mirrored. Spruce, spruce and beech woods host deer, deer, chamois and migratory birds; a path climbs to the Zacchi Refuge and to Capanna Ponza.

  • Where Laghi di Fusine, Tarvisio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Lago del Predil

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

According to the natural lake of Friuli Venezia Giulia, in a glacial basin near the Slovenian border, with the Forte del Lago; in summer you take a bath, in winter ice. A circle of 5.4 km (about 1 hour and 40, difficulty T2) surrounds it between beech and equipped sections, not recommended for families with small children.

  • Where Lago del Predil, Tarvisio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it

Parco internazionale geominerario di Raibl

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

The lead and zinc mine of Raibl-Cave del Predil, for centuries among the most important in Europe, closed in 1991, had 120 km of tunnels on 19 levels up to 520 m of depth. You can visit only with a guide (about an hour), on foot or with an electric train; the Exhibition of the mining tradition tells the country, the work of miners and the closure. A cumulative ticket with the military historical museum.

  • Where Largo Giuseppe Mazzini 7, Cave del Predil, Tarvisio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museitarvisio.it

Sources: museitarvisio.it · italia.it · turismofvg.it

Museo storico militare delle Alpi Giulie

Museums · City history · Paid entry

It tells the military history of the Julian Alps between Veneto, Carinthia and Slovenia: an introductory multimedia room and it rises on the Napoleonic Wars, the First and the Second World War. Outside there is an open-air museum with the strong Carinthias and the works of Vallo Littorio, partly in recovery. A cumulative ticket with the Geominerary Park €10, reduced €8.
