What to see in Vernante

Country of the Vermenagna Valley known as the village of Pinocchio for the more than 150 murals taken from the illustrations of Attilio Mussino, who lived here the last years; above the village the ruins of the castle of Tourousela and, in the surroundings, the reserve of Palanfré in the Park of the Maritime Alps.

4 places to visit in Vernante, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Murales di Pinocchio e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Since 1989 Bruno Carletto and Bartolomeo Cavallera (Meo and Carlet) have painted on the facades of the historic center over 150 murals and great illustrations that retrace the adventures of Pinocchio in the style of Attilio Mussino, the first color illustrator of Collodi's novel. They are mainly located along Via Maestra and in the side alleys, in Via Molino Stotto and near the parish, between fountains, squares and courtyards; Casa Carlet has painted facades and a garden with works on the characters.

  • Where Via Maestra, Vernante
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitcuneese.it

Museo Attilio Mussino - Museo di Pinocchio

Museums · Themed

In the basement of the ancient church of the Confraternita, he collects works and memories left to the village by Margherita Martini, second wife of illustrator Attilio Mussino, who spent the last years in Vernante founding a free art school open to all; at the cemetery a bas-relief shows Pinocchio crying on his grave.

Visit on request to the Tourist Office of the Municipality

  • Where Antica chiesa della Confraternita, Vernante
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitcuneese.it

Castello Lascaris «La Tourousela»

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The hexagonal tower with remains of walls that dominates Vernante: it is what remains of the castle's mast, built at the end of the thirteenth century by the counts of Tenda (Lascaris) as a tolling place on the road of the Colle di Tenda, destroyed by the Spaniards about three centuries later. You reach it with a dirt road in the woods.

  • Where Vernante
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · italia.it

Riserva di Palanfré

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the Grande valley, inside the Natural Park of the Maritime Alps, lakes, woods and a restored alpine village; Palanfre is the starting point for excursions, including the beech forest of the Bosco Bandito.

  • Where Palanfré, Vernante
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · italia.it
