What to see in Novate Mezzola

Porta della Val Codera, valley without roads inhabited all year round, on the shores of Lake Mezzola: the stairway of thousands of steps to the village of Codera, the ethnographic museum of the valley and the Museum of Picapreda, memory of the granite quarries Sanfedelino that paved Milan, Como, Pavia and Bologna.

4 places to visit in Novate Mezzola, in the province of Sondrio (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della picapreda

Museums · Themed

Images, tools and testimonials of the granite stonemasons Sanfedelino, which takes its name from the first open quarry next to the Sanctuary of San Fedele and paved streets and squares of Milan, Como, Pavia and Bologna: in the five years before the Great War, more than 82,000 tons of stone left the station of Novate, and between the twenties and thirty the quarries were twenty; today one remains active. Open a few days a week; guided tours by reservation.

  • Where Piazza Europa, di fronte al Municipio, Novate Mezzola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valchiavenna.com

Sources: valchiavenna.com

Museo della Val Codera

Museums · Themed

Historical, ethnographic and naturalistic eco-museum founded in 1982 by the Association Amici della Val Codera and recognized by Regione Lombardia, divided between the villages of Codera and San Giorgio: domestic life, weaving, breeding and milk, chestnut, school, handicrafts and minerals (aquamarine and garnet) in the House of Valle; in San Giorgio the historical section, with the Thirty Years War, and the outdoor reconstruction of the work of the picapreda. You can only reach it on foot; free admission.

  • Where Codera e San Giorgio, Val Codera, Novate Mezzola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.valchiavenna.com

Sources: valchiavenna.com · cultura.gov.it

Val Codera e borgo di Codera

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valley without road access but inhabited all year round, rare case in the alpine arch. From the parking lot of Novate a staircase of 2,500-3,000 steps in the centuries-old chestnut grove, with views of Lake Mezzola and parapets in the exposed points, climbs to the village of Codera; then the path continues sweet up to Bresciadega and to the Rifugio Brasca (10 km, 466 m of difference in altitude, about 2 hours and a half, medium difficulty), between ancient stone houses and the granite quarries of San Fedelino.

  • Where Imbocco del sentiero per la Val Codera, Novate Mezzola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.valchiavenna.com

Sources: in-lombardia.it · valchiavenna.com

Lago di Mezzola

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lake north of the Lario between Verceia and Novate Mezzola, with an unusual mild climate for an Alpine valley: calm waters for kayak, canoeing, stand-up paddle, rowing and boat trips, a bike path along the shore between the two villages and reeds towards the mouth of Mera. From Novate you can reach the Romanesque temple of San Fedelino by boat.

  • Where Lungolago, Novate Mezzola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valchiavenna.com

Sources: valchiavenna.com · lombardiafacile.regione.lombardia.it
