What to see in Trevico

The oldest village of Baronia, at 1094 meters, from which the look embraces Campania, Puglia, Molise and Basilicata; medieval capital of Baronia di Vico and bishop's seat until the nineteenth century, preserves the Cathedral of the Assumption with the frescoed crypt, the ruins of the castle and the sixteenth-century Port'Alba. The director Ettore Scola was born there.

Cripta museo della Cattedrale

Museums · Old masters

The museum occupies the crypt under the Cathedral, re-emerged after the 1980 earthquake. It exhibits a cycle of apse frescoes of about 11 square meters, probably of Neapolitan students of Giotto and restored by the Superintendence, with the Crucifix enthroned among Saints Peter, Catherine of Alexandria, Antonio Abbot, Giovanni Battista, Guglielmo da Vercelli and a Madonna with the Child; next, the lid of a Roman tomb with inscription, a mensory stone to measure cereals, two Corinthian capitals and two wooden sculptures of the Madonna of the Free of the XIV and XVI century.

The MiC card (updated 2020) indicates closed every day, while the site of the Municipality says visitable every day from 9 to 12; contact tel. 0827 96066

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta (Madonna della Libera)

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

It was the cathedral of the diocese of Trevico from 1058 to 1818, when the original Greek cross plant had already been transformed into a long central nave; several times rebuilt after the earthquakes, it collapsed in the roof with the 1980 earthquake and was reopened to worship on August 8, 1998. Facade in stone and eighteenth-century bell tower with civic clock; inside the main altar in precious marbles with the canvas of the Assumption, the wooden choir of the eighteenth century, the bishop's throne and, in the chapel of the Rosary, the baptismal font in stone of 1618; the parish archive preserves miniature books and medieval parchments. Open during function hours.

Ruderi del Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the highest point of the old town, among the oaks of the Empire woods, with views of the valley of Baronia di Vico. The first document on the fortress dates back to 1078, when it was sold to Roberto il Guiscardo; it was a stronghold of Baronia and then a residence of barons and marquises, until the remains of stones and bricks caused its abandonment. There are still the cylindrical tower on the east side, the belt with six large windows and parts of the living environments; it is freely seen from the outside.

Port'Alba

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Also known as Porta Jacovella, it was built in 1578 by the Loffredos, lords of Trevico until the abolition of feudality in 1806, and is the only survivor of the three gates of the village. A round arch on two pillars of lava stone, surmounted by a small loggia and noble coats of arms including that of the Loffredo; traditionally the brides pass under the wedding day.
