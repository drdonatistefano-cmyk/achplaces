What to see in Santa Maria del Cedro

The small capital of the Riviera dei Cedri, on the Cosentine High Tyrrhenian Sea in the regional park of the same name, linked to the Cedar of Calabria DOP that every year the rabbis gather here for the Jewish feast of Sukkot; to visit the Museum of the Cedar, the archaeological park of Laos and the medieval complex of the Abatemarco.

Parco archeologico di Laos

History and architecture · Archaeology

On the hill of San Bartolo, in the hamlet of Marcellina, about 60 hectares preserve the remains of Laos, achea colony later became autonomous (italia.it date 510 BC): established in 1994 after the Italian-French excavations, it shows the main north-south road originally 12 meters wide, crossed every 96 meters from east-west roads, and large houses around uncovered courtyards, almost all with facilities for rain water. A wooden fence follows the ancient streets; the finds are exposed to the Antiquarium of Scalea.

visits only by reservation, with seasonal timetable (MiC): tel. 0985 9969, Sabap-cal.scalea@cultura.gov.it

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo del Cedro

Museums · Themed

In the Palazzo Gabriele Marino, he tells with educational panels and multimedia stations the history of the Cedar of Calabria DOP, the collection, the processing and the ritual use in Judaism; from the museum start an art journey in the historic center and an archaeological towards Laos.

  • Where Località Impresa, Santa Maria del Cedro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it

Complesso di San Michele all'Abatemarco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval site on the hill with the ruins of the castle of San Michele, also called the Abatemarco, and the church of the same name, entirely frescoed, built on an ancient Basilian monastery.

  • Where Località Abatemarco, Santa Maria del Cedro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it
