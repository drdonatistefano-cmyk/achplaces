What to see in Barzio

This is the pearl of Valsassina, which has been a holiday destination since the 19th century. Barzio preserves the seventeenth-century Palazzo Manzoni, the church of Sant'Alessandro and the Museo Medardo Rosso; from the village the historic cable car for the Piani di Bobbio starts.

Museo Medardo Rosso

Museums · Modern art

Managed by the artist's family, he was born when his son Francesco, after the death of the sculptor in 1928, collected works and materials from the studies of Paris and Milan in Barzio and bought an ancient seventeenth-century church near the oratory (the oratory of St. John the Baptist), with the advice of Piero Portaluppi. It exhibits about 25 sculptures, including waxes and plasters such as "Man who reads" (1893), "Bookmaker" (1894), "Sick at the hospital" (1899), "Conversation in the garden" (1899) and "Ecce Puer" (1906), in addition to rotational drawings; the original set-up with stands and vintage windows is preserved.

  • Where Via Baruffaldi 4, Barzio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · in-lombardia.it

Funivia e Piani di Bobbio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

From Barzio climbs the historic cable car to the Piani di Bobbio, renowned for winter sports; the name comes from the abbey of Bobbio (Piacenza), of which they were alpine pastures. In summer hiking rings between 1,800 and 2,000 meters around the Campelli and towards Artavaggio, easy (2h30-3h30) or challenging as the Path of the Roads with equipped stretches.

  • Where Partenza funivia, Barzio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
