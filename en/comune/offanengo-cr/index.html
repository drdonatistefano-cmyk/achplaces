What to see in Offanengo

In Cremasco, about 5 km from the Serio Park, Offanengo preserves the Oratory of San Rocco, erected during the plague of 1527-28 and the oldest monument of the village, the Collegiate Church of Santa Maria Purified in 1896 with bell tower of over 50 meters, the eighteenth-century church of Madonna del Pozzo and the Museum of Rural Civilization.

Oratorio di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the first half of the sixteenth century, on the occasion of the pestilence of 1527-28, in honor of the patron saint of the plague patients: it is the oldest monument in Offanengo. An example of Lombard architecture of the period, it preserves frescoes in the apse area, after 1566.

Collegiata di Santa Maria Purificata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The present church was built in 1896, when the previous one was demolished; the documents remind churches of Mary Purified and St. Lawrence dating back to the IV-V century. The interior has three aisles separated by alternate arches with semi-colons of classical taste; the bell tower exceeds 50 meters.

Chiesa della Madonna del Pozzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built at the end of the eighteenth century instead of a small oratory, it has simple lines and a very decorated internal altar. The name comes from the well of the little house of the romit, today the housekeeper's residence; on the square there was a fair on September 8th, still remembered by the Fair of the Madonna del Pozzo.

Museums · Themed
