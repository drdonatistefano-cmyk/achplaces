What to see in Camogli

Marinar village on the Gulf of Paradiso with the basilica on the harbor and the castle of Dragonara, and in its territory the abbey of San Fruttuoso reachable only on foot or by sea, Punta Chiappa and San Rocco.

9 places to visit in Camogli, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Abbazia di San Fruttuoso

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Founded in the 8th century and rebuilt in the 10th century as a Benedictine monastery; cloister, church and tomb of the Doria of the 13th century, with the tower of 1562; well FAI reachable only on foot or by sea.

  • Where San Fruttuoso, Camogli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
