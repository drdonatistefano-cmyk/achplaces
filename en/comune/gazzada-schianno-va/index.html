What to see in Gazzada Schianno

The town unites the two villages of Gazzada and Schianno, between the high plain and the morainic hills that go up towards the Prealps; in Gazzada stands Villa Cagnola, romantic residence with English park and a precious collection of gold-bottom paintings, while in Schianno the church of the Saints Cosma and Damiano preserves the only bell tower-portico (clocher-porche) of Lombardy.

Villa Cagnola e parco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

In 1611 a first building with garden belonged to the Perabò, which in the eighteenth century enlarged the villa; passed to the Melzi d'Eril and in 1850 to the Cagnola, it was transformed into a romantic residence by Luigi Clerichetti (1856-58) for Carlo Cagnola and renovated in 1900-01 by Achille Majnoni d'Intignano for Guido Cagnola, who in 1946 donated it to the Holy See; it is home to the Higher Institute of Religious Studies, today ISSR Blessed Paul VI, and it is also a convention center with hotel and restaurant. The English park, west and north of the villa, has tall trees such as the cedar of Lebanon and the sophora of Japan, a terraced garden closed by a long balcony and a Neo-Medieval tower with terracotta trifores. The park is open from 8 to 19 (April 1-30 September) and from 9 to 16 (October 1 to March 31), with access only from the main entrance; dogs allowed on leash.

Collezione d'arte di Villa Cagnola

Museums · Old masters

Guido Cagnola expanded his paternal collections, forming one of the most beautiful private collections of paintings: above all gold funds of Tuscan and Venetian painters of the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries and of Lombards between the fourteenth and sixteenth centuries, with works by Ercole de' Roberti, by the Master of the Madonna Cagnola, by Jacopo Bellini, by Vivarini and, for the eighteenth century, by Francesco Guardi. The collection of ceramics, majolica and oriental porcelains, Flemish and French tapestries, antique furniture, plates and bronze sculptures is completed. Visit by reservation (groups of at least 8 people every day); guided tours open to the public on the second and last Sunday of the month at 4:00 p.m.

Chiesa dei Santi Cosma e Damiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Cited in the documents from 1288 but probably before the Mille, it has a massive tower with the entrance to the base: it is the only example in Lombardy of clocher-porche, the bell tower that acts as an entrance porch. Inside there are frescoes from the XV-XVI century.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Oratory of the Assumption was born from the will of Antoniotto Ghiringhelli on March 18, 1488 and was consecrated by Carlo Borromeo on June 27, 1574; abandoned by the patrons and reduced to woodshed, returned to the parish around 1900 and was restored on several occasions, from the years of Guido Cagnola mayor until the works completed in 1996 and the final restoration started in 2006. It preserves a cycle of frescoes from the fifteenth century attributed to Galdino da Varese and his school.

Chiesa parrocchiale della Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It is the parish church of Gazzada, born after in 1567 Carlo Borromeo divided in two the only parish of Gazzada Schianno. It preserves two 16th century frescoes by Francesco De Tatti with the Crucifixion and Adoration of the Magi.
