What to see in Ruviano

Municipality of Alto Casertano with the historic hamlet of Alvignanello, where the parish museum of sacred art collects statues, ex-vote, vestments and ceramic fragments of the local excavations; in the capital stands the parish church of San Leone Magno and in Alvignanello the convent of Santa Maria degli Angeli, built between the sixteenth and eighteenth centuries.

Museo di arte sacra

Museums · Themed

Parish Museum in the hamlet of Alvignanello that preserves statues and votive gold, ex-vote, liturgical objects, sacred vestments presented in their historical evolution, paintings linked to religious tradition and ceramic fragments from local excavations, together with findings donated already studied by archaeologists. It addresses educational activities in particular to young people; contacts: 0823 863388, 339 3469741.

Chiesa Madre di San Leone Magno

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of the Diocese of Alife-Caiazzo: according to the CEI census was built in 1326, it became parish in 1590 and was rebuilt in 1774 with the title change, with restorations in 1805 and 1876. The ICCD card describes the eighteenth-century building, the work of local workers, with a single nave and rectangular plan, with tuff masonry, barrel vault and roof with layers in tiles.

Convento di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Convent in the hamlet of Alvignanello, built between the middle of the XVI and the middle of the XVIII century by local workers and still used as a convent, owned by the Bishop's Curia Alifana-Caiatina. The ICCD card describes it as a rectangular plan, in tuff masonry, with barrel vaults and roof in tiles, in good state of preservation; it is protected as a cultural asset.
