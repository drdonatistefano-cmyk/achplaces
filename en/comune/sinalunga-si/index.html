What to see in Sinalunga

Borgo sulla Valdichiana, home of the Chianina breed, with a historic centre on a small plateau.

6 places to visit in Sinalunga, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Sinalunga in the guide with map and filters

Palazzo Pretorio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Built between 1337 and 1346, with the bell tower reminiscent of the Torre del Mangia, the coats of arms of the Sienese and Medici podestàs and the gogna next to the main portal.

  • Where Piazza IV Novembre, Sinalunga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Collegiata di San Martino e Chiesa di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In Piazza Garibaldi the Collegiate Church of 1588 with Our Lady and Child and Saints of Sodom; not far from Santa Croce with the Bride of the Virgin of Luca Signorelli's school.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Sinalunga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Le Stanze di Larth

Museums · Archaeological

Museum space dedicated to the Etruscans with the findings of the necropolis of San Giustino and the settlement of the Prisons, in the municipal territory.

  • Where Centro storico, Sinalunga
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Fonte del Castagno e Sentiero dell'Acqua

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

At the entrance of the village the source dated from an inscription to 1265; the neighboring underground tunnels form the Water Path, which can be visited by reservation at the municipal tourist office.

  • Where Sinalunga
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Convento di San Bernardino

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Church of 1449 in Poggio Baldino with convent of Friars Minor: icons attributed to Sano di Pietro, the Annunciation of Welcome by John (1470) and works by Guidoccio Cozzarelli.

  • Where Poggio Baldino, Sinalunga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Pieve di San Pietro ad Mensulas

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the hamlet of Pieve di Sinalunga one of the oldest churches in the area, with a Romanesque basilica on much older foundations.

  • Where Pieve di Sinalunga, Sinalunga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
