What to see in Sant'Alessio con Vialone

A small town in the countryside of Pavese, where a square-shaped castle, isolated from the fields, has been home to the Oasis of Sant'Alessio since 1973, a park for the breeding and re-introduction of wild species; in the hamlet Vialone the courthouse that gave its name to the rice Vialone.

Oasi di Sant'Alessio - Parco faunistico

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry

Biodiversity reserve founded in 1973, when Antonia and Harry Salamon bought the castle of Sant'Alessio with the plan to raise endangered species to repopulate its nature; it is dedicated to the protection of fauna and the re-introduction of endangered, even non-native species. It is a stop of migratory birds such as herons, seafood fishermen and water hens and protects amphibians like frogs and toads; the visit follows the European route, the tropical one and the butterflies and hummingbirds section. Open from April to mid-November (until November 15 only Saturday, Sunday and holidays), closed in January; tickets in cash or online, without reservation; dogs are not allowed.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.oasisantalessio.it

Castello di Sant'Alessio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Castle in isolated position in the countryside of the village, square plan with concentric shorts and a high tower with loggia that had military function of sighting. Purchased in 1973 by the founders of the Oasis, it is still part of it: from the castle you can access the wildlife park.

Cascina Vialone

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry

Closed courtyard farmhouse, with buildings arranged at right angles around the courtyard, today dedicated mainly to the breeding of cattle and pigs. Dai de' Giorgi passed in 1554 to Count Giovanni Battista Busca, in 1642 to Bellisomi (until 1820), then to Marozzi and, by legacy, to the Holy See in 1970; since 1973 it belongs to the Panigati family. Here a workman of the Devecchi brothers isolated from a cultivation of rice \"ranghino" three different ears, from which was born the variety Vialone, spread since 1904.

private property, visible only from the outside

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Alessio confessore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of the diocese of Pavia already documented in the apostolic visit of Angelo Peruzzi in 1576, when he belonged to the vicariate of Marzano; in 1807 there was annexed the oratory of St. Biagio. It was the seat of the Vicariate between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries and since 1989 it has been part of the Vicariate IV of the diocese.
