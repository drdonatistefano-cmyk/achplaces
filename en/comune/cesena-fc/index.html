What to see in Cesena

The city of Malatesta: the Malatesta Library, the first civic library of Europe and UNESCO World Memory, the Fortress on Garampo Hill and Piazza del Popolo with the Masini Fountain.

26 places to visit in Cesena, in the province of Forlì-Cesena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 7 of the 12 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Biblioteca Malatestiana

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Wanted by Novello Malatesta and opened between 1452 and 1454, it is the first civic library in Europe: the classroom with three naves with the plutei and the codes has arrived intact until today. It has been registered in the UNESCO World Memory Register since 2005.

  • Where Piazza Bufalini 1, Cesena
  • Entry €8 full, €5 reduced; free on the first Sunday of the month, for residents of Cesena and up to 18 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website sititematici.comune.cesena.fc.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: sititematici.comune.cesena.fc.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · ticketing.coopculture.it
