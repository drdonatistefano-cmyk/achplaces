What to see in Castroreale

Borgo dei Peloritani overlooking Capo Milazzo and the Aeolies, born around the castle of Federico II d'Aragona: the 17th century Mother Church with the sundial of 1854, the churches with works by Antonello Gagini, the Civic Museum in the oratory of the Filippines and the Parish Museum in Santa Maria degli Angeli.

Chiesa Madre dell'Assunta e campanile

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the first thirty years of the seventeenth century, with a sixteenth-century bell tower and clock working: it preserves a Santa Caterina in marble (1534) and a water saint (1530) by Antonello Gagini, works by Andrea Calamech, Rinaldo Bonanno and Filippo Jannelli, baptismal font of 1625, pulpit of 1648, choir of 1612 and silver seventeenth-century Messinese. On the floor runs the dark chamber sundial made in 1854 by Nicolò Perroni Basquez.

  • Where Piazza delle Aquile, Castroreale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Museo civico di Castroreale

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Founded in 1984 and opened in 1989 in the former oratory of the Filippini Fathers (1632) and in the church of San Filippo Neri, on two floors of works Messinese from the fifteenth to the eighteenth century: a Salvator Mundi attributed to Polidoro da Caravaggio, a painted cross of the fourteenth century, a wooden crucifix of the fifteenth century, the funeral monument of Geronimo Rosso by Antonello Gagini, a polyptych of the late fifteenth century and a table of Antonello de Saliba.

  • Where Via Guglielmo Siracusa 35, Castroreale
  • Entry €3, reduced €1,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.castroreale.me.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Museo parrocchiale in Santa Maria degli Angeli

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

From 2005 the church of Santa Maria degli Angeli houses the Parish Museum of Sacred Furniture, with sacred paintings from the fifteenth century to the beginning of the seventeenth century: five small tables of the late fourteenth century with stories of the Virgin, a Flemish triptych of the sixteenth century and the Madonna dell'Accomandata by Antonello Freri (1510).

  • Where Via Guglielmo Siracusa 31, Castroreale
  • Entry €2
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiese di Sant'Agata e Santa Marina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Complex in the lower part of via Siracusa: Santa Marina combines Norman elements, an Aragonese fortification and Baroque decorations; St. Agatha, largely rebuilt in 1857, preserves an Annunciation by Antonello Gagini (1519), a Sant'Agata del Montorsoli (1554), a Madonna by Michelangelo Naccherino (1601) and the Christ Long, carried in procession on 23 and 25 August and in Holy Week.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa della Candelora

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the fifteenth century with Arabic-style dome, probably chapel of the castle of Frederick II of Aragon: the high altar has a wooden grandstand, example of the Baroque carving of Messinese.

Piazza delle Aquile, via Moschita e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the Piazza delle Aquile, with the tombstone surmounted by three eagles for the privileges of Philip IV of Spain, you can see Capo Milazzo and the Aeolian Islands. Via Moschita preserves portals and wrought iron grates of the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries and the arch of the ancient synagogue; in Piazza Pertini the Palazzo Peculio (municipio) and the Monte di Pietà seventeenth century; the Porta Raineri, to the north, was rebuilt at the beginning of the nineteenth century.

  • Where Piazza delle Aquile, Castroreale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa del SS. Salvatore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built towards the end of the fifteenth century in the ancient Giudecca, then enriched with baroque stuccoes, with Gothic portal, marble altar and bell tower of 1560; today it is used as auditorium.

  • Entry To be checked

Chiesa di Gesù e Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the fourteenth century, probably the first mother church, reached by a double stone staircase; the wooden altar, called the biscuit of the abbess, was traditionally designed by the nuns of the convent.

Biscotto castricianu

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

"U biscottu castricianu" or "da Badissa" (a long secret recipe of the Clarisses of the monastery of Santa Maria degli Angeli), celebrated with a festival in the second half of August; to try also the homemade macaroni and the black Christmas rice.
