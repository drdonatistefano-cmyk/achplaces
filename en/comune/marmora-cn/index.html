What to see in Marmora

Municipality of Valle Maira scattered in small stone hamlets, connected by the ring of the Giro between the villages: in Vernetti, seat of the town hall, the ethnographic museum outdoors; in Toulouse and San Sebastiano the frescoes of the itinerant painter Giors Boneto (1807-1808); in Brieis the tower called "casa d'avoucat" .

5 places to visit in Marmora, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo etnografico all'aperto

Museums · Themed · Free entry

It develops among the narrow streets of the hamlet Vernetti, at the Locanda Ceaglio: photographs and tools of the past tell the peasant life and the labors of the ancestors. Always visitable, but it is better to check first (tel. +39 0171 998117).

  • Where Locanda Ceaglio, frazione Vernetti, Marmora
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Giro tra le borgate

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring that starts from Vernetti (1,223 m), near the town hall, on mule tracks, dirt roads and paths among the larch trees: touches the remains of an ancient watchtower, the sanctuary of the Madonna del Biamondo, the villages of San Sebastiano, Arata, Toulouse, Urzio, Reinero and Sodà, rises to the Parish (1,548 m) that dominates the whole valley and returns from Brieis (1,416 m), with optional deviation to Sagna Rotonda.

  • Where Partenza da borgata Vernetti, Marmora
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.marmora.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Borgata Tolosano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Dry walls, stone paved sections, round pillars, large roofs and balconies in larch, a bread oven put into operation and the chapel of Saints George and Anna at the entrance; on the façades the frescoes of Giors Boneto, including a Crucifixion with St. Anthony Abbot and St. John the Evangelist and a Madonna of the Seven Dolori with the dead Christ. A carved beam carries an inscription on the brevity of life.

  • Where Borgata Tolosano, Marmora
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.marmora.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Borgata San Sebastiano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Two cores of houses: high up the former school and stone portals, at the bottom the chapel of Saints Sebastiano and Fabiano. A fresco by Giors Boneto, traveling painter of Paesana, active in the villages in 1807-1808, with the Assunta, San Martino dividing the mantle and San Sebastiano pierced by arrows.

  • Where Borgata San Sebastiano, Marmora
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.marmora.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Borgata Brieis

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Dominated by a tower with four sloping roofs, called in the tradition of the avoucat house; the oven resting on the rock above the fountain has the roof only one sloping and the chapel of the Holy Trinity preserves a fresco dated 1677. A path connects it directly to Vernetti.

  • Where Borgata Brieis, Marmora
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.marmora.cn.it · visitcuneese.it
