What to see in Perugia

Umbrian capital on a hill between Etruscan walls and medieval palaces: Piazza IV Novembre with the Fontana Maggiore and the Palazzo dei Priori, the Umbria National Gallery and the underground of the Rocca Paolina.

25 places to visit in Perugia, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza IV Novembre e Fontana Maggiore

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Fontana Maggiore, among the greatest examples of medieval Italian sculpture, was built between 1278 and 1280 by Nicola and Giovanni Pisano to celebrate the new aqueduct, on a project by Fra Bevignate and with the hydraulic part of Boninsegna from Venice.

  • Where Piazza IV Novembre, Perugia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: comune.perugia.it · turismo.comune.perugia.it · italia.it

Palazzo dei Priori

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Venue of the Municipality, the National Gallery of Umbria and the two major medieval guilds, the Nobile Collegio della Mercanzia and the Nobile Collegio del Cambio; above the portal the bronze copies of the grifo and the Guelph lion, whose originals (1271-81) are in the atrium.

  • Where Corso Vannucci, Perugia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.perugia.it · italia.it

Galleria Nazionale dell'Umbria

Museums · Old masters

From 1878 to the upper floors of the Palazzo dei Priori, it is considered among the most prestigious Italian art museums, with numerous paintings by Pietro Vannucci, the Perugino, and his students.

  • Where Corso Pietro Vannucci 19, Perugia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · regione.umbria.it

Nobile Collegio del Cambio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between 1452 and 1457 as the seat of the guild of money changers, inside the Palazzo dei Priori; the last room is that of the Legists, with the seats in walnut of the early seventeenth century.

  • Where Corso Vannucci, Perugia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Rocca Paolina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

The cyclopic papal fortress, born from events related to the salt trade, at the southern end of the historic centre; after the restorations you cross on foot, with escalators and tunnels, in the subsoil of the city.

  • Where Piazza Italia, Perugia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Pozzo Etrusco

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

One of the city's symbolic monuments, at the Colle del Sole on the ancient acropolis; also known as Pozzo Sorbello, is an exceptional work of hydraulic engineering of the second half of the third century BC.

  • Where Piazza Danti 18, Perugia

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry
