What to see in Gangi

Borgo delle Madonie dominated by the Castello dei Ventimiglia, with the fourteenth-century Torre dei Ventimiglia becoming a bell tower, the Mother Church with the Last Judgment of Giuseppe Salerno, the eighteenth-century Palazzo Bongiorno and the four museums of Palazzo Sgadari.

9 places to visit in Gangi, in the province of Palermo (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre dei Ventimiglia

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

A fourteenth-century tower on Piazza del Popolo, born as a watchtower and transformed into a bell tower with the construction of the Mother Church; built in several phases for structural failure, it is divided into three orders and surmounted by an eighteenth-century clock. From the top you can see the roofs of the village and the countryside.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Gangi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Chiesa Madre di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The seventeenth-century cathedral is embedded in the clock tower, wanted by the Knights Gerosolimitani: it preserves the great canvas of the Last Judgment (1629) by Giuseppe Salerno, the Zoppo di Gangi, inspired by the Judgment of Michelangelo in the Sistine.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Gangi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Palazzo Bongiorno

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in 1756 by the noble family of the same name, it is one of the best examples of 18th century architecture: living stone exterior, rooms with ceilings, stuccoes and trompe-l'oeil decorations by Gaspare Fumagalli and his father-in-law Pietro Martorana.

  • Where Gangi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Musei di Palazzo Sgadari

Museums · City history

The palace houses four museums: the Archaeological Museum with finds of the territory, the Pinacoteca Gianbecchina, the Museum of Arms and the Ethno-anthropological Museum with objects related to bread and pastoral care.

  • Where Gangi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Castello dei Ventimiglia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built in the first half of the 14th century by Francesco I Ventimiglia, it dominates the village from the top and is an example of late medieval architecture, already the residence of the family.

  • Where Gangi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Chiesa di San Cataldo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the first half of the 14th century towards the end of Corso Umberto, with wooden statues of Quattrocchi and paintings of Giuseppe Salerno of 1618.

  • Where Corso Umberto I, Gangi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Corso Umberto, Abbadia, San Paolo e Palazzo Mocciaro

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Along the course you will find the Church of Abbadia, with a living stone façade on three orders and a sumptuous portal, the nineteenth-century Palazzo Mocciaro with frescoed rooms, one of which depicts the Gangi of the past, and, in the square of the same name, the sixteenth-century Church of São Paulo with a seventeenth-century portal with bas-reliefs.

  • Where Corso Umberto I, Gangi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Santuario dello Spirito Santo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Baroque building just outside the village: according to legend a farmer found there the miraculous effigy of the Eternal Father painted on a rock, today kept in a niche.

  • Where Fuori dall'abitato, Gangi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info

Torre Saracena e Convento dei Cappuccini

History and architecture · Towers

On the slopes of Monte Marone, the cylindrical tower bears witness to the Arab domination; next to it stands the Convent of the Capuchins.

  • Entry To be checked
