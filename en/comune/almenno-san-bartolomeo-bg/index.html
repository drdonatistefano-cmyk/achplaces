What to see in Almenno San Bartolomeo

Borgo Bandiera Arancione at the entrance of Valle Imagna, in the Park of Romanico (the ancient Lemine), known for the Romanesque roundabout of San Tomè; in the village the parish with works by Moroni and Vivarini, the Museum of carpenter Tino Sana and the nineteenth-century Fornace Parietti.

Rotonda di San Tomè

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church with circular plan, built between 1130 and 1150 with three overlapping cylinders (nave, tiburium and lantern), perhaps inspired by the Holy Sepulchre of Jerusalem: capitals of reuse all different, fragments of frescoes (Madonna on throne with Child, Annunciation in the Matroneo) and portals bas-relief. At the equinoxes a ray of sun crosses the roundabout and hits the tabernacle; the former monastery next door houses the European Romanesque Antenna study centre.

Visit Bergamo indicates the church closed for restoration, without reopening date

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Bartolomeo di Tremozia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Consecrated in 1453, parish since 1520, visited by Saint Charles Borromeo in 1562, rebuilt in the eighteenth century and renovated in 1867. He guards the Madonna and Child of Bartolomeo Vivarini (1485), the Mystical Bride of Saint Catherine of Giovan Battista Moroni (1567-70), the Education of the Madonna of Piccio (1826) and works of Storer, Coghetti and Temperello.

Museo del Falegname Tino Sana

Museums · Themed

Designed by the entrepreneur Tino Sana and opened since 1987, it occupies 3,500 m2 on three floors: rebuilt shops (chair, inlayer, sculptor, luthier, cage, turning machine), the path from tree to wheel with ploughs, barrels, wagons and boats, a village street with taverns and crafts, a puppet theatre, a collection of bicycles with section on Felice Gimondi, historical motorcycles and a wooden plane of the First World War. Open Mon-Fri 9-12, Saturday 15-18, Sunday 9.30-12 and 15-18; closed in August and on holidays.

Fornace Parietti e Parco della Fornace

Museums · Themed

A nineteenth-century factory where from 1835 to 1960 the Parietti family produced tiles and bricks with the clay of the nearby quarries; restored with contemporary architectural solutions, it preserves the octagonal gallery oven with 16 vaulted rooms. In the Fornace park there is a giant bench accessible to people with motor disabilities, among the first in Europe.
