What to see in Palermo

Sicilian capital in the Golden Conca, with the Arab-Norman UNESCO Heritage itinerary, the baroque of the popular districts and the historical markets of Ballarò and Vucciria.

54 places to visit in Palermo, in the province of Palermo (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo dei Normanni

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The oldest royal residence in Europe, a 9th century Arab fortress enlarged by the Normans, seat of the kings of Sicily and today of the Regional Assembly: Room of Ruggero with hunting mosaics, Room of Hercules, Pompeiana Room and Chinese Room.

  • Where Piazza del Parlamento 1, Palermo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: italia.it · federicosecondo.org

Cappella Palatina

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Built in 1132 for Ruggero II as a private chapel of the royal family, with Byzantine gold mosaics, Islamic muqarnas, porphyry floors and the great Christ Pantocrator in the dome.

  • Where Palazzo dei Normanni, Palermo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it

Cattedrale di Palermo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Built around 1185 for Archbishop Gualtiero Offamilio on a basilica that became a mosque, with domes, towers and blackbirds: the royal tombs of Frederick II, Henry VI and Constantine of Altavilla, the chapel of Santa Rosalia and the treasure with the tiara of Constance.

  • Where Via Vittorio Emanuele, Palermo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it

Piazza Pretoria e Fontana Pretoria

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The fountain was built in Florence in 1554 by the sculptor Francesco Camilliani for the garden of Don Luigi di Toledo and arrived in Palermo in 1574, bought by the Senate; groups of river gods, tritons, nereids and mermaids in Carrara marble. The square is closed by Palazzo Pretorio, Santa Caterina and the Bonocore and Bordonaro palaces.

  • Where Piazza Pretoria, Palermo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
