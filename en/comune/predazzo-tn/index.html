What to see in Predazzo

Country of the Val di Fiemme at the foot of the Latemar, with the Geological Museum of the Dolomites, seat of the MUSE, the museum of the Alpine School of the Guardia di Finanza and, on the road to Passo Rolle, the visitor center of Paneveggio in the Forest of the violins.

6 places to visit in Predazzo, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo geologico delle Dolomiti

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

Location of the MUSE di Trento born in 1899: rocks, minerals and fossils tell with videos, games and installations the geological history of the valley; in summer workshops and excursions to the fossil atoll of Latemar.

  • Where Piazza SS. Filippo e Giacomo 1, Predazzo
  • Entry €5 whole, €4 reduced; free with Trentino Guest Card
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitfiemme.it · visittrentino.info

Museo storico della Scuola alpina della Guardia di Finanza

Museums · City history

Opened in 2013 as a section of the Historical Museum of the Guardia di Finanza: in five sections heirlooms, uniforms, weapons, documents and paintings on the Alpine school founded in Predazzo in 1920, with ski equipment and mountaineering; braille captions and objects to touch.

  • Where Via Fiamme Gialle 8, Predazzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · gdf.gov.it

Centro visitatori di Paneveggio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

In a former sawmill of 1919 near the lake of Forte Buso, tells the Forest of the violins, whose red firs give wood to the lute lute; not far away the deer fence and the path Marcì, without barriers, in the forest.

  • Where SS50 n. 11, Paneveggio, Predazzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website parcopan.org

Sources: parcopan.org · visittrentino.info · italia.it

GeoTrail del Dòs Capèl

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring at the height between Feudo Pass and the Alpe di Pampeago with panels on the rocks: layers of ancient beaches and seabeds, fossils and traces of the ancient volcano of Predazzo; also suitable for families.

  • Where Passo Feudo, Predazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Chiesa dei Santi Filippo e Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Neo-Gothic, late nineteenth century, overlooking the square of the historic center; smaller and older the church of San Nicolò in the area of the cemetery.

  • Where Piazza SS. Filippo e Giacomo, Predazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Biolago di Predazzo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Lake beachable to the Fontanelle, close to the cycle, with purified water for phytopuration without chemical treatments and meadows around.

  • Where Località Fontanelle, Predazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info
