What to see in Codrongianos

Logudoro Country about 20 km from Sassari, born from the castrum Gordianus Roman III century: in the countryside the Camaldolese Basilica of the Holy Trinity of Saccargia, with the white and black facade and the only Romanesque frescoes survived in Sardinia; in the village the churches of São Paulo and Santa Croce and, in the territory, the Nuraghe Nieddu.

Basilica della Santissima Trinità di Saccargia

Places of worship · Basilicas

Built in the first decades of the 12th century by the Camaldolese monks in the plain of Saccargia, about 5 km from the village: 20 m long, 7 m wide and 14, with bichromatic façade in white limestone and black basalt and an imposing bell tower recognizable from afar. The apse preserves rare Romanesque frescoes, the only survivors in Sardinia. The feast of the Most Holy Trinity is celebrated on Sunday after Pentecost. Open every day 9-18 according to Italia.it.

  • Where SS597, Codrongianos
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Paolo e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the upper part of the village (Cotronianu and susu), the parish church of São Paulo dates back to the 12th century and houses paintings from the 17th century; around it the historical centre developed between the 17th and 18th centuries.

  • Where Cotronianu e susu, Codrongianos
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Nuraghe Nieddu

History and architecture · Archaeology

The best preserved nuraghe of the area, of the Bronze Age (II millennium BC): a single 11 m high tower with tholos cover, in dark basalt blocks from which the name \"nieddu" is black. In the territory there are about 60 nuraghi.

  • Where Territorio di Codrongianos
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
