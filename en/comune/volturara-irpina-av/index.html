What to see in Volturara Irpina

Country of the Picentini Mountains at 697 meters, at the foot of Mount Termino, overlooking the Dragon Plain, karst basin whose waters disappear in the swallowery of the Bocca del Dragone; on the hill the ruins of the castle of San Michele, in the village the Ethnographic Museum and the historic center with the Gothic houses.

Piana del Dragone e Bocca del Dragone

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

A basin of 1100 hectares with clayey background closed by the mountains, Site of community importance and geosite: rain and spring water are collected which form the so-called Dragon Lagoon, subject to flooding. The water is swallowed by the Dragon's mouth, a karst hole formed after the earthquake of 1456, capable of absorbing about 900 liters per second and entrance door of a complex underground net; the legend wants the holes to be those left by the three heads of a dragon killed by Prince Jesus. In the summer you walk on the edge of the water; a nature trail leads to the lake. Always accessible.

Museo etnografico della Piana del Dragone

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 1999 by Antonio Marra, it tells the peasant and artisan civilization between the late nineteenth and the sixties of the twentieth century: it rises on the daily life marked by the seasons, religious festivals and work in the fields, with attention to the patriarchal family and the agricultural economy of the Plain. Telephone 0828 982073.

Ruderi del castello di San Michele

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill at 890 meters, a castle perhaps Longobard (mid IX century), of Norman-Svevo plant, rebuilt by the Aragonese nobility; in 1556 Luigi Carafa of Stigliano bought Volturara and added a chapel of San Michele. Ruined by the earthquakes of 1561, 1688 and 1694, the mast remains, a quadrangular court of about 25 by 30 meters and four corner towers up to 12 meters high; the earthquake of 23 November 1980 destroyed much of the church of San Michele Arcangelo, of which remains the skeleton.

Centro storico e chiesa di San Nicola

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village, documented as early as 797 as "Veterale" is known for its Gothic houses; in the main square the church of San Nicola di Bari with the bell tower of 1754. The patron saint is celebrated on the first Sunday of August and 6 December.

Sentiero CAI 101 verso il monte Terminio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Tratto dell'Alta via dei Monti Picentini: 11.5 km from the Piana del Dragone (687 m) in Campolaspierto (1307 m), with 1120 m climb and 520 m descent, EE difficulty, about 6 hours. It passes through the source of Acqua delle Logge (1250 m), the Porta di Annibale, the summit of Monte Termino (1806 m), Colle Lungo and the Rifugio degli Uccelli, between chestnut woods and beech woods.
