What to see in Corciano

Castle of the Perugino countryside with the tower of Porta Santa Maria, Piazza Coragino and the church of Santa Maria Assunta with the altarpiece of Perugino.

4 places to visit in Corciano, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the XIII century near the main square: above the main altar the altarpiece of the Assunta del Perugino (1513) and the gonfalone painted by Benedetto Bonfigli in 1472.

  • Where Corciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Torrione di Porta Santa Maria

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The tower started in 1482 to strengthen the defences of the castle at the entrance of the village.

  • Where Corciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Piazza Coragino e i palazzi civici

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The square with its sixteenth-century well, with the Municipal Palace, the Palace of the Captain of the People and the Palace of the Priors.

  • Where Piazza Coragino, Corciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa-Museo di San Francesco

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

The museum church in Gothic style, with frescoes, among the sights of the village.

  • Entry To be checked
