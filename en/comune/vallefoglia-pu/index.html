What to see in Vallefoglia

Municipality of the lower Foglia valley born from the union of Sant'Angelo in Lizzola and Colbordolo: the hamlet of Montefabbri, among the most beautiful villages in Italy, with the walls and parish church of San Gaudenzio; the castle of Colbordolo, home of the painter Giovanni Santi; the sixteenth-century Mulino di Pontevecchio with the Museo della Mezzadria and the remains of the bridge on the Foglia.

10 places to visit in Vallefoglia, in the province of Pesaro e Urbino (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo di Montefabbri

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small fortified village on a relief clearly visible from the ridge between Vallefoglia and Urbino, documented from 1216: the walls of the XII century cross through the entrance arch, where once there was the drawbridge, surmounted by a Madonna suckling in sandstone of the XV century. It is one of the most beautiful villages in Italy.

  • Where Montefabbri, Vallefoglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.letsmarche.it

Pieve di San Gaudenzio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of perhaps the VII-VIII century dedicated to the first bishop of Rimini, with crypt of the XII century that preserves the relics of Santa Marcellina, arrived from Rome in 1666, and fifteenth-century bell tower of 25 meters. Inside works in white and black scagliola of the late seventeenth century, including the altar post of 1687 with St. Francis of Paola, a marble balustrade of the seventeenth century and the baptismal font obtained from a Roman cyp.

  • Where Montefabbri, Vallefoglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.letsmarche.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · letsmarche.it

Castello di Colbordolo e torre civica

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Castle documented since 1213, with the first stone walls between the end of the thirteenth and early fourteenth centuries, looted and burned in 1446 by the troops of Sigismondo Malatesta; to the ancient nucleus you enter from an arch under the civic tower, already bell tower of the church of San Rocco (1604-1840). In the village, the late 19th century wash-house with a permanent nativity scene and the public oven of about one hundred loaves.

  • Where Via Castello 6, Colbordolo, Vallefoglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.letsmarche.it

Sources: comune.vallefoglia.pu.it · letsmarche.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church perhaps of the XII-XIII century, documented in 1337, consecrated in 1726 and remodelled in neo-Romanic forms in 1855 and 1928, when a fresco of 1421 emerged; it preserves a Madonna with the Child by Claudio Ridolfi (1605), an Immaculate with Saints of G. Pandolfi (1615), a shovel attributed to the Barocci school and the relics of Blessed Publicia.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.letsmarche.it

Parco delle Muse e delle Ninfe e mostra su Giovanni Santi

Museums · Themed

Former cultural center Giovanni Santi, born in 1994 for the sixteenth century anniversary of the death of the painter and became in 2019 a path of reading the landscape, with a permanent exhibition on Giovanni Santi, painter, writer and stage designer of the court of Urbino: reproductions of works such as the Visitation of Fano and St. Jerome of the Vatican Art Gallery and the poem in 23,000 verses about the life of Federico da Montefeltro.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Mulino di Pontevecchio e Museo della Mezzadria

Museums · Themed

Mill on the Foglia known since the sixteenth century, belonged to the Dukes of Urbino and then to the counts Paciotti, who for five centuries macinò wheat and from the twentieth century produced electricity; there were wounded in 1862 two brigands of the Banda Grossi. Recovered since 2002, it hosts a new hydroelectric power station and the Mezzadria Museum since 2018: thirteen rooms of objects of rural culture donated by Adriano Campanari, from the kitchen to the cellar with 18th century press, a 1888 birock and historical turbines. Open Tuesday 15-18 and Saturday 9-12.

  • Where Via Pontevecchio, Vallefoglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.letsmarche.it

Sources: comune.vallefoglia.pu.it · letsmarche.it

Ponte di Pontevecchio sul Foglia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Of the four-arched bridge, about 70 meters long and over 20 meters high, dated from some sources to the Roman age and from others to the sixteenth century, remains a single arch after the destruction by the Germans in 1944; the river forms small waterfalls on a natural dam of limestone. Here a Flaminia diverticulum between Pisaurum, Urvinum Metaurense and Pitinium was released.

  • Where Via Pontevecchio, Vallefoglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.letsmarche.it

Sources: comune.vallefoglia.pu.it · letsmarche.it

Monte di Colbordolo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Colle of about 400 meters 1 km from the castle towards Montefabbri, picnic destination and walks with playground and nature trail; at the crossroads with Via Vittorio Veneto the small neoclassical church of the Madonna del Monte.

Sant'Angelo in Lizzola

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The capital lies on the hills to the right of the lower Foglia valley and takes its name from the family of the Liciola, whose fortified residence dominated the village in the 13th century on a castle of older origin; Giovanni Branca (1571-1645), architect and mathematician, creator of the first water steam machine was born there.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
