What to see in Montecarlo

Village walled on the hill of Cerruglio, among the vineyards of the Wine Route of the Lucca Hills and Montecarlo.

3 places to visit in Montecarlo, in the province of Lucca (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Montecarlo in the guide with map and filters

Fortezza del Cerruglio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The 12th century castle that dominates the village, contested between Lucca, Pisa and Florence: from here Castruccio Castracani led the troops to the victory of Altopascio in 1325.

  • Where Montecarlo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Teatro dei Rassicurati

History and architecture · Historic theatres

One of the smallest theatres in Europe, founded in 1795: oval hall with 22 stages and about 200 seats; Puccini and Ungaretti attended.

  • Where Via Carmignani 14, Montecarlo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Collegiata di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1332-1334 and renovated around 1783, with the chapel of the Madonna del Soccorso, a source of the sixteenth century, a Saint Anthony Abbot of Francesco di Valdambrino, the frescoed crypt and the organ of 1742.

  • Where Piazza Francesco Carrara 6, Montecarlo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
