What to see in Clauzetto

Country of the Carnic Prealps overlooking the Balcony of Friuli, with the parish church of San Giacomo, a pilgrimage destination, and on the Pradis plateau the karst caves with the Orrido and the Grotta Museum dedicated to prehistory.

4 places to visit in Clauzetto, in the province of Pordenone (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Grotte di Pradis e Orrido

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the Pradis plateau, more than 500 meters in the heart of the Carnic Alps, hundreds of karst cavities; the visit includes the Cave of the Madonna, the Orrido with waterfalls, natural arches and caves, and a ring walk over the gorge. Free for children under 6 years old, people with disabilities and residents; guided tours for groups by reservation (€10-12).

seasonal opening from the end of March to the beginning of November, in low season only some weekends (common calendar)

  • Where Via Pradis di Sotto, Clauzetto
  • Entry €6, reduced €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.grottedipradis.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: grottedipradis.it · italia.it

Museo della Grotta

Museums · Archaeological

It exhibits the prehistoric finds of the excavations of Pradis and testimonies of life on the plateau in the bronze and Roman era; in the territory the Cave of Clusantin and the Cave of Rio Secco document the passage of the last Neanderthal hunters. Open at the same time as the caves.

Parrocchiale di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Built between the 13th and 14th centuries on the Balcony of Friuli, with views of the hamlet Triviât and the plain of Tagliamento; Baroque interior with main altar and lateral altars attributed to the Peschiutta family of Gemona and to local artists. It is a place of pilgrimage with the feasts of Perdon Grant and the Little Perdon.

  • Where Clauzetto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.grottedipradis.it

Sources: grottedipradis.it · italia.it

Cimitero di guerra in Val da Ros

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

He remembers the battle of Pradis and honors the Italian and German soldiers who fell into the battle.
