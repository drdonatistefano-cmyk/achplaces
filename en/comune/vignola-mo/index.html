What to see in Vignola

The agricultural and industrial centre of the Panaro valley known for its cherries, Vignola has its heart in the medieval fortress with three towers with the frescoed chapel of the Contrari and, in front, in the Palazzo Barozzi with the famous spiral staircase; complete the visit the civic museum of paleontology and mineralogy, the Renaissance oratory of Santa Maria Rotonda and the paths along the river.

Rocca di Vignola

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress of medieval origin (the sources date back to the 8th century, at the behest of the abbot of Nonantola; first cited in 936), passed to the Estensi in 1336 and infeudated in 1401 to Uguccione Contrari, who transformed it into a residence; from 1507 to the Boncompagni Ludovisi until the mid-Twentieth century, since 1998 it is of the Foundation of Vignola. Square plan with three towers, drawbridge and moat; climb the towers for the view of the village and the countryside. In the Chapel of the Contrari late-Gothic frescoes with the Stories of Christ, by an anonymous Ferrara painter from the fifteenth century. Open Friday, Saturday and Sunday 9:30-13:30 and 15:00-18:30 (last entry one hour before), tickets on site.

  • Where Piazza dei Contrari, Vignola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.roccadivignola.it

Sources: roccadivignola.it · visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Palazzo Contrari-Boncompagni (Palazzo Barozzi) e la scala a chiocciola

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Renaissance Palace of the XVI-XVII centuries in front of the Fortress, perhaps on a project by Jacopo Barozzi called the Vignola. Celebrate the spiral staircase with oval plan, self-supporting, 106 steps, attributed to Barozzi and built by Bartolomeo Tristano.

  • Where Piazza dei Contrari, Vignola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo civico «Augusta Redorici Roffi»

Museums · Science and university · Free entry

Founded in 1978 and managed since 1995 by the association of volunteers ÂAL Palèsi Â, it has a paleontological path and a geomineral path, plus a short path of paleoanthropology. Among the finds mollusk and pliocenic crabs of Vignola and Marano, a rare jaw of tapir, remnants of cetacean, shark teeth, parts of ichthyosaur and Cretae ammonites of the Apennines, the Crespellani collection, rocks and minerals between Reno and Secchia with septari and ... Open on Sunday 10:30-12:30; on weekdays by telephone reservation, guided tours also to the fossiliferous outing.
