What to see in Allumiere

The village of Monti della Tolfa was born from the quarries of alunite discovered around 1460 by Giovanni da Castro and closed in 1941: the Klitsche de la Grange Museum in the Palace of the Apostolic Chamber, the Way of Miners and the ghost village of Farnesiana, next to the Faggeto, one of the oldest woods of Lazio, and the Trajan Aqueduct of 107 AD.

7 places to visit in Allumiere, in the province of Rome (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Klitsche de la Grange (Palazzo della Camera Apostolica)

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Archaeological, naturalistic and mining museum founded in 1956 in the Palace of the Reverend Apostolic Chamber (1580). With original finds, plastic and reconstructions in four sections (paleontological, archaeological, geominerary and naturalistic) tells the Tolfa Mountains from prehistory to the Renaissance and the extraction of the alum, used in tannery, dyeing and papermaking, from which the village takes its name; the quarries, to whose undertaking the Park attributes the European primacy of capitalist organization in the sixteenth century, were painted by Pietro da Cortona (painting to the Capitolini Museums). Open Tuesday-Saturday 9-13.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica 29, Allumiere
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · retemusei.regione.lazio.it · parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com

Monumento Naturale Faggeto di Allumiere

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Beech woods and clearings where they graze free Maremma cattle and horses, indicated as one of the oldest woods of Lazio, with trees of over five hundred years; the Natural Monument is managed by the Municipality of Allumiere.

  • Where Monti della Tolfa, Allumiere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it

Cave di alunite e Cammino dei Minatori

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

For more than three centuries, the alunite was excavated in open air, leaving crevices, craters and vertical walls over 50 meters tall similar to canyon; the invention of the artificial alum at the end of the eighteenth century led to the definitive closure in 1941. The CAI has traced the Miner's Way, a network of rings between old quarries and chestnut and beech woods.

  • Where Dintorni di Allumiere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it

Acquedotto Traianeo Innocenziano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Built in 107 A.D. in the Trajan age to bring water to the port of Centumcellae then under construction, it is located just outside the village surrounded by greenery; VisitLazio indicates it as the stage not to be missed.

  • Where Appena fuori dal centro, Allumiere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Borgo della Farnesiana

History and architecture · Abandoned villages

Ghost village on the northern slopes of the Tolfa Mountains, built by Giovanni da Castro for miners; since 1590 the Farnesian Jesuits settled there and in the mid-nineteenth century the neo-Gothic church of Santa Maria della Farnesiana was built. Today it belongs to a farm but is open to visitors.

  • Where La Farnesiana, Allumiere
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Santa Maria Assunta in Cielo e Santuario della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

On the main square are the church of Santa Maria Assunta in Cielo, dedicated to the patroness, and the Sanctuary of Our Lady of Graces.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica, Allumiere
  • Entry Free

Palio delle Contrade e pane giallo

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry

On Sunday after 15 August six districts (Burò, Ghetto, La Bianca, Nona, Polveriera, S. Antonio) run a run of donkeys, born in 1965 and according to the tradition designed in the first sixteenth century by Agostino Chigi on the Sienese model, with procession in costumes of the sixteenth century, Carroccio towed by oxen and flagners. The protagonist of the festival is the yellow bread of Allumiere, with durum wheat semolina ground twice and black truffle of the Monti della Tolfa; in June there is Asinitaly, national fair of the donkey.

  • Where Allumiere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
