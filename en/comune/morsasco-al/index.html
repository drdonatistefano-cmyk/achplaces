What to see in Morsasco

The hilly village of the Acquese is dominated by the castle, enlarged by the Malaspina, the counts of Lodron and the Genoese princes Centurione, with views of the Bormida valley from Monte Rosa to the Ligurian Apennines. Next, in Piazza Castello, the sixteenth-century parish church of San Bartolomeo; outside the center the country church of San Pasquale.

Castello di Morsasco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Manor in dominant position over the Bormida valley, born smaller and enlarged by the Malaspina in the fifteenth century, by the counts of Lodron in the sixteenth and by the Genoese princes Centurione in the early eighteenth century; drawbridge and moat were replaced in 1740 by a stone bridge. Inside a spacious living room once used for the ballcord, the castle apartments, two frescoed rooms with ancient fireplaces and prisons with graffiti and inscriptions; on the southeast tower the coat of arms of the Scotto Centurion, on the corners of the tower the heads of lion of the Lodrone. It is privately owned: the tours are organized and announced on the site of the castle, which also offers visits on request to the contemporary collection (tel. +39 334 376 9833).

Parrocchiale di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the sixteenth century, already mentioned in the pastoral visit of Mgr. Regazzoni of 1577, with a single nave decorated in the last century by the ponzonese painter Ivaldi called "il Muto" . It has a valuable 17th century high altar and side altars with 17th century paintings, including those of San Bovo and the Holy Rosary of Monevi; in the ancient sacristy a seventeenth-century canvas attributed to Beccaria. The outdoor porch was wanted in the eighteenth century by the Delfini family. Visitable during religious services.

Chiesa campestre di San Pasquale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church dedicated to Santa Maria, San Pasquale and Sant'Isidoro, patron saint of the peasants, probably built between the end of the seventeenth and early eighteenth centuries, perhaps on the remains of a more ancient church of Santa Maria: in 1699 it was very ruined, in 1714 it was well provided. From 1831 she was raised next to the cemetery, moved in 1944 to San Vittore. A painting behind the altar shows the original church and the ancient castle of Morsasco. Visitable during religious services.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: comune.morsasco.al.it

Piazza Vittorio Emanuele, campanile e casa del Boia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the square stands the bell tower with clock of 1697. Nearby is the House of the Boy, a memory of the criminal jurisdiction, also capital, which the Centurions, princes of the Holy Roman Empire since 1654, exercised over the subjects of Morsasco.

  • Entry Free
