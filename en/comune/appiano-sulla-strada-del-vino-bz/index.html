What to see in Appiano sulla strada del vino

The largest wine-growing municipality in South Tyrol (941 hectares of vineyards) and the richest area of castles in the province, with more than one hundred castles, noble residences and ruins: above Missiano Castel d'Appiano with the Romanesque chapel called "Cappella Sistina delle Alpi" (Sistine Chapel of the Alps), Castel Boymont and Castel Moos-Schulthaus, in São Paulo the large parish church called "Duomo di campagna" (country dome) and in the wood of Monticolo two emerald green lakes.

Castel d'Appiano (Hocheppan)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built around 1130 for Count Ulrico II on a rock spur above Missiano, at 636 meters, it is considered one of the most important fortifications in South Tyrol, with views of the Adige Valley, Bolzano and the Dolomites. It has a pentagonal mastic 23 meters high, the "Wolfsgrube" and the "Kreidenturm" , an educational itinerary of 11 panels and the "Conte Egno" room dedicated to Count Egno of Appiano; there is a tavern of the castle and you can try the archery. Season 2026 from 28 March to 8 November, closed on Wednesdays (in June and July also on Thursdays); guided tours of castle and chapel every half hour from 11 to 16.

  • Where Via Hocheppan 16, Missiano, Appiano
  • Entry €6, reduced €5 (over 65, students, disabled, groups), children 6-14 €4, family €16
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.hocheppan.it

Sources: hocheppan.it · musei.provincia.bz.it · eppan.com

Cappella di Castel d'Appiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

An isolated chapel in the courtyard of the castle, consecrated around 1131 and known as the Sistine Chapel of the Alps for the Romanesque frescoes of the 13th century, exceptionally well preserved: stories of Jesus and the Apostles, the foolish Virgins, the Visitation, a hunting scene and the famous menu of canederli. It is the stop of the Romanesque Alpine Road between Chur and Lake Garda; guided tours of the historical and artistic half an hour, on the days of the opening of the castle.

  • Where Castel d'Appiano, Via Hocheppan 16, Missiano, Appiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.hocheppan.it

Sources: hocheppan.it · eppan.com · musei.provincia.bz.it

Castel Moos-Schulthaus

Museums · House museums

Around 1270 there were already a small castle and two farms, then incorporated into the castle of Moos; Moos and Schulthaus had different lords and were renewed in the Three and fifteenth centuries, until the estate passed to the von Schulthaus for marriage. In 1958 he bought it and restored the Bolzan merchant Walther Amonn, whose Foundation (1982) owns it; museum since 1983. Buildings of the 16th and 17th centuries with unpainted walls, a tower on the northeast corner, wall paintings, a Gothic-style stube, vintage tools, an exhibition room and the 14th century chapel, of which since 1988 a room has returned to its original use. Open from Tuesday to Sunday, guided tours by reservation, usually between 11 and 16 (info@moos-schulthaus.it, 0471 053906).

  • Where San Michele, Appiano (sopra la chiesa di Santa Caterina)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.eppan.com

Sources: eppan.com

Castel Boymont

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built in the 13th century by the counts of Appiano as a residential castle, today it is a restored ruin; the mast can be climbed and offers views of the Oltradige, to Bolzano and the Adige valley. There is a tavern of the castle; you reach it on foot, last stretch on steep asphalt road.

  • Where Sopra Missiano, Appiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.eppan.com

Sources: eppan.com · suedtirol.info

Triangolo dei castelli

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of 5.1 km (373 m of difference in altitude, about 1 hour and 45) that from the center follows path 12 towards Missiano, passes the Gothic tower of São Paulo and Castel Korb (of Roman origin, today hotel), climbs to Castel d'Appiano and, after 60 meters of metal steps a gorge in the porphyry, reaches the ruins of Castel Boymont; return between orchards and vineyards with views of Bolzano and the Dolomites.

  • Where Centro di San Paolo o Castel Korb, Missiano, Appiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.eppan.com

Sources: suedtirol.info · eppan.com

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The country dome, dedicated to the Conversion of St. Paul, is one of the most important sacred buildings in South Tyrol: the parish, one of the richest in the province since about 1147, built a church oversized in over 250 years, German late dogotics in the base and in the central and baroque Italian body in the onion dome. The bell tower 86 meters high has nine bells, including the second largest in South Tyrol (3.8 tons, merged from Grassmayr to Innsbruck); the interior is a three-nave hall church with a major altar and two side of Michael Stolz, noble coats of arms, busts of the apostles and a Madonna with the Child of the fifteenth century. Guided tour once a week and audio guide.

  • Where Centro di San Paolo, Appiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.eppan.com

Sources: eppan.com

Chiesa del Calvario (Gleif)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Funeral church on the hill behind San Michele, begun in 1716, with two small dome towers, cross vaults and stuccoes; it closes the Via Crucis of Appiano, created by the friars between the Seven and the nineteenth century on the model of the first Calvary of Bolzano, whose last figures were completed in the nineteenth century. About 25 minutes walk from the center of San Michele, with views over the whole Oltradige.

Deviant path via Pigenò for work at the ice stage

  • Where Collina del Gleif, San Michele, Appiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.eppan.com

Sources: eppan.com

Laghi di Monticolo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers

Two emerald green lakes, large and small, in the thick woods of Monticolo near San Michele, in a protected landscape area of 100 hectares frequented by hikers, knights and bathers; from the large lake stands among the reeds the "Seeschlössl" . A signposted path runs around both lakes starting from the parking lot.

Buche di ghiaccio

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

At the foot of Mount Ghent, between Appiano and Caldaro, a basin about 200 meters long and 50 meters wide in a cluster of porphyry debris, which remains fresh even in summer. You can walk from San Michele to Pigenò, Castel Gandegg and the hotel Stroblhof.

Residenze nobiliari di Appiano

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

The territory has over one hundred castles, palaces, noble residences, farms and ruins, mostly of the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries. A 7.4 km ring (about 3 hours, suitable for strollers) starts from the square of the municipality of San Michele and touches the church of Santa Giustina, the residence Melag, Castel Valentin, the complex of Castel Freudenstein, Castel Moos-Schulthaus, the church of San Sebastiano and the castles Englar and Gandegg, still inhabited, with views of the Sciliar, the Alpe di Siusi and the Latemar.

  • Where Piazza del Municipio, San Michele, Appiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.eppan.com

Sources: suedtirol.info · eppan.com

Strada del vino ad Appiano

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

Appiano is the largest wine-growing municipality in South Tyrol, with 941 hectares of vineyards and about 300 days of sunshine a year, along the South Tyrolean Wine Road; in autumn the tradition of Törggelen is renewed and every year Spatium Pinot Blanc is held, dedicated to the European white Pinot.
