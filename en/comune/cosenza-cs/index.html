What to see in Cosenza

The Athens of Calabria at the confluence of Crati and Busento: the old town on the Pancrazio hill with the Duomo, the Norman-Svevo Castle, Palazzo Arnone and the Museo dei Brettii, and at the bottom the new city with the Museo al fresco Bilotti along Corso Mazzini and the Calatrava bridge.

14 places to visit in Cosenza, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cosenza Vecchia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient city on the Pancrazio hill, along the banks of Crati, with narrow and winding alleys; since the last decades of the nineteenth century the new city has developed in the plain below.

  • Where Centro storico, Cosenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Duomo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Declared by UNESCO in 2011 "Patrimony witness of a culture of peace" dates probably back to the middle of the 11th century: tripartite façade with Cistercian Gothic rosettes, the chapel of the Madonna del Pilerio, the funeral monument of Isabella d'Aragona and a wooden crucifix of the fifteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Cosenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Diocesano

Museums · Old masters

Between the Archbishop's Palace and the Cathedral: the Stauroteca, a 12th century gold reliquary cross linked by the tradition to Frederick II and the consecration of the Cathedral in 1222, a polyptych of the Annunciation of 1545, ancient glasses and paintings by Luca Giordano and Andrea Vaccaro.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Cosenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello normanno-svevo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the hill Pancrazio, probably built by the Saracens on the remains of a brute fortress, restored by Ruggero II in 1130 and restored by Frederick II, who in 1239 added the octagonal tower; you can visit the Angevin corridor, the arms room, the throne room and the nineteenth-century bastions.

  • Where Colle Pancrazio, Cosenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

MAB - Museo all'aperto Bilotti

Museums · Modern art · Free entry · Viewpoint

Outdoor art gallery along Corso Mazzini, set up in 2006 and commissioned by the Bilotti family: sculptures by Salvador Dalí (San Giorgio and the dragon), Giorgio De Chirico, Giacomo Manzù, Mimmo Rotella, Emilio Greco, Pietro Consagra, Sasha Sosno and a Head of Cariatide by Modigliani.

  • Where Corso Mazzini, Cosenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Teatro Alfonso Rendano

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Designed by architect Zumpano, built from 1887 and inaugurated in 1909 with Aida: about 800 seats on three orders of stages and the historic curtain of Paolo Vetri with the arrival in the city of Louis III of Anjou and Margaret of Savoy.

  • Where Piazza XV Marzo, Cosenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · calabriafilmcommission.it

Galleria nazionale di Cosenza - Palazzo Arnone

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the sixteenth century Palazzo Arnone, former prison of the city: Italian art from the sixteenth to the twentieth century with attention to the South, with works by Mattia Preti, Sebastiano Conca, Corrado Giaquinto, Luca Giordano and Umberto Boccioni.

  • Where Via Gian Vincenzo Gravina, Cosenza
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo dei Brettii e degli Enotri

Museums · Archaeological

Since 2009 in the 15th century complex of Sant'Agostino: finds from prehistoric times to the Enotri and Brettii, which in 356 BC had in Cosenza their center, accessories of the necropolis of Torre Mordillo and relics of the Risorgimento linked to the Bandiera brothers.

  • Where Via Sant'Agostino, Cosenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Ponte di Calatrava

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Stralled bridge of Santiago Calatrava sul Crati, with a single 104-metre-high inclined pylon, dedicated to St. Francis of Paola; the shape recalls a harp.

  • Where Cosenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Complesso di San Domenico e BoCs Museum

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Church built between 1441 and 1468 at the behest of the Sanseverino family; in the convent restored by the Municipality the BoCs Museum, opened in 2017 with the works of contemporary art of the artistic residences BoCs Art.

  • Where Piazza Tommaso Campanella, Cosenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Galleria d'arte provinciale Santa Chiara

Museums · Modern art

In the monastic complex of Santa Chiara, next to Corso Telesio: rooms for temporary exhibitions under the dome of the ancient church and the Library of the Province.

  • Where Corso Bernardino Telesio, Cosenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Planetario Giovan Battista Amico

Museums · Science and university

Museum of Science and Planetary with a large dome, entitled to the astronomer Cosentino of the sixteenth century Giovan Battista Amico.

  • Where Cosenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Piazza XV Marzo e Villa Vecchia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The square with the statue of the philosopher Bernardino Telesio, the Teatro Rendano and the Palazzo del Governo, next to the historic garden of the Villa Vecchia.

  • Where Piazza XV Marzo, Cosenza

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
