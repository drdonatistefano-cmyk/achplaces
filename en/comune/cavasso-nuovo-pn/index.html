Museo dell'Emigrazione (Museo della vita contadina «Diogene Penzi», sezione Lavoro ed emigrazione)

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Inaugurated on 16 September 2000 in Palazzo Polcenigo-Fanna, it gathers about 400 photographs, documents and objects in twelve sections on the emigration of workers in western Friuli, the "Pal Mont": flows and destinations, crafts such as mosaicists and terraces, work abroad and life of communities emigrated, with a path curated by Javier P. You're big. A room is dedicated to the drawing school of Cavasso with elaborates by master Ernesto Calligaro. Managed by ERPAC; open Thursday and Friday 10-13, Saturday and Sunday 10-13 and 15-17.

Palazzo Polcenigo-Fanna («Palazat»)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Built between 1562 and 1594 by the Counts Fantusio and Antonio di Polcenigo Fanna, who left the castle of Mizza for the plain, takes up the patterns of the castle with corner towers, central tower and rustic around portals and windows. It was the seat of the local jurisdiction until the eighteenth century, plundered by Napoleonic troops in 1797; traces of the facade frescoes remain, while the cycle of Antonio Basoli is lost. Restored after the earthquake of 1976, since 2000 it is the seat of the Municipality and the museum.

  • Where Piazza del Plebiscito, Cavasso Nuovo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.cavassonuovo.pn.it

Sources: patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it · comune.cavassonuovo.pn.it

Orgnese, campi della cipolla rossa e chiesa di San Leonardo

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the countryside of the village of Orgnese is cultivated the red onion of Cavasso Nuovo, which the cycle path Pedemontana signals as a DOP product to buy in the village; among the fields you can enjoy the view of hills and mountains and stands the church of San Leonardo, of 1544, dedicated to the patron saint of prisoners and pregnant women.

  • Where Piazza di Orgnese, Cavasso Nuovo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.cavassonuovo.pn.it

Sources: ecomuseolisaganis.it · italia.it · comune.cavassonuovo.pn.it
