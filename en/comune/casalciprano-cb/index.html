What to see in Casalciprano

Village on the Molise hills of Lombard origin, with a historic center remained intact and set up as an open-air museum of Molise peasant memory, between murals and bronze statues. Outside the village the Badiale Sanctuary of Our Lady of the Annunciation and woods with views of Montago and Matese.

Museo a cielo aperto della Memoria Contadina

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Route through streets, stretches and ancient houses of the historic center, with works that combine objects of rural tradition with contemporary sculptures and paintings. Realized by the Municipality and managed by the Cultural Association Arx Prandi, it was set up in 2004 and enriched in 2010 with the painted Muri and in 2011 with the "Sofitta dei Ricordi" (Ricordi's Attic).

  • Where Centro storico, Casalciprano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: visitmolise.eu

Murales «Casalciprano wall drawings»

Museums · Modern art

Project realized in 2010 and among the first paintings in Molise: 8 large murals, 2 collectives and 6 individuals, of internationally renowned artists under the artistic direction of Lorenzo Canova. In 2011 two murals by Alessia Cerio were added dedicated to the grape harvest and pressing, in a corner of the village with arches and stone steps.

  • Where Centro storico, Casalciprano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: visitmolise.eu

Santuario Badiale della Madonna dell'Annunziata

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Sacred place about 3 km from the town, which over the centuries has attracted religious and noble. It is surrounded by a park suitable for relaxation and sport.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Boschi e sentieri di Casalciprano

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint
