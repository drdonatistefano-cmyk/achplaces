What to see in Casteldelfino

An agricultural and holiday country of the high Varaita Valley, at the confluence of the valleys of Chianale and Bellino, which takes its name from Castrum Delphini built in 1330 by the last Dolphin Umberto II; it is the gate of the Bosco dell'Alevé, the largest Decemberta of the Alps, with its center visit, the ethnographic museum and the open-air museum of the "Santi del popolo" (Saints of the People).

Centro visita Alevé

Museums · Science and university

Dedicated to the nearby Bosco dell'Alevé, it reproduces in real scale a stretch of December in a single large diorama that can be walked along a path painted between the pine trees and a rocky wall typical of the highest altitudes. Among the vegetation the animals of the forest, dead specimens due to accidental causes (investments, power lines, windows) and prepared for popularity. Managed by the Monviso Park.

Seasonal closure: the Parco del Monviso indicates reopening on July 15th 2027

  • Where Via Roma 38, Casteldelfino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.parcomonviso.eu

Sources: parcomonviso.eu · visitcuneese.it

Museo etnografico «Ier a la vilo»

Museums · Themed

It collects traditional work tools, the reconstruction of an ancient kitchen and the façade of a house; among the objects the portal of the parish, lace tombolo and traditional costumes. Open on request.

  • Where Via Circonvallazione, Casermette, Casteldelfino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Bosco dell'Alevé

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest pine forest in the Alps, a base of excursions and walks starting from Casteldelfino.

  • Where Casteldelfino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · parcomonviso.eu

Museo en plein air «I santi del popolo»

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Open-air museum inaugurated on 22 July 2012 in the presence of the bishop of Saluzzo, with free access.

  • Where Piazza Dao Bernardo 2, Casteldelfino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.casteldelfino.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Cappella di Sant'Eusebio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque chapel with bell tower of the fourteenth century, restored with important conservation works; it recalls Villa Sant'Eusebio, name of the village from the 10th century.

  • Where Casteldelfino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it
