What to see in Montichiari

Art city of the Brescia plain: the neo-Gothic Bonoris Castle on the hill of San Pancrazio, the Romanesque parish church with the San Pancrazio del Romanino, the eighteenth-century Duomo with its Last Supper, the Lechi Museum with seven paintings by Giacomo Ceruti and a system of seven civic museums.

9 places to visit in Montichiari, in the province of Brescia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Bonoris

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the hill of San Pancrazio, where a fortified building has been documented since 1107, the owner Gaetano Bonoris rebuilt his residence in neo-Gothic style since 1890, on the model of the medieval village of Turin of 1884 (inspired by the castles of Fénis and Issogne): project by Antonio Tagliaferri, completed with Carlo Melchiotti; frescoes by Giuseppe Rollini (1898-1900) in the chapel and baronale salon, furnishings by the Arboletti brothers, park redesigned in 1901 by Giuseppe Roda. Municipal since 1996. Open from April to October with guided tours on weekends; admission fee.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.montichiari.it

Sources: montichiari.it · visitbrescia.it

Pieve di San Pancrazio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the 12th century, among the best preserved Romanesque monuments of Lombardy, on the homonymous hill reachable on foot from the center with a staircase in the green; it preserves a fresco of San Pancrazio del Romanino (about 1527) and works by Paolo Caylina and Callisto Piazza. Free admission on Sunday afternoon in summer.

  • Where Colle di San Pancrazio, Montichiari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrescia.it

Sources: montichiari.it · visitbrescia.it

Duomo di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

18th century (first stone in 1729), with monumental façade, dome of 18 meters of diameter and floor in white Carrara marble and Bardiglio; at the altar of the Blessed Sacrament the Last Romanino Supper (about 1542). Free admission every day.

  • Where Centro storico, Montichiari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrescia.it

Sources: montichiari.it · visitbrescia.it · duc.montichiari.it

Museo Lechi

Museums · Old masters

Born from the donation of the notary Luigi Lechi to the Municipality (2005): 185 paintings, drawings and prints, a hundred porcelains and a library of 1,500 volumes of art history, with the Lombard school from the sixteenth to the eighteenth century and the painting of reality in the center. Among the works the Moretto and seven paintings by Giacomo Ceruti, the Pitocchetto, including ...The woman who makes the stocking (about 1730). More than 1,500 m2 on two floors in the Tabarino Palace, already town hall. Open from Wednesday to Sunday; admission fee.

  • Where Palazzo Tabarino, Corso Martiri della Libertà 33, Montichiari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrescia.it

Sources: montichiari.it · visitbrescia.it

Museo archeologico PAST – Palazzo dell'Archeologia e della Storia del Territorio

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Museum \"identitario" of about 800 m2 with research materials in the municipal territory and in the Brescia plain, including more than 120 casts of finds emerged from the excavations. Open Saturday and Sunday; free of charge up to 12 years old.

  • Where Piazza Santa Maria 34, Montichiari
  • Entry 5 €
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrescia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · duc.montichiari.it · visitbrescia.it

Pinacoteca Laura e Antonio Pasinetti

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

In the nineteenth century, the former civil hospital, together with the Treccani library: more than 400 paintings, sketches, letters, prints and drawings by the portrait artist Antonio Pasinetti (1863-1940), donated in 2006 by his nephew Laura. Free admission.

  • Where Ex ospedale civile, Montichiari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrescia.it

Sources: montichiari.it · cultura.gov.it · visitbrescia.it

Museo demologico Giacomo Bergomi

Museums · Themed · Free entry

About 6,000 traditional objects from the Alpine valleys and the Brescia plain collected by the painter Giacomo Bergomi: agriculture, breeding, crafts, domestic work and rituals of the cycle of the year and life, with gallery for temporary exhibitions and library. Free admission; closed in August.

  • Where Centro Fiera del Garda, Montichiari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrescia.it

Sources: montichiari.it · cultura.gov.it · visitbrescia.it

Museo storico del Risorgimento «Agostino Bianchi» – Museo delle armi

Museums · City history

Inaugurated in 1934 and re-arranged in 1983 in the former choir of the church of Santa Maria del Suffragio: documents, weapons, flags, photographs and relics from the battle of Castiglione (1796) to the Risorgimento and the world wars, including a bolt of the engine of the plane of Francesco Baracca. Open only on Sunday afternoon from April to early October.

  • Where Piazzetta del Teatro, Montichiari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrescia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · duc.montichiari.it · visitbrescia.it

Teatro Sociale Gaetano Bonoris

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Opened in 1774 and expanded in 1890 (re-opened with the Verdi Finder), reopened in 2003 after the restorations: 375 seats with U stage, two rows of stages and gallery; season of prose, opera, dance and dialect theatre.

  • Entry To be checked
