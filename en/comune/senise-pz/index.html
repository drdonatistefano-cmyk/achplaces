What to see in Senise

A small town on the edge of the Pollino National Park, known for its peperone di Senise; next to the village the Senise dam forms the lake of Monte Cotugno, the largest artificial lake on clay in Europe. In the historic centre, next to the Mother Church, the Ethnographic Museum tells about the peasant civilization of the Senese.

Museo etnografico del senisese

Museums · Themed

Opened in the summer of 2003, it collects materials gathered in about twenty years by the restorer Antonio Guerriero on the peasant life of the Senish: agricultural tools, weaving tools, vintage clothes and household objects. It occupies rooms of a noble palace of the fifteenth century in the historical center, next to the Mother Church; other rooms house sacred furnishings, a permanent nativity scene set in Senise and a projection room with vintage photos, and nearby are rebuilt a peasant house and a shoemaker's workshop.

MiC card indicates closed every day; contact the museum (0973 686576) before visiting

Lago di Monte Cotugno e Osservatorio

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Formed by the Senise dam, Europe's largest clay dam, it is referred to as the largest artificial lake on clay in Europe and the third in the world. The shores alternate Mediterranean maquis and forests of oaks and pine trees; on a promontory of the lake, in the Pollino National Park, stands an observatory for the sighting of numerous species of sedentary and migratory birds. On the lake you can practice rowing, canoeing and sailing, in the surroundings trekking, horse riding, climbing and mountain biking.
