What to see in Vimercate

Città della Brianza orientale con il Ponte di San Rocco, unico ponte romano sovrivato in Lombardia (III century A.D.), la Collegiata di Santo Stefano, Palazzo Trotti con i suoi saloni affrescati e Villa Sottocasa, sede del MUST - Museo del territorio; in the hamlet of Oreno there are Villa Gallarati Scotti and the Borromeo hunting casino.

MUST - Museo del territorio vimercatese

Museums · City history

Civic museum opened in 2010 in Villa Sottocasa, tells the history, art, economy and society of the Brianza est, between Lambro and Adda, in 14 rooms on two floors (about 1000 square meters): on the ground floor a chronological path from the first Roman settlements to the nineteenth century, on the first floor industry, transformation of the territory and local memory. It exhibits archaeological finds, paintings, 3D models, multimedia installations, photographs and historical movies; in 2012 it received the ICOM Museum of the Year Award for the best Italian setup. Opening hours: Wednesdays and Thursdays 10-13; Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays 10-13 and 15-19.

Villa Sottocasa e parco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Neoclassical villa built at the end of the eighteenth century in the heart of Vimercate, with nineteenth-century frescoes and an English park of over 6 hectares; today it houses the MUST.

Palazzo Trotti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The seat of the Town Hall since 1862, takes its name from the Trotti, who took over in 1739 from the Secco Borella who gave it the present form; towards the park it has the U pattern of the villas of Lombard delight. The interior has three cycles of mythological frescoes: the rooms of Cleopatra, Semiramide, Hercules and Minerva (1705-1706), the room of Bacchus attributed to Carlo Donelli called the Vimercati and the rooms painted around 1750 by the Ticino Giuseppe Antonio Orelli.

entrance by reservation for organized groups and school groups, with extraordinary openings during the year (tel. 039 6659488)

Ponte di San Rocco

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

The only surviving Roman bridge in Lombardy, dating back to the third century A.D.; the towers were added in the Middle Ages and retain defensive elements such as the slitholes.

  • Where Via Cavour, Vimercate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Collegiata di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The most important church of the Vimercate parish dates back to the 10th century and is a key example of pre-Romanesque architecture in Brianza, with a Romanesque bell tower. The façade dates back to the 16th century, the interior was renovated in 1807 by Leopold Gambark; it preserves the cycle of frescoes ...Stories of Saint Stephen , painted in 1566 by Lattanzio Gambara.

Santuario della Beata Vergine del Rosario

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Designed by Fabio Mangone and Francesco Maria Richino, Milanese architects of the seventeenth century. The Salvatore's chapel, with stuccoes, frescoes and Baroque paintings, and the chapel of St. Catherine, with a altarpiece by Lattanzio Gambara, stand out.

Oratorio di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between the 13th and 14th centuries in the Visconti period, it preserves fifteenth-century frescoes of the Passion of Christ in the international Gothic style and the 17th-century canvas "La Gloria di San Carlo" by the Lambugnani brothers, which shows the Vimercate of the time.
