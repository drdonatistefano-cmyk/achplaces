What to see in Castiglione delle Stiviere

The birthplace of St. Louis Gonzaga, the world's patron saint of youth, and a place where Henry Dunant created the Red Cross after the battle of Solferino in 1859: the heart is Piazza Dallo, with the Basilica of St. Louis, the Aloisian Museum, the International Museum of the Red Cross and the Bondoni Pastorio Museum.

12 places to visit in Castiglione delle Stiviere, in the province of Mantua (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Internazionale della Croce Rossa

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated at Palazzo Longhi, a patrician residence of the mid-eighteenth century, on June 24, 1959, a century after the battle of Solferino: it tells the birth of the Movement designed by Henry Dunant, with wooden beds of the late nineteenth century, motor ambulances of the thirties, surgical field irons, philatelic collections and the original text of "A memory of Solferino" .

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Basilica Santuario di San Luigi Gonzaga

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Sanctuary elevated to minor basilica in 1964 by Paul VI: it preserves the relic of the Saint and is the center of Aloisian devotion and destination of pilgrimages. Open every day 8-12 and 15-18.

  • Where Piazza San Luigi 3, Castiglione delle Stiviere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.castiglione.mn.it

Sources: comune.castiglione.mn.it

Museo Aloisiano e Collegio delle Vergini di Gesù

Museums · Old masters

The museum, opened since 1969 on the initiative of Mgr. Luigi Bosio, is located in the College of the Virgins of Jesus, founded in 1608 by Cinzia, Olympia and Gridonia Gonzaga, nephews of St. Louis, with wrought iron gates of the seventeenth century; he preserves paintings and objects belonging to the Saint and his family. Free entrance with the pilgrim's bag of the Pastoral Unit.

  • Where Via Perati 6, Castiglione delle Stiviere
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.castiglione.mn.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it · lombardiabeniculturali.it · comune.castiglione.mn.it

Casa museo Palazzo Bondoni Pastorio

Museums · House museums

House inhabited by the Pastorio family for almost six centuries, which hosted Henry Dunant in June 1859: the Hall of Landscapes with monochrome views and imaginary Roman ruins, the Hall of the Coat of Arms, the statue of Francesco Gonzaga by Giovanni Antonio Carra (1610-1617), furnishings, archive, library, paintings, prints, laces, toys and clothes. Open Saturday and Sunday 15-18.30.

  • Where Via Marconi 34, Castiglione delle Stiviere
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.castiglione.mn.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it · comune.castiglione.mn.it

Duomo dei Santi Nazario e Celso

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Viewpoint

Built by Giovanni Battista Groppi in 1761 on a panoramic terrace preceded by cypresses: it preserves a Santa Rosalia by Pietro Novelli, a mystical wedding of Saint Catherine of Camillo Procaccini and the tomb of Marta Tana of Santena, mother of Saint Louis.

  • Where Castiglione delle Stiviere
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.castiglione.mn.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it · lombardiabeniculturali.it · comune.castiglione.mn.it

Castello e chiesa di San Sebastiano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the upper part of the village remain the access tower, the walls and the watchtower in stone and brick; inside the walls the church of San Sebastiano wanted by Ferrante Gonzaga. The Fortress was demolished in 1600 and part of the castle destroyed by the French in the war of Spanish succession.

  • Where Castiglione delle Stiviere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.castiglione.mn.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria dei Disciplini

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Oratory founded by the grandfather of St. Louis for the Confraternity of the Disciplines, established at the beginning of the sixteenth century; the portal of the Renaissance façade recalls the sermon on the Eucharist held by the Saint on March 3, 1590. Since 2006 it is the church of Santa Maria dei Disciplini della Pace.

  • Where Castiglione delle Stiviere
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.castiglione.mn.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it · comune.castiglione.mn.it

Convento di Santa Maria e villa romana

History and architecture · Archaeology

In 1584 Luigi retired to pray in a 16th century Gonzaga country house; since 1846 he has been a member of the College of Virgins. It preserves a spring source of Etruscan times and traces of a Roman villa with mosaic with white and black tiles of the I-II century AD and a circular underground room of about 6 meters.

  • Entry To be checked

Galleria d'arte moderna e contemporanea - Palazzo Menghini

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

Location of the info point and the Galleria civica with the permanent exhibition "Sign and identity of a Community." Great artists of the Novecento Castiglionese. Open Friday 15-18, Saturday and Sunday 9-12 and 15-18.

Teatro Sociale

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Born as an extension of Palazzo Principe, designed by Luigi Canonica and inaugurated in 1843, it hosted opera and prose until 1926; reopened in 1990. Visitable during the shows; groups by reservation free of charge.

  • Where Via Teatro, Castiglione delle Stiviere
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Cimitero Monumentale

Places of worship · Monumental cemeteries · Free entry

On the outskirts in the locality of Bottega, it houses works by artists such as Wildt, Brigoni, Rossi and Ferlenga. Open every day 8-17 in winter, 8-19 in summer.

  • Where Via Botteghino 71, Castiglione delle Stiviere

Piazza Dallò e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Piazza Dallo is the heart of the city and in ancient times it had castrum function; in the center there are patrician palaces built between the fifteenth and nineteenth century, including Palazzo Pastore with its park.
