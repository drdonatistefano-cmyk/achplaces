What to see in Carbonia

The city was founded in 1938 for miners of the Sirai-Serbariu basin: the large mine became the Coal Museum, the Phoenician and Carthaginian colony of Mount Sirai and the prehistoric necropolis of the territory.

5 places to visit in Carbonia, in the province of Sud Sardegna (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo del Carbone – Grande Miniera di Serbariu

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The deposit was active from 1937 to 1964, with nine wells and a hundred kilometers of tunnels up to 179 meters deep, recovered and reopened in 2006 as a museum; in the lamp shed, tools, photographs and videointerviews.

  • Where Grande Miniera di Serbariu, Carbonia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.sardegnaturismo.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it · museodelcarbone.it

Parco archeologico di Monte Sirai

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In the north-west suburbs, houses, squares, temple and necropolis of a colony first Phoenician then Carthaginian, preserved in an intact way as in few other places.

  • Where Monte Sirai, Carbonia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it
