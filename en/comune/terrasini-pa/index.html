What to see in Terrasini

Coastal village west of Palermo, probably of late medieval origin, with a historic center of low and white houses along straight roads that go down to the sea; on the seafront Palazzo d'Aumale houses the Regional Museum of Natural History and the permanent exhibition of the Sicilian cart, while to the west the reserve of Cape Rama and Cala Rossa close the coast.

Palazzo d'Aumale, Museo regionale di storia naturale e mostra del carretto siciliano

Museums · Themed · Free entry

The Regional Museum of Natural History, with naturalistic collections and a permanent exhibition dedicated to the Sicilian cart, is located in Palazzo d'Aumale, on the promenade next to Praiola beach. Free entrance to the ground floor; open from Tuesday to Friday 9-19, Saturday and Sunday 9-13, closed on Monday.

  • Where Lungomare Peppino Impastato, Terrasini
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: comune.terrasini.pa.it · visitsicily.info

Riserva naturale orientata Capo Rama

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Reserve of about 57 hectares established in 2000 and managed by WWF Italy: limestone rocks rich in fossils of about 200 million years ago, with three geosites (one of national importance), a cliff of about 35 meters with alofil vegetation and a plateau covered with dwarf palm, the only spontaneous palm of Europe. On the promontory the watchtower, one of the first built on the Sicilian coast, which gives its name to the head and signaled the pirates with fire and smoke; it is the only circular tower of the coast. Suitable for mountain bikes and sunsets.

  • Where Capo Rama, Terrasini
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: orbs.regione.sicilia.it · visitsicily.info

Cala Rossa e Torre Alba

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Cala Rossa takes its name from the reddish stripes of the cliff; in front of the islet it becomes snorkeling. Near the Tower Alba, with a very suggestive view, often hosts exhibitions and exhibitions.

Lungomare e spiaggia della Praiola

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The seafront of Terrasini is suitable to admire the sunset and to run in the morning; the Praiola beach, next to Palazzo d'Aumale, is also recommended for a night swim.

Centro storico, Chiesa Madre e porticciolo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The oldest nucleus has a regular plant of long blocks of low and white houses along straight roads that converge towards the sea. There are the monumental Mother Church of Maria SS. delle Grazie, the eighteenth-century Palazzo La Grua, now Town Hall, and the eighteenth-century Palazzo Cataldi, seat of the municipal library, linked to the princes of Carini. The main square is closed to traffic; at the marina at dawn you buy fish directly from the fishermen.

  • Where Centro storico, Terrasini
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: comune.terrasini.pa.it · visitsicily.info
