What to see in Isca sullo Ionio

The municipality of Soveratese sullo Ionio, with a hilly historical centre born in the IX century to escape the Saracen pirates and the hamlet of Isca Marina, where stretching 4 km of fine sand; in the village the Museum of the instruments of the popular music of Calabria preserves over 60 traditional instruments.

Museo degli strumenti della musica popolare calabrese

Museums · Themed

It is based at the Conservatory of Popular Music in Calabria .Antonio Procopio and is managed by the ARPA, a consortium that studies the folk traditions of Calabria. It exhibits over 60 traditional instruments all working, including aerophones, cords, members and idiophones; library and media library with about 200 hours of audio, 100 of video and over 120,000 photos. Phone 0967 45357.

Centro storico medievale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The hilly village was founded around the ninth century by the inhabitants of ancient Sanagasi, on the coast, fleeing from Saracen raids; it was long in the Barony of Badolato. The earthquake of 11 May 1947 destroyed most of it, but it remains narrow alleys, portals decorated in granite, a substantial stretch of the ancient city walls and several noble palaces. Every seven years there is represented the Tragedy of St. Martial, one of the few surviving hagiographic tragedies.

Spiaggia di Isca Marina

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

About 4 km of fine sandy coast, on whose beaches was recorded the nesting of the sea turtle Caretta caretta; the marina, repopulated stablely after the earthquake of 1947, has grown since the Sixties with seaside tourism.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.iscasulloionio.cz.it

Sources: comune.iscasulloionio.cz.it

Valle Oscura

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Along the river Valle Oscura there are waterfalls and ancient mills, enhanced by the municipality as naturalistic routes, in a mountain hinterland of beech, holm oaks, firs and pine trees.
