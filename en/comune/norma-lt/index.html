Parco archeologico dell'antica città di Norba

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Roccaforte founded at the beginning of the fifth century BC against Volsci (Roman Colony from 491 BC), was taken by Silla in 81 BC and, according to Appiano, burned by the inhabitants themselves; never rebuilt, it is called the "Pompei Republicana dei Lepini" . The polygonal drywalls run for about 2.5 km around 44 hectares, with four gates including the monumental Porta Maggiore; inside paved roads, monumental baths, twelve domus, two acropolis with the basements of the temples (including that of Diana) and the sanctuary of Juno Lucina. Museo civico archeologico «Padre Annibale Gabriele Saggi»

Museums · Archaeological

Virtual Archaeological Museum inaugurated in October 1995 by Regione Lazio e Comune: with photographs, casts, plastics and virtual reality stations reconstructs roads, public and private buildings and daily life of Norba, a Roman city of the Republican age, and exhibits finds from excavations. MiC timetable: Tuesday-Saturday 9-18.

  • Where Via della Liberazione 19-20, Norma
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo del cioccolato

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The first such museum in Italy, in a former chocolate factory: it tells the history of cocoa from Maya to today and the phases of processing from plant to seeds, with advertising prints, vintage packages, scales and ancient chocolate makers and a room that reconstructs a factory with original machinery; children can make their own tablet and the visit ends with a tasting.

  • Where Via Capo dell'Acqua 20, Norma
  • Entry €4.50, reduced €2.50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · visitlazio.com

Chiesa rupestre di Santa Maria sopra Monte Mirteto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Also known as San Michele Arcangelo or Sant'Angelo del Mirteto, it is a karst cave transformed into a church in 1183, in an uninhabited area on the slope that looks at Ninfa; it inspired the abbey of Santa Maria di Monte Mirteto founded by Gregorio IX. On the rock are still frescoes, including a Madonna enthroned between St. Michael and St. Lucia, and a primitive altar; it is curated by volunteers of the association "Opera di Sant'Angelo" in Monte Mirteto.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village preserves the parish church of the Santissima Annunziata, the sanctuary of the Madonna del Rifugio, the church of the Madonna del Carmine (since 1781) in Piazza Roma with the Monument to the Fallen of 1922, the Palazzo Felici attributed to the architect Fontana and the oratory of San Rocco with a surviving Romanesque portal.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Norma
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.norma.lt.it · italia.it

Belvedere sull'Agro Pontino e volo libero

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the cliff the view embraces the coast from Neptune to Terracina, the Pontine islands and the Oasis of Ninfa; from the terraces of Norba take off the paraglidings, in one of the most spectacular launch sites in Europe, overlooking the Pontina Plain, Lepini and Ninfa.

  • Where Norma
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it
