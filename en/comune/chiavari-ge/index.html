What to see in Chiavari

The city of the arcades at the confluence of five valleys: the Straight Carruggio, the cathedral of Our Lady of the Garden, Palazzo Rocca with the civic gallery and the archaeological museum of the necropolis of the Iron Age.

10 places to visit in Chiavari, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e Carruggio Dritto

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Borgolungo with its porticoed streets and nineteenth-century buildings, crossed by the Carruggio Stratto with artisan shops and bars.

  • Where Carruggio Dritto, Chiavari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Cattedrale di Nostra Signora dell'Orto

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built after the apparition of the Virgin to Sebastiano Descalzo, recalled on July 2, on the homonymous square next to the Bishop's Palace.

  • Where Piazza Nostra Signora dell'Orto, Chiavari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
