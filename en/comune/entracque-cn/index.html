Centro Uomini e Lupi

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

The first fauna centre of the Italian Alps entirely dedicated to the wolf, inaugurated in 2010: the center visit to Piazza Giustizia e Libertà and the fauna area in Casermette, home of the Maritime Alps Park.
