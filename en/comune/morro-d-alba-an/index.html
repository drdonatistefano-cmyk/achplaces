What to see in Morro d'Alba

Fortified village between Senigallia and Jesi, surrounded by the Scarpa, the walk of covered round about 300 meters from which you can see from the Sibillini to the Conero; in the basement of the castle the Museum Utensili on the sharecropping culture, and in the vineyards around the Lacrima of Morro d'Alba DOC.

La Scarpa, camminamento di ronda e mura

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Covered walk about 300 meters long, flanked by arches, which runs along the walls, with origins in the first half of the seventeenth century; according to Italia.it is the only Italian fortified village with a round walk along the entire belt. Along the route, panoramic medieval towers are discovered; from the large windows the view goes from the Sibillini to the Conero and the sea.

  • Where Centro storico, Morro d'Alba
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Museo Utensilia

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Museum of the middle-class culture in the basement of the medieval castle: in eight rooms a reasoned collection of traditional work tools, such as ploughs, frames, wagons and presses, that peasant families built in winter turning to the blacksmith only for metal parts.

Palazzo comunale e pala di Claudio Ridolfi

Museums · Old masters

The 18th-century Town Hall, on the main square, houses a gallery with works by Claudio Ridolfi, including the altarpiece of the Coronation of the Virgin and saints dated 1630.
