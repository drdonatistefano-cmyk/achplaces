Pirrera Sant'Antonio (Cava del Barocco)

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Ancient underground quarry of white limestone of the Climati Mountains, of about 2,500 m2, with tunnels over 280 meters long, times up to 27 meters high and large irregular pillars left by the quarrymen, the 'pirriaturi' . Probably dug from the 15th century and especially after the earthquake of 1693, it provided the stone for buildings of Syracuse, Catania, Messina, Genoa and Malta; in July 1943, during Operation Husky, it was the basis of British troops, who left graffiti. Guided tours by reservation.

Museo di storia naturale di Melilli

Museums · Science and university

Multidisciplinary Museum in three sections: archaeological, with marine and terrestrial finds from excavations in the territory; ethno-anthropological, with carts, models of boats and objects of material culture; naturalistic, with a geopaleontological sector and paleontological, malacological, entomological, ornithological and mammal collections. The naturalistic and ethnographic collections belong to the Regional State. Open 9-13.30 and 14-18.45; tel. 0931 552193.
