What to see in Malesco

Borgo Bandiera Arancione della Valle Vigezzo, the gateway to the Val Grande National Park, where the history of the valley is told through the ollar stone.

3 places to visit in Malesco, in the province of Verbano-Cusio-Ossola (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo archeologico della pietra ollare – Museo del Parco nazionale della Val Grande

Museums · Archaeological

In a historic building restored in 2006, it tells the oldest history of Val Vigezzo and Val Grande with local finds, in particular from the Roman and late ancient necropolis of Craveggia.

  • Where Piazza Ettore Romagnoli, Malesco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it · visitossola.it

Ecomuseo della Pietra Ollare e degli Scalpellini

Museums · Themed

Ecomuseum dedicated to local culture, with historical buildings and sites of the mill, wash-house and museum of the park.

  • Where Malesco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Parco nazionale della Val Grande

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint
