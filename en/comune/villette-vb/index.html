What to see in Villette

Country of the Vigezzo Valley distributed on three plains of the Costa di Faedo, with the hamlets of Gagliago, Vallaro and Londongo: it is known for its forty sundials scattered among the houses, and preserves the remains of the castle of Ferrari, the parish church of San Bartolomeo frescoed, the oratory of San Rocco and the peasant museum "La Ca di Feman da la Piaza" .

Museo «La Ca di Feman da la Piaza»

Museums · Themed

Museum of rural art in a 17th century stone house in the oldest part of the country, dedicated to the three single sisters that the inhabitants called \"feman da la piza" . On the ground floor tools for agriculture, milk processing, chestnut harvesting and fishing, a large fireplace with branches and a nineteenth-century bedroom; on the upper floor tools for textile fibres and footwear, the model of a mill, a working frame and ancient school benches. Open in July Friday-Sunday and August every day (16.30-18.30), in the rest of the year by reservation.

Le meridiane di Villette

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

Villette has about forty sundials, many in a courtyard of Vallaro, spread since the early seventies by Eng. Giacomo Brindicci Bonzani, then mayor; the parish church has two, one of the early nineteenth century and one of 1981. They are visible all year round; guided tours are organized on request and a map is available.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1626 in Gagliago, instead of a 14th-century chapel, it preserves Romanesque features in the bell tower. Inside are frescoes by Bernardino Peretti and Antonio Cotti: the twelve apostles around the choir, the Trinity behind the main altar and the Annunciation; the baptistery is precious. On the façade there is the Baptism of Jesus, a Madonna, the coat of arms of Ferrari Ghibellines, the Borromeo coat of arms and several sundials.

Oratorio di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1655 by Lorenzo Tadina, the last lord of the castle, when the plague killed more than half of the inhabitants. He preserves paintings with the apostles, an Annunciation with the banner of the Bonzani donated by an emigrater and recent decorations of the painter Vagliani; at the feast of the village the statue of the saint comes out in procession.

  • Where Via ai Monti, località Vallaro, Villette
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villette.vb.it

Sources: comune.villette.vb.it

Castello di Vallaro

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Remains of the medieval fortification lined up with the Ferrari, Ghibelline family who ruled the Lower Ossola, and dependent on the castle of Vogogna: there are two granite lunettes with the anvil, the pinnacle and the hammer of the Ferrari and an ogival portal in serizzo surmounted by a Gothic window. It rises on a hill in the village, at the border guard; the access is free and free.

  • Where Piazza Brindicci Bonzani, Villette
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
