What to see in Samarate

Municipality of the province of Varese in whose hamlet Cascina Costa is located the Museo Agusta, a business museum founded in 2002 that tells with motion MV Agusta and helicopters the history of the family company Agusta; next, Villa Agusta (1935) preserves the historical Archives of the Foundation. In the village stands the neo-medieval Villa Ricci Montevecchio (1898-1899), designed by Cecilio Arpesani.

Museo Agusta e Villa Agusta

Local life and traditions · Motors · Paid entry

Museo d'impresa founded in 2002 by Fondazione Museo Agusta, with motorbikes MV Agusta, helicopters, aircraft engines and mechanical and avionic components of Agusta companies; the motorbike section recalls the models of the Motomondiale and pilots such as Giacomo Agostini and John Surtees, that helicopter the evolution of vertical flight, with a simulator of 129 on the first floor. The visit is accompanied by former workers' guides from the Agusta Group. The nearby Villa Agusta, built in 1935, furnished with antique furniture, houses Historical Archives and Library and can be visited on Sundays with the same ticket. Times indicated: Tuesday and Wednesday 14-18, Saturday 9-12 and 14-17, Sunday 9.30-12.30 and 14.30-18; reduced for 14-18 years, over 60 and groups, free up to 14 years; recommended booking on weekends.

Varese Turismo indicates museum and Villa Agusta closed in winter (reopening indicated on March 1st)

  • Where Via Giovanni Agusta 506, Cascina Costa di Samarate
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: vareseturismo.it · in-lombardia.it

Villa Ricci Montevecchio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between 1898 and 1899 by the will of Commendatore Carlo Ricci, entrepreneur of modest origin with international interests, then returned home as mayor and promoter of public works. The project is by Cecilio Arpesani, in neo-medieval style with castle shapes, a scenic tower and the brick of the Padano Lombard language. Inside it contains three frescoes from the fifteenth century coming from Bergamasca, integrated in the decorations of the painter Ernesto Rusca, pupil of Brera; the villa is surrounded by a park.

  • Where Via Vittorio Veneto 49, Samarate
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · in-lombardia.it
