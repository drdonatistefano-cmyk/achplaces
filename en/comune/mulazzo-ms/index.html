What to see in Mulazzo

Ancient capital of the Malaspina of Spino Secco, where Dante was guest in 1306: in the village the Tower of Dante hexagonale, the House of Dante and the Via Dantis, the Museum of Malaspina Archive; in the territory the Castle of Lusuolo with the Museum of Emigration, Montereggio village of the booksellers and the Sanctuary of the Madonna del Monte.

8 places to visit in Mulazzo, in the province of Massa-Carrara (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Casa di Dante in Lunigiana

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Cultural space of the Lunigian Centre of Dante's Studies on the link between Dante and Lunigiana, opened in 2003 as the Museo Dantesco Lunigianese: occupies the noble floor of the last house-tower of the thirteenth century walls, with Renaissance trifora and wooden ceiling with traces of sixteenth century friezes. Showcases with historical books of the Dante tradition, library and educational panels. Visits only by reservation.

  • Where Via Pompeo Signorini 2, Mulazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.mulazzo.ms.it · visittuscany.com

Torre di Dante e Via Dantis

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Rest of a fortified structure of six floors and more than 30 meters, reduced to about one sixth after the partial demolition around 1750: it is the only hexagonal tower of Lunigiana, with under the statue of Dante in marble by Arturo Dazzi (1965). The Via Dantis, completed in 2021, is an open-air journey of nine stages from the medieval aqueduct to the tower: totem shaped stele statues with bas-reliefs in Carrara marble by Giampietro Paolo Paita on the songs of the Comedy, with QR code actually increased.

  • Where Via alla Torre di Dante 6, Mulazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.mulazzo.ms.it · visittuscany.com

Archivio Museo dei Malaspina

Museums · City history

In the Palazzo Malaspina at the top of the village, in front of the Tower of Dante, opened in 2005: over 9,000 books, 600 periodicals and 25,000 manuscript documents collected by Dario Manfredi, with permanent exhibitions on the expedition around the world of the navigator Alessandro Malaspina (born in Mulazzo in 1754), under the command of the Escubierta and Atrevida corvettes from 1789 to 1794. Visitable on request.

  • Where Piazza Alessandro Malaspina 5, Mulazzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Centro storico di Mulazzo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Capital of the Malaspinian fief of Spino Secco since 1221, where the Malaspinas welcomed Dante in the spring of 1306; it has two doors, the remains of the aqueduct and the Malaspina castle, the remains of a Byzantine tower and some noble palaces.

  • Where Mulazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Castello di Lusuolo e Museo dell'emigrazione «Gente di Toscana»

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Malaspinian castle made autonomous by Azzone Malaspina, partly demolished by the Campofregoso in the mid-15th century and enlarged by the Grand Duchy of Tuscany in the sixteenth century; damaged by the earthquake of 1920, passed to the State. You can visit the kitchen with large fireplace, stables, chapel of soldiers, noble floor and walk of panoramic round. Since 2004 it houses the Museum of Emigration with 66 panels in eight sections of photos, letters, passports and documentaries, including the history of the booksellers of Montereggio.

  • Where Castello di Lusuolo, Mulazzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Montereggio, paese dei librai

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

A mountain village, the only one in Italy: from the nineteenth century its street vendors brought books all over central-northern Italy, passing from stone to books in 1858. Here in 1952 was born the Bancarella Prize; in August the Festa del Libro fills streets and squares of meetings with authors.

  • Where Montereggio, Mulazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Santuario della Madonna del Monte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

At about 1,000 meters near the Passo dei Casoni, documented from the twelfth century and built around 1100 by the Benedictine monks of Borzone as a monastery and hospice for pilgrims; Romanesque, of the Malaspinas since 1500 and returned to the Church around 1800. Surrounded by a pine forest with a panoramic path from the Apuan to the Apennines and picnic areas; open every Sunday.

  • Where Via Madonna del Monte 2, Mulazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Castello Malaspina di Castevoli

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Founded around 1000 by the Estensi, it passed to the Malaspinas in 1195 and in 1306 hosted Dante; abandoned after the revolt of 1797, it was rebuilt on the ruins since 1990 by the painter and sculptor Loris Nelson Ricci and his wife Erika H. End is opened in 1998, with sculptures, paintings and architectural projects.

  • Where Castevoli, Mulazzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
