What to see in Sant'Anastasia

The municipality of the National Park of Vesuvius, known for its copper craftsmanship, St. Anastasia preserves the Sanctuary of Our Lady of the Arch, built between 1593 and 1610 around the miraculous image of 1450 and destination every Monday in Albis of the pilgrimage of the Fujenti.

Santuario della Madonna dell'Arco e Museo degli ex voto

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

The cult was born from the image of the Madonna and Child painted on a wall along the road to Somma, near the remains of the arches of a Roman aqueduct: according to tradition on April 6, 1450 a young man, angry for a lost game, struck her and the painting bled. The sanctuary was built from 1593 to 1610 on a project by Giovan Cola di Franco incorporating the newsstand and the first church, and in 1595 passed to the Dominicans; inside the choir in carved walnut, the Adoration of the Magi by Luca Giordano and the temple of the image commissioned in 1621 to Bartolomeo Picchiatti, outside portals with biblical scenes in copper. The complex, with a portico cloister and crypt, preserves a rich collection of ex vows (the oldest probably dates back to 1499) in museum spaces with entrance from Via Romani; every Monday in Albis come the fujenti, the pilgrims dressed in white with red and blue bands.

Chiesa di Santa Maria la Nova

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church of the historic centre with its characteristic octagonal bell tower, indicated by the Vesuvius National Park as the most significant monument in the country.
