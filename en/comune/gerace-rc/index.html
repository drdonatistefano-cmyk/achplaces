What to see in Gerace

Medieval village on the cliff above the Locride: the Cathedral consecrated in 1045, the largest in Calabria, the Orthodox church of San Giovannello and the remains of the Norman castle on the cliff.

8 places to visit in Gerace, in the province of Reggio Calabria (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Gerace in the guide with map and filters

Cattedrale di Gerace

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Romanesque-Norman, consecrated in 1045 and again in 1222, partly built on the rock, with a Greek crypt perhaps of the eighth century and the chapel of Our Lady of Itria, obtained in 1261 from a rock church.

  • Where Piazza Tribuna, Gerace
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Museo diocesano e Tesoro della Cattedrale

Museums · Old masters

Attached to the Cathedral, it preserves in the chapel of St. Joseph silvereries, vestments, chorals and a 17th century Flemish tapestry.

  • Where Gerace
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Giovannello

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Greek Orthodox Church of the 11th century in Piazza delle Tre Chiese, considered the oldest Orthodox church in Italy.

  • Where Piazza delle Tre Chiese, Gerace
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Francesco d'Assisi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church and Gothic convent erected in 1252, with a major altar in polychrome marble of 1644 and the sarcophagus of Nicola Ruffo of 1372.

  • Where Piazza delle Tre Chiese, Gerace
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria del Mastro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in Norman times in 1084, with 17th-century façade, on Piazza della Repubblica next to Palazzo del Balzo.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica, Gerace
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Castello normanno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Remains of the castle of the 11th century on the edge of the cliff, remodelled in the 16th century, reachable from the piazza del Baglio.

  • Where Gerace
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Piazza del Tocco e Porta del Sole

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The central square where seven streets converge, with Palazzo Grimaldi-Serra seat of the town hall; you arrive there from the Baroque Porta del Sole.

  • Where Piazza del Tocco, Gerace
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
