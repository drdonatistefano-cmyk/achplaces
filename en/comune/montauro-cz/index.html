What to see in Montauro

Golfo di Squillace on the Ionian side of the Serre, with the historic center on the hill rich in noble palaces six-seventh-century and, on the coast, the seaside resort of Pietragrande, which takes its name from the large rock near the shore, 13 meters high and loved by those who dive.

Scoglio e spiaggia di Pietragrande

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The seaside and tourist resort has long been known on the Ionian coast, divided between Montauro and Stalettì: it takes its name from a large rock near the shore, 13 meters high, from which dives are made. Clear waters and cliffs are the frame of the beach; italia.it indicates it as an indispensable stop of the Calabrian summer.

  • Where Pietragrande, Montauro (CZ)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Centro storico di Montauro

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small hillside center on the Ionian side of the Serre, with origins that some sources date back to the 8th-IXth century; grew with the Normans and the Carthusian monks, since the 16th century the Turkish raids pushed the population on the hills and under the Bourbons flourished with the processing of silk. Between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries there arose many noble palaces; in the village there is the church of San Pantaleone.

  • Where Centro storico, Montauro (CZ)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it
