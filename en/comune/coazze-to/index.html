What to see in Coazze

Municipality of Alta Val Sangone, seat of the Ecomuseum Alta Val Sangone with the museum of the Resistance of the valley; in the territory you can visit the mine of talc Garida, active until 1968, the giant bench on the Castle Hill and the ski resort of Pian Neiretto.

4 places to visit in Coazze, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ecomuseo Alta Val Sangone ed Ecomuseo della Resistenza

Museums · City history · Free entry

The museum tells the Resistance lived by the people of the valley: facts, people and places become symbol, up to the Liberation and beyond, with today's reading and suggestions for visitors; in the valley it is flanked by other places of memory such as the Ossario and the Common Fossa. In the same building the Ecomuseum Alta Val Sangone has a ground teaching workshop (LABSOL), a weaving workshop, temporary exhibition rooms and the tourist office. Free admission; open during the tourist office hours, which vary with the season.

  • Where Viale Italia '61 1-3, Coazze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Miniera di talco Garida

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Former talc mine: the first tunnels were dug at the end of the 19th century and the extraction ceased in 1968. The underground path, about one hour long, shows the evolution of mining techniques; inside there are an average of 10 °C, plenty of humidity and stretches with water, and the helmet with light is supplied at the entrance. Only by reservation (two visits per month on Saturday and Sunday); you leave your car at the junction for hamlet Flizzo and walk about 15 minutes on dirt road.

  • Where Forno di Coazze, Coazze
  • Entry €15, reduced €11, 7-12 years €13
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Panchina «Ognuno a modo suo»

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Giant bench on the Hill of the Castle above the village, inspired by the motto "Everyone in his own way" engraved also on the bell tower of Coazze. It is reached on foot in 30-40 minutes from the center and offers a wide panorama.

  • Where Collina del Castello, Coazze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Pian Neiretto

Nature and landscape · Mountains

Small ski resort of the Upper Val Sangone with snow-making programmed: descent (2 red and 1 black slopes, 3 lifts) and cross-country (2 blue slopes), plus snowshoes, bobs and sled trips suitable for all ages, with equipment rental.

  • Where Pian Neiretto, Coazze
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org
