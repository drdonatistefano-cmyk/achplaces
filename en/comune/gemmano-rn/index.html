Grotte e Riserva naturale di Onferno

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Karst complex in the most important casts in Italy: over 750 meters of tunnels and a colony of over 6,000 bats. Tradition has it that he inspired Dante's Hell.

  • Where Via Provinciale Onferno 50, Gemmano
  • Entry €10 full, €8 reduced (reserve, visitor center and museum included)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.facebook.com

Sources: facebook.com · riviera.rimini.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Resti delle mura di Gemmano e Linea Gotica

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village crossed by the Gothic Line, destroyed in the Second World War, preserves the remains of the walls of the ancient Malatesta bulwark between Conca and Ventena.

  • Where Gemmano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: riminiturismo.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Riserva naturale orientata di Onferno

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The sheltered area of 274 hectares around the caves, with dense vegetation, rare fauna and outings of chalk, part of the UNESCO site of the Gessi dell'Emilia-Romagna.

  • Where Onferno, Gemmano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
