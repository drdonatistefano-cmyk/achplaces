What to see in Civitella del Tronto

Border village between the Kingdom of Naples and the Papal State, on a rock of travertine dominated by the sixteenth-century fortress of Philip II, the last Bourbon bulwark fallen on March 20, 1861; in the village the Ruetta, medieval churches and the sanctuary of Santa Maria dei Lumi.

8 places to visit in Civitella del Tronto, in the province of Teramo (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Fortezza e Museo delle armi e delle mappe antiche

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Among the largest military works in Europe (over 500 m long, about 25,000 sqm), commissioned in the 16th century by Philip II of Spain in place of an Aragonese fortress; inside galleries, gun squares, cisterns, barracks, the church of St. James and the remains of the Governor's Palace. It was the last Bourbon stronghold to surrender on March 20, 1861. The museum, opened in 1988 in the former artillery stores, exhibits weapons from the Renaissance to the battles of 1806 and 1860-61 and historical maps.

  • Where Largo Vinciguerra, Civitella del Tronto
  • Entry €3, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it · gransassolagapark.it

Piazza Pepe e la Ruetta

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Piazza in the medieval heart with a long panoramic balcony from Gran Sasso to the Adriatic, overlooking Palazzo Portici and the church of San Lorenzo; between the alleys you can reach Porta Napoli, Palazzo Ronchi and the Ruetta, defined the narrowest street in Italy (about 40 cm).

  • Where Piazza Filippi Pepe, Civitella del Tronto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

14th-century church with Romanesque-Gothic façade and 14th-century stone rosette, wooden choir in walnut from the 15th century and a single Baroque nave; part of the former convent houses the town hall.

  • Where Civitella del Tronto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: gransassolagapark.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Lorenzo martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish of the 13th century, transformed into a bastion during the siege of 1557 and rebuilt inside the walls; rebuilt in Baroque forms in 1777, it preserves an eighteenth-century organ and the baroque statue of the patron saint Sant'Ubaldo.

  • Where Piazza Filippi Pepe, Civitella del Tronto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: gransassolagapark.it · italia.it

Santuario di Santa Maria dei Lumi

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

On a hill facing the walls: originally dependent on the abbey of Montesanto, it passed to the Franciscans from the 13th century, who around 1466 built the present complex, still inhabited by the friars minor convents. Convent around a 14th century cloister and church with Romanesque portico; at the high altar the Madonna and Child in polychrome wood by Giovanni di Blusuccio (1489).

  • Where SP8 68, Civitella del Tronto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · gransassolagapark.it

Museums · Themed

Monumento a Matteo Wade

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Monument of 1829 commissioned by Francesco I of the Two Sicilies in honour of the Irish officer who defended the fortress during the siege of 1806.

  • Where Civitella del Tronto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: gransassolagapark.it · italia.it

Gole del Salinello

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

A few kilometers away, in the Laga Mountains and in the National Park, spectacular gorges with paths between woods and waterfalls: the cave of Sant'Angelo with finds of the upper Paleolithic and Neolithic, the ruins of Castel Manfrino and the waterfall of Salinello or Caccamo; in the surrounding area also the hermitages of Santa Maria Scalena and Grotta di San Marco.

  • Where Ripe di Civitella, Civitella del Tronto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · gransassolagapark.it
