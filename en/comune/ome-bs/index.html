What to see in Ome

Franciacorta municipality known for the Borgo del Maglio, group of rural buildings among small streams with a fifteenth-century forge moved by a hydraulic wheel still working and the Museum House Pietro Malossi; on the hill of Goiane the church of San Michele, with frescoes of the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries and views of the plain.

Museo Il Maglio Averoldi

Museums · Themed

A fifteenth-century forge operated by a water wheel still in operation, a rare testimony of the ...bruzafèr... the iron processing once widespread in Franciacorta: a document from 1556 mentions a "mulino della Grotta" and a date of 1430 was visible on an external wall. Between the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries it passed to the Averoldi, who produced agricultural tools there; Andrea Averoldi, among the last blacksmiths in the area, worked with his wife Aldina Barbi until 1984. Purchased by the Municipality in 1997 and open to the public in 1999, it retains its original appearance with hydraulic hammer, stone hydro wind trumpet, pulleys with wooden wheels, wheels and tools, including the 'sappa with horns'; periodically expert craftsmen show live the forging of damask steel blades.

Casa museo Pietro Malossi

Museums · House museums

In the Borgo del Maglio, on two floors, exhibits the private collection of the antiques Pietro Malossi and works by the engraver Francesco Medici: weapons, furniture, prints, paintings, ancient books, coins and sculptures, with information panels and audioguides. In the village also parking, bar and hostel.

  • Where Via Maglio 51, Ome
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.fondazionemalossi.org

Sources: fondazionemalossi.org · visitbrescia.it

Chiesa di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

It rises on the homonymous hill in Goiane and preserves most of the original characters: it was born from a primitive chapel with a single nave, probably Romanesque, with a nave in three bays and a quadrangular presbytery turned into a barrel; between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries were added bell tower, altar, sacristy and guardian's house. It preserves frescoes from the XV and XVI centuries and votive decorations of local artisans; behind the church a park with tables and benches, and on clear days the view reaches up to the Apennines.

  • Where Via S. Michele 17, Ome
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
