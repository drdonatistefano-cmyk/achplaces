What to see in Castellina Marittima

A hill country on the Val di Cecina, documented by the 13th century but perhaps of Etruscan origin, linked to the quarries of the alabaster "Bianco di Castellina": this is where the central museum of the Alabaster Ecomuseum is located; around the village, with the Romanesque parish church and the remains of the fortress, five ring paths in the woods with a view to the sea and Corsica.

Ecomuseo dell'alabastro, punto museale centrale

Museums · Themed

In the former Palazzo Opera "Massimino Carrai" is the museum point of the itinerary of the excavation of the Ecomuseum, which follows the alabaster from the quarries of Castellina and Santa Luce to the processing and trade in Volterra. He tells the extract from the Etruscans to date with archive documents, tools of quarry, objects of daily use and testimonies on the life of the quarrymen, in an exhibition that evokes the visit to a quarry. Managed by ASTREA.

Borgo, pieve di San Giovanni e resti della rocca

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village has been documented since the 13th century, but some historians assume its Etruscan origin. The parish church of San Giovanni (Baptist according to Visit Tuscany, Decollato according to Terre di Pisa) is of Romanesque origin and according to tradition stands on the foundations of a more ancient temple; the ruins of the ancient fortress remain.

  • Where Castellina Marittima
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Percorsi verdi

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Five signposted rings, about 20 km in all, start from the village square among wooded hills, maquis, clearings, streams, ridges and narrow gorges; in winter snow is not uncommon. From the panoramic points you can see to the south the gulf of Baratti and the promontory of Piombino, to the west the sea, the Tuscan archipelago and Corsica, to the north the plain of Pisa with the Tower and the Apuan Islands. Along the trails you can meet foxes, roe deer and raptors; from 1 October to 31 January the Municipality recommends to walk them only Tuesday and Friday, days of venatory silence.

  • Where Piazza del paese, Castellina Marittima
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
