What to see in Verbania

The garden on Lake Borromeo, born from the union of Intra and Pallanza: the Botanical Gardens of Villa Taranto, the lakefront of Pallanza, villas and churches on Castagnola, Suna and, at Fondotoce, reeds and memory of the Resistance.

16 places to visit in Verbania, in the province of Verbano-Cusio-Ossola (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Giardini Botanici di Villa Taranto

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Between Intra and Pallanza, the gardens wanted by the Scottish captain Neil McEacharn, who bought the villa in 1931: 16 hectares on three parts with 70 meters of altitude and over 20,000 species from all over the world. You will also arrive by boat, at the boat of the villa.

  • Where Via Vittorio Veneto 111, Verbania Pallanza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.illagomaggiore.com

Sources: illagomaggiore.com · distrettolaghi.it

Lungolago di Pallanza

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the most beautiful lakes in Lake Maggiore, overlooking the Borromeo Gulf and the islands, with colorful facades, arcades, terraces and cafes: here lived Arturo Toscanini from 1927 to 1952.

  • Where Pallanza, Verbania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.illagomaggiore.com

Sources: illagomaggiore.com

Centro storico di Pallanza

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Ancient streets and squares with vintage buildings, decorated portals, porticoes and capitals, of Roman origins as evidenced by the votive cippo of the first century A.D. preserved in Santo Stefano.

  • Where Pallanza, Verbania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.illagomaggiore.com

Sources: illagomaggiore.com

Museo del Paesaggio

Museums · Modern art

In the Palazzo Viani-Dugnani, built between the Six and the eighteenth century and seat of the museum since 1909: collections of painting, sculpture and archaeology; at Palazzo Biumi-Innocenti the section on popular religiosity and ex-vote.

  • Where Via Ruga 44, Verbania Pallanza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Parco Nazionale della Val Grande

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Since 1992 the protected area of Verbano-Cusio-Ossola, the wildest of the Piedmont Alps, with bivouacs, shelters and fauna of chamois, deer and wolves; Verbania is part of it.
