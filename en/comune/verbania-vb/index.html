What to see in Verbania

The garden on Lake Borromeo, born in 1939 from the union of Intra and Pallanza: the Botanical Gardens of Villa Taranto, the lakefront of Pallanza, the churches of Pallanza, Intra and Suna, at Fondotoce the natural reserve and the memory of the Resistance, behind the Val Grande National Park.

Giardini Botanici di Villa Taranto

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Between Intra and Pallanza, the garden created by the Scottish captain Neil McEacharn, which in the 1930s bought about 20 hectares of land; opened to the public since 1952, preserves about 1,000 non-native species and 20,000 among species and varieties of botanical interest. They boarded by the entrance. Opening season and tickets on the official website.

  • Where Via Vittorio Veneto 111, Verbania Pallanza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.villataranto.it

Sources: villataranto.it · viviverbania.it

Lungolago di Pallanza

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Overlooking the Borromeo Gulf: the old port and the boat with the statue of San Dazio, the City Palace with the portico of 32 pink granite pillars of Baveno, Villa Giulia and the public park. In front, a few meters from the shore, the Isolino di San Giovanni: it is private and you do not visit, you can see it from the lakefront.

  • Where Pallanza, Verbania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page viviverbania.it

Sources: viviverbania.it

Museo del Paesaggio

Museums · Modern art

In Palazzo Viani Dugnani the Gipsoteca by Paolo Troubetzkoy and the Pinacoteca: painting, sculpture, photography and archaeology, collected since 1909 by Antonio Massara, with sculptures by Arturo Martini. In summer open 10-18, closed on Tuesdays; in other periods by appointment.

  • Where Via Ruga 44, Verbania Pallanza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodelpaesaggio.it

Sources: museodelpaesaggio.it · viviverbania.it

Villa Giulia e il parco pubblico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

Neoclassical villa on the marina of Pallanza, commissioned in the mid-nineteenth century by Bernardino Branca, the inventor of Fernet: it hosts exhibitions and exhibitions. The garden is a public park with kiosk, overlooking the lake and the islands.

  • Where Corso Zanitello, Verbania Pallanza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page viviverbania.it

Sources: viviverbania.it · abbonamentomusei.it

Collegiata di San Leonardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church of Pallanza, built between 1535 and 1590, with the monumental staircase and the bell tower of about 65 meters, the highest of Lake Maggiore. Inside, rose granite columns of Baveno, four canvases by Tanzio da Varallo and an organ by Eugenio Biroldi.

  • Where Via San Leonardo 6, Verbania Pallanza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page viviverbania.it

Sources: viviverbania.it

Parrocchiale di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque church of the second half of the 12th century, rebuilt in the 17th century, in the Villa district on the slopes of Castagnola. Inside, the Ara delle Matrone: Roman votive altar in marble of Candoglia, dedicated by the freedom Narciso.

  • Where Piazza Rosario 9, Verbania Pallanza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page viviverbania.it

Sources: viviverbania.it

San Remigio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the most important examples of Romanesque in the territory, built in three phases between the 10th and 12th centuries on the hill of Castagnola, with frescoes from different eras. It's usually closed. Next to it, Villa San Remigio is closed to the public, with the garden being restored.

  • Where Collina della Castagnola, Verbania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page viviverbania.it

Sources: viviverbania.it

Madonna di Campagna

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Renaissance church built on a Romanesque building, of which the bell tower remains: facade with hut with rose window, octagonal tiburium in loggia. On March 25 and August 15, around 5 pm, a ray of sun enters the rose window and illuminates a fresco of Our Lady.

  • Where Viale Azari, Verbania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page viviverbania.it

Sources: viviverbania.it

Basilica di San Vittore

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

In the center of Intra, the main church of Verbania: inaugurated in 1889 instead of a Romanesque church mentioned already in 1031, with the large copper dome. Inside, the "Predica del Battista" by Daniele Ranzoni, works of the Four and Five hundred and the organ Serassi.

  • Where Piazza San Vittore, Verbania Intra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page viviverbania.it

Sources: viviverbania.it

La Casa del Lago

Museums · Themed

Museum of the lake and water culture, in the villa of the patriot Francesco Simonetta where Garibaldi was guest in 1862: multimedia rooms, exhibitions and workshops where samples of the lake water are analysed. Visits and workshops also on request.

  • Where Via Felice Cavallotti 16, Verbania Intra
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.lacasadellago.it

Sources: lacasadellago.it · viviverbania.it

Suna, Santa Lucia e Oratorio dei Ss. Fabiano e Sebastiano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the lakefront of Suna the church of Saint Lucia, of the sixteenth century, with eight rounds painted by Mario Tozzi in 1923-1924 and, outside, the monument to the fallen of Tozzi himself. At the top of the stairway is the Oratory of the Ss. Fabiano and Sebastiano, Romanesque of the 12th century: in summer open on Saturday and Sunday, in the rest of the year you can see from outside.

  • Where Suna, Verbania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page viviverbania.it

Sources: viviverbania.it

Casa della Resistenza

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

At Fondotoce, next to the place where on June 20, 1944 43 partisans were shot (one survived). Library, activities for schools and the Park of Memory and Peace, with a wall of over 1,200 names. Open from Monday to Friday, free entry, recommended booking (mandatory for groups).

  • Where Via Filippo Turati 9, Verbania Fondotoce
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page viviverbania.it

Sources: viviverbania.it

Riserva naturale di Fondotoce

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

360 hectares protected at the mouth of Toce, with reedwood and over one hundred species of birds, fish, amphibians and mammals. Cycling and pedestrian trails between Lake Maggiore and Lake Mergozzo, connected to the centre of Verbania and the station.

  • Where Fondotoce, Verbania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page viviverbania.it

Sources: viviverbania.it · italia.it

Parco Nazionale Val Grande

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established in 1992 and expanded in 2023, when Verbania, Mergozzo and Ornavasso joined the group: the largest protected wild area of the Italian Alps, with the first integral reserve of the Alps (1967) and a UNESCO World Geographic Park. Crossing it requires experience: along the trails there are no services and the phone takes little. For accesses, visitor centers and guided excursions, you can contact the Park and its official guides.

  • Where Sede del Parco: Piazza Pretorio 6, Vogogna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.parcovalgrande.it

Sources: parcovalgrande.it · viviverbania.it · isprambiente.gov.it
