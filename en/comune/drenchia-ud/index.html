What to see in Drenchia

The highest municipality of the Natisone Valleys, on the ridge at the border with Slovenia at the foot of the Kolovrat: villages of rural architecture such as Obenetto and Cras, the small Museum of the rural house in Trinco and the trenches of the third Italian defensive line of the Great War.

3 places to visit in Drenchia, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della casa rurale di Drenchia

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Set up in the former elementary school of the village of Trinco, at the foot of the Kolovrat, it collects objects and tools of rural life from the end of the nineteenth century to the middle of the twentieth century: an anthropological and ethnographic journey on life in the Natisone Valleys, a Slovenian linguistic and cultural area of Friuli. It also preserves relics and photographs of the Great War. Visits only by appointment (Pro Loco Nediške Doline).

  • Where Trinco 1, Drenchia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Museo all'aperto del Kolovrat, terza linea difensiva italiana

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the Kolovrat plateau, along the border with Slovenia, the positions of the third Italian defensive line of the First World War, linked to the defeat of Caporetto: trenches clearly visible at the Zagradan Pass (1042 m), positions on Mount Klabuk restored by the Poti miru v Posočju Foundation, remains of machine gun nests and military buildings on Mount Piatto. It starts from Passo Solarie, where at 50 meters stands the cyppo alpino Riccardo Di Giusto, the first Italian fallen of the Great War, following the path CAI 746; on the return you can turn to Clabuzzaro and San Volfango, with the large memorial on the site of a former military cemetery.

  • Where Passo Solarie, SP45 via Drenchia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Borgate di Obenetto e Cras e chiesette votive

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Drenchia stretches on the ridge of a Moorish amphitheatre in the highest part of the Natisone Valleys; the villages of Obenetto and Cras preserve the traditional rural architecture of the valleys, made of stone houses. The territory houses two votive churches, Santa Maria Assunta and San Volfango, and numerous panoramic paths towards the Kolovrat and a stretch of the Path of Peace.

  • Where Obenetto e Cras, Drenchia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it
