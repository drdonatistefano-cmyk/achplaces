What to see in Villa Lagarina

Noble village of Vallagarina on the banks of the Adige, linked to the Counts Lodron: around Piazza Riolfatti the church of the Assunta, between the most beautiful Baroque monuments of Trentino, and the Free Palaces (Diocesan Museum), Guerrieri Gonzaga and Camelli; in the hamlets the flosser of Piazzo, the castle of Castellano and, higher up, the lake of Cei.

7 places to visit in Villa Lagarina, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Diocesano Tridentino, Palazzo Libera

Museums · Old masters

Section of the Diocesan Museum in an eighteenth-century palace on three floors, with the patrimony of the counts Lodron, patrons of the church of Santa Maria Assunta: on the first floor the hall of the Lodron with five portraits, liturgical vestments and lace; on the second antique fabrics, jewelry and the large archive wardrobe commissioned by Carlo Ferdinando Lodron in 1692; on the third a white satin parade and taffeta embroidered in gold. From 4 July to 31 December 2026 open Friday 15-17.30 and Saturday 9-12; guided tours to museum and church by reservation.

  • Where Via Garibaldi 10, Villa Lagarina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodiocesanotridentino.it

Chiesa arcipretale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Considered one of the most beautiful and important Baroque monuments of Trentino, imposing church of the center of which the Counts Lodron had the patronage, symbol of their political and religious power; its furnishings are now exhibited in the nearby Diocesan Museum, which organizes guided tours also to the church.

  • Where Centro storico, Villa Lagarina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · visitrovereto.it

Centro storico, piazza Riolfatti e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Piazza Riolfatti is the heart of the village, with the fountain-washer framed by a double row of Hippocastani; the center is decorated with marble fountains, including the large water fountain, and noble palaces: Palazzo Guerrieri Gonzaga in via Cavolavilla, built around the seventeenth century by the counts of Festivals of Braunsfeld, Palazzo Camelli (now Town Hall), Palazzo Madernini and Palazzo Marzani.

  • Where Piazza Riolfatti, Villa Lagarina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · visitrovereto.it

Parco Guerrieri Gonzaga

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

One of the most evocative and best preserved green oases of Trentino, with a large lemon grove lined with bosso hedges.

Filatoio di Piazzo

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Water-based silk yarn with the rotating shaft still visible, rare testimony in Europe: at the beginning of the nineteenth century there were three torcoils on five floors, moved by a hydraulic wheel, and a canning machine.

  • Where Piazzo, Villa Lagarina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitrovereto.it · visittrentino.info

Castello di Castellano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Ancient bishopric of Trento who, with the castles of Corno and Noarna, watched the streets towards the lake of Cei; conquered by the Castelbarco in the middle of the thirteenth century, in 1456 passed to the counts Lodron, who transformed it into a noble residence. Today it belongs to the Miorandi family; in August it hosts Castelfolk.

private property, visitable only on telephone agreement with the owner

  • Where Castellano, Villa Lagarina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitrovereto.it · visittrentino.info

Lago di Cei

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small lake, just under 1,000 meters, born from an ancient landslide, between beech and coniferous woods and connected to the Lagabis pond, with water lilies, reeds and an island of larch, birch and fir trees; it is part of the Biotope Prà dell'Albi-Cei. The tour of the lake, almost all on the floor, lasts about an hour and is also suitable for strollers; from the lake you reach the waterfalls of Strafalt, with caves, mills and the arch of a bridge maybe Roman. Balneation only in permitted areas.

  • Where SP 20 del Lago di Cei, Castellano, Villa Lagarina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info
