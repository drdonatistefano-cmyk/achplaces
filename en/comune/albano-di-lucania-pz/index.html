What to see in Albano di Lucania

Country on Monte San Leonardo, in the Lucane Dolomites, overlooking the valley of the Basento between the monoliths of the Rocca del Cappello and the Devil's Chair; it was a Longobard fortress and preserves the mother church of the Assunta, the museum of poor toy and the wood of Cupolicchio.

Museo interattivo del giocattolo povero e del gioco di strada

Museums · Themed

It is located in the attic of the Institute including and studying, documenting and enhancing popular games and toys: over 250 contemporary works of art on the theme of the game and a hundred ethnographic toys, especially from Basilicata and Puglia, with exhibition curated by Donato Allegretti.

Chiesa madre dell'Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It dates back to 1200 but has been renovated several times; it is recognized from the high bell tower in square blocks of stone. Inside a canvas of the Madonna della Snow attributed to the school of Raphael and the Madonna of the Rosary of Pietrafesa.

  • Where Albano di Lucania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it

Cento macine

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Large blocks of stone carved into the rock that emerge from the ground; they served to press grapes and olives, but according to some hypotheses they would also be pagan altars.

  • Where Albano di Lucania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry
