What to see in Neoneli

A village of about 700 inhabitants of Barigadu, near Lake Omodeo, with houses in red trachite and windows in Aragonese style, the 17th century parish church of San Pietro and, on the slopes of Mount Santa Vittoria, the Faunistic oasis of Assai with its small museum.

Oasi naturalistica di Assai

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Nearly a thousand hectares between Neoneli and Nughedu Santa Vittoria, on the slopes of Mount Santa Vittoria: secular holm oaks and gravy among granite peaks, once a source of coal for the whole island. Since 1983, with the opening of a forest yard, it is a permanent oasis of wildlife protection: Sardinian deer, deer, wild boar, wild cat, marten and, in recent years, the royal nesting eagle again. Walking, mountain biking or horseback riding trails lead to the view of Borta Melone (almost 900 meters), to an artificial pond and to the granite rock of Sa Crabarixa, about 50 meters high.

  • Where Oasi di Assai, Neoneli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Museo faunistico dell'Oasi di Assai

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Small museum in a former shepherd's shelter inside the oasis: stuffed animals of the Sardinian fauna (sardinian deer, deer, wild boar, wild cat, marten, weasel, imperial raven, owl, barbagianni, Sardinian pernice) and a royal eagle in a forest diorama, with a collection of minerals and fossils. Outside there is a charcoal mill and a traditional hut (pinnettu); wild boars, deer and deer used to man are often gathered around the museum. Open every day, October-April 8-16, May-September 8-20; guided tours, numerous groups by reservation.

  • Where Località Su Mortoggiu, Oasi di Assai, Neoneli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Centro storico in trachite rossa

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village preserves houses in red trachite with Aragonese-style windows and basalt ornaments, and artisan workshops of cork carving, leather processing and blacksmiths. At Carnival the masks sos Corriolos animate the rites with a bonfire in the central square; at the end of September the festival Licanias is dedicated to rural landscapes.

  • Where Centro storico, Neoneli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it
