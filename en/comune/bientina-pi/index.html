What to see in Bientina

Country on the plain of the former lake of Bientina, once the largest in Tuscany, with the parish church of Santa Maria Assunta that preserves the remains of Saint Valentino and its Museum of sacred art, the fourteenth-century Mastio and the Museum of the ancient history of the territory in the former oratory of San Girolamo; on the edge of the ancient lake the reserve of Bosco Tanali.

7 places to visit in Bientina, in the province of Pisa (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

On the main square, which became a parish church in the fifteenth century and consecrated in 1644: simple exterior and 18th century interior rich in stuccoes and paintings, with altar paintings by Tuscan painters from the seventeenth to the eighteenth century, wooden ceiling painted in Rococo style and monumental organ of the seventeenth century in golden wooden case. Since 1699 he has kept the relics of Saint Valentine, patron saint of the country, to whom it is said that Gian Gastone de' Medici paid homage.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II 28, Bientina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Museo d'arte sacra della pieve

Museums · Old masters · Free entry

It is annexed to the parish church and collects over 120 sacred objects (sinks, candlesticks, glasses, lamps, monstrances and reliquaries) from Lucca, Pisa, Florence and Neapolitan and French schools, furniture in noble metals from the 16th to the 19th century and liturgical vestments from the 16th to the 18th century. Open on the fourth Saturday and Sunday of the month, 9.30-12.30 and 15.30-18.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II 28, Bientina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Museo della storia antica del territorio «Vittorio Bernardi»

Museums · Archaeological

In the former seventeenth-century oratory of San Girolamo, it tells of the transformation of the landscape from about 1000 to the 5th century BC into three sections: kitchen ceramics, fragments decorated with Villanovian style, buccheri and the set of a tomb emerged during the reclamation of the padule, with protoetruscan and Etruscan-Roman finds of the inhabited submerged by the lake.

Terre di Pisa indicates it is closed at the same time.

  • Entry To be checked

Mastio e mura medievali

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The 14th century Mastio annexed to the Civic Tower, once a high part of the fortress and barracks of the village, connected to a stretch of walls; damaged in 1944, it returned to public use in 1997 and houses the municipal library (more than 3,000 volumes) and the historical archive, with documents from 1402 to 1866 and the maps of the Cosci and Gerini families. In the centre there are still sections of the medieval walls and towers.

  • Where Piazzetta dell'Angiolo 10, Bientina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · terredipisa.it

Padule di Bientina (ex lago di Sesto)

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lake, once the largest in Tuscany, was drained with works begun at the time of Cosimo I and with the reclamation of Leopoldo di Lorena between 1824 and 1859; today it is a vast agricultural plain between Monti Pisani and Cerbaie, and the drain has returned Etruscan and Roman remains.

  • Where Piana di Bientina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Riserva di Bosco Tanali

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Natural area on the edge of the ancient riverbed of the lake, with periodically flooded meadows, humid woods, reeds and small water mirrors; birdwatching destination, with hiking excursions organized by Legambiente Valdera.

  • Where Bosco Tanali, Bientina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Mercato dell'antiquariato

Local life and traditions · Historic markets · Free entry

Among the most traditional markets in Tuscany, every month; the most important edition is BientAntiquaria from 22 November to 27 December 2026.
