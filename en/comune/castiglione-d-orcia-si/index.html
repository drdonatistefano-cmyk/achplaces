What to see in Castiglione d'Orcia

Ancient castle on the Via Francigena on the border between Val d'Orcia UNESCO and woods of the Amiata, with the Fortress of Tentennano and the thermal baths of Bagni San Filippo.

5 places to visit in Castiglione d'Orcia, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca di Tentennano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

The fortress of the thirteenth century on a spur of limestone, of the Salimbeni in the Three and fifteenth centuries, where St. Catherine also stayed: he controlled the south of the Sienese State.

  • Where Rocca d'Orcia, Castiglione d'Orcia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Sala d'Arte San Giovanni

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage

Small museum in the former oratory of St. John the Baptist with three Madonnas with the Child of Simone Martini, of the Vecchietta and of John of Paul, painted for Castiglione and Rocca d'Orcia.

  • Where Castiglione d'Orcia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Piazza Vecchietta e chiese del borgo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The square dedicated to the painter Lorenzo di Pietro called the Vecchietta, with the Town Hall, the Romanesque Santa Maria Maddalena and the church of Saints Stefano and Degna.

  • Where Piazza Vecchietta, Castiglione d'Orcia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Bagni San Filippo

Local life and traditions · Spas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Spa area famous for the white limestone concretions along paths in the green, with the cave of San Filippo Benizi, site of pilgrimage.

  • Where Bagni San Filippo, Castiglione d'Orcia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Vivo d'Orcia ed Eremo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Frazione born around an ancient Camaldolese hermitage, transformed in the sixteenth century with the Palazzo Cervini by Antonio da Sangallo the Younger; a path between beech and chestnut trees leads to the springs of the Vivo.

  • Where Vivo d'Orcia, Castiglione d'Orcia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
