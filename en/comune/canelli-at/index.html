What to see in Canelli

The city of the first Italian sparkling wine, Flag Arancione, with the underground cathedrals digged into the tuff, part of the UNESCO site of wine landscapes.

4 places to visit in Canelli, in the province of Asti (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 3 of the 4 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Cattedrali sotterranee – Cantine Bosca

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · UNESCO World Heritage

One of the underground cathedrals: galleries dug into the tuff between the 16th and 19th centuries up to 32 meters deep, at 12-14 degrees constant, where they refine bottles of classic method. Visit by reservation.

  • Where Via G. B. Giuliani 23, Canelli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it
