What to see in Berchidda

Country on the southern slopes of the Limbara, the land of Vermentino and the seat of the Time in Jazz festival: the Wine Museum with the Regional Enoteca, the ruins of the medieval castle of Montacuto, the seventeenth-century churches and the forest of Monte Limbara.

5 places to visit in Berchidda, in the province of Sassari (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo del vino - Enoteca regionale della Sardegna

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

At the foot of the hill of Sant'Alvara, on the outskirts of the village, tells the history of viticulture in Sardinia from a cultural and ethnographic point of view; the associated regional wine shop offers tasting and sale of wines.

  • Where Colle di Sant'Alvara, Berchidda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Castello di Montacuto

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

At about 500 meters above sea level near the village, it was built in the 11th century as a political and military center; contended between Pisa and Genoa, it passed from the judges of Torres and Arborea to the Catalans. Today they remain ruins of walls, a tower and a cistern.

  • Where Vicino a Berchidda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Centro storico, San Sebastiano e chiesa del Rosario

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Liberty-style houses arranged in semicircle along the steep streets of the center; the parish church of San Sebastiano houses a Baroque wooden altar and the Rosary church is from the seventeenth century. From the main square you can see the valley and Mount Acuto.

  • Where Centro storico, Berchidda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiese campestri di Santa Caterina e Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Two campestri churches of the seventeenth century; in Santa Caterina, during the Time in Jazz festival, musicians improvise by day, and the feast of the saint falls in early June.

  • Where Campagna di Berchidda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Monte Limbara

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Berchidda occupies the southern side; the highest peak, Punta Balistreri, reaches 1,362 meters. Nearly 7,000 hectares are managed by the Forestas agency, with paths such as that of the Tafoni (9 km), the summit of the river "Salistreri" (12 km, up to Punta Balistreri) and "La via delle acque" (30 km, 29 sources); in the forest you will find the ancient staces of transhumance.

  • Where Versante sud del Limbara, Berchidda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
