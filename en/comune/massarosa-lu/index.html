What to see in Massarosa

A common place between the hills of Versilia and the lake of Massaciuccoli, stop of migratory birds protected by the LIPU Oasis; on the shores of the lake the archaeological area of Massaciuccoli Romana with the villa of Venulei, on the hills the Romanesque parish church of San Pantaleone in Elici, the village of Gualdo and 27 noble villas, including Villa Baldini linked to Paolina Bonaparte.

Massaciuccoli Romana e Padiglione «Guglielmo Lera»

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the shores of the lake remain the ruins of the villa of the Venulei family, from the beginning of the first century AD, with a monumental spa complex in panoramic position on the lake, coast and sea. The exhibition pavilion "Guglielmo Lera" includes a second Roman building discovered in the 1930s, with a small thermal environment, part of the heating system and the mosaic of the frigidary to fantastic animals in black and white tiles, and tells with a selection of objects the daily life of Massaciuccoli between I and IV century AD; in front, under a large cover, new excavations have brought to light other environments.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Oasi LIPU di Massaciuccoli

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Protected area of 47 hectares on the lake, between reeds and water lilies, ideal for birdwatching: tarabusini, diving ducks, divers, cormorants, folagues, herons and birds of prey like the royal dawn. Overelevated passerelle on the water lead to observation huts; the tour centre hosts the Museum of Pallustrated Ecology and offers guided tours, boat trips, canoeing, kayak or rowing boat, workshops and summer camps.

  • Where Via del Porto 143, Massaciuccoli, Massarosa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Lago di Massaciuccoli e porto

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

The largest humid zone in Tuscany, born from an ancient brackish lagoon slowly reclaimed, borders the beaches of Versilia and is a stopover for migratory birds; it is part of the Park of Migliarino, San Rossore and Massaciuccoli. From the small port of Massaciuccoli you can observe the birds along the shore trails and in summer they start crossing by boat, even evenings with observation of the stars or opera shows; on the other side you can see Torre del Lago, the village of Puccini.

  • Where Porto di Massaciuccoli, Massarosa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Pieve di San Pantaleone a Elici

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Romanesque church on a ridge of Monte Pitoro, with 360 degrees view over the plain of Massarosa, the lake and the Versilia coast. The origins are linked to the evangelization of St. Frediano and the first document that quotes it is of 892; the current building, with a Latin cross with three naves, semicircular apse and bell tower, is a reconstruction of the XII century that preserves a wall of the original church. On the main altar a marble triptych from 1470 with the Madonna, San Pantaleone and San Giovanni Battista; the name Elici would come from the holm oaks (ilex) around the church.

  • Where Via della Croce 100, Pieve a Elici, Massarosa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Gualdo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Well preserved medieval village, a few hairpin bends from Elici, overlooking the sea. The fairytale village in August hosts the Fairy Festival.

  • Where Gualdo, Massarosa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Sentiero delle Sughere e ville di Compignano

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The territory has 27 noble villas, built mainly from the nineteenth century, some of rich merchants. The Sughere Trail passes from the village of Crocetta next to Villa Minutoli Tergimi, built on the remains of a Longobard period tower, then touches Villa Baldini, with an Italian garden, where Paulina Bonaparte lived for a short time, and Villa Hernandez; with Mount Aquilata on the left, a road in pebbles leads to the church of San Frediano and the village of Compignano.

  • Where Crocetta - Compignano, Massarosa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
