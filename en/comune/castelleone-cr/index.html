What to see in Castelleone

Borgo founded in 1188 as a defensive cornerstone of Cremona, Castelleone preserves the 12th century Torre Isso, the long Via Roma porticata, the Renaissance parish church of Saints Philip and James and, in the countryside, two Renaissance churches: the Sanctuary of Mercy and Santa Maria in Bressanoro.

Torre Isso (Torrazzo)

History and architecture · Towers

Square brick tower about 47 meters high, with walls 1.75 meters thick, only surviving part of the medieval defenses of the village; according to tradition it was spared when Frederick Barbarossa destroyed the castle in 1186. From 1948 to 1997 he housed the tank of the aqueduct; in 1848 Carlo Borsieri hoisted the tricolor during the motions.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Via Roma e portici medievali

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The porticos were born in the Middle Ages, when the village was rebuilt under Bishop Sicardo, and run on two rows along Via Roma, with shops and shops, from Torrazzo towards Borgo Serio; they were refurbished in 1849. The porticoed houses have medieval and Renaissance structure. Every second Sunday of the month the centre hosts the antique market, born in April 1988, the first of the province of Cremona.

  • Where Via Roma, Castelleone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Sources: comune.castelleone.cr.it · turismocremona.it

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Filippo e Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Renaissance church on Piazza del Comune, designed by Agostino de' Fondulis (1517-1551), with facade adorned with terracotta bands. The interior with three naves contains paintings by Giulio Campi, Genovesino and Gian Giacomo Barbelli.

  • Where Piazza del Comune, Castelleone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Sources: turismocremona.it

Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

It is located in via Roma in the eighteenth century Palazzo Brunenghi-Salvago, together with the civic library, and documents the human presence in the territory from the Mesolithic to the Early Middle Ages.

Santuario della Beata Vergine della Misericordia

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Started in 1513 on a project attributed to Agostino Fondulo and concluded in 1525, it rises in the green about one kilometer from the center, on the place of the apparitions of the Virgin on Sunday Zanenga. The restoration of Amos Edallo (1964-1965) has recovered its original forms; it preserves frescoes by G.B. Dordoni, the wooden Madonna of Paolo Maltempo (1560), paintings by Angelo Bacchetta (1862) and an organ Serassi of 1836.

  • Where Piazzale Santuario, Castelleone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Sources: comune.castelleone.cr.it · turismocremona.it

Santa Maria in Bressanoro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church in the open countryside, about 2 km from the center, rebuilt in 1460-1461 by vote of Bianca Maria Visconti and Blessed Amedeo Mendes da Silva; it is one of the first central churches of the Lombard Renaissance, with polygonal choir added in 1505. Inside a vast cycle of frescoes from the late fifteenth century with stories of the life and Passion of Christ, culminating in a great Crucifixion.

  • Where Via Santa Maria, Castelleone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · turismocremona.it
