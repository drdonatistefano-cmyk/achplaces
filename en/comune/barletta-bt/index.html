What to see in Barletta

The city of the Disfida of 1503: the historic center gathered around the castle with the Civic Museum, the cathedral, the Colossus of Heraclius, the Cantina della Sfida, Palazzo della Marra with the works of Giuseppe De Nittis and, in the countryside, the battlefield of Canne.

8 places to visit in Barletta, in the province of Barletta-Andria-Trani (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello e Museo Civico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The imposing castle around which the historic centre is gathered houses the Civic Museum, with the only existing bust of Emperor Frederick II.

Palazzo della Marra – Pinacoteca «Giuseppe De Nittis»

Museums · Modern art

Since 1914 it has been guarding the richest collection of the Impressionist painter, born from the donation of his wife Léontine: over 200 works; the rear loggia looks at the sea and Porta Marina.

  • Where Via Cialdini 74, Barletta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Parco archeologico e Antiquarium di Canne della Battaglia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The place of the victory of Hannibal over the Romans in 216 BC: the Antiquarium with prehistoric, classical and medieval finds, the remains of the Byzantine castle and the citadel crossed by the decuman.

  • Where Strada Provinciale 142 Barletta-Canosa, Barletta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sigecweb.beniculturali.it · italia.it
