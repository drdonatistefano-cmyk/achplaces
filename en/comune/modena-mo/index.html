What to see in Modena

Cathedral, Ghirlandina Tower and Piazza Grande have been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1997. A unique ticket opens Galleria Estense, Museo Civico, Gipsoteca and room immersive Avia Pervia.

36 places to visit in Modena, in the province of Modena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di Modena

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The Romanesque cathedral of Lanfranco and Wiligelmo consecrated in 1106, with the reliefs of Genesis on the facade, the Porta della Pescheria and the crypt of St Geminiano.

  • Where Corso Duomo, Modena
  • Entry Presbytery €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Torre Ghirlandina

History and architecture · Towers · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The bell tower of the Duomo is almost 90 meters high, symbol of the city, with the Room of the Torresani frescoed and the view on the rooftops of Modena.
