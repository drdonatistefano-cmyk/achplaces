What to see in Massa e Cozzile

Two fortified villages of the Valdinievole: Massa, with the Porta ai Campi of the XIV century, the church of Santa Maria Assunta, the Museum of San Michele and the Loggia del Podestà in Piazza Matteotti; and Cozzile, higher up, with San Jacopo, Palazzo de Gubernatis and a view that on clear days arrives at Elba.

Borgo di Massa e Piazza Matteotti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

From the Porta ai Campi (14th century) on Piazza Cavour you can climb up via Roma, lined with medieval buildings and houses, to Piazza Matteotti, the heart of the castle since its foundation, with the 13th century Podestà Loggia covered with noble coats of arms and the Palace of the Municipal Historical Archive. Of the three doors there is also Porta Fontana, to the west; at the top of the village the remains of the medieval fortress among the olive trees.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church on Piazza Cavour, rebuilt in the 17th century together with a wing of the Monastery of the Visitation, with facade of the 16th century and bell tower of the 11th modified in the 16th century; the Renaissance interior preserves a crucifix of the 15th century. Next to it, the small church of the monastery has 18th century decorations.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · valdinievoleturismo.it

Museo di San Michele

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated in 2004 in the chapel and in two rooms of the former monastery, it exhibits liturgical furnishings and documents of the church of Santa Maria Assunta and of the Municipal Historic Archives: the "Presentation of Mary at the Temple" probably attributed to Luigi Crespi, the wooden choir of the late eighteenth century, silk and gold vestments, the silver monsory of 1663 by Paolo Laurentini, the processional cross of 1664 and the reliquary of the Holy Cross of 1722. The chapel has paintings of 1714-1718.

  • Where Via Roma, Massa e Cozzile
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Borgo di Cozzile

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

At about one kilometer above Massa, probably founded at the end of the XII century as a viewer and still in the fourteenth century guarded for the Lucchesi; it has fishbone plant Â Â Â along the main road, with the oratories of San Filippo Neri and the Company and at the end the church of San Jacopo, rebuilt and enlarged in the sixteenth century. The walls and the doors to Massa, Vecchia and Nuova remain; just outside Porta Nuova the panorama embraces the Valdinievole and on clear days Volterra and Elba.

Palazzo de Gubernatis

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Large building with scenic façade on the valley, next to San Jacopo, built at the end of the nineteenth century on the perimeter of the ancient fortress: the residence of the Polidori from the sixteenth century, then of the Giani and the abbot dantist Gian Battista Giuliani, who made it a villa dedicated to Dante, was renovated by the Sienese architect Giovanni Paciarelli for Count Angelo De Gubernatis. For access contact the Municipality.
