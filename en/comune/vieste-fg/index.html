What to see in Vieste

Called the "pearl of Gargano," Vieste combines a medieval old town with a castle and cathedral to a coast of high cliffs, sea caves and beaches like the Scialara, where stands the monolith of Pizzomunno, symbol of the city. In the village there is also a museum with thousands of shells from all over the world.

Centro storico, Cattedrale e Piazzetta Petrone

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval old town is a maze of narrow streets and ancient houses, with souvenir shops and crafts. The tour stages are the castle and the cathedral; from the viewpoint of Piazzetta Petrone you can enjoy a beautiful view of the sea.

Castello Svevo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Of early medieval origin, it corresponds to the "castellum quod dicitur Besti" quoted in a parchment of 1059. It was restored around 1240 by Frederick II of Swabia in the program of fortification of the Apulian coast and profoundly modified by the Spanish in the first half of the 16th century. It dominates the coast from a cliff above the Scialara beach.

  • Where Località Castello 1, Vieste
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Museo Malacologico

Museums · Themed

It collects over 12 thousand shells from all over the world, in a collection born in 1975 from the passion of the owners for travel and the sea; some rare specimens come from Philippines and Vietnam. A section is dedicated to marine fossils, some from Madagascar and Morocco.

  • Where Via Pola 2, Vieste
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Pizzomunno

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

The limestone monolith is about 25 meters high at the beginning of the Mattei promenade, south of the centre, considered the symbol of the city; it is also admired from the top by via Bracco, in the historic center. According to legend he was a young man turned into stone by the jealousy of the Sirene, who prevented his love with Cristalda.

  • Where Lungomare Enrico Mattei, Vieste
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it

Spiaggia della Scialara

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

About 3 km of fine white sand with shallow and crystal clear waters, in the Gargano National Park, with the historic centre on the limestone massif behind. At the beginning there is the Pizzomunno and the castle swabble dominates it from the cliff, from which the nickname of "Beach of the Castle." It has facilities with sun loungers, umbrellas, showers and restaurants, but also quiet stretches; you can walk to the promenade Mattei.

  • Where Lungomare Enrico Mattei, Vieste
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
