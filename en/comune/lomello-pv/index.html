What to see in Lomello

The Roman Laumellum, post office station on the road between Pavia and France, where according to tradition they married Teodolinda and Agiulfo; today it is the village of Lomellina with one of the oldest baptisteries in Italy, San Giovanni ad Fontes, next to the proto-Romanesque basilica of Santa Maria Maggiore, and with the Castle Crivelli frescoed, seat of the Municipality, and some sections of the ancient walls.

Basilica di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Basilicas · Paid entry

Collegiate defined by Lombardy Cultural Heritage as one of the greatest achievements of the first European Romanesque architecture: built between 1025 and 1040 on the remains of a Longobard building, when the Palatine counts of Pavia settled in the castle. Latin cross plan with three naves with protruding transept and three apses, about 60 metres long and 19 metres wide, with alternating pillars and arches; facade and first spans collapsed in the unknown era and the bell tower was rebuilt in the nineteenth century. Behind the altar a staircase descends to the crypt with remains of a previous building; the tradition preserves relics of the Holy Cross donated by Guglielmo de Grossis, who died in Lomello in 1370.

  • Where Via Castrovecchio, Lomello
  • Entry €3 (entrance to the religious complex)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Battistero di San Giovanni ad Fontes

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Paid entry

To the south of the basilica, it is one of the most important buildings in Lombardy and, for Italia.it, among the masterpieces of early Christian art in Lombardy: the lower part is dated to the V-VI centuries, the upper one to the VII. Small brick building with octagonal plan with alternate circular and square niches, illuminated by eight windows and a lantern at the top of the dome; the hexagonal baptismal font with painted cross is unusual in Lombardy and typical of the area of Aquileia and Istria. Rediscovered under the road plan in 1935 and recently restored, it preserves high medieval frescoes.

  • Where Via Castrovecchio, Lomello
  • Entry €3 (entrance to the religious complex)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Museo degli stucchi della Basilica di Santa Maria Maggiore

Museums · Old masters

Set up in the parish canonical, it exhibits fragments of the stucco decoration that once adorned the central nave of the basilica. It is visited together with the baptistery and the basilica; the complex is part of the parish of Lomello.

  • Where Via Castrovecchio, Lomello
  • Entry To be checked

Castello Crivelli

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortified preside over a relief in the center of the village, probably of the 10th century and rebuilt in the 11th and early 15th centuries; in 1381 Gian Galeazzo Visconti had the village fortified with a fortress and since 1449 Antonio Crivelli, infeudated by Francesco Sforza, rebuilt it with moat and tower guarding the drawbridge. The columns of the courtyard loggia are probably from marbles purchased in Stradella in 1549 by Cardinal Alessandro Crivelli. On the ground floor two frescoed rooms in the sixteenth century: a profane cycle of Flemish taste with seasons, zodiac and agricultural works and a religious one with the life of Saint Catherine of Alexandria and Dante's Paradise; in the library room a Roman mosaic. Since 1948 it has been the seat of the Municipality.

  • Where Piazza Castello 1, Lomello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
