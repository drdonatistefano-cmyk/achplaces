What to see in Ponzano Monferrato

Comune del Monferrato casalese with two castles and their historical parks: that of Ponzano, mentioned already in 1014, with nineteenth-century gardens and a panoramic tower, and that of Salabue of the Cozio counts, as well as an infernot excavated in the Pietra da Cantoni.

Castello di Ponzano e parco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Cited for the first time in 1014 in the diploma with which Arrigo il Santo donated the territory to the church of Vercelli, from the thirteenth century it was fief of the de Ponzano, Castellazzo, Saliceto and de Setaria; an act of 1431 describes palace, tower of the nobles and moat. He then passed to the Donato, counts from 1635 to the Cattaneo, the Tizzoni and the Marquis Church of Cinzano; in the nineteenth century the Camozzi knight created a flower garden (1877) and at the end of the century the garden on the eastern side was built with hedges of boxwood and stone bath. The park has centuries-old trees and a tower with panoramic views; for the visit you should contact the property.

Castello di Salabue

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The complex was built at the end of the thirteenth century in a shelter and documented since 1349; from 1431 it passed to Tizzoni, Facerio, Bossi, Natta, Nuvoloni, Striggi, Fassati and Bruschio and finally to the Cozios, Counts of Salabue, who transformed it into a residence of representation filling the moat. After 1935 the Davico had it modified by the architect Gianni Ricci and decorated by the Italian painters Alfredo Parachini and Vittorio Accornero, who around 1948 redesigned the hanging garden with geometric flower beds, bosso hedges, roses and a stone well. You can see it from the outside.

Infernot

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The infernot is a small underground chamber excavated in the Stone by Cantons, without light or ventilation, usually reached from a cellar and used to store the wine in bottle at constant temperature and humidity.
