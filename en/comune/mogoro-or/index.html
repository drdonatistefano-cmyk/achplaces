What to see in Mogoro

Country on the southern slopes of Monte Arci, known for the archaeological area of Cuccurada, for the churches of San Bernardino and Santa Maria Carcaxia and for the Fair of the artistic handicrafts of Sardinia, which is held every summer since 1961.

Area archeologica di Cuccurada

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Pre- and protohistoric fortress on the edge of the plateau of Sa Struvina: a protonuraghe in corridor of the age of the ancient and middle Bronze (about XX-XIV century BC), with a tholos cell intact, was incorporated in the XIV-XIII century BC in a four-tower quadrilobed bastion around a large courtyard, where huts built inside the walls were dug, case without comparison in the nuragic world. There is also a megalithic wall at the end of the third millennium B.C.; in the late Roman age the site was re-used for pagan, Christian and Jewish cults. Guided tours from the park guides, with information center; views of the Rio Mogoro valley, the Costa Verde and the Campidano.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Parrocchiale di San Bernardino da Siena

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

At the centre of the village, late-Romanesque with Baroque additions, with a single nave with frescoes of the life of the saint; it houses a relic linked to a Eucharistic miracle of 1604.

  • Where Centro storico, Mogoro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Carcaxia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Perhaps of the year 1000 and repeatedly rebuilt, the last in 1921, in white stone and basalt; it rises in the site of a medieval village destroyed by Saracen raids in the fifteenth century, among vineyards and olive groves.

  • Where Campagna di Mogoro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built at the beginning of the fourteenth century in Romanesque-Gothic style, it belonged to a Carmelite convent until 1855; in the seventeenth-century convent there is an archaeological museum for the findings of Cuccurada.
