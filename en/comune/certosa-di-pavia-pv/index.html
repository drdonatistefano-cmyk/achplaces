What to see in Certosa di Pavia

The municipality takes its name from the Certosa founded in 1396 by Gian Galeazzo Visconti as a mausoleum of the Milanese dynasty, masterpiece between Gothic and Renaissance, with the museum set up in the Ducal Palace; an educational itinerary in the Visconteo Park connects it to the Visconti Castle of Pavia and next passes the cycle path of the Naviglio Pavese between Milan and Pavia.

Certosa di Pavia, chiesa e chiostri

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry

Wanted by Gian Galeazzo Visconti, who laid the foundation stone on 27 August 1396, as a dynastic mausoleum to dissolve a vow of his wife Catherine; she was entrusted over time to Carthusians, Cistercians and Benedictines. The marble façade of Carrara, serpentine and porphyry has at the base medallions with heads of ancient characters and, higher up, stories of Christ and the Old Testament. The three-nave church, inspired by the Gothic Cathedral of Milan, has starry sky vaults, fourteen chapels with works by Perugino and Guercino, the funeral monument of Gian Galeazzo and that of Ludovico il Moro and Beatrice d'Este del Solari; next to the small cloister and the large cloister (1472). The €10 ticket replaced the free offer to the monastic community; from Tuesday to Sunday, winter (25/10/2026-25/3/2027) 10-16, summer 10-18, last entry 30 minutes before; online tickets to Museitaliani or on site.

  • Where Viale del Monumento 4, Certosa di Pavia
  • Entry €10, planned reductions; free on the first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website museilombardia.cultura.gov.it

Sources: visitpavia.com · in-lombardia.it · italia.it · museilombardia.cultura.gov.it

Museo della Certosa (Palazzo Ducale)

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the Ducal Palace, summer residence of the Visconti-Sforza with facade rebuilt in 1625 by Francesco Maria Richini; set up by Luca Beltrami in 1911, it is permanently open since 2008. On the ground floor the Gipsoteca with about 200 casts of facade reliefs and cloisters; on the first floor the Pinacoteca with fourteenth-century frescoes by Bernardino de Rossi, the Madonna enthroned by Bartolomeo Montagna (1490 circa), the Santi Ambrogio and Martino attributed to Luini, the form of the Flagellation by Antonio Mantegazza and the Studiolo frescoed in the sixteenth century with the Sogno di Costantino. From January 2, 2026 open from Tuesday to Sunday 10-16, closed on Monday.

  • Where Viale del Monumento 4, Certosa di Pavia
  • Entry Included in the ticket of the complex (€10)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museilombardia.cultura.gov.it · visitpavia.com · italia.it · in-lombardia.it

Parco Visconteo

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

Built by the Visconti together with the Castle of Pavia between 1360 and 1366 as a place of delight for the recreations of the court and hunting reserve, on a flat, fertile and rich of water; of large size, it was divided into old park and new park and surrounded by walls, with woods, wild and exotic animals and fields of rye, wheat and vine, and was taken as a model for the royal parks of Europe until the eighteenth century. Today a didactic itinerary curated by the Civic Museums of Pavia crosses the park connecting the Monument of the Certosa to the Castle Visconteo.

  • Where Tra la Certosa di Pavia e il Castello Visconteo di Pavia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitpavia.com · comunecertosadipavia.it · italia.it

Naviglio Pavese e ciclabile Milano-Pavia

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Naviglio Pavese is part of the system of canals built between the XII and the XIX century to make these lands more fertile; along the lift runs the cycle route Milan-Pavia, about 32 km (about 1 hour and 45 minutes, 149 m of difference in altitude, bottom to 90% asphalt), which meets at regular intervals the basins used to bring the boats back from the Po towards Milan and, after Binasco, touches the Certosa before arriving to Pavia.

  • Where Alzaia del Naviglio Pavese, Certosa di Pavia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it
