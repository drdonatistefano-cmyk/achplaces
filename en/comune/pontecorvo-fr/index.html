What to see in Pontecorvo

Cittadina sul Liri 125 km from Rome, born around the castle of the Longobard Rodoaldo (about 860): to see the Basilica of San Bartolomeo with the treasure in the crypt, the walls with Porta Santo Stefano, the Museo delle Battaglie on Mount Leuci and the Chapel of San Giovanni Appears.

7 places to visit in Pontecorvo, in the province of Frosinone (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Basilica Cattedrale di San Bartolomeo e Torre di Rodoaldo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Built in the 11th century on the ruins of the castle, next to the Tower of Rodoaldo then transformed into a bell tower; it conserved frescoes attributed to the Cavalier d'Arpino and the Baciccia, but was almost completely destroyed in the Second World War and rebuilt in Romanesque style.

  • Where Centro storico, Pontecorvo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website basilicasanbartolomeo.net

Sources: basilicasanbartolomeo.net · visitlazio.com

Museo Diocesano e Tesoro di San Bartolomeo

Museums · Old masters

In the crypt of the Basilica of St. Bartholomew there is the Diocesan Museum and the treasure of St. Bartholomew.

  • Where Basilica di San Bartolomeo, Pontecorvo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Castello, mura medievali e Porta Santo Stefano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

The castle and tower were erected around 860 by the Longobard king Rodoaldo near the bridge over the Liri, which dominated already in the IX century; the high walls that surrounded the village are visible for about 300 meters, with the Porta Santo Stefano in the homonymous district.

  • Where Quartiere Santo Stefano, Pontecorvo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Museo delle Battaglie

Museums · City history

On Mount Leuci, established in 2004, exhibits war finds, photos, vintage newspapers and documents on the country's attacks in World War II.

  • Where Monte Leuci, Pontecorvo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Museo della Cultura Agricola e Popolare del Tabacco

Museums · Themed

Five sections on tobacco cultivation, processing and production and on the festivals and cults related to this crop, plus the section "Pontecorvo" and the history dedicated to tobacconists and smuggling.

  • Where Pontecorvo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Cappella di San Giovanni Appare

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

On the shore of Liri, where in 1137 St. John the Baptist appeared to the farmer Giovanni Mele; the sanctuary was built by the archpriest Grimoaldo. Every year, on the second Sunday of May, there comes a procession from the cathedral of St. Bartholomew.

  • Where Riva del Liri, Pontecorvo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Big Bench n. 261 al Parco di Monte Menola

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The giant bench no. 261 in the Monte Menola Park offers views of the abbey of Montecassino, the lower valley of Liri, the area of Cassino and southern Lazio.

  • Where Parco di Monte Menola, Pontecorvo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
