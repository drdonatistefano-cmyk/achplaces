What to see in Serramonacesca

Small village of shepherds and peasants at 290 meters on the northern slopes of the Majella, above the valley of Alento, born around the Longobard castle of Polegra and linked to the Benedictine abbey of San Liberatore in Majella, from which it takes its name (Terra dei monaci) .

Abbazia di San Liberatore a Majella

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Benedictine monastery from legendary origins (a tradition ties it to the victory of Charlemagne over the Longobards of 781), with community attested around 884; severely damaged by the earthquake of 990, it was rebuilt from the abbot Theobaldo. Façade with three gates (the age of the abbot Desire, the 11th century, and Angevin), three apses, interior with three naves with rebuilt ambo decorated with reliefs of the 12th century and mosaic floor of 1275; the bell tower is among the surviving parts of the monastery and houses the information center of the Park. Abandoned in the nineteenth century, it was recovered from 1958-59.

Complesso rupestre di San Liberatore

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Rock monument hidden by vegetation under a rock wall, about 20 meters from the road to the abbey (335 m): pediment right by two columns, central niche and two cells, one with long side seat and the other square covered in barrels. It is believed to be the work of the monks of San Liberatore, used as graves or hermit cells.

  • Where Strada Serramonacesca-San Liberatore a Majella
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcomajella.it · italia.it

Torre di Polegro e tombe rupestri

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Itinerary of grade E (about 2 hours and 30, 210 m of difference in altitude) that from the abbey goes up the valley of the Alento, among high medieval rock tombs dug in the rock near the river, up to the tower, panoramic point on the valley and the plateaus of the Maielletta; the tower is what remains of the castle of Polegra, the original nucleus of the village.

Eremo di Sant'Onofrio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

We will be under a rocky rock at about 725 meters in the Fosso di Sant'Onofrio, probably linked to the monastery of San Liberatore; church rebuilt in 1948 with the statue of the saint and, behind the altar, the Culla di Sant'Onofrio, bed excavated in the cliff; on the lower floor two rooms turned to barrels. It is reached on foot from Brecciarola district with the path S (the path of the Spirit) and on the evening of June 11 the fire of Sant'Onofrio is lit and the procession is done on the 12th.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Castel Menardo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

An ancient medieval stronghold that dominates the valley of Alento, of which the defensive front remains; from the top the look reaches the Gran Sasso and the Adriatic.

Area faunistica del Capriolo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry
