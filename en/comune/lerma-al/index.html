What to see in Lerma

Borgo dell'Alto Monferrato mentioned since 1184 as "Lelma" ("Lelma"), a fief of Spinola from the 15th to the 17th century: on a spur above the stream Piota the fifteenth-century castle and the cottage, the parish church with the table of Barnaba da Modena, outside the village the Romanesque parish church of San Giovanni al Piano frescoed by the "Maestro di Lerma" and the sanctuary of the Rocchette on the salt road.

Castello di Lerma

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built by Luca Spinola at the end of the fifteenth century on a 12th century complex, it stands on a tuff cliff above the Piota: irregular pentagonal plan, transition building between fortress and mansion, with double windows, slits under the windows, square tower towards the village, large Spinola coat of arms painted on the east side and fifteenth-century triangular courtyard with arches and stone columns. Inside paintings, antique furniture and a gallery of coats of arms; it is still owned by Spinola. A legend wants three gold roses hidden in the courtyard left in 1565 by Isabella Corvalan.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Borgo e ricetto di Lerma

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village, on a ridge between two overhangs, was almost impregnable: you enter from an arched door, where once there was the drawbridge, in the square between castle and church. The original nucleus, the Herma, is a shelter on spurs with two accesses and a regular system of comb streets.

  • Where Centro storico, Lerma
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: comune.lerma.al.it · alexala.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The fifteenth-century, in the castle square, uses as an apse a medieval cylindrical tower. It preserves the table of the Virgin with Child of Barnabas of Modena (active 1361-1383), already in the sanctuary of the Rocchette, an apse fresco of the Baptism of Jesus of 1608 attributed to the school of Giambattista Paggi with the feudal Agostino and Cecilia Spinola, sixteenth-century paintings and a crucifix and a wooden Madonna of the workshop of Anton Maria Maragliano. Restored in 2008.

  • Where Piazzetta del castello, Lerma
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: comune.lerma.al.it · alexala.it

Pieve di San Giovanni Battista al Piano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church outside the walled village, probably rest of the ancient town of Rondinaria destroyed in 1166, was parish until the sixteenth century and today is a cemetery chapel: hut roof, bell tower, semicircular apse. Inside the cycle of the Stories of the Passion of the Master of Lerma, with St. Christopher on the façade, Christ in almond with the Tetramorph in the basin and the twelve prophets. You can reach it with a short path from the old town (about 20 minutes walk from the accommodation), starting from Palazzo Baldo, home of the Park.

  • Where Cimitero, Lerma
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: comune.lerma.al.it · alexala.it · areeprotetteappenninopiemontese.it

Santuario di Nostra Signora delle Grazie (delle Rocchette)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Built in the 13th century on overhangs of pudding as a hospice of the monastery of Santa Maria di Banno along the salt road between Alto Monferrato and Liguria, it was expanded by Spinola between the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries. Along the road the newsstands of a Via Crucis; the last Sunday of May there is held the New Festival. It is reached by a side road before the bridge over Piota, on the road between Tagliolo and Lerma.

  • Where Strada Tagliolo-Lerma, prima del ponte sul Piota, Lerma
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it
