What to see in Subiaco

The cradle of Benedictine monasticism in the valley of Aniene: the Sacro Speco dug in the rock of Monte Talio and the monastery of Santa Scolastica, where in 1465 the first printing house of Italy was born. Above the village, the Abbey Rocca; around, the Parco dei Monti Simbruini and Monte Livata.

12 places to visit in Subiaco, in the province of Rome (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Monastero di San Benedetto – Sacro Speco

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built from the end of the 12th century on the side of Mount Taleo in custody of the cave where St. Benedict lived as a hermit for three years: two churches entirely painted over, the statue of the saint in prayer by Antonio Raggi, pupil of Bernini (1657), and in the chapel of St. Gregory the oldest known portrait of St. Francis, painted when he was still alive, without aureole or stigmata. Petrarch called it the threshold of Paradise.

  • Where Largo San Benedetto 1, Subiaco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: direzioneregionalemuseilazio.cultura.gov.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlazio.com

Monastero di Santa Scolastica

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Founded by Saint Benedict around 520, it is the oldest existing Benedictine monastery and the only survivor of the thirteen of the valley: three cloisters (cosmatesque of the thirteenth century, Gothic of the fourteenth century, Renaissance), Romanesque bell tower and neoclassical church of Giacomo Quarenghi (1770). The tour is guided and lasts about an hour.

  • Where Via dei Monasteri 22, Subiaco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: direzioneregionalemuseilazio.cultura.gov.it · italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlazio.com

Biblioteca Statale del Monumento Nazionale di Santa Scolastica

Museums · Themed

In the monastery, where in 1465 the German clerics Pannartz and Sweynheym implanted the first typography of Italy: it holds codes, parchments and the first incunabula printed in Italy, with about 100,000 volumes. Access to the library requires the age and identity document.

  • Where Via dei Monasteri 22, Subiaco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: biblioteche.cultura.gov.it · direzioneregionalemuseilazio.cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Museo Luigi Ceselli

Museums · Archaeological

In the monastery of Santa Scolastica, the archaeological and anthropological collection collected by Luigi Ceselli since 1837: paleontological, prehistoric, pre-Roman and Roman sections and the materials of the excavations of the villa of Nerone.

  • Where Piazzale Santa Scolastica, Subiaco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Rocca abbaziale (Rocca dei Borgia)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Founded in the 11th century by the abbot Giovanni V of Santa Scolastica, since 1456 it was the residence of the Commendator Abbots, including Rodrigo Borgia, then Pope Alexander VI; it was restored in the mid-eighteenth century by Giovannangelo Braschi, then Pope Pius VI. It dominates the medieval village.

  • Where Centro storico, Subiaco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlazio.com

Convento e chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1327 on the chapel donated to Saint Francis when he visited the Sacred Speco in 1223: on the high altar the wooden triptych of Antoniazzo Romano (1467) and in a chapel frescoes of the Sodom circle and a Nativity attributed to the Pinturicchio or his school.

  • Where Subiaco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlazio.com

Chiesa di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

On a rocky spur almost overhanging the river, it is the reconstruction of the first parish church, the oldest religious building in the territory; around it was born the first nucleus of Subiaco.

  • Where Subiaco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Borgo medievale e Piazzetta della Pietra Sprecata

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval center is surrounded by the Fortress; the square is born from the encounter of irregular and winding alleys. On the left bank of the Aniene, the Village of the Opifici, the oldest part, with the ancient artisan workshops, and the triumphal Arch wanted by Pius VI.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Monte Livata e Parco dei Monti Simbruini

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Over 1,400 metres, between beech and oak woods of the Regional Natural Park of the Simbruini Mountains: in winter downhill and cross-country trails in La Bandita, Campo dell'Osso and Fossa dell'Acero, in summer a network of trails; in autumn the woods are yellow and red.

  • Where Monte Livata, Subiaco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
