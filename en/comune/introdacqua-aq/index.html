Chiesa Madre della SS. Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1474 and 1510, with three naves, with Baroque altar, Renaissance frescoes, an eighteenth-century painting of San Biagio and the relics of the patron San Feliciano; on the right of the southern portal a frescoed San Cristoforo, today under the road level for a flood. The 17th century bell tower in local stone.

  • Where Introdacqua
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Palazzo Trasmondi e Porte della terra

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The Palazzo del Marquis, called Trasmondi by the last feudal lords, dates back to the fifteenth century and lies in the center of the village; the north gate, the most interesting of the two doors of the earth, was placed on the palace, which once closed at night or in case of danger.

  • Where Centro storico, Introdacqua
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Fontavecchia

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

A rectangular stone tank with the coat of arms of the village and date 1706; for a long time it was the only water source for the inhabitants, who otherwise had to go to the witch's source.

Monte Genzana

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hiking destination known for its gentian blooms; the Forca Ristoppia hut, about 1,800 meters away, is reached from the valley of Sant'Antonio. From up there you can see the valley of Sulmona, Morrone, Maiella, the Plan of the Cinquemiglia, Fucino, Sirente and Gran Sasso.

  • Where Valle di Sant'Antonio, Introdacqua
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: abruzzoturismo.it · italia.it
