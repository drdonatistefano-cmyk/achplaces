What to see in Brendola

The municipality presents Brendola as the gate of the Berici at the foot of the Berici Hills. It is the birthplace of Saint Maria Bertilla Boscardin, whose peasant house is a destination of devotion, and the Town Hall of Villa Piovene hosts a permanent exhibition of archaeological finds.

Casa natale di Santa Maria Bertilla Boscardin

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

A peasant house where the saint was born in Brendola in 1888 and died in Treviso in 1922, she spent her first youth; next door a small church was built where many faithful come to pray. The house is preserved almost intact, with the small doors, beds and utensils of the peasant life of the time and some civil and religious clothes of the saint; numerous ex-votes, especially for intercessions in favour of children, which the saint assisted as a nurse. Beatified by Pius XII in 1952 and canonized by John XXIII on May 11, 1961, the place is cared for by the Dorotean Sisters.

  • Where Via S. Bertilla 71, Brendola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.brendola.vi.it

Sources: vicenzae.org · comune.brendola.vi.it

Esposizione archeologica nel Municipio di Villa Piovene

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

The large council hall of the Town Hall, in Villa Piovene, hosts a permanent exhibition of archaeological finds of the territory, mostly ceramics, from the Bronze Age to the late imperial age, arranged in four large windows. Free entrance during the opening hours of the Town Hall.

  • Where Piazza Marconi 1 (Villa Piovene, Municipio), Brendola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.brendola.vi.it

Sources: archeoveneto.beniculturali.unipd.it · comune.brendola.vi.it
