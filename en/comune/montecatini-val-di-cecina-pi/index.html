What to see in Montecatini Val di Cecina

Medieval village of the Alta Val di Cecina linked to the copper mine of Camporciano, active until 1907 and in the nineteenth century the largest in Europe, today Museo delle Miniere. In the village the Belforti Tower, the fourteenth-century Palazzo Pretorio with the Documentation Centre of the museum and the church of San Biagio; in the surroundings the medieval villages of La Sassa and Querceto and the route of the old railway of the mines.

Museo delle Miniere

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Site of industrial archaeology about one kilometer from the village, in the copper mine of Camporciano, active until 1907: in the nineteenth century it was the largest copper mine in Europe, with ten levels and about 35 km of tunnels, and hence took the name of the Montedison. You can visit mining facilities, laverie, offices, part of the 19th century galleries, the Alfredo Well Tower with the original hoist of the elevator, from which the miners descended for more than 300 meters, and the church of Santa Barbara, patron saint of the miners. The descent, a staircase of about 100 meters, leads to the 4th level. Tickets and guided tours can be booked online on the museum website.

  • Where SP32 5, Montecatini Val di Cecina
  • Entry €10, children €7
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.museodelleminieremontecatini.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: museodelleminieremontecatini.it · terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Palazzo Pretorio e Centro Documentazione del Museo della Miniera

Museums · City history

The 14th-century Palazzo Pretorio, in the historic centre, 3 km from the mine, houses the Documentation Centre of the Museum of Mines. The first floor is dedicated to the copper mine of Camporciano, the second to the mineral richness of the valley, with salt on copper, salt, alabaster, geothermal and limedonia.

  • Where Centro storico, Montecatini Val di Cecina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: terredipisa.it

Torre Belforti

History and architecture · Towers

Tower of the historic center, today privately owned, with very thick walls in black stone. During World War II it was used as a refuge from the bombings.

private tower, guided tours only on scheduled dates (September 2026)

Percorso della vecchia ferrovia delle miniere

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A 17 km walk along the track of the mine railway, which runs from 1872 to 1928, connecting Casino di Terra with the station of Villetta, where lignite was loaded. From the municipal territory you can also walk to the village of Miemo with the route ... between the giants of the wind and the giants of the woods, that crosses the Wind Park.

  • Where Casino di Terra - Villetta, Montecatini Val di Cecina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: terredipisa.it
