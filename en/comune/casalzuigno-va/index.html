What to see in Casalzuigno

Valcuvia municipality a few kilometers from Lake Maggiore, with the sixteenth-century Villa Della Porta Bozzolo del FAI and its terrace garden, and on the hills the village of Arcumeggia, the painted village, transformed since 1956 into an open-air fresco gallery.

Villa Della Porta Bozzolo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Country residence of the sixteenth century transformed in the eighteenth century into a noble villa of delight, with U-shaped structure around the courtyard of honour, Rococo and Baroque decorations, trompe-l'oeil and frescoed vaults; in the salon and gallery of the noble piano paintings of the Milanese Salvione and Bosso. The Italian garden rises on four terraces connected by stairs with balustrades, statues and fountains, up to the lawn of the theater closed by an eighteenth-century fish pond; in the park also the Filanda. Bene del FAI, open from March to November with variable times (in autumn from Wednesday to Sunday 10-17).

  • Where Viale Camillo Bozzolo 5, Casalzuigno
  • Entry €12, reduced €5 (6-18 years and students up to 25), free FAI membership
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website fondoambiente.it
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: fondoambiente.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Galleria all'aperto dell'affresco di Arcumeggia

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fraction about 570 meters, three kilometers from the center, which since 1956, on the initiative of the Provincial Tourist Board of Varese and against the depopulation of the mountain, has become a painted country: rare open-air museum dedicated to the fresco, with works by Aligi Sassu, Ernesto Treccani, Gianni Dova, Achille Funi, Giuseppe Migneco, Aldo Carpi and Remo Brindisi on the facades of the houses and the 14 stations of the Via Crucis at the church of Sant'Ambrogio. Free and free visit.

  • Where Via Sant'Antonio, Arcumeggia, Casalzuigno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it

Casa del Pittore

Museums · Modern art · Viewpoint

Built in 1956-1957 to host the artists involved in the frescoes of Arcumeggia and home to the school of frescoes (summer courses since 1961), it preserves sketches, cardboards, tiles, sketches and ceramics that document the birth of the works; it has a large panoramic terrace on the valley and Lake Maggiore. Owned by the Province of Varese, it hosts exhibitions and guided tours.

  • Where Via Usellini 6, Arcumeggia, Casalzuigno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it
