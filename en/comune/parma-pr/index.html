What to see in Parma

Duke's city of Farnese and Bourbon, UNESCO Creative City for Gastronomy since 2015. A unique ticket from the Civic Museums opens six museums, including the Chamber of São Paulo and the house of Toscanini.

29 places to visit in Parma, in the province of Parma (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers 17 of the 29 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Complesso monumentale della Pilotta

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
