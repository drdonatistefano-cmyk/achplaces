What to see in Caraglio

Porta della Valle Grana, the land of Castelmagno DOP and the garlic of Caraglio garrison Slow Food; it houses the Red Filatoio of 1676-1678, one of the oldest examples of industrial archaeology in Europe, a medieval historic centre and the sanctuary on the site of the ancient castle.

Filatoio Rosso – Museo del Setificio Piemontese

Museums · Themed

Built between 1676 and 1678 by the Serico entrepreneur Giò Gerolamo Galleani and reached almost intact until today, it is one of the oldest examples of industrial archaeology in Europe: a building with the appearance of an almost fortified noble residence, with two courtyards, cylindrical turrets at the corners and decorations in stucco and terracotta. The guided tours show the torccioi rebuilt in operation, the spinner, the spinning mill and some rooms; it hosts temporary exhibitions and the info point of the Gate of Valle.

  • Where Via Matteotti 40, Caraglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.filatoiocaraglio.it

Sources: filatoiocaraglio.it · visitcuneese.it · comune.caraglio.cn.it:443

Ex convento dei Cappuccini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The Capuchin friars settled in Caraglio in 1611, restored the churches in ruins and distinguished themselves during the plague of 1630. The former convent, renovated, is the seat of the Cultural Association Marcovaldo; next to the former Romanesque church of Saints Peter and Paul with wooden altar, under restoration.

The MiC card indicates closed every day; former church of the SS. Pietro e Paolo in phase of restoration according to the Municipality

  • Where Via Cappuccini 29, Caraglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website marcovaldo.it

Sources: marcovaldo.it · comune.caraglio.cn.it · comune.caraglio.cn.it:443 · cultura.gov.it · visitcuneese.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint
