What to see in Magliano de' Marsi

Santa Maria in Valle Porclaneta

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

What remains of a Benedictine ward isolated between secular oaks at the slopes of Velino, in the Sirente-Velino Regional Park: the first news dates back to the middle of the 11th century, when the monastery passed under Montecassino. It is one of the most important examples of Abruzzi Romanesque, with Arabic-Hispanic, Byzantine and Longobard influences; it preserves the ambo and ciborium of the 12th century (1150) of Nicodemus and Roberto masters, the iconostasis with plutei decorated with griffins, eagles and lions and a very rare wooden architrave, frescoes of the fifteenth century and the external apse rebuilt in polygonal form in the thirteenth century. Borgo di Rosciolo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the top of a hill to the south of Velino, it appears in 1080 when its church is registered near Montecassino; in 1485 it received defensive tower and walls designed by the architect Berardino Clario, whose name is engraved on the main door. There are still sections of walls, buttresses and doors, and in the oldest spontaneous architecture of the fifteenth century; in the center the mother church of Santa Maria delle Grazie and, just before the village, the rural church of San Sebastiano.

Centro storico di Magliano

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village rises on an isolated hill below Mount Velino, with a wide view of the Palentine Fields; the writer Edward Lear described it. In the route between the churches of the capital, from the church of Saint Lucia, you will find the small church of Our Lady of Loreto, of the fourteenth century, with Marian frescoes, the convent and the church of Saint Dominic, of the fifteenth century, and the shrine of the fallen inaugurated in 1932.
