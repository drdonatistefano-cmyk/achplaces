What to see in Valfurva

Val d'Alta Valtellina valley, crossed by the Frodolfo stream and included largely in the Stelvio National Park, with the ski resort of Santa Caterina, the Forni glacier, the Val Zebrù, the Passo Gavia, ancient churches such as San Rocco di Uzza and the frescoed church of Teregua, and the Vallivo Museum in Sant'Antonio.

8 places to visit in Valfurva, in the province of Sondrio (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santa Caterina Valfurva

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Country at 1,800 meters in the Stelvio National Park, renowned summer and winter station that hosted World Cup Ski World Cup World Championships; starting point for hiking and biking among wooden huts to the glaciers and birthplace of Deborah Compagnoni, to which is dedicated a 3.5 km track exposed to the north.

  • Where Santa Caterina, Valfurva
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: bormio.eu · valtellina.it · in-lombardia.it

Ghiacciaio dei Forni e sentiero glaciologico

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest glacier in the Stelvio National Park, in the Ortles-Cevedale group, is reached from Rifugio Forni: the glaciological path passes through the remains of fortresses of the First World War and, in the high path, crosses two Tibetan bridges to the mouths from which flows the melting water.

  • Where Località Forni, Valfurva
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: bormio.eu · valtellina.it · in-lombardia.it

Val Zebrù

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Side valley frequented by ungulates and gipeto, reachable on foot or by bike from Niblogo along the road to the bridge of Peceneccia.

  • Where Niblogo, Valfurva
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: valtellina.it · in-lombardia.it

Passo Gavia

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valico at 2,618 meters open only in summer, entered the history of the Giro d'Italia in 1960 and several times Cima Coppi; on top high altitude landscape and a lake.

  • Where Strada del Passo Gavia, Valfurva
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: bormio.eu · in-lombardia.it

Chiesetta di Teregua

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small sixteenth-century church at 1,375 meters with sailing bell tower; presbytery and apse have frescoes dating back to 1564, and the Trinity returns to the bezel of the façade, to the altar polyptych and to the ceiling.

  • Where Teregua, Valfurva
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: bormio.eu · in-lombardia.it

Chiese di Uzza: San Rocco e Beata Vergine della Misericordia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Uzza rise the church of San Rocco, probably of 777 and rebuilt in 1491, and the octagonal church of the Blessed Virgin of Mercy, of the early eighteenth century.

  • Where Uzza, Valfurva
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Museo Vallivo Mario Testorelli

Museums · Themed

The civic museum of the valley in Sant'Antonio, with a reconstruction of the peasant life; in the village there is also a visitor center of the Park with virtual reality viewers. Visits during the opening hours or by reservation for guided tours.

  • Where Piazza Forba, Sant'Antonio, Valfurva
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.museovalfurva.it

Sources: museovalfurva.it · comune.valfurva.so.it · valtellina.it · in-lombardia.it
