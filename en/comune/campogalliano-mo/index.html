What to see in Campogalliano

Common at the gates of Modena, documented by 962 and then feud of the Estensi of San Martino in Rio, known for over a century for the balance industry: the Museum of Libra, the Curiel lakes and the reserve of the Secchia expansion boxes, the sanctuary of Sassola and the Clock Tower.

7 places to visit in Campogalliano, in the province of Modena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Bilancia

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The only museum of this kind in Italy, founded in 1989 in a territory that produces scales since 1860: over 9,000 objects between scales, swing and stade, with posters, catalogues and vintage photos, from the Middle Ages to today. The route includes the Labyrinth of phenomena (we weigh with the gravity of the Moon and Jupiter), the Gallery of Crafts, the Canal of History and the Gallery of the Swings and the great staders for Parmigiano Reggiano, barrels and salt. Saturday, Sunday and holidays 10-12.30 and 15.30-19 (15-18.30 with sunshine); weekdays by reservation.

  • Where Corso Garibaldi 34/A, 41011 Campogalliano (MO)
  • Entry €5, reduced €2,50, family €5 (including guided tour)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodellabilancia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: museodellabilancia.it · visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Laghi Curiel

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lakes born from ancient gravel quarries filled with groundwater, used for recreational and sporting activities; the wetlands around host more than a hundred species of birds.

  • Where Campogalliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it

Riserva naturale Casse di espansione del Secchia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Area built to contain the Secchia and today protected nature reserve, next to the Curiel lakes.

  • Where Campogalliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Giardino dei Frutti Antichi

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Free entry

Educational fruit grove on the grounds of the Parks Authority, between the Curiel lakes and the Secchia expansion box, with about sixty local varieties of fruit, vines and native trees arranged as an Emilian plantation; next to the municipal vinegar mill of Casa Berselli.

  • Where Via Albone 14, Campogalliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Santuario della Beata Vergine della Sassola

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Built in 1745 to preserve an image of the Madonna painted on a table, probably seventeenth century, found among the stones on the banks of the Secchia, from which the name; simple facade, inside with a single nave and a collection of ex-vote from the eighteenth century to today.

  • Where Campogalliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it

Piazza Castello, Torre dell'Orologio e Parco delle Montagnole

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Piazza Castello, the original heart of the village surrounded in the Middle Ages by a moat, with the 17th century Clock Tower; next to the Parco delle Montagnole it preserves underground traces of the medieval fortification and the subsequent feudal residence. Not far from the contemporary Piazza della Libra.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Campogalliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it

Ponte della Barchetta

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

Modern bridge with a large iron suspended arch of 80 meters, reserved for pedestrians, cyclists and mopeds, that connect Modena and Campogalliano.

  • Where Campogalliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it
