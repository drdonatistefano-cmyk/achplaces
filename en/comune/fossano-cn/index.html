What to see in Fossano

The city of the Acajas, dominated by the fourteenth-century Castle of the Princes of Acaja with its four towers; along the portico via Roma you will find the neoclassical cathedral of Mario Ludovico Quarini and the baroque church of the Battuti Rossi by Francesco Gallo, while panoramic avenues overlooking the ancient walls overlook the valley of Stura and the Alps.

Castello dei Principi d'Acaja

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It was built by Filippo I d'Acaja between 1324 and 1332 as a fortress and was then transformed by the Savoys into a residence: Bona di Savoia and Cristina di Francia stayed there and in November 1562 Emanuele Filiberto signed the Treaty of Fossano; later it was the prison of the Waldenses and barracks. Four corner towers joined by crenellated walls, courtyard with loggia with fifteenth-century columns, the Room of the Grotesques frescoed by the Flemish Giovanni Caracca and a panoramic tower with views of the Monviso; in the sixteenth-century loggias the civic library (over 150,000 volumes). Guided tours only, from Wednesday to Sunday at 11 am and at 3 pm (reservation recommended); the route has about one hundred steep steps to spiral without handrail.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Cattedrale di Santa Maria e San Giovenale

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Consecrated on September 25, 1791 on the project of the Chierese Mario Ludovico Quarini, it stands on the site of the thirteenth-century collegiate; neoclassical façade with Corinthian columns and interior decoration largely nineteenth-century, with paintings by the Flemish Claret, wooden choir and major altar with silver busts of doctors of the Church. The bell tower dates back to the work of the late fourteenth century promoted by Blessed Oddino Barotti, with the seventeenth-century octagonal spire of Giovenale Boetto; the late Dogotic reliquary bust of San Giovenale is exhibited on the first Sunday of May.

  • Where Via Roma 97, Fossano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · visitfossano.it

Chiesa della SS. Trinità (Battuti Rossi)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1730 and consecrated in 1739 for the Confraternita dei Battuti Rossi, it is the work of Monregalese Francesco Gallo, who had also built the adjoining hospital; Greek cross plan, squares of the brothers Pozzo, frescoes by Michele Antonio Milocco, stuccos by Beltramelli and Barelli and twelve wooden lacquered matrons from which the sheltered follow the functions. Open on Sundays from 15 to 18.30.

  • Where Via dell'Ospedale 2, Fossano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · visitfossano.it

Chiesa di San Filippo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

What remains of the complex of the Oratorians in Borgo Vecchio, rebuilt between 1706 and 1713; Baroque interior with stuccoes, polychrome wooden statues and illusionistic frescoes of the brothers Pozzo and Milocco, the canvas of the Visitation of Sebastiano Taricco (1682), Crucifix and Immaculate of the Luganese sculptor Carlo Giuseppe Plura. Guided tours by reservation at the Tourist Office.

  • Where Borgo Vecchio, Fossano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitfossano.it · visitcuneese.it

Mu-Sa Museo geologico Federico Sacco

Museums · Themed · Free entry

It tells of the geological origins of the province of Cuneo, the Alps and the Langhe with minerals, fossils of Langhe and Roero, finds of the fossil forest of Fossano and the Rolleri collection of minerals from all over the world; it is dedicated to the geologist of Casca Federico Sacco and occupies the ancient vault of the Cassa di Risparmio. Open on Sunday 15-18 (also on Saturdays from 15 April), free admission.

  • Where Via Roma, Fossano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitfossano.it · visitcuneese.it

Via Roma e Borgo Vecchio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Via Roma is the ancient heart of the city, with porticos, noble palaces and internal courtyards and the weekly market on Wednesday; at its extremes the church of Battuti Rossi and the Bastione del Salice, the rest of the walls that surrounded the city until the Napoleonic age. The Borgo Vecchio, an original nucleus, preserves medieval buildings, narrow alleys and low porticos.

  • Where Via Roma, Fossano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Viale Mellano e Viale Bianco

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Viale Mellano is a walk along the ancient walls that delimits the Old Town overlooking the valley of Stura; the White Avenue, returned to the city in 2012, follows the sixteenth-century wall from the Bastione del Salice to the Porta di San Martino, with traces of the Bastione dell'Annunziata and a panel that indicates names and heights of the peaks of the alpine arch.

  • Where Viale Bianco, Fossano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitfossano.it · visitcuneese.it

Sentiero del Pescatore

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

A 9 km ring path that starts from the centre, suitable for children; the most suggestive stretch passes under the railway bridge over the river Stura along a brick corridor.

  • Where Centro di Fossano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
