What to see in Monterchi

Border village on the hill above the Valtiberina, where Piero della Francesca painted the Madonna del Parto.

2 places to visit in Monterchi, in the province of Arezzo (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Madonna del Parto

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Since 1991 it has kept the fresco of the Madonna del Parto painted by Piero della Francesca for the church of Santa Maria a Momentana: the Virgin pregnant between two angels opening a pavilion.

  • Where Monterchi
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo delle Bilance

Museums · Themed

The collection of weights and scales considered the most important in Europe, with a weight of the fourteenth century.
