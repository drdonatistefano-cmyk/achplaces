What to see in Loro Piceno

Country on top of a hill between Macerata and San Ginesio, considered the home of cooked wine: historic center almost intact with the Brunforte Castle of the Lords of Loro, the Tower of Victory overlooking the Conero alla Maiella, the thirteenth-century Santa Maria di Piazza and the church of San Francesco with the exhibition of the tools of cooked wine.

5 places to visit in Loro Piceno, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village is located in the south of the Fiastra stream, with the historic centre almost intact, except for the area near Porta Pia near the castle, modified in the 1980s of the twentieth century; in the thirteenth century the free municipality entered the orbit of the castles of Fermo. Along the winding streets the noble houses retain architectural elements for pressing the grapes and boiling the must in large copper boilers.

  • Where Piazza Giacomo Matteotti, Loro Piceno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Castello Brunforte

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

In the upper part of the village, on the remains of a Roman castrum and above the ancient walls: it belonged to the Farfenses and was the residence of the Lords of Loro; the fortified nucleus included the palace of the priors and the palace of justice, which since 1692 hosted the Monastery of Corpus Domini.

  • Where Porta Pia, Loro Piceno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Torre della Vittoria

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

21 meters high tower from which the panorama ranges from Monte Conero to Maiella, from the Sibillini to the Adriatic.

Chiesa di San Francesco e Mostra permanente del vino cotto

Museums · Themed

The church is full of stuccoes and gilding; in the surroundings next to the cloister the permanent exhibition of equipment and tools for cooked wine, the drink for which the village organizes every August the Festival of cooked wine, born in 1948 as a feast of grapes.
