What to see in Mirto

Country of the Nebrodi at about 430 meters, mentioned as "Myirti" in a privilege of King Ruggero of 1134 and from 1643 principality of the Filangeri; in the Cupane Palace the Museum of Costume and Sicilian Fashion, while the churches retain a crucifix attributed to Friar Umile from Petralia and a Madonna of Giuseppe Gagini.

Museo del Costume e della Moda Siciliana

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the Palazzo Cupane, acquired and restored by the Municipality in 1990, the museum was created from the private collection of clothes and accessories by the architect Giuseppe Miraudo and was inaugurated in the years 1993-97. It preserves over a thousand pieces (1,500 according to the card) including dresses, even evening and wedding dresses, accessories, costumes and underwear of Sicilian fashion from the eighteenth to the early twentieth century; some pieces were exhibited in Brazil and the USA. Open Monday-Friday 9.30-12.30, Saturday and Sunday 16-19.

  • Where Via Ugo Bassi (ingresso da Via Cupane), Mirto
  • Entry €3,50, reduced €2,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.cittametropolitana.me.it

Sources: comune.mirto.me.it · cittametropolitana.me.it

Chiesa Madre, Santa Maria del Gesù e Chiesa del Crocifisso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Mother Church preserves a wooden crucifix of the seventeenth century attributed to Friar Umile of Petralia; the church of Santa Maria del Gesù houses a Madonna with the Child carved by Giuseppe Gagini; the church of the Crucifix has a valuable late-medieval portal. In the village there are also carved choirs and cornices in 18th century and 18th century golden wood, linked to the intense activity of wood carving lasting until the 18th century.

  • Where Mirto (ME)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.cittametropolitana.me.it

Sources: cittametropolitana.me.it
