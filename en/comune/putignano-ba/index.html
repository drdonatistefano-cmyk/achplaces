What to see in Putignano

A town of about 27,000 inhabitants on three hills 375 meters, less than 40 km from Bari, known for the Carnival as the oldest in Europe. The old town, a maze of alleys and squares, gathers around Piazza Plebiscito with the Palazzo del Balì and the Seat; in the countryside the Grotta del Trullo, karst cavity with stalactites and stalagmites of alabaster.

Centro storico e Piazza Plebiscito

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Defined the only historic center of Puglia where you can miss it completely, it is made of narrow streets and few, tiny squares. The heart is Piazza Plebiscito, surrounded by important buildings, including the Palazzo del Balì and, in front, the Sedile, once the seat of the University.

  • Where Piazza Plebiscito, Putignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · interregaismart.regione.puglia.it
