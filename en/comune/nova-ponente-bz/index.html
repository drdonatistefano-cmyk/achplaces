What to see in Nova Ponente

Municipality of Val d'Ega with the Baroque sanctuary of Pietralba, linked to the apparition of 1553 and visited by John Paul II in 1988, the Romanesque church of Sant'Elena frescoed around 1400 by the school of Bolzano and the museum of sacred art in the tower of Castel Thurn.

Santuario di Madonna di Pietralba

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

According to tradition in 1553 the peasant Leonhard Weißensteiner, who fell into a ravine, saw the Virgin appear and built her a chapel, then enlarged; the original chapel is still inside the Baroque building, with the piety of Our Lady of Sorrows, the high altar covered with gold and silver sheets, the frescoes of Adam Mölk's vault and the lateral altars of Pußjäger. On 24 August 1885 the statue was crowned in the presence of 15,000 faithful and in July 1988 Pope John Paul II went up there; outside the chapel of St. Pellegrino Laziosi and along the side entrances numerous ex-vote. Open 7:30-19.

Chiesetta di Sant'Elena

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church probably of the twelfth century, entirely frescoed around 1400 by masters of the school of Bolzano (reconsecrated in 1410), with paintings similar to those of the parish church of Terlano and of Saint Catherine in Aica di Fiè, perhaps commissioned by the nobles Niederthor, whose coat of arms re-emerged in 1885. Outside St. Helena, St. Christopher and the Crucifixion; inside the evangelists in the vault, the Christ judge in the apse, Cain and Abel and Creation on the triumphal arch, the stories of Jesus' childhood on the walls; altarpiece signed J. G. Plazer 1723. About 30 minutes walk from the Pfösl stop; the key is asked at the farm in front of the church.

Museo territoriale di Nova Ponente (Castel Thurn)

Museums · Old masters

In the 13th-century Romanesque tower of Castel Thurn, annexed to the current town hall, former residence and seat of judicial hearings around 1341, passed to the Municipality in 1911 that restored and opened it to the public. It exhibits sacred art: ancient altarpieces and consecrated tables of the sixteenth century, statues of saints of the first Baroque and religious paintings that tell the history of the Val d'Ega.
