What to see in Ferentillo

Valnerina municipality divided by the Black in the two villages of Precetto and Matterella, each with its own fortress: in Precetto the crypt of Santo Stefano with the famous mummies, in Matterella the church of Santa Maria; little to the north the Longobard Abbey of San Pietro in Valle, and around the Black River Park and the climbing walls.

Museo delle Mummie (cripta di Santo Stefano)

Museums · Themed

In the Romanesque crypt of the church of Santo Stefano, in Precetto: about twenty natural mummies discovered in 1805 after a Napoleonic edict on the exhumations, the oldest of about four centuries ago, the most recent of the nineteenth century; the conservation is due to the microorganisms of the soil and constant ventilation. The church, built over an older building, has three aisles, Baroque apse, the Martyrdom of Santo Stefano by Giuseppe Rosi (1759) and a crib frescoed by Pierino Cesarei (1595). Info: tel. 0744 780708.

Abbazia di San Pietro in Valle

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Benedictine Abbey of Longobard foundation, about five kilometers from Ferentillo along the Black, considered the most significant testimony of the Duchy of Spoleto: founded by Duke Faroldo II on the church initiated by the hermits John and Lazarus, then mausoleum. It preserves a votive stele, Roman fragments, several sarcophagi, a Longobard altar, remains of frescoes and a two-level cloister of the 12th century. Church and cloister are visited by contacting the parish; other parts house a hotel in a private historical residence. From Ferentillo a trail of 7.8 km to Gabbio, Lorino and Forca.

Chiesa di Santa Maria a Matterella

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Collegiate of the 13th century enlarged in the 15th century with three naves, with Romanesque bell tower with two-fold windows and frescoes of the Umbrian and Byzantine school, including figures of Jesus, St. Rocco, St. Anthony of Padua, St. Anthony Abbot and St. Lucia. A legend places the Holy Grail in it: the initials of the holy paintings would form the word "Grail."

  • Where Matterella, Ferentillo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Rocche di Precetto e Matterella

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The two fortifications that guard the villages on the banks of the Black: in Precetto a castle on the slope with triangular plan with square watchtower on top, in Matterella a square mast with cylindrical bastions.

  • Where Precetto e Matterella, Ferentillo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Castello di Umbriano

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry

An abandoned village in the woods at the abbey, with a square tower, bastions and an ogival Gothic gate; the fortification dates back to 890 to defend the abbey from the Saracens and was strengthened in the fifteenth century and 1570.

  • Where Presso San Pietro in Valle, Ferentillo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Parco fluviale del Nera

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Protected area managed by the Consortium of the Park established in 1995; in Collestatte is located the Documentation Centre of the Park, museum on flora, fauna and history. On the Black, rafting is practiced.
