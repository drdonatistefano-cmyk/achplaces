What to see in Pietrasanta

Historical capital of Versilia and marble, called "little Athens" for the artists who live there, including Botero and Mitoraj.

3 places to visit in Pietrasanta, in the province of Lucca (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 4 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Piazza del Duomo e Duomo di San Martino

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

The rectangular square with large contemporary sculptures and the Romanesque-Gothic cathedral of the fourteenth century, with the bell tower from the helical staircase.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Pietrasanta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa e convento di Sant'Agostino e Museo dei Bozzetti

Museums · Modern art

Gothic Church of the fourteenth century with nine painted altars; in the convent, with the frescoed cloister, the library and the Museo dei Bozzetti Pierluigi Gherardi with 700 models in plaster of sculptors from all over the world.

  • Where Via Sant'Agostino 1, Pietrasanta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo Archeologico Versiliese

Museums · Archaeological

In the Palazzo Moroni finds from prehistory to the Renaissance.

  • Where Palazzo Moroni, Pietrasanta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
