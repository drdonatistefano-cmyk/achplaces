What to see in Stradella

Centro dell'Oltrepò Pavese of probable Roman origin along the via Postojna, considered the cradle of the accordion: since 1876 44 factories, told in the Dallapè Museum; of the fortified city remains the Civic Tower in Piazza Vittorio Veneto, while on the hill of Montalino stands the Romanesque Basilica of San Marcello.

Museo civico della Fisarmonica «Mariano Dallapè»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Established in 1997 and inaugurated on 20 May 1999 in the presence of Paolo Conte, it tells the accordion industry that made Stradella famous in the world: the origins of the instrument and the 44 factories active in the city since 1876, when Mariano Dallapè opened his first laboratory. Three sections in five halls (historical context, didactic part on the instrument's operation, street models in chronological order) with the first instrument built by Dallapè, the precious Organfisa \liturgica \"the organ sound and the reconstruction of a craft workshop. Free admission on the first Sunday of the month; reservation recommended also to individuals.

  • Where Via Montebello 2 (Centro Culturale, Palazzo Garibaldi), Stradella
  • Entry €5, under 16 €2; with Naturalistic Museum €8
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.comune.stradella.pv.it

Sources: comune.stradella.pv.it · visitpavia.com · in-lombardia.it

Civico Museo naturalistico «Ferruccio Lombardi»

Museums · Science and university

Born as the Po Museum, for the finds and fossils returned from the river, it documents today the natural environment of the whole Oltrepò Pavese: geology with minerals, rocks and traces of gold of the Po, a small anthropological section with human and fossil remains of the Paleolithic and Neolithic, a large paleontological section with mammals of the Pleistocene found in the Po, plant and animal species of the territory, collections of butterflies and snails, an area on bees and silk bugs and reconstructions of environments and climates. A cumulative ticket with the Museum of Accordion (€8).

  • Where Via Montebello 2, Stradella
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: visitpavia.com · comune.stradella.pv.it · in-lombardia.it

Torre civica e piazza Vittorio Veneto

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Civic Tower, symbol of Stradella, is the only testimony still standing in the ancient fortified city, surrounded in 1300 by Bishop Guido of Langosco by brick walls about a mile long. The square occupies the area of the lower Visconti fortress, demolished to make room for the market of agricultural products, fine wine and textiles; next to the seventeenth-century Palazzo Isimbardi, seat of the town hall, and the 17th-century parish of Saints Nabore and Felice.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Veneto, Stradella
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · visitpavia.com

Basilica di San Marcello in Montalino

Places of worship · Basilicas · Viewpoint

Romanesque monument of great importance built at the end of the 12th century on the hill of Montalino, where there is also the homonymous fortress (today private residence not visitable, with sixteenth-century loggia closed by a veranda).

  • Where Collina di Montalino, Stradella
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · visitpavia.com
