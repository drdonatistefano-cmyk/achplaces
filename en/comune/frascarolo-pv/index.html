What to see in Frascarolo

Borgo della Lomellina on the border with Piedmont, between the rice fields, with the Visconti-Forzesco castle (private) in whose agricultural court is located the Museo del Contadino, the neoclassical parish church and, just outside the village, the Abbey Cistercian of Acqualunga.

Museo del Contadino e Castello di Frascarolo

Museums · Themed

In the castle's agricultural courtyard, in a former rural complex renovated with a stable of over 600 square meters, porticos and Italian garden, about 800 objects in three sections (the House, the Crafts, the Earth) tell the peasant life of the Lomellina from the nineteenth century to the sixties: wagons, carriages, threshers, a nineteenth-century typography, a riceria, the wooden sculpture The castle, with square plan with cylindrical towers at the corners and moat transformed into a garden, documented in 1409 and rebuilt by the Sforza at the beginning of the sixteenth century, was transformed into a neo-Gothic residence in 1882 by the architect Vandone for the Vochieri; it is a national monument from the beginning of the twentieth century but is private and not visitable.

museum open only on the first Saturday of the month from 10 am to 12 pm (schools on reservation); private castle not accessible; museum not accessible to people with disabilities

Abbazia di Acqualunga

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Cistercian Abbey founded by the monks of Rivalta Scrivia, of the Burgundian line of La Ferté (1180 according to the abbey card, 1204 according to that of the village), which takes its name from an ancient branch of the Po called Acqua Lunga; the bishop of Vigevano is still the abbot. The complex includes the monastic church of the XII-XIII century, the abbey palace renovated in the sixteenth century by the abbot Galeazzo Pietra, the first bishop of Vigevano, and agricultural structures of the eighteenth-nineteenth century.

church can be visited only by appointment (tel. 0384 84188)

Parrocchiale della Beata Vergine Assunta e San Vitale Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Neoclassical church built in 1841-1842 on the area of the ancient castle of the Community and open to worship in 1852; the interiors were decorated between 1878 and 1887 by Giovanni Valtorta, Achille Secchi and Luigi Morgari, and there is a Crucifixion on canvas by Francesco Sampietro of 1852. Next to it, the coevo municipal palace preserves in the council room part of the fifteenth-century structure of the old castle.

  • Where Piazza Grande, Frascarolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.frascarolo.pv.it · in-lombardia.it

Santuario della Beata Vergine del Romito

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Small sanctuary along the provincial road for Torre Beretti, which preserves a Renaissance fresco of the school of Gaudenzio Ferrari.
