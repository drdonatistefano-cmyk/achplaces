What to see in Lombriasco

Torinese Country was born around the castle of the Marquises of Romagnano, who founded in 1173 the Prevostura of Augustinian canons; the castle, then Benedictine monastery, has since 1894 a Salesian community, next to the parish of the Immaculate Conception.

Castello-Monastero

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fragments of Roman tombstones already indicate an important building in Roman times; before the Mille, with the Marquises of Romagnano, it became a castle with walls, moats and a tower used as a bell tower. In 1173 Manfredo II of Romagnano obtained the Prevostura of Lombriasco with at least twelve Augustinian monks; assaulted several times in the fourteenth century, in 1382 the fief passed to Antonio Ponte and in the eighteenth century the castle was transformed into a noble palace. Dead without heirs in 1850 the last lord, Nicolao Cesare Ponte, in 1878 bought him the Benedictines of Mondovì and in 1894 Don Rua, the first successor of Don Bosco, established a Salesian community there.

Chiesa parrocchiale dell'Immacolata Concezione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It replaced the ancient Santa Maria, chapel next to the castle, seat of the parish founded in 1173 by Manfredo II of Romagnano (the funeral stone is preserved). The new church was decided in 1560 by the prevosto Giacomo Ponte; since 1674 Don Paolo Ponte began restoration and extension lasting about a century, which gave it the present form, and in 1779 it was reconsecrated by Cardinal Costa. In 1890 it had the organ; at the beginning of the twentieth century it was entirely repainted and the main altar rebuilt. Visitable during functions.
