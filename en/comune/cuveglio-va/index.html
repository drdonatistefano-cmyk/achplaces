What to see in Cuveglio

Cuveglio, a long spiritual center of Valcuvia, preserves in Canonica the bell tower crenellated thirteenth century of the collegiate of San Lorenzo, ancient parish church of the valley; in the hamlet of Cavona there is an unusual museum dedicated to the Far West and the Indo-American civilizations.

American Southwest Museum (Museo civico del Sud-Ovest americano Lorenzo Vescia)

Museums · Themed · Free entry

It was born from the legacy of the surgeon Vescia, who collected the artifacts during his stays in the United States: donated by his wife in 1989, the collection of about 400 pieces was arranged by the Municipality in 1990 in the former nursery school of Cavona, and in 2020 it was enriched by the collection of Fabrizio Colonna from Alexandria. It exhibits exhibits of the pre-Columbian civilizations of New Mexico, Arizona and northern Mexico, Winchester rifles, Navajo capes and native American handicrafts, as well as vintage documents of the American Civil War. Free admission with optional free offer; tel. 0332 650107.

  • Where Via Fabio Filzi 18, Cavona, Cuveglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Torre campanaria della Collegiata di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In Canonica there was the collegiate church of San Lorenzo, spiritual center of the parish church of Cuvio and the entire Valcuvia, linked to the main historical events of the municipality. The imposing bell tower of the 13th century remains.
