What to see in Tropea

The city on the cliff of the Costa degli Dei, with the sanctuary of Santa Maria dell'Isola on the rock, the Norman Cathedral, the palaces of Via Boiano and the white sandy beaches.

6 places to visit in Tropea, in the province of Vibo Valentia (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario di Santa Maria dell'Isola

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Benedictine shrine on a cliff just off the city, among the most photographed places in Tropea.

  • Where Tropea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Sources: italia.it

Cattedrale di Tropea

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

From Norman foundation, several times remodeled after the earthquakes, with two Gothic portals and works of art inside; at the entrance two unexploded bombs recall the protection attributed to Our Lady.

  • Where Tropea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di San Demetrio e Largo Galluppi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

On the wide Galluppi, among Baroque buildings, the church preserves a chapel of the fourteenth century.

  • Where Largo Galluppi, Tropea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Via Boiano e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
