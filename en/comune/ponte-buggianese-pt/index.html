What to see in Ponte Buggianese

Center of the lower Valdinievole on the river Pescia, born after the drying of Lake Fucecchio, known for the Sanctuary of the Madonna del Buon Consiglio frescoed by Pietro Annigoni and his students since 1967. In the village also the bronze memorial of Jorio Vivarelli in Piazza Banditori; the territory opens on the Padule di Fucecchio, the largest internal marsh in Italy.

Santuario della Madonna del Buon Consiglio (San Michele Arcangelo)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Born from an oratory of the early sixteenth century, it was rebuilt in 1628 with three naves with a river stone porch on the Baroque façade; the detached bell tower is of the second half of the nineteenth century. Since 1967 Pietro Annigoni and his students have frescoed the walls with scenes such as the prophets Isaiah and Jeremiah, the Last Supper, the Deposition and the Resurrection, the Four Knights of the Apocalypse, the Resurrection of Lazarus and Pentecost; a side room, called the "Anigonians" is entirely painted by the students. In 1978 the Ministry recognized it as a monumental building of great artistic interest.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Museo del Santuario della Madonna del Buon Consiglio

Museums · Themed

Small museum in the premises next to the sacristy, born as an oratory. It has sacred vestments of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, sacred statues, reliquaries, candelabras, sacred vases, coins, prayer books, crosses and liturgical objects, as well as ancient texts including an edition of 1832 of the "Promises Sposi" (Promises).

  • Where Via del Popolo 6, Ponte Buggianese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Piazza Fratelli Banditori e memoriale di Jorio Vivarelli

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Inaugurated on June 19, 1993 and dedicated to the fallen of all wars, the square hosts the bronze complex of the Pistoiese sculptor Jorio Vivarelli - Via della Resistance - historical parabola - the last challenge: a four-metre-high figure represents the dominated and killed humanity, from which a road of Resistance and hope leads to a second sculpture, container of life. Next to the former elementary school is the bust of the discolored father Filippo Cecchi, physicist and director of the Ximenian Observatory since 1872.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Padule di Fucecchio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

With 2081 hectares between the provinces of Florence and Pistoia it is the largest inland marsh in Italy, protected since 1996 by the Natural Reserve "Padule di Fucecchio" (Fucecchio Padles), it hosts the most important gauze in central-southern Italy for number of herons, as well as ibis, ducks, raptors and, for some years, pink flamingos. A dense network of paths, which can be walked, by bike and on horseback, crosses the Crater and can flood in part after intense rains; in the municipal territory there are the Capannone, an ancient port with an old customs, and the tobacco dryer of Pratogrande, where tombstones remind the victims of the Nazi massacre of 1944.
