What to see in San Prisco

At the gates of the ancient Capua, along the Appia street stands the Roman mausoleum of the Old Prisons; in the village the chapel of Santa Matrona preserves early Christian mosaics of the fifth century, and behind stands Mount Tifata, with the remains of the temple of Jupiter Tifatino.

Mausoleo delle Carceri Vecchie

History and architecture · Archaeology

Roman funerary mausoleum with circular plan, built along the ancient Appian street in the first imperial age, which preserves remains of the decoration in stucco; not far is the mausoleum called Conocchia. It is entrusted to the Regional Direction National Museums Campania, which organizes extraordinary openings with guided tours, as for European Heritage Days.

Visitable only on request with reservation required (tel. 0823 844206, drm-cam@cultura.gov.it)

Cappella di Santa Matrona e i mosaici paleocristiani

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A small funerary sacellus, perhaps annexed to the primitive early Christian basilica, with square plan with bare columns that hold four arches; in the apse a marble pool, perhaps a Capuan fountain of imperial age, is an altar and traditionally welcomes the remains of the saint. The mosaics, dated between the beginning of the fifth and sixth centuries, cover the vault with branches of vine, birds and a golden crater on a blue background and the lunettes with the blessing Christ, the divine throne with the roll of the seven seals and the symbols of the Evangelists.

Monte Tifata e il tempio di Giove Tifatino

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A relief of 602 meters at the northern edge of the bell plain, called the holm oak, where according to Silio Italico, Hannibal camped around 216 BC. On a minor summit, about 526 meters, remain the foundations of the temple of Jupiter Tifatino oriented towards Capua, located in 1996; on top there are ancient walls and a chapel linked to the hermit Saint Offa, and on the side towards Capua remains of villas, aqueducts and Roman sepulchres. From the ridge the view reaches the sea and Caiazzo.

  • Where Monte Tifata, San Prisco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · cultura.gov.it
