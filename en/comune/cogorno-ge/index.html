What to see in Cogorno

The town of Chiavari, linked to the Fieschi, with the medieval village of San Salvatore gathered around the basilica, the church of San Salvatore il Vecchio and the Comital Palace.

3 places to visit in Cogorno, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Cogorno in the guide with map and filters

Basilica dei Fieschi

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Romanesque-Gothic monument in Piazza Innocenzo IV, linked in the middle of the thirteenth century to the popes Fieschi Innocenzo IV and Adriano V.

  • Where Piazza Innocenzo IV, San Salvatore, Cogorno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it

Sources: fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it · fondoambiente.it

Chiesa di San Salvatore il Vecchio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In front of the basilica, in the medieval square among olive trees, vegetable gardens and small houses.

  • Where San Salvatore, Cogorno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it

Sources: fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it

Palazzo Comitale e Centro culturale dei Fieschi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The noble house of the village, home to the Cultural Center that collects evidence of the ancient productive activities and the history of the Fieschi family.

  • Where San Salvatore, Cogorno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it
