What to see in Padua

City of Giotto and Sant'Antonio, with frescoed cycles of the fourteenth century "Padova Urbs picta" and the oldest botanical garden in the world, both UNESCO World Heritage, the University since 1222 and Prato della Valle.

25 places to visit in Padua, in the province of Padua (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in enlargement: the guide covers for now 5 of the 14 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Cappella degli Scrovegni

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · UNESCO World Heritage

Made by Enrico Scrovegni in 1303-1305 and frescoed by Giotto in 855 days: 38 stories of the Virgin and Christ on a thousand square meters, the Virtues and the Vizi, the universal Judgment and the starry sky; it stands on the area of the Roman amphitheatre.

  • Where Piazza Eremitani 8, Padova
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it

Basilica di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The patronal basilica of the eight domes, a destination of over 6.5 million pilgrims a year, the heart of the Antonian Citadel and part of the frescoed cycles of the fourteenth-century UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  • Where Piazza del Santo 11, Padova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Oratorio di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage

The oratory on the piazza del Santo frescoed in 1384 by Altichiero da Zevio with the stories of San Giorgio, of strong influence from Giotto.

  • Where Piazza del Santo, Padova
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Prato della Valle

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

One of the largest squares in Europe, 88,620 square meters, reclaimed in 1775-76 by Andrea Memmo: the elliptical canal with 78 statues of characters linked to Padua and the University and the island Memmia in the center.

  • Where Prato della Valle, Padova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Basilica di Santa Giustina

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Abbey Basilica founded in the 5th century on the burial of the martyr, rebuilt in the 16th century and consecrated in 1606, one of the largest in Italy: it preserves the altarpiece of Veronese with the Martyrdom of Santa Giustina.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Ferrari 2/A, Padova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Orto Botanico

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · UNESCO World Heritage

Founded in 1545, the oldest botanical garden in the world still in its headquarters, UNESCO World Heritage with over 3500 species and avant-garde research laboratory.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Palazzo della Ragione

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Built in 1218-1219 for the courts: the 82 for 27 meters salon with ship hull cover, fifteenth-century frescoes, the stone of the Vituprio and the large wooden horse.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
