What to see in Avezzano

The capital of Marsica overlooking the Fucino plain, rebuilt after the earthquake of 1915: the Orsini-Colonna castle with the Pinacoteca d'arte moderna, the cathedral of the Marsi, the Hague of museums on the draining of the lake and the Roman tunnels wanted by the emperor Claudio.

7 places to visit in Avezzano, in the province of L'Aquila (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Orsini-Colonna e Pinacoteca d'arte moderna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Quadrangular fortress with four circular towers; in a room the Pinacoteca d'arte moderna exhibits an important collection with works, among others, by Schifano, Bargaglia and Brindisi.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Avezzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

L'Aia dei Musei

Museums · City history

City museum polo with two permanent exhibitions: the lapidary "Le parole della pietra" (The words of the stone), with inscriptions that tell the history of the Marsica, and the multimedia museum of the drying up of the Fucino "Il filo dell'acqua" (The water thread).

  • Where Via Nuova 33, Avezzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Cunicoli di Claudio

History and architecture · Archaeology

Roman galleries dug by Emperor Claudio from 41 A.D. to regulate the waters of Lake Fucino and protect the houses from the floods; you can visit them with guided tours.

  • Where Avezzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Cattedrale dei Marsi

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Cathedral on Piazza Risorgimento, the central square of the city, known for its mosaics.

  • Where Piazza Risorgimento, Avezzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
