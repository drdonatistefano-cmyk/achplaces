What to see in Pont-Saint-Martin

The first village of the Aosta Valley coming from Piedmont, born around the spectacular Roman bridge in a single span on Lys.

5 places to visit in Pont-Saint-Martin, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Pont-Saint-Martin in the guide with map and filters

Ponte romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the first century BC, a rare example of a bridge with a single span (36.65 metres of rope, 5.82 wide), which can be walked from Via Roma; next to the Museo del Ponte Romano.

  • Where Via Roma, Pont-Saint-Martin
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it
