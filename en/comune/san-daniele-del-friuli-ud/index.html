What to see in San Daniele del Friuli

The city of ham on the Friuli hills overlooking the Tagliamento: the Guarneriana Library, the oldest public library in Friuli, and the church of Sant'Antonio Abate with the cycle of frescoes by Pellegrino da San Daniele.

6 places to visit in San Daniele del Friuli, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Biblioteca Guarneriana

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1466 to accommodate 173 codes donated by the humanist Guarniero d'Artegna, it is the oldest public library in Friuli: more than 600 manuscripts, including a Byzantine Bible of the late 12th century and a Divine Comedy of the late 14th century, about 80 incunabula and 12,000 ancient volumes. The ancient section is visited by reservation and is closed in August.

  • Where Via Roma 1, San Daniele del Friuli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A fifteenth-century church, today deconsecrated, with one of the most beautiful cycles of frescoes in Friuli, painted between 1498 and 1522 by Martino da Udine called Pellegrino da San Daniele.

  • Where Via Garibaldi, San Daniele del Friuli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: touringclub.it · turismofvg.it

Duomo di San Michele

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The main square dominates with its size, a few steps from Sant'Antonio Abate; next door, in the former town hall, is the Guarneriana Library.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, San Daniele del Friuli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Museo del Territorio

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the seventeenth-century cloister of the ancient hospital, already a Dominican convent: sections of archaeology (covered from the 11th-VIII century BC to the Middle Ages), sacred art (two wooden altars from the churches of Sant'Antonio Abate and Santa Maria della Fratta, fragments of frescoes of the Tre-Quattrocento) and ethnography. Open Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday 8.30-12.30.

  • Where Via Udine 4, San Daniele del Friuli
  • Entry €3.50 with guided tour, €2.50 without, students up to 26 years €2; free with FVG Card
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · touringclub.it

Prosciuttifici di San Daniele

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The San Daniele ham, known all over the world, matures thanks to the microclimate of the hills, where the humid winds of the Tagliamento meet with the air of the mountains; it is tasted in the ham shops of the city.

  • Where San Daniele del Friuli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Fiume Tagliamento

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Unique river in Europe for the biodiversity of its branches; the pebble beaches become beaches to rest and points for the canoe, and in its waters you raise the trout, the queen of San Daniele.

  • Where San Daniele del Friuli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it
