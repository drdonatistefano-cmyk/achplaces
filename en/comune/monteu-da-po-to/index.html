What to see in Monteu da Po

On the right bank of the Po, near the confluence with the Dora Baltea, it preserves the archaeological area of the Roman city of Industria with the sanctuary of Isis and Serapide; on the highest hill the ruins of the medieval towers of the Grep and in the village the Baroque parish of San Giovanni Battista.

Area archeologica della città romana di Industria

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Area of 26,500 m2 with the center of the Roman city of Industria, implanted in Augusta age at the Celtic-Ligurian settlement of Bodincomagus and river port on the Po remembered by Pliny the Elder. You can see the large semicircular sanctuary of Isis and Serapide with monumental hedra and two small temples, the podium of a temple with staircase and part of the blocks with houses, taverns and shops; many bronzes produced here are at the Museum of Antiquity in Turin. The northern margin is marked by the remains of the medieval parish church of San Giovanni di Lustria, cited in a diploma of Otto III of 997. Visits through the Athena Cultural Association.

  • Where Frazione San Giovanni, Monteu da Po
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museipiemonte.cultura.gov.it

Sources: museipiemonte.cultura.gov.it · comune.monteudapo.to.it

Ruderi delle torri medievali del Grep

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Two ruins of towers, remains of a castle built not before the year 1000, on the top of the highest hill to the south-west of the village, about half a kilometer from the village .

  • Where Località Torre del Greppio, Monteu da Po
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.monteudapo.to.it

Sources: comune.monteudapo.to.it

Parrocchiale di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Probably born in the fifteenth century with the union of the parishes of Industria and Monteacuto, it was set on fire by the Spanish troops in 1625 and rebuilt between 1631 and 1636 with the stones of an ancient house, whose ruins with a stone coat of arms of Monteu are still visible. Baroque with three naves, has the main altar in seven types of polychrome marbles, a fresco of the Nativity in the choir and a baptistery in carved wood.

  • Where Via Municipio 2, Monteu da Po
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.monteudapo.to.it

Sources: comune.monteudapo.to.it · turismotorino.org

Chiesa di San Sebastiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Very ancient church of the seventeenth century type, once chaplainry, with a single nave with the main altar and a lateral altar of the Madonna of 1743; the churchyard acts as a small square of the village.

open only on holidays and recurrences

  • Where Frazione Mezzana, Monteu da Po
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.monteudapo.to.it

Sources: comune.monteudapo.to.it
