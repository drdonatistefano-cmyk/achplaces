What to see in Montopoli di Sabina

The village is 55 km from Rome, on a hill of the Nazzano Tevere-Farfa Reserve, with the Ugonesca Tower built around the Mille by Abbot Ugo I of Farfa, the triangular square with octagonal fountain and the church of Santa Maria degli Angeli; in the hamlet of Bocchignano the Buccinian Castle and, in Vezzano, the Modern Automata Museum.

Modern Automata Museum

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Museum of small mechanical sculptures in paper, wood and metal that visitors themselves put in motion, between irony and invention: the collection has about 200 automatons of artists from Japan, United States, Germany, United Kingdom, France, Canada, South America and Italy, half exposed permanently and half on exhibition itinerant. It is located in part of the small castle of Vezzano, of early medieval origin (IX century); it organizes guided tours and construction courses for children and adults. Open Monday-Friday 9-17.

  • Where Via Case Nuove 7, Montopoli di Sabina
  • Entry €3, reduced children up to 18 years €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Torre Ugonesca

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The tower has been integrated since the year about 1000, built by Abbot Ugo I to defend the Abbey of Farfa; it offers views of the surrounding countries.

  • Where Centro storico, Montopoli di Sabina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Former Franciscan monastery with frescoes from the middle of the seventeenth century attributed to Vincenzo Manenti and decorations in baroque stucco; VisitLazio indicates it as the stage not to be missed.

  • Where Montopoli di Sabina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Centro storico, Porta Romana e Vicolo Baciadonne

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

You enter from the Roman Porta or Porta Maggiore, Renaissance; Piazza Comunale is triangular, with an octagonal fountain whose water flows from metal mouths in the shape of a lion, and Piazza Pietro Oddi recalls the humanist, poet and commentator of classics born and active in Montopoli around 1425. The narrow Vicolo Baciadonne connects Piazzetta Montecavallo and Piazza Campo di Fiori.

  • Where Piazza Comunale, Montopoli di Sabina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church with bell tower in the historic center, dedicated to the patron saint: the festival, from 20 to 29 September, combines religious, sports and gastronomic events, including the Festival of Pizza Fritta.

  • Where Centro storico, Montopoli di Sabina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Castello Bucciniano di Bocchignano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The hamlet rises on the ancient Castrum Buccinianum: the castle on the hill, at the confluence of three streams, has three walls and three natural moats and contains Piazza d'Armi, Palazzo Tranquilli and the Piazzetta with the stone arch. On 26 December and 6 January there is the Medieval Itinerant Nativity scene, inspired by Jacopo da Varagine's Legend Aurea and a visit by a cardinal to the castle in 1343; between late May and early June the Sabina Jazz Festival, founded by Bruno Castracucchi.

  • Where Bocchignano, Montopoli di Sabina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Riserva Naturale Regionale Nazzano Tevere-Farfa

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

The village rises on a hill of the reserve, established in 1979 as the first regional nature reserve of Italy: about 800 hectares between Castelnuovo di Farfa, Fara in Sabina, Montopoli di Sabina, Nazzano and Torrita Tiberina, around the wetland born between 1953 and 1955 from the barrier ENEL sul Tibere, Ramsar area since 1977 and among the best places in central Italy for birdwatching.

  • Where Territorio di Montopoli di Sabina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parchilazio.it · italia.it

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry
