What to see in Chiomonte

Country of the high valley of Susa with the prehistoric site of the Maddalena, inhabited by the end of the fifth millennium BC and told in an archaeological museum; in the village the Romanesque oratory of Santa Caterina, founded by the Knights Hospitallers, and on the slopes the village Ramats and the paths up to the fort of Gran Serin.

Museo archeologico «La Maddalena»

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in June 2004, it documents the site of the Magdalene from the recent Neolithic period (end of the fifth millennium BC) to the second Iron Age (IV century BC), with the findings of the excavations; it also exhibits paintings of the landscape landscaper G.A. Levis (1873-1926), pupil of Delleani and mayor of the village at the beginning of the twentieth century. Guided visits and workshops with archaeologists upon reservation.

The MiC card indicates "Closed" every day; the city reports guided tours by reservation (tel. 0122 54104)

Oratorio di Santa Caterina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque chapel built around 1210 by Provençal workers next to the domus hospitalis founded around 1165 by the Knights Hospitallers for pilgrims and travelers direct to the Monginevro; dedicated first to St. John the Baptist, around 1250 passed to St. Catherine of Alexandria. Restored, it preserves fourteenth-century frescoes and the baroque altar wanted at the end of the seventeenth century by the prevosto Fantin. Visits by reservation (tel. Borgata Ramats e mulattiera della Maddalena

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the Maddalena a centuries-old paved mule track rises to Ramats, with a windmill, the chapel of Sant'Andrea, consort ovens, terraces and \'barme \', shelter under rock. In the square of the hamlet of Sant'Antonio, the parish church of the Immaculate Conception, mentioned since 1586, blessed in 1698 and with bell tower of 1888, which houses a sixteenth-century retablo of the Melezet carving school. From Ramats trails to the White Chapel, a panoramic point, and the Gran Pertus, hydraulic work of 1533.

  • Where Frazione Ramats, Chiomonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.chiomonte.to.it

Sources: comune.chiomonte.to.it

Gran Serin e Ciantiplagna

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the right side, for Frais, the paths go up to the Gran Serin, military fortress of 1897 at 2589 meters overlooking a glacial lake, and to the Ciantiplagna (2849 m), highest point of the municipality, along a panoramic former military road towards the Colle delle Finestre and the Testa dell'Assietta. In summer, in the Rhododendrons, alpine stars, gentian, arnica and genepì; among the fauna of chamois, deer, roe deer and marmots.

  • Where Frazione Frais, Chiomonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.chiomonte.to.it

Sources: comune.chiomonte.to.it
