What to see in Canal San Bovo

The capital of the Vanoi valley, with streets in porphyry and many hamlets under the peaks of the Cauriòl and the Totoga: the Ecomuseum of Vanoi brings together the exhibition on the Great War on the Lagorai and the Ethnographic Path of Caoria, the Venetian sawmill of Valzanca and the mills of Ronco; from Zortea, Prade and Gobbera you go up to the lake of Calaita, in the Park of Paneveggio.

5 places to visit in Canal San Bovo, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Mostra permanente della Grande Guerra sul Lagorai

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Set up in 1991 by the Alpine Group of Caoria, it exhibits Italian and Austro-Hungarian uniforms, weapons, equipment and personal objects of soldiers found on the field or donated by the inhabitants, with vintage photos on the life at the front and in the rear, and also tells the suffering of civilians. Open from 28 June to 13 September every day 15:30-18:30; admission included in the Trentino Guest Card.

  • Where Caoria, Canal San Bovo
  • Entry €3 (single Ecomuseum ticket, valid in day for all sites)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.visittrentino.info

Sources: ecomuseo.vanoi.it · visittrentino.info

Sentiero etnografico del Vanoi

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Main element of the Ecomuseum, built and managed by the Paneveggio Park, salt from Caoria (850 m) to Malga Vesnota di Sopra (1850 m) with thematic links between rural buildings of the subsistence economy, in four areas: village, meadows, forest and pastures. It starts from the Casa del Sentiero Etnographico, open from 28 June to 13 September 9-12 and 15-18:30; guided tours by reservation.

  • Where Casa del Sentiero Etnografico, Caoria, Canal San Bovo
  • Entry €3 (Hiking house, unique Ecomuseum ticket)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.visittrentino.info

Sources: cultura.trentino.it · ecomuseo.vanoi.it · visittrentino.info

Siéga de Valzanca

Local life and traditions · Crafts · Paid entry

Venetian hydraulic sawmill built in 1872 by Crisanto Orsingher and active until 1954, with drawer wheel and long vertical blade: it sawed about 3,000 cubic meters a year of timber from the Redòs and Valzanca valleys. Disassembled after the closure, it was rebuilt in the late 1990s by the Paneveggio Park with the whole hydraulic machine. In the summer demonstrations on Sunday 14:30-17.

  • Where Pont Stel, Valzanca, Canal San Bovo
  • Entry €3 (single Ecomuseum ticket)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.visittrentino.info

Sources: ecomuseo.vanoi.it · visittrentino.info

Mulini di Cainari

Local life and traditions · Crafts · Paid entry

Still functioning water mills; in summer, on Tuesday, the activity "Dal Chicco al Mulino" is 15-18 with a walk.

  • Where Ronco Cainari, Canal San Bovo
  • Entry €3 (single Ecomuseum ticket)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.visittrentino.info

Sources: ecomuseo.vanoi.it · visittrentino.info

Lago di Calaita

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small morainic lake at 1,605 meters, about half a kilometer long, in the Paneveggio-Pale Natural Park of San Martino, with a vast peat bog of rare plants studied since the nineteenth century and views of Lagorai and Pale. Walk around the lake, hikes to the Calaita fork and the alpine pastures, in winter snowshoeing and cross-country skiing; it is not bathing. It is about 12 km from Canal San Bovo, with parking nearby.

  • Where Lago di Calaita, Canal San Bovo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info
