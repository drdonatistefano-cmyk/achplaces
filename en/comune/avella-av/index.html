What to see in Avella

The ancient Abella to the slopes of the Avella Mountains, in the upper valley of the Clanio: the Roman amphitheatre leaning against the walls, the funerary monuments of the late-Republic and imperial ages and, on the hill, the castle of San Michele with the Lombard and Norman surroundings.

Anfiteatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology

The first nucleus of the archaeological park of ancient Abella: built in the first century BC in the south-eastern part of the city, on an area previously occupied by sannitic houses, in cross-linked work on the walls, with substructions on the south-western side for the average and the summa cavea, the latter not preserved.

Necropoli monumentale dell'antica Abella

History and architecture · Archaeology

Funeral monuments from the last republican phase to the first century of the empire; in the oldest tombs of the territory amphorae and vases testify the influence of the Greek colonies of the coast from the eighth century BC.

Visiting times and methods not published on readable sources; the Ministry of Culture announces extraordinary openings at events.

Castello di San Michele

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the hill to the right of the Clanis, in control of the road towards the valley of Saturday: the first elliptical longobard belt with ten semitowers, the second polygonal Norman belt with nine towers and carraia gate, and on top the fortress with the tower-mastio angioina almost 20 meters high; between the walls remains of houses and a large cistern.

The Park Authority does not indicate timetables, tickets or methods of access.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
