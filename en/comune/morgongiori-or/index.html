What to see in Morgongiori

A land of high Marmilla in the Park of Monte Arci, known for the weaving of carpets and tapestries of MUVAT, for the summit of Sa Trebina Longa and for the hypogeic nuragic temple of Sa Scaba 'e Cresia.

MUVAT - Museo vivente dell'arte tessile

Museums · Themed

It preserves carpets, tapestries and saddlebags woven on ancient horizontal frames, with live demonstrations of the women of the country; in August the exhibition of the tapestry and carpet is held.

Sa Trebina Longa e pineta di Is Benas

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Sa Trebina Longa (812 m) is the highest peak of Mount Arci, a neck, that is a fossil volcanic duct. The pine forest of Is Benas can be reached on foot or by mountain bike from Morgongiori, along a path with springs, waterfalls, caves, walls for clean climbing and wild horses. The park houses the largest obsidian deposit in Sardinia; the entrance is free.

  • Where Parco del Monte Arci, Morgongiori
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

History and architecture · Archaeology

In Prabanta, two neolithic domus de Janas (3200-2800 BC) related to the popular character of Luxia Arrabiosa and the menhir Su Furconi.

Sa Scaba 'e Cresia

History and architecture · Archaeology

The hypogeic Nuragic Temple, perhaps of the recent Bronze (XIV-XII century BC), in a rock split about 150 meters long: a staircase of squared basalt blocks descends into three ramps (24 and 22 visible steps) up to the water, with possibly shiny cavities and breast-shaped reliefs linked to the mother goddess. In Punic-Roman age it was the sanctuary of Demeter; just outside there is the Funtana de on Prantu, tholos structure with five niches.

the original entrance is blocked by a landslide; access reserved for expert speleologists

  • Where Presso Punta 'e Santu Marcu, Parco del Monte Arci, Morgongiori
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
