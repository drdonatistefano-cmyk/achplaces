What to see in Pulsano

The municipality is 40 metres above sea level in the Salento area of Tarantino, overlooking the Gulf of Taranto with about 8 km of coast between cliffs and sandy coves, from Luogovivo to Lido Silvana. In the historic center the Castle de Falconibus, enlarged in 1435, and the church of Santa Maria La Nova; in the necropolis territory and remains of villages of the fourteenth century BC, unique traces of the Bronze Age so far discovered in the Gulf of Taranto.

Castello de Falconibus

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

In the old town, born as a single square tower, it was enlarged and completed in 1435 by the prince Marino Delli Falconi with five towers of different shapes and heights; once surrounded by a moat, from a meeting place for hunting became garrison of soldiers. Purchased by the Municipality in 1912 and municipal seat until 1993, partly restored, today it houses municipal councils, a representative room and the tourist information point (Pro Loco, tel. +39 099 533 9036).

Centro storico e chiesa di Santa Maria La Nova

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The historic centre preserves in architecture and artistic details the most recent history of the village, gathered around the Castello de Falconibus and the church of Santa Maria La Nova, which preserves a reproduction of the cave of Lourdes.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Marina di Pulsano e Torre Castelluccia

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 8 km of coast on the Ionian coast, in the Gulf of Taranto, alternate cliffs and soft sand coves: from Luogovivo to La Fontana, from Le Canne to Montedarena and from the Capparone Hospital to Lido Silvana. This last stretch is dominated by the Torre Castelluccia, linked to the fear of Saracen incursions, and preserves specimens of juniper cuddly; swimming you reach a rocky islet. Largely low floors, suitable for families, with bathing establishments and accommodation facilities.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Spiaggia di Lido Silvana

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Beach of soft white sand on a semicircle coast, behind dunes covered with Mediterranean maquis, with sandy stretches alternated with rocks. Crystal clear and turquoise water and sandy bottom; free stretches and establishments with sun loungers, umbrellas, showers and bars. The Romans already built their villas there.

  • Where Lido Silvana, Marina di Pulsano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
