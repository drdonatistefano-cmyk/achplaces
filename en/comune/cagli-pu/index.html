What to see in Cagli

Cities along the Via Flaminia at the foot of the Nerone and Petrano mountains, with the Medieval Public Palace then residence of Federico da Montefeltro, which houses the Archaeological Museum and the Via Flaminia, the Torrione of Francesco di Giorgio Martini today Centre for Contemporary Sculpture, the Cathedral rebuilt after the earthquake of 1781 and the Roman Bridge Mallio; around forre and waterfalls of Monte Nerone.

Palazzo Pubblico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Venue of the Municipality on Piazza Matteotti, joins the Palazzo Maggiore and the Palazzo del Podestà, built from 1289 perhaps with Eugubine workers; in 1476 it was donated to Federico da Montefeltro, who transformed it into a ducal residence, perhaps with Francesco di Giorgio Martini. On the façade the Madonna and Child in Istria stone (1680) and the clock; inside the vestibule with stone stones and frescoes by Giovanni Dionigi (1563), the Hall of Arms (1889), the Courtyard of the Cosmos with the Cosmic Order of Eliseo Mattiacci and the ancient prisons.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Museo archeologico e della Via Flaminia

Museums · Archaeological

Housed in the Palazzo del Podestà, part of the Palazzo Pubblico, it contains numerous archaeological finds of the city and the territory from prehistory to the Middle Ages; the museum is connected to the lapidary in the vestibule of the palace, restored in 2007.

  • Entry To be checked

Torrione e Centro per la Scultura Contemporanea

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

In 1481 Francesco di Giorgio Martini built for Federico da Montefeltro the fortress on the Cappuccini hill, demolished in 1502 by Guidobaldo so that it would not fall into the hands of Cesare Borgia. It remains intact, downstream, the elliptical mast, connected to the fortress by the tunnel of the "Socrso Coverto" recently restored; today it houses the Center for Contemporary Sculpture linked to Eliseo Mattiacci.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Basilica Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Of medieval origins, it collapsed in part in the earthquake of June 3, 1781, during the Mass, with 73 dead; rebuilt since 1790, it has been basilica since 1982. On the unfinished façade the two portals of 1842 by Michelangelo Boni, pupil of Valadier; on the left side remains a Gothic portal of 1424 of the ancient cathedral. Inside three aisles 54 meters long.

  • Where Corso XX Settembre 10, Cagli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria della Misericordia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Along the Via Flaminia, home to one of the oldest confraternities in the city, dates back to the 14th century; portal of 1537 and sixteenth-century fresco on the façade. Inside the polychrome terracotta group of the Madonna della Misericordia, the "Strage degli Innocenti" and the "Visitation" by Claudio Ridolfi and fragments of fourteenth-century frescoes (Crucifixion, Martyrdom of St. Apollonia, Commentary on the dead Christ).

  • Where Via Flaminia, Cagli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: visitcagli.it · letsmarche.it

Monastero dei Santi Pietro e Cecilia

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Benedictine community founded before the thirteenth century by Blessed Santuccia Terrabotti of Gubbio; the church of St. Peter was consecrated in 1388. Paid up in 1799, damaged in the last war and renewed since 1956, it preserves Baroque and eighteenth-century interiors, a wooden Crucifix considered miraculous, a wooden choir of the eighteenth century and ancient vestments. It offers hospitality for short spiritual retreats.

  • Where Via Pian del Vescovo 5, Cagli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: letsmarche.it · eventi.turismo.marche.it

Centro storico, palazzi e chiese di Gaetano Lapis

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Around Piazza Matteotti, with the fountain of Giovanni Fabbri (1736) designed by Anton Francesco Berardi, there are the fifteenth-century Palazzo Preziosi-Brancaleoni and the sixteenth-century Palazzo Tiranni-Castracane; the churches of Sant'Angelo minore, San Pietro, San Giuseppe, Santa Chiara and Santi Filippo and Bartolomeo preserve paintings of the painter Gaetano Lapis (1706-1773). In August, the Goose's Game was held in 1543.

Ponte Mallio

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Bridge over the river Bosso, the most important Roman site of Cagli: the central fornic with the buttresses, in large blocks of stone, dates back to the Republican era.

Fosso Pian dell'Acqua e cascate de I Cupi

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

The ditch descends from the south-western limestone slope of Mount Nerone to the Giordano Fosso, in a narrow and wooded valley of beech and broad-leaf trees; in the mid-high stretch it forms high waterfalls and narrow holes.
