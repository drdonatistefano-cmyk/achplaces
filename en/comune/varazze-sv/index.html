What to see in Varazze

A small town in the middle of the Ligurian coast, 25 km from Savona and 44 from Genoa, with the historic centre gathered around the Collegiate Church of Sant'Ambrogio, sandy beaches and the Lungomare Europa towards Cogoleto; inland, in the Park of Beigua, the prehistoric sites of Alpicella and the botanical path of the Desert Hermitage.

10 places to visit in Varazze, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e Palazzo Beato Jacopo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the historic centre stands the Palazzo Beato Jacopo da Varazze, already seat of the civic administration, with the statue of the Blessed on the internal facade; near the Oratory of San Bartolomeo it houses a processional case of the school of Margliano. The medieval ramparts and the ruins of the Romanesque church of Sant'Ambrogio remain.

  • Where Piazza Beato Jacopo, Varazze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: lamialiguria.it · parcobeigua.it · italia.it

Collegiata di Sant'Ambrogio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Collegiate parish church of the historical centre, recognizable for the Baroque façade and the bell tower with three orders of Romanesque-Gothic triphores; inside a table of the 16th century Ligurian painter Luca Cambiaso.

  • Where Centro storico, Varazze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · lamialiguria.it

Chiesa di San Domenico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church known for the frescoes of the Sienese school and for a polyptych of the sixteenth century.

  • Where Varazze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Mostra archeologica permanente di Alpicella

Museums · Archaeological

In the village of Alpicella exhibits the finds of the Riparo under the rock of Fenestrelle, discovered in 1977 by Mario Fenoglio: the shelter was frequented in the Neolithic, then abandoned and reused by hunters and shepherds in the Bronze Age.

visits to be agreed with the Cultural Office of the Municipality of Varazze (tel. 019 9390239)

  • Where Alpicella, Varazze
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parks.it · lamialiguria.it · italia.it

Riparo di Fenestrelle e Strada megalitica di Alpicella

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Two short nature trails in the Park of Beigua: from Ceresa to the megalithic road (about 45 minutes, 80 m of difference in altitude), a paved path of neolithic era with a circular area in stone perhaps of ritual use; from Alpicella to Riparo under rock of Fenestrelle (about 20 minutes, 35 m of difference in altitude).

  • Where Alpicella, Varazze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parks.it · italia.it

Piani d'Invrea e castrum del Parasio

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The territory is rich in archaeological sites: on the top of the Piani d'Invrea a small Neolithic menhir dominates the city and the sea; on the hill of Parasio, near the ancient church of San Donato, you can see the remains of the Roman-Byzantine castrum.

  • Where Piani d'Invrea e collina del Parasio, Varazze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Lungomare Europa

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

A walk of about 4.5 km built on the old unique railroad of Genoa-Ventimiglia, which connects Varazze to Cogoleto; flat, passes in part in the tunnels once used by the trains, between brooms and small rocky beaches, and along the route you will meet the castle of the Invrea. Frequented on foot and by bicycle.

  • Where Lungomare Europa, Varazze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · lamialiguria.it · parcobeigua.it

Percorso botanico dell'Eremo del Deserto

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

A thematic route of about 2.3 km (about 1 hour, tourist difficulty) made in 1992 by the Community Montana del Giovo and now managed by the Park of Beigua, a ring on the square of the Hermitage; crosses the remains of the walls of the ancient monastic complex between holm oaks, oaks, black pine trees and beech trees, touches the Source of Sant'Elia with the rare fern and counts about 50 panels on the species.

  • Where Eremo del Deserto, Varazze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcobeigua.it · fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Spiagge di Varazze

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Long sandy beaches, frequented also by surfers, from Bagni Mauro, the last plant in the province of Savona, to Bagni Pinuccia further west; the Nautilus Beach is the largest beach in the municipality and has a space for dogs. In front of the beachfront lighthouse you can also surf at night.

  • Where Lungomare, Varazze
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcobeigua.it · lamialiguria.it

Porto turistico

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Where the ancient Roman naval ship was located, a shipyard that supplied timber for boats, today there is a marina surrounded by cliffs and pine trees, with piers where to stroll and dine; the yards of Varazze are specialized in superyacht.

  • Where Porto di Varazze
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcobeigua.it · lamialiguria.it · italia.it
