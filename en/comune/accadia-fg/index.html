What to see in Accadia

Borgo del Subappennino South Dauno near the border with Campania, with the Rione Fossi, the medieval citadel abandoned after the earthquake of 1930, and a center gathered around the Monumental Fountain and the Clock Tower. In the surroundings, the Paduli Wood and the Punta Stone offer centuries-old springs and oaks.

Rione Fossi

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient medieval citadel, formerly known as the "Fossa dei Greci" due to its caves and partly prehistoric houses, abandoned after the earthquake of 1930: it preserves selcian streets, alleys, medieval houses, remains of churches and fortifications. In the underground a network of cavities dug into the sandstone, used as cellars and passages until before the war; the restorations have recovered the square, some houses along the walls, the walk around the district and part of the cellars, while the other caves remain difficult to access. The district is a pilot village of a PNRR project and hosts the Accadia Blues Festival every year, on the third weekend of July.

  • Where Via Ranuccio Zannella, Accadia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

MOA - Museo delle Origini di Accadia

Museums · Archaeological

Archaeological and civic museum inaugurated on December 6, 2023 in the Palazzo Di Stefano, former prison, at the end of Via Borgo in front of Piazza della Torre dell'Orologio, door of Rione Fossi. It collects sculptures and finds first scattered in the municipal buildings, including the archaeological collection of the ground floor of Palazzo Vassalli and the statue of the Goddess Mother, with immersive installations.

  • Where Via Borgo (Palazzo Di Stefano), Accadia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: press.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Museo civico di Palazzo Vassalli

Museums · Themed

Since 1999 in the sixteenth century Palazzo Vassalli, a long residence of the homonymous family and purchased by the Municipality in the sixties: rooms of archaeological finds of the territory (steles, cippi, vases, statues) and medieval, and in the underground cellar an ethnographic section on the peasant and artisan civilization of Accadia and Capitanata, with tools of carpenter, blacksmith and shoemaker. Guided visits, tel. 0881 981012.

from December 2023 the archaeological collection of the ground floor of Palazzo Vassalli has been re-arranged in the new MOA (communicato Regione Puglia); check what remains exposed

  • Where Via Borgo 16, Accadia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Fontana Monumentale e Torre dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the square of the Clock, at the beginning of Via Borgo, the nineteenth-century stone fountain with the coat of arms of the village, shaped like a small neoclassical temple that recalls a Pantheon, next to the Clock Tower, among the most valuable architectural elements of the center. Not far away the viewpoint of Piazza Matteotti opens onto the hills of Subappennino Dauno.

  • Where Piazza della Torre dell'Orologio, Accadia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Bosco Paduli e Pietra di Punta

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Green areas around the village, with springs of water, outlying rocks and grassy expanses, suitable for a picnic in the shade of secular oaks.
