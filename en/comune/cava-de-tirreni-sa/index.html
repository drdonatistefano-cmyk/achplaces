What to see in Cava de' Tirreni

City of the arcades in the valley between Salerno and the Coast, with the Benedictine Abbey of the SS. Trinity founded in 1011 by Saint Alferio and the walled village of Corpo di Cava.

5 places to visit in Cava de' Tirreni, in the province of Salerno (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Badia della SS. Trinità

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded in 1011 by St. Alferio Pappa Carbone in the Selano valley: the basilica of 1761 by Giovanni del Gaizo preserves an ambo from the 12th century and sculptures by Tino da Camaino; you can also visit the Romanesque cloister, the chapels of the ancient basilica, the catacombs, the Longobard cemetery, the museum and library with over 80,000 volumes and an archive of parchments since 792.

  • Where Via Morcaldi, Cava de' Tirreni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · admin-istituzionale.touringclub.it

Corpo di Cava

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The 11th century village was built next to the monastery, protected by high walls and ramparts, the first inhabited nucleus of Cava.
