What to see in Traversetolo

The village of the Parma's foothills between Enza and Termina, known for its large Sunday market, founded in 1449; in Mamiano the Magnani-Rocca Foundation, the Villa of masterpieces, and in the village the Renato Brozzi Museum, while Guardasone and the WWF reserve of Cronovilla offer history and nature.

8 places to visit in Traversetolo, in the province of Parma (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Fondazione Magnani-Rocca

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The Villa of Masterpieces, opened to the public on April 17, 1990, houses the collection of Luigi Magnani (1906-1984) with works by Gentile da Fabriano, Tiziano, Van Dyck, Rembrandt, Goya, Canova, Monet and Morandi; twice a year it hosts temporary exhibitions. Around the villa there is a large romantic park that can be visited by itself. Open from Tuesday to Sunday, closed on Mondays.

  • Where Via Fondazione Magnani Rocca 4, Mamiano di Traversetolo
  • Entry €9 whole (shows and collection), reduced €5 or €7; only park €3
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.magnanirocca.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: magnanirocca.it · comune.traversetolo.pr.it · parmawelcome.it

Museo Renato Brozzi

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

Dedicated to the sculptor and sculptor Renato Brozzi (Traversetolo, 1885-1963), who donated his works to the country: over 8,000 drawings, 400 photographs, about 80 plasters and about fifty sculptures and works of applied art, with the artist's library. A section tells of the connection with Gabriele d'Annunzio, who called him the greatest Italian animalist after Pisanello, with a hundred letters signed (1920-1936). Closed Monday and all August; the ticket gives a discount to Magnani-Rocca.

  • Where Centro civico La Corte-Bruno Agresti, Via Fratelli Cantini 8, Traversetolo
  • Entry €5, reduced €3, students €2; free on the first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page parmawelcome.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · comune.traversetolo.pr.it

Corte civica Bruno Agresti e Sala Proferio Grossi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Rural court of the seventeenth century that houses the Brozzi Museum, the municipal library and the Hall of Columns for the exhibitions; in the Council Hall 28 paintings of Proferio Grossi (born in Guardasone in 1923) are permanently exhibited inspired by the works of Verdi. On the façade of the town hall, in via Vittorio Veneto, the 'Vittoria angulare' of Brozzi (1923).

  • Where Via Fratelli Cantini 8, Traversetolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page parmawelcome.it

Sources: comune.traversetolo.pr.it · parmawelcome.it

Mercato domenicale di Traversetolo

Local life and traditions · Historic markets · Free entry

Born in 1449 on the concession of Alessandro Sforza, it is one of the largest in Emilia-Romagna: about 200 benches every Sunday (except Easter and Christmas), with the agricultural market AgriCultura at 0 km in Piazza Garibaldi. Three fairs a year: Spring Fair (May), September Fair and San Martino Fair (November).

  • Where Piazza Fanfulla e piazza Garibaldi, Traversetolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page parmawelcome.it

Sources: comune.traversetolo.pr.it · parmawelcome.it

Borgo di Guardasone

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The most panoramic historical village of the municipality, between the valleys of Enza and Termina: castle on Monte Lugol founded at the beginning of the thirteenth century by Atto Baratto (today private residence, not visitable), watchtower of the Guardiola documented from the early fourteenth century and sixteenth-century church of San Lorenzo on Mount Oca. Nine panels and a guide describe the routes on foot and by mountain bike; here it is said to be born Fanfulla, hero of the Challenge of Barletta.

  • Where Guardasone, Traversetolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page parmawelcome.it

Sources: comune.traversetolo.pr.it · parmawelcome.it

Riserva naturale WWF di Cronovilla

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

About 60 hectares on the left bank of the Enza, with wetlands, observation huts and ponds for sport fishing; there were 188 species of birds.

  • Where Vignale, Traversetolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page parmawelcome.it

Sources: comune.traversetolo.pr.it · parmawelcome.it

Pieve di Cazzola e calanchi del Rio Mariolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The church rises 298 meters at the highest point of the village; the parish church has been documented since 1230 and the apse is the oldest part. Not far away are the calanchi towards the Rio Mariolo, rich in fossils.

  • Where Cazzola, Traversetolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page parmawelcome.it

Sources: comune.traversetolo.pr.it · parmawelcome.it

Barboi di Berzora

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Small emissions of gorgling mud, already known in Roman times and used by the Middle Ages as healing mud for the skin; in Berzora also a house-tower of the seventeenth century.

  • Where Berzora, frazione Torre, Traversetolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page parmawelcome.it

Sources: comune.traversetolo.pr.it · parmawelcome.it
