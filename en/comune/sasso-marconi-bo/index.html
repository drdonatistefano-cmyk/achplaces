Villa Griffone e Museo Marconi

Museums · Science and university

In the villa where Guglielmo Marconi did the first experiments, a three-storey museum with functional apparatuses; next to the Mausoleum of 1941.

  • Where Via Celestini 1, Pontecchio Marconi
  • Entry To be checked on the Marconi Foundation website
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry
