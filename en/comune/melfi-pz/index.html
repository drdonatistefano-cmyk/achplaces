What to see in Melfi

Norman city in the Vulture, inside the fifteenth-century walls: the castle where Frederick II spent the summer and which today houses the National Archaeological Museum of Melfese with the Sarcophagus of Rapolla.

5 places to visit in Melfi, in the province of Potenza (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Melfi in the guide with map and filters

Castello di Melfi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built by the Normans at the end of the 11th century at the intersection between Campania and Puglia, summer residence and hunting of Frederick II, then residence of Mary of Hungary from 1284 and feud of the Doria until 1952: ten towers, seven rectangular and three pentagonal, and four entrances; it resisted the earthquakes of 1851 and 1930.

  • Where Via Normanni, Melfi
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo archeologico nazionale del Melfese «Massimo Pallottino»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the castle: daunie and magnagreche ceramics, Greek and Etruscan bronzes and jewels in gold, amber and silver (VI-III century BC), medieval glass with Islamic inscriptions and Doria rooms; in the Clock Tower the Sarcophagus of Rapolla, Roman marble of the II century A.D. carved in Asia Minor.

  • Where Via Normanni, Melfi
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Mura e Porta Venosina

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The reinforced walls between 1456 and 1460 by Giovanni II Caracciolo on the Norman circuit restored by Frederick II; the Venosina Gate is the only one of the four doors that survived the earthquake of 1851 and is still in use.

  • Where Melfi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
