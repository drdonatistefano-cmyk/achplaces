What to see in Pitigliano

The "little Jerusalem" of the Maremma, built vertically on a tuff cliff, with an ancient Jewish community and the Etruscan hollow streets.

4 places to visit in Pitigliano, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 3 of the 4 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Ghetto ebraico e Museo della Cultura Ebraica

Museums · City history · Paid entry

The district of the Jewish community present since the fifteenth century: synagogue, museum, unleavened oven, kasher cellar dug into the tuff, butchery and ritual bathroom.

  • Where Pitigliano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Palazzo Orsini e musei

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Aldobrandesco building restored by the Orsini between the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, home to the Museum of Palazzo Orsini and the Civic Archaeological Museum.

  • Where Piazza della Fortezza Orsini 25, Pitigliano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Vie Cave e Museo Archeologico all'aperto Alberto Manzi

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Corridors dug into the tuff by the Etruscans, some over a kilometer long with walls up to 20 meters high; at the Gradone the open-air museum with the routes of the city of the living and the dead.

  • Where Pitigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Cattedrale e acquedotto mediceo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The Cathedral of Saints Peter and Paul, the church of San Rocco and the 16th century aqueduct in the small town to be walked.

  • Where Pitigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
