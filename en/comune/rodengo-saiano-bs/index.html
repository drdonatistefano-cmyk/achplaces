What to see in Rodengo Saiano

The town of Franciacorta with its centre in Ponte Cingoli, known for the olivetan abbey of San Nicola, documented from 1050 and rebuilt between 1450 and 1534 with three cloisters and works by Moretto, Romanino and Lattanzio Gambara; it also houses a seat of the MUSIL, the Museum of Industry and Work, and cellars of Franciacorta like La Valle, in a farmhouse of the fifteenth century at the foot of Mount Delma.

Abbazia olivetana di San Nicola

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry

The cornerstone of the Benedictine presence in Cluniac, in Franciacorta, documented since 1050, passed in 1446 to the Olivetans who rebuilt it between 1450 and 1534 with the best artists of Brescia; suppressed in 1779, it is again olivetan since 1974. The church (1480-1490, renovated in the nineteenth century) has a portal with protire and polychrome ceramic arches, eighteenth-century frescoes, Christ with Saints Peter and Paul of Moretto (1545) and a wooden choir inlaid in 1480. Three cloisters: the small (1455-1460) with terracotta frieze, the large (1480-1490) with porch and loggia, that of the Cisterna (1580-1590) with wrought iron well and three sundials of 1648; the antirefectory has the Visions of the Apocalypse of Lattanzio Gambara (1570) and the guesthouse painted by Romanino.

MUSIL - Museo dell'industria e del lavoro

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Headquarter of the project MUSIL, set up in a department store, collects machinery and artefacts from the Brescia and national manufacturing industry: an ancient lithographic press, the prototype of a caravan, two models of nuclear power plants, an Ansaldo auto-bolded 1941 diesel engines, leather processing machines and the Franchi-Gregorini mill of 1930. It also has a permanent exhibition on cinema with machines, films, drawings, projection room and laboratory, and a small mechanical workshop.

temporarily closed for inventory and restoration of the collections according to Touring; guided tours and workshops for schools and groups by reservation

Cantina La Valle

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

It takes its name from a small valley at the foot of Mount Delma; since 1991 the Pezzola family has cultivated its historic vineyards and produces Franciacorta Metodo Classico made from Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc grapes. The site is in a farmhouse of the fifteenth century also used for meetings, tastings and events, flanked by a structure for production and vinification since 2010. For visits and tastings contact the cellar (tel. 030 611404).
