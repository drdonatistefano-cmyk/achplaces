What to see in Anghiari

Medieval village of Valtiberina along its steep "ruga"; under the walls, June 29, 1440, the battle that Leonardo painted in Palazzo Vecchio.

6 places to visit in Anghiari, in the province of Arezzo (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Museo della Battaglia e di Anghiari

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the sixteenth century Palazzo del Marzocco prehistoric and Roman finds, relics of medieval and Renaissance wars and a section on the battle of 1440 and Leonardo's lost painting.

  • Where Palazzo del Marzocco, Anghiari
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo di Palazzo Taglieschi

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The state museum with a polychrome wooden Madonna of Jacopo della Quercia, Robbian terracotta and a sixteenth-century table organ still working.

  • Where Palazzo Taglieschi, Anghiari
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Borghetto e Campano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The oldest medieval part, closed by sixteenth-century walls, with the massive tower of the Campano which is the lighthouse to the village.

  • Where Anghiari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Palazzo Pretorio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The fourteenth-century town hall in L with the coats of arms of the vicars, the prisons and a fresco of justice attributed to Antonio di Anghiari.

  • Where Anghiari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Small brick church of the VII-VIII century, with central plan with three absides of Byzantine-Ravenna taste, with a Madonna of Domenico by Michelino.

  • Where Anghiari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church with the Deposition from the Cross of Domenico by Bartolomeo Ubaldini called Puglio, his best work according to Vasari.

  • Where Anghiari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
