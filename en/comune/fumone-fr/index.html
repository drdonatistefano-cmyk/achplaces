What to see in Fumone

Borgo Ciociano on a cone hill, with paved streets and stone houses, dominated by the castle Longhi De Paolis where he was imprisoned and died Pope Celestine V in 1296; on the vaults of the castle the hanging gardens at 800 meters, and in the territory the karst lake of Canterno.

Castello Longhi De Paolis e cella di Celestino V

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Well-preserved medieval fortress of the Marquis Longhi De Paolis since the end of the sixteenth century; there was imprisoned Pope Celestine V in 1296, who according to tradition died there after ten months using the step of the altar as a pillow. You can visit the cell, the small sanctuary of Celestino V built by the Longhi, furnishings and relics mostly nineteenth century and the case with the embalmed body of the little Francesco Longhi Caetani, who died at five years. Visits by reservation required.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.castellodifumone.it

Casa museo Ada e Giuseppe Marchetti Longhi

Museums · House museums

On the second floor of the castle, the rooms reconstruct a patrician residence with furniture, furnishings and small collections of objects of affection; among the pieces exhibited the wedding dress of Emilia Caetani of 1830 and a collection of nineteenth-century soldiers of lead. Times indicated by the MiC: Monday-Saturday 9:30-13 and 15-19, Sunday 9:30-19, reservation required.

  • Where Via Umberto I 27, Fumone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Giardini pensili del castello

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Made by the Longhi family in the 17th century on the vaults of the walkways, in the moat and between the towers: 3,500 square meters with secular trees about 800 meters above sea level, presented as the tallest hanging gardens in Europe, with a multisensory path (olfactory, tactile, audio and LIS). The view extends from the Colli Albani to the Itri valley.

  • Where Castello di Fumone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · visitlazio.com

Collegiata di Santa Maria Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the historic center from 1100, restored between the late eighteenth and nineteenth centuries; in the apse an Annunciation of Buttaragi (1786), in the nave a fresco with the death of Celestino V, the wooden statue of the patron Saint Sebastian with the relics and the golden processional machine of the seventeenth century. For the feast of the patron they throw doughnuts from the bell tower.

  • Where Centro storico, Fumone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Borgo, mura e porte del Castrum

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village rises on a cone hill, with paved streets and stone houses; the walls of Castrum with Porta Romana, the main entrance to Rome, and Porta Napoletana or Portella, emergency exit to Naples. At the end of August the Castri Fumonis Ludi evoke the attempted siege of Henry VI of 1186.

  • Where Fumone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Lago di Canterno

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Oasis in the Ernici Mountains, it is a karst ghost lake: it was formed in 1800 when debris blocked a swallower, and disappears and reappears depending on whether the debris dissolve or reaccumulate.

  • Where Riserva Lago di Canterno, Fumone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it
