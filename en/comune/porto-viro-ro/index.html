What to see in Porto Viro

The Po Delta between the Po di Venezia and the Po di Levante, with a part of the mainland and a lagoon of fishing valleys, born from the union of Donada and Contarina: the Pineta di Fornaci on the fossil dunes, the 63 meters high bell tower of Donada, the eighteenth-century church of San Bartolomeo with the organ Callido, the Venetian cut of the Po and, on the sea, the fishing village of Porto Levante.

8 places to visit in Porto Viro, in the province of Rovigo (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pineta di Fornaci

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

The largest green area of Porto Viro, north of the center, on fossil dunes: the pine trees were planted from the early twentieth century, especially in the 1940s and 1950s, and today return native species like holm oak and roverella. It is the site of Community importance of the Natura 2000 network.

  • Where Ingresso sud tra via Mantovana e via Cao Marina, Porto Viro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.portoviro.ro.it · italia.it

Porto Levante

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village of Porto Viro, a settlement of fishermen with low and colorful houses, a small church of the eighteenth century, a wooded area and an old glacier destination of walks. The port is a starting point for excursions in the lagoon, in the sea and on the river, and the lagoons around lend themselves to the observation of migratory birds.

  • Where Porto Levante, Porto Viro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

In front of Porto Levante there is the Sacca Cavallari, separated from the Adriatic by a scano called Porto Levante beach; in summer a ferry connects the port to the beach.

Chiesa di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The oldest church in the city, begun in 1726; in 1787 received an organ by Gaetano Callido and conserves a baptismal font in marble of the eighteenth century, a wooden statue of the patron saint of the seventeenth century and frescoes of the ceiling dated 1829. Next to it stands Villa Contarini-Carrer, the oldest villa in Porto Viro, built in the seventeenth century by the Venetian Contarini family.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Porto Viro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.portoviro.ro.it · italia.it

Campanile e Chiesa della Visitazione di Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The 63-metre-high bell tower is the highest of the Eastern Polesine: the original nineteenth-century, struck by lightning in 1922, was rebuilt in 1927 in a form that recalls that of San Marco in Venice.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Il Taglio di Porto Viro

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Between 1600 and 1604 the Republic of Venice dug an artificial canal of about 7 km to divert the Po southwards, in the direction of Sacca di Goro. The territory, between Po di Venezia and Po di Levante, you can cycle along the canals between hydraulic artifacts and abandoned chimneys of brick kilns.

  • Where Argine del Po, Porto Viro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.portoviro.ro.it · italia.it
