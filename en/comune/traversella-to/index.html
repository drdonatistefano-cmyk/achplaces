What to see in Traversella

Ancient village of the Valchiusella at 827 m, with stone houses, wooden balconies for drying chestnuts and corn and roofs in lose; its mining past is recounted in the mineralogical museum of the Ecomuseo "Il ferro e la diorite" with galleries of the Geoparco, while the paths lead to the rock engravings of the Path of the Souls and the cantons of the Via dei Cantoni.

Ecomuseo «Il ferro e la diorite» – Museo Mineralogico e Geoparco Minerario

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Ecomuseum dedicated to the mining past of the country. The museum, in the Minerary Centre, exhibits a collection of about 300 minerals, a systematic mineralogical collection and the tools of work recovered in the mine; outside didactic paths lead to the Geoparco Minerario, where you can visit some of the galleries, the extraction well and the engine room. Accessible, with podactile and audioguide in Italian, English and LIS; in 2026 open Saturday and Sunday from May 1st to September 27th, 14-18.

  • Where Località Miniere, 10080 Traversella
  • Entry Museum + gallery €10; gallery only €8; museum only €4, reduced €2 (5-12 years)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website comune.traversella.to.it

Sources: comune.traversella.to.it · turismotorino.org · italia.it

Borgo di Traversella

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient and characteristic village at 827 m, with about 300 inhabitants: stone buildings with wooden balconies used to dry chestnuts and corn, arched windows and roofs in lovable.

  • Where Traversella (TO)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Sentiero delle Anime

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hiking route that starts from Traversella, on the left orographic of Valchiusella, with numerous pre-Christian engravings between Piazza Refuge and the Cappia Plans: anthropomorphic figures, crosses, cups and cross-linked figures linked to the afterlife. The Piedmontese name "Senter dj'anime" comes from the oral tradition according to which the souls of the deceased passed by.

  • Where Partenza da Traversella
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · italia.it

Via dei Cantoni

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of about 26 km with departure and arrival in Traversella, which touches 10 ancient cantons with typical Canavesan architecture and eighteenth-century bridges.

  • Where Partenza da Traversella
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Palestra di roccia di Traversella

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Above the village, 500 climbing routes for all levels divided into 21 sectors, one of which is dedicated to children; among the most famous cliffs in the North-West.

  • Entry Free
