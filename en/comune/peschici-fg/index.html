What to see in Peschici

Village of Slavic origins (the ancient Pesclizzo) on a cliff of more than 70 meters overlooking the sea, in the Gargano National Park: white houses with steps, the castle and the Tower of the Bridge in the Baronial Recinct, the church of Sant'Elia Prophet and beaches Blue Flag among the most awarded of the promontory.

Castello di Peschici

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It was built by the Normans between the 10th and 11th centuries against Saracens and pirates and was also the residence of the monks of the Abbey of Tremiti, and Frederick II added the tower called imperial rock and baronial enclosure, and in the eighteenth century it was rebuilt in its present form. It is privately owned, but you can visit the dungeons, once prisons and weapons depots, with instruments of torture from the Middle Ages to the Renaissance, and a small terraced garden on the sea.

  • Where Recinto Baronale 1, Peschici
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcogargano.it

Centro storico di Peschici

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village, the northernmost town of Puglia, rises on a cliff over 70 meters high that looks at the coast from Manaccore to Rhodes. Born around the castle and closed by the Baronial Recinct, it grew along the ridge with terrace streets, stairs dug into the rock and white cube houses with domes, up to exit the walls between 1851 and 1901. From the viewpoint the view ranges from the pinewoods of Aleppo and the watchtowers of the coast.

  • Where Centro storico, Peschici
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcogargano.it · italia.it · interregaismart.regione.puglia.it

Torre del Ponte

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Gateway to the historic center near the castle; the base of a stumped shoe suggests an angioin construction of the 13th-XIV century. It is one of the monumental properties of the Puglia Region.

  • Where Centro storico, Peschici
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcogargano.it · interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Elia Profeta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the historical center that preserves the polychrome wooden statue of the Madonna of Calena, coming from the abbey of Calena, today reduced to ruins behind the marina.

Spiagge di Peschici

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Peschici Bay is one of the largest beaches in the area, suitable for families and, for exposure to wind, surfers; Jalillo is more secluded and shaded in the afternoon; towards Rodi Garganico follow the long beaches of Calenella, Cento Scalini (of the Tufare) and San Menaio, while Suspicious is pebbles between rocky walls. They are among the most awarded Blue Flag beaches in Gargano.

  • Where Litorale di Peschici
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
