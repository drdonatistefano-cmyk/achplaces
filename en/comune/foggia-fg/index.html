What to see in Foggia

The capital of the Tavoliere and the Capitanata: the Romanesque cathedral of the Iconavetere, the underground underneath the historic center, the Civic Museum of Palazzo Arpi with the daunie tombs, the Neolithic village of Passo di Corvo and the Bosco dell'Incoronata with its sanctuary.

6 places to visit in Foggia, in the province of Foggia (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta (Iconavetere)

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Erected in the 12th century and modified several times, it retains much of the Romanesque perspective; it venerates the ancient icon of the Iconaveter, at the origin of the first church.

  • Where Centro storico, Foggia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sigecweb.beniculturali.it

Museo Civico e Pinacoteca – Palazzo Arpi

Museums · Archaeological

Established in 1931 at Palazzo Arpi: archaeological and ethnographic sections, picture gallery, lapidarium and the reconstruction of two daunie tombs of the IV-III century BC, the Tomb of Medusa and that of the Knights, with the mosaics of the House of lions and the Panthers of Arpi.

  • Where Piazza Vincenzo Nigri, Foggia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Ipogei del centro storico

History and architecture · Archaeology

A network of underground environments runs under the historic center; the most important is the Ipogeum of San Domenico.

  • Where Centro storico, Foggia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Parco archeologico Passo di Corvo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The Neolithic village of 40 hectares, one of the largest entrenched villages of the Tabletop; visits by reservation.

  • Where Contrada Arpinova, Foggia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Parco naturale regionale Bosco dell'Incoronata

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Protected area about 12 km from the city, with the valley of Cervaro: birds, deers, wild boars and reptiles.

  • Where Incoronata, Foggia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Santuario della Madonna dell'Incoronata

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

A well-known pilgrimage destination in the area of the Bosco dell'Incoronata.

  • Where Incoronata, Foggia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
