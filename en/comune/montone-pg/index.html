What to see in Montone

Medieval village on a hill above the valley of Carpina and the Upper Tiber Valley, home of the leader Braccio Fortebracci: three gates and three districts, the Gothic church of San Francesco with the municipal museum, the Collegiate Church that preserves the Santa Spina and, in the surroundings, the Rocca d'Aries and the old Pieve.

Complesso museale di San Francesco

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Municipal museum since 1995 in the Gothic church of San Francesco (founded around 1300) and in the former convent: the frescoes of the apse, with Stories of San Francesco, are by Antonio Alberti (1422-1424) on commission of Braccio da Montone. The picture gallery exhibits the wooden group of the Deposition (1260-1270) from the Pieve di San Gregorio, the gonfalone of the Madonna della Misericordia di Bartolomeo Caporali, an Annunciation of the Signorellian School (1532), the "Perugine Tablecloths" and finds of a Roman villa of the second century A.D. Open from April to September Friday-Sunday and holidays 10-13 and 16-18.30; from October to March Saturday, Sunday and holidays 10.30-13 and 15-17.30.

  • Where Via San Francesco 4, Montone
  • Entry €5, reduced €4, boys 6-14 years €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museomontone.com

Centro storico, mura e porte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village from the origins between the IX and the X century, closed by the walls with three doors (Verziere, Borgo Vecchio and Monte) corresponding to the three districts; from Piazza San Francesco, climbing the staircase, opens the view on the Umbrian hills.

Rocca di Braccio e Polo di Santa Caterina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The fortress, designed by Fioravante Fioravanti from Bologna for the Fortebracci in the highest point of the country, was destroyed in 1478 at the behest of Pope Sixtus IV: the remains of the mastium remain. On its ruins stands the former convent of Santa Caterina, seat of the library and the Municipal Historical Archives, which conserves papal bubbles from the 14th to the 17th century and the Statute of Montone of 1586.

  • Where Montone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Collegiata di Santa Maria e San Gregorio Magno

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient parish church which became collegiate in 1317 and rebuilt in baroque forms in 1668, with a single nave with a coffered ceiling: it preserves a Last Supper by the Flemish Denijs Calvaert (1611) and the relic of Santa Spina, in a silver reliquary since 1635, exposed on Angel Monday and the penultimate Sunday of August, when Carlo Fortebracci's donation is recalled.

  • Where Montone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Pieve vecchia di San Gregorio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest church of Montone, outside the walls in the valley of the Lana stream: built around the year 1000 in Romanesque-Byzantine style and renovated in the sixteenth century, fortified, with three aisles, with frescoes from the Umbrian school in the apse (Annunziata, Madonna on the throne) and the golden wooden edicule that housed the thirteenth century Deposition.

  • Where Valle del torrente Lana, Montone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A Benedictine church of the 12th century, where in the 16th century the Vitelli founded the Confraternity of the Blessed Sacrament, entrusted in 1577 to the Roman basilica of Santa Maria sopra Minerva; from here comes the Last Supper of Calvaert today in the Collegiate.

  • Where Montone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Rocca d'Aries

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Impressive castle about six kilometers from the center, going up the valley of Carpina north-east, restored and restored to the ancient splendor; according to a tradition, perhaps invented by Braccio, was founded in the early Middle Ages.

  • Where Valle del Carpina, Montone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Osservatorio astronomico di Coloti

Museums · Science and university

In the hamlet of Coloti, about 8 km from the center, in a village today uninhabited: opened since 2000, it has a telescope under a dome of 7 meters, twelve rooms, the former church of the village used as meeting room and the Sala Sirio for events.
