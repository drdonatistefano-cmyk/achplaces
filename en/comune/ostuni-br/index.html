What to see in Ostuni

The white city is on the last buttresses of the Murge: the medieval core of the Earth with alleys, whitewashed houses and stairs, the fifteenth-century cathedral at the top of the hill, the Aragonese walls with their towers, the museum that houses the Woman of Ostuni about 25,000 years ago and, less than 10 km away, the marina and the Park of the Coast Dunes.

11 places to visit in Ostuni, in the province of Brindisi (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico (la Terra)

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval nucleus on the top of the hills, called Terra, is the labyrinth of alleys, whitewashed houses and stairs that gave Ostuni the name of "white city." Of the original fifteen Aragonese towers, eight remain, which dominate the streets of the center.

  • Where Centro storico, Ostuni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Built at the end of the fifteenth century at the highest point of the old town, it has a tripartite Gothic façade with a rosette decorated; the interior is baroque and preserves valuable art works.

  • Where Piazza Beato Giovanni Paolo II, Ostuni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ostuni.br.it · italia.it

Via Cattedrale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Ascent road towards the cathedral, flanked by noble palaces: at the beginning the Baroque palace Zevallos, at number 35 a palace with Renaissance elements; by detouring via Bixio Continelli you reach the fifteenth-century church of San Giacomo di Compostella.

  • Where Via Cattedrale, Ostuni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Piazza della Libertà e Colonna di Sant'Oronzo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The pedestrian heart of the city, overlooking the Town Hall, built in the nineteenth century from the former Franciscan monastery of the fourteenth century, the church of St. Francis and the seventeenth century church of the Holy Spirit with a Renaissance portal to bas-reliefs. At the centre is the baroque column of Sant'Oronzo, about 20 meters high, carved in 1771 by Ostuni Giuseppe Greco as a thank you to the patron for having saved the city from the plague, with four statues of saints and on top the blessed saint.

  • Where Piazza della Libertà, Ostuni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ostuni.br.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Viale Oronzo Quaranta e mura aragonesi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The panoramic avenue called "tradone" follows the semicircular course of the Angevin-Aragonese walls, with the defensive towers and the two surviving doors, Porta Nova and Porta San Demetrio; on the other side the view extends over the Plain of Olives and, on clear days, over the Adriatic.

  • Where Viale Oronzo Quaranta, Ostuni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ostuni.br.it · italia.it

Museo di civiltà preclassiche della Murgia meridionale

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 1989 in the former Carmelite monastery of Santa Maria Maddalena dei Pazzi, it exhibits prehistoric and protohistoric finds of excavations started in the territory since 1980 and, in the most recent rooms, medieval and modern materials. In a large room the skeleton of the Woman of Ostuni, a twenty-year-old lived about 25,000 years ago in the Paleolithic, buried with the fetus in the womb in the cave of Santa Maria di Agnano. The adjoining church of San Vito Martire, known as the Monacelle (1750-1752), marks the beginning of the rococo in Ostuni.

  • Where Via Cattedrale 15, Ostuni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Parco archeologico di Santa Maria di Agnano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The park around the prehistoric cave used as a place of burial and worship since the Paleolithic, where the Woman of Ostuni was found; it dominates centuries-old olive groves and the Adriatic sea. In summer it is open with guided tours; paths only partially accessible.

  • Where Contrada Agnano, Ostuni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ostuni.br.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Santuario di Sant'Oronzo e San Biagio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

At about 3 km north-west of the city, at the foot of the mountains of the Badessa, a sanctuary of the seventeenth century with a frescoed Basilian crypt, where according to tradition the saint hid in 68 to escape the persecutions of Nero, and a source attributed to him. Not far away, surrounded by greenery, the rock sanctuary of San Biagio, carved into the rock with Byzantine frescoes from the 12th century. Visits on request.

  • Where Ostuni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Frantoio ipogeo Locopagliaro

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Medieval mill excavated underground, today museum of rural civilization, destination of lovers of rural archaeology: preserves ancient wooden presses, stone millstones, ovens and housing of the workers.

  • Where Contrada Lamacavallo, Ostuni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Parco naturale regionale Dune Costiere

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Protected area from 2006 along the coast between Torre San Leonardo and Torre Canne, between Ostuni and Fasano: sand dunes, wetlands, ancient olive trees and ancient oil mills, with excursions and birdwatching; some paths are also accessible to people with disabilities.

  • Where Strada Provinciale 19, Ostuni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ostuni.br.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Marina di Ostuni e Villanova

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The seaside resort of the white city, less than 10 km from the village, is made of more settlements along the coast and has obtained for several years the Blue Flag; in Villanova a tourist marina dominated by a small castle, in Monticelli the remains of a village of the Bronze Age.

  • Where Marina di Ostuni, Ostuni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it
