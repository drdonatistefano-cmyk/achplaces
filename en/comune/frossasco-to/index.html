What to see in Frossasco

Borgo del Pinerolese of Roman foundation that preserves the walls of Filippo d'Acaja with tower and four doors, fifteenth-century houses with gothic windows in terracotta, the church of San Donato of 1220 and the chapel of the frescoed Boschetto; it houses the Regional Museum of Migration and the Museum of Gusto.

Centro storico, mura e porte medievali

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The walls wanted by Filippo d'Acaja at the end of the fourteenth century preserve the medieval tower and four doors: Porta di Pinerolo and Porta di San Giusto intact, Porta di Torino restored after the damage of 1977, Porta di San Donato rebuilt after 1965; on the arches the ducal stuccos painted in 1586 by Bernardino Lanino. In the square two houses of the fifteenth century (of the Rolletti di Bricherasio and of the Vicar of Pinerolo) with Gothic windows in terracotta and frescoes of the fourteenth century; through the streets votive frescoes and sundials from the seventeenth to the nineteenth century.

  • Where Centro storico, Frossasco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.frossasco.to.it

Chiesa di San Donato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It dates back to 1220, with successive changes; the bell tower is 1571-73. It preserves an ancient fresco of San Cristoforo, a Virgin with the 17th century Child and a pulpit between the end of the 16th and early 17th century; on the side façade a sundial.

  • Where Centro storico, Frossasco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.frossasco.to.it

Sources: comune.frossasco.to.it · turismotorino.org

Chiesa di San Bernardino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Baroque church designed by Gerolamo Buniva and built between 1760 and 1770, with wooden choir behind the main altar. It preserves an organ of the fifteenth century, one of the oldest of the Pinerolese, inserted among the seven organs to be saved of the provincial project "Organalia" .

  • Where Centro storico, Frossasco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.frossasco.to.it

Sources: comune.frossasco.to.it

Cappella del Boschetto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the 15th century, it preserves a cycle of frescoes on the history of the Virgin attributed to the Master of Cercenasco, restored in 2003-2004. Since 2019, he has been a member of the project "Churches with open doors": it opens automatically through the homonymous app, with a multimedia path in five languages.

  • Where Frossasco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: comune.frossasco.to.it

Museo Regionale dell'Emigrazione

Museums · Themed · Free entry

The halls historically and visually reconstruct the countries that have welcomed immigrants and the Italian and Piedmontese protagonists who have contributed with their work to build entire nations. Open on Saturday 10-13 and 14-18; educational workshop for schools, accessible.

  • Where Piazza Donatori di Sangue 1, Frossasco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.frossasco.to.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.frossasco.to.it
