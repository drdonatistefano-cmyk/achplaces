What to see in Pontecagnano Faiano

It rises on the territory of the ancient Picentia of Etruscan origin, today told by the National Archaeological Museum of the Etruscan border and by the urban archaeological park; on the Gulf of Salerno the beach of Magazzeno extends.

Museo archeologico nazionale di Pontecagnano

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Dedicated to the Etruscan border, it exhibits the findings of the Villanovian and Etruscan-Campan center, especially the collections of over 10,000 graves dug in the necropolis in about fifty years. Parco archeologico urbano dell'antica Picentia

History and architecture · Archaeology

About 22 hectares behind the modern city with the remains of the Etruscan-Campano town, explored only in part; the excavations of the Universities of Salerno and Molise have exposed an entire block of the Roman colony of Picentia, with the decuman and construction phases until late antiquity. It houses an Environmental Education Centre, an outdoor library and urban gardens treated with Legambiente.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Spiaggia di Magazzeno

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 7 km of golden sand coast with dune stretches where the rare sea lily grows; the view embraces the Gulf of Salerno from Punta Licosa to Capri.

  • Where Litorale di Magazzeno, Pontecagnano Faiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it
