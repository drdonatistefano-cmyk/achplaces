What to see in Roncegno Terme

Valsugana's thermal resort at the foot of Mount Fravòrt, opposite Cima Dodici: around the spa establishment there is a historic park of over four hectares and the Palace Hotel in Art Nouveau style, in the village the parish church of Saints Peter and Paul with the altarpiece of Francesco Guardi and the Museum of Popular Musical Instruments, in Marter the House Museum of the Scarecrows in the former mill Angels, and on the Lagorai lake of the Graves.

Casa Museo degli Spaventapasseri

Museums · Themed

Museum in the former mill Angeli, an ancient mill where wheat and other cereals were ground, in the hamlet of Marter. The permanent collection is dedicated to scarecrow, the result of researches by photographer Flavio Faganello in Trentino on these figures of straw, with legends and curiosities; it offers educational activities for schools and families.

  • Where Via San Silvestro 2, Marter, Roncegno Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website lacasadeglispaventapasseri.net

Parco delle Terme di Roncegno

Nature and landscape · City parks

Spa park of more than four hectares, between clearings and denser areas connected by avenues, with exotic species such as calocedar, sequoia and cryptomeria next to beech trees, hippocastans, plane trees and lindens; it preserves a chapel, an artificial hill, a circular kiosk belvedere and a fountain with spout. The first initiatives to exploit the source date back to 1860, the plant in the green is documented from 1875 and the complex was completed in the early twentieth century with Grand Hotel and Palace Hotel; since 1972 it is of the Autonomous Province of Trento and has been recently recovered.

  • Where Piazza de Giovanni 5, Roncegno Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: cultura.trentino.it · visitvalsugana.it · visittrentino.info

Chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church built between 1757 and 1782 on the design of the Lombard Whites, with a single nave with four side chapels and the main altar of the Venetian Temanza; the bell tower of about 60 meters is 1886-1888. It preserves the altarpiece of Francesco Guardi with the Holy Trinity that appears to Saints Peter and Paul (1778 about), frescoes by Valentino Rovisi, pupil of Tiepolo, and the Evangelists by G. Battista Chiocchetti; the Pugina organ was inaugurated in 1931.

  • Where Roncegno Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitvalsugana.it · visittrentino.info

Museo degli Strumenti Musicali Popolari

Museums · Themed

Opened in 2011 and managed by Chorus S. Osvaldo di Roncegno, exhibits popular musical instruments from all over the world and a collection of sacred texts from the nineteenth and twentieth centuries; with the guide the instruments can be touched and played, and outdoors the Music Park has instruments for children. Open from Tuesday to Sunday 10-12 and 15:30-18.

  • Where Piazza A. De Giovanni 1, Roncegno Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodellamusicaroncegno.it

Sources: museodellamusicaroncegno.it · visittrentino.info

Palace Hotel

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in the Belle époque, it soon became the fashionable spa resort of the Central European aristocracy; destroyed in the First World War, it was rebuilt in Liberty style and inside it contains frescoes by Carlo Coppedè.

  • Where Roncegno Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitvalsugana.it · cultura.trentino.it · visittrentino.info

Lago delle Prese

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Alpine lake in the Lagorai called the "Smeraldo del Lagorai" due to its colors, still little known. You climb up with the trail 372B from the area of the Pozze di Roncegno towards the refuge Serot and, reaching the asphalted road, take the path to the lake; the return goes down a forest road above the lake to the former Agritur Rincher.

  • Where Lagorai, Roncegno Terme (partenza dalle Pozze)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitvalsugana.it · visittrentino.info
