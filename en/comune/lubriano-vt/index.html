What to see in Lubriano

Borgo della Tuscia overlooking the Valle dei Calanchi and Civita di Bagnoregio, 110 km from Rome: around Piazza San Giovanni the church of San Giovanni Battista, born from a Benedictine cell of the IX century, the Palazzo Monaldeschi and the fountain of Pucciotta; the Naturalistic Museum accompanies the Monaldeschi Tower and the Balza Natural Monument of Seppie.

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The origins date back to the 9th century, when there was a Benedictine monastic cell of which remain sculptures in Longobard-Carolinge interweaving; the current building is from 1120 and the Baroque façade was added after the earthquake of 1695. It preserves frescoes of the XVI-XVII centuries restored in 2017 (Saints Paul, Andrea and John the Baptist), the Annunciation of Jerome Topo, pupil of Carlo Maratti, coming from the convent of the Clarisses, a Deposition and the Decollation of the Baptist; since 1855 it preserves the relics of Saint Procolo.

  • Where Piazza San Giovanni Battista, Lubriano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Museo naturalistico e Torre Monaldeschi

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Active since 2009 to protect the landscape of the Valle dei Calanchi and the rural traditions related to biodiversity, organize excursions, educational workshops and courses on bees and honey. Visit by appointment and guided tours of the historic centre include the ascent to the Monaldeschi Tower, in the Ortale district, with 360 degree views of Lubriano, the Valle dei Calanchi and Civita di Bagnoregio. Tel. 327 0289027.

  • Where Piazza Col di Lana 12, Lubriano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: retemusei.regione.lazio.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Monumento naturale Balza di Seppie

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Terrace of 1.26 hectares on the Valle dei Calanchi, between Lake Bolsena and the Tiber, established in 2016 and managed by the Municipality, which helps to read the geomorphological processes of a lunar but luxuriant landscape. The point of view can be reached by car or by a 9-seater shuttle to be booked at least 7 days before with the Guides (tel. 328 5430394); reference is the Naturalistic Museum.

  • Where Balza di Seppie, Lubriano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parchilazio.it · italia.it

Piazza San Giovanni, Palazzo Monaldeschi e fontana La Pucciotta

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The "salotto" of the village, a civil and religious centre. At the centre is the fountain La Pucciotta, a square bathtub with a putto that holds a swan from whose beak comes out of its spout; in front of the church the Palazzo Monaldeschi, built on a convent before the Mille, owned by the Monaldeschi for over 500 years and renovated in baroque style at the end of the seventeenth century, with hanging gardens, geometric hedges and perspective decorations, today seat of events.

  • Where Piazza San Giovanni, Lubriano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Centro storico e belvedere sulla Valle dei Calanchi

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village is a natural viewpoint over the Valle dei Calanchi, landscape of volcanic rocks, sandstone and clays among the most famous in Tuscia: from the small maze of arches, steps and medieval squares suddenly open views of the calanchi and Civita di Bagnoregio.

  • Where Centro storico, Lubriano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Santuario della Madonna del Poggio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Just outside the village on the road to Bagnoregio, it houses a small fresco from 1619 considered miraculous; the current Baroque church was financed in 1732 by the Marquis Pompeo del Monte and his wife Anna Rosa Monaldeschi and above the portal bears the inscription ÂFabricaverunt sinneres Â . On Sundays after Ascension, the feast with procession distributes the "panettuzzi," small hosts of unleavened bread marked with a cross and blessed.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Infiorata e Sagra della Bistecca Chianina

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

On the day of the Ascension the procession crosses the streets decorated with carpets of flowers and leaves, an ancient tradition; in July the Festival of the Chianina Steak and the Festival of wine of the Valley of the Calanchi on the Teverina Wine Road, in autumn the Dinners in the Cellars.
