What to see in Tuscania

The city of the sarcophagi is on the Via Clodia, inhabited by almost three millennia: the Romanesque basilicas of San Pietro and Santa Maria Maggiore, the Etruscan museum in the former convent of the Rest and the necropolis.

6 places to visit in Tuscania, in the province of Viterbo (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 5 of the 6 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Outside the medieval walls, the basilica with its shapes that intertwine influences and styles, with cosmatesque floors, Etruscan sarcophagi in the left nave, frescoes and crypt; escaped the earthquake of 1971.

  • Where Tuscania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Facade in stone with a superb rosette and incredible decorative richness; inside the baptismal font of the thirteenth century and the Last Judgment frescoed in the fourteenth century.

  • Where Tuscania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Museo Archeologico Nazionale di Tuscania

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

In the former Franciscan convent of Santa Maria del Riposo, founded in 1247, Etruscan and Roman sarcophagi and furnishings of the local necropolis, including the sarcophagus of the Curunas family.

  • Where Largo Mario Moretti 1, Tuscania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Necropoli Madonna dell'Olivo – Grotta della Regina

History and architecture · Archaeology

One of the Etruscan necropolis around Tuscania, from which come many finds of the archaeological museum.

  • Where Tuscania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com · cultura.gov.it

Centro storico, piazza Basile e piazza Bastianini

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Walled center of towers and churches, with Etruscan sarcophagi used as urban furniture, the Town Hall in Piazza Basile and the seventeenth-century Great Fountain in Piazza Bastianini; from the walls one of the most beautiful landscapes of Tuscia.

  • Where Tuscania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Mura e castello del Rivellino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The walls with the Rivellino castle, among the medieval jewels of Tuscania.

  • Where Tuscania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
