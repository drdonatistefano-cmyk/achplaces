What to see in Picinisco

Medieval fortified village at 725 meters in the Comino Valley, at the foot of Mount Meta on the Lazio side of the National Park of Abruzzo, Lazio and Molise, dominated by a castle of 1054; Orange Flag since 2022, linked to the stay of D.H. Lawrence in 1919.

6 places to visit in Picinisco, in the province of Frosinone (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e Piazza Capocci

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village preserves the medieval system with four ancient doors, alleys, small squares and palaces with stone portals and balconies. Piazza Ernesto Capocci, the heart of the village with a centenary plateau, houses the Palazzo degli Orologi and the church of San Rocco of 1500 and looks over the Valley of Comino; in Piazza Fucina there is a sundial.

  • Where Piazza Ernesto Capocci, Picinisco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque church of 1110, the oldest in the country and among the oldest in the diocese, with a fresco of the fourteenth century; it was probably founded on the remains of an ancient necropolis.

  • Where Picinisco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sentiero della «Ragazza perduta» e Casa Lawrence

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The path, which takes its name from the novel "The Lost Girl" inspired by the village, leads to the locality Cervi and Casa Lawrence, a villa where the writer D.H. Lawrence stayed with his wife Frieda in 1919.

Monte Meta e Parco Nazionale d'Abruzzo, Lazio e Molise

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village is located on the Lazio side of the park, at the foot of the Meta massif (2,242 meters), between beech, chestnut and oak woods; between the paths that of Ponte Lebranche and that towards the artificial lake of Grotta Campanaro, and in winter the ski resorts of Prati di Mezzo. The Morge del Corvo, walled in the woods on the Val Canneto and the Melfa river, are a climbing destination.

  • Where Dintorni di Picinisco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com · touringclub.it
