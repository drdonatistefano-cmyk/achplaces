What to see in Novara di Sicilia

Borgo dei Peloritani was born, according to legend, around the Saracen castle, from whose ruins you can see the Aeolians: the sixteenth century Cathedral, the thirteenth-century church of San Francesco, the Municipal Theatre and, about 5 km, the Cistercian Abbey of Santa Maria La Noara; it is the home of the Majorchino and his Carnival tournament.

Duomo di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Built in the 16th century, with monumental sandstone façade on a large staircase and Renaissance interior with three naves with monolithic stone columns and circular apse; it preserves the altar of the Sacrament in carved marble and the baptistery in local chives covered by a wooden dome, with a statue of the Assumption and a large wooden crucifix.

Ruderi del castello saraceno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Remains of the castle on a steep cliff, around which according to legend the village arose: the look extends over the valley that descends to the sea and, on clear days, on the Aeolian Islands. Via Dante Alighieri connects him to the Duomo.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Abbazia di Santa Maria La Noara

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

About 5 km from the centre, it dates back to the 12th century, at the time of Ruggero II, on the impulse of Sant'Ugo Abate; it is considered the most significant example of Cistercian architecture in Sicily, with a sober style that reflects poverty and simplicity.

  • Where Località Badia, Novara di Sicilia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Chiesa di Sant'Ugo Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

17th century, built in the same period as the Cistercian monastery, then destroyed: it preserves a wooden reliquary, the "brocca di Sant'Ugo" (an Arabic vase), a wooden crucifix and the Annunciation of Stethera (1570).

Museo etnoantropologico

Museums · Themed

It preserves historical, artistic and cultural finds of the territory from prehistory to today, in 12 thematic sections (including Antica pharmacy, Sacred Art, Home Fireplace, Geology, Music and theater, Four seasons); the scientific collections come from the University of Messina, the rest from donations and loans.

Maiorchino, nocciole e dolci

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The Mallorchino is a sheep cheese aged over eight months, in 10-12 kg shapes, protagonist of the Majorchino Tournament, practiced since the seventeenth century: at Carnival 16 teams from three players roll the forms for about two kilometers. The hazelnuts, celebrated with a festival in October, come into sweets such as risu niru and cassatelli and in the hazelnut sausage; to try also baked ricotta, ravioli and the ncasciata of Ferragosto.
