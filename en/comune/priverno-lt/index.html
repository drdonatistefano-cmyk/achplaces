What to see in Priverno

Medieval village on the Red Hill, in the Lepini Mountains above the valley of the Amaseno, Orange Flag: the Cistercian Abbey of Fossanova, the Roman colony of Privernum and the cathedral with the relic of St. Thomas Aquinas.

6 places to visit in Priverno, in the province of Latina (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Priverno in the guide with map and filters

Abbazia di Fossanova

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Jewel of Cistercian architecture imported from Burgundy: the abbey church with facade and rose window, the capitular hall, the cloister and the lantern that stands out over the landscape.

  • Where Via San Tommaso d'Aquino, Fossanova, Priverno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Parco archeologico Privernum

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Outdoor museum of twelve hectares of excavations in the locality Mid-August: the Roman colony founded at the end of the II century BC and inhabited until the XII century, with domus with mosaic floors and a large early medieval church.

  • Where Mezzagosto, Priverno
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Archeologico – Palazzo Guarini Antonelli

Museums · Archaeological

Since 2013 in the medieval palace in front of the Duomo, enlarged in the sixteenth century and decorated at the beginning of the twentieth century.

  • Where Piazza Giovanni XXIII, Priverno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Annunziata

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

On the main square next to the Town Hall, it preserves what is considered the skull of St. Thomas Aquinas.

  • Where Piazza Giovanni XXIII, Priverno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Centro storico e cinta muraria

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval nucleus around the Red Hill, closed by walls with seven doors.

  • Where Priverno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello e Parco di San Martino

Nature and landscape · City parks · Viewpoint

Villa built in the middle of the sixteenth century by Cardinal Bartolomeo Gallio, then by the Borghese, with almost three hectares of wood and a clearing.

  • Where Priverno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
