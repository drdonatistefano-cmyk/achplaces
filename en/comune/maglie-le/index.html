What to see in Maglie

Città del Salento with a historic Baroque centre along Via Roma, the eighteenth-century Palazzo Capece, the church of the Madonna delle Grazie and the cultural complex L'Alca with the Museum of Paleontology and Paletnology .Decio de Lorentiis is the birthplace of Aldo Moro.

Museo civico di Paleontologia e Paletnologia «Decio de Lorentiis» (L'Alca)

Museums · Archaeological

Founded in 1960 and since 1999 in the Palazzo Sticchi-Ruberti, noble residence of the early twentieth century that with the Municipal Library forms the cultural complex L'Alca; it is the only museum of the Mezzogiorno dedicated to the reconstruction of the prehistory of Salento, with sections of geology and paleontology, paleolithic and prehistoric art, Neolithic and age of metals, ethnography with African art objects. Touring hours: Mon-Fri 9-13 and 16-18:30, Sat-dom 16-18:30.

MiC cards indicate closed every day, the Touring reports opening hours

Centro storico e via Roma

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Historical center of remarkable artistic and architectural value; along Via Roma follow the forms of the Lecce Baroque and in the center stands the eighteenth-century Palazzo Baronale Capece.

  • Where Via Roma, Maglie
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa e colonna della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church built between 1624 and 1648, with convex façade and bell tower reminiscent of the Duomo of Lecce; next is the column of the Madonna delle Grazie.

  • Where Maglie
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
