What to see in San Lorenzo Bellizzi

Heart of the Calabrian side of the Pollino National Park, a village of stone houses with a view overlooking the Timpa di San Lorenzo, with most of the Raganello Gorges in its territory and the churches of San Lorenzo and Carmine.

5 places to visit in San Lorenzo Bellizzi, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Gole del Raganello

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

State nature reserve in the Pollino National Park, in the municipalities of San Lorenzo Bellizzi, Civita and Cerchiara di Calabria; most of the gorges cross the municipal territory. The river digs a 17 km long canyon from the Lamia Spring to Civita and the Devil's Bridge, also run by rafting.

  • Where Riserva naturale Gole del Raganello, San Lorenzo Bellizzi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.sanlorenzobellizzi.cs.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Well-preserved village of typical stone houses with view, in panoramic position among valleys overlooking the Raganello Gorges. The origins go back to the Neolithic, documented by a burial of about 7,000 years ago in the Cave of Pietra Sant'Angelo; from 1534 passed to the Marquis Pignatelli and preserves traces of the noble families. The name comes from a baronial family that built the first chapel of San Lorenzo. From the village, for the Great Gate, you go up to the highest part of the Park.

  • Where San Lorenzo Bellizzi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · calabriastraordinaria.it
