What to see in Andrate

Country at 836 meters overlooking the Ivrea Moraine Serra and its lakes, with the steep alleys of the ancient Villa, the eighteenth-century parish church of San Pietro in Vincoli, the Museum of peasant civilization and, higher up, the Big Bench of the Pinalba Alpe.

4 places to visit in Andrate, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Ethnographic museum with over 700 objects and tools of the life of a mountain community, set up as thematic workshops: haymaking, milk processing, crafts of the blacksmith, carpenter and mason, and the rooms of the peasant house. A collection of 50 photographs documents the rural life of Andrate; in the park equipped with vintage agricultural means, a lever press and parts of the old forge of the country. Open on Sundays in summer-autumn, 10-12 and 15-18 (calendar 2025: from 6 July to 19 October).

  • Where Regione Salamia, Andrate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org · ecomuseoami.it

Centro storico e parrocchiale di San Pietro in Vincoli

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The inhabited nucleus, once called Villa, preserves its steep alleys; the eighteenth-century parish church of San Pietro in Vincoli, with a separate bell tower overlooking the centre, stands in front of a belvedere. From the village, at 836 meters, the panorama embraces the Ivrea Moraine Serra and its five lakes.

  • Where Via della Parrocchia, Andrate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org · ecomuseoami.it

Santuario di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Rebuilt in 1714 on the remains of one of the first churches dedicated to the Virgin in Canavese, of which remains walls and the Romanesque bell tower; it is located near the Museum of Rural Civilization.

  • Where Presso Regione Salamia, Andrate
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: ecomuseoami.it · turismotorino.org

Big Bench Alpe Pinalba

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Great Orange Bench n. 155 of the Big Bench Community Project of Chris Bangle, at the Alpe Pinalba at 1584 meters: views of the Piedmont Alps, the Monviso, Turin, the Monferrato, the rice fields between Vercellese and Biellese, the Moorish amphitheatre and the Serra d'Ivrea. You come in part by car and partly on foot: from San Giacomo, on the road Ivrea-Mombarone, about 30 minutes on an agro-silvo-pastoral road closed to the cars. Passport stamps in some country exercises.

  • Where Località Pinalba, Andrate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org
