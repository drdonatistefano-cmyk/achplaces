What to see in Santa Severina

Borgo on a tufa spur above the Neto valley, which at dawn looks like a large stone ship: the Norman castle with the museum, the Byzantine Baptistery and the Cathedral of St. Anastasia.

7 places to visit in Santa Severina, in the province of Crotone (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Santa Severina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Norman fortress attributed to Robert Guiscard, on about 10,000 square meters: square mast with four cylindrical towers and bastions, moat, underground labyrinths, stables with medieval frescoes and Baroque paintings by Francesco Giordano. The restorations 1994-1998 brought to light Greek materials, a church and a Byzantine necropolis.

  • Where Piazza Campo, Santa Severina
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Battistero bizantino

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

The oldest Byzantine monument in Calabria, with a circular plan with a Greek cross, reachable from a small port of the Cathedral.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Cattedrale di Sant'Anastasia

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built between 1274 and 1295, with a Latin cross with three naves, dedicated to the patroness.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Museo diocesano di arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

In the Archbishop's Palace, opened in 1998: sacred art from the High Middle Ages to the twentieth century, the Treasure of the Cathedral with an angioin clasp in gold and enamel, the reliquary arm and the silver statue of St. Anastasia in 1792.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
