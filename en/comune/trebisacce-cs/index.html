What to see in Trebisacce

The balcony of the High Ionian Sea, on a plateau overlooking the sea: historic center with the sixteenth century belt against the Saracens, the Mother Church of St. Nicholas of Mira with the Byzantine domes and, in Broglio, the mycenean remains and a Hellenic necropolis.

4 places to visit in Trebisacce, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa Madre di San Nicola di Mira

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1040 in the Byzantine period and rebuilt between the Five and the eighteenth century in Baroque style, it preserves the domes with trullo of concentric circles of tiles and the bell tower with cuspidate tower; inside, with a Latin cross with three naves, the Madonna of the Rosary of the late eighteenth century, a wooden St. Nicholas and a painting of the Trinity of the eighteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Progresso, Trebisacce
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.trebisacce.cs.it · italia.it

Centro storico e mura cinquecentesche

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The upper part of the village, on the plateau that gives the name to Trebisacce (the small table) surrounded in the sixteenth century by walls against Saracen raids; from the village the view extends over the gulf, so much so that it is worth the name of the balcony of the High Ionian.

  • Where Via Bastione, Trebisacce
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.trebisacce.cs.it

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Situated in Broglio, where finds of a Greek necropolis and remains of mycenaean age have been found.
