What to see in Costigliole Saluzzo

Borgo at the entrance of the Varaita Valley among the orchards of the Saluzzo plain: noble palaces, the eighteenth-century bridge over Varaita and the parish with the Christ of Pietà attributed to Hans Clemer.

7 places to visit in Costigliole Saluzzo, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo Sarriod de la Tour

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Ancient eighteenth-century residence of the Saluzzo-Paesana, completely restored, today municipal library and exhibition space.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
