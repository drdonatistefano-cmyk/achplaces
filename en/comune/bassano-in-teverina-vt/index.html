Torre dell'orologio e campanile di Santa Maria dei Lumi

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

A defensive tower with an eighteenth-century majolica clock that hides the 11th century Romanesque bell tower, with stairs illuminated by mirrors and 360° panorama at the top.
