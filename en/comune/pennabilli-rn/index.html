I luoghi dell'anima, museo diffuso di Tonino Guerra

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

Poetic installations scattered in the village and in the valley: the Garden of forgotten fruits, the Angel with mustache, the Rifugio delle Madonne abandoned.

  • Where Pennabilli
  • Entry Free outdoor course; some paid spaces
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Casa dei Mandorli, casa di Tonino Guerra

Museums · House museums

The house where the poet lived 23 years, with his works and memories of cinema (a drawing by Wim Wenders, a watercolor by Antonioni).

  • Where Pennabilli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Belvedere della Gioconda

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The panoramic point dedicated to the landscapes that are thought to have inspired Leonardo, on the valley of the Senatello.

  • Where Pennabilli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
