Museo civico preistorico «Pietro Fedele»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Founded in 1959 by the then mayor Pietro Fedele and re-arranged in 2001, it tells in 40 areas the prehistory of southern Lazio from about a million years ago. It preserves the cranial cap of the Man of Ceprano, called Argil, found by Italo Biddittu in 1994 and dated to over 800,000 years, among the oldest human fossils in Europe; hundreds of original finds (stone and bone instruments, bones and tusks of elephants) and life-size casts of Lucy, Homo habilis and Homo erectus, with many replicas to be touched even for the blind. Open Saturday and Sunday 15.30-18.30; guided tours, workshops and simulated excavations especially for schools.

Castello Baronale e Torre dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The complex is divided into three parts: the ancient courtyard, the Baronial Palace and the Romanesque Clock Tower. The Palazzo dei Colonna, in dark basalt, rebuilt much of the ancient castle between two corner towers of the urban belt; abandoned in the eighteenth century, was restored in the early nineteenth century. The 36-metre-high Romanesque tower was transformed into a bell tower.

  • Where Centro storico, Pofi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1100 in the castle fortress, it was demolished in the early eighteenth century and replaced by a new Greek cross church with six chapels.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Sant'Antonino Martire e il Giudizio Universale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It preserves a fresco of the Last Judgment dating from the 14th to the 15th century, restored with a contribution from the Lazio Region and presented to the community on September 30, 2026, the first major intervention on the work after more than thirty years.
