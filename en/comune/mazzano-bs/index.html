What to see in Mazzano

Municipality of the Brescian plain towards Garda: in Ciliverghe the eighteenth-century Villa Mazzucchelli brings together the museum house of the literary Gianmaria Mazzucchelli and the collections of the Fondazione Giacomini Meo Fiorot.

4 places to visit in Mazzano, in the province of Brescia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa Mazzucchelli – Casa Museo Gianmaria Mazzucchelli

Museums · House museums

Villa of the eighteenth century with a U-shaped plan with palladian pronaos, enlarged by Federico Mazzucchelli and his son Gianmaria until 1753: the museum house preserves period furnishings and frescoes by Francesco Savani.

  • Where Via G. Mazzucchelli 2, Ciliverghe, Mazzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.gardatourism.it

Sources: gardatourism.it
