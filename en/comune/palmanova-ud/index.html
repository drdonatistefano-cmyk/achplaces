What to see in Palmanova

The nine-pointed star city-fortress founded by Venice in 1593, ideal Renaissance city, national monument since 1960 and UNESCO heritage since 2017 with Venetian defence works; among the most beautiful villages in Italy since 2018.

6 places to visit in Palmanova, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Palmanova in the guide with map and filters

Fortificazioni e bastioni

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The three circles of fortifications of the Five, Six and Nineteenth Century, with curtains, bastions, ditches and revells; the third line, the Lunetta, was added by the French of Napoleon in 1805. Route on the walls of about 360,000 square meters.

  • Where Palmanova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismofvg.it

Piazza Grande

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hesagonal square in the center of the fortress, arms square with the main palaces and the eleven statues of the general providers; it houses the permanent exhibition with the machines to build the fortress in real scale.

  • Where Piazza Grande, Palmanova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismofvg.it

Duomo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Cathedral of the sixteenth century overlooking Piazza Grande.

  • Where Piazza Grande, Palmanova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Porte monumentali e Porta Cividale

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The three monumental gates of access to the city; in the donation of the Cividale Porta is located part of the military historical museum.

  • Where Palmanova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo storico militare

Museums · Themed · UNESCO World Heritage

On three locations: Palazzo del Governore alle Armi, Donjone di Porta Cividale with documents, uniforms and weapons of the garrisons from the Serenissima (1593) to today, and external route on the walls; guided tours.

  • Where Piazza Grande, Palmanova
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Civico museo storico

Museums · City history · UNESCO World Heritage

Cinquecento cards, plants of the French fortress, drawings of Venetian engineers (1593-1797), coins and medals of the foundation and documents of the Italian period until the retreat of Caporetto.

  • Where Borgo Udine 4, Palmanova
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
