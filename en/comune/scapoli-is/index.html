What to see in Scapoli

Borgo dell'alta valle del Volturno, known as the capital of zampogna, one of the few countries in Italy where the instrument is still played and the craftsmen build it with traditional techniques; it is recounted by the International Museum of Zampogna in Palazzo Mancini and the International Festival with market exhibition, while the Museum of the Italian Liberation Corps recalls the battle of Monte Marrone.

Museo internazionale della zampogna «P. Vecchione»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 2002 in the historic Palazzo Mancini, it is spread over three floors with zampogne from all over the world and different eras, wind instruments, iconographic and literary documentation, historical photographs and the reconstruction of the workshop of the artisans of Scapolesi; there are also an acoustic listening room, a film library and a permanent nativity scene of the Neapolitan school of the Capuan masters of San Gregorio Armeno. A multimedia app allows you to listen to the sounds of the instruments. Timetable indicated by italia.it: Friday-Sunday 10-12 and 16-18.

  • Where Via Santa Maria 1, Scapoli
  • Entry €1, reduced €0.50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Botteghe dei costruttori di zampogne

Local life and traditions · Crafts · Free entry

In Scapoli survives the artisan construction of the zampogna, handed down by a small group of artisans to the new generations; the instruments are sold in the shops of the district Fontecostanza. The village hosts the International Festival of Fampogna with market exhibition and on 22 April the archaic Falò of San Giorgio.

  • Where Quartiere Fontecostanza, Scapoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it · visitmolise.eu

Museo del Corpo Italiano di Liberazione «Aldo Moro»

Museums · Themed

Dedicated to the Italian Liberation Corps, the unity born in the South after the armistice of 1943 that fought alongside the Allies until September 1944, is based in Scapoli, where was the Allied command and the operation of Monte Marrone was planned. Seven rooms trace the birth of CIL, Montelungo, the Mainarde sector on the Gustav Line and the advance on the Adriatic, until the visit of Aldo Moro to Scapoli in 1968, with original documents and films.

  • Where Scapoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitmolise.eu · italia.it
