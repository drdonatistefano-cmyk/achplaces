What to see in Palizzi

Village of the Aspromonta Greek area, narrow between a bend of the Palizzi river and a rocky spur, dominated by the castle, national monument; on the coast the Lighthouse of Capo Spartivento.

Castello di Palizzi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Rocca on a rocky ridge with steep walls, 300 meters above Palizzi Superiore, perhaps from the thirteenth century or built by the Ruffo in the fourteenth; born as a fortified building dug into the living rock, it has been renovated several times and is declared a national monument. It is the subject of a consolidation and restoration for the tourist usability financed by the MiC. View of the valley and the Ionian Greek coast.

  • Where Palizzi Superiore, Palizzi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page sabap-rc.cultura.gov.it

Sources: sabap-rc.cultura.gov.it · programmazionestrategica.cultura.gov.it

Centro storico di Palizzi Superiore

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village between a river bend and a rocky spur, with narrow streets, steep staircases and stone houses leaning against the rock; it is part of the Hellenophonous area of Aspromontana, of which it preserves culture and language.

  • Where Palizzi Superiore, Palizzi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page sabap-rc.cultura.gov.it

Sources: sabap-rc.cultura.gov.it

Faro di Capo Spartivento

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Coastal lighthouse, visible from the outside; archaeological investigations of the last twenty years have found traces of human attendance from the age of Copper to the Greek era in the area. The MiC indicates opening every day 8-18, without reservation.

  • Where Capo Spartivento, 89038 Palizzi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page sabap-rc.cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sabap-rc.cultura.gov.it
