What to see in Saint-Oyen

Small village at 1373 meters at the foot of the Comba di Bosses, on the Via Francigena towards the Gran San Bernardo.

3 places to visit in Saint-Oyen, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Château Verdun

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Grande casaforte donated in 1137 by Amedeo III of Savoia to the canons of the Gran San Bernardo, who still own it; stop on the Via Francigena, today home.

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Eugendo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
