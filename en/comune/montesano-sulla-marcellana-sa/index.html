What to see in Montesano sulla Marcellana

Spa village at 850 meters overlooking the Vallo di Diano, the easternmost municipality of Campania, with the scenic church of Sant'Anna in the square, six-nineteenth-century palaces, the MuMont ethnographic museum and, south of the village, the ancient abbey of Cadossa linked to San Cono.

Chiesa di Sant'Anna

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1954 and 1959 by the Italian-Venetian entrepreneur Filippo Gagliardi, in Gothic style; it incorporates the remains of the ancient church of Santa Sofia. Facade rich in statues and rosettes, two high bell towers (one with sundial, the other with clock), interior with three naves with lateral altars, frescoes and sculptures.

Centro storico e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village, which traditionally was born from the inhabitants of the ancient Marcellinum climbed on the hill, dominates the Vallo di Diano. Among the historical buildings the church of the Assumption of the early eighteenth century, the church of Sant'Andrea of the fourteenth century, Palazzo Cestari of the seventeenth century, Palazzo Gerbasio of the eighteenth century with stone portal and Palazzo Abatemarco of the second half of the nineteenth century.

  • Where Centro storico, Montesano sulla Marcellana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

MuMont - Museo Montesano

Museums · Themed

Already Ethno-anthropological Civic Museum, reopened with a new name on November 5, 2021, with the exhibition curated by Alessandra Aufiero. It tells the traditional agricultural and craft world: yokes and finishes, the corner of the blacksmith with zippers and locks, a bedroom rebuilt with rocking iron cradle, the sideboard and the tools for the oven, measures for the wheat and stadera, the corner of the shoemaker, traditional clothes and documents. It also hosts temporary exhibitions and workshops.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Abbazia di Santa Maria di Cadossa

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

To the south of the village, in a place rich in water and vegetation: documented from the thirteenth century, passed between 1294 and 1306 to the hospitable order of the Holy Trinity of Venosa and, by tradition, lived there San Cono di Teggiano. In 1514 Pope Leo X joined her to the Charterhouse of Padula, of which she became a grange; the new church of 1578 has baroque stuccoes and the Assumption on the high altar. Succeeded in 1866, in 1869 it was bought by the Barons Gerbasio and used as a residence of the settlers, storehouse and stable. On the first Sunday of August there is the pilgrimage in honor of San Cono.

Purchased by private individuals in 1869; the source does not indicate how to visit (Common 0975 865211)

Complesso termale

Local life and traditions · Spas · Viewpoint

The village is surrounded by forests and springs of water with low mineral content (in Santo Stefano the water is bottled). The spa complex, among the main attractions of the village, offers well-equipped paths among the trees and wide views of the mountains of Basilicata.
