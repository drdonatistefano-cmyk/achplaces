What to see in Pozzomaggiore

Logudoro-Meilogu country with the late-Gothic parish church of San Giorgio, the sanctuary of San Costantino where since 1923 Ardia has been running on 6 and 7 July, the birthplace of Blessed Edvige Carboni and the Horse Museum in the former Augustinian convent; the territory has about 40 nuragic sites and the Roman bridge of Oinu.

6 places to visit in Pozzomaggiore, in the province of Sassari (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e parrocchiale di San Giorgio Megalo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church was built in late Gothic forms in the middle of the sixteenth century: five spans, a mayor capilla covered with a starry vault and facade with buttresses and stairs, whose portal recalls that of St. Peter extra muros of Bosa. On the historic center overlooking noble residences like Palazzo Parpaglia.

  • Where Centro storico, Pozzomaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Casa natale della beata Edvige Carboni

Museums · House museums · Free entry

The house in which he was born on 2 May 1880 and spent his youth Edvige Carboni, mystic died in Rome on 17 February 1952 and proclaimed blessed, was brought back to its original conditions and inaugurated on 17 February 2002. According to the Mic tab opens Saturday afternoon and Sunday (different hours between summer and sun) and also on midweek holidays, except for the main holidays; off time on notice.

  • Where Via Edvige Carboni 16A, Pozzomaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Museo del Cavallo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the former convent of the Augustinians, active from the early seventeenth to the second half of the nineteenth century and then barracks of the Carabinieri on horseback, ten rooms tell with historical, anthropological and ethnographic cut the role of the horse, central in the culture of the country; it also houses the Donation Pesarin of architraves, capitals, columns and stone frames recovered from the historic center.

Temporarily closed for renovation works (SardiniaCulture card updated to 13/10/2026); can open for parties and events

  • Where Piazza Convento 39, Pozzomaggiore
  • Entry €2 (before closing), free up to 10 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Church of Neo-Romanesque-Byzantine installation, theater of the Ardia di San Costantino, the equestrian race that takes place from 1923 on 6 and 7 July.

  • Where Pozzomaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it
