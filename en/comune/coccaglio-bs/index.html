What to see in Coccaglio

The town of Franciacorta at the foot of Mount Orfano, with a historic heart enclosed in the walls of an ancient Roman fortified castrum in the 12th century; it houses the Museum of Oriental Art with the Japanese collection of the traveller Pompeo Mazzocchi and, in the Park of the Alps, the Montorfano Geological Park.

Museo d'Arte Orientale - Collezione Mazzocchi

Museums · Themed

It gathers about 500 items, mostly Japanese, brought by Pompeo Mazzocchi (1829-1915), merchant and entrepreneur of Coccaglio who traveled to Europe, Africa and Asia, especially in Japan, on the trail of the silkworm to save the Lombard silk-growing. Weapons, ivory, xilographies, lacquers, jewelry, paintings, ceramics, fabrics, photographs and bronzes are ordered in four rooms: Gelso (Mazzocchi e la bachicoltura), Cherry (history of Japan), Bamboo (daily objects) and Acero (prints ukiyo-e). The Municipality indicates opening on Wednesdays from 15 to 18; guided tours and workshops on request; ticket at variable cost.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website maomazzocchi.altervista.org

Parco Geologico del Montorfano

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

A path inside the Park of the Alps, carried out by the volunteers of the Speleo Group, which aligns rocks and formations of different geological eras to tell the history of the entire province of Brescia: 18 stages with panels and audioguide, from the mica-scists of Maniva to the formation of Collio, from the limestone of Esino to the Maiolica and the stone of Sarnico, to the conglomerate of Montorfano. Open every day from 8 am to 11 pm, accessible to disabled people; guided tours also for schools on request.

  • Where Parco degli Alpini, Via delle Calcine 32, Coccaglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page franciacorta.wine

Sources: comune.coccaglio.bs.it · franciacorta.wine

Centro storico e cinta medievale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The historic heart is enclosed by the walls of an ancient Roman castrum, fortified in the 12th century with towers. In the square of Santa Maria Calchera stands the palace built in the second half of the sixteenth century by the Coccaglio family, extinct at the end of the seventeenth century. For the feast of saints patrons Maurizio and Giacinto squares and alleys of the castle are animated by stalls of local products. Coccaglio is also home to the madrigalist Luca Marenzio (1553-1599).

  • Where Via Castello, Coccaglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page franciacorta.wine

Sources: franciacorta.wine · comune.coccaglio.bs.it

Parco Belvedere

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

Large green area overlooking Mount Orfano, next to the bike path that connects Coccaglio and Cologne, with benches and games for children; free access and accessible for the disabled.

  • Where Via San Pietro, Coccaglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page franciacorta.wine
