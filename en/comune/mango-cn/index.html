What to see in Mango

The village of the top of the Moscato Langa, dominated by the castle of the Marquises of Busca, seat of the Regional Enoteca Colline del Moscato and the Pinacoteca delle Langhe; in the village of San Donato the ethnographic collection "Cà delle memorie" and, for families, the thematic path Gnomovia between vineyards and hazelnuts.

3 places to visit in Mango, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello dei marchesi di Busca ed Enoteca Regionale Colline del Moscato

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It rises on the remains of a fortress of the twelfth century; the current building was built in 1630 by the Marquises of Busca, renovated in Baroque style and recently restored. On the ground floor, the Enoteca Regionale Colline del Moscato collects the products of over 80 wine producers and 20 food and wine producers of 52 municipalities between Cuneo, Asti and Alessandria, with tastings dedicated to Moscato d'Asti DOCG, Asti Spumante, Canelli DOCG and Strevi DOC; the castle also houses the Pinacoteca delle Langhe, concerts, exhibitions and events. Dominates the old village with views of the hills.

Wine shop closed in January and from Tuesday to Friday according to Visit MRL

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre 19, Mango
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlmr.it · visitpiemonte.com

Gnomovia

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Thematic path for families partly dirt and easy to cross, starting from via Cossano Belbo: panoramas on vineyards, hazelnuts and woods, installations such as "Bice la frona felice" (the happy frog), "Fruteto Magico," the Fairy Terraces of Gnomo Rob (locality Navirotto) and the Tree of the vine, the painted Cisterna, thematic alleys with tiles and painted antinas, 16 points linked to the work of Fenoglio and a path of meridians and frescoes of Tore Milano. It can be used with pushchairs, not entirely in wheelchairs; recommended from spring to autumn.

  • Where Via Cossano Belbo, Mango
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it

Raccolta permanente «Cà delle memorie»

Museums · Themed

Small permanent collection created in 1987 as the venue of exhibitions and activities of the Cultural Association . The Arvangia , with the reconstructions of a rural cuisine and a traditional bedroom.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlmr.it
