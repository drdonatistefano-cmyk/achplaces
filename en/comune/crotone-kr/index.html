What to see in Crotone

The ancient Kroton on the Ionian: the Castle of Charles V on the Greek acropolis with the Viceroyal Walls, the National Archaeological Museum with the treasure of Hera, and a few kilometers the promontory of Capo Colonna with the only surviving column of the temple of Hera Lacinia.

12 places to visit in Crotone, in the province of Crotone (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parco archeologico di Capo Colonna

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the former promontory called Lakinion Akron, overlooking the Marine Protected Area of Capo Rizzuto: the shrine of Hera Lacinia, linked to the Greek colony of Kroton, of which only one column of the great Doric temple remains on the edge of the cliff; next to a more ancient place of worship, a building for banquets, a Roman-age settlement and baronial villas of the eighteenth century.

  • Where Via Hera Lacinia, Crotone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo archeologico di Capo Colonna

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 2002 inside the park, on a floor with three rooms (the Earth, the Sacred, the Sea): a corinzio helmet in bronze of the fifth century, a stone with the dedication to the athlete Phayllos, a statue of Zeus Meilichios and a small group in marble of Love and Psyche recovered at sea; around the Garden of Hera.

  • Where Via Hera Lacinia, Crotone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Torre Nao

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Quadrangular coastal tower of the sixteenth century at Capo Colonna, completed in 1568; after the Unity of Italy hosted a brigade of the Guardia di Finanza.

  • Where Capo Colonna, Crotone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa della Madonna di Capo Colonna

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

The small church next to the ancient shrine of Capo Colonna, the destination of the May pilgrimage with the icon of Our Lady brought from the cathedral.

  • Where Capo Colonna, Crotone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sigecweb.beniculturali.it

Museo archeologico nazionale di Crotone

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Born from the collections of the Civic Museum of 1909 and opened by the State in 1968, on two floors: on the ground floor the history of Kroton with athletes, doctors and philosophers and the necropolis of Carrara; on the first floor the sanctuaries, with the treasure of Hera Lacinia, the gold diadem and the bronze nuragic boat of the VII century BC, and the askos shaped as a siren of the fifth century BC.

  • Where Via Risorgimento, Crotone
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Castello di Carlo V

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the ancient acropolis of Kroton, at the highest point of the city, between the sea and the countryside: rebuilt in the current forms since 1541 by the Viceroy Pedro de Toledo on a project by Giangiacomo D'Acaja and Giovanni Maria Buzzacarino, a polygonal plan with the bastions of San Giacomo and Santa Caterina; it houses the Civic Museum and the Municipal Library.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Crotone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo civico

Museums · City history

In the Castle of Charles V, in the barracks Undercampana and in the Tower Helping: tiles and ceramics of the historic center, coins, hunting daggers and swords of the eighteenth-nineteenth century, ceramics of Gerace, Seminara and Squillace, three colubs recovered at sea and the coats of arms of noble families.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Crotone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

MACK - Museo Arte Contemporanea Crotone

Museums · Modern art

In Palazzo Barracco, Italian art from the second post-war period to today, with works by Mimmo Rotella, Bruno Ceccobelli, Mario Merz and international artists such as Vanessa Beecroft and Jannis Kounellis.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Crotone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Cattedrale

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

It dates back to the 9th century, with neoclassical façade and three Baroque naves inside: it houses a baptismal font from the 12th century and the icon of the Black Madonna of Capo Colonna.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Crotone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Piazza Pitagora

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The main square of the city, meeting point of the crotonese, surrounded by porticoes of the second half of the nineteenth century; from here Via Vittoria leads to Piazza Duomo.

  • Where Piazza Pitagora, Crotone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Mura e bastioni vicereali

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The belt rebuilt after 1541 by order of Pedro de Toledo with five pentagonal bulwarks (Don Pedro, Toledo, Marquis, Villafranca, Pedro Nigro) and two rivets, the Bastione del Fosso with date 1550 and the Bunny of 1595.

  • Where Centro storico, Crotone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
