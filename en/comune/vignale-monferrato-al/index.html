What to see in Vignale Monferrato

Medieval village on one of the highest hills of Monferrato, among the nine municipalities of the core zone In the center of the eighteenth century Palazzo Callori, seat of the Regional Winery of Monferrato; every summer the Vignale Monferrato Festival brings to the village the international dance.

Palazzo Callori

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The main monument of the village, of the eighteenth century but built on medieval foundations and several times restored and expanded: two factory bodies connected by a staircase to crossed ramps, with ancient stone cellars. It houses the Regional Winery of Monferrato and a restaurant with local specialities, wines and aromatized grappas.

  • Where Centro storico, Vignale Monferrato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: alexala.it · old.visitpiemonte.com

Infernot Belvedere

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · UNESCO World Heritage

Visitable infernot of the country: the infernots are small environments excavated in limestone stone and used for the aging of wine, symbol of the core UNESCO zones "Il Monferrato degli Infernot" . Visits on Sunday 15-18 from April to October, on the other days by reservation (info@monferratodegliinfernot.it); you should call first.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
