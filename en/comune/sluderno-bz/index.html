What to see in Sluderno

The village of the upper Val Venosta dominated by Castel Chur, built in 1259 and for over 500 years by the Trapp family, with its famous armory; in the village the Vuseum, the museum of the Val Venosta, from which a path along the Leitenwaal and Berkwaal routes rises to the prehistoric site of the Ganglegg.

Castel Coira (Churburg)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Built in 1259 and transformed from a medieval fortress into a Renaissance residence, it is one of the best preserved castles in South Tyrol and has belonged to the Trapps for over 500 years. The courtyard is surrounded by a painted Renaissance loggia; inside the Romanesque chapel, the room of Giacomo with carved ceiling, the hall of the Matsch with family portraits, the oldest sounding organ of South Tyrol and the armory, the most important private dynastic armory in the world, with armor made for family members. Guided tour only; from the village about 20 minutes walk.

closed from early November to late March, reopens on 20 March 2027 (churburg.com, vinschgau.net)

  • Where Churburg 1, Sluderno
  • Entry €16, families €36, children under 6 years free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.churburg.com

Sources: churburg.com · vinschgau.net

Vuseum - Museo della Val Venosta

Museums · City history

It tells how the inhabitants of the valley have adapted in the millennia to difficult conditions: finds of the site of the Ganglegg from the Neolithic to the Roman age, finds along the Via Claudia Augusta, the history of the roges (the Waale of the Malser Haide are from 2023 immaterial heritage UNESCO), the ... children of Swabia emigrant to work from the seventeenth century, the street vendors ...Karrner and the stallion Avelignes Folie, born in 1874, parent of the race. Changing exhibitions and film screenings; 5 minutes walk from the station.

  • Where Via Merano 1, Sluderno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.vinschgau.net

Sources: vinschgau.net

Sentiero delle rogge Leitenwaal e Berkwaal e sito del Ganglegg

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

It is a ring of 6.8 km, about 3 hours and 290 meters of difference in altitude, suitable for families: from the Vuseum the path 18 climbs to the archaeological site of Ganglegg, with settlements of the Bronze and Iron Age; then the path 17 along the Leitenwaal Roggia to the gorge and the descent on the Berkwaal (17A) passing under Castel Coira, to the station. Water has been flowing in the streams since April.

  • Where Partenza dal Vuseum, Via Merano 1, Sluderno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.vinschgau.net

Sources: suedtirol.info · vinschgau.net
