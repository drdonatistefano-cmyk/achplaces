What to see in Pollein

Municipality of plain and woods at the gates of Aosta, under the Mont Emilius, already of the lords of Quart and then of the bishops of Aosta.

3 places to visit in Pollein, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Pollein in the guide with map and filters

Parco arboreo e Giardino delle rocce

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Free entry · Viewpoint

A path through boulders of the main rock types of the Valdostane mountains, surrounded by shrubs and tall trees, accessible by the stroller.

  • Where Grand-Place, Pollein
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt at the end of the 17th century and renovated several times, with a beautiful Romanesque-Gothic bell tower.

  • Where Pollein
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Area verde Grand-Place

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 10 hectares of meadows for the popular sports of the Val d'Aosta such as rebatta, fiolet, tsan and palet, reachable by bike from the bike path along the Dora.

  • Where Grand-Place, Pollein
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it
