What to see in Teggiano

The ancient Diano, Sanseverino city perched on a hill overlooking the Vallo di Diano: Aragonese castle, cathedral with fourteenth-century tombs, Franciscan churches and a network of small museums in the old walled town.

9 places to visit in Teggiano, in the province of Salerno (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Teggiano in the guide with map and filters

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Cathedral of the diocese, documented since 1146, has guarded the remains of San Cono patrono since 1261: portal and pulpit in stone (1271) of Melchiorre da Montalbano, the tomb of Enrico Sanseverino di Tino di Camaino (1336), the tombs of Bear Malavolta and Stasio d'Heustasio, the chapel of San Cono. Closed on Monday.

  • Where Via Discesa S. Maria, Teggiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.teggiano.sa.it · italia.it

Castello di Teggiano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress of probably Norman origin of the Sanseverino, strengthened by Ferrante d'Aragona after the Conspiracy of the Barons: pentagonal belt with angular towers, the mast and the Tower of the Cimmaruca; from the terrace you can see the whole Vallo di Diano. Private property, partly exhibition space; in August the re-enactment of the siege of 1497.

  • Where Piazza Portello, Teggiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.teggiano.sa.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Franciscan Church was completed in 1307 and has a fourteenth-century portal of the mystical lamb, a holy waterworks held by the ancient synagogue, an eighteenth-century wooden pulpit and a guazzo-painted ceiling of 1745 by Francesco De Martino. Closed on Monday.

  • Where Piazza dello Stato di Diano, Teggiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.teggiano.sa.it · italia.it

Museo diocesano «San Pietro»

Museums · Themed

In the former church of St. Peter, built on a classical temple: stone sculptures of the ancient and medieval city, liturgical furnishings, statues and paintings from the Middle Ages to the contemporary age, and the fresco of the Madonna del Latte. Closed on Monday.

  • Where Piazza Valentino Vignone, Teggiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.teggiano.sa.it · italia.it

Lapidario Dianense

Museums · Archaeological

Section of the diocesan museum opened in 2016 in the ancient chapel of Sant'Eligio: 1700 years of Teggiano's stone, with Roman funerary newsstands, medieval capitals, coat of arms, the arch of the Malavolta chapel and an Aragonese plate linked to the Conspira dei Baroni; the visit continues in the frescoed succorpe of Santa Venera. Closed on Monday.

  • Where Via Rampa Sant'Angelo, Teggiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo delle Erbe

Museums · Science and university

Reconstruction of a 1480 dianense spices shop with plant spices, animals and minerals, section on natural remedies, bank of ancient seeds of the Cilento Park, herbarium and the external Viridarium with medicinal plants.

  • Where Piazza Santissima Pietà, Teggiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.teggiano.sa.it · italia.it

Museo delle arti e tradizioni popolari

Museums · Themed

Objects of everyday life, work and religiosity popular between the late nineteenth and mid twentieth centuries: tools of peasants, spinners, carpenters and blacksmiths, and photographic plates of the historic center. Open Monday and Sunday.

  • Where Via Santa Barbara, Teggiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.teggiano.sa.it · italia.it

Museo di San Cono

Museums · Themed

Paintings, posters, vestments and books on the life of the monk Cone, patron of the city, and on the devotion brought by the immigrants overseas. Open Monday and Sunday.

  • Where Via Roma, Teggiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.teggiano.sa.it · italia.it

Centro storico, mura e belvedere

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town is still partly surrounded by the walls, with churches and remains of previous eras re-used in the buildings; from the viewpoint the view stretches over the Diano Valley.

  • Where Teggiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
