Santuario di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Born after the apparitions of the Archangel of 490, 492 and 493, it is a continuous pilgrimage destination: from the octagonal tower of 27 meters, 86 steps descend to the bronze gates merged in Constantinople in 1076 and to the cave with the altar and the statue of the saint; in the Lombard crypts the inscriptions of the dukes of Benevento. Longobard tracks have been a UNESCO heritage since 2011.

  • Where Monte Sant'Angelo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Abbazia di Santa Maria di Pulsano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill of Pulsano, on rocks of more than 200 meters, founded in the sixth century according to the tradition of St. Gregory the Great; 24 hermitages depend on it dug into the rock, connected by paths and steps. Opened to worship in 1997.

  • Where Pulsano, Monte Sant'Angelo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Foresta Umbra

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 10,500 hectares in the Gargano National Park, between Vieste, Vico del Gargano and Monte Sant'Angelo, UNESCO natural heritage since 2017 for beech woods: trails of various difficulties, the Lake of Umbra and 25 picnic areas.

  • Where Foresta Umbra, Monte Sant'Angelo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
