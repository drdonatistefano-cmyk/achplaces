Torre del Cerrano

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The coastal tower is made of square brick on a small height near the beach, built in 1568 in the sighting line desired by Carlo V against the incursions from the sea; the upper and lateral parts were added in the last century by the families who lived in it. Since 1981 it has been from the Province of Teramo; the historians link the archaeological remains in front of the tower to the port of ancient Hatria (Atri). Today it houses a research and veterinary training center, a library, the information point of the Marine Protected Area and a Museum of the Sea in preparation; guided tours.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Spiagge e pineta litoranea

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the most popular seaside resorts in the Middle Adriatic, awarded with the Blue Flag. The pine forest of Pinus pinea was planted in the first decades of the twentieth century, when the area was almost depopulated, at the initiative of the Filiani family of Atri, after the levelling of the ground and the contribution of fertile land.

  • Where Lungomare, Pineto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Villa Filiani e Museo Etnomusicologico d'Abruzzo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

An ancient residence of the Filiani family wanted by Giacinto Filiani, in rural style with double central staircase and internal altar frescoed; until the fifties some rooms served to the bachiculture, from which the mulberry trees of the garden. Restored by the Municipality in 2005, it is the seat of the Museo Etnomusicologico d'Abruzzo and hosts exhibitions, conferences and concerts; accessible with monastery. Information to the IAT Office (055 9491745).

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
