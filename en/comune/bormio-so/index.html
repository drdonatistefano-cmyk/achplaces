What to see in Bormio

Alpine centre of the Alta Valtellina at the foot of the Ortles-Cevedale group, skiing and spa destination; the historic centre, flourishing between the 14th and 16th centuries, revolves around the Kuerc and the Torre delle Ore, with frescoed churches, the Civic Museum in Palazzo De Simoni and the Bridge of Combo.

Centro storico di Bormio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

For the position on the ancient trade routes between north and south of Europe Bormio enjoyed long autonomy and wealth: for about seven hundred years it was a democracy in the heart of the Alps. The center preserves medieval remains and civil and religious buildings of the XIV-XVI centuries, narrow streets with portals, frescoes of the fifteenth to sixteenth century on the facades, fountains and wash-houses; in Piazza Cavour dominates the collegiate church of Saints Gervasio and Protasio, and the church of San Vitale has fourteenth-century frescoes on the façade.

  • Where Via Roma, Bormio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Piazza del Kuerc e Torre delle Ore

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Kuerc (the cover) is a loggia symbol of Bormio, was born in wood in 1387, burned in 1855 and was rebuilt: here justice was administered and popular councils were gathered; noteworthy are the dragon-shaped showers. The fourteenth-century Torre delle Ore, with coats of arms, sundial and painted clock, housed the Bajona bell that called the population to assemblies, parties and emergencies. On the square there is also the Cortivo, an ancient office of archives, council and warehouses, with signs of the Milanese period.

  • Where Piazza Cavour, Bormio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: bormio.eu · italia.it

Chiesa del Santo Spirito

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Deconsecrated church built before the 14th century, called the "Sistina" of Bormio: the interior is decorated with frescoes of the Four-Fifteenth century dedicated above all to the Trinity and the Holy Spirit, with the Annunciation in the presbytery, saints wanted by private clients on the walls and once sixteenth century with Trinity, Apostles, Virgin, Evangelists, Fathers of the Church and Prophets.

Chiesa del Crocefisso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Simple façade with rosette and a fresco by Imago Pietatis, 18th century bell tower in Lombard style. Inside the frescoes of the Three-Fourteenth century with the Passion of Christ, the Coronation of the Virgin by the Trinity and the life of St. Anthony Abbot; in the right chapel a wooden crucifix considered miraculous, in the left one the former vows.

Ponte di Combo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

An arched bridge built directly on the rock, in its current form since 1591, with two eighteenth-century newsstands (one dedicated to St. John Nepomuceno, protector from the floods). In the Middle Ages it was the only crossing of the Frodolfo torrent, with a customs for those who arrived from the pass Gavia.

  • Where Strada per il Passo Gavia, Bormio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: bormio.eu · italia.it

Museo civico di Bormio (Palazzo De Simoni)

Museums · City history

In the seventeenth century Palazzo De Simoni, built on the remains of a medieval castle of which the tower remains, 27 rooms and over 4,000 objects between historical-artistic and ethnographic section: carpenter and slipper shops, tools of milk, fields and weaving, old skis, relics of the First World War, paintings, shovels and furniture, and a great diligence in service on the Stelvio until 1915 with carriages and sleds.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · bormio.eu · italia.it

Museo Mineralogico e Naturalistico

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1972 on the initiative of Edy Romani, it exhibits more than 14,000 crystals and minerals from the surrounding valleys and from all over the world, with a naturalistic area that reconstructs the mountain environment with flora and fauna; annexed a laboratory of hand working of the hard stones. Open every day 9-12.30 and 15-19.

Giardino botanico alpino Rezia

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Free entry · Viewpoint

Founded in 1979, at about 1,500 meters at the foot of Mount Reit near the Stelvio National Park: about 15,000 m2 with over 2,500 species in four environments (flora of the Park, of the European and world mountain ranges, Arctic and Antarctica); it collects and reproduces rare plants. Open from July to September.

  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Cantine del Braulio

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

In the underground tunnels under the historic center ages for two years in oak barrels of Slavonia the bitter Braulio, recipe of 1875 of the pharmacist Francesco Peloni with local herbs such as gentian, juniper and absinthe; the visit to the infusion room and filtration, alcohol storage and cellars. Visits Wednesday and Saturday.

Bormio 2000 e Cima Bianca

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Bormio 2000, the heart of the ski area, can be reached by cable car; another cable car climbs to the panoramic Cima Bianca (3.012 m), from which the legendary Stelvio track leads to the village (1.225 m). Bormio is also a base for excursions in the Ortles-Cevedale group.
