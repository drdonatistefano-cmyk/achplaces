Villa Medicea La Magia

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Casa-torre dei Panciatichi passed to the Medici in 1583 and renovated by Buontalenti, base for hunting in the Royal Barco; Italian garden, lemon and romantic park; in the halls the Museo del Filet.

  • Where Via Magia 10, Quarrata
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website villegiardinimedicei.it

Sources: villegiardinimedicei.it · visittuscany.com

Parco della Magia e opere di land art

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

The park of 30 hectares with works by international artists spread in the landscape and the Wood of Magic, protected area with 26 species of butterflies.

  • Where Villa La Magia, Quarrata
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Collezione d'Arte Sacra della Ferruccia

Museums · Old masters

In the church of Saints Philip and James works, furnishings and liturgical vestments guarded for centuries.
