What to see in Torralba

The village of the nuragic palace of Santu Antine and the Nuraghi Valley, thirty towers and ten tombs of the Giants in 37 square kilometers.

3 places to visit in Torralba, in the province of Sassari (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Nuraghe Santu Antine – Sa Domu de su Re

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The most imposing complex after Su Nuraxi: today's 17-and-a-half-metre mast, which is estimated to have reached the 24, triangular bastion with three towers, the largest known courtyard and a village of 14 huts.

  • Where Valle dei Nuraghi, Torralba
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Museo della Valle dei Nuraghi

Museums · Archaeological

It collects the finds of the excavations of the palace and valley, including a model of nuraghe and a small bronze in the shape of a dog, with an ethnographic section.

  • Where Torralba
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Valle dei Nuraghi e nuraghe Oes

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The plain of Cabu Abbas, among the highest density areas of nuraghi; the nuraghe Oes, 800 meters from Santu Antine in the territory of Giave, is almost an architectural unicum.

  • Where Cabu Abbas, Torralba
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
