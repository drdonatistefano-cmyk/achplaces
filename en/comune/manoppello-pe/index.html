What to see in Manoppello

Borgo on a hill at 257 m rises on the ruins of the ancient Pollitium, known for the Basilica of the Holy Face that has guarded the veil with the face of Christ since 1506; in the plain the Cistercian abbey of Santa Maria Arabona with the nearby Roman villa.

Basilica del Volto Santo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

It preserves the Holy Face, a very fine and transparent veil of about 17 x 24 cm with the image of a face of man, arrived at Manoppello according to tradition in 1506, and preserved in a silver reliquary of Neapolitan eighteenth century on the main altar in polychrome marble (1914). The complex, rebuilt between 1848 and 1965 with a façade in white and pink stone, preserves of the seventeenth century phase only the chapel of 1686; annexed to the "treoro" with a wooden tabernacle and a seventeenth-century painting of the Emilian school.

  • Where Via dei Cappuccini 26, Manoppello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.parcomajella.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it · parcomajella.it

Abbazia di Santa Maria Arabona

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Cistercian Abbey probably born from a donation of 1197, with works started by 1208 and interrupted after choir, transept and capitular hall; in the chorus sculptures thirteenth century and frescoes attributed to Antonio da Atri. From the end of the sixteenth century to the eighteenth century the Franciscans stayed there, then passed to the Zambra family until 1968 and then to the diocese. The Superintendence indicates it as an example of the Cistercian Gothic in Italy.

Villa romana di Santa Maria Arabona

History and architecture · Archaeology

A few tens of meters from the abbey, a site destined to productive activities in the late Roman age, with paved mosaics and a thermal system. Private area, available upon reservation (085 859 0003).

  • Where Località Santa Maria Arabona, Manoppello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.parcomajella.it

Sources: sabapchpe.cultura.gov.it · comune.manoppello.pe.it · parcomajella.it

Centro storico e chiese

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village, rebuilt by the Longobards in the 8th century on the ruins of Pollitium, preserves small noble palaces of the 18th to the 19th century in the stone of Manoppello and the castle donated in 874 by the emperor Ludovico II to the abbey of San Clemente in Casauria. To see the church of San Nicola (XIV century) with its portal, the SS. Annunziata beside the former convent of the Clarisse and San Pancrazio.

  • Where Corso Santarelli, Manoppello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.parcomajella.it

Sources: comune.manoppello.pe.it · parcomajella.it
