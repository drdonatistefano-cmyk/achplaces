What to see in Formello

Borgo dell'Agro Veientano along the Via Francigena, on the edge of the Veio Park: Palazzo Chigi with the Museo dell'Agro Veientano and the tower of Andrea Bruno, the church of San Lorenzo with the dark room sundial, the baroque villa of Versaglia, the catacomb of Monte Stallone and the Mola in the Valley of Sorbo.

Palazzo Chigi e Museo dell'Agro Veientano (MAV)

Museums · Archaeological · Viewpoint

Born as watchtower at the end of the thirteenth century, castle Orsini (stone of 1373) and noble residence in the present form since 1464, with porticoed courtyard with octagonal columns of pepper; from 1661 of the Chigi, with cardinal Flavio who created a museum of curiosity. Re-opened in 2011 after 25 years of restoration, it hosts the MAV (established in 1992), which tells the territory from the necropolis of the Iron Age to the Etruscan age, to the Roman conquest of Veio in 396 BC and to the feuds Orsini and Chigi; the rebuilt steel tower Corten by Andrea Bruno has a staircase with multimedia settings and views of the Agro Veientano.

  • Where Piazza San Lorenzo, Formello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Chiesa di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

On the main square of the historic center, of medieval origin with bell tower of the fourteenth century and sixteenth-century forms, with three naves on pillars; it preserves two frescoes by Donato Palmieri, a major altar in marble of the eighteenth century and the dark chamber sundial inserted in the floor in 1796 on the model of that of Santa Maria degli Angeli in Rome.

  • Where Piazza San Lorenzo, Formello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Villa Versaglia

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Summer residence designed by Cardinal Flavio Chigi and built from 1665 on a project by Felice della Greca, with tower, church of San Francesco di Sales, Casino, housing of shepherds, citroniera and cavities dug into the tuff; fallen after 1693, it has been of the Municipality since 2009 and restored since 2020 in the Network of Historic Houses of Lazio, frame of the concerts of Formello Chigi Festival.

access only by reservation, during events or guided tours of the MAV (Municipality of Formello)

  • Where Via della Villa 98, Formello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.formello.rm.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Catacomba di Monte Stallone

History and architecture · Archaeology

A small early Christian cemetery discovered in the 1960s by the archaeologists of the British School at Rome, used between the 4th and 5th centuries: an underground with five short corridors and a cubicle, loculi closed by mortar and bricks and walls plastered with white, without elaborate frescoes.

not open to the public, visit by reservation through the Museo dell'Agro Veientano (Italy.it)

  • Where Via della Villa 101, Formello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Casolare 311 – Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Farmhouse built in the fifties by the Maremma with the reform of agriculture, transformed by the owners into a museum: manual agricultural tools and animal traction from the thirties to the sixties and a traditional cuisine rebuilt; it is also educational farm and information point of the Park of Veio.

  • Where Via dei Santi Martiri 12, Formello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parchilazio.it · italia.it

Valle del Sorbo e Mola di Formello

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the Regional Natural Park of Veio (about 15,000 hectares on the territory of the ancient Etruscan city, crossed by the Via Francigena), the Valle del Sorbo is a Site of Community Interest between Campagnano and Formello with small waterfalls, streams, oaks and meadows; there stands the Mola, water mill built by the Orsini in the fourteenth century and active until 1950.

  • Where Valle del Sorbo, Formello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Centro per l'Incisione e la Grafica d'Arte

Museums · Modern art

Opened in 2003 in a former wash-house, it hosts exhibitions, courses and conferences of art graphics; in Palazzo Chigi the Sala Velly offers exhibitions in rotation of the engravings of Jean-Pierre Velly.

  • Entry To be checked
