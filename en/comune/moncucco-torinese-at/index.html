What to see in Moncucco Torinese

Country of the Basso Monferrato on the border between Asti and Turin, dominated by a large castle of medieval origin that houses the Museum of plaster, material obtained here from the seventeenth century; complete the visit the Wine shop, linked to Freisa, and the neoclassical parish church of San Giovanni Battista.

Castello di Moncucco e Museo del gesso

Museums · Themed · Free entry · Viewpoint

The castle (XIV-XV century, enlarged in the 18th and 19th centuries) was already mentioned in 1164 in a diploma of Frederick I. It is surrounded by walls with two towers; it was born in the 13th century by the Templars Nicolao and Iacopo, and since 1855 it has been a municipal property. The museum tells about the pre-industrial processing of plaster, which was obtained here from the seventeenth century, and its use in the architecture of the Basso Monferrato from the sixteenth to the early twentieth century: crystals of selenite, tools, a model of furnace fed by sarmenti and dozens of panels from Piedmonti ceilings. From the castle the view ranges from Monferrato to Superga and the Alpine arch. Open on the first Sunday of the month (November-April 10-17, May-October 11-18), visit of about 45 minutes; on request you can also visit the plaster floors of the Town Hall and the Wine shop.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Bottega del Vino di Moncucco

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Born in the late 1970s, in a period of crisis in the wine market, and inaugurated in 1981 by local producers and municipalities to promote wines in the area, in particular Freisa; the building preserves original plaster floors that can be visited with the path of the Museum of plaster.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of medieval origin, renovated several times in the first decades of the nineteenth century, with a neoclassical appearance and imposing façade; the main altar is attributed to Filippo Juvarra.
