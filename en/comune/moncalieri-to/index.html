What to see in Moncalieri

On the hill above the Po, the Savoy castle where Vittorio Emanuele II signed in 1849 the Proclamation of Moncalieri.

4 places to visit in Moncalieri, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Reale di Moncalieri

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Medieval fortification expanded in the seventeenth century in royal residence, UNESCO heritage since 1997: here on 20 November 1849 Vittorio Emanuele II signed the Proclamation of Moncalieri. You can visit the Royal Apartments with the House of Queen Maria Adelaide, the Chinese Cabinet, the Proclamation Hall and the Royal Chapel, reopened in 2017 after the fire of 2008. Visit by reservation.

  • Where Piazza Baden Baden 4, Moncalieri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it · turismotorino.org

Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II e Porta Navina

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The heart of the old town at the foot of the castle, reached by the Porta Navina of 1560: houses and palaces porticoed, the Palazzo del Comune, the church of San Francesco rebuilt in 1788.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Moncalieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · turismotorino.org · italia.it

Collegiata di Santa Maria della Scala

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The Gothic church in the historic centre, overlooking Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, with a polychrome terracotta Pietà from the 15th century in the chapel on the left.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Moncalieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · turismotorino.org · italia.it

Faro della Vittoria

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lighthouse of the Park of the Remembrance on the hill of the Maddalena, marked as the highest point in the city.

  • Where Colle della Maddalena, Moncalieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
