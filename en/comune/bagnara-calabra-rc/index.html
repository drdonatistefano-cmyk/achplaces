What to see in Bagnara Calabra

Marinar village of Costa Viola overlooking the Strait of Messina, capital of the swordfish and home of the IGP nougat; rebuilt in chessboard after the earthquake of 1783, it preserves the Aragonese tower of Capo Rocchi, the church of Carmine with the Museo Angelo Versace and the Castle Ruffo on the promontory of Marturano.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The first settlement, born around the abbey of Santa Maria and the XII Apostles wanted by Ruggero II (consecrated in 1117), was perched on the promontory of Marturano. Satin to the ground by the earthquake of 1783, it was rebuilt lower on a project by Vincenzo Ferrarese for Ferdinand IV, with a chessboard system, squares, tree-lined avenues and fountains; the earthquake of 1908 destroyed almost all the churches except those of Carmel and Santa Maria di Portosalvo.

Chiesa del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

It rises in a square with a panoramic view of the old town; the church of Carmel is among the few escapes to the destruction of the earthquake of 1908.

Museo Angelo Versace

Museums · Old masters

Museo dell'Arciconfraternita di Maria Santissima del Monte Carmelo, in two sections: sacred art, with liturgical furnishings and vestments of Messinian and Neapolitan workshops of the 18th and 19th centuries, and archaeology, with finds from the Eneolithic to the Byzantine-Norman age.

Lungomare e spiagge

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The promenade overlooks the Strait of Messina; the beaches, very white, have low seabed and clear waters, and in the summer the marina is filled with colorful wooden fishing boats. In the village there is also the monument to the sisters Bertè, Loredana and Mia Martini, born in Bagnara.

  • Where Lungomare, Bagnara Calabra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · calabriastraordinaria.it

Torrone di Bagnara e pesce spada

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Bagnara is considered to be the capital of the swordfish, and its symbol is the "bagnarota" with a basket in the head from which the sword of the fish comes; the Torrone di Bagnara IGP is still handmade with traditional ingredients, and the area produces the Costa Viola IGT wine.
