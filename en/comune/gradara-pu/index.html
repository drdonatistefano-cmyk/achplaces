What to see in Gradara

Fortified village of Pesarese, Orange Flag and among the most beautiful villages in Italy, dominated by the Malatestian fortress linked to the history of Paolo and Francesca sung by Dante in the 5th Song of Hell; around, two circles of walls and paths of the Bosco di Paolo and Francesca.

Rocca di Gradara

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Born as a guard tower of the Griffos in the 12th century, it has the current plant of the Malatestian period (1293-1324): quadrilateral plant with angular towers and the mast, the strongest tower. Giovanni Sforza renovated it in 1494 for Lucrezia Borgia (temmas and inscriptions in the courtyard, frieze of the Passion and battle scene of Friend Aspertini and students); Umberto Zanvettori restored it in 1921-23 by furnishing the noble plan, and in 1928 passed to the State. Inside the room of Francesca, the dressing room of Lucrezia Borgia, a glazed terracotta altarpiece by Andrea della Robbia and the altarpiece of Giovanni Santi, father of Raffaello, with the oldest image of Gradara.

  • Where Piazza Alberta Porta Natale 1, Gradara
  • Entry €10, reduced €2 (EU citizens 18-25 years), free under 18 EU; cumulative with Doge's Palace of Urbino €16
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museinazionalimarche.cultura.gov.it · italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Mura e camminamenti di ronda

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The Malatesta walls form two circles: the first, close to the fortress, with Guelph and Ghibelline blackbirds; the second, wider, contains the historic center with fourteen towers and a fortified door with the Sforza, Della Rovere and Farnese coats of arms. The outer belt, with square crenellated towers, can still be walked along the round walkways.

  • Where Gradara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Borgo fortificato

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The walled village, dominated by the fortress, rises on a hill at about 142 m; it is theater at the end of July of the Assedio al Castello, re-enactment of the siege of 1446, and in winter of the Castle of Christmas.

  • Where Gradara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Bosco di Paolo e Francesca e Passeggiata degli amanti

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Viewpoint

Paths that surround the castle hill, with views of the Pesaro countryside between fields and Mediterranean maquis and up to the sea; the Passeggiata degli amanti turns around the castle.
