What to see in Terlizzi

The city of flowers, with the thirteenth-century oratory of the Rosary, the gallery of the neoclassical painter Michele De Napoli, the museum of peasant civilization and, in August, the gigantic triumphal chariot of the Madonna di Sovereto.

3 places to visit in Terlizzi, in the province of Bari (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pinacoteca civica «Michele De Napoli»

Museums · Modern art

Paintings, oil sketches, portraits, studies, cartoons and drawings donated to the Municipality by the neoclassicalist painter Michele De Napoli (1808-1892), author of the design of the triumphal chariot of the Madonna di Sovereto; complete the collection of architectural fragments of the Seven-nineteenth century and a carriage of the late eighteenth century restored.

  • Where Corso Dante Alighieri 9, Terlizzi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.terlizzi.ba.it

Sources: touringclub.it · comune.terlizzi.ba.it

Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Born from the donation of the collector Damiano Paparella, it has thousands of objects of agricultural and artisan life between the end of the nineteenth and mid twentieth centuries: a peasant house of the twenties with kitchen, room, wash and stable, the sections on terracotta and iron, the cycles of oil and wine and a miniature of the chariot of the Madonna di Sovereto. Stage of the Olive Oil-Terra d'Ulivi Road, with audio guides.

  • Where Viale Pacecco, Terlizzi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.terlizzi.ba.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · comune.terlizzi.ba.it
