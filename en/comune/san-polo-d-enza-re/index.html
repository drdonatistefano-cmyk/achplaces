What to see in San Polo d'Enza

Borgo where the Enza valley opens onto the plain, already inhabited in the Etruscan age (Servirola) and the land of the Canossa; the medieval nucleus brings together the Rocca, today the town hall with the Museum of Resistance \Salvo D'Acquisto, and the Church of the Castle with a fresco of Niccolò dell'Abate.

4 places to visit in San Polo d'Enza, in the province of Reggio Emilia (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca (Castello) di San Polo d'Enza

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Castello dei Canossa, where in 1092 the Emperor Henry IV stopped; then he passed to Visconti, Estensi and from 1603 to the Gherardini, who in the seventeenth century transformed him into a noble residence. Since 1796 the town has been home; the community bought it back in 1884 for 14,000 lire and since 1978 it has been restored. You enter from a massive tower with two ogival doors, one for the wagons and a pedestrian, and the wounds of the drawbridge. Today it is home to the Town Hall, accessible during the times of the municipal offices.

  • Where Piazza 4 Novembre 1, San Polo d'Enza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Chiesa del Castello (Santi Pietro e Paolo e Oratorio di San Giovanni Battista)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Inside the Fortress, parish since 1948: two rooms connected by a triple arch. The oratory, before 1201, preserves the Adoration of the Magi of Niccolò dell'Abate and a 16th-century fresco of San Giovanni Battista in a stuccoed ancon; the church of Saints Peter and Paul, with a single nave without facade, has two works by Francesco Viacavi (late 17th century) and a wooden pulpit of the eighteenth century.

Chiesa di Grassano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church mentioned in 1070 as the chapel of San Prospero of the goods of Bonifacio di Canossa, from the end of the fourteenth century dedicated to Santa Maria; it was probably rebuilt in 1697, date carved on the façade in sandstone blocks. An interior with a single nave with a barrel vault; next to the canonical with arched loggia on columns. In the hamlet of Grassano, on the hills towards Canossa, known for its stone houses.

  • Where Via Grassano Basso, San Polo d'Enza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
