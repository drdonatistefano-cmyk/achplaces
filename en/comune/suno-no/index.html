What to see in Suno

Country of the Colline Novaresi about 24 km north of Novara, among horse pastures and vineyards of the Colline Novaresi DOC. To see the ancient Pieve di San Genesio, documented since 1132, the center with the eighteenth-century parish and the nineteenth-century castle, the astronomical observatory Galileo Galilei and the Ring of the Horses.

Antica Pieve di San Genesio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Sunesi church of the Devil is mentioned as a parish head already in the 11th century and mentioned in a bull of Innocent II of 1132; the surviving masonry, in pebbles and bricks with spine strokes of fish and hanging arches on the south side, is attributed to the first half of the 11th century. In 1843 the facade and choir were demolished and the orientation changed. In the choir there is the tomb with the relics of San Genesio; above the tomb of the Della Porta, an ancient fresco with Madonna enthroned, San Genesio and the Archangel Michael, while the vault of the nave is frescoed in the eighteenth century. Roman finds were found in the area, today at the Broletto di Novara.

ATL describes it under precarious structural conditions

Centro storico, castello e Villa Biscaretti di Ruffia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

From the triangular square of 14th December, with the Town Hall and the 18th century parish church of the Holy Trinity, the cobbled Via Dottor Bigatti descends to a stone bridge; in Via Olmi there is an ancient fresco. The castle, dating back to the late nineteenth century, stands on a medieval fort surrounded by a large park and its tower is now part of a residence for the elderly; in the village also the baroque Villa Biscaretti of Ruffia, near the monument to the fallen.

  • Where Piazza XIV Dicembre, Suno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismonovara.it

Sources: turismonovara.it

Osservatorio astronomico Galileo Galilei

Museums · Science and university

Observatory with planetary, open on the first and third Wednesday evening of the month; organizes courses, workshops and public observation evenings, especially on the occasion of eclipses and comets.

  • Where Suno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.osservatoriogalilei.com

Sources: osservatoriogalilei.com · turismonovara.it

Suno e l'Anello dei Cavalli

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of 13 km (about 4 hours, difference in altitude 60 m, maximum 300 m) on asphalt and dirt, between horse farms (Cascina Costabella, Cascina Colombera), vineyards and birch woods, with Monte Rosa on the horizon and better colors in autumn. Touch the observatory, the erratic mass Preja da Scalavè (unmarked deviation), the hamlets Imperio and La Piana and the Pieve di San Genesio; almost absent signs, water only at the fountains of the village. Parking in via Europa, near the cemetery; it can be used all year round, suitable for children over 5 years old, not strollers.

  • Where Partenza: via Europa, Suno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismonovara.it

Sources: turismonovara.it
