What to see in Camerino

University city at 670 meters on the hill between the valleys of Chienti and Potenza, capital of Varano until 1539: the Doge's Palace with the Botanical Garden, the fort and the convent of Renacavata where the Capuchins were born. The center is still coming out of the 2016 earthquake.

Palazzo Ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Great example of civil architecture of the da Varano, now the seat of the University: there are the Room of the Sposi with frescoes of the fifteenth century and the Valentinian Library.

Pinacoteca e Museo civico – Complesso di San Domenico
