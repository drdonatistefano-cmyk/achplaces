What to see in Monticelli d'Ongina

Country of the Po golena, feud of the Pallavicino since 1413, with the fifteenth-century fortress that houses the chapel frescoed by Bonifacio Bembo and the Aquarium and Ethnographic Museum of the Po, the collegiate church of San Lorenzo and the oasis along the river.

6 places to visit in Monticelli d'Ongina, in the province of Piacenza (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca Pallavicino Casali e Cappella Bembo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Made by Rolando Pallavicino from 1420, a rectangular plan with moat, cylindrical towers, mast and round paths; it contains a chapel with a cycle of Renaissance frescoes commissioned by Carlo Pallavicino, bishop of Lodi, attributed to Bonifacio Bembo, perhaps with his brother Benedetto (mid-15th century).

  • Where Piazza Casali, Monticelli d'Ongina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Acquario e Museo etnografico del Po

Museums · Themed

Collection set up since 1974 in the cellars of the fortress: about twenty tanks with the fishes of the Po, dioramas with reptiles, mammals and birds, instruments of river crafts and a prehistoric pirogue of about 4,000 years ago, then ethnographic sections on the rural life of Padana and archaeological with paleontological finds of 15-20 thousand years ago. Open on Sunday afternoon, except January, July, August and December.

  • Where Piazza Casali 10, Monticelli d'Ongina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo civico

Museums · City history

Documents on the political and cultural history of the country, relics of the composer Amilcare Zanella and other illustrious mountains, paintings of local contemporary artists and a showcase dedicated to music; during the fairs it hosts temporary exhibitions. Open on Sunday 15.30-17.30.

  • Where Piazza Casali 10, Monticelli d'Ongina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Collegiata di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built between 1471 and 1480 on a design by Giovanni Battagio, with neo-Gothic façade by Edoardo Arborio Mella built in 1877; it houses canvases by Malosso (S. Lucia, 1585), the chapel of the Rosary with a table by Altobello Melone and frescoes rediscovered in 1975 attributed to Agostino Pesenti, the presbytery with frescoes by Robert De Longe on the life of San Lorenzo and a Crucifix of the Chiaveghino (1594).

  • Where Monticelli d'Ongina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Oasis near the Po, destination of bike rides and observation of protected water birds.

Torrente Chiavenna e oasi di Pinedo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

The Chiavenna stream in San Nazzaro is suitable for birdwatching, near the basin of the hydroelectric power plant; on the road to Carso the oasis of Pinedo is a naturalistic observatory.

  • Where San Nazzaro, Monticelli d'Ongina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
