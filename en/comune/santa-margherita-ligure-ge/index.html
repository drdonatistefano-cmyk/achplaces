What to see in Santa Margherita Ligure

Cittadina della Riviera di Levante in the eastern part of the Promontory of Portofino, in the heart of the Gulf of Tigullio, about 35 km from Genoa: a seaside village with marinas and fishing boats, the sixteenth-century castle on the promenade, Villa Durazzo with its seventeenth-century park, the abbey of Cervara overlooking the sea and the bay of Paraggi towards Portofino.

Villa Durazzo Centurione e parco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Born as a fortress on a hill overlooking the city, it was rebuilt in 1678 by the Durazzo family as a summer residence on the model of the Genoese villas of the seventeenth century; since 1973 it is of the Municipality. Inside there are statues, frescoes, majolica floors and Genoese granite, Murano chandeliers, antique furniture, a painting gallery of Genoese painters from the 17th and 18th centuries (Piola, Borzone, Paggi, De Ferrari) and the reconstruction of the study by writer Vittorio G. Rossi; in front of the entrance a square in white and black stones. The park, of about three hectares overlooking the Gulf, brings together Agrumeto, Giardino all'italiana, romantic English wood (voluted in 1919 by Alfredo Chierichetti) and Secret Garden of the Princes Centurione, and since 2006 is part of the Great Italian Gardens; the entrance to the park is free. Ticket €3 from 6 to 12 years, €4 for groups of 15 people and members FAI and Touring (MiC card updated to 2020). Tel. 0185 293135.

  • Where Piazzale San Giacomo 3, Santa Margherita Ligure
  • Entry €5.50, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.villadurazzo.it

Castello e Museo multimediale del Mare

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built in 1550 by the will of the Doge of Genoa to defend the coast from pirate raids, on a project attributed to Antonio de Càrabo, already author of the castle Rapallo: rectangular plan with rounded corners, tower in the south-west corner and main hall with a large central pillar and vaults. Stairs and trails connect it to Villa Durazzo. Restored in 2000, it hosts art exhibitions and multimedia halls of the Museo del Mare, with videos, speaking squares and interactive totems on the backdrop and the maritime history of the city.

Lungomare, porto e borgo marinaro

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The promenade runs around the water mirror where recreational boats and a fleet of fishing boats live together, keeping alive the marine tradition: the arrival of boats loaded with fish in the middle of the afternoon is a must-see sight, and the shrimps of Santa Margherita are a local specialty. Around the boutique, bar for the aperitif and beaches of the area Ghiaia, the most beautiful near the center.

Basilica di Santa Margherita d'Antiochia

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

In the historic centre, very wide, it has a Latin cross plan, three naves with domes and two side bell towers; the first stone was laid in the mid-sixteenth century on a place of worship of the thirteenth century. On the high altar, in a marble tabernacle, the wooden painted statue of the Madonna with the Child and a rose, brought according to the tradition by medieval sailors; during the works of 1672 under the altar there would be a vase of fragrant water, origin of devotion to the Madonna della Rosa, celebrated on Sunday before the Ascension with the blessing of roses.

  • Where Piazza Caprera, Santa Margherita Ligure
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Abbazia di San Girolamo della Cervara

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

The 14th century monastic complex on a promontory overlooking the sea along the road to Portofino, founded by the Carthusians of San Bartolomeo di Rivarolo; in the 15th century it was the seat of an important Benedictine congregation and was locked in the tower-prison Francesco I of Valois after the defeat of Pavia. National monument since 1912, restored in 1990, is now private and hosts events. The visit includes a seventeenth-century portal, a 16th-century defence tower, a lower cloister with a foundation epigraph, a chapel, a top garden with sixty pillars covered with vegetation and a bouganville pergola and a terrace overlooking the monumental Italian garden, with views from the Cinque Terre in Portofino.

Private property, only guided tours by reservation (visite@cervara.it) on the 1st and 3rd Sunday of the month from March to October, groups of at least 30 people all year round

Baia di Paraggi

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

The cove of clear waters on the state road to Portofino, with a small sandy beach equipped with lush vegetation, the castle of Paraggi (nineteenth-century adaptation of a Genoese fortress), restaurants and clubs. The seabeds, suitable for the growth of red coral, attract divers, which can also see a ship sunk in 1917; since the twelfth century the hamlet had numerous mills, some still existing in the locality Acqua Viva. The walking path from Santa Margherita to Portofino passes through the bay and Cervara.

Museo Scampoli Multimediali Camillo Sbarbaro

Museums · Themed

In the historic centre, a short walk from Villa Durazzo and the castle, a multimedia and interactive setting on the life and work of the writer Camillo Sbarbaro, born in Santa Margherita; is part of the museum complex of Villa Durazzo.
