What to see in Alezio

City of oil and wine at the gates of Gallipoli, Alezio houses in the eighteenth century Palazzo Tafuri one of the most important archaeological collections on the Messapi and, in the heart of the village, the Sanctuary of Santa Maria della Lizza, erected between the 12th and 13th centuries and once entirely frescoed.

Santuario di Santa Maria della Lizza

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Sanctuary of the patroness built between the XII and XIII century, at the end of a large square in the center of the village; the pronao was added for defensive purpose by Charles of Anjou. Once entirely frescoed, it preserves three pictorial phases (a Byzantine face of Our Lady, fragments of Basilian origin, profiles of monks and a Dormitio Virginis) and paintings by Gian Domenico Catalano. Under the floor, collapsed in the 1960s, a cemetery hypogeum with ten cells re-emerged. The patronal celebration of August 15 culminates in a great pyrotechnic race.

Museo civico Messapico (Palazzo Tafuri)

Museums · Archaeological

Its seat is in Palazzo Tafuri, also known as Villa Picciotti, an eighteenth-century residence with an almost octagonal shape, once the residence of a noble Gallipoli family. It preserves one of the most important archaeological collections on the Messapians, with funeral sets, balsamaries, trozzelle and divinities in terracotta; in front of the museum there is the archaeological park.

the Municipality warns that the Museum-Biblioteca is closed for works in progress
