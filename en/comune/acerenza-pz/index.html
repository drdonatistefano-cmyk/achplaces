What to see in Acerenza

Village on top of a hill with a wide panorama, dominated by the 11th century Romanesque Cathedral with the frescoed crypt and the diocesan Museum.

4 places to visit in Acerenza, in the province of Potenza (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Acerenza in the guide with map and filters

Cattedrale di Acerenza

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

Symbol of the village, of the 11th century, Romanesque with Gothic influences: the crypt is decorated with figures of Apostles, Doctors of the Church and founders of Orders, and the church houses sixteenth-century tables and frescoes.

  • Where Acerenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo diocesano d'arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

Paraments, sculptures, paintings, Neapolitan silvers of the eighteenth century, manuscripts and an archaeological section from the ninth century BC to the fourth AD; the main work is the marble bust of Giuliano l'Apostata, once on the facade of the cathedral.

  • Where Largo del Seminario, Acerenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Lago di Acerenza

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lake near the village with view and walk around; the fog of the lake often surrounds the village.

  • Where Acerenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
