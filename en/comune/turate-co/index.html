What to see in Turate

Comune della provincia di Como con la Casa militare Umberto I, structure of the late nineteenth century with a large park and a museum of Risorgimento and Garibaldini relics, the fourteenth-century sanctuary of Santa Maria in Campagna with frescoes on the Virgin and the parish church of Saints Peter and Paul frescoed by Giovanni Garavaglia.

Casa militare Umberto I

Museums · City history

A valuable structure of late nineteenth century, huge, with large lounges and a large park, the only green area of Turate according to the FAI; the activity began in 1898 hosting some garibaldini. Today it houses a museum with relics of the Risorgimento, including objects related to Giuseppe Garibaldi, and occasionally hosts events such as classical music concerts. Reported in the FAI census in 2003 , 2014, 2016, 2018, 2020 and 2022.

according to the FAI the structure is almost unused for about a decade and opens only occasionally for events

Santuario di Santa Maria in Campagna

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

A first nucleus is remembered as the church of San Maurizio da Goffredo da Bussero around 1270; in the middle of the fourteenth century the two churches were demolished to make room for the new church, saving the fresco of the Madonna today venerated on the altar of a side chapel, with baroque decorations and polychrome stuccoes of saints, angels and prophets. The presbytery has a cycle of frescoes on the childhood of the Virgin and a large cycle of sixteenth-century Stories of the Virgin. Cardinal Schuster raised him to the sanctuary in 1945; reported in the FAI census "I Luoghi del Cuore" in 2016, 2018, 2020 and 2022.

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish, after various extensions and renovations, was enlarged in the 19th century and consecrated by Cardinal Schuster on July 13, 1934; in 2004 Cardinal Tettamanzi consecrated the main altar. Between 1949 and 1952 Giovanni Garavaglia, of the Angelic Blessed School, painted the cycle of frescoes on the life of the Apostles Peter and Paul; among the paintings there is a seventeenth-century St. Charles in adoration of the Holy Nail.
