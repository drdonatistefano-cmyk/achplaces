What to see in Grugliasco

Common at the gates of Turin known for the Museo del Grande Torino and the legend Granata, housed in the seventeenth-century Villa Claretta Assandri and in its historical park; in the center the church of San Cassiano and the fifteenth-century Civic Tower, while the Museum of Agriculture of Piedmont preserves about 1,600 objects of the peasant world.

5 places to visit in Grugliasco, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo del Grande Torino e della Leggenda Granata

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Born in Superga and since 2008 in the Villa Claretta Assandri, he is curated by volunteers of the Association Historical Memory Granata and tells the story of the Turin Football Club about 650 square meters: finds of the Philadelphia Stadium demolished, the trumpet of the "Trombettiere" that gave the charge to the Grande Torino, wheel and propeller of the plane fallen in Superga in 1949, the Fiat Balilla by Gigi Meroni, shirts, photos and documents. Only guided tours of about an hour and a half, with mandatory booking; open Saturday 14-19 and Sunday 10-19. Free under 10 years and with Museums or Torino+Piemonte Card.

  • Where Via G. B. de la Salle 87, Grugliasco
  • Entry €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.grugliasco.to.it

Parco di Villa Claretta Assandri

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Historic garden with centuries-old trees around the villa, whose oldest part dates back to the last thirty years of the seventeenth century and which today houses the Museum of the Great Turin.

  • Where Via La Salle 87, Grugliasco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Museo dell'agricoltura del Piemonte

Museums · Themed

Supported by the homonymous association founded in 1977 by teachers of the Faculty of Agriculture of the University of Turin, it preserves about 1,600 objects, from simple tools to agricultural machines of historical interest, all inventoryed and catalogued. Times to check with the museum.

  • Where Via Leonardo da Vinci 44, Grugliasco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismotorino.org

Torre Civica

History and architecture · Towers

Late medieval bell tower of the 15th century, born with civic and defensive functions and linked to the first town hall, later became bell tower of the nearby parish church of San Cassiano; in the 18th century hosted a telegraph. Visitable only on occasion of events.

  • Where Piazza San Cassiano 2, Grugliasco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.grugliasco.to.it · turismotorino.org

Chiesa di San Cassiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church of the centre, whose origins date back to 1047, has been the subject of a Baroque reconstruction.

  • Where Via Francesco Cravero 18, Grugliasco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.grugliasco.to.it · turismotorino.org
