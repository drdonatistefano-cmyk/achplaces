What to see in Belvì

Barbagia village at 660 meters on Mount Genna de Crobu, between the limestone heels and Gennargentu: a museum of natural sciences with about a thousand Sardinian minerals, the nuragic complex of Cuccuru Nolza, the forest of Uatzo crossed by the Green Train.

6 places to visit in Belvì, in the province of Nuoro (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo di scienze naturali

Museums · Science and university

It exhibits about 1,000 Sardinian minerals, 300 fossils from Paleozoic to Quaternary, insects and lepidopterans, about 400 species of Sardinian birds, 70 mammals, 200 types of sea shells, amphibians, reptiles, fish and a large turtle Caretta caretta.

  • Where Belvì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Complesso di Cuccuru Nolza

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the plateau of Pranu: a nuraghe with mastium and quadrilobed bastion and the village that surrounds it.

  • Where Altopiano di su Pranu, Belvì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Tomba di Giganti di Troculu

History and architecture · Archaeology

Rebuilt along the state road Nuoro-Lanusei, with funerary compartment in granite blocks and exedra with a stairway wall.

  • Where Statale Nuoro-Lanusei, Belvì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Foresta di Uatzo e Trenino Verde

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Forest with picnic areas, starting point of marked trekking where eagles and hawks are observed. The Green Train crosses the northern stretch, stops at the old Belvì station and continues on a panoramic curve over the valleys.

  • Where Foresta di Uatzo, Belvì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Nature and landscape · City parks

  • Entry To be checked

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Strada narrow and winding, paved or cobbled, with examples of traditional rural architecture; at the beginning of June the cherry festival with wood carving exhibition and iron processing.

  • Where Centro storico, Belvì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
