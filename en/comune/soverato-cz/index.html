What to see in Soverato

The Ionian Pearl on the Gulf of Squillace, Blue Flag and Green Flag: three kilometers of promenade, the Regional Marine Park of the Bay of Soverato with the seahorses, the Botanical Garden Santicelli, the Tower of Carlo V, the ruins of Soverato Vecchia and, in Soverato Superiore, the Pietà by Antonello Gagini.

Chiesa di Maria Santissima Addolorata e Pietà di Antonello Gagini

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the right aisle of the archpriest church of Soverato Superiore is the marble Pietà by Antonello Gagini, commissioned in September 1520 and built in 1521 for the Augustinian convent of Santa Maria della Pietà, today in Petrizzi. The Virgin has the traits of an elderly mother with popular features, with Christ lying on her knees; the scannello depicts St. Michael, St. John the Baptist, St. Thomas Aquinas and Averroes. Damaged by the earthquake of 1783, the restoration of 1968 at the Opificio delle Pietre Dure in Florence gave it its white color.

  • Where Soverato Superiore, Soverato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Spiaggia di Soverato

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Kilometers of white sand and clear water on the Gulf of Squillace, awarded with Blue Flag and Green Flag; the beach is pet friendly and in the free stretches the rescue is carried out by Labrador of the SICS dogs units. Lidi equipped with services and sports activities.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Parco marino regionale Baia di Soverato

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Established in 2008, it protects submerged archaeological finds and protected species such as the needlefish, the shellfish Tonna galea and two species of seahorses (Hippocampus hippocampus and Hippocampus guttulus), from which the name of "Baia dell'Hippocampo" is named; the horse is an indicator of environmental quality.

  • Where Baia di Soverato, Soverato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Torre di Carlo V

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

A 16th century square-plan sighting tower, also known as Turrazzo or Galilee, built against the incursions of Turkish pirates; privatised in 1863, it was rebuilt in neo-Gothic style and renamed by Charles V. It rises near the Botanical Garden.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Soverato Vecchia

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Viewpoint

Ancient fortified village on a hill, mentioned as Suberatum in a text dated 1096, with walls and two towers, the main church, the church of Santa Caterina d'Alessandria and a hermitica laura; destroyed by the earthquake of 1783, remains the ruins of tower-houses, the Baronial Palace and the ancient parish.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.soverato.cz.it · italia.it

Zona archeologica di Poliporto

History and architecture · Archaeology

In San Nicola, north of the town, graves with incineration attributed to the Siculi (the caves that are dug in the rock) and the remains of a Greek-Roman settlement, perhaps a commercial landing place, with warehouses, millstone quarries and a large dolium; part of the structures is submerged and resurfaces with the winter marshes.

  • Where Località San Nicola, Soverato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.soverato.cz.it · fondoambiente.it · italia.it
