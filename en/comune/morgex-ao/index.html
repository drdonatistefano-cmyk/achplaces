What to see in Morgex

Country between villages, forests and the highest vineyards in Europe from the point of view of Mont Blanc, with one of the oldest towers in the region.

5 places to visit in Morgex, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Morgex in the guide with map and filters

Tour de l'Archet

History and architecture · Towers

Tower of the late 10th century in the center of the village, one of the oldest in the region, seat of the Natalino Sapegno Foundation and the Bibliomuseum of comics.

  • Where Morgex
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Medieval church of 1176 with Baroque altar, the Last Supper of 1559, frescoes of the Four-Fifteenth century and museum of sacred art.

  • Where Morgex
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it

Riserva naturale del Marais

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Wet area for summer walks from the valley floor, flanked by a research center with educational activities.

  • Where Morgex
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Lago d'Arpy

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

It can be reached with an easy walk from the Colle San Carlo; in winter cross-country trails and rackets.

  • Where Arpy, Morgex
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it

Parco della lettura

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

Green area with a large goose game and a tree house for word hunting, with accessible and inclusive installations.

  • Where Morgex
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it
