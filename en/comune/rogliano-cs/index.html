What to see in Rogliano

The centre of the Savovato valley with the Museum of Sacred Art "San Giuseppe" which collects works from the sixteenth to the eighteenth century of the local master carvers, and in the territory the section of the Middle Savovato with the waterfall of the Cannavina and the ancient bridges.

Museo di arte sacra «San Giuseppe»

Museums · Old masters

It is located in the former church of Santa Maria Maggiore in Santo Spirito, an eighteenth-century building wanted by the Congregation of the Noble Ones; it collects local heritage from the 16th to the 18th century, largely from the former convent of the Capuchins: paintings, silver sacred vestments, liturgical vestments and carved wooden works of the local masters, active especially in Rogliano and other areas of Calabria. It has a room for educational activities.

Medio Savuto: cascata della Cannavina e antichi ponti

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The middle course of Savovato, between the territories of Rogliano, Altilia, Parenti, Scigliano, Belsito, Carpanzano and Marzi, known for the waterfall of Cannavina, about 15 meters high. Within 20-30 km there are four historical bridges: of the Roman Ischia (16th century, in Balzata), of Tavolaria (16th century, about 200 m downstream of the confluence of the Cannavina), of the Fratte (late medieval reconstruction of a perhaps Roman structure) and of Sant'Angelo, called The place is reported several times in the FAI census "The Places of the Heart."
