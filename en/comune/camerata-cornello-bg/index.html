What to see in Camerata Cornello

In Val Brembana, the medieval village of Cornello dei Tasso, on a spur above the Brembo and reachable only on foot, was the first market after Bergamo on the Via Mercatorum and the cradle of the Tasso family, which organized the European postal service: today it preserves the portico street, the Romanesque church of Saints Cornelius and Cipriano and the Museum of Tasso and postal history.

Borgo di Cornello dei Tasso

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Among the best preserved medieval villages of Bergamasca, on a rocky spur above the Brembo, you can only reach it on foot for ancient mule tracks. Built on several levels (market at the bottom, houses at the center, church at the top), it has a high street porticoed with stone arches, wooden beams and cobbled; the imperial eagle granted to the Tassos is still visible on a wall. The valley road of 1592 isolated it, preserving its appearance.

Museo dei Tasso e della storia postale

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Civic museum founded in 1991 in three buildings of the village with six rooms: the territory and the Tasso, the postal activity of the family, Bernardo and Torquato Tasso, the journey, the signs on letters, epidemics and disinfection, until the transmission of information from the nineteenth century, mail art and philatelia. It has a letter from 1840 freed with Penny Black, the world's first stamp, and a poster horn. Guided tour €3 per person; open from Thursday to Sunday (from April from Wednesday), 10-12.30 and 14.30-18 from March to October, 10-13 and 14-17 from November to February.

Chiesa dei Santi Cornelio e Cipriano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On the highest level of the village, of the 12th century and then renovated, with Romanesque bell tower with bifore, rare in Val Brembana; the restorations have brought to light a cycle of frescoes perhaps even of painters of Averara, the Baschenis. The frame of the altarpiece of 1635 with the Crucifixion bears the coat of arms of the Tasso, with the horn of mail and badger.

Via Mercatorum

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

An ancient commercial route that climbed from Bergamo up the Val Brembana towards the Valtellina and the Grisons, making Cornello the most important market in the valley until the construction of the Via Priula at the end of the sixteenth century valley; the mule tracks are still the only access to the village.
