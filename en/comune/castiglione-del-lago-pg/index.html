What to see in Castiglione del Lago

On the promontory overlooking Trasimeno, the village of Della Corgna: the Doge's Palace connected by a covered walk to the Fortress of Leone, between medieval walls with three doors.

Palazzo della Corgna

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The Doge's Palace wanted by Ascanio della Corgna in 1560, designed by Vignola or Galeazzo Alessi, the most important building in the village.

  • Where Piazza Gramsci, Castiglione del Lago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Rocca del Leone

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The medieval fortress that gives its name to the village, reachable from the palace with a covered walkway; one of the most interesting examples of medieval Umbrian military architecture.

  • Where Castiglione del Lago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Chiesa di San Domenico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Erected in 1636, with a beautiful wooden coffered ceiling and a walkway destined to the tomb of the Della Corgna.

  • Where Castiglione del Lago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Palazzo del Capitano del Popolo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

In Piazza Mazzini, the 13th-century palace in the historic center enclosed by the walls with the doors Fiorentina, Senese and Perugina.

  • Where Piazza Mazzini, Castiglione del Lago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it
