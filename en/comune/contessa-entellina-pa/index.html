What to see in Contessa Entellina

Arbëreshë village of Alto Belice, founded by Albanians who arrived in Sicily in the 15th century, with the Mother Church of the Greek-Byzantine Rite; in the territory the Rocca d'Entella, an ancient town with the cave in the casts protected as a reserve, the Antiquarium of its finds and the abbey of Santa Maria del Bosco.

Rocca d'Entella, area archeologica

History and architecture · Archaeology

Relief of 557 meters on the Belice Sinistro, inaccessible on three sides for three large rocky bastions, inhabited by the end of the Eneolithic in 1246: it was one of the three helimes cities with Erice and Segesta, occupied in 404 BC by the mercenaries of Campania and in the thirteenth century the last stronghold of the Islamic resistance against Frederick II. The excavations of the Scuola Normale di Pisa with the Superintendence have brought to light Hellenistic fortifications with towers and bastions, a public barn of the fourth century BC, the necropolis and, on Pizzo della Regina, a medieval fortification with cistern, as well as a castle and a fortified palace with hammam.

Antiquarium comunale di Contessa Entellina

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 1995, it collects the finds of Entella: the casts of the famous decrees of Entella, bronze tablets inscribed; ceramics from prehistory to the fourth century B.C., including an amphora of the VII century B.C. with plastic and geometric decoration; the materials of the granary and necropolis with the reconstruction in real scale of a Hellenistic burial; the medieval phase, the daily life and the finds of the sanctuary of Demeter and Kore of contrada Petraro. In the final hall of the barn, amphorae, epigraphs and coins.

Riserva naturale integrale Grotta di Entella

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Reserve of 19.8 hectares established in 1995 and managed by CAI Sicilia, which protects the cave and part of the Rocca di Entella, isolated chalky outcrop: the cavity, excavated in the macro-crystalline plasters of the Messinian, develops on three levels of tunnels for over 700 meters, with chalky and carbonate cavings and rare iron-manganesive deposits; on the surface dolines and karrens, rock vegetation and steppe, bats and raptors nesting on the walls.

Abbazia di Santa Maria del Bosco

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Complex in the current form of a vanvitellian school, with a church, now destroyed, and two cloisters: cloisters and refectory are available and host musical and cultural meetings and events. There were scenes of New Paradise Cinema.

Chiesa Madre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the Arbëreshë community where the Greek-Byzantine rite is celebrated, especially on 5 and 6 January and on Easter holidays; it preserves an iconostasis and frescoes of the vault in false mosaic.
