What to see in Santa Flavia

Municipality of the coast to the east of Palermo, with the Hellenistic-Roman city of Solunto on Mount Catalfano and its necropolis, Villa Filangeri seat of the Town Hall, the harbours of Santa Flavia and Porticello and the ancient port of Solanto with the castle.

Area archeologica e Antiquarium di Solunto

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Hellenistic-Roman city rebuilt on Mount Catalfano after Dionisio of Syracuse, at the beginning of the fourth century BC, destroyed the oldest center on the coast; a dedication to Fulvia Plautilla, wife of Caracalla (202-205 A.D.), marks the traditional end of the city. You walk along a regular road system with blocks, peristyle houses with cisterns, shops on the main street, the agora portico, the spas and, higher up, theater, bouleuterion, the so-called Ginnasio, the House of Arpocrate and a sacred area with terraces. The Antiquarium exhibits amphorae, remains of statues, marine finds, architectural elements and parietal paintings.

  • Where Via Collegio Romano, Santa Flavia
  • Entry €6, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website turismo.cittametropolitana.pa.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Necropoli di Solunto

History and architecture · Archaeology

On the eastern side of Mount Catalfano, in the locality Campofranco-Olivetano, a vast area of burials excavated in limestone, from the beginning of the third century BC at least to the end of the first century A.D.: loculi for a single burial and tombs to one or two hypogeic rooms with dromos and steps. She returned to visit, for the first time after 15 years, with a project of the East Metropolis LAG under the scientific direction of the Archaeological Park.

Villa Filangeri e Basilica Soluntina

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa built around 1600 by the family Fillangeri, now home of the Town Hall: it preserves liberty frescoes, has a staircase to two ramps and a large park with many species of Mediterranean flora. Next is the Basilica Soluntina, an example of the late Sicilian Baroque.
