What to see in Cerignola

Città del Tavoliere linked to wheat: the Piano delle Fosse preserves more than 600 grains excavated in the ground, next to the Museum of Wheat in the former Dominican convent; in the territory there are Torre Alemanna, the only fortified settlement of the Teutonic Knights remained in the Mediterranean, and the sanctuary of the Madonna of Ripalta on the Ofanto.

Piano delle Fosse (Piano San Rocco)

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Wide area of about 26,000 m2 south of the center, with bell-shaped grain pits dug into the ground, covered with stone or brick and closed with boards and earth; each pit is marked by a stone chip about 90 cm high with the initials of the owner and a number. Documented since 1225, in 1902 there were about 1,100: today 625 remain, about 200 still in use to store cereals, with average capacity of 500 quintals; the area is bound since 1982.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.cerignola.fg.it

Museo del grano

Museums · Themed

It is located on the Piano delle Fosse in the complex of the former Opera Pia Monte Fornari: the ground floor was a Dominican monastery of the sixteenth century, to which the first floor was added in the nineteenth century; after being barracks and orphanage, the building with internal cloister was recovered by the municipality with European funds through the LAG Piana del Tavoliere to house the civic museum of wheat.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Museo etnografico cerignolano Stuppiello

Museums · Themed

Ethno-anthropological Museum designed and built by Professor Matteo Stuppiello in 1979; after several locations he found a place in a nineteenth-century building, where he exhibits in more rooms about 2,000 objects catalogued in chronological order.

  • Where Viale Giuseppe Di Vittorio 70, Cerignola

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Torre Alemanna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The only fortified settlement of the Teutonic Order still existing in the Mediterranean area, born from the command created between 1226 and 1231 on lands donated by Frederick II: the 24-metre tower, of the 13th-XIV century, incorporates the choir of a pre-existing church with remains of a cycle of frescoes of the late thirteenth century (saints, Passion, Lignum vitae) and graffiti in German. The complex includes the Palazzo dell'Abate of 1570, the eighteenth-century loggia and the sixteenth-century church; since 2016 there has been a museum with mainly Renaissance ceramics and archaeological finds.

Santuario della Madonna di Ripalta

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

It rises in the open countryside about 9 km from Cerignola, on a spur overlooking the river Ofanto from which it derives its name (the high ripa) . The church of the patron saint holds for six months a year the Greek-Byzantine icon of Our Lady, probably of Basilian origin, found in 1172; in the area was found a stone with inscription to the goddess Bona. Diocesan shrine since 1992, has been several times among the Places of the FAI Heart.

  • Where SP91, Cerignola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
