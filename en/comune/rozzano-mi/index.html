What to see in Rozzano

City of the Metropolitan City of Milan with the castle Cassino Scanasio, already Trivulzio and Visconti of Modrone, the Museum of pots in history, the astronomical observatory at Cascina Grande and the protected naturalistic oasis of the Smeraldino and Sorgiva.

5 places to visit in Rozzano, in the province of Milan (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Cassino Scanasio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Beni in the place called "Casinae Scanasane" are mentioned already around the year 1000; restored in the fifteenth century, when the village became a fortified farmhouse, at the beginning of the sixteenth century passed to the Trivulzio, which transformed it from a fortress into a country house, of which the cylindrical towers remain. In 1836 he passed to the Visconti di Modrone. It preserves graffiti decorations and Gothic arched windows; a part has been renovated under the protection of the Fine Arts.

currently undergoing restoration (FAI), no information on openings

  • Where Cassino Scanasio, Rozzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rozzano.mi.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · comune.rozzano.mi.it

Museo delle pentole nella storia

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1985 by AMC Italia in a wing built specifically in 1984 at the company's headquarters, it collects cooking instruments from all over Italy from the 3rd millennium BC to today: Roman and Apulian finds, terracotta pots and metal pots, the first pressure cooker (1930), metal-coated pots typical of the coasts and kitchenettes working in the 19th century, as well as a workshop of the rebuilt and working calderaio.

can be visited only by appointment for groups on weekdays (FAI)

  • Where Via Eugenio Curiel 242, Rozzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rozzano.mi.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · comune.rozzano.mi.it

Osservatorio astronomico civico alla Cascina Grande

Museums · Science and university

Inaugurated on 27 May 2023 in the outdoor area of the cultural center Cascina Grande, realized with the Astrofili Group Rozzano, active in the city for over 40 years. The dome, almost 7 meters in diameter with petal opening, offers a 360 degree view; according to the Municipality it is the largest of its kind in Europe and the second in the world.

visits only by reservation (tel. 02 8925931)

  • Where Viale Palmiro Togliatti 105, Rozzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rozzano.mi.it

Sources: comune.rozzano.mi.it

Oasi Smeraldino e Casa della Natura

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

First and largest protected natural area of Rozzano, curated by the volunteers of APE Natura, where there were once the rice fields of the Count of Belgioioso and, in the fifties, clay quarries for a brick furnace. Inside the House of Nature, born as a refuge for swallows, is a small open-air museum with the original mill mill of Cascina Follazza, nests, eggs, animal skeletons and panels on the local flora and fauna.

visits only by reservation (tel. 338 8713534), driven every Sunday

  • Where Via Monte Amiata 38 bis, Valleambrosia, Rozzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rozzano.mi.it

Sources: comune.rozzano.mi.it

Oasi La Sorgiva

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry

Protected natural area of six hectares, a small naturalistic gem that protects biodiversity; from the natural observatory you can photograph small birds that frequent mangers and water troughs. The guided tours last about two hours (from 15 to 17); recommended boots, binoculars and camera.

visits only by reservation (tel. 338 8713534), last Sunday of the month

  • Where Rotatoria tra via Eduardo De Filippo e via Massimo Troisi, Rozzano
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rozzano.mi.it

Sources: comune.rozzano.mi.it
