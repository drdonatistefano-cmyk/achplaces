What to see in Casalmaggiore

Cittadina sul Po around the seventeenth-century Piazza Garibaldi with its "listone" , with the neoclassical Cathedral of Santo Stefano, the Museo del Bijou that tells the golden century of the house jewelry, the Diotti Museum in the home of the painter and, outside the city, the Sanctuary of the Fountain where the Parmigianino rests.

8 places to visit in Casalmaggiore, in the province of Cremona (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza Garibaldi e Torrione

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Made in the 17th century where there was a pond, measuring 96 by 38 meters and is divided by the Neo-Gothic Town Hall (1788) by Piazza Turati; in the center the marble "listone" is the meeting point of the housewives, the Saturday market and in November the San Carlo Fair. Not far away, near the embankment in Piazza San Martino, the fifteenth-century Torrione, the only rest of the fortifications.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Casalmaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Sources: turismocremona.it · comune.casalmaggiore.cr.it

Duomo di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Great neoclassical church with Greek cross designed by Fermo Zuccari and begun in 1840 with the gift of Giovanni Vicenza Ponzone, with dome about 60 meters high on 16 arches and bell tower of 1897-1899. Inside the monuments of Pietro Civeri, the Martyrdom of Santo Stefano by Pietro Verzetti in the apse, painted from the Five to the nineteenth century (also by Giuseppe Diotti) and a copy of the Pala di Casalmaggiore del Parmigianino, whose original is in Dresden.

  • Where Piazza Marini 1, Casalmaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Sources: turismocremona.it · comune.casalmaggiore.cr.it

Complesso di Santa Chiara

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

In front of the Duomo, founded in 1504 and enlarged in the eighteenth century: the octagonal church with double loggia was consecrated in 1531 and the monastic church annexed preserves late medieval and Renaissance frescoes; today it houses the Santa Chiara Postgraduate Institute.

  • Where Presso il Duomo, Casalmaggiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Sources: comune.casalmaggiore.cr.it · turismocremona.it

Santuario della Beata Vergine della Fontana

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Built in the second half of the fifteenth century on the site of a source considered miraculous, Gothic with Renaissance portal in terracotta: in the crypt a seventeenth-century tub still guards the source under the image of the Madonna del latte; frescoes from the fifteenth to the seventeenth century, including a cycle of Giovanna d'Arco of the Pesenti brothers of Sabbioneta, and in the second left chapel the funerary stele of Parmigianino, died in Casalmaggiore in 1540. It is reached on foot in about half an hour along a tree-lined avenue.

  • Where Viale del Santuario 2, Casalmaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Sources: comune.casalmaggiore.cr.it · turismocremona.it

Museo del Bijou

Museums · Themed

Established in 1986 in the basement of the eighteenth century College of Santa Croce, unicum in Italy: over 20,000 objects produced by Casalmaggiore factories between the late nineteenth and seventies, with tools, machines, photos and catalogs of the industrial gold century of the city, plus a didactic workshop of fusion and gilding. Open Tuesday-Saturday 10-12 and 15-18, Sunday and holidays 15-19; free on the first Sunday of the month.

  • Where Via Azzo Porzio 9, Casalmaggiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · turismocremona.it

Museo Diotti

Museums · Modern art

Opened in 2007 in the house-study of the neoclassical painter Giuseppe Diotti (1779-1846), director and professor of the Carrara Academy of Bergamo, is the main headquarters of the Civic Collections of Art, with works from the late eighteenth century to today of the main local artists and temporary exhibitions.

  • Where Via Aldo Formis 17, Casalmaggiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Sources: comune.casalmaggiore.cr.it · turismocremona.it

Teatro Comunale

History and architecture · Historic theatres

From the Austrian period, after Casalmaggiore had the title of city in 1754, designed by architect Mones with the approval of Giuseppe Piermarini: it is a small version of the Scala of Milan.

  • Where Via Cairoli 57, Casalmaggiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Sources: comune.casalmaggiore.cr.it · turismocremona.it

Argine e golena del Po

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the sanctuary of the Fountain you can enjoy the view of the city and the Po's bend; along the bank you walk to the church of Santa Maria dell'Argine, and the Park of the Po and the Golena is indicated for cycling.

  • Where Argine del Po, Casalmaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Sources: turismocremona.it
