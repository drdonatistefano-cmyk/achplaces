What to see in Aulla

Historical stop of the Via Francigena at the confluence of Magra and Aulella, with the abbey of San Caprasio founded in 884 and its museum on the pilgrimage, and the Fortress of Brunella that houses the Museum of Natural History of Lunigiana; in the territory the villages of Bibola and Caprigliola and the mechanical nativity scene of Pallerone.

Abbazia di San Caprasio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

The oldest monument of Aulla, founded in 884 by Adalberto I marquis of Tuscany and dedicated first to the Virgin, then from 1077 to St. Caprasio, Provencal hermit. Benedictine Abbey linked to the Malaspinas, was the main stop of the Via Francigena: Sigerico, archbishop of Canterbury, between 990 and 994. The excavations concluded in 2003 revealed the monumental tomb of the 10th century saint with a rare stucco reliquary (bones belong to a man of the 5th century) and remains of apses of the 8th-IXth century; the relics are in the high altar.

  • Where Piazza Abbazia 13, Aulla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · viafrancigena.visittuscany.com

Museo di San Caprasio

Museums · Themed

In the abbey complex, dedicated to the history of the Via Francigena in Lunigiana: it houses a medieval pilgrim mannequin and a 14th-century column in local stone on the remains of a bell furnace of the 12th century. The route touches church, capitular hall, room of the sculptures of Oberto Ferlendi and cloister-garden, with coins, ceramics, capitals and a portal from the excavations of 2003 and the "Gospel of stone" , capitals with winged dragons of Ferlendi.

  • Where Piazza Abbazia, Aulla
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Fortezza della Brunella

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On a height at the confluence of Magra and Aulella, it is the most powerful military work of the Lunigiana: Renaissance fortress with square plan attributed to Jacopo Ambrogio Malaspina (end of the fifteenth century) or to Giovanni dalle Bande Nere (1522), perhaps according to design by Antonio da Sangallo the Elder, already efficient in 1553. It was taken by the Spaniards on 24 December 1733 after three weeks of siege, it was the residence of the British Lina Duff Gordon and Aubrey Waterfield since 1920 and since 1977 it has been of the State, entrusted to the Municipality. Guided tours every 45 minutes without reservation; free for under 6 and residents.

  • Where Viale Rimembranza 4, Aulla
  • Entry €6, reduced €3 (guided tour)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.aulla.ms.it · visittuscany.com

Museo di storia naturale della Lunigiana

Museums · Science and university · Viewpoint

Established in 1981 in the Fortress of Brunella and reopened in 1997: botanical section with Ferrarini herbarium and rare Apuan plants, zoological section with the fauna of Lunigiana and entomological collections, mineralogical section with fragments of a fang of mastodon from Pontremoli, and a roof garden panoramic on Lunigiana, Apennines and Apuans. Open from April to October, on weekends and holidays; every day in July and August (9,30-12,30 and 16-19).

  • Where Fortezza della Brunella, Viale Rimembranza 4, Aulla
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Area naturale protetta del fiume Magra e percorso sull'ex ferrovia

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Two protected natural areas of local interest along the Magra, with willow and alder forests and numerous bird species. In the city, the cycle path on the former railway, open in June 2025, is 665 meters long between Via Piave and Via della Resistance, with views of the river. From Aulla start the stages of the Via Francigena towards Pontremoli and Sarzana.

  • Where Via Piave - Via della Resistenza, Aulla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Bibola

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hillside village with origins dating back to the 7th century, with the ruins of the tower of the Malaspina castle, the church of San Bartolomeo with mosaic church of Ligurian type and stone houses with covered passages.

  • Where Bibola, Aulla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
