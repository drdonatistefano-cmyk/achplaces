What to see in Resia

The valley of the language and the Resian culture, largely inside the Julian Pre-Alps Natural Park and dominated by Mount Canin: the Museum of Arrotino in Stolvizza, the sanctuary of Santa Maria Assunta in Prato and the widespread ecomuseum.

6 places to visit in Resia, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museums · Themed

In Stolvizza, tools and photographs of the ancient craft of the Knife.

Museo della gente della Val Resia

Museums · Themed

In a typical ancient house of Stolvizza, with a section dedicated to the local tradition of fairy tales.

  • Where Stolvizza, Resia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Ecomuseo Val Resia

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Viewpoint

Museum spread all over the valley, made above all of paths to walk to know its characteristic places and natural and human history, with the participation of the community. Visits every day only by reservation.

  • Where Via Roma, Resia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Santuario di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

The parish church of Prato, cited in documents since 1098, consecrated in the present form in 1718 and sanctuary since 2019.

  • Where Prato di Resia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint
