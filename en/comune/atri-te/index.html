What to see in Atri

Art city on the three hills between the Adriatic and the Gran Sasso, dominion of Acquaviva: the cathedral with the great cycle of frescoes by Andrea de Lithium, the ducal palace and, a few minutes away, the Calanchi reserve.

7 places to visit in Atri, in the province of Teramo (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Atri in the guide with map and filters

Basilica concattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built between the 13th and 14th centuries on a church of the 9th century, with large portal, rose window and bell tower; in the choir the largest cycle of frescoes in the region, masterpiece of Andrea de Lithium, and inside Renaissance chapels and a sixteenth century baptistery.

  • Where Piazza Duomo 2, Atri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismo.abruzzo.it

Chiesa di Santa Reparata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Attached to the cathedral, begun in 1355 with a Greek cross and transformed into a Baroque style, with the large wooden canopy by Carlo Riccione from the late 17th century.

  • Where Corso Elio Adriano 1, Atri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it · italia.it

Palazzo dei Duchi d'Acquaviva

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Erected in 1395 on the remains of Roman buildings, for almost four centuries residence of the Acquaviva d'Aragona, today seat of the Municipality; it preserves a nativity scene of the sixteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Duchi d'Acquaviva, Atri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it · italia.it

Porta San Domenico

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The only surviving of the seven gates of the fourteenth-century brick walls of the mid-500s, on the model of the sharp arched doors.

  • Where Atri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it · italia.it

Rione San Giovanni e quartieri storici

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The centre of medieval plant with the districts Capo d'Atri, Santa Maria and San Giovanni, where some alleys are so narrow that you pass one at a time.

  • Where Atri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismo.abruzzo.it

Area archeologica e resti del teatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Remains of the Roman city, including the ancient theatre.

  • Where Atri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Riserva Naturale Regionale Calanchi di Atri

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Reserve of about 380 hectares established in 1995 and managed by the WWF: calanchi with roverella, licorice and capers, raptors and barbarians, paths to walk alone or with a guide.

  • Where Via Colle della Giustizia, Atri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it · fondoambiente.it · italia.it
