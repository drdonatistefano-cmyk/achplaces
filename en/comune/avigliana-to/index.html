What to see in Avigliana

Borgo Bandiera Arancione on the edge of a morainic basin with two lakes, where the medieval village rises towards the ruins of the castle and the porticoed square of the Count Rosso.

7 places to visit in Avigliana, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 2 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Piazza Conte Rosso

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The square with its porticoed buildings with a well of the fourteenth century, dominated by the ruins of the castle.

  • Where Piazza Conte Rosso, Avigliana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Rovine del castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The remains of the Arduini castle that dominates from above the square and the village.

  • Where Avigliana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Torre dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Octagonal construction of the XIII-XIV century; it is said to be the first public clock of Piedmont.

  • Where Via dell'Orologio, Avigliana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Important Romanesque-Gothic church on top of a staircase of the village of San Pietro, founded perhaps by the Benedictines between the X and XI century.

  • Where Borgo San Pietro, Avigliana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Erected in 1324, it houses numerous sacred works of art among frescoes and paintings.

  • Where Piazzetta San Giovanni, Avigliana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: vallesusa-tesori.it · italia.it

Parco naturale dei Laghi di Avigliana

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Protected area of the Cozie Alps with the two morainic lakes at the foot of the city.

  • Where Avigliana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · touringclub.it · italia.it

Ecomuseo del Dinamitificio Nobel

Museums · Themed
