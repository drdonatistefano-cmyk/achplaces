What to see in Monterosso al Mare

The most western village of the Cinque Terre, divided by the hill of San Cristoforo between the ancient center at the mouth of Buranco and Fegina with the statue of the Giant; places of Montale and the sanctuary of Soviore.

6 places to visit in Monterosso al Mare, in the province of La Spezia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Monterosso al Mare in the guide with map and filters

Borgo di Monterosso e piazza Garibaldi

History and architecture · Villages · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The ancient center at the mouth of the Buranco stream, with parallel streets Rome and Vittorio Emanuele and the tree-lined Piazza Garibaldi of the Two-Three hundred; one of the five villages of the UNESCO site.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Monterosso al Mare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · whc.unesco.org

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In Piazza Garibaldi, facade with black bands and large sculpted rosette; the bell tower was a sighting tower, in the bezel an eighteenth-century fresco of the Baptism of Jesus and inside, renovated in the Baroque age, the baptismal font.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Monterosso al Mare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Convento dei Cappuccini e chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill of San Cristoforo; in the chapels a Crucifixion attributed by some to Van Dyck but probably of a local painter and an eighteenth-century altarpiece of the Immaculate of Giuseppe Palmieri.

  • Where Colle di San Cristoforo, Monterosso al Mare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Santuario della Madonna di Soviore

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 418 meters between holm oaks and cypresses, reachable by path 509 from Via Roma: church of the end of the thirteenth century with a Pietà of the fourteenth century, the oldest sanctuary of Liguria according to italia.it.

  • Where Soviore, Monterosso al Mare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Fegina e statua del Gigante

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

The new part of the village, where Eugenio Montale stayed with the family; on the beach, sand now fine now in pebbles, the statue of the Giant (Neptune), symbol of the country.

  • Where Fegina, Monterosso al Mare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Punta Mesco e luoghi di Montale

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The places linked to Eugenio Montale, from Punta Mesco to the "Casa del Doganiere" (Casa del Doganiere) with the Cinque Terre Card free excursions from April to August.

  • Where Punta Mesco, Monterosso al Mare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
