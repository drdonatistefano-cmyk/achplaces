What to see in Collecchio

At the gates of Parma between the Taro and the woods of Carrega: the Romanesque parish church of San Prospero on the pilgrim's street, the Court of Giarola with the Museums of Pomodoro and Pasta, the museum of the daily newspaper of Ettore Guatelli in Ozzano Taro and two regional parks.

Museo Ettore Guatelli

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Born in the granary of the farm Bella Foglia and then extended to the farmhouse, it is the work of the elementary master and collector Ettore Guatelli (1921-2000): about 60,000 objects among agricultural tools and craftsmen, furniture, toys, musical instruments, clothes and photographs, arranged on walls and ceilings in geometric compositions, in rooms dedicated to glass, ceramics, tin, shoes, suitcases, watches. Sections on emigration from Parmense to England and on the wanderings of the show. Open Tuesday-Friday 10-13, Saturday and holidays even 15-18, with guided tours.

Closed from 14 December 2026 to all January 2027, reopened scheduled to February 2027 with date to be defined

  • Where Via Nazionale 130, Ozzano Taro, Collecchio
  • Entry €8, reduced €6
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website museoguatelli.it

Sources: museoguatelli.it · cultura.gov.it · parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo del Pomodoro

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the Corte di Giarola, an ancient medieval agricultural complex near the Taro and the Via Francigena, tells the tomato from the Americas to the red gold of Parmense: the arrival in Italy around 1600, the birth of the preserve, the Experimental Station of the Conserves of 1922, the reconstruction of an ancient factory with vintage machinery and the seven stages of processing, up to advertising, sculptures, paintings and recipes. Open from 1 March to 8 December Saturday, Sunday and holidays 10-18; from December to February only groups on request.

  • Where Strada Giarola 11, Collecchio
  • Entry €5, reduced €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website pomodoro.museidelcibo.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: pomodoro.museidelcibo.it · italia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · museidelcibo.it

Pieve di San Prospero

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Founded in the 11th century and renovated around 1200, it was a stop for the pilgrims of Romea Street, then known as Francigena: it preserves the original apse with stone, terracotta and majolica decorations, capitals with zoomorphic figures of the 11th-XIII century, a baptismal font of the 12th century and a white marble bas-relief with the Baptism of Christ of a 12th-century master champion.

  • Where Collecchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: pasta.museidelcibo.it · parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Corte di Giarola e Parco fluviale del Taro

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Court of Giarola is home to the Regional River Park of the Taro, a belt of about 2,600 hectares along twenty kilometers of river, accessible on foot or by mountain bike, where more than 200 species of birds have been observed, with willows, poplars and the rare Coriaria myrtifolia. Free entrance from sunrise to sunset; the naturalistic area Le Chiesuole is open Saturday and Sunday 9-12 and 15-18 (€1).

  • Where Strada Giarola 11, Collecchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: pasta.museidelcibo.it · pomodoro.museidelcibo.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Parco regionale dei Boschi di Carrega

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

First regional park, established in 1982 on 1,270 hectares between 110 and 470 meters, already hunting reserve of the Farnese and the Bourbon: oaks, chestnuts, pine trees, firs and beech trees, streams, lakes and meadows, accessible only on foot or by mountain bike. The seat is in the neoclassical Casino dei Boschi, designed in 1789; on the side of Collecchio you enter from the parking lot of the Serraglio in via del Conventino.

  • Where Via del Conventino, Collecchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: pasta.museidelcibo.it · pomodoro.museidelcibo.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Parco Nevicati e Villa Soragna

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

On the hill above the village, the public park Fortunato Nevicati, with centuries-old cedars and tall shade trees, surrounds Villa Soragna, neoclassical building of the early nineteenth century near the old church, today multipurpose cultural center.

  • Where Via le Valli 2, Collecchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
