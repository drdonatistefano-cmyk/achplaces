What to see in Brunico

Town of Val Pusteria founded in the 13th century as a fortified village, with Via Centrale and four doors still preserved, dominated by the castle of the principal bishops that houses the Messner Mountain Museum Ripa; in the hamlet of Teodon the Provincial Museum of customs and customs, the oldest South Tyrolean regional museum.

Castello di Brunico e Messner Mountain Museum Ripa

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Bishop's castle of the 13th century on a hill above the city, once a summer residence of the bishops princes, then renovated and with walls renovated in wood, glass and steel; the main entrance is the south gate, once with drawbridge. Reinhold Messner has set you up MMM Ripa, an interactive museum on mountain peoples of Asia, Africa, South America and Europe (Sherpa, Inca, Tibetans, Mongols), with their cultures, religions and tourism, including objects, documentaries and meetings.

  • Where Vicolo Castello 2, Brunico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.messner-mountain-museum.it

Sources: messner-mountain-museum.it · cultura.gov.it · bruneck.com · italia.it

Museo provinciale degli usi e costumi di Teodone

Museums · Themed

The oldest South Tyrolean regional museum, outdoors, tells of the rural life of the past: the noble residence Mair am Hof of the late seventeenth century with a baroque room, ethnographic collections of historic vehicles and agricultural tools, and a path among traditional farms, gardens, pets and craft shops.

  • Where Via Duca Teodone 24, Teodone, Brunico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.museo-etnografico.it

Sources: museo-etnografico.it · italia.it · suedtirol.info

Centro storico, Via Centrale e porte cittadine

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Fortified village of the 13th century with four doors still preserved: Via Centrale (Stadtgasse), long way between the medieval doors with Erker facades, painted decorations and wrought iron insignia, shops and coffee; Ragen di Sopra at the foot of the castle, with Porta Rain to the east; the high town dominated by Palazzo Sternbach, the great parish and Piazza Cappuccini with the church of the Holy Spirit and the convent of the Capuchins. A historical tour of about 2 km (2 hours) combines the stages.

  • Where Via Centrale, Brunico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · suedtirol.info · bruneck.com

Chiesa delle Orsoline (Chiesa del Salvatore)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in the fifteenth century on the wall: its bell tower was part of the defenses and under it passed the road of ringing. It overlooks Tschurtschenthaler Square, starting point of the historic tour.

  • Where Piazza Tschurtschenthaler, Brunico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · suedtirol.info

Cimitero militare

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Near the historic centre, on the hill towards Riscone, it preserves the graves of the fallen of the Great War and is one of the most important memorial of the First World War in South Tyrol; it is reached by path no. 22, along which is also the Fountain of tears.

  • Where Sentiero n. 22, Brunico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it

Museums · Modern art

Museum of art in the center with modern and contemporary art, a historical collection and sacred works of art, as well as exhibitions of contemporary artists; it is located beyond Porta Rienza, in via Fratel Willram.

  • Where Via Fratel Willram, Brunico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Lanificio Moessmer e Ulbrich Textil Manufaktur

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Two places of the city's textile tradition: the wool mill Moessmer works wool from beginning to end and produces for fashion brands such as Prada and Armani; the Ulbrich Textil Manufaktur weaves for over a hundred years textiles for the home in natural fibres.

  • Where Brunico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it
