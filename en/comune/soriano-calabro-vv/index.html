What to see in Soriano Calabro

Vibonese Serre Country was founded around the Dominican Convent founded in 1510, destroyed by earthquakes of 1659 and 1783: today the ruins of the ancient complex, the Basilica Sanctuary of San Domenico and, in the spaces of the convent, the Marmi Museum and the Earthquake Museum.

Basilica Santuario di San Domenico

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Example of late Baroque architecture, built in 1838 on the ruins of the ancient convent. The canvas of San Domenico is venerated there, with the book on the right and the lily on the left: according to legend, it was handed over to Fr. Lorenzo da Grotteria Maria Maddalena and St. Catherine of Alexandria, between 14 and 15 September 1530. Free access, with platform for those who have difficulty walking.

  • Where Via San Domenico 2, Soriano Calabro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sorianocalabro.vv.it

Sources: comune.sorianocalabro.vv.it

Ruderi dell'antico Convento di San Domenico

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Founded in 1510, the Dominican monastery reached its maximum splendor in the 17th century: about 23,000 square meters with five cloisters and a Latin cross church with six side chapels, entirely decorated in marble by mostly Roman and Neapolitan artists. Thomas Campanella stayed there. Destroyed by the earthquake of 1659 and rebuilt by Fr. Bonaventura Presti, it was ruined by the earthquake of February 5, 1783; it remains legible, with the facade of the ancient church declared a national monument.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Soriano Calabro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sorianocalabro.vv.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · comune.sorianocalabro.vv.it

Museo dei Marmi (Museo del Convento di San Domenico)

Museums · Old masters

In a vaulted wing of the ancient convent, more than one hundred marble sculptures, also fragmentary, from the seventeenth-century church of San Domenico destroyed in 1783, presented as the most important collection of Baroque sculpture in marble from Naples down: a protome of Santa Caterina da Siena attributed to Gian Lorenzo Bernini, the Allegoria of the Religion of Orpheus Boselli, an Angel by Giuseppe Corradini, the Bust of San Domenico by Giuliano Finelli, the statues of San Vincenzo Ferrer and San Tommaso by Matteo Bottigliero and Domenico Antonio Vaccaro. The museum also exhibits sacred objects, ceramics, silverware and an ivory crucifix; the attached library preserves incunabula and five hundred.

MuTerr - Museo del Terremoto

Museums · Themed

In the underground spaces of the ancient Convent of San Domenico, it tells the seismic phenomena with attention to Calabria and Italy, from historical, anthropological and architectural perspectives. Designed by Schiavello Architects Office with architects Ciconte and Prestanicola, it has a room with corrugated wooden ceiling of more than a thousand pieces and a second environment, Scuri, with barrel vaults; it also hosts exhibitions and events.

  • Where Via Daffinà 19, Soriano Calabro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sorianocalabro.vv.it
