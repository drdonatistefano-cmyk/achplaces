What to see in Succivo

The municipality of the agri have been built in the area of the ancient Atella, a city flourished from the fourth century BC under the influence of Capua: the National Archaeological Museum of the Atellan Agro tells the story, while the village of Teverolaccio preserves a sixteenth-century house and the eighteenth-century farmhouse.

Museo archeologico nazionale dell'Agro atellano

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Since 2002 in a municipal building that was sentmental prison in the nineteenth century, it tells the southern part of the ager Campanus and the ancient Atella, a city with a regular urban system still recognizable, grown since the fourth century BC under the influence of Capua and involved in the war between Hannibal and Rome. Among the pieces, an ivory with Dionysus and Ariadne and finds of the necropolis of the fourth century BC of Caivano. Tickets only online or with the app Italian Museums.

In June 2024 the MiC announced a new exhibition of the museum in the former Town Hall of Atella in Naples in Sant'Arpino: check the location and times (tel. 081 5012701)

  • Where Via Roma 5, Succivo
  • Entry €5, reduced €2 (18-25 years EU), free under 18
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.succivo.ce.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · museicampania.cultura.gov.it · comune.succivo.ce.it

Casale di Teverolaccio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

About 1 km from the center, on the road to Gricignano-Aversa, the medieval village brings together a 16th century tower without entry to the ground (guards climbed with ropes, whose signs remain on the windowsills), guarded the streets between Aversa, Acerra, Capua and Naples, and an 18th century farmhouse with a large courtyard, aisles and cellars, belonged for a period to the Pignatelli. In 1713 the bridge of Teverolaccio was the scene of a clash with the French. The municipality indicates free access.

  • Where Via XXIV Maggio 1, Succivo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.succivo.ce.it

Sources: comune.succivo.ce.it

Chiesa della Trasfigurazione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church of the 16th century with a Latin cross with a central dome, transformed after a lightning lightning in 1670 set the wooden coffered ceiling on fire. In the presbytery there is an imposing restored organ; it preserves wooden statues of the seventeenth century escaped from the fire and 17 rounds on canvas with Christ, the Apostles and the Evangelists painted by Thomas De Vivo in 1864.

  • Where Piazza IV Novembre 18, Succivo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.succivo.ce.it

Sources: comune.succivo.ce.it

Chiesetta di San Sossio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Small 18th-century church with a majolica terracotta floor with a fine marble portal, probably coming from a more important church and destroyed.

  • Where Succivo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.succivo.ce.it

Sources: comune.succivo.ce.it
