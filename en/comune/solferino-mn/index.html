What to see in Solferino

Village of the Moorish hills linked to the battle of June 24, 1859, which inspired Henry Dunant the foundation of the Red Cross: on the hill the Rocca Spia d'Italia, the Risorgimento Museum, the Ossario Chapel and the Red Cross Memorial.

5 places to visit in Solferino, in the province of Mantua (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo del Risorgimento di Solferino e San Martino

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Founded in 1931, in three halls he exhibits documents, weapons and paintings from 1796 to 1870: French and Austrian firearms, uniforms of zuavi and the Guardia, a French cannon rigged in comparison with an Austrian obice, an autograph order of Napoleon III and six portraits of French generals by Giulio Carlini.

from 16 October to 16 March open only to groups by reservation (Turismo Mantova)

  • Where Via Ossario 6, Solferino
  • Entry €2.50, reduced €2, children 6-10 years €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · turismo.mantova.it · italia.it

Rocca di Solferino, «Spia d'Italia»

History and architecture · Towers · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Square tower 23 m high on the 206 m hill, documented from 1022, passed in 1315 to Rinaldo Bonacolsi called Passerino and restored in 1611 and 1880. Inside a small museum with finds of the battlefield and the Hall of the Sovereigns with portraits of Vittorio Emanuele II and Napoleon III; a wooden ramp leads to the panoramic terrace on the Garda, the Tower of San Martino and the Apennines.

  • Where Via Ossario, Solferino
  • Entry €2.50, reduced €2, children 6-10 years €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it · turismo.mantova.it

Cappella Ossario di San Pietro in Vincoli

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Church transformed into an ossuary in 1870, reached by a avenue of cypresses: it conserves 1,413 skulls and the remains of about 7,000 soldiers of all nationalities; at the entrance the bronze bust of Napoleon III, the monument to General Auger, mosaics on the facade and five busts of French generals.

  • Where Via Ossario, Solferino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Memoriale della Croce Rossa

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Built for the centenary of the battle, he recalls Henry Dunant and the volunteers who helped the wounded: a red glass cross and a white stone wall with the names of the countries belonging to the Red Cross, made with marble donated by those countries. You can reach from the Fortress along the avenue of cypresses of San Luigi Gonzaga.

  • Where Viale San Luigi Gonzaga, Solferino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Piazza Castello

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Rectangular square with the remains of the sixteenth-century fortress of Orazio Gonzaga: the entrance door, a stretch of walls, a watchtower and the church of San Nicola; wide view up to Lake Garda.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Solferino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it
