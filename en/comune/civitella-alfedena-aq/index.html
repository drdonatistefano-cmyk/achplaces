What to see in Civitella Alfedena

The smallest village in the Abruzzo, Lazio and Molise National Park, on a hill on the southern shore of Lake Barrea, with a fourteenth-century tower, the Apennine Wolf Museum and the wildlife areas; it is the starting point for the Chamosciara and the Rose Valley.

5 places to visit in Civitella Alfedena, in the province of L'Aquila (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village on a hill overlooking Lake Barrea, with narrow well-maintained streets, a tower of the 14th century and the church of San Nicola, as well as shops of local products and museums.

  • Where Civitella Alfedena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo del lupo appenninico e aree faunistiche

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In a former stable next to the Park Visitor Centre, documents the Apennine wolf with a rebuilt skeleton, a naturalized specimen and a diorama of the den; from here start the routes to the fauna of the wolf, on the hill in front of the village, and that of the lynx, in the upper part of the village. Closed on Tuesdays.

  • Where Via Santa Lucia, Civitella Alfedena
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Chiesa-santuario di Santa Lucia

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On a hill with grassy square about 300 meters from the village, overlooking the Sangro Valley and Barrea Lake.

  • Where Via Santa Lucia, Civitella Alfedena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Riserva della Camosciara

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

The Park's integral reserve between Opi and Civitella Alfedena: limestone amphitheatre and dolomia under Mount Sterpi d'Alto and the Balzo della Chiesa, between beech woods and black pines, where you can spot the chamois. You can visit only on foot and on the paths, also starting from Civitella; an easy path along the creek Scerto leads to the waterfalls, active all year round.

  • Where Via Gravare, Civitella Alfedena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Val di Rose e Rifugio di Forca Resuni

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

I1 trail of the Parco da Civitella Alfedena (about 3 hours, difficulty E, 845 m of difference in altitude) between beech and pine trees up to Passo Cavuto (1,980 m) and the refuge of Forca Resuni, with frequent sightings of chamois.

in July and August quota access, only with guide and reservation at the Park offices (dates indicated by the source referring to 2016)

  • Where Civitella Alfedena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcoabruzzo.it · italia.it
