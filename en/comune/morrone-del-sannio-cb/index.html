What to see in Morrone del Sannio

Small hilly municipality of Molise, on the Celano-Foggia tracturo, where you can visit the complex of Santa Maria di Casalpiano: Roman villa, medieval necropolis and Romanesque abbey linked to Montecassino since 1017.

Complesso di Santa Maria di Casalpiano

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Viewpoint

On the plateau above the Biferno valley, along the Celano-Foggia tracturo, stood a large Roman rustic villa (II-I century BC, enlarged in imperial age with a thermal sector), inhabited until the 5th century, with white, black and greenish tiles floors. On its ruins there was a necropolis of more than 70 crated tombs (VI-VII century) and in 1017 the churches of Casalpiano were donated to Montecassino. After the earthquake of 1456 the church was rebuilt next to the original: the new Romanesque façade with rose window, portal of the 12th century and three apses, flanked by cloister and Renaissance hostel, while the ancient church remains three well preserved apses. The site is state-owned.
