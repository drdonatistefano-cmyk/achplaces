What to see in Radicofani

Borgo-strada at the southern border of the UNESCO Valley, dominated by one of the most spectacular fortresses in Tuscany, at the long border between the Grand Duchy and the Papal State.

5 places to visit in Radicofani, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Radicofani in the guide with map and filters

Fortezza di Radicofani e Museo del Cassero

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The fortress visible from tens of kilometers, linked to the figure of Ghino di Tacco, now home to the Museo del Cassero with the archaeological finds of the territory.

  • Where Radicofani
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Romanesque church of the thirteenth century, damaged in war and restored in 1946, with a splendid collection of Robbian terracotta and wooden statues, including the Madonna and Child of Francesco di Valdambrino.

  • Where Piazza San Pietro, Radicofani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa di Sant'Agata e Palazzo Pretorio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

On the main street Sant'Agata with a large terracotta dossal of the workshop of the Della Robbia; not far away the Palazzo Pretorio covered with stone coats of arms.

  • Where Radicofani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Palazzo della Posta

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Medici villa on the Cassia street, then customs and mail station that hosted many illustrious travelers.

  • Where Via Cassia, Radicofani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Bosco Isabella e Giardini del Maccione

Nature and landscape · City parks · Viewpoint

The Maccione gardens with the statue of Ghino di Tacco, the "Robin Hood of Val d'Orcia" and the Bosco Isabella, signposted by Visit Tuscany among the nearby places.

  • Where Radicofani
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
