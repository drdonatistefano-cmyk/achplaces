What to see in Monasterolo di Savigliano

Country of the plain of Cuneo dominated by the Castle of Solaro, fortress thirteenth century of the Marquises of Saluzzo then of the counts Solaro, today seat of the Town Hall, next to the great parish of Saints Peter and Paul in modern Baroque style, inaugurated in 1904.

Castello dei Solaro

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The imposing manor dates back to the first half of the thirteenth century (Visit Cuneese indicates 1241, at the behest of Thomas I of Saluzzo); destroyed in the wars of 1357-1363, it was rebuilt by the Marquises of Saluzzo between 1363 and 1378. In 1604 he passed to the Solaro di Moretta, who took the title of Counts of Monasterolo, and in the seventeenth century the salons were painted and decorated. Massif building adorned with turrets and interrupted by a large tower, it is of the Municipality since 1928 and hosts the municipal offices; it is visited by reservation.

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Wanted by the parish priest Don Antonio Cavatorta, it was built between 1899 and 1904 on a project by Giuseppe Gallo from Turin, in modern Baroque style with Corinthian capitals, and inaugurated on 1 October 1904 in the presence of Cardinal Richelmy; the bell tower is 1924. Four side altars dedicated to the Souls of Purgatory, to the Immaculate Heart of Mary, to the Madonna del Rosario and to the Sacred Heart; next to the canonical house of 1825.
