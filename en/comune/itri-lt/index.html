What to see in Itri

Country between the Aurunci Mountains and the sea, home of Fra' Diavolo and the Itrana olive (called Gaeta's), dominated by the medieval castle on the hill of Sant'Angelo; along the Appia Antica remain about two kilometers of Roman basolate in the gorge of Sant'Andrea, while on Mount Fusco stands the sanctuary of the Madonna della Civita.

Castello di Itri

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built in several phases in four centuries: the pentagonal tower was wanted in 882 by Docibile I, ipato of the Duchy of Gaeta, to spot Saracen raids; the nephew Marino I added the square mastium and around 1250, with the Caetani, rose the cylindrical tower called the Crocodile, linked to the legend of a reptile in the moat. Inside the private chapel of the Caetani with a fresco of Sant'Antonio Abate and a panoramic terrace on the Ulysses Riviera; from the cavea you can see Vico Giudea, the ancient Jewish ghetto.

  • Where Via Sant'Angelo 60, Itri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Museo del brigantaggio

Museums · Themed

Anthropological demoethanological museum inaugurated in 2003 in a former kindergarten converted with transparent facades, along the Appia route; dedicated to Michele Pezza called Fra' Diavolo, born in Itri in 1771. The path, curated by Vincenzo Pavilione, has three sections: "Ragioni della Storia" (a century of repression), "Ragioni del Mito" (flying leaves, operas, novels, films) and "Ragioni del Luogo" (the "Terra dei briganti" (the "Terra dei briganti") as heritage. MiC timetables: closed on Mondays; morning Thu, Sat and Sun 10-13, afternoon Mar-Dom 16.30-19 (winter) or 17.30-20 (summer).

Appia Antica e gola di Sant'Andrea

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

A route of about two kilometers follows the original path of the Appia with the Roman basolate, crossing the gorge of Sant'Andrea between Roman supporting walls in square and polygonal work and the remains of a Bourbon fortification, the Fort of Sant'Andrea.

  • Where Gola di Sant'Andrea, Itri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Santuario della Madonna della Civita

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

On Monte Fusco, about 12 km from the village, in the Aurunci Mountains Natural Park, is a pilgrimage destination; from the terrace the view ranges from the Circeo promontory to the plain of Fondi and Monte Ruazzo. On the seven Sundays (or Saturdays) before July 21, the procession rises at dawn from Raino.

  • Where Monte Fusco, Itri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Sentiero di Raino al Santuario della Civita

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the square of the hamlet of Raino, on the southern side of Mount Fusco, the CAI 949 trail climbs to the sanctuary in about 3.5 km and 370 m of difference in altitude (one hour and a half) between terraced olive groves of Itrana, dry walls, stations of the Via Crucis and, in the final section, a holm oak wood.

  • Where Contrada Raino, Itri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
