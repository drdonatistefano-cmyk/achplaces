What to see in Volpago del Montello

At the foot of Montello, the karst hill theater of the Battle of Solstizio of 1918, Volpago del Montello preserves in Venegazzù the eighteenth-century Villa Spineda and the church of Giordano Riccati, and in Selva a church with canvases of the Guards and Fontebasso.

Villa Spineda Gasparini Loredan

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Designed by Francesco Maria Preti (1701-1774) and completed by Miazzi, who reworked the project, it is indicated by the Municipality as one of the most successful examples of eighteenth-century Palladianism: a dominical body with central pronao and two ariosian side boats. In front of the façade the garden is surrounded by a fish pond, on the back stretches up to the slopes of Montello. The Benetton Group has been renovated on a project by Afra and Tobia Scarpa and by Prof. Mora.

accessible only at events (card of the Municipality)

Chiesa di Venegazzù

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Designed by Giordano Riccati, a contemporary and friend of Francesco Maria Preti, in the same period as Villa Spineda, has the facade dominated by a large pronao. The interior with a single nave is marked by semi-colons, among which are placed six large canvases of the Venetian school.

Chiesa di Selva del Montello

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church was among the richest in art in the diocese of Treviso when the air trumpet of July 24, 1930 destroyed it; the new church, large and sober, was designed by architect F. Shield to welcome the recovered works. It preserves the dossal of the main altar of Giorgio Massari, coming from the demolished Certosa del Montello, and six large canvases with stories of Moses by Giannantonio and Francesco Guardi and Francesco Fontebasso, restored by the Cassamarca Foundation.

Montello

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A dome of about 13 for 5 km that stands out in the Treviso plain, with a karst character with numerous dolines (up to 74 per km2); the highest point is the Collesel Val dell'Acqua (371 m), where stands the church of Santa Maria della Vittoria. On the slope towards the Piave many places recall the Great War: here the Battle of Solstice was fought (15-23 June 1918), which started the Austro-Hungarian retreat.
