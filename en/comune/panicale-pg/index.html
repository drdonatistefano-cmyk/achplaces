What to see in Panicale

Natural terrace between the Trasimeno and the valley of the Nestore: the village walled on three squares, with the Martyrdom of San Sebastiano del Perugino and the tradition of embroidery on tulle.

4 places to visit in Panicale, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 3 of the 5 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Chiesa di San Sebastiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries and modified in 1623, near the door Perugina, it preserves the frescoes of Perugino with the Martyrdom of San Sebastiano.

  • Where Panicale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Teatro Cesare Caporali

History and architecture · Historic theatres

In the heart of the historic centre, it is worth visiting for its elegant interior structure richly decorated and for the season of shows.

  • Where Panicale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Museo del Tulle «Anita Belleschi Grifoni»

Museums · Themed

In the former church of Sant'Agostino in Piazza Regina Margherita, it celebrates the Ars Panicalensis, the embroidery on tulle, with wedding dresses and sacred vestments.

  • Where Piazza Regina Margherita, Panicale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Collegiata di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Overlooking the homonymous square next to Piazza Umberto I, where stands the Praetorian Palace.

  • Where Panicale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
