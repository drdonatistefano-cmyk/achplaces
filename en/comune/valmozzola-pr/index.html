What to see in Valmozzola

Parma Apennines municipality, located in Mormorola (565 m), along the valley of the Mozzola stream that descends from Monte Barigazzo and La Tagliata to the Taro; among the places to see the Museum of the Resistance, the thirteenth-century ruins of the Fortress of Gusaliggio and the nearby parish church of Santa Maria Assunta.

Museo della Resistenza

Museums · Themed

Established in the spring of 2005, in the 60th of the Liberation, and dedicated to the partisan Gian Paolo Larini, who worked as a doctor in the valley; the fruit of thirty years of research, he exhibits in chronological order, from 8 September 1943 to 9 May 1945, about 600 original pieces among objects of war, letters and photographs, over 50 oral testimonies and documents launched by the Allied planes on the Apennines.

discordant times: the MiC card indicates Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday 9-18 and Saturday morning, the guide of the City weekend or by reservation

Rocca di Gusaliggio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortress of the 13th century on opholithic cliffs overlooking the Mozzola stream, among the possessions of the Marquis Pallavicino from the early thirteenth century: here in 1269 he died besieged the leader Uberto Pallavicino called the Great. Today remains the ruins.

  • Where Gusaliggio, Valmozzola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page parmawelcome.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · comune.valmozzola.pr.it

Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta di Gusaliggio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the 11th century, rebuilt in the Baroque period, with interior renovated in neo-Gothic style and neo-Romanesque façade of 1965; it contains frescoes, a altarpiece of the Assumption and eighteenth-century statues.

  • Where Località Pieve Gusaliggio 32, Valmozzola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page parmawelcome.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · comune.valmozzola.pr.it

Monte Barigazzo e La Tagliata

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Monte Barigazzo (1284 m), a destination for panoramic excursions, forms the watershed between Ceno and Taro with La Tagliata; the woods between Barigazzo and Monte Grosso are Site of Community Interest. From the hamlet of Mariano starts the path CAI 811 (about 11.8 km, difficulty E); in the valley also trails 803 and 811c and the Ring of the Val Ceno.

  • Where Mariano, Valmozzola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page parmawelcome.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · comune.valmozzola.pr.it · parmawelcome.it
