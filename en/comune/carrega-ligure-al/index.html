What to see in Carrega Ligure

The municipality of the upper Val Borbera on the border with Liguria and Emilia, whose territory coincides with the Natural Park of the Upper Val Borbera (2019): ruins of the castle Malaspina Fieschi Doria on Monte Carmo, abandoned hamlets like Reneuzzi and Casoni and the Ecomuseo dei Sette Ricordi.

5 places to visit in Carrega Ligure, in the province of Alessandria (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Malaspina Fieschi Doria

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Rude of a massive tower with bastion on a rocky spur of Monte Carmo, above the village, tied to the ancient mule track of the Passo delle Capanne. Carrega is documented since 1153; the fief passed from the bishops of Tortona to the marquises of Gavi and Parodi, then to the Malaspina; in the fifteenth century it was contested by the Fieschi. In 1475 a lightning strike struck the tower and Duke Galeazzo Sforza ordered its smoothing. The tower-tank to the south, the residence to the north and a barrel vaulted room remain; the area has been recovered from the town and from the ruin you can enjoy the view.

Parco naturale dell'Alta Val Borbera

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established by regional law of 27 March 2019 at the request of the Municipality, it exceeds 5,000 hectares and coincides with the municipal territory, between 600 and 1,669 meters of Monte Legnà, with the peaks of Monte Carmo, Antola and Porreio. More than three quarters of the area are beech and chestnut woods; there are arid grasslands rich in orchids, species of Alpine wrecks, the southern tulip, a stable population of wolves, six species of bats and 94 species of butterflies. It is described as the only example in Piedmont of Mediterranean-mountain silvo-pastoral habitat.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.carregaligure.al.it

Reneuzzi e Casoni, i villaggi abbandonati

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry

Two hamlets completely depopulated with the industrial boom of the 1950s and 1960s, reachable by the path of the "Villages of Pietra" . The inhabitants of the area preserve the original urban planning and examples of traditional architecture.

  • Where Frazioni Reneuzzi e Casoni, Carrega Ligure
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.carregaligure.al.it

Sources: alexala.it · comune.carregaligure.al.it · areeprotetteappenninopiemontese.it

Capanne di Carrega e crinale dell'Antola

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The pass of Capanne di Carrega, with the historic tavern and post station on the ancient salt streets, is crossed by the hiking itinerary "Via del mare" and by the long ridge that connects Monte Carmo to Monte Antola. On the homonymous pass stands the historic church of San Fermo.

  • Where Capanne di Carrega, Carrega Ligure
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.carregaligure.al.it

Sources: comune.carregaligure.al.it

Ecomuseo dei Sette Ricordi

Museums · Themed

Ecomuseum spread in the municipal territory, without walls, which you visit along prepared routes. It recounts seven memories of mountain life: the bands, the wheat and the mills; the mules and the mule-trees; the piper, the dances and the dark ones; the chestnut and the chestnut tree and the dry-faced, the pasture; the charcoal (the carbunin-a-clad); the casini, the dry-stone walls and the serves.
