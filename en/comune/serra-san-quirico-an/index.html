What to see in Serra San Quirico

A galley-shaped village set in the rock, inside the Regional Park Gola della Rossa and Frasassi, with its ... covers along the walls, the Baroque Saint Lucia with the historical Cartoteca delle Marche and, along the Esino, the Romanesque-Gothic Abbey of St. Helena.

9 places to visit in Serra San Quirico, in the province of Ancona (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e copertelle

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historical centre, well preserved and the medieval plant practically intact, has the shape of a ship; along the walls run the 'coverelle', covered passages and porchs surmounted by the houses.

  • Where Serra San Quirico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Piazza della Libertà e Torre del Cassero

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The central square has a sixteenth-century fountain; not far is the Torre del Cassero, of the fourteenth century.

  • Where Piazza della Libertà, Serra San Quirico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Lucia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

From the severe and simple façade, it is one of the best examples of seventeenth-century baroque in the Marches: it preserves paintings by Pasqualino Rossi and a rare organ by Giuseppe M. Head of 1675, still working.

  • Where Via Marcellini, Serra San Quirico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Cartoteca storica delle Marche

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the former convent of the Silvestrini next to Saint Lucia, reopened on July 29, 2001 after the restorations followed by the earthquake of 1997, it collects about 300 historical maps, atlases and prints that document the historical, geographical and administrative evolution of the Marches; in the third room there is a faithful reproduction of a globe of Vincenzo Coronelli.

  • Where Via Marcellini, Serra San Quirico
  • Entry €2, reduced €1,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Museo dei fossili «Don Giuseppe Mattiacci»

Museums · Science and university

Indoor paleontological collection, at the Sanctuary of the Immaculate Heart of Mary, with fossils especially of the Marche Apennines of different geological epochs.

Unpublished times, to be requested (tel. 0731 86030)

  • Where Via Aldo Moro 4, Serra San Quirico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: isprambiente.gov.it · italia.it

Abbazia di Sant'Elena

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

About 7 km from the village, on the banks of the Esino, it was founded by Saint Romualdo and in 1180 entered the Camaldolese congregation. Romanesque-Gothic and compact as a church-fortress, it has sailing bell tower, decorated Romanesque portal, three naves with figurative capitals and a crypt under the presbytery; only the church can be visited.

  • Where Località Sant'Elena 34, Serra San Quirico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Borgo di Domo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Fraction from the pretty old town with the church of San Paterniano; the castle, of very ancient origins, would have taken on the fortified appearance between 1356 and 1391.

  • Where Domo, Serra San Quirico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Parco regionale Gola della Rossa e di Frasassi

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The park, which is located in the municipality, surrounds the village with more than 35 paths of various difficulties between woods, hermitages and gorges, suitable for hiking, mountain biking, horseback riding and climbing.

  • Where Serra San Quirico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint
