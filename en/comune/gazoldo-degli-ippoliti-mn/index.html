What to see in Gazoldo degli Ippoliti

Municipality of Alto Mantovano, ancient feud of the Ippoliti with wide autonomy also under the Gonzaga and right to beat coin: Villa Ippoliti has been home to since 1980 the MAM, a museum of modern and contemporary art dedicated to Mantua art and Lombard Chiarismo, and in the village remain the sixteenth-century Palazzo Ippoliti, today seat of the Municipality, and the Palazzo Pigozzi or Pretorio.

MAM - Museo d'arte moderna e contemporanea

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Founded in 1980 in Villa Ippoliti, the sixteenth-century residence of the local feudalists, it is thought as a place of research and study as well as of conservation. The scientific program was born from the studies that have re-evaluated Archimede Bresciani from Gazoldo and Mario Lomini and has brought attention to the Lombard Chiarismo and the contribution of Mantua to that movement. Open from Monday to Saturday in the morning (Thursday until 5 pm); during the temporary exhibitions also Saturday, Sunday and holidays 15-18.

  • Where Villa Ippoliti, via Guglielmo Marconi 126, Gazoldo degli Ippoliti
  • Entry €2, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.gazoldo.mn.it · turismo.mantova.it

Palazzo Ippoliti e piazza del monumento ai caduti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Also called Palazzo Castello, it was built in the sixteenth century by the counts Ippoliti in the golden period of their imperial manor, lasted until the Italian countryside of Napoleon in 1796; the rooms of the noble floor preserve stuccoes and floral decorations of the eighteenth century and Liberty decorations of the early twentieth century. The central part was demolished, leaving two separate bodies; today it is the seat of the municipality and in the past it hosted an institute for orphans of the war 1915-1918. On the square in front of the palace stands the monument to the fallen of the Great War, inaugurated by King Vittorio Emanuele III (in 1925 according to the Municipality).

  • Where Via Guglielmo Marconi 126, Gazoldo degli Ippoliti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it · comune.gazoldo.mn.it

Palazzo Pigozzi o Pretorio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Ancient building with austere appearance six-seventh-century, just outside the village in the direction of Redondesco: although with the characteristic elements of the villa, it was born as a public palace. The double nature is explained by the history of Gazoldo, the true lordship of the Ippoliti with a special area of autonomy even during the Gonzaga domination and the right to beat its own currency.

  • Where Strada per Redondesco, Gazoldo degli Ippoliti

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it
