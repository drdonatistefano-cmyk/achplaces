What to see in Castel San Giovanni

Walled village founded between the 12th and 13th centuries as a bulwark against Pavia, now a lively town in Val Tidone: the 14th century Collegiate Church with the Crucifix of Del Maino and the Spousalizio attributed to Bernardino Luini, the church of San Rocco and Villa Braghieri with the Ethnographic Museum.

4 places to visit in Castel San Giovanni, in the province of Piacenza (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Collegiata di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The fourteenth century, built on a church of 1130, with large sharp arches on the facade and baroque portals; inside the wooden Crucifix of Giacomo and Giovan Angelo del Maino (1496), the baptismal font in marble of Luni dei Casella (1550), the Bride of the Virgin attributed to Bernardino Luini, a polyptych of 1448 and in sacristy the great polyptych signed by Sebastiano Novelli in 1540.

  • Entry Free

Chiesa di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1476 on the site of a pre-Romanesque oratory documented in 954 and restored by Giulio Ulisse Arata at the beginning of the twentieth century: traces of frescoes from the fifteenth century and two wooden statues, a Madonna with Child and a Deposition, attributed to Jan Hermansz Geernaert.

  • Where Castel San Giovanni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Villa Braghieri e Museo etnografico della Val Tidone

Museums · Themed

Villa at the eastern entrance of the city, which hosted the Duchess Maria Amalia wife of Ferdinand of Bourbon, with furnishings and frescoes of the Seven and Nineteenth Century: today it is home to the municipal library, the ethnographic museum of Val Tidone, with materials on the peasant life, fossils and fragments of Roman objects, and the museum dedicated to Cardinal Agostino Casaroli.

Centro storico e architetture liberty

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The regular plant centre was built around the castle built in 1290 by Alberto Scotti, with the quadrangular system between the streams Lora and Carona; along the main streets examples of liberty architecture of the early twentieth century.
