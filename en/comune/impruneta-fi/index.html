Basilica di Santa Maria all'Impruneta

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

One of the three most famous Marian sanctuaries in Tuscany, with Michelozzo's temple and the Crucifixion of Luca della Robbia; in the loggia the Treasure Museum, by appointment.

  • Where Piazza Buondelmonti 28, Impruneta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com
