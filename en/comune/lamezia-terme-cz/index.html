What to see in Lamezia Terme

City of the Lametina plain born from the union of Nicastro, Sambiase and Sant'Eufemia: the Norman-Svevo castle on the hill, the convent of San Domenico with the Archaeological Museum and the coastal tower of the Knights of Malta.

3 places to visit in Lamezia Terme, in the province of Catanzaro (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo archeologico lametino – Complesso di San Domenico

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

On the first floor of the former Dominican convent founded between 1506 and 1521 where he studied Tommaso Campanella: prehistoric, classical sections (Terina and the Greeks, the treasure of Acquafredda with 55 Sibari statemen) and medieval, with a cannon of bombardment from the castle of Nicastro.

Closed for PNRR accessibility work

  • Where Complesso di San Domenico, Nicastro, Lamezia Terme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Castello normanno svevo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill of San Teodoro a Nicastro, fortified by the Normans in the second half of the 11th century and enlarged by Frederick II with the pentagonal mast: four cylindrical towers, bastions and buttresses with loggia; after the earthquake of 1638 lost its military function.

Unvisitable: routes not secured

  • Where San Teodoro, Nicastro, Lamezia Terme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · cultura.gov.it

Bastione di Malta

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Coast tower built around 1550 by order of Viceroy Don Pedro of Toledo against the Saracens and entrusted to the Knights of Malta: square plant, pyramidal trunk, four rooms and terrace on top; appears in the city coat of arms.

  • Where Sant'Eufemia, Lamezia Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · cultura.gov.it
