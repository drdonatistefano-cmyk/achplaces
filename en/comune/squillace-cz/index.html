What to see in Squillace

A bishop's city that gives its name to the gulf, perched on a hill overlooking the Costa degli Aranci and dominated by the Norman Castle; it is known as the "Città della Ceramica" for the shops of the historic center and houses the cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta, rebuilt after the earthquake of 1783, and the diocesan Museum in the Bishop's Palace.

Castello Normanno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built by the Normans in the 11th century at the end of the conquest of Calabria, it occupies the highest point of the hill; the polygonal tower on the east side probably owes to Frederick II. He passed to the Lancia, Monfort, Del Balzo and Marzano, then to the Aragonese and in 1494 to the Borgia, whose marble coat of arms is on the portal, for the marriage between Goffredo Borgia and Sancha d'Aragona; the earthquake of 1783 destroyed the village.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Borgo Tirone, Squillace
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Concattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Made to be built by Count Ruggero I in the 11th century to affirm the Latin rite, it was damaged by the earthquake of 1643, rebuilt in 1737 by Bishop Michele Abati, destroyed again in 1783 and reconsecrated in 1798 by Bishop Nicola Notaris. Domina piazza del Duomo, is a cathedral of the Archdiocese of Catanzaro-Squillace and since 2015 a minor basilica.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Squillace
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.squillace.cz.it · italia.it

Museo diocesano di arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

Founded in 1984 on the initiative of Archbishop Antonio Cantisani, it is located in the Bishops' Palace of 1564. The six halls display works from the 14th to the 19th century, including monsters, chalices, candlesticks, vestments, paintings, wooden sculptures and marbles, largely coming from the treasure of the Norman cathedral destroyed by the earthquake of 1783.

Centro storico, palazzi e botteghe della ceramica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historic centre on the hills preserves under the Duomo square the ruins of the church and monastery of Santa Chiara, begun in the early seventeenth century and severely damaged in 1783, the small Gothic church of Santa Maria della Pietà, the Baroque façade of Palazzo Olivieri and the courtyard of Palazzo Pepe. Squillace is known as "City of Ceramics": the shops, especially along the Pepe course, produce traditional forms such as pine cone and pomegranate.

  • Where Corso Pepe, Squillace
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Spiaggia di Squillace

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

It opens in the Gulf of Squillace, in front of the medieval village, in a bay awarded with the Green Flag and indicated among the most unspoilt in the province.
