What to see in Ariano Irpino

The center of Irpino is more than 800 meters on the Apennine watershed: the Norman castle with the museum of Norman civilization, the tradition of majolica and, in the fields, the ancient Roman street node of Aequum Tuticum.

3 places to visit in Ariano Irpino, in the province of Avellino (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Normanno e Museo della civiltà normanna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

At 811 meters, in a strategic position between Irpinia, Sannio and Puglia with views of the valleys of Ufita, Miscano and Cervaro: almost trapezoidal plant with corner towers of 13-16 meters in diameter and the ruins of the Norman mast; restored at the beginning of the 2000, since 2009 it houses the museum of Norman civilization.

  • Where Via San Tommaso, Ariano Irpino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo civico e della ceramica

Museums · Themed

Opened in 1991 on the second floor of Palazzo Forte: about 250 local ceramics from the 12th to the 21st century, to enhance the ancient folk majolica of Ariano, and finds from the 9th to the 15th century from the excavations along the ancient walls.

  • Where Via R. D'Afflitto 14, Ariano Irpino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Area archeologica di Aequum Tuticum

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Nodo viario romano in the locality of Sant'Eleuterio, where the Via Aemilia, Via Traiana (109 A.D.) and the Herculia passed: first century A.D. baths with black and white mosaics, rooms of the second century, a large room with polychrome mosaic and medieval blocks around a courtyard with well; an earthquake damaged it in 346 A.D.

  • Where Località Sant'Eleuterio, SP 90bis, Ariano Irpino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it
