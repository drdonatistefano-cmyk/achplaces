What to see in Bastiglia

Country of the Modena plain among the vineyards of Lambrusco di Sorbara, born as a transit place on the Naviglio di Modena: here in 1432 the octagonal basin of the Support was built and the mill extended to sixteen millstones; today the Museum of peasant civilization and the Baroque sanctuary of San Clemente.

Museo della civiltà contadina (MUC)

Museums · Themed

Established by the Municipality in 1977 in a historic building of the square next to the town hall, it preserves more than two thousand finds on the rural life of Modena of the first half of the twentieth century, on the history of Bastille, of the fifteenth-century basin on the Naviglio and the extensive mill. Themed rooms on hemp, cellar and lambrusco, housekeeper, laundry, worldly, school and ancient crafts, with the suggestive hall of the fireplace; in the Pavilion of the agricultural machines vintage tractors, the threshing machine "Carra" and the prototype of the tractor Fiat 60R (1940-41), buried to remove it from the Nazi troops and recovered in May 1945. Damaged by the earthquake of 2012 and the flood of 2014, it was re-arranged also with multimedia media; it has the regional certification of Museum quality. Closed in August and on national holidays; for times contact the museum (museo@comune.bastiglia.mo.it).

Piazza della Repubblica, il mulino e il Sostegno

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The square occupies the site of the Support, the basin with octagonal plan built in 1432 by Niccolò III d'Este on the Naviglio, with saracinesche that maintained constant water level: according to VisitModena the first in Italy, taken from Leonardo in Milan about fifty years later; it was underground after the detour of the channel at the beginning of the twentieth century. The mill remains, of nineteenth-century appearance but of more ancient origins: with sixteen millstones it was the largest in the region, it also operated a millstone, a shiner for rice, a public sawmill and then the dynamo that illuminated the country; closed in the twenties.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica, Bastiglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Santuario della Beata Vergine delle Grazie di San Clemente

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In the countryside between Bastille and Modena, where already in the 11th century there was a church linked to the abbey of Nonantola. In 1689 the roof collapsed under the snow but a fresco of the 15th century of the Madonna and Child was saved, and the pilgrimages financed the reconstruction: a Baroque church with a Greek cross with dome designed by Antonio Loraghi, with a canvas by Giovan Battista Cipriani, a Martyrdom of Saint Ignatius of Antioch by Sigismondo Caula, a Pietà by Antonio Verni da Guercino and dozens of votive tablets.

  • Where San Clemente, Bastiglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Naviglio e percorso ciclopedonale verso Bomporto

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

The ancient canal that connected Modenese to the Adriatic, fed also by spring waters; along the banks runs a bike route and run about 3 km towards Bomporto.

  • Where Naviglio, Bastiglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it

Ciclabile Terre del Sorbara

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Cycle path of about 8 km on the old SEFTA railway track that leads directly to Modena, among the vineyards of one of the finest qualities of Lambrusco.

  • Where Bastiglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
