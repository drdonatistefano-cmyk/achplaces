What to see in Tremezzina

The municipality was founded in 2014 by the union of Tremezzo, Mezzegra, Lenno and Ossuccio on the western shore of Lake Como: here are Villa Carlotta, Villa del Balbianello (FAI), the Sacro Monte di Ossuccio UNESCO heritage and the Isola Comacina, the only island of the lake, connected by Greenway.

Villa Carlotta e giardino botanico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Baroque villa built in the seventeenth century by the Marquis Giorgio Clerici, today a museum with sculptures by Antonio Canova and paintings by Francesco Hayez. The park of about 20 acres combines Italian garden with hedges, statues and water games, romantic English garden and forests of rhododendrons, camellias and azaleas that make it a botanical garden. Open from March to November, with variable times; admission fee.

Villa del Balbianello

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

A Franciscan monastery has been built on the Dosso di Lavedo since the 13th century, transformed at the end of the 18th century by Cardinal Angelo Maria Durini into a literary retreat with the Panoramic Loggia. After several families, in 1974 it was bought by explorer Guido Monzino, the first Italian on Everest, who left it at FAI (1988). You can visit interiors with 18th century French furnishings, tapestries from Beauvais and extra-European art, the Museum of Shipments with the North Pole sleigh 1971 and the garden with topiary prunings and terraces on the lake.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Sacro Monte di Ossuccio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the nine sacred pre-Alpine Mountains UNESCO heritage: built in the mid-17th century and completed in 1710, it has fourteen chapels with 230 statues in stucco and terracotta, mostly of Agostino Silva, and frescoes by Carlo Gaffuri, Francesco Innocenzo Torriani and Giovan Paolo Recchi; the route climbs to the sanctuary of the Madonna del Soccorso (XVI century, modified until the eighteenth century). The sanctuary is not reached by car; parking space at the first and fourth chapels.

  • Where Via Pasquale Castelli 6, loc. Ossuccio, Tremezzina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sacrimonti.org · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Isola Comacina e Antiquarium

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The only island of Lake Como, was Roman military garrison, political centre and important religious center of the diocese; theatre of the fight against Barbarossa, then abandoned, from the early twentieth century is archaeological site with Roman columns, early Christian testimonies, fragments of mosaic and remains of various Romanesque churches. The exhibits are exhibited at the Antiquarium of Ossuccio, from which you cross the lake by boat. Island and museum open from March to October.

  • Where Isola Comacina, loc. Ossuccio, Tremezzina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Campanile di Santa Maria Maddalena e Oratorio di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In Ossuccio the church of Santa Maria Maddalena all'Ospedaletto has a famous bell tower: a cell adorned with bricks was built over the Romanesque bell tower, which ended just above the current bifora. In the village also the Oratory of San Giacomo, Romanesque church at an apse (XI-XII century) restored by Pietro Lingeri, with some of the oldest frescoes in the area (XII-XV century).

  • Where Ospedaletto, loc. Ossuccio, Tremezzina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Museo del Paesaggio del Lago di Como

Museums · City history

In Villa Mainona (1670-1672) in Tremezzo, it collects ancient prints of the lake from the sixteenth to the nineteenth century, historical photographs between the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, a multimedia system with maps and a section on the films shot on the lake. Open from Easter to the end of October, Thursday-Sunday, 10-12 and 14-18.

  • Where Via Statale 4835, Tremezzina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Parco Teresio Olivelli e Parco di Villa Mayer

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

The public park Teresio Oleveli, in front of Villa Mainona, has a panoramic terrace-darsena. Not far from the municipal park of Villa Mayer, a 19th century villa damaged by a fire in 1919 and renovated by the rationalist architect Pietro Lingeri, recreates the Italian garden of Villa Colonna in Rome.

  • Entry Free

Rogaro e Santuario della Madonna degli Eremiti

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fraction with a Baroque square; the shrine houses a black Madonna brought by Swiss Catholics at the time of the Lutheran reform, linked to the abbey of Einsiedeln. The feast of the sanctuary is held every year on the third Sunday of October.

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In Mezzegra, the parish church of Sant'Abbondio has a baroque interior and ceiling painted by Giulio Quaglio; in the hamlet of Bonzanigo, Palazzo Brentano and Palazzo Rosati recall the trade of oil and citrus with Northern Europe.

Greenway del Lago di Como

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

An easy walk of about 10.5 km in 7 stages, in about 3 and a half hours, from Colonno to Cadenabbia di Griante: it follows at times the shore and the ancient Via Regina and crosses Ossuccio, Lenno, Mezzegra and Tremezzo, touching Villa del Balbianello, Villa Carlotta, Villa Mainona and the Park Levels.

  • Where Da Colonno a Griante, via Tremezzina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it
