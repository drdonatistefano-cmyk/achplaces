What to see in Procida

Flegrea Island of the Gulf of Naples, reachable by ferry from Naples or Pozzuoli in about an hour, with the colorful houses of Marina Corricella, the fortified village of Terra Murata with Palazzo d'Avalos and the abbey of San Michele Arcangelo, and the islet of Vivara connected by a bridge.

11 places to visit in Procida, in the province of Naples (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Terra Murata

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village on the highest point of the island, born as a Roman settlement and fortified in the Middle Ages; the watchtowers and a second city walls date back to the era of Saracen raids. It is reached from Marina Corricella with the steep Castle ascent.

  • Where Salita Castello, Procida
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Palazzo d'Avalos

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Impressive 16th century building that dominates the eastern side of the promontory of Terra Murata; in 1529 the village became a fief of the family of Avalos and between 1830 and 1988 the palace hosted a prison.

  • Where Terra Murata, Procida
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Abbazia di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The first nucleus dates back to the 11th century; it preserves works of art, a library of the 16th century, a permanent nativity scene and a necropolis ossuary. Under the abbey a museum complex is spread over three floors.

  • Where Terra Murata, Procida
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Margherita Nuova

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Built in the 16th century by the Dominican monks, who had left the cenobio of Santa Margherita Vecchia alla Chiaiolella for Saracen raids; the ridge on which it stands took the name of ... The already secured parts host exhibitions and cultural events.

The source indicates that the church is being restored; only the parts that are safe can be visited

  • Where Terra Murata, Procida
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Marina della Corricella

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The oldest fishing village on the island, reachable only from the sea or on foot along various steps: houses with bright colors arranged amphitheatre on the sea, domes, arches and stairs, fishing boats and nets lying to dry, without car. The best view is from the Belvedere dei Cannoni.

  • Where Via Marina di Corricella, Procida
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Santuario di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

On the site of a chapel of 1521, the present church was built in 1679 at the behest of the archbishop Innico Caracciolo and the large dome was added in the first half of the twentieth century; baroque Greek cross, with eighteenth-century stuccoes. On the high altar the painting of Our Lady of Graces, enriched with gold and silver by the inhabitants in 1854 for the escape of danger of the plague; appears in the film Il Postino by Massimo Troisi.

  • Where Piazza dei Martiri 1, Procida
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo del mare di Procida

Museums · Themed

The museum has two sections: a bibliographical, with a historical archive on the island's maritime tradition, and a dedicated to the ex-vote and religious tradition linked to the sea. Open Monday to Saturday 9-13, Sunday closed, without reservation.

  • Where Via della Libertà, Procida
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

On the western side of the island, it is called Spiaggia del Postino because it was one of the sets of the movie Il Postino (1994) by Massimo Troisi.
