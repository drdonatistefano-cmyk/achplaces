What to see in Bisceglie

City on the sea north of Bari, called dolmen: the ancient center with the Romanesque cathedral, the church of Sant'Adoeno of 1074 and the Swabian castle, the marina and in the countryside the Dolmen of Chianca of the Bronze Age.

8 places to visit in Bisceglie, in the province of Barletta-Andria-Trani (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Dolmen della Chianca

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

A corridor tomb between the seventeenth and fifteenth centuries BC, the best preserved of the Murgian territory: a cell of about 2 for 1,6 meters, 1.80 meters high, closed by three plates and covered by a 2.40 for 3.80 meters, with a 10 meters long access corridor facing east; identified in 1909 by Angelo Mosso and Francesco Samarelli.

  • Where Bisceglie
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Cattedrale di Bisceglie

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Romanesque, from the XI-XIII centuries, restored after an earthquake of the eighteenth century: inside three naves with semi-colons, truss cover, matroneo with triphor and carved wooden choir; the façade unites Romanesque portal and baroque window.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Bisceglie
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it
