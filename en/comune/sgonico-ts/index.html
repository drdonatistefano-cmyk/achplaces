What to see in Sgonico

Municipality of the Trieste Karst, about 18 km from Trieste, known for the Giant Grotto, discovered in 1840 and opened to the public since 1908, whose Grande Sala is indicated by Touring as the largest cavity in the world open to tourists; between Sgonico and Gabrovizza, in a dolina, the Carsiana Botanical Garden collects the spontaneous flora of the Karst.

3 places to visit in Sgonico, in the province of Trieste (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Grotta Gigante

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry

Discovered in 1840 by Antonio Federico Lindner, it was opened to the public in 1908 with stairs, walkways and candlelight and then acetylene; the electric lighting came in 1957. The Great Hall measures 389 metres in length, 65 metres in width and 107 metres in height, with the Ruggero Column 12 metres high, the Hall of the Altar and, towards the exit, the skeleton of a cave bear; a seismographic station with large pendulums measures the effects of the tides and the underground floods. Guided tour of about an hour with 500 steps downhill and 500 steps uphill, not accessible to people with motor disabilities; temperature of about 11 °C all year round; closed on Mondays.

  • Where Borgo Grotta Gigante 42/A, Sgonico
  • Entry €13, reduced €10
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.grottagigante.it

Sources: grottagigante.it · cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it

Museo speleologico della Grotta Gigante

Museums · Science and university · Free entry

At the entrance of the cave, it traces the history of the cavity from the Neolithic, when the first visitors reached the most accessible part of it, at the nineteenth century of the birth of scientific and sports caving and of the first researches on the underground Timavo. Accessible and free; just outside the cave, in a short gorge, a small botanical garden with plants typical of the entrances of the karst chasms.

  • Where Borgo Grotta Gigante 42/A, Sgonico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: grottagigante.it · cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it

Giardino botanico Carsiana

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Founded in 1964 by pharmacist Gianfranco Gioitti with Livio Poldini and Stanislao Budin and opened to the public since 1978, it occupies a karst dolina of 5,000 square meters where 15 environments of the Karst are rebuilt to collect and illustrate the spontaneous flora; there were recorded 77 species of butterflies. Managed by the Region through the Rogos Cooperative.

  • Where Sgonico 55, strada provinciale Sgonico-Gabrovizza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: regione.fvg.it · cultura.gov.it
