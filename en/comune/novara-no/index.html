What to see in Novara

Between Sesia and Ticino, between plains and rice fields, the city of the dome of San Gaudenzio di Alessandro Antonelli, with the early Christian baptistery, the Broletto and the Visconteo-Sforzesco Castle.

21 places to visit in Novara, in the province of Novara (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Basilica e Cupola di San Gaudenzio

Places of worship · Basilicas · Viewpoint

The 121-metre-high brick dome of Alessandro Antonelli, among the most daring brick buildings in the world, climbs with a guided tour up to 100 meters, and up to the Hall of the Compasso also in a wheelchair.

  • Where Via San Gaudenzio, Novara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitpiemonte.com

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Rebuilt in neoclassical style on the project of the Antonelli, it incorporates parts of the Romanesque cathedral; the central portal is 19 meters high and inside there is the Mystical Bride of Santa Caterina di Gaudenzio Ferrari.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica, Novara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitpiemonte.com

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

From the fourth-fifth century, in the quadriportico of the cathedral, among the major early Christian buildings of northern Italy, with frescoes of the Apocalypse of the late tenth century and a Last Judgment of the fifteenth century.
