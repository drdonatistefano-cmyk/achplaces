What to see in Châtillon

Ancient village at the entrance of the Valtournenche, rich in castles and towers, with historical parks open to the public.

5 places to visit in Châtillon, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 2 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Castello Gamba – Museo d'arte moderna e contemporanea

Museums · Modern art · Viewpoint

Built between 1901 and 1905 for Baron Carlo Maurizio Gamba on a project by Carlo Saroldi, it houses the regional collections of painting, sculpture and graphics collected since 1948; park with monumental trees.

  • Where Châtillon
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Castello di Ussel

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

14th-century castle built by Ebalo di Challant, above the village; it can be visited only during the exhibitions.

  • Where Châtillon
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it
