What to see in Monreale

Borgo over the Golden Conca with the Norman cathedral with gold mosaics, part of the Arab-Norman UNESCO itinerary.

3 places to visit in Monreale, in the province of Palermo (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale di Monreale

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Started in 1174 by William II, with over 6,000 m2 of gold mosaics, the largest mosaic in Italy, second only to Saint Sofia, and the Christ Pantocrator in the apse.

  • Where Piazza Guglielmo II 1, Monreale
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiostro della Cattedrale

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The cloister and the cathedral museum, with columns and capitals richly decorated.

  • Where Monreale
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets

Sources: italia.it

Belvedere sulla Conca d'Oro

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the cathedral a spectacular view of the plain of Palermo.

  • Where Monreale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
