What to see in Castles

The village of the majolica at the foot of the Gran Sasso: the church of San Donato with the majolica ceiling that Carlo Levi called the Sistine of the majolica, the houses of the potters and the Museum of ceramics.

8 places to visit in Castles, in the province of Teramo (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di San Donato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Built on a cliff near the center at the beginning of the 17th century, it has a majolica ceiling painted between 1615 and 1617, made of 780 votive bricks with profiles, coats of arms, astronomical symbols, animals and flowers; In 1963, Carlo Levi called it the Sistine Chapel of Italian majolica.

Museo delle ceramiche di Castelli

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the Franciscan monastery enlarged at the end of the seventeenth century, with the cloister frescoed in 1712 with stories of the Virgin; it tells the tradition of the capital of the ceramics of Abruzzo.

  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

At the end of the sixteenth century, with monumental staircase in white stone: inside the chapel of the Holy Cross, a palace of 29 tiles in majolica of 1617 and a altarpiece framed by tiles of 1647.
