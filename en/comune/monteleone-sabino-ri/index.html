What to see in Monteleone Sabino

Sabina village about 60 km from Rome built on the remains of Trebula Mutuesca, pre-Roman center of the cults of Feronia, Angitia and Vacuna and then Roman town hall: the amphitheatre rebuilt by Traiano and the findings of the Civic Archaeological Museum, next to the Romanesque sanctuary of Santa Vittoria with its catacombs.

Area archeologica di Trebula Mutuesca e Anfiteatro

History and architecture · Archaeology

Pre-Roman settlement mentioned by Virgilio for its olive groves, entered the Roman orbit in 290 BC and became municipium in Augustian age, with hole, thermal baths and cisterns; in 146 BC. Lucio Mummio sent three votive dedications. The elliptical amphitheatre (94 x 66 m), partly dug into the rock, was rebuilt at the behest of Trajan as attested by two inscriptions of 115 A.D., and under the arena runs a tunnel turned once connected to the hole.

Museo civico archeologico Trebula Mutuesca

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 1995, he documents the site from the pre-Roman age to the Middle Ages: at the entrance a funeral lion of the 1st century BC, then ex-vote and miniaturistic offerings of the cults of Angitia and Feronia (IV-III century BC) from the sacred area near Santa Vittoria, reliefs with gladiator games, a room on the amphitheatre with fragments of bronze and marble statues and at the center the monumental epigraph of Traiano, an epigraphic section and late antique and medieval finds.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museomonteleonesabino.it

Santuario di Santa Vittoria e catacombe

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

It rises on the Colle Foro, inside the ancient Trebula, where the young Victoria was martyred in the middle of the III century; documented by 817 as the possession of Farfa, it was rebuilt between the XI and XII century and restored by the Orsini in the fifteenth century. Three aisles, bell tower with double windows, marble façade and spolia, a devotional well in the central nave and a ciborium on marble columns; from the right nave you descend to the catacombs with graves dug in the rock, a strigatory sarcophagus and a frescoed archesolio with the martyr.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village rises on the remains of Trebula Mutuesca and in the walls of the houses emerge Roman fragments like capitals and inscriptions; passed by various feudal families, at the beginning of the seventeenth century went to the Apostolic Chamber. The village is located on the fourth stage of the Way of Saint Francis, from Poggio San Lorenzo to Ponticelli di Scandriglia.

Olio Sabina DOP e fettuccine alla trebulana

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The festival of bruschetta celebrates bread with extra virgin olive oil Sabina DOP; the fettuccine alla trebulana have their feast in the first week of August; other specialties are pancakes and aniseed doughnuts.
