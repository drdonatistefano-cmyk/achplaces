What to see in Vernio

Ancient Bardi feud in the upper Val di Bisenzio, with the Rocca and Casone di San Quirico, seat of the Municipality with the Oratory of San Niccolò; in Montepiano the Romanesque Badia of Santa Maria and the Prato del Sole of Bruno Saetti, in Mercatale the MUMAT textile machinery museum.

Badia di Santa Maria a Montepiano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

The church and monastery complex was founded in the 11th century and entrusted to the Vallombrosans, with buildings of the 12th century; the monastery was abandoned in the 16th century and partly demolished in the 19th century after an earthquake. The church, a small Romanesque jewel in sandstone sketches with arched frieze, has a portal with cross architraves between snakes, lions and dragons and a bezel with a figure with raised arms; in the aisle only frescoes from the XIII to the XVIII century, including a Saint Michael weighing souls. Visits by appointment.

  • Where Via della Badia, Montepiano, Vernio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: pratoturismo.it · italia.it

Prato del Sole e Casa del Mulino

Nature and landscape · City parks · Viewpoint

A large public lawn in Montepiano with the large red enameled metal sculpture in the shape of a sun donated by Bruno Saetti, who chose Montepiano as a place of life and work; next to the geometric garden and the House of the Mill, his house-atelier, today home of an art school.

  • Where Via del Bisenzio 373, Montepiano, Vernio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · comune.vernio.po.it · italia.it

MUMAT - Museo delle macchine tessili

Museums · Themed

In the former Meucci factory, founded in 1813 as a calificio and flanked in 1897 by a charcoal, active until 1988 and then purchased and restored by the municipality, it collects machines and textile tools from the end of the nineteenth century to the mid-nineteenth century, including a turbine still working and a rare wooden frame restored; it tells the industrial past of the Val di Bisenzio and the recycling of the typical rags of Prato. It is part of the Prato Museum Network; guided tours and workshops for schools.

  • Where Via del Bisenzio 377, Mercatale, Vernio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Casone dei Bardi e Oratorio di San Niccolò

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The eighteenth-century Casone, formerly the residence of the Bardi Counts who moved the seat of power in the seventeenth century, is today the town hall; the adjoining oratory, of the early eighteenth century, has paintings of the eighteenth century, one of the last Traeri organs still working and a permanent exhibition on the Gothic Line.

  • Where San Quirico, Vernio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com · comune.vernio.po.it

Rocca di Vernio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortification of medieval origin from the time of the Counts Cadolingi, then of the Alberti until 1332, when the Florentine bankers Bardi bought it; the defenses were destroyed in the battles of the XIV and XV century. The ruins, the well-preserved medieval village and the chapel of Sant'Agata remain; the next building is a private residence, recently restored. From San Quirico you can go up the medieval path near Casone or by car before Sasseta.

private property, visits to be agreed with the owners

Pieve dei Santi Ippolito e Cassiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church, the most important church of the feud of Vernio, documented since 998 and in the current forms probably of the XII century, with medieval facade and porch on columns of the XVI-XVII century. In the single aisle baroque altars in sandstone, a Deposition of 1579 by Giovanni Bizzelli with the coat of arms of Cardinal Girolamo de' Bardi and a Madonna of the seventeenth-century Rosary; in canonical a Adoration of the Shepherds of Girolamo Ristori.

  • Where Via Sant'Ippolito, Vernio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · comune.vernio.po.it · italia.it

Chiesa dei Santi Leonardo e Quirico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of thirteenth-century origin several times renovated, with bell tower in low and thick stone and facade of the twenties of the twentieth century; in the single nave with neo-Gothic trusses a Madonna del Rosario with Saints Gregorio Pagani (1597) and a relief in majolica of Robbiano style with the Madonna, the Child and St. John.

  • Where Piazza San Leonardo 15, San Quirico, Vernio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Parco Memoriale della Linea Gotica

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

On a relief between the valleys of Bisenzio and Sieve, with the remains of a German settlement: the height, for the Allies Hill 810, was disputed for days between the 334th German Division and the 34th US Infantry Division.

  • Where Vernio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.vernio.po.it · italia.it

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Protected natural area of local interest on an Apennine ridge, with mountain landscape shaped by water and wind, destination of hikers.

Via della Lana e della Seta

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The path between Bologna and Prato crosses Vernio in two of its six stages: along the path wild horses, karst caves and streams like the Nosa and Rio Buti; from the Altomedieval settlement of Cavarzano, linked to transhumance, you walk up to Montepiano.

  • Where Montepiano, Vernio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it
