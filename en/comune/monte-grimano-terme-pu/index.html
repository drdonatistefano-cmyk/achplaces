What to see in Monte Grimano Terme

Borgo del Montefeltro among the most beautiful villages in Italy, 600 meters on a spur above the valley of the Conca, once disputed between the Montefeltro of Urbino and the Malatesta of Rimini: historic center with spiral with the fifteenth-century civic tower, Palazzo Massaioli and the neoclassical San Silvestro; the thermal baths fed by the waters of Mount San Paolo are now temporarily closed.

Centro storico a chiocciola

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval village, an ancient settlement in the battles between Montefeltro and Malatesta, has a spiral system with narrow roads that go up the hill, about 300 meters above the valley of the Conca. Two gates mark the ancient gates of the castle, disappeared with the drawbridge; the walls were incorporated into the houses in the seventeenth century, while the current wall dates back to the early twentieth century. At the entrance the seventeenth-century Palazzo Massaioli, seat of the Town Hall; Piazza Garibaldi, for its acoustics, hosts concerts and events.

Torre civica

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

It is 18 meters high and dates back to the first half of the XV century, it is what remains of the medieval palace of the castle and is the symbol of the village; it houses three bells dated 1613, 1797 and 1811.

  • Where Centro storico, Monte Grimano Terme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Silvestro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built at the end of the eighteenth century on a project by Cosimo Morelli of Imola, in neoclassical style then renovated; it preserves eight large paintings between the seventeenth and nineteenth centuries, a 16th century Crucifix and an eighteenth-century organ of little less than 500 reeds, inventoried as a cultural heritage of the Marche Region.

Stazione idroterapica di Monte Grimano

Local life and traditions · Spas

The alkaline, sulphurous and sauce-bromo-iodic waters flowing from the slopes of nearby Mount San Paolo feed the hydrotherapy station, with herbal and mud treatments and a wellness centre for medical and aesthetic treatments.

Boschi e sentieri della valle del Conca

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Around the village gardens, woods and paths on the slopes of the valley of the Conca, where you can meet squirrels, hares, deer and hawks.
