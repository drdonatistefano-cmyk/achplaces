What to see in Ortacesus

Farmland of Trexenta north of the Flumini Mannu, dedicated to the cultivation of wheat: the Museum of the wheat in the ancient house Serra, together with the Gothic-Catalan parish church of St. Peter and the Romanesque country church of St. Bartholomew.

Museo del grano

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the ancient courthouse of the Serra family, on the main road to Guasila: objects on the cultivation and use of wheat, many donated by the inhabitants, with rooms divided between the male work in the fields and the female work of the bakery and weaving; stands out a rare wooden wheel instead of stone. A room features the film of the early 1960s by Danish scholar Weis Bentzon on sowing, working and collecting, and a section recalls the launeddas player Dionigi Burranca, who died in Ortacesus in 1995. Workshops and the route "The way of bread" with tasting; open all year round, from Tuesday to Sunday 9-13 and 16-19, free for over 65 and under 5.

  • Where Casa Serra, Ortacesus
  • Entry €2, reduced €1,50 (groups over 10) and €1 (schools)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Pietro Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between the end of the sixteenth century and the beginning of the seventeenth century in Gothic-Catalan style, it preserves a precious wooden altar and statues of the Madonna del Rosario and various saints of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries.

  • Where Centro del paese, Ortacesus
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A church dating back to the 12th century about a kilometre from the village, built on an ancient Roman pagan temple; according to tradition it was the church of the original village, before the inhabitants moved to the present site.

  • Where A circa 1 km dal paese, Ortacesus
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
