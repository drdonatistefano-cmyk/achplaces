What to see in Anzio

Città di mare sulla Riviera di Roma, patria di Nerone e teatro delle disembarkazione alleato del 22 gennaio 1944; in the seventeenth-century Villa Adele the Civic Archaeological Museum and the Museum of Landing, on the coast the remains of the Imperial Villa and, towards Lavinio, the Natural Reserve of Tor Caldara.

Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 2002 on the ground floor of the seventeenth-century Villa Adele (Villa Pamphilj), in nine rooms it tells the ancient Antium from its origins to the imperial age: protohistoric funerary furnishings of the IX-VIII century BC, votive deposits of the Republican age, underwater finds, mosaics and frescoes of the Imperial Villa and a cycle of plaster paintings of the second half of the II century A.D.; on loan from the National Roman Museum the Nero's music niche with Ebbro Hercules. Closed on Mondays; reservation not required.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Museo dello sbarco

Museums · Themed

Opened on 22 January 1994 for the fiftieth anniversary, in a hall of Villa Adele, it recalls Operation Shingle of 22 January 1944, when the Allies landed between Tor San Lorenzo and Torre Astura. Four sections (English, American, German and Italian) with medals, uniforms, flags, weapons, battle plans, motor vehicles and vintage photos, more photo library, tape library, library and hemeroteca; some finds come from the backdrops of Anzio, the wrecks of British ships Janus and Spartan and the hospital ship St. David. Closed on Monday, Wednesday only morning.

  • Where Via di Villa Adele 2, Anzio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.sbarcodianzio.it

Sources: sbarcodianzio.it · visitlazio.com · comune.anzio.roma.it

Villa di Nerone - Parco archeologico

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The imperial residence extends on the coast between the root of Cape of Anzio and the promontory of the Muto Arch, with phases from the II century BC to the III century AD: late-Republican aristocratic domus, then a long curved portico of Augustian age and, at the end of the II century A.D., a building with double portico on the sea over 700 meters long, a spa complex on four levels and the so-called library. The excavations of 1889 and 1929-1933 restored figurative mosaics, including Hercules and Acheloo; Nero was born in Anzio on December 15, 37 A.D.

Vallo Volsco

History and architecture · Archaeology

The first settlement of Anzio was built on the hill of Santa Teresa in the 8th century BC; here are the first fortifications of the city, called Vallo Volsco: a sandstone wall regularized to the west, stretches of walls in sandstone blocks to the north and deep defensive cuts to the east. The park, managed by the Santa Teresa Committee in agreement with the Municipality, was inaugurated on 9 August 2017.

temporarily closed to the public (Comune di Anzio, updated on October 2, 2025)

Riserva naturale di Tor Caldara

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 44 hectares of coast between Anzio and Lavinio, established regional reserve in 1988, with fossil dunes frequented by the grunts and Mediterranean maquis, rare rest of the ancient coastal forests of southern Lazio. The 16th century circular tower, built with materials of Roman buildings and arranged around 1565 by Marc'Antonio Colonna, defended from pirates; from the 16th century a sulphuratara was active, and on the promontory were emerged in 1999 remains of a Roman maritime villa of the I-III century AD. You can walk the Path of the Tower.

  • Where Via Ardeatina km 34,600, Anzio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com

Porto di Anzio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The port dates back to the Roman age; today, among colorful buildings overlooking the sea, it is a marina and sports with winter regattas, restaurants and departures for the Pontine Islands. It is one of the favorite places for sunset.
