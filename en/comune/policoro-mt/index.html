What to see in Policoro

On the Ionian coast, where the Greek colonies of Siris and Herakleia arose: the National Museum of Siritide with the archaeological park of the acropolis and the shrines, the castle on the hill of Heraclea, the reserve of the Pantano Wood and the beaches of Lido di Policoro.

Museo archeologico nazionale della Siritide

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 1969 next to the archaeological park, it exhibits the finds of the Greek cities of Siris and Herakleia and of the indigenous centers of the interior: votive statues and accessories of Siris with some of the oldest figurative ceramics of Magna Grecia (VII-VI century B.C.), matrixes, votive objects and accessories of Herakleia with red-figure ceramics and gold jewels in filigree, bronze armour, silver jewels, gold and amber. Closed on Tuesdays.

  • Where Via Cristoforo Colombo 8, Policoro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · musei.basilicata.beniculturali.it

Parco archeologico di Herakleia

History and architecture · Archaeology

On the site of Siris the settlers of Taranto founded Herakleia in 434-433 BC: in the park remain the acropolis with the grid of the streets, houses with environments around courtyards, late Hellenistic peristyle houses with mosaic floors, the artisans district with furnaces, the sanctuary of Dionysus with the foundations of the temple and agora with the altar and the sanctuary of Demeter on two terraces.

  • Where Via Cristoforo Colombo 13, Policoro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello baronale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified building on the eastern tip of the ancient hill of Eraclea, in the Old Borgata: it testifies to the feudal past of Policoro, with under the "casalini" the small houses of the farm workers. You can see it from the outside.

  • Where Borgata Vecchia, Policoro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: policoro.basilicata.it · italia.it

Riserva Bosco Pantano e Oasi WWF Policoro-Herakleia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Regional reserve of about 1,200 hectares, Natura 2000 area, with one of the last slices of coastal forest flooded the peninsula: riparian forest, maquis, wetlands and dunes near the sea, over 170 species of birds, otter and nests of sea turtles. The WWF oasis (21 hectares) has a fauna recovery center; guided tours by reservation.

  • Where Piazza Siris 1, località Idrovora, Policoro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: wwf.it · basilicataturistica.it · italia.it

Lido di Policoro

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 3 km from the city, beyond the pine forest of the reserve: about 3 km of sandy beaches, the fishing village, the marina and the bay where dolphins and sea turtles are often seen.

  • Where Marina di Policoro, Policoro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it · italia.it
