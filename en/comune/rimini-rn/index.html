What to see in Rimini

Two thousand years in a few steps: the Arch of Augustus, the Bridge of Tiberius, the Domus of the Surgeon, the Temple Malatestano, Castel Sismondo and the Fellini Museum, then the sea.

28 places to visit in Rimini, in the province of Rimini (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 10 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Arco d'Augusto

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

The honorary arch erected in 27 BC at the end of the Via Flaminia, the oldest preserved Roman arch.

  • Where Corso d'Augusto, Rimini
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: riviera.rimini.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Ponte di Tiberio

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The stone bridge of Istria started by Augustus and ended by Tiberius (14-21 AD), still accessible today, with the village San Giuliano on the other bank.

  • Where Ponte di Tiberio, Rimini
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: riviera.rimini.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Domus del Chirurgo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

The house of a doctor of the third century with mosaics and the richest surgical equipment of antiquity ever found, museumized where it was excavated.

  • Where Piazza Ferrari, Rimini
  • Entry €7 full, €5 reduced (one ticket with the City Museum); Art Card with the Fellini Museum €14, reduced €10
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museicomunalirimini.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: museicomunalirimini.it · riviera.rimini.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
