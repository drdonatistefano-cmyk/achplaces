What to see in Rubiana

The mountain town of the lower Val di Susa, mentioned in 1029 as a flourishing parish church in a document of the Abbey of San Giusto di Susa, with a hundred villages; at its border the Colle del Lys, with the natural park and the Ecomuseum of the Resistance.

2 places to visit in Rubiana, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ecomuseo della Resistenza del Colle del Lys

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

On the watershed between the Lanzo Valleys and the Susa Valley, it tells the history of the Resistance and the events that took place in this territory with films shot with witnesses, CD-ROMs, touring exhibitions and brochures on the routes; the territory of the Ecomuseum has returned to live through rediscovered paths and offers wide views.

  • Where Piazzale Colle del Lys, Rubiana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Parco naturale del Colle del Lys

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Protected area between the municipalities of Rubiana and Viù, managed by the Metropolitan City of Turin. The hill marks the border of the territory of Rubiana: the provincial road that goes up from Almese crosses the village and connects the Valley of Susa with the Lanzo Valleys.

  • Where Colle del Lys, Rubiana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitpiemonte.com

Sources: visitpiemonte.com · comune.rubiana.to.it
