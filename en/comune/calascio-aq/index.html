What to see in Calascio

Borgo della Baronia di Carapelle in the National Park of Gran Sasso and Monti della Laga, dominated by the Rocca Calascio, at about 1,460-1,500 meters the highest fortification of Abruzzo, set of "Ladyhawke" and "The name of the rose" with next to the octagonal church of Santa Maria della Pietà; in the village the Franciscan complex of Santa Maria delle Grazie.

Rocca Calascio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Born around the Mille as a simple watchtower, fortified and enlarged in the fifteenth century by the Piccolomini, destroyed by the earthquake of 1703 and today consolidated: square plan in white stone with four corner towers and entrance about 5 meters from the ground, reached perhaps with a retractable wooden staircase. Chiesa di Santa Maria della Pietà

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Small church under the Fortress, built in the sixteenth century instead of a votive aedicule: octagonal plan covered by an eight-sliced dome, with the sacristy leaning on one of the facades.

  • Where Rocca Calascio, Calascio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Chiesa e convento di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Franciscan complex founded in 1500 next to the church of Santa Maria delle Grazie, which preserves carved wooden altars, a 17th century ciborium and a candelabra.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
