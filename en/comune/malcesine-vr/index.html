What to see in Malcesine

Medieval village on the Verona shore of Garda dominated by the Scaligero Castle, National Monument from 1902 and Goethe stage in the ...Travel to Italy. From the village the cable car with rotating cabins rises to Monte Baldo, while to the south, in Cassone, flows the Aril, just 175 meters long.

Castello Scaligero

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Rocca probably Longobard, destroyed by the Franks in 590 and rebuilt, took its name from the Scaligeri who held it from 1277 to 1387; then passed to Visconti, Venice and Austria and was declared National Monument on August 22, 1902. The pentagonal mast is 31 meters high (70 meters on the lake) and the viewpoint is 24 meters on the water; it houses the Museum of Natural History of Baldo and Garda (multimedia route), the Hall of Galee on the navigation and transport of Venetian ships by land in the fifteenth century, and the Goethe Hall in the former Austrian powder keg, with bust of the poet in the garden. In summer the lawn of the Lacaòr serves as an outdoor theatre.

Palazzo dei Capitani

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built by the Scaligeri between XIII and XIV century on Roman and Romanesque remains and then rebuilt in Venetian style; in 1618 Verona bought it as a residence of the Captain of the Lake and is National Monument since 1902. Today it hosts exhibitions, concerts and the library: ground floor and garden are open with free entrance, the first floor can be visited at events.

  • Where Via Capitanato, Malcesine
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · tourism.verona.it · italia.it

Centro storico e porticciolo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The marina, animated by bars and restaurants, is the heart of the medieval village; there is moored the Siora Veronica, sailboat of 1926 with which you can still sail on Garda. In the squares and in the castle theater folk and cultural events are held in summer.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: tourism.verona.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Founded in the 8th century; in the 9th century Bishop Rotaldo had the bodies of the saints Benigno and Caro laid there. He holds a Deposition by Girolamo Dai Libri and an eighteenth-century medallion on the ceiling; feast of the patron on 26 December.

  • Where Malcesine
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Funivia Malcesine-Monte Baldo

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Two trunks, Malcesine-San Michele and San Michele-Monte Baldo; the cabins of the second rotate on themselves offering a 360° view of the lake, the Alps and the plain, and are wheelchair accessible. At altitude hiking and Nordic walking trails, downhill MTB trails and a paragliding takeoff; Baldo hosts almost half of the flora of the Alps.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · tourism.verona.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Cassone, fiume Aril e Museo del Lago

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

Small village a few kilometers south with a fishing boat port and the typical bisse; there flows the Aril, referred to as the shortest river in the world (175 meters), which passes under three bridges, forms a waterfall and flows into the lake. On the port the Museum of the Lake exhibits fishing gear, objects of use and photos of ancient sailing boats.

  • Where Cassone, Malcesine
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: tourism.verona.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Navene is the last village before the old Italian-Austrian border; from here starts the path to Bocca Navene, a panoramic terrace overlooking the lake.
