What to see in Issogne

The village of one of the most elegant castles in the Valle d'Aosta, the Renaissance residence of Giorgio di Challant with the fountain of pomegranate.

4 places to visit in Issogne, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Issogne

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Rebuilt by Ibleto di Challant at the end of the fourteenth century and transformed a century later by Prior Giorgio di Challant into a luxurious residence between the late Gothic and early Renaissance; in the courtyard the fountain with the wrought iron pomegranate. At the end of the nineteenth century Vittorio Avondo restored decorations and furnishings.
