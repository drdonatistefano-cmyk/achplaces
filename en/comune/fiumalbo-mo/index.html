Borgo di Fiumalbo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval stone village at the foot of the Cimone, with Trebbo square, the carved "marcolfe" and the Celtic huts of Doccia in the surroundings.

  • Where Fiumalbo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Oratorio di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

At the entrance to the historic centre, the Renaissance oratory with frescoes painted in 1535 by Saccino Saccaccini da Carpi.

  • Where Fiumalbo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Chiesa di San Bartolomeo Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church was built in 1220 and rebuilt in 1592, with the Renaissance façade in stone, Romanesque sculptures and the cross of Fiumalbo in 1494.

  • Where Centro storico, Fiumalbo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it
