What to see in Naturno

The village of the lower Val Venosta among the orchards, where the small church of San Procolo houses frescoes among the oldest of the pre-Romanesque mural art in the Alps, told by the underground museum in front of it; in the hamlet of Compaccio the house of the Gruppo di Tessa Natural Park is dedicated to water. Above the village Castel Hochnaturns and, towards Plaus, Castel Dornsberg, both private.

Chiesetta di San Procolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small church among the orchards of the valley floor, at the eastern entrance of Naturno, probably built at the end of the eighth century in honor of San Procolo, bishop of Verona. Inside a cycle of frescoes discovered in 1923, with angels, saints, the famous figure ... on the swing and a herd of oxen, among the oldest examples of pre-Romanesque mural painting of the Alpine arch; author and meaning of some images remain unknown and scholars propose dates between the 8th and 10th centuries, with the decorations of the triumphal arch more ancient than those of the nave.

Museo San Procolo

Museums · Archaeological

In front of the church, built entirely under the ground: from the outside you can see only the entrance door. The route crosses four epochs, late antiquity, high Middle Ages, low Middle Ages and Thirty Years War with a devastating outbreak of typhus, and exhibits detached Gothic frescoes and numerous finds found in the church.

closed for the winter break from 1 November 2026 to 15 March 2027, reopens on 16 March 2027

Centro visite del Parco naturale Gruppo di Tessa

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Snail-shaped building with large windows and terrace towards Mount Sole di Naturno, with a permanent exhibition of 600 m2 on water ('Verso l'acqua') that rises from the Adige to the glaciers through the altitude bands of the park: interactive stations on habitats from the Mediterranean to the Alps, roges, alpine pastures and farms, a game on the rocks and minerals of the park and an entrance 'in the heart of a landslide. Next to it, a small park with water tank dedicated to the naturalist and butterfly collector Bernardin Astfäller, Naturno native; in 2026 opened from March to the end of November, 9:30-12:30 and 14:30-18:00.

  • Where Piazza Bernardin Astfäller 1, Compaccio, Naturno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it
