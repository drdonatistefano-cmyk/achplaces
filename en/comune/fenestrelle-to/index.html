What to see in Fenestrelle

High Val Chisone country linked to the largest fortified structure in Europe, with a covered staircase of about 4000 steps.

4 places to visit in Fenestrelle, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Forte di Fenestrelle

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The largest fortified structure in Europe: three strong, three small and two batteries connected by a covered staircase of about 4000 steps in a gallery of almost two kilometers; inside two museums.

  • Where Via del Forte 1, Fenestrelle
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: old.turismotorino.org · turismotorino.org · cr.piemonte.it

Scala coperta

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

About 4000 steps excavated in an artificial gallery of almost two kilometers that connects the complex.

  • Where Forte di Fenestrelle
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org · cr.piemonte.it

Forte San Carlo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the piazza d'armi overlook the governor's palace, the pavilion of the officers and the church; inside three military quarters, the powder keg of St. Ignatius and a royal door. This is where the covered staircase starts.

  • Where Forte di Fenestrelle
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org
