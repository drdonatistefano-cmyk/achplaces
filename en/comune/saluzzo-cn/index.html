What to see in Saluzzo

Ancient capital of the Marchesato at the foot of the Monviso, with the medieval village of cobbled streets dominated by Castile and the masterpieces of the Saluzzo Renaissance.

8 places to visit in Saluzzo, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Saluzzo in the guide with map and filters

La Castiglia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The ancient castle of the Marquis that dominates the city; it houses the Museum of Civilization Cavalleresca and the Museum of Prison Memory, with two multimedia installations.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Saluzzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · visit.terresmonviso.eu

Casa Cavassa – Museo civico

Museums · Old masters

High example of the Saluzzo Renaissance: it preserves the frescoes in grisaille with the feats of Hercules by the Flemish Hans Clemer and the Madonna della Misericordia.

  • Where Via San Giovanni 5, Saluzzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · visit.terresmonviso.eu

Duomo di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Just outside the historic centre, symbol of the power of the Marchesate, it houses a masterpiece by the Flemish painter Hans Clemer.

  • Where Saluzzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visit.terresmonviso.eu · visitcuneese.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

One of the stages of the historic center reported by the tourist consortium of Monviso.

  • Where Via San Giovanni, Saluzzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visit.terresmonviso.eu · visitcuneese.it

Casa Pellico

Museums · House museums

Birthplace of Silvio Pellico, writer, patriot and playwright born in Saluzzo in 1789, today museum.

  • Where Piazzetta dei Mondagli, Saluzzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · visit.terresmonviso.eu

Antico Palazzo Comunale e Torre Civica

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Built between 1440 and 1442 and town hall until 1831, it houses the Pinacoteca Matteo Olivero.

  • Where Via San Giovanni, Saluzzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visit.terresmonviso.eu · visitcuneese.it

Borgo medievale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval center with cobbled streets, noble villas with painted facades and the commercial pedestrian island.

  • Where Saluzzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
