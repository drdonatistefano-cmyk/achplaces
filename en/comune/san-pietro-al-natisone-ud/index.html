What to see in San Pietro al Natisone

The capital of the Natisone Valleys for the population, where in the SMO museum the cultural landscape of the Slovenian provinces of Udine, Gorizia and Trieste is told in a multimedia way; in the hamlet of Biarzo a cavity along the river has returned the oldest human traces of the area, of the upper Paleolithic and of the Neolithic; in the municipal territory there are the latedogotic churches of San Quirino, San Bartolomeo and Sant'Antonio Abate and the castellieri of Roba and Barda.

SMO, Museo di paesaggi e narrazioni

Museums · Themed

Museum of narration and not collections, multimedia and interactive, dedicated to the cultural landscape from the Julian Alps to the sea and to the history and currentity of the area in which they historically live the Slovenian provinces of Udine, Gorizia and Trieste, with interactive paths and multimedia frescoes; it is also center of documentation, archive and research. Admission fee according to the Touring; open daily, 10-13 and 14-16, with times that can change.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Chiesa di San Quirino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Late-Gothic church of Škofja Loka school: on the apse an inscription shows the date 1493 and the name of the builder, Martin Petric. Under its lindens were gathered the assemblies of the valleys, including the Great Arengo of Schiavonia, convened for the last time in 1804; in the surroundings emerged a necropolis of the Iron Age and Roman and Longobard finds.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church in Goregnis di Vernasso with polygonal Gothic apse at the time ribbed. The golden altar, of 1689, is the work of the engraver Bartolomeo Ortari of Caporetto; the frescoes of the Apostles in the apse are attributed to Bartolomeo da Loka (1530-1535).

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church in the hamlet of Clenia with late-Gothic apse that houses a golden altar of 1601-1602, then modified by Bartolomeo Ortari; on the walls of the aisle there is a cycle of frescoes from the fifteenth century.

Riparo di Biarzo

History and architecture · Archaeology

I shelter under rock along the left bank of Natisone, near a mill of the seventeenth century, inhabited for over 5,000 years between the upper Paleolithic and the Neolithic: the oldest finds are about 10,000 years old. The Regional Speleological Catasto records it as a small cavity of 17 meters, excavated in 1978, which returned junk worked. You will arrive for a cart from Biarzo or for a path shaded by Ponteacco.

  • Where Biarzo, San Pietro al Natisone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: catastogrotte.regione.fvg.it · italia.it

Castellieri di Roba e Barda

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Fortified heights perhaps part of a defensive line of the late bronze age; the top of Mount Barda was frequented by the Romans between the II and the I century BC, and Roman coins and other finds are preserved at the National Archaeological Museum of Cividale. A nature trail climbs from the Klanac saddle to the Roba (301 m) and Barda (260 m).

  • Where Sella di Klanac, San Pietro al Natisone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
