What to see in Palau

Born in the late 19th century, a port of departure for La Maddalena: the Rock of the Bear, a natural monument and symbol of the village, the Fortress of Monte Altura of 1887-1889, the Ethnographic Museum and a coast of beaches and windy bays such as Porto Pollo and Isuledda.

6 places to visit in Palau, in the province of Sassari (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Roccia dell'Orso

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Granite outcrop of the upper Paleozoic modelled by the wind in the shape of a bear facing La Maddalena, on a rise of more than 120 meters; already Ptolemy indicated it as "Areti Promontorium" and in 1993 the Region declared it a natural monument. Go up with a panoramic path of about half a kilometer (about ten minutes) from the fort of Capo d'Orso, with many steps; from the top the view embraces the archipelago and Corsica. Access for a fee, also with a cumulative ticket with Fortress and Ethnographic Museum (€7).

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.rocciadellorso.com

Fortezza di Monte Altura

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built in two years, from 1887 to 1889, with the granite of the mountain, in the era of the "Great Fortifications," together with the batteries of Capo d'Orso and Baragge; it is considered one of the largest fortresses built in Italy in the nineteenth century. On two levels connected by a staircase, with ramparts on granite ridges, barracks, warehouses, powder keg and a monumental ramp towards the shooting area; since 1990 it has passed to the Superintendence and in summer it hosts concerts. Visit only with guide, from the end of March to the beginning of November.

  • Where SS 133, strada per Porto Rafael, località Monte Altura, Palau
  • Entry €5, reduced 11-18 years €2,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.fortezzamontealtura.com

Sources: fortezzamontealtura.com · sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Museo etnografico

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Two large halls and six sections on the traditional life of Gallura: house (bread and cheese tools, a carved cone of the wedding races), vine and wine with palms and presses, agriculture, breeding, carriages with oxen and crafts of blacksmith, carpenter and shoemaker. Open from the end of March to the beginning of November; in the rest of the year by reservation.

  • Where Via Nazionale 111, località Montiggia, Palau
  • Entry €2.50, reduced 11-18 years €2
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · italia.it

Porto Pollo e Isuledda

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Insect of turquoise water and white sand halfway between Palau and Santa Teresa Gallura, at the mouth of the Liscia, connected by a tongue of sand to the Isle of Gabbiani (Isuledda), natural area of about 18 hectares with dunes of lavender, lentisco and juniper. Very windy, it is a reference for windsurfing, kitesurfing and sailing with international competitions; low backdrop, parking also for campers, accessible to people with disabilities.

  • Where Località Porto Pollo, Palau
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Spiagge di Palau: La Sciumara, Cala Trana, Porto Faro

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Along the coast of the municipality alternate the sandy beach of Sciumara, Cala Trana, a small beach protected by dunes between granite rocks with sand tending to pink, and the coast of Porto Faro; Porto Rafael is a marina surrounded by olive trees, myrtle and lavender.

  • Where Costa di Palau
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa campestre di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small church built in 1675 by a group of hermits on Mount Casteddu, in a sacred area from prehistory; under the floor were found burials and the altar guards bones of the ancient inhabitants. The feast is held on the first Sunday of May; very close is the tomb of giants of Sajacciu.

  • Where Monte Casteddu, campagna di Palau
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
