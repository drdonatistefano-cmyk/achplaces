What to see in Pomponesco

Borgo Gonzaghesco sul Po, collected around the scenic Piazza XXIII Aprile, porticoed and remained substantially unchanged from the seventeenth century; around the church of Santa Felicita, the small Jewish cemetery, the Theatre 900 and, beyond the embankment, the Garzaia Nature Reserve.

Piazza XXIII Aprile e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Piazza Gonzaghesca, symmetrical and theatrical, surrounded by porticoes; the palaces and porticoed houses were built between 1590 and 1630 and the square remained substantially unchanged from the seventeenth century. The hexagonal castle of the prince was destroyed by the French at the end of the eighteenth century, leaving the square empty; the streets follow the sixteenth-century plant wanted by Giulio Cesare Gonzaga, and the square of Montecitorio opens towards embankment, river and docking. They say he inspired Zavattini, Soldiers and Bertolucci.

Chiesa arcipretale di Santa Felicita e dei Sette Fratelli Martiri

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church documented since 1339, with the current structure due to the restorations of the architect Giovan Battista Vergani in 1829 and 1831; the facade, with large arches, is from 1921 in hammered concrete. The interior has three naves, coffered ceilings and transept; its bell tower faces the town hall tower.

  • Where Piazza XXIII Aprile, Pomponesco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Cimitero ebraico

Places of worship · Monumental cemeteries

Small Jewish cemetery linked to the presence of the Cantons, one of the most important Jewish families in the country; there is buried the writer Alberto Cantoni (1841-1904), friend of the painter Gerolamo Trenti (1824-98), among the main Lombard landscapers of the nineteenth century.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Riserva naturale della Garzaia, argine maestro e ciclabile del Po

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Beyond the main embankment lies the Garzaia Nature Reserve; the bike path accompanies along the banks of the Po between poplars and herons flights. Outside the village, barns, ailes and farmsteads mark the countryside.

  • Where Argine del Po, Pomponesco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it
