What to see in Poggibonsi

Valdelsa town dominated by the Medici fortress of Poggio Imperiale, designed by Giuliano da Sangallo for Lorenzo il Magnifico.

3 places to visit in Poggibonsi, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Fortezza di Poggio Imperiale e Parco archeologico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The belt of the Medici fortress of the early sixteenth century, never completed, with the pentagonal castle; the archaeological and technological park tells the hill from the early medieval huts onward.

  • Where Poggio Imperiale, Poggibonsi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Archeodromo

Museums · Archaeological

Outdoor reconstruction of a village of the Carolingian age, where archaeologists and young people in costume animate daily life.

  • Where Poggio Imperiale, Poggibonsi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Basilica di San Lucchese

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

The basilica on the hill of Poggibonsi, signalled by Visit Tuscany among the ancient testimonies of the city.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
