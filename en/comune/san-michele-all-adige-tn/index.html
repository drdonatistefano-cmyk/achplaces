What to see in San Michele all'Adige

Borgo della Piana Rotaliana sull'Adige, along the Via Claudia Augusta, which grew up around the large Augustinian monastery of the mid-12th century with the Baroque church of San Michele Arcangelo; the Edmund Mach Foundation and the METS, one of the most important museums in Trentino, are all located. On the hills above the village there is Faedo, a medieval village among the high-altitude vineyards.

METS - Museo etnografico trentino San Michele

Museums · Themed

In the south wing of the ancient Augustinian monastery, the museum created by the ethnographer Giuseppe Šebesta defines itself as a museum of technique and popular technology.About 12,000 objects in 43 rooms tell how Alpine people faced the scarcity of resources. There are the agricultural hall with the collection of ploughs, the large water machines (mill, sawmill, sweat and noria, in operation until the sixties of the twentieth century; the mill was dismantled piece by piece and reassembled here by Šebesta), the rooms of the peasant house with the stüa and curiosity as the bugs scare bears. Since 2002 the Provincial Archives of oral tradition has been located there; open from Tuesday to Sunday and Monday holidays, 10-18.

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo e convento agostiniano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The convent was founded by the Augustinians in the middle of the 12th century (1145 or 1147 according to the pages of the tourist consortium), also due to the strategic position of the village on the Adige; today it no longer hosts a religious community. The church of San Michele Arcangelo, with a baroque façade visible from all over the country and from those arriving from Trento, South Tyrol or Val di Non, is rich in paintings and decorations; in the north wing is the Edmund Mach Foundation, an agricultural and environmental research centre.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Faedo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Fraction on the hills above San Michele, a few minutes drive away, which preserves the medieval system with many vaulted passages under the houses. Above the village is the castle of Monreale-Königsberg, dating back to the fourteenth century, once the administrative seat of the territory, which is now not open to visitors; in the village the church of Sant'Agata, of the early thirteenth century, and that of the SS. Redeemer, 1900. In the woods an easy trekking trail touches the remains of the ancient silver mines; the vineyards rise up to 650-750 meters.

  • Where Faedo, San Michele all'Adige
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitrotaliana.it · italia.it

Cantine e Trento Doc

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

San Michele is the reference area of Trento Doc, with the highest concentration of producers in Trentino, between international and autochthonous varieties; in Faedo Müller Thurgau grows higher and Chardonnay, aromatic Traminer, Lagrein and Schiava lower. The tourist consortium refers to the list of wineries that can be visited; Italia.it also remembers the distillates of the hilly area of Faedo.
