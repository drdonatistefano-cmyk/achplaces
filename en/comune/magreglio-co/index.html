What to see in Magreglio

In the Lariano Triangle, 754 m between the two branches of Lake Como, Magreglio is the village of Ghisallo: the sanctuary of Our Lady proclaimed patron of cyclists in 1949 and, next to, the Museum of Cycling; from the village a path in chestnut trees leads to Menaresta, source of Lambro.

Santuario della Madonna del Ghisallo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

The present church rises in 1623 on a previous building; the venerated image is a Madonna del Latte di mano populare of the sixteenth century. For the commitment of the parish priest Don Ermelindo Viganò, a short pontifical of 13 October 1949 proclaimed the Virgin of the Ghisallo patron of cyclists: inside the effigy is surrounded by bicycles, shirts, trophies and photographs of champions, and on the square there are the statues of Gino Bartali and Fausto Coppi. It is celebrated on the Easter of cyclists and National Bicycle Day.

  • Where Piazzale del Ghisallo, Via Campolungo 27, Magreglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Museo del Ciclismo Madonna del Ghisallo

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Inaugurated in October 2006 by the Foundation chaired by Fiorenzo Magni, at 754 m altitude, it preserves over 2,000 objects of great champions: the largest collection of pink shirts, made with the Gazzetta dello Sport, yellow tops of the Tour, the bicycles of Bartali, Coppi and Magni, the bikes of the records of the hour of Baldini, Rominger and Moser, military bikes of the early twentieth century including a Bersaglieri folding Whites, sections on cyclocross, MTB, paracycling and female cycling and a wall dedicated to the cyclists who died in the race. From the viewpoint next to the museum you can see Lake Como from above; open from Tuesday to Sunday.

  • Where Via Gino Bartali 4, Magreglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodelghisallo.it

Sources: museodelghisallo.it · in-lombardia.it · comune.magreglio.co.it

Menaresta e Bocc de Pegur

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

From the village a path of about half an hour crosses the chestnut groves and goes up the course of Lambro up to the Menaresta, its source: a natural siphon from which the water flows at intervals. Going up the slope in front, with the entrance hidden by a rock, you will find the Bocc de Pegur, a series of caves communicating with stalagmites.

  • Where Sentiero dal borgo di Magreglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.magreglio.co.it · in-lombardia.it

Borgo di Magreglio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the old town you can see some valuable portals, the 16th century Monastery (today Ca' Moiana) and the Cà del Tay with its characteristic "lobbie" .

  • Where Centro storico, Magreglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.magreglio.co.it · in-lombardia.it
