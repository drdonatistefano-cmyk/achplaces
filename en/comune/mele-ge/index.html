What to see in Mele

The municipality of Val Leira, where the map has been produced since the 15th century: the Paper Museum in the eighteenth-century paper mill of Acquasanta, the sanctuary of Our Lady of Acquasanta and the sulphur water baths, with the paths of the Park of Beigua and the High Via.

Museo della Carta di Mele (Cartiera Sbaraggia)

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 1997 in the ancient paper mill of Acquasanta (Piccardo, former Sbaraggia), founded in 1756 and active until 1985, it preserves machinery and tools, production materials and samples of cards, including one of the late 19th century donated in 2009. The route shows how rags of vegetable fibers and macero paper became new paper, in the valley where it has been produced since the 15th century; guided tours and handmade paper workshops. Accessible in the wheelchair. Tel. 347 1679042.

Santuario di Nostra Signora dell'Acquasanta

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

According to tradition, it rises on an ancient pagan temple, where shepherds found a statue of Our Lady considered miraculous; the cult dates back to at least the 15th century and the present building is largely six-seventh century. In 1832 Ferdinand II of Bourbon and Maria Cristina of Savoy were married there; it is still a destination for pilgrimages.

  • Where Acquasanta, Mele
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
