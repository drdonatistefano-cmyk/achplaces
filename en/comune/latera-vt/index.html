What to see in Latera

Medieval village on a hill inside the volcanic Caldera of Latera, 124 km from Rome: Palazzo Farnese on Piazza della Rocca, the Pieve di San Clemente from which starts the Tour of the seven churches, the historical fountains and the Museum of the Earth in the Grancia of San Pietro; from here passes the Path of the Briganti towards Lake Mezzano.

6 places to visit in Latera, in the province of Viterbo (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Terra

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 2001 in the Altomedieval complex of the Grancia di San Pietro and in the adjacent former municipal slaughterhouse, it tells with objects and multimedia materials the story of Latera around the theme of the earth. The core is the Poscia Collection, about 2000 utensils of peasant life, craftsmanship and daily (house and workshop, wood and plain, markets, weights and measures); an audiovisual path on the immaterial heritage includes the Gallery of the processions, the Four Cantons and the Bauli of memory; outdoor local crops and a vegetable garden of aromatic herbs and medicinal. From the museum starts the Trail of the three churches towards abandoned quarries of industrial archaeology; audioguides available.

  • Where Via dell'Osteria, Latera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · retemusei.regione.lazio.it · visitlazio.com

Palazzo Farnese e Piazza della Rocca

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palazzo Ducale, residence of the Farnese family, who owned it until the extinction of the family in 1668; is part of the Latium Historical Houses Network and overlooks Piazza della Rocca, indicated among the things not to be missed.

  • Where Piazza della Rocca, Latera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Pieve di San Clemente e Giro delle sette chiese

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The Pieve di San Clemente, the main church of the village with paintings and baptismal font, is the starting point of the Tour of the seven churches, a route of about 4 km that touches the Madonna del Soccorso, the Madonna del Carmine (ascending from Via Piave), San Giuseppe on the Corso Vittorio Emanuele III, San Sebastiano, San Rocco and the Madonna della Cava.

  • Where Latera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Borgo medievale e fontane storiche

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Historic centre of alleys to walk on a hill inside Caldera. The historical fountains are the Fontana del Piscero (1576), with beautiful views of the village, the Fontana del Ponte with octagonal plan, the Fontana di Canale, from which spring two springs of sparkling water, and the Fontana Trincheri.

  • Where Centro storico, Latera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Caldera di Latera e Sentiero dei Briganti

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Trail of the Briganti, an archaeological-naturalistic itinerary of about 100 km that crosses part of the Reserve of Monte Rufeno and reaches the sea, in Latera starts from the parking lot behind the cemetery and crosses the Caldera, valley of volcanic origin, towards Lake Mezzano.

  • Where Parcheggio del cimitero, Latera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Sagra del Marrone e Cristo Morto

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

During the last two weekends of October the Festival of the Brown of Latera is held; on Good Friday the night procession of the Dead Christ runs along the streets of the country with liturgical songs of oral tradition.

  • Where Latera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
