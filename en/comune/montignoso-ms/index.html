What to see in Montignoso

Country at the foot of the Apuans between Massa and Versilia, dominated by the Aghinolfi Castle, the cornerstone of the Gothic Line in 1945; downstream Villa Schiff Giorgini with the documentation center on the war, the churches of San Vito and Modesto and of Sant'Eustachio, Mount Pasquilio dear to Ungaretti and Montale, the oasis of Lake Porta and the sea of Cinquale.

Castello Aghinolfi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress on the slopes of the Apuan Islands, strategic for the proximity to the sea and the control of the Via Francigena, one of the oldest of the Lunigiana and already Byzantine garrison at the Longobard border; the name refers to the Aghinulfo mentioned in a document of the 8th century. A circle of walls with towers contains two spaces; the mast is built on a large octagonal building, perhaps of the twelfth century, connected to the circular tower of San Paolino. In 1945 it was one of the main German strongholds of the Gothic Line; restored since 1998, it has a panoramic terrace and a room with glass floor on the stratifications of the building.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Villa Schiff Giorgini e Centro di documentazione sulla Linea Gotica

Museums · City history

A nineteenth-century villa rich in white marble in a park of centuries-old trees, linked to the family Giorgini Lucca and renovated by Matilde Giorgini with her husband Roberto Schiff; between July 1944 and April 1945 it was the seat of the German command on the Gothic Line and was repeatedly bombed. Today it is the seat of the Municipality and in the halls of the ground floor houses the Documentation Center on the Gothic Line, with images, documents, helmets, uniforms, ammunition and video testimony of survivors on the war in the Apuan Riviera and Versilia.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa di San Vito e San Modesto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the XII century, the oldest among those mentioned by the sources, which preserves the altar triptych painted in 1482 by the Lucca Michele Ciampanti with the Madonna and Child and the Saints Giovanni Battista, Vito, Modesto and Pietro.

  • Where Via Fondaccio, Montignoso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com

Chiesa e borgo di Sant'Eustachio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the medieval village of Sant'Eustachio, also called Villa di Mezzo, the church of 1495 houses two fifteenth-century tables attributed to the Master of the Tondo Lanthrop and to the Master of Stratonice and a Madonna with the wooden Gothic Child of the fourteenth century of the Pisan school, attributed to Tino di Camaino; the altarpiece of Vincenzo Frediani depicts the Madonna and Child among saints.

  • Where Sant'Eustachio, Montignoso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com

Monte Pasquilio e sentieri della Linea Gotica

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

A wooded area under Monte Carchio, in the regional park of the Apuan Alps, with varied landscapes and spectacular views of the coast, loved by the poets Ungaretti and Montale; in the second world war it was shelter and theatre of clashes, and among chestnut and fir woods the route of the Gothic Line preserves traces of the German outposts. You reach it through Cerreto, with views of the Apuan and Versilia coast.

  • Where Pasquilio, Montignoso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Lago di Porta

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Protected wetland, already Lake Perotto, managed by Legambiente and WWF: it hosts rare and threatened species such as the butterfly Lycaena dispar and birds like heron ash, folaga, water chicken, pig, swamp falcon (nidifying) and tarabusino; in early autumn there are large numbers of swallows before migration.

  • Where Lago di Porta, Montignoso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Cinquale e Green Beach

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

The Cinquale is a well-known locality of Versilia that attracted artists and writers such as Enrico Pea, Gabriele d'Annunzio, Carlo Carrà and Mino Maccari; here the Green Beach is a free and accessible beach, designed for people with disabilities or motor difficulties.

  • Where Cinquale, Montignoso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it
