What to see in Capua

The Capua Longobarda on the handle of the Volturno: the Campano Museum of Matres Matutae in the Palazzo Antignano, the Duomo with the bell tower of the IX century, the churches "a Corte" , the Norman castle of the Stones, the castle of Charles V and, in Sant'Angelo in Formis, the largest cycle of medieval frescoes of the South.

7 places to visit in Capua, in the province of Caserta (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Provinciale Campano

Museums · Archaeological

Founded in 1870 in the fifteenth-century Palazzo Antignano of Catalan taste: the Matres Matutae, votive statues in tuff from the VII to the first century BC, vases from the Iron Age to the Attic Ceramics, terracotta, bronzes, coins, epigraphs, mosaics from Sant'Angelo in Formis and Nola, the federician room with the remains of the towers of Frederick II and the Bourbon, Liani and Savoy rooms.

  • Where Via Roma 68, Capua
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.capua.ce.it

Basilica di Sant'Angelo in Formis

Places of worship · Basilicas

Benedictine Basilica dedicated to Saint Michael, rebuilt in 1072 for the abbot Desire on the temple of Diana Tifatina with materials of the ancient sanctuary; the interior is entirely frescoed by the Campania-Byzantine school with stories of the Old and New Testaments and the universal Judgment, the largest medieval pictorial cycle of southern Italy.

  • Where Via L. Baia 120, Sant'Angelo in Formis, Capua
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.capua.ce.it

Duomo di Santa Maria Assunta e campanile

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Founded in 856, with quadriportico of the 11th century and basilica with three naves on 24 granite columns of 1120; destroyed by the bombings of 1943 and rebuilt by 1958. In the eighteenth-century crypt a Christ by Matteo Bottiglieri; the Lombard bell tower, rebuilt in Norman age, rests on Roman blocks.

  • Where Piazza dei Giudici, Capua
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.capua.ce.it · italia.it

Chiese longobarde «a Corte»

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the center there are churches of the Longobard Age: San Giovanni a Corte of the IX century, San Salvatore a Corte founded in 960 by the Countess Adelgrima with bell tower of the early thirteenth century, San Michele a Corte of the IX-X century with Byzantine elements.

  • Where Centro storico, Capua
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.capua.ce.it · italia.it

Castello delle Pietre

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Castrum Lapidum founded in 1062 after the Norman conquest with stones of the Campania Amphitheatre; hospital in 1734 and arsenal since 1806, its tower is national monument since 1875.

  • Where Centro storico, Capua
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.capua.ce.it · italia.it

Castello di Carlo V

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortress of 1542 on a project by Gian Giacomo dell'Acaya, about 8,500 square meters with lanceolate bastions; political prison between 1848 and 1852 and then pyrotechnic factory, has been restored since 1982.

According to the municipality you visit only on special occasions

  • Where Capua
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.capua.ce.it · italia.it

Ponte romano sul Volturno

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The bridge over the Volturno around which the city was born, restored several times and rebuilt after the bombings of 1943.

  • Where Capua
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
