What to see in Zubiena

Municipality of Serra morainica between Biella and Ivrea, on the right of the Elven, which shares with Borriana, Cerrione and Mongrando the Natural Reserve of Bessa, the heaps of pebbles left by the Roman gold mine. In the hamlet of Vermogno, next to the visitor center of the reserve, the Museum of Gold and Bessa tells the search for gold over the centuries.

Museo dell'Oro e della Bessa

Museums · Themed

Cell of the Ecomuseo del Biellese in Vermogno that documents the history and techniques of the search for gold over the centuries, explains how the native gold has been brought downstream by glaciers and exhibits auriferous samples of Italian rivers and streams. All year round guided tours, excursions and workshops on reading the landscape of Bessa and on the search for gold (geology, techniques, tools). Contacts: tel. 340 6114854.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.atl.biella.it

Riserva naturale della Bessa

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Reserve of about 10 km2 established in 1985 between Borriana, Cerrione, Mongrando and Zubiena: an artificial landscape of heaps of stones up to 10 meters high, residue of the auriferous exploitation conducted by the Romans between the II and the I century BC with the work of the Ictimuli, with washing channels, roads of the wagons and remains of huts; today colonized by oaks, cherry, birch and chestnuts, with owls and barbagianni. From the Vermogno tour centre, the easy routes start: Ciapei Parfundà (2,07 km, panels on geology, history and archaeology) and Rive del Ger (1,75 km); from Zubiena, also the rock engravings (3,8 km) and the "Mezza Bessa" (4,1 km, about 2 hours); from Vermogno, the parking lot for the bike routes. Spring is the best time.

  • Where Centro visite, frazione Vermogno, Zubiena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.atl.biella.it

Sources: atl.biella.it · ecomuseo.it
