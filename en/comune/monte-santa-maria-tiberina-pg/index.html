What to see in Monte Santa Maria Tiberina

Medieval village of the Upper Tiber Valley closed by walls, with the sixteenth-century castle of the Bourbon del Monte, lords of an imperial marquisate from 1250 to 1815, today Palazzo Museo; near the parish church of Santa Maria with the chapel of the Marquis and, in the surroundings, the castle of Lippiano.

Palazzo Museo Bourbon del Monte

Museums · City history

The 16th-century castle, the heart of the village, houses the museum that tells the Bourbon del Monte family and its marquisate, imperial fief from 1250 to 1815, and the connection with the city and territory of the Tiber Valley. Recently the route has been enriched with an archaeological section with the findings of the territory.

Chiesa di Santa Maria e Cappella Bourbon

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built at the beginning of the 11th century, it has a simple Romanesque stone façade and Latin cross plan; the bell tower is a nineteenth-century reconstruction. The Bourbon Chapel was built in 1613 by the Marquis Gianbattista Bourbon del Monte; inside a sixteenth-century baptismal font in sandstone with the coat of arms of the Marquis, a 12th-century slab and, on the main Baroque altar, a Madonna with wooden Child of the fourteenth-century carrying the silver keys of the village, deliver them for the feast of Ascension. The crypt-sepulchre of the Bourbon del Monte is not open to the public.

Borgo medievale e mura

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village is enclosed by medieval walls, with cobbled alleys and stone houses. From the village, the view stretches over ancient Etruscan roads and centuries-old chestnut groves to the Apuan Alps and, on clear days, to the Gran Sasso.

  • Where Centro storico, Monte Santa Maria Tiberina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello di Lippiano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

In the surroundings of the capital, the castle is distinguished by the high square tower, from which you can enjoy a wide panorama between Umbria and Tuscany.
