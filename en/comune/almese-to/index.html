What to see in Almese

Municipality of the lower valley of Susa at the foot of the Musiné, with the Roman villa of the first century AD of Grange of Rivera, the tower and the Benedictine shelter of San Mauro and the waterfall of the Goja del Pis along the Mass.

5 places to visit in Almese, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Almese in the guide with map and filters

Villa romana di Almese

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Patron villa of a vast agricultural sidedone of the first century A.D., referred to as the largest suburban villa in northwestern Italy. The ground floor, part of the monumental entrance, the central courtyard and the large terrace facing south towards the valley, are preserved. Free guided tours with the association Ar.c.A., access on the floor.

  • Where Località Grange di Rivera, Almese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.almese.to.it

Sources: comune.almese.to.it

Torre e Ricetto di San Mauro

History and architecture · Villages · Viewpoint

In 1029 there was already a church with a bell tower on an outlying rock, around which the Benedictine monks built the center of their curtis; about 250 years later the defenses were strengthened. The tower is visited with the guides of the FIE and some rooms of the shelter host exhibitions and events.

  • Where Borgo di San Mauro, Almese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.almese.to.it

Sources: comune.almese.to.it

Goja del Pis

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Waterfall of about 14 meters falling into a tank of about 30 meters of diameter, shaped by erosion in the serpentinites. The path starts from the bridge of Sant'Anna in the square and goes up the Mass for the villages of Fucinassa and Gambabosco: about 50 minutes and 150 meters of difference in altitude.

  • Where Ponte di Sant'Anna (partenza sentiero); parcheggio in via Alma Bertolo, Almese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.almese.to.it

Sources: comune.almese.to.it

Parco Robinson

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Green area of over 9,500 m2 on the Via Francigena, between the centre of Rivera and the industrial area, with picnic tables, children's games, fountain and pond; in summer it hosts concerts and tastings.

  • Where Frazione Rivera, Almese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.almese.to.it

Sources: comune.almese.to.it
