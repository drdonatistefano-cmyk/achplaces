What to see in Sesto

Country of Alta Pusteria at the foot of the Sesto Dolomites, a UNESCO World Heritage Site: from the Val Fiscalina, in the Tre Cime Nature Park, you climb to the Tre Cime, while the iconic Sesto iconic trail marks the hours with its peaks; in the village the parish with the macabre dance of Rudolf Stolz and the museum dedicated to the painter, at the height of the Reinhold Messner Haus on Mount Elmo and the open-air museum of the Great War.

8 places to visit in Sesto, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Rudolf Stolz

Museums · Modern art

Two rooms with more than 160 works by the painter Rudolf Stolz (1874-1960), largely self-taught and considered, after Egger-Lienz, among the most important Tyrolean artists of the first half of the twentieth century: sketches, sketches for frescoes, watercolors and studies, with paintings by Albert Stolz. Every summer a temporary themed exhibition.

  • Where Via Dolomiti 16b, Sesto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.dreizinnen.com

Sources: suedtirol.info · sexten.it · dreizinnen.com

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo e cimitero

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Neoclassical parish church with frescoes by the brothers painters Stolz, the "Danza macabra" by Rudolf Stolz, baptismal font and altarpieces by C. Dusi; the curated cemetery preserves wood and sandstone sculptures. Open every day of the year, 8-19.

  • Where Via della Chiesa 11, Sesto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.dreizinnen.com

Sources: dreizinnen.com

Val Fiscalina

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valley almost 4.5 km long in the heart of the Tre Cime Natural Park, from Moso to Rifugio Fondovalle: larch forests and flowered meadows in summer, in winter cross-country skiing or horse-drawn sled. The path 102 is also accessible by bike; the bus 440 and 446 pass alternating every 15 minutes. The Dolomites of Sesto are a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  • Where Val Fiscalina, Moso, Sesto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.dreizinnen.com

Sources: dreizinnen.com

Tre Cime dal Rifugio Locatelli

Nature and landscape · Mountains · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

From Sesto you can reach the Rifugio Tre Cime (Locatelli) with path 102 in about 3 and a half hours and about 950 meters of difference in altitude; alternatively shuttle from Sesto (tickets online or at the tourist office) or car to the Rifugio Auronzo, with obligatory booking.

  • Where Rifugio Locatelli, Sesto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.dreizinnen.com

Sources: dreizinnen.com

Meridiana di Sesto

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The peaks Cima Otto, Cima Nove, Cima Ten, Cima Eleven, Cima Twelve and Cima Una indicate the time of the day according to the position of the sun: a show that strikes especially on clear days in winter.

  • Where Sesto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.dreizinnen.com

Sources: dreizinnen.com

Reinhold Messner Haus sul Monte Elmo

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

The old mountain station of the cable car, transformed by Reinhold Messner and his wife Diane in the center for sustainability, art and culture and new home of the mountaineer: personal objects of his expeditions, conferences and exhibitions. Go up in the cable car from Sesto; on the top also the park Olperl families and panoramic paths.

  • Where Stazione a monte, Monte Elmo, Sesto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.dreizinnen.com

Sources: suedtirol.info · sexten.it · dreizinnen.com

Museo all'aperto Anderter Alpe (Croda Rossa)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

At 2,150 metres on the Croda Rossa are still trenches, stations and barracks of the front of the First World War between Austria-Hungary and Italy, with panels and guided tours of the Bellum Aquilarum association; the exhibition "Unvergessen" and the fort of Mitterberg are linked.

  • Where Croda Rossa, Sesto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.dreizinnen.com

Sources: sexten.it · dreizinnen.com

Centro visite Haus der Berge

Museums · Themed · Free entry · Viewpoint

Visitor center and modern information with interactive exhibition on the Dolomite front of the First World War and on the first historical ascents, and panoramic terrace with free entrance. From 26 May to 27 September 2026 open Monday-Friday 8.30-17, Saturday 8.30-14.30, Sunday 10-12 (5 July-6 September).
