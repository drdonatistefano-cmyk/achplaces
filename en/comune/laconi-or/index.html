What to see in Laconi

Sarcidano Country, in the center of Sardinia, Orange Flag of Touring: it has the Aymerich Park, the largest urban park on the island, the Museum of the prehistoric statuary in the palace of the Marquises Aymerich and is the birthplace of Sant'Ignazio, the most venerated saint of the island.

Menhir Museum - Museo della statuaria preistorica in Sardegna

Museums · Archaeological

In Palazzo Aymerich, the ancient residence of the Marquises of Laconi: ten of the eleven halls exhibit menhirs and anthropomorphic statues of Sarcidano, Grighine and Mandrolisai, dated between IV and III millennium B.C., in three types (protoanthropomorphs, asexual anthropomorphs and menhir statues by sex); the eleventh room, on the inner court, collects vases, spearheads and blades in flint with megalithic tombs. The laconese pieces are in local trachite; the research began in 1969 in Genna Arrele.

  • Where Via Amsicora, Laconi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Parco Aymerich

Nature and landscape · City parks · Viewpoint

The largest urban park in Sardinia, about 22 hectares on the edge of the village, belonged to the Marquises Aymerich until 1990: the botanist Don Ignazio Aymerich Ripoll introduced rare plants from the mid-nineteenth century. Lecci, olive trees and carobs, caves, streams, waterfalls and ponds, orchids also endemic, cedars of Lebanon and the Himalayas and a great magnolia.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Castello Aymerich

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Ruins inside the Aymerich Park: the main tower dates back to the 11th-XII century and in the 18th century became a prison; the castle has two floors and a valuable portico. During World War II the nearby caves served as a refuge.

Casa natale e Museo di Sant'Ignazio

Museums · House museums

The birthplace of Sant'Ignazio (1701-1781), the most venerated saint of Sardinia, in the historic center, houses a small museum and is the first stage of the devotional itinerary; in the festival week of late August pilgrims from all over the island and from abroad arrive there, and on August 31 the procession takes place with knights in costume and confraternities.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Ambrogio e Ignazio e Museo parrocchiale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church built in the 15th century and modified several times in the 19th century, also dedicated to St. Ignatius since 1951. The attached museum preserves a silver reliquary with a bone of the saint's hand, its rosary and documents of the cause of canonization, silver sacred of the sixteenth-sixth century, vestments of the sixteenth-century, partly Spanish statues, two 17th-century canvases, Roman finds, coins and objects of missions in Africa and Australia.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Menhir e dolmen di Corte Noa

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Seven "perdas fittas" are 1,2 to 2,2 meters high on the side of the Zerfaliu basin, carved with hammers with protoanthropomorphic traits and dated between IV and III millennium B.C.; about 200 meters a dolmen tunnel about 9 meters long, whose kits are in part at the Menhir Museum. You can reach from Laconi towards the farm Genna 'e Corte, then walk over a gate.

  • Where Località Corte Noa, Laconi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Nuraghe Genna 'e Corte

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

The complex nuraghe of the Bronze Age in the locality of Stunnu, about 10 km north of the village, called \"nuraghe azzurro" due to the color of some stones in a tower: mastium of about 12 meters, five secondary towers (there remain two) and eastern tower with tholos cover, in trachyte.

the source describes it in a bad state of conservation and reachable with about 11 km of dirt and a stretch on foot

Tartufo nero di Laconi

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The microclimate favors black truffle: Laconi has the most abundant harvest in Sardinia, the season goes from December to June and every June there is a dedicated festival; in the village there are oil, preserves and truffle honey.
