What to see in Bassano Romano

Borgo della Tuscia halfway between the lakes of Bracciano and Vico, which owes its bloom to the Justinians: the Palazzo Giustiniani Odescalchi frescoed by Albani, Domenichino and Tempesta with the park of 40 hectares, and on the hill the church of San Vincenzo Martire with the first Christ Portacroce of Michelangelo; all around a beech of over 400 hectares.

Palazzo Giustiniani Odescalchi (Villa Giustiniani)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Born from the reconstruction of the castle of the Anguillara, of which remain basement and ground floor, was purchased by the Justinians in 1595: the Marquis Vincenzo added the noble floor connected to the Italian garden with fountains, avenues and water games; the Odescalchi gave it to the State in 2003. On the sides of the portal four marble heads of the II century A.D.; in the halls the Fall of Fetonte by Francesco Albani (1609), the Hall of Love and Psyche by Bernardo Castello (1605), the Hall of Eternal Happiness by Paolo Guidotti (1610), the Camerino di Diana di Domenichino (1609) and decorations by Antonio Tempesta; on the ground floor a private theater with wooden boxes, under renovation. Open Mar-dom 8.30-12.30, Monday closed; booking not required.

Chiesa e Monastero di San Vincenzo Martire

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

On a panoramic hill, commissioned by Vincenzo Giustiniani in the seventeenth century as a family mausoleum: monumental façade between two bell towers with the large bust of the Redentore, inside a Latin cross with dome, decorated only with stuccoes; the monastery, after the war, was donated by Prince Odescalchi to the Silvestrini Benedictines. It preserves the first Christ Portacroce by Michelangelo, abandoned in 1514 for a dark vein on the cheek, purchased by Giustiniani in 1607 and completed by another sculptor (perhaps Bernini), brought here in 1644 by his nephew Andrea; the restoration of the Superintendence of 2001 confirmed the attribution. In 2025 he was exposed to the Osaka Expo.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Completed in 1703 next to Palazzo Giustiniani, it has the local stone façade with clock and coat of arms of the Justinians; in the single nave an organ of the eighteenth century, a nineteenth-century choir in painted wood, the Mater Dolorosa, the Assumption of the Virgin and the relic of the head of St. Gratilian.

Via Maria Giustiniani e Borgo San Filippo Neri

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The oldest street in the village crosses the center between the two ancient doors: at number 27 a small building with portal in pepper and cardinal's coat of arms attributed to the Chigi, between 24 and 26 a painted porcelain Virgin of the eighteenth century. Beyond the medieval gate, the village wanted by Andrea Giustiniani in the seventeenth century, with Palazzo Savelli and the church of San Filippo Neri (first half of the seventeenth century, four altars, wooden ceiling, Madonna della Pietà); in the Oratory of the Confraternita della Santissima Trinità a banner by Francesco Ciaci.

  • Where Via Maria Giustiniani, Bassano Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.bassanoromano.vt.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria dei Monti

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

On a hill just outside the village, reached by a characteristic staircase; the legend wants it erected in the fifteenth century after the Virgin expelled the evil spirits that tormented the shepherds. It preserves frescoes and since 1479 it is the destination of a solemn procession every year.

Chiesa di San Gratiliano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Erected in 1546 in honor of the young martyr protector of Bassano, today in the modern part of the village: a single nave with St. Paul and St. Peter and, on the sides of the apse, frescoes with the life of the saint.

Faggeta

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails

Wood of beech trees of over 400 hectares below 500 meters of altitude, along the road that descends from the village in the countryside, with many species of plants and animals, including the Alpine Rosalia; area equipped with tables and barbecue and nature trails.
