What to see in Temù

Country of the upper Camonica Valley at the foot of Adamello, in the Pontedilegno-Tonale area; to see the White War Museum in Adamello, the parish church of San Bartolomeo, the small churches of Sant'Alessandro and Santa Giulia and the Val d'Avio, the largest valley of the Adamello Park.

Museo della Guerra Bianca in Adamello

Museums · Themed

The Adamello Glacier was war-fighted in 1915-18, with combats at about 3,000 meters in extreme conditions; the museum, in the center of the country, exhibits finds on the glacier by volunteers (arms and cannons, equipment, gavette, tools, uniforms and documents) and is the center of documentation and study of the Great War in Lombardy, with places detached from the forts of Colico. With Adamello Card and FAI card discount of 1 euro on the full ticket. According to Touring, it opens Saturday and Sunday 15-18 and is closed in January, February, March, November and December.

  • Where Via Roma 40, Temù
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museoguerrabianca.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The 17th century church was profoundly renovated in the 18th and 19th centuries, with a single hall with three vaulted bays and a portico on granite columns; in the façade three large mosaics by Don Mino Trombini with Saints Alessandro, Bartolomeo and Antonio Abate, and bell tower with Ghibelline battlements and marble mullioned windows of Vezza. Inside the main altar of Giovan Battista Zotti and the altar of the Rosary of the workshop of Ramus, with a Nativity palace whose shepherds are inspired by the locals.

  • Where Via Adamello 12, Temù
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Sources: comune.temu.bs.it · turismovallecamonica.it

Chiesa di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A place of worship already in the 12th century, rebuilt in the 16th century and rebuilt in Baroque forms in the 17th century with frescoes; it has a marble portal of Vezza of 1748 and a side in white marble of 1902, and was consecrated to Saint Martin of Tours in 1921.

  • Where Temù
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Sources: turismovallecamonica.it

Chiesetta rurale di Sant'Alessandro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church in a hut along the road between Vione and Lecanù, with fourteenth-century bell tower on the north side, restored after a period of degradation lasted until the 1930s; tradition binds it to St. Charles Borromeo, with a stone object of veneration.

  • Where Strada per Vione, Lecanù di Temù
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Sources: turismovallecamonica.it

Chiesetta di Santa Giulia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Small alpine church at 1,860 meters above sea level in the larch tree above Mezzullo, linked in the sixteenth century to the Chiericato di Santa Giulia, which in 1567 raised controversy during the visit of Bishop Domenico Bollani.

  • Where Sopra Mezzullo, Temù
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Sources: turismovallecamonica.it

Val d'Avio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

A valley of glacial origin about 5 km wide, the largest of the Adamello Park, with Adamello and Garibaldi refuge in the upper part and the artificial lakes of Avio, Benedetto and Veneroccolo, built between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries; it was the scene of battles in the First World War. Walk along path no. 611 (Caldea, Avio, Garibaldi refuge).

  • Where Val d'Avio, Temù
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Sources: turismovallecamonica.it
