What to see in Bellano

Borgo on the eastern shore of Lake Como, crossed by the stream Pioverna that dug there the Orrido, which can be crossed on walkways; in the old town the late Dolomian church of Santi Nazaro and Celso and the fourteenth-century church of Santa Marta, while above the village pass the Path of the Wanderer and, in Val Muggiasca, the churches of Vendrogno and Mount Muggio.

Orrido di Bellano e Cà del Diavol

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Natural gorge excavated by the Pioverna stream (according to the sources about 15 million years ago, with the competition of the Adda glacier), with large pots and caves, open on suspended bridges and walkways. At the entrance stands the Cà del Diavol, pentagonal tower with a fresco of the devil, linked to legends of satanic rites, which houses a multimedia exhibition. It is reached from the staircase to the right of the church of Saints Nazarus and Celsus; indicated times: every day 11-13 and 15-19.

Chiesa dei Santi Nazaro e Celso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Late Romanesque church in the historic center, national monument, with facade in black and white marble, frescoes and rosettes; the saints patrons celebrate on July 28.

  • Where Piazza San Giorgio, Bellano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di Santa Marta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the XIV century in front of the Saints Nazarus and Celsus, which preserves the wooden group of the "Pietà" by Giovanni Angelo Del Maino.

  • Where Piazza San Giorgio, Bellano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Santuario di Lezzeno

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

  • Entry Free

Lungolago e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village, documented since 905 and plundered by the Venetians in 1447, preserves in the historic center stretches of walls, palaces and the parish church; the lakefront with pebble beaches and the port of the boats complete the walk. On the evening of January 5, the Vegia Pesa recalls in costume an edict of 1605.

  • Where Lungolago, Bellano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Sentiero del Viandante

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient half-cost mule track along the eastern shore of the lake, 68 km in six connected stages in 1992, reachable from the top of the Orrido; certified by the Touring Club between the walks.

  • Where Sommità dell'Orrido, Bellano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiese di Vendrogno: Sant'Antonio Abate e San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the hamlet of Vendrogno, in Val Muggiasca, the church of Sant'Antonio Abate was built in 1362 and contains frescoes from the sixteenth century; that of San Lorenzo has a Baroque façade and Romanesque bell tower.

  • Where Vendrogno, Bellano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Monte Muggio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the top the view stretches over Valsassina, Valtellina, lakes of Como and Lugano and, on clear days, up to Monte Rosa; the territory of Val Muggiasca includes three alpine pastures, seven hamlets and twelve churches.

  • Where Val Muggiasca, Bellano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
