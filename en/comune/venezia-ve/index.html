What to see in Venice

The city on the water, on 118 islands of the lagoon, with calluses, canals and palaces on the Grand Canal; Venice and its lagoon are a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

33 places to visit in Venice, in the province of Venice (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza San Marco

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The only "piazza" of Venice, trapezoid, 175 for 72 meters, defined by Napoleon the most beautiful living room in Europe; it is the lowest area of the city and flooded with high water. Under the New Procuratie the Florian Coffee, the oldest in Italy.

  • Where Piazza San Marco, Venezia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it
