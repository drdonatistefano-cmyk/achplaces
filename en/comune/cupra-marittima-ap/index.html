What to see in Cupra Marittima

The seaside resort of the Riviera delle Palme with the medieval fortified village of Marano (Cupra Alta) on the hill, a free commune from the 11th century with fortified port, and to the north the Archaeological Park of the Roman Cupra Maritima, with the Forum and the remains of a temple; in the village the Piceno Malacological Museum, with over 900,000 shells.

9 places to visit in Cupra Marittima, in the province of Ascoli Piceno (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo di Marano (Cupra Alta)

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval fortified nucleus of Cupra Marittima, with traces from the IX-X century and free commune from the XI century with a fortified port and trade up to Venice, Ragusa and Durazzo; it is still surrounded by walls with square and polygonal towers wanted in the XV century by Francesco Sforza. You enter the Porta Maggiore or Marina; in the village the Palazzo Brancadoro, built by the Sforza, the remains of the fortress and the panorama of the sea.

  • Where Via Castello, Cupra Marittima
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it · comune.cupra-marittima.ap.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria in Castello

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Museo malacologico piceno

Museums · Science and university

Museum of shells with over 900,000 specimens, indicated as one of the most important malacological collections in the world, with also some specimens of shark; educational services, guided tours and book shop. Afternoon and seasonal opening: better call first (0735 777550).

Parco archeologico e naturalistico di Cupra Maritima

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

About 1.5 km north of the center, on the height of the Civita, the Forum of the Roman city with the remains of a rectangular temple of the Ariane era and of two arches; in the Picena age the place was known for the sanctuary of the goddess Cupra. Visits by reservation (375 739 0077).

  • Where Via Piana Santi, Cupra Marittima
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.marche.it · comune.cupra-marittima.ap.it · italia.it
