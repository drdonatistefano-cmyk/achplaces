What to see in Offida

Borgo on a rocky spur dug by the two branches of the Lama stream, famous for its tombble lace: the triangular Piazza del Popolo with Palazzo Comunale and Teatro Serpente Aureo, the Romanesque-Gothic Santa Maria della Rocca and the museum pole of Palazzo De Castellotti.

Chiesa di Santa Maria della Rocca

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

Built in 1330 on a Longobard castle donated in 1039 to the Abbey of Farfa, it is the Romanesque-Gothic monument in the most important terracotta of Offida, on a steep cliff. The three-nave crypt contains fourteenth-century frescoes by the Master of Offida with the stories of Saint Catherine of Alexandria and Saint Lucia; in the apse of the upper church prophets, musical angels and virgins of Ugolino di Vanne, and a Madonna with Child attributed to Vincenzo Pagani. Open Saturday and Sunday.

  • Where Via Roma 98, Offida
  • Entry €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Piazza del Popolo e Palazzo Comunale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Triangular square, heart of the village, reorganized at the end of the eighteenth century. The Town Hall in brick has a loggia of thirteen columns in travertine and a fifteenth-century portico; the tower is of the fourteenth century and the crowning of swallows of 1924.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Offida
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Teatro Serpente Aureo

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Designed by Pietro Maggi, built in 1820 and modernized in 1862, it is reached from the portico of the Town Hall: horse-iron plant with 50 stages on three orders, gallery and audience, with stuccoes and golden decorations.

Santuario del Miracolo Eucaristico in Sant'Agostino

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

The large brick building of the former Augustinian convent (1338-1441), houses the relics of the Eucharistic miracle of Lanciano in 1273, arrived at Offida in 1280; part of the host is preserved in a fourteenth-century gold-plated silver cross of a Venetian goldsmith. The relics are exhibited on May 3rd; open Saturday and Sunday.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Ciabattoni 4, Offida
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.offida.ap.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
