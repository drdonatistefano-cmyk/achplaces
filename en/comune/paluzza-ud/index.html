What to see in Paluzza

Town of the high But valley towards the Passo di Monte Croce Carnico: the Torre Moscarda of the thirteenth century with museum and botanical garden, in Timau the museum and the Ossario temple of the Great War and, in summer, the lake Avostanis above Malga Pramosio.

5 places to visit in Paluzza, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre Moscarda «La Torate»

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Tower of just over 10 meters square base, only surviving of the defensive-doganal system of Castrum Muscardum built in the middle of the thirteenth century where the valley narrows, to bar the road towards the Passo di Monte Croce Carnico (the tower on the opposite bank of the But was destroyed in 1840). Restored in 1993, it houses a Museo del Territorio with botanical garden; along the paths of access military works of the two wars. In 2026 open from Tuesday to Sunday 15-18 (from 10 to 23 August also 10-12).

  • Where Sopra Paluzza (accesso a piedi da est e da nord)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Museo «La zona Carnia nella Grande Guerra»

Museums · Themed

In Timau, a hamlet at the foot of the Passo di Monte Croce Carnico, it collects documents and objects found on the battlefields of the First World War.

  • Where Timau, Paluzza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Tempio Ossario di Timau

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Near Timau, he guards the remains of 1763 Italian and Austro-Hungarian soldiers.

  • Where Timau, Paluzza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Trincee del Pal Grande, Pal Piccolo e Freikofel

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Fortifications, trenches and walkways of the Great War on the Pal Grande, Pal Piccolo and Freikofel mountains, reachable by footpaths from Timau and the Passo di Monte Croce Carnico; in summer guided tours.

  • Where Timau e passo di Monte Croce Carnico, Paluzza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Lago Avostanis e Malga Pramosio

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the parking lot of Casera Pramosio (1521 m), reached by Laipacco on the dirt road, the CAI 402 trail leads in about two hours to the lake, next to the Casera Pramosio Alta refuge (1940 m). Along the way the Morgante-Casera Malpasso refuge, an ancient medieval silver mine, quarries disused with marmots, the tombstone of the Carnic carrier Maria Plozner Mentil and remains of military posts.

  • Where Laipacco, Paluzza (strada per Malga Pramosio)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: italia.it · turismofvg.it
