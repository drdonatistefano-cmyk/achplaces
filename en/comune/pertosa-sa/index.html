What to see in Pertosa

Country at the foot of the Alburni Mountains known for the Pertosa-Auletta Caves, karst complex crossed by the underground river Negro that is visited in part by boat, and for the integrated Museums of the environment (MIdA) dedicated to karstism, archeology of the caves and soil.

Grotte di Pertosa-Auletta

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Karst complex in the Alburni massif, on the left of the Tanagro, which occupies the path of an ancient underground river with caves and tunnels for over 2 km. In 1897 there were found remains of two stilts and objects from the Neolithic to the early Iron Age; there is also a newsstand dedicated to St. Michael Archangel. Open to the public since 1932, they visit with a stretch of boat on the Negro river and then on foot between the Wonder Hall, the Throne Hall and the Grand Cave; the route is suitable for all ages. Admission fee, reduced with Touring card; Touring indicates Tuesday-Sunday 10-12 and 14-18, which may vary.

MIdA - Musei integrati dell'ambiente

Museums · Themed

Managed by the MIdA Foundation, in two sections. The geo-spele-archaeological one (MIdA 01) tells the karst and the underground environment with finds of the Caves of the Angel, reconstructions of caves in real scale and a multimedia path that starts with a virtual elevator. The agri-environmental one (MIdA 02, Museo del Sole) has live plants, historical and contemporary herbal plants with 800 exposed leaves, collections of soils, seeds, woods and bulbs and an open-air historical orchard.

  • Where Piazza De Marco (MIdA 01) e località Muraglione (MIdA 02), Pertosa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.pertosa.sa.it · touringclub.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Cited in the ecclesiastical documents from the 13th century and renovated several times, it mixes Romanesque, Renaissance and Baroque elements: facade plastered with sailing bell tower, internal nave with marble side altars. On the main altar the statue of Our Lady of Graces, from the 17th to the 18th century, celebrated during the patron saint's feast.

  • Where Via Santa Maria delle Grazie, Pertosa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.touringclub.it

Sources: comune.pertosa.sa.it · touringclub.it
