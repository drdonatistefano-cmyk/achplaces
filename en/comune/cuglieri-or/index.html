What to see in Cuglieri

Montiferru country dominated by the basilica of Santa Maria della Neve, the first minor basilica of Sardinia, with the ruins of the castle of Casteddu Ezzu; on the coast the villages of Santa Caterina di Pittinuri, S'Archittu and Torre del Pozzo, the Punic and early Christian city of Cornus and the waterfalls at the sea of Capo Nieddu.

Basilica di Santa Maria della Neve

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill Bardosu, at the top of the village, the first Sardinian church to obtain the title of minor basilica: the current building is probably a reconstruction of the late seventeenth century on a sanctuary attested from the fifteenth century, with neo-Gothic façade of 1913 between two bell towers and bas-reliefs on the legend of the statue. Inside there is a single nave with four chapels on each side, dome on the presbytery, chestnut choir and the painted stone statue of the Madonna della Neve (XV century). It is always open; it hosts the rites of the Holy Week of the Rosary fraternity.

  • Where Colle Bardosu, Cuglieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the upper part of the village rustic houses, narrow alleys and noble palaces; From 1927 to 1971, Cuglieri hosted the Pontifical Theological Faculty, the first outside Rome. The other churches are visited during the festivities, open monuments and Holy Week.

Casteddu Ezzu (Castello del Montiferru)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On a basaltic hill just over 3 km from the village, along the road to Macomer: a court castle built by Ittcorra, brother of Judge Barison II, attested in 1196. In 1668 Countess Francesca Zatrillas took refuge before fleeing from Sardinia; shortly afterwards he was abandoned. There are still traces of the perimeter wall, watchtowers and underground, and from the top the view ranges to 360 degrees to the coast.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Antica città di Cornus e Columbaris

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Founded by the Carthaginians at the end of the 6th century BC on a nuragic settlement, it was razed to the ground for resisting in Rome and then became a early Christian episcopal center. The Punic phase remains walls and burials; on Columbaris hill, facing the sea, three basilica buildings, a small baptistery, stone sarcophagi still in situ, cappuccina tombs and canteens for the coolerium. It can be reached from the SS 292 to about 25 km, following the signs . Cornus-Columbaris.

  • Where Località Columbaris, SS 292, Cuglieri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Santa Caterina di Pittinuri e la sua torre

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Borgata marina di Cuglieri: beach in a cove of white limestones with golden, reddish and black pebbles and low sea suitable for children, frequented by windsurfers and kiter with maestral. On the promontory to the north is the 16th-century tower that gives its name to the resort; little further north is the wild stretch of Riu 'e sa Ide.

  • Where Santa Caterina di Pittinuri, Cuglieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

S'Archittu e le spiagge di Torre del Pozzo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 2 km from Santa Caterina, the village of S'Archittu takes its name from the arch of limestone rock almost 10 meters high, shaped by the maestral, with a small ochre beach. Near the beach Stella (the priests' beach, for the ancient summer seminar), the cove of Sa Capanna loved by those who snorkel and, on the promontory of the Whale, Torre del Pozzo.

  • Where S'Archittu, Cuglieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
