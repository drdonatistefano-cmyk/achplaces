What to see in Brugherio

The municipality of Briana was built to the east of Lambro, whose name comes from the "Brugo" shrub of the heathers; the parish church of San Bartolomeo houses the relics of the Magi Kings, preserved for centuries in the church of Sant'Ambrogio, and in Moncucco stands the Temple of San Lucio, Renaissance chapel brought here from Lugano.

Tempietto di San Lucio e Villa Andreani Sormani

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The villa of Moncucco, attributed to Giovanni Ruggeri and built in the first half of the eighteenth century on a land where there was a castle, has a U-shaped plan and a loggia of three arches on binated columns; from its park Paolo Andreani made in 1784 the first human flight in balloon. In the garden was rebuilt at the beginning of the nineteenth century the oratory of San Lucio, the Tempietto of Moncucco: Renaissance chapel of the first half of the sixteenth century coming from San Francesco in Lugano, carried stone after stone along the watercourses, with medallions in stone of Saltrio; in 1815-1816 Giocondo Albertolli added the Ionic pronao and the dome. The temple was restored between 1987 and 1994.

the villa is privately owned for residential use; the temple was opened with guided tours of the FAI Delegation of Monza

Chiesetta di Sant'Ambrogio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises in the Cascina Sant'Ambrogio, of origins dating back to the fourth century, which Sant'Ambrogio bought as a summer residence and left to the Church of Milan with the usufruct to his sister Marcellina, who lived in monastic form. From 1098 to 1613 the church customized the relics of the Magi Kings; inside two side frescoes depict Marcellina, Sant'Ambrogio and Satiro and the saints Sebastiano and Pope Fabiano.

Chiesa di San Bartolomeo e reliquie dei Magi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church of San Bartolomeo preserves, in a silver reliquary of the eighteenth century, the relics of the three Magi Kings, kept from 1098 to 1613 in the small church of Sant'Ambrogio.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Brugherio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · fondoambiente.it

Parco Increa

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

Park of about 33 hectares in the south-eastern part of the municipality, on the border with Cernusco, obtained from a sand and gravel quarry active since the sixties: the environmental recovery began in 1985 and in 1987 the area passed to the Municipality. The lake occupies just under half of the park; there are cycle paths and two parking lots.

  • Where Parco Increa, Brugherio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · in-lombardia.it
