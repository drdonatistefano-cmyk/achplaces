What to see in Frascati

The door of the Roman Castles overlooking Rome: the Tuscan villas of the popes and cardinals with Villa Aldobrandini on the head, the Stables with the Tuscolano Museum, the Cathedral of St. Peter and, above the city, the ruins of Tuscolo with the Roman theatre.

8 places to visit in Frascati, in the province of Rome (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa Aldobrandini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

The most famous patrician villa of Frascati, begun in 1598 by Giacomo Della Porta and completed around 1620 by Maderno and Fontana: frescoed rooms of the seventeenth century, central water lilies, chapel of San Sebastiano and park with fountains and terraces; called \"Belvedere" for the view of Rome. Park open on weekdays.

  • Where Via Cardinale G. Massaia 18, Frascati
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: touringclub.it · visitlazio.com

Scuderie Aldobrandini – Museo Tuscolano

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the seventeenth-century stables re-arranged by Fuksas: finds from prehistory to the Middle Ages, especially of republican and imperial age, the plastic of the Tuscan villas and sculptures related to the cult of Bacchus, including the "Dioniso Braschi" .

  • Where Piazza G. Marconi 6, Frascati
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: touringclub.it · visitlazio.com

Parco archeologico culturale di Tuscolo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ruins of the ancient Tusculum just outside the city, with the Roman theatre of 75 BC; a short distance from the Tuscolo Cross (677 m), with a view of the Castles and, on clear days, the Tyrrhenian Sea.

  • Where Tuscolo, Frascati
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · parcocastelliromani.it

Museums · Themed · Free entry
