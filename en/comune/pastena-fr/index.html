What to see in Pastena

Ancient Castellum Pastina in the Ausoni Mountains, closed by a walls of about 650 meters with fifteen surviving towers; in addition to the village with the collegiate church of Santa Maria Maggiore and the Museum of peasant and olive civilization, is known for the Pastena Caves, open to the public since 1927 and among the major caving complexes of Italy.

Grotte di Pastena

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry

Crossing caves almost a kilometer long, explored in 1926 by Baron Carlo Franchetti and open to tourists in 1927, on two levels: a lower active branch with underground river and a higher fossil branch rich in concretions. At the entrance there is a curtain of polychrome stalactites, then ten rooms: the Sala del Lago Blu, with a waterfall of about 10 meters in a deep lake 5, the Sala del Salice Ciangente and the Sala dei Bpistrelli, which still houses a colony. Guided tour only, in groups up to 12 people; summer (1 April-1 November) 9-19, winter 10-16; recommended booking on weekends, pets not allowed.

  • Where Via delle Grotte, Pastena
  • Entry €9, reduced €6 (6-12 years, over 65, schools), groups €7
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: comune.pastena.fr.it · parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com

Museo della civiltà contadina e dell'ulivo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Anthropological demoetanological museum opened in 1994 in the basement of the town house, a former mill built in 1879 by the Trani family. More than a thousand objects in thirteen rooms, from the Frantoio Room with the ancient animal-tracted millstone to the Mestieri Room, the bedroom and the Wheat Processing Room, tell the last two centuries of agriculture and breeding. The MiC card indicates opening on Tuesday-Sunday 10-16, closed on 25 December and 1 January; tel. 0776 546006.

  • Where Via Porta Napoli, Pastena
  • Entry €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Mura, porte e Piazza del Maggio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village is closed by a defensive belt of about 650 meters with round and square towers: of the original 25 remain 15, testimony of the ancient Castellum Pastina. You enter from Porta Roma and Porta Napoli; the main square, Piazza del Maggio, is in the highest part of the village and there is the birthplace of Nino Manfredi, which houses photographic exhibitions and a school of theatre. Narrow paved streets, stone houses with wrought iron balconies; the paved road is irregular.

  • Where Piazza del Maggio, Pastena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · comune.pastena.fr.it
