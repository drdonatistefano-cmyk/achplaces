What to see in Brentonico

The capital of the plateau on the Trentino side of Monte Baldo, in the Natural Park, called by the botanists ...Giardino d'Italia...: Palazzo Eccheli Baisi with the Fossil Museum and the Botanical Garden, the church of Saints Peter and Paul with the Romanesque crypt, the Zeni Mulino alle Sorne and paths through flowery meadows and trenches of the Great War.

9 places to visit in Brentonico, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo del fossile del Monte Baldo

Museums · Science and university

In Palazzo Eccheli Baisi, a noble residence between the Five and the seventeenth century next to the archpriest's church, preserves more than 1,000 fossils collected on Mount Baldo northern (collezione Osvaldo Giovanazzi), which document the marine animals that succeeded in 150-180 million years after the Triassic, from protozoa to crustaceans; outstanding for the gasteropods of the Sorne field, with 134 classified species. The palace is also linked to the mines of Baldo's Claudonite, from which the Brentonic Green pigment.

  • Where Via Mantova 6, Brentonico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitrovereto.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · visitrovereto.it

Giardino botanico di Palazzo Eccheli Baisi

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

About 3,500 square meters dedicated to the flora of Monte Baldo, which counts 1,655 varieties of plants and flowers: geometric flowerbeds, the Garden of the simple and an area of spontaneous species. Guided tours also include the halls of the palace and the fossil museum.

  • Where Via Mantova 6, Brentonico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitrovereto.it

Sources: visitrovereto.it · visittrentino.info

Chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo e cripta di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the district of Vigo, built in the second half of the sixteenth century: under the presbytery opens, through a hatch, a Romanesque crypt of the tenth century. Inside six altars in local marble and a seventeenth-century organ by Giovanni Bertè.

  • Where Vigo, Brentonico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitrovereto.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · visitrovereto.it

Chiesa di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Baroque church in the district of Lera, rebuilt in the seventeenth century for a vow linked to the plague of 1630; it has three altars in local marble and the altarpiece of the Addolorata of Biagio Falcieri. Around, the historic districts of Fontana, Lera, Vigo and Fontechel with stone houses and rural architectures.

  • Where Contrada Lera, Brentonico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitrovereto.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · visitrovereto.it

Mulino Zeni

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Mill of 1806 in the valley of the Sorna, among the few traditional mills still working in the region: a large wheel with boxes and a smaller one in iron, used to grind feed for poultry farming. It offers educational and demonstration visits.

  • Where Sorne, Brentonico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitrovereto.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · visitrovereto.it

Altopiano di Brentonico e Monte Baldo

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Highland on the Trentino side of Mount Baldo, between Adige and Garda, in the Natural Park: paths in the woods with lake view, alpine pastures where you can taste cheeses, the trail of the Regole on the ancient medieval paths and the wild Val del Parol, rich in flowers. In the spring, a tour goes from Polsa to Bocca d'Ardole and the Horn of Fear to Monte Vignola; in winter you can ski in Polsa, San Valentino and San Giacomo.

  • Where Polsa, San Valentino, San Giacomo, Brentonico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitrovereto.it

Sources: visitrovereto.it · visittrentino.info

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Castione e i suoi castagneti

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry

Birthplace of the sculptor and architect Cristoforo Benedetti (1657-1740), author of works in the Cathedral of Trento, in the sanctuary of the Madonna del Monte in Rovereto and the Column of St. Anne in Innsbruck. It is known for chestnuts, protagonists in autumn of festivals and menus; chestnuts are also visited in spring with local guides.

  • Where Castione, Brentonico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitrovereto.it
