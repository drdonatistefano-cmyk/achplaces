What to see in Cerveteri

The Etruscan Caere: the necropolis of the Banditaccia excavated in tuff, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2004 with Tarquinia, the national museum in the castle of the Ruspoli with the crater of Eufronio, and in the surroundings the village of Ceri and the waterfalls of the Ceriti mountains.

8 places to visit in Cerveteri, in the province of Rome (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Necropoli della Banditaccia

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Etruscan necropolis excavated in tuff (IX-III century BC), along the Via Sepolcrale that descends into the valley as Via degli Inferi: mounds and graves like those of the Capanna, the Cornice, the Capitelli, the Greek vases and the Reliefs, with 13 niches in painted stucco.

  • Where Cerveteri
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.visitlazio.com
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: visitlazio.com · pact.cultura.gov.it

Museo archeologico nazionale Cerite

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

In the thirteenth-century castle of the Ruspolis, on two floors: urns of the necropolis of the Sorbo, orientalizing kits and the crater of Eufronio (about 515 BC) and the kylix of Onesimos, returned to Italy in 2015.

  • Where Castello Ruspoli, Piazza Santa Maria, Cerveteri
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Palazzo Ruspoli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Historic residence on Piazza Santa Maria, frequented by Händel, with a Salone delle Feste of over 200 square meters; visits from Friday to Sunday, in week by reservation.

  • Where Piazza Santa Maria, Cerveteri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The oldest church in the city, built on an Etruscan temple.

  • Where Piazza Santa Maria, Cerveteri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Among the oldest churches: a large wooden statue attributed to the Bernini school, frescoes attributed to Lorenzo da Viterbo and Umbrian frescoes of the fourteenth century.

  • Where Cerveteri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Ceri e Palazzo Torlonia

History and architecture · Villages · Viewpoint

The village of Ceri with the Palazzo Torlonia (Ceri Castle), still inhabited, frescoed rooms and hanging garden Italian with citrus grove; visits by appointment or during events.

  • Where Ceri, Cerveteri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Borgo del Sasso e Palazzo Patrizi

History and architecture · Villages · Viewpoint

Fortified village of the early sixteenth century around a thirteenth-century tower, with terrace garden on the Tyrrhenian Sea; open especially for events.

  • Where Sasso, Cerveteri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Cascate dei monti Ceriti

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Five waterfalls (Vaccinello or Cascata del Moro, Arenile, Braccio di Mare, Ospedaletto and Mola) reachable with easy paths suitable for children from the new cemetery of Cerveteri.

  • Where Cerveteri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
