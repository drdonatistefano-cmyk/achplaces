What to see in Carpineto Romano

Borgo dei Monti Lepini from the historic centre, mostly medieval, home to Pope Leo XIII (Gioacchino Pecci), which enriched it with restorations, churches and fountains by Michele Tripisciano; to see the Palazzo Pecci, the Palazzo Aldobrandini with the civic tower and the convents of Sant'Agostino and San Pietro.

Palazzo Pecci, casa natale di Leone XIII

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between the 12th and the 18th century at the highest point of the hill, in the part of the "Dammonte" is the birthplace of Pope Leo XIII. The north side is medieval and belonged to the castle De Ceccano; the works of the architect Augusto Bonanni have preserved the appearance of the buildings of the late sixteenth-early seventeenth century. In front, in Piazza Giuseppe Pecci, the Tripisciano fountain with three dolphins and an inscription in Latin disticoli.

Palazzo Aldobrandini e Torre campanaria

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

On the cliff under the tower, preceded by a large garden with centuries-old plants, it was the seat of the baronial power of the Duchy of Carpineto and in the seventeenth century it was bought by the Municipality; there was transcribed the act of peace of 1477 between Carpineto and the neighboring communities. Today it hosts the museum "Reggia dei Volsci" (Flights Reggia). The tower, part of the medieval castrum, has a sixteenth-century clock by master Caesar from Subiaco and a 17th-century majolica exhibition.

Piazza Regina Margherita, Collegiata e Fontana del Tripisciano

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

On the square the Collegiate designed by Domenico Schiero (1750-1767), with mosaic apse of the Sacred Heart (C. De Angelis, 1950), a altarpiece of the Flagellation attributed to Giulio Romano and a bell of 1247 from the abbey of Valvisciolo; in front of the monumental fountain of Michele Tripisciano, inaugurated in 1890 with the arrival of the water of Mount Carpino wanted by Leo XIII, with dolphins, cups supported by telamoni and a lion.

Chiesa e convento di San Pietro Apostolo

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry

Built in 1612-1628 by Cardinal Pietro Aldobrandini for the Franciscans of the Reformation and restored by Leo XIII. It preserves seventeenth-century sculptures by Fra' Stefano da Piazza Armerina, the "Stimmate di San Francesco" attributed to Simon Vouet and the "Immaculate" of Fr. Diego da Tavello; in the cloister lunettes frescoed by Serbuccio da Tivoli with the life of San Francesco (stems of 1685).

  • Where Via San Pietro, Carpineto Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Chiesa e convento di Sant'Agostino

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry

12th century monastery, before the Antonians and since the 14th century Augustinian; abandoned in the Napoleonic age, it was restored by Leo XIII on a project by A. Bonanni and A. Camaiti. Two Cistercian protire with stylophorous lions, frescoes in T's basin. Troja (1888), wooden choir and a Gothic organ donated to Leo XIII by the diocese of San Gallo; in the convent the Ritiana art gallery.

  • Where Via Sant'Agostino, Carpineto Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page jubileum.cittametropolitanaroma.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria del Popolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

15th century national monument, built under Sixtus IV as a former vote against the plague, with five fornics narthex. To the altars a votive picture for the plague of 1657, a fresco in memory of the battle of Lepanto (1592), a sixteenth-century Crucifix and a marble group of thirteen figures of Saints Rocco and Sebastiano (16th century).

Chiesa di San Leone Magno

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Neoclassical church erected in 1888 by Leo XIII on a project by F. Fontana, with the Madonna del Rosario by V. Monti and at the altar a marble copy of Sosnowski's Pietà.
