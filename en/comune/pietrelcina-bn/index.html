What to see in Pietrelcina

The birthplace of Padre Pio, mentioned in the documents since 1133: the house where he was born in 1887, the convent of the Capuchins with the museum, the churches of St. Anne and St. Mary of the Angels and, little to the north, the place of the first stigmata; every year in December the nativity scene.

5 places to visit in Pietrelcina, in the province of Benevento (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Convento dei Cappuccini e Museo Padre Pio

Museums · Themed

The convent of the Capuchins, the order of Father Pio, was completed in 1928 on a place chosen by the saint and houses a small museum of objects of his life; the path tells his biography, even in difficult times, and exhibits paintings of Francesco Gentile on the landscapes of his history. Open every day 9-13 and 15-19.

  • Where Convento dei Cappuccini, Pietrelcina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it

Casa natale di Padre Pio

Museums · House museums

The house where Francesco Forgione was born in 1887, then Padre Pio, ordained a priest in 1910 in the Cathedral of Benevento and proclaimed a saint in 2002.

  • Where Viale Cappuccini, Pietrelcina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt after the earthquake of 1688, the patroness Madonna della Libera was venerated there; here Padre Pio celebrated his first mass.

  • Where Pietrelcina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Anna

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt after the earthquake of 1688, it preserves the relics of another Saint Pius donated by the Carafa family at the beginning of the nineteenth century.

  • Where Pietrelcina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Luogo delle prime stimmate (Pietra Romana)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Locality just north of the country where, according to tradition, the saint received the first manifestation of stigmata; today it is a destination of pilgrimage.

  • Where Pietra Romana, Pietrelcina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
