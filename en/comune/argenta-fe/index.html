What to see in Argenta

4 places to visit in Argenta, in the province of Ferrara (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Argenta in the guide with map and filters

Valli di Argenta e Oasi di Campotto

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry

About 600 hectares of wetlands of fresh water and 100 hectares of forest, with one of the largest colonies of cormorants of Italy; the Museo delle Valli in the Casino di Campotto tells about the reclamation.

  • Where Via Cardinala, Campotto di Argenta
  • Entry Guided tours for a fee
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Santuario della Beata Vergine della Celletta

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Sanctuary designed by Giovan Battista Aleotti, remained intact in the earthquake of 1624 that destroyed Argenta.

  • Where Argenta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Delizia di Benvignante

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage

Extensive delights owned by the Municipality, with three hectares of park, along the Ring of the Borgia by bike.

Ecomuseo di Argenta: Museo della Bonifica e Museo Civico

Museums · Themed

The Liberty Reclamation Pumps Room, a monument of industrial archaeology, and the Civic Museum in the church of San Domenico with Scarsellino and Garofalo.

  • Where Argenta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: parcodeltapo.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
