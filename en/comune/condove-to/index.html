What to see in Condove

Municipality of the lower Val di Susa at the entrance of the valley, where remain the ruins of the castle of the Count Verde (Château of Caprie) linked to the Longobard Churches; on the mountain of the valley of Grave, between Mocchie and Frassinere, the ethnographic museum . La Ghindana , the rock engravings and the parish church of Santo Stefano, while in the former Officine Moncenisio is located the Museo valsusino della Resistance.

Museo etnografico «La Ghindana»

Museums · Themed

Ethnographic collection of Mocchie set up in a museum form, presented by the historian Luca Patria: it tells the material culture of the mountain community of the valley of Grave, with a special attention to dairy production and alpine life, but also to mining activities, weaving, mills and other protoindustrial structures. Not indicated hours: information to the Municipality (tel. 011 9643102).

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Museo valsusino della Resistenza

Museums · City history

It is located in the premises of the former Officine Moncenisio and exhibits relics, finds and photographic and graphic material donated by private and institutions, including documents on workshops during the war and on the arrival of the Allies. Library and media library preserve the audiovisual archive of the competition Historical Memory of the Valsusa Filmfest and the maps of the "Sentieri della memoria" on the mountain of Condove. Opening by reservation.

  • Where Via Torino 36, Condove
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.condove.to.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.condove.to.it

Castello del Conte Verde (resti)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ruins on a relief that dominates the entrance of the valley between Pirchiriano and Caprasio, near the Dora Riparia: the first defensive traces would date back to the clash between Charlemagne and Desire alle Chiuse (771-774), while the castle of Caprie is mentioned from the thirteenth century as the center of an abbey castle, seat of court and prison. There remain perimeter walls, a tower, a seventeenth-century chapel of the Assumption and a large erratic boulder with an inscription on the passage of Charlemagne. Parking a few meters and short walk up.

  • Where Via Conte Verde 52, Condove (SS 24)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.condove.to.it

Sources: comune.condove.to.it

Incisioni rupestri di Buia e Siliodo-Camporossetto

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the locality of Buia, a mass with 13 cups, a cross, canalets, two dates (1630 and 1914), a square basin and two half-livings, perhaps the "Petra Perforata" mentioned by sources of the fourteenth century; in Siliodo-Camporossetto, about 950 m, the "Roccia degli Asterischi" is the rock of the Asterischi, a mass of snakes with a large number of sharpeners, considered lucky charm, and near two other rocks, one with about 150 sharpeners. Dark is reached on foot by mule track from the bottom of Via Generale Pezzana; Siloid from SP 200.

  • Where Via Generale Armando Pezzana (Case Bodrola), Condove; Siliodo-Camporossetto lungo la SP 200
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.condove.to.it

Sources: comune.condove.to.it

Chiesa di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient Romanesque parish church of the 12th century next to the old cemetery, dedicated first to Santa Maria del Prato, then to San Pietro and finally to San Rocco; several times renovated for the Rio della Rossa, which buried the first floor of the bell tower, had the orientation reversed before 1643. From the excavations fragments of the fresco of the triumphal arch and the ancient apse have emerged, perhaps the oldest in the Val di Susa.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.condove.to.it

Sources: comune.condove.to.it

Parrocchiale di San Pietro in Vincoli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Modern church of the Sixties designed by Carlo Siffredi, with internal walls decorated by Luciano Bartoli; the bell tower, begun in 1929 by Corrado Meano, is inspired by that of San Giorgio in Venice. In the baptistery there is a paleo-Christian marble relief with two deer under a vine branch; there is also the seventeenth-century painting of the Virgin of the Suffragio from San Rocco.

  • Where Via Roma 4, Condove
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.condove.to.it

Sources: comune.condove.to.it

Chiesa di Santo Stefano di Frassinere

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

The parish church of the Frassinere hamlet at 948 m, built on a Romanesque building of which remains the bell tower, very renovated; enlarged between 1744 and 1749 and rebuilt in 1862, has three naves and semicircular apse, dimensions due to the fact that Frassinere was autonomous municipality until 1936. Used again in the summer months.

  • Where Località Frassinere 1, Condove (SP 200)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.condove.to.it
