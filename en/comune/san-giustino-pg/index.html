What to see in San Giustino

Borgo dell'alta valle del Tibere on the border with Tuscany: the Bufalini Castle with the Italian garden and the bosso maze, the 17th century villa of Celalba with the finds of the Villa of Plinio il Giovane, the tobacco museum and the memory of the Republic of Cospaia.

4 places to visit in San Giustino, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Bufalini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortilizio dei Dotti destroyed by the Florentines and rebuilt after 1487 for Niccolò Bufalini, transformed into a noble dwelling in the 1930s; rooms decorated by Cristofano Gherardi, the Galleria delle Donne Forti, the moat and the Italian garden with rose garden, green gallery, lemon house and one of the rare historical bosso labyrinths. Open Friday, Saturday, Sunday and holidays.

  • Where Largo Crociani 3, San Giustino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Villa Magherini Graziani e Museo della villa di Plinio

Museums · Archaeological

Ancient castle transformed in 1616 into a late Renaissance villa designed by Antonio Cantagallina, with loggia and Italian gardens; it houses the archaeological museum with the excavations of the villa in Tuscis of Plinio il Giovane.

  • Where Celalba, San Giustino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Museo storico e scientifico del tabacco

Museums · Themed

Opened in 2004 at the former Consorzio Tabaccoltori near the station: cultivation, care in the towers, manufacture and social history of the tobacconists of the high valley of the Tiber.

  • Where Presso la stazione, San Giustino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Cospaia e Sentiero dei Contrabbandieri

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

From 1440 to 1826 a strip of land born from a border error between the Papal State and Tuscany was an independent republic, where tobacco was cultivated from 1575; the Scroller Path crosses it remembering the times of smuggling.

  • Where Cospaia, San Giustino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it
