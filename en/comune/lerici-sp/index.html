What to see in Lerici

Municipality of the Gulf of Poets with the seaside village of Tellaro, among the most beautiful villages in Italy, the castle of San Giorgio, Palazzo Doria and the houses of Shelley and artists.

7 places to visit in Lerici, in the province of La Spezia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Tellaro e chiesa di San Giorgio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Marinar village on the cliffs; the sixteenth-century church of San Giorgio stands directly on the sea, from which starts a walk between houses and cliffs.

  • Where Tellaro, Lerici
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Castello di San Giorgio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Palazzo Doria e piazza Garibaldi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The palace where in 1528 Andrea Doria, today museum and exhibition space, took refuge, on the square between castle and port.

Alta Via del Golfo e sentiero di Portesone e Barbazzano

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

From Tellaro a path between the olive groves rises to the abandoned villages of Portesone and Barbazzano up to Lerici; the Alta Via del Golfo, more challenging, crosses the Caprone towards Zanego and Montemarcello.

  • Where Tellaro, Lerici
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it
