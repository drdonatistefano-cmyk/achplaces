Piazza San Benedetto

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The centrepiece of the urban system was already in the early Middle Ages, stratified by the 14th century Gothic to the nineteenth-century renovation, with the Town Hall, the Basilica of San Benedetto, the cathedral and Castellina.

  • Where Piazza San Benedetto, Norcia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it · touringclub.it
