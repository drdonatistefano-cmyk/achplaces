What to see in Castelbianco

Municipality of Pennavaira valley whose village of Colletta, among the most beautiful villages in Italy, is reborn from the ruins of the earthquake as a medieval village telematic on a project by Giancarlo De Carlo.

5 places to visit in Castelbianco, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Castelbianco in the guide with map and filters

Colletta di Castelbianco

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Stone village recovered from the almost total ruin by Giancarlo De Carlo as one of the first telematic medieval villages in Europe: caruggi, piazzette, terraces and white windows, with views of the valley.

  • Where Colletta, Castelbianco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
