What to see in Rende

Città dell'Università della Calabria along the Crati, with the medieval historic centre on the hill called "Borgo dei Musei": the Norman castle of 1095, the churches of Santa Maria Maggiore and the Rosary and art museums from the sixteenth century to the contemporary.

7 places to visit in Rende, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico, il Borgo dei Musei

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill preserves the medieval system and the ancient names of the streets: Piazza degli Eroi ("u sieggiu") the Door of Cosenza on the road to Catanzaro and Giudecca with narrow alleys and arches.

  • Where Centro storico, Rende
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello Normanno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The Gigante di Pietra is built in 1095 on a naturally defended hill; the two side towers are in the city coat of arms since 1222 and in the atrium you can see the coats of arms of the Magdalone and the Alarçon de Mendoza.

  • Where Centro storico, Rende
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The 12th century Latin cross church and three naves, with the original rose window on the portal, restored after various earthquakes; paintings by Cristoforo Santanna, Giuseppe Pascaletti and Giuseppe Grana.

  • Where Centro storico, Rende
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa del Rosario

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Baroque and Rococo church of 1679 by the De Bortolo brothers, with facade in tuff of Mendicino, marble altar and inlaid wood, nativity scene with Neapolitan statues and organ of the eighteenth century.

  • Where Centro storico, Rende
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Civico – Palazzo Zagarese

Museums · Old masters

In the seventeenth century Palazzo Zagarese, about 3,000 objects on the civil history of Calabria and paintings from the sixteenth century to today, with works by Priests, Solimena, Carrà, Balla, De Chirico, Guttuso and Sironi.

  • Where Centro storico, Rende
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

MAON – Museo d'Arte dell'Otto e Novecento

Museums · Modern art

In Palazzo Vitari, a permanent collection of Otto e Novecento dedicated to Calabria and the South, with an archive of works, posters, catalogues, photographs and videos.

  • Where Palazzo Vitari, Rende
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo del Presente

Museums · Modern art

Space of about 3,500 square meters for temporary exhibitions of modern and contemporary art, photography, performances and presentations.

  • Where Roges, Rende
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
