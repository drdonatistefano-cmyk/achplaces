What to see in Villetta Barrea

Country of the Abruzzo National Park on Marsicana, with stone houses, squares, a medieval tower, the Transhumance Museum and the Water Museum in the ancient mill; overlooking the basin of Lake Barrea.

8 places to visit in Villetta Barrea, in the province of L'Aquila (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Villetta Barrea in the guide with map and filters

Museo della transumanza

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the "Baraccone" , rural building nineteenth century bought by the Municipality in 1996, panels and an audiovisual tell the transhumance: life and work of the shepherds, wool and cheese, temporary equipment and shelters; educational area and workshop of products related to transhumance, laboratory by reservation.

discordant times: MiC every day 17-20, Municipality only July and August

  • Where Via Roma, Villetta Barrea
  • Entry €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villettabarrea.aq.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.villettabarrea.aq.it

Museo dell'acqua

Museums · Themed

In the ancient mill mentioned in the second half of the fifteenth century and active until the 1950s, next to the hydroelectric plant inaugurated in 1910: life-size reconstruction of the mill with hopper and original millstones, section on wool and transhumance, route on wetlands and lakes and river diorama with rare aquatic animals of the Park. Open on weekends and holidays, every day in July and August.

  • Where SP 59, Villetta Barrea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villettabarrea.aq.it

Sources: comune.villettabarrea.aq.it

Torre medievale e museo della parrocchiale

History and architecture · Towers

A circular tower probably in the mid-15th century, restored in the nineties, which houses the museum of the old parish church of Santa Maria Assunta destroyed by the earthquake of 1915: furnishings of the church, sculptures and sacred vestments of over three centuries.

open only in summer (July Saturday and Sunday, August every day, 16:30-19:30)

  • Where Centro storico, Villetta Barrea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villettabarrea.aq.it

Sources: comune.villettabarrea.aq.it

Centro storico e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Stone houses and squares along the Marsicana: Palazzo Fontana (about 1700, lowered after the earthquake of 1915), with the stone fountain of 1925, Palazzo D'Orazio, eighteenth century and municipal seat, Leonardo Dorotea square with the former town hall and the birthplace of the pastor poet Benedetto Virgilio (1600).

  • Where Piazza Leonardo Dorotea, Villetta Barrea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villettabarrea.aq.it

Sources: comune.villettabarrea.aq.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

New parish church started on 13 June 1926 after the earthquakes of 1901 and 1915 to the old church, of which the ruins remain (cited already in 1447); it houses the relics of San Vincenzo martyr, arrived in 1778 from the catacombs of Rome.

  • Where Villetta Barrea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villettabarrea.aq.it

Sources: comune.villettabarrea.aq.it

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small church in the cemetery, on the site of the monastery of Sant'Angelo in Barreggio founded around the middle of the eighth century and damaged by the anguar raids of 937; it preserves a sixteenth-century frescoed ciborium.

  • Where Cimitero, Villetta Barrea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villettabarrea.aq.it

Sources: comune.villettabarrea.aq.it

Chiesa di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Cited in an inventory of 1613, home from 1838 to about 1960 of a fraternity of good death; it preserves an organ of 1834.

  • Where Villetta Barrea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villettabarrea.aq.it

Sources: comune.villettabarrea.aq.it

Lago di Barrea

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lake basin, which takes its name from the nearby village of Barrea, includes Villetta Barrea and Civitella Alfedena; paths and bike paths run along it and there is a small beach, La Gravara. Some excursions are only for experienced hikers; dogs on a leash.

  • Where Lago di Barrea, Villetta Barrea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villettabarrea.aq.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.villettabarrea.aq.it
