What to see in Bivongi

Village of the valley of Stilaro nestled in Mount Consolino: the Marmarico waterfalls, the highest in Calabria, the Norman-Byzantine monastery of San Giovanni Theristis and the mills and spas of an ancient industrial valley.

6 places to visit in Bivongi, in the province of Reggio Calabria (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Bivongi in the guide with map and filters

Cascata del Marmarico

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the high course of the Stilaro river, in the Folea valley: the highest water jump in Calabria and the southern Apennines, reachable by a trip that ends with a bath.

  • Where Bivongi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Monastero di San Giovanni Theristis

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Jewel of Norman-Byzantine art in Calabria, seat of Greek-Orthodox monasticism; near remain the remains of the Grange of the Apostles.

  • Where Bivongi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
