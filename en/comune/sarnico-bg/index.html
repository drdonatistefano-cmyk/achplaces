What to see in Sarnico

A small town on the Bergamo shore of Lake Iseo, where the lake becomes the Oglio river, with one of the longest lakes in Sebino, the medieval centre of the Contrada, the Liberty villas by Giuseppe Sommaruga and the Gianni Bellini art gallery in Palazzo Gervasoni.

8 places to visit in Sarnico, in the province of Bergamo (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Lungolago di Sarnico

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the longest and most panoramic walks of Lake Iseo: from the panoramic staircase to the bridge of Via Roma, which connects Sarnico to Paratico, to the public gardens of Lazzarini near the historic center; the Garibaldi lakefront hosts Christmas markets.

  • Where Lungolago Garibaldi, Sarnico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it

Contrada, il centro medievale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The medieval nucleus has a semicircle shape with its backs to the mountain, on the path of the ancient walls: via Lantieri with shops and architectural remains, via Scaletta that goes down to the lake, the alley Aie with the portico building that was a town hall until the eighteenth century and the civic tower of via Paris of the twelfth century.

  • Where Via Lantieri, Sarnico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Museo civico d'arte e del territorio Gianni Bellini

Museums · Old masters · Free entry

Opened in 2002 in Palazzo Gervasoni, fifteenth-century building and former convent with cloister in the highest point of the historic center. On three floors about 150 works donated by Don Gianni Bellini, especially painted from the sixteenth to the eighteenth century of Lombard, Venetian, Emilian, Tuscan, Roman and Neapolitan schools: Palma il Giovane, Alessandro Magnasco, Carlo Ceresa, Antonio Cifrondi, Nicolas Regnier, Francesco Cairo, as well as statues, furniture and processional crucifixes.

  • Where Via San Paolo 8, Sarnico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitbergamo.net · comune.sarnico.bg.it · italia.it

Ville Liberty di Giuseppe Sommaruga

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

For the Faccanoni family the Milanese architect Giuseppe Sommaruga signed in Sarnico villa Pietro Faccanoni (1907, former spinning mill, irons by Alessandro Mazzucotelli and wood by Eugenio Quarti), villa Giuseppe Faccanoni (since 1907, with bas-reliefs by Ernesto Bazzaro and terrace garden open on the lake), villa Luigi Faccanoni today Villa Surre with high tower, the childhood nursery of 1912 and the mausoleum Faccanoni in the cemetery, with sculptures by Ambrogio Pirovano.

  • Where Via Vittorio Veneto 56, Sarnico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · visitbergamo.net · italia.it

Chiesa di San Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Medieval church built in 1428 on the remains of the castle tower, next to Palazzo Gervasoni; its clock bell tower, rebuilt in the mid-nineteenth century, can be seen coming from the lake.

  • Where Via San Paolo, Sarnico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitbergamo.net · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Martino Vescovo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of the fifteenth century rebuilt in the eighteenth century, with baroque facade, frescoed ceilings and a wooden altar by Andrea Fantoni.

  • Where Sarnico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitbergamo.net

Lido Nettuno

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

Lido on the way to Predore with more than 40,000 m2 of greenery, sports fields and a walkway-solarium on the water; in the village also the Lido Fosio, free area of more than 10,000 m2.

  • Where Via Predore, Sarnico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Sentiero Sarnico-Monte Bronzone-Monte Gombo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring that starts after the monumental cemetery following the signs for the Chapel of the Alps: climb to the Forcella, with views of Sarnico and the Basso Sebino, then to the Colle Cambline and among chestnuts, birch and agrifogli to the top of the Bronzone (1.334 m), with panoramic view of the lake, Monte Isola, Orobie and Pianura Padana; return from Mount Gombo, site of prehistoric finds.

  • Where Partenza dal cimitero di Sarnico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it
