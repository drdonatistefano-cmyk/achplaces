What to see in Gualdo Tadino

Ceramic city at the foot of the Apennines, dominated by the Fortress Flea, rebuilt by Frederick II in 1247 and today the Civic Museum with the polyptych of the pupil; in the center the cathedral of St. Benedict with the large rose window, the Civic Tower, the Museum of Emigration

Rocca Flea e Museo civico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the hill of San Michele Arcangelo, the place of origin of the city, it was one of the most important fortresses of Umbria: built in the twelfth century and rebuilt by Frederick II in 1247, in the middle of the sixteenth century it became residence of the papal legates and in 1888 prison, before the recent restoration. The Civic Museum has three sections: archaeology from prehistory to the early Middle Ages, ceramics above all nineteenth century inspired by the sixteenth-century majolica of Gubbio and Deruta, and picture gallery with the great polyptych of Niccolò di Liberatore called the pupil (1471), by San Francesco, and fifteenth-century works by Matteo da Gualdo. It is part of the museum's unique ticket (€6). Info: tel. 075 916078.

Cattedrale di San Benedetto

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Benedictine Abbey founded in 1006 by Count Offredo outside the walls and transferred to the city in 1256, as recalls a tombstone on the left side; cathedral since 1915. Late-Romanesque-Gothic façade with three portals and a large double crowned rose window of tortile columns, one of the most beautiful in the region; on the right side a sixteenth-century fountain. Inside the nineteenth century on a project by Virginio Vespignani (1875), with three naves with matrons, altar greater than the end of the fourteenth century with bas-reliefs of the stories of San Benedetto attributed to William Ciani and wall paintings by Ulysses Ribustini (1907-1924).

  • Where Piazza maggiore, Gualdo Tadino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Museo regionale dell'emigrazione «Pietro Conti»

Museums · Themed

In the Palazzo del Podestà, entitled to Pietro Conti, the first president of the Umbria Region, is indicated as the first museum in Italy entirely dedicated to emigration: it tells with voices, sounds, images, documents and objects the Italian emigration from the late nineteenth century to the sixties, with a path backwards from the arrival to the journey to the departure. It is also a study and documentation centre with video library and library. It is part of the unique ticket of the museum (€6). Info: tel. 075 9142445.

  • Where Piazza del Soprammuro, Gualdo Tadino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.emigrazione.it

Museo Opificio Rubboli

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The workshop founded in 1878 by Paolo Ruboli, a Pesaro ceramist who arrived in Gualdo in 1875 to produce gold and ruby majolica, restored and transformed into a museum in 2015 by the last heir Maurizio Tittarelli Ruboli. About 150 majolica in lustro of the family from 1878 to the 1960s, four rooms on the production phases, the room of the majolica (indicated as the last surviving example in the world) and a contemporary section created for the Triennale of Ceramics in 2009. Open Friday-Sunday 10-13 and 15-18.

  • Where Centro storico, Gualdo Tadino
  • Entry €3, reduced €2; single ticket Polo museum €6
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website polomusealegualdotadino.it

Sources: polomusealegualdotadino.it · umbriatourism.it

Palazzo Comunale e Torre Civica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Palazzo Comunale, with a beautiful eighteenth-century façade, was built after the earthquake of 1751 destroyed the previous Palazzo dei Rectori delle Arti e dei Priori; the 12th-century Civic Tower is the only surviving part of the Palazzo del Podestà. In the center also Santa Maria di Tadino, the oldest church in the historic center, today seat of the Giochi de le Porte (revocation in the last week of September).

  • Where Centro storico, Gualdo Tadino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
