What to see in Vivaro

The village of the Magredi, among the graves of Cellina and Meduna at the foot of the Friulan Dolomites: arid prairies with rare flora, the Antiquarium of Tesis, the mill on the roggia and the turnaria dairy.

4 places to visit in Vivaro, in the province of Pordenone (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Magredi di Vivaro

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Arid meadows where you will encounter the graves of Cellina, Meduna and Colvera, a place of community importance: steppe vegetation with orchids, wild thyme, flax of fairies and the rare Tatarian Crambe, with views of the Friulian Dolomites; flat paths suitable for cycling and an observatory of flora and fauna.

  • Where Vivaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · ecomuseolisaganis.it

Antiquarium di Tesis

Museums · Archaeological

Finds of the plain and the pedemontana from the Bronze Age to the Roman and Middle Ages: bronze, ornaments, amulets, agricultural tools in iron, ceramics, cinerary and balsamary urns in glass, coins. It is part of the Lis Aganis Ecomuseum; times to ask the Cellina Meduna Archaeological Group.

  • Where Via della Roggia 1, Tesis di Vivaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Mulin da la Miuta

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Latteria turnaria di Tesis

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Typical stone building with a square view by the stonemasons, where to deepen the machining of cheese of the Magredi.
