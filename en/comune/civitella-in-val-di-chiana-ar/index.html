What to see in Civitella in Val di Chiana

A walled village between Val d'Ambra and Valdichiana, dominated by the fortress of Lombard origin destroyed in 1944, with the Porta Aretina, the 14th-century Palazzo Pretorio and the church of Santa Maria; it preserves the memory of the massacre of 1944 and a contemporary art gallery born from the Premio Città di Civitella. In the territory of Villa Oliveto, the museum of the vine and wine of Ciggiano and the reserve of Ponte a Buriano and Penna.

Rocca di Civitella

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Considered one of the best preserved pieces of the fortifications of Lombard origin, it stands on a hill between Val d'Ambra and Valdichiana, strategic point for the control of the valleys. The castle is a square-shaped castle with a mast on the western corner, a portal with an acute arch and low arched windows; in 1944 the German army damaged much of the country and the fortress was destroyed, hence its present appearance. The sources indicate different dates (VI-VII century, or 1200 at the behest of the bishop of Arezzo Guglielmino of Ubertini).

Borgo murato, Porta Aretina e Palazzo Pretorio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village is enclosed by a medieval wall of elliptical shape with square towers and the gateway called Aretina, connected to the fortress. Inside the Palazzo Pretorio of the fourteenth century and, in Piazza Lazzeri, a medieval cistern.

Galleria comunale d'arte contemporanea (Pinacoteca)

Museums · Modern art

It gathers the works of the artists of the national competition of painting "Premio Città di Civitella" ("Premio Città di Civitella Award"), founded in 1964 by Pro Loco for the cultural rebirth of the country after 1944, alternated in the seventies with the sculpture exhibition "S"Objectively. About 200 paintings (one hundred exhibited in the gallery, in a renovated former school) and almost 70 sculptures, of which 28 large permanently installed in the village; the oldest work is a black and white painting of 1971 by the American Ellisse Pogafsky Hakrs.

Museo del Sarapino

Museums · Themed

The Sala delle Lance retains the prizes won by the four districts in the editions of the Sarapino Giostra, a joke version of the Saracino di Arezzo born in the post-war period, in which the horse is replaced by an Ape car; also exhibited the costumes of the participants, the accessories of the carousel and works of the local craftsmanship.

  • Where Via Martiri di Civitella 13, Civitella in Val di Chiana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo della vite e del vino di Ciggiano

Museums · Themed

Opened in May 2009 in the old mill in the center of the country and managed by Pro Loco in agreement with the Municipality, it illustrates the phases of the vinification, from the grape harvest to the fermentation to the bottling, and exhibits traditional agricultural tools.

Villa Oliveto

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa on the hills that descend from Civitella, near Oliveto, with the current appearance due above all to a nineteenth century intervention: mills and cellars, noble floor and service floor, surrounded by a park of large trees and olive groves terraces. During the Second World War it was a concentration camp and today it hosts the Center of documentation on the concentration camp.
