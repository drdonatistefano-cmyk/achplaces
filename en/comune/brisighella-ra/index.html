What to see in Brisighella

Village of the three hills, between Rocca, Torre dell'Orologio and Sanctuary, in the heart of the Vena del Gesso.

5 places to visit in Brisighella, in the province of Ravenna (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Via degli Asini

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The medieval road is elevated and covered, illuminated by irregular arches, where once the birocciai with donkeys used to pass.

  • Where Centro storico, Brisighella
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Parco regionale della Vena del Gesso Romagnola

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

A 25 km long chalk dorsal with caves and dolines, part of the UNESCO site on karst in the evaporites.

  • Where Tra Brisighella, Riolo Terme, Casola Valsenio e Borgo Tossignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
