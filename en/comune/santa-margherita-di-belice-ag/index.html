What to see in Santa Margherita di Belice

The village of the Belice Valley linked to the Gattopardo: Palazzo Filangeri Cutò, where Giuseppe Tomasi of Lampedusa spent the summers of childhood and model Donnafugata, with the museum and literary park, the former Mother Church became Museum of Memory of the earthquake of 1968 and the ruins of the ancient country.

Palazzo Filangeri Cutò e Parco-Museo letterario del Gattopardo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The seventeenth-century palace of Corbera, passed to the Filangeri of Cutò, the maternal family of Giuseppe Tomasi of Lampedusa, who spent the summers from 1905 to 1914 and made it the model of the palace of Donnafugata; lost by the family due to debts and almost destroyed by the earthquake of 1968, which spared only the facade, was partly rebuilt and houses the Town Hall, the Teatro Sant'Alessandro and the museum. The museum exhibits the manuscript and typescript of the novel, letters, notes and vintage photos, multimedia posts, the unpublished 1956 recording of the writer reading Â"Lighea" and a small wax museum with a scene of the film by Luchino Visconti.

  • Where Piazza Giacomo Matteotti 1, Santa Margherita di Belice
  • Entry €6
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.santamargheritadibelice.ag.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo della Memoria

Museums · City history

In the former Mother Church, severely damaged by the earthquake of 1968 and rebuilt, it tells the night of the earthquake that destroyed in a few seconds the villages of the Belice Valley, and then the emergency, solidarity and protest: hundreds of photographs of the countries before and after the earthquake, literary quotations, paintings, audiovisual, newspapers and magazines of the time from private archives and collections.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
