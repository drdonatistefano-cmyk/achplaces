What to see in Castellammare di Stabia

The ancient Stabiae of the thermal waters and Roman villas on the Gulf of Naples, at the foot of Mount Faito: the Varano hill with Villa San Marco, the Reggia of Quisisana with the archaeological museum and the medieval castle.

8 places to visit in Castellammare di Stabia, in the province of Naples (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa San Marco – Scavi di Stabia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

On the Varano hill, where the open-air excavations began in 1950 with Libero D'Orsi: villa of about 11,000 square meters with Corinthian columns atrium, spa district, large swimming pool with exedra, frescoed reception rooms and upper garden with tortile columns. Stabiae was a place of holiday; in one of his villas Pliny the Elder died.

  • Where Collina di Varano, Castellammare di Stabia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it · pompeiisites.org

Reggia di Quisisana e Museo Archeologico di Stabia «Libero D'Orsi»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Angevin summer residence of the 13th century, rebuilt by Charles III of Bourbon between 1765 and 1790 with English gardens; restored until 2009, from 6 March 2024 it houses the museum with 507 finds from the villas established: murals, marbles, ceramics and bronzes.

  • Where Castellammare di Stabia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: pompeiisites.org · italia.it
