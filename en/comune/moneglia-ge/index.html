What to see in Moneglia

Borgo della Riviera di Levante among the most beautiful villages in Italy, narrow between the towers of San Giorgio and Santa Croce and dominated by the Genoese fortresses of Villafranca and Monleone.

6 places to visit in Moneglia, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Moneglia in the guide with map and filters

Chiesa di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Founded by the Benedictines in the fourteenth century, with Franciscan cloister of the fifteenth century, two Gothic polyptychs, a large canvas by Luca Cambiaso and a seventeenth-century altar in polychrome marble.

  • Where Moneglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

18th century church with mosaic church of pebbles, wooden sculptures, a Byzantine crucifix and fine marbles.

  • Where Moneglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Oratorio dei Disciplinanti

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The oldest building in Moneglia, probably from the 10th century, with frescoes from the 12th to the 18th century restored; on the same square the house of the poet Felice Romani.

  • Where Moneglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Fortezza di Villafranca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Genoese fortress of the twelfth century, destroyed in war and partly recovered, surrounded by a garden of Mediterranean plants; from the top the panorama of the village.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it
