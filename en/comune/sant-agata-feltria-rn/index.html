What to see in Sant'Agata Feltria

4 places to visit in Sant'Agata Feltria, in the province of Rimini (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Rocca Fregoso, Museo Rocca delle Fiabe

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The fortress on the "Wolfstone," retouched by Francesco di Giorgio Martini for Federico da Montefeltro in 1474, today's fairytale museum.

  • Where Sant'Agata Feltria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Teatro Angelo Mariani

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Small wooden theatre in the Palazzo Fregoso of 1603.

  • Where Palazzo Fregoso, Sant'Agata Feltria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Petrella Guidi, borgo semiabbandonato

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The small semi-abandoned agglomeration that preserves intact the structures of the village and medieval castle, with the fortress of the Tiberti of the XIII century.

Partly uninhabited village: you walk along the public streets, without entering the buildings

  • Where Petrella Guidi, Sant'Agata Feltria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · riminiturismo.it

Convento e chiesa di San Girolamo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The convent complex with the church of 1568, which at the main altar preserves the Madonna with the Saints Jerome and Cristina of Pietro da Cortona.

  • Where Sant'Agata Feltria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
