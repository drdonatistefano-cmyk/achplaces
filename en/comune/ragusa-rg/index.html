What to see in Ragusa

The Baroque town of Val di Noto, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2002: Ragusa Ibla with the Cathedral of San Giorgio di Rosario Gagliardi, the Ibleo Garden and dozens of churches; the cathedral of San Giovanni and the archaeological museum; the Castle of Donnafugata in the countryside.

10 places to visit in Ragusa, in the province of Ragusa (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ragusa Ibla

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The oldest district, rebuilt in Baroque style after the earthquake with the local stone and at least 50 churches; with the Baroque towns of Val di Noto is UNESCO heritage since 2002.

  • Where Ibla, Ragusa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Duomo di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Masterpiece of the Sicilian Baroque designed by Rosario Gagliardi in 1738 and completed in 1775, on top of a staircase: three-order facade and a neoclassical dome of 43 meters; next to the Duomo Museum with fragments of the church destroyed by the earthquake of 1693.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Ragusa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info

Cattedrale di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

The imposing cathedral of Ragusa Superiore, at the intersection of Via Roma and Corso Italia, with large facade, high bell tower and a panoramic terrace.

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint
