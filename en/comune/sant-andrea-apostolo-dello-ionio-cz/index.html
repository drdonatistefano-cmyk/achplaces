What to see in Sant'Andrea Apostolo dello Ionio

Municipality of the Nausicaa Riviera on the Gulf of Squillace, between sea, hills and mountains, with clear waters and beaches as intact as the stretch of granite sand between Fosso Cupid and Alaca stream; in the village the Palazzo Scoppa, ancient Carthusian crab, and the Museum of Memory.

Palazzo Scoppa e convento delle Suore Riparatrici

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

The palace, originally a grange of the Carthusians of the twelfth century, has a loggia on sixteen granite columns overlooking the Gulf of Squillace and preserves eighteenth-century majolica hand painted and the apartment of Baroness Enrichetta Scoppa with furniture of the second nineteenth century. It preserves the Pala degli Arcangeli (1603), of Michelangelo's school and attributed to Bernardino Poccetti, found in 2007; italia.it signals the chapel.

Spiaggia tra Fosso Cupido e torrente Alaca

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Over three kilometers of wide white granite sand beach, one of the few stretches of the Calabrian coast without intensive construction and not crossed by the railway, with spontaneous vegetation, wildlife and marine lilies; threatened by building projects, has been defended by the mobilization of the Places of the FAI Heart since 2006.

  • Where Litorale nord, Sant'Andrea Apostolo dello Ionio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Museo della memoria

Museums · Themed

Small museum of the material culture of the territory, with tools of the peasant civilization and reconstructions of environments of the rural house and artisan workshops.
