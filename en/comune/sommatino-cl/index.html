What to see in Sommatino

The city of the zolfare of the Nisseno, known as the town of sulphur until the 1960s, preserves the Museum of natural history and mining art and, towards Riesi, the Trabia-Tallarita Mine, one of the oldest Sicilian sulphites; in the center, grown around the Palazzo dei Principi of Trabia, the Civic Tower with the Liberty clock, Palazzo Di Bartolo with the artistic nativity scene and the ethno-anthropological museum.

Museo di storia naturale e arte mineraria

Museums · Themed

It consists of a geological section and a paleontological section dedicated to mining art, with drilling machines, lamps, masks and instruments for gas detection; also suitable for schoolchildren and families with children.

Miniera Trabia-Tallarita

History and architecture · Archaeology

One of Sicily's oldest sulphites, excavated perhaps from 1600, owned by the princes of Trabia and Butera; around 1920 it produced about 12% of the world's sulphur with about 3,000 miners. A fire in 1883 killed dozens of workers; the cable car of about 9,800 meters, of 1900, brought sulphur to Ravanusa. Closed in 1975, it was purchased by the Region between 2000 and 2003; it remains a stretch of cable car, castelletti and calcaroni, and in the center of Palladio has restored an interactive didactic path with simulated descent into the well, Tosi engines and the photographic exhibition "Sulfaro and Sulfatari" .

Museo etnoantropologico «Invito alla Storia»

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In the Palazzo Cigno-Constantino, probably built between the end of the seventeenth and early eighteenth centuries and already the residence of the princes of Trabia, exhibits finds of agriculture and handicrafts as a wooden press of the eighteenth century and a frame of the late eighteenth century, with the farmer's room and halls for exhibitions. Free admission.

  • Where Piazza Calogero Chinnici 4, Sommatino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: comune.sommatino.cl.it · visitsicily.info

Palazzo Di Bartolo e Mostra del Presepe artistico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The nineteenth-century building is surmounted by a tower, with halls decorated with paintings by the Basile school and original Art Nouveau furnishings; the ground floor opens onto a beam. It houses the permanent exhibition of the artistic nativity scene depicting Sommatino in miniatura.

Centro storico, Torre civica e Palazzo dei Principi di Trabia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The centre has grown around the Palazzo dei Principi di Trabia, lords of the territory since the mid-sixteenth century; the building, recently renovated and with free access, preserves volumes and books. Around the Civic Tower with Art Nouveau clock of 1848, the church of Santa Margherita with the wooden statue of Our Lady Odigitria dated 1500, Belvedere Square and the Climb Lampasona, panoramic view requalified with street art.

  • Where Via Trabia, Sommatino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info
