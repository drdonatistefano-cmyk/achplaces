What to see in Montecorice

Municipality of Cilento coast in the Cilento National Park, Vallo di Diano and Alburni, between hilly villages of medieval plant and the coast of Agnone, Capitello and Baia Arena: to see the Marchesal tower of Orthodox, the Lucanian site of the Hill of the Cross and the panoramic stretch of the Red Ripe.

Ortodonico, torre marchesale e chiesa di Sant'Antuono

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village was born in the Lombard period around the marquisal tower, built to control the course of Rio Lavis, a route from the sea to Mount della Stella; tower and church of Sant'Antuono have always been its center. The feud was of the Sanseverino until their fall in 1552, and since the eighties the cultural life of the country revolves around the Museum of peasant civilization.

  • Where Ortodonico, Montecorice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.montecorice.sa.it

Collina della Croce

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill remain traces of a place of worship of the 5th century B.C.: on the surface were found a gorgonion and ceramic pieces of the V-IV century B.C., while the precise function of the structure remains uncertain.

  • Where Ortodonico, Montecorice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.montecorice.sa.it

Sources: comune.montecorice.sa.it

Case del Conte, borgo antico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The old part of the village remained almost intact along an ancient paved street in local stone, divided in two by the highway; in the lower part there is the church of San Pasquale. You arrive through the Plain of San Marco, and beyond the village the hills are covered by a forest of pine trees of Aleppo.

  • Where Case del Conte, Montecorice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.montecorice.sa.it

Sources: comune.montecorice.sa.it

Ripe Rosse e Tempa Rossa

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

A stretch of landscape and naturalistic interest along the SS 267, which follows the high coast up to about 150 meters above sea level and ends with the Tempa Rossa, a cliff overlooking the sea; the Rio dell'Arena, crossed by a bridge, marks the border with Castellabate.

  • Where SS 267, Montecorice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.montecorice.sa.it

Sources: comune.montecorice.sa.it

Baia Arena

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

White sandy beach with rare examples of coastal vegetation, including the sea lily, an example of the biodiversity of the Cilento National Park, Vallo di Diano and Alburni.

  • Where Baia Arena, Montecorice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.montecorice.sa.it

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Sandy beach and pebbles in front of the main square of the hamlet, which in summer becomes a pedestrian island; the name derives from ... Lagnone , stagnant water, for the ancient marshy mouths.
