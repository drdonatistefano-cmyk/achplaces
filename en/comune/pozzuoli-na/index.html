What to see in Pozzuoli

The ancient Puteoli, the largest port of the Empire: the Rione Terra on the spur of tuff, the Macellum with the columns marked by bradisism, the Flavian Amphitheatre and, towards the sea, the acropolis of Cuma with the Antro della Sibilla.

10 places to visit in Pozzuoli, in the province of Naples (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Pozzuoli in the guide with map and filters

Anfiteatro Flavio

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built in the second half of the first century AD under Nero, Vespasian and Titus, third in size after the Colosseum and the amphitheatre of Capua: up to 40,000 spectators on three orders of steps; the underground at 7 meters of depth, the best preserved part, with devices for raising animals and sets.

  • Where Corso Terracciano, Pozzuoli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Rione Terra

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The oldest part of the city, on a spur of tuff about 30 meters above sea: of Greek origin, had the greatest splendor with the Romans, when Pozzuoli was the main port of the Empire; an archaeological route reveals its streets and buildings.

  • Where Rione Terra, Pozzuoli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · discovercampania.it

Macellum (Tempio di Serapide)

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Public market of flavia age, not a temple: porch courtyard with 36 shops and a thòlos in the center; the columns show the holes of marine molluscs that record the rise and descent of the ground for bradisism.

  • Where Pozzuoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Parco Archeologico di Cuma

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The first Greek colony of the West, founded in the second half of the 8th century BC by Euboic settlers of Pithekoussai: on the acropolis the temples of Apollo and Jupiter united by the Sacred Way and, at the foot of the hill, the Antro della Sibilla dug into the tuff; in the lower town forum, Capitolium and spa.

  • Where SP 164, Pozzuoli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
