What to see in Canneto sull'Oglio

Borgo sul'Oglio in the Oglio Sud Park, defensive outpost in Gonzaghescan age: from the medieval Porta Torre, the rest of the castle of San Genesio, the streets open in checkerboard towards Piazza Matteotti; it is the home of Furga, the first factory of Italian dolls, told in the Civic Museum.

Museo Civico

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the former late 19th century brick school on Piazza Gramsci, inaugurated in 1994, it brings together the Giulio Superti Furga Toy Collection (hundreds of dolls and toys, largely of local production: Furga, founded by Luigi Furga Gornini around 1880, was the first factory of dolls in Italy), the Oglio-Church Ecomuseum with fossils, archaeological finds, peasant tools, dioramas and the Visitor Centre of the Oglio Sud Park, the donation Bellani and the Mortara Collection of Lombard painting between Scapigliatura and Divisionismo (Tranquillo Cremona, Previati, Longoni, Tosi). Open Saturday afternoon, Sunday and holidays.

Closed approximately from late December to end of February, Easter and Easter, in July and August (Common)

  • Entry €5, reduced €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Porta Torre e piazza Matteotti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval Porta Torre is what remains of the castle of San Genesio; from here the streets branch out to checkerboard, a sign of the role of the village as a defensive outpost in the Gonzaghescan age. Beyond the door opens Piazza Matteotti with the neoclassical Town Hall designed by the architect G.B. Vergani.

  • Where Piazza Giacomo Matteotti, Canneto sull'Oglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.mantova.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built at the end of the fourteenth century and renovated in the eighteenth century, with frescoes in the bell tower indicating an earlier building from the first half of the fourteenth century. It preserves painted wooden statues attributed to Clement Zamara (1478-1540), including the Complainant on the Dead Christ, a group of eight life-size figures.

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the 11th century on a raised hill near the river Oglio, it preserves an altarpiece with the patron saint.

Parco Oglio Sud lungo il fiume

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The municipality is included in the Oglio Sud River Park, whose marked trails along the Oglio run on foot or by bike; the Park Visitor Centre is inside the Civic Museum.
