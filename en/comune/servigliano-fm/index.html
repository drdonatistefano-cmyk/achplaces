What to see in Servigliano

Neoclassical village with quadrangular plan, rebuilt after the collapse of 1771 by Pope Clement XIV with the name of Castel Clementino, with three monumental doors and two main streets that cross. From 1915 to 1955 there was a large prison camp, today the Park of Peace, told by the House of Memory.

Centro storico di Castel Clementino

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient Castel San Marco, abandoned in the eighteenth century for hydrogeological failure and collapsed in 1771, was rebuilt downstream by Pope Clement XIV as Castel Clementino and completed under Pius VI; it took the name of Servigliano in 1863. The neoclassical village has a quadrangular plan, three monumental doors and two main streets that cross. In the week of August, the donation of the Piana di San Gualtiero by the abbot of Farfa (1450) was recalled.

  • Where Centro storico, Servigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Casa della Memoria

Museums · City history

Multimedia teaching hall in the former railway station, with historical archive and materials on the life of those who passed through the camp of Servigliano: built at the beginning of the twentieth century for about 10,000 prisoners, it housed about 10,000 in the First World War and about 20,000 people in the Second (followed, Jews and families helping them), then between 1945 and 1955 between 40,000 and 50,000 refugees, including Julian-Dalmati. Times indicated: July and August Mon-Fri 9-14, Sat 15-20; other months Mon-Fri 9-14 (afternoon by reservation), Sat 15-20 (morning by reservation), Sunday on request.

  • Where Via E. Fermi 16 (ex stazione ferroviaria), Servigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: regione.marche.it · italia.it

Parco della Pace (ex campo di prigionia)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Park at the entrance of the country in the area of the former camp, used for prisoners of the First World War and then as a refugee camp; since 1970 it is play and sports area and in 2001 it took the name of Park of Peace. The Region has approved two 600,000 euro projects to recover two buildings of the field damaged by the earthquake 2016-2017, destined to ticket office, bookshop and information point and laboratory space.

  • Where Ingresso del paese, Servigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: regione.marche.it · italia.it
