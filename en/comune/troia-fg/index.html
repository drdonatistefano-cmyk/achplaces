What to see in Troia

Borgo dei Monti Dauni from the historic centre of medieval plant along Corso Regina Margherita, famous for the Romanesque Cathedral (1093-1120) with the rose window to eleven columns and for the three rolls of the Exultet kept in the Treasury.

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Started in 1093 under Bishop Girardo da Piacenza and finished in 1120 in Romanesque-Trojan style: it incorporates an earlier church of Santa Maria (1080-1086), still visible in the apse. The rose window (1160-1180), of Arab and Angevin influences, has eleven columns with Corinthian capitals and twenty-two stone tunnel fields; the Gate of the Madonna on the left side was restored in the thirteenth century. Open every day 9-12.30 and 16-20.

  • Where Piazza Papa Giovanni XXIII, Troia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.troia.fg.it · italia.it

Tesoro della Cattedrale e Exultet

Museums · Old masters

Next to the cathedral it houses silver (statue of the seventeenth century, candlesticks of the fifteenth century, a tabernacle of the seventeenth century, a pastoral of the sixteenth century), golden bronzes, vestments, avori from the twelfth to the eighteenth century and parchment miniate, among which three liturgical rolls Exultet of the twelfth and thirteenth century wrapped on a wooden rod called umbilicus; according to Italia.it there are only 32 specimens in the world.

  • Where Piazza Episcopio, Troia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters

It is located in the sixteenth-century monastery of San Benedetto and exhibits architectural elements and statues of the ancient marble furnishings of the cathedral, partly rebuilt, bronzes, paintings of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries and sculptural groups from various town churches. Guided tours available.

  • Where Via Ospedale, Troia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo civico di Palazzo D'Avalos

Museums · City history

Set up since 1971 on the ground floor and in the underground of the sixteenth century Palazzo D'Avalos and opened in 1981, it is divided into five sections: on the ground floor the finds of contemporary times, in the underground those of modern, medieval, Roman and pre-Roman age.

  • Where Via Regina Margherita 72, Troia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Basilio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest church in the village, the center of the rituals of Holy Week that close with the Procession of the Kiss on Easter day; during the restoration of the cathedral there was temporarily placed the Ambone.

  • Where Troia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.troia.fg.it

Centro storico e corso Regina Margherita

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The old town has a medieval structure and you can visit along the Corso Regina Margherita on foot, from which there are numerous side streets.

  • Where Corso Regina Margherita, Troia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Nero di Troia nelle cantine del borgo

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The most typical wine of the place is the Nero di Troia DOC, served in wine bars, wine cellars and restaurants of the village; the cooperative winery Decanto has invested in the territory since 2004.
