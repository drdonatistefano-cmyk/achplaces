What to see in Bertinoro

4 places to visit in Bertinoro, in the province of Forlì-Cesena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 2 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Open Bertinoro in the guide with map and filters

Piazza della Libertà e Colonna delle anella

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The square of the "Balcony of the Romagna," with the Column of hospitality: the noble families tied the horse to the ring and fought the guest.

  • Where Piazza della Libertà, Bertinoro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Rocca vescovile e Museo Interreligioso

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The 10th century fortress, home to the University of Bologna residential centre, with a panoramic terrace to the sea and the Interreligious Museum on Judaism, Christianity and Islam.

  • Where Rocca di Bertinoro
  • Entry To be checked: weekend visits or by reservation for groups
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Pieve di San Donato in Polenta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

The Romanesque parish church of the 10th century, about 5 km from the village, praised by Giosuè Carducci and linked to the memory of Dante.

  • Where Polenta, Bertinoro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Terme di Fratta

Local life and traditions · Spas

The spa of the hamlet of Fratta Terme, about 5 km from Bertinoro.

  • Where Fratta Terme, Bertinoro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
