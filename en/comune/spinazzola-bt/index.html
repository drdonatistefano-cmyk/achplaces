Miniere di bauxite

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Former mining site in Zone A (full reserve) of the National Park of Alta Murgia, recovered by the Park Authority: the quarries are observed from above from educational areas and panoramic points, while the descent is forbidden. From September 2024 the visits are regulated and managed by Pro Loco Torre and Pino, only by reservation from Friday to Sunday, with hourly shifts (9-12 and 16-18); free standard visit, specialized environmental guide for payment.

only by reservation (tel. 378 4165988), descent into the forbidden quarries

  • Where Info Point c/o Centro ARIF «Azienda Cavone», SP 138 km 7, Spinazzola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.comune.spinazzola.bt.it

Ponte dei 21 Archi

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

A 250-metre-long railway viaduct, built between 1889 and 1891 on the historical line Gioia del Colle-Rocchetta Sant'Antonio that united Puglia and Basilicata, today no longer crossed by trains: round arches and 19th-century brick pylons between fields and hills. You can reach it from paths and country roads; you can not climb the tracks.

no services on site; the Municipality recommends contacting the Info Point Mines of Bauxite for accesses and routes

  • Where Contrada Macchia, agro di Spinazzola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.spinazzola.bt.it

Sources: comune.spinazzola.bt.it

Museo «R. De Cesare»

Museums · City history

Dedicated to politician and writer Raffaele De Cesare (1845-1918), he collects ceramics, coins and instruments of the Roman age, an ethnographic section with tools from the nineteenth century to the mid-Twentieth century and a geological section with plio-pleistocene and clay materials of the territory.

MiC card (updated to February 2021) is closed every day

  • Where Corso Umberto I, Spinazzola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.spinazzola.bt.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.spinazzola.bt.it

Chiesa dell'Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

At the Town Hall, a former Franciscan monastery built with funds from the local university under license of Pope Boniface IX of 5 December 1391.

  • Where Presso il Municipio, Spinazzola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.spinazzola.bt.it

Sources: comune.spinazzola.bt.it

Chiesa Madre di San Pietro Apostolo e ruderi del castello

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the historic center the Mother Church rises next to the ruins of the castle, built according to the Municipality by the Beneventan princes and by Ludovico II, then abandoned and demolished by decision of the authorities in the thirties of the twentieth century. The Madonna del Bosco is brought to the Mother Church in procession on Tuesday after Easter.

  • Where Centro storico, Spinazzola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.spinazzola.bt.it

Sources: comune.spinazzola.bt.it

Santuario di Maria Santissima del Bosco

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

At the municipal woods, it preserves the image of Our Lady of the Bosco, brought in procession to the Mother Church on Tuesday after Easter and accompanied back to the sanctuary in August.
