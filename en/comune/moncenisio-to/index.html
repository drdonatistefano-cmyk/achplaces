What to see in Moncenisio

Small alpine village at 1,461 meters near the French border, with the capital Ferrera at the foot of the Moncenisio pass: the Ecomuseum "Le Terre al Confine" tells smugglers, pilgrims and armies past here, the Via Francigena, the Strada dei Marroni and the Fell railway; in the surroundings the Lake Foppa, a fishing reserve.

Ecomuseo «Le Terre al Confine»

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Born in 2004 in Ferrera, it documents the millennial history of the Moncenisio and Val Cenischia pass as a frontier land: via del sale, Via Francigena, Chemin de la Ramasse, Chemin des Marrons, Fell railroad, exile and Glorious Return of the Valdesi, mountain crafts and popular traditions. The route includes the documentation centre in the former after-work, an exhibition on work in the former mill, the old restored common oven and, at the entrance of the village, an outdoor exhibition on the Fell railroad that from 1866 to 1871 connected Susa to Saint-Michel-de-Maurienne. Opening hours 2026: from May to September Friday 14-19, Saturday and Sunday 10-18; from October to April Tuesday 10-12, Friday 14-19, Saturday 10-18.

Lago Foppa

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

Lake Moncenisio open to the public and members; the fishing is reserved for holders of the card of the US Susa, which can be purchased on site in the wooden house near the lake, where also the good fishing is released.
