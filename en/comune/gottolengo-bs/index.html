What to see in Gottolengo

Country of Lower Brescia with the parish church of Saints Peter and Paul and the church of St. Gottardo in the ancient Lazzaretto.

2 places to visit in Gottolengo, in the province of Brescia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Gottolengo in the guide with map and filters

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Single nave church with side chapels and rectangular presbytery concluded by the apse, decorated with reliefs and mural paintings; on the south side the slender bell tower with square plan with octagonal cusp.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Garibaldi 2, Gottolengo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di San Gottardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of Lombardy dating from the 17th to the 18th century, in the area of the ancient lazzaretto.

  • Where Via del Lazzaretto, Gottolengo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page catalogo.cultura.gov.it

Sources: catalogo.cultura.gov.it
