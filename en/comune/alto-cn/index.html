What to see in Alto

Small village on a hill above the Pennavaire Valley, dominated by the 14th-century castle of Counts Cepollini, with the loggia from which on clear days you can see the sea; three kilometers away is the sanctuary of the Madonna del Lago, the theatre in July of the Procession of Christs.

Castello dei Conti Cepollini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Castle-fortress built in 1320 by the Counts Cepollini, lords of Alto and Caprauna, on a spur above the Val Pennavaire; the wing towards the valley with the loggia is of the fifteenth century. Plucked and partly destroyed by the French in 1796, at the end of the nineteenth century it was transformed with the lowering of the towers and a layered roof, under which remain the Ghibelline blackbirds. Visits only by reservation.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.alto.cn.it

Santuario della Madonna del Lago

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

On the shore of a small lake about three kilometers from the village: the first church, seventeenth-century and dedicated to the Visitation, was enlarged in 1842 and today houses an inn, while the current sanctuary is of the late nineteenth century. On the first Sunday of July there is the Procession of the Christs, with the Ligurian and Piedmontese confraternities that carry great crucifixes around the lake; from here start trails towards the High Via dei Monti Liguri and the Armetta, Dubasso and Galero mountains.

  • Where Località Madonna del Lago, Alto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.alto.cn.it

Sources: comune.alto.cn.it

Oratorio di San Sebastiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Just outside the village, at the entrance of the ancient mule track for Caprauna and near the cemetery, it preserves a fresco from the 15th century.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.alto.cn.it
