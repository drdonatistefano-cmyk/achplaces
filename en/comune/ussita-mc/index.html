What to see in Ussita

Country of the Sibillini Mountains National Park at the foot of Mount Bove, with Romanesque churches such as Santi Vincenzo and Anastasio (consecrated in 1093) and Sant'Ercolano, the slopes of Frontignano and the valley of the Ussita stream; after the earthquake of 2016 historical center, monuments and museums are only partially usable.

8 places to visit in Ussita, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di Santa Lucia a Sasso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Sasso, a Romanesque church built in the 13th century; it contains a painting of Saint Lucia from the 17th century and frescoes from the 15th century.

Park warns that descriptions refer to before the 2016 earthquake and that many historic buildings have been damaged

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta alla Pieve

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of the Pieve, 14th century Gothic church with ogival portal on two columns, central rose window, lateral cusp windows and gray stone arches; inside a table with oil by Camillo and Fabio Angelucci di Mevale.

Park warns that descriptions refer to before the 2016 earthquake and that many historic buildings have been damaged

Museo del Cardinal Gasparri

Museums · City history

In the Town Hall he collects writings, photographs and historical and artistic documents related to Cardinal Pietro Gasparri (1861-1934), Secretary of State of the Church and signatory of the Lateran Pacts of 1929. In the same palace, the Park indicates a fifteenth-century tempera table with the Crucifix between the Madonna and St. John and two frescoes attributed to Paolo da Visso.

the regional data sheet indicates closed museum for earthquakes

  • Where Piazza XI Febbraio 5, Ussita
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: regione.marche.it · sibillini.net · letsmarche.it

Monte Torrone, Monte Bove e Monte Bicco

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the heart of the Sibillini Park, alpine landscape with rich flora and fauna; Mount Torrone is the base for excursions on Mount Bove (vette of 2,112 and 2,169 m) and Mount Bicco (2.052 m), walls also known for mountaineering. On clear days you can see the Adriatic.

  • Where Monte Torrone, Ussita
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · sibillini.net

Frontignano

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

About 9 km from Ussita, at the foot of Mount Bove: ski resort between 1,340 and 1,980 m with 11 slopes for 14 km (including Canalone and Saliere), chairlifts, snow park and the Saliere, Christ of the Snows and Cornaccione refuges; in summer the bike park with chairlift of Selvapiana up to the Rifugio Cristo delle Nevi.

  • Where Frontignano, Ussita
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Valle del torrente Ussita e Cascata delle Callarelle

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

The river that gives the name to the municipality was born in the Panic Valley; a ring path of about 4 hours, from the road to Calcara, goes up the high valley to the Callarelle waterfall and continues up to Ussita.

  • Where Strada per Calcara, Ussita
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it
