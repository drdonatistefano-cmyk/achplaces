What to see in Montopoli in Val d'Arno

The "lllustrated castle" of Boccaccio between Pisa and Florence, linked to the battle of San Romano painted by Paolo Uccello.

3 places to visit in Montopoli in Val d'Arno, in the province of Pisa (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre e Arco di Castruccio e Torre di San Matteo

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The defences of the castle and the fifteenth-century tower of the Florentine Republic, with a view that on clear days arrives to the sea.

Museo Civico di Palazzo Guicciardini

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The museum with the archaeological collections Baldovinetti-Mainoni and Falchi, Etruscan and Roman finds from all over Tuscany.

  • Where Montopoli in Val d'Arno
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Pieve dei Santi Giovanni Evangelista e Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Gothic church of the first fourteenth century in Piazza della Pieve; in the village also the oratory of San Sebastiano and the Madonna del Soccorso linked to the plague.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
