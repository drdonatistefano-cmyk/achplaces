What to see in Pontinia

City of foundation of the Agro Pontino inaugurated on December 18, 1935, with the original nucleus intact, the Museum of the Agro Pontino and, on the Appia, the Roman state Ad Medias in Mesa with the eighteenth-century Palazzo della Posta.

MAP - Museo dell'Agro Pontino

Museums · City history

It tells the migrant communities that populated the valley Pontina between malaria and reclamation, with objects of daily life and oral testimonies on the transformation of the territory and customs; VisitLazio indicates it as a stop not to be missed, with testimonies of before and after the reclamation.

MiC card (updated 2020) indicates "closed" every day: check opening (tel. 0773 841501)

  • Where Piazza J.F. Kennedy, Pontinia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Centro di fondazione

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Inaugurated on December 18, 1935, a year after the laying of the first stone, Pontinia preserves intact the original nucleus: the Town Hall with the fascist vintage writings on the walls, the Post Office, the cinema-theatre, the parish church of Sant'Anna and the tower of the aqueduct.

  • Where Piazza centrale, Pontinia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Museums · Themed

Mesa: statio Ad Medias e Palazzo della Posta

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

At km 85 of the Via Appia, Mesa is identified with the Roman state Ad Medias; the Palazzo della Posta, of the eighteenth century, was wanted by Pius VI and in the atrium it preserves two milestones of the Appia and numerous funerary gravestones. Nearby stands a large Roman mausoleum without the covering, which a tradition attributes to the freedom of Clesippo and to the matron Gegania.

  • Where Via Appia km 85, Mesa, Pontinia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
