Padule di Fucecchio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Italy's largest inland swamp, reserves for thousands of birds: over 190 species to observe.

  • Where Fucecchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · destinationflorence.com

Museo civico e diocesano, Palazzo Corsini

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the park of Palazzo Corsini, the museum with sections of archaeology, art and nature, and a Madonna dello Scheggia, brother of Masaccio.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Veneto 27, Fucecchio
  • Entry €3 (with the Torre di Mezzo)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com
