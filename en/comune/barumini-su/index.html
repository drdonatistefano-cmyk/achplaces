What to see in Barumini

At the foot of the Giara, in the Marmilla, the most famous nuragic complex in Sardinia, Su Nuraxi, UNESCO heritage since 1997, and Casa Zapata with another hidden nuraghe.

3 places to visit in Barumini, in the province of Sud Sardegna (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Su Nuraxi

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1997: nuraghe and village inhabited by the ancient Bronze Age to the end of the Iron; the 12-metre-diameter mast on three floors with terrace, a four-tower bastion and a well of over 20 meters in the courtyard.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.sardegnaturismo.it

Casa Zapata e nuraghe Su Nuraxi 'e Cresia

Museums · Archaeological

House built at the end of the sixteenth century, inside which there is another nuraghe, Su Nuraxi 'e Cresia.

Centro Culturale Lilliu e museo delle launeddas
