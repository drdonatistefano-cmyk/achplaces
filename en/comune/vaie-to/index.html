What to see in Vaie

Country of the lower Val di Susa at the foot of the Sacred of San Michele, along the Via Francigena, known for the Neolithic site of Riparo Rumiano, the Museum Laboratorio della Preistoria and the sanctuary of San Pancrazio on the rocks above the village.

3 places to visit in Vaie, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Laboratorio della Preistoria

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Founded in 2000 in the former Town Hall of the historic centre, it is dedicated to pre- and protohistoric archaeology and ancient technologies: experimental reconstructions, a tactile collection, the casts of the finds of Vayes, the inside of a neolithic hut and illustrated panels. Open on Sundays (April to November), other days by reservation; guided tour €6.

  • Where Via San Pancrazio 4, Vaie
  • Entry €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org · cultura.gov.it

Riparo Rumiano e percorso archeologico

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Didactic path that from the Pradera picnic area on the ancient road of France touches a former cave of Renaissance age gneiss and the Riparo Ruminano, discovered by Rumiano in 1890: shelter of Neolithic shepherds where the excavations have returned sixteen stone instruments, ceramics and animal bones. It then climbs to the Baità, probably home of a prehistoric settlement, crosses chestnut groves and a marshy area and ends at the panoramic promontory of San Pancrazio and the museum.

  • Where Strada antica di Francia, Vaie
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.vaie.to.it · turismotorino.org

Santuario di San Pancrazio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

On the rocks above the village, it is the ancient parish dedicated to the holy healer after the vow made by the inhabitants during a serious epidemic, according to tradition in 1878; every year in May there is held the sacred representation of the martyrdom of the saint.

  • Where Via San Pancrazio, Vaie
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.vaie.to.it
