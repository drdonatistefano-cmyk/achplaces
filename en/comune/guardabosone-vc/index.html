What to see in Guardabosone

Small village on a panoramic hill between Valsesia and Valsessera, with origins around the year Mille and walls and courtyards closed of medieval plant; it is called "Country Museum Vivo" for its museums, including the Museum of Natural Sciences of the cav. Carlo Locca and the Casa dei Mestieri, and for the botanical garden Pier Carlo Bussi.

Borgo medievale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village, whose origins date back to the year 1000, rises on a panoramic hill between Valsesia and Valsessera and preserves a medieval atmosphere thanks to the restorations of the walls, the inner enclosed courtyards and winding streets.

  • Where Via Stretta, Guardabosone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Sources: visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Museo di Scienze Naturali

Museums · Themed

The result of about fifty years of research on the cav. Carlo Locca, exhibits finds of the Stone Age, hundreds of animals embalmed between mammals, fish, reptiles and birds, thousands of insects and shells, fossils from the Primary to Quaternary and minerals; the route includes an ancient cellar, medieval buildings and late nineteenth century and an outdoor park with ostriches, lamas, deers and roe deer. Visits to be agreed by contacting the museum.

  • Where Via Roma 34, Guardabosone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Sources: visitvalsesiavercelli.it · comune.guardabosone.vc.it

Casa dei Mestieri

Museums · Themed

In a typical building with wooden loggia, on the ground floor reconstructs the workshops of bricksmiths, blacksmiths, roasters, slippers, slippers and carpenters with their tools; on the upper floors houses an entomological collection indicated among the five largest in the world. The route is connected to the Museum of Natural Sciences; visits to be agreed.

  • Where Via Roma 9, Guardabosone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Sources: visitvalsesiavercelli.it · comune.guardabosone.vc.it

Museo degli antichi mestieri e delle attività agricole

Museums · Themed

It collects the tools of the peasant world for spinning and weaving hemp and for the production of walnut oil. Visits to be agreed with the Municipality.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica 2, Guardabosone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Sources: visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Orto botanico Pier Carlo Bussi

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

On a hill, it was born in 1989 on the initiative of fans of medicinal herbs with educational, scientific and conservation purposes; it hosts about four hundred varieties of ornamental plants, tall trees and numerous aromatic and curative species, and has recently also become an ornithological station.

  • Where Guardabosone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Sources: visitvalsesiavercelli.it
