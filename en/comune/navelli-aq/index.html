What to see in Navelli

Grey stone village on a hill on the plateau, enriched by the trade of saffron (PDO since 2005), with alleys with steps carved in the rock, the seventeenth-century Palazzo Santucci and the church of San Sebastiano; in the village of Civitatenga the monastery of Sant'Antonio and the ancient Jewish ghetto.

Centro storico e porte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Grey stone houses on narrow and winding alleys, with steps carved into the rock; from Porta Castello, the only surviving of the ancient doors, begins the oldest part, with the steep Via del Macello and Via San Pasquale, between noble houses and stone hands that indicate the direction. Porta Santa Maria (1475), Porta Villotta with Palazzo Onofri (1498) and Porta San Pelino were born with the expansion of the walls after the earthquake of 1456; along the route the "pilucce" , stone kisses where the donkeys were watered.

Palazzo Santucci

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in the 17th century on the ruins of the medieval fortress: in the courtyard a well dated 1632, year of completion; two stone staircases go up to a loggia with arches and the rooms preserve monumental chimneys.

Chiesa di San Sebastiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built on the ruins of the church of San Pelino, it has for its bell tower the ancient tower of the medieval castle; restored in baroque style after the earthquake of 1703, the side portal has a carved wooden door.

Chiesa del Rosario

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

An eighteenth-century church with a Crucifixion by Venetian painter Vincenzo Damini and an organ by Hadrian Fedra from 1782, in a monumental wooden chest with golden reliefs.

Santa Maria in Cerulis e chiese dei tratturi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Outside the village Santa Maria in Cerulis, from the 11th century, is the oldest church of Navelli; at the crossroads of two tractures rise the Renaissance Santa Maria delle Grazie, with Gothic elements in the rose window, and the rural church of the Madonna del Campo.

Campi di zafferano

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry · Viewpoint

The saffron, DOP since 2005, grows around Navelli and was the heart of the economy of the village; the flowering attracts visitors every year and the harvest, in October, is done at dawn with flowers picked and handmade.
