What to see in Stroppo

Common to 1087 meters in the center of the Maira Valley, from the XIII century de facto capital of the Confederation of the twelve communities of the high valley. You can visit the church of San Peyre with its fourteenth-century frescoes, the fifteenth-century lazzaretto of Caudan, the sanctuary of Santa Maria a Morinesio and the village of San Martino.

Chiesa di San Peyre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Built between the 12th and 13th centuries on a rocky spur 1233 meters above the valley, with a hut façade, Gothic bell tower on the fence of the old cemetery and portal under a large Romanesque arch. Inside, cycles of four painters: an anonymous master of the second half of the fourteenth century (Christ throne between Saints Peter and Paul, the twelve apostles, the Evangelists on the blue vault), a second artist in the chapel, a perhaps Lombard master of the first fifteenth century in the minor apse (Nativity, Adoration of the Magi, Dormitio Virginis, with a rare shepherd playing the bagpipes) and a painter of the first sixteenth century. The key goes back to the Locanda Al Torch, in the hamlet of Paschero.

Lazzaretto di Borgata Caudano

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Built in 1463 for the interest of the Agnesi family as a hospital to separate the plague patients from the healthy, it remained active for 130 years. The façade, one of the most imposing in the valley, has two cross-windows (one with the knot of Solomon, symbol of the mountain community), megalithic portals and a covered passage; today it is of the Municipality, restored, and often hosts exhibitions. It is reached from Paschero on a narrow municipal road that starts from the cemetery.

Santuario di Santa Maria e borgata Morinesio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

The sanctuary stands on the remains of a fourteenth-century chapel dedicated to Saint Margherita: sanctuary from 1511, enlarged in the eighteenth century for pilgrimages, is surrounded by double arched arcades; the frescoes of the dome and the exterior are by Francesco Agnesotti of Sampeyre, late nineteenth century. You can reach it on foot along the ridge east of the village, one of the most orderly of Stroppo, with megalithic portals, covered passages, round columns under the roofs, a sink-oven in the square and a small fresco of Giors Boneto in an alley. In clear weather you can see the whole valley and the plain of Cuneo. Visits: tel. 335 6020527 or 335 6820158.

Borgata San Martino

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Last village of Stroppo on the road to Elva, with a profile suspended in the sky which is a symbolic image of the Maira Valley. Ancient settlement: there was found a bronze bracelet from the IX-VIII century BC, today at the Civic Museum of Cuneo. To see the church of San Martino rebuilt in 1699, an ancient windmill with circular tower, the stone arch in the center, oven, washbasins and fountains; from the mule tracks start excursions to the high mountain.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Museo l'escolo de mountanho

Museums · Themed

It tells the mountain school from 1900 to 1970 reconstructing a classroom with benches, chair, blackboard, registers and original reports of the early twentieth century, accompanied by many vintage photographs. Open from Tuesday to Friday in the morning (Wednesday and Friday also in the afternoon) and on the first Saturday of the month; guided tours on request (tel. 0171 999112).
