What to see in Tertenia

A country of southern Ogliastra crossed by the river Quirra, with about 12 km of coast between Foxi Manna and Foxi Murdegu, the tower of San Giovanni di Sarrala, almost 80 nuraghi including the Aleri and the museum dedicated to the sculptor Albino Manca.

8 places to visit in Tertenia, in the province of Nuoro (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Albino Manca

Museums · Modern art

Dedicated to the sculptor and painter Albino Manca (Tertenia 1898 - New York 1976), who succeeded in Italy and the United States: he hosts his donation of bronze, marble, chalk, medals, paintings, silver and wax, including the Sleeping Fancill and a small bronze copy of the Battery Park eagle in New York. Admission for a fee.

Nuraghe Aleri

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Complex nuraghe on a hill above the marina of Sarrala and Foxi Manna: central tower at tholos of the beginning of the Middle Bronze (XV century BC) with three secondary towers added up to the 11th century BC, in granite boulders. The inner chamber has about 4,5 m of diameter and 8 m of height, with two niches to ogiva; on the slope remains of a village not excavated. You get there by leaving the car and continuing on foot on a path.

  • Where Strada per la marina, capo Sferracavallo, Tertenia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Spiaggia di Foxi Manna

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

About a kilometer of white sand, fine and soft, with low seabed bordered by rocks, suitable for snorkeling; the bay is dominated by the nuraghe Aleri. Rentals, camping, bar and ample parking also for campers; according to the source is never crowded.

  • Where Marina di Tertenia (SS 125, bivio per Morosino)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Torre di San Giovanni di Sarrala

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Tower of sighting on the promontory between Foxi Manna and Foxi Murdegu, transformed into bunkers in the Second World War (the regional boards date to the XVI or the beginning of the XVIII century); below, a small pebble beach. Here stood the original village, perhaps heir of the Roman city of Saralapis; to the south of the marina the port of Santoru, used between the eight and twentieth centuries for minerals.

  • Where Sarrala, Marina di Tertenia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Archeologia del territorio

History and architecture · Archaeology

Caves at the foot of Mount Julea inhabited by the recent and final Neolithic (IV-III millennium BC), necropolis to domus de janas in Magalàu and Santa Lugia, almost 80 nuraghi, about thirty tombs of giants, about twenty Nuragic villages and a sacred well.

Monte Ferru e foreste di lecci

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

A complex rich in woods and paths with the Tabcu Mannu and Mount Arbu; around the holm oak forests and vineyards of Crabiolu, Fustiragili and Bingioniga and, in the background, the limestone heels of Ogliastra. The river Quirra and its tributaries change color from blue to green.

  • Entry Free
