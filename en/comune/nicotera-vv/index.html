What to see in Nicotera

Terrace at 212 meters on the slopes of Monte Poro, overlooking the Tyrrhenian Sea, the Aeolian Islands and the Strait: the castle Ruffo, the Cathedral, the ancient Giudecca and the marina with shallow and clear waters.

5 places to visit in Nicotera, in the province of Vibo Valentia (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello dei Ruffo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Rebuilt from 1764 on a project attributed to Ermenegildo Sintes on medieval remains, with a quadrangular plan of about 50 by 45 meters with angular towers; the earthquake of 1783 caused the south wing to collapse. From its position you can see the Aeolians and the Strait.

  • Where Centro storico, Nicotera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it
