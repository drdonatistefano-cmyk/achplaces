What to see in Ronco Canavese

The municipality of Valle Soana largely inside the National Park of Gran Paradiso, with over thirty hamlets from the six-seventh-century houses, sundials and frescoes in Cernisio and Tressi and the rascards of Servino; here you can visit the copper forge of 1675, the heart of the Rame Ecomuseum, the visitor center of the Park and the sanctuary of the Crest, while in the valley of Forzo the village Pezzetto is filled with cribs at Christmas.

Fucina del rame – Ecomuseo del Rame

Museums · Themed

Forging the Catalan style dated 1675 by an inscription on stone ('IHS Glaudo Calvi 1675) with a large forge for copper and a small for iron and coal, sweaters and mantic moved by the Soana water; it remained active until 1952 and was restored by the Park. It is the core of the Ecomuseum of Copper: it shows the phases of pre-industrial processing, it has a didactic workshop with documentaries on the use of copper in peasant life and an exhibition on the magnin, the now streetwalkers of the valley, some of which then worked on the bodies of the Turin automotive industry. Information hours (Tel. 011 8606233).

Centro visitatori del Parco «Tradizioni e biodiversità in una valle meravigliosa»

Museums · Science and university · Free entry

Center of the Gran Paradiso National Park in a house built by the municipality, dedicated to the biodiversity of the Soana Valley: ecosystems, products of the territory, history, traditions, legends and ancient crafts, with traditional furnishings, panels, collections of insects and a space where artisans and producers show their techniques. Times to ask in the center (tel. 0124 817377).

  • Where Via Alpetta, Ronco Canavese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.pngp.it

Sources: pngp.it

Santuario del Crest (la ciapelà del Creht)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Sanctuary of Our Lady of the Snow and the Emigrant, with documented origins in 1484 and altar rebuilt in 1778-1779; it houses 68 bronze plates with the names of migrants dead at work, a Madonna with the Child and altars with St. Sebastian and the Visitation. Outside, two monuments of the sculptor Jacques Peraducto and the façade painted in 2004 by Costanza Maria Tibaldeschi; feast on the first Sunday of August.

  • Where Strada provinciale della Valle Soana, di fronte al cimitero, Ronco Canavese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.pngp.it

Sources: comune.roncocanavese.to.it · pngp.it

Pezzetto, la borgata dei presepi

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

In the valley of Forzo, about 4 km from the provincial, inhabitants and holidaymakers transform every year the alleys of the village into an outdoor exhibition of cribs made with natural materials of the mountain tradition; the tour, easy with a few steps, takes about 15 minutes. Visitable every day, even in the evening, from 8 December to 6 January.

  • Where Borgata Pezzetto, vallone di Forzo, Ronco Canavese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.pngp.it

Sources: comune.roncocanavese.to.it · pngp.it
