What to see in San Marcello

Walled village of the Vallesina with rectangular plan, with square and cylindrical towers: in the Renaissance Palazzo Marcelli is home to the Telephone Museum, with over 200 antique appliances; around the churches of San Marcello and Santa Maria del Rosario.

Museo del Telefono

Museums · Themed

On the noble floor of Palazzo Marcelli, more than 200 vintage and modern phones in chronological order, collected since 1956 worldwide by the collector and restorer Giuseppe Renzini and almost all still functioning. Mura castellane e Palazzo Marcelli

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village is closed by walls with rectangular plan reinforced by square and cylindrical towers; inside the Renaissance Palazzo Marcelli, with portal bugnato and loggia with four arches, and the historic Ferrari Theatre.
