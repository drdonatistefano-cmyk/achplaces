What to see in Santa Cristina e Bissone

Country of the Lower Pavese oriental mentioned already in the tenth century on the itinerary of Sigerico along the Via Francigena, with the ancient monastery of Via Dante (capitals of the eighth century from the royal palace of Corteolona), the peasant Museum of the Lower Pavese and a monumental plane tree.

Museo contadino della Bassa Pavese (Museo della Civiltà Contadina e dell'Età Contemporanea)

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Founded in 1984 by a group of citizens, it occupies a building of the twenties of the twentieth century with over 200 m2 of exposure: more than 2000 tools grouped according to use, along a path in monothematic sections that tells the life and work in the Lower Pavese until the decade following the Second World War. The collection of photographic exhibitions, photographic archive, library, historical documents and videos with testimonies of peasants are completed. Open from Tuesday to Sunday in two-hour bands (closed Monday); free entry and guide, welcome an offer; groups and schools by telephone reservation.

  • Where Piazza XXV Aprile, Santa Cristina e Bissone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museo.comune.santacristinaebissone.pv.it

Sources: museo.comune.santacristinaebissone.pv.it · in-lombardia.it

Antico monastero di Santa Cristina

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The village appears as "Sce Cristine" (Sce Cristine), stage XL of the itinerary of Sigerico (X century), the oldest travel report on the Via Francigena; its spiritual and logistical importance derives from the monastery, founded in the Lombard period, where Corradino of Swabia also stayed. The medieval system and the 17th century renovations remain recognizable; inside a Gothic bifora there are four columns with capitals of the 8th century coming from the royal palace of Corteolona.

  • Where Via Dante, Santa Cristina e Bissone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · comune.santacristinaebissone.pv.it

Platano di Santa Cristina e Bissone

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Monumental Platan along the central street, near the ancient monastery: the stem slightly exceeds the 15 meters high and has a circumference of almost 5 meters (160 cm of diameter); for pruning it develops more in width than in height. It is freely open to visitors.

  • Where Via Dante angolo Via Fiume, Santa Cristina e Bissone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
