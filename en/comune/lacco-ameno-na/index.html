What to see in Lacco Ameno

Small town on the north coast of Ischia, amphitheatre overlooking the sea at the foot of the Epomeum, with the Mushroom of green tuff as a symbol; here stood Pithecusae, the oldest Greek colony of the West, whose finds, including the Nestor Cup, are exposed to Villa Arbusto.

Museo archeologico di Pithecusae

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Opened in 1999 on the first floor of Villa Arbusto, Ischia tells from prehistory to the Roman age through the artifacts of Pithecusae, founded in the eighth century BC by Greci dell'Eubea and dug by Giorgio Buchner since 1952: accessories of the necropolis of San Montano, the crater of the shipwreck of local production and the Cup of Nestor, with an epigram in Euboic alphabet that recalls the Iliad Cup. A section illustrates businesses with Near East, Carthage, Etruria and Sardinia.

  • Where Corso Angelo Rizzoli 210, Lacco Ameno
  • Entry €8, reduced €6, 6-13 years and residents €3
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.pithecusae.it

Sources: pithecusae.it · cultura.gov.it · sabapmetropolitanana.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Villa Arbusto e giardino, Museo Angelo Rizzoli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

The eighteenth-century villa, formerly the residence of the Duke of Atri, on a hill above Piazza Santa Restituta in front of Monte di Vico; in 1952 the publisher and producer Angelo Rizzoli, who collected rare plants from the five continents, from the domestic pines symbol of Ischia to jacaranda, ginkgo and eucalyptus. The garden is visited with the museum; in the villa the Museo Angelo Rizzoli exhibits more than 500 photographs of the years when Ischia became the destination of politicians, divines and journalists.

  • Where Corso Angelo Rizzoli, Lacco Ameno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: pithecusae.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Il Fungo

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Symbol of the village, it is an ancient rock of green tuff about ten meters high that emerges from the sea a few meters from the shore: erupted from Mount Epomeo, it was shaped by the sea in the form that made it famous.

  • Where Lungomare di Lacco Ameno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Basilica di Santa Restituta

Places of worship · Basilicas

Papal Basilica on the square of the same name: works on the foundations since 1036, enlargement in 1470, nineteenth-century renovations with inauguration in 1886. In 1635 the Turkish corsairs profaned the statue and stole the bells; the saint is celebrated on 16, 17 and 18 May, in memory of his arrival in the bay of San Montano. Under the church is the archaeological area with the museum of Santa Restituta.

museum and archaeological area of Santa Restituta closed for the redevelopment project (July 2025); finds transferred to Villa Arbusto; available a virtual tour at the tower info point

Terme Regina Isabella

Local life and traditions · Spas

Spa complex of late nineteenth century engineer Nicola Cianelli, rethought between 1950 and 1954 by Ignazio Gardella: the Ionic colonnade becomes an abstract diaphragm, a rose body connects to the historical facade and an air bridge combines thermal baths and hotels; inside remain the double-height atrium and a Neapolitan majolica floor.

  • Where Piazza Santa Restituta 1, Lacco Ameno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: atlantearchitetturacontemporanea.cultura.gov.it · italia.it
