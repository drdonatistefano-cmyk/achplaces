What to see in Scarlino

Medieval village on the Gulf of Follonica, dominated by the Pisan Fortress: the treasure of Scarlino at the documentation centre Francovich, the museum of the ancient Roman port at the Puntone, the Etruscan tombs of Poggio Tondo and the coves of the Bandite reserve, including Cala Violina.

6 places to visit in Scarlino, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Scarlino in the guide with map and filters

Rocca Pisana

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Also called Rocca Aldobrandesca: built in the Middle Ages for the Aldobrandeschi and then passed to the Pisani as a watchtower on the coast and the plain, on a site inhabited since the late Bronze Age. Irregular pentagonal plan connected to the walls, three corner towers of different shape (round, triangular, quadrangular), restored at the end of the twentieth century; in summer it hosts events.

  • Where Via Ercole Cecchi 20, Scarlino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

MAPS – Museo archeologico del Portus Scabris

Museums · Archaeological

In a hydraulic tollgate of the early twentieth century overlooking the Padule di Scarlino: amphorae, ceramics and objects fallen into the sea during the loading and unloading of the ships, recovered in diving excavations of 2000-2001 in the ancient commercial port, in the rada of Portiglioni; it also tells the work of underwater archaeologists with instruments, techniques and reconstructions. Open every day 10-13 and 16-19 according to the card of italia.it.

  • Where Puntone, Scarlino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com

Chiesa di San Donato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Inside the walls from the middle of the fourteenth century, a facade with a shack with a horned rose window and a Latin cross plan; it preserves a table with Christ crucified between the Saints Rocco and Jerome.

  • Where Centro storico, Scarlino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Necropoli etrusca di Poggio Tondo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Four mounded tombs of the middle of the 7th and 6th century BC, at the foot of the hills of the Alma Valley; reachable only on foot, with information panels.

  • Where Valle dell'Alma, Scarlino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
