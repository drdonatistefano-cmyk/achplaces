What to see in Cassacco

The hilly town to the north of Udine, dominated by the rectangular castle with two high towers, restored in 1480 by the brothers Montegnacco; in the hamlets of Conoglano and Montegnacco there are the churches designed or restored between the seventeenth and twentieth centuries and the exhibition of peasant culture in the former Ferrant Mill.

Castello di Cassacco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortress with a symmetrical rectangular structure with two high towers at the ends, stone and brick with ceilings and wooden roofs; an internal inscription recalls the restoration of the brothers Iacopo and Sebastiano di Montegnacco in 1480. Consolidated after the earthquake, today it is home with exhibition function: check the possibility of visiting.

Mostra della cultura contadina di Conoglano

Museums · Themed

Born from donations of the inhabitants, it is set up in the Ferrant Mill, former mill of the eighteenth century, and collects copper, sieves, wagon wheels, yokes and other tools, with photographs of the work in the fields.

the MiC card (updated to 2020) indicates the exhibition closed every day: ask the Municipality (tel. 0432 881319)

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Consecrated in 1571 and already decorated with paintings and sculptures, it was rebuilt between 1880 and 1892 on a project by parish priest Angelo Noacco with different orientation, crypt and baptistery obtained from the ancient apse, which preserves part of the frescoes; the old bell tower (1638-1775) was maintained and a new built not far away. Interior with three naves with polygonal apse.

  • Entry Free

Chiesa dei Santi Filippo e Giacomo a Conoglano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The original church of 1601, surrounded by the cemetery and with the frescoed apse, was enlarged between 1629 and 1850 with a chapel of the Madonna; the current building with three naves with polygonal apse was designed by Mgr. Angelo Noacco was built between 1889 and 1921.

  • Where Conoglano, Cassacco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cassacco.ud.it

Sources: patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it · comune.cassacco.ud.it

Chiesa di San Giusto martire a Montegnacco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Small church in the hall cited in the will of Brunetto dei Montegnacco, was radically rebuilt in 1626: the original construction remains a sculpted spire of 1486, placed on the tympanum. Between 1790 and 1796 Girolamo di Montegnacco embellished it with stones from the demolished bell tower of Collalto di Tarcento.

  • Where Montegnacco, Cassacco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cassacco.ud.it

Sources: patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it · comune.cassacco.ud.it
