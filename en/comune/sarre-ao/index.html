What to see in Sarre

The village between orchards and vineyards at the gates of Aosta, with the royal castle where the Savoys used to hunt in the valleys of the Gran Paradiso.

3 places to visit in Sarre, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Sarre in the guide with map and filters

Castello Reale di Sarre

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Rebuilt in the early eighteenth century, purchased in 1869 by Vittorio Emanuele II as headquarters for hunting in Cogne, Rhêmes and Valsavarenche; in the salon and gallery 3612 horns of chamois and ibexes wanted by Umberto I.

  • Where Frazione Lalex, Sarre
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Maurizio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The Romanesque bell tower with double-fores and triple-fores on three levels testifies to its ancient origins; inside wooden sculptures, goldsmiths and sacred fabrics.

  • Where Sarre
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Strada dei Salassi

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient pre-Roman route between the Gran San Bernardo and the upper valley, partly on the hill of Sarre along the new panoramic road.

  • Where Sarre
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it
