What to see in Pollenza

Borgo between the valleys of Potenza and Chienti, the ancient Pollentia rebuilt around the Mille as Montemilone and renamed Pollenza in 1862, known for over five centuries for ceramics, woodworking and restoration of antique furniture; in the center the Collegiate of San Biagio, the Verdi Theatre and Palazzo Cento, in the countryside the Altomedieval Abbey of Rambona.

Abbazia di Santa Maria di Rambona

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Built around 891-898 at the behest of Queen Ageltrude, wife of Emperor Guido of Spoleto; in 1443 it was sacked and set on fire by Ciarpellone, captain of Francesco Sforza, and the monastery was destroyed. There are still presbytery and crypt: the three sandstone apses retain the original characteristics, inside the frescoes of Sant'Amico that enshrine the wolf and the Madonna enthroned with the Child.

closed following the earthquake of 2016 according to the regional portal

Museo civico di Palazzo Cento

Museums · City history

In the sixteenth-century noble palace renovated in the seventeenth-century, which housed the family of Giacomo Leopardi: archaeological collection with mosaics and Roman statues from Montefrancolo, picture gallery of Pollento painters, ceramics produced in Pollenza between 1810 and 1905, antique furniture, a collection of crucifixes and the Vespa Museum curated by Marco Romiti. In the same building library and historical municipal archive.

closed due to the earthquake of 2016 according to the regional portal

  • Where Via Roma 32, Pollenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Collegiata di San Biagio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Great neoclassical church with Greek cross at the Porta della Croce, designed by De Mattia di Treia, with the dome frescoed by Virginio Monti; it preserves a polychrome wooden crucifix of the sixteenth century.

  • Where Presso Porta della Croce, Pollenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Chiesa e convento dei Santi Francesco e Antonio

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry

Of medieval origin, in the thirteenth century depended on the abbey of Rambona and in 1215 hosted Saint Francis on his way to Loreto; a cloister connects her to the Teatro Verdi. It houses frescoes and a altarpiece with Sant'Antonio da Padova on a gold background by Lorenzo d'Alessandro da San Severino.

  • Where Pollenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Teatro «Giuseppe Verdi»

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Municipal theatre inaugurated in 1883 on a project by Ireneo Aleandri, with a horseshoe room, two orders of stages and a loggia, up to 250 seats.

Piazza della Libertà e Palazzo Comunale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The heart of the town life, closed by the Town Hall, rebuilt in 1775 on a project by Alessandro Rossi instead of the Palazzo dei Priori, the Teatro Verdi and the noble palaces: the Palazzo di Spilimbergo, whose façade was rebuilt in 1907 in symmetry with the loggia in front, and the seventeenth-century church of the Immaculate Conception between the facades of the palaces.

  • Where Piazza della Libertà, Pollenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Borgo storico di Pollenza

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Borgo with Roman traces and medieval walls, known for over 500 years for its ceramic and majolica handicrafts, woodworking and the restoration of antique furniture; in mid-July it hosts the Exhibition of antiques, restoration and artistic handicrafts. Among the palaces, Palazzo Narducci of the sixteenth century with portal in rustic stone and Palazzo Lazzarini, already of the Lazzarini in 1342.

  • Where Centro storico, Pollenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Palazzo Ricci Petrocchini e Studio Failla

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace of the Ricci Petrocchini family, to which belonged Father Matteo Ricci, protagonist of the mission in China started in 1582. Inside the studio of the self-taught painter Fabio Failla (Lucca 1917 - Roma 1987), descendant of the Ricci.

Santa Lucia in Sylvis was built in the thirteenth century on the remains of the medieval castle of Castel Gualdo and is linked to the Franciscan presence; restored after the earthquake of 1997, it is among the monumental buildings of the Marches.

Luoghi della Battaglia di Cantagallo e Tempio-Ossario

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

On 2-3 May 1815 in the territory of Pollenza, then Monte Milone, the battle of Cantagallo was fought, one of the bloodiest of the Napoleonic period, considered by many scholars the symbolic beginning of the Risorgimento. The church of Santa Maria Ausiliatrice di Cantagallo, commissioned by Don Nazareno Boldorini, is a temple-hospital in honour of the fallen.

  • Where Cantagallo, Pollenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: turismo.comune.pollenza.mc.it · letsmarche.it

Monte Franco

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hill north of the centre with the oldest traces of human presence, of Picena age: there was found a necropolis of the IX-VII BC centuries, and around the X-XI century there stood the medieval castle of Castel Franco.

  • Where Monte Franco, Pollenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
