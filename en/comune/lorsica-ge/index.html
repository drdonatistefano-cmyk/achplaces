What to see in Lorsica

The village of Val Fontanabuona is halfway through the Tirello valley, under the Monte Ramaceto (1345 m), known at least from the sixteenth century for the hand weaving of damasks and other silks: the Damask Museum and the De Martini weaving preserve its tradition, around the linear village collected around the parish of Maria Annunciata.

Museo del damasco

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

On four floors of an ancient building tells the story of the village and of the weaving: Lorsica was among the main centers of the Genoese manufacture, whose fabrics in the sixteenth-sixth century were known in the world as "Velours de Gênes" . It exhibits frames, tools for spinning, canning and dyeing and antique fabrics (damasks, lamps, linen macramè, an embroidered planet of the parish). According to the Metropolitan City opens Saturday and Sunday 16-18 from April to October and 15-17 from November to March, and by reservation on other days (tel. 0185 95019).

Tessitura De Martini

Local life and traditions · Crafts

A family workshop with a tradition dating back to the 16th century and over 300 exclusive designs handed down from generation to generation: silk damasks, lampshades, voiles, shantungs, satin and linen, and recently cashmere and silk cashmere; Giuseppe De Martini has built and patented a frame that reproduces the fabrics once handmade. Open Monday to Saturday 9-18 (tel. 0185 977302).

Borgo e chiesa di Santa Maria Annunciata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The capital lies on a hill opposite Monte Ramaceto, which shelters it from the weather: it is a linear village, with the houses arranged in ribbon on the sides of the parish of Maria Annunciata. In church a shovel attributed to G. De Ferrari with the appearance of Christ to Saint Catherine and the statue of Saint Catherine of Genoa carved in 1860 by Paul Olivari; in May the feast of the saint involves the whole village.

  • Where Lorsica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.cittametropolitana.genova.it
