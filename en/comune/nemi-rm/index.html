What to see in Nemi

Borgo dei Castelli Romani overlooking the volcanic lake of Nemi, with the medieval Castle Ruspoli, the viewpoints on the lake and the Sanctuary of the Blessed Crucifix; on the shore stand the Museum of the Roman Navi, built for the ships of Caligola, and the remains of the sanctuary of Diana Nemorense.

7 places to visit in Nemi, in the province of Rome (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo medievale e Castello Ruspoli

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village rises along the hill with towers, squares and alleys with colorful facades; the Castle Ruspoli, of the tenth century, is indicated as the oldest in the area. The Pullarella is the surviving part of the original medieval village, once accessible only from the entrance of the castle; in the village the Fontana della Medusa (2008) by Luciano Mastrolorenzi recalls the bronze decorations of the ships of Caligula.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Nemi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Belvedere Ceyrat e Terrazza degli Innamorati

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Ceyrat viewpoint is the highest point of the village, with views of the roofs, the volcanic lake and the sea; the Terrace of the In love, under Palazzo Ruspoli, looks at the lake and takes its name from the myths of Diana and Virbio and Numa Pompilio and the nymph Egeria.

  • Where Belvedere Ceyrat, Nemi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlazio.com

Museo delle Navi Romane

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Built between 1933 and 1939 for the two great ships of Caligola recovered from the lake in 1929-1931, the first Italian museum designed around its contents; the ships were destroyed by a fire in 1944. Today it exhibits anchors, the roof in reconstructed bronze tiles, 1:5 scale models and the life-size reconstruction of the stern of the first ship, as well as votive materials of the territory and the sanctuary of Diana.

Extraordinary closure until 31 March 2027 for PNRR (MIC) work

  • Where Via Diana 13-15, Nemi
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website direzioneregionalemuseilazio.cultura.gov.it

Sources: direzioneregionalemuseilazio.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Santuario di Diana Nemorense

History and architecture · Archaeology

Ancient pilgrimage site dating back to the 6th century BC, near the lake, on the descent to the water; the remains of the temple are located in the woods on the banks.

  • Where Riva del lago di Nemi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Lago di Nemi

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Crateric lake at the bottom of an ancient volcano active up to 30,000 years ago, surrounded by reeds and greenhouses of strawberries, in the Park of the Roman Castles suitable for excursions, trekking and mountain biking.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint
