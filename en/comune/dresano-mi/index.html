What to see in Dresano

Municipality of Parco Agricolo Sud Milano, in an area healed by the monks' bonifiche; in the historic center the nineteenth-century parish church of San Giorgio Martire and the old Belpensiero farmhouse, mentioned already in the sixteenth century, with a secular plane tree in the courtyard.

2 places to visit in Dresano, in the province of Milan (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giorgio Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church built in the second half of the nineteenth century on the site of a more ancient cult building, in the center of the village.

  • Where Via Roma 4, Dresano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.dresano.mi.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · comune.dresano.mi.it

Centro storico e Cascina Belpensiero

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The old Belpensiero farmhouse, mentioned in 16th century sources and still surrounded by fields, is located in the historic centre, with a centuries-old plateau in the courtyard; the health and fertility of the area are due to the monks' reclamations, which healed a swampy area.

  • Where Centro storico, Dresano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.dresano.mi.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · comune.dresano.mi.it
