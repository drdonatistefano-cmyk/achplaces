What to see in Anversa degli Abruzzi

Medieval village of the high valley of Sagittario, 604 meters away, with the ruins of the Norman castle where d'Annunzio set the torch under the moggio, the churches of San Marcello and Madonna delle Grazie and the Gole del Sagittario; in the hamlet of Castrovalva the panorama that inspired a lithography of Escher.

Riserva naturale Gole del Sagittario

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Deep canyon dug in thousands of years by the Sagittarius river, WWF oasis since 1991 and regional nature reserve since 1997; English travellers of the late nineteenth century called it "fearful and beautiful." A panoramic view of the gorges can be reached from via Duca degli Abruzzi.

Castello normanno dei Conti di Sangro

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built by the Normans in the 12th century to control one of the southern entrances of the Peligna Valley, it was severely damaged by the earthquake of 1706: the ruins of the fourteenth-century tower, the residential complex and an arch of the early sixteenth century remain. In 1904 Gabriele d'Annunzio set the tragedy under the bush.

Chiesa della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the XVI century with Renaissance portal in limestone of 1540, unique in Abruzzo for the rare iconographic motif, and rosette of 1585 with snakes twisted to the compasses and the coat of arms of the Sangro. Inside a polychrome statue of San Rocco of 1530 of local ceramists, a sixteenth-century wooden tabernacle gilded in 1664 and a copy of the Triptych of Antwerp, whose original was stolen in 1981.

Chiesa di San Marcello

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the 11th century with late Dogotic portal of 1472, decorated with sculptures with ornamental, vegetable and anthropomorphic motifs; in the bezel a fresco of the Madonna with Child and two saints, one with a grindstone around the neck, symbol of martyrdom.

Centro storico, Case dei Lombardi e Porta Pazziana

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village with houses that make up walls, narrow alleys and arched underpasses; the Houses of the Lombards, built by workers of the North between the fifteenth and seventeenth centuries, are fortified houses that exhibited the prestige of the families. Near a carved portal of 1666 with symbols of the cult of San Domenico; the Pazziana Porta, surviving of the walls, leads to a terrace on the Sagittarius and Castrovalva.

Castrovalva

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Anversa fraction, once an independent feud, a well preserved ridge settlement which is accessed by a Gothic arched gate; there are the parish church of Santa Maria della Snow (16th century) and the church of San Michele Arcangelo (XII century). In 1929 Maurits Cornelis Escher drew a lithograph of it: from the last corner before the village you will find the same view.
