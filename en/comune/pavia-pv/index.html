What to see in Pavia

Ancient Lombard capital on Ticino and one of the oldest university cities in the world: the Visconti Castle with the Civic Museums, San Michele Maggiore where Barbarossa, the Ark of Sant'Agostino, the Duomo and the Covered Bridge were crowned.
