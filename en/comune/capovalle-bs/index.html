What to see in Capovalle

The village of Alta Valle Sabbia between Lake Garda and Lake Idro, facing like an amphitheatre on the banks of Brescia del Garda and formed by three medieval districts; on Mount Stino, panoramic between the two lakes, there is the Museum of war finds, set up in the galleries of the fortifications of the Great War.

Museo della Guerra 1915-18 e dei reperti bellici

Museums · Themed

Public museum excavated in the rock in Monte Stino, outside the town: it occupies two galleries with barrel vaults of the stations that the military command had fortified since 1914. Founded in 1980 in the same fortifications, it is owned by the Municipality and managed by volunteers of a non-profit association; exhibits war finds of the First and Second World Wars, with guided tours.

Monte Stino

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Panoramic place between Lake Idro and Garda, reachable on foot or by car, with a shelter at about 1,400 m; from the mountain you can practice paragliding and in July, for over forty years, the Alps celebrate a mass at the church on the summit. On the mountain are the military fortifications of 1914 that house the museum of war finds.

  • Where Monte Stino, Capovalle
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · lombardiabeniculturali.it

Contrade di Zumiè, Vico e Viè

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village, of about 350 inhabitants, consists of three medieval districts and historically lives of shepherds, timber and charcoal; until the First World War it was a place of trade with Austria. On weekends, Vico's trattorias, including the Trattoria Bianca (since 1901), offer the "capovallese spiedo" cooked with coal and wood fire; in the shops there are local products such as Tombea cheese.

  • Where Capovalle
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
