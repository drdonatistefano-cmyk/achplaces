What to see in Bardonecchia

The alpine basin of the high valley of Susa, at the entrance of the Fréjus tunnel: the Borgovecchio with sundials, chapels and the remains of the Tur d'Amun, the church of Sant'Ippolito with the Romanesque bell tower, the Fort Bramafam with the museum of the Royal Army and the museum of Alpine religious art of Melezet.

Museo Forte Bramafam

Museums · City history · Viewpoint

Built between 1885 and 1894 on the hill to the south-east of the basin, on the ruins of the Château du Bramafam, it was the most important fortification of the Cozie Alps; restored since 1995 by the Association for Studies of History and Military Architecture, it houses on more than 4.000 m2 the museum of the Royal Army with reconstructions of environment from 1890 to 1945, uniforms, photographs, diaries and letters. Seasonal opening.

Museo civico etnografico

Museums · Themed

In the historic centre next to the church of Sant'Ippolito, in the nineteenth-century former town hall, wanted by Monsignor Bellando in 1953: on two mobile floors, domestic objects, sacred art and religious antiquities, working tools, photographs and a small collection of local stones.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Museo d'arte religiosa alpina di Melezet

Museums · Old masters

In the chapel of the Madonna del Carmine on the main street of the hamlet Melezet: wooden statues, goldsmiths, paintings, fabrics and vestments from the 15th to the 19th century coming from churches and chapels of the basin, over 300 objects displayed in rotation according to the theme of the year.

Borgovecchio e meridiane

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Walk in the ancient village: the pear of about 300 years of Piazza Suspize, the sundials with Latin mottos of the houses Allemand and Ambrois, the chapel of the Madonna delle Grazie (1712), the chapel of San Rocco of the vote after the plague of 1630 and Casa Pellerin with the gate of 1555.

Tur d'Amun

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Remains of the ancient castle of the lords of Bardonecchia, who between 1330 and 1335 submitted to the Dolphins; in 1562 there died the Protestant refugees in the tower after a battle. First restoration in the context of the archaeological park project.

  • Where Vicolo del Castello, Bardonecchia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.bardonecchia.to.it

Sources: bardonecchia.it · comune.bardonecchia.to.it

Museo etnografico e mulino di Rochemolles

Museums · Themed

In the hamlet Rochemolles, in the former school: objects and tools of everyday life, the traditional costume, sacred furnishings of the chapel of the Madonna degli Angeli and tombolo lace; on the stream the horizontal wheel water mill with two millstones, in service until 1961.
