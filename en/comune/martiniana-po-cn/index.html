What to see in Martiniana Po

The village of the small fruits is in a wooded and raised position with a view of the Saluzzo plain and the Monviso: in the historic center the Baroque Palazzo Filippa, Gauthier square with the birthplace of the painter Giovanni Borgna and the eighteenth-century parish church of Sant'Andrea; on the slopes, paths towards Pian Pramalano and the chapel of San Bernardo.

4 places to visit in Martiniana Po, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

18th century church built on the remains of a medieval church, of which remains the base of the bell tower; inside baroque decorations, a baptismal font in white marble, frescoes in the presbytery and a wooden door of 1700.

  • Where Centro storico, Martiniana Po
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Centro storico, Palazzo Filippa e piazza Gauthier

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Entering the historic center you will find the imposing Palazzo Filippa, a Baroque building; in the central square Gauthier stands the birthplace of Giovanni Borgna, painter formed at the Albertina Academy of Turin who decorated many sacred buildings between Basso Piemonte and Liguria. In the first half of July, the Festival of Small Fruits brings stalls and tastings of blueberries, raspberries, black currants and gooseberries to the village.

  • Where Piazza Gauthier, Martiniana Po
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Pian Pramalano e belvedere sul Monviso

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Radura surrounded by birch trees on the watershed between Po and Varaita, about an hour's walk on paths suitable for mountain biking, with a green stone stone cippo of Monviso located in 1992 by the Martiniana Alps; not far from a viewpoint on the Monviso massif.

  • Where Pian Pramalano, Martiniana Po
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.martinianapo.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Cappella di San Bernardo e croce

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Chapel at 1,419 meters on the border with Brossasco, reachable going up the valley beyond the hamlet Paris, with a nice view; from here a path leads to the foot of a cross 7 meters high, visible also from the village. Along the paths of the slope also the chapel of San Grato in the woods (750 m) and the village of San Michele on the border with the Val Bronda.

  • Where Vallone oltre borgata Paris, Martiniana Po
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.martinianapo.cn.it · visitcuneese.it
