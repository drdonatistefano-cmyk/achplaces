What to see in Valentano

Borgo della Tuscia on the heights above Lake Bolsena, dominated by Rocca Farnese, where Cardinal Alessandro Farnese was born in 1520; after the destruction of Castro (1649) became the capital of the Farnesian Duchy. In the Rocca the Prehistory Museum of Tuscia, in a collegiate village, churches and Renaissance doors.

Museo della Preistoria della Tuscia e Rocca Farnese

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Opened in 1996 in the Rocca Farnese, medieval fortress transformed into a palace for Pier Luigi Farnese, with the Courtyard of Love and the well of Antonio da Sangallo the Younger, the octagonal tower over 25 m high and a Santa Scala, replica of that of the Lateran, with sixteenth-century frescoes of the Passion. The prehistoric section exhibits finds from the Upper Lazio, from the Paleolithic site of Settecannelle to the Neolithic village of Poggio Olivaster, to the necropolis of the Bronze Age of Selvicciola and to the palafittic village of Lake Mezzano, with two golden bronze swords; on the second floor ceramics from the upper Middle Ages to the modern age, including Viterbo majolica of the fifteenth century. In the tower the Etruscan collection D'Ascenzi and the enigmatic tablet with the Caldera di Latera.

  • Where Piazza della Vittoria 11, Valentano
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.museovalentano.it

Sources: museovalentano.it · cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Collegiata di San Giovanni Evangelista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

12th century church next to the Rocca Farnese, linked to Cardinal Alessandro Farnese, born in Valentano in 1520; its bells come from the ancient city of Castro.

  • Where Valentano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Complesso della Chiesa di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Outside the historical walls, it preserves above the main altar a Umbrian-Sienese fresco of the mid-15th century, two other altars and a door made by the local artist Mario Balestra; it reopened in 2025 after a long restoration.

  • Where Valentano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Centro storico, Palazzo del Comune e porte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Under Martin V the village was surrounded by walls and towers, partly preserved. The Palazzo del Comune, Renaissance, has a loggia with five arches with the coat of arms of Pope Martino V Colonna joined to the Farnese and a plaque for Giuseppe Garibaldi. You enter from the Porta di San Martino, with a panoramic terrace, and from the Porta Magenta, designed by Vignola, called "Maskone" for the face carved at the top.

  • Where Piazza della Vittoria, Valentano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it
