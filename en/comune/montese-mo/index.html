What to see in Montese

Country of the Modena Apennines among chestnut and beech woods, grown around the thirteenth-century fortress that today houses the Historical Museum and the contemporary sculptures of the Artis Forum; in the hamlets the collection of Iola and the fourteenth-century frescoed oratories.

4 places to visit in Montese, in the province of Modena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca di Montese, Museo storico e Forum Artis Museum

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Rocca mentioned as a castle from the twelfth century, Matilde di Canossa and from 1212 of the Montecuccoli, then Este and from 1756 of the Malaspina: two walled enclosures side by side, the feudal palace and the tower rebuilt in 1393 with shoe base and Ghibelline blackbirds. It was struck in the last war and restored by the Municipality and hosts the Historical Museum, from the first archaeological evidence to the post-war period, and the Artis Museum Forum with a hundred contemporary sculptures by artists from 16 countries.

  • Where Via P. Cuoghi 38, Montese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches
