What to see in Pacentro

Medieval village on the slopes of the Morrone, gate of the Majella National Park, dominated by the towers of the castle Caldora-Cantelmo; in the center the churches of Santa Maria Maggiore and San Marcello, outside the walls the convent of Minors Observing with the frescoed cloister.

Castello Caldora-Cantelmo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Born in the 10th century in the defensive system of the Peligna Valley, it has three square towers of the 14th century on a quadrilateral plan surrounded by a large moat, reinforced by round towers in the 15th century; the northeast tower, 24 meters high, was erected in the 13th century, then the Caldora extended the castle and the Orsini added the cylindrical towers. It dominates the entire hair pelvis.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between the 13th and 16th centuries, with three naves divided by octagonal pillars, with façade in stone from the end of the 16th century with carved frame and sundial; the vault is entirely covered with stuccoes, the Baroque pulpit was decorated by Abruzzo carvers and the bell tower is the second highest in the valley after that of the Annunziata di Sulmona.

Chiesa di San Marcello

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest church in the village, founded in 1047, contains frescoes and a wooden portal carved by local craftsmen and dated 1697.

Convento dei Minori Osservanti e chiesa dell'Immacolata

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Just outside the village, the Franciscan monastery of 1589 has a cloister decorated with a 1618 pictorial cycle on the life of St. Francis; next to the Baroque church of the Immaculate Conception, a single nave with a barrel vault, with on the main altar a large painting of the Virgin of the Flemish Spranger.

Centro storico, Canaje e Preta tonna

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Urban fabric rich in medieval and Renaissance remains: via del Castello, via di Sotto e Porta della Rapa, the buildings Tonna, Avolio, Massa and Grenata, the seventeenth-century fountain with octagonal tub of Piazza del Popolo, the stone wash-house of the Canaje and the "Preta tonna" (Price tonna), measured for the wheat where the insolvent debtors were exposed to the gogna. From the west side the view of the Peligna Valley.

  • Entry Free
