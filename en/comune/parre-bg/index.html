What to see in Parre

The village of the Upper Seriana Valley with a history of over 3,000 years: in Castello the archaeological park and Antiquarium Parra Oppidum degli Orobi tells the town mentioned by Plinio; in the historical center the parish church of San Pietro with a altarpiece of Giovan Battista Moroni, on Mount Cüsen the sanctuary of the Holy Trinity and, above the village, the paths towards Monte Alino and Mount Vaccaro.

Parra Oppidum degli Orobi. Parco archeologico e Antiquarium

History and architecture · Archaeology

On a river terrace at about 580 m, on the right of the Serio at the confluence with Val Nossana, it is probably the Parra Oromobiorum Oppidum cited by Plinio. In 1883 there was found a storeroom of bronzes of the fifth century B.C. (the saved part is at the Civic Archaeological Museum of Bergamo); eleven excavation campaigns between 1983 and 1994 have exposed 2,300 m2 of an inhabited area of over 13,000 m2, founded in the final Bronze and lived until the Roman conquest, with stone houses and wood of "alpine house" type. Opened to the public since 2014, it includes the park (Sector A) and the Antiquarium with pottery, ornaments, coins and instruments, which illustrates in particular the processing of copper, bronze and lead.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the historic centre, one of the most important works is a sixteenth-century altarpiece by Giovan Battista Moroni and works by Fantoni, including the wooden choir and the main altar.

Arco di San Cristoforo e chiesa di San Rocco

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

A short distance from the parish church, the arch of San Cristoforo is inserted in a sixteenth-century house with a fresco of the saint; in Piazza San Rocco stands the church of the same name, whose origins date back to the end of the fifteenth century. From San Cristoforo start the step-by-step path and the mule track to the sanctuary of the Holy Trinity.

  • Where Piazza San Rocco, Parre
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Sources: valseriana.eu

Santuario della Santissima Trinità

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the top of Mount Cüsen, with a wide panorama of the Serian Valley, it has been attested since 1565 (visit of Bishop Cornaro) and was wanted by Giacomo Cominelli called Mistrù; for centuries there lived a guardian romantic. The living stone bell tower dates back to 1610, the guardian's house and the eighteenth century Sarnico stone loggia; the church is oriented north-east by the rock and preserves a fresco of the Madonna with Child venerated for almost five centuries. Walk up from San Cristoforo with a steep path on steps or with the mule track "Via di Prècc."

  • Where Via Campella, Parre
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Sources: valseriana.eu

Casa della Pierina

Museums · House museums

Casa del Cinquecento on three floors, inhabited until a few years ago by the town's seamstress, which shows how you lived in Parre at the beginning of the twentieth century: kitchen, "selter" (the coolest room used as a refrigerator), tailoring workshop, stable, rooms, room for honey and loft. Many objects were handmade by Pierina's father, often with recovery materials; notebooks, fashion magazines, drawings, ribbons and lace document his craft.

visits only by appointment or in extraordinary openings, via Infopoint Discover Parre (tel. 331 774 0890)

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The church of Sant'Antonio is in a dominant position on Mount Alino; on the mountain there is also the giant bench, 80a of the Big Bench Circuit project, panoramic view over the valley.

Sentiero da Parre al Monte Vaccaro

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Trekking of medium difficulty (8 km, about 3.30 hours, 1,373 m of climb) that starts a little upstream of the village, where the private traffic ends (about 703 m): crosses the forest, passes just below Sant'Antonio di Alino and runs along the pastures up to the mountain hut Vaccaro, managed by the Parrese Hiking Group; from the refuge to the summit about one hour, with a challenging climb and a wide view. Recommended in spring and summer.

  • Where Partenza a monte dell'abitato di Parre
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Sources: in-lombardia.it · valseriana.eu
