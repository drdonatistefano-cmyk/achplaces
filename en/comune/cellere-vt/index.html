What to see in Cellere

Borgo della Maremma Lazio, perhaps heir of the Roman Cellae Cerris, flourished under the Farnese, who left there the church of Sant'Egidio Abate of Antonio da Sangallo the Younger; today it is the village of the brigand Domenico Tiburzi, told by the Brigantage Museum and by the path that crosses the Park of Timone up to the village of Pianiano.

Museo del Brigantaggio

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

He tells with anthropological cut the Maremma brigandage and the figure of Domenico Tiburzi, born in the hamlet of Pianiano, from the second half of the nineteenth century to today. On the ground floor sources and historical reasons in a setting that evokes the forest (the tradition) and the train (modernity disappointed), among sounds, drawers to open, newspapers and films; on the second floor multimedia installations on the myth of Tiburzi; finally the 'taverna of the brigante's on the contemporary uses of his image. It is part of the Bolsena Lake Museum System; open on Friday, Saturday and Sunday, free for residents.

  • Where Via Guglielmo Marconi 20, Cellere
  • Entry €3, reduced €1,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Sant'Egidio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Wanted by Cardinal Alessandro Farnese and designed by Antonio da Sangallo the Younger at the beginning of the sixteenth century, with frescoes from the sixteenth century and hexagonal floor. The patron saint is celebrated on September 1st with procession and the traditional cookies of Sant'Egidio.

  • Where Cellere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Parco del Timone e Grotta della Mercareccia (Grotta Tiburzi)

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

The Park of the springs of Timone, a tributary of the Flower, has over a thousand plant species, including the rare Etruscan artemisia and the saintly, and 39 species of wild orchids; there is a waterfall and old hydraulic pumps that supplied Cellere. Along the path of the brigand you will meet the Grotto of Mercareccia, the hideout of Tiburzi and his companions; the "Sentiero bandito" (Whipped Path), marked and in five stages, starts from the museum and crosses the park to Pianiano.

  • Where Parco del Timone, Cellere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · simulabo.it

Borgo di Pianiano e chiesa di San Sigismondo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Small medieval fortified village about 4 km from Cellere, Farnesian fief, birthplace of Tiburzi; depopulated at the beginning of the eighteenth century, it was repopulated in the eighteenth century by Albanian families fleeing persecutions, remembered by the painting of the Madonna degli Albanesi in the early medieval church of San Sigismondo martyr, who also has a saintly water with three Farnesian lilies. In July it hosts the MUSA festival.

  • Where Pianiano, Cellere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: simulabo.it · visitlazio.com
