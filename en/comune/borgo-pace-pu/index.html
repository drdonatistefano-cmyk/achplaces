What to see in Borgo Pace

A popular village of the Pesaro Apennines, with hamlets built around medieval castles: in Lamoli the Benedictine abbey of San Michele Arcangelo houses the Museum of Natural Colors.

3 places to visit in Borgo Pace, in the province of Pesaro e Urbino (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo dei colori naturali «Delio Bischi»

Museums · Themed

In the cloister of the abbey of Lamoli, it tells of vegetable pigments from ancient times to the early twentieth century, when synthetic dyes replaced them: archive documents, books and herbs, including the ford (Isatis tinctoria), resource of the valley in the Renaissance. Color extraction laboratory and dye plants cultivated in the meadows around; visits only by reservation (+39 347 860 7585).

  • Where Via dell'Abbazia 7, Lamoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Abbazia di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

An ancient Benedictine complex not far from the town of Lamoli, where the monks kept the knowledge about dye and medicinal plants; in the cloister is the Museum of natural colors.

  • Where Via dell'Abbazia, Lamoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
