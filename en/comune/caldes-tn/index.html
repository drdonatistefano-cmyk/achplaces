What to see in Caldes

Borgo all'incocco della Val di Sole, on the banks of the Noce between woods and apple orchards, entered among the most beautiful villages of Italy in 2023: the name comes from an ancient hot spring today disappeared. Castel Caldes dominates it, a Gothic castle dating back to the thirteenth century of the Thun family today in the Buonconsiglio circuit, with the legend of Olinda prisoner; in the village the cemetery church of San Rocco and a center of houses with stone portals and mullioned windows.

Castel Caldes

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Gothic castle was built after 1230, when Bishop Gerardo allowed Arnold of Cagnò to build a fortified house outside the village; in 1464 he passed to the Thun, who expanded it, and in 1880 he was sold to the Malanotti, before the restoration of the Province. It contains frescoed ceilings, a stone spiral staircase, the stube of the restored count, the painted room called Olinda's prison, linked to the legend of the daughter of the Count in love with the minstrello, and the chapel of the Nativity of Mary frescoed by Elia Naurizio in 1620. It is part of the Castles of Buonconsiglio, it is open in summer with exhibitions, concerts and guided tours and is a stop of the Trenino dei Castelli.

Chiesa cimiteriale di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1510-1512 to the west of the village, it houses a wooden altar attributed to the workshop of Simone Lenner; the bell tower has a stone pyramid cusp.

  • Where Caldes
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visittrentino.info

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Borgo dei Borghi più Belli d'Italia since 2023, preserves buildings with stone portals and double windows such as Casa Fattarsi, Casa Lorengo and Palazzo Manfroni, as well as the parish church of San Bartolomeo. The name, in the Caldis or Caldesio scrolls, comes from an ancient hot spring today disappeared.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Cappelle delle frazioni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The hamlets of Bozzana, Bordiana, San Giacomo and Samoclevo have grown around ancient chapels, which preserve structures and altars from Gothic to Baroque.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Torrente Noce e ciclopedonale

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

At the foot of the village the Noce, indicated by the National Geographic among the ten best rivers in the world for rafting, descends with professional guides; along the stream runs the cycle path of the Val di Sole between meadows and pastures, and the paths in the woods around the village are crossed in every season on foot or by bike.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info
