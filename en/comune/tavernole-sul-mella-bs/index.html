What to see in Tavernole sul Mella

The historic capital of the Val Trompia, linked to the iron processing along the Via del Ferro and the Mines, is worth seeing the fifteenth-century melting furnace now museum, the medieval complex of San Filastrio with the oratory of San Domenico and the parish church of San Calocero in Cimmo.

Museo Il Forno di Tavernole

Museums · Themed

15th century melting furnace on the banks of Mella, one of the most important monuments of industrial archaeology in the area: the iron ore of the mines of Collio, Bovegno and Pezzaze worked, in the 19th century it was transformed into a sawmill, it was abandoned in the 1950s and since 2002 it houses the museum. Panels, audiovisual and audioguide in Italian and English tell about the fusion; on the ground floor the Giuseppe Ganzola Collection of peasant tools, tools and weapons related to the art of the "ferrarezze" (16th-XIX centuries). Stage of the Via del Ferro e delle Miniere dell'Ecomuseo; open all year round, from October to June on Sunday 15-18, to July-September Saturday and Sunday 15-19.

Complesso di San Filastrio e oratorio di San Domenico

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

One of the best examples of medieval architecture in the Val Trompia, in the area of the cemetery just before the village climbing the valley: hut church with slender stone bell tower, wooden portal of the fourteenth century and frescoed exterior walls, and inside paintings from the late Middle Ages to the full Renaissance, including rare fourteenth century frescoes. Outside, the chapel of San Rocco with portico and frescoes of saints; the oratory of San Domenico preserves a cycle of the half fifteenth century attributed to the Cremonese workshop of Bembo, called "Cappella Sistina della Valle Trompia."

Parrocchiale dei Santi Filippo e Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises where in 1523 there was a chapel, of which remains the portal in dark stone; the current building, designed by Carlo Melchiotti, was started in 1899 and is frescoed by Joseph and Vittorio Trainini (1924). It houses a polyptych of Bellinian inspiration of about 1530 coming from San Filastrio, a school altarpiece of Celesti and two wooden statues attributed to Antonio Callegari.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitvalletrompia.it

Sources: comune.tavernolesulmella.bs.it · visitvalletrompia.it

Parrocchiale di San Calocero a Cimmo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Cited from 1302, it depended on the parish church of Bovegno and became a parish in the fifteenth century; the current building derives from extensions of the early eighteenth century, with stuccoes and frescoes by Pietro Scalvini (1751-1765), a Madonna with Child and Saints by Pier Maria Bagnatore (1583) in the middle of the main altar and a frame carved in 1749 by a woodwork of Levrange.

  • Where Cimmo, Tavernole sul Mella
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitvalletrompia.it
