What to see in Scaldasole

Small agricultural town of Lomellina dominated by an imposing castle with shelter, one of the most significant fortified complexes of the province of Pavia, where they stayed Isabella d'Aragona, Massimiliano I of Habsburg and Carlo V; inside the Strada family preserves an archaeological museum with finds of the Lomellina.

Castello di Scaldasole

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Castello e ricetto, a building type unique in Lombardy and spread in the nearby Piedmont: the nucleus probably dates back to the X-XII centuries; in 1404 the Folperti obtained from the Visconti to expand it and build the ricetto, then passed to the Malaspina (with portico and loggia of the fifteenth century) and in 1582 to Cardinal Tolomeo Gallio, who had built chapel, garden and stables. Visconti plant with courtyard and well in brick, battlements, corner towers, moat and barbacan with drawbridge; inside Renaissance fireplaces, loggia of Bramante style and 19th century rooms. ==References====External links==* Official website

Raccolta archeologica Antonio Strada

Museums · Archaeological

Archaeological museum housed in the castle, with objects from all over the Lomellina, including terracotta statuettes and rare Roman glass, such as the white-green cup signed by Aristesas in Albonese.

The National Museums of Lombardy can be visited with the castle only by reservation for groups of at least 15 people; the National Museums of Lombardy have pages on the ...Collezione Strada and on the National Archaeological Museum of Lomellina, not readable: check where the collection is displayed today
