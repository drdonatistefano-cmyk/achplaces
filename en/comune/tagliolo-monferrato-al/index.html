What to see in Tagliolo Monferrato

Borgo dell'Alto Monferrato near Ovada, fief of the count Gentile since 1498 and 1745 of the Pinelli Gentile, who still live in the castle. The castle, with its 10th century tower and wine cellars that have been making wine for five centuries, dominates the medieval village with its ancient churches.

Castello di Tagliolo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Among the best preserved of the Alto Monferrato, it is still inhabited by the Marquis Pinelli Gentile, who received it in 1745 with the marriage between Teresa Gentile and Costantino Pinelli. The square tower, part of the oldest, is from the 10th century and from the top offers a 360-degree view of the hills; the complex brings together towers, houses, a church and a big garden, with frescoed halls, furnishings and arms collections. Wineries have been producing and preserving local wines for 500 years; it is part of the Castelli Aperti circuit (tel. 0143 89195).

Borgo medievale e chiese antiche

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Around the castle there are medieval houses, including the so-called "Casa del Boia" (House of the Boya). In front of the castle the parish church of San Nicolò, documented since 1576; next door, near the ancient public oven, Santa Maria, quoted in 1514. Outside the village the oratory of San Rocco (documents of 1600), San Vito, perhaps the first church of the place (VI century), today the cemetery chapel, and San Defendente, between the end of the sixteenth and early seventeenth centuries, on the road to Belforte.
