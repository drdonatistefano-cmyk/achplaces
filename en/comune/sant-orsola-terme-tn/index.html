What to see in Sant'Orsola Terme

Country at 925 meters on the right side of the Mòcheni Valley, going up from Pergine Valsugana, known for the cultivation of small fruits and for the farms scattered on the sunny slope; in the ancient seventeenth-century town hall the Museum Pietra Viva tells minerals and history of the valley of Fersina.

Museo Pietra Viva

Museums · Themed

In the ancient town hall of Sant'Orsola, a 17th century building frescoed by the popular painter Pietro Albertini of Lavis, the museum tells with minerals and objects of material culture the territory and the history of the valley of Fersina. Highlights a large quartz crystal cave, the municipal council hall and a stream in the garden where to search for minerals; each visit includes two workshops, the search for stones in the stream and the construction of a fossil. Guided tours by reservation in Italian or English, audioguides in German, English and Dutch; open Monday, Tuesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday 10-12 and 14-17.

Passeggiata lungo il torrente Fersina

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Walking or mountain biking path along the Fersina which leads to the old sawmills, mills and one of the oldest mines in the valley, with the feeling, writes the portal, of an open-air museum.

Piccoli frutti e prodotti dei masi

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The country grows widely strawberries, blackberries, raspberries, black currants and blueberries: the small fruits "Sant'Orsola" are now sold all over Italy. In the local shops there are also honey millefiori of mountain, acacia and rhododendron, aromatized grappas and mixtures of herbal teas; two restaurants serve typical dishes.
