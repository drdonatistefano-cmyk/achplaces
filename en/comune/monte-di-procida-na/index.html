What to see in Monte di Procida

Country of the Campi Flegrei overlooking Procida and Ischia, in the Regional Park of the Campi Flegrei: to see the Roman necropolis of Cappella along the ancient Miseno-Cuma road, the Mother Church of the Assumption, the belvedere on the gulf, the islet of San Martino and the marines of Acquamorta and Torrefumo.

Necropoli di Cappella (Misenum)

History and architecture · Archaeology

Hypogeal tombs along the ancient road that connected the municipality of Misenum to Cuma: the excavated section includes at least seven quadrangular funerary buildings and barrel-shaped vaults, aligned in a single fifth, with a pediment kiosk on the back wall and niches on the walls, plus a multi-storey underground mausoleum behind. The oldest monument is late Republican, the others of the first century A.D.; the Municipality recalls the doumbars of officers of the Imperial fleet of Miseno, with portraits and birds frescoed and an inscription citing a military of the ship Virtus.

opening only on request with reservation required (pa-fleg.communication@cultura.gov.it, 393 31188993)

Chiesa Madre di Maria Santissima Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Behind Piazza XXVII January and facing the sea and Procida, it was built in the seventeenth century by Cardinal Ascanio Filomarino and dedicated to the Assumption; enlarged in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, took its present form at the end of the nineteenth century. It preserves the statue of the Assumption of the Neapolitan sculptor Francesco Verzella (1814) and an ancient image of the Madonna considered miraculous, destination of the faithful on 15 August.

  • Where Piazza XXVII Gennaio, Monte di Procida
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.montediprocida.na.it

Belvedere Caduti in Mare e Stupor Mundi

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Two panoramic points signalled by the Municipality: the Belvedere Caduti in Mare, in via Guglielmo Marconi, looks at the islands of Procida, Ischia and Ventotene; the Belvedere Supor Mundi, in via Panoramica, embraces the coast from Capo Miseno to Punta Campanella.

  • Where Via Guglielmo Marconi e Via Panoramica, Monte di Procida
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.montediprocida.na.it · italia.it

Isolotto di San Martino

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Isolotto of about 1600 sqm, accessible for a fee through a tunnel and a jetty; probably detached from the coast with a tidal wave in 1488. In the 5th and 17th centuries it was a base for tuna fishing, in the 1930s for testing the torpedoes of the torpedo factory of Baia and in the post-war meeting of the "sweet life" Neapolitan; today it is a seaside destination and for diving, with deep waters rich in fish and ancient finds.

  • Where Costa di Monte di Procida
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Torrefumo, oasi e spiaggia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural area to be explored on foot, which takes its name from a tower once used to communicate with the mainland by smoke signals. From the beach of Torrefumo entered the village in 1814 the statue of the Assumption, welcomed by a great procession.

  • Where Torrefumo, Monte di Procida
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Marina di Acquamorta

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Marina and beach of the village, mentioned among the observation points of the volcanic geology of the territory; there are several summer events, including the Festival of the Flegrea Sea and the celebrations of Ferragosto with the fireworks show.

  • Where Acquamorta, Monte di Procida
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: oldsite.comune.montediprocida.na.it · italia.it
