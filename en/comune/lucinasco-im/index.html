Museo «Lazzaro Acquarone»: arte sacra e sezione etnografica

Museums · Themed

The museum has three sections: the collection of sacred art with paintings and sculptures from the 15th to the 18th century, the ethnographic section and the peasant house. The ethnographic section, opened in 1998 next to the Museum of Sacred Art, tells of the rural civilization of an area where olive growing is still the main resource: tools and reconstructions of the environment in an ancient barn, a "sala dei lavori" with hundreds of objects of carpenters, blacksmith, carpenter and casket, a "blood" mill moved by animals, a cellar in an ancient cistern, a stable and a country house furnished next to the Oratory of the Disciplinanti. Tel. 0183 52534.

Chiesa monumentale della Maddalena

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the fifteenth century, declared a national monument, in an intact environment surrounded by woods; it is reached by an asphalt road about 1 km beyond the crossroads to Vasia, passing next to a pond with a weeping willow.

Chiesa di Santo Stefano e laghetto

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The eighteenth-century church, already parish and rebuilt in the eighteenth century, is located about 1 km west of the village next to a small and picturesque pond; the park around has football and tennis courts.

Borgo, parrocchiale e resti del castello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village, about 500 meters away, is said by La mia Liguria \'il borgo della luce Â" and offers a 360 degree panorama of mountains, valleys and sea; there you climb from Chiusavecchia among dry walls with age-old olive trees of Taggiasca. The Baroque parish church of 1688 has frescoed interiors, a marble altar attributed to G. Door and B. Stella and an organ of G. Cavalli di Lodi; nearby remain the ruins of the feudal castle of the twelfth century, linked to the Ventimiglia. It was a feud of the Counts of Ventimiglia and Lascaris, passed to the Savoys in 1575 and became an autonomous municipality in 1688.
