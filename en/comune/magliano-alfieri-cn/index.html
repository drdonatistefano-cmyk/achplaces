What to see in Magliano Alfieri

Roero country dominated by the Baroque Castle of the Alfieri, 17th century residence built on the site of the medieval fortress, on a hill overlooking the valley of Tanaro and the Langhe: in its halls the Museum of the culture of plaster, the Landscape Theatre and the chapel of the SS. Crucified.

Castello degli Alfieri

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In the seventeenth century Count Catalano Alfieri, general of the sabauda infantry, had built a baroque residence on the site of the medieval fortress, completed by his son Carlo Emanuele; in 1797 he passed to the Support Alfieri and in 1952 the last descendant left him to the parish, which gave him to the municipality. The door in walnut with carved baroque tiles, the staircase of honor, the fairytale hall and the chapel are outstanding; the tradition that binds you to the inspiration of the Saul by Vittorio Alfieri is judged incorrect by the same source. Open from April to October on weekends, 10.30-18.30; accessible by elevator.

  • Where Via Alfieri 4, Magliano Alfieri
  • Entry €5 a museum, €7 both
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.castellodimaglianoalfieri.com

Sources: castellodimaglianoalfieri.com · comune.maglianoalfieri.cn.it

Museo delle Arti e Tradizioni Popolari - Cultura del Gesso

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Documentation centre opened in 1994 in the east wing of the noble floor of the castle: it collects more than one hundred decorative motifs of the plaster ceilings of the Piedmontese peasant houses, the oldest dated 1580, with salt on the extraction of the plaster, its uses in the construction and finds of the peasant life.

  • Where Castello, Via Alfieri 4, Magliano Alfieri
  • Entry €5, both museums €7, families €10
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.castellodimaglianoalfieri.com

Sources: castellodimaglianoalfieri.com · comune.maglianoalfieri.cn.it · visitpiemonte.com

Teatro del Paesaggio

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Exhibitive route in the castle, led by Antonio Adriano, which reconstructs the long-lasting history of the landscape of Langhe and Roero through documents and material and intangible knowledge, putting the peasants at the centre as protagonists.

  • Where Castello, Via Alfieri 4, Magliano Alfieri
  • Entry €5, both museums €7, families €10
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.castellodimaglianoalfieri.com

Sources: castellodimaglianoalfieri.com · comune.maglianoalfieri.cn.it · visitpiemonte.com

Cappella gentilizia del SS. Crocifisso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Chapel of the castle, stage of the Sentieri dei Frescanti del Roero: ceilings in trompe-l'oeil with angels, attributed to the Operts and in baroque style, including an angel showing the Holy Shroud.

  • Where Via Asilo Alfieri 1, Magliano Alfieri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.maglianoalfieri.cn.it

Sources: comune.maglianoalfieri.cn.it
