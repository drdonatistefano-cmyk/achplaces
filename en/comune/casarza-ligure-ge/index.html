What to see in Casarza Ligure

Borgo della Val Petronio a few kilometers from the sea of Tigullio, with Roman origins and a name that tradition derives from the "casa arsa" , between olive groves and chestnut groves it preserves the geological formations of pillows, the Lake of Roccagrande and the mineralogical museum Parma Gemma.

Museo mineralogico Parma Gemma

Museums · Science and university

Born from a private collection of Apennine minerals, it exhibits thousands of pieces, some visible only under the microscope, in three rooms with three hundred and fifty windows: copper, manganese, sulphur and plaster minerals of the Ligurian-Tuscan-Emilian Apennines, as well as specimens from other regions and from abroad. Outside a litological museum gathers hundreds of large mineralized rocks along a route; the museum also organizes free guided tours to the mines in the area. Tel. 0185 46229.

Pillows

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

In the territory of Casarza, there are pillows, geological formations that testify to ancient underwater volcanic eruptions.

Lago di Roccagrande

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small lake of the hinterland of Casarza born from phenomena dating back to the glacial era, in a territory of olive groves, chestnut groves and panoramic paths.
