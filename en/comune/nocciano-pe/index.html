What to see in Nocciano

A hilly country in the valleys of the Swan, Pescara and Nora, dominated by the castle De Sterlich-Aliprandi, owned by the municipality and seat of the Museum of Arts dedicated to contemporary Abruzzo artists.

Castello De Sterlich-Aliprandi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

An irregular double-plan castle with a polygonal main façade reached by a large stone ramp; the cobbled courtyard leads to a three-arched loggia with Ghibelline blackbirds on the noble floor. Some scholars date to the year 1000 the western tower with a polygonal sloping base; the family De Sterlich-Aliprandi, present from the XV-XVI century, owned it until the mid-Twentieth century, when the building had already lost its military function. With the houses-walls, it delimits a ring space around the hill; the restoration was completed in 1993 and many rooms conserve vaults, arches and stone pillars.

Museo delle Arti (Museo e archivio degli artisti abruzzesi contemporanei)

Museums · Modern art

Permanent collection of contemporary Abruzzo artists in the rooms of the castle, with works by Franco Summa, Michele Cascella, Nino Caffè and Remo Brindisi, and temporary exhibitions with activities including visual arts, literature, theater, cinema, music and new technologies. Hours (MiC card): Saturday 10-12:30, Sunday 16-18:30; other days and guided tours by telephone (349 6096186).
