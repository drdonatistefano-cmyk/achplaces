What to see in Campiglia Marittima

Medieval walled village of the Val di Cornia on the hill of the Fortress, overlooking the Gulf of Baratti and the Tuscan Archipelago; in the north the Archaeological Park of San Silvestro tells three thousand years of mines.

6 places to visit in Campiglia Marittima, in the province of Livorno (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parco archeominerario di San Silvestro

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the hills north of the village, 450 hectares of the mining district exploited from the Etruscan era until 1976: the galleries of the Temperino Mine, the Lanzi-Temperino tunnel by mining train and the medieval village of Rocca San Silvestro, a village of miners and founders of the Della Gherardesca.

  • Where San Silvestro, Campiglia Marittima
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visittuscany.com

Rocca di Campiglia e museo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the highest hill in the country, at 281 meters: the cassero, an ancient cistern, a crenellated wall with a bifora of the XI-XV and an aqueduct of the Thirties; in the castron the excavations, a helmet, a armour and medieval weapons.

  • Where Campiglia Marittima
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Palazzo Pretorio e Museo del Minerale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The highest building in the centre, the ancient seat of political and military power, with the clock tower, houses the historical archive, the Carlo Guarnieri Museum and the Museum of the Mineral of the Toscano Mineral Circle.

  • Where Campiglia Marittima
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
