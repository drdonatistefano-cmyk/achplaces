What to see in Roseto degli Abruzzi

Adriatic seaside resort with Blue Flag beach since 1999, promenade and cycle path of the Adriatic Cyclovia; on the hill the medieval village of Montepagano with doors, church of the Annunziata and Museum of Material Culture, and to the north the Borsacchio Reserve.

Spiaggia e lungomare

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The low town overlooks the Adriatic with fine sandy beach and stretches of free beach; shallow slopes gently, Blue Flag since 1999 and Green Flag for the beaches suitable for children. On the local seafront and restaurants and the cycle path of the Adriatic Cyclovia.

  • Where Lungomare, Roseto degli Abruzzi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Montepagano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The oldest nucleus of Roseto, on a hill north of the mouth of the Vomano overlooking the sea: of medieval origin, the inhabitants of the coast would have taken refuge to escape the Turkish raids; the name comes from the Latin "mons" and "pagus" . There are still parts of the fourteenth-century walls with the Door of Feet, unique arched with sharp sixth and overlooking Roseto, and the Door of Borea, where the duties were collected, with the coat of arms of Acquaviva di Atri.

  • Where Piazza del Municipio, Montepagano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitroseto.it

Chiesa della Santissima Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt at the beginning of the seventeenth century in place of an ancient temple (later portal dated 1611), with brick façade and simple stone portal; single nave interior with four chapels on the side, transept and octagonal dome, eighteenth-century altars and paintings of the seventeenth century.

  • Where Via del Borgo 44, Montepagano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitroseto.it · italia.it

Museo civico della cultura materiale

Museums · Themed

Born in 1987 on the initiative of citizens of Montepagano with publicist Luigi Braccili, he is set up in a peasant house: agricultural tools, furnishings of the late nineteenth century, cooking branches, wedding accessories and vintage clothes, a herbarium with dialect names of plants, photographs from 1850 to 1950, coins and posters of the Second World War. An audioguide is available.

Civica raccolta d'arte

Museums · Modern art

Inaugurated on August 29, 1981 in the municipal villa, he exhibited works by local painters Pasquale Celommi (born 1851), author of "Il Ciabattino" and his son Raffaello Celommi (born 1881), known for the marines; also present a terracotta by Alfonso Tentrelli and a canvas by Gigino Falconi.

the MiC card indicates every day as closed; times to check (tel. 085 8930101)

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Riserva del Borsacchio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Reserve of about 1,100 hectares established in 2005 between Roseto, Cologna Spiaggia and Montepagano, with hilly and coastal landscapes, embryonic and consolidated dunes and about 120 plant species, many rare in Abruzzo; on the protected beach nests the friar. The project "Viti in Riserva" recovers abandoned vineyards.

  • Where Via Nazionale 21, Roseto degli Abruzzi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitroseto.it · italia.it

Pontile

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Born as a pier to defend the beach from the Vomano gravels and the marshes, it became a pier with the renewal of the area in 1983; it stretches for more than one hundred meters to a roundabout overlooking the coast. In front, in a green square, the bronze statue of Daniele Guerrieri dedicated to the fallen of the sea.

the source indicates important renovations in progress on the dock

  • Where Lungomare Trieste, Roseto degli Abruzzi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitroseto.it · italia.it

Pineta Savini

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Pineta publishes near the center, overlooking the northern promenade, acquired by the Municipality in the late 1970s: benches, a dry tub designed by the painter Luigi Celommi, play area and free fitness area. It bears the name of Sigismondo Savini, whose villa rises in front.

  • Where Via Nazionale 48, Roseto degli Abruzzi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitroseto.it · italia.it

Botte della Cantina Mazzarosa

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

In the cellar of Villa Mazzarosa, wanted by Senator Giuseppe De Notaris, there is a barrel that could contain 1,000 hectolitres of wine, presented at an exhibition in 1905 and indicated as the largest in Europe.

Fonte dell'Accolle

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Ancient source of Montepagano, probably in use since the 11th century when the village took shape, composed of fountain, watering trough for the cattle in a niche and washroom covered in stone where women washed the clothes.

  • Where Via Accolle, Montepagano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
