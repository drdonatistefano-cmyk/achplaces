What to see in Bagno di Romagna

4 places to visit in Bagno di Romagna, in the province of Forlì-Cesena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 2 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Centro termale e Palazzo del Capitano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The spa village on the way to Tuscany, with the Capitano Palace and the Basilica of Santa Maria Assunta.

  • Where Bagno di Romagna
  • Entry Free walk; paid spa
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Parco Nazionale delle Foreste Casentinesi

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Forests of beech and fir trees on the ridge of Tuscany-Romagna. In the park there is the integral reserve of Sasso Fratino, one of the ancient beech woods UNESCO World Heritage.

  • Where Bagno di Romagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Diga e lago di Ridracoli

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

The 1982 dam, 103,5 meters high, forms a lake in the heart of the park; there is the Hydro-Ecomuseum and you sail with an electric boat.

  • Where Ridracoli, Bagno di Romagna
  • Entry To be checked: visitor center and boat for a fee
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sorgenti del Tevere e Monte Fumaiolo

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

On Mount Fumaiolo, on the border with Tuscany, the Tiber and Savio are born, starting point of excursions in the woods.

  • Where Monte Fumaiolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
