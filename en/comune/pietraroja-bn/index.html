What to see in Pietraroja

Borgo del Matese beneventano where about 110 million years ago there was a lagoon: here Ciro was found, the Scipionyx samniticus, the first dinosaur discovered in Italy, told in the Paleolab of the geopaleontological park; in the village the church of Santa Maria Assunta and the alleys of the center.

Parco geopaleontologico e Paleolab

Museums · Science and university · Free entry

The park museum exhibits the fossils of the site, which affects naturalists from the seventeenth century: fishes (Belonostomus), reptiles (Chometokadmon, Derasmosaurus) and amphibians (Celtedens) of the lagoon about 110 million years ago. It tells the story of Cyrus, a young theropod found in 1980 and recognized in 1993, with some internal organs preserved: his discovery changed the reconstruction of the Italian paleogeography, which was believed to be all submerged in the Mesozoic. The original fossil is kept at the headquarters of the Superintendence in Benevento.

  • Where Via Fontanelle 14, Pietraroja
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.pietraroja.bn.it · italia.it · sabapce-bn.cultura.gov.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque church in the center of the village, with a single nave with the altar dedicated to the Assumption and ancient works of sacred art, among frescoes and wooden sculptures. The portal is supported by a lioness and a bear that feeds two small ones.

  • Where Piazza San Nicola, Pietraroja
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.pietraroja.bn.it · italia.it

Vicoli del centro e prosciutto di Pietraroja

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry

A village of narrow streets, squares, ancient houses and churches; in Via Torino an relief with a disfigured face, attributed by the belief to an exorcism, and an inscription in a mysterious language. The local ham, without preservatives, remains about twenty days in the big salt, is pressed with the 'prigiotto' and seasoned in the cellar from 12 to 20 months; among the dishes there are the carrati with sheep sauce.

  • Where Centro storico, Pietraroja
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
