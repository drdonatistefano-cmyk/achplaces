What to see in Ancona

The capital of the Marches overlooking the Adriatic, between the hill Guasco with the Cathedral of San Ciriaco, the ancient port with the Arch of Traiano and the Mole Vanvitelliana, up to the cliffs of the Passetto and the bay of Portonovo.

26 places to visit in Ancona, in the province of Ancona (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di San Ciriaco

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Romanesque-Gothic basilica was built on the remains of a temple dedicated to Venus and a early Christian basilica, which dominates the city and the port from the hill of Guasco in a spectacular way.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Ancona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Diocesano "Cesare Recanatini"

Museums · Old masters

Founded in 1834, it has more than 460 paintings from the 14th to the 19th century, sculptures, fabrics, jewels, coins and the treasure of the Cathedral; since 1952 it has been located in the former episcopio next to the Duomo.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Ancona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: anconatourism.it · italia.it

Mole Vanvitelliana

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Built between 1733 and 1743 on a project by Luigi Vanvitelli at the behest of Clement XII as a lazzaretto, cargo depot and port defense, it rises on an artificial island connected to the mainland by three bridges; today it houses the Homer Museum, exhibitions, concerts and a summer cinema.

  • Where Banchina Giovanni da Chio, Ancona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: anconatourism.it · museoomero.it · italia.it

Museo Tattile Statale Omero

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1985 and at Mole Vanvitelliana since 2012, it allows you to know art with touch: casts of Greek masterpieces, sculptures by Michelangelo and Canova and works by contemporary artists such as De Chirico and Marini.

  • Where Banchina Giovanni da Chio 28, Ancona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: anconatourism.it · museoomero.it · italia.it

Arco di Traiano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Erected in 115 A.D. and attributed to Apollodoro of Damascus, in large blocks of marble imetius on a stone podium of the Conero.

  • Where Porto antico, Ancona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: anconatourism.it · italia.it

Porto antico

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Roman port brought to light by the excavations of 1998-2001, with the Captain's House of the 13th century, the only medieval building survived the bombings of 1943, and sections of walls of the 2nd century B.C.

  • Where Porto antico, Ancona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: anconatourism.it · italia.it

Arco Clementino

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Wanted by Pope Clement XII in the eighteenth century, designed by Luigi Vanvitelli and completed by Filippo Marchionni, is the gateway from the port to the city.

  • Where Porto antico, Ancona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: anconatourism.it · italia.it

Anfiteatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the saddle between the hills of Guasco and Cappuccini, overlooking the port and the old town, it dates back to the Augustan age and was expanded under Traiano; you visit only with guide, departing from the National Archaeological Museum.

  • Where Colle Guasco, Ancona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: anconatourism.it · italia.it

Museo Archeologico Nazionale delle Marche

Museums · Archaeological

Reperti dal Paleolithic al Medievalo, tra cui la Venus di Frasassi e oggetti di Piceni e Senoni, nel cinquecentesco Palazzo Ferretti, sede del museo dal 1958.

  • Where Palazzo Ferretti, Ancona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: anconatourism.it · italia.it

Pinacoteca Civica "Francesco Podesti"

Museums · Old masters

Founded in 1884, it preserves the Gozzi Palace of Tiziano, the Madonna and Child by Carlo Crivelli and the Sacred Conversation of Lorenzo Lotto; since 1973 it has been located in Palazzo Bosdari, with a section in the nearby Palazzo Bonomini.

  • Where Via Ciriaco Pizzecolli 17, Ancona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: anconatourism.it · italia.it

Loggia dei Mercanti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Started in 1442 on a project by Giovanni Pace called Sodo, with the façade made by Giorgio Orsini from Šibenik between 1454 and 1459.

  • Entry Free

Fontana del Calamo (delle Tredici Cannelle)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Documented as early as 1426, it has twelve bronze masks of satyrs and fauns around the Rising Knight in stone, emblem of the city.

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Ancient urban gate near the Mole and the port, designed by Filippo Marchionni; the papal coat of arms and the friezes were chiseled during the French occupation of the late eighteenth century.

Viale della Vittoria e Monumento ai Caduti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The tree-lined avenue of the thirties connects the port to the cliffs of the Passetto; at the end, in Piazza IV Novembre, the Monument to the Fallen by Guido Cirilli (1927-1930, inaugurated in 1932).

Spiaggia del Passetto

Urban beach under the cliffs, with the rock called Seggiola del Papa and a small white stone beach; you go down on foot, by elevator or with the stairway under the Monument to the Fallen.

Grotte del Passetto

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 500 cavities dug by fishermen from the mid-nineteenth century to the sixties to store nets and boats, with colored doors and recovery materials.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Baia di Portonovo

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The most frequented bay of the Conero, with free and equipped pebble beach (Blue Flag since 2010), the Romanesque church of Santa Maria di Portonovo of the 11th century cited by Dante, the Tower De Bosis, the Napoleonic fort and two brackish lakes.
