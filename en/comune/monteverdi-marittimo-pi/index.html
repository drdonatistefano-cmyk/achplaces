What to see in Monteverdi Marittimo

Small fortified village on the Metallifere Hills, born around the abbey founded by St. Walfredo in 754.

3 places to visit in Monteverdi Marittimo, in the province of Pisa (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Monteverdi Marittimo in the guide with map and filters

Riserva di Monterufoli-Caselli e Cascata della Sterza

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The reserve between the Trossa and Ristoto streams, rich in wild orchids, lakes and the Sterza Waterfall, which is thrown into a clear puddle.

  • Where Monteverdi Marittimo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Ruderi dell'Abbazia di San Pietro in Palazzuolo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The remains of the monastery founded in 754 by the Longobard Walfredo, reachable by a nice walk.

  • Where Monteverdi Marittimo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa di Sant'Andrea e le "rughe"

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The church with the relics of St. Walfredo, the starting point of the "wrinkles," the maze of alleys of the village.

  • Where Monteverdi Marittimo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
