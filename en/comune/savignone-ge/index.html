What to see in Savignone

Country of the Upper Valley Scrivia on the Padano side of the Ligurian Apennines, dominated by the remains of the castle of the Fieschi, with a square of historical palaces (Palazzo Fieschi, Palazzo della Meridiana, the old hospital) and the church of San Pietro; the territory is part of the Regional Natural Park of the Antola and is crossed by the High Via dei Monti Liguri.

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It dates back to the thirteenth century and rises on the remains of an ancient abbey; after a fire in the seventeenth century was rebuilt in 1691 by Urbano Fieschi, Count of Lavagna and Marquis of the fief of Savignon. Inside seventeenth-century paintings, including a canvas by Giovanni Battista Carlone, a Visitation of Saint Catherine from Genoa and a wooden statue of the 17th century Immaculate attributed to the school of Maragliano. Info: tel. 010 936627.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa 1, Savignone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Palazzo Fieschi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in the second half of the sixteenth century for the Fieschi family, who lived there until 1856; it became a hydrotherapy plant that exploited an internal source. Restored in the late 1990s, it is now a historic residence used as a hotel, meetings, banquets and ceremonies. Info: Municipality, Service culture and tourism, tel. 010 9360103.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa 14, Savignone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it · italia.it

Piazza del paese e palazzi storici

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The town square is dominated by historic buildings: the Palazzo della Meridiana, the old eighteenth-century hospital, Palazzo Fieschi and the church of San Pietro. The village, with traces of settlement from the fourth century BC, passed in 1242 to Genoa, which infeudated it to Spinola, and at the end of the thirteenth century to the counts Fieschi of Lavagna; from the mid nineteenth century became a holiday destination of the Genoese families.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa, Savignone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcoantola.it

Ruderi del castello dei Fieschi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortility of the 13th century, castle and manor residence that barred the road to the enemies of the Republic of Genoa, for centuries of the Fieschi. The best preserved part is the tower; behind stood the real castle, leaning on a rocky spur that preserves traces of the internal layout. The site is especially valid for the remarkable landscape value.

  • Where Savignone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it · parcoantola.it · italia.it

Museo degli alpini

Museums · Themed

Managed by the section of Genoa of the National Alpine Association, documents the history of the Alpine troops and the association from the foundation to today with photographs, documents, uniforms, badges and relics, with attention to the Alpine traditions of the Ligurian valleys. It has a library, two exhibitions (on the War of Liberation and on the history of the Alpine troops) and two audiovisuals, distributed between the Tricolor Hall, the Hall of Fighters, the Hall of Father Basil, the Hall of Delfo Pieramati and the Hall of Cuneense Division; guided tours available. The MiC card (updated to 2020) does not show usable times: contact the museum (tel. 010 936930, museumeglialpini@libero.it).

  • Where Via Alpino Angelo Caprile, Savignone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Sentieri del Parco dell'Antola e Alta Via dei Monti Liguri

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The territory, hilly and mountain on the two banks of the High Scrivia, partly falls into the Regional Natural Park of Antola and is crossed by the High Via dei Monti Liguri; the network of roads and paths allows quiet walks and more challenging excursions, including spring and summer blooms, mushrooms, birds, deer settled in the woods for some years and traces of the wolf.

  • Where Savignone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it · italia.it
