What to see in Garbagnate Milanese

The town of the northern hinterland of Milan overlooks the Groane Park, with the Museum of Siolo dedicated to peasant life and furnaces, the historical park of Villa Gianotti and the Basilica of Saints Eusebio and Maccabei.

4 places to visit in Garbagnate Milanese, in the province of Milan (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo di Siolo – Mostra permanente della vita contadina e delle fornaci

Museums · Themed

In Siolo, it hosts a permanent exhibition on peasant life and kilns, with numerous vintage instruments, and various events; access is linked to the initiatives of the association A.Mu.Si (Friends of the Museum of Siolo).

the city card does not indicate times: opening linked to the events of the association A.Mu.Si

  • Where Piazza del Lavatoio, località Siolo, Garbagnate Milanese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.garbagnate-milanese.mi.it

Sources: comune.garbagnate-milanese.mi.it

Parco delle Groane

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Regional protected area of about 7,700 hectares, established in 1976 and natural park since 2011, with residues of oak trees, pinewoods of silvestre, moors where the calluna blooms at the end of summer, ruins of furnaces and patrician villas with gardens. It is accessible throughout the perimeter, also near the hospital and the train station, and is crossed by about 50 km of bike paths.

  • Where Via della Polveriera, Garbagnate Milanese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.parcogroane.it

Sources: parcogroane.it · comune.garbagnate-milanese.mi.it

Parco di Villa Gianotti

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Partly historic garden with gazebo, in the historic center overlooking Largo Arturo Anelli, cool in summer thanks to a vegetation partly centuries old; there is an open bar kiosk except in winter. From via Milano 23 or via Monza 12, from the gate at the end of the library parking lot.

  • Where Largo Arturo Anelli, Garbagnate Milanese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.garbagnate-milanese.mi.it

Sources: comune.garbagnate-milanese.mi.it

Basilica dei Santi Eusebio e Maccabei

Places of worship · Basilicas

Parish church built in 1940 and completed in the 1940s, in the city center, with a Latin cross plan with three aisles; the facade has three entrances.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa, Garbagnate Milanese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.garbagnate-milanese.mi.it

Sources: comune.garbagnate-milanese.mi.it
