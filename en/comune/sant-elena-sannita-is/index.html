What to see in Sant'Elena Sannita

Small town of Alto Molise, called Cameli until 1896 and now known as "the town of perfumers": the Museum of Perfume, opened in 2014 with over 1,400 pieces of perfumery and a botanical garden, tells this story. In the village the churches of San Michele Arcangelo and Santa Maria delle Grazie have frescoes by the local painter Mario De Tollis.

Museo del Profumo e orto botanico

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated on 17 August 2014, it exhibits on two floors about 1,400-1,500 pieces of perfumery, especially from the early 20th century, collected in over a century by the perfumers of the country and donated to the Fondazione "Il Camino del Profumo" by the Muzio family. The route starts from the wheel of the "arrotino," crosses the sensory botanical garden (2015) and goes from the "low perfumery" to the perfumes from the Egyptians to the Renaissance to the great modern brands. From September to July you visit by reservation (at least one day before); in August it is open every day 9:30-13:00 and 16:30-20:00.

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo e Chiesa dell'Addolorata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

The parish church, on the highest hill of the village and reached by a stone staircase, was built in 1600, rebuilt in 1805 after a earthquake and redesigned in 1937; the vault is frescoed by Mario De Tollis with the Ascent of the Virgin. Next to it, the Church of the Sorrowful preserves a wooden Madonna of the sixteenth century, two paintings by Carmine Muliere and the "Deposition" frescoed in 1924 by Arnaldo De Lisio.

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Considered coeval at the birth of the village, wanted by the feudal lords next to the Baronial Palace and originally called Santa Maria in Castello. It collapsed in the earthquake of 1805 and rebuilt in 1826, it has three naves with three apses and frescoes by Mario De Tollis in the aisles.

Chiesa dei Santi Cosma e Damiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

About a mile from the village: the church built by Domenico Verdile, abandoned in 1870 and reopened in 1895, is flanked by the new church inaugurated in 1987. He preserves a canvas by Mario De Tollis with the two saints.
