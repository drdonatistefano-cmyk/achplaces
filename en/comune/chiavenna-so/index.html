What to see in Chiavenna

The "Chiave delle Alpi" is 333 metres along the Mera at the confluence between Val Bregaglia and Val San Giacomo: a historic centre with elegant palaces, ollar stone fountains and via Dolzino, the Collegiate Church of San Lorenzo with the baptismal font of 1156 and the Peace of the Treasure Museum, the Parco del Paradiso and the Marmitte dei Giganti, and the crotti of Pratogiano.

Collegiata di San Lorenzo e Battistero

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church with three naves, with four portico columns of ollar stone built between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries and bell tower of 1597, once a civic tower; in the octagonal baptistery the Romanesque baptismal font of 1156. Inside paintings by Pietro Ligari (1738) and the Madonna with Child and Saint Anthony by Giuseppe Nuvolone (1657), which also frescoed the cupola of the chapel.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo del Tesoro di San Lorenzo

Museums · Old masters

On the ground floor of the rectory, in four rooms with works from the lower Middle Ages to the eighteenth century: sacred paintings, Baroque liturgical fabrics, Renaissance wooden sculptures, reliquaries and illuminated codes. The symbol piece is Chiavenna's "Peace" (Peace), one of the most important medieval jewels in Europe. Inaugurated in 1957, closed in 1981 after attempted theft, reopened on 3 May 1998.

  • Where Piazza don Pietro Bormetti, Chiavenna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · valchiavenna.com · italia.it

Riserva naturale delle Marmitte dei Giganti

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Regional reserve set up in 1983 on the north side of the city: pots dug into the rock by the glacial fusion waters in the Quaternary, among the most complete examples in Europe, with smoothed and striated rocks, erratic boulders, rock engravings and ancient oily stone quarries. Paths on old mule tracks with panels and viewpoints on the waterfall of the Aquafraggia. Free access.

  • Where Chiavenna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: valchiavenna.com · italia.it

Parco Archeologico Botanico del Paradiso

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the hills of Paradiso and Castellaccio, separated by the gorge of the Caurga, ancient Roman quarry of ollar stone, and connected by a suspended walkway; since 1955 it is part of the Museum of Valchiavenna, with rare flowers, walls and fortifications and an archaeological path in four sections from Prehistory to the Middle Ages. From Tuesday to Sunday 10-12 and 14-18 (17 in winter), Monday closed.

  • Where Parco del Paradiso, Chiavenna
  • Entry €3, reduced €1,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: valchiavenna.com · italia.it

Via Dolzino e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The city's living room, once an urban stretch of the road to the Passo del Settimo: palaces with portals, coats of arms and balconies of families like the Salis, Palazzo Pestalozzi-Salis of the sixteenth century where Ludovico Castelvetro died, the Piazza Ploncher with the stone of the gogna and sixteenth-century frescoed houses; traces of the walls remain.

  • Where Via Dolzino, Chiavenna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · valchiavenna.com · italia.it

Pratogiano e i crotti

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry

Crotti, natural cellars at constant temperature under the rocks on the left bank of Mera, used since ancient times to age the wine and season cold cuts and cheeses, today partly in taverns; the square with plane trees and nineteenth century hippocastans hosts in September the Festival of Crotti.

  • Where Pratogiano, Chiavenna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · italia.it

Mulino di Bottonera

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1867 in the artisan district of Bottonera, next to the bakery of Carlo Moro: on three floors, with pulleys, belts and millstones, he worked day and night until the sixties; today the museum of the cereal cycle, from cleaning to flour.

temporarily closed for restoration work
