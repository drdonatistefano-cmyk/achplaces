What to see in Castelnuovo di Porto

Medieval village on a ridge of tuff between the valleys of Chiarano and Mola, gathered around the Fortress Colonna with the Sala Pinta frescoed by Federico Zuccari; the territory is part of the Regional Natural Park of Veio, with Etruscan-capenati necropolis.

Rocca Colonna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

At the highest point of the country; the first fortification, before the Thousand, was of the Roman prince and senator Alberico. The columns, lords from the fifteenth century, the walls with circular towers and Alessandro and Sciarra Colonna transformed it into a Renaissance palace. It preserves frescoed rooms, ancient prisons, a small medieval church and the Sala Pinta, frescoed by Federico Zuccari with the foundation of Rome, Roman legends, battles and the possessions of the Colonna.

Collegiata di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Inside the walls, rebuilt in Baroque style between 1753 and 1756 on a more ancient church of which remains the bell tower of the thirteenth century. In the Chapel of the Effects, he preserves the Triptych of the Saviour of 1501, attributed to the school of Antoniazzo Romano.

Chiesa di San Sebastiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small medieval church along the path of the ancient Vetus Bell, with a frescoed chapel; a fresco of the fifteenth century probably depicts the village as it was originally.

Parco di Veio e necropoli di Belmonte

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

The Veio Regional Natural Park, established in 1997, covers about 15,000 hectares north of Rome with 99 km of trails marked by the CAI, including the Via Francigena. Around the village it contains the Etruscan-Capenati necropolis of Belmonte, Monte Fiore and Vaccareccia; in Belmonte a large rock necropolis and the ruins of a medieval village with a thirteenth-century tower.

  • Where Parco di Veio, Castelnuovo di Porto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com
