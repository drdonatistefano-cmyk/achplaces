What to see in Pietralunga

Medieval village of the Upper Tiber Valley overlooking the Carpinella valley, with walls of the 8th century, the characteristic "vigoli" , the remains of the Longobard fortress and the parish church of Santa Maria; just outside the walls the pine forest of Candeleto with the Silvio Children Ornithological Museum.

7 places to visit in Pietralunga, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico, mura e vigoli

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village preserves the appearance of a medieval village surrounded by walls of the eighth century, covered by narrow 'vigoli , of the three original gates only the Porta del Cassero is well preserved. Rebuilt after the barbaric invasions under the name of Plebs Tuphiae, it was an autonomous commune from the 11th to the 14th century.

  • Where Centro storico, Pietralunga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Rocca longobarda

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortress of the eighth century, gastaldale headquarters and military outpost, several times modified, with a pentagonal watchtower called "Rocca" (Rocca), today remains the ruins and the doorway, in front of the parish, whose original level was higher than the current one.

  • Where Piazza Fiorucci, Pietralunga
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Pieve di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Pieve da origini tra VII e VIII secolo, tra i titoli più antichi della diocese di Città di Castello, con portale romanico del 1279; transformed at the beginning of the twentieth century, has a single nave with oval vaults, a fresco of the Martyrdom of San Sebastiano attributed to Raffaellino del Colle and a copy of the polyptych of Ottaviano Nelli, whose original is at the National Gallery of Umbria.

  • Where Piazza Fiorucci, Pietralunga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Museums · Science and university

Pineta e Oasi di Candeleto

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Pineta just outside the walls with lush vegetation and areas for camping, refreshment and relaxation, where the ornithological museum is located.

  • Where Candeleto, Pietralunga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Santuario della Madonna dei Rimedi

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Ancient extra-urban parish church about 2 km south of the center, erected between VII and VIII century along a medieval path between Adriatic and Tyrrhenian; it became a center of Marian devotion for the apparitions narrated by tradition, was expanded in the XVII century and, according to tradition, St. Francis stopped there going to Verna.

  • Where Circa 2 km a sud del centro, Pietralunga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Chiesa de' Saddi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

To the south-west of the village, it is one of the oldest churches in the area of Città di Castello: early Christian basilica built around the crypt that guarded the tomb of San Crescenziano, a legionary martyrized here under Diocletian, with a bas-relief of the 8th century of the saint who kills the dragon.

  • Where Saddi, Pietralunga
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it
