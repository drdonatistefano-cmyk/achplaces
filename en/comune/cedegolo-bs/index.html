What to see in Cedegolo

A land of the average Camonica Valley, for a long time among the most important in the valley for iron workshops and trade along the ancient Valeriana Street, of which the noble palaces remain; today it is known for the Musil, the hydroelectric energy museum in the central of 1910, and for the parish church of San Girolamo.

MUSIL - Museo dell'energia idroelettrica di Valle Camonica

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 2008 in the former hydroelectric power plant built in 1909-1910 on a project by the engineer and architect Egidio Dabbeni from Brescia and remained in operation until 1962, overlooking the Oglio. On 3,200 m2, 6 floors and 12 halls, 17 installations follow the path of water from rain to dams (with the case of the Gleno), from the forced pipe to turbine, alternator and transformer to consumption, with a tribute to Ermanno Olmi and a central in virtual reality; panoramic balcony on the river. Since 2015, it has also been a point of the European Network of Industrial Heritage ERIH. Season 2026: 4 April-1 November, Saturday and Sunday 14-19, in August every day; groups by reservation.

seasonal opening: season 2026 closed on November 1st, reopening scheduled for April (MUSIL site)

  • Where Via Roma 48, Cedegolo
  • Entry €7, reduced €5, residents €3; free under 6 years
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.musilbrescia.it

Sources: musilbrescia.it · visitbrescia.it · touringclub.it · turismovallecamonica.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Girolamo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of the eighteenth century appreciated for the portal, with frescoes by A. Capello. The 17th-century high altar has a monumental tabernacle and an ancon attributed to Pietro Ramus; the palace is the work of Giovanni Giuseppe Piccini.

  • Where Via S. Girolamo, Cedegolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Sources: comune.cedegolo.bs.it · turismovallecamonica.it

Villa Simoncini (ex Panzerini) e Casa Panzerini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The villa was built between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries by the influential Panzerini family and then passed to the Simoncini; it is accessed near the parish church of San Girolamo, but the interior is not visitable. A little further north, Casa Panzerini hosts associations, library, historical archives and exhibition and cultural halls.

  • Where Via S. Girolamo 22, Cedegolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Sources: turismovallecamonica.it

Vasche di Grevo

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hydro-electric basins can be reached on foot from the village of Grevo or by asphalt road from Isola di Cevo, with panoramic views of the valley bottom of Sellero.

  • Where Grevo, Cedegolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Sources: turismovallecamonica.it
