What to see in Pescopennataro

Borgo dell'Alto Molise at about 1,200 meters, overlooking the whole valley of Sangro and known as the village of the stone for its historical maestros chiara chiara chiara Marinelli, told by the Museum of the Stone; around woods of white fir, red fir, beech and cerro, with the rock hermitage of San Luca.

Museo civico della Pietra «Chiara Marinelli»

Museums · Themed

Founded in 2006, it tells of the tradition of stone processing in the country, where in the eighteenth century a school of stonemasons was born and where the stonemasons still work. It exhibits prehistoric finds of Alto Molise, inhabited already in the lower Paleolithic and Neolithic, including jewels and idols collected by the spouses Pietro Patriarch and Fortuna Ciavolino, who dedicated it to his nephew Chiara Marinelli, who disappeared young, and a handcrafted crib of polished stones.

  • Where Largo Caduti per la Patria, 86080 Pescopennataro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa madre di San Bartolomeo Apostolo e belvedere

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Mother church well maintained on the slope of the village, rebuilt in the twentieth century after the world wars, with a golden wooden high altar that Visit Molise indicates among the most beautiful in the region. A steep road above the church leads to a viewpoint over the Sangro valley, from which with clear sky you can see the Adriatic.

  • Where Pescopennataro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitmolise.eu · italia.it

Chiesa della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the center of the village, inside creates the optical illusion of three naves and preserves lateral altars with original stucco decorations and statues of saints.

  • Where Centro storico, Pescopennataro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitmolise.eu · italia.it

Eremo di San Luca

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small chapel made in a cave in the woods of the "Abetti Soprani"; tradition binds it to St. Luke, without documentary evidence. The fireplace and the wooden mezzanine show that there lived hermits; from the site starts a path with a view of the Sangro valley.

  • Where Bosco degli Abeti Soprani, Pescopennataro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Boschi di abete bianco e sorgenti del Rio Verde

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Around the village there is the Wood of the Empire and other fir woods, a rare species in the Apennines, spruce, beech and oak, with nature trails, cross-country skiing trails, equipped areas, water courses and fountains; a few kilometers away are the springs of the Rio Verde.

  • Where Bosco dell'Impero, Pescopennataro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu
