What to see in Tradate

The town of Varesotto, an ancient feud of the Pusterla and then an industrial village thanks to the factory of motorbikes Frera (1905-1936), whose sheds now house museum and library; next door, the largest collection of vintage petrol pumps in the Museo Fisogni and, in the woods of the Pineta Park, an astronomical observatory with planetary.

Museo della Motocicletta Frera

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Opened on July 17, 2005, centenary of the brand, in the former plant in via Zara where in 1905 Corrado Frera started the workshop to build bicycles and motorcycles: the company Anonima Frera produced models appreciated for about thirty years, transformed Tradate from an agricultural village into an industrial town and closed in 1936. One section exhibits 25 motorcycles and 5 Frera bikes, the other bike of historical Lombard and Italian brands such as MV Agusta, Cagiva, Gilera and Moto Guzzi. Open Thursday 20-22, Saturday and Sunday 15-18 (variable hours on holidays and summer); in the library and cafe building.

  • Where Via Zara 37, Tradate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.tradate.va.it

Sources: comune.tradate.va.it · in-lombardia.it · vareseturismo.it

Museo Fisogni della Stazione di Servizio

Museums · Themed

Private museum founded in 1966 by the entrepreneur of the Guido Fisogni fuel sector at Palazzolo Milanese and transferred in 2015 to Tradate, in renovated spaces attached to the historic Villa Castiglioni. It is the world's largest collection of antique petrol pumps, certified by the Guinness World Record: thousands of pieces from 1892 to the years 2000 collected in over 40 years, with oil signs, advertising posters, oil cans, compressors, hydrometers and promotional gadgets. Admission fee.

  • Where Via Giacomo Bianchi 25/b, Tradate
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.tradate.va.it

Sources: comune.tradate.va.it

Osservatorio astronomico e Parco Pineta

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Opened on 13 May 2007 in the wooded area of the Parco Pineta di Appiano Gentile e Tradate, chosen for peace and natural darkness, by the collaboration between Parco, Comune and Gruppo Astronomico Tradatese. It includes the main specula, an educational observatory with auxiliary telescopes, the Nature Path with orienteering gym and sensory strip, the EcoPlanetario and the Scientific Teaching Centre, which is also the Park Visitor Centre.

  • Where Via ai Ronchi 75, Tradate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.tradate.va.it

Sources: comune.tradate.va.it
