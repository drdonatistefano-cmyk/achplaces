What to see in Cerchio

Small village of the Marsica in the Sirente-Velino Regional Park, which legend wants to rise around a Roman theatre linked to the drying up of the Fucino of 51 AD; it preserves the churches of San Bartolomeo and Madonna delle Grazie and a civic museum in the former Augustinian convent.

Museo civico di Cerchio

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Born in 1986 thanks to the scholar Fiorenzo Amiconi in the former convent of the Augustinians Scalzi, in 1990 he enriched himself with the ethnographic collection: about 1,500 objects of peasant civilization and the trades donated by families, in four rooms and in the cloister (weights and sizes, spinning, domestic life, babes in rags and firework of the parties, tools of carpenters, crotch, shoemaker and blacksmith). In the basement sacred art from the fifteenth to the twentieth century from the churches of the village, with a processional cross of Sulmonese school, a Nativity of 1571 and a Holy Family of the eighteenth century. MiC timetable: Monday-Saturday 10-18, Sunday 9-19.

  • Where Piazza Sandro Pertini, Cerchio
  • Entry €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · abruzzoturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the thirteenth century mentioned in the bull of Boniface VIII of 1302; it preserves the portal of the sixteenth century, some terracotta statues and a cross. On August 24th, the historical festival takes in procession ladies, knights and bishops in medieval dresses.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the XVI century built together with the Augustinian monastery, dominated by white, with internal walls decorated with paintings on the life of the Madonna delle Grazie.
