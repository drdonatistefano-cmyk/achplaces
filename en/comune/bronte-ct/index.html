What to see in Bronte

Ethnic town of the green pistachio DOP, with a historic centre of alleys, steps and courtyards of Arab origin, the churches of the Trinity, of St. John and the Annunciation, the Collegio Capizzi and, in the territory, the Ducea of Nelson with the Castle and the Abbey of Santa Maria di Maniace.

Ducea di Nelson e Castello Nelson

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

The complex was founded in the 12th century as a Benedictine abbey of Santa Maria di Maniace, partly destroyed by the earthquake of 1693 and then donated to Admiral Horatio Nelson. It includes the residence of the Dukes Nelson-Bridport (called the Castle) with the Nelson Museum among furnished apartments, family portraits, documents and relics, the church of Santa Maria in Arab-Norman style, the granaries transformed into congress halls, the Celtic cross in honor of the admiral in the courtyard, an English garden and a park with lava stone sculptures and the English cemetery of the ducal family.

  • Where Contrada Erranteria, Bronte
  • Entry €1,50, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Pistacchio verde di Bronte DOP

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry

Cultivated on the lava soils of Etna, introduced according to the tradition by the Arabs, it is still harvested entirely by hand, shaking the branches on curtains or dropping the fruits in containers carried on the shoulder; the green pruning is handed down from father to son. In October the Festival of the pistachio animates the center with stands and shows.

  • Where Centro storico, Bronte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it
