What to see in Luserna

Lusérn, at 1,333 meters on the plateau between Folgaria, Lavarone and Passo Vezzena, is the last island where the Cimbrian language, ancient Bavarian dialect, is still spoken fluently: the Haus von Prükk museum house, the documentation center and, on Cima Campo, the fort of the Great War.

4 places to visit in Luserna, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo cimbro di Lusérn

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 1,333 meters south-east of Trento, on the edge of a vast plateau of pastures between Folgaria, Lavarone and Passo Vezzena; it is the last linguistic island in which the cimbrian, ancient Bavarian dialect, is spoken fluently by about 90% of the inhabitants.

  • Where Luserna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Centro di documentazione Lusérn – Dokumentationszentrum

Museums · Themed

Museum that preserves and enhances the Cimbrian culture, with sections on history and traditions, Great War and local fauna; it is also the reference point for visits to the fort and the house museum.

  • Where Via Trento 6, Luserna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.lusern.it

Sources: lusern.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Casa museo Haus von Prükk

Museums · House museums

Farmhouse of the nineteenth century restored with original furnishings, to show how it was a typical Cimbrian house; on summer evenings it hosts stories about the past.

  • Where Piazzetta C. Battisti/Pill, Luserna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: alpecimbra.it · italia.it

Forte Werk Lusérn

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built between 1908 and 1912 at the top of the Cima Campo, at 1,549 meters, called "Padreterno": with the fort Busa Verle was guarding the Vezzena Pass, armed with four bikes in armored domes, two guns and stations for 19 machine guns; in the first days of the Great War it was heavily bombarded and the garrison was close to surrender. You can reach it on foot from the village (path of the Baites, path Cimbri imaginary, path ... From stories to history) or from Malga Millegrobbe; in summer guided tours inside, passing through the Oberwiesen outpost.

  • Where Cima Campo, Luserna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: alpecimbra.it · italia.it
