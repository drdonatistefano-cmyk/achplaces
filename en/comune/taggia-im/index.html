What to see in Taggia

Village of the Argentina valley among the most beautiful villages in Italy, 3 km from the sea: the medieval bridge with fifteen arches, the convent of San Domenico with the Adoration of the Magi del Parmigianino, the castle and the beach of Arma di Taggia.

6 places to visit in Taggia, in the province of Imperia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ponte Antico

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ponte di 275 metri a 15 arcate, started in the thirteenth century (Romanic the first two) and expanded until the seventeenth-seventh century.

  • Where Taggia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Convento di San Domenico

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Dominican monastery of the fifteenth century with cloister with black stone columns; the gallery in the former dormitory preserves polyptychs and frescoes by Ludovico Brea and Canavesio and the Adoration of the Magi del Parmigianino (1528-29).

  • Where Taggia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Castello di Taggia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress of the 12th century rebuilt in 1560-64, today open air theatre with views of the village.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Basilica della Madonna Miracolosa

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Rebuilt in 1675 as a collegiate of Saints James and Philip and then minor basilica; it preserves the statue of St. Revelli declared miraculous by Pius IX in 1856.

  • Where Taggia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
