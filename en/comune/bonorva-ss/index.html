What to see in Bonorva

Country of the Logudoro with the necropolis of Sant'Andrea Priu, about twenty domus de janas with the Tomb of the Cape transformed into a frescoed rock church, since 2025 among the seventeen Sardinian necropolis UNESCO Heritage; in the territory the almost abandoned village of Rebeccu, the nuragic source of Su Lumarzu, the Romanesque San Lorenzo and, in the village, the civic archaeological museum.

Necropoli di Sant'Andrea Priu

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage

Twenty domus de janas of the Neo-Eneolithic (IV-III millennium BC, first phases of Ozieri's Culture) excavated in an outcrop of trachytes about 10 meters high. The Cape Tomb, about 250 m2 and 18 rooms, is among the largest hypogeas of the Mediterranean: re-used as a rock church dedicated to Sant'Andrea, it preserves Christian frescoes with scenes of the New Testament; next to the hut tomb, with conical ceiling with ray and votive cups, and the chamber tomb with two pillars and double sloping ceiling, plus the monolith of the sacred tower. According to the MiC open from March to October every day without appointment, from November to February by reservation (tel. 348 5642611). Since 2025 it is part of the UNESCO site of the domus de janas.

Rebeccu

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village on a ridge overlooking the plain of Santa Lucia, about six kilometers from Bonorva, already the capital of the curatoria of Costavalle on the border between the judges of Arborea and Torres; in 1353 a Catalan detachment burned it and a few decades later in his church was signed a peace between Eleonora of Arborea and the king of Aragon. Today, almost uninhabited, with a few stone houses, a small church and a deconsecrated cemetery, it is linked to the legend of Donoria and the "curse of the thirty houses."

Chiesa di San Lorenzo di Rebeccu

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Romanesque church of the second half of the 12th century, dated also thanks to a seal of Barisone II, Judge of Torres, found inside in 1831; a single nave with apse, in limestones with basalt inserts with bichrome effect, sailing bell tower and hanging arches. Partly demolished at the beginning of the 19th century, it was restored in 1982 with the reconstruction of the southern side and the wooden roof.

Fonte sacra di Su Lumarzu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Small nuragic monumental source about 300 meters from Rebeccu: a paved atrium with seats along the walls leads, through a trapezoidal entrance obtained in a monolithic slab, to a basalt cell covered with tholos that collects the water vein. It remained in use until the late antiquity (a.c. fourth century coins) and in the Christian age there was engraved a cross.
