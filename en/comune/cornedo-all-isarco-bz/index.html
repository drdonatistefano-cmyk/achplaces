What to see in Cornedo all'Isarco

Municipality at the entrance of the Val d'Ega, above Bolzano: the castle of Cornedo, of the twelfth century, dominates the gorge; in Collepietra the museum of Tyrolean rural life and in San Valentino in Campo the only astronomical observatory of South Tyrol and the Planetarium.

4 places to visit in Cornedo all'Isarco, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castel Cornedo (Burg Karneid)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Symbol of the municipality, on the gorge of the Val d'Ega: built in the 12th century as the seat of the Greifensteins, in the fourteenth century passed to the lords of Fiè, then to the balivo Heinrich Gessler and to the Counts Lichtenstain; it preserves almost intact the sixteenth-century appearance, with the rooms of the knights and an ancient chapel with valuable frescoes. Private, visit only with guide: April, May, June, September and October on Friday at 15, with telephone reservation.

  • Where Via Cornedo 21, Cornedo all'Isarco
  • Entry €7, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Museo di Collepietra

Museums · Themed

Museum of Tyrolean history and rural life, with its religious roots: almost forgotten crafts, war memories, rooms and workshops rebuilt in original style, an exhibition on local fauna and video interviews with witnesses of the time. Open from Palm Sunday at the end of October, only with guided tour in German or Italian (mar-ven 10:10 and 11:10; Saturday, Sunday and holidays 17:00), by reservation; not without barriers.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa 2, Collepietra, Cornedo all'Isarco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Osservatorio astronomico Max Valier e Osservatorio solare Peter Anich

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The only astronomical observatory in South Tyrol, about 1,340 meters to San Valentino in Campo, run by volunteers of the Astrofili Max Valier association, with several telescopes and an observation platform; public tours all year round on Thursday and Friday evening (in summer from 9 pm), cancelled with covered sky. Next, the Peter Anich solar observatory with a special filter telescope to see the surface of the Sun and the sunspots (in summer visit Friday at 2 pm), at the Untereggerhof farm on San Valentino di Sopra; always mandatory booking.

  • Where Presso maso Untereggerhof, San Valentino di Sopra, Cornedo all'Isarco
  • Entry Solar Observatory: €8, children €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.sternwarte.it

Sources: sternwarte.it · eggental.com · suedtirol.info

Planetario dell'Alto Adige

Museums · Science and university

On the Valentine's Day of Above: up to 53 spectators and a "Cosmos Simulator" for virtual trips around the Earth, between the rings of Saturn and deep space; regular shows in German and Italian, by reservation.

  • Where San Valentino di Sopra, Cornedo all'Isarco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.planetarium.bz.it

Sources: planetarium.bz.it · eggental.com · suedtirol.info
