What to see in Maiolati Spontini

Medieval village of Vallesina, in the Verdicchio dei Castelli di Jesi area, linked to the composer Gaspare Spontini (1774), of which you can visit the birthplace, museum, tomb and the Parco Colle Celeste; in the valley, in Moie, the Romanesque abbey of Santa Maria and the former furnace of 1923 transformed into a library.

Museo Gaspare Spontini

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

In the neoclassical house that Spontini bought and rebuilt with his wife Celeste Erard for his return to Maiolati. The first floor follows the master's career between Italy, France and Berlin; he exhibits autograph scores, manuscripts, epistolaries, sketches of French works, two Erard fortepiano of 1820, family furnishings (with the first bathroom of a private house in the country), medals of the Institut de France (1839) and the festival of Halle (1829), as well as the archive and a music library. A room is dedicated to Celeste Erard.

mandatory telephone reservation; MiC card ( 2021) indicates closed every day

  • Where Via Spontini, Maiolati Spontini
  • Entry €2.50, reduced €1.50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Casa natale di Gaspare Spontini

Museums · House museums

House where the composer was born in 1774, declared a national monument in 1924; it retains the essential aspect of the dwelling of his childhood.

Parco Colle Celeste

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

Public garden wanted by Spontini on his property and dedicated to his wife Celeste, on a hill east of the village: a long tree-lined avenue leads to a grove and a balcony overlooking the valley of the Esino and, on clear days, on the Adriatic.

  • Where Parte orientale del paese, Maiolati Spontini
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Borgo di Maiolati e mura castellane

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The castle was built in the beginning of the 13th century, destroyed in 1428 and then rebuilt, of which traces of the fifteenth-century walls remain; in the historical center the Community Palace, once the entrance of the castle, and the church of Santo Stefano.

  • Entry Free

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Abbazia di Santa Maria delle Moie

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

One of the main Romanesque monuments of the Marches, probably born as a private monastery of the Attoni-Alberoni-Gozoni family in the marshland area ('moja') near the Esino; the sources date its construction between the 11th and the first decades of the 12th century. In blocks of limestone, it has an inscribed Greek cross plan, three aisles and five apses, a central nave with barrels and a portal with three arches decorated with branches and braids; the cylindrical bell tower remains the base.

  • Where Piazza Santa Maria delle Moie 10, Moie, Maiolati Spontini
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Via dei Tesori, ciclopedonale lungo l'Esino

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Cycle path of about 11 km suitable for families, from Moie (Via della Chiusa, Parco dei Caduti di Nassiriya) to Jesi along the Esino, between irrigation channels, countryside with rural roundabouts and river parks.
