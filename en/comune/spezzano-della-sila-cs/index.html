What to see in Spezzano della Sila

The municipality of the Silano plateau, in the Sila National Park, which includes Camigliatello Silano. On the shores of Lake Cecita the Cupone Visitor Centre combines geological garden, botanical garden and paths; at the Torre dei Camigliati the Museum of Emigration La Nave della Sila; in the reserve of Fallistro the secular larch pine trees of the Giants of Sila, well of FAI.

Centro Visita Cupone e Giardino geologico

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Environmental education center of the Sila National Park on the shores of Lake Cecita, built around the ancient State sawmill, where from the early twentieth century were produced also colophony and Trementina. It includes nature trails, wildlife observers, a naturalistic museum, the geological garden with the typical rocks of Sila and a botanical garden of 10,400 m2 at 1,156 m of altitude, with 115 native species and path accessible to blind and visually impaired (panels, audioguides, braille cards); next to a picnic area on the lake and an archaeological site under study with finds from Neolithic to Eneolithic.

La Nave della Sila e Parco Old Calabria

Museums · Themed

Museum of Italian emigration in an ancient dairy restored by architect Sila Barracco, in the park of Torre dei Camigliati inspired by the travel diary written by Norman Douglas in 1915. The itinerary includes the stage reconstruction of a ship designed by Anna Cilia and photographs, illustrations and covers of vintage magazines, with texts by Gian Antonio Stella and Calabrian insights such as the history of a family of Morano Calabro.

I Giganti della Sila e Casino Mollo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

In the Biogenetic Reserve of Fallistro, established in 1987 and part of the world network of UNESCO Biosphere Reserves, over sixty larch pines and mountain maples up to 45 meters and about 350 years old, planted in the seventeenth century by the Barons Mollo, owners of the nearby hunting casino (the purchase of land is documented by an act of 1631). Between the seven and nineteenth century the estate had mill, spinning mill and chapels; since 2016 trees and Casino Mollo are entrusted to FAI.

reserve closed since July 2026 after the fall of two trees, pending the investigation of the oldest specimens; the Casino Mollo remains open as a reception point (FAI)
