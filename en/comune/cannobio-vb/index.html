What to see in Cannobio

Borgo Bandiera Arancione on Lake Maggiore, last before Switzerland, with the fourteenth-century nucleus of Palazzo della Ragione and the sanctuary of Pietà on the lakefront.

5 places to visit in Cannobio, in the province of Verbano-Cusio-Ossola (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Cannobio in the guide with map and filters

Santuario della Pietà

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the lakefront, built by Carlo Borromeo in 1583; in January the Luminari Festival recalls the miracle.

  • Where Lungolago, Cannobio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Palazzo della Ragione e Torre campanaria

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The fourteenth-century nucleus of the tower and the Palazzo della Ragione of 1291, called "Parasio" (Parasio), now home of tourist promotion and exhibitions.

  • Where Cannobio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it · italia.it · illagomaggiore.com

Collegiata di San Vittore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The most important church in the village, of the eighteenth century.

  • Where Cannobio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · illagomaggiore.com

Orrido di Sant'Anna

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Deep rocky gorge dug by the Cannobino in the Cannobina Valley, dominated by the seventeenth-century church of St. Anne.

  • Where Traffiume, Cannobio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · illagomaggiore.com · distrettolaghi.it · italia.it
