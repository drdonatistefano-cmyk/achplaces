What to see in Gemonio

Borgo della Valcuvia, reachable by train from Milan, Gemoni preserves the Romanesque church of San Pietro, perhaps founded by the Lombardy, and the Civic Museum dedicated to the sculptor Floriano Bodini, born here in 1933, in an eighteenth-century farmhouse in the center.

Museo civico Floriano Bodini

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 1998 at the initiative of the Municipality of Varese and Comunità Montana della Valcuvia, it occupies an eighteenth-century rural farmhouse known as the "cà della Maria Gill" which was renovated in the nineties by architects Annig Sarian and Gianni Pozzi. He exhibited the works of Floriano Bodini, animator in the post-war era of existential 'realism and author of medals and great public works such as the Paul VI of the Sacred Mount of Varese and the Duomo of Milan and the monument to the quarrymen of Carrara; every year he hosts exhibitions of Italian and foreign artists, workshops, concerts and meetings. Open Saturday and Sunday 10.30-12.30 and 15-18, on other days by appointment.

  • Where Via Marsala 11, Gemonio
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museobodini.it

Sources: museobodini.it · in-lombardia.it

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Probably founded in the Lombard age on older foundations, it was rebuilt in the 10th century with a single nave; the bell tower, high and decorated with hanging arches, and the side aisles were added later. Inside the medieval altar has decorations with geometric motifs of the tenth century, and walls and apses preserve frescoes of the fourteenth-fiveth century; it was parish until 1669.

  • Where Gemonio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Chiesa di San Rocco e della Beata Vergine Addolorata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Modesta chapel in 1578 was enlarged from 1643 and became parish in 1669, inheriting the title from St. Peter; the pronao with granite columns of St. Fedelino is from 1764 and the present aspect is due to the enlargement of 1893-98 on a project by Cesare Nava. It preserves wooden furnishings by Bernardino Castelli (pulpit, group of the Addolorata, Crucifix) and decorations of about 1923-33 of Innocent Salvini and frescoes by Giovanni Battista Jemoli.

  • Where Piazza della Vittoria 3, Gemonio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it
