What to see in Spilimbergo

Spilimbergo, one of the most famous cities in Friuli, is famous for its historic centre and for its mosaic tradition linked to the Mosaicist School of Friuli; the monumental heart is formed by the Cathedral of Santa Maria Maggiore, the Castle with the Painted Palace and the towers that close Corso Roma. From here starts the Cyclovia del Tagliamento towards Lignano.

7 places to visit in Spilimbergo, in the province of Pordenone (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Built from 1284 to 1359 in Romanesque style and then modified in Gothic forms for the long duration of the yard; the portal, attributed to Zenone by Campione and dated 1376, is surrounded by the coats of arms of the nobles of Spilimbergo, and the façade on Via Beato Bertrando has seven oculi. The three-nave interior houses in the apses a cycle of fourteenth-century frescoes with Stories of the Old and New Testaments, well preserved in the lower register because it remained hidden for centuries behind the wooden choir, a great Crucifixion, the organ doors painted by Pordenone, an altarpiece of the early sixteenth century in the Chapel of the Rosary and the crypt.

  • Where Via Dante Alighieri 15, Spilimbergo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello e Palazzo Dipinto

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The castle has existed since at least the 12th century, on the site of a Roman fortress, and it was expanded mainly between the 14th and 15th centuries; it includes several buildings decorated with friezes and frescoes. The Palazzo Dipinto, with its frescoed façade and stone balconies carved in the fifteenth century by the Ticino Giovanni Antonio Pilacorte, who also worked in the Duomo.

  • Where Castello, Spilimbergo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Scuola Mosaicisti del Friuli

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The mosaic school that has made known Spilimbergo, institute of international fame: you can visit classrooms and workshops, where you can observe techniques and students at work, and the gallery with a chronological path from the Greek-Roman and Byzantine mosaic to the contemporary one, with a section on the artists of Friuli Venezia Giulia on the first floor. Single tour from Monday to Friday 8-12 and 13-16 (last entrance half an hour before), with registration at the entrance to the courtyard; guided tours every day by reservation to the Tourist Information Office (0427 2274), groups up to 50 people with authorized guide.

  • Where Via Corridoni 6, Spilimbergo
  • Entry €3 (free under 12 accompanied)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website scuolamosaicistifriuli.it

Sources: scuolamosaicistifriuli.it · italia.it

Corso Roma, Torre Occidentale e Loggia Municipale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historical centre, famous together with the tradition of mosaic, crosses Corso Roma: at the beginning the Western Tower of the 14th century with a Lion of St Mark frescoed, on the other side the Oriental Tower, beyond which open the Municipal Loggia and the Duomo.

  • Where Corso Roma, Spilimbergo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiese di via Mazzini: San Giovanni Battista e San Giuseppe e Pantaleone

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In via Mazzini there are the church of San Giovanni Battista, a 14th century building with a frescoed vault in the 18th century, and the church of San Giuseppe and San Pantaleone, which preserves a wooden choir from the late 15th century.

  • Where Via Mazzini, Spilimbergo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Ciclovia del Tagliamento (FVG6)

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Cycle path of about 80 km from Spilimbergo to Lignano along the Tagliamento, between woods and open spaces, with different areas equipped for the stop.

  • Where Partenza da Spilimbergo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
