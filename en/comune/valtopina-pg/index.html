What to see in Valtopina

Access to the Parco del Monte Subasio, Valtopina rises on the slopes of the mountain in the valley of the river Topino, from which it takes its name: in the municipal palace the Museum of Embroidery and Textile, on the hills the Castle of Poggio of the 11th century, the village of Gallano, the church of Santa Cristina and the Roman remains linked to the Via Flaminia.

Museo del Ricamo e del Tessile

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 2007 on the ground floor of the town hall, it was born from donations collected since 2000, stimulated by the School of Embroidery of Pro Loco: objects of historical families of Foligno, Spello and Perugia and, later, Tuscan and Ligurian artifacts, including a collection arrived thanks to contacts with the Museo del Merletto di Rapallo. About 400 pieces between women's fashion of the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, underwear and accessories, household linen with the "learned" ones, the samples on which the girls showed the techniques learned, and child clothing. According to the page of the Municipality (updated in 2020) is open Wednesday and Saturday afternoon (15.30-18 in winter, 16-18.30 in summer) and from Tuesday to Friday 10-12 on request; tel. 0742 74625.

Castello di Poggio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Castle of the 11th century, documented with certainty from the second half of the thirteenth century, when it was subject to Perugia; it was for centuries seat of the Visconty, the county and the municipality, passed to the Trinci di Foligno, then in 1495 to Perugia and from 1560 to 1616 to the Bentivoglio counts of Gubbio. It remained the municipal seat until 1867, when with the opening of the railway station the administration moved to Valtopina. The well-preserved tower, which housed the town hall, Mount Frumentario, the guard body and the prison, the walls and the castle church, lies below the village called Casa Coccia.

Castello e borgo di Gallano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

12th century castle with its medieval village, now an old restored village that houses a resort; the gate of the castle is always open to visitors. It is reached by climbing for about a kilometer from the dirt road that passes from the Laghetto di Sasso.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di Santa Cristina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Medieval church, probably the oldest in the territory, which preserves some original frescoes; one, of the fifteenth century, is now at the Bishop's Museum of Foligno.

Villa rustica romana

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

The excavations of the 1994 Archaeological Superintendence at Casa Orlando have brought to light the remains of a rustic villa dating from the 1st century BC to the 2nd A.D., including an area for the production of wine; other remains are visible along the road to Santa Cristina, marked by a sign.

  • Where Strada per Santa Cristina, Valtopina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · comune.valtopina.pg.it

Ponti romani della Via Flaminia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Works related to the construction of the ancient Via Flaminia: in Ponte Rio an imposing wall of support of the Adriatic age, in Pieve Fanonica a viaduct of Augustan age, and the remains of bridges on the Topino at Foro, Ponte Centesimo and the Iron Bridge, where the foundations of two pylons of a bridge with multiple arches remain.

  • Where Ponte Rio e Pieve Fanonica, Valtopina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · comune.valtopina.pg.it

Itinerario 1: Villa romana, Santa Cristina, Poggio, Ponte Rio

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ring of 9 km (about 4 hours, maximum altitude 614 m) proposed by the Municipality: starts from the Piazzetta del Teatrino, runs along the Topino along the Green Route, climbs to the remains of the Roman villa and to the church of Santa Cristina, crosses the forest towards the Madonna Contadina near Carié, reaches the Castle of Poggio and descends to Ponte Rio; a variant leads to the Laghetto di Sasso and to the Castle of Gallano.

  • Where Piazzetta del Teatrino, Valtopina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: comune.valtopina.pg.it · umbriatourism.it
