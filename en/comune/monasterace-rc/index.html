What to see in Monasterace

Municipality of the Costa dei Gelsomini divided between the medieval village on the hill, surrounded by four gates and dominated by the castle, and the marina, where on the promontory of Punta Stilo the archaeological park and the museum guard the ancient Kaulon, achaea colony with the Doric temple and the great mosaic of dragons and dolphins.

Parco archeologico dell'antica Kaulon a Punta Stilo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the promontory of Punta Stilo rises Kaulon, a colony founded by the Achei of Crotone, with phases from the second half of the VII century BC and a regular Hellenistic system in long blocks; here Paolo Orsi identified in 1890 the remains of the Doric temple. In the park there is the large sandstone peripteral temple (470-460 BC), whose remains seem to rise from the waters of the Ionian Sea, the House of the Dragon and the Baths of Nannon, house transformed into a bathroom in the fourth century BC with the polychrome mosaic of dragons and dolphins, discovered in 2012 and among the largest in southern Italy. On the seabed in front of Monasterace Marina remain Greek architectural elements of the 5th century BC, visible only in immersion. The ticket indicated is that of the place MiC Museum and Park, open Tuesday-Sunday 9-18 (ticket office until 17.30), closed on Monday; tel. 0964 735154.

  • Where Punta Stilo, Strada Statale 106 Ionica, 89040 Monasterace (RC)
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo archeologico dell'antica Kaulon

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The museum, over the SS 106 compared to the park, exhibits the votive weapons of the temple, the Tabula Cauloniensis (bronze table dedicated to Zeus in achean alphabet), silver coins, the kits of the Bretty necropolis and, in a section of underwater archaeology, rare finds recovered from the bottom of Punta Stilo: bases and Ionic capitals and amphora still full of pitch. It is part of the place MiC Museum and Archaeological Park of the ancient Kaulonìa: open Tuesday-Sunday 9-18 (ticket office until 17.30), closed on Monday.

  • Where Strada Statale 106 Ionica, 89040 Monasterace (RC)
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · calabriastraordinaria.it · italia.it

Centro storico e Castello normanno-bizantino

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval village rises on a hill about 3 km from the coast; according to tradition its oldest nucleus was founded by the survivors of the destruction of Kaulon. It is enclosed by four Urbian gates and preserves walls and the remains of the Norman-Byzantine castle, which dominates the highest points of the town and has been recently restored. In the local traditions the Easter Cunfrunta and, every three years, the procession of Sant'Andrea d'Avellino () are located.

  • Where Monasterace (centro storico)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
