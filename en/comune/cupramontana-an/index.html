What to see in Cupramontana

Verdicchio Country, 505 m, whose name comes from the goddess Cupra Picena: in the caves of the former monastery of Santa Caterina houses the Museums in the cave with the International Museum of Wine Label, and in the surrounding area the Hermitage of the White Friars, inhabited for over four centuries by the Camaldolese. There was born the engraver Luigi Bartolini, author of the novel from which he is drawn "Ladri di biciclei" (bike thieves).

Musei in Grotta e Museo internazionale dell'etichetta del vino

Museums · Themed

In the caves of the Monastery of Santa Caterina, the Museums in Grotta (MIG) space houses the museum with labels from all over the world: a historical collection with rare pieces, some from the late nineteenth century, and a contemporary collection with hundreds of sketches inspired by the wine of Italian and foreign artists. Eremo delle Grotte (Eremo dei Frati Bianchi)

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

It takes its name from the white saio of the Camaldolese, who lived for over four centuries, and is considered one of the most significant testimonies of religious settlement of the origins in the Marches. Here were laid the foundations of the Congregation of Monte Corona and in the 16th century there found refuge two minor friars who founded the Order of the Capuchins; the caves also served as a hiding place for the friars persecuted by the Inquisition. The cellars can only be visited in a group.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Chiesa di San Leonardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1760 on a project by Cristoforo Moriconi, where there was a church of 1151, it preserves the "Circumcision" by Antonio Sarti (1615), the "Madonna with the Child," known as della Colonna" (1492), a wooden palace carved by Andrea Scaccianti (1681) and an "Immaculate Conception" by Clemens Kapp; in canonical a fourteenth century processional cross and a silver monstrory of the seventeenth century.

  • Where Piazza IV Novembre, Cupramontana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Chiesa di San Giacomo della Romita

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Priory of Camaldolese already before the thirteenth century, called "Romitella delle Mandriole" passed to the Franciscans in 1452; the present building was rebuilt between 1782 and 1793 on a design by D. Apollonio Tucchi. Blessed John Righi is buried there by Fabriano.

  • Where Via Romita 8, Cupramontana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Parco Colle Elisa

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

Park with pine trees of the thirties and view to the sea, equipped with hostel, picnic area and barbecue, sports fields, bars and games for children; the huts for the holidays are booked at the municipality.

Percorsi in bici tra le vigne del Verdicchio

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Three marked cycling routes, yellow (7.5 km), green (10 km) and blue (12 km), and the network with numbered nodes of the Verdicchio Castles to combine walking and cycling routes between rows and villages; the tourist office rents e-bikes.
