What to see in San Cipirello

The agricultural center of the inland Palermo welded to San Giuseppe Jato, at the foot of Mount Iato, where stood the ancient Iaitas: theatre, agora, Hellenistic houses and an antiquarium in Case D'Alia.

Area archeologica di Monte Iato

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On a mountain of 852 meters in control of the valley of Iato, protected on three sides by rock walls, over 200 hectares of the city of Iaitas, founded by indigenous people at the beginning of the 1st millennium BC, Hellenized in the 6th century BC. (Aphrodite temple) and destroyed by Frederick II in 1246. Around 300 B.C. it was renovated with the agorà porticata and the bouleuterion; to the north-west the theatre with 35 rows of steps for about 4400 spectators, then the thermal baths, the sanctuary of Demeter and the House to peristyle 1, of about 1600 square meters on two floors. Excavations of the University of Zurich since 1971.

  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it

Antiquarium di Monte Iato (Case D'Alia)

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Opened in July 2010 in a 19th century rustic villa inside the park: on the ground floor architectural elements and finds of the agora and theater, with statues of satyrs and menads, on the first floor the reconstruction of the peristyle of the House; among the pieces a cup attic to red figures of about 500 BC. Sections also on medieval and numismatic cities.

  • Where Contrada Perciana, Case D'Alia, San Cipirello
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: visitsicily.info · parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · italia.it
