What to see in Riomaggiore

The easternmost village of the Cinque Terre, from which starts the Via dell'Amore towards Manarola: the church of San Giovanni Battista, the remains of the castle and the sanctuaries of Montenero and of Health on the terraced highlands.

7 places to visit in Riomaggiore, in the province of La Spezia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo di Riomaggiore e via Colombo

History and architecture · Villages · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

One of the five villages of the UNESCO site; via Colombo rises to the upper part of the country.

  • Where Via Colombo, Riomaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · whc.unesco.org

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Large church of the upper part with neo-Gothic façade and rosette in Carrara marble; inside a wooden Crucifix of Margliano and a triptych of the Madonna with Saints Rocco and Sebastiano.

  • Where Riomaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello di Riomaggiore

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Manarola

History and architecture · Villages · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the five villages of the Cinque Terre, an ancient agricultural settlement transformed into a seaside village, at the other end of the Via dell'Amore.

  • Where Manarola, Riomaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Santuario della Madonna di Montenero

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Reachable with a path, it holds an image of the Assumption; from the square you dominate the entire Gulf of the Cinque Terre. It is the departure of the Via dei Santuari.

  • Where Montenero, Riomaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
