What to see in San Quirico d'Orcia

A village of probably Etruscan origins on the Via Francigena, in the heart of the UNESCO Valley, with the thermal hamlet of Bagno Vignoni.

6 places to visit in San Quirico d'Orcia, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open San Quirico d'Orcia in the guide with map and filters

Collegiata dei Santi Quirico e Giulitta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Romanesque church documented from the 8th century and rebuilt in the 12th century, with three portals, including that towards Siena attributed to Giovanni Pisano, a rare example of Lombard style in Tuscany; inside a altarpiece of Sano di Pietro.

  • Where San Quirico d'Orcia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Horti Leonini

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Italian garden designed by Diomede Leoni in the sixteenth century, on two levels along the ancient bastions, with the statue of Cosimo III de' Medici (1688) and a rose garden; in summer it hosts the sculpture exhibition "Forms in the Green."

  • Where San Quirico d'Orcia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Palazzo Chigi e Chiesa di San Francesco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Next to the Collegiate Palace Chigi, seat of the Municipality; in Piazza della Libertà the church of San Francesco with a Madonna attributed to Andrea della Robbia.

  • Where San Quirico d'Orcia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Piazza delle Sorgenti di Bagno Vignoni

Local life and traditions · Spas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The square of Bagno Vignoni is a bathtub of thermal water 49 meters long and 29 meters wide, already known in Roman times; it is not bathing, but at sunset the steam envelops the stone houses.

  • Where Bagno Vignoni, San Quirico d'Orcia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Parco dei Mulini

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the slope that descends from the village towards the river, the remains of medieval mills that exploited the thermal water.

  • Where Bagno Vignoni, San Quirico d'Orcia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Cappella della Madonna di Vitaleta

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

The small chapel on a hill between San Quirico and Pienza, reachable by a white road: it is the most photographed place in the Val d'Orcia.

  • Where Strada tra San Quirico d'Orcia e Pienza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
