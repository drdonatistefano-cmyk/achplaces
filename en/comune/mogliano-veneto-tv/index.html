What to see in Mogliano Veneto

Between Treviso and Venice, Mogliano Veneto houses at Villa La Marignana the museum of the sculptor Toni Benetton, with iron works in the cellars of the villa and a park of over 20,000 m2 with its macrosculptures outdoors; in the center the church of Santa Maria Assunta, completed in the 11th century and consecrated in 1570.

Museo Toni Benetton, La Marignana

Museums · Modern art

Artist's house in the villa that sculptor Toni Benetton bought to expose his works in the open space near the workshop: the exhibition park, built in 1967 and with centuries-old trees on over 20,000 m2, houses iron sculptures from 1951 to 1995, from the ...Sculptures livable in the Seventies to macrosculptures and ...Townscapes. In the cellars of the villa a first room with terracotta, plaster and bronzes and other rooms with irons, from the figurative period to the great macrosculptures; the museum has been recognized by the Veneto Region since 2000. Timetable March-October 9-12.30 and 15-19, otherwise by appointment (regional card updated to 2013).

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church of the diocese of Treviso from ancient origins: the census of the churches of the CEI records pre-existences around 1000 and the completion between 1043 and 1074, a portion of cloister of 1184, war destruction between 1192 and 1356 with reconstructions in 1234 and 1311, frescoes of 1344 and consecration in 1570. The bell tower was rebuilt in 1682, the main façade dates back to 1906 and in 1938 the chapel of San Benedetto was frescoed; inside also the chapels of Santa Francesca Romana and of the Addolorata and a baptismal font of 1860. The building was restored in 1991.

  • Where Via Don Bosco 39/A, Mogliano Veneto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.regione.veneto.it

Sources: chieseitaliane.chiesacattolica.it · regione.veneto.it
