What to see in Pontelandolfo

Borgo del Sannio, still partly surrounded by walls, dominated by the medieval tower with the Carafa coat of arms; in 1861 it was the scene of a massacre of more than 400 civilians. The Civic Textile Museum tells the tradition of handlooms.

Torre medievale

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Built between the end of the fourteenth and the beginning of the fifteenth century, it is 21 meters high with a diameter of 14 and a large portal with the coat of arms of Carafa; inside flowerbeds with busts and inscriptions and a family chapel. From the top we read the development of the village, still partly surrounded by walls.

Borgo antico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The name derives from a bridge over the Lenta river of the Longobard Prince Landolfo; documents from 1064 mention the abbey of Santa Teodora and the castle. Besieged in 1138 and 1462, struck by the earthquakes of 1456 and 1688, from 1466 it was of the Carafa of Cerreto; on August 14, 1861 the troops of the Royal Army burned it for retaliation killing over 400 civilians.

  • Where Via Piano della Croce, Pontelandolfo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fondoambiente.it

Museo civico tessile e dell'arte contadina

Museums · Themed

Born to recover and preserve the traditions of artisan and peasant Pontelandolfo, grown with the breeding: weaving, embroidery, iron, wood and stone processing. It preserves artifacts from the 17th to the 19th century, especially handmade fabrics from families and institutions, and a photo library.
