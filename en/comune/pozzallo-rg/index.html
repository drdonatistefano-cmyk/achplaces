What to see in Pozzallo

Southeast Sicilian coastal resort known for its sandy beaches, clear sea and marina with connections to Malta and the Aeolian Islands; in the historic center the fifteenth-century Cabrera Tower, symbol of the city, and the sixteenth-century Villa Tedeschi, now a municipal library.

6 places to visit in Pozzallo, in the province of Ragusa (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre Cabrera

History and architecture · Towers

The 15th-century defence tower, built to protect the cargo warehouses of Pozzallo from pirate raids; it is the symbol of the city and rises in the historic centre. Villa Tedeschi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

16th century building whose premises and gardens tell the history of the city and of the noble families that succeeded each other; symbol of the family that ruled Pozzallo, today houses the municipal library.

  • Where Pozzallo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.pozzallo.rg.it · italia.it

Villa Comunale

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

Garden with plants typical of the Mediterranean maquis, overlooking the sea, surrounding the City Palace.

  • Where Palazzo di Città, Pozzallo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.pozzallo.rg.it · italia.it

Spiaggia Pietrenere

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Beach of about 1500 meters of fine sand, cleaned every day, with separate collection, showers and bathing facilities; Visit Sicily lists it among the beaches Green Flag 2022 suitable for children.

  • Where Lungomare Pietrenere, Pozzallo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.pozzallo.rg.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Spiaggia Raganzino

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Beach equipped to the west of the port, with shallow waters and a cove suitable for children; Green Flag 2022 according to Visit Sicily.

  • Where A ovest del porto, Pozzallo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
