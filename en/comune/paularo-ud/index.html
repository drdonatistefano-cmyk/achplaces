What to see in Paularo

Country of the Val d'Incarojo, since 2022 in the network of Villages of the mountaineers: Palazzo Calice Screm, prototype of the Carnic house, and the eighteenth-century Palazzo Linussio Fabiani, the collection of musical instruments of Mozartina, the church of San Vito and Santa Maria Maggiore in Dierico, with the mountains and Lake Dimon around.

8 places to visit in Paularo, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

La Mozartina

Museums · House museums

In an eighteenth-century building, the house of the composer master Canciani houses on three floors a collection of musical instruments that illustrates the history of organ, harpsichord and piano, with eighteenth-century manuscripts of unpublished music and rare ancient books; the tour is guided by his son Daniel Canciani. It is a section of the Ecomuseum Mistirs; visits by reservation (0433 70162).

  • Where Via Jacopo Linussio 24, Paularo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Palazzo Calice Screm e palazzo Linussio Fabiani

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

In the center, Palazzo Calice Screm is a complex with superimposed loggias, considered the prototype of the Carnic house; the eighteenth-century palace Linussio Fabiani was the residence of the family of Jacopo Linussio, founder of one of the major European textile manufactures of the eighteenth century.

  • Where Centro di Paularo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Chiesa di San Vito

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1772 on a project by Domenico Schiavi and completed in the following century, with frescoes by Antonio Schiavi and paintings by Francesco Pillizzotti, follower of Nicola Grassi; the main altar in white marble was donated by Jacopo Linussio in 1747.

  • Where Paularo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore a Dierico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Bell tower with cusp of 1577, 16th century frescoes with stories of the Old and New Testament by Giulio Urbanis, pupil of Pomponio Amalteo, and in the apse a wooden altar painted and gilded by Antonio Tirone of 1522.

  • Where Dierico, Paularo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Santuario della Madonna del Sasso

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In Castoia di Salino, a chapel dedicated to Mary Help of Christians built in 1870 on the spot where in 1810, after a flood of the Malmidili river, a stone was found with the image of Our Lady with the Child; a pilgrimage destination on May 24th.

  • Where Castoia, Salino, Paularo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Cascata di Salino

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

In Salino, on the road to Paularo, the waterfall is just past the village, behind a curve.

  • Where Salino, Paularo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Lago Dimon e monti Paularo, Dimon e Neddis

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Under the grassy slopes of the Paularo, Dimon and Neddis mountains, frequented in every season, the Dimon lake (1852 m); the 14.8 km ring (about 875 m of altitude difference, 5 hours and 20, difficulty T2) starts from Castel Valdajer. In summer, the Zermula, Lodin Alta and Pizzul mountain huts offer dairy products and local specialities.

  • Where Monte Dimon, Paularo (partenza da Castel Valdajer, Ligosullo)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

La Palme

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry
