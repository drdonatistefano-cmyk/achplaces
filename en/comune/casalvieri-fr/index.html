What to see in Casalvieri

Borgo della Valle di Comino with a historical centre walled from the 13th century and palaces from the 16th to the 19th century, the Baroque collegiate church of Giacomo Del Sole and, in the hamlet Roselli, the seventeenth-century sanctuary of Santa Maria delle Rose; the civic museum houses the remains of Cominium and the former Roman vows of Pescarola.

Collegiata dei Santi Giovanni Battista ed Evangelista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Baroque three-nave church of the architect Giacomo Del Sole, built between 1726 and 1760. The main altar decorated in pure gold preserves the relics of the patron Saint Honorius; it preserves frescoes attributed to the Cavalier d'Arpino and a sacristy with wooden sculptures.

  • Where Centro storico, Casalvieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: provincia.fr.it · visitlazio.com · comune.casalvieri.fr.it

Santuario di Santa Maria delle Rose

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

National monument in the hamlet Roselli, begun on April 20, 1636 above a chapel that remembered the appearance of Our Lady to a shepherd; rustic façade with diamond tip and two lateral naves added in the nineteenth century. It houses two wooden sculptures of Neapolitan school: a Crucifix of the mid-sixteenth century and an Annunziata of the eighteenth century.

  • Where Frazione Roselli, Casalvieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: provincia.fr.it · visitlazio.com

Museo civico «Padre Michele Jacobelli»

Museums · Archaeological

Archaeological collection in the church of Santa Maria Assunta, started in 1958 by Father Michele Jacobelli and acquired by the Municipality in 1978: fossils, blocks of the walls of the pre-Roman city of Cominium and former Roman anatomical vote (III-I century BC) from the votive crest of Pescarola; also exhibits musical instruments, agricultural tools and popular costumes from various parts of the world.

opening on request (tel. 0776 639327)

  • Where Via Jacobelli, Casalvieri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Centro storico, Porta di San Giovanni e palazzi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

You enter from the Gate of St. John, in the walls presumably of the thirteenth century, between the Torrione and the Torre Grande. Along the Strada di Sotto the seventeenth-century Palazzo Rezza, partly leaning against the walls, and Palazzo Ianni; near Porta Maggiore Palazzo Fanelli (formerly De Vecchis, early seventeenth century) with a baroque balcony and frescoed andron, next to the church of San Nicola; near the Collegiata the complex of Palazzo Iacobelli (XVI-XVIII century) and far Morelli the massive Palazzo Boncompagni with buttresses, of the late sixteenth century.

  • Where Strada di Sotto, Casalvieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: comune.casalvieri.fr.it · provincia.fr.it · visitlazio.com

Palazzo Cellitto, «Villa dei Misteri»

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Building in the locality Collefosse-Fratta di Rose, belonged to the De Vecchis and then to the Fanelli, known for the Masonic friezes set in the façade.

Area archeologica di Pescarola

History and architecture · Archaeology

Small excavation area with a votive deposit of the fourth century BC and a Roman thermal facility in the locality Le Casette; the former anatomical votes found are at the civic museum.

  • Where Località Le Casette, Casalvieri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
