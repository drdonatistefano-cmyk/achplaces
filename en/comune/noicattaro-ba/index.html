What to see in Noicattaro

16 km from Bari, between the blade of San Vincenzo and the blade Paradiso, Noicàttaro has a historic centre of stone alleys with external stairs and votive newsstands gathered around the Romanesque Collegiate Church of Santa Maria della Pace; in the former convent of the Palazzo della Cultura you can visit the Civic Gallery of Modern and Contemporary Art.

Centro storico e piazza Umberto I

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Narrow streets and houses in live stone or tuff with external stairs and stone walls, sometimes open on small squares full of votive newsstands. In the main square, Piazza Umberto I, overlook the Clock Tower, the church of the Immaculate One called "Foresi" (the Foresi), the entrance arch of the castle and a refined newsstand decorated with stucco; along Via Oberdan there is the ancient ornamental fountain of the Testuggini (the Testuggini). Notice the rites of Holy Week with the crucifers.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Noicattaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page interregaismart.regione.puglia.it

Collegiata di Santa Maria della Pace (Chiesa Matrice)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque-style mother church around which the historical centre is gathered, built in Norman-Svevian age between the 12th and 13th centuries.

Galleria civica d'arte moderna e contemporanea (Palazzo della Cultura)

Museums · Modern art

In the Palazzo della Cultura, a former Carmelite convent renovated in 2001 and became a cultural centre accessible to all: paintings and sculptures by many local artists, the Nojani Arts Hall, the studio room (Sala Pende) dedicated to Professor Pende, a Nojan scientist and a room with permanent exhibition on the painter Dyalma Stultus. An archaeological section tells of the findings of the sixth century BC. (vases, bronze armor, worked sheets, goldsmiths) of the peucet necropolis of the districts Cipierno, Trisorio and Calcàro, including a bronze belt with a quadriga in running; according to the Municipality and the MiC these finds are kept or exhibited in the archaeological museums of Bari and Taranto. Booking not necessary.

  • Where Via Console Positano 11, Noicattaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
