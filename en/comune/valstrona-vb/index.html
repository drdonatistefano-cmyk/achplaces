What to see in Valstrona

Scattered municipality of the Strona Valley, near the north-western shore of Lake Orta, formed by the villages of Strona, Luzzogno, Inuggio, Fornero, Forno, Sambughetto and Campello Monti; among woods and alpine pastures offers the sanctuary of the Colletta, the Museum of sacred art of Forno, the caves of Sambughetto and the Walser village of Campello Monti in the Natural Park of Alta Valsesia and Alta Val Strona.

Museo di arte sacra di Forno

Museums · Old masters · Free entry

Two halls in the sacristy of the parish of Saints Peter and Paul preserve about 500 works from churches and oratories of the high Valstrona, from the fifteenth century onwards: wooden sculptures, altars, crucifixes, relics, tabernacles, silver, silk vestments from the seventeenth century and paintings by local artists or donated by migrants. There are two pairs of golden and polychrome reliquary busts, a Virgin of the Pietà of the early eighteenth century carved into a single trunk and the former vows that recall the snowfall of 1888 and a shipwreck of migrants headed to America.

open only on request (tel. 0323 885101)

  • Where Parrocchiale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo, frazione Forno, Valstrona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Campello Monti

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The last village of the valley, at 1,300 meters, founded by the Walser of Rimella that brought the cattle from 1338 (the name ...Campello) appears in 1442); it grew from the middle of the nineteenth century to 1946 thanks to the nickel mines, long the active suns in Europe. There is still the dialect titsch; you can reach it by car from Omegna or on foot from Forno for the ancient Vegia Stro Vegia.

the municipality indicates the village inhabited only from May to October

  • Where Frazione Campello Monti, Valstrona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.valstrona.vb.it · distrettolaghi.it · italia.it

Ecomuseo di Campello Monti «Walsergemeinschaft Kampel»

Museums · Themed

Ecomuseum dedicated to the Walser culture of Campello Monti (Kampel), promoted since 1993 by the Walsergemeinschaft Kampel association with research initiatives; on the first Saturday of August it hosts the annual conference "Campello" and the Walser" .

Santuario della Madonna della Colletta

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Sanctuary on the hill north of Luzzogno, reached with a short climb on a cobbled road; the small churchyard is a viewpoint on the village and the Alps. Every three years, in September, it hosts a party with torchlight from the village and bonfire.

  • Where Luzzogno, Valstrona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Grotte di Sambughetto

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Beyond Piana di Fornero, the most interesting underground complex in the valley, with winding tunnels, small waterfalls and holes that tell the geological history of the area; the main cave (Cave of the Witches, old mine) is 707 meters long.

for security the visit requires an authorized expert guide (contact the guide or Geoexplore)

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it · comune.valstrona.vb.it · italia.it

Parco naturale dell'Alta Valsesia e dell'Alta Val Strona

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The western part of the municipality is part of the park, which extends from the high Valsesia to Campello Monti, its easternmost point and gateway; Valstrona is the only municipality in the park outside the province of Vercelli. Chamois, ibex, roe deer and royal eagle; many paths, with information from the municipality and from the visitor centers in Valsesia.

  • Where Campello Monti, Valstrona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
