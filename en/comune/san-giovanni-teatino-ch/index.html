What to see in San Giovanni Teatino

The town of Chietino is located a few kilometres from Pescara, of which is the village of Sambuceto, where stands the church of San Rocco designed by Mario Botta; in the capital there is a small civic museum of natural sciences.

2 places to visit in San Giovanni Teatino, in the province of Chieti (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di San Rocco di Mario Botta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish complex designed by Mario Botta (project of 2009) for an urban area growing, with church, working chapel, parish center, colonnade and parish church. Outside it is a compact volume that slims upwards: the plant is a large square with a central apse and two lateral ones, but tilting up the building takes on a cross shape recognizable in the landscape, and inside the light descends from a Greek cross skylight.

Museo Civico di Scienze Naturali

Museums · Science and university

Municipal museum with collections of mineralogy and natural sciences, which offers an overview of the fauna and minerals of the area. Visiting hours should be requested from the museum.
