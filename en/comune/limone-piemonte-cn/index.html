What to see in Limone Piemonte

Alpine railway station Orange Flag at the confluence of Vermenagna and San Giovanni, with a historic center in stone and wood almost intact around the Gothic San Pietro in Vincoli.

6 places to visit in Limone Piemonte, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parrocchiale di San Pietro in Vincoli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Gothic, on the main square, among the best preserved examples of medieval architecture in the area; documented since the 13th century, with Romanesque bell tower.

  • Where Via Roma 16, Limone Piemonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it · vermenagna-roya.eu · visitcuneese.it

Centro storico e piazza del Municipio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Almost intact center of streets with stone houses and wooden balconies; in the square the town hall and the beautiful stone fountain of the sixteenth century.

  • Where Via Roma, Limone Piemonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: vermenagna-roya.eu · touringclub.it · italia.it
