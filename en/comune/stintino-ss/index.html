What to see in Stintino

Fishermen's village born in 1885 from 45 families displaced from Asinara, at the north-western end of Sardinia: the beach of Pelosa with its Aragonese tower, Capo Falcone, ponds, the Saline beach and the Tonnara Museum; from the port you leave for the Asinara National Park.

Spiaggia della Pelosa e Pelosetta

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Light sand and low seabed for tens of meters, suitable for children; the sea is calm because sheltered by Capo Falcone, the Piana Island and the Asinara rocks. Next, the Pelosetta, closed by the islet with the tower. It's better to be here soon.

in the summer months access quota and only by reservation

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Torre della Pelosa

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Aragonese tower of 1578, about ten meters high, on the islet that closes the Pelosetta and reachable on foot; on the nearby Isola Piana remain the ruins of another Spanish tower.

  • Where Isola della Pelosa, Stintino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

MUT - Museo della tonnara

Museums · Themed

Opened on 18 June 2016 in the former ALPI plant on the Minori port, it tells the tuna trap of the Saline, active until the seventies, Stintino and Asinara with videos narrated (subtitles in English), vintage images and testimonies. He exhibits tools, diaries, articles, the dresses of the mattanza, models of boats and trap and paintings of Sardinian artists such as Stanis Dessy and Ausonio Tanda. Admission for a fee.

  • Where Via Lepanto 36, Stintino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it · mutstintino.com

Borgo e porti di Stintino

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Fishing village founded in 1885 by 45 families of Cala d'Oliva, displaced from Asinara to make room for the penal colony; Autonomous municipality since 1988. From 1983 to the end of August, it hosts a regatta of Latin sail gozzos.

  • Where Stintino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Le Saline

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Along the beach of small white quartz pebbles between the sea, the pond and the ancient salt pans built in the 13th century by the monks of Santa Maria di Tergu; low and sandy seabed, wind for windsurfing and kite, track for runners and cyclists behind. To the north are the old traps and the Spanish tower of the sixteenth century; between the Saline and Monti Frattu 17 bunkers of the Second World War, which can be visited with guide during Open Monuments.

  • Where Uscita sulla SP 34, Stintino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Capo Falcone e la sua torre

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Wild promontory with a Spanish tower, the highest of the Nurra, reachable by a winding path; a panoramic terrace about 200 meters away looks at the Piana Island and the Asinara. Pilgrims and hawks of the queen fly there.

  • Where Capo Falcone, Stintino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

  • Where Costa ovest, Stintino
  • Entry Free
