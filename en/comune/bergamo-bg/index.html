What to see in Bergamo

Art city divided between the Upper City, closed by the Venetian Walls UNESCO heritage, and the modern Lower City: Old Square and the Campanone, Santa Maria Maggiore and the Colleoni Chapel, the Carrara Academy and the places of Gaetano Donizetti.

28 places to visit in Bergamo, in the province of Bergamo (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza Vecchia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The heart of Città Alta, with the Contarini Fountain and its two sphinxes addressed to the Angelo Mai Library and the Palazzo della Ragione, the Palazzo del Podestà and the Campanone; on the opposite side the neoclassical Palazzo Nuovo, seat of the Biblioteca Angelo Mai.

  • Where Piazza Vecchia, Bergamo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: visitbergamo.net

Accademia Carrara

Founded in 1796 on the initiative of Count Giacomo Carrara, it houses more than 600 masterpieces by Botticelli, Raffaello, Tiziano, Lotto, Moroni and Canaletto, in a five-century itinerary of Italian painting.
