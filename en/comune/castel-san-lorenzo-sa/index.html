What to see in Castel San Lorenzo

A hilly village about 350 metres away in the Cilento National Park, Vallo di Diano and Alburni, above the Calore river: medieval historic centre with the remains of Palazzo Carafa, the Museo della Civiltà Contadina in the old Town Hall and a long winemaking tradition.

Centro storico, Palazzo Carafa e Palazzo Vigna della Corte

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The old town is of medieval origin: in the small Piazza Umberto I there are the old Town Hall and the remains of the Palazzo dei principi Carafa, built between 1272 and 1337. Palazzo Vigna della Corte, from the 17th century, has four corner towers and, according to legend, a secret passage to the castle built by Frederick II, today destroyed.

Chiese e Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish preserves the image of Our Lady of Graces with the bare breasts, which some scholars link to the cult of Artemis. In the village there is also the church of St. John the Baptist, Santa Maria del Monte of the 18th century, the chapel with the newsstand of the 12th century Madonna della Stella and the sanctuary of the Saints Cosma and Damiano.

  • Entry Free

Fiume Calore e sentiero del Mulino del Principe

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Calore is born from the slopes of Monte Cervati and runs crooked under the hill of the village, in some stretches rich in trout; there are canoeing championships. Cantina Valcalore e vini DOC

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The plants of the social cellar, largely industrial archaeology, have been hosting since the 1960s an experience of wine cooperation; in 2018 the new company of partners bought the spaces of the former winery of 1960 and restarted the production of wine and extra virgin olive oil. Every year from 12 to 19 August, the Festa dei Sette Vini DOC is held.
