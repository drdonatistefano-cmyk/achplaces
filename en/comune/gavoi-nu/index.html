What to see in Gavoi

Borgo della Barbagia di Ollolai, Orange Flag of the Touring, with granite houses arranged amphitheatre at almost 800 meters: the parish church of San Gavino, the Porcu Satta House Museum, the Museum of the Sardinian Flower, the country shrine of Our Lady of Itria and, about 2 km, the lake of Gusana.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Exposed houses of granite of barbaric type, many with wooden balconies, arranged amphitheatre on a slope at almost 800 meters of altitude. In 1388 Gavoi participated, with other centers of Barbagia, in the signing of the peace treaty between Eleonora of Arborea and John I of Aragon.

  • Where Centro storico, Gavoi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Gavino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Late Aragonese parish church on the hill of the historic center, probably begun between the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries (inscriptions of 1555 and 1695 in two chapels). Façade in pink trachite with Mannerist portal and rose window of about 3 meters, bell tower of about 30 meters with sundial; inside a single nave with communicating chapels, presbytery with star vault and keys depicting St. Gavino on horseback, eighteenth-century polygonal baptismal font and Baroque pulpit. Feast of the saint on October 25.

  • Where Piazza San Gavino, Gavoi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Casa Museo Porcu Satta

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Ethnographic museum in the master house of the doctor Giovanni Porcu Duras and his wife Rosina Satta Mulas, with furnishings designed by the sculptor Francesco Ciusa. It exhibits traditional gavoesi clothes, about 100 jewels and amulets in silver filigree of the goldsmith Nanni Rocca and the collection "Jocos" of more than 3,000 objects among traditional toys, musical instruments (including on tumbarinu, the drum of the carnival) and scale reconstructions of agropastoral workplaces. cumulative ticket with the Museo del Fiore Sardo €7, reduced €5.

structure hardly accessible for disabled people for architectural barriers; closed Monday

  • Where Via Roma 187, Gavoi
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.gavoi.nu.it · sardegnacultura.it · italia.it

Museo del Fiore Sardo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the noble house of the Lai family, it is dedicated to the cheese Fiore Sardo DOP: stone rooms with barrel vaults for sensory paths and tastings, traditional and modern tools of the cheesemaking with photographs of the Slovenian Žiga Koritnik, the documentary "Fiore Sardo" by Fabio Olmi and a plan for temporary exhibitions. A cumulative ticket with the Porcu Satta Museum House €7, reduced €5.

  • Where Via Margherita 9, Gavoi
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.gavoi.nu.it · italia.it

Lago di Gusana

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Artificial basin about 2 km from the village, built between 1959 and 1961 with the dam on the river Taloro, between the mountains Littederone and Nodu nos Arcos and surrounded by holm oaks and roverelle. Canoeing, sport fishing (according to the seasonal rules) and horseback riding in the woods; with the low water, the remains of a Roman bridge emerge.

  • Where SS 128 Gavoi-Fonni, Gavoi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Santuario di Nostra Signora d'Itria

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the plateau of Lidana, about 8 km east of the village: church in granite cones rebuilt at the beginning of the twentieth century on a sanctuary existing at least from the middle of the sixteenth century, surrounded by horseshoe from the cumbessias, the houses of the pilgrims. To the south of the church knows Perda de sa Itria, granite monolith about 4 meters high; in the surroundings domus de janas, tombs of giants and menhirs. Feast on the last Sunday of July with novena and the palio on Palu de sa Itria.

  • Where Altopiano di Lidana, Gavoi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
