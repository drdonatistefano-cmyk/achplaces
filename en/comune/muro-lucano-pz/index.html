What to see in Muro Lucano

Country on a limestone spur overlooking the Valle del Marmo Platano, with the medieval district of Pianello, the castle and the National Archaeological Museum in the sixteenth century Seminary; it is linked to San Gerardo Maiella.

5 places to visit in Muro Lucano, in the province of Potenza (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo archeologico nazionale

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Opened in 2003 at the Bishops' Seminary of the 16th century, on three floors: the five-section route goes from the archaeologist's craft to life in an imperial Roman villa. Among the main pieces the mosaic with the Seasons and Aion by Masseria Ciccotti of Oppido Lucano; cumulative with the National Museum of Power €8.

  • Where Via Seminario 6, Muro Lucano
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museiitaliani.it

Sources: museiitaliani.it · cultura.gov.it · basilicataturistica.it

Museo Capitolare di Arte Sacra Benedetto XIII

Museums · Old masters

In the former bishop's palace, 14 rooms on two floors with sacred art from the palace, the cathedral and the churches damaged by the earthquake of 1980: wooden and paper sculptures, a portrait of Pope Benedict XIII, a pontifical service in golden silver by Luigi Valadier, vestments and processional umbrellas. In the underground under the cathedral the remains of an 11th century church and a sepulchral crypt; visits only by reservation, for groups of at least 6 people.

  • Where Via Seminario 2, Muro Lucano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · basilicataturistica.it

Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Longobard stronghold of the tenth century, passed to the Sanseverino, Ferrillo and Orsini of Gravina; it is remembered for the imprisonment and killing of Queen Joan I of Anjou. It collapsed with the earthquake of 1694, it was rebuilt and expanded.

  • Where Centro storico, Muro Lucano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: catalogo.cultura.gov.it · basilicataturistica.it

Centro storico e quartiere Pianello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village rises on a spur of limestone rock on the Valle del Marmo Platano, with houses leaning like in a nativity scene: at the top the Pianello preserves the medieval system with the houses leaning against the ancient walls and traces of Rome, at the bottom a row of Renaissance buildings. In the village you can wind the hierarchy on the places of San Gerardo Maiella.

  • Where Muro Lucano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it · catalogo.cultura.gov.it

Convento di Sant'Antonio e chiesa di Sant'Andrea Apostolo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The convent houses the church of St. Andrew the Apostle, rich in works of art of the seventeenth century.
