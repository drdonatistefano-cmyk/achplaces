What to see in Colorno

Centre of the lower Parma, from the seventeenth century summer residence of the dukes of Parma: the Reggia redesigned by Ferdinando Galli Bibiena with the historical garden, the Aranciaia with the MUPAC, the ducal chapel of San Liborio with the organ Serassi, the Cathedral of Santa Margherita and the oratory of the SS. Annunciated.

Reggia di Colorno

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

Fortress transformed into a residence by the Sanseverino family, enlarged by Ranuccio II and redesigned between 1697 and 1707 by Ferdinando Galli Bibiena for Francesco Farnese, with facades between four corner towers; since 1749 the Bourbons renovated its interiors with Ennemond Petitot, Boudard and the Rusca stuccoer. On the noble floor the Sala Grande with rococo-neoclassic stuccoes (1755-56) and the new apartment of Don Ferdinando di Borbone, frescoed in 1787-89; after the Unity the furnishings were scattered between Quirinale and museums. Guided tours at 10, 11.30, 15 and 16.30 (Sunday even 17), audio guide Saturday and holidays; closed Mondays not holidays of a few months.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Giardino storico della Reggia

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

Born in the fifteenth century with Roberto Sanseverino, renovated by Francesco Farnese with the fountains of Proserpina and Trianon by Giuliano Mozzani, then French with the Bourbons (parterre by François Anquetil called Delisle on a project by Petitot) and English with Maria Luigia (1816-1823), with the lake and the island of love.Renovated between 1998 and 2000 restoring the parterre del Delisle. Open every day from 7am to 6pm (to 8pm with daylight saving time); audioguide €3 at the ticket office.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi 26, Colorno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: reggiadicolorno.it · parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

MUPAC - Museo dei paesaggi di terra e di fiume

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Ethno-anthropological museum of artisan and agricultural tools on the first floor of the Aranciaia, the building of the early eighteenth century, perhaps of Bibiena, which in winter repaired the citrus fruits of the court and today also hosts temporary exhibitions. Open from March to June, September, October and first weekend in November, Sunday and holidays 10.30-12.30 and 15.30-17.30, with guided tours at 11 and 16; weekday groups only by reservation.

  • Where Aranciaia, Piazzale Vittorio Veneto, 43052 Colorno (PR)
  • Entry €3,50, reduced €3, family €7
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Cappella ducale di San Liborio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of court behind the Reggia, with architecture and furnishings of the last quarter of the eighteenth century; damaged by the earthquake of 2012, reopened in 2013. Three aisles with B stuccoes. Bossi and dome frescoed by D. Muzzi; canvas by P.M. Ferrari, Laurent Pécheux and G. Baldrighi, a Terracotta Pietà by Laurent Guyard (1775) and the Preaching of St Liborius by G. You call on the high altar. The wooden choir by Michel Poncet and Ignazio Marchetti (1775-77) and the organ by Giuseppe Antonio Serassi (1792-96) are among the most remarkable pieces.

  • Where Dietro la Reggia, Colorno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · parmawelcome.it

Duomo di Santa Margherita

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the sixteenth century with significant nineteenth-century interventions, with three naves; in the fourth Baroque stucco chapel perhaps by D. and Leonardo Reti and an Last Supper by Giovanni Venanzi in 1668, in the presbytery the ducal gallery of 1760 carved by Marc Vibert and on the main altar a Martyrdom of St. Margherita of the mid seventeenth century of Veronian taste.

  • Where Colorno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Oratorio della SS. Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the Duchesses of Parma, built in 1718 by G. Mozzani for Dorotea Sofia of Neuburg, wife of Francesco Farnese: Greek cross plan and an unusual roof shaving of colored glazed terracotta; restored in 1997. Behind, on the same axis, a unique hexagonal mess.

  • Where Colorno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
