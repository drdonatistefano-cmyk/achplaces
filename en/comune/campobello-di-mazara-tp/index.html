What to see in Campobello di Mazara

The town of Trapani, which houses the Cusa quarries, from which the columns were extracted for the temples of Selinunte, abandoned in 409 BC; on the coast the towers of Tre Fontane and Torretta Granitola with the lighthouse of 1862, in the village the Mother Church with the crucifix of Fra' Umile da Petralia.

Cave di Cusa

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Cava of limestoneite that provided the column rocks for the temples of Selinunte, at 11-12 km; the work suddenly interrupted in 409 BC with the Carthaginian siege and the blocks remained where they were: over sixty cylindrical boulders and a rough capital. Visited by Jean-Pierre Houel between 1776 and 1779, in 1999 he received the international award "Carlo Scarpa" (Carlo Scarpa). Open from 9 am to 1 pm; in the afternoon only by reservation for groups of at least 20 people.

  • Where SP Tre Fontane, Campobello di Mazara
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.visitsicily.info
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In Baglio Florio, an ancient farmhouse with a large courtyard at the entrance of the Cave Park of Cusa, collects tools and objects of traditional crafts, even disappeared: fishing, carpenter, blacksmith, shoemaker, barrel, ropemaker. It was born from an exhibition of May 27, 1977.

  • Where Baglio Florio, ingresso Cave di Cusa, Campobello di Mazara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: comune.campobellodimazara.tp.it · old.comune.campobellodimazara.tp.it · visitsicily.info

Chiesa Madre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1825 by the architect Sacchetti, with Baroque and neoclassical influences, where in 1587 there was a small church; it houses the wooden crucifix of Fra' Umile da Petralia donated to the city in 1666 and paintings by Luigi Lo Jacono (1843) and Domenico Provenzano (1902).

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Torretta Granitola e faro

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

At Capo Granitola there are two towers built around 1553-1554, the circular-plan Mazara Tower and the Saurel Tower known as the ancient lighthouse, and the 38-metre-high military lighthouse with 153 steps, completed in 1862, visible up to 23 miles.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Torre di Tre Fontane

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

A 16th-century square watchtower, about 20 meters high, part of the coastal defensive system designed by Camillo Camilliani and Tiburzio Spanocchi; restored in 1995 by the Superintendence of Trapani.

Tonnara Amodeo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Active since the nineteenth century, renovated by Attilio Amodeo after 1944 with stone of Favignana, closed in 1972; today it houses a unit of the Institute for Marine Environment Coast CNR.

Torre dell'Orologio e Palazzo Accardi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Clock Tower, about 20 meters high, was built from 1859 and inaugurated on March 6, 1877, with original mechanism of the Solari brothers of Pesariis replaced in 1975; in Piazza Garibaldi, from the balcony of Palazzo Accardi, Garibaldi spoke to the crowd on July 22, 1862.

Santuario dell'Eremita

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Neo-Gothic church built in 1904 and blessed in 1926, with an older chapel; according to local legend in 1805 there was held for two days a mysterious character identified with Giuseppe Bonaparte.
