What to see in Vizzini

Vizzini rises on three hills (Castello, Maddalena and Calvario) on the plateau of the Iblei, in Val di Noto, and is linked above all to Giovanni Verga, who set there pages of Mastro Don Gesualdo and of the novels. To see the Duomo of San Gregorio Magno, the Basilica of San Giovanni Battista, the Vergian palaces and the abandoned village of Cunziria.

7 places to visit in Vizzini, in the province of Catania (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di San Gregorio Magno

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The Mother Church, whose foundation is of uncertain epoch, has on the Gothic portal of the south side, with double columns and with two ramps staircase, the inscription of 1539; on the same wall remain part of the facade of the ancient jury house and four arms in stone flag bearer. The main façade is late Baroque; inside paintings by Filippo Paladino (Martirio di San Lorenzo, Madonna della Mercede), a copy of the Last Supper by Leonardo di G. Emanuele Passanisi, the vault of Bonajuto (1786) and a triptych fourteenth century.

  • Where Largo Matrice 19, Vizzini
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.vizzini.ct.it · italia.it

Basilica di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Basilicas · Viewpoint

16th century building rebuilt in the 18th century, it is a monumental Baroque and Rococo church on the southern side of the village, with panoramic views of the countryside.

  • Where Vizzini
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Il Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Probably built in Norman times on the remains of a Byzantine fortress, it was used as a prison in Bourbon times. In the Middle Ages the village was limited to the top of the Castle Hill, surrounded by walls of which today remains trace in one place, with one of the defence towers.

  • Where Colle Castello, Vizzini
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.vizzini.ct.it

Santuario della Madonna del Pericolo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Small sanctuary next to a cave used by the first Christian communities for secret worship; the entrance of the cave is still visible, closed by a grate next to the facade.

  • Where Vizzini
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Cunziria

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient industrial location at the foot of the north side of Vizzini, among hills of figs of India: the name comes from ...conceria , the activity that took place there thanks to the summacco and the springs. The village was renovated in the twentieth century and abandoned in the 1960s. It preserves the forms of the late 18th and 19th centuries and was set by Cavalleria rusticana by Franco Zeffirelli (1982) and La Lupa by Gabriele Lavia (1996).

  • Where Contrada Cunziria, via Masera, Vizzini
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Palazzo Verga

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace where Giovanni Verga lived, remembered by a plaque (in this house he lived with the creatures of his high imagination Giovanni Verga) the building is presumably between the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries. The upper floors are incomplete for an argument with the nearby Cannizzaro, documented in the municipal historical archive.

  • Where Via Masera 9, Vizzini
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.vizzini.ct.it · italia.it

Palazzo La Gurna

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Neoclassical palace of the first half of the nineteenth century, with two columns on high lava stone bases on the sides of the entrance. In the novel Mastro Don Gesualdo there is the wedding banquet of Gesualdo and Bianca Trao; the name La Gurna was chosen by Verga and is not that of the owners.

  • Where Via Vittorio Emanuele 137, Vizzini
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.vizzini.ct.it · italia.it
