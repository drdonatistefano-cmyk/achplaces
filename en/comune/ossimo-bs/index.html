What to see in Ossimo

Municipality of the Plateau of the Sun, in Valle Camonica, divided between Ossimo Superiore and Ossimo Inferiore; to see the archaeological park of Asinino-Anvòia, megalithic sanctuary of the Copper Age, the ethnographic Museum "Ossimo Yesterday" and the seventeenth-century and eighteenth-century parishes.

Parco archeologico di Asinino-Anvòia

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Megalithic shrine and ceremonial area of the Copper Age (III millennium BC) on the plateau of Ossimo-Borno, on the hill of Anvòia, in a dense forest about 850 meters between two torrential valleys. Nine excavation campaigns, conducted between 1988 and 2002 by Professor Francesco Fedele with an international team, revealed four boulders lined with stylized faces engraved and, on the M3 mass, Remedello daggers; the originals were removed for preservation and replaced by resin reproductions. Free entrance and always visitable, with solutions for access to people with disabilities.

Museo etnografico «Ossimo Ieri»

Museums · Themed

Set up in the old parish house of Ossimo Superiore, it reconstructs the life of the early twentieth century and restores the value of the tools that accompanied the life of mountain people, in relation to the land and the surrounding nature. It is part of the Museum System of Valle Camonica.

visits only by reservation (Comune di Ossimo, tel. 0364 41100)

Parrocchiale dei Santi Gervasio e Protasio e Oratorio dei Disciplini

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church of Ossimo Superiore is located on the presumed site of a fifteenth-century building, perhaps a castle; it has facade of the seventeenth century and inside seventeenth-century stuccoes and a altarpiece of 1843 by Antonio Guadagnini. Next to it, the Oratory of the Disciplines, home of the confraternity, dated 1658, with portal in stone of Sarnico and closed arched window.

Parrocchiale dei Santi Cosma e Damiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of Ossimo Inferiore rebuilt in 1730 under the direction of the Milanese foreman Domenico Tettamanti; the bell tower was completed in 1780 and in 1905 Fortunato Canevali made important changes.
