What to see in San Ferdinando di Puglia

Municipality of the province of Barletta-Andria-Trani with a nineteenth-century center: in Piazza Trieste the former Palazzo di Città of 1847, now the museum seat, and the Matrice Church, the "Church Grande" rebuilt on a project by Marino Marrone and reopened to worship in 1963.

Chiesa Matrice («Chiesa Grande»)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church parish mother of the country, popularly called "Church Great." The previous church was demolished from May 1, 1948; after years of obstacles the faithful gathered in a warehouse of the Consortium (1948-1955) and then in the crypt (1955-163), until the new church, designed by Marino Marrone and wanted by the parish priest Fr Peppino Gallo (1946-1966), welcomed the first celebration on January 27, 1963; it was dedicated on May 30, 1999. Inside a pictorial cycle of Sfrule-brown.

Ex Palazzo di Città

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

19th-century palace dated 1847, work of Apulian workers, of public property, overlooking Piazza Trieste with a second access from Piazza Giovanni Paolo II. The main body is now used as a museum.
