What to see in Montottone

Borgo del Fermano remained largely enclosed in the ancient walls of the castle, known for centuries as "lu paese de li coccà" (the village of the potters). The medieval cistern, the canvas by Vincenzo Pagani in Santa Maria Ausiliatrice and the Ceramic Museum are worth seeing.

Centro storico e mura castellane

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village remained largely unchanged inside the ancient walls, remains of the fortifications of the castle. In June there is the Palio delle Grazie, with parades and competitions in Renaissance style.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Cisterna medievale

History and architecture · Archaeology

Built about the 12th century in front of the church of Santa Maria Ausiliatrice, it is about 15 meters deep, divided into two floors and measuring 6 x 6 meters; it collected water for periods of drought or siege. The two rooms can be visited thanks to routes along the edges, with audio guides in Italian and English.

  • Where Davanti alla chiesa di Santa Maria Ausiliatrice, Montottone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: montottone.eu · letsmarche.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Ausiliatrice e tela del Pagani

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It preserves the canvas of Vincenzo Pagani "Madonna enthroned with Child and Saints Giovanni Battista, Giovanni Evangelista, Maddalena and Caterina" (mystical bride of Saint Catherine), of 267 x 215 cm, which after a restoration carried out in Urbino was relocated on the right wall of the left arm of the transept. The municipal source indicates the influences of Raphael, in particular the Madonna del Pesce and Madonna di Foligno.

  • Where Montottone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: montottone.eu · letsmarche.it

Museo della ceramica e della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Museum workshop hosted in the State Middle School Perlasca, exhibits the terracotta objects used by the Fermano families until the 1960s and the potter's instruments; next to there is a laboratory to experiment with the techniques of processing. During the school year, the students visit it, during the summer months visitors are accompanied by a tour operator.

  • Where Via Galileo Galilei, Montottone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: montottone.eu · letsmarche.it

Bottega dei vasai della famiglia Bozzi

Local life and traditions · Crafts

The art of potters is documented in Montottone already between 1200 and 1300; the emigration of the Sixties and Seventies drastically reduced the number and today only one workshop, that of the family Bozzi. The clay is handmade, the objects are modeled on the footwork and baked in a wood-burning oven with traditional paints.
