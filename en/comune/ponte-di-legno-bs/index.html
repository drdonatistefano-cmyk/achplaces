What to see in Ponte di Legno

Locality at 1,260 meters at the northern end of Valcamonica, where the Oglio was born, connected by cable car to the Passo del Tonale and defined in 1912 by the Touring the first Italian resort of tourism and winter sports.The village, destroyed by the Austro-Hungarian bombing of 27 September 1917, preserves the Baroque church of the Holy Trinity with the sacred art museum, while the hamlets rise towards the Stelvio Park, the Houses of Viso and the Passo di Gavia.

Chiesa della Santissima Trinità

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of the historic centre, rebuilt in 1685 in Baroque style on a previous cult building, from which some works were recovered. It preserves numerous wooden furnishings, including the imposing anchor of the high altar, dated 1640 and attributed to Giovan Battista Ramus.

  • Where Piazza Paolo VI, Ponte di Legno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Parrocchiale d'Arte Sacra

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Set up in 1980 with the renovation of the parish house next to the Holy Trinity, it preserves furnishings, furnishings, hand-embroidered sacred vestments, cups and monstrators of the seventeenth century. According to the site of the Municipality is open every day from 10 am to 6 pm (the 2019 catalogue indicated it was open on request: best to call the parish, 0364 91223).

  • Where Piazza Paolo VI, Ponte di Legno
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ponte-di-legno.bs.it · italia.it

Centro storico e itinerario «Guerra e bombardamento»

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

On September 27, 1917 the Austro-Hungarian troops bombed the country and the fire that followed destroyed much of the town. A multimedia itinerary with QR code in the historic centre shows what the town was like before and after the bombing and reconstruction project; the center, pedestrian and crossed by small wooden bridges, revolves around Piazza XXVII September.

  • Where Piazza XXVII Settembre, Ponte di Legno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · pontedilegnotonale.com

Trampolino Gigante

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ski jumping trampoline was inaugurated in 1929 and remained in operation for almost forty years, until the 1966 Konsberg Cup: memory of the primacy of Ponte di Legno, which in 1912 the Italian Touring Club defined as the first Italian resort of tourism and winter sports. You can walk along the life path on the slopes of the Horn of Aola.

  • Where Percorso vita, pendici del Corno d'Aola, Ponte di Legno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Apollonio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On the way to the Passo di Gavia, it is one of the oldest churches in the upper Camonica Valley: the foundation is assumed around the year 1000. In the apse frescoes of the fourteenth century with Christ blessed surrounded by the symbols of the evangelists.

  • Where Strada del Passo di Gavia, Sant'Apollonia, Ponte di Legno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Sant'Apollonia e la fonte minerale

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Locality at 1,580 meters in the Stelvio National Park, at the beginning of the road to the Passo di Gavia, among the most interesting natural areas of the surroundings for landscapes and fauna along numerous paths. The source of mineral water, to which were once attributed healing virtues, was the object of never realized thermal projects between the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries and has remained free: it is customary to stop at the fountain before the excursions.

  • Where Sant'Apollonia, Ponte di Legno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Area faunistica di Pezzo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Educational-recreational structure of the Stelvio Park where you can see deer, deer, ibexes and chamois, with information point and visitor center with panels on characteristics, habits and winter adaptation of the species, also designed for children. You can visit freely following the established route or with guided tour by reservation.

  • Where Via Viso, Pezzo, Ponte di Legno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ponte-di-legno.bs.it · italia.it

Case di Viso

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Alpine village at 1,753 meters in the Valley of Viso, with wooden and stone huts with medieval origins overlooking the stream; long isolated and linked to agriculture and breeding, he was struck by Nazi reprisals in the Second World War. View of the Ortles-Cevedale and Adamello groups; it is a base of excursions to the plain of Ercavallo.

  • Where Case di Viso, Ponte di Legno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Passo di Gavia e Valle delle Messi

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valico at 2,652 meters of the Southern Rhaetian Alps in the Stelvio National Park, reached by the panoramic state road 300 and very frequented by cyclists and motorcyclists. Dominated by Mount Gavia, with the White Lake and the Trenches of the Great War; excursions to Punta Gaviola (3,050 m, about 1 hour and 30 by military road) and, for experts, to the Horn of the Three Lords (3,360 m, 3 hours and 30).

  • Where Valle delle Messi, Ponte di Legno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Sacrario militare del Passo del Tonale

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Erected between 1922 and 1924 as a sacred enclosure with a bronze copy of the Alata Victory of Brescia, it was then expanded with the ossuary that houses the remains of 847 Italian soldiers, eight Austro-Hungarian soldiers and, in a crypt, five unknown soldiers found in 1964 on the Lobbia Alta glacier. Since 1983 the Ministry of Defense has entrusted its care to the Municipality of Ponte di Legno.

  • Where Passo del Tonale, Ponte di Legno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Above the village, at the foot of Mount Salimmo and in the Adamello Park: Lake Valbione is about 45 minutes walk from the cable car Ponte-Tonale, and at 1,530 meters there is the golf course surrounded by the woods.

  • Where Valbione, Ponte di Legno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Local life and traditions · Crafts · Free entry

Handicraft workshop run by three generations of the Sandrini family, which creates wooden artifacts on its own design and restores wooden works.
