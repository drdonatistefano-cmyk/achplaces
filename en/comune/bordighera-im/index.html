What to see in Bordighera

The city of palm trees between Sanremo and the Côte d'Azur: the tall city with pentagonal walls, the villas of Charles Garnier, the Bicknell museum, the exotic garden Pallanca and the Argentina promenade.

10 places to visit in Bordighera, in the province of Imperia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.
