What to see in Scafati

Cities on the slopes of Mount Vesuvius along the Sarno, once navigable with the boat, boat similar to a gondola from which comes the name; to see the Baroque sanctuary of the Madonna dei Bagni, the angioin abbey of Real Valle, the church of Santa Maria delle Vergini and the park Wenner.

5 places to visit in Scafati, in the province of Salerno (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario della Madonna dei Bagni

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Baroque-style shrine built near a spring whose waters, according to legend, heal skin diseases.

Abbazia di Real Valle

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The Abbey wanted by Carlo d'Angiò to celebrate the victory over the Swabians, destroyed by the earthquake of 1456; then abandoned, it became the home of the Franciscan Alcantarine Sisters, and today the complex combines Romanesque-Gothic, Baroque and modern styles.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: italia.it

Real Polverificio borbonico

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Complex of buildings of different eras in a green area with tree-lined avenues, built since 1851 by Ferdinand II of Bourbon to produce gunpowder instead of that of Torre Annunziata; since 1894 it was intended for tobacco (then experimental tobacco institute) and, damaged by the earthquake, was abandoned in 1980. It is entrusted to the Archaeological Park of Pompeii, which provides for the redevelopment of deposits, laboratories, auditoriums and exhibition spaces.

The MiC card indicates "Closed" every day (site abandoned, waiting for redevelopment); italia.it indicates instead that since 2010 it hosts the City Museum

  • Where Via Pasquale Vitiello, Scafati
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
