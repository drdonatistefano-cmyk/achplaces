What to see in Sorrento

City on the Gulf of Naples with the living room of Piazza Tasso, the Cathedral where Torquato Tasso was baptized, the Basilica of the patron Sant'Antonino and the Correale Museum in an eighteenth-century villa among citrus trees; under the cliff Marina Grande and Marina Piccola, at Capo di Sorrento the Bagni della Regina Giovanna with the remains of the Roman villa Pollio Felice.

Piazza Tasso e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Piazza Tasso, the living room of the city with the statue of Torquato Tasso, can be reached from Corso Italia a few steps from the station. Palazzo Veniero (XIII century, late Byzantine style), Palazzo Correale (XIV century, Catalan style) and the loggia of Vico Galantariaro (XV century, Aragonese age) are located in the Vico di Santa Maria della Pietà.

  • Where Piazza Tasso, Sorrento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Correale di Terranova

Museums · Old masters

Opened in 1924 in the eighteenth-century villa of Correale, with citrus garden and terrace on the sea: over 10,000 pieces in 24 rooms on three floors, from the Greek, Roman and medieval finds of the peninsula to the Neapolitan and Flemish painting, the Sorrentine tarsia, the landscapes of the School of Posillipo (Pitloo, Gigante), porcelain, watches, shepherds of the nativity scene and fans; the library contains original writings of Torquato Tasso. The garden with the ancient araucaria and the avenue of the plane trees leads to the Belvedere on Vesuvius and Gulf. Amedeo Maiuri called him the most beautiful Museum of Italian province.

the museum remains closed to the public from 6 January to 2 April, open from Tuesday to Sunday (source: museumcorreale.it); tickets also online

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Museobottega della Tarsialignea

Museums · Themed

Private museum opened in 1999 in the eighteenth-century Palazzo Pomarici Santomasi, where the restoration brought to light frescoes and ceilings in hand painted paper. On four floors it tells the Sorrento wooden inlay: masters from the 15th to the 18th century, the nineteenth-century schools, techniques and materials, the local School of Art, with a layout of the city; on the ground floor a modern collection. Open every day 10-18, with audioguide.

  • Where Via San Nicola 28, Sorrento
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museomuta.it

Sources: museomuta.it · cultura.gov.it · comune.sorrento.na.it · italia.it

Cattedrale dei Santi Filippo e Giacomo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Consecrated on March 16, 1113, rebuilt after the Turkish invasion of 1558 and made baroque around 1700; of the fourteenth century remains the portal with two columns of pink marble. Inside the fountain where Torquato Tasso was baptized, with reliefs attributed to Andrea Pisano, the pulpit of 1573, the paintings of the Malinconico (1711), the choir inlaid by Sorrento artisans (1936) and a fifteenth-century Nativity of Sienese taste. The bell tower rests on four ancient columns and has on top the watch in 18th century majolica.

  • Where Via Santa Maria della Pietà 44, Sorrento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sorrento.na.it · italia.it

Basilica di Sant'Antonino

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Dedicated to the patron dead in 625, it existed already in 835 and was elevated to papal basilica in 1924: Romanesque façade in grey tufa, in the porch whale bones linked to the miracle of the saved child, a side door attributed to the tenth century with columns and classical capitals, the ceiling with paintings by Giambattista Lama (1734). The crypt, the most visited part, has eight columns of marble, paintings by Carlo Amalfi, former seafaring vote and a fresco of the fourteenth-century Madonna delle Grazie, the oldest Marian image of Sorrento.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Antonino, Sorrento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sorrento.na.it · italia.it

Chiostro di San Francesco

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry

Among the most ancient monuments of Sorrento, in the historic center; restored in the 15th century and restored in the early 20th century. In addition to religious use, it served the city administration: the councils were gathered there and the seal for municipal acts was kept there. Today it also hosts outdoor weddings.

  • Where Via San Francesco 12, Sorrento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sorrento.na.it · italia.it

Bagni della Regina Giovanna e Villa di Pollio Felice

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

A natural triangular swimming pool closed by the rocks, linked to the legend of Joan II d'Angiò, next to the remains of the Roman villa of Pollio Felice (1st century A.D.) on the cliff of Capo di Sorrento: six rooms turned with traces of stuccoes, a porch with marble columns, covered passages, stairs and terraces towards the sea, with views of Vesuvio, Ischia, Punta Campanella and Capri. About 30 minutes walk from the center, free access from the path; better at sunrise or sunset in high season.

  • Where Traversa Punta Capo, Sorrento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it
