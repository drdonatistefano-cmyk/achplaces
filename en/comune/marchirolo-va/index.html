What to see in Marchirolo

Marchirolo, in Valmarcarcarolo, keeps in the Gipsoteca Spazio Sculpture Pellini Bozzolo the plasters of the sculptors Eugenio and Eros Pellini and Adriano Bozzolo, linked to the local tradition of the gessers and the "picapietra" (paintings) and from the parish church of San Martino you go up to the church of San Paolo, a panoramic point and leads the paths of the Cadorna Line towards Monte La Nave.

Gipsoteca Spazio Scultura Pellini Bozzolo

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

Inaugurated in 1996, it contains about thirty works by Eugenio Pellini (1864-1934), his son Eros Pellini (1909-1993) and Adriano Bozzolo (1927-2011), in a country linked to the tradition of the plasterers and the "picapietra" ('picapietra'). Chiesa di San Paolo e conventino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

From the parish church of San Martino you can reach the church of San Paolo with the annexed convent, one of the churches listed by the municipality. From here you can enjoy a wide view of the Valganna, Valmarcarcolo, the valleys and the mountains of the Canton of Ticino and Lake Ceresio.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sentiero della Linea Cadorna: Alpe Paci e Monte La Nave

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Viewpoint

From the church of San Paolo the path in the woods in Alpe Paci crosses the works of the Cadorna Line: escape routes, niches where supplies were kept and two small barracks to shelter. Continuing towards the summit of Mount La Nave, two paths lead to the observatory, with an entrance arch.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
