What to see in Acqualagna

Borgo sulla via Flaminia nato dai sovrivati della romana Pitinum Mergens, is known as the capital of truffle for the national autumn fair; its territory includes the Furlo Gorge, with the tunnel dug under Vespasian in 76 AD and the state nature reserve, and the remains of the abbey of San Vincenzo al Furlo.

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Approximately 38 metres long, 5,47 metres wide and about 6 metres high, it retains on the walls the signs of the Roman tools and an inscription at the north-east entrance that reminds of their construction; it is still accessible by pedestrians and vehicles. Riserva Naturale Statale Gola del Furlo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established in 2001, it covers 3,626,94 hectares between Acqualagna, Cagli, Fermignano, Fossombrone and Urbino: a limestone gorge excavated by Candigliano between Monte Pietralata and Monte Paganuccio, with jurassic layers and clay rich in ammonites. Leccete, oaks, a beech forest on the Paganuccio and highland meadows; a couple of royal eagles have been nesting there for years, along with the pilgrim hawk, and in the woods live wolf, roe deer and deer.

  • Where Gola del Furlo, Acqualagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Museo del territorio Gola del Furlo

Museums · Themed

It tells the protected area of the Furlo Gorge by combining natural history, geology and landscape with the history of man, architecture and culture, with the role of the mountain at the centre. There's no need to book.

Antiquarium Pitinum Mergens

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 2002 in the former sixteenth-century town hall, it exhibits the excavations of the Roman city of Pitinum Mergens, probably born with the assignments of the Lex Flaminia (232 BC) and the construction of the Flaminia and destroyed in the sixth century during the Gothic war; there are also exposed the remains of the Roman rustic villa of Colombara (II century BC) and a section of contemporary art.

Chiesa di San Vincenzo al Furlo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Remains of an 8th century abbey where St Romualdo (1011) and St. Pier Damiani (1042) stayed, remained autonomous until the 11th century, then passed under the influence of Fonte Avellana. Nearby is a small Roman bridge.

Tartufo bianco e Fiera del Tartufo

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Acqualagna is the capital of the truffle: the national fair, held every year from 1965 between late October and early November and now international, offers exhibition-market, cooking shows with starred chefs, tastings and workshops. The Tuber magnatum Pico grows in the woods of Furlo; the harvest runs from 1 October to 31 December and the area holds the national record of sales.
