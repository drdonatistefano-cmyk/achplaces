What to see in Montereale Valcellina

Porta della Valcellina at the foot of the Friulian Dolomites: the former Malnisio hydroelectric power station with original turbines and generators, the archaeological museum in Palazzo Toffoli, the necropolis of the Iron Age of Dominu in Grizzo and the Forra del Cellina, the largest canyon in the region.

Ex centrale idroelettrica «Antonio Pitter» di Malnisio

Museums · Science and university

Central of the early twentieth century that transformed the power of the Cellina stream into energy, with original turbines and generators. The multimedia route, in Italian and English, has totem actually increased, a room on the geology of the territory, the construction of the socket and interactive stations on the architecture and the workers of the plant. Visits by reservation through Eupolis.

  • Where Via Alessandro Volta 27, Malnisio, Montereale Valcellina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Museo archeologico di Montereale Valcellina (MAMV)

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the seventeenth-century complex of Palazzo Toffoli, exhibits the excavations carried out in the municipal territory in the last twenty years of the twentieth century: testimonies from the recent bronze age (XIV century BC) to the Middle Ages, including the supplies of the necropolis of Dominu. Open from May to September Saturday and Sunday 15-18, from October to April the first Sunday of the month 15-18.

  • Where Palazzo Toffoli, Montereale Valcellina
  • Entry €4 (reduced €3)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it

Necropoli del Dominu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Small necropolis with incineration of the iron age, used for about a century between the VIII and VII century BC: in the excavations of the eighties twenty tombs in three parallel rows emerged, the most recent protected by limestone slabs, today partially reconstructed. It is located in the Dominu municipal park, at the foot of the Grizzo hill; the furnishings are at the archaeological museum.

  • Where Parco comunale del Dominu, Grizzo, Montereale Valcellina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it · turismofvg.it

Riserva naturale Forra del Cellina

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 300 hectares between Andreis, Barcis and Montereale Valcellina, managed by the Friulian Dolomites Park: the largest limestone gorge in the region, with perennial water, waterfalls, erosion forms and rare plants. It crosses the old Valcellina road between Monte Fara and Pala d'Altei; there is a 55-metre Tibetan bridge. The visitor center is in Ponte Antoi (Barcis).

  • Where Montereale Valcellina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it
