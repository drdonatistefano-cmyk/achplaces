What to see in Noviglio

In the South Milan Agricultural Park, Noviglio hosts the Kartell Museum, in the factory designed in the sixties by Anna Castelli Ferrieri and Ignazio Gardella, and historic farmsteads such as the Castellazzo, fortified complex before the fifteenth century with traces of a Visconti coat of arms, and Tavernasco.

Kartell Museo

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Founded in 1999 for the company's 50th anniversary by President Claudio Luti, in 2000 he won the Guggenheim Enterprise & Culture Prize as the best business museum. It is based in the Kartell factory, headquarters since 1967, designed by Anna Castelli Ferrieri and Ignazio Gardella and considered one of the most interesting examples of industrial architecture in Lombardy. The collections count over 8,000 objects, 5,000 drawings and 15,000 photographs; the permanent exhibition, on more than 2,000 square meters with Ferruccio Laviani's set-up and the care of Elisa Storace, goes from 1949 to the present, between car accessories, housewives, lamps and furnishings become icons of Italian design.

visits only by reservation, without fixed times, according to In Lombardia and Museimpresa

  • Where Via delle Industrie 3, Noviglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.kartell.com

Sources: kartell.com · in-lombardia.it · museimpresa.com · luoghidelcontemporaneo.cultura.gov.it

Cascina del Castellazzo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortified agricultural complex before the fifteenth century, divided into two courts by the municipal road. Along the road a mill and a building with fifteenth-century characters, then transformed into houses for workers; in the main court the ancient body of the castle preserves traces of a fresco with Visconti coat of arms, while the southern court is of service, with a long array of houses. Both courts are still used for agriculture.

private property, accessible only the ground floor according to the Municipality

  • Where Via Antonio Vivaldi 11, Noviglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.noviglio.mi.it · in-lombardia.it

Cascina Tavernasco

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Large courtyard farmhouse: to the north, along the road, an oratory with the chaplain's house and the castle, the only surviving building of the ancient court. Along the avenue of access the pigsty and the court of the housekeeper with portico; on the west side of the main court dairy with carpentry and dryer, manor house and pile; to the south the house of the farmer with stables and the beef barn, to the east a portico and to the north the new bull stable, rebuilt after a fire.

accessible only the ground floor according to the Municipality

  • Where Cascina Tavernasco, Noviglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.noviglio.mi.it · in-lombardia.it
