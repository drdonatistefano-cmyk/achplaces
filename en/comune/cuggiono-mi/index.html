What to see in Cuggiono

Cuggiono is the village of Villa Annoni, neoclassical residence of the architect Giuseppe Zanoja with a village park of 23 hectares, the second in Lombardy for extension after that of the Villa Reale of Monza; in the rooms of the villa is the Civic Historical Museum, while in the hamlet of Castelletto the Villa Clerici overlooks the Naviglio Grande.

Villa Annoni e il suo parco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

At the end of the eighteenth century Count Giampietro Annoni bought the land and entrusted the villa to the Genoese Giuseppe Zanoja, also known for the façade of the Duomo of Milan; presumably completed in 1809, since 2007 it hosts the Town Hall after the restoration of the frescoes. The park of 23 hectares, built between 1819 and 1825 as an English country garden, is the second in Lombardy for extension after that of the Villa Reale di Monza: a perspective telescope leads to the Ionic temple erected after 1825 in memory of Count Alessandro Annoni, and along the avenues you will meet the coffee house, the house of the deers, the artificial cave, the pond and the greenhouse-limonaie fifteen arches, with over 160 tree species including cedars of Lebanon of the original plant. The municipality opens it freely (from 25 October to 31 December 2026 every day 9-18.30, closed in case of rain or wind); guided tours with the Cultural Guides Association of Cuggiono.

  • Where Piazza XXV Aprile 4 (ingresso al parco da via Magenta), Cuggiono
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page comune.cuggiono.mi.it

Museo Storico Civico Cuggionese

Museums · Themed

Born in 1983 at the invitation of the mayor Bandino Calcaterra, who asked the citizens to donate agricultural and handicraft items, he occupied the premises of Villa Annoni once destined for servitude, recovered by volunteers. In 15 rooms he collects tools and tools of agricultural crafts and artisans, documents and objects related to wars, testimonies on emigration, associationism and the first forms of social security and social assistance. According to the municipality, it opens every afternoon from May to October, together with the park of the villa.

  • Where Villa Annoni, Piazza XXV Aprile 4, Cuggiono

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Villa Clerici e il Naviglio Grande a Castelletto

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

On a terrace overlooking the Naviglio Grande, in the hamlet of Castelletto, the villa has the present eighteenth-century appearance: H-shaped plant on the ground floor, mezzanine and first floor, lower bodies around the courtyard and a staircase that descends to the Naviglio, originally the main entrance. It is considered one of the most sumptuous villas of Naviglio and Milanese and closes the itinerary of the Villas of Delight of Naviglio Grande.

private property, almost completely abandoned and waiting for restoration according to in-Lombardy
