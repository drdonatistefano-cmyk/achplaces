What to see in Biella

Town of wool at the foot of the Alps, a UNESCO creative city, on three levels: the Piano with Duomo and Romanesque baptistery, the medieval village of Piazzo, since 2025 among the most beautiful villages in Italy, and the Sanctuary of Oropa on the Sacred Mount UNESCO heritage.

14 places to visit in Biella, in the province of Biella (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario di Oropa e Sacro Monte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

At 1180 meters, twelve kilometers from Biella, the complex where the Black Madonna is venerated and where in the seventeenth century the best architects of the Savoy court worked; part of the Sacred Mountains UNESCO, with the Museum of Treasures.

  • Where Via Santuario di Oropa 480, Oropa (Biella)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitpiemonte.com

Sources: italia.it · visitpiemonte.com

Battistero di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Symbol of Biella, from the beginning of the 11th century, among the most interesting examples of Piedmontese Romanesque; on the portal a Roman bas-relief, because under there was a Roman sepulchre.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Biella
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitpiemonte.com

Sources: visitpiemonte.com · italia.it · touringclub.it

Duomo di Santo Stefano e campanile

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built for a vote at the Madonna di Oropa after the plague of 1399; next to the bell tower of Santo Stefano of the 11th century.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Biella
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitpiemonte.com

Sources: visitpiemonte.com · italia.it

Basilica di San Sebastiano e Museo del Territorio

Places of worship · Basilicas

Renaissance church of elegant frescoes by Gerolamo Tornielli in 1540, the most beautiful in the city; in the cloister the Museo del Territorio biellase with Roman finds.

  • Where Via Quintino Sella 59, Biella
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitpiemonte.com

Sources: italia.it · visitpiemonte.com

Borgo del Piazzo e funicolare

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval village founded in 1160 by Bishop Uguccione on the hill, reachable with the panoramic funicular: low porticos, cobbled streets and houses decorated in terracotta; since 2025 among the most beautiful villages in Italy.

  • Where Biella Piazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitpiemonte.com

Sources: atl.biella.it · italia.it · touringclub.it · visitpiemonte.com

The heart of Piazzo, with its restaurants under the arcades, the sixteenth-century façade of the Princes' Palace Dal Pozzo della Cisterna and the terracotta arches of Casa Teccio.
