Santuario della Madonna della Neve

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Built in 1580, in the middle of the Counter-Reformation, about 600 m of altitude between woods and cultivated fields; according to the tradition in August, in mature wheat, it snowed and the Madonna appeared where the sanctuary stands today. The bell tower, very rare in Langa, has the shape of the papal triregno with three concentric crowns.

  • Where Strada del santuario, Saliceto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
