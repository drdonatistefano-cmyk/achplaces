What to see in Messina

The gate of Sicily on the Strait, reborn after the earthquake of 1908: Piazza Duomo with the Norman cathedral, the largest astronomical clock in the world and the Fountain of Orione, the Annunziata dei Catalani, the MuMe with Antonello and Caravaggio, the Madonnina del Porto and Capo Peloro.

12 places to visit in Messina, in the province of Messina (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di Messina

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Norman cathedral consecrated at the end of the 12th century, damaged by the earthquake of 1908 and the bombs of the war and rebuilt in the original forms: three naves, transept and three apses.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Messina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page international.unime.it

Sources: international.unime.it

Campanile e Orologio astronomico

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The 60-metre bell tower houses the largest and most complex astronomical clock in the world, made by the Ungerer company in Strasbourg and completed in 1933: every day at noon 54 bronze figures animate the history of the city for a quarter of an hour.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Messina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page international.unime.it

Sources: international.unime.it

Fontana di Orione

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The work of Giovanni Angelo Montorsoli with Martino Montanini (1547-1551), celebrates the first city aqueduct with the rivers Nile, Tiber, Ebro and Camaro.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Messina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page international.unime.it

Sources: international.unime.it

Fontana del Nettuno

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa della Santissima Annunziata dei Catalani

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the second half of the twelfth century in Norman age, the only building survived the earthquake of 1908; the west side is of the thirteenth century.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Garibaldi 111, Messina

MuMe – Museo regionale di Messina

Museums · Old masters

Sections of painting, sculpture and archaeology; among the masterpieces the Polyptych of San Gregorio di Antonello da Messina (1473) and two canvases of Caravaggio, the Adoration of the Shepherds and the Resurrection of Lazarus.

  • Where Viale della Libertà 465, Messina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page international.unime.it
