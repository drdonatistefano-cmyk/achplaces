What to see in Sant'Angelo Lodigiano

Birthplace of Saint Francesca Cabrini, patron saint of migrants: the Bolognini Castle on the Lambro with the tower of Regina della Scala and three museums, the cabrinian basilica, the birthplace of the saint and the thirteenth-century church of San Bartolomeo.

Castello Bolognini e Museo Morando Bolognini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortress in brick dating back to the 13th century between two branches of Lambro, probably on a Roman settlement: Queen of the Scala, wife of Bernabò Visconti, made it a summer residence and built the Mastra tower (36 meters) with bifores; in 1452 Francesco Sforza donated it to Michele Matteo Bolognini, from whom the name, and Giacomo Casanova there was guest in 1763. Restored at the beginning of the twentieth century by Count Gian Giacomo Morando Bolognini, it is managed by the Morando Bolognini Foundation (1933). In the 24 rooms of the Morando Bolognini Museum furniture from the 15th to the 17th century, weapons, paintings, sculptures, ceramics and a historical library; frescoed rooms and chapel with fifteenth-century tombstone. Guided tour, cumulative ticket for the three museums.

Openings only for guided tours on some Sundays and for groups by reservation; the MiC card (2021) indicated the museums closed until the date to be allocated: check with the secretariat (0371 211140)

Museo lombardo di storia dell'agricoltura e Museo del pane

Museums · Themed

In the stables of the castle: the agriculture museum tells about the agricultural innovations from 10,000 BC, with a section dedicated to the Lower Padana; the bread museum, in five rooms, the cycle from cereal to bread, the forms of Italian bread and the world and historical tools. They are visited with the castle, with cumulative ticket and guide.

Openings only for guided tours on some Sundays and for groups by reservation; the MiC card (2021) indicated the closed museums until the date to be dedicated

Casa natale di Santa Francesca Cabrini

Museums · House museums · Free entry

The modest 19th-century rural house where Francesca Cabrini, founder of the Missionaries of the Sacred Heart and assistant of the immigrants in America, was born on July 15, 1850, with six rooms on two floors, with panels, letters and documents in the ancient kitchen, the Christmas room with period furnishings and a chapel with a relic of the saint; documentation centre with hundreds of publications in more languages. Free admission; closed on Sundays.

  • Where Via Madre Cabrini 77, Sant'Angelo Lodigiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it · cultura.gov.it

Basilica dei Santi Antonio Abate e Francesca Cabrini

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

First shrine in the world dedicated to the saint, built between 1928 and 1938 in forms that recall the Bramantesque Renaissance, above a church documented at least from 1210: chapel of the Madonna del Rosario of the fifteenth century with frescoes of the Morazzone on the vault, seventeenth-century baptistery where Francesca Cabrini was baptized in 1850, chapel dedicated to her frescoed by Galizzi and Secchi and reliquary on the altar. Next, in the square, the monument to the saint and emigrater of Enrico Manfrini.

  • Where Piazza XV Luglio, Sant'Angelo Lodigiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it
