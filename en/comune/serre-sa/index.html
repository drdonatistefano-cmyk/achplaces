What to see in Serre

Small village of medieval origin on the slopes of the Alburni Mountains, crossed by the Sele: in the territory there are the WWF Oasis of Persano, famous for its otter, and in the hamlet of Persano the ancient Bourbon hunting pavilion of the Reggia, where the breed of horses of Persano was bred.

Oasi WWF di Persano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Large green area crossed by the Sele, within the Regional Nature Reserve Foce Sele-Tanagro: 110 hectares, about 70 occupied by the dam basin completed in 1934, wetland of international importance for the Ramsar Convention. It hosts one of the most important populations of otter, as well as foxes, wild boars, fairies and weasels, and is stop and wintering for migratory birds: 184 species, 47 nesting.

Reggia di Persano

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Bourbon hunting pavilion in the hamlet of Persano, today inside a vast military area: baroque palace with porch courtyard and four octagonal turrets with spiral staircases, and in the atrium a large staircase of honor with the marble statue of a guard dog attributed to Canova. In the eighteenth-century estate was selected the breed of horses of Persano, from which it is said to derive the palace of Ferrari.

Inside a military area: visit only on the occasion of events organized by the Italian Army
