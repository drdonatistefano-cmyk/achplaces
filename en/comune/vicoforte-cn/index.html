What to see in Vicoforte

The village of the Sanctuary Regina Montis Regalis, a Baroque masterpiece with the largest elliptical dome in the world.

4 places to visit in Vicoforte, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario Regina Montis Regalis

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Founded on July 7, 1596 by Carlo Emanuele I of Savoia, with the largest elliptical masonry dome in the world, by Francesco Gallo, completed in 1733; over 6,000 square meters of frescoes by Felice Biella and Mattia Bortoloni (1746-1748).

  • Where Piazza Carlo Emanuele I, Vicoforte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Salita alla cupola (Magnificat)

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

  • Entry To be checked

Chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo a Fiamenga

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Among the oldest places of worship in the area, with frescoes from the fourteenth century at the entrance.

  • Where Fiamenga, Vicoforte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Palazzata e monastero cistercense

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The semi-Otagonal palace with its shops and tourist office and the former Cistercian monastery.

  • Where Vicoforte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
