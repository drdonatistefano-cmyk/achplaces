What to see in Alessandria del Carretto

The highest village in the Pollino National Park, once called Torricella and refounded in 1633 by Prince Alessandro Pignone del Carretto, from which it takes its name and surname; among its churches stand out St. Alessandro and St. Vincenzo Ferreri, and on the last Sunday of April in Spinazzeta is celebrated the feast of Pita.

Centro storico e piazze

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Borgo refounded in 1633 by Prince Alessandro Pignone del Carretto, the only Italian town to bear the name and surname of the founder. Piazza del Popolo and Piazza Municipio are the heart of the village's life, with markets and parties; at the entrance of the historic center the chapel of San Rocco, in local stone.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Alessandria del Carretto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.alessandriadelcarretto.cs.it
