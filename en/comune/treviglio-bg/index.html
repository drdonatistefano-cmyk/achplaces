What to see in Treviglio

City of the Bergamo plain built between the 10th and 11th centuries around a castle; in the square the Basilica of San Martino with the Polyptych of Butinone and Zenale and a bell tower of 68 meters, not far from the Sanctuary of the Blessed Virgin of the Tears, linked to the miracle of 28 February 1522, and the civic museums.

Basilica di San Martino e Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Basilicas

On a small church of the fifth century dedicated to the Assumption, rebuilt in 1008 and enlarged between the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries, the basilica has the current layout since the last decades of the eighteenth century, with late Baroque façade by Giovanni Ruggeri and nave decorated with illusionistic frescoes by the Galliari brothers. It houses the Polyptych of San Martino, tempera on wood by the Treviglians Bernardino Butinone and Bernardo Zenale (1485-1490), with the center San Martino giving the mantle to the poor, presented in an exhibition space with multimedia path. The bell tower, 68 meters high, is among the highest in Lombardy.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Santuario della Beata Vergine delle Lacrime

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

It recalls February 28, 1522, when an image of Our Lady and Child painted at the end of the fifteenth century on a wall of the Monastery of St. Augustine shook tears and the French general Odet de Foix (Lautrec) spared the city from looting; on February 28 it has since been a town festival. After the authentication desired by San Carlo Borromeo, the first stone was laid on March 25, 1594 and the building was completed in 1619, when the image was taken to the main altar. It preserves the sword and helmet of Lautrec, works of different eras including Bernardino Butinone and contemporary mosaics of Trento Longaretti; it is a stop of the Via di Sant'Agostino. Open every day 7.30-18.30.

  • Where Via Fratelli Galliari, Treviglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · visitbergamo.net · italia.it
