What to see in Teolo

Municipality of the Euganean Hills, seat of the Regional Park: at the foot of Mount Lonzina stands the Benedictine Abbey of Praglia, with fifteenth-century cloisters and the monumental Library; in the sixteenth-century Palazzetto dei Vicari there is the contemporary art museum Dino Formaggio and on the summit of the Monte della Madonna the sanctuary entrusted to Praglia in 1508.

Abbazia di Praglia

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry

Benedictine monastery at the foot of Mount Longina, between the Middle Ages, late Gothic and Renaissance; the abbey church of Santa Maria Assunta has been a minor basilica since 1954. Three of the four cloisters (botanical, double and wall) are of the second half of the fifteenth century; in the monumental refectory a "Crucifixion" by Bartolomeo Montagna of the late fifteenth century and paintings by G.B. Zelotti. Guided tours without reservation from the concierge (weekly except Monday at 15 and 16 in winter, 15.30 and 16.30 in summer; Sunday and holidays afternoon); closed on Monday and on some holidays; offer for restorations, with at least €5 you receive the photo guide.

  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website praglia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Biblioteca monumentale di Praglia

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The National Monumental Library of the Abbey preserves about 100,000 volumes; in the upper room 17 paintings by the late 16th century painter G.B. Zelotti decorate the wooden ceiling. You usually visit the second and fourth Saturdays of the month together with the visit to the monastery, with tickets bookable on Eventbrite; to climb there are about 50 steps.

  • Where Via Abbazia di Praglia 16, Teolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website praglia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: praglia.it · fondoambiente.it · italia.it · eventbrite.it

Museo d'arte contemporanea «Dino Formaggio» - Palazzetto dei Vicari

Museums · Modern art

In the Palazzetto dei Vicari, 16th century building, the museum opened in October 1993 by the Municipality with Professor Dino Cheese, which donated the collection: about 200 paintings and sculpture from the end of the 19th century to today, including works by Medardo Rosso, Aligi Sassu and Tito Gasparini. Open Saturday and Sunday 9-12 and 16-19, without reservation.

  • Where Via Molare, Teolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Santuario della Madonna del Monte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

On the summit of the Mount of the Madonna, second relief of the Euganean Hills, a church is already attested by a testament of 1253; in 1508 Pope Julius II entrusted it to the Abbey of Praglia. Rebuilt largely in 1940, it has next to a small monastery with cloister built between 1955 and 1965; the restoration brought to light the walls of the sixteenth century chapel and in the presbytery there is a Madonna with the fourteenth-century Child in polychrome stone attributed to Andriolo de' Santi. On Sundays and holidays, the monks of Prague celebrate there.

  • Where Via Fontana Maggiore 1, Teolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Parco Regionale dei Colli Euganei

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Protected area of about 18,694 hectares in the province of Padua, based in Teolo and the first Veneto Regional Park (since 1989): about one hundred volcanic reliefs, none more than 600 meters. A network of walking, cycling and horseback riding trails includes the 78 km cycle ring of Euganean Hills and the 41 km trekking Alta Via dei Colli.

  • Where Teolo (sede del Parco)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it
