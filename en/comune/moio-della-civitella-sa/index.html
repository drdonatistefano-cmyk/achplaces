What to see in Moio della Civitella

The town of Cilento, where, in the hamlet of Pellare, the Museum Polo "Giuseppe Stifano" gathers in the eighteenth century Palazzo Torrusio the first and most articulated ethnographic museum of the interior Cilento and a water mill of 1747, while on the hill of Civitella, among the chestnut trees, remain the walls of a fortified center of the fourth century BC.

Museo della Civiltà Contadina

Museums · Themed

Housed in the noble Palazzo Torrusio of the eighteenth century, it is described as the first and most articulated ethnographic museum of the interior Cilento: over a thousand objects from the seventeenth to the twentieth century, born also from the work of the local elementary schools, in domestic, historical, arts and crafts, textiles, weights and measurements, olive growing, wine, cereals and religion (the latter in the nearby church of San Bartolomeo). The main piece is the base of a sandstone wine press with symbolic engravings, found in Pellare in 1978. Part of the Municipal Museum Pole founded in 2016, open from Tuesday to Sunday 9-18, closed on Monday; recommended reservation, mandatory for groups.

Scavi archeologici della Civitella

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the hill of Civitella, in the shadow of a chestnut grove, the excavations have returned terrace walls, tiles, vases, an arched door and foundations of the fourth century BC, remains of a fortified center that controlled the valleys towards the sea of Velia. On the south side there is a well preserved door with narrow corridor and stone hinges of the wooden door; the central plateau, excavated only in part, has a paved space and a building perhaps of worship, as suggested by a votive deposit.
