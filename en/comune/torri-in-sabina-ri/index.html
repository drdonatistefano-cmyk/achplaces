What to see in Torri in Sabina

The Sabina municipality mentioned in 747 in the Regesto di Farfa: in the plain of Vescovio the church of Santa Maria, Sabina cathedral until 1733 with frescoes of the Cavallinian school, rises on the remains of the Roman town hall of Forum Novum; on the gorge of the Laia are facing the twin fortresses of Rocchette, still inhabited, and Rocchettine, abandoned village.

Santa Maria di Vescovio

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Also known as Santa Maria della Lode, the ancient cathedral of the diocese of Forum Novum and then of Sabina until 1733: a single nave with raised presbytery and semi-anular crypt of the 11th century, cycle of frescoes of the 2rd-Three hundred with stories of the Old and New Testaments and, in counterfaced, a great universal judgment of the school of Pietro Cavallini, rediscovered under the skilful in 1930; five-order bell tower of double and triple windows. Open every day 8-20 according to the MiC card.

  • Where Vocabolo Vescovio, Torri in Sabina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · visitlazio.com

Area archeologica di Forum Novum

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Born in the second century BC as a pastoral market at the intersection of Flaminia and Salaria, it became a town hall in the Cesarian age and is mentioned by Pliny; not a city but a complex of public buildings: civil basilica, forum, temple perhaps dedicated to Hercules, thermal baths, amphitheatre in wood, tabernacles and aqueduct. Among the olive groves next to the church are stretches of paved road, walls in cross-linked work and arches of the aqueduct.

  • Where Strada Santuario Vescovio 68, Torri in Sabina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Rocchettine

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient Rocca Guidonesca, fortress of the thirteenth century on the Laia gorge guarding the road between Rieti and the Tiber valley, long belonged to Savelli; abandoned after the war, it preserves intact the medieval system between ruins of houses and shops. The only restored building, in the eighteenth century, is the church of San Lorenzo, usually closed, where every August 10 comes in procession the people of Rocchette.

free access but the source recommends caution among the ruins

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it

Rocchette

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient Rocca Bertalda, twin Rocchettine on the other side of the gorge, still inhabited by a few residents: door of 1601, stone palaces, excavated alleys and houses leaning on the medieval walls; open all year round.

  • Where Via Palazzo, Rocchette, Torri in Sabina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com
