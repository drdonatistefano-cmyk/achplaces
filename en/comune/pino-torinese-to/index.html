What to see in Pino Torinese

Municipality on the hill of Turin, next to the Astronomical Observatory, seat of Infini.to, the Planetarium of Turin and Museum of Astronomy and Space, reached by Superga from the Path of the Planets; it also houses the Alvar Aalto - MAAAD Institute founded by Leonardo Mosso.

Infini.to - Planetario di Torino, Museo dell'Astronomia e dello Spazio

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

Scientific dissemination center opened in 2007 on the hill of Pino Torinese, next to the Astronomical Observatory. The four-storey interactive museum makes us experiment with the laws of the cosmos (simulated gravity, speed of light, gravitational vortices, great structures of the universe) and retraces the history of space exploration; the digital planetarium, in a sphere that evokes a red supergiant star, offers projections for all ages, with workshops, conferences and telescope observation evenings. Free with Museums and Torino+Piemonte Card subscription; online purchase recommended.

  • Where Via Osservatorio 30, Pino Torinese
  • Entry €13, reduced €11
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.planetarioditorino.it

Sources: planetarioditorino.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it · turismotorino.org

Istituto Alvar Aalto - Museo dell'Architettura, Arti Applicate e Design (MAAAD)

Museums · Modern art

Founded by architect and artist Leonardo Mosso with Laura Castagno, his father Nicola and friends artists and architects: born as an association in 1979, became a museum in 1984. It preserves collections and testimonies of the European twentieth century and the studios and archives of the two founding artists.

MiC card (updated in 2021) is closed every day: check opening before visit

  • Where Via Antonelli 6, Pino Torinese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Sentiero dei Pianeti

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Thematic path designed by Pro Natura Torino that connects the square of the Basilica of Superga, where a 1.6 m steel sphere represents the Sun, in Infini.to: 13 stations with planets on scale and information panels along the GTC track in the Natural Park of the Superga Hill, about 8 km, 3 hours and 20 minutes, 400 m of altitude difference uphill. Inaugurated on October 5, 2025.

the stretch Jupiter-Saturno is interrupted to Baldissero Torinese for a yard, with alternative path indicated from the Municipality

  • Where Da piazzale della Basilica di Superga (Torino) a Via Osservatorio 30, Pino Torinese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.pinotorinese.to.it · italia.it
