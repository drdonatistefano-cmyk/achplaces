What to see in Cingoli

The Balcony of the Marches, Borgo dei Borghi 2026: historical center closed by almost intact walls, the Madonna del Rosario of Lorenzo Lotto in the Town Hall, the collegiate church of Sant'Esuperanzio and, below, the lake of Castreccioni.

14 places to visit in Cingoli, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo comunale e Madonna del Rosario di Lorenzo Lotto

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The palace, built in several phases with the oldest part perhaps of the twelfth century, houses in the Hall of the Coat of Arms the "Madonna del Rosario" by Lorenzo Lotto, originally painted for the church of San Domenico.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Cingoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it
