What to see in Roccascalegna

Village clinging to the steep side of Mount San Pancrazio, about 30 km from the sea, with houses that seem to come out of the rock, dominated by the Norman-Svevo castle with four towers; in the village the church of San Pietro (1260) and the abbey of San Pancrazio.

Castello di Roccascalegna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortification of Norman-Sveva origin between the XI and XII century, with four towers, in a dominant position on top of a rocky spur above the village.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village clings to the steep side of Mount San Pancrazio and the houses seem to emerge from the rock, an example of integration between architecture and landscape. Feast of Saints Cosma and Damiano with the Parade of Conche and Conocchia; typical products honey, oil, cheese, cold cuts and truffle.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it
