What to see in Todi

City on the hill above the Tiber Valley, closed by three walls: Piazza del Popolo with medieval palaces, the Duomo, San Fortunato with the tomb of Jacopone and, under the walls, the Renaissance Temple of the Consolation.

11 places to visit in Todi, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Todi in the guide with map and filters

Piazza del Popolo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Well-preserved medieval complex, surrounded by the Duomo and public buildings: the Palazzo del Popolo (1214-1228), among the oldest civic buildings in Italy, the Palazzo del Capitano of the late thirteenth century and the Gothic Palazzo dei Priori (1334-1347) with the square tower.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Todi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Museo Civico e Pinacoteca

Museums · City history

On the upper floor of the communicating Palazzo del Popolo and Palazzo del Capitano, from pre-Roman to modern times; there is the Coronation of the Virgin of Spain, already in the monastery of Montesanto.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Todi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Tempio di Santa Maria della Consolazione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Among the highest Renaissance buildings in Umbria, started in 1508 on a project attributed to Bramante and completed in the early seventeenth century.

  • Where Todi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Fortunato e campanile

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built from 1292 to 1465 on top of a staircase, with unfinished façade and three carved portals, three aisles of equal height and thirteen chapels; in the crypt of 1596 Jacopone da Todi rests, inside the Madonna and Child of Masolino; the bell tower can be visited, with 360 degrees view.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Todi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Concattedrale della Santissima Annunziata

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The Romanesque-Gothic Duomo, from the 12th to the 14th century, with a richly decorated rose window and portal, a fourth nave on the right side and in counterfaced the Last Judgment of Ferraù Fenzoni.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Todi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Monastero delle Lucrezie e Museo Lapidario

Museums · Archaeological · Viewpoint

Monastery of the XIV-XV centuries restored by the Municipality: it houses the Lapidary Museum with Roman funerary monuments and medieval and modern works, and a panoramic cloister on the countryside.

  • Where Via del Vescovado 12, Todi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Parco Beverly Pepper e Rocca

Museums · Modern art · Free entry · Viewpoint

Free-entry park established in 2019 with 16 works by the American artist, who lived in Todi since 1972; the Todi Columns stand before the 14th-century castle of the Fortress commissioned by Pope Gregory XI.

  • Where Todi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Casa Dipinta

Museums · House museums

House of Irish artist Brian O'Doherty, transformed in forty years into a cycle of frescoes that involves the entire building.

  • Where Via delle Mura Antiche, Todi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Convento di Montesanto

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Convent where St Bernardino retired at the beginning of the fifteenth century; in front of the entrance the linden planted on that occasion.

  • Where Montesanto, Todi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint
