What to see in Oglianico

Canavesian country without castle or walls, with a fourteenth-century shelter almost intact in the plan, defended by a medieval tower-door and with the frescoes of the first fifteenth century of the chapel of Sant'Evasio, next to the parish church of 1628.

Ricetto, torre-porta e cappella di Sant'Evasio

History and architecture · Villages

Fortified village of the 14th century used as a storage of crops and shelter for people and animals during the raids: almost square plant of about 70 m of side (4,900 m2), eight blocks and 62 cells, today largely houses, with broad roads for the intersection of two wagons and no square. The only entrance is defended by a medieval tower-gate in river pebbles, open towards the inside and on four floors, then civic and bell tower (the bells passed to the new bell tower in 1928, the clock remained until 1942). In a cell survives the apse of the chapel of Sant'Evasio (later Santo Spirito), rural of the XI-XII century and documented since 1329, with frescoes of the first fifteenth century with intense colors.

Chiesa parrocchiale della Santissima Annunziata e San Cassiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The ancient parish church of San Cassiano stood outside the walls and existed in 1329; in 1583 the Municipality bought the land next to the shelter for a new church, finished in 1628 and dedicated to the Annunciation. In 1817 the parish passed from the diocese of Ivrea to that of Turin.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
