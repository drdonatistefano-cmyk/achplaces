What to see in Riofreddo

Border village between Lazio and Abruzzo on the ancient via Valeria, feud of the Colonna and then of the Del Drago: the castle of the XI-XII century, the Oratory of the Annunziata of 1422 entirely frescoed, the Roman bridge of San Giorgio and the Museum of Culture in the villa of Ricciotti Garibaldi.

4 places to visit in Riofreddo, in the province of Rome (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Oratorio della Santissima Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was created in 1422 by a Romanesque church at the behest of Antonio Colonna, lord of Riofreddo, under the pontificate of Martino V (the date is on a marble plaque on the façade). The only room with a barrel vault is entirely painted with a cycle of Gothic taste: the Crucifixion in the bezel above the entrance, the Annunciation on the wall of the altar and, in the vault, the Triumph of Christ with angels, the four Evangelists and the Doctors of the Church.

Museo delle Culture di Villa Garibaldi (MUDECU)

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the villa built at the end of the nineteenth century by Ricciotti Garibaldi, the last son of Giuseppe and Anita, he tells of a border territory between the Papal State and the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies: at the entrance the plastici of the convent of San Giorgio, the castle and the villa; on the lower floor are found from prehistory (fossil of Elephas antiquus italicus) to the imperial age, with the dagger of a fair warrior from the necropolis of Casal Rotondo; on the upper floor tools and crafts of the peasant and pastoral tradition, the local festivals and relics of the Garibaldi family. Accessible to the disabled.

  • Where Via Costanza Garibaldi, Riofreddo
  • Entry €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website crossfitorobica.it

Sources: crossfitorobica.it · visitlazio.com · cultura.gov.it

Castello Colonna-Del Drago-Pelagallo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built between the XI and XII century by the Columns on a Roman outpost guarding Via Valeria, with quadrangular plan with cylindrical towers at the corners (later reduced to two) and a front tower. The Colonna, lords from the 12th century, succeeded the Marquises of the Dragon until the 19th century; in 1859 it passed to the Pelagallos. Today it is private property, bound by the Superintendence since 1995.

not available since 2022 for restoration (Visit Lazio)

Ponte di San Giorgio

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Roman arched bridge with all sixth in square work on the stream Bagnatore, along the Valeria Nova street of the time of Nerva (97 A.D.); from the taxes protrude five shelves that held the wooden centers of the building. It takes its name from the nearby monastery of San Giorgio, of which the ruins remain; the Superintendence restored in 2008-2009 the nineteenth-century viaduct.

  • Where Torrente Bagnatore, Riofreddo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
