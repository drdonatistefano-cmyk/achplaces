What to see in Dolo

Cittadina della Riviera del Brenta, river port since the 15th century thanks to its basin, with its monumental squero; in the hamlet of Sambruson the eighteenth-century Villa Badoer-Fattretto with the Museo del Villano, the Roman antiquarium Lino Vanuzzo and Villa Venier Velluti.

5 places to visit in Dolo, in the province of Venice (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Antica conca di Dolo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

From the fifteenth century Dolo was a river port thanks to its basin of navigation on the Brenta; today the closed is underground and transformed into a small square.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Villa Badoer Fattoretto e Museo del Villano

Museums · Themed

An eighteenth-century villa surrounded by a park on the right bank of the Naviglio del Brenta, in Sambruson, where it also produces Venetian wine in bottle. In eight rooms, for about 1,000 square meters, the Museo del Villano collects about 20,000 objects of peasant life (equipments, instruments, documents) gathered by the founder Luigino Fattoretto, of sharecropping origins; the hall Â of edicts Â exhibits customs letters and notices of the Riviera del Brenta, also of Napoleonic era, next to clocks, musical instruments, carriages and calessi.

Museo archeologico Lino Vanuzzo (Antiquarium di Sambruson)

Museums · Archaeological

It preserves mainly Roman finds found in 1950 in the excavations of prof. Lino Vanuzzo: domestic ceramics, fragments of frescoes and mosaics and building elements, which suggest a settlement of some importance. The ancient sources here recall a mansio called Maio Medauco, near a branch of the Brenta.

MiC card indicates the museum closed every day; times to check

  • Where Via Brusaura, Sambruson, Dolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Villa Venier Velluti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa on the left bank in Sambruson, of which the park, the passageway and the last room on the left of the ground floor, the monumental staircase and the central hall of the noble floor are open. Open to the public every first Saturday of the month from 10am to 1pm.

  • Where Via Argine sinistro 105, Sambruson, Dolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: veneto.cultura.gov.it · italia.it
