What to see in Cermes

Country at the foot of the hiking area of Monte San Vigilio, near Lana, referred to as "Villa Cerones" in 857: above the village Castel Monteleone (Lebenberg), of the thirteenth century, with frescoed chapel and rococo garden, and from here starts the Waalweg of Marlengo towards Tel.

Castel Monteleone (Lebenberg)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built in the 13th century by the lords of Marlengo, also known as Lebenberg, on a morainic hill at 505 meters above the village; it was counts Fuchs from 1426 to 1828 and since 1925 it belongs to the family van Rossem, who lives there. You can visit the courtyards, the late-Gothic chapel with frescoes in part fourteenth-century recently rediscovered, the Rococo room of mirrors, the hall of the knights with the large family tree of Fuchs, the armory, the peasant stube with Gothic furniture, the staircase of the coats of arms (from 1216) and the French garden with a mulberry of over 200 years. Open from the end of April to the end of October with visits 10:30-12:30 and 14:00-16:30; Saturday only on request.

Waalweg di Marlengo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

12 km long path along the irrigation canal from Cermes to Tel, at the entrance of the Val Venosta, half coast with wide views of the Merano basin, the Dolomites and the Gruppo di Tessa; along the tavern and inn path with gardens and panoramic terraces.

  • Where Partenza da Cermes
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it
