What to see in Lanusei

An ancient mountain village on the southeastern slopes of Gennargentu, the bishop's seat of Ogliastra and the station of the Green Train: in the center the cathedral with the frescoes of Mario Delitala and the diocesan Museum, on the plateau of Selene the Nuragic archaeological park and, on Mount Armidda, the astronomical observatory.

Parco archeologico di Selene

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the granite plateau of Selene, almost 1,000 meters in a wood of holm oaks, chestnuts and oaks about 6 km from the village: remains the base of the nuraghe Gennacili, surrounded by about 200 huts and defensive walls, with two tombs of giants about 80 meters from each other and sacred sources. The smaller Tomb I has central stele and exedra with seat and chamber of about 7 meters of the fifteenth century BC; the Tomb II, about 12 by 5 meters, had a crowning pyramid trunk with three holes for the betiles (XIV-XII century BC). Picnic area; time indicated by MiC: summer 10-13 and 16-20, winter 10-13 and 15-sunset, closed on Mondays.

  • Where Bosco di Selene, Lanusei
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo diocesano dell'Ogliastra

Museums · Old masters

On the first floor of the Bishop's Seminary, a building dating back to the early 20th century, the four sections of the course range from minerals, fossils and Nuragic, Punic and Roman finds to the history of the diocese from the judicial period, to the sacred art of Ogliastra (silver, vestments, parish registers and rare books) and to documents on the arrival of the Salesians. Among the main pieces a parchment in Hebrew and Latin of the thirteenth century found in the binding of a sixteenth-century book, a Madonna of Monserrato in sandstone of the fourteenth century and a small bronze of Hellenistic Hercules.

  • Where Via Roma, Lanusei
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it
