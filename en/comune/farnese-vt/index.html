What to see in Farnese

Borgo on a spur of tufa in Tuscia, near the lake of Bolsena, linked to the Farnese family and known as the village of Pinocchio for the screenplay of Comencini 1972: Rocca, Porta Nuova, convents, the civic museum Rittatore Vonwiller and the Nature Reserve Selva del Lamone, which covers over a third of the territory.

Museo civico Ferrante Rittatore Vonwiller (MuRV)

Museums · Archaeological

In the former municipal warehouse of wheat ('Ammosso'), behind Palazzo Ceccarini-Chigi; born in 1980, museum since 1990, re-assembled in March 2003, it bears the name of the archaeologist of the University of Milan (1919-1976) who studied these places. It exhibits materials from prehistory to the modern age: Paleolithic, Neolithic and Eneolithic finds, the village of the final Bronze of Sorgenti della Nova with reconstructions, an Etruscan section (Naviglione, Rofalco, tombs of Palombaro) and the ceramics of medieval throws, which tell the daily life from the 14th to the beginning of the 17th century. It is part of the Bolsena Lake Museum System.

  • Where Via Colle San Martino, Farnese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Riserva Naturale Regionale Selva del Lamone

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 2,030 hectares of wild and rugged forest on the border with Tuscany, on a plateau of lava of the last phase of the volcano of Latera, with ancient eruptive cones, piles of lava called " " "murce" , craters of sinking and forre. Lecceta, mixed woods, beech trees at unusually low altitudes; traces from the Middle Paleolithic, necropolis, fortified villages, Roman roads and rural churches. It is crossed by the Trail of the Briganti and by about 50 km of white roads that can be used by car, as well as a network of paths.

  • Where Selva del Lamone, Farnese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com

Centro storico, Porta Nuova e palazzi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Porta Nuova, built in 1613 by the architect Ettore Smeraldi, combines the medieval and Renaissance parts of the village. In the center of Palazzo Chigi, of the eighteenth century, today seat of the Town Hall, and Palazzo Platonio, former residence of the Farnesian administrators; in the tuff are dug the medieval butts, wells used to preserve water and wheat.

Monastero delle Clarisse e chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Built in 1560 for the Friars Minor by will of the will of Giulia Acquaviva, wife of Pier Bertoldo Farnese; it includes a garden of about 5.000 m2, the cells of the nuns and a guesthouse. The church contains frescoes from the early sixteenth century, including that of the wedding of Galeazzo Farnese and Isabella degli Anguillara (1514-1518).
