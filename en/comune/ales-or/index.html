What to see in Ales

Birthplace of Antonio Gramsci on the eastern slopes of Monte Arci, home to one of the smallest dioceses in Italy: the Baroque cathedral of San Pietro di Domenico Spotorno with the Diocesan Museum, the space dedicated to Gramsci di Giò Pomodoro and, in the hamlet of Zeppara, the Museum of traditional toy of Sardinia.

8 places to visit in Ales, in the province of Oristano (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale di San Pietro Apostolo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Rebuilt several times, the current church was designed by Domenico Spotorno and consecrated in 1688, on the remains of a church wanted by woman Violante Carroz, Marquis of Quirra. Baroque façade between two twin bell towers united by a balaustrate porch and 36 meters high dome; inside a Latin cross painted with the stories of Saints Peter and Paul, a rare crucifix of the fourteenth century and an organ \"ad ala Â" of Roman school, the oldest preserved in Sardinia.

Museo diocesano d'arte sacra

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Opened in 2009 at the initiative of Mgr. Antonino Orrù on the second and third floors of the former Tridentino Seminary, 19th century building: works from the cathedral and other parishes of the diocese, including four bells from the 16th to the 18th century (the oldest cast in 1573 by Pietro Tolo) and two stone lions from the 17th century. Open on midweek holidays; from Monday to Thursday by reservation.

  • Where Piazza Cattedrale 1, Ales
  • Entry €3, reduced €1,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Museo del giocattolo tradizionale della Sardegna

Museums · Themed

Born from a research of the students of the middle school of Ales (1993-96) with the support of the University of Cagliari, opened in 2002 in the former elementary school of Zeppara and reopened in 2013 after the restoration: handcrafted toys of the Sardinian agropastoral world made with local materials, from the dolls of rags and pumpkin to the carts of asphodelo, trottole and sound instruments, with a carpentry workshop to build them.

  • Where Via San Simeone 9, frazione Zeppara, Ales
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · idese.cultura.gov.it

Piazza Gramsci e casa natale di Antonio Gramsci

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The house where Antonio Gramsci was born (1891-1937) is marked by a plaque. The space dedicated to him, the "Piano d'uso collectivee A. Gramsci is designed by the sculptor Giò Pomodoro and inaugurated on May 1, 1977, it has triangular plant with a fountain and a fireplace and was built with the participation of the whole population.

  • Where Piazza Antonio Gramsci, Ales
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · comune.ales.or.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of 1535 at the entrance of the Riu Mulinu valley, so called for a watermill that stood nearby; from 7 to 10 September there is celebrated the feast of Santa Maria, with the rosary recited in Sardinian, the bonfire, the "goccius" sung and processions with knights in costume and banners embroidered.

  • Where Valle del Riu Mulinu, Ales
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Parco del Monte Arci

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ales rises on the eastern slopes of Monte Arci, rich in obsidian deposits linked to prehistoric activity; the regional park includes the centuries-old Lecceta di Aquafrida.

  • Where Monte Arci, Ales
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it
