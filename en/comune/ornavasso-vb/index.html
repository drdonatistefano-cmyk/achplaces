What to see in Ornavasso

Country of the lower Ossola on the plain of Toce, with the sanctuaries of the Madonna del Boden and the Madonna della Guardia on the hill, the ancient marble quarry that from the fourteenth century refurbished the Duomo of Milan, the fortifications of the Cadorna Line and the Partisan museum dedicated to the Republic of Ossola.

8 places to visit in Ornavasso, in the province of Verbano-Cusio-Ossola (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario della Madonna del Boden

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

At 475 meters on a terrace between the chestnut trees (the boden in Walser means plateau), dominates the village and the plain of Toce: remember the Virgin who in the sixteenth century led home the shepherd Maria Della Torre, lost in the woods; built from 1530 and then expanded, has three naves and a fresco of the Virgin with Child. You can reach it by car or on foot; in front of the church a courtyard with spring water fountain. Open every day 9-17.

  • Where Via al Boden, Ornavasso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ornavasso.vb.it · distrettolaghi.it · italia.it

Raccolta ex voto della Madonna del Boden

Museums · Themed

1,147 ex-vote, about 40% before the twentieth century, once hung on the walls of the sanctuary and now exposed in two large halls next to the guardian's house: testimony of the devotion and daily life of the community.

  • Where Via al Boden, Ornavasso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ornavasso.vb.it · distrettolaghi.it · italia.it

Santuario della Madonna della Guardia

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Built between 1674 and 1772 on the headland of the Guardia, it takes its name from an ancient tower of signaling today bell tower: remained unfinished, it is considered the most important baroque testimony of the area and has a remarkable major altar in black marble.

  • Where Via al Boden 23, Ornavasso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Nicolao

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1542 and 1587, probably extending an older church, on the slope above the town to defend itself from the streams and full of the San Carlo stream; it preserves numerous paintings of St. Nicholas. Open every day 9-17.

  • Where Via al Boden, Ornavasso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ornavasso.vb.it · italia.it

Museo parrocchiale di arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

Sculptures and paintings from the 14th to the 18th century: the fourteenth-century wooden statue of St. Anthony the Abate, the oldest, a Madonna of the Rosary of the fifteenth century, works attributed to Julius Gualio, the Compplanted on Christ who died in 1612, the first wooden Baroque altar of the Shrine of the Guardia and a Last seventeenth-century Supper; historical library with volumes from the 16th to the 19th century.

  • Where Via Di Dio 99, Ornavasso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it · italia.it

Museo partigiano «Alfredo Di Dio»

Museums · Themed

Four halls of artifacts, photographs and documents on the "Valtoce" and on the "Grouping of Patriotic Divisions" (Alfredo Di Dio) were autonomous formations that supported the Republic of Ossola: after the Battle of Ornavasso in October 1944 partisans and civilians repaired in Switzerland, to return to fight until Milan on April 24-25, 1945.

  • Where Via Alfredo Di Dio 131, Ornavasso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it · italia.it

Antica cava del marmo

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Marble known from the Roman era (stiles and altars of the I-II century are at the Sforzesco Castle); from about 1380, via Toce, Lago Maggiore, Ticino and Naviglio Grande, refurbished the Duomo of Milan, the Certosa and the Duomo of Pavia and the Arch of Peace. It is among the first examples of excavation in the gallery; visits only by reservation, in front of the entrance a covered picnic area.

  • Where Via al Boden, Ornavasso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ornavasso.vb.it · italia.it

Linea Cadorna e Forte di Bara

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

An easy 3.3 km route (200 m of altitude difference, 1-3 hours) from Punta di Migiandone on a mule track with galleries in the rock, trenches and machine guns stations, to the Fort of Bara (410 m), with cannons and mortars and a plaque on the Battle of Ornavasso in October 1944; view of Lake Maggiore, Mergozzo and the Corni di Nibbio, and continue to the marble quarry.

in some stretches the road has no protection; bring a torch to the tunnels

  • Where Parcheggio di Punta di Migiandone, Ornavasso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it · italia.it
