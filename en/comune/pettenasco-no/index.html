What to see in Pettenasco

Small village on Lake Orta, known for the tradition of turning the wood told in the museum made in an ancient tournament; to see the parish church with the Romanesque bell tower, the lake promenade and the hilly hamlets of Crabbia and Pratolungo.

6 places to visit in Pettenasco, in the province of Novara (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo dell'Arte della Tornitura del Legno

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Set up in the ancient Maulini family tournament, which operates from 1886 to 1970 on the Roggia Molinara, exhibits tools, machinery and objects of the old workshops: turned wood has characterized the cusian handicrafts until the 1950s. On one accessible floor, managed by Pro Loco, it is part of the Ecomuseo del Lago d'Orta and Mottarone and hosts exhibitions, guided tours, workshops and a tourist information point.

  • Where Via Vittorio Veneto 10, Pettenasco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: comune.pettenasco.no.it · distrettolaghi.it

Parrocchiale dei Santi Audenzio e Caterina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A single nave church built in 1778 on a Romanesque chapel of the 12th century, dedicated to St. Audentius, friend of St. Julius. It has two bell towers: the Romanesque bell tower on the north side, among the oldest of the Riviera, in pebbles with slits and mullioned windows, and one in 1792. Inside the Adoration of the Shepherds by G. Cesare Procaccini and restored frescoes, including the Visitable during the time of the Masses.

  • Where Via Parrocchiale, Pettenasco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: comune.pettenasco.no.it · distrettolaghi.it

Piazza del Municipio e Casa medievale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

On the main square there is the nineteenth-century Town Hall, which preserves documents on the ancient origins of the village, and the medieval house that houses the tourist office "Pettenasco Nostra" and an exhibition of the local wines, including the Nebbiolo-Ghemme "Riviera di San Giulio" .

  • Where Piazza Unità d'Italia, Pettenasco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

A path along the coast of Lake Orta, away from noise and traffic, passing through villas, flower gardens and small coves.

Crabbia e Oratorio di San Martino

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small village on Monte Barro of restored ancient buildings, some noble, other rural. The oratory of San Martino, with a single nave with an elegant arched porch, has as a altarpiece a canvas of Sant'Anna; at the entrance of the hamlet a large wall painting depicts St. Martin on horseback. On the day of San Martino is held the Office of the Secchia and blesses bread for families; near is the viewpoint of Punta Crabbia on the lake.

  • Where Crabbia, Pettenasco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: comune.pettenasco.no.it · distrettolaghi.it

Pratolungo, San Grato e Madonna della Neve

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Fraction on a sunny hill terrace, known for its orchards. The ancient nucleus revolves around the small church of San Grato, more ancient than the main church, with a seventeenth-century altarpiece of the saint between the Virgin and other saints, invoked against hail and lightning; the church of Our Lady of the Snow (1683) preserves the venerated image framed by a polychrome wooden structure shaped like a mantle, with angels and cherubs.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
