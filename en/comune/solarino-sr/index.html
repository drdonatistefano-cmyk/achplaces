What to see in Solarino

The village of the hinterland of Siracusa, between gentle hills and countryside; on Piazza del Plebiscito overlook the Mother Church of St. Paul the Apostle, started in 1764, and the eighteenth-century Palazzo Requisenz, residence of the lords of the feud.

Palazzo Requisenz

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in 1758 by Don Giuseppe Antonio Requisenz as a country residence of the lords of the fief of Solarino, it has characteristics of Catalan art, of which the Municipality indicates it as the only specimen in Sicily. Since 1993 the Sicilian Region has recognized it as a monument of historical and artistic interest.

Chiesa Madre di San Paolo Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Dedicated to the patron saint of Solarino, it was built from 1764 on commission of the feudator, under the direction of the foreman Siracusan Luciano Ali; stopped the work for the economic difficulties of the client, it was carried out with the voluntary work of the population. Opened entirely to worship in 1853, when facade and interior were not yet finished, it was consecrated on February 19, 1938 and renovated in the seventies and eighties.

  • Where Piazza del Plebiscito, Solarino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Grotta del Ventaglio

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Karst cave under the village, with stalactites and stalagmites, discovered by chance on October 27, 1987 during the excavations for the sewer in via Dante Alighieri. It takes its name from a large thin and translucent stalactite in the shape of a fan in the center of the cavity; the tunnels suggest that it extends for tens of meters and is connected to other caves of the area.

closed to the public for its position, which can only be visited by qualified researchers; in 2023, the Municipality placed inspections to assess the reopening of the project.
