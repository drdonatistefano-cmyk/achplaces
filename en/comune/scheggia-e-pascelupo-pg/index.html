What to see in Scheggia e Pascelupo

Municipality of the Apennines along the Via Flaminia, the ancient post station Ad Ensem, entirely included in the Park of Monte Cucco between the Catria and the Cucco; in the territory the Romanesque abbeys of Sant'Emiliano in Conjunctoli and Santa Maria di Sitria and the Camaldolese Hermitage of San Girolamo.

Abbazia di Sant'Emiliano in Congiuntoli

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Church in white stone probably built in 1286, as a plaque says, transforming a chapel of 1201; two aisles of different width, perhaps one for the monks and one for the inhabitants, divided by massive octagonal columns, façade in hut with stone shelves. There are still vaulted monastic environments, part of the cloister and a tower.

  • Where Confluenza tra Sentino e Rio Freddo, strada Scheggia-Sassoferrato

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Abbazia di Santa Maria di Sitria

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

According to the historian Iacobilli founded in 1017 by San Romualdo, who spent the last years there; of the ward remains the Romanesque church in square stone to T, with an ogival barrel vault, altar in travertine of the thirteenth century on 14 columns and a crypt straight from a single column with Corinthian capital, perhaps of the sixth century. Reduced to the farmhouse after 1861, it returned to the monks of Fonte Avellana and was restored in 1972.

  • Where Valle del fosso Artino, strada Scheggia-Isola Fossara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Eremo di San Girolamo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Hermitage of the Camaldolese of Monte Corona near Pascelupo, on the eastern side of Monte Cucco, at the foot of high limestone walls: medieval cells carved into the rock between beech and chestnut trees, refectory, library and terraced gardens. Blessed Thomas da Costacciaro lived there for 65 years; reduced to rubble after World War II, it was restored since 1981 and reopened by the monks in 1992.

  • Where Presso Pascelupo, strada Scheggia-Sassoferrato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Centro storico di Scheggia e Torre Civica

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village was born on the Roman station Ad Ensem della Flaminia preserves the Civic Tower of the fourteenth century, the last surviving door of the ancient castle, the church of Sant'Antonio Abate of 1665, the parish church of Saints Philip and James of the sixteenth century with numerous works of art and the church of Mount Calvario of the seventeenth century.

  • Where Scheggia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Parco del Monte Cucco

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Protected area since 1996 that includes Costacciaro, Scheggia, Fossato di Vico and Sigillo, with gorges, valleys and pure waters; the Cave of Monte Cucco is among the largest in Italy and the massif offers excursions for everyone, even simple for children, as well as abbeys and Romanesque and Gothic hermitages.

  • Where Scheggia e Pascelupo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it
