What to see in Proceno

Medieval village in the extreme north of Lazio, the first Lazio village on the Via Francigena for those coming from Tuscany, dominated by the castle of the twelfth century with views of Lazio, Umbria and Tuscany; in the square the sixteenth century Palazzo Sforza, home of the museum of peasant civilization.

Castello di Proceno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress of the twelfth century with square tower on a spur of volcanic rock, border fortress of the Heritage of St. Peter, with walls that go down to overhang; according to the municipality the village was fortified by Pope Gregory V in 997, destroyed by the troops of Henry IV in 1083 and rebuilt by the Counts Bovacciani of Chiusi in 1097, and in 1444 it surrendered after months of siege the castle Bernardo d'Utri. From the blackbirds you can see 360 degrees on Lazio, Umbria and Tuscany, from Monte Amiata and Radicofani to the valley of Paglia; today it is a hotel with restaurant and from here starts the Lazio section of Via Francigena.

Palazzo Sforza

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Built in the middle of the sixteenth century for Cardinal Guido Ascanio Sforza, camerlengo and governor of Proceno, demolishing the ancient town hall; on the door remains his coat of arms. His brother Paul, Marquis of Proceno, completed it with a chapel and loggia on the valley of Paglia, largely lost in the collapses of 1873 and 1890. Purchased by the Municipality in 1985 and restored, it preserves frescoed vaults attributed to the school of Taddeo and Federico Zuccari, a coffered ceiling and a courtyard with a travertine well; it houses exhibitions and conferences.

Museo della civiltà contadina (MUD)

Museums · Themed

Born from a collection of the eighties of the association "Guido Ascanio Sforza" and opened by the Municipality in 1993 in four halls of Palazzo Sforza, exhibits about two thousand domestic objects and tools from the 16th to the 20th century. In June 2026 it was re-assembled on the top floor of the palace in the MUD - Urban Museum Diffuso, a journey on sharecropping and agrarian culture in five sections (history, land, hands, community, future) extended to the whole village.

MiC card (updated 2020) says it is closed every day and run by volunteers; re-arranged in the MUD in June 2026, unpublished times

Centro storico e chiese del borgo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The center preserves medieval and Renaissance buildings, partly in ruins, and stretches of the walls. The churches: San Martino, from the 13th century, with works from different eras; Sant'Agnese, of medieval foundation, dedicated to the patron saint of the village; Santa Maria della Neve, with the portal that bears the coat of arms of the boar and the pontifical keys, symbols of Proceno; Santa Maria del Giglio, with altars and decorations from the sixteenth century onwards.
