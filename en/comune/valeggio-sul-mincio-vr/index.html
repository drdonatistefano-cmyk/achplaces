What to see in Valeggio sul Mincio

The town of Scaligero dominates the village and, with the Ponte Visconteo of 1393, it formed the Serraglio as a guard of the river; at its feet Borghetto, among the most beautiful villages in Italy, with water mills, while the Parco Giardino Sigurtà extends 60 hectares of blooms, labyrinth and water mirrors.

Parco Giardino Sigurtà

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

A 60-hectare park, born from the "cinto de mura" , purchased by Gerolamo Nicolò Contarini in 1407 and grown around the Villa Sigurtà, a late seventeenth-century villa that in the nineteenth century was the headquarters of Francesco Giuseppe and Napoleon. Secular oaks, about one million tulips (March-April), a avenue of 30,000 roses (May-September), eighteen mirrors of water, labyrinth, garden of the medicinals, horizontal sundial, Hermitage, votive cave and farm with animals. Appointed the most beautiful park in Italy in 2013 and Europe in 2015; open from March to November, 9-18.

Borghetto sul Mincio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fraction of a few houses along the Mincio, with water mills grinding wheat and cereals, the turrita belt and the taverns where to stop; the name, fortified settlement, dates back to the Longobards. It is one of the most beautiful villages in Italy; in spring wisteria blooms. Cuisine of Valeggio tortellini and river fish (luccio, trota, eel).

  • Where Borghetto, Valeggio sul Mincio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Ponte Visconteo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified dam, the long bridge, built from 1393 to 1395 for Gian Galeazzo Visconti: 650 meters long and 25 meters wide, connected to the Scaliger Castle by two high crenellated curtains, formed with it the Serraglio, the defensive system of the Scaligero on the Mincio.

  • Where Borghetto, Valeggio sul Mincio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Castello Scaligero

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the hill above the village, it preserves the fortifications of the fourteenth century; the oldest part was almost destroyed by the earthquake of 1117 and remains the Tonda Tower with horseshoe. Originally had three drawbridges, today one. Towers and masts rise from the end of March to the end of October; the panoramic terrace looks at the morainic hills and in summer the courtyard hosts shows and outdoor cinemas.

Chiesa di San Marco Evangelista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of Borghetto, neoclassical reconstruction of 1759 of a Romanesque church of the 11th century dedicated to Santa Maria; it contains two frescoes from the fourteenth century.
