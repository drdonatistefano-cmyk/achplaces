What to see in Grottammare

Locality of the Riviera delle Palme with about 5 km of fine sandy beach, promenade of palm trees and Liberty villas, and behind the medieval village overlooking the sea: the Torrione della Battaglia with the works of Pericle Fazzini, the Museo Sistino dedicated to Pope Sixtus V, the church of Saint Lucia and the Orange Theatre.

Borgo medievale, Piazza Peretti e Teatro dell'Arancio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The original village on the edge of the hill, documented by the 11th century as Grocte or Grupte, is dominated by the remains of the fortress erected between the 9th and 10th centuries. From Piazzetta Peretti, near the Clock Tower, you can enjoy an exceptional view from the balcony porch of the building of the eighteenth-century Teatro dell'Arancio; from the Madonna park you can reach the ancient Incasato, with the portrait of the poet Mario Rivosecchi carved by Pericles Fazzini.

  • Where Piazza Peretti, Grottammare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Torrione della Battaglia, Museo Pericle Fazzini

Museums · Modern art · Viewpoint

Fortification of the castle's walls, built after the sacking of 1525 by the pirate Dulcigno and transformed into a museum in 2004: about 230 works by the grottammarese sculptor Pericles Fazzini, including bronzes, silver, gold, drawings, pastels, engravings and two portraits of Giuseppe Ungaretti, with the silver sketch of the Resurrection of the Sala Nervi in the Vatican.

  • Where Largo il Tarpato, Via Torrione, Grottammare
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · letsmarche.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista e Museo Sistino

Museums · Old masters

The present church, built in 1802 by Pietro Maggi, has a single nave decorated in the early twentieth century by Giuseppe Pauri and an organ by Carlo Carlo Carletti installed in 1884; reopened after the restoration concluded in 2002, it houses the Museo Sistino with the cup donated by Sisto V to his birthplace, a medal with the effigy of his sister Camilla Peretti, the Sacred Conversation of Vincenzo Pagani and two tables by Vittore Crivelli with San Rocco and San Sebastiano.

Chiesa di Santa Lucia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1597 by Camilla Peretti in memory of his brother Felice Peretti, Pope Sixtus V, next to the pontiff's birthplace; he conserves an organ by Francesco Fedeli of 1752.

MIC, Museo dell'Illustrazione Comica

Museums · Themed

Set up in the Kursaal Room, built in 1952 and found among the most popular on the Adriatic Riviera in the 1960s: sketches, movie posters, cartoons and satirical drawings of Italian artists, many arrived from the Festival Umorismo nell'Arte, with works by Dario Fo, Milo Manara and Sergio Staino and a room dedicated to Angelo Maria Ricci. Chiesa di San Pio V

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The centerpiece of the city's expansion to the sea, designed by Pietro Augustoni in 1779; was at Mass Franz Liszt, who played the organ Callido. Step of the Lisztian itinerary with Palazzo Fenili, where the musician stayed, and the square with the bronze statue of Sisto V by Aldo Sergiacomi (1984).

  • Entry Free

Lungomare, ville Liberty e spiagge

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 5 km of fine sandy beach with numerous establishments and a pedestrian promenade of palm trees imported from the Canaries in the early twentieth century; on viale Colombo, born in 1890 as Viale Marino, the Liberty villas. The bike path, about 20 km in all, connects San Benedetto del Tronto to the south and Cupra Marittima and Pedaso to the north.
