What to see in Sant'Anna d'Alfaedo

Municipality of western Lessinia, Sant'Anna d'Alfaedo is linked to the Ponte di Veja, the natural arch of about 40 meters that tradition brings to Dante and that Mantegna painted in the Chamber of Sposi, and to the Palaeontological and Prehistoric Museum, where is exposed a Cretodus fossil shark from the quarries of the Stone of Prun; on Mount Treasure stands a fort today owned by the municipality.

Ponte di Veja

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Natural arc with a light of about 40 meters, minimum thickness of 9 meters and width of 20 meters, indicated as the largest in Europe: according to one hypothesis it is what remains of an ancient karst cave (covolo) eroded by water, according to another is linked to the earthquake of 1223. Under the collapsed boulders at the base and in two caves were found remains of the Paleolithic, exposed in the nearby museums. Andrea Mantegna depicted it in the frescoes of the Chamber of the Sposi in Mantua and the tradition has it that Dante, exile to Verona guest of Cangrande della Scala, was inspired by the Malebolge. Walking paths and stairs with stone signs descend under the arch; accessible 24 hours a day, with parking at the Trattoria Ponte di Veja.

Museo paleontologico e preistorico

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 1998 and titled Don A. Blessed, he tells the story of Western Lessinia in two rooms. The prehistory section exhibits instruments from the Paleolithic lower than the age of metals, many from the Cave of Fumane and from the Riparo Tagliente of Stallavena: flints, engraved bones, painted stones, the cast of a burial of the upper Paleolithic, the "Sciamano" and a skeleton of bison. The section of paleontology shows marine fossils of the Stone quarries of Prun, including curls, ammonites and large sharks, in particular a cretodus of about 2 meters preserved with a sea turtle, perhaps his last meal: it is the most complete fossil known of this shark. Ground floor accessible by wheelchair; open from July to October Friday, Saturday, Sunday and holidays 9.30-18, the rest of the year by reservation.

Forte Monte Tesoro

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Historical structure of fortified military architecture, today also cultural center, acquired by the City at the end of 2013 with the federalism state, thanks to an agreement with the State and the Ministry of Culture. You will visit with guided tours on special dates, such as National Castle Days (May 2024 Sundays, 15-16.30, adults €10 and under 14 €5), with recommended reservation on the fort's website.
