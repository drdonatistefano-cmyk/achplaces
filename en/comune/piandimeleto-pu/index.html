Castello dei Conti Oliva

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Conquered in 1445 by Francesco Sforza, it was rebuilt by the Olivas: the construction was promoted in the 15th century by Count Carlo Oliva, patron saint of the courts of Sigismondo Malatesta and Federico da Montefeltro, remodeling an older fortress. Outside it has battlements and walkway from fortress, inside decorations in the style of the Ducal Palace of Urbino; on the noble floor the salon of honor is the Picta Room and the Heraldic Room, next to a Renaissance circular tower. It also houses the "Erbario delle Marche," with a section on the woods of Montefeltro.

Museo del lavoro contadino e Museo di scienze della terra

Museums · Themed

The Museo del Lavoro Contadino was founded in 1982 on the initiative of the Municipality with the Institute of History of the University of Urbino: on the ground floor of the castle, sections on the production of wheat, the farmhouse and crafts. On the first floor the museum of earth sciences, born from the donation of prof. Renzo Pieruccini, exhibits about 4,000 pieces on Earth, minerals and rocks.

Raccolta d'arte Ugo Ubaldi

Museums · House museums

Left to the Municipality in 1968 with the library of Commendatore Ugo Ubaldi, it is exhibited in its former residence, a building of the fifties that also houses the library: 220 pieces very heterogeneous, among archaeological fragments, statues, paintings, prints, furniture and souvenirs, left in the rooms with their ornamental function; stand out some funeral urns and twentieth century ceramics of factories Pesaro.

Centro storico medievale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The centre of medieval plant has chessboard streets and narrow houses, gathered around the Renaissance castle; at the end of July there is the Palio dei Conti Oliva, which evokes the life of court of the fifteenth century.

San Sisto

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

It was born in the 12th century from the union of neighbouring villages, with official foundation on March 14, 1123; it passed from the pontifical state to the Oliva counts.
