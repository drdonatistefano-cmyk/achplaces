What to see in Sirmione

Peninsula on the southern shore of Garda, closed at the entrance from the fourteenth-century Scaliger Castle with its dock and, at the tip, from the remains of the large Roman villa called Grotte di Catullo; in the middle a historic center of churches of different eras, sulphurous spas known from the Roman age and white rock beaches.

Castello Scaligero (Rocca Scaligera)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built on the water after the middle of the 14th century by Della Scala di Verona, under Cansignorio and Antonio II, to defend the territory of Verona that the family ruled from 1259 to 1387; then, lost importance in favor of Peschiera, it was storage and barracks, and the restorations since 1919 gave it the current appearance. It is among the best preserved castles of Italy and has its own dock, a rare example of fortification for port use: you can visit the courtyard, walks of the round, dock and panoramic mast; in the internal porch a Roman-Medieval lapidary. Ticket office at the entrance with seasonal opening; it is part of the Musei del Garda circuit with Caves of Catullo and Villa Romana of Desenzano.

Grotte di Catullo e Museo Archeologico

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Remains of a Roman villa of Augustine age (late 1st century BC-early 1st century AD) of about two hectares at the end of the peninsula: the name was born from the travellers of the 15th century who exchanged the ruins covered with vegetation for caves, but the villa was built after the death of Catullo. You can see the "Botteghe" , the Great Cryptoporic , the Hall of Giants , the large cistern and the thermal sector , in an olive grove of about 1,500 plants. The museum, opened in 1999, exhibits frescoes, stuccoes and mosaics of the villa and Lombard finds of the monastery of San Salvatore, including a pluteo with dedication to Desire and Adelchi. Times indicated by the MiC: Tuesday-Saturday 8.30-17, Sunday 8.30-14, Monday closed; cumulative valid 7 days with Castle and Villa Romana of Desenzano.

Centro storico di Sirmione

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Strade paved, ancient palaces and religious buildings of different eras on the village that dominates the lake; the walk from the Castle to the tip of the peninsula touches the church of San Salvatore with medieval remains, Roman archaeological sites, Piazza Carducci with Palazzo Callas (seats of exhibitions since 2002, over 400 m2) and San Pietro in Mavino. The route "Callas," Arie On Air" takes in 10 stages in the footsteps of Maria Callas with QR code to listen to the airs.

  • Where Piazza Carducci, Sirmione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: visitsirmione.com · in-lombardia.it

Chiesa di San Pietro in Mavino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

The oldest church of Sirmione, of Lombard origin (VIII century), on the hill of Mavino among the olive trees; the Romanesque bell tower was renovated in 1070 and the building rebuilt in 1320. Inside the 14th century frescoes and a fourteenth-century altarpiece with the Madonna enthroned with the Child and a saint.

  • Where Collina di Mavino, Sirmione
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: visitsirmione.com · in-lombardia.it

Spiaggia della Giamaica

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Under the Caves of Catullo, overlooking the Fortress of Manerba: large slabs of polished white rock that descend into clear water. Partly free and partly with rental beds, free showers; you can reach it from a gate just before the entrance to the caves with a dirt road and steep descent among the olive trees. Dogs on leash, forbidden camping and fires.

  • Where Sotto le Grotte di Catullo, Sirmione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: visitsirmione.com · in-lombardia.it

Lido delle Bionde

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Beach between the gardens of Villa Cortine and the Caves of Catullo, whose name comes from "biunda" closed place; in front of the shore a small stone fence where sometimes comes hot water from the thermal baths. Free access, showers, bar and restaurant, pets allowed; sun loungers on reservation the day before.

  • Where Lungolago, Sirmione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: visitsirmione.com · in-lombardia.it

Punta Grò e Casa del Pescatore

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Park along the lake at the end of Sirmione, on the border with Peschiera, overlooking Lake Lugana: centuries-old poplars, a beach and wide panorama. It is the starting point of Sirmione's Literary Route; at the Casa del Pescatore an outdoor exhibition on traditional lake fishing.

  • Where Punta Grò, Sirmione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: visitsirmione.com · in-lombardia.it

Terme di Sirmione (Aquaria)

Local life and traditions · Spas

Sulfurean waters, salso-bromo-iodiche from the Bojola hot spring, already known in Roman times. The main facilities are the Terme Virgilio (Columbare) and Catullo (historic centre), with inhalations, mud, baths and massages; the Aquaria center has about 3,500 m2 covered and 6,500 m2 of park, with indoor and outdoor pools overlooking the lake open all year round, even in the evening. Booking recommended.

  • Where Piazza Don A. Piatti 1, Sirmione
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · visitsirmione.com
