What to see in Oleggio

Cittadina del Novarese with one of the most important Romanesque churches in northern Italy, San Michele, frescoed around 1060; in the center there is the parish church of Saints Peter and Paul with the Museum of Religious Art, the Ethnographic and Archaeological Museum Fanchini and the neoclassical Palazzo Bellini.

Chiesa di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque parish already mentioned in 973 and built in the middle of the 11th century, in pebbles of river with fishbone, three naves and three apses with elevated presbytery on a vaulted crypt, the only Romanesque crypt left in Novara. The cycle of frescoes, of about 1060, is among the most important of the Romanesque in northern Italy: Last Judgment in counterfaced, Christ blessing with the Deacons, Apparitions of St. Michael in Gargano; later the Resurrection attributed to Franceschino Cagnola and St. Michael of Joannes Maria de Rumo. Abandoned in the sixteenth century for the new parish, it rises in the municipal cemetery.

  • Where Via Mezzomerico 5 (cimitero), Oleggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.oleggio.no.it

Sources: comune.oleggio.no.it · turismonovara.it

Museo Civico Etnografico Archeologico C.G. Fanchini

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In a former Franciscan monastery around a portico cloister, born from the collection of Carlo Giacomo Fanchini (first exhibition in 1969, museum inaugurated in 1974): the ethnographic section has over 200,000 pieces in a sixty-seven salt, with domestic environments and a school classroom of the early twentieth century, the silkworm and the search for gold in the Ticino; the archaeological section exhibits Goolasecchiani finds from Pombia (VII-V century BC) and accessories of the necropolis of Oleggio-Loreto (II century BC-IV century AD). Accessible, with tactile path; free entrance also with guided tour.

  • Where Vicolo Chiesa 1, Oleggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page comune.oleggio.no.it

Sources: turismonovara.it · comune.oleggio.no.it

Museo d'Arte Religiosa "Padre Augusto Mozzetti"

Museums · Old masters · Free entry

Founded in 1970 by Father Augusto Mozzetti in the oratories of the ancient confraternities on the sides of the main altar of the parish, with a section in the oratory of Santa Croce. He collected works from the 14th to the 20th century: a fresco of the Madonna with Child, a wooden sculpture of the 15th century, the "Madonna delle rose" attributed to Bernardo Zenale, a painted iron dragon used in the Rogations and a large collection of ex-vote tablets.

  • Where Piazza Don Bertotti 2, Oleggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.oleggio.no.it

Sources: comune.oleggio.no.it · turismonovara.it

Chiesa Parrocchiale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A neoclassical church built between 1853 and 1858 on a project by Alessandro Antonelli, with four granite columns pronao and dome on the main altar; decorations by Luigi Morgari (dome and apse, 1898) and polychrome windows by Giovanni Da Re (1927-1943), including the It preserves an Immaculate Morazzone and a altarpiece of the Madonna enthroned attributed to Bernardino Lanino.

  • Where Piazza Don Bertotti 2, Oleggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.oleggio.no.it

Sources: comune.oleggio.no.it

Palazzo Bellini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Neoclassical palace renovated in the last decade of the eighteenth century by the architect Stefano Ignazio Melchioni, with colonnade porch, staircase with stone balustrades and frescoes of classical statues; the salon has faux marble walls and the round of Nesso that kidnaps Deianira, a small room four rounds with the myth of Enea and Didone. Split between private and municipality, which organizes exhibitions and events.

partly private property: the municipal spaces are visited during exhibitions and events; not accessible to people with motor disabilities

  • Where Piazza Martiri della Libertà, Oleggio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.oleggio.no.it

Sources: comune.oleggio.no.it

Oratorio di Santa Maria in Galnago

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque oratory with a single nave with semicircular apse and facade in hut, quoted in 1347. On the façade traces of fifteenth-century paintings; in the basin the Christ Pantocrator with the Apostles, on the apse an Annunciation and below a Madonna del Latte attributed to Joannes Maria de Rumo; on the walls sacred images of the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries.

opening on request

  • Where Via Sempione, Oleggio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.oleggio.no.it
