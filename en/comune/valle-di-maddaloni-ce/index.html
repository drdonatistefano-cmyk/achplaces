What to see in Valle di Maddaloni

The village of the Ponti della Valle, the three-ordered viaduct of Carolino Aqueduct designed by Luigi Vanvitelli for the Palace of Caserta and part of the UNESCO site; around, the medieval castle mentioned in the Baronum Catalogus and the churches of San Pietro and the Annunziata.

Ponti della Valle (Acquedotto Carolino)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The large canal bridge of the Carolino Aqueduct, which Carlo di Borbone entrusted to Luigi Vanvitelli to feed the water games of the Reggia: he worked from 1753 for 17 years, with a cost of 622,424 ducats, for a pipe of about 38 km from the sources of Fizzo at the foot of the Taburno. Here the aqueduct emerges in a structure with three orders of arches 60 meters high and about 500 long, remembered by two epigraphs of 1759 at the level of the road. From the foot of the arches a road climbs along the Longano mountain between the ruins of the church of San Carlo and the mills built by Ferdinand IV in 1795. Free access every day; the upper walkway opens only during the initiatives of the Reggia.

  • Where SS265, Valle di Maddaloni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it

Sources: reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · whc.unesco.org

Chiesa di San Pietro Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Quoted in 1308 as Monasterium S. Petri de Valle was located outside the village in Votta; in 1601 Bishop Julius Santucci ordered its reconstruction in the village and the new church was inaugurated in 1621. The façade is simplified by a nineteenth-century restoration, inside with three naves with six side chapels and barrel vault, holy waterworks with shell in red marble of Levanto; the bell tower with square base on three orders marks the profile of the village.

  • Where Piazza San Pietro 99, Valle di Maddaloni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: comune.valledimaddaloni.ce.it · cultura.gov.it

Chiesa della SS. Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Already in 1514 the pastoral visit recorded two chaplains, one hospital and seven chapels or altars. Longitudinal plan with three naves with side chapels and in the transepts, vaulted on the central nave and cruising on the side; on the vault the fresco of the Annunciation painted in 1749 by Giovanni Cosenza, then the main altar in marble inlaid with two putti and a wooden choir with two sections decorated by two griffi.

  • Where Via Sannitica Commerciale 21, Valle di Maddaloni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: comune.valledimaddaloni.ce.it · cultura.gov.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rural chapel of Renaissance origin, patronage of the University of Valle, modified from 1840 with works for the Holy Camp; closed to worship in 1987 for injuries at a time, it was reopened after the work started in 1997. The original rustic style was enriched with frescoes, stuccoes and bas-reliefs, with an elegant facade and an imposing bell tower; in spring and autumn a procession brings the statue of the Madonna between the chapel and the parish.

  • Where Via Madonna delle Grazie 12, Valle di Maddaloni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: comune.valledimaddaloni.ce.it · cultura.gov.it
