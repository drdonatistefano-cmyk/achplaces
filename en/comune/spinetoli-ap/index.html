What to see in Spinetoli

The hilly village of the Tronto Valley is still surrounded by the walls of the medieval castle, with the sixteenth-century municipal palace and the civic tower, the eighteenth-century Sanctuary of the Madonna delle Grazie and the Museum of Sculpture born from the National Sculpture Contest; in the plain, near Pagliare del Tronto, the Oasis La Valle with the museum of peasant civilization and the gauze of the herons ashes.

Centro storico, mura e Palazzo comunale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village is still surrounded by the walls of the medieval castle; there is the sixteenth-century town hall flanked by a high civic tower. On the hills around, patrician villas with gardens built between the seventeenth and nineteenth centuries.

historical center, monuments and museums can be visited only partially according to the regional portal

Santuario della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Built in 1759 on the remains of a votive newsstand, it houses a painting on tuff considered miraculous with a former gold vote, a fresco of the 15th century Madonna and Child and a wooden statue of St. Rocco of the 15th century.

  • Where Spinetoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Museo della Scultura

Museums · Modern art

In the historic centre, it collects the works acquired by the municipality as prizes in the various editions of the National Sculpture Competition of Spinetoli; the exhibition space also hosts educational workshops on materials.

Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Set up in the restored farmhouse of the Environmental Education Centre Oasi La Valle, open in 2013 for schools and visitors: exhibits agricultural tools of local farmers and a late nineteenth century frame for linen sheets.

Sources: cultura.gov.it · letsmarche.it

Oasi La Valle

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Wet area along the Tronto, near Pagliare, with two ponds fed by the layer, riparian vegetation, poplars and centuries-old oaks; it nests the heron ash, symbol of the oasis, along with folagues, hens and germans. A path accessible with panels crosses the ponds on a wooden walkway; there is a wildlife recovery center, an eco park in recycled materials, fences with courtyard animals and Marche cattle and free bikes for bikes.

  • Where Via Piave 58, Pagliare del Tronto, Spinetoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it
