What to see in Crucoli

Borgo del Crotonese, known as the village of Sardella, with the historic center on the hill and the marina of Torretta sullo Ionio; it houses the Palopoli archaeological collection, the Pacchiana museum and an astronomical observatory on a plot confiscated from crime.

Museo archeologico Melissa Palopoli

Museums · Archaeological

It houses the archaeological collection of Ernesto Palopoli, about 2,000 pieces from the protohistory to the Byzantine age, largely from Krotoniatide: among the materials Brettii a clay doll with moving arts, between the Byzantine buckles belt and two box fibula with peacocks, one of which is the museum logo.

Spiaggia e lungomare di Torretta

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The beach takes its name from a sighting tower of the second half of the fifteenth century built to defend the coast; alternates free stretches and equipped shores. The Lungomare Kennedy, on the Ionian coast, offers sandy beaches, restaurants and a walk.

  • Where Lungomare Kennedy, Torretta di Crucoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.crucoli.kr.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.crucoli.kr.it

Osservatorio astronomico Borgo delle Stelle

Museums · Science and university

It rises on a ground taken from organized crime, today a garrison of legality and culture: under a dome of more than three meters a professional telescope for photographs and educational activities, with filters to observe the Sun safely.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Castello normanno in realtà virtuale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

A 3D reconstruction allows you to virtually visit the castle as it was in the fifteenth century, with a serious narrative game about medieval life and a web app with georeferenced and audioguide paths.
