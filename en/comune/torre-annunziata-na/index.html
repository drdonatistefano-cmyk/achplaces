What to see in Torre Annunziata

A town built on the remains of the ancient Oplontis, suburb of Pompeii buried by the eruption of 79 A.D. and part of the UNESCO site with the Villa of Poppea; born around the chapel of the Annunziata of the fourteenth century, it was famous for the Real Fabbrica d'Armi borbonica, the port and the pasta factories, and today offers a seafront overlooking Vesuvius and the thermal baths.

Scavi di Oplontis - Villa di Poppea

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Large otium villa on a cliff overlooking the sea, buried by the eruption of 79 A.D.: the oldest nucleus, of the first century B.C., revolves around a Tuscany atrium with paintings of II style, and in the middle of the first century A.D. the complex expanded eastward with a pool of 61 by 17 meters. The excavations, begun in the Bourbon era and resumed in the twentieth century, have exposed about 90 rooms on more than 10,000 square meters, with paintings of III style like Hercules in the garden of the Esperides and gardens rebuilt with Paleobotanic studies; an inscription on amphorae the league to the family of Poppea Sabina, wife of Nero. It is the only monument of the ancient Oplontis open to the public: the nearby Villa B of Lucius Crassius Tertius, center of wine and oil processing where in 1984 were found the "Ori di Oplontis" (Ori di Oplontis) is not open to visitors.

Basilica Pontificia della Madonna della Neve

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

The main religious building of the city: it was born from the chapel dedicated to the Annunziata Virgin, for which Charles of Anjou granted land in the fourteenth century, around which the village developed. It preserves the fourteenth-century icon of the Madonna with the Child fished at sea by fishermen off the rock of Rovigliano; every 5 August it is remembered the finding with the "dispute among the fishermen" and 22 October is celebrated the votive feast of the patroness. Open every day 8-12 and 16:30-20.

Lungomare Marconi e porto

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Long walk with palm trees and benches on the Gulf of Naples, dominated by Vesuvius; on clear days you can see Capri, Ischia and the Sorrento peninsula. The beach is free of black volcanic sand and establishments for a fee, and the nearby port, one of the most important in Campania, at sunset is animated by seagulls, boats and fishermen. The station Circumvesuviana Torre Annunziata Oplonti is about 10 minutes walk.

  • Where Via Guglielmo Marconi, Torre Annunziata
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.torreannunziata.na.it · italia.it

Terme Vesuviane Nunziante

Local life and traditions · Spas

The thermal plant is still in operation, already active in Roman times: the remains of the ancient complex are located under the current building. It takes its name from the Bourbon general Nunziante who restored it and rises between the rock of Oncino and the volcanic sandy beach of Marina del Sole.
