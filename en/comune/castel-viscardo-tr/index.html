What to see in Castel Viscardo

Orvietano country in the valley of Paglia, collected around the Castle of the Dukes of Montevecchio and known for its brick and terracotta kilns; in the territory the Etruscan necropolis of Caldane, the villages of Monterubiaglio and Viceno and the S.T.I.N.A. Park with the sulphur springs.

6 places to visit in Castel Viscardo, in the province of Terni (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello dei Duchi di Montevecchio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Medieval castle known as the Madonna's Castle, with irregular polygonal plan with large moat crossed by a bridge; baroque entrance with round arch, niches with statues and a clock of the eighteenth century. Local stone walls and framed windows in Bagnoregio stone. Fortress of the Monaldeschi until the sixteenth century, then feud of the Spada princes.

  • Entry To be checked

Chiesa di Maria Santissima Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of 1682: along the aisle four chapels with paintings by Carlo Maratta (1683-84), Wernle, German painter at the service of the Spada family, and Bernabei di Perugia.

Centro di documentazione delle terrecotte

Museums · Themed

In a former wash-house and barn of the 1930s, it is the first place to welcome the country: multimedia stations on the history and techniques of the handicraft production of terracotta and brick of Castel Viscardo; book guided tours to the kilns of the territory. Info: tel. 0763 361010.

Necropoli etrusca di Caldane

History and architecture · Archaeology

Part of the Archaeological and Environmental Park of Orvieto, in a wooded area north-west of the village in the valley of Paglia: small chamber graves dug into the rock, preceded by a dromos, mostly the second half of the 6th century BC, dug in 1986. The intact tombs have returned orvieto painted ceramics, buccheri and metal objects, including an Etruscan bronze mirror engraved with mythological scenes. Info: tel. 0763 361010.
