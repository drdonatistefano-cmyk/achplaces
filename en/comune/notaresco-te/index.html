What to see in Notaresco

The hilly town of the Vomano valley: in the hamlet of Guardia Vomano stands the church of San Clemente al Vomano, from the 11th century, with the oldest Norman ciborium in Abruzzo.

1 place to visit in Notaresco, in the province of Teramo (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Abbazia di San Clemente al Vomano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Church of the 11th century with three naves leading to the presbytery, where the Norman ciborium is preserved carved in the 12th century by Roberto di Ruggero, the oldest in Abruzzo.
