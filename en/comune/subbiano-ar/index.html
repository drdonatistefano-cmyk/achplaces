What to see in Subbiano

Porta del Casentino for those arriving from Arezzo, on the left bank of the Arno at the foot of the Alpe di Catenaia: the castle built before the 1000 at the control of the river, the thirteenth-century church of Santa Maria della Visitazione with the Documentation Centre of Archaeological Culture, and in the surroundings the neo-medieval castle of Valenzano and that of the Fioraia in Castelnuovo.

Castello e centro storico di Subbiano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The castle, built on a spur of rock before the Mille (XI century according to Discover Arezzo) to control the passage over the Arno, protected a bridge, an inhabited and a series of mills; remain the mighty crenellated mast and sections of the walls, which mirror themselves in the river and are seen from the walk along the embankment. Degradation and restoration of the Eight and twentieth centuries have changed its system. In the center the Palazzo del Podestà, Palazzo Ducci and Palazzo Subiani-Ducci.

  • Where Lungarno, Subbiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · discoverarezzo.com · visittuscany.com

Chiesa di Santa Maria della Visitazione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in the thirteenth century to replace the first church of the castle, considered too small, it was renovated from the end of the seventeenth century, when the loggia was added to the façade; the bell tower is from the nineteenth century and the current appearance dates back to the second post-war period. With the castle forms the core of the country.

  • Where Subbiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · discoverarezzo.com

Centro di documentazione della cultura archeologica

Museums · Archaeological

Housed in part of the church complex of the Visitation and part of the network of the Ecomuseum of Casentino since 2005-2007, it exhibits in two rooms finds of the territory from the VII century BC to the XIV century: Etruscan funeral rites, forms and uses of terracotta and history of the ancient disappeared churches of the area.

  • Where Subbiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it · ecomuseo.casentino.toscana.it

Castello di Valenzano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Castello documented from the 11th century, of the Ubertini and then divided with the monks of Camaldoli, submitted to Florence in 1386; from 1870 the Bastogi family had it rebuilt in neo-Gothic and neo-romantic style by an architect who had worked in Brolio. Rotate around a cloister with the small church of Santa Maria a Valenzano; today it is private and houses a hotel, with the stone mill still active and exhibitions in the cellars. You can visit the garden; in July the Valenzano Symphony festival.

  • Where Valenzano, Subbiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.subbiano.ar.it · discoverarezzo.com · visittuscany.com · italia.it

Castello della Fioraia, Castelnuovo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The hilly village of Castelnuovo, on the Arno at the border with the territory of Arezzo, is dominated by a large castle with a high tower, built after the 1000 and changed several times; it is named after the Fioraia family, who received it at the beginning of the fifteenth century as a prize for the services to the Florentine Republic.

  • Where Castelnuovo, Subbiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: discoverarezzo.com · comune.subbiano.ar.it · italia.it

Alpe di Catenaia e mulini di Falciano

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Relief that separates the Casentino from the Valtiberina, on the 1,100 meters with the summit of Monte Castello, rich in water and covered with oak, chestnut and beech woods; suitable for hikers of all levels, offers views from the top. On the ascent, the village of Falciano, gate of the Alpe, preserves two ancient mills still in operation.

  • Where Falciano, Subbiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · comune.subbiano.ar.it · discoverarezzo.com · italia.it
