What to see in Senigallia

Seaside town on the Adriatic known for its velvet beach: the Round Sea on the sand, the Roveresca Rocca dei della Rovere and a historic centre with the Annunario Forum and the Herculanean Porticos.

7 places to visit in Senigallia, in the province of Ancona (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca Roveresca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built between 1476 and 1482 for Giovanni della Rovere, lord of Senigallia and nephew of Pope Sixtus IV: Luciano Laurana designed the residential part and Baccio Pontelli completed the defensive structure. It is a plain fortress with quadrangular plan with cylindrical towers at the corners, designed for firearms.

  • Where Senigallia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
