What to see in Pandino

Country of the Cremasca plain dominated by the Visconteo Castle, built around 1355 by Bernabò Visconti and Regina della Scala as a hunting residence; in the territory the rural village of Gradella, among the most beautiful villages in Italy, the sanctuary of the Madonna del Riposo with its secular hornbeam and the fountains of the Tormo Park.

Castello Visconteo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Erected around 1355 by Bernabò Visconti, lord of Milan, and Queen of the Scala as a hunting residence and convivial encounters, she is among the best examples of fortified Visconti architecture of the fourteenth century. Square plan with four corner towers around a large courtyard, arcades on the ground floor and loggias on the upper floor; a recent restoration brought to light the original painted decorations with floral, geometric and polychrome motifs. Today it is the municipality and hosts the library in the ancient stables, exhibitions and events; open Friday morning, Saturday and Sunday morning and afternoon.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Borgo di Gradella

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Small agricultural village about one kilometer from Pandino, inserted in 2005 among the most beautiful villages in Italy: yellow houses and red bricks, farmhouses with communicating courtyards, arcades and loggias along the fronts, surrounded by fields; the recovery respected traditional construction techniques. The parish church of the Holy Trinity and San Bassiano, dating back to 1895; just outside, Villa Maggi, dating back to the 17th century, renovated between the 19th and 20th centuries.

  • Where Gradella, Pandino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · turismocremona.it · in-lombardia.it

Chiesa di Santa Marta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built probably in the second half of the fifteenth century, it preserves frescoes from the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries.

Santuario della Madonna del Riposo e Carpino monumentale

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Also known as the Shrine of Thomas, it recalls the apparition of Our Lady in 1432 to a young man, Thomas, to dissuade him from a murder; the fifteenth-century building, partly recovered, was rebuilt in the nineties. Next to the oldest part there is a centuries-old white hornbeam about 7 meters high with trunk of almost 3 meters of circumference, rare dimensions for the species, freely visitable.

  • Where Via Borgo Roldi, Pandino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · turismocremona.it

Parco del fiume Tormo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

A plain park created to safeguard the network of fountains and springs of the Tormo, which was born from the Roggia Murata in Arzago d'Adda and flows into the Adda in Abbadia Cerreto, in a landscape marked by the ancient morphological escarpments of the river Adda.

  • Where Pandino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismocremona.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · turismocremona.it
