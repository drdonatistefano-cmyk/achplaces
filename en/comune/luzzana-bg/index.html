What to see in Luzzana

Small town of the Val Cavallina on the right bank of the Cherio, about 18 km from Bergamo, gathered around the Castle Giovanelli that houses the Museum of Contemporary Art - Donazione Meli; a popular museum leads to the works of Alberto Meli in the village, to the bas-relief of the Giant carved in the rock by Joshua Meli and, going up, to the sanctuary of St. Anthony and the Prati Alti.

Museo d'arte contemporanea di Luzzana - Donazione Meli

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

Opened in 2003 in Castello Giovanelli, bought by the Municipality in 1989, it contains more than 220 works donated by the sculptor Alberto Meli and his wife, the painter Ester Gaini Meli. The route goes from the neoclassical sculptures of Giosuè Meli to the dada works of Arp, Richter, Probst and Valenti, linked with the work of Alberto Meli by artistic coordinator Fernando Noris; it continues outdoors with the diffused museum and has an extension in the parish church. Open Wednesday 14-18.30, Saturday 9-12.30 and Sunday 15-18; the museum indicates that visits are made by reservation (info@museoluzzana.it), guides for 20 € up to 5 people.

  • Where Via Castello 73, Luzzana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museoluzzana.it

Sources: museoluzzana.it · visitbergamo.net

Castello Giovanelli e borgo medievale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

The castle of Luzzana, dating from the commune between the thirteenth and sixteenth centuries, was part of a defensive network that included the nearby Castle of Terzo, called the "key of the Valley." It is the central element of the medieval village; purchased by the municipality in 1989, it was recovered as the site of the museum with the installation of Carlo Pinessi and architects Alberto Trussardi and Luciano Doneda.

  • Where Via Castello, Luzzana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: comune.luzzana.bg.it · museoluzzana.it · visitbergamo.net

Parco del Gigante

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The area that hosts the Giant, also known as the Giant supporting the mountain, is a relief of about 4 meters for almost 5 sculpted in a large block of living rock, the Moon's Stone, by the Luzzane sculptor Giosuè Meli, who completed it in 1841 at the age of 25. It is located on the steep slope of the Valle dell'Acqua, in the Costa district, near the river Bragazzo; the posture of the figure recalls the iconography of Pietà.

  • Where Contrada Costa, Valle dell'Acqua, Luzzana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: museoluzzana.it · visitbergamo.net

Itinerario dei Prati Alti e santuario di Sant'Antonio

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of about 2 hours and 476 m of difference in altitude, without difficulty, from Piazza Giosuè Meli (where there is the Monument to the life of Alberto Meli): via Belvedere with Villa Pasinetti, the Cascine Benti and Moioli and the three deodara cedars of 1939 go up to the sanctuary of Sant'Antonio (550 m), documented from 1780, with a bronze of Sant'Antonio abbot and one of Pope John XXIII of Meli and a picnic area with fountain. Higher up the Prati Alti (780 m) with a large roar and the small church of the Madonna del Rosario of 1790, then the Pizzo Casgnola (791 m) with views of the Prealpi Orobie and plain; return to the Pizzo Boscaglia and Costa.

  • Where Partenza da piazza Giosuè Meli, Luzzana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: museoluzzana.it · visitbergamo.net
