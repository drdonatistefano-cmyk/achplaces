What to see in Palena

Borgo della Majella on the high Aventine, dominated by the 11th century ducal castle that houses the Geopaleontological Museum; in the former convent of Sant'Antonio the Museum of the Marsican bear with the fauna area, and under Mount Porrara the hermitage of the Madonna dell'Altare.

Castello ducale e Museo geopaleontologico Alto Aventino

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

The castle, built around 1100 on a rocky spur in the upper part of the village, is on three levels and houses a museum pole: the Geopaleontological Museum with Abruzzo fossils, including the Prolagus of the collection Di Carlo, many from the geosite of Capo di Fiume; the Pinacoteca Casa degli Artisti e degli Men Illustri di Palena and the room dedicated to the critic and screenwriter Ettore Maria Margadonna.

  • Where Castello ducale, Palena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it · touringclub.it

Museo dell'orso marsicano MOM

Museums · Themed

Managed by the Majella Park in a wing of the former convent of Sant'Antonio, active since 2005: five sections with panels, dioramas and a night forest rebuilt on biology, habits, myth and conservation of the bear. From June to August open every day 10-13 and 17-20; from September to May Saturday 16-19 and Sunday 10-13.

  • Where Località Sant'Antonio, Palena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Area faunistica dell'orso

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 2015 in the locality Colleveduta, about 800 m of altitude on 10.770 m2 of fir forest, bushes, fruit trees and pastures; you can visit with a guide along a dirt track and hosts three brown bears born in captivity. Visits by reservation from March to November (season hours), closed in December and January for hibernation.

  • Where Località Colleveduta, Palena (ritrovo al Museo dell'orso)
  • Entry €10, reduced €5, free up to 5 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcomajella.it · italia.it

Teatro Aventino Ettore Maria Margadonna

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built in 1843, 96 seats with audiences, two orders of stages and loggia; it is among the smallest theatres in Europe.

  • Where Centro storico, Palena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Quartiere medievale e Fontana municipale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Next to the castle a network of alleys, stairs and stone portals; the municipal fountain, already existing in the Middle Ages and enlarged in 1812 under Murat, has five cinnamons with masks. In the center also the galleries of 1826, the only remaining of the many on the Aventine, used for the processing of wool cloths.

  • Where Centro storico, Palena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa della Madonna della Neve

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A Greek cross church consecrated in 1436, with a wooden statue of the Madonna of the sixteenth century, an organ of the eighteenth century, a painting by Oreste Recchione and a rose window.

  • Where Palena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Eremo della Madonna dell'Altare

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 1,272 m below Monte Porrara, it dates back to the fourteenth century, built by the Celestines, probably where Fr.Pietro Angelerio, the future Pope Celestine V, lived for three years.

  • Where Monte Porrara, Palena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Sorgenti dell'Aventino e geosito di Capo di Fiume

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

The river Aventino was born in Capo di Fiume, a geosite from which many fossils of the museum come, crosses the village and after about 45 km flows into the Sangro; the Belvedere Mascij overlooks one of its waterfalls.

  • Where Capo di Fiume, Palena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Monte Porrara

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Climbing Monte Porrara offers a magnificent view; Palena is known as the village of the orchids, with 64 species reported in the municipal territory.

  • Where Monte Porrara, Palena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it
