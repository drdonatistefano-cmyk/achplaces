What to see in Santa Croce del Sannio

Small center of Sannio, called the village of the palaces, with a walled nucleus around the baronial palace, noble palaces houses including the birthplace of the Enlightenment Giuseppe Maria Galanti, the churches of Santa Croce and Santa Maria Assunta, and the passage of the Royal Tratturo Pescasseroli-Candela.

5 places to visit in Santa Croce del Sannio, in the province of Benevento (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e case palaziate

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient nucleus is enclosed by walls with three doors around the baronial palace, born from a first fortress and today seat of the Town Hall. The village is called "the village of the palaces": the most famous palaces are those of Galanti, Capozzi-Giovene, De Nigris-Gioia, De Mariarosa, Bochicchio-De Maria, Gioia-Bernando and Vitelli.

  • Where Corso Giuseppe Maria Galanti, Santa Croce del Sannio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Palazzo Galanti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Birthplace of Giuseppe Maria Galanti (1743-1806), economist and historian illuminist, with environments, gardens and cellars.

Chiesa di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Church overlooking the village, together with a Franciscan monastery; the sources refer to an older temple mentioned in graduations of the eighth century.

  • Where Santa Croce del Sannio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page admin-istituzionale.touringclub.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Mother church built in 1761, with facade in limestone and Latin cross plan; it houses a marble altar in polychrome tarsies and a wooden statue of Paolo Saverio di Zinno.

  • Where Santa Croce del Sannio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page admin-istituzionale.touringclub.it

Sources: admin-istituzionale.touringclub.it

Regio Tratturo Pescasseroli-Candela

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Ancient via della trashumanza 211 km long, that crosses 4 regions, 6 provinces and 41 municipalities connecting the Abruzzo montano to the Tavoliere Apulia; it is subject to archaeological constraint, and transhumance is registered since 2019 in the UNESCO List of the Cultural Heritage Immaterial. From the village you can reach Campo del Monaco, about 5 km.

  • Where Campo del Monaco, Santa Croce del Sannio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page admin-istituzionale.touringclub.it

Sources: admin-istituzionale.touringclub.it
