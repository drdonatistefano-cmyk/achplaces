What to see in Sciacca

Spa and ceramics on the coast of Agrigento: the vaporous caves of Monte Kronio, the Steripinto Palace with a diamond tip, the Norman Cathedral and the enchanted castle of carved heads.

7 places to visit in Sciacca, in the province of Agrigento (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Sciacca in the guide with map and filters

Monte Kronio e Stufe di San Calogero

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

On the mountain north of the city, a complex of karst caves from which come hot vapors, the Stufe di San Calogero still exploited; the Antro di Daedalo, described by Diodoro Siculo, is the oldest thermal structure of Sciacca.

  • Where Monte Kronio, Sciacca
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.buongiornoceramica.it

Sources: regione.sicilia.it · buongiornoceramica.it

Antiquarium di Monte Kronio

Museums · Archaeological

On the top floor of the spa complex at the top of the mountain, it exhibits prehistoric and historical finds of the excavations conducted in the caves between the sixties and eighties.

  • Where Monte Kronio, Sciacca
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.buongiornoceramica.it

Sources: regione.sicilia.it · buongiornoceramica.it

Castello Incantato

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Viewpoint

In an olive grove, hundreds of faces carved in the rock and trees by Filippo Bentivegna (1888-1967), who lived there in hermitage for about half a century; it is among the most visited sites in the province after the Valley of the Temples.

  • Where Via Filippo Bentivegna 16, Sciacca
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.buongiornoceramica.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it:443 · buongiornoceramica.it

Palazzo Steripinto

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Palace of 1501 with rustic diamond tip, double-fores and blackbird in Sicilian-Catalan style.

  • Where Sciacca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.buongiornoceramica.it

Sources: buongiornoceramica.it

Duomo di Sciacca

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry
