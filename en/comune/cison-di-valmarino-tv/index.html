What to see in Cison di Valmarino

Medieval village at the foot of the Treviso Alps, home to the Brandolini feud, dominated by CastelBrando; between Piazza Roma, the Loggia and the Via dell'Acqua, with the Forest of the Penne Mozze and Rolle among the hills of the UNESCO World Heritage Prosecco.

8 places to visit in Cison di Valmarino, in the province of Treviso (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

CastelBrando

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Fortified complex on a spur above the village, the medieval "Castrum Costae" with about two thousand years of history from Roman finds to medieval walls; Antonio Maria Brandolini (died 1530) added the Venetian façade. Today it is a hotel with museum areas, reachable also by funicular; guided tours (1 hour or 1 hour and 15) touch the sections "CastelBrando in armi" and "Potere e giustizia" (Power and justice) and, in the path "B," the church of San Martino. Visits on Sundays, on other days by reservation.

  • Where CastelBrando, Cison di Valmarino
  • Entry €13 (path A), €15 (path B)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: castelbrando.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Piazza Roma e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The central square brings together the late Baroque parish church, the Loggia theatre and the town hall; the Brandolini buildings are recognized by the red darks all the same, and the seventeenth-century Marian Houses host the exhibition of the Craftsmanship in August.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Cison di Valmarino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Bosco delle Penne Mozze

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Memorial park inaugurated in October 1972, on about 16,000 square meters, with over 2,400 wrought iron stems dedicated to the fallen alpine of the province of Treviso and beyond; since September 1998 every evening it plays a bell in their memory.

  • Where Valle del Rujo, Cison di Valmarino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: collineconeglianovaldobbiadene.it · italia.it

Via dell'Acqua e Valle del Rujo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

From the centre the path follows the Rujo stream towards the Valley of San Daniele, passing next to ancient mills; the route ring from Piazza Roma (9,1 km, about 3 hours and 30, difference in altitude 501 m, difficulty E) touches the churches of San Silvestro and San Gaetano and the Bosco delle Penne Mozze and goes back along the Via dell'Acqua.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Cison di Valmarino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · collineconeglianovaldobbiadene.it

Rolle

History and architecture · Villages · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fraction between vineyards and wooded hills, with a small waterfall that descends from a rock wall, in the area of the Prosecco hills of Conegliano and Valdobbiadene UNESCO World Heritage.

  • Where Rolle, Cison di Valmarino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · collineconeglianovaldobbiadene.it

Strada dei 100 giorni e Passo San Boldo

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Mountain road built by the Austrian army in 1918, challenging and panoramic, with hairpin bends in the gallery (five galleries) going up to the San Boldo Pass.

  • Where Tovena, Cison di Valmarino
  • Entry Free
