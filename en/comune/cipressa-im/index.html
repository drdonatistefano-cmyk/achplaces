What to see in Cipressa

Municipality of the Riviera dei Fiori with the hilly village of Lingueglietta, among the most beautiful villages in Italy, feud of the Lengue: turned caruggi, the civic loggia and the church of San Pietro transformed into a fortress.

5 places to visit in Cipressa, in the province of Imperia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Cipressa in the guide with map and filters

Lingueglietta

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village among the olive trees with longitudinal caruggi connected by ramps and ladders under vaults and medieval arches; the civic loggia preserves the stone measurements for oil, wine and wheat.

  • Where Lingueglietta, Cipressa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Late-Doroma church with restored apse, military bulwark and place of faith.

  • Where Lingueglietta, Cipressa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale della Natività di Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Stone core of the thirteenth century, portico and portal in marble of the seventeenth century; interior with three naves with ancient frescoes.

  • Where Lingueglietta, Cipressa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint
