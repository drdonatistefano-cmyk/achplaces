What to see in Mogliano

Borgo on the ridge between the valleys of Cremone and Ete Morto, overlooking the Sibillini, known for the tradition of wicker weaving; it houses the altarpiece of Lorenzo Lotto della Madonna in glory with the saints (1548), the baroque staircase of San Gregorio Magno, the remains of the 14th-century fortress and the Apollo Theatre of 1844.

MASM Museo di Arte Sacra e pala di Lorenzo Lotto

Museums · Old masters

Parish Museum inaugurated in 1979 above the sacristy of Santa Maria di Piazza, in the ancient oratory of Our Lady of Mercy in 1420, with furnishings, statues and objects of the churches and confraternities of Mogliano. The altarpiece of Lorenzo Lotto with Our Lady in glory and Saints John the Baptist, Antonio da Padova, Maria Maddalena and Giuseppe (oil on canvas, 330 x 215 cm), commissioned in 1547 by Mayor Jacomo Boninfante for 130 gold shields and delivered in 1548 by the pupil During Nobili.

Palazzo Forti e Museo civico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palazzo di fine Cinquecento, renovated in the eighteenth century, built as an autonomous residence of the Forti family and today town hall, with three floors above the road and two below. Rooms with coffered ceilings and eighteenth-century decorations, works from the seventeenth century, remains of frescoes from the fifteenth to the eighteenth century and, in the library, picen and Roman finds.

the Municipality indicates temporary closure for earthquake damage

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di San Gregorio Magno

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish was born outside the castle moat and rebuilt in the eighteenth century, when the large Baroque staircase was added. It preserves a canvas by Durante Nobili da Caldarola, pupil of Lotto, dated 1546, and a painting by G.B. Fagiani with the view of Mogliano in the eighteenth century; reopened after a recent restoration.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di Santa Colomba

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Coffered ceiling painted by G.B. Fagiani between 1750 and 1752 and, behind the altar, a table of Durante Nobili of 1554 with the Madonna enthroned with the Child and saints; cloister with frescoed lunettes and a chapel with the reproduction of the Holy Sepulchre of Jerusalem.

Monastero di San Giuseppe e Chiesa dei SS. Grisogono e Benedetto

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Monastery built in 1630 by the Benedictines of Cassino and from 1855 of the nuns of San Giuseppe di Torino, with painted doors with saints and landscapes in the cloistered rooms. The adjoining church, Rococo, has painted doors and windows, coffered ceiling and organ.

Santuario del SS. Crocifisso

It preserves a fresco of the late fifteenth century with the crucified Christ that comes out of the tomb; after a miraculous event in 1809 the church was rebuilt in neoclassical forms according to the design of G. Lucatelli is declared shrine by the viceroy of Italy Eugenio Beauharnais.

Rocca medievale e Santa Maria del Suffragio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Of the fortress erected by Gentile da Mogliano in the fourteenth century remain the bastions, excellent panoramic point; inside the deconsecrated church of Santa Maria del Suffragio (1698). From the fortress you can see the former hospital of San Michele (1782) and the Autopalace liberty, a former garage restored as an exhibition space.

  • Where Centro storico, Mogliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.mogliano.mc.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Complesso di Santa Maria da Piedi e porta di Levante

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

It was inserted in the walls next to the gate of Levante and takes its name from "Santa Maria a pede castri": Gothic arch with iron hinges of the ancient gates and five collections of coats of arms removed by Napoleon's soldiers after the battle of Tolentino (1797).

Teatro Apollo

History and architecture · Historic theatres

The 19th-century theatre opened in 1844, with 38 stages on three orders, restored; in the proscenium two pillars in scalola of the mogliano Luca Seri.

Chiesa del SS. Crocifisso d'Ete

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Country church at the crossroads for Montegiorgio and Francavilla d'Ete, lambita dall'Ete Morto; the first stone was placed on November 11, 1579 by the bishop of Fermo and the management entrusted to the brotherhood of the SS. Sacrament with bubble of Gregory XIII.
