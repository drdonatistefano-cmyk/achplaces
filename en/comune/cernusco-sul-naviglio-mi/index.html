What to see in Cernusco sul Naviglio

Crossed from east to west by the Naviglio della Martesana, Cernusco sul Naviglio preserves the eighteenth-century Villa Alari by Giovanni Ruggeri, the Villa Greppi seat of the Town Hall and Villa Uboldo, and a pedestrian historical centre with a path dedicated to silk.

Villa Alari Visconti di Saliceto

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa of U delight designed by Giovanni Ruggeri for Giacinto Alari, with works from 1702 to about 1721 and chapel of St. Teresa blessed in 1725; garden towards the Naviglio. In 1772-1776 the governor of Lombardy resided there, Archduke Ferdinand of Habsburg, with Beatrice d'Este; since 1831 of the Visconti of Saliceto, in 1948 he passed to the Fatebenefratelli and has been public since 2007. Villa Biancani Greppi (Municipio)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The residence of the early eighteenth century was then enlarged, with a H-shaped plan with courtyard and garden, restored porticos and eighteenth-century molded windows; once attributed to Giuseppe Piermarini, attribution today considered wrong. Renovated, since 1978 it is the seat of the Municipality and can be visited by reservation. It overlooks Piazza Unità d'Italia.

  • Where Via Tizzoni 2, Cernusco sul Naviglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Villa Uboldo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Complex started in 1808 on a project by Carillo Rougier for the collector Ambrogio Uboldo, who conceived it as a museum house; the collections were largely dispersed since 1867 to support the shelter for elderly people he wanted, opened in 1877. The south façade recalls Piermarini and Prunek; on the ground floor three rooms with neoclassical frescoes restored in 1971 and 2014. The English garden is today a public park.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Centro storico e percorso della seta

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Among the first pedestrian areas of Milan's hinterland (eighties), the center has a path dedicated to silk: Piazza Gavazzi, where the spinning mill stood, with the statue of a baco; Piazza Repubblica with the sculpture of Nado Canuti on the metamorphosis of the baco; Piazza Matteotti with the fountain and a mulberry of about 130 years; Piazza Padre Giuliani with the row of mulberry trees; Piazza Unità d'Italia with the "Tuba Mirum" by Arman.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Cernusco sul Naviglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.cernuscosulnaviglio.mi.it · in-lombardia.it

Naviglio della Martesana

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

The Naviglio Piccolo, built at the end of the fifteenth century with the contribution of Leonardo da Vinci, was born from Adda and crosses Cernusco from east to west; for centuries it was the main economic road in the area. The lifts, protected by the Naviglio Park, are equipped for about two kilometers with greenery, paths and bike path, part of a municipal network of over 70 km of bikes.

  • Where Alzaia del Naviglio Martesana, Cernusco sul Naviglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · opencms10.cittametropolitana.mi.it

Santuario di Santa Maria Addolorata

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Documented already in the IX century and substantially transformed since 1642, it rises near the Naviglio; in 1998 a new bronze portal and a new altar were placed.
