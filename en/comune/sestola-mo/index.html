What to see in Sestola

3 places to visit in Sestola, in the province of Modena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Sestola e Musei della Rocca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The fortress extended on a rock spur, rebuilt in 1570 with the Museum of Montanara Civilization and the Museum of Mechanical Musical Instruments.

  • Where Via dei Faggiotti, Sestola
  • Entry To be checked: admission to museums at extra charge
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Lago della Ninfa e Monte Cimone

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lake is 1500 meters high and the highest peak of the northern Apennines (2165 m), from which on clear days you can see the Alps, the Adriatic and the Tyrrhenian.

  • Where Monte Cimone, Sestola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Giardino Botanico Alpino Esperia

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

At the Passo del Lupo, over two hundred species of Apennine flora and alpine, Himalayan and Arctic plants along paths among the habitats.

  • Where Passo del Lupo, Sestola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
