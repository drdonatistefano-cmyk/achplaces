What to see in Niscemi

Center on the plateau overlooking the plain of Gela, rebuilt in the eighteenth century after the earthquake of 1693, with the Baroque churches of Santa Maria d'Itria and Addolorata, the Sanctuary of Maria SS. del Bosco, the Belvedere on the plain and the Reserve of the Sughereta, the last great relic of corket of central-southern Sicily.

Riserva naturale orientata Sughereta di Niscemi

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Reserve of 2.939 hectares established in 1997, about 330 meters in the southern part of the plateau: it protects the most important relic of cork mixed with holm oak of central Sicily, which with the Bosco of Santo Pietro di Caltagirone is what remains of the largest cork grove of central-southern Sicily. Between corks, holm oaks, thorny oaks, cysts, heathers, myrtle and arbutus live wild cat and istrice, buzzard, ghetto, eschulapius columbra and leopardin columbra.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page orbs.regione.sicilia.it

Belvedere

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Baroque panoramic terrace of the early nineteenth century, once known as Tunnu, with wrought iron railings and benches, overlooking the plain of Gela and the Maroglio valley; below opens the avenue with steps dedicated to Angelo D'Arrigo.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page orbs.regione.sicilia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria d'Itria e Palazzo di Città

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

On the main square the church of Santa Maria d'Itria, rebuilt from 1742 after the earthquake of 1693 on the project of the Messinese Giuseppe La Rosa, with interiors decorated in 1863-1864 and facade remained unfinished at the top; in front of the neoclassical Palazzo di Città, built between 1870 and 1882.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page orbs.regione.sicilia.it

Santuario di Maria SS. del Bosco

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Built between 1749 and 1758 by Silvestro Gugliara, it conserves the stones of the discovery of 1599 and a copy of the painting of Our Lady, whose original was lost in a fire of 1769; four paintings of 1927 and a painting of 1929 are by Giuseppe Barone. In the crypt the Chapel of Holy Water, baptistery since 1998.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page orbs.regione.sicilia.it
