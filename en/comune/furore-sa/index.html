What to see in Furore

Village of the Amalfi Coast about 600 meters, among terraces of olive trees, vineyards and lemons, known for the Fiordo with its fishing village and for the more than one hundred ' artist walls that make it a painted village.

5 places to visit in Furore, in the province of Salerno (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Fiordo di Furore

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

A split in the rock frames a small wedge beach, joined above by a bridge between the two walls; the fishing village with the monazzens, ancient tool stores, has been restored. It was set of the film L'Amore by Roberto Rossellini with Anna Magnani, and on the first Sundays of July it hosts dives from cliffs from almost 30 meters.

Low parking: the source recommends leaving the car in Praiano (2 km on foot) or using the Amalfi-Agerola bus with stop on the bridge

  • Where Via Anna Magnani, Furore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Open-air art gallery with more than 100 art walls between murals and sculptures, which make Furore a painted village.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiese di San Giacomo, Sant'Elia, San Michele e Santa Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage

Churches with domes covered with majolica; in the church of San Giacomo have been recently discovered frescoes of a cycle attributable to the Giottesca school.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Mulini e cartiere della valle

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage

In the valley around the village there are two mills for flour and two paper mills, examples of industrial architecture that exploited the power of water, near the fishing village.

  • Where Valle del Fiordo, Furore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
