What to see in Pievepelago

Centro dell'alto Frignano famous for the Corpus Domini infiorata along Via Tamburù, with the hamlet of Roccapelago, where the castle of Obizzo da Montegarullo preserves the mummies discovered in 2011, and Lake Santo, the largest lake of the Apennines.

Castello di Roccapelago e museo «Sulle orme di Obizzo da Montegarullo»

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On a spur over 1000 m on the Pelago basin, it was of the Longobards and Matilde of Canossa and from 1240 of the Montegarullo; Obizzo made it the Guelph fortress of Frignano until he was defeated in 1408. The arms room became parish church in 1586: in its crypt in 2011 more than 300 bodies were discovered buried between the Five and the eighteenth century, almost one hundred mummified, exhibited with the accessories in the museum of the fortress. In the parish of São Paulo a golden tabernacle of 1603 and a Madonna of the Rosary of G.B. Bertusio (1627); at the foot of the Captain's House.

  • Where Salita della Chiesa 5, Roccapelago, Pievepelago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Centro storico e via Tamburù

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient Tamburù street covers every year the flower carpets of the Corpus Domini Infiorata, born in 1927; the symbol of the village is the fountain of Piazza Ricci surmounted by an obelisk.

  • Where Via Tamburù e piazza Ricci, Pievepelago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it

Chiesa plebana di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt in 1868, with classical façade and three naves inside: it houses the Assunta altarpiece by Carlo Rizzi, a Santa Filomena by Adeodato Malatesta and a San Luigi Gonzaga by Luigi Asioli. Next to it, the oratory of San Luigi and the Oratory of the Whites.

  • Where Pievepelago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it

Ponte della Fola

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

A kilometer from the village on the stream Scoltenna, stone bridge with donkey back with asymmetrical arches, indicated as the only stone bridge with two arches of Emilia.

  • Where Torrente Scoltenna, Pievepelago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it

Lago Santo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 1501 m, with a perimeter of 1,5 km, deep about 20 m and an area of about 58,000 m2, it is the largest of the Apennine lakes; it is reached by car and on foot and is starting point towards Mount Giovo (1991 m), which overlooks it with an overhang of 500 m, and the Rondinaio (1964 m).

  • Where Lago Santo, Pievepelago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: comune.pievepelago.mo.it · visitmodena.it

Lago Baccio e Lago Turchino

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The small Lake Baccio, of glacial origin at 1554 m, can be reached in half an hour from Lake Santo under Mount Rondanio (path CAI 523); the circular Lake Turchino, at 1600 m, is three hours from the parking lot of Lake Santo.

  • Where Lago Santo, Pievepelago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: comune.pievepelago.mo.it · visitmodena.it

Olmo secolare di Casa Mordini

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Mountain elm of about 600 years, described as the oldest in Italy, with next to the trunk a Marian chapel and near a spring.

Sant'Andrea Pelago e le capanne celtiche

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village in the Frignano Park with Celtic huts with a staircase roof, medieval remains of the Torrione, the oratory of San Rocco, the fountain of Baronio and other fountains; in the surrounding area you can walk through the eighteenth-century Via Vandelli. One of the best preserved huts is in Roncacci - Casoni.

  • Where Sant'Andrea Pelago, Pievepelago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint
