What to see in Clusone

The main centre of Val Seriana, just over 30 km from Bergamo, with a historic centre on several levels: in Piazza dell'Orologio the planetary clock of Pietro Fanzago (1583), in front of the Basilica dell'Assunta the Oratory of the Disciplines with the Triumph and the Dance of Death (1485), and the MAT in Palazzo Marinoni Barca.

Oratorio dei Disciplini e Danza Macabra

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the fourteenth century by the Confraternita dei Disciplini Bianchi, enlarged in the fifteenth century and dedicated to Saint Bernardino in 1452. On the east façade the frescoes of 1485 by Giacomo Borlone de Buschis: at the top the Triumph of Death with the encounter of the three living and the three dead, at the center the Dance Macabra with people of every class led by skeletons. Inside the Stories of Jesus in 42 squares on three bands (31 visible) and a Crucifixion over the arch of the presbytery.

  • Where Via Pier Antonio Brasi 14, Clusone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Piazza dell'Orologio e Orologio planetario Fanzago

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

On the tower of the Palazzo Comunale, the planetary clock completed in 1583 by Pietro Fanzago: a single hand, turning counterclockwise on a 24-hour dial, and three rotating discs indicate hours, days, months, zodiac signs, duration of day and night and moon phases. It has been charged by hand every day for over 400 years.

  • Where Piazza dell'Orologio, Clusone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Sources: valseriana.eu · inviaggio.touringclub.it

Palazzo Comunale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Two-storey building, with six round arches on the ground floor, windows on the upper floor and the high clock tower next to it; on the façade the frescoed coats of arms of the families who ruled the city, a walled Gothic loggia and a fresco of the Madonna and Child between the Saints Sigismondo and Christopher.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Andrea 1, Clusone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Sources: italia.it · valseriana.eu

Basilica di Santa Maria Assunta e San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Built between 1688 and 1698 and Basilica minor since 1961, monumental single nave with eight altars and entrance staircase: the main altar is Andrea Fantoni, the altarpiece of the Assumption of Sebastiano Ricci, other paintings by Carpinoni, Vicentino and Querena. The Basilica Museum has three oratories and eight rooms.

  • Where Di fronte all'Oratorio dei Disciplini, Clusone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Sources: valseriana.eu · inviaggio.touringclub.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

MAT Museo Arte Tempo e Parco Nastro Azzurro

Museums · Old masters · Free entry

Opened in 2005 in the seventeenth-century Palazzo Marinoni Barca with frescoed ceilings: paintings, drawings, sculptures and bas-reliefs from legacies of families included, the Suardo collection with works from the sixteenth to the twentieth century (including Giovan Paolo Cavagna and Palma il Giovane), two sculptures by Giacomo Manzù and the Gorla collection of mechanisms of tower clocks. In the garden three giant sequoias of about 30 meters brought from America at the end of the nineteenth century. Open Friday 15.30-18.30, Saturday and Sunday 10-12 and 15.30-18.30.

  • Where Via Clara Maffei 3, Clusone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museoartetempo.it

Sources: museoartetempo.it · comune.clusone.bg.it · valseriana.eu

Chiesa dei Santi Defendente e Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The extension of an older oratory, renovated in 1477 after a pestilence, with a four-column portico of 1575; in 1630 it was lazzaretto. A single nave, with mostly votive frescoes and a 1634 altarpiece with the Transit of St. Joseph; recently restored after years of closure.

  • Where Via San Defendente, Clusone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: italia.it · valseriana.eu

Centro storico e Palazzo Fogaccia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historic centre, on several levels, alternates shops, bars and palaces with Venetian frescoes; Palazzo Fogaccia was built between 1693 and 1709 on a project by Giovan Battista Quadrio, who worked at the Duomo in Milan. Monday is the market.

  • Entry Free

Chiesa della Santissima Trinità sul Monte Crosio

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Church built between 1590 and 1595 on top of the hill Crosio, in 1630 quarantine place for soldiers returning from the plague; it is reached on foot with a path from Gusmini Avenue and offers a view of Clusone and the whole plateau. Visitable only from the outside.

  • Where Monte Crosio, Clusone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Sources: comune.clusone.bg.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · valseriana.eu

Oratorio montano di San Lucio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small church at 1,027 meters on Mount Pianone, built before 1529 by the herdsmen who spent most of the year on the plateau, dedicated to St. Lucius, patron saint of shepherds, herdsmen and housewives; view on the whole plateau. Interior open only on request.

  • Where Via San Lucio 130, Clusone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · valseriana.eu
