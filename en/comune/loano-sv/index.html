What to see in Loano

Seaside town of the Riviera di Ponente, imperial fief of the Dorias, with a historic center among the best preserved of Liguria: Palazzo Doria (seat of the Municipality, with a mosaic of the third century), the Castle of the Dorias, the Convent of Monte Carmelo, the Pentagonal Tower and the Decagonal Cathedral of San Giovanni Battista; long sandy beaches Blue Flag and one of the largest ports of the Ponente.

10 places to visit in Loano, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo Doria

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between 1574 and 1578, perhaps on a project by Galeazzo Alessi, today it is the seat of the Town Hall and preserves a mosaic floor of the third century; in 1685-1687 Taddeo Carlone built two fountains for the garden. It is connected by a balcony to the Pentagonal Tower.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Castello dei Doria (Borgo Castello)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It was founded in the 12th century as a fortified outpost of the bishops of Albenga, it was of the Fieschi from 1505 to 1547 and between the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries took the form of a Renaissance palace; in 1736 it passed to the Kingdom of Sardinia.

Duomo di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church, called the Cathedral of Loano, rises in the village of Dentro: it has a decagonal plan and was erected between 1633 and 1638 by the Dorias.

Oratori dei Bianchi e dei Turchini

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the Borgo di Dentro, the oratory of St. John the Baptist is the seat of the Confraternity of the Disciplinanti (Disciplinanti), founded in 1262, and preserves an urn with part of the ashes of the saint; the oratory of Our Lady of the Rosary, built in 1660, is the seat of the Disciplinanti .

Centro storico, Borgo di Dentro e Porta Passorino

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Borgo di Dentro was born on July 19, 1309, when 71 families settled there at the invitation of the feudalist Raffo Doria; in Piazza Rocca the house Richeri (1310) has a brick portico, while in the Borgo di Fuori stands the house Maccagli (1711). Porta Passorino, from the time of Gianandrea Doria, is one of the four doors that closed the village in the west, with a clock installed in 1774; in the streets, the fountains of the project ...Loano Fontane d'Arte.

  • Where Piazza Rocca, Loano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.loano.sv.it · lamialiguria.it · italia.it

Spiaggia e lungomare

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

The old town is bordered by the long sandy beach that runs from the station to the port; beaches and sea have obtained the Blue Flag for the twelfth consecutive time in 2021. To protect the marine environment the city has built an artificial barrier that favors the return of flora and fauna. IAT office in Lungomare Nazario Sauro 12.
