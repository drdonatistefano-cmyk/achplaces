What to see in Capaci

A small town on the slopes of Monte Rullo Rosso, near the Falcone and Borsellino airport, feud granted by Frederick II in 1241, Baronia since 1556 and then county; you can visit the octagonal Mother Church with the frescoes of Giuseppe Trisca, the caves and the prehistoric necropolis of the territory and the House Museum Villa Africa of African ethnography.

3 places to visit in Capaci, in the province of Palermo (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa Madre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1741 and 1743 with an octagonal plan, it is accessed by an elegant double staircase; inside the frescoes by Giuseppe Trisca of 1744.

  • Where Piazza Matrice, Capaci
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.cittametropolitana.pa.it

Sources: turismo.cittametropolitana.pa.it

Casa Museo Villa Africa

Museums · Themed

Cultural center of African ethnography in the Sommariva Village, with a collection of masks, fetishes, weapons, paintings and statues.

  • Where Viale A. Palladio 11, Villaggio Sommariva, Capaci
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.cittametropolitana.pa.it

Sources: old.comune.capaci.pa.it · turismo.cittametropolitana.pa.it

Grotte preistoriche e necropoli di Ciachea

History and architecture · Archaeology

The caves of the Carrubella, Santa Rosalia, La Portella and Pizzo Miletta districts and the necropolis of Ciachea district testify to the oldest human presence in the territory; the findings of the necropolis are preserved at the Archaeological Museum of Palermo.

  • Where Contrade Carrubella, Santa Rosalia, La Portella, Pizzo Miletta e Ciachea, Capaci
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismo.cittametropolitana.pa.it

Sources: turismo.cittametropolitana.pa.it
