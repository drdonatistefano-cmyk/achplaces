What to see in Specchia

Borgo del Salento at the foot of the Serra Magnone, with one of the most beautiful historical centers in the area: alleys without cars and stairs around the sixteenth century Castello Risolo, the underground oil mills excavated between the fifteenth and nineteenth centuries and the convent of the Black Franciscans with the 36-column crypt.

Centro storico e piazza del Popolo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

On a hill overlooking the valley, it preserves the medieval system developed in the fifteenth century around the castle, with the 'rua' angioina, traffic-free alleys united by stairs and houses mostly of the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries; behind the castle the most suggestive part, made of ladders, shorts and outdoor life. Of the walls remain fragments on the west side and, to the east, one of the oldest coats of arms of the country with the almond tree on the rocky mountain; Pro Loco organizes guided tours.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Specchia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it · touringclub.it

Castello Risolo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortified building of the sixteenth century, originally isolated, with two high square towers at the corners, the eighteenth-century court on two levels towards Piazza del Popolo and the rustic portal surmounted by the coat of arms and two statues; the Protonobilissimi, marquis in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries, transformed it into a marquisal palace. Today it hosts exhibitions, shows and conferences.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Frantoi ipogei

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Almost all underground route under the streets of the village, between olive mills excavated between the 15th and 19th centuries and recently restored, reminiscent of the weight of oil in the economy of the Land of Otranto; you can visit with guided tours.

Convento dei Francescani Neri, cappella di Santa Caterina e cripta

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The convent dates back to 1531; the chapel of Saint Catherine of Alexandria, dating back to 1532, is frescoed with scenes of the life and martyrdom of the saint, and the crypt excavated in the rock rests on 36 slender columns in four rows, with traces of frescoes.

Chiesa di Sant'Eufemia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Medieval building of the late 9th-beginning 10th century, originally of Greek rite, with polygonal apse in local stone cones facing east according to the Byzantine tradition and a large biphora that illuminates the interior.

Museo di Specchia, dalla civiltà contadina al bosco e alle fonti rinnovabili

Museums · Themed

Museo comunale su tradizione e ambiente in tre temi, vita rurala, foresto e energie renewablei: racconta l'uso del focolare e del bosco per l'energia domestica e la produzione di coale che fede l'acquagnia locale, compreso l'officina del gas città chiusa nel 1938.
