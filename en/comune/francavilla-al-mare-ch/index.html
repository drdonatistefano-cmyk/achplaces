What to see in Francavilla al Mare

Popular seaside resort from the second half of the nineteenth century, with fine sandy beaches, Blue Flag sea, a long seafront and the Pontile della Sirena; on the border between Pescara and Chieti begins the Costa dei Trabocchi. In Palazzo San Domenico the MuMi houses the great paintings by Francesco Paolo Michetti and the works of the Michetti Prize.

MuMi - Museo Michetti

Museums · Modern art

In Palazzo San Domenico, seat of the Fondazione Francesco Paolo Michetti established in 1952, a few years after the National Prize of painting born in 1947. He has exhibited great works by Michetti, including "The Numbs" and "The Snakes" and the paintings of the artists of the Prize; since 2006 the foundation has collected over 260 works, including painting, photography, video, sculpture and installations, and hosts monographic exhibitions on Michetti and contemporary art.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Spiagge, lungomare e Pontile della Sirena

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Beaches of fine and quiet sand, well equipped, on a Blue Flag sea; the promenade is on foot or by bike between bars, restaurants and ice cream parlors, and the Pontile della Sirena is the panoramic point to see at sunset.

  • Where Lungomare, Francavilla al Mare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
