What to see in Capaccio Paestum

The ancient Poseidonia, founded at the end of the 7th century BC, part of the UNESCO site of Cilento: three Doric temples, the Roman forum and the National Archaeological Museum with the Tomb of the Diveper.

Area archeologica e templi di Paestum

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The Greek city of Poseidonia, founded at the end of the 7th century BC: you can visit the Doric temples of Hera, Neptune and Athena, among the most important monuments of Magna Grecia.

  • Where Via Magna Graecia 917, Paestum
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: whc.unesco.org · italia.it

Museo archeologico nazionale di Paestum

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The park museum, with a section of prehistory and protohistory and the Greek paintings of the Tomb of the Diveper.

  • Where Via Magna Graecia, Paestum
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · whc.unesco.org

Comizio, Agorà e Anfiteatro

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The public spaces of the ancient city, inside the archaeological park, which tell the daily life and the organization of Paestum.

  • Where Paestum
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: italia.it · parchipaestumvelia.cultura.gov.it
