What to see in Carcare

The centre of the Val Bormida, probably of Roman origin and mentioned as Bugile in 998-999; it houses Villa Maura with the museum of the writer Anton Giulio Barrili Garibaldi and the Alpine Museum.

Villa Maura e Museo Anton Giulio Barrili

Museums · House museums

Villa built in 1885 by writer and politician Anton Giulio Barrili, volunteer of Garibaldi, who dedicated it to his mother. On the first floor, already family apartment, four rooms with personal objects and collections of the writer: rare books, five hundred, prints, pamphlets, scores and manuscripts of his lectures at the University of Genoa; the lounges on the front house art exhibitions and the ground floor, since 1981, the civic library. Accessible even in a wheelchair.

  • Where Via Barrili 12, Carcare
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.carcare.sv.it

Museo Alpino

Museums · Themed

Opened in 2004 by the Alpine Group of Carcare to preserve the memory of an important part of Italian history: letters and postcards from the front, photographs, helmets, uniforms and casings. Open Saturday and Sunday 10-12, without reservation.

  • Where Via Garibaldi - Lungobormida, Carcare
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.carcare.sv.it
