What to see in Piasco

Country at the entrance of the Varaita Valley, already of the Marquises of Saluzzo and then feud of the Porporato, seat of the Museum of the Harp Victor Salvi; in his churches and chapels he worked in the first fifteenth century Pietro da Saluzzo, the Master of the Villar.

3 places to visit in Piasco, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo dell'arpa Victor Salvi

Museums · Themed

Entitled to Victor Salvi, it presents itself as the first and only museum in the world dedicated entirely to the harp, with its history and its sound universe.

Visit Cuneese indicates it is temporarily closed and invites you to contact the museum before visiting

  • Entry To be checked

Chiese e cappelle affrescate di San Giovanni, Sant'Anna e San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

During the golden age of the marquisate of Saluzzo, the painter Pietro da Saluzzo, called Maestro del Villar, painted the apse of the church of San Giovanni and the ancient pylon in front of it (frescoes of the pylon of the XV century) and also worked in the chapel of Sant'Anna. The chapel of San Rocco conserves frescoes from 1534; the chapel of Santa Brigida, with Romanesque apse, was frescoed in Saluzzo by unknown artists.

  • Where Piasco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.piasco.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Parrocchiale, ex chiesa di San Sebastiano e campanile

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Baroque parish was designed by Bartolomeo Ricca di Savigliano and built between 1779 and 1783 on the site of an ancient covered market, with bricks cooked in a furnace opened specifically outside the village. The church of San Sebastiano, built from the first fifteenth century, is now home to the Town Hall; next to it stands the bell tower of the fifteenth century.

  • Where Centro di Piasco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.piasco.cn.it · visitcuneese.it
