What to see in Bagnara di Romagna

4 places to visit in Bagnara di Romagna, in the province of Ravenna (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 4 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Rocca sforzesca e Museo del Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The fortress belonged to Visconti, Alidosi and Riario Sforza; the museum winds through salt, walkways, mast and underground, where in 2005 an archaeological area emerged.

  • Where Bagnara di Romagna
  • Entry To be checked on the official website
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Il castrum medievale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

According to Emilia Romagna Turismo is the only still complete medieval castrum of the Emilia-Romagna plain, with walls and green moat.

  • Where Bagnara di Romagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Chiesa dei Santi Giovanni e Andrea e Museo parrocchiale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Archpriest's church of the fifteenth century near the fortress, with the Madonna of the Public Vote in terracotta, a fifteenth-century tabernacle and an organ of the eighteenth century; in the museum a altarpiece of Innocent Imola and a crucifix of Donatellian school.

  • Where Bagnara di Romagna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Santuario della Madonna del Soccorso

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

18th century Sanctuary of Cosimo Morelli about 2 km from the center on the road to Lugo, with a Madonna and Child painted terracotta pilgrimage destination.

  • Where Strada per Lugo, Bagnara di Romagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
