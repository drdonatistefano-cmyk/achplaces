What to see in Pico

Medieval village of the Ciociaria in the Aurunci Mountains Nature Park, with concentric alleys and steps around the Farnese Castle; it is the birthplace of Tommaso Landolfi, to which is dedicated a literary park, and Eugenio Montale set there the Elegia of Pico Farnese in 1939.

8 places to visit in Pico, in the province of Frosinone (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e Porta San Rocco

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village (X-XI centuries) with concentric streets connected by stairs, surrounded by walls that had several doors: only Porta San Rocco remains intact, entrance to the historic center. From the village the view reaches the Abbey of Montecassino and the mountains of Abruzzo.

  • Where Porta San Rocco, Pico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Castello Farnese e Torre dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Erected around the year 1000 by Giovanni Shinto, lord of Aquino (cited in 1049 as "Castrum qui nominatur Pika"), he passed to the D'Aquino, the Angioini, the Della Rovere and from 1542 to the Farnese. Today it is largely in ruins over Largo Carbonaro, but some towers are open to visitors, including the one with the Renaissance clock still working.

  • Where Largo Carbonaro, Pico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Marina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the 11th century, originally dedicated to the saints Biagio and Marina and from 1200 to the only Santa Marina; in 1049 Giovanni Shinto and his wife Alfarana donated it to the abbot of Montecassino. On July 17 a procession starts from the church of Sant'Antonino.

  • Where Largo Santa Marina, Pico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchiletterari.com · italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Antonino Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Baroque Latin Cross Church, founded before the year 1000 and restored for the first time in 1781. On September 2, the statue of the saint is carried in procession to the main square.

  • Where Via Landolfi, Pico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in the sixteenth century outside the walls as a vow for the end of a pestilence, it was saved from degradation in 1890 by the Congregation of Charity; it is located at the garden of San Rocco, at the entrance of the village. On 16 August the saint is celebrated with the procession of the bread of San Rocco.

  • Where Largo San Rocco, Pico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Casa natale di Tommaso Landolfi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

House with arched windows and internal garden along via Landolfi, inserted in the Network of the Historical Houses of Lazio; here Montale visited his friend Landolfi in 1937, before writing the Elegia of Pico Farnese. The writer, who died in Ronciglione, is buried in Pico.

  • Where Via Landolfi, Pico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchiletterari.com · comune.pico.fr.it · italia.it

Parco Letterario Tommaso Landolfi

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established by the Municipality in 2014, the first literary park of the Ciociaria, winds through the alleys of the historic center with twelve panels in wood and aluminum on the places of the stories of Landolfi (the lunar stone and others); a second path, the "Paese del P." follow the mountain paths of the territory.

  • Where Centro storico, Pico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
