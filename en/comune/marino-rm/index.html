What to see in Marino

Città dei Castelli Romani on a spur of pepper next to the Bosco Ferentano, overlooking the lake Albano 23 km from Rome: under the village there is the net of caves of Marino Underground, with the mitreus frescoed of the second century; on the surface Palazzo Colonna, the basilica of San Barnaba, the Fontana dei Quattro Mori and the Civic Museum "Umberto Mastroianni" in the former church of Santa Lucia.

Museo civico «Umberto Mastroianni» (ex chiesa di Santa Lucia)

Museums · Archaeological

Civic museum inaugurated in 2000 in the former Gothic church of Saint Lucia, the Gothic Temple, built on a Roman cistern of imperial age then transformed into a Christian oratory: the first cult building of 1102, desecrated in 1669 for the construction of the Collegiate Church of San Barnaba, damaged by the bombings of 1944 and purchased by the Municipality in 1974. Exhibits of the territory: classical statuary, sepulchral cippes, sarcophagi, inscriptions, protohistoric depositions.

MiC card indicates the museum closed every day, with opening at exhibitions

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Marino Sotterranea (Marino Ipogea)

History and architecture · Archaeology

Labyrinth of caves and galleries connected, for tens of kilometers, dug from the Middle Ages in the pepper under the historic center to obtain building material; for centuries there were preserved barrels of wine and in war served as anti-aircraft shelters. Some galleries have been cleaned up and transformed into an exhibition path with the finds of the restorations; visible the walls with the eruptive phases of the Lazio volcano. Guided tours all year round on weekends, by reservation at Archeoclub Colli Albani.

  • Where Via Garibaldi 112 (partenza visite da Piazza Matteotti), Marino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Mitreo di Marino

History and architecture · Archaeology

It was built in the cistern of a Roman villa and preserves the Tauroctonia fresco with Mitra that kills the white bull, from the second half of the second century A.D.; it was discovered by chance in 1962 during works in a cellar at the foot of the historic centre. It is one of three Italian mitrei with a frescoed tauroctonia, with the Mitreo Barberini of Rome and that of Santa Maria Capua Vetere.

Palazzo Colonna

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between 1532 and 1622 on the previous Rocca degli Orsini on a project by Antonio da Sangallo the Younger, from the early twentieth century it is the seat of the Town Hall; it can be visited underground caves.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Fontana dei Quattro Mori

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The seventeenth-century fountain in Peperino, symbol of the victory of Lepanto; the "miracle" of the fountain that gives wine occurred the first time on October 4, 1925 and is repeated with the Festival of Grapes in early October, with the historical procession reminiscent of the return of Marcantonio Colonna da Lepanto.

  • Where Centro storico, Marino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Vino Marino DOP e ciambelle al mosto

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Marino DOP wine was already known to the Roman patricians of Colli Albani; the donuts with must, with local must, extra virgin olive oil and raisins, follow a recipe of the seventeenth century and have their feast on the third Sunday of October. Other specialties: honey and chocolate mustaccioli and bride cookies, for sale in the shops of the historic center.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
