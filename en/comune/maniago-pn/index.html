What to see in Maniago

The city of knives, seat of the knife-making district: Piazza Italia with the late Dome of San Mauro and Palazzo d'Attimis Maniago with the lion march attributed to Pomponio Amalteo, the ruins of the castle on the hill and the museum of art craftsmanship in a former knife factory.

6 places to visit in Maniago, in the province of Pordenone (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo dell'arte fabbrile e delle coltellerie

Museums · Themed

Opened in 1998 in the building that since 1907 hosted the first large knife factory in Maniago, documents the processing of steel in the factories of the area and its technological evolution, rebuilding the working environments and exhibiting past and present productions. It also houses the wood and iron section of the Provincial Museum of Rural Life Diogene Penzi. Free with FVGcard.

  • Where Via Maestri del Lavoro 1, Maniago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it

Piazza Italia e Loggia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Heart of the community, unique square with a beautiful fountain in the center that disguises itself to every carnival, framed by the Loggia and the main historical buildings.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Palazzo d'Attimis Maniago

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace of the eighteenth century on the square, with the lion of San Marco frescoed on the walls, attributed to Pomponio Amalteo and sign of the long Venetian domination; gardens and stables are visited during Open Castles.

  • Where Piazza Italia, Maniago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Rovine del castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill behind the palace, the remains of the castle probably built in the 11th century and abandoned during the 17th century; on clear days good views over the plain.

  • Where Colle del castello, Maniago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Botteghe dei coltellinai

Local life and traditions · Crafts · Free entry

Ancient knives and weapons are bought at the Consortium or the dealers known for forging; the tradition comes from the road of the Colvera stream, granted to Nicolò di Maniago in 1453, along which arose the beatings.

  • Where Maniago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it
