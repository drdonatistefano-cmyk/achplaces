What to see in Grottaglie

Città della ceramica cinta da gravine, with the Ceramic Quarter and its workshops dug into the rock, the 14th century Episcopal Castle that houses the Ceramic Museum and the Church of Carmine with the crib of Stefano da Putignano.

5 places to visit in Grottaglie, in the province of Taranto (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Episcopio e Museo della Ceramica

Museums · Themed · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Bishop's fortress built in the second half of the fourteenth century by Archbishop Giacomo D'Atri on a structure of the thirteenth century, on the edge of a gravina, with a tower greater than twenty blackbirds, ground floor dug into tuff and sixteenth-century garden. Since 1999 its 11 rooms house the Ceramic Museum, about 500 works from the 8th century BC to today. Opening hours from the source italia.it: weekdays 10-13 and 15:30-19:30, Sunday 10:30-13 and 16:30-21.

Museo didattico delle maioliche

Museums · Themed

Established in the school year 1937-38 at the State Institute of Art, on the edge of the Ceramic Quarter, it preserves about 300 artifacts from 1887 to today: grottaglian majolica of the XVI-XVII centuries, works of the Institute of the early twentieth century and pieces made for national and international competitions.

Chiesa del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the monastery of the same name of the sixteenth century; in the last chapel on the left it preserves a nativity scene carved in 1530 by Stefano da Putignano. Open 8-12:30 and 16-20.

  • Where Via Mastropaolo, Grottaglie
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Gravine di Grottaglie

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village is surrounded by the largest ravines of Riggio, from the north-west to the south-east, with Basilian frescoes and Greek inscriptions, of Fantiano, Fullonese, with a seventeenth-century Calvary carved in the tuff in a cave, and of San Biagio, with a frescoed rock church.

  • Where Strada comunale esterna Belvedere, Grottaglie
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it
