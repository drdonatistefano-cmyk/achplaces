What to see in Marciana

Medieval village of the western Elba on the slopes of Mount Capanne, with the Pisan Fortress of the twelfth century, the Archaeological Museum in the Palazzo del Pretorio and the cable car to the top of the island; in the territory the village of Poggio with the Fonte di Napoleone and the beaches of Procchio, Sant'Andrea and Chiessi.

9 places to visit in Marciana, in the province of Livorno (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Fortezza Pisana e Casa del Parco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built by the Pisans in the 12th century in the highest part of the village and enlarged in the 15th century by the Appians with four bastions, it was the refuge of the inhabitants during the pirate raids; from the walk the view goes from Monte Capanne to the coast. It houses the Park's Environmental Education Centre, with free admission, on the geology of the Archipelago.

  • Where Piazzale della Fortezza, Marciana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · visitelba.info · islepark.it · italia.it

Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

In the seventeenth-century Palazzo del Pretorio, it preserves Elbani finds in store by the Superintendence: prehistoric, protohistoric sections (with Villanovian materials from Monte Giove and Madonna del Monte) and Etruscan, documentation on granite and materials of the Roman wreck of Procchio.

  • Where Via del Pretorio, Marciana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visittuscany.com · italia.it

Museo numismatico didattico della Zecca

Museums · Themed

In an underground environment excavated in the historic center, he tells the town with coins from the end of the 16th to the end of the 17th century, documenting the mint shop and the guys from Piombino.

  • Where Centro storico, Marciana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Borgo di Marciana

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Borgo from ancient origins (35 BC), rich in archaeological remains, with a large medieval layout, entrance doors and narrow alleys; the church of San Cerbone is dedicated to the saint who took refuge there from Populonia in the 6th century.

  • Where Marciana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com

Chiesa romanica di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient Romanesque parish destroyed by the Turks in 1554, now recognized as a national monument.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Santuario della Madonna del Monte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the northern side of Mount Capanne, a place of worship since the 14th century; the present building dates back to 1595 and took its form today in 1799 after various restorations.

  • Where Madonna del Monte, Marciana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Monte Capanne e funivia

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

With 1019 meters it is the highest peak of Elba and the province of Livorno; you walk up the CAI trails or in a few minutes with the cable car near the village, active in summer.

  • Where Monte Capanne, Marciana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Poggio e la Fonte di Napoleone

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Medieval village between holm oak and chestnut woods where the Fountain of Napoleon flows, already Fonte Acquaviva, so called for the emperor's stops in exile; there is a fountain for free supply.

  • Where Poggio, Marciana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com

Spiagge di Procchio, Sant'Andrea e Chiessi

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Various coasts: sand in Paolina, Procchio and Spartaia, sand and rock in Sant'Andrea, rocks in Punta Nera and Chiessi, gravel and rock in Pomonte and Patresi; from Chiessi a challenging path with views of Capraia and Corsica.

  • Where Costa di Marciana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it
