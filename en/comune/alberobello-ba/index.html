What to see in Alberobello

The capital of the trulli, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1996: the Monti and Aia Piccola districts with over 1,600 trulli, the Trullo Soverano with two floors, the house museum of Casa Pezzolla and the Santa Lucia viewpoint on the grey roofs of white houses.

9 places to visit in Alberobello, in the province of Bari (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rione Monti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest and most famous neighborhood of the trulli, with 1,030 dry stone houses and conical roofs according to the UNESCO site: once the most popular district, today almost only dedicated to tourism. In the evening, illuminated trulli create a crib effect.

  • Where Rione Monti, Alberobello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · whc.unesco.org

Rione Aia Piccola

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The smallest and quietest district in the old town: about 400 trulli still inhabited (590 according to the UNESCO data sheet), without shops or locals.

  • Where Rione Aia Piccola, Alberobello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · whc.unesco.org

Trullo Sovrano

Museums · House museums · UNESCO World Heritage

The largest trullo of Alberobello and the only two-storey one, with internal staircase in masonry: nucleus to the left of the early seventeenth century, the rest built in the first half of the eighteenth century for the family of the priest Cataldo Perta. The dome of about 14 meters dominates twelve minor cones; among the first trulli built with mortar, national monument since 1923. Vintage furnishings and garden.

  • Where Piazza Sacramento, Alberobello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo del territorio Casa Pezzolla

Museums · City history · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Complex of 15 trulli communicating in the Aia Piccola district that tells the story of the settlement and peasant civilization. Free admission.

  • Where Rione Aia Piccola, Alberobello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Trulli Siamesi

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Two trulli melted in the center in a single building, linked to the legend of love and hatred between two brothers.

  • Where Centro storico, Alberobello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa a trullo di Sant'Antonio di Padova

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church built in the form of trullo in 14 months in 1927 and very modified in the sixties, at the top of the hill along Via Monte San Michele.

  • Where Via Monte San Michele, Alberobello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Basilica dei Santi Medici Cosma e Damiano

Places of worship · Basilicas

Church of the seventeenth century in the north of the village, in front of which is the Sovereign Trullo.

  • Where Piazza Sacramento, Alberobello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Casa d'Amore

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Historic house of 1797, the first building built after the liberation of Alberobello from the feudal bond: the first stone house tied with mortar, which marks the passage to the palaces of the nineteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Ferdinando IV, Alberobello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Belvedere di Santa Lucia

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The most evocative panoramic point of the city, on the expanse of white houses with grey roofs; recommended at sunset.

  • Where Belvedere Santa Lucia, Alberobello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
