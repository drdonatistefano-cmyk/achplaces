What to see in Sefro

Medieval village of the upper Maceratese among woods and streams, leads to the Karst plateau of Montelago and the Scurosa Valley: the tower of Da Varano, the museum of agricultural tools in the castle, the waterfall of Scarzito in the village and the Enchanted Wood in the public gardens.

8 places to visit in Sefro, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Sefro in the guide with map and filters

Torre Varano

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Last rest of the medieval castle of Da Varano, lords of Camerino, who found refuge in Sefro after the sack of their city in 1259.

  • Where Sefro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.sefro.mc.it · turismo.comune.sefro.mc.it

Museo degli attrezzi agricoli «Florindo Ferretti» (civiltà contadina)

Museums · Themed

In the castle of Da Varano, collection of tools of the local peasant and artisan civilization that tells the rural traditions of the village.

  • Where Castello dei Da Varano, Sefro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sefro.mc.it · italia.it

Cascata dello Scarzito

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The jump of the Scarzito stream is seen at the entrance of the village; once operated a mill for wheat and lime of the monastery of Valle Ermita. The torrent is born from the source Acqua di San Giovanni and feeds trout farms and sport fishing.

  • Where Ingresso del paese, Sefro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.sefro.mc.it · italia.it

Il Bosco Incantato

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Permanent artistic installation in public gardens: a short and accessible path with wooden sculptures, paintings, tree houses and fairy-tale figures that tell the history of the forest; for children there is audio history "The Awakening of the Bosco" on the tourist portal of the municipality.

  • Where Giardini pubblici, Sefro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.sefro.mc.it · italia.it

Piazza Bellanti e chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

On the town hall square, the church built between 1887 and 1893, probably on the remains of the parish church of Santa Maria of the 11th century; it is advisable to check the opening hours.

  • Where Piazza Bellanti, Sefro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sefro.mc.it · italia.it

Chiese di San Pietro, Sant'Albertino e Madonna dei Calcinai

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

St. Peter, probably fourteenth-century plant, stands near the stream Scarzito; Sant'Albertino, on the hill north-west of the center, has a classroom, presbytery and bell tower; the Madonna dei Calcinai, on the road to Pioraco, contains frescoes from the sixteenth century.

  • Where Sefro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sefro.mc.it · italia.it

Altopiano di Montelago

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

karst area of more than four square kilometers around 900-1000 meters, made of meadows, wetlands, sinkholes, springs, caves and ditches; there is no permanent lake, but in winter the water is collected in the sinkholes in small pools often frozen. In the summer better go in the late afternoon, because there is little shade; the services at altitude can be limited.

  • Where Montelago, Sefro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismo.comune.sefro.mc.it

Valle della Scurosa

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Protected natural area along the provincial Sefro-Sorti, with dense woods, undergrowth and the Scarzito stream, suitable for walks and bikes.

  • Where Strada provinciale Sefro-Sorti, Sefro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.sefro.mc.it · italia.it
