What to see in Montecchia di Crosara

The village of Val d'Alpone is surrounded by vineyards and cherry trees: on the hill of Piazza Castello, where the ancient manor stood, there is the Romanesque church of San Salvatore with the crypt on Roman capitals; the Enomuseum Ca' Rugate tells of a century of vinification.

Chiesa di San Salvatore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Romanesque rectorial church of the 10th century on the hill above the center, once part of the manor of Piazza Castello; it has a single nave interior with wooden trusses and frescoes, recently restored, attributed to Martino da Verona and Battista da Vicenza, students of Pisanello. The oldest part is the small crypt on pillars with Roman capitals; from 1855 every August 5th we go up on pilgrimage to Our Lady of the Snow.

  • Where Piazza Castello 53, Montecchia di Crosara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it · comune.montecchiadicrosara.vr.it

Enomuseo Ca' Rugate

Museums · Themed

Inside the cellar of the Tessari family, in a farmhouse of the first post-war period, exhibits more than 150 tools used in a century of vinification and follows the path from the arrival of the grape in the threshing area to the aging and bottling in the caneval; it is recognized of regional interest. The tour with tasting is booked with the wine cellar.

  • Where Via Pergola 36, Montecchia di Crosara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Marcia della Ciliegia, anello di Monte Mirabello

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the village you go up towards Monte Mirabello between olive groves and cherry trees, follow the panoramic ridge up to the Cross of Monte Bastia, with views of the Val Tramigna and the Val d'Illasi, you go down to Castelcerino and to the woods to Contrà Meggiano, returning among the vineyards to Piazza Castello. Recommended during the Cherry Festival, the second week of June.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Montecchia di Crosara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it
