What to see in Piuro

Val Bregaglia, a few kilometres from Chiavenna, marked by the landslide of 1618 that buried the ancient village: in Prosto survives the Renaissance Palazzo Vertemate Franchi, in Borgonuovo you can visit the excavations of Belfòrt, the multimedia center and the Waterfalls, while the sacristies of Sant'Abbondio house the Museum of Excavations.

Palazzo Vertemate Franchi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palazzo elegante, built in the second half of the 16th century by the brothers Guglielmo and Luigi Vertemate Franchi, rich merchants of Pirus: it is the only building of the family that survived the landslide of 1618. Behind a sober façade, the Renaissance interiors have walls and frescoed vaults with mythological scenes inspired by the Metamorphosis of Ovid, stüe and inlaid ceilings. Around, Italian garden with fish pond and double balustrade exedra, orchard, vineyard, chestnut grove, wine press, wine cellar, stables and glaciers; in summer it hosts outdoor concerts. Guided tours by reservation through the Valchiavenna Tourist Board.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo degli scavi di Piuro

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the sacristies of the church of Sant'Abbondio tells the Pirus before and after the landslide: a bell melted in 1398, a seat and a pool in ollar stone, a silver head of Galeazzo Maria Sforza, pins, buckles and buttons in copper, swords, rifles and iron lights, and a room with the excavations of 1988 in wood, iron, leather and copper. Open from June to September on Saturday and Sunday 15-17; free admission with the ticket of Palazzo Vertemate Franchi or the Waterfalls.

  • Where Chiesa di Sant'Abbondio, Piuro
  • Entry €2, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: valtellina.it · italia.it

Belfòrt e Info Piuro Centro Multimediale

History and architecture · Archaeology

In Borgonuovo the archaeological site of Belfòrt preserves the remains of the Palazzo dei Piaceri buried by the landslide of 1618, with large rooms, cellars and crotti built with care. In the nearby rural building the multimedia center has panels, video totems, a holographic projection on the landslide and a touch-screen; from here starts a path with audioguide downloadable via QR code.

  • Where Borgonuovo di Piuro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: valtellina.it · italia.it

Cascate dell'Acquafraggia

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The stream was born at the Pizzo di Lago (3,050 m) and descends through two glacial suspended valleys; the name derives from the fracta-water, water continuously interrupted by waterfalls, and the lowest jumps, double jumping, are the most beautiful. You can walk from Borgonuovo leaving the car at the entrance of the park; a panoramic path climbs the side with views of the waterfalls, the valley and Chiavenna. In summer meadows and pools at the foot of the waterfalls are frequented for the bathroom, with caution for current and slippery rocks.

  • Where Borgonuovo di Piuro
  • Entry €3 on holidays and pre-festive June-September and every day of August; free under 5 and over 75 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it
