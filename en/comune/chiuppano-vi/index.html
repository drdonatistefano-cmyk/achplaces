What to see in Chiuppano

At the foot of the hills of the Bregonze, Chiuppano is the village where fossil teeth and bones of the Anthracotherium were found in 1948-1949, an animal lived about 30 million years ago, today told by the Naturalistic Museum of the Bregonze; in Via Roma stands the parish church of San Michele Arcangelo, built between 1929 and 1942.

Museo naturalistico delle Bregonze

Museums · Science and university

Museum managed by the Naturalistic Group • Anthracotherium , born in 2001 to rediscover and enhance the hills of the Bregonze, and recognized by the Veneto Region as meeting the requirements of the museum; it wants to be a live museum related to the territory, its flora and its fauna. The name of the association recalls the Anthracotherium, whose fossil remains, some 30 million years ago, were found in Chiuppano in 1948-1949; the museum also hosts meetings on lignite mines and the fossil deposits of the Bregonze.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish of the diocese of Padua. The census of the churches of the CEI records its long planning (1870-1928), the purchase of land in 1936, the construction between 1929 and 1942 and the blessing in 1957; between 1960 and 1970 the presbytery had a structural intervention. The municipality lists it among the places of the village, with free access.

  • Entry Free
