What to see in Albino

A small town in the lower Seriana Valley, which can be reached by tram from Bergamo, home of the 16th century painter Giovan Battista Moroni, whose works he preserves in the churches of San Giuliano, Madonna del Pianto and Santa Barbara; in the hamlets the medieval bridge over Serio, the ethnographic museums of Comenduno and Valle del Lujo and the museum of Blessed Pierina Morosini in Fiobbio.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giuliano Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the historic centre, it houses paintings by Moroni, Salmeggia, Cavagna, Zucco, Cignaroli, Barbello and Paduanino and frescoes by the recently discovered Marinoni workshop. Di Moroni the Crucifix between the Saints Bernardino and Antonio da Padova (about 1553-1575), the Trinity (1555), destined to the church of Fiobbio, and a standard painted on both sides in the sacristy.

Santuario della Madonna del Pianto

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Designed by Antonio Piccinelli, who joined two oldest sacred buildings since 1898, he has a Latin cross plan with three naves and a frescoed dome on octagonal drum with the Coronation of the Virgin. It preserves the Christ cross holder of Moroni (1570-1575, from the ancient church of San Rocco), works of Salmeggia and Zucco and many ex vows.

Ponte Romanico

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval bridge on the Serio with four arches of increasing width towards the beehive, with a road of about 2.2 meters in steep slope: dated to the thirteenth century and documented by the fourteenth century, it connected Albino with Cene, Val Gandino and Val Cavallina. Dismissed in 1911, it was restored and crossed with the Val Seriana bike path.

  • Where Ponte Romanico, Albino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Sources: valseriana.eu

Museo etnografico della Torre di Comenduno

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1989 by volunteers in the tower of the former Villa Briolini, where the Briolini silkiers took over the main part of the sharecropping: it tells the first half of the twentieth century on average and low Val Seriana, from the chain of wheat to the rebuilt peasant house, to the cellar and the shops of shoemaker, slut, carpenter, turner, blacksmith and watchmaker. Open on Sunday 15.30-18.30.

  • Where Via Santa Maria 10, Comenduno di Albino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Sources: scoprialbino.it · valseriana.eu

Museo etnografico della Valle del Lujo

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1995 in Casale, it documents the rural and artisan life of the valley: agricultural tools, pastoral and forestry, miners' trades, coalsmiths, blacksmiths, blacksmiths and shoemakers, the rural house and collections on chestnut and ancient varieties of apples and pears.

Sentiero del Castagno e Parco di Casale

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring path suitable for all that starts from the church of Casale, on the slopes of Monte Pranzà, and crosses the chestnut grove with picnic areas and benches; Casale is known for the via delle Castagne and the panorama of the valley up to Pizzo Arera.

Fiobbio e Museo della beata Pierina Morosini

Museums · Themed

Fraction on the slopes of Mount Misma with origins in the twelfth century and the church of the Holy Trinity of the fifteenth century. The young Pierina Morosini, beatified in 1987, rests in the parish of Sant'Antonio da Padova; a small exhibition space is dedicated to her life.
