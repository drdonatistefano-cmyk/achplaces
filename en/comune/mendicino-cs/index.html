What to see in Mendicino

Medieval village at the foot of Mount Cocuzzo, a few kilometers from Cosenza, called "country of silk": in the river park on the Acheron the ancient Gaudio spinning mill and the Silk Museum, in Piazza del Duomo the church of San Nicola di Bari with the facade in tuff of Mendicino, in the Castello the Clock Tower.

6 places to visit in Mendicino, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della seta e antica filanda Gaudio

Museums · Themed

Museum of industrial archaeology with ancient spinning mills, documents and uses related to domestic sericulture, spread throughout Calabria until the last century; it is located in the river park together with the ancient spinning mill Gaudio and the dynamic silk museum, expression of the Calabrian textile.

MiC card (updated 2020) indicates closed every day; times to be checked with the Municipality

  • Where Parco fluviale, centro storico, Mendicino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Parco fluviale dell'Acheronte

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Green area in the center of the village, close to the historic core, along the banks of the Acheron river, whose spring waters fed the mills and the local silk.

  • Where Parco fluviale, Mendicino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Centro storico e chiese di Piazza del Duomo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Piazza Municipio and Piazzetta San Giuseppe, next to the amphitheatre and Palazzo Del Gaudio Campagna, the cultural centre of the village; in Piazza del Duomo the church of San Nicola di Bari, with the facade in tuff of Mendicino, and the church of San Sebastiano.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Mendicino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Torre dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

In the ancient Rione Castello, it is one of the architectural symbols of the village.

Monte Cocuzzo

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village stands at its feet; the summit, which can also be reached through stages of the Italia CAI Trail, offers a wide panoramic view.

  • Where Monte Cocuzzo, Mendicino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
