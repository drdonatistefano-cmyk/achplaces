What to see in Longobucco

Borgo del Parco Nazionale della Sila known for the hand weaving of blankets and tapestries and for the ancient silver mines; in the center the Matrice Church with the Norman Bell Tower, the carved stone portals and the Museum of the Silano Craftsmanship in the former Franciscan monastery.

7 places to visit in Longobucco, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa Matrice di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Of 12th century origins and Marian sanctuary since 1960, it has a black stone façade with Romanesque and Baroque elements and 18th century Baroque interior: main altar in polychrome marble of chisels of Rogliano, choir, pulpit, confessional and organ carved by craftsmen of Serra San Bruno, the frescoes of the Nativity and Adoration of the Magi of C. Santanna da Rende; in the eighteenth-century silver sacristy obtained from the local mineral.

  • Where Centro storico, Longobucco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Torre campanaria normanna

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Symbol of the historic center next to the Matrix Church, it was built in the 12th century as a watchtower and defence against Saracen incursions and transformed into a bell tower around 1700; 32 meters high, it has masonry in square blocks of tuff exposed on a base of large granite pebbles.

  • Where Accanto alla Chiesa Matrice, Longobucco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcosila.it · italia.it

Centro storico e portali scolpiti

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The centre preserves examples of the local stone processing, such as the portals of Palazzo Citino with the traditional masks and residences overlooking Piazza Matteotti.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Longobucco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Sentiero Via delle Miniere

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Historical-naturalistic path that starts from the village and touches the ancient silver quarries: the first information of extraction dates back to the twelfth century, when Henry VI sent Peter of Livonia to oversee the works; in 1506 the mines passed to Galeazzo Caracciolo, in 1566 they returned to the Royal Court and were exploited until 1783. Panels illustrate the extraction and processing of silver galena.

  • Where Partenza da Piazza Matteotti, Longobucco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: archivio.parcosila.it · parcosila.it · italia.it

Museo dell'Artigianato Silano e della Difesa del Suolo

Museums · Themed

Ecomuseum of the Sila National Park in the former convent of the Franciscan Friars Minor, dedicated to the handicrafts of Silano, the defence of the soil and the extraction of silver and goldsmithery.

  • Where Via Roma angolo Piazza Monumento, Longobucco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcosila.it · italia.it

Mostra permanente di tessuti artigianali

Local life and traditions · Crafts

In the historic centre, it exhibits the tradition of carpets, blankets and embroidery of the village: Longobucco is one of the few places in Calabria where weaves again on handlooms, with the design woven into the weft and not embroidered; its fabrics were exhibited in Rome between 1921 and 1925 and awarded with the silver medal in Barcelona (1929) and Milan (1930).

  • Where Centro storico, Longobucco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · archivio.parcosila.it

Le cantine del centro storico

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Typical taverns in the alleys of the center, called by the name or nickname of the owners (such as "a dua Sarbaturu" or "a dua Cicciu") where the local wine is served in the traditional cup.
