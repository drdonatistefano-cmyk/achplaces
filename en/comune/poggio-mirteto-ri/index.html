What to see in Poggio Mirteto

Village of Sabina in the Tiber Valley, 66 km from Rome, with its medieval core surrounded by fourteenth-century walls: the Cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta, the Sabino Diocesan Museum, the thirteenth-century church of São Paulo and the chimney of the Glassworks Fajella.

7 places to visit in Poggio Mirteto, in the province of Rieti (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Begin in 1641 on a project by Martino Longhi, blessed in 1684 and consecrated in 1779, it became a cathedral in 1841 when Gregory XVI raised Poggio Mirteto to a diocese. Facade in brick; inside with three naves rich in stuccoes, with altar in red marble of Cottanello, the altarpiece of the Assunta by Giovanni Baglione (1613), paintings attributed to Giacinto Brandi and Antonio Gherardi and, on the vault, the canvas of Metello Helzel (1931) with Saint Gaetano of Thiene; organ of the brothers Martinelli (1847).

Museo Diocesano Sabino

Museums · Old masters

In the old Episcopal Palace, with a renovated route in 2024, it contains about 150 sacred works of art saved from the abandoned churches of Sabina: an eighteenth-century canopy in Chinese style commissioned by Cardinal Annibale Albani, the standard processional painted on two sides by Plautilla Bricci, silver processional crosses (the oldest of 1306) and a room of the masters of the sabino baroque like Vincenzo Manenti and Girolamo Topo.

  • Where Piazza Mario Dottori 14, Poggio Mirteto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di San Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Outside the village, it is the oldest church of Poggio Mirteto: church, bell tower and crypt are of the thirteenth century. The aisle, with arched ceiling, is frescoed between the 12th and 14th centuries; arch and apse house the Coronation of Maria del Torresani (first half of the 16th century) and there is the cycle of the White Bats, movement arrived in Sabina at the end of the fourteenth century.

Centro storico, mura, Porta Farnese e Palazzo Abbaziale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval core is surrounded by some preserved fourteenth-century walls, with narrow streets with an ancient appearance; you enter them from the Porta Farnese, opened in 1576, and the route touches the Palazzo Abbaziale (1285-1300) to the Porta di Sotto. From the village you can see the Tiber Valley and the Soratte; on July 6, 1849 Garibaldi camped there with about 4,000 men and Anita.

  • Where Porta Farnese, Poggio Mirteto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Ciminiera della Vetreria Fajella

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

In Piazza della Vetreria remains the chimney, the only remaining of the Fajella Glassworks of the early nineteenth century, considered the first industrial glassworks in Italy; the nearby buildings, restored, house bars and restaurants.

  • Where Piazza della Vetreria, Poggio Mirteto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Castello Pinci a Castel San Pietro

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

In the hamlet of Castel San Pietro, it is the first fortified residence of the local feudalist, documented from 1003-1009, restored by the architect Enzo Pinci.
