What to see in San Venanzo

A hilly country between the middle Tiber valley and Orvieto, known for its three small active volcanoes 265,000 years ago and for its Venanzite, a rare rock that bears its name: in the center the Park and volcanological museum, Villa Faina with its public park and medieval tower; around Mount Peglia, UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, with the Park of the Seven Friars and the Etruscan excavation of Poggio delle Civitelle.

Parco e museo vulcanologico

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Opened in 1999 in a building dating back to the early nineteenth century next to Villa Faina, the museum has four rooms on the geology of local eruptions: Venanzite, lava rock with almost unique composition, a dinosaur egg from China and the skull of Elephas southernis found in the surroundings. The open-air park is about 2 km long on the large venanzite casting, partly emptied in the 1970s, between cones, craters, casts, rare minerals and the remains of a millstone quarry.

Villa Faina e parco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Born from a medieval palace of the fortified village, of which remains sections of walls, from 1830 belonged to the Counts Faina, who at the end of the nineteenth century gave it the current appearance: three floors with wing of the greenhouse to L, portal bugnato in terracotta, windows with parapets in cast iron and terracotta decorations perhaps of the sculptors Perugini Angeletti and Biscarini; inside the living room of the parties, dining room, gallery and skyscraper. Municipal since 1962, it is the seat of the City Council and a congress center; the large public park hosts parties and events.

Centro storico, torre medievale e chiesa della Madonna Liberatrice

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village is dominated by a medieval tower and preserves remains of the castle; in the church of the Madonna Liberatrice a table of Perugino school. In the hamlets (Rotecastello, San Vito in Monte, Pornello, Ripalvella, Poggio Aquilone, Civitella dei Conti, Collelungo) there are medieval towers and castles.

  • Where San Venanzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Riserva della Biosfera UNESCO del Monte Peglia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

UNESCO MAB reserve since 2018 between the Tiber and Peglia basins, formed by the municipalities of San Venanzo, Orvieto, Parrano and Ficulle: holm oak, cerro and maple woods, 44 species of wild mammals and the extinct volcanoes of San Venanzo, with rocks present in very few other places in the world, including China, Uganda and Brazil.

  • Where Monte Peglia, San Venanzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Poggio delle Civitelle

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The excavations have returned Etruscan materials: the site seems to have been a defensive outpost of Orvieto and a weapons production center, judging by the large amount of iron found.
