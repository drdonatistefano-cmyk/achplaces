What to see in Fauglia

Medieval village of the Pisan Hills developed along the ridge after the destruction of the castle in 1433, the second home of the Macchiaioli, to see the Giorgio Kienerk Museum in the former prisons, the church of San Lorenzo, the Municipal Theatre of 1852, the villas as Villa Find yourself of Marta Abba and the hamlet of Luciana.

6 places to visit in Fauglia, in the province of Pisa (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Giorgio Kienerk

Museums · Modern art

In the cells of the 19th-century prisons of the former pretura, he collects oil paintings, bas-reliefs, sculptures, engravings and lithographs of the Florentine Giorgio Kienerk (1869-1948), pupil of the macchiaioli Cecioni and Signorini, close to symbolism, divisionism and Liberty, who spent in Fauglia the summers from 1919 to death and painted the hills; the works were donated by his daughter Victoria. Open Saturday 9-13 and Sunday 15-19.

  • Where Via Chiostra prima 13, Fauglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com · terredipisa.it

Chiesa di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It was mentioned already in 1251, it collapsed with the earthquake of 1846 and was immediately rebuilt in a more central position, with simple facade and three naves inside. It preserves a relief in terracotta painted with the Madonna del Soccorso, the eighteenth-century altar of the Crucifix in polychrome marble, a Madonna of the Rosary of the eighteenth century and a San Lorenzo attributed to Luigi Secchi da Pisa.

  • Where Fauglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · terredipisa.it

Teatro Comunale di Fauglia

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Small circular theatre of almost 300 seats, born in 1852 when the Logge del Mercato were moved; it was then cinema and home of the end of the year and, after a long closing, was reopened by the City in 2014.

  • Where Piazza Trento, Fauglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: terredipisa.it

Centro storico, ville e Villa Trovarsi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Along the only road of the village there are villas and palaces of the XVII-XIX century, the Town Hall and the Market Square with the old cereal warehouses. Among the villas, loved by intellectuals and writers, Villa Find yourself by actress Marta Abba, who called it as the comedy dedicated by Pirandello, then center Pirandelli's studies; from the ridge glimpses among the most beautiful of the Pisan Hills.

  • Where Fauglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Luciana

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

A fraction once submerged by the sea in the Pleistocene, where fossils of shells and sperm whale bones were found. The church of Saint Lucia, dating back to 1740, contains the chapel of Our Lady of the Rosary, two seventeenth-century works attributed to the school of Pietro da Cortona and a nineteenth-century cycle in the apse; in the village also the church of the Company and the villas Justiniani and Melenchini, damaged by the earthquake of 1846.

  • Where Luciana, Fauglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: terredipisa.it
