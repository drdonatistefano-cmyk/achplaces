What to see in Brosso

Mining country of Canavese: the mines, closed in 1964, closed a cycle of twenty centuries and are told in the museum of Cà ad Martolo; on the high ground dominates the parish church of San Michele Arcangelo, consecrated in 1545.

3 places to visit in Brosso, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Mineralogico – Cà ad Martolo

Museums · Themed · Free entry

The closure of the mines in 1964 concluded a production cycle lasting twenty centuries; with donations of documents, photographs, tools and minerals the Municipality has set up the museum in the house Cà ad Martolo, which reconstructs the life of a family of miners-farmers in five sectors (territory, people, history, minerals, tools). On the ground floor is rebuilt the entrance of a mine. Open by reservation.

  • Where Via Giovan Battista Trono 5, Brosso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.brosso.to.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.brosso.to.it

Parco della Brossasca e siti metallurgici dell'Assa

History and architecture · Archaeology

Next to the museum, the Brossasca Park preserves ancient industrial buildings dedicated to the different phases of mineral processing; along the river Assa are visible roasting furnaces, washing basins, forges and mechanical sweaters used to produce iron without passing through the cast iron.

  • Where Lungo il torrente Assa, Brosso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.brosso.to.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.brosso.to.it

Parrocchiale di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

It rises on a hill overlooking the Serra d'Ivrea, according to the tradition on the ruins of a temple to the sun god; certain news started from the fourteenth century, was consecrated in 1545, assumed rustic Gothic forms in the sixteenth century and was expanded in 1700. Romanesque façade with pronaos on two columns and frescoes of San Michele; single nave interior with nine altars, 16th century paintings, Baroque presbytery and organ Bossi and Squarcina.

  • Where Via Enrico Migliore, Brosso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.brosso.to.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.brosso.to.it
