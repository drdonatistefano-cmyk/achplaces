What to see in Stigliano

Country on a ridge about 900 meters that marks the northern limit of the Calanchi and looks over the nearby woods; the Mother Church of the Assumption preserves a polyptych of Simone from Florence, the church of the convent of Sant'Antonio a wooden crucifix attributed to Fra Umile from Petralia, and not far away opens the Bosco di Montepiano.

5 places to visit in Stigliano, in the province of Matera (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa Madre di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Original nucleus of the seventeenth century on a previous structure, Baroque facade, central nave with golden wood vault and side naves with domes; in the crypt, ancient burial place, some frescoes have recently emerged. It preserves the polyptych of Simone da Firenze (1520) with the statue of the Madonna del Polittico in the center, coming from the destroyed convent of Sant'Antonio, a statue of the Assumption of a Venetian school donated in 1522, a painting of Sant'Anna with the Madonna of an anonymous Neapolitan master of the early fifteenth century and the wooden choir of the nineteenth century wanted by the archpriest Correale.

  • Where Via Chiesa 10, Stigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: comune.stigliano.mt.it · basilicataturistica.it

Chiesa e convento di Sant'Antonio da Padova

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry

Originally a convent church dedicated to Santa Maria la Nova, with an imposing façade in stone cones and bell tower with Arabic-style dome. The main work is the great wooden crucifix attributed to Fr. Umile da Petralia: according to tradition on 1 October 1656 the face of Christ turned and the country was freed from the plague. It preserves a Madonna and Child by Antonio Stabile (dated 1580 from the regional portal) and an Immaculate with a 18th century monk attributed to Domenico Guarino.

  • Where Vico III Giuseppe Zanardelli, Stigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: comune.stigliano.mt.it · basilicataturistica.it

L'angolo della memoria

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Space in the ancient part of the village, open to the public free of charge, with household objects, agricultural and work equipment, traditional costumes and a stone mill collected by Rocco Derosa and Antonio Fanelli; it includes a restored country house and a documentation centre with texts and images of local and regional history.

MiC card (updated to April 2020) indicates closed daily; contact the museum (0835 565179) before visiting

  • Where Via S. Colombo, Stigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · basilicataturistica.it

Bosco di Montepiano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Forest of 800 hectares included in the Parco di Gallipoli Cognato Piccole Dolomiti Lucane, with large columnar oaks up to 30 meters high among dense woods and rocky spires.

  • Where Bosco di Montepiano, Stigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it

Belvedere sul crinale

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village rises on a high ridge at about 900 meters that dominates the territory and marks the northern limit of the Calanchi; from here start excursions to the nearby woods.

  • Where Stigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it
