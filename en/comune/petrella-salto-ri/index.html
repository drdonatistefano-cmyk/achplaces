What to see in Petrella Salto

The town of Cicolano overlooking Lake Salto, the largest artificial basin of Lazio, created in 1940; above the village the Cenci Rocca, while in Borgo San Pietro rises the monastery of Santa Filippa Mareri, rebuilt stone for stone after the submersion, with its museum and works by Giorgio De Chirico.

Monastero e Museo di Santa Filippa Mareri

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

St. Philipp, who met St. Francis between 1221 and 1225, founded in 1228 the monastery of St. Peter de Molito, submerged in 1940 by the lake together with the old Borgo San Pietro: church and monastery were dismantled and rebuilt stone for higher stone, and the chapel preserves frescoes from the sixteenth century and the reliquary with the heart of the saint. The museum, opened in 1977 and re-assembled in 2000, exhibits capitals, coats of arms and bells from the 12th to the 18th century, the wooden portal of 1511 by Maestro Giacomo di Bernardino, a wooden Madonna of the 14th to the 15th century, the statue of Saint Filippa of the 15th century, tools of pharmacy and leather trunks of the Clarisse, as well as paintings by Giorgio De Chirico, including a self-portrait of 1960. Open every day 8.30-12.30 and 16-18; tel. 0746 558134.

Lago del Salto

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Created in 1940 by barring the Salto river to power the hydroelectric power station of Cotilia at the service of the Terni steel works, it is the largest artificial lake in Lazio, with wild banks jagged by coves similar to fjords. The ride by car or by bike crosses oak and chestnut woods; the beaches of Fiumata and Borgo San Pietro take a swim and rent pedalos, canoes and wakeboards, and divers can explore the old submersible village through specialized centres.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Rocca Cenci

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The fortress, linked to Beatrice Cenci and to a tragedy of the sixteenth century, is reached with a short climb from the center of Petrella Salto; from above the view extends over the village, the woods of Cicolano and Lake Salto.

  • Where Sopra il centro storico, Petrella Salto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Santuario di Santa Maria Apparì

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

On the way down to Borgo San Pietro, rises where in 1562 the Virgin appeared to a girl named Persiana. The bas-relief of the apparition is carried in procession the evening before the feast, the last Sunday of May, the day when cherries are blessed.

  • Where Strada per Borgo San Pietro, Petrella Salto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Grotta di Santa Filippa Mareri

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the hamlet of Piagge, you can reach it with the Pilgrim's Path, a mountain path in the woods; here the founder of the Clarisse of the place spent a year in prayer, and today the cave is a small rock church overlooking the mountains of Cicolo.

  • Where Piagge, Petrella Salto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
