What to see in Piedimonte Matese

The southern gate to the Matese massif was a centre of wool and textiles production between the sixteenth and eighteenth centuries thanks to the energy of two springs; in the historic center there are the Dominican complex of San Tommaso d'Aquino with the MuCiRaMa civic museum, the chapel of San Biagio frescoed and the Palazzo ducale dei Gaetani.

Museo civico «Raffaele Marrocco» (MuCiRaMa) e complesso di San Tommaso d'Aquino

Museums · City history

It occupies part of the Dominican monastery built between 1394 and 1414 on the initiative of Sveva Sanseverino, inhabited by the Dominicans until 1809; the cloister contains medieval frescoes. It collects the civic archaeological collection expanded by the founder Raffaele Marrocco, the lapidary room and the Bibliotheca Scriptorum loci (since 1953), the exhibition on the Sanniti ...Gens fortissima Italiae ... (2014) and, on the first floor, an exhibition from the Risorgimento to the Second World War; it was reopened on 16 June 2013.

Cappella di San Biagio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built at the end of the fourteenth century, probably for the Jacobucci family, as oratory for strangers and pilgrims, and national monument since 1926. It has an ogival portal and walls almost entirely covered by late-Gothic frescoes of the first decade of the fifteenth century, in two cycles: the first tells the history and miracles of St. Biagio, bishop and martyr.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Stone houses, eighteenth-century buildings and medieval palaces with ogival windows; in the center are the basilica of Santa Maria Maggiore, the church of the Most Holy Savior with interiors in polychrome marble and majolica floors and the Doge's Palace, residence of the Gaetani d'Aragona (feudatarians from 1383 to 1806) rebuilt between 1700 and 1714. At the top there is a Gothic church dedicated to St. John, with views of the valley of Alife.

  • Where Centro storico, Piedimonte Matese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it
