What to see in Sassari

The capital of the Cape of Sopra: Piazza d'Italia and the Fountain of Rosello, symbols of the city, the Gothic-Catalan cathedral of San Nicola, the Museo Sanna and the National Art Gallery, Santa Maria di Bethlem dei Candelieri and, out of town, the prenuragic altar of Monte d'Accoddi.

12 places to visit in Sassari, in the province of Sassari (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza d'Italia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The "Salotto dei Sassaresi" (Saints Hall), with the monument to Vittorio Emanuele II in the center; with the Fountain of Rosello is the symbol of the city.

  • Where Piazza d'Italia, Sassari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismosassari.it

Sources: turismosassari.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Fontana di Rosello

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Citation already in the Statuti del Duecento, it assumed the current aspect in 1605-1606: four statues of the seasons and twelve mouths for the months of the year.

  • Where Corso Trinità, Sassari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismosassari.it

Sources: turismosassari.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Cattedrale di San Nicola

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Raised in 1480 in Gothic-Catalan style, with baroque façade of the early eighteenth century; it preserves a fourteenth-century table of the Madonna with Child and the mausoleum of the Count of Moriana.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Sassari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismosassari.it

Sources: turismosassari.it · italia.it

Pinacoteca nazionale

Museums · Old masters

In the former Jesuit College built between the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries, more than four hundred works from the Middle Ages to the end of the twentieth century.

  • Where Sassari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismosassari.it

Sources: turismosassari.it

Museo archeologico nazionale «G. A. Sanna»

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 1931, it exhibits artifacts from the Paleolithic to today, with rooms dedicated to Monte d'Accoddi and Nuragic civilization.

  • Where Via Roma, Sassari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismosassari.it

Sources: turismosassari.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Palazzo di Città – Museo della Città

Museums · City history

Built in 1826-1829 on a project by G. Cominotti on the area of the medieval town hall, houses the City Museum and the Civic Theatre inspired by the Carignano di Torino.

  • Where Sassari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismosassari.it

Sources: turismosassari.it

Palazzo Ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

It rises between 1775 and 1805, the seat of the Town Hall with the Council Hall and the ancient chapel; the museum "Le Stanze e le Cantine del Duca" is also visited in the basement on a suspended walkway.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Cinta muraria

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Wall of the thirteenth century on Tuscan models, with four doors and 36 towers; remain short stretches in course Trinità, Via Torre Tonda and Piazza Sant'Antonio.

  • Where Corso Trinità, Sassari
  • Entry Free

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Santuario prenuragico di Monte d'Accoddi

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Sacred pyramid building with ramp, unique in Sardinia and the Mediterranean, with village and menhir: phases from the first half of the fourth millennium BC.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Domus de Janas di Montalè

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint
