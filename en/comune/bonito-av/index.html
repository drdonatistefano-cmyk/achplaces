What to see in Bonito

A hilly village of medieval origin between the valleys of Calore and Ufita, the birthplace of Salvatore Ferragamo: the castle with cylindrical towers, the walls painted by street art since 2011, the mother church with the urn of San Crescenzo and the Sanctuary of the Madonna della Neve.

Castello e Torre normanna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

A quadrangular castle built around 1130 by Guglielmo Gesualdo, relative of Ruggero II, on the border with the Pontifical Benevento, and enlarged in the fifteenth century with four cylindrical angular towers; in the mid-500s it became residence of the feudalists (Bonito, Orsini, D'Aquino, Pisanello, Garofalo) and in 1829 it was sold to private individuals. After the earthquakes of 1702 and 1980 there are two towers: the one on the Town Hall square contains paintings probably seventeenth century on the walls and on the wooden ceiling.

Street art nel borgo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Since 2011 the walls of the village have been decorated with works of urban art, on the idea of the BOCA Collective to retrain the village; Francisco Bosoletti, Millo, Tellas, Giulio Vesprini, Alex Senna, Milu Correch, Camilla Falsini, Guerrilla Spam and Bifido have worked there among others, and some works are dedicated to Salvatore Ferragamo, born in Bonito.

  • Where Centro storico, Bonito
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa madre dell'Assunta e urna di San Crescenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the right wall of the mother church there is a golden rococo urn (about 2 × 2 m) with the remains of the child martyr Crescenzo, who died at 11 years probably during the persecution of Diocletian: five centuries remained in the Roman cemetery of Santa Ciriaca, were found by Father Luigi Vincenzo Cassitto, native of Bonito, and transferred here on July 27, 1800 after the Papal authorization. Free access, reachable by wheelchair.

  • Where Via Roma 10, Bonito
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.bonito.av.it · italia.it

Santuario di Maria SS. della Neve

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

The Marian church was built on an ancient pagan temple, linked to the legend of an apparition of the end of the sixth century and visited, according to tradition, by Popes, Frederick II and St. Francis in 1222. The name comes from the snowfall that in the spring of 1492 blocked for more than forty days Charles VIII and his soldiers; there is venerated an icon of Byzantine style of Our Lady with Child. Free and free access, reachable by wheelchair.
