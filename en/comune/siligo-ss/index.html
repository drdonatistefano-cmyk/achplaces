What to see in Siligo

Logudoro-Meilogu village 400 meters on the slopes of Mount Sant'Antonio, about 30 km from Sassari, home of the singer Maria Carta and writer Gavino Ledda: in the territory the nuragic complex of Monte Sant'Antonio, the medieval churches of Santa Maria di Bubalis and Sant'Elia di Monte Santo and the volcanic craters of the Meilogu.

6 places to visit in Siligo, in the province of Sassari (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Maria Carta

Museums · House museums · Free entry

Two rooms of about 90 m2 on two levels dedicated to Maria Carta, singer, musicologist, poetess and actress born in Siligo in 1934 and died in Rome in 1994: sections on biography, music and research on the folk traditions Sardinian, poetry (with "Song of ritual" , 1975, unpublished manuscripts and a film in which she plays), theatre and cinema and commitment as city councilwoman in Rome; photos, stage costumes, prizes, press reviews and relics. Monday-Friday 10-13 and 16-19, Saturday and Sunday on request.

  • Where Via Aldo Moro 1, Siligo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.fondazionemariacarta.it

Sources: fondazionemariacarta.it · sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Complesso nuragico di Monte Sant'Antonio

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the north-western saddle of Mount Sant'Antonio, with ample view of the valleys, a complex linked to the cult of the waters with phases from the end of the Middle Bronze to the Iron Age: well temple with circular chamber of about 4.80 m in blocks of white tuff, paved square of about 15 x 13 m crossed by a canal in basalt, a round one with central fireplace, rectangular buildings with seat along the walls and a corridor towards a nuraghe of about 15 m of diameter in large basalt blocks. Digged in 1988-89 by Fulvia Lo Schiavo and Anna Sanna after severe vandalisms; near the medieval castle called Capula and the medieval village abandoned in the fifteenth century.

site not guarded; the municipal road of access is disconnected and passable with off-road

  • Where SP 23, località Funtana Carchinada, Siligo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Santa Maria di Bubalis (Nostra Signora di Mesumundu)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Campestre church in Biddanoa, zone of mineral springs and thermal baths: the Byzantine circular central body of the VI-VII century, about 7 m high and 10 m wide, covered with dome, rises on a Roman thermal building and preserves traces of the water system; the oldest parts are in opus listatum of bricks and black basalt. In the 11th century it passed to the Benedictines of Montecassino, who added the apse and a compartment to the north. Feast on Monday of the Angel; a trail of almost 6 km leads to Monte Santo.

  • Where Località Biddanoa, Siligo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di Sant'Elia di Monte Santo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Seat of a Benedictine hermitage on the summit plateau of Monte Santo, donated in 1065 by the judge Torchitorio-Barisone I of Torres, together with Santa Maria di Bubalis, to the abbey of Montecassino. It has two naves with barrel vaults: the north perhaps of the late XI century, the widening of the second half of the XII; traces of bichromy remain in the arches between the naves. From the 360 degree peak, on clear days to the Gulf of Asinara.

reachable with difficulty: from the SP 41 the last stretch of Monte Santo climbs on foot

  • Where Monte Santo, Siligo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Crateri vulcanici del Meilogu

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural monument since 1994: reliefs formed by volcanic activity of 500,000-600,000 years ago, with the Monte Santo torso of cone and Monte Ruju with its volcanic branches; Montiju Tundu, Badde Ortolu and sas Baddes the view sweeps over the terraced valleys dug by streams.

  • Where Territorio di Siligo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Centro storico, Santa Vittoria e luoghi di «Padre padrone»

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the center the parish church of Santa Vittoria, from which at the end of August the statue of San Vincenzo Ferreri goes in procession, and the oratory of Santa Croce with the oak cross of the Holy Week; in the village also the places of the novel and the film "Father master" of Gavino Ledda, born in Siligo.

  • Where Centro storico, Siligo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
