What to see in Alassio

Seaside resort of the Riviera di Ponente with the Wall of the lovers, the Budello parallel to the promenade and the Benedictine church of Santa Croce overlooking the sea.

8 places to visit in Alassio, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Muretto di Alassio

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The famous wall with works inspired by love, including Les Amoureux by Raymond Peynet and Gli innamorati by Ero Pellini, and a mailbox for love letters.
