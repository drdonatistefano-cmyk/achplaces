What to see in Romagnese

Medieval village of upper Oltrepò Pavese, not far from the Passo del Penice, grown around the castle of Dal Verme, in whose tower the museum of peasant civilization is located; also worth seeing is the parish church of San Lorenzo, the oratory of Penicina and the botanical garden of Pietra Corva.

6 places to visit in Romagnese, in the province of Pavia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello dei Dal Verme

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Castle built in the fourteenth century by the Dal Verme family, who ruled the territory; around it develop the houses of the village. The original complex remains the tower and a sandstone building with a 19th century portal; today it houses the town hall.

  • Where Centro del borgo, Romagnese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Museo civico di arte contadina nella torre del castello

Museums · Themed

Museum of the Lombard Apennines of the Four Provinces set up in the castle tower: it preserves tools, tools and machinery of the local agricultural and craft life.

  • Where Torre del castello, Romagnese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Oratorio della Madonna di Caravaggio a Penicina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Oratory of the hamlet Penicina dedicated to the Madonna di Caravaggio, with a Pisan-style facade with alternating bands of bricks and sandstone; the pillars date back to the 16th century.

  • Where Penicina, Romagnese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Giardino botanico di Pietra Corva

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

3-hectare garden founded by Dr. Antonio Ridella, at about 930-950 meters at the foot of Monte Pietra di Corvo, outskirts of dark volcanic rock. Thanks to a particular microclimate it hosts mountain, subalpine and alpine flora of the Alps, with Apennine, Pyrenean and other chains such as Himalayas and Andes; notable communities of Primula, Saxifraga, Gentiana and Iris. It is recommended to visit it with the staff, who informs about the accessibility and conditions of the trails (tel. +39 0382 597788).

closed from 15 September 2026 for extraordinary works at the water tanks to protect amphibians, reopened in spring 2027
