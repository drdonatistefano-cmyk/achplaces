What to see in Villa d'Almè

Common at the entrance of the Brembana Valley in the Parco dei Colli di Bergamo, mentioned already in 927 and in 1025 as "Villa Lemensis": on the hills the rural hamlets of Bruntino, San Mauro and Foresto, in the village the eighteenth-century parish church of the Saints Faustino and Giovita and the Civic Museum of Natural Sciences "Severo Sini" .

Museo civico di scienze naturali Severo Sini

Museums · Science and university · Free entry

Born in 1973 from the collections of naturalist Severo Sini (1930-1997), who collected herbs, minerals and prehistoric finds from the Tuscan-Emilian Apennines to the Karst and then participated in archaeological excavations in Nicaragua. Sections of mineralogy, zoology, malachology (shells), paleontology, a herbarium and a scientific library. Free admission: Tuesday 10-12, Saturday 9.30-12.30 and 15-18, before Sunday of the month 10-12.

  • Where Via Sereno Locatelli Milesi 16, Villa d'Almè
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museosini.org

Sources: museosini.org · comune.villadalme.bg.it · parcocollibergamo.it

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Faustino e Giovita

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church built between 1771 and 1795, indicated by the Parco dei Colli among the most important buildings of the municipality; the cult of San Faustino in Villa is already attested in a parchment of 927.

  • Where Villa d'Almè
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.parcocollibergamo.it

Sources: parcocollibergamo.it · comune.villadalme.bg.it

Frazioni collinari di Bruntino, San Mauro e Foresto

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the hills of the Parco dei Colli di Bergamo, the hamlets of Bruntino, San Mauro and Foresto I and II preserve traditional rural architecture. Bruntino, joined Alme in 1926, returned autonomous in 1948.

  • Where Bruntino, San Mauro e Foresto, Villa d'Almè
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.parcocollibergamo.it

Sources: parcocollibergamo.it · comune.villadalme.bg.it
