What to see in Campli

Borgo at the foot of the Laga Mountains with the Santa Scala to be climbed on knees, the Palazzo Farnese and, in the convent of San Francesco, the furnishings of the pre-Roman necropolis of Campovalano.

7 places to visit in Campli, in the province of Teramo (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Scala Santa e Chiesa di San Paolo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

28-step staircase in oak wood that the pilgrims climbed on their knees to obtain the plenary indulgence; the name dates back to 1772.

  • Where Campli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Archeologico Nazionale di Campli

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the thirteenth century convent of San Francesco, inaugurated in 1989, exhibits the accessories of the necropolis of Campovalano (scavations since 1967), with the rich tombs of the VII-VI century BC: jewelry, a necklace in magnagreca gold foil, weapons, bucchero and a Phoenician head in vitreous paste.

  • Where Piazza San Francesco 6, Campli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museiabruzzo.cultura.gov.it · italia.it
