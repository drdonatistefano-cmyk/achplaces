What to see in Cerreto Sannita

Ceramic city at the gates of the Regional Park of Matese, rebuilt after the earthquake of 1688 with a regular and wallless system: Piazza San Martino, the Cathedral of the Holy Trinity, the Civic Museum of Ceramics and, in the surrounding area, the Hannibal Bridge, the gorges of the Titerno and the Leonessa.

Museo civico e della ceramica cerretese

Museums · Themed

Housed in the former eighteenth-century convent of Sant'Antonio, it is one of the main attractions of the village. The ceramics of Cerrete lived the golden period in the eighteenth century, after the earthquake of 1688, when Neapolitan workers joined the local ceramists; it was also appreciated at the Bourbon court.

  • Where Ex convento di Sant'Antonio, Cerreto Sannita
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.cerretosannita.bn.it

Piazza San Martino e Collegiata di San Martino

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Large square with baroque palaces and the Collegiate Church of San Martino, designed by the royal engineer G. B. Manni was completed in 1733, with the facade preceded by a curved ramp staircase. The Palazzo del Genio is also located on the square.

  • Where Piazza San Martino, Cerreto Sannita
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Cattedrale della Santissima Trinità

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Cathedral of 1739, next to the Episcopio (1696) and the former Bishop's Seminary, frescoed in 1780 by Francesco Palumbo; it has 12 marble altars and two bell towers with semi-cupole covered with yellow-green majolica.

  • Where Cerreto Sannita
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Gennaro e Museo di arte sacra

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Baroque church with elliptical dome covered with majolica hembrics; it houses the sacred art section of the Ceramic Museum, with a calendar of reliquary cesellato that houses relics of various saints and a fragment of the Cross.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Centro storico settecentesco e palazzi signorili

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Rebuilt after the earthquake of 1688 with anti-seismic criteria, with regular plan, without walls and with large avenues; private palaces of the eighteenth century (Ciaburro, Ungaro, del Viceconte, Magnati, Nardella, Giordani, Carizza, Villa Langer) with portals to bugs and masks, and road plates and furnishings decorated in ceramic.

  • Where Cerreto Sannita
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Ponte di Annibale

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Roman ass-back bridge on the river Titerno, 13 m long and about 1.50 m wide, with a light of 9.15 m; tradition ties him to the passage of Hannibal in the Second Punic War. It is reached by a path north of the village, near the road to Cusano Mutri.

  • Where Sentiero a nord di Cerreto Sannita
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Morgia Sant'Angelo (la «Leonessa»)

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

A 35 m high limestone block to the east of the village, shaped by the atmospheric elements with feline profile; the cave, inhabited in the Neolithic, was transformed by the Longobards into a chapel dedicated to the Archangel Michael. Wide view.

  • Where A est di Cerreto Sannita
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it
