What to see in Besano

Borgo della Valceresio, part of the Italian-Swiss Palaeontological site of Monte San Giorgio, UNESCO World Heritage Site: the Civic Museum of Fossil Exhibits the Besanosaurus, while the fossil path leads to the Rio Ponticelli Park and the former shale mines; in the village the parish church of San Martino and, above, the Colle San Martino with a view of the Ceresio.

Museo civico dei fossili di Besano

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Founded in 1981 and renovated in 2020 in a building from the beginning of the 20th century in the centre, it tells in five rooms the tropical sea of about 240 million years ago (average Triassic): marine reptiles, fish, invertebrates, fossil plants and history of discoveries, started in the mid-nineteenth century by the Museum of Natural History of Milan. The strong piece is the Besanosaurus, ichthyosaur of almost 6 meters with four embryos in the belly; also presented the Saltriosaur, the first great Italian carnivorous dinosaur. Open Tuesday and Saturday 14-17.30, Sunday 10.30-17.30.

  • Where Via Prestini 5, Besano
  • Entry €4.50, reduced students €3, free under 4 years, residents and Lombardy Museums Card
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museodibesano.it

Sources: museodibesano.it · comune.besano.va.it · italia.it

Parco Rio Ponticelli, sentiero dei fossili ed ex miniere

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Park built by the Municipality in 2019, a 20 minute walk from the village along the fossil path: hundreds of fossils emerged in the excavations of the 1970s and 1980s in the riverbed of the Rio Ponticelli, which contributed to the UNESCO recognition. Another 15 minutes lead to the former bituminous shale mines, from which light bulb oil and ichthyol were obtained and where fossils were discovered in the nineteenth century; viewpoint on Prealpi, Switzerland and Ceresio. The ring of Varese Turismo da Besano is 5 km, about 2 hours, easy.

  • Where Piazzale Monte San Giorgio, Besano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.besano.va.it · italia.it · vareseturismo.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

17th century church dedicated to the patron saint San Martino di Tours, with eighteenth-century paintings by Petrini da Carona, Croce di Varese, Valtorta and Maroni, the ancient marble main altar of the Viggiute Giovanni Maria Giudici and Giovanni Rizzi and the baroque sacristy carved by the hermit friar Antonio Rigola, author with the same collaborators of confessionals and pulpit. The stone bell tower of Viggiù, dating back to 1804, is the highest in the area.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa, Besano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.besano.va.it · italia.it

Colle San Martino

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill, probably Celtic fortification then Roman castrum guarding the streets of Ceresio, stands a small Marian church of Romanesque system rebuilt in the eighteenth century, with seventeenth-century decorations and a Madonna with Child traditionally fifteenth-century; higher up a stone with the statue of the Madonna. The Via Crucis of 14 chapels rises from the square, repainted by Franco Vasconi and restored in 1988-89; view of the village, Valceresio, Ceresio and Prealpi.

  • Where Da Piazza della Chiesa, Besano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.besano.va.it · italia.it
