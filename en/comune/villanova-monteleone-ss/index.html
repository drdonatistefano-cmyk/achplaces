What to see in Villanova Monteleone

The village of Alghero, 16 km from the sea and 14 km from the coast, rich in archaeology: the Neolithic necropolis of Puttu Codinu and the nuragic park of Appiu on the plateau of Chentu Mannas; in the village the Gothic-Aragonese parish of San Leonardo, outside the sanctuary of Our Lady of Interrios, and on the sea the beach of Pòglina.

Parco archeologico Nuraghe Appiu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the plateau of Chentu Mannas, about 500 meters at the foot of Mount Cuccu, overlooking the coast from Capo Caccia to Capo Marargiu: quadrilobed nuraghe with 15 meters high mastium and two overlapping rooms, covered courtyard, village of about 200 huts of the beginning of the Iron Age (X-IX century BC), tomb of giants in the cork grove, single tower nuraghe Punta 'e su Crabile, two dolmen, menhir and megalithic enclosure with megagon temple. Entrance fee; located 150 meters from the car park on easy path.

  • Where Località Monte Cuccu, SP 12 direzione Bosa, Villanova Monteleone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · idese.cultura.gov.it

Necropoli di Puttu Codinu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Nine domus de janas excavated on two rocky outings near the rivers Temo and Curos, used by the Neolithic end at the ancient age of Bronze (cultures of Ozieri, Campaniforme and Bonnanaro); known since 1903 and excavated in 1987-88. The tomb VIII stands out, with beams and roof of carved hut, where a statue of goddess mother was found, and the tomb IX with ritual fireplace and cups in the floor; on the walls carved taurine horns. Open Tuesday-Sunday 10-13 and 15-18, with guided tours.

  • Where SS 292, Villanova Monteleone
  • Entry €2, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Leonardo da Limoges

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A 16th century church in Gothic-Aragonese style, rebuilt in neo-Gothic forms in 1789, which preserves two wooden altars of the eighteenth century; feast on 11 June. In the village also the seventeenth-century church of the Madonna del Rosario and the oratory of Santa Croce of the sixteenth century.

  • Where Centro storico, Villanova Monteleone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Santuario di Nostra Signora di Interrios

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

A rural church erected in the 16th century about 3 km from the village among secular trees, with a modified facade in the 18th century; there are the Nativity of the Virgin on 8 September and St. John the Baptist at the end of August.

Spiaggia di Pòglina

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village's marina, about half a kilometer of clear and thin sand south of the Riviera del Corallo between Alghero and Bosa, with low seabed and outlying rocks and Mediterranean maquis behind; often moved by the mistral, is loved by surfers and windsurfers. To the north is the ancient tower of Poglina of the Spanish era, next to the church of Hope; restaurant, rental equipment and parking suitable for campers.

  • Where Litoranea Alghero-Bosa (SP 49), Villanova Monteleone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Monte Minerva

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Bastion from the flat summit, residue of a volcano extinguished and today covered by a forest; at its slopes a tomb of eight domus de janas.
