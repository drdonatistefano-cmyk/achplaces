What to see in Urgnano

Borgo della piania bergamasca at the intersection of the ancient streets Cremasca and Francesca, dominated by the Rocca Albani, Visconti castle then Colleoni and Albani; in Piazza Libertà the parish church of Saints Nazario and Celso with the stone bell tower of Luigi Cagnola, just outside the small Sistine of the Holy Trinity and, in the hamlet Basella, the sanctuary and the African Museum.

Rocca Albani

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Visconti Castle enlarged in 1354 at the behest of Giovanni Visconti, passed with Venice in a feud to Bartolomeo Colleoni, then to the Longhi and from 1539 to Cardinal Gian Gerolamo Albani; in 1842 Wenceslas Albani restored it, created the hanging garden and rebuilt the Ghibelline Merlature, and since 1953 it is part of the municipality. It preserves moat, mastic with secret passages, fifteenth-century staircase, courtyard with Renaissance porticoes, frescoed rooms (Late 16th century grotesques, Four Seasons), room of the Maps and walkway; guided tours by reservation.

  • Where Via Rocca 108, Urgnano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.urgnanoturistica.it

Chiesa dei Santi Nazario e Celso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Neoclassical parish church started in 1762 on the project of Count Alessandro Alessandri and inaugurated in 1783, with the façade of Vittore Farina in Brembate strain; about 70 meters long, it has six lateral altars. It preserves a deposed Christ attributed to Tintoretto, twelve statues of apostles, two wooden pulpits of the Fantoni school and frescoed by Servalli in the restoration of 1924.

  • Where Piazza Libertà, Urgnano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.urgnanoturistica.it

Sources: urgnanoturistica.it

Torre campanaria del Cagnola

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Campanile designed by Luigi Cagnola, the architect of the Arch of Peace in Milan, built between 1824 and 1829 in a gentle strain of Brembate: 54 meters high, circular plan with three cylinders degrading in the Doric, Ionic and Corinthian orders and a balcony of caryatids under the dome; inside, a spiral staircase of 235 steps. The tourist portal indicates it as the highest stone tower in Italy; today it has 12 bells.

  • Where Piazza Libertà, Urgnano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.urgnanoturistica.it

Sources: urgnanoturistica.it

Chiesa della Santissima Trinità («piccola Sistina»)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small fifteenth-century church outside the ancient walls, at the crossroads between Francesca and Cremasca, restored in 1979 by the Alps. In the presbytery the Trinity with the Apostles and the Assumption; on the walls a cycle inspired by the Sistine Chapel, from Creation to the golden calf, and in counterfaced the Last Judgment, of a painter perhaps near Callisto da Lodi.

  • Where Via San Filippo Neri 6, Urgnano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.urgnanoturistica.it

Sources: urgnanoturistica.it

Santuario della Basella

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In memory of an apparition of Our Lady in 1356; around 1460 Bartolomeo Colleoni had the church enlarged and in 1462 the Dominicans of Santo Stefano di Bergamo arrived there, who joined a convent.

  • Where Piazza Santuario 10, Basella, Urgnano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.urgnanoturistica.it

Sources: visitbergamo.net · urgnanoturistica.it

Museo d'arte e cultura africana «Villaggio Africano»

Museums · Themed

In the convent built by Bartolomeo Colleoni, passed in 1920 by the Dominicans to the Passionists, who in 1984 created the museum dedicated to the culture of sub-Saharan Africa; the proceeds go to their missions in Kenya and Tanzania. You can touch objects, participate in workshops and follow a multimedia guide.

  • Where Convento della Basella, Urgnano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.urgnanoturistica.it

Sources: visitbergamo.net · urgnanoturistica.it
