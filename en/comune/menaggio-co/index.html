What to see in Menaggio

Borgo Bandiera Arancione on the western shore of Lake Como, divided between the upper medieval part, with the cobbled streets and the remains of the castle destroyed in 1523, and the nineteenth-century lakefront with walks, hotels and the Rationalist Lido of 1934; in the hamlet of Loveno the Villa Mylius Vigoni with the English park, behind the Val Sanagra Park.

Lungolago, piazza Garibaldi e Lido

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The 19th-century lakefront, tree-lined, with hotels like the Grand Hotel Victoria of 1885, offers views of Bellagio and Varenna; the Long Lake Benedetto Castelli the Monument to the marble weaver of Carrara by Francesco Somaini, donated by the Mantero family, and along Via Roma the Lido of 1934, example of rationalist architecture with beach and pool. Piazza Garibaldi, the heart of the village, is surrounded by cafes and restaurants.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Menaggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: menaggio.com · italia.it

Borgo del Castello e Chiesa di San Carlo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval castle, perhaps between the 8th and 10th centuries, controlled the Via Regina at the crossroads towards the Alto Lago and Lake Lugano and was destroyed in 1523 by the Grigionese militias: the perimeter walls remain, followed by the houses of Via Castellino. On the ruins stands the church of San Carlo, built in 1614 by Cinzio Calvi as a sepulchre for himself and his wife, with a sailing bell tower in Spanish style; along the bas-relief streets of the 11th and 12th centuries, like the head of the bull of San Luca and the fountain of the Savior.

  • Where Via Castellino da Castello, Menaggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: menaggio.com · in-lombardia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Of ancient origins, with the entrance to the ancient Strada Regina: three aisles frescoed in 1899 by Tagliaferri, with the martyrdom of Saint Stephen in the vault of the apse, two works by the painter Menaggino Castelli in the presbytery, a copy of the Madonna di Menaggio by Bernardino Luini (the original is at the Louvre) and seventeenth-century stuccos by the masters of the Sacred Heart. Open every day 8-19.

  • Where Via Annetta Lusardi, Menaggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · menaggio.com · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Marta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

16th century church in via Calvi: on the façade the Roman plaque of Minicio Esorato, imperial official of the first century, found in the lake and located here by the humanist Francesco Calvi, and a key of fifteenth century arch with the castle of Menaggio, today municipal coat of arms; inside seventeenth century paintings and a medieval source.

  • Where Via Calvi, Menaggio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: menaggio.com · italia.it

Villa Mylius Vigoni e Loveno

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The 19th-century villa in the hamlet of Loveno, purchased by Mylius in 1829 and enlarged to house the collections of paintings, statues and furnishings, with an amphitheater-style English park designed by Giuseppe Balzaretto; left by Ignazio Vigoni to the Federal Republic of Germany, is the seat of the Italian-German Centre for European Dialogue. Guided tours by reservation (tel. 0344 361232). In Loveno there are also the villas Bel Faggio, Bolzano, Garovaglio Ricci and Govone and the parish church of Santi Lorenzo and Agnese (1725-1728).

  • Where Via Giulio Vigoni 1, Loveno di Menaggio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.villavigoni.eu
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: villavigoni.eu · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Nobiallo e Santuario della Regina della Pace

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fishing village with narrow streets and steps towards the lake, the parish church of Saints Bartholomew and Nicola with Romanesque bell tower dating back to the 13th century and the 17th century sanctuary in a panoramic position, whose name recalls the peace of the Pyrenees of 1659; from here the ancient Strada Regina rises to the Sasso Rancio.

  • Where Nobiallo, Menaggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · menaggio.com · italia.it

Belvedere La Crocetta e Linea Cadorna

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Monte Crocetta was part of the fortifications of the First World War (Linea Cadorna), with trenches and restored walkways; the small church of San Maurizio, built in 1975 by the Alpini di Menaggio with two frescoes by Bruno Azimonti, overlooks a shrine in the bunker. From the viewpoint, about 30 minutes of steep climb from Cross, views of the Lake Centre, the Lecco branch, the Orobie and the Upper Lake.

  • Where Croce, Menaggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · menaggio.com · italia.it

Anello nel Parco Val Sanagra

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring hike of 10 km (3 and a half hours, medium difficulty) from Piazza Garibaldi: Loveno, the Piamuro alpine pasture, the Galli furnace, the Chioderia (former mill and kiosk), the Carliseppi mill, the monolith Sass Corbée, the lime furnace and the village of Barna.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Menaggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: menaggio.com · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Orrido della Val Sanagra

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Ring path of 5 km (about 2 hours) from Piazza Garibaldi through Loveno, Piamuro, Tobi, Cardano and Burgatto, in the gorge where the Sanagra flows between high walls: excavated sections in the rock and suspended bridges, not recommended for those suffering from vertigo.

The tourist site of the Municipality indicates it closed by landslides from 8 July 2024, without reopening date

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Menaggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: menaggio.com · italia.it
