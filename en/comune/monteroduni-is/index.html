What to see in Monteroduni

Borgo on a rocky spur at the foot of the Matese, overlooking the Volturno valley, with the historic centre of Longobard plant gathered around the church and the Castle Pignatelli, feud of the Pignatelli since 1668.

Castello Pignatelli

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Of probable Longobard origin, under control of the Volturno valley against Saracen incursions, it had a walls in Norman times; from 1503 to 1668 it was the D'Afvolta, then the Pignatelli, who transformed it into residence. A door with a Renaissance balcony and a staircase lead to the loggia; on the first floor the hall of representation has a large marble fireplace, a terracotta floor with the coat of arms of the Pignatelli (three 'pignate's) and a wooden ceiling of the eighteenth century painted in tempera with chivalry motifs, while a tower preserves a room covered with majolica. On the second floor it runs the way of rounds with slits and cadio; on the ground floor kitchens, ovens and cellars with large barrels. Visits on Saturday and Sunday (10:30-12:30 and 17:00-18:30) only by reservation at least 2 days before, on phone 0865 491586.

  • Where Via Principe Pignatelli 1, Monteroduni
  • Entry €5, students €2, free up to 7 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Centro storico e chiese di Santa Maria in Altissimis e San Michele Arcangelo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historic centre preserves the Longobard urban layout, with medieval alleyways and houses gathered around the church and castle, on a rocky spur overlooking the Volturno valley. The ancient churches of Santa Maria in Altissimis and San Michele Arcangelo preserve sacred works.

  • Where Centro storico, Monteroduni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: visitmolise.eu
