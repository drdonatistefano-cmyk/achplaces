What to see in Penne

City of bricks on the hills of the residences, among the most beautiful villages in Italy: the Cathedral known from 868 with the Romanesque crypt, churches and palaces between Renaissance and Baroque and, just outside, the Reserve of Lake Penne, the first WWF oasis of Italy.

7 places to visit in Penne, in the province of Pescara (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale di San Massimo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Note from 868 when the relics of St. Maximus were transferred to the church: 13th century rose window, 14th century bell tower, 9th century Romanesque crypt, 15th century terracotta pilasters and 12th century bishop Odorisio's altar.

  • Where Penne
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it
