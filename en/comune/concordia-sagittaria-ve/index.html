What to see in Concordia Sagittaria

Heir to the Roman colony of Iulia Concordia, it preserves around the cathedral of Santo Stefano the Byzantine baptistery of 1089, the bell tower of 1150 and the archaeological areas with the early Christian remains of the Trichora Martyrum and the Basilica Apostolorum, as well as the Roman bridge, the thermal baths and the walls of the ancient city.

Cattedrale di Santo Stefano, campanile e battistero

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Cathedral built between VII and VIII century, rebuilt in 1466 and enlarged at the beginning of the twentieth century, with Venetian façade that faithfully reconstructs the fifteenth century; next to the massive bell tower of 1150 and the Byzantine baptistery of 1089, frescoed in the same period.

  • Where Centro storico, Concordia Sagittaria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Area archeologica sotto la Cattedrale

History and architecture · Archaeology

The remains of Julia Concordia: two Roman sepulchral enclosures and the ruins of two early Christian buildings of the fourth century, the Trichora Martyrum and the Basilica Apostolorum, built on an older house; it also opens with guided visits and extraordinary openings of the Ministry.

  • Where Presso la cattedrale, Concordia Sagittaria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it
