What to see in Merate

A hilly town of the Brianza Lecchese of pre-Roman origins, known for the complex of Villa Belgiojoso with the large garden terraces and for the headquarters of Merate of the Astronomical Observatory of Brera (INAF), with the domes of the telescopes Ruths and Zeiss open for visits.

2 places to visit in Merate, in the province of Lecco (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Osservatorio Astronomico di Brera, sede di Merate

Museums · Science and university

The Merate headquarters of the Astronomical Observatory of Brera (INAF) offers daily visits every first Friday of the month, from 10:30 am to 11:30 am: an astronomical guide accompanies Ruths and Zeiss in the park and in the two domes of the telescopes, telling the story of the observatory, today's research and the technology of the laboratories, with explanations designed also for non-experts. There are also Open Nights with visits to workshops and evening observations of the sky, usually 3-4 times a month with the Moon in the first quarter. Online booking via POEFactory; the path in the park is on gravel paths slightly uphill.

the source indicates that the day-to-day visit from January 2027 will be paid, without fee

Villa Belgiojoso Brivio Sforza e giardini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

It is the most famous complex of Merate: U-shaped building with a large terrace garden, inhabited for centuries by the same families, hence the name (Brivio Sforza, Trivulzio, Barbiano di Belgiojoso d'Este). The FAI opened its doors to the Fall Days on October 11-12, 2025, with a path between facade and porch, Italian garden, English park, French garden and the carpinata that leads to the viewpoint.

  • Where Via Roma 26, Merate
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
