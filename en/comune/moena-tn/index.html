What to see in Moena

Porta della Val di Fassa and Ladin village between Catinaccio, Latemar and Monzoni, in the Alpine Pearls since 2011: above the center of squares and districts dominates the church of San Vigilio, while the memory of the Great War lives in the exhibition La Gran Vera at the Navalge Theatre and in the stable-museum of Someda, next to the Austro-Hungarian fort of 1898.

8 places to visit in Moena, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di San Vigilio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

In an elevated position with a view of the Val San Pellegrino and the Monzoni, it was consecrated in 1164, reconsecrated in 1216 and again in 1534 after the Gothic reconstruction desired by Bernardo Clesio; it retains the Gothic part and the original apse, while the current aspect is due to the extension on a project by Giovanni Tiella (1929). The three-nave interior houses works by the Moenese painter Valentino Rovisi, the golden wooden altar by Valentino Poschiavin (1715), the main altar by Martino Gabrielli and the crucifix, the windows and the portals by Cirillo Dellantonio.

  • Where Strada Don G. Iori 10, Moena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · fassa.com

Chiesa di San Volfango

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Dedicated to the German patron saint of the woodsmen, it preserves a cycle of frescoes from the middle of the fifteenth century; the origins are uncertain and could be the first place of worship of the country, consecrated in 1164.

  • Where Moena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: fassa.com · visittrentino.info

La Gran Vera - mostra sulla Grande Guerra

Museums · City history

Themed exhibition opened in 2014 at the Navalge Theatre, promoted by Istituto Culturale Ladin, association On the front of memories and municipalities: exhibits the vast Federspiel-Caimi collection with finds of the Ladin de Fascia Museum and private, on about 500 m2 and three floors, in four sections (Galizia 1914, La trenches, Dolomites 1915, War on War) with dioramas, vintage photos, audio, video and reconstructions of trenches, posts and caves; every year a new temporary section. Accessible to people with disabilities.

  • Where Piaz de Navalge 4, Moena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.granvera.it

Sources: granvera.it · visittrentino.info

Museo Sul fronte dei ricordi (Stala de la Nenola)

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Set up in 2007 in an old stable in Someda, it collects private collections and finds recovered in the restoration of the posts of the Costabella, Monzoni and Cima Bocche front: photographs, weapons, ammunition, uniforms and objects of the soldiers, with reconstructions of a trench, a machine gun nest and a comrade; it tells about the three years of Moena near the front line. Without fixed hours: visits in other days on request telephone for groups of at least 8 people.

  • Where Strada Frate Emilio Chiocchetti, Someda, Moena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.frontedeiricordi.it

Sources: frontedeiricordi.it · cultura.gov.it · fassa.com · visittrentino.info

Forte di Someda

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Impressive Austro-Hungarian construction of 1898, already obsolete at the outbreak of the Great War and therefore used only as a deposit; it is seen from the outside, next to the exhibition of Someda.

  • Where Someda, Moena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: fassa.com · visittrentino.info

Bottega del bottaio «L Pinter»

Museums · Themed

Section inaugurated in 1984 in the old house of the Presmessaria, already workshop of the workshop Domenico Dellantonio (1851-1937): the tools are ordered for processing phases, with vats, buckets, containers for grain and other household objects typical of the Fassano crafts of the workshop.

  • Where Via Dolomiti, Moena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.istladin.net

Sources: istladin.net · cultura.gov.it · visittrentino.info

Centro di Moena

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Squares, fountains and districts with shops, cafes and buildings linked to Ladin culture; in Piaz de Ramon, Piaz de Sotegrava and Piazzatta Perla Alpina are held the Fairy Markets (edition 2026 from November 28 to January 6), with the pedestrian center in the late afternoon.

  • Where Piaz de Navalge, Moena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info
