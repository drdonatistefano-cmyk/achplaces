Museo archeologico nazionale di Calatia

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 2003 at the Casino of Starza Penta, Masseria in 1552 and then hunting casino of the Carafa of Stadera, lords of Maddaloni since 1465, of which remain the coat of arms on the entrance vault, original decorations and an eighteenth-century chapel; here on April 9, 1734 the Neapolitan nobility symbolically delivered the keys of the city to Carlo di Borbone. On two floors the remains of the necropolis and the town of Calatia, from the end of the 8th century BC to the late Roman age: a section on the Roman streets with room immersive on the stretch of Calatia-Rome of the Appia and one on the feminine costume with rings, fibulas and scarabs. Convitto Nazionale Giordano Bruno e chiesa di Sant'Antonio da Padova

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

A former Franciscan convent that the tradition ties to the passage of St. Francis in 1220; after the suppression of the convents decreed by Giuseppe Bonaparte in 1807 it became a national boarding house, attended from 1821 to 1827 by Luigi Settembrini, who later named it at Giordano Bruno. Portale in piperno of 1758; in the living room the ceiling houses the largest canvas in the world (700 sq m), painted in 1756 by Giovanni Funaro and workshop with the Immaculate Conception, St. Francis and St. Anthony within architectural squares. In the adjacent church of Sant'Antonio da Padova, on the sides of the marble altar, two angels, head of the altar by Giuseppe Sammartino (1761).

Chiesa della SS. Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of Gothic origin remodelled in the seventeenth century: the coffered ceiling of 1604 is the work of the Florentine Giovanni Balducci and in the chapel of the transept there is the altarpiece "Christ tempted by Satan" by Mattia Preti.
