Castel del Monte

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built by Federico II from 1240 on a hill 540 meters on the western Murge: octagonal plan with eight towers on the edges and eight rooms per floor, monofore windows on the ground floor and bifore on the upper floor, except the trifora facing Andria; three spiral staircases of 44 steps, facades in limestone and white marble and an arc portal with classic tympanum. UNESCO heritage since 1996.

  • Where Castel del Monte, Andria
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it · unesco.cultura.gov.it

Parco nazionale dell'Alta Murgia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The western Murge plateau, where you enter, climbing from Andria towards Castel del Monte, which rises inside the park.

  • Where Castel del Monte, Andria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta e San Riccardo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Romanesque, wanted by the Norman counts above a Byzantine church that forms the crypt, where according to tradition Jolanda of Brienne and Isabella of England, wives of Frederick II; the chapel of St. Richard was built in 1438 by Francesco II Del Balzo; bell tower of the XII-XIV century and wooden ceiling of the transept.

  • Where Centro storico, Andria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museodiocesanoandria.it · italia.it

Porta Sant'Andrea

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The only survivor of the Urbian doors, of Swabian origin and rebuilt in 1585 with volute pedione; bears the inscription dedicated by Frederick II to the andriesi ...Andria fidelis nostris affixa medullis.

  • Where Via Federico II di Svevia, Andria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sigecweb.beniculturali.it · italia.it

Palazzo ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The residence of the lords of Andria, from Del Balzo to Carafa who rebuilt it after 1552: three floors in limestone around courtyards, with chapel, two external monumental stairs and the Carafa coat of arms on the facade.

  • Where Via La Corte, Andria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sigecweb.beniculturali.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Agostino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Dedicated to San Leonardo when it belonged to the Teutonic Knights (1230-1358), then to the Augustinians: portal of the 13th century with Christ between San Leonardo and San Nicola in the lunette and two lions on the sides; on the side portal the coats of arms of the Teutonics, the Angiò and the architect Sergio Bove.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Agostino 1, Andria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museodiocesanoandria.it · italia.it

Chiostro di Sant'Agostino

Local life and traditions · Historic markets · Free entry

The covered cloister of the former Augustinian convent, with figurative capitals, now used as a food market; the convent houses the public library.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Agostino, Andria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museodiocesanoandria.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Started in the second half of the thirteenth century and completed in 1346 by Bonanno da Barletta: unique nave with polychrome marbles and stuccoes of the eighteenth century, two portals and a Gothic monophorus; the cloister, once frescoed with stories of St. Francis, hosts cultural events.

  • Where Centro storico, Andria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museodiocesanoandria.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Domenico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Founded in 1398 for the legacy of Sveva Orsini, widow of Francesco Del Balzo: sixteenth-century portal with the Madonna and Child in a shell, unique nave with four altars in polychrome marble of Neapolitan taste; in sacristy the mummified body of Duke Francesco II Del Balzo.

  • Where Centro storico, Andria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museodiocesanoandria.it · italia.it

Basilica di Santa Maria dei Miracoli

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Born after the discovery in 1576 of an image of the Madonna and Child in the blade of Santa Margherita, on three levels: the grotto with the marble and silver temple, the intermediate church of the Benedictines with frescoes of the Passion, prophets and sibilles, and the upper church of Cosimo Fanzago with coffered ceiling of 1633.

  • Where Lama di Santa Margherita, Andria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museodiocesanoandria.it · italia.it

Chiesa rupestre di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church excavated in tuff in the 10th century in the Lagnoni district, enlarged between the Three and the fifteenth century: it preserves the frescoes of the Inventio Crucis with the history of Elena in search of the Cross, a rare subject in Puglia.

  • Where Quartiere Lagnoni, Andria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museodiocesanoandria.it · italia.it

Museo diocesano San Riccardo

Museums · Old masters

Established in 1972 and today in Palazzo Margiotta: on the first floor the history of the diocese up to the nineteenth century with sacred works, the second renewal of the cult of St. Richard, with the relics of the saint in the golden wooden urn, the silver bust, the mitre and the pastoral.

  • Where Via De Anellis 48, Andria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museodiocesanoandria.it · italia.it

Museo del Confetto «Giovanni Mucci»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the ancient jam shop opened in 1894 by Nicola Mucci, in a liberty building in the historic center: four rooms with documents, tools, equipment and moulds collected in over a century of confectionery production.

  • Where Via Museo del Confetto 12, Andria
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
