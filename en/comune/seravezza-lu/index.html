Palazzo Mediceo di Seravezza

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built by Cosimo I between 1561 and 1564 as a defensive outpost and residence for visits to the marble quarries, in a valley with the Apuans in the background: UNESCO heritage with the other Medici villas.
