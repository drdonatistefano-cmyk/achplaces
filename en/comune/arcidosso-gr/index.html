What to see in Arcidosso

Borgo dell'Amiata dominated by the Rocca Aldobrandesca, which houses the David Lazzaretti Study Centre, the Museum of Medieval Landscape, the collection of arms and the MACO; in the surroundings the sanctuary of the Madonna delle Grazie, the Romanesque parish church of Santa Maria ad Lamulas and Monte Labbro with the Jurisdavid Tower and the Faunistic Park.

Castello Aldobrandesco e Museo del paesaggio medievale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Among the best preserved medieval castles of Amiata, developed by a palace perhaps erected by the Marquis Ugo of Tuscany between 970 and 995, expanded by the Aldobrandeschi, besieged by the Sienese in 1331 under Guidoriccio da Fogliano and then passed to the Medici State. Inside the collection of arms Emo Mecheroni (about 175 weapons and helmets from the Middle Ages to the twentieth century, first floor) and, on the top floor, the Museum of the medieval landscape on the fortifications of Amiata and Maremma, with finds of Castel Vaiolo, texts in Italian and English and tactile paths; from the same floor you climb on the tower of 28 meters.

  • Where Piaggetta del Castello, Arcidosso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Centro studi David Lazzaretti

Museums · City history

In the medieval halls of the castle, he tells of the life and movement of David Lazzaretti (1834-1878), the "prophet of the Amiata" founder of the Jurisdavid Community, with paintings, objects, photographs, clothes and banners; among the pieces the garments of Lazzaretti, finds from the Museum of criminal anthropology of Turin and a letter in which Fr Giovanni Bosco offers to testify in his favor.

  • Where Castello di Arcidosso, Via Roma 4, Arcidosso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

MACO - Museo di arte e cultura orientale

Museums · Themed

In the former Chancellery of the castle complex, created by the Municipality with the Dzogchen Community of Merigar: over 5,000 oriental art objects and ethnographic finds of the Namkhai collection and documents of Himalayan cultures, with panels, multimedia and interactive installations, soundscapes and videos.

  • Where Piaggetta del Castello, Arcidosso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Santuario della Madonna delle Grazie (Madonna dell'Incoronata)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Among the most venerated sanctuaries of the Amiata, at the entrance of the village, with works of Sienese school: the Virgin in glory between the Saints Sebastiano and Rocco of Ventura Salimbeni and, on the high altar, a Madonna and Child of the first fifteenth century coming from the Palazzo della Capitaneria.

  • Where Arcidosso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Centro storico, chiese di San Leonardo, San Niccolò e Sant'Andrea

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Among the paved streets are the medieval church of San Niccolò and, in the third quarter of Codaccio, San Leonardo, of the 12th century and remodelled in the 16th century, which even with the interior destroyed in the Second World War preserves the "Decollation of the Baptist" (1588-89) by Francesco Vanni; just outside Porta Talassese the church of Sant'Andrea, of 1118. At the entrance of the village the Monument to the Fallen at work and, next door, the stone that recalls the killing of David Lazzaretti in 1878.

  • Where Arcidosso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Pieve di Santa Maria ad Lamulas

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

On the road to Montelaterone, among the chestnut groves: built in 1268 on the remains of an older building, it is considered one of the most significant Romanesque buildings in southern Tuscany.

  • Where Strada per Montelaterone, Arcidosso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Riserva naturale del Monte Labbro e Torre Giurisdavidica

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the summit, at about 1,200 metres, the remains of the tower, the church and the hermitage built by David Lazzaretti and his followers, who called the mountain "Nuova Sion"; the roof of the tower collapsed already in the late nineteenth century, but the tower can be visited and offers a wide view. You can reach by car by dirt road to a parking lot and then in about 15 minutes walk; walking and biking trails connect the reserve to the Tibetan center of Merigar and the Faunistic Park. Access adjusted seasonally by the managing body.

  • Where Monte Labbro, presso Aiole, Arcidosso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.arcidosso.gr.it · visittuscany.com

Parco faunistico del Monte Amiata e Centro visite

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

In the Reserve of Monte Labbro, with guided routes to observe deer, deer, roe deer, mouflons and Apennine wolf, as well as horses and the donkey Amiatino; equipped picnic areas and a trek of about 3 hours to the summit of Labro. The Visitor Centre has five rooms with prints, maps and minerals on the ecosystem of the Amiata, two habitat dioramas, classroom and audioguide.

  • Where Località Podere dei Nobili, Arcidosso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · cultura.gov.it
