What to see in Veroli

Città degli Ernici sulli Monti Ernici, with polygonal walls, medieval alleys and seventeenth-century palaces: the Basilica of Santa Maria Salome with the Scala Santa and, in plain, the Cistercian abbey of Casamari.

8 places to visit in Veroli, in the province of Frosinone (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Abbazia di Casamari

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Founded in 1005 as a Benedictine monastery, passed to the Cistercians in 1140 and consecrated in 1217: among the major examples of Cistercian Gothic, it preserves intact church, cloister, capitular hall and refectory; next to the cloister the museum and the gallery with prehistoric finds, Roman antiquities and paintings of the seventeenth century, and the National Library with over 500,000 volumes.

  • Where Casamari, Veroli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Basilica di Santa Maria Salome

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Built in 1209 where the remains of the saint were found, it preserves the Holy Staircase commissioned by Pope Benedict XIV and built between 1715 and 1740, in which the eleventh step is a relic of the Cross.

  • Where Veroli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Cattedrale di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Cathedral of the thirteenth century built on the site of a temple of ancient Verulae, with baroque interior with three naves renovated in the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, surrounded by noble palaces.

  • Where Veroli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Museo civico archeologico «I luoghi del tempo»

Museums · Archaeological

In the basement of the Town Hall: a Roman cryptoportico, inscriptions, capitals and sections of polygonal walls of the fourth century BC, with a reproduction of the Verolani Fasti.

  • Where Veroli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Palazzo Marchesi Campanari

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built by Campanari at the beginning of the fourteenth century in via Umberto I and expanded from the seventeenth century, with the representation hall and a glass chandelier of Murano; municipal since 2019 and open to the public.
