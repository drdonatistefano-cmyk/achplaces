What to see in Cavaion Veronese

Municipality of the Verona hinterland of Garda, about twenty kilometers from Verona; the Civic Archaeological Museum in the town hall exhibits prehistoric and Roman finds, while on Monte San Michele there is the Archeoparco of Bastia, with a medieval fortified settlement and some of the best views of the lake.

Museo Archeologico Civico

Museums · Archaeological

Housed in the town hall, it exhibits prehistoric finds of Cavaion, of the pond of Cà Nove and the nearby municipalities: ceramics, bronzes, dagger blades, bone and stone artifacts, amber, tablets and frame weights. It also preserves the remains of a necropolis and a Roman villa.

  • Where Piazza Fracastoro 8, Cavaion Veronese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.lagodigardaveneto.com

Archeoparco della Bastia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

It is a site with medieval finds on Monte San Michele, where there was a fortified settlement on two terraces, probably a destination for pilgrimages.

  • Where Monte San Michele, Cavaion Veronese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.lagodigardaveneto.com

Sources: lagodigardaveneto.com

Monte San Michele e Monte Ceriel

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Monte San Michele (342 m), on the southern slopes of Monte Moscal, is the highest point of the municipality; with Monte Ceriel it offers some of the best views of Lake Garda.

  • Where Monte San Michele, Cavaion Veronese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.lagodigardaveneto.com
