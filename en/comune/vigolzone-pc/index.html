What to see in Vigolzone

In the lower Val Nure, Vigolzone joins the fourteenth-century castle of Anguissola to the hamlet of Grazzano Visconti, a neo-medieval village born in the early twentieth century around the castle and the large park of the Visconti di Modrone; among the wine hills are the Museum of Vine and Wine in La Tosa, the villa of Collegio Alberoni in Veano and the panoramic points of Chiulano and Bagnolo.

Borgo di Grazzano Visconti

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

A village built in neo-Medieval style at the beginning of the twentieth century by Giuseppe Visconti di Modrone, recognized as an art town in 1986. In the centre of the Biscione square, with the Palace of the Institution, the Hotel of the Biscione and the well; in the Cortevecchia, under the long porch, a small museum of agricultural tools; the statue of Aloisa recalls the legend of the ghost of the village. Closed to traffic, with two large parking spaces at the entrances (free in the weekdays, paid on weekends); artisan workshops open all year round, the houses are private.

  • Where Grazzano Visconti, Vigolzone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page visitpiacenza.it

Sources: visitpiacenza.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Castello e parco di Grazzano Visconti

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Castle built in 1395, renovated and refurbished in eclectic style by Giuseppe Visconti di Modrone and also of the Duke's heirs: the guided tour includes courtyard, halls of representation on the ground floor (library, dining room) and rooms on the first floor. The park of about 15 hectares, designed by Visconti himself, combines Italian garden and English park with statues, fountains and a small miniature house built for the daughters of the duke. Guided tours from March to October, for individuals on Saturdays, Sundays and holidays; admission fee.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page visitpiacenza.it

Castello di Vigolzone

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Built in 1330 by the Anguissola, as evidenced by a plaque on the main tower, then summer residence of the Landi marquis: rectangular plan with front to the east, two large towers with alternate corners and a massive angular tower, well preserved. It is private and can be seen only from the outside; the seventeenth-century chapel opens only on special occasions.

  • Where Vigolzone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page visitpiacenza.it

Sources: visitpiacenza.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo della vite e del vino «F. Pizzamiglio»

Museums · Themed

Born in 1995 in the farmhouse La Tosa, it recreates an old cellar: about 250-300 objects between the end of the 19th century and 1930, almost all of the province of Piacenza, illustrate the phases of the production of wine, the manufacture of casks, vinegar and grappa, agriculture and domestic life. The path begins with signs along the road on the soils and vines and ends in the library of about 900 volumes (XIII-XX centuries) on viticulture and wine making, with an album of about 120 vintage postcards.

MiC card indicates closed every day; Visit Piacenza invites you to call first for timetables and guided tours

Strada panoramica del Bagnolo, Chiulano e Bicchignano

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The road of Bagnolo leads to Ponte dell'Olio between churches, villas and remains of fortifications; Chiulano, of Roman origin, is the highest hamlet of the municipality and a famous viewpoint, Bicchignano a panoramic point just below the pass of the Bagnolo.
