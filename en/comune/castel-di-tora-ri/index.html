What to see in Castel di Tora

Medieval stone village on a spur overlooking Lake Turano, in the Natural Reserve Monte Navegna and Monte Cervia, dominated by the polygonal tower of the 11th century; in front, on Mount Antuni, the abandoned village with Palazzo del Drago and the Hermitage of San Salvatore.

Torre e Palazzo Scuderini Rossi

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The polygonal tower of the 11th century, struck by lightning in 1718 and then restored, is the only remnant of the original castle and symbol of the village; the eighteenth-century palace next to it, simple and linear, follows the fan-shaped shape of the tower and preserves a cistern of 30 cubic meters for the rainwater connected to a stone fountain at the entrance.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Stone houses with wooden roofs and tiles, typical medieval rural architecture, between alleys with arches, stairs and caves; the urban layout remained almost unchanged until the mid-Twentieth century. It preserves the fifteenth-century towers of Via Turano and Via Cenci (the latter incorporated into a dwelling) and, in Via Umberto I, columns, friezes and Latin inscriptions of Roman times.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Borgo di Antuni e Palazzo del Drago

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Viewpoint

Ghost village on Mount Antuni, reached crossing a small isthmus on the lake and then with a steep climb; the inhabitants left it after the dam of the 1930s, creating the lake, put an end to agriculture and breeding, and was restored in the nineties. Palazzo del Drago (XV-XVI century) rises there, with 365 windows, with hanging gardens overlooking Abruzzo, and a seven-circle maze built with only local materials, first sensory path in a protected area.

Lago del Turano

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Artificial lake born with the dam 80 meters completed in 1939, about 10 km long with 36 km of shores, about 536 meters at the foot of Mount Navegna, at the edge of the Natural Reserve Monte Navegna and Monte Cervia. You can swim, kayak, canoeing, sailing and SUP, fishing all year round; in Castel di Tora nautical circles rent boats and kayaks.

  • Where Lago del Turano, Castel di Tora
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com
