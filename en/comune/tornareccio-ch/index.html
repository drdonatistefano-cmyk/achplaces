Area archeologica di Monte Pallano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the plateau of Monte Pallano, on the road between Tornareccio and Colledimezzo, a settlement lived from the 4th century BC to the 2nd A.D., fortified outpost under control of the Val di Sangro during the Sanniti wars. The excavations have brought to light, next to Fonte Benedetti, a large late-Hellenistic square on three sides, identified as a forum, with shops and houses, and at least three temples recognized by the architectural terracotta. The visit starts from the Documentation Centre at Lake Nero; guided tours by reservation.

  • Where Loc. Montepallano, Tornareccio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sabapchpe.cultura.gov.it

Mura Paladine

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

To the north of the settlement, cyclopean walls that legend attributed to the paladins of Charlemagne: the preserved stretch is about 180 meters long and 5 meters high, in blocks of local limestone just rough, with three small doors (about 80 cm for 2.3 meters) lined up with paths still in use. The polygonal belt, about 4 km long between built sections and rock walls, should not be older than the 5th century BC.

  • Where Loc. Montepallano, Tornareccio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: museiabruzzo.cultura.gov.it · sabapchpe.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it
