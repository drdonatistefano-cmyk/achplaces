What to see in Perdifumo

The municipality of the Cilento National Park, with its hamlets Camel, Mercato and Vatolla; in Vatolla the philosopher Giambattista Vico lived from 1686 to 1695 in Palazzo Vargas, now the seat of the Vico Foundation, and according to tradition he read under a secular olive tree of the Convent of Pietà.

Palazzo De Vargas e Museo vichiano a Vatolla

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The philosopher Giambattista Vico lived in Palazzo Vargas in Vatolla from 1686 to 1695, as tutor of the grandchildren of Monsignor Rocca, and spent the study days there. The palace is home to the Giambattista Vico Foundation, which houses the Library of the Park and a museum dedicated to the philosopher in the place where he lived for nine years; in the courtyard of the castle is held every summer the Festa della Cipolla.

Convento della Pietà e olivo di Vico

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

In front of the library of the Convent of Santa Maria della Pietà, in Vatolla, at 430 meters above sea level, there is a monumental olive tree recorded by the Campania Region: about 400 years, 295 cm of circumference and 5 meters of height. The popular tradition is that Giambattista Vico sat there in a stone seat to read Aristotle and other Greek and Latin authors.
