What to see in Sanremo

City of flowers and Festival, between the Medieval Pigna with the sanctuary of the Madonna della Costa, the Casino liberty, the Russian church, the Fort of Santa Tecla and the villas with exotic gardens.

13 places to visit in Sanremo, in the province of Imperia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Sanremo in the guide with map and filters

La Pigna

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town, called for its shape, arose around the 1000 against the Saracen pirates: alleys, steep climbs, the fourteenth-century Porta di Santo Stefano and the covered passage of the Rivolte di San Sebastiano.

  • Where Porta di Santo Stefano, Sanremo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Santuario Madonna della Costa

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

A symbol of the city on top of the Pigna, linked to the feast of chains that remembered the liberation from the feudal domination of the Doria.

  • Where Piazzale Santuario Assunta 15, Sanremo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Giardini Regina Elena

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

Forte di Santa Tecla

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Triangular fortress built by the Genoese in eleven months and inaugurated on March 12, 1756 to curb the drive of independence of the city; next to the Old Port.

  • Where Via Giardini Vittorio Veneto 34, Sanremo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
