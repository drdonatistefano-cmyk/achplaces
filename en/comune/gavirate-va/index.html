What to see in Gavirate

Village overlooking Lake Varese, once also known as the "lake of Gavirate," along an ancient street between the Oltralpe, Lake Maggiore and Milan; to see the Romanesque Cloister of Voltorre, the First Italian museum of the pipe and the lakefront from which starts the bike path of the lake. Typical sweet are the "Brutti ma Buoni," recipe of 1876.

Chiostro di Voltorre

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

On the site of a first monastic complex of the 5th century a Romanesque church arose in the 11th century; the cloister, behind the church of San Michele, and the bell tower dates back to the 12th century, with the cloister of Lanfranco da Ligurno. The colonnade has capitals of different styles; national monument since 1911, today houses modern art exhibitions.

Primo museo italiano della pipa (Museo Paronelli)

Museums · Themed

Born from the collection of J.M. Alberto Paronelli, a wholesaler and then a pipe designer who had been collecting since the 1940s and in 1984 founded the Académie Internationale de la Pipe in Gavirate, with a meeting room in the museum; run by the family for three generations. About 30,000 pieces in 9 rooms on two floors: terracotta pipes, plaster, ceramics, rootwood, boxwood, cork and sea foam, tobacconist, mouthpieces, lighters and a prehistoric chocolate of herbal heather petrified by Sardinia. From Monday to Friday only by reservation (tel. 340 744 4130).

  • Where Via del Chiostro 1/A, Gavirate
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Lungolago e pista ciclabile del Lago di Varese

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lakefront of Gavirate, with bars, restaurants and benches on the shore, is the starting point and arrival of the cycle path with a ring of 28 km (about 2 hours, easy), almost all flat, among meadows, woods and views of the Campo dei Fiori and, with the clear sky, Mount Rosa; in the first stretch towards Biandronno there is the boarding of the boat for Isolino Virginia and the ring returns after Voltorre. Parking in Gavirate.

  • Where Lungolago, Gavirate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: vareseturismo.it · in-lombardia.it

Sentiero da Pozzolo al Forte di Orino

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Itinerary No. 13 of 4 km, about 3 hours and 800 m of difference in altitude, classified difficult: starts from Pozzolo, historical hamlet of Gavirate, with an ancient wash-house, rises between woods and rural houses towards Cà dei Monti, crosses the Nature Reserve of the Campo dei Fiori and the "Road Red" and ends at the Fort of Orino, garrison of the Cadorna Line, with 360 degrees view over Prealpi, the Piedmont Alps, plain and lakes. We recommend the GPS.

  • Where Pozzolo, Gavirate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: vareseturismo.it · in-lombardia.it
