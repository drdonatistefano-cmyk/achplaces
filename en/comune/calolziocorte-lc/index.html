What to see in Calolziocorte

Among the most populated villages of the Valle San Martino, on the banks of the Adda, Calolziocorte preserves the Monastery of Santa Maria del Lavello, founded by the Servants of Mary at the end of the fifteenth century, and the church of San Martino with its high portico; from the village start the cycle path along the river and the paths to Valcava and Mount Treasure.

Monastero di Santa Maria del Lavello

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

According to tradition in 1480 a source considered miraculous sprang up under an ancient tomb, making the place a pilgrimage destination; in 1486 the Servants of Mary arrived from Bergamo built a new church there and in 1510 the convent began. The nave was enlarged at the end of the sixteenth century; the damage of mercenaries in 1629 closed the period of prosperity and the complex was restored at the beginning of the two thousand years. Today it hosts exhibitions and concerts; festivals on 25 March (the ...Fera dei Cavagnöi) and 8 September. There is an integrated ticket with Villa Monastero di Varenna (full €16 with the house museum, €13 with the garden only), valid for 15 days.
