What to see in Greccio

Medieval village about 705 meters on Mount Lacerone, overlooking the Santa Valley of crime, where St. Francis set up the first crib on Christmas night of 1223; to see the Franciscan sanctuary clinging to the rock, the church of St. Michael the Archangel and the International Museum of the Nativity.

Santuario di San Francesco

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Sanctuary of the Holy Valley clinging to the rock in a wood of holm oaks, home to St. Francis since 1217; here in Christmas 1223 he set up the first nativity scene. In the Chapel of the Nativity scene, dug into the rock, a fresco of the fifteenth-century school of Giottesca with the Nativity and the Nativity of Greccio and, on the outer wall, a fresco of the Master of Fossa; you can also visit the dormitory of San Francesco, the church of San Bonaventura (XIII century), the refectory and the cave of Blessed Giovanni da Parma. Open every day from 9 am to 6 pm.

Borgo medievale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

It preserves the medieval castrum plant founded in the 11th century, with walls, towers and the ruins of the castle at the highest point; it was destroyed in 1242 by the troops of Frederick II and plundered in 1799 by the Napoleonic forces. In the main square there is a sixteenth-century fountain; in the heart of the village the small chapel of St. Francis, where according to tradition the Saint announced his retreat in hermitage.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Greccio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo e campanile

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church from its origins in the 11th century on the top of the hill, closed in 1799, reopened in 1808 and consolidated after the earthquake of 1915 until 1944. Inside stuccos attributed to Gregorio Grimani (1636) in the chapel of the Immaculate, paintings by Vincenzo Manenti, squasantiere in pink marble by Cottanello and an organ of 1831 by Count Alderano Spada. At about 50 meters the seventeenth-century bell tower, built on one of the six towers of the walls, next to a medieval arched door.

  • Where Via della Parrocchia 5, Greccio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Museo Internazionale del Presepe

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Opened in 2016 in the former church of Santa Maria, mentioned already in 1398, and in an adjacent building purchased and restored by the Municipality; it exhibits dozens of nativity scenes from all over the world, including the porcelain Triptych, the Ceramic Book of Life and great scenery such as "Rome that is no longer there," with views of the valley. It hosts exhibitions, conferences and workshops.

  • Where Via San Francesco, Greccio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it
