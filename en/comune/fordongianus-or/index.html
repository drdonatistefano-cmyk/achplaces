What to see in Fordongianus

Country of red trachyte on the shore of the Tirso, the ancient Forum Traiani, where the hot springs feed the Roman baths of Aquae Ypsitanae, the most important of Sardinia; in the village the Aragonese late-Gothic house and the parish of San Pietro, a short distance from the Romanesque San Lussorio with the paleo-Christian crypt.

Terme romane di Forum Traiani (Aquae Ypsitanae)

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the banks of the Tirso, in Caddas, Traiano had the main Roman thermal complex built on the edge of the Forum Traiani market, exploiting a spring that flows at about 54 °C, already sacred in pre-Nuragic and nuragic times. You can visit the large rectangular tub of the tepidarium, once turned into a barrel and porch, the lateral collection tanks, the nymph with niches for statues and ex-vote, and a second circuit with calidaria, frigidarium and changing rooms; in the area also two statues of the god Bes. Dismantled in part in the Middle Ages to build churches and convents.

  • Where Via delle Terme, Fordongianus
  • Entry €6 with Aragonese House, reduced €5 (groups of at least 20) or €3 (6-14 years)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · sardegnacultura.it

Casa Aragonese

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

Aristocratic residence between the end of the sixteenth and early seventeenth centuries, entirely in trachite, rare well-preserved example of Sardinian architecture, inhabited until 1978: a single floor with thirteen connecting rooms, courtyard and a portico on the road; red volcanite frames, windows with lozenges and diamond tips, portal with bulls and shells and Christological monogram. It houses the exhibition "Forum Traiani" and "Sardinia Romana" (Roman Sardinia), 18 panels based on 19th century engravings by La Marmora and Spano. According to SardiniaCulture open every day 9:30-17:30 from October to April and 9:30-19:30 from May to September; audioguide in four languages.

  • Where Via Traiano 10 (Pratza de Is Ballus), Fordongianus
  • Entry €6 with Terme, reduced €5 or €3
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di San Lussorio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church on a low hill about two kilometers from the village, near the thermal baths and a Roman amphitheatre, built around 1100 by the monks Vittorini on a early Christian crypt; the murestenes around are animated by novenanti for the festival of late August.

  • Where Località San Lussorio, Fordongianus
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · idese.cultura.gov.it

Parrocchiale di San Pietro Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the sixteenth century in red trachyte, rebuilt in modern times; the stone comes from the local quarries of red trachyte, green and grey worked by the stonemasons of the village.

  • Where Centro, Fordongianus
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
