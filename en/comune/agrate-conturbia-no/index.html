What to see in Agrate Conturbia

Municipality of the Novara hills formed by Agrate and Conturbia: in Agrate the Romanesque baptistery of St. John, with the circular base of the 10th century and the octagon of the 12th, stands in front of the parish church of San Vittore, mentioned already in 976. In Conturbia the church of San Giorgio, consecrated by Bishop Litifredo, and the Faunistic Park La Torbiera, in the peat bog where remains of stilts have emerged.

Battistero di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rare example of Romanesque architecture: the lower part, circular and in irregular stones with pebbles with fishbone, dates back to the tenth century (perhaps around 930); the upper octagon, with blind triphors and hanging arches, is of the XI-XII century and was consecrated between 1122 and 1151 by Bishop Litifredo. Inside a 16th century fresco well preserved and, on the vault, what seems to be the biscione visconteo. Visit to the parish on request.

  • Where Piazza Roma, località Agrate, Agrate Conturbia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.agrateconturbia.no.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Vittore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

First mentioned in 976 as "basilica" in the castrum of Agrate, it was rebuilt in Romanesque times and rebuilt several times. It has three aisles and a tripartite façade with a 12th century painted bezel. He's in front of the baptistery.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.agrateconturbia.no.it

Chiesa di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish of Conturbia, consecrated between 1122 and 1151 by Bishop Litifredo: facade in hut, wall in serizzo blocks of the twelfth century, porch added in the seventeenth century. On the south side there is a frescoed bezel from the middle of the 12th century with St. Peter; inside there is a 15th-century Crucifixion, an 18th-century high altar and the Baroque wooden pulpit carved around 1701 by Antonio Francesco Viano da Oleggio.

  • Where Via Giovanni XXIII 1, località Conturbia, Agrate Conturbia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.agrateconturbia.no.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria in Valle

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It dates back to about 1100 and is perhaps the oldest church in the country, with stone walls with fishbone and semicircular apse. In the apse frescoes between the late fifteenth and early sixteenth century: Christ in almond with the symbols of the Evangelists and, below, prophets, apostles and a Madonna del latte.

Parco faunistico La Torbiera

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry

Centre for the reproduction and study of endangered species, with attention to Italian and European fauna and rarest cats; participates in conservation programmes. It is located in the peat bog of Conturbia, a protected regional area between ponds, woods and wetlands, where the excavations for peat have brought to light remains of stilts, ceramics and worked flints. According to the card of the Municipality is open from 1 March to 30 September, 10-17 hours, closed on Friday and from December to February.

  • Where Via delle Torbiere, Agrate Conturbia
  • Entry €15, reduced €10 (4-11 years), senior €12
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.latorbiera.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: latorbiera.it · comune.agrateconturbia.no.it

La Schiavenza

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

A noble rural complex that occupies almost the entire west side of the square, with large courtyard, stables and arcades; already shown on the Teresian map of 1725 and reordered in the Baroque age. The palace on the square, with facade to neo-Gothic motifs, was primary school until 1975.

Ex castello di Agrate

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

In the historic centre remain the walls of the ancient castle of the Counts of the Castle, lords of Agrate in the 11th century, partly destroyed in the 15th century; the intact coat of arms remains. At the bottom of Vicolo Castello you can see the three-level tower-door with a portal in sharp arched brick. It is private property, visible only from the outside.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
