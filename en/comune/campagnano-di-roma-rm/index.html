What to see in Campagnano di Roma

Borgo 37 km from Rome on the Via Francigena, with historic center that opens from the Porta Romana, the Fontana dei Delfini attributed to the Vignola, the Collegiate Church of San Giovanni Battista and Palazzo Venturi, home of the Archaeological Museum dedicated to the Roman mansio of Baccano sulla Cassia; outside the village the church of the Pietà and the Sanctuary of the Madonna del Sorbo.

Museo Archeologico del Pellegrino (Palazzo Venturi)

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in December 1989 after the excavation of the Roman mansio of Baccano sulla Cassia, it is located at Palazzo Venturi, seventeenth-century palace next to the Roman Gate, in the former stables of the pontifical carabinieri on horseback (1818). The route goes from the geology of the Sabatino district and of the lake of Baccano disappeared to the Etruscan furnishings of Quarticciolo and Vallelunga, to the mansio room with a sea mosaic with thermal baths, lamps, a bronze statue of Jupiter, coins up to Constantino (including a denarius of Marco Antonio) and panels on brigands, taverns and via Francigena.

  • Where Palazzo Venturi, presso Porta Romana, Campagnano di Roma
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismo.comunecampagnano.it

Centro storico, Porta Romana e Fontana dei Delfini

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The medieval village with the seventeenth-century enlargement opens from the Roman Gate; among medieval towers stand out the Fontana dei Delfini, sixteenth-century and attributed to Vignola, the Secca Fountain with the coats of arms of Campagnano and Orsini, Palazzo Venturi dei Chigi (who obtained the village in 1662) and the ...Tifo , a medieval figure in relief that tradition compares to the Pasquino of Rome.

  • Where Porta Romana, Campagnano di Roma
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it
