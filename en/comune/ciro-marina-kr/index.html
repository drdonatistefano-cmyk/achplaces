What to see in Cirò Marina

The seaside resort of the Ionian coast of Crotonese was built on the area of ancient Krimisa, between Punta Alice and the Lipuda stream, between the vineyards of Gaglioppo from which Cirò DOC was born; it preserves the remains of the temple of Apollo Aleo, the civic archaeological museum, the sixteenth-century Castello Sabatini and Blue Flag beaches.

Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

It hosts, with the Archaeology Superintendence of Calabria, materials coming from the temple of Apollo Aleo and materials of the historical phase of the Brettii. At the inauguration of the museum, on April 1, 2015, they returned to Cirò Marina after 91 years head, feet and hand of the Apollo acrolite.

Tempio di Apollo Aleo

History and architecture · Archaeology

At Punta Alice, at the mouth of Lipuda, the remains of the sanctuary that tradition binds to Filottote, founder of Krimisa. The archaic temple of the end of the sixth century BC (cell of about 27 x 7.90 m) was expanded at the end of the fourth century BC, after the conquest of Bretty, in a stone doric peripter temple of about 46 x 19 m; Paul Orsi identified it in the excavations of 1924. From here comes the acrolith of Apollo, a Greek work of 440-430 B.C.

  • Where Punta Alice, Cirò Marina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page servizi.comune.ciromarina.kr.it

Sources: servizi.comune.ciromarina.kr.it · comune.ciromarina.kr.it

Castello Sabatini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

At the foot of the hill of Madonna d'Itria, it was built in the first decades of the sixteenth century by the Carafa with quadrangular plant and central light well, then equipped with four corner towers; in the seventeenth century Vespasiano Spinelli transformed it into a fortress and a noble residence. Since 1845 it belongs to the Sabatini family, from which the name; from the plateau the look reaches the coastal towers.

  • Where Collina di Madonna d'Itria, Cirò Marina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page servizi.comune.ciromarina.kr.it

Sources: servizi.comune.ciromarina.kr.it

Santuario di Maria Santissima d'Itria

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

The castrum of Alichia, along the road to Cirò, was built in the middle of the fifteenth century and was a temple and then the Knights of Malta. Rebuilt from 1940 at the behest of the Passionist Bishop Eugenio Raffaele Faggiano, buried here in 1982, it houses a nineteenth-century painting of the Madonna with Child in Byzantine style and is run by the Passionist Fathers.

  • Where SP7 24, Cirò Marina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page servizi.comune.ciromarina.kr.it

Sources: servizi.comune.ciromarina.kr.it

Fontana del Principe

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

A mirrored nineteenth-century fountain with three arches surmounted by a marble slab with Spinelli's coat of arms, in grotesque-style brick and decorated with lava stone, shells and mosaics; it was part of the ancient Prince's Park.

  • Where Lungo la SS 106, a nord-est del Castello Sabatini, Cirò Marina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page servizi.comune.ciromarina.kr.it
