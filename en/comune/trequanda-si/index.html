What to see in Trequanda

The hilly village of the Sienese Valdichiana with the castle of the Cacciaconti and the hamlets of Castelmuzio and Petroio among the olive trees.

5 places to visit in Trequanda, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Castello Cacciaconti

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The castle with the high white stone tower and the crenellated walls, with an Italian garden and a courtyard inside.

  • Where Trequanda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque church in Piazza Garibaldi with its particular façade, with the sixteenth-century urn of the Sienese school of Blessed Bonizzella Cacciaconti.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Trequanda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Torre del Molino a Vento

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

An eighteenth-century dovecote just outside the centre, with about 500 terracotta nests for pigeons.

  • Where Trequanda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo della Terracotta di Petroio

Museums · Themed

It tells the production of terracotta that gave work to the whole country, with the "Little Tuscany" of the potter Rodolfo, which reproduced in terracotta the villages of the area.

  • Where Petroio, Trequanda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo d'Arte Sacra di Castelmuzio

Museums · Old masters

In the headquarters of the Confraternity of the Holy Trinity and of San Bernardino, ancient stop of pilgrims, works of John of Paul and Giuliano Traballesi and a reliquary of San Bernardino.

  • Where Castelmuzio, Trequanda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
