What to see in Fasano

Municipality between the Selva di Fasano and the sea, with the historic center of the Church Mother of St. John and Piazza Ciaia, the Archaeological Park and National Museum of Egnazia in Savelletri, the rock park of Lama d'Antico and the coast of Torre Canne in the Park of the Dune Costiere.

9 places to visit in Fasano, in the province of Brindisi (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo archeologico nazionale «Giuseppe Andreassi» e Parco archeologico di Egnazia

History and architecture · Archaeology

The remains of ancient Egnazia, excavated from 1912, include Messapian walls and necropolis, the Via Traiana, the Civil Basilica with the Sala delle Tre Grazie, the Sacellus of the Eastern Gods, the cryptoporic, the thermal baths and the Christian basilicas of the IV-VI centuries. The museum, built in 1975 and named after the Superintendent Giuseppe Andreassi, tells in ten rooms the Messapian, Roman, Late Antique and Medieval era, with the marble head of Attis and the mosaic of the Three Graces. Open every day 8:30-19:30.

  • Where Via degli Scavi 87, Savelletri di Fasano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it · museipuglia.cultura.gov.it

Museo dell'olio d'oliva

Museums · Themed

The church of San Lorenzo Martire was built in 1693 and is housed in an 18th-century olive mill restored in front of the farm Sant'Angelo de' Graecis, also called Abazia di San Lorenzo, born in the 12th century from the rocky settlement of Lama di San Lorenzo. It exhibits tools of olive-tree processing; behind the palace there is an ancient citrus grove. Open Friday and Saturday 17:30-20:00, best contact first.

  • Where Contrada Sant'Angelo 5, Fasano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Centro storico, Chiesa Madre e piazza Ciaia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Village of narrow streets, white houses, arches and small squares. The 17th century Mother Church was built by the Knights of Gerosolimitani and dedicated to St. John, with rosette and protyro on the façade; on Piazza Ciaia overlook the Palazzo dei Cavalieri of Malta, renovated in the eighteenth century, and the 16th century Palazzo dell'Università.

  • Where Piazza Ciaia, Fasano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Parco rupestre di Lama d'Antico

History and architecture · Archaeology

Not far from the railway station, it includes the rock churches of San Giovanni and San Lorenzo, with decorations of the XI and XII centuries, and a well preserved rock village with streets, churches and workshops; in the countryside around there are numerous caves once used as shelter houses.

  • Where Nei pressi della stazione ferroviaria, Fasano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Tempietto di Seppannibale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Along the highway 16 towards Monopoli, 4 km from Fasano: a small church of the 10th century with three naves with cupolette in axis, indicated as the first example of the genre in the region. Three kilometers further, on the same road, is the crypt of San Procopio, perhaps frescoed in the fourteenth century.

  • Where SS 16 verso Monopoli, 4 km da Fasano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Selva di Fasano

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hillside location at 397 meters, reached by a panoramic road with hairpin bends, rich in trulli and woods of holm oak, carobs, arbutus and lentischi.

  • Where Selva di Fasano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Parco naturale regionale delle Dune Costiere

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 1,100 hectares long 8 km of coast between Fasano and Ostuni, from Torre Canne to Torre San Leonardo: posidonia prairies, juniper dunes with specimens of 500-600 years, paleodune, humid areas retrodunal like the Morelli River and age-old olive groves with underground mills along the Via Traiana. La Casa del Parco, in a former railway station, rents bikes; bike and walking tours by reservation.

  • Where Da Torre Canne a Torre San Leonardo, Fasano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.parcodunecostiere.org

Sources: parcodunecostiere.org · italia.it

Torre Canne, spiaggia e faro

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fasano fraction inside the Dune Costiere Park, with fine and clear sand, dunes of Mediterranean maquis and sloping seabed suitable for children; several times Blue Flag and Green Flag, is also known for its thermal baths. The 35-metre-high lighthouse was built in 1928 and is the symbol of the Fasano Navy.

  • Where Torre Canne, Fasano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Savelletri e il porticciolo

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Locality on the Adriatic with wide golden sandy beaches and rocks, backdrops to explore with mask and mouthpiece, a marina of fishing boats and ancient white houses near the cliffs; the second Sunday of August is celebrated St. Francis of Paola with the festival of the swordfish.

  • Where Savelletri, Fasano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
