What to see in Bucine

Municipality of Valdambra between Florence, Arezzo and Siena, border land since the Roman age and the gate of Chianti: the Tower of Galatrona with the parish church of Della Robbia, the castle of Cennina, the Badia a Ruoti and the Museum of the memory of San Pancrazio.

8 places to visit in Bucine, in the province of Arezzo (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre di Galatrona

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Rest of one of the most important castles in the area, documented by 963 and perhaps already Etruscan settlement; the castle, which had two towers, survives the watchtower about 27 meters high, about 500 meters above sea level. Inside a multimedia path on the history of Galatrona and the castle; climbing five floors of stairs you have a 360 degree view of the Valdarno and the Chianti Mountains. Open from April to October, usually on Saturday and Sunday.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Pieve di San Giovanni Battista a Petrolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church with three naves near the Tower of Galatrona, perhaps built on the site of an ancient pagan temple. It preserves three glazed terracottas of Giovanni della Robbia made between 1517 and 1521: the baptismal font, the statue of St. John the Baptist and a ciborium.

  • Where Galatrona, Bucine
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Castello di Cennina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Borgo on a hill at 470 meters, the castle that best preserves the medieval appearance among the fortifications of the area: built in 1167 by Count Ghibelline Brandaglia Alberico d'Uguccione, is closed by a belt up to 15 meters high with a turrita door. Inside, the square of the Cisterna with stone and brick houses and the 12th-century castle; from the church on the north side, the view extends over Pratomagno, valleys of the Arno and the Ambra, Valdichiana, Casentino and La Verna.

  • Where Piazza Cisterna, Cennina, Bucine
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Badia a Ruoti (abbazia di San Pietro)

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Abbey dating back to the year 1000, originally belonged to the Camaldolese monks, then besieged and transformed. Inside the frescoes of the sixteenth century and the Coronation of the Virgin of Neri di Bicci (1472); near there is a secular oak tree, one of the "trees" of memory.

  • Where Badia a Ruoti, Bucine
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo della memoria e Sacrario di San Pancrazio

Museums · City history

Opened on 29 June 2007 in the cellars of the former Palazzo del Podestà, where on 29 June 1944 German soldiers of the armored division Hermann Göring gathered and killed the inhabitants of San Pancrazio before setting the building on fire. It documents the massacre with photos of the Canadian Military Archive and a historical map of the massacres of Nazis between Valdichiana and Valdambra. Santuario di Santa Maria della Ginestra

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Place of worship very well known, reached by the Via della Ginestra, route between country roads, vineyards, cypresses and villas (about 7.5 km, 2 hours and a half). Next to the sanctuary was found an ancient Etruscan well.

Ponte di Pogi e Pogi Alta

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Romanesque bridge with five stone arches on the Ambra stream, a few kilometers from Bucine, along an ancient important itinerary; near, the hilly village of Pogi Alta, grown around a medieval fortress.

  • Where Pogi, Bucine
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Castello e centro di Bucine

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Medieval castle fortified with walls and towers, whose remains are embedded in the houses of the center. In 1564 the fief was entrusted to Giulio Vitelli, who became a Marquis; the name derives from "bucina" (a fishing net or hunting net that appears in the coat of arms). The village, of the counts Guidi di Modigliana, was placed in 1225 under the protection of Arezzo and was taken by force by the Tarlati around 1325.

  • Where Centro storico, Bucine
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
