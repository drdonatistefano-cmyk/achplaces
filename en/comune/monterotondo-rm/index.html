What to see in Monterotondo

Cities 23 km from Rome, built around Palazzo Orsini Barberini, fortress of the Orsini of the 13th century transformed by the Barberinis into a ducal residence, today the town hall and seat of the Archaeological and Multimedia Museum; in the center the Cathedral of Santa Maria Maddalena, Porta Garibaldi, which recalls the taking of the city by Garibaldi on 26 October 1867, and Piazza del Popolo with the Fountain of the Lions; around, the reserves of Macchia di Gattaceca and Macchia del Barco and, in November, the Fasti d'Autunno.

Palazzo Orsini Barberini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built by the Orsini in the 13th century as a fortress to control the territory between Salaria and Nomentana, it was purchased in 1626 by Barberini who, at the impulse of Pope Urban VIII, transformed it into a ducal residence; then passed to the Grillo and Boncompagni-Ludovisi and today it is the seat of the Municipality. The rooms of the noble floor, decorated at the behest of Cardinal Franciotto Orsini, have the myth of Adone attributed to Girolamo Siciolante da Sermoneta (about 1553), the Stories of Joseph the Jew, frescoes by Giacinto Calandrucci and a view of the city painted by Paul Brill in 1581. The tower was rebuilt after the earthquake of Avezzano in 1915; in the courtyard a marble cistern of the sixteenth century, the Swan Garden and the Cippo-Ossario garibaldino.

Museo archeologico e multimediale

Museums · Archaeological

It tells the history of the territory from Prehistory to the seventeenth century with over 4,000 archaeological finds, plastic, reconstructions of environments, holograms, video and virtual reality. The route is divided into two locations: the section of Roman archaeology on the ground floor of the Municipal Library "P." Angelani and that of pre-Roman archaeology, with frescoed rooms, on the first floor of Palazzo Orsini. Accessible even to disabled people.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.mucim.it

Duomo di Santa Maria Maddalena

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Wanted by the Council of the Forties in 1621 and started in 1630 under the Barberini on a plant of the Vignola, it received the visit of Urban VIII in 1641 and was elevated to a minor basilica in 1845, when Gregory XVI was welcomed. Baroque façade on the main square; the ceiling is frescoed with work attributed to Domenico Pistrini and the altar, a sarcophagus of the second century, preserves the martyrs Sixtus and Bonifacio. It is the departure of the sixth stage of the Way of St. Francis.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Pincetto

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Panoramic terrace built in 1903 by engineer Luca Betti to connect the lower square to the ancient center, to the right of the basilica; the fountain has a statue of the three hills on which the city stands and two tombstones remember the fallen in Libya (1913) and Fabio Giovagnoli (1909).

  • Entry Free

Piazza del Popolo e Fontana dei Leoni

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The square, the main meeting point of the city, was placed under the Barberini; the Fontana dei Leoni dates back to the first form in 1845 and in 1927 the sculptor Romeo Liberati replaced the terracotta lions with those in travertine.

Porta Garibaldi

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Designed by the architect Frontoni in 1722 and completed in 1751, with a round arch, it is the last still intact of the four gates of the historic center; it takes its name from the conquest of the city by Garibaldi on October 26, 1867.

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The oldest church in Monterotondo, in the Santa Maria district; the annexed monastery, wanted by Giacomo Orsini in 1448, was demolished in 1932. It preserves a Holy Family attributed to Savonanzi and the equestrian relief of Giordano Orsini on a fifteenth-century tomb; it was restored in the early two thousand years after the damage of the earthquake.

Convento dei Cappuccini e chiesa di San Francesco d'Assisi

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

The new convent was approved in 1605 and built from 1609 under Antonio Del Grande; lived there San Crispino da Viterbo (1703-1709), Garibaldi used it in 1849 and in 1867 and Pius IX stopped there for lunch in 1853. The church, with a baroque façade, contains paintings from the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries; in the garden the marble statue of St. Francis of Fra' Remo Rapone.

Riserva Naturale Macchia di Gattaceca e Macchia del Barco

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Reserve of 997 hectares between Monterotondo, Mentana and Sant'Angelo Romano, between the valley of the Tiber and the Cornicolani Mountains, with hills, pastures, olive groves and oak woods; the karst has created caves, swallowholes, sventators and sinkholes, among which the Well of Merro, sinking deep about 80 meters with a lake on the explored bottom up to about 392 meters. It is reached by train with the FM1 and the Cotral buses.

  • Where Tra Monterotondo, Mentana e Sant'Angelo Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.monterotondo.rm.it · italia.it · visitlazio.com
