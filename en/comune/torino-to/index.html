What to see in Turin

The ancient capital of Sabauda at the foot of the Alps, the cradle of the Risorgimento: the Egyptian Museum, the Mole Antonelliana with the Cinema Museum, the Royal Museums and the Palazzo Madama in Piazza Castello, the Basilica of Superga.

50 places to visit in Turin, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers 36 of the 57 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Museo Egizio

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The oldest museum in the world dedicated to Egypt, founded in 1824 by the union of the collections of Drovetti and Carlo Felice: more than 30,000 finds among statues of pharaohs, sarcophagi, papyri and mummies, renovated in 2015.
