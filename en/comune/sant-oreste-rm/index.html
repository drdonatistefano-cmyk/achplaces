What to see in Sant'Oreste

Borgo perched on the southern buttress of Monte Soratte, limestone massif isolated in the Tiber valley 58 km from Rome: inside the walls with three gates there are the sixteenth-century Palazzo Caccia-Canali, attributed to the Vignola and seat of the Naturalistic Museum, and the church of San Lorenzo; on the mountain the Hermitage of San Silvestro and the galleries of the Bunker Soratte.

Museo naturalistico del Monte Soratte e Palazzo Caccia-Canali

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The museum occupies the Palazzo Caccia-Canali, a project attributed to Jacopo Barozzi called the Vignola, completed in 1589, with frescoes, friezes and large sixteenth-century fireplaces; since 2020 it is part of the network of historic residences of Lazio and also houses the Pinacoteca. The geological, anthropological, botanical and fauna routes tell the Riserva del Soratte: traces of the animals of the woods, typical plants, the formation of the caves and the prehistoric tools of the first populations.

  • Where Piazza Cavalieri Caccia, Sant'Oreste
  • Entry €1, guided tour €3 more
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Bunker Soratte

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

About 4.5 km of concrete tunnels excavated since 1937 by Mussolini as an air raid shelter for the military leaders, disguised as a Breda weapons factory; since September 1943 for about ten months there was the headquarters of the South Command of Field Marshal Kesselring, and in 1967, in NATO, about 1.3 km became an anti-atomic bunker for government and President of the Republic. Recovered by the municipality, it has been a museum since 2003 and is visited as a "Memory Path" with guided tours of about two hours, including military means, radio and maps.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Mura e porte del borgo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old village, perched on the upper part of the southern buttress of the Soratte, is closed by well-preserved walls interrupted by three gates: Porta Valle (or San Silvestro), Porta La Dentro (or Sant'Edisto) and Porta Costa (or Santa Maria).

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Chiesa di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The sixteenth-century church was built with the contribution of the Caccia family, whose design the Vignola had worked; in the central aisle an artistic pulpit with the coat of arms of the family.

Ex Monastero di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Next to Palazzo Caccia-Canali, it contains the ancient Palazzo Abbaziale; in 1598 the Augustinian cloistered nuns moved there. The inner church preserves an altarpiece with the Saints Elena, Augustine and Nicola and a wooden choir.

Eremo di San Silvestro

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the Monte Soratte Nature Reserve, you can reach it on foot from the village and offers a 360 degree view of the Tiber Valley. It is always accessible to the outside; for guided tours we contact the Cultural Association Adventure Soratte.

Riserva Naturale del Monte Soratte

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Regional reserve established in 1997, 444 hectares between the Via Flaminia and the Tiber, also a site of community interest: the Soratte is an isolated limestone massif of not even 700 meters, crossed by 14 CAI paths including the Path of Hermits (205), Santa Romana and Meri (203), Le Grotte (206) and the Military Road (204). The mountain is home to numerous karst caves, which can be visited only with equipment and expert guides.

  • Where Sant'Oreste
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com

Panemmolle, acquacotta e torta gigante

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Among the typical dishes are the acquacotta, the frascarelli with ricotta cheese, the gnocchi with beans and the breadcrumbs, pasta with chickpeas, rosemary and tomato served on breadcrumbs; the giant cake is enjoyed at the feast of the patron Saint'Edisto, in October, with historical procession. On the last Sunday of May the Madonna of May brings the torchlight with bundles of reeds lit on the side of the mountain.
