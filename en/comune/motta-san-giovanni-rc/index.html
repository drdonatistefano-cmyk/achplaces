What to see in Motta San Giovanni

From the hills of Aspromonte to the Strait: above the village the Castle of Santo Niceto, byzantine fortress of the 11th century shaped as a ship; on the coast, in Lazarus, the white cliff of Leucopetra with the lighthouse of Capo d'Armi, the remains of the villa where Cicero and the Antiquarium with the Jewish skylight with the menorah.

Capo d'Armi, rupe di Leucopetra e faro

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Leucopetra, white stone in Greek, is the rocky wall about 150 meters high on the sea that dominates the cape of Capo d'Armi; it appears in the Tabula Peutingeriana and Strabone placed there the end of the Apennines chain. Since 1867 the lighthouse that marks the border between the Strait and the southern Ionian coast has been operating there. In 43 B.C. Cicero, fleeing to Greece, was stopped here by a storm and hosted by his friend Publio Valerio, as the Letters to Atticus recall.

Castello di Santo Niceto

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Byzantine fortification built in the first half of the 11th century on a rocky hill to defend against Saracen raids, a long refuge of the Regins; the name comes from San Niceto, an admiral of the Byzantine monarchy venerated in Sicily. It is indicated as the only pre-Norman military complex in Calabria still almost intact: ship-plant with the stern towards the Strait and the bow towards the Aspromonte, walls about 3,5 meters high, monumental entrance, two square towers and the remains of a cistern; from the top you can see the Etna. At the foot of the ascent the church of the Annunziata with the Christ Pantocrator frescoed in the dome, in recovery phase. Antiquarium Leucopetra

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

Archaeological civic museum inaugurated in 2016 in the former barracks of the Guardia di Finanza, Liberty building built after the earthquake and tidal wave of 1908, recognized as a museum of regional interest in 2019. It exhibits the findings of the territory from the excavations of the Superintendence (1995-2004): the cleft of Ferrina dedicated to Demeter (III century BC), the materials of the fortress of Santo Niceto, the remains of the Roman villa with mausoleum, the lead slingshots of the X Legio Fretensis and a Jewish skylight of the IV-V century A.D. with the menorah, exhibited in 2017-2018 at the MEIS in Ferrara. Open every day 9-12.30 and 16-20 (Common); access with stairway, without ramp or elevator.

Area archeologica di Lazzaro

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Between the localities Lia and Capo d'Armi emerged the remains of a Roman villa, probably of the patrician Publio Valerio who hosted Cicero: excavations near the stream San Vincenzo revealed six environments, three with mosaics. From the territory are also acorns missiles related to the naval war of 42 BC between Octavian and Sixth Pompey, the votive cleft to Demeter found in 1948 (end V-early III century BC) and proto-Christian and Jewish testimonies.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Centro storico di Motta San Giovanni

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village rises from the coast of the Strait towards the slopes of Aspromonte and is formed by two districts: the medieval nucleus around the castle (Suso), once walled and with a gate, and the eighteenth-century-nineteenth-century quarter of Praci to the east. Walls and many palaces have been lost, but the streets still follow the concentric circles of the ancient ramparts. It is reached by train to Lazarus and then by bus.

  • Where Via Giovan Battista Zumbo, 89065 Motta San Giovanni (RC)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
