What to see in Mammola

Locride Municipality in the Aspromonte National Park, known for MuSaBa, the open-air museum park created since 1969 by Nik Spatari and Hiske Maas in the ancient monastic complex of Santa Barbara; on the plateau of Limina stands the Sanctuary of San Nicodemo, linked to the patron saint of the village.

MuSaBa, Parco Museo Laboratorio Santa Barbara

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Outdoor museum park of about 7 hectares in the valley of the Torbido, created since 1969 by artists Nik Spatari and Hiske Maas in the ancient monastic complex of Santa Barbara and managed by the Foundation Spatari/Maas; it is also workshop where new works are born. Among olive and cypresses monumental sculptures, mosaics and installations; in the church restored the great fresco Â The dream of Jacob (240 sq m), called the Sistine Chapel Calabria, and in the cloister of the Foresteria a mosaic of 1.400 sq m. According to the official website is open every day from 10 am to 2 pm (last entrance 13), closed December 25, January 1 and Easter; guided tours of about 1 hour by booking for groups.

  • Where Viale Parco Museo Santa Barbara, Mammola
  • Entry €10
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.musaba.org

Sources: musaba.org · cultura.gov.it · italia.it · comune.mammola.rc.it

Santuario di San Nicodemo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

It rises on the plateau of Kellerana or Limina, 12 km from Mammola, in the Aspromonte National Park, where it lived with other monks Saint Nicodemus, a Calabrian monk of the tenth century and of Byzantine rite. In 1501, the relics were brought to Mammola, who proclaimed him a patron in 1638; the church, rebuilt between 1960 and 1969 and renovated in 2018, is a diocesan sanctuary since 2002. It is celebrated here on Sundays after 11 May and many pilgrims climb on foot along the ancient path of the Seja, which once joined the Ionian coast to the Tyrrhenian one.

  • Where Monte Limina, 89045 Mammola (RC)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
