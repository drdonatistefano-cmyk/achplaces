What to see in Saint-Pierre

Country between apple orchards and vineyards at 731 meters with two castles: the fairy-tale with four turrets and the Sarriod de la Tour with the "sala delle teste" (sala of heads).

3 places to visit in Saint-Pierre, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Saint-Pierre in the guide with map and filters

Castello Sarriod de la Tour

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortified complex of the XI-XII century, of the Sarriod until 1923 and of the Region since 1970: the chapel has frescoes of the thirteenth century and the 'sala delle teste' a wooden ceiling of the late fifteenth century on 171 shelves carved with monsters and fantastic animals.

Castello di Saint-Pierre – Museo regionale di Scienze naturali

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

One of the oldest manors in the valley, with a fairytale appearance for the four towers; inside the regional museum of natural sciences.
