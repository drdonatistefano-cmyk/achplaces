What to see in Labro

Stone village 600 meters between the Reatini Mountains and Lake Piediluco, accessible only on foot, dominated by the castle Nobili Vitelleschi and restored from the seventies of the twentieth century by the Belgian architect Ivan Van Mossevelde; Orange Flag of the Touring.

3 places to visit in Labro, in the province of Rieti (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Nobili Vitelleschi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval fortress of the De' Nobili, passed to the Vitelleschi in the Renaissance, with tower and imposing walls that draw the profile of the village and dominate the Valle Santa Reatina at the border with Umbria. In the seventies of the twentieth century the Belgian architect Ivan Van Mossevelde bought the village almost abandoned and restored it respecting the local limestone.

private property, you can admire from the outside

  • Where Via Santa Maria Maggiore 4, Labro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Already armory of the fortress of the Noble, lords of the country since 956: after the excommunication of Sixtus IV the main tower was demolished and the armory became a sacred place, elevated to collegiate in 1508 by Cardinal Colonna. The sixteenth-century portal was the entrance of the castle; inside, a single nave with frescoes in starry sky, a baptismal font of the fifteenth century with three carved fishes, an Annunciation attributed to Bartolomeo Torresani and, on the upper floor, the Chapel of the Rosary with portal of 1494 carved with reptiles and scorpions.

  • Where Via Santa Maria Maggiore 6, Labro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Centro storico e Porta Reatina

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the Porta Reatina you can walk through paved alleys, stairs and small houses with natural colors up to the fortress; from the village the view ranges from the mountains to the lake, and for the night sky of San Lorenzo Labro is on the list of the most beautiful Cieli of Italy.

  • Where Porta Reatina, Labro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it
