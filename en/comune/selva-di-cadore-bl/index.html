What to see in Selva di Cadore

Centro della Val Fiorentina, mentioned already in 1234 for the church of San Lorenzo, Selva di Cadore houses in the Civic Museum Vittorino Cazzetta the mesolithic burial of the hunter of Mondeval, about 7,500 years ago, and the cast of the boulder of Pelmetto with the dinosaur prints of the upper Triassico.

Museo civico Vittorino Cazzetta

Museums · Archaeological

Established in 1982 and re-arranged and expanded in 2010 on four floors, it is a geological-paleontological, archaeological and historical museum. The best known piece is the burial complete with a mesolithic hunter lived about 7,500 years ago, found in 1987 in Mondeval de Sora at about 2,150 m; next, the cast of the Pelmetto mass with dinosaur prints, a paleoveneta stele of Monte Pore, copies of the rock inscriptions of the Civetta and sections on the Neolithic site of Mandriz, on the Gothic alpine and on the mines of Fursil. It is part of the UNESCO Dolomites museum network; book-shops, cafés, libraries and educational workshops.

Orme di dinosauro del Pelmetto

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

The collapse of dolomia boulders detached from the Pelmetto has brought to light a walk of dinosaurs of the upper Triassic, the period in which the first dinosaurs appeared. The cast of the rock with the fingerprints and reconstructions of the animals are exposed to the Museo Vittorino Cazzetta.

  • Where Pelmetto, Selva di Cadore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.selvadicadore.bl.it

Sources: comune.selvadicadore.bl.it
