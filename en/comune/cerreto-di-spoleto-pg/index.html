What to see in Cerreto di Spoleto

Valnerina municipality divided between the perched capital, with medieval walls, civic tower, the former monastery of San Giacomo and the Ciarlatano Museum, and the hamlets of Borgo Cerreto, Ponte and Triponzo, where sulphurous waters flow known since the Roman age.

Ex Monastero di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Fortified complex founded in the thirteenth century and enlarged in the fifteenth century, with courtyard, well, cellar, oratory and dorm; today it houses the Municipal Historical Archives and the CEDRAV, center of anthropological documentation of Valnerina. In the church a Visitation of 1573 by Camillo Angelucci da Mevale, seventeenth-century paintings and frescoes of the fifteenth century (Annunciation, Crucifixion and Saints, Christ in glory with angels), some close to the Master of Eggi; in a room of the monastery a universal Judgment of the first half of the fifteenth century.

Ex chiesa di San Nicola e Museo del Ciarlatano

Museums · Themed

Only the perimeter walls remain of the thirteenth-century church: nave and transept have become an open-air theatre, while the space below houses the Ciarlatano Museum.

Centro di documentazione Baronio Vincenzi (ex chiesa dei Santi Maria e Gesù)

Museums · Themed

It is located in the seventeenth-century former church of Saints Mary and Jesus of Borgo Cerreto and is dedicated to the doctor and surgeon Baronio Vincenzi, lived in Cerreto between the end of the sixteenth and the beginning of the seventeenth century; in the catalogue of the Ministry of Culture is registered as Mummies Museum of Borgo Cerreto.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Chiesa di Santa Maria Delibera

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Renaissance church leaning on a tower of the walls, which became the apse; it has two sacred rooms superimposed with Umbrian-Marches frescoes from the 15th to the 16th century. The name comes from an image of Our Lady of Liberator, called "Madonna de Libera" (Madonna de Libera).

Santuario della Madonna della Stella

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Former hermitage of Santa Croce built in 1308 by Augustinian monks at the oldest Benedictine monastery of San Benedetto in Faucibus; the site dates back to the 8th century, was abandoned in 1652 and restored in the 19th century after events considered miraculous.

Antiche terme di Triponzo

Local life and traditions · Spas

Sulfurean waters known since the Roman age and mentioned in the Aeneid of Virgil; in 1488 the thermal baths were already in operation, donated by the bishop of Norcia Bucchi-Accica to the Municipality of Cerreto. According to the source are the only thermal baths of Umbria fed by sulphur water, at 30 °C all year round.
