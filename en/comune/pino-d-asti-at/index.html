What to see in Pino d'Asti

Small castle village of the Bass Monferrato astigiano, near the forests of Muscandia, dominated by the Castle Gavotti; in the former Municipal Forn the Ecomuseo of the Bass Monferrato Astigiano exhibits a collection of carpenter and cabinetmaker objects.

Castello Gavotti

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

It rises on the top of the hill, surrounded by the houses of the village; probably of the thirteenth century, it has been renovated several times and in 1944 it was subjected to the bond of the Fine Arts. Families linked to its walls include the Roots, the Avogadro, the Miroglio, the Frejlino and Scotland. It has lost its imposing circular tower and today has an eighteenth-century façade that hides the medieval structure.

Museo del Legno – collezione di oggetti da falegname ed ebanista

Museums · Themed

It is part of the Ecomuseum of the Bass Monferrato Astigiano and preserves, in the former Municipal Furnace, a collection of carpenter and cabinetmaker objects. The Romanesque itinerary from Castelnuovo Don Bosco mentions Pino d'Asti among the small villages of Castelnuovo with the Wood Museum.
