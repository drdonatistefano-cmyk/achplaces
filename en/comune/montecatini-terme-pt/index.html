Terme Tettuccio

Local life and traditions · Spas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The "thermal temple" created in 1916 by Ugo Giovannozzi evokes the Roman thermal baths with colonnades, courtyards and fountains, immersed in the park.

  • Where Montecatini Terme
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Funicolare per Montecatini Alto

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Two red iron carriages in Art Nouveau style, never replaced since 1896, which go up to the castle of Montecatini Alto.

  • Where Montecatini Terme
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
