What to see in Brindisi

The door of the East at the end of the Appian Way: the Roman Columns on the Scalinata Virgiliana, Piazza Duomo with the cathedral and the Ribezzo Museum, the Roman remains of San Pietro degli Schiavoni, the Swabian Castle, the Forte a Mare and the Monument to the Marinaio d'Italia on the port.

8 places to visit in Brindisi, in the province of Brindisi (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Colonne Romane e Scalinata Virgiliana

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Roman columns at the top of the Scalinata di Virgilio mark the end of Via Appia; the monumental staircase accommodates up to 900 people during the events.

  • Where Scalinata Virgiliana, Brindisi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · italyformovies.com

Piazza Duomo e Cattedrale di San Giovanni Battista

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The oldest square in the city, with the cathedral, the portico De Catiniano, the loggia of Palazzo Balsamo and the Archaeological Museum.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Brindisi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · italyformovies.com

Museo Archeologico «Francesco Ribezzo»

Museums · Archaeological

Established in 1884 in the Temple of St. John the Sepulchre and opened in 1958, it collects the civic collection and the findings emerged in the historical center and province.

  • Where Piazza Duomo 7, Brindisi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Area archeologica di San Pietro degli Schiavoni

History and architecture · Archaeology

The ancient district preserves the remains of the Roman Brindisi: a paved road, parts of houses and a spa complex.

  • Where Rione San Pietro degli Schiavoni, Brindisi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italyformovies.com · italia.it

Monumento al Marinaio d'Italia

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint
