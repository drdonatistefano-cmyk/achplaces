What to see in Abbadia Lariana

Country on the eastern branch of Lake Como at the foot of the Grigne, known for the Civic Museum Setificio Monti, with one of the largest silk torcitoi operating in Europe; among the villages Borbino, Linzanico and Crebbio and, in the mountains, the Cascata del Cenghen.

Civico Museo Setificio Monti

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Around 1818, the silksmith Pietro Monti transformed an ancient cloth crowd, moved by a hydraulic wheel on the stream Val Zerbo, into a silk spinning mill; in 1869 the spinning mill was added, closed in 1903, and the spinning mill worked until 1934. Purchased by the Municipality in 1978 and restored since 1981, the museum opened in 1998: the heart is the large circular torch-piece of 1818 (5 m in diameter, 11 m in height, 972 casts), put into operation, with three rectangular torcuits and, since 2021, an tapestry embroidered in silk by Filippo Biagioli on the legend of Grigna.

  • Where Via Nazionale 107, Abbadia Lariana
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.abbadia-lariana.lc.it · lombardiabeniculturali.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Cascata del Cenghen

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the heart of the Grigne group, it is the highest waterfall of the group: a 50 meters jump from rocky walls to a crystal clear pool (Italy.it also calls it the Val Monastero waterfall). You can reach it with an easy path with little difference in altitude from Linzanico or Crebbio, through the village of Calech with views of the lake and the Southern Grigna, or from the Path of the Wanderer.

  • Where Val Monastero, Abbadia Lariana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Borbino

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Frazione in panoramic position on the lake and starting point of excursions, including the Path of the Wanderer; it has three churches, the Immaculate, the Madonna of the Snow and St. Martin, of the thirteenth century, at the entrance of the village. Linzanico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

It is a Romanesque settlement, once in the Duchy of Milan, which became a municipality in the eighteenth century, annexed to Mandello in 1809 and finally united to Abbadia in 1928. It preserves some eighteenth-century buildings and from here starts the path to the Cascata del Cenghen.

  • Where Contrada alla Fontana, Abbadia Lariana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Crebbio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small village of medieval origin on the slope next to the center, part of a defensive system that included Mandello, with a surviving house tower and view of the lake; the parish church of Sant'Antonio Abate, renovated until the seventeenth century, preserves a canvas of the mid seventeenth century with the territory of the parish. The patron saint's feast, the Sunday closest to 17 January, provides for the blessing of animals.

  • Where Strada per Maggiana 15, Abbadia Lariana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Spiagge di Abbadia Lariana

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Three free beaches on the eastern branch of Lake Como: Pradello, with grassy shore and ample parking five minutes from the station; Park Church Rotta, with bar, cabins, deckchairs and services, accessible to people with disabilities; Poncia, small green beach with cabins and showers.

  • Where Lungolago, Abbadia Lariana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it
