What to see in Vasanello

Medieval village between the Cimini Mountains and the Tiber, with artificial caves, Etruscan tombs and walls dug in the cliffs, grown along the Amerina street of which today is a stop of the Way: it dominates the Castle Orsini and the Romanesque Santa Maria Assunta, and with Civita Castellana is the most famous center of Tuscia for ceramics, told in the museum of Palazzo Celestini.

Castello Orsini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

In 1278 Bear Orsini tore Vasanello from the Church and built a first fortification on a falisco-Roman bastion; the fief then returned to the Orsini and in 1489, for the wedding of Orsino Orsini, was commissioned the first cycle of frescoes, probably linked to the workshop of the Pinturicchio, enriched in 1505 with references to the Della Rovere. Passed to the Colonna and in 1700 to the Colonna-Barberini of Sciarra, in 1907 it was purchased by Monsignor Luigi Misciattelli, whose heirs still own it; in 1946 the ancient stables became a ceramic factory, closed in 1978. A spiral staircase leads to a panoramic point.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the present form it dates back to the 11th century and was probably born as a parish church for the pilgrims of the nearby Via Amerina, leaning against a bastion of medieval defenses; the bell tower rises on a watchtower. The façade and the apses combine Burgundy and Lombard sobriety with the Romanesque of Tuscia; the interior with three aisles has columns with capitals in pepper with unique symbolism, and the two lateral apses retain 12th century frescoes of Byzantine influence. In the crypt, also with three naves, the relics of the patron Saint Lanno are preserved.

Chiese di San Salvatore e San Lanno

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

San Salvatore is recognizable for the high Romanesque bell tower and preserves the great fresco of the "Pietà" with Saints attributed to Piermatteo d'Amelia; to the same Renaissance painter is attributed the "Madonna on throne" with Child in the chapel of San Lanno, dedicated to the patron saint of the village.

  • Entry Free

Museo della Ceramica (Palazzo Celestini)

Museums · Themed

In the Palazzo Celestini, in the heart of the historic center, tells the local ceramic manufacture favored by clay deposits, from the falisco period to the modern age: excavations from 1984-1985, pieces of the factory "Ceramica Bassanello" (active until 1978) donated by the Misciatelli family and statues of Giulio Francesconi. Vasanello is with Civita Castellana the most famous town of Tuscia for ceramics, and its potters are called "cocciari" (cocciari).

Torricella e Ponte di Valle Gaudenzio

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Along the ancient Via Amerina, north of the village, stands the Torricella, tower probably part of a sighting system; not far away remain the side parapets of the Bridge of Valle Gaudenzio, Etruscan bridge to a single arcade of the second century. Vasanello is the stop of the Via Amerina Way, 60 km recognized by the Lazio Roads Network.

  • Where Via Amerina, a nord di Vasanello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Centro storico e rupi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval village rises on cliffs where artificial caves, Etruscan tombs and ancient walls open; the Via Amerina brought him economic and cultural prosperity over the centuries and the Way enters through the ancient valley and fortification up to Piazza della Repubblica.
