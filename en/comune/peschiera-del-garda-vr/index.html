What to see in Peschiera del Garda

Fortified city on the southern shore of Garda, where the lake becomes Mincio: the Venetian pentagonal fortress, UNESCO heritage since 2017, you visit entering from Porta Brescia or Porta Verona and walking on the bastions; inside the walls the Ponte dei Voltoni, Piazza Ferdinando di Savoia with the church of San Martino and the remains of the Roman Arilica, the Habsburg military district and the Historical Palazzina, outside the walls the Sanctuary of Madonna del Frassino.

Fortezza veneziana e cinta bastionata

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Peschiera rises on one of the three fortified islands that connect Garda to the Mincio; the fortress, enlarged by the Scaligeri at the end of the thirteenth century, owes its pentagonal plan to the walls built by the Republic of Venice, which renewed the fortifications with terrapies and bastions at the five corners of the medieval belt. It was a link between Venice and its most western territories beyond the Mincio, played a role in the Napoleonic countryside and in the wars between the Austrian Empire and the Unity of Italy, and the Venetian defence works have been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2017. Walls, gates, canals and water ditches surround a historic centre of cobbled streets.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Bastione San Marco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built in 1553, it is the second bastion for the importance of the Venetian pentagonal fortress and preserves intact its military structures; the southern section played a role in the sieges of Napoleonic and Risorgimento wars. From the walk above Porta Verona you can see the entire eastern front of the fortress, the railway bridge rebuilt after the bombings of 1944, the back of the Ponte dei Voltoni and in the distance the Bastione Cantarane; you can climb it from a stone staircase in the Piazza della Caserma d'Artilleria.

  • Where Porta Verona, Peschiera del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: tourismpeschiera.it · italia.it

Porta Verona

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Monumental entrance in golden stone on the eastern side, with a solemn and well preserved exterior façade; in the center there was the Lion of St. Mark, destroyed by Napoleon after the Treaty of Campoformio of 1797, and a reconstruction at the beginning of the bridge shows the door as it was. Next to it stands the neoclassical Caserma Francesco I (1822), today Scuola di Polizia di Stato.

  • Where Porta Verona, Peschiera del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: tourismpeschiera.it · italia.it

Ponte dei Voltoni

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Five-arched stone bridge built in 1556 by the Republic of Venice, immediately after the completion of the fortifications, under the Provveditore Iacobo Gauro remembered by a plaque in Via Dante; it is considered the architectural symbol of the city. Under the arch towards Piazza Ferdinando di Savoia there was Pescarezzo, a fixed fish pond; the only structure of this type still visible on Garda, recently restored, can be seen from the Longolago Bonomi.

  • Where Canale di Mezzo, Peschiera del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: tourismpeschiera.it · italia.it

Area archeologica di Arilica e Chiesa di San Martino

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

At the Church of San Martino there is the archaeological area of the ancient Roman city of Arilica: the excavations have brought to light Roman remains dating from the 1st and 3rd centuries AD. The church dates back to around the year 1000 and according to a hypothesis supported by excavations rises where were found ruins of a temple of Jupiter.

  • Where Piazza Ferdinando di Savoia, Peschiera del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · tourismpeschiera.it

Piazza Ferdinando di Savoia (ex Piazza d'Armi)

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Large green space used for events, concerts and markets; on the south side the complex of the Fortress, adapted for military use by the late Roman Empire, fortified by the Scaligeri (1275-1385) and renovated under Venice (1550-1700), when the square became naval Arsenale. The present appearance dates back to the Austrian period.

  • Where Piazza Ferdinando di Savoia, Peschiera del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: tourismpeschiera.it · italia.it

Quartiere militare asburgico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Beyond the Ponte dei Voltoni extends the Austro-Hungarian military district of the nineteenth century, with the former Artillery House, the long Austrian Palleria (deposit of ammunition overlooking a white marble square), the Commander's Palace and the Pavilion of the Officers, built in 1856 with brick vaults separated by an embankment of about 1.5 meters against the bombings, which can be seen from the outside along the Canale di Mezzo.

  • Where Oltre il Ponte dei Voltoni, Peschiera del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: tourismpeschiera.it · italia.it

Palazzina Storica, Museo della Conferenza Interalleata

Museums · City history

Command building of the fortress built in 1854; on November 11, 1917, after Caporetto, King Vittorio Emanuele III brought together the Allied commanders asking not to withdraw. Today it houses the museum that recalls the encounter with documents, photographs and testimonies, and every year exhibitions of classical and contemporary art.
