What to see in Oltre il Colle

The mountain town of the Orobie Bergamasche, between Val Brembana and Val Seriana, at the foot of Pizzo Arera (2.512 m); in Zorgene the Museum of the mine and the minerals recalls the mines closed in 1980, while on the climb to the Arera stands the Cathedral of Giuliano Mauri.

Museo della miniera e dei minerali

Museums · Themed

Opened in 1994 at the former elementary school in Zorge, it recalls the mining history of the area, which ended in 1980 with the closure of the mines. On the ground floor the mining section, from the time of Leonardo da Vinci to the twentieth century, with original tools and the life-size reconstruction of the entrance of a gallery; on the first floor more than 2,000 minerals and 450 fossils of the Val Brembana and the world, including galena, staggered and local wulfenite and a showcase of fluorescent minerals; on the second floor multimedia and teaching materials.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Cattedrale Vegetale

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

The work designed by the Lodigian artist Giuliano Mauri, stands on a sheltered hill along the ascent to Pizzo Arera: over 20 metres high, it has a gothic five-nave plant with 42 beech columns, supported by about 1,800 spruce poles, 600 chestnut branches and 6,000 metres of hazel branches taken from the nearby woods and tied with ropes, pins and nails. In time the beech trees will grow until they no longer need scaffolding, destined to deteriorate.

  • Where Salita al Pizzo Arera, Oltre il Colle
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Pizzo Arera e Capanna 2000

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

It is 2.512 m high between Val Brembana and Val Seriana, among the best view of the Orobie, with views from Presolana to Legione. From Zambla Alta a mule track goes up to the hut Capanna 2000, also suitable for families; beyond the hut the path gets steep on meadows and rocks, with little men and a short stretch equipped not exposed before the summit. The steepest shortcut with path 222; other accesses from the Alpe Arera (parking fee) and via Plassa. In winter only for experienced and equipped hikers.

  • Where Zambla Alta, Oltre il Colle
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitbrembo.it · italia.it

Sentiero dei Fiori

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

It starts from the hut Capanna 2000, in the territory of Oltre il Colle, and leads to the Passo del Branchino, with a view of the homonymous lake, through flowery meadows that color the slopes especially between June and July; from the hut 2000 it is also possible to walk as a family ring.

  • Where Rifugio Capanna 2000, Oltre il Colle
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitbrembo.it · italia.it
