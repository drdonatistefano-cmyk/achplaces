What to see in Noli

The ancient marine republic protected by Genoa, among the most beautiful villages in Italy: the Porta di Piazza, the Town Hall with the tower, the Loggia della Repubblica, the medieval red brick towers and the castle of Monte Ursino above the village.

6 places to visit in Noli, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo Comunale e Torre del Comune

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The palace on the square, the heart of the political life of the village, with four Gothic windows and a sundial; next to the thirteenth-century tower on a green stone base.

  • Where Noli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Porta di Piazza e Loggia della Repubblica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The main gate in the second wall (XII-XIII century) and the two large fourteenth-century and fifteenth-century arches of the Loggia towards Piazza Dante.

  • Where Noli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Torre della Marina e Palazzo Viale-Salvarezza

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

On Piazza Dante, the thirteenth-century tower donated in 1673 to Agostino Viale and next to the seventeenth-century palace.

  • Where Piazza Dante, Noli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Castello di Monte Ursino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Castello dei marquisi Del Carretto from the 12th to the 14th century, restored by Genoese architects in 1522, with central mast and polygonal belt; the last refuge of the population.

  • Where Monte Ursino, Noli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di San Pietro e Casa Repetto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church dated 1572 on Romanesque foundations, renovated in Baroque age; next to the medieval house of the fourteenth century.

  • Where Noli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Spiaggia dei Pescatori

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

The beach is linked to the life of fishermen, with cliffs overlooking the sea, coves and hills with paths among the pine forests.

  • Where Noli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it
