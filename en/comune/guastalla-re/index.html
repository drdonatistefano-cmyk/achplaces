What to see in Guastalla

For almost two centuries the capital of a branch of Gonzaga, with the sixteenth-century plan still legible: the Doge's Palace with the City Museum, the cathedral consecrated by Carlo Borromeo, the statue of Ferrante Gonzaga of Leone Leoni, the Maldotti Library and, just outside, the millennial Basilica of the Pieve.

8 places to visit in Guastalla, in the province of Reggio Emilia (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo Ducale e Museo della Città

Museums · City history

Residence of the Torelli and then of the Gonzaga, which hosted Torquato Tasso and Guercino: rebuilt by Francesco Capriani called the Volterra and Tommaso Filippi, with decorations by Bernardino Campi, was completed around 1570. It houses the City Museum with the Maldotti Quadreria, about fifty works including Luca Ferrari, Marcantonio Franceschini, Giuseppe Bazzani, a watercolor of Hayez and an oil of De Nittis, and temporary exhibitions. Open Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday 9.30-12.30 and 15.30-18.30.

  • Where Via S. Gonzaga 16, Guastalla
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Cattedrale di San Pietro e statua di Ferrante Gonzaga

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Designed by Francesco da Volterra and consecrated in 1575 by Carlo Borromeo: bishop's stables of the seventeenth century, a Holy Family of Lombard school and a 17th century silver palace. On Piazza Mazzini in front of the statue of Ferrante I Gonzaga di Leone Leoni (1564).

  • Where Piazza Mazzini, Guastalla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Basilica della Pieve di San Pietro

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Cited in 864, rebuilt around 915 by Berengario I and consecrated in 997; held a Urban II Synod in 1095 and a Paschal Council II in 1106. The present Romanesque aspect comes from the works of 1926-31; inside a baptismal font of the IX-X century and a Madonna and Child in terracotta attributed to Guido Mazzoni.

  • Where Pieve di Guastalla, Guastalla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa della Santissima Annunziata dei Servi e piazza Garibaldi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Designed by Francesco da Volterra in 1598, it preserves a canvas by Giuseppe Maria Crespi and an Annunciation by Pietro Rotari; it overlooks Piazza Garibaldi, whose center is the octagonal oratory of the Madonna della Concezione (1579), next to the church of the Capuchins (1673).

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Guastalla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Biblioteca Maldotti

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Founded in 1801 on the bequest of the abbot Marcantonio Maldotti, since 1857 Garibaldi was in the process where the Jesuit church of San Luigi (1741) stood: about 100,000 volumes, about twenty incunabula, paintings, prints, coins, medals and herbari, with a sixteenth-century puteal in the atrium. Open from Monday to Friday 14-18.

  • Where Corso Garibaldi 54, Guastalla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: reggioemiliawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Oratorio di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small Romanesque building, probably from the 12th century, already mentioned in the donation of Ludovico II of 864: restored several times, it preserves fragments of frescoes. Just outside the center.

  • Where Guastalla
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Teatro Ruggero Ruggeri

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Neoclassical theatre restored in 1814 and again in recent times, center of the city's cultural life.

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint
