What to see in Serra San Bruno

Mountain centre in the heart of the Regional Park of the Serre, born around the Certosa founded by Bruno di Colonia, the first Carthusian monastery in Italy. The old town, rebuilt after the earthquake of 1783, has carved granite portals and late Baroque churches, among fir woods and beech trees crossed by the Frassati Path to the sanctuary of Santa Maria del Bosco.

Certosa e Museo della Certosa

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry

Founded between 1090 and 1101 by Brunone di Colonia on the land of the plateau donated by Count Ruggero il Normanno, it is the first Carthusian convent of Italy and the second of the Order after Grenoble; almost destroyed by the earthquake of 1783, it was rebuilt in neo-Gothic style. The cloister is not visitable (no access to women, closed library): the museum, wanted in 1993 by the monastic community, which reconstructs the rooms of the monastery from the cell to the convent church, with rooms on the iconography of San Bruno, on the Carthusian saints, on the liturgy and on the library.

  • Where Contrada Certosa, Serra San Bruno
  • Entry €4, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · parcodelleserre.it · italia.it

Santuario di Santa Maria del Bosco e laghetto di San Bruno

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Here in 1091 San Bruno built the first monastic settlement, the hermitage of the Tower; the church was consecrated in 1094 by the Archbishop of Palermo Alcherio and in the early sixteenth century there were found the relics of the saint. Between ancient white fir trees and young beech trees, with a granite staircase and the dormitory of San Bruno, is the place of the most tourist and religious attraction of the village; next there is the pond where the saint prayed immersed in the freezing waters in sign of penance. The processions take place on Monday and Tuesday of Pentecost.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Chiesa Matrice di San Biagio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Building of the late eighteenth century, already documented as church of San Biagio in the early seventeenth century, with facade in local granite. In the nave the marble statues of St. John the Baptist, St. Stephen, St. Bruno and the Virgin with the Child from the ancient Certosa and two bas-reliefs of David Müller of 1611; in the choir the Martyrdom of St. Stephen by Bernardino Poccetti (1608 about) and the Trinity with Carthusian saints by Francesco Caivano (1633). It preserves an 11th century reliquary rebuilt in the 18th century, which tradition wants to donate from Adelaide del Vasto to San Bruno.

Chiesa dell'Addolorata (Maria SS. dei Sette Dolori)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1721, it has a semi-elliptic façade designed by Biagio Scaramuzzino. Inside the ciborium of Cosimo Fanzago and Andrea Gallo, the apparition of the Virgin to St. Bruno by Paolo De Matteis (1721) and the Death of St. Anne (1645), attributed to Reynaud Levieux; the bronze outer door is by Giuseppe Maria Pisani jr. (1961).

Chiesa dell'Assunta (Spinetto)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the nineteenth century, it contains works of Serresi workers: a wooden Crucifix of the Scrivo workshop, St. Joseph of Antonio Scrivo and statues of Raffaele Regio. The statue of the Assunta, of the Neapolitan school of the early eighteenth century, comes out in procession on the "varia" by Bruno Barillari (mid-nineteenth century).

Centro storico e Corso Umberto I

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village, destroyed by the earthquake of 1783 and rebuilt, preserves houses with carved granite portals, wooden loggias and wrought iron railings, work of the craftsmen called for in the centuries by the presence of the Certosa. The axis is Corso Umberto I, paved in granite in the second half of the nineteenth century and flanked by late Baroque churches.

  • Where Corso Umberto I, Serra San Bruno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcodelleserre.it

Sentiero Frassati e bosco Archiforo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of 21,34 km, mostly on dirt road, of medium difficulty (about 8 hours, difference in altitude 254 m, between 860 and 1,114 m), which leaves and returns to the Tower of San Michele behind the Certosa. The first stretch, about 5 km to the sanctuary of Santa Maria del Bosco, crosses abetina and beech woods Archiforo, with traces of irons, stonemasons and carbonai.

  • Where Torre di San Michele, Serra San Bruno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcodelleserre.it · italia.it

Museo Botanico Villa Bonitas

Museums · Themed

Erbarium and botanical garden with about 1,300 species of cataloged plants; it also exhibits ancient tools for wood processing, the four phases of the transformation of small logs into coal and a classification of many species of mushrooms.
