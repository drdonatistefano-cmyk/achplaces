What to see in Collepardo

Borgo dei Monti Ernici about 100 km from Rome, known for the Certosa di Tsulti, a national monument since 1873, for the karst caves of the Bambocci and for the great chasm of the Well of Antullo.

5 places to visit in Collepardo, in the province of Frosinone (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Certosa di Trisulti

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

A 13th century monastic complex renovated in the Baroque age, about 800 m above sea level, a national monument from 1873 and dedicated to the Virgin Assumption, St. Bartholomew and St. Bruno. The single nave church has paintings by Filippo Balbi, the chorus of the chorus of the choristers of the late sixteenth century carved by Jacobò and on the vault the "Gloria" of Blesseds and Saints of Caci (1683); on the square the Palazzo di Innocenzo III houses an important library. The visit is completed by the Italian gardens and the bell tower with three quadrants that mark only the minutes.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Antica farmacia della Certosa

Museums · Themed

Pharmacy and liquor store with 17th century furnishings, vases and crystals, frescoed by G. Not with Pompeian themes; it preserves original showcases and containers of medicines and, in front, a living room decorated by Balbi once used as a guesthouse.

  • Where Certosa di Trisulti, Collepardo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Grotte di Collepardo (dei Bambocci)

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Karst caves known for stalactites and stalagmites with bizarre shapes, from which the name "dei Bambocci" is named; the name "Regina Margherita" recalls the visit of the first queen of Italy in 1904. Guided tours by reservation.

  • Where Collepardo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.grottepastenacollepardo.it

Sources: grottepastenacollepardo.it · visitlazio.com

Pozzo d'Antullo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Large karst chasm born from the collapse of a cave, about 60 m deep and about 300 m broad, about 1 km from the village on the slopes of the mountains La Monna and Rotonaria; on the bottom grows a vegetation with trees up to 20 m tall. In the past, sheep with ropes for seasonal grazing were dropped.

  • Where Circa 1 km da Collepardo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · grottepastenacollepardo.it

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens
