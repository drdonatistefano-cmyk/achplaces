What to see in Villasalto

Gerrei mining country, in south-eastern Sardinia: the antimony mine of Su Suergiu, active from 1880 to 1987, houses the archaeological-industrial museum in the building of the direction; around, the parish church of San Michele Arcangelo, the sanctuary of Santa Barbara and Monte Genis with its secular holm oak and the nuragic complex of Sa Dom'e sa nì.

Miniera di Su Suergiu e Museo archeologico-industriale

Museums · Themed

Antimony mine active from 1880 to 1987, the most important in Italy for the extraction and processing of this metal: during the world wars its foundry, built in 1882, produced 90% of the Italian antimony. The village preserves the foundry, workshops, chemical laboratory warehouses, the canteen and accommodations; the building of the direction, nineteenth century, is a Liberty style villa with Corinthian pronaos and friezes of leaves, festivals, shovels and picks, and houses the archaeological-industrial museum with minerals, materials and equipment that reconstruct the production cycle and the history of the miners. It is reached from a avenue of pine trees; it is part of the Park Geominerario historical and environmental Sardinia.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church built around the seventeenth century: it contains paintings, wooden statues of the seventeenth century and an organ of the eighteenth century. The patron saint is celebrated at the end of September.

Santuario di Santa Barbara

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Founded by Byzantine monks and rebuilt in the mid-nineteenth century, it is dedicated to the patron saint of miners; around it grew the most recent part of the country. The feast takes place at the beginning of June and is repeated on July 10 along with the goat festival; the faithful reached the sanctuary for a long path in the woods.

Monte Genis e complesso nuragico Sa Dom'e sa nì

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Granite relief covered by a centuries-old holm oak, Faunistic oasis with mouflons and panoramic summit. At about 700 meters rises the large nuragic complex of Sa Dom'e sa nì (the house in the snow) with towers, walls and circular structures, then used to preserve the ice; under the eastern ridge there is the nuragic village of Arcu 'e sa Tuppa.
