What to see in Casacalenda

Medieval village in the hills of Molise transformed from the nineties into a museum of contemporary art in the open air, the MAACK, with the civic gallery and the museum of the buffoo, the tool barrel of tradition.

6 places to visit in Casacalenda, in the province of Campobasso (Molise). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Casacalenda in the guide with map and filters

MAACK – Museo all'aperto d'arte contemporanea Kalenarte

Museums · Modern art · Free entry · Viewpoint

Born in the early 1990s from an idea of architect Massimo Palumbo with the Municipality and Kalenarte Association: 21 permanent installations between alleys, stairs, squares and outskirts, to be visited on three colorful itineraries; the last stop is the ...Poet of Casacalenda... of Kostas Varotsos, 9 meters high, in the green outside the village.

  • Where Casacalenda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Galleria civica d'arte contemporanea Franco Libertucci

Museums · Modern art

Founded in 1997 and renovated in 2007 in the City Hall, it exhibits works by MAACK artists and donations, a collection of 114 works including paintings, installations and sculptures.

  • Where Via Emilio di Gennaro 83, Casacalenda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: luoghidelcontemporaneo.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museums · Themed

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Fonte del Duca e Chiesa della Vergine Addolorata

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

  • Entry Free

Palazzo Ducale e Porta di Capo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

At the end of Vicolo Terravecchia the great palace born for defence and then residence; the Porta di Capo is the only surviving door of the medieval walls.

  • Where Vicolo Terravecchia, Casacalenda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
