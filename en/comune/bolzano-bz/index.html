What to see in Bolzano

The "door of the Dolomites" between Mediterranean and Central European culture: the Medieval Porticos, Walther Square, Ötzi at the Archaeological Museum, Castel Roncolo frescoed and the cable cars towards Renon, Colle and San Genesio.

27 places to visit in Bolzano, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The Gothic Cathedral from the bell tower considered one of the most beautiful in the region, seat of the diocese of Bolzano-Bressanone since 1964, with the pulpit in sandstone, the frescoes of the Chapel of the Graces and the decorated roof restored in 2009.

  • Where Piazza Walther, Bolzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Piazza Walther

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The "living room" of the city, built in 1808 and dedicated to the poet Walther von der Vogelweide, with the monument of 1889, elegant coffee, the flower market in spring and the Christmas market.
