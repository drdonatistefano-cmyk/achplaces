Parco della Memoria Storica, il borgo distrutto

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Viewpoint

Ruins of the medieval village (X-XI century) destroyed between 8 and 16 December 1943 and rebuilt further downstream, declared a national monument in March 2008: it preserves the plant with churches, passages and walls with square towers, crossed by paved alleys to steps. In the centre, in the ...Picco della terra" (Picco della terra) the mother church of San Michele Arcangelo, documented from about 1513 and symbol of the village; also remains the Arch of the Barons, Gothic, probable entrance of the old castle, with traces of frescoes. This is where John Huston shot the Fifth Army ... The Battle of St. Peter and Monicelli scenes of the Great War.

Museo Parco della Memoria Storica - Centro visite

Museums · City history

Inaugurated in December 2008 in an old oil mill restored and inspired by John Huston's film, it offers seven stage settings, including the history of the country, the descent of darkness, the refuge in caves, an optical theatre and the battle of St. Peter and Montelungo; the visit takes place in the dark, with music in quadriphonie and the projection of a film. The municipality assigns the multimedia project to the stage designer Carlo Rambaldi.

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites
