What to see in Genazzano

Borgo on a spur of tufa of the Prenestini Mountains, home of Pope Martin V, with the Colonna Castle, the Nymph of Bramante and the sanctuary of the Mother of Good Council; on the first Sunday of July the center is covered with the Inflorata of the Sacred Heart.

5 places to visit in Genazzano, in the province of Rome (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Colonna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built by the Colonnas and renovated several times, it guards the country from the north: military fortress, transformed into a noble residence by Pope Martin V in 1417 and then papal residence in the sixteenth century; between 1611 and 1639 Philip I Colonna renewed the north-eastern tower, with a chapel frescoed in 1616 by Marzio Ganasini.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Ninfeo di Bramante

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Commissioned by Cardinal Pompeo Colonna between 1507 and 1511 and designed by Bramante on the model of the Roman thermal baths, with three spans facade and octagonal niche room; he is part of the Network of the Historical Houses of Lazio.

  • Where Genazzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Basilica Santuario della Madre del Buon Consiglio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Founded in the 13th century, it is a pilgrimage destination linked to numerous miracles; in the first weekend of September it hosts the celebrations of the Mother of Good Council with the traditional fair.

  • Where Corso Cardinale Vannutelli, Genazzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Parco degli Elcini

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Renaissance park annexed to the castle, today municipal garden, with the remains of an octagonal fountain and a well fed by the Roman aqueduct.

  • Where Genazzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Centro storico e Infiorata

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village lies on a spur of tuff; since 1883, on the first Sunday of July, the families of the village stretch out a carpet of flowers over a kilometre and a half long, recognized in 2012 by the Guinness of the Primates as the world's largest inflorate (1,642.57 m2).

  • Where Genazzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com
