What to see in Fagnano Castello

Country of the Cosentine Coast Chain with the Lakes of Fagnano, the only natural lakes of Calabria, in the Natural Park of Monte Caloria and Natura 2000 site; in the hamlet of San Lauro, overlooking the plain of Sibari, the Palace and the church of San Nicola di Bari.

Laghi di Fagnano e Lago dei Due Uomini

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Group of small lakes on the Coast Chain, the only natural lakes in Calabria, Site of Community Interest (IT9310060) in the Natural Park of Monte Caloria; the largest is the Lake of the Two Men, where in 1982 the herpetologist Alain Dubois discovered the subspecies of triton alpestre Triturus alpestris inexpectatus. An easy and flat walk touches the lakes Trifoglietti, of the Two Men, Fonnente and Paglia, with a stretch of the Trail Italy.

San Lauro

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Frazione 550 meters, about five km from the center: from the square you can see the Pettoruto, the plain of Sibari and, on clear days, two stretches of the Ionian. The Palace, which dates back to 1126, is the oldest building, in Renaissance-baroque style, with the chapel of the Immaculate Church in front of it; the church of San Nicola di Bari, with a single nave with wooden pulpit and bell of 1791, preserves the Madonna della Pietà of supposed Neapolitan school, disputed in 1881 with San Marco Argentano.
