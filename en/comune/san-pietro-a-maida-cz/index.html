What to see in San Pietro a Maida

Hill country of the Lambetino mentioned from the fifteenth century as "Sancto Petro de Mayeda" , with the parish of San Nicola di Bari rose on the site of an ancient abbey of San Pietro , the historic districts of the Piazza , the Travone and Gibia and Palazzo Aiello , where in 1860 was guest Garibaldi .

3 places to visit in San Pietro a Maida, in the province of Catanzaro (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di San Nicola di Bari

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish from millenarian history, built on the site of an ancient abbey dedicated to St. Peter; almost entirely destroyed by the earthquake of 1783. It is reached by a staircase; a side entrance has a ramp for people with disabilities.

  • Where Piazza Guglielmo Marconi, San Pietro a Maida
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sanpietroamaida.cz.it

Sources: comune.sanpietroamaida.cz.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village is divided into the historical districts of the Piazza, an economic and cultural centre, the Hospital (from the ancient Hospital of the Poor or Hospital of San Giovanni), the Travone or Gurnelle, a artisan district with stone tubs to macerate the linen, and the Gibia, a public water reserve in the upper part. It is home to the 16th century abbey church, reopened about a decade after the earthquake of 1783 without the ancient bell tower, the churches of San Giovanni and Santa Maria del Carmelo (cited in 1597) and the houses of the families Votta and Fabiani.

  • Where Piazza Guglielmo Marconi, San Pietro a Maida
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sanpietroamaida.cz.it

Sources: comune.sanpietroamaida.cz.it

Palazzo Aiello

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The residence of Captain Ferdinando Aiello, who on 29 August 1860 hosted Giuseppe Garibaldi; next to the entrance a stone post for the centenary recalls the visit (in these walls he had hospitality Giuseppe Garibaldi on 29 August 1860).

  • Where San Pietro a Maida
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sanpietroamaida.cz.it

Sources: comune.sanpietroamaida.cz.it
