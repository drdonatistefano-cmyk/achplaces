What to see in Gaeta

Seaside town on the Ulysses Riviera, with the medieval district of Sant'Erasmo dominated by the Angevin-Aragonese castle, the Duomo with the Arab-Siculo bell tower and the complex of the SS. Annunziata; on the promontory of Monte Orlando the sanctuary of the Mountain Spaccata with the Grotta del Turco and the mausoleum of Lucio Munazio Planco, and about ten kilometers of beaches, including Serapo.

Santuario della SS. Trinità alla Montagna Spaccata

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the western side of Mount Orlando, overlooking the sea, the 11th century sanctuary takes its name from three cracks of the rock that tradition binds to the earthquake at the death of Christ. Baroque interior with a single nave with a canvas by Raimondo Bruno (1850) on the main altar and the bed of San Filippo Neri, a stairway in the slit descends to the Chapel of the Crucifix (16th century), built on the fallen stone in 1434, and along the wall you can see the "Mano del Turco" (Turco's Hand). Freely visitable, entrusted to PIME missionaries; not accessible to disabled people.

  • Where Via Santissima Trinità 3, Gaeta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parchilazio.it · italia.it · comune.gaeta.lt.it

Grotta del Turco

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry · Viewpoint

It is the first crack of the Spaccata Mountain, to the left of the entrance of the sanctuary: it descends for about 300 steps, secured, up to the reflections of light on the sea; according to legend it was a refuge of pirates. Opened in 2025 after ten years; times indicated by Visit Lazio: Tuesday-Sunday 9-12 and 14:30-17:30, free admission.

  • Where Via Santissima Trinità, Gaeta
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it · italia.it

Quartiere medievale di Sant'Erasmo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Documented already at the time of the duchy of Gaeta and the maritime republics, with many houses of the twelfth century, it is an intricacy of alleys, stairs, subways and arches that still bears the signs of the bombings of the Second World War. There are panoramic walks of Viale degli Innamorati and via Fontania and the square dedicated to Goliarda Sapienza, with the wall of poetry and benches shaped like a book.

  • Where Lungomare Giovanni Caboto, Gaeta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Duomo e campanile

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The cathedral of Sant'Erasmo, consecrated in the 12th century, preserves a paschal candelabra in marble; outside the medieval bell tower in Arabic-siculous style and cosmatesque of the late 12th century, 57 meters high, with interwoven arches, inlays and majolica.

  • Where Quartiere Sant'Erasmo, Gaeta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it

Complesso della SS. Annunziata e Cappella d'oro

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry

The fourteenth-century church with façade rebuilt since 1621; it houses the Golden Chapel (or Golden Grotto), decorated in wood and pure gold, and the codes of the Amanuense monks.

  • Where Via Annunziata, Gaeta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Museo archeologico e Pinacoteca (Centro Storico Culturale)

Museums · Old masters

Opened in 1976 and transferred to the former hospital of the SS complex. Annunziata (1355): Roman finds of the II-III century A.D. and medieval (ceramics, glass, Arab majolica, tombstones), the crowds of the duchy of Gaeta (X-XII century) and a gallery from the XII to XVIII century with separate frescoes of Saint Lucia and works of Luca Giordano, Sebastiano Conca, Giovanni da Gaeta, Beinaschi and Paolo De Matteis; also exhibited the Standard of the battle of Lepanto attributed to Girolamo Siciolante. MiC timetables: every day 16:30-19.

  • Where Via Annunziata 7, Gaeta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · parchilazio.it
