What to see in San Cassiano

Borgo del Salento about 40 km from Lecce, surrounded by countryside and olive groves in the territory of the Parco dei Paduli, dotted with pajare. The heart is Piazza Cito with Palazzo Cito and the sixteenth-century Chiesa Matrice di San Leonardo; not far away is the Doge's Palace and the crypt of the Madonna della Consolazione. Every March 19, the rite of the Tables of St. Joseph is renewed.

Palazzo ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

With roots in the 16th century, when it had watchtowers and defensive structures, it was renovated in 1720 by the Lubelli family, which eliminated most of the towers: one remains visible. A stone portal between two columns leads into an open square atrium; a three-arched loggia preserves traces of painted decorations. Owned by the municipality since 1982, it is the seat of the Town Hall and has a congress hall.

Piazza Cito e Chiesa Matrice di San Leonardo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Piazza Cito is the heart of the village, with the elegant Palazzo Cito; the side of the sixteenth-century Chiesa Matrice di San Leonardo, with two bell towers, overlooks the square. The historic center retains an architecture of great value and beauty.

Cripta della Madonna della Consolazione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches
