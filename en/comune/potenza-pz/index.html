What to see in Potenza

The capital of Basilicata is more than 800 metres away, the city with its escalators: the historic centre along Via Pretoria, with the Guevara Tower and the Cathedral of San Gerardo, and the national and provincial archaeological museums; the bridge over the Basento of Sergio Musmeci.

15 places to visit in Potenza, in the province of Potenza (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico (Sopra Potenza)

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient nucleus on the highest point of the town, at more than 800 meters, with the most significant testimonies of the past of the city and panoramic views.

  • Where Centro storico, Potenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Via Pretoria

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The oldest street in the city, which once led to the sixteenth-century castle of which the Guevara Tower remains.

  • Where Via Pretoria, Potenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo archeologico nazionale della Basilicata Dinu Adamesteanu

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the sixteenth century Palazzo Loffredo, on two floors, from the previous phase of Greek colonization to the Roman conquest: furnishings of the necropolis of Vaglio with armour and jewelry between the end VI and mid-V century BC, and the materials of the sanctuary of Rossano di Vaglio of the fourth century BC, rebuilt in a hall.

  • Where Via Andrea Serrao, Potenza
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo archeologico provinciale

Museums · Archaeological

Building on five levels: on the first floor the permanent exhibition on Enotri, Greci and Lucani from the archaic age to the Romanization, on the ground floor and on the second floor temporary exhibitions.

  • Where Via Ciccotti, Potenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Pinacoteca provinciale

Museums · Modern art

The permanent exhibition entitled to Concetto Valente, with painting and sculpture of the nineteenth century of the provincial collection: works by Giacomo Di Chirico, Angelo Brando and Michele Tedesco and portraits of Giuseppe Mona, Vincenzo La Creta and Andrea Petrone.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
