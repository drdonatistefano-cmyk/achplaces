What to see in Portico e San Benedetto

4 places to visit in Portico e San Benedetto, in the province of Forlì-Cesena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cascata dell'Acquacheta

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The waterfall mentioned by Dante in the canto XVI of Hell, reachable on foot from San Benedetto in Alpe in less than two hours.

  • Where San Benedetto in Alpe, Portico e San Benedetto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Borgo di Portico di Romagna

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Medieval village Orange Flag, with stone houses, donkey back bridge and cribs at Christmas.

  • Where Portico di Romagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Ponte della Maestà e La Chiusa

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The bridge over the Montone and, a little further, the "beach" of the Chiusa, with a waterfall and large slabs of rock where to sunbathe.

  • Where Portico di Romagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · turismoforlivese.it

Abbazia di San Benedetto in Alpe

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Of the abbey mentioned by Dante remain part of the crypt, the cloister with the well, the defensive tower and the cells of the monks; in the old mill there is the visitor center of the Park.

  • Where San Benedetto in Alpe
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: turismoforlivese.it · parcoforestecasentinesi.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
