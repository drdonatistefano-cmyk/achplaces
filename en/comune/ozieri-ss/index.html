What to see in Ozieri

Centro del Logudoro which gave its name to the 'culture of Ozieri' of the final Neolithic: the Cave of San Michele in the village, the Romanesque Basilica of Sant'Antioco di Bisarcio on the plain of Chilivani and a historic center of terraced squares.

7 places to visit in Ozieri, in the province of Sassari (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Ozieri in the guide with map and filters

Grotta di San Michele

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Karst cave on the edge of the residential district, about 160 meters long, of which about sixty accessible: used for funeral and religious purposes between 3200 and 2800 BC, gives the name to the culture of Ozieri, the first widespread throughout Sardinia.

  • Where Ozieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Basilica di Sant'Antioco di Bisarcio

Places of worship · Basilicas · Viewpoint

On the plain of Chilivani, outside the village: among the highest expressions of the Romanesque in Sardinia, built in volcanic stone in three phases from the 11th century, with a portico with six spans and decorated apse; it was diocesan seat.

  • Where Piana di Chilivani, Ozieri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
