What to see in Montecarotto

Castello della Vallesina at about 388 meters, Verdicchio's land: walls of 1509 by Albertino di Giacomo da Cremona, the Clock Tower with the clock of Pietro Mei of 1849, the 19th-century Municipal Theatre that houses the Museum of Mail Art, the only Italian public museum dedicated to this art form, and the Collegiata with the organ Vici of 1776.

Torrione dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Symbol of the village, rest of the medieval fortifications: it has the current appearance since 1903, after the demolition of part of the walls and the civic tower. The two towers of the entrance are joined by a covered walkway; the cylindrical one, crenellated, carries the clock built in 1849 by Pietro Mei, still working and loaded by hand. From the high view of the valleys of Misa and Esino.

Accessible only on request, to do a few days before (Italia.it)

Teatro comunale

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Small Italian theatre of the second half of the nineteenth century, with a porticoed façade with brick columns and richly decorated interior; still used for shows and concerts.

Museo civico e della Mail Art (MAM)

Museums · Modern art

Founded in 1984, it has its headquarters in the Municipal Theatre and is the only Italian public museum dedicated to mail art; it also preserves prints of Orfeo Tamburi and Pericles Fazzini and the Lauretane Carte by Arnoldo Ciarrocchi.

Collegiata della Santissima Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish church with an organ from 1776 of Saverio and Sebastiano Vici, restored in 2009: tower clocks and reed organs testify to the artisan tradition of the village. In the village also the churches of San Filippo, the Crucifix, Santa Maria del Popolo and the convent church of San Francesco.

  • Where Montecarotto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Mura castellane e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The 1509 walls designed by Albertino di Giacomo da Cremona, which restored and strengthened a medieval circuit more ancient; Palazzo Baldoni is located on the south side. Near the Torrione the Town Hall and the Fountain of the Unity of Peoples.

Monumento ai Caduti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Vigne e cantine del Verdicchio

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

The village is located on calcareous-clay soils, with climate mitigated by the Apennine and Adriatic currents, between Verdicchio vineyards and olive groves; Italia.it recommends visiting a wine cellar by booking in advance. During the first week of July, Verdicchio in Festa in Florence, with tastings, local cuisine and shows.
