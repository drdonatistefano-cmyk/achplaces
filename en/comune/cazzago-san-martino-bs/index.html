What to see in Cazzago San Martino

The Franciacorta municipality is surrounded by vineyards and morainic hills overlooking Lake Iseo; in the village of Bornato there are the castle with the Renaissance Villa Orlando and the ancient parish church of San Bartolomeo, as well as the permanent collection of the Nativity scenes of the World.

Castello di Bornato e Villa Orlando

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

13th century castle built on the remains of an older building, with crenellated walls, towers, buttresses, moat, drawbridge and underground; inside the Gandini family had built in 1564 a Renaissance villa with frescoed halls, rare example of a Renaissance villa inside a medieval castle, since the 1930s of the Orlando family. The park has an Italian garden and an English garden with centuries-old trees, including a sophora japonica of about 200 years old; you can also visit the nineteenth-century secret cave, the ancient cellars under the main tower and the sacristy of the private chapel of St. Francis with sacred books of the eighteenth century and a Biblia of 1536. Guided tours and tastings for a fee (tel. 030 725006).

Presepi del Mondo

Museums · Themed

Home gallery set up as a permanent exhibition by Carlo Battista Castellini, carpenter of Bornato who collects nativity scenes since childhood: over a thousand pieces from more than 130 countries. The group Nativity scenes of the World was born in December 1998; during the Christmas period the collection is opened as an event, during the year it is visited on request telephone (030 725385, 348 7800332). Entry not accessible in wheelchair.

outside the Christmas period visitable only on telephone request (In Lombardy)

  • Where Via Tito Speri 11, Bornato, Cazzago San Martino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · comune.cazzago.bs.it

Antica Pieve di San Bartolomeo

History and architecture · Archaeology

Ancient parish church of Bornato dating back to the X-XI centuries, now an archaeological site; the Municipality has set up a visitor path with panels and audio guide (Italian and English) that leads from the castle to the parish. Each year it is the destination of the landscape and archaeological walk on the traces of Christendom in Franciacorta, organized by Pro Loco with the association U.S.P.A.A.A. (€5; info pievesanbartolomeobornato@gmail.com).

  • Where Via Pieve Vecchia, Bornato, Cazzago San Martino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · visitbrescia.it · comune.cazzago.bs.it
