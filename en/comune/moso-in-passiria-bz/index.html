What to see in Moso in Passiria

The municipality of Val Passiria and a member of the Alpine Pearls, with the hamlets of Corvara, Plata, Stulles and Plan: in a bunker of the Second World War the Bunker Mooseum tells the valley, the Nature Park Tessa Group and Monteneve mine, while around the village the waterfalls of Stieber and Stulles fall.

6 places to visit in Moso in Passiria, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Bunker Mooseum

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Museum opened in 2009 in a former bunker of the Second World War: it tells the history and nature of Val Passiria with the visitor center of the Gruppo di Tessa Nature Park, sections on the high Passiria, the mine of Monteneve and on geology and local nature, and the life of Moso and its inhabitants during the war. Outside a fence of the ibex and a climbing wall; guided tours and workshops on request.

  • Where Paese, Moso in Passiria
  • Entry €4.50, reduced €4/€2.50, family €9
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.bunker-mooseum.it

Sources: bunker-mooseum.it · cultura.gov.it · suedtirol.info

Miniera di Monteneve (Museo provinciale Miniere)

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The mining district of San Martino Monteneve, reachable only on foot from Val Passiria, includes a mining village at 2,355 m and the extraction area, inhabited and worked in very harsh conditions from the Middle Ages to 1967: it was the most important deposit of lead and zinc in Tyrol and between 2,000 and 2,500 m is presented as the highest mine in Europe, with over 150 km of tunnels. Today there are refuge, exhibition and mine adventure, with half day or full day excursions with the train of the mine; guided tours (Tuesday and Thursday 9:15, Saturday and Sunday 8:30) only by reservation before 16 of the day before, from the car park of Monteneve Bridge.

  • Where Corvara 52/53, Moso in Passiria
  • Entry Small tour €9, reduced €8; big tour €26, reduced €23
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.museominiere.it

Sources: museominiere.it · cultura.gov.it · suedtirol.info

Cascata dello Stieber

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Plan stream (Pfeldererbach) is thrown into the Passirio with two jumps of about 19 and 18 meters, admirable by panoramic wooden walkways: from Moso you cross the first road bridge and after about 50 m you take left the path to the walkways. Bus stops ...Moos... Dorf... or Moos.

  • Where Moso in Passiria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Cascata di Stulles

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Just below the village of Stulles, presented as the third highest waterfall in Europe: a first jump of about 112 meters and then about 230 others. Good points of view are Breiteben, the road of the Passo del Rombo and the path of the gorge of the Passirio.

  • Where Stulles, Moso in Passiria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Belvedere «I Granati»

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

Panoramic view of the rocky slope at Stulles with views of Moso and the bottom of the Val Passiria: two structures in the shape of garnet, inspired by the rocks of the area, are an exhibition hall and a panoramic platform and tell the highest waterfall in South Tyrol, the country with the highest birth rate in Europe and the most haystack. Managed by the MuseumHinterPasseier.

  • Where Dorf 29a, Moso in Passiria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Sentiero del lago di Kummer

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring for families around the ancient basin of Lake Kummer, about 1,340 m near Corvara, linked to a local legend: 11.6 km with eight educational stations on the history of the lake and its territory, starting from the Bunker Mooseum (1,007 m). Not suitable for strollers and wheelchairs.

  • Where Partenza dal Bunker Mooseum, Moso in Passiria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info
