What to see in Polignano a Mare

The pearl of the Adriatic Sea, birthplace of Domenico Modugno, is a village of white houses on a cliff excavated by about twenty sea caves: you enter the historical center from the Marchesal Arch, you go down to the famous Lama Monachile under the Bourbon bridge and you visit the Pino Pascali Foundation Museum, in the former slaughterhouse.

10 places to visit in Polignano a Mare, in the province of Bari (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e Arco Marchesale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village of medieval white houses on the cliff, inhabited since the Neolithic, with traces of Arabs, Byzantine, Spanish and Norman and the remains of four defensive towers. You enter it from the Arch of Marchesale (Porta Grande), of the sixteenth century, until 1780 only access to the village surrounded by a moat with drawbridge, today covered by Piazza Garibaldi. In the alleys: the Palazzo Marchesale dei feudatari, via Giudea (the ancient Jewish ghetto) with the churches of Purgatory and Santo Stefano, the verses painted by the street artist Guido Lupori (the "Guido il Flâneur") and the belvedere on the sea, like wide Cave Ardito at sunset.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Polignano a Mare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · interregaismart.regione.puglia.it

Palazzo dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Palace of medieval origin, today hidden by the rococo decorations of the windows, already seat of the university; it is accessed from the Arch of the Gate. The ground floor served as a storeroom of food and oil and the back from prison, of which remains a grate in via Tanese, the ancient Via Giudea. The tower, which had a sundial, houses a rare public clock with cables of the nineteenth century, surmounted by a statue of San Vito, patron, and a sailing bell tower.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Polignano a Mare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Fondazione Museo Pino Pascali

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Museum dedicated to the work of the sculptor and artist Pino Pascali, born in Polignano and died in the 1960s, in the former municipal slaughterhouse: Italia.it indicates it as the only permanent museum of contemporary art of Puglia and hosts contemporary art exhibitions. Open from Wednesday to Sunday, 10-13 and 16-20.

  • Where Via Parco del Lauro 119, Polignano a Mare
  • Entry €5, reduced available
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.fondazionepascali.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Lama Monachile (Cala Porto)

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Free beach of white pebbles between two high rock walls, in an ancient blade that cuts the cliff a few steps from the old town: the most famous and photographed view of the Bari coast, Blue Flag and 4 Vele di Legambiente. Very crowded in summer: it is better to arrive early.

  • Where Via San Vito, Polignano a Mare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Ponte Borbonico sulla Lama Monachile

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Bridge built by the Bourbons at the beginning of the nineteenth century about 15 meters above sea, panoramic point especially at sunset; from here you can see the path of the ancient Via Traiana between Benevento and Brindisi and a small Roman bridge restored to a single arch in blocks of tuff, still walkable. A staircase goes down to the cove.

  • Where Piazza Giuseppe Verdi, Polignano a Mare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · interregaismart.regione.puglia.it

Grotte di Polignano

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Twenty caves dug from the sea in the cliffs, accessible by boat, canoe or SUP and some swimming: the Palazzese, the largest, under a palace today hotel with restaurant; that of the Archbishopric, which is said to be connected by a passage to the bishop's palace; of the nuns, of the Rondinella, Ardito (with a natural column), Stampagnata (without roof) of Sella with a natural bridge and Chiangella.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small cobblestones at the bottom of a blade of Mediterranean maquis, between two spurs of rock: Blue Flag and 4 sails of Legambiente. You descend from a rocky slope (serve suitable shoes); free parking on the road above.
