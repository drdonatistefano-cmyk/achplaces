What to see in Chivasso

One of the most important centers of Canavese, with the late Dome of 1415 and the octagonal tower of the ancient castle of the Marquises of Monferrato, the prehistoric stele of Lapis Longus in piazza Castello, Palazzo Santa Chiara with its theater and the entrance of the nineteenth-century Canale Cavour on the Po.

6 places to visit in Chivasso, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo Collegiata di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Started in 1415, it has a precious façade in latedogotic terracotta of 1420 with a doorway, ghimberg, rosette and terracotta friezes. Inside is a polychrome terracotta group of the 15th century Pietà with eight figures and a Deposition from the 16th century Cross of Defendant Ferrari, born in Chivasso. Next to it, the octagonal tower is the only remnant of the castle built in the 12th century by the Marquises of Monferrato.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica, Chivasso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismotorino.org · comune.chivasso.to.it

Palazzo Santa Chiara e Teatrino Civico

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Building of the late eighteenth century born as a convent of the Clarisses and then became town hall. On the ground floor, obtained from the inner chapel of the former convent, the small horseshoe theatre with the audience and gallery designed in 1834 by Fausto Gozzano, father of the poet Guido; was inaugurated on 16 October 1864 by the Philodramatic Amateurs.

  • Where Piazza Generale Carlo Alberto Dalla Chiesa 6, Chivasso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.chivasso.to.it

Lapis Longus

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Stone stele about 4 meters high, dated to the VII-VI century BC, today in the center of Piazza Castello. It was found in 1499 near the church of San Michele, demolished in 1649.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Chivasso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Palazzo Luigi Einaudi e l'orologio rivoluzionario

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

17th century palace on Piazza d'Armi, born as barracks and now home to exhibitions and events. On the tympanum a rare decimal clock of the revolutionary age, with day divided in 10 hours by 100 minutes of 100 seconds, system abolished by Napoleon in 1806.

  • Where Lungo Piazza d'Armi 6, Chivasso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.chivasso.to.it

Canale Cavour e Edificio della Presa delle Acque

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Wanted by Camillo Benso di Cavour and built between 1862 and 1866, it is the third canal of Italy for length (almost 85 km): it brings the Po water to the rice fields of Vercellese and Novara up to Ticino. The stone and brick gripping building is 40 meters long, with 21 lights and three orders of gates; next to the bronze monument of 1898 by Francesco Porzio to the engineer Carlo Noè, designer of the work. The interior is visited only by reservation during the extraordinary openings with guided tour.

  • Where Viale Cavour, Chivasso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · italia.it

Via Torino, portici e nocciolini

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry

The old town is marked by medieval porticoes; along Via Torino, between porticoes and pastry shops, you can buy peanuts, small sweets typical of Piedmont hazelnuts, sugar and white. The square in front of the Duomo is one of the largest stretches in the centre.

  • Where Via Torino, Chivasso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismotorino.org
