What to see in Reggiolo

Ancient Canossian court of the Bassa Reggiana, then fortified outpost of the Municipality of Reggio and from 1432 of Gonzaga: the fortress with the mast of 1242, Martiri Square with the eighteenth-century theater Giovanni Rinaldi, the art collection of Antonio Ruggero Giorgi in town hall and the valleys reclaimed towards Novellara.

5 places to visit in Reggiolo, in the province of Reggio Emilia (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca di Reggiolo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

An example of the medieval low-plan military architecture, with corner towers and central mast of 1242; Reggiolo was a fortified outpost of the Municipality of Reggio from the 13th century and passed to Gonzaga in 1432. Damaged by the 2012 earthquake.

Damaged by the 2012 earthquake and not accessible according to the Touring itinerary; visible from outside

Teatro comunale Giovanni Rinaldi

History and architecture · Historic theatres

One of the oldest theaters in Italy: a "Teatro della Comunità" is mentioned in the Austrian cadastre of 1750 and the origins are traced back to a painting by Ambrogio Viarana of 1614. Renovated in 1832-1838 by the architect Luigi Sottili with the consent of Maria Luigia, he has three orders of 18 poplar stages with paintings trompe-l'oeil and horseshoe platform; around 1930 Giuseppe Moscardini painted the ceiling with the Music and medallions of Donizetti, Rossini, Verdi and Giovanni Rinaldi, grandfather of Nino Rota. Restored after the 2012 earthquake.

Open to the public in general only during shows or events

  • Where Piazza Martiri, Reggiolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Museums · Modern art

Pinacoteca housed in the Town Hall which exhibits oil paintings and graphic works by the contemporary Reggiolese artist Antonio Ruggero Giorgi.

Piazza Martiri, Palazzo Sartoretti e chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The main square, the heart of the village life, with historic buildings, cafes and shops. Nearby, the eighteenth-century Palazzo Sartoretti with a decorated lounge and views of the park, and the church of Santa Maria Assunta with a Corinthian façade.

  • Where Piazza Martiri, Reggiolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.reggioemiliawelcome.it

Sources: reggioemiliawelcome.it

Valli di Novellara e Reggiolo

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient marshes reclaimed between the twelfth century and the beginning of the twentieth century, today a naturalistic site of interest with the birdlife of the wetlands. Between canals and cultivated fields you birdwatching and biking.

  • Where Valli, Reggiolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.reggioemiliawelcome.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · reggioemiliawelcome.it
