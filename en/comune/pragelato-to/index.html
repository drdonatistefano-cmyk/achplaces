What to see in Pragelato

The municipality of the upper Val Chisone which hosted the Nordic ski races of the Olympic Games of Turin 2006: almost all the territory is in the Natural Park of the Val Troncea, and in the village you can visit the park museum, the Costume Museum and the House of the Escartons.

Parco naturale della Val Troncea

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Established in 1980, it covers 3,280 hectares (89% of the municipality) between about 1,670 and 3,280 meters, up to the Rognosa del Sestriere, the highest peak of the Val Chisone: larch, pine and pine crochet, chamois, ibex, marmots, deer, the wolf returned in the late nineties and over sixty species of birds including gipeto and royal eagle. In the villages of Seytes, Laval and Troncea; at the hill of Beth the mines of calcopite, where in 1904 an avalanche overwhelmed 81 miners. In winter it is a cross-country skiing destination.

  • Where Val Troncea, Pragelato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.parchialpicozie.it

Sources: parchialpicozie.it · turismotorino.org · visitpiemonte.com

Museo naturalistico del Parco Val Troncea

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In the park: panels on woods, traditional architecture and history of the territory, flora and fauna with stuffed animals, the presence of man in the valley over the centuries; outside an ethnobotanic path.

  • Where Via della Pineta 5, frazione La Ruà, Pragelato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org · parchialpicozie.it

Museo del Costume e delle Tradizioni delle Genti Alpine

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Local agricultural objects and a rich collection of women's costumes from the Western Alps, including those from Pragelato and Fenestrelle, with typical headphones and multicolored fabrics, and costumes from the Val di Susa and Queyras; the ticket includes a guided tour of about an hour and a half.

  • Where Via Rivet 2, Pragelato
  • Entry €4, free under 8 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Casa degli Escartons «Alex Berton»

Museums · City history · Free entry

In a building of the middle seventeenth century run by the Park, tells the Escartons, an autonomous federation that from 1343 to 1713 managed administrative and fiscal affairs: it houses a wardrobe of the sculptor Guido Ronchail with seven keys, symbol of the agreement between the communities, the archive of Remigio Bermond and a library of about 3,000 volumes. Open by reservation.

  • Where Via Nazionale angolo via San Giovanni, frazione Rivet, Pragelato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org
