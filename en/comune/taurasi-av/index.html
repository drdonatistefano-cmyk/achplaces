What to see in Taurasi

Irpino village dominated by the Marchionale Castle, probably of Lombard origin and rebuilt by the Normans, with next to the Baroque Collegiate of San Marciano; in the municipal palace the Archaeological Museum exhibits the eneolithic finds of the excavations of San Martino district.

Museo archeologico di Taurasi

Museums · Archaeological

In the municipal palace, next to the church of the Most Holy Rosary, it collects the finds of the excavations conducted between 1993 and 1996 in the district of San Martino from the then archaeological Superintendence of Salerno, Avellino and Benevento: funeral and housing structures of the Eneolithic (about 5000-3000 BC), with ceramics and urns found inside the huts, sign that the deceased were buried in the house or next door. The route is chronological and topographic, a showcase for hut with drawings and reconstructions; among the accessories a dagger and a pin, among the oldest metal objects attested in Italy. Different times between the MiC card and the provincial one: it is better to call (0827 74004).

Castello Marchionale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Well-preserved structure and several times transformed, probably of Longobard origin; devastated by the Saracens, it was rebuilt by the Normans. Families such as the Gesualdo, the Filangieri, the Ludovisi and the Carafa stayed there.

  • Where Largo Duomo 1, Taurasi
  • Entry To be checked
