What to see in Cisterna d'Asti

Country in hill ridge, the only municipality of Astigiano that belongs geographically to Roero, among vineyards, woods and rocks; the castle, noble residence from the eighteenth century with a tower of the thirteenth century, houses the Museum Arts and Crafts of a time.

Castello di Cisterna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It was mentioned for the first time in a document of 911, it was a fortified fence that in Baroque times became a noble country residence; it took its present form in the eighteenth century. From the medieval phase remain the corner tower of the thirteenth century, used as a lookout and last shelter, and the imposing walled wall; in the inner courtyard the cistern that would give the name to the village. You enter from an ancient arch with the coat of arms of Pope Innocent XII, memory of the lordship of the Church.

Museo Arti e Mestieri di un Tempo

Museums · Themed

Born on October 5, 1980 in the rooms of the castle, the Pro Loco group collects objects of use, tools, machines and tools of the traditional Piedmontese peasant and craftsmanship from the 17th to the 20th century, distributed in about twenty rebuilt workshops. Midweek visits and in the morning by reservation for groups of at least 15 people; Monday closed.

Bottega del Vino

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

In an 18th-century brick building, with the typical crutin excavated in the sandstone. The wine of the village is the Cisterna DOC, red from croatine grapes (traditionally called Bonarda); among the local specialties Meliga biscuits, apple pie, honey and grape mustard.
