Parco dei Gessi Bolognesi e Calanchi dell'Abbadessa

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 5,000 hectares of chalk and calanchi at the gates of Bologna, part of the UNESCO site on karst in the evaporites.

  • Where San Lazzaro di Savena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Grotta del Farneto

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Grotto in plaster discovered by Francesco Orsoni in the nineteenth century and reopened in 2008, with an important protohistoric settlement; Luigi Fantini found a necropolis of the Copper Age.

  • Where Centro visite Casa Fantini, Via Jussi 171, Farneto, San Lazzaro di Savena
  • Entry Guided tours for a fee: fare with reservation
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website enteparchi.bo.it

Sources: enteparchi.bo.it · cultura.gov.it · bolognawelcome.com · appenninobolognese.cittametropolitana.bo.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Abbazia di Santa Cecilia della Croara

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Church in the park with a altarpiece attributed to Hannibal Carracci; recognized by UNESCO as a "monument messenger of culture of peace."

  • Where Croara, San Lazzaro di Savena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
