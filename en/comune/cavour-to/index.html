What to see in Cavour

Country at the foot of the Rocca di Cavour, isolated inselberg in the plain and nature reserve since 1980; at the entrance of the village stands the Abbey of Santa Maria, which houses a crypt with the oldest altar of Piedmont and the Archaeological Museum Caburrum, dedicated to the Roman town hall of Forum Vibii Caburrum from which derives the name of the village.

Abbazia di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Benedictine abbey complex of municipal property, at the entrance of the village at the foot of the Fortress, where stood the Roman settlement of Forum Vibii Caburrum. Restored in 1037 at the behest of the bishop of Turin Landolfo, it was destroyed in 1592 by the French troops of Lesdiguières and rebuilt in Baroque forms in the eighteenth century, smaller than the original; the early Christian crypt, with vault on Lombard columns of recovery, preserves the altar considered the oldest of Piedmont. The building of the vats, where the grapes of the monks were worked, is now an exhibition hall. Open Saturday 15-19 and Sunday 10-19, week by reservation.

  • Where Via Saluzzo 72, Cavour
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org · piemonteparchi.it · comune.cavour.to.it

Museo archeologico Caburrum

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

Housed in the Abbey of Santa Maria complex, it exhibits mainly terracotta, stone and funerary finds, partly collected by the parish priest in the second half of the twentieth century; from the territory come amphorae, skylights, tears, fibula and a funerary stele with inscription of Quinto Mannio Stellatino. Open on weekends with free entry.

  • Where Abbazia di Santa Maria, Via Saluzzo 72, Cavour
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: piemonteparchi.it · turismotorino.org

Riserva naturale della Rocca di Cavour

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Inselberg of gneiss eyedino del Carbonifero, the last strip of the Cozie Alps isolated from alluvial sediments: about 75 hectares, regional natural park since 1980 and 1995 reserve of the Po cuneese Park, the only wooded area within ten kilometers, with over 100 species of birds observed. From the parking lot of Santa Maria, a marked path reaches the top in less than an hour, passing through the "Pera d'la pasa" (Pear of the Pansa) at the top of the public garden overlooking the village, the Tower of Bramafam (a medieval sighting), the remains of the castle of the lords of Piossasco and stretches of the medieval walls.

  • Where Parcheggio dell'Abbazia di Santa Maria, Via Saluzzo, Cavour
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: piemonteparchi.it · turismotorino.org

Fontana del 1828 e Scala Santa

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the square of the parish church, where centuries of the town's history are concentrated, the fountain of 1828 rises on the site of the Roman thermal baths; another fountain at the Santa Scala has baths supported by elements of imperial age.

  • Where Piazza della chiesa parrocchiale, Cavour
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: piemonteparchi.it · turismotorino.org
