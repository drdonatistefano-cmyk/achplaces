What to see in Masullas

Country of the high Marmilla at the foot of Monte Arci, Masullas has in the former convent of the Capuchins the GeoMuseum "Stefano Incani" and the church of San Francesco, in addition to the Romanesque San Leonardo, the oldest in the country. In the territory of the park of Monte Arci there are the forest of Taraxi and the park of the obsidiana of Conca 'e Cannas.

GeoMuseo del Monte Arci "Stefano Incani"

Museums · Science and university

In the former seventeenth-century convent of the Capuchins, next to the church of San Francesco, it tells the geology of Sardinia and Marmilla, from the formation of the Mediterranean to the evolution of Monte Arci, an ancient volcano today regional park. It exhibits thousands of minerals and about 1,800 fossils, including fossil fish, colored diaspris, hydrothermal minerals such as quartz, limedonia, fluorite and large crystals of calcite, dioramas on the activity of the volcano and a room with fluorescent minerals. The collections are born from the cataloguing work of Vincenzo Incani.

Chiesa di San Leonardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest place of worship in the country, dating from the construction characteristics around the middle of the thirteenth century, on a building previously perhaps Byzantine; it belonged to the Vallombrosan abbey of Thamis and was then used as a granite mountain, until the first documented restoration of 1859. It has a unique nave with semicircular apse, sandstone and trachyte cones, facade with false loggias, bifora, sail bell tower and portal with monolithic architrave; inside capitals with vegetable decorations and a face carved on the monophora of the apse.

Parrocchiale della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built to unite the two districts of origin of the village and rebuilt at the end of the seventeenth century with wooden roof and new facade. It preserves a early Christian baptismal font (VI-VII century A.D.) and a wooden retablo of 1676.

Monte Arci: bosco di Taraxi e parco dell'ossidiana di Conca 'e Cannas

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

In the territory of Masullas the park of Monte Arci includes the Taraxi wood, the rocky wall of on Columbariu, the underwater lava formation of on Carongiu de Fanari, the split mass of Sa Perda Sperrada and the park of the obsidian of Conca 'e Cannas, the major Sardinian deposit of obsidian prehistoric, exploited by the 6th millennium BC. In the territory there are also the prenurgical menhirs of Sa Pedra Isposa and the remains of twelve nuraghi, including Su Para.
