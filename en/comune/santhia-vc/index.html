What to see in Santhià

City of the Vercelli plain along the Via Francigena, surrounded by rice fields and famous for the historical Carnival documented since 1328: the Cathedral of Sant'Agata with the Romanesque crypt of Santo Stefano and the 12th century bell tower, the church of SS. Trinità, the village of Vettignè with its fifteenth-century castle and, in the countryside, the hydraulic works of the Cavour Canal.

8 places to visit in Santhià, in the province of Vercelli (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di Sant'Agata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built between 1836 and 1839 on a project by Giuseppe Talucchi, with neoclassical facade with six columns and frescoes by Luigi Hartmann; inside, with three naves, the frescoes on the life of Sant'Agata (1858-1862) by Paolo Emilio Morgari, Luigi Hartman and Carlo Costa, the nineteenth-century organ of the Serassi, the polyptych of Gerolamo Giovenone of 1531 and the chapel of Sant'Ignazio da Santhià, canonized cappuccino in 2002. The 12th century Romanesque bell tower, about 35 metres high, lost its summit in 1555 under the cannons; with the crypt it is a national monument. Open every day 7.30-12 and 17-18.30.

  • Where Centro storico, Santhià
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Cripta romanica di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Crypt of the twelfth century under the apse of the Duomo, three naves with vaults cruising on two rows of unequal columns, with traces of the door and walled windows and an altar rested on two fifteenth century foliage capitals. It recalls the parish church of Santo Stefano, the first Christian church of the village (about 5th century), built near a Roman sacellus of which remain a stele to Jupiter and two capitals. Visitable during the cathedral hours.

  • Where Sotto l'abside del Duomo, Santhià
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: santhiaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa della SS. Trinità

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built around the middle of the fifteenth century by the Brotherhood of the SS. Trinity, which in 1634 welcomed that of the Holy Apostles: from the union comes the asymmetrical form with two communicating naves, the minor closed by the late Renaissance chapel of the Madonna del Carmine. It preserves a wooden altar of the late seventeenth century and a sixteenth-century altar of Vercelli's school with the Trinity and Saints Peter and Paul; baroque bell tower of 1745-1750. Recently restored, open mon-ven 8.30-12.

  • Where Centro storico, Santhià
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: santhiaturismo.it · italia.it

Castello e borgo di Vettignè

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The castle dates back to about 1460, with a quadrangular plan around a large courtyard, with a cylindrical tower, a square tower and a tower where you can still see the entrance with drawbridge; transformed between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, passed to the Savoys in 1867 with the wedding of Maria Vittoria From the Well of the Cisterna and Amedeo of Savoy, who built the royal apartments there, and was sold to private individuals after the Second World War. The village, which had more than 500 inhabitants at the beginning of the 20th century, is now almost depopulated and in precarious state, but offers views of the rice fields with the Alps in the background.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: santhiaturismo.it · italia.it

Canale Cavour e tombe sifone dell'Elvo

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

At Vettignè the Canale Cavour, built between 1863 and 1866 and 82 km long from Chivasso al Ticino, passes under the Elvo stream with a syphony tomb tunnel, while the nearby Canale Vanoni climbs over it with a bridge-channel; at the entrance door 110 m3/s, which go up to 180 with the Farini and the Naviglio d'Ivrea.

  • Where Presso Vettignè, Santhià
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: santhiaturismo.it · italia.it

Stazione idrometrica sperimentale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Early twentieth-century building of liberty and eclectic taste, with three-level turret, on the right bank of the Naviglio d'Ivrea, built to measure the flow rates of the channels born after the Cavour Canal and regulate the distribution of water; it worked little for the failures caused by the silhouette of the Dora Baltea. Relying on the Consorteria Canali Cavour, it is a pole of the Ecomuseo delle Terre d'Acqua and is visible only from the outside.

  • Where Naviglio d'Ivrea, Santhià
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: santhiaturismo.it · italia.it

Galleria civica d'arte moderna – Auditorium San Francesco

Museums · Modern art

In the former church of San Francesco, part of the ancient hospital, which since the eighties hosted the National Exhibition of Contemporary Painting "Santhià" promoted by the Pro Loco founded in 1962: the paintings awarded in various editions form the Civic Gallery of Modern Art City of Santhià, with over 350 works exhibited on the walls of the Auditorium. Rotating art exhibitions are also held at the Jacopo Durandi Cultural Centre.

La Mandria

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

18th century farmhouse for the breeding of horses, built in 1741 on a project by Giacinto Baijs, collaborator of Benedetto Alfieri, along the ancient road to Cavaglià and Ivrea; of the plant remains the perimeter of the large court closed by two pavilions, the main one with red brick facade in need of restoration.

  • Where Strada per Cavaglià, Santhià
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
