What to see in Sanfront

The village of Valle Po at the foot of Monte Bracco, with medieval porticos, the fifteenth-century parish church of San Martino and the Borgo Vecchio along the Lea; on the slopes of the Bracco are the hamlet-museum of Balma Boves, built under a huge rock, the rock engravings of the Rocca la Casna and the climbing walls.

Borgata Museo Balma Boves

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Settling built entirely under a huge mass of gneiss on Mount Bracco, about 650 meters, in the chestnut woods; settled until the 1950s-sixties, today it is an ecomuseum. You can visit stone houses with flat roofs (used to repair hay and charms), stables, barns, tool store, dryer, sink and bread oven, some houses with original furnishings. It is reached on foot in about half an hour from the hamlet Rocchetta, for mule tracks between secular chestnut trees; guided tours from June to October.

  • Where Frazione Rocchetta, loc. Balma Boves, Sanfront
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.balmaboves.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church with original structure of 1494, transformed at the end of the nineteenth century, which preserves Gothic traits in the central nave and the pentagonal apse. Outside there is a fresco of the 15th century of the Virgin with the Child in terracotta arches; inside there are nineteenth-century frescoes by Netu Borgna, eighteenth-century wooden sculptures and a white stone stove from 1563.

  • Where Centro, Sanfront
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.sanfront.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Piazza Statuto, portici e Borgo Vecchio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

On Piazza Statuto overlook the Palazzo Comunale, the church of the Confraternita del Gonfalone with Baroque bell tower and the Wing of the Market, imposing 19th century building. In via Mazzini run the low medieval porticos, with a capital carved from the fifteenth century of the destroyed portico of justice; from Borgo Vecchio the tree-lined avenue of the "Lea", along the river Albetta, rises to the hill of the ancient castle, conquered by the Savoys in 1487 and destroyed in the seventeenth century.

  • Where Piazza Statuto, Sanfront
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.sanfront.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Rocca la Casna, incisioni rupestri

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Site of rock art about an hour's walk from Balma Boves along a hiking trail, with cups and anthropomorphic figures engraved in the rock; the engravings of the area testify to a human presence much older than the hamlet.

  • Where Rocca la Casna, loc. Solie, Sanfront
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · comune.sanfront.cn.it

Monte Bracco ed escursioni

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

It is reachable on foot with 738 meters of difference in altitude, with views of the plain and the western Alps; other destinations are the Montebracco Cross, the Bric La Plata (1731 m), the Madonna d'Oriente (782 m) in a plateau between high chestnut trees, the Chapel of San Bernardo Agnelli (1125 m, also by mountain bike) and that of San Bernardo di Gilba (1517 m).

  • Where Pendici del Monte Bracco, Sanfront
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.sanfront.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Palestra di roccia del Bracco

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural walls on the slopes of Bracco, frequented also in winter thanks to the exposure to the sun, with the Ninfe Falesia and other routes from 4a to 7c+; there is also a boulder area with 11 blocks and over 90 passages.

  • Where Pendici del Monte Bracco, Sanfront
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · comune.sanfront.cn.it
