What to see in Mignano Monte Lungo

The town of Casertano between hills and mountains, known for the battle of Monte Lungo on December 8-16, 1943, the first of the renewed Italian army against the Germans, remembered by the military shrine, the only one dedicated to the war of Liberation; above the village dominates the medieval castle.

Sacrario militare e Museo storico di Monte Lungo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Ossario inaugurated in 1951 that preserves over 900 corpses: the soldiers of the renewed Italian army fallen in the first battle against the Germans (8-16 December 1943) between the gorges of Mignano and the slopes of Mount Long, and the fallen of the war of Liberation (1943-1945) collected by war cemeteries of the peninsula; it is the only military shrine dedicated to this war. The attached museum reconstructs the phases of the battle and the Italian participation in Liberation with weapons, allied and German relics and photographs. Summer time 8:30-17:15, winter 8:30-15:45.

  • Where Via Casilina Nord 1, Mignano Monte Lungo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.mignanomontelungo.ce.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · comune.mignanomontelungo.ce.it

Castello di Mignano Monte Lungo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval fortification from the mighty walls, on a hill overlooking the village and the surrounding landscape; born with military function, today it is a historical symbol of the country and houses exhibitions, concerts, theatre shows, art exhibitions and local crafts and an information point for tourists. According to the municipality is open Monday-Friday 9:30-13 and 15-18, Saturday and Sunday 10-18, partially accessible to people with disabilities.

  • Where Corso Umberto I 9, Mignano Monte Lungo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.mignanomontelungo.ce.it

Sources: comune.mignanomontelungo.ce.it
