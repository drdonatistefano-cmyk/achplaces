What to see in Larciano

Municipality between Montalbano and the Padule di Fucecchio: high up the walled village of Larciano Castello with the tower and the Civic Archaeological Museum, then the village of Cecina with San Niccolò, Castelmartini with Villa Banchieri and the Garden of Memory for the massacre of the Padule of 1944, and in the plain the center visits to the reserve and the museum of the peasant civilization Casa Dei.

8 places to visit in Larciano, in the province of Pistoia (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Larciano Castello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village that preserves its character within thirteenth-century walls, with access by one of the three ancient surviving doors; the position made it a strategic fortification to guard the Valdinievole and defend Pistoia.

  • Where Larciano Castello, Larciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Castello di Larciano e Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Viewpoint

Ancient stronghold at the highest point of Larciano, the last refuge of the population in wartime, with irregular plan following the hill; inside the walls a church, a palace with tower, the mast, an ancient cistern and the foundations of the Captain's house. The 36-metre tower-cassero offers a wide view and since 1975 hosts the Civic Museum: kitchen ceramics, metals, polychrome majolica, brickwork, burials and everyday objects, with a didactic section and reconstruction of a tomb to the cappuccino of the Roman age. The sources date the castle between the XI-XII and the XIV century.

  • Where Piazza Castello 55, Larciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Borgo di Cecina e chiesa di San Niccolò

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small village on a high hill of just over 100 meters, with name that goes back to an Etruscan family and walls preserved by the role of control in the Middle Ages; the small Romanesque church of San Niccolò holds an important wooden crucifix and from there opens the panorama on the Valdinievole. In autumn there is the Castagnata.

  • Where Cecina, Larciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Giardino della Memoria e monumento «Lo Stupore»

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

In Castelmartini, the Garden of Memory, designed by the artist Andrea Dami for the victims of the massacre of the Padule di Fucecchio in August 1944, with the work "Paysage" of 175 plasticized iron signs, one per victim, and "My brother is here" by Simone Fagioli; near the monument "Lo Stupore" by the master Gino Terri, inaugurated by President Ciampi.

  • Where Castelmartini, Larciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Riserva del Padule di Fucecchio e centro visite

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

The largest inland marsh in Italy, about 1,800 hectares between the provinces of Pistoia and Florence, of which about 230 in nature reserve: mirrors of water between reeds, florid and carican ferns, more than 200 species of birds and the most important gauze of central-southern Italy with seven species of herons, as well as black cranes and storks. In Larciano the visitor centre of the reserve of the Padule di Fucecchio and Castelmartini, with exhibitions, guided tours of nature and history and workshops.

  • Where Via Don Franco Malucchi 115, Larciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo della civiltà contadina Casa Dei

Museums · Themed

In a farmhouse in the Padule di Fucecchio, founded by the Dei family and in the regional museum network since 2012: agricultural machines and tools, everyday objects, books, photographs, documents and postcards to tell the link between landscape, field work and domestic life, with educational activities for adults and children.

the MiC card indicates "closed" every day without hours; telephone contacts. 335 7789139, associationcasadei@gmail.com

  • Where Via Traversa di Brugnana, località Bagnolo, Larciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museocasadei.it
