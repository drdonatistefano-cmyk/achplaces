What to see in Borgosesia

At the foot of Mount Fenera, the main economic centre of Valsesia: the parish church of Saints Peter and Paul with works by Bernardino Lanino and Tanzio da Varallo, the Sacred Mount of Sant'Anna di Montrigone and the Carlo Conti Museum with prehistoric finds of the caves of Fenera; famous Carnival with the Mercu Scurot, born in 1854.

Santuario di Sant'Anna di Montrigone

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Complex of the system of the Sacred Mountains pre-Alpine on the hill of Mount Rigone, with Mount Fenera in the background. The climb between the pines touches 13 newsstands of the Via Crucis painted by Lorenzo Peracino and erected by 1763; on top the church with six chapels on the life of the Virgin, about 150 polychrome terracotta statues and the dome with the Assumption. Built around 1648 for a vow during the plague, with the commitment of the miller Gian Battista Daij called "Paniga" , remembered on the facade. Open for Sunday celebrations.

Museo di Archeologia e Paleontologia «Carlo Conti»

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 2007 and dedicated to the sculptor Carlo Conti, honorary inspector of the Superintendence, exhibits palaeontological and archaeological finds from the excavations begun in the 1930s in the caves of Monte Fenera: casts of bone remains of Neanderthal, the skeleton re-composed of a cave bear, the pleistocene fauna and the human presence from the Middle Paleolithic. Open on Saturday 15-18.

  • Where Via Combattenti d'Italia, Borgosesia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museocarloconti.it

Sources: museocarloconti.it · cultura.gov.it · visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Centro storico e Parco Magni

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The historic centre preserves the ancient village with lively streets and squares; in the main square the ceremony of the Busecca opens the Carnival. The Magni Park overlooks the left bank of Sesia.

  • Where Piazza Martiri, Borgosesia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Sources: visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Monte Fenera

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Reachable on foot from Borgosesia, it keeps the caves of prehistoric finds exposed to the Museo Conti; for trekking you go up to the village Fenera San Giulio and follow the signs to Cima Bastia.

  • Where Fenera San Giulio, Borgosesia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page visitvalsesiavercelli.it

Sources: visitvalsesiavercelli.it
