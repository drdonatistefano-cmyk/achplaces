What to see in Biassono

Crossed by Lambro and among the main accesses to the Parco di Monza, Biassono gathers around Piazza Italia the Baroque Villa Verri, frequented by the Lombard illuminators, the Palazzo Croce Bossi and the Civic Museum Carlo Verri; in the historic center the parish church of San Martino.

Museo civico Carlo Verri

Museums · City history

Founded in 1977 mainly for archaeological finds of Biassono of the Group of Archaeistorical Research of Lambro, next to Villa Verri. On the ground floor there is the ethnological section with ploughs, sowers, wine presses, a leaf cutter for mulberry, a rare incubator for silk baco eggs and the reconstruction of kitchen and bedroom; on the upper floor there are local finds and the numismatic collection, with the storage room of 2235 Roman bronze and silver coins found at the Roman villa of Cascina Sant'Andrea. Detachable sections: since 2006 ©Segno Scrittura Stampa , on the history of communication, next to the library, and since 2009 the peasant world under the arcades of Cascina Ca' Nova.

  • Where Piazza Italia, Biassono
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museobiassono.it

Sources: museobiassono.it · in-lombardia.it

Villa Verri

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Early eighteenth century building in Lombard Baroque style, now home of the Town Hall; guests were protagonists of the Enlightenment such as the brothers Pietro and Alessandro Verri, Cesare Beccaria and Giuseppe Parini.

  • Where Piazza Italia, Biassono
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Palazzo Croce Bossi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Historical building in the 16th century, with sandstone portal, porch with loggia and painted decorations on walls and wooden ceilings. It is a municipal property and houses the civic library and a classroom of the Verri Museum.

  • Where Piazza Italia, Biassono
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The first testimonies date back to the thirteenth century, but the foundation is considered to be earlier. It preserves valuable mural paintings in the presbytery and, of the same period, the wooden furnishings of the sacristy.

  • Where Via Asperto, Biassono
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Santuario della Madonna della Brughiera

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Small church built in 1834 after an epidemic of cholera. It houses an altarpiece with a Madonna adoring Bolognese school of the early sixteenth century and, in a chapel, an Annunciation attributed to a pupil of the Nuvolone school.

  • Where Via Don Consonni, Biassono
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
