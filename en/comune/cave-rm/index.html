What to see in Cave

Cittadina dei Monti Prenestini was born from the medieval Castrum Cavarum, linked to the sculptor Lorenzo Ferri, whose civic museum preserves the gipsoteca and the Monumental Nativity with statues of over 4 meters; outside the village the Sanctuary of the Madonna del Campo and the Park of Villa Clementi with the Fountain of Santo Stefano.

Museo civico Città di Cave - Museo Lorenzo Ferri

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Dedicated to the sculptor, restorer and sindonologist Lorenzo Ferri (1902-1975), brought to Cave by the parish priest Mgr. Lorenzo Castellani. Inaugurated in December 2013 in the palace of the former Mattei Hospital, it exhibits on three floors the plasters from the 1920s to 1975: sketches of the statues of Trilussa and Giolitti, Ecce Homo, Christ the Redeemer, reliefs for the competition of the bronze doors of St. Peter, the Alata Victory, the bas-relief of almost three meters ...The legend of Orpheus and the oil of Eva in Eden. Tactile path for people with visual disabilities; open Wednesday-Sunday 10:30-12:30 and 16:30-19:30.

  • Where Via Cavour 23, Cave
  • Entry €3, reduced €2,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museolorenzoferricave.eu

Sources: museolorenzoferricave.eu · comune.cave.rm.it · visitlazio.com

Presepe Monumentale di Lorenzo Ferri

Museums · Modern art

Section of the Civic Museum inaugurated in December 2012 in the underground rooms of the Palazzo Comunale, former convent of the Augustinians: nine plaster statues over 4 meters tall representing the Epiphany, carved by Ferri in 1947-48 after winning a competition of the Pallottini Fathers for Sant'Andrea della Valle in Rome. Visitable all year round.

  • Where Palazzo Comunale, Piazza Giuseppe Garibaldi 6, Cave
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: comune.cave.rm.it · visitlazio.com

Santuario della Madonna del Campo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

In the locality Campo, where tradition places the battle of 267 BC narrated by Livio and where the Castrum Trebarum stood. On April 27, 1655, during road work desired by Cardinal Girolamo Colonna, the fresco of the Madonna on the throne with the Child and Saints Peter and Paul re-emerged; the church was consecrated in 1707 and repeatedly restored after the earthquake of 1915. A unique nave with frescoed apse by Luigi Leggeri, a coffered ceiling of the early twentieth century and a double staircase that descends to the crypt with the fresco; the Madonna del Campo has been patron saint of Cave since 1880.

  • Where Via Madonna del Campo 133, Cave
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: comune.cave.rm.it · visitlazio.com

Parco Naturale Monumentale Villa Clementi

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Natural monument recognized by the Lazio Region, includes the municipal park, the nineteenth-century villa of the Clementi family, tree-lined avenues, fountains and the mineral spring of Santo Stefano, whose water, it is said, also helped Michelangelo. During the war the villa was occupied by the Germans and bombed; at the end of the seventies the family donated the part above the SS155 to the municipality. Today lindens, acacias, cypresses, firs and chestnuts on a slope, with ample parking.

  • Where Via Madonna del Campo 13, Cave
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: comune.cave.rm.it · visitlazio.com
