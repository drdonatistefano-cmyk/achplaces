What to see in Mazzè

Country of the Canavese on a hill on the right of the Dora Baltea, once on the border between Canavese and Vercellese: at the top of the historic center, with the remains of the shelter and nineteenth-century palaces, stands the castle of the Valperga with the museum of the instruments of the Inquisition in the underground; from the castle a path descends to the Dora.

Castello di Mazzè e Museo degli strumenti della Santa Inquisizione

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortified complex on top of a hill on the right bank of the Dora Baltea, in a strategic position between Canavese and Vercellese. It was of the Counts Valperga di Mazzè from 1141 to 1840 (the building on ancient Roman ruins is dated to 1316, with the house-fortress shortly after), then of the Brunetta d'Usseaux and the Salino di Cavaglià, current owners, who restored it in about twenty years after the lootings of the seventies. In 1515, he was occupied by Francesco I of France and hosted Vittorio Emanuele II in 1859; seat of the German command, here in May 1945 the surrender to the allied troops was signed. In the underground, in part of Celtic and Roman times, the Museum of the Instruments of the Holy Inquisition.

Centro storico di Mazzè

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the top of the hill that dominates the current town: remains of the ancient shelter, noble palaces of the nineteenth century, houses of the same era, the parish church and the castle. The old 18th-century municipal building, today in abandonment, is remarkable. Pro Loco organizes guided tours from May to September on Sunday from 15 to 18; in other periods by reservation (discover.mazze@prolocomazze.it).

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Gervasio e Protasio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Basilica-style building with three naves with Baroque façade, built on the family chapel of the Counts Valperga, with first news from the twelfth century. It is accessed from a large staircase.

Sentiero naturalistico e Forra della Rocca

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Path that starts from the castle and goes down to the Dora; along the route you will find the Forra della Rocca, where the hydraulic works of reclamation of the canavesan marsh are supposed to have occurred.
