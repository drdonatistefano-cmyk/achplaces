What to see in Lana

The largest common fruit of South Tyrol, between Merano and Bolzano: in Lana di Sotto the parish with the door-to-door altar by Hans Schnatterpeck, the largest in the Alps, and the Museum of Fruit Growing; around the Gaul Gorge, the path of the Brandis Roggia, the Foiana Farm Museum and Monte San Vigilio by cable car.

6 places to visit in Lana, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parrocchiale dell'Assunta e altare di Schnatterpeck

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church consecrated in 1492 on the site of a more ancient Romanesque church. It keeps the altar with carved doors in chestnut wood and gilded with pure gold by the sculptor Sveto Hans Schnatterpeck in about eight years and consecrated around 1511: about 14 meters high and 7 meters wide, it is indicated as the largest Gothic altar in the Alps, with a rare Throne of Grace between the life-size statues of Peter and Paul. It was paid by about 800 inhabitants of Lana.

church open only during guided tours, closed altar in Lent

  • Where Via Schnatterpeck, Lana di Sotto

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.meranerland.org

Museo altoatesino della frutticoltura

Museums · Themed

In the medieval residence Larchgut, a monument protected with a tower mentioned in 1301, about 1,000 square meters of exposure on the history of fruit growing and rural life: from the objects of viticulture, including the oldest wine press in South Tyrol (1570), to the tools of the nineteenth century, vehicles and machines in the barn; tells the boom of fruit after the reclamation of the Val d'Adige. Behind the parish with the altar of Schnatrepeck.

  • Where Via Brandis 4, Lana di Sotto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.obstbaumuseum.it

Sources: obstbaumuseum.it · suedtirol.info · meranerland.org

Museo contadino di Foiana

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Opened in 1977 in an annex of the rectory (former oven, woodshed, laundry and pigsty), it tells the hard peasant life of the Seven and Nineteenth Century: on the ground floor a rebuilt stube, tools, baskets and linen processing, with a wheat mill of Auna di Sopra; the upper floor is dedicated to spinning and weaving. Open from April to the end of October; welcome offers. Nearby starts the path of the chestnut of Foiana.

  • Where Via dei Bagni 2, Foiana, Lana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.meranerland.org

Sources: meranerland.org

Gola di Gaul

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Deep and wild gorge at the entrance of Val d'Ultimo, with walls of porphyry quartz, gneiss and granite, where the Valsura flows; fresh in summer, with lush vegetation. The walk of the Gaul starts from the bridge over the Valsura in Lana di Sopra on wooden bridges to the meadow under Castel Braunsberg and a waterfall (about 20 minutes). Always accessible; in winter ice rink in the throat. Do not go down into the riverbed: the level can rise quickly.

  • Where Ponte sul Valsura, Lana di Sopra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.meranerland.org

Sources: meranerland.org

Sentiero della roggia di Brandis

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

An easy walk of about 3 km (one hour) along the road built by the lords of Brandis in 1835, today without water, from Lana di Sopra to the waterfall of Brandis to Lana di Sotto: orchards, vineyards and woods, view of Lana and Merano, the chapel of Santa Margherita, the convent of Lanegg, a shelf of open books and views of the ruins of Castel Brandis. Suitable for strollers and wheelchairs, accessible all year round.

  • Where Incrocio Am Gries-Via Palade, Lana di Sopra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.meranerland.org

Sources: meranerland.org

Monte San Vigilio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Mountain of the house above Lana, about 1,800 meters, reached by a modern cable car from Lana di Sopra up to 1,486 meters; the cable car of Monte San Vigilio is indicated as the second oldest in Europe. The church of San Vigilio, probably fourteenth-century, is located on an ancient place of worship at 1,790 metres, the Gampl mountain pasture and the Bärenbad alpine pasture; in winter a small ski area.

  • Where Stazione a valle, Lana di Sopra
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.meranerland.org

Sources: meranerland.org
