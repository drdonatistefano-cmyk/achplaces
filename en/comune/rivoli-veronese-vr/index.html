What to see in Rivoli Veronese

Country on the right bank of the Adige, between the slopes of Mount Baldo and the Chiusa dell'Adige, not far from Garda, known for the battle of 14-15 January 1797 won by Napoleon over the Austrians of Alvinczy, which gave the name to the Rue de Rivoli of Paris: the Napoleonic monument on the plain of Canale and the Napoleonic museum, today in transfer; in Incanale the Romanesque church of San Zeno and along the Via Corone the rock engravings.

Museo Napoleonico

Museums · City history

Napoleonic Museum and the Risorgimento in the center of Rivoli, on the site of the battle of 14-15 January 1797: exhibits weapons, uniforms and documents of the time and a plastic that reconstructs the battle.

Closed for transfer work at Forte Rivoli (comune page)

  • Where Piazza Napoleone I, Rivoli Veronese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rivoli.vr.it

Sources: comune.rivoli.vr.it · visitverona.it

Monumento Napoleonico

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

To celebrate the victory of January 1797 and honor the fallen, Napoleon had it erected in 1806, on the height that dominates the plain of the Adige, a Doric column about 20 meters high; in 1814 after its fall, the Austrians demolished it and some pieces remain at the foot of the white marble base. In the plain of Canale today there is a mausoleum, near the remains of the French fighters. Free access.

  • Where Località Canale, Rivoli Veronese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rivoli.vr.it

Sources: comune.rivoli.vr.it

Chiesetta di San Zeno a Incanale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church built around the thousand with Roman materials of the place, among the oldest in the Val d'Adige; in 1796 it was largely damaged by Napoleonic soldiers, but the perimeter walls, the entrance and the 18-metre-high square bell tower remain intact, and the frescoes of the apse arch have been recently restored.

  • Where Incanale, Rivoli Veronese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rivoli.vr.it

Sources: comune.rivoli.vr.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Luca

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in the most inhabited nucleus when the church of San Zeno needed restoration, it was established parish in 1597, enlarged in 1681, renovated and frescoed in 1921-1922 and completely restored in 1975.

  • Where Canale, Rivoli Veronese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rivoli.vr.it

Sources: comune.rivoli.vr.it

Incisioni rupestri della Via Corone

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Along the Via Corone, which climbs from Villa di Incanale to Tessari, Preabocco and Brentino to Pozza Gallet and the Sanctuary of Our Lady of the Crown, rock slabs polished by the Würmian glacier and shaped by water carry about fifty engraved figures, including a steliform anthropomorphic figure in three registers. The date is discussed: for Bagolini and Nisi they would go back to the early age of metals (mid or late of the third millennium BC), for Gaggia and Fasolo would be more recent.

  • Where Via Corone, Incanale, Rivoli Veronese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rivoli.vr.it

Sources: comune.rivoli.vr.it

Rocca di Rivoli

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the fortress there was a fortification around 1100, linked to the legend of Countess Emma who resisted the siege of Prince Marcualdo and saved himself disguised as a monk; there are traces of Neolithic settlements of the Vase Culture in Bocca Quadrata. The excavations of the late 1970s, promoted by Pro Loco with archaeologists from the universities of Birmingham and Cambridge, have returned weapons, furnishings and perimeter walls and part of the floor of a small church.

  • Where Rocca di Rivoli, Rivoli Veronese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rivoli.vr.it

Sources: comune.rivoli.vr.it

Busto di Massena nel Municipio

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

In the hall of the Town Hall there is the bust of André Massena, appointed by Napoleon Duke of Rivoli, donated to the community in 1902 by the grandson of the general.
