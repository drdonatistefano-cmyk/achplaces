What to see in Gignod

A village on the medieval road to Valais, with views of the Valpelline mountains and the Grand Combin.

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Ilario

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

From the 15th century, with a major baroque altar and a cycle of 15th century frescoes among the highest of the painting of the time in the region.
