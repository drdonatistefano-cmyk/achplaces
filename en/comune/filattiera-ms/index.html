What to see in Filattiera

Medieval village of Lunigiana crossed by the Via Francigena, with the church of San Giorgio that houses the stone of Leodegar of 752 and, just outside, the Romanesque parish church of Santo Stefano in Sorano with its archaeological area; in the surroundings the stone villages of Ponticello and Caprio.

Borgo medievale di Filattiera

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The name derives from the Byzantine watchtowers erected during the wars against the Longobards; the current village began to form between the 10th and 11th century and preserves intact the first nucleus of the 13th century, with paved roads and stone houses. From 1221 it was the capital of the fief Malaspina dello Spino Fiorito; it is crossed by the Via Francigena. Park in the castle area and continue on foot for about 50 meters to the square.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di San Giorgio e lapide di Leodegar

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A 12th-century church with a single apse, with a defensive bell tower next to it; a second nave added in the 14th century was demolished in the 20th century. It preserves the tombstone of Leodegar (752), one of the oldest Christian testimonies of Lunigiana, which recalls a high-ranking character, probably a bishop, who broke the pagan idols.

  • Where Via Carrà 8, Filattiera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Pieve di Santo Stefano a Sorano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Jewel of the 11th century Lunigian Romanesque, with stone façade and walls of river pebbles, built along the Via Francigena probably on a medieval building (VIII-IX century); it is mentioned by Sigerico di Canterbury at the end of the 10th century and in a papal bull of 1148. Nearby were found statues-stele of the Bronze Age and the remains of a Roman village.

  • Where SS62, Sorano, Filattiera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Area archeologica di Sorano e mostra «Archeologia a Filattiera»

History and architecture · Archaeology

The site tells of the human presence in Val di Magra, from the Ligurians Apuani to the Romans, from the Byzantines and Longobards to the medieval pilgrims, thanks to excavations initiated by Tiziano Mannoni with the Institute of Material Culture of Genoa and the Superintendence. Panels and casts of statues-stele accompany the visit; the exhibition curated by Enrico Giannicedda (inaugurated in October 2023) exhibits finds of almost six thousand years, including statues-stele, urns, Roman crockery, medieval weapons, coins and keys. Show available by reservation.

  • Where Centro Didattico Sorano, Filattiera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Borgo di Ponticello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Small village entirely in stone, among the best preserved medieval centers of the area, with squares, courtyards, alleys, numerous covered passages and tower houses.
