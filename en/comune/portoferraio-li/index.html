What to see in Portoferraio

The capital and main port of Elba, the Cosmopolis fortified by Cosimo I de' Medici since 1548 and residence of Napoleon in exile: the Medici fortresses, the Palazzina dei Mulini, Villa San Martino and the white beaches of Capo Bianco and Samson.

12 places to visit in Portoferraio, in the province of Livorno (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Fortezze Medicee

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Piazzaforte started in 1548 by Cosimo I with the architects Bellucci and Camerini, then Bernardo Buontalenti: the forts Falcone, Stella and Linguella united by walls and walkways, which in 1553 resisted the assault of the corsair Dragut; from Forte Falcone a wide panorama of the sea.

  • Where Portoferraio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Palazzina dei Mulini

Museums · House museums · Viewpoint

Official residence of Napoleon during the exile, between the Fort Falcone and the Fort Stella, overlooking the sea: it combines two grand-ducal houses furnished in Empire style, with reception room, officer room, study and emperor's room.

  • Where Portoferraio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Villa San Martino

Museums · House museums

Summer residence purchased by Napoleon in June 1814 with rooms decorated by Pietro Ravelli as the Egyptian Hall and the Sala delle Colombe; Prince Anatolio Demidoff built the Neoclassical Gallery there, today with Napoleonic prints.

  • Where San Martino, Portoferraio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo Archeologico della Linguella e Villa romana

Museums · Archaeological

In the ancient warehouses of salt at Linguella, next to the Martello Tower, finds of Elba and the Tuscan islands from the end of the 8th century BC to the 5th century AD; a few steps away the remains of the Roman villa of Linguella.

  • Where Linguella, Portoferraio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Castello del Volterraio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On a spur between Portoferraio and Rio, the oldest fortress on the island, of Etruscan origin and transformed by the Pisans, strengthened around 1440; from the top you can see Mount Capanne and, on clear days, Corsica.
