What to see in Solopaca

Country of Sannio at the foot of the Taburno-Camposauro, with a center of churches and palaces six-seventh-century linked to the Dukes Ceva Grimaldi: the Doge's Palace, the parish church of San Mauro, the food and wine museum in Palazzo Cutillo and, on the Monte delle Rose, the sanctuary of Maria SS. del Roseto.

7 places to visit in Solopaca, in the province of Benevento (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo Ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between 1672 and 1682 by the Duke Antonio Maria Ceva Grimaldi, feudalist of Solopaca, who settled there with the family.

Parrocchia di San Mauro Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Palazzo Cutillo e MEG - Museo Enogastronomico

Museums · Themed

Palace of 1826 in the historic center, with two entrances at different altitudes on Corso Cusani and via Procusi, seat of the MEG: a didactic itinerary on food and wine and industrial food with a collection of food labels from the end of the nineteenth century to today and a section on the "false food" edited by the University of Salerno Museum. The museum also organizes guided tours, exhibitions, conferences and concerts.

The MiC card (updated to May 2020) indicates "Closed" every day; contact 0824 977901, meg@artsanniocampania.it

  • Where Corso Cusani, Palazzo Cutillo, Solopaca
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.solopaca.bn.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.solopaca.bn.it
