What to see in Roccavivara

Country of the valley of the Trigno known for the Sanctuary of Santa Maria del Canneto, ancient Benedictine abbey with the statue of the Madonna del Sorriso, next to the remains of a Roman villa of the III-IV century AD with polychrome mosaics.

Santuario di Santa Maria del Canneto

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Complex founded around the VI-VII century, to whose construction according to historical sources contributed the Benedictine monks of San Vincenzo al Volturno; the current church is of the XI-XII centuries and the bell tower, 25 meters high, was completed in 1329. The Benedictines remained until 1474; then, abandoned, the church was almost submerged by the debris of the torrent Musa Bridge (1845-1850) and was reopened and restored by Don Duilio Iemme since 1929. At the center is the wooden statue of the Madonna called "del Sorriso" (Smile), the main feast is September 8 and pilgrimages are frequent from May to October.

Villa romana di Santa Maria del Canneto

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the area of the sanctuary there are still the rooms of a Roman villa of the III-IV century AD, belonging to important families of the Roman town of Terventum: the excavations have brought to light polychrome mosaics and containers for the conservation of oil and wine.
