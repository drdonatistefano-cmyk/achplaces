Kainua, città etrusca, e Museo nazionale etrusco Pompeo Aria

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

The Ministry of Culture defines Marzabotto as a unique case among the Etruscan cities: from the end of the sixth to the middle of the fourth century BC. Kainua occupied the Pian di Misano and the hill of Misanello, with roads, isolated, acropolis and necropolis still legible in the park. The museum next door preserves the excavations that began in the nineteenth century, including bronze kouros and votive bronze bronzes.

  • Where Via Porrettana Sud 13, Marzabotto
  • Entry Ticket only museum + archaeological area: current rate on the Ministry's website (in 2022 €3, free up to 18 years); free on the first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Parco storico di Monte Sole

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

More than 6,000 hectares between Marzabotto, Monzuno and Grizzana, places of the 1944 massacre, with the path of memory.

  • Where Centro visite Il Poggiolo, Marzabotto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
