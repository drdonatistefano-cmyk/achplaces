What to see in Solofra

A small town in the Irno Valley, 400 metres above sea level, known for its traditional tannins and a rich Baroque heritage: the heart is Piazza San Michele, with the Collegiata from 42 canvases of Guarini, the Doge's Palace Orsini and the Fountain of the Four Lions; above the village the ruins of the Longobard Castle and the path to Pizzo San Michele.

Collegiata di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built from 1522 by the University of Solofra, in the years of prosperity of the tanneries, instead of a church of Byzantine origin; the fire of 1721 transformed it from Renaissance to Baroque, and after the earthquake of 1980 was reopened in 1986. Latin cross plan, Baroque façade and Romanesque bell tower; the golden coffered ceiling houses 42 paintings with scenes of the Old and New Testament attributed to John Thomas and Francesco Guarini, and inside there are the Pentecost of Angelo Solimena, the Coronation of the Virgin of Giovan Bernardo Lama and a crypt with the chapel of St. Judas Taddeo.

  • Entry Free

Palazzo Ducale Orsini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in the second half of the sixteenth century by the Orsini di Gravina, feudal lords of Solofra, on a project by Flora Campanile and with decorations of cave workers; damaged by the 1980 earthquake and rebuilt in the original form, since 1993 it is the seat of the municipality. It has a corner turret at the viewpoint, a square courtyard with garden and well, a Renaissance staircase and frescoed rooms with the feuds of the Orsini.

Fontana dei quattro leoni

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Made in 1733 in local travertine by Nicola and Arcangelo Antinolfi of Cava de' Tirreni, in the center of Piazza San Michele, to give water to the population collecting that coming from the village of Sorbo. Four lions at the corners of the basin, in the middle a stele with the coat of arms of Solofra (a radiant sun) and on top four dolphins holding a shell tub.

  • Entry Free

Complesso monumentale di Santa Chiara

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

It includes the church of Santa Maria delle Grazie, documented from the fourteenth century, and the sixteenth-century convent of Santa Chiara, enlarged by the wishes of Beatrice Ferrella Orsini up to the Collegiata and transformed in Baroque style by the Orsini. It has baroque façade with porches, painted coffered ceiling and a altarpiece by Giovanni Tommaso Guarini; today it houses the Municipal Library and the Study Center, and the courtyard welcomes events in summer.

Chiesa di Sant'Andrea Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the homonymous hamlet, rebuilt in 1539, restored in 1633 and modernized in 1875, then consolidated after the 1980 earthquake. Baroque church with a single nave: the stucco vault has three paintings by Angelo Solimena from 1654, and on the main altar there is the Martyrdom of Sant'Andrea attributed to Francesco Guarini (1642), with other paintings in the nave.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di San Giuliano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish since the middle of the fifteenth century, several times renovated and consecrated in 1720; inside basilica with altar greater of 1785 and six altars in polychrome marble. On the main altar the canvas of 1606 by Felice Guarini, Grandpa of Francis, with the patron saint and the Madonna of Montevergine; by Francesco Guarini is the Madonna of Carmine. Open during the functions, open by reservation.

  • Where Via Fratta 51, località Fratta, Solofra

Chiesa e convento di Santa Teresa

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Church of the Blessed Crucifix built in 1686 in Sorbo Soprano for the munificence of the Ronca family, with convent of 1692-1697 became cloistered in 1703; unusual octagonal plant, with Greek cross interior. It houses a Crucifixion by Francesco Solimena (1686), a canvas by Paolo di Majo and a wooden statue of Saint Anne and the Virgin Child of the workshop of Giacomo Colombo.

Church and monastery closed, available by reservation at the hub of District 6 of Montoro

Chiesa e Conservatorio di Maria SS. Addolorata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Complex of the Volpi district, on the right bank of the Solfrana stream: the nineteenth-century church reconstructs a building already incorporated in 1758 in the monastery wanted by the canon Nicola Petrone; damaged by the 1980 earthquake, it was reopened to worship in 2008. Impressive facade with large arched window between pilasters.

Ruderi del castello longobardo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Longobard castle on the southern side of Mount Pergola-San Marco, in control of the way towards the valley of Saturday through the gorge of Granci; its two towers communicated with the defense places of Castelluccia and Chiancarola. The mastic tower and the northwest tower with cisterns remain, a residential part with small ogival vaults and stretches of walls.

Limited accessibility; parts buried by rubble or vegetation and some at risk of collapse

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sentiero Monti Picentini - Pizzo San Michele

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

A walk of about 5.5 km (4 hours and a half) starting from the Fountain of Scorza, passes through the fountain of Pietra della Madonna and the chestnut groves, to reach Monte dell'Uovo (about 1,417 m) and climbs through a steep forest to Pizzo San Michele (1,567 m), where stands the sanctuary of San Michele Arcangelo, indicated as the highest place of worship in Italy. From the summit you can see the Gulf of Salerno, the Monti Lattari, the Partenio, the Vesuvio and the Matese.

  • Where Fontana di Scorza, frazione Scorza, Solofra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page sistemairpinia.provincia.avellino.it
