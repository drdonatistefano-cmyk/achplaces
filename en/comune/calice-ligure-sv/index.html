What to see in Calice Ligure

Farmland of the valley of the river Pora, in a basin shaped like a glass with the villages of Carbuta and Eze, linked to contemporary art with the museum of the Casa del Console.

4 places to visit in Calice Ligure, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Casa del Console e Museo d'arte contemporanea

Museums · Modern art

The nineteenth-century palace is owned by the municipality, already home to diplomat Nicolò Massa, who on the noble floor houses a collection of contemporary art started in the eighties.

Le bealere

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry
