What to see in Pescarolo ed Uniti

Municipality of the Cremona plain born in an area once of stagnant and fishy waters, to which Castelnuovo del Vescovo and Pieve Terzagni were united in the nineteenth century; to see the Museo del Lino, the country shrine of Santa Maria della Senigola and the Romanesque parish of Pieve Terzagni with its mosaic floor.

Museo del Lino

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Born in 1975 on the initiative of private individuals, it collects an ethnographic section with tools and artifacts on the production and processing of flax and silk, textile activities on which the local economy was based until the 1930s, including the complete set of tools for the breeding of the silkworm. It also exhibits objects for lighting, from oil to electricity, tools and everyday objects, and preserves over 2,500 hand-embroidered items, especially in linen, from the 18th century to the early 20th century.

Santuario di Santa Maria della Senigola

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

An isolated complex in the Pesaro countryside, dedicated to the Annunciation, consisting of church, sacristy, bell tower and house of the rosit. Of ancient origin, with sixteenth-century architectural elements, the church was rebuilt at the beginning of the seventeenth century, when most of the frescoes were made; it preserves an important collection of ex-votes.

Pieve di San Giovanni Battista Decollato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Pieve Terzagni, the parish church dates back to the 12th century, when the territory was under the dominion of Matilde di Canossa, and was renovated, restored and enlarged between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. In the presbytery there is a precious Romanesque mosaic floor of the 12th century with figures and symbols.
