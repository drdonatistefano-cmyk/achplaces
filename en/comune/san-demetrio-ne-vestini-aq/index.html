What to see in San Demetrio ne' Vestini

Country of about 1,900 inhabitants on the left bank of the Aterno, in the valley once inhabited by the Vestini; in the hamlet of Stiffe, on the edge of the Regional Park Sirente-Velino, open the Grotte di Stiffe, while a short distance from the village there is the small karst lake of Sinizzo.

Grotte di Stiffe

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry

Resurgent cave at the apex of the gorge above Stiffe, where an underground stream of water returns to light after about 3 km in the heart of the mountain; discovered for modern uses in 1906 during the works for a power plant, then destroyed in the Second World War, was equipped in the eighties and the tourist complex was inaugurated in 1991. The guided tour lasts 60 minutes on a route of about 1.4 km round trip with 150 steps, touching the Sala del Silenzio (the only dry environment), the Sala della Cascata, the Sala delle Concrezioni and the great environment of Lake Nero. Inside there are constant 9 °C: they serve jacket and closed shoes; ticket office every day 10-18, to be reached 20 minutes before the chosen time. The MiC card also mentions the museum of speleology (paleontology, mineralogy, geology, speleological photography), refurbished in 2020.

Lago di Sinizzo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Small karst lake of circular shape, about 120 meters of diameter and 10 meters of maximum depth, at 719 meters of altitude, fed by two sources of drinking water. The 2009 earthquake had cracked its banks threatening its disappearance, but consolidation work recovered it; grassy banks, woods and willows make it a frequent destination on weekends, with area equipped for bivouac.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sentiero Lago Sinizzo - Grotte di Stiffe

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Trail no. 6 of the municipal network, 6 km with 150 meters of altitude and about 1 hour and 20 minutes of walk, tourist-hiking difficulty. It connects the Caves of Stiffe to Lake Sinizzo via dirt roads and next to the cemetery of San Demetrio.

Chiesa di San Demetrio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church at the foot of the village, seventeenth century but of medieval origin: the act for its construction was written by the notary Joseph Cappa on March 28, 1600 and the relics under the altars were consecrated by Bishop Domenico Tagliatatela on October 16, 1736, as recalls a plaque in the right aisle. On the north wall the plaster hides a Roman tombstone and a tile with a stretched thumb; two sundials are on the side and on the back.

Chiesa di Santa Maria dei Raccomandati

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church at the beginning of the village, completed in 1820 with baroque shapes, facade in white stone with central window and unique nave. Inside two canvases by Teofilo Patini, the ...Miracle of the Cross and ...Carlo Borromeo with a moribund.

Cappella di Santa Croce e telegrafo ottico

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

On Mount Calvario, at 853 meters, a small chapel whose oldest part dates back perhaps to the seventeenth century, built with square re-utilization stones, in a site that offers a wide view and perhaps housed the matrix church of Santa Balbina. Next remain the ruins of the station of the optical telegraph Bourbon, for which the area was sold in 1850 to the Real Telegraphic Body: perimeter walls, an oval observatory and an underground cistern.

  • Entry Free

Castello medievale del borgo di San Giovanni

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Remains of the "Castrum Sancti Dometrij" in villa Sancti Joannis is mentioned in a document from the first decades of the fourteenth century, on a rocky spur where around 1100 was built the first church of St. John. The 100-metre-long trapezoidal belt has an entrance ramp and three lines of walls towards the Valley of Hell, while seven lines of step walls protect a mule track.

Palazzo Ducale Cappelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built above the castle, it is the only fortified building and the largest of the village, with a large portico; today it houses the College of Rogationist Fathers. The country is linked to the Cappelli family: Senator Raffaele Cappelli, promoter of agricultural innovation, was born there in 1848, to which a variety of wheat is dedicated.
