What to see in Carassai

Piceno village that dominates the valleys from the Adriatic to the Sibillini, with the Castle New fourteenth century, the Antiquarium of Neolithic finds in the Palazzo Comunale and the art gallery in the oratory of Santa Monica; in the countryside the Rocca di Montevarmine and the Romanesque abbey of Sant'Angelo in Piano.

5 places to visit in Carassai, in the province of Ascoli Piceno (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca di Montevarmine

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

About 4 km from the village, it dates back to the 14th century with remains of the IX of probable Lombard origin; it is among the castles of the Marches remained intact, with a 35 meters high tower crowned by Ghibelline blackbirds, with archers and plumbings.

  • Where Strada Provinciale 58 Montevarmine, Carassai
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Abbazia di Sant'Angelo in Piano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Romanesque church about 2 km from the Fortress of Montevarmine, next to which in the first half of the 11th century arose a Benedictine monastery today disappeared; from the 14th century it was a destination of pilgrimages for the indulgences granted by the bishops of Fermo and strategic point on the road between Ascoli, Fermo and the Aso valley.

  • Where Carassai
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Antiquarium comunale

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in October 2008 on the first floor of the Palazzo Comunale, it preserves traces of the Neolithic period found in the territory.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti 1, Carassai
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Pinacoteca nell'Oratorio di Santa Monica

Museums · Old masters

Next to the Palazzo Comunale, the oratory of 1699 has a late Baroque Rococo façade; restored in 2003-2004, it houses valuable paintings and the Credenza with the statue of Saint Monica.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Carassai
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Castello Nuovo e centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The walls preserve fortifications of the 14th and 15th centuries and suggestive military walks covered; from the village the look ranges from the Adriatic to the Sibillini Mountains. In the village the church of Santa Maria del Buon Gesù, in the Baroque Renaissance style, rich in valuable works, and the public wash-house of 1901, restored, place of the memory of the community.

  • Where Centro storico, Carassai
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it
