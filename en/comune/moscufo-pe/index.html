What to see in Moscufo

A hilly village among olive groves, in the golden triangle of oil with Pianella and Loreto Aprutino; outside the village the Romanesque church of Santa Maria del Lago preserves the polychrome ambo of Nicodemo da Guardiagrele of 1159.

Abbazia di Santa Maria del Lago

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Romanesque church outside the town, the only surviving of a 12th century Benedictine abbey complex, whose name recalls an ancient forest (lucus). Façade in brick with thirteenth-century portal sculpted with vine branches and floral motifs; inside basilica with three naves with frescoes from the 12th century partly faded and, in the apse, remains of a great Christ with saints. The masterpiece is the stucco ambo commissioned in 1159 by the abbot Rainaldo to Nicodemo from Guardiagrele, with David and the bear, the stories of Jonah, St. George and the dragon and the symbols of the Tetramorphus, a rare example of sculpture with traces of polychrome; it also preserves a Madonna with Child of 1490 attributed to Andrea De Lithium.

Unpublished times (the MiC tab indicates closed every day); contact details for the visit: tel. 347 5460606 (don Giulio), 339 8778265, 333 8805225

Chiesa di San Cristoforo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Documented from 1324, but the current building is from the seventeenth century, with Baroque façade dated 1607; it preserves stuccoes of Ambrosio Piazza and a silver reliquary of St. Christopher of 1780.

  • Where Centro storico, Moscufo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: abruzzoturismo.it · italia.it

Palazzo dei Ferri

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace of the eighteenth century, several times renovated, which preserves ceramics of Tommaso and Giovacchino Cascella, paintings of Alfonso Muzii and prints of Basilio Cascella.

  • Where Moscufo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
