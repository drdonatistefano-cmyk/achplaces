What to see in Marliana

Medieval village on a hill between the valleys of Vincio and Nievole, among chestnut woods, gathered around the Pieve di San Niccolò; around Serra Pistoiese, the ruins of Sant'Andrea di Furfalo and Casore del Monte with the Museum of Rural Culture.

5 places to visit in Marliana, in the province of Pistoia (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pieve di San Niccolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was mentioned since 1313, it became a parish in the 16th century and a parish church in 1593; transformed in the 17th and 19th centuries, a restoration has recreated nineteenth century decorations and colours. The fourteenth century remains traces in the outer walls and in the stone bell tower; the interior with three naves has the central covered by false domes, marble balustrade and square choir with dome. Around the parish church there is the medieval village.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo 15, Marliana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · visitpistoia.eu

Museo della Cultura Contadina di Casore del Monte

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

It collects tools and tools recovered by Giuseppe Antonelli and other donors, with testimonies of lost trades such as the coal-making and the hay-making pastor, a section on the care of chestnuts and the processing of chestnuts for sweet flour and books on the history of the territory. Guided tour included, upon request and booking.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa 5, Casore del Monte, Marliana
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visitpistoia.eu · visittuscany.com

Serra Pistoiese

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

An important example of medieval town planning, connected to the neighbouring villages by ancient mule tracks suitable for excursions; the road to Panicagliora and Serra offers views of the castle between the peaks of the Apennines.

  • Where Serra Pistoiese, Marliana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visitpistoia.eu · visittuscany.com

Ruderi di Sant'Andrea di Furfalo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Ruins of the Romanesque church of an important medieval settlement, between Serra and Panicagliora, mentioned in the diploma of Otto III of 998.

  • Where Tra Serra Pistoiese e Panicagliora, Marliana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visitpistoia.eu · visittuscany.com

Casore del Monte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient fortified village in an almost alpine landscape, among meadows and mountain vegetation.
