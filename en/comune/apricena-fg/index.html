What to see in Apricena

On a low hill at the first slopes of Gargano, Apricena links its name to a hunting legend of Frederick II, who often stayed here: in the historic center the Baronial Palace rises on the ruins of the castle federiciano, the clock Tower and the Civic Museum in the eighteenth century Palazzo Paolicelli; in the country the ruins of the monastery of St. John in Piano.

Museo civico di Apricena (Palazzo della cultura)

Museums · City history

Born from the collaboration between Archeoclub d'Italia, Municipality and Ente Parco Nazionale del Gargano, it is located in the eighteenth-century Palazzo Paolicelli, public building of the historical center restored with funds of the Park and renamed "Palazzo della cultura" (Palace of Culture), opened in March 2010. It exhibits palaeontological and medieval finds; on the whole also the municipal library, the historical archive, a media library on Frederick II and a thematic museum on the Middle Ages and the Emperor.

Centro storico e Torre dell'orologio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The urban nucleus is born from the union of the ancient districts Terra, Borgo and Casale. According to tradition, the name comes from a hunting trip by Frederick II, who had a big boar cooked for dinner: they remember the town coat of arms, with a wild boar pierced by an arrow, and the tomb in the clock Tower, one of the few surviving ancient buildings, with the inscription "coena dat et aper nomen tibi Apricoena" (inscription "coena dat et aper nomen tibi Apricoena").

Palazzo Baronale (Torrione)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Erected since 1658 by the feudal lord Scipione Brancia, prince of Apricena, on the ruins of the castle federiciano, already semi-destroyed also by the earthquake of 1627; Frederick II stayed in Apricena about thirteen times. A bifora on the cylindrical tower is probably the only remainder of the Swabian building; the palace, with a quadrangular plan with a shoe masonry, contains a large courtyard with a central cistern and a garden.

Chiesa matrice dei Santi Martino e Lucia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The mother church of Apricena, dedicated to Saint Martin and Saint Lucia, was entirely rebuilt in two or three years after the earthquake of 1627.

Ruderi del monastero di San Giovanni in Piano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

To the north-west of Apricena, on the edge of the Murge, the monastery was founded in 1050 by Petronio, Count of Lesina; passed from the Benedictines to the Celestines in the early 1280s, in 1294 Celestine V united Holy Spirit of Sulmona. Abandoned after the transfer of the Celestines to San Severo, it was the destination of pilgrimages to the Madonna della Rocca until the twentieth century. Of the Latin cross church with apse aisle remain fragments of frescoes and the remains of wells, fish ponds and walls of the belt.
