What to see in Montechiaro d'Acqui

Ancient stone village of Langa between calanchi and woods, with views from the Ligurian Apennines to the Langhe: the medieval village of Montechiaro Alto, the parish church of San Giorgio with the relic of the Sante Spine and the museum of peasant civilization in the former church of Santa Caterina.

Museo della civiltà contadina di Santa Caterina e delle Sante Spine

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In the premises of the former cemetery church of Santa Caterina, dating back to the 16th century: tools of fields, vineyards, stables and cellars from the second half of the 19th century to the 1960s, household objects (lights, skis and snowshoes made by hand, Gerle), ancient books of the Confraternita di Santa Caterina, lanterns and processional clothes, stones and gravestones from the excavations of the destroyed castle. Visit by reservation at the municipal offices.

  • Where Piazza Cesare Battisti, Montechiaro Alto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.montechiarodacqui.al.it

Sources: comune.montechiarodacqui.al.it · touringclub.it

Parrocchiale di San Giorgio e Oratorio di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1595 in late Renaissance rural style on the slope of Montechiaro Alto, it houses the venerable relic of the Sante Spine, a 14th century stone tabernacle, a popular wooden pulpit and an eighteenth-century Madonna of Margliano school; three naves divided by columns in local sandstone. Under the church, the Oratory of St. Anthony, recovered, houses a museum on the Sacred Spines with paintings by Severino Baraldi and a copy of the Shroud. Free access during functions.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Montechiaro d'Acqui
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.montechiarodacqui.al.it

Sources: comune.montechiarodacqui.al.it

Borgo medievale di Montechiaro Alto

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified village built in the 13th century (Mons Cauri) on the traffic to the sea along the ridge streets, with cobbled streets and the remains of the castle of the 12th century. They lived there and painted it Exo Peluzzi and A. Cafassi, and in 2000 it was mostly filmed there by Johnny the Partisan of Guido Chiesa.

  • Where Montechiaro Alto, Montechiaro d'Acqui
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.montechiarodacqui.al.it

Sources: comune.montechiarodacqui.al.it

Sentiero 573 «Anello di Montechiaro»

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hiking ring of 30.55 km (about 9 hours) from the square of Montechiaro Piana, next to the high chimney of the furnace: calanchi with brooms and thyme, woods of roverella, chestnut and ash, Punta Marelli (about 470 m) on the Erro Valley, the belvedere of Costa Bella (about 485 m) towards the Maritime Alps and the village of Montechiaro Alto; short variant of about 18 km. Track GPX and downloadable map from the site of the Municipality.
