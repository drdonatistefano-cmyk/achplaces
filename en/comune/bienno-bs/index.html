What to see in Bienno

Borgo della Val Camonica has been linked to iron processing for centuries: the Vasso Re canal still moves sweaters and millstones of the forges and mill, among medieval towers, frescoed churches and the workshops of the project "Bienno Borgo degli Artisti" .

7 places to visit in Bienno, in the province of Brescia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Fucina Museo

Museums · Themed

The 17th century forge along the King's Vase, today's museum, where you can watch the iron work with the hammer moved by water, know handed down by the Frear family.

  • Where Bienno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Mulino Museo

Museums · Themed

Mill of the seventeenth century, today museum, where millstones operated by the water of the King's vase still grind the cereals, including polenta flour.

  • Entry To be checked
