What to see in Poggio Sannita

Borgo dell'Alto Molise once called Caccavone, from the "caccavo" , the large copper container for milk processing, medieval fief dei de Petra, de Sabran and Carafa born around a castle in the upper part; to see the mother church of Santa Vittoria and the medieval underground mill of Palazzo Iacovone.

Chiesa di Santa Vittoria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Mother church in the medieval village, with three asymmetric aisles on a Latin cross plan resting on the rock. Destroyed by the 1720 earthquake, it was rebuilt in 1725 by Duke Nicola Petra, whose tomb was found in 2000 under the organ, and enlarged in 1762. It preserves the statue of St. Prospero, patron saint of the village, carved in 1764 by Silverio Giovannitto di Oratino, with the relic of the saint brought in 1743 by Cardinal Vincenzo Petra, the organ of 1769 of the D'Onofrio family, restored, a stone aquasantiera of 1795, the Last Supper of 1890, works of Neapolitan school between the end of the seventeenth and early eighteenth centuries and a group in mache of St. Anne, St. Joachim and the Virgin child.

Antico frantoio Iacovone

Museums · Themed

An underground mill from medieval times entirely restored in the historic Palazzo Iacovone, registered with the Associazione Dimore Storiche Italiane, testimony of the oldest peasant culture of Alto Molise. In the palace was born in 1850 Cosmo Maria de Horatiis, considered the father of Italian homeopathy and personal physician of Francesco I di Borbone: guided tours include oil mill and hometown, by telephone or by mail.
