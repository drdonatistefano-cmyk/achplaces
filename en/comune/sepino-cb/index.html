What to see in Sepino

Borgo del Matese Molise above the valley of the Tammaro: about 3 km, in the locality Altilia, the ruins of the Roman city of Saepinum with walls, monumental gates, basilica and theatre; on the mountain the Sannite area of Terravecchia-Saipins and, in the village, the church of Santa Cristina and Palazzo Attilio.

Area archeologica di Altilia-Saepinum

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Roman city founded at the bottom of the valley after the conquest, in 293 BC, of the Sannita Saipins; archaeological park established by the MiC in 2021. There are still more than 1 km long walls with towers, doors similar to triumphal arches such as Porta Bojano, the basilica, the forum and the theatre of the first century A.D. (up to 3,000 spectators), on which peasant houses arose that follow the curve; cardoon and decuman follow ancient ways of transhumance. Open from Tuesday to Sunday 9-18, Monday closed.

  • Where SP82, località Altilia, Sepino
  • Entry €10 (one ticket with the Museum of the city and the territory)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo della città e del territorio

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Set up in the rural houses of the eighteenth century built on the structures of the Roman theatre, of which follow the curved shape, with sinks, fireplaces, ovens and niches still visible. The finds, almost all from the area of Saepinum and surroundings, range from prehistory to the Middle Ages: from the paleolithic stone instruments of the middle valley of the Tammaro to Roman life, between symposium, leisure, crafts and funeral rites.

the MiC card reports the museum closed every day; check the opening at +39 0874 790207

  • Where SP82, località Altilia, Sepino
  • Entry €10 (one ticket with the archaeological area)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Area archeologica di Terravecchia-Saipins

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Sannite archaeological area at about 950 meters above sea level, on a hill overlooking the Tammaro valley, where remains the walls of the Sannite city of Saipins conquered by the Romans in 293 BC. It is reached with a trekking route.

  • Where Località Terravecchia, Sepino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Cristina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The most important monument of the country, probably of thirteenth-century origin, remodelled several times after the earthquakes and survived that of 1805; the relics of the saint arrived in 1099 with two pilgrims headed to the Holy Land. The bell tower has a wrought iron dome; inside the tomb of Bishop Attilio (1536), two marble altars of the eighteenth century, the chapel of San Carlo Borromeo (1737), the Carafa chapel and the Treasure chapel (1609) with busts of silver copper saints.

  • Where Piazza Nerazio Prisco, Sepino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Centro storico, Palazzo Attilio e mura medievali

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Village gathered around Piazza Nerazio Prisco, on which overlooks the Town Hall and stands the bell tower shaped like a large bottle. The walls of the IX century remain with two doors and three cylindrical towers, the Renaissance Palazzo Attilio of the family that gave a bishop to Termoli and the eighteenth century Palazzo Giacchi, with portals and windows in pink stone; at the entrance of the village a large stone mask, perhaps from a monumental Roman fountain.

  • Where Piazza Nerazio Prisco, Sepino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitmolise.eu

Pianoro di Campitello

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural plateau in an unspoilt landscape of Matese, voted Place of the heart FAI in 2020; the area around is rich in waterfalls and small streams.
