What to see in Calimera

In the heart of Grecìa Salentina, where the griko is still spoken (the name itself means "good morning") Calimera preserves the main church of San Brizio, the House-museum of Rural Civilization and Grika Culture, the Greek funeral stele of public gardens and, just outside the village, the church of San Vito with its pierced stone; on the outskirts the Natural History Museum of Salento.

Museo di Storia Naturale del Salento

Museums · Science and university

Founded in 1982 and managed by the Naturalia Cooperative, it has sections of paleontology and paleoanthropology (vegetable and animal fossils even millions of years), entomology, herpetology and ornithology (with live animals), mineralogy and malacology, mammology, embryology and teratology, as well as aquariums and terrariums. Inside the Provincial Fauna Observatory recovers and rehabilitates injured wild animals, including sea turtles and raptors. Hours: Tuesday-Friday 9-13 and 15-17, Saturday and Sunday 10-17, Monday closed.

Casa-museo della Civiltà contadina e della Cultura grika (Ghetonìa)

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Set up in a typical courthouse house in the historic centre, opened in 2003 and managed by the Cultural Circle ARCI Ghetonia, it exhibits domestic objects that tell traditional crafts, customs and rites; it has a library of about three thousand publications on the anthropological, linguistic and cultural origins of the area and on the links with Greece, an emerotheque and a collection of videos. Free admission; working hours 9.30-12.30 and 17-20 (May-September) or 16-19 (October-April).

  • Where Via Costantini 52, Calimera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di San Vito e Pietra di San Vito

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Cappella campestre not far from the centre, dedicated to the patron saint of animals and cited as one of the most important architectures of Grecìa Salentina; a fifteenth-century document describes it with three doors, three altars and a frescoed dome. In the only chapel, a large pre-Christian rock pierced (about 30 cm hole) emerges from the floor: in Pasquetta, by tradition, you pass through to purify yourself or as a wish for fertility, in a feast among olive trees with music, dances and cronelli in griko.

Chiesa matrice di San Brizio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The church of the patron saint, of Greek imprint, preserves altars and Renaissance and Baroque paintings, including a Madonna of Mercy with an unprecedented Madonna pregnant. Originally two naves, it was rebuilt with a single nave at the end of the seventeenth century; hatches give access to underground burials.

Centro storico e case a corte

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Itinerary of the Municipality from Piazza del Sole: in via Costantini seventeenth-century palaces and, in Vico San Vito, the succession of public, semi-public and private spaces of the house at court (n. 41 a poor peasant house); via Montinari with the church of Sant'Antonio, the ancient 'hospitale' for pilgrims, the courts of San Calimero and San Paolo and Palazzo Montinari, which is said to be the residence of the last protopapàs of the Greek rite Sigismondo De Matteis (dead in 1621); the chapel of Carmine with date 1577; in Via Mayro Palazzo Murrone (sixth century nucleus), the birthplace of the Hellenist Vito Domenico Palumbo (1854-1918) and the court and chapel of the Crucifix of 1698, with frescoed vault and wooden crucifix seventeenth century.

Giardini pubblici e stele greca

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

In the public gardens, with the busts of Vito Domenico Palumbo, De Santi and Gabrieli, there is a funeral stele in marble of the fourth century BC donated by Athens in 1960: it depicts Thanatos accompanying a young woman and bears the inscription in griko "Zeni esù en ise ettu" 'sti Kalimera" .
