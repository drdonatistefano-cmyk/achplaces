3 places to visit in Cerreto Guidi, in the province of Florence (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa medicea di Cerreto Guidi e Museo storico della caccia

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Hunting residence of Cosimo I (1564-1566), with the Medici bridges attributed to Buontalenti, Medici portraits, tapestries and the story of Isabella de' Medici.

  • Where Via Ponti Medicei 7, Cerreto Guidi
  • Entry Single ticket to be verified; annual subscription €16 with unlimited entrances; in the Firenze Card
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website villemonumentalitoscana.cultura.gov.it

Sources: villemonumentalitoscana.cultura.gov.it · museitoscana.cultura.gov.it · destinationflorence.com · cultura.gov.it

Sentieri del Padule di Fucecchio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Itineraries on foot in the Padule di Fucecchio, which Feel Florence indicates as the largest inland marsh in Italy, between the municipalities of Cerreto Guidi and Fucecchio.

  • Where Padule di Fucecchio, Cerreto Guidi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com

Il territorio all'uncinetto

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

A miniature crochet that reproduces the territory between Vinci and Cerreto Guidi, signalled by Feel Florence as a stop not to be missed.

  • Where Cerreto Guidi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com
