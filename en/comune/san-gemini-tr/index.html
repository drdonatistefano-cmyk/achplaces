What to see in San Gemini

Medieval village on the ancient Via Flaminia, born on a Roman villa: St. Francis Square with the thirteenth-century church, the Palazzo Vecchio with the Esperia Tower, St. John with the façade dated 1199, the abbey of St. Nicholas, the museums Geolab and Guido Calori and the mineral water park Sangemini and Fabia.

Piazza San Francesco e chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On the central square, with the 18th century Town Hall, the thirteenth-century church dedicated to Saint Francis, who stayed several times in the city and performed an exorcism in 1213: Gothic portal with ancient wooden door and Gothic interior with Umbrian school frescoes.

Palazzo Vecchio e Torre Esperia

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The palace of the XII-XIII century was the seat of the Captain of the People; in the central hall frescoes with peasants working in the countryside. Next to the Esperia Tower, with the bell melted in 1318 by an Orvieto master, symbol of the free municipality, who called the citizens at the assemblies. You enter from Porta Burgi, 12th century gate on the path of Flaminia.

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On Piazza Garibaldi, with Romanesque façade dated 1199 that bears engraved the names of the architects; inside a 16th century baptismal font and a wooden Madonna of the eighteenth century. Near the cloister of the former convent of Santa Maria Maddalena.

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Duomo di San Gemine

Places of worship · Cathedrals

It preserves the relics of the saint; ancient, was restored in the early nineteenth century by an architect who probably followed the advice of Canova, with stucco decorations and paintings of the seventeenth century. You can reach it from Via Roma before the arch of Porta Romana; on the piazza Palazzo Santacroce, today hotel.

Abbazia di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The 11th century Abbey, documented since 1037; preserves a Madonna with Child by Ruggero da Todi (1295). The original portal was sold in the 1930s and today it is at the Metropolitan Museum in New York; the on-site one is a 1967 copy.

Mosaico romano di Via del Tribunale

History and architecture · Archaeology

The village is located on a Roman villa of the second half of the first century BC: the remains, with mosaic floors, are visible in a building of Via del Tribunale, a private patrician house near Piazza Garibaldi.

Geolab, Museo laboratorio di scienze della Terra

Museums · Science and university

Opened in April 1999 in the former church of Our Lady of Mercy, already hospital: rooms with interactive machines, panels, rocks and fossils on tectonic plates, earthquakes, volcanoes, internal structure of the Earth up to the nucleus and geology of Umbria; on the upper floor didactic workshops. The MiC indicates it as the first interactive museum of Earth sciences in Italy.

Museo dell'opera di Guido Calori

Museums · Modern art

Opened in 1987 in the third cloister of the former cloister of the Poor Clares, already hospital of pilgrims going to Rome, in the Rocca district, the highest point of the village: bronze sculptures, paintings, sketches, archive and working instruments of the sculptor and Roman painter Guido Calori (1885-1960), including the bronze Italica Gens awarded at the Venice Biennale of 1930. Open Saturday, Sunday and holidays 10-14.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.sangemini.tr.it · italia.it

Parco della Fonte (acque minerali)

Local life and traditions · Spas

Park with fountains where you can taste the waters of Sangemini and Fabia, two springs fed by the slopes of Monte Torre Maggiore and rich in mineral salts; the historical source dates back to 1889.
