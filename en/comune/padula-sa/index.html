What to see in Padula

Borgo del Vallo di Diano dominated by the Certosa di San Lorenzo, the largest monastic complex in southern Italy and UNESCO heritage since 1998; in the village the birthplace of Joe Petrosino and, at the Fonti, one of the oldest early Christian baptisteries in the West.

Certosa di San Lorenzo e Museo archeologico della Lucania occidentale

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry

Begin in 1306 at the behest of Tommaso Sanseverino, Count of Marsico, and expanded until the nineteenth century mainly Baroque: facade of the church of 1718, Cloister Grande of 1690 with 84 arches, elliptical staircase of 1761-1763, the historical kitchen; the monks left it in 1807. In the rooms the archaeological museum of western Lucania and, in the cells, a collection of contemporary art. Open 9-19.30, closed on Tuesdays.

  • Where Viale Certosa 1, Padula
  • Entry 14 € (reduced 2 €, free under 18 years)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · touringclub.it

Battistero di San Giovanni in Fonte

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Early Christian Baptistery among the oldest in the West, linked to Pope Marcello I from which they take their name church and village; the bathtub in the center was fed by a perennial spring. It was probably destroyed in the 6th century, rebuilt in the 12th century; it re-employses fragments of a Roman villa and preserves frescoes of the VI-VII and the 9th century. Included in the integrated ticket "Certosa and Padula" .

  • Where Località Fonti, via San Giovanni 1, Padula
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: comune.padula.sa.it · cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it

Casa museo Joe Petrosino

Museums · House museums

In the birthplace of Giuseppe Joe's Petrosino (Padula 1860 - Palermo 1909), emigrated to New York at 13 and policeman who investigated the links between the American and Sicilian mafia; killed in Palermo on 12 March 1909. Only museum house in Italy dedicated to a policeman. Times to be checked (the site of the Municipality reports two different).

  • Where Via Giuseppe Petrosino 6, Padula
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: comune.padula.sa.it · touringclub.it
