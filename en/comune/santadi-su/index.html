What to see in Santadi

Downtown Sulcis about 60 km from Cagliari, divided into two by the Mannu river: the caves of Is Zuddas with eccentric aragonites, the Phoenician-punic settlement of Pani Loriga, the Archaeological Civic Museum with the findings of the nuragic sanctuary of Su Benatzu and the house-museum Sa Domu Antiga, stage of the Mauritian Wedding in early August.

Grotte di Is Zuddas

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Karst cavities in the Cambrian limestone of Mount Meana, about 6 km from the village on SP 70 towards Teulada: discovered in the sixties, when they were marble quarry, and explored in the seventies for 1,650 meters, are open to the public since 1985 with a path of about half a kilometer and a visit of about an hour, at 16 degrees constant. Cross the Theatre Hall, the Room of the eccentric with the vault covered with aragonites that grow in every direction and the Sala dell'organ, where a crib is set up at Christmas; at the entrance there are traces of the Sardinian Prolagus, an extinct rodent.

  • Where Località Is Zuddas, SP 70 Santadi-Teulada, Santadi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Insediamento di Pani Loriga

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The most important site of Santadi, frequented from the 4th millennium BC to the 8th century A.D.: domus de Janas on the southern slopes, a nuragic complex on the summit with the nuraghe Diana incorporated in the double walls, and the Phoenician-punic settlement founded in the second half of the 7th century BC, of which remain the foundations of acropolis, residential district and sanctuary; at the sides the Phoenician necropolis with fossa and the Punic one in room. Partial motor accessibility, parking, recommended hiking shoes.

  • Where Località Pani Loriga, dalla SS 293 al km 62 strada vicinale per 1,5 km, Santadi
  • Entry To be checked (recommended guided tour, reservation at the Archaeological Museum)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: idese.cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

Reperti dal Neolithico all'età romana di Santadi e del basso Sulcis: microliti e ceramica cardiali di Cirixì, materiali del vivoli di S'Arriorgiu, Is Solinas e Sant'Anna, della necropoli di Montessu, il deposito votive nuragico della grotto Pirosu-Su Benatzu, dove una stalagmite servizio di altare e venre trovare circa 2.000 articoli in metallo, e gli escovi della forzza Phoenicio-punica di Pani Loriga. He also manages reservations for Pani Loriga.

  • Where Via Umberto I 17, Santadi
  • Entry To be checked (for a fee, cumulative with other sites)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Casa-museo Sa Domu Antiga

Museums · Themed

Sulcitan farmhouse of the early 20th century in the center of the village, with bedroom, dining room, kitchen and anteroom furnished, a wooden frame still working and tools for weaving, dyeing of wool and processing of linen; in the courtyard with the well the tools for cultivation, bread, wine and breeding, plus a room on the local handicrafts. On the morning of the Marriage the bride dresses there.

  • Where Via Mazzini 45, Santadi
  • Entry To be checked (for a fee, cumulative with other museums)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Foresta di Pantaleo e Parco di Gutturu Mannu

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Gutturu Mannu park covers about 35,000 hectares of holm oaks, corks, badgers, agrifoils and Mediterranean maquis suitable for trekking; in the hamlet of Pantaleo, a former mining village, the forest of centuries-old trees houses the Sardinian deer and the deer protected.

  • Where Frazione Pantaleo, Santadi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
