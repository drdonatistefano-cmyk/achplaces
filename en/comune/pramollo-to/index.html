What to see in Pramollo

Municipality of Waldensesi in the valley of the Risagliardo, above San Germano Chisone: at the village Plenc the museum in the school Beckwith tells the nineteenth century education; around trails towards the Gran Truc and the lake of Lauzoun, the neolithic engraving of the Rock Clapie and the places of the Resistance.

7 places to visit in Pramollo, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo/Scuola valdese Beckwith

Museums · Themed

Born in 1989 with the Waldensian church of San Germano, the museum is dedicated to school and religious education from the nineteenth century to the beginning of the twentieth century and is set up in the Beckwith school of the hamlet of the Plenc: lamps, Bibles, benches, the chair of the master, a wooden blackboard, notebooks, books and photographs donated by the community. The key is to the neighbors.

  • Where Borgata Plenc (Pellenchi), Pramollo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pramollo.to.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.pramollo.to.it

Ruata: tempio valdese e Sala Valdese del Teatro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

At the hamlet Ruata (1130 m) there are the Waldensian church of 1888 and the former Catholic church with bell tower of 1853, today Sala Valdese del Teatro; from here start the paths for the Lazarà hill, the Gran Truc and the lake of Lauzoun.

  • Where Borgata Ruata, Pramollo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pramollo.to.it

Sources: comune.pramollo.to.it

Roccio Clapie

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Rock with rock art of the Neolithic period, considered the oldest map of the territory; it is reached on foot from Sapiatti.

  • Where Nei pressi della borgata Sapiatti, Pramollo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pramollo.to.it

Sources: comune.pramollo.to.it

Gran Truc e lago del Lauzoun

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the Ruata (1130 m), on foot or by car on dirt road up to the Lazarà hill, you climb to the Gran Truc (2360 m, iron cross), at whose feet is the Lauzoun lake (2050 m); hard and challenging climb, from a day, without springs from the Lazarà to the summit. The lake is also reached from the Gardetta between rhododendrons and alders, with a spring in half; above, the lakes of Conca Cialancia.

  • Where Partenza da borgata Ruata, Pramollo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pramollo.to.it

Sources: comune.pramollo.to.it

Giardino della Rostania

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Botanical garden and shelter at 1300 m, built in 1900 in memory of the doctor Pietro Edoardo Rostan; devastated during the war, the shelter was rebuilt since 1966 by the Association Friends of Rostany. From Rue to Sangle and Pragiassaut, you can walk, drive or mountain bike.

  • Where Località Rostania, da Rue di Pramollo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pramollo.to.it

Sources: comune.pramollo.to.it

Il Ticiou

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Place in the woods where on November 11, 1944 partisans were massacred; the route that leads there is taken by ANPI. In Micialetti is located the house of "Mille" , seat of the partisan command.

  • Where Boschi di Pramollo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pramollo.to.it

Sources: comune.pramollo.to.it

Il Moulinas

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Ruins of the ancient municipal mill along the Coumbassa, near the Eiretto: on the next rock are engraved the dates of the millers, the oldest of 1770. It is located on the round of the Gardetta, which also touches easy climbing boulders.

  • Where Presso l'Eiretto, da borgata Ruata, Pramollo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pramollo.to.it

Sources: comune.pramollo.to.it
