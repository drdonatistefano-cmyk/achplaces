What to see in Salerno

The city of the Salerno Medical School: the Norman cathedral with the baroque crypt of St. Matthew, the Minerva Garden, the Longobard complex of St. Peter in Corte, the Castle of Arechi on the gulf, the provincial museums and the maritime station of Zaha Hadid.

13 places to visit in Salerno, in the province of Salerno (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale di Santa Maria degli Angeli, San Matteo e San Gregorio VII

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The Romanesque-Norman cathedral commissioned by Robert Guiscardo on a early Christian church built on a Roman temple, consecrated by Gregory VII; in the baroque crypt the relics of St. Matthew, patron saint celebrated on September 21.

  • Where Piazza Alfano I, Salerno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Giardino della Minerva

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Botanical garden terraced with about 300 species of medicinal plants of the Scuola Medica Salernitana, connected by a stairway with pergola and views of the sea and historic center; inside the Tisaneria.

  • Where Vicolo Ferrante Sanseverino 1, Salerno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · conferences.phys.unisa.it

Castello di Arechi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

About 300 metres on Mount Bonadies, of Goto-Byzantine origin, extended by the Longobard Prince Arechi II and never conquered: towers, crenellated walls, the Bastille; houses an archaeological museum with ceramics, glass and coins, a multimedia museum and a park with paths.

  • Where Località Croce, Salerno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · diocesisalerno.it

Complesso monumentale di San Pietro a Corte

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

The most important Lombard building of Salerno, seat of the court of Prince Arechi: it rests on a Roman thermal frigidarium then used as a Christian cemetery, with the chapel of the Virgin and the hypogeum; it also hosted the Salernitana Medical School.

  • Where Largo Antica Corte, Salerno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: conferences.phys.unisa.it · italia.it

Museo archeologico provinciale

Museums · Archaeological

In the complex of San Benedetto, from the prehistory of the caves of Polla, Pertosa and Palinuro to the Greek settlers; the strong piece is the bronze head of Apollo fished in the gulf in 1930, on the upper floor the finds of the necropolis of Fratte.

  • Where Via San Benedetto, Salerno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · conferences.phys.unisa.it · italia.it

Area archeologica di Fratte

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Area of 4,500 m2 set in a park, bordered by walls, with the remains of the town and of the Etruscan-Sannitic necropolis.

  • Where Fratte, Salerno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.italia.it

Chiesa di San Benedetto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Part of the homonymous monastery founded between the VII and IX century and connected to the aqueduct; destroyed by the Saracens in 844, and rebuilt by the abbot Angelario.
