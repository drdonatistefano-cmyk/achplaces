What to see in Magliano Sabina

Sabina town on the valley of the Tiber, with the historic center of Palazzo Vannicelli and the Bishop's Palace, the cathedral of San Liberatore, the Romanesque San Pietro and the Civic Archaeological Museum in Palazzo Gori; the hamlet of Foglia, on a cliff on the left bank of the Tiber, is among the most beautiful villages of Italy.

5 places to visit in Magliano Sabina, in the province of Rieti (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico di Magliano Sabina

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Medieval plant centre with Palazzo Vannicelli, now seat of the Municipality, and the Bishop's Palace of 1593; the main monuments have information panels with QR code.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Magliano Sabina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · comune.maglianosabina.ri.it

Cattedrale di San Liberatore

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

15th century cathedral with frescoes from the 18th century; the clock of the bell tower has a dial divided into six hours instead of twelve and a single hand.

  • Where Centro storico, Magliano Sabina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · comune.maglianosabina.ri.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

  • Entry Free

Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

Established by the Lazio Region in 1989 in Palazzo Gori, it tells on three floors the Sabina civilization: on the ground floor materials of the Middle and Upper Paleolithic, at the first the Bronze and Iron Age with ceramics from the necropolis and a collection of iron weapons, at the second the Hellenistic age, the Romanization and the Middle Ages. Closed on Sundays.

  • Where Palazzo Gori, Via Sabina, Magliano Sabina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Borgo di Foglia

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The only hamlet of Magliano Sabina, on a cliff on the left bank of the Tiber: the castle, mentioned by the second half of the 10th century, controlled the river port and the crossing of the Via Flaminia; destroyed in 1241 in the war between Rome and Viterbo and rebuilt, was then the Orsini. Today the castle is a private residence that hosts cultural events; in the tunnels dug into the rock there is a wellness path.

  • Where Foglia, Magliano Sabina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: comune.maglianosabina.ri.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it
