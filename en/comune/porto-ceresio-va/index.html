What to see in Porto Ceresio

The southernmost of the three Varesine municipalities of Lake Lugano, with colorful houses, a lakefront between the historic center and Liberty villas and the former railway wharf preserved on the water; its territory is part of the Italian extension of the UNESCO site of Monte San Giorgio, famous for the fossils of the Triassico.

Lungolago, centro storico ed ex pontile ferroviario

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The promenade along the lakefront, partly suspended on the water, runs along the old houses of the old town and the Liberty villas and from the embarcadero reaches almost to the Swiss border; it offers views over two branches of the lake and the mountains around. Just outside the station, the railroad's railroad-traveling iron pier is preserved on the water, reminiscent of the first line with electric trains in Italy. From the boat start the boats for the tour of the lake, also with themed cruises; dogs allowed on the leash.

Siti fossiliferi del Monte San Giorgio

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Porto Ceresio is one of the three municipalities (with Besano and Viggiù) of the Italian extension of the UNESCO site of Monte San Giorgio, 240,34 hectares evaluated by IUCN in 2010 for the succession of about 1,000 meters of limestone, dolomies and bituminous schists of the Triassico; on the Italian side, studied since 1863, are known about 35 species of reptiles and almost 100 fish. From the lakefront starts a bike itinerary (about 35 km, 5 hours, 1,100 m of difference in altitude, difficult, recommended e-bike or mountain bike) that goes up to the fossil sites, touches trenches of the Cadorna Line, Sasso Caldo where was found the Besanosaurus, and the fossil museums of Meride (Switzerland) and Besano. The Cadorna Line defended the border during the First World War from a possible Austrian break through neutral Switzerland.

Sources: whc.unesco.org · in-lombardia.it
