What to see in Licenza

Borgo dei Monti Lucretili on the valley of the Licenza stream, the ancient Digentia sung by Orazio: here you can visit the villa that Mecenate donated to the poet in 33-32 BC, the Ninfeo degli Orsini on the probable source Bandusia and the Baronale Orsini Palace with the Oratian Civic Museum.

7 places to visit in Licenza, in the province of Rome (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa di Orazio

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Country residence that Mecenate donated to Orazio between 33 and 32 BC, in the center of an estate of about 40 hectares: a domus italica of Republican age to one floor, 107 m long and 43 wide, in cross-linked work, with residential sector, large quadriportico and spa extended in flavia age, flanked by a porch garden. Digged by Angelo Pasqui since 1911; the artifacts are at the Oratian Civic Museum. Free visits by booking with Aniene Turismo.

Open only by reservation (Aniene Turismo), without regular hours

  • Where Via Licinese snc, Licenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sabap-met-rm.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Ninfeo degli Orsini

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built between the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries against the rocky wall and remodeled in the seventeenth century, it has a spectacular fifth with columns and volute pediment from which a waterfall descends for about three meters in a semicircular tub. It probably rises on the source Bandusia celebrated by Orazio; a more ancient tank in front of the basin could be Roman. You can reach it with a short walk in the woods from the Villa di Orazio.

  • Where SP104a, Licenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Palazzo Baronale Orsini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

It dominates the main square with a square mast of the twelfth century; in the seventeenth century Roberto Orsini and his son Mario, bishop of Tivoli, transformed the fortress into a noble residence, entrusting the decoration to the painter Sabino Vincenzo Manenti. In the main room the frescoes of the four elements, the four continents and the human temperaments, with the Rat of Europe in the center of the vault stucco. In 1632 part of the feud passed to the Borghese, who had it entirely in 1761.

  • Where Piazza del Palazzo, Licenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo civico oraziano

Museums · Archaeological

Housed since 1911 in Palazzo Baronale Orsini, with original arrangement of Angelo Pasqui expanded in 1993, it preserves the finds of the Villa of Orazio: Corinthian capitals, marble slabs engraved, a gap with acantum leaves, frogs and crabs, part of a male statue, lead fistulas, lights, coins, ceramics, everyday objects and fragments of frescoes, stuccoes and mosaics.

MiC card reports "Closed" every day of the week (card updated to 2020)

  • Where Piazza del Palazzo, Licenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Giardino dei 5 Sensi

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

In the Natural Park of Monti Lucretili, with an art journey of seven sculptures in Roman travertine made in 2011 by students of the course of environmental sculpture of the Academy of Fine Arts of Rome.

  • Where Parco dei Monti Lucretili, Licenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Civitella di Licenza e Monte Pellecchia

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The hamlet, an ancient fortress linked to the Abbey of Farfa, has a viewpoint with a wide view of the Parco dei Monti Lucretili and houses the MusAq, museum dedicated to the royal eagle of Monte Pellecchia. From here start the valleys of the Pellecchia massif (1368 m), the highest elevation of the park, where nests a pair of royal eagles.

  • Where Civitella di Licenza, Licenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · parcolucretili.it · italia.it
