What to see in Pignataro Maggiore

In Caleno, Pignataro Maggiore houses a small civic museum dedicated to tools and tools of peasant and artisan life and, in Piazza Umberto I, the church of Santa Maria della Misericordia, decorated with stucco; on the hill towards San Pasquale remain the ruins of Castellone dei Ranallo.

Museo della civiltà contadina e artigiana

Museums · Themed

A small civic museum that houses and exhibits tools and tools of peasant and craft life; it is also a cultural center with conferences and field researches on popular songs, flora and fauna, sayings, food and crafts.

Chiesa di Santa Maria della Misericordia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was born from a small chapel annexed to the Bishop's House, where there remains a fresco of the Madonna and Child between two saints dating from around 1500; in 1742 it became parish church in place of St. George Martyr on the hill, and the works, begun in 1766, ended in 1913. The interior with a single nave, with five chapels on each side and semicircular apse, is richly decorated with stucco and preserves a sixteenth-century table of the Virgin of the Rosary with the fifteen Mysteries and a bas-relief of the Flagellation on the pulpit; on the stone portal the inscription "Laicalis Ecclesia S. Maria Mercy.
