What to see in Pietracamela

Medieval stone village on the slopes of the Corno Piccolo, in the National Park of Gran Sasso and Monti della Laga, with houses from the fifteenth century, the churches of San Giovanni (1432) and San Rocco (1530); in the territory the ski resort of Prati di Tivo and the hamlet of Intermesoli.

7 places to visit in Pietracamela, in the province of Teramo (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The precious village of stone: palaces of the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, narrow alleys with small arches, balconies-barn, workshops dug in the rock and ancient fountains, among newsstands, icons and ancient Spanish inscriptions. In Palazzo Dionisi the visitor center of the National Park.

  • Where Pietracamela
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Ancient church with date 1432 engraved on the architrave of the portal, surrounded by houses with portals dated from 1471 to 1616.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Casa Signoretti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

An ancient residence in Via Vittorio Veneto with two stone windows: one shows a head with open scissors, perhaps symbol of wool carders, the other a knight who plays the trumpet, with a face in front and one in profile.

  • Where Via Vittorio Veneto, Pietracamela
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · gransassolagapark.it · italia.it

Prati di Tivo

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Locality at 1450 meters at the foot of the Corno Piccolo, the main ski resort of the Teramano: a quadriposto and a two-seater chairlift go up to 2000 meters to the Arapietra, with about 20 km of downhill slopes and a cross-country trail; in summer base for excursions on the Gran Sasso.

  • Where Prati di Tivo, Pietracamela
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: gransassolagapark.it · italia.it

Sentiero da Porta Fontana a Intermesoli

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

A path that descends from the village to the hamlet of Intermesoli (770 meters), an ancient hamlet of wool in the woods, passing through the public wash-house, the remains of the church of the Madonna and the old mill on the Rio Arno; in Intermesoli the churches of Santa Maria, San Lorenzo and San Rocco, with the parish church from the Baroque altars, and in October the chestnut festival.

  • Where Porta Fontana, Pietracamela
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: gransassolagapark.it · italia.it
