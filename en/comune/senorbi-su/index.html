What to see in Senorbì

The main centre of Trexenta, about 40 km from Cagliari, Senorbì is called "cradle of civilization" for the Carthaginian settlement of Santu Teru and the necropolis of Monte Luna, whose equipments are exhibited in the museum sa Domu Nosta; in the village the Romanesque church of Santa Mariedda and the manor houses of the historic center.

Museo archeologico sa Domu Nosta

Museums · Archaeological

In a nineteenth-century manor house in the center of the village exhibits the finds of the park of Monte Luna and Santu Teru: amphorae, jugs, dishes, ointments and skylights, coins, Egyptian taste objects, Greek imports and center-italic (IV-III century BC), gold and silver jewels with scarab rings, pendants, necklaces, diadems and numerous amulets. It documents the continuity of population of the territory from the recent Neolithic to the Middle Ages, with the Nuragic finds of about Nuraxi of Sisini and of the nuraghi Simieri and Mount Uda, and has an ethnographic section. The famous bronzetto "miles cornutus" was found near the village in 1841, and is instead the National Archaeological Museum of Cagliari.

Parco archeologico di Monte Luna e Santu Teru

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The settlement of Santu Teru (Teodoro), about 2 km from the village at the confluence of two streams, was founded at the end of the 6th century BC as a Carthaginian outpost and developed as an agricultural center between the 5th and the 3rd century BC, when it was abandoned: the acropolis, a walls, houses outside the walls and perhaps fusion workshops remain. On the slopes of Monte Luna, a few tens of meters, more than 120 tombs have been identified, mostly a well with side rooms, but also a pit, a chest, loculus and enchytrismos for children, with votive trays and niches with traces of geometric paintings in red ochre. In Roman times Monte Luna became the most important center of the area.

Chiesa di Santa Mariedda (Nostra Signora della Neve)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest sanctuary of Senorbì, presumably built in the last quarter of the 13th century as the church of Santa Maria of the village of Segolay, attested from 1215 and disappeared at the end of the 17th century. In square sandstones, it had semicircular apse and unique hall with wooden trusses; the current façade incorporates the Romanesque one with pointed-shaped hanging arches, with a portal and a large sailing bell tower. Party in early August.

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Narrow streets with manor houses and rural houses; the main church is the parish church of Santa Barbara, while the church of San Sebastiano dates back to the 17th century.

Sisini: Nuraghe su Nuraxi e Villa Aresu

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the hamlet of Sisini the nuraghe on Nuraxi has an unusual plant, similar to that of a well temple; its Nuragic finds are exhibited at the museum sa Domu Nosta. In the hamlet also Villa Aresu, a nineteenth-century residence, and the arrival of the trot races is Cuaddus de santa Vida for the feast of Santa Vitalia.
