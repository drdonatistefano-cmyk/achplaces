What to see in Bologna

Monumenti, chiese, musei, parchi e angoli nascosti del comune di Bologna, con biglietti, orari e come arrivare. Ogni scheda cita le sue fonti ufficiali.

27 places to visit in Bologna, in the province of Bologna (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: la salita alla Torre degli Asinelli è sospesa da fine 2023 e non ha ancora una data di riapertura (la stampa locale ipotizza il 2028): l'alternativa ufficiale è la Torre dell'Orologio. La terrazza di San Petronio è chiusa in modo definitivo.

Piazza Maggiore e Fontana del Nettuno

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The civic heart of Bologna since the thirteenth century: San Petronio, Palazzo d'Accursio, Palazzo del Podestà and, next to it, the Neptune of Giambologna.

  • Where Piazza Maggiore / Piazza del Nettuno, 40124 Bologna
  • Entry Free, always accessible
  • Suggested time 30 min
  • Official website www.bolognawelcome.com

Sources: bolognawelcome.com

The Two Towers: Asinelli and Garisenda

History and architecture · Towers · Paid entry · Viewpoint

A symbol of the troubled city. The donkey (1109-1119) is 97.20 m high; the Garisenda, lower and inclined, is not visited inside.

Donkeys closed from end 2023 for Garisenda's safety work. The reopening does not have a certain date: the local press indicates as hypothesis the 2028. Alternative: Clock Tower.

  • Where Piazza di Porta Ravegnana, 40126 Bologna
  • Entry Ascent normally paid (indicated €5 full / €3 reduced from secondary source). Now suspend.
  • Suggested time 45 min (climb)
  • Official website www.bolognawelcome.com

Sources: bolognawelcome.com · bolognastationsuites-com.pages.dev · ilrestodelcarlino.it · finestresullarte.info

Torre dell'Orologio e Collezioni Comunali d'Arte

History and architecture · Towers · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Inside Palazzo d'Accursio: you climb between the clock mechanism up to two terraces with 360° views of Piazza Maggiore and the rooftops. The ticket includes the Municipal Art Collections.

  • Where Palazzo d'Accursio, Piazza Maggiore 6 (ingresso da Sala Farnese, 2° piano)
  • Entry €10 whole · €7 reduced · free with Bologna Welcome Card (booking)
  • Suggested time 40 min tower + 1 h collections
  • Official website www.bolognawelcome.com

Sources: bolognawelcome.com

Archiginnasio e Teatro Anatomico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Ancient university seat, with courtyard and loggias covered with coats of arms. On the first floor the Anatomical Theatre in carved wood (1637) and the Sala dello Stabat Mater.

  • Where Piazza Luigi Galvani 1, 40124 Bologna
  • Entry Courtyard free of charge. Anatomical Theatre: guided tour €12 / €10 reduced, or audioguide €10 / €8 reduced
  • Suggested time 45 min
  • Official website www.bolognawelcome.com

Sources: bolognawelcome.com · archiginnasio.it

Portici di Bologna (UNESCO): the Pavaglione

History and architecture · Portici UNESCO · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

From July 28, 2021 the porticos are World Heritage: 12 sections chosen from 62 km of the city. The Pavaglione, next to San Petronio, is the most central stretch.

  • Where Portico del Pavaglione, via dell'Archiginnasio, 40124 Bologna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time Free
  • Official website portici.comune.bologna.it

Sources: comune.bologna.it · bolognawelcome.com

Biblioteca Salaborsa e scavi archeologici

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Under the crystal floor of the covered square you can see the remains from the Villanovian age to the Roman Bononia, up to the botanical garden of Aldrovandi.

  • Where Piazza del Nettuno 3, 40124 Bologna
  • Entry Free entrance (excavations with free offer; guided tours by reservation)
  • Suggested time 30 min
  • Official website www.bolognawelcome.com

Sources: bolognawelcome.com · italia.it

Basilica di San Petronio

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Gothic, started in 1390 and never ended up on the façade. Inside the sundial of Cassini, 67.27 m, the longest in the world, and the Chapel of the Magi.

The panoramic terrace is permanently closed after restoration.

  • Where Piazza Galvani 5, 40124 Bologna
  • Entry Free basilica. Route of the three chapels (King Magi, San Sebastiano, San Vincenzo Ferrer) for a fee: €5 full / €3 reduced according to sources 2026. Free with BW Card
  • Suggested time 45 min
  • Official website www.basilicadisanpetronio.org

Sources: basilicadisanpetronio.org · bolognawelcome.com · idealista.it

Complesso di Santo Stefano (Sette Chiese)

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

A set of churches, cloisters and courtyards that arose in different eras, the oldest religious nucleus in the city, with the Courtyard of Pilate and the Benedictine cloister.

  • Where Via Santo Stefano 24, 40125 Bologna
  • Entry Free access with offer
  • Suggested time 45 min
  • Official website www.santostefanobologna.it

Sources: bolognawelcome.com

Santa Maria della Vita: il Compianto di Niccolò dell'Arca

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Paid entry

In the Quadrilatero, the Baroque church preserves the Complaint on Christ who died in terracotta (second half of the fifteenth century), seven figures in life size.

  • Where Via Clavature 10, 40124 Bologna
  • Entry Complained €8 whole / €5 reduced. Free with BW Card and Card Culture
  • Suggested time 40 min
  • Official website genusbononiae.it

Sources: bolognawelcome.com

Basilica di San Domenico

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

It preserves the Ark of San Domenico, begun by Nicola Pisano and completed by Niccolò dell'Arca, with three statues carved by a young Michelangelo.

Bologna Welcome reports restoration work with an end scheduled for July 2026: check if the yard is finished.

  • Where Piazza San Domenico 13, 40124 Bologna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30 min
  • Official website sandomenicobologna.it

Sources: bolognawelcome.com

Santuario di San Luca e il suo portico

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the Colle della Guardia, reachable from Porta Zaragoza with the longest portico in the world: 3.796 m and 666 arcades, stretch of the UNESCO Porticos.

  • Where Via di San Luca 36, 40135 Bologna (partenza a piedi da Porta Saragozza)
  • Entry Free of charge, both the porch and the sanctuary
  • Suggested time 3-4 h walk round-trip
  • Official website portici.comune.bologna.it

Sources: portici.comune.bologna.it · fondoambiente.it · bolognawelcome.com

Certosa di Bologna, cimitero monumentale

Places of worship · Monumental cemeteries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Etruscan necropolis, then Carthusian monastery, cemetery since 1801: cloisters, sculptures and monuments of the nineteenth century. Connected to San Luca by a porch.

  • Where Via della Certosa 18, 40133 Bologna
  • Entry Free (Guided tours on the calendar)
  • Suggested time 1-2 h
  • Official website www.storiaememoriadibologna.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · bolognawelcome.com

Pinacoteca Nazionale di Bologna

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The great Bolognese pictorial school from the fourteenth to the eighteenth century: Vitale da Bologna, Raffaello (Estasi di Santa Cecilia), Carracci, Guido Reni, Guercino.

  • Where Via delle Belle Arti 56, 40126 Bologna
  • Entry €10 full · €6 affiliated · €2 from 18 to 25 years · free under 18, before Sunday of the month and with Bologna Welcome Card or Card Culture. During the exhibitions the price can change. The ticket also applies to Palazzo Pepoli Campogrande within three months
  • Suggested time 1.5-2 h
  • Official website pinacotecabologna.cultura.gov.it

Sources: pinacotecabologna.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo Civico Archeologico

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the fifteenth century Palazzo Galvani: from prehistory to Etruscan and Roman Bologna, with an Egyptian collection of over 3,500 objects among the most important in Europe.

  • Where Via dell'Archiginnasio 2, 40124 Bologna
  • Entry About €6 whole (data 2026 of the Ministry); free with BW Card and before Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 1.5 h
  • Official website www.museibologna.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · museionline.info

Museo Civico Medievale

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In Palazzo Ghisilardi, the Bentivoglio age: tombs of the Doctors of the Studio, weapons, avori and the large golden copper statue of Bonifacio VIII (1301).

  • Where Via Manzoni 4, 40121 Bologna
  • Entry €6 full · €3/€2 reduced · free with BW Card and Card Culture
  • Suggested time 1 h
  • Official website www.museibologna.it

Sources: bolognawelcome.com

MAMbo e Museo Morandi

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

In the former Forno del Pane: Italian art from post-war to today and the largest public collection in the world dedicated to Giorgio Morandi.

  • Where Via Don Minzoni 14, 40121 Bologna
  • Entry €6 full / €4 reduced, Morandi Museum included. Free with BW Card and Card Culture
  • Suggested time 1.5 h
  • Official website www.museibologna.it

Sources: bolognawelcome.com

Casa Morandi

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

The house in Via Fondazza where Giorgio Morandi lived and worked from 1933 to 1964, with the rebuilt studio, the library and the artist's documents.

  • Where Via Fondazza 36, 40125 Bologna
  • Entry Free (recommended to book your free ticket online)
  • Suggested time 30 min
  • Official website cultura.gov.it

Sources: bolognawelcome.com

Museo della Storia di Bologna (Palazzo Pepoli)

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the fourteenth-century Palazzo Pepoli, a multimedia path from the Etruscan Felsina to the present day, also designed for families.

  • Where Via Castiglione 10, 40124 Bologna
  • Entry €10 whole (with audio guide if booked online); reductions for over 65, students and residents
  • Suggested time 1.5 h
  • Official website palazzopepoli.it

Museo di Palazzo Poggi

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

The eighteenth-century collections of the Institute of Sciences: anatomical and obstetric waxes, nautical charts, physics and the museum of Ulisse Aldrovandi, among frescoes by Pellegrino Tibaldi.

  • Where Via Zamboni 33, 40126 Bologna
  • Entry €10 whole / €6 reduced. On the ground floor Sala Alma Mater free
  • Suggested time 1.5 h
  • Official website sma.unibo.it

Sources: bolognawelcome.com · italia.it

Orto Botanico ed Erbario dell'Università

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Free entry

Founded in 1568 by Ulisse Aldrovandi, one of the oldest in Italy: about 2 hectares in the university area up to the ancient walls, with greenhouses and monumental trees.

  • Where Via Irnerio 42, 40126 Bologna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 45 min
  • Official website sma.unibo.it

Giardini Margherita

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

The largest and most popular park in the city: 26 hectares of English opened in 1879, with a pond and an Etruscan tomb found during the works.

  • Where Viale Giovanni Gozzadini, 40136 Bologna (ingressi da Porta Santo Stefano e Porta Castiglione)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1 h
  • Official website www.bolognawelcome.com

Sources: bolognawelcome.com

Parco di Villa Ghigi

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Almost 28 hectares on the first hills outside Porta San Mamolo: meadows, woods, orchards and one of the most beautiful viewpoints on the city. From here start the CAI 904 trail towards Ronzano and Gaibola.

  • Where Ingressi in via San Mamolo, via Martucci e via Gaibola (parcheggio), 40136 Bologna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1.5-3 h
  • Official website www.fondazionevillaghigi.it

Sources: bolognawelcome.com

Quadrilatero e Mercato di Mezzo

Local life and traditions · Historic markets · Free entry

The streets of the medieval market behind Piazza Maggiore still bear the names of the guilds: Pescherie Vecchie, Drapperie, Orefici, Clavature. Meat shops, cheeses, fresh pasta.

  • Where Via Pescherie Vecchie, via Drapperie, via Clavature, 40124 Bologna
  • Entry Free access
  • Suggested time 1 h
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Finestrella di via Piella

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

A small window in the wall opens onto the Canale delle Moline, one of the few stretches of water that have been discovered: the 'small Venice' in Bologna.

  • Where Via Piella 16/a-18/a, 40126 Bologna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 15 min
