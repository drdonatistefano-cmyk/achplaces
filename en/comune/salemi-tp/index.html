What to see in Salemi

Village between hills of vineyards and olive groves in Trapani, dominated by the Norman-Svevo castle wanted by Frederick II: historic center in stone "campanedda" with the districts of Giudecca and Rabato, the remains of the Mother Church collapsed in 1968, the Museum Pole in the former Jesuit College and the village of the Bronze Age of Mokarta.

9 places to visit in Salemi, in the province of Trapani (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello normanno-svevo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built around 1225 under Frederick II of Swabia, it preserves three of the four corner towers and is among the best preserved in Sicily despite the earthquakes of 1693, 1783, 1794 and 1968.

  • Where Salemi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Polo museale - Museo civico nell'ex Collegio dei Gesuiti

Museums · Old masters

In the seventeenth century Collegio dei Jesuiti, with works from the Renaissance sculpture to Mannerist and Baroque painting: the section of sacred art exhibits works saved from the churches damaged by the earthquake of Belice of 1968, including pieces attributed to Domenico Gagini and Francesco Laurana; the archaeological section finds from the excavations of the territory. A wing reconstructs the Holy House of Loreto with frescoed vaults.

  • Where Via Francesco D'Aguirre, Salemi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Museo del Risorgimento (Mostra di cimeli del Risorgimento)

Museums · City history

Founded in 1960 and since 1999 in the former Jesuit College, it collects weapons, uniforms, photographs and original documents on the role of Salemi in the 1848 revolution and in the 1860 Mille expedition.

  • Where Via Francesco D'Aguirre, Salemi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Chiesa del Collegio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Resti della Chiesa Madre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built in 1615 on the highest point of the country, probably on the site of an elymous temple of Venus and a mosque, with a Latin cross: the 1968 earthquake caused a large part of it to collapse, but the apse remained standing.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Centro storico, Giudecca e Rabato

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Churches and buildings in local sandstone called "campanedda": partly destroyed by the earthquake of 1968, the center still retains 28 churches, the ancient Jewish district of the Judecca and the Islamic one of the Rabato with its alleys.

Area archeologica di Mokarta

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village inhabited by the Sicans since the second millennium BC and destroyed by a sudden event at the end of the Bronze Age: fifteen circular huts with an entrance "a forcepe" are seen; from hut 15 are the skeleton of a young woman, called Nina, and over one hundred almost intact vases. Visit by appointment with the archaeological park of Segesta.

  • Where Contrada Mokarta, Salemi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · regione.sicilia.it · italia.it
