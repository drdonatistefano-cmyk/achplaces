What to see in Prata Sannita

Borgo dell'Alto Casertano along the river Lete: the medieval fortified village of Prata Inferiore is dominated by the castle of the Pando, today a cultural hub with the historical military museum and the museum of peasant civilization.

Castello Pandone

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The first nucleus dates back to the year 1000, but the present aspect is mainly fourteenth-century, when the counts Pandone enlarged and fortified it: cylindrical towers around a courtyard, with chapel, hall and armory on the ground floor and princely apartments, terraces and cisterns on the upper floor; in the tower used as a prison remain graffiti of suns, flowers and birds. The walls dominate the Volturno valley; from the Scuncio family for over 150 years, today it is a cultural hub with three museums.

Borgo medievale di Prata Inferiore

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified village born in the IX century, when the inhabitants moved to the height to escape the invasions; it preserves the medieval aspect with views of great suggestion along the river Lete.

  • Where Prata Inferiore, Prata Sannita
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it

Museo storico militare

Museums · Themed

Created and edited by Vittorio Scuncio in the castle, it has a section on the Great War (Italian-Austrian front 1915-1918, until the Bulletin of Victory) and one on the Second World War, with relics of Italian, German and allied troops (1943-1945), divided soldiers lodged in the castle, documents on the lines Gustav, Barbara and Winter and oral testimonies of the Pratos. Patronage of the Ministry since 1995.

MiC card indicates closed every day; check opening

Museums · Themed
