What to see in Uscio

Sunny village of the hinterland of the Golfo Paradiso, about 15 minutes drive from the Riviera, with the motto "janua patet" in the coat of arms: here is the historic factory of Trebino tower clocks, with its museum, and the Romanesque parish church of Sant'Ambrogio.

Museo degli orologi da torre «Roberto Trebino»

Museums · Themed

The first museum dedicated to the tower clock and bells, dedicated to the founder Roberto Trebino and set up in the old Trebino plant: you can see how a clock is built and how a bell is made, in about thirty historical pieces, from the French, German and Austrian watches of the seventeenth century to the modern ones radio-synchronous; the oldest piece is a medieval bell of 1290 of about three quintals. The history of the Trebino family began in 1824 with a first wooden tower clock, then installed in the church of San Siro di Struppa in Genoa. Visitable in office hours by appointment (tel. 0185 919410.

Pieve e chiesa di Sant'Ambrogio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church dates back to the 11th century, in Romanesque style of Lombard taste, and houses the polychrome wooden statue of St. Ambrose of the 15th century, a national monument since 1920. Next to it, the church of Sant'Ambrogio was built from 1893 and completed at the beginning of the twentieth century with the staircase in slate, the facade and the pulpit; damaged in the Second World War and restored in the middle of the century, preserves paintings and the Madonna dei Sette Dolori attributed to the school of Margliano.
