What to see in Pagnacco

A hilly town north of Udine, where in the village of Fontanabona the castle with its hamlet and the land forms a complex of about 200 hectares remained almost intact, also home to the Museum of rural history (now closed for renovation).

Museo di storia contadina di Fontanabona

Museums · Themed

In two rural buildings of the ancient fiefdom of Fontanabona, on the last hills before the plain, it collects rustic furniture, ceramics, household objects and agricultural tools and craftsmen: a working frame of the first half of the nineteenth century, the samples of the textile company Spezzotti (since 1857), tools for the stable, the bachiculture and the harvest and a large collection of ploughs. He's got a specialized library. It is normally open from April to November by appointment.

The site of the Municipality indicates closed museum for renovations

Castello e parco di Fontanabona

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The first castle dates back to the 10th century; since the 12th century the Fontanabona built a manor house, loggia, stables, chapel, circular tower and walls. Today it is a country villa of the eighteenth century on three levels, with arched portal and coat of arms, a chapel of kindness of sixteenth-century forms and a section of the wall of belt with a sharp sixth window; a lightning destroyed the tower at the beginning of the nineteenth century. Since 1969 it is owned by the Region: the castle is inhabited, while the park annexed is indicated for use of public park.
