What to see in Trisobbio

Castello e torre

Castle of the 12th century, today restaurant with hotel; the tower, about 30 meters high, can be visited by reservation.

Palazzo De Rossi-Dogliotti e belvedere

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

18th century Palace municipal seat, with cellars that house ancient press, well, glacier and infernot; front, a view with ample panorama.
