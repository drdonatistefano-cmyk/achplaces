What to see in Naso

Borgo on a hill at 495 meters in the first hinterland of Nebrodi, with a square overlooking Aeolian, Nebrodi and Etna: the Mother Church with the Chapel of the Rosary of Bartolomeo Travaglia, the Temple of San Cono with the Museum of Sacred Art in the catacombs, the church of SS. Salvatore with works of the Gagini and the convent of the Minor Observers.

Museo d'Arte Sacra (Catacombe di San Cono)

Museums · Old masters · Free entry

Set up on about 260 square meters in the catacombs of the Temple of San Cono, in four sections: wooden and majolica sculptures of the majolica that were active in Naso from the fifteenth to the seventeenth century; vestments, a bronze bell and objects of the confraternities; paintings and stone works, including the table "Madonna" with dormant Child, dating from the first decades of the sixteenth century, the circle of Joos van Cleve, and two ovals attributed to Olivio Sozzi; liturgical silvers and ex-vote. Open Monday 9-13, the other days 9-13 and 15-18.

  • Where Via San Biagio 6, Naso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.naso.me.it

Sources: comune.naso.me.it

Chiesa Madre dei Santi Filippo e Giacomo e Cappella del Rosario

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Chapel of the Rosary, masterpiece of the Carrara Marmoratorio Bartolomeo Travaglia in white Carrara marble and pink San Marco d'Alunzio marble, decorated with a thick series of symbols: the Holy Grail, the blue rose and the red rose, the pelican, the knot of Solomon, the eight-pointed star, the black Madonna, the lions.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Naso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.naso.me.it

Sources: comune.naso.me.it

Chiesa di San Cono

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

The Temple of San Cono preserves paintings from the seventeenth century and, in the crypt, the relics of the saint, a place of worship and a great pilgrimage.

  • Entry To be checked

Chiesa del SS. Salvatore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the center of the old town district, it has a large Baroque façade with local terracotta parishyard and inside works by Gagini and Camarda.

Convento dei Minori Osservanti

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The ruins of the cloister, a Gothic entrance gate dating from 1475 and a wooden altar dating back to 1694, remain among the Observing Friars Minor, who arrived at Naso in 1475, and there is also a Madonna attributed to Gagini.

  • Where Naso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.naso.me.it

Sources: comune.naso.me.it

Centro storico e belvedere della piazza

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historic centre, always open to visitors, has one of the five gates of the walls in the eastern part, the Monte di Pietà (recreational circle since 1868) and the Hospital of Santa Maria della Pietà; the castle, destroyed by the earthquake of 1786, remains the name of the promenade, where in 1873 the Teatro Alfieri was born. From the central square you can see the Aeolian Islands, Nebrodi and Etna.

  • Where Centro storico, Naso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.naso.me.it

Sources: comune.naso.me.it · italia.it
