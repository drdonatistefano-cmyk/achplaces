What to see in Cannara

The town of the Umbra sul Topino valley, linked to the presence of St. Francis (the Sacred Tugurio, the foundation of the Third Order in 1221, the Preaching to the birds of Piandarca) and rich in works of the pupil; in its territory the hamlet of Collemancio and the archaeological area of the Roman town hall of Urvinum Hortense, whose finds, including a large Nilotic mosaic, are exhibited in the village.

Museo Città di Cannara

Museums · Archaeological

In a former Salesian monastery, he exhibits the findings of Urvinum Hortense, including the mosaic of the 65 square meters of thermal baths (late I-early II century AD) with nilotic scenes and a central peach pane, as well as frescoes, paintings and sculptures by Dono Doni and Marcantonio Grecchi.

  • Where Via del Convitto, Cannara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Area archeologica di Urvinum Hortense

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Roman town hall on a ridge at 526 meters overlooking the Umbra valley, a few hundred meters from Collemancio: plant of the second century BC, maximum development between the end of the first century BC and beginning II AD, decline in the fifth century. The walls remain sections, the urban layout along the road to Mevania and Vettona, a temple of the II century BC on podium of 23.80 x 17.80 meters, a cistern and a spa complex of over 400 square meters; with Roman materials was built the early Christian basilica of Santa Maria de Orbinum.

  • Where Collemancio, Cannara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it
