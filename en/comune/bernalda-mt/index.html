What to see in Bernalda

Medieval village with a fifteenth-century castle and, in its territory, Metaponto: the Greek colony founded in the 7th century BC with the temple of Hera of the Palatine Tables, the archaeological park and the national museum.

5 places to visit in Bernalda, in the province of Matera (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Bernalda in the guide with map and filters

Tempio di Hera – Tavole Palatine

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Doric Temple dedicated to Hera, on the right bank of the Bradano about three kilometers from the ancient city: there are 15 of the 32 original columns, 10 on the north side and 5 on the south side; dug in 1926 and restored in 1961, in the Middle Ages it was called "Mensae Imperatoris."

  • Where SS 106 Jonica, Bernalda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo archeologico nazionale di Metaponto

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Ceramics, sculptures, coins, votive objects and instruments of the Greek colony founded in the 7th century BC, in chronological order from the traces of the Bronze and Iron Ages to the Roman conquest.

  • Where Via Dinu Adamesteanu 21, Metaponto
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
