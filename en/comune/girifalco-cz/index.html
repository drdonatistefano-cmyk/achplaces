What to see in Girifalco

Country on the slopes of Mount Covello, founded according to the tradition by refugees of Toco and Caria fleeing from the Saracens (836) and devastated by the earthquake of 1783: the baroque fountain of 1663, the six-cinette Canaletta, the Ducal Palace of the Caracciolo, the Matrice Church, St. Rocco and the Addolorata, the former psychiatric hospital and the ruins of Pietra dei Monaci.

Fontana barocca (Fontana del Diavolo)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Fountain in stone, octagonal outside and circular inside, with a tub; bears the inscription of the mayor Carlo Pacino and the date 1663, while the upper part is dated 1830. Legend has it that it appears in a single day: it was actually mounted in a few hours by the local stonemasons, who had prepared the modules in the cave of the stonemasons near the Stone of the Monks. Unknown author.

  • Where Centro storico, Girifalco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it

Palazzo Ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Born in the thirteenth century as a fortification after the sixth crusade (there is a tower, the perimeter walls on the back and on the east side and the supporting stone structure), it was transformed into a residence in 1655 by Duke Fabrizio Caracciolo. It belonged to the Ravaschieri, the Caracciolo and then to the Magno Oliverio, with various modifications after the earthquake. You can see the entrance to the feudal history.

  • Where Corso Garibaldi e Piazza Umberto I, Girifalco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.girifalco.cz.it · italia.it

Chiesa Matrice della Madonna delle Nevi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt in 1789 on the site of the chapel of the Dominican monastery, whose ruins are visible to the right of the church; the previous, near San Rocco, was destroyed by the earthquake of 1783. Next to it stands the church of the Rosary (XVIII-XIX century). Open during functions.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Girifalco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.girifalco.cz.it · italia.it

Chiesa dell'Addolorata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

On a small hill, founded in the fifteenth century just outside the town and rebuilt several times, with the current appearance following the earthquake of 1783: three small naves with golden columns and decorations, statue of the Addolorata on the ancient pink and white altar, bell tower on the left side and stations of the Via Crucis in front of the façade. Open during functions.

  • Where Via Guglielmo Marconi, Girifalco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.girifalco.cz.it · italia.it

Ex ospedale psichiatrico e Archivio storico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Former provincial asylum founded in 1881 in a building that had been a seventeenth-century convent of the Friars Minor; with its historical archive it was opened for the FAI Fall Day 2023 and has been repeatedly reported to the Places of the Heart.

Pietra dei Monaci e Pioppi Vecchi

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The oldest sites in the country, destroyed by the earthquake of 1783, of which many ruins remain; near the rock of the Stone of the Monks there is the cave of the chisels where the modules of the Baroque fountain were prepared.
