What to see in Fara San Martino

The city of pasta, the Orange Flag of Touring, at the eastern gates of the Majella National Park: behind the village open the Gorges of San Martino, which lead to the remains of the Benedictine abbey of San Martino in Valle, while downstream flow the springs of the Green River; in the historic center the medieval village of Terravecchia is reached from the Sun Gate.

6 places to visit in Fara San Martino, in the province of Chieti (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Gole di San Martino

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hole dug by water behind the village, with rock walls that almost touch each other; they are a geosite of the UNESCO Geopark of Majella. Legend has it that St. Martin has widened the strait with his elbows to make the shepherds pass towards the mountain, and the "marmittes" in the rock would be the marks left. You will walk along the path H1: to enter the gorges the Park strongly recommends helmet and suitable footwear.

  • Where Piazza Municipio (partenza sentiero H1), Fara San Martino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcomajella.it · italia.it

Monastero benedettino di San Martino in Valle

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Beyond the narrowing of the gorges, between high limestone walls inhabited by the Apennine suede, the remains of the Benedictine abbey appear, brought to light entirely from the archaeological excavations of 2009: the ruins of the abbey and the guesthouse range from the IX to the XV century. It is one of the monastic rocks of the Park. The H1 ring from the village (gorges, monastery, green springs and Terravecchia) takes about 3 hours, with 150 m of altitude difference, difficulty E.

  • Where Gole di San Martino, Fara San Martino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcomajella.it · italia.it

Sorgenti del fiume Verde

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Green, a tributary of Aventine, flows abundant at about 410 meters at the foot of the limestone walls of the eastern Majella, in two emerald green tanks due to the algae of the bottom, with some artificial waterfalls along the promenade. The average capacity is about 2,500 liters per second: one part feeds the aqueduct of about fifty municipalities of Chietino, another the central ones that serve the pasta factories. From the municipal villa you go down towards the pasta factories along Via De Cecco and you go up to the left the pedestrian path: about 1 km.

  • Where Via San Pietro, Fara San Martino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcomajella.it · italia.it

Museo naturalistico e Centro visita del Parco

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

Attached to the Park Visitor Centre in the main square, it presents the environments and caves of the eastern side of Majella, from high altitudes to rocky walls, to moors, gorges and underground environments, with photos, illustrations, panels and natural finds; it reconstructs a pastoral cave with the habitat of the yellow-bellyed ululon, rare amphibian, and an audiovisual panel makes you listen to the songs of the birds of the Park. It is the starting point for the Valley of Santo Spirito and the green springs.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Fara San Martino
  • Entry €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Terravecchia, Porta del Sole e San Remigio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the historic centre, the ancient medieval village of Terravecchia can be reached through the Porta del Sole, an eighteenth-century gate considered the most remarkable architectural monument of Fara, or from the parish church of San Remigio; along the way you will find the old communal wash-house.

  • Where Terravecchia, Fara San Martino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcomajella.it · touringclub.it

Vallone di Santo Spirito e Monte Amaro

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Through the gorges you enter the Vallone di Santo Spirito, which climbs up to the summit of Monte Amaro in the Majella National Park: a long and tiring climb. In the valley live the chamois all year round.

  • Where Gole di San Martino, Fara San Martino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcomajella.it
