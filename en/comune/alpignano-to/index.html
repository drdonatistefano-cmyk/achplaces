What to see in Alpignano

City on the Dora Riparia linked to Alessandro Cruto, inventor of the carbon filament bulb: its opificio of 1885 hosts the eco-museum "Dream of light"; to see also the parish church of San Martino, Santa Maria del Ponte overlooking the river and the forest of the Ghiaro.

Ecomuseo «Sogno di luce» – Opificio Cruto

Museums · Themed

The factory was built in 1885 by Gerolamo Taddei for Alessandro Cruto, to produce on an international scale its carbon filament bulbs; the building remained almost intact, with the chimney of the high factory. Since 1996 it houses a museum in three sections: the life of Cruto and the impact of the factory on Alpignano, the history of lighting with instruments and lamps, the invention in its historical context.

  • Where Via Matteotti 2, Alpignano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Parrocchiale di San Martino di Tours

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Rebuilt in 1695 on the initiative of the Confraternity of Santa Croce and enlarged and made parish in 1807; the bell tower is the oldest part. It preserves a Madonna by Stefano Maria Clemente, an eighteenth-century wooden Crucifix by Carlo Giuseppe Plura, the medallion of the vault by Giovanni Enea Coda (mid-nineteenth century) and an organ by Achille Baldi from 1903.

  • Where Piazza Parrocchia 3, Alpignano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Cappella dei Caduti (antica San Martino)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The ancient parish of San Martino, a small Romanesque church first mentioned in 1031 in a document from the Abbey of Novalesa, today the Chapel of the Fallen.

  • Where Piazza Caduti 15, Alpignano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.alpignano.to.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria del Ponte

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Built on the Dora to protect travelers and pilgrims, it was enlarged in 1855; inside the choir and gallery of 1840 on four stone columns, in façade a mosaic of 1957.

  • Where Presso il ponte sulla Dora, Alpignano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.alpignano.to.it

Sources: comune.alpignano.to.it

Bosco del Ghiaro

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Hardwood of almost 39,000 m2 on the right bank of the Dora Riparia, upstream of the village, on moraine deposits of the glacier of the Valley of Susa: lindens, elms, poplars, tremoli, farnie, ash and birch. Walk along dirt road and path along the river.

  • Where Accessi lungo la Dora dal centro e da via Chiri, Alpignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.alpignano.to.it

Sources: comune.alpignano.to.it
