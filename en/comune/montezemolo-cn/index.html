What to see in Montezemolo

The city of honey is about 750 metres, at the extreme south of the Langhe: here the Belbo is born, protected by the Belbo Nature Reserve with its wetlands and orchids, and in the village Villa overlooks the Cuneo plain the fortified castle of Cordero di Mondovì.

Riserva naturale Sorgenti del Belbo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Small undulating plateau between Montezemolo, Camerana and Saliceto where the Belbo was born, established by the Piedmont Region to protect rare seasonal marshlands among cultivated, woods and humid meadows. Censite 352 vegetable entities, including 22 species of orchids (rare the incarnate orchid and the aquatic one) and the martagon lily. A naturalistic itinerary with panels goes from Montezemolo to the Pilone del Foresto; then there is the itinerary New visitor center.

  • Where Strada provinciale 661, Montezemolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.sorgentidelbelbo.areeprotettealpimarittime.it

Sources: sorgentidelbelbo.areeprotettealpimarittime.it · visitcuneese.it

Castello fortificato di borgata Villa

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortified castle owned by the Cordero di Mondovì family: quadrangular palace along the road to Camerana, overlooking a large park with views of the Cuneo plain. Next is the parish church of San Benedetto.

Uninhabited for many years, sources do not indicate public openings

  • Where Borgata Villa, Montezemolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: sorgentidelbelbo.areeprotettealpimarittime.it · visitcuneese.it
