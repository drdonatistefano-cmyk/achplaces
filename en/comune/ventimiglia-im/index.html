What to see in Ventimiglia

Border town with France: the medieval centre with the Romanesque Cathedral of the Assumption, the Roman Albintiminium in Nervia, the prehistoric caves of the Balzi Rossi and the Hanbury alla Mortola botanical gardens.

11 places to visit in Ventimiglia, in the province of Imperia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint
