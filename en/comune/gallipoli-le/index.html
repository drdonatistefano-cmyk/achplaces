What to see in Gallipoli

Ionian City with the old town on a limestone island connected to the mainland by a masonry bridge and surrounded by the ancient walls: the Angevin Castle, the Baroque Cathedral of Sant'Agata, the Purity Church, the Greek Fountain and, outside the center, the beaches and the Natural Park of the Island of Sant'Andrea and Punta Pizzo.

Città vecchia, mura e riviera

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town occupies a limestone island connected to the mainland by a masonry bridge and preserves the original urban layout with Baroque churches and palaces. It is surrounded by a bastion fence of about 2 km, built after the Turkish sack of Otranto (1480), of which remains the lower part after the demolitions of 1879-1887; the panoramic road of the Riviera runs along the walls and allows the tour of the island.

  • Where Riviera, Gallipoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.gallipoli.le.it · italia.it

Castello angioino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Angevin fortress with circular bastions, almost entirely surrounded by the sea, the fulcrum of the twelve towers and bastions that surrounded the island; in 1269 34 rebel barons resisted against Charles I of Anjou for seven months.

  • Where Piazza Imbriani, Gallipoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.gallipoli.le.it

Cattedrale di Sant'Agata

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built between 1629 and 1696 at the highest point in the historic centre, it is the most representative Baroque monument in the city, a minor basilica since 1946. It is so rich in paintings that it is considered a picture gallery: paintings by Giovanni Andrea Coppola (Martyrdom of Sant'Agata, Adoration of the Magi, Assunta) and Nicola Malinconico, choir and wooden pulpit by Giorgio Aver.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Gallipoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.gallipoli.le.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria della Purità

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1664 as an oratory of the confraternity of the dockers, it has the interior entirely covered with eighteenth-century canvases, mostly of Liborio Riccio da Muro, and wooden stables finely carved; on the marble altar the Madonna between St. Joseph and St. Francis of Assisi of Luca Giordano (1664-65). Opened in 2009 after four years of restoration.

  • Where Riviera Cristoforo Colombo, Gallipoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.gallipoli.le.it · italia.it

Fontana greca

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

A sixteenth-century fountain near the bridge that connects the old and new cities, the result of several interventions: a counter-façade was added to the ancient façade, facing the south in the eighteenth century. It has three tanks supported each by three putti and three high reliefs with mythological scenes and Latin inscriptions.

  • Where Piazza Aldo Moro, Gallipoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.gallipoli.le.it · italia.it

Museo civico "Emanuele Barba"

Museums · City history

Founded in 1823 by Canon Carmine Fontò and then curated by Emanuele Barba, who created a zoological cabinet: weapons, costumes, fossils, works by the Gallipolin painter Giovanni Andrea Coppola, sarcophagi and messapic vases with inscriptions, portraits of illustrious Gallipolini and a numismatic collection. It also includes the Museum of the Sea and the Coppola Collection Hall; open from Tuesday to Sunday.

  • Where Via Antonietta de Pace 108, Gallipoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.gallipoli.le.it · italia.it

Museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters

Born from an initiative of Bishop Aldo Garzia in 1983, it occupies the former Seminary built in 1751 and restored between 1999 and 2004: about 900 m2 on three floors with fifteen exhibition rooms and multimedia stations.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Frantoio ipogeo di Palazzo Granafei

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Underground mill under Palazzo Granafei, a significant example of the eighteenth-century factories for the production of oil, with presses, a millstone of the seventeenth century and other tools for the processing of olives.

  • Where Via De Pace, Gallipoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Teatro Garibaldi

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built in 1825 by the noble Bonaventura Balsamo as "Teatro del Giglio" in honour of the Bourbons, it was bought by the Municipality in 1874, restored by engineer Oronzo Bernardini and named after Garibaldi in 1879: stages with golden stuccoes and damasks, carvings by Buccarella, Epifani and Nocera and decorations by Melchiorre Zalardi. Visitable with guided tours by reservation.

  • Where Via Garibaldi, Gallipoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.gallipoli.le.it · italia.it

Parco naturale regionale Isola di Sant'Andrea e litorale di Punta Pizzo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established in 2006, it includes the Island of Sant'Andrea, flat limestone island with the only nesting colony of seagull run on the Adriatic and Ionic coasts of Italy, and the coast of Punta Pizzo with Mediterranean maquis, gariga, orchids (including the rare Orchis italica) and wetlands near the channel Li Foggi, stop of migratory birds.

  • Where Litorale di Punta Pizzo, Gallipoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.gallipoli.le.it · italia.it

Spiagge di Gallipoli

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Long sandy beaches alternated with cliffs, with a sea of colors that change with light: Rivabella, Lido San Giovanni, Baia Verde, Punta della Suina and Lido Pizzo.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
