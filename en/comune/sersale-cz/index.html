Canyon delle Valli Cupe

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In the south-eastern sector of Sila, towards the Gulf of Squillace, the regional reserve has deep gorges and holes formed by the erosion of conglomerates and sandstones, which italia.it indicates as part of one of the largest canyons in Europe; the vegetation crosses the bands of Lauretum, Castanetum and Fagetum, and Monte Raga emerge pink granite banks. The trail of the river and the canyon is 1.3 km long (about 2 hours round trip, 190 m of altitude difference, tourist difficulty).

  • Where SS 109 della Piccola Sila, Sersale
  • Entry €3
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.riservanaturalevallicupe.it

Sources: riservanaturalevallicupe.it · italia.it · fondoambiente.it

Cascata Campanaro

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Paid entry · Viewpoint

One of the most famous and accessible waterfalls of the Valli Cupe, with a jump of 22 meters, reached by a path excavated naturally in the rock between red algae and ancient ferns; the route is 300 meters (about 1 hour round trip, tourist difficulty). The name, according to oral sources, would recall the bell tower of the nearby monastery of Santa Maria di Acquaviva.

  • Where Valli Cupe, Sersale
  • Entry €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page fondoambiente.it

Sources: italia.it · riservanaturalevallicupe.it · fondoambiente.it

Cascate Rupe, Salice e Inferno

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

In the reserve there are about one hundred waterfalls, up to 100 meters high: the Rupe, near rare prehistoric ferns, and the Salice are reached with a trail of 1.1 km (about 2 hours and a half, difficulty E); the Inferno Waterfall, whose name comes from the belief that the pool arrives to hell, is on a 600 meters route for experienced hikers, with 4 km of off-road access.

  • Where Valli Cupe, Sersale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page fondoambiente.it

Sources: riservanaturalevallicupe.it · italia.it · fondoambiente.it

Monoliti di Petra Aggìallu e Misorbo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Two routes of the reserve between canyons, gorges and monoliths: Petra Aggiallu (1.6 km, about 2 hours and a half, difficulty E, with the source of the Three Fountains) and Misorbo with the Campanaro stream (1.4 km, about 2 hours, difficulty E).

  • Where Valli Cupe, Sersale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page fondoambiente.it

Sources: riservanaturalevallicupe.it · fondoambiente.it

Giganti di Cavallopoli e Gigante Buono

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails

Secular chestnuts of the reserve: the Gigante Buono can be reached with the Salice-Greco route (800 meters, about 1 hour), the Giants of Cavallopoli along a path of 3.2 km towards the waterfall of the same name, tourist to the trees and then for expert hikers equipped.

  • Where Valli Cupe, Sersale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page fondoambiente.it

Sources: riservanaturalevallicupe.it · fondoambiente.it

Museo «Agorà dell'arte»

Museums · Modern art

Founded in 1987 by Bruno Caristo to enhance local and emerging artists, he collects painting, sculpture and photography by Calabrian authors, with modern and vintage reproductions in the photographic section; he has a photo library and a teaching room.

  • Where Salita Silvio Pellico, Sersale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fondoambiente.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · fondoambiente.it
