What to see in Alba

The capital of the Langhe, the city of the hundred towers and the white truffle, the heart of the UNESCO wine landscape: the cathedral, the medieval towers, San Domenico, the Maddalena di Vittone and Alba underground.

8 places to visit in Alba, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Alba in the guide with map and filters

Cattedrale di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

On a church of the 6th century with a baptismal dive font, the late-Gothic cathedral of Bishop Novelli rebuilt in neo-Gothic style in the mid-19th century: wooden choir of 1512 by Bernardino Fossati, chapel of St. Teobaldo with the marble ark; the bell tower rises to 40 meters.

  • Where Piazza Risorgimento, Alba
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it

Cripta di San Pietro e Alba sotterranea

History and architecture · Archaeology

The Diocesan Museum in the crypt with the lapidary from the Roman to the late Middle Ages, from which you reach the underground environments with the baptismal font of the sixth century; guided tours to the remains of Alba Pompeia.

  • Where Piazza Rossetti, Alba
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it

Torri medievali

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The towers of the city of the hundred towers are: the Sineo Tower (35 m) in front of the cathedral with the triphores on top, the Bonino Tower on via Maestra and the Asthesian Tower (30 m). They're private, they look up from the outside.

  • Where Piazza Risorgimento, Alba
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it

Chiesa di San Domenico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The only national monument of the city, founded in 1292 and completed in 1474 in Lombard Gothic, with frescoes of the fifteenth century.

  • Where Via Calissano, Alba
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it

Chiesa della Maddalena e Museo Eusebio

Museums · Archaeological

In the convent founded in 1441 by Blessed Margherita di Savoia, the baroque church with an elliptical plan by Bernardo Vittone and the Archaeological Civic Museum and Natural Sciences . Federico Eusebio is held in 21 rooms; in the courtyard there is the White Truffle Fair.

  • Where Via Vittorio Emanuele, Alba
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The oldest after the cathedral, with Baroque façade and works by Macrino d'Alba and Gandolfino da Roreto.

  • Where Via Cavour, Alba
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it

Via Maestra e Casa-forte Marro

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The main street with medieval and Renaissance buildings, tower houses and terracotta loggias; the Marro House-fortress has a glazed walkway on the foundations of a Roman temple. In the small square of the Singer, he opened the first Ferrero pastry shop.

  • Where Via Vittorio Emanuele, Alba
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it

Centro Studi Beppe Fenoglio

Museums · House museums

In the writer's house, with the documentation on the history of Alba and the "Anticamera della morte" by Pinot Gallizio.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
