What to see in Monte Porzio Catone

Borgo dei Castelli Romani gathered around the seventeenth-century Duomo of the Borghese: the Tuscan villas, with Villa Mondragone where in 1582 the reform of the calendar was promulgated, the astronomical observatory with the laboratory Astrolab, the Borghese Barco with its Roman inscriptions and the wine museum in the old station.

9 places to visit in Monte Porzio Catone, in the province of Rome (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa Mondragone

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

The largest of the Tuscan villas, about 80,000 square meters, built since 1567 for Cardinal Marco Sittico Altemps: here in 1582 Pope Gregory XIII promulgated the bull "Inter Gravissimas" that introduced the Gregorian calendar. Italian Garden, the Dragon Fountain on the Terrace and tree-lined avenues; since 1981 it has been University of Rome Tor Vergata and is a congress centre.

Parco archeologico del Barco Borghese

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

In the ancient property Altemps and then Borghese linked to Villa Mondragone, a large Roman complex of the imperial State: an underground guided itinerary leads to a rare set of Roman inscriptions drawn with brush, charcoal and graffito on the plaster; the front with semi-colons doriche is fifth to the shows and the esplanade overlooks Rome. Guided tours by reservation on weekends.

  • Where Via Frascati, Monte Porzio Catone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Osservatorio astronomico e Astrolab

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

The Observatory, designed in 1939 and completed in 1965 on the remains of the Roman villa of Matidia, relative of Hadrian, hosts Astrolab, an interactive laboratory of astrophysics from the Solar System at the first moments of the universe, with observation of the Sun on request. Visits for schools and groups of at least 20 people by reservation. It was set from the film about boys from Panisperna Street.

  • Where Via Frascati, Monte Porzio Catone
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: diva.oa-roma.inaf.it · cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Duomo di San Gregorio Magno

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish Church designed by Carlo Rainaldi and built in 1666 at the behest of a Prince Borghese, heart of the historic center together with the seventeenth-century Palazzo Borghese.

Museo della Città

Museums · City history

In four restored rooms of the seventeenth-century nucleus of the Duomo, finds from Tusculum and Tuscolane villas (an iron age anforet, a polychrome mosaic, frescoes, statues and coins), a medieval papal bull, majolica, seventeenth-century paintings and original documents.

Villa Parisi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Built in 1604-1605 as the summer residence of Cardinal Ferdinando Taverna, then of Scipione Borghese, with the nymphaeum and the secret garden of Giovanni Vasanzio and a hanging garden; lived there for a long time Paolina Bonaparte. The Parisi family since 1896, is in the network of the historic residences of Lazio.

Eremo Tuscolano di Camaldoli

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Founded in 1607 by the Camaldolese Hermits of Monte Corona and completed in 1613 with the church dedicated to San Romualdo; there is still a strict rule that does not allow women to enter.
