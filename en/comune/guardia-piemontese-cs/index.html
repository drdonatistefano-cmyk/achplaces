What to see in Guardia Piemontese

Borgo at 514 meters on the Cosentine Tyrrhenian founded by Waldenses from the Piedmont valleys, the only center of Calabria where the Occitan language survives: the Blood Gate recalls the massacre of the Waldenses of 1561, the Museum of peasant civilization keeps Occitan clothes and on the coast there are partly the Luigiane Thermal Baths.

Porta del Sangue

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Ancient entrance door of the village, dating back to about 1500, which owes its name to the bloody massacre of the Waldenses of June 1561. The Waldensian community, which arrived from the valleys of Piedmont between the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries, was repressed by the Inquisition after joining the Reformation, with severe penalties for the survivors and the prohibition of speaking the Occitan.

  • Where Via Gian Luigi Pascale 34, Guardia Piemontese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page calabriastraordinaria.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · icpi.cultura.gov.it · calabriastraordinaria.it

Borgo occitano di Guardia Piemontese

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village, about 514 meters away, was surrounded by walls for defence, incorporating a more ancient watchtower, one of the many fates on the Tyrrhenian coast between the XI and XII century against pirates and Saracens. It is the only one of the Waldensian settlements of Cosentino that has preserved the ancient Occitan language and, up to recent times, the traditional costume; in the center are held rites like the Bèlla Stela of Epiphany, torched from the convent square to the parish church.

  • Where Centro storico, Guardia Piemontese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page calabriastraordinaria.it

Sources: comune.guardiapiemontese.cs.it · calabriastraordinaria.it

Museo della civiltà contadina di Guardia Piemontese

Museums · Themed

Opened in 1989 in one of the oldest houses in the village to recover the memory of the peasant and occitane traditions, it reconstructs a typical house with bedroom, kitchen, exhibition room with tools and Occitan clothes, attic with the ancient oven, stable and cellar; there is also a photographic section. Telephone 0982 94663.

MiC card (updated in 2020) indicates "Closed" every day of the week; call before the visit

Terme Luigiane

Local life and traditions · Spas

The Luigiane Thermal Baths, dedicated to the care of health and well-being, are located in part in the territory of Guardia Piemontese, on the Riviera dei Cedri.
