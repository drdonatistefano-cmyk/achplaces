What to see in Avigliano Umbro

Small village on the ancient Via Amerina, between Todi and Amelia, among the youngest municipalities in Italy but with protohistoric and Roman settlements; in the hamlet of Dunarobba the Fossil Forest preserves trunks of coniferous giants of the Pliocene, and in the territory rise the castles of Dunarobba, Sismano, Toscolano and Santa Restituta.

Foresta fossile di Dunarobba e Centro di paleontologia vegetale

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Giant evergreen coniferous logs, similar to the present sequoias, grown in the Pliocene on the shore of the ancient Lake Tiberino and buried from the debris of the floods up to the roots; some reach about 1.5 m of diameter and have been preserved thanks to a process of chemical fossilization. Umbriatourism indicates it among the most important paleontological sites in the world, discovered by chance in the seventies during the extraction of clay. The visit starts from the Centre of Plant Paleontology, east of the country, and is suitable for children.

  • Where Voc. Pennicchia 46, Dunarobba, Avigliano Umbro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.forestafossile.it

Centro storico e Teatro comunale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

You enter from the Old Gate with the coat of arms of the Municipality of Todi (a marble eagle, memory of the medieval dispute between Todi and Amelia); traces of the medieval fabric on the walls remain and a large cylindrical tower from which the public clock leans. The small town theatre in Liberty style, of the early twentieth century, is well preserved inside.

  • Where Avigliano Umbro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Chiesa della Santissima Trinità

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish of the seventeenth century was built within the castle of the Vici family. It preserves a Madonna del Rosario by Andrea Polinori, painter of Todi, and frescoes attributed to Bartolomeo Barbiani from Montepulciano.

  • Where Avigliano Umbro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Rocca di Dunarobba

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Renaissance fortress with quadrangular plan, with large cylindrical towers on the four sides, in the hamlet of Dunarobba near the Fossil Forest.

Toscolano, castello e Maestà affrescata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Toscolano, next to the castle, the church of the Santissima Annunziata with a single nave has on the base wall a newsstand (the Majesty of Toscolano) with frescoes attributed to Pier Matteo d'Amelia.
