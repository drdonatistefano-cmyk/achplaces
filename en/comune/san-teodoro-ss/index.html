What to see in San Teodoro

San Teodoro, in the south-eastern Gallura, is known for its white sandy beaches, from the Cinta about 5 km long to Cala Brandinchi and Lu Impostu, overlooking the marine protected area Tavolara-Punta Coda Cavallo. Behind it are the lagoon frequented by pink flamingos and the granite massif of Mount Nieddu.

La Cinta

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Arch of about 5 km of white and thin sand dunes that degrade towards a low and clear sea, without rocks, between juniper, marine lily and helicry; in front there are Punta Coda Cavallo and the island of Tavolara. The belt of dunes separates the sea from the lagoon of San Teodoro. There are parking lots, information points, rentals, sailing school and kitesurfing with a kite zone; you can walk to the northern outskirts of the country.

Laguna di San Teodoro

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lagoon behind the Cinta, from which the cord of dunes separates it. There live folks, friars, red herons and royal Germans, and during migrations there are pink flamingos: it is a place suitable for birdwatching.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Cala Brandinchi

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches

Beach nicknamed "Tahiti," one of the most popular in Sardinia, in the locality of Capo Coda Cavallo almost in front of the island of Tavolara. White sand and blue-turquoise water, with rocks that attract marine fauna and make it suitable for snorkeling; it is surrounded by a large pine forest and small dunes. According to the source, Garibaldi left here on October 17, 1867 to free Rome. It is reached from the SS125 turning towards Capo Coda Cavallo and continuing on a dirt road.

Lu Impostu

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches

Beach about one kilometer long on the promontory of Capo Coda Cavallo, near Cala Brandinchi, with white and fine sand, dunes with juniper, mimosa and marine lilies and a low bottom that gently descends, suitable for children. It is part of the marine protected area of Tavolara and Capo Coda Cavallo; behind is the second lagoon of San Teodoro, connected to the sea by a canal. It has access for people with disabilities, ample parking also for campers, refreshment points and rentals.

Capo Coda Cavallo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Promontory of granite with clear beaches and transparent waters, sheltered from the islands of Tavolara and Molara and the islet of Proratora, so that the sea remains calm with every wind; the vegetation is Mediterranean maquis. It is part of the marine protected area Tavolara-Punta Coda Cavallo, about 15,000 hectares from Capo Ceraso to Punta s'Isuledda, with diving sites such as the Pope's Secca and Molarotto. Around there are Baia Salinedda, Cala Suaraccia (Beach of the Butterflies) and Cala Ghjlgolu. Free admission from the SS125.

  • Where Capo Coda Cavallo, San Teodoro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Puntaldìa

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches

It is located at the northern end of the Cinta, separated by San Teodoro from the mouth of the lagoon, overlooking Tavolara, with fine sand, shallow and transparent waters and rocks smoothed by the wind. It is sheltered by tramontana and mistral and is suitable for children. The Marina di Puntaldìa has a marina and a square with restaurants, bars and shops; in the area there are golf courses.

Monte Nieddu e rio Pitrisconi

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Granite massif between Padru and San Teodoro, at the southern end of Gallura; from the peaks of Punta Majore and Punta Coloredda you can see the Gennargentu, the beaches of San Teodoro and Tavolara. In the woods of holm oaks, junipers and oaks flows all year round the Pitrisconi river, with waterfalls, natural pools (such as that of Pitriolu), the waterfall of Scala Taddata and a gorge in granite; a ring itinerary follows the course and there are picnic areas. There live mouflons and nest the royal eagle.

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The historic centre preserves churches and granite houses. The church of the patron Saint Theodorus was rebuilt in the middle of the twentieth century.

  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo delle civiltà del mare

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

It exhibits finds from the backdrops of the coast of San Teodoro and donations, from Punic age to modern age: a punic treasure trove, a Greek-Italian amphora linked to the Punic Olbia, Roman amphorae, ceramics and glass from a wreck from the III-IV century AD in Baia Salinedda, a fragment of Roman helmet and a Byzantine tremisse of gold found in the village. It also tells Coclearia, station and port on the Olbia-Cagliari road mentioned in the Itinerary Antonino. It has a naturalistic section with a collection of seashells from all over the world. Prices for the period before closure.

temporarily closed for renovation work, date of reopening not known (Sardinia Cultura)
