What to see in Trecastagni

Ethnian country linked to the cult of the holy martyrs Alfio, Filadelfo and Cirino, whose seventeenth-century sanctuary rises where tradition wants the three brothers to pause in 252 and holds almost a thousand tablets ex-vote painted between 1870 and 1970; in the historical center the Mother Church of St. Nicholas and the church of Mercy.

5 places to visit in Trecastagni, in the province of Catania (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario dei Santi Martiri Alfio, Filadelfo e Cirino

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

It dates back to the middle of the seventeenth century: the central aisle was built on the ruins of an ancient newsstand erected by the early Christians in the place where in 252 the three martyr brothers stayed. Impressive architecture with a majestic access staircase; inside relics and religious symbols.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Alfio 1, Trecastagni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.trecastagni.ct.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · comune.trecastagni.ct.it

Collezione di ex voto del Santuario

Museums · Themed

In the shrine are preserved little less than a thousand tablets ex-vote painted in about a century, between 1870 and 1970: accidents, especially road accidents, with means that pass from animal towing to cars and trucks, diseases such as cholera and Spanish and surgical scenes from the bed of the house to the operating room. The Sicilian Region has studied them in the volume "Miracles to the front"

  • Where Piazza Sant'Alfio 1, Trecastagni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.trecastagni.ct.it

Sources: www2.regione.sicilia.it · comune.trecastagni.ct.it

Chiesa Madre di San Nicola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Dedicated to St. Nicholas, patron saint of the city, it dominates the historic centre; inside frescoes, sculptures and works of sacred art, and hosts religious ceremonies and cultural events.

  • Where Piazzale Matrice, Trecastagni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.trecastagni.ct.it

Sources: comune.trecastagni.ct.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria della Misericordia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Sacred building of the historic centre of elegant architecture, with sacred art works and liturgical decorations inside; it has architectural barriers.

  • Where Largo dei Bianchi 10, Trecastagni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.trecastagni.ct.it

Sources: comune.trecastagni.ct.it

Piazza Guglielmo Marconi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

One of the main squares, between shops and bars, where stands and markets are often held; in the center a fountain with three balls that recall the three patrons Alfio, Cirino and Filadelfo.

  • Where Piazza Guglielmo Marconi, Trecastagni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.trecastagni.ct.it

Sources: comune.trecastagni.ct.it
