What to see in Castel Goffredo

Cittadina dell'Alto Mantovano from the Renaissance heart, capital of a Gonzaghesque fief since 1511, with the texture documented from the thirteenth century and today textile district; on Piazza Mazzini overlook the prepositural of Sant'Erasmo, the Gonzaga-Acerbi Palace, the Municipal Palace and the Tower of Castelvecchio, a stone's throw from the MAST city museum.

MAST - Museo della città

Museums · City history

In the historic center near Piazza Mazzini, in a Renaissance building, tells the city from prehistory to today following two threads, water and weaving, documented as early as the 13th century and became in the 20th century textile district. The first open section goes from the Lombard age to the end of the sixteenth century and brings together marble, silver, codes, wooden sculptures and painted fabrics. Open from Wednesday to Sunday and holidays, closed in most of August; fully accessible, with elevator.

Prepositurale di Sant'Erasmo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in the second half of the sixteenth century at the behest of the Marquis Alfonso Gonzaga, with the contribution of the congregations and the Magnificent Community, is attributed to Bernardino Facciotto or his son Girolamo. Latin cross plan with three naves on monolithic columns and ample dome with lantern. It preserves the fifteenth-century Crucifix Miraculous in wood, altars in polychrome marble and salesmen of the Florentine workshop of Corbarelli, paintings of Brusasorzi and of the Mantua school of the sixteenth century, Baroque paintings of Giuseppe Fali and Giuseppe Bazzani, the eighteenth-century choir of Ceratelli and the self-supporting pulpit of Rizzardo Carboni.

  • Where Piazza Mazzini / Via Andrea Botturi 1, Castel Goffredo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.castelgoffredo.mn.it

Torre di Castelvecchio - Museo Corrado Bocchi

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Born in the 13th century as the gate of Castelvecchio, the first nucleus of the city, it became a tower in 1438 under the Gonzaga with the first clock and was then raised to the present 27 meters: it is the tallest building of Castel Goffredo. Seven floors and 117 steps, with a museum path on walls, doors, moat and vellum of the two defensive belts; a spiral staircase crosses the bell cell to the Belvedere, overlooking the Po Valley, the Prealps and, on clear days, the Apennines. Open from Thursday to Sunday, last ascent at 16:45; hosts the Infopoint.

Palazzo Gonzaga-Acerbi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

It occupies the entire north side of Piazza Mazzini, between the Civic Tower and the Torrazzo, and was the residence of all the lords of Castel Goffredo. Born from two buildings of the first fortified nucleus, it was transformed into a residence from 1480 by Bishop Ludovico Gonzaga with the architect Ermes Flavio de Bonis; from 1511 the Marquis Aloisio Gonzaga made it the seat of the feud that included Castiglione and Solferino. In 1776 it was sold to Colonel Giacomo Acerbi; the eighteenth-century façade is by Gaspare Turbini, the entrance loggia of the sixteenth-century has entirely frescoed vaults. There were born the writer Giuseppe Acerbi and the Garibaldi patriot Giovanni Acerbi. It is private: porch and some rooms are open for events.

  • Where Piazza Mazzini 20, Castel Goffredo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Palazzo municipale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in the second half of the fifteenth century on the west side of Piazza Mazzini, in front of Sant'Erasmo; the façade has a loggia with a coffered ceiling and on the first floor four windows and the balcony. The council room, with painted ceiling, in the nineteenth century was used as a theatre. It houses the Municipal Art Collection with works from the twentieth century linked to Castel Goffredo and, in the stairwell, a sculpture by Ferruccio Bolognesi.

  • Where Piazza Mazzini 1, Castel Goffredo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Parco giardino Tenuta di Sant'Apollonio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry

Over 70,000 m2 owned by the Fondazione Senza Frontiere, born in 1973 from the plantations of Anselmo Castelli and designed by Gianfranco Paghera imitating nature: forest of native species of the Po Valley, wetland with fauna, orchard of ancient varieties, rose garden and medicinal garden, with educational trail. The Ethnographic Museum documents the Kanaka del Sepik (Papua New Guinea) and the Krahô del Tocantins (Amazon Brazilian) with masks, musical instruments and tools. Open from April to October, visits by reservation.

  • Where Strada S. Apollonio 6, Castel Goffredo
  • Entry For a fee, price to check
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.parcosantapollonio.com
