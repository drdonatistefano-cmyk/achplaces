What to see in Somma Vesuviana

Cities at the foot of Monte Somma, with the medieval village of Casamale closed by the Aragonese walls, the castle of Alagno of 1458, the complex of Santa Maria del Pozzo with the museum of peasant civilization and the excavations of the Roman villa called "Augustea" .

Casamale, borgo medievale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The oldest neighborhood, still surrounded by the Aragonese walls with four gates (of the Earth, Piccioli, of the Castle, Formosi), is a network of narrow alleys and lava stone buildings. There are the Durazzesco-Catalan portal of Palazzo Secondulfo (XV century), Palazzo Sirico (XV-XVI), Palazzo Orsini and Colletta, the church of the Alcantarines or of the Child Jesus (XVII), the church of San Domenico and the Insigne Collegiata, from the early seventeenth century.

Castello d'Alagno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortified house built in 1458 by Lucrezia d'Alagno, beloved of King Alfonso I of Aragon, who withdrew there after the death of the king; rises outside the Aragonese walls, just behind the Casamale, in front of Mount Somma. He replaced a more ancient castle near Santa Maria a Castello; among the subsequent owners was Prince De Curtis (Totò), and in 1998 he bought it to make it a museum and library.

Complesso di Santa Maria del Pozzo

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry

A 15th-century church that, with the monastery and the crypt, forms a complex of historical, artistic and architectural interest, declared a national good of archaeological interest; it rises in the homonymous hamlet, lower than Casamale.

Museo della civiltà contadina «Michele Russo»

Museums · Themed

Ethno-anthropological museum in the garden and cellar of the former convent of Santa Maria del Pozzo, run by an association and recognized museum of regional interest: over 3,000 objects of rural life before mechanization and about 3,000 photographs, in 20 sectors (transport with soma animals, wine room, crafts, peasant food, etnomusicology), plus a nativity scene with figures in eighteenth-century clothes, stables with animals and educational garden. It is educational farm with laboratories (bread, cheese, beekeeping).

MiC card (updated 2020) indicates closed every day: check with the museum (tel. 081 5318496)

Villa romana detta «Augustea»

History and architecture · Archaeology

Discovered in the 1930s and excavated since 2002 by the mission of the University of Tokyo, the villa was buried by the eruption of 472 A.D. (Pollena's) The believed statue of Augustus is a Dionysus with a puppy of panther; monolithic columns in marble of Asia Minor, white mosaics, apses with tritons and nereids and a wine cell with over thirty dolias. On site there are copies: the originals are at the Archaeological Museum of Nola.

Sentiero n. 3 Monte Somma

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

A 7,65 km ring (about 5 and a half hours, high difficulty) from the church of Santa Maria a Castello a Punta Nasone (1,132 m), the highest peak of Somma, above the Valley of the Giant: the first stretch on the Via Traversa is equipped for the blind and disabled, then woods of robinia and chestnut, the cognoli of Trocchia and Sant'Anastasia with views of the gulf and the Gran Cono, and on top a chapel and a large cross linked to the Feast of the Mountain.
