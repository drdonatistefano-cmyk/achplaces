What to see in Ampezzo

Borgo della Carnia at 560 meters in the valley of Tagliamento, already mentioned in 762 as \vicus Ampicio and in 1944 capital of the Free Zone of Carnia; Palazzo Unfer brings together the exhibition on the Resistance, the Geological Museum of Carnia and the Pinacoteca Marco Davanzo, in front of the cathedral of San Daniele and the seventeenth-century Palazzo Beorchia-Nigris.

Museo geologico della Carnia

Museums · Science and university

Fossils and rocks of Carnia recount over 400 million years of geological evolution of the Carnic Alps, from paleozoic fossils of when the area was covered by the sea up to changes prior to the alpine orogenesis. Panels, interactive teaching tools and laboratories on fossilization, classification and recognition of rocks, with specialized library; it is part of the CarniaMusei network. Times to ask CarniaMusei.

  • Where Palazzo Unfer, Piazza Zona Libera della Carnia 1944, Ampezzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.carniamusei.org

Sources: carniamusei.org · turismofvg.it · italia.it

Pinacoteca Marco Davanzo

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

On the second floor of Palazzo Unfer, it collects the works of the Ampezzo landscaper Marco Davanzo (1872-1955): landscapes in different seasons, high mountain atmospheres and scenes of rural life. The paintings belong to the Ministry of Culture and are in storage at the Municipality; it is part of the CarniaMusei network. Times to ask CarniaMusei.

  • Where Palazzo Unfer, Piazza Zona Libera della Carnia 5, Ampezzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.carniamusei.org

Sources: carniamusei.org · turismofvg.it · italia.it

Palazzo Unfer e mostra sulla Resistenza

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

The main cultural centre of the country, in 1944 was the seat of the government of the Free Zone of Carnia and Upper Friuli, the territory that was liberated for a short time from the Nazi-fascist occupation; today it houses a photographic-documentary exhibition on the Resistance.

  • Where Piazza Zona Libera della Carnia, Ampezzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismofvg.it

Duomo di San Daniele Profeta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Also known as the parish church of San Daniele and dedicated to the Blessed Virgin of the Rosary and to San Daniele Prophet, it is an eighteenth-century church that combines baroque and neoclassical elements; it preserves paintings by Nicola Grassi (1725) and a Baroque altar by Giovanni Saidero da Venzone (1641).

  • Where Piazza Zona Libera della Carnia, Ampezzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismofvg.it

Centro storico e Palazzo Beorchia-Nigris

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center is known for the arched houses and the typical Carnic houses of the patriarchal period; in front of Palazzo Unfer stands Palazzo Beorchia-Nigris, called Casa Grande, noble residence of the seventeenth century today seat of the town hall.

  • Where Ampezzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismofvg.it

Monte Tinisa e Foresta di Ampezzo al Passo Pura

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Monte Tinisa, 2120 metres, is the highest peak in the municipality; from Passo Pura you reach the dense high altitude Forest of Ampezzo, crossed by hiking trails.

  • Where Passo Pura, Ampezzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it
