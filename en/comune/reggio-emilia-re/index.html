What to see in Reggio Emilia

The city of the Tricolor, born here on January 7, 1797. All the locations of the Civic Museums are free of charge, including Palazzo dei Musei, Museo del Tricolore and Galleria Parmeggiani.

25 places to visit in Reggio Emilia, in the province of Reggio Emilia (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo dei Musei

Museums · City history · Free entry

A trip from the Paleolithic to the nineteenth century: the Atrium of the mosaics, the museum of Lazzaro Spallanzani, the Palethnology of Gaetano Chierici and the naturalistic collections.

  • Where Via Spallanzani 1, Reggio Emilia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: musei.re.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo del Tricolore e Sala del Tricolore

Museums · City history · Free entry

The museum dedicated to the Republic of Cispadana and the Italian flag, born on January 7, 1797 in the hall of the Town Hall today used for councils and weddings.

  • Where Piazza Casotti 1, Reggio Emilia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website emiliaromagnaturismo.it
