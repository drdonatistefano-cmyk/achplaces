Museo della cultura contadina Alta Val Trebbia

Museums · Themed

Opened in 1990 in the former convent of the Augustinians next to the parish of Santa Maria Assunta. The ethnographic section collects tools of arts and crafts, agricultural machinery, chariots and calexs, an ancient still working mill and rebuilt peasant environments (stalla, cucina, camera); the section of sacred art exhibits objects of worship, banners and pictures, while the cloister houses photographic documentation on the churches of the valley. Tel. 010 95033.

Santuario di Nostra Signora di Montebruno, chiostro e refettorio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Built in 1486 by the Augustinians after the apparition of the Virgin (1476, according to tradition) to a silent shepherd who regained his voice, preserves late-fourth-century ogival arches, baroque enlargements and a neoclassical façade of 1897. On the high altar the wooden statue of Our Lady, covered for centuries by a plate of copper painted sliding; inside a wooden Crucifix of about 1600, a canvas of Augustine Ratti of 1750, a confessional of the seventeenth century and the fresco of the miracle of G. Quinzio. In the former convent, the restored cloister and the refectory with the Last sixteenth-century dinner frescoed in front of St. Augustine.

Ponte sul Trebbia e borgo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The monumental four-arched stone bridge, erected by the Dorias, lords of the village from 1547 to the eighteenth century, joins the village on the left bank to the religious complex on the right. The village, in 1164 a fief of Malaspina for Barbarossa's concession, had at the end of the seventeenth century a mint with the effigy of Violante Doria; every year a balloon festival recalls Sophie Blanchard, landed here in 1811 after a balloon accident.
