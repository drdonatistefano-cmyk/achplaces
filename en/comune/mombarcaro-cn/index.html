What to see in Mombarcaro

The "Monte delle Boche" (mountain of boats), almost 900 metres away, is the "Vetta delle Langhe" (vegetable of the Langhe): almost vertical village of stairs and sternìe, of which there remains an arch of the ancient walls of the fourteenth century, with views of the Langhe and the Ligurian Sea. Outside the center the chapel of San Rocco with the 'Cavalcata dei Vizi' and, in the hamlet Lunetta, the medieval bridge near the Reserve Belbo.

Centro storico e Arco medievale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village stands on a hill overlooking the Langhe: once surrounded by high walls, of which only one arch of the fourteenth century remains, it is a almost vertical village of stairs and sternìe ( steep streets) paved in red bricks, remained closed until the sixties between two arches with acute sixth. From the panoramic points you can see the Langhe and, on clear days, the Ligurian Sea. It was quoted in 998 in a document by Otto III; the Municipal Statutes are dated April 3, 1301.

Cappella di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

An ancient church porched on the road to Niella Belbo, once a stop for travellers and lazzaretto during the epidemics. The cycle of frescoes shows the rare "Cavalcata dei Vizi" (Ride of the Vizi), with the seven deadly sins in vintage figures on horseback of beasts, chained and headed to hell; on the back wall of San Rocco and San Sebastiano, on the right the temptations of Sant'Antonio Abate. Lunetta e Riserva del Belbo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Lost Village of Lunetta is a small stone village with a medieval bridge, an ancient mill and the church of San Giovanni, near the Belbo Reserve: the springs of the stream are natural reserve, walkable, on horseback, by bike and in winter with cross-country skiing.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
