What to see in Lesina

Country on the south-western shore of the Lesina lagoon, with the Visitor Centre of the Gargano National Park that brings together naturalistic museum, aquarium and ethnographic museum, and the Bosco Isola that separates the lagoon from the sea.

4 places to visit in Lesina, in the province of Foggia (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Laguna di Lesina

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Bracket lagoon 22 km long and at most 3 km wide, a little more than 2 meters deep, which with that of Varano forms the largest brackish complex in southern Italy. In the eastern part the State Nature Reserve houses reeds with red herons and tarabuses and mud where they nest the knights of Italy; they do birdwatching and exits in catamaran or on the sandals, the boats of Lesine wood.

  • Where Lungolago, Lesina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcogargano.it

Centro Visite e Museo naturalistico con acquario

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

Born in 1995 as an environmental documentation center and inaugurated in 2002 as a Visitor Center of the Park, it brings together an aquarium of 14 tanks (about 21,000 liters) connected to the lagoon, a diorama of the lagoon flora and fauna, the botanical garden Â Via delle Erbe Â Â Â Â and two sheds for birdwatching on the terrace. Park hours: 9:30-12:30 and 16-19, in summer 9-12:30 and 17-20.

Museo etnografico «Casa del Pescatore»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Set up on the lakefront in the building of the Visitor Centre, it reconstructs a fishing house of the early twentieth century with objects donated by the Lesinesi and an ancient sandal, telling the lagoon culture of the Capitana, between land and water.

MiC card (updated 2021) indicates closed every day, the Park reports opening hours

  • Where Via Banchina Vollaro, Lesina
  • Entry €2, residents €1
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · parcogargano.it · italia.it

Bosco Isola e Oasi della Duna di Lesina

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry

A sandy strip between the lagoon and the sea with reeds, Mediterranean maquis, lagoon basins and jockeys of the Lipu Oasis, which can also be crossed by mountain bike; from the Visitor Centre there are guided tours of about an hour on the educational path on the catwalk (€2) and catamaran excursions to the Acquarotta Canal (€5).

  • Where Bosco Isola, Lesina
  • Entry €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcogargano.it
