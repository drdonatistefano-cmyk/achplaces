What to see in Volterra

The Etruscan Velathri, on top of a hill with a view for almost 40 km, alabaster town with Etruscan, Roman and medieval monuments.

7 places to visit in Volterra, in the province of Pisa (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.
