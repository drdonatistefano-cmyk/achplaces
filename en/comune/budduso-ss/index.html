What to see in Buddusò

Country of granite on the plateau of Mount Acuto, about 750 m between pastures and corks, with about 60 domus de janas and 32 nuraghi: the necropolis of Ludurru at the gates of the village, the nuraghe Loelle along the road to Bitti and the sculptures of the Wood Symposium and the Granite scattered along the streets.

Museo d'arte contemporanea e sculture del Simposio

Museums · Modern art

The museum preserves the statues made during the editions of the Symposium of Wood and Granite between 1980 and 2000, when sculptors from all over the world carved granite and wood in the streets and squares of the village; other statues are exhibited throughout the town.

  • Where Centro abitato, Buddusò
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Necropoli di Ludurru

History and architecture · Archaeology

Six domus de janas of the final Neolithic (about 3200-2800 BC) excavated in a single granite outcrop between philirea and cork oaks. The tomb I, the most articulated, has anticell, central chamber and two secondary environments; the IV keeps a counter and traces of two semi-colons. Outside a false door carved on a rock and on top a block that was probably an altar.

  • Where A circa 500 m dalla SS 389 verso Alà dei Sardi, Buddusò
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Nuraghe Loelle

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Nuraghe of mixed type, to corridor and tholos, probably a protonuraghe transformed in successive phases: the central tower leans on a trilobed bastion and a staircase leads directly to the second level, from which you go up to the main hall to the third. Around the remains of a village of circular huts and, about 100 meters, a tomb of the giants and another tomb with a small dolmen.

  • Where SS 389 Buddusò-Bitti, a circa 10 km dal paese, Buddusò
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Altopiano di Buddusò

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Highland about 750 m covered with pastures, cork woods and Mediterranean maquis, regularly home to special tests of Rally Italia Sardinia; there are born the river Mannu, which goes down to the Coghinas lake, and the river Altana, which flows into the Tyrrhenian Sea.

  • Where Altopiano del Monte Acuto, Buddusò
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
