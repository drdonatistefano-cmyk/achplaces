What to see in Isolabona

Borgo della Val Nervia perched on a hill and surrounded by walls, built on a small island of the river Nervia: dominion of the Doria from the early thirteenth century, preserves the castle of the Doria, the octagonal fountain of 1486 and the parish church of Santa Maria Maddalena; the harp is the symbol of the village.

Castello dei Doria

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the hill north of the village, above the road to Apricale, it has hexagonal plant and square mast in local sandstone. It was mentioned in the sources since 1287, linked to the arrival of the Doria at the end of the thirteenth century, and was born as a fortress (1330) and then took residential characters; around 1520 it was briefly held by the Grimaldi of Monaco. Damaged by the earthquake of 1887, it was restored and consolidated in 1989 and in summer it hosts evenings of theatre and music and the Festival of harps.

MiC card indicates closed every day; open at summer events (Common 0184 208127)

Centro storico, fontana ottagonale e porta degli angeli

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village, perched and closed by walls, has old stone houses with small windows built directly on the banks of the stream. After passing a bridge you pass under a door adorned by two bronze angels, one of which plays the harp; in Piazza Piccola there is the octagonal fountain of 1486 and, little further, the statue of King David, master of the harp, symbol of the country taken in various reliefs.

  • Entry Free

Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Maria Maddalena e oratorio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Overlooking Piazza Grande, the parish church of Santa Maria Maddalena and the oratory are baroque buildings.
