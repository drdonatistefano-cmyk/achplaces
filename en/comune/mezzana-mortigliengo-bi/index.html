What to see in Mezzana Mortigliengo

A small town in eastern Biellese with the village Bonda transformed from the eighties into an open-air gallery of contemporary frescoes and sculptures, and the Museum of the workshop of the Mortigliengo in Mino, dedicated to the traditional crafts of the area.

2 places to visit in Mezzana Mortigliengo, in the province of Biella (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Bondarte, galleria d'arte moderna all'aperto

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

Outdoor gallery of frescoes and contemporary sculptures that decorate the houses of the village Bonda, started in the eighties by the Vercellian painter Gastone Cecconello (1942). It is visible outdoors every day without reservation; on the upper pedestrian street of the village is the Association Amici della Bonda.

  • Where Frazione Bonda, Mezzana Mortigliengo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Museo laboratorio del Mortigliengo

Museums · Themed

Ethnographic Museum of Mortigliengo with demonstration laboratories of traditional activities: production of walnut oil and apple vinegar, cultivation and drying of chestnuts, hemp processing.

the MiC card (Add. 2021) indicates closed every day: check opening with the Municipality
