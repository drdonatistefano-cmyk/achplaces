What to see in Seggiano

Medieval village in the hills on the slopes of Mount Amiata, closed by the walls, land of oil PDO: the church of San Bartolomeo with the Sienese polyptych, the Oratory of San Rocco with the frescoes of Girolamo di Domenico, the sanctuary of Our Lady of Charity, the Museum of Oil with the olive tree in the Cisternone and, two kilometers away, the Garden of Daniel Spoerri.

7 places to visit in Seggiano, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Giardino di Daniel Spoerri

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Open-air art park of 16 hectares on the slopes of the Amiata, in the place called "the Paradise" for the mild climate: the Swiss artist Daniel Spoerri bought the estate in 1991 and exhibited his bronzes from the 1980s; monograph until 1996, open to the public in 1997, today has 113 installations of 55 international artists among trees, meadows and paths, curated by the Hic Terminus Haeret Foundation. The route takes about three hours; restaurant and accommodation on site. Seasonal timetables, to be checked on the site.

  • Where Strada Provinciale Pescina, Seggiano
  • Entry €12, students €9, free under 8 years
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.danielspoerri.org

Sources: danielspoerri.org · italia.it · comune.seggiano.gr.it · cultura.gov.it

Borgo di Seggiano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small village in the hills that has preserved the medieval character, with the walls that enclose the monuments; next to the parish stands the neo-Gothic town hall. From the road that exits the village opens the view of the village and the massif of the Amiata.

  • Where Centro storico, Seggiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com

Chiesa di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Pieve of the 13th century, rebuilt in the 1930s; preserves a polyptych of the Sienese school of the fourteenth century with the Madonna and Child enthroned among the Saints Bartholomew, Michael and John the Baptist.

  • Where Centro storico, Seggiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com

Oratorio di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1486 near the town, it is decorated with frescoes by the painter Girolamo di Domenico.

  • Entry To be checked

Chiesa del Corpus Domini

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Dedicated to the Body of Christ and to San Bernardino da Siena, it houses a Madonna and Child of the late fourteenth century and a reliquary belonging to the same Saint Bernardino.

  • Where Seggiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Santuario della Madonna della Carità

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Just outside the center, built towards the end of the sixteenth century to alleviate a serious famine; it has adorned façade, paintings and frescoes and a four-piece brick dome that recalls that of Brunelleschi in Florence.

  • Where Presso il centro, Seggiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Museo dell'Olio e Olivo nel Cisternone

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Museum diffused in the center of the village starting from the suspended olive tree: a specimen of Seggian olive tree with its roots hung in the ancient water tank, nourished by water vapor and nebulized nutrients, presented as the largest plant in the world supported with aeroponic technique, a project born with the neurobiologist Stefano Mancuso. The heart is the ancient oil mill and orciaia, with tasting of the oil Seggiano DOP.

  • Where Centro storico, Seggiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it
