What to see in San Nazzaro Sesia

Country of the Novarian plain near Sesia, known for the Abbey of the Saints Nazario and Celso, founded in 1040 and fortified in the thirteenth century, with frescoed cloister; in the countryside towards Villata stands the Sanctuary of the Madonna della Fontana.

Abbazia dei Santi Nazario e Celso

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded in 1040 by the bishop of Novara Riprando with the accounts of Biandrate and entrusted to the Benedictines, it was fortified in the 13th century near a ford of Sesia: there remain sections of moat and walls and three of the four corner towers. The Romanesque church has a two-storey atrium and a pebble bell tower with fishbone; the 15th-century Gothic-Lombard church has a hut façade with rosette and three naves, and the fifteenth-century cloister preserves a cycle of frescoes on the life of St. Benedict. Among the frescoes of the church, Santa Caterina and San Rocco of 1480 attributed to Giovanni Antonio Merli; open every day from 8:30 to sunset.

Santuario della Madonna della Fontana

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Country shrine between San Nazzaro Sesia and Villata, with devotion attested from 1591 and pilgrimages from the seventeenth century; the church was rebuilt in 1752-68 on a previous structure. Under the marble altar, with a palotto made of hard stones and a rococo eraser made of wrought iron, a spring gushes out; it preserves frescoes of Peracino (1767) and G. Avondo (1817) and a small 16th century painting of the Miracle.

  • Where S.P. 16 direzione Villata, tra San Nazzaro Sesia e Villata
