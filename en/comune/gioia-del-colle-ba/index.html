What to see in Gioia del Colle

City of the Murge with the Norman-Svevo castle that houses the National Archaeological Museum and, a few kilometers away, the archaeological park of Monte Sannace, center peucetius flowered between the VI and III century BC.

3 places to visit in Gioia del Colle, in the province of Bari (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello normanno-svevo e Museo archeologico nazionale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Fortress of federician age with square courtyard with corner towers and rustic fender, 360 meters on the road between Bari and Taranto, with pre-Norman, Norman and Swabian phases and restorations of the twentieth century. Since 1977 it houses the National Archaeological Museum with the findings of Monte Sannace: geometric vases, kantharoi, attic, ionic and corinthian ceramics, a gorgonic antefix and a black figure crater of the painter of Memnon. Open 8:30-19:30, calendar Sundays.

  • Where Piazza dei Martiri del 1799 1, Gioia del Colle
  • Entry €8, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Parco archeologico di Monte Sannace

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Inhabited from the Iron Age to the First Imperial Age, with the greatest splendor between the VI and III century BC and defensive walls of the second half of the IV century BC that separate acropolis and inhabited in the plain. From the Masseria Montanaro start an archaeological and naturalistic itinerary: houses, large tombs, stoà, a Hellenistic house, painted tombs, a viewpoint and a trullo in the Murgia landscape. Open every day 8:30-15:00.

  • Where SP 61 Gioia-Turi km 4,5, Gioia del Colle
  • Entry €6, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · italia.it
