What to see in San Secondo Parmense

The agricultural centre of the Bassa Parmense on the Strada del Culatello, a feud and residence of the Rossi until 1817: the Rocca dei Rossi preserves frescoed salt like that of the Golden Ass, and among the fields stands the Romanesque parish church of San Genesio; the village is known for its cooked shoulder and Fortana wine.

Rocca dei Rossi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Wanted by Pier Maria Rossi II called the Magnificent in the fifteenth century, damaged at the end of the century and rebuilt in Renaissance forms at the beginning of the sixteenth century. The frescoed rooms are inspired by the fairy tales of Esopus and classical mythology: in the Sala dell'Asino d'Oro a cycle taken from the novel of Apuleio, in the Sala delle Gesta thirteen large paintings-work with the enterprises of the house from 1199 to 1542. From 1 March to 8 December 2026 open Friday and Saturday 10-12 and 15-18, Sunday 10-18, with guided tours every hour.

Pieve di San Genesio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Documented in 1084 and Parish since 1195, in the thirteenth century it was one of the most important of Parmense; it decayed after 1470 and in 1787 lost facade and first three spans. Rebuilt in the 13th century above a church of the 11th, it has three naves of seven spans and three apses, whose lower band is in Roman bricks of recovery; the restorations of 1967-72 brought to light the thirteenth-century structures. It rises isolated between fields and channels.
