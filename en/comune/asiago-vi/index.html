What to see in Asiago

The capital of the Highland of the Seven Municipalities, rebuilt entirely after the Great War: visit the Military Shrine, one of the main ossuaries of the First World War, the Astrophysics Observatory founded in 1942 by the University of Padua and the municipal museums, from the Naturalistic Museum Patrizio Rigoni to the open-air museum of Monte Zebio.

9 places to visit in Asiago, in the province of Vicenza (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Sacrario militare

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

One of the main military ossuaries of the First World War, where the remains of 54,000 fallen rest; it dominates the town, entirely rebuilt after the Great War.

  • Where Asiago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Osservatorio astrofisico e telescopio Galileo

Museums · Science and university

Founded in 1942 by the University of Padua at 1045 meters above sea level, it houses the Galileo telescope, a reflector with a primary mirror of 122 cm, the largest in Europe when it was inaugurated and still used for research and didactics. Guided tours, by reservation, include the Galileo dome and a multimedia room connected to the telescope; a few kilometers away, in Cima Ekar, there is the 182 cm Copernicus telescope.

  • Where Via Osservatorio 8, Asiago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: astronomia.musei.unipd.it · italia.it

  • Entry To be checked

Museo all'aperto di Monte Zebio

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Trenches, galleries, cave stations and walkways of the Austrian defensive line, active from summer 1916 to autumn 1918, connected by a ring path. Here on June 8, 1917 at 5.30 p.m. the mine exploded under the Lunetta, killing 120 Italian soldiers and over forty officers of the Catania Brigade, on the eve of the battle of the Ortigara (10-25 June 1917).

  • Where Nei pressi del Rifugio Stalder e della Crocetta dello Zebio, Asiago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.asiago.vi.it · italia.it

Museo della Grande Guerra Battaglia dei Tre Monti

Museums · Themed

Dedicated to fighting for Col del Rosso, Col d'Ecchele and Monte Valbella, from the Strafexpedition of 1916 to the battles of January and June 1918 to the last Italian reconquest in October 1918. In summer it hosts exhibitions, conferences and screenings.

  • Where Sasso di Asiago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.asiago.vi.it · italia.it

Museo Le Carceri

Museums · Modern art

The former prisons of Asiago, renovated and converted into a museum in 2001, host temporary exhibitions, especially of contemporary art, also set up in the ancient cells.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Museo dell'acqua

Museums · Themed

In a rural house in Kaberlaba, not far from the center, tells in seven sections, from the water cycle to pollution, the role of water in the life, work and imagination of the plateau; near the museum starts the didactic itinerary "The Ring of Water" .

  • Where Località Kaberlaba, Asiago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.asiago.vi.it · italia.it

Centro e Corso IV Novembre

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center, rebuilt after the Great War, is small and turns on foot through narrow streets and colorful buildings; the heart is Corso IV Novembre, the shopping street. From the center you can walk to Lake Lumera.

  • Where Corso IV Novembre, Asiago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Percorso della vecchia ferrovia

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Walking and cycling path on the old railway of Asiago, from the Millepini Park to Tresché Conca, with views of the villages of the plateau.

  • Entry Free
