What to see in Ultimo

The municipality of Val d'Ultimo, with the hamlets of Santa Valburga, Pracupola, San Nicolò and Santa Gertrude: in San Nicolò the museum of peasant life, in Santa Valburga the Culten archaeological documentation centre, in Santa Gertrude the three thousand-year-old larch trees and the visitor center of the Stelvio National Park in the ancient Lahnersäge sawmill.

5 places to visit in Ultimo, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Val d'Ultimo

Museums · Themed

The San Nicolò Museum offers a wide view of the daily life of the peasants of the valley. Open approximately from mid-May to late October in the afternoon (in 2026 from 17 May to 25 October, 14.30-17.30); guided tours on request.

  • Where 39016 San Nicolò, Ultimo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it

Sources: merano-suedtirol.it

Centro di documentazione Culten

Museums · Archaeological

On the hill of the church of Santa Valburga documents the human presence in the area from the stone to the iron age, with the replica of a sacrificial altar; the surrounding site preserves one of the largest collections of human remains of the region, from the 6th century BC. Next to the F'Hochhaus, a fifteenth-century log house moved and rebuilt, where coins, dice, forks and a 15th-century deck of playing cards have emerged, perhaps the oldest cards from .Watten in South Tyrol; outside traditional crops. Open from mid-May to late October.

  • Where Fachhaus 33, 39016 Santa Valburga, Ultimo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it

Sources: merano-suedtirol.it

Centro visitatori Lahnersäge

Museums · Themed

Visitor center of the Stelvio National Park in the ancient Lahnersäge sawmill, where until the eighties the peasants of the valley brought the wood to be cut: it explains the importance and the functions of the wood and offers virtual excursions in the park environments. Hours 9.30-12.30 and 14.30-17.30 in the summer and winter seasons; closed from November to New Year and in April.

  • Where Santa Gertrude 62, 39016 Ultimo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.nationalpark-stelvio.it

Sources: nationalpark-stelvio.it · merano-suedtirol.it

Larici millenari

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Three larch trees along the Via dei Masi della Val d'Ultimo, considered the oldest conifers in Europe: the legend gives them about 2,000 years, an age that only the analysis of the rings could confirm. Visitable all year round.

  • Where Santa Gertrude, Ultimo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it

Sources: merano-suedtirol.it

Cascata di Hartungen

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Waterfall along the Via dei Masi della Val d'Ultimo, a few minutes walk from the millenarian larch trees; recommended especially from May.

  • Where San Nicolò, Ultimo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it

Sources: merano-suedtirol.it
