What to see in Casalincontrada

The municipality of Chietina hills, not far from the Majella National Park, known for the numerous rural houses of raw land built with the technique of the "massone" and for the CedTerra, the documentation center that every September animates the Earth Festival.

CedTerra - Centro di documentazione permanente sulle case di terra cruda

Museums · Themed

Permanent exhibition set up by the Municipality on rural houses of raw earth, built with the technique of the "massone" , a mixture of straw, water and land; recognized for over thirty years (celebrated in 2023), it is located in Villa De Lollis and is the heart of the Festa della Terra in September, with workshops, seminars and exhibitions on architecture on earth.

the MiC card (updated 2020) indicates as the address Via F. P. Michetti and score closed every day; the Superintendence (2023) places the CedTerra at Villa De Lollis

Case di terra cruda

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

The municipal territory preserves many rural houses in raw land made with the technique of the "massone" (straw, water and land); among those touched by the path of the Feast of the Earth 2023 the Superintendence mentions The House of Teresa, the Houses Aceto and the Borgo Malandra Vecchia.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
