What to see in Chiusa

Medieval town of the Isarco Valley dominated by the Sabiona cliff, the acropolis of Tyrol with its monastery; the old town has paved streets in porphyry and Gothic churches, the Civic Museum in the former convent of the Capuchins houses the Treasury of Loreto and Albrecht Dürer designed it in 1494 from the point now called Dürerstein.

Monastero di Sabiona

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the cliff that dominates the city, called the "Acropolis of Tyrol": the church of Santa Croce was the diocese's cathedral for about 400 years, until the bishop was transferred to Brixen around the year 1000, and is one of the oldest pilgrimage sites in Tyrol. The Benedictine nuns remained there for over 335 years, until November 2021. You can visit the Chapel of Graces, the church of the monastery, Santa Croce and the church of Our Lady with archaeological documentation; you can only walk up, in 30-40 minutes.

  • Where Monte Sabiona, Chiusa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: klausen.it · italia.it

Museo civico di Chiusa e chiesa dei Cappuccini

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Founded in 1914, with the collections transferred to Bolzano in 1938 and returned in 1978 to the former convent of the Capuchins, reopened in 1992: it houses the Treasure of Loreto, donated around 1700 by Queen Maria Anna of Spain to her confessor Gabriel Pontifeser, with vestments, sacred furnishings and paintings of Spanish and Italian workshops of the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries, including the altar from field of King Charles II; sacred art from the fifteenth to the nineteenth century and works of the artistic colony of Chiusa (1874-1914). The visit includes the Baroque church of the Capuchins (1699-1701), with the main altarpiece of Paolo Pagani (1702).

  • Where Fraghes 1, Chiusa
  • Entry €4, reduced €2.50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · klausen.it · italia.it

Castel Branzoll

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Castle over the city built around 1250, ruined by a fire in 1671, rebuilt by Otto Piper since 1895 and completed by Karl Traut, who made it a meeting place of artists, musicians and writers until 1929.

inhabited by a family, not visitable inside

Gudon: chiesa di San Martino e Museo locale

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the hamlet of Gudon the local museum, founded in 1972 in the Pfleghaus, exhibits agricultural tools and artisans, a collection of paintings, sacred and profane art and documents of local history (open from Easter to All Saints). The fifteenth-century church of San Martino has the sacristy, called the Koburger chapel, frescoed by Ambrosius Gander; the Castel Summersberg, of the fourteenth century, opens the courtyard only on Monday evening in summer by reservation.

  • Where Pfleghaus, Gudon, Chiusa
  • Entry €4, 12-18 years €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: klausen.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Croce di Lazfons

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

7.4 km hike (about 2 hours and 50, 507 m of altitude difference) from the parking lot Jochalm-Kaseregg, for trails no. 15 and 1 between woods and meadows, to the refuge at one of the highest pilgrimage churches in Europe, at 2,341 meters; in summer there is a bus excursionists from Lazfons by reservation.

  • Where Parcheggio Jochalm-Kaseregg, Lazfons, Chiusa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it
