What to see in Castiglione Olona

Borgo sull'Olona wanted in the fifteenth century by Cardinal Branda Castiglioni as a small Renaissance town \"Toscana" in Lombardy, remained almost intact: the Collegiate and the Baptistery frescoed by Masolino da Panicale, the Branda Palace with the study of the Cardinal and the Brunelleschi Church of Villa.

5 places to visit in Castiglione Olona, in the province of Varese (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Complesso della Collegiata, Battistero e Museo

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the highest hill, where the fortress stood, the Collegiata was built between 1422 and 1425 by the Solari brothers for Cardinal Branda: in the apse stories of the Virgin and Saints Stefano and Lorenzo of Masolino da Panicale (volta), Vecchietta and Paolo Schiavo, and the tomb of the cardinal. The Baptistery, obtained from a tower of the castle, has the cycle of the Life of the Baptist of Masolino (1435) with the Banquet of Herod; in the ancient Canonica the museum exhibits the Crucifixion of Blacks of Bicci, the Annunciation of Apollonius of John, goldsmiths and Ambrosian codes. Closed on Mondays; seasonal hours (from April to September Tuesday to Sunday 10-13 and 15-18).

  • Where Via Cardinal Branda, Castiglione Olona
  • Entry €6, reduced €4, family €16
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.museocollegiata.it

Sources: museocollegiata.it · in-lombardia.it

Palazzo Branda Castiglioni e Museo civico

Museums · House museums

House of Cardinal Branda, built in two phases (the fourteenth-century wing and fifteenth-century joined by chapel and Renaissance loggia): the cardinal's room has Lombard frescoes of 1423, the study of the Hungarian Landscape of Masolino (1435), the chapel of San Martino the frescoes of the Vecchietta (1437-1439) rediscovered in the restoration of 1982; in the sixteenth-century picture the family portraits. The Civic Museum preserves the testimonies of the humanist thought of the cardinal, with frescoes and sculptures of Masolino and Vecchietta.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Mazzini 23 (Piazza Garibaldi), Castiglione Olona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · in-lombardia.it

Chiesa di Villa (Santissimo Corpo di Cristo)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1437 and 1444 on the main square, it follows closely the Brunelleschi models and is defined the first Brunelleschi building outside Tuscany: square body with grooved sandstone pilaster, octagonal drum and hemispheric dome, inside similar to the Old Sacristy of San Lorenzo in Florence. Open Tuesday-Saturday (winter 9.30-12.30 and 14.30-17.30, summer 10-13 and 15-18), Sunday and holidays 10-13 and 15-18.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi 6, Castiglione Olona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · lombardiabeniculturali.it

Borgo rinascimentale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Wanted by Cardinal Branda Castiglioni in the fifteenth century as a Renaissance village for himself and the family, after his death it was not transformed and the main buildings came almost intact; you walk up narrow streets to the terrace of the Collegiata, with views over the valley of the Olona. In the village are held the Carnival and the Giostra dei castelli.

  • Where Centro storico, Castiglione Olona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
