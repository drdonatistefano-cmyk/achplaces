What to see in Soncino

Walled village of the Cremona plain on the Oglio, with the Fortress of 1473 which houses the Aquaria archaeological museum, the fifteenth-century walls of about two kilometers and the House of Printers, where in 1488 the complete Jewish Bible was printed.

8 places to visit in Soncino, in the province of Cremona (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca Sforzesca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Wanted by the Duke Galeazzo Maria Sforza and started in 1473 by the Cremonese Bartolomeo Gadio, entirely made of bricks, with three square towers and a cylindrical double apparatus to protrude, an underground room used as a prison and the Tower of the Captain able to defend itself. Private residence of the Print since 1536, was restored by Luca Beltrami in 1886-1895; you enter from a bridge on the rivet.

  • Where Via Ferrari Enea, Soncino
  • Entry Cumulation with Aquaria, Museum of Fighters and churches €7, reduced €5, family €16
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: turismocremona.it · italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · comune.soncino.cr.it

Museo civico archeologico Aquaria

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the Rocca's rooms, it enhances the archaeological heritage of the north-east of Cremona with the findings of about thirty years of research by the Archaeological Group and the discoveries of the Superintendence, in a path from prehistory to the seventeenth century. Closed on Monday, open Tuesday-Friday in the morning and on weekends also in the afternoon.

  • Where Via Ferrari Enea 1, Soncino
  • Entry Cumulation €7, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: comune.soncino.cr.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Mura e centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The walls of the second half of the fifteenth century run for about two kilometers with three doors (to Morning, Sera and Sotto); you enter from Porta San Giuseppe. In the Town Hall square the Civic Tower (1128), the Palazzo del Podestà with nineteenth-century façade and the Palazzo Nuovo 15th century; along the streets Palazzo Azzanelli with Gothic windows, Palazzo Barbò-Cropello and the mill of Sant'Angelo.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

In the fourteenth century, modified at the end of the fifteenth century, with a fourteenth-century bell tower and cloister of 1456-68; it preserves the Complaint on the dead Christ of Agostino de' Fondulis, a wooden choir of 1508 and two windows of 1495. Open every day 9-18.

Museo della Seta

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In the former Meroni spinning mill, the large yellow building visible from the Fortress, tells the entire cycle from the breeding of the baco to the processing of silk. Free admission on the second and third Sunday of the month, 14.30-17.
