What to see in Alzano Lombardo

At the entrance of Val Seriana, Alzano Lombardo houses the Baroque basilica of San Martino, designed by Gerolamo Quadrio, with the three sacristies inlaid by Fantoni and Caniana and the Museum of sacred art; on the hill the medieval village of Olera preserves the polyptych of Cima da Conegliano.

Basilica di San Martino Vescovo

Places of worship · Basilicas

A first church was attested in 1023; the fifteenth-century building was renovated from 1659 on a design by Gerolamo Quadrio and the façade was completed in the early twentieth century on a design by Virginio Muzio; since 1922 it is a minor basilica. Three naves with eight chapels: the pulpit (1711-1751) by Gian Battista Caniana with Andrea Fantoni has four tarpaulins in Carrara marble, the Chapel of the Octagonal Rosary (1679) houses the canvas "Agar" and "Ismaele" of Piccio, the altars painted by Piazzetta and Cignaroli and on the singing house a Serassi organ of 2242 reeds.

Museo d'arte sacra San Martino e Tre Sagrestie

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The three sacristies, designed by Gerolamo Quadrio, were furnished in a little over fifteen years with wardrobes, kneelers and chests carved and inlaid by the shops Fantoni and Caniana, between stuccoes and frescoes, in a passage from baroque to rococo. From the third you enter the museum, opened in 1995 in a seventeenth-century palace purchased by the Pellicioli in 1670: 14 rooms with miniature chorals, vestments, the "San Cristoforo" of Tintoretto, the "Martyrdom of Saint Peter of Verona" of Palma the Old and 44 small wooden sculptures of the eighteenth-century Via Crucis. Closed on Tuesdays; groups by reservation.

Borgo di Olera

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fraction about 520 meters, 5 km from the center, referred to as "Holera" in a parchment of 1165; the name comes from the containers in ollar stone. Houses placed around the parish, with arches, gates and lanes; next to San Bartolomeo the church of the Holy Trinity, the oldest, with a golden wooden ancon of the sixteenth century. It is the birthplace of Blessed Thomas of Olera (1563), beatified in 2013.

Chiesa di San Bartolomeo a Olera

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Consecrated in 1471 and reformed in neo-Gothic style between 1875 and 1880 by Angelo Cattò, in the square of the village. Behind the main altar is the polyptych of Cima da Conegliano (1489), nine compartments on two orders around a wooden statue of St. Bartholomew; it also houses a Venetian-Cretan icon of the sixteenth century and paintings by Vittoriano Urbini (1588) and Giuseppe Brina. Visitable during opening hours and during functions.

  • Where Piazza di Olera, Alzano Lombardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Sources: comune.alzano.bg.it · valseriana.eu

Percorso del falco

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of about 4.6 km (2 hours and 10 minutes, altitude difference about 380 m) from the car park of Via Lacca in Olera: terraces with vines and orchards, woods of hornbeam and roverella, arid meadows and the Canto Basso (900 m) with views of Poscante and the Val Brembana, passing near Monte di Nese. Recommended clockwise.
