What to see in Ragogna

Municipality of Friuli hilly overlooking the Tagliamento, with the castle of San Pietro and its parish church, the Mount of Ragogna theater of the battle of 1917 after Caporetto and, in San Giacomo, the civic museums and the Great War.

4 places to visit in Ragogna, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di San Pietro e pieve

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified complex (VI-XVIII centuries) on a hill overlooking the Tagliamento valley, with crenellated walls, mast and towers; after the earthquake of 1976 it was restored again the last phase, of the seventeenth century. The church of St. Peter (1246) is annexed, where the archaeological excavations with the phases of the building are visible. Free admission without reservation according to the Superintendence.

  • Where San Pietro di Ragogna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.ragogna.ud.it

Sources: sabapfvg.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it · comune.ragogna.ud.it

Monte di Ragogna e sentiero di cresta

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

512 meters of hill near the narrow of Pinzano, at the western end of the Morainic amphitheatre of Tagliamento, once a hairy mountain for grazing and now entirely wooded. It was the Italian fortified line used only in the retreat of Caporetto, when some departments resisted to slow down the Austro-Germanic advance. The CAI trail 813 of ridge (about 5 km, 2 hours and 30, difficulty E) starts from the parking lot to the second hairpin bend of the scenic road Via al Monte, touches two belvederi and returns to the military mule track "Las Cengles" passing through the church of San Giovanni; on the ridge it is necessary to be careful for the overhanging side. In the sky the griffins of the nearby Lake Cornino.

  • Where Via al Monte, San Pietro di Ragogna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.ragogna.ud.it

Sources: regione.fvg.it · comune.ragogna.ud.it

Museo della Grande Guerra di Ragogna

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 2007 and curated by historian Marco Pascoli, with bilingual Italian-German imprint, it is dedicated to the battle of Ragogna after Caporetto: panels and vintage images, a large plastic of the battlefield with trenches and sides at 31 October 1917, objects recovered on battlefields, a section on Risorgimento and Second World War and a multimedia room. Open Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 15.30-18 according to the Municipality.

  • Where Centro culturale, Via Roma 23, San Giacomo di Ragogna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.ragogna.ud.it

Sources: comune.ragogna.ud.it

Museo civico «Antonio Cerutti»

Museums · Archaeological

Born in 1981 by the Archaeological Group Reunia led by Antonio Cerutti, on the ground floor of the Cultural Center: naturalistic section on the morainic amphitheatre of Tagliamento and on the ecosystems of the river and lake of Ragogna, with dioramas; archaeological section from the Mesolithic to the nineteenth century, with lithic instruments of the lake, an axe in serpentine, a fibula with bronze knight (II-III century), Lombard reliefs, the holy water-capitello of San Remigio (XI-XII century) and coins from the Roman age in Venice. Different times between MiC and Municipality: to be checked.

  • Where Via Roma 23, San Giacomo di Ragogna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.ragogna.ud.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.ragogna.ud.it
