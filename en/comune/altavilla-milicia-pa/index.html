What to see in Altavilla Milicia

Country of the coast of Palermo that for about three hundred years has guarded the Marian cult of the Madonna della Milicia, in the sanctuary on the Belvedere at the entrance of the village; on the hill near the sea remain the Norman ruins of Santa Maria di Campogrosso, the "Churchzza" .

Basilica Santuario Madonna della Milicia e Belvedere

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

The Latin Cross Church on the Belvedere at the entrance of the village, elevated to a sanctuary on 31 May 2004; it took its present form with the works of 1623, 1826 and 1908. It houses the table of the Madonna della Milicia of the late fourteenth century, of Giottesca school (restored in 1990), statues and paintings of Bagnasco, the dome frescoed by Totò Bonanno in 1996 with the Pentecost and the bronze portal of Vincenzo Gennaro (2000). On the Belvedere, during the procession of September 8th, the painting stops towards Palermo.

Chiesa di Santa Maria di Campogrosso (Chiesazza)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Norman fortified church of the 11th century, probably of 1068, erected after the victory over the Arabs at Misilmeri and flanked by a Basilian monastery; it is among the first Arab-Norman buildings of the island. Today in ruins, it has a single nave, three apses and crypt, on a hill of about 56 meters near the Tyrrhenian Sea; in 2016 under the church were found about thirty burials and coins, still in the studio.
