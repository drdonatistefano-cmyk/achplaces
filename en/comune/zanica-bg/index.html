What to see in Zanica

Municipality of the Bergamo plain whose parish church of San Nicolò preserves paintings of Palma il Giovane, Sante Peranda and Francesco Cavagna and a wooden choir of Giuseppe Caniana; the territory is part of the Agricultural Park of Rio Morla and the Rogge.

2 places to visit in Zanica, in the province of Bergamo (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish dedicated to St. Nicholas bishop, preserves paintings of Palma il Giovane, Sante Peranda and Francesco Cavagna, a polyptych of the workshop of Marinoni and a wooden choir of Giuseppe Caniana.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Parco agricolo del Rio Morla e delle rogge

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Local park that protects the Morla stream, its irrigation streams and the surrounding countryside; Zanica is among the municipalities that are part of it.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
