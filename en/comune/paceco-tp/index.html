What to see in Paceco

Trapanese Municipality that reaches the sea with the salt pans of Trapani and Paceco, nature reserve managed by the WWF between tanks and windmills; in the hamlet of Nubia, known for its red garlic, the Salt Museum in a seventeenth-century beam, while in the village the Tower of Ligny houses the Prehistoric Museum.

Riserva naturale orientata Saline di Trapani e Paceco

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

One of the most important coastal wetlands, managed by the WWF: the chessboard of the tanks, made from the ancient coastal pantani, is dotted with windmills, the Dutch-style star ones with six wooden blades and the American ones with sheet metal blades, used to move water between the tanks and grind the salt. There are flamingos, spatulae, big white herons, garzettes, swamp falcons and small limits, to be seen especially in autumn (September-November) and in spring (February-May); the Via del Sale, to be followed also by bike, connects Trapani to Marsala.

Museo del Sale

Museums · Themed

In the district of Nubia, in a seventeenth-century lamp where salt was worked (the Casa Salaria), it tells centuries of salt culture of western Sicily; next to it there is a large windmill that raised sea water and ground the raw salt. It exhibits the original tools of the salt-salters, a vine of Archimedes, sacks of jute, millstones, mill blades and photographs in black and white; it was born on the initiative of Alberto Culcasi with the provincial body for tourism, the Municipality and the faculties of Letters and Architecture of the University of Palermo, and is part of the project Via del Sale.

Torre di Ligny e Museo preistorico

Museums · Archaeological

In the Tower of Ligny, built in 1671 at the behest of Viceroy Claudio La Moraldo, Prince of Ligny: on the ground floor prehistoric and protohistoric finds of the province of Trapani, on the first floor over one hundred objects recovered in the Mediterranean sea bottom, between amphorae, anchors and objects of Greek, Roman and Punic use, with a bronze helmet of the IV-III century BC fished off Trapani. From the terrace the view ranges from Capo San Vito to Marsala and Mozia, with Trapani, Erice and the Egadi.

The MiC card (updated to March 2021) is closed every day; tel. 0923 881991
