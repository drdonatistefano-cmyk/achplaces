What to see in Sonnino

Medieval village on the hill Sant'Angelo, between the Ausoni Mountains, with the castle tower, the gates of the ancient walls, the church of San Michele Arcangelo and the Collegiate Church of San Giovanni; the Museum of the Lands of Confine tells its border history between the Papal State and the Kingdom of Naples, and in the territory the karst of Campo Soriano opens.

7 places to visit in Sonnino, in the province of Latina (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e porte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village preserves the medieval system: four districts of stone houses, alleys, steps and towers. It was surrounded by walls with 13 semi-circular towers; the five doors remain, open in the morning and closed at sunset: Portella, Porta San Pietro, Porta San Giovanni, Porta di Tocco and Porta Riore.

  • Where Centro storico, Sonnino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Castello e torre

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The castle was built around the ninth century on the hill Sant'Angelo by the lords of Sonnino; it remains the high cylindrical tower of four floors that dominates the maze of alleys.

  • Where Colle Sant'Angelo, Sonnino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo (Santuario della Madonna delle Grazie)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Built around 1210 by the masters of the Abbey of Fossanova, with Gothic-borgognoni influences; it preserves remains of a fifteenth-century ciborium and, on the high altar, a thirteenth-century table of the Madonna and Child. Since 1947 it has been the Sanctuary of Our Lady of Graces.

  • Where Colle Sant'Angelo, Sonnino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it · parchilazio.it

Collegiata di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the center of the village, built around 1200 at the behest of Pomponio de Magistris, bishop of Terracina, restored and reconsecrated in 1604; the Romanesque altar has a sculptural work attributed to Bernini or his school.

  • Where Centro storico, Sonnino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Museo delle Terre di Confine

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Anthropological demoetno museum opened in 2008 in Palazzo Mancini, with interactive and multisensory path and audio guide. Sonnino tells the land of border between the Papal State and the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies: the brigandage, the travellers of the Grand Tour and the artists who portrayed it (Bartolomeo Pinelli, Léopold Robert, Edward Lear), the Procession of the Torches, the decree of Pius VII of 1819 that ordered its destruction, figures such as the brigand Antonio Gasbarrone and Cardinal Giacomo Antonelli. Open Monday-Friday 9-13 and 14-19, Saturday and Sunday by reservation.

Monumento naturale di Campo Soriano e Rava di San Domenico

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

The karst area between Sonnino and Terracina, one of the largest in the Mediterranean basin; the symbol is the Rava di San Domenico, known as the Cathedral of San Domenico, an 18-metre limestone pinnacle shaped by water, the most photographed attraction in the area.

  • Where Campo Soriano, Sonnino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it
