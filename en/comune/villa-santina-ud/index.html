What to see in Villa Santina

Country of Carnia on a flat triangle at the confluence of Tagliamento and Degano, with the 18th-century parish church of San Lorenzo, the early Christian site of Col di Zuca (V-VII century) and the village of Invillino, one of the oldest villages of Carnia, with the Pieve di Santa Maria Maddalena on the Colle Santino.

Basilica paleocristiana di Col di Zuca

History and architecture · Archaeology

On the small hill of Col di Zuca (cuel di Cjucie), west of the village, stood a place of early Christian worship used between the V and VII century AD; to the oldest phase belongs a basilica with a Mosaic presbyterial bench of Aquileian type, and remains visible part of the elegant mosaic floor with geometric and floral motifs.

Invillino, Colle Santino e Pieve di Santa Maria Maddalena

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Invillino, about 1.5 km from the center, was for centuries the administrative and economic nucleus of the municipality and preserves houses with the characteristics of the minor Carnic architecture. On the hill of Santino, high above the Tagliamento valley, the excavations of the University of Monaco (1964-72) documented a Roman settlement of the first century, iron and glass processing activities in the late Imperial era and a fortified village of the VI-VII century (the Longobard castle of Ibligine, then covered). On the hill stands the Pieve di Santa Maria Maddalena, one of the oldest in Carnia, with three documented construction phases and present form of the fifteenth century (probably 1431); its wooden altar of Domenico da Tolmezzo is today at the Diocesan Museum of Udine.

Chiesa di San Lorenzo Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

18th-century parish church in the center of the village, neoclassical, with tetrastyle pronao stone columns and classical tympanum that suggest an ancient temple; rectangular hall with two lateral rooms and bell tower facing north. Italia.it underlines the austerity of the whole carnica.

  • Where Centro di Villa Santina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it

Santuario della Madonna del Ponte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Built in the mid-nineteenth century at the Tagliamento on the site of a sixteenth-century votive ancona, it was wanted by the rafts to protect the loads transported along the river.

  • Where Presso il Tagliamento, Villa Santina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.villasantina.ud.it · italia.it

Pineta di Villa Santina

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

About 60 hectares of forest between the village and the river Degano, with narrow streets, streams, horse riding for hippotherapy, pond for sport fishing and refreshment; from here start the paths towards Invillino and Enemonzo (Clap Foràt).

  • Where Pineta, Villa Santina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.villasantina.ud.it · italia.it

Cascata Plera e Fortino Plera

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Next to the waterfall, surrounded by vegetation, on the right bank of Tagliamento there is a cave work of the Vallo Alpino del Littorio of the 1940s; the fort has been restored and you can visit with guided tours on weekends.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
