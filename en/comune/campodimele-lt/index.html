What to see in Campodimele

Borgo dei Monti Aurunci known as the village of longevity, seat of the Ente Parco; the medieval center is closed by a wall integrated with twelve cylindrical towers, crossed outside from the Passeggiata dell'Amore, and dominates the landscape up to the Gulf of Gaeta.

6 places to visit in Campodimele, in the province of Latina (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e cinta muraria

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval nucleus, built according to tradition on the ancient Latin city of Apiola, is enclosed by a whole preserved city walls, with twelve cylindrical towers recently restored. Inside, paved and winding alleys, steep steps, arches and stone houses, accessible only on foot, with views of Monte Faggeto and the Gulf of Gaeta; in Piazza del Municipio a secular elm is the meeting point of the village.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Campodimele
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it · comune.campodimele.lt.it

Passeggiata dell'Amore

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Walking path along the exterior of the walls, overlooking valleys and mountains; Visit Lazio advises you to walk it at sunset.

  • Where Campodimele
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the 11th century in the highest point of the village, built on a previous pagan temple; it is the reference of the historical center.

  • Where Centro storico, Campodimele
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.campodimele.lt.it

Convento di Sant'Onofrio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

A small medieval monastery built by the Benedictines of Montecassino, according to Visit Lazio in the 11th century, on a hill west of the village; the feast of the saint is held on June 12.

  • Where Campodimele
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Monte Faggeto

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The peak is 1256 metres from the Aurunci Mountains, reachable from Campodimele with a comfortable walk with a stop at the source Faggetina; from the top the view spans over the Piana di Fondi, the Valle del Liri, the Tyrrhenian and the abbey of Montecassino.

  • Where Monti Aurunci, Campodimele
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Vecchio mulino del «malo tempo» in località Taverna

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Shortly and easily walking distance from the village, the mill operated only with bad weather, when the mountain water was enough to spin the wheel; in the area there are tables, wooden benches and grills.

  • Where Località Taverna, Campodimele
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it
