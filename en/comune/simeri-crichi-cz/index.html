What to see in Simeri Crichi

Municipality between the valley of the river Simeri and the Gulf of Squillace: on the hill of Simeri, the oldest village of medieval origin, the Byzantine castle and the Collegiate of Santa Maria d'Itria; on the coast the beach of Simeri Mare, known as "Costa dei Gigli" .

Castello bizantino di Simeri

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Byzantine castle built presumably in the 10th century, dominates the valley of the river Simeri and the Gulf of Squillace. It preserves almost intact the outer walls, an imposing cylindrical tower and much of the citadel, including the Palacium Castri, seat of power. Several times reported in the FAI census "The Places of the Heart" (from 2012 to 2022).

Collegiata di Santa Maria d'Itria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1121 and 1198 by the counts of Loritello, it had originally three naves; damaged by the earthquake of 1744, it was rebuilt to a single nave preserving the portal in limestone with floral motifs. Inside, the niches of the lateral altars with traces of colour and baroque stuccos remain; behind, the bell tower shows wall stratifications from the Norman foundation to the twentieth century. Facade and roof collapsed with the earthquake of 1905, after which it was abandoned in favor of the nearby church of Santa Maria Assunta.

MiC's record of abandoned property: abandoned since 1905, conservation status • bad • accessibility not indicated

Spiaggia di Simeri Mare

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Lyrics on the Ionian Sea at the foot of the hill of Roccani, called "Costa dei Lili" (Costa dei Lili) for the rare marine lilies growing on the dunes: almost white sand, crystal clear green sea, eucalyptus behind. In Chiusa resort tourist villages, and along the local coast, restaurants and services; Soverato and Catanzaro Lido are close.
