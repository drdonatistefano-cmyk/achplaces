What to see in Rocca Pia

Small village about 1,050 meters in the upper Valle del Gizio, on the historic Via della Lana between Sulmona and the Piano delle Cinquemiglia, crossed by the Tratturo Celano-Foggia; it preserves the medieval structure with loggias and 'viginali', the ruins of the castle above the Castelluccio, the Palazzo Fidei, the parish church of Santa Maria Maggiore and, outside the village, the rural church of the Madonna del Casale.

Centro storico e Castelluccio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village preserves the medieval structure adapted to the slope, with loggias, porticoes and the 'vignali', external ladders with covered landing. The fortified part, still known as Castelluccio, stands under the hill with the ruins of the Norman castle, of which the triangular plan remains, defensive towers, a stretch of the Norman tower in the upper outpost and a well preserved cantelmo tower.

  • Where Centro storico, Rocca Pia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: abruzzoturismo.it · parcomajella.it

Chiesa della Madonna del Casale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A rural church dating back to the 13th century in the locality Colleguidone-Casale, outside the village: the Gothic portal is dated 1315 and has a relief with the crucifixed Lamb, and on the stone walls remain graffiti of travellers and shepherds transhumant from the 17th century to the mid 19th century. The side bell tower could be of Norman age; inside a medieval fresco of the Annunciation and the semicircular apse.

  • Where Località Colleguidone-Casale, Rocca Pia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: abruzzoturismo.it · parcomajella.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish of the 13th century modified in the 16th century, with Gothic façade and rose window, Renaissance portal and unique nave with Baroque decorations and a canvas of the Assumption.

  • Where Rocca Pia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: abruzzoturismo.it · parcomajella.it

Palazzo Fidei

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Duke's residence of the sixteenth century in rectangular stone, with windows in Gothic style; the side watch tower is now clock tower.

  • Entry Free

Centro visita del Parco e Palazzo De Meis

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

The information center of the Majella Park is next to the eighteenth-century Palazzo De Meis, which housed the stables of a noble family, and is the starting point for guided excursions to Monte Rotella and the Regional Nature Reserve Monte Genzana Alto Gizio, among beech woods, oaks, pine trees and firs with deer and bears. Open from July 16th to September 4th on Saturday, Sunday and August 10th to 13th and 16th-19th.

  • Where Via Conte di Torino 18, Rocca Pia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: parcomajella.it · abruzzoturismo.it
