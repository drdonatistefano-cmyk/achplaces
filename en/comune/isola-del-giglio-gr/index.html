What to see in Isola del Giglio

Granite island of the Tuscan Archipelago with three centers, Giglio Porto, Giglio Castello and Campese, clear sea and vineyards of Ansonica.

4 places to visit in Isola del Giglio, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Isola del Giglio in the guide with map and filters

Giglio Castello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The fortified village closed by high medieval walls and towers, with arched alleys and stairs in the rock; in the church of San Pietro Apostolo an ivory crucifix of the sixteenth century attributed to Giambologna.

  • Where Giglio Castello, Isola del Giglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Giglio Porto e Torre del Saraceno

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The marina between terraces and vineyards, with the Saracen Tower of 1596 and, at the Caletta del Saraceno, the remains of the Roman fish pond and the Villa dei Domizi Enobarbi.

  • Where Giglio Porto, Isola del Giglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Spiaggia di Campese e Torre Medicea

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest beach on the island, of reddish sand, between the faraglione and the Medici tower; famous for its sunsets.

  • Where Campese, Isola del Giglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Fondali del Giglio

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Transparent waters and rich seabeds for diving and snorkeling, with seahorses and walls covered with sponges and gorgonies.

  • Where Isola del Giglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
