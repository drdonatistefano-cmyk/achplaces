What to see in Pomezia

A city founded in 1939 35 km from Rome, with a rationalist centre and, in Pratica di Mare, the village built on the ancient Lavinium of Enea: to see the Civic Archaeological Museum Lavinium, the area of the Thirteen Altars and Heroon of Enea, the coast of Torvaianica and the Sughereta.

Museo Civico Archeologico Lavinium

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 2005 next to the Borgo di Pratica, on the ruins of Lavinium, a religious center of the Latins linked to Enea: five thematic rooms from the 10th century BC to the Roman age, with the large statue of Minerva Tritonia (5th century BC), the votive terracotta of the shrine of the goddess and, in the last room, the funeral set of the monument to Enea (sword, spears, sacrificial knife). Multimedia path with holograms and tactile path free of charge upon reservation.

The MiC card reports hours closed every day, but the Superintendence announces events at the museum in October 2026: check opening (tel. 06 91984744)

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Borgo di Pratica di Mare e Castello Borghese

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

A fraction arose on the remains of the ancient Lavinium, which the tradition wants founded by Enea; there rises the Castle Borghese, in the Network of the Historical Houses of Lazio. In the 1960s the peasants of the estate and various figures of culture lived there.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Tomba di Sergio Leone

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

In the cemetery of Pratica di Mare, director Sergio Leone is buried, who asked to rest here, where he had shot some of his films from the 1960s.

Centro di fondazione razionalista e Museo Città di Pomezia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Founded in 1939, Pomezia has a rationalist historical centre, in the Latium Historical Houses Network, with its representative core (Municipio, Civic Tower, Poste, Church of San Benedetto, Casa del Fascio, GIL school) on Independence Square. In the center the Museo Città di Pomezia is the workshop of the '900 tells the city from its foundation to the 1950s and 1960s; the Ugo Tognazzi Library (about 25,000 volumes) has an external mural inspired by the myth of Enea.

  • Where Piazza Indipendenza, Pomezia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · comune.pomezia.rm.it

Costa di Torvaianica e Villa romana di Via Siviglia

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The coast of Torvaianica is indicated as the place of the landing of Enea, near a lagoon crossed by the Numico (now Fosso di Pratica di Mare); the coastal dunes are protected area and in Via Seville remain the remains of a Roman villa.

  • Where Torvaianica, Pomezia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Riserva naturale della Sughereta di Pomezia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Sughere forest (Quercus suber), an evergreen typical of the Mediterranean coasts here usually overwhelmed by holm oak; open to the public, is managed since 2016 by the Regional Park of the Roman Castles.

  • Where Via Cincinnato, Pomezia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: comune.pomezia.rm.it · visitlazio.com
