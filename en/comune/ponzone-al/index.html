What to see in Ponzone

Ancient aleramic marquisate between the Bormida, Erro and Orba valleys, Ponzone is the largest municipality in the area, with ten hamlets among mushrooms and truffles woods; from the viewpoint you can see the Alps, Apennines Ligurian and Monferrato, and in the territory you will find the parish church of San Michele of 1627, the sanctuary of the Pieve and the Partisan shrine of Piancastagna.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Started in 1577 on a project by Bartolomeo Scapitta and consecrated in 1627, it is three naves with vaults and eight pillars in local stone; the main Baroque altar is inlaid black bardiglio marble. It preserves an ancon signed by Scapitta and dated 1619, a canvas of Moncalvesca area with St. Francis who establishes the Tertiaries, late sixteenth century frescoes in the left aisle, the body of St. Justin martyr and a nineteenth-century organ. Visitable during functions.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Santuario della Pieve

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Sanctuary of Our Lady of the Pieve, about 3 km from the village in the valley of the Erro, before the Mille and practically rebuilt in 1694, destination of pilgrimages, with avenues that go up to a Via Crucis. The stone portal is by G. B. Solar; on the main altar a Renaissance statue of the Madonna, the vault and the apse frescoed in 1859 by Tomaso Ivaldi of Toleto, canvases of the seventeenth-seventh century in the lateral altars and a silver reliquary of the Astigian goldsmith Bernardino Badino. Free access during functions.

  • Where Via Cascinali 184, Ponzone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: comune.ponzone.al.it · alexala.it

Belvedere di Ponzone

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

From the town's viewpoint the view spans the Alps, the Ligurian Apennines, the Monferrato and the towers of the nearby villages; the town has been recognized as the second most panoramic view of Italy.

Chiesa di San Bernardo e pieve di San Colombano a Ciglione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Ciglione, the church of San Bernardo, a Baroque jewel, has the apse frescoed by Pietro Ivaldi called "il Muto" (the Muto), native to nearby Toleto; in the cemetery stands the parish church of San Colombano, of Romanesque origin.

Sacrario di Piancastagna

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

At the entrance of the Piancastagna hamlet, the Shrine recalls the fighting, the killings of civilians and the fires of the raking of October 7-10, 1944. On October 10, about sixty partisans of the Ligurian-Alessandrin division led by the captain of the Domenico Lanza .Mingo , gold medal of the military valor, stopped the German columns with four machine guns; Mingo fell covering the retreat, and with him seven other partisans. In the autumn the Festa della Montagna is held in the hamlet.

  • Where Frazione Piancastagna, Ponzone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: comune.ponzone.al.it:443 · alexala.it

Anello Piancastagna, Pianazzi e Moretti

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Along the itinerary, which can be walked on foot, on horseback and mostly by mountain bike, can be divided into two sections: it starts from the Shrine of Piancastagna and touches Cascina Tiole, Bricco, Batresca, Pessina, a chestnut dryer, the ruins of Sodovi, the carbonaias and the rock shelters of the Pianazzi, Pian Siriti and Siriti, returning to Moretti on asphalt. From the top of the Pianazzi the panorama is 360°. The Municipality also offers the ring Piancastagna, Abasse, Cascina Gorello, Cascina Viazzi, Moretti, with ford of the river Miseria.

  • Where Sacrario di Piancastagna, Ponzone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it
