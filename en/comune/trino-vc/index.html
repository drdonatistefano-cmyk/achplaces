What to see in Trino

A small town in the Vercelli plain surrounded by rice fields, just north of the Po: in its territory there are the forest of the Sorti della Partecipanza, the last edge of the ancient plain forests, and the Cistercian abbey of Santa Maria di Lucedio, founded around 1123.

5 places to visit in Trino, in the province of Vercelli (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Bosco delle Sorti della Partecipanza

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Natural park in the Vercelli plain, completely surrounded by rice fields: a planiziale Padano forest predominantly of querco-carpineto on the relief of the Costa, with a black alder anet in the northern marshland and rich in resurgences. According to the Municipality, the rules of care still in force date back to 1275, when the Marquis of Monferrato Guglielmo the Great donated the wood to the families of Trinesi \"participants" .

  • Where Bosco della Partecipanza, Trino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.trino.vc.it

Sources: visitpiemonte.com · comune.trino.vc.it

Abbazia di Santa Maria di Lucedio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded around 1123 by Cistercians who came from La Ferté on lands donated by Marquis Ranieri I del Monferrato; in 1457 it became commendable, in 1707 passed to the Savoys and in 1861 the estate was bought by Duke Raffaele de Ferrari of Galliera. There are still the octagonal Lombard Gothic bell tower, the cloister, the chapter hall of the middle of the thirteenth century, the Hall of the Conversi and the Baroque abbey church of 1767-1770 designed by Valente de Giovanni.

  • Where Lucedio, Trino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.trino.vc.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · comune.trino.vc.it

Santuario Madonna delle Vigne

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Church of the Most Holy Name of Mary on a height between Lucedio and Montarolo: the abbot of Lucedio Vincenzo Grimani had the octagonal part enlarged, designed by Antonio Bertola and then by Giovanni Battista Scapitta, and was blessed in the new form on July 21, 1713. Several times reported to the FAI census

building in serious state of degradation and abandonment since the 1970s (FAI)

  • Where Montarolo, Trino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.trino.vc.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · comune.trino.vc.it

Museo civico «Gian Andrea Irico»

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

Inaugurated in 1978, designed by Vittorio Viale and curated by Silvino Borla, it collects artifacts and photographic documentation of archaeological research in Trino; in the structure there is an audioguide. Open on the first and third Saturdays of the month from 15 to 18, or by appointment; free entry.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi 7, Trino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.trino.vc.it

Sources: comune.trino.vc.it · fondoambiente.it
