What to see in Castel San Niccolò

Municipality scattered on the slopes of Pratomagno, with the capital Strada in Casentino, center of the working of the serene stone dominated by the castle of the counts Guidi; to see the Romanesque parish church of San Martino a Vado, Piazza Matteotti with the Logge del Grano, the village of Borgo alla Collina and the two antennas of the Ecomuseo del Casentino.

6 places to visit in Castel San Niccolò, in the province of Arezzo (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pieve di San Martino a Vado

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built at the beginning of the 11th century by the counts Guidi, according to the tradition of Matilde di Canossa, it is one of the most important Romanesque churches in Casentino. Three aisles divided by six monolithic columns in serene stone with all different capitals, work of Lombard workers of the twelfth century; the frescoes walls coming from the parish church of San Niccolò (Crucifixion and St. Nicholas with Saints) and a seventeenth-century canvas with St. Simon Stock receiving the scapular from Our Lady.

Piazza Matteotti e Logge del Grano

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The central square of Strada in Casentino is surrounded by the Vector and Vector-Tommasi palaces and dominated by the Logge del Grano, five round arched bays in serene stone with the ancient roof; next, the former municipal seat today library and the sixteenth century Chapel of the Visitation with a Virgin with Child and Saints of Giovan Francesco Guerrieri (about 1600). Higher up, in the panoramic part of the village, the eighteenth-century "Collegio" is already a seminary of Jesuits, Salesians and dioceses of Fiesole.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Strada in Casentino, Castel San Niccolò
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Castello dei conti Guidi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The castle overlooking Strada in Casentino and gives its name to the municipality. The counts Guidi held it until 1349, when the inhabitants rebelled and submitted to the Florentine Republic; from the fifteenth century the territory was administered directly from Florence.

  • Where Castel San Niccolò, Strada in Casentino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo della Pietra Lavorata

Museums · Themed

An interpretation centre of the Ecomuseum of Stone in the former complex of the Salesian College: it documents the material and immaterial heritage linked to the stone of the Solano Valley, with sections on stone in the landscape, medieval architecture, legends and tradition of stonemasons of Strada in Casentino, and offers thematic routes in the territory. Every two years, in the first days of September, the town hosts the Pietra Lavorata exhibition with stonemasons and sculptors from all over Italy.

seasonal openings: the Ecomuseum indicates July-August times from 2022 and booking through the Proloco

Ecomuseo del Carbonaio

Museums · Themed

Antenna of the itinerary of the Ecomuseo del Casentino wood, in Cetica, dedicated to the trade of carbonai linked to the forest economy for centuries. Itinerary in three sections with a multipurpose room for audiovisual, educational workshop and tasting of traditional products, the Banca della Memoria Porto Franco G. Baldini with recordings on material culture and practices silvo-pastoral and, outdoors, the village of the coalmaker with rebuilt huts and a charcoal mill, in addition to the village with squares and water mills.

Open only from 20 June to 30 September, Saturday and Sunday 15:30-18:30 (Ecomuseo del Casentino)

  • Where Cetica, Castel San Niccolò
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.visittuscany.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · ecomuseo.casentino.toscana.it · visittuscany.com

Borgo alla Collina

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Borgo on a ridge that divides the valleys of the Arno and Solano, with views of the Casentino, narrow alleys between leaning houses and a square tower castle that dominates it. Every year there is the cattle fair dedicated to the Chianina breed.

  • Where Borgo alla Collina, Castel San Niccolò
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
