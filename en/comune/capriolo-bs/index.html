What to see in Capriolo

Borgo della Franciacorta at the foot of Monte Alto, between the river Oglio and the hills of the vineyards, mentioned for the first time in a document of 879; the historical center of medieval appearance is dominated by the Castle, which became a convent in 1692, and preserves the parish church of San Giorgio with works of Romanino and Callisto Piazza; on the estate Ricci Curbastro is home to the Agricultural Museum and wine.

Museo agricolo e del vino Ricci Curbastro

Museums · Themed

Set up in the rural buildings of the Ricci Curbastro estate, unique in Franciacorta, it collects thousands of tools and objects of rural life with particular attention to viticulture. Opened in 1986 after more than thirty years of research by Gualberto Ricci Curbastro and expanded by his children, it hosts temporary exhibitions, cultural events and guided tastings, and a library of over 2,000 volumes of agricultural history and local traditions. Admission fee (tel. 030 736094).

Castello-Convento e chiesa di Santa Maria degli Angeli

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the top of the hill that dominates the village, it was the defensive center of the Brescian bank of Oglio, disputed between Brescians and Bergamoschi and then between Visconti and Venice; in 1265 it was besieged and conquered by Roberto di Flanders on behalf of Carlo d'Angiò. In 1610, in 1692 it became a female convent by initiative of Chiara Foscarini Duolo and the parish priest Francesco Lantieri de Paratico, with the church of Santa Maria degli Angeli consecrated two years later and reopened in 1835 with the arrival of the Orsolines. From its remains you can enjoy the view of Franciacorta and the Oglio valley.

Parrocchiale di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

17th century church in the center of the village, on the slopes of the mountain, with two registers façade and portal in stone of Sarnico; the interior with three naves with transept and central dome, restored at the beginning of the twentieth century, preserves the Resurrection of Jerome Romanino, the Martyrdom of Saints Gervasio and Protasio of Callisto Piazza and the fifteenth century wooden statue of the Virgin called "Madonna Vecchia" .

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: franciacorta.wine · inviaggio.touringclub.it · italia.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village retains its medieval appearance, with alleys, ancient houses and panoramic views. The western part was an outpost of the castle, with a complex of houses-forts and perhaps seven or eight towers, probable center of power and seat of public prisons; tradition speaks of underground tunnels to the castle, like the tunnel of Casa Ochi, walled up after the Second World War. He has been a candidate for the FAI Heart Places several times.

  • Where Centro storico, Capriolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it
