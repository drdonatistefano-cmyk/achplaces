What to see in Saint-Denis

Country on a natural terrace overlooking the central valley, dominated by the ruins of the castle of Cly, one of the oldest in the Aosta Valley.

3 places to visit in Saint-Denis, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Cly

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The origins date back to 1027; on a spur at 780 meters he watched the feud of Cly's Challant. The central tower and the walls remain, partly in ruins, which can be visited in summer.

  • Where Saint-Denis
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it

Villaggio di Marseiller e Ru Marseiller

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Well-preserved village from which starts the Ru Marseiller, route between Verrayes, Saint-Denis and Chambave that also passes from the castle.

  • Where Marseiller, Saint-Denis
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: italia.it · lovevda.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Dionigio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
