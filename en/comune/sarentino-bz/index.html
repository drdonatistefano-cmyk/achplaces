What to see in Sarentino

The Val Sarentino, north of Bolzano, is an Alpine valley with more than one hundred peaks and traditions: lakes such as Valdurna, panoramic platforms on the Dolomites, the mysterious Stoanerne Mandln and the geographical centre of South Tyrol; in Sarentino the farm-museum Rohrerhaus tells about the peasant life from the fourteenth century.

Museo Rohrerhaus

Museums · House museums

The Rohrerhof, a farm protected in Ronco, at the southern edge of Sarentino, is documented by the end of the thirteenth century in the Urbar of Count Mainardo II of Tyrol; the oldest parts date back to 1325/26, the Gothic hall to about 1530 and the beam of peak to 1703. The municipality bought it and the association, founded in 2004, restored it and set up in museum between 2004 and 2007: smokehouse, room and Gothic rooms, garden farmer, bread ovens and temporary exhibitions on rural life. Traditions such as Sarner Reggele pipe and Sarner Striezl rye are recounted; concerts, evenings of stories and bakery courses are planned in the old oven.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.rohrerhaus.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Lago di Valdurna

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small and romantic mountain lake at the end of the Valdurna valley, in the Sarentine Alps, a naturalistic protection area; a destination for fishermen and ring excursions. From the beginning of winter natural ice allows you to skate.

  • Where Valdurna, Sarentino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: sarntal.com · suedtirol.info

Lago Kratzberg

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Crystal clear lake between rocks and granite blocks in the western Sarentine Alps, from which the river Sagbach comes down towards Aberstückl; to the west the Verdinser Plattenspitze, to the north the view of the White Horn of Sarentino.

  • Where Alpi Sarentine occidentali, Sarentino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: sarntal.com · suedtirol.info

Panoramic road that crosses the Pennes Pass at 2,211 meters; from mid-June to mid-July the blooming of the rhododendrons transforms the steep pastures on the sides of the road into a red sea.

Piattaforma panoramica Sattele

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

In the ski and hiking area of San Martino in Valle (Reinswald), in the eastern Sarentine Alps at 2,460 meters: you climb the cable car and then walk from the Pichlbergalm or Pfnatschalm; in winter a chairlift leads from the Pfnatschalm to the Sattele. 360 degree view all year round, including the Dolomites.

Antiche miniere di Asten e del Toten

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

In Asten, on the steep slopes below the Nid-Schupfen, lead and fluorite were extracted until 1890 and remains a tunnel under the Sulzspitze; on the Toten (Silberberg) were excavated galena, blenda and pyrite especially in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries, with activity attested by an act of donation of 1140, and the entrance of the Johanni gallery is well preserved.

  • Where Asten e Alpi Sarentine, Sarentino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: sarntal.com · suedtirol.info

Tornitura Fritz

Local life and traditions · Crafts

The master turner Fritz Unterkalmsteiner shows in about 20 minutes how wood was worked on the lathe over a hundred years ago, from the growth and cutting of trees to finished products; from mid-June to mid-September, every Thursday 10-12 and 14-18.
