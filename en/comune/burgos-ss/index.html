What to see in Burgos

Borgo del Goceano, about 600 meters, founded in 1337 by Mariano IV d'Arborea at the foot of the castle of the Gocean, a judicial fortress of 1134 on a granite cliff; in the village the Museo dei Castelli di Sardegna and, in the hamlet of Foresta Burgos, the horse breeding centre, the church of San Salvatore and the nuraghe Costa.

Castello del Goceano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built around 1134 under Gonario I di Torres on a granite peak about 650 meters away, visible from afar: triple walls in U, central square tower of about 15-16 meters in limestone with red volcanic stone cantonals, a large cistern turned and an underground environment. He died there in 1259 Adelasia di Torres, whose ghost according to the tradition wanders over the towers in full moon nights; passed to the Dorias, the Arboreas and the Aragonese, resisted in 1478 to a siege of more than a year and in 1516 was still in good condition, then decayed. The guided tour of the castle follows the tour of the museum.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Museo dei Castelli di Sardegna

Museums · City history

In the manor house of the late nineteenth century of the family of owner Massaiu, restored and on three floors: a path on the history of the four judged Sardinians with fortresses, wars and treatises, thematic maps of the Spanish coastal towers and strategic points of defense, photographs of the castles, an area on the peasant life, temporary exhibitions and a multimedia room on the about one hundred castles of Sardinia. From here a cobbled path climbs in a few hundred meters to the castle.

  • Where Vicolo Castello, Burgos
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · cultura.gov.it

Borgo medievale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village was born in 1337 by the will of Judge Mariano IV of Arborea, who sent a colony of 24 families at the foot of the castle; next to the new part it preserves a medieval nucleus near the fortress, with narrow streets and double sloping roofs.

  • Where Centro storico, Burgos
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Foresta Burgos

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Fraction just over seven kilometers from the village, born in the late nineteenth century: in 1906 the Royal Army opened a horse breeding centre for police and carabinieri, passed after the fifties to the Institute of horse ride of Ozieri, which raises Anglo-Arabic-Sardi horses, Sardinian pony and white donkeys of Asinara. Between woods and old buildings of the military village start paths born for the knights; a tree-lined avenue leads to the Neo-Medieval church of San Salvatore, with bichrome facade, portal and gothic rosette.

  • Where Frazione Foresta Burgos, Burgos
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Nuraghe Costa (Sa Reggia)

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The site of the most important Bronze Age of the area, of the fourteenth century BC, on a hill of 800 meters between oaks and holm oaks: pentalobed nuraghe of about 450 m2 with basalt mast of 14 meters of diameter and four towers, a village of about forty huts and an antemural about 70 meters long and tall 3, with walkway, almost intact. Parking is possible at the Scuderie Costa di Foresta Burgos and you can walk up for about 1 km.

  • Where Foresta Burgos, Burgos
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
