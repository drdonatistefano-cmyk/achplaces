What to see in Castello di Godego

Country of the Treviso plain along the Muson, whose name recalls a Roman fortress and then barbaric destroyed in 1229: Villa Priuli, seventeenth century and frescoed by Paolo Piazza, with the archaeological civic museum, the church of San Pietro and the nature path of the Prai.

Villa Priuli e Museo civico

Museums · Archaeological

Villa of the XVII century wanted by the Priuli family, with a large cycle of frescoes by the seventeenth-century painter Paolo Piazza di Castelfranco; today owned by the municipality, it houses cultural center, library and the Civic Museum. The archaeological collection includes ceramics from the Middle-Recent Bronze Age from the Motte di Castello di Godego, bowls, olles, millstones and millstones, recovered since 1975 by the Historical Archaeological Group of Castelfranco Veneto and Godego Castle, and the Longobard sets with lunula earrings found in San Pietro in 1952. Free access or upon reservation (tel. 0423 761185).

Chiesetta di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Sacello of early Christian origin (IV century A.D.), ancient multi-pageng parish church on the eastern bank of the Muson, at the edge of the Prai. It contains a fragment of white stone sarcophagus cover with head of Medusa, of the second century AD, found in 1975 and two fragments of bas-reliefs with Longobard crosses (VII century) inserted in the walls of the apse and the facade; in the surroundings were found Longobard burials.

Sentiero natura dei Prai

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Ring path that starts and arrives at the little church of San Pietro, in the countryside along the Musone stream, in nine stages: the remains of the Botte di San Pietro where Muson passed under the Muson, the Roi canal with dotted tritons and water scorpions, the five great elms, hedges of farnia, robinia and hawthorn, the closed field, the large permanent meadows (228 hectares, 17% of Prai), a typical farmhouse and the grove along the Muson.

Chiesa abbaziale di San Pietro e Santa Maria Nascente

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church of Godego is one of the oldest in the diocese of Treviso; in 1754 Pope Benedict XIV raised it to abbey church on the initiative of Archpriest Nuzio Quirini. Next to the eighteenth-century church was built a larger one in the mid-Twentieth century, designed by architect L. Candiani.
