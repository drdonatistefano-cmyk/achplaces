What to see in Aicurzio

Small town of the Eastern Brianza along the cycle paths of the parks of Rio Vallone and Molgora, with one of the accesses to the Park of Rio Vallone; in the hamlet Commenda rises Castel Negrino, rest of ancient defensive settlements of the Knights of Malta and the Templars.

2 places to visit in Aicurzio, in the province of Monza e della Brianza (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castel Negrino e la Commenda

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

In the hamlet Commenda, Castel Negrino is what remains of ancient defensive settlements belonging to the Knights of Malta and the Templars; it meets along the regional cycle route between Mezzago and Ronco Briantino. It is reported in the FAI census (2010-2022) and the route from the sanctuary of Campegorino to Castel Negrino was opened for the FAI Days of Spring 2024.

Castel Negrino and the Commenda are now private residence (in Lombardy)

  • Where Frazione Commenda, Aicurzio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.aicurzio.mb.it

Parco del Rio Vallone

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

A green area of about 480 hectares along the river Rio Vallone, a green lung in a very urbanized area, with rich flora and fauna (including 13 species of small mammals such as the wreath) and a cyclopedonal and horseback riding route that touches historical buildings such as the Sanctuary of Ornago and the lazzaretto of Cavenago di Brianza. It has seven entrances, one for each municipality, including Aicurzio; it borders the Molgora Park, the Adda Nord Park and the Montevecchia Park and the Curone Valley. It is also crossed by the regional bike route from Gessate to Carnate and Arcore.

  • Where Accesso da via Roma o via della Vittoria, Aicurzio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.aicurzio.mb.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · comune.aicurzio.mb.it
