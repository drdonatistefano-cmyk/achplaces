What to see in Gavi

Borgo Bandiera Arancione from the Ligurian imprint to the confluence of the Neirone with the Lemme, among the vineyards of the Cortese, dominated by the great star fort.

5 places to visit in Gavi, in the province of Alessandria (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Gavi in the guide with map and filters

Forte di Gavi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Podere fortress built between the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries with a starry plan with six bastions; abandoned in the nineteenth century, it was prison and prison camp in the wars. You visit with guided tours.

  • Where Salita del Forte, Gavi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · isral.it · italia.it

Parrocchiale di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built around 1165 in Romanesque style and renovated in Baroque times, with portal adorned with reliefs and numerous valuable works inside.

  • Where Gavi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Santuario di N.S. della Guardia

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint
