What to see in Zuccarello

Borgo della val Neva founded in 1248 by the Marquises of Clavesana, all along a single porticoed road between two gates, with the castle, the Romanesque bridge and the route of the Roso towards Castelvecchio.

8 places to visit in Zuccarello, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Via porticata e porte del borgo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Along the only road of the village there are arcades with arches and pillars of different shapes, closed by the Gate of Neva Bridge and the Gate of Molino flanked by towers.

Castello di Zuccarello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Remains dated between 1200 and 1248; the side tower, the best preserved part, shows traces of frescoes from the fifteenth century.

Palazzo Marchionale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Via del Roso

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Mulattiera of about three kilometers, an hour and a quarter on foot, which goes up gently to Castelvecchio; dedicated to Ilaria Del Carretto, takes its name from a fifteenth-century farm of Del Carretto.
