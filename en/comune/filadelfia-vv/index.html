What to see in Filadelfia

City rebuilt after the earthquake of 1783 by the inhabitants of Castelmonardo on the model of the Philadelphia of William Penn: orthogonal plant in four quadrants, each with its own church, and a large central square; outside the village the Fontana Ficarazza and the ruins of the disappeared city.

Centro storico e le quattro chiese

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

After the earthquake of 1783 the city was rebuilt under the name of Philadelphia, wanted by Bishop Serrao to love the inhabitants as brothers, and with the scheme of the Philadelphia by William Penn: two large arteries that cross form four quadrants, each with a church (San Francesco di Paola, San Teodoro, Santa Barbara and Madonna del Monte Carmelo), around a large square; the population distributed in the quadrants according to the social class.

  • Where Centro storico, Filadelfia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Fontana Ficarazza

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Fountain of about 1850 at the entrance of the village, with three mouths and apotropaic masks representing Odio, Love and Oblio; an epigraph attributed to Mgr. Giovanni Andrea Serrao celebrates the water of Caprifico brought to the village at the expense of the citizens, another in Latin by Domenico Cordopatri invites the matrons to rejoice at the feasts of Caprifico.

  • Where Strada Provinciale 1, Filadelfia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Ruderi di Castelmonardo

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill south-east of the village, on the last slopes of the Serre, the remains of the city destroyed by earthquakes of the late eighteenth century, lying on three hills between two valleys; excavation campaigns are bringing to light the main structures and in August there is a pilgrimage.

  • Where Collina di Castelmonardo, Filadelfia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it
