What to see in Campagna Lupia

Overlooking the southern lagoon of Venice, the municipality includes the WWF oasis of Valle Averto, state nature reserve and Ramsar wetland, and in the fishing valleys the Casone di Valle Zappa, a Dutch-style hunting casino of the 1920s; in the village the town hall is located in the sixteenth-century Villa Colonda Marchesini.

Oasi WWF di Valle Averto

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

State nature reserve of about 200 hectares in the lagoon of Venice, managed by the WWF and wetland of international importance (Ramsar) since 1989: brackish water basins, reeds, humid meadows, riparian woods and canals, with about 400 plant species and two European priority habitats (falasco limestone bogs and black alder and ash woods). There are about 250 species of birds passing through; they nest swamp falcon, avocetta, common stern, tarabusino, nittochora and have it small, and there is a good population of marsh turtles. You can visit only with a guide and by reservation: 3.5 km, about two hours. It can be reached from Romea (SS 309) at km 112,800, at Lugo.

  • Where Via Pignara 4, Campagna Lupia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.wwf.it

Casone di Valle Zappa

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hunting and fishing casino built between 1923 and 1928 for Mario Malvezzi on a project by Duilio Torres, which brought to the lagoon the model of the Dutch farm: main building, boat and sighting tower with external spiral staircase. In the 1930s and later, Ernest Hemingway and Salvador Dalí spent holiday periods there, and in 1976 it was a set of a film by Tonino Cervi; today it belongs to the Roncato family. You can admire it from the water, with boat excursions in the southern lagoon from Chioggia or Venice, or from the surrounding area.

private property, not open to visits: you can see only from the outside

Villa Colonda Marchesini, già Zorzi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Villa of the sixteenth century, now municipal seat; in its noble hall the Municipality organizes exhibitions with free admission, like that of 2026 on the lagoon landscapes of the painter Aldo Bergamini.

  • Where Via della Repubblica 34, Campagna Lupia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.campagnalupia.ve.it

Sources: comune.campagnalupia.ve.it
