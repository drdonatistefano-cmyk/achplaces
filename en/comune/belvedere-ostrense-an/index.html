What to see in Belvedere Ostrense

Borgo della Vallesina between hills of vineyards and olive groves, closed by Renaissance walls with small loggias and polygonal towers; it houses the International Museum of Postal Image, linked to the patron Saint Rufo, protector of the portals, and outside the walls the collegiate of Santa Maria della Misericordia and the sanctuary of the Madonna del Sole.

Centro storico e mura rinascimentali

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The town is surrounded by Renaissance walls adorned with small loggias and polygonal towers; inside the walls are the parish church of San Pietro, in the homonymous square, and the Town Hall. Around, gentle hills covered with vineyards and olive groves and small river valleys.

Collegiata di Santa Maria della Misericordia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Collegiate church outside the walls, built at the end of the fifteenth century and rebuilt in neoclassical forms between 1796 and 1849 on a project by Raffaele Grilli; the porch was completed in 1985.

Santuario della Madonna del Sole

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Outside the walls in the direction of San Marcello, it owes name and fame to a fresco of 1471 Bartolo di Andrea di Jesi, coming from a rural church more ancient and neighboring. On the second Sunday of May there is celebrated the Feast of the Madonna del Sole, with games of the rural tradition such as the "Quarantotto" and the shooting of the rope.
