What to see in Vigone

A small town of the Pinerolian plain of Roman origin, mentioned for the first time in a diploma of Ottone III; it preserves palaces of the Princes of Acaja, porticos, the Selve Theatre called "Little Carignano," churches from the Middle Ages to the nineteenth century and the works of the Vigonese painter Michele Baretta.

8 places to visit in Vigone, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico, portici e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Portici along Via Umberto I and Piazza Palazzo Civico, the Palazzo del Comune restored, three fourteenth-century palaces of the Princes of Acaja, the eighteenth-century Palazzo Sillano, Palazzo Baudi di Selve and the building of Via Umberto I, administrative seat of the assets of the Acaja in the fourteenth century and modified by the seventeenth century; in the former hospital, now a retirement home, a loggia of the fourteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Palazzo Civico 18, Vigone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.vigone.to.it

Sources: comune.vigone.to.it

Pinacoteca comunale (Donazione Michele Baretta)

Museums · Modern art

Three rooms annexed to the civic library of Palazzo Luisia exhibit about fifty works donated by the Vigonese painter Michele Baretta (1916-1987), mostly preparatory studies for sacred frescoes performed between 1936 and 1962; the library also preserves the cartoons of his frescoes.

MiC card (Add. 2021) indicates closed every day: contact the library (tel. 011 9801243)

  • Where Via Umberto I 17, Vigone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.vigone.to.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.vigone.to.it
