What to see in Premana

The centre of Valvarrone under Mount Legnone, with houses that slope along the slope, Premana owes its fortune to iron and is known in the world for scissors and knives; the Ethnographic Museum tells about its culture and from the village start the paths to the lakes of Deleguaggio and Pizzo Tre Signori.

Museo Etnografico di Premana

Museums · Themed

It collects traces of the past agricultural and pre-artisan activity of the country and documents uses, customs, traditions and local culture; the regional portal indicates it as one of the most valid initiatives realized in the Lombard Prealps. It tells a country whose history is linked to iron and to the processing of scissors and knives, still flourishing crafts at an international level.

Laghi di Deleguaggio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Trail that starts from Premana: about 2 hours to the alpine pasture, an extra hour to the Lower Lake and another 30 minutes to the Upper Lake, from which you can climb to the Upper Pizzo. In the village you can rent bikes with pedal assistance to reach also the Alpe Campo and the Alpe Premaniga.

  • Where Partenza da Premana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Valle del Varrone e rifugi Casera Vecchia e Santa Rita

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Excursion of about 4 hours from Premana along the Varrone river to the Casera Vecchia refuge and then to the Santa Rita refuge, 2000 m, with stretches of via ferrata; continue until the Bocchetta di Piazzocco and Pizzo Tre Signori. In the waters of Varrone trout are fished.

  • Where Partenza da Premana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
