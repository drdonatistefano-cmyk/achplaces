What to see in Mesagne

The centre of Brindisi with its roots in the Iron Age, whose historic centre was profoundly transformed in the 17th and 18th centuries, when the Mesagnese architect Francesco Capodieci guided the reconstruction of many Baroque churches; among the main destinations the Norman-Svevo Castle with the archaeological museum MATER, the Mother Church of All Saints and the archaeological area of Vico Quercia.

9 places to visit in Mesagne, in the province of Brindisi (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico, Porta Grande e Porta Nuova

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries the historic centre underwent profound urban changes and many churches were rebuilt or renewed under the guidance of the Mesagnese architect Francesco Capodieci. The route recommended by the Municipality starts from Porta Grande (Porta Napoli), the rest of the ancient city walls, crosses Piazza Orsini del Balzo and Vico Castello until Piazza IV Novembre, the heart of the city, and passes through Porta Nuova, historical access that united the bourgeois quarter to the ancient village.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II (Porta Grande), Mesagne
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.comune.mesagne.br.it

Castello Normanno Svevo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

The castle is located in the heart of the historic center and is considered by the Municipality a monument of high architectural value, witness to the lordship that have succeeded one another in the territory. With that of Oria it marked the ridge of the Via Appia, as intermediate stage between Taranto and Brindisi. It hosts the MATER Museum and has the same times: in summer (from 1st June 2025) Monday 17-22, Tuesday-Friday 9-13 and 17-22, Saturday and Sunday 9-13 and 17-23, last entry half an hour before closing.

  • Where Via Castello, Mesagne
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

MATER - Museo Archeologico del Territorio «Ugo Granafei»

Museums · Archaeological

In the castle, it gathers materials from the Bronze Age to the Middle Ages, with particular attention to the Messapic finds of the settlements of Muro Lieutenant and Muro Maurizio and an important collection of Greek and Roman coins. Admission fee.

Chiesa Madre di Tutti i Santi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Considered one of the most original works of Baroque Lecce, it had the present form by the Mesagnese priest-architect Francesco Capodieci; the origins, uncertain, would date back to the fourteenth or fifteenth century. The three-order façade has niches with the apostles, a bas-relief of the Madonna del Carmine and, above the portal, statues of Saint Eleutery, Anthia and Corebo. Inside, with a Latin cross with chapels and altars in finely carved stone, the Adoration of the Shepherds began by Gian Pietro Zullo and completed by Andrea Cunavi and Domenico Pinca, and a Madonna of Carmine by the Neapolitan Giuseppe Bonito.

Chiesa di Sant'Anna e piazza Orsini del Balzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The church, which the Municipality defines as a masterpiece, overlooks Piazza Orsini del Balzo and has a history linked to that of the castle; it is one of the four Baroque churches of the itinerary "Mesagne," the Baroque heart of Salento.
