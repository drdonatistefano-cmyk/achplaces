What to see in Rocca San Giovanni

Borgo on a rocky hill of the Costa dei Trabocchi, with first nucleus of 1076 soon transformed into a castle and stronghold of the monastery of San Giovanni in Venere; sections of walls of the thirteenth century, the church of San Matteo, the terrace on the sea and, on the coast, the overflows of Punta Tufano and Punta Cavalluccio.

6 places to visit in Rocca San Giovanni, in the province of Chieti (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico, mura e torrione

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The first nucleus dates back to 1076 and soon became a castle; of the 13th century belt remains a stretch with walk and the medieval tower. The alleys are decorated with buckets, tools, washbasins and pots among the flower pots, with the corner of the kisses.The nineteenth-century palaces like Palazzo Colizzi have large interior gardens.

  • Where Centro storico, Rocca San Giovanni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di San Matteo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque parish with the original Gothic arches, erected by Oderisius II around 1200.

  • Where Centro storico, Rocca San Giovanni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Terrazza panoramica

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Closes the village on its rocky spur, overlooking the hill and the sea; from above you can see the district of Vallevò, with low houses overlooking the gardens.

  • Where Rocca San Giovanni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Palazzo Comunale e Museo del Municipio

Museums · Modern art

19th century building started in 1862, after the insurrection of 1860 led by Giustino Croce; it hosts a permanent exhibition of sculptures and paintings by contemporary artists.

  • Where Piazza degli Eroi 14, Rocca San Giovanni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Trabocco Punta Tufano

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Viewpoint

Trabocco in the hamlet of Vallevò, about 10 minutes drive from the center, reached by a wooden footbridge next to the Via Verde dei Trabocchi; the owner tells the story of the overflows, the laws that protect them, how they are built and how the net works. The other overflows of the territory are Punta Cavalluccio, Punta Isolata, Sasso della Cajana and Punta Punciosa.

  • Where Vallevò, Rocca San Giovanni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Spiaggia del Cavalluccio e Via Verde dei Trabocchi

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Via Verde, 42 km bike path between Ortona and Vasto, touches the rocky beach of the Foce and, further on, the beach of the Cavalluccio, indicated among the most beautiful of the coast.

  • Where Costa di Rocca San Giovanni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it
