What to see in Barberino Tavarnelle

Two villages in Chianti: Barberino Val d'Elsa, Bandiera Arancione, and San Donato in Poggio, among the most beautiful villages in Italy, with the Badia a Passignano not far away.

3 places to visit in Barberino Tavarnelle, in the province of Florence (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Barberino Val d'Elsa

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The walled village was born from the ruins of Semifonte, razed to the ground by the Florentines in 1202, with the Hospital of the Pilgrims of the fourteenth century.

  • Where Barberino Val d'Elsa, Barberino Tavarnelle
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: inviaggio.touringclub.it · touringclub.it · destinationflorence.com

San Donato in Poggio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified village of Chianti, one of the most beautiful villages in Italy, with the Romanesque parish church and Palazzo Malaspina, seat of the tourist office.

  • Where San Donato in Poggio, Barberino Tavarnelle
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · destinationflorence.com

Badia a Passignano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

The Vallombrosana abbey fortified between cypresses and vineyards of Chianti, with the Last Supper of the Ghirlandaio in the refectory.

  • Where Badia a Passignano, Barberino Tavarnelle
  • Entry To be checked: guided tours on limited times
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · destinationflorence.com
