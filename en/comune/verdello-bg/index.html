What to see in Verdello

Municipality of the Bergamo plain linked to the Gambarini family: Villa Giavazzi, built by Carlo Maria Gambarini in Napoleonic age and today seat of the Town Hall, with its nineteenth-century romantic park, the Neoclassical Mausoleum of 1823 and, in a factory abandoned on the avenue of entrance, the Museum of the territory dedicated to the peasant civilization.

Museo del territorio - La Fabbrica sul viale

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Ethnoanthropological Museum recognized as Museum Collection by the Lombardy Region in 2007, born from over two thousand objects from the peasant world collected from the eighties by the farmer Andrea Baraldini and set up in a factory abandoned along the avenue of entry to the village. Agricultural and artisan tools (falegname, cardatore, blacksmith, shoemaker, saddlery), domestic furnishings and toys on 300 square meters covered, plus two outdoor areas with wagons, calexs, ploughs, harrows and a military field kitchen; library, archive and photographic fund. Free admission; open Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday 8.30-11.30, Saturday 9.30-11.30 and 14.30-17; extraordinary guided tours on request.

  • Where Via XI Febbraio 4, Verdello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.verdello.bg.it

Villa Comunale Giavazzi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Villa Gambarini-Cagnola-Giavazzi, built as a family residence by Carlo Maria Gambarini in the French period (1797-1815). It passed by inheritance to Rosa Gambarini and her husband, the Milanese nobleman and deputy Giovanni Battista Cagnola, and in 1902 to Giovanni Javazzi and his sons; occupied by the Germans in the Second World War and then abandoned, it was bought by the Municipality in 1972 and since 1980, after the restoration, it is the seat of the Town Hall. Free access to public opening hours.

  • Where Via Cavour 23/A (ingresso pedonale da piazza Aldo Moro), Verdello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.verdello.bg.it

Sources: comune.verdello.bg.it

Parco Comunale

Nature and landscape · City parks

Park of the villa of almost 24,000 sqm, with central lawn and wooded belt perimeter rich in trees of the first plant. It preserves the romantic "fabbriche" of the nineteenth century: the turret on the Colleonesca Roggia (1815-1816), the "gothic" ruins of 1849 designed by Angelo Cattò with Ghibelline turret, a cave with log stone nymph, the "alpine" chalet with glacier (now in ruins) and the statues of Venus and Ceres, the hermitage of Pan and the tuft for the passage of the emperor Francesco I of Austria. Flat paths, accessible.

  • Where Via Cavour 23/A (ingresso da piazza Aldo Moro), Verdello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.verdello.bg.it

Mausoleo Gambarini-Cagnola-Abbiati

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Wanted in 1823 by Carlo Maria Gambarini for himself and the family, designed by Giovanni Battista Capitanio and built under the direction of Giuseppe Catto; sculptures by Antonio Gelpi il Giovane, including two painful winged figures and the great statue of Time on the pediment, and Masonic and esoteric symbols. Inside there is an octagonal chapel with the tombs of the Gambarini by Luigi Cocchi and a bust by Rosa Tarsis Cagnola by Vincenzo Vela; in the crypt is buried the music critic and composer Franco Abbiati.

not open freely: open only on special occasions announced on the website of the Municipality and on the social media of the library
