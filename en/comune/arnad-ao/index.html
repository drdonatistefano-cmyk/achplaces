What to see in Arnad

Country between vineyards and chestnut groves in the Dora plain, famous for the Lard d'Arnad DOP, with three castles, a stone bridge and the most important Romanesque church in the region.

5 places to visit in Arnad, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The most important example of Romanesque architecture in the Aosta Valley, with three naves with Gothic vaults and late Gothic frescoes in the attic.

  • Where Arnad-Le-Vieux
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it
