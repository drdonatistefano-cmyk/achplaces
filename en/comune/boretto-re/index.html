What to see in Boretto

Po country sheltered from the bank, already a Venetian port of call on the river: the basilica of San Marco with the dome of 46 meters, the museum-cantiere Po 432 dedicated to navigation, the Casa dei Pontieri and the marina from which depart the motorboats on the great river.

7 places to visit in Boretto, in the province of Reggio Emilia (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Boretto in the guide with map and filters

Basilica di San Marco Evangelista

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Parish church built between 1871 and 1883, with a 46-metre-high dome collapsed in 1988 and rebuilt; in the left arm a Madonna with Child, Saint Anthony of Padua and angels of 18th-century Venetian school, by Giacomo Guerrini. Remember the role of Boretto as port of call of the Venetians on the Po.

  • Where Piazza San Marco, Boretto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Po 432 – Museo del Po e della navigazione interna

Museums · Themed

Museo-cantiere in buildings of the gorge near the river port, dedicated to the river and navigation: industrial archaeology with tools and machinery used especially in the first half of the twentieth century by the Civil Genio. The name recalls the 432 km that separate Boretto from the source of the Po al Monviso.

  • Where Porto fluviale, Boretto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo Casa dei Pontieri «D. Gialdini»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

It tells how the Po has crossed over time: images and plastic of the bridge of boats between Boretto and Viadana, whose impalced was destroyed in 1967, collected by the son of the last bridge leader. Open Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 9-12 and 15-18.

  • Where Via Argine 3, Boretto
  • Entry €1 symbolic offer
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: reggioemiliawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo multimediale della Bonifica

Museums · Themed

Sounds and images projected on the walls of a hydraulic knot in the 1920s style: the hydrovora is open to the public and the museum shows its mechanisms.

  • Where Boretto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · reggioemiliawelcome.it

Casa museo Pietro Ghizzardi «Al Belvedere»

Museums · House museums

In the hamlet of Santa Croce, the house of the artist Pietro Ghizzardi, which preserves the largest collection of his paintings, sculptures, graphic works and manuscripts.

  • Where Santa Croce, Boretto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: reggioemiliawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Piazza San Marco e Sala del Consiglio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The town hall square with its tower; the Council Hall has Art Nouveau decorations by Marcello Nizzoli, the designer of the Olivetti typewriter Letter 22.

  • Where Piazza San Marco, Boretto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Porto turistico e Lido Po

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

National and regional river port from which the motorboats start on the Po; in spring and summer, on request, cruises to Mantua. In the Lido Po area the Big Bench, inaugurated in 2023, of the project of Chris Bangle; along the river passes the cycle path towards Gualtieri and Brescello.

  • Where Lido Po, Boretto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
