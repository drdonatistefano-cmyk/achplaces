What to see in Corato

Oil Town in Puglia Imperiale, surrounded by the Alta Murgia National Park, less than 15 km from Andria and about 40 km from Bari. The medieval center has a ray system around Largo Cesare Battisti, with the Matrix Church of Santa Maria Maggiore, palaces like De Mattis and a nineteenth-century theater; in the territory the necropolis to mound of San Magno and the Dolmen of the Paladini.

Centro storico e Largo Cesare Battisti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The medieval nucleus with an urban ray system, built on an ancient Norman fortress: the walls, of about 1046, wanted by Pietro I il Normanno, counted 24 towers. Natural starting point is Largo Cesare Battisti, central tree-lined avenue; around the Church of the Incoronata, the monastic complex of San Benedetto (baroque convent church erected in 1627, especially in the bell tower) and the palaces Capano, Laconica Vecchio, De Mattis (1579, Patrons Griffi, with rustic diamond-pointed) and the Palazzo di Città, seat of the City in a former convent, whose council hall preserves a painting with the motto attributed to Federico II.

Chiesa Matrice di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The first major building work of Corato, financed by the Decurinato citizen shortly after the foundation of the city, recognizable by the portal to acute sixth. Damaged by the earthquake of 1627, it was consolidated with a high arch, still visible, supporting the left side wall.

Teatro comunale di Corato

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Theatre of the late nineteenth century overlooking Piazza Marconi, enlarged to a semicircle to embrace it, with boxes, loggia and refined decorations, once considered a jewel of southern Italy. The 19 sets were created by the sets of the Teatro San Carlo di Napoli and the curtain, designed by Ponticelli, represents the Challenge of Barletta; in 1876 the citizenship defended him from the rebels who wanted to set him on fire. After a period as a cinema it was reopened in 2012 after an important restoration; guided tours, tel. +39 080 5242244.

Museo della Città e del Territorio

Museums · City history

It is located in the former prison of Via Trilussa and preserves the historical and archaeological memory of Corato. Since 1 July 2025 it is the center of the Integrated Museum System (with Necropolis of San Magno, Dolmen dei Paladini and Underground Corato), managed by VIVARCH APS with events, workshops, guided tours and temporary exhibitions. Contacts: corato@sistemamuseo.it, tel. +39 080 8720861.

Necropoli di San Magno

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

In San Magno, about 13 km south-southeast of Corato in the Alta Murgia Park, a necropolis of mound graves dating from the 7th to the 4th century BC: in the center of each tomb a large, mostly rectangular cyst, surrounded by blocks and megalitic slabs that give it a dolmenic appearance. There were found objects in bronze and iron and pottery; the municipality indicates it as one of the most important Dolmenic complexes of the Alta Murgia.
