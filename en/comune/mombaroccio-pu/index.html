What to see in Mombaroccio

Medieval village among the olive groves of DOP oil of Cartoceto: in the woods not far from the village the Franciscan sanctuary of Blessed Sante, with 14th century cloister and a gold basement gallery; in the village the museum of peasant civilization in the underground environments of the convent of San Marco and the tradition of embroidery, alive from the fifteenth century.

4 places to visit in Mombaroccio, in the province of Pesaro e Urbino (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario e convento del Beato Sante

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the woods of oaks, maples and chestnut trees near the village, the Franciscan monastery founded according to tradition in 1223, when St. Francis was still alive, with fourteenth-century cloister with arches; from 1423 it bears the name of Blessed Giansante Biancorsini, who lived there since 1362, whose body rests in a golden urn of the eighteenth century. During World War II, some 300 people, including many Jews, found refuge there.

  • Where Via Passo 28, Mombaroccio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Pinacoteca conventuale «Beato Sante»

Museums · Old masters

In the cloister of the sanctuary: the polyptych of Zanino di Pietro, the Madonna of Humility by Andrea di Bartolo, the Meeting of Gioacchino and Anna of the second fifteenth century and, in church, a crucifix of 1320-1330. Visits on request.

  • Where Via Passo, Mombaroccio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

In the underground environments of the convent of San Marco, objects from the eighteenth century to the seventies of manual and animal work: carpenter's workshop, snowshoe, weaving room, mill with oil tools, cellars and a threshing machine from the early twentieth century.

  • Where Piazza Barocci, Mombaroccio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Ricami di Mombaroccio alla Torre civica

Local life and traditions · Crafts

At the base of the 17th-century civic tower, the former Museum of Embroidery is today a workshop-association that exhibits ancient and recent embroideries of the women of the country, a tradition documented since the fifteenth century.

No more museum: openings to be agreed (0721 470218)
