What to see in San Michele Salentino

Country born about two centuries ago around an eighteenth-century farmhouse of the Dentice princes of Frasso, about 30 km from Brindisi, with the matrix church of the late nineteenth century on Piazza Dante, the Pinacoteca "Salvatore Cavallo" with 260 works of the Italian twentieth century, the ancient village of Ajeni and, among olive groves and trulli, the Grotto of Augeluzzi frequented by Neanderthals.

Pinacoteca «Salvatore Cavallo»

Museums · Modern art

In a large building in the center, it exhibits 260 works donated by Cavallo, artist and local collector, to which is dedicated a monographic section: paintings, sculptures and graphics, from the two small works of Lombard school to a large nucleus of the 1950s and 1960s, with works by Guglielmo Pizzirani, Remo Brindisi, Concept Pozzati, Alberto Sughi, Luigi Filocamo, Salvatore Fiume and Giulio Turcato. A detached seat in the Town Hall hosts the municipal collection in rotation.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Chiesa Matrice di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small church of the historic centre built between 1876 and 1882, with a modest façade on Piazza Dante; the sober interior with a single nave houses in the presbytery a wooden altar, a baptismal stone font and statues of St. Michael the Archangel and the Risen Christ, as well as a Pietà by Michele Pomes di Grottaglie on a sketch by the local sculptor Cosimo Carlucci.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The ancient heart of the village, about a kilometer from the village, where village festivals and living nativity scenes are held.

Grotta di Augeluzzi

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

In the district of Augeluzzi, between olive groves and trulli, the cave dates back to the Middle Paleolithic and testifies to the presence of Neanderthal man in the territory.
