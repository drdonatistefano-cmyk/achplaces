What to see in Verrayes

Municipality of villages on the hill above Chambave, with ancient medieval russ, the frescoes of Giacomino d'Ivrea and the reserve of the pond of Loson.

6 places to visit in Verrayes, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Verrayes in the guide with map and filters

Cappella di San Michele a Marseiller

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Next to the house Maison Saluard, commissioned in the mid-Fifteenth century by the notary Saluard with a cycle of frescoes by Giacomino d'Ivrea recently restored.

  • Where Marseiller, Verrayes
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Martino e Santa Barbara

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt in the nineteenth century on an older building, with 15th century bell tower.

  • Where Verrayes
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Riserva naturale dello Stagno di Loson

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

At about 1500 meters, wetland with about seventy species, some rare or unique in Valle d'Aosta.

  • Where Verrayes
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Free entry · Viewpoint

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Plastico della Valle d'Aosta dell'abbé Vescoz

Museums · Themed

Plastic of the region created by the canon Pierre Louis Vescoz between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, which can be visited by reservation.

Ru de Joux e Ru de Chavacour

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval irrigation channels: the first of about 1250 brings water from the valley of Saint-Barthélemy, the second of about 1350 was disputed with Torgnon.

  • Where Verrayes
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it
