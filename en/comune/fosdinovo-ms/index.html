What to see in Fosdinovo

Fortified village of Lunigiana that dominates the plain of Magra and Luni, the last village before the sea; Orange Flag since 2014.

Castello Malaspina di Fosdinovo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The best preserved castle of Lunigiana, built from 1340 on towers prior to 1200: antique furnishings, paintings, legend of Bianca Maria Aloisia walled alive and the tradition of Dante's stay in 1306.

  • Where Via Papiriana 2, Fosdinovo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website castellodifosdinovo.it

Sources: castellodifosdinovo.it · visittuscany.com

Torre Malaspina e mostra archeologica

Museums · Archaeological

Entering the Gate of Sopra, the tower with the tourist office, the remains of the ancient Mark Mint and the exhibition of the finds of the Ligurian-Apuana necropolis of Pulica (on request).

  • Where Fosdinovo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Pieve di San Remigio e Oratorio dei Bianchi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The church with the fourteenth-century statue of the saint, the tomb of Galeotto Malaspina (1367) and the organ Serassi; next to the oratory with the seventeenth-century crucifix of the "Christ in agony."

  • Where Fosdinovo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
