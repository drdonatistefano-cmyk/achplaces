What to see in Maiori

One of the most famous and popular villages of the Amalfi Coast, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, with the largest beach in the area; rebuilt largely after the flood of 1954, it preserves the Collegiate Church of Santa Maria a Mare with the sacred art museum in the crypt, the abbey of Santa Maria de Olearia towards Capo d'Orso and the Trail of Lemons that leads to Minori.

Collegiata di Santa Maria a Mare

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage

A church of duodecimesque origin, several times rebuilt over the centuries, with majolica dome and bell tower of the eighteenth century: the interior with three naves has a coffered ceiling of the sixteenth century and on the main altar a fifteenth-century wooden statue of the Madonna with Child. It is the most famous church of Maiori and keeps ex-vote linked to the local rites; from its churchyard starts the Trail of Lemons.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Museo d'arte sacra «Don Clemente Confalone»

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Set up in the 18th century crypt of the Collegiate Church on about 232 m2 and three levels, with a panoramic garden of about 200 m2, it traces the history of the sanctuary with objects from the 12th to the 18th century. The most precious piece is the English alabaster palace with scenes of the Christian mysteries and the saints Margaret and James; there are then a box of the workshop of the Embriaches (early XV century) with the legend of Mattabruna, two fifteenth-century antiphonaries, the reliquary bust of St Triphon, the original statue of the Madonna dell'Avvocata, vestments from the seventeenth to the nineteenth century and Neapolitan silvers. According to the Touring it is open every day 16-18, closed in January and February, with admission fee.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Abbazia di Santa Maria de Olearia

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

One of the most famous monastic settlements of the ancient Duchy of Amalfi, leaning on a rocky wall between Capo d'Orso and the centre of Maiori; its value lies in the medieval pictorial cycles preserved inside. The tours are guided and by reservation at the tourist office of the Municipality; the Touring indicates opening Wednesday and Saturday 15:30-18:30 and Sunday 10-13, on request the other days, closed from November to March, free admission.

  • Where SS Amalfitana 43, Maiori
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Santuario di Maria Santissima Avvocata

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Sanctuary on Mount Falerzio, born in 1485 when the pastor Gabriel, after a dream related to the Virgin, had an altar built in a cave and became a hermit; followed the chapel, a small church with three naves, the hermitage and then a church on the rock above with the bell tower. Recto da hermiti e poi dai Camaldolesi, was closed in 1807, rebuilt at the end of the 19th century and bought in 1913 by the Badia di Cava. In the cave frescoes with the Virgin, the Annunciation and the Last Supper; on Pentecost Monday is the destination of a pilgrimage with traditional songs and dances.

  • Where Monte Falerzio, Maiori
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Sentiero dei Limoni

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Walking path of medium-low difficulty, with about 400 steps and about an hour of walk, which from the Collegiate Church of Santa Maria a Mare crosses the lemon gardens (including the Sfusato Amalfitano, IGP Lemon Coast of Amalfi) and the spontaneous vegetation, touches the village of Torre with the church of San Michele and goes down to Minori, with views of the promontory between the two villages.

  • Where Partenza dalla Collegiata di Santa Maria a Mare, Maiori
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Palazzo Mezzacapo e giardini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage

Along Corso Reginna, the central street, the palace was the residence of the Marquis Mezzacapo and today houses the municipal library, the historical archive, a cultural workshop and offices of the municipality; the gardens, known for the scenic fountains, are home to shows and events.

  • Where Corso Reginna 71, Maiori
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Torre Normanna e spiaggia di Salicerchie

History and architecture · Towers · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Saracen Tower, an ancient defensive spot, connected to beaches and coves reachable also by sea; beyond the tower you go down with 160 steps to Salicerchie beach. In the hamlet of Erchie stands another tower, the Cerniola; later on the beach of the Dead Horse is not accessible due to the risk of landslides and you can only admire from above.

  • Where Torre Normanna, Maiori
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
