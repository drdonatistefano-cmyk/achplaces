What to see in Castelmagno

Town of villages at the top of the Grana Valley, in the Southern Cozie Alps, home of the DOP cheese: the San Magno sanctuary with the fifteenth-century frescoes by Pietro da Saluzzo, two small museums of mountain life, the Way of St. Magnus and the Colle Fauniera with the monument to Marco Pantani.

5 places to visit in Castelmagno, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario di San Magno

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

At 1760 meters, in an area formerly dedicated to the cult of Mars, it was a hospice for travellers between Valle Grana and Valle Stura; the complex combines an ancient part and an extension between the Seven and the Nineteenth Century. The fifteenth-century frescoes attributed to Pietro da Saluzzo depict evangelists and doctors of the Church, the Passion and the holy martyrs of the Thebes. Open on Saturdays and Sundays until June, every day from June to October (indicatively 9-18); guided tours on Sundays in July and August at 14:30 (5 €). On 19 August, for the patron saint's feast, the Baia di Castelmagno parade.

  • Where Via Don Giacomo Mascarello 1, Castelmagno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Piccolo museo della vita di quassù (Pichot muzeou d'la vita d'isi)

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Ethnographic Museum in the hamlet Colletto: objects and tools of everyday life in a set-up of other times, each documented and described in Occitan and Italian. Open every day from July to September, with guided tours on Saturday and Sunday from 16:30 to 18:30.

  • Where Frazione Colletto, Castelmagno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Muzeou dal travai

Museums · Themed

In a house of 1684, about 250 objects once used by the small community of Castelmagno, in the upper Grana Valley.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitcuneese.it

Cammino di San Magno

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient mule track recovered that starts from Campomolino and climbs to the sanctuary passing through the villages Nero, Chiotti and Chiappi, through a basin rich in vegetation.

  • Where Campomolino, Castelmagno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Colle Fauniera e monumento a Marco Pantani

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valico at 2481 meters, one of the most challenging climbs faced by the Giro d'Italia, where stands the monument dedicated to the cyclist Marco Pantani; around the high valley pastures where Castelmagno DOP is produced.

  • Where Colle Fauniera, Castelmagno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
