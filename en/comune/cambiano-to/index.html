Munlab - Ecomuseo dell'Argilla

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Born to pass on the knowledge related to the processing of clay, since 2001 it deals with the memory of the places, regeneration of furnaces and quarries, signs of man in the landscape, design in clay and art; it also includes an oasis in a renaturalized quarry, habitats of wild species. It offers guided tours, handicraft workshops and experiences. Open every day only by reservation (349 1396240); free for under 14, over 65 and Museum Subscription holders.

  • Where Via Camporelle 50, Cambiano
  • Entry €10, reduced €8
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.munlabtorino.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · munlabtorino.it

Pininfarina Collection

Local life and traditions · Motors

At the headquarters of Cambiano, where in 1982 the Pininfarina Research and Studies Centre was established, the company visit shows unique pieces, prototypes, standard cars, cabriolets and coupes documenting the company's contributions to aerodynamics, safety and aesthetics, as well as industrial design objects such as the Olympic torch of Turin 2006. Company visit, partially accessible.

  • Where Via Nazionale 30, Cambiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.munlabtorino.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · munlabtorino.it

Installazione diffusa «Con altri occhi»

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

160 small portraits in terracotta hidden between tiles, cornices and branches of the country, realized in 2018 by 30 participants and by inhabitants for ...Change as Montmartre , curated by the artist Juan Sandoval with Munlab and the support of the City.

  • Where Centro storico, Cambiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.munlabtorino.it

Sources: munlabtorino.it
