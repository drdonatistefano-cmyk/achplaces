What to see in Cento

The town of Guercino, 25 km from Bologna: porticoed centre, the Pinacoteca reopened after the earthquake and the Carnival.

3 places to visit in Cento, in the province of Ferrara (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Civica Pinacoteca Il Guercino

Museums · Old masters

Opened after eleven years of closure for the 2012 earthquake: 120 works, 46 drawings and 20 frescoes by Guercino, with Scarsellino, Guido Reni and Ludovico Carracci.

  • Where Cento
  • Entry To be checked on the Pinacoteca website
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.civicapinacotecailguercino.it

Sources: civicapinacotecailguercino.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Piazza Guercino e centro porticato

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The square with the Governor's Palace and the statue of the painter, between the long Padan arcades and the Borgatti Theatre of the nineteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Guercino, Cento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Rocca di Cento

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortress of the fifteenth century strengthened by Cardinal Giuliano della Rovere, future Pope Julius II.
