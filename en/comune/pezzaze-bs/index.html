What to see in Pezzaze

The municipality of the Upper Trompia Valley is linked to the mining tradition: you can visit by train and on foot the galleries of the Marzoli Mine with the Museum . The World of Miners and the Iron Art , the ORMA Museum in the ancient Broletto of Mondaro and the church of Sant'Apollonio Vetere with frescoes of the Renaissance of Brescia.

Miniera Marzoli e museo «Il Mondo dei Minatori e l'Arte del Ferro»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Iron and fluorite mine opened in 1886 and closed in 1972, among the first realizations of industrial archaeology museum of the valley. A small train leads to the heart of the mountain, then you walk along with the guides for about 700 meters of tunnels between the explosive's reserve, the room of the geodes covered with crystals, the altar of Santa Barbara and the system of the flying machine; silhouettes of miners and visual reproductions and sound animate the side galleries. The visit (about 1 hour and 30) includes the external museum with exhibitions, exhibits and the Mine Archive; inside there are about 12 °C and 95% humidity, serving warm clothes and closed shoes.

open from March to December on limited days (Sundays, Saturdays in summer, every day 4-24 August) with mandatory reservation according to the Montana Community

  • Where Via Miniera 14, Pezzaze
  • Entry from €10
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.miniereinvaltrompia.com

Sources: miniereinvaltrompia.com · in-lombardia.it · cm.valletrompia.it · visitbrescia.it

Museo ORMA - Broletto di Mondaro

Museums · Archaeological

First archaeological museum of the Trompia Valley, opened in 2018 in the ancient Broletto di Mondaro, fifteenth century building with exposed stone tower that the excavation of 1996 dates after 1220, with portal fourteenth century on the ground floor. It exhibits finds and reproductions on the origins of the valley, coming from the Superintendence and the Civic Museums of Brescia; in the medieval tower the ethnographic collection of Costanzo Caim with agricultural tools of the nineteenth and twentieth centuries. It offers educational workshops.

  • Where Frazione Mondaro, Via San Giovanni 1, Pezzaze
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museoorma.it

Sources: museoorma.it · visitbrescia.it · in-lombardia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Apollonio Vetere

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church dedicated to St. Apollonius, the fourth bishop of Brescia, whose relics were then transferred to the New Cathedral. During maintenance work in 1958 under eighteenth-century layers of lime frescoes were discovered considered important for the Renaissance of Brescia.

  • Where Pezzaze
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
