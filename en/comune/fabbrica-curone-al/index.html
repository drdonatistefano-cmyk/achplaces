What to see in Fabbrica Curone

Municipality of the high Val Curone made of many villages: in the capital the Romanesque-Gothic parish of Santa Maria Assunta, in Lunassi and Morigliassi two museums of the peasant civilization with free entrance, in Caldirola the plateaus of Gioia between the peaks of Giarolo, Panà, Ebro and Chiappo.

5 places to visit in Fabbrica Curone, in the province of Alessandria (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church presented by the municipality as a Romanesque-Gothic parish church; according to the archival documents cited by the parish priest in a pastoral visit, around 1200 was officiated by a chapter of canons, and the first building dates back to the previous century. Subsequent pastoral visits record changes and adaptations; wheelchair accessible.

  • Where Via Roma, Fabbrica Curone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.fabbricacurone.al.it

Sources: comune.fabbricacurone.al.it

Museo della Civiltà Contadina di Morigliassi

Museums · Themed · Free entry

About 300 m2 on two floors with over 2,000 original objects, some rare: a kitchen and a room rebuilt with antique furnishings, agricultural tools on the ground floor and, on the upper floor, hunting, transport, weights and measurements, lights and lanterns, woodworking, wool, butter, cheese, wine and honey, with the workshops rebuilt of carpenter, shoemaker and blacksmith. Open all year round; the second floor is reached only by stairs.

  • Where Frazione Morigliassi, Fabbrica Curone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.fabbricacurone.al.it

Sources: comune.fabbricacurone.al.it

Museo della Civiltà Contadina di Lunassi

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Instruments, tools, machinery, documents and objects of life in a difficult land, ordered in 26 sections with more than 600 exhibits, of which about half is exposed; a video tells the "carbunein" , the charcoal for the charcoal that the Circolo Lunasse proposes during its holidays. Open from 1 April to 30 September at 10-19, from October to March on request; access by stairs.

  • Where Frazione Lunassi, Fabbrica Curone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.fabbricacurone.al.it

Sources: comune.fabbricacurone.al.it

Caldirola e i pianori della Gioia

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Caldirola (1,012 m), cited in documents of about 1190, rises on a rocky spur; above, at the Gioia (1,299 m), in the fifties was born the only winter sports station of the province. A chairlift also active in summer, or the mule tracks, lead to the vast plateaus between 1,500 and 1,700 m between the peaks of Giarolo, Panà, Ebro and Chiappo, with wide views.

  • Where Frazione Caldirola, Fabbrica Curone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.fabbricacurone.al.it

Sources: comune.fabbricacurone.al.it

Le borgate dell'alta Val Curone

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The municipality brings together numerous villages: Bruggi (1.023 m), the highest center on the right of the Curone, parish since about 1643 and largely destroyed by two fires in 1866; Forotondo, with a chapel of Santa Margherita already existing in 1069 and autonomous municipality until 1928; Samoni, whose old church was destroyed by a landslide in 1854; Selvapiana, panoramic view at the foot of Mount Bogleglio.

  • Where Frazioni Bruggi, Forotondo, Salogni, Selvapiana, Fabbrica Curone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.fabbricacurone.al.it

Sources: comune.fabbricacurone.al.it
