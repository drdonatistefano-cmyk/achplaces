What to see in Falerone

Borgo del Fermano rises next to the Roman center of Falerio Picinus, founded in 29 BC and monumentalized by the Augustan age: today it remains the archaeological park with the Roman theatre, among the best preserved of the Marches, and the Archaeological Civic Museum. After the earthquake of 2016 historic center, monuments and municipal museums are partially usable.

Parco archeologico di Falerio Picenus e Teatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The park, along the State Road 210, covers about 30 hectares with the urban area of the Roman city, the necropolis and some suburban villas. The theatre, of Augustan age, is isolated in the countryside under the shadow of an oak tree, at the bottom of a short driveway: it has a cavea of 49.20 m of diameter and preserves, thanks to recent restorations, the average and the caveaima, part of the scenic building and the proscenium with circular and rectangular niches. About the middle of the second century A.D. was embellished with statues donated by Antonia Picentina, priestess of the Diva Faustina; it still hosts summer theater seasons and the TAU/Ancient United Theaters.

Museo Civico Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

Housed in the premises of the former convent of the Franciscan friars next to the church of San Fortunato, about two kilometers from the park, exhibits materials of daily life, handicraft productions, cippi and funerary are, statues and architectural sculptures and inscriptions on administration and public works of Falerio; there are statues of Augustian age coming from the theatre.

after the earthquake of 2016 municipal museums are partially usable (Let's Marche)

Chiesa di San Fortunato (San Francesco)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Already church of Santa Maria, the Franciscans moved there in 1292 together with the annexed monastery; it was renamed St. Fortunato in 1352, but the Fallerones still call it St. Francis.

closed due to the earthquake of 2016 (Let's Marche)

Chiesa della Madonna degli Angeli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded in 1542 on a hill near the village by the Clare monks who left the convent of the Piagge; above the high altar there is a fresco by Vincenzo Pagani.
