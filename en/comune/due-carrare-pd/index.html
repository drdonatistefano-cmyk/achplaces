What to see in Due Carrare

A few kilometres from the spas of Abano and Montegrotto, the Castle of San Pelagio a Due Carrare, with its fourteenth-century Carrara tower and from which Gabriele D'Annunzio left on 9 August 1918 for the flight on Vienna, houses the Museum of Air and Space and a park with labyrinth, while in the municipality the Abbey of Santo Stefano houses the sarcophagus of Marsilio I da Carrara.

Castello di San Pelagio - Museo dell'Aria e dello Spazio

Museums · Themed

Castle of medieval origin (around 1340, as shown by the watchtower of Carrara), also called Villa Zaborra from the counts that adapted it to villa in 1775, museum since 1980; during the restorations a Roman spa has emerged. The museum tells the story of the flight from Leonardo's drawings to space exploration with over 300 models of planes, airships and balloons, original uniforms, engines and relics, including the Lindbergh plane for the Atlantic crossing and, in the cellars, the space section with models of the LEM and Space Shuttle Columbia, the Solar Sailing of the University of Padua and the images of the Giotto probe. On the noble floor, the section dedicated to D'Annunzio, which from here took off on 9 August 1918 with the squadron "La Serenissima" for the flight to Vienna: the frescoed rooms where he lived for a year keep original furniture, uniforms and personal objects.

Giardini del Castello di San Pelagio e Labirinto del Minotauro

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

The castle is surrounded by gardens with English and ancient roses, centuries-old trees, a avenue of centuries-old hornbeams, the brolo (ancient walled garden), the secret garden, a fish pond, a glacier, a belvedere and the labyrinth of Minotauro.

Abbazia e Chiesa di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Abbey founded from 1027 on a small church before 910; the complex was largely demolished in the second half of the eighteenth century and remain the church, the bell tower of 1293 (abbot Allebene), the ancient cemetery, the rectory and a stone well of Istria. Inside the sarcophagus-Mausoleum of Marsilio I da Carrara (first half of the fourteenth century), an ancient pavement mosaic (X-XIII century) and a polychrome terracotta group of the Deposition attributed to Andrea Briosco (XVI century).

Chiesa di San Biagio a Cornegliana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church belonged to the family from Rio; it conserves fourteen bas-reliefs of the Via Crucis in white marble of Carrara by Antonio Bonazza (1750-1760), originally on the outer wall towards the cemetery.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa parrocchiale di Carrara San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1947 with a single nave with bricks from the former airport of San Pelagio; it houses the body of San Clemente and a bronze statue of San Giorgio on horseback, with the bell tower of 1912. The previous church was consecrated in 1776.

Chiesa parrocchiale di Terradura

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Dedicated to the Blessed Virgin Immaculate, cited in a status of Padua of 1276 with its ancient bell tower and consecrated in September 1488 by the bishop of Padua Pietro Barbarigo; frescoes at the high altar are documented in 1572.
