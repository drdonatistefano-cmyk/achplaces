What to see in Rovereto

The City of Peace in Vallagarina: the Mart with the dome of Mario Botta, the Futurist Art House of Depero, the Castle with the War Museum and the Bell of the Fallen that plays every evening.

7 places to visit in Rovereto, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Mart – Museo di arte moderna e contemporanea

Museums · Modern art

Opened since 2002, it can be recognized by Mario Botta's dome, it has a permanent collection of 20,000 works and hosts large temporary exhibitions.

  • Where Rovereto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittrentino.info

Casa d'Arte Futurista Depero

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The house-museum designed by Fortunato Depero, restored by Renato Rizzi: on three floors about 3,000 objects including paintings, posters, furnishings, puppets and tarsias in cloth sewn by his wife Rosetta, with The procession of the great doll of 1920.

  • Where Rovereto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: italia.it · visittrentino.info · mart.tn.it

Castello e Museo Storico Italiano della Guerra

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Late medieval fortress overlooking the valley, home to the museum with uniforms, armor, weapons and documents from the Renaissance to the First World War.

  • Where Rovereto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittrentino.info

Campana dei Caduti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

The bell of peace, 3,36 meters high, merged in 1924 with the bronze of the cannons of the nations in war: every evening it rings 100 chimes for the fallen; it is reached with half an hour on foot from the center.

  • Where Colle di Miravalle, Rovereto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · italia.it

Museo di Scienze e Archeologia

Museums · Science and university

Among the oldest museums in Italy, founded in 1851: archaeology, zoology, botany, astronomy, geoscience and robotics.

  • Where Rovereto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · italia.it

Palazzo Sichardt – Museo della Città

Museums · City history

18th-century Palace of Norimbergese merchants Sichardt, since 2019 a participatory and interactive museum on the history of the oaks.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di San Marco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Here Mozart held his first concert in Italy in 1769; the dedication recalls the Venetian period.

  • Entry Free
