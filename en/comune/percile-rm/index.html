What to see in Percile

Medieval village of stone houses about 600 meters in the high valley of the Aniene, inside the Parco dei Monti Lucretili, with the Palazzo Borghese, the church of Saint Lucia and the Lagustelli, two karst lakes protected by the Ramsar convention.

Lagustelli di Percile (laghi Fraturno e Marraone)

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Two small Karst lakes in the Ustica Valley, at the foot of the Faieta Hill, formed by collapses due to the erosion of groundwater; they are a wetland protected by the Ramsar Convention, one of the six of Lazio. The Fraturno, the largest and the only accessible, has banks with poplars, reeds, horseback rides, willows and small sandy beaches; the Marraone, funnel and about 20 meters lower, can be seen only from above from a fence and in dry summers can dry up. You can reach it on foot or by mountain bike from Percile, Licenza, Mandela, Riofreddo and Cineto Romano.

  • Where Lagustelli, Percile
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Ruderi di Castel del Lago (Colle Morella)

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

A little above Lake Fraturno, on the Morella Hill, the remains of Castel del Lago, a medieval fortress and one of the fortified castra of the Lucretili Mountains.

  • Where Colle Morella, Percile
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village preserves the medieval structure, with narrow alleys, stone houses and panoramic views of the Cimate delle Serre.

  • Entry Free

Chiesa di Santa Lucia e Palazzo Borghese

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The sixteenth-century church of St. Lucia, patron saint of the village, was the private chapel of the lords; next to it stands the Palazzo Borghese, today seat of the Town Hall.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
