What to see in Acri

Large village at the gates of Sila: the Basilica of Sant'Angelo with the remains of Blessed Angelo and its museum, Palazzo Sanseverino-Falcone with the frescoes of the eighteenth century and the MACA dedicated to Silvio Vigliaturo, the church of the Annunziata, the oldest in the city.

7 places to visit in Acri, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Basilica di Sant'Angelo

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Built between 1893 and 1898 on a project by Guido Quercioli and dedicated to the Immaculate Basilica since 1980: facade in Mendicino stone with statues of Ernesto Biondi, two 31-metre bell towers and 35-metre dome, bronze door, a single nave of about 50 meters with a frescoed vault by Vincenzo Montefusco and organ of about 3,000 reeds; in the chapel the bronze urn of Blessed Angel.

  • Where Piazza Beato Angelo d'Acri, Acri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.acri.cs.it · italia.it

Museo del Beato Angelo

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Opened in 1992 in the sanctuary and in the convent of the Capuchins, it is dedicated to the Capuchin Lucantonio Falcone (1669-1739), the Blessed Angel: relics, personal objects, documents and publications about his life, with paintings, statues and ancient texts of the heritage of the order.

  • Where Piazza Beato Angelo d'Acri, Acri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Palazzo Sanseverino-Falcone e MACA

Museums · Modern art

The seventeenth-century palace of the princes of Bisignano, with frescoed rooms attributed to Donato Vitale (1714-1718) and the Sala delle Colonne, donated to the Municipality in the eighties. From 2006 it houses the MACA with 237 works by Silvio Vigliaturo glass master (paintings and sculptures in glass from 1961 to 2004) in 11 halls, exhibitions and workshops, and part of the Museum of Rural Civilization and the Museum of the Risorgimento. Closed on Monday.

  • Where Piazza Giovanni Falcone 1, Acri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museomaca.it

Sources: museomaca.it · comune.acri.cs.it · italia.it
