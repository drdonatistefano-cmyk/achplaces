What to see in Villandro

The village of the Isarco Valley is rich in mining and prehistoric history: the mine, one of the most important in the medieval Tyrol, the Archeoparc del Plunacker inhabited since the Mesolithic and the parish with glass windows of 1520 on the life of the miners; above the village the Villanderer Alm, the second mountain hut of South Tyrol, with the Totenkirchl, Lake Totensee and Mount Villandro, geographical center of the province.

Miniera di Villandro

Museums · Themed

Among the most important mining areas of Tyrol in the Middle Ages, closed at the beginning of the twentieth century and recovered by local associations: 16 galleries for about 20 km, for two thirds excavated by hand, from which were extracted silver, galena, copper and blend, advancing 8-12 meters per year. Guided tour of the Elisabeth gallery (about 1 and a half hours) and, on Wednesdays in July and August, of the Elisabeth and Lorenz galleries (about 2 and a half hours).

only guided tours, season from 6 April to 8 November 2026 (Tuesday and Thursday 10.30 and 14.30, Sunday 10.30); off-season by reservation

  • Where Oberland 36, Villandro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.klausen.it

Sources: klausen.it

Archeoparc Villandro

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Archaeological park at the Plunacker, inhabited without interruption by the Mesolithic, with remains of the Stone, Copper, Bronze and Roman era; typical buildings rebuilt with their furnishings revive the first settlements.

can be visited with the guided tour of the country on Wednesday at 16.30 from May to October; groups on request

  • Where Plunacker, Villandro
  • Entry €3, reduced €1 (6-14 years), free under 6 and with KlausenCard
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.klausen.it

Sources: klausen.it

Parrocchiale dei Santi Stefano e Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Consecrated in its present form in 1521 (an earlier church has been documented since 1234); the 1520 stained glass windows depict scenes of the miners' life. Altars of 1884 and 1903, pulpit of 1867, Madonna of the Baroque Rosary of about 1700 and the canvas of the stoning of St. Stephen by Franz Sebald Unterberger; the neo-Gothic decoration of the vault followed the fire of 1900.

  • Where Villandro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.klausen.it

Sources: klausen.it

Cimitero e chiesa cimiteriale di San Michele

Places of worship · Monumental cemeteries · Free entry

A few meters from the parish church, the church of San Michele, patron saint of the dead, was inaugurated in 1344 and on the lower floor houses the mortuary chapel; the cemetery is described as one of the most suggestive of the region, with wrought iron crosses facing the opposite side of the tombs.

  • Where Villandro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.klausen.it

Sources: klausen.it

Chiesa di San Valentino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Along the road from Chiusa to Villandro, documented from 1303 and rebuilt after a fire around 1400 with Gothic starry vault; a single nave with lateral tower, has a neo-Gothic altar of about 1870 with the two Valentine's Day next to the Madonna and geometric ornaments of the Style Bello.

  • Where Strada Chiusa-Villandro, Villandro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.klausen.it

Sources: klausen.it

Chiesa di San Maurizio a Sauders

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Documented since 1406, renovated in Baroque forms in 1670 and set on fire by French troops in 1793; three times cruising on pillars, the patron's altarpiece between the statues of Saints George, Florian and two bishops, and the Fourteen Saints Helpers in small niches.

  • Where Sauders, Villandro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.klausen.it

Sources: klausen.it

Villanderer Alm

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The second mountain hut in South Tyrol by extension, in the geographical center of the province, with marked paths for families, the thematic path of the mountain pine with demonstration distillery, family-run huts and, in winter, panoramic cross-country trails; 360 degree view of the Dolomites.

  • Where Villanderer Alm, Villandro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.klausen.it

Sources: klausen.it

Totenkirchl e lago Totensee

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Church at 2,186 meters on the mountain hut, in the current form of 1899 and probably built by miners, indicated as perhaps the oldest pilgrimage site in South Tyrol; inside a group of the Crucifixion and the saints in memory of the dead. Below, in the basin, Lake Totensee with wooden beds and views of the Dolomites; Vie Crucis from Villanderer Alm and Val Sarentino go up there.

  • Where Villanderer Alm, Villandro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.klausen.it

Sources: klausen.it

Monte Villandro

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the most panoramic peaks of the Sarentine Alps: climbing through forest roads, huts and grassy slopes passing through the Totenkirchl and the Totensee; on top the cross and a granite globe mark the geographical centre of South Tyrol, with 360 degrees panorama. Excursion from early summer to autumn.

  • Where Monte Villandro, Villandro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.klausen.it

Sources: klausen.it
