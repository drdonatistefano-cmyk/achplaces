What to see in Caramanico Terme

Fortified village about 650 meters on the western side of Majella, between the rivers Orta and Orfento, known for the sulphurous thermal baths active from the early twentieth century, the medieval churches and the reserve of the Orfento Valley.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified village on a ridge at about 650 meters, with elegant mansions, carved portals, fountains, flowered balconies and gardens; among the monuments the Romanesque church of St. Thomas the Apostle, the church of St. Dominic with acute portal, the church of St. Nicholas with entrance to two rows of columns decorated with plant motifs and, little further, the Fountain of St. Leonard with three masks of the eighteenth century.

Terme di Caramanico

Local life and traditions · Spas

Historic plant named after the La Salute spring, inaugurated on 4 August 1901 after the first rudimentary baths of 1836; sulphurous waters rich in bromine salts and iodine (sources La Salute, Gisella, Pisciarello and Zolfanina, described already in 1576). It offers spa and wellness area with thermal pool, sauna and Turkish bath.

Museo naturalistico archeologico "Paolo Barrasso" e area della lontra

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Founded in 1986 by the State Forestry Corps, it reconstructs the habitats of Majella (beech, rocky walls, mugheta), with sections of geology and fossils, the path "Tana della Lontra" and an archaeological section with finds from the Paleolithic below the Roman age, accessible to blind and visually impaired. Next to it is the fauna area of the Eurasian otter; it is also information center of the Park.

  • Where Via del Vivaio, frazione Santa Croce, Caramanico Terme
  • Entry €2,70, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · parcomajella.it · italia.it

Valle dell'Orfento e Sentiero delle Scalelle

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of about an hour and a half (difficulty E, 120 m of difference in altitude) from Santa Croce: descends steeply to the river Orfento in the woods, follows it towards Ponte di Caramanico on wooden walkways between rocky walls and climbs with steps. The Park recommends that you withdraw the free access to the Carabinieri Biodiversity Visit Centre.

  • Where Santa Croce, Caramanico Terme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcomajella.it · italia.it

Eremo di San Giovanni all'Orfento

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the most famous Celestinian hermitages, perched on almost inaccessible rocky cenes; it is reached with a long and challenging excursion of about 15 km from Decontra, with about 1,000 m of altitude difference, passing from the viewpoint of Piana Grande.
