What to see in Isera

Village of Vallagarina above Rovereto, home of Marzemino, with eighteenth-century frescoes (Fedrigotti, Vannetti, de Probizer with the Wine House), the church of San Vincenzo, the remains of a Roman villa of Augustan age, the postcard museum and the Castel Corno overlooking the Adige.

Museo della cartolina «Salvatore Nuvoli»

Museums · Themed

In Palazzo Galvagni, over 35,000 postcards in 19 categories from 1869 to today, many rare: panoramas, patriotic propaganda, First World War, Austro-Hungarian empire, political satire, with the history of Trento and Bolzano, Italy and Europe. In addition to the cardboard, there are examples in leather, wood and cork, and one of the smallest Cuban postcards in the world. Suitable for families.

  • Where Via Galvagni 10, Isera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · visitrovereto.it

Villa romana di Isera

History and architecture · Archaeology

Urban-rustic villa of Augustan age, at the Lagarian path of the Via Claudia Augusta, discovered during the construction of a childhood school: the urban pars with representative rooms, living rooms and spas decorated with frescoes and mosaics, and the rustic pars for the production activities. The first excavation was conducted in 1973 by Adriano Rigotti; many finds are exhibited at the Museo civico di Rovereto. Accessible to people with motor disabilities.

  • Where Piazza San Vincenzo, Isera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.fondazionemcr.it

Sources: fondazionemcr.it · visittrentino.info

Castel Corno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortified settlement can be reached by car, bike or on foot; in summer it hosts exhibitions, shows and seasonal dining points. From the viewpoint the look reaches the remains of Castel Pradaglia above the Adige.

  • Where Isera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitrovereto.it · visittrentino.info

Palazzo de Probizer e Casa del Vino

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

18th century palace with mythological frescoes, seat of the Wine House; it houses art exhibitions, concerts and cultural events. Around, the hills of vineyards of Marzemino, to be seen at best between the end of September and the beginning of October, in harvest.

  • Where Isera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitrovereto.it · visittrentino.info

Centro storico: chiesa di San Vincenzo e palazzi Fedrigotti e Vannetti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient church of San Vincenzo was rebuilt between 1655 and 1683 and its bell tower would be part of the keep of an ancient castle. Next to it, two eighteenth-century palaces with frescoed rooms: Palazzo Fedrigotti, today the town hall, and Palazzo Vannetti.

  • Where Piazza San Vincenzo, Isera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · visitrovereto.it
