What to see in Orosei

Centre of Baronia on the gulf that bears its name, with a historic center of seventeen consecrated churches, the tower of Sant'Antonio with the church frescoed in the fourteenth century, the district of Palatzos Betzos with the Museum Don Nanni Guiso of miniature theater and a coast of beaches that goes from Marina di Orosei to Cala Liberotto to the oasis of Bidderosa.

Museo Don Nanni Guiso

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Opened in 2000, when Don Nanni (Giovanni) Guiso donated to Orosei the ancient residence of the noble Guiso, then barracks of the Royal Carabinieri, along with his collection: vintage theatres from all over Europe and theatre production from the eighteenth century to today, diorami and graphics of international artists including drawings of the Roman School of 1930-1945, ancient and rare books on Sardinia, dresses and costumes from 1880 to today and works of art from Sienese. From May to September open Monday, Tuesday and Friday 9-13 and Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 17-21; from October to April only by reservation.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Musio 22, Orosei
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Torre e chiesa di Sant'Antonio Abate

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The medieval square tower, much renovated over the centuries, is the rest of the restored castle and dates back to the period when Orosei was the main center of its curatory. In the same enclosure stands the church of Sant'Antonio Abate, in volcanic stone, with a single nave with wooden roof and a cycle of frescoes of the fourteenth century; next, on 16 January, the fires of Saint Anthony light up.

  • Where Piazza Torre di Sant'Antonio Abate, Orosei
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Centro storico e Palatzos Betzos

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The old town has seventeen consecrated churches, including the Baroque parish church of San Giacomo, celebrated on July 25, and the Anime church with a wooden pulpit of the eighteenth century. The Palatzos Betzos district, ancient houses and palaces dating back to the 16th-18th century, with the old Spanish prison, is located around the castle's castle.

  • Where Centro storico, Orosei
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · sardegnacultura.it

Marina di Orosei

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Beach about two kilometers one kilometer from the village along the SS 129, of clear and fine sand mixed with shells and pebbles, with clear water and seabed that descends quickly; the hills of maquis behind frame a pond and the mouth of the Cedrino. Often windy and frequented by surfers; about two miles away lies the wreck of the KT, half a German landing of about 70 meters sunk in 1943. Accessible to the disabled, with equipment rental, large parking suitable for campers, bar and refreshment.

  • Where Marina di Orosei, Orosei
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Cala Liberotto

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Emerald-coloured bay about 14 kilometers from the village, with golden sand with big grain, clear rocks on the sides and three large rocks reachable on foot on the bottom; behind the pond of sos Alinos, with reeds and agaves, where they nest water birds. A pine forest leads to Cala Ginepro and the small beaches of sas Linnas Siccas and sa Curcurica. In summer you can enjoy underwater fishing, surfing in winter; free entrance, parking, rental, refreshment and access for disabled people.

  • Where Cala Liberotto, Orosei
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Oasi di Bidderosa

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Park of 860 hectares entrusted to the Municipality of Orosei, 13 kilometers north of the village to the beach of Berchida: five white sand coves, about one kilometer long each, between granite reliefs, age-old junipers, pine trees, holm oaks and cork. The Curcurica sa lagoon hosts sedentary and migratory birds that can be observed from birdwatching stations. You enter by car from the SS 125 stopping at the command of the Forest Office; on foot or by bike no seasonal limit, in compliance with the park regulations.

entrance by car and motorbike with closed number from May to end of October
