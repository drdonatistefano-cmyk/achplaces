What to see in Manciano

Borgo in the hills in the heart of the Maremma, with the thermal baths of Saturnia and the medieval village of Montemerano in its territory.

4 places to visit in Manciano, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cascate del Mulino e terme di Saturnia

Local life and traditions · Spas · Free entry · Viewpoint

Sulfural water at about 375°C of the ancient Etruscan and Roman settlement of Saturnia; at the Waterfalls of the Mulino natural outdoor pools famous in the world.

  • Where Saturnia, Manciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Montemerano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village surrounded by walls built by Aldobrandeschi, with Piazza del Castello and a fairytale atmosphere: among the most beautiful villages in Italy.

  • Where Montemerano, Manciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Cassero di Manciano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress of the 12th century in the center of the village, municipal seat from the Grand Duchy, with a museum of history and art and the panoramic tower.

  • Where Manciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
