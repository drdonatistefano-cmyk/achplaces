What to see in Oriolo

Borgo dell'Alto Ionio Cosentino on the slopes of the Pollino National Park, about 20 km from the sea, Orange Flag of Touring; it dominates the fifteenth-century castle of the Sanseverino and the Mother Church of San Giorgio of Norman origin, while Palazzo Giannettasio houses the Museum of peasant civilization.

Castello di Oriolo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

15th century castle, the only surviving part of the ancient defenses of the village, with square mast and angular cylindrical towers of the Aragonese era. It belongs to the Sanseverino and recently restored, it preserves the original structure with two watchtowers and the guard body; inside a museum with interactive classrooms, educational and multimedia and a conference room.

  • Where Centro storico, Oriolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa Madre di San Giorgio Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of Norman origin with fifteenth-century portal surmounted by a bas-relief and two lions guarding the central gate, dated 1264. Inside there is a monumental Baroque wooden altar with a mother-of-pearl tabernacle, a fifteenth-century statue of the Madonna and Child, an Ecce Homo in 18th century terracotta, the relics of St. George and St. Francis of Paola, a collection of silver and the crypts of the early church.

  • Where Centro storico, Oriolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Centro storico e palazzi nobiliari

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village, 450 meters above sea level, preserves the 17th century urban system: noble palaces overlooking the main road that connects the residence of the feudalist to the fifteenth century walls, today in ruins.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
