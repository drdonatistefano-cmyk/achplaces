What to see in Lavarone

Common spread of the Alpe Cimbra with the karst lake loved by Sigmund Freud and the Fort Belvedere-Gschwent (1908-1914), between the Austro-Hungarian fortresses best preserved in Trentino, today Museum of the Great War; in Slaghenaufi the Austro-Hungarian military cemetery.

Forte Belvedere-Gschwent

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Austro-Hungarian fortress built between 1908 and 1914 at 1,177 m above the upper Valle dell'Astico, with concrete roof of about 2,10 m, three 10 cm obici in armored domes, 22 machine gun stations and four outposts connected by galleries; the motto was "Per Trento" is enough for me. Buyed by the Municipality in 1997 and restored with the Province, it is a museum on the three floors of the block casematte: system of the forts, war at altitude, medicine of war, rebuilt trench and the multisensory .

  • Where Via Tiroler Kaiserjäger 1, Lavarone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.fortebelvedere.org

Sources: fortebelvedere.org · visittrentino.info · alpecimbra.it

Lago di Lavarone e Sentiero di Freud

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lake about 1,080 m was born over two thousand years ago by the sinking of a karst doline (on the bottom of logs dated to 210 BC), of about 64,000 m2 and 17 m of depth, bathing and several times Blue Flag; half of the Central European aristocracy between the eight and twentieth centuries, it stayed there several times Sigmund Freud. Ring of about 30 minutes suitable for strollers, the Freud Path from the village and two equipped beaches (Bertoldi and Marzari) in summer.

  • Where Lago di Lavarone, Lavarone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Museo del Miele

Museums · Themed

In Tobia, near Gionghi: hives and instruments from various parts of the world, explanations on the hierarchy of the hive and on the dance of the bees, educational workshops and guided tours, with sale of honey of the Highlands and derived products.

  • Where Località Tobia 5, Lavarone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.museodelmiele.com

Sources: museodelmiele.com · visittrentino.info · alpecimbra.it

Cimitero militare di Slaghenaufi

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 1,280 m on the slopes of Mount Cimone, between pastures and firs, 748 crosses on several levels of fallen on the front of the Highlands, with many Hungarian and Bosnian names; next stood the military hospital of the Knights of Malta, and the chapel on the north side was rebuilt in 1954 and restored in 1992. From Bertoldi about 1.5 km, then 300 m walk.

  • Where Slaghenaufi, Lavarone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · alpecimbra.it

Bertoldi e le frazioni

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Bertoldi (1,173 m) is a characteristic village with a maze of narrow streets, fountains, porticoes and ancient passages; near Stengheli and Lanzino, the latter with compact houses arranged in terraced.

  • Where Bertoldi, Lavarone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: alpecimbra.it · visittrentino.info

Chiesetta votiva di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In Ginghi, built in 1878 for the vow made after the cholera of 1855, where before a rock with niche housed a statue of the Madonna; inside the picture of the Madonna exhibited from 1915 to 1918 in the church of Braunau, in Austria, where the population of Lavarone had taken refuge.

  • Where Gionghi, Lavarone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: alpecimbra.it · visittrentino.info
