What to see in San Salvo

Center on the hill above the mouth of the Trigno, born around the medieval abbey: the Quadrilatero Archaeological Park with Roman mosaic and underground aqueduct, and on the coast the marina with 3 km of Blue Flag beach and a FAI botanical garden.

7 places to visit in San Salvo, in the province of Chieti (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parco Archeologico del Quadrilatero e Museo Porta della Terra

Museums · Archaeological

The square-plan urban space of the historic centre, born in the 13th century with the abbey of Saints Vito and Salvo on a late-Roman settlement; the Museo Civico Porta della Terra collects Roman and Frentani finds, the Museo dell'Abbazia the monastic life between Trigno and Osento.

  • Where Piazza San Vitale, San Salvo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: italia.it · turismo.abruzzo.it

Isola archeologica del mosaico romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Mosaic of the centuries I-III A.D. with branches of lives coming out from amphorae, between walls in cross-linked work and brick.

  • Where Piazza San Vitale, San Salvo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: italia.it · turismo.abruzzo.it

Isola archeologica del chiostro

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Remains of the medieval cloister in an area that also includes a Roman domus with impluvium and central well.

  • Where Piazza San Vitale, San Salvo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: italia.it · turismo.abruzzo.it

Acquedotto romano ipogeo

History and architecture · Archaeology

Underground Roman aqueduct about seven meters deep under the old town, still in operation: it feeds the Old and New fountains.

Chiesa di San Giuseppe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In Piazza San Vitale, built on the ancient abbey of San Salvo: single nave interior with chapels, baroque and neoclassical appearance, and in the crypt traces of the medieval monastery.

  • Where Piazza San Vitale, San Salvo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it · italia.it

Giardino Botanico Mediterraneo

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

About 5 hectares between via Paolucci and the northern promenade, recovered in the 2000s from a former parking area: plants and animals of the Mediterranean coast, with marsh turtle and earth turtle.

  • Where Lungomare Nord C. Colombo, San Salvo Marina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · turismo.abruzzo.it

Spiaggia di San Salvo Marina

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

About 3 km of sandy beaches with gently degrading seabeds, Blue Flag waters, free and equipped stretches and an animated summer promenade.

  • Where San Salvo Marina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it
