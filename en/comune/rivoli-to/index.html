What to see in Rivoli

On the morainic hill at the entrance of the Valley of Susa, the unfinished Sabauda residence of Juvarra, which since 1984 houses the first museum of contemporary art in Italy.

3 places to visit in Rivoli, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Rivoli – Museo d'Arte Contemporanea

Museums · Modern art · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Fortress, palace remained unfinished on the project of Juvarra, barracks and then museum: since 1984 the first museum of contemporary art of Italy with masterpieces of the Poor Art, the Long Channel and since 2019 the Cerruti Collection (Picasso, De Chirico, Kandinsky, Bacon). From the panoramic sport the view of Turin. UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  • Where Piazza Mafalda di Savoia, Rivoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website turismotorino.org
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: turismotorino.org · castellodirivoli.org
