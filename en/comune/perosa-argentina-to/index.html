What to see in Perosa Argentina

Country at the meeting between Val Chisone and Val Germanasca, which grew around the textile industry since the nineteenth century: the Ecomuseum tells the story of spinning, silk mill and cotton mill with the route 'From wire to wire, while the anti-aircraft shelters, the exhibition on the Resistance and the remains of the seventeenth-century fort at the Park of Peace remind the story.

Ecomuseo delle attività industriali (EMIT) e percorso «Di filo in filo»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The association, founded in 1996, has at its headquarters a documentation centre with panels, photographs, documents and machinery on the history and production processes of the perosine textile industries: the mill Bolmida of 1835, the silk waste silk factory started by Berthelot in 1870 and then taken over by the Gütermanns, the cotton factory of Jenny and Ganzoni of 1883. The route is about 3 km long, with ten panels in four languages, and crosses the country between working residences, boarding houses, kindergartens, after-work and hydroelectric power stations. Self-guided tour of about 2 hours; guided tours by reservation.

  • Where Loc. San Sebastiano 33, Perosa Argentina
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.ecomuseoperosa.it

Sources: ecomuseoperosa.it · turismotorino.org · comune.perosaargentina.to.it

Rifugi antiaerei

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

World War II shelters: one, from 1941, could accommodate from 61 to 82 people; the one in Via Roma, from 1943-44, had a capacity of 344 people and a fireplace of 17 meters. The tour, guided only and with sound effects, is held in Italian, English, French and German for groups up to 25 people, by reservation at Discoverminiera; the refuges of Via Roma are fully accessible by wheelchair.

  • Where Parco E. Gay, via Re Umberto 12, e via Roma 63, Perosa Argentina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.perosaargentina.to.it

Sources: comune.perosaargentina.to.it

Mostra sulla Resistenza

Museums · City history · Free entry

Photographic and documentary exhibition set up in an octagonal building in Park E. Gay, dedicated to the local partisan war and German extermination camps. Guided tour only by reservation (tel. 0121 81384); not accessible by wheelchair.

  • Where Parco E. Gay, Perosa Argentina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.perosaargentina.to.it

Sources: comune.perosaargentina.to.it

Parco della Pace, Borgata Forte e Faro della Libertà

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Above the Park of Peace, an easy walk on an asphalt road leads to Borgata Forte, where walls and urban layout of the Forte de La Perosa of the 17th century, with a well, a cannonball and the entrance arch. A little further, at the Lighthouse of Freedom dedicated to the fallen of the partisan struggle, the remains of a round tower of the castle of Poggio Oddone, probably before the sixteenth century, were re-emerged in 2002. Here is also the red Big Bench, from which the look dominates the lower Val Chisone and the village.

  • Where Loc. Poggio Oddone, Parco della Pace, Perosa Argentina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.perosaargentina.to.it

Sources: comune.perosaargentina.to.it · turismotorino.org

Bec Dauphin e Borgata Ciapella

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ruin of Bec Dauphin is what remains of an ancient military garrison, probably before the fortress of Vitozzi. Higher up, in Borgata Ciapella, a boina with the acronyms P and M marked the ancient border between Perosa and Meano; here probably stood the fortress built in 1597 and dismantled in 1796. Free access.

Canale Rossetto

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

A flat cycle path along the canal, from the Plan de la Tour (Palaplan) pavilion to the Black Earth Mill, a graphite mill near Bec Dauphin, for which there is a redevelopment project.

  • Where Palaplan, Perosa Argentina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
