What to see in Termini Imerese

The thermal town on the Tyrrhenian Sea, founded as Thermai by the Carthaginians in 407 BC: the Civic Museum of the Baldassare Romano in an ancient hospital of the fourteenth century, the seventeenth-century Cathedral, the Municipal Villa with the remains of the Roman curia, the spa with the Grand Hotel and, in Buonfornello, the archaeological area of the Greek colony of Himera.

Area archeologica e Antiquarium di Himera

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Greek colony founded in 648 B.C. by Greeks of calcidese and Doric origin at the mouth of the Northern Impera, the most western of the north coast of Sicily, destroyed by the Carthaginians in 409 B.C.: remain the high and low city, archaic shrines, the necropolis and the Temple of Victory, erected after the battle of 480 B.C. The Antiquarium of Franco Minissi (opened in 1984) exhibits on four levels the excavations, with the gold foil with Gorgone from the Shrine of Athena and the Golden Phial of Caltavuturo (IV-III century BC); the Museum Pirro Marconi (2016) is dedicated to the Western necropolis and the Temple of Victory.

Museo civico «Baldassare Romano»

Museums · City history

Founded in 1873 in the building of one of the oldest hospitals in Sicily, established in 1376 by the will of Gregorio Saglimpi and then entrusted to the Fatebenefratelli. It exhibits prehistoric finds, Greeks from Himera, Romans (a series of marble togates, mosaics, imperial portraits), Arabs and Normans with an inscription in kufic characters; in the artistic section the fifteenth-century polyptych of Gaspare da Pesaro, a Flemish Annunciation of the sixteenth century, a sketch of St. Jerome attributed to Bernini, works by Vincenzo La Barbera and Pietro Novelli, then the nineteenth-century termite, sculptor Filippo Sgarlata and a frescoed chapel of the fifteenth century.

Terme e Grand Hotel delle Terme

Local life and traditions · Spas

The spa of the seventeenth century rises on the ruins of an older one and uses hyperthermal volcanic waters at almost 43 °C for baths and mud; in the nineteenth century there was added the Grand Hotel delle Terme, neoclassic, designed by Damiani Almejda, loved by the Palermo nobility and base of Ignazio Florio during the Targa Florio.
