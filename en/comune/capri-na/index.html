What to see in Capri

The island of the Faraglioni and Piazzetta: the imperial villa of Tiberio on Mount Tiberio, the 14th century Certosa of San Giacomo with the archaeological museum, the Gardens of Augustus above Via Krupp and the villas of the great foreign guests of the 20th century.

8 places to visit in Capri, in the province of Naples (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Capri in the guide with map and filters

Villa Jovis (Palazzo di Tiberio)

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The largest of the twelve residences of Tiberius, mentioned by Tacitus, 334 meters on Mount Tiberius, at the eastern end of the island: in the center a large square with cisterns, an atrium with columns of chives, the thermal baths with apsed calidary and, on the summit to the north, the private apartments of the emperor with the terrace on the Gulf.

  • Where Viale Amedeo Maiuri, Capri
  • Entry €6 full, €2 EU citizens 18-25 years old, free under 18
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.capritourism.com
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: museicapri.cultura.gov.it · capritourism.com

Certosa di San Giacomo e Museo archeologico

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry

A monastery from the 14th century, which later became a prison, now houses the museum dedicated to the painter Karl Wilhelm Diefenbach, the National Archaeological Museum of Capri and art exhibitions.

  • Where Via Certosa, Capri
  • Entry €10 full, €2 EU citizens 18-25 years old, free under 18
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.capritourism.com

Sources: capritourism.com · museicapri.cultura.gov.it

Giardini di Augusto

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Public gardens with Mediterranean vegetation and views of Marina Piccola, Via Krupp and the Faraglioni.

  • Where Viale Giacomo Matteotti 14, Capri
  • Entry €2.50; free up to 12 years
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.capritourism.com

Sources: capritourism.com

Museo del Centro Caprense Ignazio Cerio

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

In the house of the doctor and mayor Ignazio Cerio, more than 20,000 naturalistic and archaeological finds of the island.

  • Where Piazzetta Ignazio Cerio 5, Capri
  • Entry €4 full, €2 18-25 years, free under 18
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.capritourism.com

Sources: capritourism.com

Villa Lysis (Villa Fersen)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Liberty villa built in 1905 on a cliff overlooking the sea, now owned by the City of Capri.

  • Where Via Lo Capo 65, Capri
  • Entry €8 full, €6 reduced; free up to 12 years
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.capritourism.com

Sources: capritourism.com

La Piazzetta e il centro di Capri

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The central square of the island, between alleys, shops and outdoor cafes; from here Via Vittorio Emanuele leads to Via Tragara.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Capri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.capritourism.com

Sources: capritourism.com

Belvedere di Via Tragara e Faraglioni

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Walking along Via Tragara you arrive at the viewpoint from which you can see the Faraglioni, the symbol rocks of the island, admirable also from the sea with the boat tour.

  • Where Via Tragara, Capri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.capritourism.com

Sources: capritourism.com

Marina Piccola e Via Krupp

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Beach among the cliffs, alternative to Marina Grande; from the Gardens of Augusto descends the panoramic Via Krupp.

  • Where Marina Piccola, Capri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.capritourism.com

Sources: capritourism.com
