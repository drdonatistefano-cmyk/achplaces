What to see in Bettola

Val Nure village at 329 meters, formed by two villages on the sides of the stream, San Bernardino and San Giovanni, traditionally linked to the family of Christopher Columbus: the tower-museum of Pradello, the statue in Piazza Colombo, the sanctuary of Our Lady of Quercia and the Farnese tower.

8 places to visit in Bettola, in the province of Piacenza (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre Colombo e Museo colombiano

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Stone tower house built around 1200 for defensive and residential purposes in the agricultural hamlet of Pradello; originally 28 meters high, it was lowered at the end of the nineteenth century to avoid its collapse. According to some scholars it belonged to the family of Christopher Columbus before the transfer to Genoa, and the documents indicate it as property of the grandfather. In 1957 he bought it and restored it as a Colombian Museum Molinari, Piacenza emigrated and then director of the Waldorf Astoria in New York; today he is from the Municipality and is visited only by reservation (328 8714929). From the museum starts a path towards Ebbio, in the red lands.

  • Where Pradello, 29021 Bettola (PC)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.bettola.pc.it

Sources: comune.bettola.pc.it · visitpiacenza.it

Statua di Cristoforo Colombo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Monument erected in 1892, for the four hundred years of the discovery of America, in the main square of the village of San Giovanni, to remember the bond of the village with the family of Colombo.

  • Where Piazza Colombo, Bettola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.bettola.pc.it

Sources: visitpiacenza.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · comune.bettola.pc.it

Santuario della Beata Vergine della Quercia

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Church of San Giovanni, completed in 1884 in neo-Romanesque style on a project by William of the Cell to commemorate a Marian apparition of 1496; facade with white and black stone bands, frescoes by Luciano Ricchetti, sculptures by Paolo Perotti (1970) and Giorgio Groppi (1987) and a wrought iron gate by the craftsman Leonardi of Grazzano. From the end of August to the end of September, the statue of Our Lady descends from the throne to the presbytery, and then goes back to the rite of the "risalita" .

  • Where Piazza Colombo, Bettola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.bettola.pc.it

Sources: visitpiacenza.it · comune.bettola.pc.it

Cappella dell'Apparizione

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Chapel built in 1954 in Colle dei Frati, just outside Bettola, where according to tradition in 1496 the Virgin appeared to a shepherd among the branches of an oak; after the apparition there arose a church and a Franciscan monastery. Inside a mosaic by Luciano Ricchetti with the scene of the apparition; from April 2026 next door there are a reception center for pilgrims, a screening room and the reconstruction of the house of the pastorella.

  • Where Colle dei Frati, Bettola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.bettola.pc.it

Sources: visitpiacenza.it · comune.bettola.pc.it

Chiesa di San Bernardino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

17th century parish church dedicated to Saint Bernardino da Siena, which gives its name to one of the two villages of the village, on the right of the Nure; it preserves stuccoes and frescoes by G.B. Galluzzi of 1716.

  • Where Borgo San Bernardino, Bettola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.bettola.pc.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · comune.bettola.pc.it

Torre Farnese

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Tower erected in 1540 by Pope Paul III Farnese to calm the struggles between the factions of the Nicelli and the Camia; it rises about 2 km from the village in the direction of Bramaino, on the hill above Bettola.

  • Where Verso Bramaiano, Bettola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.bettola.pc.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · comune.bettola.pc.it

Castello della Caminata

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortilizio dei Nicelli with loggia in Bramaino, 440 meters; it is believed to be of the tenth century, but the first written documents date back to the fifteenth century.

  • Where Bramaiano, Bettola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.bettola.pc.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · comune.bettola.pc.it

Paese dei due borghi e ponte sul Nure

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

An interesting urban centre formed by the villages of San Bernardino and San Giovanni, on the two banks of the Nure, joined by a picturesque bridge; a lively village especially in summer, where you can taste the Bettolese village De.Co., a water pancake and flour served with cold cuts and cheeses.

  • Where Bettola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.bettola.pc.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · comune.bettola.pc.it
