What to see in Bosco Marengo

The birthplace of Pope Pius V (Antonio Ghislieri, 1504-1572), the only Piedmontese pontiff, who built the Dominican complex of Santa Croce in 1566 with works by Giorgio Vasari and today the seat of a museum; in the center remain his birthplace and the parish of Saints Peter and Pantaleone.

Complesso di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Church and Dominican convent built by Pius V since 1566 in his native country, with the participation of Giorgio Vasari: example of Roman Mannerism in northern Italy, with two cloisters, the library modeled by San Marco in Florence, works from Vasari to Moncalvo and the mausoleum of Pius V (the remains are in Rome). Museo di Santa Croce

Museums · Themed

In the monumental complex, with entrance to the left of the church: reopened on July 7, 2022 with a path designed by the architect Loredana Iacopino, which tells the complex and the Roman and Florentine art of the sixteenth century with lights, sounds, images and interactive audiovisual. Open Saturday and Sunday (16-19 in summer, 15-18 in winter).

Casa natale di Pio V

Museums · House museums

Home where Antonio Ghislieri was born on 17 January 1504 and lived until 1518, when he entered among the Dominicans of Voghera: four rooms, central staircase, porch with farmhouse, courtyard with well and stable; in 1936 it was furnished in sixteenth-century style and the birth room on the upper floor became chapel. Visitable only by telephone reservation.

  • Where Piazza Castelvecchio 1, Bosco Marengo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: comune.boscomarengo.al.it · alexala.it
