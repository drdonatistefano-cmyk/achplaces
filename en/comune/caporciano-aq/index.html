What to see in Caporciano

Fortified village of the plateau of Navelli, with the castle and the restored fourteenth-century tower; in the hamlet of Bominaco the oratory of San Pellegrino, entirely frescoed in the thirteenth century, and the abbey church of Santa Maria Assunta, national monument since 1902.

Oratorio di San Pellegrino a Bominaco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises where, between the third and fourth centuries, the martyr Saint Pellegrino was buried; the frescoes of the thirteenth century entirely cover vaults and walls. You visit by calling the number indicated on the gate.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta a Bominaco

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Church of the ancient monastery, mentioned already in 1027, before 1180 and 1223, dates engraved on the ambo and on the altar; declared national monument in 1902. You can visit with the same service as the oratory of San Pellegrino.

  • Where Bominaco, Caporciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.caporciano.aq.it

Sources: comune.caporciano.aq.it · cultura.gov.it

Castello di Caporciano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified enclosure of the end of the 12th century, enlarged in the 16th and the second half of the 17th century: the main tower, about 19 meters high and now bell tower, was restored between 1990 and 1995; two original doors and three towers transformed into houses remain. Free access, with views of Gran Sasso, Maiella and Sirente.

  • Where Caporciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.caporciano.aq.it

Sources: comune.caporciano.aq.it
