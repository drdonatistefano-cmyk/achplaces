What to see in Sarsina

4 places to visit in Sarsina, in the province of Forlì-Cesena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Sarsina in the guide with map and filters

Museo Archeologico Nazionale di Sarsina

Museums · Archaeological

The findings of the Roman city of Sassina, home of Plauto, including large funeral monuments.

  • Where Via Cesio Sabino 39, Sarsina
  • Entry To be checked on the website of the Ministry of Culture
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Basilica di San Vicinio e Piazza Plauto

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

The Romanesque cathedral of Bishop Vicinio, a pilgrim's destination, overlooking the square dedicated to the playwright.

  • Where Piazza Plauto, Sarsina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo diocesano d'Arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

Next to the basilica of San Vicino, vestments, liturgical objects and paintings of the parishes of the ancient diocese of Sarsina.

  • Where Palazzo vescovile, Sarsina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Monumento funerario di Murcius Obulaccus

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

The funerary kiosk monument with pyramidal cover of the early Augustian age, rebuilt in a public garden at the entrance of the village, from the necropolis of Pian di Bezzo.

  • Where Ingresso del paese, Sarsina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
