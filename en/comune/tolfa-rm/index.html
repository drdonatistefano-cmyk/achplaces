What to see in Tolfa

Borgo dei Monti della Tolfa, Cittaslow known for its leather processing, with the medieval centre clinging to the hill of the Fortress: the ruins of the castle Frangipane and the sanctuary of the Fortress, the palaces born with the alum, the sanctuary of the Madonna della Sughera with the Civic Museum, over 17,000 hectares of hills and one of the most beautiful skys in Italy.

6 places to visit in Tolfa, in the province of Rome (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca: castello dei Frangipane e santuario della Madonna della Rocca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

On Mount della Rocca the ruins of the castle of the Frangipane, medieval fortress last bulwark of defence for centuries, that protected the Tolfetani also from the Napoleonic army; next, the sanctuary of the Fortress, built to protect the small church from above, with a panoramic churchyard on the village and hills.

  • Where Monte della Rocca, Tolfa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: comune.tolfa.rm.it · visitlazio.com

Centro storico, Piazza Vecchia e i palazzi dell'allume

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Stone houses on the hill of the Fortress with plant around the Mille; in via Frangipani the Palazzaccio, ancient Palazzo dei Priori with the clock tower, and the Baronial Palace where lived Pietro and Ludovico Frangipane in the fifteenth century; the church of Sant'Egidio has the apse in the ancient walls. At the foot of the village Piazza Vecchia (Piazza Matteotti), from which the village grew after the discovery of the alum, and the Panetti palaces (courtyard with octagonal cistern), Buttaoni (loggia on three levels) and Celli.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Tolfa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: comune.tolfa.rm.it · visitlazio.com

Museo civico di Tolfa

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 1954 and re-arranged in the former monastery of the Augustinians, restored and reopened in April 2001: Etruscan finds of the necropolis of Pian della Conserva and Riserva del Ferrone with a rebuilt funeral chamber, votive of the sanctuary of Grasceta dei Cavallari, an amphora black figures attributed to the Tolfa Master (VI century BC), Roman materials of the rustic villas and ceramics and medieval coins; a section tells the era of the alum. Audioguide available; tel. 0766 92127.

  • Where Largo XV Marzo 1799, Tolfa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.tolfa.rm.it · visitlazio.com

Santuario della Madonna della Sughera

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Wanted by Agostino Chigi, the noble Sienese who at the end of the fifteenth century had rented the alum quarries; next, in the sixteenth-century convent of the Augustinian Fathers, the Cultural Pole with Civic Museum, municipal library and historical archive is located.

  • Where Tolfa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: comune.tolfa.rm.it · visitlazio.com

Monti della Tolfa

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 17,000 hectares between the Tyrrhenian and the river Mignone's bends, which are just over 600 meters long: pastures and forests of Mediterranean maquis, chestnut and beech, with wild orchids and rare raptors; the unpolluted sky is certified by Astronomitaly among the most beautiful skies of Italy.

  • Where Monti della Tolfa, Tolfa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: comune.tolfa.rm.it · visitlazio.com

Big Bench n. 291

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Giant fuchsia bench between the green and large trees, with 360° views of the hills and the village with the Fortress; you can reach following the signs "rainbow" in the area of Macchiosi - Grasceta dei Cavallari, destination of hikers and picnics.

  • Where Macchiosi - Grasceta dei Cavallari, Tolfa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
