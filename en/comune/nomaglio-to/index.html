What to see in Nomaglio

A small village with a short detour with a view of the plain, known for the Ecomuseum of Castagna, with the ancient water mill still working, and for the Fontana 'l Burnel, a prehistoric coppellate mass.

2 places to visit in Nomaglio, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Nomaglio in the guide with map and filters

Mulino superiore ed Ecomuseo della Castagna

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Ecomuseum spread born from the municipal project of recovery of material culture started in 1996, dedicated to chestnut, once a basic food of the mountain (the bread tree) The heart is an ancient water mill still working, which grinds chestnuts to make flour and houses over 700 ancient tools; from the square a path marked salt in the chestnut groves among centuries-old trees, "benne" for the leaves, curls and dryers. Free admission, also for the holders of Museum Subscription; in 2025 it was open on Sundays from July to October, 10-12 and 15-18. Accessible, with workshops for schools.

  • Where Via del Mulino 28, Nomaglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.nomaglio.to.it · turismotorino.org · italia.it

Fontana 'l Burnel

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Ancient coppellate mass, part of the village's life for centuries, then transformed into a fountain. On the surface you can see the cups engraved in prehistoric times and the furrows much more recent left by sharp blades and knives exploiting the stone and running water.

  • Where Via Maestra 55, Nomaglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.nomaglio.to.it · italia.it
