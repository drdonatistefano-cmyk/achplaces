What to see in Rocca di Papa

Borgo dei Castelli Romani at 680 meters, in the Regional Park of the Roman Castles, collected under the Pontifical Fortress mentioned already in 1181 and the Geodynamic Observatory that houses the Geoscience Museum; around, more than 1,400 hectares of wood with the Sacred Way towards Monte Cavo, the Campi di Annibale and the Pratoni del Vivaro.

Museo di Geoscienze MUGEOS (Museo Geofisico)

Museums · Science and university

It is located in the Geodynamic Observatory built in 1886 by Michele Stefano De Rossi next to the ancient Fortress, one of the first Italian seismological observers and still active; between 1922 and 1935 William Marconi used it for broadcasting experiments. Managed by the ENG in agreement with the Municipality and inaugurated as a museum on 26 February 2005, it presents Earth Sciences: Geology of the Albani Hills, seismology with seismic station, geodesia with GNSS station, astronomy with telescope, geochemical monitoring of volcanoes and remote sensing. Guided tours by reservation from the museum site; you park at the Campi di Annibale and follow the directions.

Fortezza pontificia e belvedere

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Remains of the fortress at the top of the country, whose first mention is in a letter from Pope Lucius III of 1181; it was demolished in the mid-500s by the will of Paul III Farnese. After four years of excavation, funded by the Region with 300,000 euros, the site was inaugurated in May 2011. The viewpoint dominates the village with a view of the territory to the sea.

Duomo dell'Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built between 1664 and 1754 on a project by Antonio Del Grande, it collapsed in 1814 and was rebuilt between 1815 and 1845 by Domenico Palmucci; neoclassical facade of L. Arms between two bell towers. Inside works by Corrado Giaquinto (1739), the ciborium of 1517, a baptismal font of 1490 and a painting of an uncertain author with a Christ with almost feminine traits.

Santuario della Madonna del Tufo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

According to tradition in 1490 a huge tuff rock detached from Monte Cavo stopped a few meters from a traveler who had invoked the Virgin; on the site there was a chapel, a destination of pilgrimages. The current church, designed by Don Salvatore Spadaio, was inaugurated in 1932 by the Trinitarians in 1892, the ceiling has wooden panels with coffers and the image of the Madonna on the rock is attributed by the Municipality to Antoniazzo Romano.

  • Where Via Ariccia 1, Rocca di Papa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · www2.comune.roccadipapa.rm.it · visitlazio.com

Via Sacra, Campi di Annibale e Monte Cavo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

An ancient paved road of Roman republican era, 2,65 meters wide, that through the woods rises to about 1,000 meters to the place of the Temple of Lazial Jupiter on Monte Cavo, with panoramic views of the lakes of Albano and Nemi; on the summit stands a building built in 1783 by the Cardinal of York. At the Camps of Hannibal, where according to tradition Hannibal camped in the Second Punic War, it is possible to see the basolate.

  • Where Campi di Annibale, Rocca di Papa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
