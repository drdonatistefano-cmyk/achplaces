What to see in Teramo

The capital between the Gran Sasso and the Adriatic, the ancient Interamnia: the Cathedral with the silver palace of Nicola da Guardiagrele, the Roman theatre and amphitheatre, the domus of the center and the civic museums.

14 places to visit in Teramo, in the province of Teramo (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Teramo in the guide with map and filters

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

On Piazza Orsini, it houses the silver palace of Nicola da Guardiagrele and the polyptych of Jacobello del Fiore; the bell tower is about 50 meters high and in the crypt you worship San Berardo.

  • Where Piazza Orsini, Teramo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it

Teatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The remains of the theatre of the II century AD in the center of the city, which could accommodate about 3,000 spectators.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Pinacoteca civica

Museums · Old masters

The collection was born in 1869 to accommodate the works of closed cult buildings, almost all between the fifteenth and eighteenth centuries; it is located in the neoclassical building of 1923 in the gardens of the municipal villa.

  • Where Viale Bovio, Teramo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismo.abruzzo.it
