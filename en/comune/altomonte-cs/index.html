What to see in Altomonte

Borgo at the foot of the Pollino National Park, with a medieval centre perched on the hill and overlooking the plain of Sibari; it boasts the church of Santa Maria della Consolazione, the highest example of Gothic Angevin in Calabria, the Civic Museum with the "San Ladislao" by Simone Martini, the Norman castle and the Pallotta Tower. It is the city of bread.

Chiesa di Santa Maria della Consolazione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built around 1336 by Filippo Sangineto, the first Earl of Altomonte, on a Norman church, dedicated in 1342 and passed to the Dominicans in 1443. Latin cross plan with a single nave, facade with rose window and portal of French derivation and massive bell tower; inside the tomb of the Sangineto (about 1360, perhaps of the Master Durazzesco), the monument of an unknown knight of the first half of the fourteenth century, the tomb of Cobella Ruffo and a wooden choir of 37 stables.

  • Where Piazza Tommaso Campanella, Altomonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo civico Santa Maria della Consolazione

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated in 1980 in the cells of the former Dominican convent next to the church. The medieval section, born from the collection of the Sangineto, preserves the "San Ladislao" by Simone Martini (1326), two tables by Bernardo Daddi (1328), french alabaster plates of the fourteenth century and the "Madonna delle Pere" by Paolo di Ciacio; the Dominican one painted by saints of the order, the "Madonna di Trapani" (about 1650), silver, vestments and illuminated codes.

Torre Pallotta e Museo Franco Azzinari

Museums · Modern art

Fortified tower with quadrangular plan with Norman foundations, probably built in the 14th century, which takes its name from the Pallotta, lords of Brahalla between two and fifteenth century; restored in 1991. Since 2002 the painter Franco Azzinari has exhibited there, born in San Demetrio Corone: forty works of the collection "Twenty years with nature."

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval nucleus is built on a pre-existing fortified structure; the houses have stone portals built between the seventeenth and nineteenth centuries by local stonemasons. In Piazza Tommaso Campanella the monument to the philosopher, who stayed in the Dominican monastery; between the noble residences Palazzo Pancaro (16th century) and Palazzo Giacobini, an ancient hospital called "home of pilgrims" (1584).

  • Where Piazza Tommaso Campanella, Altomonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Castello normanno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint
