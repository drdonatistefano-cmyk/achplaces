What to see in Piano di Sorrento

On the coast of the Sorrento peninsula, the Villa Fondi de Sangro, built in 1840, houses the archaeological museum named Georges Vallet and a park in 19th century style on a terrace overlooking the sea, overlooking the Gulf of Naples and the village of Marina di Cassano.

Museo archeologico della penisola sorrentina «Georges Vallet»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

It is named after archaeologist Georges Vallet (1922-1994), and is part of the Villa Fondi de Sangro, built in 1840 and restored after the 1980 earthquake. The route goes from prehistory to the imperial age: the Roman maritime villas with the colossal statue of peplophoros found in Sorrento in 1971 and the plastic of the villa of the Cape of Sorrento, the mosaic nymph of the first century A.D. by Massa Lubrense, the four lions in tufa of the sixth century B.C. of the monument to Liparos, the archaic and classical necropolis of the peninsula, the ex vows of the sanctuary of Athena in Punta Campanella and the relief with sacrifice to Diana.

  • Where Via Ripa di Cassano 1, Piano di Sorrento
  • Entry €7, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Parco di Villa Fondi

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

Public garden in mid-nineteenth-century style, owned by the Municipality, on a terrace overlooking the sea in a park of Mediterranean maquis; from the terrace you can see the Gulf of Naples and the village of Marina di Cassano. In the gardens is partly rebuilt the mosaic nymph of a Roman villa and the ancient greenhouse is today a refreshment point.

  • Where Via Ripa di Cassano 26, Piano di Sorrento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it
