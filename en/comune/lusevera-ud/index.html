What to see in Lusevera

Municipality of the upper Val Torre, Slovenian language and culture, in part in the Julian Prealps Natural Park: the Caves of Villanova, discovered in 1925, the Ethnographic Museum in the former dairy, the Pian dei Cyclamini and the nature trails of the Park.

Grotte di Villanova

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

The karst complex of four large cavities with more than 20 km of development; the Cave Nuova, discovered in 1925 by inhabitants who noticed a slit from which came wet air, has almost 9 km of development and is among the most extensive in the world because formed to the contact between two types of rock. The guided tourist route, illuminated and with walkways, crosses galleries, halls and canyons with stalactites and stalagmites to the Corner of the Crystals and the Hall of the Gran Portone with its natural arch (about 1 hour and 15 minutes, 11 °C constant); on reservation easy speleological excursions of 3 or 5 hours. It is on the regional list of tourist caves.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Museo etnografico di Lusevera

Museums · Themed

Born from a group of scholars of the Cultural Research Centre of Lusevera and reopened in the former dairy factory after the long closure due to the earthquake of 1976, it was re-arranged with experts of the Gorordning Muzej of Nova Gorica: three rooms with domestic tools, tools of craftsmen and peasants and tools for the dairy and dairy production, almost all donated by private individuals of the upper Torre Valley.

MiC card indicates opening only on request

  • Where Via Capoluogo, Lusevera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.lusevera.ud.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.lusevera.ud.it

Borghi di Villanova e chiesa di San Floriano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 670 meters, the villages Ruša and Dolina preserve the historical rural architecture and, between 1993 and 1998, the artists of the association \ Stories from the Lingobards have left works in the villages, even on the seven fountains. The church of San Floriano has an altar of the Madonna of 1777, a work of the Resurrection and a miniature Way of the Cross; from the square of the church opens the view of the valley.

  • Where Villanova delle Grotte, Lusevera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.lusevera.ud.it

Sources: comune.lusevera.ud.it

Chiesa di San Giorgio martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The church of the capital was designed by architect Gianni Avon; in front, three lindens symbol of the Holy Trinity recall the ancient custom of linden as a meeting place. In Hrastje there is the "Villaggio Mozzoni-Crespi" (Village Mozzoni-Crespi), built after the earthquake and donated by the owners.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Torrente Vedronza

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

In Vedronza (326 m) the torrent forms natural springs and pools; here stood the hydroelectric power plant of Arturo Malignani, built in 1906-1907, abandoned in 1968 and demolished after the earthquake of 1976.

Pian dei Ciclamini e Passo Tanamea

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Location of the Julian Pre-Alps Natural Park with information point and guesthouse; 8 km cross-country trail also used as a gymnico and mountain bike trail, and a path between beech woods and peak prairies towards the shelter of Monte Maggiore, Sella Kriz and Punta di Montemaggiore, with views of the Friulian plain.

Sentiero natura Valle Musi

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Easy route from Simaz to Pian dei Cyclameni (2 hours and 50, 200 m of altitude difference, all year round): panoramic view of the high valley of the Tower, the former military range with birch and star broom, the ruins of the Tanatcoda, Tanatcason and Trepetnica barracks, the village of Sriegnibosch with beech and orchids, the Mea stream and well-preserved dry terraces.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.lusevera.ud.it

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint
