What to see in Tirano

An ancient town of commercial tradition on the two banks of the Adda, where the Poschiavo Valley meets the Valtellina; to see the Renaissance shrine of the Madonna di Tirano, the walled village with its narrow doors, noble palaces such as Palazzo Salis and the station from which starts the Red Bernina Train, UNESCO World Heritage line since 2008.

Basilica della Madonna di Tirano

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

According to tradition, the Virgin appeared here to Blessed Mario Omodei on 29 September 1504; the sanctuary, designed by Tommaso Rodari with a Latin cross with three naves, transept and dome, was begun in 1505, had the bell tower in 1576 and the dome in 1580, and in 1927 was raised to a minor basilica by Pius XI. Inside the golden statues of the Apparition by Giovan Angelo Del Majno (1519), five great Marian paintings by Giovan Battista Recchi (1634-1637), the main altar in black marble of Varenna (1748) and especially the organ of Giuseppe Bulgarini (1608-1617), with about 2,200 reeds, whose carved case is the most admired work. It is the only basilica in the province of Sondrio and the main example of the Renaissance of Valtellina.

  • Where Piazza Basilica 1, Tirano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · comune.tirano.so.it · in-lombardia.it

Museo etnografico tiranese

Museums · Themed

It is located in the 18th century Casa del Penitentiere, on the south side of the square of the Basilica, and is dedicated to the traditional Valtellina culture: objects and reconstructions of the environment on the peasant and artisan life of the valley, as well as the sacred vestments of the Basilica donated by Cardinal Richelieu in 1636. Hours indicated by the city guide: June-September Tuesday-Sunday 10-12 and 15.30-18.30; October-May Saturday 10-12 and 14.30-17.30; after hours by reservation.

Trenino Rosso del Bernina e stazioni di piazza

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Next to the sanctuary there is the terminus of the Railway Retica, which connects Tirano to St. Moritz in the Engadine through the Bernina Pass; the line In Piazza delle Stazione there are the FS station (1908-1909) and the Rhaetian Railways (1926-1927, in Art Deco style).

Palazzo Salis

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

A noble residence born in the early seventeenth century from the union of two sixteenth-century houses, still owned by the descendants of the powerful Swiss family of Salis: facade with two towers and baroque portal designed by Vignola, courtyard of the frescoed sundial, staircase, Salone delle feste and Saloncello with frescoed ceilings in the eighteenth century, cellars and glaciers. The museum trail of 10 rooms exhibits antique furniture, paintings and documents on the family and Valtellina; the Italian garden is a rarity in the Alpine area. Guided tours of about 50 minutes from April to the end of October, in the rest of the year only by reservation (minimum 4 people).

  • Where Via Salis 3, Tirano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website palazzosalis.com

Sources: palazzosalis.com · comune.tirano.so.it · in-lombardia.it

Casa Museo d'Oro Lambertenghi

Museums · House museums

Palace probably built in the fifteenth century by the Lazzaroni family and then expanded, purchased in 1881 by the notary Giuseppe Lambertenghi; the family, who still lives there, shows rooms with rich furnishings, fixtures and art collections. According to the city guide it is open from 10 May to 10 October on Thursday, Friday and Saturday (10-12.30 and 14-16) and in the rest of the year on the first Monday of the month, with visits every 30 minutes.

  • Where Via Ligari 7, Tirano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Chiesa di Santa Perpetua

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Medieval church on a rocky spur above the city, erected by a community of monks, perhaps humiliated, and also used as xenodochius for pilgrims and strangers. In the apse there are early medieval frescoes discovered in 1987 and restored, among the oldest wall paintings in the province; from the churchyard you can enjoy a panorama of Tirano.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Chiesa di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

An octagonal church dating back to 1526, along Via Rasica towards the Poschiavo Valley: according to the tradition of Giangiacomo de Medici called the Medeghino, a fortress against the Temple-masked Graubünden began there; discovered the deception in 1531, the Tyranese completed it as a church. It has an eighteenth-century portal and a wooden ancone with a statue of the saint.

  • Where Via Rasica, Tirano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.tirano.so.it · in-lombardia.it

Parrocchiale di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the patron in the ancient nucleus on the left bank of the Adda, built in the thirteenth century and extensively renovated in the seventeenth; the Romanesque-Lombard bell tower is of 1479, the organ of the Serassi Brothers of 1852 and on the south side there is a sundial of 1674. You will arrive from Via XX Settembre, the main street of the historic center.

  • Entry To be checked

Mura sforzesche, porte e Palazzo Pretorio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The walls were commissioned by Ludovico il Moro in 1487 after a first raid of the Grisons; of the three gates (Bormina, Milanese and Poschiavina) the best preserved is the Poschiavina Gate, with frescoes of the sixteenth century, which is unique body with the Palazzo Pretorio, ancient seat of the Grigion Podestà with a large portal and coats of arms frescoed in the hallway.

  • Where Porta Poschiavina, Tirano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.tirano.so.it · valtellina.it · in-lombardia.it

Castello di Santa Maria (Castellaccio)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Made by Ludovico il Moro together with the walls to defend the Valtellina from the incursions of the Grisons, it takes its name from the ancient church of Santa Maria and its hospital, existing at least from the twelfth century and incorporated into the belt. It had a more civil role than military; today the ruins and the tower remain, restored by the Municipality. You will climb up from Via Torelli, Via Trivigno and the municipal road Castello.

  • Where Strada comunale Castello, Tirano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.tirano.so.it · in-lombardia.it

Palazzo Quadrio Curzio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Former Venosta Palace, a residence of the Cinque-Seicento in front of the parish church of San Martino; in the eighteenth century it had the present entrance with the loggia above and preserves a porch courtyard with stone doors surmounted by stuccoes.
