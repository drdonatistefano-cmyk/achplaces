What to see in Castellucchio

The village of the Middle Mantovano between Oglio and Mincio, Castellucchio preserves of the Gonzaghesca fortress, rebuilt in 1379 by Ludovico I Gonzaga, the only civic tower, now home to the Museum of the ancient weapons Fosco Baboni; complete the visit the parish of San Giorgio and the churches and villages of the villages.

Torre civica e Museo delle armi antiche Fosco Baboni

Museums · Themed

The defensive tower with square base, about 24-26 meters high with blackbirds and trifore bell cell, is the only rest of the fortress, strengthened by Gonzaga and consolidated in 1844 by engineer Jacopo Martinelli; restored in 1987, houses the museum. The collection was born from the donation of lawyer Fosco Baboni (61 white weapons, 34 firearms) and today exceeds 400 pieces, from the Middle Ages to the Great War, Western and Eastern, in thematic rooms. Open Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Saturday 9-12.30.

  • Where Piazza Arturo Pasotti 26, Castellucchio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museodellearmi.comunecastellucchio.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giorgio martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Three-nave church with frescoes on the central cap, on the plumes and in the presbytery; in the choir the great altarpiece of San Giorgio painted by Felice Campi in 1795.

  • Where Castellucchio (MN)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it

Centro storico, Palazzo Venturini e ville

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center gathers around Piazza Pasotti and the Civic Tower, the highest point of the village and still today leads to the village; among the buildings there are Palazzo Venturini and several manor villas.

Chiesa parrocchiale del Santissimo Nome di Maria a Gabbiana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Completed in the eighteenth century in place of the little church of Santa Maria del Bosco, cited in the mid-500s; a recent restoration brought to light eighteenth-century temperas and frescoes in the presbytery.

  • Where Gabbiana, Castellucchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Ospitaletto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built between 1873 and 1876, it preserves the bell tower of the previous church, collapsed; inside a painting from the first half of the sixteenth century with Saint Francis of Assisi and Saint Francis of Paola and an eighteenth-century organ. The village was a stopover for travellers and hosted a convent of Saint Lucia.

  • Where Ospitaletto, Castellucchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it
