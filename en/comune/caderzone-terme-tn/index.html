What to see in Caderzone Terme

One of the oldest settlements in the Val Rendena, in the Adamello Brenta Natural Park between the Adamello-Presanella glaciers and the Brenta Dolomites: the Lodron Bertelli Palace with the Museum of the Malga, the Maso Curio, the hot springs of the ferruginous water of Sant'Antonio and, at altitude, the lakes of San Giuliano and Garzonè.

9 places to visit in Caderzone Terme, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo Lodron Bertelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The origins date back to the beginning of the fourteenth century, coeve to Maso Curio; transformed into a noble palace by the Bertelli, it was for centuries the center of the political and social power of the country. It preserves the ancient façade, an austere androne, the monumental staircase, a stucco room, a room with wooden ceiling, the chapel of the accounts and the portal of Marcantonio.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Antonio, Caderzone Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: campigliodolomiti.it · cultura.trentino.it · italia.it

Museo della Malga

Museums · Themed

Set up in the early 2000s in the former stables on the ground floor of Palazzo Lodron Bertelli, it tells the life of the alpine pasture with original tools collected in the pastures and dairy farms of Rendena and Giudicarie: three rooms on pasture and milking, on milk processing and on the daily life of the malgari. Open from Tuesday to Saturday (in July and August also on Sunday), 9.30-12.30 and 15.30-18.30.

  • Where Rione Lodron-Bertelli, Piazza Sant'Antonio, Caderzone Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.trentino.it · campigliodolomiti.it · italia.it

Cappella gentilizia Bertelli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1677 and dedicated to St. Anthony the Abbot, it houses a golden wooden altar of the same year with the so-called "Madonna del Dito" (Madonna del Dito).

Chiesa di San Biagio e campanile

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church already existed before 1361, demolished in 1853-1854 and rebuilt larger, consecrated in 1868: it has five altars and an organ of 1931 with 701 reeds. The granite bell tower dates back to the second half of the thirteenth century.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Maso Curio e parco agricolo

History and architecture · Villages · Viewpoint

One of the oldest rural buildings in the area, documented from the beginning of the fourteenth century, in the grassy plain between Caderzone and Pinzolo: the beams still bear the signs of the fire of 1537 and outside there is an ancient votive fresco with St. Anthony Abbot and St. Barbara. It is still used for demonstration purposes.

Lazzaretto di Santa Maria

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The 16th century Lodron country house, enlarged by the Bertelli family and adapted to the lazzaretto during the 1630 epidemic, is now a barn and warehouse. Outside, the fresco "Madonna" with Child and the Saints Giuliano and Antonio Abate" by Giuseppe Chinatti.

Terme Val Rendena (Borgo della Salute)

Local life and traditions · Spas

A complex spread in the historic centre, active since 2004, using the mineral water ferruginous of the Fonte Sant'Antonio, praised as early as 1635 by the prince bishop Carlo Emanuele Madruzzo: inhalations, balneotherapy, hydropinic treatments, wellness area with sauna, Turkish bath and salt cave, outdoor swimming pool and in summer Kneipp routes along the Sarca. Seasonal opening from spring to November 30.

  • Where Via D. Chiesa 2, Caderzone Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · campigliodolomiti.it · italia.it

Laghi di San Giuliano e Garzonè

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Two glacial lakes about 1,935-1,940 meters in the Adamello group, between Corno Alto, La Cingla and La Costaccia, reachable only on foot from Diaga or Pozza delle Vacche (paths 230 and 221) in 1 hour and half-2 hours. On the shore is the nineteenth-century church of San Giuliano in stone and wood and the San Giuliano refuge of the 1930s; around the alpine pastures Campo and Germenega. Recommended period from late June to early October.

  • Where Pozza delle Vacche, Caderzone Terme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · campigliodolomiti.it · italia.it
