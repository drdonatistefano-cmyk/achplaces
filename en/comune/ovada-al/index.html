What to see in Ovada

A small town between the streams Stura and Orba from the historic centre of Liguria, with facades painted in trompe l'oeil legacy of the long Genoan domination: the Baroque Our Lady Assunta with the altar of Alessandro Antonelli, the Oratory of St. John the Baptist with the sculptures of Maragliano, the birthplace of St. Paul of the Cross and the Palaeontological Museum

6 places to visit in Ovada, in the province of Alessandria (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient nucleus retains an aspect of Ligurian style, with houses separated by narrow alleys and facades of noble palaces decorated with trompe l'oeil by the Genoese, who controlled the village for a long time; there pass the processions of the confraternities with large Crucifixes and sculptural groups. Among the places in the center via Cairoli, Piazza Mazzini and Piazza Castello.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Ovada
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · alexala.it

Chiesa di Nostra Signora Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Recognizable for its white Baroque façade, the circular dome and the two bell towers that mark the view of the town, it was consecrated in 1801. The main altar is designed by Alessandro Antonelli and the canvas with the Estasi di Santa Teresa is by Luca Giordano.

  • Where Via Cairoli, Ovada
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · alexala.it

Museo paleontologico «Giulio Maini»

Museums · Science and university

Housed in the fourteenth-century church of Sant'Antonio, it collects mainly local collections, including the Calappilia mani, species of crab present only in this area.
