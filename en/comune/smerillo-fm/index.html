What to see in Smerillo

The "Tetto delle Marche": fortified village over 800 metres, also known as the "Citadella della Poesia" with the Romanesque Santa Caterina, two small museums (natural sciences and children's art) and, around, the Smerillo Wood, the Fossa rich in fossils and the Fosso delle Anguille.

10 places to visit in Smerillo, in the province of Fermo (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo fortificato, mura e rocca

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small village on a rocky spur at more than 800 meters, probably founded in the IX century, with the remains of the walls and medieval fortress of the ancient Castrum Smerilli; on clear days the view arrives to the Sibillini, the Gran Sasso, the Conero and the Adriatic.

  • Where Smerillo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Belvedere Adriatico

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural terrace just beyond the houses, reachable on foot in a few minutes leaving the car out of the center: view of the valley and the Sibillini Mountains and, on clear days, on the Adriatic.

  • Entry Free

Museo di scienze naturali (fossili e minerali)

Museums · Science and university · Free entry

Local fossils of the lower Pliocene and a didactic collection from the Primary to the Quaternary; a hall of minerals from deposits from all over the world with experiments of fluorescence and crystallization. It is the seat of the Environmental Education Centre Bosco di Smerillo, which organizes walks on geological paths.

  • Where Via A.C. Nobili 11, Smerillo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: touringclub.it · letsmarche.it

Museo Pinacoteca dell'arte dei bambini

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Opened on September 19, 1999 with the theme "The Child and Peace," it collects drawings, writings, poems and photographs about peace; it has an open space called "Peace Thoughts" and a workshop of education for peace.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

La Fessa

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Breaking into the sandstone rock rich in fossils of Pliocene, destination of tourists and paleontologists; the route to reach it is among the most characteristic of the area.
