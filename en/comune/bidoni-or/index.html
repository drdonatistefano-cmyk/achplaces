What to see in Bidonì

Small village of Barigadu on a hill at 260 m overlooking Lake Omodeo, with a historic center of houses in red trachite: the Temple of Jupiter on the hill of S'Onnarìu, the Romanesque church of St. Peter, the country shrine of Santa Maria di Ossolo and the house-museum Sa 'Omo' and sa majarza on the witchcraft in Sardinia.

Tempio di Giove

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Built around 50 BC on the S'Onnarìu hill, on the northern outskirts of the country, it is indicated as one of only three temples of this type found so far in the Mediterranean. Two inscriptions on the side of an altar, "Dei" and "Iovis" confirm its dedication; the fragments of ceramics indicate a frequentation between II and I century B.C.

  • Where Colle di s'Onnarìu (Monte Onnariu), Bidonì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque country church of the second half of the 12th century, restored in the 17th century, cited in the act of founding the Camaldolese monastery of Santa Maria di Bonarcado (the dome of sanctu Petru de Vidoni). A unique nave about eight meters long with an apse to the east, in polychrome trachite from red to light yellow, a hut façade with a small stone cross and a wooden cover with trusses; on the left of the altar a Roman funerary stele of the II century A.D. with Latin inscription.

  • Where Presso il cimitero comunale, Bidonì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Santuario di Santa Maria di Ossolo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

A novenary and country church of the 13th century, rebuilt in 1632. At the beginning of September there is one of the most important festivals in the country, with the procession from the church to the parish.

  • Where Località Ossolo, Bidonì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Centro storico in trachite rossa

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Cobbled streets, medieval monuments and ancient houses in red trachyte; there stands the parish church of St. John the Baptist, whose patron saint is celebrated at the end of June.

  • Where Centro storico, Bidonì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Lago Omodeo e pineta di Istei

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village dominates from above Lake Omodeo, suitable for trekking, mountain biking and canoeing and wildlife oasis with rare birds; under the waters lie the remains of a nuragic settlement. At the lake, the pine forest of Istei hosts the festival of s'anzone on Monday.

  • Where Lago Omodeo, Bidonì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
