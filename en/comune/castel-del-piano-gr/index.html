What to see in Castel del Piano

Country on the slopes of Monte Amiata: the Baroque church of the Opera with the paintings of the Nasini, the sixteenth-century Palazzo Nerucci with civic museum and art collection, the Piazza degli Verdi with the loggia, the Palio of 8 September in Piazza Garibaldi and, in Montenero d'Orcia, the museum of vine and wine.

7 places to visit in Castel del Piano, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa dell'Opera (Propositura dei Santi Niccolò e Lucia)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In Piazza della Madonna, baroque and completed in 1870, with ten chapels and numerous paintings by the Nasini family, including the Nativity of Mary by Giuseppe Nicola Nasini.

  • Where Piazza della Madonna, Castel del Piano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Palazzo Nerucci: Museo civico, Antiquarium e Raccolta d'arte

Museums · Old masters

In the palace of 1554, recently restored, the civic museum and the art collection of a private collector: above all landscapes and views, with a pastel self-portrait of Rosalba Career, a portrait of Cosimo III de' Medici embroidered in silk and a sketch of Giuseppe Nicola Nasini.

  • Where Via Roma 1, Castel del Piano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Centro storico, Torre dell'Orologio e Piazzetta degli Ortaggi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Going up to the historic centre you pass under the arch of the Clock Tower to the picturesque square of the Vegetables, with a loggia of the sixteenth century well preserved; at the end of the village the churches of San Leonardo and Piccina.

  • Where Centro storico, Castel del Piano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Museo della vite e del vino di Montenero d'Orcia

Museums · Themed

In the outbuilding of the large farmhouse of the Avanzati family, used until the 1950s: panels on wine culture, tools for viticulture and winemaking, chestnuts and oil of the Amiata and pijauva, 18th century press used until 1910.

  • Where Via Grandi 5, Montenero d'Orcia, Castel del Piano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Monte Amiata e Parco delle Macinaie

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the village you can climb to the beech and fir woods of Amiata, ideal for trekking and walks; surroundings with the Benedictine churches of Santa Lucia, San Biagio and Santa Flora in Noceto and the ruins of the convent of San Processo.

  • Where Le Macinaie, Castel del Piano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
