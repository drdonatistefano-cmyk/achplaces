What to see in Sampeyre

Country of the Varaita Valley mentioned in the sources written around the Mille and linked to the Marchesato di Saluzzo until the passage to the Savoy in 1601; on the square the parish church of Saints Peter and Paul with the Romanesque portal and the cycle of the Biazaci brothers, then the historical ethnographic museum with the drapes of the Baio, the sanctuary of the Becetto and the Sentiero dei Sarvanot in Rore.

Museo storico etnografico

Museums · Themed

In about ten rooms he collects tools for agricultural work, bakery and wool processing, the reconstruction of an old class with benches and blackboards, the workshops of the blacksmith and blacksmith, traditional costumes, a collection of models with the characters of the Baio and the authentic drapes of the four Sampeyre Bays. Open on weekends in spring and September, from Tuesday to Sunday in July and August, closed in October and November (check first, tel. 0175 977148).

  • Where Via Roma 25, Sampeyre
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the 15th century on a Romanesque building and renovated in the 19th century, it has only one nave with two side hats. The main portal in green stone, with vegetable frieze, mozzarella heads and a two-faced gianus, is among the best examples of the Romanesque Saluzzo; a lateral portal bears the date 1462. On the right wall the cycle of the brothers Tommaso and Matteo Biazaci (1460-1470) and the octagonal baptismal font in white Zabreri marble, dated 1482.

  • Where Piazza del paese, Sampeyre
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · comune.sampeyre.cn.it

Santuario di Nostra Signora del Becetto

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Built in 1201, it has enjoyed since the early times of great fame and devotion among the populations of the valley and the plain; it preserves an unusual Black Madonna and is a stop of the Varaita Trekking Valley that climbs from Verzuolo.

Chiesa parrocchiale di Villar

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It is mentioned already in the list of the cathedral of Turin of 1336, it has two Romanesque portals in green stone of Brossasco (the largest on the side), a single nave with bundles of columns similar to those of the parish church of Sampeyre and a bell tower renovated of Gothic structure; on the square a stone cross.

Sentiero dei Sarvanot e cascata Tumpi la Pisso

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

From Rore the marked path goes up for 1 km (about 45 minutes a way, from 890 to 1,010 m) the Ciantorane stream in the forest, between irrigation channels, wooden and stone bridges and a rocky gorge, up to the Tumpi waterfall the Pisso that falls into a rocky pool; it is the theme of Sarvanot, the hairy and spiteful goblins of tradition. With a short steep detour you will reach the Big Bench of Chris Bangle, a panoramic view over the valley. Suitable for everyone but not for strollers, snow-free; beautiful in autumn.

  • Where Parcheggio in fondo alla frazione Rore, Sampeyre
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
