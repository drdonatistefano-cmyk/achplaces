What to see in Abbasanta

Village of houses in dark stone on the basaltic plateau, with the nuraghe Losa, one of the symbols of the Nuragic age.

2 places to visit in Abbasanta, in the province of Oristano (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Nuraghe Losa

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Entirely in large basalt blocks: trilobed nuraghe of the Middle Bronze (XV-XIV century B.C.), antemural with towers and remains of a village of huts until the Iron Age.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Nuraghe Zuras e tomba dei Giganti di sos Ozzastros

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The monotorre Zuras, between the 14th and 12th centuries BC, remarkable for its construction precision, the tomb of the Giants of Sos Ozzastros and the sacred well of Calegastea.

  • Entry Free
