What to see in Darfo Boario Terme

Porta della bassa Valle Camonica, Darfo Boario Terme combines the Boario thermal park, with the Liberty dome of 1913 and four sources, with the rock art of the UNESCO site n. 94 in the Park of Luine and the Corni Freschi, at Lake Moro of glacial origin and at frescoed churches like Santa Maria del Restello, with works by Callisto Piazza.

Terme di Boario

Local life and traditions · Spas

Spa park of about 130,000 square meters with Italian garden and rare botanical species; the sources, already known in the seventeenth century and appreciated by Alessandro Manzoni, are four: Antica Fonte, Fausta, Igea and Boario. At the entrance the colonnade with the Liberty dome of 1913, symbol of the spa. In addition to bathrooms, mud and wellness area with swimming pools, saunas and salt room, the park hosts the Boario Park Adventure with Tibetan bridge and a slittovia (Alpine Coaster) from the Fausta Pavilion to the high park. Spa hours: Mon-Fri 12:30-22:00, Saturday, Sunday and holidays 10:00-22:00, recommended booking.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.termediboario.it

Parco Archeologico Comunale di Luine

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

In a dominant position over the village, it preserves over 100 surfaces engraved on outbuildings of Simona stone, of violet colour, with the oldest engravings of the camuno cycle, dating back to the Mesolithic period; the place was then frequented as a place of worship from the late Neolithic and especially in the Bronze and Iron Ages. The Rock 34, almost entirely engraved, shows an animal shape of about 10,000 years ago, warriors of the Iron Age almost a metre tall, a labyrinth and a camuna rose; exceptional the number of alabards and weapons of the age of Copper and Bronze. It is part of the UNESCO website n. 94 Cupestrian art of the Camonica Valley. Winter time (until March 31st) Mar-dom 9:00-17:00, closed Monday; tickets also online.

Corni Freschi e colle Monticolo

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the base of the Monticolo hill, on the right bank of the Oglio, a ritual mass of the Copper Age (second half of the III millennium BC) called "Rock of the Alabards" for the nine alabards carved almost life-size; the excavation of 2002 revealed under them a second composition of fifteen daggers. The site is free and always visitable; at the Corni Freschi there is also a climbing wall and on the Monticolo the Giant Bench n. 72.

  • Where Località Gattaro, Darfo Boario Terme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Sources: vallecamonicaunesco.it · turismovallecamonica.it

Lago Moro

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small lake of glacial origin in the hamlet of Capo di Lago, between the hills of the Sorline and Rodino at the foot of Monte Pora; the steep walls of Lombardy reddish-brown wart darken the waters, surrounded by chestnut woods, swamp straw and florid fern. Since 2000 it has been protected as a Local Park of Sovercomunal Interest established with Angolo Terme; in summer it is frequented for bathing and boat trips, and is surrounded by hiking trails.

in summer access by car to Capo di Lago forbidden during daytime hours, shuttle service of the Municipality (Lombardy)

MALP - Museo degli Alpini

Museums · Themed

In new spaces, it tells the history of the Alpine Corps with images and finds: original equipment of the Second World War (uniforms, weapons, ammunition, radio devices), an area dedicated to the Russian Campaign and the memory of the Battalion Edolo and the Bergamo Group. Open Saturday and Sunday 15:00-18:00; groups and schools by reservation.
