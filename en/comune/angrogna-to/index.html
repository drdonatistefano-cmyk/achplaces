What to see in Angrogna

The side valley of Val Pellice, the heart of Waldensian history: the meadow of Chandforan of the Synod of 1532, the cave-refuge of the Gheisa d'la Tana, the schools of beards in Pradeltorno and the ancient schools of hamlet.

5 places to visit in Angrogna, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Angrogna in the guide with map and filters

Chanforan

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Large lawn with a stone stele in the center reminiscent of the Synod of 1532, with which the Waldenses joined the European Protestant Reformation.

  • Where Angrogna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lang.fondazionevaldese.org

Sources: lang.fondazionevaldese.org

Gheisa d'la Tana

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Wide natural cave which tradition indicates as a refuge and place of worship of the Waldenses in the periods of persecution.

  • Where Angrogna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lang.fondazionevaldese.org

Sources: lang.fondazionevaldese.org

Coulege dei Barba

Museums · City history

In Pradeltorno, small ancient buildings in dry stone, probably school of biblical preparation of the itinerant medieval Waldensian preachers called "barba" , visits by reservation with the museum system of the Valdese Foundation.

  • Where Loc. Pra del Torno, Angrogna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lang.fondazionevaldese.org

Sources: lang.fondazionevaldese.org · turismotorino.org

Museo scuola Odin-Bertot

Museums · Themed

Hall of the 19th century hamlet school with wooden counters, chair, stove and teaching materials.

  • Where Loc. Odin, Angrogna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lang.fondazionevaldese.org

Sources: lang.fondazionevaldese.org

Museo Scuola Beckwith

Museums · Themed

It reproduces the installation of a Waldensian confessional school, developed in the nineteenth century thanks to English colonel Charles Beckwith, with documents on Waldensian history.

  • Where Frazione Odin, Angrogna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lang.fondazionevaldese.org

Sources: cultura.gov.it · lang.fondazionevaldese.org
