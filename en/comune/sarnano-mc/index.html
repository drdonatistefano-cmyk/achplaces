What to see in Sarnano

Medieval village with concentric circles around Piazza Alta, at the foot of the Sibillini Mountains: Santa Maria Assunta, the Pinacoteca with works by Vittore Crivelli and Simone De Magistris, the thermal baths of oligomineral waters and the Via delle cascatelle lost.

Centro storico e Piazza Alta

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval castrum plant with concentric circles, steps and views; Piazza Alta, the highest and oldest point of civil and religious life, is closed by the small Palazzo dei Priori and Palazzo del Podestà in bricks.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1265 and 1286, with bell tower of a century later and white stone portal with the Assumption in the bezel. He preserves works by Lorenzo d'Alessandro (1483), Niccolò Alunno, Paolo Bontulli (1530) and the Madonna of Mercy by Pietro Alemanno, who also frescoed the crypt in 1494.

damaged by the earthquake of 2016 (Italy.it)

  • Where Piazza Alta, Sarnano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Pinacoteca e Musei civici

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the former convent of the Clarisse di Santa Chiara, restored after the earthquake of 1997-98: on the ground floor the gallery around the courtyard, with works by Vittore Crivelli (Madonna with Child) and Simone De Magistris; on the upper floor the collections of weapons, avifauna and hammers and the collection of modern art by Mariano Gavasci.

  • Where Via Giacomo Leopardi, Sarnano
  • Entry €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · eventi.turismo.marche.it

Palazzo del Popolo e Teatro della Vittoria

History and architecture · Historic theatres

The Palazzo del Popolo, in the second half of the 13th century, was transformed into the Victory Theatre, built in 1831 and recently restored.

Local life and traditions · Spas

Via delle cascatelle perdute

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Easy ring of about 6 km with minimal difference in altitude around the historic center, accessible all year round, which touches three natural waterfalls returned accessible thanks to recovery interventions; suitable for families.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Piani di Ragnolo

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Sibillini plateau at 1509 m, between the municipalities of Fiastra and Sarnano: panorama of the peaks of the Marche and the Adriatic, violet blooms, narcisi, peonies and orchids between May and June, walking and mountain biking trails and winter ski slopes.

  • Where Piani di Ragnolo, Sarnano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · eventi.turismo.marche.it

Abbazia di San Biagio a Piobbico

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Benedictine Abbey of Santa Maria founded in 1059, today dedicated to San Biagio, at the foot of the Sassotetto; it remains the single nave church with trusses and crypt, frescoed by the Master of Piobbico, Paolo Bontulli and Nobile da Lucca.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
