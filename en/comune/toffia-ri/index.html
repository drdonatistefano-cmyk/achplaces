What to see in Toffia

Medieval village of Sabina above the valley of Farfa, divided for centuries between the Orsini, in the lower part, and the Colonna, in the upper part: Porta Maggiore with the fifteenth-century palaces, Palazzo Orsini seat of the Municipality, Santa Maria Nova on the site of the Colonna fortress, the church of San Lorenzo outside the walls and the museum of Maria Petrucci.

7 places to visit in Toffia, in the province of Rieti (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Porta Maggiore e i palazzi di via di Porta Maggiore

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

It was the main entrance of the castle of Toffia: the holes of the chains of the drawbridge, the guard posts and the wounds remain; the coat of arms of the village shows the church of San Lorenzo between two towers. Palazzo Ruffetti (today Bufalieri, XV century) is located on the street, where the seminary of the abbey of Farfa from 1637 to 1705, and the fourteenth-century Casa degli Oddoni with a Sabino portal and crossed windows in travertine.

  • Where Via Porta Maggiore, Toffia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Palazzo Orsini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The largest building in the historic center, built by the Orsini in the fifteenth century and today seat of the Municipality: the most visible sign of rivalry with the Colonna. Façade with crossed windows in travertine and loggia originally four arches, three of which preserved; on the noble floor, today council room, a large fireplace and two doors surmounted by the coat of arms Orsini in marble.

  • Where Via Porta Maggiore 9, Toffia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Casa degli Orsini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Residence of the fourteenth century, the smallest of the three Orsini properties in the village, with an internal cloister in common with the Oratory of the Most Holy Stimmates; above the entrance the rose of the Orsini, inside a more elaborate coat of arms above the door of the loggia, travertine windows with stone seats and, in the basement, a preserved stone mill.

  • Where Via di Castel di Dentro 2, Toffia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Nova

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

At the highest point of Toffia, built in the seventeenth century on the site of the Palace-Castle of the Columns: the bell tower reuses the male of the castle and the apse one of its towers. Originally a single nave, then divided into three naves; it preserves a Virgin with the Child of Carlo Maratta school and a round with the Virgin and Child of the Florentine school of the fifteenth century.

  • Where Parte alta del borgo, Toffia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.toffia.ri.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Outside the walls on the Cacultian hill, elevated to cathedrals as the second seat of Sabina after the Saracen destruction of Foronovo. In the material façade of Roman tombs, two Lusoriae tabulae and a Roman bas-relief with a seated emperor; inside the chapels of San Sisinio (1656) and San Saturnino (rebuilt in 1782) and at the main altar the Martyrdom of San Lorenzo di Vincenzo Manenti.

  • Where Colle Cacultiano, Toffia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.toffia.ri.it · italia.it

Museo delle bande musicali di Toffia - Museo Maria Petrucci

Museums · House museums

In a 17th-century house in the historic centre, founded in 1991 by the artist Maria Petrucci in honor of his father Raniero: four rooms on several floors with wooden sculptures, oil paintings, the old fireplace, the orcs for oil and legumes and the studio of the artist; a fifth room collects objects of peasant life. Reservation not required according to the MiC card; tel. 0765 326248.

  • Where Via della Rocca, Toffia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Marmo di Piazza

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

In Piazza Lauretana, in front of the church of Our Lady of Loreto, a stone of Roman times slightly oval (about 110-135 cm of diameter, 20 thick) used as a bank to conclude business: coins were beaten there, judged good by sound.

  • Where Piazza Lauretana, Toffia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it
