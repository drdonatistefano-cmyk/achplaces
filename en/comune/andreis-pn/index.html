What to see in Andreis

Small village at 455 meters at the entrance of the Valcellina, inside the Natural Park of the Friulian Dolomites, with stone houses and wooden balconies (showers) is the village of the eagles for the avifauna area and the recovery center of the raptors, and houses the Museum of Art and Rural Civilization.

Centro visite del Parco «L'avifauna del Parco»

Museums · Science and university

The exhibition "L'avifauna del Parco" opened in 2001, exhibits more than 200 naturalized specimens of birds of the park and the region, with teaching room, projection room and conferences and naturalistic laboratory; it is connected to the avifauna area with the recovery center. Seasonal openings, especially on weekends and in August every day.

seasonal openings only, autumn closing from September to early December according to the calendar of the Park

Area avifauna e centro recupero rapaci

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Above the country, recovery aviaries and settings with veterinary surgery for injured birds: four cages for specimens no longer liberable and a large aviary where those healed train before release, often public. You can see from close up the royal eagle, buzzard, pilgrim falcon, kestrel, scatter, astore and allocco; they must be approached in silence without touching the nets.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo civico dell'arte e della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Ethnographic museum opened in July 1981 in the former town hall next to the square: reconstruction of the typical house of Andreis, forge of the blacksmith, combs in bone, Easter ravenelle (cràceles), the working of the shoes and the objects in wood and bone of the "sedoners" , the walking craftsmen of the valley.

  • Where Ex municipio, Andreis
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcodolomitifriulane.it · italia.it · turismofvg.it

Borgo di Andreis

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small well-preserved center on a green terrace sheltered from the Raut, Resettum and Fara mountains, with stone houses, dark wooden balconies (daltz) and external stairs; in the village the seventeenth-century church of Santa Maria delle Grazie and that of San Daniele, 1700.

  • Where Andreis
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcodolomitifriulane.it

Sentiero naturalistico del Monte Ciavac e Linea Periadriatica

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

The route shows the Periadriatic Line (line Barcis-Staro Selo), which is over 100 km long, born from the push of the African plaque against Europe, which separates the Main Dolomia from rocks of different ages: its rock band is fractured above the meadows of Andreis, under the woods of Mount Raut.

  • Where Monte Ciavac, Andreis
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcodolomitifriulane.it

Orrido del torrente Molassa

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Gorge of the Molassa stream visible from the road between Barcis and Andreis, part of the Forra del Cellina Nature Reserve; you can explore with the Valcellina Train, currently not in service.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sentiero di Pala Barzana

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A winding path that climbs up to the fork of Pala Barzana and then descends, with steep and dry hairpin bends, towards the Colvera Valley.
