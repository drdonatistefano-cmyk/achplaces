What to see in Montescaglioso

Borgo on the hills of the Valle del Bradano dominated by the Benedictine Abbey of San Michele Arcangelo, one of the most important monuments of Basilicata, with historical convents, hundreds of wineries dug into the tuff and rock churches in the Murgia.

7 places to visit in Montescaglioso, in the province of Matera (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Abbazia di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Benedictine Abbey in the upper part of the historic centre, on the remains of the acropolis of the Italian and Greek city (in the necropolis cloisters from the VII century BC). It is spread over three floors around the cloister, with cellars excavated in the rock; on the upper floor frescoes of the XVI-XVII century, including the cycle of the library attributed to Girolamo Todisco, and sculptures of Altobello and Aurelio Persio; the church has stuccoes of the early eighteenth century and four frescoed chapels on the side.

  • Where Centro storico, Montescaglioso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Centro storico, Piazza Roma e belvedere

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient center preserves several churches; Piazza Roma is dominated by the Church of San Rocco and from the highest point you can see the Valle del Bradano and, in the distance, the Gulf of Taranto.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Montescaglioso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · basilicataturistica.it

Chiese rupestri della Murgia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

In the Park of Murgia there are rock churches of the X-XI century; the abbey had rock churches in the Murgia of Sant'Andrea and near the village there is the crypt of Cozzo Sant'Angelo (XI-XII century).
