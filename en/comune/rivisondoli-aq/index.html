What to see in Rivisondoli

Village of medieval origin at about 1,320 meters, at the foot of Monte Calvario and at the edge of the plateau of the Cinquemiglia, between the parks of Abruzzo and Majella; it preserves doors and fortifications and the church of San Nicola di Bari, and is known for the living nativity scene, among the oldest in Italy, held every 5 January since 1951.

Centro storico e Porta Antonietta

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Medieval village of narrow and steep alleys, with an ancient system of fortifications still well readable: the fifteenth-century Porta Antonietta and the Porta di Mezzo remain. Every 5 January, since 1951, the village hosts one of the oldest and most famous living nativity scenes in Italy, with shepherds, women in costume and Magi and the Child interpreted by the last born of the country.

  • Where Centro storico, Rivisondoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di San Nicola di Bari

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the village still visitable despite the earthquakes have severely damaged the architecture of the area; the municipality signals its portal, the bell tower and the stained glass windows inside, in a harmonious building and well inserted in the town.

  • Where Piazza San Nicola da Bari 3, Rivisondoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.rivisondoli.aq.it · italia.it

Monte Calvario e sentiero per Monte Rotella

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the upper part of the village a dirt road climbs through the meadows of Monte Calvario; the trail T1 of the Park continues on the ridge of Monte Rotella to Rocca Pia (about 14 km, 5 hours, difficulty EE), with views of the plateaus of the Quarto del Barone and the Quarto Grande, the Plan of the Five Miglia, the mountains of the Parco d'Abruzzo and the Bosco di Sant'Antonio. For the mountain bike there is the mountain bike ring MTB 47 of Monte Calvario (10.1 km, difficult).

  • Where Parte alta del paese, Rivisondoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcomajella.it · italia.it
