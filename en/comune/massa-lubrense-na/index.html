What to see in Massa Lubrense

Common at the end of the Sorrentine peninsula overlooking Capri, with the former cathedral of Santa Maria delle Grazie, the fishing village of Marina della Lobra and, in the Protected Marine Area of Punta Campanella, the Bay of Ieranto del FAI, the fjord of Crapolla and the promontory of Punta Campanella.

6 places to visit in Massa Lubrense, in the province of Naples (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ex cattedrale di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Domina Piazza Vescovado: built between 1512 and 1536 and renovated in 1760, it preserves in the apse a Madonna delle Grazie by Marco Cardisco (16th century) and in sacristy cupboards and eighteenth-century stuccos. From here starts the walk signposted towards Marina della Lobra.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Marina della Lobra e Santuario di Santa Maria della Lobra

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fishermen's village with marina and small beach, reached by stairs; above the village the sanctuary of Santa Maria della Lobra, dating back to 1528, built on a temple perhaps dedicated to Minerva, which gave the name to the city (Lobra da In front is the Scoglio del Vervece, where every year the fallen of the sea are remembered; the cliffs are a good point for the sunset. The walk from Piazza Vescovado is the shortest and most accessible of the Marine Protected Area.

  • Where Marina della Lobra, Massa Lubrense
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · puntacampanella.org

Baia di Ieranto

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Insect in front of the Faraglioni di Capri, site of community interest in the Marine Protected Area of Punta Campanella, donated to the FAI by Italsider in 1987; once limestone quarry abandoned in the 1950s, today among Mediterranean maquis, olive groves and terraced citrus groves. You can reach it on foot from Nerano (about 40 minutes downhill, 1 hour uphill, medium difficulty) passing next to the sixteenth-century tower of Montalto, or by sea with authorized boats; the FAI offers guided tours by reservation.

Punta Campanella

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Promontory in front of Capri that closes the Sorrento peninsula, the heart of a protected marine area with about 40 km of coast. A flat path of just under 2 km starts from the hamlet of Termini and leads to the fourteenth-century tower built on the site of the ancient temple of Athena-Minerva; near the grotto of the Sirene with an inscription in the Osk of the II century BC discovered in 1985. The return can follow the via Minervia, in part still paved.

  • Where Termini, Massa Lubrense
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it · puntacampanella.org

Monte San Costanzo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A ring of about 3.5 hours (medium-high difficulty, recommended in spring and autumn) from Piazza Santa Croce di Termini: the chapel of San Costanzo on the lower summit (485 m) looks at the gulfs of Naples and Salerno and the islets of the Galli; from the summit of 496 m, the highest of the territory, you see Capri; the path then descends towards Punta Campanella.

  • Where Piazza Santa Croce, Termini, Massa Lubrense
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: puntacampanella.org · italia.it

Fiordo di Crapolla

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural inlet on the Gulf of Salerno, in zone B of the Marine Protected Area, reached by Torca (via Nula) in about 1 hour of descent and 1 hour and a half challenging ascent, in the sun and without water. In the seaside village there are Roman cisterns, the viceroyal tower against the Saracens and the remains of the abbey of San Pietro (beginning of the year 1000), with votive chapel, stems of columns and marble bases.

  • Where Via Nula, Torca, Massa Lubrense
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: puntacampanella.org · italia.it
