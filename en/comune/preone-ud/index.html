What to see in Preone

Mountain country on the right of the Tagliamento, in the Carnic Alps, in a geosite of supranational interest where fossils of the Triassic have reappeared like the pterosaur Preondactylus buffarinii, told in the exhibition of Palazzo Lupieri.

Esposizione «Preone 200 milioni di anni»

Museums · Science and university

In Palazzo Lupieri, a six-seventh-century Carnic style residence, collects the fossils of the Preone valley and illustrates the geological and biological history of the Carnic Prealps: the first room reconstructs with dioramas environments and animals of the territory, the second tells the area from 200 million years ago with some important fossils. It is part of the CarniaMusei network. Summer time indicated by MiC: June-September 16-18 (data 2020, to be verified).

Geosito paleontologico e sentiero Stavolo Lunas

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

The territory of Preone is classified as a geosite of supranational interest for the fossil fauna of more than 200 million years ago (Triassic); in the early eighties there were recovered the rare remains of the Preondactylus buffarinii, one of the most ancient species of pterosaur. From the village, close to the exhibition, starts the short trail to the Ring Lunas Stavolo with panels on geology, paleontology, fauna and vegetation.

  • Where Partenza dal paese, Preone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
