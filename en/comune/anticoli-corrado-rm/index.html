What to see in Anticoli Corrado

Medieval village perched on the Ruffi Mountains above the Aniene valley, about 58 km from Rome, known as the village of artists and models: from the nineteenth century there were northern European painters and then Kokoschka, Pirandello and Silone, and the Civic Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art preserves about 700 works; in Piazza delle Ville there is the fountain of Arturo Martini.

Civico Museo d'Arte Moderna e Contemporanea

Museums · Modern art

Born in 1935 as a gallery founded by Riccardo Assanti and Pietro Gaudenzi with 20 works created on site and became a museum in 2001, it occupies on three floors the Brancaccio Palace in the area of the ancient castle then a baronial palace. About 700 works including drawings, paintings, sculptures and prints from the late nineteenth century to the seventies, donated by artists related to the country: Kokoschka, Felice Carena, Arturo Martini, Adolfo De Carolis, Giulio Aristide Sartorio, Augusto Corelli, Pietro Gaudenzi, Giuseppe Capogrossi, Fausto Pirandello, Rafael Alberti and the model became painter Pasqualosa Marcelli Bertoletti.

  • Where Piazza Santa Vittoria 2, Anticoli Corrado
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · luoghidelcontemporaneo.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Centro storico e Palazzo Baronale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village with arches, alleys and portals bearing the coats of arms of Colonna, Barberini and Brancaccio, overlooking the valley of the Aniene; the name joins the Roman Anticulum and Corrado d'Antiochia, descendant of Barbarossa and count of these lands in the thirteenth century. The seventeenth-century Baronial Palace, built on a fortress of the 12th century, is indicated by VisitLazio as the stop not to be missed.

  • Where Centro storico, Anticoli Corrado
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Piazza delle Ville e fontana di Arturo Martini

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

In the square, the fountain "Arca di Noè" is carved by Arturo Martini, who lived in Anticoli for three years.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the 11th century in the village; in the churches of the Aniene valley the faces of the women of Anticoli still appear, becoming models very requested by the painters.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Cazzaregli, pizze fritte e ciambelle all'anice

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Cazzaregli are a dough of wheat flour and maize usually seasoned with garlic, oil, chilli and tomato; then fried pizzas and focaccia cooked in oil, sweet or salty, and aniseed doughnuts.
