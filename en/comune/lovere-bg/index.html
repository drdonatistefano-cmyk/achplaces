What to see in Lovere

Borgo on the north shore of Lake Iseo, with Piazza XIII Martiri on the port, the medieval village and its towers, the fifteenth-century Basilica of Santa Maria in Valvendra and the Galleria dell'Accademia Tadini, with works of Canova, in the neoclassical palace on the lakefront.

7 places to visit in Lovere, in the province of Bergamo (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Galleria dell'Accademia Tadini

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the neoclassical palace wanted by Count Luigi Tadini and built between 1821 and 1824 on a project by Sebastiano Salimbeni, with the windows on the lake and the ceilings painted by Luigi Dell'Era; the Academy, established in 1829, is one of the oldest museums in Lombardy. In 23 rooms: the Religion and the Tadini Stele by Antonio Canova, paintings by Jacopo Bellini, Antonio and Bartolomeo Vivarini, Paris Bordon, Giacomo Ceruti and three paintings by Francesco Hayez, as well as porcelain by Meissen, Sèvres and Naples.

  • Where Via Tadini, Lovere
  • Entry €7
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page visitlakeiseo.info

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitbergamo.net · visitlakeiseo.info

Basilica di Santa Maria in Valvendra

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Started in 1473 as a votive sanctuary by families enriched with the trade of wool cloths and consecrated in 1520, from the late Dogotic to the Lombard-Veneto Renaissance, with portal of Damiano Benzoni (1519). Inside the frescoes by Floriano Ferramola (1514), the organ of 1518 with the doors painted by Ferramola and Moretto, the main altar of Andrea Fantoni and the 16th century inlaid choir.

  • Where Via Fratelli Pellegrini, Lovere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page visitlakeiseo.info

Sources: visitlakeiseo.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Borgo antico e torri medievali

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval village preserves the Alghisi, Söca and the round Torricella towers; in Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, an ancient administrative center where the narrow medieval streets converge, stands the bell tower, with an internal uphill path and views of Lovere and the lake. There is also the church of San Giorgio, dating back to the 14th century.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Lovere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page visitlakeiseo.info

Sources: visitlakeiseo.info · in-lombardia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Piazza XIII Martiri e lungolago

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient piazza of the port and market, one of the most beautiful of Lake Iseo, opens on the lakefront of about 2 km walkable; next there is the marina, one of the largest of the European lakes, from which to leave by boat or sailing boat.

  • Where Piazza XIII Martiri, Lovere
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page visitlakeiseo.info

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlakeiseo.info

Museo civico di scienze naturali «Alessio Amighetti»

Museums · Science and university

Established by the Municipality in 1996 and dedicated to the nature of the Camuno-Sebino territory, it bears the name of the naturalist priest who in the nineteenth century introduced the lake of Iseo; it exhibits birds, mammals, shells, butterflies and minerals. Open from Tuesday to Sunday 15-18.

  • Where Via Marconi, Lovere
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page visitlakeiseo.info

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlakeiseo.info

Santuario dei Santi Capitanio e Gerosa

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Sanctuary dedicated to the Loverese saints Captain and Gerosa, a pilgrimage destination from all over northern Italy.

Santuario di San Giovanni in Monte Cala e Corno di San Giovanni

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The sanctuary is reached on foot from Piazzale Bonomelli and has a refreshment point; above the village rise the gray limestone formations of the Corno di San Giovanni and the Rupi della Loverina.
