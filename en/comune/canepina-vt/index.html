What to see in Canepina

Borgo dei Monti Cimini between centuries-old chestnut groves, with the Anguillara Castle, the sixteenth-century Palazzo Farnese seat of the Municipality and the Museum of popular traditions in the former Carmelite convent; on the hill the thirteenth-century church of Santa Corona, destination of a pilgrimage among the chestnut trees.

Museo delle tradizioni popolari

Museums · Themed

It occupies most of the ancient seventeenth-century convent of the Carmelites; set up since 1986 and inaugurated on 17 January 1988, it exhibits in 20 sections objects of rural life, crafts and domestic life, such as ploughs, hoes and looms. During the works there are frescoes of the cloister (1610-1627) attributed to the school of Giuseppe Sebastiani by Macerata and paintings of the mid eighteenth century of the Viterbo Domenico Corvi. Times and access on contact (tel. 0761 327677).

Chiesa di Santa Corona e pellegrinaggio tra i castagni

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

An ancient church of the 13th century on the hill, restored thanks to the Committees of Santa Corona; from the effigy of the saint in Piazzale 1° Maggio starts the path-pilgrimage among the chestnut trees to the church. The patron saint's feast is celebrated on May 14.
