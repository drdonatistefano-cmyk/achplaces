What to see in Vigevano

The city of Sforza in the Ticino Park: Ducal Square wanted by Ludovico il Moro and closed by the Duomo façade, the Sforzesco Castle with the Tower of Bramante and museums ranging from the ancient Lomellina to the shoes.

7 places to visit in Vigevano, in the province of Pavia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Vigevano in the guide with map and filters

Piazza Ducale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built between 1492 and 1494 for Ludovico il Moro, designed by Leonardo da Vinci: rectangular plan with three portico sides and the fourth closed by the façade of the cathedral; the current appearance is due to Bishop Caramuel in the seventeenth century.

  • Where Piazza Ducale, Vigevano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello Sforzesco e Torre del Bramante

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Complex in the highest point of the city, residence of the dukes and then military use; from the square a ramp rises to the courtyard passing under the Tower of Bramante.

  • Where Piazza Ducale, Vigevano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo archeologico nazionale della Lomellina

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the third stable of the castle, opened in 1998 and expanded in 2006 and 2018: finds of the Lomellina from prehistory to the seventh century, including Roman glass, the tomb of a warrior of Valeggio and a treasure of about 1,400 coins.

  • Where Piazza Ducale 20, Vigevano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museilombardia.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo internazionale della calzatura

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the castle since 2003, tells Vigevano as the center of the footwear and the evolution of the shoe as an object of fashion and design.

  • Where Piazza Ducale, Vigevano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
