What to see in Cene

A village of the Seriana Valley at the foot of Monte Bue, along the cycle path of Serio, known for the paleontological park where the Eudimorphodon ranzii, the oldest pterosaur in the world, was discovered; in the village the church of San Zenone, the medieval Torre del Mazzo and the nineteenth-century cotton mill Valty-Wildy.

Parco paleontologico di Cene

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry

After a landslide in 1965 the professor of science Don Antonio Canova found the first fossil, a fragment of fish; since 1970 the area has been protected and dug by the Civic Museum of Natural Sciences of Bergamo, which conserves thousands of fossils of the Triassic (about 220 million years ago): fish, crustaceans, molluscs, reptiles and plants. In 1973 there were found the remains of the Eudimorphodon ranzii, the oldest pterosaur in the world. Opened in 2002, it is a geosite of international importance: visitor center with multilingual panels, LIS and Braille supports, interactive stations and microscopes, naturalistic path to the fossiliferous site and simulated excavation area; free guided tours and workshops during the summer weekends (2026: 13 June-27 September, 14-19). Accessible to the disabled.

open only on weekends from June to September with obligatory booking; in the other months visits by reservation (ValSeriana)

  • Where Via Cava 28, Cene (parcheggio in via Bellora 140)
  • Entry free (reservation required)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Cotonificio Valty-Wildy

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Industrial complex of 1874, built by Swiss entrepreneurs from which it takes its name (today Texcene SpA): the factory and the workers' houses are divided by the Serio, of which it exploited the waters, and connected by a stone bridge. From the second half of the twentieth century it is divided into small artisan activities, but the original structure is still well readable.

  • Where Lungo il Serio, Cene
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Sources: valseriana.eu
