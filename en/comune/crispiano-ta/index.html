What to see in Crispiano

Small rural center about 15 km from Taranto, between Valle d'Itria and gulf, located on the two sides of the Lizzitello Vallone and on the eastern edge of the Regional Natural Park Terra delle Gravine. It is famous for its ... Cento Masserie, awarded in 2015 as the European Destination of Excellence EDEN, for the Pianelle Wood, the Torre Cacace and the rock churches of the Basilian monks.

Le Cento Masserie

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

In the municipal territory there are about one hundred Apulian farms built between the fifteenth and nineteenth centuries, different in size, style and use, some fortified against pirate raids. Many have been restored and welcome guests for food and wine tourism and wellness, others remain agricultural centers; Masseria Lupoli has a museum of peasant civilization. In December 2015, the European Commission (EDEN project) recognised the area . Cento Masserie . European destination of excellence; among the oil products, Negroamaro and Primitivo wine and yellow-red tomato, Slow Food garrison.

Torre Cacace

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Symbol of Crispiano, built at the end of the nineteenth century by the wealthy engineer Carlo Cacace as a family funeral mausoleum. From the top, the view extends over the ravines to the Ionian Sea.

Chiesa rupestre dei Santi Crispo e Crispiniano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Just outside the village, on the eastern side of the Vallone near the Li Castelli farm, a crypt excavated in tuff by the Basilian monks and erected in abbey around the year 1000. It has two rooms, the first with three naves with two entrances, and frescoes of saints of Greek tradition including the Virgin, St. Michael Archangel, St. Nicholas and the Christ Pantocrator; next to an ancient mill. Guided tours, free parking, also hosts an exhibition of handcrafted nativity scenes; tel. +39 099 616453.

Grotta della Stinge (Grotta delle Perle)

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

The most famous cave in the area, at the eastern edge of the Parco Terra delle Gravine where karstism shapes the landscape, with stalactites, stalagmites and characteristic roundish karst formations.
