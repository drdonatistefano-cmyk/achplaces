What to see in Grosseto

The heart of the Maremma, closed by intact hexagonal Medici walls, leads to the Maremma Park, to Etruscan Roselle and to the beaches of Marina and Principina.

25 places to visit in Grosseto, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers 4 of the 6 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Mura Medicee

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Walls born in the 12th century and redesigned in 1574-1593 by Baldassarre Lanci for Francesco I de' Medici: an almost perfect hexagon of about 3 km with five bastions and a citadel, today a green walk with the Old and Corsican gates.

  • Where Mura di Grosseto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Cattedrale di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Started at the end of the thirteenth century by Sozzo Rustichini, with façade in white and pink marble: inside the source and altar of the Madonna by Antonio Ghini, a painting by Matteo di Giovanni and a crucifix attributed to the Vecchietta.

  • Where Piazza Dante, Grosseto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Area archeologica di Roselle

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

One of the most important Etruscan cities, Romanized at the end of the III century BC and abandoned in the Middle Ages: large stretches of walls, the Roman city, a small amphitheatre and the thermal baths.

  • Where Roselle, Grosseto
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
