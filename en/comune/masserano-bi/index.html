What to see in Masserano

Borgo del Biellese orientale, for centuries pontifical fief of Ferrero Fieschi with its own mint: the historic center preserves the seventeenth-century Palazzo dei Principi, today municipality, the Collegiate of the Annunziata and the church of San Teonesto, the oldest in the country, among the hills of vineyards of Bramaterra.

Palazzo dei Principi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

He replaced the ancient fortress: in 1597 the Marquise Claudia di Savoia and his son Francesco Filiberto Ferrero Fieschi built it. In 1624 Masserano, tired of the prince's oppressions, invaded him by destroying the second floor, rebuilt and enlarged by 1634. In the fifth room, called the Zodiac or Throne, ceilings attributed to the Tanzius and friezes attributed to the Gianoli; exposed some coins of the Mint of Masserano. Today it is the seat of the town hall.

  • Where Viale Giovanni Battista Cassinis (ingresso), Masserano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.atl.biella.it

Sources: comune.masserano.bi.it · atl.biella.it

Chiesa di San Teonesto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It is the oldest church in the village, with Romanesque bell tower and simple and elegant façade, reached through narrow cobbled streets and low arcades. It was a parish until 1507, then passed to the Friars Minor Reformed Observants of St. Francis, who expanded and built a convent and cloister with a series of sundials.

Collegiata della Santissima Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church of Masserano along the main road was built in 1486 because Saint Theonesto remained outside the walls. He preserves paintings, wooden sculptures and sacred vestments.

Borgo antico e Piazza Mercato

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient nucleus of the village: in the Lower Borgo there was a small square with the oratory of the Holy Family; in Piazza Mercato stood the Mint, when Masserano was a pontifical fief.
