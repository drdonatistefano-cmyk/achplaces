What to see in Conza della Campania

The centre of the Upper Irpinia on the Ofanto, heir to the ancient Compsa: the ancient village, abandoned after the 1980 earthquake, preserves the Roman forum and the cathedral in the archaeological park, while downstream the artificial lake is an oasis WWF.

Parco archeologico di Compsa

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the hill of Toppa Muraglia, under the ancient village destroyed by the 1980 earthquake, the remains of Compsa, inhabited by the Iron Age, then center of Irpino, Roman town hall and bishop's seat. You can see the hole with part of the porch and the paved with traces of the bronze inscription of Quinto Antisio, the podium of a temple, the area of the amphitheatre then occupied by a castrum, spas, a domus and the road network; at the end of the forum the cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta.

Visitable only by reservation to the Municipality of Conza (0827 39013)

Oasi WWF Lago di Conza

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Artificial invasion of the seventies of about 800 hectares, protected by the WWF since 1999 and indicated as the largest wetland in Campania, with riparian woods, pastures and steppe environments. It has visitor center, didactic workshop, nature trail and observation sheds.
