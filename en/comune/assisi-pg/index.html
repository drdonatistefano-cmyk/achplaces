What to see in Assisi

The city of San Francesco and Santa Chiara on Mount Subasio, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2000: the Basilica frescoed by Cimabue, Giotto, Lorenzetti and Simone Martini, the Temple of Minerva and the Franciscan places outside the walls.

14 places to visit in Assisi, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Basilica di San Francesco

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Started in 1228, two years after the death of the saint, and consecrated in 1253: in the lower church Romanesque arches and frescoes of Cimabue, Giotto and school, Pietro Lorenzetti and Simone Martini (Chapel of St. Martin); in the upper Gothic church the facade in white stone with the rosette and scenes of the Apocalypse of Cimabue.

  • Where Assisi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: whc.unesco.org

Basilica di Santa Chiara

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Started in 1257 by Fra' Filippo da Campello at the opposite end of the historic centre compared to San Francesco: a Latin cross plan, three rampant arches, a square bell tower and a cycle of frescoes on the legend of the saint.

  • Where Assisi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: whc.unesco.org

Cattedrale di San Rufino

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Rebuilt by Bishop Ugone around 1036 and consecrated in 1253, with the Romanesque façade of Umbria; the interior was renovated by Galeazzo Alessi in 1571 and the excavations revealed walls of Roman age.

  • Where Assisi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: whc.unesco.org

Tempio di Minerva

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Temple of Augustine age, 1st century BC, among the best preserved of Roman sacred architecture: it was church, then prison in 1212, again church in 1456 and since 1539 Santa Maria della Minerva.

  • Where Piazza del Comune, Assisi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: whc.unesco.org

Piazza del Comune

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Above the ancient Roman forum, it brings together the Temple of Minerva, the Palazzo del Podestà, the Tower of the People and the municipal palaces.

  • Where Piazza del Comune, Assisi

Eremo delle Carceri

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Sanctuary about 800 meters in the woods of Subasio, 4 km from Assisi, where Francis and the first companions withdrew in prayer; This is a place that is secluded. You can visit the Cave of San Francesco, the cloister with the well, the oratory of Santa Maria and the Maddalena chapel; it was expanded at the beginning of the fifteenth century by the will of San Bernardino da Siena.

  • Where Via Eremo delle Carceri 38, Assisi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it · whc.unesco.org

Basilica di Santa Maria degli Angeli e Porziuncola

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Renaissance Basilica of the sixteenth century by Galeazzo Alessi, in the plain, built to guard the Porziuncola, the chapel where Francis founded the order and where he died; the façade was rebuilt in the nineteenth century after an earthquake.

Parco del Monte Subasio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The 7,169 hectares reserve established in 1995 between Assisi, Spello, Nocera Umbra and Valtopina: olive groves, meadows and oak and holm oak woods, hiking trails, mountain biking and horse riding and paragliding flights; the pink stone of Subasio is that of the monuments of Assisi.

  • Where Assisi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page whc.unesco.org

Sources: italia.it · whc.unesco.org
