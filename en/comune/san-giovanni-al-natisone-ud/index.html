What to see in San Giovanni al Natisone

Municipality of the eastern Friulian plain where Villa de Brandis, built between 1718 and 1724 and left to the Municipality in 1984 by the last Countess Caterina de Brandis, houses a painting from the fourteenth to the nineteenth century and an English park open to the public.

Villa de Brandis e pinacoteca comunale

Museums · House museums

The museum occupies the first floor of the villa, built between 1718 and 1724 by the de Brandis, family documented in Cividale since 1226, and donated to the municipality with all the furnishings by the countess Catherine, last descendant, died in 1984. The picture gallery has about seventy paintings from the fourteenth to the nineteenth century, with names like Jacopo da Bassano, the Domenichino, Francesco Solimena and Pietro Longhi, next to wooden and furnishings from the sixteenth to the nineteenth century, about thirty eighteenth-century prints, landscape frescoes attributed to Antonio Picco and grotesques of the late nineteenth century; library and family archives are in the adjacent Civic Library. Visit by appointment, reservation required (tel. 0432 939590).

Parco di Villa de Brandis

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

Old English park at the back of the villa, with a central pond, designed at the end of the nineteenth century and attributed to the architect Giuseppe Rho; today it is public park, open every day during the day.
