What to see in Mondavio

Medieval village in red bricks surrounded by 780 meters of walls, dominated by the Rocca Roveresca di Francesco di Giorgio Martini (1482-1492) with the historical museum of re-enactment and the siege machines in the moat; in the center the Apollo Theatre, the church of San Francesco and the Collegiata.

10 places to visit in Mondavio, in the province of Pesaro e Urbino (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca Roveresca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Designed by the Sienese Francesco di Giorgio Martini for Giovanni della Rovere and built between 1482 and 1492, it was never attached: inclined walls to divert the blows, ten-sided mast with entrance to the third floor; seen from above a part resembles a crossbow. In the moat are exposed rebuilt siege machines in real size (catapults, overflows, bombs).

  • Where Piazza Giovanni della Rovere, Mondavio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Museo di rievocazione storica e armeria

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the Rocca, two sections: the armory with offensive and defensive weapons from the Middle Ages to the seventeenth century and the reconstruction of environments, furnishings and Renaissance costumes with mannequins. Open every day 9-12 and 15-19; 33% discount with the Terra di Tresori Card.

  • Where Piazza Giovanni della Rovere, Mondavio
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo civico e Pinacoteca

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the cloister of the former convent of San Francesco: paintings of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, paintings of the Barocci school and one attributed to Ridolfi from the convent of the Capuchins, ancient books from the fifteenth to the nineteenth century, ceramics, wooden tabernacles including one of 1620 and a ciborium of Friar Liberale da Macerata, and the table of Olivuccio di Ciccarello da Camerino (1390-1400 circa).

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Mondavio
  • Entry €4, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · letsmarche.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Centro storico, mura e Porta San Francesco

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Red brick village enclosed by a wall of 780 meters; Porta San Francesco is the main entrance and the only remaining of the three doors. In the centre of Piazza della Rovere, Palazzo Giorgi Pierfranceschi, Palazzo dei Malatesta and the fourteenth-century Palazzo della Comunanza.

  • Where Centro storico, Mondavio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it
