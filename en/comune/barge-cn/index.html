What to see in Barge

The village of stone at the foot of Monte Bracco, the "Montagna di Leonardo": the historic center with the castle above Piazza della Madonna, the Baroque parish church of San Giovanni Battista, the ethnographic museum La Brunetta and the former railway workshop of the Barge-Bricherasio line.

5 places to visit in Barge, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo etnografico «La Brunetta»

Museums · Themed

In a building of an ancient rural settlement in the upper part of Torriana, on the slopes of Monte Bracco, exhibits objects from the peasant and artisan world, with exhibitions on the cultivation of chestnut and vine and on the production of wine.

  • Where Località Torriana, Barge
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Designed by Francesco Gallo, master of the Monregal Baroque, it has a floor in local quartzite ('Bargiolina'), portal and niche in Piedmontese Baroque (the statue of the Baptist was placed there in 1911); Romanesque bell tower and baptismal font remain of the oldest building.

  • Where Centro, Barge
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Centro storico e Piazza della Madonna

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The narrow downhill streets lead to the castle that dominates the small and characteristic Piazza della Madonna; the village preserves numerous monuments and churches of historical and artistic importance.

  • Where Piazza della Madonna, Barge
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Ex Officina Ferroviaria

Museums · Themed

Former steam locomotive shed at the end of the Barge-Bricherasio line (launched in 1883, inaugurated on 6 September 1885): two-storey building with central body and two wings, raised roof for the vent of fumes and steam and pit for ashes and water; on the upper floor the drivers stayed. It is indicated as a railway museum, open Saturday and Sunday 16-22.

  • Where Via Assarti, Barge
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.barge.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Monte Bracco

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

It dominates the territory of Barge and is called "Montagna di Leonardo" because Leonardo da Vinci described its stone quarries as "white" as Carrara marble.

  • Where Monte Bracco, Barge
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
