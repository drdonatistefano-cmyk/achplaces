What to see in Badia

Ladin municipality of Alta Badia, with the villages of Badia, La Villa, Pedraces and San Cassiano: under the Sas dla Crusc the sanctuary of La Crusc/Santa Croce, a pilgrimage destination from the sixteenth century, the flowered meadows of the Armentara, the Oies farm where St. Joseph Freinademetz was born and in San Cassiano the Museum Ladin dedicated to the cave bear of the Conturines cave.

5 places to visit in Badia, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museum Ladin Ursus ladinicus

Museums · Science and university

Section of the Museum Ladin, the provincial museum of South Tyrol, opened in 2011 on three floors: at the top the formation of the Dolomites through the fossils typical of the surroundings of San Cassiano, at the center the discovery and excavations of the Conturines cave with the remains of the Ursus Ladinicus, the cave bear lived over 50,000 years ago, and on the ground floor the reconstruction of the cave with the bear hibernating and the puppy. Fully wheelchair accessible; open 10-18 hours from 26.12.2026 to 15.03.2027 and from 02.05.2027 to 05.11.2027.

  • Where Str. Micurá de Rü 26, San Cassiano, Badia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museumladin.it

Santuario di La Crusc/Santa Croce

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Historic pilgrimage site at 2,045 meters below the Sas dla Crusc: perhaps an ancient pagan place of worship, in 1010 there arose a chapel and in the fifteenth century a small church; desecrated by the emperor at the end of the seventeenth century and left in ruins, it was rebuilt by pilgrims and valleys and in the mid eighteenth century enlarged and equipped with bell tower. Documents of 1511 attest to the pilgrimage; the altar preserves relics of saints including Leonardo, Ippolito and Virgilio. In the summer processions go up there, with an important function on the first Sunday of June. It can be reached with the lift from Badia.

  • Where Santa Croce, Badia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Prati dell'Armentara

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Meadows known for the abundance of flowers, crossed by paths 15 and 15A along the stations of the Way of the Cross, with a biotope pond and views of Sas dla Crusc, Marmolada, Sella, Puez-Odle and Putia. The ring from the sanctuary of La Crusc returns to Badia in 9 km (about 2 hours and 45, 793 meters of descent) passing through the Ütia Ranch from André and the farms of Cialaruns, Alfarëi, Ruac and Fussé.

  • Where Badia (partenza dal santuario di La Crusc)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Oies, casa natale di San Giuseppe Freinademetz

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Fraction and place of pilgrimage where Josef Freinademetz, missionary of the Society of the Divine Word died of typhus in China in 1908, beatified in 1975 and canonized in 2003, the first South Tyrolean saint. The winery of the farm was transformed into a chapel, decorated by the artists Kollreider of Lienz and Lois Irsara of Badia. Open every day at 8-20.

  • Where Str. Oies 6, Badia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.freinademetz.it

Sources: freinademetz.it · altabadia.org · suedtirol.info

Grotta delle Conturines

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

High-altitude cave with entrance to more than 2,800 meters, reachable for a long gravel, discovered in 1987 by Willy Costamoling: it is the highest site in the world with remains of bear and lion of caves, and the radiometric dating it already exists 5.8 million years ago, the oldest dating of this type in the Alps.

  • Where Conturines, San Cassiano, Badia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: dolomitiunesco.info · altabadia.org · suedtirol.info
