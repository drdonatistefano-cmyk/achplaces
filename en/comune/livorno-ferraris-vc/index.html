What to see in Livorno Ferraris

Country of the Vercellian plain that took its name from Galileo Ferraris, the 'father of the three-phase current', remembered by the museum in the family house and by the monument in the square; to see also the eighteenth-century parish church of San Lorenzo, the medieval tower, the archaeological museum MAVO with the Roman necropolis and the temple church of Santa Maria d'Isana.

8 places to visit in Livorno Ferraris, in the province of Vercelli (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo sacrario «Galileo e Adamo Ferraris» (Palazzo Ferraris)

Museums · House museums

The museum is housed in Palazzo Ferraris, an ancient residence of the family (16th-XX centuries) overlooking the main square, and is dedicated to Galileo Ferraris and his brother Adam. Galileo, born in the country, demonstrated in 1885 the existence of the rotating magnetic field and in 1891 was recognized in Frankfurt . A cumulative ticket with the MAVO €5.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Monumento a Galileo Ferraris

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The monument to the scientist, born in the village then called Livorno Vercellese, rises in the square dedicated to him next to the museum, in front of the church of San Giovanni Decollato. Ferraris founded the first school of electrical engineering in Italy in 1889 and in 1896 the Italian Electrical Association; he died that year at 49 years of age.

  • Where Piazza Galileo Ferraris 1, Livorno Ferraris
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.livornoferraris.vc.it

Sources: comune.livornoferraris.vc.it

Museo archeologico del vercellese occidentale (MAVO)

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Born from the excavations of preventive archaeology for the high-speed line Turin-Milan (2001-2003), which brought to light the Roman necropolis of Livorno Ferraris (I-IV century A.D.). In a corridor and four halls exhibits local funeral equipment and materials from Balocco, from the necropolis of Crescentino and Motta de' Conti; it is the result of the collaboration between Superintendence, Municipality and Royal Museums of Turin. Open Wednesday and Saturday 9.30-11.30, other days by reservation; cumulative with the Ferraris Museum €5.

  • Where Via Martiri della Libertà 35, Livorno Ferraris
  • Entry €3, reduced €1,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.livornoferraris.vc.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Lorenzo Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient parish church mentioned in 1186, rebuilt several times: the current church, designed in 1728 by Giuseppe Castelli and completed in 1778 by his son Filippo, has a single nave with side chapels and a pronao of four Corinthian columns on six steps. It preserves paintings attributed to Giorgio Alberini (1620 about), palaces by Bernardino Galliari (1776), a wooden crucifix of the sixteenth century and the silver reliquary of San Lorenzo, of the Roman school of the eighteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Galileo Ferraris 39, Livorno Ferraris
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Torrione medievale

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Ancient tower-door of the village, the only remaining of the three walls, renovated and called ...Parsun in 1693. Originally it had a driveway and a pedestrian poster; the traces of the drawbridge remain. The date 1388 engraved on three terracotta tiles recalls a reconstruction after the battle fought that year in the countryside of Livorno between the Red Count and Facino Dog.

  • Where Via Dante Alighieri 12, Livorno Ferraris
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.livornoferraris.vc.it

Sources: comune.livornoferraris.vc.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria d'Isana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small church of the Assumption, today part of the Cascina Isana 3 km from the center: rebuilt after the earthquake of 1117 by the Templars, who had a "mansio" documented since 1208, is indicated as the only temple church of Piedmont reached us. A votive procession takes place on foot every August 15.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.livornoferraris.vc.it

Chiesa della Beata Vergine di Loreto

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Chapel authorized in 1625 and built in the manner of the Loreto house, flanked in 1720 by a building for spiritual exercises and then by a porch. Since 1896 it was the mother house of the Sisters of Santa Maria di Loreto, who bought it back in 1988-1989, restored and transformed into a center of spirituality. Inside a wooden statue of the Virgin in a glass niche.

  • Where Strada SP3 5, Livorno Ferraris
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.livornoferraris.vc.it

Sources: comune.livornoferraris.vc.it

Ecomuseo della coltura del riso

Museums · Themed

In the premises of Tenuta Torrione della Colombara, it collects objects donated and restored by the citizens: kitchen and bedroom furnishings, the workshops of the blacksmith, the masquerade and wicker braider, a school hall of epoch, cars and agricultural wagons. In the same estate a modern rice farm shows the rice supply chain, from cultivation to packaging.

  • Where La Colombara 2, SP7, Livorno Ferraris
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.livornoferraris.vc.it

Sources: comune.livornoferraris.vc.it
