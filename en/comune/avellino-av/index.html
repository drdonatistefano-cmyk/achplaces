What to see in Avellino

The capital of Irpinia: the 36-metre-high Clock Tower, the Cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta and San Modestino, Piazza della Libertà with the Bishop's and Caracciolo palaces, the ruins of the Longobard Castle and the Irpino Museum in the former Bourbon Prison.

7 places to visit in Avellino, in the province of Avellino (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.
