What to see in Quarto

A common plegreo in the basin of the ancient crater of Quarto, crossed by Via Consolare Campana: the Spaccata Mountain, a Roman road cut in the rim of the crater, and the Villa del Torchio, a Roman rustic villa entered into the Archaeological Park of the Campi Flegrei, are flanked by funerary monuments such as the Fescina.

3 places to visit in Quarto, in the province of Naples (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa del Torchio

History and architecture · Archaeology

Roman rustic villa discovered in 2006, one of the few preserved in the Flegrei Fields: quadrangular plant preceded by a courtyard with porch on pillars, probably of the last years of the II century BC and inhabited until the IV-V century A.D. The press (torcularium) and the wine cell, with a consistent number of coins, indicate a wine production center. Since 2021 it is part of the archaeological Park of the Campi Flegrei; you can visit it on request by writing to pa-fleg.communication@cultura.gov.it.

MiC card indicates closed every day, open on request by email

Montagna Spaccata

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Artificial cut in the southern rim of the crater of Quarto, open to pass the Via Consolare Campana: about 78 meters wide at the top, 50 meters high and 290 long, with supporting walls in opus reticulatum and vittatum; to achieve it were removed over 220,000 cubic meters of land. The Municipality indicates it as one of the most magnificent testimonies of Roman road engineering in the Phlegrei Fields.

  • Where Via Campana, Quarto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.quarto.na.it

Sources: comune.quarto.na.it

La Fescina

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Funeral monument of Augustan age or of the first century A.D., so called for the cusp shape similar to a basket to collect; the municipality considers it almost the symbol of the Roman architecture of Quarto.

  • Where Via Brindisi, Quarto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.quarto.na.it

Sources: comune.quarto.na.it
