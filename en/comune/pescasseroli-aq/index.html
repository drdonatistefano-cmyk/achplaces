What to see in Pescasseroli

The main center of the National Park of Abruzzo, Lazio and Molise, at 1167 meters at the foot of the Monte delle Vitelle, where the park was established on September 9, 1922; birthplace of Benedetto Croce, with the historic center in stone, the church of Saints Peter and Paul, Palazzo Sipari, the ruins of Castel Mancino and the Visitor Center of the Park.

7 places to visit in Pescasseroli, in the province of L'Aquila (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Narrow streets, noble palaces and workshops of artisans who produce traditional objects; in the buildings you use the gentle stone, which takes its name from one of the surrounding mountains. The village rises in a wide plain in the heart of the park, where the National Park was established on 9 September 1922.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Pescasseroli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcoabruzzo.it

Chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church (already abbey) of the 12th century, rebuilt in Gothic style after an earthquake and restored several times. In a chapel it houses the wooden statue of the Black Madonna of the Crowned, coming from the church of Castel Mancino, today destroyed.

  • Where Pescasseroli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcoabruzzo.it

Palazzo Sipari

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The nineteenth-century palace belonged to the family of the mother of Benedetto Croce, who was born there in 1866; in time it has hosted illustrious personalities.

  • Where Pescasseroli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcoabruzzo.it

Castel Mancino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

A couple of kilometers from the village, ruins of a strategic fortress, probably belonged to the ancient Peligni, on a cliff near the springs of Sangro; from the site you can enjoy a beautiful view.

  • Where Castel Mancino, Pescasseroli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcoabruzzo.it · italia.it

Centro Visita del Parco e Area faunistica

Museums · Science and university

The National Park Visitor Centre includes the naturalistic museum and the wildlife area, which houses local animals born in captivity or wounded and then cured and rehabilitated in the center. Timetable indicated by Italia.it: 10-17:30, closed on Tuesday.

  • Where Via Colli dell'Oro 31, Pescasseroli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Giardino appenninico

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

Garden that collects numerous plant species of the Apennines and includes a pond.

  • Where Pescasseroli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Piste da sci e Bike Park

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

About 20 km of slopes with cable car, numerous ski lifts, ski school and various levels of routes, more cross-country skiing and ice skating; in summer hiking, horse riding or cycling and a new bike park for mountain biking.

  • Where Pescasseroli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcoabruzzo.it · italia.it
