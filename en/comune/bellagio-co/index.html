What to see in Bellagio

The pearl of Lario is on the tip of the promontory where Lake Como is divided into its branches: the historic centre with steps with the Romanesque San Giacomo, the parks of Villa Melzi and Villa Serbelloni and the hamlets overlooking the water like San Giovanni, with the Museum of instruments for navigation, and the ancient fishing village of Pescallo.

11 places to visit in Bellagio, in the province of Como (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Giardini di Villa Melzi

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Summer residence built in the early nineteenth century for Francesco Melzi d'Eril, Napoleon's ally; the villa remains private and closed, but the English garden, designed by Luigi Canonica and by the botanist Luigi Villoresi (authors also of the Park of Monza), stretches for almost a kilometer along the lake. You can visit exotic plants, sculptural groups (including Dante and Beatrice), the Moorish temple, the Japanese pond with water lilies, the chapel and a small museum; open every day 10-19 (ticket office until 6.30 pm) from the end of March to the beginning of November.

  • Where Via Lungolario Manzoni, Bellagio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.giardinidivillamelzi.it

Parco di Villa Serbelloni

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Park on the steep promontory between the two branches of the lake, wanted by Alessandro Serbelloni, with 18 km of avenues, paths and terraces and the remains of the ancient castle of Bellagio; the villa, of 1854 according to Italia.it, is of the Rockefeller Foundation and hosts conferences and study stays. Not to be confused with the Grand Hotel Villa Serbelloni.

only guided tours (about 90 minutes, departures 11 and 15.30, closed on Monday, groups minimum 6 people) from mid-March to early November, I meet at the office Promobellagio in the tower of Piazza della Chiesa

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa 14, Bellagio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · bellagiolakecomo.com · lakecomo.is · italia.it

Chiesa di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Jewel of the Lombard Romanesque built between 1075 and 1125 by the Masters Comacini, national monument from 1904, with mosaics inside of the early twentieth century; the eighteenth-century bell tower rests on the base of a medieval defensive tower. From the churchyard you can enjoy a beautiful view of the old town.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa, Bellagio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: bellagiolakecomo.com · italia.it

Museo degli strumenti per la navigazione

Museums · Themed

Private collection of more than 200 nautical objects, in an ancient tower house of the village of San Giovanni: Venetian telescopes of the eighteenth century, compasses, sundials, astrolabi, sea chronometers, an armillary sphere and a brass planetarium, with a story of life on ships of the nineteenth century.

seasonal opening (from Easter/April to September-October, morning 10-13; afternoon only by reservation), the two sources Promobellagio give different periods

  • Where Piazza Don Miotti, San Giovanni di Bellagio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website sites.google.com

Sources: sites.google.com · in-lombardia.it · bellagiolakecomo.com · italia.it

Centro storico, salite e lungolago

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village runs through steps, colorful facades and artisan workshops up to the recently renovated lakefront; the Salita Serbelloni was in the Middle Ages the Fossato, main defense of the village, and on the right you can still see the ancient walls. Along the way, the Grand Hotel Villa Serbelloni, a private villa that became a hotel in 1873, and the neo-Gothic Villa Gotica, former Anglican church of the late nineteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Mazzini, Bellagio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · bellagiolakecomo.com · lakecomo.is
