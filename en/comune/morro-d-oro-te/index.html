What to see in Morro d'Oro

The hilly village of Teramano known for the abbey of Santa Maria di Propezzano, linked to a Marian apparition of 715, and for the fourteenth-century church of San Salvatore in the center of the village.

3 places to visit in Morro d'Oro, in the province of Teramo (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Abbazia di Santa Maria di Propezzano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Benedictine monastery that tradition links to an apparition of Our Lady of May 10, 715; the three-nave church and the square cloister are of the fourteenth century, the bell tower of the fifteenth. The first fourteenth-century Santa Porta is attributed to Raimondo di Poggio, school of Atri; frescoes from 1499 with the foundation and an Annunciation, stories of the Virgin in the refectory (1597) and lunettes of the cloister of Sebastiano Majewski (XVII century).

MiC card (updated 2020) indicates closed every day, the municipality indicates free access during opening hours

  • Where SP 22c, Contrada Propezzano, Morro d'Oro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.morrodoro.te.it

Sources: comune.morrodoro.te.it · cultura.gov.it

Chiesa di San Salvatore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the center of the village, in the sixteenth century dependent on Propezzano and from the first fourteenth century of patronage Acquaviva; an inscription cites Pope John XXII, Robert of Anjou, Francesco Acquaviva, 1331 and the Magister Gentilis de Ripatransoni. Rectangular plant of about 20 x 40 meters with three aisles without apse, very similar to Propezzano but later.

  • Where Centro storico, Morro d'Oro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.morrodoro.te.it

Sources: comune.morrodoro.te.it

Giardino Officinale S. Maria di Propezzano

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Garden of medicinal and aromatic herbs with hundreds of varieties (culinary, aromatic, perfume, medicinal, ornamental perennials), present since 1986 in the agricultural estate next to the abbey and recently expanded.

  • Where SP 22c, Contrada Propezzano, Morro d'Oro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.morrodoro.te.it

Sources: comune.morrodoro.te.it
