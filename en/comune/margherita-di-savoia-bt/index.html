What to see in Margherita di Savoia

City of the largest salt pans in Europe, which run for about 20 km parallel to the Adriatic and are a natural reserve since 1977; in the center of the sixteenth-century torrione, the Historical Museum of Salina and the thermal baths that use the mother waters and the sludge of the salt pans.

Riserva naturale delle Saline

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

4,500 hectares, of which about 4,000 are evaporation tanks: the largest salt pans in Europe, born where the sea flooded the low parts of the ancient Lake Salpi and developed especially in the nineteenth century under Ferdinand I of the Two Sicilies; protected since 1977, they host many species of aquatic birds such as herons ashes, gauzes, nitticolar and sgarze tuft. Guided tours only by reservation, every day from sunrise to sunset, for a fee.

Museo storico della Salina

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In an ancient salt plant next to the sixteenth-century Torrione, it collects about a thousand industrial finds (evaporigraphs, scales, scientific instruments, tools for salt collection and processing, samples and containers), copies of letters and documents of Vanvitelli, who designed the work of reclamation, and plastic of the organization of the work; outside working salt pans, machines, railway wagons, locomotives and generators. Unique of its kind in the Central-South.

  • Where Corso Vittorio Emanuele 99, Margherita di Savoia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Pinacoteca comunale «F. Galante Civera» nel Torrione

Museums · Modern art

In the Torrione, a sixteenth-century tower of sighting and defense against Turkish incursions about 12,67 metres high, built after the restoration of the castle of Barletta by the military architect Evangelista Menga (1540 approximately): over 150 paintings, sculptures and drawings by modern artists, including the Pastorello di Francesco Galante, painter of the Neapolitan school of the late nineteenth century, and reproductions of works by Raffaello, Masaccio and Guido Reni.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.margheritadisavoia.bt.it · italia.it

Terme di Margherita di Savoia

Local life and traditions · Spas

Thermal complex facing the sea, on 15,000 m2 of greenery, which uses the mother waters and the sludges of the salt pans: inhalations with paediatric ward, broncholungal treatment and respiratory rehabilitation, aesthetic medicine and Spa Club. The first wooden factory opened in 1930, replaced in 1947 by a masonry building renovated in 1988.

  • Where Piazza Libertà 1, Margherita di Savoia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.termemargherita.it
