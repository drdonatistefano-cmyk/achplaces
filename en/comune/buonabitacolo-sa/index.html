What to see in Buonabitacolo

Village of the Diano Valley at 501 meters, founded on November 2, 1333 by Guglielmo Sanseverino, with a historic center of buildings of the Three-Fourteenth century, frescoed churches and the Museum of the ancient knowledge in the Baronial Palace Picinni-Leopardi.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Founded in 1333 by Guglielmo Sanseverino, lord of Padula, Sanza and Policastro, it preserves many 14th and 15th century buildings, largely restored, including Palazzo Sanseverino and the church of Santa Maria delle Grazie, with a cycle of 15th century frescoes. The heart of town life is Piazza Sandro Pertini, where concerts, fairs and parades are held.

  • Where Piazza Sandro Pertini, Buonabitacolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.buonabitacolo.sa.it

Sources: comune.buonabitacolo.sa.it

Museo degli antichi saperi

Museums · Themed

In the historic rooms of the palazzo baronale Picinni-Leopardi, born from an idea of Maurizio Vignati with the Municipality and the Palazzo Picinni-Leopardi Association thanks to the objects donated by citizens: work tools divided by sections (drawing, leather goods, tailoring, joinery, iron, kitchen) that tell who made and used them.

  • Where Via Roma, Buonabitacolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.buonabitacolo.sa.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.buonabitacolo.sa.it

Chiesa della Santissima Trinità

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It preserves a Renaissance canvas by Orazio Iacobotta da Spinazzola dated 1583, with the family of Alfonso Abbatemarco in prayer, rich in details on clothes, objects and environment, which offers a glimpse of the ancient Good cockpit.

  • Where Via Santissima Trinità, Buonabitacolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.buonabitacolo.sa.it

Sources: comune.buonabitacolo.sa.it

Chiesa di San Domenico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Testimony of the Dominican presence, it combines Renaissance and Baroque elements: facade with stuccoes and a refined portal, interior with a single nave with frescoes on St. Dominic, the apostles and the Dominican saints, side altars with marble decorations and statues.

  • Where Via Roma 149, Buonabitacolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.buonabitacolo.sa.it

Sources: comune.buonabitacolo.sa.it

Cappella di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded in 1703, a place of Marian devotion, with interiors with elaborate decorative details and luminous frescoes of sacred scenes.

  • Where Via Madonna degli Angeli 8, Buonabitacolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.buonabitacolo.sa.it

Sources: comune.buonabitacolo.sa.it

Capitello figurativo dell'antica Certosa

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

A capital carved with intricate motifs, enigma for historians: according to the prevailing hypothesis comes from the ancient Certosa of San Lorenzo, perhaps from a pagan temple that stood on the site.

  • Where Buonabitacolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
