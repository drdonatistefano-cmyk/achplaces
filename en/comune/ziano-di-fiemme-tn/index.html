What to see in Ziano di Fiemme

The village of the Val di Fiemme is 983 m away, with the villages lined up along the river Avisio in front of the Lagorai chain; a base for excursions between Latemar and Lagorai, in summer it is surrounded by the Dolomites cycle path and in winter by the Marcialonga track. In Val Sadole, at the Baita Monte Cauriol, a small museum collects the relics of the Great War.

Piccolo Museo della Guerra alla Baita Monte Cauriol

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Inside the Baita Monte Cauriol, in Sadole (1,600 m), a collection of relics of the First World War found in Val Sadole and in Ziano, collected by Aldo Zorzi and others; the hut also has a small library on the Great War and the mountain. It is reached from the village of Bosin by via Sadole, about 8 km partly dirt road with about 600 m of altitude difference, by car or on foot. Free visit in summer, closed in winter.

  • Where Via Sadole, Località Sadole, Ziano di Fiemme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitfiemme.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · visitfiemme.it

Cima Cauriol, sentiero della Grande Guerra

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Difficult 14.5 km return trip (about 6 hours, 990 m of altitude difference) from the Cauriol Refuge in the Sadole Valley along the Austrian Route, a stone mule track between the remains of tunnels, barracks, walkways, stone stairs, stations and cross-linked roads. On the Italian and Austrian peaks, the peak was contested from winter 1915 to August 1916 and the bombings lowered it by six meters; the summit cross rises between rocks and craters. Exposed traits on porphyric rock.

  • Where Rifugio Cauriol, Val di Sadole, Ziano di Fiemme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitfiemme.it

Sources: visitfiemme.it

Museo all'aperto di Ziano

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Easy ring path of 5.9 km (about 3 hours) starting from Piazza Italia, accompanied by 20 information panels and divided into three shorter laps. Touch Villa Flora, already home to the 13th Austro-Hungarian mountain brigade, and the chapel of Sant'Anna in the hamlet of Roda. Designed by the association Ziano Together with the patronage of the municipality, it is available all year round.

  • Where Piazza Italia, Ziano di Fiemme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitfiemme.it

Sources: visitfiemme.it

Parco fluviale dell'Avisio

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Easy walk of 6 km round trip (1 h 45 min) from Roda towards Predazzo along the Avisio, in a protected area of the Reserve Network: panoramic walkway, two wooden bridges on the tributaries of the Lagorai, a birdwatching tower halfway and an observation point on the Greto. Symbol of the reserve is the alpine tamarisk, which grows almost only in this part of Trentino.

  • Where Località Roda, Ziano di Fiemme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitfiemme.it

Sources: visitfiemme.it

Passeggiata delle Cascatelle

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Path through the woods above the cycle path and the hamlets of Roda and Bosin, between dry walls covered with moss, ferns, strawberries and wild raspberries and small streams that cross the path. The point of return is the Rio Sadole, which is born at the foot of Mount Cauriol; among the trees open glimpses of Ziano.

  • Where Frazioni Roda e Bosin, Ziano di Fiemme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitfiemme.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · visitfiemme.it
