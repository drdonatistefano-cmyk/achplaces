What to see in Arzachena

Heart of the Costa Smeralda, with about 90 km of coast between Porto Cervo, Cannigione, Baja Sardinia and Poltu Quatu; inland the archaeological park gathers eight sites between the V and the II millennium BC, from the necropolis of Li Muri to the nuraghe La Prisgiona and the tombs of Giants of Coddu Vecchiu and Li Lolghi.

Nuraghe Albucciu e Tomba Moru

History and architecture · Archaeology

Nuraghe is a corridor of the Bronze Age, hidden in a grove of olive groves near a granite massif, with the remains of a village around. At 80 meters there is the Tomb Moru, born from the transformation of an allée couverte into the tomb of Giants. It is part of the Arzachena archaeological park, open every day 9-19.

  • Where SS 125 Orientale Sarda, direzione Olbia, Arzachena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Tempio di Malchittu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Just over two kilometers from Arzachena, it is the only nuragic religious building that has so far been investigated in the territory: a megaron temple of the Middle Bronze (XVI-XIV century BC), more ancient than the other temples of this type in Sardinia. Dress and room of about 8 meters with seats, counter for the offers, two niches and fireplace ritual in the center; around a collapsed nuraghe, a circular hut and burials in the tafoni. Open every day 9-19.

  • Where Località Malchittu, Arzachena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Nuraghe La Prisgiona

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On a granite hill above the valley of Capichera, used from the 14th to the 8th century BC: mast with two side towers joined by a curved bastion, entrance architraves of about 3 meters and 7 tons, tholos room almost 7 meters high and a well in the courtyard about 7 meters deep. Around a village of almost one hundred huts on about 5 hectares, with a meeting hut; traces of wine were found in the jugs. Open every day 9-19.

  • Where Valle di Capichera, Arzachena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Tomba di Giganti di Coddu Vecchiu

History and architecture · Archaeology

Built in several phases between ancient and middle Bronze (XIX-XVII century BC), with a four-metre-high stele; Italia.it indicates it as the largest of its kind. It is located about 1 km from La Prisgiona along the giant trail. Open every day 9-19.

  • Where Vicino a La Prisgiona, Arzachena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Tomba di Giganti di Li Lolghi

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Tomb entirely in granite on a hill, dated to 1800 BC and called so for the large overlapping slabs; burial chamber and exedra are respectively 27 and 26 meters long. It is reached by a dirt road about 3 km from the SS 427 towards Calangianus. Open every day 10-18.

Centro di Arzachena e Monti Incappiddhatu

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Until the 17th century Arzachena was a small group of houses around the church of Santa Maria. From the centre, a short walk leads to the Incappiddhatu Mountains, the "Roccia del Fungo" shaped by the wind and used since the recent Neolithic period; in the village there is also the colorful staircase of Saint Lucia and La Box of Time, a museum on the history of the city indicated among the smallest in Italy.

  • Where Centro storico, Arzachena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Cannigione

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Former fishing village on the western shore of the largest and deepest river of the Sardinian north-east, less than ten kilometers from Arzachena: the port is a promenade and from here leave the trips to the National Park of the Archipelago of La Maddalena. Next to the port, the beach is of the city of the sea, with a low backdrop suitable for snorkelling; to the north, the promontory of Punta Occhione looks at the Rock of the Bear.

  • Where Cannigione, Arzachena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
