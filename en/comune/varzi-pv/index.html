What to see in Varzi

Medieval village of the upper Staffora Valley, a feud of Malaspina and entered the most beautiful villages of Italy in 2022: the porticoes of Via del Mercato, the towers of Porta Soprana and Porta Sottana, the Tower of the Witches, the seventeenth-century churches of the confraternities, the Romanesque San Germano dei Cappuccini and, in Cella, the Temple of the Fraternity of Peoples; to taste the Salame of Varzi DOP.

Borgo medievale, portici e porte

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Via del Mercato, considered the original nucleus of the village, is flanked by a double portico built between the 14th and 16th century where the weekly market was held; Via di Dentro, the ancient heart of the village, runs between the Tower of Porta Sottana (quadrangular plant, pointed arches and the hinges of the ancient gates) and the Tower of Porta Soprana, raised in the nineteenth century for the bells of the clock. The Via della Maiolica at the foot of the walls, the Vicolo Behind the Walls with houses built above the belt, the eighteenth-century Palazzo Tamburelli-Malaspina, now Town Hall, and Piazza della Fiera with public gardens.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Torre delle Streghe e Castello Malaspina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Square tower of the XII-XIII century, about 29 meters high, fulcrum of the Malaspina fortified complex, documented as a prison by the Statutes of 1320 and used as a prison until the sixties of the twentieth century; the name comes from the tradition according to which in 1460 there were locked up women and men accused of witchcraft before the burning in the square. With 130 steps you can climb to the panoramic terrace overlooking the village, the Staffora and, with the serene, the Castle of Oramala. The adjacent private castle combines an eighteenth-century facade with sundial, a fifteenth-century wing with the coat of arms of Spino Secco and a part of the thirteenth century.

The interior of the tower is open only on special days, events and guided tours organized by the Municipality or by associations; the castle is privately owned

Chiesa dei Cappuccini (San Germano)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient parish church mentioned as early as 702, the current building was begun at the end of the XII century and completed in the façade at the beginning of the fourteenth century, and documents the passage from the Romanesque to the Gothic: façade in stone with pale and dark bands and brick with frieze of interwoven arches, crochet capitals, triumphal arch with acute sixth. The cappuccinos settled there in 1623; the restoration of 1971-1996 brought back to light truss and semicircular apse. Inside is a statue of the Redeemer Christ of the 13th century and a fragment of the Annunciation of the Boxilius brothers (about 1484).

Chiesa dei Rossi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1636 by the Confraternity of the Most Holy Trinity, it owes its name to the red capes of the confreres; next stood a hospice for pilgrims. It preserves elaborate balustrade and altar, furnishings and carved walnut choir, a chair dated 1785, an angel in golden wood of 1684 and a painting with the Blessed Panacea.

Chiesa dei Bianchi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The seventeenth-century church of the Confraternity of the Battuti (of the Gonfalone), so called for the white clothes of the confreres, on the north side of the country; the interior has a four-leaf plant.

Tempio della Fraternità dei Popoli

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Sanctuary and shrine of peace at about 700 meters, on the remains of the church of San Martiano a Cella, wanted by Don Adamo Accosa, chaplain of the Alps, and inaugurated in 1958: it is built with stones from about one hundred places affected by the Second World War (from Coutances, Montecassino and El Alamein to Hiroshima and Nagasaki), spires of the Duomo of Milan fallen in the bombings of 1943, a baptismal source by the shutter of a cannon of the battleship Andrea Doria and a crucifix made of weapons. Open every day 9-18:30 from April to October; from November to March Saturday and Sunday 9-17, other days by reservation.

  • Where Cella di Varzi, Varzi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
