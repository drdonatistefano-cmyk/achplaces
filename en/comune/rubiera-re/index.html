What to see in Rubiera

Cittadina sulla Via Emilia al passo del Secchia, born around the thirteenth-century castle of the Municipality of Reggio then renovated by the Estensi: in the center of Palazzo Sacrati, the civic tower and the late Herberia theater liberty; outside, the fifteenth-century Hospital Court, the Romanesque parish church of Ss. Faustino and Giovita and the expansion boxes of the Secchia.

Centro storico, rocca e Palazzo Sacrati

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the thirteenth century the Municipality of Reggio built a castle to defend the Via Emilia and the passage on the Secchia, renovated by the Estensi in the second half of the fifteenth century; of the fortress there are few leftovers, mostly transformed into houses and offices. It was a prison of state: in his pit he was beheaded in 1822 Don Giuseppe Andreoli. Along the main street the Renaissance Palazzo Sacrati, already of the Boiardos, now town hall, opposite the eighteenth-century church of the SS. Annunziata, then the parish church of the Ss. Donnino and Biagio (1703-18), the former convent of Palazzo Rainusso and the civic and bell towers of the sixteenth century.

  • Where Via Emilia, Rubiera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Teatro Herberia

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Opened on 14 January 1926 with the Bohème di Puccini, designed by engineer Antonio Panizzi and architect Italo Costa; animated until the 1950s, it became a cinema. Purchased by the Municipality, restored since 1988 preserving its late liberty characters and reopened in 1998, it hosts up to 420 spectators with stage to mobile platforms.

  • Where Piazza Antonio Gramsci 1b, Rubiera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Corte Ospitale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The fifteenth-century complex was created to accommodate travellers and pilgrims at the ford of the Secchia, on the streets towards Sassuolo, Frassinoro, Lucca and Rome; it was then a farm. Today, with a porch courtyard and tower, is home to the theatre center La Corte Ospitale; in the church of Santa Maria (16th century) remains fragments of frescoes of the Garofalo (1543).

Pieve dei Ss. Faustino e Giovita

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

One of the oldest Romanesque churches in the diocese, cited in a document of 945 and of Matilda foundation, with few ancient remains. It preserves a 13th century fresco with the Madonna and Child and a school table of the Garofalo.

  • Where San Faustino, Rubiera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Parco fluviale e Casse di espansione del Secchia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The park of the Secchia, established in 1996 between Rubiera and Modenese, you can walk, bike or horse, better in spring and autumn. The expansion boxes, of artificial origin, form a reserve of wet areas between Rubiera, Campogalliano and Modena with a center of environmental education; in winter they are suitable for birdwatching.

  • Where Casse di espansione del Secchia, Rubiera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
