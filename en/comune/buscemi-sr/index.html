Museo civico «I luoghi del lavoro contadino»

Museums · Themed

Ten museum units reconstruct life and peasant work: the house of the massarium, the palment, the shop of the blacksmith, the house of the laborer, the shops of the calderaium, the carpenter, the shoemaker and the tannerbrocche and the mill ("u trappitu") in a special building the didactic workshop and sections on the wheat cycle and stone processing in the Val di Noto, as well as a water mill and a documentation centre of the iblea popular life.

  • Where Via Libertà 10, Buscemi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.buscemi.sr.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.buscemi.sr.it

Santuario della Madonna del Bosco

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill of San Nicolò, about 1 km in front of the village: the church and the ancient hermitage collapsed with the earthquake of 1693, but the fresco of the Virgin and Child, of the sixteenth century, remained unharmed and was incorporated into the new sanctuary. The patroness is celebrated on the last Sunday of August.

  • Where Abbeveratoio della Madonna del Bosco, SR9, Buscemi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.buscemi.sr.it

Sources: comune.buscemi.sr.it

Resti del Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the plateau at the south entrance of the village the Requesens built it in the sixteenth century on the ruins of an Arabic fortress; destroyed by the earthquake of 1693 and rebuilt smaller, it was a convent of Minors Observing in the eighteenth century and cemetery in the nineteenth century. There are still some walls in local white stone.

  • Where Ingresso sud del paese, Buscemi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.buscemi.sr.it

Sources: comune.buscemi.sr.it
