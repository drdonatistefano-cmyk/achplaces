What to see in Letino

Borgo del Parco Regionale del Matese between Lake Letino and Lake Gallo, with the Grotta del Cauto where the river Lete falls and, at the top of the village, the Sanctuary of Santa Maria del Castello at 1,200 meters.

6 places to visit in Letino, in the province of Caserta (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Grotta del Cauto

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Large karst cave in which the river Lete flows under the ground, explored by the cavers from the twenties of the twentieth century, with stalactites and stalagmites; on the opposite side overlooks the valley. The waters cross Mount Favaracchi and fall into the waterfall in the Rava di Prata before joining the Volturno.

  • Where Oltre la diga sul Lete, Letino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it · parcoregionaledelmatese.it

Lago di Letino

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Basin born from the artificial barrier of the river Lete, between the lake of Gallo and the village, in a well preserved natural landscape; on the lake there is an information point (the "Kioschetto").

  • Where Lago di Letino, Letino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Santuario di Santa Maria del Castello

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the highest point of the village, at 1,200 meters, on the site of a Longobard castle then enlarged by the Normans, with forms of the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries; there you go up for the most important religious festival, on the third Sunday of September.

  • Where Sommità del paese, Letino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the historic center with facade and stone bell tower.

  • Where Centro storico, Letino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Museo delle arti e tradizioni popolari

Museums · Themed

It collects ethnographic material on traditions, superstitions and arts of Matese's agro-pastoral culture, with attention to the different models of the traditional women's costume of Letino.

  • Where Via Molise, Letino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
