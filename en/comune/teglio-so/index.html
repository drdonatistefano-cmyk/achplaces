What to see in Teglio

Borgo at 856 meters on the Rhaetian side of the Valtellina, Slow Town and home of the Pizzoccheri, which according to tradition gave the name to the valley; in the center the Renaissance Palazzo Besta, the only state museum in the province of Sondrio, the tower Âde li beli miri Â Â and a sacred enclosure with the church of Sant'Eufemia and the Romanesque San Pietro.

Palazzo Besta e Antiquarium Tellinum

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

A noble residence built at the end of the fifteenth century and transformed into a Renaissance court, frequented by artists, writers and philosophers; it was the Besta until 1726, then divided and used as a farm, bought by the State in 1911 and opened as a museum in 1927. The courtyard has a double loggia on frescoed columns with episodes of the Eneide and medallions of the owners; on the noble floor the salon of honor with the Orlando Furioso, the dining room with ceiling umbrella and the Hall of Creation with stories of Genesis; on the second floor frescoes from other sites Valtellina, Stüe and an ethnographic collection. On the ground floor the Antiquarium Tellinum with engraved stem of the Copper Age (III millennium BC) found in Teglio since 1940, including the Mother Goddess of Caven. Tours from 35 to 75 minutes, also for families and accessible.

Chiesa di Sant'Eufemia e recinto sacro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Mother Church of the Castellanza di Teglio, at the foot of the castrum Tilii: under the current building, work of the XV and XVII centuries with three naves with cruising vaults, emerged the foundations of three previous churches, the first of the V-VI century. On the façade there is a marble rosette with the Madonna and the portal of the Rodari of 1506. In the sacred enclosure next to it are the Oratory of the Disciplini Bianchi, with the fifteenth-century fresco of the Danza Macabra, the Oratory of the Black Brothers and the ossuary of the old cemetery.

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the 11th century in the center of the village, with simple and pure lines: unique ornaments of the flat pilasters of the apse and the double windows of the bell tower with blind arches. Inside, frescoes from the end of the fourteenth century with Christ the Pantocrator, the parable of the Virgins, the Fathers of the Church, the Apostles and the Evangelists. Visitable on weekends.

  • Where Via Fratelli Lazzaroni 2, Teglio

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Torre «de li beli miri»

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Symbol of Teglio, on a hill south of the village among the pine trees: it is what remains of a medieval castle built on the ruins of a previous Roman foundation, with square plan with slits and raised access. From its position it dominates the Valtellina; the interior is visited with guides only in some periods of the year, by reservation at the Infopoint in Teglio.

  • Where Collina a sud del paese, Teglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · valtellina.it

Val Caronella e borgo di Carona

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valley in the Orobie Valtellinesi Park, Site of Community Importance with the Val Bondone. It starts from the ancient village of Carona, with the fifteenth-century church of Sant'Omobono: an easy dirt road to the huts of Pra' di Gianni (1,350 m) and the equipped area of Pra' della Valle (1,370 m), with views of the waterfalls; more on the Caronella mountain hut (1,860 m) and, for trained hikers, the Passo di Caronella (2,600 m, 4-5 hours) towards the lakes of Barbellino.

  • Where Carona, Teglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Mulino di Menaglio e campi di grano saraceno

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

Ancient water mill in the village of San Rocco, on the edge of the municipal territory, where the beating of the wheat takes place; from here you reach the fields of buckwheat in bloom. In Teglio, the Accademia del Pizzocchero keeps the original recipe of the pizzoccheri.

  • Where Frazione San Rocco, Teglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it
