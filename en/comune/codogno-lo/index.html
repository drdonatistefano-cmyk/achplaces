What to see in Codogno

Borgo della Bassa Lodigiana which owes its name to quince, linked to the Trivulzio and to Saint Francesca Cabrini, who founded the Missionaries of the Sacred Heart here; to see the parish church of San Biagio with paintings by Callisto Piazza and Daniele Crespi, the church of Graces with the Cabrinian Museum, the sanctuary of the Madonna di Caravaggio and the Art Collection Lamberti.

Parrocchiale di San Biagio e Santa Maria Immacolata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Chapel rebuilt in 1491 in Renaissance forms and restored in 1524, with Mannerist façade in terracotta by architect Regorino (1584) and bell tower completed in 1623. Inside, with a Latin cross and three naves, the "Assunta" by Callisto Piazza, the "Sacred Family" by Cesare Magni, long attributed to the Luini, the "Madonna" with the Child" by Daniele Crespi, baroque chapels, organ Serassi and an ostensory of coral, gold and precious stones donated by Cardinal Trivulzio.

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre, Codogno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: old.comune.codogno.lo.it · italia.it

Piazza XX Settembre e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient Piazza Maggiore became under the Trivulzio the administrative, legal and commercial center of the village; next, the Piazza del Mercato preserves the medieval function. The four-tower castle remains only traces of the foundations and the moat; between Via Verdi and Via Mazzini survives the cloister of Santa Chiara, and in Via Garibaldi the Palazzo Trivulzio (now Dansi), reworked in neoclassical forms by the Barattieri.

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre, Codogno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: old.comune.codogno.lo.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie («dei Frati»)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Wanted by Teodoro Trivulzio on a project by Marco Antonio Barattieri (1620), a single nave, it is reached from a quadriportico with the chapels of the Via Crucis (Pietro Antonio Magatti, 1725). It preserves seventeenth-century wooden bas-reliefs by Antonio and Francesco da Sirone, a wooden altar painted by Luigi Miradoro (1652), a San Luigi by Daniele Crespi and wooden statues from 1584; the eighteenth-century cloister is by Carlo Francesco Monticelli. The convent, suppressed in 1780, was purchased by Mother Cabrini for the first house of the Missionaries.

  • Where Via Santa Francesca Cabrini, Codogno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: old.comune.codogno.lo.it · italia.it

Museo Cabriniano

Museums · House museums

In the house where Saint Francesca Cabrini lived (1850-1917), born in the neighboring Sant'Angelo Lodigiano and foundress in Codogno of the Missionaries of the Sacred Heart of Jesus: objects, garments and memories of the saint and of the origins of the Institute, with the cells of the sisters visited. In the church of Tabor the relic of the heart of the saint.

  • Where Via Santa Francesca Cabrini 3, Codogno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Santuario della Madonna di Caravaggio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Carlo Antonio Albino's work on the site of a Marian sacellus: isolated temple with Greek cross with longitudinal elongated arm and central crypt lowered in polychrome marbles; bell tower completed in 1772 and dome added in 1844 on a project by Giuseppe Squassi. In the altars paintings of Lombard artists of the seventeenth century.

  • Where Codogno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa di San Teodoro («del Cristo»)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Carlo Antonio Albino's work completed in the first half of the 17th century, with a dome on high octagonal tiburium; it preserves an ancient image of the Crucifix painted on a wall, very venerated, and a Santa Rita by Enrico Groppi.

Chiesa della Santissima Trinità

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Probably started at the end of the sixteenth century, with three naves, with a high altar in rare marble and two baroque altars; it preserves canvases of Biella (XVII-XVIII century), a San Bassiano perhaps of the shop of the square and a sixteenth-century wooden statue of Saint Lucia.

Chiesa di Santa Maria della Neve

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1604 for the Confraternity of the Gonfalone and rebuilt several times, with a single nave: two wooden statues of the seventeenth century (Madonna and Archangel Gabriel), small paintings of the Evangelists, a large eighteenth-century canvas of the Risen Christ and a small precious organ in the apse.

Vecchio Ospedale Soave

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Built between 1779 and 1781 in neoclassical forms as a care and health facility; renovated, it now houses exhibitions, cultural events and the civic library.
