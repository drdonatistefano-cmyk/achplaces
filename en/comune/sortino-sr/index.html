What to see in Sortino

Iblean country between the gorges of the Anapo, one of the two accesses to the rock necropolis of Pantalica, UNESCO World Heritage with Syracuse, with the Civic Museum of the Opera of the Puppets of the Puglisi family and the museum house of traditional beekeeping.

Necropoli rupestre di Pantalica

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

In the gorge excavated by the Anapo and the Calcinara, about 5,000 grotticella tombs dug into the rock between the XII and the IX-VIII century B.C., a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2005. At the top of the remains of the Anaktoron, the Prince's Palace, in the necropolis of San Martino tombs with tholos and Byzantine catacombs such as the Cave of Sant'Anna; of the Byzantine villages remain San Micidiario (about 150 houses and a church), the frescoed oratory of San Nicolicchio and the Cave of the Crucifix.

Riserva naturale di Pantalica e Valle dell'Anapo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Reserve oriented established in 1997 on about 3,712 hectares between Ferla, Cassaro, Sortino, Palazzolo Acreide and Buscemi: poplars, willows and spontaneous oriental plane, orchids of the Iblei, macrostigma trout, hawks and eagles, and the Cave of the Bats. Along the valley the former Syracuse-Vizzini railway of the early twentieth century is today a pedestrian and cycle path with some galleries.

  • Where Valle dell'Anapo, Sortino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: orbs.regione.sicilia.it · italia.it

Museo civico dell'Opera dei Pupi «Antica famiglia Puglisi»

Museums · Themed

In the former convent of San Francesco, the collection of Don Ignazio Puglisi, who also painted the background canvases of his shows: over thirty puppets arranged in scenes and the traditional theatre; some pieces were exhibited at the Museo Teatrale alla Scala in Milan. The Opera dei Pupi has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2001.

  • Where Piazza San Francesco, Sortino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Casa museo dell'apicoltura «A Casa do Fascitraru»

Museums · Themed

Reconstruction of the beekeeper's house, the workshop where the hives were prepared and honey extracted from the bee-buyers; guided tours.

  • Where Via Gioberti, Sortino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
