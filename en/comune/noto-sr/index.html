What to see in Noto

Rebuilt on a new site after the earthquake of 1693 and protected by UNESCO among the late Baroque towns of Val di Noto: the Cathedral at the top of the stairs, Palazzo Ducezio and, on the coast, the reserve of Vendicari.

5 places to visit in Noto, in the province of Syracuse (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Noto in the guide with map and filters

Cattedrale di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Started at the beginning of the eighteenth century and completed around 1776, with the large façade between two bell towers at the top of a staircase; partly collapsed in 1996, it was rebuilt by hand after brick; in the naves the Stations of the Via Crucis by Roberto Ferri.

  • Where Piazza del Municipio, Noto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · whc.unesco.org

Palazzo Ducezio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Venue of the Town Hall in front of the Cathedral, built between 1742 and 1761 on a project by Vincenzo Sinatra, with portico of Ionic columns on three sides; inside the Hall of Mirrors, oval with stuccoes in Louis XV style; the upper floor was added in 1951.

  • Where Piazza del Municipio, Noto
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · smarteducationunescosicilia.it

Centro storico barocco

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The entire old town, rebuilt on a new site after 1693, is part of the UNESCO site of the late Baroque towns of Val di Noto.

  • Where Noto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: whc.unesco.org · italia.it

Riserva naturale di Vendicari

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Protected wetland of 1,512 hectares on the Ionian coast between Noto and Marzamemi, with ponds, marshes and ancient salt pans, the tuna trap, the Swabian Tower and the beaches of Eloro, Marinelli, Calamosche and Vendicari.

  • Where Costa di Vendicari, Noto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Villa Romana del Tellaro

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Late-Imperial villa on the right bank of Tellaro, identified in 1971 after underground excavations; over twenty years of excavations have brought to light the central body around a peristyle.

MiC card indicates closed every day

  • Where Contrada Tellaro, Noto
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
