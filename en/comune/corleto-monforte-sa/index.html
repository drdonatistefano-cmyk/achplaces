What to see in Corleto Monforte

In the heart of the Alburni Mountains, inside the Cilento National Park, Vallo di Diano and Alburni, Corleto Monforte has been hosting since 1997 the Museo naturalistico degli Alburni, with over 1,300 vertebrates and 20,000 insects of European fauna, and just outside the village the Sanctuary of Maria SS.ma della Selice, overlooking the valleys of Fasanella and Calore.

Museo naturalistico degli Alburni - Fondazione I.Ri.Di.A.

Museums · Science and university

Founded in 1997 in a central position with respect to the Alburni Mountains, reference of its activities, it is managed by the I.Ri.Di.A. Foundation. (Institute for Environmental Research and Didactics). The permanent exhibition of European fauna has over 1,300 vertebrates, with a remarkable collection of birds (about 1,200 European species according to the Park), over 60 species of mammals, decapod crustaceans of the Mediterranean and over 20,000 insects; the museum also carries out research, teaching and wildlife monitoring.

Santuario di Maria SS.ma della Selice

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

At the slopes of the Monticelli hill, just over a kilometer from the village, look over the valley of Fasanella and of the heat up to the Gulf of Naples on clear days. The origins of the cult are dated back to the sixth century; the name Selice comes from the mule track that led to the first sacellus. The present building, rebuilt in 1960 after a landslide on the original plan, has a single nave covered in travertine, mosaic rosette, stained glass windows with the seven sacraments and a stone bust of the Madonna in Byzantine style; next, the ancient stone fountain called "Moccio" . It has been a diocesan shrine since 2016 and the festival falls on Tuesday after Pentecost.
