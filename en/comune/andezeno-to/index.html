What to see in Andezeno

Chierese hilly country gathered around the "chiocciola" of the historic center, with the medieval tower of the castle become civic bell tower, the Baroque parish church of San Giorgio di Giovan Battista Casasopra, the farmhouse and the mill of Cesole and the small museum of rural traditions of the Balbiano winery.

Chiocciola di Andezeno (centro storico)

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The original nucleus on the hill overlooking the territory, with a panoramic view. Almost destroyed in 1543, it retains a few medieval traces and the walls of the eighteenth-century bastion; among the buildings, the former fifteenth-century brick nursery with four sharp arched windows, the church of the "Batù" (1596), the sixteenth-century palace of the Counts Balbiano, the Palazzo degli Ormea and the eighteenth-century Villa San Carlo of the Villa family.

  • Where Via Abate Villa, Via Balbiano, Via Cavour, Andezeno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.andezeno.to.it

Sources: comune.andezeno.to.it

Castello e torre campanaria

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The "castrum" of Andezeno is mentioned in the peace of 1261 between the accounts of Biandrate and the Municipality of Chieri. The nucleus of the 12th century was destroyed in the sixteenth century by the Spanish army; on the top remains the defence tower, then transformed into a bell tower and the clock in the facade, while parts of the structure were incorporated into the omea, Berta and Villa residences.

  • Where Vicolo Comunale, centro storico, Andezeno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.andezeno.to.it

Sources: comune.andezeno.to.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Designed by Giovan Battista Casasopra, pupil of Vittone, built in 1753-1759 and consecrated in 1764: baroque, with a single nave with three chapels connecting on the side and brick facade marked by pilasters and columns. Inside the relics of Santa Giustina on the main altar, frescoes of the vault of Luigi and Rodolfo Morgari (1853 and 1858), organ of 1866 and altarpieces attributed to Antonio Sariga and workshop.

  • Where Vicolo Comunale 2, Andezeno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.andezeno.to.it

Sources: comune.andezeno.to.it

Mulino e Cascina Cesole

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

In 1259 Chieri and Andezeno met in the village since 1034; in 1259 they met. Today we see an eighteenth-century farmhouse closed with barn, stable and a baroque chapel of the Madonna del Rosario, repainted at the beginning of the twentieth century; along the road, among the brambles, remain the walls and the wheel of the old watermill on the Rio Santena. You reach it on a tree-lined avenue.

  • Where Via Cesole 23, località Cesole, Andezeno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.andezeno.to.it

Sources: comune.andezeno.to.it

Museo Balbiano

Museums · Themed

Small museum of rural traditions was born when, at the end of the eighties, Francesco Balbiano transformed the family farm and decided to restore the objects of peasant life instead of eliminating them; it is set up in the old carriagehouse and was inaugurated in 1990. Visits by reservation.

  • Where Corso Vittorio Emanuele II 1, Andezeno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.balbiano.com
