What to see in Soragna

Borgo della Bassa Parmense was born around the Rocca Meli Lupi, a 14th-century castle that became a princely residence and still inhabited by the family: inside the stuccoes of the Bibiena and the Galleria dei poeti; in the village the neoclassical synagogue with the Jewish Museum Fausto Levi and the Parmigiano Reggiano Museum in an ancient cheese factory.

Rocca Meli Lupi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built in 1347 by the Marquis Bonifacio and Antonio Lupi, with a square plan with four corner towers and moat, it was transformed into a princely residence at the end of the sixteenth century and is still part of the Meli Lupi family. Inside the Sala del Baglione with the grotesques of Cesare Baglione (1580), the Sala Gialla with the Fatigue of Hercules of Niccolò dell'Abate, the Gallery of the poets and the Sala degli Stucchi of Bibiena (1695); in the oratory of Santa Croce the Gothic tomb of Ugolotto Lupi (1351). Behind the Fortress there is a garden that leads to a neo-Gothic fort.

  • Where Piazza Meli Lupi 5, Soragna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sinagoga e Museo ebraico «Fausto Levi»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Neoclassical synagogue inaugurated in 1855, with Corinthian columns, stuccoes by Antonio Rusca, grate of golden bronze in the matroneo and the Ark saint. The museum, entitled to the president of the Jewish community of Parma who collected the materials, tells in seven sections the Jewish communities of Parma and Piacenza since 1555: documents, ritual objects, ancient prayer books, wedding contracts (ketubbot), racial laws and the Shoah. Guided tour of about 45 minutes; from March to June and from September to November on Sunday 11-13 and 15-18, during the other months by reservation.

  • Where Via Cavour 43, Soragna
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Museo del Parmigiano Reggiano

Museums · Themed

In an ancient cheese factory with circular plan close to the wall of the garden of the Fortress: tools and tools of cheese processing and historical photographs. It is part of the Museums of Food in the province of Parma.

  • Where Soragna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Centro storico, chiese e oratori

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified village with a regular system with parallel blocks of the early sixteenth century. In the village the oratory of Sant'Antonio in Piazza Garibaldi (1696, project by Francesco Galli Bibiena), the church of the Blessed Virgin of Carmine founded in 1661 by Diofebo III Meli Lupi with Baroque altar, the church of San Giacomo (1755-1769) with organ of the brothers Serassi and the Park of the Remembrances of 1926.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Soragna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parmigianoreggiano.museidelcibo.it · italia.it
