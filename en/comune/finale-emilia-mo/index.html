What to see in Finale Emilia

Ancient river port extended over the Panaro, wounded by the earthquake of 2012: the Cathedral of Saints Philip and James reopened in 2024, the ruins of the Castle of the Fortresses and the Tower of Modena became symbol of the earthquake, the Jewish cemetery and the oasis of the Meleghines.

7 places to visit in Finale Emilia, in the province of Modena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo dei Santi Filippo e Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Involucre and bell tower of the fifteenth century, facade and interior with three aisles of the eighteenth century: Baptism of Christ of Bastianino, fifteenth-century Crucifix and paintings by Giuseppe Maria Crespi and other Emilian painters. Seriously damaged by the earthquake of 2012, with the collapse of part of the facade and of the times, it was restored with seismic improvement and reopened to worship in May 2024.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Castello delle Rocche (Rocca Estense)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built on the medieval fortifications guarding the Panaro: the current structure is from 1402, designed by Bartolino da Novara, enlarged in 1425-30 with Giovanni da Siena; extensive eagles, courtyard with triple loggia on Gothic pillars and frescoes of the fifteenth century. With the 2012 earthquake the tower collapsed and the castle was badly damaged.

Seriously damaged by the 2012 earthquake, not open to visitors: only seen from outside

  • Where Finale Emilia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it

Torre dei Modenesi

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The clock tower of 1212, rebuilt in the fifteenth century, next to the gate of San Lorenzo in 1250: collapsed with the earthquake of 2012, with the dial of the broken clock in the middle became the symbol of the earthquake.

Remain the rubble of the tower, visible from the outside

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Cimitero ebraico

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the oldest and most evocative of Emilia-Romagna: you enter it from an iron gate with the five-pointed star and the word "shalom"; 57 burials have been identified.

Not always open: check timetables and access with the cemetery office before visiting

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it

Oasi faunistica Le Meleghine

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Artificial wetland born as an example of phytopurification of the waters, recognized by the Region as a special protection zone: real germans, swans, cormorants and herons, along a cycle-naturalistic itinerary. From Mirandola you will find the Chico Mendes cycle path on the old railway.

  • Where Finale Emilia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it

Museo del Territorio

Museums · City history

In the building of the bus station: materials of local culture including ancient measuring instruments, a plastic of the center as it was before the deviation of the Panaro (1880-1886) and a section on the final Jewish community of the late nineteenth century.

  • Where Viale Stazione 1, Finale Emilia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Chiesa del Santissimo Rosario

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Started in 1572 and rebuilt between 1676 and 1689, with stuccoes by Paolo Frisoni and Michele Rigoli, the altarpiece of the Madonna del Carmine by Giovanni Masuti (1695) and works by Francesco Stringa and Carlo Cignani.

After the earthquake of 2012 many buildings in the center remained unworkable: check if the church is open
