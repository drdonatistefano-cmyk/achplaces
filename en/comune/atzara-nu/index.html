What to see in Atzara

Medieval village of Mandrolisai, on the south-western slopes of Gennargentu, with the urban installation of the Catalan era and the low granite houses; portrait by the Spanish and Sardinian painters of the early twentieth century, today collected in the MAMA museum, has the Gothic-Catalan parish church of Sant'Antioco and is land of Mandrolisai wine.

MAMA, Museo d'arte moderna e contemporanea Antonio Ortiz Echagüe

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

In the historic house Sias, on three floors, tells the school of Atzara, born when in the early twentieth century Eduardo Chicharro, Antonio Ortiz Echagüe and Bernardo de Quirós, attracted by the costumes of the village, stayed there, followed by Ballero, Ciusa, Figari, Biasi and Delitala. About one hundred works from the late nineteenth century to the early thirteenth century, including the Mujores de Mamojada of Ortiz and the evening appeal of Ballero, a photographic section on the village and a room for temporary exhibitions; wheelchair accessible.

MiC card (Add. 2021) indicates the museum closed every day (tel. 0784 65508)

  • Where Piazza Antonio Ortiz Echague, Atzara
  • Entry €2, reduced €1,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Antioco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Gothic-Catalan church of the late fifteenth century with trachyte façade; inside two wooden altars of local woodcarvers, a sixteenth-century statue of the Madonna with Child and silver of the sixteenth century. In mid-November it hosts the feast of the patron.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Antioco, Atzara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Centro storico e magasinos

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Urban plant of Catalan-Aragonese era with low granite houses and ceilings with oak beams, frames of doors and windows carved in chisel in the oldest districts (Josso, Sa Montiga, Fruscu); next to Sant'Antioco the peasant houses with large portals and walled courtyards, the Muggianu house with two Aragonese windows and, among alleys and squares, the Magasinos, private cellars like that of the Su Conte palace.

  • Where Centro storico, Atzara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
