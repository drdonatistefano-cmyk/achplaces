What to see in Varallo Pombia

Country north of Novara, on the road to Lake Maggiore, in the area of the civilization of Golasecca and mentioned for the first time in a document of 885. The heart is the seventeenth-century Villa Soranzo, now the seat of the Municipality with the Archaeological Museum and the Pinacoteca "Cesare Belossi" (Cesare Belossi), next to the Romanesque parish church of Saints Vincenzo and Anastasio and the sanctuary of Our Lady of the Rosary.

Villa Soranzo e giardino

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Built after the middle of the seventeenth century by Cardinal Federico Caccia, he then passed to Ferrero, Gallarati Scotti and Simonetta; in 1972 the heirs of Countess Teresa Bollini Mocenigo Soranzo sold it to the municipality, which has its seat with library, museum and picture gallery. The stairs of honour in pink granite climbs into an octagonal room with four eighteenth-century statues of the Seasons; on the south façade the coats of arms of the Hunt and of Pope Innocent XII and a large baroque sundial. The romantic garden has camellias, azalee, rhododendrons and palm trees and a rare Chinese michaelia at the beginning of the camellias avenue.

  • Where Via Simonetta 3, Varallo Pombia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismonovara.it

Sources: turismonovara.it

Museo archeologico di Varallo Pombia

Museums · Archaeological

Born from the initiative of a group of young people with the support of the Superintendence of the Antiquities of Turin and the Municipality, it exhibits in chronological order finds of the area: from the culture of Golasecca of the Iron Age (cinerary urns, bronze fibulas, funeral kits; the first showcase shows the evolution of the urn from biconic to ovoid) to the first Roman finds. It is located on the first floor of Villa Soranzo, with entrance from the municipal seat. Not indicated hours: information to the Municipality, tel. 0321 95355.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Pinacoteca comunale «Cesare Belossi»

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

Exhibition halls at the end of the eighties in the former barn of Villa Soranzo; since 1993, with the artist Ausilio Cattaneo, they host an intense activity of exhibitions, with artists of international and local importance. It was named on 22 February 2009 at the Councillor Cesare Belossi and resumed its activity in 2019 with a new management committee. It does not have a permanent collection: we visit it on the occasion of temporary exhibitions.

  • Where Piazza Mazzini 1 (Villa Soranzo), Varallo Pombia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.varallopombia.no.it

Sources: comune.varallopombia.no.it

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Vincenzo e Anastasio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Probably born at the end of the 11th century as a Romanesque church with a single nave, of which traces remain as the bell tower, older than the church; enlarged and reduced in Baroque forms, it was reconsecrated on July 30, 1758. At the altar of St. Anne one of the first works of Carlo Francesco Nuvolone (the canvas of 1645) with the Madonna and Child between St. Anne, St. Joseph and St. Vincent; on the left entering a fresco of St. Defender of the Gagnolesque school. Open 8-12 and 15-19.

  • Where Via Parrocchia, Varallo Pombia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.varallopombia.no.it

Sources: comune.varallopombia.no.it

Santuario della Madonna del Rosario

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Also known as "Madonna della Belta" (Madonna of Belt), it is a seventeenth-century reconstruction of the previous Romanesque church, close to the parish, which has been a popular destination for centuries. We venerate an image considered miraculous of the Madonna and Child enthroned between four angels; on the sides of the main altar two great paintings of the twentieth century of an artist of the village, and on the altar of Santa Cristina a precious altarpiece with the martyrdom of the saint. Open during Sunday Mass at 18.15.

  • Where Via Madonna, Varallo Pombia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismonovara.it

Sources: turismonovara.it

Casa Priuli Caccia e oratorio di San Giuseppe

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

In the center of the village, it was born from the transformation of a medieval castle, of which the external walls of a tower remain. Inside the oratory of San Giuseppe, founded by the abbot Antonio Caccia in 1726, and a park rich in centuries-old trees.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
