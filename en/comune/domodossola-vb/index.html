What to see in Domodossola

The capital of the Ossola, leads to the Sempione: the Renaissance Market Square, the medieval village and the Sacro Monte Calvario UNESCO World Heritage Site.

6 places to visit in Domodossola, in the province of Verbano-Cusio-Ossola (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Sacro Monte Calvario

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill of Mattarella, reached on foot along the Via Crucis, built from 1656: fifteen chapels with terracotta statues in life size and the sanctuary; here in 1828 Antonio Rosmini founded the Institute of Charity. UNESCO World Heritage Site, in a nature reserve.

  • Where Borgata Sacro Monte Calvario, Domodossola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it · illagomaggiore.com

Piazza Mercato

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Renaissance living room with balcony houses and loggias, wrought iron railings and 15th-century granite porticos, the Galletti Theatre and the big Saturday market that dates back to 917.

  • Where Piazza Mercato, Domodossola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it

Via Briona e torri medievali

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The most characteristic street, with the tower of the ancient Bishop's Palace of the fourteenth century; of the fourteenth-century walls remain the tower of Via Monte Grappa, a stretch in Via Facchinitti and the corner tower of Via Canuto.

  • Where Via Briona, Domodossola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it

Palazzo San Francesco – Musei Galletti

Museums · City history

Built on the remains of a church of the thirteenth century, today the museum of the Association Musei d'Ossola.

  • Where Piazza Convenzione, Domodossola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it

Palazzo Silva

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries, a rare example of aristocratic residence of the late Renaissance.

  • Where Piazza Chiossi, Domodossola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it

Collegiata dei Santi Gervasio e Protasio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built between 1792 and 1797, with a Romanesque portal in serpentine, frescoes by Lorenzo Peretti and in the chapel of San Carlo a painting by Tanzio da Varallo (1615).

  • Where Domodossola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it
