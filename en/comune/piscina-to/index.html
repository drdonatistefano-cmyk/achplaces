What to see in Piscina

Country of the Pinerolian plain with a museum of contemporary art outdoors, Open Art Pool, born in 1991 with about seventy works in the streets, the 18th-century parish church of San Grato di Gerolamo Buniva and the municipal wing of 1699.

4 places to visit in Piscina, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piscina Arte Aperta

Museums · Modern art

Museo d'arte contemporanea a venta tra piazzas e vie del paese, started in 1991 by the Piscinese painter Antonio Panino to represent the currents of Piedmontese art at the end of the millennium: about seventy works by artists of different generations and schools, along a city itinerary.

  • Where Piazza Buniva 5, Piscina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org · visitpiemonte.com · comune.piscina.to.it

Parrocchiale di San Grato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Since 1766 Gerolamo Buniva transformed and enlarged the small church already existing before 1609: three aisles separated by binated columns with Corinthian capitals, a dome similar to those of Guarini in San Lorenzo in Turin and Vittone al Vallinotto, a brick facade with sinuous profiles.

  • Where Piazza Buniva 4, Piscina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.piscina.to.it · turismotorino.org

Ala comunale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Roof built in August 1699, as a stone recalls, at the expense of the parishioners of San Grato and the territories of Tavernette, Scalenghe, Airasca and Marsaglia, on a land purchased by the Municipality in 1695, for the rest and shelter of residents and foreigners. Always accessible.

  • Where Via Umberto I angolo via Roma, Piscina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.piscina.to.it · turismotorino.org

Museo etnografico 'L Rubat

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Museum of peasant civilization of the Pinerolian plain created by the association Gruppo Ricerca Piscina (in 1979 according to Turismo Torino, in 1981 according to the Municipality): tools, objects of daily use up to the mid-Twentieth century, agricultural machinery, tools for bachiculture, photos and documents; the name comes from the rubat, stone roller and wood for threshing. Opening on request.

the museum is not accessible: building in general renovation (the site of the Municipality)

  • Where Via Umberto I 64, Piscina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.piscina.to.it · turismotorino.org
