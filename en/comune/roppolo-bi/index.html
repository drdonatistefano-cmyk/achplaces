What to see in Roppolo

Borgo della Serra morenica overlooking the lake of Viverone, dominated by the castle built around a tower of the tenth century and rebuilt by the Glasses; around the church of San Michele with the ancon of Gaspare Serra, the country shrine of San Vitale and, among forests and erratic boulders, the lake of Bertignano with its prehistoric pirogues.

Castello di Roppolo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Roppolo appeared in 963 in a diploma of Ottone I as a court with a castle-tower in the center; the Glasses rebuilt it in the thirteenth century adding the porch to two arches, and the coffered ceilings are fifteenth century. Passed to the Savoys in 1427, it was of the Valperga until 1837, when Ignazio Anselmi transformed it into a country residence; from 1981 to 2015 it hosted the Regional Enoteca della Serra. From the courtyard and the terrace you can enjoy the panorama of Lake Viverone and the Canaves lakes.

Chiesa di San Vitale

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

A single nave church born from an oratory, with a simple façade and a frescoed presbytery with altar of the late eighteenth century; there is preserved the relic of Saint Vitale in a 17th-century metallic bust (the skull, stolen in 1747, was found on 26 December). Every first Sunday of May, the pilgrimage from San Germano Vercellese goes up there for a vow of 1613. Around area equipped with tables, fountain, garden of aromatic plants, tactile path and parking.

  • Entry To be checked

Ruderi di Sant'Elisabetta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church founded in 1599 and completed in 1638 by the foreman Giovanni Battista Pianca, restored last time in 1931 and then abandoned: there are three perimeter walls. Tactile panel and picnic area; it is the crossroads of the paths to Lake Bertignano, Mount Orsetto and the Roc d'la Regina.

Roc d'la Regina

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Large erratic rock near Cascina Tavolara (about 7.2 x 3.25 m, 1.25 m tall) with a rectangular cavity similar to a tomb on top and, along the edges, seven round cups and two rectangular ones. Tradition ties him to a barbaric queen, without any evidence; rainwater collected in the cups was used by women as eye drops.

  • Where Presso Cascina Tavolara, Roppolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.roppolo.bi.it · italia.it

Monte Orsetto

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Wooded hill of 452 m between Lake Bertignano and Peverano, known for its prehistoric finds: the ruins of a stone fence, the Castelliere, suggest a use in the Iron or Celtic Age, perhaps later adapted by the Longobards as a viewing place. At the top an erratic rock with a smaller stone at the base, to which some historians attribute a ritual use. You will pass the Grand Crossing of Biellese.

  • Where Tra Lago di Bertignano e Peverano, Roppolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.roppolo.bi.it · italia.it

Lago di Bertignano

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

An intermorenic lake formed by the withdrawal of the ancient Balteo glacier, Special Conservation Zone with flat and accessible shore; it is reached on foot from the castle, also by bike on dirt road. There were found two pirogues, one of about 250 A.D. (1912) and one of about 1450 B.C. (discovered in 1978), and the remains of a palafittic village of the final Bronze; some finds are at the Museo del Territorio Biellese and the Museo di Antiquità in Turin.

  • Where Bertignano, Roppolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.roppolo.bi.it
