What to see in Calasetta

Marinar village at the northern end of the island of Sant'Antioco, founded by Ligurian families arrived from Tabarka in 1769, with white or pastel-coloured houses: the Sabauda tower with the archaeological museum, the MACC of contemporary art in the former slaughterhouse, the Spiaggia Grande and the cliffs of Mangiabarche.

MACC - Museo d'arte contemporanea di Calasetta

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

Founded in 1966 by Ermanno Leinardi with Italo Utzeri, Ugo Ugo and Tonino Casula, it is located in the former renovated municipal slaughterhouse: two floors lit by a skylight, with a building art room (Sonia Delaunay, Aurelie Nemours, Michel Seuphor and others) and one of abstract art (Capogrossi, Dorazio, Fontana, Bice Lazzari, Veronesi and others), works collected by Leinardi with exchanges between artists.

  • Where Via Savoia 2, Calasetta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Torre di Calasetta

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The sabauda tower, with a cone trunk, in blocks of volcanic stone, about 11 meters high, built before the foundation of the village guarding the canal between Sant'Antioco and San Pietro; the lower level was a cistern, the entrance is about 4 meters high and a staircase in the thickness of the wall rises to the stands, from which the look ranges for about 20 km. The ground floor houses an archaeological museum of Phoenician-Punic finds, the room on the first floor temporary exhibitions.

  • Where Centro storico, Calasetta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Spiaggia Grande

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

More than a kilometer of white sand and very fine, among the most extensive of the Sulcis archipelago, with dunes of junipers, shallow bottom that slopes gently and sheltered bay between tuffy rocks and granite; bar, large parking, rentals, boat excursions and surf school. You can reach in a few minutes from the village towards Punta Maggiore, passing in front of the beach of La Salina, with dunes and a pond that is avifaunistic oasis.

  • Where Strada per Punta Maggiore, Calasetta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Mangiabarche

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the west side, cliffs overlooking the sea guarded by a lighthouse on the cliff opposite; on the same coast open Cala Lunga, emerald green creek with a sheltered beach, and Cala Tuffi, a natural swimming pool between rocky walls accessible only by boat, walking or mountain bike.

  • Where Costa occidentale, Calasetta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
