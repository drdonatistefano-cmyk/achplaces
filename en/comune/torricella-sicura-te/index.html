What to see in Torricella Sicura

Municipality to the north-west of Teramo, gateway to the Laga Mountains, probably born between the IX and the X century as a fortified village; it preserves medieval churches in the hamlets, the ethnographic museum "Le genti della Laga" and the abandoned village of Valle Piola.

Museo e presepe etnografico Le genti della Laga

Museums · Themed

Set up by Gino Di Benedetto in more than thirty years of research, it preserves about sixteen thousand ancient objects in three sections: 1:5 scale miniatures of scenes and objects of traditional life, made by old craftsmen of Torricella; vintage objects and ancient environments of real size; a virtual section on the website.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Chiesa di San Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church already mentioned in a papal document of 1153, preserves works of fine art; the parish of St. Paul the Apostle organizes the Infiorata del Corpus Domini, with carpets of flowers exhibited during the procession.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.gransassolagapark.it

Valle Piola

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Abandoned village about 1,000 meters at the foot of Mount della Farina, perhaps the most evocative of the Laga: a dozen buildings mostly of the late nineteenth century, uninhabited since 1977, and the church of San Nicola. You can reach it with a short hike, on horseback or by mountain bike from Torricella and you can continue up to Magliano da Capo, with remains of walls of an Italian fortified settlement.

  • Where Valle Piola, Torricella Sicura
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.gransassolagapark.it

Sources: gransassolagapark.it
