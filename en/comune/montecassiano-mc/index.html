What to see in Montecassiano

A village walled on a hill between the valleys of Potenza and Musone, with concentric streets around Piazza Leopardi: the fifteenth-century Palazzo dei Priori with the five-arch loggia, the collegiate church of Santa Maria Assunta with the glazed terracotta altar of Mattia della Robbia, the thirteenth-century oratory of San Nicolò and the civic collections.

8 places to visit in Montecassiano, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza Leopardi e Palazzo dei Priori

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The old town has concentric streets around the main square, where the Palazzo dei Priori, rebuilt by Antonio Lombardo in 1467, with five-arch loggia and the large arch towards Palazzo Compagnucci, overlooks; in the Council Hall a Madonna in throne between saints and musical angels by Johannes Hispanus (1506).

  • Where Piazza Leopardi, Montecassiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Collegiata di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The 12th century, rebuilt in 1234 by the monks of the Abbey of Fiastra and modified by Antonio Lombardo, has terracotta façade with rosette. Inside the glazed terracotta altar of Mattia della Robbia, the Coronation of Maria di Giacomo da Recanati, a Callido organ of 1775 and the astile cross of Lorenzo d'Ascoli (1414).

  • Where Centro storico, Montecassiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Oratorio di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

At the entrance to the village, it dates back to the 13th century: it has a bell dating back to 1382 and fifteenth-century frescoes from the Umbrian-Marche school.

  • Where Ingresso del borgo, Montecassiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Pinacoteca civica «G. Buratto» e Raccolta archeologica

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated in 2011, it collects sacred and profane painting of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries from churches and suppressed convents of the village, including a San Nicola da Tolentino that intercedes for the souls of Purgatory; the archaeological collection of piceno finds of municipal property is in the nearby church of San Marco, renovated in the eighteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Unità d'Italia, Montecassiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Museo di arte sacra nella chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Museums · Themed

In the sixteenth-century church with an octagonal Baroque plan, reachable from the steps of Via Roma after Porta San Giovanni: paintings of the ancient altars and oratory, thrones, processional machines, candelabra and restored crucifixes.

  • Where Via Roma, Montecassiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Giacomo e cortili storici

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

17th century, near Porta Diaz and the headquarters of the Confraternities, with a fresco of the Madonna enthroned with the Child attributed to Vincenzo Pagani; in the village you can also visit the cloister of the Augustinians, the garden of St. James and the courtyard of the Clarisse, of thirteenth century.

  • Where Porta Diaz, Montecassiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Mura e Porta di Santa Croce

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The walls, built since 1437, still surround the historic center; you go up to the square from the crenellated door of Santa Croce, in Via Cesare Battisti.

  • Where Via Cesare Battisti, Montecassiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Parco del Cerreto

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

Near the walls, it offers a beautiful view of the surrounding hilly landscape.

  • Entry Free
