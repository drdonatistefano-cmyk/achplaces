What to see in Piverone

Ancient thirteenth-century village of Canavese, on the Canavesan route of the Via Francigena, with the gate of the shelter (1210), the towers of the walls and, among the vineyards, the Romanesque ruins of the Gesiùn; in the village the ethnographic museum "La Steiva" on the peasant civilization.

Centro storico e ricetto

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

A very characteristic thirteenth-century village, with the gate of the ancient ricepto dated 1210 and the remains of the walls: in Via Flecchia, the ancient main street, the surviving tower of curtain is an apse to the chapel of the Confraternita dei Disciplinati, near to parts of the crenellated walls; the moat was fed by the sources of the Serra. The tower of San Giacomo, circular with swallow-tailed blackbirds, is in a private garden.

  • Where Centro storico, Piverone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismotorino.org

Torre d'angolo di nord est

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Angular tower of the medieval wall curtain, to the right of the turret gate: it was used as a prison and then incorporated as a staircase in the old school building; today it is isolated next to the school building.

  • Where Piazza Lucca, Piverone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · italia.it

Torre-Porta Campanaria

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Massiccia porta turrita today used as a bell tower and clock tower: originally open towards the inside and without cover, with a bertesca, round arch, wounds for the boltoni of two drawbridges (carraio and pedestrian), hanging arches and ghibelline blackbirds with swallow tail. The twin tower-door, the ... torrion, was demolished in 1750 and gives the name to the street; visible from the road.

  • Where Via del Torrione, Piverone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.piverone.to.it · turismotorino.org · italia.it

Torre di San Pietro

History and architecture · Towers

In the region of San Pietro, perhaps rest of a Romanesque bell tower; at the base are faded frescoes with the coat of arms of the De Contesa next to San Sebastiano, dated 1448 and attributed by the scholar Aldo Moretto to Domenico della Marca d'Ancona.

  • Where Regione San Pietro, Piverone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · italia.it

Chiesa del Gesiùn (San Pietro di Sugliaco)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ruins of a small Romanesque church, dated between the end of the 10th and the half of the 11th century, rectory of the lost village of Livion cited in 1209: single nave of 4.62 x 3.80 m, three arches on two columns towards the presbytery, a bell tower almost to tibury over the presbytery and a fragment of fresco with a hand holding a book, perhaps a St. Peter. The name means "chiesona" (church), it is located isolated at the intersection of two country roads between the vineyards, along the Via Francigena.

  • Where Strada Aronco 3, località Torrone, Piverone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · italia.it

Museo Etnografico «La Steiva»

Museums · Themed · Free entry

The name comes from the hold, the handle of the plow. It collects photographs from the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, costumes and military uniforms, rebuilt environments (farm kitchen, room, local for hemp weaving, carpenter's workshop, cellar, classroom) and agricultural tools, telling viticulture, beekeeping, baching and weaving of the hemp of the area.

  • Where Via Torrione, Piverone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.piverone.to.it · turismotorino.org · italia.it
