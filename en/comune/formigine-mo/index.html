What to see in Formigine

Municipality of the Modena plain gathered around the castle, fortified by Modena in 1201 and reshaped by the Pio di Carpi since 1405, today museum with archaeological park; in the territory, rich in more than 80 villas and residences, Villa Gandini with the Resistance Park and the parish church of San Giacomo di Colombaro.

Castello di Formigine

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortified by the Municipality of Modena in 1201 after a defeat against Reggio, from 1405 he passed to the Pio di Carpi who gave him the current layout with the rock, the brand building and the clock tower; in 1599 he returned to the Estensi, who in 1648 infeuded him to the Calcagnini. Seriously damaged by the bombings of the Second World War, it was a municipal seat and then restored and opened to the public: the Marchan Palace houses the reception and wall drawings of David Tremlett, and the archaeological park of over 4,000 m2 preserves the remains of the parish church of San Bartolomeo with bell tower and burials.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Museo e Centro di documentazione del Castello

Museums · City history

Opened in September 2007 in the rock, the fortified core first closed to the public, tells the story from the 10th century to today with multimedia installations by Studio Azzurro. The route touches the corticella, the clock tower with bell and sixteenth-century clock, the south-east tower with the comparison between the current and late-middle-valeval landscape, the guard body and the excavations of San Bartolomeo: ceramics from the late Middle Ages to the nineteenth century and funerary furnishings with rosaries, buttons, buckles and rings.

  • Where Piazza Calcagnini 1, Formigine
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it

Villa Gandini e Parco della Resistenza

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

Neoclassical villa (former Villa Aggazzotti) of the Gandini since 1791, designed by Francesco Vandelli, with paintings and decorations of the time; it is the seat of the Municipal Library and is visited during the opening days of the library. The park is dedicated to the Resistance and on the south side has two majestic ginkgo biloba, spectacular between October and November.

  • Where Formigine
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it

Pieve di San Giacomo di Colombaro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was linked to the Matilda parish church of San Giacomo, which was mentioned in 1127. It also had a pilgrim's hospice mentioned in 1162, probably a stage of medieval pilgrims. Church with a nave with an eighteenth-century bell tower; the external sandstone wall and a small bifora remain, while the façade was rebuilt in 1963.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
