What to see in Gandino

Borgo della Val Gandino, in Valle Seriana, with the Basilica of Santa Maria Assunta, one of the most famous Baroque monuments of Bergamasca, the museum that preserves its treasures together with nativity scenes and fabrics, the Palazzo del Vicar portico and, at altitude, the paths of Monte Farno.

Museo della Basilica di Gandino

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 1929 by the prevosto Giovanni Bonzi at the invitation of Angelo Roncalli, future John XXIII, in a Gandinese Renaissance residence connected by a overpass to the churchyard of the basilica. The section of sacred art exhibits vestments, goldsmiths, Flemish tapestries by Frans Guebels and Mattens, illuminated codes, wooden statues, paintings by Sebastiano Ricci, Antonio Balestra and Antonio Cifrondi and twenty-three macabre panels with skeletons used in the Triduo of the Dead. The museum is completed by the section of the nativity scenes, coming from dozens of countries and dedicated to St. John Paul II, and the textile one, with machines such as the gauze machine for the Pannilana of the valley.

  • Where Piazza Emancipazione, Gandino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · in-lombardia.it

Basilica di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Basilicas

It is indicated by the regional portal as one of the most famous Baroque monuments of Bergamasca, overlooking Piazza Emancipazione; a overpass connects the churchyard to the first floor of the adjacent museum, which preserves its treasures.

  • Where Piazza Emancipazione, Gandino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · lombardiabeniculturali.it

Borgo storico e Palazzo del Vicario

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The route in the historic village starts from the Palazzo del Vicario, with a portico typical of Lombard brolets, and continues to the basilica.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Veneto, Gandino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Monte Farno

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Mountain area above Gandino with easy paths and longer routes to Montagnina, Pizzo Formico and Paralightmine, where the refuge offers 360° views; the regional portal signals it for trekking and paragliding. Infopoint open Saturday and Sunday 9-12.30 and 14-17.30; parking fee (Gratta and Sosta, €3).

  • Where Località Monte Farno, Gandino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.gandino.bg.it · in-lombardia.it
