What to see in Pescolanciano

Ancient Norman village, 'porta dell'Alto Molise', grown along the tratturo Castel di Sangro-Lucera around the Castello d'Alessandro, transformed in the eighteenth century into a noble residence; in the surroundings the tower of Santa Maria dei Vignali between Sannitic fortifications and, beyond the Trigno, the white fir woods of Collemeluccio.

Castello d'Alessandro

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On a limestone spur on the high valley of the Trigno, it probably stands on ancient Sannitique positions; the village is closed by a belt of about 750 meters with houses incorporated. Originally it was made up of distinct fortified bodies, a small church, a cylindrical tower, a fortress and a wall; in the seventeenth century the d'Alessandro added drawbridge, courtyard and warehouses and transformed it into a noble residence. There they had a famous horse farm (in 1711 Giuseppe d'Alessandro published the treatise "Pietra di Paragone dei Cavalieri") and, between 1780 and 1795, a factory of majolica and competing porcelain of Capodimonte, remembered by the Museum of Ceramics in the former tavern of the manor. On the first floor the chapel with marbles, stuccoes, a canvas of a student of Solimena and the relics of St. Alexander brought in 1787; hosted Theodor Mommsen during the visit to the excavations of Pietrabbondante.

Torre e area archeologica di Santa Maria dei Vignali

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Outside the village, on a hill reachable from the tracturo, the tower of Santa Maria dei Vignali of the XII-XIII century, surrounded by more ancient Sannitic fortifications and the remains of a medieval village.

  • Where Località Santa Maria dei Vignali, Pescolanciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu

Tratturo Castel di Sangro-Lucera

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historic centre was born around this ancient grassy transhumance road, which crosses the village and runs on foot or on horseback through hills, cultivated fields and woods, up to the area of Santa Maria dei Vignali.

  • Where Centro storico, Pescolanciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitmolise.eu · italia.it
