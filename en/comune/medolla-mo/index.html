What to see in Medolla

Centro della Bassa Modenese documented since about 776 when Carlo Magno donated to the Abbey of Nonantola the church of Camurana, and for centuries a place of commerce on the border between the Duchy of Este, Lombardo-Veneto and the Papal State: today it offers the faunistic oasis of Via Rubadello, the Chapel of the Duke in Villafranca and seventeen ancient country villas; the 2012 earthquake has made unworkable many historical buildings.

Oasi San Matteo e Oasi faunistica Le Cicogne

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

In via Rubadello, a few kilometers from the centre, an area of ecological rebalancing that recreates the typical environments of the Po Valley, with nature trails and observation points; the oasis Le Cicogne has stork aviary, house and garden of butterflies, area for ungulates, lakes and a classroom. Guided tours and for schools by reservation (Environment Office of the Municipality or Le Cicogne Association).

  • Where Via Rubadello, Medolla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Cappelletta del Duca

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Small oratory with circular plan with hemispheric dome, completed around 1860, erected by Duke Francesco V of Austria-Este to thank for having escaped the attack on 16 November 1848 of the Mazzini Luigi Rizzati of Cavezzo. It rises in a roundabout of the State Road 12 of Canaletto.

  • Where SS 12 del Canaletto, Villafranca, Medolla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Chiesa di Camurana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Probably one of the oldest churches in the Lower Modena: a church in Camurana is mentioned in Carlo Magno's donation to the Abbey of Nonantola of about 776.

Seriously damaged by the 2012 earthquake, currently closed

  • Where Camurana, Medolla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
