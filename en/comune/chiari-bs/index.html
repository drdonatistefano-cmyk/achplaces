What to see in Chiari

City of the Brescia plain between Brescia and Bergamo, born from a village of Lombardy circular shape and surrounded by walls up to the Venetian age: the Cathedral of Saints Faustino and Giovita with the civic tower of 55 meters, Santa Maria Maggiore, the Morcelli Repossi Foundation, the City Museum, the market documented since 1406 and the liberty Villa Mazzotti with its park.

Duomo dei Santi Faustino e Giovita

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Cited in a papal bull of 1148, it was rebuilt from 1432 (date engraved on a brick) and consecrated in 1500; the facade and the current layout of the three aisles entirely frescoed dates back to 1846. Inside the "Trionfi" of Saints Faustino and Jovita of the Flemish (1621-1633), two altarpieces of Pompeo Batoni (1750 and 1763) and the mausoleum of Stefano Antonio Morcelli of Gaetano Matteo Monti (1828); the crypt of Sant'Agape is visited on request at the parish office.

  • Where Piazza Zanardelli, Chiari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.chiari.brescia.it

Torre civica

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Designed by Abbot Antonio Marchetti and started in 1757, it is 55 meters high, with rustic base and bell cell with eleven bells; the four dials of the clock have the colors of the four squares of the city. The expected crowning was never realized. Visits only by appointment with the City's Culture Office; no elevator.

  • Where Piazza Zanardelli, Chiari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.chiari.brescia.it

Sources: comune.chiari.brescia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was mentioned already in 1289, and was rebuilt and enlarged in 1667-1670 by the School of the Holy Rosary; façade completed in 1816 by Antonio Marchetti, with the stone statue of the Madonna of about four tons placed in 1873. Inside the fifteen Mysteries of the Flemish Rosary, the main altar in inlaid marble by Angelo Ogna (1707), the frescoes of the choir of Agostino Avanzati and Alessandro Sampilli and a fresco in the left nave.

  • Where Via De Gasperi, Chiari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.chiari.brescia.it

Sources: comune.chiari.brescia.it · in-lombardia.it

Chiesa di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the fifteenth century by the Quadra di Villatico on Venetian models, renovated in the eighteenth century: double façade with stone elements of Sarnico, interior frescoed with two naves, seventeenth-century altarpieces by Andrea Asper and a canvas from 1645 with the view of Chiari Murata. It is one of the five suburban churches related to the Quadre, protagonists in September of the Palio delle Quadre (since 1978).

  • Where Via San Rocco, Chiari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.chiari.brescia.it

Sources: comune.chiari.brescia.it

Fondazione Biblioteca Morcelli Pinacoteca Repossi

Museums · Old masters · Free entry

In the palaces donated by Mgr. Pietro Faglia in 1776: the Morcellian Library (from the Morcelli legacy of 1817) with about 70,000 volumes, 58 incunabula, manuscripts and parchments, and the Repossi Art Gallery (legato of 1854) with paintings, sculptures, furniture and art objects from the 15th to the 21st century, for thematic itineraries; then gipsoteca, oriental icons and over 800 nativity scenes, many of the 18th century Neapolitan.

  • Where Via Bernardino Varisco 9, Chiari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.chiari.brescia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitbrescia.it · comune.chiari.brescia.it

Museo della Città

Museums · City history

In the historic buildings of Piazza Zanardelli, entrance under the arcades towards Piazza delle Erbe: the former prisons (used from the Venetian age to 1974) for the exhibitions, and in the underground the archaeological and multimedia path "Chiari from Village to City" with the excavations of 2007-2009, including those of a house in wood fired from the tenth century. The archaeological route opens on the 2nd and 4th Saturday of the month, 10-12.

  • Where Piazza Zanardelli, Chiari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Piazza delle Erbe e mercato storico

Local life and traditions · Historic markets · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lounge of the city is with a neo-Renaissance stone fountain by Rezzato inaugurated in 1924. The market has been held since 1406, when Carlo Malatesta granted it for Thursday; Venice added Tuesday and Saturday. Today it takes place Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday morning between Via Garibaldi, Piazza Zanardelli, Via Villatico and Piazza Martiri della Libertà, and on Friday there is the fish market with local producers.

  • Where Via XXVI Aprile 5, Chiari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.chiari.brescia.it

Sources: comune.chiari.brescia.it

Città murata e Rocca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

A Visconti castrum novum is documented at the end of the fourteenth century, renovated by Pandolfo III Malatesta and with a revelt of the sixteenth century; in 1701 Eugenio of Savoia used it at the Battle of Chiari. Of the five-door walls there is nothing left on the surface, but the quadrangular plan and the corner towers of the fortress are read in the building that dominates Martiri della Libertà square, and the ring road follows the moat.

  • Where Piazza Martiri della Libertà, Chiari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.chiari.brescia.it

Sources: comune.chiari.brescia.it

Villa Mazzotti Biancinelli e parco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

Villa liberty built in 1911-1916 for Count Lodovico Mazzotti Biancinelli on a project by Antonio Vandono di Cortemiglia and Alessandro Citterio, with peristyle, skylight and original furnishings; his son Franco was among the creators of the Mille Miglia. The park of about ten hectares, redesigned in 1927, has fountains, outdoor theatre, gazebo and statues; it is open free (9-17 in winter, 9-20 in summer), the villa only for events, on request or with guided tours.

  • Where Viale Mazzini 39, Chiari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.chiari.brescia.it

Sources: comune.chiari.brescia.it · in-lombardia.it
