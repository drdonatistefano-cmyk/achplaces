What to see in Amalfi

Ancient maritime republic and center of the Amalfi Coast, UNESCO heritage since 1997: the Cathedral of Sant'Andrea at the top of the stairs, the Cloister of Paradise and, behind the village, the Valley of Ferriere with the ancient paper mills.

3 places to visit in Amalfi, in the province of Salerno (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di Sant'Andrea e Chiostro del Paradiso

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Romanesque church built on the oldest Basilica of the Crucifix of the IX century, with the façade of the late nineteenth century with mosaics of Byzantine taste and the crypt with the relics of Saint Andrew; the Cloister of Paradise, born as a cemetery of the nobles, preserves five sarcophagi decorated around a garden of palm trees. The statue of the saint runs down the stairway on 27 June and 30 November.

  • Where Piazza Duomo 1, Amalfi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo della Carta

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In a 13th-century paper mill, the wooden sweaters moved by the hydraulic wheel, the Dutch machine of 1745 and the presses of the eighteenth-century still work with the water of the Canneto stream; you can make by hand a sheet of Amalfi paper.

  • Where Via delle Cartiere 23, Amalfi
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Valle delle Ferriere

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valley rich in waterfalls behind Amalfi, where there were paper mills and a ironworks moved by water; there grows the endemic fern Woodwardia rootans and the path descends along the paper mills disused to the center.

  • Where Amalfi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it
