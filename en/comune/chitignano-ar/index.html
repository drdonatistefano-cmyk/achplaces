What to see in Chitignano

The smallest village of Casentino, mentioned already in 967 and lordship of the Ubertini until the eighteenth century, known from the Middle Ages for the sulphurous and ferruginous waters and for the smuggling of tobacco and gunpowder; to see the Castle of the Ubertini, the Ecomuseum of gunpowder and smuggling and the source Buca del Tesoro.

Castello dei Conti Ubertini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Among the oldest fortifications in Casentino, it dates back to the 10th century: it belonged to the counts of Chiusi and from 1261 to Ubertini, who kept it almost uninterruptedly until 1830; there was lord Bishop Guglielmino Ubertini, who died in Campaldino in 1289. A driveway between the cypresses leads to the arms square with the bell tower and a bell tower of the fifteenth century; inside the court with frescoes, guard body with the arms of the Ubertini, Room of Arms with coffered ceiling and fireplace in serene stone, baroque chapel, bishop's room and, on the top floor, the room of the judgments with a small prison.

Private property, not always open to the public: for visits contact the Municipality or ADSI

Ecomuseo del contrabbando e della polvere da sparo

Museums · Themed

It is part of the Casentino's ecomuseums and tells of the production and trade of gunpowder, which marked Chitignano in the nineteenth century, both in the authorized powder kegs and in the "pilli" hidden in the woods, together with the tobacco smuggling favored by the privileged tax regime granted by the Grand Duchy.

  • Where Piazza Generale Angelo Arrigucci 1, Chitignano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Sentiero della Polveriera dell'Inferno

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Itinerary in the woods along the Rio stream leading to one of the major ancient sites of production of gunpowder, obtained by mixing sulphur and salter; along the path the "pillo" is a hole in the sandstone used as a mortar, and the "mazzapicchio" is a wooden instrument moved by the force of water.

  • Where Torrente Rio, Chitignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: discoverarezzo.com · visittuscany.com

Sorgente Buca del Tesoro

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Ferruginous and sulphurous waters already known in ancient times, as shown by the votive bronze found in the surroundings, frequented by the pilgrims of the Via Romea in the Middle Ages and valued by the Ubertini in the seventeenth century. The municipality has built the Park Buca del Tesoro, a green area along the river with a fountain with three taps to draw water; near the village also the springs of Rio and Galastri.

  • Where Via Rosina, Chitignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Podesteria

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Building built by the Florentines at the beginning of the fifteenth century, after the submission of Arezzo to Florence; it preserves a fresco of the Annunciation of 1610.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: discoverarezzo.com · visittuscany.com

Chiesa dei Santi Vincenzo e Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the XVII century consecrated in 1614 by Pier Francesco Ubertini; the bell tower with the clock is of the mid-nineteenth century.
