What to see in Castelvecchio Subequo

Small village on a spur of Mount Putano, in the Sirente-Velino Regional Park, heir to the ancient Superaequum, one of the three municipalities of the Peligni: it preserves the early Christian catacomb, the church of Sant'Agata, the Palazzo castellano dei Conti di Celano and the complex of San Francesco with the Museum of Sacred Art.

Chiesa e Convento di San Francesco

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

The complex was founded in 1288 and built outside the walls by the Counts of Celano; in 1294 it was hosted by Pietro del Morrone, future Celestine V, on his way to L'Aquila for the coronation. The church preserves cycles of frescoes on the life of the saint (XIV-XV century) and baroque altars in wood, stone and stucco of the mid-Sixteenth century; the convent houses the Museum of Sacred Art, with reliquaries, an altar cross of 1403 and the "Pasquarella" , Virgin with the Child and two silver angels donated by the Counts of Celano, and next to the Archaeological Museum with the finds of Superaequum. The MiC tab indicates opening every day 9-17.

Catacomba di Superaequum

History and architecture · Archaeology

Paleochristian catacomb discovered in 1943, among the rare of Abruzzo: a hypogeum of two galleries dug in the limestone rock, with graffiti linked to Christian worship; the funerary kit given its use between the IV and the beginning of the VII century. It testifies to the first spread of Christianity in the region.

Chiesa di Sant'Agata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Rural church dating back to 1114 (XI-XIII century according to the Park), in front of the medieval fountain, probably built on the remains of a temple of Hercules in the area of the ancient Superaequum. On February 4 and 5, the 'pagnotta di Sant'Agata' is blessed, bread in the shape of a wet bosom in the water of the Fonte di Sant'Agata and linked to the protection of puerpers.

Fontana medievale e acquedotti romani

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

At the foot of the village, the medieval fountain was fed by an abundant spring and, with two Roman aqueducts of which visible traces remain, distributed water in the area; its structure suggests a spa complex of Roman age.

Palazzo castellano dei Conti di Celano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

It dominates the village and shows construction phases of different eras, with a tower, a body added over time and a loggia; the Park signals the castled palace with Gothic arched door and saracinesca. Castelvecchio was a fief of the Counts of Celano between the 13th and 15th centuries, then from 1484 of the Piccolomini, then of the Colonna, of the Pietropaoli and from 1789 of the Barberini.

Centro storico, case medievali e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The original nucleus, built thanks to a flourishing economy, preserves medieval houses with mullioned windows and porticos and numerous palaces of kind, including Palazzo Ginetti-Lucchini with seventeenth-century façade; there are also the church of San Rocco, with a 16th-century fresco, and that of Saints John the Evangelist and Baptist.

Resti di Superaequum

History and architecture · Archaeology

Ruins of the Roman town hall of Superaequum in Macrano and Colle Caprelle: the excavations have returned houses with mosaic floors, paved streets and sacred buildings, including one dedicated to Hercules Winner with bronzes and votive inscriptions. In the territory also the fortified italic center of Colle Cipolla and the necropolis of Le Castagne.
