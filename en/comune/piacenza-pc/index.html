What to see in Piacenza

Roman city on the Via Emilia and stop of the Via Francigena, called the city of the hundred churches: frescoed domes from Guercino and Pordenone, Palazzo Farnese and Piazza Cavalli with the equestrian monuments of Mochi.

25 places to visit in Piacenza, in the province of Piacenza (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 7 of the 13 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Musei Civici di Palazzo Farnese

Museums · City history · Paid entry

The Vignola Palace and the Visconti Citadel with the art gallery, the Tondo di Botticelli, the Etruscan Bronze Liver and the Museum of the Bodies.

  • Where Piazza Cittadella 29, Piacenza
  • Entry €10 all sections; €7 reduced; €4 from 6 to 25 years; €1 first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Piazza Cavalli e Palazzo Gotico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

From the thirteenth century the political center of the city, with the public Palace of 1281 in white marble and red terracotta and the baroque equestrian statues of the Farnese by Francesco Mochi.

  • Where Piazza Cavalli, Piacenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: comune.piacenza.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Duomo di Piacenza

Places of worship · Cathedrals

The Romanesque cathedral began in 1122, a stage of medieval pilgrims, with the 71-metre bell tower, the paratic panels and the dome frescoed by Guercino.

  • Where Piazza Duomo 33, Piacenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Basilica di Sant'Antonino

Places of worship · Basilicas

The basilica of the patron founded in the fourth century, cathedral until the ninth, with the Paradise Gate, the octagonal bell tower and the cloister; here the foreplay of the peace of Constance was prepared (1183).

  • Where Piazza Sant'Antonino, Piacenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
