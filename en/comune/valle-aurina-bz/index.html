What to see in Valle Aurina

Along the Alpine valley on the border with Austria, linked to the copper mine of Predoi, which is visited with the train, and to the granary of Cadipietra with the collection Enzenberg; at the end of the valley the church of Santo Spirito in Casere, stop of those who crossed the Tauri.

5 places to visit in Valle Aurina, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo provinciale delle miniere – miniera di Predoi

Museums · Themed

Ancient copper mine, one of the largest in Italy about six centuries ago: a small train leads into the mountain and the guided tour of the gallery of Sant'Ignazio, excavated by hand, shows tools, machinery and a copper vein; in the mountain there is also the climate center of Predoi.

  • Where Vicolo Hörmann 38a, Casere-Predoi, Valle Aurina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: italia.it · ahrntal.com · suedtirol.info

Museo delle miniere nel granaio di Cadipietra

Museums · Themed

In the historic barn next to the church, rebuilt in 2000, the Enzenberg collection: wooden models, paintings, mining maps, documents and finds of the mines of Predoi, with an exhibition on the life of miners and their families.

  • Where Cadipietra 99, Valle Aurina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it

Museo mineralogico Kirchler

Museums · Science and university

Museum of the Kirchler family with minerals of the Aurina Valley, the High Tauern and all over the world; open every day in July and August.

  • Where Mühlegg 101, San Giovanni, Valle Aurina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Maranatha – Museo dei presepi e dell'arte popolare

Museums · Themed

Museum of nativity scenes and folk art in Lutago, reported by the tourist association of the valley.

  • Where Weißenbachstraße 17, Lutago, Valle Aurina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: ahrntal.com · suedtirol.info

Chiesetta di Santo Spirito a Casere

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Consecrated in 1455 by Cardinal Nicolò Cusano and enlarged around 1500, he served travellers heading beyond the Tauri to Salzburg; his cemetery welcomed those who died in the pass and the miners of Predoi. Next to the large boulder "Schliefstein" .

  • Where Casere, Predoi, Valle Aurina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: ahrntal.com · suedtirol.info
