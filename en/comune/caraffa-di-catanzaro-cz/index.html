Istituto della Cultura Arbëreshe «Giuseppe Gangale»

Museums · Themed

Museum of Italian-Albanian culture dedicated to Giuseppe Gangale, glottologist and professor at the University of Copenhagen: exhibits tools of peasant and craftsman work, the permanent nativity scene "Nativity Arbereshe" with typical costumes and original documents of Gangale, including manuscripts and testimonies of the alphabet with which he transcribed the Albanian oral heritage. It has permanent and temporary exhibitions, photo library and teaching room.

not indicated times: the MiC card reports "closed" every day, the Municipality recommends to contact the offices (tel. 0961 953043)

Chiesa di Santa Domenica

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1936 in neo-Gothic style, it has three aisles with reinforced concrete Y-pillars, singing hallway, flat apse with large neo-Gothic window and marble floors. It houses a polychrome tabernacle in green marble of Gimigliano embedded in the high altar, the statue of the patroness attributed to the school of the Droses of Satriano, paintings and liturgical furnishings. Unvisible during functions.

  • Where Piazza Skanderbeg, Caraffa di Catanzaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.caraffadicatanzaro.cz.it

Sources: comune.caraffadicatanzaro.cz.it

Chiesa dei Maiorana (Madonna delle Grazie)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Modest eclectic country church, built by local workers and dedicated to the Madonna delle Grazie, with frescoes dated to the late Romanesque period. You can reach it by a rural path that comes off the road Façade Preti or from the locality Purraphele.

The church is located on private land, on rough terrain not accessible to disabled people and without services

  • Where Località Usito, Caraffa di Catanzaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.caraffadicatanzaro.cz.it
