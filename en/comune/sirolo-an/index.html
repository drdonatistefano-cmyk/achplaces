What to see in Sirolo

Medieval village overlooking the sea on the southern side of Mount Conero, born around an 11th century castle, with the square of the church of San Nicola and its viewpoint on the coast. The territory includes beaches such as the Two Sisters, the Picena necropolis of the Pini and the Badia of San Pietro on the top of the Conero.

8 places to visit in Sirolo, in the province of Ancona (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e belvedere di San Nicola

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historic centre was built around an 11th-century castle, of which traces remain in the bell tower and the walls. The alleys lead to the square of the church of San Nicola di Bari, patron, which dominates the main square from the thirteenth century and has a view on the coast; in the center there is also the church of the Holy Rosary. At the beginning of May the Palio di San Nicola is run.

  • Where Piazza della chiesa di San Nicola, Sirolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Spiaggia delle Due Sorelle

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

It takes its name from the two twin faraglioni that close one end of the beach. In summer you can reach by boat from the port of Numana; Legambiente counts it among the ten most beautiful beaches in Italy and there are released sea turtles. A little north is the beach of the works, pebbles and stones; the faraglioni are also seen from the top of the cliff along a path of 5.4 km of the Park.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Parco regionale del Monte Conero

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Regional park established at the end of the 1980s thanks to the committees of citizens and environmental associations. It offers about 20 hiking, horseback and mountain biking trails, including the crossing of the promontory (8 km), the rock engravings (700 m), the Roman caves and a hidden quarry (2.3 km), and the panoramic trail on the Two Sisters (5.4 km). The visitor centre of the park in Sirolo hosts the reconstruction of the Queen's Tomb.

  • Where Monte Conero, Sirolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Area archeologica I Pini e Tomba della Regina

History and architecture · Archaeology

One of the largest Picene necropolis, dating from the 6th century BC, the only area of the Marche with archaeological excavations according to italia.it. There is the Tomb of the Queen of Sirolo, which contained a cart, a carriage and ornamental objects; the finds are exposed to the State Antiquarium of Numana and a reconstruction is in the center visits of the Conero Park in Sirolo.

  • Where Località I Pini, Sirolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · eventi.turismo.marche.it
