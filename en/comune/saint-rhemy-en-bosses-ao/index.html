What to see in Saint-Rhémy-en-Bosses

Last municipality before Switzerland, at the foot of the Gran San Bernardo Hill along the Via Francigena, home of the Jambon de Bosses PDO.

5 places to visit in Saint-Rhémy-en-Bosses, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Saint-Rhémy-en-Bosses in the guide with map and filters

Colle del Gran San Bernardo

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valico at 2472 meters reachable by car in summer; the hospice still welcomes the pilgrims of the Via Francigena and in front of a museum tells the history of the hill from ancient cults to the hospitality of today.

  • Where Colle del Gran San Bernardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it

Castello dei Signori di Bosses

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The nucleus of the 11th century, entirely rebuilt in the 15th century, with a square and massive plan like the first Valdostan strong houses; it is visited at exhibitions and events.

  • Where Bosses, Saint-Rhémy-en-Bosses
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Chiese parrocchiali di San Lorenzo e San Leonardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parishes of Saint-Rhémy and Bosses, both frescoed in the nineteenth century by the Stornone brothers of Ivrea.

  • Where Saint-Rhémy-en-Bosses
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Via Francigena verso il Gran San Bernardo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

An ancient pilgrim's street that climbs to the hill, which can be walked.

  • Where Saint-Rhémy-en-Bosses
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it

Fonte di Citrin

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ferruginous water that descends from the 1800 meters of the Citrin valley and flows from the fountain in the town hall square.

  • Where Saint-Rhémy-en-Bosses
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it
