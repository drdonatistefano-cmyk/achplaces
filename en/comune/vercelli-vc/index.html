What to see in Vercelli

The capital of rice and the stop of the via Francigena, the only city in Piedmont: the basilica of Sant'Andrea, among the first Gothic churches in Italy, the Vercelli Book and the collections of the museums Leone and Burgundy.

21 places to visit in Vercelli, in the province of Vercelli (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Basilica di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Basilicas

Started in 1219, among the first Gothic churches in Italy: facade with two towers in green stone, brick and white plaster, octagonal tower, and next to the monastery with the cloister of the thirteenth century and the chapter room with nine spans.

  • Where Piazza Roma 35, Vercelli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitpiemonte.com

Sources: italia.it · visitpiemonte.com
