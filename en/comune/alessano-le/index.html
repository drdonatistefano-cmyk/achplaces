What to see in Alessano

At the foot of the Serra dei Cianci, Alessano has one of the best preserved historical centers of Salento, with the Baroque Collegiate, the Doge's Palace and the Renaissance buildings Sangiovanni and Legari; in the territory the hamlet of Montesardo, the rock village of Macurano and the coast of Marina di Novaglie.

Centro storico di Alessano

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

One of the best preserved historical centers of Salento, at the foot of the Serra dei Cianci. It was closed by a walls with four doors (Porta la Terra, Porta Nova, Porta Castello, Porta Lo Chiuso), demolished in 1867; among the Renaissance palaces there is also Palazzo Orsi, with loggia with arches and battlements, and along Via Scipione Sangiovanni line up the villas of the notables of the late nineteenth and early twentieth century.

  • Where Centro storico, Alessano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.alessano.le.it

Chiesa madre di San Salvatore (Collegiata)

Places of worship · Cathedrals

The 18th-century Baroque Collegiate Church, built in the place of the ancient Romanesque Cathedral (1150-1200): Alexander was probably bishop's seat from the Norman era until 1818. The nineteenth-century façade has remained unfinished; inside a painting of the Caravaggio school, Tobiolo and the Angel.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.alessano.le.it · italia.it

Palazzo Sangiovanni (Palazzo dei «Diamanti Zigzagati»)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Renaissance Palace of the late fifteenth century, built under the lordship of Del Balzo and passed to the Sangiovanni in 1643. The façade, unique in its kind, has a zigzag diamond-pointed bulwark; in the center the arched portal with phytomorphic motifs and two floors of arched windows, those of the noble floor framed by paraste. It is private property: it is admired from the outside.

  • Where Piazza Castello 3, Alessano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.alessano.le.it · italia.it

Chiesa e convento dei Frati Cappuccini

In the church there is a wooden altar carved and carved from the eighteenth century and a altarpiece with the "Perdono di Assisi" (Forgiveness of Assisi), probably attributed to Friar Angelo da Copertino.

Insediamento rupestre di Macurano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Rock village about two kilometers south-east of the village, along the road to Marina di Novaglie: two settlements of caves dug in the tufa counter, with engraved crosses and remnants of millstones and piles that reveal its use as hypogeal mills ('trappeti'). The site has been restored by the Municipality.

  • Where Strada Alessano-Marina di Novaglie, Alessano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.alessano.le.it · italia.it

Montesardo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fraction at about 180 meters above sea level, referred to as "Mons arduus" along the route of the Via Traiana; in the Middle Ages it was home to important schools of mathematics and music. It preserves the castle of the XV-XVI century and messapic remains: a walls of the fourth century BC, graves and foundations of buildings.

  • Where Montesardo, Alessano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.alessano.le.it · italia.it

Marina di Novaglie

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient seaport of Alessano, where the ruins of a watchtower remain; Torre Novaglie is still known on the Rio Canal. Near Punta Rossa there is the Blue Grotto, while south of Novaglie the Cipollian and Prazziche caves, neolithic stations, are reachable only from the sea.

  • Where Marina di Novaglie, Alessano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.alessano.le.it · italia.it
