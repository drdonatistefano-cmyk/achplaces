What to see in Giussano

The city of Brianza is linked to the family of the ... Giussano, from which tradition leads Alberto, legendary captain of the Company of Death at the battle of Legnano in 1176. Villa Sartirana

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The residence dates back to the 15th century, belonged to Cattani and Gallizia and purchased in 1640 by Cesare Sartirana, captain of the Spanish army in Milan; at the end of the 18th century it was modernized with internal decorations partly attributed to the school of Andrea Appiani. Buyed by the Municipality in 2001 and restored, it is a historic property of regional value: on the first floor it houses the Civic Library "Don Rinaldo Beretta" with parquet rooms and frescoed ceilings, on the ground floor the Limonaia that gives on the park rich in exotic essences.

Casa Mazenta

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between 1644 and 1665 for Giovanni Battista Giussani on a project attributed to Pellegrino Tibaldi, he then passed to the Mazenta, extinct in 1875, and finally to the Maggioni. Abandoned in the twentieth century, it has been recovered since 1988 and opened in 1998: it preserves the square courtyard system and the large internal porch with six groups of binated columns in pink granite and the staircase with marble balustrade. Today it is a civic hall for events, conferences and exhibitions.

Vetrate di Aligi Sassu nella Sala Consiliare

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

The Council Hall dedicated to Aligi Sassu preserves windows made by the artist with the master glassmaker Tito Toneguzzo of Monza. In one, the mathematician Gabrio Piola (1794-1850) is portrayed among moving numbers that break into colorful forms: Sassu recovers his futurist youthful passion.

Visitable with free guided tours by reservation (villaaperte.info) at Ville Aperte in Brianza

  • Where Piazza Aldo Moro 1, Giussano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
