What to see in Parrano

Small medieval village gathered around a castle of the year 1000 on Roman ruins, on the hills north of Orvieto; under the village the karst gorge of the Devil's Tane, with prehistoric finds, and the thermal waters of the Devil's Bath.

5 places to visit in Parrano, in the province of Terni (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Tane del Diavolo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Complex of karst caves that opens onto a limestone wall overlooking the Fosso del Bagno, under the village; there were found finds from the Paleolithic to the late Bronze Age. At the beginning of the gorge there is a spring at about 28 °C; guided tours of the gorge and caves are possible.

  • Where Fosso del Bagno, Parrano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Centro di documentazione territoriale (Museo del Territorio)

Museums · Themed

Small exhibition space at the entrance of the village, on the ground floor of the primary school Sante De Sanctis, with sections on geology and formation of the gorge of the Tane, excavations and finds from the ancient Paleolithic to the late Bronze Age, the Etruscan tomb of Soriano with vases of the Orvieto Group (VI century BC) and the history of the castrum of Parrano, with copies of the Statute of 1559. It can be combined with the excursion to the Tane.

  • Where Ingresso del paese, Parrano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Centro storico e castello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

A well-preserved medieval centre built around the castle, built around the year 1000 on Roman ruins, with walls and a high watchtower; free municipality from 1500, from the 16th century was part of the Papal State until 1860.

  • Where Centro storico, Parrano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Bagno del Diavolo, acque termali

Local life and traditions · Spas

At the foot of the village, near the river Bagno, there are bicarbonate waters hypothermal alkalines at about 30 degrees, already praised by Andrea Bacci in 1571 as "Argenteae Aquae Sub Parrano Castro"; in the area there is a thermal pond in which to swim.

  • Where Torrente Bagno, Parrano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Fiume fossile e spiaggia di San Lazzaro

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Along the fossil river you will encounter a bed of prehistoric river intact with its pebbles; the fossil beach of San Lazzaro testifies to the ancient presence of the sea, with bivalves, echinoderms, gastropods and a shark tooth found in 1998.

  • Where San Lazzaro, Parrano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it
