What to see in Fontainemore

Country of the lower Lys valley at the gates of the Mont Mars Nature Reserve, between lakes, peat bogs and the historic procession towards Oropa.

5 places to visit in Fontainemore, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Fontainemore in the guide with map and filters

Riserva naturale del Mont Mars

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest in the region, at the foot of the homonymous mountain, among woods, pastures, Alpine meadows, lakes, streams and peat bogs; crossed by the path of the Oropa procession.

  • Where Fontainemore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Centro visitatori della Riserva del Mont Mars

Museums · Science and university

In the capital, with exhibitions on the fauna of the reserve and on the procession of Oropa.

  • Where Fontainemore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Ecomuseo della media montagna

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

In Pra dou Sas, at the limits of the reserve, a village whose buildings have preserved the characteristics of the past becoming a museum of themselves.

  • Where Pra dou Sas, Fontainemore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Ponte di Guillemore

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

  • Entry Free
