What to see in Cervara di Roma

Medieval village about 1,050 metres above the Aniene valley, the highest in the province of Rome, leads to the Parco dei Monti Simbruini and for two centuries the town of artists: from the Fortress and the Collegiate with the Museum of the Mountain to the Sculpted Mountain with sculptures and murals in the rock, up to the meadows of Campaeglio.

Museo della Montagna «Transumanti e Pitturi»

Museums · Themed

Housed in the premises under the Collegiate Church, including the barn of the archpriest and the granary of the curia with original graffiti, the museum documents the transhumance that involved almost all the men of the country, the life of women and traveling trades, religious festivals and fraternities, in particular the sanctuary of Santa Maria della Portella. He expounds ex-vote, vestments and liturgical books, traditional costumes of cervaroli, pastoral tools and works of the artists who have portrayed Cervara, from Giuseppe Antonio Koch and Bartolomeo Pinelli to Giuseppe Ciotti, in addition to the section of modern and contemporary art "Il nuovo Icaro" . It is part of the MEDANIANE museum system; not indicated times, information to the Municipality.

Rocca medievale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built at 1,053 meters to control the Aniene valley, it is the nucleus around which the village developed; in 1052 it passed to the monastery of Santa Scolastica of Subiaco and in 1273 it was occupied by Pelagio by Jenne, who resisted the siege three years. In 1513 the abbot of Subiaco Pompeo Colonna had it rebuilt. From the stairs around the fortress opens the panorama of the valley.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Montagna scolpita e Scalinata degli Artisti

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

Open-air museum engraved in the rock, born as a tribute to the artists who climbed in Cervara in the footsteps of the Grand Tour from Koch and Pinelli to Samuel Morse and Ernest Hébert from the early nineteenth century. The Staircase of the Artists and the Staircase of Peace are walks decorated with sculptures, totems and murals of artists from all over the world; the poet Rafael Alberti called the village a sculpture in the sky.

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Campaegli e Parco dei Monti Simbruini

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Cervara is an access to the Regional Natural Park of the Simbruini Mountains, 30,000 hectares, the largest protected area of Lazio, with paths that can be travelled all year round for excursions, mountain bikes and horse riding. The Campaeglio plateau, on the border with Abruzzo, offers in winter cross-country skiing and dog-drawn sleds; Ennio Morricone sought inspiration for over thirty years and in the small cemetery is buried the documentary writer Marino Bruno Vailati.
