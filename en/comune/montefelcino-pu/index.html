What to see in Montefelcino

Walled village of the Metauro valley: in the Palazzo del Feudatario the Collection of contemporary graphics and the Museum of the ancient itinerant crafts with the bicycles of the street vendors; remains the cistern of the fortress of Francesco di Giorgio Martini, and in Monte Montanaro a shovel of Gianfrancesco Guerrieri.

Palazzo del Feudatario: Raccolta di grafica contemporanea e Museo degli antichi mestieri itineranti

Museums · Themed

Great palace inside the walls wanted by Count Fabio Landriani, with severe arched portal with rustic frames. It hosts the municipal collection of contemporary, figurative and abstract graphics, often artist's proofs (Adriano Calavalle, Mario Bellagamba, Walter Piacesi, Dino Ciacci, Sandro Tombari, Mario Borghi), a work by Anselmo Bucci, a tablet by Emilio Antonioni and the bicycles of the craftsmen and street vendors from the twenties to the fifties, equipped with barbers, crockery, carpenter, shoemaker, freighter, for bread, milk and fried fish. In summer there is the Market of the Feudatario of handicrafts and antiques.

  • Where Piazza Francesca da Rimini 1, Montefelcino

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiese di Santa Maria Scala Coeli e dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Santa Maria Scala Coeli has two frescoes, one of which is attributed to the school of Perugino; the church of Saints Peter and Paul was built in 1290.

Chiesa di San Marco a Monte Montanaro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Monte Montanaro, it preserves a large altarpiece by Gianfrancesco Guerrieri from Fossombrone with the Madonna and Child and Saints Francis, Marco, Biagio and Lorenzo.
