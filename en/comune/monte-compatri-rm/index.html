What to see in Monte Compatri

Borgo dei Castelli Romani on a side cone of the Latium volcano, between the woods and near the lakes Albano and Nemi, 28 km from Rome: the medieval center is among the best preserved in the province, with the Angel Fountain, the cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta and, in the upper part, the Convent of San Silvestro with its Mannerist and Caravaggio's art gallery.

Convento e Pinacoteca di San Silvestro

Museums · Old masters · Viewpoint

In the highest part of the village, the convent has a first settlement of the mid-15th century but it is largely late seventeenth century. The gallery contains paintings from the Mannerist and Caravaggio school: the Trasverberazione di Santa Teresa di scuola bolognese, paintings from Luca Fiammingo, Maddalena penitente and Christ at the Mount of Olives of Cerrini, Christ crowned with thorns and Emmaus dinner of the Caravaggio school, works by Lucas de la Haye and Giovanni Della Miseria; the San Giuseppe carpenter of Gerrit van Honthorst was stolen. The MiC card (2020) does not indicate opening days.

Fontana dell'Angelo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

At the entrance of the village, the sculpture depicts a winged genius with pickaxes that light a fuse, in homage to the workers who brought in the water of the springs of Carpinello, some of whom died using explosives to break the rock.

  • Where Ingresso del paese, Monte Compatri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Centro storico e Fontana del Belvedere

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The medieval village is described as one of the best preserved in the province of Rome; in the square near the Duomo the Belvedere Fountain, where women drew water with traditional jugs.

Monte Salomone

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A relief of natural and geological interest with hiking trails and panoramic views of the volcanic landscape and Tuscolo; from here a walk continues towards the Asinelli Hill, with activities for children and adults.
