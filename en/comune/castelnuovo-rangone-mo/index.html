What to see in Castelnuovo Rangone

Country of the high plain 13 km from Modena, documented since 1025 and for almost four centuries feud of the Rangoni, known for the processing of pigmeat: the Torrione della Rocca, the monument to the pig, the museum of the salumery and, in Montale, the archaeological park of Terramara and the tomb of Luciano Pavarotti.

Parco archeologico e Museo all'aperto della Terramara di Montale

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Park of 23,000 square meters dedicated to the terracing, villages of the Bronze Age of the Po Valley arose towards the middle of the II millennium BC and disappeared around 1200 BC. Under a cover are observed the excavations with over four centuries of life of the village; the open-air museum reconstructs life-size moat, embankment with palisade, door and two large houses furnished with copies of pottery, tools and weapons. On opening days 9.30-13.30 and 14.30-18.30, guided tours every 45 minutes, recommended booking.

  • Where SS 12 Nuova Estense, Montale, 41050 Castelnuovo Rangone (MO)
  • Entry €7, reduced €5 (6-13 years), free up to 5 years and 65 years
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.parcomontale.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: parcomontale.it · visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Torrione e Palazzo Comunale

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The 15th century civic tower, the main monument of the town, the rest of the Rangoni Rocca: testifies to the X century castle strengthened by the Rangons in the fourteenth century; recently there are traces of walls of the thirteenth century. The castle, rebuilt after the Second World War, houses the Town Hall.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Castelnuovo Rangone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Celestino Papa

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church mentioned already in 1181 and rebuilt between 1856 and 1866 on a project by engineer Cesare Costa; it preserves paintings by Adeodato Malatesta, including St. Celestine I of 1873 at the high altar and the figures of St. Louis Gonzaga and St. Anthony Abbot.

  • Where Centro, Castelnuovo Rangone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Monumento al Maiale

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Bronze statue of the sculptor Kee Sansen, inaugurated on December 14, 1997 between the Torrione and the church of San Celestino, symbol of the economy of the country linked to the processing of pigmeat.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Castelnuovo Rangone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

MuSa - Museo della Salumeria Villani

Museums · Themed

The first Italian museum dedicated to salami, with a multimedia exhibition next to the historic headquarters of Villani, founded in 1886 and considered the oldest salami factory in Emilia-Romagna.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Ciclabile Jack Kerouac e Bacheche poetiche

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Cycle track on the old provincial railway track, inaugurated in October 2000 as part of the Modena-Vignola route through Castelnuovo Rangone, Spilamberto and Castelvetro, with plates of texts by Kerouac at the former tollgates. Since June 1998, parks, bikes and historic center have hosted 22 glass bulletin boards with poetry, pages of novels, songs, letters and diaries.

  • Where Castelnuovo Rangone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
