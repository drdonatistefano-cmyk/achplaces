What to see in Borgomale

The village of the Langhe, known as the "borgo delle mele" (the "borgo delle mele") is dominated by the castle of the five towers that the Falletti di Alba built from 1429 on a hill above the Bern stream; in the village the parish church of Sant'Eusebio, rebuilt in 1662.

Castello di Borgomale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

It was built in 1429 by the Falletti di Alba on the foundations of a fortification of the 12th century; in the Baroque age it had an external staircase covered with architectural frescoes and medallions with knights. It was accessed by a drawbridge; inside the arms room, stairs of honor, halls of Caminata and fresco and round path. He hosted Pope Pius VII on his way to France for the coronation of Napoleon; from 1631, with the Treaty of Cherasco, the fief passed to the Savoys.

Privately owned and used as a summer home, it is not normally open to visitors: for visits to municipal offices (open in the past during the open castles)

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Eusebio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of the diocese of Alba, mentioned for the first time in 1635 and described in the visits of 1644 and 1730; it was rebuilt in 1662 and partially rebuilt in 1956. In the seventies of the twentieth century the presbytery had a structural intervention and in 2015-2016 the cover was restored.

  • Where Piazza della Parrocchia, Borgomale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.borgomale.cn.it

Sources: chieseitaliane.chiesacattolica.it · comune.borgomale.cn.it
