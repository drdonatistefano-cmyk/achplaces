What to see in Reggio di Calabria

City on the Strait of Messina, rebuilt after the earthquake of 1908 on roads parallel to the sea: the National Archaeological Museum with the Bronzes of Riace, the Lungomare Falcomatà with the Greek walls and the Roman baths, the Aragonese Castle, the Church of the Ottimati and the Duomo.

MArRC - Museo archeologico nazionale

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Palace designed by Marcello Piacentini on Piazza De Nava, one of the rare buildings created to accommodate museum collections. At level D the Bronzes of Riace, masterpieces of the Greek bronze of the fifth century BC found in 1972, with the Kouros, the Head of the philosopher, the Head of Basel, the Dioscuri and the pinakes.

  • Where Piazza De Nava 26, Reggio Calabria
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Lungomare Falcomatà

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Walk through palm trees, magnolias, flowerbeds with exotic plants and classical and late liberty palaces, with a view of the Strait; D'Annunzio called it the most beautiful kilometer in Italy.

  • Where Lungomare Italo Falcomatà, Reggio Calabria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Arena dello Stretto e Athena Promachos

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Open air theatre inspired by Greek tradition, where before 1908 there was the Pier of Porto Salvo. At the top, almost at sea, the marble chip of 1932 by the Messinian sculptor Bonfiglio dedicated to Vittorio Emanuele III, with the bronze statue of Athena Promachos.

Castello Aragonese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortification of Byzantine origin (IX-XI century), with round towers wanted by Ferdinand I of Aragon; in 1911 the Ministry of War allowed its demolition for building purposes and the oldest part was demolished.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Reggio Calabria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sigecweb.beniculturali.it

Chiesa degli Ottimati

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of Byzantine origin (X century), re-used in the XII century as a private chapel of the Norman kings, who reached it from the castle with an underground passage. It preserves columns and cosmatesque pieces of floor; on the high altar an Annunciation of Tuscan school of 1597.

  • Where Via Castello, Reggio Calabria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Duomo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Rebuilt after the earthquake of 1908 with a twentieth-century façade that reinterprets Romanesque and Gothic forms, with the statues of Saints Paul and Stephen of Nicaea by Francesco Jerace. At three aisles, it has in the Chapel of the Blessed Sacrament inlays of polychrome marbles and a canvas of 1665 by Domenico Marolì; at the bottom of the right aisle the fragment of the column linked to the preaching of Saint Paul.

  • Where Via Tommaso Campanella 46, Reggio Calabria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Basilica dell'Eremo

Sanctuary of the Madonna della Consolazione, patron saint of the city, in the upper part of Reggio: it houses the icon painted by Nicolò Andrea Capriolo in 1547-1548. The current building, designed by Anna Anastasi Sbarracani, was completed in 1961 and in 1971 elevated to a minor basilica.
