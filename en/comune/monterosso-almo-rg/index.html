What to see in Monterosso Almo

The first village of the Iblei Mountains chain, about 700 meters away, already documented in 1168 as "Monte Jahalmo" and rebuilt after the earthquake of 1693: the piazza "u chianu" with San Giovanni Battista and the noble palaces, the Neo-Gothic Mother Church, Sant'Antonio Abate and the medieval Quarter of Cava; in the surroundings the necropolis of Monte Casasia.

Piazza San Giovanni («u chianu») e Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Large pedestrian square closed by the Church of Sant'Anna, with marble balustrade, and overlooking the Palazzo dei Baroni Noto, the Town Hall, the Palazzo Sardo and the monumental Palazzo Cocuzza, neoclassical nineteenth century. At the bottom of a large staircase, St. John the Baptist has a baroque and colonnade façade that holds the bell tower; inside stuccoes, statues in alabaster of Our Lady Assunta (16th century) and a Madonna dormant in oriental iconography wax.

  • Where Piazza San Giovanni, Monterosso Almo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info

Chiesa Madre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt after the earthquake of 1693 with a neo-Gothic façade, rare in the area, preceded by a stairway church; of the church of the XIII century there are two waterworks and part of the stone floor behind the altar. The wooden crucifix is attributed to Fra' Umile da Petralia.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Antonio, Monterosso Almo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt after the earthquake of 1693, it has portal and three-cell bell tower with stone balustrade; the only aisle preserves a large altarpiece of 1525, by unknown author, with the Martyrdom of San Lorenzo.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Antonio, Monterosso Almo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info

Quartiere della Cava

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Reached by the stairway to the left of Sant'Antonio Abate and by a bridge, it is the district that best preserves the medieval aspect; between the Matrice and the Addolorata, at Christmas the historic district houses a living crib with a natural cave. Along Via Roma you can see the Baroque Palazzo Burgio and the nineteenth-century Casa Barone.

  • Where Quartiere Cava, Monterosso Almo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info

History and architecture · Archaeology
