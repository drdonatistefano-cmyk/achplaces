What to see in Monselice

Walled city at the foot of the Colle della Rocca, in the Euganean Hills: from the Piazza Mazzini with the Civic Tower and the Loggetta you go up to Castello Cini, to the ancient Pieve di Santa Giustina, to the Jubilee Sanctuary of the Seven Churches of Vincenzo Scamozzi with Villa Duodo, up to the Mastio Federiciano of 1239 on the top; from the center also starts the path of Mount Ricco.

Castello di Monselice (Castello Cini)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

At the foot of the Colle della Rocca, it brings together four nuclei: the Romanesque House (end of the XI century) and the Castelletto (XII century), the Ezzelinian Tower of the XIII century, with the chimneys and the towers, wanted by the Da Carrara in the fourteenth century, and Ca' Marcello, built by the Marcellos after 1405, with the Library of the end of the sixteenth century and the Venetian courtyard with the chapel. In 1935 Count Vittorio Cini restored him and furnished him with Gothic and Renaissance furniture and a rich armory; since 1981 he has been from the Veneto Region. In the Library are the Antiquarium Longobardo and the Museum of Rareness Carlo Scarpa. Open every day except Tuesday, with guided tours.

Mastio Federiciano (Rocca di Monselice)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the top of the hill, the fortress was rebuilt in 1239 by Frederick II of Swabia on the remains of a Byzantine castle conquered by the Longobards in 602, demolishing the ancient parish church of Santa Giustina; Monselice became the seat of the imperial court of appeal for the Trevigiana Brand. The pyramid trunk base is covered with blocks of trachyte and supports a quadrangular tower, today uncovered; you can visit the excavations with the crenellated walls and the apse of the parish church, and in the Mastio are exhibited ceramics, tools and silvers with a plastic of the fortification. The last siege was that of the League of Cambrai in 1509.

Santuario giubilare delle Sette Chiese

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Devotional path of the end of the sixteenth-early seventeenth century on the slopes of the hill: six chapels designed by Vincenzo Scamozzi and built between 1605 and 1615 imitate the Roman basilicas ('Romanis Basilicis pares') and keep canvases of Palma il Giovane; the path ends in the church of San Giorgio, already oratory of the Duodos, which exposes the bodies of martyrs including Valentine's Day. Hours 9.30-11.30 and 14.30-17.30.

  • Where Via delle Sette Chiesette, Monselice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.monselice.padova.it · italia.it

Villa Duodo ed Esedra di San Francesco Saverio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built by the Venetian family Duodo on the site of the ancient castle of San Giorgio: the oldest wing is from 1593 on a project by Vincenzo Scamozzi, the façade of 1740; on the left side there is the large 17th century stone staircase, the Exedra dedicated to San Francesco Saverio. You visit only from outside, along the path of the Seven Churches.

  • Where Via delle Sette Chiesette, Monselice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: castellodimonselice.it · italia.it

Antica Pieve di Santa Giustina (Duomo Vecchio)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1256 in late Romanesque style with Gothic elements, with fifteenth-century pronaos on the portal, after the ancient parish church on the top had been demolished for the fortress of the federian. Inside paintings, remains of frescoes, a copy of the Madonna dell'Umiltà of the 14th century, a fifteenth-century polyptych of the Venetian school and four seventeenth-century bas-reliefs attributed to Giovanni Marchiori.

Piazza Mazzini, Torre Civica e Loggetta del Monte di Pietà

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Civic Tower was built in 1244 by order of Frederick II through the vicar Ezzelino da Romano and elevated in 1504 for the municipal bell .Martino is a national monument and the clock is loaded by hand. On the square overlook the Castle Cini and the fifteenth-century palace of Monte di Pietà, with the seventeenth-century loggia with Doric columns, now home to the Tourist Office and the Library.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo San Paolo

Museums · City history

The monumental complex of São Paulo, built on the remains of a medieval church, houses the Civic Museum that tells the history of the city from the Roman era to the twentieth century; the early Christian crypt preserves a fresco of St. Francis, indicated as the oldest image of the saint in Veneto.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Chiesa e convento di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

I was born in 1162 as a hospital for the poor and pilgrims, then a Benedictine monastery and since 1677 the seat of the Franciscan Friars Minor; inside a pictorial cycle of Michele Desubleo and works of Palma the Young and Giambattista Maganza. In the convent there is a Franciscan ethnological museum with objects from missions to East Asia (especially China), Africa and Polynesia, which can be visited through the missionary center.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sentiero del Monte Ricco

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A path of about 5 km between woods and clearings, easy and without steep slopes, with a deviation of 1.7 km to Monte Castello (2-3 hours, recommended in spring and autumn). It climbs to the Red House (250 m) and to the panoramic terrace with the statue of Hercules that holds the globe, from which you can see the Rock and Euganean Hills; the mountain has been modelled by the trachyte quarries, now disused.

  • Where Partenza da piazza Mazzini o dalla stazione, Monselice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.monselice.padova.it
