What to see in San Paolo Albanese

The smallest municipality of Basilicata, founded around 1534 by Albanian refugees in the Sarmento valley in the Pollino National Park; the Arbëreshe community preserves language and Byzantine rite, told in the Museum of Arbëreshe culture.

4 places to visit in San Paolo Albanese, in the province of Potenza (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della cultura arbëreshe

Museums · Themed

Born as an agropastoral exhibition in 1975, it has since 1993 a home made from old houses in the historic center: everyday objects, traditional costumes, tools and a path on the processing of the broom, from the collection to the fabrics, with many pieces donated by the inhabitants; it has a specialized library and a craft workshop.

MiC card (updated to April 2020) indicates closed every day

Sentieri verso il Massiccio del Pollino

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the village start numerous paths that cross thick woods up to the highest and most rugged ridges, habitat of the rare pine loricato, symbol of the park.
