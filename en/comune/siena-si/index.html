What to see in Siena

Medieval town on the three hills, historical UNESCO heritage centre since 1995, with the Campo, the Duomo and the 17 districts of the Palio.

31 places to visit in Siena, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza del Campo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The shell-shaped square was created as a space for civic events, closed by the Palazzo Pubblico and the Torre del Mangia, with the Fonte Gaia, a nineteenth-century copy of the original Jacopo della Quercia.

  • Where Piazza del Campo, Siena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.terredisiena.it

Sources: terredisiena.it · visittuscany.com

Palazzo Pubblico e Museo Civico

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The Palazzo della Signoria built between 1297 and 1310, now seat of the Municipality, with the Civic Museum: the Majesty and the Guidoriccio of Simone Martini and the cycle of the Good and Bad Government of Ambrogio Lorenzetti (1338-1339).

  • Where Piazza del Campo 1, Siena
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museocivico.comune.siena.it
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: museocivico.comune.siena.it · terredisiena.it

Torre del Mangia

History and architecture · Towers · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The fourteenth-century tower of the Palazzo Pubblico, 88 meters high according to Terre di Siena: you climb on foot, to contingent groups, up to the view over the whole city.

  • Where Piazza del Campo 1, Siena
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Duomo di Siena e Pavimento

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Gothic-Romanesque cathedral with the pulpit of Nicola Pisano (1265-68), the young statues of Michelangelo on the Piccolomini altar and the San Giovanni Battista di Donatello; the marble tarsie floor is discovered only in some periods of the year.
