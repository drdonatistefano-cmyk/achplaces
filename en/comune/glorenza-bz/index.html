What to see in Glorenza

The smallest town in South Tyrol, in the upper Val Venosta, preserves intact the sixteenth-century walls with three tower-doors, rebuilt at the behest of Massimiliano I after the battle of Calva in 1499: inside, the street of the porticos of the market of 1291 and the Renaissance residences; outside the walls, the parish church of San Pancrazio and the church of San Giacomo in Söles.

Mura e torri-porta

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The last intact city belt of South Tyrol: about 10 meters high, the walls were rebuilt in the sixteenth century by order of Emperor Maximilian I after the battle of Calva on 22 May 1499, the Confederates plundered the city; the extension, proposed by military architect Jörg Kölderer, was completed in 1580. There are three door-towers and the ring of walls around the old shopping centre; sections of the oldest walls are seen in the alley San Pancrazio.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Via dei Portici, piazza e residenze storiche

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

On 24 August 1291 Mainardo II of Tyrol opened the market of San Bartolomeo: under the porticos of Laubengasse there were trade of Inntal salt, Valtellina wine, sheep and wheat, today shops and coffee. Around the square, with two large chestnut trees and a fountain, and in the nearby streets: Frölich house (1570) with coats of arms and allegory of the seven deadly sins, the hotel Corona wanted in 1562 by the knight Reinprecht von Hendl, the town hall of 1573-1591 (Hendlspurg residence from 1604), the ancient court of about 1510 and the tower Flurin, prison from 1825 to 1931. The church of the Hospital, dedicated to the Virgin, was rebuilt in 1665-1669 after the fire of 1664.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Torre di Porta Sluderno: «Stazioni di una piccola città»

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Since 2011 the tower hosts on two floors the exhibition on the history of Glorenza: trade and defence, market and crafts, local curiosities, with panels on the 16th century reconstruction and fortification. From the tower you walk along the path of the round and the blackbirds and you visit the round corner tower. Open from May to September, Tuesday-Friday 14-17 (July and August also Monday afternoon), in October 14-16; free entry with the Reschensee Guest Card.

  • Where Porta Sluderno, Glorenza
  • Entry €3, children 11-15 €1,50, over 60 and groups €2,50
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: glorenza.eu · suedtirol.info · italia.it

Museo Paul Flora

Museums · Modern art

On the second floor of the Tower of Tubre (door of the church), since 2011 a permanent exhibition on the life and works of Paul Flora, graphic designer and illustrator born in Glorenza who for about 25 years designed cartoons for "Die Zeit"; he lived mainly in Innsbruck, but Glorenza remained his point of reference.

  • Where Via Flora, Torre di Tubre, Glorenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Pancrazio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Outside the walls, beyond the Adige: mentioned for the first time in 1227, preserves the Romanesque phase only the bell tower, while nave and choir were replaced by Gothic forms; the Baroque onion dome is after 1664. Open during Sunday and festive Masses.

  • Where Fuori le mura, Glorenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: glorenza.eu · italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di San Giacomo a Söles

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

It was mentioned in 1220 and 1249, burned in 1499 after the battle of Calva and rebuilt in Gothic forms in 1570-1580; the excavations found an earlier church of the VI-VIII century and fragments of Romanesque frescoes of excellent quality. You can reach it on foot to the Sölesweg; guided tours in German only on request.

  • Where Sölesweg, Söles, Glorenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Mulino civico

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Three-century mill restored on the Mühlbach, channel derived from the Adige, in front of the remains of the ancient walls in the alley San Pancrazio.

  • Where Vicolo San Pancrazio, Glorenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: suedtirol.info · glorenza.eu · italia.it

Distilleria di whisky PUNI

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

First distillery of whiskey in Italy, built in 2012 on a project by Werner Tscholl just outside the walls: a 13-metre brick cube inspired by the perforated walls of the barns, with vats and stills in a vaulted basement.

  • Where Via Mühlbach 2, Glorenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
