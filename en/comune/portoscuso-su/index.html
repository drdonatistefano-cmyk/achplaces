What to see in Portoscuso

Sulcis center on the south-western coast, born at the end of the sixteenth century as a village of fishermen and coralmakers, in front of the island of San Pietro: the Spanish tower, the trap of Su Pranu still active between May and June, the church of Santa Maria d'Itria and the town beach of Portopaglietto.

Torre spagnola di Portoscuso

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The tower was built between the end of the sixteenth and the beginning of the seventeenth century, 16 meters high with a base of about 22 meters, on a promontory about 30 meters above sea in visual connection with the towers of Calasetta and Carloforte; in 1767 it was guarded by an alcaid, an artillery and five soldiers, it was disarmed in 1851 and, restored, it is now of the Municipality. Inside once a dome on four ribs; the original entrance was 5 meters high, with spiral staircase in the wall thickness. It hosts exhibitions, events and conferences.

  • Where Via Torre 1, Portoscuso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Tonnara di Su Pranu e chiesa di Sant'Antonio da Padova

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Tennara was built in the middle of the 17th century next to the tower, still active every year between May and June for the mornings; inside is the church of Sant'Antonio da Padova.

  • Where Tonnara Su Pranu, Portoscuso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria d'Itria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church built in 1655, dedicated to the patron saint of the tunarotti; the festival is celebrated 50 days after Easter with sporting events, music and folk.

  • Where Portoscuso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Spiaggia di Portopaglietto

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient small port for the islands of Sulcis, today a town beach of about 250 meters in the northern part of the town, with golden sand, low bottom suitable for children and two cliffs of trachytes that shelter it from the mistral; behind a pine forest with playground, in front of the rock of the Ghinghetta with the red and black banded lighthouse and the island of San Pietro. A path from the beach leads to the Spanish tower.

  • Where Portopaglietto, Portoscuso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Capo Altano

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Promontory north of Portopaglietto, reachable by the road that runs along the pine forest, with abandoned military fortifications and excellent panoramic points for the sunset.

  • Where Capo Altano, Portoscuso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Necropoli punica di San Giorgio

History and architecture · Archaeology

Necropolis with eleven burials (770-750 BC), the greatest testimony of Phoenicians, Punics and Romans in the area; in the territory there are also neolithic domus de janas and nuraghi Atzori and Bacu Ollasta with villages of huts.

  • Where Località San Giorgio, Portoscuso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
