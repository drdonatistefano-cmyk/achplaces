What to see in Govone

On the hill of the Roero overlooking the Tanaro, the castle of Guarino Guarini chosen by Carlo Felice as a summer residence, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Castello Reale di Govone

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built by the accounts Solaro from 1675 on a project by Guarino Guarini and completed around 1780 with the staircase adorned with marbles and sculptures of the Fontana d'Ercole della Venaria; of the Savoy since 1795, summer residence of Carlo Felice. You can visit royal apartments, party hall and Chinese halls. UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  • Where Piazza Roma 1, Govone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website fondoambiente.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Borgo di Govone

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village at the top of the hill, with one of the most beautiful views of the Roero on the valley of Tanaro, among uphill and local roads that promote wines in the area.
