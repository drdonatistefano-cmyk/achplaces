What to see in Salzano

Salzano, where Giuseppe Sarto was parish priest from 1867 to 1875 before becoming Pope Pius X, houses the Museo San Pio X, the Filanda Romanin-Jacur of 1872 with its multimedia museum, the romantic Roman-Jacur Park and the nineteenth-century church of San Bartolomeo.

Museo San Pio X

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated on 3 October 1999 for the Jubilee of 2000 on 160 m2, it contains sacred furnishings and vestments of the parish where Giuseppe Sarto, future Pius X, was parish priest from 1867 to 1875: on the ground floor cups from the eighteenth to the twentieth century, including the golden cup with patena donated by Pius X in 1906, a 1754 piss, processional crosses of the seventeenth century, reliquaries, an 1867 monstrance and ex-vote; on the first floor vestments from the fifteenth to the twentieth century, including a sixteenth-century planet in red velvet and one of 1904 donated by Pius X, wooden furnishings and the Madonna della Roata, a rare example of dressed statue.

  • Where Via Roma, Salzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.salzano.ve.it

Sources: museoditorcello.cittametropolitana.ve.it · comune.salzano.ve.it

Filanda Romanin-Jacur e Museo della Filanda

Museums · Themed

An ancient silk factory built in 1872 by Leo Romanin-Jacur, with a central body for the machinery and spinning rooms and two side wings porticoed; inside the Museo della Filanda, multimedia and immersive, revives the work of adults and children in the ancient spinning mill with a sensory experience.

  • Where Via Roma 166, Salzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.salzano.ve.it

Sources: comune.salzano.ve.it

Parco Romanin-Jacur

Nature and landscape · City parks

Romantic park of 48,000 m2, with canals, caves and paths, built in 1854 by the Jacur family in addition to the villa.

  • Where Salzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.salzano.ve.it

Sources: comune.salzano.ve.it

Chiesa di San Bartolomeo Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish church of the first half of the nineteenth century in classic Tuscan style; on the façade, on the sides of the portal, two niches house the statues of St. John the Baptist and St. Bartholomew.

  • Where Via Roma, Salzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.salzano.ve.it

Sources: comune.salzano.ve.it
