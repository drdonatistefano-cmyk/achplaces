What to see in Cossato

Cittadina del Biellese orientale sul creente Strona; in the hamlet Castellengo, on the edge of the plateau of the baragges, there are the castle of the Frichignono, the Romanesque parish church of the Saints Peter and Paul with late-Gothic frescoes and the Eco-Museum of Cossathes and Baragge; in the village the eighteenth-century Villa Ranzoni.

Ecomuseo del Cossatese e delle Baragge

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In the former school of the hamlet Castellengo, which also houses the Park of Baraggia, it is dedicated to the history and rural civilization of the Cossatian area: traditions, nature and memory of work and local life. It is part of the Biellese Museum Network; it opens on established days, with free and free entrance (tel. 015 351128).

  • Where Via Castellengo 16F, Castellengo (Cossato)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo di Castellengo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient church isolated between woods and fields at the foot of the castle, with next to the cemetery; cited in a bull by Frederick I of 1155. The central nave is Romanesque, the Gothic right with hanging arches, the left behind 1460, the year of St. Christopher painted on the façade; portico of the seventeenth century, apse enlarged at the end of the eighteenth century. The restorations have brought to light late-Gothic frescoes among the best preserved of the Biellese.

  • Where Frazione Castellengo 1, Cossato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.cossato.bi.it

Sources: comune.cossato.bi.it

Castello di Castellengo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Across a hill on the plain, it dates back to the 10th century with Alberico di Monterone; from 1198 to 1406 the Bulgarians, occupied by Bando di Firenze and reconquered by Amedeo VIII di Savoia in 1409, passed then to the Frichignonos, counts since 1630, whose coat of arms is next to the sundial. Since the end of the 17th century it has been transformed into a noble French residence, abandoned after 1883, and has been restored since 1990: frescoed rooms on the noble floor, terrace gardens, chapel of St. John the Baptist.

Villa Ranzoni

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Villa-style noble residence on the right bank of the Strona, with original nucleus probably before 1722 (birth of Benedetto Felice Ranzoni, court doctor); facades and decorations unified in the early twentieth century. Scalone d'honore on the noble floor. Owned by the municipality since 1988, it hosts offices, cultural services and the municipal library; free access.

  • Where Via Ranzoni 24, Cossato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.cossato.bi.it

Sources: comune.cossato.bi.it
