What to see in Fontanellato

3 places to visit in Fontanellato, in the province of Parma (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 2 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Open Fontanellato in the guide with map and filters

Rocca Sanvitale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The fortress surrounded by the moat in the center of the village, with the small room of Diana and Atteone frescoed by Parmigianino and the optical chamber.

  • Where Centro storico, Fontanellato
  • Entry €12 whole; €5 from 6 to 16 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Labirinto della Masone

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Viewpoint

The largest bamboo maze in the world wanted by publisher Franco Maria Ricci, with the art collection and the pyramid in the center.

  • Where Fontanellato
  • Entry To be checked: ticket on the Labyrinth website
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets

Sources: parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Santuario della Beata Vergine del Rosario

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

The sanctuary built by the Dominicans between 1641 and 1660, the destination of pilgrims, with the neo-baroque façade of Lamberto Cusani of 1913.

  • Where Fontanellato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · castellidelducato.it
