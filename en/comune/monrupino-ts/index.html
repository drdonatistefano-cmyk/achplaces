What to see in Monrupino

The smallest municipality in the province of Trieste, on the Karst at the Slovenian border: the fortified fortress with the sanctuary of the Assumption dominates the plateau, in Rupingrande the Carsica House tells about the peasant life of the nineteenth century and every two years it hosts the karst weddings.

Rocca e santuario di Monrupino (Tabor)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Born as a prehistoric castle, the hill was the scene of clashes between Romans and Istrians, then castrum and finally fortress, refuge of the population during the raids, especially Turkish. Inside the walls stands the sanctuary of the Blessed Virgin Assumption, a pilgrimage destination, built in 1512 and repeatedly expanded and restored; according to tradition, Our Lady left a mark on a nearby rock, considered a symbol of fertility. The wedding of the karst weddings is celebrated there.

Casa Carsica (Kraška hiša)

Museums · Themed

On the edge of Rupingrande, a typical rural house of the Karst, perhaps two to three hundred years old, with the current appearance of 1831 and restored in 1968: roof in stone slabs, kitchen and cellar on the ground floor, bedroom and barn on the first floor, furnishings, tools and costumes of the nineteenth century donated by the inhabitants. In the summer barn are held contemporary art exhibitions and events; every two years, at the end of August of the odd years, it hosts the karst weddings, re-enactment of a Slovenian wedding rite of the late nineteenth century. From April to November open Sunday and holidays 11-12.30 and 15-17. It is also an information point of the reserve of Monte Orsario.

Riserva naturale regionale del Monte Orsario (Medvedjak)

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Reserve of 156 hectares on the Trieste Karst at the Slovenian border: karst land partly bushy, mature pinewood of black pine, mixed forest and limestone outskirts with furrowed fields; live there the wild cat and the hedgehog, while brown bear and golden jackal appear only rarely. A path, which starts from the road between Col and Fernetti, climbs to the top where a wooden tower offers a 360 degree view of the sea and Slovenia; a short detour leads to an artificial pond. Information points at the Casa Carsica in Repen and the Town Hall of Col.
