What to see in Mezzolombardo

Borgo della Piana Rotaliana, the vineyard that Goethe called the most beautiful in Europe and the land of Teroldego, with the ancient district of Piaz, the Castle of the Tower, the church of San Pietro sul Colle and the bridge suspended towards the Toresela; from the early nineteenth century it distills Villa de Varda, with its family museum.

Giro della Toresela, ponte sospeso e chiesa di San Pietro

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

A short ring of 1.38 km (about 30 minutes, 65 m of difference in altitude, rather steep) that starts behind the main church, climbs to the hill of San Pietro, where stands the oldest church of the village with views over the whole valley, and crosses the suspended bridge of 130 meters up to the panoramic tower of the Toresela. From the bridge you can see on one side the colorful roofs of the village and the vineyards of the Piana, on the other the mountain with the mountaineers on the ferrata of Val del Rì. In the summer, the Toresela restaurant opens on Friday, Saturday and Sunday from 10am to 6pm, until the last weekend of September.

  • Where Via San Pietro 8, Mezzolombardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitrotaliana.it

Sources: visitrotaliana.it · visittrentino.info

Castello della Torre

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Symbol of the village, in the historic center of the Piaz at the foot of the mountain: the nucleus is a tower of the fifteenth century, then incorporated in the baronial palace flanked by two ornamental towers; in the panoramic garden the church of Sant'Apollonia of the sixteenth century. Over the centuries, it has hosted noble houses and bishop-principles; today it is part of the Tamanini family and it welcomes events, weddings and art exhibitions.

private property, not visitable: you can see it from the alleys or at events

  • Where Via G. Romedio De Scari 66, Mezzolombardo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitrotaliana.it

Sources: visitrotaliana.it

Il Piaz, centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The old quarter, with alleys, palaces and noble villas along the main streets; on the main street residences of the eighteenth and early nineteenth century and family shops, once tailors and shoemakers, today fashion shops and handicrafts. The village was defended by the castles of the Tower and San Pietro and the Rocchetta, which guarded the road near the gorge of the Walnut.

  • Where Piaz, centro storico, Mezzolombardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitrotaliana.it

Sources: visitrotaliana.it · visittrentino.info

Monte Fausior e Val del Rì

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The mountain dominates the village: the ferrata of the Val del Rì can only be walked with appropriate equipment, while the SAT 602B trail is a simple excursion with panoramic points on Mezzolombardo that starts from the cemetery and arrives at the beginning of Fai della Paganella.

  • Where Partenza dal cimitero, Mezzolombardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitrotaliana.it

Sources: visitrotaliana.it · visittrentino.info
