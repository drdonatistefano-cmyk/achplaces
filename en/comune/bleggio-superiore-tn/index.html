Borgo di Rango

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Last inhabited before the Passo Durone (the name comes from the Celtic "Randa" , limit), was crossroads on the Imperial road between Giudicarie, Rendena, Garda and plain. From the stone stairs and the arch with the Renaissance bifora you enter a maze of judiciary houses made of stone and wood, intact from the eighteenth century, joined by androns ("vòlt"), porticoes and shorts ("portech") with large portals, loggias, racks for drying corn and hay and the square fountain where the flocks were watered. From mid-November to the end of December the arcades host Christmas markets.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Pieve di Santa Croce del Bleggio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The seat of the ancient Pieve del Bleggio is one of the rare sacred buildings in pure Renaissance style of Trentino, rebuilt in the sixteenth century by the Lombard architect Antonio Gras on the model of Santa Maria Maggiore in Trento; a Romanesque crypt leads to the temple of the XI-XII century. Frescoes by Carlo Donati and, in the chapel, a wooden altar with Pietà and scenes of the Passion that has guarded the Holy Cross considered miraculous since 1624. Open every day 9-17.

Murales di Balbido

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Balbido, which tradition wants refuge of witches (at the entrance of the valley were erected five iron crosses, one still visible), has the façades of the houses painted by the itinerant group murals naïf: today are 222 works with a naive technique or graffito, with themes such as the legend of witches, the dream of the chimney sweep, the stories of grandmothers and the palio of the horses of Santa Giustina.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Passo Durone

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valico at 986 meters above Rango, a large valley of lawn surrounded by woods overlooking the Carè Alto and Cima Sèra; picnic area and starting point for excursions. Along the old road from the Val Marcia to the pass are the "filagne" , walls of vertical slabs of granite.

  • Where Passo Durone, Bleggio Superiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sentiero della Noce

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

An easy ring of 9.6 km (about 2 hours and 30, 265 m of difference in altitude) from the parking lot near the Town Hall of Santa Croce through Bivedo, Cavrasto, Balbido, Santa Giustina and Madice, among the walnuts of the Bleggiana Variety, cultivated since Roman times and Presidio Slow Food since 2016.

  • Where Santa Croce, Bleggio Superiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info
