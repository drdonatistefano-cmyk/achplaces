What to see in Sermoneta

Borgo Bandiera Arancione on the Lepini Mountains overlooking the Pontina Plain, dominated by the Caetani Castle of the 13th century and closed by Renaissance walls; at the foot of Mount Corvino the Cistercian Abbey of Valvisciolo, linked to the legend of the Templars.

8 places to visit in Sermoneta, in the province of Latina (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Caetani

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Founded by the Annibaldi in the 13th century, it remains the mastic and counter tower called "maletto"; the Caetans expanded it and created the Camere Pinte. Towers, bastions, gun square with cistern, Hall of the Cardinal of the sixteenth century and kitchen with large fireplace; restored by Gelasio Caetani and managed by the Fondazione Roffredo Caetani. Set of "We only have to weep" and "The story of the stories."

  • Where Via della Fortezza, Sermoneta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Abbazia di Valvisciolo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Dedicated to Saints Peter and Stephen, it is the oldest church in Cistercian Gothic of Marittima and Country, built between 1150 and 1170 after a presence of Basilian monks; tradition binds it to the Templars and wants the architraves to crack in 1314 at the stake of Jacques de Molay. Façade with 12 columns rosette, XII century capitular hall and square cloister with the palindrome

  • Where Via Abbadia 14, Sermoneta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Museo dell'Abbazia di Valvisciolo (Galleria Abate Stanislao White)

Museums · Old masters

Opened in 2003 in the former dispensary of the Cistercian cenobium: prints by Dürer, Rembrandt, Canaletto, Piranesi and Goya, drawings by Luca Giordano and Camuccini, paintings by Pomarancio and the circle of Sassoferrato, with sections of lithography, sculpture and contemporary art. Open every day 9-12.30 and 14.30-16.30.

  • Where Via Badia 14, Sermoneta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Museo della Ceramica di Palazzo Caetani

Museums · Archaeological

In the Palazzo Ada Caetani, built in 1500 by the De Marchis and passed to the Caetani in 1600, with Salone Nobile wooden ceiling coffered and 17th century frescoes. Since 2004 the museum has exhibited about 300 pieces from prehistory to 1900 and the remains of the Caracupa necropolis; free entry every weekend and every day of August.

  • Where Palazzo Caetani, Sermoneta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Museo diocesano d'arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

In the oratory of the Battenti of the Cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta: paintings and sacred furnishings from the sixteenth to the eighteenth century, with works by Girolamo Siciolante, Francesco da Castello, Matteo Rosselli, Giovanni Domenico Fiorentini and Odoardo Vicinelli.

Loggia dei Mercanti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Gothic portico built in the middle of the fifteenth century by Onorato Caetani: it was a municipal seat, business centre and place of public assemblies, with stables under the arches.

  • Where Centro storico, Sermoneta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Centro storico, chiese e belvedere

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village preserves the collegiate church of Santa Maria Assunta, the churches of San Giuseppe (patron), San Michele Arcangelo and dell'Annunziata, the former synagogue and the convent of San Francesco with a holm oak considered of about 500 years; from the viewpoint the view on the Pontina Plain. The signposts guide the visitor to the historic center.

  • Where Centro storico, Sermoneta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · touringclub.it · italia.it

Mura urbane e bastioni

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Renaissance belt commissioned by Onorato III Caetani in 1448 and completed in the first half of the seventeenth century, with the gates of the Well, of the Walnuts, St. Nicholas and Sorda and the bastions of the Torrenuova (at the tail of swallows) and of San Sebastiano with drawbridge. The route of about 1 km between ancient olive groves offers views from the Roman Castles to the Circeo and the Pontine Islands.
