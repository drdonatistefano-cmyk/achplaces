What to see in Centola

Municipality of Cilento coastal to which belongs Palinuro: Capo Palinuro with 32 caves, including the Blue Grotto, the lighthouse, the beaches of Molpa and the Natural Arc, the Antiquarium and, inside, the medieval village abandoned of San Severino on the gorge of Mingardo.

Capo Palinuro e le grotte

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Promontory between the Gulf of Velia and the Gulf of Policastro, with walls peaking up to 50 meters and 32 caves above and below sea level. The Blue Grotto, the largest, has a triangular entrance of about 6 by 10 meters and lights up blue for an underwater siphon, with sulphurous springs, stalactites and stalagmites; then the Blood Grotto with reddish walls, the Monks Grotto, the Silver Grotto and the Fetente Cala with the smell of sulphur. The Bay of Buondormire can only be reached by boat.

  • Where Porto di Palinuro, Centola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.centola.sa.it

Faro di Palinuro

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built in 1970 on the tip of the telegraph of Capo Palinuro, on the bases of an ancient watchtower of which traces remain; about 70 meters high, it is second in Italy for luminous flow, visible up to Sicily. The interior cannot be visited because the military area; you can reach it on foot or by car.

  • Where Capo Palinuro, Centola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.centola.sa.it · italia.it

Spiaggia e sentiero della Molpa

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

White pebble beach and clear water between the Marinella and the Natural Arch, sheltered by the Molpa cliffs, frequented by climbers. A path climbs to the hill with the ruins of a castle, from which you can see the Buondormire, the Marinella and the Scoglio del Rabbit.

  • Where Torre della Molpa, Spiaggia Marinella, Centola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.centola.sa.it · italia.it

Arco Naturale

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Arch of rock near the mouth of Mingardo, on the border with Camerota, which is a passage towards a tongue of fine golden sand on the Tyrrhenian Sea; from the beach you can see the Rabbit's Scoglio.

beach currently not accessible due to risk of falling boulders

  • Where Foce del Mingardo, Centola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.centola.sa.it · italia.it

Antiquarium di Palinuro

Museums · Archaeological

Building of the Sixties on an overhang over the cove of Ficocella, built for the finds found in the territory since 1948: materials of a Greek settlement and a necropolis of the sixth century BC and finds of ancient wrecks. You go down there with a staircase in front of the church, among the figs of India. The MiC card reports closed every day: check before the visit.

  • Where Via Indipendenza, Palinuro, Centola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Borgo di San Severino

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village born between the 10th and 11th centuries on a cliff above the valley of Mingardo, linked to the Sanseverino and abandoned between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries. The castle remains guarding the Devil's Gola, the fifteenth-century baronial palace and the churches of Santa Maria degli Angeli and San Nicola; the Cilento Museum of the Emigrant is located there. Walk up from a small parking lot.

  • Where Via Stazione 1, San Severino, Centola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.centola.sa.it · italia.it

Oasi Panoria e ponte ferroviario

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the banks of the Mingardo in the Devil's Gola, under San Severino, you can bathe at the foot of the railway viaduct made of red bricks of 1928, with eight arches, in service until 1965; the upper viaduct is walkable.

  • Where Gola del Diavolo, San Severino, Centola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.centola.sa.it · italia.it
