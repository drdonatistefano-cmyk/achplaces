What to see in Cavallino

Country of Salento about 5 km south of Lecce, built on an important Messapico settlement today visitable in the Museo Diffuso managed with the University of Salento. In the historic centre there is the Baronial-Marchesal Palace of Castromediano-Limburg, the Assumption Church and the former Dominican convent.

Museo Diffuso di Cavallino

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry · Viewpoint

Landscape Museum inaugurated on December 21, 2003 by the University of Salento and the Municipality, with archaeological remains, rural buildings, dry walls and spontaneous flora. It encloses the archaic Messapian city surrounded by walls about 3100 meters long (often 3,50-4 meters, with moat) on about 69 hectares: village of the Bronze Age, reoccupied in the 8th century BC, fortified in the middle of the 6th and suddenly abandoned within the first decades of the 5th century BC. Three pedestrian and cycle paths (yellow, red, green) and at the entrance a panoramic platform, the Open from Tuesday to Sunday, 9-20 from May to September and 9-15 from October to April; closed Monday; groups and schools by reservation.

  • Where Piazza Fratelli Cervi (ingresso), Cavallino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page interregaismart.regione.puglia.it

Sources: unisalento.it · interregaismart.regione.puglia.it

Palazzo baronale-marchesale Castromediano-Limburg

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

In the old town, home to the Castromediano-Limburg family, recognizable by the bastion of lace. It was the Duke Sigismondo Castromediano, with his discoveries, who started the protection of the Messapian area from which the Museo Diffuso was born.

  • Where Centro storico, Cavallino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa parrocchiale dell'Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Mother church of the historic center, with a bell tower that is the second highest in the area after that of the Cathedral of Lecce.

  • Where Centro storico, Cavallino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page interregaismart.regione.puglia.it

Ex Convento dei Padri Domenicani

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Convent and adjacent church erected between 1625 and 1635 at the behest of the Marquis Francesco Castromediano and his wife Beatrice Acquaviva, who then donated them to the friars. Today it houses the school of excellence ISUFI of the University of Salento and the direction of the Museo Diffuso.

  • Where Corso Umberto I, Cavallino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page interregaismart.regione.puglia.it
