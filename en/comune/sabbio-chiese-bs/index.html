What to see in Sabbio Chiese

The municipality of Valle Sabbia on the river Chiese, halfway between Lake Garda and Lake Idro, dominated by the Fortress: fortress of the IX-X centuries on a rocky spur, from 1527 sanctuary of the Madonna della Rocca with two churches superimposed; on the ascent to the sanctuary, in the former church of San Nicola, there is the Museum of peasant civilization and crafts.

Rocca e Santuario della Madonna della Rocca

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortress built between the IX and the X century on a Dolomite cliff, originally a castle with three walls and shelter for the inhabitants; in 1527, at the beginning of the Venetian rule, it became the sanctuary of the Madonna della Rocca. Today it houses two overlapping churches, both dedicated to the Annunciation and connected by internal stairs, with seventeenth-century porch, bell tower already sighting, the entrance portal with the guard body and numerous underground passages; at the entrance you arrive with 107 steps. Open from Easter to September on Saturdays, Sundays and holidays 15-18; guided tours all year round by reservation (tel. 339 6856430).

  • Where Piazza Rocca 1 (accesso da via Silvio Moretti 49), Sabbio Chiese

Museo della civiltà contadina e dei mestieri

Museums · Themed

Opened in 1992 in the former church of San Nicola, along the ascent to the sanctuary of the Fortress, it is part of the Valsabbia Museum System. Along the aisle, for thematic areas, hundreds of objects of breeding, handicrafts, domestic life and weaving: carpenter's shops, blacksmith, beekeeper, shoemaker, host and tanner, an old kitchen, a bedroom and an ancient frame still working for wool carpets; each object has the name in Italian and dialect. Timetable and rates: tel. 0365 85119.

  • Where Piazza Rocca 11, Sabbio Chiese

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
