What to see in Golasecca

Municipality of the Ticino Park that gives its name to the culture of Golasecca (XII-V century BC): the archaeological area of Monsorino, state since 2001, is the most important monumental testimony in northwestern Italy, with funerary stone circles; finds of the Iron Age are exhibited in the small museum of the town hall.

Area archeologica del Monsorino

History and architecture · Archaeology

State since 2001, it is the most important monumental testimony of the Celtic culture of Golasecca (XII-V century BC) in north-western Italy, identified in the nineteenth century and excavated from the end of the century. Three circular funeral enclosures and two rectangular 8th-VII century BC are visible, delimited by large stones (the cromlech), with tombs grouped in small groups probably familiar; the cinerary urns with ceramic, bronze and iron sets are at the Civic Museum of Sesto Calende and at the Castello Sforzesco in Milan. For the visit the Park of Ticino refers to its authorized naturalistic guides.

the MiC card indicates the closed area every day; the Ticino Park indicates visits with authorized guides (tel. 02 972101)

Museo archeologico e Antiquarium

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Small exhibition space in the town hall with finds from the early Iron Age. The MiC card (updated to 2020) indicates opening from Monday to Thursday 9-12.30 and 14.30-16.30, Friday 9-12, Sunday 15-18.
