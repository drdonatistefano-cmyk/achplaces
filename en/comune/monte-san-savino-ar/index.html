What to see in Monte San Savino

Borgo della Valdichiana aretina who lived his golden season between the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries with the family Di Monte and Pope Julius III: along Corso Sangallo the Palazzo Di Monte di Antonio da Sangallo the Old, the Logge dei Mercanti di Nanni di Baccio Bigio and the Palazzo Pretorio with the civic tower; then the Cassero Senese with the ceramic museum, the church of Santa Chiara with the shovels of Andrea Sansovino and the former synagogue of the ghetto.

Museo del Cassero

Museums · Themed

In the Fortress of Cassero, a fortress built by the Sienese between 1382 and 1384 and attributed to Bartolo di Bartolo, with the Porta Fiorentina. Established in 1989 and reopened with a renovated itinerary from July 1, 2025, documents the country's ceramic tradition: ceramics of daily use of the Seven and Nineteenth Century (broaks, scaldini, pots for pasta) and productions of the local factories Giotto, Lapucci and Aretini; also exhibits a wooden crucifix from the fourteenth century, the Marzocco in serene stone of 1506, sixteenth century sculptures, finds of the excavations of the Fortress and the picture gallery of the Mercy with paintings of the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries.

Palazzo Di Monte

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Commissioned in 1500 by Cardinal Antonio Ciocchi Di Monte to Antonio da Sangallo the Elder, author of a plan, rustic façade in sandstone and courtyard; completed in the mid-500s, under Julius III, by Nanni di Baccio Bigio with the rear facade and the hanging garden all'italiana. Since 1822 it has been the municipality: you can visit the courtyard with loggia on Tuscan pillars and columns, the prisons of 1824 with original furnishings, the hanging garden on two levels with open-air theatre, the portrait gallery and the Council Hall with the sixteenth-century wooden door of Giovanni da Verona.

Logge dei Mercanti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In front of Palazzo Di Monte, they were financed in 1550 by Pope Julius III for his brother Baldovino and completed by Count Fabiano after 1556 with 700 shields; they are attributed to Nanni di Baccio Bigio. Five round arches on Corinthian fluted columns, with the coats of arms of the three mountains in laurel crown in the plumes and barrel porch; used for centuries as a covered market, on the upper floor are now exhibits.

  • Where Corso Sangallo, Monte San Savino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Palazzo Pretorio e Torre civica

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The fourteenth-century palace attributed to the Perugians, who would begin it in 1339; there they administered the justice podestà and Florentine vicars until 1824 and since 1864 it is of the Commune. Two wings enclose the civic tower, about 30 meters high and can be visited up to the top, with clock, battlements and sailing bell tower, from which you can see the historic center and the Valdichiana; in the Hall of Audiences a Pietà frescoed in 1538 by the Savinese Stefano Veltroni.

  • Entry To be checked

Cisternone

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Cisterna pubblica di mezzo Cinquecento ascribed to Nanni di Baccio Bigio, perhaps according to design by Antonio da Sangallo il Vecchio, built for the community (the smallest "Cisternino" served Palazzo Di Monte). Two aisles divided by round arches, crossed by a metal catwalk; it is the second largest cistern in Tuscany after the nineteenth century of Livorno and today its water irrigates the hanging garden of the Town Hall. The visit inside is free.

  • Where Piazza San Francesco, Monte San Savino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Chiesa di Santa Chiara

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built for the Clarisses on the surviving section of the caster's moats and completed in 1661, after the suppression of 1793 passed to the Galletti and then to the Gamurrini, who enriched it with works: two shovels by Andrea Sansovino (San Lorenzo, San Sebastiano and San Rocco; Madonna with Child and saints in glazed terracotta), two Robbian altarpieces with Adoration of Shepherds and Miracle of the Snow, fragments of frescoes from the church of San Rocco di Arezzo and, on the main altar, the Presentation at the Temple of Salvi Castellucci in 1663. Under the altar there is the mysterious San Teofilo, a wooden nativity scene from the eighteenth century and an oratory frescoed in grotesques in the early nineteenth century.

Ex Sinagoga e ghetto

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

The Jewish community, present in the fifteenth century and formally constituted in 1627, was removed in 1799; the building, with parts of 1729, passed to the Israelite University of Siena and in 1924 to the Municipality. Two factory bodies: on the upper floor the synagogue, in the rest probably school and home of the rabbi; inside a painted window, the niche of the aron ha-kodesh and terracotta pipes perhaps of the miqveh.

Palazzo Galletti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Considered the first Renaissance building of the village, linked to the Galletti family (the house on the Ajalta would have been bought by Neri Galletti in 1397), hosted Duke Alessandro de' Medici, Julius III, Bianca Cappello, Pius VII and the future Leo XIII; since 1891 of the Municipality, today it is the seat of the municipal library. Façade with sandstone window supported by telamoni, vaulted hall, living room with beamed ceiling painted with the coats of arms of the Savinese families and an outdoor theater.

  • Where Piazza Gamurrini, Monte San Savino
  • Entry To be checked

Palazzo della Cancelleria

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palazzo cinquecentesco traditionally attributed to the Sansovino and probably the residence of the Di Monte, whose coat of arms it bears; since 1761 the headquarters of the Community Chancellery, today it houses the Municipal Historical Archives with over 4000 pre-unitary units. La Guglia

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

I obelisk in serene stone erected by the community in 1644 in honor of Mattias de' Medici, brother of Grand Duke Ferdinand II, invested in the fief of Monte San Savino; designed by Don Vitale Perini, prior of Vertighe, and carved by the Lucignano Antio and Ippolito Bracciali, is crowned by a sphere with the Florentine lily.
