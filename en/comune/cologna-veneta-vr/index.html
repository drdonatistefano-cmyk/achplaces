What to see in Cologna Veneta

On the hydrographic left of the Adige, Cologna Veneta houses in Piazza Duomo, in the palace of the former Monte di Pietà, the Civic Archaeological Museum born in 1896 after the discovery of the necropolis of Baldaria; here ends the cycle path of Treviso-Ostiglia, which from 2025 continues towards Cerea.

Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

Founded in 1896 after the chance discovery of the necropolis of Baldaria, on the initiative of the municipal secretary Cesare Gardellini, since 1991 it has been located in the late nineteenth century palace of the former Monte di Pietà. Three rooms tell the left Adige from the Neolithic to the Lombard age: the inhabitants of the Bronze Age of Santa Giustina di Baldaria and Sabbionara di Veronella, the necropolis of the Iron Age of Baldaria (IX-VI century BC), amphorae and Roman inscriptions and late antique funerary furnishings, including a mutilate statue of Emperor Magnenzio. Free admission, with Italian, English, German and French cards.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Cologna Veneta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cologna-veneta.vr.it

Sources: archeoveneto.beniculturali.unipd.it · comune.cologna-veneta.vr.it

Ciclovia Treviso-Ostiglia

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Cycle path on the chair of the Treviso-Ostilla railway, built in 1930 for military purposes and abandoned after the Second World War, between countryside, watercourses, country churches and Venetian villas. The historic cycle path ends in Cologna Veneta; the new 23 km stretch towards Pressana, Minerbe, Legnago and Cerea was inaugurated on 4 August 2025.

  • Where Cologna Veneta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cologna-veneta.vr.it

Sources: italia.it · regione.veneto.it · comune.cologna-veneta.vr.it
