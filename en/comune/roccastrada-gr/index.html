What to see in Roccastrada

Medieval village on a cliff of magma rock at 475 meters, in the Park of the Metalliferous Hills with a view of the Amiata, a mining center of silver and copper in the Middle Ages; the territory includes the Aldobrandesco castle of Montemassi, the village of Roccatederighi among the boulders of riolite, the mining park of Ribolla and the reserve of the torrent Farma.

Centro storico di Roccastrada e Porta del Madonnino

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Castello degli Aldobrandeschi mentioned in 1140 (until 1188 Rocca di Fabiano), then Sienese and from 1555 Medici, seat of Podestria under Medici and Lorraine. The center is spread on a terrace of Riolithic rock towards the ancient "carceri" with a stretch of east wall; the only door left, very restored, is the Madonnino Gate, between Vicolo Basso and Piazzetta dell'Incroccia. At the entrance from Piazza Gramsci a trachite sculpture by Renaux François.

  • Where Corso Roma, Roccastrada
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di San Niccolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Cited from 1276, with an inscription of 1283; in the seventeenth century had the Latin cross plan, in 1822 the medieval apse was demolished and in 1953 covered the façade. Inside, frescoes from the middle of the sixteenth century attributed to Giovanni Maria di ser Giovanni Toulouse and a baptismal font from the sixteenth century.

Teatro dei Concordi

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built in 1870 by engineer Timoleone Crocchi, inspired by the Pergola of Florence, and inaugurated on August 6, 1872. In 1925 a cyclone destroyed its roof; after the war it was cinema and dance hall, closed in the seventies and reopened in 1985, after the restoration, with ÂPensaci, Giacomino! by Pirandello.

Castello e borgo di Montemassi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

In the eleventh century of the Aldobrandeschi and stop on the salt routes, it is among the greatest examples of Gothic architecture of the northern Maremma: the mast keeps original doors and windows and the rectangular tower to the south has two large rooms with vaults. The village, built around the citadel, has alleys carved into the rock and an ancient cistern that supplied the whole community.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Borgo di Roccatederighi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Probably the Fortress Norsina already known around 952, takes its name from the sons of Tederigo, vassals of the Aldobrandeschi (1239); it passed to Siena, to the Salimbeni in 1369 and in 1618 was a fief of the Malaspina. The village is camouflaged between large boulders of riolite, 'i bossi', with arched door, alleys, arches and stairs; the remains of the casero near the Clock Tower, the wash-houses of the early twentieth century and, outside the center, the liberty Villa del Paradiso.

  • Where Roccatederighi, Roccastrada
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Chiesa di San Martino a Roccatederighi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On a rocky spur at the southern end of the village, with a single nave with a flat apse and wooden roof; a 1489 plaque on the façade recalls great works, other extensions at the end of the eighteenth century. Inside a baptismal font in marble of the fifteenth century and a sixteenth-century table of Sienese school with the Madonna and Child and Saint Augustine who holds the castle of Roccatederighi.

Parco minerario di Ribolla

Museums · Themed

Gate of the National Park of the Metalliferous Hills on the lignite route: of the mine of Ribolla you visit most of the original complex, with accommodation and dormitories, recreation room, lighthouses and wells. Here in 1954 a gas leak killed 43 miners, one of the most serious mining tragedies of the post-war period: the site is also a place of memory today.

Riserva naturale del Torrente Farma

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Established in 1996 between Roccastrada and Monticiano, on the border between the provinces of Siena and Grosseto: in the valley bottom beech, maples, birch and badgers, on the slopes north chestnuts and cork; live marten, skunk, wild cat and two species of bats. Along the Ferriere path you will find the Ferriera Mill, in the middle of the thirteenth century and well preserved.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

I Muracci

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Remains of the lock on the Bruna river wanted by the Republic of Siena in 1462, designed in 1468 and finished in 1481 for the fish culture, considered the oldest brick closed entirely on a river; a flood destroyed it eleven years later. It is partly located in Roccastrada (123 m on the left bank) and partly in Gavorrano.

  • Where Strada provinciale tra Giuncario e Ribolla, Roccastrada
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Fonti di Roccastrada

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

In the oak, oak and chestnut woods around the village: the Fonte della Lama, in stone, cited in Leopoldini documents and for a long time a public wash-house; The Fonte di via della Fonte, with arch and seventeenth-century tub in trachyte already from the Abbey of Giugnano; the Fonte dell'amore e della passione, in trachyte, made by the artists of the Stone Road.
