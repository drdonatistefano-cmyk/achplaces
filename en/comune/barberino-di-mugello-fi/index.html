What to see in Barberino di Mugello

3 places to visit in Barberino di Mugello, in the province of Florence (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa medicea di Cafaggiolo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage

Ancient castle transformed by Michelozzo from 1443 for Cosimo il Vecchio, loved by Lorenzo il Magnifico.

Closed to the public for restoration works

  • Where Cafaggiolo, Barberino di Mugello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · destinationflorence.com

Lago di Bilancino

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The artificial lake of Mugello, where you can swim and there are restaurants with views at sunset.

  • Where Lago di Bilancino, Barberino di Mugello
  • Entry Free shores; paid bathing services
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · destinationflorence.com

Collezione permanente Giuliano Vangi

Museums · Modern art

In Palazzo Pretorio the only public collection of graphics by the sculptor born in Barberino: 79 engravings from 1964-2006 and the polychrome plaster "Girl with wool dress" with an interactive table.

  • Where Piazza Cavour 36, Barberino di Mugello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com
