What to see in Travagliato

Borgo della piania Brescia of Roman origins, known for the Horse Fair at the end of April-early May; in the center the Piazza della Libertà redesigned by Mario Botta with the bell tower, the Baroque Cathedral, the nineteenth-century buildings of Rodolfo Vantini and the church of Santa Maria dei Campi with frescoes by Vincenzo Civerchio.

Piazza della Libertà e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The square, once surrounded by gardens, was redesigned by Swiss architect Mario Botta; in the center stands the bell tower, 36 meters high. Around, the historic centre offers palaces, hidden courtyards between alleys and squares, decorated facades and stone portals, the Cathedral of Saints Peter and Paul, example of Baroque architecture with works by local artists, and the former Hospital designed by Rodolfo Vantini in the nineteenth century, today the municipal library.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di Santa Maria dei Campi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church with frescoes by Vincenzo Civerchio da Crema; the regional portal describes it in two pages with different dates: one dates back to the 14th century, the other says it was built in the 16th century, with remarkable 16th century frescoes.
