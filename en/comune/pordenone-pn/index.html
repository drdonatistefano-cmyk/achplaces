What to see in Pordenone

The "Urbs Picta" on the Noncello: the Vittorio Emanuele course with frescoed facades closed by the municipal loggia, Palazzo Ricchieri and the Cathedral of San Marco with the works of the Pordenone, the civic museums and a city of parks, from Valentine's Day to the rose garden of Galvani.

Loggia comunale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The loggias of the palace of the Municipality close the perspective of the course and symbolize the autonomy of the city maintained also under Venice; for centuries seat of assemblies and proclamations, with the walls adorned with coats of arms and bas-reliefs.

  • Where Corso Vittorio Emanuele II, Pordenone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismofvg.it

Museo civico d'arte - Palazzo Ricchieri

Museums · Old masters

Born in the thirteenth century as a fortified tower of the Ricchieri and transformed into a residence between the Three and the sixteenth century, donated to the city in 1949: wooden ceilings, fifteenth-century frescoes with the history of Tristan and Isotta and works from the thirteenth to the nineteenth century with the center of Friulian art and Pordenone; the starting nucleus were the paintings left by Michelangelo Grigoletti in 1870.

  • Where Corso Vittorio Emanuele II 51, Pordenone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Duomo concattedrale di San Marco

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built from the 13th century and never finished, slightly raised on Piazza San Marco, with brick facade and fifteenth-century bell tower; inside the Pordenone altarpiece, the frescoes of the Montereale Mantica chapel of Calderari and Amalteo and sculptures of Giovanni Antonio Pilacorte.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
