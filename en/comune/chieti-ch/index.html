What to see in Chieti

The ancient Teate Marrucinorum on a hill between the Maiella and the sea: the Warrior of Capestrano at the Museum of Villa Frigerj, the Civitella, the theatre, the thermal baths and the Roman temples, the Cathedral of San Giustino and the Marrucino Theatre.

Museo archeologico nazionale d'Abruzzo – Villa Frigerj

Museums · Archaeological

In the villa in the center of the homonymous park, it houses the Warrior of Capestrano, symbol of Abruzzo, and the Stele of Guardiagrele.

  • Where Via Costanzi 2, Chieti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it

Museo archeologico nazionale La Civitella

Museums · Archaeological

On the summit of the height that dominates the city, once a place of worship and acropolis of Teate, leveled in the mid-nineteenth century as a piazza d'armi and then sports field; between the sections recorded by the MiC the goddess of Rapino and the tomb of Lusius Storax.

  • Where Via Generale Pianell, Chieti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismo.abruzzo.it · comune.chieti.it
