What to see in Goni

Gerrei's country known for the archaeological park of Pranu Muteddu, the Sardinian stonehenge with about sixty menhirs among the corkshops; on the plateau above the village the monotorre nuraghe of Goni looks at Lake Mulargia.

Parco archeologico di Pranu Mutteddu

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

About 200,000 m2 of cork and Mediterranean maquis with the necropolis of Pranu Muteddu and Nuraxeddu, a group of huts, the domus de Janas of Genna Accas with three tombalous circles and the dolmen of Baccoi. The tombs date back to the final Neolithic (culture of Ozieri, 3200-2800 BC) with frequentations up to 2600 BC; there is the highest concentration of menhirs in Sardinia, about sixty, with an alignment of winds perdas fittas oriented according to the course of the sun. The Tomb II has entrance, anticell and cell carved into two blocks of rock. Reception area with multimedia equipment and guided tours; Sardinia Tourism indicates it among the 17 prehistoric Sardinian sites recognized by UNESCO. Recommended the visit in the early morning.

  • Where SP 23, Goni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website pranumuttedu.com

Nuraghe Goni

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

One of Sardinia's best preserved single tower nuraghi, the Bronze Age: blocks of limestone in regular rows, about 10 m in diameter and over 8 m in height, with two construction phases for a restoration of the Nuragic age. Inside the tholos vault is integrated, three cross niches, a triangular window and a staircase interrupted after five steps. It is reached from a dirt road towards the dam of the Mulargia and then on foot on a steep slope (about 15 minutes).

  • Where Altopiano sopra l'abitato, strada per la diga del Mulargia, Goni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Sito geologico di Peinconi

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

About 500 metres from the village, the site preserves the graptolites, rare fossils of the Paleozoic.

  • Where Località Peinconi, Goni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
