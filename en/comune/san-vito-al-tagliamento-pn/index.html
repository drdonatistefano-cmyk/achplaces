What to see in San Vito al Tagliamento

Medieval town on the right of the Tagliamento which preserves the moat and three access towers; around the porch Piazza del Popolo overlook the Palazzo Altan-Rota, the Antico Teatro Sociale in the old loggia, the eighteenth-century Cathedral and the church of Santa Maria dei Battuti frescoed by Pomponio Amalteo, while the Palazzo Tullio Altan houses the Museum of Rural Life "Diogene Penzi" .

Piazza del Popolo e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The medieval heart of the city, a square with porticoed sides and beautiful palaces reached by Via Amalteo; the village rises on the right of the Tagliamento and preserves the moat and the entrance doors.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Mura, fossato e torri

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The ancient belt with the medieval moat and the Raimonda, Scaramuccia and Grimana towers remains.The Grimana Savorgnana Tower, along Via Altan, is one of the three gateways to the city.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Palazzo Altan-Rota e il suo giardino

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Venetian-style palace among the oldest buildings in the city, now home to the Town Hall; behind you lie an Italian garden inside the walls of the historic center and a large park with exceptional tree monuments.

Antico Teatro Sociale

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Small Italian theatre hosted since the eighteenth century in the Old Public Loggia, the first seat of the City, where shows and events are held.

Duomo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built in the middle of the eighteenth century on the foundations of a fifteenth-century cathedral by the will of the last Patriarch of Aquileia, Daniele Delfino; it houses works of different eras and next stands the 73-metre-high bell tower.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, San Vito al Tagliamento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it · italia.it · touringclub.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria dei Battuti

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Next to the Duomo, linked to the hospital of the Battuti founded in 1360, was rebuilt in 1493 with the portal of Pilacorte; the unique hall is decorated with fake marble squares and the apse and dome host the cycle of frescoes by Pomponio Amalteo, with Melchizedek offering bread and wine. Since 1933 it has been home to the shrine of the fallen of the First World War; the old annex house houses exhibitions and conferences.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, San Vito al Tagliamento
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it · italia.it

Castello e Museo civico «Federico De Rocco»

Museums · City history

The castle is the nucleus from which the historic center has developed, decorated with frescoes inside and outside; it houses the Civic Museum "Federico De Rocco" with a vast collection of artifacts and works of various eras.

  • Where San Vito al Tagliamento
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Museo provinciale della vita contadina «Diogene Penzi»

Museums · Themed

In one of the boats of Palazzo Tullio Altan, one of the most illustrious in the territory, tells on several floors the rural life: the kitchen with the fogolâr, the room with the mattresses of corn leaves, the bachiculture with many types of cocoons, spinning and weaving. The complex includes an Italian garden and, beyond the wall in the Chamber, a restored romantic park with a collection of vintage roses.

  • Where Palazzo Tullio Altan, via Altan, San Vito al Tagliamento
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it · italia.it
