What to see in Spoleto

The capital of the Longobard Duchy on the Umbra Valley: the Basilica of San Salvatore, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Duomo with frescoes by Filippo Lippi, the Albornoziana Rock and the Tower Bridge; since 1958 the city of the Festival of the Two Worlds.

19 places to visit in Spoleto, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di Spoleto – Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

Romanesque cathedral with mosaic in façade, rebuilt at the end of the 13th century: in the apse the Stories of the Virgin frescoed by Filippo Lippi between 1467 and 1469, inside the bronze bust of Urban VIII of Bernini and the painted Cross of Alberto Sotio of the 12th century.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Spoleto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · regione.umbria.it · italia.it

Ponte delle Torri

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Bridge-aqueduct about 80 meters high and about 230 long, on 9 pylons joined by ogival arches in local limestone; the current aspect is of the XIII-XIV century and connects the city to the wood of Monteluco.

  • Where Spoleto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Rocca Albornoziana e Museo Nazionale del Ducato

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Fortress built from 1360 on the hill Sant'Elia for Cardinal Egidio Albornoz on a project by Gattapone, seat of the papal governors and prison until 1982; since 2007 it houses in fifteen halls the National Museum of the Duchy, with materials from the fourth to the fifteenth century.

  • Where Spoleto
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: regione.umbria.it · italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Basilica di San Salvatore

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Basilica outside the walls, beyond the Tessino stream, of the VII-VIII centuries: rare example of Lombard architecture with Byzantine and classical influences, part of the UNESCO site of the Longobards in Italy.

  • Where Spoleto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it
