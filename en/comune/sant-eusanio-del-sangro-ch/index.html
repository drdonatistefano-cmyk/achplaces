What to see in Sant'Eusanio del Sangro

Borgo della valle del Sangro, where the poet Cesare De Titta (1862-1933) was born: his liberty house, the "Cenacolo di Fiorinvalle" is now a museum house; in the historic center the buildings Auriti and Finamore, and on the edge of the territory the WWF Oasis of Lake Serranella.

Casa museo Cesare De Titta (Palazzo de Titta)

Museums · House museums

Liberty villa of the early twentieth century in the historic center, birthplace of the poet and literary Cesare De Titta (1862-1933), who called it "Cenacle of Fiorinvalle": there were artists and writers of Abruzzo of the time like D'Annunzio and Michetti, and also Pirandello and Gentile. It preserves the original furnishings, objects of the poet, postcards and vintage photos, a bust, the library and the historical archive; it is among the Italian museum houses recorded by the Ministry.

Centro storico, palazzi Auriti e Finamore

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Walk in the center of the village between the neoclassical Palazzo Auriti, built with pebbles and sandstone, and the nineteenth century Palazzo Finamore in Piazza Monteclo, with Italian garden and back garden. At the end of the path the brick wall fountain with two beccucci, from the late nineteenth century, celebrated in the poems of De Titta.

  • Where Piazza Monteclo, Sant'Eusanio del Sangro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.abruzzoturismo.it

Sources: abruzzoturismo.it

Oasi WWF di Serranella

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Artificial lake born at the confluence of the rivers Sangro and Aventino, important rest area for migratory birds; the oasis is managed together with the municipalities of Altino and Casoli and can be reached from the district Brecciaio towards Casoli, near the district Castellata-Tori.
