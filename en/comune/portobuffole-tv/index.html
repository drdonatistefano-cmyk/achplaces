What to see in Portobuffolè

Small village walled on the Livenza, dominated by the municipal tower and closed by the doors Friuli and Trevisana; around the square overlooking Dogana, Monte di Pietà and Loggia of Venetian age, the Duomo born from a synagogue and the house of Gaia da Camino, cited by Dante, with frescoes fourteenth century.

Piazza e palazzi veneziani

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The square is surrounded by the public buildings of the Venetian era: the Dogana of the XIV century, the Monte di Pietà founded in the sixteenth century with a rare moeca "Leon" above the door (now seat of the IAT office), the municipal loggia of the XVI century, remaking of the Gothic foundation, and the Town Hall with inscriptions and sixteenth-century coats of arms of the Podestà; near there is the small Piazza Beccaro with frescoed facades.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Portobuffolè
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Casa Gaia da Camino

Museums · House museums

A fourteenth-century house-tower with trilobed mullioned windows where he lived until his death in 1311, Gaia da Camino, quoted by Dante in the 16th canto of Purgatory, lady of the village from 1307 with her husband Tolberto. The frescoes by unknown author (XIV-XV century) depict court life, warriors in armor, personifications of the Sciences and landscapes; today it is the museum and art gallery.

  • Where Via Businello, Portobuffolè
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Duomo (ex sinagoga)

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Upon the site of a synagogue after the expulsion of the Jewish community, it was consecrated in 1559; during the restorations of the nearby house of the archisinagogus a stone with the seven-armed candlepiece emerged. It preserves a fifteenth-century wooden crucifix of German school and a Callido organ with 472 reeds.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Portobuffolè
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.portobuffole.tv.it · veneto.eu · italia.it

Torre comunale

History and architecture · Towers

28-metre-high brick tower, the only surviving tower in the castle, with the Government Palace at the base; from an opening in the clock dial the prisoners were lowered into the cell below. It houses the Museum of Rural Civilization.

  • Entry To be checked

Porta Friuli e Ponte Friuli

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The door of 1513 leads the Lion of St Mark on the outer arch and a Napoleonic inscription on the rights and duties of man and citizen; the stone bridge of 1780, with two large arches with six hillsides, replaced a wooden drawbridge.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa dei Servi (Santa Maria dei Servi)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Wanted by the legacy of Benedetto Rizzo of 1497 and consecrated in 1505, it was officiated for about two centuries by the Servants of Mary; reduced between 1786 and 1792 by Count Orio, it preserves the golden wooden main altar with the altarpiece of Paolo Ongaretto, a wooden statue of St. Prosdocimo, a Crucifixion of 1828 and the tomb of the family Orio in sacristy.

  • Where Via Provinciale 1, Portobuffolè
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.portobuffole.tv.it · italia.it

Museums · Themed

Barcaroli e Chiesa di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In addition to Porta Friuli, a tree-lined avenue leads to the hamlet of Barcaroli, a quarantine place for goods and merchants with the remains of the hospital of the 15th century Battuti; the church of San Rocco, invoked against the plague, served residents, travelers and boatmen in quarantine and guards the Madonna della Seggiola.
