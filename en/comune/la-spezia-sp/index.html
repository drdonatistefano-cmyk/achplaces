What to see in La Spezia

In the middle of the Gulf of Poets, the city chosen by Cavour for the Arsenale della Marina: the Castle of San Giorgio with the stele statues, the Via del Prione and its museums, the Morin promenade on the sea and the rationalist cathedral of Adalberto Libera, with the Sunsets and Monesteroli on the hills towards the Cinque Terre.

Castello San Giorgio – Museo Archeologico Ubaldo Formentini

Museums · Archaeological · Viewpoint

Fortress of the thirteenth century, rebuilt in 1371 and enlarged in the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, on the height of Poggio overlooking the center; it houses the museum with the 19 stele statues of the Lunigiana of the Bronze and Iron Age and the finds of the ancient Luni. Walk up or take the urban elevator.

  • Where Via XXVII Marzo, La Spezia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Civico Amedeo Lia

Museums · Old masters

In a convent of the seventeenth century, the collection donated by Amedeo Lia: liturgical objects, medieval miniatures, archaeological finds and paintings from the fourteenth to the eighteenth century, including the Portrait of Pontormo and the Portrait of a gentleman of Tiziano.

  • Where Via del Prione 234, La Spezia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Tecnico Navale della Marina Militare

Museums · Themed

At the entrance of the Arsenale: models of ships, nautical instruments and memorabilia of sunken or disused units, with the room dedicated to Guglielmo Marconi and the Polene room.

  • Where Viale Amendola 1, La Spezia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · marina.difesa.it

Via del Prione

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The "Drito" carugio degli spezzini, the main pedestrian street of the city, parallel to Corso Cavour, with shops and museums: the Ethnographic and Diocesan, the Lia Museum and the Museum of Seal.

  • Where Via del Prione, La Spezia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo del Sigillo – Palazzina delle Arti

Museums · Themed

In the neo-Gothic building of the 1920s, connected to the Lia Museum by a steel and glass gallery: about 1,500 seals from ancient Egypt, Rome, Pre-Columbian America and the Far East, from the fourth millennium BC to today.

  • Where Via del Prione, La Spezia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Etnografico Giovanni Podenzana e Museo Diocesano

Museums · Themed

In the former fifteenth-century oratory of San Bernardino: folk traditions, agricultural work and domestic life of the Spezia and the Cinque Terre from the eighteenth to the twentieth century, and masterpieces of the Ligurian Baroque in the Diocesan Museum.

  • Where Via del Prione 156, La Spezia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

CAMeC – Centro d'Arte Moderna e Contemporanea

Museums · Modern art

Opened in October 2024 with the permanent collection reordered: over 200 works, from the Gulf Prize founded in 1933 on the idea of Marinetti to donations Cozzani, with works by Lucio Fontana and Marina Abramović. The 1879 building was a female school.

  • Where Piazza Cesare Battisti 1, La Spezia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · camec.museilaspezia.it

Cattedrale di Cristo Re

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Designed by Adalberto Libera, started in 1956 and consecrated in 1975: a white circular plant, inside twelve columns, one per apostle, and an eighteenth-century wooden crucifix.

  • Where Piazzale Papa Giovanni XXIII, La Spezia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Monesteroli e la Scala Grande

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Borgo dei Tramonti, in the municipality of La Spezia, reachable only on foot along a staircase of more than 1,200 steps; in its cellars the grapes of the vineyards were vinified around. Place of the FAI Heart, with the staircase restored in 2022.
