What to see in Villasimius

Seaside resort at the south-eastern end of Sardinia, with about twenty beaches in the protected marine area of Capo Carbonara, between Porto Giunco and the pond of Notteri with flamingos, Simius, Campulongu and Punta Molentis, the islands of Cavoli and Serpentara, the Spanish Old Fortress and the archaeological museum of submerged treasures.

Fortezza Vecchia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On a promontory overlooking the sea to the west of Capo Carbonara, about 3 km south of the village: to the first polygonal nucleus, probably Aragonese, the Spanish added a complex with four towers, three of which pointed, with walls with two entrances and internal courtyard, in defence of the barbaric incursions of the port of Capo Carbonara and the mouth of the river; restored at the end of the eighteenth century, was abandoned in 1847. Outside, the permanent exhibition "Enemigos de la fe," pirates and coastal defenders, on the assaults on the Sardinian coast between the sixteenth and nineteenth centuries; from the wounds you can see the towers of Capo Boi and Porto Giunco and the lighthouse of Capo Carbonara.

Open only from June to October (Tuesday-Sunday 9-13 and 16-19), closed from November to May; the source advises you to contact the site before visiting

  • Where Località Torre Vecchia, Villasimius
  • Entry €3, reduced €1,50; less €1; free up to 6 years; cumulative with archaeological museum and Casa Todde €8
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Museo archeologico di Villasimius

Museums · Archaeological

In a rural house in the courtyard of the nineteenth century on two floors and four rooms, with finds from the Nuragic Age to the Spanish domination, many from the backdrops: punic amphorae of the fifth century BC recovered in front of the island of the Cavoli, ex vow and objects of the Phoenician-punic sanctuary of Cuccureddus, Roman materials from the necropolis of Cruccuris and Accu is Traias, and in the last room cannons and azulejos of the Spanish wreck sunk in the fifteenth century near the island of the Cavoli. Open Tuesday-Sunday 9-13 and 16-18.

  • Where Via A. Frau 5, Villasimius
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Area marina protetta di Capo Carbonara

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 86 km2 of sea between Capo Boi and the island of Serpentara, divided into two bays from the cape of Capo Carbonara: granite pinnacles with sea daisies and red gorgonies, posidonia prairies and the pinna nobilis, barracuda to the Variglioni, groupers and breams to the dry of Cala Caterina and wrecks including a Roman jail, a 15th century Aragonese sailboat and the steamboat Egle (1943). Forbidden fishing, year-round diving with authorized diving; a path in the spot connects the head to Porto Giunco.

  • Where Capo Carbonara, Villasimius
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Spiaggia di Porto Giunco e torre

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

It is called "the two seas" and is a soft sandy tongue with pink reflections due to the granite fragments between the sea and the pond of Notteri, with low seabed and dunes of junipers and lentisks; on the southwestern promontory the Spanish tower of the seventeenth century, about 9 meters high, reachable on foot. Large parking lot from which a path among the eucalyptus runs along the pond; establishments, rental and kiosks.

  • Where Località Porto Giunco, Villasimius
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · ampcapocarbonara.it · italia.it

Stagno di Notteri

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the narrowest part of the peninsula of Capo Carbonara, separated from the sea from the beach of Notteri or Giunco, the pond is about half a metre deep and without connections to the sea hosts pink flamingos, which on the southeast coast are located only here and in the ponds of Colostrai and Feraxi; it is admired from the top of the cliff that delimits it.

  • Where Località Notteri, Villasimius
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Spiaggia di Campulongu

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

At the base of Capo Carbonara, on the western side, an arc of about one kilometer of white sand and soft between granite cliffs and coves of pebbles, with dunes, pine trees and junipers behind; bottom for tens of meters, suitable for families. Parking, bar, restaurant, rental and diving center; accessible to people with disabilities.

Spiaggia di Punta Molentis

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Small beach at the crescent at the mouth of the river Trottu, sheltered from the winds, closed by a promontory that degrades to the cliffs and from rocks and Mediterranean maquis; there is a abandoned granite quarry and the name comes from "su molentu" (the donkey carrying the extracted material).

Spiaggia di Is Traias e necropoli

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches

A half-moon of white and very fine sand about 150 meters next to Simius, protected by the mistral by two promontors and in front of the island of Serpentara, with cliffs for snorkeling; not far from the Roman necropolis of Accu is Traias (1st century BC-III century AD), about thirty burials in the pit, urns and amphorae.

Isola di Serpentara

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Uninhabited granite island of about 134 hectares about 4 km from the coast, a site of community interest where they nest herons, seagulls courses and marangoni from the tuft; on the highest point, about 50 meters, the Spanish tower of San Luigi, to the north the large cliffs of Variglioni with natural pools. You can reach it by boat or by excursions from the port of Villasimius, landing in Caletta d'Approdo.

  • Where Isola di Serpentara, Villasimius
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Isola dei Cavoli

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches
