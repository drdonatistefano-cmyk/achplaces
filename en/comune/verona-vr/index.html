What to see in Verona

The city of Juliet on the handle of the Adige, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2000 for Roman monuments merged with the art of Scaliger and Renaissance: the Arena, the squares, Castelvecchio and San Zeno.

38 places to visit in Verona, in the province of Verona (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Arena di Verona

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Roman amphitheatre built under Claudio around 41-42 AD, decades before the Colosseum, in stone of Valpolicella: axis of 152 and 123 meters, up to 30,000 spectators; since 1913, with the Aida of Verdi, it is the largest opera house in the open air in the world.

  • Where Piazza Bra, Verona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it

Museo di Castelvecchio

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage

The Scaligera fortress built between 1354 and 1356 on the Adige, civic museum since 1926 with the setting of Carlo Scarpa: sculptures of the fourteenth century, scaligere goldsmiths and works of Pisanello, Bellini, Mantegna and Veronese.
