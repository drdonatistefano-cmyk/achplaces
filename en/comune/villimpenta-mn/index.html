What to see in Villimpenta

The village of the risotto is on the right of Tione on the border with Veronese, with origins in the Bronze Age: the Castello Scaligero restored and opened to the public, the sixteenth-century Villa Gonzaga-Zani referring to the students of Giulio Romano and the Museo Francioli Nuvolari on the Rising Mantua and life in rice.

4 places to visit in Villimpenta, in the province of Mantua (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello scaligero

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

It was mentioned already in the 11th century, and was guarded by a strategic ford on Tione. The castle-recinct with an original irregular pentagonal plan, contains two polygonal towers, a corner tower with Guelph battlements and an imposing mast; completely restored, it is open to the public.

Villa Gonzaga-Zani

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa of the sixteenth century with rectangular plan, for a Gonzaghesque period, in a scenic setting from the avenue of entrance to the park-side grove. For a long time attributed to Giulio Romano, it is today referred to his students Bernardino Facciotto and Pompeo Pedemonte.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Museo Francioli Nuvolari

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Opened in 1993 in the seventeenth-century house Francioli to exhibit documents, reordered in the sixties by Ugo Francioli, on Giovanni Nuvolari and Carlo Francioli, figures of the Risorgimento mantua; he also exhibits tools and objects of rural life and a section on life and work in rice. Open on Sunday 9-12; guided tours only by reservation.

  • Where Piazza Roma 65, Villimpenta
  • Entry €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it

Chiesa parrocchiale e Oratorio del Santissimo Sacramento

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the center of Villimpenta the parish church, built in 1675, and the Oratory of the Blessed Sacrament, of 1777.
