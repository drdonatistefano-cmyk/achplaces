What to see in Triggiano

Centre of the Conca of Bari, about 10 km from the capital, with the church of Santa Maria Veterana, its most important monumental testimony, the Baroque Santa Maria della Croce and the Convent of the Capuchins of 1616; the territory is crossed by the Lama San Giorgio.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Veterana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Founded perhaps around 1080 next to the castrum moat, it was rebuilt in the middle of the sixteenth century as a three-nave basilica on the medieval complex, whose remains are re-emerged in the excavations of the 1982 floor together with an underground chapel under the apse. Restored after the storm of 1681 and decorated in 1746 by Nicolò De Filippis, pupil of Paolo De Matteis, with a cycle of biblical and hagiographic canvases; in 1907-1913 the sixteenth-century façade was largely lost (it remains the great rose window) and the interiors had Liberty decorations. Open every day 7-12 and 16-20.

  • Where Via Carroccio 70, Triggiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Lama San Giorgio

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest blade in the province of Bari, erosive incision in the mesozoic limestones that crosses the territory with karst caves and different vegetable environments, partly modified by agriculture. Along the course remains of settlements from the Neolithic to the classical age and nineteenth-century villas on the margins; in Triggiano the archaeological area of Monte Telegrafo. There is no well-equipped route: the walk through fragno and roverella woods to the sea is just a project.
