What to see in Atena Lucana

One of the oldest settlements of the Diano Valley, on a promontory overlooking the valley: ancient center with double acropolis dominated by the castle, the Sanctuary of San Ciro on the temple of Cibele, the Archaeological Museum Elena D'Alto and, outside the village, the Sanctuary of the Colomba and the megalithic walls of the Serrone.

Centro antico e acropoli

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Elliptical plant with double acropolis with Roman and pre-Roman walls: the Romans built a hole and temples in Cibele and Esculapio, in the Middle Ages there were added watchtowers and the castle on the highest point, from the sixteenth century palaces and churches with large portals. Today it is a maze of alleys, stairs, arches and small stone buildings, with an epigraphic route of Roman inscriptions scattered among palaces and streets designed in 2000.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.atenalucana.sa.it

Museo Archeologico «Elena D'Alto»

Museums · Archaeological

Set up in 2005 in the nineteenth-century premises of the former town hall and named after the archaeologist who studied the territory in the seventies and eighties, he exhibits funerary furnishings from the necropolis (on about 250 excavated tombs) from the VII to the fourth century BC, with indigenous geometric ceramics, red figured Lucan vases and a bronze helmet from the beginning of the fifth century BC. The Roman section includes loricated and togata statues and a female bust of the first century BC, stele and a funerary cippus with the Lucanian wolf.

can be visited only by reservation through the Municipality (tel. 0975 76001, cooparchiatena@gmail.com); visit by about 40 minutes

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Documented from 967 and collegiate since 1541, it rises on the remains of the Roman forum; rebuilt from 1741 in Baroque forms and decorated with stucco after 1857, it is called the church of the cherubs for its 108 angel heads. He preserves a cycle on the life of Our Lady and paintings by Nicola Peccheneda di Polla, including eight apostles of 1751.

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo – Santuario di San Ciro

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

One of the oldest churches in the country, built on the site of a Roman temple dedicated to Cibele (Magna Mater); it has a late Baroque stone portal from 1739 and has been a sanctuary since 2003. Inside the wooden statue of San Ciro by Michele Abruzzi (1863) and, in the sacristy, a fresco of Ecce Homo.

Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Perhaps Lombard fortification enlarged by the Normans, on the highest point of the ancient center; the tower was built by the Sanseverino after 1399, while the Caracciolos dismantled a large part of it in 1620, reusing the material for their palace.

Monumento di Atteone

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Symbol of the city at the entrance of the acropolis, in front of Palazzo Spagna: a deer and the figure of Atteone placed after 1866 on a Roman funerary stele of the first half of the first century BC; not far, in Largo Garibaldi, a nineteenth-century slab with the deer rests on a Roman funerary shrine surmounted by a tuft.

Palazzo Spagna e Palazzo De Benedictis

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Palazzo Spagna has a neoclassical portal of 1807 by Francesco Bitetti di Padula, stone staircase of Padula and chapel of the Addolorata; Palazzo De Benedictis, from the seventeenth century, is placed on a medieval tower and leads to the southern façade the oldest honorary epigraph in the country, dedicated to Marco Tresio Fausto. You can see them from the outside.

Santuario di Santa Maria della Colomba

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Outside the centre, surrounded by greenery and overlooking the Vallone dell'Arenaccia, it preserves a cycle of Marian frescoes from 1713 by Anselmo Palmieri di Polla and an altar by Andrea Carrara di Padula; the festival is July 2. It is the starting point of a walk in nature.

Mura ciclopiche del Serrone

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the district of Serrone remains a megalithic wall with a thickness of more than three meters, testimony of the antiquity of the settlement: dated from the national portal italia.it to the fourth century BC.

Grotte dei Saraceni e Vallone dell'Arenaccia

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural cavities of the Vallone dell'Arenaccia inhabited in prehistoric and protohistoric age, explored in the nineteenth century by Patrons and Lacava; the name comes from a legend. At the foot of the hill, in Via Sotto gli Orti, there is a cliff equipped for climbing.
