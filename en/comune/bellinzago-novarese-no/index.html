What to see in Bellinzago Novarese

The town of Novarese on the edge of the valley of the Terdoppio: the hamlet of the Badia di Dulzago, 12th century abbey of the Augustinian canons became an agricultural village with the Romanesque church of San Giulio, the works of Alessandro Antonelli in the center, the church of San Vito a Cavagliano and the portico sanctuary of the Madonna della Neve.

5 places to visit in Bellinzago Novarese, in the province of Novara (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Badia di Dulzago

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Founded in the 12th century by Augustinian canons on morainic hills near the resurgences of the Terdomio, in the Middle Ages it was a great agricultural center: from the sixteenth century the cloister became home to peasant families and in the eighteenth century the houses of the wage earners arose, like the Court of the Pigionanti; the community had oven, school, underground glaciers, cheese factory and mill. Today there are few families, with a trattoria and a restaurant; part of the buildings of the XV-XVI century and the cloister are degraded. Free entrance and always open; at the end of January the traditional berry.

  • Where Località Dulzago, Strada Provinciale 102, Bellinzago Novarese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismonovara.it

Sources: comune.bellinzago.no.it · turismonovara.it

Chiesa di San Giulio alla Badia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in the 12th century and consecrated by the bishop of Novara Litifredo, three-nave brick with three semicircular apses with blind arches; it preserves the most ancient frescoes (1132-1151) with angels and apostles on the tibury and saints of the sixteenth century, while chapels, stuccos and Baroque frescoes (Francesco Belloto, workshop of Carlo Antonio Morino) date back to the late seventeenth-seventh century; facade of the second half of the eighteenth century with bezel of Clemente Salsa, bell tower of 1756. Open on Saturday afternoons and Sundays (parish house 0321 985469).

  • Where Frazione Badia di Dulzago, Bellinzago Novarese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismonovara.it

Sources: comune.bellinzago.no.it · turismonovara.it

Chiesa di San Clemente e opere di Antonelli

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Alessandro Antonelli worked for Bellinzago from the mid-19th century to 1888: he expanded the parish church of San Clemente with vaults made without scaffolding, designed the Asilo De Medici with a staircase with a canopy in slabs of beola, the parish house and the square with the arcades; the bell tower, similar to the Mole di Torino, remain only unfinished pillars. The church is always visitable, the kindergarten only with guided tours.

  • Where Piazza Monsignor Raspini 14, Bellinzago Novarese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismonovara.it

Sources: comune.bellinzago.no.it · turismonovara.it

Chiesa di San Vito a Cavagliano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the cemetery of Cavagliano, a single nave with semicircular apse decorated outside with terracotta tiles; inside frescoes of the Gaudenzian school of the second half of the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, with the Virgin among the Apostles and a copy of that of Gaudenzio Ferrari in Varallo. Not indicated hours: information to the parish (0321 98027).

  • Where Strada del Molino di Cavagliano 5, Cavagliano, Bellinzago Novarese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismonovara.it

Sources: comune.bellinzago.no.it · turismonovara.it

Chiesa della Madonna della Neve

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Built in the first half of the eighteenth century in a place of Marian devotion around a venerated fresco of the Madonna with Child and Saints, today above the marble altar; brick, with an elegant portico on three sides under which runs an eighteenth-century Way of the Cross. The Municipality points out that it is very degraded and open only on holidays.

  • Where Via Bornago 15D, Bellinzago Novarese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismonovara.it

Sources: comune.bellinzago.no.it · turismonovara.it
