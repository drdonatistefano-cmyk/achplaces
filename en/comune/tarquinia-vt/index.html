What to see in Tarquinia

One of the most important Etruscan centers: the necropolis of Monterozzi with painted tombs, UNESCO heritage since 2004, the Alati Cavalli of Palazzo Vitelleschi and the medieval town of the towers, between the salt pans and the sea.

10 places to visit in Tarquinia, in the province of Viterbo (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 3 of the 5 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Necropoli dei Monterozzi

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

A UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2004, with Etruscan tombs painted from the 6th to the 2nd century B.C.; in the Calvary area there are twenty-two hypogeas.

  • Where Necropoli dei Monterozzi, Tarquinia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Museo Archeologico Nazionale – Palazzo Vitelleschi

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 1924 in the fifteenth-century Palazzo Vitelleschi, it has as masterpiece the statue of the Alati Horses.

  • Where Palazzo Vitelleschi, Tarquinia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · cultura.gov.it

Centro storico e torri di Tarquinia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Enclosed by medieval walls, with numerous towers and palaces such as the Palazzo dei Priori and the Palazzo Comunale with the Sala degli Frescoes.

  • Where Centro storico, Tarquinia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Chiesa del Salvatore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Small Romanesque church of the twelfth century on the cliffs of the city, with apse of Lombardy and Pisan taste and remains of frescoes from the end of the fourteenth century.

  • Where Via San Giacomo 57, Tarquinia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Palazzo Vipereschi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
