What to see in Morigerati

Medieval village of Cilento, the village of the hotel is born from a settlement of Basilian monks, with the Ethnographic Museum and the WWF Oasis of the Grotte del Bussento, where the river re-emerges from the rock.

Oasi WWF Grotte del Bussento

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Canyon covered with vegetation of about 600 hectares in the Cilento National Park: from the village a path along the stream, between springs, waterfalls and an ancient stone mill, descends to the cave where the Bussento re-emerges, with a portal about 20 meters high, a staircase dug in limestone and wooden walkways; it is geosite of the park. Visits by reservation, with seasonal times (last admission two hours before closing time).

visits only by reservation, closed on rainy days

Museo etnografico

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Born in 1976 as an exhibition of the Florenzano foundation and municipal museum since 1994, in the former convent of Sant'Anna: more than 900 objects from the late nineteenth century to the sixties on agriculture, linen weaving, crafts and sacred vestments, with a multimedia room and sound biographies of Women's Voci.

  • Where Via Granatelli 3, Morigerati
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Borgo medievale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small medieval village whose origins would date back to a settlement of Basilian monks, today the village hotel is with about thirty reception facilities.
