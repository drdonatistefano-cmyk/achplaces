What to see in Gazzola

On the western shore of the Trebbia, 21 km from Piacenza, in a territory rich in castles: the jewel is the walled village of Rivalta, in the Trebbia Regional Park, with the castle of the Landi and the fifteenth-century church of San Martino; at the entrance of the village the statue of the elephant recalls the battle of 218 BC between Hannibal and the Romans.

Castello di Rivalta

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Fortification attested in the 11th century as possession of Malaspina, passed in the 14th century to the Landi who in the second half of the 15th century transformed it into a residence: the courtyard with loggia has friezes, capitals and medallions in terracotta by Antonio da Lugano and Antonio da Pavia, the facade with tympanum and the staircase are eighteenth century. Visit the 25-metre hall with monumental fireplace and coffered ceiling, the green and falcon rooms, the gallery, the frescoed playroom, the large kitchen with original furnishings, the basement and the dungeons; the weapons museum houses three flags of the Sarmato saxophones on the Christian ships in Lepanto (1571) and finds collected in the Amazon by Ermanno Stradelli at the end of the nineteenth century. Visits only by reservation (at least 24 hours before).

  • Where Borgo di Rivalta, Gazzola
  • Entry €10, reduced €8
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.castellodirivalta.it

Sources: castellodirivalta.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Borgo di Rivalta

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified village on the Trebbia at the foot of the first Piacenza hills covered with vineyards, gathered around the castle, closed to traffic and paved in pebbles, with parking for cars and buses nearby. With Statto, Montechiaro and Rivergaro, it controlled the access to the Val Trebbia along the Caminus Ianuae, the road to Genoa and the sea; the Trebbia cycle path connects it to Piacenza.

  • Where Rivalta, Gazzola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The fifteenth-century church of the village of Rivalta with wooden trusses ceiling and terracotta decorations; it houses paintings by the seventeenth-century painter Ferrante of Bologna.

  • Where Rivalta, Gazzola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: italia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Statua dell'elefante

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

It welcomes those who come from Piacenza and recalls the battle of 218 BC on the Trebbia between Hannibal and the Romans, defeated also thanks to the surprise effect of the elephants.

  • Where Ingresso del paese da Piacenza, Gazzola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
