What to see in Pralormo

A hilly village about 30 km from Turin, known for the Beraudo Castle, still the family residence, and for the nineteenth-century park of Xavier Kurten where every spring, the tulips of "Messer Tulipano" bloom; to see also the sanctuary of the Blessed Virgin of Spina and the parish with the triptych of Jacopino Longo.

Castello Beraudo di Pralormo e Trenino del Conte

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

It was founded in the 13th century between the fortifications fought between Asti and the Biandrate, and passed to the Roeros and from 1680 to the Beraudo, made by Maria Giovanna Battista of Savoia Nemours. In 1840 the court architect Ernesto Melano transformed it: removed moat and drawbridge, added entrance porch and staircase, covered the courtyard to form a double-height living room. The visit goes from the cellars to the Salone delle Feste and to the Chamber of the Minister; the "Trenino del Conte" is a railway model of the late nineteenth century with locomotives and wagons from 1896 to 1939. Closed in August.

  • Where Via Umberto I 26, Pralormo
  • Entry Castle €8, reduced (4-12 years) €5, free under 4 years; Train €12, reduced €10, children €8; combined visit €15, reduced €10
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Parco del castello e Messer Tulipano

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

The English park was designed in the nineteenth century by Xavier Kurten, architect of the Savoy court; at the end of the century there were built the Orangerie and a glass and iron greenhouse of the Lefevre brothers of Paris. Every spring, between April and May, since 2000 there is held "Messer Tulipano" with about 100,000 blooms of tulips and daffodils, including a collection of black tulips; in the park there are a coffee, a picnic area and a shop of flowers and local products.

  • Where Via Umberto I 26, Pralormo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pralormo.to.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.pralormo.to.it

Santuario della Beata Vergine della Spina

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

According to tradition, it was born from an image of the Virgin painted on a pylon that, wounded to one eye by a thorn, began to bleed; already in 1585 the bishop of Asti described there a great turnout of faithful. Next to it stands the monastery of the middle seventeenth century, inhabited by Trinitarians, Cistercians and other orders, since 1991 of the Adoring nuns of the Cottolengo; in 1877 the painter Felice Barucco painted the vaults. Feast on August 15th.

  • Where Strada del Santuario 22, Pralormo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pralormo.to.it

Sources: comune.pralormo.to.it

Parrocchiale dei Santi Giacomo e Donato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1931-32 with the work of the inhabitants, who brought sand and gravel from the Rio Verde; in the apse the triptych of Jacopino Longo with the Madonna and Child and the Saints Giacomo and Donato (1546), stolen in 1997 and returned by the Carabinieri in 2000. The organ is by Antonio Mola (1898); on the same square remains the old parish church of the late seventeenth-early eighteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II 1, Pralormo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pralormo.to.it

Sources: comune.pralormo.to.it

Pilone votivo di San Donato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

An ancient place of worship that testifies to the first inhabited nucleus, cited in 1662 as an ancient parish; demolished in 1872 because it was dilapidated, was rebuilt by the Municipality in 1874. It is reached with a naturalistic path on dirt; on August 7th the statue of the saint arrives in procession from the parish.

  • Where Via San Donato 3 (SP 134, verso Cellarengo), Pralormo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pralormo.to.it

Sources: comune.pralormo.to.it

Sentiero delle Beatitudini

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring that follows for a stretch the path of the Lake Spina, with a wide panorama of the hills and the Alps, then deviates to the sanctuary of Spina and returns to the starting point; along the path signs report the Beatitudes.

  • Where Partenza nel territorio di Pralormo (traccia GPX sul sito del Comune)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pralormo.to.it

Sources: comune.pralormo.to.it
