What to see in Lavello

Medieval village on a hill overlooking Puglia, next to the daunio-Roman center of Forentum: in the territory the ruins of the Roman villa called "Casa del Diavolo" , the medieval frescoed church of Santa Maria del Principe and, in the village, the Museum of peasant civilization and the Civic Antiquarium.

4 places to visit in Lavello, in the province of Potenza (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Permanent exhibition set up in two rooms on the ground floor of Palazzo Pignatelli, seat of the town hall: since 1994 it exhibits about 255 pieces of the territory of Lavello, including furnishings, agricultural tools, costumes and photographs related to the wheat cycle and the peasant house.

  • Where Via Cavour, Lavello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.lavello.pz.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.lavello.pz.it

Civico Antiquarium

Museums · Archaeological

Municipal Museum whose foundation was decided by the City Council on July 9, 1926; it hosts the exhibition "I culti della ferttura nel II millennio b.C." on the hypogeum 1036, with ceramics from the beginning of the Middle Bronze, a support for hourglass perhaps ritual and objects of ornament from the Neolithic to the Bronze Age.

  • Where Lavello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.lavello.pz.it

Sources: comune.lavello.pz.it

Casa del Diavolo (villa romana)

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

On a hill on the right of the river Olivento, a few kilometers from the village, remain the ruins of a Roman villa of imperial age with rooms in red brick and an ancient thermal system; it is the most important Roman archaeological structure of the territory, excavated since 1998. The visible area is not fenced.

  • Where Lungo la SS655, territorio di Lavello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.lavello.pz.it

Sources: comune.lavello.pz.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria del Principio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Medieval church with three aisles at the cemetery, with frescoes on the inner walls and under the vault; next door stands the building of the bishop's summer residence.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
