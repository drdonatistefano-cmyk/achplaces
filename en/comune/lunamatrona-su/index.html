What to see in Lunamatrona

The Marmilla village between the Gesturi Giara and the Siddi Giara, Lunamatrona preserves Su Cuaddu de Nixias, indicated as the oldest tomb of Giants of Sardinia, and the Trobas protonuraghe. On the basalt plateau of Sa Corona Arrubia, between Lunamatrona and Collinas, there is the naturalistic museum of the territory "Giovanni Pusceddu."

Museo naturalistico del territorio "Giovanni Pusceddu" - Sa Corona Arrubia

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

It rises on the basaltic plateau of Sa Corona Arrubia, covered with red lichens, between the countryside of Lunamatrona and Collinas, and is dedicated to Giovanni Pusceddu, historian mayor of Villanovaforru. It has three permanent sections: fauna, with eight life-size dioramas of jars, holm oak woods, riparian environments and arid pastures; botany, with mycotheque, xyloteca, herbarium and medicinal plants; anthropic, with the reconstruction of a prenuragic dwelling, reproductions of sites of the area and tools of the peasant life of the Marmilla. Outside there is a geobotanic park with educational trails; open every day 9:00-19:00, free guided tours by reservation (data updated at 8/5/2025).

  • Where Località Spinarba s.n.c., Strada Collinas, Lunamatrona
  • Entry €6, reduced €4, family €15
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Tomba di Giganti Su Cuaddu de Nixias

History and architecture · Archaeology

It is indicated as the oldest tomb of Giants: it was born as a lithic cyst grave of the second half of the third millennium BC. (Culture of Monte Claro), on which in the Middle Bronze (XVI-XIV century BC) was added a dolmen room with a corridor just over 10 meters long. At the center of the exedra, about 14 meters long, stands a stele centinata in sandstone of about 3 meters; a circular hole opened in the following period gives it the name, because according to legend the horses were tied there. The northeast/southwest orientation is unusual.

Nuraghe Trobas

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Nuraghe with single tower on a modest relief, with external diameter of about 13 m and preserved walls up to 2 m; the circular chamber has two lateral niches and the door of the staircase opens in the entrance hall, facing south. It collapsed in the Middle-Recent Bronze and was then re-used as a hut, until a violent fire caused its abandonment; the excavation of 1982-1983 returned about a thousand fragments of obsidian, basalt millstones, ceramics and a bronze dagger. It is located about 270 m south-west of the tomb On Cuaddu de Nixias and is an unmanaged site.

  • Where Strada comunale Lunamatrona-Collinas, Lunamatrona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Parrocchiale di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the center of the village, it is located on the area of a Benedictine church of the 11th century; the present building, classicist with 17th century reworkings, dates from the end of the 16th and the beginning of the 17th century. It has a single nave with a barrel vault and four chapels on the side; on the first right there is the retablo of Santa Maria, coming from the church of the same name. The feast of the saint is celebrated at the end of June.
