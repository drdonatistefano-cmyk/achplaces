What to see in Otranto

The easternmost city of Italy, on a rocky spur on the sea: the Cathedral with the pavement mosaic of 1166 and the Martyrs' Chapel of 1480, the Byzantine church of St. Peter, the Aragonese Castle and, outside the walls, the Alimini Lakes, the Bay of the Turks and the lighthouse of Punta Palascìa.

10 places to visit in Otranto, in the province of Lecce (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Annunziata e mosaico

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Romanesque Apulian in clear stone, rebuilt in 1068 by Norman bishop William: fourteen granite columns divide the three naves and the floor is covered by the mosaic of 1166 of the monk Pantaleone, with the Tree of Life supported by two elephants, biblical scenes, cavalry and arturian cycles, beasts and the months of the zodiac. Under the apse the crypt with 68 columns and Byzantine paintings; in the chapel of the Martyrs, wanted by Ferdinand I, seven large treasures preserve the remains of the inhabitants killed in 1480.

  • Where Piazza Basilica, Otranto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Castello Aragonese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Designed by Ciro Ciri with Francesco di Giorgio Martini and built between 1485 and 1498 for Ferdinando I d'Aragona on the Swabian fortifications: irregular pentagonal plant surrounded by the moat, three cylindrical towers and a rampart to launch towards the sea with the 1578 ramps, the coats of arms of Antonio de Mendoza and Pedro de Toledo and the great coat of arms of Carlo V on the portal.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Otranto
  • Entry €12, reduced €10 (adults) and €8 (under 18)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On a hill in the historic centre, probably the first cathedral of the city: Byzantine church with Greek cross with dome, three circular apses, eight columns and Byzantine frescoes that tell the story.

  • Where Centro storico, Otranto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Centro storico, Porta Alfonsina e bastioni

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

You enter from the Alfonsina Gate, with the Duchess and Ippolita towers, built in the Aragonese defenses after the Turkish devastation of 1480; inside, stone paved alleys that descend to the sea, granite bomb balls and the walk on the bastions.

  • Where Porta Alfonsina, Otranto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Torre dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

In the center of Piazza del Popolo, recently restored: the Latin inscriptions date back to 1644, with the latest changes around 1770.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Otranto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Colle della Minerva

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The hill where in 1480, after the Turkish siege, the inhabitants of Otranto were beheaded who refused to deny the Christian faith.

  • Where Colle della Minerva, Otranto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Abbazia di San Nicola di Casole

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 1.5 km from the centre on the coast towards Leuca, the ruins of the 12th century Basilian cenobius, a great center of culture whose manuscripts are now in the main museums of the world, destroyed by the Turks in 1480.

  • Where Litoranea per Santa Maria di Leuca, Otranto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Faro di Punta Palascìa

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

White stone tower on Cape d'Otranto, the easternmost point of Italy, where the first sun rises; the lantern would be built in Paris by a pupil of Gustave Eiffel and the lighthouse hosts the multimedia museum of the sea on marine fauna and flora.

  • Where Capo d'Otranto, Otranto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Laghi Alimini

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Two lakes north of the city: the Great Alimini, brackish and once connected to the Adriatic, with Mediterranean maquis and sand, and the Small Alimini or Fontanelle, fed by fresh water sources, with marsh vegetation and rare plants such as the water chestnut and swamp orchids; the bay of Alimini has low and transparent seabeds.

  • Where Laghi Alimini, Otranto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Baia dei Turchi

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

A long white sandy crescent beach with low seabed, marked at the beginning by the Torre Fiumicelli, between pine forests and spot in the protected oasis of the Alimini Lakes; the name recalls the landing of the Turks in the fifteenth century during the siege of Otranto.

  • Where Località Baia dei Turchi, Otranto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
