What to see in Albenga

The town of the towers on the plain of Centa, with the medieval old town on the Roman plant, the early Christian baptistery of the fifth century and the Naval Museum with the amphorae of a Roman ship.

11 places to visit in Albenga, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.
