What to see in Carinola

In the northern Casertano, Carinola preserves the ruins of the Norman castle of Count Riccardo (1134) rebuilt by the Aragonese with Catalan traits, the thirteenth-century convent of San Francesco a Casanova and the Sanctuary of Maria SS. delle Grazie with a fifteenth-century icon.

3 places to visit in Carinola, in the province of Caserta (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ruderi del Castello di Carinola

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Built in Norman times by Count Riccardo in 1134 and rebuilt by the Aragonese, who gave him Catalan traits. In the fifteenth century it passed to the feudalist Giovanni Antonio Petrucci; confiscated, it was donated to Consalvo de Cordova and then passed to the Capua, the Grillo and the marquisa of Clarafuente, wife of the prince of Angri. Today the ruins remain, with an imposing tower, decorations wanted by the sovereigns of Naples and frames in tuff.

Convento di San Francesco a Casanova

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The 13th century complex consists of a church and a convent with cloister, which some sources attribute to Saint Francis himself. Tradition indicates the rock anfratto where the saint would rest; in sacristy an inscription contains the Gospel verse "Faciem tuam lava" (Matthew 6:16-18).

Santuario di Maria SS. delle Grazie

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

The church rises where, around 1600, the Madonna delle Grazie would appear to a young woman of the place; however, the structure could be older, due to the presence of previous frescoes and an icon of the Virgin of the fifteenth century, still object of lively devotion. It preserves a crypt with drainholes for the burial of the dead.
