What to see in Gressoney-Saint-Jean

Elegant Walser resort of the Lys Valley, Orange Flag since 2017, vacation of Queen Margherita at the foot of Mount Rosa.

6 places to visit in Gressoney-Saint-Jean, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Gressoney-Saint-Jean in the guide with map and filters

Castel Savoia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Started in 1899 and finished five years later on a project by Emilio Stramucci for Queen Margherita, in eclectic style with five cuspidate towers and a staircase in double helicoidal oak; original interiors and rocky garden.

  • Where Località Belvedere, Gressoney-Saint-Jean
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.touringclub.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: lovevda.it · touringclub.it · italia.it · valledaostaheritage.com

Villa Margherita

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Summer residence of the queen from 1889 to 1903, in Art Nouveau style, today seat of the Municipality and the Walser Cultural Center.

  • Where Villa Margherita 1, Gressoney-Saint-Jean
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

On the upper square, renovated in 1733, with portico of 1626 and 14 newsstands of the Via Crucis frescoed in the eighteenth century.

  • Where Obre Platz, Gressoney-Saint-Jean
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Alpenfaunamuseum «Beck-Peccoz»

Museums · Science and university

Built in 1903, about 2000 hunting trophies and specimens of regional fauna.

  • Where Gressoney-Saint-Jean
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Lago Gover

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Artificial lake between pine and fir trees with views of Monte Rosa, with playground, summer fishing and winter skating.

  • Where Gressoney-Saint-Jean
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Nucleo storico e case walser

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Stone and wood houses around the two squares, including the first wooden inn of 1717.

  • Where Gressoney-Saint-Jean
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it
