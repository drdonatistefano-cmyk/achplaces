Tresigallo, la città metafisica

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Rationalist city built in the thirties, stopped in 1940: Piazza della Rivoluzione in the shape of D, pastel-coloured buildings and the former public bathrooms with the inscription SOGNI.
