What to see in Murialdo

The town of Val Bormida di Millesimo, called "the village of the churches" for its twenty churches and chapels: in the hamlet of Ponte there are the Gothic parish church of San Lorenzo (1440-1445) and the Oratory of Sant'Agostino, FAI places, while in the hamlet of Riofreddo the Museum of C'era once has its seat.

Chiesa di San Lorenzo e Oratorio di Sant'Agostino

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

At the bridge that connected the castle to the inhabited, the new parish church of San Lorenzo was started in 1440 and ended in 1445, as the epigraph of the portal says, by the master Francesco Garone, with local masters similar to those of San Lorenzo di Genova; in 1676-1681 the Baroque reconstruction incorporated the Gothic columns into the pillars, but remain the portal, the rosette in terracotta, the gate On a column re-emerged in the restorations is painted Santa Caterina d'Alessandria with date 1459. The nearby Oratorio di Sant'Agostino was born from a fourteenth-century fortification, adapted to oratory at least from 1573, and preserves a late-fourth-century cycle of the Passion on all walls, now in rediscovery.

  • Where Borgata Ponte 19, Murialdo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.provincia.savona.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · provincia.savona.it

Museo del C'era una volta

Museums · Themed

Established by the Cultural Association Riofreddo Together to preserve the memory of a hard and modest life, it collects about 2000 objects from the eighteenth to the first half of the twentieth century: documents of a Ferriera, a weight of Elban origin, Albisola ceramics, nineteenth century furnishings and early twentieth century clothes, sacred objects, banners and clothes of fraternities, materials on crafts, domestic life and leisure. Open on Sunday 14-18, without reservation.

  • Where Frazione Borgo di Riofreddo, Murialdo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.provincia.savona.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · provincia.savona.it

Percorso della spada nella roccia presso il Castello Del Carretto

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

Murialdo is one of the two Ligurian localities with a sword in the rock: it is located not far from the Castle Del Carretto, along a path that the regional portal defines very beautiful and suitable for families.

  • Where Presso il Castello Del Carretto, Murialdo

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
