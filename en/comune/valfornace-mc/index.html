What to see in Valfornace

The municipality was born from the fusion of Pievebovigliana and Fiordimonte, Orange Flag at the gates of the Sibillini Mountains: the parish church of Santa Maria Assunta with the Romanesque crypt with five naves, the Romanesque roundabout of San Giusto, the bridge of Pontelatrave on the Via Lauretana, the castle of Beldiletto, the museum pole in the Town Hall and the frescoes of Girolamo di Giovanni in Fiordimonte; after the earthquake of 2016 monuments and museums are partially usable.

Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta e cripta romanica

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque parish church of the XI-XII centuries in the upper part of Pievebovigliana (a plaque probably bears date 1162), of which remain the eastern apse with monofore and the stone wall; facade, bell tower and interior were renovated from the eighteenth century. The coeva crypt, rediscovered in the twentieth century, has five naves on four rows of columns and three apses, altar columns in piperin, a Roman plaque of the second century A.D. and a fresco of Saint Lucia attributed to Archangel of Cola (1420-25 about): it is among the most famous Romanesque monuments of the Marches.

Chiesa di San Giusto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Romanesque roundabout of the XI-XII centuries on a hill near the castle of San Maroto, cited as priory in 1240, among the most singular Romanesque works of the Marche: circular plan with four apses decorated with hanging arches and concentric stone ring dome built without centimeters. The bell tower dates back to the first fourteenth century; inside a Madonna enthroned from the second half of the thirteenth century and a table of the Madonna del Rosario di Venanzo da Camerino.

  • Where San Maroto, Pievebovigliana, Valfornace
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sibillini.net · italia.it

Ponte di Pontelatrave

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Romanesque bridge of the fourteenth century with five arches in sandstones, on Roman foundations of the first century A.D.: known as "Pons de trabe Bonantis" was one of the main post stations of the Lauretana street, where the roads from the South to Norcia and Visso continued towards Camerino, and the main access to the valley of Pievebovigliana, between the castle of Beldiletto and the convent of San Francesco.

  • Where Pontelatrave, Valfornace
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · sibillini.net · italia.it

Castello di Beldiletto

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Castle started in 1380 by Giovanni Spaccalferro and adapted to a Renaissance villa in the fifteenth century; there stayed sovereigns and popes, including Julius II.

Polo museale di Pievebovigliana

Museums · City history

In the municipal palace, it tells the social and artistic history of the territory from prehistory to today: the Archaeological Museum "Valerio Cianfarani" (Valerio Cianfarani), the Civic Museum "Raffaele Campelli" (Raffaele Campelli), the Historical Museum of the territory (home weaving with 15th century canvases, brick kilns, history of the Varnelli distillery), the xylographies of Maria Ciccotti and the collection Gino Marotta in the council hall, with the fortepiano of composer Filippo Marchetti.

Gualchiera-Tintoria Cianni

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Rare example in central Italy of hydraulic factory to crowd and dye with natural colors cloths of wool and hemp, at the foot of the castle of Pievebovigliana: the plant dates back to the seventeenth century, was managed by Stefano Cianni in the nineteenth century and in 1822 worked about 10,000 arms of fabric a year. There are still foundations, walls and a stretch of the canal fed by the river Capodacqua; the municipality provides for its recovery as a museum of industrial archaeology.

  • Where Strada Provinciale 95, Pievebovigliana, Valfornace
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.valfornace.mc.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio a Castello

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Inside the walls of the castle of Cornacuna in Fiordimonte, of which remain a stretch and the entrance portal: church to a nave oriented to the north for defensive needs, with stone portal and coat of arms; on the background wall of frescoes by Girolamo di Giovanni of 1456, restored in 1992-93, and a fresco of 1613 attributed to Peregrino di Milano.

  • Where Castello, Fiordimonte, Valfornace
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sibillini.net · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta di Nemi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church isolated on the hill of Nemi, mentioned already in 1218 among the appliances of the Pieve di Bovigliano; it preserves a series of images of Paolo da Visso of 1447 and a painting of the Boccati with the Madonna crowned with the Child between the Saints Matteo and Macario.

  • Where Nemi, Valfornace
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sibillini.net · turismo.comune.valfornace.mc.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maddalena

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Small isolated Romanesque church at 1112 meters between Poggio della Pagnotta and Monte Fiegni, at the border of four municipalities; it preserves fragments of frescoes with a Crucifix and the Maddalena, perhaps of Cola di Pietro (1370-80).

  • Where Località Santa Maria Maddalena, Valfornace
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sibillini.net · turismo.comune.valfornace.mc.it · italia.it

Sentiero Natura N2 del Parco dei Sibillini

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Route of the Sibillini Mountains National Park of 4-6 km starting from Pievebovigliana, about two and a half hours; the municipal portal connects it to the chestnut and oak woods around Monte San Savino.

  • Where Pievebovigliana, Valfornace
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
