What to see in Cormons

The capital of Collio Gorizia, with a Habsburg character, at the foot of Monte Quarin: squares with palaces in the Habsburg style, the Cathedral of Sant'Adalberto, the sanctuary of the Mystical Rose, Palazzo Locatelli with the Civic Museum and the town wine shop, among vineyards and typical products such as smoked ham.

Centro storico e Piazza XXIV Maggio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The oldest nucleus is the 'centa doppia', a defensive settlement of houses arranged around a natural rise; the Piazza XXIV Maggio is surrounded by palaces in the Habsburg style and was redesigned in the eighties by the architect Podrecca. Among the historic buildings the sixteenth-century Neuhaus House, the seventeenth-century Tacò Palace in via Dante and the Municipal Theatre of 1908 in via Nazario Sauro.

  • Where Piazza XXIV Maggio, Cormons
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.cormons.go.it · turismofvg.it

Palazzo Locatelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Elegant 18th-century Palladian residence built by the Bergamo family Locatelli, who lived there since the mid-eighteenth century; the family coat of arms dominates the portal. Beyond the hall there is an Italian garden; the noble floor conserves frescoes from the second half of the nineteenth century by Antonio Picco. Today it is the seat of the municipality, the town wine shop and the Civic Museum of the territory.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · comune.cormons.go.it · italia.it

Museo civico del territorio

Museums · City history

In the outbuildings of Palazzo Locatelli, with entrance from the inner garden: on the raised floor a thematic section dedicated to the cormonese sculptor Alfonso Canciani (1863-1955); ground floor and first floor host temporary exhibitions of history, art and local culture during the year.

MiC card (updated 2020) indicates "closed" every day; check openings with the Municipality (tel. 0481 637152)

Duomo di Sant'Adalberto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in the 18th century on the site of a 13th century chapel, it rises on the slopes of Monte Quarin near the original nucleus of the town, where there was a section of the walls; next to the House of Plevan.

  • Where Via Riva della Torre, Cormons
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.cormons.go.it

Raccolta d'arte sacra S. Adalberto (Museo del Duomo)

Museums · Themed

In the House of Plevan next to the Duomo, it collects documents and sacred art from the parish and the territory, ordered by typological sections from the 13th to the 20th century; multimedia stations.

Piazza della Libertà e monumento a Massimiliano I

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The square houses the statue of Massimiliano I of Habsburg, by Edmund Hofman, remembered as the first foreign monarch in Friuli (Cormons passed to the Habsburgs between 1497 and 1521), and the statue of the Freedom of Marcello Mascherini.

  • Where Piazza della Libertà, Cormons
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.cormons.go.it

Santuario della Rosa Mistica (chiesa di Santa Caterina)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Baroque church of the first decades of the eighteenth century, designed by Carlo Corbellini, with two onion bell towers; it stands in front of the monument to Massimiliano I.

Monte Quarin, chiesa del Soccorso e resti del castello

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The hill above the city preserves the church of the Blessed Virgin of the Rescue, erected in 1636 by Luca Del Mestri, and the remains of the castle demolished by the Habsburgs in 1514; the strategic position played a defensive role from the Roman era to the Middle Ages.

  • Where Monte Quarin, Cormons
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.cormons.go.it · italia.it

Enoteca di Cormòns e vini del Collio

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The town wine shop is located in Palazzo Locatelli; Cormons is the center of Collio, a land of wines and products such as sweet smoked ham with cherry and laurel wood, radicchio Rosa di Gorizia and gnocchi of plums.
