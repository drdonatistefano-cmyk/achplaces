Borgo di Compiano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The fortified village in Val Taro, capital for almost four centuries of the small state of the Landi, with stone roads, palaces and tower houses.

  • Where Compiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Castello di Compiano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The castle with trapezoidal plan with round towers on the spur above the Taro, then prison, female college and private residence until 1987.
