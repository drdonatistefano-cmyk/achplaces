What to see in Curtatone

At the gates of Mantua, on the banks of the Mincio, the village of Grazie houses the Gothic-Lombard Sanctuary of the Blessed Virgin of Graces (1399-1406), famous for the crocodile hanging at the time and for the National Meeting of the Madonnas of Ferragosto; around, the Mincio Valleys and the places of the battle of Curtatone and Montanara of 1848.

6 places to visit in Curtatone, in the province of Mantua (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario della Beata Vergine Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Wanted by Francesco Gonzaga as a former vote for the end of the plague, designed by Bartolino da Novara (architect of the Castle of San Giorgio in Mantua), started in 1399 and consecrated on 15 August 1406; basilica minor since 1907. The Gothic-Lombard interior with a single nave houses a wooden frame with 53 polymaterical statues in niches, numerous ex-vote, the mummified crocodile hanging at a time and, in the first chapel on the right, the mausoleum of Baldassarre Castiglione by Giulio Romano. Bertolucci shot a scene of ... nine hundred.

  • Where Piazzale Santuario, Grazie di Curtatone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.santuariodellegraziecurtatone.it

Borgo di Grazie

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village of a few hundred inhabitants in the Natural Reserve of the Mincio Valleys, born around an ancient fishing community and the square of the Sanctuary: between the late eighteenth and early nineteenth century the district had about twenty houses. The entire village can be visited freely as an urban museum.In Ferragosto there are the ancient fair and, since 1973, the National Meeting of the Madonnas on the churchyard.

  • Where Grazie di Curtatone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.curtatone.mn.it · in-lombardia.it

Museo dei Madonnari

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Set up at the Sanctuary, it exhibits reproductions and preparatory cartoons of the works realized in the various editions of the Meeting of Our Lady. Valli del Mincio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the largest and most important wetlands in northern Italy, in the Mincio Regional Park and in the Natura 2000 site and Valli del Mincio , which surrounds the village of Grazie; in 2009 it was among the five Italian towns awarded with the European recognition EDEN.

  • Where Grazie di Curtatone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.curtatone.mn.it · in-lombardia.it

Corte Spagnola

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Court of the end of the seventeenth century of the Montanara possession, belonged to the Marquis Zanetti and then to the Cavalcabo; it was one of the crucial places of the battle of Curtatone and Montanara of 29 May 1848. Sold to the Municipality in 2002 and recovered, since 2004 it is the seat of the Town Hall.

  • Where Piazza Corte Spagnola 3, Montanara di Curtatone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.curtatone.mn.it · in-lombardia.it

Pioppo nero di Serraglio

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Monumental tree next to a canal, with a trunk of 4.5 meters of circumference and crown over 25 meters of height; it is a green monument freely visitable.

  • Where Via Guglielmo Marconi 2, Serraglio di Curtatone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it
