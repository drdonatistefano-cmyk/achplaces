What to see in Domus de Maria

Country of the eighteenth century in the extreme south-west of Sardinia, with the hamlet of Chia and about seven kilometers of coast: the Spanish tower, the beaches of Su Giudeu and Porto Campana, Cala Cipolla and the lighthouse of Capo Spartivento, the remains of the Phoenician city of Bithia, whose finds are in the House museum of the village, and the wood of Is Cannoneris.

Casa museo di Domus de Maria

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

It exhibits Phoenician, Punic and Roman finds from the municipal territory, in the area of ancient Bithia: everyday objects, funerary and votive furnishings such as lamps, plates and amphorae; the strong piece is the reconstruction of a Phoenician-Punic tomb with its furnishings. Open every day in summer, in the other months by reservation; guided tours.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele, Domus de Maria
  • Entry €1,60 reduced €1
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · touringclub.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Torre di Chia

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built in the 17th century by the Crown of Spain against the barbaric corsairs, it dominates the coast and gives access to the long sandy beach.

  • Where Chia, Domus de Maria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Antica città di Bithia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Phoenician-punic centre then Roman, whose remains were brought to light by a tide: a tophet, the ancient road to Nora and a necropolis with the temple of the god Bes, whose statue is at the National Archaeological Museum of Cagliari.

  • Where Chia, Domus de Maria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Su Giudeu e le spiagge di Chia

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

To the west from the tower follow Sa Tuerra, Porto Campana with its dunes, De su Sali and Su Giudeu, considered the most beautiful: light sand, dunes with centuries-old junipers and an islet reachable on foot thanks to the bottom; it is often set of films and commercials.

  • Where Chia, Domus de Maria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Cala Cipolla e Faro di Capo Spartivento

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the western end of the coast, Cala Cipolla is a small sheltered creek reachable only on foot; from here a panoramic path rises to the lighthouse of Capo Spartivento, which dominates the southern coast of Sulcis.

  • Where Capo Spartivento, Domus de Maria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Bosco di Is Cannoneris

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Wood of holm oaks, conifers and Mediterranean maquis crossed by a road and hiking trails, with Sardinian deer and deer; you can see the royal eagle and the pilgrim falcon.

  • Where Is Cannoneris, Domus de Maria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
