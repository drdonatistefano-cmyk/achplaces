What to see in Magliano Vetere

Borgo del Cilento is 650 m in the Cilento National Park, Vallo di Diano and Alburni, with remains of towers and baronial palaces; Longobard stronghold, was destroyed in 1669 and the survivors founded the hamlet of Magliano Nuovo, on the Gole del Calore. In the village the paleontological museum tells the fossils of the Cretaceous territory.

Museo paleontologico di Magliano Vetere

Museums · Science and university

Museum, didactic and scientific center of the Park born from a geo-paleontological research started in 1984 on the fossiliferous deposits of the Alburni Mountains and the dorsal Vesole-Chianiello; Magliano was chosen for the presence of a site with fossil plants of the Cretaceous. It comprises four paleontological sections, a large hall with the reconstruction of a Paleoambiente of the Cretaceous, three workshops and two educational areas, with guided tours for schools and non-specialized public. The FAI reports a 30% discount for members.

Gole del Calore a Magliano Nuovo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The fourth of the five piercings excavated by the Lucan Warm in the upper valley, with limestone walls that exceed twenty meters and the giants' mufflers excavated by the reels of the floods. You walk along a path always open from the medieval bridge Â"a back of mule" of Magliano Nuovo to Remolino, and with a second, summer, from Remolino; a stretch is also seen in canoe. Along the way, the suspended boulder of the Petratetta Bridge, Bernardo's grotto linked to a brigand and the ruins of the hydroelectric power plant, live in it otters, brown trout, water blackbirds, fishermen martins and sunglasses.
