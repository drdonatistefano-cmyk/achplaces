What to see in Tesero

Borgo della Val di Fiemme known for its wood craftsmanship and for the about one hundred cribs set up at Christmas in the alleys: in the center the parish church of Sant'Eliseo, the Gothic church of Santi Leonardo and Gottardo and Casa Jellici; in Stava the documentation center and the path of memory on the disaster of 19 July 1985, in Lake Tesero the stadium of the bottom and in Pampeago the art park RespirArt under the Latemar.

8 places to visit in Tesero, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro di documentazione Stava 1985

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Managed by the Stava Foundation 1985, it tells of the collapse of the decantation basins of the Prestavèl mine of July 19, 1985: causes, responsibility, reconstruction and parallels with the tragedies of Vajont and Cermis. The 1-1,5-hour visit usually begins with the short documentary "Stava 19 July" (in English on request). Open every day from 15 June to 15 September from 15 to 18; in the rest of the year on Saturdays by reservation (minimum 8 people, by 12 on Friday).

  • Where Piazzale Sgorigrad, Stava, 38038 Tesero
  • Entry free (15/6-15/9), off-season €5, families €10
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.stava1985.it

Sources: stava1985.it · visittrentino.info

Sentiero della Memoria «Stava 1985»

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Path between nature and memory created by the Municipality on the proposal of the Foundation: from the documentation center the path "The mountain of discoveries" (2-3 hours of easy walk) passes through the former mining sites and basins, with a view at 1,500 m on the cast; then the church of the Palanca in Stava, with the bronze hand of Toni Gross donated by the people of Vajont and a plate of glass blessed by John Paul II on 17 July 1988, and finally the church of San Leonardo in Tesero with the cemetery of the victims. Always open, snow permitting.

  • Where Località Stava, Tesero
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · stava1985.it · visitfiemme.it

Pampeago e RespirArt

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

At the Alpe di Pampeago, the stage finishes in five editions of the Giro d'Italia, you will climb by chairlift towards the Latemar, where in summer there is RespirArt, an art park with contemporary installations and concerts at the Teatro del Latemar; in winter the Ski Center Latemar, connected to Predazzo and Obereggen, has about 45 km of slopes. At Malga Pampeago you learn to milk and make butter.

  • Where Pampeago, Tesero
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitfiemme.it · visittrentino.info

Centro del Fondo di Lago di Tesero

Nature and landscape · Landscapes

Nordic Ski Stadium that hosted the World Cup Championships of 1991, 2003 and 2013 and World Cup races, with rings of different length, evening lighting in some days, biathlon area and outdoor skating rink; in summer walks and bikes along the valley floor and the Val di Fiemme bike path.
