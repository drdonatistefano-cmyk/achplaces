What to see in Conegliano

Birthplace of the Renaissance painter Giovanni Battista Cima, gathered around the hill of Giano with the castle tower that houses the civic museum; along the portico Contrada Grande line up the Duomo with the Hall of the frescoed Battuti, Palazzo Sarcinelli and Piazza Cima with the Accademia Theatre. The municipality is in the buffer zone of the UNESCO site of the Prosecco hills of Conegliano and Valdobbiadene (2019).

Castello e Museo civico del castello

Museums · City history · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The museum occupies the surviving tower of the castle at the top of the hill of Giano, called the Bell Tower: base staircase with slits, windows of 1467, bell tower rebuilt after the collapse of 1491 and elevated with Ghibelline blackbirds in 1847-55. The collections, begun in 1946, include a fresco of the Pordenone of 1514, organ doors of the workshop of Cima (1510-1517), a large canvas of Palma il Giovane, terracotta of Brustolon and Arturo Martini and, on the upper floors, archaeological finds from the Neolithic to the Roman age; on top a panoramic terrace.

  • Where Piazzale San Leonardo 7, Conegliano
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitconegliano.it · italia.it

Duomo e Sala dei Battuti

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Paid entry

Church started in 1345 by the Confraternity of the Battuti and hidden behind the painted façade of their room: on the high altar the altarpiece of Cima of 1493 with the Madonna and Child and saints, the only original work of the painter remained in the hometown, as well as paintings by Beccaruzzi and Palma il Giovane. The Sala dei Battuti, with nine arches frescoed by Pozzoserrato at the end of the sixteenth century, preserves a cycle from the Creation to the Judgment painted above all by Francesco da Milano on models of Dürer; in the Hall of the Chapter five Flemish tapestries of about 1560. Open room Saturday and Sunday.

  • Where Via XX Settembre 44, Conegliano
  • Entry Free Battuti Room
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitconegliano.it · italia.it

Casa Museo di Giovanni Battista Cima

Museums · House museums

Birthplace of the painter, behind the Duomo, identified thanks to documents of 1516-1578 and restored with the patronage of Camillo Vazzoler: during the works ceramic fragments of the recent Bronze Age (XIV-XIII century B.C.) and an inscription of 1492 emerged. It houses reproductions of the works of Cima, a small archaeological museum and the Cima Foundation; in front of the entrance a bronze statue of the painter inaugurated in 2024. Open Saturday and Sunday afternoon.

  • Where Via Cima 24, Conegliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.fondazionegbcima.com

Sources: fondazionegbcima.com · visitconegliano.it · italia.it

Palazzo Sarcinelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace built in 1518 by a branch of the Sarcinelli Census, which housed Bona Sforza in 1556 and Enrico III of France in 1574: Renaissance façade with three levels with five arches and quadrifora porch on columns of pink marble, interior with stuccoes and monochrome friezes attributed to Conegliano craftsmen. Since 1988, the site of the gallery of modern and contemporary art and then of exhibitions on painting of the sixteenth century; in the attic the Galleria Novecento. Visits to the exhibitions.

  • Where Via XX Settembre 132, Conegliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitconegliano.it · italia.it

Contrada Grande e Piazza Cima

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Contrada Granda, the main street in the historic center between Via XX Settembre, Piazza Cima and Via Beato Marco Ongaro, is almost all porticoed and line Casa Piutti, the Town Hall, Palazzo Sarcinelli, the Montalban palaces and the Monte di Pietà with the frescoed façade, between Porta Dante and Porta del Monticano with the Leone di San Marco. Piazza Cima, slightly sloping on the hill of Giano, is closed by the eighteenth-century Town Hall of Ottavio Scotti and the neoclassical Teatro Accademia by Andrea Scala (1859-1869); here Pietro Germi shot scenes by "Lord & Lords" (1966).

  • Where Piazza Cima, Conegliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Ex Convento di San Francesco

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Franciscan complex built between 1371 and 1411 with two cloisters, a single nave church, a wing of the novitiate of 1708 and a library of 127 codes. Today it remains the main cloister of Renaissance imprint, with the seventeenth-century well surrounded by arches on columns; the cloister is open every day 9-12,30 and 15-17,30.

Chiesa della Madonna della Neve

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Oratory on the calle that descends from the castle, obtained in a tower-door of the Carrara walls (the Porta della Castagnera) and documented since 1544: it preserves the fresco of the Madonna of milk attributed to Giovanni Antonio da Meschio (a little after the half of the fifteenth century), with musical angels added by Francesco Beccaruzzi. Restored by the Alps in 1992; open on Sunday afternoon, other days by reservation.

  • Where Calle Madonna della Neve, Conegliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Brolo di San Francesco

Nature and landscape · City parks · Viewpoint

The garden of the convent of San Francesco sul Colle, between the Calle Madonna della Snow and a stretch of the fourteenth-century walls: for over four centuries vineyard and vegetable garden of the friars, donated to the Municipality in 1986 and re-arranged by Italia Nostra since 1994 with vines, olive trees and fruit trees; from the vines you get a strawberry wine.

Cimitero ebraico

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Built in 1545 on the hill called "Cabalan" and used until about 1882-1884, it preserves about 130 stonestones in sandstone or limestone, mostly facing Jerusalem, of families of Conegliano and Ceneda; among the coats of arms the squirrel of the Coneian, the family of Emanuele Conegliano, then Lorenzo Da Ponte, librettist of Mozart. Guided tour on the first Sunday of the month 10-12, other days by reservation at IAT.

  • Where Viale Gorizia, Conegliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitconegliano.it · italia.it

Scuola Enologica Cerletti e Museo Manzoni

Museums · Science and university

The oldest wine school in Italy, founded in 1876, with a teaching cellar since 1927 and a classroom decorated by Antonio Maria Morera; from 2016 on the first floor the museum dedicated to Luigi Manzoni (1888-1968), author of the Manzoni crossroads such as Bianco 6.0.13, with instruments, botanical collections, cameras and microscopes of the early twentieth century. Visit by telephone reservation.

  • Where Via XXVIII Aprile 20, Conegliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitconegliano.it · italia.it
