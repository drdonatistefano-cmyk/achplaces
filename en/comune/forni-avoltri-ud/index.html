Collezione etnografica «Cemùot chi erin»

Museums · Themed

In the former town hall, a four-storey eighteenth-century palace with elements of the noble carnival house, which became an exhibition venue in 1992; the name means "How We Were." It reconstructs the life of local communities with kitchen, bedroom, hut and haymaking, wool processing, Gerle manufacturing, crafts and a sector particularly rich in costumes and accessories; in the same building an exhibition by photographer Gino Del Fabbro and the cast of a stone found in Pierabech with prints of tetrapod of the lower Triassic (about 250 million years ago). It is part of the CarniaMusei network; information to the Tourist Office (0433 72202).

Mostra permanente «Forni Avoltri nella Grande Guerra»

Museums · Themed

It has been set up since 2008 on the ground floor of the former town hall and documents the war artifacts of Passo Volaia, Monte Navagiust, Passo Cacciatori, Monte Avanza and Monte Chiadenis.

Lago di Bordaglia

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lake of glacial origin reached by Pierabech with the signs CAI 141 and 142, between larch woods and about 900 meters of difference in altitude; along the path the source Fleons, at 1,370 meters, from which flows the water Drop Carnia. It is a wildlife oasis with cedar roosters, marmots, deer, roe deer and royal eagles, gentians and orchids.

  • Where Lago di Bordaglia, Forni Avoltri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.forni-avoltri.ud.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.forni-avoltri.ud.it

Lago di Volaia

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Alpine lake in the shape of a heart at the foot of Monte Coglians, reached by the Tolazzi Refuge (hill fraction) with dirt road and CAI 144 path: almost two hours and about 600 meters of altitude difference. On the opposite shores, two refuges, the Austrian Pichlhütte and the Italian Lambertenghi-Romanin, reminiscent of two lieutenants of the Great War.

  • Where Passo Volaia, Forni Avoltri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.forni-avoltri.ud.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.forni-avoltri.ud.it

Monte Coglians

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

With 2.780 meters it is the highest peak in the region: classic itinerary in summer, challenging in winter with skis or rackets. On the way to Rifugio Marinelli (2,120 m), about two hours from Tolazzi with the CAI 143 trail.

  • Where Collina, Forni Avoltri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.forni-avoltri.ud.it

Sources: comune.forni-avoltri.ud.it · italia.it

Forra di Bordaglia

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Deep engraving up to 30 meters, flowing with an average flow rate of about 300 liters per second; it is reached from the CAI 141 trail and faces from a walkway with parapet along the edge.

  • Where Sentiero CAI 141, Pierabech, Forni Avoltri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.forni-avoltri.ud.it

Sources: comune.forni-avoltri.ud.it

Stretta di Fleons

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Canyon excavated from the Rio Fleons, reachable from the marble quarry of Pierabech; the spring is at 1,350 meters.

  • Where Pierabech, Forni Avoltri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.forni-avoltri.ud.it

Fornèts e Madre Natura

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

Giant wooden sculptures, including Frino, Spirito del Bosco, along the paths of the villages of Forni and Avoltri, and the statue of Mother Nature that overlooks the village.
