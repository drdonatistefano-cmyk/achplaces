What to see in Gais

Country at the entrance of the Aurina Valley, in the Dolomiti Plan de Corones region, with the Romanesque church, the ruins of Kehlburg and the castle of Neuhaus linked to Ezra Pound; at Villa Ottone a museum with over 700 fire helmets from all over the world and above the village, from Montassilone, the panoramic ridge towards the Riesi Mountains.

4 places to visit in Gais, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo degli elmi dei pompieri

Museums · Themed

About 200 m2 with more than 700 fire helmets from all over the world: collection pieces in leather, steel and brass, helmets from Egypt, Thailand, Guatemala and New York, and the French metal helmets, particularly elaborate and often decorated with feathers. Visits from 10 am to 6 pm only by telephone (+39 0474 504117).

  • Where Via Castel Neuhaus 7, Gais
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Antica cappella mortuaria (Beinhaus)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The ancient ossuary has been transformed into a small museum: at the center a cycle of late-Gothic frescoes of the Last Judgment, with other paintings on the redemption of souls who were a catechism for the faithful who could not read. In the showcase are the precious vestments called Sonnenburg and, in a reliquary, the entire skeleton of St. Constantine II. Opening on request.

  • Where Ulrich von Taufers 22, Gais
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Sentiero culturale di Gais e Castel Neuhaus

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of about 2 km (37 minutes, a hundred meters of difference in altitude) which climbs to the castle of Neuhaus, halfway through, and passes through the former ...Pflegerhaus. Along the way works of art recall the troover Oswald von Wolkenstein, the American poet Ezra Pound, his daughter Mary de Rachewiltz and the family of artists Bacher; at first a man-sized cage symbolizes the prison of Pound. From the path you can see the village with the Romanesque church and the ruins of Kehlburg on the hill opposite. In winter it can be frozen.

  • Where Partenza alla rotonda all'ingresso di Gais (fermata bus «Gais Kreisverkehr»)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Escursione «Panorama» da Montassilone allo Schönbichl

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Long ridge between the Wielental and the Tesselberg in a dense and almost intact forest: from Montassilone (1,470 m) to the trails 3 and 7 to the Hühnerspiel and, if the conditions permit, to the top of the Schönbichl (2,452 m), with return to the Tesselberg hut; 11.3 km, about 5 hours and a half, 800 m of altitude difference. View of the peaks over 3000 m of the Riesi Mountains, the Zillertal and Ötztal Alps, the Tauern Highs and the Dolomites south.

  • Where Montassilone, Gais
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info
