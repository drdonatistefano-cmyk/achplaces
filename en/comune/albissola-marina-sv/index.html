What to see in Albissola Marina

Country of ceramics on the Riviera del Beigua, open-air museum with the promenade of the Artists of 1963, the furnaces of Pozzo Garitta and the eighteenth-century Villa Faraggiana.

8 places to visit in Albissola Marina, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Albissola Marina in the guide with map and filters

Lungomare degli Artisti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Walk inaugurated in 1963 with a mosaic of 800 meters in ceramic stoneware made by twenty artists including Lucio Fontana, Aligi Sassu, Giuseppe Capogrossi and Emanuele Luzzati.

  • Where Lungomare, Albissola Marina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Pozzo Garitta

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Ancient little square still today home to furnaces and art studios, including Lucio Fontana.

  • Where Albissola Marina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Fornace Alba Docilia e Muro Raku

Museums · Themed

Fornace active from the early seventeenth century to 1982, today exhibition space with the permanent exhibition of Macachi, statues from the albisolesi nativity scenes; in front, the Wall Raku of 1200 tiles of 77 artists.

  • Where Via Stefano Grosso, Albissola Marina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Centro Esposizioni MuDA

Museums · Modern art

Venue of the Albisola Museum, with works by artists of the twentieth century including Lucio Fontana, Wifredo Lam, Agenore Fabbri and Emanuele Luzzati.

  • Where Via dell'Oratorio, Albissola Marina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Fondazione Museo Giuseppe Mazzotti 1903

Museums · Modern art · Viewpoint

Garden-museum with over 50 twentieth-century ceramic works, including the great Crocodile by Lucio Fontana of 1936.

  • Where Albissola Marina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Casa Museo Villa Jorn

Museums · House museums · Viewpoint

Residence of the artist Asger Jorn on the hills of the village, with paintings, ceramics, sculptures and murals.

  • Where Località Bruciati, Albissola Marina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di Nostra Signora della Concordia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the historic centre, it preserves a processional case of Maragliano and a Nativity in polychrome ceramic tiles of about two meters high 1576.

  • Where Centro storico, Albissola Marina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Villa Faraggiana

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

18th century villa with Italian garden of cedars, magnolias and camphors, and rooms with sculptures, stuccoes, majolica and nineteenth century furniture by the cabinetmaker Henry Thomas Peters.

  • Where Albissola Marina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
