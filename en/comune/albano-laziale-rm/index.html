What to see in Albano Laziale

Città dei Castelli Romani on the south-western shore of Lake Albano, built around the Castra Albana, the camp of the II Legion Partica wanted by Settimio Severo around 202 A.D.: there remain Porta Pretoria, the Cisternoni, the Amphitheatre and the Cellomaio Thermal Baths, together with the Tomb of the Orazi and Curiazi, the Round on the water lilies of Domitian and the Civic Museum of Villa Ferrajoli.

Museo Civico «Mario Antonacci» (Villa Ferrajoli)

Museums · Archaeological

Neoclassical villa of the Marquis Ferrajoli in a park, with pronao tetrastyle and pediment with the myth of Ceres that teaches agriculture in Triptolemo; the interiors are decorated by Giovan Battista Caretti in the early nineteenth century. Twenty-three floors rise from the Paleolithic (fauna of 300,000-30,000 years ago) to the Middle Ages: the finds of the palafittic village of the Macine submerged in the lake (age of the Middle Bronze, XVIII-XVI century B.C.), the Lazio civilization of the period "Albano" (XI-IX century B.C.), the polychrome head of a Latin warrior of the 5th century B.C., the finds of the villas of Cavallacci (head of Tiberio Gemello) and Pompeo Magno, and a collection of graphics from the 17th to the 20th century with many works by Piranesi. Sunday opens only the 1st and 3rd of the month.

Castra Albana e Porta Pretoria

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Camp wanted by Settimio Severo around 202 AD for about 6,000 legionaries of the II Legion Partica, on about 10 hectares: there are still sections of walls, towers and doors. The Porta Pretoria, main entrance from Via Appia, 36 m wide and 14 m high on two floors, was brought to light by the bombings of 1944; next door is the Porta Principalis Left.

  • Where Inizio di via Saffi, Albano Laziale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.albanolaziale.rm.it

Cisternoni

History and architecture · Archaeology

Large cisterns dug into the rock by the 2nd Partic Legion to supply the field with water: five naves on 36 pillars, capacity of about 10,000 m3, still functioning and fed by Roman pipelines.

  • Where Via Saffi, Albano Laziale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Anfiteatro Severiano

History and architecture · Archaeology

Built by the workers of the legion in the early third century, with an axis greater than 113 m and an estimated capacity between 15,000 and 16,000 spectators; in the Middle Ages it was used as a quarry and Christian cemetery. It is located behind the church of São Paulo.

Terme di Cellomaio

History and architecture · Archaeology

Baths built by Caracalla to reconcile with the legionaries of Albano: building on three floors in cement work covered with brick, in the Middle Ages transformed into stronghold and then into houses. Visitable by appointment.

Tomba degli Orazi e Curiazi

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Mausoleum of Republican age (I century BC) in pepper at the southern end of the village, with architecture similar to the Etruscan tombs of Volterra; despite the name is not of the legendary brothers and some scholars consider it a reconstruction of the tomb of Arunte wanted by the Arruntia family.

  • Where Estremità sud dell'abitato, Borgo Garibaldi, Albano Laziale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com · comune.albanolaziale.rm.it

Villa Doria-Pamphili e Villa di Pompeo Magno

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

Public park built on the eighteenth-century villa of Cardinal Fabrizio Paolucci, then of the Doria (the palace, bombed in 1944, was demolished in 1951); from Piazza Mazzini you can enjoy the view of the Roman countryside and the park. Underneath the park extends in part the about nine hectares of the villa of Pompeo Magno, built between 61 and 58 BC with the spoils of the mitridatic war and then passed to Augustus. In the summer it hosts the Albano Estate showcase and a bar with outdoor pool.

  • Where Piazza Mazzini, Albano Laziale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.albanolaziale.rm.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A circular church erected on the nymph of the villa of Domitian, consecrated in 1060 and restored in 1938; it has a Byzantine icon and a 13th century fresco attributed to Cavallini.

Chiesa di San Pietro Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It was created in a room of Roman thermal baths by Pope Ormisda (514-523), passed to the Savelli family in 1440; it preserves frescoes and a Romanesque bell tower of the 13th century.

Cattedrale di San Pancrazio

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

It rises on the ruins of the Constantinian basilica and is dedicated to St. John the Baptist and St. Pancrazio; behind the façade of 1772 by Carlo Buratti a crypt preserves columns and capitals of the original early Christian basilica. Current appearance since 1913.

Catacombe di San Senatore e Santa Maria della Stella

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Paid entry

Catacombs along the Appian Way to the fifteenth mile, under church and convent of Santa Maria della Stella (mid XVI century, current appearance since 1687), used for burials from the end of the third-beginning IV century; the central crypt was frescoed in successive epochs. Next to the cemetery of cholera, where in 1867 more than 500 citizens were buried. Free admission with voluntary contributions.

Lago Albano ed Emissario

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The deepest volcanic basin of Lazio, where fishing, sailing and canoeing are practised (the Italian canoeing team is trained there) and which can be turned by mountain bike to the remains of Alba Longa. The emissary, 1,425 m long and according to Tito Livio excavated in 398-397 BC, comes into the locality Le Mole.
