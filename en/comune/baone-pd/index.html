What to see in Baone

Municipality of the Euganean Hills between Este and Monte Cero: on the top of Monte Gemola stands Villa Beatrice, of the Province of Padua, with exhibitions on Euganean flora and fauna, and on the hill above the village remain the ruins of the castle of the da Baone, sold in 1183 to Obizzo d'Este.

Villa Beatrice sul Monte Gemola

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

On the summit of Monte Gemola, the villa is owned by the Province of Padua and is open to the public: inside two educational exhibitions on the flora and fauna of the territory of Euganean, whose visit takes 30-45 minutes. From the Brolo and the meadows you can see Mount Fasolo, Venda, Monte Rusta and Cinto Euganeo.

  • Where Monte Gemola, Valle San Giorgio, Baone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.baone.pd.it

Sources: comune.baone.pd.it

Giro del Monte Gemola

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of about 5 km, without difficulty and suitable for every season, just over two hours: it starts from Villa Mantua Benavides in Valle San Giorgio, rises between arable land, meadows and vineyards touching the sources of the Pissarotto del Tòrmene and the Fontanelle up to the saddle between the Rusta and Gemola mountains, and returns on CAI path. Along the road emerge the basaltic tuffs of the first volcanic phase of the Hills and the 'basalto a risetti of Mount Gemola , with cavities full of calcite, natrolite and analcime.

  • Where Villa Mantua Benavides, Valle San Giorgio, Baone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.baone.pd.it

Sources: comune.baone.pd.it

Ruderi del castello dei da Baone

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

In 1183 the Paduan family of the da Baone gave the castrum Baonis and the lordship to the Marquis Obizzo d'Este. The castle, in trachyte with a large tower that was also the residence of the lord, controlled the eastern entrance to Este together with the castle of Monte Cero and the fortification of Calaone; in the mid seventeenth century it was already destroyed and the walls transformed into vineyard. The ruins can be reached with a stone staircase along the path n. 8.

  • Where Monte Cecilia, Baone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.baone.pd.it

Sources: parcocollieuganei.com · comune.baone.pd.it

Sentiero del Monte Cecilia

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Trail No 8 of the Park, ring from the square of Baone: 5 km, 170 m of difference in altitude, about 1 hour and 30 minutes, tourist difficulty, of high interest for flora and panorama. Crossing hedges, an olive grove, rocks of latites with onion exfoliations and woods of black hornbeam, touches the ruins of the castle and of house Bolcato and the "vegri" , arid meadows with the Ruta patavina and various orchids.

  • Where Piazza di Baone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.baone.pd.it

Sources: parcocollieuganei.com · comune.baone.pd.it
