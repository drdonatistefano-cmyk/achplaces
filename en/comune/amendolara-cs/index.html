Museo archeologico nazionale «Vincenzo Laviola»

Museums · Archaeological

Dedicated to the physician and scholar of antiquity Vincenzo Laviola, it traces the population of the High Ionian from the indigenous cultures of Bronze and Iron to Greek colonization. It exhibits the collection Laviola (metal objects and protohistoric ceramics of the XII-VIII century BC) and the findings of the excavations of the Superintendence coordinated by Juliette de la Genière: necropolis of Paladino and Mangosa and archaic settlement of St. Nicholas (late VII-VI century BC, about 20 hectares), from which come over a thousand weights from the frame, some with female names in achean alphabet. Two multimedia stations in four languages.

the MiC card indicates "Closed" every day of the week; call the museum (+39 0981 911329) before the visit

Castello di Amendolara

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

In the Old Rion, with a triangular plan with a single surviving polygonal tower, probably of Norman origin; in the Swabian age was Domus Imperialis and in 1263 hosted Elena of Epirus and the court of Manfredi. Conquered by the Angioins in 1268, it passed among many feudal families until 1808, when the State sold it to the Marquis Gaetano Gallerano. Restored by privates, today it houses a restaurant; from the square with colonnade you can see the woods of Straface and the Dolcedorme Serra.
