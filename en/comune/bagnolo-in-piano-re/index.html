What to see in Bagnolo in Piano

Agricultural and industrial center about 8 km north of Reggio Emilia, with the fourteenth-century Torrazzo dei Gonzaga, the Art Nouveau theatre in Piazza Garibaldi and the Romanesque Pieve Rossa.

7 places to visit in Bagnolo in Piano, in the province of Reggio Emilia (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torrazzo

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Tower of the fortress built in 1354 at the behest of the Gonzaga to fortify the village, transit point on the road between Reggio Emilia and Mantua; the French destroyed the fortress at the beginning of the eighteenth century leaving only the tower, several times restored.

  • Where Bagnolo in Piano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: visitemilia.com

Teatro comunale Gonzaga - Ilva Ligabue

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Autonomous building in the central Piazza Garibaldi, in a simple late Art Nouveau style: designed in 1919 by engineer Aldo Govoni, it was inaugurated in 1924.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Bagnolo in Piano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: comune.bagnolo.re.it · visitemilia.com

Museo dei burattini Fondazione Famiglia Sarzi

Museums · Themed

It preserves the collection of Otello Sarzi (1922-2001), among the main innovators of the puppet theatre: about 3,500 objects used in about twenty shows, puppets of the Commedia dell'Arte, the masks of Emilia Sandrone and Fagiolino, about twenty barracks and theatres, archive and library.

materials available only for scholars waiting for the new setup

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi 2 (presso il Municipio), Bagnolo in Piano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitemilia.com

Chiesa di San Francesco da Paola e Piazza Garibaldi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The large parish church of San Francesco da Paola overlooks the central Piazza Garibaldi, where there are also theater and the Town Hall.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Bagnolo in Piano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: visitemilia.com · comune.bagnolo.re.it

Pieve Rossa

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Pieve in the homonymous hamlet, with a Romanesque plan with three naves with terracotta pillars and trusse roof, several times renovated.

  • Where Pieve Rossa, Bagnolo in Piano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: visitemilia.com

Collezione Salsapariglia

Local life and traditions · Motors

Private collection of Nello Salsapariglia in the hamlet of Pieve Rossa, with motorcycles, bicycles, tractors, engines, radio and gramophones from the end of the nineteenth century to the twentieth century.

  • Where Pieve Rossa, Bagnolo in Piano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: visitemilia.com

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry
