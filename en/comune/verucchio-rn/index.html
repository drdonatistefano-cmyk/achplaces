What to see in Verucchio

The cradle of the Malatesta and, many centuries before, the center of the Villanovian civilization: the Rocca del Sasso and the Archaeological Museum. Orange Flag since 2005.

3 places to visit in Verucchio, in the province of Rimini (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Rocca Malatestiana del Sasso

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On an isolated cliff, enlarged in 1449 by Sigismondo Malatesta: one of the best preserved Malatesta fortifications.

  • Where Verucchio
  • Entry With the Archaeological Museum €13; with the Fortress of San Leo €14,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: pass.visitrimini.com · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo civico archeologico villanoviano

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the former convent of Sant'Agostino, the furnishings of the Villanovian necropolis (IX-VII century BC), with very rare woods and fabrics.

  • Where Via Sant'Agostino, Verucchio
  • Entry €8 whole; with the Rocca €13 (guided tours); children up to 7 years free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: riviera.rimini.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Complesso di Sant'Agostino

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The former monastery of the Hermits of the 14th century which houses the archaeological museum, with the Augustinian church of the village.

  • Where Verucchio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · riminiturismo.it
