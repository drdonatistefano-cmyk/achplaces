What to see in Busca

Borgo at the foot of the hill, mentioned for the first time in a document of 6 December 1123, with historical center of medieval installation; on the hill the neo-Gothic Castle of Roccolo of the Marquis Tapparelli d'Azeglio with its romantic park, chapels frescoed by the brothers Biazaci and the Hermitage of Belmonte.

6 places to visit in Busca, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello e Parco del Roccolo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

A neo-Gothic castle with three towers built since 1831 by the Marquis Roberto Tapparelli d'Azeglio, Massimo's brother, as a summer residence; Queen Margherita stayed there for many summers. Lounges with frescoes and stuccoes and part of the furniture of the family of Azeglio; around a romantic park of about 50 hectares attributed to Xavier Kurten, with panoramic paths, cave, lake, fountains and monumental greenhouses for citrus. Open on the first and third Sundays of the month from May to October, 14:30-19:00, with guided tours.

  • Where Strada Romantica 17, Busca
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.castellodelroccolo.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: castellodelroccolo.it · visitcuneese.it

Eremo di Belmonte

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Born in the thirteenth century with a chapel dedicated to the Madonna and a female convent of Carthusians, it passed in the fifteenth century to the Dominicans and the early seventeenth century to the Camaldolese, becoming a pilgrimage destination. In the nineteenth century it was transformed into a villa with mythological paintings by Francesco Gonin; the bombings of the Second World War severely damaged the abbey church, of which the ruins remain. On the slope of the hill the alabaster quarries. Visits by reservation.

  • Where Eremo di Belmonte, Busca
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Cappella di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

On a sunny hill, built before the year 1000, it has one of the oldest facades of Cuneo and is frescoed by the brothers Biazaci, painters of Buschesi who in the fifteenth century realized more than thirty fresco works and on table in the territory.

  • Where Collina di San Martino, Busca
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Centro storico, Porta di Santa Maria e chiese della Rossa e della Bianca

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center preserves the medieval system with Romanesque, Gothic and sixteenth-century architectures; of the walls remains little, including the Porta di Santa Maria in corso Giolitti, built between the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries. To see the church of the Holy Trinity, called "la Rossa" and the Santissima Annunziata, "la Bianca" (the White), a Piedmontese baroque with a Greek cross designed by Francesco Gallo; recently restored the chapel of St. James.

  • Where Corso Giolitti, Busca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Colline di Busca, da Monte Pagliano a San Costanzo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The hills behind the village, with the Alps in the background and the plain in front, offer numerous hiking and cycling routes, including the one from Monte Pagliano to San Costanzo.

  • Where Monte Pagliano, Busca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
