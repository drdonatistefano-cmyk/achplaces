What to see in Massello

Municipality of the Upper Germanasca Valley linked to the Waldensian history: at Balsiglia a small museum in the former school recalls the siege of 1690 after the Glorious Repatriation, and from here passes the hiking path that traces the last stages of the return of the Valdesi from exile.

Museo storico valdese della Balsiglia

Museums · City history

On the ground floor of the former school hall of the village, a historic layout of the 1930s, realized for the 250th anniversary, recalls the return of the Valdesi to Piedmont between 1686 and 1690 and the siege of the Balsiglia of May 1690, when about a thousand Waldenses, returned the year before after three years of exile with the "Glorious Repatriation," resisted in this last trench. Audioguide available; management of the Fondazione Centro Culturale Valdese.

the MiC card (Add. 2021) indicates closed every day and sources do not report times: contact the Valdese Foundation

Sentiero del Glorioso Rimpatrio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Itinerary on trails and mule tracks that retrace the last three stages of the return of the Valdese from exile in Switzerland in 1689, through the Valdese valleys, with also naturalistic and landscape value; touches Massello with the museum of Balsiglia, Prali and Salza di Pinerolo. Trail card and GPS track downloadable from the site of the Ecomuseo.

  • Where Località Balsiglia, Massello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: ecomuseominiere.it · turismotorino.org
