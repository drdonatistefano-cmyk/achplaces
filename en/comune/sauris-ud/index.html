What to see in Sauris

Carnic village between 1000 and 1400 meters, on the shores of the artificial lake of the Lumiei valley, chosen by the Ministry of Tourism to represent Italy at the Best Tourism Villages UNWTO 2022: the church of San Lorenzo in Sauris di Sopra, the sanctuary of Sant'Osvaldo with the altar of Michael Parth in Sauris di Sotto, the ethnographic center and the historiographic center.

6 places to visit in Sauris, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Lago di Sauris

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Artificial lake in the Lumiei valley, whose waters change tonality with the seasons; you can reach from the road 52 in Ampezzo going up the gorge of the Lumiei Valley, crossed by a bridge 150 meters above the stream, or from the pure pass. Canoes, sup and skates are rented in summer.

  • Where Val Lumiei, Sauris
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it

Santuario di Sant'Osvaldo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In Sauris di Sotto, it conserves an altar with doors in carved wood, golden and painted by Michael Parth by Brunico in 1524, numerous ex-vote and a relic of Saint Osvaldo, venerated for centuries by pilgrims.

  • Where Sauris di Sotto, Sauris
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Chiesa di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In Sauris di Sopra, it has a bell tower with a high cusp covered with scalds; inside an important altar of the sixteenth century, work of Michael Parth.

  • Where Sauris di Sopra, Sauris
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Centro etnografico S'Haus van der Zahre

Museums · Themed

In a rural building of stone and wood (former barn with nineteenth-century barn), typical example of Alpine architecture, exhibits work tools and objects of the local families and hosts temporary thematic exhibitions. It is part of the CarniaMuseum network; times to ask at the museum (0433 86262) or at the tourist office.

  • Where Sauris di Sopra 3/A, Sauris
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Centro storiografico Museo di Sant'Osvaldo

Museums · City history · Free entry

It exhibits parchments, furnishings, vestments, sacred goldsmithery, ex-vote and a crib, with objects from the 15th to the 19th century coming from the church of Sant'Osvaldo. Open Tuesday 9.30-11.30, Wednesday and Saturday 15-17.

  • Where Sauris di Sotto, Sauris
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Lateis e passo Pura

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From Lateis, reachable with a challenging climb, you can enjoy the panorama; between Ampezzo and Sauris you pass the Pura pass (1425 m) with the Tita Piaz refuge next to, and the Tiziana Weiss nature trail climbs up to almost 2000 meters.

  • Where Lateis, Sauris
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: italia.it · turismofvg.it
