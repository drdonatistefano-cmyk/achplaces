What to see in Romano di Lombardia

City in the middle of the Bergamo plain, home of tenor Giovan Battista Rubini, with the medieval old town, the Visconti fortress with four towers and the museum in the neoclassical palace of the singer.

4 places to visit in Romano di Lombardia, in the province of Bergamo (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca viscontea

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Attested from the 13th century on the north-east side of the walls, it owes its name to the transformation of the Visconti; it was residence of feudal lords and Venetian podestà and store of food and ammunition. Quadrangular plan with four corner towers of different eras, blackbirds tail of swallows and squares, courtyard with Venetian symbols; the moat, fed by resurgences, today is a meadow, with sixteenth-century terragli on the north and west sides. Guided tours of about an hour and a half on the first Sunday of the month, even on towers and walkways, not accessible to people with mobility difficulties.

  • Where Piazza della Rocca 3A, Romano di Lombardia
  • Entry €5, free up to 6 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · visitbergamo.net · comune.romano.bg.it · italia.it

Museo memoria della comunità

Museums · City history · Free entry

Collection of weapons and relics of the world wars and of the twentieth century, which can be visited free of charge during the morning guided tours of the Fortress.

  • Where Piazza della Rocca 3A, Romano di Lombardia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.romano.bg.it · italia.it

Museo Giovan Battista Rubini

Museums · House museums · Free entry

Founded in 1872 at the will of the widow in the neoclassical palace that tenor Rubini (1794-1854), the first Elvino of Bellini's Sonnambula, had built, with six ionic columns, frescoes, stuccos and Venetian mosaic floors. In the five rooms on the ground floor the billiard room with private memories and the Pirate room with the memories of the career; among the works the Last Supper by Giovan Battista Moroni, works of the Caniana and Trento Longaretti. Open on Sunday 10-12.

  • Where Via Adelaide Comelli Rubini 2, Romano di Lombardia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitbergamo.net · italia.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center retains the medieval facility with churches, houses, noble palaces and gates; the name of the city would come from the "castrum romanum" on which it stood.

  • Where Romano di Lombardia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
