What to see in Mairago

In the plain about six kilometers south of Lodi, two kilometers from the South Adda Park, Mairago preserves the Cascina Grazzanello, typical closed court of Lodi documented already in the cadastral map of 1723, today didactic and eco-friendly farm with the old mill, and the Provincial Astronomical Observatory inaugurated in 1999 in the former kindergarten.

Ecomuseo della Cascina Grazzanello

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Lodigiano's closed courtyard farmhouse, already present in the cadastral map of 1723, with the crenellated dovecote tower and the entrance porch documented in 1897 and the stable of the beefs dated 1888; belonged among others to the Talentes and Madonini. Restored since 1981 with original construction techniques, since 1993 it is a farm-teaching farm on about 60 hectares: the former barn with barn houses the museum of agricultural and domestic tools, and you can visit the ancient peasant houses, the former stables, the wood oven, the granaries, the threshing floor and the old water mill on the roggia, certified as rice pile already in 1723.

opening by reservation, rates for pupils with guided tour and workshop (Visit Lodi, tel. +39 0371 487261)

  • Where S.S. 9 Via Emilia, 26825 Mairago
  • Entry €7 per pupil half day, €14 full day
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlodi.it

Sources: visitlodi.it · lombardiabeniculturali.it

Osservatorio Astronomico Provinciale

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

Inaugurated on November 14, 1999 in the premises of the former nursery school in Mairago thanks to the collaboration between the Municipality, Province of Lodi and Fondazione Cariplo, it is managed by the Astrofili Messier 42 Group, born in 1992 in Lodi Vecchio. It includes a dome with the main telescopes and a CCD room for real time viewing and photographing, a videoconference room and two bedrooms (25 beds). The evenings begin with a presentation to the video projector of 30-50 minutes, followed by a visit to the dome; with clear sky you can also see with small telescopes in the courtyard.

open about two Saturdays a month from 21, calendar on the website of the Astrofili Messier 42 Group; groups and schools by reservation (Visit Lodi)

  • Where Piazza Roma 2, 26825 Mairago
  • Entry €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlodi.it

Sources: visitlodi.it
