What to see in Cansano

Country at 835 meters on a hill between Maiella and Monte Rotella, home to an information center and excursions of the Majella National Park and stop of the Railway of the Parks; a few kilometers you will find the archaeological park of Ocriticum, italic-Roman sacred area with three temples.

Parco archeologico di Ocriticum

History and architecture · Archaeology

An ancient settlement founded by the Peligni in the locality Tavotto-Pantano, depopulated around the ninth century for the Saracen raids that pushed the inhabitants on a cliff further east. It is above all a sacred area with three cult buildings arranged on a two-level plateau at the foot of the Mitra Hill: an Italic temple of Hercules, a sanctuary of Jupiter and a small sacellus of a female deity, of which the perimeters have been carefully reconstructed. It can be reached about 5 km from the village on an asphalted country road and is close to the train stop of the Railway of the Parks, active since summer 2023 on weekends.

Centro di documentazione Ocriticum

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 2004 together with the Archaeological Park, it exhibits the finds of the excavation campaigns at the foot of the Mitra Hill, the sacred area of the Italic temple of Hercules, the sanctuary of Jupiter and the sacellus of a female deity. According to the MiC card is open in July and August (9-13 and 16.30-19.30), in the rest of the year by reservation.

Centro storico e fontane

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Italia.it describes the old town as picturesque; the village, already a feud of various families and autonomous from Campo di Giove only from 1904, preserves the memory of the ancient medieval castle (Castrum Cansani) and fountains along the streets. After the earthquake of 1933 collapses and landslides from the highest wall pushed the village to move further downstream.

  • Where Centro storico, Cansano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcomajella.it

Chiesa Madre di San Salvatore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches
