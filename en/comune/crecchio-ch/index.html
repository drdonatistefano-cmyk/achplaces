What to see in Crecchio

A small medieval village in the Chietina hills, built around an ancient Norman tower and dominated by the 12th century Ducal Castle, which in September 1943 hosted the royal family fleeing Rome and today houses the Byzantine and Alto-Medieval Abruzzo Museum.

2 places to visit in Crecchio, in the province of Chieti (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Ducale De Riseis

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The 12th century castle overlooking the medieval village, with four factory buildings and corner towers around a quadrangular courtyard open on a loggia; lost the defensive role became residence of royals and nobles, and on September 9, 1943 the royal family found hospitality in escape from Rome occupied. Restored in the 1970s, it is linked to the legend of the ...Torre dell'ulivo" (Torre dell'ulivo) where the new owners De Riseis planted an olive tree in peace to drive out ghosts.

Museo dell'Abruzzo bizantino e altomedievale

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the Ducal Castle, exhibits the excavations conducted from 1988 to 1991 by the Archeoclub of Crecchio with the Superintendence in the Roman-Byzantine Villa of Vassarella, recovered from a cistern used as a landfill that has exceptionally preserved the wood materials, together with ostrogoth and Longobard finds: they tell the Byzantine life in Abruzzo and the Gothic wars between the end VI and the beginning of the VII century, and the trade with the East and Egypt. The Alberto Carlo Fraracci room hosts an Etruscan collection donated in 1995. Seasonal hours according to the MiC card (from 15 June to 15 September every day 10-12 and 16-20), otherwise by reservation.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Crecchio
  • Entry €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
