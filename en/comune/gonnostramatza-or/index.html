What to see in Gonnostramatza

Marmilla Country divided by the Rio Mannu into two districts united by three bridges: in the parish church of San Michele the retablo dell'Annunziata signed by Lorenzo Cavaro in 1501, in the former Granatic Mountain the multimedia museum Turcus and Morus on the barbaric incursions, in the countryside the church of São Paulo of the disappeared village of Serzela.

Museo multimediale Turcus e Morus

Museums · Themed

In the former restored Granatic Mountain, it tells of a thousand years of encounters and especially clashes between two worlds, from the 8th to the 18th century, with the incursions of the barbarian pirates at the center; it takes its cue from the inscription in the country church of São Paulo that recalls the destruction of the nearby village of Uras by the Barbarossa in 1515. Multimedia panels, interactive video portraits and life-size holograms, reconstructions of coastal towers and bridges of ships, dioramas, models and vintage weapons, digital reproduction of the retablo of Lorenzo Cavaro and a section on the disappeared village of Serzela. Accessible to the blind and disabled, with educational workshops.

  • Where Ex monte granatico, Gonnostramatza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Rebuilt between 1680 and 1715, perhaps by the same workers of the cathedral of Ales, on a Gothic-Aragonese church documented in 1524, of which remain the late dogotic apse with cruising vault and part of the sacristy; restored after the Second World War, it has eighteenth-century bell tower with dome of colored majolica. It preserves the retablo dell'Annunziata, signed and dated 1501 by Lorenzo Cavaro under the mantle of Our Lady, coming from the church of São Paulo di Serzela, and a wooden crucifix of the sixteenth century.

  • Where Piazza del rione grande, Gonnostramatza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa campestre di San Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient parish church of the deceased village of Serzela, dating back to the 13th century and rebuilt in the 17th century; a stone in the country sanctuary recalls the destruction of the nearby village of Uras by pirates led by Barbarossa.

  • Where Località Serzela, circa 2 km a sud-ovest del paese, Gonnostramatza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Centro storico e ponti sul Rio Mannu

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Ancient houses in traditional Campidano architecture; the Rio Mannu divides the village into the "grande" and "small" districts, connected by three bridges.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
