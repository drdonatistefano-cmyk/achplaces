What to see in Rimella

The municipality is 1,182 metres in the Alta Valsesia Natural Park, made up of 16 terrace hamlets: it is the oldest Walser colony in Valsesia, inhabited since the 13th century, and preserves the Filippa Museum, the first civic museum in Piedmont (1836), the Walser Ethnographic Museum and the parish church of San Michele.

Museo antropologico «Giovanni Battista Filippa»

Museums · Themed

Born in 1836 from the donation of the collections of Giovanni Battista Filippa (1778-1838), a soldier of Napoleonic troops in the Spanish countryside, is indicated as the first museum of civic ownership established in Piedmont. Filippa called it the "cabinet of curiosity and rare things": in a building of 1415 it conserves minerals, shells, stuffed animals, books, coins, utensils and parchments, enriched then by the painter Maurizio Antonini, the parish priest Gaudenzio Cusa and donations of inhabitants and emigrants. Visits by reservation (tel. 0163 55203).

Museo etnografico walser

Museums · Themed

Set up in the 17th century Casa Eredi Vasina, it tells of the Walser colonization and the daily life of a time with reconstructed environments, original objects and panels: crafts, mountain agriculture, religious and ritual customs, family structure and the Walser language, the "titsch" . Visits by reservation (tel. 0163 55203).

Frazioni walser di Rimella

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The 16 hamlets are placed on a terrace on the slopes; the Walser colony, the oldest in Valsesia, settled from the thirteenth century probably through the filling of the Dorchetta from the Anzasca Valley, and language and traditions are still alive. Due to the scarcity of larch, the Walser architecture is less developed than elsewhere and houses have closed loggias. Among the villages: Church, Sella, San Gottardo, Villa Inferiore, Roncaccio Superiore. In the past gold, nickel and cobalt were extracted.

  • Where Rimella (VC)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Sentiero 548 Rimella - Bocchetta di Campello

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Historical connection with the ancient hamlet of Campello Monti in Valle Strona, part of GTA, Via Alpina, Sentiero Italia CAI, Grande Traversata Walser and an itinerary of the Resistance. We leave shortly after the chapel of the Madonna del Rosario near San Gottardo and climb, with 748 meters of difference in altitude and medium difficulty (2-3 hours), touching the Posa dei Morti, the Alpe Selletta, the Alpe Pianello with the CAI Refuge Città di Borgomanero and the Bocchetta di Campello (1,924 m), with the rock altar dedicated to Father Giovanni Gallino.

  • Where Partenza presso San Gottardo, Rimella
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page visitvalsesiavercelli.it
