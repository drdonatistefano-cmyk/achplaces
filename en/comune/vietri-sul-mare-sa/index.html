What to see in Vietri sul Mare

The first pearl of the Amalfi Coast, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1997, famous for its ceramics: the majolica dome of the Cathedral of St. John the Baptist, the Municipal Mosaic Villa, the Ceramic Museum of Villa Guariglia in Raito and the village of Albori, among the most beautiful villages in Italy.

9 places to visit in Vietri sul Mare, in the province of Salerno (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage

At the highest point of the historic centre, it is the symbol of the ceramics of the Vietrese: the seventeenth-century church has the dome covered with blue and yellow majolica.

  • Where Centro storico, Vietri sul Mare
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage

Centro storico e botteghe della ceramica

Local life and traditions · Crafts · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Viuzze, shops and colorful houses with the workshops of the master ceramicists who paint by hand; the tradition dates back at least to the Middle Ages and was renewed in the twenties of the twentieth century by German craftsmen with a new style inspired by daily gestures. Among the buildings symbolizes the Fabbrica Solimene, designed in the fifties by the architect Soleri.

  • Where Centro storico, Vietri sul Mare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it

Nature and landscape · City parks · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

  • Entry Free

Museo della Ceramica di Villa Guariglia

Museums · Themed · UNESCO World Heritage

The Provincial Museum of the Vietrese ceramics occupies the Torretta and the ground floor of Villa Guariglia in Raito, with terraced park: it exhibits the most representative pieces of the local production from the seventeenth century, between votive plates and domestic holy waterworks, up to the second half of the twentieth century.

  • Where Via Nuova Raito, Raito, Vietri sul Mare
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Marina di Vietri e spiagge

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

The Marina has fine sand and shallow and sloping seabed, suitable for families, with free and equipped beaches; near the beach La Crestarella dominated by the tower, the Schiarata and the free beach of Cancelluzzo, pebbles and sand, reachable only from the sea (for example by pedal boat from the Marina).

  • Where Marina di Vietri, Vietri sul Mare
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Albori

History and architecture · Villages · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The fishing village of white houses on the hills of Vietri, among the most beautiful villages in Italy: in the center the church of Santa Margherita with frescoes of Neapolitan school including those of Francesco Solimena (feast on July 20), votive chapels along the streets and the square where in the evening you will find yourself. About 200 steps down to two small beaches.

  • Where Albori, Vietri sul Mare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
