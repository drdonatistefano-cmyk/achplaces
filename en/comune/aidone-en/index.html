What to see in Aidone

Country of the Enne hinterland linked to the ancient Morgantina, a Sicilian and Greek city excavated by the University of Princeton since 1955: in the Archaeological Museum, in the former convent of the Capuchins, the goddess of Morgantina has returned; in the village the churches of San Domenico, Sant'Anna and Santa Maria La Cava with the Adelasia Tower and the ruins of Castellaccio.

Area archeologica di Morgantina

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Ancient Sicilian and Greek city brought to light since the fall of 1955 by the mission of Princeton University: excavations document over a thousand years of history, from the foundation in prehistoric age to the decline in Roman imperial age. The most visited area conserves remains from the middle of the V to the end of the first century BC: the Agora, the Theatre, the residential districts, a paved road and furnaces for ceramics.

  • Where Contrada Morgantina, Aidone
  • Entry €6, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitsicily.info · parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it

Museo archeologico regionale di Aidone e Dea di Morgantina

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the former convent of the Capuchins of the seventeenth century, with the adjoining church of San Francesco used as auditorium, eleven rooms tell Morgantina from the Bronze Age to the Roman-Republican era: ceramics of the Morgets, the Serra Orlando room with busts of Persephone and ex-vote, the silvers of Morgantina and the acrolites of Demeter and Kore. The symbol piece is the goddess of Morgantina (425-400 BC, 2,24 m high, face and arms in Paro marble), passed by J. P. Getty Museum of Malibu and returned; also exposed the head of Hades ...Barbablu's terracotta polychrome.

  • Where Largo Torres Truppia, Aidone
  • Entry €6, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · cultura.gov.it · visitsicily.info

Castellaccio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the highest point of the village remain the ruins of a fortress built by the Arabs as an outpost and observation point, then transformed into a castle; today it is a cluster of ruins with panoramic views of the valley.

  • Where Aidone (parte alta del paese)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Chiesa di Santa Maria La Cava e Torre Adelasia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In Piazza Cordova, dominated by the Adelasia Tower of Norman times, is the destination of the votive pilgrimage of the Feast of St. Philip the Apostle, the black saint, in the night before May 1.

  • Where Piazza Cordova, Aidone
  • Entry Free
