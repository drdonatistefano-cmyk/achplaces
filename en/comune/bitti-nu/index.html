What to see in Bitti

A stone village of northern Barbagia, more than 500 meters in a valley 40 km from Nuoro, home of the tenor singing (UNESCO intangible heritage) told in a multimedia museum; in the territory the nuragic village-sanctuary of Romanzesu.

4 places to visit in Bitti, in the province of Nuoro (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villaggio-santuario nuragico di Romanzesu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the plateau of Poddi Arvu, in a forest near the Tirso springs about 13 km from the village: about seven hectares entirely granite with a hundred huts, a well temple with a corridor of over 40 meters leading to a basin surrounded by six steps amphitheatre, two megaron temples, a rectangular temple and a spiral labyrinth. Well and labyrinth date back to the 13th-IXth century BC; the name comes from a Roman settlement of the II-III century A.D. Admission fee.

  • Where Località Poddi Arvu, SS 389, Bitti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo multimediale del Canto a Tenore

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Opened in 2005, it is dedicated to the Sardinian polyphonic singing proclaimed by UNESCO as a masterpiece of the oral heritage: in four rooms with headphones and four totems in a circle allow you to listen to the voices bassu, contra, mesu goche and goose separated or together, with music from the different areas of the island. It is also a polyphony research center.

  • Where Via Mameli 52, Bitti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Museo della civiltà contadina e pastorale

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In a manor house in the center, in the same seat of the museum of tenor singing, it tells the dairy tradition, in particular of pecorino cheese, and the ancient gestures with which the typical products are still produced.

  • Where Via Mameli 52, Bitti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Borgo antico e Fontana de su Cantaru

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

First mentioned in 1170 as Bitthe, the village has stone houses arranged amphitheater and connected by narrow alleys, developed around the parish church of San Giorgio martyr; in the center there is the legendary Fontana de su Cantaru.

  • Where Centro storico, Bitti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
