What to see in Cetona

Borgo della Valdichiana Sienese at the foot of Mount Cetona, whose caves have been inhabited since the Neanderthals.

3 places to visit in Cetona, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Civico per la Preistoria del Monte Cetona

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Tools in stone, remains of hunted animals and finds that tell the continuous home of the territory from prehistory; a small museum known for the educational setup.

  • Where Strada Provinciale della Montagna di Cetona 2, Cetona
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Parco Archeologico Naturalistico di Belverde

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the slopes of Mount Cetona the caves inhabited in prehistory and then by saints and hermits, visitable in the park linked to the Museum for Prehistory.

  • Where Monte Cetona, Cetona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Monte Cetona

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The relief that separates the Valdichiana from the Val d'Orcia, an ideal destination for hiking and cycling.

  • Where Monte Cetona, Cetona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
