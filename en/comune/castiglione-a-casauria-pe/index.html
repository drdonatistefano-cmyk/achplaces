What to see in Castiglione a Casauria

Country of the high valley of Pescara, with origins in a dress centre, famous above all for the Benedictine Abbey of San Clemente in Casauria, founded in the ninth century on the Via Claudia Valeria and rebuilt in the twelfth century by the abbot Leonate, which houses the Antiquarium ...Pier Luigi Calore.

Abbazia di San Clemente a Casauria

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry

Benedictine Abbey on the Via Claudia Valeria near the Pescara, wanted by Emperor Ludovico II: the Chronicon Casauriense gave its foundation in September 871 (873 second Abruzzo Turismo), with the relics of Pope San Clemente transferred from Rome with the consent of Adriano II. Damaged by the Saracens and the earthquake of 990, it was rebuilt by Abbot Guido in 1025, reconsecrated by Grimaldo in 1105 and rebuilt in the current forms by Abbot Leonate in the second half of the 12th century with Cassino workers. The three-arched portico precedes the bronze portal of 1191 to 72 tiles (48 original survivors according to Abruzzo Turismo); inside three aisles ambone of the twelfth century, candelabra for the Easter candle, fourteenth-century ciborium and crypt. Closed on Monday; open on Tuesday and Saturday 9-19.30, other days 9-13.30 (Sundays all from April to September, only 1a, 3a and 5a from October to March); with bad weather can stay closed, better call (085 8885162).

  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Antiquarium «Pier Luigi Calore»

Museums · Archaeological

In the abbey, dedicated to the intellectual Pier Luigi Calore, scholar of the Abruzzo territory between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, exhibits archaeological and medieval evidence. Born at the end of the nineteenth century as a stonestone for the work of Calore and Antonio De Nino, it collects gravestones and other finds from the excavations of the nearby Roman settlement of Interpromium; according to the Touring many Roman inscriptions are exposed in the courtyard of the abbey waiting for the new museum, which will also show the findings of recent excavations at the church.

Moscatello di Castiglione

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

White wine from an ancient native grape, produced in a sweet (passed) or dry version; among the wineries of the village Tenuta Cecolo IX in Vicenne, which also produces Montepulciano and Cerasuolo d'Abruzzo, Pecorino and sweets Moscatello (tel. 085 7998193; visits and tastings to verify).
