What to see in Cassino

The city rebuilt at the foot of Montecassino, the abbey founded by Saint Benedict in 529 and destroyed in 1944: below, the archaeological area of Casinum with theatre, amphitheatre and mausoleum, and the Fortress Janula on the Gustav Line.

5 places to visit in Cassino, in the province of Frosinone (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Abbazia di Montecassino

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Founded by Saint Benedict of Norcia in 529 on the site of an ancient tower and temple of Apollo, destroyed by the bombings of 15 February 1944 and rebuilt; the museum exhibits codes, choral books, vestments, works and paintings of the eighteenth century Neapolitan.

  • Where Montecassino, Cassino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: direzioneregionalemuseilazio.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo archeologico nazionale «G. Carettoni» e area archeologica di Casinum

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Museum opened in 1966 on Roman structures, with the "Hero" of Cassino and a sword enrolled among the oldest attestations of the name "Roma" (Rome), the area preserves the urban stretch of the Via Latina with Porta Campana, the amphitheatre attributed to Umida Quadratilla, the mausoleum and the theatre.

  • Where Cassino
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Rocca Janula

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Castle wanted by the abbot Aligerno in the 10th century to defend Montecassino, with the pentagonal tower of 20 meters of the 12th century; key point of the Gustav Line, devastated in the war, restored and opened in 2015.

  • Where Via Montecassino, Cassino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Terme Varroniane

Local life and traditions · Spas

Terme surrounded by greenery on the site of the villa of the writer Marco Terenzio Varrone, with oligomineral waters.

  • Where Cassino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Ninfeo Ponari

History and architecture · Archaeology

Roman Domus with two decorative phases, around the middle of the first century BC and around 100 AD, near the theatre.
