Dune e spiaggia di Piscinas

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Golden sand dunes modelled by the maestral, up to 60 meters tall, that push for kilometers inland; the beach is about seven kilometers long and from 2018 a stretch of about 800 meters is naturist beach. Clear seabed that descends quickly, with the wreck of an English ship about 200 meters from the shore; sea often moved, frequented for windsurfing, kite and surfing even in winter. Secular, lentisque and olive ginepri; on the shore nest the sea turtles.

last stretch of access on dirt and sandy roads

  • Where Piscinas, Arbus
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Villaggio minerario di Ingurtosu

History and architecture · Abandoned villages

Arbus fraction about 10 km from the village, in the valley of Is Animas that descends towards Piscinas, now a ruined village and desert. The silver, lead and zinc mines were active from 1855 to 1968, with the greatest development under British society Pertusola and Lord Thomas Brassey; seven villages with 900 rooms housed up to 2,500 people. The granite direction of the castle remains, called \"castle" (1870, Neo-Medieval style), the church of Santa Barbara (1916) and the villas Wright and Ginestra.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Miniera di Montevecchio

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Mining complex between Arbus and Guspini, part of the Park Geominerario of Sardinia: the first concession was given in 1848 to Giovanni Antonio Sanna and the activity lasted until the final closure of 1991; in 1865 it was the most important mine of the Kingdom with 1,100 workers. Four itineraries of visit: the Palazzina of the direction (1870-77) with the blue room and the church of Santa Barbara, the well Sant'Antonio, the workshops with the foundry of 1885 and the Piccalinna route with the winch of the late nineteenth century.

  • Where Montevecchio, Arbus e Guspini
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Museo minerario di Montevecchio

Museums · Science and university

Building built at the end of the 1940s for the geological service of the Montevecchio company, with the mineralogical-petrographic laboratory and the collection of samples of the deposit and other mines, then enriched by samples from all over Italy; acquired by the Municipality of Arbus and renovated in 2004 for visits.

MiC card (updated to March 2021) is closed every day

  • Where Piazza Rolandi, Montevecchio, Arbus
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Museo del coltello sardo

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Created by the master knifemaker Paolo Pusceddu next to his workshop "Arburesa": the history of Sardinian knifemaking from the Neolithic, with the reproduction of a obsidian dagger, sixteenth-century pieces, horn handles carved with Sardinian fauna, works of knives of Villacidro, Dorgali and Pattada and the reconstruction of the smith's workshop, Pusceddu made in 1986 the largest knife in the world (3.35 m, 80 kg), which was passed in 2002 by a handle wrench of 4.85 m and 295 kg. Saturday and Sunday by reservation.

  • Where Via Roma 15, Arbus
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.museodelcoltello.it

Sources: museodelcoltello.it · sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Monte Arcuentu

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Relief of 785 meters of tuffs and basalts with spires and towers, about 11 km from Arbus near the former mines of Montevecchio: from the summit the view extends over the Gulf of Oristano, the Costa Verde and the Campidano. An easy path taken care of by the CAI starts from the former mines; on top the ruins of a castle documented since 1164, then Vallombrosan monastery. Lecci, spot, deer, wild boar, royal eagle and pilgrim falcon.

  • Where Monte Arcuentu, Arbus
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Spiagge della Costa Verde: Scivu, Marina di Arbus, Portu Maga, Funtanazza

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Along the coast of Arbus follow the beach of Scivu, with about three kilometers of dunes, Marina di Arbus, about two kilometers long, Portu Maga and Funtanazza, up to Capo Pecora, a diving destination at the southern end.

  • Entry Free
