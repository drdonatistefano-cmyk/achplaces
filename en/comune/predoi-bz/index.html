What to see in Predoi

The last municipality of Valle Aurina, with the hamlet of Casere: here copper was extracted for over five hundred years, today told by the Provincial Museum of Mines in the gallery of Sant'Ignazio; at the end of the valley the church of Santo Spirito, consecrated in 1455, and a 20 km path between farms and antiquities.

Museo provinciale delle miniere - Miniera di Predoi

Museums · Themed

Along the Rötbach copper was extracted for over 500 years: first citation in 1426, definitive abandonment in 1971, museum opened in 1996. A mine train takes more than a kilometer into the tunnel of Sant'Ignazio, excavated from 1761 and arrived at the deposit after 43 years; two ring routes with reconstructions show tools, machinery and life of the miners, and a preserved copper vein can be touched. Even today, cementation copper is obtained with the help of bacteria. Visits only by reservation, last entry at 15, closed on Mondays not festive and in winter until Easter.

  • Where Vicolo Hörmann 38a, Casere, Predoi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.ahrntal.com

Sentiero dei minatori

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Didactic path of 4.4 km and 556 m of difference in altitude (between 1,515 and 2,062 m) that climbs from the gallery of Sant'Ignazio to the Cross of the Red Valley: it touches the gallery of San Giorgio, excavated shortly after 1528, the remains of the Jakober Stuben at the gallery of San Giacomo, where it was found mineral already in the fifteenth century, and the gallery of San Guglielmo, the "forziere" of the mine; the landscape is largely man's work, excavated also with gunpowder in the eighteenth century.

  • Where Galleria di Sant'Ignazio (partenza), Casere, Predoi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.ahrntal.com

Sources: suedtirol.info · ahrntal.com

Chiesetta di Santo Spirito

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the present form of the fifteenth century, it was consecrated in 1455 by Cardinal Nicolò Cusano; it rises in an alpine basin at the end of the valley considered a place of energy and peace, with benches for the rest and one of the best photographic views of the area. There passes the Path of Health (6,6 km, about 2 hours) which also crosses the historical area of the mines, the house of the miners of Mösern with its chapel and the deviation to the Röt waterfall.

  • Where Casere, Predoi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.ahrntal.com

Sources: ahrntal.com · suedtirol.info

Chiesa di San Valentino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1489 and consecrated in the presence of Bishop Konrad Reichard; the bell tower was completed 62 years later. Independent parish since 1891; next door, the old canonical church (Altes Widum) of 1495.

  • Where Predoi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.ahrntal.com

Sources: ahrntal.com

Sentiero dei masi e delle antichità

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

A pedestrian ring of about 20 km, to be done in two or four stages with bus stops, starting from the Götsch farm after the gorge (Klamm), whose road was built in 1814 for the mine. Along the route historic farms such as the Steger (1618 about, 1821 stube), the Innerbichler with Gothic barn and 1612 stube, the late Dogotic Brugger, the Nothdurft (1554), the Großbach mill (1705) and the Auer mill, the chapel of St. Anthony wanted by the director of the mine and the Verweserhof with coats of arms of 1696.

  • Where Maso Götsch (partenza), Predoi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.ahrntal.com

Sources: ahrntal.com
