What to see in Zungoli

Irpino village with its medieval structure lying on the slope, dominated by the Norman castle of the 11th century; under the old town there are caves excavated in the tuff where the Podolic caciocavallo matures, and the village is crossed by the Regio Tratturo Pescasseroli-Candela.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town is spread along the slope following the original medieval plant and culminates in the Norman castle, to which you climb for a ramp with the municipal coat of arms in porphyry stone.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello normanno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Norman manor of the 11th century, passed to the Siginulfo in the 13th century; quadrangular plant with four angular cylindrical towers of the Aragonese era, one of which destroyed by the earthquake of 1456. In the 16th century the Loffredos transformed it into a noble residence; since 1825 it has been the Susannas, the Marquises of Sant'Eligio, who made it a country residence.

  • Where Via Castello 7, Zungoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Convento di San Francesco e chiesa dell'Incoronata

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The convent of the Friars Minor Reformed rises at the foot of the village, outside the walls; it dates back to the second half of the XVI century, on the foundations of the church of San Cataldo collapsed after the earthquake of 1456, and was largely rebuilt after the earthquake of 1930. In the adjacent little church, recently restored, the Madonna dell'Incoronata, patron saint of shepherds and tractures, is kept.

  • Where Zungoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Grotte nel tufo

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Under the old town there are caves dug into the tuff, on several levels and connected to each other, dating back to the Byzantine era and used in time as a refuge and cellar; today there matures the podolic caciocavallo, aged in cave for at least two years.

  • Where Centro storico, Zungoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Regio Tratturo Pescasseroli-Candela

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient tratturo of transhumance crosses the village and is frequented for horse riding and trekking; on the first Sunday of September Zungoli celebrates the Festa della Trashumanza with tasting and sale of typical products.

  • Where Zungoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
