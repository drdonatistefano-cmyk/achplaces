What to see in Tagliacozzo

Borgo della Marsica on the slopes of Monte Civita, with Piazza dell'Obelisco, the Doge's Palace of the Orsini with frescoes, the church and cloister of San Francesco and, outside the village, the sanctuary of Santa Maria dell'Oriente with the Eastern Museum.

Piazza dell'Obelisco

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Already Piazza da' Piedi, it is the center of the village from the eighteenth century and you enter it from Porta dei Marsi; the porticos were closed in 1810 by order of Gioacchino Murat and around 1825 the fountain with the obelisk replaced an ancient stone seat.

  • Where Piazza dell'Obelisco, Tagliacozzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · turismo.comune.tagliacozzo.aq.it · italia.it

Palazzo Ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Founded by the Orsini and already habitable at the beginning of the fourteenth century; the windows of the first floor with polygonal columns are of the second half of the fifteenth century, under Count Roberto Orsini, and the Colonna then replaced the Orsini coats of arms with their own. The cap contains late-Gothic frescoes with stories of Christ.

  • Where Centro storico, Tagliacozzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.tagliacozzo.aq.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa e chiostro di San Francesco

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry

Church completed in 1270, with a single nave with oval vaults, portal to pointed sixth and rose window; the convent dates back to the first decades of the thirteenth century and the cloister, the only surviving earthquake of 1915, has frescoed lunettes with the life of St. Francis taken from the major legend of St. Bonaventure.

  • Where Largo San Francesco, Tagliacozzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismo.comune.tagliacozzo.aq.it · italia.it

Santuario di Santa Maria dell'Oriente e Museo orientale

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Sanctuary of pilgrimages near Tagliacozzo, existing already in the sixteenth century, with the thirteenth-century icon of the Madonna dell'Oriente, according to the tradition brought by Ravenna; the Eastern Museum holds ex-vote, Byzantine icons, sacred furnishings and Middle Eastern coins.

  • Where Via Oriente, Tagliacozzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Teatro Talia

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Former Benedictine convent used as a theatre in 1686 and renovated in 2002, with three orders of stages and over 200 seats.

  • Where Tagliacozzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa dei Santi Cosma e Damiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque church once linked to the Benedictines of Montecassino, in the district of the Doge's Palace; it houses the venerable painting of the Holy Face.

  • Where Centro storico, Tagliacozzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · turismo.comune.tagliacozzo.aq.it · italia.it

Mura, porte e rocca sul Monte Civita

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The center rises on the slopes of Monte Civita, where the ruins of a medieval fortress with four bastions remain; the walls of 1410 originally had five doors, including Porta Romana, Porta Valeria, Porta San Rocco and Porta dei Marsi.

  • Where Monte Civita, Tagliacozzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.tagliacozzo.aq.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
