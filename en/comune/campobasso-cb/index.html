What to see in Campobasso

The capital of the Molise in two parts: the medieval village that rises towards the Monforte Castle, with Romanesque churches and the Sannitico Museum, and below the Muratian city of the nineteenth century with the Cathedral and Villa De Capoa.

16 places to visit in Campobasso, in the province of Campobasso (Molise). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Monforte

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the hill that dominates the city, with square plan with cylindrical towers at the corners; it bears the name of the fifteenth-century leader Nicola Monforte, count of the city after the earthquake of the mid fifteenth century. From the top the view reaches Majella and the Table-table; on the same hill remain ruins of a Sannitic fortification.

  • Where Campobasso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · molise.camcom.gov.it

Museo Sannitico - Museo archeologico nazionale

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Born in 1881 and since 1995 in the eighteenth-century Palazzo Mazzarotta: on the ground floor weapons of the Bronze Age and ambre of the Iron Age, at the first the Sannites with votive statues of Hercules, at the second the romanization with materials from Sepino and the tombs of Bulgarian knights of the Plain of Bojano, with a warrior and his horse barred in silver.

  • Where Via Chiarizia 12, Campobasso
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · molise.camcom.gov.it

Museo dei Misteri

Museums · Themed

It exhibits the thirteen "ingenious processionals" created by Paolo Saverio Di Zinno (1718-1781), who parade through the city on the day of Corpus Domini, with costumes of the fifties, vintage films and a screening room; edited by the Association Mysteries and Traditions.

  • Where Via Trento 3, Campobasso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: molise.camcom.gov.it · italia.it

Città murattiana

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The nineteenth-century districts under the castle, expansion beyond the walls desired by Gioacchino Murat, king of Naples: regular blocks, green squares, public offices and shops; on Piazza Gabriele Pepe overlook the Government Palace, the Bank of Italy and the Banco di Napoli.
