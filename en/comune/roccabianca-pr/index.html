Rocca (Castello) di Roccabianca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Built between 1450 and 1465 by Pier Maria Rossi for Bianca Pellegrini, from which the name is: plain castle with walls, central courtyard, corner towers and high mast; on the noble floor frescoes and stuccoes. The cycle of the Stories of Griselda from the Decameron, which decorated the first tower, is today at the Castello Sforzesco in Milan. After the Pallavicino and Rangoni (1524) he passed in 1901 to the Facchi and then to the knight Mario Scaltriti, who restored it and opened it to the public. Guided tours at fixed times on weekends from March to November and on the first weekends in December; closed January to February.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi 5, Roccabianca
  • Entry €10, reduced 7-12 years €5, groups and passport Castles of the Duchy €8
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · italia.it

Museo della Distilleria

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

In the ancient cellars of the castle, it tells the story of liqueurs, spirits and balsamic vinegar; it includes the seasoning salt of the culatello.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi 5, Roccabianca
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Il Mondo piccolo

Museums · Themed

In the former elementary school, it is a path on landscapes, environments and work between the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries; the main sections are dedicated to Giovannino Guareschi, with videos, and to the cooperative experience of Faraboli. Restaurant and bookshop; recommended for families with children.

  • Where Strada Villa 18, Roccabianca
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · italia.it
