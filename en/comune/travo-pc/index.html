What to see in Travo

Small centre of Val Trebbia, 176 metres, gathered around the castle Anguissola, home to the archaeological museum, and the millennial parish church of Sant'Antonino overlooking the river; in Sant'Andrea the park of the Neolithic village, one of the most important prehistoric sites of northern Italy, and above the valley the ophiolitic rocks of Pietra Perduca and Pietra Parcellara.

Museo civico archeologico nel Castello Anguissola

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Founded in 1997 in the castle of the Anguissola, the family that ruled Travo for over five centuries, tells about the ancient population of the average Val Trebbia, where the reconnaissance of the group "La Minerva" have identified 175 sites: paleolithic instruments of about 100,000 years ago, mesolithic hunting tools, Neolithic finds of the culture of the Vases in Bocca Square from the village of Sant'Andrea, Ligurian testimonies, Celtic and Etruscan and Roman inscriptions on the cult of Minerva. Open on weekends, with evening openings in July and August.

  • Where Piazzetta Trieste 16, Travo
  • Entry €5, reduced €4 (6-13 years and over 65 €2), cumulative with the park €7
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website parcoarcheologicoditravo.it

Sources: parcoarcheologicoditravo.it · cultura.gov.it · visitemilia.com

Parco archeologico del Villaggio neolitico di Sant'Andrea

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

State park of about one hectare, born in 2006 on the west bank of the Trebbia to protect a village of about 6000 years ago discovered in the eighties and excavated since 1995: exposed houses, wells, walls in pebbles and ovens, with three neolithic huts rebuilt in real scale in 2010. Since 2021, a museum area has rebuilt part of the high medieval necropolis of 117 tombs (V-VIII century). It is a Museum of Quality of the Emilia-Romagna Region and is part of the European EXARC network.

closed from November 1 to March 31; only accessible with guided tour, departures every hour on weekends

  • Where Sant'Andrea, Travo
  • Entry €5, reduced €4, cumulative with the museum €7
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website parcoarcheologicoditravo.it

Sources: parcoarcheologicoditravo.it · sabap-pr.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it · visitemilia.com

Pieve di Sant'Antonino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Pieve of the middle of the 11th century, recently restored, in the heart of the village with a balcony-square overlooking the river, suggestive at sunset; according to the tradition Sant'Antonino, patron saint of Piacenza, would have been martyred in Travo in 303.

  • Where Centro storico, Travo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitemilia.com

Oratorio di Santa Maria a Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small church north of the town, in the hamlet of Sant'Andrea, which preserves outside traces of a building of the middle of the 11th century and inside frescoes of the 15th and early 16th century.

  • Where Sant'Andrea, Travo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitemilia.com

Pietra Perduca e Pietra Parcellara

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

The two peaks that dominate the valley, the Perduca Stone (659 m) and the Parcellara Stone (836 m), formed by ophiolitic rocks called the "rocks of the devil."

  • Where Bobbiano, Travo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com

Sources: visitemilia.com

Oratorio sulla Pietra Perduca

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Oratory of the fifteenth century set in the serpentinous rock of the Stone Perduca, reachable by a path following the sign for Bobbiano.

open to the public in particular on the day of Saint Anne and the Assumption

  • Where Pietra Perduca, Travo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com
