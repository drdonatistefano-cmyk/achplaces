What to see in Rieti

The capital of Sabina, the "Umbilicus Italiae" of Varrone: the Roman city under the streets of Rieti Subterranea, the bridge over Velino, the Cathedral and the Palazzo Papale, the Flavio Vespasian Theatre; around the reserve of the lakes Lungo and Ripasottile, the Terminillo and the Franciscan sanctuaries.

Palazzo Papale (Vescovile)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Built in 1283 on an existing building and preceded by the monument to St. Francis of 1927, it is famous for its portico with scenic vaults and a giant crib; in the Hall of Audiences, since 2005, the art gallery of the Diocesan Museum.

  • Where Via Cintia 87, Rieti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com
