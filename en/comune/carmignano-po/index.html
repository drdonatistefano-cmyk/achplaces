Villa Medicea La Ferdinanda

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

The "villa of the hundred chimneys" built by Bernardo Buontalenti for Ferdinando I between 1596 and 1600, with rectangular plan with fortifications at the corners, above the village of Artimino.

  • Where Artimino, Carmignano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
