What to see in Spello

A Roman village on the slopes of Subasio, right from Baglioni until 1583: the Baglioni Chapel frescoed by Pinturicchio, the Roman gates and Villa Fidelia.

4 places to visit in Spello, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 6 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Santa Maria Maggiore e Cappella Baglioni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The collegiate church of the XII-XIII centuries houses, on the left side of the nave, the Baglioni Chapel frescoed in 1501 by Bernardino di Betto called the Pinturicchio.

  • Where Centro storico, Spello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Villa Fidelia

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Villa of the sixteenth century, extensively transformed and enlarged in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, with the park and gardens recommended for a walk.

Porta Consolare

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The entrance door to the village, the first stage of the official itinerary that from the Roman origins leads to the Middle Ages and the Renaissance.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Cappella Tega

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches
