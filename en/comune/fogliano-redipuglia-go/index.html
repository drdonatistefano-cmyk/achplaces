What to see in Fogliano Redipuglia

Municipality of Karst isontino that houses the military shrine of Redipuglia, inaugurated in 1938 on the slopes of Mount Six Busi with the remains of over one hundred thousand Italian soldiers of the Great War, the Shrine museum and the Austro-Hungarian cemetery with 14,550 fallen.

Sacrario militare di Redipuglia

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Designed by the architect Giovanni Greppi and sculptor Giannino Castiglioni and inaugurated on 19 September 1938 on the slopes of Mount Six Busi, it collects the remains of 100,187 soldiers who fell in the area: about 40,000 known in the 22 steps in karst stone with names on bronze plates, about 60,000 unknown on the top near the three crosses and the chapel, which became church in 2014. The complex includes the tombs of the Duke of Aosta and five generals and the Heroic Way with 38 bronze tombstones of the battles of the Karst and the Isonzo; on the nearby Colle di Sant'Elia stood the first Cimitero degli Invitati with over thirty thousand corpses. Restored in 2021, always open.

Museo del Sacrario militare di Redipuglia

Museums · Themed

Two small halls behind the last step of the Shrine display war materials, relics, photographs of the first shrine on the Colle di Sant'Elia, documents and paintings of Ciotti decorating the first tomb of the Duke of Aosta, with reconstructions of military posts. Not far, at an altitude of 89, an observatory with the plastic of the front line at dawn of 24 October 1917. It depends on Onorcaduti (Ministry of Defense); the MiC card (2021) indicates Tuesday-Saturday 9-17 with pause 12-14.
