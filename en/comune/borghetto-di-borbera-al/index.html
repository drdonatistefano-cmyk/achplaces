What to see in Borghetto di Borbera

The town of Val Borbera with castles and churches scattered among the hamlets: the thirteenth-century tower of Molo Borbera, the village of Torre Ratti with the fourteenth-century Casa del Fante, the church of Persi with a altarpiece of Luca Giordano and the Strette di Pertuso, canyon of about 4 km excavated by the stream Borbera.

Le Strette di Pertuso

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Oligocene gorge (about 37-26 million years ago) about 4 km long between Persi and Pertuso, on the border with Cantalupo Ligure: a canyon a hundred meters deep and about ten wide, crossed by the Borbera stream. Woods of chestnuts and oaks in the initial section, bare rocks in the final one; deer, deer, birds of prey, blackbird and martin fisherman are seen. From the SP 140 there are many paths to the gorge; the view from above is from Monteggio, and other paths lead to the abandoned villages of Rivarossa and Avi.

  • Where Strada Provinciale 140, frazione Persi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.borghettodiborbera.al.it

Sources: comune.borghettodiborbera.al.it

Castello di Molo Borbera

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

13th century tower in good condition, only rest of the abbey castle of the monastic complex of Pier, at the top of the hill above the village; inside, a cistern of about 30 cubic meters and holes for rain water. From the top the view stretches over Monte Leco, Monte Tobbio, the sanctuary of the Guardia di Gavi and the Castle of Sorli. Visible from outside, free parking in front of the tower.

  • Where Via Castello, frazione Molo Borbera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.borghettodiborbera.al.it

Sources: comune.borghettodiborbera.al.it

Santa Maria Assunta in Persi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Three-nave church of the lords of Torre de' Ratti, with the coat of arms of Rati Opizzoni in counterfacade and decorations of the twenties by Clemente Salsa. It preserves the Assumption of the Virgin of Luca Giordano (1690), a Madonna with Child and Saints (1633) attributed to a pupil of the Fiasella, the seventeenth-century altar of San Carlo, a wooden choir of the nineteenth century, the Assumption of Carlomagno Parodi and a crucifix perhaps of the workshop of Maragliano.

  • Where Frazione Persi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.borghettodiborbera.al.it

Sources: comune.borghettodiborbera.al.it

Casa del Fante e Museo del giocattolo

Museums · Themed

Building of the fourteenth century with ceilings and wooden beams and facade with two arched windows with stone posts, south of the tower-door of the ancient village of Torre Ratti. Since 30 October 2016, the Toy Museum has hosted 250 unique pieces of Pop Art inspiration made of recycled materials and donated by the painter Enrico Debenedetti. Visit by reservation.

  • Where Frazione Torre Ratti, Borghetto di Borbera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.borghettodiborbera.al.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.borghettodiborbera.al.it

Cappella di San Colombano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small chapel in the green, restored by the Municipality in 2006, on the site of a more ancient church mentioned in 1531; interior entirely frescoed with the Madonna in the center and Sant'Antonio, sign of the influence of the monastery of Bobbio. In the area traces of a Roman rustic villa (1st century BC-VI century AD), of a late ancient necropolis with cappuccino tombs and two medieval burials.

  • Where Regione San Colombano, Borghetto di Borbera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.borghettodiborbera.al.it

Sources: comune.borghettodiborbera.al.it

Oratorio di Liveto

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Small oratory with two frescoed rounds on the façade: St. Anthony Abbot and St. Louis Gonzaga who received the first communion from St. Charles Borromeo.

  • Where Frazione Liveto, Borghetto di Borbera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
