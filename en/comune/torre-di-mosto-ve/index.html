What to see in Torre di Mosto

Municipality of the reclamation plain of the eastern Veneto; in the hamlet of Sant'Anna di Boccafossa two museums tell the territory: the Museum of peasant civilization, born from the community of reclamation, and the Museum of the landscape with Venetian painters of the twentieth century.

Museo del paesaggio

Museums · Modern art

The museum collects works of 20th century painters working mainly in Veneto, with a permanent collection dedicated to the regional landscape: coast and lagoons, reclamation plains, hills and villas of the mainland up to the Alps; during the year it hosts temporary exhibitions of painting.

Museo della civiltà contadina di Sant'Anna di Boccafossa

Museums · Themed

Founded by a group of volunteers to document the origins of the community, linked to the reclamation, it collects about 2,800 pieces of tools, equipment, machines and tools for earth processing, with a photographic archive; the route also shows how the peasants adapted and invented tools. Every year in July the harvest festival with agricultural machinery exposed at the entrance. Visits on request, with audio guides and guided tours.
