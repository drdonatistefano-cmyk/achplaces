What to see in Medole

At the foot of the morainic hills between Castiglione delle Stiviere and Castel Goffredo, Medole was marquisato dei Gonzaga di Castiglione from 1602 and on June 24, 1859 he saw the first battle of the battle of Solferino and San Martino; to visit the Gonzaghesque tower with the Civic Collection of Art, the parish with a canvas of Tiziano, the Pieve di Santa Maria and the former convent of the Annunciata.

Torre Civica e Civica Raccolta d'Arte

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

The collection is housed in the Gonzaghesque Civic Tower, a rare example of a tower with three masonry sides and one open, with an eighteenth-century annex and an exhibition hall. Born at the end of the seventies on the initiative of fans linked to the Pro Loco and donated to the Municipality in 1995, it brings together paintings, prints, graphics and sculptures mainly of Mantua artists from the early twentieth century to today, including Alessandro Dal Prato, Arturo Cavicchini, Giuseppe Lucchini and Mimì Buzzacchi Quilici, and hosts temporary exhibitions. Open Monday, Wednesday and Thursday 15-18.30, Friday 9-12.30 and 15-18.30, Sunday 9-12.30 and 15-19 (Sunday by reservation, except the first of the month); free entry and guided tours, accessible route.

  • Where Piazza Castello / Piazza Marconi, Medole
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church overlooks the large square of the center, built around the ancient castle. It preserves the altarpiece of Titian with the risen Christ that appears to the Mother.

  • Where Centro storico, Medole
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · turismo.mantova.it

Pieve di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque single nave church with hut roof, semicircular apse and bell tower leaning behind the apse; the walls are in river pebbles with brick and stone inserts, and the two terracotta portals of the south side suggest a dating after the mid-twelfth century. Inside are still well preserved frescoes from the early fourteenth century.

  • Where Via Giacomo Matteotti, Medole
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · in-lombardia.it · turismo.mantova.it

Ex Convento dell'Annunciata

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Born from the donation of a private oratory with house and vegetable garden by Guglielmo Luchino Venturella of Castel Goffredo, it was entrusted in 1455 to the Hermits of St. Augustine with a brief by Pope Callisto III. Enclosed by a high wall of about a kilometer, it is an example of late Lombard Gothic in terracotta: there are still parts of the two Gothic cloisters, living rooms with vaulted ceilings and drawers and the small church, where in 1885 Bishop Giuseppe Sarto, future Pius X, confirmed the celebration of the Mass.

visits only by reservation; the complex hosts weddings and private events

  • Where Via Annunciata 19, Medole
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · turismo.mantova.it
