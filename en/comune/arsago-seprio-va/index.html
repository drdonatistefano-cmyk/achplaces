What to see in Arsago Seprio

Borgo del Varesotto inhabited by Golasecchiani, Insubri, Romani and Longobards: it preserves the Romanesque complex of the basilica of San Vittore and the octagonal baptistery of San Giovanni, an archaeological museum with a Lombard necropolis visible in the garden and, in the woods, the Pollini Swamp of the Ticino Park.

Basilica di San Vittore e Battistero di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry

Romanesque complex on a site already occupied by a palaeo-Christian cult building, whose fragments are visible outside the apse. The basilica, with three naves with three apses, columns and Corinthian capitals of re-use and wooden trusses, was liberated from the successive additions in the restorations of Luca Beltrami (1890-1892); in the bell tower is walled at half height a votive stone dedicated to Jupiter. The octagonal baptistery of the mid-12th century has a sixteen-window-pierced dome, a matroneo on columns with capitals with plant and animal motifs, Roman epigraphs and a millionth of re-use, and there is exposed the epigraph of the young Arnolfo, who died in 893. Baptistery open Saturday and Sunday, the other days on request (Pro Loco, tel. 340 6433070).

Civico Museo Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

Born in 1983 in the middle school, after the discovery in 1972 of a Lombard necropolis during the expansion of the building; closed for flooding in 1991, it reopened definitively in 1998. Three rooms with finds from the Bronze Age to the Renaissance (also from the Roman necropolis of the vicus), a paleontological room with the collection G. C. Politi (more than 400 pieces, including fossils of Solnhofen and Bolca), a stone-stone under the porch and in the garden the Longobard graves, indicated as the only Lombard necropolis preserved on sight in Italy. Open Saturday 14.30-18.30 and Sunday 10-12 and 14.30-18.30; closed in August, from 20 to 31 December, from 1 to 6 January and at Easter. Accessible.

Palude Pollini

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Special Area of Conservation of the Natura 2000 network in the Ticino Park, rich in biodiversity: it is the site chosen by the Park for the repopulation of the gloomy pelobate, the rare and protected "rospo della vanga" about 4 cm long. A ring of about 4 km starts from the SS oratory. Cosma and Damiano, cross the historic center and enter the woods of farnie, chestnuts, silvestri pine trees and holm oaks, alternating agricultural, wooded and humid areas; dirt ground with muddy stretches, not suitable for people with motor disabilities. The Park also offers the ring with guide on reservation.
