What to see in Suzzara

Cittadina dell'Oltrepò Mantua inhabited by Etruschi and Romans and then dominion of Bonacolsi and Gonzaga until the eighteenth century; it is known for the Suzzara Prize, born in 1948, whose Gallery preserves works of the realism season, and preserves the Civic Tower, the only rest of the ancient castle.

Galleria del Premio Suzzara

Museums · Modern art

Permanent Museum of municipal property with works acquired by the Suzzara Prize, established in 1948 by Dino Villani and the mayor Tebe Mignoni with the support of Cesare Zavattini, on the idea of equaling art to the products of work: the winners also received cheese, animals and eggs. The collection documents the season of realism with works by Guttuso, Sassu, Birolli, Romagnoni, Zigaina and Ligabue; the Prize is renewed every two years and temporary exhibitions alternate during the year.

  • Where Via Don Bosco 2/a, Suzzara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it

Torre civica (Torre dell'Orologio)

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The only remains of the ancient castle of Suzzara, remembers the family of Matilde di Canossa and is the symbol of the historical past of the city; the provincial source has a date of it not unique (XIII or XIV century).

  • Where Piazza Castello, Suzzara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it
