What to see in San Felice sul Panaro

Centre of the Lower Modena with the 14th-century Estense fortress with a square plan, seriously affected as the historic center by the 2012 earthquake; surroundings with the botanical garden La Pica in San Biagio in Padule and the seventeenth-century church of Rivara, along the Modena-Finale Emilia bike path.

6 places to visit in San Felice sul Panaro, in the province of Modena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca Estense

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Made by the Marquis Obizzo d'Este in 1340, with Bartolino da Novara, and restored in 1406 for Niccolò III by the same military architect, then consolidated in 1421: example of medieval architecture with quadrilateral plan with corner towers and courtyard with covered loggia staircase. It is the largest monument of the municipality.

Damaged by the 2012 earthquake, in restoration: visitable only from the outside

  • Where Via Mazzini, San Felice sul Panaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo archeologico «Giuseppe Venturini»

Museums · Archaeological

Archaeological exhibition with finds of the Bronze Age and Romans and Renaissance ceramics, transferred from the Rocca to via Mazzini 15.

  • Entry To be checked

Teatro comunale

History and architecture · Historic theatres

On June 9, 2025 the restoration and restoration of the municipal theatre, planned in about two years for 8,37 million euros financed largely by the Region, were delivered. At the end the theatre will have 468 seats.

In restoration since June 2025, expected duration about two years

  • Where San Felice sul Panaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: comune.sanfelice.mo.it · visitmodena.it

Piazza Matteotti e chiese del centro

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

On the square the Palazzo del Monte di Pietà (XVIII century), the eighteenth-century church of Santa Croce and the monument to the Fallen of Aldo Roncaglia; the 17th-century Clock Tower collapsed in the 2012 earthquake. The parish church of San Felice, fifteenth century with neoclassical façade, preserves a wooden crucifix of the sixteenth century, a terracotta bust of the Christ died of the fifteenth century and a altarpiece of Bernardino Loschi (1500).

Historic and parish center severely damaged by the 2012 earthquake; the church was inaccessible according to the source

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, San Felice sul Panaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it

Chiesa della Natività della Vergine a Rivara

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Facade of the first decades of the seventeenth century with four semi-pillars and tympanum, where in 1958 Giovanni Botti of Domodossola painted the Assumption during the restoration. The bell tower, about 26 meters high and detached from the church, was reopened in 2019 after the earthquake damage of 2012.

  • Where Rivara, San Felice sul Panaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Giardino botanico La Pica

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

In San Biagio in Padule, 21,000 square meters with over a thousand plant species of all continents, in 81 flowerbeds along paths that form a spiral of about three kilometers.

  • Where San Biagio in Padule, San Felice sul Panaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
