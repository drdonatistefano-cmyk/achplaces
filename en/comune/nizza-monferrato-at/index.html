What to see in Nizza Monferrato

The capital of Barbera in Monferrato UNESCO, with the "Campanon" symbol of the city and the museums of wine and taste.

3 places to visit in Nizza Monferrato, in the province of Asti (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre civica «El Campanòn»

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

More than 30 meters, called "Campanon" for the large bronze bell; symbol of the city on the Town Hall, you climb up to the top.

  • Where Piazza del Comune, Nizza Monferrato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it

Palazzo Crova – Museo del Gusto ed Enoteca Regionale

Museums · Themed

The palace houses the Museum of Taste, the Art'900 collection by Davide Lajolo and the Regional Enoteca of Nice.

  • Where Nizza Monferrato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it

Museo Bersano delle Contadinerie

Museums · Themed

Museum of peasant tools and ancient prints on wine that covers four centuries of wine history.

  • Where Nizza Monferrato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it
