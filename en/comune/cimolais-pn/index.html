What to see in Cimolais

Country 652 meters high in Valcellina, of late-Roman origins and donated in 762 to the abbey of Sesto al Reghena, seat of the Natural Park of the Friulian Dolomites; it is the door of the Campanile of Val Montanaia and of the Cimoliana Valley, with the visitor center of the Park and the fauna area of Pianpinedo.

5 places to visit in Cimolais, in the province of Pordenone (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro visite del Parco «La fauna del Parco»

Museums · Science and university

On the ground floor of the Park's headquarters, the exhibition ...The fauna of the Park, opened on May 24, 2008 and renewed over time, is divided into four sections with interactive and multimedia path, the interactive floor ...Sensitive Floor and walls painted by Mario Alimede. Open all year round on weekends (10-12 and 14-17/18.30) and every day from the end of July to the beginning of September and at the end of the year.

  • Where Via Roma 6, Cimolais
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.parcodolomitifriulane.it

Sources: parcodolomitifriulane.it · italia.it

Parco faunistico di Pianpinedo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry

Faunistic area entirely fenced of 35 hectares in the Pinedo Plain, between Cimolais and Claut, on the edge of the Park, where there are specimens of the local fauna; with a visitor center dedicated to the senses of nature, the reconstruction of a house of woodcutters and a restored lime furnace. 9-18 hours (last entry into the courtyard at 17); dogs not allowed.

seasonal opening: Sundays from mid-April to mid-October and every day from July 26 to August 31 (calendar 2026)

  • Where Piana di Pinedo, Cimolais
  • Entry €6, reduced €4 (only visitor center €2)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcodolomitifriulane.it · italia.it

Campanile di Val Montanaia

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Rock spire about 300 meters high, climbed for the first time in 1902, in the territory of the Park above Cimolais, destination of hikers and mountaineers.

  • Where Val Montanaia, Cimolais
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Sentiero dei Mulini e Val Cimoliana

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

3-4 hour ring from the Park's headquarters, recommended from March to October and without any points of support: remains of water mills and sawmills with panels, the Mugolio Foresteria (former Mugo pine oil distillery), a former brick furnace today breeding of camosciate goats, the Compol and Lesca bridges above a small gorge, the terraced meadows and the stables of Bresin and the view on the Cima dei Preti, the highest peak of the Park.

  • Where Parcheggio della sede del Parco sul torrente Cimoliana, Cimolais
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: app.parcodolomitifriulane.it · italia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches
