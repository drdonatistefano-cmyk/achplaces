What to see in Poggiardo

Salento Country a few kilometers from the sea, with the Mother Church of Sant'Antonio di Padova with its eighteenth-century façade, the Doge's Palace of Guarini and the Byzantine crypt of Santa Maria degli Angeli, whose original frescoes are in the museum of the Municipal Villa; in the village of Vaste the Warriors Park and the Museum of Messapian civilization.

Cripta di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church-Crypt of Greek rite rose around the thousand under the current Via Don Minzoni, abandoned in the fifteenth century, found by chance in 1929; three apsed aisles with stone iconostasis partly collapsed. The detached frescoes are in the museum, while since 1999 the crypt houses copies of fireproof polystyrene in the original positions. You go down with a metal staircase from the side of the Doge's Palace.

  • Where Via Don Minzoni, Poggiardo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.poggiardo.le.it · italia.it

Museo degli affreschi della Cripta di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated on June 12, 1975 with Aldo Moro, in the Villa municipale exhibits the original frescoes of the crypt, detached and restored at the Central Institute of Restoration in Rome and mounted in the original layout, with the reconstruction of the plant: among these the Madonna and Child between the archangels Gabriel and Michael (first half of the XII century), St George on horseback, Christ blessing on throne, the saints Cosma and Damiano and the Archangel Michael.

  • Where Villa comunale, Piazza Episcopo, Poggiardo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.poggiardo.le.it · italia.it

Museo archeologico della civiltà Messapica di Vaste

Museums · Archaeological

In the Baronial Palace of Vaste, documents the Messapian city from the 4th century BC with sets of sarcophagus tombs (craters, bacils, strigili, the "trozzelle" of the female burials), a capital with funerary column rosettes, a treasure of 150 silver states and 17 lusorias in ivory of Roman age; chronological route from the places of archaic worship to the Middle Ages and virtual reconstruction on large screen.

  • Where Piazza Dante, Vaste, Poggiardo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it

Parco dei Guerrieri di Vaste

History and architecture · Archaeology

Great archaeological area with numerous remains of Messapian civilization: traces of the walls of the IV-III century BC of Bastae, center mentioned by Pliny the Elder and Ptolemy and destroyed around 1160 by William the Malo.

  • Where Vaste, Poggiardo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Collina dei Santi Stefani

History and architecture · Archaeology

To the north of Vaste, the excavations brought to light a early Christian basilica built on a martyrion of the fifth century, surrounded by a vast necropolis with tombs of various eras; near is the crypt of Saints Stefani.

  • Where Vaste, Poggiardo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Piazza di Vaste, chiesa parrocchiale e Palazzo Baronale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Pleasant square with the 18th-century parish church, which behind the main altar preserves a large nineteenth-century mache group of the Saints Martyrs, and the Baronial Palace of the sixteenth century built on previous structures, with next to a more ancient tower.

  • Where Piazza Dante, Vaste, Poggiardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it
