What to see in Buccheri

Borgo dei Monti Iblei at 820 meters, the highest and coldest municipality in the province of Syracuse: the remains of the Norman castle on Monte Castello, the scenic staircase of Sant'Antonio Abate, the Neviere del Monte Lauro and the gorges of the Stretta.

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Founded in 1212, it dominates the village from the top of a staircase built in 1911; it has three aisles, it preserves two paintings by William Borremans (1728) and a sixteenth-century altarpiece.

  • Where Buccheri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Chiesa Madre di Sant'Ambrogio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Main church dedicated to the patron saint of St. Ambrose: above the main altar there is a wooden crucifix of the sixteenth century by unknown author; it also contains a seventeenth-century canvas of St. Michael and a painting of St. Ambrose.

  • Where Buccheri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maddalena e monastero di San Benedetto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church and monastery date back to 1453 and survived the earthquake of 1693; in the church a statue of a Gagini.

  • Where Buccheri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Neviere del Monte Lauro

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the plateau of Monte Lauro (986 m) the lava stone, cave, dome or square snowshoe remains, where from the seventeenth century to the end of the Second World War it collected and conserved snow, used first in medicine and then for sorbets. Rural routes with views of Mount Etna and the Calabrian coast.

  • Where Altopiano di Monte Lauro, Buccheri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info

Gole della Stretta

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Rocky gorges dug by the river San Leonardo, where canyoning is practiced by swimming or canoeing and, in some stretches, climbing; on the walls there are artificial caves, castelluccia tombs of the Bronze Age.

  • Where Gole della Stretta, Buccheri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Andrea

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry
