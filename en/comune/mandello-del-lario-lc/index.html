What to see in Mandello del Lario

Country on the eastern branch of Lake Como at the foot of the Grigne, historical seat of the Moto Guzzi founded in 1921, with the museum in the historical factory; in the village the archpriest church of San Lorenzo with the Museum of Sacred Art, the sanctuary of the Blessed Virgin of the River and, in Maggiana, the Tower of Barbarossa, while above the village passes the Path of the Wanderer with the frescoed church of San Giorgio.

Museo Moto Guzzi

Local life and traditions · Motors · Paid entry

Set up in the historic factory where the brand was born in 1921, with a new wing of 3,000 square meters from which you can see the current assembly lines, exhibits more than 160 series motorcycles, sports, prototypes and restored engines. Among the unique pieces the first bike built by Carlo Guzzi in 1919, the only one signed G.P. (Guzzi-Parodi), the Otto Cylinders 500 of 1957 by Giulio Cesare Carcano, Norge of 1928, the Guzzi 4V of 1924, the 350 World Champion in 1955 and the V7 record of the 19 record speeds in Monza in 1969; sections on cinemas, races, travels and military and police vehicles.

  • Where Via Emanuele Vittorio Parodi 63, Mandello del Lario
  • Entry €12, reduced €10
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa arcipretale di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The present building dates back to the 17th century and replaced a previous church; the Romanesque bell tower, of the 12th century, was one of the defensive towers of the castrum. Inside the 17th century baroque decoration with carved ciborium and canopy altar in polychrome wood, and an organ Serassi of 1764 sculpted and gilded; in front stands the ancient Chapel of the Octagonal Dead.

  • Where Piazza Giovanni XXIII 12, Mandello del Lario
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo d'Arte Sacra di Mandello del Lario

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated on December 7, 2019 next to San Lorenzo, in the ancient headquarters of the confraternity of the regulations of Saint Martha, it makes the heritage of the archpriest accessible. It exhibits a fresco of the Crucifixion attributed to a Lombard painter between the late fifteenth and early sixteenth centuries, the fifteenth-century cross astile (the most representative work), a Madonna with Child sculpted from the fifteenth century, the canvas of the Circumcision by Francesco Zucco, liturgical wallpapers and ex-vote painted by the church of Santa Maria Nascente di Debbio.

  • Where Piazza Papa Giovanni XXIII, Mandello del Lario
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Santuario della Beata Vergine del Fiume

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In the lower part of the village, it was born from a miracle of the seventeenth century: after a flood of Meria the waters deposited a lot of wheat, carried away by a mill, in front of an image of the Madonna with the Child painted on the wall of a vineyard. To welcome the image the archpriest Giovanni Battista Sambuca promoted the construction with the offerings of the community; the interior has a rich Baroque decorative apparatus.

  • Where Via Beata Vergine del Fiume 3, Mandello del Lario
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Torre del Barbarossa e Museo etnografico

History and architecture · Towers · Paid entry · Viewpoint

A square tower in Maggiana, 40 arms high, with ogival windows and slits overlooking the lake: according to tradition in 1158 the Mandelli family hosted Frederick Barbarossa, who from here would observe the naval battles between Milan and Como; a granite plaque with date 1158, found in 1828, was lost. Owned by the Amici di Maggiana Group from 1976, it houses an ethnographic museum with objects and images from the end of the nineteenth century to the middle of the twentieth century on the life and work of the hamlet.

  • Where Via San Giorgio 8/c, Maggiana, Mandello del Lario
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.mandello.lc.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Medieval church with façade, single nave with wooden trusses and square presbytery with cruising vault, along the Path of the Wandering. The walls are covered by a unitary cycle of frescoes from 1475-1485 with an eschatological theme: the Crucifixion in the presbytery, Christ the Judge on the triumphal arch, the righteous welcomed in Paradise by Peter, Paul and the Virgin and, on the opposite wall, the damned in Hell.

  • Where Via del Viandante 4, Mandello del Lario
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Sentiero del Viandante, tratto di Mandello

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A journey of about 45 km in four stages between Abbadia Lariana and Piantedo, on paths that In Lombardy attributed to the ancient Romans. In the first stage passes through San Giorgio and bypasses Mandello from above through Maggiana and Rongio, crosses the shady gorge of the river Meria, climbs to a viewpoint on the Olcio and descends between woods and olive trees towards Lierna; the villages are connected by the train, so you can shorten the route.

  • Where Maggiana e Rongio, Mandello del Lario
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it
