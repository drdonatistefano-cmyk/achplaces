What to see in Tossicia

Medieval village in the Gran Sasso and Monti della Laga National Park, at 409 meters: houses with double windows, ogival portals and coats of arms of the Orsini, the large Gothic portal of Sant'Antonio Abate and the Palazzo marquisale with the museum.

5 places to visit in Tossicia, in the province of Teramo (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It has a large Gothic portal of Andrea Lombardo dated 1471 and a wooden crucifix of the fifteenth century.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta (Santa Sinforosa)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Rustic façade with two fifteenth-century portals by Nicola da Penne; inside a painted and golden terracotta statue of Our Lady of the Divine Providence of the sixteenth century, wooden altars Baroque and Renaissance furnishings.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Palazzo marchesale e Museo delle Genti del Gran Sasso

Museums · Themed

Elegant Renaissance building that houses the museum of material culture of the people of Abruzzo, with a permanent exhibition on the painter Naïf Annunziata Scipione.

Cappella della Madonna della Neve (Cona di Santa Teresa)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches
