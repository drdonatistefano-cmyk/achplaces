What to see in Alife

The ancient Allifae in the middle valley of Volturno: the Roman belt almost intact with a rectangular plan with four doors, the amphitheatre discovered in 1976, the mausoleum called "Pantheon of Alife" , the Norman cathedral with the crypt and the national archaeological museum.

Museo archeologico nazionale dell'antica Allifae

Museums · Archaeological

The history of the territory from prehistory to the Roman era: weapons, stone instruments, ceramic and glass vases and metal objects from Monte Cila, Roccavecchia di Pratella and from the necropolis, a part of fresco of IV style from a domus on the decuman and fragments of Roman mosaics; there are also coins, jewels and skylights from the excavations of the cryptoportico.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Mura romane e porte

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Cinta of about 1.9 kilometers in uncertain work, with perfect rectangular plan, with round and square towers and four doors: Porta Napoli to the east, Porta Fiume to the south, Porta Roma to the west and Porta Piedimonte to the north; the doors have been partially restored.

  • Where Centro storico, Alife
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.alife.ce.it

Sources: comune.alife.ce.it

Anfiteatro romano di Allifae

History and architecture · Archaeology

Built in the first decades of the first century AD and discovered in 1976 thanks to aerial photos: 107 meters by 84, for 12,000-14,000 spectators according to estimates, with the arena later lowered and a forum for notables; in part it is still under the modern houses and today it hosts shows and recreational activities.

  • Where Via Caduti sul Lavoro 136, Alife
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.alife.ce.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.alife.ce.it

Mausoleo detto «Pantheon di Alife»

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

From the first century AD, just outside the eastern door: cylindrical body with dome on a square base of about 13 meters, circular interior with eight niches; it was church of St. John Gerosolimitano from the end of the thirteenth century and votive chapel to the fallen since 1924, and today houses exhibitions. Attribution to the Acili Glabrions is not proven.

  • Where Piazza XIX Ottobre, Alife
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.alife.ce.it

Sources: comune.alife.ce.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta e cripta normanna

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built between 1132 and 1135 for Count Rainulfo Drengot on Lombard buildings, it has neoclassical façade, bell tower of 23.5 meters and Latin cross interior with three naves with the chapel of San Sisto, patron saint; under the Norman crypt of the XII century, with cross vaults on different columns, rose rooms of the Roman baths of the forum.

  • Where Alife
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.alife.ce.it

Sources: comune.alife.ce.it
