What to see in Sant'Arpino

Sant'Arpino was founded in the territory of ancient Atella, the Oscian city cradle of the Fabulae Atellane, and it was named after Sant'Elpidio and was a fief of the Sanchez de Luna, which built the Doge's Palace in front of the mother church of Sant'Elpidio between 1573 and 1592.

Palazzo Ducale Sanchez de Luna

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between 1573 and 1592 by Alonzo Sanchez de Luna d'Aragona, treasurer of the Kingdom of Naples and then duke of Sant'Arpino, on the site of the ancient church of Sant'Elpidio; embellished in 1798 for the wedding of the sixth duke, then passed to Caracciolo and in 1903 was purchased and restored by Giuseppe Macrì, who in 1932 left it to the Municipality. It has a square plan around a courtyard, a pipe doorway with doric columns, a balcony with the ducal coat of arms and traces of paintings in the vaults of the noble floor; today it is the seat of the Town Hall.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di Sant'Elpidio Vescovo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Wanted in 1590 by Alonso III Sanchez de Luna in front of the Doge's Palace, instead of the church of the same name demolished, it was embellished in 1754 on the initiative of Mgr. Marco De Simone reached its current appearance in 1884: 44 meters long Latin cross plan, facade with two bell towers and eleven altars. It preserves the eighteenth-century high altar with baroque balustrade in polychrome marble, paintings of Luca Giordano school, wooden statues of the Madonna di Atella, of Sant'Elpidio and of San Francesco di Paola and a mechanical organ of the nineteenth-century Criscuolo, restored. Open every day 7-13 and 16-20:30.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Sant'Arpino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.santarpino.ce.it

Sources: comune.santarpino.ce.it

Chiesa e Romitorio di San Canione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Next to the church built in 1969 there is the ancient romoratory, which the Municipality dates back to the third century and describes as an early Christian oratory of the Atellan era, considered the tomb of the saint before the transfer to Acerenza; on the façade a fresco of the Madonna delle Grazie between St. Elpidio and St. Nicholas. The interior has been almost entirely restored; the hermitage is open to the faithful in the week after Easter.

  • Where Via S. Canione 23, Sant'Arpino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.santarpino.ce.it

Sources: comune.santarpino.ce.it
