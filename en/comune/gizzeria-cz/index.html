What to see in Gizzeria

Country of Arbëreshë roots overlooking the sunsets of the Tyrrhenian Sea, with the church of San Giovanni Battista on the remains of the castle and the parish museum; on the coast of the Gulf of Sant'Eufemia Gizzeria Lido, capital of kitesurfing, the Tower of Capo Suvero and the Isthmus of the Lighthouse with the beach of Turrazzo.

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The church was built between the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries in an elevated position on the remains of the ancient castle, with a nineteenth-century staircase; the worship of the saint was introduced by the Knights of Malta, whose cross remains on the pediment of the high altar. Damaged by the 1638 earthquake, it had a barrel-shaped dome around 1700 and a restoration in 1850; in 1964 it was profoundly modified. At the center of the vault is the Baptism of Jesus of Joseph Count; it preserves pulpit, ancient baptismal font and saintly water.

  • Where Piazza Malta 6, Gizzeria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.gizzeria.cz.it · italia.it

Museo parrocchiale di arte sacra e contadina

Museums · Themed

Two sections: sacred art of the XVIII-XIX century with a statue of the Addolorata of the 1700, vestments, a noble 19th century dress with golden embroidery, sacred images, plates for hosts, silver and a front of a tabernacle in inlaid marble; local life with peasant tools and artisans, household objects, traditional customs of Gizzeria (for maiden, married and widow), a stone millstone, clay bricks and straw and covered with broom.

the MiC card indicates "Closed" every day of the week; contact the parish (0968 403720) before the visit

  • Where Piazza Malta, Gizzeria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria del Convento

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the Franciscan monastery disappeared, founded in the sixteenth century by Minori Conventuali: a single nave with a square apse covered by dome on plumes, stone portal with inscription of ...Andria Tuscano and date 1559, triumphal arch dated 1615 and an altar with fluted pilgrims and stylized roses.

the FAI describes it today to a large extent ruin invaded by vegetation, needing restoration

  • Where Via Albania 22, Gizzeria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Centro storico di Gizzeria

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The town, just above the coast, has historical ties with the Arbëreshë community (in italo-Albanian "Jacari") and offers views of the sunsets of the Tyrrhenian Sea.

  • Where Gizzeria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Gizzeria Lido

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

A seaside village on the Gulf of Sant'Eufemia, along the Riviera dei Tramonti: one of the best kitesurf spots, with strong wind and deep water all year round, and a promenade with equipped and local establishments. Each year it hosts the Kite Foil Youth World Champion.

  • Where Gizzeria Lido, Gizzeria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
