What to see in Genoni

Small village of Sarcidano, at the border with the Marmilla, at the foot of the hill of Santu Antine with the remains of a Punic fortress and one of the deepest sacred Nuragic wells of Sardinia; about a third of the Giara di Gesturi falls in its territory, with the Museo del Cavallino della Giara and the Paleo Archeo Centro.

Colle di Santu Antine: fortezza punica e pozzo sacro

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Volcanic hill of about 600 meters detached from the Jar for erosion: on top the remains of a Punic fortress of the fourth century BC with six towers and a building that tradition considers a temple of Juno, from which would derive the name of the village. There is also a 39-metre deep nuragic sacred well, indicated as the deepest in Sardinia and then re-used in the Christian age, where a rare Roman-era winch was found; next to the remains of the Romanesque church of St. Helena and St. Constantine the Great, celebrated in early August with a procession of one hundred knights.

Paleo Archeo Centro (P.Ar.C.)

Museums · Archaeological

Museum with sections of paleontology, archaeology and didactics: local fossils, including two sandstone blocks covered with sea urchins from the Duidduru geosite, and Nuragic, Punic and Roman finds of the territory. A model reconstructs the hill of Santu Antine with Punic fortifications, nuraghe, well and church, and a diorama shows the bottom of the well with reproductions of the finds; workshop for schools and families with excavation simulation. Paid admission, total motor accessibility.

  • Where Località Santu Antine, Genoni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: idese.cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Museo del Cavallino della Giara

Museums · Themed

In a typical campidano house, it tells the wild horses of the Giara, the last survivors of a species once diffused throughout the island and considered introduced by the Phoenicians: photo gallery on the life cycle, the environment of the plateau, the tools for tamination, ironing and marking (now replaced by microchip). An ethnographic section, with objects donated by the inhabitants and video-interviews to the elderly, documents the agropastoral culture; educational space for schools and families.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Giara di Gesturi (sa Jara Manna)

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 1,700 of the 4,200 hectares of the plateau fall in the territory of Genoni: endemic corks, holm oaks, roverelle, arbutes and myrtle, with paulis, seasonal water mirrors, and over 600 wild horses. The park has about 25 km of marked trails.

  • Where Giara di Gesturi, Genoni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Geosito Cava di Duidduru

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Geopaleosito visitable, described as unique in Sardinia and with few comparisons in Italy; part of the recovered fossils is exposed at the Paleo Archeo Centro at the foot of the hill.

  • Where Località Duidduru, Genoni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Centro storico e rovine del convento

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center is divided into eight historic districts; the ruins of the former Franciscan monastery of 1638 stand out, with the church of Our Lady of the Sacred Heart, celebrated in May. A map of Insula Sardinia from 1550 already bears the name of the country.

  • Where Centro storico, Genoni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
