What to see in Vinci

The village of Leonardo: the Leonardo Museum in the castle of Conti Guidi and the birthplace of Anchiano, among the olive trees of Montalbano.

4 places to visit in Vinci, in the province of Florence (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 3 of the 4 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Open Vinci in the guide with map and filters

Museo Leonardiano

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

The oldest collection of machines and models dedicated to Leonardo engineer and scientist, between Palazzina Uzielli and Castello dei Conti Guidi.

  • Where Piazza dei Guidi, Vinci
  • Entry €11 whole, €9 reduced (with the "Leonardo and Painting" section); cumulative with the whole Christmas house €14, €11 reduced; family €40
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museoleonardiano.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: museoleonardiano.it · destinationflorence.com

Casa natale di Leonardo ad Anchiano

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The house where Leonardo was born on 15 April 1452, about 3 km from Vinci, with a 3D multimedia story.

  • Where Via di Anchiano, Vinci
  • Entry €7 whole, €5 reduced (with the section "Leonardo and Painting")
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website museoleonardiano.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: museoleonardiano.it · destinationflorence.com

Chiesa di Santa Croce e fonte battesimale di Leonardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The thirteenth-century church rebuilt in neo-Renaissance style, with the baptismal font where according to tradition Leonardo was baptized on 16 April 1452 and the cycle of sculptures by Cecco Bonanotte on the History of Salvation (2010).

  • Where Via dei Martiri 2, Vinci
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · destinationflorence.com

Percorso d'arte contemporanea: Cavallo, Piazza dei Guidi e Uomo di Vinci

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the historic center the Bronze Horse of Nina Akamu inspired by the Sforza monument, the Piazza dei Guidi designed by Mimmo Paladino and, behind the castle tower, the Man of Vinci by Mario Ceroli who reinterprets the Vitruvian Man.

  • Where Centro storico, Vinci
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: toscananelcuore.it · visittuscany.com · destinationflorence.com
