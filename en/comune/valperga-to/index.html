What to see in Valperga

Ancient Canavesian village at the foot of the hill of Belmonte, with the castle of Valperga and the Sacro Monte Franciscan UNESCO World Heritage Site.

3 places to visit in Valperga, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Sacro Monte di Belmonte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Above Valperga at about 700 meters, the sanctuary and the chapels of the Passion on an isolated granite hill, in a reserve of 349 hectares rich in rare flora. UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2003.

  • Where Sacro Monte di Belmonte, Valperga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · sacrimonti.org · italia.it

Castello di Valperga

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Percorso del borgo a Belmonte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the historic centre to the top of the hill, touching the churches of Sant'Aventino, San Giorgio and Santa Apollonia, the castle and the pylon road to the sanctuary.

  • Entry Free
