What to see in Vestenanova

The municipality of the Alpone Valley, in the eastern Lessinia, of which Bolca is part, indicated as the richest fossil deposit in the world with more than 250 animal and vegetable species: the Museum of Fossil and Pesciara, with the galleries dug in the limestones, form with the Monte Postale a paleontological park. Around, paths on the extinct volcano of Monte Purga and on the crosses of the pastor Celestino Baldo.

Museo dei fossili di Bolca

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

Dedicated to the fossils of the Bolca field, studied for centuries: the didactic path explains the techniques of extraction and the process of fossilization, and two aquariums recreate the sea covering the area about 50 million years ago, with a tropical-subtropical coral reef and a warm temperate coast. Open every day 9-12 and 14-18.30; barrier-free (prices updated to 2023).

  • Where Via San Giovanni Battista, Bolca, Vestenanova
  • Entry €5, reduced €4 (7-14 years)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: comune.vestenanova.vr.it · visitverona.it

La Pesciara di Bolca

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry

A well-known place for over 500 years and famous worldwide for the number and conservation of the fossils of the tertiary era, especially fish and plants, in five layers superimposed of fine limestone; it is a mountain above Bolca crossed by tunnels, which with the Monte Postale forms a paleontological park. Visit the inner gallery and search the land for small fossils.

  • Where Via San Giovanni Battista 50, Bolca, Vestenanova
  • Entry Guided tour of the gallery €3, reduced €1; fossil research laboratory €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it · comune.vestenanova.vr.it

Passeggiata paleontologica

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Opened in 2004, it has three routes of different length and altitude, all departing from the museum, passing through the fossiliferous sites overlooking the valley; the longest measures about 9 km and takes about four hours, suitable for children.

  • Where Museo dei fossili, Bolca, Vestenanova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Monte Purga, sentiero rosso

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of about 2 hours from the Museum of Fossil: climb to the parish church and to the top of Monte Purga, with 360 degrees view over the valleys of the Alpone and Chiampo, Veneto plain and Small and Great Dolomites; the return to the road of the Madonna is through the forest of Purga with its columnary basalts.

  • Where Museo dei fossili, Bolca, Vestenanova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Giro del Monte Purga, sentiero blu

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of about 2 hours around Monte Purga, about 800 meters, starting from the museum: from the church of Bolca view of the valleys of the Alpone and Chiampo, then in the woods the city of the stones, a large lava flow never completely covered by vegetation, and outbursts of lignite.

  • Where Museo dei fossili, Bolca, Vestenanova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Sentiero delle croci di Celestino Baldo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of about 5 hours from the small church of Sant'Antonio Abate, on the Colle della Fratta from the 12th century, passing through Bolca, Monte Spilecco (where shark teeth were found) and Monte Pergo up to Vestenavecchia, touching the crosses erected between 1894 and 1899 by the pastor Celestino Baldo (1828-1900).

  • Where Chiesetta di Sant'Antonio Abate, Vestenanova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it
