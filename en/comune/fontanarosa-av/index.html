What to see in Fontanarosa

Borgo Irpino del grano e della pietra, Fontanarosa is famous for the 28-metre straw obelisk pulled every 14 August in honor of Our Lady of Mercy: the cart is told in the Civic Museum of Popular Craftsmanship, next to the Shrine of Mercy and the Church of St. Nicholas.

Museo civico delle produzioni artistiche dell'artigianato popolare

Museums · Themed

In the valley of the village, a few meters from the ancient nucleus along the ancient road of the valley of Fredane, it develops on three levels: municipal library, cart room and sculpture room on the ground floor, and on the first floor a monumental nativity scene of about 35 square meters in stocks of elm and olive trees. The wooden and straw wagon, about 30 meters high, is exposed with facade, 1:5 scale model and dome; more than half of the works are gifts of Italian and foreign artists, and the garden has sculptures, a fountain and a copy of the Nike of Samothrace. Hours from the MiC card: Monday-Friday 9,30-12 and 15-17,30; Saturday and Sunday by reservation (0825 475003, 320 6913966).

Santuario di Maria SS. della Misericordia

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Born according to tradition as a chapel at the well where the statue of Our Lady was found, it was enlarged in 1596 and radically transformed between 1700 and 1731, with stone portal, apse (1710) and high altar with inlays of polychrome marbles of master Raguzzino (1726). A three naves, it has paintings of the ceiling and frescoes by Arnaldo De Lisio (first twentieth century), a wooden triptych of the Madonna del Rosario donated by the Gesualdos at the end of the sixteenth century and the medieval statue of the Madonna of Mercy, which never comes out of the niche; it is the Madonna to whom every August 14th is dedicated the Obelisk of straw. Visitable during religious services.

Chiesa di San Nicola Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The municipality describes it as monumental architectural work of Norman times, linked to devotion to the patron saint of St. Nicholas, with statues of saints and valuable paintings. Free entry during functions.

  • Where Piazza Cristo Re, Fontanarosa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it
