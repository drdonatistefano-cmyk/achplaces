What to see in Cetraro

A small town on the Tyrrhenian coast of Cosenza, whose name evokes the ancient cultivation of cedar: the fortified old town revolves around Piazza del Popolo with the Fontana del Nettuno and Palazzo del Trono, seat of the Museo dei Brettii e del Mare; on the coast the Torre di Rienzo, the Scogliera dei Rizzi and a large marina.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Borgo di viuzze, arches and glimpses, once fortified: you enter it from three doors, Sea, Low and Over. Churches and manor palaces overlook squares with characteristic names, such as the "giorgia" , an ancient market place, and the "miezzu a curta" , in the center of the old village.

  • Where Centro storico, Cetraro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.cetraro.cs.it

Sources: comune.cetraro.cs.it

Torre di Rienzo

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Tower of sighting on a rocky ridge near the sea, of the system of coastal towers built between 1559 and 1567 against Turkish raids; square plan on three floors (deposit, accommodation of soldiers, battery), with entrance at the top reachable by a staircase. It was called Torre d'Acqua Perropata for a nearby waterfall; the tradition binds to San Benedetto a cannon blow that sank two Turkish ships, remembered every year by a procession of boats.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.cetraro.cs.it
