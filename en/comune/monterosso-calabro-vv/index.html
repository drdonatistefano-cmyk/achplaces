What to see in Monterosso Calabro

A hilly village of the Parco delle Serre between Lake Angitola and the Serre plateau, at the foot of Monte Coppari, with noble palaces with granite portals, a medieval tower and two museums: that of peasant and artisan civilization, awarded at the European Museum of the Year Award, and the Museum Polo in the former nineteenth-century spinning mill.

Museo della civiltà contadina e artigiana della Calabria

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1983 on the initiative of Pro Loco after a collection of over 3,000 objects started in 1978, he received a mention at the European Museum of the Year Award. Six sections (farm art, textile fibers, terracotta, wood, iron, costumes) with a wooden wine press and a working 18th century lathe, a Calabrian frame, a 12th century host mold, a rebuilt forge and traditional costumes from different countries along the steps. It is located in the seventeenth-century Palazzo Aceti-Amoroso; the Parco delle Serre presents it in the Museum Polo La Filanda. Hours according to the municipality: Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday 9-12, Sunday 16-18.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Museo multimediale delle Serre calabresi - La Filanda

Museums · Themed

In the former nineteenth-century mill, industrial complex with large windows and large arches on the ground floor, scientifically curated by Rosario Chimirri: four rooms with panels and large projections tell geography, flora, fauna and paths, urban planning and architecture, work and food, dialect, traditional music, cult of the saints and feasts of the Serre.

  • Where Ex Filanda, Monterosso Calabro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page parcodelleserre.it

Sources: parcodelleserre.it

Centro storico e palazzi nobiliari

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Narrow and winding streets with ups and downs and passages with a tunnel, between palaces and palazzotti of the Seven-nineteenth century with granite portals, coats of arms and decorations of the families Massara, Amoroso-Aceti, Basile and La Grotteria.

Chiese di San Nicola, del Rosario e del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Buildings of five-sixth-century origin rebuilt after the earthquake of 1783 and enriched by statues of Serresi artists: the parish church of St. Nicholas (of 1600 according to the Municipality), the church of the Rosary (nineteenth century) and the church of Carmine.
