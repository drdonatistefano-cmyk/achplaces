What to see in Tusa

The hilly village of the Nebrodi overlooking the valley and the sea, with the seaside hamlet of Castel di Tusa; in the territory there are the ancient Sicilian-Greek town of Halaesa Arconidea and the first work of the Fiumara d'arte, "La materia potre non essere" by Pietro Consagra.

Area archeologica di Halaesa Arconidea e Antiquarium

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

A city founded in 403 B.C. by Arconides, tyrant of Herbita, on a hill above the Tyrrhenian coast and the valley of Tusa; the first Sicilian city to take sides with Rome in the first Punic War, was one of the five "civitates liberae et immunes" and was abandoned at the time of the Arab occupation. You can visit the walls of the fourth century BC with towers and two gates, the agora of the second century BC with colonnade portico, the cardo maximus, two houses peristilius, the bases of two temples on the acropolis (one perhaps dedicated to Apollo) and the church of Santa Maria delle Palate; the Antiquarium exhibits Greek and Latin sculptures and epigraphs from the second century BC to the third AD.

the MiC card (updated to March 2021) indicates hours \"closed" every day and the Park describes the antiquarium in preparation: check opening and ticket (MiC: €2, reduced €1)

Fiumara d'arte: «La materia poteva non esserci»

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

First work of the Fiumara d'arte, the open-air sculpture museum designed by Antonio Presti and inaugurated on October 10, 1986, which extends for about 200,000 square meters of state property among the most common. The sculpture of Pietro Consagra, placed in 1986 on the gret of Tusa's river in memory of Presti's father, is in concrete, 18 meters high, with two inclined elements, one white and one black, among which you can walk.

  • Where SP 174 km 1,5, Tusa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: luoghidelcontemporaneo.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it · visitsicily.info

Chiesa Madre di Maria Santissima Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt in the eighteenth century on the original sixteenth-century plant; the main altar is adorned with a marble triptych of Gagini.

Centro storico, chiese e belvedere

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village has a winding road; on the square there are ancient re-trained buildings and in the village you can visit the churches of Sant'Antonio Abate, San Giovanni Battista, San Nicola di Bari, San Michele Arcangelo, Santa Caterina d'Alessandria and Santa Maria di Loreto. The view overlooks the valley and the sea and is suitable for sunset.

  • Where Tusa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Castel di Tusa e spiaggia delle Lampare

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fishing village on the sea, with crystal clear waters; the beach of Lampare has been awarded for several years by the Blue Flag. The bottom is pebbles (recommended rubber shoes) and a large rock shaped by erosion is the guardian of the bay; from the promontory you can see the 38th Parallel Pyramid of the Fiumara d'arte. Free parking along the road.

  • Where Castel di Tusa, Tusa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info
