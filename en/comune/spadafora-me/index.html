What to see in Spadafora

A small town on the Tyrrhenian coast dominated by the sixteenth-century castle of the Spadafora family, also known as Castello Samonà, now restored by the Region and used for cultural events; on the hill the village of San Martino, of ancient origins, with its churches.

5 places to visit in Spadafora, in the province of Messina (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Spadafora (Castello Samonà)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

16th century coastal fortress built by the Spadafora family on an ancient tower, with four angular spurs and garrets; the attribution to the Florentine architect Camillo Camiliani is now questioned. It was used more as a noble residence than as a defence, and inside it preserves elegant decorative stuccoes on the ceilings. Owned by the Region, restored with the Superintendence of Messina, it is used by the Municipality and the Pro Loco for cultural events; it can be visited free of charge on request.

  • Where Via Umberto I 59, Spadafora
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.spadafora.me.it

Sources: comune.spadafora.me.it · italia.it · visitsicily.info

Borgo di San Martino e chiesa di San Martino

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Historic nucleus of ancient origins above Spadafora, which until 1817 was a fraction of it; the feud included five houses registered under Frederick II. The Santa Colomba square, inaugurated in 1990, dominates the slope with a circular fountain. The church of San Martino, rebuilt in the twenties-thirties of the twentieth century, conserves altars in polychrome marble of the eighteenth century recovered from the original church; opened during the celebrations.

  • Where Via Incenzo Fontana, San Martino, Spadafora
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.spadafora.me.it

Sources: comune.spadafora.me.it

Chiesa della Madonna del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in the early nineteenth century, it retains its original simplicity; on the high altar the statue of Our Lady of about 1955, restored by artisans of Barcelona, in the niche built in 1958. Free admission during the celebrations.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele III 44, Spadafora
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.spadafora.me.it

Sources: comune.spadafora.me.it

Chiesa del Sacro Cuore di Gesù

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1939 by engineer Barbaro of Messina, with a churchyard, staircase and three portals; on the main altar the canvas of Bonanno of Messina in 1945 with the appearance of the Sacred Heart. Open during functions.

  • Where Via Riolo 2, Spadafora
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.spadafora.me.it

Sources: comune.spadafora.me.it

Chiesa di San Giuseppe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A unique nave with polygonal apse covered with ribbed dome, round arches on Corinthian columns and trabeation, raised presbytery and singing room on two levels; a hut façade with iron frame bell tower. Open during functions.

  • Where Via Umberto I 77, Spadafora
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.spadafora.me.it

Sources: comune.spadafora.me.it
