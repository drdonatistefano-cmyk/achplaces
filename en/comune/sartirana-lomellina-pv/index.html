What to see in Sartirana Lomellina

The village of Lomellina is dominated by the Visconti castle of the fourteenth century, surrounded by a large moat and a high circular tower, which with the adjacent Pila houses the museums and exhibitions of the Fondazione Sartirana Arte; in the surroundings the meandering lake formed by the sesia loops.

Castello di Sartirana

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built by the Visconti around 1370, after Galeazzo Visconti took Sartirana to the Counts of Lomello, it is the largest castle of Lomellina: quadrangular plan in red bricks with moat, inner courtyard covered with ivy and four angular towers, dominated by the cylindrical tower of north-east. About 1462-1463, under the Sforza, Aristotile Fioravanti strengthened him by adding to the tower the dodecagonal base and the terracotta bells; restored in the nineteenth century with eclectic decorations, since 1975 he is of the Foundation of Studies of Lomellina. Beyond the moat, to the north-west, a garden connected by a crenellated catwalk; next to the parish church and the gun square rise.

Musei della Fondazione Sartirana Arte (castello e Pila)

Museums · Modern art

Since 1993 the Fondazione Sartirana Arte has been managing the interior of the castle with one of the most complete museum routes of Lomellina: contemporary silver and artist jewels, and cataloged works including sculptures by Fausto Melotti, Arnaldo Pomodoro, Alik Cavaliere, Marcello Pirro and Paolo Cristiani; exhibitions of painting, sculpture and handicraft are held in the castle. La Pila, an eighteenth-century building for the processing of rice restored as an exhibition space between 1990 and 1996, hosts antiques and vintage design exhibitions and a permanent exhibition dedicated to designer Ken Scott. Italia.it advises you to check the timetables before the visit.

Lago di Sartirana, sito di nidificazione

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

Large lake formed by the bends of the river Sesia, indicated as one of the most picturesque and less polluted places of Lomellina, with very varied flora and fauna; it is a nesting site.
