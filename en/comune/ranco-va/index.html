What to see in Ranco

Country on the Varese shore of Lake Maggiore, with beaches, parks and numerous erratic boulders left by glaciers: the largest is the Sasso Cavallazzo, on the shore of the Gulf of Quassa Park that shares with Ispra.

2 places to visit in Ranco, in the province of Varese (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parco del Golfo della Quassa

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The gulf stretches between the tip of Ranco and the tip of the Fornace of Ispra and is indicated as the only extended stretch of coast of Lake Maggiore facing north. It is one of the few stretches of shore far from large roads and railways, little inhabited, with dense vegetation, numerous erratic boulders, low and sandy seabeds and morainic deposits. The Verbano Trail, cycle path between Ispra, Angera and Sesto Calende, crosses Ranco. Free access.

  • Where Golfo della Quassa, tra Ranco e Ispra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.ispra.va.it · in-lombardia.it

Sasso Cavallazzo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

The "Sass Cavalasc" is a colossal erratic rock on the shore of the Verbano, in the territory of Ranco near the border with Ispra, which emerges from the water next to a beach. Like the other erratic boulders in the country, it was transported by glaciers that covered the entire Lake Maggiore during the glaciations.

  • Where Sasso Cavallazzo, Ranco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
