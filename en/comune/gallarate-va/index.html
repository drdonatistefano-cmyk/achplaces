What to see in Gallarate

City of Varesotto with MA*GA, one of the most important contemporary art museums in Italy, the Neoclassical Basilica of Santa Maria Assunta in Piazza Libertà, the Romanesque church of San Pietro, a national monument since 1864, and the museum of the Gallarate Society for Patri Studies in the former Franciscan convent of 1234.

MA*GA Museo Arte Gallarate

Museums · Modern art

Founded in 1966 around the works acquired with the National Visual Arts Award of the city, established in 1950; since 2009 it is managed by a Foundation with founding members the Ministry of Culture and the Municipality, and since 19 March 2010 it has new name and headquarters in Via De Magri, with permanent collection, exhibitions and educational activities. Tuesday-Friday 10-18, Saturday and Sunday 11-19, Monday closed.

  • Where Via De Magri 1, Gallarate
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.museomaga.it

Sources: museomaga.it · in-lombardia.it · comune.gallarate.va.it

Museo della Società Gallaratese per gli Studi Patri

Museums · City history

Archaeological, historical and artistic museum in the remains of the Franciscan monastery of 1234, with half of a Gothic cloister that houses a Roman sarcophagus and medieval tombs: finds of the Varesotto palafittes, a monoxile piroga from the lake of Monate, materials of Golasecca and Malpensa, epigraphs and Roman finds, the marble pluteo of San Giovanni di Castelseprio, and on the upper floor of the gallery, coat of arms and sculptures. The Society was founded in 1896.

  • Where Via del Borgo Antico 4, Gallarate
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.gallarate.va.it

Sources: comune.gallarate.va.it

Basilica di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Prepositural built between 1856 and 1861 on a project by Giacomo Moraglia, with façade of Camillo Boito, on the site of a church mentioned in 974, of which remains the late dogotic bell tower in terracotta begun in 1458. Dome of 27 meters frescoed by Luigi Cavenaghi (1887-1891), seventeenth-century paintings of Morazzone and Daniele Crespi, eighteenth-century copy of the Shroud in crypt donated by the Savoys in 1710, altar and ambo by Claudio Parmiggiani of 2018.

  • Where Piazza Libertà 6, Gallarate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.gallarate.va.it

Sources: comune.gallarate.va.it · in-lombardia.it

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque church attributed to the Comacini Masters (XI-XII century), documented since 1364, transformed into fortification by the Lomeno and returned to worship by order of St. Charles in 1570; national monument since 1864, restored by Gaetano Moretti with decorations by Ernesto Rusca and reconsecrated in 1911. Façade with mosaic bezel of 1920, trussed on sight, a copy of the Virgin of Częstochowa donated by Karol Wojtyła and the Good Shepherd with fake mosaic in the apse.

  • Where Piazza Libertà, Gallarate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.gallarate.va.it

Sources: comune.gallarate.va.it
