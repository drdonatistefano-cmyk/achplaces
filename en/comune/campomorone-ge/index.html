What to see in Campomorone

Municipality of the Polcevera valley with the seventeenth-century Palazzo Balbi, home of the museum of puppets of Angelo Cenderelli, and in its territory the lakes of the Gorezente.

3 places to visit in Campomorone, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Campomorone in the guide with map and filters

Palazzo Balbi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Municipal building, belonged to Balbi and then to Spinola, which since 1996 also houses the museum of puppets.

  • Where Via Gavino, Campomorone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cittametropolitana.genova.it · italia.it

Museo delle Marionette «Collezione Cenderelli»

Museums · Themed

Re-exposed in 2013, he exhibits seventy puppets, one hundred scripts, costumes and sets by the genoese puppetist Angelo Cenderelli, with a theater for performances during guided tours.

  • Where Palazzo Balbi, Campomorone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cittametropolitana.genova.it · italia.it

Laghi del Gorzente e sentiero naturalistico

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring path of about 5 hours, traced in 1996 by the CAI of Bolzanoto, between the artificial basins of the Gorzente at the border with the Park of the Capanne of Marcarolo; bathing is not allowed.

  • Where Campomorone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it
