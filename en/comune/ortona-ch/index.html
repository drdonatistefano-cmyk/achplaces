What to see in Ortona

The city of San Tommaso, the first stop on the Costa dei Trabocchi: the Aragonese Castle on the sea, the Cathedral with the relics of the apostle and Palazzo Farnese, with about 20 km of beaches.

6 places to visit in Ortona, in the province of Chieti (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Ortona in the guide with map and filters

Castello Aragonese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress of about 1450-1470 with a quadrangular plan, overlooking the sea in front of the Costa dei Trabocchi; damaged by the bombings of 1943 and by a landslide in 1946, restored in the 2000s, houses in a tower a small museum.

  • Where Largo Castello 3, Ortona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismo.abruzzo.it

Cattedrale di San Tommaso Apostolo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Since 1258 it has kept the relics of St. Thomas arrived from Chios; destroyed in the Second World War and rebuilt, it preserves the Gothic portal of Nicola Mancino of 1311 and, in the left nave, access to the Diocesan Museum.

  • Where Piazza San Tommaso, Ortona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Palazzo Farnese

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Renaissance Palace of the sixteenth century built for Margherita d'Austria on a project by Giacomo Della Porta; it houses the Museum of Contemporary Art and the Pinacoteca Cascella.

  • Where Largo Farnese, Ortona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it · italia.it

Passeggiata Orientale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Long panoramic walk on the eastern side of the promontory, on the Adriatic, which rises towards the castle.

  • Where Ortona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismo.abruzzo.it

Riserva naturale di Punta dell'Acquabella

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Traction of sand coast and pebbles south of the port, with clear waters from which it takes its name.

  • Where Ortona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it · italia.it

Spiagge di Lido Riccio e Punta Ferruccio

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

To the north is the clear sand of Lido Riccio and the pebbles and cliffs of Punta Ferruccio, on about 20 km of Ortonese coast.

  • Where Ortona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it · italia.it
