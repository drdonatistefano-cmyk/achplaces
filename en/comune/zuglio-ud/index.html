What to see in Zuglio

Small town of Carnia on the But valley, heir to Iulium Carnicum, the northernmost Roman city in Italy on the road from Aquileia al Norico: remains of the forum, archaeological museum and the Matrix Church of St. Peter.

3 places to visit in Zuglio, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Civico Museo Archeologico Iulium Carnicum

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the seventeenth century Palazzo Tommasi Leschiutta, on three floors: prehistory of Carnia with the reconstruction of part of the necropolis of Misincinis (VIII-IV century BC), finds of forum, baths and domus of Zuglio with Pompeian frescoes of the frigidarium, and the life of the Roman city; also exhibits five wooden sculptures of the altar of Domenico da Tolmezzo recovered by the Carabinieri in 2016. Open Thursday-Sunday from June to October, Friday and Saturday from November to May.

  • Where Via Giulio Cesare 19, Zuglio
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Area archeologica del Foro romano di Iulium Carnicum

History and architecture · Archaeology

Remains of the Roman city of Iulium Carnicum: the civil basilica of the first imperial age with the cryptoportico and the staircase on the first floor, the base of a portico with Tuscan capitals, a stretch of the paved square with statues bases and the podium of the temple.

Visitable only by groups and schools by reservation, during guided tours of the museum

  • Where Zuglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Pieve matrice di San Pietro in Carnia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The parish church dominates the But valley from above and is the final stage of the Carnic section of Romea Strata da Cercivento. The Zuglio Museum displays the furnishings of a first basilica dating from the 6th to the 9th century and the sculptures of the altar of Domenico da Tolmezzo.

  • Where Zuglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it
