What to see in Urbania

Ancient Casteldurante, the city of ceramics in a loop of the Metauro, with fifteenth-century plant and arcades; Orange Flag of the Touring, is linked to Montefeltro-Della Rovere, of which it preserves the Ducal Palace with museum and library and the Doge's Barco, and houses the Mummies Cemetery.

Palazzo Ducale e Musei civici

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built by Francesco di Giorgio Martini from the end of the fifteenth century for the Montefeltro-Della Rovere, it was built above the Brancaleoni fortress and enlarged in the sixteenth century by Girolamo Genga, it was the last seat of the Roveresche court. The route of more than 20 rooms exhibits paintings, drawings, prints and cartographic material left by the last Duke Francesco Maria II, with works by Raffaellino del Colle and Barocci, Renaissance ceramic fragments and a ceramic room used by all Italy; the library has about 40,000 volumes, including a Commedia di Dante of 1491, and in the underground there is the Museum of the history of agriculture and craftsmanship. From the loggia of the tower you can see the center and the river; accept the Map Musei Marche.

  • Where Corso Vittorio Emanuele II 23, Urbania
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo Diocesano «Mons. Corrado Leonardi»

Museums · Old masters

In the former Bishop's Palace of the late sixteenth century, renovated in Renaissance forms with interventions by Francesco di Giorgio Martini, Gerolamo Genga and the stonemasons of Sant'Ippolito. From the lapidary you pass to the rooms of silver, vestments, Madonnas and the metauren mannerists (Federico Zuccari, Giovanni Giacomo Pandolfi, Claudio Ridolfi) and to a section of majolica that documents the production of Castel delle Ripe, Casteldurante and Urbania from the XIII to the XX century. Italia.it indicates opening from Tuesday to Sunday 11-13 and 16-18.

  • Where Via Urbano VIII 7, Urbania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Barco Ducale e Sentiero del Duca

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

A hunting reserve built by Federico da Montefeltro in 1465, with park, villa and convent of San Giovanni Battista (founded in the fourteenth century and rebuilt in the eighteenth century in style inspired by Vanvitelli); it was the retreat of Pietro Bembo, Tasso and Ariosto and a favourite place of Francesco Maria II Della Rovere. It is reached from the Doge's Palace with the Duke's Path, 1.5 km along the Metauro; there are ceramic workshops held by the Friends of Ceramics.

  • Where Località Porta Parco, Urbania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Cattedrale di San Cristoforo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Facade with an eighteenth-century appearance but completed at the end of the nineteenth century, with older bell tower; inside a Crucifix by Pietro da Rimini suspended in the apse and paintings by Mannerist painters of the late sixteenth century.

Centro storico, Piazza San Cristoforo e Teatro Bramante

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Located in a loop of the Metauro, the center has fifteenth-century plant, regular streets and porticos; from the small Piazza San Cristoforo, with the column of the patron, radiate the central streets, and next to stands the nineteenth-century Bramante Theatre, one of the jewels of the region. Along Via Roma the public gardens with the monument to the Great War.

  • Where Piazza San Cristoforo, Urbania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Botteghe della ceramica

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Ceramic city from the Middle Ages, when it was called Castel delle Ripe, worked the clay of the Metauro; under the Montefeltro the shops multiplied and here was born in 1524 the ceramist Cipriano Piccolpasso. Today the majolica workshops organize courses throughout the year.

  • Where Urbania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
