What to see in Riva del Garda

Cities at the northern end of Lake Garda, between Rocchetta and Monte Brione: around Piazza III Novembre with the Apponale Tower and the Pretorio Palace are tightened the medieval center and the fortress of the twelfth century, seat of the MAG, while above the city watches the Venetian Bastion and towards Ledro rises the Strada del Ponale; the church of the Inviolata is presented as the most important Baroque church of Trentino.

Rocca di Riva e MAG Museo Alto Garda

Museums · City history · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Castle built in the twelfth century with moat to defend port and inhabited, then noble residence and barracks; it houses the MAG with the gallery of landscapers of the nineteenth century attracted by Garda and works of Pietro Ricchi, Vincenzo Vela and Francesco Hayez, the archaeological section with the prehistoric statues-stele of Arco, finds of the palafittes of Ledro and San Martino di Campi, and the history of Riva and Bass Sarca between Scaligeri, Visconti and Venice until the years of Thomas Mann and Franz Kafka. From the Mastio view of the city and lake; the garden of the Rocca is also open to closed museum.

  • Where Piazza C. Battisti 3/A, Riva del Garda
  • Entry €5
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.museoaltogarda.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: museoaltogarda.it · gardatrentino.it · visittrentino.info

Torre Apponale

History and architecture · Towers · Paid entry · Viewpoint

34-metre tower on Piazza III Novembre, documented in the thirteenth century to guard and defend the city; on top the golden angel-signavento "Anzolim de la Tor" is one of the symbols of Riva. 165 steps up to the panoramic terrace on the center and lake; free under 15 years.

Piazza III Novembre e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The civic heart of the city overlooking the port, with the Palazzo Pretorio (now town hall) and porticos that once housed shops and exchange counters. From here, the medieval centre, built on lands once submerged by the lake, runs through Piazza delle Erbe, Porta Bruciata towards Piazza San Rocco and Piazza Catena, Porta San Marco called "Porta Veneziana," Piazza Cavour and Porta San Michele; in Via Roma you can see the excavations of the Roman thermal baths.

  • Where Piazza III Novembre, Riva del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Quartiere Marocco

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

One of the oldest areas of the city, at the foot of the Rocchetta and near the medieval walls: the houses rest on large blocks of rock, the "marocche" (marocche), from which the neighborhood takes its name.

  • Where Quartiere Marocco, Riva del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Chiesa dell'Inviolata

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Church of pilgrimage for a miraculous image of Our Lady, commissioned by Gaudenzio Madruzzo and Alfonsina Gonzaga, built in 1603 and consecrated in 1636: square outside and octagonal inside, with five altars, three doors, frescoed vaults, carved confessionals and marble floors. White, black and golden plasters by Davide Reni (1609), altarpieces by Palma il Giovane, paintings by Pietro Ricchi called the Lucchese; presented as the most important Baroque church in Trentino.

  • Where Largo Marconi, Riva del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: gardatrentino.it · visittrentino.info

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Main church of the city, in Piazza Cavour near the lake, with Baroque appearance due to the eighteenth-century reconstruction: a single nave with side chapels, a large painting of the Assumption on the main altar and the Sorrows of Craffonara, a local artist.

  • Where Piazza Cavour, Riva del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Bastione

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Cylindrical fortress in grey stone at the foot of the Rocchetta, built at the beginning of the sixteenth century (1507-1508 according to Visit Trentino) to protect the village, mined and destroyed by French troops in 1703 and restored under the Province's Superintendence. Walk up in about 20 minutes or with the panoramic elevator from via Monte Oro (about two minutes); on top panoramic terrace over town, port and coast up to Torbole and a bar-restaurant.

  • Where Via Monte Oro (ascensore), Riva del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: gardatrentino.it · visittrentino.info

Centrale idroelettrica di Riva (Hydrotour Dolomiti)

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Central monument built on the lakefront in the 1920s, designed by Giancarlo Maroni, the architect of D'Annunzio and Vittoriale; an interactive and multimedia route for adults and children explains how water becomes energy. Guided tours by reservation, in Italian, English and German.

  • Where Via Giacomo Cis 13, Riva del Garda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: gardatrentino.it · visittrentino.info

Strada del Ponale

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Road built from 1851 in the rock overlooking the lake to connect Garda to Valle di Ledro, replaced by a new tunnel at the end of the eighties and transformed into a path in 2004: 6.4 km and about 3 hours from Via Monte Oro, with views of the lake at each curve and the tunnels and trenches of the Austro-Hungarian Tagliata del Ponale (since 1860, normally closed). Shared with cyclists; from the Belvedere continue for Ledro or Pregasina.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Sources: gardatrentino.it · visittrentino.info

Monte Brione e i suoi forti

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Relief between Riva and Torbole, a protected area for Mediterranean flora and orchids and inhabited by rare Mediterranean birds, with ancient olive groves; it was the fulcrum of the Austro-Hungarian defensive system at the border with the Kingdom of Italy. The ring of 6.1 km (about 3 hours) of the Path of Peace from Porto San Nicolò touches Forte San Nicolò (1860-62), Forte Sant'Alessandro (1881) on the summit, Batteria di Mezzo (1900) and Forte Garda (1907).

  • Where Porto San Nicolò, Riva del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: gardatrentino.it · visittrentino.info

Spiagge di Riva

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Beaches with free access on grass or white pebbles: the Sabbioni, the largest and equipped with services, rentals and sports areas; the Pini beach (Purfina), more informal and frequented by windsurfers; Punta Lido at the confluence of the streams, close to the center. The Pelèr in the morning and the Hour in the afternoon make regular sailing and windsurfing.

  • Where Spiaggia dei Sabbioni, Riva del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Sito archeologico di Monte San Martino

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On a spur of Monte San Martino (850 m) above Campi, the remains of a large building of the II-IV century A.D., probably a sanctuary dedicated to female deities, built on places of worship more ancient and frequented until the Middle Ages; the finds are at the Museum of Riva. Always open.

  • Where Località Campi, Riva del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.archeosanmartino.it

Sources: archeosanmartino.it · gardatrentino.it · visittrentino.info

Villino Campi

Museums · Science and university

Inaugurated in 1998, it is the main provincial centre dedicated to the lake environments, the water cycle and the ecosystem of Garda, with activities of environmental education and attention to sustainable tourism; it is open on request for educational activities.

  • Where Via Christoph von Hartungen, Riva del Garda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visittrentino.info
