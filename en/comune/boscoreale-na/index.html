What to see in Boscoreale

Common on the slopes of Vesuvius, in the National Park, where the Archaeological Park of Pompeii manages the Roman farm of Villa Regina and the Antiquarium dedicated to life in the Vesuvian countryside before 79 AD; next, the Museum of the National Park of Vesuvius.

3 places to visit in Boscoreale, in the province of Naples (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa Regina

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Small Roman farm of Sillan age discovered in 1977 and buried by the eruption of 79 A.D.: the fulcrum is the wine cell with 18 dolia underground, capable of up to 10,000 liters of must, with press, porch courtyard, triclino with paintings of III style, barn, cistern and barn. A Bacchus hermitage among the finds. Included in Pompeii+, Pompeii Plus and MyPompeii cards; closed on December 25 and January 1st.

  • Where Viale Villa Regina, Boscoreale
  • Entry €8, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website pompeiisites.org
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Antiquarium di Boscoreale

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Set up in 1991 next to Villa Regina, it illustrates with educational tools the life and environment of the Vesuvian countryside in Roman times, with finds from houses of Pompeii and the rustic villas of Boscoreale (Villa della Pisanella, villa of Numerius Popidius Florus and others). Since the reopening of October 2023 a room is dedicated to the excavations of the villa of Civita Giuliana, with the ceremonial cart found in 2021. Same ticket as Villa Regina.

  • Where Viale Villa Regina, Boscoreale
  • Entry €8, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website pompeiisites.org
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: cultura.gov.it · pompeiisites.org · italia.it

Museo del Parco Nazionale del Vesuvio

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

A five-room walk with multimedia panels and installations on the geological evolution of Somma-Vesuvius, soil formation, biodiversity and the relationship between man and volcano, with the models of recent eruptions and a farmhouse. Realized by the Park Authority with the Municipality, the Osservatorio Vesuviano-INGV and the Fondazione Cives. Open Friday, Saturday and Sunday 10-16.

Reservation required by email, maximum 30 visitors per time slot

  • Where Centro culturale di Villa Regina, Boscoreale
  • Entry €4, under 18 free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parconazionaledelvesuvio.it · italia.it
