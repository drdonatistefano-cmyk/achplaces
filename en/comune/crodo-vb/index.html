What to see in Crodo

The water village in Valle Antigorio: the cold sources from which the Crodino was born, the national museum of mineral waters at the Parco delle Terme, the central of Verampi designed by Piero Portaluppi, the parish church of Santo Stefano with the bell tower of 1026 and the stone churches of Cravegna and Salera among the woods.

6 places to visit in Crodo, in the province of Verbano-Cusio-Ossola (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo nazionale delle acque minerali «Carlo Brazzorotto» e Museo mineralogico ossolano

Museums · Themed

Started in 1993 by Centro Studi Piero Ginocchi and opened in 2006: five historic bottling plants, over 15,000 full bottles and more than 80,000 labels; the oldest document is a decree by the Grand Duke of Tuscany of 1583 on the waters of Monte Catini. The mineralogical section exhibits minerals of Verbano Cusio Ossola, including the rare gasparite, roggianite and paraniite, a new species recognized in 1995. Open seasonally or by reservation.

  • Where Via Bagni 18 (Parco delle Terme), Crodo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.crodo.vb.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The mother church of all the others of the Valle Antigorio: originally Romanesque, lower and narrower, with the bell tower dated 1026; enlarged in 1616 and 1777.

  • Where Via Pellanda, Crodo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.crodo.vb.it · italia.it

Centrale idroelettrica Ettore Conti di Verampio

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Designed by the architect Piero Portaluppi and built in 1912-1914, among the oldest power stations in Italy, with the building richly decorated with geometric motifs. It's seen from the outside.

  • Where Frazione Verampio, Crodo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.crodo.vb.it

Chiesa di San Giulio a Cravegna

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Santuario della Madonna di Salera

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Oratory collected and silent among the woods, destination of a devotional itinerary that starts from Cravegna along a mule track with the chapels of the mysteries of the Rosary (the Sacred Mount of Salera).

  • Where Località Salera, Crodo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.crodo.vb.it

Big Bench di Crodo

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The first giant bench of the Ossola valleys (b. 186), yellow on a green lawn, overlooking the peaks of the Valle Antigorio and the hamlet of Viceno; it is easily reached from the road to the hamlet Foppiano.

  • Where Strada per Foppiano, Crodo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it · italia.it
