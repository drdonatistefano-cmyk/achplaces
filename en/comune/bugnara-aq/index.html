What to see in Bugnara

Medieval village almost 600 meters at the foot of the Colle Rotondo, overlooking the Peligna Valley, with the remains of the Doge's Palace called Fortezza dello Scorpio, the church of the Madonna della Snow built on a Roman temple and the church of the SS. Rosary with the fifteenth century fountain.

4 places to visit in Bugnara, in the province of L'Aquila (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa della Madonna della Neve

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Three-nave Gothic church built around the year 1000 on the remains of a Roman temple dedicated to Ceres (or Bona), of which remains the fishbone floor; it preserves statues and ancient frescoes, a plaque dedicated to Helvia, priestess of Ceres, and a bas-relief that portrays it during a rite.

  • Where Centro storico, Bugnara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Palazzo Ducale (Fortezza dello Scorpione)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built around the year 1000 with defensive structure then adapted to residence; its remains, symbol of the noble past of the village, still seem to control the whole territory. A restoration project is planned for the building.

  • Where Bugnara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa del SS. Rosario e fontana quattrocentesca

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

At the entrance of the village, church completed in 1602 in late Baroque style, a single nave with relief ceiling, large wooden altar, friezes and stucco; reopened to worship in December 2000 after a long restoration. Next to the fifteenth century fountain, with the arched niche surmounted by the coat of arms of the Di Sangro.

  • Where Piazza del SS. Rosario, Bugnara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Centro storico e mura megalitiche

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient village, developed mainly in the Middle Ages among hills and woods above the Peligna Valley, preserves remains of megalithic walls and fortifications, the Palazzo dei Papi, the palace of Baron Alesi dei Paparelli and the church of Santa Maria degli Angeli.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
