What to see in Serrapetrona

Borgo 500 meters between the valleys of Potenza and Chienti, home of Vernaccia di Serrapetrona DOCG: the fourteenth-century church of San Francesco with the polyptych of Lorenzo d'Alessandro, the square with Santa Maria and Palazzo Claudi, the lake of Caccamo and the castle of San Venanzo on Mount Letegge.

8 places to visit in Serrapetrona, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Erected by the Friars Minor in the 14th century at the western end of the village: on the high altar the polyptych of Lorenzo d'Alessandro, long attributed to Niccolò Alunno and returned to his author thanks to the balance document of the contract, dated 1496.

  • Where Via San Francesco, Serrapetrona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Palazzo Claudi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Ancient palace of the center restored and reopened in 2012, the Marche headquarters of the Claudi Foundation: it preserves the archive of the philosopher and poet Claudio Claudi and the paintings of his mother Anna, and houses exhibitions, concerts and meetings.

  • Where Centro storico, Serrapetrona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.comune.serrapetrona.mc.it

Sources: turismo.comune.serrapetrona.mc.it

Pinacoteca d'arte sacra contemporanea «Galleria del Caburro»

Museums · Modern art

In a fifteenth-century building on three levels along Via San Francesco: religious iconography and expressionism in painting, sculpture, lithography and engraving, with works by Valerio Trubbiani and emerging artists. The interior is inaccessible after the earthquake of 2016; it remains a path for images.

Museo dell'Uomo

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In the watchtower of the 12th century restored in 1995: agricultural tools, millstones and tools of peasant life before industrialization, donated by the families of the country. Free admission; temporarily closed for damage to the earthquake 2016.

Lago di Caccamo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hydro-electric basin built in 1954 at 297 meters, about 3 km long on 66 hectares: fishing place (trot, carp, tinche), rowing and canoeing, with regattas on the summer weekends.

  • Where Località Caccamo, Serrapetrona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.comune.serrapetrona.mc.it

Sources: turismo.comune.serrapetrona.mc.it

Castel San Venanzo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified village on a spur of Mount Letegge, contested in the thirteenth century between Camerino and San Severino and fortified by Camerino at the beginning of the fourteenth century; there are the church of San Lorenzo and, downstream, the church of Madonna del Ponte.
