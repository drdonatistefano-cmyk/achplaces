What to see in Mercogliano

At the foot of the Partenio, the municipality of the sanctuary of Montevergine, founded in 1126 by St. William of Vercelli, and the eighteenth-century Abbey Palace of Loreto which houses the library of monks.

3 places to visit in Mercogliano, in the province of Avellino (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Mercogliano in the guide with map and filters

Santuario di Montevergine

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Monastery complex founded in 1126 by St. William of Vercelli, one of the most visited Marian shrines in the South: in the ancient basilica the Gothic portal of the end of the thirteenth century, the cosmatescan canopy and the wooden choir of 1573; in the new church (1952-61) the table of the "Mamma Schiavona" of Montano d'Arezzo, of the end of the thirteenth century; in the crypt the tomb of the founder.

  • Where Via Santuario, Mercogliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it

Museo dell'Abbazia di Montevergine e Mostra del Presepe

Museums · Old masters

The museum preserves the abbey chair of the 12th century with inlays of Arabic taste, the Madonna of St. William of the late 12th century, a wooden crucifix of the 13th century and oriental icons; next to the permanent exhibition of Neapolitan and world nativity scenes from the 17th to the present day.

  • Where Via Santuario, Mercogliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it

Palazzo abbaziale di Loreto e Biblioteca statale di Montevergine

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between 1734 and 1750 on a project by Domenico Antonio Vaccaro and Michelangelo Di Blasio after the earthquake of 1732, example of Neapolitan Baroque with octagonal plan with frescoed rooms and stuccoes; national monument since 1868, houses the state library with almost 200,000 volumes, 24 codes and 38 incunabula.

  • Where Via D. A. Vaccaro 1, Mercogliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: bibliotecastataledimontevergine.cultura.gov.it · italia.it
