What to see in Framura

Municipality of five villages climbed from the mountain to the sea, Castagnola, Costa, Setta, Ravecca and Anzo, among the most beautiful villages in Italy: towers against pirates, the church of San Martino with the pala di Bernardo Strozzi and the bike path on the old railway track.

6 places to visit in Framura, in the province of La Spezia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di San Martino e torre di Costa

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church built before 1128 with the altarpiece of the Madonna del Rosario by Bernardo Strozzi; the bell tower is a defensive tower of the IX century.

  • Where Costa, Framura
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di San Lorenzo a Castagnola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt several times between the Five and the nineteenth century, it preserves a work by Luca Cambiaso.

  • Where Castagnola, Framura
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Anzo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified village on the sea with sixteenth-century tower, walls, chapel and door against pirates, became in the nineteenth century a holiday place with Mediterranean buildings and gardens.

  • Where Anzo, Framura
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Setta

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

  • Where Setta, Framura

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint
