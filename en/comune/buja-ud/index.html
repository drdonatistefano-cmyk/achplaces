What to see in Buja

In the ancient village of San Lorenzo in Monte, the Museum of Art of the Medal tells about the school of Bujesi medalists of the twentieth century and the history of the territory, next to the parish church of San Lorenzo, mentioned already in 792 and frescoed at the end of the fourteenth century.

Museo d'arte della medaglia e della città di Buja

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 1998 and since 2006 housed in a renovated 18th century rural house, on two floors and six rooms, it has an archaeological section with finds from the Roman age at the beginning of the 19th century and one dedicated to the art medal of the twentieth century, with works by the Bujesian masters and engravers Pietro Giampaoli, Celestino and Vittorio Giampaoli, Guerrino Mattia Monassi, Piero Monassi, Pietro Galina and Enore Pezzetta. A room is dedicated to the earthquake of 1976 with over one hundred and twenty medals of fifty Italian artists; there is also a didactic itinerary on how a medal was born. Timetable to be requested from the Municipality (tel. 0432 960151).

Pieve di San Lorenzo in Monte

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was mentioned already in a diploma of Charlemagne of 792 and consecrated in 1248, revealed with the excavations of 1980-81 a cistern before the fifth century, a furnace for the bronze of the sixth and the phases of the church from the early medieval hall to the Romanesque building of the XII-XIII centuries. The current layout, with a Latin cross with a single nave, dates back to 1871-1883, with pentagonal bell tower renovated in the sixteenth century and a southern Gothic portal with carved architraves (bue, eagle, sun and moon). Inside, a chapel with Stories of the Virgin frescoed at the end of the fourteenth century by a painter from Friuli, paintings by G.B. Grassi dedicated to San Lorenzo and remains of a wooden ancon of 1558.
