What to see in Borrello

Borgo del Chietino known for the Green Falls, indicated as the highest of the Apennines, protected by the Regional Nature Reserve of 287 hectares entirely in the municipal territory, WWF oasis since 1997; in the Town Hall the Civic Museum Memories of peasant life collects the tools of work in the fields.

Riserva naturale regionale Cascate del Verde

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The Green River, fed by perennial waters, falls in three jumps of 40, 90 and 30 meters before joining the Sangro: they are indicated as the highest waterfalls of the Apennines and, since 2016, they are in the National Inventory of ISPRA geosites together with the calanchi of Red Blades. The reserve, established in 2001, WWF oasis since 1997 and ZSC . Abetina of Rosello and Rio Verde Falls, combines mountain and Mediterranean species: white holm oaks and firs in the gorges, river prawns (with an incubator of the LIFE CRAInat project), brown trout, blackbird, pilgrim falcons and royal nibs. From the visitor center with bar start the paths; the main one has steps and slopes towards the viewpoint. Open all year until sunset; the ticket is paid when services and facilities are open.

  • Where Strada Provinciale Sangritana, Borrello
  • Entry €3, children 11-17 years €2, free up to 10 years and members WWF
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.borrello.ch.it

Museo civico Memorie della vita contadina

Museums · Themed

Museum dedicated to the defatigant human work for survival.Museum: tools, instruments and ancient mechanical remedies that survived over the centuries tell the story of the furrow that separates peasant civilization from industrial civilization. The oldest, such as baskets, forks, scythes, mortars and excavation sticks, recall the Neolithic passage from the harvest of spontaneous fruits to work to produce what is needed to live. Open from June to September, 9-14.

  • Where Piazza G. Marconi 13, presso il Palazzo Comunale, Borrello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: isprambiente.gov.it · italia.it
