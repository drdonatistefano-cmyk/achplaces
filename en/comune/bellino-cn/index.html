What to see in Bellino

With Pontechianale, the highest municipality in Val Varaita, divided into ten hamlets of stone and wood: it houses 34 restored sundials dated between 1734 and 1934, the Museum of Time and Meridian in Celle, the fifteenth-century parish church of San Giacomo and the eighteenth-century church of the Holy Spirit.

Museo del Tempo e delle Meridiane

Museums · Themed

It tells the sundials preserved among the old houses of Bellino, painted on private houses and chapels and along the paths, recovered since 1999 on impulse of the Municipality with European funds of the LAG Valli del Viso. Open in June, July and September Saturday and Sunday 15-18, in August every 15-18; in winter on call for groups (tel. +39 0175 689 629).

  • Where Borgata Celle, Bellino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.bellino.cn.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · comune.bellino.cn.it

Percorso delle meridiane tra le borgate

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A heritage of 34 restored solar quadrants (plus two new ones), dated between 1734 and 1934, recovered with the project "Bellino Solare." The recommended itinerary starts from Ribiera (1412 m) and Chiesa, goes up to Fontanile and along the GTA in the woods, passes under Pleyne, crosses Prafauchier and Celle and arrives at Chiazale (1710 m): about 12 km round trip, 300 m of difference in altitude; the villages are also reached by car.

  • Where Da borgata Ribiera a borgata Chiazale, Bellino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.bellino.cn.it

Sources: comune.bellino.cn.it

Parrocchiale di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The fifteenth-century parish church in the hamlet of Chiesa, the centre of the municipality; on its walls is painted the motto "Sans soleil ne dit rien du tout c'est a-dire" (Sans soleil ne dit rien du tout c'est a-dire).

  • Where Borgata Chiesa, Bellino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.bellino.cn.it

Sources: comune.bellino.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Borgate di pietra e legno

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ten villages (Bals, Celle, Chiazale, Chiesa, Fontanile, Masdelbernard, Pleyne, Prafouchier, Ribiera, Sant'Anna) retain traditional stone and wood architecture, with round columns in masonry up to 10 meters high, among woods, pine forests and alpine pastures.

  • Where Borgate di Bellino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.bellino.cn.it

Sources: comune.bellino.cn.it
