What to see in Costigliole d'Asti

One of the most important villages in Piedmont, the kingdom of Barbera: the Fortress with the castle of the Asinari, linked to the Countess of Castiglione and the seat of the ICIF cooking school, the Museum of Barbera in the municipal palace, the hamlets of Loreto and Burio and the panorama of Bricco Lù.

6 places to visit in Costigliole d'Asti, in the province of Asti (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello degli Asinari

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

On the Fortress, castle of the Tre-Quattrocento restored in Gothic style and revisited in the nineteenth century, with a juvarrian staircase and a large park. There lived the Countess of Castiglione, who married Count Francesco Verasis Asinari of Costigliole very young; today hosts ICIF, Italian cooking school for foreigners, and the Consortium Barbera d'Asti and Vini del Monferrato. Visitable in some periods, closed from November to March.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II 8, Costigliole d'Asti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it

Museo della Barbera

Museums · Themed

In the nineteenth-century palace, at the Cantina Comunale dei Vini: the route starts from the study of the land of Costigliole, the "Città del Vino" and continues with the utensils of the wine tradition of the territory, yesterday and today.

  • Where Via Roma 9, Costigliole d'Asti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlmr.it

steep alleys and stairs go up to the Fortress, dominated by the castle; in the center the Confraternity of San Gerolamo, home of the Museum of Sacred Art.

Frazioni di Loreto e Burio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Loreto was rebuilt in a lower position and hidden after the Asti people destroyed the castle in 1255, so much so that today it is not seen from the nearby villages; the parish church of Our Lady of Loreto has an altar attributed to Juvarra. In Burio a castle-fortress remained faithful to the original architectures.

  • Where Frazione Loreto, Costigliole d'Asti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it

Bricco Lù

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Panoramic point with a view over the whole Monferrato and beyond, linked to a demonic legend with protagonists Poldo and Gentucca; there stands the Monument to the Country Woman.

  • Where Bricco Lù, Costigliole d'Asti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it

Vigneti di Barbera e Riserva del Rio Bragna

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

With Santo Stefano Belbo is the most lively village in Piedmont: in the hamlets dominates Barbera, among farmsteads, ciabòt, bourgeois villas and campestri churches, and survives the small production of Uvalino linked to Count Filippo Asinari of San Marzano, who introduced Chardonnay in Piedmont. Between Costigliole and Isola d'Asti extends the Nature Reserve of Rio Bragna.

  • Where Frazioni di Costigliole d'Asti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlmr.it

Sources: visitlmr.it
