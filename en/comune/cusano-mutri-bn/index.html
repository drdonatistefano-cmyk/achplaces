What to see in Cusano Mutri

Small mountain village in the Matese Regional Park, with a historic centre of alleys and stairs, the church of San Giovanni Battista with the Santa Spina and, about 5 km, the Forre di Lavello dug by the Titerno.

5 places to visit in Cusano Mutri, in the province of Benevento (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

A labyrinth of alleys and stairs: from Piazza Orticelli, going up Via Cerro, you will encounter the remains of the Tower and Castle, Piazza Lago, the Tower of Via Ripe, the Long Quarter with the Porta di Mezzo, the parish church of San Nicola and Piazza Roma with its stone staircase.

  • Where Piazza Orticelli, Cusano Mutri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa dei Santi Apostoli Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Church in a panoramic position in the historic center, recognizable from afar for the bell tower and the dome.

  • Where Centro storico, Cusano Mutri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista e Spina Santa

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

He kept the Holy Spina, which tradition attributed to the crown of Christ and which according to the chronicles was reddened in 1693 and on February 3, 1710; a notary act of August 3, 1805 recalls another episode during a penitential procession, repeated again every August 3.

  • Where Cusano Mutri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Forre di Lavello e Ponte del Mulino

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Canyon of the Matese Regional Park between Mount Erbano and Mount Cigno, excavated by the Tierno in limestone with walls in some points over 30 meters; a path with wooden walkways follows an ancient track Sannita and touches the Grotta dei Briganti, the Grotta delle Fate, the Grotta delle Streghe, the Belvedere, the Mulino di Zì Fiore and the Bridge of the Mulino, an ancient arched bridge used to reach the mill until the 1950s.

  • Where Circa 5 km dal centro, Cusano Mutri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Museo civico del territorio

Museums · Themed

On the ground floor of the Town Hall, with a section of peasant civilization (about 300 objects, domestic interiors of the late nineteenth century, costumes, the collar nailed sheepdogs and a wooden spoon of 5.32 m) and a geopaleontological section with casts of fossils of Pietraroja and the diorama of Cyrus, the Scipionyx samniticus.

  • Where Via Municipio, Cusano Mutri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it
