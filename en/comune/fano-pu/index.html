What to see in Fano

The ancient Fanum Fortunae, where the Via Flaminia arrives to the Adriatic: the Augustus Arch of 2 AD and the Roman walls, the basilica designed by Vitruvio re-emerged in piazza Andrea Costa, the Palazzo Malatestiano with museum and gallery, the Pala di Perugino in Santa Maria Nuova and the beaches of Lido and Saxony.

13 places to visit in Fano, in the province of Pesaro e Urbino (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Arco di Augusto e mura augustee

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Roman triumphal arch of 2 AD, wanted by Augustus: it was the gate of the city of Fanum Fortunae and marks the point where the Via Flaminia reaches the Adriatic; around remains traces of the Roman walls.

  • Where Via Arco d'Augusto, Fano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Museo archeologico e Pinacoteca del Palazzo Malatestiano

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the Palazzo Malatestiano, 16 rooms with archaeological sections, ceramics and numismatics, from prehistory to the Roman age, and the art gallery with paintings of the following eras. Closed on Monday.

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre 4, Fano
  • Entry €4 full, €2 reduced; free under 18 and the second Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museocivico.comune.fano.pu.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Pinacoteca San Domenico

Museums · Old masters

In the monumental church of San Domenico, with the tomb of Jacopo del Cassero, the Fondazione Cassa di Risparmio di Fano exhibits sacred art from the 14th to the 18th century: the Marriage of the Virgin of Guercino (1649), works by Simone Cantarini, Warriors, Barocci, Palma il Giovane and Zuccari.

  • Where Via Arco d'Augusto, Fano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Basilica di Vitruvio

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

The remains of the basilica designed by Vitruvio, born in Fanum Fortunae, the only building that the architect claims to have followed in person: re-emerged in the excavations of Andrea Costa square, corresponds in form and proportions to the description of De Architectura.

  • Where Piazza Andrea Costa, Fano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo della Via Flaminia

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The museum dedicated to the Via Flaminia, the Roman road that reached the sea in Fano; open on Friday afternoon, Saturday and Sunday.

  • Where Via Arco d'Augusto, Fano
  • Entry €4 full, €3 reduced; single ticket with the Civic Museum €10
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museocivico.comune.fano.pu.it · italia.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built in the second half of the 12th century on the ruins of a church destroyed by a fire, with three naves: pulpit with Romanesque reliefs of the childhood of Christ, the Nolfi Chapel frescoed by Domenichino (1618-1619) and a altarpiece by Ludovico Carracci.

  • Where Via Arco d'Augusto, Fano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Nuova

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It preserves the Pala di Durante di Perugino, with the Madonna enthroned and saints, the Pietà and the predella with stories of the Virgin, returned to its altar in January 2026 after the restoration at the Opificio delle Pietre Dure; guided tours to the altars of Perugino and Giovanni Santi on Saturday and Sunday.

  • Where Fano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.fano.pu.it · eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Corte Malatestiana

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

The palace wanted by Pandolfo III and built between 1414 and 1421 in late-Gothic style, with portico and double-fores in terracotta, in front of the ancient houses of the Malatesti; after 1463 it was the residence of the Gonfaloniere and the Priors and since 1954 it hosts in summer opera, theatre and concerts.

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre, Fano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Teatro della Fortuna

History and architecture · Historic theatres

The main theatre of Fano, named after the ancient Fanum Fortunae: it hosts opera, prose and dance; renovated and reopened in 2002.

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre 1, Fano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.fano.pu.it · italia.it

Mediateca Montanari

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

It rises on an important area of the Roman Fano, a large space surrounded by arcades with brick columns; the building was designed by Giuseppe Balducci reusing the materials of a demolished convent.

  • Where Fano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · museocivico.comune.fano.pu.it · italia.it

Rocca Malatestiana

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The fortress of the Malatesti on the northern corner of the Roman walls, where an ancient tower is supposed to be incorporated.

  • Where Fano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Eremo di Monte Giove

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill of the same name (223 m) about two miles from the centre, founded in the first decades of the seventeenth century by the Camaldolese of Monte Corona and completed in 1627; the church of the Savior was rebuilt in 1741 by Gian Francesco Buonamici. Cristina stayed there in 1657.

  • Where Monte Giove, Fano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Spiagge del Lido e della Sassonia

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

On both sides of the fishing port: the long pebble beach of Saxony, with a large promenade, south-east of the railway, and the shorter beach of the Lido to the north-west.

  • Where Lungomare, Fano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it
