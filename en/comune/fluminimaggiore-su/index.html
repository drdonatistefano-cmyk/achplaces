What to see in Fluminimaggiore

The country of the Iglesiente surrounded by about thirty sites of mining archaeology, with the Roman temple of Antas dedicated to the Sardus Pater, the caves of Su Mannau linked to an ancient water cult, the water mill Licheri of 1750 and, about 10 km, the beach of Portixeddu.

Tempio di Antas

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In a valley of the Iglesiente dominated by Mount Conca s'Omu, about 10 km from the village: sacred place already in the Nuragic age, with a necropolis with wells and a bronze of male deity, then punic sanctuary of Sid Addir (V century BC, renovated at the end IV) and finally Roman temple of Augustian age dedicated to the Sardus Pater Babai, restored under Caracalla in 213-217 A.D. as the epigraph of the pediment recalls. The pronao has ionic columns about eight meters high; the cell, eleven meters deep, preserves traces of white mosaic and the dyton two tanks for purification rites. Rediscovered by La Marmora in 1836 and restored in 1967; about 20 minutes walk to the Roman quarry and an ancient path to Su Mannau.

  • Where Strada per Antas (bivio dalla SS 126), Fluminimaggiore
  • Entry For a fee; cumulative with the Mill Licheri €7
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it · sardegnacultura.it · cultura.gov.it

Grotte di Su Mannau

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Karst complex about 5 km from the village, which the Touring Club has included among the ten most beautiful caves in Italy: a route on suspended walkways of about 500 meters between red rocks, white stalactites and lakes fed by the underground rivers Placido and Rapido, with the Pozzo Rodriguez 23 meters deep and a stalagmitic column of 7 meters. In the Archaeological Chamber were found many oil lamps, a sign of a cult of water already pre-nuragic; in the lakes lives the Stenasellus nuragicus. Visit of about an hour, every day 9:30-17.

  • Where Località Su Mannau, Fluminimaggiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Museo etnografico Antico mulino ad acqua Licheri

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Water mill of 1750 in the center of the village, still moved by the Riu Mannu that flows under the building and able to grind wholemeal flour. The halls, ordered by master Bruno Murtas, exhibit tools of the peasant house, of the shepherds and of the crafts (brands for the cattle, wooden, cork and iron measures, tools for cheese, weaving and bread, furnishings, accessories and toys) and two vehicles of the late nineteenth century, an ox-cart and a carriage. Tuesday-Sunday, with seasonal hours (July-August 10-13 and 15-19); wheelchair accessible entry.

  • Where Piazza Antonio Gramsci, Fluminimaggiore
  • Entry €3, reduced €2; cumulative with the Temple of Antas €7
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Spiaggia di Portixeddu

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the tourist hamlet about 10 km from the village, a long beach of fine ochre sand divided by the mouth of the Rio Mannu and closed by a cliff with natural pools; along the coast also the coves of Guardia is Turcus, Sa Perdixedda Manna and Sa Perdixedda Pittica.

  • Where Portixeddu, Fluminimaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
