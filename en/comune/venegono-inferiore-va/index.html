Municipality of Varesotto known for the great Archbishop's Seminary of Milan, wanted by Cardinal Schuster and started in 1928, which houses the Museum of Natural History .

Seminario arcivescovile di Venegono

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Viewpoint

A vast complex, with an area of about 40 times the camp of San Siro, whose first stone was laid on February 6, 1928, wanted by Cardinal Schuster to form the Ambrosian priests away from the city. The 64-metre tower stands out, which since the 1930s has hosted the Observatory of Earth Physics for almost half a century; the library has more than 140,000 volumes and the theology chapel is decorated by A. Bergagna. Library and chapel are to be visited on the occasion of the Feast of the Seminary on 1 May.

Museo di storia naturale «Antonio Stoppani»

Museums · Science and university

Set up in the Seminary in 1930 with fossils collected by Antonio Stoppani, geological collections of Giuseppe Mercalli and shells, titled to Stoppani in 1952: 104 windows with almost 6,000 finds among zoology (about 1,400 naturalized animals, including a great crocodile of the Nile), paleontology (about 2,500 fossils for geological era, total collection over 12,000 pieces centered on the Lombard Triassic), geology and mineralogy and archaeology. It offers educational routes for schools.

  • Where Via Pio XI 32, Venegono Inferiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.seminario.milano.it

Sources: seminario.milano.it
