What to see in Revello

Borgo Bandiera Arancione at the entrance of the Po valley, dominated by the Mombracco, the mountain of Leonardo, with the chapel of the Marquises of Saluzzo and the Cistercian Abbey of Staffarda.

Abbazia di Santa Maria di Staffarda

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded by the Cistercians in 1135 in the Saluzzo plain a few steps from the Po, it is one of the most important medieval monasteries of Piedmont; in 1690 the battle of Staffarda was fought. Owned by the Foundation Ordine Mauriziano.

  • Where Piazza Roma 3, Staffarda, Revello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitcuneese.it · ordinemauriziano.it · touringclub.it

Cappella Marchionale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Late-Gothic environment entirely frescoed in the cylindrical tower of the current town hall, once Castello Sottono, a favourite residence of the Marquises of Saluzzo; the pictorial cycle of the early sixteenth century was wanted by Margherita di Foix.

  • Where Piazza Denina 4, Revello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · visitcuneese.it · italia.it

Collegiata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

From the 15th century, with a precious Renaissance portal and altarpieces, two attributed to Pascale Oddone and one from 1503 to Hans Clemer.

  • Where Revello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · visitcuneese.it · italia.it
