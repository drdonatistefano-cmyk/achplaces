What to see in Valtorta

Mountain village in the upper Val Brembana, at the foot of the Bobbio Planes: a museum path that combines the ethnographic museum in the House of the Pretura, the mill and the sweater with the wind trumpet of the Oasis of the Bolgià, reached by a mule track and a stone bridge, and the water sawmill on the stream Val Marcia.

5 places to visit in Valtorta, in the province of Bergamo (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo civico etnografico dell'Alta Val Brembana

Museums · Themed

In the historic House of the Pretura, tools and objects of use of the Upper Brembana Valley with rebuilt living environments, to understand how farmers and artisans have adapted their instruments over the centuries; tickets to the Pro Loco.

  • Where Via Torre 1, Valtorta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valtorta.bg.it

Sources: comune.valtorta.bg.it

Oasi del Bolgià: mulino e maglio

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Along the CAI 104 mule track, beyond the Stabina stream on a stone donkey-back bridge, the corn, rye and barley mill, active until the eve of World War II, and the hammer with forge, mole and the wind trumpet blowing air into the fire; the wheels are moved by the water of the Grobbia Valley.

  • Where Località Bolgià, Valtorta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valtorta.bg.it

Sources: comune.valtorta.bg.it

Segheria idraulica

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Didactic structure rebuilt with pieces of an ancient sawmill in the valley, on the stream Val Marcia: the water wheel and the mechanisms show how wood was cut with the force of water. Visitable only by reservation.

  • Where Via Comm. Pietro Busi, Valtorta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valtorta.bg.it

Sources: comune.valtorta.bg.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built between 1898 and 1904 incorporating the ancient church, visited by St. Charles Borromeo in 1566: two Renaissance polyptychs, a Madonna on copper by Pietro Mera, the 17th century altar in golden wood and, on the ancient apse, 16th century frescoes with the lion of St. Mark.

  • Where Piazza Stefano Gervasoni 1, Valtorta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valtorta.bg.it

Sources: comune.valtorta.bg.it

Piani di Bobbio e pista Tre Signori

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

The Valtorta-Piani ski area of Bobbio-Barzio, with the cross-country trail Tre Signori and, in summer, mountain bike trails.

  • Where Piani di Valtorta, Valtorta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valtorta.bg.it

Sources: comune.valtorta.bg.it
