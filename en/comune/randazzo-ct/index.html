What to see in Randazzo

Medieval town on the north side of Mount Etna, in the middle of three protected areas (Etna, Nebrodi and Alcantara), built around three lava stone churches (Santa Maria, San Nicolò and San Martino) that correspond to the Latin, Greek and Lombard districts; in the Swabian Castle the Archaeological Museum Paolo Vagliasindi is located.

Castello svevo e Museo archeologico Paolo Vagliasindi

Museums · Archaeological

The museum is housed in the Swabian Castle, the last of the eight towers of the walls, called the Man for its key role in the defence of the city; in the thirteenth century its secrets were used as torture chambers for prisoners and condemned. It collects finds from the VI-IV century BC, largely from the nineteenth-century collection of Paolo Vagliasindi and mainly from the area of Sant'Anastasia, in the territory of Randazzo.

Basilica di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Basilica minor and main church of the city, in lava stone and in Norman-Svevo style; it houses the Salvation of Randazzo, sixteenth-century table that shows the city as it was at the time.

  • Where Centro storico, Randazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info

Chiesa di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The largest church in the city, dating back to the fourteenth century, rebuilt at the end of the sixteenth century, with an eighteenth-century bell tower; it preserves works by Gagini, family of sculptors and architects.

  • Where Centro storico, Randazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The Swabian church of the thirteenth century, known for its large bell tower next to it; it was the church of one of the three historic districts of the city.

  • Entry Free

Centro storico, Via degli Archi e Porta Aragonese

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The medieval village is spread around the three churches of Santa Maria, San Nicolò and San Martino, which correspond to the Latin, Greek and Lombard districts; not to be missed is the romantic Via degli Archi, while at the Porta Aragonese there are still parts of the walls.

  • Where Centro storico, Randazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo civico di scienze naturali

Museums · Science and university

Established in 1983 and opened in 1989, it has its core in the ornithological collection begun by Angelo Priolo in 1939: about 2,000 specimens of about 400 species, precious for the study of Sicilian birdfauna, with extinct species on the island such as the Francoline and the latea. The geological collection of Luigi Lino includes fossils, minerals, rocks and shells especially Sicilian, pleistocene fauna, volcanic products of Etna and fossil fish; the diorama of the valley of the Rosemary stream stands out, from which the griffins disappeared in 1965 by poisoning and where they were re-introduced with LIPU and Nebrodi Park.

The MiC card (updated to March 2021) is closed every day; tel. 095 921615

  • Where Via Umberto I, Randazzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Ferrovia Circumetnea

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

Railway line of the end of the nineteenth century that turns around the Etna from Catania Borgo to Riposto, 110 km in about 3 hours; Randazzo is located halfway through and is the far north of the Catania-Randazzo stretch where you can climb with your bike.

  • Where Stazione di Randazzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
