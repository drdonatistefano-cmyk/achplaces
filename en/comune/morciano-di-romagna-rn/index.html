Antica Fiera di San Gregorio

Local life and traditions · Historic markets · Free entry

One of the most important fairs in Romagna for centuries, in March: exhibitions of animals, agricultural machinery, handicrafts, temporary taverns and amusement park.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Morciano di Romagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
