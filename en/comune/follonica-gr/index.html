What to see in Follonica

The seaside town of the Gulf of the same name was born as a steel pole: the former Ilva area with the MAGMA museum and the Library of Ghisa, the church of San Leopoldo with the cast iron pronao and the Etruscan site of the ovens of Rondelli; sandy beaches and the pine forest of Boschetto.

9 places to visit in Follonica, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

MAGMA, Museo delle Arti in Ghisa della Maremma

Museums · Themed

Born in 1970 and since 1998 in the Forno di San Ferdinando, the oldest building in the city, tells Follonica, the main iron centre of the Grand Duchy since 1836, on three floors with interactive installations, wooden models and nineteenth-century castings; the Etruscan section exhibits the finds of Rondelli, with a 6th century BC oven. It is a gateway to the Park of the Metalliferous Hills.

  • Where Area ex Ilva, Via Amorotti, Follonica
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.comune.follonica.gr.it

Sources: comune.follonica.gr.it · cultura.gov.it · visittuscany.com · parcocollinemetallifere.it

Comprensorio ex Ilva e Biblioteca della Ghisa

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient steel complex, among the main sites of industrial archeology of Tuscany, opens with a monumental gate in cast iron of the nineteenth century; closed in 1960, houses the MAGMA, the Foundry Theatre Leopolda and the Library of the Ghisa in the The old town coincides with the fence that enclosed the ironworks.

  • Where Area ex Ilva, Follonica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · parcocollinemetallifere.it

Chiesa di San Leopoldo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Wanted by Grand Duke Leopold II, begun in 1836, consecrated in 1838 and completed in 1841, it is neoclassical Latin cross with pronao, columns, balustrade, arch and friezes (of the sculptor Nencini) in cast iron of the Ilva foundries, among the first Tuscan examples of iron architecture; cast iron also pulpit, balustrade of the presbytery and candlesticks.

  • Where Via Roma 85, Follonica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Pinacoteca civica

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

Founded in 1995 for public works of art and donations, it is located in a late-liberty palace of 1922-1923 on the promenade square and houses in two temporary exhibitions of modern and contemporary art; admission to free exhibitions.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo 2, Follonica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.follonica.gr.it · visittuscany.com

Area archeologica dei Forni fusori di Rondelli

History and architecture · Archaeology

Etruscan site of steel production between the beginning of the 6th and the end of the 5th century BC, linked to Populonia and Vetulonia, where the hematite of Elba was worked: an industrial area with ovens and a housing; the land conserves slag, coal and cookware.

  • Where Rondelli, Follonica
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visittuscany.com

Casello idraulico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Nineteenth-century building of the custodians of the reclamation works, rectangular plan with central tower; on the back a large garden with exhibition room.

  • Where Via Roma, Follonica
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

MeQ, mercato coperto

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry

Covered market inaugurated in 2023 with fresh fish, tortelli maremmani, oven and sea specialties such as cacciucco and fried; the unsold is donated to Caritas.

Spiagge di Pratoranieri e del Boschetto

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

The coast from the sandy bottom slowly descending, several times Blue Flag; Pratoranieri, about 5 km from the center, has golden sand and clear waters, the Boschetto is in a pine forest behind high dunes.

  • Where Follonica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · parcocollinemetallifere.it

Parco interprovinciale di Montioni

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Area of 4.494 hectares in the province of Livorno and 1.990 in that of Grosseto, with oak and holm oak woods linked to the production of coal and wood; inside remains of alum quarries and a mining village of Napoleonic era.

  • Where Montioni, Follonica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
