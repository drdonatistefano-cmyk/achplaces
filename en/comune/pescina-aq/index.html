What to see in Pescina

Marsicano village from the medieval center dominated by the tower of the Rocca Vecchia dei Piccolomini, home of Cardinal Giulio Mazzarino and writer Ignazio Silone, both remembered by museum houses.

4 places to visit in Pescina, in the province of L'Aquila (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Casa museo Giulio Mazzarino

Museums · House museums

Municipal Museum dedicated to Cardinal Giulio Raimondo Mazzarino, Prime Minister of France, born in Pescina on July 14, 1602 and died in France in 1661: documents on his life, reading room, specialized library on Abruzzo and space for contemporary art exhibitions, in a building of 1971-72. Open Tuesday and Thursday 10.30-19, Saturday and Sunday 10.30-12.

  • Where Viale Francia / Piazzale Rancilio, Pescina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Casa museo e Centro studi Ignazio Silone

Museums · House museums

The Study Centre keeps documents and testimonies on the life and works of the writer, born in Pescina in the house today museum, and organizes every year the Literary Prize Silone.

Basilica di Sant'Antonio di Padova

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Saint Francis of Assisi stayed four months in Pescina and began the construction of the current papal basilica; on the right column of the Romanesque-Gothic portal there is a cross linked to his visit.

  • Where Pescina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Centro storico e torre della Rocca Vecchia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Centre with medieval structure with numerous churches, including the cathedral, the Basilica of Santa Maria delle Grazie, bishop's seat of the diocese of Avezzano; it dominates the tower created from the remains of the Old Rocca of the Piccolomini. In the territory also the Torre di Venere dei Marsi of the 13th century and remains of cyclopean walls.

  • Where Centro storico, Pescina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.pescina.aq.it
