What to see in Majano

The hillside municipality of Friuli, where, on the hill of Susans, stands the seventeenth-century castle of Colloredo, a Tuscan villa in Medici style with four corner towers reached by a avenue of cypresses; in the hamlet of San Tomaso the church of San Giovanni dei Templari preserves frescoes from the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries.

Castello di Susans

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

It is in the form desired in 1636 by Count Fabrizio di Colloredo, educated in Tuscany at the court of Ferdinando I: a residence in Medici style, a Tuscan villa, built on medieval ruins and inhabited by the Collored for over three centuries, until the end of the First World War. The project is probably attributed to the Florentine Matteo Nigetti: cubic body with four corner towers, large outer staircase with ramps opposite with balcony and Collored coat of arms, central hall passing high over two floors and chapel on the noble floor. A avenue of hundred-year-old cypress trees leads to the façade. Damaged by the earthquake of 1976 and renovated, today hosts exhibitions, cultural events and celebrations.

Chiesa di San Giovanni dei Templari

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the hamlet of San Tomaso that the Touring indicates, with the castle of Susans, as one of the two notable historical-artistic sites of the municipality: it preserves frescoes of the XIII and XIV century.
