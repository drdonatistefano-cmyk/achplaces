Country of Valsugana on the shore of Lake Caldonazzo, with lakefront, beaches and piers; behind the ancient mine of pyrites, closed in 1964 and today mining park and museum, and on the hill the Pieve dell'Assunta and the church of Sant'Ermete, built on a Roman cepe dedicated to Diana.

Parco minerario di Calceranica

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

The pyrite mine, known since the Middle Ages, was an important resource for the country until 1964; the park, inaugurated on July 29, 2008, also in memory of the miners, some of whom lost their lives, is visited along the gallery Leyla, with equipment and materials arranged in a thematic itinerary set up with the Museo Tridentino di Scienze Naturali. The network of tunnels extends for about 40 km until under Bosentino and Vattaro, but most are not accessible. At the Center of mining reception an exhibition of minerals of Trentino and lamps, open from May to August 2026 on Saturday and Sunday 17-19, free admission.

Visit Valsugana indicates the park "Closed simultaneously" (diction "closed in 2020"); morning visits only by reservation at 340 7555284

Museo delle attività minerarie

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Mining equipment from the oldest to the most recent, documents, photographs and maps, drilling machinery, ventilation and transport and panels on the geology of the area. It tells of the first workings in the Mandola valley from 1600 and the industrial exploitation of Montecatini, especially from the second post-war period to 1964, to extract pyrites destined for sulphuric acid. Open all year from Monday to Friday 8.30-12.30.

Lungolago e spiagge del Lago di Caldonazzo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest lake entirely in the province of Trento, about 5 km long just under 450 meters, with waters awarded by the Blue Flag and bathing from May to September. In Calceranica the lakefront, one of the best stretches to walk near the water, connects beaches, meadows and piers to the train station, with kiosks, bars and accesses to the shore, also suitable for strollers; from the piers you come out by sailing boat, rowing, canoeing or dragon boating.
