What to see in Pescia

The "city of flowers" of Valdinievole, home of vivaism; in its territory Collodi, the village of Pinocchio.

5 places to visit in Pescia, in the province of Pistoia (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 3 of the 9 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Parco di Pinocchio

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Opened in 1956, not a carnival but a collective work of artists: the statue of Pinocchio and the Fairy of Emilio Greco, the square of the mosaics of Venturino Venturi, works of Consagra and the landscape of Pietro Porcinai.

  • Where Collodi, Pescia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Giardino Garzoni

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Baroque garden among the greats of Europe, with stairs, statues, labyrinth, theater of verzura, the water staircase and the statue of Fama; the villa "of the hundred windows" is not open to visitors.

  • Where Piazza della Vittoria 1, Collodi, Pescia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Butterfly House di Collodi

Nature and landscape · City parks · Paid entry

Stone and crystal greenhouse in the Garzoni garden with over a thousand species of butterflies from all over the world.

  • Where Collodi, Pescia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Borgo di Collodi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval village whose houses descend as a cascade from the hill to the villa Garzoni; here Carlo Lorenzini passed his childhood and took his pseudonym.

  • Where Collodi, Pescia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo della Carta

Museums · Themed

The museum that tells the paper mills that, with silk factories and tanneries, made the economy of Pescia.

  • Where Pescia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
