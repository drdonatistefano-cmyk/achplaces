What to see in Pollica

Borgo del Cilento on the slopes of Monte Stella, in the National Park, with the castle of the princes Capano, the Franciscan monastery of Santa Maria delle Grazie, the hamlet of Celso with Palazzo Mazziotti and the seaside resorts of Acciaroli and Pioppi, Blue Flag for ten years second italia.it.

Castello dei Principi Capano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

In 1290 he passed to Guido d'Alment, who arrived in Italy with Carlo I d'Angiò, then to the Capanos; the present aspect dates back to the restoration of 1610 wanted by Vincenzo Capano, XV prince of Pollica, and since 1997 he is of the Municipality. It has a massive three-storey square tower and preserves intact ugliaro, oil mill, guard posts and stables; the tradition wants St Alfonso de' Liguori to stay there. It hosts the Centre for Studies on the Mediterranean Diet "Angelo Vassallo" opened in 2010.

Convento francescano di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded in 1611 by the inhabitants with the patronage of Prince Vincenzo Capano, it has the cloister, a frescoed refectory and a single nave church with four altars on its side, three with the Capano coat of arms; the paintings of the ceiling are attributed to Nicola Malinconico, of the school of Luca Giordano, the wooden chest of drawers is by Giuseppe Marrocco da Celso and the stucco by Pietro Sernicola. On July 2 the statue of Our Lady of Graces comes out in procession.

Palazzo Mazziotti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palazzo dei Mazziotti, barons of Celso and opponents of Bourbon absolutism, with fifteenth century plant and current appearance of the late eighteenth century; declared national monument, it preserves frescoes by Matteo Cilento and surrounds three sides of the main square of Celso with a facade with twelve balconies and two watchtowers. The chapel of St. Nicholas is placed on it; in 2010 it appears in the film "We believed" by Mario Martone.

MuSea Museo Vivo del Mare

Museums · Themed

Opened in 1996 in Palazzo Vinciprova in Pioppi, it houses about one hundred marine species in tanks distributed in three rooms that recreate coastal habitats of Cilento, with a tactile tub; on the upper floor the Museum of the Mediterranean Diet dedicated to Ancel Keys, with its library. Since 2013 it is managed by Legambiente with the Mare Nostrum Association and the Municipality.

Sentiero Pioppi - Pozzo dell'Uva Nera - Celso

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

It starts in front of the Museo Vivo del Mare di Pioppi and in about 2 km of easy path through woods reaches the waterfall of the Black Grape Well, so called for a nearby vine; from there about 100 minutes walk through woods and ridges lead to Celso. The path has been restored by Legambiente volunteers.

According to the municipality the trail is not always practicable in winter; ask for updated information from the competent authorities
