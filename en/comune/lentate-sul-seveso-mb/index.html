What to see in Lentate sul Seveso

The municipality of Brianza sul Seveso, on the edge of the Groane Park, which preserves two fourteenth-century oratories linked to the Porro family, Santo Stefano with the cycle on the life of the saint and Santa Maria di Mocchirolo, as well as the MuDi with the civic naturalistic collection and the Lake Azzurro in the park.

Oratorio di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

The private chapel of the fourteenth century (the municipality celebrated 650 years of it in 2019, counting since October 1369) with a cycle painted on the life of Saint Stephen; behind the altar a Crucifixion frescoed by a disciple of Giotto. Stefano Porro appears several times in the frescoes of the nave, in front of his patron saint or hidden among the scenes. Visits by reservation on Sunday, writing to the City Culture Office.

  • Where Piazza San Vito 26, Lentate sul Seveso
  • Entry €5, reduced €3; with guide or audioguide €7, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.lentatesulseveso.mb.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · comune.lentatesulseveso.mb.it · fondoambiente.it

Oratorio di Santa Maria di Mocchirolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises in the large courtyard of Cascina Mocchirolo, very close to the route of the ancient Via Cantura. Dated 1355, it was built by Lanfranco Porro, official of the Visconti; inside there are traces of frescoes attributed to Pecino da Nova, while the original pictorial cycle is today at the Pinacoteca di Brera in Milan. Visits by reservation at the City's Culture Office.

  • Where Via per Mocchirolo, Lentate sul Seveso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.lentatesulseveso.mb.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · comune.lentatesulseveso.mb.it

MuDi Museo Diffuso

Museums · Themed

The civic museum, in the Egidio Isaac Space, houses the naturalistic civic collection, temporary exhibitions and other cultural initiatives. From 17 February 2026 it is open Tuesday and Thursday 9:30-12 and 14-17, Wednesday and Friday 9:30-12, Saturday 9:30-11:30; closed Sunday and Monday except events.

  • Where Via Aureggi 25, Lentate sul Seveso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.lentatesulseveso.mb.it

Sources: comune.lentatesulseveso.mb.it

Lago Azzurro e sentieri del Parco delle Groane

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small lake born from an ancient clay quarry, immersed in the vegetation of the Groane Park and the Briantea Brughiera; in summer the water lilies bloom, many species of birds are observed and you can fish. From the lake start paths between oaks, pine forests and moors.

  • Where Parco delle Groane, Lentate sul Seveso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.lentatesulseveso.mb.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · comune.lentatesulseveso.mb.it
