What to see in Folgaria

Centro dell'Alpe Cimbra marked by the Great War, with the Austro-Hungarian forts of Supreme High and Cherle and the military cemetery, and by the Cold War with the Base Thunder missile base; among the hamlets the eighteenth-century Maso Spilzi, the sanctuary of the Madonna delle Grazie and Guardia, the painted country.

Base Tuono

Museums · Themed

Exhibition on the Cold War (1949-1989), indicated as unique in Europe on the theme, in the former missile base of Passo Coe: missiles pointed to the sky, a hangar with a Nike-Hercules missile with inspection windows, electronic wagons, radar and an underground bunker. The guided tour of about one hour ends in the control room with the simulation of the launch sequence.

  • Where SP143 16, Passo Coe, Folgaria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.basetuono.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: basetuono.it · alpecimbra.it

Maso Spilzi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Maso built in the second half of the eighteenth century by the rich family Spilzi, unique example of alpine settlement between noble residence and fortified complex: buildings around a deep court, west side protected by high walls with walkway, barn gate with wounds for the ropes of a drawbridge. The barn hosts temporary exhibitions and cultural events. It can also be reached on foot through the Ecken Biotope.

  • Where Località Maso Spilzi, Costa di Folgaria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alpecimbra.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · alpecimbra.it

Guardia, il paese dipinto

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

An ancient rural village of Cimbrian origin at 980 meters, under Mount Finnchio, born by tradition as a guard post of Castel Beseno: since 1988 the exhibitions of mural art have painted old and new houses along an itinerary that tells traditions and local customs. The Hofentol waterfall, illuminated in winter, is 15 minutes in the woods.

  • Where Guardia, Folgaria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alpecimbra.it

Sources: alpecimbra.it

Casa Museo Cirillo Grott

Museums · House museums

In the ancient church of Guardia, then atelier of the painter, sculptor and poet Cyril Grott, born in the village: collection-exhibition of his paintings and sculptures, with the public section on the ground floor.

  • Where Località Guardia, Folgaria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website cirillogrott.com

Sources: cirillogrott.com · visittrentino.info · alpecimbra.it

Cascata dell'Hofentol

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Water jump between high rock walls, reachable in about an hour from the parking lot on the highway towards Rovereto through the forest of the Gon, or in a quarter of an hour walk from Guardia; in winter it is illuminated and becomes a monument of light and ice.

  • Where Guardia, Folgaria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.alpecimbra.it

Sources: alpecimbra.it

Forte Sommo Alto

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the seven great Austro-Hungarian forts on the line of about 30 km from Cima Vezzena to Serrada, built between 1911 and 1914 to connect the forts Dosso delle Somme and Cherle: three floors of which one only above ground, three revolving steel domes, covered from 2.80 to 3.80 meters after the first battles of 1915. You can reach it in about 15 minutes from Rifugio Stella d'Italia or 20 from Passo Coe.

Forte Cherle

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fort Austro-Hungarian at 1,445 meters near Malga Cherle, built to bar the plateau: block casemates and battery block connected by a corridor, with 4 domes for obici and a garrison of 5 officers and 128 soldiers. Bombarded immediately by the Italian batteries of the Campomolon, in 1936 it was ruined by the recoverers; you visit some internal passages and from the cover you can see up to Lavarone, Luserna and Cima Vezzena. Easy walk 500 meters from the Hotel Cherle.

  • Where Malga Cherle, Folgaria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alpecimbra.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · alpecimbra.it

Cimitero militare austro-ungarico

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Set up in summer 1915 under the civil cemetery and the parish church of San Lorenzo, expanded in the 1930s with the remains of the cemeteries near the front and reordered in 1971-72: it gathers in four large crypts the remains of 2,500 Austro-Hungarian soldiers, 750 of whom unknown, along the perimeter the monuments of the cemeteries of field.

  • Where Viale Trento, Folgaria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alpecimbra.it

Sources: alpecimbra.it

Santuario della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

At about 2 km from Folgaria near Costa, with origins in the sixteenth century: traditionally a friar built it at the request of Our Lady in 1588; the current building derives from the restorations after the damage of the First World War. Dedicated to the patron saint of Italian skiers, it preserves a terracotta statue on the main altar and on the parish church a golden bronze copy of the Madonna del Duomo of Milan donated by the city in the sixties; on September 8 the statue is carried in procession by ski instructors. Always open.

  • Where Via Madonna delle Grazie 70, Costa di Folgaria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alpecimbra.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · alpecimbra.it
