What to see in Acquasparta

Borgo is between the waters of the Amerino and Furapane sources, dominated by Palazzo Cesi, residence of Federico Cesi founder of the Accademia dei Lincei, which in 1624 hosted Galileo; in the center and territory medieval churches and baroque and castles like Casigliano.

Palazzo Cesi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between 1561 and 1579 on a previous fortress, on a project by Guidetto Guidetti and then by Giovan Domenico Bianchi; here lived Federico Cesi, founder of the Accademia dei Lincei (1603), who hosted Galileo in 1624. On the ground floor mythological vaults inspired by the Metamorphosis of Ovid, on the noble floor wooden coffered ceilings (Throne Room, Emperors' Room, Muse); since 1964 it is of the University of Perugia and hosts guided tours, exhibitions and the Museum of Bees and Honey.

In 2025 began the restoration of the rooms of the noble floor; schedules and tickets on the site of the palace

Centro storico, Corso dei Lincei e Piazza Federico Cesi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

From the Porta Vecchia the Corso dei Lincei crosses the village to the square dedicated to Federico Cesi, on which the palace overlooks; the name of the village comes from the sources of the Amerino and Furapane, which place it between the waters.

  • Where Corso dei Lincei, Acquasparta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Cecilia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Born in the fourteenth century on an oratory of the 11th century and built on the site of a Roman basilica (remaining visible at the apse in Via Colonna), it was transformed by the Cesi and restored in 1761; a single nave with eight chapels and works from the late sixteenth to the late eighteenth century. Chiesa del Santissimo Sacramento

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Wanted in 1684 by the Venerable Company of the Blessed Sacrament and today museum space; the restoration of 1994 brought to light a fragment of Roman mosaic of the first century believed to come from Carsulae and recovered a Last Supper of the eighteenth century.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1294 at the request of Cardinal Matteo Bentivenga, outside the walls, in simple Franciscan style; the fifteenth-century chapel of San Bernardino contains fragments of frescoes and wooden works of the fourteenth century, including the Madonna della Stella and a crucifix from San Giovanni de Butris.

  • Where Acquasparta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa della Madonna del Giglio (del Crocifisso)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Of fourteenth-century origin and rebuilt in the seventeenth century, it is linked to the Compagnia della Madonna del Giglio, founded on 1 November 1300; on the main altar a fresco of the 15th century Madonna del Giglio with the Child.

Casigliano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Fortified village donated to the Abbey of Farfa in 1118 by the Count of Todi and rebuilt from 1524 by Ludovico degli Atti on drawing by Antonio da Sangallo the Younger; the church of San Biagio is of the sixteenth century.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
