What to see in Colleferro

A city founded in 1935, designed by engineer Riccardo Morandi for the workers of the BPD explosive factory, with the church of Santa Barbara, more than 6 km of air-raid shelters excavated in the pozzolana and the Archaeological Museum of the Tolerian territory; on the plateau remain the ruins of the Old Castle.

Museo archeologico del territorio toleriense

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Municipal Museum inaugurated on December 4, 1986 under the patronage of the BPD Defense and Space and the collaboration of the Archaeological Superintendence for Lazio: a continuous path of finds from the Paleolithic to the Middle Ages. The main attraction is the life-size reconstruction of an elephant with straight fangs, lived in the area about 350,000 years ago. It has a specialist library, workshops and teaching room, and coordinates the archaeological mission at the castle of Piombinara.

  • Where Via Fontana dell'Oste 70, Colleferro
  • Entry €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Città Morandiana

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The entire urban plant was designed in 1935 by Riccardo Morandi, with a rationalist plan, for the workers of the Bombrini Parodi Delfino plant (BPD), which exploded in 1938 causing 60 victims. Today, Colleferro is called "Città dello Spazio" for the Avio plant that produces components of ESA launchers.

  • Where Centro, Colleferro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Rifugi antiaerei

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

About 6 km of galleries 3-4 meters high and 3 wide, with 15 entrances, born as pozzolana quarries to build the first houses of the BPD workers. From November 1943 to June 1944 more than 3,000 people were accommodated, with cellar, infirmary, registry, chapel, common rooms and niches for families; restored since 1985, there is also a living crib.

open for the patronal feast of December 4, on the occasion of events or by reservation

Castello Vecchio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Remains of the ancient castle, inserted in the Network of the Historical Houses of Lazio: on a plateau, controlled the Sacco valley and the Latina street. From the castrum Fluminariae would derive the name of the locality.
