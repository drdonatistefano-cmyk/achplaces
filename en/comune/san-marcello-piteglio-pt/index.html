What to see in San Marcello Piteglio

Municipality of Montagna Pistoiese was born in 2017 from the fusion of San Marcello Pistoiese and Piteglio, among chestnut woods, beech woods and streams: the suspended Bridge of Ferriere on the Lima, the medieval Bridge of Castruccio, the Towers of Popiglio, Gavinana with the Ferrucciano Museum and the parish church with the monumental organ, the Ferriera Papini of Maresca and the Observatory of Pian de' Termini.

Ponte sospeso delle Ferriere

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Walking pass 227 meters long and 36 meters high on the Lima stream, between Mammiano Basso and Popiglio, inaugurated in 1923 after about two years of work: the scaffolding rests on four steel ropes without intermediate supports. Proposed by engineer Vincenzo Douglas Scotti to shorten the route of the workers of Popiglio to the ironworks on the other bank, it was the longest suspended pedestrian bridge in the world until 2006.

  • Where Via Anghiari 195, Mammiano, San Marcello Piteglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Ponte di Castruccio (Ponte di Campanelle)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built in 1317 by Castruccio Castracani in stone and river pebbles instead of a Romanesque bridge on the border between Pistoia and Lucca, he climbs over the Lima with a single arch with a donkey back without batteries, from which the name of Ponte del Deavolo. On the banks of the Bell Mill (XVIII-XIX century) and on the other side the Old Customs between Pistoia and Lucca; you can reach from Lolle or Popiglio.

  • Where Località il Cavone, San Marcello Piteglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Torri di Popiglio (Rocca Securana)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Remains of the medieval fortress Securana above Popiglio, probably built on a Roman settlement of the second century BC, grown in Lombard and Carolingian age and under the Counts Guidi, passed to Pistoia in the thirteenth century: it guarded the border with Lucca. Two square towers remain, the one upstream at about half the original height. You can reach it with a short path in the beech forest from a nearby car park or with the Way of St. Bartholomew.

  • Where Sopra Popiglio, San Marcello Piteglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta di Popiglio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church founded in 1271 in an ancient feud of the Guidi Counts; it houses an Assumption of the Virgin, a Last Supper of Sebastiano Viti and a Baroque organ. Next to it, the Diocesan Museum of Sacred Art is the center of the itinerary of the sacred art of the Ecomuseo.

  • Where Popiglio, San Marcello Piteglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo Ferrucciano

Museums · City history

Opened in 1931, in the fourth centenary, in the former Casa Battistini, on whose threshold according to the tradition Francesco Ferrucci was killed by Fabrizio Maramaldo at the Battle of Gavinana on August 3, 1530; re-established in 1957. It has 32 armored loans from the Bargello, 156 coins, documents and relics, a diorama of the battle, two paintings by Massimo d'Azeglio from the Brera Academy and costumes and flags of the historic Florentine Calcio; the Capanna di Ferrucci is part of it. In the square the equestrian statue of Ferrucci by Emilio Gallori; the Feruccian celebrations are held in the first ten days of August.

  • Where Via Geri 9, Gavinana, San Marcello Piteglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museoferrucciano.it

Sources: museoferrucciano.it · visittuscany.com · cultura.gov.it

Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta di Gavinana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of origin of the twelfth century, with sixteenth century aquasantiera in white marble, the Madonna del Rosario of Florentine Domenico Pugliani, the Assumption of the Virgin of Spinamonte (1615) in the apse, two glazed terracottas in the presbytery and two side altarpieces of Santi Buglioni. On the singing hall, the monumental organ, purchased in 1811 by Domenico Achilli from the convent of San Lorenzo di Pistoia: built by Pietro Agati with part of a sixteenth-century instrument by Cesare Romani da Cortona, expanded in 1824 and 1856, has about two thousand rods, three keyboards and two pedals and effects such as roaring and grancassa; every year Italian and foreign organists play there.

  • Where Piazza Aiale 1, Gavinana, San Marcello Piteglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Palazzo Achilli e MuNAP

Museums · Science and university

The sixteenth-century palace left in 1845 by Domenico Achilli to the people of Gavinana, today of the Municipality, is home to the center of the Ecomuseum of the Pistoiese Mountain with information point, exhibitions and workshops, and the Natural Archaeological Museum of the Pistoiese Apennines (Munap), renovated in 2018, with animals, rocks, minerals, fossils and the reconstruction of the skeleton of a cave bear; educational garden with hydraulic machines.

  • Where Gavinana, San Marcello Piteglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.ecomuseopt.it

Sources: ecomuseopt.it · visittuscany.com

Ferriera Papini

Museums · Themed

Considered the oldest ironworks in Tuscany, operating from the fourteenth century until the eighties of the twentieth century, it is an example of industrial archaeology: the mountain steelmaking used water and local woods to work the Elba mineral, enhanced by Cosimo I in the sixteenth century and by the Grand Ducal road of 1778. You can see sweaters, wheels and forges and in the workshops you can try the techniques of the blacksmith; it is the main stage of the itinerary of the Iron of the Ecomuseo.

  • Where Via del Teso 263A, Maresca, San Marcello Piteglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.ecomuseopt.it

Sources: ecomuseopt.it · visittuscany.com

Pian de' Termini, Osservatorio e Parco delle Stelle

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

About 3 km from Gavinana, a view of the valley, the river Limestre and the Apennines, with the Astronomical Observatory of the Pistoiese Mountain and the Park of the Stars, a scientific educational garden with great artistic installations on the Solar System; it is also reached with a CAI path.

San Marcello Pistoiese e la chiesa di San Marcello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint
