What to see in Malnate

A hilly town 8 km from Varese between the Morone and Casnione mountains, crossed by the Lanza stream: here you will find the Civic Museum of Natural Sciences .Mario Realini is located in Villa Braghenti, the Valle del Lanza Park with the quarries of Molera and ancient churches like San Matteo, from the Romanesque apse of about 1000.

Museo civico di scienze naturali «Mario Realini»

Museums · Science and university · Free entry

Born in 1979 at the initiative of the Municipality and Mario Realini, with donations mainly of minerals and fossils; in 1984 Realini discovered the shell Emiliomya malnatensis. Recognized by the Region as a museum collection in 2004, since 2007 it has been in the liberty Villa Braghenti, in the public park of the same name, with eight sections: psammophile, litological, mineralogical, paleontological, palethnological, gemological, zoological and temporary exhibitions. Open on Friday 9-12 and 14-18, Saturday 9-12, first Sunday of the month 14.30-17.30; guided tours and workshops on reservation for a fee.

  • Where Viale Kennedy 2, Malnate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website comune.malnate.va.it

Sources: comune.malnate.va.it · isprambiente.gov.it

Cave di Molera

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

On the eastern side of the Lanza valley, between Malnate and Solbiate with Cagno, the sandstone called molera stone was extracted, used to make the molera; abandoned the activity, the site has become a natural environment, a natural monument since 2015. A dozen main cavities, deep and with high vertical sandstone walls, open mostly along the Path of Fondovalle, between mixed woods of broadleaf trees; remain short stretches of the ancient consort road in cobbled.

  • Where Sentiero di Fondovalle, valle del Lanza, Malnate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.malnate.va.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · comune.malnate.va.it

Parco Valle del Lanza

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Local park of supramunicipal interest with Malnate leader, along with Bizzarone, Cagno, Valmorea and Rodero, along the Lanza stream that descends from the Canton of Ticino to the Olona. Woods, meadows, wetlands, ridges, rural buildings and industrial archaeological sites; along the stream a path connects the village Folla to Mendrisio, on foot or by mountain bike, and the park is crossed by the Valmorea railway, today touristic from Mendrisio to Malnate.

  • Where Partenza dalla frazione Folla, Malnate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page comune.malnate.va.it

Sources: comune.malnate.va.it · vareseturismo.it · in-lombardia.it

Chiesetta di San Matteo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The first historical monument that you will encounter climbing from Folla along the Briantea: the Romanesque apse with blind arches dates back to about 1000, sacristy and bell tower are successive and the front body was rebuilt in the second half of the eighteenth century, with facade remained unfinished. Inside the frescoes of the sixteenth century.

  • Where Lungo la SS 342 Briantea salendo da Folla, Malnate
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.malnate.va.it

Sources: comune.malnate.va.it

Chiesetta di Santa Maria di Monte Morone

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Of ancient Lombard origin, it rises on the homonymous hill and is dedicated to the Madonna: it preserves a sixteenth-century wooden statue of the Madonna della Cintola and remarkable frescoes.

  • Where Monte Morone, Malnate
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.malnate.va.it

Sources: comune.malnate.va.it

Cappella di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the first half of the sixteenth century, it is open on three sides with arches on pillars; on the back wall a fresco with the Mystical Marriage of Saint Catherine.
