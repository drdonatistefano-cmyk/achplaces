What to see in Malo

The centre of Alto Vicentino, marked for centuries by the processing of silk and clay, is known for its Carnival of allegorical chariots: at Palazzo Corielli, next to the homonymous spinning mill, live the Museum of Seric and Laterian Art and the Museum-workshop of mache masks by Guerrino Lovato, while the Casabianca Museum houses over 1200 contemporary graphic works.

Museo Casabianca - Laboratorio per l'arte contemporanea

Museums · Modern art

Active since 1978 thanks to the building offered by the Morandi Bonacossi family and the private collection of GioBatta Meneguzzo, it is a permanent museum of contemporary graphics from the 1960s to the 1990s: over 1200 works by 700 artists including drawings, art prints and multiples, from the Informale to the Trans avant-garde through Pop Art, kinetic art, Nouveau Réalisme, conceptual and poor art, American graffiti artists and German neo-expressionism. Audio guides, guided tours and bookshops.

Museo dell'arte serica e laterizia

Museums · Themed

Opened by the Municipality in 1994 on the initiative of a group of volunteers, it occupies the second floor and the attic of Palazzo Corielli, next to the homonymous spinning mill active until 1962. Two sections: the seric art, from the breeding of the baco to the trature, and the processing of clay from prehistory to large furnaces, with objects, photos and documents; the didactic model of a furnace of the twentieth century with Hoffmann oven on a continuous fire.

  • Where Via Cardinal De Lai 2, Malo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: venetoaroundme.regione.veneto.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo Mondonovo maschere

Museums · Themed

On the first floor of the eighteenth-century Palazzo Corielli, the art master Guerrino Lovato rebuilt his famous Venetian workshop of masks made of mache, transferred to Malo and entrusted to the Municipality to tie it to the local tradition of Carnival; inaugurated on September 25, 2011, it preserves about 250 mache works, about 500 dies, a library-archive, tools and antique furniture. Guided tours by booking for groups up to 25 people; closed in August, Easter and Christmas.

  • Where Via Cardinal De Lai, Malo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it
