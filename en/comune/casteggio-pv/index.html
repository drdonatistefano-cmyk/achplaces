What to see in Casteggio

The historical and cultural centre of Oltrepò Pavese, between plains and hills, the ancient Clastidium of the battle of 222 BC; in the upper part, around the hill of the Pistornile, the parish church of San Pietro Martire and the eighteenth-century Palazzo Certosa Cantù with the Civic Archaeological Museum, and in front of the town hall the Alata Victoria by Pier Enrico Astorri.

Civico Museo Archeologico di Casteggio e dell'Oltrepò Pavese

Museums · Archaeological

Born in 1974 after the discovery of two tombs of Roman age, thanks to Municipality, Archaeological Superintendence, enthusiasts and private donations; it is recognized by the Lombardy Region and is part of the Oltrepò Pavese Museum System. Four sections: geology and paleontology, prehistory and protohistory, Roman and medieval age, private collectionism; among the important pieces the Roman statues of Mercury and Jupiter and the medieval ceramics found in Stradella. Open Monday-Thursday in the morning (Thursday also afternoon) and Sunday afternoon, closed Friday and Saturday; elevator for the first floor.

Palazzo Certosa Cantù

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built between 1700 and 1705 by the Carthusian monks, it passed to private individuals in the nineteenth century; the last owner, Professor Luigi Cantù, left it as a legacy to the City. The municipality indicates it as the most important artistic testimony of the 18th century Casteggiano: restored, it is the center of the cultural life of the city and houses archaeological museum, civic library with multimedia room and events. From the residence you can enjoy a wide view of the Oltrepò hills.

  • Where Via Circonvallazione Cantù 62, Casteggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.casteggio.pv.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Pietro Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

On the Piazza del Pistornile, in the upper part, it was born in 1817 from the transformation of the sixteenth-century college on a project by the architect Giuseppe Marchesi from Pavia. Façade in travertine slabs, bell tower half Romanesque and half Gothic, interior with three naves with frescoed central vault (restored in 1972) and 18th century main altar surmounted by a marble temple. Organ of the Lingiardi brothers, two eighteenth-century paintings, baptistery in polychrome marble, fifteenth-century sacristy with Gothic ribs and a Romanesque plaque in the wall of the rectory.

  • Where Piazza Chiesa 15, Casteggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.casteggio.pv.it · italia.it

Monumento della Vittoria Alata

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Bronze statue of the Alata Victory, symbol of Casteggio, 18.50 meters high, dedicated to the fallen of the First World War: work of the sculptor Pier Enrico Astorri, was inaugurated on 21 November 1926 by Umberto di Savoia; to melt it they served over 25 tons of bronze and a foundry built on purpose. It rises in the Giardino delle Rimembranze in front of the town hall, with two Austrian cannons of the Great War and, since 1937, the travertine urn of the explorer Giuseppe Maria Giuletti, killed in Dancalia. The monument is not accessible but can be seen from Via Castello.

  • Where Parco delle Rimembranze, Via Castello, Casteggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.casteggio.pv.it · italia.it

Pistornile e itinerario del «Castello»

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The oldest part of Casteggio rises on the hill of the Pistornile and its slopes. The municipal itinerary touches Vittoria Alata, the arcades built around 1767 to enlarge the Piazza del Pistornile (where the market was held on Wednesday), Casa Marangoni, which between 1896 and 1899 hosted Albert Einstein, the parish church, Palazzo Mezzabarba (original only towards Via Baratta) and the Certosa Cantù.

  • Where Piazza del Pistornile, Casteggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
