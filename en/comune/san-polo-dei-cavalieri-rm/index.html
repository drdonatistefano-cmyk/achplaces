What to see in San Polo dei Cavalieri

Borgo 651 meters on the southern slopes of Monte Morra, in the Regional Natural Park of the Lucretile Mountains, 51 km from Rome: dominated by the Castle Orsini-Cesi-Borghese, which in 1603 was the seat of the Accademia dei Lincei, preserves the churches of San Nicola di Bari and Santa Lucia and is the starting point of the path to the Pratone di Monte Gennaro.

Castello Orsini-Cesi-Borghese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Rectangular fortress with central mast and semicircular towers, born between 1029 and 1081 when the monks fortified a watchtower in the Fundum Sancti Pauli; passed to the Orsini (feud since 1429), in 1558 to the Cesi, who in 1603 established the headquarters of the Accademia dei Lincei, and then to the Borghese. Acquired by the Municipality in 1989 and restored, today it is privately owned and not accessible: it is admired from the outside, in the square of the village.

Private property, not accessible (MiC: closed daily)

Chiesa di San Nicola di Bari

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish church built between the 13th and 14th centuries, with an ancient tower of the walls transformed into a bell tower. Inside a Baptism of Jesus of the end of the sixteenth century with Castrum in the background and, in the middle of the ceiling, the Glory of St. Nicholas, trompe-l'œil that simulates the sky with saints, angels and God.

  • Where San Polo dei Cavalieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Santa Lucia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The oldest church in the village, in Castrum Sancti Pauli, which preserves almost intact the original forms: Greek cross plan, wooden trusses ceiling and bell tower of about 1400.

Sentiero CAI 303 al Pratone di Monte Gennaro

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hiking trail (E) of 8 km, about 2 hours and 30, with 400 m of climb, walkable, by bike and horse, which starts from the last houses of the village along the road to Monte Morra. Crossing karst fields with outlying rocks and wide view of the Aniene valley, Marcellina and Palombara Sabina, go back Valle Cavalera between secular beech trees and agrifogli, where you will meet horses in the wild, up to the Pratone, karst field with sinkholes and swallows at the foot of Monte Gennaro.

  • Where Strada provinciale per Monte Morra, San Polo dei Cavalieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com
