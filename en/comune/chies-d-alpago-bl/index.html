What to see in Chies d'Alpago

Municipality of the Alpago basin, dominated to the north by the chain that goes from Mount Teverone to the Horse; it houses the Museum of Natural History of the Alpago and is crossed by the Path of Sensitivity, footpath designed also for visually impaired people.

Museo di Storia Naturale dell'Alpago

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

In the former primary school, designed for about 25 years by naturalist Ivan Fossa. On three floors: zoology of the vertebrates of Belluno; insects, molluscs, geology, mineralogy, paleontology (with a rare jaw of dental) and Roman finds from the site of Staol of Curago; botany with dioramas, mushrooms, xiloteca and fishes, including the Sampierolo, endemic to Lake Santa Croce. Guided tours by reservation for groups of at least 6 people.

  • Where Viale Manzoni 215, Chies d'Alpago
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.dolomitiprealpi.it

Sources: dolomitiprealpi.it

Sentiero della Sensibilità

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The footpath is 24 km from Carota (Pieve d'Alpago) to Sant'Anna di Tambre, which connects Alpago, Chies d'Alpago and Tambre to the base of the main mountain groups of the valley, with historical evidences and natural landscapes; between the intermediate accesses San Martino d'Alpago, Funes and Pian Formosa. The difference in height does not exceed 300 m, it is also designed for visually impaired people and two stretches can be traveled by wheelchair; a free app offers descriptive audio tracks.

  • Where San Martino d'Alpago, Chies d'Alpago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.dolomitiprealpi.it

Sources: dolomitiprealpi.it
