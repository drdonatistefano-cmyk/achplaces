What to see in Murlo

Medieval village walled above the valley of Crevole, between Val di Merse and Val d'Arbia, in a land of Etruscan sites.

Borgo murato di Murlo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The small medieval village closed by the walls that dominates the valley of Crevole, a dense forest between Val di Merse and Val d'Arbia.

Riserva Naturale del Basso Merse
