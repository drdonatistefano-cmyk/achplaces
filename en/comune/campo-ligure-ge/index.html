What to see in Campo Ligure

Borgo della valle Stura, among the most beautiful villages in Italy, capital of the filigree with the museum Pietro Carlo Bosio, dominated by the castle and crossed by the medieval bridge on the Stura.

6 places to visit in Campo Ligure, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Filigrana Pietro Carlo Bosio

Museums · Themed

About 200 works in filigree ordered by origin: Europe, Asia, Africa and Latin America; the tradition comes from the workshop opened in 1884 by Antonio Olivieri.

  • Where Campo Ligure
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The 12th century walls and the most recent tower, restored in 1310 and abandoned in the 18th century; today it houses concerts and events.

  • Where Campo Ligure
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Ponte medievale sullo Stura

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Built in the 9th century and rebuilt several times for the floods, the last in 1841, preserving the ancient appearance.

  • Where Campo Ligure
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Palazzo Spinola

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

On the main square, built in the first half of the fourteenth century by the Marquises Spinola and expanded in 1693.

Oratori dei Santi Sebastiano e Rocco e di Nostra Signora Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The first, Baroque in 1647, with a martyrdom of San Sebastiano from the Piola school; the second, documented since 1585 and rebuilt in the eighteenth century, with altars and a Crucifix.
