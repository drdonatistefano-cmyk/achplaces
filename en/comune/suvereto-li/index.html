What to see in Suvereto

Medieval village of the Val di Cornia surrounded by walls, among cork woods that give it its name; City of Wine, Oil and Slow Food.

5 places to visit in Suvereto, in the province of Livorno (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Suvereto in the guide with map and filters

Rocca Aldobrandesca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the top of the hill, outside the walls, the fortress reminiscent of the ancient settlement and offers a beautiful view of the village.

  • Where Suvereto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Convento di San Francesco e Piazza della Cisterna

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

The monastery of the thirteenth century with the cloister, now the venue of concerts; the Cisterna Square was the cloister of the monastery founded in 1286.

  • Where Suvereto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa di San Giusto e Loggia dei Giudici

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The Romanesque church of San Giusto inside the walls and the Gothic loggia of the Judges next to the entrance door.

  • Where Suvereto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo Artistico della Bambola

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

A collection of ancient and modern dolls with costumes, which traces the history of the toy.

  • Where Suvereto
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Parco di Montioni

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

A natural area of about 7,000 hectares between Grosseto and Livorno, with wild boars, roe deer, deer and istrices, and the alum quarries linked to Elisa Bonaparte.

  • Where Montioni, Suvereto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
