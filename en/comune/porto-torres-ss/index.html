What to see in Porto Torres

City on the Gulf of Asinara founded by the Romans in the first century BC at the mouth of Riu Mannu as Turris Libisonis, then capital of the Judicate of Torres: the archaeological park with the Roman bridge and the Antiquarium, the basilica of San Gavino, the largest Romanesque church of Sardinia, the necropolis of Su Crucifissu Mannu (UNESCO heritage since 2025) and, offshore, the National Park of Asinara, which is part of the municipality.

Basilica di San Gavino

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Erected in the 11th century (between 1030 and 1080) by the will of the judge of Torres with Pisan maestranzes, on Mount Angellu, where there were a Roman and early Christian necropolis and two churches of the 5th and 7th centuries; it was the cathedral of the diocese from 484 to 1441. Along 58 meters, with two opposite apses and without facade, it has three naves divided by 22 columns of pink granite and grey marble of strip, a Romanesque portal with Adam and Eve to the north and a Gothic-Catalan to the south. In the crypt, reached by a gallery, the sarcophagi with the relics of the martyrs Gavino, Proto and Gianuario, found in 1614.

  • Where Monte Agellu, centro di Porto Torres
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Museo Antiquarium Turritano

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Built in the early 1970s and opened in 1984 in the area of Turris Libisonis, it exhibits in two sections the statuary and architectural sculpture of the cult buildings and the objects of daily life from spas, tabernacles, houses and necropolis, mostly of the I-III century AD, including the Ara di Bubastis in marble of the first century AD and the marble mask of the Domus del Satiro; it preserves a heated environment of a spa and from the windows of the upper floor you can see the excavation. Open from Tuesday to Saturday 9-16 (ticket office until 3 pm), closed on Sunday and Monday.

  • Where Via Ponte Romano 99, Porto Torres
  • Entry €5, reduced €2; cumulative with the archaeological area €8
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website antiquariumportotorres.cultura.gov.it

Sources: antiquariumportotorres.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Parco archeologico di Turris Libisonis e ponte romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The Roman colony, perhaps founded by Caesar or Ottaviano and at its apogee at the beginning of the III century AD, was the main port of call of the Roman Sardinia: you can visit the central baths called the Palazzo di Re Barbaro, the Maetzke and Pallottino thermal baths with four columns of the peristyle, the Domus of Orpheus with the mosaic of the poet playing the lira and the Domus of the mosaics, paved streets and workshops. On the Riu Mannu the bridge of the first century A.D., seven arches for 135 meters, is the largest Roman bridge on the island and was used until the mid-Twentieth century; remains traces of the aqueduct and the necropolis of Tanca Borgona, with 32 frescoed and mosaic burials. Guided tours from Tuesday to Saturday at 9:30, 10:30, 12:15, optional booking.

  • Where Via Ponte Romano, Porto Torres
  • Entry Cumulation with the Antiquarium €8
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it · cultura.gov.it

Necropoli di Su Crucifissu Mannu

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage

Necropolis of 22 domus de Janas excavated in a limestone bank not far from the town, in the Nurra, built by the recent Neolithic (3200-2800 BC) and used until the ancient Bronze Age (16th century BC), less than six kilometers from the altar of Monte d'Accoddi. Since 2025 it is one of the 17 Sardinian necropolis inscribed in the UNESCO World Heritage.

  • Where Poco fuori l'abitato di Porto Torres, nella Nurra
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Gavino a mare (Balai vicino)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small church built around 1850 on a cliff overlooking the sea, in the venerated place as the first burial of the martyrs Gavino, Proto and Gianuario: three rooms partly dug into the rock, of Roman and early medieval funerary use, a barrel chapel probably of the seventh century and, over a grate on the square, a Roman hypogeum. Normally closed, it opens throughout the month of May, when you bring there the wooden simulations of the three saints kept in the basilica.

Normally closed, open throughout the month of May according to SardiniaCultura

Spiaggia di Balai

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small golden sand cove mixed with pebbles, a hundred meters long and sheltered by limestone cliffs, on the seafront Christopher Columbus about one kilometer from the center, with behind the park Chico Mendes; the seabed soon gets deep and the rocks on the sides lend themselves to snorkeling. It is the most popular beach in the city, known for its New Year's Bath.

  • Where Lungomare Cristoforo Colombo, Porto Torres
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Parco nazionale dell'Asinara

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The island is about 50 km2 and 110 km of coast which is part of the municipality of Porto Torres: it was quarantine station, penal colony and prison camp in the Great War, and since 1997 it is national park, with marine protected area since 2002. The white donkey, symbol of the island, mouflons, wild boars and horses live in the wild; it is turned by train, jeep, bus or bicycle along the 25 km road, while Cala Sant'Andrea is a complete reserve with forbidden access. The crossings start from Porto Torres and Stintino.

  • Where Imbarco dal porto di Porto Torres
  • Entry Free entrance; boat crossing for a fee
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Cala d'Oliva

History and architecture · Villages · Viewpoint

Village of low and white houses in the north-east of Asinara, at the end of the 25 km road, with an Aragonese tower of the seventeenth century: housed from 1885 to 1997 the penal colony of maximum security, of which remain in the upper part the buildings and bunkers, and today the seat of the park with the Botanical Observatory and a hostel; near, two white sandy beaches, including Sa Murighessa called the beach of the Detainees. You arrive only with the boats authorized by Porto Torres or Stintino.

  • Where Cala d'Oliva, Isola dell'Asinara (Porto Torres)
  • Entry To be checked (with the crossing authorized by the Park)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
