What to see in Villamaina

A small village on a hill about 500 metres, near the Valle d'Ansanto sung by Virgilio: thermal resort for the Terme di San Teodoro, known since the second half of the seventeenth century, is home to the botanist Giovanni Gussone and houses a museum collection of prehistoric, Sanniti and Roman finds.

Raccolta museale comunale

Museums · Archaeological

In the municipal seat, it collects 933 artifacts ordered by origin, from prehistory to the Sannitic and Roman age up to the modern age: among these a protome wolf-head from a public building, probably a temple, the head of a votive statuette, many tiles with stamp of Roman workshops and a stone-stone with an epigraphic corpus useful to reconstruct the history of the country.

  • Where Via Roma 77, Villamaina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Terme di San Teodoro

Local life and traditions · Spas

About 3 km from the center, which has been reported since the second half of the seventeenth century, put into operation after a long abandonment; the water of the source of San Teodoro flows at about 28 °C, rich in minerals, and is used for crenotherapy. The complex, close to the geosite of the Mefite in Valle d'Ansanto, includes a hotel, restaurant, swimming pools and wellness centre, and next to a small ancient chapel. Open Monday to Saturday 7:30-13 and 14:30-18:30.

  • Where Via Bagni 20, Villamaina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page sistemairpinia.provincia.avellino.it

Sources: sistemairpinia.provincia.avellino.it

Fonte di Formulano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Ancient spring on the southern side of the hill, of Roman origin: in the nineteenth century there were found remains of Roman buildings with mosaic floors and the site would host a production of bricks with the brand . Waters, thermal and rich in carbon dioxide, flow at 28 °C and for centuries have served people, fields and livestock.

  • Where Località Formulano, Villamaina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page sistemairpinia.provincia.avellino.it

Sources: sistemairpinia.provincia.avellino.it

Parco comunale Giovanni Gussone

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Green area near the center dedicated to the botanist of Villamaina Giovanni Gussone (1787-1866), professor at the Frederick II and court botanist, who identified in the area of the Mephyte the broom he called Anxantic Genista. The main avenues of olive trees, football and basketball courts, bowls, children's games and a small outdoor amphitheatre.

  • Where Via Pianello, Villamaina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page sistemairpinia.provincia.avellino.it

Sources: sistemairpinia.provincia.avellino.it

Bosco della Rocca

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Small wooded and steep hill near the Mefite area, halfway between Villamaina and Rocca San Felice, with brambles, stingings, brooms and tall trees, especially oaks, elms and poplars.

  • Where Tra Villamaina e Rocca San Felice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page sistemairpinia.provincia.avellino.it
