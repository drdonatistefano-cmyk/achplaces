What to see in Rorà

Small Waldensian village of Val Pellice linked to the Stone of Luserna and to the figure of Giosuè Gianavello, with the Waldensian Museum, the Ecomuseum of Stone to the Cava del Tupinet, the temple of 1846 and historical and panoramic paths.

7 places to visit in Rorà, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo valdese di Rorà

Museums · City history

Born in 1954 in the center of the village, in the former Hotel du Chamois, to preserve the historical memory of Rorà: it reconstructs environments of daily life (kitchen, stable, bedroom, school) and has rooms dedicated to the history of Waldensian, stone and lime processing. Open in July and August on Sunday 10-12 and 16-19, in other periods on request; accessible to those with reduced mobility only on the first floor.

  • Where Centro del paese, Rorà
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rora.to.it

Sources: comune.rora.to.it

Ecomuseo della pietra - Cava del Tupinet e fornaci

Museums · Themed

Historical cable in Tupinet, on the road to the Montano Park, abandoned after the Second World War: the route shows with wooden figures the manual phases of stone processing in the nineteenth century, with original elements of the quarry and panels. Along the main road there is a rebuilt lime furnace. Visits only in small groups, with authorized guide and favorable time (information Office Il Barba).

  • Where Località Tupinet, Rorà
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rora.to.it

Sources: comune.rora.to.it

Tempio valdese

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The current temple was inaugurated in 1846, after more modest buildings existing since the mid-500s; floor (1925) and wooden galleries (1938) give it an excellent acoustic appreciated for concerts, and the bell tower houses the municipal clock.

  • Where Rorà
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rora.to.it

Sources: comune.rora.to.it

Sentiero dei Bandì

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Ring of about 2 hours (difficulty E) above the localities Gianavella and Tribulèt, where Joshua Gianavello, the "Lion of Rorà," and his had hiding places during the Waldensian resistance of 1655.

  • Where Località Gianavella, Rorà
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rora.to.it

Sources: comune.rora.to.it

Anello di Pietra

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient municipal mule track of about 2.5 km and 300 m of difference in altitude between the hamlet Rumer (1100 m) and Aia delle Balme (1400 m), recovered together with the quarries of the Polo extractive, overlooking the area of the three municipalities of the Pietra di Luserna. In some stretches it is steep, with overhanging areas; forbidden to bicycles.

  • Where Borgata Rumer, Rorà
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rora.to.it

Sources: comune.rora.to.it

Frassino monumentale

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Ash of about 250 years, 23 m tall and with 480 cm of circumference, declared monumental tree, at the hamlet Rounzèi at about 1250 m; can be reached in 40-45 minutes from Fournais or, more easily, from the Park Montano in the beech wood.

  • Where Borgata Rounzèi, Rorà
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rora.to.it

Sources: comune.rora.to.it

Parco Montano comunale

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Born in the 1970s for sustainable tourism, it has bowling courts, areas equipped with benches and tables, services and parking, the Koliba restaurant and since 2000 a weekend of summer concerts.

  • Where Rorà
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rora.to.it

Sources: comune.rora.to.it
