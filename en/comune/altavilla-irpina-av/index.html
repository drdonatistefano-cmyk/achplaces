Palazzo Baronale (Palazzo Comitale De Capua)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Built in the second half of the 16th century at the behest of the feudal lord Andrea De Capua, it is considered one of the most important examples of the bell renaissance: square plan, large portal, courtyard and double staircase towards the noble floor, with stone decorations. It hosts exhibitions and events and offers a view of the country and the countryside; you visit it by reservation (tel. 0825 991009).

Museo della Gente Senza Storia (MuGeSS)

Museums · Themed

Opened in 1997 in the Comital Palace De Capua, it exhibits archaeological finds from prehistory to the Middle Ages, liturgical vestments of the local clergy of the XVI-XVIII century and clothes and fabrics of the early nineteenth century found in the crypt of the Collegiate of the Assumption, with an annexed restoration laboratory. Three beans found in a dead man's waistcoat have been studied and the bean "Norman of Altavilla" is now cultivated in an experimental project.

Santuario dei Santi Martiri Pellegrino e Alberico

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Already the Collegial Church of San Pellegrino, it is known for its bronze portal and the imposing bell tower; it is dedicated to San Pellegrino Martire, the town's companion, and to Sant'Alberico Crescitelli.
