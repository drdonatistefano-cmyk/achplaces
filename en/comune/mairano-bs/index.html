What to see in Mairano

Municipality of the Brescia plain with the parish, Palazzo Tinti and the Museo della Civiltà Contadina born in the seventies by the master Dino Gregorio; in the hamlet of Pievedizio stands Palazzo Soncini, with two towers that recall the ancient strong houses.

3 places to visit in Mairano, in the province of Brescia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Civiltà Contadina «Dino Gregorio»

Museums · Themed

Born in the seventies on the initiative of the primary teacher Dino Gregorio, who with his pupils gathered the testimonies of rural life that were disappearing. It preserves tools of peasant and handicraft work, objects of use of peasant houses and the memory of dialect words, stories and proverbs remembered only by the elderly.

  • Where Mairano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Palazzo Soncini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Residence with two towers with rustic cantonals that recall the ancient strong houses, renovated in the eighteenth century with frescoes and period decorations. It is one of the stages of the Fontanile Cyclable.

  • Where Pievedizio, Mairano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Ciclabile dei fontanili

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

52 km ring with departure and arrival in Lograto, easy and suitable for all, on asphalt and dirt track with minimal slopes (about 5 hours, recommended MTB or hybrid). Touch numerous fountains, the layers that surface in the plain, and pass from Mairano, with the Museum of Rural Civilization, and from Palazzo Soncini to Pievedizio.

  • Where Partenza da Lograto; passaggio a Mairano e Pievedizio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
