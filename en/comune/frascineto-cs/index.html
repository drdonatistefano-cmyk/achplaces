What to see in Frascineto

Arbëreshe del Pollino country, founded by Albanian refugees in the fifteenth century, which preserves Byzantine rite and language: the museum of icons, that of dolls and costumes and, in the hamlet of Eianina, a rock chapel of the Basilian monks.

6 places to visit in Frascineto, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo delle icone e della tradizione bizantina

Museums · Old masters

The collection of the archimandrita Paolo Lombardo on three levels: introduction to the Byzantine world, rare icons, bronze icons of the Old Russian believers, Greek and Russian vestments and liturgical books from the 17th to the 20th century.

  • Where Piazza Albania, Frascineto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo delle bambole e del costume Arbëreshë

Museums · Themed

Traditional costumes with gold embroideries and accessories, in two sections: Albanians in southern Italy from 15th century immigration, with dolls and historical photos, and traditions in Albania.

  • Where Frascineto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa Madre di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt at the end of the eighteenth century on the remains of a church of the fifteenth century.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Santuario della Madonna degli Armi (Shën Mëria Këtje Lartë)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Above Eianina, in an unspoiled area, today in ruins: it remains a rock chapel of the X-XI centuries where the Basilian monks withdrew.

  • Where Eianina, Frascineto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
