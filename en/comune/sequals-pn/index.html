What to see in Sequals

Municipality of the high Friulian plain between the Meduna and Cosa streams, the land of mosaicists and the home of the boxer Primo Carnera, whose liberty villa is today the symbol of the village; a route through the mosaics of the center.

5 places to visit in Sequals, in the province of Pordenone (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Sequals in the guide with map and filters

Villa Carnera

Museums · House museums

Liberty style villa with rationalist innovations, on two floors, built for Primo Carnera, born in Sequals in 1906 and world heavyweight champion since 29 June 1933. It hosts an exhibition on the life and career of the boxer with photos, movies and objects, and his gym, with facade and mosaic floor. It is part of the Lis Aganis Ecomuseo.

  • Where Via Roma, Sequals
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · comune.sequals.pn.it

Percorso dei mosaici nel centro

Local life and traditions · Crafts · Free entry

Itinerary in nine stages among the mosaic works of the village: the Monument to the Fallen by Pietro Pellarin (1925), the birthplace of Gian Domenico Facchina (1826-1904), pioneer of the modern mosaic, the SOMSI headquarters, the Fountain of frogs (1936), the mosaic by Piergiorgio Patrizio for the centenary of Carnera (2006) and Palazzo Domini (1750), today city hall, with coat of arms in mosaic.

  • Where Centro di Sequals
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: comune.sequals.pn.it · turismofvg.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The choir floor is by Gian Domenico Facchina, built in Paris in 1900; in the apse the mosaic announcement (2004) on a sketch by Antonio Boatto, in addition to the crowned Christ of thorns and the "Addolorata" (1914) by Pietro Pellarin da Guido Reni and the Via Crucis di Gino Avon.

  • Entry Free

Museo vecchie arti e mestieri e civiltà contadina (SOMSI)

Museums · Themed

Agricultural tools and instruments of ancient crafts and crafts, with furniture of houses and shops: carpenter materials, lumberjack, mosaicist, miller, norcino, beaterrame and others. Visits only by reservation required.

Chiesetta dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A small church that preserves a mosaic-shaped San Pietro by Giuseppe Mazziol, in front style on a gold background that recalls Byzantine art; it is a stage of the mosaics.
