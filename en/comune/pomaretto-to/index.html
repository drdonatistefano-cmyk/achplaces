What to see in Pomaretto

Country at the entrance of the Germanasca Valley, in the Waldensian valleys, with the Latin School that hosts the exhibition "The ancient crafts" and the heroic vineyards of the Ramie, from which the Pinerolese Ramie was born produced only here and in Perosa Argentina.

3 places to visit in Pomaretto, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Scuola Latina ed esposizione «Gli antichi mestieri»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Local culture centre inaugurated in 2006 and managed by the Friends of the Latin School, part of the Ecomuseum of Mines and the Germanasca Valley and the Museum System of the Waldensian Valleys. The permanent exhibition "The ancient crafts" presents the Ferrero Collection, over 150 models in boxwood with scenes of mountain and peasant life, and tells about the cultivations of fields and vineyards and the mine; in the same building the Library of the Pathouà dedicated to the occitan and the language desk. A video guide is available on Sundays, without reservation.

  • Where Via Balziglia 103, Pomaretto
  • Entry €3, free members and children
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.scuolalatina.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · ecomuseominiere.it · scuolalatina.it

Vigneti dei Ramìe

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails

The grapes Avanà, Avarengo and Neretto del Pinerolese Ramie are cultivated on the first slopes of the Germanasca Valley, produced only in Pomaretto and Perosa Argentina. A path crosses the Ramìe vineyards with panels of the municipality on the territory and on the wine, connected to the visit of the Latin School.

  • Where Vigneti dei Ramìe, Pomaretto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: ecomuseominiere.it · turismotorino.org

Big Bench di Pomaretto

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

Blue giant bench, more than two meters high, of the network created by American designer Chris Bangle (the first was installed in Clavesana in 2010); placed in a panoramic point, allows you to look at the landscape from a different perspective. With the Big Bench passport you collect the stamps of the benches.

  • Where Pomaretto (posizione esatta non indicata dalla fonte)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org
