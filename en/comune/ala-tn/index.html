What to see in Ala

The velvet town of Vallagarina, which thrives between the end of the 17th and 18th centuries thanks to the silk velvet: a baroque center among the best preserved in Trentino, with the Palazzi de' Pizzini where Mozart stayed and the Museum of the ancient piano, the parish church of the Assunta and, in the surroundings, the Romanesque San Pietro in Bosco and the Ronchi Valley.

Centro storico barocco

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The centre, one of the best preserved in Trentino, owes its prestige to the production of velvet between the end of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries: baroque palaces, courtyards, gardens and paved streets from the medieval path, with 67 fountains that once welcomed travellers. In July, the costume party "Ala Città di Velluto" animates squares, palaces and gardens.

  • Where Ala
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitrovereto.it · visittrentino.info

Palazzi de' Pizzini von Hochenbrunn

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Three buildings built between the end of the 17th and end of the 18th century, once united by an air pass similar to the Bridge of Sighs. In the eighteenth century they housed Maria Teresa of Austria, Carlo III of Spain, Napoleon and Mozart, who stayed there three times and played in the room of the noble piano, with excellent acoustics and frescoed ceiling attributed to Antonio Gresta; the upper palace, donated to the municipality, hosts exhibitions and concerts. Guided tours by reservation.

  • Where Via Santa Caterina 2, Ala
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.trentino.it · italia.it

Museo del pianoforte antico

Museums · Themed

On the first floor of the Palazzo de' Pizzini there are antique pianos linked to Mozart, Schubert, Beethoven and Chopin, from the private collection of the pianist Temenuschka Vesselinova. It is not open to the general public: visits for groups of at least five people by reservation.

  • Where Via Santa Caterina 1, Ala
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitrovereto.it · visittrentino.info · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Of very ancient origins, it rises in a dominant position on the site of a chapel destroyed in the medieval struggles between Prince Bishops of Trento and Castelbarco; damaged in the Great War, it was reconsecrated in the present form in 1929. It has a baroque portal surmounted by the statue of the Assumption, nine altars in polychrome marble, a altarpiece of Saint Lucia patron saint of the velvet makers attributed to the school of Cignaroli and a collection of vestments in 18th century alense velvet.

  • Where Piazza Bonacquisto, Ala
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ala.tn.it · visitrovereto.it · italia.it

Palazzo Taddei

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palazzo di proprietà comunale, dal 2023 nella rete musale del Castello del Buonconsiglio: the rooms on the noble floor have frescoes from around 1870 and furnishings from the eighteenth-nineteenth century. The Provincial Textile Museum is being set up here.

museum in preparation, inauguration scheduled for 2026; noble floor open only temporarily with guided tours by reservation (Trentino Cultura)

Palazzo Malfatti Azzolini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Residence of the late eighteenth century that closes St. John's Square: high sober facade with four orders of windows and two hillsides with stone balustrade; on the first floor the living room and other frescoed rooms, on the ground floor stables and garden.

Chiesetta di San Pietro in Bosco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Romanesque church among the vineyards a few kilometers from Ala, with origins in the 6th century, high bell tower with cusp in terracotta Verona and Romanesque bifore; above the portal a Christ in majesty and inside a Saint George who kills the dragon. Tradition places there the 589 meeting between Theodolinda and Autari king of the Longobards. Free guided tours on the third Sunday of the month from May to September, at 10 am from Piazza San Giovanni.

  • Where Corso Verona, SS12, Ala
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitrovereto.it · italia.it

Santuario di San Valentino

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

An ancient place of worship north of the city along the Roman route: devotion dates back to tradition to before the thirteenth century, the foundation in the fourteenth century; expanded in Baroque forms in 1763, has interior to three aisles sober and luminous with altar greater in painted marble, two wooden altars and numerous ex-vote.

  • Where Via del Santuario, Ala
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ala.tn.it · visitrovereto.it · italia.it

Fucina Cortiana

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Ancient mill transformed into a workshop where until the eighties the blacksmith Francesco Cortiana forged cutting tools for fields and woods with the water of the river Ala; today his nephew Mario tells the work of iron. It is reached by a short walk in the Ronchi Valley.

  • Where Valle dei Ronchi, Ala
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · italia.it

Valle dei Ronchi e Piccole Dolomiti

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Ronchi Valley, with views of the Small Dolomites, is the starting point of the excursions in the group; nearby are the highlands of Lessinia, suitable for Nordic walking and mountain biking among mountain huts with stone roofs.

  • Where Valle dei Ronchi, Ala
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitrovereto.it · visittrentino.info · italia.it
