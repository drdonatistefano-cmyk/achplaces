What to see in Città della Pieve

Brick village on the border with Tuscany, the birthplace of Perugino: five of his works between the Duomo, Santa Maria dei Bianchi, Santa Maria dei Servi and San Pietro, the Palazzo della Corgna and the narrowest alley in Italy.

10 places to visit in Città della Pieve, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Città della Pieve in the guide with map and filters

Cattedrale dei Santi Gervasio e Protasio

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

From the 13th century, renovated in the 16th century, on Piazza del Plebiscito: it houses two works by Perugino, the Madonna with Saints (1514) and the Baptism of Christ (1510), a wooden Crucifix of the Giambologna school and frescoes by Pomarancio.

  • Where Piazza del Plebiscito, Città della Pieve
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Oratorio di Santa Maria dei Bianchi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

Along Via Vannucci, it preserves the Adoration of the Magi del Perugino (1504).

  • Where Via Vannucci, Città della Pieve
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Palazzo della Corgna

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

The residence of the middle sixteenth century designed by Galeazzo Alessi for Ascanio della Corgna, with frescoes by Salvio Salvini and Pomarancio; it houses the Museum of natural history and the territory and the municipal library, and in the basement the monumental Nativity scene.

  • Where Piazza del Plebiscito, Città della Pieve
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Museo civico-diocesano di Santa Maria dei Servi

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Outside Porta Santa Maria, Gothic church transformed into a Baroque age with the fresco of the Deposition of Perugino and students (1517) and the civic-diocesan art gallery.

  • Where Città della Pieve
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Already dedicated to Saint Anthony Abbot, it preserves the Saint Anthony Abbot between the Saints Marcello and Paul Hermit of Perugino, detached and transferred on canvas after the earthquake of 1861.

  • Where Città della Pieve
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Torre Civica

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

39 meters high, built in the 12th century in Lombard Romanesque style next to the cathedral.

  • Where Piazza del Plebiscito, Città della Pieve
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Rocca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Vicolo Baciadonne

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

One of the narrowest alleys in Italy, a few tens of centimeters wide.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di San Francesco e Oratorio di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Outside Porta Perugina, the church with gothic façade of the thirteenth century; next to the oratory with the fourteenth-century Crucifix called "Il Pianto degli Angeli" by Jacopo di Mino del Pellicciaio.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Il Giardino dei Lauri

Museums · Modern art

A space of contemporary art in the countryside founded in 1990 by collectors Angela and Massimo Lauro, with about 300 works in a former shed and in a garden.
