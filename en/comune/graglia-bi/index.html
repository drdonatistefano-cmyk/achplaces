What to see in Graglia

Country of the Biellese that rises from 600 to more than 2000 meters up to the Mombarone, to the borders with Canavese and Valle d'Aosta; it is known for the seventeenth-century Sanctuary of the Madonna of Loreto, with the octagonal dome 38 meters high and the perspectives of Fabrizio Galliari, and for the churches of the center such as the parish church of Santa Fede and the confraternity of Santa Croce.

Santuario di Graglia (Madonna di Loreto)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Born on the initiative of the parish priest Don Nicolao Velotti on the model of the Sacro Monte di Varallo: the project included a large reception complex around the church and a Sacro Monte of over one hundred chapels, largely never realized. The works began in 1616; the first stone of the temple was laid on September 20, 1659 with the support of the Savoys and the works ended in 1888. The Greek cross church (42 x 32 meters) has an octagonal dome 38 meters high with perspectives of Fabrizio Galliari; in the 1930s the Garden of the Madonna was created with the stone fountain called "burnel" . More than 800 meters of altitude between beech and chestnut trees, with a balcony on the plain where you can hear the echo endecasillabo.

  • Where Via Campiglie 1, Casale Campiglie, Graglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website santuariodigraglia.com

Sources: santuariodigraglia.com · italia.it · comune.graglia.bi.it

Mombarone e monumento al Redentore

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

It rises 2373 meters to the border with Canavese and Valle d'Aosta, dominated by the monument to the Redeemer, 13 meters high and surmounted by a bronze statue of Christ, next to the Rifugio del Mombardo. From Graglia there are marked paths.

  • Where Vetta del Mombarone, Graglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.graglia.bi.it · italia.it

Rifugio Alpe Pianetti

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Former municipal alpine pasture at 1321 meters turned into a refuge by the Graglia alpine mountains, reachable from San Carlo by a dirt road; stop of the marked paths towards the Mombarone, Salvine and Netro.

  • Where Alpe Pianetti (da San Carlo), Graglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.graglia.bi.it · italia.it

Centro storico, Santa Fede e confraternita di Santa Croce

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Around Piazza Astrua there are the confraternity of Santa Croce, built in 1639, which houses precious reliquaries, the ancient tower and the monument to the Alps; not far from the parish church of Santa Fede, consecrated in 1601 and enlarged in 1667. The oldest nucleus is the canton of Valle; in the public gardens the monument to the stone paver.

  • Where Piazza Astrua e Via della Parrocchia, Graglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.graglia.bi.it · italia.it
