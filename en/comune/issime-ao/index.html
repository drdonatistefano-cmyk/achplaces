What to see in Issime

The first Walser village in the Lys valley, where the töitsu is still spoken, with the church of the Last Judgment and the valley of San Grate.

4 places to visit in Issime, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giacomo Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

From the sixteenth century on a more ancient building, with the large fresco of the Last Judgment on the facade; the parish museum preserves among other things a reliquary of 1675.

  • Where Issime
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Scanni del tribunale

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

In front of the former municipal palace, the scans and the armchair of the sixteenth-century Vallaise court.

  • Where Issime
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Vallone di San Grato

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint
