What to see in Riva Ligure

The seaside village of the Riviera dei Fiori, born as Villaregia in the 11th century as a settlement of sailors and fishermen: at the head Don preserves the archaeological area of Costa Balenae with the early Christian basilica, in the center the 18th century parish church of San Maurizio, and is crossed by the cycle path of the coastal park.

Area archeologica di Costa Balenae

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The most important archaeological site of Riva Ligure: a late Christian early basilica that remained in use in the Middle Ages, with three apsed aisles with octagonal baptismal font, whose lateral naves later housed a cemetery. First it was the landing place of a pre-Roman castle, then Roman maritime villa on the Via Iulia Augusta, perhaps to be identified with the ancient place name Costa Balenae. It is visible from the outside, with access from the bike path; a virtual visit is made to the multimedia exhibition space SEM in the Town Hall.

  • Where Via Lamboglia, presso il capo Don, Riva Ligure
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.provincia.imperia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comunedirivaligure.it · provincia.imperia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Maurizio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the heart of the village, on the narrow road parallel to the coast, replaced the ancient church by insufficient: authorized in 1693 by the Parliament of Taggia, it was slowly built for the whole eighteenth century by the foreman revisited Girolamo Arlotti, with the help of local families like the Philippi. It has façade with stuccoes and statues, high bell tower, single nave with side chapels and Ligurian paved church of 1760; inside the canvases of Carlo Giuseppe Aicardi (1775), sculptures of Maragliano and paintings of Morgari, and the main altar in 19th century marble.

  • Where Centro storico, Riva Ligure
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.provincia.imperia.it

Sources: comunedirivaligure.it · provincia.imperia.it

Palazzo Lombardi (Municipio)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Country residence of the family of Lombardi, today municipal seat: in 1714 Elisabetta Farnese was hosted, betrothed to the king of Spain. In the Town Hall there is the multimedia exhibition space SEM, with the virtual visit of the archaeological area of Costa Balenae.

  • Where Municipio, Riva Ligure
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.provincia.imperia.it

Sources: comunedirivaligure.it · cultura.gov.it · provincia.imperia.it

Pista ciclabile del Parco costiero della Riviera dei Fiori

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Cyclopedonal path on the sea that connects San Lorenzo al Mare to Sanremo, between tunnels and bridges: in Riva Ligure it crosses the historic center, between fields and alleys, after Santo Stefano al Mare and before Arma di Taggia. Along the route bike rentals and rickshaws and large parking lots.

  • Where Centro storico, Riva Ligure
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.provincia.imperia.it

Sources: italia.it · lamialiguria.it · provincia.imperia.it
