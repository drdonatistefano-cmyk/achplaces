What to see in Montanaro

Country of Canavese a few kilometers from Chivasso, for centuries under the jurisdiction of the abbeys of Fruttuaria: the abbey castle then of the counts Frola, the main medieval porticoed street with Casa Bricca and the Baroque churches.

3 places to visit in Montanaro, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello dei Conti Frola

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

It is the seat of the civil, judicial and economic power of the abbots of Fruttuaria, rebuilt in 1526 by Cardinal Bonifacio Ferrero, who installed a tick in a special tower (on the tower of the donjon the coat of arms Ferrero, a blue lion in the silver field). Purchased in 1801 by the lawyer Pietro Giuseppe Frola, it was rebuilt by Secondo Frola with the architect Camillo Boggio (veranda, portico and neo-Gothic tower since 1885); the northern towers, the prisons and the tower of the mint remain. He's from the city today.

Visitable only ground floor and park, degraded upper floors (FAI); visit by telephone reservation (Tourism Turin)

  • Where Via Mazzini 5, Montanaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.comune.montanaro.to.it

Sources: comune.montanaro.to.it · turismotorino.org · fondoambiente.it

Via maestra e Casa Bricca

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The historic centre combines medieval origins and Baroque architectures and Rococo additions between the sixteenth and eighteenth centuries; the main street is flanked by large medieval porticoes. Casa Bricca, a severe and imposing building of the Quarini, pupil of Vittone, today seat of the Municipality, preserves frescoes by the mountain painters Giovanni and Andrea Ponchia.

  • Where Via maestra, Montanaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Chiese di Montanaro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church of San Nicolao and of the Assumption, built between 1644 and 1649, with a Latin cross and a single nave; the church of the Confraternity of Santa Marta, with a smooth and harmonious façade; the Sanctuary of the Madonna of Loreto, of 1681; the church of Santa Maria dell'Isola, dating back to about the year 1000.

  • Where Montanaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org
