What to see in Candelo

Flag Orange and the most beautiful village in Italy, it holds one of the best preserved medieval recipes: about 200 stone houses among walls, towers and cobbled rue.

4 places to visit in Candelo, in the province of Biella (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Candelo in the guide with map and filters

Ricetto di Candelo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortification of the XIII-XIV centuries of about 13,000 square meters with about 200 small rural houses, four round towers at the corners and a large tower-door to the south; shelter in war and warehouse of grapes, wine and wheat in peace, with rue cobbled in slope.

  • Where Via Pietro Micca, Candelo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it

Palazzo del Principe

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in 1496 for Sebastiano Ferrero when he became lord of the manor.

  • Where Ricetto, Candelo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Cited from 1182 at the Roggia Marchesa, it has Romanesque façade in river pebbles and inside capitals and frescoes of the fifteenth century.

  • Where Candelo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Ecomuseo della vitivinicoltura

Museums · Themed

The headquarters of the Ecomuseum of Biellese dedicated to vine and wine, in the recipe that was stored grapes.
