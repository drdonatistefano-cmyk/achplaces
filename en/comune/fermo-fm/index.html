What to see in Fermo

City on the hill Sabulo between the Sibillini and the Adriatic: the Renaissance Piazza del Popolo with the Palazzo dei Priori and the Sala del Mappamondo, the underground Roman cisterns and the Cathedral on the Girfalco, a few kilometers from the beaches.

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Palazzo dei Priori – Pinacoteca e sezione archeologica

Museums · Old masters

Palazzo del Cinquecento with the statue of Sisto V del Sansovino above the entrance: it houses the Pinacoteca, with the Stories of Saint Lucia of Iacobello del Fiore and the Adoration of the Shepherds of Rubens (1608), and the archaeological section "From Villanoviani to Piceni."

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Fermo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · regione.marche.it

Sala del Mappamondo – Biblioteca Spezioli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

In the Palazzo degli Studi, the municipal library with about 400,000 volumes and the room that takes its name from the world map made in 1713 by cartographer Moroncelli.

Cisterne romane

History and architecture · Archaeology
