What to see in Velturno

Country of chestnuts on the ridge half coast of the Isarco Valley, with Castel Velturno, Renaissance summer residence of the princes bishops of Brixen, and the Archeoparc of the Tanzgasse with its place of worship of the Copper Age; high up the Velturno mountain hut and the Radlsee lake at 2,250 meters, at the bottom the waterfall of Schrambach 63 meters high.

Castel Velturno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built in 1580 as the summer residence of the prince bishop of Bressanone Johann Thomas von Spaur, once with aviary and deer park; purchased by the Province of Bolzano in 1978 and restored in 1979-83. Ten rooms with wooden walls and wall paintings make it one of the best preserved Renaissance residences in the Alps; on the first floor the archaeological museum with finds of Velturno and the Middle Isarco Valley, temporary exhibitions on the ground floor and in the Schreiberhaus.

seasonal: from 1 April to 8 November 2026, 10-17, closed Monday and Tuesday; guided tours and tickets on the site of the castle

Archeoparc Velturno - Tanzgasse

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Central site is the Tanzgasse, with burials and remains of ceramic production; there were found a place of worship of the Copper Age with a megalithic structure of large stones and a figurative stele (menhir). The first communities of farmers and breeders date back to the fifth millennium BC and the area was frequented from the Neolithic to the Middle Ages; among the findings bones, gold and ceramics. The excavations are still underway.

  • Where Tanzgasse, Velturno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.klausen.it

Chiesa di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the heart of the village, built between 1286 and 1295 and enlarged around 1400; Gothic, with walls of the Romanesque nave. The bell tower, added in the sixteenth century, is leaning for weak foundations.

  • Where Velturno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.klausen.it

Sources: klausen.it

Parrocchiale di Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A chapel of Our Lady built in 1112 became parish church in 1170; rebuilt in 1499-1515 and again in 1894-1899 in late Dogotic style according to the design of the Viennese architect Weber.

Malga di Velturno e lago Radlsee

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The pasture begins at about 2,000 meters; the Radlsee lake, at 2,250 meters, is reached in about 2 and a half hours of walk and is flanked by the Radlsee refuge, with panoramic views of the mountains. A network of trails leads to the Königsanger (2,439 m) and the Lazfons and Villandro alpine pastures; according to legend the lake would be an ancient crater connected to the Mediterranean.

  • Where Malga di Velturno, Velturno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.klausen.it

Sources: klausen.it
