What to see in Ocre

Common on the slopes above the valley of the Aterno: the fortified village of the Castle of Ocre with seven towers, the Cistercian monastery of Santo Spirito of 1222 and the Franciscan monastery of Sant'Angelo on the rock spur.

3 places to visit in Ocre, in the province of L'Aquila (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Ocre

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

More than a castle a fortified fence: about 470 meters of walls with seven towers (six squares and a semicircular) and an ogival gate of the thirteenth century, which enclose terraced houses along four converging streets on a church of which remains the trace; for the Municipality is the best preserved walls of Abruzzo. Free entrance, not accessible to disabled people.

Monastero di Santo Spirito d'Ocre

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

First Cistercian settlement of the Aquilan valley, founded in 1222 by Placido da Roio and Cistercian since 1248: square cloister with church, refectory, dormitory and capitular hall; in the church frescoes of about 1280, in the chapel-sacred stories of the Blessed Placido of the late fourteenth century. Managed by private individuals: accommodation on reservation, museum available by telephone appointment.

Convento di Sant'Angelo d'Ocre

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Franciscan monastery of 1481 on a rock spur 35 meters high: cloister porticoed with lunettes painted in 1661 with stories of Saint Anthony of Padua, church with choir and door of 1481, refectory of 1748 with a Last Supper of the first sixteenth century.

Closed on visits waiting for restoration and consolidation, according to the Municipality
