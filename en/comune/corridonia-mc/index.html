What to see in Corridonia

The town of Macerata, the ancient Montolmo, with the Romanesque parish church of San Claudio al Chienti, the Madonna del latte by Carlo Crivelli in the parish art gallery, the Teatro Velluti and the birthplace of Filippo Corridoni; historical centre, monuments and municipal museums are only partially accessible.

San Claudio al Chienti

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

One of the most important examples of Romanesque of the Marches, on the site of the Roman city of Pausulae: documented from the 11th century, it was a parish church and not an abbey. Square plan with semicircular apses, two cylindrical towers on the sides of the façade of Ravenna taste and above all two overlapping churches; the Gothic portal in Istria stone of the upper church is an addition. It preserves almost intact the original appearance.

Pinacoteca parrocchiale

Museums · Old masters

Next to the church of the Ss. Pietro, Paolo and Donato, born on the initiative of Father Pallotta: preserves the Madonna of milk by Carlo Crivelli (1470-1473, tempera and gold on board, center of a missing polyptych), the Madonna of humility by Andrea da Bologna (1373), works by Lorenzo d'Alessandro (1481) and Vincenzo Pagani (1517) and a polyptych by Antonio and Bartolomeo Vivarini.

The adjoining church is closed for the earthquake 2016; information to the parish, 0733 431832

Chiesa dei Ss. Pietro, Paolo e Donato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt on a project by Giuseppe Valadier on a structure of the thirteenth century, of circular shape: three naves that curve to form a dome; it preserves a polychrome wooden crucifix of the thirteenth century in front of which, traditionally, prayed St. Francis, and a crypt that can be visited on request.

Closed due to the 2016 earthquake

  • Where Via Cavour, Corridonia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di San Francesco e Pinacoteca comunale

Museums · Old masters

The ancient Santa Maria in Castello, built around the 1000 and passed to the Franciscans in 1225, preserves Romanesque-Gothic remains and a fifteenth-century bell tower; it houses the Pinacoteca municipale. In the nearby Palazzo Persichetti-Ugolini, inside the former convent complex, there is the Pinacoteca civica with the collection of sacred art.

Teatro Gian Battista Velluti

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Born from the deconsecrated church of Sant'Antonio, transformed into a theatre by the Municipality at the beginning of the nineteenth century; in 1817 a condominium of 28 members promoted by Giacomo Nobili started the construction and was probably inaugurated on 26 December 1819. It has an elliptical audience, unique and favorable for acoustics, baroque interiors and a Liberty ceiling frescoed by Sigismondo Martini; restored, it still hosts a theatrical season.

Museo Casa natale «Filippo Corridoni»

Museums · House museums

Dedicated to the trade unionist Filippo Corridoni, born here (then Pausula) on 18 August 1887 and died at the Trencea delle Frasche on 24 October 1915: the path reconstructs its figure as a revolutionary interventionist, then taken as a symbol by fascism, to relocate it to its historical dimension. Open Monday to Saturday 9-13.30, by reservation (0733 439901).
