What to see in Voghiera

3 places to visit in Voghiera, in the province of Ferrara (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 4 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Delizia di Belriguardo e Museo civico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The first of the extensive delights, begun for Niccolò III around 1435 and called the "Versailles degli Estensi"; the museum has archaeological, Renaissance sections in the Vigna Room, modern art and industrial archaeology.

  • Where Via Provinciale 274, Voghiera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · cultura.gov.it

Necropoli romana di Voghenza

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

A large portion of the Roman necropolis, excavated since 1976, visible from the outside near the sports field.

  • Where Voghenza, Voghiera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Voghiera

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish with a fresco of the Madonna with angels attributed to Carlo Bononi and a Saint Lucia dello Scarsellino.

  • Where Voghiera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
