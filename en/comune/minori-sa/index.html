What to see in Minori

Small village of the Amalfi Coast, UNESCO heritage, more collected than the nearby Maiori, overlooking the sea between churches and lemon groves and known as a city of taste; it houses the Roman maritime villa of the first century A.D., the basilica of the patron saint Trofimena and the arrival of the Trail of Lemons.

Villa romana e Antiquarium

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Villa marittima d'otium of the Roman aristocracy, among the best preserved of the Tyrrhenian area: built in the early years of the first century A.D. (Julio-Claudia age) and in use until the 7th century, extends to the sea along the river Reginna Minor, on two or more floors around the triclinium-nympheo turned into a barrel, with mosaics of a hunting scene and a marine tiase. There are rooms decorated with stuccoes and frescoes and part of the viridarium surrounded by arcades and a large bathtub; discovered by chance in 1932, it was completely freed after the flood of 1954. On the terrace the Antiquarium displays frescoes, amphoras and finds of other villas in the area and excavations of Vietri and Tramonti.

Closed to the public since August 25, 2025 for restoration of decorated surfaces and mosaics and for accessibility; reopening to be communicated on official channels

Basilica di Santa Trofimena

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage

The most famous of the thirty churches of the village, near the promenade: of origin XI-XII century and rebuilt in the nineteenth century, has a crypt rearranged in the eighteenth century with a coeva urn that guards the remains of the patroness. In the presence of the relics is linked the survival of the rites of Holy Week, with the processions of the "baptants" and the traditional songs.

Sentiero dei Limoni

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Walking path of medium-low difficulty, with about 400 steps and about an hour of walk, which connects Minori and Maiori passing from the village of Torre to the church of San Michele, between lemon gardens (including the Sfusato Amalfi, lemon IGP Costa d'Amalfi) and views of the promontory between the two countries.
