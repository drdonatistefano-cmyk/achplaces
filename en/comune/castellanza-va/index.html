What to see in Castellanza

City of Valle Olona with the parish church of San Giulio, whose 93-metre bell tower is among the highest in Lombardy, the eclectic villa Pomini of 1919, the Pagani Museum of Modern Art with outdoor sculptures and the Alto Milanese Park on the border with Busto Arsizio and Legnano.

Museo d'arte moderna Pagani

Museums · Modern art

Designed in 1965 as an innovative environmental space, on the model of the Maeght Foundation of Saint-Paul-de-Vence and the Louisiana Museum of Humlebamycin, with hexagonal building; the founder's idea was to bring the works of art out in the open. Hundreds of sculptures and mosaics by Italian and foreign artists in marble, stone, bronze, iron, steel, wood and glass, from the second half of the twentieth century, including works by Jean Arp and Alexander Archipenko, are exhibited on a large area.

the Municipality (July 2025) indicates the museum normally closed to the public, with only extraordinary openings; the MiC card instead shows opening Saturday and Sunday

Chiesa di San Giulio e campanile

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Parishyard dedicated to Giulio d'Orta, patron saint celebrated on 31 January: documented from the 14th century (a tomb inscription is 1386), consecrated in 1557, has the current appearance from the expansion desired in 1926 by Don Testori; presbytery with Venetian marble mosaics of 1932, imposing ciborium and organ Mascioni opus 1175 of 2006. In 1936 the demolition of the old bell tower revealed a fresco of the Holy Trinity. The new bell tower, completed in 1948, is 93 meters high, third of Lombardy: about 250 steps up to the view on Valle Olona, Monte Rosa, Resegone and skyline of Milan, climbable only in organized visits (FAI, April 2026).

  • Where Piazza Paolo VI / Via Vittorio Veneto 2, Castellanza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.castellanza.va.it

Sources: comune.castellanza.va.it · fondoambiente.it

Villa Pomini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in 1919 for Ottorino Pomini in a late eclectic style: three floors and a panoramic tower, exposed brick facades and plaster with two-lighted windows, arches, loggias and graffiti, a stone plinth of Moltrasio and a long portico with red brick arches. In 1982 Luciano Pomini offered it to the municipality as a cultural centre: today it houses exhibitions, courses and the library donated by the family, in a park of about 5,300 m2.

  • Where Via don Testori 14, Castellanza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.castellanza.va.it

Sources: comune.castellanza.va.it

Chiesa di San Bernardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the district of Castegnate, which began in 1958 and has been a parish since 1971, it replaces the homonymous church demolished in 1953 after the damage of a flood of the river, remembered by a stele in Piazza Castegnate. Brick hut façade with white pronao and mosaic on the portal, detached bell tower, tiburium with rhomboidal stained glass windows; preserves paintings from the 16th to the 18th century coming from the Chapel of San Carlo. Feast August 20th.

  • Where Via San Camillo 1, Castellanza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.castellanza.va.it

Sources: comune.castellanza.va.it

Parco Alto Milanese

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Green area protected by the consortium of municipalities of Busto Arsizio, Legnano and Castellanza, with about 10 hectares equipped between the entrance of Castellanza and that of Legnano: refreshment point, play area, picnic tables and two life paths, including the Pojeco suitable for people with motor and visual difficulties. The park centre is Cascinetta, a Lombard farmhouse renovated between 2004 and 2007. Free and accessible access.

  • Where Parcheggio presso l'ITIS Facchinetti, Castellanza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.castellanza.va.it

Sources: comune.castellanza.va.it
