What to see in Rosciano

The municipality of the hills of Pescia, in whose territory stands Villa Badessa, a community of Arbëreshë arrived from Epirus in 1743: its church of Santa Maria Assunta, unique of Greek-Byzantine rite in Abruzzo, preserves a rich collection of icons, and a museum tells its origins and traditions.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta e collezione di icone di Villa Badessa

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The only Greek-Byzantine church in Abruzzo, dependent on the Eparchy of Lungro in Calabria, conserves 75 icons painted on a table from the 15th to the 19th century: for the Ministry it is the main collection of Byzantine and post-Byzantine icons of Western Europe. Visit on request (tel. 388 1655479).

  • Where Piazza Skanderberg, Villa Badessa, Rosciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo storico etnoantropologico di Villa Badessa

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

It documents the origins of the Albanian community (arbëreshë) of Abruzzo, established here in 1743 by Epirus to escape the religious persecutions of the Turks, and preserves its traditions. Open on request (tel. 338 1655479).

  • Where Via Case Vecchie 1, Villa Badessa, Rosciano
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it
