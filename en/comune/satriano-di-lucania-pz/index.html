What to see in Satriano di Lucania

Village between three rocks (the Castle, the Piesco and the Madonna della Rocca) on the river Melandro, birthplace of the painter Giovanni de Gregorio called the Pietrafesa, with over 500 murals in the historic center and two museums in the Loreti Palace.

4 places to visit in Satriano di Lucania, in the province of Potenza (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e murales

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village lies between three rocks, the Castle, the Piesco and the Madonna della Rocca, overlooking the river Melandro; in the historic center there are over 500 murals, which increase every year.

  • Where Satriano di Lucania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Cappella di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It preserves the frescoes above the altar of Giovanni de Gregorio known as the Pietrafesa, painter born in Satriano and active in the first half of the seventeenth century, among the most famous of Basilicata.

  • Where Satriano di Lucania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it · italia.it

Museo archeologico «Satriano le Origini»

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

It tells the story of Satrianum from the Bronze Age to the destruction in 1420, with finds from the V-III century B.C. from a shrine and necropolis (ex-vote, weapons, imported ceramics and locals) and medieval majolica ceramics; open on request.

  • Where Via De Gregorio 27, Satriano di Lucania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Set up in 1997 in two rooms of the eighteenth-century Palazzo Loreti, seat of the town hall: one reconstructs the peasant house with kitchen, room and shelter of the animals, the other exposes the agricultural tools donated by the elderly, each with its dialect name.

  • Where Via De Gregorio 27, Satriano di Lucania
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
