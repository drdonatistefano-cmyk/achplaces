What to see in Gressoney-La-Trinité

The last village of the Lys valley at the foot of Mount Rosa, a Walser culture, a base for ascents to the Capanna Regina Margherita.

4 places to visit in Gressoney-La-Trinité, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ecomuseo Walser

Museums · Themed

Three structures: an ancient rural house of the eighteenth century, the museum house with permanent exhibitions and the hut of Binò Alpelté, a small alpine pasture built under a rock that acts as a roof.

  • Where Piazza Tache, Gressoney-La-Trinité
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Chiesa parrocchiale della Santissima Trinità

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1671 on a 15th-century building, with a major Baroque wooden altar and cemetery with ancient stone tombstones carved by local craftsmen.

  • Where Gressoney-La-Trinité
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Cappella della Madonna delle Nevi di Oagre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Monte Rosa, Stafal e Capanna Regina Margherita

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

From Stafal start the ski tours to Punta Gnifetti (4459 m) and Capanna Regina Margherita; in summer excursions to lakes, huts and panoramic points such as Sant'Anna.
