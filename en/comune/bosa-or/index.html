What to see in Bosa

Colorful village on the Temo, the only navigable river of Sardinia: the district of Sa Costa under the Malaspina castle, the ancient tanneries, the Malvasia and the coast of Bosa Marina and Capo Marragiu.

10 places to visit in Bosa, in the province of Oristano (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Serravalle (dei Malaspina)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Fortress of 1112 overlooking Sa Costa, around which the new city grew after the abandonment of the ancient one; it offers a panorama of the town.

  • Where Bosa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di Nostra Signora de sos Regnos Altos

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Inside the walls of the castle, with a cycle of paintings from 1370; at the end of September there are the most heartfelt festivals of the year.

  • Where Bosa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Quartiere di Sa Costa

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval district of colorful houses on the Serravalle hill, with alleys linked by steps in trachyte and cellars where you taste the Malvasia; there you will find funtana manna in red trachyte.

  • Where Bosa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Concerie di Sas Conzas e Museo delle Conce

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

The nineteenth-century neighborhood of tanneries on the opposite bank of the river, a national monument of industrial archaeology, evidence of the production of leather between the mid-nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.

  • Where Bosa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Basilica di San Pietro extra muros

Places of worship · Basilicas

Romanesque Basilica of 1062 in the open countryside, in Calmedia, rest of the ancient Bosa vetus, with the thirteenth-century façade in red trachyte.

  • Where Calmedia, Bosa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it
