What to see in Bolognetta

Country of the inland Palermo born around a foundation along the direction trazzera, called Santa Maria dell'Ogliastro until 1882; it preserves the Mother Church of 1603 and the Museum of the Farm House.

2 places to visit in Bolognetta, in the province of Palermo (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Casa Contadina

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Founded in 1985 by the municipal administration in order to preserve the etnoanthropological traditions: a single compartment on the ground floor reconstructs a land house of the early twentieth century, with tools and objects of the cultivation of wheat, vine and olive trees; in the façade a remarkable eaves in coppi. Quoted from the guide "Musei 2008" of the Touring Club. Open Tuesday and Thursday 9-13, other days by appointment with guided tours of the staff of the Municipal Library.

  • Where Via A. Diaz 45, Bolognetta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.cittametropolitana.pa.it

Sources: turismo.cittametropolitana.pa.it

Chiesa Madre della Madonna del Carmelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1603 next to the foundation around which the village was born and became parish in 1605 at the request of Marco Mancino; the foundation no longer exists, but its site is recognized in Madrice square along the current Via Roma, the ancient directing trazzera.

  • Where Piazza Madrice, Bolognetta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.cittametropolitana.pa.it

Sources: turismo.cittametropolitana.pa.it
