What to see in Biccari

Borgo dei Monti Dauni at about 450 meters, Orange Flag of the Touring and the "Tetto di Puglia": the Byzantine tower of the 12th century with the ethnographic museum, the Cathedral of the Assumption, the Lake Pescara and the ascent to Monte Cornacchia, the highest peak of the region.

7 places to visit in Biccari, in the province of Foggia (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre bizantina

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

A cylindrical tower 23 meters high, with an external diameter of about 13 meters and walls of more than 3 meters at the base, dating from 1116 to 1122 thanks to a parchment of the treasure of the Cathedral of Troy; one of the few traces of the Byzantine walls and buildings. Restored, it houses cultural spaces and the small ethnographic museum; the municipality indicates free access.

  • Where Via Fuori Porta Torre 12, Biccari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.biccari.fg.it · italia.it · touringclub.it

Museo etnografico della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

It tells the rural life of the Dauni Mountains: the peasant house, the work in the fields, the handicrafts and the popular religiosity. Born from the initiative of senior members of Pro Loco, it is located on the second floor of the Byzantine Tower.

  • Where Via Municipio 7, Biccari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Cattedrale di Maria Santissima Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Neoclassical church in the center of the village, rebuilt (since 1850 according to the Municipality) on the site of a church of the seventeenth century demolished for breaks: three naves with Latin cross, the colored striped dome and in the central vault a large painting of Neapolitan school of 1775.

  • Where Centro storico, Biccari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it · comune.biccari.fg.it

Centro storico e Piazza Matteotti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The tour of the center starts from Piazza Matteotti with its monumental fountain and touches the Civic Tower and the cathedral; in August the alleys of the medieval village host the Borgo Vecchio festivity with food and wine itinerary, singing and dancing.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Biccari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Lago Pescara

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural basin of about three hectares reachable from the center through the forest, habitat of carp, barbs and alborelle; around woods, picnic areas, a short ring path and kiosks with local products.

  • Where Lago Pescara, Biccari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Monte Cornacchia e Panchina gigante

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The highest peak of Puglia (1,151 meters), the ... the roof of Puglia... from Lake Pescara there is a hike of about four hours. At the top, at the highest panoramic point of the Capitana, there is a giant bench.

  • Where Monte Cornacchia, Biccari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Parco Avventura Daunia

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Area equipped near the lake, indicated by Italia.it as the largest adventure park in the region: acrobatic trails with walkways suspended among the trees, didactic woods, Trail Frassati and a village on the trees.

  • Where Lago Pescara, Biccari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.biccari.fg.it · touringclub.it
