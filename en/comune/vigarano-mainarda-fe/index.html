Pieve di Vigarano e Villa Beltrami

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In Vigarano Pieve, perhaps the ancient Vicus Varianus, the parish church rebuilt in 1760-77 according to design by Antonio Foschini; at the entrance of the village the villa Beltrami, rural complex with sixteenth-century tower.

  • Where Vigarano Pieve, Vigarano Mainarda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
