What to see in Locana

Municipality of Valle Orco 62 km from Turin, one of the largest in Italy (13,274 hectares, almost one hundred hamlets), which rises from 539 meters of the valley floor to 3,692 of the Torre del Gran San Pietro; to the north the Vallone di Piantonetto is part of the Gran Paradiso National Park. In the capital is the 17th-century parish church of San Pietro in Vincoli, the sanctuary of the Madonna del Cantellino and the Park visitor centre dedicated to chimney sweeps.

Centro visitatori «Antichi e Nuovi Mestieri - Spaciafurnel»

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Visitor centre of the Gran Paradiso National Park in the deconsecrated church of San Francesco, in the historic center: it reconstructs an alpine domestic environment where milk became butter and cheese, tells with an animated theater, a documentary and an interactive sound installation the life of chimney sweeps, men and often children of the valley who in winter went down to work in the city, and presents a plastic of the dams and hydroelectric power plant of the valley of Piantonetto. Open in the summer months and on Christmas and Easter holidays; accessible.

  • Where Via Roma 4, Locana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.locana.to.it

Sources: pngp.it · turismotorino.org · comune.locana.to.it

Parrocchiale di San Pietro in Vincoli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built on the ruins of the church of San Meieiro destroyed by a landslide in 1628 and consecrated in 1681, it has three aisles and facades and Baroque decorations; of the old building remain the Romanesque mullioned windows in micascisto (about 1330) of the bell tower. Inside the main altar in wood sculpted as high as the nave, side altars with baroque and rococo friezes, the Collino organ of 1863 and the decorations of the brothers Ponchia (1911).

  • Where Piazza San Pietro, Locana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.locana.to.it

Sources: comune.locana.to.it

Santuario della Madonna del Cantellino

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Sanctuary with two naves in the capital, preceded by a pronao on pillars with round arches, roof in stone louse, frescoed facade with religious subjects and statue of the Madonna with Child on the tympanum, brick bell tower. The Cantellino is linked to the patronal feast of the Nativity of Mary.

  • Where Via Roma angolo Via Aldo Moro, Locana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.locana.to.it

Sources: comune.locana.to.it

Ponte romanico di Vernè

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

A single arched bridge, lowered and slightly sharp on the Rio Vallunga near the hamlet Vernè, of a system certainly before the nineteenth century and once very frequented along a commercial itinerary; it is made of local stone split with original parapets and is still accessible. It is reached in short with a path in the woods along the river.

  • Where Borgo Vernè 1, Locana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.locana.to.it

Sources: comune.locana.to.it

Vallone di Piantonetto

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the northern part of the territory, with the Valsoera valley, it is part of the Gran Paradiso National Park and is known for its protected flora and fauna, alpine lakes, glaciers and peaks suitable for trekking, mountaineering and climbing; there pass the Great Alpine Traverse and the High Via del Canavese with huts and bivouacs.

  • Where Vallone di Piantonetto, Locana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.locana.to.it

Sources: comune.locana.to.it
