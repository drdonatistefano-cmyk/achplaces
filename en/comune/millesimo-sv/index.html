What to see in Millesimo

Borgo dell'alta Val Bormida founded in 1206 by Marquis Enrico II Del Carretto, among the most beautiful villages in Italy: the fortified bridge of the Gaietta, the castle and the Napoleonic Museum in Villa Scarzella; truffle land.

6 places to visit in Millesimo, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Millesimo in the guide with map and filters

Ponte della Gaietta

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Symbol of the village and rare Italian example of fortified bridge, with original body of the twelfth century; not far away the house where Enrico II Del Carretto signed in 1206 the act of foundation.

  • Where Millesimo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Castello Del Carretto

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built by Enrico II to defend the village, it formed with Cengio, Cosseria and Roccavignale a defensive quadrilateral on the road between Piedmont and Liguria; stone tower and eastern walls are the oldest parts.

  • Where Millesimo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Villa Scarzella e Museo Napoleonico

Museums · City history

Villa of 1855 with garden at the foot of the castle, municipal since 1989; houses the museum with prints, maps, posters and Napoleonic vintage notices.

  • Where Millesimo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Millesimo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Already of the monks of Sant'Antonio Abate, consecrated in 1467 by Piero del Carretto; marble saintly waterworks of 1484 and a baroque crucifix of the Margliano school.

  • Where Millesimo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria extra muros

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Outside the center, cited in 998 as St. Petri de Melesino; it preserves a baptismal font and Gothic frescoes.

  • Where Millesimo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Monastero di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Purchased by Henry II Del Carretto and donated in 1216 to the Cistercian nuns of Santa Maria de Betton.

  • Entry To be checked
