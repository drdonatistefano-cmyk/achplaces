What to see in Gadoni

The village of Barbagia di Belvì is about 700 meters on the slopes of Gennargentu, founded in the fifteenth century, with the ancient area paved in red and black stone: a few kilometers away the copper mine of Funtana Raminosa, today the museum of the Geominerario Park, and the plateau of Corongia with the rocks of Is Breccas on the valley of Flumendosa.

Miniera di Funtana Raminosa

Museums · Themed

Copper mine already exploited by the Nuragics and then by Phoenicians, Carthaginians and Romans (there remain galleries, a ingot and the remains of an imperial miner); excavations since 1517, flotation plant since 1920, mining village since 1936 with school, infirmary, shop and church, about 300 workers in the 1960s and closure in 1983, when nineteen miners occupied the wells for twenty days to 400 meters deep. Open to the public since 2020 as an area of the Geominerario Park: you can visit the Phoenician and Roman galleries, the laundry left as it was on the last day of work, cable car, trains and village, with former miners such as guides and helmet.

visit only by reservation

Is Breccas e altopiano di Corongia

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Path in the forest of Corongia, on a plateau overlooking the valley of the Flumendosa, among cliffs, labyrinths, pinnacles and caves covered by holm oak woods, badgers, terebinths and junipers, with wild peonies and orchids: the pinnacle On Bell Towers (84 m), the walls of Tinazzu up to 100 meters high and the cave Sa Corongia. Walking and mountain biking; equipped picnic area in Bauzzoni.

  • Where Foresta di Corongia, Gadoni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Rione antico e chiese del paese

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The oldest district, with houses arranged in semicircle, paved streets in red and black stone, stairs and walls; in the center of the village the parish church of the Assunta and the church of Santa Marta, the patroness celebrated at the end of July. The women of Gadoni weave to the horizontal frame sa burra, traditional carpet born as a blanket.

  • Where Centro storico, Gadoni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
