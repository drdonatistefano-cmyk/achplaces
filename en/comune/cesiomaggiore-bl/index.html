What to see in Cesiomaggiore

The town of Feltrino at the foot of the Dolomiti Bellunesi National Park, the gateway to Val Canzoi, the glacial valley of the Caorame stream with the gorges of Ponte Umin, the Stua lake and the ancient calchere; in Seravella the ethnographic museum of the Dolomites in a nineteenth-century villa, and in the village the historical museum of the bicycle.

5 places to visit in Cesiomaggiore, in the province of Belluno (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Etnografico Dolomiti (Villa Avogadro degli Azzoni, Seravella)

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

In the 19th century country villa of the counts Avogadro degli Azzoni, with rustic outbuildings, more than 4 hectares of meadows, woods and fields, an ancient rose garden and a didactic apiary, at the foot of the Park. It collects objects from Bellunese from the eighteenth century to today on crafts, home, religion, clothing and emigration, with sections on the baliatic and emigration to Brazil of the late nineteenth century; thousands of recordings of stories, songs and prayers, photographic and film archives and the collection of mountain art by Giuseppe Mazzotti. Open Mar-Fri 9-13, Sat and Sun 15-18.30; closed Monday.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Museo storico della bicicletta «Toni Bevilacqua»

Museums · Themed

Born from the collection of Sergio Sanvido (1928-2015), restorer and collector of bicycles, donated in part to the Municipality in 2007: from the precursors, like a celerifer in wood of late eighteenth century, two Michaux and the bike Grand-bi, to the bikes from work, from city, military and as a child, to the bikes from race tied to Bottecchia, Coppi, Bartali, Saronni, Moser and Pantani, with accessories, shirts and covers of the Sunday of Corriere. Autonomous visit with tablet or app; open 10-13 and 15-18 on weekends from April to October (also on Fridays in July and August).

seasonal opening, closed from November to March

Val Canzoi, gole di Ponte Umin e lago della Stua

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Glacial valley engraved in the heart of the Park by a branch of the ancient glacier of Piave, which crosses the entire rocky series of the Bellunesi Dolomites; the Caorame stream dug the gorges of Ponte Umin and Stua, with pots and small waterfalls. From Le Ave an easy road climbs to the Stua artificial lake, with deviations to the pond of Sant'Eustachio and to the source of Fraina bass, where in spring you can see tritons, frogs, salamanders and watermelons; by bike from Soranzen to Casa Al Frassen are 18 km (393 m of altitude difference) passing through the church of Santa Eurosia.

  • Where Soranzen, Val Canzoi, Cesiomaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.dolomitipark.it

Sources: dolomitipark.it

Calchere della Val Canzoi

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Large circular lime kilns placed on the slope, partly underground and open at the top, used until the 1950s: of the 30 recorded there remain 17, some restored for documentary purposes. The easiest to see are those of Fraìna Alta, La Guarda and Pattachin, the latter visible from the valley floor road.

  • Where Strada della Val Canzoi, Cesiomaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.dolomitipark.it

Sources: dolomitipark.it

Pendana e Casera Brendòl

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Alm complex at 1,686 meters on the plateau of Erèra-Brendòl, documented from 1466 and abandoned in the fifties: the 27 meters long casera and the pendant, 91 meters canopy with 25 stone arches for the cattle, collapsed in part in the thirties and after the earthquake of Friuli 1976 and restored by the Park. Go up from Lake Stua to the CAI 802 mule track.

  • Where Altopiano di Erèra-Brendòl, Cesiomaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.dolomitipark.it

Sources: dolomitipark.it
