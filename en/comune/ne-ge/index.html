What to see in Ne

The capital of Val Graveglia, in the Aveto Regional Natural Park, a valley of great geological and mineralogical interest; there is the manganese mine of Gambatesa, now a museum, and in Reppia a mineralogical museum.

3 places to visit in Ne, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Ne in the guide with map and filters

Miniera di Gambatesa

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the upper Graveglia Valley, less than 20 km from Lavagna, the manganese deposit in the Monte Alpe diaspers was the most important in Europe; it has given numerous rare and new minerals. Museum since 2000, owned by the Aveto Park Authority since 2009: more than 8 of the more than 25 km of galleries are equipped for the visit, reached with the mining train, with routes of different lengths and difficulties and guided tours. Visitor center with bar, multimedia room, mineral collections, picnic area, guesthouse and self-guided paths. Tel. 0185 338876.

the MiC card is closed every day during the hours; check opening on the mine site

  • Where Via Botasi, Ne
  • Entry €11, reduced €8
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lamialiguria.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · lamialiguria.it

Museo mineralogico di Reppia

Museums · Science and university

It exhibits minerals and rocks from the mining area of eastern Liguria and northern Apennines, sorted by extract element and type of mine, with samples from the mines of the provinces of Parma, Modena, Piacenza, Genoa and La Spezia. An ancient miners' trail (about 2.5 km) connects it to Gambatesa. Tel. 0185 338876.

the MiC card (updated to December 2020) reports "Closed" every day at the times

  • Where Via Corte, Reppia, Ne
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lamialiguria.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · lamialiguria.it

Val Graveglia

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valle del Parco dell'Aveto considered a geological heritage of considerable scientific, naturalistic and economic interest, where to observe geological, mineralogical, geomorphological and karst aspects.

  • Where Val Graveglia, Ne
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lamialiguria.it

Sources: lamialiguria.it
