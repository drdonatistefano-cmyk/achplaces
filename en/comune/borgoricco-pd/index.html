What to see in Borgoricco

In the heart of the Roman grateful north-east of Padua, Borgoricco has in the civic center designed by Aldo Rossi its symbolic place, with the theater and the Museum of the Roman Centuration; in the territory, the eighteenth-century parish church of San Leonardo, the ancient parish church of Sant'Eufemia and the oratory of San Nicolò a Favariego, built in part with Roman material.

Museo della Centuriazione Romana

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the civic center designed by Aldo Rossi, it tells the Roman centuriation of the territory between Villa del Conte and Camposampiero, bordered by the Muson Vecchio, the territory of Mirano and the Brenta: from the imposition of the agricultural grid to agriculture, breeding, trade and domestic life, to the transformations followed by barbaric invasions. All the finds come from the centuriate area, including those of the recent excavations in Borgoricco with the Superintendence; among the most valuable pieces the bronze strigili and the architectural slabs in terracotta decorated with vegetable bas-relief, considered unique. Guided visits, workshops for schools, temporary exhibitions and activities with the University of Padua. Closed on Mondays; reduced for schools, 7-14 year olds and over 65 years olds.

  • Where Viale Europa 12, Borgoricco
  • Entry €3, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismopadova.it

Centro Culturale Aldo Rossi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The civic centre of Borgoricco is a multifunctional space designed by Aldo Rossi, one of the most revolutionary architects of the twentieth century: the project takes up the multifunctional role of the Venetian villa and its geometric shapes as well. It houses a large theatre and the Museum of the Roman Centuration and is barrier-free. In 2026 the project Visit Camposampierese proposed free evening openings on reservation with guided tour of the architecture.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Graticolato romano

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

To the northeast of Padua, around Borgoricco, the countryside preserves the network of straight roads that cross at the right angle of the centuriation favoured by Ottaviano Augusto: squares, called centuries, of about 710 meters on the side, divided into lots assigned to the settlers. The municipality describes it as perfectly preserved; along the intersections rise votive capitals.

  • Where Campagna di Borgoricco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismopadova.it

Sources: comune.borgoricco.pd.it · turismopadova.it

Parrocchiale di San Leonardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was already mentioned in a donation of 1085, the current church was built in 1772 with a single nave. It preserves a pulpit in wood carved with golden profiles and five altars in polychrome marble; two neoclassical altars of the mid-19th century preserve paintings of the Venetian Sebastiano Santi, and the fresco of the ceiling by G.B. Canal depicts the Assumption of Mary.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismopadova.it

Arcipretale di Sant'Eufemia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient parish church that gave its name to the village, mentioned in the testament of Speronella of 1192; rebuilt in 1946 with a single nave, it preserves a baptismal font in marble dated 1490 and three altarpieces of remarkable artistic value.

  • Where Sant'Eufemia, Borgoricco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismopadova.it

Sources: comune.borgoricco.pd.it · turismopadova.it

Oratorio di San Nicolò a Favariego

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient oratory, built in part with Roman material of recovery, on a small rise surrounded by a moat; cited in the testament of Speronella of 1192, contains inside two fourteenth-century frescoes with St. Nicholas and St. Christopher.
