What to see in Oderzo

The ancient Opitergium, commercial crossroads between Veneto Euganeo, Alpine and Eastern: the Roman forum with the patrician houses, the archaeological museum among the oldest in Italy and the scenic Piazza Grande.

6 places to visit in Oderzo, in the province of Treviso (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Archeologico «Eno Bellis»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Opened in 1881 and today in the Barchessa of Palazzo Foscolo: Venetian inscriptions, votive bronzes, the three-dimensional reconstruction of Opitergium, monuments of the Roman necropolis and late antique polychrome mosaics of a rich urban house.

  • Where Via Garibaldi 63, Oderzo
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: venetoaroundme.regione.veneto.it · italia.it
