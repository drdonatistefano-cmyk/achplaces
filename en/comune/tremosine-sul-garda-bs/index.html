What to see in Tremosine sul Garda

Highland in the Alto Garda Bresciano Park, with the village of Pieve overlooking a cliff excavated by glaciers overlooking the lake; there climbs the spectacular Strada della Forra of 1913, while on the lake the village of Campione is an industrial village become paradise of windsurfing.

5 places to visit in Tremosine sul Garda, in the province of Brescia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pieve e Piazza Cozzaglio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The houses of Pieve line up on the edge of the plateau, on top of a cliff excavated by glaciers about 423 meters above the lake. From the churchyard you arrive at Piazza Cozzaglio, with the old town hall and fountain and a wonderful view of the lake and Mount Baldo; in Via Scala Tonda 7 Arturo Cozzaglio was born and on the wall of the municipal archive the lion of San Marco recalls the Venetian domination. Nearby, thrills are suspended 350 meters above the lake.

  • Where Piazza Cozzaglio, Pieve, Tremosine sul Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Pieve

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

An eighteenth-century church whose bell tower is what remains of the Romanesque parish church demolished around 1570; it preserves wooden works by Luchini and a altarpiece by Francesco Barbieri (1687). For three days a year, at the beginning of Lent, on the high altar is set up the machine of the triduum, eighteenth-century wood scenery painted with 365 candles.

  • Where Pieve, Tremosine sul Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Strada della Forra

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built in 1913 on the initiative of the parish priest to connect Pieve to the port, salt for about 6 km from the lake (65 m) to Pieve (423 m) along the deep gorge of the Brasa stream, between tunnels and narrow hairpin bends, sometimes dug into the rock and sometimes overhanging. At the opening a correspondent of the Frankfurter Zeitung called it the most beautiful street in the world. It is recommended to moderate speed and avoid holiday weekends.

  • Where SP 38, Tremosine sul Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Campione del Garda

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The only village on the shore of the lake, on a small conoid at the base of the mountain: in 1896 Giacomo Feltrinelli built a cotton mill around which an industrial village was born with working houses, school, theatre and church. Closed the factory in 1981, the country reinvented itself with tourism: beaches and constant wind (Peler in the morning, Now in the afternoon) make it a paradise for windsurfing, kitesurfing and sailing, with schools and sailing circle.

  • Where Campione del Garda, Tremosine sul Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Mulattiere e sentieri del confine

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Mulattiere go up along the slopes to the places that marked the border between Italy and Austria-Hungary until 1918, with well preserved positions of the First World War; from San Michele and Bondo the paths lead to Tremalzo, towards the valley of Ledro.

  • Where San Michele e Bondo, Tremosine sul Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
