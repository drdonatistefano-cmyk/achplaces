What to see in Minervino Murge

Panoramic village called "balcony of the Apulia" overlooking the valley of Ofanto and the National Park of the Alta Murgia; the historic center, the "Scesciolia" is a labyrinth of tuff and white facades, with the Duomo, and at the foot of the town opens the Cave of San Michele, a place of thousand year old worship.

5 places to visit in Minervino Murge, in the province of Barletta-Andria-Trani (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico (la Scesciola)

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town, called "Sciscola" is a name of probable Arab origin meaning maze, is an intricate of tuff and white facades. The village, in a privileged position on the valley of Ofanto, on the Park of Alta Murgia and on the nearby Basilicata, is called the "balcony of Puglia" (Puglia), according to the tradition part of the name derives from a temple of Minerva on which the village arose.

  • Where Centro storico, Minervino Murge
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Grotta di San Michele

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Karst cavities formed about two million years ago at the foot of the village, dug into the rock, with a descent of about 20 meters along a staircase as wide as the cave; born as a place of pagan worship, it is mentioned for the first time in a parchment of the year 1000 preserved in Montecassino. It has a neoclassical entrance, frescoed vault, columns and a marble statue of the Archangel, celebrated every year on 29 September. Visits on request, entrance fee.

  • Where Minervino Murge
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Chiesa rupestre della Madonna della Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A single nave rock church, rich in folk frescoes, consecrated or consecrated in 1628 by the bishop of Minervino Altobello Carissimi; you arrive there with a path from the Grotto of San Michele.

  • Where Presso la Grotta di San Michele, Minervino Murge
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Faro monumento ai caduti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lighthouse built in 1932 in memory of the fallen of the First World War: from the top of its 32 meters dominates the Murge and the valley of Ofanto.
