What to see in Numana

Locality of the Riviera del Conero divided between Numana Alta, the ancient village on a promontory with the Costarella and the Arch of Torre, and Numana Bassa with the marina and the long beach towards Marcelli. The state Antiquarium exhibits the tomb of the Queen of Sirolo, the Sanctuary of the Crucifix a Byzantine wooden crucifix.

Antiquarium statale di Numana

Museums · Archaeological

It tells the development of ancient Numana and the Riviera del Conero from prehistory to the Roman age, with attention to the trade of the Numana store in the Mediterranean. On the first floor the rich burial of the Queen of Sirolo (VI century BC), with attic and picene ceramics, bronzes, avori, amber jewelry and the remains of a horse and a war chariot, flanked by the picena tomb of a warrior; on the second floor the chronological section, and in closing the collection of Girolamo Rilli. Among the pieces, a Roman tile with a ship engraved in the fresh clay. Times indicated: Tuesday to Saturday 8:30-19:45, Sunday 14-19:45, Monday closed.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Numana Alta e la Costarella

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

It is the oldest part, with white stone houses and narrow alleys; the Costarella, a staircase that fishermen used to go down to the port, connects the village to the beaches. There are the Town Hall, in a building of 1773 already summer residence of the bishops of Ancona, a seventeenth-century fountain with five cinnamons fed by a conduit of the ancient Roman aqueduct and the gardens of Piazza Nova, with views of the limestone massif of Conero.

  • Where Numana Alta, Numana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Arco di Torre e belvedere

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the promontory above the port, the arch is what remains of the bell tower of a missing church or a watchtower, destroyed by the earthquake of 1930; next stands a bronze monument to fishermen. The nearby viewpoint dominates the port and coast.

  • Where Numana Alta, Numana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it
