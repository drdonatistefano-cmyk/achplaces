What to see in Maierà

Borgo del Tirreno cosentino about 8 km from Diamante, born in the 11th century around the castle, with a historic center rich in murals and ceramics and an ancient palace that houses the Museo del chiperoncino dell'Accademia Italiana del Peperoncino.

Museo del peperoncino

Museums · Themed

In the Palazzo Ducale, managed by the Accademia Italiana del Peperoncino di Diamante: four rooms with objects and utensils of all origins, the routes of diffusion of the plant, seeds, spices and sauces, and a video room with advertising posters and satirical cartoons on the chili. Between the pieces a lithography by Renato Guttuso and the image of chili taken from a recipe book of the sixteenth century found in Frankfurt.

MiC card (updated 2022) is closed every day; times to be checked with the Accademia del Chiperoncino

Centro storico di Maierà

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

A ridge village whose first traces date back to the Basilian monks between the 6th and 7th centuries; in the 11th century the town grew around the castle and until the abolition of the fief in 1806 feudal families such as Matera, Loria and Carafa were succeeded. The narrow streets preserve works of modern art, murals and ceramics of various artists; among the historical buildings the parish church of Santa Maria del Piano and the ancient palace seat of the Museum of Chili pepper.

  • Where Via Portella, Maierà
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sigecweb.beniculturali.it · italia.it
