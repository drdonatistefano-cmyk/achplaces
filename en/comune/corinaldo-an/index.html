What to see in Corinaldo

Marche village in bricks enclosed by a 15th-century wall among the best preserved of the Marche, with the tower of the Sperone attributed to Francesco di Giorgio Martini, the staircase of the Piaggia with the Polenta Well, the Goldoni Theatre, the Civic collection of art "Claudio Ridolfi" and the places of Santa Maria Goretti, born here in 1890.

Mura e torri

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

A 912-metre-long road that remained almost intact from the fifteenth century, with the two circles of 1367 and 1480: gates, bastions, Ghibelline blackbirds with swallow tails and round walkways. The Torre dello Sperone, pentagonal and 18 meters high, attributed to Francesco di Giorgio Martini, the Torre dello Scorticatore, where the walls reach 15 meters, the Round of enlargement completed in 1490, the Landroni porticoati and Porta San Giovanni; from the terrace near the Bargello the view arrives at the Conero. You can also visit them with a guide.

La Piaggia e il Pozzo della Polenta

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

109 steps up to the top of the hill, flanked by red brick houses arranged with fishbone; in the middle there is the Polenta Well, where according to legend the inhabitants cooked polenta during the sieges. The legend lives again in July in the Polenta Pozzo Contesa, with over 300 re-enactors.

  • Where Via della Piaggia, Corinaldo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Civica raccolta d'arte «Claudio Ridolfi»

Museums · Old masters

Since 2012 in the former convent of the Hermits of St. Augustine, it collects sacred art from demolished churches and suppressed orders: works of Hercules Ramazzani, Domenico Peruzzini and two of Claudio Ridolfi, a Veronese painter who died in Corinaldo in 1644, eighteen Sicilian bust reliquaries of the early seventeenth century, a silver processional cross of 1615 and a section on the Prince's Tomb, a Piceno necropolis of the VII century BC, with a princely helmet. It is part of the Cultural Places card for sale at IAT.

  • Where Largo XVII Settembre 1860 1, Corinaldo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Santuario di Santa Maria Goretti

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In the upper part of the old town, it dates back to the mid-eighteenth century and incorporates the ancient medieval church of San Nicolò; inside a large wooden choir with organ of Callido of 1767. It is dedicated to Maria Goretti, born in Corinaldo in 1890 and canonized in 1950.

  • Entry To be checked

Teatro Carlo Goldoni

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Municipal theatre built between 1861 and 1869, reopened in 2005 after the restoration; originally had a mechanism to raise or lower the audience. Today it hosts prose, concerts and dance and is in the circuit of the Cultural Places card.

  • Entry To be checked

Chiese del centro: San Francesco, Suffragio e Addolorata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The collegiate church of San Francesco dates back to 1265, renovated in the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries; the church of Suffragio, reopened in 1779, preserves a painting by Claudio Ridolfi; the Addolorata, consecrated in 1755, has an organ of Callido above the entrance.
