What to see in Calitri

Borgo dell'Alta Irpinia of the 13th century, known as the "Positano dell'Irpinia" (Positano dell'Irpinia) for its triangular plant and medieval structure with alleys and views; it was the epicenter of the earthquake of 1910 and is known for its bread and ceramics, told in the museum of Borgo Castello. Since 2013 it hosts every summer the Sponz Fest designed by Vinicio Capossela.

Museo della ceramica del Borgo Castello

Museums · Themed

Established on 29 September 2008 in the restored buildings of Borgo Castello, the castle complex destroyed by the earthquake of 1694, then inhabited village and municipal property after the earthquake of 1980. The route goes from the finds of the Fossakultur of Oliveto-Cairano and of Roman age to the half medieval majolica and the Renaissance majolica, up to the production of the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, to the historical bricks, industrial ceramics and the works of contemporary craftsmen.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Borgo Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

It is reached from Piano San Michele, site of an ancient church destroyed before the earthquake of 1910, climbing the staircase next to Palazzo Mirelli; the premises of the village, built on the castle collapsed in 1694, have been restored by the Superintendence of Salerno and Avellino.

  • Where Via Castello, Calitri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.calitri.av.it · italia.it

Centro storico, palazzi e portali

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The route starts from Piazza della Repubblica, where the mother church of San Canio was demolished in 1883, and passes through the stone portals of Palazzo Rinaldi (today library) and Palazzo Zampaglione, the Arch of Zampaglione with its mask, Palazzo Berrilli with the clock stopped from 23 November 1980, the alley San Nicola, the narrowest of the village, and the loggettes of Via Alfonso del Re. From the steps of Via dell'Arco di Zampaglione you can see Mount Calvario with the church of Santa Lucia.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica, Calitri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.calitri.av.it · italia.it

Chiesa e monastero dell'Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The sixteenth-century church, with a Renaissance bezel portal, is among the few remaining standing after the earthquake of 1694; next door, the monastery founded in 1557 for the Benedictines and finished in 1586 is now the Town Hall, and its portal on Via Roma was the entrance of the inner garden.

  • Where Via Roma, Calitri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.calitri.av.it · italia.it

Palazzo Baronale Mirelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Built by Prince Mirelli after the collapse of the castle in the earthquake of 1694, reusing materials of the castle, including the majestic portal on Via Roma that was once its entrance.

  • Where Via Roma, Calitri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
