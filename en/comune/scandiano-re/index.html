What to see in Scandiano

A small town of the first hills of Reggio Emilia from the centre of Renaissance plant, home to the poet Matteo Maria Boiardo and naturalist Lazzaro Spallanzani; around the Rocca dei Boiardo there are piazza Spallanzani with the statue of the scientist, his birthplace and the churches of the center, while in the hamlets rises the castle of Arceto.

Rocca dei Boiardo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Fortress of the Da Fogliano with walls, moat, drawbridge and towers, become residence of the Boiardo (1423-1560) and transformed into a palace by the Thiene (1565-1623), who entrusted Giovan Battista Aleotti called the Argenta staircase, the south facade and the west tower; Bentivoglio and Este added baroque decorations. Here Matteo Maria Boiardo, author of the Orlando in love, was born, and Spallanzani experimented in the underground; the frescoes of Nicolò dell'Abate with stories of the Aeneid were detached at the end of the eighteenth century and are today at the Galleria Estense in Modena. Since 2011 it hosts the regional wine shop.

Casa Spallanzani

Museums · House museums

18th century building near the fortress, birthplace of naturalist Lazzaro Spallanzani (1729-1799), from which the scientist started his research on the Reggiano Apennines; it was declared a national monument in 1939.

  • Where Centro storico, Scandiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.scandiano.re.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitemilia.com · italia.it

Piazza Spallanzani

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Main square of the city, with urban layout of the first fifteenth century and surrounded by ancient porticos; in the center the statue of Lazzaro Spallanzani, work of Guglielmo Fornaciari of 1888.

  • Where Piazza Spallanzani, Scandiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitemilia.com · comune.scandiano.re.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Vergine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish church on Piazza Boiardo with a fifteenth-century bell tower with two windows; it preserves the sixteenth-century polychrome group of Our Lady of the Rosary, a painting by Bartolomeo Passarotti with Saint Catherine of Alexandria and the tomb of Lazarus Spallanzani.

  • Where Piazza Boiardo, Scandiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitemilia.com · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Giuseppe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the eighteenth century with facade of the time; it preserves a tabernacle of the school of the Ceccati and remains of frescoes attributed to Nicolò dell'Abate.

  • Where Piazza Duca d'Aosta, Scandiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa e convento dei Cappuccini

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

The seventeenth-century complex, with history dating back to the early seventeenth century, and the church from the façade of 1902; inside the large painting of Stefano da Carpi with the Exaltation of the Franciscan Saints and the wooden tabernacle of Fra' Fedele da Scandiano at the high altar.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Castello e borgo di Arceto

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Hamlet at 72 meters, town mentioned already in the IX century, with the church of Santa Maria Assunta and the castle transformed in the eighteenth century into a noble residence; in the courtyard of the castle there is the small oratory of San Rocco and in Piazza Pignini the eighteenth-century civic tower of the clock.

  • Where Arceto, Scandiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitemilia.com · comune.scandiano.re.it · italia.it

Ciclopedonale del Tresinaro e Sentiero Spallanzani

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

A simple and safe route that runs along the river Tresinaro to the village of Cà de Caroli and to the bridge of Via del Cristo, with resting areas and panoramic points; it is part of the Spallanzani Path, which in 1988 had the official starting point in Ventoso.

  • Where Lungo il Tresinaro, Scandiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitemilia.com · italia.it
