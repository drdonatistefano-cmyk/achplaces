What to see in Santena

Santena is the city of Camillo Benso di Cavour: the eighteenth-century family castle houses the Memorial Cavour and the tomb of the statesman, surrounded by an English park of 23 hectares designed by Xavier Kurten; in the village, the parish church of Saints Peter and Paul with its high bell tower.

3 places to visit in Santena, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Cavour e Memoriale Cavour

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

An ancient residence renovated several times, today of eighteenth-century appearance on a project by the architect of court Francesco Gallo, country house of the Benso di Cavour where the statesman was born, formed and worked. It is a museum house dedicated to Camillo Benso and the lifestyle of the Piedmontese ruling classes under the Savoy: the Memorial, reopened on March 17, 2021 after a long restoration, includes the tomb of Cavour in the crypt of the chapel, declared a national monument. Reservation required; free with Torino+Piemonte Card and for children under 6 years.

  • Where Piazza Visconti Venosta 2, Santena
  • Entry €10 multimedia visit, €16 guided tour; reduced from €2.50 to €8
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website fondazionecavour.it

Sources: fondazionecavour.it · turismotorino.org · comune.santena.to.it · italia.it

Parco del Castello Cavour

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

English-style landscaped park designed by Xavier Kurten, spread over 23 hectares with age-old plane trees and a botanical garden, around the castle. Open all year round: in winter 9-16:30, in summer 8-19; you can have a packed lunch, but there are no picnic areas equipped.

  • Where Piazza Visconti Venosta 2, Santena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website fondazionecavour.it

Sources: fondazionecavour.it · turismotorino.org · italia.it

Chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish church of Santena with high bell tower and neo-baroque façade.

  • Where Centro storico, Santena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
