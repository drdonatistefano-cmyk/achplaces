What to see in Rosazza

The most beautiful village in Italy in the upper Cervo Valley, at the meeting between Cervo and Pragnetta, redesigned in the nineteenth century by Senator Federico Rosazza Pistolet with the architect Giuseppe Maffei.

5 places to visit in Rosazza, in the province of Biella (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Rosazza in the guide with map and filters

Chiesa nuova e castello neogotico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The new church of 1876 and the neo-Gothic castle, wanted by Federico Rosazza Pistolet (1813-1899) with the architect Giuseppe Maffei, along with the town hall.

  • Where Rosazza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Cimitero monumentale e ponte di pietra

Places of worship · Monumental cemeteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Cemetery on four levels with marble and granite sculptures rich in esoteric and Masonic symbols, reached by a stone bridge with three arches.

  • Where Rosazza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Fontane parlanti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Twenty-seven fountains of water springed in the village and in the hamlets, each with an engraved message; some decorated with five-pointed roses and stars.

  • Where Rosazza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Casa Museo dell'Alta Valle del Cervo

Museums · House museums

An eighteenth-century building of 17 rooms that documents the life and culture of Rosazza and Valle Cervo, with the precision instruments of the technical school.

  • Where Rosazza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Oratori della Beccara

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The oratories of Carmine and San Defendente, of the Five and of the seventeenth century, in the hamlet Beccara.

  • Where Beccara, Rosazza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
