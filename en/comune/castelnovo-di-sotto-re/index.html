What to see in Castelnovo di Sotto

Borgo della bassa Reggiana was born around its fortress, now seat of the Town Hall, with a long porticoed road, the church of the Madonna with paintings of Lionello Spada and Palma the Younger, the Museum of Mask tied to the Carnival of Castlein and a museum of vintage shutters.

Museo della maschera del Carnevale

Museums · Themed

The museum mainly documents the tradition of the Castlein Carnival and the history and manufacture of the mask: the nucleus is a collection of 222 pieces among masks, models in canvas and plaster, metal moulds and printing materials, acquired by the Municipality in 1997 and enriched in 2002 by masks donated by the collector Eugenio Gabrielli. Some rooms are dedicated to the mask factory of the Guatteri family, founded in 1810 by Prospero Guatteri and active until 1933.

  • Where Piazza IV Novembre 1, Castelnovo di Sotto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo delle tappatrici d'epoca

Museums · Themed

Private collection of ancient corking machines, dated between 1600 and 1950 (over 200 according to the MiC, almost 300 according to Emilia-Romagna Turismo), with tools and objects related to the bottling of wine.

  • Where Via Repubblica 4, Castelnovo di Sotto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Rocca di Castelnovo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

The fortress, of early medieval origin (IX century according to Emilia-Romagna Turismo), is the historical symbol of the village and testifies its castle origins; today it houses the Town Hall.

Chiesa della Beata Vergine della Misericordia (chiesa della Madonna)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

He preserves seventeenth-century Padan canvases, including the Nativity of the Virgin by Lionello Spada and a Crucifixion of Palma il Giovane.

Centro storico e via porticata

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center crosses along a long road flanked by porticoed houses, between streets and ancient houses; in February the carts of the Carnival of Castlein parade.
