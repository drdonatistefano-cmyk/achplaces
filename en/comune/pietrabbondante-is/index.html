What to see in Pietrabbondante

Small village of Alto Molise about 1,000 meters on the slopes of Monte Saraceno, of Sannita origin, with the ancient nucleus framed by the two great rocks of the Morgia of the Tower and the Morgia of the Castle; just south there is the Italic Sanctuary, the most important place of cult of the Sannitic state, with theater, temple and Domus publica.

Santuario italico di Pietrabbondante

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On a panoramic hill overlooking the valley of the Trigno, in Calcatello, it was the main place of worship of the Sanniti Pentri and the political center of the confederation, the most important place of worship of the Sanniti state. Between the third and first centuries B.C., the Samnites built a smaller temple on the remains of a building destroyed by the Carthaginians of Hannibal (beginning of the second century B.C., the podium remains), the major temple completed at the beginning of the first century B.C. with its imposing podium and semicircular cavea known as the Theatre of the end of the second century B.C., probably destined for assemblies and particularly well preserved. In 2002 on the south-west terrace was identified the Domus publica, probably the seat of the supreme Sannite magistrate; the excavations are still ongoing. Hours indicated by the Regional Direction Museums: Tuesday-Sunday 8.45-19.15, last entrance 30 minutes before closing, Monday closed.

  • Where Località Calcatello, 86085 Pietrabbondante
  • Entry €6, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.musei.molise.beniculturali.it

Sources: musei.molise.beniculturali.it · italia.it

Fortificazioni sannitiche di Monte Saraceno

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On Mount Saraceno (Caraceno), about 1,000 meters above the valley of the Trigno and about one kilometer from the village, remains the traces of a Sannite settlement with fortifications of the fourth century BC, dismantled by the Romans after the third Sannite war; the sanctuary rises little lower. Visit Molise indicates them as a stop for archaeology enthusiasts and indicates paths through meadows, shrubs and woods with a view of the valley.

  • Where Monte Saraceno, Pietrabbondante
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu

Centro storico, Morge e Palazzo baronale Marchesani

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village, at 1,027 meters, has a medieval structure built also with stones and finds taken from the archaeological area; the oldest part, to the north-west, is framed by the two great rocks of the Morgia della Torre and the Morgia del Castello, which give the village its profile. The centre is dominated by the Baronial Palace of the Marchesani, linked to the family's events in the insurrections of 1860 and in the Second World War; in the tower Francesco Marchesani hid the family during the nineteenth-century revolts.

  • Where Centro storico, Pietrabbondante
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu
