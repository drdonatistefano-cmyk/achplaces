What to see in Ponte Nossa

The village of the average Seriana Valley at the confluence of Nossana nel Serio, whose springs fed for centuries the sweaters (forge documented from the fifteenth century) today told by the Maglio Museo; at the entrance of the village the sanctuary of the Madonna delle Lacrime, linked to the apparition of 2 June 1511 and famous for the embalmed crocodile.

Santuario della Madonna delle Lacrime

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Also known as Santa Maria Annunziata and considered a national monument, it replaced a small medieval church on whose façade was a fifteenth-century painting by Giacomo Busca with Our Lady and the Crucifix: according to tradition, on June 2, 1511 a shepherd saw the Virgin move her eyes and cry tears of blood. A unique nave in five bays, a façade with a rose window, renovated at the beginning of the twentieth century on a project by Giovanni Muzio. Above the right entrance is hung an embalmed crocodile of about three meters, documented since 1594.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di San Bernardino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the 17th century on a previous religious building, it consists of two churches side by side and very similar, with two entrances and two naves of four spans. In the left aisle the paintings of the Last Supper and of the Virgin with saints, on the right the altar of the Suffragio with the altarpiece of the Madonna with Child; on the back wall the statue of the saint carried in procession. For the patronal feast (20 May) the bonfire of San Bernardino is lit at the bridge next to the church.

  • Where Via Provinciale (ex SS 671), Ponte Nossa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Maglio Museo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Set up in the eighties in the former Maglio Beltrami, it tells the system of sweats moved from the waters of Nossana: the iron of the mines of Scalve and Bondione was worked at the Maglio Fusore and then forged in the lesser, large and larger sweats to obtain nails, spades, shovels and hoes. The forges of Ponte Nossa are documented from the fifteenth century; the ciodarol lived there with the family until the seventies. Visits only by reservation (035 704388, info@comune.pontenossa.bg.it).

  • Where Via Magli 7, Ponte Nossa
  • Entry €5; schools €2; free 0-6 years and disabled
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Sources: valseriana.eu

Sorgenti della Nossana e via Sorgenti

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

The Nossana, the main tributary of the Serio right, is born from sources that feed an aqueduct of 900-1,500 liters per second, sufficient for 118,000 people in the province of Bergamo, and for centuries moved the sweaters of the village. Along Via Sorgenti remain the traces of a mill mill and the fish hatchery; frequent destination of the schoolchildren, it is combined with the visit to the Maglio Museum.

  • Where Via Sorgenti, Ponte Nossa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Sources: valseriana.eu

Madonna degli Alpini sul Pizzo Guazza

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Statue of the Madonna degli Alpini 4.20 meters high, laid on May 19, 1971 at 911 meters on the mountain overlooking the village (the card calls it both Pizzo Guazza and Corno Falò); on the first Sunday of September the alpine celebrates Mass. On the same summit on May 1st, the "Mas" was replanted, the fir tree brought to the town on April 25th, which on the evening of June 1st became the opening bonfire of the Feast of the Apparition.

  • Where Via Spiazzi, Ponte Nossa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Sources: valseriana.eu
