What to see in Luras

Country of granite of Gallura at the foot of Limbara, which preserves four of the 78 dolmen of Sardinia, the Galluras Museum with the "martello" of the agabbadora feminine, the collection of cork art by Tonino Forteleoni and, towards the Liscia lake, the millennial olive grove S'Ozzastru, estimated in over 3,000 years.

7 places to visit in Luras, in the province of Sassari (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo etnografico Galluras - Museo della femina agabbadora

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

First ethnographic museum of Gallura, in a granite building of the late eighteenth century in the historic center: on three floors it reconstructs the rooms of the Gallura house, with the vinification on the ground floor, kitchen, dining room and bedroom on the first floor and the processing of wool and cork at the last. The symbol piece is the "martello" of the feminine agabbadora, a branch of olive grove of about 40 cm found in an abandoned building near the village, linked to the figure that according to tradition ended the agony of the dying.

  • Where Via Nazionale 35, Luras
  • Entry €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Collezione Forteleoni

Museums · House museums

Works in cork exhibited in the house where he lived the last thirty years Tonino Forteleoni, artisan who exhibited in exhibitions and biennials, collaborated with the ISOLA and taught in the cork section of the Institute of Art of Calangianus, introducing the bas-relief and mosaic in the art of cork. Subjects mainly religious, but also fronts of chest (one in Liberty style), headboards of bed, furniture and tables; the atelier house is next to the church of Santa Croce.

  • Where Via Carlo Alberto, Luras
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Dolmen di Ladas e di Ciuledda

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The recent Neolithic "sepulturas de zigantes" (3500-2700 B.C.). Ladas, the most imposing of the four dolmens of Luras, is a tunnel about 6 meters long and over 2 meters high, covered by two large slabs and closed by an apse; the back cover plate measures about 15 m2. Next to it is Ciuledda, smaller and semicircular, on a granite bench overlooking the Limbara; from both come ceramics of the third millennium BC. Ladas can be reached with a walk of about 15 minutes from the village.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Dolmen di Alzoledda e di Billella

History and architecture · Archaeology

The other two Luras dolmen: Alzoledda, inside the town, is rectangular with trapezoidal chamber, over 2,5 meters long and over 1,5 meters tall, with the background wall formed by a single slab; Billella, between the Vermentino and Nebbiolo vineyards, is about 2,5 meters long and 80 cm high, with the cover slab shaped to fit the supports.

  • Where Luras
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

S'Ozzastru, olivastri millenari

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Olivaster of about 12 meters of circumference with an estimated age in over 3,000 years, considered one of the oldest in Europe and among the twenty centuries old trees of Italy recognized natural monuments. It is located in the valley of Carana, next to the country church of San Bartolomeo, on the northern shore of Liscia lake along the road to Sant'Antonio di Gallura.

  • Where Località Santu Baltolu, Luras
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it · idese.cultura.gov.it

Lago Liscia

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lake, close to the village and to the ancient olive trees, is surrounded by granite; along its banks passes the line of the Green Train, which stops in Luras, and from Sant'Antonio di Gallura you can reach the olive grove also with a shovel boat.

  • Where Lago Liscia, Luras
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiese di Nostra Signora del Rosario e di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The eighteenth-century parish church of Our Lady of the Rosary, in the center of the village on a large churchyard, houses three paintings (the Virgin of the Rosary of the seventeenth century, the Pentecost of 1874 and the Purging Souls of 1927) and two marble lions. In front of it stands Santa Croce, in 1677, the seat of the fraternity that takes care of the rites of Holy Week. In the village also St. Peter (XVII century) and the Purgatory (XVIII century).

  • Where Centro storico, Luras
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
