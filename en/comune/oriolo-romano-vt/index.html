What to see in Oriolo Romano

Borgo 60 km from Rome founded in 1560 by Giorgio Santacroce according to a planned plant, collected around Palazzo Altieri, manorist palace-villa with the gallery of portraits of all the Popes; around the Sabatini Mountains, the Faggeta of Monte Raschio, UNESCO heritage since 2017, and the Natural Monument La Mola on the Mignone.

Palazzo Altieri e Galleria dei Papi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

The manorist palace-villa built between 1578 and 1585 by Santacroce, with a loggia with five arches; the project is traditionally attributed to Vignola, but the Ministry excludes it because it died in 1573. In the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries the Altieri, with Carlo Fontana, added the northern wings with the courtyard, the clock tower, the entrance with basalt bridge and the fountain of the square. The rooms have stuccoes and frescoes with landscapes and fairy tales of Esopo; the Galleria Altieri, initiated by Cardinal Paluzzo Altieri, exhibits about 240 portraits of all the Popes from St. Peter to Francis, the only complete collection of the genre, with copies from Raffaello, Tiziano and Caravaggio. In the garden a rate of over 100 years. Open Tuesday-Sunday 8.30-19.30 (ticket office until 6.30 p.m.), closed on Monday.

  • Where Piazza Giorgio Santacroce, Oriolo Romano
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website villemonumentalituscia.cultura.gov.it

Faggeta di Monte Raschio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Beech of about 150 hectares in the Bracciano-Martignano Regional Natural Park, since 2017 part of the UNESCO site of the ancient primordial beech forests of the Carpathians and other regions of Europe, with beech trees next to oaks and pine trees. It crosses the CAI 175C trail, a 8.4 km ring (about 2 hours, 205 m of climb, difficulty T) that starts from the main square under the municipality and is walkable, by bike and horseback, with picnic area and water point in the locality Cerratina.

  • Where Partenza dalla piazza principale, Oriolo Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it · italia.it

Monumento naturale La Mola

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Protected area of about 17 hectares established in 2022 and managed by the municipality, which takes its name from the remains of an ancient mill on the river Mignone, with a waterfall and a pond. Along the river Biscione and Mignone grow forests with a black alder tunnel; there are prairies of Agrostis linked to hydrothermal events, a beech wood at unusually low altitude and a maple of exceptional size.

  • Where Località La Mola, Oriolo Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Borgo pianificato e antica Via Clodia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The first settlement was founded by Giorgio Santacroce in 1560 according to a planned plant; the Altieri took control of the village in the seventeenth century. The ancient Via Clodia is a wide avenue with commercial activities.
