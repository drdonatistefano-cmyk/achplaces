What to see in San Maurizio d'Opaglio

Common on the south-western shore of Lake Orta, cradle of the district of the faucet and valve of the Cusio: here is the Museum of Faucet and its Technology, unique in its kind; on the lake the Oratory of San Giulio alla Fontana and the free beaches of Prarolo (Blue Flag) and Pascolo, touched by the Blue Ring around the lake.

Museo del Rubinetto e della sua Tecnologia

Museums · Themed

Museo dell'Ecomuseo del Lago d'Orta e Mottarone dedicated to the relationship between man and water: it was born from the permanent exhibition "L'uomo e l'acqua" opened on April 15, 1995. About three hundred finds of industrial archaeology tell the evolution of the tap and craft techniques, with the copy of a faucet of about 25 AD from the villa of Tiberius in Capri; it also explains the birth of the industrial district of the tap and valve in the Cusio. Upstairs totem on the latest innovations; accessible with elevator.

  • Where Piazza Martiri 1, San Maurizio d'Opaglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodelrubinetto.it

Oratorio di San Giulio alla Fontana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Oratory of the sixteenth century in a narrow valley between Opagliolo and the tip of Casario, built above the Fountain of San Giulio that flows from the rock under the porch: according to legend the saint stopped there to pray before crossing the lake. After the visit of San Carlo Borromeo on 12 June 1573 it was transformed into a church; it has roof in piods, arched portico and a single altar dedicated to San Giulio. It was a destination of pilgrimages and several times visited by Bishop Odescalchi, then Pope Innocent XI.

  • Where Via Monte Bianco 27A, loc. Opagliolo, San Maurizio d'Opaglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sanmauriziodopaglio.no.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Maurizio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish of San Maurizio was established on September 2, 1568, separating it from San Filiberto di Prorio; the small single nave church was repaired and enlarged in 1582-83 and consecrated by Bishop Cesare Spedano on September 9, 1590. The pastoral visits of 1572 and 1575 describe it with three altars, the major dedicated to St. Maurizio, and a stone baptismal font.

  • Where Piazza Martiri, San Maurizio d'Opaglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sanmauriziodopaglio.no.it

Sources: comune.sanmauriziodopaglio.no.it

Spiaggia comunale Prarolo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Free pebble and sandy beach about 150 meters long, with a large lawn and a pier, awarded with the Blue Flag FEE 2023-2024. Kiosk bar open from May to September, toilets and showers, lifeguard on weekends; always open and accessible by wheelchair.

  • Where Lungo Lago F.lli di Dio, loc. Lagna, San Maurizio d'Opaglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sanmauriziodopaglio.no.it

Spiaggia comunale Pascolo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Free beach on the lake, always open, with lawn, pier, kiosk bar open from May to September and toilets. It is one of the points touched by the Blue Ring, the tour of Lake Orta on foot and by mountain bike.

Anello Azzurro, tratto di San Maurizio

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The tour of Lake Orta (39.6 km, on foot and by mountain bike, free access, departure from the Forum of Omegna) enters the municipality from the hamlet Pascolo and runs along the coast near Punta Casario, the fountain of San Giulio and the villas Guadagnini and Castelnuovo; after a dirt climb reaches Lagna and runs along the oratory of San Rocco. Along the stretch you will find the beaches of Pascolo and Prarolo, the port of Lagna and the Roman Bridge.

  • Where Da loc. Pascolo a loc. Lagna, San Maurizio d'Opaglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sanmauriziodopaglio.no.it
