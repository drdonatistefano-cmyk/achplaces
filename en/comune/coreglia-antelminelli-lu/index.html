What to see in Coreglia Antelminelli

Mountain village of the Serchio Valley, outpost of Lucca, home of the figurines who brought the plaster statues into the world.

3 places to visit in Coreglia Antelminelli, in the province of Lucca (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Figurina di Gesso e dell'Emigrazione

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Over 800 models in plaster, the result of the secular profession of figurines: the eighteenth-century cats blackened by smoke, sacred subjects, domestic scenes and ancient crafts, and the history of emigration.

  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the IX century with works of art, including a Madonna of the Rosary painted by Pietro Sorri in 1598.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Rocca di Coreglia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The imposing fortress, probably from Romanesque times, historic residence of Castruccio Castracani, with the nearby Palazzo del Forte.
