What to see in Velo Veronese

Country of Lessinia between stone districts and pastures: in Camposilvano the Geopaleontological Museum, with admonites, dinosaur footprints and a skeleton of the cave bear, gives access to the Covolo, karst cavity more than 80 meters deep. Above the village the church of the Transfiguration on Mount Purga, at 1257 meters, looks up to the Po Valley.

Museo geopaleontologico di Camposilvano

Museums · Science and university

Founded by the knight Attilio Benetti, to whom some ammonites and fossils are dedicated, it traces the evolution of life in the geological eras: a large collection of ammonites, remains of marine reptiles of tropical seabeds that were once Lessinia, dinosaur footprints and a skeleton complete with cave bear more than 3 meters high. From 15 June to the beginning of September open every day except Monday 9.30-12.30 and 15-18; in the rest of the year Saturday, Sunday and holidays 10-12 and 14.30-16.30; closed from mid-January to mid-March.

  • Where Via Covolo 1, località Camposilvano, Velo Veronese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Covolo di Camposilvano

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Ancient large cave in the Ammonitic Red whose vault collapsed, forming an amphitheatre basin over 80 meters deep, almost entirely covered with vegetation; a steps path about 500 meters from the museum leads to a panoramic balcony on the entrance. Man's shelter for at least 50-70,000 years, used in Roman times as a natural dispensation and in the Middle Ages; many historians consider it a possible source of inspiration for Hell and Dante's Cocito. Access only from the museum, at the same times.

  • Where Via Covolo 1, località Camposilvano, Velo Veronese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Chiesa della Trasfigurazione sul Monte Purga

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built in 1854 at 1257 meters, where stood a fortress or watchtower of Roman times (there were found vases, bones and flints); a hut façade with large ogival window, in limestone of Lessinia and red marble of Verona. You can reach it on foot from the cemetery; enter only in summer or on the occasion of some celebrations, with Mass on the first Sunday of August.

  • Where Monte Purga, Velo Veronese (partenza a piedi dal cimitero)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Monumento ai Caduti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The honorary column in red marble of Verona with decorations in Carrara marble, surmounted by a bronze eagle, inaugurated on 18 June 1922 with the contribution of the families of the country; on the side towards the town hall the marble statue of Carrara Restored after the early 2000s, it is one of the symbols of the community with the oratory of Monte Purga.

  • Where Piazza della Vittoria, Velo Veronese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Anello dei tesori di Azzarino

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of 10 km, about 3 hours, hiking difficulty, from Piazzale Crosara between the districts of the ancient town of Azzarino: the stone cross of 1884 in Purga, the Gal Cross in ammonitic red at 1170 meters, the nineteenth century church of Contrada Campe, the sundial and the fresco of contrada Riva, the 'barbari volti' fifteenth century contrada Cross and the barn of Ballarin with San Giorgio and the dragon in contrada Fontani.

  • Where Piazzale Crosara, Velo Veronese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Anello di Camposilvano e malghe della Lessinia

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of 8.4 km, about 3 hours and 300 meters of difference in altitude, easy/medium, from the parking lot of Camposilvano: touches the Valle delle Sfingi, the Buse di Sotto and Buse di Sopra alpine pastures, the Cross of the North, the Lausen refuge and the Gal Cross, to return next to the Covolo and the museum.

  • Where Parcheggio all'ingresso di Camposilvano, Velo Veronese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Covoli di Velo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Complex of horizontal galleries, including the Lower Grotto and the Upper Grotto, excavated by karst in the Grey Calcars for over 500 meters, once inhabited by the cave bear. Inside concretions and forms of cave life; you can reach it with 200 meters on foot downhill from the road to the districts Tezze and Toldi.

difficult access: visits only possible for expert speleologists, others enter only for a few meters

  • Where Strada per le contrade Tezze e Toldi, Velo Veronese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it
