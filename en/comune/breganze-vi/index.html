What to see in Breganze

At the foot of the hills, the land of the Torcolato DOC, Breganze revolves around Piazza Mazzini and the 90-metre bell tower, the third of Veneto; around, a plot of six-seventh-century villas also linked to the Great War, fifteenth-century dovecotes, the ancient opifici of Maglio Tamiello and Officina Radin, the Museo Moto Laverda and the Colle di Santa Lucia with the panorama of the plain.

Piazza Mazzini e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Described by the city guide as one of the most beautiful squares in Veneto, it is closed on the east side by eighteenth-century buildings and dominated by the bell tower; there are buildings from the sixteenth to the nineteenth century. On the square stands Palazzo Brogliati, built in the seventeenth century, renovated in 1748 and donated to the Municipality by Giovanni Brogliati in 1834, now Town Hall, and the sixteenth century Palazzo Aviani, restored at the beginning of the eighteenth century by the Fioravanzo. From here start almost all the walking routes of the municipality.

Duomo di Santa Maria Assunta e campanile

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Of thirteenth-century origins, it was rebuilt in 1840 after the collapse of the old bell tower (1838) and completed in the present form in 1930; inside seven altars dedicated to the saints and one to the Madonna, a relic of Blessed Bartholomew, an eighteenth-century altarpiece and the frescoes of Mattielli (1929-1939) on the ceiling and presbytery. The bell tower, begun in 1842 on a project by the Venetian Antonio Diedo and completed in 1893, is 90 meters high, the third of Veneto, and hosts 16 bells (the largest weighs 2.830 kg). The summit, with 360 degrees view, is accessible during the Prima del Torcolato.

Antico Maglio Tamiello

Museums · Themed

The forge of the sixteenth century operated by the water of the Roggia di Breganze through a wheel, still working: the hammer called "head of donkey" is the fulcrum of the ethnographic museum, and the last blacksmith was Angelo Giusto Tamiello. The city guide defines it as one of the best preserved in Europe and a rare example of Vicenza protoindustry of the sixteenth century. Visit by appointment; it is the destination of the Trail of Maglio and the route of the ancient opifici.

Antica Officina Radin, Museo dell'artigianato breganzese

Museums · Themed

Artisan workshop of the early twentieth century, today a museum of local crafts supported by the Gruppo Ricerca Storica di Breganze. It is located along the path of the ancient opifici that leads from via Castelletto to Maglio Tamiello. Visits by reservation, lasting about one hour.

Colle di Santa Lucia e Sentiero dei Degani

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The hill (222 m) was the seat of the first fortified town and of the medieval village, with finds of the Bronze Age; a staircase of a hundred steps rises to the sacellus of Blessed Bartholomew and to a large cross, erected as a vow at the end of the Second World War. The Degani-Costa Path (about 4 km, only on foot) starts from Piazza Mazzini and touches Casino Brogliati, a seventeenth-century residence among olive groves, and Torre Randi, probably at the end of the fifteenth century; in the surroundings several wineries of DOC Breganze.

Colli di Riva e chiesetta di Santo Stefano

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

An easy walk of about 5.5 km from Piazza Mazzini on the eastern hills, among historic villas and vineyards, up to the small church of Santo Stefano (XIII-XIV centuries), a building of Benedictine origin with neo-Gothic forms and characteristic sailing bell tower, no longer officiated and visible only from the outside.

Ville di Breganze e la Grande Guerra

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

A path of about 4 km from the centre touches the villas of the village, almost all private and visible only from the outside: Villa Diedo (Sixth century) with the chapel of the Annunziata consecrated in 1684, the seventeenth-century complex of Villa Mascarello-Noventa with walls, barchesse and oratory, Villa Arrigoni-Poli, where on June 4, 1916 Cadorna and Vittorio Emanuele III, Villa Scaroni and Villa Chiericati, headquarters of the command of the VI Armata in 1918 and 1917, and the sixteenth-century Villa Monte (Ca' Ostile), prototype of a Venetian villa in barchessa.

Villa Malvezzi ex Laverda e antica ghiacciaia

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The only Liberty-style villa in Breganze, with a corner tower, built at the beginning of the twentieth century and the headquarters of the command of the Genio in the Great War; today it houses the Astico Mountain Union. In the garden, the ancient 19th century icebox, semi-basement and brick, restored and open for events such as the San Martino Fair.

Antico Torrione

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Medieval fortification of the second half of the thirteenth century, renovated in 1938, with high-pitched windows and frescoes from the early fifteenth century; today it houses a community of Orsoline Sisters. From Torrione starts a route of about 9 km towards the valley of Chiavone Bianco.

Colombara Pagello-Monza (Miotti)

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

15th-century Colombara, owned by the municipality, with the coats of arms of Monza (lion) and Pagello (lince) and frescoes on the four sides, partially restored. It is one of the 11 remaining doves of the 18 counted at the beginning of the twentieth century, including the Colombara della Cuca (XV century) and that of San Gaetano (Sixth century).

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Erected in 1760 by Alvise and Pietro Monza on a design by Ottone Calderari, it has neoclassical façade; the interior fresco reproduces a work by Giambattista Tiepolo today at the Civic Museums of Vicenza.

Torcolato e Strada dei vini DOC Breganze

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Torcolato, sweet DOC wine made from grapes dried waspaiola for about four months, is produced only in the area of Breganze. In January, the Prima del Torcolato brings the first pressing with guided tours and tastings to the square; from May to September Open Wineries, to August the Wine Nights in Piazza Mazzini and in November the San Martino Fair with torresani on the spit.
