What to see in Imperia

Two cities in one on the Riviera dei Fiori: Oneglia, with Calata Cuneo, the porticoes of Piazza Dante, the Museo dell'Olivo and Villa Grock, and Porto Maurizio, with Parasio, the great Cathedral of San Maurizio, the Loggia of Santa Chiara and the Naval Museum on the sea.

14 places to visit in Imperia, in the province of Imperia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa Grock – Museo del Clown

Museums · House museums · Viewpoint

The house of Charles Adrien Wettach, in Grock art, born as Villa Bianca and transformed with the garden; after the death of the clown in 1954 decayed, it was acquired by the Province and restored. The park has columns, exotic trees and Masonic symbols; inside the Clown Museum with interactive settings.

  • Where Via Fanny Roncati Carli, Imperia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it
