What to see in Alcamo

Trapanese City at the foot of Monte Bonifato: in Piazza della Repubblica the fourteenth-century Castle of the Counts of Modica, with the regional Enoteca and the Museum of Popular Traditions; along Corso VI April the Baroque Mother Church with the frescoes of Guglielmo Borremans and the works of the Gagini, the Museum of Sacred Art and the Church of Saints Paul and Bartholomew; at the top of Bonifato the Reserve of the Bosco d'Alcamo.

Castello dei Conti di Modica

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Built probably between 1340 and 1350 by Enrico and Federico Chiaramonte, it is named after the Counts of Modica who owned it from 1410 to 1812; it resisted also the assault of the corsair Barbarossa and in the nineteenth century passed to the Municipality. Rhomboidal plant with four alternating towers, two cylindrical and two square, double and Gothic triple windows in the courtyard and staircase in red marble of Monte Bonifato. It hosts the Regional Enoteca of Western Sicily, with tastings, and the Museum of Popular Traditions, which reconstructs rural interiors with tools, tools and costumes and documents the production of wine. It overlooks Piazza della Repubblica, one of the city's largest green spaces. Open Tuesday-Sunday 9:30-12:30 and 15:30-18:30, closed on Monday.

Basilica di Santa Maria Assunta (Chiesa Madre)

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

It was built around 1332 in Gothic-Catalan forms and preserves from that time the bell tower with two-fold windows and the chapel of the Holy Spirit (1430) with the reliquary of the Sacred Spina (1636). The current Baroque church of 1669, is the work of Angelo Italia and Giuseppe Diamante, with three naves divided by monolithic columns of red marble; the facade is of 1786 (Emanuele Cardona). Between 1735 and 1737, the Flemish Guglielmo Borremans frescoed the vault with 38 scenes; he preserves the Crucifix (1523), the marble triptych (1519) and the "Transito della Vergine" (1529) by Antonello Gagini, the Last Supper by Giuseppe Carrera (1614) and the statue of the Madonna dei Miracoli by Lorenzo Curti (1721), carried in a procession on June 21. Basilica since 1969; in 1995 Paolo Portoghesi designed the chapel of Don Giuseppe Rizzo.

Chiesa dei Santi Paolo e Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Born from the vow of the priest Adam Morfino (1533) to found a hospital with chapel, it became parish in 1615 and was rebuilt between 1692 and 1705; the facade is of 1782. A Latin cross with three aisles and monolithic columns of red marble of Bonifato, has stuccoes of Vincenzo and Gabriele Messina of Serpotti's school, frescoes of Antonio Grano and paintings of Giuseppe Felice (1701-1703). It preserves the "Madonna del Miele" (Madonna of Honey), painted in about 1300, the oldest of Alcamo, coming from the first mother church of Santa Maria della Stella.

Monte Bonifato e Riserva naturale Bosco d'Alcamo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

==References====External links==* Official website At the top of the mountain is the Bosco d'Alcamo Nature Reserve, established in 1984 on 280 hectares, with Mediterranean maquis, holm oaks, oaks and conifers of reforestation; at the top is the Sanctuary of Our Lady of the High. Near one of the springs is the Funtanazza, a rectangular medieval reservoir of about 17 by 8 meters, which contained about 1,200 cubic meters of water. The presence of stairs, not accessible to people with disabilities.
