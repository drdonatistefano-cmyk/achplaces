What to see in Cervarese Santa Croce

Comune Padovano sul Bacchiglione at the foot of the Euganean Hills, with the Castle of San Martino della Vaneza, whose tower dates back to the 11th century and which houses the river museum, and the Oratory of the Holy Cross, a small church of Lombard origin already documented in 874.

Castello di San Martino della Vaneza e Museo del fiume Bacchiglione

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The tower, part of the oldest, dates back to the 11th century and was raised around 1385 by Francesco il Vecchio da Carrara. It is located in the middle of the river Bacchiglione, on the border between Padua and Vicenza. In 1324 Padua donated it to the Carraresi; then passed to Venice and to the Vendramin, who transformed it into a villa (according to tradition there was guest Giorgione). Recovered in the eighties, it houses an exhibition space on the river Bacchiglione with finds from the late Neolithic to the modern age. Guided visits: tel. 351 8401432.

Oratorio della Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small church built in the Lombard period, probably on a Roman cemetery area, already owned by the bishop of Padua in 874. Rectangular plant with semicircular apse and a single nave with wooden trusses; it was parish until 1872. The next tower, of Carolingian origin, became bell tower; the municipal restoration ended in 1984.

Villa Trento

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

16th century residence built around 1575 by Count Ottavio Trento, with Palladian-inspired façade; next to it is the Colombian tower, the only rest of a previous Benedictine rural complex.

Argini del Bacchiglione

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The river returned in 1983 a monoxyl pirogue about 6.5 meters long; from the banks you can see the Berici and Euganean Hills. The old nearby mill, today in the territory of Veggiano, dates back to the 11th century.
