What to see in Cerva

Country of chestnut woods in Catanzarese: the municipality works on the recovery and enhancement of centuries-old chestnut trees, linked to a long peasant tradition, and the Museum of chestnut and rural art.

2 places to visit in Cerva, in the province of Catanzaro (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della castagna e dell'arte contadina

Museums · Themed

The museum tells the local chestnut culture with a three-section itinerary: the first dedicated to the history of chestnut and the tools used for processing, the second with the reconstruction of the interiors of mountain houses, with furnishings and household objects, the third didactic-scientific cutting with paper and computer archives.

the Municipality presents it as a project in phase of realization and the MiC card indicates closed every day: opening to verify

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cerva.cz.it

Castagneto secolare di località Melitani

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

A centuries-old plant of chestnut trees with imposing trees, one of which is called "Castagna del cielo" (Castagna del cielo) and the Municipality is responsible for its recovery, protection and valorization.

  • Where Località Melitani, Cerva
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cerva.cz.it
