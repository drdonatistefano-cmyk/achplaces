What to see in Palaia

Medieval village of Valdera, one of the richest in history in the area, with the Parish Church of San Martino of 1279, the church of Sant'Andrea and the ancient door became clock tower; around Montefoscoli with the Museum of Rural Civilization and the Temple of Minerva Medica, the ghost villages of Villa Saletta and Toiano, over 65 km of paths and the hills of white truffle.

Borgo di Palaia e Torre dell'orologio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The origins date back to the fourth century BC, as suggested by the Etruscan finds of the area; the castle, the Mons Magnifridum mentioned already in 986, was contested by Pisa, Florence and Lucca and in 1435 resisted the siege of Niccolò Piccinino, from which the said At the centre of the village the ancient civic gate called Arco del Podestà, with Ghibelline blackbirds, which became Torre delle Ore in 1655, and the three-storey town hall on Piazza della Repubblica. During the tourist season, the Tourist Office organizes a walk through Palaia, a free guided tour of about two hours on Tuesday morning, in Italian and English, which ends with the view from the highest point of the country.

Pieve di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1279, during the prosperity of the village, it rises just outside the walls: a large building, mainly in terracotta, with stone facade and Romanesque and Gothic elements. It is very popular for weddings and choral concerts.

  • Where Fuori le mura, Palaia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Chiesa di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the first half of the thirteenth century, which according to Visit Tuscany rises on a Roman temple dedicated to Saturn. It houses works of Tuscan school: on the main altar a Crucifix painted on wood attributed to Andrea Pisano and a Madonna with Child by Francesco di Valdambrino.

  • Where Centro storico, Palaia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

San Gervasio is the ancient castle of Valdera; the parish church was built in the 11th century near the walls of the castle and has been recently restored. The village is the stop of the network of paths of Palaia.

Museo della Civiltà Contadina e Casa Museo Vaccà Berlinghieri

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Opened in 1996 in the rural buildings of the Palazzo Vaccà Berlinghieri and run by a local association, exhibits tools donated by the inhabitants along a path between warehouses and cellars dedicated to the sharecropping from the eighteenth to the first twentieth century, from harvest to sowing, harvest and crushing, with an underground reconstruction of the life of the farm. The Casa Museo, opened in 2005, recalls the surgeon Andrea Vaccà of the University of Pisa, doctor of Paolina Bonaparte, with surgical instruments and a library of medical treatises linked to the Pisan years of Leopardi, Byron and Shelley. In summer open on Sunday 10:30-12:30 and 15:30-19:30, other days upon reservation; free entry and guided tours.

  • Where Via Vaccà 49, Montefoscoli, Palaia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museociviltacontadinamontefoscoli.it

Sources: museociviltacontadinamontefoscoli.it · terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com · cultura.gov.it

Tempio di Minerva Medica

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Neoclassical temple built in 1823 by the Vaccà Berlinghieri family in a dense forest near Montefoscoli, a unique building linked to stories between science, esotericism and Freemasonry. Villa Saletta

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Viewpoint

Ancient castle walled today ghost village, built as a village-farm between the sixteenth and eighteenth centuries: remains of the castle, a row of abandoned buildings along the main street and a clock tower with the coats of arms of the owners on the large courtyard, which in summer hosts a festival of popular theatre. The night of St. Lawrence, Good Morning Babylon and Flower of the Taviani brothers and Napoleon of Paolo Virzì.

Sentieri ad anello di Palaia

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Over 65 km of dirt roads and marked paths on the Valdera hills, organized in rings with departure and arrival in the same spot, to walk, by bike or on horseback; touch the villages of Alica, San Gervasio and Colleoli. The best known is the Great Nature Path; the tracks are on the profile Wikiloc .

  • Where Partenza da Palaia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Tartufo bianco e Strada del Vino delle Colline Pisane

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

The white truffle (Tuber Magnatum Pico) grows on the hills of Palaia, which matures from September to December and to which the first card of identity has been dedicated here; in Forcoli, an ancient castle, every October for over thirty years the Exhibition of Truffle and Porcino is held. The Wine Route of the Pisan Hills starts from Palaia and crosses the Valdera between farms and historical estates with tastings and visits to the cellar.

  • Where Palaia e Forcoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com
