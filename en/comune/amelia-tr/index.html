What to see in Amelia

Pre-Roman city on the Amerina street, surrounded by polygonal walls with six doors: the Roman cistern under Piazza Matteotti, the museum with the bronze of Germanico in the former convent of San Francesco, the Duomo with the dodecagonal tower and the noble palaces of the Five and seventeenth centuries.

Mura poligonali e Porta Romana

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The walls in large blocks of stone laid without mortar date back to the IV-III century BC and are covered by an urban trek signposted; of the six doors four are in use. Roman gate, the main entrance, has the sixteenth-century appearance in travertine with brick fallway and the vault frescoed with the city coat of arms.

  • Where Porta Romana, Amelia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Museo civico archeologico e Pinacoteca «Edilberto Rosa»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the former convent of San Francesco: italic, Roman and late antique artifacts, including the bronze statue of Germanic of the first century AD, and in the picture gallery painted from the fifteenth to the eighteenth century with the Sant'Antonio Abate of Piermatteo d'Amelia (1474 circa).

  • Where Piazza Augusto Vera 10, Amelia
  • Entry Single ticket with Cisterna and Palazzo Petrignani: €7 full, €5 reduced, €2 from 6 to 18 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: ameliamusei.it · umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Cisterna romana

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Built in the first century B.C. under the hole, it is about 57 meters long: ten parallel rooms with barrel vaults carved into the rock; in the last room the lead fistulae that fed it.

  • Where Piazza Giacomo Matteotti 75, Amelia
  • Entry In the single ticket (€7 full, €5 reduced)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: italia.it · ameliamusei.it
