What to see in Palombara Sabina

Borgo gathered around the Savelli Torlonia Castle, among olive trees and cherry trees at the foot of Monte Gennaro in the Parco dei Monti Lucretili, about 40 km from Rome; in the surroundings the abbey of San Giovanni in Argentella, the abandoned village of Stazzano Vecchio, the castrum of Castiglione and the Baths of Cretone.

Castello Savelli Torlonia e Muro del Soccorso

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Medieval castle on top of the hill, with more than one hundred rooms on about 10,000 square meters; the tower was born as a bulwark at the end of the ninth century and at the end of the fifteenth century the Savelli transformed it into a Renaissance baronial residence. Inside the Palazzo di Troilo, the Palatium degli Ottaviani (X-XI century), the Palazzo di Giacomo, three courtyards, a chapel and four towers, with sixteenth-century frescoes from the Raphael school wanted by Troilo Savelli. From the fortress starts the Wall of the Rescue, covered walk of 83 meters with 37 wounds, escape route in case of siege, accessible in full. Not indicated times: check on the site of the Municipality.

Centro storico e chiesa di San Biagio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The historical centre develops with a concentric scheme on the top of the hill around the baronial palace, built in the 11th century when the counts of Palombara held the lordship until the mid-Twentieth century. The church of San Biagio was built in the 11th century on the site of a small older church.

Abbazia di San Giovanni in Argentella

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

About 3 km from the village, in a valley sheltered by the Cornicolani Mountains: probably Benedictine abbey, cited from the 10th century, entrusted to the Guglielmites in 1286 and abandoned by the monks in 1445; remains the church, national monument since 1900. Interior with three naves on columns, iconostasis of 1170 of the Marmorario Centurius, high medieval ciborium with Carolingian weaving stuccoes, fragments of frescoes and Roman materials of reuse. It takes its name from the spring flowing through the crypt, whose water was considered miraculous: on June 24th, the feast of St. John the Baptist, we bathed there.

Stazzano Vecchio

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ghost village halfway between Palombara Sabina and Moricone, grew around a castle of 1600, with a single access between the most external tower and the church of Santa Maria. It was abandoned after the earthquake of 1901 and its remains were used to build Stazzano Nuovo; from the limestone spur you can see Mount Gennaro and Soratte. Freely accessible.

  • Where Stazzano Vecchio, tra Palombara Sabina e Moricone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Monte Gennaro e Parco dei Monti Lucretili

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The regional park, established in 1989, covers about 18,000 hectares between the valleys of Tiber and Aniene; the main peaks are the Pellcchia (1,368 m) and the Gennaro (1,271 m). From Palombara Sabina starts the classic ascent to Monte Gennaro: difficulty excursion E, about 900 m of difference in altitude and 6 hours, between holm oaks, storace and Judas tree to the beech forest; from the summit the 360 degree panorama embraces Soratte, Rome, Roman Castles, Circeo and the nearest Pontine islands.

  • Where Partenza da Palombara Sabina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parchilazio.it · italia.it
