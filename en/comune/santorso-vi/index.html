What to see in Santorso

At the foot of Mount Summano, Santorso preserves the neoclassical sanctuary of Sant'Orso designed by Ottone Calderari and the environments designed and inhabited by Alessandro Rossi: the nineteenth-century villa with the romantic garden and the model farm; in the center and on the slopes of the mountain, numerous fountains.

3 places to visit in Santorso, in the province of Vicenza (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa del Santo (Santuario di Sant'Orso)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Sanctuary dedicated to Sant'Orso, patron saint of the village, and to the Madonna del Monte Summano, is the building of most architectural interest among the churches of Santorso. Designed by Ottone Calderari in 1777 and built in the early nineteenth century in neoclassical style, it houses interior decorations and paintings by Alessandro and Gian Battista Maganza.

  • Where Via del Santuario, Santorso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page venetoaroundme.regione.veneto.it

Parco Rossi e Fattoria Rossi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

A set of historical environments designed and inhabited by Alessandro Rossi: a nineteenth-century villa, the park and a model farm. The Rossi Park is a romantic garden at the foot of Mount Summano.

  • Where Santorso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page venetoaroundme.regione.veneto.it

Sources: venetoaroundme.regione.veneto.it
