What to see in Controguerra

Borgo della Val Vibrata among hills and olive groves, Città dell'Olio and land of DOC and DOCG wines celebrated by the Wine Festival. The historic centre is dominated by the brick tower of 1370, the only trace of the castle of Corata, next to the Doge's Palace; outside the walls the church of the Madonna delle Grazie and the chapel of the Icon, and in Via San Giuseppe the Museum of peasant civilization in Val Vibrata.

Torrione e centro storico

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The brick tower, built in 1370, dominates the centre from the top of a steep hill and controls the valley; it is the only trace of the castle of Corata, whose massive walls opened with Porta Maggiore or Porta dell'Angelo. The route along the ancient walls of the old town, with a medieval atmosphere, arrives at the Torrione, from which you can enjoy the view of the Vibrata Valley; from the benches of the village you can see the teraman coast and the Laga Mountains.

  • Where Centro storico, Controguerra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: abruzzoturismo.it · italia.it

Palazzo Ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Connected to the tower, it was built in 1279 on the remains of Roman buildings and testifies to the dominion of Acquaviva, dukes of Atri, from the mid-15th century. In a hall of the Town Hall are exhibited some archaeological finds of the territory, others are preserved at the Museo Pigorini of Rome and the Florentine Museum of Prehistory.

  • Where Centro storico, Controguerra
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the XVI century just outside the walls, with a golden wooden altar and painted in late Baroque style that has in the center a terracotta altarpiece of the Madonna with Child, work of a pupil of Silvestro dell'Aquila.

  • Where Fuori dalle mura, Controguerra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: abruzzoturismo.it · italia.it

Museo della civiltà contadina in Val Vibrata

Museums · Themed

Collection of objects and documents ordered by monographic sectors: the cycle of hemp, wheat and wine, bachiculture and hairdressers; the most complete section is that on hemp, with photos and captions from sowing to finished products. It is located at a winery and agritourism; the MiC card indicates opening every day 9-19.

  • Where Via San Giuseppe 26, Controguerra
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.controguerra.te.it · italia.it

Colline del vino e Percorso del Vino

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

The territory is a landscape of hills and olive groves that descend towards the coast; the village is part of the Città dell'Olio and produces DOC and DOCG wines to be tasted in the local farms. The Wine Route, about 30 km between hills and tastings, starts from Controguerra and reaches Torano Nuovo, Ancarano, Corropoli and Colonnella; in summer the Wine Festival and the Wine Bike animate the historic center and vineyards.

  • Where Controguerra (TE)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: abruzzoturismo.it · italia.it
