What to see in Netro

Country of the Elvo Valley where, along the river Ingagna, from the end of the sixteenth century the first iron workshops arose; in the area the industry grew more than elsewhere. In the premises of the former Rubino factory, founded in 1896, the Documentation Centre of the Ecomuseum of Biellese tells about this tradition.

Centro di documentazione sulla lavorazione del ferro (Officine di Netro)

Museums · Themed

Cell of the Ecomuseum of Biellese opened in 1996 in the buildings of the former Rubino factory, founded in 1896 by Giovan Battista Rubino with his sons Ernesto and Colombo (now Bono Netro). It tells the story of the forge born in the seventeenth century in small workshops, the "boite" along the river Ingagna: tools produced by Rubino and samples, over a thousand drawings, an archive of documents from 1901 to 1964, technical standards for car and motorbike components and an exhibition of historical machinery. Guided tours, workshops and excursions by reservation; collaborate with Fucina Morino in Mongrando. Ecomuseum Contacts: +39 349 3269048.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta antica (chiesa del cimitero)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church, already parish, that the catalogue of the Ministry dates back to the first half of the 11th century; bound since 1908, is owned by the Municipality and was extensively restored in 1985. The façade in pebbles with fishbone preserves the primitive forms, simple and harmonious, despite repeated interventions; inside a cycle of frescoes of the first fifteenth century attributed to Pseudo Domenico della Marca d'Ancona.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
