What to see in Roccavione

At the entrance of the Vermenagna Valley, Roccavione has a historic centre at the foot of the cliff of the Santo Sudario, inhabited since the Bronze Age, with the Confraternity of Santa Croce and votive chapels from the 16th to the 18th century; also worth seeing is the Ethnographic Museum Vecchio Mulino and two monumental sequoias.

Museo etnografico Vecchio Mulino

Museums · Themed

Museum in three sections: the mill, with three millstones and the machinery to produce flour; the ethnographic section, with the reconstruction of the shoemaker's workshop and the old crafts; a section of natural sciences. Open only by reservation, to be sent at least 15 days before (tel. +39 0171 767108).

  • Where Via Molino 11, Roccavione
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Chiesa della Confraternita di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Indicated as the most valuable artistic element of the country, it has a Greek cross plan; documented from the end of the sixteenth century, it was completely rebuilt shortly after the mid eighteenth century.

  • Where Centro storico, Roccavione
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Centro storico e cappelle votive

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Small streets and lanes at the foot of the cliff of the Holy Sudarium, in a place where archaeological research indicates Celtic-ligur settlements since the Bronze Age. In the center and surroundings there are votive chapels from the 16th to the 18th century, including those of the Assumption and of the Saints Rocco and Biagio, and Liberty villas of the early decades of the 20th century.

  • Where Centro storico, Roccavione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Sequoie monumentali

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

In the park of the ancient villa of the Counts Salazar, destroyed during the war, two redwoods of about one hundred years recorded among the monumental trees Italian dominate firs and cedars: the highest measures about 50 meters, the other has a circumference at the base of 10,5 meters. In the park there is also a large araucaria and two rare specimens of Thujopsis dolabrata; in the area stands Casa Auxilium, of the Salesian religious.

  • Where Zona collinare verso Robilante, Roccavione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.roccavione.cn.it · visitcuneese.it
