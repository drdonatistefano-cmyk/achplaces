What to see in Busso

Molise village near Campobasso with the parish church of San Lorenzo Martire next to the former ducal palace and, on Mount Vairano towards Baranello, the walls of a city of the Sanniti Pentri.

2 places to visit in Busso, in the province of Campobasso (Molise). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di San Lorenzo Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Patron parish church, perhaps from the sixteenth century: a plaque on the portal bore the date 1533, probable restoration or extension, destroyed by the earthquake of 1805; restored until at least 1822, rebuilt in the roof after the collapse of 1874. Facade with a hut with bell tower on the left, a single nave of 30 m with four marble altars; it is located next to the former ducal palace, today of the family Venditti.

  • Where Via Armando Diaz 2, Busso
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: comune.busso.cb.it · visitmolise.eu

Parco archeologico-naturalistico di Monte Vairano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Between Busso and Baranello, one of the richest centers of the Sanniti Pentri: a stone belt of about 3 km contains about 50 hectares and the peaks of 968 and 997 m, with three doors visible to oblique corridor (two excavated). The excavations have found homes, including the LN house at the south gate, and a circular cistern about 6 m deep.

  • Where Monte Vairano, Busso
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: comune.baranello.cb.it · visitmolise.eu
