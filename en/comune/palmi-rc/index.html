What to see in Palmi

The center of Costa Viola, under Mount Sant'Elia: the beach of Tonnara with the Scoglio dell'Ulivo, the archaeological park of the Taurians and the museums of the House of Culture Leonida Repaci; at the end of August the Varia, an immaterial UNESCO heritage.

8 places to visit in Palmi, in the province of Reggio Calabria (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Palmi in the guide with map and filters

Spiaggia della Tonnara e Scoglio dell'Ulivo

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Beach of almost 2 km of gravel and clear sand under Mount Sant'Elia, with beaches and free stretches and waters suitable for snorkeling and diving; in front, accessible by swimming, the rock with a spontaneous olive tree of over 400 years, symbol of the coast.

  • Where Tonnara, Palmi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Monte Sant'Elia

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Promontory of Aspromonte with the shape of a crouched lion, reachable on foot; from the viewpoint of the Tre Croci the view reaches Sicily and the Aeolian Islands.

  • Where Palmi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Parco Archeologico dei Tauriani

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On a plateau 70 meters above sea level, frequented by the Neolithic: villages of the Bronze Age, the Italian and then Roman city of Tauriana with the mosaic house, the sanctuary, the building for shows, a basal road and the crypt of San Fantino, late antique nymph with medieval frescoes.

  • Where Località Taureana, Palmi
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Casa della Cultura Leonida Repaci

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Museum Polo inaugurated in 1960: the Pinacoteca donated by Leonida and Albertina Repaci with works among others by Guercino, Manet, Corot, Fattori and Guttuso, the Gipsoteca by Michele Guerrisi, the Museum of ethnography and folklore "Raffaele Corso" , the Museum dedicated to Francesco Chilea and Nicola Antonio Manfroce and the Antiquarium with the artifacts of Tauriana.

  • Where Via Felice Battaglia, Palmi
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Grotte marine della Costa Viola

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

The Sirens Cave, the Arcudace Cave and the Perciata Cave, to be reached by boat along the coast.

  • Where Palmi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Sentiero del Tracciolino

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Panoramic route between Palmi and Bagnara Calabra at about 300 meters above sea level, between promontories and Mediterranean maquis, with views of the Costa Viola.

  • Where Palmi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

La Guardiola – Villa Repaci

Nature and landscape · Landscapes

Balcony overlooking the Tyrrhenian Sea in front of the Aeolians, in a park of about five hectares around the house of the Repaci family, left by the writer to the city.

Type of visit not indicated by the source: ask the Municipality

  • Where Via Roma 93, Palmi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it
