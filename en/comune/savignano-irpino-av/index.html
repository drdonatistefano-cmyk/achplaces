What to see in Savignano Irpino

Irpino village dominated by the Castle of Guevara, with the sixteenth-century Grand Gate, the Mother Church, the ancient Via dei Finestroni and the viewpoint of the Tombola; in the woods the sulphuric source of the Refieto and the tratturo of transhumance.

Castello di Guevara

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Longobard fortification (VII-VIII century) transformed into a fortress by the Normans in the 12th century and then into a noble palace. The 1990 restoration revealed a layer of ash from the eruption of Vesuvius in 1944, portals, stairs, ovens, a well and a basement perhaps used as a prison.

Centro storico, Porta Grande e via dei Finestroni

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The Porta Grande, an ancient sixteenth-century access on the south side of the walls, separates the districts under the Windows and the Courtyard; the Via dei Finestroni, one of the oldest in the village, has lava stone flooring and arched houses. In the village also Palazzo Orsini, seat of the town hall, built in 1727 by Cardinal Orsini, then Pope Benedict XIII.

Chiesa Madre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the 16th century above a medieval church, it has three aisles and five chapels (Sant'Anna, recently restored, Sacred Heart, Addolorata, Blessed Sacrament and St. Joseph), high altar of 1738 and baptistery of 1514.

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

In the district of Licese, at the foot of Mount Sant'Angelo, in the wood of Savignano of 12 hectares; its waters are known for its healing properties.
