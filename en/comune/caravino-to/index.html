What to see in Caravino

Country of the Canavese at the foot of the Serra d'Ivrea, dominated by the Castle of Masino, residence for over nine centuries of the Valperga and today well of the FAI, with park, 18th century labyrinth and museum of carriages.

2 places to visit in Caravino, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello e Parco di Masino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Millennial castle on a hill of the Serra morainica d'Ivrea, held for over nine centuries by the Valperga, which transformed it from a fortress into a noble residence and summer villa; the FAI bought it in 1988. Frescoed halls, ambassadors' apartments, library of over 25,000 volumes and Savoy Hall with views of 22 Sabaude cities, reopened in 2022. Outside, Italian and French gardens, romantic English park and an eighteenth-century labyrinth of about a thousand hornbeams; café on the Oleandri Terrace. In 1578 there was guest San Carlo Borromeo. Tickets online on ticket.fondoambiente.it.

Museo delle Carrozze

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Twelve carriages of the Valperga di Masino family, mostly nineteenth-century: coupé, vis-à-vis, gala carriage and a wheelchair for children, with accessories and fabrics of coating, works by the Turinese builders Goggia, Cesare Sala and Giuseppe Panigoni. Opens on selected dates.

  • Where Via al Castello 1, Caravino
  • Entry €7 (included in the Castle ticket)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fondoambiente.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · fondoambiente.it
