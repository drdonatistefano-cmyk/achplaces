What to see in Lenola

A village between the Ausoni and Aurunci Mountains Park, 129 km from Rome, known for the Basilica Sanctuary of the Madonna del Colle, consecrated in 1610, reached by the Stairway of Peace mosaic; around, the Monte Appiolo and the Natural Monument Cima del Monte - Acquaviva - Quercia del Monaco.

Basilica Santuario della Madonna del Colle

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill at 490 m, among secular trees, the sanctuary was born from the conversion of the young Lenole Gabriele Mattei, who according to tradition on 15 September 1602 found among the thorns and thorns a early Christian image of the Virgin with the Child, among the oldest in Europe, and collected alms throughout Europe to build around the church, consecrated on 8 September 1610 and elevated to the Papal Basilica in 2015. The interior with a single nave preserves the eighteenth-century painting of the Virgin attributed to the architect Ambrini; in the churchyard there is the Mosaic of the Emigrants and from the travertine of the façade grow for more than four centuries the cypresses of the "miracle of the Cypresses." Open every day 8-18:30, in summer 8-20:30.

  • Where Piazzale Santuario, Lenola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Scalinata della Pace

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

110 steps decorated with mosaics on the theme of peace, made by the French master Bruno Amman on sketches of international artists and completed in 1987 respecting the structure of 1925; the pavement depicts childhood games such as rope and ball and in the square a sculpture in the rock of Peppino Quinto shows children playing under the gaze of a mother who is nursing.

  • Where Lenola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · comune.lenola.lt.it

Centro storico medievale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Houses leaning along narrow alleys that go up towards the area of the ancient Baronial Palace, today disappeared; around the village remain walls, water pipes and rustic villas of Roman age. Along the streets you will find the works of the local sculptor Andrea Gandini, who carves the folds of the tree trunks, including a trunk depicting Lenola with its perched houses.

  • Where Centro storico, Lenola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Monte Appiolo

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The peak is almost 1000 meters in the Aurunci Mountains Park, which the old shepherds call ... the Cima della Croce for a cross that once stood there; the view extends over the village, the Piana di Fondi, the Pontine Islands and the Abruzzo mountains.

  • Where Parco dei Monti Aurunci, Lenola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Monumento naturale Cima del Monte - Acquaviva - Quercia del Monaco

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the Monti Ausoni and Lago di Fondi Natural Park, wood with spontaneous orchids and archaeological remains, land of brigands at the time of the unification of Italy, with views of the Ausoni Mountains, the Fondi Lake and the Circeo; it is crossed by the ring CAI 540 Oak of Monaco - Cima del Monte - Acquaviva.

  • Where Quercia del Monaco, Lenola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it

Festa della Madonna del Colle

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

From 5th to 15th September, the feast attracts thousands of faithful: on September 5th, after the noon procession, the ...Calo del Quadro will exhibit the Virgin at the entrance of the village and on September 15th at 8pm an artistic procession with torches culminates with the illuminated bell tower; on August 16th San Rocco is celebrated.

  • Where Lenola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
