What to see in Alghero

The Catalan city of the north-west, between the park of Porto Conte and Capo Caccia, with the nuragic complex of Palmavera and the UNESCO necropolis of Anghelu Ruju.

Nuraghe Palmavera

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

In the park of Porto Conte, central tower of the XV-XIV century BC, second tower of the IX century, antemural and village with the large hut of meetings and the seat of the head.

  • Where Porto Conte, Alghero
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Necropoli di Anghelu Ruju

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage

38 tombs dug in the sandstone between 3200 and 2800 BC, discovered in 1903, in the UNESCO heritage of the domus de janas.

  • Where Alghero
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Villaggio di Sant'Imbenia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the bay of Porto Conte, a nuragic settlement dating back to the 14th century B.C. became one of the oldest commercial ports on the island, with the nearby Roman villa of the 1st century B.C.

  • Where Porto Conte, Alghero
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Capo Caccia – Isola Piana

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The protected area in the territory of Alghero which includes the bay of Porto Conte between the promontory of Capo Caccia and Punta Giglio.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
