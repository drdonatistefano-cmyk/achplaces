What to see in Salò

A small town on the Gulf of Garda, with a pedestrian promenade of about 3 km, the late Dogotic Cathedral of Santa Maria Annunziata with works by Moretto, Romanino and Paolo Veneziano, the Palazzo della Magnifica Patria and the MuSa museum in the complex of Santa Giustina.

8 places to visit in Salò, in the province of Brescia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di Santa Maria Annunziata

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Late-Gothic, designed by Filippo delle Vacche with first stone laid on October 7, 1453 on the site of a pagan temple and the parish church of Santa Maria. It preserves the polyptych of Paolo Veneziano, two paintings by Romanino, works by Moretto, Zenone Veronese, Palma il Giovane, Andrea Celesti and Angelo Landi, the chapel of the Most Holy decorated by Malosso and an ancient wooden crucifix by Giovanni Teutonico. In July, the concerts of the Summer Musicale are held on the churchyard.

  • Where Via Canottieri 2, Salò
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Lungolago Zanardelli

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fully pedestrian walk of about 3 km along the gulf, born from the reconstruction after the earthquake of 1901 that destroyed the houses overlooking the lake and inaugurated in 1906; it connects the historical center, the square of the Town Hall and the pedestrian walkway, with views of the gulf in every season.

  • Where Lungolago Zanardelli, Salò
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Palazzo della Magnifica Patria e Palazzo del Podestà

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The two historic buildings on the lakefront house the municipal offices; the atrium is frescoed by Angelo Landi and under the porch that unites them there are ancient coats of arms and memorial plaques.

  • Where Lungolago, Salò
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

MuSa – Museo di Salò

Museums · City history

Inaugurated on June 6, 2015 in the monastery and church of Santa Giustina, already home to the Somaschi Fathers, Salò and Garda from the Roman era to today with archaeological finds of the Civic Archaeological Museum Anton Maria Mucchi, illuminated codes, paintings and musical instruments. It includes the Pio Bettoni meteosismic observatory (1877), the anatomical preparations of Dr. Rini, the Civic Collection of the Drawing with over 800 drawings of the post-war period and from 2023 a section on the Italian Social Republic.

closed from 5 October 2026 for redevelopment work, reopening scheduled in spring 2027

Museo del Nastro Azzurro

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1949 and unique in Italy, it is located in the Santa Giustina complex, in the basement of the MuSa: military valor decorations, Risorgimento relics and materials of the two world wars to tell the decorated from 1793 to today, with uniforms, flags, weapons and coins.

Piazza Serenissima

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Piazza-garden overlooking the lake, with centuries-old olive trees, which joins the lakefront Falcone Borsellino to the port of Canottieri Salò; in the center a stele with the winged lion of Venice in bronze by the sculptor Salolino Aime.

  • Where Piazza Serenissima, Salò
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

I Cipressi di Salò

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

The most beautiful row of cypress trees in Lombardy, in the area of the cemetery on the southern shore of the gulf, is defined: the 133 largest specimens, close to the lake, exceed the 25 meters of height and some trunks over 4 meters of circumference.

  • Where Via Tavine, Salò
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Monte San Bartolomeo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A relief of 528 meters behind Salò, among cypresses, olive trees and oleanders, reachable by a panoramic path suitable also for untrained walkers; at the top the church of San Bartolomeo with views of the lake and the nearby villages.

  • Where Monte San Bartolomeo, Salò
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
