What to see in Castelleone di Suasa

Borgo on a hill to the right of the Cesano, known for the Archaeological Park of Suasa, the Roman city with the Domus dei Coiedii and its mosaics, and for the Archaeological Museum "Casagrande" in the Renaissance Palace of the Rovere; in September the Festa della Cipolla.

Parco archeologico di Suasa e Domus dei Coiedii

History and architecture · Archaeology

Remains of the Roman city of Suasa, abandoned in the 6th century A.D., excavated by the University of Bologna with the Superintendence: base road, commercial forum, two necropolis, houses and the amphitheatre (end of the 1st century BC, discovered in 1990), where performances are held in summer. The heart of the park is the Domus dei Coiedii, from the 1st century A.D., at the most splendid in the 2nd century: under a modern roof, with raised walkways, mythological, floral and geometric mosaics and a floor with more than fifteen types of marble form the most important unitary complex of the Marches. Opening hours: 24/4-30/6 and 1/9-8/12 Saturday, Sunday and holidays 15:30-19:30; July-August Tuesday-Saturday 16-20, Sunday 10:30-12:30 and 16-20.

Museo civico archeologico «A. Casagrande»

Museums · Archaeological

In the Renaissance Palace Della Rovere, it exhibits statues, epigraphs and materials emerged from the excavation campaigns of the archaeological park of Suasa.

Chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of the second half of the sixteenth century: it preserves an Annunciation on canvas of Baroque school, perhaps attributable to Antonio Viviani, and a Madonna with Child, Saint Peter martyr and Saint Sebastian.

  • Entry To be checked

Castello e centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village was born around the medieval castle, of which there are visible traces; in the village the church of San Francesco di Paola (of the Blessed Crucifix), next to the former convent of the Minimi friars that now houses the municipal seat.
