Abbazia di Sant'Eutizio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

On a high cliff on the Castorian Valley, about 5 km from Preci: according to the Dialogues of St. Gregory the Great was born in the fifth century from the oratory of the Syrian hermit Saint Spes, to which St. Euticio followed; from the eighth century the Benedictines settled. Around the Mille it was a center of culture with library and miniature school (hence the formula of confession of 1095, among the oldest texts in vulgar) and then surgical school. The church, begun in 1190, has a hut façade with rosette among the symbols of the Evangelists, seventeenth-century bell tower, a Crucifix by Nicola da Siena, the tomb of St. Euticio of Rocco of Tommaso (1514) and a wooden choir of the sixteenth century.

damaged by the earthquake, ongoing restoration; Umbriatourism indicates it at the moment not to be visited, information at the Tourist Office Valnerina-Cascia

Museo dell'Abbazia di Sant'Eutizio

Museums · Old masters

It occupies several halls of the abbey complex: on the first floor four rooms dedicated to the treasure of the abbey, to the abbot Giacomo Crescenzi (the town of San Matteo di Caravaggio for San Luigi dei Francesi), to the precian surgical school and to a pharmacy and a rebuilt alchemical laboratory; works of art, liturgical objects, wooden sculptures, paintings and goldsmiths.

Museo della scuola chirurgica preciana

Museums · Science and university

In the former church of Santa Caterina tells of the surgical school born in the Abbey of Sant'Eutizio: when in 1215 the Lateran Council IV prohibited clergy surgery, knowledge passed to the laity of Preci, surgeons requested until the sixteenth century at the courts of France and Spain and in the hospitals of Rome and Florence, specialists of cataracts and hernias; Andrea della Croce wrote the Surgery universalis opus absolutum. Exposed tools such as scalpels, pliers and needles.

the MiC card indicates it closed every day; check with the Municipality (0743 93781)

Centro storico, castello e chiese di Santa Caterina e Santa Maria

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

In the village the Town Hall on the main square, the castle of the fourteenth century, the church of Santa Caterina with Gothic portal and Romanesque façade, and the church of Santa Maria, which preserves a fifteenth century Pietà and a baptismal font of the sixteenth century; the Arch of the Finocchioli, rest of the ancient walls, opens onto a viewpoint on the river Campiano.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Castello di Todiano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Built in the 13th century after the decline of the dominion of the monks of Sant'Eutizio, he saw the troops of Frederick II (1227), Charles of Anjou (1265), Braccio da Montone and Francesco Sforza passing through and was equipped with towers; the fourteenth-century semi-cylinder tower of the Fabbi family remains.
