Ecomuseo delle Rocche del Roero

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Museo diffuso founded in 2003 on the initiative of the Piedmont Region, dedicated to the dorsal of the Rocks, geological formation of about 250,000 years born from the change of course of the Tanaro and more than 30 km long, and to the eight historical villages arose after the Mille on the ridges. Paths through woods, vineyards and Rocche to walk or mountain bike, with geolocalized audio guides on legends and nature; map and audio guide to the site, free GPS tracks on the site. Office open from Tuesday to Saturday 9.30-12.30.

  • Where Piazzetta della Vecchia Segheria 2/b, Montà
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.ecomuseodellerocche.it

Sources: ecomuseodellerocche.it · italia.it

Castello dei Morra Lavriano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built in 1363 by the Roeros and completely rebuilt by the Isnardi in 1647; it was plundered in 1544 by the imperial troops of the Marquis of Vasto and in 1691 the French of Catinat burned a branch of it. It has a round decorated tower, loggia, hall of honor, scenic portal and a large garden; inside frescoes and trompe-l'oeil, a tunnel, a well with wheel for water and prisons.

  • Where Centro storico, Montà
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.ecomuseodellerocche.it

Sources: ecomuseodellerocche.it

Centro storico e torre campanaria

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Walk in the historic center of the summit along the castle and its park. The bell tower was erected by Asti after 1257 as a symbol of an autonomous community and rebuilt in 1754 by the master wall Antonio Broglio, with dials of the clock of 1756 and 1780; behind the tower a panoramic balcony.

  • Where Centro storico, Montà
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.ecomuseodellerocche.it

Sources: ecomuseodellerocche.it

Parrocchiale di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Made by the Malabailas in the sixteenth century instead of an ancient oratory near the castle, of late dogotic plant with modifications in the seventeenth and nineteenth centuries, when bell tower and facade were built today.

  • Where Centro storico, Montà
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.ecomuseodellerocche.it

Sources: ecomuseodellerocche.it

Confraternita di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built by the Compagnia dei Disciplinati between 1845 and 1854, with decorations from 1907 by Lorenzo and Costantino Mossello and gilded by Bernardo Arduino; since 1910 it houses a cave of the Virgin of Lourdes. It was the church of the Servant of God canon Francis Church.

visits on request, today used for conferences

  • Where Piazza San Michele, Montà
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.ecomuseodellerocche.it

Sources: ecomuseodellerocche.it

Sacro Monte dei Piloni

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

The smallest Sacro Monte Piemonte, of pre-Christian origins and destination of pilgrimages, overlooking the Valdiana. The church of Saints James Minor and Philip, already existing before the thirteenth century and rebuilt at the end of that century, combines Romanesque and Gothic elements; the Via Crucis has thirteen hexagonal chapels with statuary groups in colored plaster and at the highest point there is the chapel of the Holy Sepulchre, modelled on that of Jerusalem.

  • Where Montà
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.ecomuseodellerocche.it

Sources: ecomuseodellerocche.it
