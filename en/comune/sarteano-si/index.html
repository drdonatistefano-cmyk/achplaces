What to see in Sarteano

Village with castle of the X century and some of the most important Etruscan tombs in Tuscany, including the Tomb of the Infernal Quadriga discovered in 2003.

4 places to visit in Sarteano, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Civico Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the sixteenth century Palazzo Gabrielli the finds of the necropolis of the territory, from the bronze age to the late empire, with canopies, buccheri, fetid stone statues and the real-size reproduction of the Tomb of the Infernal Quadriga.

  • Where Palazzo Gabrielli, Sarteano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Tomba della Quadriga Infernale

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Tomb dug into the travertine at a depth of 5 meters in the necropolis of the Pianacce, with paintings of the fourth century BC intact: the demon Charun drives a cart drawn by lions and griffins.

  • Where Necropoli delle Pianacce, Sarteano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Castello di Sarteano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The X century square castle that dominates the village, the most evident testimony of the medieval past of the village.

  • Where Sarteano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

San Martino in Foro e Collegiata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The two main churches of the village, San Martino in Foro and the Collegiata dei Santi Lorenzo and Apollinare, which preserve valuable works; in the center also the small Theatre of the Arrischianti.

  • Where Sarteano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
