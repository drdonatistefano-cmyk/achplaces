What to see in Sessa Aurunca

The ancient Suessa between the Roccamonfina volcano and Garigliano: the Romanesque cathedral, the Roman theatre with the cryptoportico, the archaeological museum in the ducal castle and the long Roman bridge of the Aurunci.

4 places to visit in Sessa Aurunca, in the province of Caserta (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale di Sessa Aurunca

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

One of the most important works of the Romanesque Campania.

  • Where Sessa Aurunca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Teatro romano e criptoportico

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Theatre Augusteo expanded under Antonino Pio, with cavea of 110 meters to three orders for 7,000-10,000 spectators, dug and restored between 1999 and 2003; near, on the terrace of the hole, the cryptoporic of the syllable age with three arms with graffiti stuccos of names of poets and verses of Virgilio.

From 17 November 2025 the theatre is temporarily closed for works

  • Where Sessa Aurunca
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo archeologico nazionale

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the ducal castle: finds of Ausoni and Aurunci from the Iron Age to the Roman conquest, the votive of the sanctuary of the goddess Marica sul Garigliano, the villas and the production of the Falerno, and the theatre room with the statue of Matidia minor.

  • Where Piazza Castello 2, Sessa Aurunca
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Ponte Aurunco (Ponte Ronaco)

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Roman bridge of the end of the 1st century AD, about 172 meters long on 21 arches, which connected the axis of the Appia and Sinuessa to Sessa.

  • Where Sessa Aurunca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it
