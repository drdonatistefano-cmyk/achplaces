What to see in Manfredonia

Seaside town at the foot of the Gargano: the Swabian-Ange castle with the most important collection of daunie stems, the Archaeological Park of Siponto with the metallic wire basilica of Edoardo Tresoldi, the abbey of San Leonardo and the wetlands of Frattarolo and Lake Salso.

7 places to visit in Manfredonia, in the province of Foggia (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo nazionale archeologico e Castello

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The project of the castle is attributed to Manfredi, son of Frederick II; Charles of Anjou completed walls and residence, the Aragonese added circular towers and outer belt, the Spanish viceroys the pentagonal bastion of the Annunziata. Donated to the State in 1968, it has been a museum since 1980: the core are the daunie stems recovered by Silvio Ferri in the 1960s, the most important collection of daunie stems in the world, in a path from prehistory to the archaic age. Closed on Monday.

  • Where Piazzale Ferri, Manfredonia
  • Entry €8, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Parco archeologico di Siponto e Santa Maria Maggiore

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Roman colony from 194 BC, port and bishop's seat, abandoned after the burial of the port and earthquakes of 1223 and 1255. It preserves the early Christian basilica of the 4th century with mosaic floors, on which since 2016 stands the installation in metal mesh of Edoardo Tresoldi .Where art reconstructs the time, 14 meters high; next, the Romanesque basilica of Santa Maria Maggiore (end XI-beginning XII century), a cubic plan with dome, crypt and portal with columns on lions. Open from Wednesday to Sunday 10.30-19.30.

  • Where Viale Giuseppe Di Vittorio, Manfredonia
  • Entry €5, reduced €2

  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Abbazia di San Leonardo di Siponto

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Built in the 11th century with Archbishop Gerardo I and Augustinian canons, from the second half of the 13th century to 1480 he was of the Teutonic Knights; suppressed in 1810, he reopened to worship after the restorations of the 1950s. Rich lateral portal with Christ between two angels in the bezel and rosette with eleven petals that acts as a sundial: at the summer solstice he draws a crown of light on the floor. The Domus Hospitales for pilgrims to Monte Sant'Angelo also remains.

  • Where Circa 7 km da Manfredonia, presso Monte Aquilone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcogargano.it · italia.it

Oasi naturale del Lago Salso

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

A wet area of about 540 hectares with a visitor centre of the Gargano National Park; in winter it hosts pink flamingos, herons, Italian knights and swamp hawks.

  • Where Lago Salso, Manfredonia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Riserva naturale Palude Frattarolo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

About 260 hectares at the mouth of the Candelaro, the last strip of the great marshes of the Capitana and SPA in the Gargano Park: freshwater ponds and brackish with Italian knights, fighters, pittims, chiurli and beccaccini. Information on visits to the Park Visitor Centres.

  • Where Foce del Candelaro, Manfredonia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcogargano.it · italia.it

Porto antico e Marina

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old port, used mainly by fishing vessels, is a starting point for exploring the coast of Gargano and the Adriatic; the Swabian-Ange fortress dominates it from the sea.
