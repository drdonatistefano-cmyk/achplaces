Museo paleontologico

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In the premises of the primary school, managed with the Centro Studi Storici Alta Valle Scrivia, exhibits fossils collected over the years between the Antola and Aveto mountains, to make the territory better known to those who live or frequent the valleys of the Ligurian central Apennines. The finds cover the ages from the Cretaceous beyond the Miocene and come mostly from deep sea environments.

visit only by reservation (tel. 347 8436982)

  • Where Via alla Chiesa, Crocefieschi

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Centro storico, Palazzi Gemelli e parrocchiale di Santa Croce

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The core of the village develops along a narrow paved street, originally with river pebbles and ........................................................................................................................................................................................................................................................................................................................................................................................... The Gemelli Palaces, two square buildings with double-lighted loggias and tower, were originally three brand buildings built between the sixteenth and seventeenth century by the Fieschi along the mule track for Vobbia; after the fire of one in the eighteenth century the two remaining were residence of the podestà, and today one houses town hall and library, the other private houses. The parish church of Santa Croce was built in 1578 and resumed in 1686.

  • Where Piazza comunale, Crocefieschi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it

Rocche del Reopasso

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Excellent hiking itinerary, challenging for the rocky conglomerate and steep slopes, which in about an hour of walk leads to the summit called Carrega del Diavolo, an exceptional panoramic point at almost 1,000 meters. Nearby there is the Castagneu, large chestnut wood with a rural house.

  • Where Fraz. Semino, Crocefieschi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it · italia.it
