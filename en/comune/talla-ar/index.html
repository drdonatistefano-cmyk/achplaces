What to see in Talla

Country of Casentino among the forests of Pratomagno, traditionally linked to the birth of Guido Monaco, the reformer of the musical notation, whose house in Castellaccia is now a museum; in the territory the villages of Pontenano and Capraia and the ruins of the Badia of Santa Trinità in Alpe, the first Benedictine monastery of Casentino.

Casa natale di Guido Monaco

Museums · House museums

In Castellaccia, the ancient part of Talla, in a building of the tenth century restored in 1996 where tradition wants to be born the Benedictine monk Guido, to which the invention of musical notation is attributed. It is part of the Ecomuseum of Casentino: it exhibits instruments and miniature codes of the Sanctuary of Verna and illustrates the development of the notation; the path "The Via Musicalis" by the artist Marco Ermini connects the house to sound installations, some interactive. Visits only by reservation through Pro Loco.

  • Where Piazza Guido Monaco 8, Talla
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website ecomuseo.casentino.toscana.it

La Castellaccia

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The highest part of Talla, a medieval village with a small church and a row of narrow and leaning houses; here the small fortress from which the Ubertini ruled the area until 1384, when Arezzo finally submitted to Florence.

  • Where Castellaccia, Talla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · discoverarezzo.com

Chiesa di San Niccolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1644 by the Compagnia del Sacramento and the Ducci family; two lateral stone altars conserve 17th century paintings: on the left the Death of Saint Joseph, on the right the Madonna del Rosario with the Saints Domenico, Nicola, Francesco, Caterina da Siena, Caterina d'Alessandria and Maria Maddalena.

  • Where Piazza Licio Nencetti, Talla
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · discoverarezzo.com

Badia di Santa Trinità in Alpe

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ruins about 950 meters in the thick of the forest, reachable from Pontenano following the channel of Capraia. Founded between 950 and 961 by the hermits Peter and Eriprando, it was the first Benedictine monastery of the Casentino; it had the greatest splendor between XI and XII century, passed to the Vallombrosani and at the end of the seventeenth century was in ruins. There remains the large semicircular apse with monophora and the sandstone fence that divided the classroom.

  • Where Da Pontenano, Talla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: discoverarezzo.com · visittuscany.com

Pontenano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Borgo where underground tombs of the 4th century BC were found; the castle, built by the bishops of Arezzo in the 11th century and one of the strongest in Pratomagno, passed to Florence in the second half of the 14th century and was demolished in 1426. According to tradition, Hannibal stayed there during the Punic Wars; there is the church of Santi Margherita and Biagio, documented from the fourteenth century.

  • Where Pontenano, Talla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: discoverarezzo.com · visittuscany.com

Capraia e chiesa di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Mountain village whose castle was destroyed in 1502 by the troops of Vitellozzo Vitelli; the church of Santa Maria houses a Madonna with Child in glazed terracotta of the fifteenth century. Good starting point for summer excursions.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
