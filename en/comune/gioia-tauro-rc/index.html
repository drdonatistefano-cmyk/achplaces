What to see in Gioia Tauro

City of the Plain heir to the Greek polis of Metauros, on the Gulf of the same name: the Archaeological Museum in the eighteenth century Palazzo Baldari, the medieval historical center of Piano delle Fosse with the church of Sant'Antonio and the Duomo, the industrial archeology of the former Gaslini factory and, in August, the festival of the slushing on the seafront.

Museo archeologico di Metauros

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the eighteenth century Palazzo Baldari, used by the Bourbons as an armory, tells Métauros, a settlement founded by the people of Zancle (Messina) and passed in the sixth century BC under the influence of Locri. Most of the finds come from the necropolis excavated along the coast (VII-V century B.C.); also exhibits funerary furnishings of Roman age (II-III century A.D.) and medieval and Renaissance ceramics. Re-opened on October 11, 2024 after the restoration of the palace.

Centro storico di Piano delle Fosse

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient medieval nucleus on a panoramic hill, once fortified with walls and Saracen towers; between the alleys the church of Sant'Antonio and the Cathedral of Sant'Ippolito Martire.

  • Where Piano delle Fosse, Gioia Tauro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Opificio ex Gaslini

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry
