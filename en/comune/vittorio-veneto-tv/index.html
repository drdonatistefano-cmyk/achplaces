What to see in Vittorio Veneto

City born in 1866 from the union of Serravalle and Ceneda along the Monticano, at the foot of the Treviso Prealps: the medieval village of Serravalle and, in Ceneda, the museum of the battle that closed the Great War in 1918.

5 places to visit in Vittorio Veneto, in the province of Treviso (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Vittorio Veneto in the guide with map and filters

Serravalle e Duomo di Santa Maria Nova

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient neighborhood between two hills of the Treviso Alps, with the cathedral of Santa Maria Nova surrounded by medieval alleys and buildings.

  • Where Serravalle, Vittorio Veneto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo del Cenedese

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the Palazzo della Comunità di Serravalle (1462-1476) in Piazza Flaminio: archaeology from the Neolithic to the Longobards, the Dormitio Virginis dei Moranzone, a Madonna attributed to Jacopo Sansovino, Gemito's Aquarium, a Pinacoteca and the Gipsoteca of the victories.

  • Where Piazza Marcantonio Flaminio, Vittorio Veneto
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Oratorio dei Santi Lorenzo e Marco dei Battuti

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

15th century oratory entirely frescoed, monumental section of the Museo del Cenedese.

  • Where Serravalle, Vittorio Veneto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo della Battaglia

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Opened in 1938 in the municipal loggia of Ceneda on the collection of Luigi Marson: the trench, the year of Austrian occupation after Caporetto and the final battle of autumn 1918.

  • Where Piazza Giovanni Paolo I, Vittorio Veneto
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: venetoaroundme.regione.veneto.it · italia.it

Ceneda e Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

In the larger and airier district of Ceneda, the cathedral overlooks the square of the Museo della Battaglia.

  • Where Ceneda, Vittorio Veneto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · venetoaroundme.regione.veneto.it
