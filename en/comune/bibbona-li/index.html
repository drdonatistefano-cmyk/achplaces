What to see in Bibbona

Medieval village of the Costa degli Etruschi with paved alleys, the parish church of Sant'Ilario and the Renaissance Santa Maria della Pietà; in the territory the Etruscan tombs of the Palazzino and the yellow caves, the Macchia della Magona and, on the sea, the Fort of the Cavalleggeri and the Blue Flag beaches of Marina di Bibbona.

8 places to visit in Bibbona, in the province of Livorno (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo medievale e Arco di Bacco

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Village of Etruscan origin, with narrow and paved alleys, medieval squares and exposed brick houses; the Arch of Bacchus has signs attributed to the temple presence. In the center the medieval palace of the Old Town, with the archive of local history, and the ancient fortress, former residence of the noble family. Guided walks by reservation with the Proloco.

  • Where Vicolo delle Mura, Bibbona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.bibbona.li.it · visittuscany.com

Pieve di Sant'Ilario

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the 11th century with trapezoidal plan with the intact Romanesque part and symbols engraved on the stones; inside two baptismal sources in marble and the canvas of the Madonna del Rosario.

  • Where Bibbona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · comune.bibbona.li.it

Necropoli etrusca del Palazzino

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The most important archaeological area of the municipality: almost fifty late-Etruscan burials arranged on terraces overlooking the village, with an ancient ritual quarry road that connected the sources of Bacchus and the stream to the upper part, where probably stood a natural sanctuary.

the MiC card indicates "closed" every day without hours; summer guided tours reported by the MiC for the area: contact the Accademia degli Etruschi (tel. 348 5106404)

  • Where Il Palazzino, Bibbona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visittuscany.com

Grotte gialle

History and architecture · Archaeology

Etruscan tombs in the countryside, excavated in limestone, an ancient seabed of about one million years old, with an ear of rabbit and two V-shaped chambers divided by a false column; remodelled in more recent times. Frequent guided tours, especially in summer, with the Proloco di Bibbona.

  • Where Campagna di Bibbona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.bibbona.li.it · visittuscany.com

Forte dei Cavalleggeri

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Fort eighteenth-century (1780 according to the municipality card) of a series of coastal garrisons against Saracens and pirates, with guard body and stables of the chivalry who guarded the coast and the border, marked by a stone chip. It gives Marina di Bibbona the name of Marina del Forte and periodically hosts exhibitions and events.

  • Where Piazza del Forte 1, Marina di Bibbona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.bibbona.li.it · visittuscany.com

Spiaggia di Marina di Bibbona

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Wide sandy beaches with dunes and Mediterranean tomboli of maquis, between two pine forests towards Marina di Cecina and Marina di Castagneto; Blue flag, equipped and free stretches, dog area, path accessible from Piazza degli Anemoni and a bike path of 5 km in pine forest to Cecina Mare.

  • Where Via del Mare, Marina di Bibbona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.bibbona.li.it · visittuscany.com

Macchia della Magona

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Protected natural area of more than 1,600 hectares, already a wood reserve, open all year round, with about 50 km of hiking trails, horse and mountain bike among hardwood woods, pine trees and Mediterranean maquis; wild boars, roe deer, deer, mouflons, horseback riding and badgers live there, and migratory birds such as beak and buzzard.

  • Where Strada Provinciale della Camminata, Bibbona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
