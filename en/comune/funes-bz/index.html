What to see in Funes

The municipality of Val di Funes at the foot of the Odle: in Tiso the Geodi Museum formed over 290 million years ago, in Santa Maddalena the late Dogotic church of 1492 and the church of San Giovanni in Ranui of 1744, from which starts the path to the Geisler Alm mountain hut.

4 places to visit in Funes, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo dei minerali di Tiso

Museums · Science and university

Dedicated to the famous geodes of Tiso, formed for volcanic activity over 290 million years ago, with rock crystals, smoked quartz, garnets and other alpine minerals: it is the collection of a life by Paul Fischnaller. A short film introduces to the formation of the Alps and minerals; on Tuesdays and Thursdays, by reservation, you go with Fischnaller to look for the geodes.

  • Where Teiser Straße 12, Tiso, Funes
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.mineralienmuseum-teis.it

Sources: mineralienmuseum-teis.it · suedtirol.info

Chiesa di San Giovanni Nepomuceno a Ranui

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Church founded in 1744 by the mining owner Michael von Jenner, in an extraordinary landscape; to see the altarpieces of Franz Sebald Unterberger, painter of Val di Fiemme. It is privately owned and the entrance fee is charged; you ask not to enter the fenced meadows. From the bus stop Ranui 5 minutes walk; parking fee.

private property with paid entry; the Südtirol tab indicates it closed

  • Where St. Johann 2, Santa Maddalena, Funes
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Chiesa di Santa Maddalena

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The present late-Gothic building dates back to 1492, with Baroque interior; in the choir's vault the paintings of 1928 by the Ladin painter Johann M. Pescoller. The access road is closed to the cars: you leave your car at Putzen car park and you walk up in about 30 minutes (or from Filler stop).

  • Where Peterweg 17, Santa Maddalena, Funes
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Da Ranui alla Geisler Alm

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of 14.1 km (about 3 hours and 50 walk, 735 meters of difference in altitude, between 1,378 and 1,989 meters) from the Ranui parking lot for the forest 28 and the 34A trail in the wood to the Geisler Alm (2,000 m), with play area, on a beautiful pasture at the foot of the Odle; return to the paths 34 and 33 along the river Funes.

  • Where Parcheggio di Ranui, Santa Maddalena, Funes
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info
