What to see in Venosa

The ancient Venusia, a Latin colony from 291 B.C. and home of Orazio, in the Vulture: the Abbey of the Holy Trinity with the Unfinished of Normans, the castle of Pirro del Balzo with the archaeological museum and the Palaeolithic park of Notarchiricus.

8 places to visit in Venosa, in the province of Potenza (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Abbazia della Santissima Trinità e Incompiuta

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Complex born between the fourth and fifth centuries and transformed by Longobards and Normans: since 1069, at the behest of Robert Guiscardo, it preserves the remains of the Altavilla; the great enlargement of the twelfth century, built also with stones of the Roman amphitheatre and never finished, is called the Unfinished. In the Foresteria the Museo del Territorio.

  • Where Venosa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · admin-istituzionale.touringclub.it · italia.it

Castello Pirro del Balzo e Museo archeologico nazionale «Mario Torelli»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Castle built from 1460 by Pirro del Balzo on the model of Castelnuovo di Napoli, with moat and cylindrical towers; in the basement the museum tells Venosa from the fourth century B.C. to the Renaissance, with the cippi of the Auguraculum of Banzi, the Tabula Osco-Bantina and a head of Diadumeno.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I 49, Venosa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · admin-istituzionale.touringclub.it · italia.it

Parco archeologico di Venosa

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

In San Rocco, just outside the city on the street of Ofantina: thermal baths of the I-III century A.D., a domus with mosaic and the first early Christian basilica with hexagonal baptismal font.

  • Where Località San Rocco, Venosa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: admin-istituzionale.touringclub.it · italia.it

Parco paleolitico di Notarchirico

History and architecture · Archaeology

Site of 670-695,000 years ago with nine levels of excavation: the skull with tusks of an ancient elephant, a fragment of femur of Homo heidelbergensis, the oldest human presence in Italy, and the most ancient Acheulean bifacials of Western Europe.

Open only on educational occasions or events, by reservation, waiting for the end of the work

  • Where Notarchirico, Venosa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Casa di Orazio

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The house where according to tradition the poet Quinto Orazio Flacco was born, visible only from the outside; in the center also the statue dedicated to him.

  • Where Centro storico, Venosa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Fontana di Messer Oto

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry
