What to see in Montefortino

12th century fortified village on the right of the Tenna, at the foot of the Sibilla and Priora mountains in the Sibillini Mountains National Park, with walls, towers and three original gates; in Palazzo Leopardi the Pinacoteca civica Fortunato Duranti, in the territory the Sanctuary of the Madonna dell'Ambro, the parish church of Sant'Angelo in Montespino and the Gole dell'Infernaccio with the hermitage of San Leonardo.

12 places to visit in Montefortino, in the province of Fermo (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico, Porta Santa Lucia e case-torri

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified village built in the 12th century on a hill on the right of the Tenna, of which there are traces of the walls, towers and three original doors; you enter from the Porta di Santa Lucia and go up along the terrace system to the church of San Francesco, passing from the Town Hall and the neoclassical temple of the Clock (Casa delle colonne) next to Palazzo Leopardi. Outside the walls there are sixteenth-century tower houses.

  • Where Porta Santa Lucia, Montefortino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sibillini.net

Palazzo Leopardi e Pinacoteca civica Fortunato Duranti

Museums · Old masters

Renaissance noble palace of the 16th century with Guelfe windows and rustic portal, houses the Civic Art Gallery dedicated to Fortunato Duranti (1797-1863).

  • Where Palazzo Leopardi, Montefortino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sibillini.net

Museo d'arte e fauna dei Sibillini (Raccolta di fauna appenninica)

Museums · Themed

Museum dedicated to the art and fauna of the Sibillini, also housed in the sixteenth-century Palazzo Leopardi next to the Pinacoteca Duranti.

  • Where Palazzo Leopardi, Montefortino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie (Madonna della Fonte)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Founded in 1647, it has a Greek cross plan with three chapels and altars and an octagonal dome with lantern decorated with Baroque stuccoes; the portal in travertine was rebuilt in 1953.

  • Where Centro storico, Montefortino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sibillini.net · italia.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In Romanesque-Gothic style, originally called the Church of the Girone, stands on top of the terraces of the village and preserves a bell of 1310; the Franciscan convent, documented since 1549, was suppressed in 1810 and the Municipality bought the church in 1858. Today it is used as an auditorium.

  • Where Centro storico, Montefortino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sibillini.net · italia.it

Pieve di San Michele Arcangelo, Sant'Agostino e Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church of San Michele Arcangelo was built between 1860 and 1870 in neoclassical style, designed by G.B. Carducci di Fermo, with a single nave and a peristyle of eight columns around presbytery and apse. In the center also Sant'Agostino, of the twelfth century, with ancient portal and arched windows, and Sant'Andrea, born with the first hospital authorized in 1421, with neoclassical structure and façade of the second half of the eighteenth century.

  • Where Centro storico, Montefortino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sibillini.net · italia.it

Santuario della Madonna dell'Ambro

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

At 683 meters, it is described as the oldest Marian shrine of the Marches and the second most visited after Loreto, called "Little Lourdes of the Sibillini" (Sibillini); the tradition links the first chapel to the apparition of the Madonna to the shepherdella muta Santina, around the year 1000. The present church, with six chapels, was rebuilt from 1603 with decorations by Martino Bonfini by Patrignone, Domenico Malpedi (1634) and Virginio Parodi (1928); since 1897 it has been guarded by the Capuchins.

  • Where Località Ambro, Montefortino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sibillini.net

Pieve di Sant'Angelo in Montespino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Romanesque church on the summit of Montespino, over 800 meters, mentioned for the first time in a document of January 977; basilica plant with three naves with crypt, probably rest of the primitive church, and watchtower of the fifteenth century. It was one of the most important parish churches in the diocese of Fermo.

  • Where Montespino, Montefortino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sibillini.net

Gole dell'Infernaccio e Pisciarelle

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Gorges excavated from the Tenna, among the most suggestive of the Umbrian-Marche Apennines, with waterfalls and clear pools. The path starts from the entrance of the gorges, at an artificial gallery and the small waterfalls of the Pisciarelle, passes a wooden bridge and enters the beech wood; at the crossroads you go up to the hermitage of San Leonardo or continue towards Capotenna, the source of the Tenna, passing the hidden waterfall.

  • Where Gole dell'Infernaccio, Montefortino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Eremo di San Leonardo al Volubrio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Nestled in the rocks above the Gorges of Infernaccio, it is reached by a path in the beech woods. Benedictine Priory linked to Fonte Avellana from the 12th to the 15th century and then to the Camaldolese of Monte Corona, it was rebuilt by the foundations of the Capuchin Father Pietro Lavini.

  • Where Gole dell'Infernaccio, Montefortino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sibillini.net · italia.it

Gole dell'Ambro

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Ambro river forms about 2 km of jumps, narrow streets, high tunnels and heights up to 55 meters, with a very variable flow rate depending on the season; in the lower part stands the sanctuary of the Madonna dell'Ambro. The route, about 14 km, is recommended for experienced hikers.

Tourism Marche: after the earthquake of 2016 the area is not accessible

  • Where Località Ambro, Montefortino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Monte Sibilla e Monte Priora

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village is located at the foot of Monte Priora (2,32 m) and Monte Sibilla (2,173 m), in the Sibillini Mountains National Park; Montefortino is a stop of the Grande Anello dei Sibillini, 120 km long trail that can be crossed in nine days.

  • Where Montefortino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sibillini.net
