What to see in Roccagloriosa

Country of the Cilento National Park, Vallo di Diano and Alburni overlooking the Gulf of Policastro, once a bulwark guarding the valleys of Bussento and Mingardo; on Monte Capitenali stood a fortified Lucana city (VIII century BC), whose finds and the ori of the necropolis of the Scala are exhibited in the Antiquarium and in the Civic Archaeological Museum.

Antiquarium «Sabato Balbi» e Museo civico archeologico «Antonella Fiammenghi»

Museums · Archaeological

Two exhibition sites with the Lucanian finds (IV-III century BC) of necropolis, inhabited and sacred areas: the Antiquarium in Piazza del Popolo preserves monumental tombs, such as the 19 and 24 tombs of the Scala with vases with red figures and metal ornaments; the museum of Borgo Sant'Antonio funerary furnishings, gold jewels, bronze mirrors, terracotta statuettes and bronze sheets with inscriptions in Greek and Osco, in addition to the Roccagloriosa's tomb 9 gold ornaments. From 1 July to 15 September every day 10-13 and 17-20, with reservation required (349 2201533, 349 1111491, 347 6321268); in other periods guided tours of the Pro Loco by reservation for groups of at least 4.

the MiC card indicates opening only from 1 July to 15 September, with obligatory booking

Abitato lucano e necropoli della Scala

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the western side of Mount Capitenali stood a Lucanian city founded in the 5th century BC and abandoned in the 3rd century, surrounded by walls in the 4th century, with regular houses around paved courtyards and small sacelli for family cults; a fragment of bronze table in Osco reports civic norms. In the necropolis of the Scala the tomb "princepesca" 6 (late 5th century BC) returned Etruscan-campani bronzes; the excavations were conducted from 1982 to 1991 with the University of Alberta. The Cilento Park indicates the excavations of La Scala among the most interesting in Cilento.

Percorso Capitenali - la Scala

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Nature trail on the mountain of Bulgheria to the necropolis of Fistelia, Lucan city, without difficulty and with a regular background, also suitable for families, elderly and people with disabilities. Along the centuries-old olive trees, Mediterranean maquis with brooms and myrtle, dry stone terraces and the fountain of San Nicola, with views from the Bulgheria to the Gulf of Policastro.
