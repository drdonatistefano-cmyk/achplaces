What to see in Zignago

Municipality of Val di Vara, in the hinterland of La Spezia: in Novà di Zignago was found on 29 December 1827 the first stele statue of Lunigiana, and the territory preserves the Ligurian castle and the ancient Via dei Monti towards Mount Dragonone.

Castellaro di Zignago

History and architecture · Archaeology

Castellaro, a fortified high-rise settlement of the ancient Ligurians, which dominates some mountain routes between the Val di Vara and the castle of Pignone; together with the Mount Dragonone it testifies the Ligurian settlements of the area.
