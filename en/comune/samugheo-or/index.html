What to see in Samugheo

Mandrolisai and Brabaxianna country known for its artisan weaving, with red trachyte houses and murals with carpets: the MURATS textile art museum, the churches of San Sebastiano and San Basilio, the sanctuary of Santa Maria di Abbasassa and, among woods and gorges, the Byzantine Castle of Medusa.

7 places to visit in Samugheo, in the province of Oristano (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

MURATS, Museo unico regionale dell'arte tessile sarda

Museums · Themed

Museum on the outskirts of the village with wooden frames and Sardinian textile artifacts (mats, tapestries, blankets), including five eighteenth-century tapinu de mortus, on the eight known in Sardinia, and the affaciadas exhibited during the Corpus Domini.

  • Where Samugheo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Castello di Medusa

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Byzantine castrum of the IV-V century with phases up to the XII, border castle between judged, perhaps the castrum Asonis cited in 1189 and restored by Eleonora of Arborea in 1389; the name There are still sections of walls, a cistern and two towers, surrounded by vegetation, on a cliff halfway between Samugheo and Asuni. Asphalt road up to about 500 meters, then on foot or off-road in the woods.

  • Where Strada per Asuni (SP 38), Samugheo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Sebastiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church perhaps of the thirteenth century, enlarged in the fifteenth with Latin cross plan and late-Gothic decorations; in mid-January the fires of San Sebastiano anticipate the carnival.
