What to see in Cinisello Balsamo

Cities at the north gates of Milan, rich in delightful villas: the main is Villa Ghirlanda Silva, with one of the first English parks of Italy and home of the MUNAF - National Museum of Photography; the territory is between the North Park Milan and the agricultural park of the Grugnotorto.

Villa Ghirlanda Silva e parco storico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

17th century villa with frescoed rooms, today municipal. The formal Italian garden of Silva, known from the engravings of Marc'Antonio Dal Re (1726), was transformed at the end of the eighteenth century by Ercole Silva into one of the first landscape parks in English in Italy; Silva theorized its model in the treatise • Of the art of English gardens • (1801). The park, about 5,44 hectares open to the public and almost intact, preserves obelisk, neoclassical exedra dedicated to Health, Scottish lodges, Swiss chalet of the 19th century and a sarcophagus cover with the inscription "Et in Arcadia ego"; in the courtyard of honor there is a monumental magnolia with sinuous trunks.

  • Where Via Frova 10, Cinisello Balsamo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · in-lombardia.it

MUNAF - Museo Nazionale di Fotografia

Museums · Modern art

Born as Museum of Contemporary Photography (MuFoCo), opened in April 2004 as the first Italian public museum dedicated to photography and technological image, and today MUNAF - National Museum of Photography. It occupies the southern wing of Villa Ghirlanda and preserves more than 2 million photos from the post-war period to the present day of more than 1,000 Italian and foreign authors, with funds on landscape, portrait, social photography and artistic research, and a specialized library; some exhibitions are held at the Triennale in Milan.

  • Where Via Frova 10, Cinisello Balsamo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Parco Nord Milano

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Regional park since 1975, born to retrain the outskirts north of Milan, of about 1,000 hectares between Milan, Sesto San Giovanni, Cinisello Balsamo, Bresso, Cormano, Cusano Milanino and Novate Milanese. Lawns with rows, groves (about 100 hectares of wood in the north-east sector), fields and wetlands, including the pond of Cinisello; the agricultural area of Balossa is a stopover and nesting place for migratory birds.

  • Where Parco Nord Milano, Cinisello Balsamo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: parconord.milano.it · in-lombardia.it
