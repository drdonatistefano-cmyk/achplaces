What to see in Trana

Municipality of the lower Sangone Valley known for the Rea Botanical Garden of San Bernardino, section of the Regional Museum of Natural Sciences with over 2.000 species; on the territory the Sanctuary of Our Lady of the Star and chapels like Santa Teresa d'Avila, frescoed by Francesco Gonin, overlooking the lakes of Avigliana.

Giardino Botanico Rea

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Paid entry

Born in 1967 as a private institution on the initiative of Giuseppe Giovanni Bellia, owner of the Vivai di San Bernardino, who in the seventies developed the collections of exotic plants, especially succulent, and sought rare species in Val Sangone. In 1989 the Piedmont Region bought it and joined it to the Museum of Natural Sciences in Turin; today it grows more than 2,000 species from all over the world and an expanded collection of wild plants of Piedmont. Free for children under 14 years of age and Museum holders; in the season 2026 open from 2 May to 30 September, Monday-Friday 9-12 and 13-17, Sunday 14-18, Saturday closed.

  • Where Frazione San Bernardino, Via Giaveno 40, 10090 Trana
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Santuario di Nostra Signora della Stella

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Built in the eighteenth century on the site of an ancient church of Roman times, of which remain the arch of the altar and a niche; the bell tower is from 1885. The interior is rich in decorations and furnishings and preserves an ancient cedar statue. Visitable during religious services.

  • Where Via Santuario 24, Trana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.trana.to.it · turismotorino.org

Cappella di Santa Teresa d'Avila

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

An 18th-century little church with elliptical plan with curved façade and a small bell tower, at the entrance of the threshing floor of the hamlet Cascina Usseglio, on a hill of the dorsal that closes the Mareschi and the lakes of Avigliana, above the erratic rock \"lou roc grò" . Frescoed by Francesco Gonin: in the center of the vault the Holy Family with Saint Anne, in the cloisters the saints Jerome, Rocco, Teresa and Giovanni Evangelista; completes the works a painting of Saint Teresa.

  • Where Località Usseglio (Cascina Usseglio), Trana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.trana.to.it · turismotorino.org
