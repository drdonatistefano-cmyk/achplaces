What to see in Pinasca

Municipality of the lower Val Chisone with the museum of furniture and culture of Valligiana "Abitare in Valle" , the Big Bench of Serremarchetto overlooking the plain of Pinerol, the Fontana dell'Orsa and the mechanical nativity scene of Dubbione.

4 places to visit in Pinasca, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo «Abitare in Valle»

Museums · Themed

Museum of furniture and culture in Valligiana: documentation centre on the furnishings and culture of the valleys, with objects and furniture that show how the valley populations conceived their home. It also hosts temporary themed exhibitions. Opening hours on request (tel. 0121 800716).

  • Where Corso Galliano Rocco 2, Pinasca
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Big Bench – Panchina gigante di Serremarchetto

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Viewpoint

It is reached in about twenty minutes on foot following the old ring of the Serremarchetto cross-country track of the seventies and eighties, a quiet walk among shady beech woods suitable for everyone. From the bench the view spans the lower Val Chisone and the plain of Pinerol and, on clear days, the Alpine arch from the Marittime to the Monviso.

  • Where Località Serre Marchetto, Pinasca
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Fontana dell'Orsa

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

One of the best known places in Pinasca, with waters appreciated also by those who come from afar to draw them; the first official documents on the source date back to the early twentieth century. The name recalls the bear, common in the valleys until the nineteenth century, and a legend on a bear that went down every evening to drink; in 1933 an attempt of the podestà to divert the water towards the town hall failed after the letter of a young woman from the village to Mussolini.

  • Where Via Antica di Fenestrelle 15, frazione Dubbione, Pinasca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.pinasca.to.it · turismotorino.org

Presepe meccanico di Dubbione

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Born in 2002 with the event "There was once Christmas" and since 2014 set up in the Oratory S. Paolo, is entirely self-built, set in the Waldensian valleys with a section dedicated to the Germanasca Valley, and is registered with the Italian Association of Friends of the Nativity scene. According to the Town Hall page ( 2019 poster) it is open from 8 December to 6 January on Saturday and Sunday from 15 to 18, with visits for schools and groups by reservation.

  • Where Oratorio S. Paolo, via Serre, Dubbione, Pinasca
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.pinasca.to.it · turismotorino.org
