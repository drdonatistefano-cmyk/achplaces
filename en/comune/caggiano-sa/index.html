What to see in Caggiano

Borgo 828 meters in the Cilento National Park, Vallo di Diano and Alburni, second highest municipality in the province of Salerno, with a medieval center dominated by the Norman castle, ancient churches linked to Greek monks and Templars and the Antiquarium with the remains of the funerary monument of the Insteii.

Castello medievale e mura

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It rises between IX and XI century on the highest point of the country: an act of donation of 1092 attributed it to William of Caggiano, first lord of the place. It had a central body with three high towers, a tower and two forts; in the fifteenth century it became a noble residence. The walls had four gates and the eastern one, with the drawbridge, still gives the name to the area of ...Bocca del Ponte. Mostly municipal and restored, it hosts exhibitions and events.

Centro storico e truzzulatùr

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Alleys, stairs and squares of medieval imprint, where many entrances have heavy wooden doors with iron doors in carved stone arches and coats of arms: they are the Truzzulatùr, a sign of the importance of the family that lived there. In the center there is also the chapel of San Luca, next to which until 1864 stood the hospital founded around 1600 at the behest of Cardinal Gesualdo.

  • Where Centro storico, Caggiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.caggiano.sa.it

Museo-Antiquarium civico

Museums · Archaeological

In the former convent of the Reformed Fathers, next to the municipal library: at the center the high medieval tomb (VIII-IX century) excavated in Fontana Caggiano and the pieces of the funerary monument erected around 40 AD by Gresia Terzia for her husband Quinto Insteio Cimbrio, with the original inscriptions. It also hosts permanent State Police exhibitions, brigandage (one hundred images), the Fortunato Capero cabinetmaker and Gandhi, with gifts from the Indian Embassy.

Chiesa e monastero di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The monastery of the Reformed Fathers, built in the seventeenth century with the contribution of all families and delivered in 1634, has a square plan around the cloister; passed to the Municipality with the laws of 1866. The two-nave church contains paintings by Domenico Guarino (1721-1729), Peccheneda and the Solimena school, an eighteenth-century organ case, the cantoria of 1742, the monument to Emanuela Vitilio, who died of childbirth in 1712, and two crosses in mother-of-pearl of Byzantine origin.

Chiesa madre del Santissimo Salvatore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The most important of the village, with a single nave, with facade with circular carvings and side chapels with the coats of arms of the most influential families. The main altar is in veined marble, perhaps from the early seventeenth century, with a canvas attributed to Pucciarelli; the Virgin of the Rescue is of Peccheneda and the Baroque pulpit in wood, with scenes of the Gospels, is of Fortunato Capero.

Chiesa di Santa Caterina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church vaulted cruising, restored, which preserves six works of Nicola Peccheneda dated 1769, including the "Christ" and the "adultera" and "The Blood of the Redeemer."

Chiesa di Santa Maria dei Greci

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Traditionally, the first church in the village, with a Greek plan until about the eighteenth century and then a Latin cross; the Greek cult is documented until 1570. Legend has it that on the outer left wall a stone is walled up brought by the Templars from Palestine.

Not open to worship, under renovation for damage to the 1980 earthquake

Percorso medievale e Santa Veneranda

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the Portuccia, a panoramic path runs along the eastern edge of the historic centre to the ruins of the church of Santa Veneranda, a single nave, typical of the churches of the 10th century, with near a baptismal font for immersion baptisms; along the way caves used by the Eastern monks for meditation and a large natural arch. From the viewpoint, in good weather, you can see the Gulf of Salerno.

  • Where Portuccia, Caggiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.caggiano.sa.it · italia.it
