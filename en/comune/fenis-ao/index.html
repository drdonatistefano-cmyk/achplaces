What to see in Fénis

The village of the most famous castle of the Aosta Valley, fortress and residence of the Challant with the frescoed courtyard, at the entrance of the valley of Clavalité.

5 places to visit in Fénis, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Fénis in the guide with map and filters

Castello di Fénis

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Rebuilt in the middle of the fourteenth century by Aimone di Challant and completed at the beginning of the fifteenth century by his son Bonifacio I, who decorated the courtyard and interior with frescoes of the international Gothic, including San Giorgio who kills the dragon; Alpine furnishings collected in 1935-36. Visit accompanied.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

MAV – Museo dell'Artigianato Valdostano di tradizione

Museums · Themed

A few meters from the castle, about 800 objects among artifacts and sculptures of the Valdostano handicrafts, with a teaching space for children.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Vallone di Clavalité

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The only valley in the region with a small salt deposit, up to Bivacco Borroz; on top the small church of Notre-Dame-de-la-Neige.

  • Where Fénis
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Tzanté de Bouva

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Large area at the castle with picnics, games and sports in a forest of oaks, cherry, birch and ash trees; from here starts a loop bike of over 5 km.

  • Entry Free
