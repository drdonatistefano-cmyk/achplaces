What to see in Nus

Country between chestnut groves and vineyards at the entrance of the Valley of Saint-Barthélemy, with the castle of Pilate and the regional astronomical observatory under one of the darkest skies of the Alps.

4 places to visit in Nus, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 2 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Osservatorio astronomico e Planetario di Lignan

Museums · Science and university

Regional Observatory of the Valle d'Aosta at more than 1600 meters in Lignan, base of scientific research, chosen for the low light pollution; guided tours and dissemination events.

  • Where Lignan, Nus
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it

Castello di Pilato

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the upper part of the village, to protect the village; according to tradition, Pontio Pilate would have stayed there exiled from Caligola.

  • Where Nus
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Santuario di Cunéy

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 2656 meters above Saint-Barthélemy, reachable on foot, dates back to 1659 and is dedicated to the Madonna of the Snows; the current building was rebuilt in 1861.

  • Where Saint-Barthélemy, Nus
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Valle di Saint-Barthélemy

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valley with intact landscapes and bare, first winding and steep, which in Lignan opens in a vast panoramic plateau.

  • Where Saint-Barthélemy, Nus
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: italia.it · lovevda.it
