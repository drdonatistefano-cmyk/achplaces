What to see in Scarperia e San Piero

3 places to visit in Scarperia e San Piero, in the province of Florence (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Scarperia e San Piero in the guide with map and filters

Palazzo dei Vicari e Museo dei Ferri Taglienti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

The palace of the Florentine vicar (1306), with the facade covered with coats of arms also Robbiani, and the Museum of knives of Scarperia with the workshop of the knifemaker.

  • Where Piazza dei Vicari, Scarperia
  • Entry Palace €5 whole; Museo dei Ferri Taglienti from €4 to €7
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com

Villa medicea del Trebbio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Villa-castle of Michelozzo for Cosimo il Vecchio, with crenellated tower, Italian garden and 17th century pergola, 500 meters above Mugello.

  • Where Località Trebbio 11, San Piero a Sieve
  • Entry To be checked: visits only by appointment
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · destinationflorence.com

Museo d'arte sacra e Centro di documentazione archeologica di Sant'Agata

Museums · Archaeological

Next to the parish church of Sant'Agata: works of the church such as the Spousalizio di Santa Caterina di Bicci di Lorenzo (1430) and Robbian terracotta, and finds of Mugello from the Paleolithic to the age of metals with the reconstruction of a prehistoric village.

  • Where Via della Pieve 3, Sant'Agata, Scarperia e San Piero
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com
