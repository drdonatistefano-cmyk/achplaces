What to see in Barolo

The village that gives its name to the winery "Bandiera Arancione," in the UNESCO wine landscape of the Langhe, collected around the castle of Falletti.

4 places to visit in Barolo, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Barolo in the guide with map and filters

Castello Falletti e WiMu

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

The castle on a fortification of the 10th century, the Falletti since 1325 and purchased by the municipality in 1970, hosts since 2010 WiMu, one of the most innovative wine museums in the world, and the Regional Winery.

  • Where Piazza Falletti, Barolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Museo dei Cavatappi

Museums · Themed

In a former cellar with barrel vaults in front of the castle, about 500 corkscrew from the eighteenth century to today in 19 sections.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Barolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Chiesa di San Donato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The eighteenth-century parish church with three naves with octagonal dome and six altars, where the Fallets are buried.

  • Where Piazza Falletti, Barolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Castello della Volta

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval castle in ruins on the brooch of the Violes, on the road to La Morra: inaccessible, you can admire the cylindrical tower from afar; the legend wants that in 1713 the vault of the salon collapsed during a dance.

  • Where Strada per La Morra, Barolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it
