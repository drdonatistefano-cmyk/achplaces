What to see in Verzegnis

Carnia municipality that rises from the plain of Tagliamento to the top of Monte Verzegnis, with fractions at different altitudes around an artificial lake; at Villa di Verzegnis the collector Egidio Marzona created the Art Park, open-air museum with works by Merz, LeWitt, Long and Penone.

Art Park - Parco di arte contemporanea

Museums · Modern art · Free entry · Viewpoint

Open-air museum among meadows and woods born from the vision of the collector Egidio Marzona, who since 1989 invited artists to stay in Villa: site-specific installations made of stone, glass, steel and concrete by Mario Merz, Sol LeWitt, Richard Long, Giuseppe Penone, Bruce Nauman, Lawrence Weiner and Dan Graham, from Minimalism to Land Art, from Conceptual Art to Poor Art. Many works invite interaction and are suitable for children; there are no fences or tickets. It is part of the CarniaMusei network.

  • Where Via Marzona 11, Villa di Verzegnis
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it

Lago di Verzegnis

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Large artificial lake created from the fifties of the twentieth century with a dam on the river Ambiesta, used for hydropower: bathing is not allowed, but you take bike walks and excursions on the paths along the banks and trout fishing. From Tolmezzo take the provincial road 1 towards Sella Chianzutan; for the best panoramic point you leave the provincial road at Chiaicis via Ambiesta, and a road bridge crosses the lake. Nearby, the abandoned village of Pusea can be reached only on foot or by bike.

  • Where Lago di Verzegnis, Verzegnis
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
