What to see in Manerbio

The centre of the low plain of Brescia, gathered around Palazzo Luzzago di Bagno, home to the town hall and the Archaeological Civic Museum with the only coin known in Italy by the Longobard King Ariperto I, stands out the eighteenth-century parish church of San Lorenzo with a painting of Moretto, the churches of the Discipline and of San Rocco and the neo-Gothic Palazzo Ghirardi.

Museo civico archeologico di Manerbio e del territorio

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

In the seventeenth-century wing of Palazzo Luzzago, already a guest house, born in 1985 from the exhibitions of the local Archaeological Historical Group and today on about 500 square meters in two halls set up by the architect Andrea Perin. The chronological path tells the population of the low plain from Neolithic to the post-Medieval age with finds of the excavations conducted since 1983 under the direction of the Superintendence: ceramics, daggers, funerary furnishings, silver drachma of the Galli Cenomani, Renaissance ceramics and the only coin known in Italy of King Ariperto I (653-661); the second room hosts temporary exhibitions.

  • Where Piazza Cesare Battisti 1, Manerbio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrescia.it

Sources: comune.manerbio.bs.it · visitbrescia.it

Palazzo Luzzago di Bagno

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Born in the early seventeenth century from the union of two buildings of the noble Luzzago, connected in the eighteenth century by the staircase to two ramps with railing in wrought iron, attributed to the abbot Gaspare Turbini and frescoed probably by Pietro Scalvini; salons and stairs have squares and imaginary landscapes. In 1908 the Di Bagno gave it to the municipality, which has the town hall; the wing of the guest house library and museum, the rider became the Little Theatre after the recovery ended in 2002.

  • Where Piazza Cesare Battisti 1, Manerbio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrescia.it

Sources: comune.manerbio.bs.it · old.comune.manerbio.bs.it · visitbrescia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Lorenzo Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1715 and 1742 on a project by Antonio Tubino da Lugano, a Latin cross with nine polychrome altars, marble staircase and facade of Carlo Corbellini with statues of Luca Calegari; the bell tower was designed in 1603 by Francesco Dattari called Pizzafuoco. It contains a 16th century painting of Moretto from the ancient parish church, the Pietà by Giovanni Battista Pittoni, the San Francesco del Malosso, a wooden Crucifix of 1480 and the marble group of Antonio Calegari at the altar of the Rosary; decorations by Angelo Cominelli of the second nineteenth century.

Chiesa della Disciplina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded in 1337 by an association founded for a vote against an epidemic, on a land donated by Giovanni Trinca, it was renovated in the seventeenth century and has the facade very modified in the early twentieth century. He preserves works by artists from Brescia and Veneto, including a painting by Antonio Paglia with Blessed Fedele da Sigmarringen, a 16th century Nativity and an Annunciation of the 17th century.

Castelletto (Palazzetto Cocciano-Di Rosa)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

On the ancient road Pretoria, it was born from a castle probably guarding the bridge; in the sixteenth century the Cocciano-Di Rosa built the present building, restored by the Ziletti family, today owner. You can see the cornice with shelves, the portal in marble with rustic rocks and the coat of arms.
