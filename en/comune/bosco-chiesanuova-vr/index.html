Museo etnografico «La Lessinia: l'uomo e l'ambiente»

Museums · Themed

With original objects, photographs and plastics, it tells the life and the environment of Lessinia: prehistory, alpine pasture, haymaking, milk processing, production of coal, ice and lime, carpentry, spinning and weaving, popular religiosity. It has two separate sections a few kilometers away, the Baito della Coletta and the Jassàra del Gritz. In autumn open Saturday and Sunday, Christmas and summer every day except Monday; guided tours by reservation.

  • Where Via Mercato 28, Bosco Chiesanuova
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Baito della Coletta

Museums · Themed

Rectangular building dated 1729, in Coletta locality about 1.5 km from the center on the road to Erbezzo, once used to transform milk: it preserves the original tools for butter and cheese and in summer it hosts workshops as a day as a housewife. Section of the ethnographic museum, open during the summer; guided tours by telephone reservation.

  • Where Località Coletta, Bosco Chiesanuova
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Giassàra del Grietz

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

A circular icebox partly underground with a roof of stone slabs, built around 1870 by Innocent bricklayer Menegazzi: in winter the ice was cut from the pond next door, which in summer was sold in Verona and in the plain, until 1935-36. It has two bocàre, one to store the blocks and one to the level of the road to load the wagons. It is only seen from the outside, with explanatory panel; about 4 km from the center on the road to Malga San Giorgio.

  • Where Contrada Grietz, Bosco Chiesanuova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Sentiero delle contrade cimbre

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of 6 km (about 2 hours, +192 m) of the path Walkbosco n. 1 starting from Piazza della Chiesa: for old mule tracks, reach the Coletta district (1113 m), the Biancari with an ancient six-arched portico, the Tander, Der, Lesi and Croce districts, between woods and a vajo.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa, Bosco Chiesanuova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Da Grietz a Bocca di Selva tra le malghe

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

A marked path of 4.5 km (about 1 hour and 10, +290 m) starting from the parking lot of Gritz, next to the restored jassàra, and passing through the Dossetti, Baston and Moscarda huts to the Bocca di Selva refuge.

  • Where Grietz, Bosco Chiesanuova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Monte Tomba da Bocca di Selva

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Excursion indicated as easy of 7-8 km (about 3 hours) from Bocca di Selva, reached by Bosco following the signs for San Giorgio: you go up to the refuge Podestaria (1655 m) and then to Mount Tomb, with views of the Carega, the Dolomites and Mount Baldo.

  • Where Bocca di Selva, Bosco Chiesanuova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it
