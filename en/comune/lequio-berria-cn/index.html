What to see in Lequio Berria

Comune dell'Alta Langa which houses the italo-Russian collection of pastel paintings donated by artists, the chapel of San Rocco with fourteenth-century frescoes of the Passion and, along the SP281, the fifteenth-century sanctuary of the Madonna della Snow.

Collezione permanente del Pastello Soffice

Museums · Modern art

Collection of 49 pastels donated to the Municipality by Italian and Russian artists, made between 1996 and 2001 mainly on velvety paper: views of St. Petersburg, Jaroslavl and Rostov alongside Italian subjects such as Sanremo, Monviso with the hills of the Langhe and Lequio Berria, with works by Renzo Magnanini, Vladimir Emelianov, Luigi Carbone, Peter Pavlov and others. The City page does not indicate exhibition center or timetable: information in the Municipality, Via Roma 36, tel. 0173 52402.

Cappella di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small ancient chapel in the center of the village, enlarged and partly rebuilt in the sixteenth century (1593), with bell tower probably seventeenth century; it was temporary parish during the construction of the new church and was deconsecrated after 1930. In 1971, during the transformation into a gym, there were paintings on the left wall of the second half of the fourteenth century with Christ before Pilate and Herod and other scenes of the Passion; the façade was restored in 1996.

Santuario della Madonna della Neve

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Built in the fifteenth century and still parish church of San Giorgio between the late sixteenth and early seventeenth centuries, it preserves a remarkable bell tower of the time; the interior with three naves without transept, with rectangular apse, shows frescoes from the fifteenth century that emerge from the plaster and in the apse a Madonna with Child dated 1400. Visitable during religious services.
