What to see in San Felice Circeo

A walled village about 100 meters above sea level, on the promontory of the Circeo linked to the myth of Ulysses and the Maga Circe: the Tower of the Templars with the exhibition "Homo sapiens" and habitats, the Grotta Guattari of the Neanderthals, the acropolis of Circe and the paths towards the Circe Peak in the Circeo National Park.

10 places to visit in San Felice Circeo, in the province of Latina (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e Piazza Vittorio Veneto

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village is located on a natural platform about 100 meters above sea level, inside the walls; you enter from an arched door in Piazza Vittorio Veneto, heart of the historic center. The origins date back to the Latin colony of Circei (VI-IV century BC); then it was property of the Templars, feud of the Caetans and fortress of the Papal State.

Torre dei Templari e mostra «Homo sapiens e habitat»

Museums · Archaeological

The tower on the main square was built between 1240 and 1259, when the Templars held the fortress; the clock, moved from the door of the baronial palace, was placed there at the beginning of the nineteenth century. It houses the permanent exhibition "Homo sapiens e habitat" (Homo sapiens and habitats), started in 1978 by the Ecology Centre of the Quaternary: five rooms on the general prehistory and the Circeo, with human and animal bones, instruments and tools from the caves of the promontory.

Palazzo Baronale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Built by the Caetani in the 14th century next to the Torre dei Templari, today it is the seat of the Town Hall; the courtyard is accessed by an arch on the right side of the square.

Grotta Guattari

History and architecture · Archaeology

Among the most important prehistoric sites in the world for the study of Neanderthal's man: here in 1939 the skull was found known as the Circeo Man, about 60,000 years ago; the excavations returned about ten adult individuals and remains of elephants, rhinos, hippopotamuses, deer and hyenas (between 50,000 and 100,000 years ago). The visit route includes a virtual reality experience with the cave as it was 60,000 years ago and fossil holograms.

Visitable only in the extraordinary openings of the Superintendence (in 2026 in July and August); guided tours through the Pro Loco

Acropoli e mura ciclopiche di Circei

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the cliff overlooking the village, the acropolis was the last stronghold of the city of Circei; polygonal walls are preserved on top and in the historic center, dated from the sources between the VI-IV century BC. (VisitLatium) and the beginning of the third century B.C. (Circle Park).

Picco di Circe e sentiero 750

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The summit of 540 meters is reached by path no. 750 between woods and Mediterranean maquis, starting from Torre Paola on the seafront of Sabaudia or from Piazzale delle Crocette in the upper part of San Felice; from the promontory the view embraces dunes, coast of Sabaudia, lakes of the Park and Gulf of Gaeta to the Pontine islands. One way goes up through one holm oak, the other along the side of the mountain.

Grotte marine del promontorio

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Along the coast of the promontory are the Blue Grotto, the Cave of the Nativity, the Capre, the Fossellone, the Hanging and the Maga Circe, with the coastal places of the Battery, Punta Rossa, the Cava dell'Alabaster and the Precipice; the boat ride around the promontory is among the suggested experiences. In the caves of the Circeo were found traces of human presence of the Middle Paleolithic.

Torri pontificie e Torre Olevola

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Round three-storey coastal towers wanted by Pope Pius IV in the second half of the sixteenth century against pirates and built by the Caetans along the cliff; Moresca, Cervia and Fico were destroyed by British ships in 1809. The Olevola Tower, one of the 52 coastal towers of Lazio, was erected around 1560 on the remains of a watchtower, is considered perfectly preserved and is among the historic residences of Lazio.
