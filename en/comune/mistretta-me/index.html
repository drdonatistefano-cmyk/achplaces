What to see in Mistretta

Village of the Nebrodi Park on a panoramic hill about 1000 meters, dominated by the ruins of the Arab-Norman Castle: a historic center with 25 churches, including the Mother Church of Saint Lucia, noble palaces, Porta Palermo and many fountains, the Regional Museum of Silvo-pastoral Traditions and, in the surroundings, Lake Quattrocchi and the Cascate di Ciddia.

Museo regionale delle tradizioni silvo-pastorali «Giuseppe Cocchiara»

Museums · Themed

It is located in the eighteenth century former Palace of Justice, born as a convent with the adjoining church of the Souls of Purgatory. About 1,500 exhibits and thousands of photographs, videos and oral documents tell the cycles of wheat, wine and oil, shepherds and breeders, charcoal makers, woodcutter and hunters and pastoral aesthetics; there are also a naturalistic section curated by the Nebrodi Park Authority, one on the traditional culture of Mistretta, touch screen and an immersive hall.

  • Where Via Libertà 184, Mistretta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Chiesa Madre di Santa Lucia (Santuario della Madonna dei Miracoli)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Mother Church, also known as the Sanctuary of the Madonna dei Miracoli, dates back to the Norman era: it has a characteristic bell tower, a carved portal and works of art in the aisles and chapels.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Centro storico, palazzi nobiliari e Porta Palermo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center has 25 churches, including the SS. Trinity, the oratory of the SS. Salvatore and San Giovanni, and numerous fountains fed by springs. Porta Palermo is the only remnant of the medieval walls, today incorporated into more recent buildings; among the palaces stand Palazzo Scauto, among the oldest, with statues and bas-reliefs, the eighteenth-century Palazzo Russo and the nineteenth-century Palazzo Tita, Salomone-Giaconia and Passarello.
