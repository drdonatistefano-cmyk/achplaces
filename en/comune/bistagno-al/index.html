What to see in Bistagno

Borgo della Valle Bormida refounded in 1253 as a villa fortified by the bishop of Acqui Enrico Del Carretto and home of the sculptor Giulio Monteverde and the statesman Giuseppe Saracco: to see the Gipsoteca Monteverde, the Sanctuary of the Pieve, the parish church of San Giovanni Battista with the medieval tower and the red Big Bench of Roncogennaro.

4 places to visit in Bistagno, in the province of Alessandria (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Gipsoteca Giulio Monteverde

Museums · Modern art

Civic museum with the original plaster models of the sculptor Giulio Monteverde, born in Bistagno in 1837, which cover his production from 1870 to 1917; part of the collection was donated by his daughters in Genoa in 1919 and partly returned to Bistagno with donations and loans. Open Saturday and Sunday from spring to autumn, in other periods by reservation (tel. +39 0144 79301, +39 366 5442354); adheres to Castelli Aperti.

  • Where Corso Carlo Testa 3, Bistagno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it
