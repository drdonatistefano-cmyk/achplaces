What to see in Casoria

At the gates of Naples, Casoria houses the CAM, a contemporary art museum founded in 2005 and reopened in 2025 in renewed spaces, and the baroque Basilica of San Mauro, the oldest church in the city.

CAM Casoria Contemporary Art Museum

Museums · Modern art

Founded in 2005 and directed by Antonio Manfredi, it is a center of research, production and exhibition of contemporary art with painting, sculpture, photography, video, multimedia art and installations, a section on Neapolitan art from the second post-war to today and one on contemporary African art. After more than a year of closure, it reopened on 17 January 2025 in completely renovated spaces: 2,000 sqm exhibition, a permanent collection of more than 2,200 works, concert area, theatre space, art library and area for temporary exhibitions.

Basilica di San Mauro

Places of worship · Basilicas

It is the main and oldest church of Casoria, attested by 1092; the present building, Baroque, was built between 1606 and 1621 and completed in the seventeenth century, and the Municipality is patron and owner of it. It houses works by Pietro di Martino and Giovan Vincenzo d'Onofrio called Forlì, including the altarpiece of the Madonna di Monserrato (1599), the last paintings by Domenico Antonio Vaccaro, a pipe organ of the eighteenth century and a ceiling with golden drawers of local carvers.

  • Where Largo San Mauro, Casoria

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
