What to see in Troina

At 1,120 meters in the Nebrodi Park, Troina was in 1060 the seat of Count Ruggero and the first Norman capital of Sicily, with the first Norman cathedral of the island; today you can visit the Torre Capitania with the portrait of Paolo III of Tiziano, the museum of war photos of Robert Capa, the medieval quarter Scalforio, the ruins of the Basilian monasteries of San Michele, Monte Muganà and Lake Sartori.

Centro storico e Piazza Conte Ruggero

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The centre preserves narrow medieval streets and, from above, views of Mount Etna, the Ionian Sea with the Gulf of Augusta and the Nebrodi woods. The square dedicated to Count Ruggero is the heart of the city that in 1060 the Norman chose as its headquarters, the first capital of Sicily; next to the square the Palazzo Pretura houses the museum dedicated to Robert Capa.

Ex Cattedrale normanna di Maria Santissima Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

First Norman cathedral of Sicily, probably born as the first church of the Latin rite after the expulsion of the Arabs and became cathedral in 1082, when Troina had the first bishopric of the island; it has a beautiful Norman tower. The Oratory of the SS. Sacramento replaces the crypt and transept and the Oratory of the Whites, under the church, preserves the predella d'altar on which Pope Urban II celebrated Mass in 1088.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Castello e cittadella normanna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The castle, built on sections of the Hellenistic walls on the rocky plateau of the mountain, was taken to the Saracens by the Grand Count Ruggero with the help of the Basilian monks; the fortified citadel extended for over a kilometer from the Mother Church to the ruins of the Church of the Name of Jesus, protected by overhanging walls and walls with towers. There are also sections of the city and the Hellenistic-Roman walls of the 4th century BC.

  • Where Troina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Complesso Museale Torre Capitania

Museums · City history

The 12th-century Mastio of the Arab-Norman citadel, seat of the Captain of Justice and under the Borbons sentmental prison until the 1970s. It houses the Civic Art Gallery "Gaetano Zito" (since 2022) with the Portrait of Paolo III Farnese in Tiziano, the only work of the painter to the south of Naples; the Archaeological Antiquarium with exhibits of about 6,000 years from the excavations of the Universities of Messina and Cambridge; the Civic Lapidarium and the exhibition of the ancient book, with a History of Italy of Guicciardini of 1604. Times indicated: 10-13 and 16.30-19.30.

Museo della Fotografia di Robert Capa

Museums · Themed

Opened on October 1, 2021 in the Palazzo Pretura, it is entirely dedicated to the photojournalist: the collection "Fragments of War in Sicily" brings together 62 vintage prints, many unpublished, from the original negatives taken by Capa in Sicily during the Second World War, with battlefields, destruction and civil life. Closed on Monday; Tuesday-Sunday 10-12.30 and 16.30-19; admission fee.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Chiesa di San Silvestro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises where in the second half of the thirteenth century was found the body of the patron, the Basilian monk Silvestro; completed in 1436, it incorporates the chapel of St. Bartholomew and the cave of the discovery and has the current appearance from the restorations of the early nineteenth century: three naves, dome, monolithic corinthian columns, neoclassical facade and late Renaissance walnut choir. The tomb of the saint, with an alabaster slab attributed to the school of the Gagines, is in a chapel behind a gate. It is the starting point of the pilgrimages of the Feast of Rami.

Quartiere medievale Scalforio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Beyond the bell tower, it marked the end of the fortified city: the name, of Arabic origin, means "outside the walls." There are the medieval church of San Biagio, that of Santa Lucia and San Nicolò, with an arch under the bell tower that was perhaps one of the city gates.

Monastero di San Michele Arcangelo «il vecchio»

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Made by Count Ruggero after the victory over the Saracens, it is indicated as the first monastery built by the Normans in Sicily: a Benedictine-Cluniac church surrounded by walls, where the monk Silvestro lived. Abandoned after the first half of the eighteenth century, today it is a ruin on a hill about 3 km from the center.

access to the ruins forbidden for safety, visit only from outside; rain, snow or strong wind can limit it

Monastero di San Michele Arcangelo «il nuovo»

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Built in the mid-eighteenth century by the Basilian monks on an ancient Hellenistic necropolis; the ruins remain the facade, the arches, the entrances and the balconies with Baroque stone decorations, and the monumental staircase that climbed to the abbey district, once adorned with golden stuccoes.

access to the ruins forbidden for security, visit only from outside

Monte Muganà

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A height of 1,121 meters northwest of the village: on the south wall a castelluccian rock necropolis (2300-1700 BC) with ten small cells dug into the rock. The linear naturalistic path of about 1.3 km (average difficulty, ±100 m) has center visits, picnic areas, panoramic point, astronomical position and on the summit a giant bench, with views of the Etna and the Gulf of Augusta; the sky is classified as "gold" by Astronomicaly.

  • Where Monte Muganà, Troina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.troina.en.it · italia.it

Lago Sartori (diga Ancipa)

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The highest artificial basin in Sicily, about 1,000 meters away, supplies with drinking water much of the internal Sicily; it is surrounded by 4,200 hectares of state forest in the Nebrodi Park, suitable for walking and horseback riding.

  • Entry Free
