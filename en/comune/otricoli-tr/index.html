What to see in Otricoli

Borgo sul Tevere enclosed by medieval walls with Porta Maior and Porta San Severino; at its feet the Archaeological Park of Ocriculum, a Roman city on the Via Flaminia with theatre, thermal baths, amphitheatre and necropolis, whose findings are told in the Antiquarium of the Palazzo Priorale and in that of Casale San Fulgenzio.

Parco archeologico di Ocriculum

History and architecture · Archaeology

Remains of the Roman city built at the bottom near the Tiber: forum, theater, thermal baths, water lilies, a stretch of the ancient Via Flaminia, funerary monuments, amphitheatre and the oldest necropolis, of the VII century BC; the "great substruttures," buildings of 12 rooms on two floors, have still uncertain function. The park is also habitat of various flora and fauna.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Antiquarium di Casale San Fulgenzio

Museums · Archaeological

Inside the archaeological park, built on a Roman cistern, it preserves vases, urns and sculptures found in the excavations between 1960 and 2005.

  • Where Parco archeologico di Ocriculum, Otricoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Antiquarium comunale (Palazzo Priorale)

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 2000 in the Prioral Palace, it illustrates the phases of the city with originals and replicas: the copy of the polychrome mosaic discovered in 1780 in the octagonal room of the thermal baths (centaurs and Greeks around a head of Medusa, original to the Vatican Museums), a statue of Augustus from the basilica of the forum, funeral inscriptions of the Via Flaminia (I century BC-II century AD), an altar table of the 6th century and the only cast of the colossal head of the Jupiter of Otricoli, discovered at the end of the 18th century.

  • Where Palazzo Priorale, Otricoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Centro storico, mura e porte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village is surrounded by medieval walls with Porta Maior and Porta San Severino, defensive towers (three rounds and two squares) and remains of a fortress of the fourteenth century; Casa Squarti-Perla has a portal that combines fragments of different eras and in the village remain two proffers in Viterbo style.

  • Where Centro storico, Otricoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Collegiata di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The pre-Romanesque church of the 7th century has been modified several times, preserving fragments of ancient architecture, altarpieces of various eras and finds of the excavations of the 20th century.

Chiesa del Santissimo Salvatore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
