What to see in Luogosanto

Granite village of Gallura on the slopes of the Ghjuanni Mountains, between Tempio Pausania and Palau, Luogosanto has 22 sanctuaries between village and country: in the center the basilica of Our Lady, the first Franciscan church of the north-east Sardinian, just outside the hermitage of San Trano. Nearby there are the ruins of the castle of Balaiana with the small church of San Leonardo and the Palazzo di Baldu in the ancient villa of Sent Steva.

Basilica di Nostra Signora di Luogosanto

Places of worship · Basilicas

Built around 1218 by the Franciscans, it is considered the first Franciscan church in the north-east of Sardinia; in 1227 Honorius III raised it to a minor basilica, the first in Gallura. In squared granite and Romanesque forms, it retains the three-nave system; it was rebuilt in the eighteenth century, when it obtained the privilege of the Holy Door, and enlarged in 1912. It preserves the wooden Madonna called "Queen of Gallura," frescoes with the Baptism of Jesus and Saints Nicolò and Trano; the bronze door of Luca Luchetti (1970s) opens every seven years for a year, September 8, the feast of the Nativity of Mary.

Eremo di San Trano

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Small chapel of the 13th century built on a large granite mass, where according to tradition in 1227 two Franciscan friars found the bodies of the hermits Nicola and Trano. The cave in which the saints lived is embedded in the wall and acts as a natural apse above the altar, made from a single block of granite; a single nave, granite floor and a roof with exposed beams. On clear days you can see the mouths of Bonifacio and Corsica; at the beginning of June the saints are celebrated with an evening procession.

Castello di Balaiana e chiesa di San Leonardo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the top of Mount San Leonardo, about 4 km from the village towards Arzachena: a holiday residence of the judges of Gallura, already built in the first half of the 12th century and destroyed by the Aragonese in the 15th. There remains a rectangular three-room building, the remains of a tower, traces of double walls in granite blocks without mortar and a cistern. A few meters west is the chapel of the castle, San Leonardo, of the twelfth century: among the smallest Romanesque churches on the island, all granite with roof in slabs of shale. You climb up with a staircase in the rock of about 100 m.

Palazzo di Baldu e villa di Sent Steva

History and architecture · Archaeology

Medieval complex of about 1,379 m2 in the locality of Santu Stevanu, in the ancient curatory of Balaiana: a quadrangular tower about 10 meters high on three floors and about twenty environments around a pentagonal courtyard, among warehouses, kitchens, stables and metalworking workshops. The tradition ties him to Lamberto Visconti or to Judge Giovanni (1238), in memory of his father Ubaldo; for masonry and openings is interpreted as residence or administrative center rather than as a castle. On the western side there is the church of Santo Stefano, from the XVII-XVIII century on a medieval church, and next to a brick furnace emerged from the excavations.

Museum Natività Beata Vergine Maria

Museums · City history

It is located in the Franciscan monastery founded at the beginning of the 13th century, among the first made with Saint Francis still alive. Renovated, it houses a documentation centre on the Middle Ages in Gallura and exhibits the ex-vote to the Madonna bambina.
