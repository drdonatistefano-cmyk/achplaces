What to see in Campi Salentina

In the heart of Salento, Campi Salentina was called "granary of Terra d'Otranto" for its agricultural tradition; the historic center has the mother church of Santa Maria delle Grazie, the marquisal palace of 1627, the "curti" and numerous noble palaces, and in the countryside the menhir Candido and Sperti. It is the birthplace of actor Carmelo Bene.

Chiesa madre di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church dominates the central square. Founded in the fifteenth century, it preserves some late-dogotic frescoes of the original structure; the façade has three orders of columns (1579) and the Baroque portal was carved in 1638.

  • Where Piazza centrale, Campi Salentina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Palazzo marchesale (Paladini-Enriquez)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The Paladini-Enriquez residence was built in 1627 above a previous castle, of which traces remain on the eastern side.

Centro storico, «curti» e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The old town has many historical monuments and residences: the typical "curti" (curti), many small churches and palaces Bari, Calabrese, Catarozzolo, Maddalo, Magi and Occhineri, as well as the brand residence.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
