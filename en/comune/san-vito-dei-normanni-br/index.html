What to see in San Vito dei Normanni

The centre of the flat toast with the Basilica of Santa Maria della Vittoria, whose cult dates back to the return of the Sanvitesi from Lepanto in 1571, and in the countryside two frescoed rock churches: the Crypt of San Biagio, with frescoes dating back to 1196, and the Crypt of San Giovanni along the Royal Canal.

Cripta di San Biagio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church excavated in the rocky bank, part of a monastic settlement of the twelfth century; today it is a single irregular environment with lateral entrance, but the traces indicate an ancient iconostasis. An inscription in Greek given the frescoes in 1196: it is one of the few examples of rock church decorated with Christological scenes (Annunciation, Nativity, Escape into Egypt, Presentation at the Temple, Entrance to Jerusalem), with stories of the saint, episodes of the Gospels apocryphal and saints of the East and West. Guided visits, tel. 0831 955236.

Basilica di Santa Maria della Vittoria (Chiesa Madre)

Places of worship · Basilicas

Church of 16th century origin, remodelled in Baroque style in the 18th century and proclaimed Pontifical Basilica in 1998; the worship of the icon of the Mother of God generating Victories dates back to 1571, after the return of the Sanvitesi from Lepanto. Baroque façade with bronze portal, inside a Latin cross with three naves; preserves paintings by Paolo De Maio (Birth of the Virgin, Crucifixion, Last Supper), the Last Judgment of Barnaba Zizzi (1816), the silver reliquary of San Vito, an eighteenth-century organ case and 16 wooden sculptures of the Passion of the early nineteenth century.

Cripta di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church in a 13th century cave, modified in the 15th and 16th centuries, dug into the ridges along the Royal Canal behind the Capharo Piccolo farmhouse, in a complex with two other caves. Rectangular plan with two naves divided by a pillar with three arches in the sixth; thirteenth century frescoes in the apse and on the south wall, including perhaps an Archangel, and inscriptions in Latin and Arabic. In the same valley the cave of the seven rooms is today largely underground. Tel. 0831 955235.

Chiesa della Madonna della Pietà o dell'Ospedale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1559 and profoundly remodelled in the last decade of the eighteenth century, it was annexed to a shelter for the poor and travellers then demolished, hence the name. Small temple with square plan with table on two levels marked by pilasters; today it is in convenient to the Municipality. Tel. 0831 955236.
