What to see in Giugliano in Campania

On the left bank of Lake Patria, north of Cuma, rise the remains of Liternum, Roman maritime colony founded in 194 BC for veterans of the second Punic war: forum, Capitolium, basilica, theatre, a stretch of Via Domitiana and, little to the south, the amphitheatre.

Parco archeologico di Liternum

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Roman maritime colony founded in 194 BC on the left bank of Lake Patria and assigned to the veterans of the second Punic war; flourished in Augustian age and between the end I and II century A.D., then decayed with the impaludation of the area. The hole with Capitolium, basilica and theatre was discovered in 1932; in the urban park of over 67,000 m2, recently redeveloped, you can see a spa complex with a bath still fed by natural springs, vaulted substruttuzioni, a wall in cross-linked work and over 80 meters of basolato of the Via Domitiana. Part of the artifacts is exhibited at the Museo Archeologico dei Campi Flegrei in Baia.

Anfiteatro di Liternum

History and architecture · Archaeology

Immediately south of the ancient city; a limited excavation identified its limits and at least two phases: probably built in the second century BC with at least two entrances (north and south), it was expanded and renovated between I and II century A.D. Like Pompeii's, it looks like it's got no basement. You can reach it from the exit Lake Tangenziale or by bus EAV 914 from Fuorigrotta.
