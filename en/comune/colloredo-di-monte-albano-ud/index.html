What to see in Colloredo di Monte Albano

Municipality of the morainic hills to the north of Udine dominated by the castle of Colloredo Mels Waldsee, started in 1302, where Ippolito Nievo wrote the "Confessions of an Italian"; in September 2026 the Region returned the castle to the community with the reopening of the Ala Nievo. In the territory there are also the tower of the ancient castle of Mels and, in Entesano, the church of San Zenone with a cycle of seventeenth-century frescoes in the presbytery.

Castello di Colloredo di Monte Albano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Erected from 1302 by William of Waldsee and completed by his sons, the first Colloredo Mels Waldsee; destroyed in 1315 by the Gorizians and rebuilt, passed to Venice in 1420, was burned by the peasants and damaged by the earthquake in 1511 and rebuilt in the sixteenth century, with the monumental staircase of 1626. On a ridge of the morainic hills includes mastium, west and east wings, guard body, tower door, red house and three towers; Ippolito Nievo, related to the Colloredos, wrote the "Confessions of an Italian" there. After the long post-sysm recovery, on 19-20 September 2026 the Region returned it to the community reopening the Nievo Wing; the West Wing is the seat of the Friuli Hill Community and cultural center.

Torre di Mels

History and architecture · Towers

Last rest of Mels Castle, built between 1026 and 1045 and granted by the Tirol counts in fiefdom to the Waldsees; burned in 1262 and partly destroyed in 1352 by the patriarch Nicolò of Luxembourg, stood on the hill north of the town with quadrangular plan, crenellated wall and three terraces. The ancient square tower remains, partly rebuilt after the 1976 earthquake, with fourteenth-century houses along the belt; the church annexed to St. Andrew was consecrated in 1056.

Chiesa di San Zenone a Entesano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The church is isolated to the south of Entesano, within the stone wall of the ancient cemetery: a single nave, a hut façade with stone and oculus portal, brick bell tower of 1907. From the first building, from the late fifteenth or early sixteenth century, remains the presbytery, decorated with a cycle of frescoes probably seventeenth century with stories of Mary and Jesus, saints, prophets and angels, attributed by tradition to Matteo Leban from Tolmino. Damaged by the 1976 earthquake and long abandoned, it was consolidated and restored from 2013 to 2016.
