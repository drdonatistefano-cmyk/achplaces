What to see in Massafra

Taranto inland town in the Regional Park Terra delle Gravine, divided by the gravina of San Marco in the districts of Terra and Santa Caterina, connected by a dizzying bridge; in the gravines there are about thirty Byzantine rock churches, and the city was the set of the Capernaum of the Gospel according to Matthew of Pasolini (1964).

Centro storico e gravina di San Marco

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The grenade of San Marco cuts into two the center: on the one hand the Terra district, an intrica of steep alleys, on the other the Santa Caterina district, built in the nineteenth century on an orthogonal plan. The two districts are joined by a high viaduct, the Garibaldi Bridge, from which you can see the churches and cave settlements overlooking the gravina. The name of the city would come from the Latin "massa fracta" (fracta), a fractured rock.

  • Where Ponte Garibaldi, Massafra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa Madre di San Lorenzo Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The most important sixteenth-century building in Massafra, consecrated on 11 February 1582 and renovated in the eighteenth century; the façade, with rose window and Renaissance portal surmounted by the angel called "Sant'Misiriedd" has a defensive battlements that recall the nearby castle. Inside, the main altar is framed by the Baroque wooden machine of 1783 and the Cappellone del Sacramento (1566) preserves intact the temples of the vault; under the apse, visible through a glass plate, a rock church discovered in the restorations of 1996.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Massafra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.massafra.ta.it · italia.it

Castello di Massafra

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The castle is mentioned for the first time in a Longobard document of the tenth century and was certainly a fortified building in Norman age. Today it houses the Historical and Archaeological Museum of the Civilization of Oil and Wine.

Santuario della Madonna della Scala

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

We descend with a Baroque staircase of more than one hundred steps; the sanctuary, begun in 1729 on a chapel older and open to worship in 1737, stands on ancient Basilian laureates. On the high altar the icon of an Odegitria della greca della XII secolo, flanked by two marble deer for the legend of the discovery; inside, three naves, canvases and organ of the eighteenth century. The Madonna della Scala has been the main patron of Massafra since 1776.

Chiesa rupestre della Buona Nuova

History and architecture · Archaeology

The 7th-century rock church, accessible from the churchyard of the sanctuary of the Madonna della Scala. The frescoes include a Virgin of the Good New Odegitria type of the late thirteenth century, a Christ Pantocrator with Deesis and a triptych with Saint Lucia, Saint Vitus and Saint Catherine of Alexandria.

Gravina e villaggio rupestre della Madonna della Scala

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Gravine almost 4 km long, about 40 m deep, with about 600 plant species, many rare and medicinal. It houses a rock village of neolithic origin, inhabited until the twelfth century, with more than 200 houses dug into the limestone: among these the Cave of the Cyclops, with blades of flint and fragments of prehistoric ceramics, and the "Farmacia of the Magic Greguro" , twelve communicating environments where according to legend the magician and his daughter Margheritella preserved medicinal herbs. Guided tours of about 3 hours, from Tuesday to Sunday, with reservation required two days before and minimum 10 participants; variable cost; do not recommend to people with motor disabilities.

  • Where SP581, Massafra (ritrovo all'info point di piazza Garibaldi)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa rupestre della Candelora

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

The 12th century rock church, considered a jewel of rock architecture for its multiple vaults. Among the paintings the Presentation of Jesus at the Temple, Jesus child walking by the hand of Our Lady and a Madonna Glykophilousa. You can visit the guided tour of the rock churches (about 2 hours on foot, at the info point of the Town Hall).

  • Entry €5 municipal ticket, plus fee of the guide

Chiesa rupestre di San Leonardo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Today, in the area of expansion of the city, until the 1950s, it stood among large olive groves. It contains well-readable frescoes, including a deep apse of great expressive power, and an almost intact iconostasis. It is part of the guided tour of the rock churches.

Chiesa rupestre di Sant'Antonio Abate

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

The underground church under the ancient hospital of Massafra, born from the union of two distinct churches, probably for the Eastern and Latin rites; the paintings range from approximately XI to XVII century. It is part of the guided route of the rock churches.
