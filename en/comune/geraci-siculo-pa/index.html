What to see in Geraci Siculo

Medieval village of the Madonie, already fortified citadel and residence of the Ventimiglia, known as the "Borgo degli Immortali" for its longevity: the ruins of the castle with the small church of Sant'Anna, the Bevaio della SS. Trinity, the Mother Church with the works of the Gagini and Geraci's treasure, the Salto dei Ventimiglia and, in the forest of Vicaretto, Santa Maria della Cava.

Chiesa Madre di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Consecrated in 1495, with simple Romanesque exterior and marble-rich interior: baptismal font in alabaster of the workshop of the Gagini (end of the fifteenth century), marble statues of the Madonna della Snow, Mercedes and with the Child, monumental wooden choir of 1650 and a sixteenth-century Annunciation. In the crypt, the Geraci's treasure, with liturgical objects in gold and silver, a fourteenth-century monstrance of Florentine goldsmith and embroidered vestments.

Ruderi del castello e chiesetta di Sant'Anna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The castle, probably of Byzantine origin and devoid of decorations, became a military fortress under the Ventimiglia; the ruins remain on a difficult height to reach, chosen for its strategic and panoramic position, reached by the Via Biscucco paved in stone. Next to it, the little church of St. Anne was the chapel of the Ventimiglia: according to legend the skull of the saint was kept there from 1242 and then transferred to Castelbuono.

Bevaio della SS. Trinità

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Ancient watering trough wanted by the Marquis Simone Ventimiglia, rectangular and about 20 meters long, with two lateral stone fountains and four mouths that pour the water in sandstone cups.

Santa Maria della Cava e bosco di Vicaretto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small church in the woods in Vicaretto district, annexed in Norman times to a Basilian cenobio (founded in 1090): Romanesque exterior with rose window and ogival portal, remains of Byzantine frescoes inside. The forest of Vicaretto is suitable for walks.

Centro storico, il «Borgo degli Immortali»

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small centre on a relief from which you can enjoy a wide panorama, which preserves the memory of fortified citadel; called "Borgo degli Immortali" (Borgo degli Immortali), it is part of the international network of the "Blue Zone" of longevity. Among the alleys there is the Largo Greco feritoia, the church of the Collegio di Maria (1738) in Piazza del Popolo and the churches of San Giuliano, San Francesco and San Rocco.
