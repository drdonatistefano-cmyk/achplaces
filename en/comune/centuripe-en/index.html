What to see in Centuripe

Village overlooking the valley of Simeto and Etna, ancient Greek-Roman city: the Regional Archaeological Museum with the head of Augustus and the Centuripini vases, the Roman monuments of the Castle of Corradino, the Customs and the Thermal Baths of Contrada Bagni, the churches of the center and the abandoned village of Carcaci.

5 places to visit in Centuripe, in the province of Enna (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo archeologico regionale di Centuripe

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

It tells the history of the city, a hub between the Plain of Catania and the interior, from the Neolithic and the Bronze Age to the growth from indigenous center to the Greek city and Roman civitas: Hellenistic houses with architectural terracotta and paintings, the famous centuripin vases with relief and polychrome, marble sculptures and epigraphs of the imperial age. Open entirely in June 2024 after ten years; there is exhibited, on loan from the Museo Paolo Orsi until 2026, a head of Augustus considered his best portrait found in Sicily.

  • Where Via Giulio Cesare 1, Centuripe
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · regione.sicilia.it · italia.it

Castello di Corradino

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Large Roman tomb of the II-III century A.D., then used as the base of a medieval tower; it is the highest point of the village, with spectacular views of Mount Etna.

  • Where Centuripe
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info

La Dogana e le Terme romane di Contrada Bagni

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

The so-called Customs is actually a Roman funeral structure of the II-III century AD; in the Bagni district remain the baths of the imperial age.

  • Where Contrada Bagni, Centuripe
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Chiesa Madre dell'Immacolata e chiese del centro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The 17th century Church of the Immaculate Conception is adorned with baroque stuccoes; the Church of the Crucifix has a majolica bell tower and that of St. Augustine holds a 16th century painting of the Madonna delle Grazie.

  • Where Centro storico, Centuripe
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Borgo di Carcaci

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry

An ancient village near the center, abandoned in the nineteenth century and today inhabited only by peacocks.

  • Where Carcaci, Centuripe
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
