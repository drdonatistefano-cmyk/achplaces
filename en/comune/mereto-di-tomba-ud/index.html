What to see in Mereto di Tomba

Municipality of the Friulian plain whose name recalls a funerary mound of the Bronze Age; in the hamlet of San Marco you can visit the collection of vintage bikes of Umberto and Flavio Nigris, in Tomb the church of San Michele with the sixteenth-century frescoes of Giulio Urbanis.

Museo moto d'epoca di Umberto e Flavio Nigris

Museums · Themed

In the hamlet of San Marco, a large salon exhibits a rich collection of motorcycles, mopeds and scooters ordered by factory and displacement; many pieces have been or still are restored by the owners of the collection. Open Monday to Friday 8-12 and 14-18, closed Saturday and Sunday, without reservation (tel. 0432 868049).

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo a Tomba

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises to the north of the town of Tomb on the site of an ancient centa; an octagonal chapel, perhaps of the X-XI century, was incorporated as an apse in the fourteenth-century church and in 1571-1572 Giulio Urbanis frescoed the vault (frescoes restored in 2013). The current building, with a single nave with Ionic pilasters, was built by the foreman Sebastiano Lotti of Bertiolo and inaugurated in 1764; the bell tower is from 1830. In the Great War she was sheltered for prisoners, then restored after the earthquake of 1976.

Tumulo dell'età del Bronzo di Tomba (Tùmbare)

History and architecture · Archaeology

To the southeast of Tomb, on the edge of a terrace of the river Corno, a funerary mound of the castellier culture: a platform of pebbles of about 20 m in diameter and 2 m in height, covered with earth and gravel held by poles and tables (the armed earth) started around 1750 BC and completed between 1620 and 1440 BC. The excavations of the University of Udine (2006-2008) found the skeleton of a young man with two objects related to the processing of metal and the remains of a cow and a horse sacrificed, among the oldest equine remains of Friuli. From here comes the name of the country.

Centa di Mereto e chiesa dei Santi Daniele e Agostino

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Medieval village fortification: a drawing from the late eighteenth century shows the church of Saints Daniel and Augustine surrounded by a wall with door tower, in turn surrounded by a second wall and a large moat, and until the sixteenth century before the curtain were held the assemblies of the village. Today, above all, there remains the rise of the ground, two-three meters high, on which rise the church and the cemetery, with the path of the moat still legible.
