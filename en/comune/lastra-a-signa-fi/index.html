Villa Caruso Bellosguardo e Museo Enrico Caruso

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The sixteenth-century villa bought in 1906 by Enrico Caruso, with the park of the Tribolo and the museum with its room and its caricatures.

  • Where Via Bellosguardo 54, Lastra a Signa
  • Entry Museum €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com

Mura di Lastra a Signa

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The walls of the medieval village, still visible in the historic centre.

  • Where Via del Prato, Lastra a Signa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com
