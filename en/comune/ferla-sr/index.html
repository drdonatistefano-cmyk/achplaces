What to see in Ferla

Ibleo village rebuilt after the earthquake of 1693, with the "Via Sacra" lineing five Baroque religious buildings (Carmine, San Sebastiano, Chiesa Madre, Sant'Antonio, Santa Maria), the medieval quarter of the Old Prisons and, a few kilometers away, the rock necropolis of Pantalica, UNESCO World Heritage site with Syracuse.

7 places to visit in Ferla, in the province of Syracuse (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Considered the most beautiful of the village: Baroque façade with rococo touches, bell towers (the left collapsed with the earthquake of 1908) and Greek cross plan with arms of 33 meters, unique in the south-east of Sicily. The octagonal dome is frescoed by Credadoro with the Triumph of St. Anthony; fourteen stucco statues depict the cardinal and theological virtues.

  • Where Via Vittorio Emanuele, Ferla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Chiesa di San Sebastiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The largest church of the village, built in 1741 on a project by Michelangelo di Giacomo, with three naves and eight chapels; the sculptural group of the facade is considered one of the most typical works of the Sicilian baroque statuary. It preserves the painting of the Martyrdom of St. Sebastian by Giuseppe Crestadoro (1789) and the wooden statue of the patron, survived the earthquake of 1693.

  • Where Via Vittorio Emanuele, Ferla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Via Sacra, Chiesa Madre, Carmine e Santa Maria

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Via Vittorio Emanuele is called "Via Sacra" for the five religious buildings that flank it among noble palaces: the Church of Carmine, with facade that unites doric and ionic order; the Mother Church, with the oldest preserved municipal coat of arms (1763) on the portal and inside full of stuccoes; the Church of Santa Maria, already convent, school and prison, with a wooden crucifix by Friar Umile from Petralia of 1633. In via Umberto civil buildings unite Baroque and Liberty.

  • Where Via Vittorio Emanuele, Ferla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Quartiere delle Carceri Vecchie, Castelverde e Calanconi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The Old Prisons district is the only medieval remnant that survived the earthquake of 1693, with narrow alleys, ancient houses and caves; from a Byzantine church starts a path of tombs and caves. Here and in the popular districts of Castelverde and Calanconi the doors preserve the "iattaruala" , hole for cats, and the "justieddu" , peephole to see without being seen.

  • Where Quartiere Carceri Vecchie, Ferla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Museo della Parrocchia e Casa della Memoria (Palazzo Mirabella)

Museums · Themed

In the Palazzo Mirabella, with its fine Baroque balcony, the museum has kept manuscripts and parchments since 1481, sacred vestments, statues, paintings and devotional objects from churches and convents of Ferla; the section Casa della Memoria reconstructs the furnishings of a typical peasant house of the village.

  • Where Via Garibaldi, Ferla
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Necropoli di San Martino

History and architecture · Archaeology

At about 1 km from the village, underground burials of Christian age obtained in caverns already used in the Bronze Age: the "Grotta di Dionisio" owes the name to the inscription of a local priest, the "Grotta di Sant'Anna" preserves a medieval fresco of the saint.

  • Where Contrada San Martino, Ferla
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Necropoli rupestre di Pantalica

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Rocky site between Sortino and Ferla, at the confluence of the valley of Calcinara in that of the Anapo: settlement of the late Bronze Age (XIII-VIII century BC) with almost five thousand graves carved into the rock, the largest rock necropolis in Europe, reoccupied in the Byzantine age with cave villages and churches like San Micidario. There is the Anaktoron, the only megalithic building of mycenaean type in Sicily. UNESCO World Heritage Site with Syracuse since 2005; from Ferla you can access the entrance near the village (about 11 km).

  • Where Ingresso lato Ferla, Pantalica
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · visitsicily.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
