What to see in Urbisaglia

A village walled on the Fiastra valley, heir to the Roman colony of Urbs Salvia, whose archaeological park of about 40 hectares is considered the most important and spectacular of the Marches; on top of the village dominates the Fortress, completed in 1507.

Parco archeologico e Museo archeologico statale di Urbs Salvia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

The Roman colony, founded in the Republican age and monumentalized by Augustus, was abandoned in late antiquity and for this reason it has been preserved in an exceptional way: a path of about one kilometer on 40 hectares descends from the hill towards the valley between cisterns of the aqueduct, Augustian walls, the theater (the most conspicuous of the Marches and the only in Italy with preserved painted decorations), the niche building, the temple of Salus Augusta with frescoed cryptoportico and the amphitheatre of the first century A.D. wanted by Lucio Flavio Silva. The State Archaeological Museum in the historic centre, founded in 1996, exhibits excavations.

limited seasonal openings (MiC: from March to October especially on weekends, 10:30-12:30 and 15-17); for the Marche Region the museum is temporarily closed after the earthquake; bookings tourist office 0733 50124

Rocca di Urbisaglia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built between the 13th and 15th centuries and completed in 1507, it dominates the village and the Fiastra valley, perhaps on the site of the ancient fortress of Urbs Salvia; it has trapezoidal plan, four towers and a mast with ghibelline merlons and is a national monument.

damaged by earthquakes 2016-2017, in 2023 approved the project of consolidation of the tower

Museo delle armi e delle uniformi

Museums · Themed

Set up in the eighteenth century former church of San Biagio, already shrine of the fallen of all wars, exhibits weapons, uniforms and relics from the seventeenth century and from the Risorgimento to the Second World War.

open by reservation (tel. 0733 512648); discordant price between sources (MiC €2, Region €3)
