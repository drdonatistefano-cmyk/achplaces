What to see in Apricale

Borgo della val Merdanzo, Orange Flag, with the square Vittorio Emanuele that looks like a theatre, the Castle of the Lizard with the museum of the history of the village, the churches of Sant'Antonio Abate and Santa Maria degli Angeli and the murals of the Sixties.

6 places to visit in Apricale, in the province of Imperia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello della Lucertola e Museo della storia di Apricale

Museums · City history · Viewpoint

Castle built on the fortress since the tenth century, destroyed in 1523 by order of Bishop Agostino Grimaldi; today of the municipality, houses the museum of the history of the village, art exhibitions and the Gallery of the Theatre with the scenery of Emanuele Luzzati. From the castle lies the bike of Sergio Bianco.

  • Where Via Castello, Apricale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it · cultura.gov.it

Piazza Vittorio Emanuele

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The high square among churches, town hall, porticos and ancient houses, where since 1990 the Teatro della Cosse of Genoa has been performing every August.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele, Apricale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Chiesa parrocchiale della Purificazione della Vergine e Oratorio di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish, largely rebuilt after the earthquake of 1887, with mosaic floor and windows; the oratory with eighteenth-century stuccoes and a polyptych of 1544.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele, Apricale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it
