What to see in San Leo

"A fortress and two churches," according to Umberto Eco: the Fort on a cliff at 639 meters where Cagliostro, the Pieve and the Romanesque Cathedral died.

3 places to visit in San Leo, in the province of Rimini (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Forte di San Leo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The fortress on the cliff, rebuilt for the Montefeltro, prison and place of death of the Count of Cagliostro.

  • Where San Leo
  • Entry €11 whole; €2 from 18 to 25 years; free up to 17 years; with Rocca and Verucchio Museum €14,50
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.san-leo.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: san-leo.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta e Duomo di San Leone

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The oldest church of Montefeltro and the Romanesque Cathedral with its bell tower: the "campo dei miracoli" of Romagna.

  • Where Piazza Dante, San Leo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo d'Arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

In the Medici Palace, works from the 8th to the 18th century by churches and convents of the territory.

  • Where Palazzo Mediceo, San Leo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
