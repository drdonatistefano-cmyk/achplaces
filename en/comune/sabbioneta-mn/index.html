What to see in Sabbioneta

The ideal city of Vespasiano Gonzaga Colonna, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2008 together with Mantua: inside the sixteenth-century walls with the Porta Vittoria and the Porta Imperiale you can visit Palazzo Ducale, the Teatro all'Antica di Vincenzo Scamozzi, Palazzo Giardino with the Galleria degli Antichi, the crowned with the tomb of the Duke and the Synagogue.

8 places to visit in Sabbioneta, in the province of Mantua (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo Ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage

The first building built by Vespasiano Gonzaga, overlooking Piazza Ducale, was built in two phases (1556 and 1590) on four levels and rebuilt in 1561 after a fire; it was the centre of public life of the small State. You can visit the Sala del Duca d'Alba, rich in wood and gold with the fireplace supported by pink marble lions, and the Sala delle Aquile with the four surviving wooden equestrian statues of the Riding (1587).

  • Where Piazza Ducale, Sabbioneta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Teatro all'Antica

History and architecture · Historic theatres · UNESCO World Heritage

Designed by Vincenzo Scamozzi and inaugurated in 1590 with the loggia of columns surmounted by statues of Olympic gods; it is considered a masterpiece of the Renaissance theatre, designed for acoustics. Restored in the second half of the twentieth century, the current fixed scene dates back to 1996.

  • Where Via Teatro, Sabbioneta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it · in-lombardia.it

Palazzo Giardino e Galleria degli Antichi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage

The Palazzo Giardino, known as "Casino" (Casino), the centre of Gonzaga's private life, was built between 1578 and 1588 and decorated by artists led by Bernardino Campi. Next to it, the Galleria degli Antichi (1584) is a unique hall 96 meters long with an external porch; in front, in Piazza d'Armi, the Column of Minerva holds a statue of Roman imperial age.

  • Where Piazza d'Armi, Sabbioneta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Mura e porte della città ideale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The walls of 1554 draw the six-pointed star plant with angular wedge bulwarks and are interrupted only by Porta Vittoria (the oldest, marble and brick) and Porta Imperiale (1579, rebuilt in the nineteenth century). Around the village run for about 18 km the surrounding embankments against the flood of Po and Oglio.

  • Where Porta Vittoria, Sabbioneta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Piazza Ducale e Chiesa dell'Assunta

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Rectangular piazza portico, always the heart of the city, with the Doge's Palace, the Palazzo del Cavalleggero and the parish church of the Assunta (1578-82) with its brick façade, with eighteenth-century decorations, the Chapel of Bibiena and a Crucifix donated by San Carlo Borromeo.

  • Where Piazza Ducale, Sabbioneta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa della Beata Vergine Incoronata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage

An octagonal church of Bramantesque inspiration (1586-88), with the tomb-mausoleum of Vespasiano Gonzaga and his bronze statue of Leone Leoni.

  • Where Sabbioneta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Museo di arte sacra «A passo d'uomo»

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Next to the parish church of the Assunta, with the ancient courtyard transformed into a botanical garden with marble fragments; it collects works of the diocese from the 16th to the 20th century and has a section in the former Oratory of San Rocco (16th century). Among the treasures reported by Touring the Golden Toson found in the tomb of Vespasian.

  • Where Via dell'Assunta, Sabbioneta
  • Entry €2.50, reduced €1.50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · italia.it
