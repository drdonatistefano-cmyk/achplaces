What to see in Codigoro

4 places to visit in Codigoro, in the province of Ferrara (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Abbazia di Pomposa e Museo pomposiano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Benedictine Abbey was born around the sixth century on the ancient Pomposia Insula: the church of Santa Maria with frescoes, the bell tower visible from afar and the museum. Here the monk Guido invented the modern musical notation.

  • Where Via Pomposa Centro 12, Codigoro
  • Entry €5 full, €2 reduced; free on the first Sunday of the month; only electronic payments
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Campanile di Pomposa

History and architecture · Towers · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The Romanesque bell tower erected in 1063 by Magister Deusdedit, in red and yellow bricks with ceramic basins imported from Egypt, Tunisia and Sicily.

  • Where Abbazia di Pomposa, Codigoro
  • Entry Included in the abbey visit
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · cultura.gov.it

Palazzo della Ragione di Pomposa

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The 11th century building where the abbots administered justice on the abbey's territories, once connected to the complex by a loggia.

  • Where Pomposa, Codigoro
  • Entry Included in the abbey visit
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Palazzo del Vescovo e Biblioteca Giorgio Bassani

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The eighteenth-century reconstruction in Venetian forms of the Domus dominicata pomposiana, today the municipal library and headquarters of the Fondazione Bassani.

  • Where Codigoro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
