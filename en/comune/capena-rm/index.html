What to see in Capena

Borgo 36 km from Rome, with its historic centre of paved alleys and Urbian gate, the Clock Tower, seat of the Civic Museum and the Altomedieval church of San Leone; in the territory the Roman city of Lucus Feroniae, with the sanctuary of the goddess Feronia and a state Antiquarium with free entry.

Area archeologica di Lucus Feroniae

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Roman city excavated since 1952: you can visit the Forum, the altar of the goddess Feronia, the basilica, the Augusteum, the spas of Menandro, a late-republican domus, a small amphitheatre and the baths of the Via Capanate. Between the end of the 4th and the beginning of the 5th century A.D. the city was abandoned and used as a quarry of precious materials. Open Tuesday-Saturday from 8.30 to one hour before sunset; Sunday only the first and third of the month.

  • Where Via Tiberina km 18,500, Capena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website sabapviterboetruria.cultura.gov.it

Sources: sabapviterboetruria.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Antiquarium di Lucus Feroniae

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

Built in 1977 as a warehouse of excavation materials and opened with a first exhibition nucleus in 1998, it presents in six sections finds from the protohistory to the imperial age, including the statuary cycle of the Augustales sacellus in a life-size reconstruction and the funeral monument with Gladiatory relief recovered in 2006 by Fiano Romano. Tuesday-Saturday 8.30-19.30; Sunday only the first and third of the month.

  • Where Via Tiberina km 18,500, Capena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: sabapviterboetruria.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Centro storico e mura medievali

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The narrow streets paved with Roman stones, small squares, arches and wooden passages and the Urbian gate; in the medieval walls mosaic benches are set with flowers, birds, ducks and cats, the work of Rosina Wachtmeister and grandson Giovanni Battista Rea. On the main square the sculpture "Il Momento" (9 meters) by the Hungarian Ervin Lorianth Hervé, a man leaning on a building that looks at the landscape.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Capena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Torre dell'Orologio e Museo civico

Museums · City history

In Piazza del Popolo, the tower houses the Civic Museum that tells the story of Capena from the fall of the Roman Empire to today; the clock marks the hours to Italian, with a single hand that makes four laps in the twenty-four hours.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Capena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Chiesa di San Leone

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

High medieval jewel with two naves among the centuries-old cypresses of the ancient cemetery; it preserves an intact iconostasis and ancient frescoes, including one of the IX century.

  • Where Cimitero antico, Capena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
