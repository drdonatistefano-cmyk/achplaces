What to see in Varese

The garden city: the eighteenth-century Palazzo Estense, a small Versailles for Leopardi, the Sacred Mount UNESCO World Heritage with its 14 chapels, Villa Panza del FAI and the villas museum among age-old parks.

9 places to visit in Varese, in the province of Varese (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.
