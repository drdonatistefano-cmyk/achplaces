What to see in Prato

A city of textiles from the elegant medieval old town, with the Duomo of Filippo Lippi and Donatello, the only Swabian castle in the centre-north and the Pecci Centre for Contemporary Art.

25 places to visit in Prato, in the province of Prato (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 8 of the 13 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Piazza del Duomo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The oldest nucleus of the city, a scene from the fourteenth century of festivals, markets and the exhibition of the Sacro Cingolo, with buildings largely fourteenth-century.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Prato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Cattedrale di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Paid entry

Gothic-Romanesque cathedral in alberese and green marble of Prato, with frescoes by Filippo Lippi on the stories of Santo Stefano and the Baptist and the Chapel of the Sacred Cingolo, exhibited every year in September.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Prato
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Pulpito di Donatello e Michelozzo

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry

The pulpit outside the corner of the facade, carved by Donatello and Michelozzo in 1433-1438 for the exhibition of the Sacred Cingolo; the original reliefs are in the Museum of the Opera.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Prato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo dell'Opera del Duomo

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Museo di Palazzo Pretorio

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the thirteenth-century palace about three thousand works on the history of Prato: Bernardo Daddi, Giovanni da Milano, Donatello, Filippo and Filippino Lippi, Santi di Tito, Allori and the gipsoteca by Lorenzo Bartolini.

  • Where Piazza del Comune, Prato
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Castello dell'Imperatore

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The only example of Swabian architecture in central-north Italy, wanted by Frederick II and built between 1237 and 1248 by Riccardo da Lentini, with portal to white and green bands and Swabian lions; in summer outdoor cinema.

  • Where Piazza Santa Maria delle Carceri, Prato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
