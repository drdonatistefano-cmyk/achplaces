What to see in Ceres

Centre of the Lanzo Valleys and terminus of the Turin-Ceres railway: in the Town Hall square the Museum of the Lanzo Valleys, then the eighteenth-century parish church of the Assumption, the donkey-back bridge of the Vana (1740), the station of 1915 and, in the village Voragno, the Chapel of the Holy Shroud with probably sixteenth-century frescoes.

Museo delle Genti delle Valli di Lanzo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

It rediscovers traditions and ancient crafts with everyday objects and work tools, rebuilding scenes of life disappeared with old maps and vintage photographs. The route is divided into themes: the Lanzo Valleys, the people, agriculture and sheep farming, handicrafts, flora, fauna and mineralogy; curious the collection of nails, once forged in the valley and exported throughout Italy. From May to October the first and third Sundays of the month, 15-18; in the other periods by telephone reservation (333 4825771). Accessible to the disabled.

  • Where Piazza Municipio 12, Ceres
  • Entry €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.ceres.to.it

Sources: comune.ceres.to.it · turismotorino.org

Stazione di Ceres

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Building on three floors built in 1915 according to project of the engineer. Scotti, like the other stations of the mountain section of the Turin-Ceres railway; the Lanzo-Ceres alpine stretch was inaugurated in 1916.

  • Where Stazione ferroviaria, Ceres
