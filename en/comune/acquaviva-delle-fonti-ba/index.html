What to see in Acquaviva delle Fonti

Centre of the Bari Murge collected around the square of the Cathedral, one of the four palate basilicas of Puglia, and Palazzo De Mari, ancient fortified residence today seat of the Municipality; outside the center the Cave of Curtomartino opens.

3 places to visit in Acquaviva delle Fonti, in the province of Bari (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale di Sant'Eustachio

Places of worship · Cathedrals

One of the four palatine basilicas of Puglia is the heart of the historic centre and is located on the same square as Palazzo De Mari.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Palazzo De Mari

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

An ancient fortified residence overlooking the central square, next to the Cathedral; today it houses the seat of the Municipality.

  • Where Centro storico, Acquaviva delle Fonti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Grotta di Curtomartino

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Karst cavities outside the town, known for the rare plants and orchids growing at the entrance and for stalactites and ancient stalagmites, testimony of the distant geological past of the area.

  • Where Località Curtomartino, Acquaviva delle Fonti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
