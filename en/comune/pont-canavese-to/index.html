What to see in Pont Canavese

Borgo at the confluence between Orco and Soana, gate of the Canavesian side of the Gran Paradiso National Park: it preserves the ruins of the medieval Ferranda and Tellaria towers, contended between Valperga and San Martino, and two museums, the ethnographic of the ancient crafts and the plastic museum pole in the former Sandretto factory.

Museo etnografico di antichi mestieri

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Founded in 1996 by the association Ij Canteir to enhance the traditions of the Orco and Soana Valleys, it reconstructs with mannequins, vintage clothes and tools the shops of rust, segantino, magnin (raio), slippers, carpenter, blacksmith, glassmaker, miner and chimney sweep; complete the path agricultural tools, a room on the costumes of the Alpine valleys and a research on the ancient shops of Via del Commercio. Open on Sunday afternoon in the summer season, on other days by reservation (tel. 340 6115520, 349 4975573).

  • Where Via Roscio 2, Pont Canavese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pontcanavese.to.it

Sources: comune.pontcanavese.to.it · cultura.gov.it · turismotorino.org

Polo museale e Museo della plastica Cannon Sandretto

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

In the Art Nouveau building of the former Sandretto plant, with a small park: the collection, started in 1985 by Gilberto Sandretto, has over 2,500 pieces in eight rooms, from hand painted calendars-posters to an injection press by the Brothers Sandretto, with a last room on the environmental impact of plastic. Re-opened in 2016 by the Cannon Group, the polo shirt also includes the Textile Manufacture Museum and the Cav Machine Tools Collection. Modesto Sandretto. Open Sundays and holidays from April to October, with guided tours every hour from 15 to 19 (season 2026).

  • Where Via Cav. Modesto Sandretto 16, Pont Canavese
  • Entry €5, free under 12 and Museums Subscription
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pontcanavese.to.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.pontcanavese.to.it

Torre Tellaria

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

X-XI century tower facing the Orco Valley, part of a Castrum dei San Martino that from the hill to the entrance of the valley controlled the shelter below; rivals Valperga owned instead the Ferranda tower. It was sacked in 1383 during Tuchinaggio, was destroyed in 1552 by the troops of Caesar Maggi in the Spanish money and since then it is a ruin, visible only from the outside.

  • Where Località Tellario, Pont Canavese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.pontcanavese.to.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.pontcanavese.to.it
