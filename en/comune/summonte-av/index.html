What to see in Summonte

Borgo del Partenio among the most beautiful villages in Italy since 2017, symbolize the Angevin Tower with the civic museum; you can also see the historic center with the Arch of San Nicola, the Baroque churches, the Grancia of Santa Maria del Preposito and the path to Campo San Giovanni.

7 places to visit in Summonte, in the province of Avellino (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre angioina e Museo civico

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

16-metre-high tower, built between the 13th and 14th centuries on the ruins of the Norman-Svevo castle; it is the symbol of the village, it houses a permanent exhibition on the history of the territory and the civic museum and offers a wide panorama of the Partenio Regional Park, the Sanctuary of Montevergine and the Gulf of Naples.

  • Where Via Castello, Summonte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Centro storico e palazzi cinquecenteschi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The center is spread in three directions from Via Borgonuovo, with Via Varra, Via Castello and the Arch of San Nicola, ancient gate of the village with a newsstand dedicated to San Nicola di Bari; there are sixteenth-century palaces Brosca, Montella and De Cristofaro, with inner courtyard and monumental entrance.

  • Where Via Borgonuovo, Summonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Nicola di Bari

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Baroque intervention of the seventeenth century on an existing structure; the main altar of 1717 is in polychrome marble with inlays.

  • Where Centro storico, Summonte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of 1559 with a single central altar and a round arch decorated with frescoes on the life of San Vito.

  • Where Centro storico, Summonte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Grancia di Santa Maria del Preposito

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

The most important complex of the territory: dates back to the 10th century, it was given to the abbey of Montevergine around 1174 and since 1229 it entered the new farmhouse of Fontanelle, whose castle and fortifications are documented since 1094.

  • Where Territorio di Summonte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Tiglio monumentale

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

In front of the town hall grows a 34-metre-high linden, included among the Monumental Trees of Italy.

  • Where Davanti al Municipio, Summonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Sentiero Summonte – Campo San Giovanni

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

6 km walk from 750 to 1224 meters up to the Forcetelle refuge; along the way you will find the church of San Silvestro of the 11th century.

  • Where Partenza dal paese, Summonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
