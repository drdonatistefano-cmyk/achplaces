What to see in Dronero

Porta della Valle Maira, of medieval origin: the crenellated bridge of the Devil of 1428 on Maira, the porticoed street of the historic center, the Civic Museum Luigi Mallé, the sound museum of the Occitan language of Espaci Occitan and the Riviera Mill.

6 places to visit in Dronero, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ponte del Diavolo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old bridge over the Maira stream, crenellated and with three unequal arches, built in 1428 and linked to local legends that gave him the name.

  • Where Ponte vecchio sul Maira, Dronero
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · italia.it

Centro storico e via porticata

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Born in the 12th century as a fortified settlement called Dragonerium, whose walls were largely demolished in the second half of the 18th century: medieval traces remain between Gothic capitals, blackbirds and porticoes of the main street. In the center medieval and baroque churches, noble palaces and the Loggia del Grano.

  • Where Centro storico, Dronero
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · italia.it

Museo civico Luigi Mallé

Museums · Old masters

In the family house left as a legacy by art historian Luigi Mallé, who died in 1979, with his collections ordered by historical period: Flemish paintings, eighteenth century pastels, ceramics, vases and vintage photographs; on the ground floor the Civic Library. Open Saturday, Sunday and public holidays from 15 to 19.

  • Where Via IV Novembre 54 (ingresso da via Valmala 9), Dronero
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · italia.it

Espaci Occitan – Museo Sòn de Lenga

Museums · Themed

In the former Beltricco barracks, also home to the Institute of Occitan Studies, a multimedia sound museum: places on literary tradition, music, history and material life, folkloric and social valleys of the òc language, consultable in Italian, Occitan, English and French. In summer open Thursday and Friday 10-12 and 15-18 and Saturday 10-12; in winter only by booking for groups over 15 people.

  • Where Via Val Maira 19, Dronero
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · comune.dronero.cn.it · italia.it

Mulino della Riviera

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Mill still in operation, where are found flour ground with natural stone. In the village also the bread of the Roofs cooked in the wood-burning oven and the Dranoresi, chocolate praline with rum liqueur cream.

  • Where Dronero
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · italia.it

Sentieri Occitani

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

From Dronero start the Occitan Paths that go up to the high valley Maira and the Path on the Maira.

  • Where Dronero
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
