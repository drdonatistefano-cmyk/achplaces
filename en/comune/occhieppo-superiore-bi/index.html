What to see in Occhieppo Superiore

Biellese country with the parish church of Santo Stefano, the seventeenth-century Villa Amosso and the oratories of Sant'Elisabetta and the Blessed Virgin of the Graces, built between the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries.

4 places to visit in Occhieppo Superiore, in the province of Biella (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Occhieppo Superiore in the guide with map and filters

Parrocchiale di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries and renovated in the following century; it retains the original apse frescoed with the Madonna and Saint Stephen and part of the bell tower. Inside two altars, one from the 17th and one from the 19th century, stucco friezes and a Via Crucis on canvas of the Serpentiero.

  • Where Occhieppo Superiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
