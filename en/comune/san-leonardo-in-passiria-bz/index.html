What to see in San Leonardo in Passiria

The capital of Val Passiria on the road of Passo Giovo: at the Sandhof, the birthplace of Andreas Hofer, the MuseumPasseier; above the village the thirteenth-century tower of Castel Giovo (Jaufenburg) and the church of Sant'Ippolito in Le Coste with the only cycle of frescoes of the valley; from the bridge of the village starts the path in the gorge of Passirio towards Moso.

6 places to visit in San Leonardo in Passiria, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

MuseumPasseier al Sandhof

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Museum in the Sandhof, home of Andreas Hofer, the host and cattle trader became Tyrolean hero. The permanent exhibition "Helden & Wir" links historic heroes to those of today, "Miër Psairer" introduces to the dialect and character of the valley, and outdoors the Haufenhof shows the typical farm at the court of Passiria. Open from 1 April to 30 October 2026 10-17 (closed Monday); ticket combined with the Bunker Mooseum €16; an entry included in the Südtirol Guest Pass Passeiertal Premium.

  • Where Passeirer Straße 72, San Leonardo in Passiria
  • Entry €12, reduced €10, free up to 18 years
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.museum.passeier.it

Sources: museum.passeier.it · suedtirol.info

Castel Giovo (Jaufenburg)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Late medieval complex that guarded the Via del Passo Giovo: only the original mast, built in the middle of the thirteenth century by the lords of Passiria as a residential tower remains. The five-storey tower, restored and detached seat of the MuseumPasseier since 2003, houses small exhibitions on the castle, the Schildhöfe farms, the lords of Passiria and the counts Fuchs, Renaissance paintings of 1538 by Bartlme Dill Riemenschneider on the fourth floor and on top the view up to Merano; below, the late Dogotic church of Santa Croce with frescoes of about 1550. Open on Monday 10-13 from June to September; from the village bridge about 30 minutes walk.

  • Where Passeirer Straße 72, San Leonardo in Passiria
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museum.passeier.it

Sources: museum.passeier.it · suedtirol.info

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Leonardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church consecrated to San Leonardo in 1116, with a major neo-Gothic altar, a white marble baptismal font linked to Andreas Hofer and a remarkable mural painting above the sacristy door.

  • Where Passeirer Straße 40, San Leonardo in Passiria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Chiesa di Sant'Ippolito a Le Coste

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The fourteenth-century church above San Leonardo with the only cycle of frescoes in the valley: scenes from the life of St. Ippolito of 1380 of a Giotto influence, attributed to the school of Bolzano and perhaps commissioned by Hiltprant von Passeier, arrived only in part; on the south façade a Saint Christopher and the coat of arms of the lords of Passiria. Cocci found near the church suggest a place of votive fires of the late Bronze Age. It can be visited only from the outside, with ample view of the valley.

  • Where Le Coste (Glaiten), San Leonardo in Passiria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Sentiero della gola del Passirio

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Excursion of about 7,8 km (2 hours and 30, 501 meters of climb) from the bridge of the village to Moso in the wild gorge of Passirio: the path follows the river with water with bridges and walkways on the walls, halfway up with views of the waterfall of Stulles and continues in the woods to the dam of Moso; you return with bus 240. Not suitable for strollers and bicycles.

  • Where Ponte del paese, San Leonardo in Passiria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info
