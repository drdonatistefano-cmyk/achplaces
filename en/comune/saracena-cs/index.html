What to see in Saracena

Borgo on the southern edge of the Pollino National Park, between beech and chestnut woods, born from the Arab settlement around the year 1000; it houses the church of San Leone, the Museum of sacred art in Santa Maria del Gamio, the Pinacoteca "Andrea Alfano" and the Moscato passito garrison Slow Food.

Chiesa di San Leone

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It rises on the remains of a Byzantine church with a Greek cross of the 11th century, dedicated to Saint Catherine and consecrated to Saint Leo in the 13th century; enriched under the Sanseverino in the 16th century, restored in the 17th century with Baroque interior. hexagonal bell tower with Romanic triphores, three naves with twelve altars, marble ciborium of 1522 of Tuscan sculptor, baptismal font with the inscription "S." Leo 1592 and frescoes of the vault with the Sacrifice of Abraham and Judith and Holofernes.

  • Where Saracena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.saracena.cs.it · italia.it

Museo d'arte sacra (Santa Maria del Gamio)

Museums · Old masters

In the church of Santa Maria del Gamio: sixteenth-century polyptych of unknown southern painter (remain side panels and predella) with the coats of arms of the Sanseverino; the throne and the monstrory of 1746 of the silversmith Salvatore Vecchio, a processional cross of the mid seventeenth century, the Madonna della Purità painted on metal plate, reliquary busts, canvases and vestments from the sixteenth to the nineteenth century. The sacristy depicts the legend of liberation from the Saracen siege.

  • Where Via S. Maria Maddalena, Saracena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.saracena.cs.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Pinacoteca «Andrea Alfano»

Museums · Modern art

In Palazzo Mastromarchi, inaugurated on May 1, 1985 and dedicated to the Castrovillarese painter Andrea Alfano, born from a donation of the former members of the association "Sestium" and works donated by artists from all over Italy. There are 236 paintings and drawings and 23 20th century sculptures, with names such as Guttuso, Turcato, Treccani, Fazzini and Emilio Greco.

  • Where Palazzo Mastromarchi, Saracena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.saracena.cs.it · italia.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient center stretches on the slope of a green valley surrounded by beech woods and secular chestnut groves; born from the Arab settlement, it is cited as an example of Arab-Turkish urbanism in Calabria, and there are relived ancient crafts and rural traditions.

  • Where Saracena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
