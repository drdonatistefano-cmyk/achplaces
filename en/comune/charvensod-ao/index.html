What to see in Charvensod

Municipality on the road to Pila five kilometers from Aosta, dominated by the Becca of Nona and Mont Emilius.

3 places to visit in Charvensod, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Charvensod

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the 14th century, the bishops of Aosta retired until the mid-19th century; they remained a circular tower and sections of walls.

Eremo di San Grato

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

White chapel at almost 1800 meters between meadows and woods, dedicated to the patron saint of the diocese; three hours of climbing from the capital or on foot from Pilaz.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Becca di Nona e Mont Emilius

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint
