What to see in Montappone

Small medieval village on a hill above the valley of the Tenna, between the Sibillini and the Adriatic, known for the hats of straw made by hand: the Museum of the Hat opened in 1991 in the old town hall, the Oratory of the SS. Sacramento on the site of the ancient castle, Palazzo Riccucci and the walk along the walls.

5 places to visit in Montappone, in the province of Fermo (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo del Cappello

Museums · Themed

Opened in 1991 to preserve the tradition of straw hat, in the former palace of Piazza Roma, documents all the phases of processing, from the collection to the sale to the pressing that gives the final shape, with photographs, panels and original machinery. The section "Marche," so much of hat "details" has hats reinterpreted by regional excellences in glass, ceramics and wood.

  • Where Piazza Roma 5, Montappone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Oratorio del SS. Sacramento

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the square built on the site of the ancient castle, it has a fourteenth-century terracotta portal surmounted by the coat of arms of the confraternity and frescoes of the sixteenth century, including a Madonna and Child of the Pagani.

  • Where Centro storico, Montappone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Palazzo Riccucci

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

It dominates the old town; recently restored to welcome visitors, it offers views of the countryside and its crops.

  • Where Centro storico, Montappone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Passeggiata delle mura e Santa Maria in Castello

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The tour along the old town belt offers views of the countryside; near the walls the church of Santa Maria in Castello, with on the altar a Madonna with Bambino del Pomarancio.

  • Where Mura del centro storico, Montappone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Maria e Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the new part of the village, rises on the remains of the Romanesque Madonna di Villa; red brick façade with portal decorated in white stone.

  • Entry Free
