What to see in Petralia Soprana

Medieval village of Madonie at 1,147 meters, natural balcony with three belvedere (Loreto, Carmine and Piazza Duomo) that look from Etna to Palermo; between alleys and squares the Mother Church with the first crucifix of Friar Umile, the elliptical church of the Savior, the Pottino palaces and, in the hamlet Raffo, the salt mine with the Salt Museum.

Centro storico, piazze e belvedere

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Founded by the Sicani, called Batraliah by the Arabs and Petra Heliae by the Normans, the village turns between Piazza Loreto (the highest part, probably the seat of the Sican and then Roman fortification), Piazza San Michele with the circular fountain, Piazza dei Quattro Cannoli with the Baroque marble fountain of Billiemi (the only water source of the village until the eighteenth century) and Porta Seri, last of the six ancient gates, of Norman origin. Three belvedere: Loreto ('u castru') towards Etna, Enna, Caltanissetta and the Impera valley; Carmine towards Palermo; Piazza Duomo towards Gangi and Etna.

  • Entry Free

Chiesa Madre dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Overlooking the scenic Piazza Duomo, it has a Norman bell tower and an eighteenth-century bell tower and three naves supported by twelve columns that recall the Apostles. Inside a Deposition attributed to Ribera, a Madonna among the angels of Gaspare Vazzano and, in the right aisle, the first crucifix of Friar Umile (1623).

Chiesa di Santa Maria di Loreto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Late Baroque church with Greek cross in the highest part of the country, with a sixteenth-century marble altar by Giandomenico Gagini with four scenes of the life of Jesus, seventeenth-century wooden statues of the saints Cosmas and Damian and two statues of saints attributed to Filippo Quattrocchi by Gangi. Next door, the Loreto viewpoint.

Chiesa del Santissimo Salvatore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The only church of the Madonie with an elliptical plan, perhaps a mosque transformed by the Normans, enlarged in the second half of the eighteenth century; it houses a St. Joseph by sculptor Filippo Quattrocchi. You will arrive from Piazza Fontana.

Piazza del Popolo e Palazzi Pottino

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

On the square the neo-Gothic Town Hall, already a convent of the Scalzi Carmelites, and the two palaces of the Marchesi of Eschifaldo: one is public and hosts temporary exhibitions and at Christmas the Crib of Enchant; the other, of the Pottino heirs, is a museum house with nineteenth-century frescoes and the table service used for Umberto II Prince of Piedmont.

Miniera di salgemma e Museo del Sale di Raffo

Museums · Themed

In the hamlet of Raffo, about 2 km from the village, a large still active salt mine produces the 'salte di Sicilia': the deposit, among the richest in Europe, covers about 2 km2 with thicknesses up to 250 meters. Inside the mine, the Museum of Salt exhibits sculptures of artists' rooms from all over the world; access is provided with protective measures.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
