What to see in Serravalle Scrivia

Cittadina at the foot of the Ligurian Apennines, at the confluence of Borbera in Scrivia, long under the Republic of Genoa: the archaeological area of the Roman city of Libarna on the Via Postojna, the hall of the artifacts in the town hall and a historical center with a Ligurian imprint.

Area archeologica di Libarna

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Roman city founded in the first century BC on the Via Postumia and flourished in the I-II century A.D., rediscovered by chance in the nineteenth century during the works on road and rail; the visitable area is about one tenth of the original city. You can see the amphitheatre, the theatre, the streets and two neighborhoods with large decorated houses, including the great mosaic with the myth of Liburgo and Ambrosia. Free admission and free visit, with seasonal hours; accessible to people with disabilities.

  • Where Via Arquata 63, frazione Libarna, Serravalle Scrivia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: alexala.it · comune.serravalle-scrivia.al.it · italia.it

Sala dei reperti di Libarna

Museums · Archaeological

Museum space on the ground floor of the town hall, about 1.5 km from the excavations, which collects finds from the site of Libarna.

  • Where Via Berthoud 49, Serravalle Scrivia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.serravalle-scrivia.al.it · italia.it

Centro storico e ponte del Lastrico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Narrow core between the hill and the Scrivia stream, with the bridge of the Lastricus, alleys, squares and houses with bright colors of Liguria; there are the Collegiate of Saints Martin and Stefano and the oratories of the "White" and "Red" with works of art. From the center you can see the ruins of the castle on the hill of the Arimanni, not to be visited.

  • Where Centro storico, Serravalle Scrivia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.serravalle-scrivia.al.it · italia.it

Villa Caffarena e Pinacoteca comunale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Historic noble residence in the municipal public park, home to the "Roberto Allegri" Civic Library and the informal art collection of the Municipal Art Gallery.
