What to see in Tarsia

Common where in 1940 Ferramonti was built, the largest fascist internment camp, today the museum of memory. In Palazzo Rossi are the dioramas of the Museum of Natural History of Calabria and the body of the Natural Reserve of Lake Tarsia, wet area with 182 species of vertebrates recorded.

Museo della Memoria di Ferramonti

Museums · Themed

The camp, built in 1940 on about 160,000 m2 with white barracks arranged in horseshoe, was the largest fascist internment camp: there were foreign Jewish prisoners, then Italian Jews, opponents of the regime, political refugees and Chinese citizens. The museum, inaugurated on 25 April 2004 in the restored administrative shacks, exhibits documents, photographs, letters, personal objects and video testimonies, with a plastic room that reproduces the field in scale and, since 2009, a section on the Shoah.

visits by appointment, also on Sundays (Common); tel. 0984 32377

Palazzo Rossi, Museo di storia naturale della Calabria (Sezione Diorami)

Museums · Themed

On the ground floor of the historic Palazzo Rossi, in 120 m2, four dioramas reconstruct the environments of the National Parks of Pollino, Sila and Aspromonte and the Regional Reserves of Lake Tarsia and the Foce del Crati. It is one of the four sections of the Natural History Museum of Calabria, created to spread the natural sciences especially among young people.

Riserva naturale regionale Lago di Tarsia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Lake basin whose level depends on the dam downstream, with Mediterranean holm maquis, elm, tamarisk and arbutus and, in the shallow waters, rafts and reeds. The Florist list counts 720 taxa and 182 vertebrates have been recorded, including heron white major, knight of Italy, white stork, swamp falcon and Hermann turtle; the visit is made on the marked paths and from the huts. The southern border is about 1 km from the Tarsia motorway junction.
