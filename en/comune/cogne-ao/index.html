What to see in Cogne

In the heart of the Gran Paradiso National Park, overlooking the lawn of Sant'Orso, between the waterfalls of Lillaz, the mines of magnetite and the lace tombolo.

9 places to visit in Cogne, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 3 of the 4 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Prato di Sant'Orso

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the largest mountain pastures in Europe, dotted with anemones at the foot of the Gran Paradiso, recognized as natural Italian wonder.

  • Where Cogne
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · gestlovevda.it

Cascate di Lillaz

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Urtier stream descends in three jumps; in winter it is a destination for ice climbing, in summer of family walks.

  • Where Lillaz, Cogne
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: gestlovevda.it · lovevda.it

Valnontey e Parco Nazionale del Gran Paradiso

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village of the national park for walks in the unspoilt nature, with an itinerary of valley bottom easy even with stroller.

  • Where Valnontey, Cogne
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: gestlovevda.it · lovevda.it

Giardino Botanico Alpino Paradisia

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Miniere di Cogne

Museums · Themed

The highest magnetite mine in Europe, with more than 100 km of galleries in Monte Creya, can be visited with guide; exhibition center of the mining park.

  • Entry To be checked

  • Entry To be checked
