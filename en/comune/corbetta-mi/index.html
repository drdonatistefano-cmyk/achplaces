What to see in Corbetta

City of the West of Milan known for the delightful villas built by the noble families of Milan and the Sanctuary of the Madonna dei Miracoli, linked to the miracle of April 17, 1555 remembered by the Feast of Forgiveness; in the center also the fifteenth-century house-museum of Pisani Dossi.

5 places to visit in Corbetta, in the province of Milan (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Casa-museo Pisani Dossi

Museums · Archaeological

The residence was built around the middle of the fifteenth century by Count Ambrogio Varese of Rosate, then passed to Francesco Mussi and Carlotta Borsani. Carlo Dossi, who left his diplomatic career, at the beginning of the twentieth century dedicated himself to archaeological excavations in the territory and set up a museum: a wing houses the collections with finds from the Bronze and Iron Ages, Roman lamps and medieval and modern objects, next to the library of Alberto Pisani Dossi. The tours are guided, with the Fondazione Pisani Dossi and the Associazione Amici del Museo; the study of the writer is usually not open to the public.

  • Where Via Francesco Mussi 38, Corbetta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.corbetta.mi.it

Santuario della Madonna dei Miracoli

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

One of the city's symbols. It is linked to the miracle of 17 April 1555: according to tradition the Child Jesus painted by Gregory Zavattari on the façade of the little church of St. Nicholas detached himself from the fresco to play with three children, one of which, deaf to death from birth, would recover hearing and speech. It is remembered by the Feast of Forgiveness, from Thursday after Easter to Sunday, with processions at the Madonna del Santuario and popular celebrations.

  • Where Centro storico, Corbetta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.corbetta.mi.it

Sources: comune.corbetta.mi.it

Villa Frisiani Mereghetti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built around the middle of the seventeenth century for Gotthard Frisiani, on a project attributed to Francesco Maria Richini, incorporating as wing a fifteenth-century body of the previous castle; the interior frescoes with a profane subject are Montalto brothers. U-shaped plant with central body, two wings and ample park: the left wing, the oldest, has ogival windows, wooden floors and fifteenth-century frescoes; to the north a portico with three fornics on granite binated columns of Baveno, inside a staircase of honour.

Private property for residential use (SIRBEC card); the villas open thanks to the owners during the walk between the villas (Common)

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Corbetta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.corbetta.mi.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · comune.corbetta.mi.it

Ville di delizia e Passeggiata tra le ville

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The villas in the center were built by the noble families of Milan as holiday residences, with the façade facing the village and the park towards the countryside; the owners called artists and architects known for monumental staircases with balustrades and painted or stucco ceilings. Among the other Villa Frisiani Olivares Ferrario, Villa Borri Manzoli, Villa Massari, Villa Mereghetti and Villa Pagani della Torre. On the first Sunday of May the Pro Loco organizes the walk among the villas, when gardens, historic buildings and churches open to the public with guides.

  • Where Centro storico, Corbetta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page comune.corbetta.mi.it

Sources: comune.corbetta.mi.it

Parco di Villa Frisiani Olivares Ferrario

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

The park with centuries-old trees of the villa that houses the seat of the municipality, with a recovered cave and a navigable pond; the access to the city parks is free.
