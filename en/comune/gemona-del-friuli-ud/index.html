What to see in Gemona del Friuli

The city symbol of the rebirth of Friuli after the earthquake of 1976, rebuilt stone on stone: the Romanesque-Gothic Cathedral, the oldest sanctuary dedicated to Saint Anthony, the castle and the palaces of the center, between Tagliamento and the mountains.

9 places to visit in Gemona del Friuli, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Masterpiece of Romanesque and Gothic architecture in the medieval city, restored to its appearance after the 1976 earthquake.

  • Where Gemona del Friuli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Museo della Pieve e Tesoro del Duomo

Museums · Old masters · Free entry

Local paintings and sculptures from the 13th to the 19th century, sacred furnishings of the churches affected by the earthquake, illuminated codes of the Bolognese-Paduana school of the Two-Three hundred and, in the Treasury, the monstrance of Nicolò Lionello of 1434 and two bronzes of Girolamo Campagna.

  • Where Vicolo delle mura, Gemona del Friuli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Santuario di Sant'Antonio e Museo Renato Raffaelli

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

The oldest sanctuary dedicated to Saint Anthony; the museum collects the works once in the Antonian Art Gallery, including numerous paintings by the Swiss painter Melchior Widmar (second half of the seventeenth century), and stuccoes of the eighteenth century recovered after the collapses of 1976.

  • Where Piazzale Padre Osmolowski, Gemona del Friuli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo civico di Palazzo Elti

Museums · Old masters

In the fifteenth century Palazzo Elti rebuilt after the earthquake: paintings and sculptures by Friulian and Venetian artists, including Cima da Conegliano, Pomponio Amalteo and Pellegrino da San Daniele, works of the seventeenth century and of hegemonic painters; open on Sundays.

  • Where Palazzo Elti, Via Bini, Gemona del Friuli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismofvg.it
