What to see in Sovizzo

The Retrone is located in the hills west of Vicenza: it houses a megalithic complex of the Copper Age, churches with works by Orazio Marinali and Francesco Maffei, Venetian villas from the sixteenth to the nineteenth century and the natural tunnels of the Spurghe di Sant'Urbano.

Complesso megalitico di San Daniele

History and architecture · Archaeology

Cultural and funerary complex of the Copper Age, dated around 3400 BC: three mounds of stones with the remains of two adults and a child of about two years, connected by ritual paths marked by stones and pebbles. Today it is housed in a glass structure and is the second megalithic complex of the Copper Age of Northern Italy.

  • Where Zona San Daniele, Via degli Alpini / Via Tiepolo, Sovizzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di Santa Maria Annunziata di Sovizzo Colle

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Archpriest's church also called Santa Maria del Colle, which tradition connects to an ancient temple dedicated to Diana; a Roman inscription dedicated to the goddess is walled in the building. It preserves works of the seventeenth century, including sculptures by Orazio Marinali and 15 copper tiles by Francesco Maffei.

  • Where Piazza Michelin, Sovizzo Colle, Sovizzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sovizzo.vi.it

Chiesa di San Bartolomeo a Montemezzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

It rises in the highest point of the municipal territory; the current building dates back to 1622. Inside works by Pietro Cavaliere, brothers Merlo, Orazio Marinali and a canvas of the Maganza.

  • Entry To be checked

Chiesetta di Santa Reparata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The restoration of 2000 brought to light an oculus of an earlier 11th century Romanesque church; in the 17th century the building was completely renovated. The painting with the martyrdom of the saint is by Rocco Pittaco.

  • Where Vigo, Sovizzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sovizzo.vi.it

Sources: comune.sovizzo.vi.it

Chiesetta di San Daniele e Ponte Balabardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The little church is mentioned from the fifteenth century together with the convent of the Servants of Mary, but it is probably older; originally dedicated to the prophet Daniel and then to the Madonna del Carmine, it preserves a Roman walled inscription. Not far away, on the river Mezzaruolo, the Balabardo Bridge dates back to 1647 but is probably older.

  • Where San Daniele, Sovizzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sovizzo.vi.it

Sources: comune.sovizzo.vi.it

Ville di Sovizzo: Villa Curti, Villa Marzotto-Schiavo, Villa Civena

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa Curti, in the historic nucleus, was Bissari since the end of the fifteenth century and Curti since 1870; Villa Marzotto-Schiavo in San Daniele is a building of the seventeenth century with a side portico of the nineteenth century; Villa Civena in Contrà Garibaldo brings the date 1592 and has a dove with spiral staircase.

  • Where Sovizzo (centro, San Daniele, contrà Garibaldo)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sovizzo.vi.it

Sources: comune.sovizzo.vi.it

Spurghe di Sant'Urbano

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

About ten narrow tunnels between rocks overhanging, for about 650 meters total, humid and rich in mosses, ferns and wildlife. The source recommends the guided tour.

  • Where Sant'Urbano, Sovizzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sovizzo.vi.it

Sources: comune.sovizzo.vi.it

Ossario di Monte San Pietro

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Inaugurated in 1965, it collects the remains of 27 fallen and brings back the names of the 79 Soviets who died in the two world wars.

  • Where Monte San Pietro, Sovizzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
