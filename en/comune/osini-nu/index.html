What to see in Osini

Ogliastra country under the Taccu: the old village abandoned after the flood of 1951, the gorge of the Scala di San Giorgio and, at a thousand meters, the nuragic complex of Serbissi.

5 places to visit in Osini, in the province of Nuoro (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Complesso nuragico di Serbissi

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

About a thousand meters on the Taccu, inhabited between the seventeenth and the 10th century BC: nuraghe with four towers with a mast of over six meters, a village of eight huts and a karst cave with two entrances used as storage.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Scala di San Giorgio

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Gorge between limestone and Dolomite walls, natural monument, with the sanctuary of the patron; legend has it that the rock has opened for a miracle of St. George bishop of Suelli.

  • Where Osini
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Taccu di Osini

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

High plateau almost a thousand meters high with views of the Ogliastra, reached through the Scala di San Giorgio.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Nuraghi Sanu e Orruttu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Two single tower nuraghi in the plain of Truculu near Serbissi, with two tombs of giants, perhaps part of a single inhabited.

  • Where Osini
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Osini vecchia

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval village, largely uninhabited after the 1951 flood, with the seventeenth-century church of Santa Susanna; it lives two days in August for the feast of the saint.

  • Entry Free
