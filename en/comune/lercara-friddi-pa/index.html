What to see in Lercara Friddi

Until the 1940s, the most important solviferous center, today told in the museum of the zolfara of Villa Rose, the Victorian villa of English entrepreneurs; in the village the Mother Church of Santa Maria della Snow and the seventeenth-century church of San Matteo, in the territory the Sican settlement of Colle Madore.

Villa Rose, Parco archeologico-industriale e Museo della zolfara

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Victorian villa of the first half of the nineteenth century, the only example of English architecture of the territory, built by the English entrepreneurs who arrived to exploit the basin of sulphur; it is the seat of the archaeological-industrial Park established by the Region in 2010. On the ground floor the archaeological museum with Sican, Etruscan and Roman artifacts (also from Colle Madore), on the first floor the museum of the zolfare with finds, multimedia, virtual reconstructions and a room immersive on the life of the miners.

  • Where Via Vittorio Emanuele III 52, Lercara Friddi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cittametropolitana.pa.it

Chiesa Madre di Santa Maria della Neve

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built between 1702 and 1721 with a Latin cross with three naves on the model of St. Matthew in Palermo, it has the high altar of 1765, the choir of the Cotton brothers (1910) and an organ of the Cimino brothers inaugurated in 2006. Among the works the SS. Trinity attributed to the Zoppo di Gangi, the Baptism of Jesus of Lo Cascio, the golden statue of the Madonna del Carmelo, patron saint of the zolfatari, and a Saint Francis of Paola carved in a trunk of pear; in the crypt a collection of nativity scenes visitable especially at Christmas.

  • Where Centro storico, Lercara Friddi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cittametropolitana.pa.it

Sources: turismo.cittametropolitana.pa.it · cittametropolitana.pa.it

Chiesa di San Matteo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded at the end of the seventeenth century by Baron Scammacca and remained largely as it was, with a single nave: it preserves the SS. Crucifix of the XVI-XVII century, brought in procession on 19 September, a Madonna of Trapani in marble of the Gaginian school, the statue of the Madonna of the Belt (1700) and a Assumption Madonna in wax; the sacristy is a small museum with ex-vote, missals and a silver cross astile.

  • Where Centro storico, Lercara Friddi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cittametropolitana.pa.it

Sources: turismo.cittametropolitana.pa.it · cittametropolitana.pa.it

Colle Madore, area archeologica

History and architecture · Archaeology

Relief of the Sican area, marked by centuries of sulphur extraction, frequented by the late Bronze Age: there were found matrixes for the fusion of bronze of the 11th century BC, a small sacred building of the 6th century BC with votive indigenous and Greek deposit (bronze belts, a newsstand with Heracles, amphora), a warehouse and a metallurgical area; part of the materials is in the museum of Lercara.

  • Where Colle Madore, Lercara Friddi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cittametropolitana.pa.it
