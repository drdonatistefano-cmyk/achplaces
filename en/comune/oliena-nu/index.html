What to see in Oliena

Borgo della Barbagia at the foot of Monte Corrasi, the highest peak of the Supramonte, Orange Flag of Touring: historic center with the district of sa Maddalena and the church of Sant'Ignazio, the source of Su Gologone and the valley of Lanaittu with the village-sanctuary of Sa Sedda 'e sos Carros and the caves Sa Oche, Su Bentu and Corbeddu.

10 places to visit in Oliena, in the province of Nuoro (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e rione di sa Maddalena

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Narrow streets of white houses with small courtyards, external stairs, pergolas and arches; the district of sa Maddalena is the best preserved part. Along the course Grazia Deledda the prison well and the remains of the seventeenth-century residence of the Major; murals and cobbled streets.

  • Where Centro storico, Oliena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Ignazio di Loyola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church next to the seventeenth-century Jesuit College: it preserves wooden statues, including the Christ de s'Iscravamentu, and a sixteenth-century retablo called St. Christopher.

  • Where Centro storico, Oliena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Ex parrocchiale di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

At the entrance of the village: built by the Pisans at the end of the thirteenth century in late Romanesque forms and then adapted in Gothic style; the current appearance derives from renovations. In Piazza Santa Maria the procession de s'Incontrau ends at Easter.

  • Where Piazza Santa Maria, Oliena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiese di Santa Croce, d'Itria e San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Santa Croce, five-sixth-century oratory with sailing bell tower, preserves the wooden crucifix used in Holy Week; Our Lady of Itria, in via Ichnusa, is seventeenth-century and hosted a confraternity founded in 1613; from the chapel of St. Francis the Christ of the procession de s'Incontu starts at Easter.

  • Where Centro storico, Oliena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Sorgente di Su Gologone

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

Karst resurgence at the foot of Mount Uddè, the most important Sardinian spring and natural monument since 1998: the water flows from a vertical rift with an average flow rate of about 500 liters per second, in shades from emerald green to blue; in 2010 it was explored up to 135 meters. It is observed from the terrace of the little church of Our Lady of the Pietà; around a forest of eucalyptuses.

  • Where Località Su Gologone, Oliena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Monte Corrasi

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 1463 meters it is the highest peak of Supramonte: limestone cliffs with overhanging cliffs, spires and caves, bare peak and holm oaks at half coast. About 650 botanical species, about sixty endemic species; they nest there royal eagle and falcon of the queen and live the muflon. 360 degree panorama to Gennargentu; challenging trails for experienced hikers.

  • Where Monte Corrasi, Oliena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Valle di Lanaittu

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valley between the territories of Oliena and Dorgali, the most accessible port of Supramonte: limestone crests with dolines, canyons and caves, holm oak woods, terebinth and age-old junipers, where you can meet mouflons and royal eagles. On its dirt paths you will find Sa Sedda 'e sos Carros and the caves of Sa Oche, Su Bentu and Corbeddu.

  • Where Valle di Lanaittu, Oliena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Villaggio nuragico Sa Sedda 'e sos Carros

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Nuragic village-sanctuary in the Lanaittu valley with numerous circular and oval huts and rooms for bronze processing, linked to the cult of water. The hut, called "source" has walls in white limestone and dark basalt with heads of muflon and carved rams, from whose mouths the water gushed in a large central tank. Admission for a fee (amount not indicated).

  • Where Valle di Lanaittu, Oliena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: idese.cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Grotte di Sa Oche e Su Bentu

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Karst complex at the end of the valley of Lanaittu, one of the largest caves in Europe: Sa Oche (the voice of the hamlet) has a large entrance with three lakes and rebombs after the storms; On Bentu it develops for about 16 km on several levels and is accessible only to expert speleologists. The river that crosses them resurfaces in Su Gologone. Admission for a fee.

  • Where Valle di Lanaittu, Oliena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Grotta Corbeddu

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

130-metre cave in three rooms, shelter of the bandit Giovanni Corbeddu Salis and described by Grazia Deledda in "L'Edera" . In the second room were found among the oldest human remains of Sardinia, with a phalanx dated about 20,000 years ago, and fossils of the Prolagus sardus.

  • Where Valle di Lanaittu, Oliena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
