What to see in Caserta

The city of the Reggia of Luigi Vanvitelli, UNESCO heritage with the Royal Park, the English Garden, the Carolino Aqueduct and the Belvedere of San Leucio; on the slopes of the Tifatini Mountains the medieval village of Casertavecchia with its Norman cathedral.

11 places to visit in Caserta, in the province of Caserta (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Reggia di Caserta – Palazzo Reale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Wanted by Carlo di Borbone and designed by Luigi Vanvitelli: 47,000 m2 on five floors with the Royal Scalone, the Royal Apartments, the Throne Room, the Palatine Library, the Court Nativity Crib, the Palatine Chapel, the eighteenth-century Court Theatre and the contemporary Terrae Motus collection.

  • Where Piazza Carlo di Borbone, Caserta
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it

Parco Reale

Nature and landscape · City parks · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The Italian garden of Vanvitelli with meadows, pools, fountains and waterfalls fed by the Carolino Aqueduct: the Via d'Acqua culminates in the Torrione and the Grande Cascata; in the Bosco Vecchio la Peschiera grande and the Castelluccia.

  • Where Piazza Carlo di Borbone, Caserta
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it

Sources: reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it

Giardino Inglese

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Wanted by Maria Carolina of Habsburg, in apparent natural disorder between watercourses and fake ruins: the Cryptoporic in the Bathroom of Venus with statues from Pompeii and from the Farnese collection, the Doric Temple and the greenhouses of Graefer.

  • Where Piazza Carlo di Borbone, Caserta
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it

Sources: reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it

Bosco di San Silvestro – Oasi WWF

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

The Royal Tenuta di San Silvestro was part of the Royal Delights of the Reggia together with San Leucio, the Royal Park and the English Garden; today it is an Oasis of the WWF that organizes visits and initiatives.

  • Where Via dei Giardini Reali 78, Caserta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it

Complesso Monumentale Belvedere di San Leucio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

UNESCO World Heritage with the Reggia: the silk colony wanted by Ferdinand IV since 1778, with manufactures, houses for workers, church and school; the Silk Museum exhibits fabrics and machines, and from the hill the viewpoint dominates all Caserta.

  • Where Via del Setificio 5, San Leucio, Caserta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it

Sources: italia.it · sabapce-bn.cultura.gov.it · reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it

Borgo di Casertavecchia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the slope of Mount Virgo, the medieval village that was the original center of Caserta, narrow around the cathedral, with the noble houses Pisano and Ferraiuolo along the uphill alleys.

  • Where Casertavecchia, Caserta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it · reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it

Duomo di San Michele Arcangelo (Casertavecchia)

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

A splendid example of medieval architecture, the cathedral was consecrated in 1153 in San Michele Arcangelo; with the bell tower and the remains of the castle is the testimony of the splendor of the village.

  • Where Piazza Vescovado, Casertavecchia, Caserta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it · touringclub.it · reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it
