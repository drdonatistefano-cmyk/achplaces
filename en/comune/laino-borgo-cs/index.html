What to see in Laino Borgo

North-western gate of the Pollino Park on the border with the Basilicata, a painted village of Longobard origin with murals and many churches; a few kilometers away the Sanctuary of the Chapels, the "Jerusalem Calabria" of 1557, and paths towards the Lao gorges. At Easter, the Judea staged the Passion.

5 places to visit in Laino Borgo, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario delle Cappelle (Sacro Monte)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Also known as Madonna dello Spasimo or "Jerusalem calabrese" (Madonna of Spasimo), about 2 km from the center on a hill: the devoted Domenico Longo started it in 1557 after dreaming of the places of Jerusalem on its soil, went to Palestine and returned with the drawings to reproduce them. It includes sixteen small chapels with scenes of the Holy Land and Passion, so small that one visitor enters it at a time; the stone fountain of the square is of 1890, year of expansion and restoration. On the third Sunday of September, the votive women parade for the feast of Sorrows.

  • Where Corso Umberto, Laino Borgo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Centro storico e murales

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

A centre of Longobard origin at about 271 meters, with many churches and chapels, including the Church Mother of the Holy Spirit, the main place of worship. In the lower part, towards the river, the murals tell the rural life (the pressing of the grapes, the harvest brought with the donkeys, the ginning of the beans); in the upper part suspended umbrellas and colored staircases.

  • Where Laino Borgo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Mostra permanente della Giudaica e raccolta di minerali

Museums · Themed

Dedicated to Jude, sacred representation of Holy Week (about 200 people, 19 scenes of the Passion from the Last Supper to the Crucifixion): rotational stage costumes and photos of the editions from 1912 to today, with paintings and sacred art objects from the eighteenth-nineteenth century and a collection of minerals.

  • Where Piazza Navarro, Laino Borgo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Sentiero delle Gole del Lao

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Easy path of 3 km (about an hour and a half) from Piazza Navarro: cross the Iannello stream, go down to the communal wash basins and follow the signs to the river Lao, crossed on a wooden bridge, with a view on a rocky spur. On the Lao you can raft between gorges and canyons.

  • Where Piazza Navarro, Laino Borgo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Anello del Sacro Monte e del torrente Iannello

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Ring path of more than 10 km that climbs from the village to the Sanctuary of the Chapels along the Iannello stream and returns to Laino Borgo.

  • Where Laino Borgo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
