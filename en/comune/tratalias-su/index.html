What to see in Tratalias

Country of Sulcis whose ancient village, abandoned in the eighties for the infiltrations of the lake of Monte Pranu, houses the former Romanesque cathedral of Santa Maria di Monserrato (1213-1282), seat of the diocese of Sulci, with the Spanish House and the Museum of the territory; around, the park of Monte Pranu and dozens of nuragic sites.

Ex cattedrale di Santa Maria di Monserrato

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Built between 1213 and 1282, as two internal inscriptions recall, it was the bishop's seat of the diocese of Sulci until 1503, when it passed to Iglesias. Late-Romanesque with Gothic omens, in sedimentary and volcanic stone, has three naves on pillars, covered with trusses, rosette on the façade, hanging arches, capitals of influence of Pisa to the southern portal and two lions on the architrave of the north portal; in the left nave a retablo of 1596.

Borgo vecchio di Tratalias

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry

The medieval nucleus, abandoned in the eighties of the twentieth century when the infiltrations of the artificial lake of Monte Pranu made the houses unworkable; there are few restored stone houses and two of the three Baroque arches that closed the village. Behind the church, artisan workshops where to see the work live. The new village is located a few hundred meters away.

  • Where Piazza Chiesa, Tratalias
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Casa spagnola e Museo del territorio trataliese

Museums · City history

In the square of the church, the manor house (first plant of the XII-XIII century, restored in the XVII century) houses spaces for exhibitions and workshops; in the added environments the museum exhibits images and reconstructions of the ancient Tratalias and the chariot of the procession of the Virgin of Monserrato, Sunday of the Ascension.

  • Where Piazza Chiesa, Tratalias
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Parco di Monte Pranu

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural oasis with marked itineraries for horseback riding or mountain biking on ancient roads of shepherds and disused railways; in the park 11 nuraghi, four villages and two tombs of Giganti.

  • Where Monte Pranu, Tratalias
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Nuraghi Is Meurras, Carroccia e Tratalias

History and architecture · Archaeology

The territory has 40 Nuragic settlements: the main is the complex of Is Meurras with village and tomb of Giganti, which with the nearby imposing Nuraghe Carroccia controlled the territory; the nuraghe Tratalias, with central tower and three perimeter towers joined by curtains, is the best preserved complex, the nuraghe Cuccu the best preserved monotorre.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
