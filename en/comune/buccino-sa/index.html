What to see in Buccino

The centre of the Tanagro valley rises on the ancient Volcei, with an archaeological heritage from the Neolithic to the late Roman era: the national museum in the former convent of the Hermits, the urban archaeological park between the streets of the center and the Norman castle with its mast.

Museo archeologico nazionale di Volcei «Marcello Gigante»

Museums · Archaeological

In the former fifteenth-century convent of the Hermits of Sant'Agostino, with a cloister of 23 columns with Corinthian capitals, it collects finds from the territory of Buccino: the feminine set of the Tomb of the Ori, chamber tomb of the fourth century BC, and the mosaic of the Banquet Hall in an environment rebuilt with polychrome stuccoes; the route tells the commercial role of Volcei in the Roman age. Open every day 9-14, until 6 pm by reservation. Titled to the Buccina philologist Marcello Gigante.

  • Where Piazza Municipio 1, Buccino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Parco archeologico urbano dell'antica Volcei

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Inaugurated in 2003, it connects in an itinerary the ancient monuments inserted in the urban fabric: Volcei had a city walls in travertine at the end of the fourth century BC and is known mainly for the Roman phase; you can visit the temple of Via S. Spirit called Caesareum and a public building of imperial age with mosaic floors in Via Canali. Free access every day.

  • Where Centro storico, Buccino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
