What to see in Seui

Medieval village about 800 meters on the southern slopes of Gennargentu, the capital of Barbagia di Seulo, with a museum itinerary spread (Palazzina Liberty, Spanish Prison, Casa Farci, Civic Gallery) and a territory of holm oaks, the forest of Montarbu and the Nuraghe Ardasai.

7 places to visit in Seui, in the province of Sud Sardegna (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzina Liberty

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Former elegant building in Art Nouveau style, seat of the museum route of Seui with sections of art, archaeology, ethnography and folk traditions and on the mining history of the country; here you can find the ticket office and all the services of the museum pole, and you can book excursions in the forest of Montarbu. The ticket is valid for all locations of the route or for a part of your choice. From October to March Tuesday to Sunday 9-13 and 15:30-17:30; from April to September 9:30-13 and 15:30-19:30.

  • Where Via Roma 241, Seui
  • Entry €5 unique ticket of the museum route; reduced €4, €3,50, €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · sardegnacultura.it

Carcere spagnolo

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Square building on two levels hidden between the houses and reached by the substep \ on Porci Â Â , it was from the middle of the seventeenth century prison of the Incontrada of Barbagia of Seulo, under the Duchy of Mandas, then of the Sabauda justice and of the Italian State until the closure in 1975. Visitors can visit the janitor's accommodation with nineteenth-century furnishings, the reception office and the female cell on the upper floor, the male cell and the punishment cell without windows on the lower floor; exposed documents of the eighteenth-nineteenth century on the transport of prisoners and medical and spice remedies.

  • Where Centro storico, Seui
  • Entry €5 unique ticket of the museum route
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · sardegnacultura.it

Casa Farci

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

19th century house where Filiberto Farci, writer and co-founder of the Sardinian Action Party was born in 1882 with Emilio Lussu: the study with objects, letters and library, the kitchen with wood-burning oven, the bedroom and a section of costumes; on the ground floor the reconstruction of a workshop of the late 19th century and a section on the mine of anthracite of Fund'e Corongiu, in the basement tools and a forge, in the cellar wooden presses and a large still in copper matricola "Cagliari 0001" (a section on emigration reminds Augusto Bissiri.

  • Where Via Roma, Seui
  • Entry €5 unique ticket of the museum route
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · sardegnacultura.it

Galleria civica

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

In the civic palazzo "S'omu comuni" of the late nineteenth century, already school and pretura: a hall for temporary exhibitions, a hundred works of contemporary painters, many born from the annual competition of painting and sculpture, and in the council hall three seventeenth-century paintings of the Caravaggio school; an external path touches the modern sculptures in the streets of the village. Tuesday-Sunday, seasonal hours.

  • Where Via della Sapienza 38, Seui
  • Entry €5 unique ticket of the museum route; reduced €4, €3,50, €1
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website museiseui.altervista.org

Sources: museiseui.altervista.org · sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Centro storico, Santa Maria Maddalena e San Giovanni Battista

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Stone houses with wrought iron balconies and roofs in coppi; the parish church of Santa Maria Maddalena preserves a marble baptismal font with reliefs dated 1644 and a polychrome wooden statue of the saint, the eighteenth-century church of St. John the Baptist the chariot of the procession of the Madonna del Carmelo at the end of July.

  • Where Centro storico, Seui
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Foresta di Montarbu

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 2,800 hectares of cliffs, valleys, streams and waterfalls, with Serra Middai, habitat of royal eagle, deer, deer and mouflon; the ascent to Punta Margiani Pubusa (1.325 m) passes through the sinkhole of Su Stampu and the monument of Perda Liana. The excursions can be booked at the Palazzina Liberty.

  • Where Foresta di Montarbu, Seui
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · sardegnacultura.it

Nuraghe Ardasai

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Monotorre in large blocks of limestone, probably of the middle Bronze (XVII-XIV century B.C.), about 1,000 meters on the slopes of Mount Tonneri, about 13 km from the village: remain the lower floor with a tholos vault and the helical staircase; around the traces of an antemural with secondary towers, huts, a village, the remains of a tomb of giants. It is visible from a panoramic road; near the fortified village of Serra on Casteddu.

  • Where Monte Tonneri, Seui
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
