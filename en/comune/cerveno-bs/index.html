What to see in Cerveno

Country at the foot of the Concarena, on the western side of the Camonica Valley, with a historic center largely fourteenth-century: it is known for the Sanctuary of the Way of the Cross, the "Capèle" with the life-size statues of Benjamin Simoni, and for the Holy Crus, the living Passion that is represented every ten years.

Santuario della Via Crucis («le Capèle»)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Next to the parish church of San Martino, a 14-station Via Crucis with 198 life-size statues in wood and plaster, commissioned in 1752 to Benjamin Simoni by the parish priest Don Andrea Boldini and completed in 1764 by Donato and Grazioso Fantoni (stations VIII, IX and perhaps X); the 14th current station is Giovanni Selleroni (1869), while the original of Simoni is in Breno. The frescoes on the walls are by Scotti and the Corbellini brothers; in the parish there are wooden sculptures of Andrea Fantoni's workshop. Guided tours for groups or individuals organized by parish and municipality, which also include the church of Our Lady of Carmine.

  • Where Piazza Roma 6, Cerveno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Casa Museo di Cerveno

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The rural house of the end of the sixteenth century of the Mondoni-Bazzoni families, purchased by the municipality and opened in 2006 as a museum collection recognized by the Lombardy Region: original vaults and limestone, tools for fields, viticulture and winemaking, breeding and crafts, and clothes, weapons, helmets and armour of the Santa Crus. Outside there is a horizontal lever press. Open Tuesday, Friday (14.30-16.30), Thursday (9-12), Saturday and Sunday (14.30-17.30); groups by reservation.

  • Where Vicolo Sonvico, Cerveno
  • Entry €2 (free residents and children up to 5 years)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Sources: comune.cerveno.bs.it · in-lombardia.it · turismovallecamonica.it

La porta del silenzio di Franca Ghitti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Monument to the Fallen of Franca Ghitti (1986): two metal panels framed on a steel portal, suspended, frame the wash-house and the mountain; iron waste represents the people connected to the community of martyrs, war and work. Visitable always.

  • Where Piazza Prudenzini, Cerveno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismovallecamonica.it

Sources: turismovallecamonica.it
