What to see in Anguillara Sabazia

Medieval village on a promontory of Lake Bracciano, with the sixteenth-century door of the clock, the Collegiate Church of Santa Maria Assunta and the fifteenth-century Tower; in front of the shore lies submerged the Neolithic village of Marmot, of which a pirogue of about 8,000 years is exposed to the Neolithic Museum.

Borgo medievale e Belvedere

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Promontory of medieval houses on the lake, about 40 km from Rome: you enter from a door of the sixteenth century with clock and you go up to the Collegiata, overlooking the lake. On the Belvedere the Fountain of the Earth (of the Anguille), symbol of the village; the sixteenth-century Palazzo Baronale, today the town hall, preserves restored frescoes. The name comes from Angularia, a Roman villa built where the shore forms a right corner, whose owner Rutilia Polla raised fish for the market of Rome.

  • Where Piazza del Comune, Anguillara Sabazia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Torrione e giardini

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The circular mast of the fortress, probably from the second half of the fifteenth century and attributed in a hypothetical way to Francesco di Giorgio Martini, on four levels: the guards on the ground floor, the prison on the second (up to the eighteenth century, with graffiti of the prisoners), residences on the high floors, where today the School Orchestra is located. The gardens, perhaps the work of Giacomo del Duca with Jacopo Barbone since 1579, are open every day 8-20; from the top of the tower you can see the lake and the Marmot area. The tower is visited by appointment with the City Culture Office.

  • Where Via di Domenico 1, Anguillara Sabazia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.anguillara-sabazia.roma.it · italia.it

Collegiata di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Church with origins in the IX century, Romanesque in origin and then renovated in several styles, overlooking the lake at the top of the historic center; inside frescoes and, on the main altar, a painting of the Assumption Madonna. Sunday Mass at 11:30.

  • Where Piazza della Collegiata, Anguillara Sabazia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.anguillara-sabazia.roma.it · italia.it

Museo Neolitico

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in October 2006 in the former agricultural cooperative near the station, it is dedicated to the village of Marmot, the oldest neolithic lake village in Western Europe, submerged at 7.5 meters deep in front of the promontory: there emerged rectangular huts, ceramics, five pirogues and instruments in obsidian Lipari. The strong piece is a 9,5 m long 8,000-year-old piroga; a small room projects a teaching cardboard and film on recovery.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo della civiltà contadina Augusto Montori

Museums · Themed

Born in the nineties from the collection of agricultural tools, pastoral, handicraft and domestic and managed by the Association Sabate: tools for pastoral and viticulture, grape presses, a vintage kitchen with early twentieth century furnishings and documents on fishing in the lake. open Saturday and Sunday 10-19, on weekdays only by appointment (MiC)

Lago di Bracciano e Parco Bracciano-Martignano

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lake occupies the basin of the Sabatino volcano off and is part of the regional park of about 17,000 hectares between Tuscia and Rome; sailing, rowing, surfing and birdwatching is practiced.

Local life and traditions · Food and wine
