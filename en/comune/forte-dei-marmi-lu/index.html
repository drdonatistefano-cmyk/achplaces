What to see in Forte dei Marmi

Versilia's seaside resort was born around the Fortino Lorena in 1778, today the seat of the Museum of satire and caricature, with the pier from which the marble of the Apuans was embarked, the fine sandy beach and the villas of Vittoria Apuana and Rome Imperiale; between the museums the house-atelier of the sculptor Ugo Guidi and Villa Bertelli.

Fortino e Museo della satira e della caricatura

Museums · Themed

The Fortino was built in 1778 by Grand Duke Pietro Leopoldo of Lorraine, following the model of the forts of Marina di Bibbona and Marina di Castagneto, to protect the boarding of the Apuan marble and favor the settlement: around it the town passed from a marble store to a seaside resort. Today it houses the Museum of satire and caricature, a center of conservation and research with original drawings by historical and contemporary authors from all over the world, a library of over 2,500 volumes, video library, multimedia archive and temporary exhibitions.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Pontile

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Symbol of the town, born to board the marble blocks of the Apuan Alps; between 1876 and 1877 the cargo was simplified on a project by Giovanni Costantini and the port of call remained in use until the mid-Twentieth century. Destroyed by the Nazis in the Second World War, the current pier (about 275-300 meters) is a post-war reconstruction, today surf spots and panoramic view at sunset on the Riviera del Marmo.

  • Where Pontile di Forte dei Marmi, Forte dei Marmi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Spiaggia e passeggiata a mare

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

A long golden sandy beach with a shallow bottom that gently slopes, suitable for children, among the first seaside resorts in Italy, frequented by royal and wealthy holidaymakers from the end of the nineteenth century; historic establishments with colored wooden cabins and an evening walk suitable for bikes, walks and skates.

  • Where Lungomare, Forte dei Marmi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com

Casa museo Ugo Guidi

Museums · House museums · Free entry

House and workshop of the sculptor Ugo Guidi (1912-1977), born in Querceta and professor at the Academy of Fine Arts of Carrara, with his working instruments and about 600 works between sculptures, drawings and temperas: from the marbles of the 1940s to the tufi of the 1950s, to the studies of horses and motherhood and marble totems. It is part of the Memory Houses of Tuscany and hosts contemporary art exhibitions; free visit only by reservation (tel. 348 3020538, museumougoguidi@gmail.com).

  • Where Via Matteo Civitali 33, Forte dei Marmi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museougoguidi.it

Sources: museougoguidi.it · cultura.gov.it · visittuscany.com · italia.it

Villa Bertelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Nineteenth-century residence in the district of Vittoria Apuana, with a theatre where evening concerts and spaces for exhibitions and events are held. The rooms tell the story of the landscape and art in Forte dei Marmi, with works by the sculptor Arturo Dazzi (1881-1966) and the masters of the twentieth century related to Versilia, including Carrà, Soffici, Rosai, de Chirico, Campigli and Guttuso, and a drawing by Henry Moore.

  • Where Viale Giuseppe Mazzini 200, Forte dei Marmi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.villabertelli.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: villabertelli.it · visittuscany.com · italia.it

Vittoria Apuana e Roma Imperiale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Two historic neighborhoods of villas: Vittoria Apuana, with elegant villas in large parks and views of the Apuan Alps, home to antique markets in winter; Rome Imperiale, the green and tree-lined garden city, ideal for cycling.

  • Where Vittoria Apuana e Roma Imperiale, Forte dei Marmi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Mercato settimanale

Local life and traditions · Historic markets · Free entry

Market with elegant stalls, every Wednesday from 8 to 14.30 in Piazza Marconi and in summer also on Sundays; on winter weekends antique markets in Piazza Marconi and Vittoria Apuana.

  • Where Piazza Marconi, Forte dei Marmi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
