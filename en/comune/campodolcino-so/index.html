What to see in Campodolcino

Country of the Spluga Valley, in a narrow glacial valley born from the union of several nuclei, linked to the traffic with Switzerland along the Via Spluga; you can visit the Museum of the Via Spluga, the bridge called Roman on the Caurga della Rabbiosa and Fraciscio the birthplace of Saint Luigi Guanella.

Museo della Via Spluga e della Val San Giacomo (MuVis)

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In a sixteenth-century building in the hamlet Corti, it tells the story of the valley and Via Spluga, a link between the Mediterranean and the lands north of the Alps: crafts, winter tourism, rural housing, female work, games and trade. It preserves historical fir staüe, including one from 1576, a section on the Opera Pia S. Antonio founded by St. Louis Guanella at the end of the nineteenth century, a scientific section on the environment with a laboratory on water and energy and the eighteenth-century chapel of St. Anthony. Open every day except Monday 9.30-12.30, Saturday 16-18.

  • Where Piazza S. Antonio, frazione Corti, 23021 Campodolcino
  • Entry €5, reduced €3, free under 6 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: valchiavenna.com · italia.it

Ponte Romano e Caurga della Rabbiosa

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

The stone arched bridge on the Via della Valle Spluga is traditionally known as the Roman bridge, but the current structure derives from successive reconstructions to keep the passage in use. Next to it, the Caurga is a deep gorge dug into the rock by the Rabbiosa stream, with vertical walls and narrow passages; paths, parapets and equipped passages lead to the bridge and to the viewpoints on the gorge, accessible all year round.

caution in case of flood of the stream (Valchiavenna)

Casa natale di San Luigi Guanella a Fraciscio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In Fraciscio was born on 19 December 1842 Don Luigi Guanella; the house, built by his father in the first half of the nineteenth century and bought by the Guanelliani in 1948, preserves the room of family prayer, the native room and the study with the ceiling painted by the saint with a scene of paradise. Outside, a 2005 monument by Alfredo Vismara depicts Luigi with his sister Catherine.

  • Where Frazione Fraciscio, Campodolcino (contatto Parrocchia S. Giovanni Battista, 0343 50118)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sentieri meditativi di don Guanella (Fraciscio-Gualdera)

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Three meditative paths start from Fraciscio: the first from the Guanella house descends to the banks of the Rabbiosa stream and continues to Gualdera, a place where the saint had the intuition of his vocation; the second passes from the churchyard of the Most Holy Trinity, the third leads to the parish of St. Alexander.

  • Where Frazione Fraciscio, Campodolcino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: valtellina.it · italia.it

Starleggia

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fraction at about 1,565 m on the right side of the valley, in a panoramic position under a large rocky bastion, overlooking Pizzo Stella, Groppera, Emet and Suretta; it is known for its blue-violet peel potatoes. From Campodolcino you climb on almost all paved road and little traffic: 6.2 km and 485 m of difference in altitude, about 55 minutes by road bike.

  • Where Frazione Starleggia, Campodolcino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: valchiavenna.com · italia.it
