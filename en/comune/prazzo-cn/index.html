What to see in Prazzo

The municipality of the Upper Maira Valley consists of Upper and Lower Prazzo and the villages of Ussolo and San Michele, at the foot of Mount Cresogno (3026 m): here you can visit the Museum of Cannabis and Women's Work and two ring paths dedicated to hemp and popular religiosity.

3 places to visit in Prazzo, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della canapa e del lavoro femminile «Fremos, travai e tero»

Museums · Themed

The museum tells of the role of women in the family and society of the past and the cultivation and processing of hemp, a long source of income for the economy of the valley, with numerous tools and artifacts and the reconstruction of period environments. It is part of the Ecomuseum Alta Valle Maira; open on request (tel. 0171 999190 or 347 1032172).

  • Where Prazzo Inferiore, Prazzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website ecomuseoaltavallemaira.it

Sources: ecomuseoaltavallemaira.it · visitcuneese.it · comune.prazzo.cn.it

Sentiero della canapa e della tessitura «La vio dal charbu»

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of about 2 and a half hours (change in altitude 358 m, medium difficulty, recommended from April to October) starting from Prazzo Superiore, signposted with illustrative panels. It crosses the ancient hemp fields, the hamlet Rabiera with the mill and the beater of hemp (the only remaining in the Maira Valley) and the maceration tanks, the hamlet Church with the house of weaving, the old town hall and sundials, the museum "La memorio di nosti soulà" in Chiotto and the Museo della canna in Prazzo Inferiore.

  • Where Partenza: Prazzo Superiore, Prazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.prazzo.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Sentiero della religiosità popolare (Ussolo)

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry

Ring of about 2 and a half hours (change in altitude 450 m, for medium hikers, recommended from May to October) with departure and arrival in the village Ussolo, where you can see a fresco of Giors Boneto on an ancient tavern and the martyrdom of Santo Stefano painted in the nineteenth century on the facade of the parish. The path touches the chapel of Sant'Anna in the Vallone hamlet and numerous votive pylons, largely after the 17th century, including one with sundial at Grange Draio and two frescoes by the twentieth century painter Gauteri at Grange Passo and Grange Chioligiera.

  • Where Partenza: borgata Ussolo, Prazzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.prazzo.cn.it · visitcuneese.it
