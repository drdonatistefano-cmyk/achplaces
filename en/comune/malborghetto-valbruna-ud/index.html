What to see in Malborghetto Valbruna

Country of the Val Canale at the foot of the Julian Alps, in the Middle Ages called Bamberghetto: the seventeenth-century Venetian Palace with the Ethnographic Museum, the Napoleonic ruins of the Fort Hensel, the Museum of the Tarvisio Forest at the Bagni di Lusnizza and, in Valbruna, the paths of the Val Saisera between resonance firs and trenches of the Great War.

5 places to visit in Malborghetto Valbruna, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo Veneziano e Museo etnografico

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the Palazzo Veneziano, elegant seventeenth-century building among the most interesting in the Val Canale, the museum opened in the late 1980s tells geology and prehistory (wild Prato Pramollo and Casera Valbertad), the mine of Raibl and the local enterprises between the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, the daily life of the valley and the traditions of Christmas and Easter with the Krampus, as well as the history of the palace. Free with FVGcard.

  • Where Via Bamberga 52, Malborghetto
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo La Foresta

Museums · Science and university

Naturalistic Museum founded in 1988 by Società Autostrade e Corpo Forestale dello Stato, dedicated to ecology, flora and fauna of the 23,000 hectares of the Tarvisio Forest, with educational exhibition and outdoor botanical garden with path of about 500 meters; trails lead to the sulphurous springs of the Bagni di Lusnizza. Monday to Saturday 8-14.

  • Where Via Acqua Solforosa, Malborghetto Valbruna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Forte Hensel

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built in 1809 on the Tschalawai promontory by the captain of the genius Friedrich Hensel, who died defending him from the French in May 1809; modernized in 1884 for 18 cannons, he was reactivated in the Great War and reduced in rubble by about 4,500 shots of the Italian artillery. There are ruins between the vegetation, a tunnel with slits, the monument of the Lion of Hensel and the view of the Val Canale.

the source indicates closed access paths for jobs and remains largely unvisitable in safety

  • Where Presso la galleria «Forte», Malborghetto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Sentiero Animalborghetto

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Viewpoint

Easy theme path for children, inspired by a fairy tale on the animals of the forest (caprioli, badgers, wild boars, hares, ibex): starts from the square of Palazzo Veneziano at Casa Krahvogl, crosses a forest of pine, hornbeam and ash trees and rises to the clearing of the chapel of Calvary, panoramic, with return along the ancient Via Crucis. At least an hour.

  • Where Piazza Palazzo Veneziano, Malborghetto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Val Saisera e Sentiero degli alberi di risonanza

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From Valbruna (Hotel Saisera, 820 m) to the Nabois plateau (1,050 m) under the walls of the Little Nabois: 5.5 km easy (about 2 hours and a half) between red firs of resonance, precious wood for the violin, with panels on the theme, the small art park Other Memories, the Sabuata spring and the theme park of the Great War Abschnitt Saisera with trenches and Austro-Hungarian posts.

  • Where Valbruna, Malborghetto Valbruna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it
