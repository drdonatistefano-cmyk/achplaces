What to see in Sambuca di Sicilia

Borgo with the Arab quarter of the seven Saracen alleys and a long course of churches and palaces between the seventeenth and nineteenth centuries, from the Theatre L'Idea to Palazzo Panitteri with the archaeological museum; in the territory the archaeological area of Monte Adranone and the reserve of Monte Genuardo.

Area archeologica di Monte Adranone

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On a hill near the village, the ruins of a center lived between the 8th and the 3rd century B.C. on the border between the Sican and the Punic-Elimo world, then Carthaginian fortress: necropolis with chamber and chest tombs, walls with the South and North gates, a sacred area with betiles, a tripartite temple on the acropolis and a Hellenistic sanctuary of Demeter and Kore. Open every day 9-17.30.

  • Where Monte Adranone, Sambuca di Sicilia
  • Entry €6, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: cultura.gov.it · parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · italia.it

Museo archeologico di Palazzo Panitteri

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The noble floor of the palace, born from the tower of the castle of the end of the sixteenth century, houses the archaeological museum of the Park of the Valley of the Temples, with Greek vases and the complement of the CLXX tomb of the middle of the fifth century BC. Open Mon-Fri 9-13, Sat-dom 9-18.

  • Where Via Panitteri 1, Sambuca di Sicilia
  • Entry €6, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Quartiere arabo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient heart of the village, formed by seven Saracen alleys similar to a casbah around the fortress of Zabut, with Navarro Square, the purres (ancient tuff quarries), Palazzo Amodei, the Rosary church with courtyard of 1752 and, at the top of the hill, the Matrix Church, closed to worship since 1968 after the earthquake of Belice.

  • Where Piazza Navarro, Sambuca di Sicilia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Corso Umberto I, palazzi e chiese

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry
