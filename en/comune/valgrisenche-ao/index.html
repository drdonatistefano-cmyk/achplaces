What to see in Valgrisenche

Wild valley closed by the Beauregard dam, where wool drags are still woven on wooden frames.

4 places to visit in Valgrisenche, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Diga e lago di Beauregard

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

1954 dam that closes the valley, today lowered and inserted in the landscape; the outside you visit freely, the interior by reservation; easy path of 5 km along the lake.

  • Where Valgrisenche
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

I tessuti della Valgrisenche - Lo Drap

Museums · Themed

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Grato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
