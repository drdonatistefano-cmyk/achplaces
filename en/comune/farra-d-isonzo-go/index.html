What to see in Farra d'Isonzo

Municipality of Isontino between Gorizia and Gradisca, known for the eighteenth-century village of Colmello di Grotta, home to the Museum of the Friulian peasant civilization, and for the Museum of Automobile and Technology with the assembly line of the Ford T.

Museo della civiltà contadina friulana di Colmello di Grotta

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

At the end of the eighteenth century, the Strassoldo family built the village of Colmello di Grotta as a residence and a place of work for about ten families of settlers, who lived there until the early sixties of the twentieth century; restored by the municipality, the complex has been a museum since 1993. It reconstructs kitchen, bedroom, cellar and stable, blacksmith shops, carpenters, casket, carraio and shoemaker, the cycle of vine and corn, the breeding of silkworms, the birth of rural coffers and popular religiosity. Open on request.

  • Where Strada della Grotta 8, Farra d'Isonzo
  • Entry €4, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it

Museo dell'Automobile e della Tecnica

Local life and traditions · Motors · Free entry

The building takes up the façade of the first Ford Italia headquarters in Trieste (1923) and inside reproduces the assembly line of the Ford model T. It exhibits vintage cars, including a Cleveland type A of 1897 and an Oldsmobile of 1902, motorcycles, bicycles and about a thousand objects, from measuring instruments to transceivers, including the full radio station of a Soviet ship. Open all year on request.

  • Where Via Gorizia 150, Farra d'Isonzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: touringclub.it · cultura.gov.it
