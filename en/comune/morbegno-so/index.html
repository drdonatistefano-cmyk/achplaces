What to see in Morbegno

The centre of the lower Valtellina, already the junction of the Strada Priula towards Bergamo, with an ancient village of narrow streets, tall palaces and ancient cellars; to see the rococo of Palazzo Malacrida, the Collegiate Church of San Giovanni Battista, the sanctuary of the Assunta and the Civic Museum of Natural History.

7 places to visit in Morbegno, in the province of Sondrio (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico di Morbegno

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Ancient village of narrow streets, high and centenarian palaces and ancient cellars, gathered around squares described as sobriety monumental. In the sixteenth century the Strada Priula connected Morbegno to Bergamo, making it a point of passage between the Republic of Venice and the north.

  • Where Piazza San Giovanni, Morbegno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it · touringclub.it

Palazzo Malacrida

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace of the eighteenth century wanted by the noble family Malacrida in the upper part of the city, the ancient district Scimicà, considered the most significant example of the rococo in Valtellina. The staircase has a medallion of Gian Pietro Romegialli with the Rat of Ganymede; in the salon of honour, on the first floor, the squares of Giuseppe Coduri said the Vignoli and the vault of Cesare Ligari with the triumph of truth about the lie. Guided tours by reservation at the Tourist Board Porte di Valtellina.

  • Where Contrada Scimicà, Morbegno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: portedivaltellina.it · valtellina.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Collegiata di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church founded in the 16th century and rebuilt in monumental Baroque forms, with canvases of Ligari and Petrini. At Christmas hosts the event Christmas Art.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: valtellina.it · italia.it

Santuario dell'Assunta (San Lorenzo)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Sanctuary on the edge of the town, begun in 1418 and consecrated in 1506, with frescoes and a grandiose golden wooden ancona on the main altar.

Museo civico di storia naturale

Museums · Science and university

Founded in 1974 with the donation of 500 minerals by Giacomo Perego, since 1983 it is located in the eighteenth-century Palazzo Gualteroni. It preserves about 1,500 minerals and fossils, including the Cassinisia orobica symbol of the museum, over 35,000 insects, 1,000 birds and 1,300 mammals, with reconstructions of alpine environments, showcases on wolf and bear and skull of a knocked bear in Val Gerola in 1887. Open Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 9-12 and 14:30-17:30.

  • Where Via Cortivacci 2, Morbegno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it
