What to see in Mentana

The ancient Nomentum, then castle of the Crescenzi, the Orsini and the Borghese, linked to the battle of 3 November 1867 between the Garibaldi and the papal and French troops: the Garibaldi museum, the Park of the Remembrance with the altar-bone, the MuCaM archaeological museum and the medieval village with the Borghese Palace and Palazzo Crescenzi.

Museo garibaldino (MuGA) - Museo nazionale della Campagna garibaldina dell'Agro romano

Museums · City history · Free entry

It preserves the relics of the Garibaldi campaign of 1867 for the liberation of Rome, ended with the defeat of Mentana on November 3rd: uniforms garibaldine, weapons to avant-load and white, rare nineteenth-century photographs, autographs of Garibaldi, the sabre of Menotti Garibaldi, a collection of carbonary relics and a tie of Mazzini. Reestablished by the State to the City in 1997; in 2017, for the 150 years of the battle, a multimedia wing dedicated to the heroes of the Risorgimento was opened. Touring hours: Tuesday to Sunday 10-12, Monday closed.

  • Where Via della Rocca 2/4, Piazza della Repubblica, Mentana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: touringclub.it · visitlazio.com

MuCaM - Museo civico archeologico di Mentana e dell'Agro Nomentano

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 2020 in the Galleria Borghese, in seven sections exhibits finds of Nomentum and Ager nomentanus: reliefs and Roman marble sarcophagi (1st century BC-II century A.D.) and materials of the sacred area of Casale Santucci, including an altar with a procession of bulls destined for sacrifice; the last section is a map of ancient marbles re-used in the alleys of the village.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Mentana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Memorial park near the Garibaldi Museum, with the ara-hosary of the fallen Garibaldi. Every 3 November in the square is celebrated the anniversary of the battle.

  • Entry Free

Borgo medievale, Palazzo Borghese e Palazzo Crescenzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historic centre preserves the medieval structure: St. Nicholas Square with the 18th-century staircase in curved brackets and the church of St. Nicholas, rebuilt in the eighteenth century on medieval foundations, the Renaissance Palazzo Borghese and Palazzo Crescenzi, dating back to the year 1000 and now home of the Municipal Library. From the village the view opens onto the plain of the Tiber.

  • Where Piazza San Nicola, Mentana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Riserva naturale Macchia di Gattaceca e Macchia del Barco

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Regional reserve of about 1,000 hectares between Mentana, Monterotondo and Sant'Angelo Romano, managed by the Metropolitan City: hills mosaic between pastures and woods (Macchia del Barco, Bosco di Gattaceca, Monte San Francesco, Bosco Nardi), karst phenomena with sinkholes and swallowholes and a stretch of the ancient Via Nomentum-Eretum. Paths CAI 436-439; Welcome Area in via Nomentana km 21+200.

  • Where Via Nomentana 320, Mentana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com
