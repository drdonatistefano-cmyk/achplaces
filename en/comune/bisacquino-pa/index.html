What to see in Bisacquino

Country of the Sicani Mountains, on a plateau among fields and vineyards, dominated by the Mother Church of San Giovanni Battista; home of the director Frank Capra, preserves the Museum of the clock in the shop of the Scibetta watchmakers and, on Monte Triona, the seventeenth-century Sanctuary of the Madonna del Balzo.

5 places to visit in Bisacquino, in the province of Palermo (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo dell'orologio

Museums · Themed

Set up in the artisan workshop of the Scibetta family, watchmakers from the 18th century to the 1980s and masters in the construction of tower clocks: exhibits watches, tools, mechanisms, drawings, photographs and a rare universal geographic clock.

  • Where Corso Umberto I, Bisacquino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Chiesa Madre di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The imposing building that dominates the village and is the center of the historic center, witness to the ancient history of the village.

  • Where Centro storico, Bisacquino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Roads with ancient palaces, sunny squares, small museums of local history and craft shops that preserve traditional crafts, on a plateau overlooking fields, vineyards and green areas. It is the country of origin of the director Frank Capra, to whom the Borgo Film Fest is dedicated.

  • Where Centro storico, Bisacquino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info

Campagna dei Monti Sicani

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Rural trails and views of fields, vineyards and green areas, suitable for excursions, photography and quiet walks.

  • Where Dintorni di Bisacquino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Santuario della Madonna del Balzo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

A 17th century shrine on Monte Triona, the sacred place most dear to the Bisacquinese, on a ridge reached by a panoramic road; the floods of 2015 and the fire of 2017 have made the slopes unstable, the object of regional works with networks and paramassi barriers.

closed since August 2017 due to risk of falling boulders, area forbidden to vehicles and pedestrians; still closed in June 2023 with ongoing work (Sicily Region)

  • Where Monte Triona, Bisacquino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: regione.sicilia.it · italia.it
