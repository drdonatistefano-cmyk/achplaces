What to see in Orroli

Sarcidano country on a plateau between the lakes of Flumendosa and Mulargia, known for the nuraghe Arrubiu, the red Giant with twenty-one towers, for the naturalistic and archaeological park of Su Motti with its domus de Janas and for the manor house Omu Axiu, ethnographic museum and embroidery.

Nuraghe Arrubiu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Nuragic complex of about 5,000 square meters and 21 towers on the relief of Su Pranu, above the medium valley of the Flumendosa, inhabited between the XIV and IX century BC: the mast, 15 meters high (about half of the original), preserves the dome in tholos and is surrounded by a bastion of five towers and a wall of seven, with courtyards equipped with cisterns, fireplaces and huts. In the 2nd century BC the Romans transformed it into a wine plant, of which two tanks remain; ceramics from the Peloponnese attest contacts with the Mycenaean world. The name, "Gigante rosso" comes from the reddish lichens that cover it. Admission for a fee.

Parco di Su Motti

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Archaeological and botanical park above the village, in a basaltic amphitheatre with centuries-old oaks, holm oaks and maquis, inhabited by the Neolithic (3800 BC). The path on the left, easy, leads to the necropolis of about fifteen domus de Janas excavated in the walls of sa Carona Arrubia; the one on the right, challenging, rises to the plateau of Taccu Idda with wide views. There are also the polylobed nuraghe is Allonis, the corridor nuraghe Su Motti and the drywalls of the "tancas" .

Casa museo Omu Axiu

Museums · Themed

A manor house of more than 1,200 square meters, also called Casa Vargiu, of the Vargiu family since the sixteenth century, today also a hotel spread: on the ground floor the environments of everyday life and the peasant trades of Sarcidano (tool room, bread and sausage storage, bedroom), on the upper floor the embroidering museum with clothes and family accessories. Paid access with guided tour included, at reception and restaurant times.

  • Where Via Roma 46, Orroli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Casa del Nuraghe Arrubiu

Museums · Archaeological

In an ancient manor house, twelve thematic rooms with panels on the researches at the nuraghe Arrubiu, conducted from 1981 to 1996 and resumed in 2012, and on the protohistory of Sardinia and the Mediterranean. Access for a fee, guided tours only in the afternoon.

temporarily closed for renovation and adaptation work (SardiniaCultura)

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Vincenzo martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Renovated in the sixteenth century in Gothic-Aragonese style on the ruins of a Christian temple of the sixth century, with red porphyry bell tower.

Laghi del Flumendosa e del Mulargia

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

The two artificial lakes surround the plateau of Orroli and partially cover the gorges of the Flumendosa: the Mulargia is nestled between reliefs that descend to the shores, on the Flumendosa valleys and sky mirror into the water. Hiking can be done by boat or canoe.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
