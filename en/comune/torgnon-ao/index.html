What to see in Torgnon

Sunny village at 1489 meters in the middle of the Matterhorn valley, with the villages of Petit-Monde and the view from the Colle di San Pantaleone.

3 places to visit in Torgnon, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo etnografico Petit-Monde

Museums · Themed

In the villages of Triatel and Étirol, between raccard, grandze and grenier, tells the history, work and social organization of this small ancient world; the visit ends at the mill on the stream.

  • Where Triatel, Torgnon
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Martino e museo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Pulpit in carved wood, wooden crucifix of the sixteenth century and a very rare Christ in the column of the fourteenth century.

  • Where Mongnod, Torgnon
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Colle di San Pantaleone

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint
