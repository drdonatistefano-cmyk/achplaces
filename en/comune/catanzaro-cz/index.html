What to see in Catanzaro

The capital of Calabria in the narrowest point of Italy, between Ionian and Tyrrhenian: the center on the hills with the Duomo, the Basilica of the Immaculate and the complex of San Giovanni on the remains of the Norman castle, the Bisantis Bridge of Riccardo Morandi, the Mediterranean Biodiversity Park and, on the coast, Catanzaro Lido.

15 places to visit in Catanzaro, in the province of Catanzaro (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The first church was built in 1121, in Norman age, dedicated to Saint Mary Assunta and Saints Peter and Paul; restored several times, it has the chapel of the patron Saint Vitalian of 1309 with the relics, bronze doors and a bell tower of about 42 meters with the statue of the Assunta. Inside is a Madonna of the Angels of Messinese school and a Madonna with 16th century Child by Antonello Gagini.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Catanzaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Basilica dell'Immacolata

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Built in 1254 in the heart of the city and enlarged over the centuries, with chapels of noble families from the altars in polychrome marble and stucco; it preserves a Neapolitan wooden statue of San Rocco of the eighteenth century and wooden statues from the suppressed church of Santa Caterina.

  • Where Catanzaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · calabriastraordinaria.it

Complesso monumentale del San Giovanni

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built between the fifteenth and seventeenth centuries on the remains of the Norman castle: it includes the Tower of Charles V, the walls of the castle, a large panoramic square and an exhibition area on two floors; since 2017 the ancient secrets of the castle are open.

  • Where Catanzaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Ponte Bisantis

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Designed by Riccardo Morandi and completed in 1962: his 120-meter-high centimeter was then the largest in the world; today it is among the highest arched bridges in Europe.

Catanzaro Lido

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The neighborhood on the Gulf of Squillace, a few kilometers from the center, with bathing establishments, the monument "Ancora" to the fallen of the sea, the tower of about 30 meters, the rest of the factory of tannin Ledoga, and the "Gutta" , circular buoy of 6 meters for the mooring of the boats.
