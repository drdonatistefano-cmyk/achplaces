What to see in Grizzana Morandi

4 places to visit in Grizzana Morandi, in the province of Bologna (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocchetta Mattei

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Count Cesare Mattei's nineteenth-century eclectic castle, father of electromeopathy, between Moorish and medieval styles; in 2026 he reopened the Arab wing, closed for thirty years.

  • Where Via Rocchetta 46a, Riola, Grizzana Morandi
  • Entry From €12 single tour, €20 full tour with the Arab wing reopened in 2026 (source of news); the Metropolitan City indicates €10
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.rocchetta-mattei.com

Sources: rocchetta-mattei.com · appenninobolognese.cittametropolitana.bo.it · bolognawelcome.com · siviaggia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
