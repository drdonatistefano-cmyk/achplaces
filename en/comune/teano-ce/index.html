What to see in Teano

The ancient Teanum Sidicinum at the foot of Roccamonfina: the great Roman theatre renovated in the strict age, the national archaeological museum and the cathedral of San Clemente rebuilt after the war.

3 places to visit in Teano, in the province of Caserta (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Teatro romano di Teanum Sidicinum

History and architecture · Archaeology

Built at the end of the 2nd century BC on a large terrace with a temple perhaps of Apollo, renovated under Augustus and then by Septimius Severus with a scenic building of three orders of columns in rare marbles about 26 meters high; cavea of about 85 meters.

The MiC card indicates the site closed every day

  • Where Teano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Museo archeologico nazionale di Teanum Sidicinum

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In a medieval building with two naves: votive of the sanctuaries of Loreto and Fondo Ruozzo, furnishings of the necropolis from the end of the 6th century BC to the imperial age, the statue of Demeter of the 6th century BC and the late antique mosaic with Epiphany.

  • Where Via Nicola Gigli 23, Teano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Cattedrale di San Clemente

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Probably risen in the second half of the 11th century and consecrated in 1106, with three naves on granite columns with Corinthian capitals; facade modified in 1603, eighteenth-century chapel of San Paride, rebuilt on a project by Roberto Pane after the destruction of the war.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Teano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: sigecweb.beniculturali.it · cultura.gov.it
