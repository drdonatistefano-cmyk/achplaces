What to see in Faenza

The city of majolica: the International Museum of Ceramics, the arcade squares and neoclassical palaces.

6 places to visit in Faenza, in the province of Ravenna (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers 4 of the 12 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Museo Internazionale delle Ceramiche (MIC)

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Founded in 1908, according to the regional catalogue, the largest collection of ceramics in the world: from the Faenza majolica to the works of Picasso, Matisse, Fontana and Burri.

  • Where Viale Baccarini 19, Faenza
  • Entry For a fee: €12 full (secondary source); with the FaenzaMuseiCard discount of €2 on the second and third museum; does not adhere to the Sunday museum
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.micfaenza.org
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: micfaenza.org · italia.it · bbcc.regione.emilia-romagna.it · palazzomilzetti.cultura.gov.it · artsupp.com · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Piazza del Popolo e Piazza della Libertà

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The two twin squares of the centre, with double loggias, the Palazzo del Podestà and the Cathedral of St. Peter.

  • Where Centro storico, Faenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Palazzo Milzetti, Museo nazionale dell'età neoclassica

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

One of the most important neoclassical residences in Italy, with rooms decorated by Felice Giani for the Milzetti family in the Napoleonic age.

  • Where Via Tonducci 15, Faenza
  • Entry €5 full, €7 with exhibition, €2 from 18 to 25 years, free under 18 and the first Sunday of the month (afternoon); FaenzaMuseiCard
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website palazzomilzetti.cultura.gov.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: palazzomilzetti.cultura.gov.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo Carlo Zauli

Museums · Modern art

  • Where Via della Croce 6, Faenza
  • Entry To be checked on the official website
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Torre di Oriolo dei Fichi

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Hesagonal tower of 1476 among the vineyards of the Faenza hills, with panoramic terrace at 360 degrees.

  • Where Oriolo dei Fichi, Faenza
  • Entry To be checked on the official website
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

  • Where Faenza
