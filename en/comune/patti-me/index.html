What to see in Patti

City on the Gulf of the same name with the Norman Cathedral of San Bartolomeo and the late Roman villa with great mosaics; in the hamlet of Tindari the ancient Tyndaris with the Greek-Roman theater, the sanctuary of the Black Madonna and, at the foot of the head, the reserve of the Lakes of Marinello.

Area archeologica di Tindari

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Greco-Roman city founded by Dionigi di Syracuse in 397-396 BC: you can visit the Greek-Roman theatre overlooking the sea and the Aeolian Islands, seat of the Tindari Festival, part of the urban system with three decumans, the houses peristilius, the thermal baths, the Romanophylle called Basilica and the walls. At the entrance the Antiquarium with statues of togati and a head of Augustus. A cumulative ticket with the Roman Villa di Patti €11.20 (reduced €5), valid for three days.

  • Where Via del Teatro Greco, Tindari, Patti
  • Entry €6, reduced €4
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.visitsicily.info
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: cultura.gov.it · parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · visitsicily.info

Santuario della Madonna Nera di Tindari

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

It rises on the promontory, on the site of the ancient acropolis; destroyed by pirates, it was rebuilt in the middle of the sixteenth century, with a Latin cross with three naves and large windows. It preserves the wooden statue of the Black Madonna, of probable oriental origin; the pilgrimages culminate on 7 and 8 September.

  • Where Tindari, Patti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Villa romana di Patti e Antiquarium

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Villa of the fourth century A.D. discovered during the works of the Palermo-Messina motorway, with large polychrome mosaics of African taste comparable to those of the Villa del Casale and Tellaro. The small antiquarium, opened in 2001, exhibits ceramics, amphorae, terracotta, goldsmiths, coins, a bronze circus card and marbles of the villa. cumulative ticket with Tindari €11.20 (reduced €5).

  • Where Via Giovanni XXIII 2, Patti Marina
  • Entry €6, reduced €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.visitsicily.info
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Cattedrale di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Cathedral Basilica founded in 1094 by the Grand Count Ruggero I d'Altavilla, next to the bishop's palace: it houses the sarcophagus of Queen Adelasia, wife of the Count, and a Adoration of the shepherds attributed to William Borremans; the façade has lava stone and white marble decorations and a portal in Arab-Norman style.

  • Where Centro storico, Patti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Centro storico di Patti

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Medieval center of alleys, stairs, churches and nineteenth-century palaces, with sections of the walls of the fourteenth century; going up from the seafront towards Via Mustazzo you will meet Villa Pisani.

  • Where Centro storico, Patti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Riserva dei Laghetti di Marinello

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural reserve oriented at the foot of Capo Tindari, of about 400 hectares of Mediterranean maquis, with three brackish lakes, the cliff, the wide beach of Marinello and, behind the head, the caves of Mongiove with natural arches, faraglioni and the cave of Donna Villa.

  • Where Capo Tindari, Patti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Sentiero Coda di Volpe

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Panoramic path of medium difficulty, with steep stretches, starting from Via Monsignor Pullano and connecting the lagoon of Marinello to the sanctuary, with views of about 180° from the Gulf of Patti to the Aeolian Islands and Capo Milazzo.

  • Where Via Monsignor Pullano, Tindari, Patti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Marina di Patti e spiagge

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Over ten kilometres of largely free beaches; Marina di Patti is the seaside and nightlife center, with the bars of the promenade and the summer breakfast with granite and brioche, while Mongiove is a seaside village suitable for a bath.

  • Where Marina di Patti, Patti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Museo etnografico di Sorrentini

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 1995 thanks to the inhabitants of the village, in the salon of the archpriest Salvatore Sberna: an ethnographic section with agricultural tools, tools of shoemaker and carpenters, pattese ceramics, and one of the sacred objects of the parish with a sixteenth-century pivial, vestments of the eighteenth century and wooden statues of the seventeenth-seventeenth century.

  • Where Chiesa Madre, Sorrentini, Patti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitsicily.info

Local life and traditions · Food and wine
