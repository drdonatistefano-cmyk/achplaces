What to see in Macomer

Marghine Center on the basalt plateau: the Nuraghe Santa Barbara symbol of the city, the complex of Tamuri with the betiles, the domus de janas of Filigosa and the museum of the ancient arts.

6 places to visit in Macomer, in the province of Nuoro (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Nuraghe Santa Barbara

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Symbol of Macomer on a basalt plateau on the slopes of Mount Manai: 15 meters high central tower, four tower bastion and a large village around.

  • Where Macomer
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Complesso archeologico di Tamuli

History and architecture · Archaeology

Nuraghe, village of a dozen huts and three tombs of giants of the Middle and Recent Bronze Age (XVI-XIII century B.C.); next to the first tomb six conical betiles, three with female forms, called "perdas marmuradas."

  • Where Macomer
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Necropoli di Filigosa

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Four domus de janas excavated in limestone in the eneolithic age, which gave its name to the culture of Filigosa-Abealzu; on the slopes of the hill the Nuraghe Ruju, monotorre of 13 meters of diameter.

  • Where Macomer
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Museo etnografico «Le Arti Antiche»

Museums · Themed
