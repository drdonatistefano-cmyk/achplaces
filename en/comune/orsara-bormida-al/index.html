What to see in Orsara Bormida

Borgo dell'Alto Monferrato between Acqui and Ovada: the castle was born as a watchtower around the Mille, with the octagonal tower of the fourteenth century, the Romanesque oratory in the shelter, the ancient parish church of San Martino among the vineyards and the ethnographic museum of the peasant civilization.

Castello di Orsara Bormida

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Guard tower mentioned around the thousand in a donation of San Guido to the Church of Acqui, enlarged after the Saracen incursions and transformed in the fourteenth century into a castle with octagonal tower; in the eighteenth century a second body with cylindrical tower made it a noble residence. Floors in majolica, furnishings and frescoes with coats of arms of the XVI-XVII centuries, cellars with brick vaults. From 1598 to 1922, from 1951 of the Remondini family, which produces wine. Visits according to the official website of the castle.

Museo etnografico dell'agricoltura

Museums · Themed

Agricultural tools, furnishings and utensils of a 19th century peasant house, linens, alarm clocks collections, purses, mantillas and Borsalino hats, works by local artists, a rebuilt school classroom and documents on emigration and the Great War. Curated by the association Ursaria Amici of the Museum.

  • Where Via Repubblica Argentina 3, Orsara Bormida
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.orsarabormida.al.it

Oratorio della Santissima Annunziata (dei Disciplinanti)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish until 1676, in Lombard Romanesque style with a single nave, with traces of the building of the Mille at the base of the bell tower; until 1810 the Orsaresi buried there. Inside stuccoes and frescoes of the eighteenth century recently restored. It rises in the castle's shelter.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa campestre di San Martino (antica parrocchiale)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill about 400 meters from the village, in front of Rivalta: parish until the XVI-XVII centuries, then country church and lazzaretto during the cholera of 1894. A unique nave with a barrel vault and, on the altar, a fresco by Antonio Monevi from the late seventeenth century. You can reach it on foot or by bike on a dirt road between the rows starting from the cemetery; the interior is not open to visitors.

  • Where Località San Martino, Orsara Bormida
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.orsarabormida.al.it

Sources: comune.orsarabormida.al.it · cultura.gov.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Baroque with a single nave, built between 1660 and 1676 and enlarged in the eighteenth century: main altar in polychrome marble, choir, pulpit and carved doors, organ Agati di Pistoia; fresco of San Martino on horseback of Pietro Ivaldi known as "il Muto" and paintings by Antonio Monevi. Open during functions.

  • Where Via San Martino, Orsara Bormida
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.orsarabormida.al.it
