What to see in Nocera Superiore

The heart of the ancient Nuceria Alfaterna: the early Christian baptistery of Santa Maria Maggiore, the Rotonda, the great Hellenistic-Roman theatre of Pareti, the domus of the Decumano with mosaics and frescoes and the archaeological park with walls and necropolis.

5 places to visit in Nocera Superiore, in the province of Salerno (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Battistero paleocristiano di Santa Maria Maggiore («la Rotonda»)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A circular plan baptistery of the sixth century, built on a Roman building of the II-III century and mentioned already in 841: a dome on fifteen arches supported by pairs of columns covers the octagonal baptismal bath, according to the Municipality the second of the genus in Italy after that of San Giovanni in Laterano. Today it is about four meters below the road plan.

  • Where Via Santa Maria Maggiore 148, Nocera Superiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.nocera-superiore.sa.it

Sources: comune.nocera-superiore.sa.it

Teatro ellenistico-romano di Pareti

History and architecture · Archaeology

Built in the second half of the second century BC on the attack of the southern walls, restored after the earthquake of 62 and the eruption of 79 and used until the fourth century: scene front with three niches, pulpitum in cross-linked work and orchestra paved in marble of almost 24 meters of diameter, the largest documented example in Campania according to the Municipality.

  • Where Via Pareti, Nocera Superiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.comune.nocera-superiore.sa.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.nocera-superiore.sa.it · cultura.gov.it

Domus del Decumano di Nuceria

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Roman house of the I-II century A.D. excavated between 1979 and 1984: atrium with impluvium in marble, rooms and two shops with dolia, mosaic floors and frescoed walls in fourth style, a stretch of the lower decuman with an inscribed altar and a dozen late Christian burials in the garden.

  • Where Via L. Petrosino 55, Nocera Superiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.nocera-superiore.sa.it

Sources: comune.nocera-superiore.sa.it

Parco archeologico di Nuceria: mura e necropoli

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

The ancient city, born in the 6th century BC on about 1,200 for 950 meters: the walls of the 2nd century BC thick about one and a half meters and five high, the necropolis of Pareti and Pizzone and the Sannitic burials with stele of San Clemente and piazza del Corso.

  • Where Via Fiuminale 6 (InfoPoint), Nocera Superiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.nocera-superiore.sa.it

Sources: comune.nocera-superiore.sa.it

Basilica santuario di Santa Maria Materdomini

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Sanctuary of great popular devotion in a monastic complex perhaps founded around the 11th century, several times rebuilt, last time in 1947.

  • Where Nocera Superiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.nocera-superiore.sa.it

Sources: comune.nocera-superiore.sa.it
