What to see in Pianello Val Tidone

Borgo della Val Tidone on a triangular shelf between the torrents Tidone and Chiarone, feud of the Dal Verme since 1378: the Rocca, now municipality, houses in the underground the Archaeological Museum of Val Tidone, while on the dorsal towards Chiarone stands the ancient Fortress of Olgisio.

Rocca d'Olgisio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Isolated on a spur of erosa sandstone on the ridge between Val Tidone and Val Chiarone, it is among the most evocative and ancient castles of Piacentino: in the X and XI centuries belonged to the monks of San Savino, then to those of San Sisto until 1296, it was taken by Obizzo Landi in 1314 and sold to the Church in 1322. In the inner courtyard a well 50 meters deep; some rooms house a B&B.

Museo archeologico della Val Tidone

Museums · Archaeological

In three rooms in the basement of the Rocca Dal Verme, it collects prehistoric and protohistoric finds (from the sixth millennium to the fifth century BC), Roman and medieval found in Val Tidone.

Rocca Dal Verme e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The historic nucleus, on a triangular shelf between the furrows of Tidone and Chiarone, is reached over a bridge; from 1378 it was a fief of the Dal Verme, who made it with Bobbio and the fortress of Olgisio one of their main cornerstones. The Rocca, the result of successive transformations and the very articulated plant, is now the seat of the town hall.

  • Where Pianello Val Tidone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitemilia.com
