What to see in Campo Tures

Val di Tures village in the valleys of the Natural Park Vedrette di Ries-Aurina, Orange Flag of Touring, dominated by Castel Tures, one of the best preserved castles of South Tyrol; around the late-Gothic parish and, in Caminata, the Riva waterfalls.

Castel Tures

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built by the lords of Tures in the twenties of the thirteenth century with the mast on the mountain, extended to residence in the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries; since 1977 it is the Südtiroler Burgeninstitut. It has 64 rooms, 24 covered in wood with coffered ceilings: armory, library, Hall of the Princes, the Chamber of the Infirms with 26 portraits of children (1564-67) and the Stube of the ghosts linked to the legend of Margarethe von Taufers; the chapel has frescoes by Friedrich Pacher and a Tardoromanic crucifix of the thirteenth century. It hosts the permanent exhibition "Hunters and prey" on the history of hunting.

  • Where Via Castello di Tures 4, Campo Tures
  • Entry €12, reduced €9, children 6-16 €6
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Parrocchiale dell'Assunta e cimitero

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Late-Gothic church built by Valentin Winkler between 1503 and 1527, with high steeple bell tower visible from afar; it preserves a high altar of the early sixteenth century inspired by the altar of Creglingen of Riemenschneider, a Gothic pulpit in stone by Leonhard Fabri, the Assumption of Karl Henrici (1783) and frescoes of the fifteenth century re-emerged from the restoration. In the cemetery, tombstones of local nobles and the fifteenth-century chapel of San Michele with three naves with upper ossuary.

  • Where Campo Tures
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Museo parrocchiale

Museums · Old masters

Set up in the former barn to protect sacred works of art of the nearby churches, after the thefts of the sixties and seventies: among these a Madonna enthroned in 1430 and the original statues of the altar of San Nicolò di Caminata.

Chiesa di San Nicolò a Caminata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Frescoes of 1425 by Erasmus da Brunico with stories of St. Nicholas, rediscovered in the restoration of 1996-97, and keys of vault painted in the early sixteenth century with saints, portraits of nobles and a monk making a smile; copies of the original statues are on display on the altar.

  • Where Caminata, Campo Tures
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Casa Neumelans

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Built in the sixteenth century in twelve months by Judge Hans Fieger, of the family of bankers who had received the castle from Massimiliano: Renaissance forms with four round corner towers on a square base, erker and steep roof. It is a private residence and can only be seen from the outside.

  • Where Campo Tures
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Cascate di Riva e Sentiero di San Francesco

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Three waterfalls of the Riva river in the Vedrette di Ries-Aurina Natural Park, fed by the melting of the snows and more spectacular in summer: the first at about 20 minutes (fit for strollers), the second at 40, the third at 55; the highest makes a jump of 42 meters and is admired by a suspended bridge. The San Francesco Trail, with 10 meditation stations and wooden sculptures, rises in about 75 minutes to the chapel of Francesco and Chiara.

  • Where Parcheggio Cantuccio/Winkel, Caminata di Tures
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · touringclub.it · italia.it

Centro visite del Parco naturale Vedrette di Ries-Aurina

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

The center recounts fauna, flora, geology and minerals of the park with films, plastics, panels and temporary exhibitions. The park, established in 1988, covers 31,334 hectares with 2,610 meters of altitude difference, borders the Hohe Tauern National Park and is part of the Natura 2000 network.

  • Where Piazza Municipio 8, Campo Tures
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parchi-naturali.provincia.bz.it · touringclub.it · italia.it
