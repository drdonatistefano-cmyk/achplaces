What to see in Vermiglio

Country of the woods in Val di Sole, on the road of Passo del Tonale, where from June 1915 to October 1918 the White War was fought: the Museum was born from the collection of Emilio Serra, together with the Fort Strino and the other Austro-Hungarian forts of the Tonale system.

5 places to visit in Vermiglio, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Guerra Bianca

Museums · Themed

A small museum born from the collection of Emilio Serra, who for about forty years has been searching for finds in the mountains of Val di Sole, the theatre of fighting from June 1915 to October 1918: weapons, everyday objects, uniforms and equipment, especially skis, crampons, snowshoes and the straw overshoes of the sentries. A soundroom in surround tells the letter of a soldier to his mother; the museum, fully accessible, organizes guided tours of the Fort Strino and the forts Zaccarana, Mero, Pozzi Alti and Presanella on request.

  • Where Via di Borgonuovo 12 (SS42), Vermiglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.vermigliovacanze.it

Sources: vermigliovacanze.it · visittrentino.info

Forte Strino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Austro-Hungarian fortress built in 1860-1861 at 1,538 meters to defend the last stretch of the road of the Tonale, the first work of the fortified system of the pass; well preserved, semicircular plant on two levels, houses an exhibition of finds and relics of the White War recovered from the recouping of Vermiglio, with introductory clips, luminous panels and touch screen station on the other forts of the area. Partially accessible.

  • Where Strada per il Passo del Tonale, Vermiglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.vermigliovacanze.it

Sources: vermigliovacanze.it · visittrentino.info

Forti Mero e Zaccarana

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Austro-Hungarian fortifications of the Tonale system built until 1914, reachable by a medium difficulty walk from the Tonale Pass; guided tours on request organized by the White War Museum.

  • Where Passo del Tonale, Vermiglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Laghetti di San Leonardo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

A short distance from the village, suitable for picnics and walks; from here you walk to the alpine farmsteads of Stavel.

  • Where Località San Leonardo, Vermiglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry
