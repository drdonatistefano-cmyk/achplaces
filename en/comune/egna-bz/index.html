What to see in Egna

Borgo della Bassa Atesina sulla Strada del Vino, with Roman origins and a portico center of about 500 meters built between the fourteenth and seventeenth century along Via Andreas Hofer; among the main places the Museum of popular culture, the church of San Nicolò and, above the village, the hamlet of Mazzon with the vineyards of Pinot Noir.

Portici e via Andreas Hofer

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Porticos built between the 14th and 17th centuries for about 500 meters, with arches of different shapes, hidden courtyards and shops: many are 'houses in the hall with front porch, courtyard and warehouses on the back, some with erker; along the street ran a drain channel in marble, the Ritsch. Along the way the Ballhaus, today the library, the Hotel Posta and the Baroque Town Hall.

  • Where Via Andreas Hofer, Egna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Museo di cultura popolare

Museums · Themed

In a historic building under the arcades, born from the private collections of Anna Grandi Müller: over 3,000 objects tell the life of a bourgeois family from 1815 to 1950, with rebuilt rooms including playroom, kitchen with pantry, living room and bedroom.

  • Where Via Andreas Hofer 50, Egna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of Romanesque origin dedicated to the patron saint of sailors, near the arcades, with Romanesque bell tower and works of art related to the medieval origins of the village.

  • Where Egna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Mazzon e chiesa di San Michele

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fraction among the first inhabited places of the valley, near the natural park of Monte Corno, known as the Italian grand cru of Pinot Noir: the first vineyards were planted in the nineteenth century by Ludwig von Barth; here passes the Pinot Noir Path. The church of San Michele, mentioned at the end of the fourteenth century, has a bell tower with Romanic double windows.

  • Where Mazzon, Egna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
