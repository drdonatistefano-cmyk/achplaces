What to see in Siddi

Small rural village of Marmilla, mentioned in 1346 as "Silli" at the foot of the Siddi Jar: among the main places the tomb of Giganti Sa Dom'e s'Orku, the park of Sa Fogaia with its nuraghe, the Romanesque church of San Michele, Casa Steri and the Ornithological Museum of Sardinia.

6 places to visit in Siddi, in the province of Sud Sardegna (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Sa Dom'e s'Orku

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Tomb of Giganti of the Middle Bronze (XVI-XIV century B.C.), among the best preserved nuragic monuments: basalt blocks in regular rows, exedra of 18 meters with door surmounted by a large architrave, funeral chamber about 10 meters long and niche for the offerings; the name means the house of the ogre. Reused in neo-opunic and Roman age.

  • Where Versante nord-occidentale della Giara di Siddi, a circa 6 km dal paese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Parco naturalistico archeologico Sa Fogaia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the eastern side of the jar, just over a kilometer from the village: the Sa Fogaia corridor nuraghe, born as protonuraghe with a plant at Y, a prehistoric village with a workshop of the obsidian of Mount Arci, holm oak and spot with over 150 plant species along the path

  • Where Località Sa Fogaia, Giara di Siddi
  • Entry €2, reduced €1; cumulative with the Ornithological Museum €3, reduced €1,50
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.museoparcosiddi.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · idese.cultura.gov.it · museoparcosiddi.it · italia.it

Museo ornitologico della Sardegna

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

The only museum on the island dedicated to birdlife, in the 19th century former Managu hospital: about three hundred sedentary and migratory birds, from raptors to sultan chicken and coral grecko, a small section on Sardinian mammals, a tactile path on the evolution of birds and on the first floor the island's natural environments, from lagoons to mountains.

  • Where Piazza Leonardo da Vinci 7, Siddi
  • Entry €2, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museoparcosiddi.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · museoparcosiddi.it · italia.it

Casa Steri - Museo delle tradizioni agroalimentari della Sardegna

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In a farmhouse in the courtyard of the seventeenth century in the historic center, it tells the food history of the island from the Nuragic age: it rises on milk and oil, a mill with a rare eighteenth-century animal traction mill, the kitchen, the stables with the tools for vinification, the barn and a room with murals liberty. Periodic workshops on pasta, bread and cakes.

  • Where Via Roma 2, Siddi
  • Entry €10.50, reduced €5, family €20
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · sardegnacultura.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

One of the smallest Romanesque churches in Sardinia, erected in the middle of the thirteenth century in marl, sandstone and basalt: two naves, one apse, and on the façade an architrave with five anthropomorphic figures, one upside down, unique in the Sardinian medieval sculpture. Inside two niches with wooden statues of St. Michael.

  • Where Località Santu Miali, periferia di Siddi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Giara di Siddi (su Pranu)

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The basalt plateau was born from a lava flow of 2-5 million years ago, in the form of a 'T' inverted, of about 2,5 per 1,5 km and about 360 meters high, covered with stain and cork; it houses about twenty nuraghi and the sacred source Conca 'e sa Cresia, and from above you can see Campidano, Marmilla, Gennargentu and on clear days the sea.

  • Where Siddi (dalla SS 197 via Villamar e Lunamatrona)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
