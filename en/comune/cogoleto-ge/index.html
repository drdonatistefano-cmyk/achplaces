What to see in Cogoleto

Cittadina del Ponente Genovese with the longest seafront of the Riviera di Ponente, about 3 km of beach, a historic center of carruggi and ancient lime kilns and the house that tradition indicates as the birthplace of Christopher Columbus; in the hinterland of Sciarborasca the house peasant museum.

5 places to visit in Cogoleto, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Lungomare Europa e spiaggia

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The longest promenade of the Ponente Ligure, to be walked or biked to Varazze, accompanies about 3 km of equipped beach.

  • Where Lungomare Europa, Cogoleto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Centro storico e antiche fornaci da calce

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Among the carruggi of the historical center remain the ancient furnaces in which for centuries the lime was produced, with the Town Hall, the parish church of Santa Maria Maggiore and the ancient towers of coastal defense.

  • Where Centro storico, Cogoleto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Casa di Cristoforo Colombo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

In Via Rati the house linked to Christopher Columbus, which Cogoleto claims as his son; Italia.it indicates it as a stage to see from the outside.

  • Where Via Rati, Cogoleto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Oratorio di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It preserves the sacred art collected over the centuries by the Confraternita di San Lorenzo: 18th century Ligurian nativity scene with wooden figures in vintage clothes, processional crucifix of the seventeenth century with silver finishes, the "Martyrdom of San Lorenzo" by G.B. Carlone and other Baroque canvases, a processional case of the Brilla, a nineteenth-century canvas by Veronica Murialdo, the large terracotta nativity scene by Luca Damonte and the paintings by Gino Grimaldi from the church of the former psychiatric hospital in Pratozanino.

  • Where Largo della Pace, Cogoleto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Casa contadina ligure di Sciarborasca

Museums · Themed

A rural building entirely renovated and furnished with antique furniture and furnishings, to show how the peasants lived; it also has a library. Contacts: 010 9188142, 338 9030383.

  • Where Via Ciosa, Sciarborasca, Cogoleto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
