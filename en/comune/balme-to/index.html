What to see in Balme

At 1,432 meters, the highest village of the Lanzo Valleys and home to the first Piedmontese mountain guides: in the ancient village the Ruciàss house above the Gorgia waterfall, a small castle of 1591 with frescoes linked to the passage of the Shroud and the Ecomuseum of the Alpine guides; higher up the plateau of Pian della Mussa and the Gastaldi refuge.

5 places to visit in Balme, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ecomuseo delle guide alpine Antonio Castagneri

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the old town hall, it tells the history of the village: the foundation by Savoiardi shepherds, medieval migrations from Bergamasca and Valsesia, smuggling after the decline of the mines and the birth, in the nineteenth century, of the mountain guides protagonists of the mountaineering in Turin. The core is a collection of great vintage photos of mountaineers and expeditions, with equipment, relics, posters and documents; complete the section itinerary on flora, fauna, minerals and Franco-Provençal culture and a historical-naturalistic itinerary. Visits by reservation (tel. 0123 82902); accepts the Museums Subscription.

  • Where Via Capoluogo 139, Balme
  • Entry €2, reduced €1, free groups and schools
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Borgo antico, casaforte Ruciàss e castello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old nucleus preserves the Ruciass House, a rustic 16th century mansion built on a rock above the Stura, and a small castle dated 1591 with frescoes linked to the route of the Shroud from Chambéry to Turin through the valley; in the village also a chapel of the Frescoed Shroud.

  • Where Balme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Cascata La Gorgia

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the centre of the village, Stura falls with a jump of some tens of meters, overwhelmed by a bridge inspired by the one built at the end of the nineteenth century by the guide Antonio Castagneri at the initiative of the CAI. Around an ancient mill, the buildings to cool the milk, an ice-box dug into the rock of the gorge and, at the base of the jump, a small power plant inaugurated in 1909 and still in operation.

  • Where Centro del paese, Balme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.balme.to.it · italia.it

Pian della Mussa

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

A few kilometres long grassy plain between 1,700 and 1,850 meters, born from the filling of an ancient glacial lake and crossed by the Stura, under the Bessainian (3,604 m) and the Ciamarella (3,637 m). The springs at the end of the plateau fed the aqueduct of Turin in the early twentieth century; in spring you can see ibex. From here start numerous itineraries, the most famous towards the refuge Gastaldi; in winter a cross-country trail starts from Balme along the provincial road closed to traffic. The road from Balme is limited traffic in some periods of the year.

  • Where Località Pian della Mussa, SP1, Balme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.balme.to.it · italia.it

Museo etnografico del rifugio Bartolomeo Gastaldi

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Museum at 2,659 meters, in the refuge Gastaldi, dedicated to the activities of the CAI and the mountaineering between the eight and twentieth century; the refuge is the destination of the most famous excursion that starts from Pian della Mussa.

the MiC card reports the museum closed every day; check at tel. 0123 55257

  • Where Località Crot del Ciaussinè, Balme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.balme.to.it · italia.it
