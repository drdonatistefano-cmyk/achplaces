What to see in Stresa

A small town on the Piedmontese shore of Lake Maggiore, mentioned already in 998 and made famous by the Sempione railway: from here you reach the Borromee Islands with the palaces of Isola Bella and Isola Madre, while in the village there are the Villa Ducale di Rosmini, the park of Villa Pallavicino and, behind, the Mottarone.

Isola Bella e Palazzo Borromeo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Until 1632 it was a cliff with a fishing village, about 400 meters from Stresa: Charles III Borromeo built the palace dedicated to his wife Isabella d'Adda, which forms the "praua" of a ship while the ten-terrace Italian garden forms its "poppa." Inside the ballroom with a high dome, the hall of Napoleon (which stayed there in 1797) in empire style, the gallery of the 16th century Flemish tapestries with the unicorn symbol of the Borromeo and the caves covered with pebbles, tufas and shells; in the garden the Atrium of Diana and the Teatro Massimo.

Isola Madre e Palazzo Borromeo

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

The largest and the furthest from the shore of the Borromean Islands (330 for 220 meters), dominated by a large English garden designed by Filippo Cagnola, with magnolias, wisteria, citrus fruits and white peacocks, golden pheasants and parrots in freedom. The sixteenth-century palace, with greenhouses and family chapel, preserves porcelain, period chandeliers, family portraits, canopy beds and a puppet theater used in the mid seventeenth century to entertain guests.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.isoleborromee.it

Sources: isoleborromee.it · italia.it

Isola dei Pescatori (Isola Superiore)

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

In front of Stresa, it is the only one of the Borromean Islands inhabited in a continuous way, with narrow alleys and a market of handicrafts; in Ferragosto a procession of fishing boats carries the statue of the Assumption around the island. The Palazzo Borromeo, already home to Arturo Toscanini, is private and not visitable.

Villa Ducale e Museo storico di Antonio Rosmini

Museums · House museums

The oldest villa in Stresa, built by the Bolongaro family around 1770, was the residence of the philosopher Antonio Rosmini from 1850 to 1855; the Study Centre can be visited includes the Rosminian Museum, Rosmini's room and the park. In via Manzoni there is the monument to friendship between Manzoni and Rosmini (1932), with crucifix and bas-relief by Pietro Canonica.

  • Where Corso Umberto I 15, Stresa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.stresa.vb.it · italia.it

Villa Pallavicino

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

The estate was founded in 1855 as a private residence and transformed by the Pallavicino di Genova into a neoclassical villa; since 1952 the Marquise Luisa welcomed animals from all over the world. The park of about 20 hectares combines centuries-old trees and botanical garden with over 50 species of mammals and birds (dain, blade, Tibetan goats, zebras, kangaroos) and the Fattoria area; since 2017 it is part of the Borromeo Lands. Open every day from May to September, 9-18.

  • Where Piazza Marconi 4, Stresa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Mottarone

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Mount overlooking Stresa and the lake, reachable on foot: from the top you can see Lake Maggiore and Lake Orta and, on clear days, Lake Varese and other minor lakes, with the Alps in the background.

  • Where Mottarone, Stresa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it · distrettolaghi.it

Centro storico e lungolago

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Historical centre of alleys to explore and lakefront with large hotels, villas and gardens overlooking the three islands; the Palazzo dei Congressi hosts international conferences and summer concerts.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
