What to see in Codroipo

In the heart of the Friulian Risorgive area: Villa Manin, residence of the last doge of Venice where Napoleon signed the Treaty of Campoformido, the Park of the Risorgive and the ancient mills.

5 places to visit in Codroipo, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Codroipo in the guide with map and filters

Villa Manin

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Villa noble of the seventeenth century, residence of the last doge of Venice, with a park of trees from all over the world; there is preserved the original furnishings of the room of Napoleon, which here in 1797 signed the Treaty of Campoformido. It hosts great events and concerts and an Infopoint.

  • Where Passariano, Codroipo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismofvg.it

Parco delle Risorgive

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Walking or cycling: in some stretches you can walk almost on the water that resurfaces after the underground path.

  • Where Codroipo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismofvg.it

Mulini di Bert e di Bosa

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

On the edge of the park, the mill of Bert, of fifteenth-century origin, still in operation and visitable, and the mill of Bosa.

  • Where Codroipo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismofvg.it
