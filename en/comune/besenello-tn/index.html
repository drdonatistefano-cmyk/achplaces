What to see in Besenello

Village of Vallagarina on the left bank of the Adige, in a sunny amphitheatre of vineyards and orchards, dominated by Castel Beseno, the largest fortified complex in Trentino, which since the twelfth century has been guarding the road towards the highlands of Folgaria and Lavarone.

Castel Beseno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The largest fortified complex in Trentino occupies the entire summit of the hill above the Adige, between Trento and Rovereto: more than a castle is a \"Burg" with streets and squares. It dominates the valley since the 12th century; in 1487 it was at the center of the battle of Calliano against the Venetians, to which are dedicated a section and immersive halls, and in 1796 it resisted Napoleon. You walk the walls, you see the bread ovens, the kitchens and the cellars and you can try helmets and armor; it houses exhibitions and re-enactments. Free with Trentino Guest Card and the first Sunday of the month from October to March; closed from 26 January to 20 March and Monday; final access steep and cobbled, without lifts.

  • Where Via al Castello 4, Besenello
  • Entry €7, reduced €4 (15-26 years) or €5 (over 65), family €14
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.buonconsiglio.it

Sources: buonconsiglio.it · visittrentino.info · italia.it

Sentiero dell'Imperatore e chiesetta di Santa Trinità

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Trekking and mountain bike path born for military purposes, starting from the farmhouse Villa Bortolazzi in Acquaviva and arriving at the church of Santa Trinità; looking to the left of the church opens the spectacular chasm of Rio Secco.

  • Where Acquaviva, Besenello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info
