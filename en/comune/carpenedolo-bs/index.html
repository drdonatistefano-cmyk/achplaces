What to see in Carpenedolo

Borgo della piania Brescia with a castle erected around the 1000 against the Hungarians and destroyed several times, under Venice from 1428 to 1797 and crossed by the Fossa Magna Viscontea; to see the Sanctuary of the Madonna del Castello with paintings by Francesco Maffei and Pietro Ricchi, the parish church of San Giovanni Battista and the Romanesque parish of Santa Maria in Carpino.

Santuario della Madonna del Castello

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Built by the community since 1750, in devotion to the Immaculate Church, where the monastery of San Pietro in Monte was built, and consecrated in 1839; the ceiling was decorated in 1928-1929 by the Brescians Vittorio and Giuseppe Trainini. On the high altar the Blessed Virgin with Child by Pietro Ricchi, known as the Lucchese, on the Nativity and Visitation sides of Francesco Maffei (1647), behind the altar the ciborium of Andrea Solarius; in the room next to a museum of parish works and sacred objects, with a canvas signed "Camillus" perhaps by Camillo Procaccini.

  • Where Via Monterocchetta 2, Carpenedolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.carpenedolo.bs.it

Sources: comune.carpenedolo.bs.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in the 17th century instead of a small chapel insufficient for the population, it has a facade dated 1901 rich in adjecting elements: at the bottom four semi-colons with composite capitals holding two tympanums, at the top elaborate pilasters and a pediment with shelves.

  • Where Via San Francesco 6, Carpenedolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.carpenedolo.bs.it

Sources: comune.carpenedolo.bs.it

Pieve di Santa Maria in Carpino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church with semicircular apse in Botticino stone, with high medieval and Roman decorative elements re-used in the walls; in the apse a fresco of the fifteenth century attributed to Bonifacio Bembo.

  • Where Carpenedolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.carpenedolo.bs.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · comune.carpenedolo.bs.it

Fossa Magna

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Canal dug by the Visconti (Bernabò Visconti second in-Lombardy) during their domination, after 1348: it left from the Churches, passed through Lonato and returned after about 15 km; in the nineteenth century it was partly covered, especially in the center of Carpenedolo. It follows a cycle route between Montichiari and Carpenedolo.

  • Where Via Fusetti, Carpenedolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.carpenedolo.bs.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · comune.carpenedolo.bs.it
