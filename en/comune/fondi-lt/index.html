What to see in Fondi

Città dei Caetani sull'Appia, with the castle of the fourteenth century that houses the Civic Archaeological Museum, the Palazzo del Principe, where in 1378 the Antipope Clement VII was elected, the Jewish district of Judea and the stretch of Via Appia Antica towards Itri, UNESCO World Heritage since 2024; between Fondi Lake and Blue Flag beaches.

Castello Caetani e Museo civico archeologico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built since 1319 by the will of Roffredo III Caetani, a rare medieval castle of plain: 33 metres high square mast, cylindrical tower and crenellated belt with three angular bastions; from the terrace you can see the Aurunci and Ausoni mountains and the plain to the sea. Here lived Giulia Gonzaga, who in the night between 8 and 9 August 1534 fled towards Itri to the attack of the corsair Barbarossa. The Archaeological Civic Museum is housed in October 8th.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica 13, Fondi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Palazzo Caetani (Palazzo del Principe)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Next to the castle, along the ancient Appia: here on September 20, 1378 Count Onorato I Caetani hosted the French cardinals who elected the Antipope Clement VII, starting the Western Schism. In the fifteenth century, Honorato II made it the "magnum palatium" with Catalan late dogotic windows, Renaissance portals and fireplaces with the coat of arms Gaetani d'Aragona (1466); Giulia Gonzaga held court there and was born there in 1531 Vespasiano Gonzaga Colonna. Restored by the Lazio Region, it preserves fragments of frescoes in the picta room, a rare profane pictorial cycle, and is the center of the Ausoni Mountains Park.

  • Where Corso Appio Claudio 3, Fondi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parchilazio.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Ancient cathedral built in the 12th century on pre-Roman structures, next to Palazzo Caetani, with façade "a vela" (sailing), in 1378 the antipope Clement VII was elected. Inside a Cosmatesque episcopal throne and, in the chapel of the Count, the Mausoleum Caetani supported by three female figures, a Majesty of Antoniazzo Romano and an Annunciation of Christopher Scacco with Saint Honored who holds the model of the castle of the late fifteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Duomo 22, Fondi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Giudea e Museo ebraico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

One of the oldest Jewish settlements in Italy, never closed in ghetto: steep stairs descend from the houses to a central court and narrow passages connect the houses. The ancient synagogue houses the Jewish Museum, called the House of Spirits, for the legend of sudden noises that caused the inhabitants to flee.

  • Where Largo Elio Toaff, Fondi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parchilazio.it

Via Appia Antica (Fondi-Itri)

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Tratto di circa 2.6 km tra Fondi e Itri in the limestone gorge of the Aurunci, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2024: without asphalt, it shows in sequence the paved road of the Caracalla age (216 A.D.), the Renaissance road of Philip II of Spain (1568) with epigraphs and bridge rebuilt in 2004, and the Bourbon road of Ferdinand IV (1768). Above the gorge, the Fort of Sant'Andrea, built in 1806, rises on the remains of a late-public sanctuary of Apollo.

  • Where Via Appia km 125,8 (lato Fondi, parcheggio e area picnic), Fondi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
