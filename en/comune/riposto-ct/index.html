What to see in Riposto

A marina town at the foot of Mount Etna with the port asked to the State in 1836 and started in 1906: in St. Peter's Square there are the City Hall and the neoclassical Cathedral, Minor Basilica since 1967; the oldest church is the Madonna of the Letter with its crypt, and the promenade leads to the fishing village of Torre Archirafi.

5 places to visit in Riposto, in the province of Catania (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di San Pietro

Places of worship · Basilicas

Neoclassical church dedicated to the patron saint, inspired by the Roman basilica of St. John Lateran: started in 1808, open to worship in 1818, completed in 1865 and parish since 1869, in 1967 received the title of Minor Papal Basilica.

  • Where Piazza San Pietro, Riposto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.riposto.ct.it

Piazza San Pietro e Palazzo Municipale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

It was built in 1874 as the main square of the city and enlarged during the police station of Giuseppe Grimaldi (1919-1920), when the Municipal Palace was built in Art Nouveau style that overlooks it together with the Duomo.

  • Where Piazza San Pietro, Riposto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.riposto.ct.it

Chiesa della Madonna della Lettera

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest church of Riposto, built on the remains of the so-called "Anticaglia di S. John little, perhaps of proto-Christian origin; the present aspect dates back to 1868 and preserves numerous works of art. The strong piece is the crypt, with artifacts of the Arab-Norman, Byzantine and Roman ages and foundations of 1100.

  • Where Presso il molo pennello, Riposto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.riposto.ct.it

Museo Parco delle Kentie

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

Botanical garden of 9,000 m2 with more than 100 specimens of plants from the early twentieth century, a greenhouse for the thermosensitable species and a museum with fossils and plants; the laboratory preserves a herbarium of about 1,800 specimens of Etna and Sicilian species. Guided tours; times to request (tel. 095 97/02092).

  • Where Via M. Carbonaro snc, Riposto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: isprambiente.gov.it · italia.it

Lungomare e Torre Archirafi

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Riposto-Torre Archirafi promenade, built under Commissioner Grimaldi and extended in the sixties to the pier foraneo, leads to the quiet fishing village of Torre Archirafi, where was built the "Scalo di Alaggio"; from the walk you can watch the waves break on the lava cliffs, especially at sunset.

  • Where Lungomare, Riposto - Torre Archirafi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.riposto.ct.it
