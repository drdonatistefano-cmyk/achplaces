What to see in Falconara Marittima

A coastal town at the gates of Ancona, with a long sandy beach and four castles: Falconara Alta, seat of the town hall with views from the Apennines to the Adriatic, the Rocca Priora, Castelferretti and Barcaglione; in the monastery of Sant'Antonio the contemporary Franciscan art gallery "In the name of Francesco" .

Castello di Falconara Alta

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the four castles of the municipality, built by the Cortesi Counts; today it houses the Town Hall and the Museum of Resistance. From the castle the view widens from the Apennines to the Adriatic sea.

  • Where Falconara Alta, Falconara Marittima
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Pinacoteca internazionale d'arte francescana contemporanea «In nome di Francesco»

Museums · Modern art

On the ground floor of the convent complex of Sant'Antonio dei Frati Minori, about 800 m2 of works by well-known Italian, European and African artists; next to it are a library of Franciscan and Picena history, the provincial historical archive of the Friars Minor of the Marche and the conference room. Guided tours by reservation.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · letsmarche.it

Fortezza di Rocca Priora

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

One of the four castles of the municipality, fortress dating back to the time of the birth of Frederick II of Swabia. In front of Rocca Priora a Mare, there is a free beach equipped for dogs.

  • Where Rocca Priora, Falconara Marittima
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Castelferretti e chiesa di Santa Maria della Misericordia

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Borgo grew up around the castle that the Ferretti family built from the fourteenth century; near the manor, in the area of the cemetery, the church of Santa Maria della Misericordia preserves frescoes from the fifteenth century.

  • Where Castelferretti, Falconara Marittima
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Spiaggia e lungomare di Falconara

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Along the sandy coast with numerous bathing establishments equipped with catering and entertainment for children; there are two areas reserved for dogs, Rocca Priora a Mare and near the station, with bathing area signposted.

  • Where Lungomare, Falconara Marittima
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it
