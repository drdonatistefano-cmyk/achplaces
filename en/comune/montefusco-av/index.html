What to see in Montefusco

Ancient capital of the Principality Ultra in the land of the Greek of Tufo: to see the castle with the Bourbon prison called the "Spielberg" of Irpinia, today museum, the Oratory of St. James frescoed, Palazzo Giordano, the churches of St. John of Vaglio, St. Francis and St. Bartholomew and the Woods of Montefusco.

Castello e carcere borbonico, Museo «Lo Spielberg del Risorgimento meridionale»

Museums · City history

The castle, founded by the Longobards to guard the streets for Benevento, hosted Norman, Swabian, Angevin and Aragonese rulers and until the nineteenth century the Head of the Principality Ultra with court and offices; today it is the seat of the Municipality. In the basement, on two superimposed lanes excavated in part in the rock, there is the prison: after the motions of 1848 the Bourbons locked you patriots like Carlo Poerio and Sigismondo Castromediano, from which the nickname "Spielberg of Irpinia" is. Closed in 1923, declared National Monument in 1928, it has been a civic museum since 1997; open Monday-Saturday 9-13, afternoons and Sundays by telephone reservation (0825 964003).

Oratorio di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient crypt of the church of Santa Maria della Piazza, one of the oldest religious buildings in the country; in 1652 it was donated to the Confraternity of San Giacomo, which had it restored. The 17th-century frescoes depict scenes from the Old and New Testaments, the glory of Mary on the vault and Saint Catherine of Alexandria on the bottom; the trapdoor 'pro confratibus' towards the ancient sepulchre is closed after the 1980 earthquake. Visit by reservation.

Palazzo Giordano

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries preserved in the original form: two floors on an entire block, portal in decentralized stone, double staircase from the garden and on the gate the coat of arms of the Jordan with two rampant lions, a palm tree and the inscription "Onofrius Giordanus 1613" . The rooms with frescoed ceilings and neoclassical motifs were the scene of historical events when Montefusco was the capital of the Principality Ultra; today it hosts events and exhibitions (and in part the barracks of the Carabinieri). Visit by reservation.

Chiesa di San Giovanni del Vaglio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Large and harmonious three-nave church divided by a double row of seven travertine columns, with an underground access to a stucco-decorated chapel and frescoed in the eighteenth century. Originally a palatina church, it was part of the area of the Lombard-Norman fortress on the current Piazza Castello.
