What to see in Erice

Medieval village at 751 meters above the summit of the mountain, above Trapani: the Castle of Venus on the sanctuary of Venus Ericina, the Garden of Balio, the Mother church of 1314 and the Cyclopean walls, with a look up to the Egadi.

10 places to visit in Erice, in the province of Trapani (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Erice in the guide with map and filters

Castello di Venere

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Norman fortress of the XII-XIII centuries on the summit, on the remains of the temple of Venus Ericina of which the materials were re-used: mighty walls joined by a drawbridge to the Towers of the Balio and, above the door, a plumbing place with the coat of arms of Charles V and a bifora.

  • Where Largo Castello, Erice
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Giardino del Balio e Torri del Balio

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

English garden built in the nineteenth century by Count Agostino Pepoli next to the towers of the defensive system, with views of Trapani, the salt pans, the Egadi, the Stagnone, Mount Cofano and the Zingaro Reserve.

  • Where Via San Francesco, Erice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa Madre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Built in 1314 by Federico d'Aragona, with quadrangular bell tower and, on the right wall, nine crosses believed to have come from the temple of Venus.
