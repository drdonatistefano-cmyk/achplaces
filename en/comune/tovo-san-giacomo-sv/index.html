What to see in Tovo San Giacomo

The municipality of Val Maremola is about three kilometers from the sea of Pietra Ligure, with the hamlets of Bardino Vecchio and Bardino Nuovo, aggregate in 1928; in Bardino Nuovo the Museum of Tower Clock G.B. Bergallo, the first museum of the kind in Italy, and around six-seventeenth-century churches, the ruins of the castle of the Folchi and a network of paths between olive groves and woods.

Museo dell'orologio da torre G.B. Bergallo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Opened in 1997, the first museum of its kind in Italy, was born from the collection donated to the village by Giovanni Bergallo, the last watchmaker of the family who built tower clocks in the workshop house of Val Maremola between 1861 and 1980. In 260 square meters of an old town hall exhibits about thirty authentic tower clocks (the oldest of the 16th century), machinery and tools, dials, hands, pulleys and objects from bell towers, documents and a video; the visit may include the Bergallo workshop house. For the twenty-year anniversary, the documentary "Time Beyond the Ocean" was shot on the clock sold in 1934 to the Salesian mission of Puerto Santa Cruz in Patagonia. Open Friday, Saturday and Sunday: October-May 15-18, June-August 17-21, September 16-20; schools €3, free for residents; guided tour of about 45 minutes.

  • Where Piazza Can. Giuseppe Folco 1, Bardino Nuovo, Tovo San Giacomo
  • Entry €5, reduced €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodellorologio.it

Chiesa di San Giacomo Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish of the early sixteenth century, in the present eighteenth-century structure then restored and enriched. In the central aisle two frescoes by the Genoese Gerolamo Graffigna (late nineteenth century) with the Madonna delle Grazie and St. James Major, patron saint of the village; the bell tower was rebuilt after the earthquake of 23 February 1887. Consolidated and restored between 1984 and 1987, with the interior frescoes restored by Giovanni Muratorio.

  • Where Piazza Don Zunino, Tovo San Giacomo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.tovo-san-giacomo.sv.it

Sources: comune.tovo-san-giacomo.sv.it

Chiesa di San Sebastiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

In Bardino Nuovo, probably the most recent of the parishes of the territory: erected between 1681 and 1708, parish since 19 November 1797 and completely renovated in 1924, has a richly frescoed interior and the highest bell tower of the valley, over 30 meters. From the front square opens a wide view of the valley and the Ligurian sea.

  • Where Bardino Nuovo, Tovo San Giacomo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.tovo-san-giacomo.sv.it

Sources: comune.tovo-san-giacomo.sv.it

Santuario della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

In Bardino Vecchio along the road to the Colle del Melogno, it was born according to tradition from an apparition of 1722, when the Virgin appeared to a mute girl who regained the word. The eighteenth-century building has three aisles; at the bottom of the right aisle the altar with the venerated wooden group of the school of Antonio Brilla, located in 1822 for the centenary of the apparition.

open only on the occasion of religious holidays

  • Where Via Madonna delle Grazie, Bardino Vecchio, Tovo San Giacomo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.tovo-san-giacomo.sv.it

Sources: comune.tovo-san-giacomo.sv.it

Castello dei Folchi e anello da Bardino Vecchio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill of the same name near the cemetery of Bardino Vecchio there are the ruins of a 'bastita' built during the War of the Final in the middle of the XV century: a fortification of 500 square meters, 80 m of perimeter and at least 6 m of height, depicted in the municipal coat of arms. A short ring starts from the square of Bardino Vecchio (oratory of San Carlo Borromeo and parish church of San Giovanni Battista), climbs steep among the pines up to the ruins and descends next to a cross on the precipice dated 1933-1934, returning between olive groves and woods along the Liguria Path. Free access on path in the woods, not accessible by wheelchair.

  • Where Bardino Vecchio, Tovo San Giacomo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.tovo-san-giacomo.sv.it

Sources: comune.tovo-san-giacomo.sv.it

Via del Ferro lungo il Maremola

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

An easy walk from Piazza Don Zunino down via Roma to the chapel of San Martino and to the Maremola stream, with the old mill of the Bringhiera and the remains of the medieval bridge destroyed by the flood of 1933; continues under a canal watering hanging up to Terra Molino, from where you go up to the museum of Bardino Nuovo or continue towards Isallo (Magliolo) and the ruins of an active ironworks from 1730 to 1860, where the mule trackers brought iron ore of Elba.

  • Where Piazza Don Zunino, Tovo San Giacomo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.tovo-san-giacomo.sv.it

Sources: comune.tovo-san-giacomo.sv.it

Sentiero dei Promontori Tovesi

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of ups and downs suitable for running and walking: from Piazza Don Zunino to Via Persego (information totem) to Casa Boragni, on mule track between the olive trees around the Cima della Brona to the balcony of Monte Grosso with a small wooden cross and picnic area, then down in the valley Bottassano, up to Bardino Vecchio to the ruins of the castle of Folchi and back to the village of Poggio. PDF map on the site of the Municipality.

  • Where Piazza Don Zunino, Tovo San Giacomo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page comune.tovo-san-giacomo.sv.it

Sources: comune.tovo-san-giacomo.sv.it
