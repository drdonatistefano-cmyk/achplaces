What to see in Vipiteno

Market town at the crossroads of the Isarco, Ridanna and Vizze valleys, on the Brenner road, at its peak in the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries: the Tower of the Twelve divides the Old City from the New Portico City, with the Gothic Town Hall, the parish church of Our Lady of the Swamp and the Multscher Museum in the Command of the Teutonic Order.

Torre delle Dodici e centro storico

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Symbol of the city, 46 meters high, built in 1469-1473 and rebuilt in the present form after the fire of 1868, with sundial; it rises on the site of the ancient gate and separates the Old City from the New City, rebuilt more sumptuous after the fires of the mid fifteenth century. Along the main street houses with wrought iron signs painted and gold, shops and hotels; walls remain short stretches.

  • Where Piazza Città, Vipiteno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · vipiteno.eu

Municipio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between 1468 and 1473 in the center of the New City and completed in 1524, with Jörg Kölderer's multi-gonal crenellated erker; the Gothic council hall has wooden walls and ceiling beams, the most beautiful of its kind in all Tyrol, and the palace houses the Sala Vigil Raber, one of the oldest theatres in Tyrol, and an art collection. In the courtyard a marble copy of a Roman relief of Mithras; the Tyrolean Diet met there for the first time in 1502 with Massimiliano.

  • Where Città Nuova, Vipiteno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: vipiteno.eu · italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Museo Civico e Museo Multscher

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the Command of the Teutonic Order, with roots in 1241: the Multscher Museum exhibits the surviving parts of the late Dogotic altar by Hans Multscher of Ulm, completed in 1459 for the parish and once among the largest and most expensive of the Alpine arch; the Civic Museum collects maps and views of the city, documents of local history and art and crafts of the place. Open from the beginning of May to the end of October.

  • Where Via Città Nuova 21, Vipiteno
  • Entry €5, reduced €2,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: cultura.gov.it · vipiteno.eu · italia.it

Parrocchiale di Nostra Signora della Palude

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Late-Gothic, one of the largest in the Alpine arch: choir of 1417-1451 and nave of 1497-1524, whose first stone was laid by Massimiliano I in 1497; mighty pillars in white marble, southern portal of Mattheis Stöberl and Baroque frescoes by Adam Mölk (1753). On the north side the Roman plaque of Postojna Victorina, found in 1497 digging the foundations.

  • Where Via della Commenda 13, Vipiteno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · vipiteno.eu · italia.it

Chiesa di Santo Spirito

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Gothic church of 1399 in Piazza Città, born with the hospital: the oldest preserved church of Vipiteno, sober outside and frescoed inside by Giovanni da Brunico.

Jöchlsthurn

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Late-Gothic building near the tower, residence with a richly carved wooden ceiling, masterpiece of the city's golden age; today it houses the Museum of Mines.

Monte Cavallo

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Reached by cable car from the valley station to the north of the city: sunny terrace with panoramic views to the Dolomites, refuges and huts. In winter about 20 km of slopes and the toboggan run illuminated and snow-covered artificially longest in Italy; in summer pastures, trails and the Rosskopf Panorama Mountain Coaster.

  • Where Via Brennero 41, Vipiteno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it
