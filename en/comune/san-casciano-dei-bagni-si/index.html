What to see in San Casciano dei Bagni

The thermal village of the Sienese Valdichiana, where since 2019 the excavations of the Sanctuary have returned dozens of votive statues in Etruscan-Roman bronze.

5 places to visit in San Casciano dei Bagni, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario Ritrovato

History and architecture · Archaeology

Great Etruscan and Roman shrine with travertine pool emerged from the thermal waters of the Bagno Grande: since 2019 more than 24 bronze statues in perfect state, the discovery considered the most important in Italy after the Bronzes of Riace.

  • Where Via della Fontaccia, San Casciano dei Bagni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Local life and traditions · Spas · Free entry · Viewpoint

Collegiata di San Leonardo e chiese del borgo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The Insigne Collegiata di San Leonardo, several times renovated, with the churches of Sant'Antonio, Santa Maria della Colonna, early Christian on a pagan temple, and San Michele Arcangelo.

Fonte della Ficoncella

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The source on which in 1607 Grand Duke Ferdinand I built a portico, a sign of the European fame of the waters of San Casciano.
