What to see in Rotella

Borgo dell'interrartra piceno at the foot of Monte Ascensione, at the confluence of the Oste stream in Tesino: the Baroque collegiate of Saints Mary and Lorenzo, the civic tower of the fifteenth century, the church of Santa Viviana with the sacred art museum and its reliquaries, the Farfense oratory of Verdiente and, in the hamlets, Poggio Canoso, Capradosso and Castel di Croce.

10 places to visit in Rotella, in the province of Ascoli Piceno (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Collegiata dei Santi Maria e Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Baroque church with a nave built in 1767 on a project by Lazaro Giosafatti, with paintings by Filippo Ricci. It preserves a Pietà in polychrome terracotta and an Eternal Father in marble of the fifteenth century, the reliquary of San Fortunato, an organ of the seventeenth century and wooden busts; the sacristy has marble furniture and a frescoed ceiling with stories of Saint Benedict.

  • Where Via Giacomo Leopardi, Rotella
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.rotella.ap.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Torre civica dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

A square-shaped tower dating back to the 15th century, which until the 18th century was the bell tower of the Farfense church built by the monks in 1430; it collapsed partly in 1755 for the erosion of the Oste stream.

  • Where Via Umberto I, Rotella
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.rotella.ap.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Viviana (delle Icone)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Rebuilt at the end of the eighteenth century at the behest of Monsignor Ciccolini from the rural church of Santa Maria a Pie' di Monte, it has the facade with two twin towers. It preserves the body of Saint Viviana, translated by permission of Pope Leo XIII, a polychrome stucco, a Virgin with Child, the crypt of St. Pontico and the reliquaries collected by Monsignor Ariodante Ciccolini, who gathered more than 400 of them.

  • Where Viale Ciccolini 5, Rotella
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.rotella.ap.it · italia.it

Museo d'arte sacra Ciccolini (Musei Sistini del Piceno)

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Museum of sacred art next to Santa Viviana, with the reliquary of San Fortunato attributed to Pietro Paolo de Aurificis (1509) and a 17th-century statue of St. Peter the Apostle; the contacts are those of the Sistine Museums network of Piceno. In summer 2025 was open from 24 June to 17 September, every day from 17:30 to 19:30; access only with stairs.

  • Where Viale Ciccolini 5, Rotella
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website museisistini.it

Sources: museisistini.it · comune.rotella.ap.it · italia.it

Oratorio del Verdiente (Santa Maria di Verdente)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Oratory of the 11th century used by the Farfensian monks, alternating prayer and work next to their agricultural courtyards: rectangular plan with round apse facing east, frescoes of the 15th century and an Annunciation on the apse.

  • Where Contrada Icone, Rotella
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.rotella.ap.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Santuario della Madonna della Consolazione (di Montemisio)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

In a clearing at the foot of Mount Ascensione, built by the monks of Farfa on the ruins of a pagan temple dedicated to Artemis; restored in the eighteenth century, it is dedicated to the Madonna of the Consolation, celebrated on 15 August. It has a nave, terracotta floor and travertine altar; in the garden an oak tree over 23 meters high.

  • Where Contrada Torbidello, Capradosso, Rotella
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.rotella.ap.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Poggio Canoso: rocca e chiesa di Santa Lucia

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

In the hamlet remains the ancient fortified fortress; the church of Saint Lucia, documented since 1381 and with Romanesque portal, was damaged by the earthquake of 1943 and reduced in the sixties, and preserves copies of four tables by Pietro Alemanno (the originals are at the Diocesan Museum of Ascoli Piceno).

  • Where Via San Fortunato, Poggio Canoso, Rotella
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.rotella.ap.it · letsmarche.it

Museo naturalistico dell'Ambiente fluviale e agrario

Museums · Science and university

In the former Baronial Palace Cornacchia di Poggio Canoso, with rooms dedicated to woods, river environment, agricultural landscape and peasant civilization, dioramas, panels and naturalistic collections, plus a didactic workshop with projection room.

  • Where Poggio Canoso, Rotella
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.rotella.ap.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Lucia di Capradosso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It preserves a gothic reliquary made of 15th-century embossed copper and a canvas attributed to the De Magistris school with the Madonna del Rosario and costume characters of the Gabrielli family.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Capradosso, Rotella
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.rotella.ap.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Severino a Castel di Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church designed by the architect Pilotti of Ascoli Piceno, with in the apse a mosaic of Gaudenzi, of the Vatican school, which depicts the Paschal Christ between Saints and Blesseds of Piceno.

  • Where Castel di Croce, Rotella
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
