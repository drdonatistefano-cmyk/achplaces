What to see in Marsciano

A small town in the middle Tiber Valley, a fief of the Bulgarelli Counts from the 12th century, then of Perugia and from 1540 of the Papal State; it is the largest Umbrian center of the production of bricks, told by the dynamic Museum of the Laterizio, and has in the territory the abbey of San Sigismondo, the castle of Papiano and a San Sebastiano del Perugino in Cerqueto.

Museo dinamico del laterizio e delle terrecotte

Museums · Themed

Museum on the territory dedicated to rural civilization, crafts and the brick industry, of which Marsciano is the largest Umbrian center. The main pole is Palazzo Pietromarchi, 14th century noble residence of the Counts Bulgarelli, with the section on the brick and a fresco of the first sixteenth century school of Pinturicchio (Madonna on throne with Child and Angels, San Silvestro and San Rocco).

  • Where Palazzo Pietromarchi, Marsciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the patron, attested in 1136 and rebuilt in 1896 with facade in Romanesque style and Gothic interior with three naves. The nineteenth-century bell tower has a statue of the saint of Teodoro Coletti and Mariano Laura and a rare mechanical clock by Salvatore Salvatorelli that also marks days, months and phases of the moon. Within four terracotta altars (three designed by Nazareno Biscarini), a wooden crucifix, a seventeenth-century tabernacle, a Madonna enthroned and saints of Perugia school of the sixteenth century and the Incredulity of St. Thomas of Vincenzo Chialli (1831).

  • Where Piazza San Giovanni Battista, Marsciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta di Cerqueto

Papiano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Small medieval village a few kilometers away, with a castle of 1277 with five watchtowers (three well preserved); the main tower became bell tower in 1823 and has a large clock and two bells. Alleys, underground tunnels, walls, arches and medieval frescoes.
