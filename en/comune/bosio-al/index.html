What to see in Bosio

Municipality of Apennines Alessandrino a few kilometers from the sea, seat of the Natural Park of Capanne di Marcarolo: in the village Capanne the Ecomuseum of Cascina Moglioni and the ruins of Benedicta, shrine of the partisan massacre of April 1944; above, Mount Tobbio with the small church of Madonna di Caravaggio.

4 places to visit in Bosio, in the province of Alessandria (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ecomuseo di Cascina Moglioni

Museums · Themed

Traditional farmhouse of the Parco delle Capanne di Marcarolo with exhibitions and multimedia facilities on the local life and economy; the roof is made of chestnut wood, around centuries-old chestnut trees, fruit trees and a new orchard of ancient local varieties of apples, pears and plums. It has a theater in nature that in summer hosts shows and meetings.

Sacrario dei Martiri della Benedicta

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

At about 750 m, the ruins of a Benedictine crab of the 11th century, then center of the possessions of Spinola, were in the spring 1944 place of command partisan; between 6 April and the following days German troops and fascist departments surrounded the area, blew up the monastery and shot 75 partisans, with 147 victims in all and almost 400 deportees. Next to the ruins stands the shrine; an open-air place, always accessible, commemorated every year.

  • Where Capanne di Marcarolo, Bosio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.areeprotetteappenninopiemontese.it

Parco naturale delle Capanne di Marcarolo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 8,200 hectares to the extreme south of the province, a few kilometers from the sea, among the green rocks of the Voltri Group where the Alps and the Apennines meet; oak woods, chestnuts, beech and black pines, artificial lakes of the Gorzente and rare flora such as the violet of Bertoloni. They live roe deer, wild boars and sometimes the wolf; symbol of the park is the biancon. The administrative seat is in Bosio.

  • Where Frazione Capanne di Marcarolo, Bosio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.areeprotetteappenninopiemontese.it

Sources: areeprotetteappenninopiemontese.it

Monte Tobbio e chiesa della Madonna di Caravaggio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Top of 1,092 m in the middle of the park, destination of an excursion of about two hours from Hermits; at the top the small church of the Madonna di Caravaggio, with nineteenth-century frescoes of more painters including the comasco Gambini.

  • Where Monte Tobbio, Bosio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.areeprotetteappenninopiemontese.it

Sources: comune.bosio.al.it · areeprotetteappenninopiemontese.it
