What to see in Laveno-Mombello

Holiday resort on the Lombard shore of Lake Maggiore, born in 1927 from the union of Laveno, Mombello and Cerro and connected by ferry to the Piedmontese bank; historic center of ceramics, with the International Museum of Ceramic Design in Cerro, and dominated by the Sasso del Ferro, reachable by cable car.

Museo Internazionale del Design Ceramico (MIDeC)

Museums · Themed

In the sixteenth century Palazzo Perabò, built at the end of the sixteenth century for the Counts Guilizzoni on the lakefront of Cerro and purchased by the Municipality in 1968; the museum opened on May 9, 1971 as Civic Collection of Terraglie. It tells the Lombard ceramic production from the mid-nineteenth century (Laveno hosts ceramists from 1856) to today, with pieces from the Richard Ginori collection, Art Nouveau objects, rare Italian and foreign sanitary items, sculptures and panels by Biancini, Campi, Andlovitz, Melandri and Gariboldi and works by contemporary artists.

Sasso del Ferro e funivia

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Mount of about 1,062 meters above Laveno, reachable in about 15 minutes by cable car with two cabins from the center to Poggio Sant'Elsa, or by car to Casere (via Cittiglio) and then walk in the woods. From the top you can see the lake, the Campo dei Fiori, the lakes of the province, the Val Grande and the Alps up to Monte Rosa; it is also a takeoff point for hang gliding and paragliding. On arrival bar, restaurant, hotel and shop of local products.

  • Where Stazione di partenza della funivia, Via Tinelli, Laveno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Centro storico, Piazza Fontana e Villa De Angeli Frua

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The heart is Piazza Fontana, among railing houses, Liberty buildings and gardens. Villa Frua, built in the mid-eighteenth century by the Tinelli family and then by the De Angeli Frua family, hosted Garibaldi in 1862 and is now home to the Municipality and the library; its park, known as a botanical park, is open to the public with a view of the lake and the islands. In front of the seventeenth-century Villa Tinelli with Italian garden and medieval towers; in the streets near Casa Terruggia and Casa Milanesi (XVIII century), the eighteenth-century church of the Immaculate and Sant'Ambrogio; in the Park of the Remembrances the monument to the fallen of Piero Portaluppi (1923).

  • Where Piazza Fontana, Laveno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria in Ca' Deserta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest church in Laveno, dating back to the 9th century although with numerous subsequent interventions. In the next cemetery stands the futurist artist Luigi Russolo.

Torbiera di Mombello

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Wet zone in a circle of morainic hills, born in the Quaternary from an ice lake then become swamp and filled by vegetation transformed into peat; at the edges typhus, reeds and horseback rides. On its banks stood a Neolithic village of stilts, whose traces were discovered in the nineteenth century. Managed with the Montana Community of Valcuvia, it has protected observation points and educational panels.

  • Where Mombello, Laveno Mombello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.laveno.va.it · in-lombardia.it

Forte Castello e fortini austriaci

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Between 1850 and 1859 Austria made Laveno a "Imperial Regia Piazzaforte" (Imperial Direction), with a system of forts designed by the Austrian school. On Monte Castello an ancient fortress was transformed into a fort, reached by a steep road; at the tower an ossuary recalls the battles of 1859 of the Hunters of the Alps of Garibaldi. There are also the barracks of San Michele, made of exposed bricks, and the circular fort of Cerro, today a private residence.

  • Where Monte Castello, Laveno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it
