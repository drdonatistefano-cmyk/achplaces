What to see in Dogliani

The capital of the Langa del Dolcetto, divided between the Borgo on the bottom of the Rea valley and the Castle, a medieval fortified village on the high ground: it is the city of Luigi Einaudi and the architect Giovanni Battista Schellino, author of the great parish church of Saints Quirico and Paul and of the neo-Gothic entrance of the cemetery; in the Town Hall are located the historical archaeological museum "Giuseppe Gabetti" and the Einaudi Museum.

Parrocchiale dei Santi Quirico e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Neoclassical Greek cross church designed by Giovanni Battista Schellino (1859-1886), 46 meters long and 23 meters wide, with an inner peristyle of 24 columns that holds the large dome. It preserves paintings from the XVI-XIX centuries, wooden statues of the Brilla, a Via Crucis of Gaetano Previati and the organ of the brothers Vittino di Centallo, inaugurated in 1883; apse, walls and columns are decorated by the painter Giovanni Albesiano from Dogliano.

Ingresso monumentale del Cimitero civico

Places of worship · Monumental cemeteries · Free entry

Made between 1855 and 1867 on a project by Schellino, considered his most significant work, is a Gothic reinterpreted with pointed spires, handmade bricks and wrought iron gates; restored between 2015 and 2018. Inside the tombs Appiani, Bonari and Cillario of Schellino himself, buried in a modest tomb in the southeast corner; in the right wing of the entrance stands Luigi Einaudi. From the cemetery start the 14 pylons of the Rosary designed by Schellino in 1872, which go up to the sanctuary of Our Lady of Graces.

  • Where SP661, Dogliani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.langheroero.it

Sources: doglianiturismo.com · langheroero.it

Dogliani Castello, il borgo fortificato

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The high core of the village preserves almost intact the features of a medieval fortified village, with narrow streets, alleys and ancient walls. On the north side stands the civic tower, also called the Town Hall or the Clock, already mentioned in the twelfth century, and there is the church of the Immaculate Conception and St. Joseph, called the new church of Castello.

Palazzo municipale (ex convento del Carmine)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

It was the ancient convent of Carmine, whose first stone was laid on 26 August 1480; entrance and portico are what remains of the nave of the Renaissance church of Carmine, of the early sixteenth century, and in the restorations of the façade a large fresco of the Madonna del Carmine has re-emerged. Between 1862 and 1864 Schellino rebuilt it by adding a third floor and the adjacent tower of the Cessi, among the first public toilets. Today it houses the municipality, the Gabetti Museum, the Einaudi Museum and, in the cellar, the Wine shop.

  • Where Piazza San Paolo 10, Dogliani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.langheroero.it

Museo storico archeologico «Giuseppe Gabetti»

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

Born in the seventies after the chance discovery in San Quirico of Roman burials, including a first century A.D. burial tomb with a rich set of glass and fine ceramics, it was inaugurated in 1983 and named after the Germanist Giuseppe Gabetti (1886-1948). Among the findings stands out a sandstone head of the medium Iron age, found in the old walls. Four rooms on the first floor of the Palazzo Comunale tell the territory from prehistory to the Roman age, with panels in Italian and English; Saturday 15-18, Sunday 9-12 and 15-18 (better phone, 0173 70107).

  • Where Palazzo comunale, Piazza San Paolo 10, Dogliani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.langheroero.it

Sources: doglianiturismo.com · langheroero.it

Museo Luigi Einaudi

Museums · City history

Dedicated to the most illustrious citizen of Dogliani, President Luigi Einaudi, was realized by the Municipality with the Luigi Einaudi Foundations of Rome and Turin and inaugurated on June 25, 2011. It is located in the Town Hall.

Museo degli ex voto e della devozione popolare

Museums · Themed · Free entry

It excels restored ex-vote chosen from about 300 recorded, dating from the seventeenth to the mid-20th century; the dozens of oldest wooden tablets, from the San Quirico Sanctuary, are a unique case among the collections of the sanctuaries of the province. It is located in an eighteenth-century church, with a frescoed Carmelite subject once. Visits by reservation (tel. 0173 70990).

  • Where Via Savona, retro dell'abside della parrocchiale, Dogliani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.langheroero.it

Sources: doglianiturismo.com · langheroero.it

Sentieri tra i vigneti del dolcetto

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Dogliani is in the heart of the Langhe, among the hills of sandy yellow soils cultivated with sweet, native vine attested already in the sixteenth century; here in 1897 the twenty-three-year-old Luigi Einaudi bought the farm San Giacomo with a vineyard. Seven routes of different lengths and difficulties cross the territory; the map of the trails costs € 4 at the information office, in the accommodation facilities and in the bars.

  • Where Ufficio turistico, Dogliani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.langheroero.it

Sources: langheroero.it
