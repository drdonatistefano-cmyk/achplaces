What to see in Mira

On the Riviera del Brenta, between Padua and Venice, Mira line up along the Naviglio dozens of Venetian villas: the Palladian Villa Foscari called La Malcontenta, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, the eighteenth-century Villa Widmann Rezzonico Foscari with the park and carriages, Villa Contarini dei Leoni seat of the Town Hall, the boats of Villa Valmarana, the village of Mira Porte and the churches of Oriago and Gambarare.

Villa Foscari detta La Malcontenta

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Built in the middle of the sixteenth century by Andrea Palladio, with the noble floor frescoed by G.B. Zelotti in the second half of the century; in 1966 Queen Elizabeth of England was guest. It is a private residence on the UNESCO site of Palladio villas (Villa Foscari, connue sous le nom de La Malcontenta, Gambara di Mira) In the 2026 season it opened from Tuesday to Sunday (Thursday only morning), 9-12 and 14.30-17.30.

open from 1 April to 1 November, closed in winter; visit to book at least 10 days before; pedestrian area without parking

  • Entry €15, reduced €10 (18-25 years, FAI and ICOM members, resident in Mira), €8 (11-17 years), family €30, free up to 10 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website lamalcontenta.com

Villa Widmann Rezzonico Foscari

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

An eighteenth-century villa in Rococo style in Riscossa di Mira: the first news dates back to 1718, when it was donated to Elisabetta Tornimbeni and to her husband, Count Diodato Seriman; in 1750 it passed to Duke Gabrio Serbelloni and after the Napoleonic era to the Widmann-Rezzonico, who adapted it to the rococo taste. The festival hall has mythological stuccoes and frescoes, including the Gloria of the Widmann family, with works by Giuseppe Angeli and Girolamo Mengozzi Colonna; Goldoni, D'Annunzio and Stravinsky stayed there. The barchessa houses a collection of ancient carriages and the park has statues, avenues of lindens and pine trees and a nineteenth-century pond; since 1984 it is public (today Venice Metropolitan City). Opening on reservation at least 48 hours before.

  • Where Via Nazionale 420, Mira
  • Entry €6, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.mira.ve.it · italia.it

Villa Contarini dei Leoni

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in the middle of the sixteenth century by the Attorney of Venice Federico Contarini, it is the seat of the Town Hall and is visited on the occasion of cultural events; it has an Italian garden park. The frescoes of Tiepolo (1754), sold in 1893, are now at the Jacquemart-André Museum in Paris; the sculptures at the entrance are not original.

  • Where Riviera Silvio Trentin 5, Mira
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.mira.ve.it

Villa Valmarana

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Of the original complex, the Dolfin and purchased in 1734 for Leonardo Valmarana, the two boats and the small church, where the first Valmarana owners are buried: the villa was demolished in 1908 by the owner Antonio Baldan. In 1960 the sculptor Luciano Minguzzi bought the guesthouse and restored the frescoes; today the guest house hosts events and ceremonies. Private property open to the public.

Villa Valier

History and architecture · Palaces and villas
