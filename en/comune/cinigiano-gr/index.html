What to see in Cinigiano

Borgo della Maremma on the slopes of Monte Amiata, between Valle dell'Ombrone and Val d'Orcia, in the Montecucco area; in the center the Clock Tower and the church of San Michele Arcangelo, in the territory the fortified villages of Porrona, Sasso d'Ombrone and Castiglioncello Bandini, the Museum House of Monticello Amiata and the Reserve of Poggio all'Olmo.

8 places to visit in Cinigiano, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Nineteenth-century civic tower, symbol of the village, built where in the Middle Ages was the municipal palace; it bears the coat of arms of the Municipality and the clock facing the square, with crenellated top that encloses the bell cell and details inspired by the Tower of Mangia di Siena. There are few remains of the medieval castle around which Cinigiano was born.

  • Where Piazza Guglielmo Marconi 4, Cinigiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Already a baptismal font in the 15th century, it was rebuilt at the end of the 16th century with brick and rosette façade. He preserves the Crucifixion with Saints Francis, Mark and Sigismondo by Francesco Vanni, a seventeenth-century painting with Our Lady who gives the scapular to Saint Simone Stock and, on the two altars of the nave, the Madonna delle Grazie by Daniele Lonati and a Trinity with angels from the Nasinian school.

  • Where Centro storico, Cinigiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Porrona, borgo fortificato

History and architecture · Villages · Viewpoint

Ancient fortified village on a hill of tuff at 254 m, about 3 km from the capital, belonged to the abbey of Sant'Antimo and then to Siena, in the fourteenth century divided between Ptolemies and Piccolomini and visited by Pope Pius II in 1459. In addition to the castle, the Pieve di San Donato, the church of the Madonna delle Grazie, ancient wash-houses and fountains and the Itinerary of the Panorami between fields, olive groves and cypresses.

The castle today is a hotel/resort and part of the village is privately owned

  • Where Via della Fiera 12, Porrona, Cinigiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.cinigiano.gr.it · visittuscany.com

Sasso d'Ombrone

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient Maremma Stone, on a rock above the river Ombrone: the castle, attested since 988, controlled the Traversa dei Monti, via del sale between Grosseto and Montalcino, and passed to Siena in 1403 and to the Medici in 1559. There are still alleys, access arch and stretches of walls, the remains of the ancient bridge over the Ombrone, the Antiche Fonti del Sasso along the path towards the river and the municipal terraced park of Bellacosta.

  • Where Sasso d'Ombrone, Cinigiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.cinigiano.gr.it · visittuscany.com

Castiglioncello Bandini

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village 606 m on a hill of the Amiata, at the foot of the Riserva di Poggio all'Olmo, born around the Aldobrandesco castle of Castiglion del Torto, with late medieval tower, Neo-Medieval tower and Renaissance courtyard. The church of St. Nicholas, with three naves, preserves the 17th-century main altar and a canvas of the Madonna with the Sleeping Child; outside the village the seventeenth-century church of the Madonnino. On clear days the view arrives in Montecristo, the Formiche di Grosseto and Corsica.

The castle is privately owned and is not open to visitors

  • Where Castiglioncello Bandini, Cinigiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.cinigiano.gr.it · visittuscany.com

Monticello Amiata e Casa museo

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

The highest center of the municipality, with a historical center to walk, marked paths between ancient chestnut groves (the Via della Castagna) and a panorama from Monte Amiata to the coast. In the old town hall the House museum dedicated to folk traditions, daily life and ancient crafts.

  • Where Monticello Amiata, Cinigiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Riserva naturale di Poggio all'Olmo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

400 hectares protected between Cinigiano and Arcidosso, between 800 and 1,000 meters, with views of Valle dell'Ombrone, Monte Amiata and Val d'Orcia; black pines, firs and centuries-old chestnuts alternate with meadows and shrubs, with species such as Linaria purpurea and Viola Etrusca.

  • Where Poggio all'Olmo, Cinigiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · comune.cinigiano.gr.it

Vigneti del Montecucco

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

The territory is part of the Montecucco denomination (red, Sangiovese, white and Vermentino); wines and oils are tasted in companies and cellars along the Montecucco Wine and Flavour Trail, and at the end of September-early October the Grape Festival brings the parade of the floats of the three districts and the open cellars.

  • Where Cinigiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
