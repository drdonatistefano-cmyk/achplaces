What to see in Fratte Rosa

Terracotta village in the valley of Cesano, with the ancient spiral nucleus of pink brick houses closed by the medieval walls and the Franciscan monastery of Santa Vittoria (1216), home of the Museum of Terracotta; in the village the workshops of the cocciari, active for centuries.

Convento di Santa Vittoria e Museo delle terrecotte

Museums · Themed

The convent was founded in 1216 in the wake of the preaching of St. Francis; on the ground floor of the western side it houses the demoethanological Museum of Terracotta, dedicated to the craftsmanship of terracotta for which Fratte Rosa is known.

Centro storico e mura medievali

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient nucleus, probably of high-medieval foundation, has a spiral plant and houses of pink bricks enclosed by medieval walls, with a malaestian well; in the center the work "The sign of fire" by the sculptor Loreno Sguanci, dedicated to the processing of terracotta.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Fratte Rosa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it
