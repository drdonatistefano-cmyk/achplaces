What to see in Bardolino

Village on the Veronese shore of Garda, with the historic center of fishing houses, the lakefront and the church of San Zeno, one of the most important Carolingian monuments in Italy; in Cisano the Romanesque parish church of Santa Maria and the Museo dell'Olio tell the landscape of olive groves and vineyards that surround the village.

Chiesa di San Zeno

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Considered one of the most important Carolingian monuments in Italy, it is a remarkable example of architecture of that time. It is part of the virtual tour of the historical sites of the municipality, with historical notes and audio narration usable as audio guide on site, also in English and German.

Chiesa di San Severo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church linked to archaeological finds of Roman times, which preserves evidence of Verona's Romanesque art. It is included in the virtual tour of the historical sites of the Municipality, with audio guide in Italian, English and German.

  • Entry To be checked

Centro storico, castello e lungolago

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historic centre preserves the typical fishing houses, flowering streets and remains of the medieval walls, with doors and watchtowers; the castle was most probably wanted by Berengario, king of Italy. The lakeside promenade is the most popular promenade, with wide beaches and, on the lake front, a panoramic wheel.

  • Where Lungolago, Bardolino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · tourism.verona.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Museo dell'Olio

Museums · Themed

It traces the history of the production of olive oil, from a long period in which the methods of processing seemed unchanged until the early twentieth century, when technological progress marked a turning point. It has ancient tools in use since the eighteenth century, including lever presses and grinders operated by hydraulic wheel; a section with videos and educational panels explains the cultivation of the olive and the production of the oil. Accessible without architectural barriers.

  • Where Via Peschiera 54, Cisano di Bardolino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.bardolino.vr.it · tourism.verona.it · italia.it
