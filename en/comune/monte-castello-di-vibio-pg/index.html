What to see in Monte Castello di Vibio

The village walled on the hill above the Tiber valley, opposite Todi: it houses the Teatro della Concordia, 99 places, indicated as the smallest historical theatre in activity, the fourteenth-century Tower of Porta Maggio with the collection of local relics and the terrace of Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, balcony on Umbria; just above the castle of Doglio.

Teatro della Concordia

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Inaugurated in 1808, wanted by nine families of the village in the Napoleonic era and called "Concordia" in the ideals of the French Revolution: 99 seats between stages and audiences, boxes entirely in wood and bell plan for acoustics; vault and boxes frescoed by Cesare Agretti and his son Luigi, then 15 years old (1892). Closed in 1951 and collapsed the roof, it was restored with the autotaxation of the inhabitants and European funds until 1993; in 1997 it was twinned with the Farnese Theatre of Parma. Gina Lollobrigida debuted there in 1945. Shows and guided tours by the Society of the Theatre of Concordia (Tel. +39 075 8780737).

Torre di Porta Maggio

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The 14th-century crenellated tower of the walls, on the south-east side towards Todi; it houses a collection of local relics with ancient weapons, noble coats of arms, cadastral maps of the late 18th and early 19th centuries, Roman finds and an autograph letter of Garibaldi from 1862. From the top you can see Todi and the Middle Valley of the Tiber.

  • Where Via Regina Margherita, Monte Castello di Vibio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · umbriatourism.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II e la terrazza

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The main square is a balcony on Umbria: from the terrace the view reaches to the mountains of Lazio and Abruzzo. In the alleys of the village a medieval well-tank and the sixteenth-century portal of the former monastery.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Monte Castello di Vibio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa dei Santi Filippo e Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Nineteenth-century parish church on the main square; it preserves the statue of the Madonna dei Portenti, considered miraculous by the inhabitants. Feast of the patrons on May 3.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Monte Castello di Vibio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · umbriatourism.it

Chiesa di Santa Illuminata (del Santissimo Crocifisso)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the historical centre, born as an oratory and modified in the nineteenth century; it houses a wooden crucifix of the fifteenth century considered miraculous and still venerated. The apse was painted by Luigi Agretti, the same painter of the theatre.

  • Where Centro storico, Monte Castello di Vibio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Doglio e Porta Fuje

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Higher on the wooded hill, on the road to Todi: it was a Ghibelline castle overlooking the Guelphs of Orvieto. You enter from the fourteenth-century Porta Fuje, with the stone coat of arms with the eagle of Todi; there rises the abbey of Santa Maria in Monte.

  • Where Doglio, Monte Castello di Vibio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · umbriatourism.it
