What to see in Ormea

Città dell'Alta Valle Tanaro on the border with Liguria, the ancient Ulmeta has a heart-shaped plant crossed by the alleys called "Trevi": the Romanesque parish church of San Martino with frescoes of the fourteenth century, the House of Lords of the fourteenth century, the ruins of the castle, the ethnographic museum and, in the surroundings, the Balma del Messere, the Tower of the Saracens and the Balcony of Ormea.

9 places to visit in Ormea, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo etnografico Alta Val Tanaro

Museums · Themed

In the house next to the church of the Madonna degli Angeli, it offers a path of memory with the faithful reconstruction of domestic environments of the past: a house from the beginning of the twentieth century. Open on Sundays from 3 pm to 5 pm or on reservation (tel. +39 0174 391101).

  • Where Via Madonna degli Angeli, Ormea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · comune.ormea.cn.it

Parrocchiale di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built around the middle of the fifteenth century on a Romanesque church and extended to the present three aisles, it has the bell tower set on a tower-door of the medieval walls, under which you can still see the ancient gate of the city. Inside the late fourteenth-century frescoes, a painting attributed to Moncalvo and the main altar and pulpit from the Dominican monastery of Garessio; the nineteenth-century façade has a fresco by Eugenio Arduino. Open every day 8-19.

  • Where Via Roma 175, Ormea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · comune.ormea.cn.it

Centro storico e Percorso del Cuore

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center has a heart-shaped plan and alleys called "Trevi"; the municipal path goes from Piazza della Libertà, with the elm symbol of the ancient Ulmeta, to the Ponte dei Corni linked to the legend of the castle Belisario, along Via Roma (covered with garlands of Mayciondolo for Corpus Domini), Piazza Nuova cobbolata and the gardens

  • Where Piazza della Libertà, Ormea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.ormea.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Casa dei Signori di Ormea

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

A noble residence of the fourteenth century called Casa Gillino, probably home of the first lords of Ormea: in the façade sculpted panels in black stone, a bifora with storied glass and a black marble frieze above the door. You can see it from the outside.

  • Where Via Tanaro, Ormea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.ormea.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Ruderi del Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

It arose around the 10th century against the incursions of Saracens and Ungari, was expanded several times and settled definitively by Maurizio di Savoia; in 1794 the French troops dismantled it and today remains the ruins.

  • Where Via del Castello, Ormea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.ormea.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Mostra permanente sulla campagna militare in Russia

Museums · Themed

On the first floor of the old primary schools, he reconstructs the war on the Russian front with panels, documents, photographs and testimonies of the veterans, with photographic documentation on the Battalion Ceva. Visits by reservation (Tel. +39 0174 391101).

  • Where Via Bassi 31, Ormea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Balma del Messere

History and architecture · Archaeology

Also known as the Saracen Cave, it is a natural cave at the foot of a rocky wall closed by a wall in stone and mortar of the tenth century, often about 70 cm at the base, tied to Saracen raids; the excavations of 1979-1982 found traces of human presence in the Neolithic. Visit Cuneese indicates it as the largest fortified cave in the Western Alps.

  • Where Frazione Cantarana, Ormea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.ormea.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Torre dei Saraceni

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Short route (1.5 km, about 30 minutes, suitable for all) from Barchi Sottana (664 m) to a knife of quartz rock (893 m) on whose top remain the ruins of a stone watch tower tied with live lime.

  • Where Frazione Barchi Sottana, Ormea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.ormea.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Balconata di Ormea

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Naturalistic and ethnographic itinerary restored by the CAI of Ormea, with low levels and divisible in stages: from Piazza della Libertà touches the villages Pronai, Chioraira, Aimoni and Biranco with a stretch of balcony on the Tanaro Valley, to Ponte di Nava. Visit Cuneese indicates it in about 40 km (about 32 km and 1,540 m of difference in altitude in the mountain bike card, recommended from April to November).

Visit Cuneese signals stretches not yet available after a flood: inquire at the tourist office of the Tanaro Valley

  • Where Piazza della Libertà, Ormea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
