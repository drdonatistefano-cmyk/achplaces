What to see in Vallepietra

Country of the Simbruini Mountains, in the Regional Natural Park, between Monte Tarino and Monte Autore and the valley of the Simbrivio; the crenellated tower of the Caetani dominates the square and at 1,300 m, in the rock of the Colle della Tagliata, stands the Sanctuary of the Holy Trinity, a destination of secular pilgrimages.

Santuario della Santissima Trinità

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small church in a natural cave against the wall of the Colle della Tagliata on Monte Autore, about 1,337 m above the springs of the Simbrovio; you go down a staircase in the rock, arriving from Vallepietra on asphalted road or on foot on the paths (including 652 and 683a). On the west wall the fresco of the Trinity of the twelfth century, with three identical figures sitting to the Greek, and under the remains of a cycle of agricultural works; on the east wall frescoes of Byzantine taste with Annunciation, Nativity, Adoration of the Magi and Presentation, plus Madonnas of the fifteenth century. The origins are uncertain, between Eastern monks and Saint Dominic of Sora (X-XII century). The festive cliffs on Sunday after Pentecost, with the "compagnie" of the Valle dell'Aniene on the way and the Pianto delle Zitelle.

Sale museali Ex Voto del Santuario

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 2003 in the halls under the square of the Sanctuary, with two entrances: it exhibits the former most significant votes donated by pilgrims and a room dedicated to the Pope's visit to the Sanctuary.

  • Where SP 45, Santuario SS. Trinità, Vallepietra

Museo civico e Centro di documentazione

Museums · City history · Free entry

A small four-room museum in the historic centre on the town's civil history, which grew up in the Middle Ages around the Caetans, and on the pilgrimage to the Trinity: sacred furnishings and vestments and relics of the church of St. John the Evangelist, confraternities, priests, ex-vote, old photographs and the Pianto delle Zitelle. Next to the Documentation Centre with videos, photos and a documentary on the Sanctuary; managed by the Cultural Association Don Salvatore Mercuri.

Parks Lazio indicates opening all year 9-12 and 15-17 with free entrance (tel. 0774 899084), but MiC card (December 2020) gives closed every day

Torre Caetani e centro storico

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The square-shaped crenellated tower on the main square is the symbol of the village, erected by the Astalli, Troili and Caetani families; the Caetani held the fief from 1297 to 1670 and remains of the ancient castle. To the village you enter the Door of the Sun and the Neapolitan Door.

  • Where Piazza principale, Vallepietra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com

Monte Autore, Monte Tarino e sentieri dei Simbruini

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The territory, in the Regional Natural Park of the Simbruini Mountains, is closed between Monte Tarino (1,959 m) and Monte Autore (1,853 m), with the valley of the Simbrivio; from Vallepietra start hiking trails (683a-d towards Monte Authore, 684 towards Monte Tarinello and the Pozzo della Neve, 685 towards Colle della Tagliata), the Coleman trails towards Jenne and Camerata Nuova and MTB routes.
