What to see in Mompantero

Mountain town of Val di Susa at the foot of the Rocciamelone (3538 m), with the hamlets along the Cenischia stream and over thirty villages on the slopes; from the sanctuary of Pietrastretta starts the mule track for the summit, where stands the highest sanctuary in Europe, and along the road there are pre-Roman rock carvings.

7 places to visit in Mompantero, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocciamelone e santuario di vetta

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The peak of 3538 m, in the Middle Ages considered the highest of the Alps and visible from the Via Francigena; the first documented ascent is of 1 September 1358, when Bonifacio Rotario d'Asti brought to the top a bronze triptych today in the cathedral of Susa. At the top is the shrine of Our Lady of the Rock, the highest shrine in Europe, and the 3 meters high bronze statue of Our Lady, inaugurated on August 28, 1899, paid by 130,000 children and brought to the top in eight pieces by the Alpine battalion From Urbiano to the Rifugio La Riposa (2205 m), then on foot to the Rifugio Ca' d'Asti (2856 m): about 4 hours and 30, recommended in summer.

  • Where Partenza dal parcheggio del rifugio La Riposa, Mompantero
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.mompantero.to.it

Sources: comune.mompantero.to.it

Santuario della Madonna del Rocciamelone (Pietrastretta)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Diocesan shrine and since 1958 parish of Mompantero, built in 1957-58 on a project by Emanuele Godone where there was a small chapel, at the entrance of the mule track for the summit. Modern building in concrete, wood and stone, with bell tower behind the apse surmounted by a statue of the Madonna and apsidal windows open on the slopes of the mountain; on August 5 there are the celebrations.

  • Where Frazione Pietrastretta 51, Mompantero
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.mompantero.to.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.mompantero.to.it

Museo della Resistenza

Museums · City history · Free entry

Owned by the Municipality and set up in 1997-98 by ANPI, which manages it with volunteers: about 400 photos, divided by partisans, alpine and garibaldini, underground newspapers, tables of deportees and internees, flags of the Star Division, German topographic maps and diaries of Ada Gobetti. Each year at the end of August the Battle of the Sevine Granges (24-25 August 1944) is remembered. Free visits by booking (museoreeistenzampantero@gmail.com, tel. 0122 32065).

  • Where Piazza Giulio Bolaffi 1, Mompantero
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.mompantero.to.it

Sources: comune.mompantero.to.it

Incisioni rupestri e Madonna dell'Ecova

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Along the road to the Rocciamelone, between the 718 m of the church of the Madonna dell'Ecova and the 1275 m of Chiamberlando, were found rock engravings and graffiti testifying a pre-Roman settlement, probably Celtic.

  • Where Strada per il Rocciamelone, dalla Madonna dell'Ecova a Chiamberlando, Mompantero
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.mompantero.to.it

Sources: comune.mompantero.to.it
