What to see in Zagarise

The village of Sila Piccola was built around the year 1000, with a Norman tower dating back to the 13th-XIV centuries that now houses an astronomical observatory, the fifteenth-century church of Santa Maria Assunta with the Museum of Sacred Art and a museum of oil in an ancient oil mill; in the territory the canyon of Tempe Rosse, the waterfalls Campanaro and Mount Gariglione.

7 places to visit in Zagarise, in the province of Catanzaro (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre Normanna

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Massive cylindrical tower dating from the 13th to the 14th century, linked to the fortifications built in Calabria by the Normans of Robert Guiscardo; it was probably a lookout tower for a more important castle and once had blackbirds and ornaments on the summit. A functioning astronomical observatory is now installed at the top. Free visit by reservation via the town control unit (0961 937014).

  • Where Piazza Cesare Battisti, Zagarise
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.zagarise.cz.it · italia.it

Museo d'arte sacra «Silvestro Frangipane»

Museums · Old masters

Named after Father Silvestro Frangipane, who lived in the convent of Zagarise between 1570 and 1650, he was set up in 1990 by the parish priest Don Egidio Pudia; the nucleus of the collection are 36 paintings of the monks of that period. It contains frescoes and paintings from the sixteenth century and paintings and sculptures from the seventeenth to the nineteenth century, all coming from Zagarise, especially from the church and the Dominican monastery and from the parish church of Santa Maria Assunta, to which the museum is annexed.

MiC card (updated 2020) is closed every day; information at 0961 937002

  • Where Via Canonica, Zagarise
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

15th century church in the historic center of the village; next to it is the Museum of Sacred Art "Silvestro Frangipane" (Silvestro Frangipane), which also houses paintings from this church.

  • Where Centro storico, Zagarise
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo dell'olio e della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Wanted by the Sila Park Authority, together with the museums of Albi and Longobucco, it is located in an ancient restored mill where olive processing plants are still visible; it is dedicated to the territory of Silano, the arts and local traditions. Zagarise is part of the National Oil City Association. Free visit by reservation (central of the City 0961 937014); accessible for disabled persons with lift.

  • Where Via Marconi, Zagarise
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.zagarise.cz.it · italia.it

Cascate Campanaro

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Paid entry

Waterfall of the river Campanaro about 20 meters high, in a forest of the Sila National Park along the road between Sersale and Zagarise. From the road edge, with few parking spaces, a marked path of a few hundred meters descends for about 70 meters of difference in altitude, with a small parking area; it is not accessible to the disabled and we recommend trekking shoes.

  • Where Strada Statale 109, tra Sersale e Zagarise
  • Entry €3 in certain periods of the year
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.zagarise.cz.it · italia.it

Monte Gariglione

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Mountain of Sila Small with picnic areas and a panorama that embraces both the Ionian Sea and the Tyrrhenian Sea; in the locality of Latteria, about 1,600 meters, is the Leo Grandinetti refuge.

  • Where Località Latteria, Monte Gariglione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
