What to see in Marostica

The walled city of the fourteenth century with two castles joined by almost two kilometers of walls and, at the foot of the Lower Castle, the chessboard square of the Partita a Scacchi to living characters; in the churches works of Jacopo Dal Ponte called the Bassano.

5 places to visit in Marostica, in the province of Vicenza (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Marostica in the guide with map and filters

Castello Inferiore

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Quadrangular fence in local stone with mast, two drawbridges and internal loggias; built in the fourteenth century under the Scaligeri, in the Venetian age it was the seat of the podestà and the Council of the Thirty and hosted the prisons until the early twentieth century. You can visit 12 frescoed rooms and the 6 cells of the tower.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Marostica
  • Entry €8, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitmarostica.eu · vicenzae.org · italia.it

Mura e Cammino di ronda

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The crenellated walls of about 1700-1800 meters with 24 torrents and the doors Vicentina, Bassanese and Breganzina, built from 1372 under Cansignorio della Scala; the round path, at 8-12 meters above sea level, runs with harness starting from the Tower of the Compagnia delle Mura.

open only Sundays and holidays from March to November, times vary with the weather

  • Where Torre della Compagnia delle Mura, Marostica
  • Entry €5, reduced €3; cumulative with Lower Castle €11
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitmarostica.eu · italia.it

Piazza Castello e la scacchiera

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The large square at the foot of the Lower Castle, with the chessboard drawn on the floor, hosts the Chess game to living characters, re-enactment Renaissance in the second weekend of September of even years.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Marostica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · vicenzae.org

Castello Superiore e Sentiero dei Carmini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the top of the Pausolino hill, it had four doors and twenty towers and is in ruins after the Venetian cannons of 1510; climbing from the Panoramic Carmini Path in the Salin Park, rebuilt stone by stone by the Compagnia delle Mura and inaugurated in 1989, with views from the Grappa to the Euganean and Berici Hills and the Asiago Plateau.

  • Where Colle Pausolino, Marostica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: vicenzae.org · visitmarostica.eu · italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Documented from 1383 and enlarged in 1730-40, with fifteenth-century cloister; on the high altar the Preaching of St. Paul at the Areopagus by Jacopo Dal Ponte called the Bassano and his son Francesco (1574), the most valuable work of art in the city, and on the ceiling frescoes by Giuseppe Graziani.

  • Where Via Sant'Antonio, Marostica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitmarostica.eu · italia.it
