What to see in Papasidero

The village perched 208 metres in the Pollino National Park, with medieval structure built around a Longobard fortress; in the territory the Romito Cave, with the famous paleolithic graffiti of the early Bos, the sanctuary of the Madonna of Constantinople overlooking the Lao and the abandoned village of Avena.

Grotta del Romito e Antiquarium

History and architecture · Archaeology

Site of the Upper Paleolithic in the locality Nuppolara, discovered in 1961: outside the 1.20 meters long graffito of two bovids (Bos primigenius), among the oldest evidence of prehistoric art in Italy, and burials of about 10,500 years ago (three double burials, including the young hunter The findings cover about 23,000-10,000 years ago; in the Neolithic layer much obsidian suggests a base for trade between Tyrrhenian and Ionian. The site has catwalks, lighting, multilingual audio guides and an antiquarium with some artifacts; the reproduction of the graffito is at the National Museum of Reggio Calabria.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Santuario della Madonna di Costantinopoli

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

A 17th-century shrine leaning against the rock on a ridge, surrounded by greenery and overlooking the river Lao; it is a destination for pilgrimages for a fresco considered miraculous in the 17th century.

  • Where Papasidero
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.papasidero.cs.it

Chiesa Madre

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in the middle of the seventeenth century and enlarged at the end of the eighteenth century and in the first half of the nineteenth century, it has a T-plant with three naves; it preserves a Byzantine fresco of the year Mille and a large fresco with the Virgin with the Child, a kneeling bishop and the archangel Michele who pierces Satan.

  • Where Papasidero
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.papasidero.cs.it · italia.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village, perched on a hill at 208 meters in the Pollino Park, preserves the medieval urban layout formed between the XI and XIII century around a Longobard fortress, then become a castle.

  • Where Papasidero
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.papasidero.cs.it

Avena

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry

Fraction close to the village, abandoned by the inhabitants in 1982 after an earthquake: a small ghost village where you can see glimpses of the daily life of the post-war period.

Fiume Lao

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

The Lao runs 51 km from the province of Potenza to the Tyrrhenian Sea near Scalea; in the Natural Reserve of the Valle del Rijeka Lao its rapids, between gorges and canyons, are destination for rafting and canoeing.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
