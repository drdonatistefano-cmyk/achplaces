What to see in Fossacesia

Hillside village about 2 km from the sea on the Costa dei Trabocchi, famous for the Abbey of San Giovanni in Venere on the promontory overlooking the gulf; on the coast there are beaches of white pebbles and sandy coves up to Punta Cavalluccio, with its overflow.

Abbazia di San Giovanni in Venere

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

On the promontory overlooking the Costa dei Trabocchi, it was founded in 1015 by the will of Trasmondo II, Count of Teate, buried in the crypt, on a small monastic cell documented between the VIII and the X century; the name would recall a Roman temple of Venus of which only the place name remains. The present aspect is due to the work of Abbot Odorisio II (1165-1204) and Abbot Rainaldo (1225-1230): the portal of the Moon, with the bezel of Christ enthroned between the Baptist and St. Benedict and the stories of St. John the Baptist, is crossed by the sun at the sunset of the summer solstice, while at the winter solstice the light enters from the apses (Porta del Sole). Inside three aisles, raised presbytery and crypt with frescoes from the middle of the thirteenth century close to the workshop of Jacopo Torriti; the 13th century cloister, partly rebuilt in 1932-1935, retains the inscription of Odorisio II. Today the Passionist Fathers live there; open every day according to the MiC card (April-October until 20, November-March until 19), guided tours by reservation.

Piazza del Popolo e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The small well-preserved old town is located on a hill about 124 meters high, with public buildings and noble villas built from the seventeenth century onwards. On Piazza del Popolo there is the parish church of San Donato Martire, rebuilt after the bombings of the Second World War, with a Latin cross with neo-Romanic façade and rose window, the noble buildings Contini and Mayer and the Fontana delle Cinque Cannelle.

Spiaggia e trabocco di Punta Cavalluccio

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small sandy cove surrounded by lush vegetation, home to the famous Trabocco di Punta Cavalluccio; it is the rocky stretch of the coast dotted with overflows, some of which house small restaurants on the sea.

Spiagge di Fossacesia Marina e Golfo di Venere

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Litorale mostly of white pebbles, with small sandy coves towards the mouth of Sangro, free and equipped beaches and waters awarded with the Blue Flag; the small Gulf of Venus opens under the abbey promontory, between brooms, fennel, orange groves and olive groves that reach almost the sea.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
