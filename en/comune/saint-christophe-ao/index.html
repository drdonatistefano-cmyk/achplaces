What to see in Saint-Christophe

Sunny hill of orchards and vineyards 3 km from Aosta, with the castle of the Counts Passerin d'Entrèves and a fifteenth-century crucifix among the masterpieces of the Val d'Aosta.

3 places to visit in Saint-Christophe, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Surrounded by a large park, transformed into a noble residence still inhabited by the accounts, with a rich collection of art; open to visitors during events.

Chiesa parrocchiale di Saint-Christophe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

High Romanesque bell tower with cusp and bifore; inside a triumphal crucifix of the fifteenth century among the most important works of the Valdostan heritage.
