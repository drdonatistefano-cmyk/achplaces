What to see in Arsoli

Borgo dei Monti Simbruini 58 km from Rome, which Pirandello called "little Paris" (the town of Paris), linked to the Massimo for over four centuries: the Castello Massimo dominates the centre, with Piazza Valeria, the church of the Salvatore di Giacomo Della Porta, Villa Morani and the Museum of Musical Traditions of the Aniene Valley.

Museo delle tradizioni musicali della Valle dell'Aniene

Museums · Themed

Opened on 4 November 2006 on the ground floor of the former Granaio Massimo, on the course of Borgo San Bartolomeo, it is dedicated to the musical culture of Arsoli and the Aniene Valley in four sections: pastoral instruments (flauts and clarinets of cane, flutes of bark, horns, bells), artisan instruments (organetto, putipù, triangle, tambourines), instruments of the rite (raganelle, battole, shells, with ample space to the Pilgrimage of the Holy Trinity) and band tradition, with nineteenth century brass and documents of the band of Arsoli, some of the eighteenth century. Many objects are gifts of the inhabitants and of the Maximus family. Opening hours: mon-ven 9.30-12.30, sab-dom 9.30-12.30 and 15.30-18.30, Tuesday closed.

Castello Massimo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Castle on three floors that dominates from the cliff the historic center, of the Massimo of Rome for over four centuries; it preserves frescoes of the late sixteenth century commissioned to the Zuccari and of the eighteenth century to the Benefial, a collection of weapons, the Sala del Trono and the Chapel of San Filippo Neri with part of the cosmatesque facade. In the basement there is the ancient oven, the storehouses of food and oil and the prison; around woods and an Italian hanging garden.

  • Where Centro storico, Arsoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it

Piazza Valeria

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Elongated heart of the village: on one head an octagonal fountain of the sixteenth century, on the other a Roman family column of the ancient Via Valeria surmounted by the phoenix, symbol of rebirth became coat of arms of Arsoli after the plague of 1656. Outside the village remain the remains of a villa of the second century BC and the cippus of the XXXVIII mile of the Via Valeria.

  • Where Piazza Valeria, Arsoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com

Villa Morani

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Among the historic residences of Lazio, built around 1880 near the station and purchased in 1902 by the painter Alessandro Morani and his wife Lili Helbig; from the window of the studio you can see the landscape that the artist painted. He hosted D'Annunzio, Pascarella and the artists of "In Arte Libertas" .

  • Entry To be checked
