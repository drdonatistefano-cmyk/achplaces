What to see in Villagrande Strisaili

High Ogliastra country on the border with Barbagia, known for the longevity of its inhabitants, with a nuragic heritage of the first floor: the village-sanctuary of S'Arcu 'e is Forros, Sa Carcaredda and Troculu, as well as the park of Santa Barbara, the natural pools of Bau Mela and the lake of the upper Flumendosa near the village of Villanova Strisaili.

Villaggio-santuario di S'Arcu 'e is Forros

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Nuragic village-sanctuary between Barbagia and Ogliastra, from the recent-final Bronze to the early Iron Age: the main megaron temple, about 17 meters long, in granite and shale with temenos and seats, a second temple with two-tone altar today largely at the Nuoro museum, isolated houses and workshops with lead ovens, and not far away a trilobed nuraghe. It is described as the most important metallurgical center of Nuragic Sardinia, with objects from Etruria, Greece and Near East; excavated since 1984 by Maria Ausilia Fadda.

  • Where SS 389 var. Buddusò-Correboi km 41,200, località S'Arcu 'e is Forros, Villagrande Strisaili
  • Entry €5, reduced €3; cumulative €8
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it

Area archeologica di Sa Carcaredda

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

At about 800 meters above sea level in a wood of holm oaks near the village, the remains of an extensive nuragic village with tombs of giants and a small temple with circular plan, rare example of nuragic temple in the antis.

  • Where SS 389 var. Buddusò, località Riu 'e Binu, Villagrande Strisaili
  • Entry €5, reduced €3; cumulative €8
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Tomba di Giganti di Troculu

History and architecture · Archaeology

Main rest of a nuragic complex with two nuraghes and a village of the Bronze Age: the tomb, in granite blocks on irregular rows with the body still intact, was disassembled and rebuilt not far from the end of the twentieth century for the construction of the Nuoro-Lanusei highway.

  • Where Località Genna Troculu, SS 389 tra Piraonni e Villagrande Strisaili
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Piscine naturali di Bau Mela

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The stream has dug the granite forming two waterfalls in succession, with a larger pool and other smaller pools of crystal clear water between granite walls; they can be reached on foot through the spot with age-old junipers and asphodles, passing next to a dam and a hydroelectric power plant of the Flumendosa high system.

  • Where Villagrande Strisaili, alta Ogliastra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Parco di Santa Barbara e cascata di Sothai

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Wood with seven tombs of giants, a megalithic tomb, 17 nuraghi and ten Nuragic villages, where at the beginning of July the saint's feast is held; from the park a path leads to the waterfall of Sothai, formed by the Flumendosa that pours into the canyon of Bau Vigo.

  • Where Villagrande Strisaili
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Lago dell'alto Flumendosa a Villanova Strisaili

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

A few minutes from the hamlet of Villanova Strisaili, the shores of the lake are frequented by hikers and mountain bikers and its waters as canoeists; in the surroundings the gorge of Pirincanes and the waterfalls of Rio 'e Forru, which are admired by a natural balcony.

  • Where Villanova Strisaili, Villagrande Strisaili
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
