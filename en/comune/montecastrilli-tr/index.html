What to see in Montecastrilli

Fortified village of the Amerino which preserves the walls with crenellated towers and the Porta Amerina with two arches, with the parish church of San Nicolò; in the hamlets the churches of Quadrelli and Casteltodino and, in Castel dell'Aquila, the collection of tools of rural civilization.

Porta Amerina e mura

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Medieval gate with two arches, also called Torre del Belvedere, is what remains of the ancient fortified complex of the village; the walls, marked by crenellated towers, are still visible. In the walls there is also a second door, the Medieval Gate.

  • Where Centro storico, Montecastrilli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built between the 10th and 11th centuries and restored in 1964 (facade and bell tower), it has six chapels, three of them baroque, with decorations attributed to the Zuccari. It preserves a wooden crucifix of the fifteenth century, an icon of the sixteenth century Madonna Refugium Peccatorum and canvases of Bartolomeo Poliziano (Sant'Antonio and Santa Lucia) and Ricci da Urbino (Madonna del Rosario).

  • Where Montecastrilli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Quadrelli, chiese di Santa Maria Assunta e del Santo Rosario

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the hamlet of Quadrelli the church of Santa Maria Assunta is Romanesque; the seventeenth-century church of the Confraternita del Santo Rosario has a trusse ceiling with terracotta tiles decorated with the coat of arms of Cardinal Nicola Canali.
