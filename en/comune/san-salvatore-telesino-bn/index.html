What to see in San Salvatore Telesino

In the Calore valley, San Salvatore Telesino preserves the remains of the Benedictine Abbey of the Holy Savior, founded in the ninth century, which houses the Antiquarium with the Sanniti and Roman finds of ancient Telesia; just outside the village the Grassano Park offers springs, trails and canoe.

Abbazia Benedettina del Santo Salvatore

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Monastery complex founded in the ninth century in the Calore valley, in the Middle Ages was one of the most important religious centers in the area and played a role in the spread of Benedictine monasticism in the South; destroyed and rebuilt several times after invasions, earthquakes and lootings. There are still parts of the ancient church, sections of mosaic floor, fragments of frescoes and other medieval structures, in an archaeological area that documents the monastic life. Open on Sunday 10:30-12:30, Monday and Saturday by reservation (tel. 0824 948144).

Museo civico di Telesia (Antiquarium)

Museums · Archaeological

Set up in rooms of the Benedictine Abbey and opened from June 19, 2010, it collects finds of Sannitica and Roman age, from the fourth century BC to the second century AD, coming from the excavations of the ancient city of Telesia; the route follows the Sannita period, the Roman colony and the destruction of the city, in an environment with frescoes of the fourteenth century and underground spaces. Booking not required; the ministerial card does not record active times, referent to Pro Loco.

Parco del Grassano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Natural oasis with clear springs and lush vegetation crossed by the river Grassano, suitable for picnics, excursions and canoeing, with areas for families, nature trails, canoe rental, play area, bars and restaurants. Open from Tuesday to Sunday from 9:30 am to 11:30 pm (in August also on Monday).

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Sources: comune.sansalvatoretelesino.bn.it

Chiesa Parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Of medieval origin and restored over the centuries, it is the main church of the village, with a simple façade of Baroque influence and bell tower; inside marble altars, devotional statues and a fresco of the Assumption of the Virgin.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
