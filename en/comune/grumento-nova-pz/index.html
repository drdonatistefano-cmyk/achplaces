What to see in Grumento Nova

Borgo dell'alta Val d'Agri with a view of the remains of the Sanseverino castle; at the foot of the hill the Archaeological Park of Grumentum, the largest Roman city of Lucania, and the National Archaeological Museum of the Alta Val d'Agri, a stone's throw from Lake Pertusillo.

Parco archeologico di Grumentum

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

On over 40 hectares at the foot of the hill, the city was born as a small Lucan center in the third century BC, crossed by Hannibal in the second Punic war, rebuilt Roman colony in the middle of the first century BC and became the main center of southern Lucania. Theatre, italic temple, domus with mosaics, hole with Capitolium, Augusteum, basilica, curia and thermal baths, Major Baths with mosaics and marbles, the first cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta and one of the oldest masonry amphitheaters known.

times between MiC card (seasonal, closed on Mondays) and italia.it; check before visiting

  • Where Contrada Città, Grumento Nova
  • Entry €4, reduced €2; cumulative with the museum €6, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo archeologico nazionale dell'Alta Val d'Agri

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

It tells the population of the valley from the prehistory (rests of Elephas antiquus about 120,000 years ago) to the Apennine civilization, to the Lucanian kits and to the ex vows of the Sanctuary of San Marco (III century BC); the Roman section on Grumentum exposes the marble head of Livia Drusilla, a ivory piss from the hole, statues from the thermal baths, epigraphs and coins, until the high-medieval abandonment of the city. Open Tuesday-Sunday 8:30-19:30.

  • Where Contrada Spineta, Grumento Nova
  • Entry €4, reduced €2; cumulative with the €6 park, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Belvedere di Piazzale Aurora Sanseverino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The square is located on the remains of the castle and is the most evocative viewpoint of the village, with panoramic telescope and musical and cultural events in summer; the ancient stable, the Sanseverino Castle Hall, is restored and hosts conferences and events.

  • Where Piazzale Aurora Sanseverino, Grumento Nova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Bosco dei Cigni e Lago del Pertusillo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

The Bosco di Maglie, called Bosco dei Signi, stretches in the plain not far from the museum to the shores of Lake Pertusillo, with picnic areas suitable for family trips.

  • Where Bosco di Maglie, Grumento Nova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
