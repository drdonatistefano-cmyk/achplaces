What to see in Civitella Roveto

Medieval village of Valle Roveto, closed by well preserved walls: the ancient part rises on an isolated spur, the modern one on the other bank of the river was rebuilt after the earthquake of 1915. To be seen are the "rue" with carved portals and votive newsstands, the church of San Giovanni Battista and the Pinacoteca Enrico Mattei.

Borgo antico, mura e rue

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The ancient nucleus, mentioned as Civitella from the 11th century (first Petrarolo) and from the 15th century seat of a barony of the Column, is surrounded by fortified walls still well preserved; the streets, called "rue" are flanked by houses with stone portals carved by local chisels, coats of arms and religious symbols on the architraves and votive newsstands dug in the walls. Among the noble palaces, Palazzo Colonna, Ferrazzilli, Villa and Libri.

  • Where Centro storico, Civitella Roveto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.civitella-roveto.aq.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church dating back to the fifteenth century, in Baroque forms, which preserves an ancient organ and in the apse frescoes on the life of St. John the Baptist. On June 24, the feast of the saint provides a ritual bath in the Liri and a procession with fireworks.

  • Where Piazza San Giovanni, Civitella Roveto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.civitella-roveto.aq.it · italia.it

Pinacoteca Enrico Mattei

Museums · Modern art

He collects paintings of Italian and foreign artists of the twentieth century, acquired over time by Pro Loco, with donations of Amadio, Carma, Casals, Eolo and Raffaele Costi, Fiore, Le Donne, Marconi, Ragionieri and Sernia.

  • Where Piazza San Giovanni, Civitella Roveto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Fonte d'Auta

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Stone fountain of 1844 from which water flows from five anthropomorphic masks.
