What to see in Piombino

Port-city for the Archipelago, with walls partly attributed to Leonardo and the gulf of Baratti with Populonia, the only Etruscan city on the sea.

3 places to visit in Piombino, in the province of Livorno (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parco Archeologico di Baratti e Populonia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In the Gulf of Baratti the necropolis of the only Etruscan city on the sea, with Etruscan settlements, Roman ruins and the medieval fortress.

  • Where Baratti, Piombino
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Mura e Fonti di Marina

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The city walls, with stretches attributed to Leonardo da Vinci, and the Marina Sources on the sea.

  • Where Piombino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Palazzo Comunale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
