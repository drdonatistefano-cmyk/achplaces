What to see in Maccagno con Pino e Veddasca

Borgo on the Varesina shore of Lake Maggiore, among the first towns of Verbano, divided by the river Giona in Borgo Inferiore and Superiore; thirteenth-century plant with the Imperial Tower, the Mandelli Castle, the Madonna della Punta on the promontory and the Parisi Valle Civic Museum on the lakefront.

Borgo antico e Rione Maggiore

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village preserves the thirteenth century plant, with two streets connected by cobbled staircases and vaulted passages; in the Rione Maggiore (Borgo Inferiore) the houses are narrow between narrow streets. Votive frescoes from the sixteenth century are scattered throughout the country, near the port there is the large seventeenth-century coat of arms of the Mandelli with three rampant red chepards, and the Imperial Mint founded in 1622, today incorporated in a hotel, preserves the road that fed the sweat. Corte Branca, with its portico, is the most expressive historical building in the country.

Castello Mandelli

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Civico Museo Parisi Valle

Museums · Modern art

At the centre of the lake promenade, the building was designed in 1980-86 by the architect Maurizio Sacripanti and since 1998 hosts a permanent collection with works by Picasso, Balla, De Chirico, Cassinari, Rijeka and Levi, as well as temporary exhibitions. The collection has grown with an acquisition project started in 2006 and has over 200 paintings and sculptures.

Passeggiata sul lungolago

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of about 4 km along Lake Maggiore separated by traffic, passing through the museum and ending in Maccagno Superiore at a rock gym; from the Borgo Superiore the view embraces much of the Verbano, from the Canton Ticino to the Borromeo Gulf.
