What to see in Marano sul Panaro

On the left of the Panaro, about 30 km from Modena, the Apennines gate between the plain and the mountains: the center grown around the rock and the church preserves the seventeenth-century Mulino Montecuccoli with the Museum of ecology and natural history, while along the river stretch the River Park and the Colombarone, and in the hamlets the Romanesque parish of Denzano and the walled village of Festà.

Museo civico di ecologia e storia naturale

Museums · Science and university

Inaugurated in 1994 and managed by the CISNIAR (Italian Centre for Artificial Nest Studies): seven rooms, the first on geology, paleontology and ecology, the others with panels, aquaterrariums and dioramas that reconstruct the environments from the Apennines to the Modena plain. Mineral, paleontological and zoological collections with thousands of specimens from all over Europe, the herbarium Branchetti and sections of mycology and lichenology; laboratory with microscopes and naturalistic library.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti 28, Marano sul Panaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it

Mulino Montecuccoli e Museo delle Energie

Museums · Themed

The seventeenth-century mill of the historic centre, with a restored arched porch, still active and crossed by the Montecuccoli channel that unites Marano to Vignola; then became the central ENEL of the village, houses the Museum of Energies, with ancient machinery of the first electric production and materials on canals, mills and opifici of Panaro and Samoggia, and the Documentation Center .

  • Where Centro storico, Marano sul Panaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · cultura.gov.it

Resti del castello medievale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the top of the old village, towards the hill, there are still walls and a tower of the fortress around which Marano grew, passed between Este, Pico, Rangoni, Pio di Carpi and Montecuccoli.

  • Where Paese vecchio, Marano sul Panaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

A few meters from the center, frequented by thousands of visitors a year, with bar, barbecue area and games; continues with walking and cycling routes and is crossed by the Nature Route that connects Modena to Casona.

Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta a Denzano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the small village of Denzano, about 7 km: the apse of the parish is considered one of the best examples of Romanesque of the Modena Apennines; next remains the square tower of the ancient castle.
