What to see in Caltanissetta

The capital of the zolfare in the heart of Sicily: the cathedral of Santa Maria la Nova with the frescoes of Borremans, the Norman Santo Spirito, the ruins of the Castle of Pietrarossa, the Tritone Fountain, the archaeological and mineralogical museums and the Vare of Holy Thursday.

9 places to visit in Caltanissetta, in the province of Caltanissetta (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale di Santa Maria la Nova

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Impressive baroque cathedral behind the Triton Fountain, with the trompe frescoes by William Borremans.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Caltanissetta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Chiesa di Santo Spirito

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built by Ruggero the Norman in 1095 on a fortified Arab farmhouse: a single nave and three apses, with a frescoed pantocrator Christ, a Norman baptismal font and a 12th century pond glass.

  • Where Santo Spirito, Caltanissetta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Castello di Pietrarossa

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Castle of Women, from the Arab Qal'at an-Nisa: towers on a cliff overlooking the valley, destroyed by the earthquake of 1567; the ruins dominate the city.

  • Where Caltanissetta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Fontana del Tritone

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Created in 1956 by Michele Tripisciano and Michele Averna: a bronze horse tamed by a triton and challenged by two sea monsters.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Caltanissetta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info
