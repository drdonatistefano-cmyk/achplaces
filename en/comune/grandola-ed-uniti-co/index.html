What to see in Grandola ed Uniti

Comune Behind Menaggio, in the Val Sanagra: in Codogna the eighteenth-century Villa Camozzi houses the town hall and the Ethnographic and Naturalistic Museum Val Sanagra, from which starts the path to the Rogolone, monumental oak protected since 1922 and symbol of the Val Sanagra Park.

Museo etnografico e naturalistico Val Sanagra e Villa Camozzi

Museums · Themed

It is located in Codogna in the eighteenth-century Villa Camozzi, today also town hall. Ecomuseum and visitor centre with sections of fauna, flora, paleontology and ethnography and a section dedicated to Val Sanagra (orography, paths, ecology). From May to September the second and last Sunday of the month, from October to April the last Sunday, 10-12.30 and 14-17.30; also indicated the opening by reservation.

  • Where Piazza Camozzi 2, Codogna, Grandola ed Uniti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: isprambiente.gov.it · in-lombardia.it

Il Rogolone e il Rogolino

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

In the forest of Grandola, the Rogolone is an oak tree with a trunk of almost 8 meters in circumference and over 22 meters in height, already remembered by documents of the sixteenth century; in 1922 it was protected as a natural monument, among the first in Italy, and is the symbol of the Park Val Sanagra. Next to it grows the Rogolino, planted in 1820. You can reach it with path no. 38 from Codogna, starting from Villa Camozzi, in about half an hour between drywalls and old rural buildings.

  • Where Sentiero n. 38 da Villa Camozzi, Codogna, Grandola ed Uniti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Parco Val Sanagra

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Valle delle Alpi Lepontine between 300 and 2,000 meters above Menaggio, of which the Rogolone is the symbol. Along the Sanagra torrent you will meet the Mill of the Valley, the Carliseppi Mill with the donkey-back bridge, the Old Chioderia, the Fornace Galli where bricks and tiles were produced and the monolith of the Sass Corbée. The main ring from Menaggio to Loveno, Piamuro and Barna measures about 10 km (about 3 and a half hours, medium difficulty).

  • Where Val Sanagra, accesso da Piamuro o da Codogna, Grandola ed Uniti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
