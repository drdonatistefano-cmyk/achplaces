What to see in Fabriano

The city of paper, in the Anconetian hinterland, UNESCO Creative City since 2013 for crafts: the Museum of Paper and Filigree in the former Dominican convent, Piazza del Comune with the Fontana Sturinalto and the Pinacoteca Molajoli.

12 places to visit in Fabriano, in the province of Ancona (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Fabriano in the guide with map and filters

Museo della Carta e della Filigrana

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the ancient convent of the Dominicans he tells more than seven hundred years of paper tradition: a medieval gully rebuilt and working, the phases of hand-working, a section on the filigree and demonstrations of the master papermakers, with workshops to make their own sheet.

  • Where Largo Fratelli Spacca 2, Fabriano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · eventi.turismo.marche.it

Piazza del Comune e Fontana Sturinalto

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The central square with the Palazzo del Podestà in the middle of the thirteenth century, to merlons with swallow tail, and the Fontana Sturinalto of the end of the thirteenth century, reduced version of the fountain of Perugia; around the Palazzo Comunale and the Loggia of San Francesco.

  • Where Piazza del Comune, Fabriano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it
