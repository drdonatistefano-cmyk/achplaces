What to see in Montenerodomo

Country of the National Park of Maiella linked to the family of Benedetto Croce, known above all for the archaeological park of Iuvanum, Sannite city and then Roman town of Carricini on a plateau at 972 meters, with hole, basilica, theatre and acropolis of the temples.

Parco archeologico di Iuvanum

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On a plateau at 972 meters dominated by Maiella, with a view to the sea, the site is frequented at least from the IV-III century BC; the visible structure dates back to the middle of the I century BC, after the birth of the Carricini municipality. Damaged by the earthquake of 354 and restored, the city remained inhabited at least until the sixth century. The excavations, begun in the 1940s, brought to light the acropolis with two temples and a sacellus, the theatre, the baths at the ancient source and the paved forum with the basilica julio-claudia, the tabernacles and the epigraph in bronze letters of the patron Ernenni Capitone and his heir Erennia Proiecta. The area is freely accessible.

  • Where Località Santa Maria del Palazzo, Montenerodomo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.parcomajella.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sabapchpe.cultura.gov.it · parcomajella.it

Museo archeologico di Iuvanum

Museums · Archaeological

On the southern side of the hill of the temples, exhibits a selection of the finds of the city and the territory and tells the excavations and who lived in the place, with a section on the history and transformation of the landscape. According to the MiC card is open in July on Saturday and Sunday, from August to the first week of September every day except Monday, in the rest of the year only by reservation.

Out of season open only by reservation (MiC card)

Resti dell'abbazia di Santa Maria in Palazzo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Benedictine monastery of Santa Maria di Palazzo, then Cistercian and later active pastoral church until the fifteenth century, was built on the acropolis of Iuvanum, above the oldest temples.

  • Where Località Santa Maria del Palazzo, Montenerodomo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.parcomajella.it

Sources: parcomajella.it · cultura.gov.it

Centro storico, chiesa di San Martino e Palazzo Croce

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the heart of the village, in Piazza Benedetto Croce, the church of San Martino houses the small altar of San Fedele, celebrated on April 24; not far away Palazzo Croce, residence of the family of Benedetto Croce, and the church of San Vito in Piazza De Thomasis.

  • Where Piazza Benedetto Croce, Montenerodomo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.parcomajella.it

Sources: parcomajella.it

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint
