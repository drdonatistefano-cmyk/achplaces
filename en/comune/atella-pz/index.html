What to see in Atella

Cittadina del Vulture-Melfese on the road between Lagopesole and Melfi: the Angevin Tower, the remaining remains of the castle destroyed by the earthquake of 1694, the cathedral of Santa Maria in Nives and the monastic complexes of Santa Maria degli Angeli and Santo Spirito.

4 places to visit in Atella, in the province of Potenza (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre angioina (Castello angioino)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The surviving mast of the Angevin fortress that resisted the sieges of 1361 and 1496 and was destroyed by the earthquake of 1694: originally square plan, four corner towers and moat; the tower recalls the Castel Nuovo of Naples.

  • Where Via IV Novembre, Atella
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.atella.pz.it

Sources: comune.atella.pz.it

Duomo di Santa Maria in Nives

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Mother church with fourteenth-century façade crowned with hanging arches and portal without comparison in the region, with a small bezel between sun and moon; a single nave interior of the eighteenth century, rebuilt after the earthquake of 1694.

  • Where Piazza Gramsci, Atella
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website comune.atella.pz.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: comune.atella.pz.it

Convento di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Franciscan monastery of 1463 with belt and three towers, church with two naves and cloister with porch with upper loggia; from 1706 entrusted to the Reformed Fathers.

  • Where Atella
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website comune.atella.pz.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: comune.atella.pz.it

Monastero di Santo Spirito e chiesa di San Benedetto

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Monastery of Benedictines of the fifteenth century, suppressed after the Unity, with bifora fourteenth century and coat of arms Angevin; in the church of San Benedetto valuable paintings and, walled in the municipal seat, the fourteenth-century portal of the disappeared church of San Francesco della Scarpa.

  • Where Via Giustino Fortunato 50, Atella

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
