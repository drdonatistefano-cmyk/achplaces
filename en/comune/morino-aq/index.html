What to see in Morino

Village of the province of L'Aquila on the edge of the Zompo il Schioppo Nature Reserve, between beech and chestnut woods, with a waterfall that descends for 80 meters from a karst spring; in the reserve there are paths and an ecomuseum.

Riserva naturale Zompo lo Schioppo e cascata

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Reserve at the village gates, made of beech and chestnut woods, peaks and streams. The waterfall comes from a karst spring near the summit of the reliefs and makes a jump of 80 meters, indicated as the highest natural jump of the Apennines; underground open caves, wells and sinkholes. You visit along hiking trails.

  • Where Morino (AQ)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Ecomuseo della riserva naturale Zompo Lo Schioppo

Museums · Themed

Multipurpose structure that combines a territorial museum on the naturalistic and historical-anthropological aspects of the place and a visitor center with spaces for teaching and research. The route includes two workshops with reconstructions of terrariums and aquariums. Hours (MiC card): Monday-Friday 9:30-17:30, Saturday and Sunday 9:30-12:30, without reservation.

  • Where Via delle Ferriere 10, Morino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
