What to see in Carignano

Town of the Torinese to the south of the capital, with the eighteenth-century cathedral designed by Benedetto Alfieri, the sanctuary of the Valinotto by Bernardo Antonio Vittone (1738), the church of the Madonna delle Grazie and a historic center of piazzas and noble palaces such as the House of the Grand Bastard Renato of Savoia.

7 places to visit in Carignano, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo dei Santi Giovanni Battista e Remigio

Places of worship · Cathedrals

18th-century cathedral designed by Benedetto Alfieri, originally frescoed by Paolo Gaidano, overlooking St. John's Square, the center of the historic centre. The parishyard and the central door have a fixed slide for wheelchairs.

  • Where Piazza San Giovanni, Carignano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.carignano.to.it

Santuario del Valinotto

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

The Church of the Visitation was commissioned in 1738 by banker Antonio Fico to Bernardo Antonio Vittone for the peasants of his fund, placing it on a pre-existing building. The frescoes are attributed to Pier Francesco Guala; in the sacristy remains the sixteenth-century fresco of the Madonna del latte, attributed to a painter near the Macrino. The original altarpiece and the palace attributed to Piffetti are kept at the Opera Pia Faccio-Frichieri. Visitable Saturday and Sunday 10:30-12:00 and 14:30-17:00; guided tours by reservation at least two weeks in advance.

  • Where Località Valinotto, strada Virle, Carignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · italia.it · comune.carignano.to.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Italia.it indicates it among the three main testimonies of the past of Carignano, together with the cathedral and the sanctuary of Valinotto. It is officiated church, with weekday and festive masses.

  • Where Piazzetta San Giovanni Bosco, Carignano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.carignano.to.it

Centro storico e Casa del Gran Bastardo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center revolves around St. John's Square, with the ancient Community Palace (the salon is visited only by permission), the medieval Provana del Sabbione Palace, the Borgovecchio Street and historical squares such as Carlo Alberto Square, Savoia Square and Liberation Square, called Piazza dell'Angelo. Among the houses stands the House of the Grand Bastardo Renato of Savoia, with terracotta ornaments and painting and sculptural decorations. The noble palaces cannot be visited inside.

  • Where Piazza San Giovanni, Carignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.carignano.to.it

Torre civica, Fortilizio di Po e chiese delle confraternite

History and architecture · Towers

The civic tower, the area of the Fortilizio di Po and the churches of Our Lady of Mercy (of the Black Battuti), the Holy Spirit (formerly of the White Battuti, in Via Vittorio Veneto 14) and of St. Joseph, today of the Romanian Orthodox parish, are accessible only with guided tour, to be booked at least two weeks before.

  • Where Via Vittorio Veneto 14 (Spirito Santo), Carignano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.carignano.to.it · italia.it

Area protetta del Po e campagna

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Protected natural area along the Po. The municipality suggests trails in the countryside towards the Po Park, the Bosco for the City and the villages La Gorra, Campagnino and Brillante.

  • Where Lungo il Po, Carignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.carignano.to.it · italia.it

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry
