What to see in Castelnuovo Scrivia

Borgo della piania dortona, home of the novelliere Matteo Bandello, with medieval and fifteenth-century houses decorated in terracotta; the collegiate of Saints Peter and Paul preserves the Romanesque portal signed by Magister Albertus in 1183, the Last Supper by Alessandro Berri and a fresco of the fifteenth-century Madonna della Misericordia.

Collegiata dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Pieve documented since 1184, built between 1165 and 1183 and assumed its current appearance in 1588, probably on a project by Pellegrino Tibaldi; in 1621 it had the title of distinguished collegiate. It has five aisles with the central one on ten large granite columns and facade never completed; the portal of 1183, signed by the Magister Albertus, has in the bezel Samson that rifts the jaws of the lion and David that saves the flock, with capitals to grifi and scenes of the seasons. Inside paintings by Pietro Grassi, Tirsi Capitini and Francesco Mensi, the Last Supper by Alessandro Berri and, in counterface, the Madonna of Mercy of the fifteenth century attributed to Quirico Boxilio da Tortona.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II 38, Castelnuovo Scrivia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.castelnuovoscrivia.al.it

Centro storico, porte e case medievali

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the center there are medieval houses with turrets and facades decorated in terracotta and fifteenth-century houses in Via Fornasari and Via Marguati; at the entrance of the road to Molino dei Torti survives an arch of the seventeenth century, one of the gates of the walls (Zibide, Molina, Strad'Alzano, Gualdonasce and Tavernelle). In Piazza Vittorio Veneto there is the birthplace of Matteo Maria Bandello.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Veneto, Castelnuovo Scrivia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.castelnuovoscrivia.al.it
