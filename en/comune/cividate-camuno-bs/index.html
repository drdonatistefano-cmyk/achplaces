What to see in Cividate Camuno

The capital of the Roman Camonica Valley after the conquest of 16 BC, preserves the archaeological park of the theatre and amphitheatre and, in the historic center, the National Archaeological Museum with the statue of Minerva from the shrine of Breno.

Museo archeologico nazionale della Valle Camonica

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Opened in 1981 and since 11 June 2021 in a new location in the historic center, it tells in eight sections the Roman Camonica Valley. Among the main pieces the Roman marble copy of the Greek Minerva of the 5th century BC from the sanctuary of Breno, two mosaics of the thermal baths (I-II century A.D.), a larch door from Pescarzo di Capo di Ponte (end II-early I century BC), among the best preserved of the alpine arch, and frescoes with water birds from the domus of Via Palazzo. Closed Monday and Tuesday; only electronic payments.

  • Where Piazzale Giacomini 2, Cividate Camuno
  • Entry €6, reduced €2 (18-25 years EU); cumulative 7 days with Naquane and MUTRE €12
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museilombardia.cultura.gov.it

Sources: museilombardia.cultura.gov.it · comune.cividate-camuno.bs.it

Parco archeologico del Teatro e dell'Anfiteatro

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Inaugurated in 2003: the theatre was built after the middle of the first century A.D. terracing the hill, the amphitheatre a few decades later on an embankment. The outer walls of the amphitheatre are entirely discovered, the theatre is visible about a third; in the upper part runs a long aqueduct that supplied both. Illustrative panels along the route; management Pro Loco Cividatese, closed on Monday.

  • Where Via Mosè Tovini 1, Cividate Camuno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cividate-camuno.bs.it

Sources: comune.cividate-camuno.bs.it
