What to see in Sardara

Country of the thermal waters on the plain of Campidano, Orange Flag of Touring: the nuragic sanctuary of Sant'Anastasia with the sacred well, the museum Villa Abbas, the churches of San Gregorio and Assunta and the ruins of the castle of Monreale.

7 places to visit in Sardara, in the province of Sud Sardegna (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario nuragico e pozzo sacro di Sant'Anastasia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Well temple of the late Bronze Age in blocks of basalt and limestone: atrium with seats, 12 steps staircase and circular chamber in tholos; next to a second well, a large fence and huts, and in the church of St. Anastasia, rebuilt in the fifteenth century, another nuragic well.

  • Where Sardara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo archeologico Villa Abbas

Museums · Archaeological

It preserves the votive material of St. Anastasia and other nuragic sites in the area, including an altar shaped like nuraghes and objects in bronze and terracotta.

  • Where Piazza Libertà, Sardara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it · corporate.coopculture.it

Chiesa di San Gregorio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Former parish church of the beginning of the fourteenth century, among the most important churches in Sardinia, in the passage from Romanesque to Gothic.

  • Where Centro storico, Sardara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Parrocchiale della Beata Vergine Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque-Gothic church with carved reliefs, a reed organ of 1758 and the retablo of Our Lady of Itria.

  • Where Centro storico, Sardara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Castello di Monreale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hills south of the village, attested in 1309 and linked to the judges of Arborea Mariano IV and Eleonora: the walls and the remains of the medieval village remain.

  • Where Sardara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Terme di Sardara e Santa Maria de is Acuas

Local life and traditions · Spas

About two kilometers from the village, the waters flow between 50 and 68 degrees where the Roman center of Aquae Neapolitanae stood; next to the sanctuary of Santa Maria de is Acuas, a pilgrimage destination in mid-September.

  • Where Sardara
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Encircled on a spur overlooking the Campidano, with paved roads, stone houses with portals and manor houses like the seventeenth-century Casa Pilloni.

  • Where Sardara
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
