What to see in Rodello

Country of the Langhe recognizable by the two eighteenth-century bell towers, that of the parish church of San Lorenzo and that of the church of the Immaculate Conception, which houses the Museum of contemporary art dedicated to the painter and sculptor Daedalo Montali; from the village the look ranges on Alba, the plain of Tanaro and, in the distance, the Alps.

Chiesa dell'Immacolata Concezione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was built at his expense in 1749 by the abbot Michele Falletti, of the family that had the lordship of Rodello since the end of the sixteenth century. Baroque, with neoclassical-inspired façade, has an interior with Greek cross with three altars surmounted by eighteenth-century shovels, stuccoes and Corinthian capitals attributed to the so-called "maestro" of 1749. Today it hosts the Museum of Contemporary Art "Dedalo Montali."

Museo d'arte contemporanea «Dedalo Montali»

Museums · Modern art

Museum of Modern and Religious Art born at the will of Daedalo Montali (Cagliari 1909 - Rodello 2001), painter, sculptor and poet, on the initiative of the widow Taja Satta Montali and the canon Mario Battaglino. Paintings and sculptures are ordained for periods and themes, from the pre-war to the end of the twentieth century, in two places: the halls of the church of the Immaculate Conception and the Residence. For visits: Municipality, tel. 0173 617107.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Started in 1766 on a project by Carlo Francesco Rangone di Montelupo, instead of the old church and on the site of the ancient castle of Falletti; bell tower of 1788. Inside the altar of the Rosary of the stuccoer Capia (1786), the altar of Sant'Antonio di Padova (1787), the altarpiece of the presbytery of Sante Conti (1891) and the decoration of 1931-1932, with friezes by Carlo Frascaroli and figures by Lorenzo Laiolo.
