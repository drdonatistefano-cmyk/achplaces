What to see in Rapino

Country of the Majella National Park, historic center of the hand-painted ceramics of Abruzzo, told in the museum of the Convent of Sant'Antonio; in its territory the Grotto of the Colle, the main sanctuary of the Marrucini where the bronze was found, the goddess of Rapino, and the sanctuary of the Madonna of Carpineto, which was the 8th of May of the procession of the Virgins.

Museo della Ceramica (Pinacoteca e centro documentale della ceramica)

Museums · Themed

Opened in December 2003 in the former Convent of Sant'Antonio, complex of about 1645, to enhance the art that has made known Rapino in the world: exhibits about 180 ceramics of authors, eras and different styles, including works of the Maiolic masters Bozzelli, Vitacolumna and Bontempo, in part as permanent exhibition of the Municipality and in part rotating in temporary exhibitions, with multimedia posts. The MiC card indicates opening Saturday, Sunday and holidays (winter 10-13 and 15-19, summer 16-20), but is updated to 2020: times to be confirmed (tel. 0871 800642).

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Grotta del Colle

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Natural cave on the north-eastern side of the Majella, frequented by the Middle and Upper Paleolithic until the high Middle Ages; at the entrance remain the remains of a church perhaps risen on an Italian temple. From the place of worship are the bronze statue of the ...Dea of Rapino , today at the National Archaeological Museum La Civitella of Chieti, and Tabula Rapinensis, lamina with a sacred law in Marrucino dialect which appoints the ...Touta Marouca , and the cult of Ceria Iovia: for this reason it is considered the main sanctuary of the Marrucini.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.parcomajella.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.rapino.ch.it · parcomajella.it

Piana della Civita

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On a hill above the Rivosecco valley, a few kilometers from the village, an Italian settlement active from the VII to the II-III century BC: about six hectares surrounded by a high dry wall in some places up to three meters, with polygonal walls, various doors, a cistern excavated in the rock, foundations of huts and terraces; at the foot of the slope a necropolis, damaged by the quarries. It has been proposed as the possible Tarincria "arce."

  • Where Piana della Civita, Rapino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.parcomajella.it

Sources: comune.rapino.ch.it · parcomajella.it

Santuario della Madonna del Carpineto

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Rural shrine was built, according to legend, where around 1200 the Virgin appeared to a shepherd on a hornbeam: the wooden statue found did not allow itself to be brought into the church of the village, becoming too heavy. On May 8th, from the church of San Lorenzo comes the procession of the Virgins, girls in silk tunic and crown of flowers, ritual born after the drought of 1794.

  • Where Santuario di Santa Maria di Carpineto, Rapino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.parcomajella.it

Sources: parcomajella.it
