What to see in Isola di Capo Rizzuto

Promontory on the Ionian coast, the ancient Byzantine Asylos: the Aragonese fortress of Le Castella on an islet joined to the coast by a thin tongue of land and the Marine Protected Area Capo Rizzuto, one of the largest in Italy, with ochre sandy beaches.

Fortezza aragonese di Le Castella

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On an islet at the eastern end of the Gulf of Squillace, joined to the coast by a thin tongue of land: remains of walls of the fourth century BC, cylindrical tower of the thirteenth century, Aragonese works concluded in 1487 and angular bastions added by Andrea Carafa between 1510 and 1526. Here, during an Ottoman raid, the future Uluç Ali Pasha was kidnapped.

  • Where Piazzale Castello, Le Castella, Isola di Capo Rizzuto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · comune.isoladicaporizzuto.kr.it

Area marina protetta Capo Rizzuto

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established in 1991, almost 15,000 hectares of sea in front of the headlands of Capo Colonna, Capo Cimiti, Capo Rizzuto and Le Castella, with low and sandy beaches and few rocky stretches; in the backdrops groupers, mullet, chestnuts and parrot fishes. Only guided and regulated tours are allowed in Capo Colonna and Capo Cimiti.

  • Where Isola di Capo Rizzuto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website riservamarinacaporizzuto.it

Sources: riservamarinacaporizzuto.it · italia.it

Acquario dell'Area marina protetta (CEAM)

Museums · Science and university

Marine education center with 22 tanks for about 20,000 liters that recreate the habitats of the protected sea: posidonia, starfish, hedgehogs, octopus, groupers, moray eels and jellyfish, with tactile tub, children's workshop and multimedia room.

  • Where Piazza Santuario, Capo Rizzuto, Isola di Capo Rizzuto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website riservamarinacaporizzuto.it

Sources: riservamarinacaporizzuto.it · italia.it

Spiaggia di Le Castella

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small beaches with shallow and sandy seabeds around the Aragonese castle, suitable for children, from which the boats for excursions to the sea depart; italia.it mentions it among the Blue Flags of Calabria.

  • Entry Free
