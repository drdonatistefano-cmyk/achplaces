What to see in Cerano d'Intelvi

Formed from the hamlets of Cerano, Veglio and Giuslino on the slopes of the conical mountain San Zeno, Cerano d'Intelvi houses the only museum dedicated to stucco and intelvese scagliola, the church of San Tomaso from the Romanesque bell tower and, at the top of the mountain, the hermitage of San Zeno.

Museo dello Stucco e della Scagliola Intelvese

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Permanent exhibition established in 1989 in a wing of the Palazzo Perlasca-Pinchetti (1608), today of the Spalla-Gandola family, of which it houses the collections of stuccoes and chess in three exhibition sections. It is the only museum entirely dedicated to this sector, the art that has made famous the masters of the valley. Open in July and August on Saturdays and holidays 15.30-18; from May to October guided tours for small groups by reservation (tel. 333 1223598, museum.scagliolaintelvese@gmail.com).

  • Where Palazzo Perlasca-Pinchetti, Cerano d'Intelvi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: valleintelviturismo.it · in-lombardia.it

Chiesa di San Tomaso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Of medieval origins (XII-XIII century) and renovated from the sixteenth century, it is known for its Romanesque bell tower. Inside a six-seventh-century altarpiece with the Unbelief of St. Thomas and stucco statues attributed to the Giani.

  • Where Via Roma, Cerano d'Intelvi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: valleintelviturismo.it · in-lombardia.it

Chiesa dei Santi Quirico e Giulitta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Isolated outside the village of Veglio, it is a seventeenth-century building with stuccoes and scagliola that preserves as a side chapel the Romanesque apse of the 11th-XII century, with medieval frescoes; of value the chapel of the Madonna delle Grazie. It meets along the 11 km route between Dizzasco, Cerano and Casasco d'Intelvi.

  • Where Fraz. Veglio, Cerano d'Intelvi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: valleintelviturismo.it · in-lombardia.it

Eremo di San Zeno

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Church built before the year 1000 in panoramic position on the lake and the valley, on top of Mount San Zeno; collapsed in 1956 for a lightning, it has been rebuilt since 1996. The ascent is marked by the stations of the Via Crucis made by the Alps with railway sleepers; inside a copy of the Corona Ferrea.

  • Where Monte San Zeno, Cerano d'Intelvi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: valleintelviturismo.it · in-lombardia.it

Museo del Latte

Museums · Themed

Set up in the ancient dairy farm of Cerano, it collects the historical tools of milk processing (churches, conga, wrap and a culdera) and offers a film of the eighties on traditional crafts.

  • Where Antica Latteria, Cerano d'Intelvi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: valleintelviturismo.it · in-lombardia.it

Parco delle Fiabe

Nature and landscape · City parks

Themed area in the woods designed to be inclusive and accessible, with statues inspired by classical fairy tales (from the Little Prince to the Little Mermaid) and the Sword in the rock, braille panels and events and shows.

  • Where Cerano d'Intelvi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it
