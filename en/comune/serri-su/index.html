What to see in Serri

Small agropastoral village about 600 meters on the edge of the Jar that gives it its name, between Sarcidano and Trexenta: on the plateau stands the nuragic sanctuary of Santa Vittoria, one of the major of Sardinia, and in the village the Romanesque parish church of San Basilio Magno.

Santuario nuragico di Santa Vittoria

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Site of about three hectares on the western edge of the Giara di Serri, discovered at the beginning of the twentieth century, with Etruscan, Phoenician and Cypriot objects that testify to the exchanges of the Nuragic age; it was frequented also in Punic, Roman and Byzantine age. Four groups of structures: the sacred area with the well-shaped temple, the paved atrium, the two-altar hypetral temple and the huts of the priest and of the chief; the large courtyard of the feasts with porches, seats, common kitchen and market; the enclosure of the double betilus; the courtyard of the supplications and the curia with about 50 seats, perhaps seat of the assembly of the leaders. The nuraghe bronzes and models found in the excavations are at the National Archaeological Museum of Cagliari. On the site the church of Santa Maria della Vittoria, attested in the seventh century and rebuilt by the victorious monks in the XI-XII century, feast on 11 September. Open every day 9-17.

Giara di Serri

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The plateau is a natural fortress, covered with oaks, oaks and centuries-old oaks and Mediterranean maquis, suitable for walks, bikes and horse riding; the view ranges from Gennargentu to the hills of Marmilla up to the south-western coast of the island.

  • Where Giara di Serri, Serri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Parrocchiale di San Basilio Magno

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque Pisa church with Byzantine influences, built around 1100; inside a major altar in marble of the eighteenth century and three wooden altars of the seventeenth century. Patron Festival at the end of August.

  • Where Centro storico, Serri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Centro storico, Sant'Antonio Abate e monte granatico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Ancient rural houses with large courtyards and arched portals; the church of Sant'Antonio Abate (1770) rises next to the former Granatic mountain, now a cultural centre, and in its square in mid-January a large bonfire is lit.

  • Where Centro storico, Serri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
