What to see in Sinnai

Common at the gates of Cagliari with a historic center of campidanesi houses, the parish Gothic-Aragonese of Santa Barbara and the Civic Archaeological and Art Gallery Museum; the territory goes up to the forest of the Seven Brothers and goes down to the sea with the coastal hamlet of Solanas.

MuA - Museo civico archeologico e pinacoteca

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the former House of the Colletta, once the Cavalleggeri barracks: the archaeological section was born from excavations and researches since 1994 and documents the territory from the recent Neolithic to the Middle Ages, with the findings of the nuraghe Santa Itroxia, Bruncu Mogumu and Papalinu. The most important piece is a punic terracotta statue of the god Bes; a fragment of the early medieval epigraphic mentions Saint Saturnino. The art gallery exhibits paintings by artists from the south-eastern Campidano from the 16th century to the present day.

  • Where Via Colletta 20, Sinnai
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · comune.sinnai.ca.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Parrocchiale di Santa Barbara e museo d'arte

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church on a hill in Gothic-Aragonese style, with subsequent interventions, altars, wooden statues and paintings; the bell tower was rebuilt between 1868 and 1870. The museum of art of the parish collects altars and baptismal sources in marble, paintings, wooden and silver statues.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Centro storico e Palazzo civico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Narrow streets and campidanese houses with internal courtyards and large wooden portals; among the historic residences Casa Cocco, Casa Olla and Casa Casella. The Municipal Palace, dating back to 1860, houses the municipal library and the historical archive.

Bruncu Mogumu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Situated on the hill north of Sinnai with traces from the Nuragic Age onwards, including a nuragic temple; from here are finds exposed to the civic museum, like the Punic statue of the god Bes. Wide view of the Gulf of Cagliari and Campidano.

Oasi dei Sette Fratelli e Monte Genis

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

About ten thousand hectares among nine municipalities, Sinnai included, with the state forest of the Seven Brothers and that of Monte Genis; the seven peaks reach the thousand meters and the highest is the Serpeddi (1067 m). From Campuomu, with the forest station Umberto Noci, a path climbs to Punta Sa Ceraxa through the Arch of the Angel, a natural monument in pink granite. Lecci, corks, arbutus; deer, mouflons, royal eagles and pilgrim hawks. In Campuomu there is also the Sardinian deer museum.

  • Where Campuomu, SS 125 al passo S'Arcu 'e Tidu
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Spiaggia di Solanas

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Coastal fraction about 36 km from the village, on the Gulf of Angels: about a kilometer of golden sand between Cape Boi and a rocky tip, with a dune, blue sea to emerald green and low seabed near shore that then descends quickly; windy, suitable for windsurfing and kite. A path on the promontory leads to the Spanish tower of Capo Boi (120 m), with views of Villasimius, Capo Carbonara and the island of Cavoli. Parking, dining and rental.

  • Where Solanas, Sinnai (SP 17)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
