What to see in Ponza

The largest island of the Pontine archipelago, reachable by ferry from Formia, Terracina, San Felice Circeo and Anzio: Bourbon port, Grotte di Pilate and Cisterna della Dragonara of Roman age, the coves of Feola and Frontone, the path of Monte Guardia and, by boat, Palmarola and Zannone.

15 places to visit in Ponza, in the province of Latina (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Porto borbonico e chiesa della SS. Trinità

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The semicircular port was rebuilt on a Roman plant designed by Antonio Winspeare and Francesco Carpi: works since 1772, operating in April 1779 with the ignition of the lantern on the pier. The neoclassical church of the SS. Trinity, with circular plan with dome, was built since 1775 in three years. At the end of the harbour basin there is a mitreum with zodiacal decorations of the III-IV century AD.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Cisterna romana della Dragonara

History and architecture · Archaeology

One of the three large cisterns built by the Romans against the scarcity of water, preserved almost intact, with underground corridors that cross the aisles and floors and walls in stone. Guided tours only: June-August at 11:00, 12:15, 18:00 and 19:15, September at 11:00 and 17:00, other times by reservation 24 hours in advance (Pro Loco, tel. 0771 80031).

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Grotte di Pilato

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

To the south of the port, under the cemetery, a Roman complex dug into the rock and partly below sea level, probably used to raise moray eels; galleries connect the tanks and a staircase led to a villa of Augustus. In the main cave a white marble statue, probably Apollo or Dionysus, recovered with a tufa altar and lamp.

  • Where Sud del porto, sotto il cimitero, Ponza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Punta della Madonna: Torre, cimitero e faro

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The tower, commissioned by Alfonso d'Aragona and mentioned in 1542, was enlarged to four floors by the Bourbons and damaged in 1813; today it is private and used as a hotel. The cemetery has the nucleus of 1772 around the chapel of Santa Maria della Salvazione and the Sepulchre Tricoli (1837); the lighthouse is from 1858 and is still in operation.

  • Where Punta della Madonna, Ponza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ponza.lt.it · italia.it

Santa Maria: monastero e chiesa di San Giuseppe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Benedictine complex of Santa Maria already existed around 540, when St. Silverius was buried there; in the thirteenth century the Cistercians sent by Innocent III settled there and in the fifteenth century the monastery was transferred to Formia. The church of St. Joseph was built in 1828 and consecrated in 1895.

  • Where Santa Maria, Ponza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ponza.lt.it · italia.it

Le Forna: chiesa dell'Assunta e case-grotta

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village was born with Bourbon colonization: the first 27 families arrived in 1772 from Torre del Greco, digging the typical cave houses; the church of the Assunta was built from 1772, with the first parish priest in 1781 and the chapel of Santa Filomena added in 1846.

  • Where Le Forna, Ponza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Cala Feola e piscine naturali

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

To the north of the island, Cala Feola has small shallow natural pools; with Cala dell'Acqua and Capo Bianco it is one of the most photographed views of Ponza.

Common: access to Cala Feola and natural pools regulated by seasonal reopening orders (ORD. 80, 83, 87, 109/2025)

Spiaggia di Frontone

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Reachable with small boats, it is very popular for the evening aperitif; near are the remains of a Roman necropolis and the Fort of Frontone, with accommodation, kitchen and cistern dug into the rock.

Common: ban on access to the cliff breakwaters (ORD. 34/2021)

Chiaia di Luna

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Beach of crescent pebbles under crags of white tuff up to 100 meters high, used as a natural port by Greeks and Romans, who dug a tunnel lit by skylights to connect it inside. After repeated landslides the tunnel and the beach have been closed: today you can only admire from the sea or from above, from the path of the Fieno.

Italia.it: tunnel and beach closed for landslides, visible only from the sea at a safe distance

  • Where Versante occidentale, Ponza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.ponza.lt.it

Sentiero del Monte Guardia

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Monte La Guardia (283 m) is the highest peak of the archipelago: the itinerary starts from the rock of Frisio and passes through Giancos, Sant'Antonio and the Dragonara up to the top in about 3 hours (average difficulty, difference in altitude about 500 m), with the unvisitable ruins of the Old traffic light and the view of the Lighthouse of the Guardia and Palmarola; in spring spontaneous orchids.

  • Where Monte Guardia, Ponza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ponza.lt.it · italia.it

Giardino Botanico Ponziano

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

On Mount Guardia, around a neoclassical building, founded in 1980: a circular path with over 70 native species and some rare orchids at risk of extinction.

  • Where Monte Guardia, Ponza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Giro in barca, faraglioni e immersioni

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

The tour of the island by boat touches coves and tips like Cala Felce, Cala del Core, Cala d'Inferno, Punta Capo Bianco (where Fellini shot scenes of the Satyricon), Punta Incenso and the faraglioni of Lucia Rosa and Calzone Muto; the seabed are known for diving and underwater photography, with Punta Papa the wreck of a ship of the Second World War.

  • Where Partenze dal porto di Ponza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Isola di Palmarola

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Uninhabited, with only one landing place in Cala di San Silverio, cliffs and clear waters; nature reserve and marine protected area, with houses dug in the rock and a restaurant on the beach.

  • Where Palmarola, Ponza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Isola di Zannone

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Uninhabited, since 1979 in the National Park of the Circeo, with landing at Punta del Varo; it is a stop of migratory birds and preserves the remains of the convent of Santo Spirito di Zannone.

Museo Comunale e Mostra permanente del Colore

Museums · City history

In the halls of the Municipal Museum of Ponza Porto is set up the Permanent Exhibition of Color, with eight panels of 4 x 1.40 meters on the chromatic reliefs of the three historic centers: Ponza Porto, Santa Maria and Le Forna; there is also a photo library.

MiC card (updated 2020) indicates "closed" every day: check (tel. 0771 80108)
