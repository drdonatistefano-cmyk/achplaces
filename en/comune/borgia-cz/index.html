What to see in Borgia

Municipality between hill and Gulf of Squillace: in Roccelletta, in an olive grove, the Museum and National Archaeological Park of Scolacium with the remains of the Roman colony and the Norman basilica of Santa Maria della Roccella; at the top the historical center rebuilt after the earthquake of the eighteenth century.

Parco archeologico nazionale di Scolacium

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

In an olive grove the remains of the Roman colony of Scolacium, heir of the Greek Skylletion (of which there is nothing left): forum, theatre, amphitheatre, paved roads, aqueducts, mausoleum and a necropolis with sacred area, a basilica and a spa. In summer the park hosts concerts and cultural events.

  • Where Via Scylletion, Roccelletta, Borgia
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · ponculturaesviluppo.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo archeologico di Scolacium

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In a 19th-century rural building on two floors of the Barons Mazza complex, renovated as a museum, exhibits the excavations from prehistoric to Middle Ages: everyday objects, valuable artifacts, statues and monumental portraits. Unique ticket with the park.

  • Where Via Scylletion 1, Roccelletta, Borgia
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: ponculturaesviluppo.cultura.gov.it · fondoambiente.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Basilica di Santa Maria della Roccella

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

Norman church of the 11th century inside the archaeological park, known for its red brick ruins surrounded by greenery.

  • Where Parco archeologico di Scolacium, Roccelletta, Borgia
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Grande frantoio di Scolacium

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Structure of modern age museum inside the park, indicated as one of the best preserved industrial archaeology sites in Calabria.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Centro storico di Borgia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

On the hill, noble palaces, monuments and churches survived the earthquake of the eighteenth century, which destroyed most of the country and redesigned its plant.

  • Where Borgia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
