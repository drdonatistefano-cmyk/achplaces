What to see in Bova

Borgo about 915 meters between the Ionian Sea and the Aspromonte, capital of Bovesia, the Greek area of Calabria where the Greek of Calabria is still spoken; under the ruins of the Norman castle it houses the cathedral with the Madonna of Rinaldo Bonanno, the church of San Leo, the museum of Paleontology of Aspromonte, the museum of the Greek-calabra language "Gerhard Rohlfs" and a steam locomotive in the square.

Ruderi del castello normanno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The remains of the castle (X-XI centuries according to the most beautiful villages in Italy; the surviving structures date back to the Angevin period according to Touring) dominate the village from a rocky spur to about 915 meters. On the rock is carved a footprint tied to the legend of a Greek queen.

  • Where Sommità del centro storico, Bova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria dell'Isodia

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

Founded in the first centuries after Christ and rebuilt several times, it has three nave basilicas. It preserves the statue of the Madonna "Isodia" with Child, attributed to Rinaldo Bonanno (1584); it stands in a panoramic position.

  • Where Bova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Leo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

The 17th century church has a single nave with side chapels and baroque marble altar; it houses the statue of St. Leo of Pietro Bernini (1582) and a nucleus of silverware. The chapel of the Relics, built in 1722, with other Baroque works.

  • Where Bova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Museo di Paleontologia e Scienze Naturali dell'Aspromonte

Museums · Themed

In the former Pretura, on about 300 m2, it traces the geological eras and the environmental evolution of Calabria with about 15,000 findings from the Paleozoic to the Quaternary collected by Demetrio Lo Giudice and acquired by the Municipality: a deer jaw of about 100,000 years old, skull bones of Pliocene whale found at 750 meters on the Aspromonte, a rib of sirenide from the Lazarus Stone. Open Monday-Friday 8-14, closed Saturday and Sunday.

  • Where Via Rimembranze, Bova
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Museo della Lingua Greco-Calabra «Gerhard Rohlfs»

Museums · Themed

Opened in 2016 and dedicated to the German philologist Gerhard Rohlfs, the first to study the Greek of Calabria; six rooms each tell an aspect of the language with photos, historical documents and audiovisual installations. Hours: Monday-Friday 16-19, Saturday and Sunday 9-13 and 15-19.

  • Where Via Sant'Antonio, Bova
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Locomotiva 740.054 in piazza

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

In the square dedicated to the Italian railway companies is exposed from 7 May 1988, in concession of the State Railways, the steam locomotive n. 054 of group 740, built in 1912 by the Ansaldo of Genoa and coming from the deposit of Castelvetrano-Trapani; it is a monument to the emigration and depopulation of the country.

  • Where Piazza principale, Bova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Palazzi storici del centro

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

In the center are Palazzo Mesiani-Mazzacuva (late 18th century), today used by the Municipality as a cultural center of Magna Grecia; Palazzo Nesci Sant'Agata (Seventeenth century) on the main square, private; Palazzo Tuscano (nineteenth century) in the upper part, seat of the Visitor Centre of the Aspromonte National Park.

  • Where Centro storico, Bova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiese del Carmine, dell'Immacolata e di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The church of Carmine (Sixth century), already of the noble Mesiani, has a late Renaissance façade; that of the Immaculate (Seventh century) a late Baroque stone portal; San Rocco (Five hundred), damaged by earthquakes and then restored, is used for the Greek-Byzantine rite.

  • Where Centro storico, Bova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
