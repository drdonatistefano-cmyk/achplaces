What to see in Palazzolo sull'Oglio

On the banks of the Oglio, between the plains, Franciacorta and Sebino, Palazzolo is dominated by the Tower of the People, about 92 meters high, erected on the Mirabella Tower of Rocha Magna; in front, beyond the ancient bridge, the Medieval Rocchetta. From 1428 under Venice, as St. Mark's Lion remembers on the tower, it is also the end of the historic Railway of Sebino.

Torre del Popolo

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built between 1813 and 1830 on the circular tower Mirabella of the walls of Rocha Magna, it measures about 92 meters including the statue of San Fedele of 7 meters; it is indicated as the highest bell tower of the circular base of Europe. Rebuilt after a fire of 1893, it has the current statue since 1896; on the wall is carved the lion of St Mark, sign of Venetian rule since 1428. The ascent, with internal and external stairs, offers views of the old town and the Oglio.

Rocha Magna (Castello)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Built between IX and X century on the left bank of the Oglio to defend Castrum Palatioli, it was the capital of Brescia against Bergamo for the control of the river and maintained garrison and castle until the sixteenth century; in 1517 Venice abandoned it and took charge of the Municipality. It was lazzaretto in 1630, ball playground in the moat and rented houses; private since 1840, it returned to the municipality in 1990 by the Kupfer family. Trapezoidal plant with high walls of river pebbles, deep moat, three round towers (Mirabella, Ruellina and the rescue door) around the square mast of the Tower of Salvation, courtyard with well and two levels of underground partly visitable.

Rocchetta

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Square tower of the defensive system of Mura, erected around the IX-X century in front of the Castle to guard the ancient Roman bridge on the Oglio; it was the head of the Bergamo fortifications on the river, with fortified door, wall and moat, at the climax in the first thirteenth century. After the peace of Mura in 1192 it lost importance, it was abandoned in the second fifteenth century and in the seventeenth century it became bell tower with bell cell and large clock; it preserves the original spiral staircase in stone of Sarnico. The outdoor area is freely accessible.

Chiesa di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Complex at the bottom of Via Gorini, next to the Torrione, with the chapel of the Suffragio dei Morti and the nearby chapel of San Rocco. The medieval church with a single nave was rebuilt in the eighteenth century: advanced facade, building almost doubled in height, new bell tower and polygonal choir. It preserves a painting by Giovanni Rampana of St. John the Evangelist on the high altar, a great altarpiece by Andrea Celesti with the Virgin intercedes for the souls of purgatory and the cycle of frescoes by Matteo Pedrali (1935) on the life of St. Jerome Emiliani, for which they settled inhabitants of the village.

Museo di Storia e Architettura Navale «Il Sestante»

Museums · Themed · Free entry

On the ground floor of Villa Kupfer, in the municipal park of the Three Villas, collects since 2004, thanks to the volunteers of the association Il Sestante, relics and models linked to the sea from the eighteenth to the twentieth century: great naval models like the H.M.S. Victory and the Polar Star, nautical charts, vintage flags, sextant, compass, axe master tools and objects of the Amerigo Vespucci school ship. The source does not indicate timetables: contact the contact person of the association.

Parco fluviale «G. Metelli»

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

Park in the center along the Oglio, on the area of the eighteenth-century tannery then Nulli, closed in 1922 and acquired by the Municipality in the late nineties. Walking paths, life path, outdoor exercise equipment, dog area, beach volleyball and kiosk; in summer it sometimes hosts courses and events.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: comune.palazzolosulloglio.bs.it · italia.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The centre preserves elegant palaces, squares, medieval fortifications, the ancient city walls and alleys that descend to the river, between Castle and Tower of the People. From the station start in summer the panoramic carriages of the historic Sebino Railway towards Paratico-Sarnico.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
