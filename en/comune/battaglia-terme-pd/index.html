What to see in Battaglia Terme

Country of water on the eastern slopes of the Euganean Hills, grown along the navigable canal and once among the major Italian river ports: the Museum of Fluvial Navigation, the Conca di navigazione, the Castle of the Catajo and Villa Selvatico with the thermal park.

5 places to visit in Battaglia Terme, in the province of Padua (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo civico della navigazione fluviale

Museums · Themed

In the former municipal slaughterhouse of Ortazzo, the old district of the boatmen, it collects hundreds of objects, boats and their parts, models, vintage photos and tools donated by boaters who transported goods and people from Vicenza to the lagoon; it tells over seven centuries of inland navigation. Open Saturday and Sunday.

  • Where Via Ortazzo 63, Battaglia Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.veneto.eu

Sources: cultura.gov.it · veneto.eu

Castello del Catajo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Started in 1570 by the Obizzi as the seat of representation, it was enlarged in the nineteenth century for the court extended in exile and became hunting residence of the Habsburgs; on the noble floor the forty squares frescoed by Giovanni Battista Zelotti with the history of the family. Park with free deer, gardens with fountains and fish ponds and panoramic terrace on the hills.

  • Where Via Catajo 1, Battaglia Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.veneto.eu

Sources: fondoambiente.it · veneto.eu

Villa Selvatico e giardino termale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Villa built from 1593 to 1647 for the Selvatico on the hill of Sant'Elena, with the sudoriferous cave frequented already in the Middle Ages; in the hall the frescoes of the Stories of Antenor of Luca Ferrari (1650) and in the dome works of Lorenzo Bedogni and of the Paduanino. It climbs with 144 steps to the romantic park of about 11 hectares redesigned by Giuseppe Jappelli, with thermal lakes.

  • Where Viale Sant'Elena 36, Battaglia Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.veneto.eu

Sources: fondoambiente.it · veneto.eu

Conca di navigazione e Arco di Mezzo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The Conca, a hydraulic work still in operation, makes the boats overcome a difference in altitude of more than 7 meters; the Arch of Mezzo still regulates the outflow of the Padua waters. The village is also visited with guides on foot or by boat.

  • Where Battaglia Terme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.veneto.eu

Sources: veneto.eu · fondoambiente.it

Sentiero Ferro di Cavallo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A signposted route of the Regional Park of the Euganean Hills with wide views and rich hill vegetation.

  • Where Battaglia Terme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.veneto.eu
