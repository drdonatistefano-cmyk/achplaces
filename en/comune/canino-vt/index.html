What to see in Canino

Land of oil DOP and Vulci, one of the most powerful city-states of Etruria: walls, temples, domus and necropolis with the Tomb François, the Castle of Badia and the Devil's Bridge on the Flower.

4 places to visit in Canino, in the province of Viterbo (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Canino in the guide with map and filters

Parco naturalistico archeologico di Vulci

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Over 120 hectares along the Fiora: the walls in tuff of the IV century BC with five doors, the Great Temple, the domus with mosaics and necropolis, including the François tombs and the Inscriptions.

  • Where Vulci, Canino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Castello della Badia – Museo archeologico di Vulci

Museums · Archaeological

A castle in lava stone documented from the IX century, already Benedictine abbey, Farnese, Luciano Bonaparte and Torlonia; the museum tells the Etruscan-Roman city with the findings of the necropolis.

  • Where Località Vulci, Canino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Ponte del Diavolo (della Badia)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The bridge of Etruscan origin completed by the Romans, 30 meters high, that crosses the gorge of the Fiora and connects the castle.

  • Where Vulci, Canino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Palazzo Comunale e Palazzo Miccinelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

In the village, the Palazzo Comunale of 1696 and the fifteenth-century Palazzo Miccinelli.

  • Where Canino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
