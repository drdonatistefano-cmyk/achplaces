What to see in Terranuova Bracciolini

New Florentine land of Valdarno founded as Castel Santa Maria and completed in 1337, with walls and fourteenth-century towers around Piazza della Repubblica; in the territory the Balze del Valdarno with Piantravigne, the sanctuaries of Pernina and Montemarciano, the frescoed oratory of Campo Arsiccio and the reserve of the Valley of Hell along the Arno.

Centro storico e mura di Terranuova

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Founded at the end of the thirteenth century as one of the three new Florentine lands of Valdarno, together with San Giovanni and Castelfranco di Sopra, with the name of Castel Santa Maria, completed in 1337; in 1862 it took its current name in honor of the humanist Poggio Bracciolini. It preserves the fourteenth-century walls with towers of different size and function; in Piazza della Repubblica the Town Hall, and in the center the fourteenth-century churches of San Bartolomeo in Pozzo and San Biagio in Mori. Two Sundays a month the square hosts the Antique Fair.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Bambina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded around the middle of the fourteenth century, it became parish church in 1443 and archipretura in 1737; the present appearance derives from an eighteenth-century restoration. It preserves paintings from the eighteenth century, a Pietà by Giovanni della Robbia and an urn with the relics of Saint Tito.

Convento di Ganghereto

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Franciscan monastery of the fourteenth century, today of the Dominican nuns; according to tradition here Saint Francis found a source considered miraculous. There was the altarpiece with San Francesco di Margarito d'Arezzo, from the second half of the thirteenth century, today at the Museum of Medieval and Modern Art in Arezzo.

Montemarciano e Oratorio della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Borgo, the historic stopover point of the pilgrims, which preserves the Porta Campana, of the thirteenth century, intact in the walls. The seventeenth-century sanctuary was built to house a miraculous tabernacle with the fresco of the Madonna and Child, once attributed to Masaccio and today recognized to Francesco d'Antonio.

Piantravigne e Pieve di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

The village is surrounded by the Erose walls of the Balze and connected by bridges over the gorges; the fourteenth-century parish church of San Lorenzo, on a natural cliff, preserves the oldest painting of the Madonna del Valdarno Superiore, recently restored in the 13th century. In the area also the fourteenth-century church of San Pietro in Treggiaia.

Balze del Valdarno

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Protected area on the slopes of Pratomagno, between the streams Resco and Ciuffenna, established to protect the Balze: ravines, escarpments and pillars shaped by erosion, warm tones and yellow, which Leonardo da Vinci painted in the background of some works. The most famous formations are the pillars of Poggitazzi and Piantravigne; from the village of Persignano you can admire the leaps around. In 2024 the site of the Pliocene and Pleistocene mammals of Valdarno entered among the GeoCollections of the IUGS.

  • Where Piantravigne e Persignano, Terranuova Bracciolini
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
