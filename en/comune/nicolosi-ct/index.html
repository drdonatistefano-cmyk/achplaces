Parco dell'Etna

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

The park was born in the 1980s around the highest active volcano in Europe, a UNESCO World Heritage site: summit craters, lava caves, the Valley of Bove, 7 km long and 1 km deep; you can visit on trails, by off-road or cable car, with expert guides to the craters.
