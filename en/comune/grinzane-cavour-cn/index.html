What to see in Grinzane Cavour

The 11th-century castle where Camillo Cavour lived, a member of the UNESCO site of wine-growing landscapes, with the first regional wine shop in Piedmont.

3 places to visit in Grinzane Cavour, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Grinzane Cavour e Museo delle Langhe

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The 11th century castle on the hill among the vineyards, where he lived and was mayor Camillo Benso of Cavour: it houses the Museum of the Langhe on the peasant civilization and the Cavurian relics. Member of the UNESCO site.

  • Where Via Castello 5, Grinzane Cavour
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: italia.it · paesaggivitivinicoliunesco.it · castellogrinzane.com

Enoteca Regionale Piemontese Cavour

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry

In the castle, opened in 1967, the first regional wine shop in Piedmont and one of the oldest in Italy.

  • Where Via Castello 5, Grinzane Cavour
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlmr.it · italia.it

In Vigna – museo a cielo aperto

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Outside the castle, the open-air museum dedicated to the cultivation of vines, winemaking and wine traditions of Langhe, Roero and Monferrato.

  • Where Via Castello, Grinzane Cavour
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
