What to see in Sadali

Medieval village of the Barbagia di Seulo, over 700 meters on the plateau of on Taccu and among the most beautiful villages in Italy, born around 1335 around the church of San Valentino: the waterfall in the center of the village, the seventeenth-century mill, the museum house of Aunt Cramella, the caves Is Janas and, on the border with Seulo, Su Stampu de su Turrunu.

8 places to visit in Sadali, in the province of Sud Sardegna (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cascata di San Valentino

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

In front of the parish, the only waterfall of Sardinia in a town: fed by the Funtana Manna resurgence, it jumps for about seven meters and disappears in sa Bucca manna, a cavity that can be visited with a stone path, to emerge about 150 meters below.

  • Where Piazza San Valentino, Sadali
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Valentino

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

The only Sardinian sanctuary dedicated to the Roman martyr of the third century, of late Byzantine origin, with wooden altar of the seventeenth century; in the restoration of 1996 a Roman furnace for intact bricks emerged. The saint, on Saintus Coiadoras, who marries, is celebrated on 14 February, 8 May and 6 October.

  • Where Piazza San Valentino, Sadali
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Mulino ad acqua

Local life and traditions · Crafts

A few meters from the waterfall, the last watermill in the area: in the seventeenth century the mills fed by Funtana Manna were eight, and this was used for over two centuries to grind the wheat. Restored, it preserves the ancient tools of work and is visitable.

  • Where Vicino alla cascata, Sadali
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Casa museo Sa omu 'e zia Cramella

Museums · House museums

House of the end of the nineteenth century in the square called "mesu idda" , of which Aunt Carmela was the penultimate owner: four rooms on the rural life with tools for wood, agriculture and breeding, the preservation of food, the attic of supplies and the corner of bread with the grinder moved by the donkey.

  • Where Piazza Eleonora d'Arborea, Sadali
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Grotte Is Janas

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

In a wood of holm oaks, which can be visited with a guide: more salt rich in stalactites, stalagmites, columns and ochre flows; in the hall "S'Omu de is Janas" three great stalagmites would be three petrified fairies, and a formation called "the organ" sounds if beaten. They host bats and geotriton. Admission for a fee.

  • Where SS 198 direzione Gadoni, Sadali
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · idese.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Su Stampu de su Turrunu

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Natural monument in the forest of Addolì, on the border between Sadali and Seulo: a threefold karst phenomenon of swallowery, cave and rise, where the river Su Longufresu jumps for about 16 meters in a pond between limestone walls covered with moss. You can reach it with a marked path or with a guide.

  • Where Foresta di Addolì, confine Sadali-Seulo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Nuraghe Accodulazzo

History and architecture · Archaeology

Nuraghe near the village, testimony of the nuragic presence: there were found obsidian, prehistoric ceramics and worms of Roman age.

  • Where Dintorni di Sadali
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village of just under a thousand inhabitants on the limestone plateau of Su Taccu, with narrow and cobbled streets from which suddenly open panoramas on the valley of the Flumendosa; among the churches also Santa Maria (XV-XVI century) and Sant'Elena. Sources and river give a cool microclimate in summer.

  • Where Centro storico, Sadali
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
