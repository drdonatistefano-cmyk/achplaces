What to see in Bomba

Country of Chietino at the foot of Monte Pallano, overlooking the artificial lake created by barring the Sangro with a dam on earth; home of Silvio Spaventa, remembered by the bronze monument of the main square, houses the Sanctuary of San Mauro Abbot, a destination of pilgrims for centuries, and an ethnographic museum on rural life.

Lago di Bomba

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Artificial lake created on the Sangro river by a earth-paved dam in the territory of Bomba, built to produce electricity. On its banks there are also the municipalities of Atessa, Colledimezzo, Pennadomo, Pietraferrazzana and Villa Santa Maria.

Monte Pallano

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The sacred mountain dedicated to Maja, on whose slopes Bomba rises, is divided between the municipalities of Bomba and Tornareccio. The well-marked trails run on foot or by mountain bike and open views of the Sangro Valley, the Adriatic and Majella. On the mountain a Hellenistic-Roman settlement is documented by the researches of 1984-1993; the megalithic walls of the 5th century BC, of rocks framed without mortar, fall in the part of Tornareccio.

  • Where Strada comunale Sambuceto-Monte Pallano, Bomba
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Santuario di San Mauro Abate

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

The primitive church of the 12th century, in the center of the village, was also a destination for pilgrims from outside Abruzzo; the current, designed by Alessio Mancini in modern Romanesque style, was started in 1954 and inaugurated in 1963. Inside the golden stuccoes of the Bravo brothers of Atessa, a reproduction of the Annunciation attributed to the Reni in the vault, the mosaic of the bezel with San Mauro among the sick and, in the niche of the altar, the polychrome terracotta image of the seventeenth century; in front of a portico and a bell tower isolated in red bricks with the 'campanella of San Mauro', which the devotees bite against the toothache. The churches are usually closed: you visit by contacting the parish (0872 860113); opened on the last Sunday of the month 7-12 and on 15 January, feast of the saint.

Monumento a Silvio Spaventa

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Bronze statue of 1898 by the Roman sculptor Adolfo Laurenti, little bigger than the natural, made in two equal copies: one is in front of the Ministry of Finance in Via XX Settembre in Rome, the other in the main square of Bomba, where stood the Fontana Grande. It remained long in the hallway of the town hall and was placed in the square only sixteen years later.

  • Where Piazza principale, Bomba
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.comune.bomba.ch.it

Museo etnografico Amuset

Museums · Themed

Established in 1990, it reconstructs the environments of the rural world: housing rooms with frame, bedrooms and kitchen with antique furniture, tool storeroom and cellar with tools for vinification, spaces dedicated to crafts, games and school, and a permanent photographic exhibition on daily life from 1850 to 1950. Open on request, entrance fee (amount not indicated).

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Antiquarium comunale

Museums · City history · Free entry

Inaugurated on 12 August 1984, it no longer houses the remains of Monte Pallano, removed after the intervention of the Superintendence; it contains photographic copies of parchments, maps, documents from the 18th to the 19th century, ancient postcards and the archaeological photographic exhibition of 1984. It follows the opening hours of the Town Hall.

  • Where Municipio, Piazza Giacomo Matteotti 6, Bomba
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.comune.bomba.ch.it
