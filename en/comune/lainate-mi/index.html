What to see in Lainate

Comune dell'hinterland milanese noto per Villa Visconti Borromeo Litta, villa of delights designed around 1585 by Count Pirro I Visconti Borromeo, with the Nymphaeum (Palazzo delle Acque) whose water games still work as originally and a historical park of about three hectares.

Palazzi di Villa Visconti Borromeo Litta

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

Villa of delights built between the sixteenth and eighteenth centuries, designed around 1585 by Count Pirro I Visconti Borromeo, which transformed an agricultural estate inspired by the Medici villas and made it one of the most renowned country houses in Milan; the project is by Martino Bassi, the decorations of Camillo Procaccini, Morazzone, Volpino and others. In the eighteenth century, Giulio Visconti Borromeo Arese added the "Fourth new" with the ballroom; the City bought the villa in 1971. Season 2026 from May to October; on Sunday the Palaces can be visited also on a free visit. Free up to 5 years and with Musei Lombardia subscription.

Ninfeo di Villa Litta (Palazzo delle Acque)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

Made to be built around 1585 by Pirro I Visconti Borromeo together with the gardens and in the eighteenth century renovated in the façades by the Marquis Pompeo Litta, it is considered the most important example of the genre: the guided tour crosses Wunderkammer decorated with mosaics, caves with rich sculptures and automatons, up to the water games still operated by the fountainers with the original hydraulic mechanisms. The Madonna Litta attributed to Leonardo was kept there in the past. Season 2026 from May to October, only with guided tour (Saturday, Sunday and Holidays, plus Thursday and Friday at 16); in summer "Nympheus under the stars" Saturday evening with dedicated lighting. Online purchase recommended.

Parco di Villa Litta

Nature and landscape · City parks

Park of about three hectares around the villa, originally with vegetable gardens and citrus groves and from the sixteenth century scenery of musical and theatrical performances; in the eighteenth century Pompeo Litta enriched it with monumental fountains and at the beginning of the nineteenth century Luigi Canonica inserted an English grove. It hosts numerous species also exotic, kept in Liberty greenhouses in iron and glass, and has been awarded among the most beautiful of Italian public parks. Italia.it indicates opening every day 8:30-19:00.
