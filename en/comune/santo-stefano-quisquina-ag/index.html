What to see in Santo Stefano Quisquina

Municipality of Monti Sicani with the Teatro Andromeda, work of the sculptor-pastor Lorenzo Reina, the eighteenth-century Hermitage of Santa Rosalia next to the cave of the saint, starting point of the Itinerarium Rosaliae, and part of the reserve of Monte Cammarata.

Teatro Andromeda

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Viewpoint

Stone theatre built almost entirely by hand by the sculptor-pastor Lorenzo Reina from the 1970s, on a plateau on top of a cliff: the cavea has 108 seats arranged as the 108 stars of the constellation Andromeda and the elliptical stage is made of 365 tiles. At the summer solstice the sun rises aligned with a window of the seabed; it is the heart of the Art Farm, together with sculptures of Reina including the Mask of the Word.

Eremo di Santa Rosalia alla Quisquina

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Monastery of the eighteenth century in a natural wood of oaks on Monte Cammarata, next to the cave where Santa Rosalia took refuge for twelve years after refusing the wedding in Palermo; it preserves an epigraph in Latin of the saint. It is the starting point of the Itinerarium Rosaliae, the path to the Sanctuary of Monte Pellegrino.

Riserva naturale Monte Cammarata

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Reserve of 2,049 hectares established in 2000 between Cammarata, San Giovanni Gemini and Santo Stefano Quisquina: includes Monte Cammarata (1.578 m), highest peak of the Sicani Mountains, with Monte Gemini, Pizzo Rondine and Serra della Coin, natural woods of holm oak and roverella and reforestation pine trees.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
