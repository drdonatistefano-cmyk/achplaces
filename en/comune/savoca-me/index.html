What to see in Savoca

Borgo on a hill between citrus groves and vineyards, known worldwide as the set of "Il Padrino" of Coppola: the Mother Church of Norman origin, the church of San Nicolò on the rocky spur, the ruins of the castle of Pentefur and the crypt of the Capuchins with the mummies of the notables.

9 places to visit in Savoca, in the province of Messina (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa Madre di Santa Maria in Cielo Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The most important monument of the village, of the XII century and of Norman origin: Romanesque capitals, a wooden throne carved with the coat of arms of the Archimandrita and late medieval mural paintings, including a San Giovanni Chrysostomo. Mummification of the dead was practiced in underground rooms.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa di San Nicolò (Santa Lucia)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Church of the early thirteenth century, with three naves, built on a rocky spur overlooking the valley; it is called of Saint Lucia because it guards the statue of the patroness. It was Michael's and Apollonia's wedding set in the "The Godfather."

  • Where Savoca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Cripta del Convento dei Cappuccini

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

In the crypt of the convent, founded in 1574, the mummies of local notables dressed in clothes of the early nineteenth century are exhibited in niches; mummification, with drying in the putridarium of the Mother Church, lasted sixty days and was practiced until 1876. The place fascinated Hercules Patti and Leonardo Sciascia.

Chiesa di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built before 1250 and loved by the Archimanders, it has Gothic-sic portals, a nave partly rebuilt in Baroque style, works of art, noble tombs and frescoes; traditionally converts climbed down its seven steps before baptism.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Castello di Pentefur

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ruins of the castle dominate the village: probably built by the Arabs and enlarged by the Normans, it rises on a more ancient fortification of uncertain origin, linked to the legendary founders of the country.

  • Where Savoca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Porta della Città e centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The fourteenth-century door with sharp arch in local stone is the only surviving of the two of the Norman walls. The center, with the remains of the Town Hall and the Archimandrital Palace and the synagogue documented until 1470, has seven districts with medieval, Renaissance, Baroque and rural buildings of the early twentieth century; at the entrance panels in ceramic bas-relief of Salvatore and Giuseppe Zona tell the history of the village.

  • Where Savoca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Palazzo Trimarchi e Bar Vitelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The eighteenth-century Palace which houses the Bar Vitelli, set de "The Godfather" of which it retains the sign; it is the first stage of the film itinerary and houses a collection of ancient objects and a Byzantine painting of St. John Chrysostom.

Eremo del Monte Calvario e Via Crucis

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient hermitage transformed into a church by the Jesuits in 1736; the Stations of the Way of the Cross are partly carved into the rock.

Museo comunale città di Savoca storico-etno-antropologico

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Faithful reconstruction of typical environments of rural houses, renewed and expanded, with tools of peasant work, objects of daily use, the work cycles of the baker and lumberjack set up on original wooden structures and photographic documentation.
