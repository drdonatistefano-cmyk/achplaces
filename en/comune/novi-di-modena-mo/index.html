What to see in Novi di Modena

The agricultural center of the Modena plain hit hard by the earthquake of 2012, today known for the murals of the Totart Festival and for the Oasi Borsari, one of the major gauzes of Emilia-Romagna; in the territory villas and palaces such as the Palazzo delle Lama and the Palazzo Gasparini Casari.

6 places to visit in Novi di Modena, in the province of Modena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Oasi faunistica Borsari

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Approximately 13 hectares of wetlands, hygrophilous forests and humid meadows that host one of the largest gauzes of Emilia-Romagna; the most common species is the heron, now sedentary. The best time to visit is spring or autumn.

  • Where Novi di Modena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Murales del Totart Festival

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Works born in the 2016 and 2017 editions of the Totart Festival, cycle of initiatives for social cohesion in the area affected by the earthquake: Ericailcane and Bastardilla on the walls of the music school Luigi Restani, Basik with Luke tuning his own picture according to its diagonal size , Giorgio Bartocci with Reversible Abstraction and Luca Zamoc with murals inspired by the Fall of the Giants of Giulio Romano and the ceramics of Filippo Tagliolini. Map on the URBANER regional portal.

  • Where Scuola di musica Luigi Restani e vie del paese, Novi di Modena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

An isolated nineteenth-century parish church along the road to Concordia and Mirandola; it preserves a marble slab of 12th century ambo with Christ Pantocrator and symbols of the Evangelists. Behind it the villa of the Roses (now Vecchi), also nineteenth century, with a large agricultural courtyard.

  • Where Strada per Concordia, Novi di Modena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it

Palazzo delle Lame

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

A vast seventeenth-century building remained unfinished, already castalderia estense, near the hamlet of Rovereto sulla Secchia.

Palazzo Gasparini Casari

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Complex of the XV-XIX centuries, already of the Pio di Savoia, along the road that runs from Rovereto along the left bank of the Secchia towards Concordia: remarkable the fortified system with four surviving towers and the Oratory of the Immaculate.

  • Where Strada per Concordia, argine sinistro della Secchia, Novi di Modena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it

Teatro sociale

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built between 1923 and 1929, long private and adapted to cinema, acquired by the Municipality in 1995: atrium with late Art Nouveau decorations, horseshoe room with three orders of loggias and remains of the decorations of the ceiling and the archo scene.

The MiC card indicates it closed every day, unused since the end of the eighties, with bad conservation status and restoration not realized due to lack of funds

  • Where Via Martiri della Libertà 2, Novi di Modena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitmodena.it
