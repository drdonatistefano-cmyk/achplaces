What to see in Paulilatino

On the basaltic plateau of Abbasanta, an open-air museum with 110 nuragic sites and the sanctuary of Santa Cristina, the most perfect nuragic sacred architecture.

3 places to visit in Paulilatino, in the province of Oristano (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Paulilatino in the guide with map and filters

Santuario nuragico di Santa Cristina

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Well temple of the final Bronze (XII century B.C.) in basalt cones, with fence to ... serratura..., staircase of 25 steps and chamber to tholos; around the hut of the meetings and, at 200 meters, the nuraghe Santa Cristina of the XV century B.C.

  • Where Altopiano di Abbasanta, Paulilatino
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa campestre di Santa Cristina e muristenes

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

The 11th century small country church that gives its name to the park among centuries-old olive trees, surrounded by the houses of the pilgrims of the novenary.

  • Where Santa Cristina, Paulilatino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Nuraghi Lugherras e Battizzonis

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Lugherras, with central tower and trilobed bastion surrounded by a four-tower wall, and the Battizzonis with mast and three-tower bastion.

  • Where Paulilatino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
