What to see in Brissogne

The municipality of the envers, from the Dora to almost 2800 meters, with the wet reserve of Les Îles and the valley of Les Laures.

3 places to visit in Brissogne, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Brissogne in the guide with map and filters

Riserva naturale Les Îles

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Wetland along the Dora, the most important regional site for migratory birds such as herons, with two lakes; it can also be cycled along the bike path along the river.

  • Where Les Îles, Brissogne
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Castello di Brissogne

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the 13th century, today it is ruined, with the remains of the cylindrical tower and part of the walls.

  • Where Brissogne
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Vallone e laghi di Laures

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural amphitheatre at about 1400 meters and, higher up, the lakes reachable in summer from Grand-Brissogne passing through the bivouac Ménabréaz.

  • Where Brissogne
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it
