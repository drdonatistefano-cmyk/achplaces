What to see in Pinzano al Tagliamento

The town overlooking the narrow Tagliamento, with the ruins of the castle on the hill and the churches of Pinzano and Valeriano that keep frescoes and the first triptych of Pordenone; in Borgo Ampiano there is a mill of the fifteenth century.

5 places to visit in Pinzano al Tagliamento, in the province of Pordenone (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Pinzano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the hill on the right bank of the Tagliamento, overlooking the narrow river, ancient way of transit; the lords of Pinzano, vassals of the Patriarch of Aquileia, are mentioned in 1164 and in the thirteenth century the castle had walls, mast and a church of St. Nicholas. Damaged by the 1348 earthquake, conquered by the Savorgnan in the fourteenth century, it lost its military function; since 1809, passed to Antonio Moro and other owners, it was used mainly as a stone quarry. Today the oval plateau, of about 175 m of perimeter, is covered with ruins; the restorations have brought to light an embankment with underground cellars and Roman and medieval finds.

Chiesa di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It houses two frescoes of the Pordenone used as altarpieces: the Madonna on throne with the Child of 1525 in the right aisle, with the inscription of the clients, and the Martyrdom of San Sebastiano among the Saints Rocco, Stefano, Niccolò and the archangel Michael in the chapel of San Sebastiano, paid between 1527 and 1535. The documents were discovered in 1967 by the parish priest Don Vittorino Zanette.

Chiesa di Santo Stefano a Valeriano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It preserves the triptych signed in 1506 .Zuane Antonius de' Sacchis resident in Spilimbergo, rediscovered in 1909 by the parish priest Fr. Giuseppe Bertoni and considered the first sure work of the Pordenone: St. Michael who tramples Satan between the Saints Valeriano and Giovanni Battista. Damaged by the 1976 earthquake, it has been restored.

Chiesa di Santa Maria dei Battuti a Valeriano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In 1524 the Pordenone frescoed the façade for 45 ducats (San Cristoforo, Adoration of the Magi, Madonna enthroned with the Savorgnan coat of arms, Ecce Homo): damaged by the earthquake, the frescoes were detached and brought inside, while outside they remain copies drawn. Inside also an Escape in Egypt, perhaps in 1524, and the Nativity of 1527, in excellent condition after the restoration.

Mulino di Borgo Ampiano

Museums · Themed

15th century mill, restored (last intervention in 2004) and part of the Ecomuseum of the Friulian Dolomites Lis Aganis: on the ground floor a small forge-falegnameria for repairs and the hall with Hungarian millstones and roller mills, on the upper floor the machines to work cereals and the turret with sieves to separate the flours. It housed the Madonna della Misericordia del Pordenone (1524-1527), since 1957 at the Civic Museum of Conegliano; today there is a work by Vittorio Basaglia. Visits on request with reservation required (tel. 0434 749622).
