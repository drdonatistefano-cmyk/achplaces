What to see in Mondovì

The city of time is divided between the high district of Piazza, on Monte Regale, and Breo at its feet, joined by the funicular of Giugiaro, with the fake dome of Andrea Pozzo and the sundials.

8 places to visit in Mondovì, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 5 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Chiesa della Missione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the vault Andrea Pozzo painted a fake dome of extraordinary effect, enhanced by the multimedia path \"Infinitum" .

  • Where Piazza Maggiore, Mondovì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Piazza Maggiore

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The heart of the Piazza district with the palaces of the Governor, of Città and Fauzone, which tell over eight centuries of history.

  • Where Piazza Maggiore, Mondovì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Cattedrale di San Donato

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

18th century cathedral designed by Francesco Gallo, next to the bishop's palace.

  • Where Mondovì Piazza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Torre e Giardino del Belvedere, Parco del Tempo

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

The symbolic tower of the city, with one-hand clocks, and the garden with views of the Alps and the Cuneo plain; in the Park of Time numerous sundials.

  • Where Mondovì Piazza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Museo della Ceramica

Museums · Themed

In Palazzo Fauzone, about two hundred years of Monregalian ceramics; here also the tourist office.

  • Where Palazzo Fauzone, Piazza Maggiore, Mondovì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Museo civico della Stampa

Museums · Themed

Between the walls of the Cultural Pole of Orphans, the history and technology of the book and the press.

  • Where Mondovì Piazza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Funicolare

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

It connects the upper city and the lower city, designed by Giorgetto Giugiaro.

  • Where Piazza San Pietro, Mondovì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Piazza San Pietro e chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the Breo district, the square decorated with sundials overlooking the church: the automata of the Moro, mask of the Carnival, beats the hours.

  • Where Piazza San Pietro, Mondovì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
