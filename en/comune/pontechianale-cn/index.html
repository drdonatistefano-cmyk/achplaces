What to see in Pontechianale

Municipality of Alta Val Varaita on the border with France, with the lake between the woods and the hamlet of Chianale, stone village at 1800 meters among the most beautiful villages in Italy.

Borgo di Chianale e ponte di pietra

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The stone village divided by the Varaita and joined by the stone bridge, symbol and heart of the village, along the ancient Chemin Royal.

  • Where Chianale, Pontechianale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

From 1459 to the end of the 17th century, the parish church of the fourteenth century, with a bell tower, a Romanesque portal and a barrel vault on decorated shelves.

  • Where Chianale, Pontechianale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Casa Martinet

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

At number 17 of Chemin Royal, once a Calvinist temple: in the sixteenth century Chianale was the only place in the valley with freedom of worship.

  • Where Chemin Royal 17, Chianale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Lago di Pontechianale

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lake is surrounded by woods at about 1620 meters, jewel of the Alta Val Varaita.

  • Where Pontechianale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it
