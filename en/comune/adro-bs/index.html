What to see in Adro

The village is almost at the western edge of Franciacorta, at the foot of Monte Alto, with views of vineyards and lake and noble buildings of the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries; the 18th century parish church with the medieval tower, the ancient parish church of Santa Maria Assunta on the hill and the sanctuary of the Madonna della Snow with the silk, hemp and linen museum.

Santuario della Madonna della Neve

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Erected in 1520 after an apparition of the Virgin who, according to tradition, healed a deaf-mute pastor; around 1750 the community of Adro demolished the original church for the influx of faithful and the present temple, according to plan of Gaspare Turbini, was completed in 1776. With a central plan, it has a large octagonal dome with eight windows, two chapels dedicated to San Carlo Borromeo and San Francesco di Paola and a fresco of the apparition; in the crypt the statue of the Virgin with the shepherd and the sheep, under a mosaic with the landscape of Franciacorta. Relyed on the Carmelites Scalzi; feast on August 5.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.adro.bs.it

Museo della seta, della canapa e del lino Marta Pozzobon in Girotto

Museums · Themed

Housed in the ancient granaries of the convent of the Carmelite Fathers Scalzi, it collects tools and tools for spinning and weaving hemp, linen and silk, personal and domestic linens, sacred vestments and naturalistic collections of silkworms, chrysalis and butterflies. The path follows the production cycles: for silk from the baco to the finished product, for hemp and flax from plant collection to fabric.

can be visited only by appointment (tel. 030 7356623) according to ISPRA

Antica parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

First parish church of Adro, rises on the top of a hill in the area of the ancient castle and offers a wide panorama. Built in the 15th century, it contains large apse frescoes and a cycle of 16th century frescoes.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.adro.bs.it

Parrocchiale di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Erected in 1769 in the center of the village, it houses a XVI-century triptych of the Romanino school, 18th-century stucco decorations and wooden works of the Fantonian workshop.

Torre medievale e resti del castello

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

High tower made of stone with square base with Ghibelline merlons, next to the parish: it is what remains of the defensive system and of the fourteenth-century castle erected to protect the village, of which the ruins of the entrance with the drawbridge remain.

  • Where Centro storico, Adro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.adro.bs.it

Sources: comune.adro.bs.it · touringclub.it

Palazzo Bargnani-Dandolo e chiesetta Bargnani

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

18th-century building, now the seat of the Town Hall, linked to the Bargnani family and their heirs Dandolo, remembered for the Risorgimento sentiments in the nineteenth century; on its right stands the Bargnani church.
