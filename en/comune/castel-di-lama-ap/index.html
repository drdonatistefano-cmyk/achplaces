What to see in Castel di Lama

Municipality of the lower Tronto valley along the Salaria, which takes its name from the Lama stream and the ancient castle; it preserves the nineteenth-century gardens of Villa Seghetti Panichi designed by Ludwig Winter, the eighteenth-century Villas of the Lama, Roman traces such as the cistern and the milestones and the collection of contemporary art .

Giardini di Villa Seghetti Panichi

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

The villa was built in the eighteenth century by Odoardo Odoardi on the remains of a medieval fortification; the garden was commissioned by Vincenzo Carfratelli Seghetti and built between 1875 and 1890 by the German landscaper Ludwig Winter. On the ridge that dominates the Tronto and the Salaria grow a rare Jubaea spectabilis, palms of the Canary Islands and dates, monumental Washingtonia, red beech and oaks; in the pond, on an island, an eighteenth-century statue in travertine of Venus and Cupid, and there is also the "romitory" typical of the gardens of Winter.

Raccolta d'arte contemporanea «Arte On»

Museums · Modern art

Founded in 2001 by the artists Vittorio Amadio and Marisa Marconi, who have recovered part of the former Sacet complex, collects paintings, graphics, sculptures, ceramics and photographs of local, regional, national and international artists; it also hosts exhibitions, concerts, conferences, poetry readings, projections and dance performances.

Chiesa di Santa Maria in Mignano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Already Santa Maria in Lama, in Piattoni: started in 1506 and remodelled several times, today it has an aspect between late Baroque and neoclassical; inside a sixteenth-century cross in gold copper on wooden support, of the Marche school. Nearby, the historical municipal buildings of the 16th and 19th centuries.

Ville della Lama

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Villa nobiliaryi of the eighteenth century spread over the municipal territory to form a mosaic, along with numerous kind speakers; among these Villa Seghetti Panichi, already Odoardi, born on a military fortification of the Odoardi of Ascoli.

Cisterna romana e cippi miliari della Salaria

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

In via Colle Cese there is a Roman cistern, testimony of the villas and settlements of Roman times in the area; on the edge of the village are visible the milestones of the ancient Via Salaria.
