What to see in Ivrea

The capital of the Canavese on the Dora Baltea: the ancient city with the Duomo and the castle of the red towers and the industrial city of the Olivetti, UNESCO heritage since 2018.

8 places to visit in Ivrea, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers 4 of the 5 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Open Ivrea in the guide with map and filters

Città industriale del XX secolo – MaAM

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Olivetti's industrial and socio-cultural project, founded in 1908 by Camillo Olivetti: along Jervis street the path of about 2 km of the open-air Museum of Modern Architecture among the ICO Workshops in red bricks, Talponia, the kindergarten and the canteen, with the UNESCO Visitor Centre.

  • Where Via Guglielmo Jervis, Ivrea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismotorino.org

Duomo di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the upper part of the city, built from the year 1000 in Romanesque style on the foundations of a Roman temple of the first century BC; next to the Palazzo del Vescovado of fourteenth century origin.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Ivrea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello Sabaudo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The trapezoidal castle with its red brick crenellated corner towers, sung by Carducci as the castle

  • Where Piazza Castello, Ivrea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Anfiteatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

The first century AD, with the axis greater than 65 meters and more than 10,000 places, of which some parts remain.

  • Where Ivrea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Civico Pier Alessandro Garda

Museums · City history

In Piazza Ottinetti, in the former 14th-century convent of Santa Chiara, three collections on the history of the city.

  • Where Piazza Ottinetti, Ivrea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

  • Where Ivrea
