What to see in Alviano

Umbrian village on the Tiber Valley, linked to the leader Bartolomeo d'Alviano and dominated by the castle Doria Pamphili, now town hall and seat of three museums; at the foot of the artificial lake born in 1963 with the dam on the Tiber, today WWF oasis of about 900 hectares loved by birdwatchers.

4 places to visit in Alviano, in the province of Terni (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Doria Pamphili (Rocca di Alviano)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Quadrangular fortress with four corner towers and a double loggia Renaissance courtyard, rebuilt and enlarged by Bartolomeo d'Alviano on the core of an older castle (the first plant dates back to the 10th century). Purchased in 1654 by Donna Olimpia Pamphili Maidalchini, it owes its name to the Doria Pamphili, the last private owners, and since 1920 it has been a municipality. On the courtyard opens the Chapel of the Rondini with 17th century frescoes of the life of St. Francis, including the saint who asks for silence to swallows.

Musei del Castello di Alviano

Museums · City history

In the fortress are located the historical and multimedia Museum of Bartolomeo d'Alviano and the captains of Ventura Umbria, the Museum of peasant civilization and the Centre of Faunistic Documentation of the Oasis of Alviano.

Oasi WWF Lago di Alviano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Protected area of about 900 hectares managed by WWF Italy around the artificial lake born in 1963 with the dam on the Tiber; a nature path with birdwatching huts, observation towers, wooden walkways and an outdoor classroom allows you to observe flora and fauna.
