What to see in Casalbore

The village of western Irpinia on the valleys of the Miscano and Cervaro, Casalbore symbolizes the Norman Tower with the castle that encircles it, and preserves the italic temple of the third century BC, the only testimony of the Sannitico sacred building found in Irpinia.

Area archeologica del Tempio Italico

History and architecture · Archaeology

Temple of Hellenistic age dated to the third century BC, in a cult area frequented at least from the sixth century BC: almost square cell, access to steps flanked by two fountains fed by lead pipes, floors and painted walls. Votive deposits suggest a dedication to the goddess Mephyte; it was destroyed during the Second Punic War (about 217-215 BC). It is the only testimony of a Sannitic sacred building found in Irpinia.

can be visited only on request, reservation required to the Municipality (0825 849005)

Castello e Torre Normanna

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The tower of the twelfth century, at the highest point of the village, is the only survivor: square base with base to escarpment and the Beneventana Port, ancient entrance on the road to Benevento; watched over the valley of the Miscano and the border between the domains of Benevento and Puglia. It is surrounded by a defensive castle in Gothic style, in local grey stone, with five entrance doors, repeatedly damaged by earthquakes and transformed over the centuries.

visits by reservation (0825 849005, info@comune.casalbore.av.it)

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museums · Themed

Centro storico e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center grew around the fortified nucleus, with houses in limestone, medieval arches and narrow alleys, and is crossed by the Tratturo Pescasseroli-Candela. Among the residences, the seventeenth-century Palazzo Maraviglia with wooden portal of local manufacture and family coat of arms, and the Palaces Gallo, Caracciolo, Bruno and de Matteis, the latter with a Gothic monophora.

  • Where Centro storico, Casalbore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sistemairpinia.provincia.avellino.it
