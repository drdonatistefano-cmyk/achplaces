What to see in Civita

Borgo arbëreshë 450 meters in the Pollino National Park, inside the Raganello Gorges Reserve, repopulated by Albanian refugees at the end of the fifteenth century: comignoli and speaking houses Kodra, churches of Byzantine rite, the ethnic museum and the Devil's Bridge on the Raganello.

Centro storico, comignoli e case Kodra

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

It rises on the site of the Latin village "Castrum Sancti Salvatoris," abandoned after the earthquake of 1465 and repopulated by refugees from southern Albania, who renamed it Civita. An itinerary touches the fifty of historical comignoli (probably built between the end of the seventeenth and early twentieth centuries, each designed by the foreman and considered capable of keeping away evil spirits) and the "speaking houses" of Kodra, with facades reminiscent of a human face, dedicated to the Albanian painter Ibrahim Kodra. The district of Sant'Antonio, the oldest, of medieval origin, has hanging ovens, loggias and the chapel of Sant'Antonio; in the Gjitonia, squares where the alleys cross, the inhabitants are found.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Civita
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Baroque mother church of the second half of the sixteenth century, of Byzantine rite, oriented to the east, with iconostasis and Greek altar; the icons of Christ Pantocrator and of the Virgin Odigitria are of Alfonso Caccese, those of the twelve feasts of the year come from Athens.

  • Where Civita
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Museo etnico Arbëresh

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1989, on two floors and four rooms (welcome, frame, totem, costume): Albanian costumes, a photographic exhibition on the countries of Italy, iconography of Byzantine rites and a library of Albanian texts. Among the notable pieces is an icon of Our Lady Odigitria and notes of a storyteller on the history of Skanderbeg.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Civita
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museoetnicoarbresh.it

Sources: museoetnicoarbresh.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Ecomuseo del paesaggio della Valle del Raganello

Museums · Themed

Permanent institute based at Palazzo Castellano, which, with the participation of the inhabitants, studies and enhances the natural and cultural heritage of the valley, considering the entire territory as a museum diffused in the landscape and traditions.

  • Where Palazzo Castellano, Civita
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Ponte del Diavolo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

A 36-metre-long donkey-back bridge that crosses the Raganello stream with a single arch; symbol of the Pollino Park, its construction is linked to myths and legends.

  • Where Torrente Raganello, Civita
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Gole Basse del Raganello

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Forra in the Raganello Gorges Nature Reserve, in the Pollino National Park, rich in biodiversity, with habitats for large raptors and rare plants; a naturalistic path follows them, with walls that recall the Dante's bolges.

  • Where Riserva Gole del Raganello, Civita
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it
