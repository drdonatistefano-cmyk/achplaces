What to see in Rutigliano

Country of the hills of the Murgia Barese born around the year 1000, with the ancient village dominated by the Norman Tower, the collegiate of Santa Maria della Colonna and San Nicola and a tradition of terracotta whistles told by the museum and the fair in January; in the territory the finds of the ancient Azetium peucezia.

6 places to visit in Rutigliano, in the province of Bari (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre Normanna

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Of the four towers of the Norman castle, demolished around 1600, there are two remaining: the Norman Tower, almost intact, dominates the ancient village and offers a beautiful view over all Rutigliano. Guided tours during the year, access by reservation.

  • Where Piazza Kennedy, Rutigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rutigliano.ba.it

Sources: comune.rutigliano.ba.it

Collegiata di Santa Maria della Colonna e San Nicola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Three-nave church with Romanesque bell tower, awarded the Blue Shield for the protection of cultural heritage: it houses the fourteenth-century icon of the Madonna delle Grazie and numerous frescoes.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Rutigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rutigliano.ba.it

Sources: comune.rutigliano.ba.it

Museo capitolare Santa Maria della Colonna e San Nicola

Museums · Old masters

In a 19th-century palace donated by the Settanni family, on the path of the ancient moat and overlooking the course: on the first floor there are rooms with painted ceilings for exhibitions of the capitular heritage, the second didactic workshops and restoration of wood; other paintings are exhibited in the ancient sacristies of the Matrice church.

  • Where Corso Mazzini, Rutigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rutigliano.ba.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.rutigliano.ba.it

Museo del fischietto in terracotta «Domenico Divella»

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Established in 2004 in the Palazzo San Domenico, a former Dominican convent of the end of the sixteenth century with the church of Santa Maria del Rosario, it preserves hundreds of whistles made by Italian artists for the National Competition linked to the whistle fair in January, for the feast of Saint Anthony Abate. Visit only by reservation (Pro Loco).

  • Where Via Leopoldo Tarantini 28, Rutigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rutigliano.ba.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.rutigliano.ba.it

Museo civico archeologico «Grazia e Pietro Di Donna»

Museums · Archaeological

It collects the finds of the ancient Azetium peucezia from the Neolithic to the Early Middle Ages, with the collections donated Colamusi, Diogguardi, Didonna and Catamo: accessories of the necropolis of Azezius and Purgatory (over 400 tombs between VII century BC and XI AD), a dolmenic tomb of about 3500 BC and traces of the Byzantine and Longobard settlement of Sant'Apollinare.

temporarily closed to the public for organisational and management reasons (MiC)

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre, Rutigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rutigliano.ba.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.rutigliano.ba.it

Ruderi della chiesa di Sant'Apollinare

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Ruins of a medieval church of the X-XI centuries with a single nave with domes in axis and apse to the east, last phase of a settlement between the VI and the X-XI century, in an area with a vast necropolis; bound since 1975. It is reached from a small road on the provincial road 122 Rutigliano-Turi.

private property ruin (MiC)

  • Where Contrada Bigetti/Purgatorio, Rutigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rutigliano.ba.it

Sources: beniabbandonati.cultura.gov.it · comune.rutigliano.ba.it
