What to see in Morra De Sanctis

Borgo dell'Alta Irpinia at 863 meters above the valley of Ofanto, home to critic Francesco De Sanctis (1817-1883) from which it took its name in 1934; the castle of the Biondi Morra princes dominates the mother church and houses the archaeological museum Antiquarium.

Castello dei Principi Biondi Morra

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Longobard fortification of the IX-X century, cited in 1137 when the Norman Roberto Morra pursued the abbot Rainaldo of Montecassino; he passed to the Giamvilla and the Caracciolo, who at the beginning of the seventeenth century made it a residence, and then returned to the Morra. Damaged by the earthquake of 1695, by the French looting of 1799, by the fire of 1911 that destroyed the library and by the earthquake of 1980, in the eighties it was donated to the municipality. The stone façade remains and part of the two circular towers at the entrance; in the atrium there is a Roman milestone of the I-III century.

Museo civico Antiquarium

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in June 2007, it presents archaeological research on the territory: at the center is the necropolis of Piano (Campo dei) Cerasuoli, with tombs of the 8th-VII century BC of the culture of Oliveto Citra-Cairano. Female sets of rank with fibula and bronze bracelets, male accessories with weapons, a ritual axe and vases with wolf protomes, totemic animal of the Irpini, a Sannitic belt, attic ceramics and materials of the inhabitants of Piano dei Tivoli, excavated between 1979 and 1985. Irpinia system indicates opening Monday-Friday 9-12 (Tuesday and Thursday 16-18 also), Saturday and Sunday 9-13.

Casa natale di Francesco De Sanctis

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

In the historic centre, Palazzo De Sanctis, with its typical stone portal, is the house where Francesco De Sanctis was born on 28 March 1817, a critic and historian of literature. A Museum of Memories was also set up in the context of the Literary Park "Francesco De Sanctis" (1999-2001) with a small collection of its memorabilia.

Centro storico e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The centre, at the foot of the castle, conserves historic buildings: among the most interesting are the eighteenth-century Palazzo Molinari, known for its stonework, and then Palazzo De Rogatis, Palazzo Del Buono and Palazzo Donatelli, with numerous churches including those of Monte Carmelo, the Presentation of Maria and San Rocco.
