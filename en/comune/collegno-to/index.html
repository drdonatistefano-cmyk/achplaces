What to see in Collegno

Cities at the gates of Turin with two complexes of great interest: the Royal Certosa, seventeenth-century monastery expanded by Filippo Juvarra and then psychiatric hospital until 1978, today the heart of the city park, and the Leumann Village, Art Nouveau Workers' Quarter wanted at the end of the nineteenth century by Napoleone Leumann. In the historic centre the Castle Provana, the civic tower and the churches of San Lorenzo, Santa Croce and San Massimo.

Villaggio Leumann

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

Worker's district built in the late nineteenth century by the Swiss entrepreneur Napoleone Leumann and designed to be self-sufficient: houses, boarding house for workers, public baths, theater, surgery, post office, hotel, kindergarten, schools, church, club and dealership. It preserves the historic station of the Turin-Rivoli line, the old primary school and several restored Art Nouveau buildings, and is still inhabited by a hundred families. Guided tours on scheduled dates (with entrance to the Village Museum): €17, reduced €8.

Chiesa di Santa Elisabetta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Catholic Church of the Leumann Village, wanted by Napoleon Leumann for his employees although he was Protestant, and is still in use. Designed in an eclectic style by the architect Pietro Fenoglio; windows and interior decorations are by a team of painters and decorators directed by Prof. Poirino emery.

Certosa Reale

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Monastery commissioned in the seventeenth century by Cristina di Francia on the pre-existing Palazzo di Bernardino (1614), on a project by Maurizio Valperga; the eighteenth-century extension and the portal are by Filippo Juvarra. The main cloister, of 1719, measures 70 by 54 meters and has a portico of 112 columns in stone of Chianocco on which the fifteen cells of the monks opened; on the whole also the church of the SS. Annunziata con le Tombe dei Cavalieri e l'Aula Hospitalis. From 1856 psychiatric hospital (the one of the "memorato" of Collegno) until 1978, it is now restored and houses offices, university classrooms and events such as Follia in Fiore.

Parco Generale Carlo Alberto Dalla Chiesa

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Main city park, of about 400,000 m2, around the Royal Certosa: wide meadows, ornamental trees such as the Certosa horse chestnut, cedars, lindens and plane trees, and the Viali della Pace dedicated to figures such as Gandhi and Emmeline Pankhurst. They nest card, finches, robins and major red peaks; play areas, sports fields and dog area. Reachable by metro (stop Fermi).

  • Where Viale Martiri XXX Aprile, Collegno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.collegno.to.it

Sources: comune.collegno.to.it

Castello Provana

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built around 1171 by Umberto III of Savoia, destroyed in the 13th century and rebuilt, then enlarged in the mid-sixteenth century; since 1599 it belongs to the Provanas, to which Charles Emanuel I granted the feud and the comital title of Collegno. The architecture is attributed to Filippo Juvarra, the current facade is by Giuseppe Talucchi. The park opens to the public during the patron saint of San Lorenzo.

  • Where Via Alpignano 2, Collegno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Centro storico, Balconetti e torre civica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Next to the chapel of the Madonnina (already existing in 1581, rebuilt in 1791, with a rare bell tower with a triangular plan) the Balconies, ...The Balcunets, offer a view of the center and go down with a staircase to the old bridge, now pedestrian; the name comes from the Dojra gate of 1356. In 1741 the municipality added a civic tower above the bell tower of Santa Croce, church of 1714 with an organ of the nineteenth century. In Piazza IV Novembre the Art Nouveau fountain of 1904 by Giacomo Guglielmotti, dedicated to Napoleon Leumann.

  • Where Piazza IV Novembre e Via Matteotti, Collegno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.collegno.to.it

Sources: comune.collegno.to.it

Chiesa di San Lorenzo Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Rebuilt from 1772 after the demolition of the original church in 1650; Count Giuseppe Provana financed side arms and choir, which gave her the Latin crossed plant. Today it is a unique hall with four side chapels and preserves eighteenth century wooden works by Stefano Maria Clemente.

Chiesa di San Massimo Vescovo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Dedicated to San Massimo, the first bishop of Turin, has origins in the first centuries after Christ: the restoration of 1949 revealed a primitive phase of the fifth century, a Romanesque (X-XI century) and a Baroque (XVIII century). He keeps a wooden statue of the saint.
