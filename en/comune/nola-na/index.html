What to see in Nola

Ancient town of the Campania plain at the foot of Vesuvius, known for the Festa dei Gigli: a museum that tells the territory from prehistory to the Middle Ages and the remains of a village of the Bronze Age buried by an eruption.

Museo storico archeologico di Nola

Museums · Archaeological

In a restored former convent, it traces the territory from the eruptions of the Somma-Vesuvius and from the Bronze Age to the origins of Nola, to the Sannites with the painted tombs and the reproduction of the Knight's Tomb, to the Roman age with the statue of Dionysus with panther from the Villa of Somma Vesuviana, to the basilicas of Cimitile.

  • Where Via Senatore Cocozza 2, Nola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · napoliforme.comune.napoli.it

Area archeologica di San Paolo Belsito

History and architecture · Archaeology

Excavations started in 1995 on a settlement stratified by the Bronze Age in the Roman era; at the museum the cast of a person fleeing the eruption of the Pomici di Avellino.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Villaggio preistorico di Croce del Papa

History and architecture · Archaeology
