What to see in Lungro

Centro arbëreshë seat of the Byzantine rite eparchy, with the Cathedral of St. Nicholas of Mira, the gjitonies gathered around the squares and one of the oldest salt mines in Europe.

4 places to visit in Lungro, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo storico della Miniera di Salgemma

Museums · Themed

The mine, already mentioned by Pliny the Elder and exploited by Greeks, Romans and Normans, then became Royal Mine of Salgemma and closed in 1976; today of the Municipality, it has been restored and since 2010 it houses the museum with documents and finds.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
