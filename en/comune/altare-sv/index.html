What to see in Altare

The glass village of Val Bormida: the first furnaces date back to the 11th century and the Statuti of the University of Glass in 1495; today the tradition lives in the Museum of Altar Art of Villa Rosa, in the glass shops and in the churches of the medieval village, with the 17th century parish church of Sant'Eugenius.

Villa Rosa, Museo dell'Arte Vetraria Altarese

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Villa Liberty designed by Nicolò Campora at the beginning of the twentieth century, purchased by the Ministry in 1992 and restored, houses the museum of the history of the glass altar: works from the eighteenth century to today, tools for craftsmanship and multimedia stations on the processing phases. In the garden a demonstration furnace of about 70 m2, with two melting furnaces and a tempera oven, active since 2000 and often lit during the year. Open Tuesday-Sunday 14-18, closed on Mondays.

  • Where Piazza del Consolato, Altare
  • Entry €7, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodelvetro.org

Sources: museodelvetro.org · cultura.gov.it · lamialiguria.it

Botteghe dei vetrai

Local life and traditions · Crafts

In the workshop I Vetri di Sandro Bormioli we can see the craftsmanship of the incandescent glass; the Artistic Showcase Amantzio Bormioli produces blown or shaped objects in borosilicate glass (trophys, furnishings, sacred art, animals) and welcomes tourist and school groups on weekdays (tel. 0109 58254).

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Eugenio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the historic center of the medieval village, it was built between 1620 and 1650 and modified especially in 1875; the interior has a single nave with four side chapels on the side and a large presbytery, the facade with two small bell towers was partly rebuilt in 1901.

  • Where Centro storico, Altare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lamialiguria.it

Sources: comune.altare.sv.it · lamialiguria.it

Chiesa della Santissima Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It rises on the foundations of the first parish church of the village, erected in the middle of the twelfth century by the Benedictine monks of Lerino of the Cenobe of Bergeggi and then named after Saint Eugenio; the current church was erected in 1650 by the will of the Altarese glassmaker Matteo Buzzone.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di Nostra Signora dei Prati

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Erected by the inhabitants around the year 1000 and dedicated to the nascent Mary, it preserves frescoes of local taste that the tradition binds to the stories of the Saints Nazarius and Celsus; in war it was a burning room for soldiers, then warehouse, and returned to worship in 1992.

  • Where Altare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lamialiguria.it

Sources: comune.altare.sv.it · lamialiguria.it

Oratori di San Sebastiano e San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The Renaissance Oratory of San Sebastiano (XVI century), with the bell tower rebuilt after the earthquake of 1887, was the provisional seat of the Glass Museum until 2004. The oratory of San Rocco, erected in 1590 and originally dedicated to Saint Filiberto abbot, protector of glassmakers, was retitled after the plague of 1628-1631 and restored in 2008: it preserves the statues of the two saints, and the seventeenth-century one of San Rocco comes out in procession on 16 August.

  • Where Altare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
