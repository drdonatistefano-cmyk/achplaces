What to see in Calcata

The "borgo degli artisti" is on a tufa spur in the Treja Valley Park, among the naturally fortified most evocative villages in Italy.

Borgo di Calcata

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Abandoned at the beginning of the twentieth century and then reborn thanks to the artists, with winding pedestrian alleys overlooking the reddish tuff holes, shops and houses dug in the rock.

  • Where Calcata
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com
