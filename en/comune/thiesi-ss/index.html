What to see in Thiesi

The Meilogu agropastoral centre of about 3,000 inhabitants, 40 km from Sassari and known for its cheeses: the Gothic-Aragonese parish church of Santa Vittoria, the sanctuary of the Madonna of Seunis on the valley of the river Molinu, the collection of graphics by Aligi Sassu and, about ten kilometers away, the painting tomb by Mandra Antine.

Parrocchiale di Santa Vittoria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built between the fifteenth and 1590 (date reported by the nineteenth-century parish priests) in late-Gothic forms with Renaissance motifs, on the model of San Giorgio di Pozzomaggiore: façade with cusp between buttresses and square bell tower, rosette and portal to the sixth, on whose architrave is carved a niche frieze, similar to the predella of a Catalan retablo, with Christ at the column between the Virgin, Saints Peter, Paul, Victoria and James. Inside a nave in four spans with vaults and the apse with key hanging depicting the patron saint.

  • Where Piazza Santa Vittoria, Thiesi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · idese.cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Collezione permanente Aligi Sassu

Museums · Modern art

Museum inaugurated in 2010 by the Municipality and the Sassu Foundation in the country where the painter (Milano 1912-Pollença 2000) spent part of his childhood: 120 graphic works from 1929-1995 donated by the heirs, among aqueforts, aquatinte, pricks and lithographs, with the folders of the Orlando Furioso (1974), of the Fallen Horses (1973), the engravings on the Apocalypse and the five lithographs Homage to Sardinia with verses by Sebastiano Satta. In the nucleus also the two mural cycles of the sixties, the Angevin Moti , on the antifeudal motions of the late eighteenth century of which Thiesi was the center promoter, and ... The Life and Nature.

Closed for renovation and installation, undefined reopening (SardiniaCultura)

  • Where Via Garau 53, Thiesi
  • Entry To be checked (for a fee)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Santuario della Madonna di Seunis

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

An eighteenth-century shrine, then renovated, on the outskirts of the village on a rocky spike overlooking the valley of the river Molinu, a destination of pilgrimages. The festival, the most important of Thiesi, is held in the first days of September with mass, panegyric, costume procession, folk groups and knights of the Meilogu.

  • Where Periferia di Thiesi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · comune.thiesi.ss.it

Necropoli di Mandra Antine

History and architecture · Archaeology

About 10 km from the village, four domus de Janas excavated in a trachytic ridge; the tomb III, called "painted" preserves colorful frescoes of the Neolithic (3rd millennium BC).

  • Where Località Mandra Antine, territorio di Thiesi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Grotta di Monte Majore

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Cave with large entrance on the eastern side of the mountain and short and easy descent: it develops for 300 meters, with concretions and a dome room, and houses a colony of about 5.000 bats.

  • Where Monte Majore, territorio di Thiesi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Torre-prigione aragonese

History and architecture · Towers

Tower built by the Aragonese between the 15th and 16th centuries for defense and used in the Sabauda era as a prison.

  • Where Thiesi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
