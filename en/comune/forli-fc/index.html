What to see in Forlì

Piazza Saffi with San Mercuriale, the Musei San Domenico with the Ebe di Canova and the great exhibitions, the Rocca di Ravaldino and a district of rationalist architecture unique in Italy.

25 places to visit in Forlì, in the province of Forlì-Cesena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 5 of the 12 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Musei San Domenico

Museums · Old masters

In the former Dominican convent, the Pinacoteca civica, with the Ebe by Antonio Canova, and the great art exhibitions of national breath.

  • Where Piazza Guido da Montefeltro 12, Forlì
  • Entry To be checked: the price changes according to the current exhibition
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.comune.forli.fc.it

Campanile di San Mercuriale

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The Romanesque bell tower of Lombard type over 70 meters high, erected in 1178-1180 by master Aliotto next to the basilica-abbey.

It only goes up on special occasions: Feast of Our Lady of Fire (February 4), St. Mercury and other events
