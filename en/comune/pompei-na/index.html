What to see in Pompei

The Roman city buried by the eruption of Vesuvius of 79 AD, with the Villa dei Misteri and the House of Fauno, is with Herculaneum and Oplontis UNESCO Heritage; the modern city is a pilgrimage destination for the Shrine of the Blessed Virgin of the Rosary founded by Bartolo Longo.

8 places to visit in Pompei, in the province of Naples (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Area archeologica di Pompei

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

On 66 hectares, about 50 excavated, the ashes and lapilli of the eruption of 79 AD have preserved houses, shops, the Forum, sacred areas, thermal baths, the theaters district and the amphitheatre; you enter from Porta Marina. Rediscovered in the sixteenth century during the excavation of the channel of the Earl of Sarno, it was excavated by the Bourbons (Tempio di Iside, Casa del Fauno); Giuseppe Fiorelli in 1858 divided it into regions and islandes and introduced the casts of the victims. Closed only on December 25th.

  • Where Via Plinio, Pompei
  • Entry €20, reduced €2; free on the first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website pompeiisites.org
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets

Sources: pompeiisites.org · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Villa dei Misteri

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Villa outside the excavation area, so called for a room overlooking the sea with a large fresco on three walls that depicts a mystery rite linked to the Dionysian cult, among the most preserved ancient works.

  • Where Via Villa dei Misteri, Pompei
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it · pompeiisites.org

Casa del Fauno

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

One of the largest residences in Pompeii, about 3,000 square meters, excavated in the Bourbon age and famous for the mosaic of the Battle of Alessandro.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: italia.it · pompeiisites.org

Foro di Pompei

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The public heart of the ancient city, surrounded by the Capitolium, the Basilica, temples, public spas, theatres and amphitheatre; on the walls of the city also political graffiti, linked to the elections scheduled for the days before the eruption.

  • Where Area archeologica, Pompei
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: italia.it · pompeiisites.org

Pontificio Santuario della Beata Vergine del Santo Rosario

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Wanted by Bartolo Longo, first stone on May 8, 1876, consecrated in 1891 and expanded between 1934 and 1939 with a Latin cross and three naves; the facade, of 1901, is dedicated to universal peace. At the high altar the Madonna of Pompeii, seventeenth-century painting of Luca Giordano's school, arrived in 1875 on a manure wagon, surrounded by the Mysteries of the Rosary on copper; the bell tower of about 80 meters can be climbed for the panorama. About two million pilgrims a year.

  • Where Piazza Bartolo Longo 1, Pompei
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.pompei.na.it · italia.it

Teatro di Costanzo

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Art nouveau theater of the 1920s, consolidated after the 1980 earthquake and reopened in 1992; 120 square meters stage and room for almost 500 seats.

  • Where Via Sacra 37, Pompei
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.pompei.na.it · italia.it

Fonte Salutare

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Ancient little sanctuary built around a source considered beneficial, where the pilgrims left offerings and prayed for healing, today a well-kept public park and quiet behind the Town Hall.

  • Where Via Sacra 8, Pompei
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
