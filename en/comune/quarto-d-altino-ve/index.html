What to see in Quarto d'Altino

Municipality on the Sile at the edge of the Venetian lagoon, in whose territory stood the Roman city of Altinum, on the Via Annia: today the National Museum and the archaeological areas of Altino, part of the UNESCO site "Venice and its lagoon," the seventeenth-century basin of Portegrandi and the naturalistic itineraries of the Sile Park.

Museo nazionale di Altino

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

It rises in the vast archaeological area of the Roman city of Altinum, along the Via Annia on the edge of the lagoon, in an area that is part of the UNESCO site "Venice and its lagoon"; it exhibits over 40,000 pieces that document the frequentation of the territory in prehistoric, Venetian and Roman times. Open from Tuesday to Saturday 8-19 and Sundays and holidays 14-19 (ticket office until 18.30), closed on Mondays; cumulative ticket with Portogruaro and Concordia Sagittaria €10 and annual subscription - Altino 365 Â€15.

Area archeologica di Altino

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The Roman city of Altinum extended for about a square kilometre, with hole, basilica, theatre, amphitheatre and spa; after the invasions of the seventh century the inhabitants took refuge in the islands of the lagoon, especially in Torcello. Today in Altino you can visit two open-air archaeological areas, one just a few steps from the museum; the ticket is that of the Archaeological Park (museum and area).

the archaeological area is open from April to October and cannot be visited in case of bad weather

  • Where Via S. Eliodoro, Altino, Quarto d'Altino
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.comune.quartodaltino.ve.it

Sources: comune.quartodaltino.ve.it · museiveneto.cultura.gov.it

Chiesa di Sant'Eliodoro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of Altino, which is still the seat of the bishop. The first church, built around 1850 at the villa of the Counts Bacchini, served the population until 1961, when the new Romanesque-inspired complex designed by the architect Forlati was built, on an area considered the center of the ancient city; a portico on three sides connects it to the museum and opens south towards Torcello.

  • Where Via Eliodoro 39, Altino, Quarto d'Altino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.quartodaltino.ve.it

Sources: comune.quartodaltino.ve.it

Conca di Portegrandi

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

A navigation basin on the Sile built around 1680 diverting the last stretch of the river, with doors built between 1682 and 1684; next is still exposed the stone plate of Istria of 1684 with the tariff for boats. It was the real center of Portegrandi until 1960; near there is a large marina from which you can reach the lagoon and its islands.

  • Where Via Trezze, Portegrandi, Quarto d'Altino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.quartodaltino.ve.it

Sources: comune.quartodaltino.ve.it

Fiume Sile e Alzaia

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Sile, 90 km long and one of the longest rivers in Europe, crosses the municipality and is protected by the Regional Natural Park of the river Sile (about 4,152 hectares in 11 municipalities). The Alzaia is a bike path on the banks from the bridge of Musestre to the basin of Portegrandi, part of the Girasile greenway; the river is navigable by boat, canoe and kayak.

  • Where Alzaia del Sile, Quarto d'Altino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.quartodaltino.ve.it

Sources: comune.quartodaltino.ve.it

Oasi di Trepalade

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Small natural environment along the Sile between Quarto d'Altino and Portegrandi, reachable by bike Girasile and equipped with a service center; can be visited all year round by reservation with the Ornithological Association Basso Piave.

Palude di San Michele Vecchio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Environmental nature trail in a botanical and ornithological area, site SIC and SPA, where lives the rare carnivorous plant Utricularia vulgaris; managed by the association A.S.T.E.A., with guided tours on request (tel. 339 5247689).
