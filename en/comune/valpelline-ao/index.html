What to see in Valpelline

Green basin of meadows and orchards at 960 meters, home of the Fontina DOP seasoned in the rock of an ancient copper mine.

Museo della Fontina e centro visitatori

Museums · Themed

It tells the history and production of the Fontina DOP; the visit includes the ripening warehouses carved into the rock, one inside the ancient copper mine.
