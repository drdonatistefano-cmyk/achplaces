What to see in Caltagirone

The town of ceramics in Val di Noto, a UNESCO World Heritage Site for Baroque rebuilt after the earthquake of 1693: the staircase of Santa Maria del Monte with its majolica lift, the Regional Museum of Ceramics and a hundred workshops.

8 places to visit in Caltagirone, in the province of Catania (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Caltagirone in the guide with map and filters

Scalinata di Santa Maria del Monte

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

130 meters long, 142 steps built from 1606 to join the lower city to the mother church; from 1954 the raised ones are covered with majolica that reproduce in chronological order decorations from the X to the XX century. In May it hosts the Infiorata and on 24 and 25 July, for San Giacomo, it lights up with colorful lights.

  • Where Caltagirone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: smarteducationunescosicilia.it · italia.it · visitsicily.info

Museo Regionale della Ceramica

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 1965, the Sicilian ceramics from prehistory to the early twentieth century, from Greek and Roman artifacts to medieval, Baroque and nineteenth-century works; among the main pieces a crater of the fifth century BC with a workshop of ceramist and Athena Ergane.

  • Where Via Giardini Pubblici, Caltagirone
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
