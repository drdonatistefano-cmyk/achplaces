What to see in Ventotene

Small island of the Pontine archipelago, exile place of the daughter of Augustus and the anti-fascist confino where the Manifesto of Ventotene was born: Roman port dug into the tuff, remains of Villa Giulia at Punta Eolo and backdrops of the Marine Protected Area, with in front of Santo Stefano and its Bourbon prison.

10 places to visit in Ventotene, in the province of Latina (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Ventotene in the guide with map and filters

Porto Romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Port excavated entirely in the rock in the Roman age (1st century BC-I century AD), on average three meters deep and sheltered from winds and marshes, with tanks for live fish and premises for repairs and deposits; in the caves today diving, restaurants and bars.

  • Where Via Olivi, Ventotene
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Area archeologica di Villa Giulia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Remains of the first century A.D. Roman villa that occupied the north side up to Punta Eolo; according to tradition the daughter of the Emperor Augustus was exiled there.

  • Where Punta Eolo, Ventotene
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Museo storico-archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

In the Bourbon castle, it preserves the most significant finds of the wrecks of the Roman age.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Ventotene
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Museo della Migrazione e Osservatorio ornitologico

Museums · Themed

It tells of the spring and autumn migration of birds, the role of the island and the historical relationship between islanders and migrants, with dioramas and models of birds of life size; about 1.5 km from the center. Admission fee, 10-12 and 18-20 hours.

Touring indicates closed March-May and September-November and varying periods in winter

  • Where Via Olivi, Ventotene
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: touringclub.it · visitlazio.com

Cisterne romane

History and architecture · Archaeology

They collected rainwater; from the 5th century AD they were inhabited by monks and hermits, then jailed under Ferdinand IV of Bourbon and air-raid shelter in the Second World War.

  • Where Ventotene
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Peschiere romane e Faro

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

From Cala Nave, walking on the cliffs, you reach the lighthouse with a view of the ancient port and the Roman fish ponds dug into the volcanic tuff, visible also in snorkeling.

  • Where Faro, Ventotene
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Paese, piazza Castello e chiesa di Santa Candida

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The modern center was built in the eighteenth century by Antonio Winspeare and Francesco Carpi, with piazza del Comune (Piazza Castello), the church of Santa Candida and Piazza De Gasperi. Spinelli, Rossi and Colorni wrote the Ventotene Manifesto in 1941-42; in 2022 the island received the European Heritage Mark.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Ventotene
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Area marina protetta Isole di Ventotene e Santo Stefano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Posidonia meadows up to about 40 meters with over 350 species of invertebrates and fish, caves with octopus and moray eels and Roman wrecks: one in Cala Rossano in very low water, one in Punta dell'Arco and five over 100 meters between the two islands.

  • Where Piazza Castello 1, Ventotene
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Cala Rossano e Cala Nave

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Cala Rossano, in the basin of the new port, has clear sand mixed with pebbles and turquoise water between high cliffs, with umbrellas and sunbeds; Cala Nave looks at the faraglioni Earth Ship and Outside Ship. The coves to the south and west (Cala Battaglia, Parata Grande, Capo dell'Arco) are less accessible.

  • Where Ventotene
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Isola di Santo Stefano e carcere borbonico

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

The island is very close to Ventotene, reachable by boat, with the Bourbon prison built in 1795 by Ferdinando I delle Due Sicilie and active until 1965. Land at Marinella (the only sheltered), Burrone, Punta dei Sighi and Bath Giulia.
