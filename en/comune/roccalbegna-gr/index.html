What to see in Roccalbegna

Medieval village of the upper valley of the Albegna, on the slopes of Mount Amiata, dominated by the spur of the Stone; in the square the church of Saints Peter and Paul with the triptych of Ambrogio Lorenzetti and, behind, the Oratory of the Crucifix; in the territory the village of Cana, the castle of Triana, the Museum of the Focarazza of Saint Catherine and the reserves of Pescinello and Bosco Rocconi.

Borgo di Roccalbegna e Torre civica

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village perched, possession of the Aldobrandeschi, then of the Republic of Siena, Medici stronghold on the Amiata and part of the county of Santa Fiora, became free municipality of the Grand Duchy of Tuscany in the eighteenth century. In the central square there is the town hall, the church of Saints Peter and Paul and the small Civic Tower with clock; from the Porta di Maremma you go out towards the church of Our Lady and the Cassero.

  • Where Piazza centrale, Roccalbegna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the central square; a collapse of the ground visibly inclined the portal architrave, giving the church an unblemished appearance. It preserves the triptych of Ambrogio Lorenzetti (1340), with the Madonna and Child and Saints Peter and Paul, a Deposition of Salimbeni and the Madonna of the Rosary by Domenico Beccafumi.

  • Where Piazza centrale, Roccalbegna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Oratorio del Santissimo Crocifisso (raccolta d'arte)

Museums · Old masters

Behind the church of Saints Peter and Paul, the oratory is now a museum: there is the Crucifix on a shaped table of the Sienese Luca di Tommè (about 1360) and there are often works by Francesco Nasini and Sebastiano Folli.

  • Where Dietro la chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo, Roccalbegna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

La Pietra e il Cassero

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Stone is the mast that dominates the village, the most famous place of Roccalbegna: you arrive on a steep road and then on narrow steps dug into the rock. The Cassero, behind the church of the Madonna, is an open panoramic space.

  • Where La Pietra, Roccalbegna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Cana

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small medieval village of alleys around a central square, with stretches of the ancient walls of the castle and wide views of woods, olive groves and valley of the Albegna. It was of the Aldobrandeschi, then of Siena and the Medici; in the square the Medici cistern of the seventeenth century collected rainwater with a system of pipes, and in the village rise the churches of San Martino and Madonna del Conforto. Every year medieval festival and, in autumn, Bindina festival with roasted chestnuts.

  • Where Cana, Roccalbegna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Castello di Triana

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Castle near Roccalbegna passed from the Aldobrandeschi to the Piccolomini of Siena in 1388; it consists of two large buildings and a mast, mostly covered with ivy.

Museo della Focarazza di Santa Caterina

Museums · Themed

In the former shop of the smith ferraio of the hamlet, documents the work and rites of the fire and the tree of the Amiata in two sections: tools for forging and objects of domestic fire, and the Focarazza, the rite for Saint Catherine of Alexandria of the evening of November 24, when the men of the districts contend between smoke and heat the "stollo," the pole of cerro in the center of the cadasta. Photos, folk art, books and a collection of traditional children's games.

  • Where SS323 n. 239, Santa Caterina, Roccalbegna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.museidimaremma.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: museidimaremma.it · visittuscany.com

Riserva Naturale di Pescinello

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

A 149-hectare reserve on the left bank of the Albegna high valley, which protects the southern slopes of the limestone complex of Monte Labbro: woods, shrubs, limestone cliffs, springs, pools and porches, with large centuries-old trees such as black hornbeam, oak and linden. Reintroduction projects for red partridges, vulture heads and river prawns; a wet area was created for the billon by the yellow belly.

  • Where Alta valle dell'Albegna, Roccalbegna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Oasi WWF Bosco Rocconi

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Reserve overlooking the river Albegna, important for birdlife: it hosts rare birds of prey such as biancon, lodolaio, sparrow and lanario.

  • Where Valle dell'Albegna, Roccalbegna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Lungo il fiume Albegna

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

A dirt track follows the river southwards: the first about 3 km you can drive or mountain bike, the rest on foot on the gravel bed, between poplars and willows, with blackbirds and fishermen martins.
