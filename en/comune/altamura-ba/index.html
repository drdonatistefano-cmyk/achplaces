What to see in Altamura

The city of DOP bread on the Alta Murgia, founded by Federico II: the Cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta between Romanesque and Gothic federic and, in the countryside, the cave of Lamalunga with the Man of Altamura.

4 places to visit in Altamura, in the province of Bari (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Altamura in the guide with map and filters

Museo nazionale archeologico di Altamura

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Opened in 1993, on two floors and six sections from the Paleolithic to the Middle Ages: the civilizations of the Upper Murgia, the origins of bread and a plan dedicated to the European Paleolithic, with the life-size reconstruction of the Man of Altamura presented in 2017.

  • Where Via Santeramo 88, Altamura
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museipuglia.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Centro visite della Grotta di Lamalunga

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

At the karst cave where, over a swallower and about sixty meters of tunnels, lies the intact skeleton of the Man of Altamura, an adult male lived about 250,000 years ago, in a phase of passage towards the Neanderthals.

  • Where Strada Provinciale 157, Altamura
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

An example of Romanesque Apulian combined with Gothic federician.

  • Where Centro storico, Altamura
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Centro storico
