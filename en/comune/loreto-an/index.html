What to see in Loreto

A shrine town built around the Basilica of the Holy House (1468-1587), which according to tradition houses the house of Nazareth, which arrived here in 1294; the fortress is surrounded by walls with round walkways and overlooks Piazza della Madonna with the Fontana Maggiore and the Apostolic Palace, seat of the Papal Museum with the paintings of Lorenzo Lotto.

Basilica della Santa Casa

Places of worship · Basilicas

Built between 1468 and 1587 to guard the Holy House, according to tradition the house of Nazareth of the Virgin came to Loreto in the night between 9 and 10 December 1294. Giuliano da Maiano brought it to the drum and Giuliano da Sangallo turned the dome, repainted by Cesare Maccari between 1895 and 1907; Istria stone façade completed in 1587 and bell tower by Luigi Vanvitelli (1750-1755). Inside the Chapel of the Annunciation frescoed by Federico Zuccari, the Sacristy of St. Mark of Melozzo da Forlì, that of St. John of Luca Signorelli and the Treasury Hall with the turn of the Pomarancio (1605-1610).

  • Where Piazza della Madonna 1, Loreto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.santuarioloreto.it

Sources: santuarioloreto.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Santa Casa di Nazareth e recinto marmoreo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

In the heart of the basilica, under the dome, the stone walls of the house traditionally linked to the birth and Annunciation of Mary, which studies indicate of Palestinian origin. It contains the marble fence designed by Bramante and built by Sansovino, with statues and reliefs considered a masterpiece of the 16th century sculpture.

  • Where Basilica della Santa Casa, Piazza della Madonna 1, Loreto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Museo Pontificio della Santa Casa (Antico Tesoro)

Museums · Old masters

In the western wing of the Apostolic Palace, founded at the end of the nineteenth century together with the restorations of the basilica of Giuseppe Sacconi. He exhibited the hall of Lorenzo Lotto, who spent the last few years in Loreto, with works for the choir of the basilica such as the Baptism of Christ and the Presentation at the Temple; five-sixth-century pharmacy majolica of Spezieria, seventeenth-century Flemish tapestries on drawings by Raffaello, a silver crucifix of Giambologna, ex-vote and a section of contemporary art. Open every day 10-13 and 15-18.

  • Where Piazza della Madonna 1, Loreto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Piazza della Madonna e Fontana Maggiore

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The square in front of the basilica, closed to the north-east by the Apostolic Palace, houses in the center the Fontana Maggiore, a Baroque masterpiece by Carlo Maderno and Giovanni Fontana, and on the churchyard the monument to Pope Sixtus V. Here work the Madonnas, who reproduce famous religious paintings in chalk.

  • Where Piazza della Madonna, Loreto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Mura e camminamenti di ronda

The walls, which began in the 14th century and strengthened as the Wall of Leo X against Turkish incursions, make the sanctuary a fortress with walks of the rounds, towers and guard bodies; the route offers views of the city and the sea.

Scala Santa

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Go up 330 steps from the station to the basilica through Porta Marina, with fifteen newsstands designed by Amerigo Staffolani in 1942 and bronze panels of the Rosary Mysteries merged in 1895 by Guarino Roscioli; on top, under the balcony of Piazzale Giovanni Paolo II, a wooden crucifix with a view to the sea.

  • Where Da Stazione di Loreto a Porta Marina, Loreto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Via Sisto V e Belvedere di Piazzale Giovanni Paolo II

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Via Sisto V is the street of silver shops, religious objects, ceramics and fabrics; from the viewpoint of Piazzale Giovanni Paolo II the panorama opens onto the Musone Valley.

Museo storico aeronautico

Museums · Themed

It is located in Loreto because Our Lady of Loreto, proclaimed in 1920 as the patron saint of aviation, protects aviators. It exhibits a unique collection in Italy of dozens of uniforms complete with the Regia Aeronautica, largely original and in some unique cases, and hundreds of pieces from the birth of the Regia Aeronautica to today.
