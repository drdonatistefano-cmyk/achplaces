What to see in Castrocielo

The village of Ciociaro on the slopes of Mount Cairo, founded in 996, overlooking the Liri Valley: in the plain it preserves the archaeological area of ancient Aquinum, home of Giovenale, with the Museum of the city of Aquino and Lake Capo d'Acqua; on Mount Asprano, the remains of the castle of Castrum Coeli and the church of Santa Maria Assunta in Cielo.

Area archeologica di Aquinum

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

About 8 hectares in the plain at the foot of Mount Cairo, one of the largest Roman areas of southern Lazio, largely still to be dug. Volscian settlement of the V-IV century B.C., quoted by Livio in 211 B.C. for the passage of Hannibal, became a colony under the Second Triumvirate and declined with the Longobards in the sixth century; there was born Juvenal. You can see Porta Capuana, the best preserved monument, the remains of the theatre and the Capitolium and the Vecciane Thermal Baths, with an inscription of about 9 meters in the frigidarium; the amphitheatre was largely destroyed in 1960-61 by the works of the Autostrada del Sole.

  • Where Via Latina, Castrocielo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo della città di Aquino

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in December 2000 in a large garden inside the Roman and medieval archaeological area of Aquinum, between a basalt stretch of the Via Latina and the church of Santa Maria della Libera. It tells the territory of Aquino from Prehistory to the modern age, in chronological sections (Prehistory and Protohistory, Roman age, Middle Ages and modern age), with educational panels and a multimedia location for the virtual visit.

  • Where Area archeologica di Aquinum, Castrocielo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Lago Capo d'Acqua

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small lake fed by springs at the foot of Mount Cairo, which for centuries refurbished the ancient Aquinum; in the sixteenth century the Boncompagni princes regulated its waters (the Aquinas Forms) to reclaim the plain. About 3 hectares of wetland, since 2017 part of the Natural Monument of Aquinum established by the Lazio Region; also accessible to people with reduced mobility, with some more difficult points.

  • Where Via Cavallara, Castrocielo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa della Madonna dei Sette Dolori (Madonna del Pianto)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small Romanesque church on the shore of Lake Capo d'Acqua, with arched porch mirrored in the water. Originally a single nave with narrow windows, in the eighteenth century it had three baroque altars; damaged in the Second World War. Its most important fresco, a Crucifixion of the 14th century, was detached and restored in 1973 and is today in the church of San Rocco, in the center of Castrocielo.

  • Where Via Cavallara 76, Castrocielo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Lucia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the historic center consecrated in 1746, with classical facade and interior with three naves on pillars, without dome, a portal for nave. It preserves two great nineteenth-century paintings by the Neapolitan painter Alfonso Simonetti with the history of the saint. Without fixed hours: easier to find it open on Sunday morning.

  • Where Via Annibale Cerase 1, Castrocielo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta in Cielo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the summit of Monte Asprano (669 m), overlooking the Liri Valley, it was built in the 14th century next to the castle of Castrum Coeli and for over five centuries it was a parish town of Palazzolo (the present Castrocielo) and Colle San Magno. It contains 18th century frescoes; restored in 1885. On Easter Monday at dawn there are two processions for the "Kiss of the Madonnas."

  • Where Monte Asprano (indirizzo di Colle San Magno)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello di Castrum Coeli

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The fortress of the sky was built by the Benedictine Abbot Mansone on top of Mount Asprano to protect the inhabitants of Aquino from Saracen raids.

  • Where Monte Asprano, Castrocielo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Monacato di Villa Eucheria

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Former Benedictine monastery in the hamlet of Villa Eucheria, with a still intact cryptoportico; it is part of the network of the Historical Residences of Lazio.
