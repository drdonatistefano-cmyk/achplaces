What to see in Celano

Marsica town overlooking the plain of the ancient lake of Fucino, dominated by the Piccolomini Castle which houses the Museum of Sacred Art of Marsica and the Torlonia Collection; home of Blessed Thomas of Celano, the first biographer of San Francesco. At the edge of the village are the Gorges of Celano, one of the most spectacular canyons of the Central Apennines.

Castello Piccolomini, Museo d'arte sacra della Marsica e Collezione Torlonia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Fortress built by Celano from 1392 at the behest of the counts, enlarged in 1451 by Leonello Acclozamora (noble floor and four corner towers) and completed as residence by Antonio Todeschini Piccolomini, grandson of Pius II, Count of Celano since 1463; it has a central court with well and loggia portico. Damaged by the earthquake of 1915 and rebuilt until 1960, since 1992 it houses the Museum of Sacred Art of Marsica, eight rooms of paintings, sculptures and goldsmiths from the 6th to the 18th century (two wooden doors carved from the 12th century, the triptych of Alba Fucens, the cross Orsini of 1334), and the Torlonia Collection with the findings emerged from the drying up of the Fucino, including a relief of the 2nd century A.D. with a city and its territory. Open from Tuesday to Sunday 9:30-18:30, closed on Mondays.

  • Where Largo Cavalieri di Vittorio Veneto, Celano
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Gole di Celano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Canyon between Celano, Aielli and Ovindoli with walls up to 200 meters high and distant in some points just 2 meters, dug by water; along the path the Fonte degli Innamorati and the ruins of the Celestian monastery of San Marco alle Foci, with griffons, royal owls and pilgrim falcons. The crossing (difficulty E+, white-red CAI trail, 3-3,5 hours) starts from the parking lot in Foci and arrives in Valle d'Arano, on the plateau of Ovindoli; the municipality recommends late spring and summer, avoiding the rainy and snowy seasons for the floods.

the Sirente-Velino Park has announced seasonal closures and reopenings of the gorges, with reservation obligatory at the reopening of 19 May; check before leaving

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Started in the second half of the 13th century and completed two centuries later by eagle workers called by the Countess Jacobella; times redone after the earthquake of 1915. The façade is made of square stone with Gothic rosette and Romanesque portal, in the bezel the Madonna and Child between St. John the Evangelist and Pope Bonifacio IV; doors in elderwood with the Piccolomini and Aragonese coats of arms. Inside the fifteenth-century frescoes of the Sienese school, the Madonna with Child by Andrea De Lithium, the wooden baptismal font of the sixteenth century and the Via Crucis in marble by Novello Finotti (1997-98); the main altar preserves the urns of the Saints Martyrs.

  • Where Largo Beato Tommaso, Celano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa della Madonna delle Grazie (San Giovanni Piedimonte)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Also known as San Giovanni Piedimonte or Capodacqua, it was built in the middle of the 11th century by Bishop Pandolfo, who laid in 1059 the relics of the Saints Martyrs; in 1223 it was the only church spared by Frederick II in the fire of Celano. On the architrave of the portal is an eagle nymbata, symbol of St. John the Evangelist; on the side is the portal of women with cruciform rosette of the 11th century and the coat of arms of the Berardi. Inside a fourteenth-century holy water and frescoes of the 14th-XV century on the vaults.

  • Where Via Madonna delle Grazie 36, Celano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.celano.aq.it · parcosirentevelino.it · italia.it

MuSè - Museo Paludi di Celano e sito palafitticolo

Museums · Archaeological

Basement building shaped as a protohistoric mound next to the excavations of the Paludi, conducted from 1985 to 1998, which brought to light a palafittic village on the banks of the ancient Fucino, the largest prehistoric excavation in Abruzzo, and a burial necropolis of the final Bronze Age with burials in excavated tree trunks. The collections tell the Marsica from the Bronze Age to the Roman era, with workshops for restoration and anthropology.

MiC card indicates the currently closed museum for maintenance work
