What to see in Reana del Rojale

Municipality north of Udine between the Torre stream and the medieval forges, known after the war for the handicrafts of the corn pulp, told by the permanent exhibition of Piazzale Centa.

Mostra del cartoccio e del vimine

Museums · Themed

It tells the craftsmanship of the corn pulp that in the post-war period made Rojale a center of bags and baskets sold in Italy and abroad; after the decline of the early 1950s, the parish priest Don Mario Fabrizio started the relaunch in 1950, with a professional school and in 1964 the Cooperative craft cartocciai Friulani (about one hundred members, closed in 1987). Two rooms: screen, bags, lampshades, footwear and a bed of 1955; then cribs, Easter bells, a Madonna of Lourdes and dolls of crafts. Visits on request (tel. 0432 852222).

Roggia di Udine e Rosta Ferracina

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

The road of Udine, derived from the torrent Torre and mentioned in a document dated 1171, crosses the municipality with the stone banks built by the Republic of Venice in the eighteenth century, called "Rosta Feracina" from the name of the builder; in Valle the road of Palma is detached. On the Tower there are the gripping works of the two roges.
