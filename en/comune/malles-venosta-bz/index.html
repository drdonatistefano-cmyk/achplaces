What to see in Malles Venosta

Borgo dell'Alta Val Venosta from the Romanesque bell towers and sixteenth-century houses, with the church of San Benedetto and its Carolingian frescoes among the oldest in central Europe; above Burgusio the Benedictine abbey of Monte Maria (about 1150), at 1,340 m.

Abbazia di Monte Maria e museo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Benedictine monastery founded around 1150 by Ulrico di Tarasp and his wife Uta, at 1,340 m among the highest vineyards in Europe: the highest Benedictine abbey in Europe, inhabited by monks for about 900 years. White complex around a large courtyard; in the Romanesque crypt frescoes of the founding era with a unique representation of the angels. The museum tells the life of monks according to the "ora et labora" with exhibitions on Marienberg and Mary. Open Monday-Saturday 10-17.

  • Where Slingia 1, Burgusio, Malles Venosta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.marienberg.it

Sources: marienberg.it · italia.it · vinschgau.net

Chiesa di San Benedetto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

Church of the eighth century, with bell tower probably added in the twelfth century; the Carolingian frescoes are among the oldest mural paintings of central Europe, with portraits of the founders, the bishop of Chur and a French gentleman in costume, without comparisons in Europe. In summer visits Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, guided (German or Italian) Monday, Wednesday and Friday; closed Sunday and holidays.

  • Where Via San Benedetto, Malles Venosta
  • Entry €3, with guided tour €5 (summer); €1.80 (winter)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it

Centro storico di Malles

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

At the entrance of the village Romanesque bell towers and stone towers; in the center houses of the sixteenth century like the Lichtegg and the Malsegg and the remains of a large round tower that was part of a castle.

  • Where Malles Venosta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo locale di Laudes

Museums · Themed

Opened in 2004 in an abandoned parish barn, it exhibits agricultural tools, domestic tools and artisan instruments collected in forty years by a collector of the village, documenting the traditions and customs of the Upper Val Venosta.

  • Where Laudes 139, Malles Venosta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Bunker23 di Tarces

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Former military refuge of the Alpine Valley, today a cultural space between art, history and memory with exhibitions, guided tours and events in its corridors; above the village, about 10 minutes walk.

  • Where Tarces, Malles Venosta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website bunker23.it

Sources: bunker23.it · suedtirol.info · italia.it
