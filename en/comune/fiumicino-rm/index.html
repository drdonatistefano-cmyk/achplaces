What to see in Fiumicino

The municipality of the great airport and the port channel of fishing boats, on the mouth of the Tiber: here stood the imperial ports of Claudio and Traiano, with the necropolis of the Sacred Island and the Museum of Ships, and along the coast the beaches of Fregene and the oasis of the Roman coast.

7 places to visit in Fiumicino, in the province of Rome (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Porti di Claudio e Traiano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The port of Claudio was started in 42 AD north of the mouth of the Tiber to bring to the sea the river traffic of Rome, today under the airport; Trajan enlarged it in 106 AD with a large hexagonal basin more internal and secure, digging the channel that created the Sacred Island.

  • Where Fiumicino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismoroma.it

Sources: turismoroma.it

Museo delle Navi

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

It exhibits five Roman wrecks found between 1958 and 1961 during the work of the airport, in the ancient harbour basin: three river vessels for the transport on the Tiber, a sea cargo ship and a fishing boat with central tub for the live catch. Reopened after a long restoration.

  • Where Fiumicino
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Necropoli di Porto all'Isola Sacra

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Over 200 funerary brick buildings from the end of the 1st to the 4th century AD along the Via Flavia Severiana, with Latin inscriptions and scenes of crafts that tell a population of traders, freedom and small entrepreneurs.

  • Where Isola Sacra, Fiumicino
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Complesso della Basilica di Sant'Ippolito

History and architecture · Archaeology

Early Christian Basilica with three naves between the end of the IV and early V century, on a Roman thermal building, brought to light in the seventies along the Fiumicino channel; in the Antiquarium the Carolingian ciborium of the time of Leo III.

Open only on a few Sundays, check the calendar

  • Where Isola Sacra, Fiumicino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Oasi WWF di Macchiagrande

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Around 280 hectares of maquis on the dunes and Mediterranean holm oak in the Statale Reserve of the Roman coast, with deer, hertrics and turtles of Hermann; together with Foce dell'Arrone and the Vasche di Maccarese forms the system of oases managed by the WWF.

  • Where Via della Veneziana, Fregene
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismoroma.it

Sources: turismoroma.it

Canale di Via della Torre Clementina e Santa Maria Porto della Salute

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

The canal to the sea with moored fishing boats and nets, where fishermen sell fresh fish; along the canal the church dedicated to the protection of sailors.

  • Where Via della Torre Clementina, Fiumicino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com

Spiagge di Fregene

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Equipped sandy beaches and pinewoods, the usual destination of the Romans.

  • Where Fregene, Fiumicino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com
