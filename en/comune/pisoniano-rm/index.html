What to see in Pisoniano

Country 532 meters on the eastern side of Mount Guadagnolo, which owes its name to the villa where Caio Calpurnio Pisone took refuge after the conspiracy against Nerone; it houses the hemp museum, the rural church of Santa Vittoria and the eighteenth-century Madonna della Neve, from which climbs the path Karol Wojtyla towards the Mentorella.

Museo civico della canapa

Museums · Themed

Since 1997 one of the very few monothematic museums on rural civilization: documents the entire cycle of hemp in the Aniene valley, from sowing to maceration, spinning, warping and weaving, with two eighteenth-century frames, arcolai, fused and conocchie, the result of ten years of research by the twins Septimio and Domenico Bernardini. VisitLazio places him in the historic Palazzo Magrini, with a section on Leonardo da Vinci's machines. According to the MiC card is open in July and August on Saturday and Sunday 10-13 and 16-19, in winter on request (tel. 062184189).

Chiesa rurale di Santa Vittoria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the woods, dedicated to the patron saint of Pisoniano: built in the 6th century in the place where Santa Vittoria lived with his sister Anatolia during the persecutions of Decius. On the main altar there is a rare table of Santa Vittoria in Byzantine style, dated to the X-XI century.

  • Where Località Santa Vittoria, Pisoniano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Chiesa della Madonna della Neve e Sentiero Karol Wojtyla

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The path Karol Wojtyla starts from Pisoniano and climbs to the Sanctuary of Mentorella; along the route you will find the eighteenth-century church of Our Lady of the Snow, which houses an image inspired by the Salus Populi Romani, celebrated on August 5.

  • Where Partenza da Pisoniano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Centro storico, Palazzo Theodoli e rione Castelluccio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In 1572 the village passed to Jerome Theodoli, prelate of the Apostolic Chamber, whose family built a large palace with a small defensive tower, collapsed on the afternoon of November 2, 1969; next stands the Castelluccio district, which takes its name from the tower. For Corpus Domini, in June, from the middle of the nineteenth century the historic center is covered with carpets of flowers on which the procession passes.

  • Where Centro storico, Pisoniano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com
