What to see in Bondone

Ancient village of Carbonai, 720 metres above Lake Idro, at the south-west end of Trentino on the border with Lombardy, among the most beautiful villages in Italy since 2018: alleys, arches and steps with devotional frescoes on the facades, the fourteenth-century parish church of the Nativity and, on a spur on the lake, the Castle of San Giovanni dei Conti Lodron.

Castello di San Giovanni

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On a rocky spur under control of the mouth of the Judicarians, it is probably the "castrum de summo lacu" mentioned in 1086; from the thirteenth century it was a fief of the Counts Lodron, who held it until the twentieth century, and with the arrival of the gunpowder became a small Renaissance fortress in granite of the quarries of Daone. Fallen in ruins, it was purchased in the fifties and restored; today it is of the Municipality and is open to the public, with door nailed on drawbridge and courtyard semicircle towards the valley. You can only reach it on foot: about 15-20 minutes by dirt road from the parking lot on the Baitoni-Bondone road, or 20-30 minutes by path from Baitoni (Miralago parking).

Centro storico, affreschi e monumento al carbonaio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Narrow alleys, arches, steep stairs, dry walls and houses with painted facades: the popular frescoes are linked to the devotion to Our Lady, who according to legend appeased the plague of 1628-1630. In Via Giusti 24 a Madonna enthroned that nurses the Child of the sixteenth century, in Via Baroni 13 a satirical image of 1672 with a king who offers money to Death. In the square the bronze monument to the coalmaker, with a girl and a kid, a man with wood and the cadasta called "poiat" .

Chiesa parrocchiale della Natività di Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Church of fourteenth-century origin with three naves, each with its own altar; from the churchyard, on a terrace at the edge of the village, opens the view of the valley and Lake of Idro.

Carbonaie di Bondone

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Until the fifties, the production of wood charcoal was essential for the economy of the country: in spring the charcoal trees climbed into the woods and built wooden huts and leaves. At some times of the year, you can see the construction of a "poiat" and the production of coal; the "A Smoky Tale" app tells the craft as a guiding thread to explore the territory.

Lago d'Idro: Porto Camarelle e Biotopo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the Trentino shore of Lake Idro, a few minutes drive from the village, the beach of Porto Camarelle has the Blue Flag of Legambiente; the Biotope connects two beaches with a wooden walkway and is a refuge for many species of birds. Canoeing and kitesurfing are practiced on the lake; in Baitoni passes the cycle path of the Valley of the Churches.

  • Where Baitoni, Bondone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info
