What to see in Imola

Città della Rocca sforzaesca e dell'Autodromo, with a historic centre full of buildings and civic museums.

6 places to visit in Imola, in the province of Bologna (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: the Rocca Sforza is closed for restoration works since March 2024 and the reopening date is not yet confirmed. Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 3 of the 9 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Piazza Matteotti e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The square overlooking the Town Hall and Palazzo Sersanti, a node of a center from the Roman plant.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Imola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Rocca sforzesca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Medieval fortress reinforced by Sforza and Caterina Sforza, with collection of weapons and ceramics.

Closed for restoration works since March 2024; reopening not yet confirmed (some sites still indicate dates already exceeded)

  • Where Piazzale Giovanni dalle Bande Nere, Imola
  • Entry Civic museums from 2026: €5 whole, €4 reduced
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · bolognawelcome.com · ilrestodelcarlino.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Palazzo Tozzoni

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

House museum of a noble family, with furnishings, paintings and kitchens intact eighteenth century.

  • Where Via Garibaldi 18, Imola
  • Entry €5 in full, €4 reduced (from 2026); 2 museums €8, 3 museums €10
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website imolamusei.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: imolamusei.it · ilrestodelcarlino.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Autodromo Enzo e Dino Ferrari

Local life and traditions · Motors · Paid entry

The circuit of Formula 1 and the World Superbike, which can be visited in about an hour and a half; at the entrance the Checco Costa Museum.

  • Where Piazza Ayrton Senna da Silva 2, Imola
  • Entry Guided tours for a fee: rates on Bologna Welcome
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: bolognawelcome.com · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Parco delle Acque Minerali

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

The large hilly park next to the circuit, with paths and pond.

  • Where Imola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: bolognawelcome.com · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo di San Domenico

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Pinacoteca and naturalistic collections in the former Dominican convent.

Bologna Welcome indicates it as currently not visitable

  • Where Via Sacchi 4, Imola
  • Entry Civic museums: €5 whole, €4 reduced
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website imolamusei.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: imolamusei.it · bolognawelcome.com · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
