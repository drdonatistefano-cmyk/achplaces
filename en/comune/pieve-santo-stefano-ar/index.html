What to see in Pieve Santo Stefano

The "Città dei Diari" in the upper Tiber Valley has been keeping since 1984 the National Diary Archive designed by Saverio Tutino, told in the Piccolo Museo del Diario at Palazzo Pretorio. Destroyed by the flood of 1855 and the Second World War, the town preserves the Town Hall, the Collegiate Church and the Sanctuary of the Madonna dei Lumi; in the territory the Hermitage of Cerbaiolo, the Lake of Montedoglio and the churches of the hamlets, while from here starts the Via di Francesco dalla Verna in Assisi.

Piccolo Museo del Diario

Museums · Themed

Hosted since 2013 in the sixteenth century Palazzo Pretorio, it tells the archive that since 1984 collects diaries, letters and memories of ordinary people. Four rooms: an installation of cupboards and drawers to be opened to listen to and see selected stories, the corner dedicated to Saverio Tutino, the Room Rabito on the autobiography of the street Ragusano Vincenzo Rabito beat by car, and the room of the sheet on which the peasant mantua Clelia Marchi wrote her own life.

  • Where SP48 13, Pieve Santo Stefano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.piccolomuseodeldiario.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: piccolomuseodeldiario.it · visittuscany.com · discoverarezzo.com

Palazzo Comunale e Logge del Grano

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The Town Hall, of medieval origin, was among the few buildings spared from the destruction of the Second World War and houses a work by Girolamo della Robbia, ...Jesus and the Samaritan at the well. Next to the sixteenth-century Palazzo Pretorio and the nineteenth-century Logge del Grano, accessible from both palaces.

  • Where Centro storico, Pieve Santo Stefano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: discoverarezzo.com · visittuscany.com

Collegiata di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Main religious building of the village, in neoclassical style, built between 1844 and 1881 on the site of the ancient medieval parish church.

  • Where Centro storico, Pieve Santo Stefano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: discoverarezzo.com · visittuscany.com

Santuario della Madonna dei Lumi

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Erected between 1590 and 1625, it preserves a fresco considered miraculous. In September the Palio dei Lumi concludes the Football in costume between the four districts of the village.

  • Where Pieve Santo Stefano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: discoverarezzo.com · visittuscany.com

Chiesa di Santa Maria del Colledestro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small octagonal temple just outside the village, on the left of the Tiber, near the Colledestro stream, with a unique arch bridge near the facade. It has been mentioned in documents since the 12th century and has the appearance of a restoration of the 16th century.It stands where once there was a pagan temple dedicated to the Tiber.

  • Where Colledestro, Pieve Santo Stefano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: discoverarezzo.com · visittuscany.com

Eremo di Cerbaiolo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Born as a Benedictine monastery in the 8th century, in 1216 it was donated to Saint Francis and passed to the Friars Minor; according to tradition Saint Francis and Saint Anthony stayed there. A popular saying binds him to the nearby Mount of Verna.

  • Where Cerbaiolo, Pieve Santo Stefano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · discoverarezzo.com

Lago di Montedoglio

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Artificial lake created by the dam started in 1977 in Madonnuccia and completed in 1993, indicated as the largest in Tuscany. Above the lake rise the Castle of Brancialino, of the twelfth century, and in Sigliano the neobyzantine church of Santa Maria della Pace, finished in 1921, with panoramic views over the water.

  • Where Madonnuccia, Pieve Santo Stefano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: discoverarezzo.com · visittuscany.com

Chiese delle frazioni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The hamlets preserve numerous churches with works of art and glazed terracottas of the school of Della Robbia: San Lorenzo a Baldignano with crypt of the eleventh century, Sant'Andrea della Torre a Mignano, documented since 1085 and linked to a small fortified village on the road to Verna, the Hermitage of the Madonna del Faggio in Cercetole, of the first fifteenth century, and the churches of Saints James and Christopher in Montalone and Saints Peter and Paul in Valsavignon, with works of the sixteenth century.

  • Where Frazioni di Pieve Santo Stefano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: discoverarezzo.com · visittuscany.com
