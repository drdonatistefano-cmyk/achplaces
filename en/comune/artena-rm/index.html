What to see in Artena

Renaissance village on the Lepini Mountains, at 630 meters, with one of the largest pedestrian historical centers in Europe that can be reached only on foot or on the back of a mule; the Borghese Palace, the church of Santa Croce and the Borghese Granaio with the Archaeological Museum, while on the Civita Plan there are polygonal walls and hydraulic works of the ancient city.

Centro storico e Palazzo Borghese

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Renaissance pedestrian village, reachable only on foot or with mules (a statue of the mule and mule tracker welcomes at the entrance). The ancient Montefortino, renamed Artena in 1873, passed from the Counts of Segni to the Colonna and then to Cardinal Scipione Borghese, nephew of Paolo V, who had the Palazzo Borghese, the Arch Borghese, the convent of San Francesco and the kindergarten of San Marco built; in front of the palace the Palazzetto del Governore on Piazza della Vittoria, with the exhibition of the historical costumes of the procession that recalls the visit of Paolo V in 1615. The village is set of films such as "Il prefecte di ferro" (1978) and "Vedo nudo" (1969).

  • Where Piazza della Vittoria, Artena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Main church of the village, at the top of the village, recognizable for the two unusual bell towers; it was built by the Counts of Tuscolo at the end of the 11th century and still dominates the town.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: visitlazio.com

Museo civico archeologico Roger Lambrechts

Museums · Archaeological

In the former Borghese Granaio, where between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries the peasants gave grain, today also the seat of the City Council; it is part of the museum system of the Lepini Mountains. It exhibits finds from the town of Piano della Civita and materials collected by the local Archaeological Group, from the Neolithic to the early Middle Ages: ceramics, architectural decorations, metal objects, inscriptions, coins and glass.

  • Where Via Fleming, Artena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Parco archeologico Piano della Civita

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the plateau at the most advanced point of the Lepini Mountains remain the remains of an ancient city, especially hydraulic works to collect and distribute water, with polygonal walls dated to the sixth century BC, terraces and fortifications; guided paths, panels and a tour center. Here is also the Big Bench 434.

  • Where Piano della Civita, Artena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com
