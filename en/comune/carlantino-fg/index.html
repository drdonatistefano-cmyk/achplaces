What to see in Carlantino

Borgo dei Monti Dauni overlooking the Fortore valley, Carlantino has a small old town closed by a double wall, gathered around the church of San Donato, and is a panoramic balcony that ranges from Gran Sasso to Tremiti, from the dam of Occhito to Gargano.

Chiesa di San Donato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It is the center of the small old town and houses the altarpiece depicting the Madonna delle Grazie.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Framed by a double wall, it has narrow streets flanked by houses with vault keys decorated on the portals; it preserves the archaeological heritage of the municipality, with evidences from the Neolithic to the Middle Ages.

  • Where Centro storico, Carlantino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page interregaismart.regione.puglia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it

Belvedere sulla valle del Fortore

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Carlantino is a strategic balcony with a very wide view: from the village you can see the Gran Sasso, the profile of the Tremiti islands, the valley of Fortore with its streams, the dam of Occhito, the Table, the coast of Gargano, the peaks of Abruzzo and the Apennines beneventano.

  • Where Carlantino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
