What to see in Pisticci

Lucan Country overlooking the Ionian, surrounded by the almost lunar landscape of the calanchi, home of the Amaro Lucano; it preserves the Mother Church of Saints Peter and Paul, the Abbey of Santa Maria del Casale and the Castle of San Basilio, with eight kilometers of beaches in Marina di Pisticci.

8 places to visit in Pisticci, in the province of Matera (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Calanchi di Pisticci

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

The territory is marked by calanchi, clay rocks shaped by erosion that give the landscape an almost lunar appearance.

  • Where Pisticci
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it

Chiesa Madre dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque-Renaissance church built on a pre-existing church in the Dirupo district, recognizable by the large hemispheric dome visible from almost all the center; inside altars and Baroque canvases.

  • Where Rione Dirupo, Pisticci
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it

Abbazia di Santa Maria del Casale

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

According to the tradition built around 1087 by Rodolfo Maccabeo and Emma d'Altavilla on the remains of a Greek-Byzantine monastery; a Romanesque church in Apulian style that preserves a wooden statue of the Virgin of the twelfth century.

  • Where Pisticci
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

On the central square, until 1860 part of the Convent of Santa Maria delle Grazie: three naves, baroque frescoes and marble altars of Carrara.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Pisticci
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it

Piazza Umberto I e Terrazzo del melograno

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Heart of the village with the Palazzocchio, home to an archive of the sixteenth century, and Palazzo Giannantonio, of late seventeenth century, now town hall; near, the Pomegranate Terrace is a small square on two levels with arches and murals of flowers and pomegranate fruits.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Pisticci
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it

Castello di San Basilio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built by the Basilian monks in the 7th century as a fortified farm, enlarged by the Normans with a central tower and then donated to the Benedictines of Santa Maria del Casale.

  • Where San Basilio, Pisticci
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it

Marina di Pisticci

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

About eight kilometers of sandy beaches on the Ionian Sea, including San Basilio, San Teodoro and Macchia.

  • Where Marina di Pisticci
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it · italia.it

Porto degli Argonauti

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Modern Marina on the Ionian for 450 boats, even over 30 meters, with two basins, yard and control tower, surrounded by pine forests and palm trees; awarded with the gold anchors.

  • Where Porto degli Argonauti, 75015 Pisticci
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.basilicataturistica.it

Sources: italia.it · basilicataturistica.it
