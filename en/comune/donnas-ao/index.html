What to see in Donnas

Medieval village born on the Roman road of the Gallie, among the terraced vineyards of the first DOC wine of the Valle d'Aosta.

5 places to visit in Donnas, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Donnas in the guide with map and filters

Strada romana delle Gallie e arco

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Tratto della via consola carved for 221 meters in the living rock, which culminates in an arched passage 4 meters high; in the Middle Ages the arch was the door of the village.

  • Where Donnas
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: italia.it · lovevda.it

Borgo di Donnas

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient village with sixteenth century windows, frescoes, inlaid walnut portals and Palazzo Enrielli of the seventeenth century.

  • Where Donnas
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it

Ecomuseo della vite e del vino

Museums · Themed

Tools that tell about mountain viticulture, difficult and tiring, and its evolution over time.

  • Where Donnas
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Ecomuseo della latteria turnaria di Tréby

Museums · Themed

The building already houses the Confraternity of the Holy Spirit, whose frescoes illustrate its activity, then became a dairy.

  • Entry To be checked

Percorso dei Barmet

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Simple path among natural shelters used as cellars, tool stores or rainwater collection for vineyards.

  • Entry Free
