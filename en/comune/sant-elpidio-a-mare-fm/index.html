What to see in Sant'Elpidio a Mare

Medieval village born from the imperial abbey of Santa Croce al Chienti, still surrounded by the walls with the fourteenth-century doors and dominated by the Gerosolimitana Tower of 28 meters: the Romanesque collegiate church of Mercy frescoed by Pomarancio, the art gallery with the polyptychs of Vittore Crivelli and the footwear museum.

8 places to visit in Sant'Elpidio a Mare, in the province of Fermo (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e mura

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval village, built around the imperial abbey of Santa Croce documented in 887, is still surrounded by the defensive walls with three fourteenth-century doors (Marina, Romana and Canale) and two towers, including the Tower of the Warriors.

  • Where Centro storico, Sant'Elpidio a Mare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Torre Gerosolimitana

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Civic tower of the 14th century about 28 meters high in the highest point of the historic center, with views from the Adriatic to the Sibillini Mountains; it had no defensive function and in the 19th century there was placed an altar to the Unity of Italy. Opening on request to the Tourist Office.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Sant'Elpidio a Mare
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sportelloamico.santelpidioamare.it · italia.it

Pinacoteca civica «Vittore Crivelli»

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Since 1998 in the former eighteenth-century convent of the Filippines: painted from the fifteenth to the eighteenth century by churches and convents; in the Crivelli room the polyptych of the Coronation of the Virgin, the triptych of the Visitation and the Franciscan Friar praying of the Venetian master, with silvers of the Brotherhood of Mercy and a section of graphics. Full 5 €, reduced 3 €, free under 19 years.

  • Where Corso Baccio 31, Sant'Elpidio a Mare
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sportelloamico.santelpidioamare.it · italia.it

Museo della Calzatura «Cav. Vincenzo Andolfi»

Museums · Themed

In the former convent of the Filippines, the evolution of footwear over the centuries: ethnological section, reconstruction of the shop of the slippers, the footwear industry Marche and shoes of famous characters, including Pope John Paul II. Open from Thursday to Sunday and holidays.

  • Where Corso Baccio 31, Sant'Elpidio a Mare
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sportelloamico.santelpidioamare.it · italia.it
