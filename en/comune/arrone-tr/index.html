What to see in Arrone

Borgo della Valnerina was born around the castle of the Arroni, with the upper part of the Terra and the district of Santa Maria with the collegiate frescoed in 1516; in the territory of Casteldilago, the sanctuary of the Madonna dello Scoglio and the Black River Park.

7 places to visit in Arrone, in the province of Terni (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

La Terra e il castello degli Arroni

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The oldest nucleus, on the left of the Black, grew around the castle that the Arroni family built in medieval times, surrounded by walls with circular bastions to defend the road between Rieti and Spoleto; from the 11th to the 12th century it controlled much of the lower Valnerina, until the passage to Spoleto in the thirteenth century. From above you can enjoy the view of the valley.

  • Where La Terra, Arrone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Gothic church in the square of the Earth; the polygonal apse preserves fifteenth-century Umbrian school frescoes, influenced by Filippo Lippi, with a Crucifixion.

  • Where La Terra, Arrone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · umbriatourism.it

Porta San Giovanni e Torre dell'Olivo

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The medieval gate with a sharp arch joins the Earth to the district of Santa Maria; the civic tower, symbol of the village, takes its name from the wild olive tree that grows there. The municipal bell tower is part of the bell tower tradition recognized by UNESCO as an immaterial heritage together with Gubbio.

  • Where Via del Vicinato, Arrone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Collegiata di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The sixteenth-century church of the Santa Maria district with portal of 1493; the cycle of main frescoes is by Vincenzo Tamagni and Giovanni da Spoleto (1516) and the left apse houses glazed terracottas of the sixteenth century. Around, Renaissance noble palaces.

  • Where Quartiere Santa Maria, Arrone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Casteldilago, there are 16th century frescoes by artists from the school of Giovanni di Pietro known as Spain.

Santuario della Madonna dello Scoglio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Parco fluviale del Nera

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Natural area with trails of varying difficulty and guided activities, known for canoeing, rafting, canyoning and climbing on natural rock walls; the park consortium was born in 1995.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
