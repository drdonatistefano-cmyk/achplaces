What to see in Mattinata

Country of Gargano from the white houses on the hills, called "Terra delle almondi" or "Farfalla bianca del Gargano" , surrounded by centuries-old olive groves and overlooking a large beach; in its territory the Bay of Zagare with the faraglioni, the path to Vignanotica, the ruins of the abbey of Monte Sacro and the National Archaeological Museum with the Sansone collection.

Museo archeologico nazionale «Matteo Sansone»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the former civic museum, he exhibits the collection of the pharmacist Matteo Sansone (1916-1992), one of the most important private collections of Puglia, donated to the state by the heirs in 2022: about 2,500 ceramic objects, metal, stone and coins especially of Daunia. Six sections: Monte Saraceno with the ... scudi" funerari in stone, the daunie stele with the schematic figures of the deceased in funeral dress, the contacts with Dalmatia and the Balkans with the ambers of Monte Tabor and Monte Saraceno, the geometric ceramics and red figures, the Hellenistic age and the finds of the Roman villa of Agnuli. Monday closed; Tuesday-Thursday 18-21, Friday-Sunday 9-13 and 18-21.

  • Where Via Torquato Tasso 1, Mattinata
  • Entry €6, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: museipuglia.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Baia delle Zagare (Baia dei Mergoli)

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

Bay between white cliffs and blue-green water, with two calcareous faraglioni a few meters from the shore, the Arch of Diomede and ...The Scissors are about 1 km of beach divided by a spur in the Bay of Faraglioni (sand and gravel) and Zagare Bay (white pebbles and sand), plus the Cala dei Morti reachable only from the sea. Most of the beach is run by hotel establishments: access for guests and with a limited number of daily passes issued by the municipality; you will also arrive by boat from Vieste or Mattinata. Better in the morning, in the afternoon it goes in the shade.

  • Where SP53, Mattinata
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Sentiero Mergoli-Vignanotica e spiaggia di Vignanotica

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Panoramic path well signposted of about 3 km, less than an hour, that starts from the SP53 and crosses the Reserve of Monte Barone between almond trees, Aleppo pines and olive trees with views of the bay; at the junction, on the right, you go down to the beach of Vignanotica, long beach at the outlet of the valley, with summer services. Free access according to the Park.

  • Where SP53, Mattinata
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parcogargano.it

Abbazia della Santissima Trinità di Monte Sacro

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

On Mount Sacro (872 m), between holm oaks and mixed woods, the ruins of the abbey born from an 11th-century hermitage dependent on Santa Maria di Calena, a Benedictine priory from 1138, influential up to Bari, in decline from the fifteenth century and abandoned in the seventeenth century; remain the pronao with arches and pillars, part of the apse and the Romanesque portal. On clear days the view extends to the coast, the Umbra Forest and Monte Spigno.

  • Where Monte Sacro, Mattinata
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcogargano.it · italia.it

Monte Saraceno

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

One of the two promontories that close the bay of Mattinata; the visits are organized through the visitor center of the Park. From the site come the stone scudi on columns used as funerary signs and the ambers exhibited in the first and third sections of the National Archaeological Museum.

  • Where Monte Saraceno, Mattinata
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcogargano.it · museipuglia.cultura.gov.it · italia.it
