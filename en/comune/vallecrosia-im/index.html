What to see in Vallecrosia

Cittadina della Riviera dei Fiori between Bordighera and Camporosso, with about a kilometer of beaches, the sixteenth-century torrione against pirates, the small church of San Rocco on the remains of a Roman temple, the ancient village of Vallecrosia Alta and the Song Museum set up on a steam train.

7 places to visit in Vallecrosia, in the province of Imperia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Canzone, Fondazione Erio Tripodi

Museums · Themed

Collection formed by Erio Tripodi in the eighties and set up in a steam train, a locomotive "Cirilla" of 1910 with carriages "centoporte": rare systems of automatic reproduction of sound still working (musical boxes, organettes of Barberia, organnets of French tabarin of the eighteenth century, mechanical floors and piano), relics of the Italian song from street music to Neapolitan melody to light music, and a section on the Sanremo Festival with photos, autographs, records, posters and sketches of sets. On a second carriage a study center with library and record archive. Guided visits; tel. 0184 291000.

  • Where Tra i Piani e Vallecrosia Alta, Vallecrosia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museodellacanzone.com

Il Torrione

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Symbol of Vallecrosia, it is a circular fortification of the sixteenth century, of more than ten meters of diameter, built to spot the Turkish-barbarish pirates plundering the western Liguria; it is the oldest monument of the village after the church of San Rocco. You can see it from the waterfront.

  • Where Lungomare, Vallecrosia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.comune.vallecrosia.im.it

Sources: turismo.comune.vallecrosia.im.it

Santuario di San Rocco

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

A place of worship already in Roman times, it was built almost certainly in the 10th century over the remains of a pagan temple; of pre-Romanesque origin, it has a sixteenth-century bastion linked to the raids of the barbarian corsairs.

  • Where In cima a Via Giovanni XXIII, Vallecrosia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.comune.vallecrosia.im.it

Sources: turismo.comune.vallecrosia.im.it

Santuario di Maria Ausiliatrice

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

It is part of the Salesian complex founded by St. John Bosco; from Via Colonel Aprosio a double staircase rises to the terrace-sagrato, with at the base a marble bust of Don Bosco. Nearby stands the new parish dedicated to the saint.

  • Where Via Colonnello Aprosio, Vallecrosia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.comune.vallecrosia.im.it

Sources: turismo.comune.vallecrosia.im.it

Borgo antico di Vallecrosia Alta

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Borgo about 3 km from the sea, municipal seat until the 1920s: stone buildings, carugi, squares, houses towers and doors painted with the imaginary shops, the houses, turned outwards, were walls. At the entrance the high Sarcena tower, of sighting, of the late fifteenth century; in the village the sanctuary of Our Lady of Graces, where St Bernard of Chiaravalle preached, the Oratory of the Assumption and the deconsecrated church of Santa Crescenza.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismo.comune.vallecrosia.im.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The church of Vallecrosia Alta was renovated in 1737 and the Baroque façade, with the statue of the saint above the portal, was completed in 1784; a nave in Ligurian Baroque style.
