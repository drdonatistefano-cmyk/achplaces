What to see in Picciano

Small hilly town of the Pescarese, in the Val del Fino, with the Museum of Traditions and Rural Arts and a historic centre gathered around Piazza Duca degli Abruzzi and the Baroque church of the Blessed Virgin Mary of the Rescue; in the hamlet of Piccianello rises the neoclassical church of San Rocco (1838).

Museo delle tradizioni ed arti contadine

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Museum born from a private initiative that collects objects of everyday rural life, with reconstructions of ancient environments and a late medieval mill recovered in Pacentro; organizes exhibitions on local traditions. Contacts: 085 8285452, info@mutac.org.

MiC card updated to April 2020 with contradictory timetables: check opening and prices before the visit

  • Where Viale dei Pini 17, Picciano
  • Entry €5, reduced €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Chiesa della Beata Vergine Maria del Soccorso e Piazza Duca degli Abruzzi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

With Piazza Duca degli Abruzzi it forms the main complex representative of the historic center of Picciano: the monumental brick façade dominates the square, about 130 cm lower than the building, and is divided into three parts by binated pilasters, with central portal between two composite columns under a triangular tympanum and side wings with broken tympanums. The interior, of baroque taste, has an inscribed cross plan, dome on the central compartment and semicircular apse. It is the parish church (fornìa di Penne).

  • Where Via Papa Giovanni XXIII 1, Picciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: diocesipescara.it · cultura.gov.it

Chiesa di San Rocco a Piccianello

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church built on a project by Aniello Francia and completed in 1838, on a hill in the middle of the hamlet, in the hilly area of Val del Fino. It has a single rectangular nave marked by Ionian binated pilasters on three spans, with the central covered by a lower dome on plumes; the neoclassical brick façade is crowned by a large triangular tympanum with denticles and has triglyphs in the stripe marchepiano.

  • Where Piazza Martiri Piccianellesi, Piccianello, Picciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: diocesipescara.it · cultura.gov.it
