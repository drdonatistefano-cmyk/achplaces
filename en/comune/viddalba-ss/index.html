What to see in Viddalba

Country of the lower valley of Coghinas, in Anglona, a few minutes from the Gulf of Asinara: the Archaeological Museum with about 80 Roman funerary stele, the sanctuary and necropolis of San Leonardo, the park of Monte San Giovanni and the summit of San Gavino.

Museo archeologico di Viddalba

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

It collects the testimonies of the lower valley of Coghinas from the Neolithic to the Middle Ages: ceramics of the culture of Ozieri, Bonnanaro finds from Monte San Giovanni, a bronze axe from the nuragic village of San Leonardo, jewels in gold, silver and vitreous paste from the Roman necropolis and Punic, Roman and Renaissance coins. The strong piece are about 80 stone funerary stems, from the end of the first century BC and from the first imperial age, with an anthropomorphic figure enclosed by a palm branch, exposed in the open-air moat together with millstones, large vases and reconstructions of graves. April-September Tuesday-Sunday 10-13 and 16-19; October-March 9.30-12.30 and 15-18.

  • Where Piazza Giovanni Maria Angioy, Viddalba
  • Entry €3, reduced €2 (10-18 years); free up to 9 years and over 65
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.mav-viddalba.it

Sources: mav-viddalba.it · sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Santuario e necropoli di San Leonardo

History and architecture · Archaeology

The sanctuary is located near the remains of a nuragic village and the necropolis of the end of the III-IV century AD, which has returned funerary furnishings and stems decorated today at the Archaeological Museum; in the church of San Leonardo the simulacrum of San Giovanni Evangelista is kept.

  • Where Località San Leonardo, Viddalba
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · sardegnacultura.it

Monte San Giovanni e chiesa di San Giovanni Evangelista

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

High with a park and panoramic verandas and the Romanesque church of St. John the Evangelist, of the twelfth century, then rebuilt from scratch; from the mountain come also eneolithic finds and coins exposed to the museum.

  • Where Monte San Giovanni, Viddalba
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · sardegnacultura.it

Monte San Gavino e chiesa di San Gavino a monte

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the summit, about 800 meters away, the church of San Gavino upstream, where it is celebrated on May 1st; the mountain has twenty routes nailed and is frequented for climbing and paragliding.

  • Where Monte San Gavino, Viddalba
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Fiume Coghinas

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The river flows through pine and eucalyptus forests; rich in fish and frequented by water birds, it is used for sport fishing, kayaking and birdwatching.

  • Where Sponde del Coghinas, Viddalba
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
