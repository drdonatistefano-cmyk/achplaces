What to see in Chignolo Po

Pavese village along the Po and the Via Francigena, dominated by the Castle Cusani Visconti Botta Adorno (Castello Procaccini), medieval fortress transformed in the eighteenth century into a villa of delight that houses the Museum of Lombardy Rural Civilization and the Museum of Lombard Wine; in the center a monumental cedar of Lebanon.

Castello di Chignolo Po e Museo lombardo del vino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Born from a medieval fortified nucleus, of which the central tower remains, it passed in 1441 from the Visconti to the Federici and then to the Cusani, owners until 1936, who between 1673 and 1730 transformed it into a villa of delight: Giovanni Ruggeri built the shelter to the north (1695-1703) and Cardinal Agostino Cusani added the four corner towers; the salon was frescoed by 1760 by Pietro Cremascoli and Bianchini, with the ceiling of the four seasons. Pope Clement XI, Napoleon and Francesco I d'Hapsburg-Lorena stayed there; around there is the French garden on terraces with the "Teatro delle Uccelliere" and in the basement the Museum of History and agricultural civilization in Lombardy - Lombard Wine Museum.

private residence, only guided tours of 60 minutes: without reservation Saturday, Sunday and public holidays, in the weekdays only groups of at least 25 people with reservation 48 hours before; forbidden to photograph inside

  • Where Via G. Aglieri 2, Chignolo Po
  • Entry €13, reduced €8 (6-18 years)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · lombardiabeniculturali.it

Cedro di Chignolo Po

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Cedar of Lebanon grown on a small shore where today there is a municipal park near the center: it exceeds 25 meters in height, with a stem of 5 meters of circumference (160 cm of diameter) and a large and globose hair. It is a green monument that can be visited freely.

  • Where Via Marconi, Chignolo Po
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Chiesa di San Lorenzo Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish church started in 1524 on a church with three naves with circular pillars, linked to the Cusani: in 1541 Giovanni Giacomo Cusani built the chapel of the Archangel Raffaele, today baptismal with a red marble bathtub, and in 1577 St. Charles blessed its bells. Marbles, paintings, altars and choirs are of Six, Seven and Nineteenth Century; at the main altar there is a wooden cross carved and gilded with a wooden group of Maddalena, Madonna and San Giovanni, in the chapels an eighteenth-century altarpiece of the martyrdom of San Sebastiano and the wooden statue of the Madonna del Rosario with the paintings of the Mysteries, as well as two confessionals of the seventeenth century. Without architectural barriers.

  • Where Chignolo Po
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.chignolopo.pv.it · in-lombardia.it
