What to see in Sansepolcro

Birthplace of Piero della Francesca and Luca Pacioli, closed by the walls with the fortress of Giuliano da Sangallo.

3 places to visit in Sansepolcro, in the province of Arezzo (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Civico di Sansepolcro

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the medieval Palazzo della Residenza the Resurrection of Piero della Francesca (1450-1463, restored in 2018), the Polyptych of Mercy, the fragments of San Giuliano and San Ludovico, the San Quintino di Pontormo and Robbian terracotta.

  • Where Sansepolcro
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Cattedrale di San Giovanni Evangelista

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The cathedral where Piero worked in 1473-1474; the painter was buried in 1492 in a chapel of the cloister of the ancient abbey.

  • Where Sansepolcro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Aboca Museum

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Museum of Herbs with over 100 medicinal plants, rebuilt laboratories, herbaries, books of botany pharmacy, mortars, ceramics and glass.

  • Where Sansepolcro
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
