What to see in Ghiffa

On the Piedmont shore of Lake Maggiore, the smallest of the Sacred Piedmontese Mountains, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and the traditional hat museum.

3 places to visit in Ghiffa, in the province of Verbano-Cusio-Ossola (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Sacro Monte della Santissima Trinità

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The smallest of the Sacred Piedmontese Mountains, overlooking Lake Maggiore among chestnut woods: the sanctuary of the Trinity, three chapels (Incoronata, San Giovanni Battista, Abramo) and the eighteenth-century porch of the Via Crucis, in a reserve of about 200 hectares. UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  • Where Via Santissima Trinità, Ghiffa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · distrettolaghi.it

Museo dell'arte del cappello

Museums · Themed

At the historic headquarters of the Panizza plant, where felt hats were produced for almost a hundred years exported all over the world.

Centro di Ghiffa

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The parish church of San Maurizio, the Blessed Virgin Assumption and the elegant Art Nouveau buildings on the lake.
