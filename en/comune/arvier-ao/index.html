What to see in Arvier

The ancient Roman Arebrigium among the vineyards of the Enfer, with two medieval villages side by side, Arvier and Leverogne, and four castles.

5 places to visit in Arvier, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello de La Mothe

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built towards the end of the 13th century, it dominates the village with its square tower.

  • Where Arvier
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it

Borgo di Leverogne e ospizio dei pellegrini

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village with the frescoes of the pilgrims' hospice founded in 1368 and the old Roman bridge, rest of the road to the Gauls.

  • Where Leverogne, Arvier
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it
