What to see in Serramazzoni

Tourist center of the Modena Apennines at 791 meters, in panoramic position, announced by the Tower of the Bastille; in the territory the Romanesque parish church of Rocca Santa Maria, the medieval villages of Pompeano and Monfestino, the Museum Garden of the Ancient Rose of Montagnana and the waterfalls of the Bucamante.

11 places to visit in Serramazzoni, in the province of Modena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The church was built between the 8th and 9th centuries and was mentioned since 971, which belonged to Bonifacio di Toscana and then to Matilde di Canossa, who donated it in 1108 to the bishop of Modena; it rises on a cliff almost inaccessible above the valley of the river Fossa, next to an ancient fortress. The interior with three naves has capitals of the 11th century with vegetable braids, leaves and volutes, among the most beautiful in northern Italy, and a base of column with heads of lion, goat, bear and grifo; the restorations of 1913-1937 have recovered the Romanesque aspect. Open every day from 9 am to 6 pm.

  • Where Via Rocca Santa Maria, Serramazzoni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: comune.serramazzoni.mo.it · visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo Giardino della Rosa Antica di Montagnana

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Park of 43 hectares at 420 meters, on a panoramic ridge about 25 km from Modena, of which three are dedicated to over 800 varieties of roses: ancient and classic rose garden, "Herbario dei sensi" (Herbary of the senses), "Le roses rediscovered" (Roses found) and a nursery of roses in pot; the shop sells roses products and can be ordered with local specialties. The cynorrhoids are admired from July to January.

open every day from sunrise to sunset only from April 15th to June 30th; in the other months visits only by reservation

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: comune.serramazzoni.mo.it · visitmodena.it

Borgo medievale di Pompeano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Feudal castle of late-medieval foundation, belonged to the Counts Da Gombola until the beginning of the fifteenth century: the small village, the thirteenth-century tower and the remains of the crenellated walls rise on a mass of serpentine, rock of underwater volcanic origin. Nearby there is the "Grotta Taxoni" (Taxoni Grotto).

  • Where Pompeano, Serramazzoni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it

Borgo di Monfestino

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village at 822 meters on a spur, about 4 km from the capital for a panoramic road; of the 11th century castle that dominates the valley of the Tiepid remains two towers and a stretch of walls, incorporated into a private property.

  • Where Monfestino, Serramazzoni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Torre della Bastiglia

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

A watchtower that precedes the town of Serramazzoni, probably built between the 13th and 14th centuries to connect the hilly fortifications to the castles of the Apennines, in support of the castle of Monfestino.

  • Where Serramazzoni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · comune.serramazzoni.mo.it · visitmodena.it

Montagnana e la campana più antica del modenese

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Frazione sulla Via Giardini about 10 km from the capital: the church of Sant'Andrea Apostolo, a single nave, designed by Modena engineer Antonio Vandelli incorporating the oldest building, houses the oldest bell in Modena, with the inscription of the year 1262. Next is the Church of the Resistance, built in 1965 in memory of the partisan struggle of 1943-45.

  • Where Località Montagnana, Serramazzoni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: comune.serramazzoni.mo.it · visitmodena.it

Sasso delle Streghe

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

A limestone monolith about 9 meters high, in an oak wood near the parish church of Rocca Santa Maria, which contains hundreds of marine fossils; it is reached with a steep path.

  • Where Rocca Santa Maria, Serramazzoni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it

Cascate del Bucamante

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Naturalistic oasis with five natural waterfalls and rare plant species on the river Bucamante, the main tributary of the Tiepid, in the valley between Monfestino and Monte Cornazzano; to the place is linked a legend.

  • Where Valle del Bucamante, Serramazzoni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Faeto e la Via Vandelli

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Borgo 4 km from the capital, on top of a road that rises to 869 meters between ancient oaks and chestnut groves; in the nearby woods remain fragments of the Via Vandelli, the road between Emilia and Tuscany of 1749 that takes its name from Domenico Vandelli, who directed the construction.

  • Where Faeto, Serramazzoni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Two outings of serpentinitic ophiolite, rare dark green rock clusters of eruptive origin and geological importance, in the two villages a few hundred meters away about 10 km from Serramazzoni.
