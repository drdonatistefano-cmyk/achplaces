What to see in Acceglio

Municipality of the Upper Maira Valley, in the Middle Ages the capital of Ugonotti: in the hamlet Villa the seventeenth-century church of the Annunziata with the Museum of Sacred Art in the Confraternita, in Chialvetta the ethnographic museum La Misoun d'en bot and, towards the French border, the springs of the Maira, Chiappera, the Provençal Rocca and the waterfalls of Stroppia.

7 places to visit in Acceglio, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo d'arte sacra dell'Alta Valle Maira

Museums · Old masters

Housed in the Oratory of the Fraternity of the Annunciation, next to the church, it is divided into five areas dedicated to local devotion, reconstruction projects and paintings and sculptures of value. Open only by reservation (tel. +39 0171 99008).

  • Where Frazione Borgo Villa, Acceglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Museo etnografico La Misoun d'en bot

Museums · Themed

Born in 1992, it reconstructs the interior of a traditional house with about 1,500 objects collected in the abandoned villages of the valley of Unerzio: on the first floor work tools and objects of daily mountain life. Opening on request (tel. +39 0171 99017).

  • Where Osteria della Gardetta, borgata Chialvetta, Acceglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Chiesa dell'Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the seventeenth century in the hamlet Villa, main testimony of the past of a village that in the Middle Ages was the cornerstone of the Huguenots and in the sixteenth century knew the condemnations of the Inquisition and the Sabauda repression of Calvinist doctrines.

  • Where Borgata Villa, Acceglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Tele di Matteo Olivero nel Municipio

Museums · Modern art

A section set up in the town hall exhibits some paintings by Matteo Olivero, a painter born in Pratorotondo, who formed at the Albertina Academy in Turin and close to the divisionism of Segantini.

  • Where Municipio, Acceglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Sorgenti del Maira

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Plenty of water flowing at the foot of the mountain, in a basin with a small artificial pond: here the stream that gives the name to the valley is born.

  • Where Sopra Saretto, Acceglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Chiappera e Rocca Provenzale

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Chiappera (1,614 m) is the last inhabited village of the Upper Maira Valley, dominated by the profile of the Provençal-Castle spur of quartzite, a climbing destination; besides, the road becomes dirt road towards the Stroppia waterfalls and the French border can be reached only on foot from the Maurin Hill. It is the base for GTA, Chamberyron Tour and Occitani Routes; between Colle Greguri and Monte Maniglia you can see ibex.

  • Where Borgata Chiappera, Acceglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.acceglio.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Cascate di Stroppia

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Waterfalls at Chiappera indicated as the highest in Italy, visible from June to early August; in winter the area around hosts cross-country skiing, snowshoeing and ski touring.

  • Where Oltre Chiappera, Acceglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · comune.acceglio.cn.it
