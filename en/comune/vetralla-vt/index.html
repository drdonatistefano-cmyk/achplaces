What to see in Vetralla

Country of Tuscia at the foot of Mount Fogliano, between the via Cassia and the via Francigena: the Romanesque San Francesco with the crypt excavated in the tuff, the church of Santa Maria di Foro Cassio with the Madonna attributed to the young Masaccio, and in the territory the Etruscan tombs facade of Norchia and the great mound of Grotta Porcina.

9 places to visit in Vetralla, in the province of Viterbo (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Necropoli rupestre di Norchia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Etruscan city along the Via Clodia, bloomed between the end of the IV and the middle of the II century BC: facade tombs dug in the cliff on two or three orders, such as the Tomb of the Three Heads, the Prostila, the Tombs Smurinas and the famous Tombs in Temple on the ditch of the Acqualta; on the plateau the medieval remains of the parish church of St. Peter, a castle and a tower, and the stretch of Via Clodia cut into the tuff at Cava Buia.

  • Where S.S. 1 Aurelia bis, tra Vetralla e Monte Romano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Necropoli di Grotta Porcina

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Tomb of a powerful Etruscan family of the 6th century BC along the Via Clodia: the mound called "Grande Ruota" is 28 metres in diameter excavated in the tuff, with rooms connected by air wells, and downstream a cylindrical altar with traces of orientalizing frieze in the middle of a sort of rock theatre. Last dirt track, free access.

  • Where Dal km 26,6 della SS Aurelia bis, Vetralla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Complesso di Santa Maria di Foro Cassio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On the remains of the Roman center of Forum Cassii, stop of the Via Francigena: in 1145 entrusted to the Order Gerosolimitano as a hospice for pilgrims, then the Knights of Malta. It preserves a great Crucifixion of the 11th-XII century, apsidal frescoes of the 12th century and a Madonna enthroned attributed to the young Masaccio (1423).

  • Where Strada Foro Cassio, Vetralla
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Chiesa e cripta di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt in Romanesque style and re-consecrated in 1207, with Roman re-enactments from Foro Cassio on the façade, a cosmatesque floor of the thirteenth century, a San Bernardino attributed to Andrea del Castagno and the white marble sarcophagus of Briobris Di Vico (dead in 1353); the crypt of the VII-VIII century, dug into the tuff, has six small naves.

  • Where Largo San Francesco 11, Vetralla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Museo della città e del territorio

Museums · City history

In the fifteenth-century Torre di Porta Marina, over a thousand finds on three floors from the Etruscans to traditional handicrafts: archaic and Renaissance ceramics, liturgical art, paintings, medieval and industrial archaeology, with audioguides and workshops.

  • Where Via di Porta Marchetta 2, Vetralla
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Palazzo Comunale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in 1731-1735 by Filippo Barigioni and included in the network of the historical residences of Lazio: on the first floor Roman and medieval marbles under the coats of arms of Pope Julius II, Henry VIII and Cardinal Christopher Bainbridge, who had jurisdiction over Vetralla in 1511.

  • Where Vetralla
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Castello dei Di Vico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Venue of the Di Vico family: here Giacomo, the last of the family, was captured, beheaded in Soriano nel Cimino in 1435.

  • Where Vetralla
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Monte Fogliano ed eremo di San Girolamo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The mountain donated to Vetralla by Pope Eugenio IV in 1432: in the beech forest, the hermitage of St. Jerome excavated in the volcanic rock, where he lived since 1525 Fr.Jerome Gabrielli; every May 8, at the convent of St. Angel, the Married of the Tree, rite of 1470 in which the mayor marries a ceremonial of the Selva del Fogliano.

  • Where Monte Fogliano, Vetralla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Santuario di Demetra a Macchia delle Valli

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Etruscan-Roman shrine at a spring, recently discovered and frequented from the end of the III century BC at the beginning of the II AD: the small cell in pepper kept the terracotta statue of the goddess seated on the throne, with the counter, a ritual table, the altar, the bathtub and a natural cave connected to the cell.

  • Where Macchia delle Valli, Vetralla
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
