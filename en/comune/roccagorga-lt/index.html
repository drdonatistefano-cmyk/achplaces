What to see in Roccagorga

A village of medieval origins of the Lepini Mountains, about 110 km from Rome on a spur of the Monte Nero, grown around the Baronale Doria Pamphili Palace that today houses the EtnoMuseum of the Lepini Mountains; 850 meters is the Hermitage of Sant'Erasmo (XI-XII century), reachable on foot between holm oaks and olive groves.

Palazzo Baronale Doria Pamphili

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The nucleus from which the village was born: from the first tower with fence it passed to a rectangular castle, transformed in the sixteenth century from military structure to noble residence; in 1747 it counted 54 rooms and in 1753 there was annexed the church of St. Maria, in part transformed into a private chapel designed by Paolo Posi. He passed to Ceccano, Caetani, Aldobrandini, Ginnetti, Orsini and Doria Pamphilj, who in 1811 placed the stone coat of arms on the portal; the Municipality bought it around 1930. Today, with the clock bell tower, it houses the EtnoMuseum, the municipal library and the hostel "Il Tarlo Baronale."

  • Where Piazza Risorgimento, Roccagorga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comuneroccagorga.it · italia.it

EtnoMuseo Monti Lepini

Museums · Themed

Anthropological demoetanological museum in the Baronial Palace, dedicated to the identity of the Lepine communities and reference for this discipline in the territorial system of the Lepini Mountains and in the Demos System. The route, based on research on the territory, focuses on sensory involvement with installations, settings and listening stations in seven environments: those of the Rock, Splendor Cinema, Red Easter, Habitus, Grani di storia, Garage-culture and Noi loro. He also remembers the massacre of January 6, 1913, when seven citizens were killed during a strike, with sculptures inspired by the "Fourth State" of Pellizza da Volpedo.

  • Where Piazza Risorgimento 4, Roccagorga
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · retemusei.regione.lazio.it · comuneroccagorga.it · italia.it

Museo civico «Assi dell'Aeronautica»

Museums · Themed

Born in 1986 on the initiative of the Association Arma Aeronautica, the year of the twinning of the country with the 9th Stormo . "Francesco Baracca" today has its headquarters in the school building "Giuseppe Restaini." It exhibits a permanent exhibition on General Umberto Nobile, the diary of Francesco Baracca, showcases on the two world wars and on the War of Liberation, the reconstruction of the plane accident of 10 November 1936 in Roccagorga in which the second lieutenant pilot Andrea Fumo (of which the saber is exposed) died and a library of about 300 volumes on aviation. Visits only by appointment with the curator.

  • Where Via Domenico Menta, Roccagorga
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comuneroccagorga.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Chiesa dei SS. Leonardo ed Erasmo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in the sixteenth century and plundered in 1798 by French soldiers, who spared only the bust of St. Erasmus.

  • Entry Free

Chiesa della Madonna delle Rose

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

He guards a Madonna with Child donated in 1875 by the German painter Franz Johann Heinrich Nadorp; tradition links the church to the miracle of Sister Claudia De Angelis, after whose stop the spiritual father found bouquets of roses on the altar.

Eremo di Sant'Erasmo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Complex of sanctuary and monastery of the XI-XII centuries at about 850 meters above sea level, frequented until the end of the sixties and today used as hostel. It is reached by the path of the Leccio.

  • Where Monte sopra Roccagorga, circa 850 m
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Sentieri dell'Ulivo-Arco e del Leccio-Eremo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The trail of the Olive-Arco runs for more than 4 km between the olive groves of the village; that of the Leccio climbs to the Hermitage of Sant'Erasmo and arrives at the slopes of the Anchevisa. The territory is suitable for hiking, hiking and mountain biking.

  • Entry Free

Uva fragola e cucina lepina

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The local speciality is the strawberry grape, celebrated in October with a festival; among the typical products also olive oil, goat meat, vegetable soup "rappagacornuti" and "ciammotte" (lumache).
