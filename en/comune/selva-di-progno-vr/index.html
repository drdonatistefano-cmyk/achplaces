What to see in Selva di Progno

The town of Lessinia in the upper Val d'Illasi, where you can still hear the "tàucias garéida": in Giazza the Cimbri Museum tells the people of Bavarian-Tyrolean origins settled on these mountains between the XII and XIV century, in San Bortolo delle Montagne the old church houses the Museum of Trumpets, and the Cyclopedonale of Val d'Illasi goes up the Progno up to the forest of Giazza.

Museo dei Cimbri di Giazza

Museums · Themed

Ethnographic Museum and documentation centre on the cimbrian people, of Bavarian-Tyrolean origin, allocated in Lessinia between the XII and XIV century; Giazza is the closest area to the original language and traditions. The multi-storey itinerary presents language, family life, work, architecture, religiousity and popular beliefs, with art and cimbri crafts; the center organizes demonstrations and courses every year. Open Saturday and Sunday 15-18.

  • Where Via dei Boschi 62, Giazza, Selva di Progno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Museo dei Trombini

Museums · Themed

In the old church of San Bortolo is exhibited the collection of trombini, heavy firearms derived from the archibugi of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, born as weapons of defense and become a festive instrument for celebrations, commemorations and festivals; St. Bortolo keeps alive the tradition of shooting. Ancient specimens, decorated and carved, with films and projections. Open from 1 April to 30 September on Sundays and holidays 10-12 and 15-18.30; from October to March and in weekdays only by appointment for groups of at least 10 people.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Veneto, San Bortolo delle Montagne, Selva di Progno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Chiesa di San Bartolomeo delle Montagne

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

San Bortolo has been inhabited by the Cimbri since 1287; the first church of San Bartolomeo dates back to the beginning of the fifteenth century, and the new, larger one, was built between 1894 and 1904 with the parish priest Don Giobatta Valentini, with facade completed in 1960. Neoclassical façade with four semi-colons Corinthians, tympanum and rose window; single nave interior with four side chapels and dome on the presbytery. Next to the old church the stone bell tower of Lessinia.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Veneto 28, San Bortolo delle Montagne, Selva di Progno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Ciclopedonale della Val d'Illasi

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

15 km of route between green hills, with a series of walkways that cross several times the Progno d'Illasi between the right and the left bank; connect Cogollo di Tregnago (starting in Marani) with the historic village of Cimbro di Giazza and ends in the forest of Giazza, running gently at the foot of the mountains.

  • Where Giazza, Selva di Progno (partenza loc. Marani, Tregnago)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it
