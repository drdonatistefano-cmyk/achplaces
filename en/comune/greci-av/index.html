What to see in Greek

The only town in Campania (Katundi), with the Mother Church of St. Bartholomew dominating the upper part of the village, the sixteenth-century Palazzo Lusi with its archaeological exhibition, the Museo Vedovato in Palazzo Caccese and the last kalives, ancient stone houses of the Arbëreshë community.

Chiesa Madre di San Bartolomeo Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Mother Church of the village, dedicated to the patron saint of Bartholomew, dominates the highest part of the village. There is celebrated the solemn Mass of the feast of Our Lady of the Carosene.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Palazzo Caccese e Museo Vedovato

Museums · Themed

The nineteenth-century building houses the museum dedicated to Giuseppe Vedovato (1912-2012), born in Greci, President emeritus of the Council of Europe.

Insediamento arbëreshë e kalive

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The nucleus of the settlement arbëreshë preserves the surviving kalives, ancient stone houses with wooden roof and tiles.
