What to see in Frigento

Borgo irpino between the valleys of Ufita and Fredane, with an eighteenth-century center rebuilt after the earthquake of 1732: the walk of the Limits with a view over five regions, the former cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta with its hypogeum, the noble palaces, the FriMact museum at Palazzo De Leo and the Roman cisterns of the first century BC.

Ex Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta e ipogeo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

The present appearance dates back to the reconstruction after the earthquake of 1732: the ceiling paintings are by Antonio Vecchione da Nola (XVIII century), with a major altar in polychrome marble and statues of the Seven-Ottocento. In the hypogeum remain the early medieval building (VIII-IX century), a Roman re-utilization column and the ancient scroungers; an inscription in the church recalls the architect Antistius, designer of the Roman cisterns.

Cisterne romane

History and architecture · Archaeology

Large water complex of Republican age (1st century BC) in uncertain work, at 911 meters at the highest point of the municipal territory: the galleries collected rain, loose snow and water from a nearby spring, then purified and distributed to the village and its surroundings. Of the eleven rooms, perhaps, there are four, three rooms that can be visited, reached by a non-original entrance.

Palazzo De Leo e Museo FriMact

Museums · Archaeological

Palazzo Gentilizio 19th century, house of the literary Marciano De Leo, today House of Culture with the Civic Library and the FriMact Museum, which combines an archaeological section on the history of the village with a collection of contemporary art among the three most important in Southern Italy.

Palazzi gentilizi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

A dozen palaces from the Seven-nineteenth century with portals made of local stone (Irpina breccia), restored after the 1980 earthquake, along the route from Via Roma to Vico San Pietro passing through the former cathedral square: Capobianco, Filippone, Pelosi, Flammia, Gella-Calò and Testa-Cipriano, with paved courtyard, well-tank and stone staircase. They are admired from the outside; in the Town Hall the plaster busts of the sculptor Carmine Genua (1851-1924).

Panoramica dei Limiti

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Urban park and pedestrian walk with a 360 degree panorama of five regions and eleven provinces, from Vesuvius to Maiella passing through Matese and Vulture.

Santuario della Madonna del Buon Consiglio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

A pilgrimage destination in Pagliara, center of Marian devotion and mother house of the Institute of the Franciscan Friars of the Immaculate Church; it houses a reed organ of 25 registers of the Venetian builder Alessandro Girotto.
