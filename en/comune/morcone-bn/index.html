What to see in Morcone

Borgo on a hill at the eastern entrance of the Matese National Park, with the Norman castle built on polygonal walls Sannite, the convent where Padre Pio made the novitiate and Casa Sania, cultural center with garden and outdoor theater; in early January the living nativity scene.

Casa Sannia e giardini

Museums · City history

House of a family perhaps descended from Catalan knights arrived in the Spanish age, inhabited until the mid-Twentieth century and transformed by the Municipality into a cultural center after 1980: portal eighteenth century in rustic, three bronze bells from destroyed churches, exhibition on emigration, historical cuisine, historical municipal archive, art museum with works collected by Enrico Crispolti in the eighties and archaeological finds. In the garden an outdoor theater for summer events.

  • Where Via Achille Sannia 51, Morcone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Castello normanno e mura poligonali

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The castle, part of Ariano County, rises on the remains of a Sannite fortification in polygonal walls; it lived there the Queen Margaret of Durazzo with her children, including the future King Ladislao, who in 1381 renewed the statutes of the country.

  • Where Morcone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village on the hill preserves the remains of the medieval loggia of the Universitas, the Comital Palace of the Gaetani, the churches of Santa Maria de Stampatis and the Santissima Annunziata, Piazza del Pozzo, Piazza San Bernardino, Porta San Marco and the ancient Mulino Florio; in the streams near the ravines for the active wool between the sixteenth and eighteenth centuries.

  • Where Morcone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it
