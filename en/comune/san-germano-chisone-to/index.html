What to see in San Germano Chisone

Country of Val Chisone linked to the history of the widemann cotton mill and the Rochon ski factory: the Villa of 1940 of the Widemanns, now Town Hall, is located in an English park of over 17,000 square meters, stop of the Pinerolese district of the hydrangeas, and hosts the Museum Skirochon on the history of skiing.

Parco comunale e Villa Widemann

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

Villa and park were built in 1940 by Vittorio Widemann, owner of the cotton mill, and purchased by the Municipality in 1979; the villa, already Prà Maria, in travertine with columns on terrace and protiro, houses the Town Hall. The landscaped park in English (17,267 m2) has over thirty botanical families among beech pendulous and purple, cedars, cryptomeries, a lake with rhododendrons and azalee, a avenue of cherry blossoms and a collection of 21 varieties of camellias of ancient Piedmontese cultivars and of the Verbano; it is part of the Pinerolese district of the hydrangeas, with guided tours in June-July. From the villa you can see the valley of Pramollo and the Gran Truc.

Museum Skirochon

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated for Turin 2006 in the Villa Widemann, it tells the origin of skiing, the birth of the Sestriere station and the history of the Rochon company, an artisan workshop that between 1930 and 1960 produced wooden skis and supplied the 3rd Pinerolo Alpine Regiment, with finds and documents preserved by the last heir Dina Pons. The hall ... The Factory. Women and work are collected from the former Waldensian Museum on the cotton factory Widemann and the female condition, donated by the Waldensian Consistory in 2009. Visits by appointment (tel. 0121 58601).
