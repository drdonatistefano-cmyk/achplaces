What to see in La Thuile

Green basin at the foot of the Colle del Piccolo San Bernardo, between the Rutor Falls, the Roman remains and the cromlech of the pass and the ancient coal mines.

8 places to visit in La Thuile, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open La Thuile in the guide with map and filters

Cascate del Rutor

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Three water jumps between rocky gorges and woods, fed by the Rutor glacier, observed by wooden and metal bridges.

  • Where La Joux, La Thuile
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Colle del Piccolo San Bernardo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The valley between Italy and France with the cromlech of the Salassi, which at the summer solstice lights up with games of light, the Roman remains of the mansio, the via delle Gallie, a small temple and a column at Jupiter, united by the easy Path of Memory.

  • Where Colle del Piccolo San Bernardo, La Thuile
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Ospizio del Piccolo San Bernardo

Museums · City history

Medieval hospice damaged by wars, now home to a museum on the history of the hill.

  • Where Colle del Piccolo San Bernardo, La Thuile
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Giardino botanico alpino Chanousia

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Viewpoint

Founded by Abbot Pierre Chanoux at the end of the nineteenth century to make known and protect the alpine flora; it blooms in summer.

  • Where Colle del Piccolo San Bernardo, La Thuile
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Lago Verney

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 2088 meters, of an intense blue and surrounded by peaks.

Percorsi delle miniere

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Paths in the woods along the ancient tracks, between tunnels and old trolleys, with an open-air exhibition of the buildings of the coal mines closed in the sixties.

  • Where La Thuile
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it
