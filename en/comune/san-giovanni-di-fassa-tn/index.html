What to see in San Giovanni di Fassa

The Ladin municipality of Sèn Jan brings together Vigo and Pozza di Fassa under Catinaccio and Latemar: the late Dogotic parish church of San Giovanni with the 67-metre bell tower, Santa Giuliana with the Georg Artzt triptych, the Ladin de Fascia Museum with its sections on the territory, the Ciampedìe terrace and the thermal baths of the only sulphur spring in Trentino.

9 places to visit in San Giovanni di Fassa, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pieve di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Late-Gothic church consecrated in 1489 on an earlier Romanesque church, symbol of the village, with the 67-metre-long bell tower covered with larch stairs; in the apse frescoes by Ruprecht Potsch (1498) and paintings by David Solbach (1578). Nearby stands the Ciasa Soldà, national monument of the sixteenth century.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Chiesa di Santa Giuliana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Documented in 1237, rebuilt and consecrated in 1452, it preserves the triptych of the Trinity made of polychrome and golden wood by Georg Artzt (1517), among the most precious wooden works of Trentino, although some statues are copies.

  • Where Vigo di Fassa, San Giovanni di Fassa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visittrentino.info

Vigo e le sue frazioni

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Until the nineteenth century Vigo was the administrative center of the valley, seat of justice, assemblies and religious power; it is a village spread in six hamlets, among the most beautiful villages since 2017. In Valle the barns, including the oldest of Vigo (1606); in Costa a large fountain with three wash-houses; in Larzonei a house with two bread-ovens; in Tamion, the highest hamlet, the church of the Trinity of 1708; in Vallonga the eighteenth-century church of San Giovanni Nepomuceno. A ring of about 3 hours, suitable for all, touches churches and barns with views of Rodella, Sassolungo and Sassopiatto.

  • Where Vigo di Fassa, San Giovanni di Fassa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · fassa.com · visittrentino.info

Museo Ladin de Fascia

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Modern Museum with ethnographic collections of the Ladin Cultural Institute, with multimedia supports and tables by the designer Milo Manara; Mobicult audioguide in six languages (including Ladin) and experience in virtual reality Pigui Experience (€2 more, by reservation).

  • Where Strada de Sèn Jan 9, San Giovanni di Fassa
  • Entry €7, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.visittrentino.info

Sources: istladin.net · visittrentino.info

L Molin, il mulino del Museo Ladin

Museums · Themed

Working mill in the area of Pezol, of the Rizzi family (Salin) from the nineteenth century: two millstones for barley, an oil mill, a pestle with vertical motion and a windmill. Restored by Giuseppe Longo and opened in 1983, it was the first section on the territory of the Ladin Museum.

  • Where Strada Jumela, Pera di Fassa, San Giovanni di Fassa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: istladin.net · visittrentino.info

Ciampedìe

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Natural terrace at 1.998 meters above Vigo, reachable on foot or by cable car, starting point of excursions; in winter there are the facilities of the Skiarea Catinaccio and there is the Kinderpark Ciampedie Laurin, among the highest baby parks in the world.

  • Where Vigo di Fassa, San Giovanni di Fassa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

QC Terme Dolomiti (Terme Dolomia)

Local life and traditions · Spas

Powered by the sulfur source of Alloch, known since the late fifteenth century and the only of this type in Trentino, which flows about 1,320 meters in the Monzoni group at 9.5 °C; the modern spa opened in 2003, with conventional care. The wellness area on more than 4,000 m2 and three levels has Japanese bathrooms, saunas, rooms of the rooms, outdoor dining rooms, Kneipp path and swimming pools.

  • Where Strada di Bagnes 21, Pozza di Fassa, San Giovanni di Fassa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: fassa.com · visittrentino.info

Val San Nicolò e Torre di Pozza

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient alpine pasture just above Pozza, with the Chapel of the Crucifix the destination of a crossroads and the Festa ta Mont every year; it is known for its traditional mountain huts and is frequented by climbers. At the entrance of the valley the Tower of Pozza, the rest of the defensive system of the Corte Bassa di Fassa, flanked by agricultural buildings of the nineteenth century.

  • Where Val San Nicolò, Pozza di Fassa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info
