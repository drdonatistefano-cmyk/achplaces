What to see in Ginosa

Country of the Taranto Ionic arch surrounded by about 3 km of ravine, with the rock villages of Rivolta and Casale, the Norman castle built in 1080 by Robert Guiscard and the sixteenth-century Matrix Church at the foot of the ravine. On the sea, the Marina di Ginosa, long beach with pine trees, Blue Flag.

Villaggio rupestre di Rivolta e gravina

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The horseshoe ravine, about 3 km of green that was the natural defense of the country during the invasions, hosts on the right side of the descent the village of Rivolta: 66 caves on five levels, considered one of the most important rock villages in Italy, with cisterns, ovens, fireplaces, gardens and palms for oil and wine, of a very high ingenuity. The first settlement was dug into the tuff and the parietal decorations are still visible. The ravine is set to "The Gospel according to Matthew of Pasolini (1964) and to "Who has seen me" (1964). Better go in spring or early autumn, morning or evening.

Rione Casale e chiese rupestri

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

On the opposite side of the ravine the village of Casale is known mainly for the numerous rock churches and frescoed crypts, including Santa Sofia and Santa Domenica. Probably the risk of landslides and sudden floods prompted the inhabitants to move to the caves of Rivolta, on the opposite side; from Casale you have the best view on Rivolta and its cave levels.

Castello normanno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Impressive Norman castle of the 11th century, erected in 1080 by Robert Guiscardo, symbol of resistance against the Saracens: it dominates the historic center and the new Ginosa, which rises from the Mother Church to the castle.

Chiesa Matrice della Madonna del SS. Rosario

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Renaissance church built in the sixteenth century in honour of Our Lady of the Rosary, at the foot of the ravine among the traditional houses, with the churchyard overlooking the ravine. Entirely in local tuff, it has two entrances (the main one with five steps flanked by columns with capitals with angel heads), bell tower with pyramid cusp and lantern, interior with three naves with barrel vault in the central and a fresco of San Martino di Saverio Ierace. Tel. 334 3050780.

Museo Civico del Territorio «Santa Parasceve»

Museums · Archaeological

The museum recounts the territory through finds of more eras: traces of frequentation of the Middle Paleolithic (developed phase of the Musterian culture), apulian craters, epichysis and kyathos of the peucet culture (VI-II century BC), objects of the Roman municipality of Genusia including a tabula patronatus of 395 A.D., lay majolica from the rock villages of Casale and Revolt and ceramics of the 11th century. It takes its name from the Benedictine monastery of Santa Parasheve, built with the castle after the Norman conquest at the end of the 11th century.
