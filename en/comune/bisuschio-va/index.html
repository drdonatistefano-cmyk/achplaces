What to see in Bisuschio

Borgo della Valceresio surrounded by mountains and hills, a stone's throw from Lake Ceresio, known for Villa Cicogna Mozzoni, one of the most famous villas of delight in Lombardy; in the village the church of San Giuseppe, considered the oldest building, and the parish church of San Giorgio.

Villa Cicogna Mozzoni

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Born as a hunting casino of the noble Mozzoni (first body before 1440), it was enlarged after 1530 and in 1559 decorated with frescoes by the brothers Campi di Cremona and their school; from the marriage of the daughter of Ascanio Mozzoni with Giovan Pietro Cicogna was born the family Cicogna Mozzoni, whose descendants still manage it. U-shaped plant with courtyard and Doric colonnade in Viggiù stone, twelve frescoed and furnished rooms (dance halls, four-poster beds, coffered ceilings, library). The Italian terrace garden wanted by Ascanio in 1560, with fish pond, double row cypresses, fountain with mask and secret garden, continues beyond the viewpoint in the romantic park. Season 2026 from 29 March to 25 October, Sunday and holidays 9.30-12 and 14.30-19; groups over 20 people also weekdays by reservation.

Chiesa di San Giuseppe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Dedicated to San Dionigi until 1721, it is considered the oldest building still existing in the village, probably of the eleventh century. The restorations brought to light the foundations of a second apse and remains of graves, clues of a cemetery area of the VI-VII century.
