What to see in Montecchio Emilia

A small town at the ford of Enza, built around the castle of the Matilda period renovated by the Estensi; in the center porticos, tower houses and the parish church of San Donnino, just outside the Sanctuary of the Olmo and Villa Aiola the museum of Parmigiano Reggiano.

Castello di Montecchio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

A first fortress rose in 1113 at the behest of Beatrice di Canossa and a document by Matilde of 1114 quotes the castle; the present mast is of the second half of the thirteenth century, the ditches of 1437-38. The Estensi turned it into a noble residence (since 1638 Prince Louis d'Este lived there); the central crenellated tower, about 35 meters high, houses the municipal library and offers views of the village.

Centro storico, mura e oratori

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The medieval nucleus, the 'cittadella', preserves a stretch of walls with three bastions almost intact, alleys, arcades and tower houses; the Town Hall is of 1577, work of the Malombri brothers, the arcades of Piazza Repubblica (13 arches) of 1823. Around the New Church (1618), the Oratory of St. Anthony Abbot documented from 1364, and the fifteenth-century Casa Cavezzi.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Chiesa di San Donnino Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The oldest church in the village, mentioned in 1233, rebuilt in 1596-1600 in Baroque forms with three naves; the chapel of the Most Holy was decorated in 1903 by Augusto Mussini, the eighteenth-century altarpiece with San Donnino is by J.B. The Bel and the nineteenth-century choir in carved wood is by the Brindani cabinetmakers.

  • Where Via E. Franchini 47, Montecchio Emilia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.montecchio-emilia.re.it · italia.it

Santuario della Beata Vergine dell'Olmo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Born from a chapel erected around 1487 around the elm of the apparition, destroyed in 1558 and rebuilt, it was completed in 1884 by Pio Casoli; eighteenth-century façade, baroque bell tower of over 40 meters, high altar of Carmela Adani (1938) and frescoes by Anselmo Govi (1935). Visitable on request.

  • Where Strada Bassa 2, Montecchio Emilia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.montecchio-emilia.re.it · italia.it

Museo storico del Parmigiano Reggiano e della civiltà contadina della Val d'Enza

Museums · Themed

Born from the ethnographic group La Barchessa founded in 1977, it tells the history and production of Parmigiano Reggiano and the peasant life of the valley: about 40 carriages, carriages and agricultural wagons inlaid between the late nineteenth and mid twentieth centuries and about 3,000 work tools. Access to be agreed with the Association La Barchessa.

  • Where Via Copellini 13, Villa Aiola, Montecchio Emilia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.montecchio-emilia.re.it · italia.it
