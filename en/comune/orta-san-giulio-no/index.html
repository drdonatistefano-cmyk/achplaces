What to see in Orta San Giulio

The village on the peninsula of Lake Orta, Orange Flag and among the most beautiful villages in Italy, with the island of San Giulio and the Sacro Monte Franciscan UNESCO World Heritage Site.

6 places to visit in Orta San Giulio, in the province of Novara (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Isola di San Giulio e basilica

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry · Viewpoint

The island of about 650 meters perimeter with the Via del Silenzio and the Meditation and the Benedictine abbey; the basilica founded in the fourth century, Romanesque plant, with the serpentine ambo of Oira and the crypt with the silver urn of 1697 with the relics of the saint.

  • Where Isola di San Giulio, Orta San Giulio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it

Sacro Monte di Orta

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

The devotional path started in 1590, with 20 frescoed chapels and life-size terracotta groups on the life of St. Francis; from the churchyard of St. Nicholas one of the most beautiful views of the lake. UNESCO World Heritage.

  • Where Via al Sacro Monte, Orta San Giulio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: distrettolaghi.it · wedding.visitpiemonte.com

Piazza Motta e il Palazzotto

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The living room of the village overlooking the lake, with the Palazzotto of 1582, seat of the Community of the Riviera of San Giulio.

  • Where Piazza Motta, Orta San Giulio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it

Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church of 1485, rebuilt in the eighteenth century.

Palazzi del borgo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The fifteenth-century Casa dei Nani, the oldest in Orta, Palazzo Gemelli with frescoed facades with mythological subjects and the eighteenth-century Palazzo De Fortis Penotti (Ubertini).

  • Where Orta San Giulio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it

Torre di Buccione

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill above the southern part of the lake, a 23-metre-high signal tower of an outpost documented since the 12th century.

  • Where Orta San Giulio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
