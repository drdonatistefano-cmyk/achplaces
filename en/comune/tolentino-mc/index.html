What to see in Tolentino

City of the Chienti valley linked to St. Nicholas and Napoleon, who signed the Treaty of 1797: the Basilica of St. Nicholas with the frescoed Cappellone, the Castle of the Rancia and, just outside, the Cistercian Abbey of Fiastra in its natural reserve.

10 places to visit in Tolentino, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Basilica di San Nicola e Cappellone

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Portal between the flowered Gothic and the first Renaissance, Augustinian cloister of the first half of the fourteenth century and the Cappellone di San Nicola with frescoes by Pietro da Rimini and students; the museum of the basilica preserves paintings, sculptures, ceramics, ex-vote and a permanent nativity scene.

  • Where Tolentino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it

Abbazia di Santa Maria di Chiaravalle di Fiastra

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Cistercian Abbey started in 1143 by the monks who arrived in 1142, built with stones of the Roman Urbs Salvia: three-nave church with pointed vaults, brick cloister, capitular hall, refectory and cellars that house the Wine Museum and the Museum of Rural Civilization.

  • Where Abbadia di Fiastra, Tolentino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it

Riserva Naturale Abbadia di Fiastra

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Over 1,800 hectares established in 1984 between Tolentino and Urbisaglia: river valleys, low hills and the "Selva" of more than 100 hectares of oak, ash and maple, with roe deer, owls and peaks.

  • Where Abbadia di Fiastra, Tolentino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it

Castello della Rancia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Just east of the city, born as a Benedictine grange in the 12th century and transformed into a castle in 1357 for the Varano; in May 1815 there was defeated Gioacchino Murat, battle re-evoked every May.

  • Where Rancia, Tolentino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it
