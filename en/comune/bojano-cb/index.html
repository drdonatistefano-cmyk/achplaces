What to see in Bojano

Town of the plain of the springs of the Biferno at the foot of the Matese, home of the mozzarella fiordilatte, with the cathedral of San Bartolomeo from the thirteenth-century portal and, high on a spur at almost 850 meters, the medieval village of Civita with the remains of the castle Pandono.

7 places to visit in Bojano, in the province of Campobasso (Molise). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

The origins date back to the 11th century; it was rebuilt several times after the Middle Ages, often following devastating earthquakes, it preserves the ogival portal and the rose window of the 13th century. Next is the Doge's Palace, transformed in the eighteenth century.

  • Where Bojano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Civita di Bojano, borgo medievale ed ecomuseo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient acropolis on a spur at almost 850 meters, just over 3 km from Bojano on a road in the woods: fortified village almost depopulated that preserves the medieval plant, today open-air ecomuseum with free access, with a view on the plain, on the summits of Matese and on the high valley of Biferno. It hosts medieval reenactments, markets, craft shops and workshops.

  • Where Civita Superiore, Bojano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu

Castello Pandone (Normanno)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Above the village of Civita, the remains of the castle about 120 meters long and 30 meters wide, built in the Lombard or Norman times as a defensive structure and then main residence of the Counts of Molise; collapsed for the earthquake of 1456. Visits with guides in several languages.

  • Where Civita Superiore, Bojano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu

Museo di Palazzo Colagrossi

Museums · City history

It is intended to collect archaeological finds, medieval architectural fragments, fossil collections and period costumes; other medieval fragments are in the gardens behind the Town Hall.

Piana di Bojano e sorgenti del Biferno

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Farmland fed by dozens of sources, including those of Biferno, the longest of the rivers entirely Molise; behind you rise Monte Gallinola (1.923 m) and Monte Miletto (2.050 m), the highest peak of Matese, with the slopes of Campitello Matese reachable from Bojano.

  • Where Bojano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu
