What to see in Canicattini Bagni

Country of the Iblei at 362 meters, crossed by the European Path E1, with a center renovated in the nineteenth century along Via XX Settembre and Art Nouveau facades carved by local stonemasons; in the cave territory, prehistoric necropolis and cave settlements such as Cugno Case Vecchie, with the Jewish hypogeum.

Centro storico liberty e Villa comunale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the second half of the nineteenth century the architecture of the town was renewed along Via XX Settembre; from the early twentieth century to the post-war period facades in Art Nouveau style arose with stone decorations of local stone chisels. Between 1880 and 1925 the Villa municipale, the new cemetery and the sports field were built. From the village the view extends over Mount Etna, the Climate Mountains and the Ionian Sea.

Ipogeo ebraico e insediamento rupestre di Cugno Case Vecchie

History and architecture · Archaeology

Rock site around the country, along the European Path E1. At Case Vecchie there are tombs of the Bronze Age and a medieval rock settlement known for Jewish and early Christian frescoes and symbols; in the Jewish hypogeum the Aditus group in Rupe has identified engravings of menorah, documented with a 3D model.

Cave d'Alfano, Ddieri e Bagni e sorgente del Cardinale

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

Canyon limestones (cave) of the territory: Cava d'Alfano, Cava Ddieri and Cava Bagni; in Alfano, Passo Ladro and Cardinale there are prehistoric necropolis with monumental tombs of the early Bronze Age. The source of the Cardinal feeds the river that supplies the country with drinking water; in 1898 about 200 liters per second were channeled for irrigation and mills, and Greek hydraulic works from the end of the 8th century B.C. carried its waters towards Syracuse.
