What to see in Ripatransone

The "belvedere del Piceno" (Piceno), high on the hill between the valleys of Menocchia and Tesino: walls of more than two kilometers with medieval doors, the narrowest alley in Italy (43 cm), the cathedral of Saints Gregorio and Margherita, the Palazzo del Podestà with the Mercantini Theatre and the civic museums.

Centro storico e vicolo più stretto d'Italia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

A hilly village known as the Piceno viewpoint: from the centre, from via Tarursi, you reach the 43 cm wide alley; along the course the seventeenth-century Palazzo Bruti Liberati and the Casa Bruni, home of Ascanio Condivide, the first biographer of Michelangelo.

  • Where Via Tanursi, Ripatransone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.ripatransone.ap.it

Sources: comune.ripatransone.ap.it · letsmarche.it

Cattedrale dei Santi Gregorio e Margherita

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built between 1597 and 1623 according to design by Giovanni Guerra, with bell tower erected between 1884 and 1902 on a project by Francesco Vespignani and surmounted by a copper Redeemer; organ of Gaetano Callido (1783) and crypt of bishops.

  • Where Ripatransone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.ripatransone.ap.it

Sources: comune.ripatransone.ap.it · letsmarche.it

Museo civico archeologico «Cesare Cellini»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

On the ground floor of the thirteenth-century Palazzo Comunale, born in 1877 from the donation of the Reverend Cesare Cellini: about five thousand finds, 10% exhibited in seven rooms with prehistoric, protohistoric and Roman sections, piceno materials from the necropolis of the Iron Age and high medieval. Whole 2,50 €, reduced 2 €.

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre, Ripatransone
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.ripatransone.ap.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.ripatransone.ap.it

Palazzo Bonomi-Gera, Pinacoteca e Gipsoteca

Museums · Modern art

Palace of the late seventeenth century bought by Uno Gera in 1966, home of the Civic Museum: art gallery, gipsoteca, ethnographic museum, Risorgimento museum and contemporary art gallery. Access with stairway and elevator.

  • Where Corso Vittorio Emanuele 13, Ripatransone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.ripatransone.ap.it

Sources: comune.ripatransone.ap.it · letsmarche.it

Palazzo del Podestà e Teatro Mercantini

History and architecture · Historic theatres

The building dates back to 1304; on the first floor the Teatro Luigi Mercantini, opened in 1824 and completed in 1843 with the Lucia di Lammermoor di Donizetti, closed in 2008 and reopened on 14 April 2012.

  • Where Piazza XX Settembre, Ripatransone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.ripatransone.ap.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · comune.ripatransone.ap.it

Mura, porte e Complesso delle Fonti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Walls built and reinforced between the 12th and the 16th century, with a perimeter of about 2,418 meters and the gates Cuprense, San Domenico, d'Agello and Monte Antico with its tower; the Complex of the Sources (XV-XVI century) has a courtyard that houses an outdoor theatre.

  • Where Porta di Monte Antico, Ripatransone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.ripatransone.ap.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · comune.ripatransone.ap.it

Santuario della Madonna di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry
