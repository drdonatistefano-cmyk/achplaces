What to see in Monastero Bormida

Borgo della Langa Astigiana, birthplace of Augusto Monti, dominated by the castle born as a Benedictine monastery in the 11th century, now town hall and seat of the Gipsoteca Edoardo Rubino; at its feet the medieval bridge with donkey back on the Bormida and, on the hills, the path to the Bricco del Moro.

Castello di Monastero Bormida e Gipsoteca Rubino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Founded in 1050 as a Benedictine monastery by monks of the abbey of Fruttuaria, from the end of the fourteenth century it was fortified by Del Carretto, then six-seventh-century residence of Della Rovere and Polleri of Genoa, which in 1876 gave it to the Municipality, today owner and with the town hall on the noble floor. The noble floor is home to the Gipsoteca "Edoardo Rubino" with the plasters of the Piedmontese sculptor, the attic temporary exhibitions, the mastic the "Torre dei Cavalieri" on weapons and medieval costumes; in the cellars are aged wines of the Langa Astigiana. In 2026 Sunday openings from May to November: guided tours at 15 and 16 (about 1 hour), theatricalized at 11 and 17 (about 1.5 hours); reduced with subscription Musei Piemonte.

  • Where Piazza Castello 1, Monastero Bormida
  • Entry Guided tour €5, reduced €3 (children up to 14 years); theatricalized tour €10, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page castelliaperti.it

Sources: castelliaperti.it · visitlmr.it

Torre campanaria romanica

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

It was built by the Benedictine monks and is separated from the castle, to which it connects only an arch in elevated stone, where the church of the monastery stood.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Monastero Bormida
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page castelliaperti.it

Sources: castelliaperti.it · visitlmr.it

Ponte medievale sul Bormida

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Late medieval ass-back bridge with four stone arches on the Bormida of Millesimo, for centuries the only one that can be passed all year round; the chapel in the center of the main arch was originally a toll guard. In the 1994 flood, he lost parapets and chapels, but the arches resisted.

  • Where Ponte sul Bormida, Monastero Bormida
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page castelliaperti.it

Sources: visitlmr.it · castelliaperti.it

Sentiero del Bricco del Moro

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Linear path of 3.5 km (about 1 hour and 30 one way, +250 m, from 175 to 400 m) on asphalt, dirt road and path, suitable for children from 6 years old but not for strollers; passes through the 17th century church of San Rocco and a panoramic panel, with short detour to the top. Recommended period from spring to autumn.

  • Where Parcheggio di Viale Indipendenza, Monastero Bormida
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page castelliaperti.it

Sources: visitlmr.it · castelliaperti.it
