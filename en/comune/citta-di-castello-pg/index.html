What to see in Città di Castello

Centre of the Upper Tiber Valley marked by the lordship of the Vitelli: the Pinacoteca with Raffaello and Signorelli, the cylindrical bell tower of the Duomo and the two locations of the Burri Collection.

18 places to visit in Città di Castello, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pinacoteca Comunale – Palazzo Vitelli alla Cannoniera

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the palace built between 1521 and 1545, with the graffiti façade of Cristofano Gherardi on a design attributed to Vasari, twenty-one rooms of works from the fourteenth to the nineteenth century: among these the Gonfalone della Santissima Trinità, a youthful work by Raffaello, and the Martyrdom of San Sebastiano by Luca Signorelli.

  • Where Via della Cannoniera, Città di Castello
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · cittadicastelloturismo.it · italia.it

Collezione Burri – Palazzo Albizzini

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

In the Renaissance palace of the second half of the fifteenth century, opened in 1981: about 130 works by Alberto Burri from 1948 to 1989 in twenty halls, from tars and sacks of the fifties to wood, irons, plastics and large cellotex.

  • Where Città di Castello
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cittadicastelloturismo.it · umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Collezione Burri – Ex Seccatoi del Tabacco

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

The former industrial sheds for the drying of tobacco, opened in 1990, welcome the great pictorial cycles and sculptures of Burri of the last decades, set up by the artist himself.

  • Where Città di Castello
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cittadicastelloturismo.it · umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Cattedrale dei Santi Florido e Amanzio

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Dedicated to the patrons, of Renaissance form on Romanesque origins, with the side portal late Dogotic, the coffered ceiling of 1699 and the lower church of the second fifteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Gabriotti, Città di Castello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cittadicastelloturismo.it · italia.it

Campanile Rotondo

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The cylindrical bell tower in Ravenna style, rare in Umbria, rest of the Romanesque-Byzantine cathedral of the 11th century, with the upper part rebuilt between Three and fifteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Gabriotti, Città di Castello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo del Duomo

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Sacred art next to the cathedral: the Treasure of Canoscio of the V-VI century, a Romanesque palace in gold and silver, a fourteenth-century pastoral and paintings of Pinturicchio, Rosso Fiorentino and Giulio Romano.

  • Where Piazza Gabriotti, Città di Castello
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Palazzo Comunale

Torre Civica

History and architecture · Towers · Paid entry · Viewpoint

A prismatic tower of the thirteenth century, known as the Tower of the Bishop, with stone coats of arms and the trace of a fresco by Luca Signorelli in 1474; it climbs up to the top.
