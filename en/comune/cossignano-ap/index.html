What to see in Cossignano

Piceno village from the historical center oval (about 180 for 90 meters) closed by the walls, with the oldest parts of the late thirteenth century: the Civic Museum of sacred art with the altarpiece of Vincenzo Pagani, the Town Hall with the San Giorgio, the Antiquarium \Pansoni Â with the finds of district Fiorano; around calanchi, wines and organic oil.

Centro storico murato

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Oval-shaped center, about 180 by 90 meters, enclosed by the walls, with the oldest parts dating back to the end of the thirteenth century. In April the patron saint of San Giorgio, in July and August the Middle Ages Festival and the fried fish festival all'ascolana.

Old town, monuments and municipal museums only partially usable (Let's Marche)

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta e Museo civico di arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

The church houses the Civic Museum of Sacred Art: 17th century paintings, works by the Crivellesque school and the oil altarpiece on the table . Antonio Abate enthroned, S. Antonio da Padova e S. Job: (2,25 x 1,70 m) by Vincenzo Pagani (1490 circa-1568); a reliquary with remains of many saints and a fragment of cross, probably medieval, considered of the cross of Christ, gift of Niccolò IV.
