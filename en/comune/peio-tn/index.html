What to see in Peio

Valley at the foot of the Ortles-Cevedale, in the Trentino area of the Stelvio National Park: the thermal baths of Peio Fonti, the fauna area of deer, the museum of the Great War of Peio Country with the finds of Punta Linke, the Ecomuseo with Casa Grazioli and the cable car Pejo 3000.

8 places to visit in Peio, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo «Pejo 1914-1918. La guerra sulla porta»

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Inaugurated in 2003, it collects objects, documents and photographs of the First World War on the Ortles-Cevedale front, recovered at altitude or donated by private: weapons, uniforms, a kitchen, a first aid place, a trenches tabernacle with portable altar and the cart of the cable car recovered in Punta Linke; with the Stelvio Park organizes evenings and excursions on the places of war.

  • Where Salita San Rocco 1, Peio Paese
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: trentinograndeguerra.it · visitvaldisole.it · visittrentino.info

Area faunistica di Peio «Casa dei cervi»

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In the Runcal area of the Stelvio National Park, along the road that climbs from Peio Fonti to Peio Paese, you can observe deer and roe deer in their environment; the deer house is a teaching center with panels on the fauna of the park designed for children and young people.

  • Where Via Novalcauda (SP87 tra Peio Fonti e Peio Paese), Peio
  • Entry €1, reduced €0.50
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitvaldisole.it · visittrentino.info

Centro visitatori del Parco Nazionale dello Stelvio

Museums · Themed

The centre of the Trentino area of the Stelvio National Park, which includes the upper Val di Rabbi and part of the Val di Peio: it illustrates the naturalistic aspects of the protected area with educational panels, photographs and information material.

  • Where Via Roma, Peio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visittrentino.info

Terme di Pejo

Local life and traditions · Spas

Baths at 1,390 meters fed by the Pèio Sources, known from the seventeenth century: three springs (Ancient Source, New Source and Alpina Source) used for carbohydrate baths, vascular care and mud; the wellness centre has swimming pool, hydromassage, Turkish bath and massages. Open in summer and winter.

  • Where Via delle Acque Acidule 3, Peio Fonti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitvaldisole.it · visittrentino.info

Ecomuseo della Val di Peio - Casa Grazioli

Museums · Themed

Ancient farmhouse of Strombiano, central building of the Ecomuseo, with intact interiors: the wood-covered stove and the open fireplace kitchens illustrate the evolution of the peasant living; on the whole also the permanent weaving laboratory Rigouts.

  • Where Strombiano, Peio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitvaldisole.it · visittrentino.info

Funivia Pejo 3000

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Opened in 2011, it leads to an altitude of 3,000 meters with views over most of the Eastern Alps; from the top a track of almost 8 km goes down to Peio Fonti. Around the Cevedale (3,764 m) and the Vioz (3,644 m).

  • Where Peio Fonti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitvaldisole.it · visittrentino.info

Laghi del Cevedale e lago di Pian Palù

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The tour of the lakes of Cevedale touches Lake Lungo, Pian Palù delle Marmotte, Covel and Careser to Malga Mare, about 2,000 meters; the lake of Pian Palù and Lake Covel can be reached also with easy paths.

  • Where Val di Peio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · visitvaldisole.it

Punta Linke

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Austro-Hungarian location at 3,632 meters in the Ortles-Cevedale group, among the highest and most logistically important of the front, with a double cableway system towards the valley floor of Pejo and the glacier of the Forni; the ice has preserved the almost intact preparations, which can be visited outside and inside along an equipped path.

Visit in the company of a guide (Trentino Grande Guerra); the page does not include updated opening periods

  • Where Gruppo Ortles-Cevedale (presso il Rifugio Vioz), Peio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: trentinograndeguerra.it · visittrentino.info
