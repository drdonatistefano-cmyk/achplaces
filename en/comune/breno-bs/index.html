What to see in Breno

Historical capital of the Camonica Valley, dominated by the largest castle in the valley; in the village the CaMus with the only camuna gallery, the church of Sant'Antonio with frescoes by Romanino, and in Spinera the Sanctuary of Minerva, sacred place from the VII century BC to the IV AD.

Castello di Breno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The largest fortified complex in the Camonica Valley, which controlled the passage between Lower and Middle Valley: it preserves traces from the medieval fortification to towers-residence and palaces of the XII-XVI centuries, under sieges in the Middle Ages and was abandoned in 1598. Inside the medieval church of San Michele, the oldest monument of Breno, a noble house-tower and the 20-metre tower can be visited; today it houses concerts, re-enactments and archery competitions with wide meadows.

Parco archeologico del Santuario di Minerva

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the Spinera plain, along the Oglio at the foot of a rocky spur full of caves, it was a sacred place from the 7th century BC at the end of the 4th A.D.: large parts of a building of Roman worship remain, built on a prehistoric cult of the waters, with mosaics and frescoed plasters. The statue of Minerva Hygeia, one of the three known copies, is now at the National Archaeological Museum of Cividate Camuno. It can also be reached with the cycle path along the Oglio da Breno and Cividate.

CaMus - Museo Camuno

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated in 1923 thanks to Don Romolo Putelli, it occupies two floors of the fifteenth-century Palazzo Federici (Palazzo della Cultura) with ten rooms and a loggia: it is the only art gallery of Valcamonica, with works by Giacomo Ceruti called the Pytocchetto, Romanino, Callisto Piazza and Franca Ghitti, carvings by the Fantons, coats of arms, furniture and artefacts from Prehistory to Roman times. Open Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday 9-12 and 15-18, Wednesday and Friday 9-12, Sunday 15-18.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Garibaldi 8, Breno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
