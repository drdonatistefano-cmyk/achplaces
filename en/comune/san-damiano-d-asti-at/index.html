What to see in San Damiano d'Asti

The town of Terre Alfieri, between Monferrato and Roero, with a historic centre with a chessboard run from the kilometer of porticoes of Via Maestra and marked by the terracotta bell tower of San Vincenzo; in Palazzo Vagnone is home to the Regional Enoteca delle Colline Alfieri and in the hamlet of San Luigi there is the Big Bench for everyone, accessible giant bench inaugurated in 2022.

Big Bench per tutti

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Inaugurated on July 24, 2022 in the San Luigi hamlet, it is the second "Big Bench" for everyone after that of Paroldo: a giant bench designed for anyone who can get up there, even in a wheelchair, thanks to a small access ramp, to the openable backrest and to a part of the seat in the plane where he can station the wheelchair.

  • Where Frazione San Luigi, San Damiano d'Asti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.sandamiano.at.it

Sources: comune.sandamiano.at.it · bigbenchcommunityproject.org

Centro storico e via Maestra porticata

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center has a chessboard system closed by the bastions (Palestro, Montebello, Magenta, Solferino) and is crossed by Via Maestra, with about a kilometer of arcades, eighteenth-century houses such as Casa Castelli and Casa Daneo, the Palazzo dei Conti Czecho of Vaglierano and the liberty Casa Garola. In Piazza Libertà, the Palazzo Vagnone, in Umbertino style and connected to the Town Hall by a gallery, houses the Regional Enoteca delle Colline Alfieri; the Town Hall, historic residence of the Carlevaris, preserves in the atrium the funeral stele of Daniele Scarampi of 1445. Along Via Maestra also the church of Addolorata, known for its terracotta architecture.

  • Where Via Maestra e piazza Libertà, San Damiano d'Asti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.sandamiano.at.it

Sources: visitlmr.it · comune.sandamiano.at.it

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Cosma e Damiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises where the underneath door was and its bell tower is one of the two towers of the gate. The original parts date back to the fifteenth century; inside the circular choir and a wooden statue of the Clement. Next to it is the brotherhood of the Annunziata of the late sixteenth century.

  • Where Piazza SS. Cosma e Damiano, San Damiano d'Asti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.sandamiano.at.it

Sources: visitlmr.it · comune.sandamiano.at.it

Chiesa di San Vincenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Behind the Town Hall, in Piazza Giroldi, the church has a terracotta bell tower considered the symbol of the village; inside a seventeenth-century painting of the battle of Lepanto.

  • Where Piazzetta Giroldi, San Damiano d'Asti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.sandamiano.at.it

Chiesa di San Giuseppe e Torre dell'Orologio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches
