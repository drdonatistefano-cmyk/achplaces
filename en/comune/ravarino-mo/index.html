What to see in Ravarino

Municipality of the Modena plain on the Panaro, with the church of San Giovanni Battista rebuilt in 1862, the courtyard of the Palazzo di Donna Clarina and the villas of the river "Riviera" such as Villa Bonasi Benucci, damaged by the 2012 earthquake.

5 places to visit in Ravarino, in the province of Modena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Ravarino in the guide with map and filters

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt in 1862 on the foundations of a building of 1100, it preserves a balcony with the organ and a baptismal font of the fifteenth century.

  • Where Ravarino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Palazzo di Donna Clarina

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Court closed with the manor building in the center and, on the sides, the buildings that housed stables and servitude houses; on the left side stands an ancient tower (the sixteenth century for VisitModena, fifteenth century for Emilia-Romagna Tourism).

  • Where Ravarino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Villa Bonasi Benucci e le ville della riviera del Panaro

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

The first half of the nineteenth century complex on the right bank of the Panaro, in the area called the "Riviera" where stands a series of ancient villas once accessible by river.

Lesionata dal seismo del maggio 2012; for Emilia-Romagna Tourism several damaged historical buildings are still closed

  • Where Riviera del Panaro, Ravarino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Villa Castelcrescente

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

In the countryside between Stuffione and Rami di Ravarino, built between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries by Marquis Aldobrandino Rangoni; today it is a private property used as a venue for events.

  • Where Tra Stuffione e Rami, Ravarino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Chiesa della Beata Vergine delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish Church that houses ancient paintings (of the seventeenth century for VisitModena, of the eighteenth for Emilia-Romagna Tourism) and an organ of Agostino Traeri.

In restoration according to both sources

  • Where Ravarino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
