What to see in San Nicandro Garganico

The town of the Gargano National Park between the lakes of Lesina and Varano, with the historic center of Terravecchia gathered around the castle and the historical ethnographic museum in Palazzo Fioritto; in the territory the Dolina Pozzatina, the largest karst phenomenon of Puglia, the Grotto dell'Angelo on Mount of Helium and the sixteenth-century Torre Mileto on the coast.

Museo storico archeologico etnografico e della Civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Born in 1997 as a private initiative and then became public, it is in the Palazzo Fioritto and preserves about 3,000 finds, largely donated by the inhabitants, in a path from the Paleolithic to recent traditions: Roman finds, fossils and prehistoric tools, the 19th century stable-cellar, plows from the 17th to the early 20th century, the carpenter's shops, roaster, shoemaker, blacksmith and tinker, the peasant's house and the traditional Sannicandrese costume; with photo library of about 3,000 images and recordings of songs and oral tradition. Open in July, August and September from 20 to 23; in winter only by reservation (tel. 0882 472369).

  • Entry Free Offer

Centro storico Terravecchia e Castello

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The original nucleus was a farmhouse on a hill surrounded by turrite walls, developed after the thousand around a watchtower and defended by a castle with gate and drawbridge; the castle, of Swabian era, passed in 1464 to Della Marra and is now privately owned. In the Terravecchia palazzi gentilizi alternate with ancient houses whitewashed with lime, between narrow alleys, arches of buttresses and external stairs.

  • Where Terravecchia, San Nicandro Garganico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcogargano.it · italia.it

Torre Mileto

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

On a small rock spur between the lagoons of Lesina and Varano, it is already mentioned in acts of Charles II of Anjou; the current structure, with a quadrangular base with a crown of five caditoias, dates back to the middle of the sixteenth century by mandate of the Viceroy Don Pedro of Toledo. It was garrison, telegraphic base for the Tremiti and barracks of the Guardia di Finanza; owned by the municipality, it was restored and opened in 2005 and houses a visitor center of the Park. From the terrace you can see the other coastal towers up to Molise.

Dolina Pozzatina

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest karst phenomenon of Gargano and Puglia, south of the municipal territory: about 600 meters of maximum diameter, more than 1,800 perimeter and more than 100 meters of depth, born from the collapse of underground cavities and surface corrosion; the red ground floor is cultivated with cereals. Free fruition.

  • Where A sud dell'abitato, San Nicandro Garganico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcogargano.it · italia.it

Grotta dell'Angelo

History and architecture · Archaeology

Karst cavities on the western side of Mount of Helium, about 150 meters above sea level: a large semi-elliptic entrance is divided into corridors, with linear graffiti on the right wall. The excavations of the University of Florence have found silci of the Paleolithic and high-medieval tombs; tradition wants it one of the three places of Garganic worship of the Archangel Michele. You can reach it from the road of the village of Torre Mileto and a short path; for the visit you need lamps.

  • Where Monte d'Elio, presso Torre Mileto, San Nicandro Garganico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcogargano.it · italia.it
