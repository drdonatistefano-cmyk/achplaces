What to see in Bracciano

A small town on the western shore of Lake Bracciano, dominated by the Castle Orsini-Odescalchi of the fifteenth century; in the village the Civic Museum in the former convent of Santa Maria Novella, on the lake in Vigna di Valle the Historical Museum of the Air Force and in the countryside the church of San Liberato with the garden of Russell Page.

Castello Orsini-Odescalchi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built by the Orsini between 1470 and 1490 on a nucleus with a tower of the 12th century of the Prefects of Vico, it passed to the Odescalchi in 1696 and is open to the public since 1952 by the will of Prince Livio IV Odescalchi. The rooms conserve fifteenth-century frescoes from the workshop of Antoniazzo Romano, the Papal Chamber with frescoes attributed to the Zuccari brothers, antique weapons, furnishings and a collection of Italian majolica especially of the sixteenth century, with the busts of the twelve Caesars and a rare hourglass of the sixteenth century; the walk along the cylindrical towers looks on the lake. Reduced €6 (6-12 years, disabled, law enforcement; over 65 only on Tuesdays); visits not bookable, ticket office closed one hour before.

Museo civico e complesso di Santa Maria Novella

Museums · City history

In the former Augustinian convent of Santa Maria Novella (founded in 1438, restored between 1998 and 2003) tells the story of Bracciano from the Etruscan settlements to the nineteenth century in five rooms: the Community, the Etruscan age, the Roman and early Christian age with part of the Panunzi collection, clients and artists with a work of Christopher States, sacred art with the ...Cristo Salvator Mundi attributed to the circle of Andrea Bregno. Next, the church rebuilt from 1765 on a project by Nicola Faggioli with the Assumption of Taddeo Kuntze in the apse.

  • Where Via Umberto I 5, Bracciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · comune.bracciano.rm.it · visitlazio.com

Museo storico dell'Aeronautica Militare

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In Vigna di Valle, on the lake between Bracciano and Anguillara, in the buildings that until 1945 housed the Experimental Centre for Flyers: aircraft and engines that tell Italian aviation in four pavilions, from pioneers and dirigibles (Troster) to the polar flights of Nobile and the Schneider Cup (Velo), to the Second World War (Badoni) to the contemporary jets (Skema). Recommended visit over three hours.

Borgo storico e Belvedere della Sentinella

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village was born as Castrum Brachiani, quoted from 1234, with shell shape; the Orsini closed it with a new belt between 1470 and 1490, of which remains the bastion of the Belvedere della Sentinella overlooking the lake and countryside. Outside it brings the Palazzo della Comunità of 1619 to Piazza IV Novembre, still seat of the Municipality, and on Via Principe di Napoli the aqueduct wanted by Livio I Odescalchi.

  • Where Piazza IV Novembre, Bracciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: comune.bracciano.rm.it · visitlazio.com

Chiesa di San Liberato e giardino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

At the ancient Forum Clodii on the Via Clodia, mentioned already under Leo IV (847-855) as ecclesia Sancti Marciani: a 9th century nave, frescoed apse of the 11th-XII century with the Saints Marco and Marciano on the sides of the Virgin, bell tower of the 12th-XIII century and chapel of San Liberato added by the Augustinians; in 1965 landscaper Russell Page created a botanical garden with native plants and precious species. Today owned by Sanminiatelli.

  • Where Via Settevene-Palo I tronco 33 (km 20), Bracciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: comune.bracciano.rm.it · parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com

Lago di Bracciano e Parco di Bracciano-Martignano

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lakes of Bracciano and Martignano occupy the basin of the Sabatino volcano extinguished and form a regional natural park out of ten municipalities; in the waters of pikes, tenches, koregoni, perch and eels, among the birds folague, ducks, flares and raptors as brown and buzzard nibble. You can practice sailing, canoeing, hiking, horse riding and sport fishing, with beaches and sports clubs on the shores.

  • Where Lungolago di Bracciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it · parchilazio.it
