What to see in Ceriale

The seaside resort of the Riviera delle Palme, an ancient village of farmers and fishermen of Roman origin: on the seafront the bastion of 1564 against pirates, inland the village Peagna with the paleontological Museum "Silvio Lai" and the small Regional Reserve of the Rio Torsero, famous for the fossils of Pliocene.

Museo paleontologico «Silvio Lai»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Founded in 1993 and re-established in 2008 in modern and colorful environments designed especially for children, it is the visitor center of the Rio Torsero Reserve and exhibits fossils of the lower Pliocene collected in the reserve. The path starts from a self-guided paleontological wardrobe on fossilization and local geology, continues with reconstructions of the Pliocene seabed and fossiliferous blocks and ends in a shell-shaped room on geological time; an optical microscope shows rocks and thin sections. Outside a botanical garden on the evolution of flora; under 6 free, over 65 free at the museum.

  • Where Via Nuova di Peagna 209, fraz. Peagna, Ceriale
  • Entry €2, combined museum + guided tour of the reserve €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lamialiguria.it

Riserva Naturale Regionale del Rio Torsero

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry

Established in 1985 to protect the fossiliferous deposit, it extends for 4 hectares in the immediate hinterland of Ceriale, about one kilometer from the beaches. The torrent has dug the rocks of the formation of Ortovero revealing layers of the Pliocene in exceptional state of conservation: the shells of molluscs predominate, with over two hundred species, but there are also corals, briozoa, otolites of fish, echinoderms and crustaceans. The site has been studied since the nineteenth century; the museum . Silvio Lai is a few hundred meters away.

  • Where Fraz. Peagna, Ceriale
  • Entry €4 guided tour (combined with the museum €5)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lamialiguria.it

Sources: parks.it · lamialiguria.it

Centro storico, Bastione e parrocchiale dei Santi Giovanni Battista ed Eugenio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the center there are still sections of the walls and a medieval tower; on the main square, along the promenade, the bastion (torrion) was erected in 1564 against the pirate raids, which did not prevent a barbaric raid. The parish church of Saints John the Baptist and Eugenio, rebuilt in 1637 after the plundering of the Algerian pirates, preserves paintings of the Genoese school (Orazio De Ferrari, Piola, Carlone), modern frescoes of Bonichi in the vault and a wooden crucifix with the hole of a cannon ball; next, the Oratory of Saint Catherine with a neoclassical façade.

  • Where Piazza principale sul lungomare, Ceriale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lamialiguria.it

Sources: parks.it · provincia.savona.it · lamialiguria.it

Borgo di Peagna e Chiesa vecchia di San Giovanni Battista

History and architecture · Villages

Medieval inland fraction, historically inhabited by noble families such as Girardenghi, Genta and Arduini. The old church of San Giovanni Battista, of fourteenth-century origin, contains frescoes from the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries and a Baroque bell tower; the parish church is eighteenth-century, in Baroque style. At the edge of the stream the source Anthia, which according to the legend of Anthia and Abrocome gives the name to the hamlet; at the end of August the library of Ligurian books hosts the exhibition "Books of Liguria" .

Area protetta Poggio Grande, Monte Acuto, Valle Rio Ibà

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The provincial protected area of about 2,417 hectares shared by Ceriale with seven other municipalities, around the valleys Ibà and Auzza and the peaks of Monte Acuto (748 m), Poggio Grande (812 m) and Poggio Ceresa (710 m). On a calcareous substratum you can see rocky ramparts, pinnacles and the "marmittes" of the giants of the river Iba; living together alpine and Mediterranean plants, including 34 species of protected orchids and the Campanula of Savona endemica, with raptors and a population of deers.
