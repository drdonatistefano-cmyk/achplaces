What to see in Viterbo

The City of the Popes: the Papal Palace with the Sala del Conclave, the medieval district of San Pellegrino, the monumental fountains, the Etruscan memories of Castel d'Asso and Ferento, the thermal springs and the garden of Villa Lante in Bagnaia. Every September 3 the Santa Rosa Machine, a UNESCO intangible heritage.

34 places to visit in Viterbo, in the province of Viterbo (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo dei Papi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Started in 1257 as the safest papal seat in Rome, with the acute arched loggia; it houses the Hall of the Conclave, where the cardinals were locked up until they elected the pope.

  • Where Piazza San Lorenzo, Viterbo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
