What to see in Lu e Cuccaro Monferrato

Born in 2019 from the fusion of Lu, a panoramic village of Monferrato rich in religious buildings, and of Cuccaro, linked to Christopher Columbus: to see the parish church of Mediliano, the collegiate church of Santa Maria Nuova, the sacred art museum of San Giacomo, the Colombian Study Centre Museum and the two Big Bench hills.

5 places to visit in Lu e Cuccaro Monferrato, in the province of Alessandria (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pieve di San Giovanni di Mediliano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Pieve del VII secolo nel cuore storico originale di Lu: the first inhabited nucleus was in Mediliano, until in the tenth century the inhabitants moved on a more defensible height, probably for the Saracen incursions.

  • Where Località Mediliano, Lu e Cuccaro Monferrato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Collegiata di Santa Maria Nuova

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1479, it preserves the canvas "The Five Virgins" of Moncalvo.

  • Where Lu, Lu e Cuccaro Monferrato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: alexala.it

Chiesa e museo d'arte sacra di San Giacomo

Museums · Old masters

The church of San Giacomo, rebuilt in the sixteenth century, houses with the sacristy and the House of the Regency a small museum of sacred art: paintings of Moncalvo and his daughter Orsola Caccia, goldsmiths and antique fabrics and the work "I Canonici di Lu" by Pietro Francesco Guala.

  • Where Lu, Lu e Cuccaro Monferrato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: alexala.it

Museo Cristoforo Colombo (Centro Studi Colombiani)

Museums · Themed

In the center of Cuccaro, the museum of the Colombian Study Centre is dedicated to Christopher Columbus and to the kinship, supported by studies and documents, between the Colombo family of Cuccaro and the navigator. Visit by reservation by phone to the Municipality (0131 771928, 9-13 hours, Saturday included).

  • Where Centro di Cuccaro, Lu e Cuccaro Monferrato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: alexala.it

Big Bench di Lu e di Cuccaro

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Two Big Bench Community Project Benches by Chris Bangle (b. 98 and 98. 99), joined by a panoramic walk. The one of Lu, yellow and blue like the balls of the ASD Beach promoter, stands on the San Sebastiano hill at the exit towards Mirabello, about 10 minutes walk from the square, with 360° views from the hills to the plain up to the mountains; between May and June lavender fields flourish.

  • Where Strada Provinciale 70, colle San Sebastiano, Lu e Cuccaro Monferrato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: alexala.it
