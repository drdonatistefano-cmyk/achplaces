What to see in Cropani

Borgo between the Ionian Sea and the slopes of Sila Piccola, once fortified against the Saracens: the Collegiate Church of Santa Maria Assunta of the thirteenth century with the relic of San Marco, the Old Porta, many churches, and at the bottom the Blue Flag beach of Cropani Marina and the mouth of Crocchio.

Collegiata di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the middle of the 13th century, with façade in Romanesque-Gothic tuff (gate and rosette with twelve columns), side sixteenth century with stone portal and dome with steps of Byzantine model; bell tower and interior were rebuilt in the eighteenth century. In a chapel the right kneecap of St Mark, donated by Venetian refugees escaped a storm; in the wooden ceiling the Assumption of Christphorus Santanna, who also painted in 1761 Jesus casts the merchants out of the temple in counterfaced, and in the apse a Dormitius Virginis of the fifteenth-century Venetian-Byzantine.

  • Where Via Messina, Cropani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Piccolo museo d'arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

Centro storico, Porta Vecchia e chiese

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village preserves the Old Gate of the 13th century and a watchtower of the time when it was fortified against the Saracen threat from the sea; among the churches Santa Lucia, Santa Caterina with the Oratory of Sant'Anna, San Giovanni from the high bell tower and Sant'Antonio with the convent of the Capuchins.

  • Where Cropani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
