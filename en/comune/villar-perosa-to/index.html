What to see in Villar Perosa

Country of Val Chisone linked to the RIV, the company of ball bearings founded in 1906, and to the Agnelli family: you can visit the Museum of mechanics and bearing, the working villages wanted by Giovanni Agnelli, the anti-aircraft shelters of 1943 and the Baroque parish of San Pietro in Vincoli.

5 places to visit in Villar Perosa, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Meccanica e del Cuscinetto

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The museum exhibits a wide collection of mechanical equipment and documents that evoke the years of the foundation of RIV and FIAT; on the ground floor is rebuilt an ancient workshop.

  • Where Via Nazionale 26, 10069 Villar Perosa
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villarperosa.to.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.villarperosa.to.it

Rifugi antiaerei

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Paid entry

Refuges started in 1943 on the hill above the working-class village, able to accommodate 2500 people, built to protect workers from bombing; today they are visitable. Fixed openings for individuals and small groups from April to September; schools and groups over 3 people book at least 7 days before.

  • Where Via della Braida 63, 10063 Villar Perosa
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villarperosa.to.it

Sources: comune.villarperosa.to.it · turismotorino.org

Villaggi operai Agnelli

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the Borgo dei Nobili there are the Villaggio Giovanni Agnelli, called Ruata del Pozzo, built in 1915-1916 with fan-shaped structure and completed in 1931 by the Rotonda, the houses of the managers of Viale Agnelli (1935-1936) and the Villaggio Edoardo Agnelli of the early 1940s, with eleven small villas around the octagonal square. Around the building remains the social buildings desired by the factory: boarding house (1909), refectory (1910), tram station (1918-1919) and the church of Sant'Aniceto, erected in 1928 by Giovanni Agnelli in memory of his mother.

  • Where Viale Agnelli, Villar Perosa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villarperosa.to.it

Sources: comune.villarperosa.to.it

Parrocchiale di San Pietro in Vincoli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish was built from 1711 at the expense of Savoy and Count Piccone and inaugurated on 26 September 1716: it is an example of Piedmontese baroque. In 1912 Giovanni Agnelli had copper dome and bell towers covered; in 1929 clock and music box were installed and in 1934 the organ from 937 reeds. It leads to the tree-lined avenue of Leja; from the side of Villa Agnelli, 185 steps up to the churchyard.

  • Where Villar Perosa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.villarperosa.to.it

Sources: comune.villarperosa.to.it
