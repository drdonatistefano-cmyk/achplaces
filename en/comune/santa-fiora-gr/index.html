What to see in Santa Fiora

Borgo dell'Amiata on a cliff of trachyte above the fountains of the Fiora, divided into the terzieri of Castello, Borgo and Montecatino; feud Aldobrandeschi and then Sforza.

3 places to visit in Santa Fiora, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Peschiera e Madonna della Neve

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

The fish pond that collects the waters of the Fiora and the small church of Pescina with frescoes by Francesco Nasini and the glass floor under which flows the spring; near the gallery of the springs.

  • Where Montecatino, Santa Fiora
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Pieve delle Sante Flora e Lucilla

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church before the Thousand, rebuilt in the thirteenth century and enlarged in 1792, with an extraordinary collection of terracotta attributed to Andrea della Robbia.

  • Where Santa Fiora
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Palazzo Sforza Cesarini e Museo delle Miniere di Mercurio

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The Count's palace, now the town hall, with the museum dedicated to the extraction of mercury on the Amiata between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries.

  • Where Castello, Santa Fiora
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
