What to see in Massa Fermana

Borgo del Fermano mentioned since 1050, linked in the thirteenth century to Brunforte: in the Pinacoteca del Palazzo Comunale is now guarded the Polittico di Carlo Crivelli of 1468, his first work known in the Marches; remains of the castle with Porta Sant'Antonio and the Museum of the ancient crafts. Historical center, monuments and museums are partially usable.

Pinacoteca comunale e Polittico di Carlo Crivelli

Museums · Old masters

In a small room of the nineteenth-century Palazzo Comunale, paintings by Vincenzo Pagani, Durante Nobili and Giovanni Andrea De Magistris, two frescoes, a bas-relief made of polychrome paper and sacred furnishings by the former convent of San Francesco; after the closing of the parish, there are the Polyptych by Carlo Crivelli, signed and dated 1468 (Madonna with Child among the Saints Giovanni Battista, Lorenzo, Silvestro and Francesco), and the Madonna with Child and angels by Vittore Crivelli. In the Council room a wooden choir of the fifteenth century.

According to the Region Marche historical center, monuments and museums are partially usable; contact the Municipality (0734 760127)

Museo degli antichi mestieri

Museums · Themed

It documents the crafts of the past, in particular those of the slippery and barber; the section of the ancient bicycle trades, with a private collection of vintage bicycles equipped for the craftsmanship, also makes known the arts of the masquerade and the watchmaker.

Open by reservation according to the Marche Region

  • Where Massa Fermana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Porta Sant'Antonio e resti del castello dei Brunforte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

It leads with a Ghibelline tower and loggia, next to the remains of the ancient castle of Brunforte, lords of the village in the 13th century; from the outside you can see also the former Franciscan monastery on Mount Stalio.

  • Where Massa Fermana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Chiesa dei Santi Lorenzo, Silvestro e Ruffino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt at the end of the nineteenth century, it was the headquarters of the Polyptych by Carlo Crivelli; in the sacristy a seventeenth-century canvas by Sebastiano Grezzi di Comunanza.

Closed after the earthquake of 2016 according to the Marche Region

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
