What to see in San Giovanni Bianco

The town of Val Brembana is home to the Baroque painter Carlo Ceresa, whose birthplace is now a museum; on the Via Mercatorum the ancient village of Oneta houses the House of Arlecchini dei Grataroli, traditionally linked to the birth of the famous mask.

3 places to visit in San Giovanni Bianco, in the province of Bergamo (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo di Oneta

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient settlement on the Via Mercatorum, connected on foot with the nearby Cornello: medieval houses restored according to the original architecture, arcade alleys, stone portals and wooden balconies. In the church of Carmine there are works by Carlo Ceresa and under the porch a great painted Saint Christopher to protect the travelers of the ancient road. Visitable all year round; there pass also the Via Mercatorum and the Ciclovia of the Val Brembana.

  • Where Oneta, San Giovanni Bianco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrembo.it

Sources: visitbrembo.it · visitbergamo.net

Museo Casa di Arlecchino

Museums · House museums

The 15th-century Palace of the Grataroli family, enriched in Venice: tradition has it that their Venetian contacts have given rise to Zanni, a rustic character from Bergamo who emigrated to the lagoon, who became the Harlequin of the Commedia dell'Arte in the 16th century. Above the entrance staircase a fresco depicts Homo Selvadego, a hairy man dressed in skins and armed with a stick, with a paper in dialect: "Chi non' de chortesia, non intragi in chasa mia" (who is not de chortesia) Visit by reservation.

  • Where Via Oneta, San Giovanni Bianco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrembo.it

Sources: visitbergamo.net · visitbrembo.it

Museo Casa Ceresa

Museums · House museums

In the birthplace of Carlo Ceresa, Baroque painter among the most important figures of the painting of Bergamo in the seventeenth century, with over 300 works attributed in Bergamasco. Upstairs, the permanent exhibition of his works, sometimes with temporary exhibitions; from the museum starts the "Percorso ceresiano" (Cesasian route), which connects sixteen paintings of the artist in eight churches of the municipality. Visitable all year round.

  • Where Via G. Milesi 1, San Giovanni Bianco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrembo.it

Sources: visitbrembo.it
