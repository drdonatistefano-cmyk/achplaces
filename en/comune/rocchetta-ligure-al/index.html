What to see in Rocchetta Ligure

The center of the Val Borbera is located around the seventeenth-century Palazzo Spinola, which houses the Museum of Resistance and Social Life and the Museum of Sacred Art, opposite the parish church of Sant'Antonio Abate and the seventeenth-century fountains.

Museo della Resistenza e della vita sociale in Val Borbera

Museums · City history

It occupies some rooms of the lower mezzanine of the seventeenth-century Palazzo Spinola, once used for defensive purposes and as accommodation of the servitude. Since 2003 it has been named after the Partisan commander Gian Battista Lazagna . Carlo , creator of the first Museo della Vallata e dei Partisani (1990). Connected to the Documentation Centre, with a library on the Resistance and interviews, photographs and original documents of the Borbera and Curone valleys. Info: Common, tel. +39 0143 90004.

  • Where Via Umberto I 26, Rocchetta Ligure
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Museo d'arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

Detachable section of the diocesan Museum of Tortona inaugurated in 2009, occupies two rooms on the noble floor of Palazzo Spinola overlooking the central hall. It exhibits paintings and sculptures, often unpublished, from the parishes of Val Borbera, many without a resident parish priest, with panels that connect each work to the church of origin.

Parrocchiale di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

17th century church in the center of the village, on a large churchyard on top of a small hill in front of the masse of Palazzo Spinola; you can climb there with a double ramp from the piazzale del palazzo.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa 3, Rocchetta Ligure
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rocchettaligure.al.it

Sources: comune.rocchettaligure.al.it

Fontane seicentesche e Palazzo Spinola

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In front of the seventeenth-century Palazzo Spinola, a nymph with niche, semi-dome and white marble mask pours water into a large stone basin; on the same square a covered washbasin and, along the main street, the fountain for the mules with copper drips, in front of the former palazzo pratorio. The fountains are fed by an ancient water system that carried the waters of Borbera through a roar, still largely in use.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa, Rocchetta Ligure
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
