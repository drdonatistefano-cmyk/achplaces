What to see in Marsala

The ancient Lilibeo, the city of wine and the landing of the Thousands: the Baroque center from Porta Nuova to the Duomo, the Baglio Anselmi museum, the historical cellars and the lagoon of the Stagnone with the salt pans and the Phoenician island of Mozia.

12 places to visit in Marsala, in the province of Trapani (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Marsala in the guide with map and filters

Isola di Mozia e Museo Whitaker

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the island of San Pantaleo in the Stagnone, a Phoenician-Punic colony of the eighth century BC destroyed by Dionysius I of Syracuse; rediscovered by Joseph Whitaker, it houses the museum with the statue of the Giovinetto of Mozia found in the late seventies.

  • Where Isola di Mozia, Marsala
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo Archeologico Baglio Anselmi

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In an ancient beam on the seafront, he tells of the foundation of Lilibeo with a rich collection of amphorae and the finds of the Punic ship found off Punta Scario.

  • Where Lungomare, Marsala

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
