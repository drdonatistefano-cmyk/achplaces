What to see in Casalbeltrame

Village of the Risaie Novaresi with the medieval elliptical ring, the ethnographic museum of the agricultural tool "L'Civel" in the ancient Cascinale dei Nobili, the parish church of Santa Maria Assunta, the temple church of Sant'Apollinare and the natural reserve of the Swamp.

Museo etnografico dell'attrezzo agricolo «'L Çivel»

Museums · Themed

Housed in the ancient Cascinale dei Nobili, a large courtyard with rural houses that opens under an arched porch and is bordered by five churches of decreasing size, called churches "matrioska" , some used as exhibition halls. A multimedia path (consultable with tablets or smartphones) tells about life in rice fields, the work of peasants and worldly people, customs and songs; the second section is a gallery of wagons, tools and machines for processing rice. Open only by reservation; accessible to disabled people.

Ricetto e centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The medieval shelter is still readable as an elliptical ring of buildings; in the center rise the neoclassical Town Hall and, in Piazza IV Novembre, the castle indicated by the Municipality as of the tenth century. On the parish churchyard there is the Monument to the Fallen.

Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

18th century parish church with a single nave with three chapels per side and vaulted barrel; the square bell tower has terracotta decorations. It preserves a group of sixteenth-century statues of the Deposition, the relics of San Novello martyr donated by a Milanese family in the sixteenth century, a fresco of the Madonna with Child, a wooden crucifix of the fifteenth century and two seventeenth-century paintings of Carlo Cane.

Complesso di Sant'Apollinare

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of medieval origins, documented in 1174 when Count Guido di Biandrate donated it to the Knights Templar; the present building is eighteenth-century, with a single nave. It is reached with a detour of just over a kilometer from the crossroads of Stroppera, along the route Vie Verdi del Riso.

Villa Bracorens Savoiroux

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palazzo dei Conti Savoiroux along the main road: a single body on three floors, of severe and massive style, without balconies or loggias, with a shady park dominated by a century-old ginkgo biloba. It hosts the two-year Casalbeltrame Award "The Golden Frog" and the Materima exhibition spaces, open on Saturday from 10 am to 6 pm.

Riserva naturale della Palude di Casalbeltrame

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Protected area of 640 hectares among the rice fields, with reservoirs always flooded surrounded by reeds and humid woods of willows, white poplars, black alders and farnies. It is above all a place for birdlife: royal germans, ladles, blackberries, garzettes, herons and major whites, nitticolar and a large colony of sacred ibis. A two-storey observatory allows you to observe from above and below the water level.
