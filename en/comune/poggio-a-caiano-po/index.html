What to see in Poggio a Caiano

The village of the "queen of Medici villas," Villa Ambra, UNESCO heritage since 2014.

3 places to visit in Poggio a Caiano, in the province of Prato (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa Medicea di Poggio a Caiano

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Wanted by Lorenzo il Magnifico and built by Giuliano da Sangallo between 1484 and 1520, prototype of the Renaissance villa: frescoes of Pontormo (Vertumno and Pomona), Andrea del Sarto, Filippino Lippi and Allori, the garden and the lemon house.

  • Where Poggio a Caiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo della Natura Morta

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

In the villa, the first Italian museum dedicated to the still life, with more than 180 paintings from the Five to the eighteenth century.

  • Where Villa Medicea, Poggio a Caiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Scuderie Medicee e Museo Ardengo Soffici

Museums · Modern art

The stables built for Cosimo I around 1548 by the Tribolo, today exhibition center with library and the Ardengo Soffici Museum and of the Italian twentieth century.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
