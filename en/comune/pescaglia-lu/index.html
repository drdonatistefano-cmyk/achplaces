What to see in Pescaglia

Municipality of the Media Valle del Serchio among the chestnut groves: in Celle dei Puccini the family home of the composer, in Vetriano the smallest historical public theatre in the world (well FAI), in Cologna the Castagno Museum and the seventeenth-century Molino di Menicone.

6 places to visit in Pescaglia, in the province of Lucca (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Pescaglia in the guide with map and filters

Museo di Celle dei Puccini

Museums · House museums

In the sixteenth century house that hosted five generations of musicians, where Giacomo Puccini spent happy years of childhood: seven rooms on two floors with photographs, letters, autograph musical manuscripts, the baptismal robe, the bed in which he was born in Lucca and the vertical piano used for many compositions. The route also shows a country house with antique furniture and tools and a kitchen remained unchanged for about two centuries; the last refurbishing is in 2008.

  • Where Via Meletori 27, Celle dei Puccini, Pescaglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodicelledeipuccini.it

Sources: museodicelledeipuccini.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it · visittuscany.com

Teatrino di Vetriano

History and architecture · Historic theatres · Paid entry

Well of the FAI, it is indicated as the smallest historical public theatre in the world, with about fifty square meters of audience; the guided tour includes the stage and the dressing rooms on the lower floor. Open to the public on Saturday and Sunday.

  • Where Località Carraia, Vetriano, Pescaglia
  • Entry €8, reduced €4 (6-18 years and students up to 25), free FAI membership
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.visittuscany.com
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: fondoambiente.it · visittuscany.com · ticket.fondoambiente.it

Museo Nazionale del Castagno

Museums · Themed

Museum run by the village committee of Cologna, dedicated to chestnut as raw material and food: it retraces the cycle of chestnut (collection, drying, processing and food uses) and the trades related to the wood. An open-air walk in the village touches a metato, a carbonaia and a straw hut. Open on holidays 15-18, closed in winter.

  • Where Via Alfredo Catalani 3A, Colognora di Pescaglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museodelcastagno.it

Sources: museodelcastagno.it · comune.pescaglia.lu.it · visittuscany.com

Molino di Menicone

Museums · Themed

The only intact water mill in the municipality: the building is from the seventeenth century, it was part of the farm of the Palazzaccio, sixteenth-century villa of the Counts Orsetti, and it worked until the eighties. It preserves the original 19th century machines for grinding wheat, corn and chestnuts; from the eighteenth century the same water operated the mantics of the Galgani ironworks. It can be visited with educational activities.

  • Where Località Menicone 2, Pescaglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Pieve di San Giovanni Battista a Monsagrati

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Pieve attested already in the Lombard age and rebuilt in 1102 on the same hill, with bell tower to the north. Romanesque, Latin cross with three naves and semicircular apse; vaults and windows of the nave are of the seventeenth century, the tabernacle of the fifteenth century.

  • Where Via per Monsagrati Alto, Monsagrati, Pescaglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Celle dei Puccini

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Frazione in a small valley excavated by the stream Pedogna, just over 23 km from Lucca: simple village of stone houses where the name Puccini is documented from the sixteenth century; it was called only Celle until 1971, when the Municipality renamed it in honor of the composer.

  • Where Celle dei Puccini, Pescaglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com
