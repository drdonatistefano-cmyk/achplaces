What to see in Montefiascone

On the highest hill of the Volsini, overlooking Lake Bolsena along Cassia and Francigena: the Rocca dei Papi, the dome of Santa Margherita and the double church of San Flaviano, land of the East! East! East!!!.

5 places to visit in Montefiascone, in the province of Viterbo (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Montefiascone in the guide with map and filters

Rocca dei Papi e giardino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The residence of the popes fleeing the sieges of Rome and the pontifical rectors of Tuscia; the garden, created in 1736 by Cardinal Aldrovandi paving a hill, looks together at the lake, the Tyrrhenian and the Apennines.

  • Where Piazza Urbano V, Montefiascone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com · archeoares.it

Cattedrale di Santa Margherita

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Started in 1483, continued by Sanmicheli and completed after the fire of 1670 with a dome of Carlo Fontana inaugurated in 1674, among the largest in Italy; Renaissance crypt and Robbian terracotta.

  • Where Via Santa Lucia Filippini 2, Montefiascone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Chiesa di San Flaviano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Two churches superimposed and oriented in opposite directions, both frescoed, with sixteenth-century loggia on the façade and the tombstone of Cardinal East East East East.

  • Where Montefiascone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Belvedere di Montefiascone

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the most spectacular panoramic points of Tuscia: the entire lake of Bolsena with the Martana and Bisentina islands and, on clear days, Mount Amiata and Tyrrhenian.

  • Where Via della Rocca 3, Montefiascone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

One of the oldest churches in the village, in the historic center near the original walls.

  • Where Montefiascone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it
