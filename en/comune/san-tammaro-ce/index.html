What to see in San Tammaro

In the countryside of San Tammaro stands the Real Site of Carditello, a Bourbon estate commissioned by Carlo di Borbone since 1744 and transformed by Ferdinand IV into a model farm, with the neoclassical residence of Francesco Collecini, the great royal galloping trough with the temple and the woods of eucalyptuses, holm oaks and cerres.

Real Sito di Carditello (Reggia di Carditello)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

In 1744 Charles of Bourbon expropriated the farm and the land of the princes of Caramanico: over 2,000 hectares of woods and fields partly swampy and covered with cardoons, from which the name. Carlo started there the real industry of buffalo straw and the breeding of the horses of Persano; Ferdinand IV built the neoclassical residence, by Francesco Collecini, co-worker of Vanvitelli, a double-t plant, and made it an experimental farm. Inside the staircase with stuccoes by Carlo Beccalli, the hall of rural painting on drawings by Philipp Hackert, the vault of the hall frescoed by Fedele Fischetti (1791), the room of the Four Seasons by Giuseppe Cammarano and the neoclassical chapel; on the ground floor the royal kitchens, the Sala dei Camini and the church of the Ascension, and in the complex the Bourbon stables and an immersive hall. Since 2016 it is managed by the Fondazione Carditello; the museum area is visited only with guided tours, Saturdays at 15 and 16 and Sunday at 11:30, 12:30, 15 and 16.

The Foundation indicates the closure from 21 December 2026 to 5 January 2027; the same info page also includes different rates (entry €2, visit accompanied €3): check prices at infoline +39 379 2981223

  • Where Via Carditello, SP229, San Tammaro
  • Entry €8, reduced €6 (over 60) or €4 (6-18), groups €7; free under 6 and people with disabilities
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.fondazionecarditello.org

Galoppatoio reale e Tempietto

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Behind the building opens a large elliptical galloping hall, bordered by two fountains with obelisks and with a circular temple in the center from which the sovereigns attended the races, especially of the horses of the Persano breed; inside the temple there was a statue of Diana of the Farnese collection, then destroyed. The Foundation presents him as the largest galloping gallery in the world inserted in the perimeter of a royal residence; along the perimeter remain traces of the ditch carrying water to irrigate the lawn. The Persano breed was born in 1744 at the behest of Carlo di Borbone and Ferdinand IV had built for the horses stables and racetrack; in the historical park you can also see the solar clocks, the ancient walls and the Bourbon moat.

  • Where Via Carditello, San Tammaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.fondazionecarditello.org

Sources: fondazionecarditello.org · cultura.regione.campania.it · cultura.gov.it

Boschi di Carditello e area pic-nic Don Peppe Diana

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Paid entry

The green areas of the estate, reclaimed and arranged, are open to all: the eucalyptus forest, behind the main entrance, has a wellness path; to the east of the estate is the holm oak forest; the oak forest, in front of the entrance, where the Bourbon kings hunted, is today the picnic area dedicated to Don Peppe Diana. The picnic area opens on Sundays from 11 am to 4 pm, with tables on reservation; you can't light fires or barbecues and dogs are allowed on a leash and muzzle.

  • Where Via Carditello, San Tammaro
  • Entry €3; free with full ticket of the museum area; table for groups €20
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.fondazionecarditello.org

Sources: fondazionecarditello.org · cultura.gov.it
