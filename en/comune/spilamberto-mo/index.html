What to see in Spilamberto

New land walled on the Panaro and capital of traditional balsamic vinegar: the Rangoni Rocca with the Court of Taste, the Torrione with the Antiquarium and the secret cell of Messer Filippo, the Balsamic Museum in Villa Fabriani and the feast of the Mast Cot in October.

6 places to visit in Spilamberto, in the province of Modena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca Rangoni

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Brick complex rebuilt in the fifteenth century on the oldest ruins, for over 650 years home of the Marquises Rangoni. The Municipality bought it in 2005 and restored it since 2011; in the Courtyard of Honor is the Court of Taste, a space dedicated to typical products of the territory.

In restoration since 2011: for now only the Courtyard of Honor is used

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Torrione e Antiquarium

Museums · Archaeological

Medieval tower at the entrance of the village, under the fourteenth-century belt. It houses the Antiquarium, in three sections from prehistory to the Middle Ages with instruments, funerary equipment and botanical and animal remains, the headquarters of the Order of the Nocino Modenese and the Cell of Messer Filippo, secret prison found in 1947.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it · italia.it

Museo del Balsamico Tradizionale

Museums · Themed

In Villa Fabriani, decorated with frescoes from the early twentieth century and seat of the Consorteria dell'Aceto Balsamico Tradizionale, tells history and production of traditional balsamic vinegar of Modena. The Touring indicates it as a provisional location pending transfer to the Rock.

  • Where Villa Fabriani, via Roncati, Spilamberto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it · museodelbalsamicotradizionale.org

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish, decorated with stuccoes, preserves a processional cross in bronze of the 11th century, a banner of Adeodato Malatesta and rich vestments.

Centro storico e via Romea

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

New walled land with parallel hosts, Modena outpost on the eastern border; it was born in 1210 as a reference point along the ancient pilgrim's road towards Rome, in front of the Bolognese castle of Piumazzo. At the end of June the San Giovanni fair with the prize of the best family vinegar, the first weekend of October the Mast Cot with the cooking of the must outdoors.

  • Where Spilamberto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
