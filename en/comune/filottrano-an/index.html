What to see in Filottrano

Città della sartoria nell'interra anconetano, with fourteenth-century walls, Piazza Mazzini and the panoramic loggia from the Conero in Loreto, the Bramantesco Town Hall, the Pieve with the tomb of the explorer Giacomo Costantino Beltrami and the museums of the Marche biroccio and the Battle of Filottrano in 1944.

9 places to visit in Filottrano, in the province of Ancona (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza Mazzini e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Heart of the village crossed by the course of the People, opened at the end of the nineteenth century demolishing Porta Romana: from the loggia the panorama goes from Monte Conero to Macerata, Recanati and Loreto. On the square the bronze statue of Ottrano, the Porta Nuova called Porticella with the staircase to the ancient part, and the desecrated Church of the Angels with the signs of the battle grenades of 1944; inside the fourteenth-century walls, Largo Bramante is another viewpoint on hills, Conero and sea.

  • Where Piazza Mazzini, Filottrano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: tdh.comune.filottrano.an.it · italia.it

Palazzo Comunale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

At the highest point of the city, a 16th century building in Bramantesco style with a civic tower and a large internal staircase; the theater that was housed there was demolished in 1934.

  • Where Piazza Cavour, Filottrano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: tdh.comune.filottrano.an.it · italia.it

Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Reachable from Porta Marina, it was rebuilt in 1679 on a Gothic church of the fourteenth century; it houses the tomb of the explorer Giacomo Costantino Beltrami, the fifteenth-century fresco of the Madonna della Colonna and, in the sacristy, a Decollation of St. John the Baptist and a Nativity.

  • Where Porta Marina, Filottrano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: tdh.comune.filottrano.an.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Cristoforo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Among the oldest churches in the city, with origins around the year 1000: a single nave with recently restored fifteenth-century frescoes. Opening by reservation through Pro Loco or Tourist Office.

  • Where Via San Cristoforo 19, Filottrano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: tdh.comune.filottrano.an.it · italia.it

Museo del biroccio marchigiano

Museums · Themed

In the rooms of Palazzo Beltrami once destined for servitude, he exhibits the traditional Marche farm wagons, known for their lively decorations, and tells the story of peasant life. Opening by reservation through Pro Loco or Tourist Office.

  • Where Vicolo Beltrami 2, Filottrano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: tdh.comune.filottrano.an.it · italia.it

Museo Beltrami

Museums · House museums

On the upper floor of Palazzo Beltrami, it preserves relics, documents and works by Giacomo Costantino Beltrami, magistrate and traveler, discoverer of the sources of the Mississippi, including a Sioux medicine drum chosen as a symbol of the Winter Olympics of Calgary in 1988.

Museo Memorial della Battaglia di Filottrano

Museums · City history · Free entry

Permanent exhibition of relics of the Second World War dedicated to the Battle of Filottrano in 1944; open every Saturday from 10 to 12.30, free entrance (curator Giovanni Santarelli, 071 7222576).

  • Entry Free

Museo della macchina da cucire

Museums · Themed

In the historic centre, born from a private collection of machines of different eras, it tells the tailoring tradition of the city, whose first textile activities date back to the 1950s.

Giardini di Villa Centofinestre

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

The eighteenth-century villa was founded as a "Casino di Delizie" by the Marquis Accoretti and transformed in the following century by the Carradori, on the road from San Biagio to Filottrano: Italian gardens, a greenery of symmetrical cypresses, hunting forest with stakeout towers, greenhouses and groves.
