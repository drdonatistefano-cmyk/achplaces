What to see in Ceglie Messapica

Ancient Messapian centre, an important military, political and religious centre between the 4th and 3rd centuries BC, today the city of art and gastronomy is known for its cookie. The ducal castle dominates the medieval old town with the Allegretti palaces (seat of the Pinacoteca Emilio Notte), Epifani and Vitale; around terraces with trulli, mirrors, caves and rock churches.

Castello ducale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Symbol of the city, built at the end of the 14th century on the site of the acropolis of ancient Caelium, with fifteenth-century towers joined by walls; in the 15th century the Sanseverino transformed it into a noble residence with frescoes and coats of arms. From the Norman tower and the mast you can see the low Adriatic; in the atrium the well to which, according to tradition, was drawn in the drought periods; the frescoed rooms of the sixteenth century overlook a park with medieval gardens. Guided visits.

  • Where Via Chiesa 11, Ceglie Messapica
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Pinacoteca comunale Emilio Notte

Museums · Modern art

In the eighteenth century Palazzo Allegretti, near the fourteenth-century Piazza Vecchia, with a façade in gentle stone and two portals surmounted by the coats of arms Allegretti and Cenci. He collects works by contemporary artists and especially by the futurist Emilio Notte (Ceglie 1891 - Naples 1982), including "Piazza Mercatale di Prato" (1916), "Zodiac" (1929) and "Triptych of the Crucifixion" (1972). In the building also the municipal library.

  • Entry To be checked
