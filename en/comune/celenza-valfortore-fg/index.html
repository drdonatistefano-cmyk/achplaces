What to see in Celenza Valfortore

Small village of the Dauni Mountains, near the border with the Molise, which dominates the valley of the Fortore. The historic centre opens with Porta San Nicolò and Porta Nova and preserves the crenellated tower and the fifteenth-century castle, the church of San Nicola and the municipal palace in the former monastery of Girolamini; in the surroundings woods, paths and Lake Occhito.

Centro storico, torre merlata e castello

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The old town is reached by two ancient gates, Porta San Nicolò and Porta Nova; there are the crenellated tower and the Castle or Baronial Palace, built in the fifteenth century, whose remains with the Mother Church tell the history of the village.

  • Where Centro storico, Celenza Valfortore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Chiesa e Monastero di San Nicola

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The church is located on the site of a more ancient, of which remains a walled inscription at the entrance of the convent; the current building bears witness to the passage from the Byzantine to the Norman dominion. Inside stand out the apse, very well preserved, and the work of local chisels, with columns, pilasters and blind arches made from unique blocks.

  • Where Via San Nicola 15, Celenza Valfortore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Palazzo comunale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

In the historic centre, in the ancient seat of a monastery of hermits, a small group of lightning stars built on the ruins of the church of Santo Stefano; here the icon of Santa Maria delle Grazie was found, today in the homonymous sanctuary just outside the village. It houses the Town Hall and the Carabinieri barracks.

  • Where Via Carlo Alberto 2, Celenza Valfortore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Lago di Occhito

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the surroundings of the village, described by the regional portal as the largest artificial invader in Italy, among woods; it is habitat of herons, ducks and wolves.
