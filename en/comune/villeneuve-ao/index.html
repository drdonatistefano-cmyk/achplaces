What to see in Villeneuve

Country on the Dora at the entrance of the valleys of the Great Paradise, surmounted by the ruins of Châtel-Argent Castle where coins were minted.

4 places to visit in Villeneuve, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Châtel-Argent

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The thirteenth century, of great strategic importance, takes its name from the coins coined there; the circular tower, the belt and the ruins of the chapel, 15 minutes walk from the village.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
