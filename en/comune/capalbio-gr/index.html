What to see in Capalbio

The southernmost village of continental Tuscany, closed by walls, a few kilometers from the sea; called "small Athens."

3 places to visit in Capalbio, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Capalbio in the guide with map and filters

Mura e camminamento di ronda

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval walls of the village, to be walked along the ancient walk with a view of the countryside; the Porta Senese preserves the doors of the fifteenth century.

  • Where Capalbio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Rocca Aldobrandesca e Chiesa di San Nicola

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The fortress of the Aldobrandeschi in the heart of the village and the medieval church of San Nicola with medieval and Renaissance frescoes.

  • Where Capalbio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Giardino dei Tarocchi

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The park opened in 1998 from Niki de Saint Phalle to Garavicchio: 22 monumental sculptures 12-15 meters high covered with mirrors, Murano glass and ceramics, with the ticket office of Mario Botta.

  • Where Garavicchio, Pescia Fiorentina, Capalbio
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website ilgiardinodeitarocchi.it

Sources: ilgiardinodeitarocchi.it · visittuscany.com
