Pontificia Fonderia Marinelli e Museo storico della campana «Giovanni Paolo II»

Museums · Themed

The family foundry has been producing bells for about a millennium with traditional methods: craftsmen prepare models, guide molten metal and shape bells, and in 1924 Pius XI granted her the papal coat of arms. The museum, founded in 1999 and named after John Paul II (visiting Agnone in 1995), exhibits sacred bronzes including a bell of the year about 1000, tools, documents and a Dutch edition of 1664 of De tintinnabulis; in the workshop you can see ovens and melting pits and you can watch a bell concert.

  • Where Via Felice d'Onofrio, Agnone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website campanemarinelli.com

Sources: campanemarinelli.com · cultura.gov.it · italia.it · visitmolise.eu

Museo della 'Ndoccia

Museums · Themed

Dedicated to the 'ducks', large torches of fir trunks and broom bundles lit and carried on shoulder in procession along the course on the night of December 24: exhibits several 'droplets, a 4 meters high, reconstructions with costume mannequins, photos of the construction phases and the trophy disputed every year from the five districts. He also recalls the 'Shower taken to St Peter's Square in front of John Paul II in 1996.

  • Where Via Caracciolo, Agnone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Quartiere veneziano e Belvedere Ripa

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the western part of the village, the Venetian imprint is read in the vaults and architraves of the entrances, in the keys of vaults all different and in the removable coats of arms of the noble houses; along Corso Garibaldi a lion of San Marco looks from the first floor of the civic 135. You will meet the goldsmith shop under Palazzo Nuonno, the house of the baglivo and Palazzo Apollonio with its stone lions; at the end of the course the Belvedere Ripa, with a blacksmith shop in front of a Venetian bifora, overlooks the valley of Verrino.

  • Where Corso Garibaldi, Agnone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu

Chiesa e convento di San Francesco

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Former convent with a museum in the press and a large collection of ancient books, considered one of the most important archives in Italy; the church, known for its paintings, has wooden ceilings painted by hand with Baroque religious images.

  • Where Centro storico, Agnone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu

Botteghe del rame e Museo storico del rame Gerbasi

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Metalworking, from gold to wrought iron, was for a long time the economic engine of Agnone: a copper foundry existed already in 1754, moved by the waters of Verrino, the Statutes of 1456 regulated the trade and the Occiary Catasto of 1753 had more than 150 families of now. The Historical Museum of the Gerbasi copper shows the phases of processing and finishing with a cantilever; in the village there are still workshops of copper and wrought iron.

  • Where Agnone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitmolise.eu · italia.it
