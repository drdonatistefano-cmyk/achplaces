What to see in Hône

Country at the entrance of the Champorcher valley, in front of the fort of Bard, to which joins a bridge over the Dora.

3 places to visit in Hône, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Of very ancient origins: the excavations have found walls of at least three previous churches, visible under the transparent floor.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

  • Entry Free

Ponte sulla Dora verso Bard

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint
