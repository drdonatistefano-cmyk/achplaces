What to see in Elini

The smallest village of Ogliastra, about 500 meters above sea level, can be reached by train: the old 19th century granite station, the parish church of San Gavino, the museum house and, at the top, the Carmine Park with the 17th century sanctuary and the ruins of two nuraghi.

Casa museo di Elini

Museums · House museums

Ancient house in the historic center on three floors, restored preserving table floors and ceilings of beams and reeds, with internal courtyard, porch and oven for bread: kitchen with fireplace, rooms with wrought iron beds, cellar with the corner of the bakery and pantry, peasant tools, costumes and embroidery of the local tradition. Guided tours by appointment with the City's cultural office; admission fee; on the day of the visit you can attend the preparation of the culurgiones and taste them.

  • Where Via Lamarmora 33, Elini
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Parco del Carmine

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the upper part of the village, walks between holm oaks and perennial sources with ample panorama; there are the ruins of two nuraghi and the sanctuary of the Madonna del Monte Carmelo. According to the canon Flavio Cocco, the origin of the country would be linked to a Roman military garrison installed right here.

  • Where Parte alta del paese, Elini
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Santuario della Madonna del Monte Carmelo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

A 17th-century shrine inside the Carmine Park; in July the village has its most heartfelt feast, with three days of celebrations and processions.

  • Where Parco del Carmine, Elini
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Gavino martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the historic centre, built in the 20th century on a structure of the sixteenth century; on 18 May the feast of St. Gavino is solemnly celebrated.

  • Where Centro storico, Elini
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Vecchia stazione ferroviaria

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Station built in the nineteenth century with granite blocks, in the center of the village; the train is indicated as the most evocative way to get to Elini, through limestone reliefs covered with vegetation.

  • Where Centro del paese, Elini
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
