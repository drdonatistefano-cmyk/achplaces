What to see in Corteno Golgi

Comune dell'alta Valle Camonica which since 1956 bears the name of Camillo Golgi, the first Italian Nobel laureate for medicine (1906), born here in 1843 and remembered by the Golgi Museum; the territory includes the Riserva delle Valli di Sant'Antonio, with the twin valleys Brandet and Campovecchio, and the ski area Aprica-Corteno.

Museo Camillo Golgi

Museums · Science and university

Born on June 10, 2006 for the centenary of the Nobel in Camillo Golgi, it occupies three floors of the old town hall renovated near the parish and combines materials of the old exhibition and new acquisitions: a rich collection of medical-scientific instruments of epoch, with the reconstruction of a clinic of the late nineteenth century and a histological laboratory. A few meters away is the birthplace with memorial stone; from the museum starts the Camillo Golgi Trail, with 18 biographical panels along the ancient Valeriana street to Aprica.

Riserva naturale Valli di Sant'Antonio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established by the Lombardy Region in 1983 at the request of the Municipality, which manages it, protects 41,68 km2 of the two Brandet and Campovecchio twin valleys, separated by a long dorsal and joined by the village of Sant'Antonio. It rises from willow and white alder woods to the skins with the rare white fir and larch, then rhododendrons and blueberries, lakes, wetlands and peat bogs; there live chamois, marmots, cedar roosters, coturnals and royal eagles. Hiking trails on foot, on horseback or by mountain bike, picnic areas and two refuges: Alpini di Campovecchio (1310 m) and Val Brandet (1360 m).

Borgo di Sant'Antonio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small nucleus between the waters of two streams, at the confluence of the valleys Brandet and Campovecchio: the houses are wooden fir cabins with blockbau joints, which recall the Walser residences, and the village is crossed by wooden bridges.

Chiesetta di San Martino Franco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Among the oldest churches in Valcamonica, dating back to the time of Charlemagne; in the sixteenth century it was used as a lazzaretto. It contains frescoes, including St. Martin on horseback that divides the mantle with the poor.

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta a Pisogneto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church built in 1425 and enlarged in the eighteenth century, visited by Saint Charles Borromeo in 1580 and Francesco Morosini in 1594. It preserves a wooden pulpit of 1746, an organ of 1805 and a Madonna with Child carved by the Del Maino brothers.

Ski area Aprica-Corteno

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

The ski resort shared with Aprica from the village of San Pietro, about 15 km from Edolo, with slopes of different difficulty; the Superpanoramica del Baradello is indicated as the longest illuminated track in Europe. In San Pietro there are also the Baradello Wild Park and, in summer, the Saturday market in Piazza Funivia.
