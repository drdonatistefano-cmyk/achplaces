What to see in Montefalcone Appennino

Borgo on a sandstone cliff about 780 meters between the valleys of Aso and Tenna, overlooking the Sibillini and the Laga Mountains: in the eighteenth century Palazzo Felici the Museum of fossils and minerals, then the ancient castle, medieval churches and, just outside, the monastery of San Giovanni in Selva.

Museo dei fossili e dei minerali (Museo dell'Alamanno)

Museums · Science and university

In the eighteenth century Palazzo Felici: about 500 local fossils of marine fauna of the lower Pliocene (cyrripeds, about sixty species of molluscs, sea urchins, shark teeth, fossil leaves) and finds from the world, including mammoth fangs of the North Sea, an egg of dinosaur from China and an egg of Aepyornis. About 1,200 minerals, including a 26 kg block of stilbite with natrolite, and a fluorescent mineral cave at the Wood lamp; on the first floor about 300 paintings made with ten thousand shells by Elio Ceccotti. In the building also the Environmental Education Center and the Ecomuseo, archive of the popular knowledge of Fermano.

Borgo, antico castello e terrazza sui Sibillini

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village takes its name from the hawks nesting on the top of the Monte del Falcone and stands on a sandstone cliff at about 780 meters; the ancient castle occupies the highest part of the village and from the terrace you can see the Sibillini and the Monti della Laga.

Chiese di San Michele Arcangelo, San Pietro in Penne e Santa Maria in Capite Scalorum

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

St. Michael the Archangel is a Romanesque church with a Greek cross; San Pietro in Penne has farfensi origins; Santa Maria in Capite Scalorum was built around 1300 around a much older aedicule.
