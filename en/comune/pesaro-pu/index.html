What to see in Pesaro

Birthplace of Rossini and UNESCO Creative City for music, between Monte Ardizio and San Bartolo: Piazza del Popolo and Palazzo Ducale, the museums of Palazzo Mosca, the Rocca Costanza and seven kilometers of beach.

25 places to visit in Pesaro, in the province of Pesaro e Urbino (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo Ducale e Piazza del Popolo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Renaissance building built by Alessandro Sforza in the mid-15th century on a Malatestan nucleus and completed by the Dukes of Urbino, with a portico of six arches; today it is the seat of the Prefecture.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Pesaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Musei Civici di Palazzo Mosca

Museums · Old masters

Since 1936 in the residence of the Moscow family: the Pala di Pesaro by Giovanni Bellini, works by Vitale da Bologna, Tintoretto, Guido Reni and Mattia Preti, the Renaissance majolica of the Mazza collection and, in the courtyard, the Medusa by Ferruccio Mengaroni; also houses the Sonosphere.

  • Where Piazza Mosca, Pesaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · regione.marche.it

Museo Archeologico Oliveriano

Museums · Archaeological

Born from the donations of Annibale degli Abbati Olivieri Giordani (1708-1789), on the ground floor of the seventeenth-century Palazzo Almerici: furnishings of the necropolis of Novilara with the famous figurative stele, a bilingual Etruscan-Latin inscription and Roman finds.

  • Where Palazzo Almerici, Pesaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Nazionale Rossini

Museums · Themed

Opened in 2019 on the noble floor of Palazzo Montani Antaldi: ten rooms of different colors follow the life of Rossini, with the composer's Pleyel piano in the Hall of Mirrors, paintings, scores, sets and videos.

  • Where Palazzo Montani Antaldi, Pesaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it · regione.marche.it

Casa Rossini

Museums · House museums

The house where the composer was born on February 29, 1792, bought by the Municipality in 1892 and national monument since 1904: prints, engravings, lithographs and portraits related to the life and works of Rossini.

  • Where Centro storico, Pesaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Teatro Rossini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Born in 1637 as Teatro del Sole in the ducal stables, rebuilt by Pietro Ghinelli between 1816 and 1818 and named after Rossini in 1855; it hosts the Rossini Opera Festival.

  • Where Pesaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

It rises on the remains of a fifth century basilica, with Romanesque façade of the thirteenth century; during the nineteenth-century restorations a large pavement mosaic emerged of about 900 square meters of the middle of the sixth century.

  • Where Pesaro
  • Entry Free

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established in 1994, about 1,600 hectares of cliffs overlooking the sea with fossil fish and chalk crystals, woods and birds like the pilgrim falcon; five paths and a panoramic road towards villages and the marina of Baia Vallugola.
