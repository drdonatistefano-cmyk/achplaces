What to see in Caldaro sulla strada del vino

Wine country on the Wine Road, about 18 km from Bolzano, overlooking Lake Caldaro, the largest and warmest bathing lake in South Tyrol: in the center the Provincial Museum of Wine and the market square from which the wine path starts, above the village the historic funicular Mendola of 1903 and in the surroundings the ice holes, the rastenbach gorge and the early Christian ruin of San Pietro in Altenburg.

Lago di Caldaro

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest and hottest lake in South Tyrol, surrounded by mountains and frequented all year round; from May you can swim, surf and sail. A 7.5 km ring tour (about 2 hours) starts from the paid parking lot at the Lido di San Giuseppe al Lago or Campi al Lago, crosses orchards and vineyards, runs south along the Nature trail (Naturerlebnisweg) and passes through the church of San Giuseppe al Lago.

  • Where San Giuseppe al Lago, Caldaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: italia.it · suedtiroler-weinstrasse.it · suedtirol.info

Museo provinciale del vino

Museums · Themed

Founded on September 18, 1955, it was the first museum born in South Tyrol after the war, to save the tools of traditional viticulture replaced by mechanization; since 1988 it is located in the former winery Di Pauli, already the judicial seat of the lords of Caldaro-Laimburg, after having been in Castel Ringberg. It exhibits large presses and other relics of the long wine history of the village; it is part of the provincial museums as a section of the Museum of customs and customs.

closed for the winter break from 9 November 2026 to 31 March 2027 (weinmuseum.it)

  • Where Via dell'Oro 1, Caldaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.weinmuseum.it

Sources: weinmuseum.it · italia.it · suedtirol.info

Piazza del mercato e sentiero del vino

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The market square is the starting point of the wine trail of Caldaro, which turns around drawing an eight among the vineyards. In autumn there is the festival with long tables and gangs, traditions linked to the Austrian past of the country; in winter there is the Christmas market. The tourist office is located in the square.

  • Where Piazza Mercato, Caldaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: italia.it · suedtirol.info

Funicolare della Mendola

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Designed by Emil Strub and in service since 1903, when it was the longest and steepest funicular, it is a symbol of the country: it climbs in about twelve minutes (845 meters according to Italia.it). From the mountain station you climb in about 3 and a half hours to Monte Roen (2116 m), with views of the Dolomites and Ortles and alpine blooms at the end of June.

  • Where Stazione a valle, San Antonio, Caldaro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: italia.it · kaltern.com · suedtirol.info

Buche di ghiaccio (Eislöcher)

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

A huge accumulation of boulders of an ancient landslide creates caves and niches so cold that ice is formed even in the middle of summer; for this reason, about 500 meters grow plants typical of 1500 meters. You can walk from Rottenburg Square in about 2 hours (mark 15A and 15) or with a ring of about 4 hours from San Nicolò.

  • Where Buche di ghiaccio, Caldaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: kaltern.com · suedtirol.info

Gola del Rastenbach

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Wild and romantic gorge traversed by stairs and bridges, sixth stage of the Path of Peace and touched by several rings from Caldaro and San Giuseppe al Lago, including that of about 1 hour and a half from the Vogelmeierhof.

  • Where Tra Altenburg e San Giuseppe al Lago, Caldaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: kaltern.com · suedtirol.info

Rovina di San Pietro ad Altenburg

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ruin of a early Christian church, probably the oldest ruin of Tyrol's church. It is the final destination of the Path of Peace (about 3 hours, 100 m of difference in altitude, almost flat in the mixed woods), of which it is the seventh stage ("Amore"); Altenburg also arrives the High Path of Caldaro and other routes.

  • Where Altenburg/Castelvecchio, Caldaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: kaltern.com · suedtirol.info

Castel Leuchtenburg

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

First mentioned in 1286 and probably built around 1200 on the foundations of a prehistoric castle, it was the seat of the lords of Rottenburg; some frescoes remain. From the ruins you can see Lake Caldaro, the Wine Road and the Adige valley; you can climb up from the parking lot of Kreithof in about an hour and a quarter.

  • Where Partenza dal parcheggio Kreithof, Caldaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: kaltern.com · suedtirol.info
