What to see in Selegas

Selegas has the parish church of Sant'Anna with the parish museum of sacred art and the facades painted by the painter Liliana Cano. In the hamlet of Seuni stands the sanctuary of Santa Vittoria; in the territory was found the Mother Goddess of Turriga, today at the National Archaeological Museum of Cagliari.

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Anna

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Gothic parish with a rectangular plan, with eight chapels, marble altar and once painted by Oreste Parenti in 1902. The bell tower has four bells: the oldest, of 1608, bears the inscription "Seligas." Next to the church there is the grotto of Our Lady of Lourdes; the feast of the patroness, at the end of July, has procession in traditional clothes, dances and poetic competitions.

Museo parrocchiale

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Set up in the parish of Sant'Anna, it exhibits statues, liturgical books, vestments and sacred furnishings from the sixteenth to the nineteenth century, including a statue of St. Rocco of the seventeenth century, a chalice with a paten of the seventeenth century, a pissy of the sixteenth to the seventeenth century, nineteenth century candlesticks, banners and a dress of the Confraternita del Santo Rosario. Open Saturday 16:30-19:30 and Sunday 8-12, on weekdays by reservation; the visit must be booked.

  • Where Via Chiesa 8, Selegas
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Murales di Liliana Cano

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

The facades of the village are decorated with the murals of the painter Liliana Cano, dedicated to local life and to Renaissance subjects. In July the event sa domu de perda is dedicated to paintings.

  • Where Centro, Selegas
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Santuario di Santa Vittoria a Seuni

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In the hamlet of Seuni, about 2 km from the village, stands the sanctuary of Santa Vittoria martyr, of sixteenth-century origin (1581) and rebuilt in the middle of the twentieth century; the saint is celebrated in May, as Saint Isidoro. In the hamlet there is a stretch of Roman road.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesette di Sant'Elia e Santa Vitalia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

St. Elijah was rebuilt in 1810 on the remains of the church of St. Peter, in a place where perhaps there was a nuragic sacred source. Santa Vitalia, in the upper part of the village, was built in 1887 and rebuilt in 1950; the saint is celebrated in early October.
