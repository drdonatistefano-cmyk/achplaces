Chiusa di Casalecchio

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The oldest hydraulic work in Europe still in operation, recognized by UNESCO as a "Message Monument of Culture of Peace."

  • Where Casalecchio di Reno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Parco della Chiusa (Parco Talon)

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Large hilly park along the Rhine, connected to San Luca by the Bregoli path.

  • Where Casalecchio di Reno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Casa Museo Nena

Museums · House museums

The Palazzo Boschi-Rivani-Garagnani in Ceretolo, documented from the late seventeenth century and opened as a museum house since 2020: three floors of furniture, paintings, ceramics and family memories, with kitchens, loggias, gardens and chapel.

  • Where Via del Lavoro 46/A, Ceretolo, Casalecchio di Reno
  • Entry To be checked on the official website
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museonena.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: museonena.it · bolognawelcome.com · appenninobolognese.cittametropolitana.bo.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
