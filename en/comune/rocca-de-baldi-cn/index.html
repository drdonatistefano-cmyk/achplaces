What to see in Rocca de' Baldi

Borgo with porticoed streets paved with pebbles, tower, square and churches, linked to the Marquis Morozzo della Rocca: the castle was born as a fortress and transformed into a palace in the seventeenth century hosts the Provincial Historical and Ethnographic Museum "Augusto Doro" on the peasant civilization.

Castello di Rocca de' Baldi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Born for defensive purpose around a thirteenth-century tower with Ghibelline battlements, at the base of which opened the door of the village, it was inherited in 1643 by the Morozzo, who expanded it with a massive body towards the square, a Doric portal and a large staircase. The residence of the family until 1823, today it is of the Municipality: it has salons restored on the first floor and a park with centuries-old trees, it houses temporary exhibitions. Visits from the end of April to October on Sundays and holidays in the afternoon, recommended booking.

Museo storico-etnografico provinciale Augusto Doro

Museums · Themed

Born from the collection of the Turin-based researcher Augusto Doro and the Society of Historical Studies in Cuneo, it has been a museum since 1996 and in 2011 it has been re-equipped with a multimedia path. It tells the story of agriculture from its origins to the twentieth century: maps and models about the birth of the rural landscape, the cultivation of cereals, frescoes and scenes of palaces such as Lagnasco and Stupinigi, the photographs of Paul Scheuermeier and Clemens Kalisher. Tour guide is enabled, included in the ticket.
