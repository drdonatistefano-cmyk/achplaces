What to see in Baselga di Pinè

Center of the Piné Plateau, a few kilometers from Trento at the foot of the Lagorai, with the lake of Serraia and the nearby lake of the Piazze (Blue Flag), the biotopes of the Laghestel and the Sternigo Paludi, the parish church of the Assunta and in Montagnaga the Sanctuary of the Madonna di Piné, the most visited Marian destination of Trentino; in Miola the Ice Rink Piné, an ice stadium with a 400 meters ring.

8 places to visit in Baselga di Pinè, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Lago della Serraia

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 974 meters between woods, reeds and meadows, you can reach on foot from the center and a pedestrian path follows its banks connecting to the lake of the Piazze; you practice SUP, kayak and dragon boat, in summer you swim and in winter you ice skate. It has the Blue Flag; near the banks the eighteenth-century capital of the Crucifix, recently restored.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Lago delle Piazze e spiaggia di Campolongo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Artificial lake created in the twentieth century just over 1,000 meters, under the Dosso di Costalta, largely in the municipality of Bedollo; the beach of Campolongo, in the homonymous village of Baselga, is among the most animated places in summer. Equipped beaches and beaches, canoeing, kayaking, windsurfing, fishing and a flat ring ride of about an hour and a half suitable for strollers; Blue Flag.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Santuario della Madonna di Piné (la Comparsa)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

The heart of Marian devotion in Trentino is the most visited Marian monument in the province: according to tradition, Our Lady appeared five times between 1729 and 1730 to the pastorella Domenica Targa. On the hill of Montagnaga, in the place called "La Comparsa" (the Comparsa), a group of natural-size cast iron recalls the first appearance; the devotional path includes the Via Crucis, the neo-Renaissance monument to the Redeemer and a reproduction of the Scala Santa di Roma.

  • Where Montagnaga, Baselga di Piné
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website santuariodipine.it

Sources: santuariodipine.it · visittrentino.info · italia.it

Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient parish church mentioned already in the 12th century, passed from Romanesque forms to a 16th century Gothic architecture, with 17th century bell tower, valuable wooden altars and a painting by Paolo Naurizio.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittrentino.info

Riserva naturale del Laghestel

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Torbous-pallus basin of 89 hectares: an ancient lake almost entirely buried in an intermorenic depression, with a mirror of residual water surrounded by reeds, humid meadows and peat bogs. It hosts 129 plant species, some very rare, and 7 species of amphibians; since 1975 in summer the waters are dyed red for the bloody Euglena algae. Protected by the Municipality in 1974, today Special Conservation Area; you can visit along the perimeter paths with information panels.

  • Where Laghestel, Baselga di Piné
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: provincia.tn.it · visittrentino.info · italia.it

Riserva naturale Paludi di Sternigo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Wetland of 24 hectares north of Lake Serraia, provincial reserve since 1993 and Special Conservation Zone, with torbose meadows, reeds and alder woods; there live yellow-bellied hum, brown nibble and have it small, and the very rare Calamagrostis canescens. It is observed from the perimeter roads; floating buoys mark the protected shore.

  • Where Sternigo, Baselga di Piné
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: tla.provincia.tn.it · visittrentino.info · italia.it
