What to see in Montorio al Vomano

Town of the valley of Vomano, on the edge of the Parco del Gran Sasso and Monti della Laga, gathered around Piazza Orsini with the Baroque collegiate church of San Rocco and the Palazzo marquisale Camponeschi-Carafa, and dominated on top of the hill by the Fort San Carlo of 1686.

9 places to visit in Montorio al Vomano, in the province of Teramo (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Collegiata di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church on Piazza Orsini was started in 1527 by the Countess Vittoria Camponeschi and enlarged by Giovanni Carafa in 1549: the façade unites two bodies born separate, connected in 1562 as it recalls the inscription on the left portal, still renaissance, while the right entrance to broken tympanum is baroque, dated 1633. The interior is a single large baroque hall with large wooden altars of the eighteenth century and paintings of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. Open every day from 7 to 19 according to the MiC card.

  • Where Piazza Orsini, Montorio al Vomano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.gransassolagapark.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · gransassolagapark.it · comune.montorio.te.it

Piazza Orsini e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Piazza Orsini is the lively heart of the village, overlooking the Collegiata and the Palazzo marquisale Camponeschi-Carafa, with a beautiful portal and traces of frescoes inside. In the center you can see the two arches of the ancient Palazzo dell'Università at the entrance of via del Municipio, the small square of the Conserva with the old wash-house and Casa Catini, with fifteenth-century facade and portal adorned with lions.

  • Where Piazza Orsini, Montorio al Vomano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.gransassolagapark.it

Sources: gransassolagapark.it

Forte San Carlo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the top of the hill, it was started in 1686 by the Spanish viceroy of Naples and the troops engaged in the fight against the brigandage were lodged there.

Chiesa degli Zoccolanti (Immacolata Concezione)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the current layout of 1755, with a single nave, it preserves a painted wooden organ, five stone altars with rococo stuccos, seventeenth-century furnishings and a painting by Santa Margherita penitent; in the 18th century noble coats of arms cloister.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Urbani 9, Montorio al Vomano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.gransassolagapark.it

Sources: comune.montorio.te.it · gransassolagapark.it

Chiesa e convento dei Cappuccini (Santa Maria della Salute)

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

On the edge of the village, founded in 1576, enlarged in the seventeenth century and reconsecrated in 1621, it has bare façade with a short frescoed porch, oculus and bell tower; in the single nave wooden altars and canvases, among which those of the main altar (Madonna della Salute, San Lorenzo and Santo Stefano) attributed to Baccio Ciarpi. On the access ramp there is a stylophorus of the XIV-XV centuries; the monastery around the cloister is today little more than a ruin.

  • Where Via della Vittoria 151, Montorio al Vomano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.gransassolagapark.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · comune.montorio.te.it · gransassolagapark.it

Santuario della Madonna della Sgrima

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Marian Sanctuary attested from 1324, with origins related to an event similar to the miracle of Loreto; it was an obligatory stop for transhumant shepherds and the current appearance derives from a restoration of the sixteenth century.
