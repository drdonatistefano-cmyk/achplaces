What to see in Roccavaldina

Small village on a hill on the Tyrrhenian side of Messinese, known for its 16th century pharmacy with 238 vases of the workshop of the Patanazzi and for the castle with baronial palace that dominates the village.

2 places to visit in Roccavaldina, in the province of Messina (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Farmacia (Farmacia cinquecentesca)

Museums · Themed

Spezieria cinquecentesca with antique tools for the preparation of medicinal products and 238 vases modeled in 1580 in the Urbino workshop of Mastro Antonio Patanazzi, decorated with biblical, mythological and historical scenes and with the coat of arms of Cesaro Candia. The kit was purchased at the auction in Messina in 1628 by Don Gregorio Bottaro for 400 onze and donated to the Brotherhood of the SS. Sacramento, who rented it to various herbalists until 1852, when the spices store ceased its activity.

Castello di Roccavaldina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

At the centre of the village, on a panoramic hill, it combines a castle with an essential military facility, with prison cells, armory room and accommodation on the ground floor and a large portal with sharp sixth on the upper floor, and a baronial palace on the back, transition between the Renaissance and Baroque; two successive cylindrical towers flank the façade, with Guelph battlements on the front and Ghibellines on the north-east side.

In renovation to make it a museum or university for the restoration (Metropolitan City of Messina); visitability not indicated
