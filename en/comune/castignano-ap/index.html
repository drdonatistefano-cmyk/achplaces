What to see in Castignano

Medieval pyramid-shaped village, called the hamlet of the Templars, dominated by a Romanesque tower with cusp and overlooking the Conero and the Sibillini as far as the Gran Sasso, the Maiella and the sea; it houses the temple church of Santa Maria del Borgo, the fifteenth-century Universal Judgment of Saints Peter and Paul and a collection of Byzantine icons, with the hamlet of Ripaberarda and the sanctuary of San Bernardino in the surroundings.

Centro storico e piazza panoramica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

A pyramid-shaped hilltop village on a large containment wall, dominated by a Romanesque tower with cusp; walls and alleys make it an open-air museum and according to Italia.it was a stop of the Templars on the road from Rome to the Holy Land. From the square the look goes from Conero and Sibillini to Gran Sasso, Maiella and the sea.

Chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo e Museo sistino di arte sacra

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The museum is set up in the fourteenth-century parish church of Saints Peter and Paul, in an elevated position, with terracotta portal and travertine rosette. It houses a large fifteenth-century fresco of the Last Judgement, a wooden statue of St. Peter of the second half of the fourteenth century and the reliquary-ostensory of the Holy Cross in golden silver, chiselled in 1488 by the goldsmith ascolano Pietro Vannini; in the crypt of the Addolorata, perhaps of Farfense era, traces of frescoes attributed to Vincenzo Pagani and Vittore Crivelli. Opening on request outside the scheduled periods: €4 per person with guided tour.

Museo delle icone e d'arte sacra

Museums · Themed

Opened in 2009 in an eighteenth-century palace belonging to De Scrilli, it was born from about forty years of mons. Vincenzo Catani. On the first floor the section of sacred art with silver, papal medals, liturgical objects, paintings and woods; on the second about 80 Byzantine icons in five rooms; the library has about 4,000 volumes and 53 letters signed (1806-1816) by Francesco Saverio Castiglioni, then Pope Pius VIII.

Santuario di San Bernardino (Santa Maria della Pieve)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

In the hamlet of Castiglioni, about 420 meters between the woods, stands next to a monastery first Benedictine and then Franciscan; the parish church is mentioned in a document of 1599 and since 1963 is entrusted to the Capuchins of Offida. At the altar there is the statue of Saint Bernardino, very venerated, and numerous ex-vote; near are the ruins of a fourteenth-century church. Celebrations on May 20th and September 8.

  • Entry Free

Ripaberarda

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient autonomous municipality, today a hamlet 7 km from Castignano at the foot of the Mount of Ascensione: it preserves walls of the XV-XVI century and a Romanesque-Renaissance bell tower 52 meters high, built in 1518.

Vini, anice e mistrà

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Private cellars and the Cantina Sociale produce Rosso Piceno, Falerio dei Colli Ascolani, Templaria, Gramelot, brandy of marc, anise, mistrà and sparkling wines. The village is also known for the historic Carnival, which closes with the night procession of the Moccoli with lights off, and for the Templaria festival at the end of August.
