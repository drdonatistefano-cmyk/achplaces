What to see in Gravina in Puglia

City of Murgia overlooking the deep ravine of the ravine: the bridge of the aqueduct that joins the two banks, the archaeological park of Botromagno, the Romanesque cathedral, the rock church of San Michele delle Grotte and the seventeenth-century palace of the Fondazione Pomarici Santomasi.

8 places to visit in Gravina in Puglia, in the province of Bari (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ponte dell'Acquedotto

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The eighteenth-century bridge that joins the two banks of the ravine and brought water to the city, symbol of Gravina: from above wide views of the ravine and the archaeological area; it was set of action scenes of No Time to Die (2021).

  • Where Gravina in Puglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Parco archeologico di Botromagno

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the west bank of the gravina preserves part of the ancient settlements, with the necropolis of the Eternal Father; open to the public with free access.

  • Where Collina di Botromagno, Gravina in Puglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Centro storico e quartieri medievali

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

A labyrinth of paved streets, stone houses and squares on the edge of the ravine, with the two medieval districts dominated by the cathedral, the church of Purgatory (Santa Maria del Suffragio) and the Four Fountains.

  • Where Centro storico, Gravina in Puglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The 11th century Romanesque building overlooking the two medieval districts, with interior decorations of different styles.

  • Where Piazza Benedetto XIII, Gravina in Puglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa rupestre di San Michele delle Grotte

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Among the oldest churches in Gravina and its first cathedral, dug into a single block of tuff between the 8th and 10th centuries at the end of the Fondovito district.

  • Where Quartiere Fondovito, Gravina in Puglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Museo della Fondazione Pomarici Santomasi

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the seventeenth-century palace left in 1917 by Ettore Pomarici Santomasi: museum, gallery, library and historical archive, with the frescoes of the crypt of San Vito Vecchio. Tuesday-Sunday 9-13 and 16-20, guided tours every hour; whole 4 €, reduced 3 €.

  • Where Via Museo 23, Gravina in Puglia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Casa museo Cola Cola

Museums · House museums

The home of the Loglisci family, with the laboratory where traditional terracotta whistles are shaped.

  • Where Gravina in Puglia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Bosco Difesa Grande

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

The large forest at the gates of the city, reachable by hiking trails towards the National Park of Alta Murgia.

  • Where Gravina in Puglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
