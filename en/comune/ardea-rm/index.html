What to see in Ardea

An ancient Latin city on the Laurentina street, now known for the Museo Giacomo Manzù, which preserves hundreds of works donated by the sculptor, for the Gardens of the Landriana designed by Russell Page and for the archaeological areas of Casarinaccio and Colle della Noce; on the sea the beach of Tor San Lorenzo.

Museo Giacomo Manzù

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

The monographic museum of the sculptor Bergamasco (1908-1991), author of the Gate of Death of St. Peter, who settled in Ardea in the early 1960s: in 1969 the Amici di Manzù collection was born, donated by the artist to the State in 1979 and opened to the public in 1981. About five hundred pieces among a hundred sculptures, graphics, jewelry, medals and theatrical sketches, with Cardinals, sketches for the Death Gate, Lovers, David of 1939 and Adam and Eve of 1929; in the garden sculptures such as \"Nastro" and the tomb of the artist. Tuesday-Sunday 9-19.30, Monday closed.

  • Where Via Laurentina km 32, Ardea
  • Entry €6, reduced €2 (18-25 years EU), free under 18 and before Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: direzionemuseiroma.cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Giardini della Landriana

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

Over 10 hectares in Tor San Lorenzo with artificial lakes, ancient varieties of roses, themed gardens and tree-lined avenues, born from the passion of the Marquise Lavinia Taverna and divided into spaces designed in 1967 by the English landscaper Russell Page; exhibitions of plants and guided tours.

Open to visitors only in spring and autumn (Visit Lazio)

  • Where Via Campo di Carne, Tor San Lorenzo, Ardea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · cultura.gov.it

Aree archeologiche di Ardea

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

At Casarinaccio, rediscovered in the mid-Twentieth century, the forum of the ancient Ardea with a large temple of the fifth century BC and a basilica of republican age; at Colle della Noce the remains of the temple of the Mount of Noce, in the sixth century BC among the major sanctuaries of the Latium Vetus, discovered in 1981; near, a Christian hypogeus oratory found in 1964, born as a nymph in the second century BC, used by Christians from the fifth century and frescoed in the twelfth century.

the Superintendence reports only extraordinary openings with free guided tour, without ordinary hours

  • Where Via Francesco Crispi 1, Ardea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: sabap-met-rm.cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Casa Museo Franco Califano

Museums · House museums

In a room of the Civic Museum on via Laurentina are exhibited memories of the singer-songwriter, buried in the cemetery of Ardea, including a letter from Mina and its 22 LP.
