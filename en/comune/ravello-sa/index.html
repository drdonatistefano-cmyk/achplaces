What to see in Ravello

The village of the Amalfi Coast, a UNESCO World Heritage Site that includes Ravello, about 400 meters above sea level: Villa Rufolo loved by Wagner, the gardens of Villa Cimbrone with the Infinito Terrace, the Cathedral with the bronze door of Barisano da Trani and the Auditorium of Oscar Niemeyer.

9 places to visit in Ravello, in the province of Salerno (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa Rufolo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Rufolo family residence with thirteenth-century nucleus around the Moorish courtyard with double gallery, extended until the end of the eighteenth century in Arabic, Siculo and Norman style; the main tower offers views of the Gulf of Salerno. The Scottish Francis Nevile Reid created in the nineteenth century the terraced garden, called "Giardino dell'Anima" (Garden of the Soul), which according to tradition inspired Wagner (visiting in May 1880) the garden of Klingsor del Parsifal; in the lower garden is held the Ravello Festival. Open every day 9-20 according to Italia.it.

  • Where Piazza Duomo 1, Ravello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Giardini di Villa Cimbrone

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Villa built in 1904 by Ernest William Beckett, today a hotel, with gardens open to the public: the Terrace of the Infinity with 18th century marble busts, the rose garden and the Tea Room with Moorish gazebo. A plaque recalls Greta Garbo's stay in the spring of 1938.

  • Where Via Santa Chiara 26, Ravello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.ravello.sa.it

Duomo di Santa Maria Assunta e San Pantaleone

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Founded in 1086-1087 on the model of the abbey of Montecassino, it has above the three Roman re-utilization portals and in the center the bronze door to 80 tiles of Barisano da Trani (1179). Inside two marble inlays in front of each other and the seventeenth-century chapel with the vial of the blood of Saint Pantaleone, patron, in Ravello since 1112.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Ravello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it · comune.ravello.sa.it

Museo del Duomo

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage

Set up in the ancient crypt of the Duomo, it collects works from the cathedral and other churches of Ravello, some no longer in operation; among the pieces of relief the so-called "Sigilgaida Rufolo." It is part of the complex with the Duomo and the Church of the Body of Christ.

  • Where Piazza Duomo 7, Ravello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ravello.sa.it · fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni del Toro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Built between 975 and 1018 by the Rogadeo, Pironti and Muscettola families and consecrated in 1276, in the district of the oldest palaces. It preserves an ambo of the twelfth century with mosaics (Jonas and the sea monster) and twenty-one majolica basins of Arab origin, a sarcophagus of the third century AD, frescoes fourteenth-century in the crypt with the Christ Pantocrator and in sacristy a risen Christ attributed to Roberto d'Oderisio or to his circle.

  • Where Via San Giovanni del Toro 4, Ravello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ravello.sa.it · italia.it

Museo del corallo

Museums · Themed · UNESCO World Heritage

Born in 1986 on the initiative of Giorgio Filocamo, a coral craftsman of Sicilian-Campana origin, exhibits ancient objects from the family collection worked according to the coral tradition.

MiC card (updated to 2021) only indicates Monday opening in the morning

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Ravello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Auditorium Oscar Niemeyer

History and architecture · Historic theatres · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Designed by Oscar Niemeyer, who donated the design to the city in 2000, and inaugurated in January 2010: a curved shape that follows the profile of the Coast, a shell on the sea, with about 400 seats under a dome. It hosts concerts and winter shows and screenings on the weekend.

visits only by reservation, free access during the events

  • Where Via della Repubblica 12, Ravello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ravello.sa.it · italia.it

Spiaggia di Castiglione

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

A stone's throw from the road, with a stairway of a couple of hundred steps, or from the sea; better go in the morning, because in the afternoon the cliffs are shaded over the bay.

  • Where Castiglione, Ravello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Local life and traditions · Crafts · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry
