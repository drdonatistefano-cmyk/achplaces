What to see in Acquapendente

The municipality north of Lazio on the Francigena, which owes its name to the waterfalls towards the Paglia: the cathedral of the Holy Sepulchre, the castle of Torre Alfina and the reserve of Monte Rufeno.

5 places to visit in Acquapendente, in the province of Viterbo (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Cattedrale del Santo Sepolcro

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

One of the oldest churches in Lazio, in the middle of the 10th century, so called because it houses a stone stained with blood that is said to come from the Sepulchre of Jerusalem.

  • Where Acquapendente
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Castello di Torre Alfina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The fairytale castle in the homonymous village, a hamlet of Acquapendente, is one of the most beautiful in Italy.

  • Where Torre Alfina, Acquapendente
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Riserva di Monte Rufeno e Museo del Fiore

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

The small naturalistic museum in the woods of the reserve of Monte Rufeno, with the Bosco del Sasseto and the astronomical oasis with planetary.

  • Where Monte Rufeno, Acquapendente
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Torri e palazzi di Acquapendente

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The towers that characterize the city, such as the Julia de Jacopo Tower and the Clock Tower, with Palazzo Visconti and the Town Hall from the neoclassical portico.

  • Entry Free

  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
