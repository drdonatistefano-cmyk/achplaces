What to see in Sperlonga

Medieval village of white houses on a spur overlooking the sea of the Ulysses Riviera, with Torre Truglia and the gates of the Caetani; at the foot of the promontory the Villa di Tiberio with the cave and the National Archaeological Museum of the Odyssey sculptural groups, and about 10 km of golden beaches.

9 places to visit in Sperlonga, in the province of Latina (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo medievale e porte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village of white houses perched on a rocky spur, with alleys and views of the sea; Portella (or Carrese) and Marina doors, with the coat of arms of the Caetani eagle, are the only remains of the 11th century walled castle. Palazzo Sabella, the oldest and most important building of the village, which in 1379 hosted the antipope Clement VII, and Piazzetta del Pozzetto.

  • Where Centro storico, Sperlonga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it · touringclub.it · visitlazio.com

Torre Truglia

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the tip of the promontory, built in 1532 on a previous Roman structure to defend the coast from pirate raids; rebuilt in 1611 and destroyed again in 1623.

  • Where Punta del promontorio, Sperlonga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built at the beginning of the 12th century, with a Latin cross with a bell tower; today it is used as a conference room.

  • Entry To be checked

Museo archeologico nazionale

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Opened in 1963, it exhibits the sculptural groups found in 1957 in the cave of the villa of Tiberio, over 15,000 fragments of marble with episodes of Ulysses: the Palladio Rat, the Pasquino group, Scilla and the Blind of Polyphemus, from which comes the famous head of Ulysses who named the Ulysses Riviera. Closed on Monday.

  • Where Via Flacca km 16,300 (ingresso da via Valle Corsara), Sperlonga
  • Entry €5 (only museum); €10 with the park; 18-25 years €2; free under 18
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website direzioneregionalemuseilazio.cultura.gov.it

Sources: direzioneregionalemuseilazio.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Villa di Tiberio e Grotta

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Maritime villa of the 1st century BC, remodeled by Tiberius that included the natural cave as a nymph; excavated at the end of the fifties, it also includes a fish pond and a spa and extended for over 300 meters along the east beach. The area closes in case of bad weather and is not accessible to people with reduced mobility.

cave temporarily closed for extraordinary maintenance (Regional direction Musei Lazio)

  • Where Via Flacca km 16,300, Sperlonga
  • Entry €10 with the museum
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: direzioneregionalemuseilazio.cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Spiagge di Sperlonga

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 10 km of golden sand at the foot of the village, with clear water; Blue Flag in 2024 and one of the 10 beaches Green Flag of Lazio, suitable for children.

  • Where Lungomare, Sperlonga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Parco regionale Riviera di Ulisse e Punta Cetarola

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The park offers hiking trails and includes the promontory of the villa of Tiberio; the cliffs of Punta Cetarola, which close the bay south, are protected by a Blue Oasis of the WWF.

  • Where Punta Cetarola, Sperlonga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it
