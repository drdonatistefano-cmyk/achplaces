What to see in Sarezzo

The municipality of Valle Trompia is linked to the tradition of iron, with the Museum "I Magli" in the sixteenth-century forge, the 17th-century parish church of Santi Faustino e Giovita, Palazzo Avogadro in Zanano with frescoes between the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries, a stretch of the Roman aqueduct of Brixia and the sanctuary of Sant'Emiliano that dominates the middle valley.

Museo «I Magli» di Sarezzo

Museums · Themed

Set up in the sixteenth-century forge of Sarezzo, it offers a museum and ecomuseum itinerary on the Valtrumpline traditions of iron: the "Sala Magli" with three great sweaters and, on the first floor, the Sanzogni collection of tools of the local blacksmiths and the Pellegrini collection with over 200 scientific instruments of the twentieth century; children's games retracing the cycle of metal. It is the first stage of the "Via del Ferro e delle Miniere" in the Valle Trompia Eco-Museum.

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Faustino e Giovita

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1633 on medieval structures and consecrated in 1652, on a project by Giovanni Battista Lantana, author in 1604 of the Duomo Nuovo di Brescia. Classic façade with four columns and tympanum, and statues of the saints holding in two niches. Inside the altarpiece of the Madonna with Child and saints of the Morettian school in a frame of Carlo Dossena, carvings of the Pialorsi called Boscaì di Levrange in the presbytery, altarpieces by Antonio Gandino and Francesco Paglia on the side altars and a sixteenth-century wooden crucifix by Maffeo Olivieri on the holy arch.

Palazzo Avogadro

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

In the historic center of Zanano, owned by the municipality, it is formed by a northern building older, with the medieval house-tower, and by a south modified in the seventies of the twentieth century. On the first floor, a fresco between the 14th and 15th centuries tells of a clash between Guelphs and Ghibellines in the Trompia Valley, with black bears armed against white bears; in the same room a Madonna enthroned with the Child and St. Martin bishop of the 15th century, and in another room a St. Jerome of 1474. It usually hosts the City's annual Art Prize.

Tratto dell'acquedotto romano di Brixia

History and architecture · Archaeology

Aqueduct called Aqua Salsa, built in the first decades of the 1st century A.D. in Auguste to bring to Brescia the water of the valley of Lumezzane with an underground path of about 20 km. Discovered in via Antonini during the excavations of the Sewerage Collector of the Trompia Valley, it was partly removed and relocated near the town hall, with restoration curated by the Archaeology Superintendence of Lombardy.

Santuario di Sant'Emiliano

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

It dominates the village of Sarezzo and the middle Valle Trompia; according to an ancient plaque the church dates back to the 13th century and the restorations have emerged the foundations of a Romanesque apse. The 18th century wooden main altar houses the statues of the Saints Emiliano and Tirso and of the Madonna del Sodalto, and the altarpiece of Giovan Battista Botti (1746) depicts the Saints Barnaba, Vincenzo Ferreri and Fermo in veneration of the Virgin. It is reached by four paths from Sarezzo, Lumezzane, Marcheno and Gardone Val Trompia, or by car from Zanano up to about 500 m from the sanctuary.
