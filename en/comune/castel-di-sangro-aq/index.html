What to see in Castel di Sangro

Città dell'alto Sangro, home of the painter Teofilo Patini: the Basilica of Santa Maria Assunta rebuilt on a Greek cross between 1695 and 1725, the historic center with Palazzo De Petra of the Lion, seat of the Patinian Art Gallery, the ruins of the castle between the Sannithic walls and, beyond the river, the fifteenth-century convent of the Magdalena that houses the Aufidenate Civic Museum.

Museo Civico Aufidenate

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the Franciscan monastery of Maddalena, built in 1487 on a pilgrim's hospice along the Via degli Abruzzi and then home to the Customs of Foggia, school and prison. The collection, opened in 1898 with 335 exhibits and 130 coins and largely dispersed in the war, has fossils and paleolithic finds, statues of the Iron Age and osque ceramics, inscriptions and Roman furnishings, votive terracotta and a Sannite bull in golden bronze, as well as medieval and modern statues and ceramics. In the same complex the botanical garden, a nativity scene of the Neapolitan school, the War Museum and the International Museum of Fly Fishing "Stanisao Kuckiewicz" . Wednesday-Sunday 10.30-18.30.

  • Where Contrada della Maddalena, Castel di Sangro
  • Entry €2, reduced €1
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · comune.casteldisangro.aq.it

Pinacoteca Patiniana

Museums · Modern art

Opened in 2006 in the fifteenth-century Palazzo De Petra, called Casa del Leone for the Marzocco at the entrance: he collects paintings by Teofilo Patini (Castel di Sangro 1840 - Naples 1906), realist painter of the Abruzzo peasant life author of the social "trilogy" (Vanga e latte, L'erede, Bestie da soma), and works by friends and students of the Aquila school that he directed. Tuesday-Friday 11-13 and 15-18, Saturday-Sunday 11-13 and 15.30-18.30, Monday closed.

  • Where Palazzo De Petra, Via del Leone, Castel di Sangro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.casteldisangro.aq.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Basilica di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Basilicas

Church mother, born in the second half of the 10th century in place of Santa Maria ad duas Basilicas (V century), destroyed by the earthquake of 1456 and rebuilt between 1695 and 1725 on a project by Francesco Ferradini with Greek cross; the appearance is largely baroque.

  • Where Piazza Santa Maria, Castel di Sangro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.casteldisangro.aq.it · italia.it

Centro storico e Via del Leone

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Itinerary of the Municipality from Piazza Plebiscito, with the fountain of 1898 and the church of San Giovanni Battista (formerly Dominican convent, relics of San Felice), on Via De Petra with registered houses, carved balconies, a bifora of the thirteenth century and a Roman column, the Palazzo De Petra and the sixteenth-century palace with loggia, up to Corso Vittorio Emanuele with the church of San Nicola, ex-grancia of the Knights of Malta where Celestino V and Carlo II d'Angiò stayed, and the fountain "Ace of cups"; then the remains of Roman villas and baths and the bridge of the Maddalena.

  • Where Piazza Plebiscito, Castel di Sangro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.casteldisangro.aq.it · italia.it

Chiesa dei Santi Crispino e Crispiniano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Already San Leonardo, documented from the sixteenth century, deconsecrated after 1800 and used as a base and slaughterhouse, was restored around 1860 by the brotherhood of the shoemakers. Romanesque portal with Malta's cross, linked to the pilgrims of the Valeria street; inside the sixteenth-century altar of the Magdalena church among the coats of arms of the Caracciolo, feudal for three centuries.

  • Where Corso Vittorio Emanuele 142, Castel di Sangro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.casteldisangro.aq.it · italia.it

Ruderi del castello e mura sannitiche

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

A path climbs in about twenty minutes to the ruins of the castle, flanked by remains of Sannitic fortifications.

  • Entry Free
