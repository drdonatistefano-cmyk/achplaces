What to see in Pietramontecorvino

Medieval village of the Dauni Mountains dominated by the Norman-Ange Tower and the Ducal Palace, with the Terravecchia district with fishbone, the Mother Church of Santa Maria Assunta and, a few kilometers away, the archaeological site of Montecorvino.

6 places to visit in Pietramontecorvino, in the province of Foggia (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo Ducale e Torre normanno-angioina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The quadrangular crenellated tower of the 13th century, almost 40 meters high and recently restored, is connected by a high wall to the Doge's Palace, of supposed Angevin origin, on three floors and about 2,500 sqm: you can visit the Hall of representation with barrel vault, two courtyards and the hanging garden; on the vault the coat of arms of the Montalto di Tocco, last feudal.

  • Where Piazza del Rosario, Pietramontecorvino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Quartiere Terravecchia

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Historical fish-bone core that climbs up the hill, with narrow and twisted alleys often closed by the defensive "meniali" and houses partly dug into the tuff; remains the Port'Alta with ogival arch, only of the three doors, and in via Tappeto the only surviving medieval house-tower. The caves "Preta" are now cellars, once a refuge of shepherds.

  • Where Terravecchia, Pietramontecorvino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa Madre di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The oldest church in the village, documented in 1328 and probably in the late 12th century, with side portal with sharp arches, monumental staircase and eighteenth-century loggia, Renaissance chapel, funeral monuments of the Tinto family (1567), baroque altar and medieval bell tower with yellow and green brick dome.

  • Where Piazza del Rosario, Pietramontecorvino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Biagio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

At the foot of the staircase of the Mother Church, with Gothic arch; on the door of the sacristy a plaque with Gothic inscription of 1375, the oldest text of the country.

Sito archeologico di Montecorvino

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

About 5 km from the historic centre, it is the original site of the village, razed to the ground in the 12th century.
