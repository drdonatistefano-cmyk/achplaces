What to see in Portomaggiore

3 places to visit in Portomaggiore, in the province of Ferrara (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 2 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Delizia Estense del Verginese

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage

Country house transformed by Alfonso I d'Este into a ducal residence for Laura Eustochia Danti; it houses the museum of the Sepulcreto dei Fadieni, a Roman family.

  • Where Gambulaga, Portomaggiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo del Sepolcreto dei Fadieni

Museums · Archaeological

In the Delight of the Virgin over 200 finds of a small Roman necropolis of the I-II century: fine-walled ceramics, skylights, amphorae, silver and bronze objects and a remarkable collection of glass.

  • Where Delizia del Verginese, Gambulaga, Portomaggiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Oasi di Porto

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Area of 11 hectares with a water mirror of 7, suitable for birdwatching and sport fishing.

  • Where Portomaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
