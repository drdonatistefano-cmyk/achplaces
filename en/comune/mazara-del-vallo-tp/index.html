What to see in Mazara del Vallo

Trapanese seaside town, among the major fishing ports of the Mediterranean, with the North African Casbah, the Norman Cathedral of the Most Holy Savior, the Norman Arch of Piazza Mokarta and the Museum of Satyr, which exhibits the Greek bronze fished in 1998 in the Sicily Canal.

Museo del Satiro

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the sixteenth century deconsecrated church of Sant'Egidio is exposed the Dancing Satyr, bronze from the end of the fourth century BC attributed to the school of Praxitele: the left leg was fished in the spring of 1997, the body on March 4, 1998 by the fishing boat Capitan Ciccio, between Sicily and Capo Bon; it depicts a dancer in ecstasy with panther skin, restored by the Central Institute for Restoration. Also displayed is an elephant paw in punic-hellenistic bronze, a medieval cauldron, amphora and two cannons of Torretta Granitola.

  • Where Chiesa di Sant'Egidio, Piazza Plebiscito, Mazara del Vallo
  • Entry €8, reduced €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Cattedrale del Santissimo Salvatore

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Founded by the Normans around the year 1000, it mixes Romanesque, Greek and Baroque elements; on the portal to the sea the sculpture of Santiago El Matamoros. Il Tocchetto, loggia on an arched bridge, connects it to the Bishop's Palace on Piazza della Repubblica.

  • Where Centro storico, Mazara del Vallo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Casbah

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient Arab quarter of narrow alleys and facades decorated with majolica, where the Tunisian community lives and architecture and perfumes recall North Africa; it is known for its fish cuscus.

  • Where Centro storico, Mazara del Vallo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info

Museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters

Born in 1993 in the monumental 18th-century seminary on Piazza della Repubblica, with porticos and loggias of the architect Giovan Biagio Amico Trapani and eleven arches on two floors; exhibits works of art in the rooms on the ground floor.

The MiC card (updated 2021) indicates closure in November and December due to lack of staff; in the other months Tuesday-Saturday 10-12.30 plus some afternoons; tel. 0923 909431.

  • Where Via dell'Orologio, Mazara del Vallo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Collegio dei Gesuiti e Museo civico

Museums · City history · Free entry

Baroque courtyard building that houses library, historical archive and civic museum, with artifacts from the Eneolithic to the late Byzantine, a vast collection of paintings and a room dedicated to a contemporary artist Mazarese with aqueforts, aquatin and reliefs. The municipality indicates it as accessible free of charge (October 2026).

  • Where Piazza Plebiscito 2, Mazara del Vallo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitsicily.info · comune.mazaradelvallo.tp.it · italia.it

Arco normanno di Piazza Mokarta

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

The only rest of the castle built under Ruggero II, of the 12th century, in limestone; restored with the green light of the Superintendence of Trapani, it returned to use in January 2024.

  • Where Piazza Mokarta, Mazara del Vallo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · comune.mazaradelvallo.tp.it · italia.it

Ruderi della chiesa di Sant'Ignazio

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The Jesuit Church was founded in 1701 with an oval plan, whose dome and two bell towers collapsed in December 1933.

  • Where Piazza Plebiscito, Mazara del Vallo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Lungomare e porto peschereccio

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

A walk by the sea from which you can look at the boats that depart from one of the major fishing ports of the Mediterranean, with about 300 fishing boats; on the seafront the church of San Vito, patron of the city, and in the restaurants the red shrimp of Mazara.

  • Where Lungomare, Mazara del Vallo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info

Riserva naturale integrale Lago Preola e Gorghi Tondi

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

A wet karst area of 335 hectares, established in 1998 and managed by the WWF: from west to east Lake Murana, Lake Preola, the largest, and the three Round Gorghi between maquis, thorny oak and the wreck holm oak of the Bosco del Cantarro; rich in aquatic birds in migration, with wild orchids and the Sicilian marsh turtle.

  • Where Mazara del Vallo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: orbs.regione.sicilia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it
