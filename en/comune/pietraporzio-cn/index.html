What to see in Pietraporzio

Mountain municipality of the upper Stura Valley at 1246 meters, of Occitan minority, with the hamlets of Pontebernardo, Castello and Murenz; in Pontebernardo are located the Eco-Museum of Shepherdship and the parish of the Assumption, while from Murenz you go up to the Red Becchi, above the passage of the Barricades.

3 places to visit in Pietraporzio, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ecomuseo della pastorizia

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Permanent Museum set up in a large and bright room, which offers a picture of sheep breeding and shepherding in the mountains of Cuneo and nearby France. Guided tours on the first weekend of the month from 15 to 18 (in July and August all weekends); on the other days you can visit independently from 9 to 18 with automatic opening by token. It is advisable to check the opening before the visit (tel. 0171 955555).

  • Where Frazione Pontebernardo, Pietraporzio
  • Entry Free guided tours; autonomous tour with coin ($1)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Parrocchiale dell'Assunta a Pontebernardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church dedicated to Maria Assunta, already existing in the Middle Ages and rebuilt between the end of the seventeenth and early eighteenth centuries: from a single aisle with three altars it passed to seven altars with continuous vault. In 1771 it was decided to replace the old sailing bell tower, damaged in 1744, with the current one; in the nineteenth century two small lateral naves were added. The treasure includes liturgical vases, a processional cross of the sixteenth century and a collection of relics.

  • Where Frazione Pontebernardo, Pietraporzio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: vallestura.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Becchi Rossi (2261 m)

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Excursion starting from Murenz, hamlet of Pontebernardo (1567 m), on a dirt road in the pine forest signposted GTA, which over 1850 m becomes a path; about 700 m of altitude difference and 2 hours of climbing, hiking difficulty, recommended from June to October. At about 1950 m there is a first fortification: the area hosted defensive works that controlled the passage of the Barricades. The top is a panoramic point.

  • Where Partenza da borgata Murenz, Pontebernardo, Pietraporzio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: vallestura.cn.it · visitcuneese.it
