What to see in Prunetto

Borgo dell'Alta Langa sulla Bormida, dominated by a castle of the thirteenth century intact on a rock overhanging, spared by an earthquake that swallowed half of the village; next, the sanctuary of the Madonna del Carmine with frescoes fifteenth century of Segrano Cigna.

Castello e la Batajera

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Medieval fortress of the thirteenth century that has preserved its original appearance except the moats, on a rock overhanging: scenic round corner tower, entrance with drawbridge, Palacium connected to the mast, inner courtyard and late fourteenth-century bifore on the south and west facades. The Batajera, a square-plan porch, was the ancient entrance. An independent audioguide (QR code or PIN, in Italian, English, German and Piedmontese) is accessible all year round and includes the exterior and courtyard of the castle; from May to October guided tours on Sundays at 15, 16 and 17.

Santuario della Madonna del Carmine

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Church of medieval origin next to the castle, extended several times over the centuries, which preserves frescoes in part of itinerant painters and in part of the Monregalese Segurano Cigna (XV century), as well as remains of older paintings, among the oldest in the valley. It is part of the tour with audioguides of the village.

Belvedere di Prunetto

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the panoramic point at about 750 meters the view ranges from the Ligurian Apennines to the Cozie Alps; coming from the Bricco della Colma along the ridge opens the unreal vision of the village dominated by the castle. It is the stage of the Great Langhe Crossing (GTL).
