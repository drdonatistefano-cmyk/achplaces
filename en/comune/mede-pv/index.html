What to see in Mede

The centre of the Lomellina is located around the Castello Sangiuliani, of which there are traces of 14th-XV century walls, a loggia and a crenellated tower; the castle houses the civic museums, with the works of the futurist sculptor Regina Cassolo and the archaeological and naturalistic collection Ugo Fantelli.

Castello Sangiuliani

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Castle built in the 12th century, seat of the Counts of Mede and then of the Sangiuliani. In the center of the village are still sections of the walls of the XIV-XV century, an elegant loggia of the following period and a crenellated tower; today it houses the Masinari library and the civic museums.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica 39, Mede
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · visitpavia.com

Museo Regina Cassolo Bracchi

Museums · Modern art

He collected the works of the futurist sculptor Regina Cassolo, donated to the city by her husband Luigi Bracchi: 52 sculptures and about 500 drawings, temperas and collages. The route goes from the Futurism of the thirties to the Movement concrete art.

  • Where Castello Sangiuliani, Piazza della Repubblica 39, Mede
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: visitpavia.com · in-lombardia.it

Raccolta archeologica e naturalistica Ugo Fantelli

Museums · Themed

About a thousand finds divided into a naturalistic section (mineralology, zoology, paleontology) and an archaeological-ethnographic one, with 780 prehistoric lithic artifacts coming almost all from the Saharan area.

  • Where Castello Sangiuliani, Piazza della Repubblica 39, Mede
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: visitpavia.com · in-lombardia.it

Raccolta di memorie e cose del tempo

Museums · Themed

Private collection of about two thousand objects, gathered by Pierangelo Boccalari, on agricultural work, the crafts and domestic life of Lomellina.

  • Where Via Di Vittorio 6, Mede
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: visitpavia.com · in-lombardia.it
