What to see in Naples

Capital of the Bourbon kingdom and one of the great cities of Europe, with the historic UNESCO World Heritage center, the castles on the gulf, the world-class museums and a millennial subsoil.

74 places to visit in Naples, in the province of Naples (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.
