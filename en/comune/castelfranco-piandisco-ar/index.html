What to see in Castelfranco Piandiscò

The villages of Castelfranco di Sopra and Pian di Scò along the Strada dei Sette Ponti, between the Balze del Valdarno and Pratomagno.

4 places to visit in Castelfranco Piandiscò, in the province of Arezzo (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre d'Arnolfo e centro di Castelfranco di Sopra

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The symbolic tower of the village, rest of the castle built by Florence, with views of the countryside and the Balze; in the village the Town Hall of the first fourteenth century and San Filippo Neri with works by Matteo Rosselli.

  • Where Castelfranco di Sopra, Castelfranco Piandiscò
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Abbazia di San Salvatore a Soffena

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Monastery complex documented in 1014 and rebuilt in the fourteenth century, with frescoes from the fifteenth century by Paolo Schiavo, Bicci di Lorenzo and an Annunciation of the Scheggia, brother of Masaccio.

  • Where Soffena, Castelfranco Piandiscò
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Pieve di Santa Maria a Scò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque parish with three aisles, with six columns from capitals to human figures, animals and plants.

  • Where Pian di Scò, Castelfranco Piandiscò
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Sentiero dell'Acqua Zolfina nelle Balze

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The path that crosses the Balze del Valdarno, pinnacles and canyons of sand and clay carved by erosion.

  • Where Castelfranco Piandiscò
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
