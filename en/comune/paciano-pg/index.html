What to see in Paciano

Medieval village on the hills of Trasimeno, closed by fourteenth-century walls with six towers and three gates and designed on three parallel roads: Palazzo Baldeschi with the museum Banca della Memoria del Trasimeno, the Museo Don Aldo Rossi in the church of San Giuseppe and, just outside, the sanctuary of the Madonna della Stella.

8 places to visit in Paciano, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico, mura e porte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village preserves the medieval layout of three parallel roads connected by perpendicular alleys, inside a 14th century wall with six towers and three gates: Fiorentina, Perugina and Rastrella. Along the water path you will find the Fontevecchia outside Porta Rastrella, the wash-houses near Porta Perugina and a well of Etruscan origin near the Fortress Buitoni.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Museo Don Aldo Rossi e chiesa di San Giuseppe

Museums · Old masters

Section of Paciano of the museum system of the archdiocese of Perugia-Città della Pieve, born from an idea by Don Aldo Rossi of 1994. In the church of San Giuseppe (XII century, partly rebuilt in the eighteenth century), restored, you can see votive frescoes, wooden statues, liturgical furnishings, a gonfalone of Bonfigli's workshop, a painting by Francesco Appiani and an organ by Adamo Rossi from 1795.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Pinacoteca della Confraternita del Santissimo Sacramento

Museums · Old masters

Second section of the Museo Don Aldo Rossi, in the former hall of the confraternity near the Town Hall: it contains a fresco of the Crucifixion of 1452, a fresco of Francesco da Città della Pieve and paintings of the XV and XVII century. Entrance from Via della Pitalessa.

Palazzo Baldeschi e Museo Banca della Memoria del Trasimeno

Museums · Themed

Rebuilt building in the seventeenth century incorporating multiple tower houses, houses a multimedia space, virtual archive of collective memories, dedicated to the knowledge of the artisan, agricultural and food and wine work of the population of Trasimeno.

Santuario della Madonna della Stella

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Church built in 1572, just outside Porta Rastrella in the southern part of the village, where according to tradition a pilgrim saw a star stopping for three days on an image of the Madonna.

Torre d'Orlando

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Documented as early as 917, it is the only remnant of the ancient settlement of Paciano Vecchio; the name ties it to the Paladino Orlando of Charlemagne. As a noble residence, it is now privately owned: you can see along the panoramic walk from Porta Perugina towards Panicale.
