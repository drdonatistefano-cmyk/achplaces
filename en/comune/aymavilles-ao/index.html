What to see in Aymavilles

Country between the vineyards at the entrance of the Cogne Valley, with the four-tower castle of the Challant and the Roman bridge-aqueduct of Pont d'Aël.

4 places to visit in Aymavilles, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Aymavilles in the guide with map and filters

Ponte-acquedotto di Pont d'Aël

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Built in 3 B.C. by Caius Avilius Caimus, it is 56 metres above the Grand Eyvia stream: great work of Roman civil engineering.

  • Where Pont d'Aël, Aymavilles
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · gestlovevda.it

Castello di Aymavilles

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

14th-century castle of Amedeo di Challant, with four towers and white facades between the Middle Ages and Baroque; exhibits the history of the Challant and the collection of the Académie Saint-Anselme.

  • Where Frazione Urbains 32, Aymavilles
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · gestlovevda.it

Chiesa di Saint-Léger

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In 1762, with a trompe-l'œil painted façade in 1857 by the painter Grange, a medieval crypt and a rare bell of the fourteenth century.

  • Where Aymavilles
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · gestlovevda.it

Villaggio di Ozein

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

At about 1400 meters, overlooking Mont Blanc and Grivola.

  • Where Ozein, Aymavilles
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · gestlovevda.it
