What to see in Santa Sofia d'Epiro

Arbëreshë community in the hills, whose historic centre is an example of the widespread agglomeration organized for families according to the model of gjitonia; in Palazzo Bugliari, the seat of the Town Hall, the Museum of the territory and the costume arbëreshë.

Centro storico arbëreshë

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The old town is described by the FAI as an open book of the widespread agglomeration system typical of the arbëreshë communities: extended family groups have defined the perimeters of their nuclei according to the model of gjitonia, and the site was chosen taking into account orography, exposure to sun and wind and availability of water.

  • Where Via Scigata, Santa Sofia d'Epiro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fondoambiente.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it

Museo del territorio e del costume arbëreshë

Museums · Themed

Housed in Palazzo Bugliari, a building in the middle of the nineteenth century also seat of the Town Hall, it exhibits a complete reconstruction of the women's clothing arbëreshë: daily dresses, festive dresses, wedding ceremony and mourning.

  • Where Largo Trapeza, Santa Sofia d'Epiro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fondoambiente.it
