What to see in Apiro

The village is walled on the balcony of the Marches at the foot of Mount San Vicino: the Baroque collegiate church of Sant'Urbano, the town hall of 1246, the Mestica theatre and, in the valley of the Esinante, the Romanesque abbey of Sant'Urbano; around the lake of Castreccioni and the beech woods of Pian dell'Elmo.

9 places to visit in Apiro, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Abbazia di Sant'Urbano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Romanesque abbey on the left bank of the Esinante, documented by the 11th century (consecrated in 1086) and rebuilt in the thirteenth century: three naves and three apses, classroom for the laity, Gothic presbytery raised for the monks and crypt; until 1442 rivalled with Apiro. On May 25, the feast of the saint, the first ray of sun enters from an opening above the apse and illuminates a circle engraved in the stone.

  • Where Località Sant'Urbano, Apiro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Collegiata di Sant'Urbano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Baroque Collegiate with three naves from 1632 in Piazza Martini, with an altarpiece from the seventeenth century, an organ from the eighteenth century and the collection of sacred art of Sant'Urbano.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Palazzo comunale e piazza Baldini

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient palace of the Priors, built in 1246, overlooking the main square where in summer events and concerts are held; you enter it from Porta Garibaldi, door of the medieval walls of which also remains the polygonal tower (XII and XV century).
