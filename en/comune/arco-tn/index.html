What to see in Arco

Cittadina dell'Alto Garda among the olive trees, with a mild climate that made it a place of care and today the world capital of sport climbing: the castle of the counts of Arc with the fourteenth-century frescoes of courteous life, the Civic Gallery dedicated to Giovanni Segantini, born here in 1858, the Arboreto of the Archducale Park and the fifteenth-century sanctuary of the Madonna delle Grazie.

10 places to visit in Arco, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Arco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Residency of the counts of Arc between the XII and XVII century, is today a vast ruin of walls, cisterns, prison (Prigione del Sasso), Torre Grande and fortress with the Renghera tower, whose bell called the peasants to prayer. In the residential body near the Torre Grande one of the most important cycles frescoed profane of the fourteenth century in the alpine area, with courteous scenes of life, departures to checkers and battles; from the Prato della Lizza and a glass platform the view extends over the plane of Sarca to Garda. Walk up among the olive trees in 20-30 minutes; free entry with Garda Trentino Guest Card.

  • Where Via Castello 10, Arco
  • Entry €5, reduced €3,50 (combined with the Galleria Segantini €7, reduced €4,50)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: gardatrentino.it · cultura.trentino.it · visittrentino.info

Galleria civica G. Segantini

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Opened in 2012 by the MAG Museo Alto Garda in the seventeenth-century Palazzo dei Panni, it is dedicated to Giovanni Segantini (Arco 1858-1899), one of the major exponents of divisionism: paintings, sculptures and works on his own paper and contemporary Trentino and Veneto such as Eugenio Prati, Vittore Grubicy and Alessandro Milesi, works by Leonardo Bistolfi, a documentary section and interactive posts. Since November 2024 he has exhibited "Autumn sun," not visible to the public since 1954. Free entrance with Garda Trentino Guest Card and Museum Pass.

  • Where Via G. Segantini 9, Arco
  • Entry €4, reduced €2; during the exhibitions €7, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · gardatrentino.it

Centro storico e Collegiata di Santa Maria Assunta

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

At the foot of the cliff of the castle narrow alleys, noble palaces, stone portals and courtyards; in Piazza III Novembre the collegiate church of Santa Maria Assunta, with facade of the seventeenth century on older structures, and the fountain of Moses with the coat of arms of accounts. Palazzo Marchetti and Palazzo dei Panni recall the civil life between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries; at the foot of the castle the Rilke Promenade touches the places mentioned by Rainer Maria Rilke, who stayed in Arco.

  • Where Piazza III Novembre, Arco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Arboreto di Arco, Parco Arciducale

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

Born in the Archduke's Park in 1873 by Archduke Albert of Austria, it is a botanical garden with more than 200 species of trees and shrubs from all over the world, from monumental conifers to Mediterranean plants, favored by the mild climate of Garda; managed by the Trento Natural Science Museum, it has a flat path with information panels.

  • Where Parco Arciducale, Arco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Santuario della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Wanted in 1478 by Count Francesco d'Arco, with the convent of the Reformed Friars completed in 1482 and the church ten years later; portal in bronze and terracotta tiles on the façade of Fra' Silvio Bottes, author also of the statue of San Francesco on the square. In the fifteenth-century cloister, a level below the church, the tombstones of Susanna Collalto and Barbara Martinengo. Church open by day.

  • Where Via Dante Alighieri 9, Ceole, Arco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Museo delle missioni francescane

Museums · Themed

Born from a missionary exhibition of the Franciscan friars of Trentino, it occupies three rooms on the ground floor of the convent-sanctuary with objects brought by the missions: from China paintings, embroidery on silk, paintings on rice paper and statuettes of divinity above all seven-nineteenth century; from the pre-incaic Andes finds in stone and ceramics, incaic images and Bolivian handicrafts; some windows on Africa. Opening on request.

  • Where Via Dante Alighieri 9, Ceole, Arco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: cultura.trentino.it · visittrentino.info

Convento dei Cappuccini e chiesa di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Founded in 1585 by the counts of Arc next to the church of San Lorenzo, with a new church consecrated in 1593; in 1630 some friars died curing the plagues. Suffered by Joseph II in 1787 and then in Napoleonic age, he was damaged in the Great War. Cloister with arches in local stone and well; in the church with a single nave the altarpiece of Paolo Farinati and a fragment of Madonna from the ancient collegiate. Visitable during the day, without barriers.

  • Where Viale S. Francesco 3, Arco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: comune.arco.tn.it · visittrentino.info

Falesie e Rock Master

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Arco is considered the world capital of sport climbing, with cliffs equipped in the surrounding area; since 1986 it hosts every year at the end of summer the Rock Master, international climbing competition, in the Rock Master Climbing Stadium.

  • Where Arco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info
