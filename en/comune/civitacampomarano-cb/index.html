What to see in Civitacampomarano

Medieval village on a sandstone ridge, dominated by the Fortified Angevin Castle in the fifteenth century and became known for the murals of the CVTà Street Fest. It is the birthplace of Vincenzo Cuoco and Gabriele Pepe.

5 places to visit in Civitacampomarano, in the province of Campobasso (Molise). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Civitacampomarano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Castle on a sandstone hill at 520 m, perhaps of Norman origin, rebuilt in the fifteenth century with quadrangular belt and three cylindrical crenellated towers; the Catalan-Aragonese portal bears the coat of arms Carafa della Spina, Di Sangro and Monforte and a dragon with upside down lilies, symbol of the betrayal to the Angioini. You can visit the six-arched loggia, the courtyard with well and cistern, the noble rooms with painting decorations, stables and barn, towers and round paths; national monument since 1979, from 2023 houses a nucleus of contemporary art with works by William Congdon, Gino Marotta and Titina Maselli. Open from Tuesday to Sunday (closed on Mondays), tickets also through the official app.

  • Where Largo Vincenzo Cuoco, 86030 Civitacampomarano (CB)
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: musei.molise.beniculturali.it · italia.it

Centro storico e murales del CVTà Street Fest

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Village of stone houses, narrow alleys and steps on a sandstone ridge. Since 2016 the CVTà Street Fest, directed by artist Alice Pasquini, has brought on the walls of the country works by artists such as Biancoshock, David de la Mano and Martin Whatson, today a call for visitors.

  • Where Centro storico, Civitacampomarano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu

Civita di Sotto

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The lower part of the village, partly depopulated, preserves the church of Santa Maria delle Grazie with an 18th century Baroque altar, the Clock Tower and the Mercante House decorated with carved stones.

  • Where Civita di Sotto, Civitacampomarano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Casa natale di Vincenzo Cuoco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The birthplace of the writer Vincenzo Cuoco, owned by the Municipality; according to Italia.it has been restored as a space for events and reception, while Visit Molise indicates it in renovation to become a Museum of Contemporary Art.

  • Where Civitacampomarano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu

Fontane storiche

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Fontana Defenza, Fontana San Giovanni, Fonte dei Frati and the fountain of sulphur water Ria Rigola, recently renovated.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
