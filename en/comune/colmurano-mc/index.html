What to see in Colmurano

Small village of Maceratese that preserves the remains of the walls with perimeter towers and an ogival door before 1200; among its churches the SS. Annunziata with the Nativity of Giovanni Andrea De Magistris (1560) and the Church of the Loggia with a Renaissance fresco; in summer it hosts the Artistrada Buskers Festival.

5 places to visit in Colmurano, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Mura, porta ogivale e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

There are still sections of the walls with the perimeter towers and an almost intact ogival arched door, before 1200. In summer, the centre hosts the Artistrada Colmurano Buskers Festival, an international street art festival.
