What to see in Barbarano Romano

Village of houses in tuff with typical profferli, gate of the Regional Park Marturanum (the Park of the Etruscans) that protects the Etruscan necropolis of San Giuliano with graves from the 7th century BC to the Roman age; in the village the Museum of the Rupestri Necropolis and churches from the 11th to the 16th century.

Necropoli etrusca di San Giuliano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

One of the most important Etruscan necropolis in central Italy and the only one that shows the entire evolution of Etruscan funerary architecture: from wells and pits of the Iron Age to large orientalizing mounds, to archaic rock tombs with porch, newsstands and chamber, to the Hellenistic nut graves (VII century BC-III century A.D.). Among the most famous tombs are the Tomb of the Deer and the Tomb of the Queen; three entrances along the road of the Quercete (Ciusa-Cima, San Simone and San Giuliano, Caiolo) and numbered paths, reachable on foot from the village with path CAI 103.

Museo Civico Archeologico delle Necropoli Rupestri

Museums · Archaeological

In the thirteenth century complex of Sant'Angelo, in the historic centre, exhibits the funeral furnishings of the necropolis of the territory, especially of San Giuliano, from the end of the IX to the III century BC: bronzes, ceramics, sarcophagi in tuff and obelischi, including an obelisk stone more than three meters high, the Sarcophagus of the Sacerdotessa and the orientalizing set of the Cima tomb with objects of Phoenician origin. A second section documents the rock necropolis of Viterbese.

Unpublished times: the MiC card indicates closed every day, opening entrusted to the Barbarano culture association (tel. 0761 414531)

Rocca e Chiesa romanica di San Giuliano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the plateau of San Giuliano remain the imposing walls in tuff of the fortress, where probably stood the Etruscan and then the medieval town of Marturanum, abandoned at the beginning of the II millennium in favor of the current Barbarano. Next, the Romanesque church with three naves with paintings from the 14th and 15th centuries and a romerium inhabited by hermits until the end of the 19th century; near the Roman bathroom, an indoor swimming pool dug into the tuff.

Parco Regionale Marturanum e Gole del Biedano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Park established in 1984 on about 1,200 hectares, with nature trails in the valleys; the path of the Biedano Gorges is dedicated to the ancient stone millstones along the river. In the equipped area of the Caiolo (parking, tables, barbecue, fountain) starts the visit to the necropolis; visitor center and naturalistic museum 100 meters before the entrance of the village.

  • Where Area del Caiolo, Barbarano Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · parchilazio.it

Via Clodia e Bosco della Bandita

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

In the southern sector of the Park (the area of the Fourth) remains sections of the Roman road, brought to light in the eighties; beyond the pastures the path disappears in the Bosco della Bandita, a high stem cerret.
