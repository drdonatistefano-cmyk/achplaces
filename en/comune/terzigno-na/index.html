What to see in Terzigno

On the eastern side of the Vesuvius, which takes its name from the third fire, the lava flow of 1631: the MATT exhibits the frescoes and the ori of the Roman villas of Cava Ranieri buried in 79 AD, the house-museum of Salvatore Emblema preserves the works of the artist and the pine forest of Piana Tonda is crossed by an accessible path of the Park.

Museo archeologico territoriale di Terzigno (MATT)

Museums · Archaeological

In the former renovated municipal slaughterhouse, since 2019 the exhibition "Pompei oltre le mura" shows frescoed walls and objects of the Roman villas of Cava Ranieri (Ville 1, 2 and 6) buried by the eruption of 79 A.D.: second style paintings with architectural perspectives, restored by the archaeological park of Pompeii, and the larario of the kitchen of Villa 6 with two Lari, the genius who sacrifices and two snakes. Since 2025, a new room has been showing the Terzigno Treasure of the Villa 2: silver (a situla with swan-head, a cup, a mirror, 21 coins) and three gold necklaces, one with emeralds. Free admission with a valid Pompeii ticket.

Museo Emblema

Museums · House museums

House-museum wanted by the artist Salvatore Emblema in his hometown as a place of study and diffusion of contemporary art: about 600 m2 of interior spaces and a park of sculptures of about 3,000 m2, with the most significant works of the artist in rotation every six months. It is home to the general archive of Emblema, a museum of regional interest since 2009, and organizes educational workshops. MiC timetable: Tuesday-Friday 9-18.30, Saturday and Sunday 10-14, Monday closed (to be confirmed on tel. 081 8274081).

Sentiero n. 11 La Pineta di Terzigno

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

A flat 1.5 km return route (about 30 minutes, low difficulty, maximum altitude 211 m) built by the Park in 2004-2005 in Piana Tonda and also designed for those with reduced mobility: wooden walkway with rope rail and panels also in Braille. It crosses a dense pine forest planted in the first half of the twentieth century on recent lavas still visible, Mediterranean maquis and pyroclastic soils, with views of the volcano side and a garden of the perfumes.

  • Where Via Vecchia Campitelli, Terzigno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.parconazionaledelvesuvio.it

Sources: parconazionaledelvesuvio.it
