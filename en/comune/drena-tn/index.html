What to see in Drena

Country between Lake Garda and Lake Cavedine, inhabited already in prehistoric times, dominated by Castel Drena with a 25-metre-long mast overlooking the Marocche, expanses of boulders left by the post-glacial landslides. Around, a historic center of stone doors and alleys, the tiny village of Braila, the centuries-old chestnut groves with the park of sculptures Drena 3000, the ferrata of Rio Sallagoni and Malga Campo.

Castel Drena

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Documented since 1175, when it passed from the Seiano to the Arco, it controlled the road between Trento and Garda; contended for centuries between local nobles, Counts of Tyrol and bishops princes, it became imperial fief of the Arco in 1433 and was made more residential in the fifteenth century. Fired by French troops in 1703, he remained in ruins until the restoration of the Province started in 1984. You can see the spiral walls on the back, the 25-metre-high mast that climbs up to the top, the perimeter of the church of San Martino (VIII-IX century), the comital palace and a fifteenth-century chapel; it houses exhibitions, concerts and shows. Reduced 12-18 years, over 65 and groups; free under 12 years and with the Garda Guest Card.

Marocche di Dro

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Spreading of rocks born from a series of landslides during the last glaciation, which lie under the castle: an almost lunar landscape crossed by a dense network of paths.

  • Where Sotto Castel Drena, Drena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittrentino.info
