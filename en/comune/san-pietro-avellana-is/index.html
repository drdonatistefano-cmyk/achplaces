What to see in San Pietro Avellana

The small village of Alto Molise on the border with Abruzzo, about 960 meters on the northern slopes of Monte Miglio, destroyed by the bombings of 1943 and surrounded by woods of beech and oaks famous for the white truffle; on the mountain the polygonal walls of Sanniti, in the village the Civic Museum built with the stones of the rubble and, to the north, the astronomical observatory "Leopoldo Del Re" .

Museo civico Arti, Mestieri, Archeologia e Costume

Museums · Themed

In the upper part of the village, in a building built with stones recovered from the rubble of the bombings of 1943, exhibits the findings of the archaic necropolis of Piano Fusaro (VII-VI century BC, 25 burials and a road in use from the third century BC to the Roman age), reconstructions of kitchen, wedding room and classroom, shoemaker's shops, housekeeper, saddlemaker, winemaker, beekeeper, carpenter and blacksmith, tools of shepherdhood and wool related to transhumance, vintage clothes from the seventeenth century to today, sacred art, a large photographic archive and relics of the bombardments. Timetable indicated by italia.it: Tuesday and Saturday 10.30-13 and 15-16.30, from Wednesday to Friday 10.30-13.

Osservatorio astronomico «Leopoldo Del Re»

Museums · Science and university

To the north of the municipal territory, named after the astronomer of Cantalupo in Sannio, it allows astrologers to observe the sky and has a planetarium that reproduces the celestial vault with 20,000 sidereal bodies.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu

Mura poligonali sannitiche di Monte Miglio

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On Mount Miglio there is a Sannitic fortification with three walls of polygonal walls, the Cyclopic walls testifying to the presence of the Sannites, reachable with marked paths of different difficulty, duration and starting point.

  • Where Monte Miglio, San Pietro Avellana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitmolise.eu · italia.it

Sorgenti e boschi di Monte Miglio

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The territory is rich in springs and streams: Pesco Bertino, Capo di Vandra and Fonte Calante are recommended stops for nature lovers, among the Rio and Vandra streams and woods of beech and oak where you look for truffle, with the possibility of excursions, climbing and canoeing on the Sangro. The black truffle is celebrated with a fair in August, the white with a market exhibition in November.

  • Where Monte Miglio, San Pietro Avellana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitmolise.eu · italia.it
