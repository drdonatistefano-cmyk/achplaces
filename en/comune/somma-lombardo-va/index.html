What to see in Somma Lombardo

The town of Varesotto sul Ticino, dominated by the Visconti Castle of San Vito, inhabited by the Visconti and rich in collections; in its territory there are also Volandia, the park and museum of the flight, the Dams of the Panperdute on the Ticino and the paths of the Brughiera del Vigano in the Ticino Park.

Castello Visconti di San Vito

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Castle built in the 13th century, developed around three quadrangular courtyards with towers and a courtyard with drawbridge; the Visconti settled there in 1448, when Francesco and Guido Visconti took refuge during contrasts with the Ambrosian Republic, and the halls of representation were frescoed since 1590, with interiors renewed in Baroque taste. You can visit the staircase, the private chapel, the Royal Chamber and unusual collections: shaving dishes, 360 stuffed birds, arms and Spanish armor of the sixteenth century and finds of the civilization of Golasecca. Open from April to October on Sundays and on holidays (10-11.45 and 14-18); free under 6 years, reduced for children 6-12 years and members FAI and TCI.

  • Where Piazza Scipione 2, Somma Lombardo
  • Entry €8, reduced €4,50, family €15
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Volandia, Parco e Museo del Volo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

A journey on the history of international aviation, with attention to the historical companies of Varesotto, Lombardy and Italian industry, divided into five areas: flight forms, fixed wing, rotating wing, modeling and convertiplani, from the first hot air balloons to planes of the early twentieth century. There are indoor and outdoor areas for children, simulators, library and historical archives. Open from Tuesday to Sunday; free under 2 years and for people with disabilities.

  • Where Via per Tornavento 15, Somma Lombardo
  • Entry €11, reduced €8, children 3-11 years €5
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Dighe del Panperduto

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Paid entry

Complex of industrial hydraulics on the banks of Ticino, along the European path E1 and the river and cycle path of the waterways, with the former tollgate of hydraulic guardianship. Guided tours every Sunday at 10.30 am by reservation (limited places; other days for groups of at least 15 people); reduced for children 6-10 years, free up to 5 years and for people with disabilities; dogs allowed on leash.

  • Where Via Lungo Canale Villoresi 4, Somma Lombardo
  • Entry Guided tour €10, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: vareseturismo.it · comune.sommalombardo.va.it · in-lombardia.it

Brughiera del Vigano e Sass di Biss

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

An easy ring of 5.5 km (about 1 hour and a half, suitable for mountain biking) in the Ticino Park: starts from the sports field of Coarezza and follows the ST/IP trail along the Strona river, among pine forests, oaks and chestnuts. Touch the dam of Porto della Torre, in operation since 1942 and today also hydroelectric power station, the Sass of Biss, erratic mass with rock engravings linked to the culture of Golasecca (IX-V century BC), and a panoramic point on the Ticino at the edge of the moor.

  • Where Campo sportivo di Coarezza, Somma Lombardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: vareseturismo.it · lombardiabeniculturali.it · in-lombardia.it

Basilica di Sant'Agnese e Palazzo comunale

Places of worship · Basilicas

The prepositural church, now a minor basilica in the historic centre, was built by the Visconti in the 15th century and renovated in the first half of the 17th century by Francesco Maria Richini, who introduced elements of the Roman Baroque; it has rich decorative apparatuses. Opposite is Palazzo Viani Visconti, former residence of the noble Visconti Viani and municipal seat since 1897.

  • Where Centro storico, Somma Lombardo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · comune.sommalombardo.va.it · in-lombardia.it

Santuario della Madonna della Ghianda

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Sanctuary in the hamlet of Mezzana, linked to the legend of a 13th century Marian apparition among the branches of an oak, scene that Michelino da Besozzo frescoed in the vault. Pellegrino de' Pellegrini enlarged the structure by designing the current sanctuary, with a large altar in front of the small original apse that leaves the fresco visible; the facade is decorated with statues.

  • Where Viale Madonna della Ghianda, Mezzana, Somma Lombardo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · comune.sommalombardo.va.it · in-lombardia.it

Murales del museo diffuso

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Since 2019 the artists Ravo and Refreshink paint on the walls of the city subjects inspired by the works of the castle, such as La Carità, Le Tre Grazie and the Archangel Gabriele; the mural "La Carità" by Andrea Ravo Mattoni is in via Milano 17.

  • Where Via Milano 17, Somma Lombardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it
