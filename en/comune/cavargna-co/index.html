What to see in Cavargna

A small mountain town in the Upper Lake Como, a landmark of Val Cavargna, with a territory that rises from over 1,000 meters to over 2,000 of Garzirola; here you will find the Museum of the Valley, the oratory of San Lucio on the homonymous pass at the Swiss border and the alpine pastures of the municipality.

Museo della Valle

Museums · Themed

Ethnographic museum founded in 1982 by Fr Federico Scanziani, parish priest of Cavargna from 1957 to 2004, to preserve traditions, customs and culture of the valley. The sections tell the magnans, itinerant ponders with their tools and the box "trida" , the traditional costume of the late nineteenth century, the life of the peasant-pastor, the woodsmen, the smuggling, the ancient mines and the iron workmanship and the cult of St. Lucy; the section of popular religiosity exhibits wooden statues of the eighteenth century, a monstrance of Ambrosian rite and vestments in part from the old parish of St. Lawrence.

Oratorio di San Lucio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Oratory near the Passo di San Lucio, at 1,542 meters on the Swiss border, attested in the thirteenth century: Carlo Borromeo stopped there to pray almost a day in the visit of 1582, Federico Borromeo described it in 1606 and had painted the frescoes for centuries. Inside the frescoes of the saints Lorenzo, Lucio, Vincenzo and Antonio Abate, a God Father in almond attributed to the school of Giovan Battista Pozzo and a fifteenth-century wooden statue of the patron saint of the housewives; tradition wants him to keep the remains of Lucio di Cavargna. You can walk on a dirt road from the provincial road to the Val Rezzo; on August 16, a big party attracts visitors from Italy and Switzerland.

Chiese di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Two churches on the same site, each with its own bell tower: the old San Lorenzo, attested in the parish church of Porlezza in the 13th century and became parish church in 1575 by the will of Carlo Borromeo, who visited it in 1582; and, little down, the new parish church, which preserves a altarpiece of the 16th century and a fifteenth-century statue of San Lucio from the oratory of the pass.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Monte Garzirola

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The top, over 2,000 meters and also referred to as Galzirola, is the highest point in the municipal territory; it is reached from Cavargna or from the area of the oratory and the San Lucio refuge, with a refuge on top and panoramic view of Lake Lugano. Route for experienced hikers; from Cavargna you cross the Sacred Wood, a wide beech wood that acts as a barrier against avalanches.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Alpeggi di Tabano, Segor e Stabiello

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Alpeggi del Comune connected by numerous excursions in a landscape of pastures; the Oasis of Segur is the starting point for exploring the mountains of Cavargna.
