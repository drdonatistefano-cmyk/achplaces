What to see in Calascibetta

The village perched on Mount Xibet in front of Enna, with medieval Norman plant: the Church Mother Royal Palatine Chapel on the ruins of the castle Marco, the church and the tower of San Pietro, the caves of Via Carcere, the Jewish quarter and, in the countryside, the necropolis of Realmese and the Byzantine village of Canalotto.

8 places to visit in Calascibetta, in the province of Enna (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico, Piazza Umberto I e Piazzale Angelo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval village, built under the Normans after the thousand, is a plot of alleys and courtyards with traces of caves. From Piazza Umberto I, one of the largest in Sicily with marble fountain and bronze statues, to Piazzale Angelo, one of the most beautiful views of the Sicilian hinterland; via Giudea recalls the Judia, the district of the medieval Jewish community.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Calascibetta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa Madre - Regia Cappella Palatina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Wanted by Pietro II d'Aragona on the ruins of the castle Marco, completed in 1340 and named Royal Palatine Chapel in 1342; with three naves, it is among the main expressions of Catalan art in the province, with column bases with winged felines and griffons. From the floor you can see the excavations of the castle and, under a glass, a early Christian rock church.

  • Where Piazza Madrice 6, Calascibetta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.calascibetta.en.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Chiesa e Torre di San Pietro

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the height of Mount Xibet, the church was the first large collective construction of the Norman period; the monolithic columns were damaged by the earthquake of January 11, 1693. The tower, the only remnant of the ancient castle and today bell tower, is the oldest building in the municipality and was the base of the army of Ruggero d'Altavilla during the siege of Enna.

  • Where Piazza San Pietro, Calascibetta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.calascibetta.en.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Grotte di via Carcere

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Typical rock settlement of Calascibetta, with caves on several levels excavated in grey limestone, used as a prison in the Middle Ages; in the rock you can see fossil shells of oysters of Pliocene. At Christmas they host the living nativity scene.

  • Where Via Carcere 7, Calascibetta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.calascibetta.en.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Chiesa di San Francesco d'Assisi e convento dei Cappuccini

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

At the end of via Giudea, the convent was inaugurated on September 21, 1589. On the main altar there is a seventeenth-century canvas with the Adoration of the Magi by Filippo Paladini; in the sacristy there is a wooden tabernacle of the seventeenth century and a library with about 7,000 ancient works.

  • Where Via Giudea, Calascibetta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.calascibetta.en.it · italia.it

Necropoli di Realmese

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Second necropolis of the Pantalian type of Sicily, with 288 grotticella tombs dug in the rock, used in the IX century BC and reused in archaic age (VI century BC); excavated in 1949-50 by Luigi Bernabò Brea, the finds are at the Museum Paolo Orsi of Syracuse. It remains a stretch of the ancient paved road.

Access only on foot, not accessible by persons with reduced mobility

  • Where Trazzera Calascibetta-Gangi, presso Cacchiamo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.calascibetta.en.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Villaggio bizantino di Canalotto

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Late-Byzantine rocky settlement in the Canalotto valley, with two two-storey churches dug into the rock and about thirty caves used as dwellings or shelters for animals, overlooking the Erei Mountains.

  • Where Vallone Canalotto, Calascibetta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Santuario della Madonna del Buon Riposo

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Outside the town, built according to tradition at the point where a Madonna-shaped rock found by miners became too heavy to carry. Feast on the first Sunday and Monday of September with cattle fair, sausage festival and Palio dei Berberi.

  • Where Fuori dal centro abitato, Calascibetta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
