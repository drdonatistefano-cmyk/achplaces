What to see in Petilia Policastro

Ancient Byzantine village at the foot of the Small Sila, among the calanchi of the Marchesato, known for the murals of Vico Leone; it preserves the main church of St. Nicholas and St. Mary Major, the oldest in the village, and outside the town the Sanctuary of Santa Spina, which since 1523 has kept a thorn of the crown of Christ.

Santuario della Santa Spina

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Born from the ancient hermitage of Santa Maria Eremitana, passed to Carthusians, Benedictines, Cistercians and Basiliani and, after 1320, to the Franciscans as Santa Maria delle Grazie; on August 22, 1523 came from Paris the Sacred Spina, relic of the crown of Christ donated by Queen Giovanna of Valois to the confessor Dionisio Sacco, Franciscan of Policastro. 14th-century bell tower, church full of works of art, cloister, vegetable garden and wood; today it is inhabited by the Mariana Family the five Stones, with guided tours.

  • Where Località Santa Spina, Petilia Policastro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.petiliapolicastro.kr.it · italia.it

Chiesa matrice di San Nicola Pontefice

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Documented from the early Middle Ages, the parish already in 1443; the façade bears the date 1651 and the inscription "P.F. 1685 , year of restoration financed by the Archconfraternity of the SS. Sacramento, with three-order bell tower and statues of Saints Peter and Paul. Three naves with barrel vaults; inside the Holy Family of the polycastrese painter Francesco Giordano, a San Sebastiano near the ways of Mattia Preti on the altar in polychrome marble, a Renaissance limestone waterworks, a Pietà in stone with traces of color and a nineteenth century choir of 24 stables.

  • Where Via San Sebastiano 30, Petilia Policastro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.petiliapolicastro.kr.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Also known as Santa Maria La Magna, it is considered the oldest church of Policastro, on the eastern side of the medieval town, in the ancient Byzantine Polycastron; documented since 1418. A single nave with pediment in a hut and portal with date 1834; inside an altar in fake marble, the ceiling with the Mystical Lamb, four canvases including a Saint Anthony of Padua of 1844 and a Redeemer Christ in paper.

  • Where Largo Luigi Carvelli, Petilia Policastro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.petiliapolicastro.kr.it · italia.it

Murales di vico Leone

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

In the historic centre the murals of the street artist Giuseppe Caruso transform vico Leone into an open-air museum, between narrow alleys and steep staircases of a village among the calanchi of the Marchesato.

  • Where Vico Leone, Petilia Policastro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.petiliapolicastro.kr.it
