What to see in Jelsi

Borgo del Molise centrale known for the Grano Festival, which every July 26th for over two centuries brings in procession in honor of Sant'Anna the 'traglie', wagons without wheels decorated with braided ears; the tradition is told in the Museo della Traglia, in the former church of Piazza Chiesa Madre, and in the Museo di Comunità hosted in the Convento di Santa Maria delle Grazie.

Museo della Traglia

Museums · Themed

Set up in an ex-church restored with European funds (on the portal dated 1363; inside remains an inscription of consecration of 1696), it collects agricultural tools, wheat-feast branches and the reproduction in wheat and straw of the Holy Door of St. Peter, paraded at the feast of 2000 and blessed that year by the Pope in Rome. The track is a carriage without wheels that slides on two wooden shoes; for the feast the ears, softened in water, are woven in figures such as the ball, the niche, the cone and the hair. Times not indicated by the MiC card: contact the Municipality (0874 710134).

  • Where Piazza Chiesa Madre, Jelsi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · icpi.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo di Comunità della Festa del Grano (MuFeG)

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the Convent of Santa Maria delle Grazie, he documents the community's connection with the Wheat Festival, celebrated every July 26 in honour of Sant'Anna for over two hundred years: documents, paintings, photographs and videos on the history of the festival, the phases of cultivation from the grain to the spike and allegorical floats of past editions; he also carries out research and teaching activities for schools and groups. According to the MiC card is open from Tuesday to Sunday 8.30-19 with mandatory booking.

  • Where Contrada Convento, Jelsi
  • Entry €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Convento di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Complex with church and bell tower in Romanesque style, built in three phases and born to welcome abandoned children, for the impulse of Father Ciro Soccio, remembered by a bronze monument in front of the cemetery. Inside a dome illuminates the frescoed presbytery; in a canopy the seventeenth-century statue of the Virgin and on the altarpiece the Last Supper in high relief.

  • Where Contrada Convento, accanto al cimitero comunale (SP162 verso Gildone, poi SP119), Jelsi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitmolise.eu · italia.it

Centro storico e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village preserves its main monuments, Casa Pinabello-D'Amico, Palazzo Civico and Palazzo Ducale Carafa, and the church of Sant'Andrea Apostolo, patron saint celebrated with the patronal feast; according to Visit Molise a thirteenth-century mausoleum was then transformed into an angioin castle.

  • Where Centro storico, Jelsi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu
