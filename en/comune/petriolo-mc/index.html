What to see in Petriolo

Medieval village enclosed by ancient walls, founded according to the portal of the Municipality around the Mille by the Farfensian monks; in the center are Piazza San Martino with the view towards the coast and the Sibillini, the Civic Tower and the Church of Saints Martin and Marco, while just outside stands the sanctuary of the Madonna of Mercy with the Museum of the wood processions.

Museo dei Legni Processionali «Mons. M. Manfroni»

Museums · Old masters

Museum of Sacred Art was born by the will of the Brotherhood of the SS. Sacramento as a place of collection of processional woods, housed in the church of Our Lady of Mercy. He exhibits processional statues, furnishings, ex-vote, plains and liturgical furnishings, with sixteenth-century paintings by Durante Nobili and Andrea De Magistris and eighteenth-century paintings by Gualtieri and Luigi Fontana.

  • Where Via Marco Martello 8, Petriolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Santuario della Madonna della Misericordia

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Built in 1496 and enlarged in the second half of the eighteenth century on a project by Pietro Augustoni; in the niches of the facade there are statues of the local artist Nello Cruciani. On the main altar there is the wooden statue of the Madonna della Misericordia by Antonio Aquilano (1525), linked to a legend; inside frescoes by Ciro Pavisa.

  • Where Via Marco Martello 8, Petriolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · turismo.comune.petriolo.mc.it

Il Castello di Petriolo, centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry
