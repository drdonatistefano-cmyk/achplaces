What to see in Mamoiada

The village of the Barbagia di Ollolai, between Gennargentu and Supramonte, famous for the Mamuthones and Issohadores who leave for the first time on 17 January, for Sant'Antonio: the Museum of Mediterranean Masks and the Museum of Culture and Work, the necropolis of Istevene, from 2025 UNESCO Heritage, the stele of Sa Perda Pintà and the alleys of the center with murals and sculptures.

Museo delle maschere mediterranee

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

It connects the Mamuthones and Issohadores with the carnivals of other Mediterranean lands: at the entrance images, texts and sounds on the origins of the Mamuthones and the oldest Mamuthone mask, perhaps early nineteenth century; then the hall of the barbaric carnival, with Boes, Merdules and Filonzana of Ottana and Thurpos of Orotelli, and the Mediterranean hall, with the carnivals of the Alpine arch and the Iberian and Balkan peninsulas. The Mamuthones carry the black face of wood, sheepskins and about 30 kilos of bells. A cumulative ticket with the Museum of Culture and Work €6, reduced €4.

  • Where Piazza Europa, Mamoiada
  • Entry €4, reduced €2,60
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Museo della cultura e del lavoro

Museums · City history · Paid entry

He tells in thematic rooms the culture, traditions and history of Mamoiada using the traditional clothes as a guiding thread: the life of men and women until the mid-Twentieth century, the social role, the moments of celebration and mourning. The main hall is dedicated to the crafts of the country. cumulative ticket with the Museum of Masks €6, reduced €4.

  • Where Via Sardegna, Mamoiada
  • Entry €4, reduced €2.50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Necropoli di Istevene (Sa Conchedda Istevene)

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage

About 2.5 km from the village, in a wood of holm oaks, six domus de Janas (locally honcheddas) excavated at different levels in granite, between the final Neolithic and the beginning of the Eneolithic (3200-2800 BC). The tomb III, the most characteristic, preserves traces of red plaster, engravings, a cup and a taurine protome in relief on a pillar: one of the very few domus decorated with symbolic motifs of the province of Nuoro. In 2025 it was registered by UNESCO in the World Heritage Site together with 16 other prehistoric Sardinian necropolises.

  • Where Località Istevene, Mamoiada
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sa Perda Pintà (stele di Boeli)

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

On the outskirts of the village, a granite monolith almost three meters high decorated with concentric circles, curved sticks and 23 cups, symbols related to fertility and the death-rebirth cycle; Italy.it dates it to 3000 BC and is perhaps coeval of the necropolis of Istevene.

  • Where Località Boeli, Mamoiada
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Centro storico, murales e Su 'Antaru Vetzu

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The alleys of the center are known for the numerous murals and sculptures; in the heart of the village there is Su 'Antaru Vetzu, ancient stone fountain, and the historical districts of 'Astru and 'Antaru vetzu, linked to a station of the Roman military road. Here are also the shops where the masks are still hand carved.

  • Where Centro storico, Mamoiada
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di Nostra Signora di Loreto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Loreto de bidda is located in the historic centre, perhaps of medieval origin, rebuilt at the end of the 17th century, with the frescoed dome; outside the village there is the little Loret'Attesu, where the feast of Our Lady of the Snow is celebrated.

Santuario dei Santi Cosma e Damiano (San Cosimo)

Places of worship · Sanctuaries
