What to see in Bortigiadas

Borgo dell'Alta Gallura, known for the Mineral Museum, has been active since 1984 with 800 specimens of minerals; in the village the seventeenth-century parish church of San Nicola di Bari and other eighteenth-century churches, in the surroundings the rocks carved by the wind of Punta Salizi and the domu de janas of Tisiennari.

Museo mineralogico

Museums · Science and university

Active since 1984, it exhibits 800 specimens of minerals for 250 species, mostly coming from the area of the village and the rest from other areas of Sardinia, with unique pieces on the island.

  • Where Centro abitato, Bortigiadas
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di San Nicola di Bari

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church, built in 1607, has a granite façade and a hut roof; inside a barrel vault on three arches, artistic stained glass windows and a painting of the late seventeenth century, ...St. Nicholas and St. Lucifer defenders of the divine motherhood. In the village also Santa Croce (XVIII century, restored in 1980) and the church of Carmel, eighteenth century in Gallura style.

  • Where Centro storico, Bortigiadas
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Domu de Janas di Tisiennari

History and architecture · Archaeology

Tomb excavated in the rock of the recent Neolithic (4th millennium BC), with four rooms decorated; a cell has a false door surmounted by Taurine horns.

  • Where Località Tisiennari, Bortigiadas
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Punta Salizi

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Rocks shaped by the wind over the centuries in natural sculptures, among Mediterranean maquis, corks and holm oaks.

  • Where Punta Salizi, Bortigiadas
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
