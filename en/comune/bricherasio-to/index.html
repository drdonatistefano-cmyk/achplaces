What to see in Bricherasio

Country at the entrance of Val Pellice with the parish baroque of Santa Maria Assunta (1602), the stone climb to the castle above Piazza Santa Maria and, in the hamlet of San Michele, the neoclassical church and the Ecomuseum of the Resistance.

5 places to visit in Bricherasio, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Baroque church built in 1602; the main point of interest is the high altar, completed in 1725. The bell tower dates back to 1828-32, the baptismal font of 1699 and the holy waterworks of 1513; the nineteenth-century organ is of the Collino brothers. He preserves paintings by Lorenzo Delleani, Barbavara, Marietti and Morgari. He put on Saturdays and preaching at 5:30 p.m., Sundays and holidays at 10:30 p.m.

  • Where Piazza Santa Maria Assunta, Bricherasio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Salita al Castello

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The sixteenth-century ramp flanked by stone walls that go up to the castle: it is formed by 15 raised slabs of stone laid cut and by stretches in pebbles of 30-40 cm.

  • Where Vicolo del Castello - Piazza Santa Maria, Bricherasio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.bricherasio.to.it

Sources: comune.bricherasio.to.it

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

18th century church in neoclassical style with longitudinal plan, with three-part bell tower and single nave interior with barrel vault; in façade frescoes with San Michele and San Giovanni. It rises in the center of the village, about 1.5 km from the village.

  • Where Strada San Michele, frazione San Michele, Bricherasio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.bricherasio.to.it

Sources: comune.bricherasio.to.it

Chiesa di San Bernardino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Baroque church with a single nave, linked to a confraternity, with dome above the apse, wooden choir, walls decorated with pilasters and golden stuccoes and bell tower with square plan. It is located about 50 meters from the main square.

Ecomuseo della Resistenza

Museums · Themed

Networked documentation centre between Angrogna, Bricherasio, Luserna San Giovanni and Torre Pellice, born to reconstruct the years of the Resistance in Val Pellice; it is located in the former fractional school of San Michele, with a permanent exhibition. Visits by appointment through the Municipality (012 59105).
