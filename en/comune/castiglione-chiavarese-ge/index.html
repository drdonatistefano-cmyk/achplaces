What to see in Castiglione Chiavarese

The town of Val Petronio between 120 and 1,094 meters, from the Mediterranean scrub to the abetaias; Masso is the oldest copper mine in Western Europe, today a museum pole, while Velva preserves a medieval pedestrian village, the Sanctuary of Our Lady of the Guard and the museum of peasant culture.

Museo archeominerario di Masso (MuCast)

Museums · Archaeological

The centre tells about 5,000 years of man's work in Val Petronio: an interactive museum in the former school of Masso, an excavation area that brings back to the Age of Copper (about 3500 BC) and the Galleria XX Settembre, a copper mine active in the nineteenth century and indicated as the only mine that can be visited in Liguria with an underground route. Excavations indicate that Mass is the oldest copper mine in Western Europe. Guided tours: from 15 June to 14 September Wednesday and Friday at 18, Saturday and Sunday at 10.30 and 18; from 15 September to 14 June on Sunday at 10.30, 14 and 16. Tel. 0185 469139.

Borgo di Velva

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

First mentioned in 1451 in a document that mentions the church of San Martino, Velva is an example of Ligurian rural architecture: the medieval fabric has been preserved because the interior of the village is almost all pedestrian, with creuze and narrow caruggi paved in stone and arches between the houses.

  • Where Velva, Castiglione Chiavarese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it

Sources: fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it

Santuario di Nostra Signora della Guardia di Velva

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

To the north of Velva, on the border with Maissana, the sanctuary was started in the 19th century on the project of architect Maurizio Dufour, at the will of the population. It preserves a wooden crucifix and the wooden group of the Madonna della Guardia carved by Antonio Canepa in 1892, brought to Velva in 1894 after the exhibition in the Genoese churches of Santa Maria delle Vigne and San Torpete. On August 29, a procession climbs from the village to the sanctuary.

Museo diffuso della cultura contadina - Ecomuseo dell'Alta Val Petronio

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Museum spread in the historical center of Velva, with its headquarters in the ancient Oratorio dei Bianchi (used to worship until the early twentieth century) and in cellars, funds and domestic environments where the objects remain in their context. The sections illustrate agricultural and domestic activities, handicrafts, transport and commercial: workshops of the carpenter-boatmaker, the carter-mulker and shoemaker, beating of chestnuts, threshing, mills and mills. From April to the end of October open on Saturday 14-18. Tel. 0185 408016.

Ruderi dell'Ospitale di San Nicolao

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On Monte San Nicolao, above Velva, remain the ruins of a Roman hospital active throughout the Middle Ages, which welcomed pilgrims and travelers on the Romea road between Liguria, Emilia and Lunigiana until the fire of 1590. It is still readable the cross plan of the XII-XIII centuries, with three semicircular apses in the horizontal arm and a small church annexed; the site is the object of excavations and archaeological studies.

  • Where Monte San Nicolao, sopra Velva, Castiglione Chiavarese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
