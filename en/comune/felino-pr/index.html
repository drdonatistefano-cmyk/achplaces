What to see in Felino

Country of the Parma hills along the Prosciutto Road and the Wines of the Colli di Parma, linked to Salame Felino IGP; above the village the 12th century castle, which in the ancient kitchens and cellars houses the Salame Museum, and in the village the 14th century torrione.

6 places to visit in Felino, in the province of Parma (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Felino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Castle built in the 12th century on a hill just outside the village, then enlarged and fortified up to the maximum splendor under Pier Maria dei Rossi; then passed to Pallavicino, Sforza, Farnese and Mensa Vescovile, and was restored by the Alessandrini family. It dominates the valley between Parma and Baganza, is part of the circuit of the Castles of the Duchy and houses a restaurant and the Salame Museum.

  • Where Strada al Castello 1, Felino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: salamedifelino.museidelcibo.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Museo del Salame di Felino

Museums · Themed

One of the Museums of the Food of Parma, set up in the cellars and in the ancient kitchens of the castle. The five-section route starts from the historical testimonies that bind Felino to salami, passes through the kitchens, the pork products and the production in the peasant houses, and ends with the current processing according to the traditional recipe of Salame Felino IGP.

  • Where Strada al Castello 1, Felino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Centro di Felino e Casa del Rigoletto

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Between via Matteotti, via Corridoni, via Carducci and the square meet the Pharmacy Bracchi and the House of Rigoletto, formerly House of the Madonnina, built in 1875 for Henri Caumont by the foreman Ludovico Tagliavini in neo-Gothic Swiss style with terracotta decorations. Along via Venturini the wall of the Caumont Caimi estate hides in part the villa, probably seventeenth-century, made neoclassical by the Counts Caimi after 1831.

  • Where Via Matteotti, Felino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: salamedifelino.museidelcibo.it · italia.it

Pieve di Sant'Antonio a Barbiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Pieve quoted from 1230 and rebuilt in the present forms in 1775 by Don Cerdelli; the Duchess Maria Luigia visited her and gave her nineteenth-century paintings by Parmesan artists, still preserved. Barbiano is inhabited by prehistory, with a Paleolithic settlement excavated on Mount Leoni.

  • Where Barbiano, Felino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: salamedifelino.museidelcibo.it · italia.it

Anello del castello tra Barbiano e la valle del Baganza

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A path proposed by the Museum that climbs from the archpriest church to Pettenello in the woods to the castle and continues along Via Ricò towards Barbiano, with views of the Rio del Castello valley and Baganza valley, bordered by the calanchi of Maiatico who hit Garibaldi in April 1861; through Via Carroni you cross forests of chestnuts, oaks and maples, inhabited by squirrels and green peaks.

  • Where Partenza dalla chiesa arcipretale, Felino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: salamedifelino.museidelcibo.it · italia.it
