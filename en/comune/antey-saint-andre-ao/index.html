What to see in Antey-Saint-André

Sunny country in the middle of the Matterhorn valley, with the sixteenth-century arches of the Rû du pain perdu and the lake of Lod.

4 places to visit in Antey-Saint-André, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Antey-Saint-André in the guide with map and filters

Rû du pain perdu

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Arcades leaning on the mountain of a 16th century irrigation channel that led the waters of Marmore to the fields of the mid-valley.

  • Where Antey-Saint-André
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It incorporates the remains of a tower of the castle of the barons of Cly, lords of Antey until the fourteenth century.

  • Where Antey-Saint-André
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Lago di Lod

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Basin of glacial origin above the village, reachable by car, with a small play area on the shore.

  • Where Lod, Antey-Saint-André
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · balteus.lovevda.it

Cappella di Fiernaz

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
