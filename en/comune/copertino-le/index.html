What to see in Copertino

Città del Salento, linked to San Giuseppe da Copertino, the "santo dei voli" (saint of flights), born here in 1603, and to the great sixteenth-century castle where Isabella di Chiaromonte, queen of Naples, was born in the fifteenth century. The old town is a maze of narrow streets with Palazzo Pappi; around sanctuaries, the crypt of San Michele and red wine cellars.

Castello di Copertino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

One of the most impressive and best preserved defensive systems of Salento, built on an earlier angioin fortress designed by the architect Evangelista Menga, at the service of Charles V. Renaissance portal with decorations, Renaissance balconies, the chapel of San Marco frescoed by the Mannerist Gianserio Strafella and the chapel of Santa Maria Maddalena; Isabella di Chiaromonte was born there in the fifteenth century, then queen of Naples. It adheres to the "Cassio" project for accessibility, with tactile aids, subtitled videos and facilitated texts.

  • Where Piazza Castello 7, Copertino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Santuario e Stalletta di San Giuseppe da Copertino

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

It rises on the stable, near the walls, where in 1603 Giuseppe Maria Desa was born, then San Giuseppe da Copertino, famous for the levitation during the Mass and repeatedly suspected by the Holy Office of Naples and the Inquisition. In 1754, one year after the beatification, the University of Copertino had the current sanctuary built, incorporating the stable of irregular stones.

  • Where Via San Giuseppe 12, Copertino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Santuario della Grottella

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

In the middle of the sixteenth century, in a cave not far from the village, a Byzantine Madonna icon was found among the bushes; in 1577 Mgr. Cesare Bovio had the present single nave church built with three altars, and in the seventeenth century the icon was set on the central altar.

  • Where Via Padre Giovanni Donato Caputo, Copertino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Cripta di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The underground structure dates back to 1314, under the reign of Roberto d'Angiò, used not only for worship but also as a refuge from the pirate raids; it preserves two altars carved into the rock and several valuable frescoes.

  • Where Località Tenuta Li Monaci, Copertino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Centro storico e Palazzo Pappi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

A maze of narrow streets scented by wineries and oil mills, with Palazzo Pappi, a noble residence in Lecce stone in Catalan-durazzesco style.
