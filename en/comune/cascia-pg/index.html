What to see in Cascia

The town of Valnerina is a world pilgrimage destination for Saint Rita (1381-1457), with the sanctuary at the top of the village and, a few kilometers away, Roccaporena with the birthplace and the Scoglio of prayer; in the walled center the churches of St. Francis, St. Augustine and the Collegiate, the museum of Palazzo Santi and the church-museum of St. Anthony Abbot, and on the plateau of Chiavano the Roman remains of Villa San Silvestro.

Basilica di Santa Rita

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Built between 1937 and 1947 on a project by Mgr. Spirit Maria Chiapetta, modified by Giuseppe Martinenghi, next to the Augustinian monastery where the saint died in 1457; shrine since 1955. Interior in Byzantine style with Greek cross with works by Eros Pellini, furnishings by Giacomo Manzù, frescoes by Luigi Filocamo, Gisberto Ceracchini and Silvio Consadori, dome by Luigi Montanarini; in the chapel of the saint the body in a crystal urn of 1930. You can also reach it with the escalators from the center.

  • Where Parte alta di Cascia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Museo comunale di Palazzo Santi

Museums · City history

Civic museum that preserves important archaeological finds and a collection defined among the most significant in Italy; it is part of the city museum circuit with the church of Sant'Antonio Abate.

  • Where Palazzo Santi, Cascia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Chiesa-Museo di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Outside the walls, with the former convent: rebuilt between the late fourteenth and early fifteenth centuries and modified in 1707, it preserves two remarkable cycles of frescoes from the fifteenth century and is part of the city's museum circuit.

  • Where Cascia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

On the main square, with the former convent annexed; the first church was founded in 1247 and the thirteenth-century building was rebuilt in 1424.

  • Entry Free

Chiesa di Sant'Agostino e resti della rocca

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Gothic church at the top of the hill, mentioned in a papal bull of 1058 and renewed in the fourteenth century, with frescoes of Umbrian and Perugia school; next to the few remains of the fortress destroyed by Pope Leo X in 1517.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Roccaporena, luoghi di Santa Rita

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Mountain village a few kilometers from Cascia where you can visit the birthplace with a late medieval look, the marital house transformed into a chapel with a seventeenth-century painting desired by Cardinal Fausto Poli, the lazzaretto of the Madonna della Concezione, the garden of the miracle and the rose garden, and the sanctuary of 1948 designed by Oreste della Piana, with a white marble porch and three naves.

  • Where Roccaporena, Cascia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Area archeologica di Villa San Silvestro

History and architecture · Archaeology

On the plateau of Chiavano there are still structures of a temple and the Roman forum, dominated by the small church of San Silvestro; studied in the twentieth century and the subject of recent research led by Filippo Coarelli, connected to an earthquake of 99 BC. In the late antiquity the buildings were transformed into production facilities and burials, and there was found a rare village of Longobard huts.

  • Where Villa San Silvestro, Cascia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it
