What to see in Bedonia

The centre of the high Taro Valley is about 500 metres away, in a basin at the foot of Mount Pelpi, with houses with colors of Ligurian influence; around the sanctuary of the Madonna di San Marco the museum pole of the Bishop's Seminary brings together the Pinacoteca, a museum of natural history, the work of Romeo Musa and a planetarium.

Musei del Seminario vescovile

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the seminary opened in 1846 next to the sanctuary: the Parmigiani Art Gallery with about 50 paintings above all sacred of Bolognese and Emilian school (Ludovico Carracci, Bartolomeo Passerotti, the Masteletta, Luigi Crespi, Filippo Pedrini); the Homnia Opera by Romeo Musa (1882-1960), painter, xilographer and poet, with more than 800 plates and illustrations of the Promessi bridegrooms; the Museum of Natural Sciences Silvio Ferrari is a small archaeological collection, a planetarium with an astronomical observatory and a library of over 25,000 volumes. Open all year round by reservation; in July and August every day except Mondays 10-12 and 15.30-18.30.

  • Where Via Don Stefano Raffi 30, Bedonia
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Santuario della Madonna di San Marco

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Modern shrine with large dome and colonnade in Carniglia stone, built where from the seventeenth century there was the Chapel of the Well dedicated to the Madonna; the first church was wanted towards the end of 1685 by Lieutenant Marco Lezoli. The original shrine, with a single nave, preserves parts of the wooden choir and the niche of the statue of the Madonna della Belt. Since 2000 it has been a jubilee church. Open every day 9-19.

  • Entry To be checked

Centro storico e sculture in pietra

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Historic nucleus on three main axes, in whose meeting point is the parish church, of medieval origin and rebuilt in the first half of the seventeenth century; the colored houses show the influence of the nearby Liguria. Through the streets and in the park of the Peschiera are exhibited the works of the International Symposium of sculpture on stone, biennial since 2000, sculpted also in the grey sandstone of Carniglia, which was dug from the early twentieth century.

  • Where Centro di Bedonia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · parmawelcome.it

Parco della Peschiera

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Park of the village where remains the 18th century entrance arch of the ancient possession Silva and where are placed part of the sculptures of the international sculpture Symposium on stone.

Monte Pelpi e passi appenninici

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Mount Pelpi (1480 m) closes the basin of Bedonia. Along the Strada del Fungo Porcino, all curves and views over the Parma hills, you can reach the steps of Tomarlo, Bocco and Cento Croci, on the border between Emilia and Liguria.

  • Where Monte Pelpi, Bedonia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Ruderi del castello di Montarsiccio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the hamlet of Montarsiccio, at 788 meters, the remains of a castle documented from the 11th century, where in 1298 Ubertino Landi died, founder of the feudal dynasty of the Landi.

  • Where Montarsiccio, Bedonia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
