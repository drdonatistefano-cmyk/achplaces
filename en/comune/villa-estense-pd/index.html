What to see in Villa Estense

The town of Bassa Padovana, where the nineteenth-century Palazzo Valentinelli, a former town hall restored in 2011, houses the Civic Museum of the Villages Scaressi, and the sixteenth-century Palazzo San Bonifacio Ardit, with garden and statues, houses the historical archive of the family.

Museo civico dei Villaggi Scomparsi - Palazzo Valentinelli

Museums · Archaeological

On the upper floor of Palazzo Valentinelli, built at the beginning of the nineteenth century, residence of the abbot from 1874, town hall from 1897 to 1977 and reopened after the restoration in 2011, the museum documents the villages of the Lower Padua disappeared between the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries, with written documents, aerial images, terracotta patinas, ollar stone and ornaments such as pearls and pins.

Palazzo San Bonifacio Ardit

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in the second half of the sixteenth century by Ercole San Bonifacio, of the Padua branch of Sant'Agata, family linked by ancient alliance to the House of Este. It is surrounded by a large garden with statues, including Lodovico III San Bonifacio and the Emperor Augustus, and by walls with wrought iron gates; the entrance is between two pillars with Minerva and Mars. It preserves the archive of family San Bonifacio Polcenigo and Fanna, today Ardit.
