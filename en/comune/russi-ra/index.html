Villa romana di Russi

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Urban-rustic villa that touched the maximum splendor between the I and II century AD, with mosaics, service environments and an area of ecological rebalancing around.

  • Where Via Fiumazzo, Russi
  • Entry €3 whole; free of charge and first Sunday of the month
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Palazzo San Giacomo, Delizia dei Rasponi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas
