What to see in Citerna

Borgo dell'Alta Valle del Tibere on the border with Tuscany, called the terrace of the Alta Valle del Tibere, is a medieval walk along the walls, underground cisterns, the Rocca with the round tower and the church of San Francesco with the Madonna and Child attributed to Donatello.

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The fourteenth-century church on Corso Garibaldi, renovated in 1508 in a sober Renaissance style and defined as a museum church: it preserves the Madonna and Child in polychrome terracotta (1415 circa) attributed to Donatello after the restoration, his only work in Umbria, a Deposition of the Pomarancio, paintings attributed to Raffaellino del Colle, frescoes linked to the workshop of Luca Signorelli, a Robbiana terracotta, a wooden choir from 1550 and a Byzantine wooden crucifix of the late thirteenth century.

  • Where Corso Garibaldi, Citerna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Camminamento medievale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Network of covered passages to arches that runs under and along the perimeter of the medieval walls: born for the defense, today is a sheltered walk with views of the Umbrian-Tuscan landscape through the arches. It is accessed from a small arch past the Palazzetto Prosperi.

  • Where Mura medievali, Citerna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Rocca e Torre rotonda

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress of Longobard origin (VII century) rebuilt in the fourteenth century, in the highest and oldest point of the village; it was largely destroyed by the Germans in 1944. The round tower, survived the earthquake of 1917, is the symbol of the village and appears in the coat of arms; from the terrace you can see Verna and Monte Fumaiolo, where the Tiber is born.

  • Where Via della Rocca, Citerna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Piazza Scipione Scipioni e Torre civica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Central square dedicated to a hero of the First World War born in Citerna, with a sixteenth-century well and the Civic Tower, which preserves a rare mechanical clock of the sixteenth century with wooden gears; the terrace looks on the basin of the Tiber towards the Apennines. The center, built between the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries, has two doors: Roman gate to the south and Porta Fiorentina to the north.

  • Where Piazza Scipione Scipioni, Citerna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Overlooking the square, built around 1680, it retains a Crucifixion of 1570 by Nicolò Circignani called the Pomarancio, a Madonna with Child (XVI-XVII century) attributed to Giovanni della Robbia and a bell dated 1269.

Cisterne e Sale degli Ammassi

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Under the former monastery next to St. Francis, today seat of the municipality with an internal cloister, there are underground rooms of the XV-XVI century: two large monumental cisterns (the main contains about 450 cubic meters of water) and the Sala degli Ammassi. The village has at least seven cisterns, vital during the sieges; just outside the walls, on the west side, there is also a medieval well that is over 20 meters deep.

  • Where Corso Garibaldi, Citerna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Teatro Bontempelli

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Born in the 18th century as a private theatre attached to a country house of the Vitelli family, it passed to the Municipality in the 19th century; after the damage of the earthquake of 1917 it was restored with a gallery in Art Nouveau style. Re-opened in 1991, it has 99 seats. For visits the source indicates the Pro Loco (tel. 075 8592237).

  • Where Via della Rocca, Citerna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Colle Plinio

History and architecture · Archaeology
