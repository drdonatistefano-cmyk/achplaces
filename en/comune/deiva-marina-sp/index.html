What to see in Deiva Marina

Borgo della Riviera di Levante among the most beautiful villages in Italy, with its ancient nucleus around the church of Sant'Antonio Abate, two towers against pirates, the long beach between the cliffs and the inland paths.

5 places to visit in Deiva Marina, in the province of La Spezia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di Sant'Antonio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Baroque church in the oldest nucleus, with the mosaic church of Ligurian pebbles and a rose of the winds.

Torri di Deiva

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The medieval square tower in front of Sant'Antonio Abate and the circular tower covered with ivy towards the sea, garrisons against pirate raids.

  • Entry Free

Santuario dell'Assunta

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

The first rural church in the area, dating back to 1002; contains a marble epigraph of the fifth and sixth centuries with the letter of Jesus Christ sent from heaven.
