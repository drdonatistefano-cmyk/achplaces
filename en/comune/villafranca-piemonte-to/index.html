What to see in Villafranca Piemonte

Country on the Po with eight centuries of history: the Mission chapel with frescoes of the fifteenth century, the parish church of Santo Stefano, the sanctuary and the remains of the castle of Cantogno and the Path of the Ochette along the river.

7 places to visit in Villafranca Piemonte, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cappella e Bosco naturalistico di Missione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Country chapel near Borgo Soave, next to a river of resurgence, of ancient foundation. Inside frescoes of about 1430, largely attributed to Dux Aimo (Aimone Duce), commissioned by Giulio de Giuli; a paper of the apse vault dates back to 1474. Above the portal an Annunciation and outside a St. Christopher of the first half of the sixteenth century. Around the Naturalistic Wood of Mission, with picnic tables.

  • Where Località Missione, Borgo Soave, Villafranca Piemonte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.villafrancapiemonte.to.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It was first mentioned in 1037, and was entrusted to the Benedictines of Cavour Abbey until 1315 and to the Augustinians from 1529 to 1802. On the façade there is an ancient sculpture of the Lamb with the Cross, perhaps from the 11th century.

  • Where Villafranca Piemonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.villafrancapiemonte.to.it · italia.it

Chiesa della Beata Vergine delle Grazie («del Monastero»)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded in 1702 in the place where the Augustinian nuns lived since 1517; in the works of 1755 the façade was designed by Francesco Valeriano Dellala of Beinasco and the main altar by Gioachino Felice Buttrini. Inside stuccoes and ten stories of the life of Mary frescoed by Carrera. Owned by the municipality since 1978.

  • Where Villafranca Piemonte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.villafrancapiemonte.to.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of San Giovanni, attested around 1200, it preserves a cycle of frescoes from 1540-1541 attributed to Jacobino Longo (the date 1541 is on the fresco of Sant'Antonio Abate); neoclassical façade and bell tower of 1928.

  • Where Frazione San Giovanni, Villafranca Piemonte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.villafrancapiemonte.to.it · italia.it

Santuario della Madonna del Buon Rimedio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Ancient parish church of the hamlet of Cantogno, today a sanctuary: it preserves a "Pietà" , fresco of the second half of the fifteenth century partially recovered. The festival is celebrated on the second Sunday of October.

  • Where Frazione Cantogno, Villafranca Piemonte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.villafrancapiemonte.to.it · italia.it

Torre e resti del castello di Cantogno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

One of the oldest artifacts of Villafranca, today reduced to remains: you can see the tower, the arch of the ancient drawbridge and frescoes of the 13th century with the Crucifix between the Saints Peter and Paul.

  • Where Frazione Cantogno, Villafranca Piemonte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.villafrancapiemonte.to.it · italia.it

Sentiero delle Ochette

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Easy ring of about 14 km between Villafranca, Moretta and Cardè along the two banks of the Po, between poplars and fields, with sculptures along the way, a river ferry in wood rebuilt (according to summer) and the bridge of Cardè of 1914, first concrete bridge on the Po. Realized in 2017 by the Association Friends of the Po; the "Route of the Ali" tells children more than 100 species of birds. About an hour by bike or 3-4 hours on foot; parking and infopoint at the bridge over the Po.

  • Where Ponte sul Po (partenza), Villafranca Piemonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
