What to see in Sant'Ambrogio di Torino

The medieval village along the Via Francigena at the foot of Mount Pirchiriano, on whose summit stands the Sacred of San Michele, symbol of Piedmont.

4 places to visit in Sant'Ambrogio di Torino, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Sant'Ambrogio di Torino in the guide with map and filters

Sacra di San Michele

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

The Benedictine abbey of San Michele della Chiusa on Mount Pirchiriano, 962 meters, a monument symbol of Piedmont since 1994: the Scalone dei Morti goes up to the Zodiac Gate, with frames carved with zodiac signs and constellations.

  • Where Via alla Sacra, Sant'Ambrogio di Torino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it · cr.piemonte.it

Borgo medievale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village preserves intact the walls and the watchtowers; in the alleys the Palazzo del Blessed Umberto, from 1347 ancient hospital of the pilgrims of the Via Francigena.

  • Where Sant'Ambrogio di Torino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · italia.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni Vincenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish of origin of the 11th century, dependent on the Sacred, rebuilt in the 18th century on a project by Bernardo Vittone.

  • Where Piazza San Giovanni Vincenzo 2, Sant'Ambrogio di Torino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: vallesusa-tesori.it · turismotorino.org · italia.it

Castello abbaziale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The castle of the Abbots of San Michele above the village, reported by Turismo Torino.
