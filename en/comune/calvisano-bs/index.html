What to see in Calvisano

Municipality of the lower Brescia 33 km from Brescia, rich in water and springs, known for the Calvisius caviar produced since 1977 by Agroittica Lombarda; it preserves palaces, villas and churches like Palazzo Lechi with its towers and the church of Santa Maria della Rosa in Malpaga.

Castello Lechi (Palazzo Lechi)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The construction was started by the Polini brothers in 1723 with the design of a castle-shaped residence with four corner towers; the building was not completed and passed to the Counts Lechi. The complex in red brick is surrounded by greenery and closed by a long wall of belt with turrets. It is one of the places reported in the FAI census "I Places of the Heart" (2010-2022).

  • Where Via Lechi 3, Calvisano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria della Rosa a Malpaga

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the village Malpaga, an autonomous rural town until 1808, the church existed already in the fourteenth century, was restored in 1460 and rebuilt in Gothic-Lombard style between 1925 and 1927. It preserves a canvas from the end of the sixteenth century with the Birth of Mary, a wooden statue of the Madonna and Child of 1720 and thirteen bricks with niche of the fourteenth century; the chapel of the Rosary is of 1720.

  • Where Frazione Malpaga, Calvisano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.calvisano.bs.it · fondoambiente.it · in-lombardia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Mezzane

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The nucleus of Mezzane was born around the chapel of Santa Maria de Mezanis, cited in a bull of Pope Urban III of 1186. The parish church dates back to the second half of the sixteenth century, with Baroque façade of 1730-1750 and bell tower of 1829; inside a altarpiece attributed to Andrea del Sarto, a Via Crucis and the bronzes of the ambone and the baptistery of the Brescia artist Oscar di Prata.

  • Where Frazione Mezzane, Calvisano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.calvisano.bs.it · in-lombardia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Viadana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Oratorio campestre della parishe di San Silvestro until its elevation to the parish on April 3, 1959, preserves a wooden statue of the Madonna and Child of the XV century, the main altar of 1711 and a marble parapet of 1736.

  • Where Frazione Viadana, Calvisano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Calvisius Caviar Experience

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Paid entry

Guided tour by reservation at the Calvisius plant in Agroittica Lombarda, a caviar producer with a genetic reserve of sturgeons: the route illustrates the life cycles of sturgeons, the fountains and an educational observatory; in the pools flows resurgence water that flows into the property. Six packages combine the visit to the tasting in restaurants in the area. Visits from Monday to Friday at 11 or 5.30 p.m., on Saturday at 11 am only for large groups.

  • Where Via Kennedy, 25012 Calvisano
  • Entry Packages from €55
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · fondoambiente.it
