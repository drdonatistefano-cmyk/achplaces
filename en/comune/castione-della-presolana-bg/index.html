What to see in Castione della Presolana

Holiday resort of the upper Seriana Valley at the foot of the Presolana (2.521 m), the "quegina delle Orobie" (Golden Flag) since 2022: between Castione, Bratto and Dorga Liberty villas from the early twentieth century, churches with furnishings of the Fantoni di Rovetta, the sanctuary of Lantana, the rural village of Rusio with the ancient San Péder, the Valle dei Mulini and the view of the Salto degli Spos.

12 places to visit in Castione della Presolana, in the province of Bergamo (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Massiccio della Presolana

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

It is 2.521 metres high in the Prealpi Orobie, called Regina, for the limestone walls compared to the Dolomites; the hamlet of Bratto-Dorga is the starting point for the climb. The territory, in the Parco delle Orobie Bergamasche, offers hiking, mountain biking, climbing and in winter skiing, cross-country skiing and snowshoes.

  • Where Bratto-Dorga, Castione della Presolana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: visitbergamo.net · touringclub.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Alessandro Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt in 1752-56 on a church mentioned in the 13th century, it preserves the main altar of the workshop of the Fantoni di Rovetta (1767), organ case and cantoria of Grazioso Fantoni (1684), a pulpit of the Fantonian supported by three telamons, canvases by Domenico Carpinoni and Antonio Cifrondi and a altarpiece by Pietro Vecchia; the local stone bell tower was finished in 1786 on a design by Camillo Zigliani.

  • Where Via San Bernardo, Castione della Presolana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: comune.castione.bg.it · italia.it · visitbergamo.net

Santuario della Madonna delle Grazie di Lantana

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

It rises on the site of a medieval oratory dedicated to St. Silvestro Pope (whose stone image is above the door), rebuilt and rebuilt several times in 1909; it houses frescoes from the 15th century on canvas, including the Madonna on the throne with Child, Christ Crucified and St. Anthony Abbot, the venerated image of the Virgin with the Blessed Child and a rich collection of ex-vote. First weekend of July.

  • Where Via Lantana, Dorga, Castione della Presolana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: comune.castione.bg.it · visitbergamo.net

Borgo di Rusio e chiesa di San Giacomo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Frazione about 2 km from the capital, rare example of rural architecture and starting point of many itineraries. At the centre is the church of San Giacomo, which preserves the original structure and a golden and polychrome wooden altar of the Fantoni workshop (XVII century), according to tradition the ancient high altar of the parish of Castione.

  • Where Via Rusio, Castione della Presolana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: touringclub.it · comune.castione.bg.it · visitbergamo.net

Chiesa di San Péder (San Pietro)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Above Rusio along the path CAI 317, is traditionally the oldest church in the territory and the first parish; rectangular plan and rich in frescoes, carries the inscription "Albinonus pingere fecit p(er) his vow on the arch. 1580. Restored in 1974.

  • Where Sentiero CAI 317 sopra Rusio, Castione della Presolana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: comune.castione.bg.it · visitbergamo.net

Antica parrocchiale della SS. Trinità di Dorga

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It preserves two wooden altars with seventeenth-century anchovies by Fantoni di Rovetta, the main altar in inlaid marble (1743) and in sacristy two fragments of fresco of the fifteenth century with St. Anthony Abbot and St. Bernardino.

open to the public only on the occasion of cultural events or religious events

  • Where Via Andrea Fantoni, Dorga, Castione della Presolana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: comune.castione.bg.it · visitbergamo.net

Chiesa parrocchiale della Natività di Maria a Bratto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1924-36 on a project by Camillo Galizzi instead of an eighteenth-century parish church, of which the lateral portal dates back to 1752; Fantonian angels at the high altar, the Crucifixion frescoed in the apse by Gian Battista Galizzi (1937) and a table "Byzantine" of the Virgin and Child.

  • Where Via Confalonieri, Bratto, Castione della Presolana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: comune.castione.bg.it · visitbergamo.net

Salto degli Sposi

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Belvedere on the valley reachable with a walk, linked to the story of the late nineteenth century Polish musician Maximilian Prihoda and his painter wife, who here threw themselves together into the void.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
