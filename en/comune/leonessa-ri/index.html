What to see in Leonessa

Mountain village with almond trees at the foot of the Terminillo, with doors and towers of Angevin, the Margaritian Fountain in Piazza VII Aprile, the churches of San Francesco and San Pietro and the Sanctuary of San Giuseppe; two thirds of the territory are woods and pastures of high altitude.

11 places to visit in Leonessa, in the province of Rieti (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa e chiostro di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque-Gothic facade in local red stone; inside a Renaissance crib in polychrome terracotta, a wooden Crucifix, paintings of the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries and in the crypt frescoes with Hell, Paradise, Madonna dell'Ulivo and biblical scenes. Church and cloister house the demo-anthropological museum.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Piazza VII Aprile e Fontana Margaritiana

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

A sixteenth-century fountain linked to Margherita d'Austria, also called Farnesiana, in the main square; around, well-preserved historical palaces like the Mongalli Palaces (one is the seat of the municipality, with a local stone portal), Palazzo Ettore, the largest in the country, and Palazzo Cherubini, where in 1899 the composer Bixio Cherubini was born.

  • Where Piazza VII Aprile, Leonessa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Porta Spoletina e Porta Aquilana

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The Porta Spoletina is a 14th-century crenellated tower in pink stone overlooking Corso San Giuseppe Leonessano; the Porta Aquilana, to the north, is a remnant of the defensive system wanted by Carlo I d'Angiò.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Torre Angioina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

A polygonal tower built by Carlo d'Angiò around 1278 on Mount Tilia, over 1,200 m, to control access to the plain of Leonessa: it is the last trace of the recently established castle of Ripa di Corno. You can reach it with a path in the woods for experienced hikers.

  • Where Via della Rocca, Leonessa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com
