What to see in Zocca

The holiday resort of the Modena Apennines at 758 meters, a market place from 1465 at the behest of Borso d'Este, Zocca tells the civilization of the chestnut in the museum of the ancient hospitable of San Giacomo; around, the stone villages of Montalbano, Montecorone and Monteombraro, the ruins of the tower of Rosola and the paths of the Regional Park of the Sassi of Roccamalatina.

9 places to visit in Zocca, in the province of Modena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo del Castagno e del Borlengo

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 2000 in the ancient hospitable of San Giacomo, it tells in three rooms the role of chestnut in the life of the mountain: tools for the care of chestnut trees and the processing, characteristics and diffusion of the species, diseases and grafts, the reconstruction of a chestnut grove and a section on chestnut in the Second World War with relics and documents. A flat path with 13 informative stages crosses the chestnut groves of Monte San Giacomo up to the woods of the Birch; in front, the Museum-Laboratory of Borlengo with a room to learn how to do it. Audioguides available.

The MiC card indicates closed every day and no source reports the times: contact the museum

Borgo di Montalbano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small fortified village at 616 meters, with narrow alleys and tower houses, dominated by the Mount of Riva. The 18th-century parish church, with a 17th-century bell tower, contains two marble statues from the fourteenth century and paliotti made of Carpigiana stone.

  • Where Montalbano, Zocca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Parco regionale dei Sassi di Roccamalatina

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 2,300 hectares between Guiglia, Marano sul Panaro and Zocca, with more than 100 km of trails in 12 itineraries for hiking, horse riding or mountain biking; there are the Sasso di Sant'Andrea and the Bosco delle Tane, chestnut groves with sandstone. The protected area recognised by the European Union is home to more than 80 species of birds, including the pilgrim hawk, and more than 40 mammals.

  • Where Zocca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
