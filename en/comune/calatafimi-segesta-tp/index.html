What to see in Calatafimi-Segesta

Municipality of the hinterland of Trapani which includes the Archaeological Park of Segesta, the main town of the Elimi, with the Doric temple of the 5th century BC and the Hellenistic theatre on Mount Barbaro; in the village remain the ruins of the Castle Eufemio and, on the hill of the battle Garibaldi of 5 May 1860, the Shrine of Pianto Romano.

Parco archeologico di Segesta

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

A site stratified with helimal, Hellenistic-Roman remains of Muslim, Norman-Svevian and medieval era: Segesta, the main city of the Elimi and rival of Selinunte, was destroyed by Agatocle of Syracuse in 307 BC and rebuilt as a Roman city. Outside the walls stands the Doric temple of the fifth century BC, with 36 columns and remarkably intact; on the top of Mount Barbaro the Hellenistic theater (mid III century BC), with the cavea partly dug into the rock, which in summer hosts classical shows, music and poetry, even at dawn. You can also visit the two acropolis, the Vanella Cave linked to the shrine of Contrada Mango, the quarries of the temples and the remains of a Hellenistic necropolis. Integrated ticket available online or at the Park ticket office.

  • Where Contrada Barbaro, SR 22, 91013 Calatafimi-Segesta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sacrario di Pianto Romano

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

On the top of the hill the theatre of the battle between the troops of Garibaldi and the Bourbon army, reachable along the provincial road: an obelisk 30 meters high, in neoclassical style with Greek elements, guards the bones of the fallen of the battle of Calatafimi of 5 May 1860. There is a ticket combined with the Archaeological Park of Segesta.

  • Where Contrada Pianto Romano, Calatafimi-Segesta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Castello Eufemio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Also known as the castle of Phimes, in the centre of Calatafimi: the ruins of the two towers remain at the north and south ends of the main façade. He was outposted by Frederick II's troops against the Muslims and then castle of the feudal men of Calatafimi.

  • Where Centro storico, Calatafimi-Segesta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
