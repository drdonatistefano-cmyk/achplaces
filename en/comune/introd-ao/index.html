What to see in Introd

Country at the entrance of the valleys of Rhêmes and Valsavarenche, with the castle of Sarriod, the rural house Maison Bruil and the bridge over the horrid, where John Paul II spent the summers.

5 places to visit in Introd, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Castello di Introd

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Wanted in 1260 by Pierre de Sarriod, lord of the Introd, surrounded by a large wooded park and open in summer; near the granaries complex.

  • Where Plan d'Introd
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it

Maison Bruil

Museums · Themed

Among the most important examples of rural architecture in Gran Paradiso, today Maison de l'alimentation with an exhibition on traditional products and the evolution of conservation techniques.

  • Where Villes Dessus, Introd
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it

Ponte di Introd

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built during World War I on a deep abyss of more than 80 meters.

  • Where Introd
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Parc Animalier d'Introd

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Faunistic park where you can observe and learn about the most typical animals of the Valdostano alpine environment and the local flora.

  • Where Introd
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it · lovevda.it

Maison Musée Jean-Paul II

Museums · House museums

In the locality Les Combes, in honor of the summer stays of Pope John Paul II.

  • Where Les Combes, Introd
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it
