What to see in Sant'Antioco

The ancient Phoenician Sulky and Punic on the island connected by an isthmus: the Tophet, the Punic necropolis transformed into a hypogeum village, the catacombs under the basilica of the saint and the museum that tells one of the major centers of ancient Sardinia.

7 places to visit in Sant'Antioco, in the province of Sud Sardegna (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Basilica e catacombe di Sant'Antioco

Places of worship · Basilicas · Paid entry

Under the parish basilica dedicated to Sant'Antioco Sulcitano, punish tombs united between the IV and VII century AD form the catacombs, with the room of the sarcophagus of the martyr, Christian paintings and inscriptions and the small catacomb of Santa Rosa.

  • Where Sant'Antioco
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · italia.it

Tophet

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Outdoor shrine of ancient Sulky where the ashes of children born dead or dead young were laid in urns: urns, punic votive stele, fences and the Carthaginian belt, with the remains of a Roman cistern.

  • Where Sant'Antioco
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · italia.it

Villaggio ipogeo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Tombs of the Punic necropolis re-used for a long time as homes by the poorest families.

  • Where Via Necropoli 18, Sant'Antioco
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo archeologico Ferruccio Barreca (MAB)

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Inaugurated at the end of 2005 near the Tophet, in four rooms it tells the civilizations of the island and the findings of the necropolis and the Tophet of Sulky, with the model of the ancient port.

  • Where Sant'Antioco
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: virtualarchaeology.sardegnacultura.it · italia.it

Istmo e ponte romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The almost 3 km road that connects the island to Sardinia, started by the Carthaginians and completed by the Romans; just before the modern bridge you can see the ruins of the Roman bridge with several arches.

  • Where Sant'Antioco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
