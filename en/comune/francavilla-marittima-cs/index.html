What to see in Francavilla Marittima

Country of Sibaritide linked to the ancient Lagaria: the sanctuary of Athena on the Timpone della Motta, the necropolis of Macchiabate with almost two hundred mounds, the archaeological civic museum of Palazzo De Santis and the museum of rural art.

6 places to visit in Francavilla Marittima, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parco archeologico Timpone della Motta e Antiquarium

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the acropolis of ancient Lagaria, linked by Strabone to the legend of Epeo, stood a sanctuary of Athena with construction phases around 700 BC and in the middle of the VII century BC; the cult of Athena was joined by that of Pan and the Nymphs, then the sanctuary was probably destroyed by the Brettii and in the tenth century there was built a Byzantine chapel. The site is still under investigation and has no defined visit routes.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Necropoli di Macchiabate

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Almost two hundred tombs of the Iron Age and of the VII-VI century BC under mounds of circular or elliptic stones, excavated only partially by Paola Zancani Montuoro since 1963: ceramic and bronze sets, weapons for men of rank and imported objects, including a Phoenician cup and Corinthian ceramics. Free access, no defined routes.

  • Where Macchiabate, Francavilla Marittima
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.francavillamarittima.cs.it

Sources: comune.francavillamarittima.cs.it

Museo civico archeologico – Palazzo De Santis

Museums · Archaeological

In the historic centre, in the birthplace of Tanino De Santis, pioneer of local archaeological research: ceramics, instruments and other finds of the excavations of the Timpone della Motta and Macchiabate.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Museo dell'arte contadina

Museums · Themed

Ten sections on the economy of the territory: the peasant house with the kitchen, the tools of the shoemaker, the blacksmith and the shepherd, the domestic utensils and the relationship between man and environment.

Chiesa Madre dell'Annunciazione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The matrix church, born in the eighteenth century as a chapel of the Rosary and remodelled several times: altar of Our Lady of the Rosary, processional statues and bronze portals with saints, scenes of the life of Christ and Marian scenes.

Chiesa della Madonna del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
