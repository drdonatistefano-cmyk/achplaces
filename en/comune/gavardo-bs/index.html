What to see in Gavardo

Downtown of the lower Sand Valley crossed by the river Churches and the Canal Naviglio, with districts arranged in a ring between the hills; to see the Civic Archaeological Museum of the Sand Valley in the historic center, the seventeenth-century convent of the Orsoline, the "isolo" on the river and the medieval hamlet of Sopraponte.

Museo civico archeologico della Valle Sabbia

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Founded in 1956 by Gruppo Grotte Gavardo and then sold to the Municipality, it occupies a fifteenth-century building in the historic center and tells about the Valley Sand and the western Garda area from the Middle Paleolithic to the post-Renaissance age, with fossils and a section of paleontology. Among the finds are the skeleton of a bear of the caves of the Hollow of the Friar, materials of the pile of the Lucon of Polpenazze (part of a UNESCO site) and the Roman necropolis of the Lugon of Salò. The exhibition itinerary is being reviewed.

  • Where Piazzetta San Bernardino 5, Gavardo
  • Entry €4, reduced €2 (4-17 years and over 65), Sunday €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museoarcheologicogavardo.it

Sources: museoarcheologicogavardo.it · visitbrescia.it · comune.gavardo.bs.it · in-lombardia.it

Convento delle Orsoline e chiesa di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Monastery built in 1600 that houses a valuable organ; inside the monastic structure there is the church of Santa Maria.

  • Where Gavardo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

L'Isolo sul fiume Chiese

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Isolotto formed by the river Churches and the Canal Naviglio, which cross the country; it is frequented mainly in summer for the coolest temperature of the water.

  • Where Gavardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Sopraponte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Ancient hamlet of Gavardo at the confluence of the stream Vrenda in the Churches, lying on a rather steep slope: along the road towards Quarena you will find houses of medieval origin, and on the side you will find the picturesque nucleus of houses of Casalicolo.

  • Where Sopraponte, Gavardo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.gavardo.bs.it · in-lombardia.it
