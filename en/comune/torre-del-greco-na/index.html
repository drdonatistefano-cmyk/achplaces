What to see in Torre del Greco

The town of coral, the most populous center of the Vesuvius National Park: the Museum of Coral Liverino, the Roman villa of Villa Sora and, on the slopes, the Villa delle Ginestre where Leopardi stayed.

5 places to visit in Torre del Greco, in the province of Naples (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo del Corallo – Collezione Liverino

Museums · Themed

Digged about six meters below ground, it collects over a thousand sculptures and jewels in coral and hard stones from the fifteenth century to today, from the East to Italy; among the pieces a nineteenth-century parure for Paolina Bonaparte and a large cameo on shell engraved in ten years. The collection was born in 1934.

  • Where Via Montedoro 61, Torre del Greco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: admin-istituzionale.touringclub.it · italia.it

Villa Sora

History and architecture · Archaeology

Maritime villa of the middle of the first century BC, with a seafront of about 150 meters and a large apse hall; it was in restoration at the time of the eruption of 79 AD and preserves the traces of the yard interrupted.

Visits only on Sunday mornings by reservation

  • Where Torre del Greco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint
