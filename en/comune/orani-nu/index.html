What to see in Orani

Barbagia Country 500 meters away, in a green basin dominated by the cone of Mount Gonare, the ancient capital of the curatorial of Dore and then marquisato: home of Costantino Nivola and Mario Delitala, offers the Museo Nivola on the hill of Su Cantaru, the Delitala Collection in the Franciscan convent, the neoclassical parish church of Sant'Andrea, the church of Itria with the graffiti of Nivola, the shrine of Gonare and the Nuraghe Nurdole.

Museo Nivola

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Founded in 1995 and run by the Nivola Foundation, it is located in the former public wash-house on the hill of Su Cantaru, overlooking the village, with pavilions designed by Peter Chermayeff and Umberto Floris and a park that contains the historical source. The permanent collection contains hundreds of sculptures, paintings and graphics by Costantino Nivola (1911-1988), a figure of modernism of the twentieth century; it also houses temporary exhibitions and artist residences on art, architecture, landscape and public art. The MiC indicates opening 10:30-19:30, closed on Wednesdays.

  • Where Via Gonare, Orani
  • Entry €7, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website museonivola.it

Sources: museonivola.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Collezione Delitala

Museums · Modern art

In the Franciscan convent, collection dedicated to the painter and engraver of Oran Mario Delitala (1887-1990): xylographies and some paintings, including portraits, sacred works and scenes of barbaric life.

  • Where Convento francescano, Orani
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Andrea Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Among the last neoclassical churches of Sardinia, of Palladian orientation, according to project of the Nurese Giacomo Galfrè (in charge in 1867): pronao tetrastyle with Tuscanic columns on top of a monumental staircase, inside a Greek cross with dome and lantern. It preserves the altarpiece of Mario Delitala with the Gloria of Sant'Andrea, a seventeenth-century pulpit in inlaid marble and a 16th-century retablo with the Madonna enthroned with the Child.

  • Where Centro, Orani
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Campanile e resti della vecchia Sant'Andrea (Su Campusantu Vezzu)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

At the southern edge of the village remain the restored bell tower, in six-order volcanic stone cones with cat spire in Gothic-Catalan style, and part of the trachyte façade with the portal, whose arch key bears the cross of St. Andrew: it was the sixteenth-century parish church, abandoned in the early nineteenth century.

  • Where Margine sud, Orani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Nostra Signora d'Itria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of late-Gothic forms whose façade is decorated by the twentieth-century graffiti by Costantino Nivola. In the village also the seventeenth-century St. John the Baptist, with a wooden pulpit carved with a Franciscan symbol, and the church of the Rosary, with frescoes.

  • Where Orani
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Santuario di Nostra Signora di Gonare e Monte Gonare

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the top of Mount Gonare (1,083 m), massif of steep walls and woods destination of excursions, the sanctuary is indicated as the highest church in Sardinia and is shared with Sarule, with which the feasts of March 25, late May and early September alternate; it appears in the novel .The streets of evil of Grazia Deledda.

  • Where Monte Gonare, Orani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Nuraghe Nurdole

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The most imposing complex among the thirty Nuragic sites of the territory: a central tower enclosed by a bastion with four towers, with a sacred source nearby. In the territory also the necropolis at domus de Janas of Sas Fossas and Badde Roma, two dolmens, six menhirs and the tombs of Giants of Istelenneru.

  • Where Località Nurdole, Orani
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
