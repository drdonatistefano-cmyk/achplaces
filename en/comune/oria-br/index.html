What to see in Oria

Ancient capital of the Messapia and Orange Flag of Touring: the castle federiciano with triangular plan, the baroque cathedral with the Crypt of the Mummies and the walled center with the Jewish Gate, where in the ninth century a Jewish community was established.

7 places to visit in Oria, in the province of Brindisi (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Oria in the guide with map and filters

Castello di Oria

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Linked to Frederick II, the fruit of transformations of many centuries: triangular plant, three towers, well-preserved walls and a large arms square.

  • Where Oria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Cattedrale e Cripta delle Mummie

Places of worship · Cathedrals

The cathedral has a Baroque appearance today; below is the Crypt of the Mummies with the confreres of Death, dating back to 1485.

  • Where Centro storico, Oria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Centro storico, Porta degli Ebrei e Sedile

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Center enclosed by the walls, with medieval squares and alleys, the Palazzo del Sedile and two doors: Porta Manfredi and Porta Taranto, called Porta degli Hebrei, which gives access to the Jewish community district established in the IX century. In August the historical procession of Frederick II and the Tournament of the Rioni.

  • Where Centro storico, Oria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

MAM – Museo Archeologico e dei Messapi «Demetra e Persefone»

Museums · Archaeological

In the Palazzo Martini Carissimo, it presents archaeological finds of the Messapic territory.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Museo archeologico "Francesco Milizia"

Museums · Archaeological

Civic museum in the basement of the Palazzo di Città, at the Civic Library De Pace Lombardi, entitled to the architect Francesco Milizia: funeral supplies of the territory from the IV to the II century BC.

  • Where Via Epitaffio, Palazzo Comunale, Oria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Santuario di San Cosimo alla Macchia e Museo etnografico regionale pugliese

Museums · Themed

Sanctuary of Saints Cosimo and Damiano about 5 km from Oria; next door, the ethnographic museum opened in 2007, with the collection collected in about 40 years by Gerardo Andriulo: over 5000 pieces in three sections, peasant world, social life and missing trades.

  • Where Contrada San Cosimo, Oria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
