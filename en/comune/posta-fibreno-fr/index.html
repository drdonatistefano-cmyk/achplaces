What to see in Posta Fibreno

Country of the Valley of Comino overlooking the Karst lake of Posta Fibreno, Regional Nature Reserve fed by numerous springs, with the floating island called "Rita" cited by Pliny the Old; in the historic center the Naturalistic Ethnographic Museum, on the shore of the water mill of 1810.

Lago e Riserva Naturale di Posta Fibreno

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Karst lake at almost 300 meters above sea level, on the border with Abruzzo and Molise, fed by a front of springs about 3 km long that descend from the mountains of the nearby National Park of Abruzzo, Lazio and Molise: the water remains at about 10-11 °C all year round. The lake (about 0.28 km2, maximum depth 15 m at the Le Codigliane spring) is surrounded by reeds where birds nest; the reserve, in the system of the regional parks of Lazio, extends for about 400 hectares and hosts the macrostigma trout, which in peninsula Italy lives only here and in the river Ninfa. Six nature trails on foot; at the bottom of the lake since 1977 the Steel Crucifix, brought back to the surface by divers at the beginning of August.

Isola galleggiante «la Rota» e Sentiero Puzzillo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Rota is a natural islet of peat, roots and marsh herbs that wind and currents move on the lake, already described by Pliny the Elder in Naturalis Historia. It is observed from the watchhouse at the end of the Nature Path •Puzzillo •Puzzillo: 0.6 km flat (30 minutes), almost all on a wooden footbridge in the reed, which can also be walked by strollers; departure signposted in Sorgentina, via Lago Fibreno, with parking in via San Venditto near the old mill.

Mulino ad acqua della Sorgentina

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Built in 1810 in the locality of Sorgentina, it was expanded between 1905 and 1908 with a small hydroelectric power plant that, immediately after the Second World War, flooded for a few months the public lighting of the neighboring municipalities. Restored in the early 2000s, with original turbines and generators back in operation, it is the center of the Reserve and has didactic use; next to a picnic area among olive trees, dry walls and terraces.
