What to see in Lanuvio

Medieval village of the Roman Castles enclosed by walls of the fifth century BC, the ancient Lanuvium of the Sanctuary of Juno Sospita, whose finds are in the Civic Museum Lanuvino; in the center the Palazzo Colonna where Marcantonio Colonna was born, the Fountain of the Scogli by Carlo Fontana and the Rocca with the wine shop of Colli Lanuvini.

Museo civico Lanuvino

Museums · Archaeological

In the historic centre, with a pre-Roman section and a Roman section on the ancient Lanuvium: a Augustan fresco with a Dionysian theme, marble fragments of the group of Licinio Murena (first century BC), a parapet with winged grifo of the Antonian age from the theatre, archaic and Hellenistic architectural plates and votive from the Sanctuary of Juno Sospita and the former anatomical vows of the Pantanacci cave, linked to the cult of the serpent of the goddess. The remains of the ancient balineum are incorporated into the exhibition spaces; the epigraphic section includes an inscription of the third century BC by Lucio Ocra on the bathroom works.

  • Where Piazza della Maddalena 16, Lanuvio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Santuario di Giunone Sospita

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the park next to Villa Sforza Cesarini, co-eval of the walls, it was dedicated to the Juno Sospita cited by Cicero. The excavations began in 1884-1892 with Lord Savile Lumley, who found there the Donary of Licinius Murena, marble group of knights and warriors of the first century BC offered by the consul after the victory over Mithridates and brought to England; the subsequent finds are in the civic museum.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Mura e Tempio di Ercole

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

The local stone walls, built from the 5th century BC, enclose the small medieval village and are still visible in some stretches; along the walls are the foundations of the Temple of Hercules of the II century BC.

  • Where Centro storico, Lanuvio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Palazzo Colonna

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Palace remained unfinished in the center of the village, where Marcantonio Colonna was born, among the architects of the victory of Lepanto (1571); on the main façade there is a Roman sarcophagus in marble of the III-IV century A.D. In his Secrets (The Cantinone) the excavation exhibition on the excavations of Lord Savile Lumley was held.

  • Where Centro storico, Lanuvio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Rocca ed Enoteca dei Colli Lanuvini

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

The 14th-century Fortress houses the Enoteca del Consorzio dei Vini Colli Lanuvini, the best place to taste the wines of the area, a small museum of rural traditions and the watchtower, symbol of the village, with panoramic views.

  • Where Rocca, Lanuvio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Fontana degli Scogli e Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The Fontana degli Scogli (1675) is the work of Carlo Fontana, a pupil of Gian Lorenzo Bernini; the church of Santa Maria Maggiore, of Byzantine origin, was rebuilt in Baroque style.

  • Where Centro storico, Lanuvio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint
