What to see in Aldino

The village of the Lower Atesina on the Porphido plateau, with Redagno, where the gorge of the Bletterbach, the Grand Canyon of South Tyrol opens, and the smallest of the nine UNESCO World Heritage Sites systems since 2009; in the village the museum of sacred Baroque art and along the stream the mills of the open-air museum.

Gola del Bletterbach (GEOPARC)

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

The smallest system of the UNESCO Dolomites (271 hectares, since 2009): a deep gorge up to 400 meters long and about 8 km long, with at least four waterfalls, which bares the rocks from the late Permian to the Anisic, with traces of the greatest mass extinction of the Earth's history and fossils of footprints of saurians, shells, plants and fishes of about 260 million years ago. The Municipality established the GEOPARC in 2005; from the Visitor Centre of Aldino start the Butterloch geopaths (about 4.6 km, 2h30, to the waterfall) and Gorz (about 6.3 km, 3h20), and daily guided tours in season; compulsory helmet, rentable at the center.

  • Where Centro visitatori, Lerch 40, Aldino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.bletterbach.info

Sources: bletterbach.info · dolomitiunesco.info · castelfeder.info · suedtirol.info

Museo GEOlogico di Redagno

Museums · Science and university · UNESCO World Heritage

In the House Peter Rosegger in Redagno di Sopra, at the other end of the GEOPARC: popularizing panels and a stratigraphic column that reproduces the sequence of rocks of the gorge, from the porphyry quartzifer of Bolzano to the sandstone of Val Gardena, to the layers in Bellerophon and Werfen up to the Formation of the Contrin. On Sundays in season guided tours in the gorge start from here.

  • Where Casa Peter Rosegger, Redagno di Sopra, Aldino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.bletterbach.info

Sources: bletterbach.info · castelfeder.info · suedtirol.info

Corno Bianco

Nature and landscape · Mountains · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Highest peak of the Bletterbach system (2.317 m), next to the Black Horn (2.439 m); from the Visitor Centre a path of 9.6 km and 940 meters of difference in altitude, about 5 hours, climbs to the Laner mountain hut, Kösertal and Grossnerwiesen meadows, with a teaching station on the formation and tectonics of the mountain.

  • Where Aldino (partenza dal Centro visitatori Bletterbach)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: castelfeder.info · dolomitiunesco.info · suedtirol.info

Mulini di Aldino

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Outdoor museum along the river in the valley (Thal): the water-powered forge with a long canal, the Schiassermühle that produced marbles, the Matznellermühle, the Thal-Mühle on the side of the hill and the old saw of Stampfer. An easy 3.5 km ring (about one hour, suitable for strollers) starts from the fire station, touches the biotope Möserwiesen and the Maria Schnee chapel; on Saturday in summer the Museum Association guides the visit and runs the mills.

  • Where Caserma dei pompieri (partenza), Aldino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museum-aldein.com

Sources: museum-aldein.com · suedtirol.info

Museo locale di Aldino

Museums · Old masters

Next to the church, in the square: not the usual town museum but a collection of sacred Baroque and Rococo objects, including a glass of Augusta of 1750 about, filigree of Bolzano, school banners of the Val di Fiemme and portraits of characters of Aldino like Cardinal Johann Baptist Franzelin. Opening by reservation through the tourist office.

  • Where Piazza del Paese, Aldino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.museum-aldein.com

Sources: museum-aldein.com · suedtirol.info
