What to see in Gesualdo

Borgo Irpino arranged in concentric circles around the castle that Carlo Gesualdo (1566-1613), the madrigalist prince, transformed into a Renaissance court; in the historical center the churches and convents founded by him, including Santa Maria delle Grazie with the pala del Perdono, the Cappellone and the palazzi Pisapia and Mattioli.

Castello di Gesualdo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Fortress with four circular bastions in shoe, of Lombard origin or of the IX century, documented from the XII century with Guglielmo d'Altavilla; at the end of the sixteenth century Carlo Gesualdo transformed it into a Renaissance court with a theatre, open to musicians and poets. Since 1855 of the Caccese, which rebuilt the façade, was severely damaged by the earthquake of 1980 and, restored since 2000, reopened at the end of 2015; it hosts concerts and events dedicated to the prince. Saturday and Sunday 10-13.30 and 15.30-19, weekdays by reservation.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval plant with concentric circles around the castle, with stone alleys that lead to terraces on the valley: to the west the narrow houses and hung from the feudal period, to the south the noble palaces, to the east squares, fountains, convents and monumental buildings. Always accessible.

Chiesa e Convento di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded by Carlo Gesualdo in 1592, as the plaque on the façade recalls: a single nave church with the prince's coat of arms on the entrance arch and the altarpiece of the pardon of Carlo Gesualdo by Giovanni Balducci (1609), with the prince in prayer next to Leonora d'Este. The Capuchin monastery, enlarged in 1629 and restored after 1980, has cloister and garden and a museum dedicated to Saint Pio of Pietrelcina, guest between November and December 1909, with his cell. Visits in the morning by reservation.

Chiesa del SS. Rosario

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Started by Carlo Gesualdo with the adjoining Dominican monastery and completed by Nicolò Ludovisi in the first half of the seventeenth century, at the same level of the castle: nine baroque altars in polychrome marble plus three others, wooden choir inlaid and the statue of Saint Vincenzo Ferreri, celebrated on the last Sunday of August with the Flight of the Angel. Visits by reservation.

Cappellone (Chiesa del Santissimo Sacramento)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It was built by Domenico Ludovisi and his son Nicolò, who completed it in 1736; in 1842 the Pesiri stonemasons built the staircase of nine steps in travertine. Inside, the painting of the Palio on the reconciliation between Charles and Emanuele Gesualdo. From here on Easter the representation of the Passion towards the castle. Visits by reservation.

Palazzi Pisapia e Mattioli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Most of the seventeenth-century complex on medieval structures, near the castle: the palace Mattioli was born combining small houses, the Pisapia was the house of the homonymous family. Restored after 1980, in 2009 they revealed mural paintings, some with scenes of 18th century navigation; today, owned by the municipality, they host exhibitions. Visits by reservation.
