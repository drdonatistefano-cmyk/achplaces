What to see in Barga

Medieval village of the Media Valle del Serchio, 10th century citadel with three-door walls and the cathedral on the acropolis; nearby lived Giovanni Pascoli.

Duomo di San Cristoforo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built between the 11th and 16th centuries in local limestone, the main example of Romanesque of the valley, with the large wooden statue of St. Christopher and a pulpit of the 12th century on columns of red marble.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Barga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Castello e mura con le tre porte

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The castle on a ridge at 410 meters, surrounded by walls with Porta Reale, Porta Macchiaia and Porta di Borgo, between cobbled streets and squares.

  • Where Barga
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

History and architecture · Historic theatres
