What to see in Tortolì

The main centre of Ogliastra, with the port of Arbatax, the Red Porfido Rocks and a coast of about 16 km of beaches and coves such as Cala Moresca, Porto Frailis and Lido di Orrì; inland the archaeological park of S'Ortali' and Monti and in the village the museum of contemporary art On Logu de S'iscultura and the former cathedral of Sant'Andrea.

Museo d'arte contemporanea Su logu de s'iscultura

Museums · Modern art

Born in the late 1990s as a sculpture park, it has an internal section for temporary exhibitions on the ground floor of the former 19th-century market, once also prison, and an external section diffused in the streets and countryside, with great environmental works by Mauro Staccioli, Umberto Mariani, Maria Lai, Pietro Coltella, Hidetoshi Nagasawa and others. Managed by the Municipality of Tortoli.

  • Where Corso Umberto 36, Tortolì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Ex cattedrale di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Built at the end of the eighteenth century in a classical style on an older church, of which two chapels remain (in one was found a statue of St Elizabeth of Hungary); it houses a sumptuous altar in polychrome marble.

  • Where Centro storico, Tortolì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Parco archeologico di S'Ortali 'e su Monti

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On two hills in San Salvatore, about 5 km from the village: a domus de Janas of the recent Neolithic, a nuraghe with a central tower in tholos, walls, three secondary towers and a dozen huts, silos and a hamlet of the mill, a tomb of Giants of the XV century BC with menhir still standing and a circle of stones of 12 meters. The site was used until Roman and Byzantine times.

  • Where Località San Salvatore, strada per il Lido di Orrì, Tortolì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Torre di San Miguel (Torre di Arbatax)

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

White control tower of Spanish era in front of the port, built in granite and porphyry towards the middle of the sixteenth century; it was part of a chain of coastal towers, of which also remains that of San Gemiliano. The name Arbatax would come from an Arabic expression that means "the fourteenth tower" .

  • Where Porto di Arbatax, Tortolì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Rocce Rosse

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural monument: high cliffs of red porphyry and black diorite emerging from the emerald green sea next to the port of Arbatax, at the foot of the hill of Bellavista; set of the final scene of Large parking lot.

  • Where Porto di Arbatax, Tortolì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Cala Moresca

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Arbatax cove protected by the Red Rocks under the promontory of Bellavista, between red and granite porphyry, with low and clear seabed and calm sea, suitable for children and snorkeling; you can reach it on foot with a path and a staircase. The name recalls the Saracen raids (is morus).

  • Where Via Batteria, Arbatax, Tortolì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Porto Frailis

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

An arched cove about 200 meters south of Capo Bellavista, one kilometer from the port, with fine sand between silver and gold, granite rocks and bottom that slopes gently, guarded by the tower of San Gemiliano; several times Blue Flag FEE.

  • Where Porto Frailis, Arbatax, Tortolì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Lido di Orrì e Cala Ginepro

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

A coast of about 16 km of coves to the south of Arbatax, with the large beach of about 3,5 km: white and golden sand, low bottom for about 300 meters, granite cliffs and pine forest behind; among the coves inside Cala Ginepro, with a grove of junipers.

  • Where Lido di Orrì, Tortolì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Porto di Arbatax

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The main port on the east coast, with a marina of about 650 berths; it is the starting point of the boat trips to the coast of Baunei and the Gulf of Orosei, between Cala Goloritzé, Cala Mariolu, Cala Luna, Pedra Longa and the caves of Fico and Bue Marino.

  • Where Porto di Arbatax, Tortolì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
