What to see in Gavorrano

Medieval village of the Alta Maremma on Poggio Ballone, once lordship of the Pannocchieschi and then mining center of the pyrite between Otto and Novecento: today the Natural Mining Park with the museum in the gallery, the Theatre of the Rocks, the Pozzo Roma and the Ravi-Marchi area. In the territory of Castel di Pietra, linked to Pia di Dante, the parish church of San Giuliano, San Biagio a Caldana and the Etruscan necropolis of San Germano.

Museo minerario in galleria e Parco delle Rocce

Museums · Themed

The museum occupies a gallery that served as a storage of explosives and tells, with images, sounds, stories and anecdotes, the phases of the mine and the hard work of the miners; the mines of pyrites, active since 1898 and among the most important in Italy, closed in 1981. The entrance is in the Park of the Rocks, with welcome and infopoint; it is part of the National Park of the Metalliferous Hills.

Teatro delle Rocce

History and architecture · Historic theatres · Viewpoint

Open-air theatre with 2,000 places in a small semicircular quarry at the foot of the San Rocco quarry, one of the limestone quarries serving the mine; it hosts shows, festivals and cultural events every summer.

Pozzo Roma

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

The mining pit is a symbol of the Gavorrano mine, at an altitude of 200 m, from which the majority of the mineral came back; excavated since 1914, it has the current castle in steel trellis of 1963 with two oblique dots, and in 1970 it was deepened up to -140 m above sea level. Next to the buildings of the winch room, the electric cabin and the compressors.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Pozzo Valsecchi

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ventilation well of 1955, 336 m, the highest point of the mine, 410 m deep: its wooden structure is the only one left in the Metalliferous Hills. Next to the winch building and the air extractor.

Area mineraria Ravi-Marchi

History and architecture · Archaeology

Site of industrial archaeology outdoors, with surface systems abandoned in 1965 that illustrate the first treatments of the pyrite, from the extraction well to the final processing; guided tours also night, workshops, shows and meetings.

Centro storico di Gavorrano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Borgo su Poggio Ballone, about 260 m, which preserves castle, arches, doors, walls and palaces with medieval towers; in the thirteenth century lands and castle were Pannocchieschi. Every August the historic center houses the Salto della Contessa, re-enactment in costume dedicated to Pia de' Tolomei.

Pieve di San Giuliano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Pieve dedicated to the patron since 1529, on a site already a high medieval sanctuary, rebuilt at the end of the eighteenth century incorporating the walls of the fortress; bell tower with ancient masonry in squared basins, three naves and tripartite facade of the restoration of 1927. It keeps the Madonna and Child in marble by the Sienese sculptor Giovanni d'Agostino (1336) and the seventeenth-century Madonna del Buonconsiglio.

Museums · Archaeological

It exhibits the excavations of the University of Siena (medieval archaeology) in Castel di Pietra, including majolica majolica mugs, one with the coat of arms of the Ptolemies; educational panels reconstruct life in the castle in the fourteenth century. Castel di Pietra

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Ruins on a cliff where the upper valley of Bruna opens onto the Grosseto plain, along the ancient metal road: inhabited since the protohistory, castrum in the twelfth century and military and noble complex of the Pannocchieschi in the thirteenth century, abandoned in the fourteenth century. The excavations 1997-2007 have brought to light walls and the remains of a tower; tradition places the death of Pia de' Tolomei, remembered by Dante in the fifth song of Purgatory, and a plaque tells the verses.

Chiesa di San Biagio a Caldana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Example of the sixteenth century Tuscany, probably designed by Antonio da Sangallo the Elder: facade with eight pilasters with Ionic capitals and dorics and large central rose window, inside with a single nave with a valuable baptismal font in marble. Caldana is one of the medieval villages of the municipality, surrounded by imposing walls.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Cave di marmo rosso di Caldana

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Cave to the north-west of Caldana, active until 1975 and documented by the sixteenth century, perhaps already in Roman times: the stone is a limestone breach called Portasanta due to the resemblance with the ancient marble of Chio used for the holy doors of four Roman basilicas. The excavation flourished in the nineteenth century with Peter Leopold and Leopold II of Lorraine.

  • Entry Free

Diga dei Muracci

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Ruins along the SP 20 near the Bruna river of a fifteenth-century dam for a fish pond, designed by Guidoccio d'Andrea and built by master comacini, completed in 1481 for over 15,000 gold florins; it broke in 1492 and was abandoned after the flood of 1532. Long 60 m on the side of Gavorrano and 123 m on the side of Roccastrada.

  • Where SP 20, presso il fiume Bruna, Gavorrano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.gavorrano.gr.it · visittuscany.com

Necropoli di San Germano e Centro di documentazione Rocca di Frassinello

History and architecture · Archaeology

On the grounds of Rocca di Frassinello, along the E80, about thirty mounded tombs recovered and restored with a tour of panels and poetic quotations on wine in the ancient world. The documentation centre, in the cellar designed by Renzo Piano with the barricade dug into the rock, exhibits the findings of the necropolis of San Germano and Santa Teresa di Gavorrano on the theme of Etruscan wine, in a project with Superintendence and University of Florence.

  • Where Rocca di Frassinello, Giuncarico, Gavorrano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
