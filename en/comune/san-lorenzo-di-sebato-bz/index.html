What to see in San Lorenzo di Sebato

The village of Val Pusteria was built on the Roman station of Mansio Sebatum: the homonymous museum, the first in South Tyrol dedicated to the ancient Romans, the milestone of the Emperor Macrino and an archaeological path between Roman ruins, castelliers and the hill of Sonnenburger Kopf; in the village the parish church with the Madonna of Michael Pacher, and in Sares the sanctuary of the Black Madonna of Maria Saalen.

5 places to visit in San Lorenzo di Sebato, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Mansio Sebatum

Museums · Archaeological

The first museum in South Tyrol dedicated to the ancient Romans: on three floors exhibits finds of San Lorenzo and surroundings on the history of the station of Mansio Sebatum, in the land of the Saevates, from 100 BC to 600 AD, with the results of researches on the Iron and Roman Age and on daily life. Closed on Sundays and holidays.

  • Where Via Josef Renzler 9, San Lorenzo di Sebato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.mansio-sebatum.it

Sources: mansio-sebatum.it · suedtirol.info

Pietra miliare romana

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Roman billionaire with the names of Emperor M. Opelius Severus Macrinus and his son Diadumeniano, found in Castelbadia (Sonnenburg) in 1857 and today at the entrance of the Museum Mansio Sebatum.

  • Where Via Josef Renzler 9, San Lorenzo di Sebato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Sentiero archeologico panoramico

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

An easy ring of 4.1 km (about 1 hour and 45, 173 meters of difference in altitude) from the parish square, with 16 panels on excavations, settlements, fortifications and finds from the Bronze Age to the late antiquity: the ruins of the Roman settlement of Sebatum, the castellieri of Schreckbühel, a place of votive ruins of the Iron Age, the hill of Sonnenburger Kopf, ancient place of worship, and the coppelle rocks of Castelbadia, perhaps of the late Bronze Age. The site of the municipality also describes an educational path of 11 boards with departure recommended by the bridge Peintner. Some stretches are not suitable for strollers and wheelchairs.

  • Where Piazza della chiesa parrocchiale, San Lorenzo di Sebato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: bruneck.com · stlorenzen.eu · suedtirol.info

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Irregular building built in several phases between the thirteenth and sixteenth centuries: it houses the famous Madonna with the Child who holds the grapes of Michael Pacher, and the adjoining chapel Egerer has frescoes of the school of Pacher.

  • Where Piazza F. Hellweger, San Lorenzo di Sebato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Santuario di Maria Saalen

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Small pilgrimage church dedicated to Our Lady of Loreto, built in 1652 (date on the entrance) and once equipped with hermitage, the most frequented pilgrimage of the Pusteria media. It is climbed along the Way of the Cross; inside the Black Madonna with the Child between the Saints Anna and Joachim, the Annunciation on the tympanum of the altar and the Saints Joseph and Silvestro above the doors.

  • Where Sares (Saalen), San Lorenzo di Sebato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info
