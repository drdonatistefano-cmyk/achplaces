What to see in Premia

The last village of the Valle Antigorio before the Formazza Valley, Premia combines the thermal baths of Cadarese, with water flowing at 44 °C, the Orridi di Urizzo excavated from the waters of the Toce glacier, the Mineral Museum with over 3,300 Ossolani minerals and the walser fractions of Salecchio, abandoned in 1966 and remained almost intact.

9 places to visit in Premia, in the province of Verbano-Cusio-Ossola (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Orridi di Uriezzo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Series of almost circular cavities, joined by narrow passages, born from the erosion of the waters flowing under the ancient Toce glacier. The Southern Horrid, the most spectacular, is about 200 meters long and 20-30 deep; the North-East Horrid, near the oratory of Saint Lucia, is a gorge of about 150 meters crossed in whole; the West Horrid is the least important. Iron ladders and walkways make sure the path is marked, but with wet or frozen bottom the risk can be very high.

  • Where Frazione Uriezzo, Premia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Premia Terme

Local life and traditions · Spas

The spa opened in 2008 in Cadarese, indicated as the most important in northern Piedmont: the spring water, classified hyperthermal sulphate-calcic, flows at 44 °C. It offers thermal baths, spa, beauty treatments and medical treatment for specific pathologies.

  • Where Frazione Cadarese, Premia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: visitossola.it · distrettolaghi.it

Museo mineralogico «Don Giovanni Bonomo»

Museums · Science and university

Opened on August 3, 2002 in the building that housed the town hall and elementary schools, in front of the square, it was born from the collection donated by the parish priest Don Giovanni Bonomo, then enriched by other collectors. It preserves 3,333 minerals found almost all in Valle Antigorio and Val Formazza, including cervandonite, gasparite, fetiasitis and asbecaite. Open from July 15 to August 30 every day and from December 27 to January 4 and Easter, 16-18; other days by contacting the Municipality (tel. 0324 62021).

  • Where Via Case Francesco 58, Premia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: comune.premia.vb.it · visitossola.it · distrettolaghi.it

Salecchio, villaggio walser

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

An ancient Walser colony founded in the 13th century by settlers from Valais, on a rocky terrace where for 700 years lived a community of no more than one hundred people; the inhabitants left in 1966 for isolation, and Lower Salecchio (1,320 m) and Superior (1.509 m) remained almost intact, with wooden houses, a restored water sawmill and the oven. Walk up from hamlet Passo on mountain path.

  • Where Salecchio Inferiore e Superiore, Premia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: comune.premia.vb.it · visitossola.it · distrettolaghi.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the capital (810 m) the parish church of San Michele, with three naves, began in the first half of the seventeenth century; from the village you can see the Rio d'Alba Falls, about 329 meters high, more spectacular with the great rains.
