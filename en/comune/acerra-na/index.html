What to see in Acerra

City of the plain of the Regi Lagni with the baronial castle of Lombard origin, built on the remains of the Roman theatre, which houses the museum of history and archeology of Acerra and Suessula.

2 places to visit in Acerra, in the province of Naples (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello baronale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Of Lombard origin (IX century), it rises on the remains of the Roman theater of Acerrae; in the twelfth century it was decorated with polychrome tarsies of tuff, in the siege of 1421 it was described as "baluardo imprendibile" and at the end of the fifteenth century it became a noble residence, with new rooms, windows and gardens.

Museo di storia e archeologia del territorio di Acerra e Suessula

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 2023 in the castle: on the ground floor the history of the territory with the findings of the preventive excavations (including those for high speed), in the former stables the accessories of Acerrae from the fourth century BC to the Roman age, a room on prehistory and one on Suessula, flourishing from the IX century BC and abandoned after the Middle Ages.
