What to see in Umbertide

A small town in the upper Tiber Valley, which is gathered around the 14th century Fortress, now the centre for contemporary art: the Deposition of Luca Signorelli in the Museum of Santa Croce, the octagonal collegiate church of Santa Maria della Reggia, the Franciscan churches and, on the hills, the abbey and hermitage of Montecorona.

Rocca di Umbertide – Centro per l'arte contemporanea

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Designed in 1374 by Angeluccio di Ceccolo, called "il Trucascio" and completed in 1389, it was imprisoned there and then lived Armcio Fortebraccio; it was jailed from 1814 to 1923 and houses until 1974, restored by the Municipality between 1984 and 1986. The square mast exceeds 30 metres, with three smaller crenellated towers; the rooms house the Centre for Contemporary Art.

  • Where Piazza Fortebracci, Umbertide
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it · luoghidelcontemporaneo.cultura.gov.it

Museo civico di Santa Croce

Museums · Old masters

In the former church of Santa Croce (1610) the Deposition from the Cross of Luca Signorelli, of 1516, with three tablets of predella on the legend of the True Cross, and the great Madonna and Child in glory between angels and Saints of Pomarancio (1577), coming from the nearby church of San Francesco.

  • Where Piazza San Francesco, Umbertide
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Abbazia di San Salvatore di Montecorona

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Abbey probably founded by St Romualdo in 1008-1009 and linked to the Camaldolese: upper Romanesque church consecrated in 1105, octagonal bell tower perhaps obtained from a 14th century defensive tower and crypt of Saint Mary of the Graces, perhaps of the 11th century.

Eremo di Monte Corona

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint
