What to see in Venzone

The best preserved fortified village of Friuli Venezia Giulia, a national monument since 1965, rebuilt for anastyles after the earthquake of 1976: Gothic cathedral consecrated in 1338, 14th century walls with moat, the chapel of San Michele with the mummies and museums of Palazzo Orgnani Martina.

Centro storico e palazzi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Historic center declared National Monument in 1965, with palaces and noble residences from the 11th to the 18th century, rebuilt for anastylosis after the earthquakes of 1976. Among the buildings: Casa Marcurele, the oldest (XI century, Romanesque), the fourteenth-century Palazzo degli Scaligeri, Palazzo Zinutti (13th century) with wrought iron balcony, Casa Calderari (14th century) which houses the tourist office and Palazzo Radiussi with Gothic-Vezian trifora.

  • Where Venzone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Duomo di Sant'Andrea Apostolo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Romanesque-Gothic T-shaped building with three priests and two towers, consecrated in 1338 by the patriarch of Aquileia Bertrando. It preserves an organ of 1792 by G. Callido and is considered an exemplary monument of post-earthquake restoration.

  • Where Venzone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Cappella cimiteriale di San Michele e mummie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

13th century chapel (built around 1200) in the cathedral churchyard, which houses the Venzone mummies, dated between 1348 and 1881. From the tombs of the cathedral were extracted about forty, starting from the mummy of the hunchback found in 1647; the natural mummification is attributed to a mold, Hypha bombicina Pers., which dehydrates the tissues.

  • Where Sagrato del Duomo, Venzone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Mura, fossato e Porta di San Genesio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Double walls with ample moat, rectangular towers, a polygonal tower on the south-west corner and two to the north-west, in the current form from about 1300; three gates, including the fourteenth-century Porta di San Genesio with the watchtower, from which you can see the walls and the moat. The entrance of Porta di Sotto is a round arch of 1835.

  • Where Venzone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · turismofvg.it

Palazzo Comunale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Gothic palace of 1410 on the Town Hall square, restored at the beginning of the sixteenth century, with clock tower, coats of arms and Gothic mullioned windows.

  • Where Piazza del Municipio, Venzone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · turismofvg.it · italia.it

Museo Tiere Motus

Museums · City history

Show permanent "Tiere Motus." History of an earthquake and its people are on the first floor of the sixteenth-century Palazzo Orgnani-Martina: photos, videos and documents on the earthquake of May 6, 1976, from the day of the shock to the reconstruction, with a 3D simulation of the collapse of the cathedral.

  • Where Via Mistruzzi 4, Venzone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.tieremotus.it

Sources: tieremotus.it · turismofvg.it · italia.it

Museo della Terra - Foreste, Uomo, Economia

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Teaching exhibition in three rooms of the Museo della Terra di Venzone, born in Udine in 1986-87 from the Friulano Museum of Natural History: models and dioramas on forest ecosystems, fauna and the uses of trees, over 300 models of mushrooms and a section on the forest in myth. Open on Saturday 14-18 and, from May to October, Sundays and holidays 14-18; other days by reservation.

  • Where Palazzo Orgnani Martina, Via Mistruzzi 4, Venzone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the 14th century of which the earthquake of 1976 has spared only the main facade, with Gothic portal to figurative and floral capitals.

Sentiero delle chiesette

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Route that connects the fifteenth-century churches around the village (Santi Anna e Giacomo, Santa Caterina, Sant'Antonio abate, Santa Lucia), along an ancient path, with views of the surrounding landscape. Part of the municipal territory is part of the Natural Park of the Julian Prealps.

  • Where Dintorni di Venzone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
