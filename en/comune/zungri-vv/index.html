What to see in Zungri

Country of the hinterland of the Costa degli Dei known for the rocky settlement of the Grotte degli Sbariati, the small Petra Calabrian , with dozens of environments dug into the rock from the 8th to the 11th century; complete the visit the Museum of the rocky and peasant civilization, the Sanctuary of the Madonna della Snow, the Grotto Trisulina and the abandoned village of Papaglioni.

6 places to visit in Zungri, in the province of Vibo Valentia (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Grotte degli Sbariati - Insediamento rupestre

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Rock village of about 3,000 square meters formed by dozens of units partly dug into the rock and partly built, used as houses, shelters for animals, for the production of wine and lime and as granaries; the oldest traces seem to date back to the VIII-XII centuries. The hypothesis about origin are two: a colony of oriental populations or a productive outpost or deposit of the nearby Kastron of Mesiano. Guided tours by reservation (info@grottezungri.it, 377 441 9886); not recommended to those who have difficulty in getting rid of steps and uneven and steep terrain.

  • Where Via Indipendenza, Zungri
  • Entry €4, reduced €3 (groups over 20 people)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.zungri.vv.it

Sources: comune.zungri.vv.it · italia.it

Museo della civiltà rupestre e contadina

Museums · Themed

Opened in 2003 in a former mill near the rocky settlement, it gathers about 3,000 objects of the peasant and rocky life of Zungri, in sections dedicated to the home, to the work of the fields, to traditional crafts, to the weaving and production of wine; a room is furnished as a vintage bedroom, with devotional images, daily men's and women's clothes and wedding costumes.

the MiC card (updated 2020) indicates closed every day; the municipality lists it among the services of the rock site, check the visit with the caves

Santuario diocesano Madonna della Neve

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

The sanctuary, called Sancta Maria ad Nives, houses the table of the Madonna della Neve (145 x 108 cm), attributed to an anonymous southern painter of the 16th century Raffaellesque school, inspired by the Little Holy Family of Raphael of the Louvre. On the site of the ancient mother church, destroyed by the earthquake of September 8, 1905, stands a small church of Our Lady of Lourdes, behind which a small road leads to the rock settlement. Wheelchair accessible.

  • Where Vico III Indipendenza 84, Zungri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.zungri.vv.it

Sources: comune.zungri.vv.it

Grotta Trisulina

History and architecture · Archaeology

Hypogeum dug into the tuff and buried on three sides, in Trisulina on a height about 500 meters: four arches on five pillars divide the interior into two naves and two side stairs give access. For a long time considered the nymph of a Roman villa, the Municipality indicates it as an ancient temple of Santa Rosalia and among the few traces of the Roman presence of Augustian age in Calabria; around there are Corinthian capitals in marble, capitals with palms and fragments of granite columns. Not recommended for those who have motor difficulties.

  • Where Località Trisulina, Zungri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Papaglionti Vecchio

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry

A small abandoned village, a hamlet of Zungri, mentioned as early as 1280; the name derives from the Greek priest, Pope Leonitius, probably owner of the first building around which the medieval town grew. Damaged by landslides and floods, it was left in the eighties, when the community moved a few kilometers higher; the buildings are mostly stone with brick inserts.

  • Where Papaglionti, Zungri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.zungri.vv.it

Sources: comune.zungri.vv.it

Palazzo Fiamingo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace of 1718, date carved on the portal, built by the Cutrullà family, then hegemony in Zungri; it was the seat of the town hall and theatre of the revolt of 1901, when hundreds of citizens broke into the municipal council against the appropriation of the former state property lands assigned to the community in 1806. Today it is home to Pro Loco (prolocozungri@gmail.com).

  • Where Via Umberto I, Zungri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.zungri.vv.it

Sources: comune.zungri.vv.it
