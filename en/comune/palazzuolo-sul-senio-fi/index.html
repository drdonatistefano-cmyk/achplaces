What to see in Palazzuolo sul Senio

3 places to visit in Palazzuolo sul Senio, in the province of Florence (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo di Palazzuolo sul Senio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Borgo dell'Apennine among the most beautiful villages in Italy, with the Palazzo dei Capitani; in July the medieval festivals of the Ubaldini.

  • Where Palazzuolo sul Senio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com

Museo delle Genti di Montagna

Museums · Themed

In the medieval Palazzo dei Capitani the agricultural tools and artisans, the domestic furnishings and devotional objects of the Apennine communities, used until the depopulation of the mountain.

  • Where Palazzo dei Capitani, Piazza E. Alpi 4, Palazzuolo sul Senio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com

In mountain bike a Lozzole

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Itinerary in mountain bike signposted by Feel Florence up to the ancient mountain village of Lozzole, in the Apennines above Palazzuolo.

  • Where Lozzole, Palazzuolo sul Senio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com
