What to see in Ceprano

Borgo della Ciociaria at the confluence of Liri and Sacco, the seventh stage of the Via Francigena del Sud, known as the City of Argil for the skull of Homo erectus about 800,000 years ago found in 1994. It preserves the remains of the Roman colony of Fregellae in the Museum . Amedeo Maiuri , the bridge over the Liri of the meeting between Innocent IV and Manfredi (1254) and the Collegiate Church of Santa Maria Maggiore.

Museo archeologico di Fregellae «Amedeo Maiuri»

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 1989 and completed in 1991, it occupies four rooms on the ground floor of the town hall and exhibits the materials of the excavations of Fregellae: talamoni, votive terracotta, architectural fragments of the sanctuary of Esculapius and mosaic floors, with panels, plastic and reconstructive graphics of the main monuments. It is thought as a didactic journey on the history and daily life of the city; the final seat is planned on the lower floors of Palazzo Ferrari. According to the MiC card is open every day (Mon, Wed, Fri 9:30-12:30; Mar and Thu 9:30-18; Sat 8-18; Sun 9-12), without reservation.

Area archeologica di Fregellae

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Colonia diritto Latino founded by the Romans in 328 BC on the left bank of Liri, in the valley between Mount Cairo and the Ausoni and Aurunci mountains; celebrated by Tito Livio, it was destroyed in 124 BC and rebuilt a little further south as Fabrateria Nova. The excavations brought to light an urban stretch of the Via Latina, the road that after the refoundation was prolonged to Capua. The site is part of the Regional Nature Reserve.

Ponte sul Liri

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

A compulsory passage through the caravans between Rome and Naples: here on 11 October 1254 Pope Innocent IV, who arrived from Anagni, met Manfredi of Swabia, who had to cross the bridge holding the bridles of the mule of the pontiff; Dante remembers the episode in Purgatory. According to the Manfredi tradition, killed at Benevento, he was buried under a bridge pylon. In mid-October, the Palio delle Corti evokes the encounter with flag-wavers, falconers, archery and fire arrows launched on the river.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Palazzo Ferrari (Palazzo Vespignani)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

In front of the bridge over Liri, it was commissioned in 1834 by Monsignor Ferrari to the architect Vespignani, from which the second name; the facade has Doric columns, pilasters and floral festoons. It is one of the historic residences of Lazio and on the lower floors is the definitive seat of the Archaeological Museum of Fregellae. You visit only by reservation.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Collegiata di Santa Maria Maggiore (Sant'Arduino)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

According to tradition, it was founded by the apostle Peter on his journey to Rome; the current neoclassical aspect dates back to reconstruction after the destruction of World War II. It preserves relics and a wooden statue of St. Arduino, the priest returning from Palestine who died of plague in Ceprano on July 28, 627, companion with St. Rocco, and the marble urn with the remains of Manfredi.

  • Entry Free

Riserva naturale Antiche Città di Fregellae e Fabrateria Nova e del Lago di San Giovanni Incarico

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

A humid area along the lake formed in 1925 by the barrier Enel sul Liri, with riparian woods with white willow and one of the few stripes of plain forest remaining in the valley of Sacco-Liri. There are 75 species of birds, 32 related to water (vaps, duffet, herons, tarabusin), and is indicated for birdwatching; among the curly mammals, istrice, fox, badger and weasel. It includes the sites of Fregellae in Ceprano and Fabrateria Nova in San Giovanni Incarico, with equipped area and visit centre.

  • Where Valle del Liri, tra Ceprano e San Giovanni Incarico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com
