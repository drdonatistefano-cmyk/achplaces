What to see in San Paolo di Civitate

Center of the High Table on a hill overlooking the Gargano promontory and the first heights of the Dauni Mountains, about 11 km from San Severo, linked to the name of Traiano and the Battle of Civitate of 1053, in which the Normans defeated the papal troops at the Fortore. In the territory the ancient Teanum Apulum with its Roman amphitheatre; in the village the Civic Museum in the seventeenth-century monastery of Sant'Antonio da Padova.

Anfiteatro romano di Teanum Apulum

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Where the daunio settlement of Tiati (IX-IV century BC) was located, allied with the Sannites against Rome and then linked to Rome after 318 BC, the Romans concentrated the municipality of Teanum Apulum between the Mengoni Cup and the Church's Pezze. At the edge of the city, in the imperial age (I-II century AD), the amphitheatre was built with irregular elliptical plan, probably obtained on the slope, brought to light by the excavation of the Superintendence of 2020: part of the cavea remains, a passage turned towards the arena and a stretch of arena bordered by parapets in brick, for about 442 m2. About 500 m to the west remains the tower called "Church of Civitate" of the medieval fortified town (XI-XV century); the area is protected by ministerial decrees of 1990-1997.

Museo Civico Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

Set up in the seventeenth-century monastery of Sant'Antonio da Padova, it collects numerous finds; the municipality collaborates in archaeological investigations on Teanum Apulum with its Museum and Archaeological Service.

Centro storico e Palazzo baronale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town, on a hill overlooking the Gargano and the Dauni Mountains, preserves traces of the various dominations; there are the Baronial Palace, formerly residence of the Carafa and Gonzaga families, the church of St. Paul the Apostle, the first of the village, and the church of St. Nicholas.
