What to see in Pavullo nel Frignano

Centro del Frignano in the Modena Apennines: the castle of Montecuccolo with its fortified village and museum centre, the Romanesque parish church of Renno, the natural reserve of Sassoguiano and the Devil's Bridge, a natural rock arch on the border with Lama Mocogno and Polinago.

Castello di Montecuccolo e Centro museale (CEM)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On a rock spur above the valley of Scoltenna-Panaro, about three kilometers from the center, it was a residence and military center of the Montecuccoli, which controlled dozens of towers guarding the streets for Tuscany; the mast is on the highest point, surrounded by three circles of walls. A Gothic portal introduces to the medieval village with the Palazzo del Podestà and the fifteenth-century church of San Lorenzo. The castle, completely restored in 2009, hosts the Montecuccolo Museum Centre with the Donation Raffaele Biolchini, the country rediscovered by Gino Covili and the Naturalistic Museum of Frignano.

Pieve di San Giovanni Battista a Renno

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque church that in 1157 Bishop Enrico Montecuccoli put at the head of a network of 30 chapels; the interior has three naves on pillars, four of which octagonal. It preserves the red marble saints water table of Verona donated in 1609 for the baptism of the leader and writer Raimondo Montecuccoli.

  • Where Renno, Pavullo nel Frignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Riserva naturale di Sassoguidano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 280 hectares of wooded plateau along the rocky ridge of Malvarone Cinghio (722 m), between calanchi, chestnut groves and clearings, with the valley of the Lerna stream between rocky walls and karst forms. More than 80 species of birds, foxes, badgers, roe deer and wild boars; at the Oratory of Sassomassiccio there is a small pond in a sinkhole, purchased by WWF Modena in 1994.

  • Where Sassoguidano, Pavullo nel Frignano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Ponte del Diavolo (Ponte d'Ercole)

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural rock arch about 33 meters long and a couple wide, on the border between Pavullo, Lama Mocogno and Polinago; in the surroundings are found finds from the protohistory to the Middle Ages. It appears in the frescoes of the late sixteenth century by Cesare Baglione in the Sala delle Vedute of the castle of Spezzano.

  • Where Confine Pavullo-Lama Mocogno-Polinago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
