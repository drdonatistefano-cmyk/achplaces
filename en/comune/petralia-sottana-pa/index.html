What to see in Petralia Sottana

Borgo Bandiera Arancione in the heart of the Madonie Park, with a 17th-century old town centre of alleys, stairs and fountains: the Mother Church with the Gothic-Catalan portal, the Gagini ancona alla SS. Trinità, the Civic Museum Collisani, the first Geological Route Urban of Europe, the hydroelectric power plant of 1908 and, at altitude, Piano Battaglia.

Chiesa Madre (Santa Maria Assunta)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The seventeenth-century church that incorporates a previous building and preserves a late-Gothic portal in the Catalan-Gothic style, decorated with the coats of arms of the Cardona.

Museo civico Antonio Collisani

Museums · Themed

Established in 2003 in the restored premises of a former 15th century prison, with the original grates: the geological section (2004) tells in an interactive way the history of the Madonie, from coral reefs to about 3000 years ago, with the Rock Library and Sala Salgemma; the archaeological section (2008) exhibits the Collisani Collection, with a room dedicated to the Grotto of the Vecchiuzzo, prehistoric site discovered by Antonio Collisani.

MiC card indicates it closed every day (aggiornamento 2021)

  • Where Corso Paolo Agliata, Petralia Sottana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitsicily.info

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Centro storico e Corso Paolo Agliata

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Center with a 17th century face, divided into five historic districts, with alleys, arcades, long steps, original paved, ancient wells in the ground floors and fountains of spring water. On the façade of San Francesco the marble table of 1862 to convert the ancient measurements into the metric system; on the bell tower of Mercy a sundial of 1882 by Father Fedele Bencivinni; the Casa du Curriva is only one meter wide.

  • Where Corso Paolo Agliata, Petralia Sottana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: touringclub.it · comune.petraliasottana.pa.it · visitsicily.info

Parco delle Madonie e Piano Battaglia

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The municipality has 5,891 hectares in the Madonie Park and numerous geosites of the Geopark Madonie UNESCO, with hiking trails, bike paths and equestrian trails; there pass the Trail Italy and the path of the Madonite Sanctuaries. In the hamlet of Piano Battaglia there are ski slopes and ski lifts.

  • Where Piano Battaglia, Petralia Sottana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: touringclub.it · visitsicily.info
