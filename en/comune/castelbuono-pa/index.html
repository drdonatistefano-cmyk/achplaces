What to see in Castelbuono

Borgo delle Madonie, in the Madonie Park, known for its ash manna: the Castello dei Ventimiglia of 1317 with the Civic Museum and the Palatine Chapel of Serpotti stuccoes, the Madrice Vecchia, the Church of San Francesco with the Mausoleum of Ventimiglia and the Naturalistic Museum Minà Palumbo.

Castello dei Ventimiglia, Museo Civico e Cappella Palatina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built in 1317 by Count Francesco I Ventimiglia on a 12th century watch tower, it houses the Civic Museum with sections of Archaeology, Urbanism, Sacred Art and Modern and Contemporary Art, as well as events and exhibitions. In the Palatine Chapel the stuccoes of Giuseppe and Giacomo Serpotta (1684-1687) and the Skull of Sant'Anna kept in a silver reliquary bust of 1521.

Museo naturalistico «Francesco Minà Palumbo»

Museums · Science and university

Dedicated to the nineteenth-century physician and botanist Francesco Minà Palumbo, who collected and classified plant species, reptiles and insects of the Madonie, whose collections the museum preserves.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Madrice Vecchia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the 14th century built on the remains of a pagan temple, with a sixteenth-century Renaissance portico and portal in Catalan style, enlarged from three to four naves at the end of the 15th century. It houses a polyptych attributed to Antonello de Saliba or Pietro Ruzzolone, the Madonna degli Angeli in marble by Antonello Gagini, the fifteenth-century frescoes by the Spousalizio delle Vergini and Santa Caterina d'Alessandria and a frescoed crypt in the sixteenth century with scenes of the Passion.

  • Where Castelbuono
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Chiesa di San Francesco e Mausoleo dei Ventimiglia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the 14th century with the octagonal mausoleum that houses the Ventimiglia tombs; the organ of 1547 is indicated as one of the three oldest reed organs in Italy with the original mechanisms.

  • Where Castelbuono
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Madrice Nuova

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the seventeenth century with a walled cross of the fifteenth century on a gold background, the Complaint on Christ died by Giuseppe Velasco and two baroque altars by Giuseppe Serpotta.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Fontana della Venere Ciprea, Banca di Corte e Torre dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Along the main street the Fountain of Venus Cyprea, rebuilt in 1614; in the center the ancient Bank of Court, from which you can access the tower whose clock beats the hours from 1885.
