What to see in Rocchetta a Volturno

Country of the high valley of Volturno, between the Mainarde and the river, linked to the events of the Benedictine abbey of San Vincenzo al Volturno, founded in the eighth century and now archaeological park with the frescoed crypt of the abbot Epifanio; above the modern village the ruins of Rocchetta Alta, abandoned village, and in the village the International Museum of World Wars.

Abbazia di San Vincenzo al Volturno

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry

Benedictine monastery founded according to tradition at the beginning of the 8th century by three Beneventan nobles, Paldo, Taso and Tato, at the springs of Volturno; placed by Charlemagne under his protection in 787, under the abbots Joshua, Talarico and Epifanio became a monastic city with about ten churches and about 350 monks, until the 881 raid devastated it. The archaeological park preserves the remains of the monastic city of the IX century, San Vincenzo Minore with the Crypt of Epifanio and its high medieval frescoes, in which the abbot appears among saints and angels, and the basilica of San Vincenzo Maggiore, rebuilt in the twentieth century, with the ring crypt of Joshua. The state archaeological complex, on the border with Castel San Vincenzo, is closed on Mondays (ticket reduced to €3 from 13 March 2026 until the completion of the works on the barriers).

  • Where SS158, Rocchetta a Volturno
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo Internazionale delle Guerre Mondiali (MIGM)

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Civic Museum founded in 2010 at the foot of Monte Marrone, near the places where the Italian Liberation Corps was formed: over 900 square meters in ten rooms with uniforms, accessories and original weapons, a room with more than 200 types of weapons deactivated and external hangars with large military means and artillery, as well as library and rare war periodicals. Seasonal hours from Tuesday to Sunday, 10-13 and 16-20 in summer, 16-19 in winter.

  • Where Via Roma 61/67, Rocchetta a Volturno
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitmolise.eu · italia.it

Rocchetta Alta

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Viewpoint

The original nucleus of the village, built along a rocky ridge with rock spurs that emerge between the houses and the castle on the highest summit, was abandoned for the damage of war, the difficult conditions of life and the high hydrogeological risk; the new center, Rocchetta Nuova, was born at the end of the nineteenth century on the plateau between the Mainarde and Volturno. Today it is a suggestive ruin among whose alleys you can walk.

  • Where Rocchetta Alta, Rocchetta a Volturno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitmolise.eu · italia.it

Santuario rupestre di Santa Maria delle Grotte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

A solitary church carved into the rock and decorated with frescoes, a precious treasure chest nestled among the rocks, reachable with a short detour from the abbey of San Vincenzo al Volturno.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
