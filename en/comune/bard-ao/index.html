What to see in Bard

The smallest town in Italy, the most beautiful village in Italy, closed in the gorge of the Dora Baltea under the fort of the nineteenth century, along the Via delle Gallie and Via Francigena.

6 places to visit in Bard, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Forte di Bard

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

One of the best examples of a barrage fortress of the early nineteenth century, almost intact; one climbs up with panoramic lifts. It houses the Museum of the Alps, the Prisons and the Museum of Fortifications and Frontiers.
