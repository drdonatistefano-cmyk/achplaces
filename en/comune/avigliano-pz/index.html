What to see in Avigliano

The municipality of the Potentino, perhaps of Sannita foundation, prosperous between the Middle Ages and the modern age as shown by the noble palaces of the historic center; in the hamlet of Lagopesole stands the castle of Federico II, which houses the Museum of Lucana emigration.

5 places to visit in Avigliano, in the province of Potenza (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Lagopesole

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

It rises on a hill on the valley of Vitalba, built on Norman structures: the current plant is Swabian (from 1242), hunting and leisure residence of Frederick II, with angioin additions. Rectangular plant with corner towers and two courtyards: in the greater the Fourth of the Emperor, the Fourth of the Queen and the Palatine Chapel, in the lower the mast. Since 1531 it was a feud of the Doria, in 1969 donated to the State; on the first floor it houses the Museum of Lucanian Emigration. Free admission with access to download on the platform Italian Museums or at the totem; spring-summer times Thursday-Sunday 9:30-13:30 and 15:30-19:30, autumn-winter Saturday and Sunday 9-14.

  • Where Corso Federico II di Svevia, Lagopesole, Avigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website melfivenosa.cultura.gov.it

Sources: melfivenosa.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it · comune.avigliano.pz.it · italia.it

Centro storico e palazzi nobiliari

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Piazzas, fountains and aristocratic buildings testify to the prosperity of the village between the Middle Ages and the modern era: the eighteenth-century Palomba and Doria palaces, the nineteenth-century Palazzo Labella and the Arch of the Piazza, medieval gate of the village; in the square dedicated to him the bronze monument to the lawyer Emanuele Gianturco. Historical districts have names such as Serritiello and Lavanga.

  • Where Avigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · basilicataturistica.it
