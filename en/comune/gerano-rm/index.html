What to see in Gerano

Borgo on Tufa soil of the Valle del Giovenzano, with a medieval historic center closed by doors, the churches of Santa Maria Assunta and San Lorenzo and the Palazzo di Corte; famous for the most ancient infiorata in Italy, told in the Museo dell'Infiorata, and for the House of the Ancient Tin Boxes.

6 places to visit in Gerano, in the province of Rome (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Casa delle Antiche Scatole di Latta

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Presented as the first museum in Italy dedicated to tin boxes, it collects over a thousand Italian boxes produced between 1890 and 1950, collected by Marina Durand de la Penne and ordered by themes, from the Sabaude wedding to the colonial countryside to the boom; between the Talmone and Venchi-Unica boxes pieces and a 1929 packaging signed by Gabriele d'Annunzio. Free visit by reservation; Visit Lazio times: Friday 10-13, Saturday and Sunday 10-13 and 15-19.

  • Where Via del Palazzo 8, Gerano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo dell'Infiorata

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated on April 26, 2003 for the feast of Our Lady of the Heart: the central room exhibits historical documents and ancient tools of the master inflorers, with the techniques and flowers used over time; another room collects the photographic archive of inflorate of Gerano, Italy and abroad, and a section is dedicated to traditional crafts and archaeological finds.

  • Where Via del Municipio, Gerano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A church of the tenth century in the heart of the village, with a bell tower of 25 meters and four bells in Gothic style, enlarged in the first half of the nineteenth century under the guidance of Valadier; it houses the Madonna of the Heart of Sebastiano Conca, who arrived in Gerano in 1729 at the center of the devotion of the infiorata.

  • Where Gerano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · comune.gerano.rm.it

Chiesa di San Lorenzo Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It dates back to the year 1000 and was renewed in the late eighteenth century; it preserves the Martyrdom of San Lorenzo by Domenico Fiorentino.

  • Where Gerano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Centro storico e Palazzo di Corte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Labyrinth of alleys built between the IX and X century, which is accessed by the gates Amato, Maggiore and Gate; the Palazzo di Corte of the fourteenth century was the seat of the court and the town hall, and in the square in front were held the poles "a candle"; in the village also the Fontana di Ciocio, Roman fountain of the second century.

  • Where Gerano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Sant'Anatolia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the sixth century near Gerano, where every July is celebrated the feast of the patroness with a centuries-old fair of goods and cattle.
