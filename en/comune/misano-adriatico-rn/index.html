Misano World Circuit Marco Simoncelli

Local life and traditions · Motors

The circuit named after Marco Simoncelli, seat of MotoGP (in September) and Superbike (in June), with guided tours in box, pit lane and race direction.

  • Where Via del Carro 30, Misano Adriatico
  • Entry To be checked: guided tours and events for a fee
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · riviera.rimini.it

Spiaggia di Misano e Portoverde

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Three kilometers of beach, the renovated seafront and the port of Portoverde in Mediterranean style.

  • Where Lungomare, Misano Adriatico
  • Entry Free beaches; establishments with fee
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
