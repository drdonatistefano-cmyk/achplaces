What to see in Abano Terme

With three thousand years of history and over one hundred thermal baths, Abano forms with Montegrotto the largest and oldest thermal resort in Europe, at the foot of the Euganean Hills: in the city the great sundial of Piazza del Sole e della Pace, the Cathedral of San Lorenzo, the hill of Montirone, the Museum Villa Bassi Rathgeb and the Museum of the Mask; on the heights the Monastery of San Daniele and the Sanctuary of Monteortone.

Museo Villa Bassi Rathgeb

Museums · Old masters

Villa veneta, formerly Secco, Dondi dall'Orologio and Zasio, built between 1566 and 1576 by the crescasco doctor Giovanni Antonio Secco, with loggia with three arches and two towers; the noble floor contains frescoes from the Metamorphosis of Ovidio (Scilla, Mercury and Argo, Apollo and Dafne, Kefalo and Procri), a hunting room and a room of Genesis, while the eighteenth-century oratory replicates the Holy House of Loreto and every 10 December a blade of light strikes the altar. Purchased by the Municipality in 1979, it houses the collection donated by Roberto Bassi Rathgeb, more than 400 works with paintings by Moretto, Moroni and Palma il Giovane, by Magnasco, Fra Galgario, Ceruti and the Longhi, and works by Lombardy of the nineteenth-nineteenth century; the museum was inaugurated in 2018 and also hosts photographic exhibitions.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.museovillabassiabano.it
