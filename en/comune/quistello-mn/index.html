What to see in Quistello

The town of Oltrepò Mantua sul Secchia, with the church of San Bartolomeo rebuilt in 1732, the Pinacoteca civica, the Romanesque-Mildic parish church of Nuvolato and the Museum dedicated to the artist Giuseppe Gorni.

5 places to visit in Quistello, in the province of Mantua (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Rebuilt in 1732 with the stones of the demolished castle, designed by Ticino Giovanni Maria Borsotto; inside a Via Crucis of the Mantua Lanfranco and a table with the Madonna delle Grazie with figures in relief.

  • Where Quistello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it · in-lombardia.it

Museo diffuso Giuseppe Gorni

Museums · Modern art

Dedicated to the Quistellan artist Giuseppe Gorni (1894-1975), architect and artist of international fame who in Nuvolato and nearby municipalities created sculptures, architecture and graphics; it is located in the former elementary schools G. Pascoli is designed and built by Gorni in 1929-1930, and tells about his career and his private life. Visits with the volunteers "Friends of the Gorni Museum."

  • Where Via Europa 58, Nuvolato di Quistello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museogorni.it

Sources: museogorni.it · comune.quistello.mn.it · turismo.mantova.it

Chiesa di San Fiorentino Martire a Nuvolato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A millenarian church in Romanesque-Maltildian style of the 11th century, with remains of frescoes from the 15th and 16th centuries; free access.

  • Where Nuvolato di Quistello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismo.mantova.it

Sources: turismo.mantova.it · comune.quistello.mn.it · in-lombardia.it

Parco delle Golene di Foce Secchia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Environmental and naturalistic path that follows the Secchia up to the confluence in the Po.

  • Entry Free
