What to see in Rocca Grimalda

Borgo dell'Alto Monferrato Ovadese which takes its name from the Grimaldi, lords from the sixteenth to the nineteenth century: on the spur above the valley of the Orba rises the castle with medieval tower and eighteenth-century garden, and the village is known for the Lachera, the ritual carnival told in the Museum of the Mask.

Castello di Rocca Grimalda

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On a spur that controlled the roads between the Ovadese Oltregiogo and the Alexandrian plain; the nucleus is a circular tower of the XII-XIII century, long prison, with five rooms connected by a helical staircase in the wall and the graffiti of the prisoners. Transformed into a residence by the Trotti in the mid-15th century and passed in 1570 to the Grimaldi, it preserves the late eighteenth-century chapel with trompe-l'oeil and stuccoes and a garden of the mid eighteenth-century in three parts (Italian, romantic grove, secret garden of medicinal herbs) with a view over the valley of the Orba. Guided tours by the owners at 15 and 16.30 on some Sundays from April to October.

  • Where Piazza Borgatta 2, Rocca Grimalda
  • Entry €15 with wine tasting, reduced €6 to 18 years
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: castelliaperti.it · alexala.it

Museo della Maschera

Museums · Themed

Born in 2000 to preserve the materials of Lachera, the late winter carnival that combines dance and theatre, with characters like Laché and Trapulin, music for violin and organ and a legend of revolt against the ius primae noctis. It exhibits costumes and masks of the Lachera and of Italian and European festivals, with temporary exhibitions in an ethnographic key; the archive has kept photos since 1909 and nineteenth-century scores.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Veneto 1, Rocca Grimalda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · alexala.it

Parrocchiale di San Giacomo Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of thirteenth century origin, restored in an important way at the end of the eighteenth century, with three naves, with an organ of 1845 in counterfaçade.

  • Where Rocca Grimalda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: alexala.it

Chiesa di Santa Limbania

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Dedicated to the patroness of the mule tracks, it rises on a hill at the south-eastern edge of the village; it could go back to the high Middle Ages, with modifications of the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries.

  • Where Margine sud-orientale del paese, Rocca Grimalda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: alexala.it

Belvedere Marconi e oratori del centro

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Terrace in the center of the village with views of the surrounding villages; near, the oratories of St. John the Baptist and of St. Mary of Graces keep processional chests of the eighteenth century.

  • Where Centro storico, Rocca Grimalda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.alexala.it

Sources: alexala.it
