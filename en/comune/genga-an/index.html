What to see in Genga

Borgo in the Park of the Red Gorge and Frasassi, famous for the Frasassi Caves, the Valadier Temple in the gorge, the Romanesque Abbey of San Vittore delle Chiuse and the castle of Genga with its museum.

9 places to visit in Genga, in the province of Ancona (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Genga in the guide with map and filters

Grotte di Frasassi

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry

Karst complex discovered in 1971, among the largest underground routes; the largest cavity seems to be able to contain the Duomo of Milan. The guided tour (Italian and English) lasts about an hour and touches the Sala Ancona and the Sala delle Candle; there are also speleological routes for about 2 and 3 hours.

  • Where Località La Cuna, Genga
  • Entry €20, reduced €18, 6-14 years €14, under 6 free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.frasassi.com
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: frasassi.com · italia.it · letsmarche.it

Tempio del Valadier ed Eremo di Santa Maria Infra Saxa

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Octagonal temple in white travertine with a lead-coated dome, desired in 1828 by Pope Leo XII and designed by Giuseppe Valadier in a cave of the Throat of Frasassi; behind the hermitage, quoted in 1029, partly dug into the rock and once Benedictine cloistered monastery. It is reached with about 700 meters of paved path.

  • Where Gola di Frasassi, Genga
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Abbazia di San Vittore delle Chiuse

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Among the most important Romanesque churches in the Marche region, with Byzantine influences, probably of the 11th century on Roman remains and restored between 1925 and 1932: facade with bell tower and cylindrical climbing tower, almost square interior with three naves with four cylindrical pillars that hold the dome.

  • Where Via San Vittore 5, San Vittore, Genga
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Museo speleopaleontologico ed archeologico

Museums · Science and university
