What to see in Predosa

Country on the left bank of the Orba stream, disputed in the fifteenth century between Sforza, Spinola and Visconti and Milanese outpost towards Monferrato and Genoa; its territory is part of the Natural Reserve of Torrente Orba, established in 1987.

Riserva naturale del Torrente Orba

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Reserve of about 257 hectares established in 1987 between Predosa, Bosco Marengo, Capriata d'Orba and Casal Cermelli, also ZSC and SPA: riparian woods of willows and poplars, oaks, small grets and arid prairies on the first terraces, between fields and poplars. There are reported more than 200 species of birds, blooms protected as snowshoe and orchids, the dragonfly Oxygastra curtisii and the return of the wolf; the visitor center with ornithological museum is in the municipal mill of Bosco Marengo. Dogs only on a leash.

Borgo di Retorto

History and architecture · Villages

On the banks of the Orba, it is a closed agglomeration formed by a central noble courtyard with palace, church and park, to which four rustic courts have been added over the centuries. It was the center of a vast feud spread over the municipalities of Predosa, Alexandria, Bosco and Fresonara, with numerous farms, meadows, pastures and streams; still today it is surrounded by cultivated fields. It was opened on FAI Spring Days 2016, 2017 and 2018.
