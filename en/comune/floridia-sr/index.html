What to see in Floridia

Center of the Iblean plateau near Syracuse, rebuilt after the earthquake of 1693: on the People's Square the Mother Church of St. Bartholomew of 1748 and the MUDAC of contemporary art, in Piazza Umberto I the Ethnographic Museum "Nunzio Bruno" on the Iblean peasant civilization.

Museo etnografico «Nunzio Bruno», civiltà contadina iblea

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

It documents the life of workers and craftsmen of the Iblean plateau: the collection was born in the early 1970s with photographer and artist Nunzio Bruno and grew with the help of the ethnologist Antonino Uccello; established in 2001 by Province and Municipality, it is open since May 2004. Six climbs on the ground floor on wheat processing, breeding, pasteurization and crafts, with the carrador shop and a lathe of 1880.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I 27, Floridia
  • Entry €2.50, reduced €1.50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.floridia.sr.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.floridia.sr.it

Chiesa Madre di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt after the earthquake of 1693 with the funds of Duke Ignazio Migliaccio since 1748, on the current Piazza del Popolo; facade with convex cylindrical tower, Tuscan columns and mechanical clock. The three bronze portals of Romeo Sandrin (2003-2004) depict the Old and New Testaments, the life of Mary and the sacraments.

  • Where Via Francesco Crispi 1, Floridia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.floridia.sr.it

Sources: comune.floridia.sr.it

MUDAC, Museo di Arte Contemporanea

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

Municipal museum in the palace on Piazza del Popolo, with permanent works by artists of the figurative arts, temporary exhibitions and meetings; open Tuesday-Friday 9-13 and 15-18, Saturday and Sunday 10-13 and 16-19, Monday closed.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Floridia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.floridia.sr.it

Sources: comune.floridia.sr.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built between 1884 and 1895 by the Franciscan Tertiaries on land donated by the priest Raffaele Ierna; on the high altar the wooden statue of St. Francis of the Palermo Rosario Bagnasco. Parish since 1951.

  • Where Via Garibaldi 216, Floridia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.floridia.sr.it

Sources: comune.floridia.sr.it
