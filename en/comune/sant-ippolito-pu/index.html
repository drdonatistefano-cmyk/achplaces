What to see in Sant'Ippolito

The village of the stonemasons is located on the hills of Metauro, where the sandstone has been working since the fourteenth century: the center is an open-air museum of portals, niches and carved frames, with the Museum of the territory, the Montefeltresca fortress and, in the surroundings, the walled castles of Sorbolongo and Reforzate.

Centro storico, il paese degli scalpellini

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Founded by the Fossombronesi between the 6th and 7th centuries, the village is described as an open-air museum: almost every house has niches, portals, shelves, frames and decorations carved by stonemasons, active here at least from the 14th century. Rocca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Rocca probably built by Federico da Montefeltro on a project by Francesco di Giorgio Martini: they remain the base of the mast and the sandstone wall, still intact, from which you can enjoy a very wide panorama.

  • Where Sant'Ippolito
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Borgo di Sorbolongo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village at 359 meters, about 5 km from the capital on the Mondaviese road, already of Fano and autonomous municipality until the beginning of the twentieth century: you enter from a small square closed by the former town hall with the iron clock tower; in the square the church of San Michele with works of the stonemasons, then houses in sandstone, alleys, arches and escarpted walls with views from the Apennines to the Adriatic. In May the Snail Festival.

  • Where Sorbolongo, Sant'Ippolito
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sources: comune.santippolito.pu.it · eventi.turismo.marche.it

Castello di Reforzate

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The castle, 324 metres away, belonged to the city of Fano, which preserves high escarpment walls; in mid-July it hosts the historical reenactment of Trebbiatura.
