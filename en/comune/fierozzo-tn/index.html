What to see in Fierozzo

Municipality of the Mòcheni valley, the valley of German-speaking settlers established since the thirteenth century: here the farm Filzerhof of the fifteenth century and the sawmill De sog van Rindel, sections of the Museum of the Mòcheni valley, and the paths to the Gronlàit, the Fravòrt and the Valcava.

3 places to visit in Fierozzo, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Maso Filzerhof

Museums · Themed

Large rural house of the fifteenth century, section of the Bersntoler Museum/Museum of the Mòcheni valley restored by the Mòcheni Cultural Institute, which illustrates the scattered settlement in isolated farms typical of the valley: house on two floors with cellar, stables, aia, barn and barn, and around vegetable garden, fertilization, fields, meadows, woods and pastures. Open at 15-17.30: in May and September on Sundays, in June Tuesdays, Thursdays, Saturdays and Sundays, in July and August every day except Mondays. Suitable for families and schools.

  • Where Poco a valle della strada Fierozzo-Palù, Fierozzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: cultura.trentino.it · visittrentino.info

Segheria De sog van Rindel

Museums · Themed

Section of the Museum of the Mòcheni valley dedicated to the wood supply chain: the route follows the cutting of trees and the transport of trunks along the slopes of the valley to the sawmill on the Balkof river, in operation until the end of the fifties to make tables for families in the area. Around larch and fir woods, pastures and huts. Open on Sundays at 15-17.30, only in July and August.

  • Where Località Rindel, Fierozzo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: cultura.trentino.it · visittrentino.info

Masi di San Francesco, Gronlàit e Valcava

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the hamlet of San Francesco you climb to the foot of Mount Gronlàit among farms still intact with the roofs in wooden dorms; from the Imperial Prati the path to the Valcava, lying out of golden larch trees. The Gronlàit and the Fravòrt are the two peaks of the municipality over 2000 meters.

  • Where Frazione San Francesco, Fierozzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info
