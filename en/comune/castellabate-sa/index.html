What to see in Castellabate

Medieval village of Cilento, 'balcone sul mare', born around the castle of which the Abbot Costabile Gentilcore laid the first stone on October 10 1123: alleys, arches and staircases in grey stone, the basilica of Santa Maria de Giulia and the Museum of sacred art; on the coast the hamlet of Santa Maria with the Port of the Cats and Villa Matarazzo, and the Marine Area protected up to Punta Licosa, linked to the myth of the mermaid Leucosia.

Castello dell'Abate

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On top of the village, wanted by St. Costabile Gentilcore, IV abbot of the Badia di Cava, who laid the first stone on October 10, 1123 to defend population and navigation from Saracen raids: four round corner towers and walls that contained houses, warehouses, ovens and cisterns. In the seventeenth century he lost his defensive function and became a noble palace. The visit, about an hour long, includes the Antiquarium Museum, the stables, the Norman Room and the courtyard, the Mastio Tower, the Aragonese walkway and the Municipal Library.

Centro storico e piazza belvedere

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village preserves the medieval structure, with narrow alleys, sudden stretches, arches, stairs and houses in grey stone; the main square, rectangular, looks to the valley to the sea of Licosa. Villa Principe di Belmonte and Villa Matarazzo are located at the two ends of the village.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Basilica di Santa Maria de Giulia

Places of worship · Basilicas

A sixteenth-century façade with a four-storey bell tower, inside a nave and two aisles: it preserves an anonymous author of San Michele Arcangelo and an altar triptych by Pavanino da Palermo dated 1472.

Museo di arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

Paintings, statues, furnishings, vestments and silvers from the 16th to the 20th century, linked to devotion to the patron and founder Costabile Gentilcore: cups, pissides and patenes dated from 1586 to 1895, an ostensory and a crown of the eighteenth century, parchments of the sixteenth century, portraits of canons, Neapolitan nativity figures from the seventeenth to the nineteenth century, a pivial of about 1700 and statues including a wooden Madonna of the sixteenth century.

Santa Maria di Castellabate e Porto delle Gatte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest village of the municipality, on the hill Sant'Angelo: a village of fishermen and farmers from the eighteenth century, a seaside resort from the sixties; in September 1943 the Allies landed there. The historic centre between Piazza Lucia and Piazza Pio was called Isca delle Guitarre; there are the nineteenth-century Villa Matarazzo, the eighteenth-century Palazzo Belmonte, the Norman-Aragonese tower of Pagliarola and the marina of the Cats, whose arches at night, seen from the sea, look like cat eyes.

  • Entry Free

Mostra permanente «Mare Antico»

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

Set up in the historic Villa Matarazzo, it exhibits a small collection of finds recovered in the backdrops in front of it, especially amphorae and anchors of Etruscan, Greek, Roman and Byzantine age. Closed in January and February.

  • Where Villa Matarazzo, Corso Sen. Matarazzo, Santa Maria di Castellabate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Area marina protetta Santa Maria di Castellabate

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Established in 2009 in the Cilento National Park: posidonia prairies, the flysch of Cilento with stratified and colored rocks that host groupers, moray eels and lobsters, and an underwater archaeological heritage. In San Marco, the Roman breakwater submerged in the ancient Greek-Roman port is visible at the modern port; off Punta Licosa the remains of a maritime villa and the cargo of a Roman ship sunk in the first century BC, with more than one hundred vinarian amphorae.

  • Where San Marco di Castellabate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Punta Licosa

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Promontory wooded Mediterranean maquis at the foot of Mount Licosa, with small beaches of rocks and pebbles over the pine forest and an islet with lighthouse, linked to the myth of the mermaid Leucosia. Cars are reserved for residents: you arrive on foot or by bike from the path of San Marco, almost everything on the floor (about 50 minutes per way), with views of the Gulf of Salerno, or by boat; some stretches of coast are private.

  • Where Sentiero da San Marco di Castellabate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it
