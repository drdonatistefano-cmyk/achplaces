What to see in Conversano

Art cities on the hills southeast of Bari, County of Acquaviva: the Norman castle with the Pinacoteca Finoglio and the Benedictine monastery of San Benedetto with the archaeological museum.

3 places to visit in Conversano, in the province of Bari (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Conversano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Norman fortress of about 1054, on megalithic walls of the VI-IV century BC, trapezoidal plan with a tower per corner: the Norman Master Tower about 25 meters high, the cylindrical tower of Giulio Antonio Acquaviva today symbol of the city and the polygonal tower for the artillery; from the XV century residence of the Acquaviva.

  • Where Corso Domenico Morea, Conversano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Monastero di San Benedetto e Museo Civico Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Tenth century complex with Romanesque church, medieval cloister of the XI-XIII and two bell towers, a Norman and a Baroque maiolic; for centuries home of mitrated abbesses, it houses the museum with the artifacts of the city from prehistory to late antiquity.

  • Where Via San Benedetto, Conversano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it
