What to see in Stazzano

Country at the confluence of Borbera in Scrivia, near the ancient Roman city of Libarna, between the valley Scrivia and the Val Borbera. To see the Civic Museum of Natural History in Villa Gardella and the Sanctuary of Our Lady of Monte Spineto, on the hill that dominates the village.

Museo civico di storia naturale

Museums · Science and university

In the Villa Gardella, donated to the Municipality in 1964, it is managed by the Naturalisti Stazzano Group (founded in 1974) and opened to the public around 1980; the fifth room, dedicated to botany, is 1996. It exhibits mainly material of the Ligurian-Piedmontese area: rocks, minerals, fossils, insects, fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds and mammals; it preserves over 5,000 leaves of herbarium, including an eighteenth-century herbarium, and more than 10,000 insects. Open on Saturday 15-18, otherwise by appointment (tel. 0143 65303.

Santuario di Nostra Signora di Monte Spineto

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

On the top of Mount Spineto (459 m), where the inhabitants took refuge in 1155 when a department of Federico Barbarossa sacked and burned Stazzano: in thanksgiving they built a chapel to the Madonna. The sanctuary was wanted by Monsignor Paolo Arese, Bishop of Tortona from 1620 to 1644; today it has three naves and three altars. According to the municipality, the view that you can enjoy on clear days is enough alone to justify the climb.
