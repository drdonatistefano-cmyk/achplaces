What to see in Syracuse

The city of Archimede, UNESCO heritage: the island of Ortigia with the Duomo born from the temple of Athena, the Temple of Apollo, the Fountain Aretusa and the Castle Maniace; on land the Greek Theatre, the side rooms with the Orecchio di Dionisio, the Museum Paolo Orsi, the catacombs and the Castle Eurialo.

16 places to visit in Syracuse, in the province of Syracuse (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Excavated in the Temenite hill and facing the sea, expanded by Ierone II in the third century BC by a theatre of the V; of the 67 rows of the cavea remain 46; every May-June hosts the cycle of classical shows.
