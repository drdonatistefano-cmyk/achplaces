What to see in Porcari

Country of the Piana di Lucca on the ancient lake of Sesto, historical cross between Via Francigena and Via Cassia, mentioned as Castrum Porcari from 780; dominates the village the white church of San Giusto at the foot of the hill of Torretta, while in the plain there is the archaeological area of Fossa Nera with finds from the Bronze Age to the Roman era.

Chiesa di San Giusto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

The main church of the village, in white marble and in a raised position, was built from the 15th century where there was a church dedicated to St. John; the current structure with three naves was completed in 1745, the neo-medieval façade dates back to 1884 and the frescoes by Michele Marcucci in the early twentieth century. Next to the bell tower and the square, from which you can see the Piana di Lucca; the patron festival falls on Monday after Pentecost.

  • Where Erta della Chiesa 4, Porcari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Area archeologica di Fossa Nera

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the "Piana delle centeno farme" it preserves traces of a village of the Bronze Age (about 1200 BC, with a decorated fibula, a torques and Baltic amber beads), a nucleus of Etruscan houses around 500 BC with Etruscan and Greek vinarium amphorae, and two Roman farms: Black Fossa A, of the II century BC, with a calcatorium for wine, and Black Fossa B, dedicated to weaving, cheese and wine. The site has dedicated tours.

  • Where Località Fossa Nera, Porcari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Aule didattiche archeologiche e mostra permanente di Fossa Nera

Museums · Archaeological

On the first floor of the former Collegio Cavanis, seat of the municipal library, the classrooms inaugurated in 2014 host workshops on the archaeology of the Piana di Lucca with diorami and a small permanent exhibition of finds, in copy and original; the originals come from the excavations of Fossa Nera.

  • Where Ex Collegio Cavanis, Porcari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: biblioteca.comunediporcari.org · visittuscany.com

Fondazione Lazzareschi

Museums · Themed

Founded in 2003, it organizes exhibitions, fairs, conferences and concerts every year; it is located in the center, next to the Town Hall, in a bright and transparent contemporary building that creates a light room around the exhibition spaces, dedicated to the memory of Giuseppe Lazzareschi.

  • Where Piazza F. Orsi 21, Porcari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.fondazionelazzareschi.it

Sources: fondazionelazzareschi.it · visittuscany.com
