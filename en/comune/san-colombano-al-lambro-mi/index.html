What to see in San Colombano al Lambro

Enclave of the Metropolitan City of Milan between Lodi and Pavia, on the hill surrounded by Lambro, the village is dominated by the medieval castle of Belgiojoso, where in 1353 Petrarca stayed; on the slopes of the hill, isolated in the plain, the wine San Colombano DOP is produced, and at Palazzo Patigno there is the Palaeontological and Archaeological Museum "Virginio Caccia" . Here was born Blessed Don Carlo Gnocchi: a tombstone in Via Vittoria marks the birthplace.

Castello Belgiojoso

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Medieval manor to guard the area, destroyed and then rebuilt by Federico Barbarossa in the twelfth century; it remains the fifteenth-century tower of entrance, in front of the parish, the western tower of the fifteenth-century called Torre Castellana or Torre Grossa and part of the walls, which enclose the most evocative stretch of the village. It belonged to Toriani, Visconti and Belgiojoso, with a share of the monks of the Certosa of Pavia; the Belgiojoso lived there until about 1960, then the property passed to the parish. A plaque recalls the stay of Francesco Petrarca in 1353.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Colombano Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Founded in 1499, commissioned according to the tradition by Pope Julius II, it was enlarged with side chapels and in the second quarter of the nineteenth century assumed the neoclassical structure of the façade. Inside the altarpiece of the Crucifixion and sixteenth-century frescoes by Bernardino Campi coming from the oratory of the castle (commissioned by the Carthusians in 1576, the oratory was demolished in 1836) and fifteenth-century cibories in the presbytery.

Borgo storico, Portone di Borgo Ratto e oratorio di San Rocco

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

At the village you enter from the Portone di Borgo Ratto, 1691. In the center the church of San Giovanni Battista, of late Romanesque foundation with Baroque appearance, the fifteenth century church of San Francesco with unfinished facade and the seventeenth century Palazzo Patigno, seat of the municipal offices. At the intersection of via Mazzini, via Vittoria and via Steffenini, incorporated in the park of a villa, the private oratory of San Rocco (early 16th century), brick, square outside and octagonal inside, attributed to Giovanni Baptaggio and Giovanni Antonio Amadeo with references to Bramante: it is not open to the public and can be seen from the outside.

Museo paleontologico e archeologico «Virginio Caccia»

Museums · Archaeological

In the seventeenth century Palazzo Patigno, since 1927 it has collected fossils of the ancient sea and finds that tell the origins and history of the Colle di San Colombano. The paleontological section includes shells of small molluscs and marine invertebrates, remains of Homo sapiens, the skull of a rhinoceros of steppes, remains of elephant, Bos taurus and Bison priscus; the archaeological one goes from the age of Copper to the Roman, medieval and post-medieval age, with the partial reconstruction of a Roman chamber tomb. Visits by appointment (tel. 0371 2931).

Collina di San Colombano e vino San Colombano DOP

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Viewpoint

An isolated relief of about 75 metres in the Po Valley, between Lambro and Olona, covered with vineyards and protected as the San Colombano Hill Park. On the slopes are cultivated Croatina, Barbera and Uva Rara, base of San Colombano DOP (frizzante, firm and reserve); the first reference to the cultivation of the hill dates back to 1371, with the reclamation started by Galeazzo Visconti, and in the nineteenth century these wines were compared to those of Valtellina. Itinerary of reference: the Strada del Vino San Colombano e dei Sapori Lodigiani.
