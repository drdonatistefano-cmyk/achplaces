What to see in Zavattarello

Medieval stone village of Oltrepò Pavese dominated by the castle of the captain of Ventura Jacopo Dal Verme, with walls over four meters thick, a contemporary art museum and a natural reserve of over 80 hectares; in the village the church of São Paulo, the fourteenth-century oratory of San Rocco and the octagonal cemetery.

Castello Dal Verme

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Born as a military outpost and for six centuries of the Dal Verme, it is a large quadrilateral building above the village and the valley, with stone walls thick up to more than four meters, about forty rooms connected by a system of internal stairs and a view from the terraces and the tower up to the plain of Padana. It rises in a nature reserve of over 80 hectares.

open from April to October, entrance only with guided tour fee (in-Lombardy)

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Museo di arte contemporanea Giuseppe e Titina Dal Verme

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

From 2003 to the last floor of the castle, he exhibited Italian art from the second half of the twentieth century to today, born from the donations of the artists who exhibited there, and paintings of Count Giuseppe Dal Verme, to whom he is entitled with his wife Titina Gavazzi.

Borgo medievale, mura e Su di Dentro

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the central square, surrounded by stone buildings, you walk along the medieval village, partly surrounded by walls and crossed by a narrow street and many passages; under the castle, towards the valley, there is the Su di Dentro, once accessible by a stone arch, where lived the shoemakers to which the name of the village is linked.

  • Where Piazza centrale, Zavattarello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Paolo e cimitero ottagonale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church with baroque façade of the eighteenth century, which preserves a wooden altar with altarpiece, paintings and relics of saints; behind it the octagonal cemetery, built at the beginning of the nineteenth century by the will of Carlo Alberto di Savoia, with the chapel of the Dal Verme.

Il Magazzino dei Ricordi

Museums · Themed

Museum of art, culture and agriculture curated by an association founded in 2004, which reconstructs the daily peasant and artisan life of the Oltrepò between the Eight and twentieth century: peasant cuisine, tailoring, barberia, school and shoemaker shops, blacksmith and blacksmith.

open only on Saturdays and holidays 15:30-18; different address between MiC (Via Vittorio Emanuele) and in-Lombardy (Cascina Mirani)
