What to see in Challand-Saint-Victor

First possession of the accounts of Challant, a small town of hamlets between chestnuts and walnuts, with the lake of Villa and the ruins of the family castle.

Castello di Villa

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The oldest manor of the Challant, built around the 10th century and completed in the first fourteenth century by Ebalo I; ruins in the reserve of Lake Villa, 30 minutes walk from the parking lot.

  • Where Villa, Challand-Saint-Victor
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

  • Entry Free
