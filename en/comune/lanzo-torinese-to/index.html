What to see in Lanzo Torinese

Medieval village in the heart of the Lanzo Valleys, famous for the fourteenth-century Devil's Bridge on the Stura and the Giant's Marmittes; in the historic center the Aymone Gate and Civic Tower of Challant and the Castle Tower, as well as small museums dedicated to minerals, rocks, textile art and mountaineering. Here, according to tradition, a Lanzian doctor invented the breadsticks for the Sabauda court.

Ponte del Diavolo e Marmitte dei Giganti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ordered in 1378 by the Credenza di Lanzo to connect the village to the right bank of the Stura, with the support of the Green Count, who followed the project, it is about 16 meters high with a single back span of donkey of about 37 meters between the mountains Basso and Buriesco; until the carriage of 1820 was forced passage between valleys and plain. The legend that the devil wants it to work is linked to the Marmitte dei Giganti, prehistoric cavities dug by the water in the rocks, seen as the pots of the devil; at the entrance the chapels of San Rocco and San Giacinto, on the opposite side the panoramic point Â"Madonna degli AlpiniÂ" .

Porta e Torre Civica Aymone di Challant

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Ancient entrance to the city, it takes its name from Aymone di Challant, a castle that ruled Lanzo for the Savoys in the mid-th century; it was accessed on a drawbridge beyond the moat and remains the hinges of the gate and the traces of the saracinesche. The façade has a pointed arch, ghibelline blackbirds and the coats of arms of the Savoy and the Estensi. Visible only from the outside.

  • Where Via San Giovanni Bosco 1, Lanzo Torinese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Centro storico e Torre del Castello

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The historic centre of the village, founded in the Middle Ages, preserves evidence of that era; it dominates the Castle Tower, dating back to the 11th century.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Museo Mineralogico delle Valli di Lanzo

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Managed by the mineralogical group of the Lanzo Valleys, it exhibits about 150 minerals of the valleys in six windows: one petrological and others dedicated to the mine of Balangero, the Valley of Ala, the Valley of Viù and the minerals of Canavese and Valle d'Aosta, with photos of the Amientifera of Balangero and a geological map of the Western Alps. Times to be agreed with the association.

  • Where Via San Giovanni Bosco 17, Lanzo Torinese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Museo dell'Utensileria Silmax

Museums · Themed · Free entry

The company museum in a building of the late eighteenth century where Paul Alessio's tools were produced since 26 November 1819, taken over in 1955 by Silmax; restored since 1990, it exhibits on three floors lathes, cutters, sharpeners and vintage tools that reconstruct the 19th century working environment and the history of mechanics in the Lanzo Valleys. Times to check.

  • Where Via Fucine 9, Lanzo Torinese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org · cultura.gov.it

Museo Etnografico-Laboratorio dell'Arte Tessile Lanzese «E. Fornara Borla»

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Civic museum re-assembled in the new headquarters in July 2021 according to the researches of Ester Fornara Borla, in three sections: tapestries and fabrics made between 1910 and 1935 by Elena Mars Albert and collaborators, a frame of late nineteenth century still working and a collection of linens, clothes and accessories between the end of the nineteenth and first half of the twentieth century. Visits only by reservation.

  • Where Via Leopoldo Usseglio 8, Lanzo Torinese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.lanzotorinese.to.it · turismotorino.org

Ecomuseo Storia dell'Alpinismo nelle Valli di Lanzo

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In the common room of the CAI headquarters of Lanzo, it collects skis from different eras, snowshoeing in wood, picks and climbing equipment used to climb the peaks of the valleys, from Ciamarella to Bessanese, from Levanne to Rocciamelon. Undedicated timetables.

  • Where Via San Giovanni Bosco 33, Lanzo Torinese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org
