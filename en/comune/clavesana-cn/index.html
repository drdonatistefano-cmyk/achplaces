What to see in Clavesana

Small village in the southernmost part of the Langhe, closed by the Tanaro's handles, between calanchi formed in millennia of erosion and vineyards of Dogliani DOCG; here, in the hamlet Gorrea, was installed in 2010 the first Big Bench of Chris Bangle, and in the ancient Town Hall the historical and ethnographic Museum is located.

Museo storico etnografico e di tradizione

Museums · Themed

Born from the generosity of the country and the work of a group of volunteers, it occupies ten rooms of the ancient Town Hall: wine production, agricultural work, the house, the sports of tradition, history and local characters. From April to October open on Sundays from 15 to 18 and by reservation; in winter and August only by reservation (tel. 0173 790103).

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele 7, Clavesana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · italia.it

Big Bench di Clavesana

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The first giant bench, from which the designer Chris Bangle started, installed in 2010 just a few meters from the home of his creator. Red and accessible to all, look over the mountain arch of the Cuneo mountains.

  • Where Borgata Gorrea, Clavesana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · italia.it

Calanchi di Clavesana e Sentiero Tanaro

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The territory is marked by calanchi excavated by erosion in millennia, surrounded by vineyards including those of Dogliani DOCG. From the locality of San Giuseppe starts stage 2 of the Tanaro Path, an easy route that from the landscape of the calanchi crosses hills and ancient villages up to Castellino Tanaro.

  • Where Località San Giuseppe, Clavesana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitpiemonte.com
