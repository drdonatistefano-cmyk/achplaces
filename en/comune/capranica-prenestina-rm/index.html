What to see in Capranica Prenestina

Medieval village about 1000 meters on the Prenestini Mountains, with the Church of Santa Maria Maddalena and the Palazzo Barberini seat of the Naturalistic Museum; in the territory the hamlet of Guadagnolo, the Sanctuary of Mentorella and the Castagneto Prenestino.

Palazzo Barberini e Museo civico naturalistico dei Monti Prenestini

Museums · Themed

The palace, built on the remains of a previous structure, preserves the architecture of the sixteenth century and was restored in the mid-18th century; it houses the Town Hall and the naturalistic museum, active for over twenty years, with digital dioramas on local habitats, environmental education and guided tours also to the educational garden in the municipal park. Accessible to people with disabilities.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village of narrow alleys about 1000 meters on the Prenestini Mountains, where in autumn it is remembered the traditional drying of chestnuts with the Festa della Mosciarella.

Santuario della Mentorella

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

At Guadagnolo, the highest town in Lazio (1218 m), the Marian shrine dates back to the XII-XIII centuries and is one of the oldest places of worship in Italy; entrusted since 1883 to the Polish Resurrectionist Fathers, it was visited several times by John Paul II. Nearby is the Cave of San Benedetto.

  • Where Mentorella, Guadagnolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlazio.com

Monumento naturale Castagneto Prenestino

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

A 166-hectare forest declared a natural monument in 2019, with centuries-old chestnuts, maples, oaks, ashes and lindens and the historic houses where the mosciarelle were dried, small sweet chestnuts.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Monte Guadagnolo

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Special Conservation Area of 570 hectares with arid prairies and over 35 species of wild orchids; the ridge walk from the village offers wide views of Prenestini and the limestone walls at 1218 meters are a historic site of climbing in Lazio.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
