What to see in Bagheria

Cities between the Gulf of Palermo and Termini Imerese, known for the villas of the Palermitan nobility built between the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries: Villa Palagonia with its stone monsters, Villa Cattolica with the Museum Renato Guttuso, Villa Valguarnera and Palazzo Butera; home of Guttuso and set of the films of Giuseppe Tornatore (Baarìa) and New Cinema Paradiso (New Cinema Paradiso).

12 places to visit in Bagheria, in the province of Palermo (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa Palagonia (Villa dei Mostri)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

Villa of 1715, known as "Villa dei Monstri" (Villa dei Monstri) and already the Grand Tour in the eighteenth century: the walls are decorated with statues of monsters, dragons and caricatures carved in Aspra stone; inside the Hall of Mirrors and rooms with mythological frescoes. Times indicated by the source: 1 April-31 October 9-13 and 16-19, 1 November-31 March 9-13 and 15.30-17.30, every day.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi 3, Bagheria
  • Entry €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website villapalagonia.it

Sources: villapalagonia.it · turismo.cittametropolitana.pa.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Villa Cattolica e Museo Renato Guttuso

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

A quadrangular villa built in 1736 by Francesco Bonanno, prince of Cattolica, with the It houses the Renato Guttuso Museum, officially established in 1990, with works by the Bagherese painter and other artists; in the garden Guttuso's tomb, the work of Giacomo Manzù. Open every day except Monday (winter 9-13.30 and 14.30-19, summer 9.30-14 and 15-19).

  • Where Via Rammacca 9 (SS 113), Bagheria
  • Entry €5, reduced €4
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.cittametropolitana.pa.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Villa Valguarnera

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The eighteenth-century complex of the Alliata princes of Villafranca, nestled in a beautiful garden, with a large oval salon of Vaccarini; Prince Francesco Alliata founded the Panaria Film.

  • Where Bagheria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Palazzo Butera

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The oldest noble residence of Bagheria, built by Giuseppe Branciforti, Prince of Pietraperzia and Count of Raccuja, with the inscription "O Corte a Dio" at the entrance; neoclassical forms, colonnade porch, picture gallery and halls frescoed with crystal chandeliers. From its terraces start the fires of the feast of Santa Rosalia on July 14.

  • Where Corso Butera, Bagheria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it
