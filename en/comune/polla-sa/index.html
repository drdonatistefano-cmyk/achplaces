What to see in Polla

The center of the Diano Valley on the Tanagro river, built around a Roman forum along the Annia street, of which remain the Lapis Pollae, the mausoleum of Caio Uziano Rufo and the Roman bridge; in the village the convent of Sant'Antonio and the civic museum of the ancient Pollesi clothes.

Museo civico «Insteia Polla» - Antiche vestiture pollesi

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Established in 1996 in the deconsecrated chapel of Santa Maria la Scala, it tells the story of Polla through the popular women's clothing between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, with thirteen reconstructions of clothes ordered for occasion (gala and parties, daily use, processions, half party, mourning), from the gala dress of the married woman to the ...suttanèlla, in 2009 the Campania Region recognized it as a museum of regional interest. Visit by reservation, about 45 minutes.

  • Where Via Santa Maria La Scala, Polla
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.polla.sa.it

Lapis Pollae (Elogium)

History and architecture · Archaeology

Second century B.C. marble slab (70 x 74 cm), found by chance in the seventeenth century in San Pietro, linked to the Roman road from Capua to Reggio, of which it appoints some stations; it had to be at the base of a commemorative monument of the street. The Municipality indicates it as the most important Roman testimony of the Vallo di Diano.

  • Where Località San Pietro, Polla
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.polla.sa.it

Sources: comune.polla.sa.it

Convento e santuario di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Ancient convent with portal of the sixteenth century; the nave is frescoed and the ceilings are decorated with canvases of the seventeenth century, in the context of pictorial cycles of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
