What to see in Piazza Armerina

Medieval town of the Sicilian hinterland with the Duomo on the highest point and, just outside, the Villa Romana del Casale, UNESCO heritage since 1997 for its mosaics.

7 places to visit in Piazza Armerina, in the province of Enna (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Piazza Armerina in the guide with map and filters

Villa Romana del Casale

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Villa of the 4th century A.D., probably Lucio Aradio Valerio Proculo, governor of Sicily: about 3,500 square meters of mosaics, including the Corridor of the Great Hunt with the capture of animals in Africa and the ten girls in bikini are engaged in sports exercises.

  • Where Contrada Casale, Piazza Armerina
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it · parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it

Duomo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the highest point of the city, an example of Sicilian baroque: it replaced a church of the fourteenth century and was rebuilt in the seventeenth century at the behest of Baron Marco Trigona.

  • Where Piazza Armerina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Palazzo Trigona – Museo della Città e del Territorio

Museums · City history

Palace of the late seventeenth century by Baron Trigona, now home to the city museum.

Parco minerario Floristella Grottacalda

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Around 400 hectares of sulphur mines active from the end of the eighteenth century to 1987: limestone, descendants and castles of extraction in an open-air museum of industrial archaeology.

  • Where Floristella
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
