Walser Museum

Museums · Themed

It tells the history and life of the Walser community of Alagna, people of Alemannian origin who colonized the valleys of Monte Rosa from the 13th century.

Case walser con le «lobbie»

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint
