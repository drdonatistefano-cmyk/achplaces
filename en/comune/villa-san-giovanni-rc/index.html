What to see in Villa San Giovanni

The "porta della Calabria" on the Strait of Messina, from which the ferries depart for Sicily: the Anthropological Naturalistic Museum in Palazzo Arena, the sixteenth-century Torre Cavallo on the cliffs towards Scilla, the seaside village of Cannitello with the felucas of the swordfish and the panoramic village of Piale.

Museo naturalistico antropologico

Museums · Science and university

Founded in 2002 and reopened in 2005 in the Art Nouveau Palace Arena, it is dedicated to geology, flora and marine and coastal fauna of the Strait and related traditions: 13 collections from about 150 to 2,000 specimens, including abyssal fish of the Strait, paleontological species studied by Giuseppe Seguenza and a collection of shells; the section Traditions folk tells about the hunting of the swordfish with documents, illustrations of the period and ancient tools. Torre Cavallo

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

A watchtower built around 1559 at the expense of the University of Scilla, part of the defensive system of the Strait, on the cliffs overhanging between Cannitello and Scilla; the name derives from the Latin Caput valleys. In the most recent era it served to spot the swordfish; it was several times in the census "The Places of the Heart" of the FAI. The Metropolitan City points to it as ruin.

  • Where Via Fiumara Alta 63, Villa San Giovanni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Cannitello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

A seaside village from which the typical Calabrian felucas leave for the fishing of the swordfish; from here you can see the Sicilian coast and the Aeolian Islands.

Traversata dello Stretto in traghetto

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

From Villa San Giovanni the ferries and hydrofoils to Messina leave continuously; during the crossing you can see the Calabrian and Sicilian coasts and the reliefs of Aspromonte and Etna.
