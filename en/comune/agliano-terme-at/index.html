What to see in Agliano Terme

Medieval village in a panoramic position about 19 km from Asti, known for the thermal springs of the Valley of the Fonti and the hills of Monferrato. Of the castle, destroyed in the seventeenth century, remains the tower rebuilt in Piazza Castello, now overlooking the vineyards up to the Monviso.

Torre dell'Acquedotto e Piazza Castello

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Of the castle, destroyed in the first half of the seventeenth century during the war between Savoy and Spanish for the succession of Monferrato, only the rebuilt tower remains; on the ruins rose the church of St. Peter, demolished in 1770. A plaque recalls the castle and Bianca Lancia di Agliano, mother of Manfredi king of Naples and Sicily cited by Dante in the third song of Purgatory, and a recent painting depicts it on one side. From the top you can see the hills, the roofs of the village and, on clear days, the Alps with the Monviso. For access the page refer to the Municipality (tel. 0141 954023).

Parrocchiale di San Giacomo Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church of the village took the place of the church of San Pietro, demolished in 1770; its bell tower, with that of the deconsecrated church of the Confraternity of San Michele, stands out on the roofs of the center.

Local life and traditions · Spas

Colline e vigneti di Agliano

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The hills around the village, which the municipality describes as 'great wrinkles of an ancient culture', are drawn by rows that change color with the seasons; among the heights there are Bric Rocche (283 m), Bric Lovera and Bric Moncucco. The Municipality suggests exploring them on foot, on horseback or by bike.
