What to see in Enna

The highest city in Sicily, almost a thousand meters away: the Lombardy Castle enlarged by Frederick II, the Fortress of Ceres with the Museum of Myth, the Duomo, the octagonal Tower of Frederick II and, 14 km away, the reserve of Lake Pergusa.

8 places to visit in Enna, in the province of Enna (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Enna in the guide with map and filters

Castello di Lombardia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Medieval fortress on the highest point of the city, in the ancient Acropolis: Frederick II expanded it from an Arab fortification renovated by the Normans; from the Pisan Tower, of the thirteenth century, the 360-degree panorama from almost a thousand meters.

  • Where Via Lombardia, Enna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Rocca di Cerere e Museo del Mito

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The remains of the sanctuary of the goddess Ceres on the same hill of the castle, with a view of the wheat fields; next to the multimedia Museum of the Myth. The Rock of Ceres Geopark has been recognized by UNESCO since 2015.

  • Where Enna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Duomo di Enna

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Dedicated to Mary Most Holy of the Visitation, wanted by Queen Eleonora d'Angiò: facade between Gothic and Sicilian Baroque, tower in facade and coffered ceiling in the central aisle.

  • Where Via Roma, Enna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Museo regionale interdisciplinare

Museums · Archaeological

The regional museum on Piazza Mazzini, opposite the Duomo.

  • Where Piazza Mazzini, Enna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Torre di Federico II

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Octagonal building on two floors isolated on a hill, attributed to Frederick II (1220-1250), once connected to the castle by a tunnel; a spiral staircase rises to the top with views up to Etna.

  • Where Enna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Porta di Janniscuru

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Gate of the Norman walls, reachable by alleys to steps from Via Mercato.

  • Where Via Mercato, Enna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Chiesa di San Marco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Baroque church with bare Carmelite convent, built on the remains of an ancient synagogue at the border of the Jewish ghetto.

  • Where Enna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Riserva naturale del lago di Pergusa

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Oval brackish lake and shallow about 14 km from the city, between reeds and eucalyptus: reserve since 1995 and stop for migratory birds heading to Africa, surrounded by a autodrome.

  • Where Pergusa, Enna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info
