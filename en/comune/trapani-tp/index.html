What to see in Trapani

The city of the sickle between two seas: the Tower of Ligny on the tip, the Clock Tower with the astronomical clock of 1596, the Mysteries of the Church of Purgatory, the Cathedral of San Lorenzo, the Pepoli Museum and the Madonna of Trapani at the Annunziata, the salt pans.

10 places to visit in Trapani, in the province of Trapani (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre di Ligny

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Built during the Spanish domination to defend the city from raids, it was the Navy's anti-air station in the Second World War.

  • Where Punta della città, Trapani
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Torre dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The oldest gate in the city, with one of the oldest astronomical watches in Europe, in 1596.

  • Where Centro storico, Trapani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Chiesa del Purgatorio e i Misteri

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

An eighteenth-century church that preserves the twenty sculptural groups of the Passion carried in procession on Good Friday.

  • Where Centro storico, Trapani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Fontana di Saturno

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Fountain of 1342 in the square of the church of Sant'Agostino, one of the three that distributed water from the slopes of Monte Erice.

  • Where Piazza Sant'Agostino, Trapani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Chiesa di Sant'Agostino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
