What to see in Montecastello

Borgo about 10 km from Alessandria, on the last leaps of the hills of the Basso Monferrato in front of the confluence between Tanaro and Bormida, dominated by the castle that was headquarters of Carlo Emanuele III in 1745.

Castello di Montecastello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built in the 13th century on the remains of the oldest fortifications and renovated in the 18th century, it dominates the village from the top of the hill with a high tower and is visible from kilometers in the Alexandrian plain. Ceded in 1180 by the Bellingers to the Municipality of Alexandria, disputed for centuries, infeudated in 1535 to the Milan Press and Sabaudo since 1707; in 1745, during the Battle of Bassignana, Charles Emanuel III fixed the headquarters there, and Charles Albert stayed there in 1842-1843.

private residence, it opens to the public on very rare occasions; the building is seen only from the outside (FAI)

Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta di Ponzano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish Church of the village, indicated by the Diocese of Alexandria as "Santa Maria di Ponzano in Montecastello" and cited by the FAI among the places of the village. Above the entrance door there is a wall organ of the Lombard school of organaria dated 1827 (inscription "Ottava basso - Monte Castello 1827"), with grey case with double pilasters, 27 canes in façade arranged with cusp, keyboard of 56 keys and 18 registers; it was reformed in 1931 by the organaro Edward Negri of Genoa Bolzano.
