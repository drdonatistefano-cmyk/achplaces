What to see in Vaiano

Borgo della Val di Bisenzio grew up around the Badia di San Salvatore, founded in 1073, with its museum; in the territory the Renaissance Villa Vai al Mulinaccio, the town-factory of Briglia, the parish church of Sofignano sulla Calvana and the woods of Monteferrato.

Badia di San Salvatore

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Monastic complex founded in 1073 by the Benedictines of Cluniac and passed to the Vallombrosans in 1090, around which the medieval village developed. The Romanesque church and the bell tower are from the 12th to the 11th century, the cloister is Renaissance and the complex also has baroque elements. Agnolo Firenzuola was abbot for a few years.

  • Where Piazza Baroni, Vaiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Museo della Badia di San Salvatore «Agnolo Firenzuola»

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated in 1993 and reopened in 1999 after the restoration, he recounts the life of the monks and the role of the ward with finds of excavations, objects of daily life and furnishings of the monastic liturgy: a fifteenth-century ciborium of the circle of Antonio Rossellino, the Commiate of Christ by the Mother attributed to Giacinto Gemignani, a great golden baroque ferculum and, in the Abbot's Chapel, original editions of the works of Agnolo Firenzuola.

  • Where Piazza Baroni 9, Vaiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Villa Vai al Mulinaccio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Villa built by Cosimo Sassetti between the late fifteenth and early sixteenth centuries, then Strozzi and Vai, which made it a estate of 36 farms; lived there by the navigator Filippo Sassetti and is a stop of the Houses of Memory. On the farm, the cucinone with fifteenth-century fireplace; the eighteenth-century walled garden has a nymph with mosaic niches and mother-of-pearl; the oratory of Sant'Antonio Abate (1722 about) has stuccoes and a altarpiece of Antonio Marini of 1845. In the park three large cedars, an ancient fruit pomary and a herbarium accessible to the blind.

Visitable only for events and organized tours

  • Where Via della Fattoria 7, Vaiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: pratoturismo.it · comune.vaiano.po.it · italia.it

La Briglia, città-fabbrica

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Former 19th century industrial city, the most important site in the valley: in the eighteenth century there was the largest paper mill in Italy, in the mid-nineteenth century a copper foundry, then the Lanificio Forti, which before the Second World War gave work to more than a thousand workers, with housing and recreational, cultural and welfare facilities for employees.

  • Where La Briglia, Vaiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.vaiano.po.it · visittuscany.com

Pista ciclabile Vaiano-Prato e archeologia industriale

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Cycle of 11.5 km on the left bank of the Bisenzio, along which you will find the Mulino della Viaccia, the oldest of Vaiano, the Lanificio Alphandery called the Macquinone, the wool mill of the island built in 1881 by Beniamino Forti with monumental gora, and the Cartaia, medieval mill activated as paper mill in 1371 by Pietro da Fabriano, then textile factory partly designed by Pier Luigi Nervi.

  • Where Vaiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.vaiano.po.it · visittuscany.com · italia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

At 410 meters above the village of Sofignano, it is one of the four parishes of the Val di Bisenzio; near the fifteenth-century village of Fornaci with a consort olive oil mill.

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

Monti della Calvana da Sofignano

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Itinerary from the Pieve di Sofignano to San Leonardo between medieval villages and pastoral places: the Buca del Tasso, a horizontal cave over 65 meters long, the Passo del Mandrione (841 m) towards Mugello, the hawthorn called Spina Vagliucci, 8 meters high and about 400 years old, the travertine of the Sasso Spugnoso on the river Nosa, Savignano with the church of Saints Andrea and Donato and Parmigno with the chapel of Santo Stefano frescoed in the fifteenth century.

  • Where Sofignano, Vaiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.vaiano.po.it · italia.it

Monteferrato e Monte Javello da Schignano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the protected area of Monteferrato Mount Javello, about 900 meters, is the highest point, with beech woods and a partisan command in the Second World War; near the Ebani, born from the reforestation of Giuseppe Vaj in the early nineteenth century, and the monumental cork on the road to Migliana. In Schignano the visitor center, the church of San Martino and the scenic road of 1967 towards Vaiano.
