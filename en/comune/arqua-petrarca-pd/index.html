What to see in Arquà Petrarca

Medieval village of the Euganean Hills where Francesco Petrarca lived the last years and died in 1374: you visit his house, the tomb in red marble in front of the church of Santa Maria Assunta and, just outside, the Laghetto della Costa, a UNESCO World Heritage site.

Casa del Petrarca

Museums · House museums · Paid entry · Viewpoint

House of the thirteenth century that preserves much of the fourteenth-century structure, with sixteenth-century loggia (1546); the rooms were frescoed in the sixteenth century with scenes inspired by Canzoniere and Africa. Petrarca moved there in 1369-70 and died in July 1374: you can see the study, the library, the chair and the mummified cat, with a permanent exhibition on the life and work of the poet.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Tomba del Petrarca e chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

On the churchyard of Santa Maria Assunta, in the main square, is the tomb of the poet, an ark in red marble of Verona inspired by the Roman sarcophagi, where Petrarch was buried after his death in 1374.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Arquà Petrarca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · veneto.eu

Oratorio della Santissima Trinità e piazza San Marco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Oratory already existing in 1181, where Petrarch went to pray; on the square a Venetian lion watches.

  • Where Piazza San Marco, Arquà Petrarca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Centro storico, via Roma e Villa Alessi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The name comes from Arquata montium, for its position on the slopes of two hills; the medieval system is intact, with paved streets, porticoes and palazzetti in Venetian style. Via Roma, with shops and buildings from the 13th and 14th centuries, leads to Villa Alessi, an ancient bishop's seat that today hosts events and concerts.

  • Where Via Roma, Arquà Petrarca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · veneto.eu

Museo di pianoforti antichi

Museums · Themed

In Villa Centanin, about forty instruments (fortepiani, grand pianos, table and vertical) mainly of the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, of German, English, French and Italian builders, plus about 120 ancient music-themed prints from the sixteenth to the nineteenth century. The instruments are restored to play as originally used in concerts of the Fondazione Musicale Masiero and Centanin.

  • Where Via degli Ulivi 12, Arquà Petrarca
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: venetoaroundme.regione.veneto.it · italia.it

Laghetto della Costa

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The largest natural water of the Euganean Hills, fed by thermal springs, with water at 17-18 degrees even in winter and reeds, willows and cypresses along the banks. There was a village inhabited mainly between 2200 and 1300 B.C., part of the UNESCO site of the stilts; the finds are at the National Museum Atestino of Este and at the Archaeological Museum of Padua.

  • Where Arquà Petrarca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcocollieuganei.com · museiveneto.cultura.gov.it · veneto.eu · italia.it
