What to see in San Bonifacio

San Bonifacio has its most important monument in the Romanesque abbey of San Pietro a Villanova, with frescoes from the Giotto school and a crypt with 24 columns, which also houses the Geopaleontological Civic Museum; besides the Alpone stands the parish church of Sant'Abbondio alla Motta, of the twelfth century, and in the hamlet Prova the seventeenth-century Villa Carlotti.

Abbazia di San Pietro a Villanova

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

The most important monument of San Bonifacio, Romanesque, was rebuilt in 1138 by Abbot Uberto after the earthquake of 1117 and restored at the end of the fourteenth century and in the mid eighteenth century by the Olivetani. It has three aisles and three apses, facade in tuff and terracotta with baroque portal, frescoes of the Giotto school on the life of San Benedetto, a fresco of the fourteenth century of Santa Caterina, a choir in walnut of the fifteenth century, a crypt with 24 columns as wide as the church, Roman finds in the floor and a bell tower-fortility of 1131 with Gothic triples; the cloister preserves Gothic arches of the first fifteenth century. Opening hours: Monday-Saturday 9.30-11.30, Sunday 15-17 (September-March) and 15-18 (April-August).

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Museo civico geopaleontologico Abate Don Giuseppe Dalla Tomba

Museums · Science and university · Free entry

Born from the collection donated to the Municipality in 1984 by Don Giuseppe Dalla Tomba, Abbot of Villanova from 1939 to 1980, and set up from 2003 in the abbey complex designed by the geologist Enrico Castellaccio: 21 windows in two rooms illustrate the geology of the territory and fossils of all ages, with specimens of Bolca, and 78 minerals. Guided tours by appointment with volunteers.

  • Where Piazza San Benedetto 1, Villanova, San Bonifacio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: comune.sanbonifacio.vr.it · visitverona.it

Pieve di Sant'Abbondio alla Motta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Small church matrix of the parish, at the foot of the hill of Motta beyond the Alpone, built in the second half of the XII century with a single nave with mixed brick and stone walls like the great churches of Verona of the time; before it the assembly of the free municipality documented in 1208 was gathered. It contains apse frescoes between the end of the fourteenth and early fifteenth centuries with an Annunciation, a fifteenth-century Saint Agatha, Madonnas and votive saints of 1491 and eighteenth-century wooden statues. Open on the second Sunday of the month, 9.30-10.30, with voluntary guides; suggested free offer.

  • Where Via Castello, San Bonifacio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it

Sources: visitverona.it

Villa Carlotti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The main house is probably sixteenth-century of the Horses, bought in 1680 by the Marquises Giulio and Bonaventura Carlotti and transformed between 1670 and 1711; the loggia has date 1704 and the octagonal chapel of San Biagio, of 1695, has an altarpiece of 1731. The original frescoes are no longer there and the rustic court is still in agricultural use.

  • Where Prova, San Bonifacio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitverona.it
