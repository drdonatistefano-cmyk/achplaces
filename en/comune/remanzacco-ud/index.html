What to see in Remanzacco

Municipality of the Friulian plain to the east of Udine, crossed by the municipal park of the Tower and Malina, which connects Cerneglons, Remanzacco and Ziracco between streams, stable meadows, mills and villas; in the old workshop Tonutti a museum tells a century of agricultural machines and houses vintage cars and motorcycles.

3 places to visit in Remanzacco, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo "Gino Tonutti"

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Housed in the sheds of the old factory (the 'farie') of Remanzacco, museum after in 1965 the production Tonutti moved to a new place, preserves machinery and atmospheres of that time and documents a century of production of agricultural tools and machines. Four sections: industrial archaeology (machines and tools, some still working), agricultural (with a steam engine operating a threshing machine), about eighty vintage cars from 1915 to today (including a Ford Model T, Rolls Royce, a DeLorean, a Ferrari F40 and Lamborghini) and about 75 motorcycles from the early twentieth century to the eighties. Open every day 9-17, without reservation; guided tours.

  • Where Via Udine, Remanzacco
  • Entry €8, reduced €6
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.tonutti.it

Sources: tonutti.it · cultura.gov.it · comune.remanzacco.ud.it

Parco comunale del Torre e del Malina

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established in 2015 (regional decree 0213/Pres) and managed by the Municipality, it extends for about 731 hectares along the streams Torre, Malina and Grivò, the river Sjava and the Civivena road, between stable meadows, woods, hedges with rows and a wet area. The itinerary of the park goes from Cerneglons to Ziracco passing through Remanzacco, on roads, bike paths, hoods and paths with wooden bridges, touching rural villages and churches, Villa della Torre Valsassina, Villa Laura and Villa Zoccolari, the mills of the roggia (there is also a "Giro delle acque e dei mulini" (Giro delle acque e dei milli), various farmhouses and the Tonutti Museum; on clear days you can see the Julian Alps and the castles of Partistagno, Cuccagna and Zucco. Access to the houses of Malina (Remanzacco), the Park of Grivons (Ziracco) and the former Public Pesa (Cerneglons); free access in compliance with the regulations.

  • Where Case del Malina, Remanzacco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website parcodeltorreemalina.it

Sources: parcodeltorreemalina.it · regione.fvg.it · comune.remanzacco.ud.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta di Ziracco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the sixteenth century to which in 1643 were added the octagonal drum and the spire; the terracotta spire collapsed with the earthquake of 1976 and was rebuilt in copper in 1986.

  • Where Ziracco, Remanzacco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.remanzacco.ud.it
