What to see in Saint-Marcel

Country of orchards at the outlet of the valley of the same name, known since ancient times for the mines of manganese and copper.

3 places to visit in Saint-Marcel, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Saint-Marcel in the guide with map and filters

Santuario di Plout

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Place dedicated to Our Lady and venerated from the fourteenth century, when there was found a statue; the current building is from 1851.

  • Where Plout, Saint-Marcel
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Miniere di Servette e Centro Viviminiera

Museums · Themed

Mines at 1800 meters, exploited by the Romans for copper and millstones, today open-air museum with guided tours (momentally closed); in the town hall the Viviminiera center tells them with plastic, reconstruction and video.

  • Where Saint-Marcel
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it

Castello di Saint-Marcel

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Rural and residential castle of the XIV-XV centuries in Surpian resort.

  • Where Surpian, Saint-Marcel
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it
