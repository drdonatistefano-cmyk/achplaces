What to see in Matelica

City of Picena origin and then Roman, home to Verdicchio and Enrico Mattei: Piazza Mattei with the octagonal fountain of 1587, the Ottoni Loggia and the Palazzo del Governo of the thirteenth century, the Globo, the Roman spherical sundial of the Archaeological Museum, the Piersanti Museum and the theatre of Giuseppe Piermarini.

7 places to visit in Matelica, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza Enrico Mattei e fontana ottagonale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The main square, dedicated to the founder of the ENI: at the center the octagonal fountain in white stone of 1587, designed by Lattanzio Ventura of Urbino, with four deities marine; around the government palace, documented since 1271, the civic tower, the loggia of 1511 wanted by Ascanio Ottoni for the trade of wool and the municipal palace. Who runs seven times around the fountain receives from Pro Loco the morning patente.

  • Where Piazza Enrico Mattei, Matelica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Museo archeologico comunale e Globo di Matelica

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the convent of San Francesco, the finds of the recent excavations of Matelica and surroundings from prehistory to the Renaissance; the symbol piece is the Globo, spherical sundial in marble with Greek inscriptions of the I-II century A.D.

  • Where Via San Francesco, Matelica
  • Entry €3 whole, €2 reduced
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo Piersanti

Museums · House museums

The palace of the Piersanti family (since 1728), museum since 1901: furniture, works and family memories, the treasure of silvers of the Sacred Chapel, sculptures and paintings from the 12th to the 18th century from the cathedral and the minor churches, the collection of Mgr. Venanzio Filippo Piersanti, master of pontifical ceremonies, and the archaeological collection Bigiaretti. Open from Thursday to Sunday.

  • Where Via Umberto I 11, Matelica
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museopiersanti.it · italia.it

Pinacoteca comunale «Raffaele Fidanza» e Palazzo Ottoni

Museums · Modern art

On the second floor of the fifteenth-century Palazzo Ottoni (1472), 52 works by the neoclassical painter Raffaele Fidanza, portraits of notables, a great Sant'Onofrio by Salvator Rosa and paintings by Diego Pettinelli; in the palace there are three rooms of a Roman domus of the second century with polychrome mosaics.

  • Where Piazza Enrico Mattei, Matelica
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Museo civico paleontologico

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

Fossil from Precambrian to Tertiary: a fragment of ancient earth's crust, a marine reptile of about 8 meters, a block with six eggs of dinosaur, great ammonites and the skull of a tiger with saber teeth.

  • Where Piazza Mattei, Matelica
  • Entry €3 whole, €2 reduced, €1 children
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The bell tower, which was incorporated into the brick façade completed in 1866; in 1927 Giuseppe Castellucci renewed it in neoclassical forms with a large dome inspired by San Lorenzo di Firenze. Inside, the wooden choir of master Diocletian (1667) and a small museum of sacred furnishings in the old sacristy.

  • Where Corso Vittorio Emanuele, Matelica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: beweb.chiesacattolica.it · italia.it

Teatro Giuseppe Piermarini

History and architecture · Historic theatres

The theatre designed around 1805 by Giuseppe Piermarini, the architect of La Scala, who gave it its name; today in the circuit AMAT.

  • Where Via Umberto I, Matelica
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
