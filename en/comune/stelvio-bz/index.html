What to see in Stelvio

Common at the foot of the Ortles (3,905 m), in the Stelvio National Park, which includes Solda, Trafoi and Stelvio Pass, at 2,757 meters the highest road pass in Italy; in Solda the Messner Mountain Museum Ortles dedicated to ice and the Museum for the region of Ortles, in Trafoi the sanctuary of the Three Holy Fountains.

Passo dello Stelvio e la strada dei 48 tornanti

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 2,757 meters between the region of Ortles and Bormio, it is the highest road pass in Italy: the road, 49 km long and called the "queen of the Alpine roads" was built between 1820 and 1825 and has since almost unchanged, with 48 hairpin bends on the South Tyrolean side; it is a challenge for cyclists and motorcyclists and stage of the Giro d'Italia. At the pass, in the Stelvio National Park, summer glacier skiing is open six months a year. During the Stelvio Bike Day, at the end of summer, the road is closed to motorized traffic.

  • Where Passo dello Stelvio, Stelvio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.vinschgau.net

Sources: vinschgau.net · suedtirol.info

Messner Mountain Museum Ortles

Museums · Themed

In Solda, about 1,900 meters at the foot of the Ortles, an underground museum dedicated to ice and glaciers: mountains of ice, Arctic and Antarctica, avalanches, skis, ice climbing and polar expeditions, with the largest collection in the world of Ortles paintings and ice tools of two centuries. Designed by Reinhold Messner with the Venostano architect Arnold Gapp, it is placed in a hillside: concrete walls bent like ice crystals, a band of windows like a crevasse and a window that frames the top of the Ortles.

  • Where Via della Foresta, Solda, Stelvio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.vinschgau.net

Sources: suedtirol.info · vinschgau.net

Museo per la regione dell'Ortles

Museums · City history

History and culture of the Ortles region: about a hundred years of tourism with shelters, funders and pioneers of Solda, an exhibition on the war of 1915-1918 and a collection of minerals in the area. Mostly set up by volunteers from a local association led by Konrad Knoll, which has funded a large part of it. Open all year round, every day 9-19.

  • Where Via Principale 23, Solda, Stelvio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.vinschgau.net

Sources: vinschgau.net

Santuario delle Tre Fontane Sante

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

According to legend, in the 13th century Pastor Moritz saw three streams flowing from the rock, each with a cross; the small church was built in the 15th century and since then it has been a Marian pilgrimage destination at the foot of the Ortles. From the tour center naturetrafoi or from the parking lot at the Hotel Bella Vista you will arrive on foot in about 40 minutes on a flat path (n. 18), walkable with stroller all year round.

  • Where Trafoi, Stelvio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.vinschgau.net

Sources: vinschgau.net

Cascate di Trafoi

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Ring that starts from the parking lot of the Three Fountains Sante: beyond the faunistic fence, the path 9 climbs up into the woods along a canal, crosses a bridge over the glacial melting water and reaches three waterfalls fed by the glacier, then descends into the wood at the starting point.

  • Where Tre Fontane Sante, Trafoi, Stelvio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.vinschgau.net

Sources: suedtirol.info · vinschgau.net
