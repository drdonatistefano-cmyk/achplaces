What to see in Orte

Borgo of Etruscan origin on a cliff of tuff about 132 meters high above the Tiber: under the houses runs Orte underground, a hydraulic system of tunnels, wells and cisterns of 2,500 years; on the surface Renaissance buildings, the basilica of the Assumption, the sacred art museum in the Romanesque church of San Silvestro and, in the plain, the Roman river port of Seripola.

Orte sotterranea

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the tuff cliff below the village, with traces from the Etruscans, a dense network of tunnels, wells, canals and cisterns that forms a hydraulic system of 2,500 years, along with cellars, stables, dovectors, warehouses and workshops; the historical-archaeological path starts from the underground fountain.

  • Where Piazza della Libertà, Orte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Fontana ipogea

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Digged in Roman times, it is the terminal point of an ancient perhaps Etruscan aqueduct and for centuries it was the only public water reserve in the city: on the stones of the pool remain the signs of the jugs; a guardian appointed by the Priors kept its key. The present form is of the early seventeenth century.

  • Where Piazza della Libertà, Orte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.orte.vt.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Museo d'arte sacra di Orte

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the former Romanesque church of San Silvestro (XI century) and in the Bishop's Palace: gold-bottomed tables from the XII to the XVI century, paintings, silver and vestments, with a Byzantine mosaic of the VIII century, a Madonna of Taddeo di Bartolo and the San Michele di Antonio da Castello.

  • Where Piazza Colonna, Orte
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.orte.vt.it · italia.it

Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

In the ancient church of Sant'Antonio, the finds of excavations in the territory in three sections: Etruscan (end VI-inizi III century BC), Roman and early medieval (VI-XI century).

Museo delle Confraternite

Museums · Themed

In the great sacristy of the church of Santa Croce, originally a hospital, the furnishings of the procession of the Dead Christ of Good Friday: the coffin of 1626, the base of the Addolorata, the white cross of Santa Croce, two eighteenth-century street lamps and a sixteenth-century table of Sant'Egidio Abate.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo 11, Orte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.orte.vt.it · italia.it

Basilica di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Founded in the IX century and enlarged in the fourteenth and sixteenth centuries, it has the Baroque aspect of the 1721 renovation, it houses an eighteenth-century organ, the wooden choir, the seventeenth-century altar with the relics of the saintly martyrs and the altarpiece of Giuseppe Bottani (1752). You visit during functions.

  • Where Via G. Roscio 10, Piazza della Libertà, Orte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.orte.vt.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the first half of the fifteenth century, it replaced the church of San Pietro alla Rocca and was renovated in 1731; it preserves the plaque of Bishop Leone (VIII-IX century).

  • Where Vocabolo Costa Deci, Orte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: comune.orte.vt.it · italia.it

Centro storico, palazzi e Piazza Belvedere

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the cliff of an elliptical plan, a dense interweaving of ancient streets with the Palazzo Nerei-Roberti of the fourteenth century, the Renaissance Palazzo Roberteschi, the Palazzo Alberti alla Rocca and the sixteenth century Palazzo dell'Orologio, up to the viewpoint on the Tiber. At the end of August-September the Eighth of Sant'Egidio with the Palio degli Arcieri in fourteenth-century costume.

  • Where Centro storico, Orte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it · comune.orte.vt.it

Area archeologica di Seripola

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Roman river port on the left bank of the Tiber, under the cliff, discovered in 1962: testifies the role of Orte as a shopping center in direct contact with Rome.
