What to see in Torremaggiore

Farmhouse of the High Tablekeeper, born around the Mille as a farmhouse of the Benedictine abbey of San Pietro and feud of the de Sangro from 1382 to 1806. The medieval village gathers around the Ducal Castle; about 9 km from the archaeological park of Castel Fiorentino, where in 1250 Frederick II died.

Parco archeologico di Castel Fiorentino

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the western side of the hill of Sterparone, the heart of the medieval town of Fiorentino, border center wanted by the Byzantines: here in 1250 Frederick II died, who dictated his will there, as prophesied by the astrologer Michele Scoto (you will die in a place named after a flower). The city was destroyed by papal troops in 1255; excavations since the 1980s have brought to light the imperial domus, of which few traces remain, a building with a cruising vault, the walls of the cathedral and a tower in part preserved. Park established in 2007; guided tours, especially in August with the historical procession in costume, tel. 0882 391114.

Castello Ducale de Sangro

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Ducal palace born from the extension of the Norman-Svevo castle, which is incorporated there, with the Male and four round crenellated towers. The north side, defensive, preserves traces of battlements; the west side decorations, coats of arms and windows on the Dauni Mountains; on the south side you can see the Quadra Tower and the remains of an Aragonese bifora. An inscription on a balcony on the east side recalls the works of the Marquis Paolo II de Sangro; an overpass connected him to the ducal theatre and the legend wants an underground passage to the Matrix Church of San Nicola.

Borgo medievale e Chiesa Matrice di San Nicola

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village grew next to the castle with the survivors of Fiorentino and Dragonara after the destruction of 1255; almost razed to the ground by the earthquake of 1627, it preserves the medieval plant. The itinerary of the Municipality starts from the Ducal Castle and the ancient nucleus of the ...Codacchio , near the Matrice Church of San Nicola.

  • Where Centro storico, Torremaggiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page interregaismart.regione.puglia.it
