What to see in Monte San Giusto

Borgo del Maceratese linked to Bishop Nicolò Bonafede, who had the palace-fortress built at the beginning of the sixteenth century, now the town hall and restored Santa Maria della Pietà in Telusiano, where the Crucifixion of Lorenzo Lotto of 1531; in the town hall the collection of ancient drawings by Alessandro Maggiori is preserved.

Santa Maria della Pietà in Telusiano e Crocifissione di Lorenzo Lotto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the 14th century restored between 1513 and 1529 by Nicolò Bonafede (1464-1534), bishop of Chiusi, as a family mausoleum. On the high altar the Crucifixion of Lorenzo Lotto (1531), on three levels, with the client in prayer on the left; also late-fiveth century frescoes of Durante Nobili, pupil of Lotto, a stone Pietà of the fifteenth century and a Madonna of Trapani attributed to Nino Pisano. Hours: every day 9-12 and 16-19.

  • Where Via Tolomei 5, Monte San Giusto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Palazzo Bonafede

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace-fortress built at the beginning of the XVI century by Nicolò Bonafede, born in the village and bishop of Chiusi, today seat of the town hall: the central body is inspired by Palazzo Venezia in Rome, the courtyard to that of the Doge's Palace of Urbino.

  • Where Via Nicolò Bonafede, Monte San Giusto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Collezione «A. Maggiori»: disegni dal '500 al '700

Museums · Old masters

In the municipal palace, it collects the ancient drawings belonging to Count Alessandro Maggiori, of the family Majori Colonna, including sheets attributed to Raffaello, Giorgio Vasari, Cavalier d'Arpino, Guercino, Domenichino and Giovanni Battista Salvi.

  • Where Palazzo municipale, Monte San Giusto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Centro storico: Palazzo dei Priori, Collegiata, Torrione e porte

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Palazzo dei Priori was built between 1763 and 1770 on the previous medieval palace; the Neoclassical Collegiate preserves a Callido organ of the eighteenth century. The tour is completed by the Torrione, Porta de Moscé and Porta della Sala, the church of Sant'Agostino, now auditorium, and the monument to the fallen made in 1955 by the sculptor Cantalamessa.

  • Where Centro storico, Monte San Giusto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it
