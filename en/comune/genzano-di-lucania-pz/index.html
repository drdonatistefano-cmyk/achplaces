What to see in Genzano di Lucania

Country of the valley of the Bradano with the neoclassical Fontana Cavallina surmounted by a Roman Ceres, the Sanctuary of Maria SS. delle Grazie, the Palazzo Marchesale De Marinis and, about 15 km, the Norman castle of Monteserico.

6 places to visit in Genzano di Lucania, in the province of Potenza (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Monteserico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Norman Castle of the 11th century on Monte Serico, 550 meters and about 15 km from the village, on the border between the Byzantine lands of the Bradano and those Lombard and Norman; it was the scene of the defeat of the Byzantines against the Normans and then masseria of the state property of Frederick II, who frequented it for raptors. It has a trunk-conical shape surrounded by a moat, central square tower, low belt with shoe and drawbridge; in the underground there are prehistoric caves. A simple chapel stands on the floor.

Fontana Cavallina

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Neoclassical fountain built between 1865 and 1893 on a project by the Melfi architect Giuseppe Antonio Loccuratolo, brick amphitheatre full with a living stone frame, two ramps and wrought iron railing. On the arch stands the statue of Ceres, called "Sand'Abbbunnanza" , about 1,5 meters high and dated between the first century BC and the I AD, found in the nineteenth century in Pila Grande. In 1978 it was depicted on a stamp of the series "Fontane d'Italia" .

  • Where Via Emanuele Gianturco, Genzano di Lucania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.genzano.pz.it

Sources: comune.genzano.pz.it

Santuario di Maria SS. delle Grazie

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Church probably at the end of the seventeenth or early eighteenth century, destroyed by the earthquake of 1860 and rebuilt in 1878, a single nave with barrel vault, decorated in 1944-45. On the central wall the image of the Madonna and Child in coloured sandstone, found on March 25, 1621 in Capo d'Acqua; the feast is celebrated on the first or second Sunday of August.

  • Where Corso Vittorio Emanuele, Genzano di Lucania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.genzano.pz.it

Sources: comune.genzano.pz.it

Palazzo Marchesale De Marinis

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Maybe from the Angevin era, enlarged by various feudal lords and summer residence of the Marquises De Marinis; after the earthquake of 1893 it was remodeled on three floors and, damaged by the earthquake of 1980, restored between 1987 and 1990. Since 1995 it has again hosted municipal offices.

  • Where Piazza Risorgimento 1, Genzano di Lucania
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.genzano.pz.it

Sources: comune.genzano.pz.it

Chiesa e Monastero della SS. Annunziata

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded between 1321 and 1327 by Aquilina di Monteserico, lady of Genzano, on the ruins of a manor of the twelfth century, it was inhabited by the Clarisses until 1905 and then used for civil functions. It was opened on the FAI Days of Spring and Autumn 2025.

recorded among the abandoned cultural heritage of the MiC; openings only on the occasion of the FAI days

Museo etnografico dei maestri artigiani e della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Also known as the Ethnographic Museum of Farming and Craftsmanship, it is housed in the Professional Institute for Agriculture and Environment and was set up in 2000 with the pupils: agricultural tools, domestic tools, shoemakers, bottai and blacksmiths and three permanent photographic exhibitions.

MiC card without opening hours, updated to 30/04/2020; contact the institute (tel. 0971 774021)
