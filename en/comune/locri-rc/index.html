What to see in Locri

On the Gelsomini Coast, the modern city next to the Magno-Greek colony of Locri Epizefiri, home to the legislator Zaleucho and the poetess Nosside: more than 230 hectares of excavations, sanctuaries, theatre and museums of the park.

Parco archeologico di Locri Epizefiri

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

More than 230 hectares along the state road 106, about 5 km from the city: the colony founded at the end of the 8th century BC, with sandstone walls, the Greek model theatre, the sanctuaries of Aphrodite, Persephone and Demeter, the sacred area of Marasà and the artisan district of Centocamere; Roman from 205 BC, abandoned in the 7th to 8th century A.D.

  • Where SS 106 Jonica, Locri
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo archeologico nazionale di Locri

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Born in 1971 as Antiquarium and National Museum since 1998, it recounts the Greek Locri with hundreds of finds, including the pinakes, 5th century BC terracotta votive tablets, and necropolis supplies.

  • Where SS 106 Jonica, Locri
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Casino Macrì

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Nineteenth-century farmhouse built on a private Roman thermal bath, with Roman and late antique materials including the statue of Togato and a tub in Chio's breach.

  • Where Parco archeologico, Locri
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo del Territorio – Palazzo Nieddu del Rio

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

In a 19th century town hall, opened in 2018: furnishings of the indigenous necropolis of the Locride (Canale Ianchina, Sant'Onofrio dell'età del Ferro) and private collections Scaglione and Candida.

  • Where Centro, Locri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
