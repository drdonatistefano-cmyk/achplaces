What to see in Benevento

City-museum between antiquity and contemporary art: the Arch of Trajan and the Roman theatre of the second century, the Lombard church of Santa Sofia UNESCO heritage with the cloister of the Museum of Sannio, the Rectors' Rock and the Hortus Conclusus of Mimmo Paladino.

10 places to visit in Benevento, in the province of Benevento (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Benevento in the guide with map and filters

Arco di Traiano

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The monument symbol of the city, well preserved and decorated with reliefs: it celebrates the Emperor Trajan and marked the exit of the Via Appia Traiana; in the Middle Ages it was incorporated into the Lombard walls.

  • Where Via Traiano, Benevento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Teatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Inaugurated in 126 A.D. under Hadrian and brought back to light around 1920, it is reached from the back of the Duomo through the district Triggio.

  • Where Rione Triggio, Benevento
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Sofia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Founded in the 8th century by a Longobard Duke, it has an original plant that combines two forms, a dome supported by columns and fragments of frescoes of the 8th century; since 2011 it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site on the site "I Longobardi in Italy."

  • Where Piazza Santa Sofia, Benevento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo del Sannio e Chiostro di Santa Sofia

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

In the monastery next to Santa Sofia, one of the main collections of Campania, with the 12th century Romanesque cloister, also UNESCO heritage; the unique ticket includes the Egyptian section at the Arcos and the complex of Sant'Ilario.

  • Where Piazza Santa Sofia, Benevento
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · retemuseale.provincia.benevento.it

Rocca dei Rettori

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The main place of power of the city, begun in the fourteenth century to protect the papal governor at the top of Corso Garibaldi; it houses the provincial administration and museum collections, with a panoramic terrace.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Benevento
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Hortus Conclusus

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Viewpoint

A closed garden in the gardens of the Dominican monastery, with installations by Mimmo Paladino between historical finds of the city and a bronze horse on the wall.

  • Where Via San Domenico, Benevento
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Duomo di Benevento

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The cathedral with its two-order façade, destroyed by the bombings of September 1943 and rebuilt since 1950; the famous bronze gate of the central portal is now rebuilt inside.

  • Where Piazza Orsini 27, Benevento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

ARCOS – Sezione egizia del Museo del Sannio

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the contemporary art museum ARCOS, the Egyptian section of the Museum of Sannio with the finds of the temple dedicated to Isis, "Lady of Benevento" .

  • Where Benevento
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: retemuseale.provincia.benevento.it · italia.it

Complesso di Sant'Ilario – Museo dell'Arco di Traiano

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Next to the Arch of Traiano, part of the provincial museum network and can be visited with the unique ticket of the Sannio Museum.

  • Where Benevento
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: rmstaging.provincia.benevento.it · retemuseale.provincia.benevento.it · italia.it

MUSA – Polo museale della tecnica e del lavoro in agricoltura

Museums · Themed

One of the museums of the Benevento Province's museum network, dedicated to agricultural technology and work.

  • Where Benevento
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: rmstaging.provincia.benevento.it · italia.it
