What to see in Usseaux

Alpine village of the Alta Val Chisone, Orange Flag and the most beautiful village of Italy, divided into five villages: the hamlet of bread and murals, the sundials of Balboutet, the lake of Laux.

5 places to visit in Usseaux, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Usseaux in the guide with map and filters

Murales di Usseaux

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

About forty mural paintings with scenes of rural life, nature, animals and characters of fairy tales, which make the capital the hamlet of bread and murals.

  • Where Usseaux
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Forno, lavatoio e mulino

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

18th century buildings survived in the village: the old oven, the wash-house and the watermill.

  • Where Usseaux
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Balboutet e le meridiane

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Laux e il suo lago

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the right bank of Chisone, with a small natural lake, one of the most intact alpine villages in the valley.
