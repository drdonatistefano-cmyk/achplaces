What to see in Usseglio

Borgo Bandiera Arancione 1200 metres in the Valle di Viù, on the border with France, among Romanesque testimonies, alpine lakes and the tome of Lanzo.

4 places to visit in Usseglio, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Antico complesso parrocchiale – Museo civico alpino «Arnaldo Tazzetti»

Museums · Themed

In the complex of the medieval church with ancient cemetery, chapel of 1547 and Romanesque bell tower, the museum preserves pre-Romanesque and Romanesque finds and collections of local flora, fauna and minerals, with temporary exhibitions.

  • Where Piazza Cibrario, Usseglio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
