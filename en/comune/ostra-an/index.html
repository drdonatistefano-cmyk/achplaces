What to see in Ostra

Borgo Bandiera Arancione on the hills between Misa and Nevola, called Montalboddo until 1881: Piazza dei Martiri with the Neoclassical Town Hall, the civic tower and the Theatre La Vittoria, the medieval belt of 1200 meters, the Collegiate Church of Santa Croce in the low town planned between two and fourteenth century, San Francesco with the paintings of Hercules Ramazzani and the Marian shrine of the Madonna della Rosa.

Piazza dei Martiri, Palazzo comunale e torre civica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The central square combines the upper and lower parts of the village; the neoclassical Town Hall (1749), the Theatre La Vittoria and the civic tower of the sixteenth century, rebuilt in 1950 after the bombings of the Second World War, which was the bell tower of the disappeared church of St. John, destroyed in war.

Teatro La Vittoria

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Embedded in the Palazzo Comunale, the current theatre of 1863, fourth in the history of Ostra, is the work of the architect Francesco Fellini of Barbara: horseshoe plant, 29 stages on two orders and loggia, decorations with symbols of the Theatre and Music, curtain with the Town Hall and ancient scenic machinery still working. Closed in 1980, it was reopened on 6 January 1998 and hosts prose, theatre for children and concerts.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Mura medievali e Ostra Alta

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval wall, about 1200 meters long, preserves nine square towers. In the highest and oldest part of the village, with narrow and winding streets, stood the Fortress, of which today remains only the name of the alley of the Fortress; inside the walls there are the noble palaces Menchetti, Luzi Fedeli Gabuzzi and Pericoli.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Basilica Collegiata di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Basilicas

The main church of Ostra, in the lower part of the old town, built in 1852 on the foundations of an abbey of the twelfth century, around which between 1280 and 1350 developed a planned neighborhood on regular and symmetrical roads; it preserves works by Claudio Ridolfi and frescoes by Cattani and Galimberti.

Chiesa di San Francesco e Palazzo dei Conventuali

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church with neo-Gothic portico and baroque crowning, decorated inside with frescoes by Filippo Bellini and several paintings by Ercole Ramazzani; a cloister connects it to the Palazzo dei Conventuali, a former 18th century Franciscan monastery that houses the Pinacoteca comunale (Museo civico), the Library and the Historical Archives.

Santuario della Madonna della Rosa

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Just outside the center, the sanctuary of 1754 houses a miraculous 17th century Madonna's shrine and votive tablets: according to tradition in 1666 a lily placed by a girl in front of the image remained fresh and fragrant for months. It is still a destination for pilgrimages, among the main Marian sanctuaries of the Marches.
