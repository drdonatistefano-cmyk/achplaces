Parco Nazionale Gran Paradiso in Valsavarenche

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valley among wooded slopes at the foot of the Gran Paradiso, where it is easy to meet chamois, ibex, marmots and foxes; many paths were built by the Savoys for hunting.
