What to see in Trissino

Country of the Agno valley, 20 km from Vicenza, linked since the century after the thousand to the noble family Trissino: in the park of the castle there are the two surviving villas, including the Villa Trissino Da Porto Marzotto completed in the eighteenth century on a project by Francesco Antonio Muttoni. Also worth seeing is the parish church of Sant'Andrea, the conciliar church of St. Peter and the Tibaldo Museum of Communication.

Villa e parco Trissino Marzotto

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

In the park of the medieval castle of the Trissino, family of Lombard origin remodelled several times over the centuries, survive two villas: the Villa Trissino Paninsacco, with Italian gardens and eighteenth-century statues of the workshops of Marinali and Cassetti, and the Villa Trissino Da Porto, now Marzotto, completed in the eighteenth century on a project by Francesco Antonio Muttoni (with interventions by Dal Pozzo and Calderari). The interiors are visited by reservation; closed in August and during the Christmas period.

  • Where Piazza G. G. Trissino 2, Trissino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.trissino.vi.it

Sources: comune.trissino.vi.it · vicenzae.org

Museo Tibaldo, museo della comunicazione

Museums · Themed

Born from the collection collected in over 40 years by Giancarlo Tibaldo: more than 10,000 working pieces that tell more than 200 years of audio, video and music communication, science and technology. A laboratory offers experiments on electromagnetism, electrostatics, battery operation and Stirling engine.

  • Where Via Stazione 44, Trissino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.trissino.vi.it

Sources: venetoaroundme.regione.veneto.it · comune.trissino.vi.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of very ancient origins and little documented; it preserves a saints waterworks of 1274, a statue of the Madonna delle Grazie of the fifteenth century and a ciborium of Niccolò da Cornedo of 1438.

  • Where Trissino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.trissino.vi.it

Sources: comune.trissino.vi.it

Chiesa di San Pietro apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Conciliar Church in the center of the village, inaugurated in 1971, realized by Francesco Vacchini on a project by Antonio Nervi. Open every day 8-12 and 15-19.

  • Where Via Dante, Trissino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.trissino.vi.it
