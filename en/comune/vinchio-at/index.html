What to see in Vinchio

Country of the Asti Monferrato, already mentioned in a document of 948 and home of the writer and partisan Davide Lajolo ("Ulysses") who dedicates to him the multimedia museum "Vinchio" is my nest. Between Vinchio, Vaglio Serra and Incisa Scapaccino stretches the special nature reserve of Val Sarmassa, established in 1993, and the hills produce the Barbera d'Asti Nice and the precious Saracen asparagus.

4 places to visit in Vinchio, in the province of Asti (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Davide Lajolo «Vinchio è il mio nido»

Museums · House museums

Multimedia Museum dedicated to Davide Lajolo (1912-1984), born in Vinchio, partisan with the name of battle "Ulisse" since 1943, director of "L'Unità" (1948-1958), deputy and Viareggio Prize 1977 with "Veder l'erba" on the side of the roots ""; retraces his life as partisan, journalist and writer. Individual and guided tours by reservation (groups up to 30 people), audioguide in Italian and English, accessible to disabled people. In the village there are also the birthplace with plaque (Via Ramaudio 4) and the bronze bust of Floriano Bodini on the stairway between Piazza Vercelli and Piazza San Marco.

  • Where Via Capitano E. Laiolo 14, Vinchio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.vinchio.at.it

Sources: visitlmr.it · comune.vinchio.at.it

Riserva naturale speciale della Val Sarmassa

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Over 230 hectares of hills between Vinchio, Vaglio Serra and Incisa Scapaccino, established by the Piedmont Region in 1993 after the inhabitants stopped the project of a landfill; chestnut and oak woods from mushrooms and truffles, outings of marine fossils of the Sabbie d'Asti, the Blue Lake pond and the large oak called Well-marked trails touch the Bricco dei Finquant'anni, the Ciabòt of Montedelmare, the partisan paths and the tuff caves used as a hiding place during the rakes; the emblem is the lizard, linked to the stories of Lajolo. Picnic areas and guided tours with the Astigian Parks Authority (Operational headquarters in Piazza Vercelli 1, tel. 0141 950237); reserve feast on the last Saturday of August.

  • Where Piazza Vercelli 1 (sede operativa), Vinchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.vinchio.at.it

Sources: visitlmr.it · comune.vinchio.at.it

Casa museo di Fratel Teodoreto

Museums · House museums

Birthplace of the Venerable Brother Theodoreto (baptized John Andrew, 1871-1954), of the Brothers of the Christian Schools, founder in 1913 of the Union of the Catechists of the SS. Crucifix and promoter of the House of Charity Arts and Crafts; transformed into a museum by Brother Carlo Torchio, it preserves furnishings and tools of an ancient country house. It is managed by the Association of SS Catechists. Crucifix; information to the church of San Marco, tel. 0141 950125.

  • Where Via Belveglio, Vinchio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.vinchio.at.it

Sources: comune.vinchio.at.it

Percorsi letterari di Davide Lajolo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Three itineraries between vineyards and woods in the places of the stories of Lajolo (the fortresses of Barbera, the green sea of the hills, the woods of the Saracens), starting from the cellar in San Pancrazio, on the border with Vaglio Serra, and from Piazza Vercelli. According to tradition on the Bricco dei Saraceni Aleramo defeated the Saracens in 935, and there was found a neolithic axe today at the Museum of Antiquity in Turin.

  • Where Piazza Vercelli, Vinchio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.vinchio.at.it

Sources: comune.vinchio.at.it
