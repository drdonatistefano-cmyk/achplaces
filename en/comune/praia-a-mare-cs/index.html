What to see in Praia a Mare

Locality of the Riviera dei Cedri, in the Pollino National Park, overlooking the Island of Dino with its sea caves; to see the Tower of Fiuzzi on the faraglione, the Sanctuary of the Madonna della Grotta inside a large natural cavity and the Blue Flag beach of Punta Fiuzzi.

Isola di Dino e Grotta Azzurra

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

An island of about 50 hectares, at most 100 meters high, in front of Cape of the Arena; the erosion dug the caves of Monaco, the Sardines (with stalagmites), the Waterfalls, the Lion and the Blue Grotto, the largest, with intensely coloured water and ancient marine fossils on the walls. You reach it by boat, canoe or pedal boat, and the seabed are rich in flora and fauna of natural interest.

Torre di Fiuzzi

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

It rises on a faraglione, on the 15 meters high rocks that bear the same name; from the sixteenth century it defended the country from the raids from the sea and served to spot the Saracen ships.

  • Where Località Fiuzzi, Praia a Mare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · enit.it

Santuario della Madonna della Grotta

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

The main place of worship of Praia rises at the foot of Mount Vingiolo, about 90 meters above sea, in three rocky cavities illuminated by natural light, reached by a long steps of pebbles of sea (53 steps according to italia.it). The statue of Our Lady was placed on a rock at the entrance in 1326; every year on 14, 15 and 16 August it is carried in procession to the country.

  • Where Via della Grotta, Praia a Mare
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.praiaamare.cs.it · italia.it · enit.it

Museo comunale di Praia a Mare

Museums · Modern art

Housed in an old military structure, it has archaeological sections, contemporary art, postcards of artist, ceramics and photography; the permanent collection of modern and contemporary art has over 200 works donated by Italian and foreign artists.
