What to see in Manerba del Garda

Valtenesi municipality on the Lombard shore of Garda, made of hamlets on a small peninsula among olive trees, vines and cypresses; the Fortress of Manerba, with the remains of the castle destroyed in the sixteenth century and the natural archaeological park, looks to the Island of San Biagio, while under the waters of San Sivino there is a village of the Bronze World Heritage.

Rocca di Manerba

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Rocky spur frequented by the Mesolithic, with open-air cults between the 10th and 8th centuries BC and perhaps a small Roman temple in Minerva; in the Middle Ages there arose a castle with more walls, demolished by the Bresciani in 1277 after having removed it from the Scaligeri and then rebuilt with two concentric belts and a tower. The Republic of Venice decreed its destruction in 1574 (1575 for Italia.it) because it was a refuge for bandits; the remains, dug recently, can be reached with an easy path up to the panoramic cross on the Island of the Rabbit, the Gulf of Salò and Mount Baldo.

  • Where Via della Rocca, Manerba del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: lombardiabeniculturali.it · visitbrescia.it · italia.it

Museo Civico Archeologico della Valtenesi

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

On the slope that goes up to the Rocca, with a large glazed wall open to the landscape, exhibits the finds of the ancient settlements of the Rocca and the territory along with its geological and naturalistic characteristics, including the materials of the UNESCO building site of San Sivino. It also organizes guided boat tours around the promontory with a stop at the submerged village.

  • Where Via Rocca 20, Manerba del Garda
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitbrescia.it · palafittes.org · italia.it

Parco Archeologico Naturalistico della Rocca e Riserva Rocca e Sasso

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Under the Fortress a natural park with about 400 plant species, part of the Nature Reserve Rocca and Sasso, covered by trails including the one that connects Pisenze and Dusano. It includes the Riparo Valtenesi, a natural terrace at the foot of Mount Sasso overlooking the lake, a place of worship since the Copper Age (3000-2500 BC) and then a burial area with collective hut tombs.

  • Where Via della Rocca, Manerba del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · lombardiabeniculturali.it · visitbrescia.it

Sito palafitticolo di San Sivino detto del Gabbiano

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage

Village on stilts of the Bronze Age (2200-1300 BC), today submerged near the shore, which extended for about 150 meters along the coast; discovered in 1971 by the Triton Sub Club of Desenzano, oak poles reveal two construction phases at least 33 years away. It is one of the components of the UNESCO serial site of prehistoric palafittic sites of the Alps.

  • Where Località San Sivino, Manerba del Garda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: palafittes.org · lombardiabeniculturali.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Sivino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small single nave church with rustic façade on the promontory of San Sivino, a rocky spur near the lake; inside arches with all sixth hold the beams of the exposed roof. In the south wall is set the stone of the covenant, tied to the legend of a miller who around 1200 would sell the soul to the devil: there you can still see an engraved cross and the footprints of the devil's hand and foot.

  • Where Promontorio di San Sivino, Manerba del Garda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Isola di San Biagio

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

Also known as Isola dei Conigli, a small island of naturalistic interest in front of the Rocca promontory, reachable by canoeing; it is one of the main subjects of the panorama from the cross of the Rocca.

  • Where Davanti al promontorio della Rocca, Manerba del Garda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
