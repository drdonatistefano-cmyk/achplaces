What to see in Tiana

Small village of the Barbagia di Ollolai at 600 meters, surrounded by woods covering 85% of the territory, famous for its orbace: along the Torrei river the museum of industrial archaeology "Le vie dell'acqua" preserves Cracchera de tziu Bellu, the last active ravine of Sardinia.

Gualchiera «Bellu» - Museo Le vie dell'acqua

Museums · Themed

About 1 km from the village along the SS 128, on the Torrei river: a mill for the wheat and two galleries to crowd the wool. Sa Cracchera de tziu Bellu, made of stone with wooden cover, has roverella sweaters powered by a hydraulic wheel that beat the wet orbace with hot water to make it soft and resistant; it ceased its activity in 1975 and back to work during the visits and to "Autunno in Barbagia" .

  • Where SS 128, rio Torrei, Tiana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Necropoli di Mancosu

History and architecture · Archaeology

Neolithic necropolis of domus de Janas, called here forreddos, in the surroundings of the country: testifies to the human presence since prehistory.

Boschi di Tiana e Monte Mungianeddu

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lecci, roverelle, cork and chestnuts surround the village; the ancient paths of transhumance lead to the slopes of Mount Mungianeddu, to the valley of Bau 'e Fonne and to the woods of Sa Costa 'e Sili and Orovole. In the valley the rivers Tino and Torrei flow into Lake Taloro.

  • Where Monte Mungianeddu, Tiana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
