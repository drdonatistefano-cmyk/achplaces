What to see in Zogno

Country of the Val Brembana from the origins around 1100, with the fifteenth-century parish church of San Lorenzo built on the ruins of a castle, a network of museums (of the Valley, of San Lorenzo, of the Soldier, House museum Belotti), the Grottoes of the Wonders, among the first tourist caves of Italy, and the departure of the cycle path of the Val Brembana.

8 places to visit in Zogno, in the province of Bergamo (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Valle

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Ethnographic and archaeological museum founded in 1979 by Vittorio Polli. The oldest finds come from the collective burials of the Buca di Andrea, discovered in 1975 by Onorato Pesenti and dating from the middle of the III millennium to the XIX century BC; next to, work tools, household objects, furnishings and clothing tell life and traditions of the valley. Timetable indicated by the Municipality: Tuesday-Saturday 9-12 and 14-17, Sunday by reservation; guide on request 15 €.

  • Where Via Mazzini 3, Zogno
  • Entry €5, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.zogno.bg.it · italia.it

Museo di San Lorenzo

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Founded in 1985 by Mgr. Giulio Gabanelli with a group of enthusiasts, collects over 2,700 pieces on four floors related to popular devotion: torn frescoes, paintings, sculptures, crucifixes, glasses, vestments (many fabrics from the nuns of the nearby convent), furnishings and devotional objects; in the courtyard stone sculptures. In 2024 he was in transformation into the Museum of Gabanelli Arts (MAG). Visit only by reservation.

  • Where Via XI Febbraio 9, Zogno
  • Entry Free Offer
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.zogno.bg.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

XV century church built on the ruins of a medieval castle, in the center of the ancient village, reached by a monumental staircase of the eighteenth century with a view from the churchyard. In the sacristy tears of late-four hundred frescoes, a fifteenth-century table with the Baptism of Jesus and a Pietà by Vincenzo Angelo Orelli; the baptistery, made from the ancient chapel of San Rocco on a project by Vito Sonzogni, is decorated with mosaics and sculptures by Claudio and Attilio Nani.

  • Where Via Umberto I 11, Zogno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.zogno.bg.it

Grotte delle Meraviglie

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Explore for the first time in 1932 by speleologists led by Ermenegildo Zanchi, in 1939 they became one of the first tourist caves in Italy; the route of visit is about 70 meters. The most spectacular room is the ...Labyrinth (Büs de la Marta), with very high vaults and stalactites and stalagmites joined in columns; internal temperature of about 12 °C. Guided tours in groups of 25-30 people managed by the caves of Wonders caves; they serve slip-resistant shoes.

  • Where Via Grotte delle Meraviglie 8, Zogno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.zogno.bg.it · italia.it

Casa museo Bortolo Belotti

Museums · House museums

Villa Liberty, home of the politician, historian and jurist Bortolo Belotti, opened to the public in 2017: the study with original furnishings and the archive, also linked to friendship with D'Annunzio, and 26 paintings from the sixteenth to the twentieth century donated by the family to the Municipality. The park of about 5,000 square meters houses sculptures by Nino Galizzi.

  • Where Villa Belotti, Zogno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.zogno.bg.it · italia.it

Museums · Themed

  • Entry To be checked

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

  • Entry Free

Ciclabile della Val Brembana

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

It starts from Zogno and runs for 21 km to Piazza Brembana with 200 meters of difference in altitude, on the path of a former railway: the gentle slope makes it suitable for everyone.

  • Where Zogno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
