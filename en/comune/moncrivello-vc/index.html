What to see in Moncrivello

The hilly village of Vercellese with a double-circle castle of walls in a dominant position, where in 1478 the Duchess Jolanda of Savoy died; around, the MoncriVillaTrails paths between vineyards of Erbaluce and Nebbiolo and the Garden of the Bees, living museum of beekeeping.

Castello di Moncrivello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built in a dominant position with the horizon up to Novara and Canavese, with a double circle of walls; the origins date back to around the Mille and in 1152 Frederick Barbarossa assigned it to the bishop of Vercelli. After the Fieschis (1394) and the Savoys, at the end of the fifteenth century the Duchess Jolanda transformed it from a Romanesque fortress into a Renaissance residence and died there in 1478; in 1650 she rejected a Spanish attack. Damaged by a fire at the beginning of the 19th century and abandoned, it has been restored since 1972 and opened to the public in 1996.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

MoncriVillaTrails

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Two routes between Moncrivello and Villareggia, with GPX tracks and map on the site of the Municipality. The Blue, without too steep stretches, is suitable for walkers, runners, cyclists and horses, you walk in the two senses and touches the two churches on the hill, panoramic points on the Alps, the plain, the Dora and the vineyards of Erbaluce and Nebbiolo, and a long stretch of the Naviglio di Ivrea; connected to the White Stones Path (about 15 km more) reaches about 25 km. Red alternates short climbs and technical descents, for experienced cyclists or hikers.

  • Where Partenza da Moncrivello o Villareggia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.moncrivello.vc.it

Sources: comune.moncrivello.vc.it

Giardino delle Api, museo vivente dell'apicoltura

Museums · Themed

Educational garden on the history of beekeeping, biodiversity and sustainability, which recovers ancient practices of nourishment of bees and hosts over one hundred species of herbs, shrubs and honeycombs. It offers sensory activities: recognizing the fragrances of plants, tasting different honeys, discovering how bees communicate with smells; designed for schools, enthusiasts and families.

  • Where Località Lissia Sabbioni, Moncrivello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.moncrivello.vc.it

Sources: comune.moncrivello.vc.it
