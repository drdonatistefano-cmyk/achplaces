What to see in Sant'Agata de' Goti

Sannita village suspended on a tufa spur above the Martorano valley, Orange Flag and among the most beautiful villages in Italy: the castle with the frescoes of Tommaso Giaquinto, the mosaic of the 12th century of San Menna, the Gothic frescoes of the Annunziata, the Duomo and the places of Sant'Alfonso Maria de' Liguori.

11 places to visit in Sant'Agata de' Goti, in the province of Benevento (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ponte Vittorio Emanuele e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The bridge crosses the Martorano valley and offers the most spectacular view of the old town and the arch system that supports the village; it leads to the castle.

  • Where Ponte Vittorio Emanuele, Sant'Agata de' Goti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Founded by the Longobards and renovated several times from the Norman age to the eighteenth century: in the courtyard arches with acute sixth, in the loggia of the first floor mythological scenes of the seventeenth century and a fresco of 1710 by Tommaso Giaquinto, ...Diana and Atteone.

  • Where Sant'Agata de' Goti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Menna

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Consecrated in 1110 by Pope Pasquale II, it has Romanesque portal and in the apse a cosmatesque mosaic floor of the twelfth century, among the oldest mosaics dated in southern Italy; it preserves frescoes of the XIV-XV century and on the altar a slab of sarcophagus of the VII century.

  • Where Sant'Agata de' Goti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa dell'Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded in 1237 and rebuilt after the earthquake of 1349, with portal and sixteenth-century bell tower; the Gothic interior houses frescoes from the end of the fourteenth to the beginning of the fifteenth century rediscovered for a few decades (Universal Judith in counter-facade, Crucifixion and Annunciation in the apse, St. Nicholas who endows three girls), a polyptych of the Annunciation of 1483 maybe by Angiolillo Arcuccio and 16 stained glass windows of 1976 designed by Bruno Cassinari.

  • Where Sant'Agata de' Goti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded in 1267 and renovated in the eighteenth century, it houses the funeral monument of Ludovico d'Artus (dead in 1370), the majolica floor of the Massa brothers, the ceiling with stories of the Old Testament and an Annunciation (1702) by Tommaso Giaquinto, a fresco of the fifteenth-century Madonna del latte and a reed organ.

  • Where Sant'Agata de' Goti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Duomo dell'Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Founded in 970 on a pagan temple, rebuilt in the 12th century and renovated in the 18th century mainly by Neapolitan artists; ceiling of 1878. The 12-column crypt contains 14th-century frescoes and remains of mosaic flooring.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Sant'Agata de' Goti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Sant'Angelo de Munculanis

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of Lombard origin with Romanesque bell tower with two double windows; the recent restoration has highlighted the medieval structure and a crypt.

  • Where Piazza Ludovico Viscardi, Sant'Agata de' Goti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Museo diocesano e Palazzo Vescovile

Museums · Old masters

Inaugurated in 1996 by the then Cardinal Joseph Ratzinger, it collects works of the churches damaged by the earthquake of 1980: the main seat in Santa Maria del Carmine exhibits sacred art, reliquaries, manuscripts and an Annunciation of Giaquinto; the section in the Bishop's Palace preserves memories of Sant'Alfonso Maria de' Liguori, the Hall of the Stemmes with the effigies of 68 bishops and a nape of 1997 by Giuseppe and Marco Ferrigno.

  • Where Piazzetta della Madonna del Carmine, Sant'Agata de' Goti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it
