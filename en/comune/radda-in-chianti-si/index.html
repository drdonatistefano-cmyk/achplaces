What to see in Radda in Chianti

Medieval village of Chianti Classico surrounded by vineyards, documented by the IX century and Florentine since the end of the thirteenth century.

Piazza Ferrucci e Palazzo del Podestà

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The heart of the village, dominated by the remains of the castle, with the fourteenth-century Palazzo del Podestà, town hall since 1865, and the proposal of San Niccolò with its neo-Gothic façade.

  • Where Piazza Ferrucci, Radda in Chianti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Castello di Volpaia

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval fortified village where the whole country works on wine and oil, with cellars in deconsecrated churches connected by an underground "wine pipeline."

  • Where Volpaia, Radda in Chianti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Santa Maria al Prato e Casa Chianti Classico

Museums · Themed

Romanesque church with a Madonna and Child of Neri di Bicci; in the former convent the Casa Chianti Classico with exhibition rooms and a route on the history of wine.

  • Where Santa Maria al Prato, Radda in Chianti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Pieve di Santa Maria Novella

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Pieve fuori Radda, which, despite the reconstructions, preserves the Romanesque plant and numerous works of art.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
