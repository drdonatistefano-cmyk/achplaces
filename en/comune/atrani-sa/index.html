What to see in Atrani

The smallest municipality in Italy by area, less than a kilometer from Amalfi along the Dragon Valley and within the UNESCO site of the Amalfi Coast: a medieval intricate alleys, arches and steps around the little square on the sea, the church of San Salvatore de' Birecto where the doges and the collegiate church of Santa Maria Maddalena were crowned.

5 places to visit in Atrani, in the province of Salerno (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e Piazza Umberto I

History and architecture · Villages · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Of Roman origin (Atranum), it retains the medieval structure considered the best preserved of the Coast: alleys, arches, courtyards, squares and stairs that go down to the sea. The little square on the sea was born as a shelter for boats in stormy days; under the arches where fishermen used to repair nets today there are some trattorias. On 22 July, St Mary Magdalene is celebrated.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Atrani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa di San Salvatore de' Birecto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

X century church with square plan with pronao, three naves and barrel vaults, overlooking the square; facade with clock, staircase and atrium are baroque. At the time of the Republic of Amalfi was the Palatine chapel where the Doges received the ducal berretta. It preserves a 12th century marble slab with two peacocks and the fourteenth-century plaque of Filippa Napolitano; the bronze doors of 1087, donated by Pantaleone Viarecta, are now kept in Santa Maria Maddalena.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Atrani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.atrani.sa.it · italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Collegiata di Santa Maria Maddalena

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

Built in the thirteenth century by the Atranese as a votive church to thank the Virgin of protection from the Saracens: a unique room with barrels with two large windows and baroque façade; in the small sacristy a Roman urn in white marble. The majolica dome and the square bell tower are the symbol of the Atrani profile.

  • Where Atrani
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Grotta dei Santi

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small natural cave overlooking a lemon terrace, under the Ziro Tower: this is what remains of the Benedictine monastery of Saints Quirico and Julitta, founded in 986 by Archbishop Leo I. The walls preserve Byzantine frescoes of the 12th century with the four Evangelists.

  • Where Sotto la torre dello Ziro, Atrani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.atrani.sa.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Spiaggia di Atrani

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small sandy beach and fine gravel, divided by the Dragon, partly free and partly equipped, with clear and calm sea; in the evening the fishermen's boats leave for fishing with lamps at night.

  • Where Atrani
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it
