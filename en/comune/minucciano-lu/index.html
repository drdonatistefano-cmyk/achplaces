What to see in Minucciano

Municipality of the high Garfagnana north of the Apuan Alps, largely inside the Regional Park, with the lake of Gramolazzo, the valley of Orto di Donna at the foot of Pisanino, the Romanesque parish church of San Lorenzo, the Hermitage with copies of the stele of the Bronze Age and, in Gorfigliano, the Museum of the identity of the high Garfagnana.

Museo dell'identità dell'alta Garfagnana «Olimpio Cammelli»

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated on May 16, 2009 on the Colle di Casa di Gorfigliano, where the castle stood, it was born from the collection of over 1300 pieces donated by Emma Orsi, widow of Olimpio Cammelli; today it exhibits more than 3000 objects. The route reconstructs Gorfigliano before and after 1900, when the extraction of marble began, tells the volunteers who have recovered the village and the crafts of quarry, blacksmith, farmer, shepherd, shoemaker and woodmaker; on the upper floor the world of carpenter and a library of over 500 volumes. Free guided tours by reservation for schools, groups and tourists.

  • Where Colle di Casa, Gorfigliano, Minucciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Pieve di San Lorenzo di Vinacciara

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the 12th century, perhaps on a high medieval church, on which depended several other churches of the Val di Magra apuana. Latin cross plan with three naves and semicircular apse; of the medieval wall remains the door in the south-eastern corner, the base of the bell tower is of the fourteenth century, the seventeenth-century portal has the architrave with the Bernardine coat of arms and frames with branches and flowers, and in the bezel a marble bas-relief of San Lorenzo placed in the restoration of 1927.

  • Where Via Vinacciara 12, Pieve San Lorenzo, Minucciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Lago di Gramolazzo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Artificial lake 600 m fed by the Acquabianca stream, just outside the Park of the Apuan Alps and about 5 km from Minucciano, with the ancient village of Gramolazzo on the shore and the hamlets of Agliano, Castagnola and Verrucolette overlooking. You can take a bath, a picnic with barbecue on the shore and rent sailing or rowing boats; from the lake passes the tour of the four lakes by bike (8.1 km, challenging).

  • Where Gramolazzo, Minucciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Orto di Donna, Val Serenaia e Monte Pisanino

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The northernmost valley of the municipality, reached by Gramolazzo, has the basin at 1050 m and the Refuge Orto di Donna, about 7 km from Minucciano: from the parking lot you climb with the path CAI 180 and from the refuge starts a ring on the paths 179, 167 (Passo della Focolaccia), 168 and 186. From Val Serenaia starts the normal route to Monte Pisanino, long and tiring, for the Foce di Cardeto (path 178), the Buca della Neve, the Pizzi Altare and Maggiore and the Canale delle Rose. Passo della Focolaccia and Foce di Cardeto are among the panoramic points of the municipality; the numerous caves require speleological preparation.

  • Where Orto di Donna, Gramolazzo, Minucciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
