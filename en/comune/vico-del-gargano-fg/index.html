What to see in Vico del Gargano

Borgo del Parco Nazionale del Gargano among the most beautiful villages in Italy, one of the largest historical centers of the promontory: the Civita, Terra and Casale districts with fireplaces and portals, the Norman castle, the fourteenth-century mill of Trappeto Maratea, the necropolis of Monte Tabor and, towards the sea, San Menaio; behind the Umbra Forest.

10 places to visit in Vico del Gargano, in the province of Foggia (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico: Civita, Terra e Casale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of Gargano's largest historic centers: the Civita, the oldest nucleus that around 970 took the name of Vicus, and the Earth inside a walls with fourteen towers; below, the 17th century farmhouse. Fishbone alleys with arches, fireplaces of various shapes, houses with coats of arms and access ladders, stone portals.

  • Where Centro storico, Vico del Gargano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcogargano.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Norman quadrangular castle (XI-XII century), with definitive appearance in the fourteenth century, which dominates the access to the Civita and the Earth; intact east and west towers, Norman bifora on the tower of via Castello, court with well and numerous wounds. It was the seat of the feudalist and sold at auction by the Savoys in 1868.

  • Where Via Castello, Vico del Gargano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcogargano.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Museo comunale Trappeto Maratea

Museums · Themed

The main exhibition space of Vico, in a mill of the fourteenth century in the medieval village about 32 meters long: wooden presses, millstone and tools for oil and agricultural work, oil tank, warehouse, stable and a kitchen where the workers fed.

  • Where Via Castello Normanno-Svevo, Vico del Gargano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Museo civico archeologico Francesco delli Muti

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

Founded in 1979 and managed by the Archaeological Group Garganico . Silvio Ferri, in two rooms of the nineteenth-century house Opera Pia Monaco with the municipal library: ceramics, arrows, spears, fibulas, jewels and ambers, mostly from the necropolis of Monte Tabor. Free admission; according to the Park open Monday-Friday 9-13.30, Tuesday and Thursday 16-19.

  • Where Via Sbrasile 11, Vico del Gargano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · parcogargano.it · italia.it

Chiesa della Beatissima Vergine Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest church of Vico, on a hill next to the castle: stone portal with date 1657, three naves and eleven altars, including that of San Valentino, patron since 1618 celebrated on February 14; quadrangular bell tower and dome with ribs.

  • Where Civita, Vico del Gargano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcogargano.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Palazzo Della Bella

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Neo-Gothic Palace built by Ignazio della Bella at the beginning of the twentieth century on the model of Palazzo Vecchio in Florence, with dove-tailed battlements and corner tower with double-fores; restored, houses events and cultural activities.

  • Where Salita della Bella, Vico del Gargano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcogargano.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Vicolo del Bacio

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

The alley of about thirty meters, 50 cm wide at most, between Via San Giuseppe and the district Terra: traditionally the lovers were meeting there, who walked it from opposite directions to touch each passage.

  • Where Via San Giuseppe, Vico del Gargano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcogargano.it · italia.it

Necropoli di Monte Tabor

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the outskirts of the village, well-tombs of the Iron Age (VI-V century B.C.) excavated in limestone and arranged in concentric rows, with the dead in fetal position; the bronze and ceramic kits are at the Civic Museum.

  • Where Monte Tabor, Vico del Gargano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcogargano.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

San Menaio

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fraction on the northern coast of the promontory, about 4 km from Vico: long sandy beaches, pinewood of Aleppo (the Marzini pine forest) alternating with citrus groves and the fourteenth-century Tower of Preposti, once defended and customs; served by the panoramic line of the Gargano Railways.

  • Where San Menaio, Vico del Gargano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcogargano.it · italia.it

Foresta Umbra

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 10,500 hectares in the Gargano National Park, between Vieste, Vico del Gargano and Monte Sant'Angelo, UNESCO natural heritage since 2017 according to Italia.it: firs, oaks and beech trees, more than 200 animal species, trails of various difficulties, the Umbrian Lake and 25 picnic areas.

  • Where Foresta Umbra, Vico del Gargano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
