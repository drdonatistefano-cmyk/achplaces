What to see in Grado

The Island of the Sun is the seaside resort of the Adriatic, connected to the mainland by two bridges, with a historic centre of calli and campielli around the early Christian basilica of Sant'Eufemia, the new National Museum of underwater archaeology with the Roman ship Iulia Felix and a lagoon of canals, casoni and islands like Barbana.

9 places to visit in Grado, in the province of Gorizia (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Basilica di Sant'Eufemia

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Completed in 579 by Bishop Elijah on the site of a basilica of the fourth century and dedicated to the Aquileian protomartyrs; three naves on columns, mosaic floor with Byzantine geometric motifs, the sea wave and about 40 inscriptions of donors, golden silver altarpiece of Donato Mazzalorsa, 11th century ambo and mausoleum with Elia's monogram. The fifteenth-century bell tower is crowned by the Angelo Michele ('Anzolo San Michele'), symbol of Grado.

  • Where Campo Patriarca Elia, Grado
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it

Lapidario della Basilica di Sant'Eufemia

Museums · Archaeological

Small evocative space next to the cathedral with hundreds of sculptural fragments and epigraphs found in the castrum gradense, especially early Christians and early medievals (IV-IX century); between the Roman pieces two fronts of sarcophagus of the first III century and a stele with portrait of magistrate of the first IV century.

  • Where Campo Patriarca Elia, Grado
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it

Basilica di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The oldest church of Grado, built around the 5th century and rebuilt in the 6th century: the floor mosaics on two levels show the two phases and contain the only fish depicted in Grado; three naves on marble columns all different and a wooden statue of the Madonna delle Grazie, a destination of devotion of the Grado.

  • Where Campo Patriarca Elia, Grado
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it

Museo nazionale di archeologia subacquea dell'Alto Adriatico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Opened on December 18, 2025 on the waterfront, it exhibits the Iulia Felix, a merchant ship of Roman age recovered in the Adriatic, to tell the economy and commerce of the Upper Adriatic in Roman times. Seasonal hours: May-September Mar-dom 11-19, October-April Friday 11-18.

  • Where Via dei Provveditori 14, Grado
  • Entry €7, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museoarcheologiasubacqueagrado.cultura.gov.it

Sources: museoarcheologiasubacqueagrado.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Ancient buildings and narrow streets with small shops and terraces to discover on foot around the Patriarch's Camp; the promenade is the recommended point for sunset. Typical dish of boreto, fish with vinegar sauce.

  • Where Campo dei Patriarchi, Grado
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismofvg.it

Laguna di Grado e casoni

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

It extends from Fossalon to the island of Amphora, between canals and the straw-roofed cottages once inhabited by fishermen; remains of a submerged Roman road connected Aquileia to the port of Grado. Tamerics, poplars, junipers and pine trees; seagulls, herons, germans and terns. It is explored with boat excursions.

  • Where Laguna di Grado
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it

Santuario dell'Isola di Barbana

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

On the island at the eastern end of the lagoon, guarded by Franciscan Friars Minor: according to tradition in 582, the patriarch Elijah had it built a first church after the discovery of an image of Our Lady brought by the waters. From 1237 it is the destination of the Perdòn, procession of boats of the first Sunday of July. Line boat in about 25 minutes.

  • Where Isola di Barbana, Grado
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.santuariodibarbana.it

Sources: santuariodibarbana.it · turismofvg.it · italia.it

Riserva naturale della Valle Cavanata

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Ancient fishing valley in the easternmost part of the lagoon, an area of international value for birdwatching, reachable from Grado by bike: four routes (Five Woods, Visitor Center, Sea, Valley), interactive tour center, bike and binocular rental; the Pagoda and the Sabbia observatory are free access.

  • Where Via Grado 1, Grado
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it

Spiaggia Costa Azzurra

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historic beach vecia is located in the western part of the island between port and lagoon, among the first stretches used for bathrooms: it alternates free stretches and establishments (Tivoli, Piper, Keywest, Côte d'Azur), with backdrops that descend faster and a seafront of locals overlooking the lagoon.

  • Where Grado, lato ovest dell'isola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it
