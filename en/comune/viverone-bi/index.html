What to see in Viverone

On the shore of the largest lake of the morainic amphitheatre of Ivrea, where remains a palafittic village of the Bronze Age UNESCO heritage.

3 places to visit in Viverone, in the province of Biella (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palafitte del Lago di Viverone

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

A circular village of 70 metres in diameter of the medium bronze (1450-1400 BC), founded on almost five thousand poles; UNESCO site since 2011 among the prehistoric palafittic sites of the Alps. You don't visit: it is only approached by boat without engine and an installation is on the opposite side, near Viverone.

  • Where Lago di Viverone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.atl.biella.it

Sources: italia.it · atl.biella.it

Lago di Viverone

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest and most touristic lake of the Moorish amphitheatre of Ivrea, between waters and hills of glacial origin.

  • Where Viverone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.atl.biella.it

Sources: italia.it · atl.biella.it

Centro di documentazione del Lago di Viverone
