Castello Pignatelli e Museo di storia e cultura montecalvese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It was quoted in a document dated 1096, named after the Pignatelli; little remains of its original appearance, because the families that possessed it adapted it to the defense. The current appearance derives from the restorations after the earthquake of 1456 and from the Carafa, feudal for over three hundred years: the arch of access to the courtyard is from 1505, the ramparts of the eighteenth century, and preserves walls and a cylindrical tower. It houses the multimedia museum with vintage photographs and testimonies of local culture; visit by reservation.

Museo della religiosità montecalvese e della memoria pompiliana

Museums · Themed

Founded on 29 December 1898 thanks to the generosity of the faithful, it is one of the oldest museums in Campania and occupies the birthplace of San Pompilio Maria Pirrotti (1710-1766). In five rooms he exhibits objects from the house of the saint and works of art from other churches of Montecalvo, including the statue of the Mother Bella dell'Abbondanza (XVI-XVII century) and a seventeenth-century portrait of the saint. Visit by reservation.

Chiesa e santuario di San Pompilio Maria Pirrotti

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Montecalvo's mother church and sanctuary dedicated to the patron saint, disciple of Giuseppe Calasanzio, founder of the Pie Schools; it is linked to the museum of the memory of the blowjob and is the stop of the hike "Sui passi di San Pompilio."

Trappeto, villaggio di case-grotta

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

An ancient village of cave houses, houses dug into the typical rock of the Mediterranean, which probably date back to the Neolithic age, if not to the upper Paleolithic.

Palazzi nobiliari del centro storico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The center preserves Palazzo Bozzuti-Pizzillo (16th century, of the noble Neapolitan family of the Bozzuti), the portal with balcony of Palazzo Caccese (second half of the 17th century), Palazzo De Cillis (XVIII century, portal in stone with floral motifs), Palazzo De Marco (XIX century, portals with stone coats of arms) and Palazzo Peluso, of which today interest the cellars extended under the whole area.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Bolle della Malvizza

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest complex of mud volcanoes in the southern Apennines: small cones emitting hydrocarbons, especially methane, and a continuous flow of slightly brackish waters. The legend sees the punishment of a host that robbed the travelers of the Via Traiana; not far away, in the locality Ripa della Conca, between cliffs and calanchi, there is the Eye of the Devil.

  • Where Località Malvizza, Montecalvo Irpino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
