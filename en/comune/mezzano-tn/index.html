What to see in Mezzano

Rural village of the Primiero at 640 meters, at the foot of the Pale di San Martino and in the Natural Park of Paneveggio Pale di San Martino: alleys in stone and wood, about 400 gardens, the outdoor exhibition Cataste & Canzei, the small museum Tabia del Rico and the church of San Giovanni Battista on the meadows above the village.

4 places to visit in Mezzano, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo di Mezzano, canisèle e orti

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Rural buildings in stone and wood along the ancient road to Feltre, with alleys (canisèle), stone fountains, about twenty murals on legends and nature, over one hundred inscriptions on the walls and about 400 gardens, the green heart of the country; themed routes on water, gardens, rural architecture and engravings. On the red chairs scattered around the village you ring the bell and arrives an inhabitant to tell stories and anecdotes of the village.

  • Where Mezzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · sanmartino.com · visittrentino.info

Cataste & Canzei

Museums · Modern art · Free entry · Viewpoint

Permanent exhibition of contemporary art made with the piles of wood, scattered among alleys, androns, squares, balconies and courtyards, which is enriched every year with new works with sculptors from all over the world: a face crying, the shuttle of the frame, a giant panicle, landscapes of woods as inlays. On the APT website you can download the route map and a brochure.

  • Where Centro storico, Mezzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: sanmartino.com · visittrentino.info · italia.it

Tabìa del Rico

Museums · Themed

Small museum dedicated to material culture and rural life, which shows what life in the village was like.

  • Where Mezzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Chiesetta di San Giovanni Battista ai prati di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Church of 1514, place of devotion for the inhabitants of Mezzano, on the meadows above the village about an hour walk from the center, with views of the Vette Feltrine and the Cimonega group. We arrive the easy walk Cordognè-San Giovanni, which departs from Poit (about 10 minutes by car) and is indicated especially during the foliage.

  • Where Prati di San Giovanni (Prati Liendri), Mezzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · sanmartino.com
