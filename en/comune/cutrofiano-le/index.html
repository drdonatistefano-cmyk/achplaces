What to see in Cutrofiano

Borgo del Salento of long ceramic tradition, documented by the Municipal Museum of Ceramics; the historic center collects Palazzo Filomarini, the church of the Immaculate and the Matrice Church, while in a former clay quarry were born the Park of Fossil and the Malacological Museum of Clays.

Museo comunale della ceramica

Museums · Themed

Established in 1985 as an exhibition of traditional terracotta, it has been enriched with donations and purchases of majolica and documents the country's long ceramic tradition with collections from prehistory to contemporary age; since 2010 it hosts the entire collection of terracotta whistles donated by the Milanese collector Mario Briosi. It shares the premises with the Municipal Library, which has a section of studies on ceramics. Hours indicated by MiC: Monday-Saturday 8:30-13:30 and 15:00-20:00, Sunday closed.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Cutrofiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Parco dei Fossili e Museo malacologico delle argille

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Former clay quarry closed at the end of the seventies, with geological layers of marine origin partially rich in fossils; after the abandonment it was reclaimed in the nineties by Colacem with the planting of 8,000 trees. In 1999 the Fossil Park and the Malacological Museum of Clays were born, set up in a farmhouse of the seventeenth century: one of the very few examples in Italy and in Europe of the recovery of a quarry abandoned for scientific and educational purposes.

  • Where Contrada Lustrelle, SS Cutrofiano-Aradeo, Cutrofiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Centro storico, Palazzo Filomarini e chiese

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The historical centre contains the most remarkable buildings of the village: Palazzo Filomarini, also known as Palazzo della Principessa, the church of the Immaculate Virgin and the Matrice Church, parish dedicated to Santa Maria della Snow.

  • Where Centro storico, Cutrofiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
