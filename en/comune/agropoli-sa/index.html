What to see in Agropoli

A coastal town of the Cilento National Park, Vallo di Diano and Alburni, on the promontory fortified by the Byzantines who called it "Akropolis": the ancient village is reached by the steps and the Gate of Earth, on top the Angevin-Aragonese castle with views of the Gulf of Salerno; along the coast the towers of San Marco and San Francesco and the church of Santa Maria di Costantinopoli overlooking the sea.

Castello angioino-aragonese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The origins date back to the Byzantines, who during the Greek-Gothic war (535-553) fortified the promontory to defend the landing places; the current structure is Aragonese, from the end of the fifteenth century, with the entrance elevated on high arches and drawbridge and the monumental portal in tuff beating. It has a triangular plan with three circular towers, a large dry moat and walls with a walk of covered rounds, blackbirds and wounds; inside the square of arms, today garden and theatre, the hall of the French to the north and the baronial building to the east. In summer, the municipality indicates opening every day 10-13 and 16-19, in winter reduced hours; guided tours by reservation (Tourist Office 0974 827480).

Centro storico e Porta di Terra

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient village on the promontory, an example of medieval urbanism, is reached by a large steps staircase that survived the modern levelings; on the entrance door is still visible the coat of arms of the Sanfelice dukes, who bought the feud in 1660. It preserves walls, the typical "scaloni" and ancient city gates such as the Gate of Earth.

  • Where Centro storico, Agropoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: promozione.cilentoediano.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria di Costantinopoli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Church on a terrace overlooking the sea, which the Cilento Park dates back to the 6th century; it preserves sacred works of art linked to the devotion of the community. Our Lady of Constantinople is at the center of the seafaring festival where the statue is carried in procession on the water, followed by fishing boats.

  • Where Centro storico, Agropoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo Archeologico - Palazzo Civico delle Arti

Museums · Archaeological

Born as an archaeological section of the Municipality in the Palazzo Civico delle Arti, it was established as a stable museum with resolution of February 2026, with statute and technical-scientific committee coordinated by Fabio Astone, in collaboration with the Superintendence. It exhibits finds from the municipal territory recovered over decades, in a collection started in the seventies; in the same palace the Spazio Mare and the Civic Library " Costabile Cilento" . Timetable indicated by the Municipality: Tuesday and Thursday 9-13, Saturday 9-13 and 15-19, other days by reservation (0974 277823).
