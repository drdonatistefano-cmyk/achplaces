What to see in Massa d'Albe

Municipality of the Sirente-Velino Park with the hamlets of Albe, Forme and Corona, where stands Alba Fucens, a Roman colony of 303 BC with hole, basilica, thermal baths and amphitheatre, dominated by the church of San Pietro obtained from a temple of Apollo.

Area archeologica di Alba Fucens

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Roman colony founded at the end of the 4th century BC in fair territory, excavated by the Belgian Academy between 1949 and 1979: polygonal walls with four doors, diribitorium, three-nave basilica, macellum, baths with suspensurae and mosaics, shrine of Hercules and elliptical amphitheatre of the 1st century A.D. In 537 it was the winter district of the troops of Belisario; it was abandoned around the sixth century. Open and freely accessible area.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.parcosirentevelino.it

Chiesa di San Pietro ad Alba Fucens

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

On the hill of San Pietro, obtained from the temple of Apollo of the third century BC, converted to worship in the sixth century and expanded in the twelfth with three aisles, apse and crypt. It preserves an ambo and an iconostasis cosmates of the 13th century with inlays in porphyry and numerous graffiti; damaged by the earthquake of 1915, it was restored in 1955-57. Visit by reservation required (340 6255973).

Ruderi del Castello Orsini e di Albe Vecchia

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill of San Nicola there are the remains of the medieval village of Albe and the Castle Orsini of the 14th century, destroyed by the earthquake of 1915.

  • Entry Free
