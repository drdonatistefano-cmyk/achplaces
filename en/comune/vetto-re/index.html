What to see in Vetto

Holiday center at 447 meters in a basin above the Enza, in the Reggiano Apennines, with the parish church of San Lorenzo mentioned in 1142; the territory is a mosaic of medieval villages linked to the Da Palù (Dalla Palude), lords loyal to the Canossa, from Legoreccio to Gottono, Crovara, Pineto and Spigone, crossed by the Path of the Ducati and the valleys of the Tassaro and the Tassobbio.

11 places to visit in Vetto, in the province of Reggio Emilia (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish church of Romanesque origin, mentioned in 1142 and rebuilt in the middle of the seventeenth century; the fifteenth-century bell tower preserves the Romanesque system. It is the main historical monument of the village.

  • Where Vetto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Borgo di Legoreccio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Ancient feudal capital of the Da Palù and among the most significant villages of the Reggian hill: the Court of Dalla Palude has a seventeenth-century court and happened there, on 17 November 1944, the massacre of the partisans remembered as Martyrs of Legoreccio; next to the sixteenth-century tower house of the Rabotti and the fifteenth-century House of the Terzi, called "La Dogana" in memory of the border between Duchy of Modena and Parma State.

  • Where Legoreccio, Vetto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.vetto.re.it · italia.it

Borgo di Gottano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village at 633 meters on the ridge with 360 degrees views of the Apennines, stone underpasses and portals of 1694 and 1770; the church of Saints Peter and Paul, documented from the thirteenth century, has for bell tower a medieval tower of the ancient fortress, where the legend places the golden cast of Matilde di Canossa. Near the oratory of the Madonna di Montalto, before 1300, and on a hill to the west the seventeenth-century oratory of the Nativity of Mary, called "della Formice" for the swarms of winged ants that manifested there every three years about.

  • Where Gottano, Vetto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.vetto.re.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Borgo e castello di Crovara

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

One of the smallest hamlets, rich in history and legends: around 1250 the castle was considered very fortified and shelter of the Parma Ghibellines, and there remain two galleries, part of a wall and the base of a four-metre-high tower. The church of San Giorgio preserves an Annunciation of the Guercinesque school and a baroque tabernacle in golden wood.

  • Where Crovara, Vetto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.vetto.re.it · italia.it

Borgo di Pineto

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Borgo on top of a sandstone hill at 663 meters: the House of Da Palude, damaged by the earthquake of 1920, has a loggia with 17th century frescoes, a portal dated 1583 and an internal oratory; in the center a tower house with 17th century stone rosette and downstream the Millaccio, an ancient horizontal wheel mill.

  • Where Pineto, Vetto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.vetto.re.it · italia.it

Borgo di Spigone

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Medieval village at 676 meters between the streams Volvara, Tassaro and Scalucchia, feud of Dalla Palude that held the salt warehouses, reached by an ancient via matildica selciata still existing; it preserves a sixteenth-century tower house and portals of the sixteenth-sixth century. Nearby is a monumental chestnut, among the oldest in the province.

  • Where Spigone, Vetto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.vetto.re.it · italia.it

Rosano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Frazione along the state road between Castelnovo ne' Monti and Vetto, overlooking the Pietra di Bismantova: the church of San Lorenzo is mentioned in a document dated 1112 and the village preserves a majesty of 1642, the tower house of the Bazzoli and eighteenth-century buildings.

  • Where Rosano, Vetto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.vetto.re.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church recently renovated, perhaps of pre-Romanesque origin, with traces of Carolingian and frescoes of the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries; it is reached leaving the provincial 513 to Rosano, about three kilometers before Castelnovo in the mountains.

  • Where Santo Stefano, Vetto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Castellaro

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Borgo with tower house mentioned in 1313 as "fortilicium" with military garrison; the oratory has vaulted vaults in brick, a rosette of the seventeenth century and an altarpiece of the seventeenth century attributed to the Emilian school. To the west is the Martin Hill, which may have hosted a Celtic-Ligurian village.

  • Where Castellaro, Vetto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.vetto.re.it · italia.it

Sentiero Natura della Valle del Tassaro e cascata

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Ring of about 2 hours and 30 minutes Scalucchia-Spigone-Pineto-Legoreccio-Crovara-Scalucchia between medieval houses and woods with roe deer, linked to the history of Matilde di Canossa; the stream Tassaro houses freshwater shrimps and vairons and a little downstream of Scalucchia forms a waterfall, reachable on foot from the Chichino Mill along the Tassobbio.

  • Where Scalucchia, Vetto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.vetto.re.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Sentiero dei Ducati

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Trekking from the plain to the Tyrrhenian, from Quattro Castella and Canossa to Luni along the valleys of Enza and Magra, renewed in 2004 by the Province of Reggio Emilia that cares for it with the CAI; marked white-red, has a stage point in Vetto (Crongolo-Vetto and Vetto-Camporella).

  • Where Vetto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.vetto.re.it · italia.it
