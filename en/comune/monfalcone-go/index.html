What to see in Monfalcone

Città dei cantieri navali sull'alto Adriatico: MuCa tells a century of great ships in the working village of Panzano, the medieval fortress watches from the Karst on the theme park of the Great War, in the center the medieval museum and the contemporary art gallery, to the sea Marina Julia.

MuCa – Museo della Cantieristica

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The only museum of this kind in Italy, on the ground floor of the former workers' hotel in Panzano, the company city built in the 1920s by the Cosulichs around the yard, next to the ports where cruise ships are still built. More than a century of history of the yard and the city with large interactive videos, a sensory tunnel, 3D reconstructions and the simulator of a crane of the twenties; the path continues outdoors in the village of Panzano.

  • Where Via del Mercato 3, Panzano, Monfalcone
  • Entry €7 (reduced €5; free with FVGcard)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it

Rocca di Monfalcone

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the karst hill above Piazza della Repubblica, the symbol of the city: prehistoric castle, castle of the Patriarch of Aquileia, Venetian fort, Napoleonic garrison and Italian observatory in the Great War. The walk on the Venetian walls preserves traces of the fighting of 1915-1917; in the mast an exhibition on the Karst territory and, according to PromoTurismoFVG, the paleontological museum. Open Saturday and Sunday 9.30-13 and 16.30-19; you walk up the paths of the Great War Park or with the RoccaBus.

  • Where Monfalcone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it

Parco tematico della Grande Guerra

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Trenches and walkways of 1915-1918 on the Karst behind the city, the scene of battles between June 1915 and May 1917. CAI trails, carts and trenches connect it to the open-air museums of the Dolina dei Bersaglieri, San Martino del Carso and Monte San Michele.

  • Where Monfalcone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it

Museo medievale

Museums · City history

On the ground floor of the Town Hall: sections of the medieval walls, remains of houses, a tannery and a warehouse, ceramics, coins, tools and a one-and-a-half-hand sword found in the area of a blacksmith shop. Open on Wednesdays 10-13 and Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays 10-13 and 15-18 (in summer 16-19), with guided tours at 11 am and at 4 pm.

  • Where Piazza Unità d'Italia 1, Monfalcone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · italia.it

Galleria comunale d'arte contemporanea

Museums · Modern art

In the restored building of the old market in the center, since 2004 member of the AMACI and among the major spaces for contemporary art of the region: a collection of drawings started in 2004 and the bottom of the Montfalcone engraver Tranquillo Marangoni (1912-1992), with over 950 cataloged xylographs.

  • Where Monfalcone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismofvg.it · patrimonioculturale.regione.fvg.it · italia.it
