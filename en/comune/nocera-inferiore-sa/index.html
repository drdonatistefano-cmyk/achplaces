What to see in Nocera Inferiore

The ancient Nuceria Alfaterna, capital of the San Marzano tomato: the castle of the Park that hosted Dante and Boccaccio, the archaeological museum of the Agro Nocerino in the convent of Sant'Antonio, the district of the Vescovado and medieval convents.

Museo archeologico provinciale dell'Agro Nocerino

Museums · Archaeological

Established in 1964 and opened in July 1965 in the convent of Sant'Antonio: plastic, graphic and photographic documentation, panels on ancient sources (Virgilio, Silio Italico, Stazio) and silver and bronze coins coined by ancient Nuceria in the Sannithian age.

Castello del Parco (Palazzo Fienga)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill of the city park, where Dante and Boccaccio stayed and St. Ludwig of Anjou was born; in 1385 Pope Urban VI imprisoned some cardinals there. The ruins of the manor and the pentagonal Norman tower next to the Palazzo dei Fienga remain; in the summer it hosts Jazz in the Park.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa e Pinacoteca di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the thirteenth century, originally dedicated to Saint Francis, wanted by brothers Guidone and Pietro Filangieri; the attached art gallery, opened in 1995, preserves works of the sixteenth century including tables by Andrea Sabatini and two tables attributed to Marco Cardisco.

Cattedrale di San Prisco e Borgo del Vescovado

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The cathedral of the first bishop St Prisco, in the center of the characteristic district of the Bishopric around the palace of the curia; the diocese, probably from the III-IV century, was restored in 1386 by Urban VI.

Convento di Sant'Anna

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Founded in 1282 by Peter, bishop of Capaccio and adviser to Charles II of Anjou, transformed in 1685-1686: Gothic traces, a frescoed Annunciation of the fourteenth century in sacristy and three great canvases of the Solimena.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.nocera-inferiore.sa.it · italia.it

Convento di Santa Chiara

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Clarisse Monastery of the late thirteenth century: in the church a canvas by Angelo Solimena from 1680, a fresco from 1677 and the altar designed by Orazio Solimena in 1789; you enter from a classic portal on the 18th state road.
