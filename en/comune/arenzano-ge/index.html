What to see in Arenzano

Locality at the entrance of the Riviera di Ponente, about 20 km from Genoa, at the foot of Mount Beigua: a historic center overlooking the sea, long beaches, the park of Villa Negrotto Cambiaso with peacocks, the Sanctuary of the Child of Prague on the hill and the cycling track to the sea up to Varazze.

6 places to visit in Arenzano, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Arenzano in the guide with map and filters

Parco di Villa Negrotto Cambiaso

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

Liberty villa with medieval-inspired decorations, once home to noble Genoese families and now home to the Town Hall, surrounded by a large park where the Mediterranean flora joins exotic plants, cedars of Lebanon and centuries-old trees; along the avenues iron greenhouses in Art Nouveau style, and peacocks live free all year round.

  • Where Arenzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: lamialiguria.it · italia.it

Santuario del Bambino Gesù di Praga

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill behind the historic centre, it is a pilgrimage destination and a sign of the link between Liguria and the Czech Republic. Born from the convent of the Scalzi Carmelites of Genoa: in 1888 Father Leopold of St. Joseph (Beccaro) chose Arenzano for a house of formation of young people dedicated to St. Teresa of Jesus.

  • Where Arenzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: lamialiguria.it · visitgenoa.it · italia.it

Pista ciclopedonale Arenzano-Varazze

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 11 km above sea level, passing through Cogoleto, the Pineta and the coastal park of the Invrea Plains, obtained in 1968 on a former railway line and today part of the Tirrenic Cycleway, with benches, fountains and shady galleries; in the stretch of the Marina Grande the murals dedicated to Fabrizio De André, who held his last concert in Arenzano in Liguria on August 8, 1998.

  • Where Marina Grande, Arenzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: lamialiguria.it · italia.it

Centro storico, lungomare e spiagge

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town, with alleys and elegant shops, overlooks the sea; the beaches are spacious and spacious and there is no shortage of rocky coves for those who love a more wild sea. On the promenade bars and restaurants offer pesto and stuffed anchovies, and in the center the freshly baked focaccia. IAT office in Lungomare Kennedy 10 (tel. 010 9127581).

  • Where Lungomare J.F. Kennedy, Arenzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitgenoa.it · lamialiguria.it

Relitto della petroliera Haven

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches

In front of the city lies the wreck of the oil tanker Haven, one of the most famous diving points of Liguria; the site is challenging and must be faced with a local diving center.

  • Where Specchio d'acqua davanti ad Arenzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitgenoa.it · italia.it

Muvita Science Center

Museums · Science and university · Paid entry

Centro di animazione scientifica, the first in Italy dedicated to the relationship between man, energy and climate, with educational paths and workshops for young and adult people on climate machine, energy sources, saving and energy efficiency. According to the Mic card (updated to 2020) opens Monday to Friday 8-18.

  • Where Via Marconi, Arenzano
  • Entry €7, reduced €6
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.muvita.it

Sources: muvita.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it
