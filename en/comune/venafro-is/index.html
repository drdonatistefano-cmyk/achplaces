What to see in Venafro

Roman city at the foot of Mount Santa Croce, among centuries-old olive groves: the Castello Pandono with the horses painted in relief for Count Enrico, the archaeological museum in the former convent of Santa Chiara, the Roman amphitheatre of Verlasce, the cathedral built with the blocks of the Roman theatre and the basilica of San Nicandro.

Castello Pandone – Museo nazionale

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

On Sannitic and Roman walls, Longobard fence of the tenth century with towers, enlarged by the Angioini after the earthquake of 1349 with three cylindrical towers and the moat; in the sixteenth century Count Enrico Pando transformed it into a residence and had the halls decorated with horses of his stable, relief painted in front, each with name, age, race and H of Henricus. 14th century staircase, noble floor, loggia, Salone dei Conti and garden; it is home to the National Museum, which also preserves the Polyptych of the Passion of Christ.

History and architecture · Archaeology

The ancient Roman amphitheatre beyond the area of the springs, incorporated into the urban fabric; part, with theater, nymph, ancient roads and an aqueduct of about thirty kilometers, of the Roman Venafro.

Cattedrale di Venafro

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Isolated among the olive trees outside the center, with the Santa Croce mountain behind: facade and apses are built with square blocks of the nearby Roman theatre; inside frescoes and marbles. The current aspect is due to the restorations of the 1960s, which eliminated the baroque parts.

  • Where Via Duomo, Venafro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: old.comune.venafro.is.it · visitmolise.eu
