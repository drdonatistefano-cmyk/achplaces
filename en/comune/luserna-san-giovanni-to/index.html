What to see in Luserna San Giovanni

The town of Val Pellice whose history is linked to the Waldenses, present since the 13th century and free to profess their worship only from 1848; to see the ancient village of Luserna with the Loggia dei Mercanti and the Palazzo dei Conti, the Waldensian Temple of San Giovanni, the Museum of the game and the toy and the astronomical Observatory of Val Pellice.

6 places to visit in Luserna San Giovanni, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo di Luserna

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient village of the village Luserna, which can be reached starting from Piazza Partigiani, home of the Town Hall. It gathers the parish church, the Loggia dei Mercanti, the Palazzo dei Conti of Luserna, the Parish House and the Tower of San Francesco.

  • Where Frazione Luserna, Luserna San Giovanni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Tempio valdese di Luserna San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Temple of the Waldensian community, also called the Bellonatti Church: the project was approved by the Napoleonic government on April 9, 1806 and the community built it with voluntary work and own contributions, completing it thanks to the gifts of foreign friends. It rises in the hamlet of San Giovanni, in front of the Catholic church of San Giovanni Battista. Visits by reservation or on Sunday morning during worship.

  • Where Frazione San Giovanni, Luserna San Giovanni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish outside the historic centre, in front of the Waldensian Temple. The façade has a central body with tympanum supported by two pairs of pilasters, decorated in Baroque style with a fresco by the Eternal Father; the trilithic portal has a high frame in white stucco with the dedicatory inscription and a niche with the statue of St. John the Baptist.

  • Where Strada del Saret 2, Luserna San Giovanni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Chiesa del Sacro Cuore di Gesù

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the twentieth century expansion area, with facade in stone blocks of Luserna. The three large, centered windows have lead windows with angels; above the door a high relief depicts Jesus Pantocrator with two young workers in early twentieth century clothes.

  • Where Via Roma 77, Luserna San Giovanni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Museo civico del gioco e del giocattolo «Giovanni Peyrot»

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Also known as "The village of the balocchi" (the "land of the balocchi") collects ancient and modern toys, prestigious games and optical illusions, a puppet theater, slide projections and ancient fairy tales.

  • Where Via Generale A. Diaz 21, Luserna San Giovanni
  • Entry €3,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Osservatorio astronomico Val Pellice

Museums · Science and university · Viewpoint

It houses a planetarium installed in 1994, indicated as the first in Piedmont for the study of stars; in the following years an antenna of radio astronomy was added and, in 1998, a tower with dome in fiberglass with optical instruments to observe and photograph objects of the deep sky. Open by reservation.

  • Where Località Bric del Colletto 1, Luserna San Giovanni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org
