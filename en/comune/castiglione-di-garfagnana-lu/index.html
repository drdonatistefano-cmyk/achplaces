Fortified village of the Garfagnana, already Roman castrum, closed by the walls and towers built by Lucca in 1371: a rare almost intact walled village.

Rocca di Castiglione

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On Castrum Leonis Romano, destroyed and rebuilt several times, strengthened by Lucca with five towers; walls 740 meters long and high up to 17. In the summer you visit the brigands' cells in the mastium.

  • Where Località Cigiana di Sopra 14, Castiglione di Garfagnana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
