What to see in Mondolfo

Walled village of the valley of Cesano, among the most beautiful villages in Italy, with two walls (the second refitted by Francesco di Giorgio Martini), the church-pinacoteca of Sant'Agostino and, outside the village, the abbey of San Gervasio of Bulgaria; downstream the seaside resort of Marotta.

Chiesa monumentale di Sant'Agostino

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Part of the Augustinian convent complex, documented by the end of the 13th century and today in the 16th century reconstruction with 18th century renovations: unique hall in red brick with barrel vault and twelve altars carved, carved and golden. It is a real art gallery, with paintings by Claudio Ridolfi, Guerrieri, Presutti, Tiarini and the workshop of Barocci, a polychrome fifteenth-century sandstone Pietà, a bar of the 16th-century Magistrate and a 17th-century cloister next door.

Mura, Bastione di Sant'Anna e belvedere

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The first circle, oval, has the oldest parts to the sixth century and the present masonry to the fifteenth; the second, of the thirteenth century, was adapted to firearms by Francesco di Giorgio Martini. The Bastione di Sant'Anna is today an Italian garden with geometric flower beds, and from the viewpoint along the walls you can see the Conero and the marina; you can also enter from Porta Fanestre and Porta Santa Maria.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Centro storico e piazza

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The large central square is dominated by the neo-Gothic Town Hall of 1931 with a civic tower; in the village the sixteenth-century Palazzo Giraldi Della Rovere and the Sferisterio for the Renaissance game of the ball with a bracelet.

  • Entry Free

Collegiata di Santa Giustina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Pieve attested in 1290, renewed and erected as collegiate by Pope Urban VIII in 1635; on the singing hall above the entrance an organ of 1776 Gaetano Callido, the greatest exponent of the eighteenth-century Venetian organ school.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Oratorio di San Giovanni Decollato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small church that in 1612 belonged to the confraternity of the "good death" (Good Death), it is accessed by a large seventeenth-century arch and houses an altar in golden wood, a crucifix considered miraculous and a canvas with Salome and the head of the Baptist.

Chiesa di Santa Maria del Soccorso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built at the end of the sixteenth century and rebuilt inside in the eighteenth century, it has three Renaissance portals in sandstone and inside paintings of masters of the seventeenth century including Claudio Ridolfi and Giuliano Persciutti.

  • Entry To be checked

Abbazia di San Gervasio di Bulgaria

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Founded between the 5th and 6th centuries, it has a basilica plant similar to the large churches of Ravenna and 18th century interior; in the crypt a 6th century sarcophagus, indicated as the largest of the Marches, with Christian symbols, crosses, laurel crowns and two peacocks.
