What to see in Claut

Valcellina municipality in the Friulian Dolomites Nature Park, with a small building, ice rink, ice park and ski slopes; it houses the Casa Clautana museum and the dinosaur footprints of the Casavento hut, the first found in the Park.

Centro visite e Museo Casa clautana

Museums · Themed

Next to the Park Information Office, the museum collects the history of the community and tells the life of the women of Valcellina, including housework, fields, stables and street selling of the wooden objects built by the men in winter; the project includes the recovery of structures related to water, stone and wood and the restoration of a fum's, ancient dwelling.

  • Where Claut
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.parcodolomitifriulane.it

Sources: parcodolomitifriulane.it

Impronte di dinosauro della baita Casavento

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Discovered by chance on September 30, 1994 by a school girl, are the first footprints of the theropod dinosaur found in the Park, imprinted in the Dolomia Principale del Triassico, more than 200 million years ago, on a rock of the Ciol de Ciasavent stream; the route is marked with signs and panels. It is the most visited site in the country.

  • Where Torrente Ciol de Ciasavent, presso la baita Casavento, Claut
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.parcodolomitifriulane.it

Sources: parcodolomitifriulane.it

Sentiero degli Alpini

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Itinerary on the track opened by the Alps in the early twentieth century, the scene of violent fighting in the First World War, today also of geological interest for dinosaur footprints near the Casavento cabin.

  • Where Claut
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.parcodolomitifriulane.it

Sources: parcodolomitifriulane.it

Sentiero natura Col de L'Piais e Truoi Arthith

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Two easy and combinable routes, suitable for everyone: from the old village of Massurie you climb with moderate slopes to the Col de L'Piais, while the Truoi Arthith is a short walk through the meadows.

  • Where Borgo Massurie, Claut
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.parcodolomitifriulane.it
