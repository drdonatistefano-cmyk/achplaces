What to see in Stalettì

Municipality of Costa degli Aranci linked to Cassiodoro, which founded the Vivarium monastery in the gulf: on the headland of Copanello remain the "Vasche di Cassiodoro" excavated in the cliff, while Caminia and Copanello are awarded beaches with the Vele Legambiente; in the village the Museo naturalistico Libero Gatti has a botanical garden overlooking the Ionian.

Museo naturalistico Libero Gatti

Museums · Science and university

The route is divided into seven sections, including a marina with more than 1000 shells, one dedicated to flowers and a diorama of the coral reef; it is part of a garden-botanical garden of about 60,000 m2 on the Ionian Sea. According to the MiC card it is open every day 16-19, in July and August 16-23.

  • Where Piazzale Elvira Marincola Cattaneo, Stalettì
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Vasche di Cassiodoro

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the promontory of Copanello three pools dug into the rock served as fish breeding for the monastery Vivarium, which Cassiodoro founded here around 555 A.D.; today they are warm water pools in the white granite cliff, where in summer they take a bath.

  • Where Copanello, Stalettì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Spiagge di Caminia e Copanello

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Award with the Vele Legambiente: in Caminia a long stretch of free beach alternates with establishments up to the promontory of Copanello, with calm sea and protected from the rocks, suitable for diving by day and night. Copanello is suitable for sailing.

  • Where Caminia e Copanello, Stalettì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Promontorio di Copanello: Grotta di San Gregorio e resti cassiodorei

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the promontory, also by sailing boat, you reach the Cave of San Gregorio; on land you can see all year round the remains of the church of Panaja, the tomb of Cassiodoro and the ancient church of San Martino, stages of the tour of the Cassiodoro places.

  • Where Copanello, Stalettì
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
