What to see in Baschi

Medieval village in the Tiber River Park, documented since 1235 as a castle of the lords of Baschi: the church of San Nicolò di Ippolito Scalza with the polyptych of Giovanni di Paolo, the Antiquarium and the archaeological area of Scoppieto with the Roman ceramic factory, the Ovoteca di Civitella del Lago and the lake of Corbara.

Antiquarium comunale

Museums · Archaeological

From 2000 on the ground floor and basement of the Town Hall, exhibits finds of the territory from the protohistory (XIII century BC) to the high Middle Ages (VI century A.D.). The ground floor is dedicated to the factory of Scoppieto, active from the end of the 1st century BC to 70-80 A.D.: production cycle of the sealed earth, model of Roman furnace and trade along the Tiber until Alexandria of Egypt, with models of ships.

  • Where Piazza del Comune, Baschi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · umbriatourism.it

Area archeologica di Scoppieto

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On a plateau above the Tiber Valley, the excavations of the University of Perugia since 1995 have brought to light a sanctuary of the fourth century BC and above it a factory of sealed earth, lamps and bricks of the end of the first century BC, whose signed vases were found throughout the Mediterranean; excavated about 2,000 m2 with potters' posts, clay processing areas and furnaces. The area was then inhabited until the fourth century AD.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · umbriatourism.it

Museo Ovoteca di Civitella del Lago

Museums · Themed

In the medieval village of Civitella del Lago, overlooking the lake of Corbara, it collects the painted eggs or carved by artists, amateurs and students for the National Competition Exhibition "Ovo Pinto" which is held every Easter since 1982, tradition of Roman origin still alive in central Italy.

  • Where Piazza Mazzini, Civitella del Lago (Baschi)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · umbriatourism.it

Chiesa di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt in 1576 by the Orvieto architect Ippolito Scalza (replaced by Antonio Carrarino for the bell tower) on a first church of the twelfth century; it preserves a polyptych by Giovanni di Paolo of about 1440.

  • Where Baschi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Borgo di Baschi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified village documented since 1235 under the Basque family; in the middle of the sixteenth century, after a massacre, the inhabitants destroyed the castle and ruled themselves until the unification of Italy.

  • Where Centro storico, Baschi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Lago di Corbara e Gole del Forello

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Artificial basin created by barring the Tiber near Corbara, with about 207 million m3 of water and 51 m of maximum depth, in the Tiber River Park established in 1995: it opens from the Forello Gorges, with travertine walls and caves; habitats of herons, deer and mouflons, fishing destination for carp, canoe and trekking.

  • Where Lago di Corbara, Baschi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it
