What to see in Sarno

Città dell'Agro nocerino-Sarnese at the sources of the Sarno river: the National Archaeological Museum in the eighteenth century Palazzo Capua, the Hellenistic-Roman theatre, the Episcopio Cathedral with the ceiling of the Solimena and the sanctuary of the Madonna di Foce.

4 places to visit in Sarno, in the province of Salerno (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo archeologico nazionale della Valle del Sarno – Palazzo Capua

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the 18th-century noble palace of the Ungaros and then of the Capuas, with courtyard and staircase open to three loggia: the history of the valley from Neolithic to the Middle Ages, with the furnishings of the tombs of the Iron Age, vases from Pithekoussai and Cuma, the painted tomb of the warrior of the late IV century BC, finds of the sacred area of Foce and of the Roman villas. Open Tuesday-Friday 9-19, Saturday and holidays 9-14, closed on Monday.

  • Where Via Cavour 7, Sarno
  • Entry 5 € (reduced 2 €, free under 18 years)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it

Teatro ellenistico-romano

History and architecture · Archaeology

Theatre of the first century BC, transformed into Augustian age with a proscenium in masonry decorated with paintings; the remains of the cavea emerged in the excavations of the sixties of the twentieth century. The first row of seats for the authorities is in tuff blocks, flanked by a sphinx and a winged lion.

  • Where Sarno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Duomo di San Michele Arcangelo a Episcopio

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Bishop's seat since 1066, inaugurated in 1627 and severely damaged by the eruption of Vesuvius in 1631; the seventeenth-century coffered ceiling of Angelo and Francesco Solimena has 21 canvases, the cone of the presbytery depicts Saint Michael who defeats Lucifer.

  • Where Località Episcopio, Sarno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comunesarno.it

Sources: comunesarno.it

Santuario di Santa Maria della Foce

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

At the Sarno river, on a more ancient structure: the current building was begun in 1631 and consecrated in 1720, the year of the high altar.

  • Where Località Foce, Sarno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comunesarno.it

Sources: comunesarno.it
