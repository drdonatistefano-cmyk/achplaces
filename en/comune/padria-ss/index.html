What to see in Padria

Borgo of just over 600 inhabitants about 50 km from Sassari and Alghero, the ancient Gurulis Vetus cited by Ptolemy: the Gothic-Aragonese parish church of Santa Giulia of 1520 with the Civic Archaeological Museum next to, the baronial palace on Palattu on the hill of San Paolo and a territory with about 40 nuraghi and the canyon of Su' Entale.

6 places to visit in Padria, in the province of Sassari (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di Santa Giulia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Wanted by Baron Bernardino de Ferrera and the bishop of Bosa Pietro de Sena and inaugurated in 1520, as evidenced by the inscription on the façade, it is considered one of the greatest examples of Gothic-Aragonese architecture in Sardinia: a single nave of five spans, portal gigliato, rosette and main chapel with keystone representing Santa Giulia on the cross. A crystal floor shows the oldest structures below, from the nuragic well to the early Christian church. The saint is celebrated on 22 May.

  • Where Centro storico, Padria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In Monte Granatico of the late nineteenth century next to Santa Giulia: Neolithic finds of the culture of Ozieri, reproductions of the nuragic bronzes of Padria preserved in Cagliari and Sassari, the votive deposit of St. Joseph linked to the Roman city of Gurulis Vetus (I century BC-III century AD) with anatomical terracotta and statues, and a Punic-Roman collection with a thymiaterion with head of goddess. From June to September open Friday, Saturday and Sunday 15:30-18:30; in the rest of the year by reservation.

  • Where Via Nazionale, Padria
  • Entry €1, free under 12 and over 65
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Piazzette with noble palaces in trachyte and limestone, volcanic stone buildings and architraves carved by local stonemasons with motifs of Catalan school. In the village also the churches of Santa Maria degli Angeli, Santa Croce and San Giuseppe.

  • Where Centro storico, Padria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Colle San Paolo e su Palattu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the three hills that protect the village: on the top there is a wall bastion 65 meters long of a Phoenician-Punic fortress, on whose ruins in the sixteenth century was built the baronial palace of the De Ferrera, called on Palattu.

  • Where Colle San Paolo, Padria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Nuraghe Longu e nuraghi di Padria

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The territory has about 40 nuraghi, almost one per square kilometer, mostly monotorre with cover to tholos and remains of huts of village around; the Longu, about two kilometers from the village, is a complex nuraghe.

  • Where A circa 2 km dal centro, Padria
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Canyon di Su' Entale

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Torrente that flows into a deep canyon before joining the Temo, forming waterfalls; it is a destination of trekking routes and marks the border with Bosa, reachable from the highway 292.

  • Where Località Su' Entale, Padria
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
