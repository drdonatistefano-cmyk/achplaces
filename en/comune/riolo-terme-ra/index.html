Terme di Riolo

Local life and traditions · Spas · Paid entry

Active plant since 1877, with salsobromoidic and sulphurous waters, in the thermal park.

  • Where Riolo Terme
  • Entry For a fee: rates on the spa website
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Monte Mauro

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

With 515 meters it is the highest peak of the Vena del Gesso, reachable by trails through white ridges.

  • Where Vena del Gesso, Riolo Terme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
