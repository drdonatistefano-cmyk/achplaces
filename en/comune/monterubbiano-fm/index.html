What to see in Monterubbiano

The hilly village was born in the 11th century as Castrum Montis Rubbiani, with its intact medieval structure, the Torrione del Cassero, the Collegiata with works by Vincenzo Pagani, the San Francesco Cultural Park and a panorama from Gran Sasso to the Adriatic.

9 places to visit in Monterubbiano, in the province of Fermo (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Polo culturale San Francesco

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

The complex opened on 23 September 2007 in a 13th century convent, with auditoriums in the former Franciscan church frescoed in the 15th century, archaeological museum, library, environmental education center and botanical garden. The museum tells the valley of Aso with a plastic of about 24 meters and preserves piceni finds, including a helmet with ram protomes and accessories of the necropolis of Contrada Crocifisso (IV century BC), as well as about 500 coins.

open by reservation (tel. 0734 257396)

  • Where Via Pagani 15, Monterubbiano
  • Entry €2.50, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · regione.marche.it · italia.it

Palazzo Comunale e Quadreria civica

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The Palazzo Comunale dates back to the fourteenth century and houses the civic art gallery, set up in the hall of the municipal council with works mostly anonymous from the sixteenth to the nineteenth century.

  • Where Monterubbiano
  • Entry €2.50 (one-time ticket, Polo and Quadreria)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · regione.marche.it

Collegiata di Santa Maria dei Letterati

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Collegiate Society houses works by Vincenzo Pagani, painter of the first half of the sixteenth century born in Monterubbiano, to whom the town theatre is also dedicated.

Teatro Pagani

History and architecture · Historic theatres · Paid entry

The 19th century theatre entitled to Vincenzo Pagani; the curtain, painted by the stage designer Alessandro Bazzani, portrays the painter at work on a terrace overlooking a coastal landscape with pine trees.

Torrione del Cassero e mura

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Of the fortifications of the castle, passed to the Sforza in 1433, remain the Torrione del Cassero and some stretches of walls, which once went beyond the town.

  • Entry Free

Centro storico e panorama

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village was born in the 11th century from the union of three fortified nuclei and preserves intact the medieval system; from the hill, at 463 meters, the view ranges from the Gran Sasso, Majella, the Vettore and Sibilla to the Adriatic.

  • Entry Free
