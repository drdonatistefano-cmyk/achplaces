What to see in Galbiate

A common place at the foot of Monte Barro, a panoramic terrace overlooking the Brianza and the lakes of Como, Annone and Garlate protected by a regional park; the Hermitage the archeological area of Gothic age and the Archaeological Museum of Barro, in the medieval village of Camporeso the Ethnographic Museum of Alta Brianza, in Galbiate the eighteenth-century Villa Bertarelli with its historical gardens.

Parco regionale del Monte Barro

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Less than 700 hectares with over a thousand plant species: beech, linden and maple in the valleys to the north, oaks and oaks on the southern slopes, prairies, karst springs and limestone walls; it is a stopover point for many species of migratory birds. Paths from very easy to challenging go up to the summit and medieval remains, with views of the Brianza and the lakes of Como, Annon and Garlate. At the Hermitage a 48 seat park hostel.

  • Where Monte Barro, Galbiate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Area archeologica gota dell'Eremo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the slopes of the Barro the excavations have brought to light a large residential and military settlement of Gothic age (V-VI century AD), indicated as the largest site of that era in Italy; the area is owned by the Park at the Hermitage, which in the twentieth century was sanatorium.

  • Where Eremo del Monte Barro, Galbiate
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Museo archeologico del Barro (MAB)

Museums · Archaeological

Antiquarium at the Hermitage Park centre, with workshops, which exhibits the main findings of the Gothic settlement (V-VI century) excavated on the mountain.

  • Where Eremo del Monte Barro, Galbiate
  • Entry To be checked

Museo etnografico dell'Alta Brianza (MEAB)

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 2003 in the closed courtyard, partly still inhabited, of the fourteenth-century agricultural village of Camporeso, among the terraces: it tells the life and work of the folk classes of the Brianza hills and of the Lecchese between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries. It is one of the few demo-ethno-anthropological museums recognized by the Lombardy Region and leader of the REBEL network.

  • Where Frazione Camporeso, Galbiate
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: parcobarro.lombardia.it · in-lombardia.it

Villa Bertarelli e giardini storici

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Attested in the Teresian cadastral of 1721, it hosted several times Carlo Porta between 1801 and 1815; the Ballabio created brolo and formal gardens with fountains, nymphs and stone mosaics, Giuseppe Bovara designed the gate in 1816 and Piero Portaluppi restored it in neo-baroque style in 1911-1913. Since 2003 it has been a municipality and park, which are located in the center of Flora Autoctona; the historical gardens are visited on Saturday morning not festive, with views of the Briantian lakes from the entrance.

  • Where Quartiere Rizzòlo, strada per Camporeso, Galbiate
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.in-lombardia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: parcobarro.lombardia.it · in-lombardia.it · eventbrite.it
