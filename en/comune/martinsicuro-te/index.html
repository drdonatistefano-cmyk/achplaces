What to see in Martinsicuro

A coastal town between the mouths of Tronto and Salinello, the first Abruzzo beach, which grew around the sixteenth-century Torre di Carlo V which today houses the Antiquarium of Castrum Truentinum.

Torre di Carlo V e Antiquarium di Castrum Truentinum

History and architecture · Towers

A sighting tower wanted by Carlo V in the middle of the sixteenth century against the Saracen raids, one of the Spanish coastal towers between Trigno and Tronto; around it grew the first inhabited. It houses the Antiquarium of Castrum Truentinum, an archaeological museum with exhibits from prehistory to the Lombard age excavated in the area of Colle Marzio.

Chiesa del Sacro Cuore di Gesù e Villa Bernabei

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church of the twentieth century has a Latin cross with an elegant portico; in front of it stands Villa Bernabei, a half-nineteenth-century residence.

Lungomare e spiagge

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The first beach of Abruzzo is part, with Alba Adriatica and Tortoreto Lido, 13 kilometers of uninterrupted beaches with Blue Flag; in summer in the evening you can enjoy establishments, beach bars and lounge bars with music.
