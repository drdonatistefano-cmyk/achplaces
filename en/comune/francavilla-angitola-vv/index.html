What to see in Francavilla Angitola

Borgo del Vibonese in the Regional Natural Park of the Serre, on the edge of the WWF Oasis of Lake Angitola: in the district Pendino the five-cruci Calvary and the museum house Il Romitorio, at Palazzo Mannacio the Museum of identity, in the surroundings the ruins of Rocca Angitola and the convent of Santa Maria della Croce.

7 places to visit in Francavilla Angitola, in the province of Vibo Valentia (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo dell'identità

Museums · City history

Inaugurated in 2010 on the noble floor of Palazzo Mannacio, it has three sections: historical documents, especially of the municipal administrative activity; sacred art linked to local devotion, including a viatic piss in copper and silver from 1598; rural and social life with agricultural and craft tools, instruments of the ancient town band, domestic objects and trunks of emigrants. The route is accompanied by vintage photographs of the twentieth century and images of the territory, such as the medieval ruins of Pendino. Open Monday and Friday 9-13, Wednesday and Thursday 15.30-18.30.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Il Romitorio

Museums · House museums · Free entry

House museum wanted by Father Tarcisio Rondinelli, Franciscan friar of the Custody of the Holy Land, who since 2010 lives in the restored building of the Pendino district. Upstairs, the friar's study and a room dedicated to Francavilla, St. Francis and the Franciscan Order; on the ground floor, exhibitions on non-Catholic Christian confessions and non-Christian religions and a "Pantheon" in the former stable, with pyrographies by Father Rondinelli, graphics by Lina Ruperto and models by Lello Torchia. Open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday 9-12 and 16-19.

  • Where Via le Torri 36, Francavilla Angitola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.francavillaangitola.vv.it · italia.it

Calvario greco-bizantino

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry

In the ancient village of Pendina, Calvary 'pentacrociato' with five crosses that recall the five plagues of Jesus, linked to the Franciscan tradition; the bronze portal of the master Giuseppe Farina di Monterosso resumes the five crosses as a symbol of the local identity. Pendina had four gates (Royal, Baffo, Portella and Mannacio) and a castle with drawbridge then became the church of San Foca. Guided tour available.

  • Where Rione Pendino, Francavilla Angitola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.francavillaangitola.vv.it · italia.it

Chiesa matrice di San Foca

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church dedicated to the patron Saint Foca, a soldier born in Antioch and converted to Christianity, celebrated on 5 March and the second Sunday of August; according to the Municipality stands on the site of the castle of Pendina.

  • Where Piazza Guglielmo Marconi 16, Francavilla Angitola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it · comune.francavillaangitola.vv.it

Oasi WWF Lago Angitola

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Natural reserve on the edge of the country, oasis of Mediterranean biodiversity and stop of migratory birds around one of the most interesting artificial lakes in Calabria; the country is part of the Regional Natural Park of the Serre.

  • Where Lago Angitola, Francavilla Angitola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Ruderi del convento di Santa Maria della Croce

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Ruins of one of the most important Augustinian convents of the Zumpani Congregation in the 17th century, which once kept works of art; they are reached during the naturalistic trekking around the lake.

  • Where Lago Angitola, Francavilla Angitola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Ruderi di Rocca Angitola

History and architecture · Abandoned villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Only the ruins remain of the Casale di Rocca Niceforo; the name recalls the Byzantine general Niceforo Foca il Vecchio, who led the campaign against the Saracens in 885-886.

  • Where Rocca Angitola, Francavilla Angitola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
