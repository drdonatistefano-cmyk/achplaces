What to see in Frosolone

Borgo dei Borghi più belle d'Italia e Bandiera Arancione about 900 meters on the Molisano Apennines, known as the "Italian Toledo" for the tradition of knives and scissors handed down from father to son; it has a medieval old town with three arched doors, the Museum of sharp irons, the Shepherd's House and, around, the beech woods of Monte Marchetta and Colle dell'Orso and the rock walls of Morgia Quadra.

Museo dei ferri taglienti

Museums · Themed

In the historic center, towards the end of via Garibaldi, it collects hundreds of objects of historical value donated by enthusiasts and heirs of scissors and knives of the twentieth century, together with military blades delivered by various Superintendences, including those of Marche and Lazio. A part of the museum is an active workshop where you can see master craftsmen forging the blades; visits are on reservation, at least 24 hours in advance, through the association Molise to Discover.

  • Where Via Garibaldi, Frosolone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Casetta del pastore

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Along the Garibaldi course, it faithfully reproduces a cuisine of the ancient peasant civilization, linked to the tradition of transhumance still alive in the village. You can watch the processing of local dairy products, scamorza, caciocavallo, ricotta, braid and manteca, and then taste them; you can visit the museum together, by reservation.

  • Where Corso Garibaldi, Frosolone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.frosolone.is.it · visitmolise.eu · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Museo del costume e del corredo antico

Museums · Themed

In Piazza Municipio, in front of the parish, exhibits wedding items, popular costumes and amulets against the evil eye. The ancient costumes come from Frosolone and its villages and many recall the colors of religious and popular festivals, such as that of Sant'Antonio.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Frosolone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · comune.frosolone.is.it · italia.it

Centro storico e Palazzo baronale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village preserves the medieval plant with three ancient arched doors (Porta S. Maria, Porta S. Pietro e Porta S. Angelo) and ancient road crosses, among stone alleys, arches, fountains and noble palaces. The Baronial Palace is located where the Longobards had built a castle: in the twelfth century it was by Raynaldo di Pietrabbondante and since 1771 it belongs to the Zampini family. The complex of Santa Chiara, founded in 1367 and also used as a prison, since May 1995 hosts the Town Hall; in the village there is also the monument to the knifemaker, a craftsman standing at the anvil.

  • Where Centro storico, Frosolone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitmolise.eu · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale dell'Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1777, it has a stone façade with a double ramp staircase and a statue of the Assumption. Inside he preserves paintings by Giacinto Diana from the late eighteenth century: a Madonna in glory, a Madonna del Carmelo and a San Vincenzo Ferreri.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Frosolone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiese di San Rocco, San Pietro, Sant'Angelo e San Nicola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

San Rocco holds two paintings by Francesco Fracanzano with St. Peter and St. Anthony Abbot; St. Peter's a Holy Family in terracotta of Amalia Dupré and an eighteenth-century wooden statue of the Immaculate of Paolo Di Zinno. Sant'Angelo preserves canvases of the Six and eighteenth centuries and San Nicola a remarkable wooden choir.

  • Where Centro storico, Frosolone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Just outside the village, next to the Capuchin monastery of the sixteenth century then suppressed. The main altar is a retablo inlaid, carved and painted, with nine tables of Neapolitan Mannerist school of the seventeenth century.

  • Entry To be checked

Faggete di Monte Marchetta e Colle dell'Orso

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the plateau of the Colle dell'Orso, the beech forests of Monte Marchetta and Colle dell'Orso grow over 1,000 meters in a difficult mountain climate; here you will find the beech tree "Pedalone" over 40 meters high and over 100 years old. Nearby there are the Grisciata Forest, with undergrowth of cyclamen, snow-blowers, porcini and finferli, and the Contessa Valley, with gooseberry meadows and rose-canine hedges; the territory offers hiking trails, mountain biking trails, an equestrian centre and a free flight school.

  • Where Colle dell'Orso, Frosolone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu

Morgia Quadra

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Complex of rocky walls of different difficulty for free climbing, indicated by italia.it as a destination for those who love untouched nature, with wild rocks and ancient stalactites. Hiking on horseback, hiking and hang gliding are practiced around.

  • Where Colle dell'Orso, Frosolone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Mura ciclopiche di Civitella e Castellone

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Remains of ancient stone walls in the towns Civitella (or Civitello) and Castellone, considered testimony of the Osca and Sannite origins of the village, perhaps the ancient Fresilia. The first official news of Frosolone dates back to the 11th century, when it was part of the County of Isernia.

  • Where Località Civitella, Frosolone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it
