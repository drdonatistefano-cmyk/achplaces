What to see in San Benedetto Po

Borgo sul Po grew up around the abbey of Polirone, founded in 1007 by Tedaldo di Canossa, grandfather of Countess Matilde: the basilica rebuilt by Giulio Romano, the cloisters, the refectory and the Civic Museum Polironiano make it one of the major Benedictine destinations of Northern Italy.

Basilica polironiana di San Benedetto Abate

Places of worship · Basilicas

Abbey church rebuilt by Giulio Romano between 1540 and 1545 without demolishing the Romanesque and Gothic structures, incorporating the oratory of Santa Maria dell'XI-XII century. It houses 32 statues of terracotta saints by Antonio Begarelli, the carved wardrobes of the sacristy of Giovanni Maria Piantavigna and the alabaster sarcophagus of Matilde di Canossa on four red marble lions (the remains were brought to St. Peter in Rome in 1633).

  • Where Piazza Teofilo Folengo 19, San Benedetto Po
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Complesso monastico di Polirone: chiostri, refettorio e cantine

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Monastery founded in 1007 on an island between Po and Lirone, donated by Matilde to Pope Gregory VII in 1077 and then linked to Cluny; Luther, Vasari, Tasso and Palladio passed there. The route touches the cloister of the Seculars, the Barberinian staircase, the late Dogotic cloister of San Simeone with the Chapter Hall, the Grand Refectory, where Abbot Gregorio Cortese commissioned a fresco to Correggio and where the Cenacolo di Girolamo Bonsignori (1514) and the sixteenth century cellars returned.

  • Where Piazza Matilde di Canossa 7, San Benedetto Po
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · museovirtuale.borghipiubelliditalia.it · comune.san-benedetto-po.mn.it · italia.it

Museo civico Polironiano

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Founded in 1977 in the former dormitories, library and scriptorium of the monastery, it is one of the major ethnographic museums in Italy: about 12,000 objects of peasant life, rural crafts and devotion, graphic and painting works by local artists of the twentieth century, a collection of puppets and ancient puppets, an archaeological section and a collection of Padan farm wagons.

  • Where Piazza Teofilo Folengo 22, San Benedetto Po
  • Entry €5, reduced €4; with Basilica €8, reduced €7
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Giardino botanico polironiano

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

The garden of the monastic complex with medicinal herbs, at the center of the guided path "The herbs and monks" on the way the monks treated the sick.

  • Where San Benedetto Po
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Argini e percorsi del Po

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Po flows slowly next to the village, among gorges, banks and rows of poplars; from the historic center start trails along the river for walkers and cyclists.

  • Where San Benedetto Po
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
