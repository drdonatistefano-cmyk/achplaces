What to see in Tiriolo

The city between the two seas, in a panoramic position on a buttress of the Sila Piccola from which you can see Tirreno and Ionio, has origins between the end of the IV and the beginning of the III century BC; the Museum Polo Tiriolo Antica brings together archaeological museum and section of the costume, while in the historical center survives the craftsmanship of the vancali, the shawls of the "Pacchiana" .

Polo museale Tiriolo Antica (Antiquarium e Museo del costume)

Museums · Archaeological

It brings together the archaeological museum, with finds of ancient, medieval and modern age from the city and the territory, and the section of the traditional costume, dedicated to the "pacchiana" , the costume richly embroidered that was part of the dowry of the bride. The archaeological park of Gianmartino and the Ecomuseo Fortezza-Monte are also part of the polo; guided tours, also in foreign language, and excursions are offered. Access with ramps.

Centro storico e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historic centre preserves the palaces De Filippis, Donati and Schettini and the artisan workshops; in Piazza Italia a sculpture of Maurizio Carnivali from the eighties recalls the hypothesis that the isthmus of Catanzaro was the Umerica Scheria dei Feaci. From the village you can see the Tyrrhenian and Ionian.

  • Where Piazza Italia, Tiriolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · calabriastraordinaria.it

Artigianato di vancali e pezzane

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Tiriolo is known for its embroidery and vancali, wool and silk shawls once part of the ...pacchiana and today objects of artistic handicrafts; also the pieces are produced, colored rugs woven to the frame, lace to the tombolo and traditional musical instruments in wood.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
