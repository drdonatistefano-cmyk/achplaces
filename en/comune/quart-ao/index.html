What to see in Quart

Town of villages scattered on the hill east of Aosta, with the castle of the lords of Quart, the Neolithic necropolis of Vollein and the walks along the rus.

5 places to visit in Quart, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Quart

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The oldest part was built in 1185 by Jacques de la Porte de Saint-Ours, founder of the lords of Quart; together of buildings within a belt that follows the rocky spur, with the donjon on the highest point.

  • Where Villair, Quart
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page lovevda.it

Necropoli neolitica di Vollein

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill of Quart, it preserves remains of the first human settlements of the Aosta Valley.
