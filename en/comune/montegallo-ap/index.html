What to see in Montegallo

Common at the foot of Mount Vettore in the National Park of Monti Sibillini, with capital in Balzo, where in the sixteenth century the inhabitants of the ancient castle of Santa Maria in Gallo moved; among the places to see the church of San Bernardino, Santa Maria in Pantano with the Sibille frescoed by Martino Bonfini and Santa Maria in Lapide with the crypt of the IX century.

Balzo: piazza Taliani, Rotonda e Palazzetto Branconi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the capital of Balzo there are the round, circular palace of 1883, the Town Hall with the monument to the fallen of the Great War in bronze and marble and the adjacent Palazzo Taliani of 1834, and the Palazzetto Branconi of 1640, with the inscription "Invidia God" and not compassion on the architrave. The Palazzetto houses the Park's Environmental Education Centre, with museum, tour centre, nature trail and library.

Chiesa di San Bernardino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

17th century church with Gothic portal; inside monolithic columns in local sandstone, marble altar, a large painting with San Bernardino da Siena and a plaque dedicated to Cardinal Taliani. From the church a path leads to a panoramic point and descends to Pineta Taliani.

  • Where Piazza Bonelli, Balzo, Montegallo
  • Entry Free

Santa Maria in Pantano (Santa Maria delle Sibille)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church whose foundation dates back to the eighth century, with origins still wrapped in legend; it preserves frescoes from the second decade of the seventeenth century by Martino Bonfini da Patrignone depicting five Sibyls: Cumana, Ellespontica, Agrippina, Frigia and Delfica.

Santa Maria in Lapide

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Church probably of 1491, dependent on the monks of Farfa, along the Rio, the main tributary of the Fluvion; it belonged to a monastic settlement of which only partially recognizes the complex. There remains a 9th century crypt and a 15th century bronze bell with Gothic inscriptions.
