What to see in Iglesias

The mining town of Sulcis-Iglesiente in the Geominerario Park of Sardinia: the historic center with the cathedral of Santa Chiara and San Francesco, the mines of Monteponi and Porto Flavia and the coast of Nebida and Masua with the Sugar Pan.

11 places to visit in Iglesias, in the province of Sud Sardegna (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Iglesias in the guide with map and filters

Porto Flavia

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Mining port excavated in the rocky wall above the sea, with a tunnel of about 600 meters: an innovative work that allowed to load the minerals directly on the ships.

  • Where Masua, Iglesias
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Grotta di Santa Barbara

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Discovered by chance in 1952 by digging a well in the mine of San Giovanni, it was formed in rocks of the lower Cambrian about 500 million years ago: walls covered with crystals of dark brown barite, a room over 25 meters high with stalactites, stalagmites and a small lake.

  • Where San Giovanni Miniera, Iglesias
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: italia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Sugarbread is a 132-metre-high scarf shaped by erosion, a natural monument; near, the Grand Canal of Nebida is a narrow valley between walls of about 100 meters that descend on turquoise waters.
