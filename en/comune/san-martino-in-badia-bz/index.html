What to see in San Martino in Badia

The Ladin municipality of the Val Badia between the natural parks Puez-Odle and Fanes-Senes-Braies: in San Martin the thirteenth-century castle of Ciastel de Tor houses the Ladin Museum on the history and culture of the Ladin valleys, and in Lungiarü, the mountaineer village, the valley of the mills (Val di Morins) opens the ascent to the Sass de Putia.

Museum Ladin Ciastel de Tor

Museums · Themed

Museum opened in 2001 in a medieval castle of the 13th century, the main site of the Ladin provincial museum: it tells history, language, culture, archaeology, geology, legends, crafts and tourism development of the Ladin valleys of the Dolomites; halls and courtyard host exhibitions, concerts and seminars. Open from 1 May to 1 November, from Tuesday to Sunday at 10-18; only partially wheelchair accessible.

  • Where Str. Tor 65, San Martino in Badia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website museumladin.it

Sources: museumladin.it · suedtirol.info

Val di Morins, la valle dei mulini

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valle del Rio Seres a Lungiarü, called "Mulini": between the hamlets of Seres and Miscì there are eight water mills, single and double (two with two wheels), including a rope mill called "Tambra" . The route begins at a bridge over the Speckstube Tlisöra.

  • Where Seres, Lungiarü, San Martino in Badia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: sanvigilio.com · suedtirol.info

Escursione da Lungiarü al Sass de Putia

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Itinerary of 10.1 km (about 4 hours and 45, 1,403 meters of climb) from Lungiarü to the valley of the mills to the refuge Genoa (about 3 hours and 30), with alpine meadows and views of the Odle and the Zillertal Alps, and for the path 4B from the Fork of Putia to the top of the Sass de Putia at 2.818 meters, with summit cross and panorama of the Dolomites. The last stretch is equipped: for experienced hikers and without vertigo, the set of ferrata is recommended.

  • Where Lungiarü, San Martino in Badia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info
