What to see in Lesa

Borgo sulla spiaggia piedmontese del Lago Maggiore, legato a Alessandro Manzoni, che soggiornò volte a Palazzo Stampa Between 1839 and 1861: center historical with arcades and lakefront, the church of San Martino, the Sala Manzoniana, the fishing village of Solcio with the Romanesque oratory of San Sebastiano and the panoramic hamlets of Comnago and Calogna.

Sala Manzoniana

Museums · House museums · Free entry

Opened in 1979 in the former farmer's house behind Villa Stampa and reopened on May 25, 2024 after the restoration, he exhibits relics and documents lent by Casa Manzoni and by the Rosminian Fathers of Stresa on Manzoni, Teresa Borri, Stefano Stampa and Rosmini, the legacy of Giulio Carcano (poet, translator of Shakespeare and among the first biographers of Manzoni) with the reconstruction of his study and bulletin boards on the fortune of the Promises married. Open from May to September on Friday 16.30-18.30, Saturday and Sunday 10-12 and 16.30-18.30 (closed on 15 August); in other periods on request at 345 1577649.

  • Where Via alla Fontana 18, Lesa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: comune.lesa.no.it · distrettolaghi.it

Palazzo Stampa

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Neoclassical villa of the late 18th century on the lakefront, residence of the accounts Print: originally in U, lost its two wings in 1806 to make room for the Napoleonic road of the Sempione. Manzoni, married in second marriage to Teresa Borri, widow Print, attended her since 1839 and remained there for a long time between 1848 and 1850, writing the discourse "Del novel storico" and the dialogue "Dell'inventione" . Today it houses a bank, apartments and offices: you can see it from the outside.

  • Where Lungolago nord, Lesa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: comune.lesa.no.it · distrettolaghi.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Overlooking the lakefront, it is documented as early as 1224, the year of a pact between the archbishop of Milan and the local nobles. It preserves the Romanesque bell tower and, inside three aisles, a Romanesque stone head walled in a bench, an architectural fragment over a holy water table and furnishings that testify to the richness of the families of the village.

  • Where Via San Martino 7, Lesa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: comune.lesa.no.it · distrettolaghi.it

Centro storico e lungolago

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The center of Lesa combines palaces, arcades and alleys to the lakefront; from Via Gibertini starts the walk towards Belgirate, which follows a stretch of an ancient Roman road.

  • Entry Free

Solcio e Oratorio di San Sebastiano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village of fishermen gathered around the port, on the shore of the lake; there stands the Oratory of San Sebastiano, Romanesque jewel with a hut facade, apse to hanging arches and bell tower with three orders of windows.

  • Where Frazione Solcio, Lesa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.distrettolaghi.it

Sources: distrettolaghi.it
