What to see in Meana Sardo

The village of Barbagia di Belvì at the foot of Mount Sant'Elia, known above all for Nuraghe Nolza, the largest of the Barbagia di Belvì, and for the sixteenth-century parish church of San Bartolomeo.

Nuraghe Nolza

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On Cuccuru Nolza (740 m), the highest point of the plateau of Pranu, about 7 km from the village: quadrilobed nuraghe with high mast today about 13 meters and four corner tower bastion, surrounded by a village of huts of about 2,5 hectares. The village dates back to the 16th-XV century BC, mastic and first towers in shale to the 14th-XIII, the other two towers in porphyry to the 12th century BC; studied by Giovanni Lilliu, excavated between 1994 and 2000, returned to the top a small courtyard with seat and fireplace. A staircase leads to the top of the tower; the stop of the Green Train Mandas-Sorgono is about 300 meters.

Parrocchiale di San Bartolomeo apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the center of the village, reached by a staircase, it was built at the end of the sixteenth century on a more ancient building mentioned in a document of 1341. The interior with three naves combines Gothic-Aragonese and Renaissance elements; portal with carved columns and reliefs of popular taste on the bell tower. The patron saint is celebrated in late August.
