What to see in Porto Cesareo

Località dello Ionio 28 km from Lecce, known for its golden beaches, Porto Cesareo lives around the marina with the fish market; its sea is one of the largest protected marine areas in Italy, flanked by the reserve Swamp of the Count, and the coast is marked by the sixteenth-century towers of Cesarea, Chianca and Lapillo, as well as the Museum of Marine Biology Pietro Parenzan.

Museo di biologia marina «Pietro Parenzan»

Museums · Science and university

Born with the Marine Biology Station of Porto Cesareo, established in 1966 with museum, library and a small laboratory, today it is located in Villa Lucisani. Over 900 finds on the fauna and flora of the sea: reconstructions of reefs and a breeding of mussels, marine molluscs, deep fishes of the Ionian Sea, tropical sea fauna and unusual or malformed specimens.

  • Where Via Vespucci 13, Porto Cesareo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Area Marina Protetta di Porto Cesareo

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Protected since 1997, it is the third protected marine area of Italy by extension: 16,654 hectares of sea along 32 km of Ionian coast between Porto Cesareo and Nardò, from Punta Prosciutto to Torre Inserraglio, divided into three zones (A integral reserve in front of the Strea peninsula and between Torre Sant'Isidoro and Casa Giorgella, B and C with activities allowed at different levels). The tour centre offers guided tours, bike rental, small boats and educational activities.

  • Where Via Cosimo Albano, Porto Cesareo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Isola dei Conigli e necropoli sommersa

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The island of the Rabbit is located in front of Torre Chianca, inside the protected marine area; between the tower and the island, in very low waters, a submerged necropolis is visible to tourists and bathers.

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Torre Cesarea

History and architecture · Towers

Begin in 1568 as the headquarters of the Superguard that led the Comarch of Caesarea, from which the name of "Torre Capitana" was demolished and completely rebuilt in 1622. It is the largest sighting tower in size: square plan of 22 meters on the side, 16 meters high, thick walls in tuff, two floors, caditoie and crowning on catchatelli; it communicated with Torre Squillace to the south and Torre Chianca to the north.

today headquarters of the Guardia di Finanza: access to the public to verify

Torre Chianca (Torre Santo Stefano)

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

A 16th century watchtower built by the Spanish against the Saracen pirates, about 1 km north of the village at the end of a sandy bay: square plan without external staircase, shoe base of about 15 meters, about 18 meters high; it communicated with Torre Casarea and Torre Lapillo and in the Second World War hosted an artillery station. Today it is part of the project "Le Torri Fortificati Vedette della Legalità" for monitoring the marine protected area.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Torre Lapillo (Torre di San Tommaso)

History and architecture · Towers

A watchtower among the largest in the territory, erected against Saracen raids: square base and large access staircase on three arches, the last recently added because originally the staircase ended with a drawbridge.
