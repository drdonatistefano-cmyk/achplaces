What to see in Monza

The city of Teodolinda and Corona Ferrea, with the Duomo and its Treasure, the Medieval Arengario, the Royal Villa of Piermarini and one of the largest enclosed parks in Europe, which houses the National Racecourse.

23 places to visit in Monza, in the province of Monza e della Brianza (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di Monza

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Founded by Teodolinda and rebuilt from the fourteenth century under the Visconti, with façade of Matteo da Campione brought back to the bichromy of the late nineteenth century, altar of Andrea Appiani and the Tree of life of Arcimboldi and Meda (1556).

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Monza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismo.monza.it

Sources: turismo.monza.it

Cappella di Teodolinda e Corona Ferrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Frescoed in the fifteenth century by the Zavattari with 45 scenes of the legend of Theodolinda, it preserves the Crown Ferrea, which according to tradition contains a nail of the Cross and with which were crowned Charles V and Napoleon.

Villa Reale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Wanted by Maria Teresa of Austria and built between 1777 and 1780 by Giuseppe Piermarini as a summer residence for his son Ferdinando: plant in U, path of 28 rooms, the apartment of the Prince of Naples, the Royal Chapel, the Round of Appiani and the Teatrino of Corte.

  • Where Viale Brianza 1, Monza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismo.monza.it

Sources: turismo.monza.it · italia.it

Parco di Monza

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established by Napoleon in 1805 as a model estate and hunting reserve, designed by Luigi Canonica: one of the largest enclosed parks in Europe, with Villa Mirabello and Mirabellino, historic farmsteads, dry mills and the Chain Bridge.

  • Where Viale Cesare Battisti, Monza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismo.monza.it

Sources: turismo.monza.it · italia.it
