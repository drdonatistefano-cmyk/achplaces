Rocca Possente di Stellata

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortress with a starry plan on the right bank of the Po, rebuilt at the end of the seventeenth century against Venice; it can only be seen from the outside.

  • Where Stellata, Bondeno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Casa Ariosto e Museo archeologico G. Ferraresi

Museums · Archaeological

In the house that belonged to the son of Ludovico Ariosto, the finds from the Neolithic to the Roman age of the territory of Bondeno, the oldest settlement of Ferrara.

  • Where Stellata, Bondeno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
