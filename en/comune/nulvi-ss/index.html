What to see in Nulvi

Anglona country known for the procession of Ferragosto of the "Three Giants," 8 meters high candlesticks: the fourteenth-century church of the Assumption with the diocesan museum, the churches of Santa Croce, San Giovanni and the convent of Santa Tecla and, in the territory, the Nuragic well temple of Irru.

7 places to visit in Nulvi, in the province of Sassari (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa dell'Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the fourteenth century, parish since 1605, known for its antiquity and ornaments. It is the church of the Feast of the Assumption: in August the procession of the "Three Giants" , candlelights of 9 tons high 8 meters, dissolves a vow made during a plague of the thirteenth century.

  • Where Nulvi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Museo diocesano e Nostra Signora del Rosario

Museums · Old masters

The diocesan museum, in the sacristy of the church of Our Lady of the Rosary (1630, with the altar of sos paladinos), preserves sacred furnishings, Mannerist paintings, silverware, gold jewels, statues of the XVII-XVIII century, ex vow and sacred vestments.

  • Where Nulvi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Complesso nuragico di Irru

History and architecture · Archaeology

Nuraghe and well temple in limestone of the X-VII century B.C.: the temple, 16 meters long and 7 meters wide, has tholos, vestibule and two atriums, with the chamber of the well 4.5 meters deep.

  • Where Località Nuraghe Irru, Nulvi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · sardegnacultura.it
