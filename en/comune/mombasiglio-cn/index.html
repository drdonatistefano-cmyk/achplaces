What to see in Mombasiglio

Small village perched on the hillside in the Mongia torrent valley, in the Mongia Valleys and Cevetta Langa Cebana: the castle, one of the best preserved of the ancient marquisate of Ceva, houses the Bonaparte Museum on the countryside of Italy of 1796; around the archaeological area of the Colle di Sant'Andrea and the natural bridge of the Masentine.

3 places to visit in Mombasiglio, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Mombasiglio e Museo Bonaparte

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

Built around the year 1000 in a panoramic position above the village, it is one of the best preserved buildings of the ancient Marquisate of Ceva, with a high tower that dominates the shelter; in 1796 there were aquartiered the Napoleonic armies. On the first floor the Bonaparte Museum preserves the largest collection of original prints and etchings by Giuseppe Pietro Bagetti on the first Italian countryside of Napoleon; in the tower a museum of marbles and local stones, with a wide view of mountains and valleys. From 21 March to October 2026 open on Saturday 14-18, Sunday and holidays 10-18; schools by reservation with CoopCulture.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Veneto 19, Mombasiglio

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Area archeologica del Colle di Sant'Andrea

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill remain the foundations of a church of the year 1000, valued as an archaeological area. At the beginning of the 20th century there were three Roman tombstones and a rare Etruscan tombstone, a sign of a very ancient visitation of the place.

  • Where Colle di Sant'Andrea, Mombasiglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Ponte naturale delle Masentine

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

In Masentine the erosion of the Mongia stream has dug over millennia a natural bridge, used for centuries as a passage between the two banks of the valley.

  • Where Località Masentine, Mombasiglio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
