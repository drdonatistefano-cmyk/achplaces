What to see in Force

Borgo dei ora nell'Ascolano, where for over half a millennium the caldera have worked copper: in the Palazzo Canestrari are located the Museum of sacred art and, in the underground, the Ramai Museum; in the village the Collegiata di San Paolo, the sixteenth-century loggia of the Palazzo del Priorato and, at Christmas, the mechanized crib in the caves of San Francesco.

Museo di arte sacra di Force (Palazzo Canestrari)

Museums · Old masters

The historic Palazzo Canestrari houses the museum, which preserves artistic artifacts from the churches of the territory, in particular works by De Magistris. It is part of the Sistine Museums of Piceno.

Museo dei Ramai

Museums · Themed

Opened at the end of 2005 in the underground rooms of the ancient Palazzo Canestrari, it collects household objects of the copper forcese handicrafts, including the stills called "tamburla" (tamburla) used to distill the mixture from wine. The educators and artisans of the Terravita association organize workshops for children and young people (SpazioLab).

Chiesa di San Francesco e presepe nelle grotte

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The nineteenth-century church was restored by the architect Sacconi; in the caves below there is a mechanical nativity scene of about 250 square meters, designed in 1992 by Fernando and Ilario Testa and Massimo Benedetti.

Collegiata di San Paolo Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of ancient origin founded by the Farfensian monks and much remodelled over the centuries; it preserves a crucifix considered miraculous, celebrated in August with the feast of the Crucifix.

Centro storico, Palazzo del Priorato e torre di guardia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the upper medieval village remain the watchtower, the only remnant of the ancient fortifications, and the tower-gate of San Francesco, one of the three gates of the village; between the buildings the Palazzo del Priorato with the loggia of the sixteenth century and the Town Hall. There are also the convent and the birthplace of Blessed Maria Assunta Pallotta. In August, Antichi Flavors through the streets of Borgo will reopen the shops of the ora with live processing.

Villino orientaleggiante di Ernesto Verrucci

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Outside the walls, built in 1935 on a project by the architect Ernesto Verrucci, emigrated to Egypt at the age of 23, named Bey and chief architect of the royal palaces of King Fuad I, who contributed to the architectural rebirth of Cairo and Alexandria.
