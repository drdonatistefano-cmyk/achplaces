What to see in Roseto Valfortore

Borgo dei Monti Northern Dauni at 654 meters, near Mount Cornacchia, with the Mother Church of 1507, the Arch of the Earth, the alleys of the stonemasons, the still active straw oven and, at the slopes, two nineteenth century water mills restored with the Museum of Ancient Art.

Arco della Terra, Palazzo Marchesale e Piazza Vecchia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Arch of the Earth, the ancient main door of the village in front of the staircase of the Mother Church, has on the outer wall a stone head that could portray a feudalist; next to the church the Palazzo Marchesale, also wanted by Bartholomew III. From Piazza Vecchia start the "stréttole" alleys built with Lombard technique, with portals, columns and bas-reliefs of the mastri chiselellini.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I e Piazza Bartolomeo III di Capua, Roseto Valfortore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it · touringclub.it

Casa di «Concetta non me la fido», bottega del fabbro e forno a paglia

Local life and traditions · Crafts

In the historic center you can visit the house of "Concetta" not me the trust, the house of craftsmanship, the shop of the blacksmith-maniscalco and the ancient straw oven, still working and used in events and recurrences for bread with yeast mother and flour ground at the old mill.

  • Where Centro storico, Roseto Valfortore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Oratorio di San Filippo Neri e chiesa del SS. Corpo di Cristo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In 1623 the archpriest De Santis brought to Roseto the cult of Saint Philip Neri: his house became an oratory that still holds a silver bust of the saint. In the center there is also the church of SS. Body of Christ, an important place of worship between the Seven and the Nineteenth Century, then restored and named after Christ the King, today desecrated; in the village the churches of St. Nicholas and St. Mary Lauretana.

  • Where Centro storico, Roseto Valfortore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Mulini ad acqua e Museo di Arte Antica

Museums · Themed · Viewpoint

At the foot of the village, two 19th century watermills, rare examples of hydraulic factories in the province, restored: the upstream mill houses an ancient art museum with didactic function, the downstream one is a recreational space. From the storage tanks, the pool is obtained .Mulini, with panoramic view.

  • Where Località Mulini, Roseto Valfortore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Bosco Vetruscelli e sorgenti del Fortore

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Near the village, the wood Vetruscelli is known for its blooms and black truffle; the river Fortore is born on the east side of the village and flows into the valley. The Frassati Trail, about 50 km between five villages of the Dauni Mountains, you can walk, by mountain bike or on horseback.

  • Where Dintorni di Roseto Valfortore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
