What to see in Desenzano del Garda

On the banks of the Brescian Garda, with the medieval castle on the remains of a Roman castrum, the Renaissance Cathedral with the Last Supper of Tiepolo, the Old Port dominated by Palazzo Todeschini, one of the most important late antique villas in northern Italy and the Rambotti Museum with the oldest plow in the world; in the hamlet of San Martino della Battaglia the tower and the ossuary of 1859.

Villa Romana e Antiquarium

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Villa built in several phases between the 1st century BC and the 4th AD, one of the most important late ancient villas in northern Italy, famous for the polychrome mosaic floors with pagan scenes; the client of the last phase could be Flavio Magno Decenzio, brother of the emperor Magnenzio, perhaps at the origin of the name of the city. The Antiquarium exhibits a second century marble Hercules, a late 4th century glass cup with Christ and the rooster and a teenage bust. Tuesday-Saturday 9-19.30, Sunday 14.10-19.30, Monday closed; cumulative ticket with Caves of Catullo and Castle of Sirmione €14 and annual pass Garda Heritage €21.

  • Where Via Crocefisso 22, Desenzano del Garda
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museilombardia.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo Civico Archeologico Giovanni Rambotti

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the former Carmelite convent of Santa Maria de Senioribus, opened in 1990 and re-arranged in 2011, documents the palafittic villages of the Bronze Age of Garda and the Moorish amphitheatre: homes, agriculture, ceramics, metals, ornaments. The most famous piece is the Lavagnone plough, found in 1978, oak with beech yoke, of about 2000 BC, with a dedicated room. Free on the first Sunday of the month and with the Museums Lombardy Subscription.

  • Where Via Anelli 42, Desenzano del Garda
  • Entry €7, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.desenzano.brescia.it · visitbrescia.it · italia.it

Torre, Museo e Ossario di San Martino della Battaglia

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Monument to the battle of 24 June 1859: the 64-metre-high tower, with statues of the protagonists of the Risorgimento, frescoed ramp by Vittorio Emanuele Bressanin and panoramic terrace on the lower Garda, the morainic hills and the plain; behind, the museum with weapons, relics, documents and objects of the soldiers; along the avenue the Ossario Church with 1,274 skulls in the apse and the remains of 2,619 fallen in the crypt. Open all year round.

  • Where Via Torre 2, San Martino della Battaglia, Desenzano del Garda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.solferinoesanmartino.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: solferinoesanmartino.it · comune.desenzano.brescia.it · visitbrescia.it · italia.it

Castello di Desenzano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built in the early Middle Ages on the remains of a Roman castrum to defend the city from invasions: it preserves the bastion, the woundholes and a panoramic observatory on the lake, and today houses exhibitions, conferences and musical events.

  • Where Desenzano del Garda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it

Duomo di Santa Maria Maddalena

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Example of the late Renaissance in Brescia and among the most important Renaissance architectures in the province, with Venetian paintings of the seventeenth century and the version of the Last Supper of Tiepolo.

  • Where Desenzano del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it

Porto Vecchio e Palazzo Todeschini

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the most picturesque corners of the city, born in the thirteenth century as a stop of the trade of wheat and cereals; on the quay Palazzo Todeschini, with fifteen arches, seat of the town hall until the seventies of the twentieth century and today seat of conferences. Nearby, Malavezzi square in the heart of downtown.

  • Where Porto Vecchio, Desenzano del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it
