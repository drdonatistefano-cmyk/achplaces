What to see in Mirabella Eclano

The Irpino village of the ancient Aeclanum on the Via Appia and the Straw Truck: the archaeological park of the Sannite and Roman city, the complex of San Francesco of 1222 and the museum of the Misteri di Cartapesta by Antonio Russo.

3 places to visit in Mirabella Eclano, in the province of Avellino (Campania). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parco archeologico di Aeclanum

History and architecture · Archaeology

The remains of the Sannite and Roman city on the Via Appia: thermal baths, walls, public buildings and commercial spaces that tell the role of ancient Aeclanum in the traffics of ancient Italy.

  • Where Mirabella Eclano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo del Carro di Paglia e dei Misteri di cartapesta

Museums · Themed

In the former stables of the Franciscan monastery a path that follows the stages of the Passion with the statues in mache, about one and a half meters tall, that the local artist Antonio Russo (1836-1914) donated to the community in 1875; multimedia rooms explain the technique of mache.

  • Where Via Municipio 61, Mirabella Eclano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.mirabellaeclano.av.it · italia.it

Complesso monumentale di San Francesco

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry

Founded in 1222 and largely rebuilt after the earthquake of 1732: single nave church with eighteenth-century portal, pulpit, choir and organ, cloister and small square with the spire of San Raffaele of 1777; in the convent are the town hall and museums.

  • Where Via Municipio 1, Mirabella Eclano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.mirabellaeclano.av.it · italia.it
