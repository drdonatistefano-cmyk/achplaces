What to see in Bolognola

One of the smallest and tallest municipalities in the Marche region (1,070 m), entirely in the Sibillini Mountains National Park, formed by three medieval centres with churches, palaces and the remains of the castle; 3 km away is the Pintura ski resort. After the earthquake of 2016 historic center and monuments are only partially usable.

Borgo delle tre Ville e resti del castello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village consists of three medieval centers, Villa da Capo (Villa Malvezzi), Villa di Mezzo (Villa Pepoli) and Villa da Piedi (Villa Bentivoglio), each with a church, a noble palace and a square; the ruins of the castle with its turrite walls remain. You can see Palazzo Maurizi, seat of the Town Hall, with the long stone staircase of the Fonte del Catenaccio, Palazzo Primavera (XVI-XVII century) with frescoed rooms and basements used as a wool mill from the early twentieth century, the birthplace of composer Filippo Marchetti (1831-1902) and monuments to the Fallen and the Snow victims.

after the earthquake of 2016 the old town is partially accessible and some areas are red areas with no access

  • Where Centro storico, Bolognola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: sibillini.net · letsmarche.it · turismo.comune.bolognola.mc.it

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo e Madonna del Rosario

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In Piazza Leopardi, the main church of San Michele Arcangelo was rebuilt in 1934 and has two altars; the table of Our Lady of the Rosary by Giulio Vergari (1519) is now exposed in the parish church.

after the earthquake of 2016 the monuments are partially usable; the card of the Park describes the state prior to the earthquake

  • Where Piazza Leopardi, Bolognola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: sibillini.net · letsmarche.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A baroque 17th century building with 18th century paintings and a fresco of the Virgin attributed to Jerome of John; in front of it stands the birthplace of Filippo Marchetti.

Chiesa di San Nicolò di Bari

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the village of Villa da Piedi, you can reach via Sotto Castello, dominated by the walls of the Fortress of the XV century; the church dates back to the mid-nineteenth century and rises on a more ancient structure, built between the sixteenth and seventeenth century.

after the earthquake of 2016 the monuments are partially usable (Let's Marche)

Pintura di Bolognola

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Frazione about 3 km from the capital with a wide view and ski resort: 1,280 m to the top of Monte Castelmanardo (1,680 m), the Panoramica Fargno slope of 2 km, snowpark, Kinderland area, a cross-country skiing ring of more than 4 km between beech woods with views of the Sibillini and slopes suitable for freeride and ski touring.

  • Where Località Pintura, Bolognola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it

Monte Rotondo, forra dell'Acquasanta e sorgenti del Fiastrone

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Under the Rotondo Mountain, the highest peak of the municipal territory, the gorge of the Acquasanta with the homonymous waterfall opens; in Bolognola the sources of the river Fiastrone are born.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
