What to see in Amantea

Città del Tirreno cosentino with a medieval village perched under the castle, churches from the thirteenth to the fifteenth century, the Tower of Coreca on the sea and a promenade with nineteenth-century villas, beaches like Coreca and Tonnara and the Scogli di Isca for diving.

10 places to visit in Amantea, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello, Torre sveva e mura

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the flat top of the rocky hill that dominates the ancient inhabited, with a quadrilateral plan with moat on two sides, masonry bridge and four corner towers (the oldest circular with a escarpment base); the first defenses date back to the Arabic period, then several times restored and expanded. On the same plateau, near the door, a cylindrical tower believed to be of Swabian age (XIII century); remains parts of the walls, renovated from the Aragonese to the Viceroyal Age, which until the nineteenth century enclosed the old town.

  • Where Centro storico, Amantea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.amantea.cs.it · italia.it

Centro storico e borgo medievale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Daedalus of alleys of the medieval village, connected to the modern part from the square of Calavecchia; a panoramic path runs along the side of the hill. Among the churches: San Bernardino da Siena (XV century) and the Cathedral of San Biagio, built on a previous Greek church dedicated to San Pantaleone. In a church-convento of the center the FAI indicates a marble altarpiece by Pietro Bernini and a Madonna with Child attributed to A. Gagini.

  • Where Piazzetta di Calavecchia, Amantea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it

Ruderi della Chiesa di San Francesco d'Assisi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Church and convent were built between 1221 and 1264 by the Friars Minor Conventuals, who arrived in Amantea in the 13th century, on a site below the castle area; today remains the ruins.

  • Where Sotto il castello, Amantea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.amantea.cs.it · italia.it

Monastero delle Clarisse

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Erected in 1603 on a ledge of the ancient cliff separated by a sea cave; it preserves polylobed windows, double windows with tortile columns, wooden ceilings and emblems of ancient noble families.

  • Entry To be checked

Ex Collegio dei Gesuiti

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Construction dating back to 1710, founded by Fulvio Verdiano, an illustrious citizen of Amantea; the perimeter walls remain standing, without cover, with the characteristic triple crowning of hembrics in a small object.

  • Where Centro storico, Amantea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.amantea.cs.it · italia.it

Casa Rurale della resa del 1807

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

In the rural house in the locality Rota on February 6, 1807 was signed the surrender of the city to the French troops after a siege of nine months; outside a memorial plaque dictated by Ernesto De Luca of Lizzano.

  • Where Località Rota, Amantea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
