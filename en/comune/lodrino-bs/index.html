What to see in Lodrino

A village in the Trompia Valley among mountains that many call the Brescia Dolomites, with the Ethnographic Museum and Farm House in the hamlet of Invico, the eighteenth-century sanctuary of the Holy Cross reachable on foot and the pine forest of the Alpine Park at the foot of the Caspai.

3 places to visit in Lodrino, in the province of Brescia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo etnografico e Casa contadina

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the premises of the old primary schools of Invico, documents the daily life in Lodrino and Valle Trompia before industrialization: over a thousand objects in about 400 m2 divided into rooms on hunting and territory, haymaking and breeding, wood processing, blacksmith's forge and a collection of medical instruments of the nineteenth century. Santuario della Santa Croce

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Small church of the eighteenth century that houses an ancient wooden cross; it is reached on foot from the village along a path of moderate slope.

  • Where Sentiero dal centro di Lodrino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrescia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · comune.lodrino.bs.it · visitbrescia.it

Pineta e Parco degli alpini

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Pineta planted in the thirties at the foot of the rocky leap of the Corna di Caspai, equipped by the alpine with benches and tables.

  • Where Località Pineta, Lodrino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbrescia.it

Sources: comune.lodrino.bs.it · in-lombardia.it · visitbrescia.it
