What to see in Mendatica

The village is almost perched at the apex of the Upper Arroscia Valley, in the Regional Nature Park of the Ligurian Alps, linked to the shepherding and Ligurian capital of the white cuisine of transhumance; it offers the museum of the civilization of the pastures, the frescoed church of Santa Margherita, the sanctuary of the Madonna dei Colombi and the waterfalls of the Arroscia.

Civiltà delle malghe: Casa del Pastore, Vecchie Prigioni e Sala delle Carte

Museums · Themed

Location of the Museum of the Territory of the Arroscia Valley . The faces of the Ubagu , is a route in five sections scattered in the country. The House of the Shepherd reconstructs on two floors a typical house: on the ground floor the stable with tools for cattle and cheese, above the kitchen with central fireplace and the room. The Old Prisons are two cells of the sixteenth century for prisoners awaiting trial of the Court of Pieve di Teco; the Sala di cartography historical exhibits reproductions of maps of the sixteenth-XVIII centuries from the archives of Turin, Genoa and Nice. The route is completed by an ancient forge with original mantle and a naturalistic laboratory with the fauna of the Ligurian Alps. Tel. Common 0183 328713.

Chiesa di Santa Margherita

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Late-Medieval church that preserves a cycle of frescoes of the sixteenth century by Pietro Guido da Ranzo; it is the starting point of the ring of the waterfalls of the Arroscia.

  • Where Località Santa Margherita, Mendatica
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Borgo, parrocchiale dei Santi Nazario e Celso e santuario della Madonna dei Colombi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

One of the rock villages of the Ligurian Alps, inhabited already in the Neolithic and documented since the 7th century. The Baroque parish church of Saints Nazarius and Celsus preserves part of the fourteenth-century Romanesque bell tower; at the south-eastern end of the village the late Baroque sanctuary of the Madonna dei Colombi was destroyed by the earthquake of 1887 and rebuilt.

  • Where Centro storico, Mendatica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page parconaturalealpiliguri.it

Sources: parconaturalealpiliguri.it · lamialiguria.it

Cascate dell'Arroscia

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

The Arroscia stream, which is born from Monte Fronté, forms a jump of about 20 meters; the ring starts from the frescoed church of Santa Margherita, reaches the waterfalls and goes back to the huts of Case Pian del Lago before returning to Mendatica.

Malghe della Val Tanarello

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Park indicates the stone pastures of the Tanarello Valley: Valcona Soprana, Valcona Sottana and Le Salse, linked to the transhumant pastoralism from which the white cuisine is born, of which Mendatica is one of the two Ligurian capitals.

  • Where Val Tanarello, Mendatica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page parconaturalealpiliguri.it
