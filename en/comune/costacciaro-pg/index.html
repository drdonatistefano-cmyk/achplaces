What to see in Costacciaro

Castle walled at the foot of Monte Cucco, bought by Gubbio in the 13th century, with four doors, the Civic Tower and the church of San Francesco; it is the gateway to the Park of Monte Cucco and its cave, among the major in Italy, with the Museum-Laboratory of the Park and the National Centre of Speleology.

Centro storico, mura e porte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The castle, purchased by the Municipality of Gubbio in the 13th century, passed to the Papal State in the 17th century and the Kingdom of Italy in 1860. It preserves the remains of the fortress and the walls with the Civic Tower of the 13th century and four doors: of the clock, of the Trione (or of the Rivellino), of Guerrino Gambucci and of the Monument (or of San Lorenzo).

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in the middle of the 13th century and enlarged at the beginning of the 18th century, it has a Romanesque façade in limestone of Monte Cucco; inside frescoes in the style of Matteo da Gualdo. Under the high altar rests the remains of Blessed Thomas, a Camaldolese monk and patron saint of the village.

  • Where Costacciaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Museo-laboratorio del Parco del Monte Cucco

Museums · Science and university

In the former church of San Marco, in the historic center: explains the geology of the Umbrian-Marche Apennines and the path of rainwater up to springs and slopes. The visit starts from the section of the Cave of Monte Cucco, whose water re-emerges about 1,000 meters below the source of Scirca; follow sections on formation of the mountains, origin of the rocks, extinct fauna, vegetation, a plastic and a documentation center on the karst areas. At about 200 m, in via Galeazzi, the laboratory classrooms \"Energy & Life" on the history of energy. Info: tel. 075 9172723.

Antico frantoio dell'olio

Museums · Themed

In an old renovated restaurant in the historic centre, it illustrates the phases of the processing of olives, from milling to pressing and oil harvesting. It probably dates back to the XVII-XVIII century, it was active until a few years ago (propriety of Carlo Bartoletti) and from the origins it was called "Il Montano" (The Montano), described as the largest and oldest oil mill in the area, preserves the mill, the oak beam with counterweight in stone and an old block of notes.

Parco del Monte Cucco

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Protected area since 1996 on the Umbrian-Marche Apennines, includes the municipalities of Costacciaro, Scheggia and Pascelupo, Fossato di Vico and Sigillo: meadows, woods, beech woods, gorges like that of the Rio Freddo towards Pascelupo, high springs and a large reservoir of water fed by rains. The summit of Monte Cucco can be reached by car or on foot on marked paths; excursions suitable for everyone, even for children, as well as caving and hang gliding.

  • Where Parco del Monte Cucco, Costacciaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Grotta di Monte Cucco

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

The most important element of the park is one of the major karst formations in Italy, with about 30 km of galleries. In Costacciaro are located the School of Speleology and the National Center of Speleology, in the former Clarisse monastery, and the Didactic Village linked to the speleological Hiking Centre; among the experiences proposed the crossing of the cave.
