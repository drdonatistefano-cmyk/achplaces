What to see in Palazzolo Acreide

Baroque city of the Iblei rebuilt after the earthquake of 1693 and among the eight centers of the UNESCO site of the late Baroque towns of the Val di Noto: the churches with façade of the tower of San Sebastiano and San Paolo, the Madonna del Laurana all'Immacolata, the Antonino Uccello Museum House and, on the hill, the ancient Akrai founded by Siracusa in 664 BC, with the theater and the rock sanctuary of the Santoni.

Area archeologica di Akrai

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Akrai, the oldest sub-colony of Syracuse, was founded in 664 B.C. on the hill of the Chromon. Theater (between III and II century BC according to sources) and the bouleuterion, the decuman with lava flooring, a circular spa building, the foundations of a temple of the second half of the 6th century BC considered of Aphrodite, the side-shaped of the Carved and the Carnival with catacombs and early Christian hypogeans and the Feral Temples are visited.

  • Where Colle dell'Acromonte, Palazzolo Acreide
  • Entry €6, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Santuario rupestre dei Santoni

History and architecture · Archaeology

Sanctuary of Hellenistic age (IV-III century BC) dedicated to Cibele, on the south side of the Colle Orbo: twelve large niches and reliefs carved into the rock, largely with the goddess seated on the throne, along with figures such as Hermes, Attis, Hecate, Dioscuri, Gauls and Coribanti. In 2021, the Superintendence started the race for its recovery.

Casa museo Antonino Uccello

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

On the ground floor of the eighteenth-century Palazzo Ferla-Bonelli, in the rooms once inhabited by the massarium, collects the objects of the peasant civilization saved by the poet and ethnologist Antonino Uccello (Canicattini 1922), established at Palazzolo in 1944: wooden spoons, orange wood nativity scenes, cart keys and iron sculptures, which he also exhibited in the galleries of the North.

MiC card indicates closed every day (aggiornamento 2021)
