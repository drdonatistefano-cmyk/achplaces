What to see in Montemurro

Country of Val d'Agri was born, according to tradition, from the refugees of Grumentum, home of the poet and engineer Leonardo Sinisgalli; it preserves the church and the Franciscan convent of Sant'Antonio, the church of San Domenico and the former Dominican convent today Town Hall.

5 places to visit in Montemurro, in the province of Potenza (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa e convento di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Franciscan complex of 1635 in the upper part of the village: in the monastery frescoes with episodes of the life of St. Anthony and St. Francis; in the church a wooden frame with canvas dated 1666, a Nativity of the eighteenth century and a Pietà of the nineteenth century.

  • Where Parte alta del paese, Montemurro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page sit.parcoappenninolucano.it

Sources: sit.parcoappenninolucano.it

Ex convento dei Domenicani e Municipio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The walls house the works of the painters Maria Padula, Antonio Liuzzi and Mario Venece, and a plaster coin with the effigy of Sinisgalli, by Antonio Masini. The building is intended for Leonardo Sinisgalli Literary Park.

  • Where Montemurro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page sit.parcoappenninolucano.it

Sources: sit.parcoappenninolucano.it

Chiesa di San Domenico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Casa natale di Leonardo Sinisgalli

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The poet's house in Corso Garibaldi has a marble slab on the façade with the poem "Monete Rossi" (Red Monete), in Montemurro Sinisgalli returned in July 1944 and composed "L'indovino" (The Indovino).

  • Where Corso Garibaldi, Montemurro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page sit.parcoappenninolucano.it

Sources: sit.parcoappenninolucano.it · parcoappenninolucano.it

Masseria Crisci

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Ancient house and farm with the farm for farm workers, since 1900 dairy farm; it is located near the dam of Pertusillo, in a tourist area of the valley.
