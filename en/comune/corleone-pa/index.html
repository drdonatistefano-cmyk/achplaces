What to see in Corleone

A small town of the inland of Palermo called "Of the hundred churches" dominated by the twin fortresses with the Castle Soprano and the Castle Sottono; a few steps from the center the canyon and the Cascata delle Due Rocche, in the village the Civic Museum "Pippo Rizzo" and the CIDMA, and in the hamlet of Ficuzza the Real Casina hunting of the Bourbons on the edge of the Bosco della Ficuzza.

8 places to visit in Corleone, in the province of Palermo (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Real Casina di caccia di Ficuzza

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Neoclassical residence with influences of the late Sicilian Baroque, built at the end of the eighteenth century on a project by Giuseppe Venanzio Marvuglia for Ferdinando di Borbone, who chose the Ficuzza as a hunting reserve and summer holiday. The interiors have undergone plundering and transformations and remains only the chapel of the original style; today it houses thematic paths and a multimedia room on the nature of the reserve.

  • Where Via del Bosco, Ficuzza, Corleone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Bosco della Ficuzza e Rocca Busambra

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Reserve that includes oak woods, Mediterranean scrub, the pastures and rocky heights of the Rocca Busambra, sanctuary of flora and fauna and in particular of rare raptors; numerous paths, including the historical points of the "Pulpito del Re" and the ancient charcoal. In the area with the Sosio Valley there are the Dragon Gorges and the Giant Marmittes.

  • Where Ficuzza, Corleone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info

Canyon e Cascata delle Due Rocche

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural park in the center of the village: the waterfall, about 4 meters high, is a jump of the river San Leonardo, tributary of Belice, which has dug a pool among limestone rocks; glauconic yellow and green walls with rock vegetation and remains of an ancient aqueduct, and above the canyon. The steep path starts from the Benedictine monastery of the SS. Salvatore and climb to the Rocca dei Maschi, with views of the canyon, the Saracen Tower, the Hermitage of San Bernardo and the Rocca Busambra.

  • Where Monastero del SS. Salvatore, Corleone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.corleone.pa.it · italia.it

Castello Soprano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the fortress to the east of the village, you can reach the churchyard of San Michele Arcangelo on a path through medieval alleys; sighting tower built by the Arabs in the ninth century, cylindrical with a dry stone dome, crowned by the tower called "saracena" of uncertain dating. Already in ruins in the eighteenth century.

  • Where Rocca Soprana, Corleone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.corleone.pa.it · italia.it

Castello Sottano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the monolith in the center of the village, on the right bank of the river: of the medieval complex are perhaps only the walls of the belt; renovated by the Bourbons around 1845, it was prison until 1968 and today houses a hermitage of the Friars Minor Renovated. In the Middle Ages it was joined to the Castle Soprano by a city wall.

  • Where Rocca Sottana, Corleone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.corleone.pa.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

Museo civico «Pippo Rizzo»

Museums · Archaeological

It is located in Palazzo Provenzano, a noble residence of the eighteenth century; it houses among other things a milestone with a Latin inscription of 252 BC and a Roman mosaic of the imperial age (1st century AD).

  • Where Palazzo Provenzano, Corleone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.corleone.pa.it · visitsicily.info · italia.it

CIDMA, Centro Internazionale di Documentazione sulla Mafia e del Movimento Antimafia

Museums · Themed

Founded in 2000 in the monumental complex of San Ludovico, former orphanage: four rooms on the fight against the mafia, with the Maxiprocess of 1986, a room of photographs of Letizia Battaglia and the room dedicated to General Carlo Alberto Dalla Chiesa, killed in Palermo in 1982.

  • Where Complesso di San Ludovico, Corleone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches
