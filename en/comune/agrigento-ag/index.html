What to see in Agrigento

The ancient Akragas founded in 581 BC: the Valley of the Temples UNESCO heritage with the Temple of Concord, the Garden of the Kolymbethra and the Museum Pietro Griffo; on the hill the Arab and Norman city with the cathedral of San Gerlando, Santa Maria dei Greci and Santo Spirito; at the Chaos the birthplace of Pirandello.

14 places to visit in Agrigento, in the province of Agrigento (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Valle dei Templi

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

The archaeological area of Agrigento, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1997, considered one of the most beautiful archaeological parks in the world for vastness and conservation; it also includes the Hellenistic-Roman quarter.

Giardino della Kolymbethra

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

In the Valley of the Temples at the Temple of the Dioscuri: about 2,500 years ago the tyrant Terone had it dug tunnels and hypogeans for water, creating a large tank; it has been entrusted to the FAI since 1999.
