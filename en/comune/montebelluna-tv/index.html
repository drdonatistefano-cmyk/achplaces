What to see in Montebelluna

City of the high plain of Treviso, capital of the sports footwear district: you can visit the Sportsystem Foundation Museum in Villa Binetti, the MeVe, multimedia memorial of the Great War in the complex of Villa Correr Pisani, and the Museum of natural history and archaeology; to the north you can reach the Bosco del Montello.

MeVe Memoriale Veneto della Grande Guerra

Museums · Themed

Interactive and multimedia space of 2,300 square meters in the complex of Villa Correr Pisani, dedicated to the conflicts and events of the twentieth century since the First World War; it hosts a permanent laboratory of civic education and is also a tourist information point. Times indicated: Tuesday-Friday 14.30-18, Saturday and Sunday 10-18, Monday closed.

  • Where Via Aglaia Anassillide 5, Montebelluna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Museo della Fondazione Sportsystem (Museo dello scarpone)

Museums · Themed

In the heart of the city, at Villa Binetti, it tells two centuries of the Montebellunese footwear district: thousands of shoes, historic objects, sketches, patents and machinery in 11 rooms on two floors, from the nineteenth century to today. Times indicated: Monday-Wednesday 9.30-13, Thursday-Friday 9.30-18, Saturday and Sunday closed.

  • Where Vicolo Zuccareda 5, Montebelluna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.montebelluna.tv.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.montebelluna.tv.it

Museo di storia naturale e archeologia

Museums · Science and university

The naturalistic section goes from astronomy, with a vaulted room that reproduces the Milky Way, meteorites and interactive exhibits, to mineralogy and geology of Treviso, to invertebrates and vertebrates: the fish room collects about 90% of the freshwater fish fauna of Veneto and an area is dedicated to Montello. There is also an archaeological section with temporary exhibitions.

  • Where Via Piave 51, Montebelluna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.montebelluna.tv.it

Sources: museomontebelluna.it · comune.montebelluna.tv.it

Bosco del Montello

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The wood covers the Montello hill, a relief stretched from east to west in the heart of the high plain of Treviso, up to 369 meters, bordered to the north and east by the Piave. Protected from Venice from the fourteenth century for the oak for ships, it was largely deforested after the Bertolini law of 1892; cross it from north to south the 20 's' open in 1893 and the underground karst houses caves like the Busa of Castel Sotterra.

  • Where Montello, Montebelluna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.montebelluna.tv.it

Sources: comune.montebelluna.tv.it
