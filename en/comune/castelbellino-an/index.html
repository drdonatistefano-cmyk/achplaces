What to see in Castelbellino

Borgo della Vallesina on a rocky spur surrounded by the Esino, among the vineyards of the Verdicchio dei Castelli di Jesi: from the remains of the castle and the Belvedere Loggetta the look comes to the sea, and Villa Coppetti hosts the Civic Museum; at Christmas a large luminous tree lights up on the hill.

Villa Coppetti e Museo Civico

Museums · Old masters

Neoclassical villa of the late eighteenth century and early nineteenth century, perhaps of the Meriggiani family, with park of tall trees. The museum exhibits Roman fragments, the Madonna of the Rosary by Benedetto Nucci, two paintings by Ernst van Schayck and a section of contemporary graphics with 260 sheets and drawings in china, from the donations Ginesi, Garibaldi Jallet and Romiti-Mosullani, the latter with material on the Garibaldi family and on Garibalda Canzio, lived in Castelbellino until 1969.

Castello e Loggetta Belvedere

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The castle of the XIII-XIV century remains ruined; on the summit the Loggetta Belvedere, Renaissance, once a covered market. From Piazza San Marco the panorama embraces the valley of the Esino to the sea.

  • Where Piazza San Marco, Castelbellino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it
