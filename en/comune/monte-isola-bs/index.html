What to see in Monte Isola

The largest island of the European lakes, in Lake Iseo, up to 600 meters high and without private cars; fishing villages like Peschiera Maraglio, the capital Siviano with the Martinengo tower, the Fortress Martinengo of the 14th century and, on top, the sanctuary of the Madonna of the Ceriola with 360 degrees view.

8 places to visit in Monte Isola, in the province of Brescia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario della Madonna della Ceriola

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

White shrine at 600 meters, at the highest point of the island above the hamlet of Cure, built on the site of a pagan building and rich in ex-vote; it is considered among the oldest places of Marian worship in Italy. Walk up (about 4 km and 1 hour and 30 from Peschiera Maraglio, path no. 1) or by minibus, with dedicated summer rides; near refreshment and picnic area.

  • Where Località Cure, Monte Isola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlakeiseo.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Rocca Martinengo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress built in the fourteenth century by the Oldofredi above Sensole and transformed into residence by the Martinengo in the fifteenth century, with the tower in the center of the castle surrounded by a moat; it is reached with a paved path that starts from Menzino.

  • Where Menzino, Monte Isola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlakeiseo.info · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Peschiera Maraglio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Borgo once inhabited almost only by fishermen, with alleys connected by arches and stairs and flowered houses of geraniums; it is the most convenient landing point (traghetti every 15 minutes from Sulzano), with restaurants and shops on the lakefront.

  • Where Peschiera Maraglio, Monte Isola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlakeiseo.info · italia.it

Siviano e torre Martinengo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The capital of the island preserves medieval traces in the walls and alleys; on the square of the town hall dominates the tower Martinengo, of feudal age, with square plan and about 20 meters high. In the hamlet of Porto there is the sixteenth-century Villa Solitudo; the parish is dedicated to Saints Faustino and Giovita.

  • Where Siviano, Monte Isola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlakeiseo.info · italia.it

Carzano e Novale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Carzano, an ancient feud of the Martinengo family, preserves the Martinengo palace (private), the sixteenth-century Casa Novali and the Ziliani palace with stone portal of Sarnico; every five years it hosts the feast of Santa Croce, when the village and Novale are covered with thousands of paper flowers. Novale, a medieval village on a terrace above Carzano, in the mid-sixteenth century was the summer residence of the bishop of Brescia Marco Morosini (Casa Morosini).

  • Where Carzano, Monte Isola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlakeiseo.info · italia.it

Masse e chiesa di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Masse, 400 meters away, is reached on foot from Siviano along an ancient road in white pebbles; there is the church of San Rocco, of the XV century and restored in the XVII. Not far away, a large red sandstone boulder marks, according to tradition, the meeting place of witches.

  • Where Masse, Monte Isola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Museo delle Reti e dei Ricordi

Museums · Themed

Private collection of documents, historical photographs and objects related to fishing and the production of nets, housed in the oldest network factory on the island.

  • Where Porto di Siviano, Monte Isola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlakeiseo.info · italia.it

Passeggiata degli ulivi

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The most picturesque route on the island: about 2 km (30 minutes) by the lake between Peschiera Maraglio and Sensole, between centuries-old olive trees and old piers of fishermen, overlooking the private island of São Paulo; picnic area and bar along the way. The full tour of the island measures about 9 km (2 hours and 30 hours).

  • Where Da Peschiera Maraglio a Sensole, Monte Isola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlakeiseo.info
