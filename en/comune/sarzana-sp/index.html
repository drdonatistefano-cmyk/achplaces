What to see in Sarzana

Historical capital of Lunigiana and border town between Liguria and Tuscany, a medieval village remodeled in Renaissance forms at the end of the fifteenth century: a walled center on the Via Francigena between Porta Parma and Porta Romana, two Medici fortresses (Firmafede and Sarzanello), the Teatro degli Imavevidi and the low and sandy sea of Marinella.

Fortezza Firmafede (Cittadella) e MUdeF

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

On the edge of the walls of the historic center, the fortress of Pisa of the thirteenth century was demolished and rebuilt between 1487 and 1494 at the behest of Lorenzo the Magnificent (architects Giuliano da Sangallo, Francione and Luca del Caprina); it passed to the Banco di San Giorgio in 1496 and to the Republic of Genoa from 1562 to 1797, then it was barracks and prison until the seventies. Restored between 1985 and 2003, it hosts exhibitions, the Festival of Mind and the National Antique Exhibition; since 2016 the MUdeF, multimedia museum of the Lunigiana fortresses in 27 rooms. Guests can visit the piazza d'armi, towers, the walk of a roundabout, tunnels and underground galleries; Tuesday-Sunday 10-13 and 16-19, Monday closed.

Fortezza di Sarzanello (Castruccio Castracani)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the hill of Sarzanello, above the city and the Val di Magra, documented by the X-XI century: Castruccio Castracani chose it as a residence as an imperial vicar (1314-1328) and Tomaso Campofregoso modified it in the fifteenth century. The current aspect is due to Lorenzo de' Medici and to the Francione, with works since 1493 concluded in 1502 by the Republic of Genoa: triangular plant with three corner towers and a large triangular rivet to defend the entrance, reached intact until today. Tuesday-Sunday 10-13 and 16-19, Monday closed; guided tours and audioguide.

  • Where Via della Fortezza, Sarzana
  • Entry €5, reduced €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website fortezzadisarzanello.com
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: fortezzadisarzanello.com · cultura.gov.it · lamialiguria.it · italia.it

Centro storico murato e Via Francigena

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Walled village with walls and towers and a geometric road system between Porta Parma and Porta Romana, along Via Bertoloni and Via Mazzini on the ancient route of Via Francigena. Sarzana is one of the capitals of the antiques; here on October 6, 1306 Dante Alighieri appeared before the Sarzano notary for peace between the Malaspina and the bishop-count of Luni.

  • Where Via Mazzini e via Bertoloni, Sarzana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: lamialiguria.it · italia.it

Teatro degli Impavidi

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built at the expense of private citizens, the "Impavidi" (Impavidi), as the Latin motto recalls in the façade dated 1809; damaged in the Second World War and rebuilt, is known for the frescoed ceiling and is the second theater for capacity of the province of Spezia.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
