What to see in Chioggia

Cities of Roman origin crossed by the canals San Domenico, Vena and Lombardo, Chioggia is called the "small Venice": along the Corso del Popolo you will meet the Clock Tower with its museum, the Cathedral of Longhena, the Temple of San Martino and the Pescheria, while towards the sea there are the beaches of Sottomarina and Isola Verde, the Walls of the Serenissima, the woods and the reserve of Bosco Nordio.

Centro storico, calli e Corso del Popolo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center is crossed by the canals San Domenico, Vena and Lombardo, which make Chioggia a small Venice, the city has Roman origins and populated with the inhabitants of the hinterland fleeing from barbarian invasions. Corso del Popolo, elegant and always crowded, was defined by Curzio Malaparte as a great coffee.

  • Where Corso del Popolo, Chioggia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: www-old.veneto.eu · italia.it

Torre dell'Orologio e Museo dell'Orologio

Museums · City history · Viewpoint

The bell tower of Sant'Andrea, about 30 meters high, dates back to the 11th-XII century; once a beacon and a watch tower, it still has a clock attested in 1386 that the city portal defines the oldest working tower clock in the world. The vertical museum exhibits historical maps of the city, ex-vote, a stole of Cardinal Aristide Naccari and an astronomical route with astrolabe; from the top you can visit the mechanism and the bell cell.

  • Where Corso del Popolo, accanto alla Chiesa di Sant'Andrea, Chioggia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitchioggia.com · www-old.veneto.eu · italia.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Example of Baroque designed by Baldassare Longhena, author of the Salute of Venice; in the aisles side works of Jacopo Palma il Giovane and a Callido organ with 1617 reeds. It rises south of the square, between Porta Garibaldi and the Tempietto di San Martino.

  • Where Piazzetta Vescovile, Chioggia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Porta Garibaldi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Basilica di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

It preserves the icon of the Madonna della Navicella, surrounded by the "tolele" , votive tablets donated by the faithful; in the altarpiece the saints of the lower part are attributed to Giambellino, the upper part to Antonio Marinetti, while the face of the Madonna comes from a fresco detached from Palazzo Granaio.

  • Entry Free

Ponte Vigo e colonna del «gatòn»

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The bridge, built in 1685 and decorated with marbles in 1762, is guarded by two lions in stone of Istria; in the nearby square the column Vigo is surmounted by a lion nicknamed "el gatòn" .

Museo Civico della Laguna Sud «San Francesco fuori le mura»

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 1997 in the former 14th-century convent of San Francesco outside the walls, on three floors: archaeological finds from the Bronze Age to the 6th century AD, the tower of the Bebe and the war of Chioggia, a medieval cistern; a room dedicated to Cristoforo Sabbadino and to the brothers Aldo and Dino Ballarin of the Great Turin; on the top floor shipbuilding and local marine with models of the bragozzo, the civic pictorial collection and the marinery of the high sea.

  • Where Campo Marconi 1, Chioggia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitchioggia.com · italia.it

Museo di Zoologia Adriatica «Giuseppe Olivi»

Museums · Science and university

In the eighteenth century Palazzo Grassi collects Adriatic marine organisms, the result of research carried out from the second half of the nineteenth century to 1943, with panels and multimedia locations on biodiversity; there is exposed a female elephant shark captured in Chioggia in 2003.

Murazzi di Sottomarina

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The work of Istria stone wanted by the Serenissima to defend the coast from the marshes; the Murazzi of Via San Felice were over 5 km long and more than 300 meters wide, built on a territory of palafitte extended to Pellestrina.

Tegnùe di Chioggia

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches

Rocky formations of the seabed a few miles from the shore, reachable by diving; the name comes from the fishermen, who saw there entangle the nets. Those in front of Chioggia are described as the largest and most spectacular of the Adriatic, so much so that they are called Adriatic coral reefs, oasis of biodiversity.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitchioggia.com · italia.it

Riserva naturale integrale Bosco Nordio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

The entire natural reserve of the Veneto Region of 113 hectares, wild wood of holm oaks, lauri and pine trees on dunes that in Roman times were part of a coastal cordon extended up to the Po and Ravenna. There live deer, heroes, badgers and hares, and birds like swamp hawks, common owls, herons and peaks; you visit with guided excursions.

  • Where Sant'Anna di Chioggia, via Pegorina (SS309 Romea km 79), Chioggia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website bosconordio.venetoagricoltura.org
