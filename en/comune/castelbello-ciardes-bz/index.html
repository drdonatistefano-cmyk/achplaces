What to see in Castelbello-Ciardes

Municipality of the lower Val Venosta inhabited for over a thousand years, among vineyards, apple orchards and ancient irrigation canals: on the cliff on the Adige the castle of Castelbello, now a cultural center, and at the top, at the entrance of the Val Senales, Castel Juval with the Messner Mountain Museum.

Messner Mountain Museum Juval

Museums · Themed · Paid entry · Viewpoint

High Renaissance-style castle on the Venosta Valley, residence of the Messner family, with about 1,500 years of history: the museum is dedicated to the spiritual dimension of the mountain, with sacred mountains (Kailash, Fuji, Ayers Rock, Ararat, Olympus), Tibetan objects, masks from five continents, frescoes, sculptures, an adventure library and meditative spaces. Open from the fourth Sunday of March to the first Sunday of November, 10-17 (last entrance 16), closed on Wednesday; from the paid parking Juval P1 on the SS38 electric shuttle and about 15 minutes walk, or trails from Ciardes and Stava (1-1.5 hours). Not accessible to those with motor difficulties.

  • Where Juval 3, Castelbello-Ciardes
  • Entry €14, reduced €12, boys 6-18 €6, family €32
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.messner-mountain-museum.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: messner-mountain-museum.it · italia.it · tickets.messner-mountain-museum.it

Castel Castelbello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

It was first mentioned in 1238 and built by the lords of Montalban on a cliff on the left bank of the Adige, from 1531 it was a fief in pledge of the Hendl, which gave it its present appearance; the fires of 1813 and 1824 devastated it and in 1956 the State bought the complex, restored under the Superintendence until 1995 and managed since 1999 by a cooperative. The chapel, at the end of the thirteenth century or early fourteenth century, has a frieze with the twelve Apostles in pairs and the texts of the Creed, with alternating normal and mirrorful inscriptions. Today it is the cultural center of the country, with exhibitions in spring and autumn; it can be visited only with a guide, from April to October.

  • Where Via Statale 5, Castelbello-Ciardes
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.schloss-kastelbell.com

Sources: schloss-kastelbell.com · suedtirol.info · vinschgau.net · italia.it

Waalweg di Ciardes e Stava

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of 8.3 km, about 2 hours and 420 meters of difference in altitude, along the historical irrigation canals of Ciardes and Stava: from the center of Ciardes the path climbs to the farm Sonnenhof and to Castel Juval, passes through the Waalerhütte and the Waalerschelle and goes back along the Stabener Waalweg.

  • Where Centro di Ciardes (parcheggio sopra la caserma dei pompieri), Castelbello-Ciardes
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it

Sentiero del vino della Val Venosta

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry · Viewpoint

A trail of about 9 km on the steep slopes of the valley, between ancient irrigation channels, vineyards and apple orchards; the stretch of Castelbello is crossed in a few hours, and between the wineries of the village that offer tastings Italy.it mentions Köfelgut and Marinus Hof. The beginning of autumn, with apples and harvest, is the recommended period.

  • Where Castelbello-Ciardes
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Cappella neogotica di San Giuseppe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Chapel founded in 1924 by Andreas Kuppelwieser as a thank you for coming back with the brothers unharmed from World War I; his initials are carved at the base of the wooden stall.

  • Where Via Statale 5, Castelbello-Ciardes
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it
