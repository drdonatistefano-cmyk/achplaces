What to see in Garessio

In the upper Tanaro Valley, towards Liguria, the four historic villages of Garessio and, in Val Casotto, the hunting palace of the Savoy born from an ancient Carthusian, UNESCO World Heritage.

Castello Reale di Casotto (Valcasotto)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Viewpoint

At 1,090 meters in Val Casotto, the royal residence further south of the Sabaudo circuit, born on the remains of the Carthusian founded in 1172 and transformed by Carlo Alberto into hunting residence. Guided tours by reservation from spring to autumn. UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  • Where Via Valcasotto, Garessio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · regione.piemonte.it · turismotorino.org · italia.it

Borgo Maggiore

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The medieval complex with the remains of the castle and the walls and the Confraternita di San Giovanni, between paved streets and squares.

  • Where Borgo Maggiore, Garessio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · italia.it

Borgo Valsorda e il santuario

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

The highest village, gathered around the sanctuary of the Blessed Virgin of the Graces of Valsorda; in Borgo Poggiolo the sources of San Bernardo.

  • Where Borgo Valsorda, Garessio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
