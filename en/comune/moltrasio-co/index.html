What to see in Moltrasio

Borgo sul Lago di Como known for the stone \"moltrasina" with the Romanesque Sant'Agata of the 11th century, the parish church of Saints Martin and Agata and historic villas like Villa Passalacqua, where Vincenzo Bellini stayed; four thematic routes (water, stone, history, panorama) cross the village.

5 places to visit in Moltrasio, in the province of Como (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa di Sant'Agata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church with two naves in Moltrasino stone, in the locality of Vignola along the ancient Via Regia: dates back to the 11th century, a document of 1197 attests to a charity dedicated to the saint and in the 17th century was used as a lazzaretto. Outside the images of St. Agatha and St. Christopher, protectors of the travelers; inside sixteenth-century frescoes including the Christ Pantocrator between the Saints Rocco and Antonio Abate (restored in 2006) and a Renaissance wooden crucifix. Next to the churchyard is the nineteenth-century chapel Lucini Passalacqua.

The parishyard houses works of art from the beginning of the sixteenth century to the eighteenth century; the patron saint of San Martino is celebrated in November.

Villa Passalacqua

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built at the end of the eighteenth century by the will of the Odescalchi on the ruins of a monastery of the Humiliates, it passed in 1787 to Count Andrea Passalacqua who transformed it into a sumptuous dwelling, with decorations by Giocondo Albertolli and frescoes by Andrea Appiani. Vincenzo Bellini stayed there between 1829 and 1833, finding inspiration for Norma, La strangere and La sleepambula.

private property, visible only from the outside

Ville storiche sul lago

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry · Viewpoint

Villa Le Rose and Villa Salterio Erker, together with Villa Passalacqua, have hosted personalities and artists; they are private property and are admired only from the outside, along the theme paths of the village.

Crotti di Moltrasio

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry

Wine cellars dug in the local stone called "moltrasina" which are ideal for preserving wine; the typical dish of the village are the missoltini served with polenta.
