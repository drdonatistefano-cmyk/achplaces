What to see in Gualtieri

3 places to visit in Gualtieri, in the province of Reggio Emilia (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza Bentivoglio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the most beautiful squares in Italy, designed by Giovan Battista Aleotti as the courtyard of honour of the palace, with the church of Santa Maria della Snow and the civic tower.

  • Where Piazza Bentivoglio, Gualtieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Palazzo Bentivoglio e Teatro Sociale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The palace wanted by Prince Ippolito Bentivoglio and built by Aleotti together with the square, with the noble halls and the theatre.

Opens only on special occasions (for example FAI Days): check before going

  • Where Piazza Bentivoglio 26, Gualtieri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo Antonio Ligabue

Museums · Modern art

In the Hall of Jupiter of Palazzo Bentivoglio, documents, paintings, sculptures and engravings of the Naïf painter who lived in Gualtieri, including a Gorilla and a self-portrait.

  • Where Piazza Bentivoglio 36, Gualtieri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitemilia.com · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
