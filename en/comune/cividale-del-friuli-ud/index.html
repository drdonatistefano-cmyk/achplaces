What to see in Cividale del Friuli

First Lombard Dukedom in Italy and stop of the UNESCO site "I Longobardi" in Italy: the Temple of Santa Maria in Valle, the Altar of Ratchis in the Christian Museum and the Devil's Bridge on Natisone, among the Eastern Hills of Friuli.

10 places to visit in Cividale del Friuli, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Tempietto Longobardo e Monastero di Santa Maria in Valle

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Palatine chapel of the Longobard court of the second half of the eighth century, then church of the Benedictine women's monastery: inside six female figures in stucco to high relief little bigger than the real, frescoes, floor in opus sectile and a wooden choir of the fourteenth century.

  • Where Via Monastero Maggiore 34, Cividale del Friuli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: longobardinitalia.it · italia.it

Museo Cristiano e Tesoro del Duomo

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

It preserves the Altar of Ratchis, a Lombard masterpiece with bas-reliefs once painted, the Baptistery of Callisto with octagonal plan with seven arches on marble columns, and the treasure of goldsmiths, codes and liturgical furnishings.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Cividale del Friuli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it

Museo Archeologico Nazionale

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

In the Palazzo dei Provveditori Veneti designed by Andrea Palladio: Roman collections, early Christians, early medievals, Romanesques and Gothics, with lapidary, epigraphic and music sections and a collection of 56 Lombard gold coins.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Cividale del Friuli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it · cultura.gov.it

Duomo di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The 14th-century cathedral on the square of the ancient Roman forum; from the right aisle you enter the Christian Museum.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Cividale del Friuli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Ponte del Diavolo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Stone bridge started in 1442 on a natural boulder in Natisone's bed: 22,5 meters high and 48 meters long, with two arches of 22 and 19 meters; destroyed in 1917 during the retreat of Caporetto, it was rebuilt in the same form in 1918. The legend wants it built with the help of the devil.

  • Where Cividale del Friuli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghibellifvg.it · italia.it

Ipogeo celtico

History and architecture · Archaeology

Underground environments dug into the rock along the Natisone, on several levels, with niches, benches and two carved faces; preroman, perhaps Celtic, of uncertain function: tomb, prison or cistern.

  • Where Via Monastero Maggiore 2, Cividale del Friuli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it · italia.it
