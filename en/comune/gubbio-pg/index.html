What to see in Gubbio

The stone city is on the side of Mount Ingino: Piazza Grande pendant with the Palazzo dei Consoli and the Tavole Eugubine, the Palazzo Ducale dei Montefeltro, the Roman theatre and, at the top of the mountain, the Basilica of Sant'Ubaldo, the destination of the Corsa dei Ceri.

12 places to visit in Gubbio, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo dei Consoli e Museo Civico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built between 1332 and 1349 on a project by Angelo da Orvieto with the Gattapone, it dominates Piazza Grande; from the ground floor to the last it houses the archaeological museum, where the Eugubine Tables in Umbrian language (III-I century BC) and the art gallery are exhibited.

  • Where Piazza Grande, Gubbio
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Basilica di Sant'Ubaldo e Monte Ingino

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the summit of Monte Ingino, 850 meters, reachable on foot or by cable car: it guards the urn with the remains of the patron and is the finish line of the Corsa dei Ceri on May 15th; enlarged in the sixteenth century with monastery and frescoed cloister, it has five naves and windows on the life of the saint.

  • Where Via Sant'Ubaldo, Gubbio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Parco archeologico del Teatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Theatre completed around 20 BC by the magistrate Gneo Satrio Rufo, capable of about 6,000 spectators, in the late-republish district of Guastuglia; the lower order of arches and some arches of the upper gallery remain standing, with the Antiquarium.

  • Where Via del Teatro Romano 1, Gubbio
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
