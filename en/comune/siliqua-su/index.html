What to see in Siliqua

Country of about 4,000 inhabitants in the valley of Cixerri, mentioned for the first time in 1272, dominated by the castle of Acquafredda, the fortress of Count Ugolino della Gherardesca remembered by Dante, on the Andesitic dome declared natural monument in 1993; in the territory also the WWF oasis of Monte Arcosu.

3 places to visit in Siliqua, in the province of Sud Sardegna (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Acquafredda

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Fortress already mentioned in a papal bull of 1215, from 1257 of Count Ugolino della Gherardesca, lord of Cagliari, immortalized by Dante; after his death (1288) passed to Pisa, in 1324-26 to the Aragonese and feudal families, until the ransom of Vittorio Amedeo III of Savoy in 1785. It climbs on three levels along the volcanic hill: the door with the surviving central tower, the village with accommodations, stables and cisterns, the cistern tower (the name comes from a spring in the rock) and the mast with drawbridge, underground cistern and terrace with Guelph merlons; in the tower, according to tradition, Vanni Gubetta was imprisoned, accomplice of the betrayal of Ugolino. Visits every day 10-18, without reservation.

  • Where Via Giovanni Pascoli / SS 293, Siliqua
  • Entry €5, reduced €3,50; guided tour €7
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · cultura.gov.it

Domo andesitico di Acquafredda

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Colle di origine vulcanica at about 256 meters, 4 km from Siliqua, declared a Natural Monument in 1993 for the geological, landscape and flora-faunistic interest: at the foot a forest of eucalyptus and pines with picnic area, suitable for trekking and birdwatching, where they nest gheppio, buzzard and hawk cricket.

  • Where SS 293, Siliqua
  • Entry Included in the castle ticket (to be checked)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Oasi WWF di Monte Arcosu

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

The largest reserve of the WWF in Italy, about 4,000 hectares of holm oak wood and cork where Sardinian deer and deer live; from the territory of Siliqua also the waterfall of Su Spistiddatroxiu and the paths towards Mount Lattias, among primary holm oaks, badgers and large oaks.

  • Where Territorio di Siliqua, verso Monte Arcosu
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it
