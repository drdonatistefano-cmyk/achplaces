What to see in Narni

Cities on the cliff above the gorges of the Black, the ancient Roman Narnia along the Flaminia: the Ponte di Augusto, the Rocca Albornoz, the Underground Narni under San Domenico and Piazza dei Priori.

9 places to visit in Narni, in the province of Terni (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Narni sotterranea

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Under the monastery of San Domenico, the underground path of aqueducts, cisterns, tunnels and crypts with the torture hall of the Court of the Inquisition; the excavation dates back to the Etruscan era.

  • Where Narni
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismonarni.it

Ponte di Augusto

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Bridge of the Via Flaminia of about 27 BC, near Narni Scalo sul Nera: of the four original arches remains standing the first, the largest; collapsed in part around the Mille, in the medieval documents was called "Ponte Rotto" .

  • Where Narni Scalo, Narni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Rocca Albornoz

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Pontifical fortress wanted by Cardinal Egidio Albornoz and completed in 1378, with a project probably attributed to Gattapone; it hosts an exhibition on daily life in the Middle Ages and from 2023 the SAM Street Art museum.

  • Where Narni
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Palazzo Eroli – Museo della città e del territorio

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the third floor Fraporta, home to the museum and the civic library: it preserves the Coronation of the Virgin by Domenico Ghirlandaio and the Annunciation of Benozzo Gozzoli, commissioned by Cardinal Bernardo Eroli, and an archaeological section.

  • Where Narni
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismonarni.it

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint
