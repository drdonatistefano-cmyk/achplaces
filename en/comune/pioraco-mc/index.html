What to see in Pioraco

Borgo dell'Alto Maceratese at the confluence between Potenza and Scarsito, with canals, washbasins and mill wheels: here you can produce paper from the middle of the thirteenth century, told by the Museum of Paper and Filigree, and a few steps from the center starts the path Li Vurgacci between waterfalls and pools.

Museo della carta e della filigrana

Museums · Themed

In the old town, it reconstructs the medieval paper mills that produced paper from rags: in Pioraco the paper is already made from the middle of the thirteenth century and it bears a watermark with the dragon as a quality mark.

Sentiero Li Vurgacci

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint
