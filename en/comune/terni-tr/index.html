What to see in Terni

The "city of steel" and Valentine's Day, in the basin of the Black: the Roman amphitheatre, the cultural center CAOS in a former factory and, a few kilometers away, the Marmore Falls and the archaeological area of Carsulae.

26 places to visit in Terni, in the province of Terni (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cascata delle Marmore

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Viewpoint

The Velino falls with three jumps for about 165 meters, the highest waterfall in Europe, created by the Romans to drain a swampy area; the paths touch the lower and upper Belvedere, the specola of Pius VI and the "balcony of the lovers."

  • Where Marmore, Terni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: turismo.comune.terni.it · italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Area archeologica di Carsulae

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The remains of the Roman town hall arose along the Via Flaminia (traced around 220 BC): forum, Gemini temples, well-preserved theatre and amphitheatre, spas, necropolis and the monumental arch of San Damiano.

  • Where Strada di Carsoli 8, Terni
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · turismo.comune.terni.it · umbriatourism.it
