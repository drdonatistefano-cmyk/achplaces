What to see in Rossiglione

Municipality of Stura Valley, north-west of Genoa, formed by the two cores of Rossiglione Superiore and Lower and included in the Park of Beigua: the canyon of the Val Gargassa, the Museo Passtempo sul Novecento italiano and the churches of the village.

4 places to visit in Rossiglione, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Rossiglione in the guide with map and filters

Canyon della Val Gargassa

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Gargassa stream has recorded conglomerates and brooks of the Oligocene (about 28 million years ago), forming canyons, ponds, spires and towers. Ring of 7 km, about 4 hours, difference in altitude 180 m, difficulty EE, from the sports field in Gargassino (SP for Tiglieto, about 3 km): you meet the ford under the tower "Muso del Gatto" , the Houses Vereira with traces of the pre-industrial production of glass, a sulphuric source and the "Balcony of the Lady" .

Path for experienced hikers with fords and exposed sections with cable; not recommended after heavy rains and storms

  • Where Campo sportivo, loc. Gargassino, Rossiglione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it

Sources: parcobeigua.it · fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it

Museo Passatempo

Local life and traditions · Motors · Paid entry

Itinerary in the memory and Italian costume of the twentieth century, the result of more than fifteen years of research by the collectors Guido Minetti and Sylvia Pizzorno: themed rooms with motorcycle workshop, '40s emporio, lounge, appliances, Olivetti machines, Vespa dealership and tribute to Guzzi, bicycles, toys, juke-box and pinball machines, many objects still working.

No fixed time: openings are communicated month by month or by phone (010 9239921)

  • Where Via Sciutto 3, Rossiglione
  • Entry €4, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museopassatempo.it

Sources: museopassatempo.it · fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it · cultura.gov.it · cittametropolitana.genova.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di N.S. Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish of the lower hamlet of Rossiglione; it preserves the processional case of the Transit of St. Joseph, built in 1866 by the Montecucco Brothers of Gavi.

  • Where Via Mazzini 4, Rossiglione
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it

Sources: fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it

Oratorio di San Sebastiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Oratory that is supposed to date back to the fifteenth century; inside is preserved a processional crucifix of the seventeenth century.
