What to see in Montale

Municipality between Pistoia and Prato born as a fortified village of the thirteenth century, destroyed by the Florentines in 1303; to see the Villa Castello Smilea, today municipal seat with the works of Jorio Vivarelli, the crypt of the abbey of San Salvatore in Agna, documented since 772, the parish church with frescoes by Luigi Sabatelli and, at the top, Tobbiana.

Villa Castello Smilea

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The name comes from ... ..sex milia... ..the six miles from Pistoia. Medieval fortress of the Panciatichi, in 1466 was removed from the Florentines for participation in the conspiracy against Piero di Cosimo de' Medici and then became villa-farm, preserving the appearance of a fortress: quadrangular building with two tall crenellated towers and sharp hanging arches, portal in squared basins and courtyard closed by two symmetrical buildings. Today it is the seat of the Municipality and houses the library, the municipal historical archive and the permanent exhibition of the works of the Montalese sculptor and graphic artist Jorio Vivarelli.

  • Where Via Martiri della Libertà 109, Montale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Abbazia di San Salvatore in Agna

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

On Via Cassia, at the mouth of the Agna stream, it is documented by 772 as a Longobard monastery with a function of hospice for travelers and pilgrims. Benedictine from 1028 to 1429, then the Canonici Regulari Lateranensi until the sixteenth century, was then deconsecrated and used as a cellar and for the breeding of silk bucs; restored almost entirely in the twenties of the twentieth century, preserves as the only original part the crypt of the X-XI century, with carved columns and valuable capitals.

  • Where La Badia, Montale
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa di San Giovanni Evangelista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish church of unknown foundation, renovated several times; inside the dome the frescoes of Luigi Sabatelli, interpreter of Florentine romantic classicism.

Piazza Matteotti e colle del castello

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

From the central square starts the visit of the village, which grew lower than the hill where at the beginning of the thirteenth century the fortified village arose; in 1303, after the victory of the Florentines over the Pistoiesis, the castle was conquered and destroyed and few traces remain.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Montale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Tobbiana e chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Tobbiana is the highest center of the municipality and starting point for excursions to the surrounding peaks; the church of San Michele Arcangelo, of the eighteenth century, offers a beautiful view over the territory.

  • Where Tobbiana, Montale
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
