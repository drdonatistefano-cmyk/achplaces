What to see in Arona

Orange Flag City of Lake Maggiore, home of San Carlo Borromeo, with the Sancarlone Colossus, the Rocca and a center of churches and porticos in front of Angera.

8 places to visit in Arona, in the province of Novara (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Arona in the guide with map and filters

Sancarlone – Statua di San Carlo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

The 35 meters high statue dedicated to San Carlo Borromeo, climbable to the head, on the square of the church of San Carlo.

  • Where Piazzale San Carlo, Arona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Rocca di Arona e Parco della Rocca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On a cliff a hundred meters high, founded by the Longobards around the Thousand and devastated by order of Napoleon; here was born Carlo Borromeo in 1538. The park, the Borromeo, has been reopened since 2011.

  • Where Arona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it · distrettolaghi.it

Collegiata di Santa Maria Nascente

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish church consecrated in 1488, with Romanesque bell tower, completed by Federico Borromeo in the seventeenth century: six paintings by Morazzone and a polyptych by Gaudenzio Ferrari in 1511.

  • Where Arona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Piazza del Popolo e Santa Maria di Loreto

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient market square with the arched arcades of the Podestà House and the church commissioned by Federico Borromeo in 1592, attributed to Pellegrino Tibaldi, with a staircase of Francesco Maria Richini.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Arona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Chiesa dei Santi Martiri

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Of Romanesque origins with Baroque façade: it preserves the relics of the patrons and the fifteenth-century altarpiece of the Bergognone.

  • Where Piazza San Graziano, Arona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

In the 19th-century covered market: finds from the Neolithic to the post-Renaissance age, including the wheel of the Lagoni di Mercurago, Golasecca's sets and the Celtic necropolis of Dormelletto.

  • Where Piazza San Graziano, Arona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Lungolago

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The walk overlooking the Fortress of Angera, on the Lombard shore.

  • Where Lungolago, Arona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: touringclub.it

Parco naturale dei Lagoni di Mercurago

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Protected area since 1980 in the hamlet of Mercurago, among peat bogs, pastures and woods; the Lagoni are part of the UNESCO heritage site as a prehistoric palafittic site of the Alps

  • Where Via Gattico 6, Mercurago, Arona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.touringclub.it

Sources: parcoticinolagomaggiore.com · touringclub.it
