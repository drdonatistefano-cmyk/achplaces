What to see in Buseto Palizzolo

Trapanese's agricultural municipality, between rural bays and countryside towards Segesta, with the Scorace Wood (Arcudaci), 750 hectares of greenery with views of the Gulf of Castellammare, and the Municipal Villa with the sculptures of Salvatore Galante.

Bosco di Scorace (Bosco d'Arcudaci)

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

About 750 hectares once almost all corkscrew, cut down largely after the Second World War and reforested by the Forest Corps with conifers and eucalyptuses; today pines, cypresses, holm oaks and aromatic herbs, with rabbits, hares and istrices. It has parking lots, areas equipped with tables, cooking points and games for children and paths up to a hilly lake; from a hill of 642 meters you can see Segesta with theatre and temple, Monte Inici, Monte Sparagio and the Gulf of Castellammare.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Villa Comunale

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Public garden in the center, next to the Town Hall and near the Mother Church, designed in 1974 by the architect Sebastiano Maltese and completed in 1984; among pines, cedars, cypresses, willows and oleanders are placed sculptures, many of the local artist Salvatore Galante. It is told the tree of witches, the cypresses in love and the avenue of lovers.

  • Where Via Trapani, Buseto Palizzolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.busetopalizzolo.tp.it
