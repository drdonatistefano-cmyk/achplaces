What to see in Ascea

The town of Cilento overlooking the Tyrrhenian Sea at the mouth of the Alento, where the ruins of Elea-Velia rise, founded around 540 BC by the Focei and home of the philosophical school of Parmenide and Zenone; along the coast the Path of the Lovers climbs from the cliff of Ascea Marina to the Telegrafo Tower; the beaches of Torre del Telegrafo, Piana di Velia and Marina di Ascea are Blue Flag 2026.

Area archeologica di Velia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Elea was founded around 540 BC by exiles of Focea and was the seat of the Eleatic school of Parmenide and Zenone; the ruins, between Mediterranean maquis and olive groves, include the walls of about 5 km of the 6th century BC, Porta Marina Sud, the Porta Rosa (one of the oldest arches in the entire west), the Adriatic thermal baths with the mosaic of animals and marine monsters, the agorò today read as sanctuary of Asclepius and the Augustan cryptopic. On the acropolis the Roman theatre, an Ionian temple, the Angevin tower, the Palatine chapel and the church of Santa Maria, antiquarium sites. Open every day 8:30-19:30, last entry 18:30.

From 21 September 2026 to 28 March 2027 the acropolis closes before the site (until 1 January at 16:00, then gradually later); after that time you visit only the lower city.

  • Where Piazzale Amedeo Maiuri, Ascea
  • Entry €15 (March-November), €10 (December-February), reduced 18-25 years €2; valid 3 days also for Paestum and its museum
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website parchipaestumvelia.cultura.gov.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: parchipaestumvelia.cultura.gov.it · cultura.gov.it · cilentoediano.it · touringclub.it

Sentiero degli Innamorati

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A path of about 2 km, with 140 m of difference in altitude, which climbs from the cliff of Ascea Marina to the remains of the Telegrafo Tower through the Mediterranean maquis; requalified by the Park Authority with the Municipality, with 12 walkways accessing the beaches to protect the dunal flora, a bike path and 8 wooden platforms. The look ranges from Capo Palinuro to the sandy coast of Ascea.

  • Where Scogliera di Ascea Marina, Ascea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: touringclub.it · cultura.gov.it

Spiagge di Ascea (Torre del Telegrafo, Piana di Velia, Marina di Ascea)

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The sandy coast of Ascea, which from the Path of the Lovers can be seen to Capo Palinuro, has three rewarded stretches with the Blue Flag 2026: Torre del Telegrafo, Piana di Velia and Marina di Ascea. As part of the redevelopment of the Park Authority with the Municipality, 12 walkways have been built to access the beaches, which protect the dunal flora, along with a bike path and wooden platforms.

  • Where Marina di Ascea, Ascea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: bandierablu.org · touringclub.it · cultura.gov.it
