What to see in Serravalle di Chienti

Mountain town at the Chienti springs, on the Colfiorito plateau on the border with Umbria: small churches scattered among the pastures, the Botte dei Varano and a museum of fossils of the Pleistocene.

6 places to visit in Serravalle di Chienti, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario della Madonna del Sasso

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Sanctuary of the XIV-XV centuries on a rocky spur above the sources of the Menotre, in the hamlet of San Martino, reachable only on foot; inside votive frescoes from the XV to the XVII century, including a Madonna of Loreto and a Saint Christopher attributed to Paolo di Visso.

  • Where San Martino, Serravalle di Chienti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sources: italia.it · eventi.turismo.marche.it

Chiesa della Madonna del Piano

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

On the Colfiorito plateau, along the road from Plestia to San Martino, a sanctuary built in the sixteenth century instead of a small crossroads kiosk; damaged by the earthquake of 1997, it was restored.

  • Where Madonna del Piano, Serravalle di Chienti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it

Chiesa di Santa Croce in Percanestro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the 10th century in the southwestern part of the Colfiorito plateau, towards Visso.

  • Where Percanestro, Serravalle di Chienti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it

Piano della Minutella, Monte Corneto e Monte Scalette

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Panoramic area of woods, pastures and springs used to water the cattle, with trails towards the Scurosa Valley and Sefro; the Colfiorito Plans, about 800 meters away, color in spring.

  • Where Serravalle di Chienti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it

Botte dei Varano

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The artificial channel was dug by Giulio Cesare da Varano to drain Lake Plestino on the plateau of Colfiorito; next runs a second channel with travertine walls.

  • Where Serravalle di Chienti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it

Museums · Science and university
