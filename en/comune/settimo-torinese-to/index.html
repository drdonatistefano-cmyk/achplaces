What to see in Settimo Torinese

Cities at the gates of Turin in the Parco del Po Piemontese: the Freidano ecomuseum in the New Mill and the medieval tower of the historic centre.

4 places to visit in Settimo Torinese, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ecomuseo del Freidano – Mulino Nuovo

Museums · Themed

In the nineteenth-century mill complex of the Mulino Nuovo, cultural space since 1996: the ethnographic museum in the silos recreates environments and work of the late nineteenth century, with a space for temporary exhibitions.

  • Where Settimo Torinese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismotorino.org

Torre medievale

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

13th century tower, reopened in 2024 with the exhibition "Nel signo dell'acqua" on five floors, dedicated to the transformations of the city related to the watercourses.

  • Where Centro storico, Settimo Torinese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismotorino.org

Cappella di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Chapel of the old town, at the foot of the tower.
