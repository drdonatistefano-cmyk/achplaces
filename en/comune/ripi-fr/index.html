What to see in Ripi

The hilly village of the Ciociaria between vineyards and olive groves, about 100 km from Rome, which preserves the design of the medieval castrum with the doors Santa Croce and Sant'Angelo; it was the site of one of the first oil discoveries in Italy, told by the Energy Museum.

Museo dell'Energia

Museums · Science and university

Opened on 3 June 2009 in the former slaughterhouse and in the ancient communal wash-house, it combines didactic laboratory, geological and scientific museum with multimedia installations and interactive models on the energy of wind, water and oil, born from the oil wells of Ripi, among the first deposits discovered in Italy. The visit lasts about an hour (up to three for schools and groups) and can be completed with the oil field in Petroglie, a few kilometers away. Reservation required.

Centro storico, porte e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The centre preserves the medieval castrum, with the two ancient gates of Santa Croce and Sant'Angelo and the remains of the castle walls around which the village grew; there are Palazzo Galloni, formerly of the Colonna, and Palazzo Candia.

  • Where Centro storico, Ripi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Chiesa arcipretale del Santissimo Salvatore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1743 on a previous church, it has a unique nave with side chapels, a mosaic of St. George, patron of the village, above the portal and inside a large canvas of the Most Holy Savior. On April 23 the Palio di San Giorgio animates the village with traditional games such as the wheelbarrow race, the cutting of logs and the shooting of the rope.

  • Where Centro storico, Ripi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Santuario di Santa Maria Succurre Miseris

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

On the Colle Santa Maria: legend tells that a hermit, trying to sculpt the face of the Lord in an olive tree trunk, saw that of Our Lady appear. Little remains of the hermitage and the church, but the pilgrims still come to worship the miraculous statue.

  • Where Colle Santa Maria, Ripi
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
