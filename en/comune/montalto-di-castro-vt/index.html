What to see in Montalto di Castro

3 places to visit in Montalto di Castro, in the province of Viterbo (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Laghetto del Pellicone e oasi WWF

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint
