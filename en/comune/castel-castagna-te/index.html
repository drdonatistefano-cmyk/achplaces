What to see in Castel Castagna

Borgo della Valle Siciliana, the ancient Castrum Castanee fortified in the late Middle Ages: in the highest part of the village, on the ancient fortification, stands the church of San Pietro Martire, while isolated in the valley of the Mavone is Santa Maria di Ronzano, a Romanesque church of the twelfth century among the most original medieval monuments of Abruzzo, with frescoes of 1181.

Chiesa di Santa Maria di Ronzano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

Romanesque church built around 1170 as part of an important Benedictine complex, whose monastery was destroyed by a fire in 1183 and never rebuilt (signs are still visible on the pillars). It recalls the Romanesque Apulian of San Nicola di Bari, with the three apses enclosed in a straight wall and the façade in brick and white stone with circular window facing the Gran Sasso; the interior with three naves has cruciform pillars and wooden trusses. The frescoes of 1181 in the central apse show the blessing Christ in almond supported by angels, saints and scenes of the Old and New Testaments, including the Annunciation; it preserves a staurotheque with a fragment of the Cross.

Chiesa di San Pietro Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

It rises in the highest part of the village, on the ancient fortification of the village, inside the walls. Attested as early as the 14th century, it owes its current appearance to an important restoration of the 16th century: two-side roof, bell tower with square base next to the facade and portal in Renaissance stone with flat architrave, surmounted by a bezel decorated with rosettes and the ray monogram of San Bernardino da Siena. In 2025 the project to repair earthquake damage 2016 was approved.
