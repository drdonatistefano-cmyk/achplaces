What to see in Orbetello

City on the lagoon between the tomboli of Giannella and Feniglia, already capital of the Spanish State of the Presidians: bastions and Spanish gates, the Mill that emerges from the water, the oasis of flamingos and, in Ansedonia, the Roman colony of Cosa.

11 places to visit in Orbetello, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Mulino Spagnolo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The only survivor of the nine mills erected by the Sienese around 1400 in the lagoon, moved by the currents; the Spaniards renovated them adding the sail blades, hence the name.

  • Where Via della Diga 16, Orbetello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com

Laguna di Orbetello – Oasi WWF

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

The lagoon between two tomboli, historical oasis of the WWF in Tuscany, where you can see numerous species of birds, including flamingos.

  • Where Orbetello
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Museo Archeologico Nazionale e città antica di Cosa

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Latin colony founded by the Romans in 273 BC on the headland of Ansedonia: walls of over a kilometer with nineteen towers and three gates, the forum and, on the acropolis, the Capitolium and the temple of Mater Matuta; the museum, born with the American Academy in Rome, exhibits the finds of the colony, the port and the necropolis.

  • Where Via delle Ginestre 70, Ansedonia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it
