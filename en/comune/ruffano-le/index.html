What to see in Ruffano

Country of the Serre Salentine between olive groves and farmhouses, about 10 km from the sea of Ugento, known for its artisan ceramics. The historic centre, with noble houses and courtyard houses, revolves around Piazza del Popolo with the Brancaccio Palace and the Matrice Church; in the hamlet Torrepaduli the Sanctuary of San Rocco and the night of the pinch between 15 and 16 August.

Palazzo Brancaccio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

In 1625, built as a noble residence on the remains of a medieval fortress, it is the heart of the country: the arches of its splendid loggia connect it to the Matrix Church, next to which it rises.

Centro storico e Palazzo Licci

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Historic center well preserved, with noble residences, ancient houses at court and places of worship. Among the noble palaces the most spectacular is Palazzo Licci in Piazza IV Novembre, with a remarkable bifora. Here is also the parish of Our Lady of Good Counsel, the oldest in the country, built in the second half of the nineteenth century on the remains of the Byzantine church of San Foca.

  • Where Piazza IV Novembre, Ruffano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · interregaismart.regione.puglia.it

Museo della Ceramica

Museums · Archaeological

Small museum next to the Matrice Church: in the underground environments, it exhibits the most significant finds of local ceramics dating from the 13th to the 18th century, recovered in the excavations of 1996 under the sacristy, among the remains of medieval houses. Tel. +39 329 437 8774.

  • Where Via Cesare Battisti, Ruffano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Museo degli arredi sacri

Museums · Themed

In front of the Matrix Church, it exhibits liturgical silvers of the XVI-XIX centuries (calics, pissides, monstrances) and vestments of the XVIII-XIX centuries, paliotti and pivials in precious fabrics embroidered in gold thread, coming from the main church of the country. Tel. +39 329 437 8774.

Museums · Themed

A small museum set up by the Association of Seniors. Pasanisi is located in the former church of Our Lady of Graces (1634), which has been restored and also houses a cultural centre: it exhibits ancient liturgical objects, devotional medals and artistic prints. Pinacoteca comunale

Museums · Modern art

Born from the project "Cento artisti per Ruffano" (1997-1999), it conserves over 300 works of contemporary art donated, including paintings, sculptures and graphics by artists such as Goliardo Padova, Luigi Veronesi, Ibrahim Kodra, Walter Lazzaro, Giovanni Conservo and Salvatore Fiume, catalogued by Carlo Franza; since 2008 it is in the former Convent of the Capuchins and hosts thematic exhibitions. Winter time: Mon, Mar, Thu 15.30-19; Wed and Fri 9-12.30 and 15.30-19; Saturday closed. Tel. 0833 1821254.

Santuario di San Rocco

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

In Torrepaduli, rebuilt in 1882 preserving the main altar in stone of 1738 with the canvas of the saint of Giovanni Grassi di Lecce (1851); mosaic floor by Michele Peluso di Tricase (1891), Latin cross plan with vaults on the edge of the Lecce and, in the transept, two nineteenth-century altars dedicated to Saint Lucia and the Baptism of Christ. Recognized as Church-Sanctuary in 1952, it is the destination of the night of San Rocco between 15 and 16 August, when the churchyard is danced the pinch until dawn.

  • Where Largo San Rocco, Torrepaduli (Ruffano)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ruffano.le.it · interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it

Cripta del Crocifisso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Also known as the SS. Crucifix or Santa Costantina, is a natural cavity transformed into a place of worship by the Byzantines in the 11th century, in the open countryside in Manfio district, on the border between Casarano, Ruffano and Taurisano: frescoed walls, paleolithic graffiti and fossils of neolithic communities; near the ruins of a cenobe of the Basilian monks and then of the olivetans. Access with stairs.

  • Where Contrada Manfio, Ruffano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ruffano.le.it · italia.it
