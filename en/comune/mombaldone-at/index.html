What to see in Mombaldone

The most beautiful village in Italy of the Langa Astigiana sulla Bormida, still protected by the medieval walls among the calanchi.

5 places to visit in Mombaldone, in the province of Asti (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Mombaldone in the guide with map and filters

Mura e porta del ricetto

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The strong walls defend the village on the side of the valley; in via Cervetti the ancient door to sharp arch, intact in medieval form, introduces to the old village.

  • Where Via Cervetti, Mombaldone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Resti del castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the 13th to the 14th century, partly demolished in 1637, with a square tower in the center; the fief passed to the Del Carretto in 1209.

  • Where Mombaldone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Oratorio dei Santi Fabiano e Sebastiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built on the castle moat in 1764 according to design by Pietro Barozzi, with decorations from 1883; venue of exhibitions and concerts.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Mombaldone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Parrocchiale di San Nicola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

By Giovanni Matteo Zucchi, 1790 with hexagonal plan: 17th century paintings, including works by Giovanni Monevi, and organ Collino of 1885.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Mombaldone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

La Portiola

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Stone cave vaulted in Via Roma that connected the main road to the descent towards the river.

  • Where Via Roma, Mombaldone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it
