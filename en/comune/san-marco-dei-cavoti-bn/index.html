What to see in San Marco dei Cavoti

Medieval village of Beneventano with doors of the fourteenth century, the circular Torre dei Provenzali and Piazza Risorgimento; in the historic center is the Museum of tower clocks, with fifty machines restored by the master watchmaker Salvatore Ricci, and along Via Roma the shops of the crunchy torrone.

Museo degli orologi da torre

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Officially inaugurated on 17 November 2000, it collects fifty old tower clock machines, some coming from abroad, which the master watchmaker Salvatore Ricci found and brought back into operation in the course of life; the collection was then purchased by the National Research Council. Since 2007 it has been permanently located in the restored rooms of a nineteenth-century palace in the historic center, between Via Rovagnera and Muro Nuovo, which in the mid-Twentieth century had been an oil mill; the students of the technical institute \Medi-Livatino Â .

Centro storico, porte e Torre dei Provenzali

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village preserves three doors of the fourteenth century (Porta Grande on Via Roma, flanked by a crenellated tower (merli added at the beginning of the twentieth century), Porta di Rosa at the Ponte Fontanella and Porta Palazzo incorporated in the Palazzo Marchesale) and remains of the walls towards Largo San Rocco. On top of the medieval center the Torre dei Provenzali, circular, of the fourteenth century, was jailed and from the early nineteenth century it is bell tower of the church of San Marco Evangelista, rebuilt in 1975 after the earthquake of 1962; front opens the viewpoint Mons. Michele Marinella.

  • Where Piazza Risorgimento e Via Roma, San Marco dei Cavoti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sanmarcodeicavoti.bn.it

Sources: comune.sanmarcodeicavoti.bn.it

Piazza Risorgimento e i palazzi signorili

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Square with square plan built at the end of the eighteenth century, with the central fountain of 1909 by engineer Giovanni Paolucci; Palazzo Jelardi, built in 1850, is overlooking it by the Jesuit Giovan Battista Iazeolla and restored between 2012 and 2016, with the Museum of Advertising in the attic, and Palazzo Colarusso, home of the Town Hall since 2015. Not far away, between Via and Largo Vicidomini, stands Palazzo Zurlo, former marquisal palace of the Cavaniglia with terraces and turret.

  • Where Piazza Risorgimento, San Marco dei Cavoti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sanmarcodeicavoti.bn.it

Sources: comune.sanmarcodeicavoti.bn.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Via Roma e il torrone croccantino

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry

Via Roma, traced in the mid-eighteenth century, is known for its workshops of torronari. The crispy name of the village is made with honey, sugar, egg white, vanilla and almonds, heated and processed in the torroneera, then cut and covered with dark chocolate; numerous Beneventano companies produce it according to traditional rules.

  • Where Via Roma, San Marco dei Cavoti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sanmarcodeicavoti.bn.it

Cascata Ripa sul torrente Tammarecchia

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Waterfall on the river Tammarecchia, reachable by a path made in December 2020; on the same stream stands one of the two Jelardi Mills, of the seventeenth century.
