What to see in Levico Terme

Valsugana's thermal resort, the European aristocracy's destination between the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, with arsenal-ferruginous waters of Levico and Vetriolo and the Habsburg Park, the largest historical garden in the province; the lake, Blue Flag, is just a few minutes walk from the centre, while on the hills are the Austro-Hungarian fort of the Benne, the frescoed church of San Biagio and the ruins of Castel Selva.

Parco degli Asburgo (Parco delle Terme)

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest historical garden in the province, over 13 hectares designed by the German landscaper Georg Ziehl and inaugurated in the early twentieth century as a thermal garden: avenues tree-lined with redwoods, cedars, beech trees, magnolias and species from various parts of the world, the Grand Hotel Imperiale and the liberty Villa Paradiso, already home to the spa society. Open every day; it hosts Ortinparco and the Christmas market.

  • Where Viale Vittorio Emanuele, Levico Terme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · touringclub.it · italia.it

Terme di Levico

Local life and traditions · Spas

The source, known since the Middle Ages in the mining excavations of Vetriolo, was brought to the village in the nineteenth century: the water was born on the Lagorai near the Panarotta and is enriched with iron, sulphates, copper and arsenic (Forte Water and Weak Water). Baths, mud and inhalations on medical prescription, also in agreement with the Health Service; wellness treatments on reservation. Season 2027: spa area from April 17th to November 27th, wellness area until December 31st.

Forte Colle delle Benne (Forte San Biagio)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built by the Austro-Hungarians in 1880-1882, twin of the fort of Tenna, 660 meters on the hill south of the lake: Porphyd and granite housematte with moat, four levels and central corridor, originally armed with four 12 cm cannons and two obici in revolving armoured domes. Disarmed in the spring of 1915, it was only an observatory and a warehouse in war; partly restored for the centenary of the Great War. From the thermal baths you go up via San Biagio and then a dirt road; guided tours in the program

Chiesetta di San Biagio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

On the hill of the same name, 571 meters, about 30 minutes from the center, at an ancient castle: built in the fourteenth century, enlarged in 1506, with a single nave with protyrus and polygonal apse. The oldest nave contains fourteenth-century frescoes by artists of the Venetian, pre-Jotan and Giotto school; the apse is frescoed by a Renaissance Friulian painter from the school of Domenico da Tolmezzo. Visits in the summer on scheduled days and times.

Ruderi di Castel Selva

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Castle built in the 8th century, probably on prehistoric structures, as a refuge during the barbarian invasions; rebuilt in the 4th-Fifth century, it was residence of the bishops of Trento and in 1799 passed to the Municipality of Levico. Remain the ruins, reachable by an easy path from the village of Selva.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Centro storico e chiesa del Santissimo Redentore

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Centro raccolto, percorribile on foot, with old shops, cafés and palaces of the thermal age; the parish church of the Santissimo Redentore is a neo-Romanesque building of the late nineteenth century, with a large statue of the Redeemer in the facade, the major altar and nineteenth century wooden works.

Vetriolo e Panarotta

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Vetriolo, a spa resort at altitude, is the starting point for excursions to Panarotta, Monte Fravort and the Lagorai chain; in winter the Panarotta offers alpine skiing, cross-country skiing, snowshoeing and ski touring.
