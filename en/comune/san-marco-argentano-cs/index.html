What to see in San Marco Argentano

Norman center overlooking the valley of the Fullone, in the Pollino National Park, which around the year Mille became the seat of Roberto il Guiscardo: it remains the Norman Tower, the Cathedral of St. Nicholas with the crypt and, just outside, the Abbey of Matina.

9 places to visit in San Marco Argentano, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Torre Normanna (Torre di Drogone)

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Added to the castle when, around the year 1000, the village became the seat of Robert Guiscard, who enlarged and fortified it. The cone-shaped tower, called "motta" is spread over five floors called rooms: Granals (underground), Prisons, Arms (entrance), Audiences and Prince.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, ingresso da Via Roberto il Guiscardo, San Marco Argentano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.sanmarcoargentano.cs.it

Cattedrale di San Nicola

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Founded by the Normans in the 11th century, according to Italia.it on the ruins of a temple dedicated to Poseidon; integrated in baroque forms between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, damaged by the earthquakes of 1905 and 1908 and then renovated. In the aisle the chapel of Our Lady of the Rosary.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, San Marco Argentano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sanmarcoargentano.cs.it · italia.it

Cripta normanna

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Under the cathedral, reachable by two internal stairs and by an external door; re-arranged in 1935 at the behest of Mgr. Demetrius Moscati, with the removal of the burials. It preserves a chapel dedicated to the Blessed Mgr. Castrillo and a half bust of Saint Francis of Paola; there is the miracle of the "Scivolenta" of 1855.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, San Marco Argentano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sanmarcoargentano.cs.it · italia.it

Abbazia della Matina

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

4 km from the village near the river Fullone, the abbey church was dedicated to Santa Maria on March 3, 1065; Benedictine until 1221, then Cistercian, from the fifteenth century date in commenda and started to decline. The Cistercian phase remains a parlour, scriptorium, staircase to the upper floors and chapter hall.

  • Where Località Matina, San Marco Argentano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sanmarcoargentano.cs.it · italia.it

Museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters

Opened on May 25, 2006 in the church of San Giovanni, no longer open to worship: on the ground floor silver sacred of the seventeenth-seventh century, on the upper floor vestments and paintings from the sixteenth to the eighteenth century. There is a 13th century reliquary cross in embossed silver and the silver bust of St. Nicholas commissioned in 1765 by Bishop Nicola Brescia, perhaps of the workshop of Philip Del Giudice.

  • Where Piazza Vincenzo Selvaggi, San Marco Argentano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Fontana di Sikelgaita

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Medieval fountain in front of the church of San Marco Evangelista, dedicated to the wife of Guiscardo, with three caryatids called Sikelgaita, Virtue and Smorfiosa; restored over time, preserves the water path and part of the sculptures.

  • Where Piazza Santo Marco, San Marco Argentano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.sanmarcoargentano.cs.it

Complesso della Riforma (Sant'Antonio)

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · Free entry

Among the oldest examples of Franciscan architecture in Calabria: the bell tower, the entrance and the wooden choir remain. In 1429-1430 St. Francis of Paola stayed there, as he remembers a letter dated A.D. 1429.

  • Where Piazza Riforma, San Marco Argentano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sanmarcoargentano.cs.it · italia.it

Chiesetta della Benedetta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the Villa Comunale Generale Dalla Chiesa, built in 1762 above a small cave and restored in 1929; it houses a nineteenth-century painting by Saint Francis of Assisi and has a small olive grove and a chestnut tree behind it.

  • Where Villa comunale Generale Dalla Chiesa, San Marco Argentano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sanmarcoargentano.cs.it · italia.it

Museo civico «Mario Morelli»

Museums · Modern art

Born from the free donation to the city of the personal collection of Professor Mario Morelli, who chose the birthplace of his master Aleandro Curatolo: paintings and valuable objects.
