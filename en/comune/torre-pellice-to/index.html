What to see in Torre Pellice

The historical centre of the Valdesi in Val Pellice, which takes its name from a year-old tower Mille: the Waldensian quarter along Via Beckwith with Temple, House Valdese and Museum, the church of San Martino with the Priory of Mauritius and the contemporary Art Gallery Filippo Scroppo; it is a base for hikers and mountain enthusiasts.

Museo Storico Valdese

Museums · City history

In the building of the Valdese Cultural Centre in Beckwith Street, it documents the origins of the Waldensian people with a historical and ethnographic section; it is also an introduction to the Waldensian museums of the valleys (Rorà, Rodoretto, Ghigo di Prali, Balziglia, Pomaretto, Angrogna). The exhibition includes the permanent collection of Paolo Paschetto and the archaeological collection Ippolito.

  • Where Via Beckwith 3, Torre Pellice
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org · cultura.gov.it · comune.torrepellice.to.it

Quartiere valdese e Casa Valdese

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The neighborhood develops along Beckwith Street: the Valdese House (1889), built for the bicentenary of the Glorious Repatriation, houses in the synodal hall frescoed by Paolo Paschetto the annual Synod; around the pastoral house (1859-1861), the English-Saxon style houses of the professors, the Unionist House, the Waldensian College of 1832 (now high school) and the Pensionnat founded in 1844 as a female school.

  • Where Via Beckwith, Torre Pellice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.torrepellice.to.it · turismotorino.org

Tempio Valdese

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Designed in 1849 on the initiative of Beckwith and inaugurated on 17 June 1852, after the emancipation of the Valdese of 17 February 1848; in Romanesque style, with three naves with apse and matroneo, has the facade with two side bell towers. Every year the Synod of Waldensian and Methodist Churches opens.

  • Where Via Beckwith, Torre Pellice
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.torrepellice.to.it

Chiesa di San Martino e Priorato Mauriziano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Wanted by Carlo Alberto on the proposal of Bishop Charvaz, it was completed in 1844 on a project by Ernesto Melano: neoclassical facade, unique nave under a very high dome, fresco of San Maurizio and 442 rosettes of the painter Fea, pale of Ayres, Serrangeli and Biscarra and organ Hill of 1844. Next door, the Priory with cloister covered and two sundials.

  • Where Piazza San Martino, Torre Pellice
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.torrepellice.to.it · turismotorino.org

Civica Galleria d'arte contemporanea Filippo Scroppo

Museums · Modern art

Municipal Gallery dedicated to the artist and critic Filippo Scroppo, who curated contemporary art exhibitions from 1949 to 1991; preserves a civic collection of Italian contemporary art since the 1950s, presented with annual exhibitions, and hosts the festival "Una Torre d'Arte."

  • Where Via D'Azeglio 10, Torre Pellice
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.galleriascroppo.org

Sources: galleriascroppo.org · turismotorino.org

Museums · Themed

Sentiero naturalistico della Ghiandaia

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Tourist ring from Torre Pellice of 3 hours and 40 with 500 m of difference in altitude, for Ponte Blancio, Colletto, Rocca Budet, Pian di Arbre and Costa Lorens; a variant passes from the house of Giosuè Gianavello to Gianavella, today museum and guesthouse.

  • Where Piazza Cavour, Torre Pellice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.torrepellice.to.it · turismotorino.org

Sentiero Coppieri - Champas - Bars d'la Tajola

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Easy hike of about 2 hours and 640 m of difference in altitude from Torre Pellice (560 m) to 1200 m of the Bars d'la Tajola, passing through Coppieri, Champas and Ruà (a stone bathtub and community oven) and Castelluzzo.

the Bars d'la Tajola is reached with a narrow and dangerous passage equipped with handrail

  • Where Torre Pellice
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org
