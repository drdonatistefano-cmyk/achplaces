What to see in San Pietro in Cerro

Municipality of the Piacenza plain, about 24 km from Piacenza and close to the Verdi lands, known for the fifteenth-century castle of the Counts Barattieri, which houses the MIM contemporary art museum and copies of the warriors of Xi'an, and for the church of San Pietro of 1522.

Castello di San Pietro in Cerro

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Rebuilt between 1460 and 1491 by the jurist Bartolomeo Barattieri, ambassador of Piacenza to Pope Julius II, as the epigraph in the Courtyard of the Knights recalls; he remained at the Barattieri until 1993. Rectangular plant with entrance mast and two round towers on the north side, square courtyard with double order of arches; you can visit it entirely, with over 30 rooms furnished and decorated, including two Halls of Honor, the Hall of Arms and the Library with about 2,000 volumes from the early times of printing. In the basement there is the exhibition "China Millennial" with more than 40 copies of Xi'an terracotta warriors; the guided tour lasts about an hour and a quarter.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

MIM - Museum in Motion

Museums · Modern art

Museum of contemporary art set up after the restorations of 2001 in the attic and along the ancient walkway between the two towers of the castle: works especially of contemporary Italian and foreign artists, also Piacenza, between painting, sculpture and photography, exposed in rotation.

  • Where Via Roma 19, San Pietro in Cerro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitemilia.com

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the heart of the village, built in 1522 and decorated with paintings of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries.
