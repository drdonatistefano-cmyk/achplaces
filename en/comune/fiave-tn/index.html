What to see in Fiavè

Country of the Foreign Judicaries known for the villages on piles built between the late Neolithic and the Bronze Age on the banks of the ancient lake Carera, today protected peat bog: since 2011 they have been on the UNESCO World Heritage List on the serial site "Prehistoric palafittic sites of the Alpine arch." You can visit the archaeological area in the biotope, the Museum of Palafitte in Casa Carli and the Archeo Nature Park; around, Castel Campo, the rural villages, the Camerona cave and the Rio Ruzza waterfall.

Area palafitticola e biotopo Fiavé-Carera

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the basin of the ancient lake Carera, formed about 14,000 years ago behind a moraine barrier and then transformed into a peat bog, remain the poles of palafittic villages inhabited between the Neolithic and the Bronze Age. Discovered in the middle of the nineteenth century during the extraction of peat and systematically investigated from 1969 to 1976 and again since 1981, the site has been UNESCO World Heritage since 2011 on the serial site "Siti palafitticoli prehistorici dell'archo alpine" . It is inside the nature reserve Carera, biotope of provincial interest and Site of Community Importance with birds, amphibians and marsh flora; you can visit freely on catwalks with information panels.

  • Where Località Fiavé, a circa 30 minuti a piedi da via 3 Novembre; in auto SP 421 verso Passo del Ballino, parcheggio al Dos Gustinaci
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: cultura.trentino.it · visittrentino.info

Museo delle Palafitte di Fiavé

Museums · Archaeological

In the ancient Casa Carli, in the centre of the village, tells about the villages on Lake Carera and the researches of the twentieth century: ceramics, a vast collection of objects in wood, bronzes and ambre, food remains and working tools, videos and a section on the biotope. The big model of the village Fiavé 6 stands out with over seventy miniature characters. Ticket combined with the Archeo Nature Park; reductions with the Trentino Guest Card.

  • Where Via 3 Novembre 53, Fiavè
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Parco Archeo Natura

Museums · Archaeological

Near the museum, it reconstructs on a real scale the huts of a spafettical village of the Bronze Age, connected by walkways, with installations, thematic routes and open-air interpreting stations; it offers educational activities designed especially for families. Ticket combined with the museum.

  • Where Fiavè, vicino al Museo delle Palafitte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · cultura.trentino.it

Castel Campo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

It rises on a rocky spur between the Duina stream and the Ruzola river, just outside the village; cited in documents of the twelfth century, it was long linked to the family from Campo and was rebuilt after a fire in the fifteenth century.

Private property, open to the public only on specific occasions (Visit Trentino)

  • Where Località Curé, Fiavè
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Borghi rurali di Fiavé, Stumiaga, Favrio e Ballino

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The four villages have traditional country houses with aisles, ramps and stone portals; in Favrio a portal of 1539 is decorated with scissors and pin, symbols of tailors. Ballino, on the pass between Giudicarie and Tennese, has houses lined up along the road and around small stretches.

  • Where Fiavé, Stumiaga, Favrio, Ballino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Grotta Camerona

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Above Ballino, on the slope of Monte Misone, opens with an entrance about 12 meters high and about 20 meters wide; it is reached with a path and in some summer periods hosts events.

  • Where Sopra Ballino, Fiavè
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Cascata del Rio Ruzza

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers

Water jump embedded in the rock around the Passo del Ballino, reachable by a path through woods and meadows.
