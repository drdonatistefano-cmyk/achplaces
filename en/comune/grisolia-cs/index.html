What to see in Grisolia

Municipality of the Pollino National Park not far from Diamante and the Riviera dei Cedri, known for its medieval stone town of Grisolia, with its houses leaning against each other, and for the beaches where the rare sea lily blooms.

2 places to visit in Grisolia, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico di Grisolia

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village is built with houses leaning one to the other according to the technique of the "Support"; the historic center, in stone of Grisolia, is a network of narrow streets and narrow streets of typical medieval system.

  • Where Via Roma, Grisolia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fondoambiente.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it

Spiagge del giglio di mare

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Along the whole coast of Grisolia grows the sea lily (Pancratium maritimum), a rare plant with white flower with petals similar to orchids, which blooms from mid-July to the end of August; the seeds, enclosed in a very light corky mass, float and are brought by the seaside along the coast. The FAI invites to protect it with the native vegetation from erosion and the spread of the shores.

  • Where Via Sofia 2, Grisolia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page fondoambiente.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it
