What to see in Ariccia

Borgo dei Castelli Romani 26 km from Rome, redesigned between the Six and the eighteenth century for Pope Alessandro VII Chigi by Gian Lorenzo Bernini and collaborators: Piazza di Corte with Palazzo Chigi and the Collegiate Church of Santa Maria Assunta, the monumental bridge over the Vallericcia, the Chigi Park and the IGP Porchette Fraschetta.

8 places to visit in Ariccia, in the province of Rome (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo Chigi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Started by the Savelli in the second half of the sixteenth century, it was transformed by the Chigi between 1664 and 1672 into a sumptuous baroque residence designed by Gian Lorenzo Bernini and the young pupil Carlo Fontana, and retains largely original environments and furnishings; Prince Agostino Chigi Albani of the Rovere gave it to the Municipality on December 29, 1988. It houses a collection of paintings, sculptures and furnishings, especially of the seventeenth century; you can visit the Rooms of Cardinal Flavio Chigi, the noble floor and the Museum of the Roman Baroque, all without barriers. This is where Luchino Visconti shot many scenes of the "Cattopardo" (Cattopardo).

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Parco Chigi

Nature and landscape · City parks

Park of 28 hectares annexed to the palace, planted by Savelli and modified by the Chigi, which owned it from 1661 to 1988: romantic garden admired by travellers of the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, with oaks, holm oaks and ancient laurels and ruins among the trees. In October 2023 only a small area was opened next to the palace, waiting for a network of paths; VisitLazio indicates the opening Saturday, Sunday and holidays 10-13 and 15.30-19.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Collegiata di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In front of Palazzo Chigi on Piazza di Corte, with its large dome, it is one of the last masterpieces of Gian Lorenzo Bernini, who drew every detail, including furnishings.

  • Where Piazza di Corte, Ariccia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it · parchilazio.it

Piazza di Corte

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Heart of Ariccia, just after the bridge, with fountains bursting; Palazzo Chigi, the Collegiata and the historic Locanda Martorelli overlook it. It was born from the redesign of the city wanted by Pope Alexander VII Chigi between the end of the seventeenth and early eighteenth centuries, led by Bernini with Carlo Fontana, Mattia de Rossi, Luigi Bernini and Giovan Battista Contini, which also included Porta Romana, Porta Napoletana and the ...Stalloni , the ancient Chigi stables.

  • Where Piazza di Corte, Ariccia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Fraschette e porchetta di Ariccia IGP

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

In the traschette of the alleys you can taste the flavors of the Roman Castles, such as pairs and wild boar sausages with wine. The pig, IGP since 2011, is seasoned with rosemary, garlic and black pepper; the festival, born in 1950, is held on the first Sunday of September with the porchettari in costume and the traditional launch of sandwiches.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
