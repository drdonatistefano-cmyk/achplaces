What to see in Diano Castello

Medieval village on the hill behind Diano Marina, among the most beautiful villages in Italy, among terraced hills in Vermentino: caruggi, the Baroque parish of San Nicola, the Romanesque Santa Maria Assunta and the underground cisterns.

5 places to visit in Diano Castello, in the province of Imperia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Nicola di Bari

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Baroque parish built between 1699 and 1725 on Piazza Matteotti, with white marble altar and rare marble inserts.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Diano Castello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Piazza de Foresta e piazza Clavesana

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Two squares from the paved worked in the area of the ancient castle of the Marquises of Clavesana (XI century), of which traces remain, between caruggi and houses united by archivolti.

  • Where Diano Castello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
