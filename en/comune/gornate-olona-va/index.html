What to see in Gornate Olona

In the hamlet of Torba, at the end of the Olona Valley, stands the Monastery of Torba, well of the FAI: a late antique tower transformed into oratory by Benedictine nuns in the 8th century, with early medieval frescoes and the church of Santa Maria. Since 2011 he has been part, with Castelseprio, of the UNESCO site "I Longobardi" in Italy.

Torre del Monastero di Torba

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Rare example of late antique defensive architecture, born in the 5th century as a Roman military outpost downstream of Castelseprio castrum and then used by Goths, Byzantines and Longobards. In the 8th century the Benedictine nuns transformed it into oratory and mausoleum; on the second floor there are frescoes with Christ blessing and angels, the Virgin and Child, a group of saints and a theory of nuns in prayer, restored from 1978 to 2019 by Pinin Brambilla Barcilon. The excavations with the University of Padua found at the entrance a horse burial of the sixth century.

  • Where Via Stazione 2, Gornate Olona
  • Entry €8, reduced 6-18 years €4, free FAI membership; with guided tour €15, reduced €11, registered FAI €7
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website fondoambiente.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria di Torba

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Founded by the Benedictine nuns together with the monastery, which lived for over seven centuries until the abandonment in 1799; then the complex became a farm and the church was used as a barn and barn. Purchased in 1976 by Giulia Maria Mozzoni Crespi and donated to the FAI, it has been open to the public since 1986; in 2025 the church received a new altar designed by Mario Botta.

  • Where Via Stazione 2, Gornate Olona
  • Entry Included in the Monastery ticket (€8, reduced €4, FAI members free)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website fondoambiente.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Pista ciclopedonale della Valle Olona

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Cycle path along the Olona that from the Collegiate of Castiglione Olona arrives in about 3 km to the Monastery of Torba, in the direction of Gornate Olona, and continues towards Castelseprio; the complete route of Varese Turismo measures 7.5 km, easy, about 2 hours and a half. Along the valley you can observe pigs, water blackbirds, peaks and cormorants.

  • Where Valle Olona, tra Castiglione Olona e Torba
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: vareseturismo.it · italia.it
