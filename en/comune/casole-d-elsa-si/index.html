What to see in Casole d'Elsa

Oval walled village on the hills between Valdelsa and Val di Cecina, with two round towers of the fifteenth century and the fourteenth-century Fortress.

5 places to visit in Casole d'Elsa, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Rocca di Casole e galleria Arte Viva

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

The fortress of the fourteenth century, now seat of the Municipality, with the contemporary art gallery Arte Viva; the oval walls of the village have two round towers of the fifteenth century.

  • Where Casole d'Elsa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Collegiata di Santa Maria Assunta e Museo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Collegiate from the 12th century with the cenotaph of Beltramo Aringhieri; next to the Archaeological Museum and the Collegiate with Etruscan art and sacred artworks.

  • Where Casole d'Elsa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa di San Niccolò

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Small church of the village with frescoes of the family of Rustici painters, signposted by Visit Tuscany.

  • Where Casole d'Elsa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Parco Selva di Sogno

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint
