What to see in Palestrina

The ancient Praeneste on the slopes of Mount Ginestro: the Sanctuary of Fortuna Primigenia of the II century BC, Palazzo Barberini built on its terraces with the National Archaeological Museum and the Mosaic of the Nile, the buildings of the Forum, the cathedral of Sant'Agapito, the Diocesan Museum and the birthplace of Giovanni Pierluigi from Palestrina.

Museo archeologico nazionale prenestino e Mosaico del Nilo

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Since 1956 in Palazzo Barberini, built on the cavea of the sanctuary: on the ground floor the cryptoporic with sculptures and inscriptions, on the first floor the materials of the cult of Fortuna (the colossal statue of Iside-Fortuna, the relief Grimani of Augustan age), the second mirrors and bronze cysts and votive terracotta, at the last the great Mosaic of the polychrome Nile, view of an Egyptian landscape during the flood, among the major Hellenistic mosaics preserved. At the entrance, a 1:50 scale of the shrine. Touring hours: every day 9-20.

  • Where Palazzo Barberini, Piazza della Cortina, Palestrina
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.palestrina.rm.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Santuario della Fortuna Primigenia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built towards the end of the second century BC, it was famous throughout the Roman world for the oracle of the lotes: it climbs on the slopes of Mount Ginestro in terraces, ramps and symmetrical staircases on Hellenistic models, up to the round temple with the statue of the goddess; in the center the Well of the Sorti. It is considered the largest late-republican complex in ancient Italy and its remains envelop the medieval village; the upper part is incorporated in Palazzo Barberini and you can visit with the museum.

  • Where Piazza della Cortina, Palestrina
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Complesso degli edifici del Foro di Praeneste

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the former bishop's palace, then seminary, today state, remain part of the buildings of the forum under the current Piazza Regina Margherita: the apsed hall, reopened in 2016, which housed the Mosaic of the Nile, the Erario, the civil basilica with four naves and the Ninfeo dei Pisces (already Antro delle Sorti), cave in the rock with the remains of a second Hellenistic mosaic; an antiquarium exhibits statues of Augustus and Faustina Maggiore.

  • Where Piazza Regina Margherita 1, Palestrina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.palestrina.rm.it · italia.it

Palazzo Barberini: Ninfeo e Sala di Urbano VIII

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The palace of the Barberini Column on the terraces of the sanctuary is still the residence of the prince; in addition to the museum you can visit the Barberini Nympheo, the garden of the prince of the second half of the seventeenth century, and the frescoed room where Pope Urban VIII received the guests.

visit only with guide on reservation, private entrance (Visit Lazio)

  • Where Piazza della Cortina, Palestrina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Rosalia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Baroque Church, Palatine chapel of the Princes Barberini at the end of the 17th century: it preserves a painting by Francesco Reali with Santa Rosalia defending Palestrina from the plague; in the Hall of Deposits stood the Pietà di Palestrina attributed to Michelangelo, today at the Galleria dell'Accademia in Florence.

  • Where Palestrina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Basilica Cattedrale di Sant'Agapito Martire

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

On the area of the Forum and on the ancient temple of Jupiter, built in 1117 (crypt dedicated in 1116): the Romanesque remains facade, bell tower and crypt. The sixteenth-century marble portal, Crucifixion and wooden throne by Girolamo Sicilante da Sermoneta in the Colonna chapel, Sant'Agapito between the lions of Andrea Camassei, the Decapitation of the Saint by Carlo Saraceni, an Annunciation by Carlo Maratta and the nineteenth-century apse frescoes by Domenico Bruschi. On August 18, the feast of the patron.

  • Where Corso Pierluigi 11, Palestrina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.palestrina.rm.it · italia.it

Museo diocesano di arte sacra

Museums · Old masters · Free entry

Opened in 2005 in the south-west wing of the Bishop's Palace, about 600 works: the Ara delle Vittorie and a sarcophagus of the II-III century, early Christian inscriptions of the area of Sant'Agapito, an 'Eolo' attributed to Michelangelo, a Madonna with the school veil of Perugino, a Decapitation attributed to Caravaggio, the bust-reliquary of Sant'Agapito of 1588 and pivials of the Cardinals Bassi della Rovere and Antonio Barberini. Open from Thursday to Saturday 9.30-12.30 and 15.30-18.30.

  • Where Via Roma 23, Palestrina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.palestrina.rm.it · italia.it

Casa natale di Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina

Museums · House museums

The sixteenth-century house where the composer was born, restored in 1994 on three levels, is home to the Fondazione Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina, research center with a library of polyphonic music; the biographical and iconographic exhibition, curated by Lino Bianchi, retraces life and works from 1525 to 1594. Open Tuesday-Sunday (variable hours, closed Monday).

  • Where Corso Pierluigi 3, Palestrina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Porta del Sole

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Completed in 1642 on a project by Francesco Contini, architect of Barberini, as entrance of the new Piazza del Sole in front of Santa Maria degli Angeli; it is decorated with the great sun and the three bees of the Barberini heraldic. Next, the archaeological area restored with the ancient ramp in basoli and a polygonal bastion of the walls.

  • Where Via Pierantonio Petrini 59, Palestrina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.palestrina.rm.it · italia.it

Villa imperiale e chiesa di Santa Maria in Villa

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

About a mile from the sanctuary, the villa known as "Adrino" for the statue of Antinoo found here (today at the Vatican Museums): structures of the second century A.D. in the civic cemetery, with vaulted rooms and a two-storey cryptoportico; a hall has become the church of Santa Maria in Villa, with stuccoes of the early seventeenth century around a fresco of the Madonna with Child and a cosmatesco altar.

  • Where SP20a 90, Palestrina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.palestrina.rm.it · italia.it

Borgo medievale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historic centre is located on the terraces of the ancient Praeneste, with alleys and stairways that go up to the highest point; the city has dominated the countryside south of Rome for almost three thousand years. On 18 August the Palio of Sant'Agapito with the Scifa Giostra.

  • Where Centro storico, Palestrina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it
