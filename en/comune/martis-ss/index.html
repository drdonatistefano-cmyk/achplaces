What to see in Martis

Village of the Anglona overlooking the valley of the Carrucana river, Martis is known for the Gothic ruins of San Pantaleo on a rocky spur and for the petrified forest of Carrucana. Just outside the village there is the waterfall of Triulintas, in the gorge of Badde Traes.

Chiesa di San Pantaleo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Just outside the village, on a rocky spur above the valley of the Carrucana river, probably dates back to the first quarter of the 14th century and is indicated as the most significant example of Italian Gothic in Sardinia before the spread of the Catalan style. It has a basilica plan with three naves with cruising vaults and arches with acute sixth, quadrangular apse on the edge of the rock, facade with portal and rosette with bichrome cones of black trachite and light limestone and bell tower partly rebuilt in the sixteenth century. It was deconsecrated in 1920 due to the risk of collapse; since 1988 works of stabilization of the church and the rock have been underway.

Foresta pietrificata di Carrucana

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

It occupies about 1,000 m2 in the lower Anglona, just over a kilometer east of Martis: fossilized and siliconized tree trunks, linked to volcanic activities of about twenty million years ago.

  • Where Valle del rio Carrucana, Martis
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Cascata di Triulintas e gola di Badde Traes

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Less than 2 km from the village, the river Masino has dug the red trachytic rocks of Badde Traes: at the confluence with the river Pontisella the waterfall makes a jump of 15 meters and ends in a pond between elms and willows. Nearby there are the remains of an active mill until the mid-Twentieth century and a picnic area in the woods. From Martis you go out towards Nulvi and take the dirt road to the right signposted "historic-environmental trail Triulintas mill"; after less than 1 km there is the parking lot, from which the path runs along the stream with two bridges up to the foot of the waterfall.

  • Where Strada per Nulvi, Martis
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Parrocchiale di San Giuseppe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Inaugurated in 1977, it houses the painting "The Miracle of St. Pantaleo" by Andrea Lusso (1595), already altarpiece of St. Pantaleo, and the statue of the Dormitio Virginis of the 14th century.
