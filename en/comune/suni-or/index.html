What to see in Suni

Planargy country among the vineyards of Malvasia di Bosa, with an archaeological park that goes from the domus de janas of Chirisconis to the Seneghe protonuraghe and the Nuraghe complex Nuraddeo.

6 places to visit in Suni, in the province of Oristano (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Suni in the guide with map and filters

Nuraghe Nuraddeo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The only complex nuraghe of Suni, on the plateau of Pedrasenta at 350 meters, of the Bronze Age (XIV-X century BC): the central tower exceeds 14 meters, with two rooms in tholos, and is surrounded by a bastion with three towers around a pentagonal courtyard.

  • Where Altopiano di Pedrasenta, Suni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Protonuraghe Seneghe

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Rame (2400-1800 B.C.) on the Modolo valley: a straight corridor of about 15 meters covered by eleven plates crosses the tower, over six meters high, with niches, slits and a staircase.

  • Where Valle del Modolo, Suni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Domus de janas di Chirisconis

History and architecture · Archaeology

Necropolis of the final Neolithic dug into the rock on the valley of the Riu Mannu: twelve domus with 21 rooms, vestibules and traces of paintings in ochre.

  • Where Badu 'e Crabolu, Suni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Casa museo Tiu Virgiliu

Museums · House museums

Historic house near the parish with over a thousand objects, tools and machines of craftsman and farmer work between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, including a shoemaker shop and a corner of weaving.

  • Where Suni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di San Pancrazio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

15th century church in the center of the village, with a statue of the sixteenth century; in mid May the feast of the saint with procession and ardy.

  • Where Suni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Stagno di Pischina 'e Paule

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Near the archaeological park, it hosts many animal species; in the area nest the griffon.

  • Where Suni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
