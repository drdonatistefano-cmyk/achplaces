What to see in Pavone Canavese

Borgo del Canavese at the gates of Ivrea, dominated by the castle of the Bishops of Ivrea that Alfredo d'Andrade bought in 1885 and restored in medieval style: around the stone shelter, the churches of Sant'Andrea, San Rocco and Madonna delle Grazie, the chapel of San Grato with frescoes of Giacomo di Ivrea and the paths of the Paraj Auta on the Morenic Amphitheatre.

8 places to visit in Pavone Canavese, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Pavone

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Castle of the Bishops of Ivrea, born from a walls of the IX-X century around the church of St. Peter, with the mast of the XI century, the round towers of 1390 and the spiral staircase of 1509-1512; in 1363 Bishop Pietro de la Chambre was held prisoner. In 1885 Alfredo d'Andrade bought it for 7,000 lire and restored it in medieval style, creating between 1912 and 1915 the Salone di Re Arduino; since 1991 it is part of the Giodice family and hosts hotels, restaurants and conference center. Private: internal visits should be requested from the property (tel. 0125 672111).

Ricetti e Torre Porta

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The medieval refuge (I Rusèt) rises to the west of the castle on the same hill, of an elongated north-south shape with an overhang to the west: there were preserved crops and in case of danger there were sheltered families and animals. The houses are in stone split up of the hill; in the Torre Porta d'accesso you can still see the tracks dug by the Saracinesca. You walk freely from the streets of the center; the buildings are private.

  • Where Via Ricetti 1, Pavone Canavese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Parrocchiale di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Consecrated in 1826 on a project by the architects Cardone and Cattaneo, it has neoclassical façade and Baroque interior with the fresco of the patron of Luigi Peretti; the organ Felice Bossi & Son of 1855, the largest to a single manual of the diocese of Ivrea, restored and inaugurated in 2019, plays in the Organale review. Open during functions.

  • Where Piazza del Municipio, Pavone Canavese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Chiesa di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Erected by vote after the plague of 1585 and restored in the eighteenth century, it is a national monument for its Baroque lines; the Baroque altar was taken away from the sanctuary of Belmonte by Napoleonic soldiers. It was a burial place from 1600 to 1835 and was restored in the eighties of the twentieth century. Open during functions.

  • Where Via San Rocco, Pavone Canavese
  • Entry Free

Chiesa della Madonna delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Of medieval origin: under the floor the walls of the church built between 1200 and 1300 in the Villa Nova di Pavone have emerged. A destination of pilgrimages, it preserves an 18th century altarpiece with the Virgin attributed to Valsesian masters. Open during functions.

  • Entry Free

Cappella di San Grato

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Dedicated to the Taumaturgo Bishop San Grato, considered of very ancient origins, it contains frescoes attributed to Giacomo di Ivrea, painter of the Gothic International, with St Andrew and St Peter; next to the romerium and an equipped area, not far from the glacial muffler

Paraj Auta (Monte Appareglio)

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A height of about 354 m, SIC area between Pavone and Ivrea in the northern sector of the Morenic Amphitheatre of Ivrea, with centuries-old woods, small marshes, rocks smoothed by glaciers, engraved cups and ruins of sighting towers. The municipal route (4.06 km, about 80 minutes, easy, all year round) starts from Sant'Andrea and reaches San Grato, the Via Crucis and panoramic points on Ivrea, the Serra and the Alpine valleys.

  • Where Piazza del Municipio, Pavone Canavese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.pavone.to.it · turismotorino.org

Museo Alfredo d'Andrade

Museums · House museums

Dedicated to the architect and painter born in Lisbon in 1839, who lived in Pavone and rebuilt the castle since 1885, he was director of the Regional Office for the Monuments of Piedmont and Liguria and among the creators of the medieval village of Turin. There is a path with interactive installations on restoration, archive, travel and relations with contemporaries.

the Municipality indicates it as a former museum, under renovation to accommodate also the municipal library
