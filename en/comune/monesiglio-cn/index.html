What to see in Monesiglio

Alta Langa village along the Bormida, dominated by the imposing Castle of the Caldera with the family chapel frescoed by Antonino Ocello da Ceva; just outside, Santa Maria dell'Acqua Dolce, the oldest church in the valley, and in the village the Silk Museum in the former spinning mill and the house-museum of the writer Maria Tarditi.

4 places to visit in Monesiglio, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello dei Caldera

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The imposing castle dominates the village; its family chapel preserves frescoes by Antonino Ocello da Ceva, marked as not to be missed.

Santa Maria dell'Acqua Dolce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest church in the valley, of Romanesque layout, erected by the Benedictines around the Thousand; it preserves frescoes with evident Byzantine influences.

Museo della Seta

Museums · Themed

Set up in the former spinning mill for which the country was known; it is visited by appointment and is indicated as accessible to people with disabilities.

Piccolo Museo di Langa, Casa di Maria Tarditi

Museums · House museums

The house of Maria Tarditi, an elementary teacher who began writing at the age of seventy and is remembered as the last writer of the Langhe, is today a museum open to the public thanks to the publisher Araba Fenice; many of his novels are set in Monesiglio, where it also takes place in part "I Sansôssi" by Augusto Monti.
