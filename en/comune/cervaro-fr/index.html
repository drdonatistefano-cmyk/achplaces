What to see in Cervaro

Country of the Ciociaria overlooking the plain of Cassino and crossed by the Via Francigena del Sud, rebuilt after the destruction of the Second World War; it preserves its goldsmith tradition in the Museum of Goldsmith Art and, at the foot of Mount Aquilone, the fifteenth-century sanctuary of Santa Maria de' Piternis.

Santuario di Santa Maria de' Piternis

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Built from 1399 around a niche older than the year 1000, where according to tradition Our Lady appeared to a sick shepherd who healed; the first church was consecrated in 1408 and the present building dates back to the reconstruction of 1692. It contains frescoes from the 15th to the 16th century: in the apse the Christ Pantocrator among apostles, saints and the Madonna, on the sides of the entrance a Madonna del Latte, on the left wall a Madonna with Child in flowery Gothic style. The patron saint's feast is September 8.

Museo dell'arte orafa

Museums · Themed

Set up by the Municipality in the premises of the former Pretura, it tells a tradition that in the early decades of the twentieth century counted about thirty goldsmith shops. It exhibits machinery, moulds and tools of the various phases of processing and jewelry produced in Cervaro between the end of the seventeenth and twentieth centuries, largely donated by families, with filigree works, mold and tunnel, caston rings and the typical rings with two braided hands; in a workshop rebuilt a craftsman shows the phases of the work. Not indicated hours: Inquire at the Municipality.

Castello di Torrocolo e Monte Trocchio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the early Middle Ages, the abbot of Montecassino built the defensive castle of Castrum Cerbari on Mount Trocchio, next to the castle of Torrocolo (VisitLatium indicates 1057); together they controlled the Cassino plain. According to tradition, the name of the village derives from a herd of deer, or from a white deer, which grazed on the height of the 'pesculum', now home to the castle.
