What to see in Colle di Val d'Elsa

The "Boemia d'Italia," where over 95% of the Italian crystal is produced, on the Via Francigena; home of Arnolfo di Cambio, divided between Colle Alta and Colle Bassa.

3 places to visit in Colle di Val d'Elsa, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 4 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Museo del Cristallo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Unique in Italy, opened in 2001 and renovated in 2023 in the former glass factory Boschi: it tells the workmanship of glass from medieval origins (vetreries known from 1331) to lead crystal of 1963.

  • Where Via dei Fossi 8A, Colle di Val d'Elsa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Piazza Arnolfo e arte contemporanea

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The square redesigned by Jean Nouvel, with the Spazio Michelucci, the Scalo Merci Don Gnocchi, the Crystal Serranda by Alessandra Tesi and the facade of the Bellini Chorus by Alberto Garutti.

  • Where Piazza Arnolfo, Colle di Val d'Elsa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Sentierelsa

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

4 km route starting from the bridge of San Martiale and following the river Elsa to Ponte di Spugna.

  • Where Ponte di San Marziale, Colle di Val d'Elsa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
