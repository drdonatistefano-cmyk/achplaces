What to see in Campertogno

Country of Valsesia with the large 18th century parish church of San Giacomo Maggiore, designed by Guarino Guarini, revised by Filippo Juvarra, the parish museum and the Art Path with fifteen chapels towards Cangello.

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giacomo Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish established in 1415; the new church, built between 1720 and 1732 and consecrated in 1760, was born from a project by Guarino Guarini (the wooden model of 1691 is in the museum) modified by Filippo Juvarra. The vault of the choir is frescoed by Carlo Borsetti (1727) and that of the nave by Giovanni Milocco; in the presbytery five great paintings by Lorenzo and Giuseppe Avondo (1839) on the life of San Giacomo, a marble altar of 1809 designed by Giovanni Molino and organ of the early seventeenth century. Bell tower of 1595 of the masons of Alagna. Visits by reservation with the association \Campertogno, art and culture Â (tel. 366 3719002).

Museo parrocchiale di San Giacomo Maggiore

Museums · Old masters

Founded by Don Pier Cesare De Vecchi, it preserves about 500 items listed: paintings, golden and painted wooden sculptures, furnishings, liturgical objects and sacred goldsmithery, mostly from the oldest parish, including a wooden pyramid altar of 1660. A painting by Melchiorre D'Enrico with the votive procession of the Confraternities of Campertogno at the Sacred Mount of Varallo stands out, after the plague that killed 28 people. Visits only by reservation (tel. 366 3719002).

Sentiero dell'Arte Campertogno - Cangello

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Mulattiera (path CAI 278) from Campertogno (815 m) to Cangello (1.364 m), about 1 hour and 30. It starts from piazza Pier Celestino Gilardi and from the stone bridge doubled in 1606, passes through the hamlet Tetti and the oratory of San Marco and goes back along fifteen chapels with episodes of the life of the Madonna, built and decorated between 1450 and 1939; in Selletto a house with fresco dated 1581. The oratory of the Madonna degli Angeli allo Scarpiolo, with square plan with octagonal dome and lantern, has frescoes by Giacomo Gnifetti (1704) and Lorenzo Avondo (1845-1855). The mule track was extended in 1820 with a legacy of Giuseppe Della Bianca.

  • Where Via Mario Grosso 20, Campertogno (partenza)
