What to see in Montepulciano

Renaissance city on the ridge between Valdichiana and Val d'Orcia, home of Vino Nobile, with Piazza Grande and the temple of San Biagio under the walls.

7 places to visit in Montepulciano, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Montepulciano in the guide with map and filters

Piazza Grande e Palazzo Comunale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Viewpoint

The heart of the city, with the well of Griffins and Lions designed by Antonio da Sangallo and the Gothic Town Hall in travertine, whose crenellated tower rises with an internal staircase up to the panorama of Valdichiana and Val d'Orcia.

  • Where Piazza Grande, Montepulciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Tempio di San Biagio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Renaissance masterpiece by Antonio da Sangallo il Vecchio, built between 1518 and 1548 on a meadow under the walls, with a Greek cross with dome; it houses the fourteenth-century Madonna of San Biagio and inspired Michelangelo for San Pietro.

  • Where San Biagio, Montepulciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets

Sources: visittuscany.com

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The Duomo designed by Ippolito Scalza at the end of the sixteenth century, with three naves, with works by Taddeo di Bartolo, Michelozzo and Andrea della Robbia.

  • Where Piazza Grande, Montepulciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Chiesa di Sant'Agostino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the thirteenth century with the façade in travertine partly designed by Michelozzo in the first fifteenth century, between Gothic and Renaissance.

  • Where Montepulciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo Civico e Pinacoteca Crociani

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the fourteenth century Palazzo Neri Orselli Etruscan and Roman finds, paintings of Sienese school, Filippo Lippi and Sodoma and Robbian terracotta.

  • Where Palazzo Neri Orselli, Montepulciano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Fortezza Medicea

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built in 1261 by the Republic of Siena, several times rebuilt, the last time by Antonio da Sangallo the Elder, from which the name Medici; restored in 1885.

  • Where Montepulciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Riserva Naturale del Lago di Montepulciano

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The lake, the Maestro Canal of Chiana and the surrounding fields: together with the lake of Chiusi is what remains of the marshes of the valley, one of the most important wetlands in central Italy.

  • Where Montepulciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
