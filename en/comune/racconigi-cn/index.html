What to see in Racconigi

The holiday residence of the Savoy, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, with its large romantic park and the neo-Gothic Margaria complex.

Castello e Parco di Racconigi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Born around the 11th century as a home in the Marche di Torino, passed to the Marquises of Saluzzo and then to the Savoy, official resort with a vast nineteenth-century romantic park up to the neo-Gothic Margaria complex; you can visit the noble floor and the nineteenth-century kitchens. UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  • Where Via Morosini, Racconigi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it

Centro Cicogne e Anatidi LIPU

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Since 1985 in the farmhouse Stramiano, former property of the Savoy: a large wetland near the castle where dozens of storks nest and stop in migration.

  • Where Via Stramiano, Racconigi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: italia.it · visitcuneese.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo Giardino della Civiltà della Seta

Museums · Themed

It tells how the Savoy supported the spinning mills that made Racconigi the most important production center of silk in Piedmont.

  • Where Racconigi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Pinacoteca civica Levis-Sismonda

Museums · Modern art

The works of Giuseppe Augusto Levis, a painter appreciated by Vittorio Emanuele III, and Carlo Sismonda; openings for events and groups.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Racconigi
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
