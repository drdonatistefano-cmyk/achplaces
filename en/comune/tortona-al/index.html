What to see in Tortona

Cities along the Via Emilia, the ancient Julia Dertona: the sixteenth-century Cathedral, Palazzo Guidobono with the Archaeological Museum, the Pinacoteca "Il Divisionismo" in the Medieval Palace, the Colle Savo with the castle tower and the Basilica of the Madonna della Guardia with the large golden statue.

MA·DE Museo Archeologico Dertona

Museums · Archaeological

In the fifteenth century Palazzo Guidobono, rebuilt with a Gothic façade in 1939-42, tells the ancient Dertona: the sarcophagus of Publius Elio Sabino, the tombstone of soldier Aurelius Veterano, a stele of gens Latinia, a togata statue, two polychrome mosaics and three fistulae of the aqueduct. Open Saturday and Sunday 15.30-19, with free guided tours on Saturday.

  • Where Piazza Aristide Arzano 1, Tortona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.tortona.al.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Pinacoteca "Il Divisionismo"

Museums · Modern art

Permanent exhibition inaugurated on May 26, 2012 in the Medieval Palazzetto of the fourteenth century: over 140 works by 50 divisionist artists with Giuseppe Pellizza da Volpedo (more than 20 paintings), together with Segantini, Medardo Rosso, Nomellini and Morbelli; a room is dedicated to the Dolonese Angelo Barabino. Open Saturday and Sunday 15-19, other days by reservation.

  • Where Corso Leoniero 2, Tortona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · alexala.it · visitpiemonte.com · italia.it

Duomo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built in 1574-1592 on the site of the ancient church of San Quirino, according to the Borromaic dictates, with nineteenth-century façade by Nicolò Bruno; frescoed vaults by Paolo Maggi (1853-56) and works by Aurelio Luini, Vermiglio, Flemish, Moncalvo and Camillo Procaccini. It preserves the urn of San Martiano and the tomb of the composer Lorenzo Perosi.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Tortona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.tortona.al.it · italia.it

Basilica Santuario Madonna della Guardia

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Wanted by Don Orione where in 1418 he preached St Bernardino da Siena, it was started in 1928 on a project by Mgr. Chiappetta and consecrated in 1931, basilica since 1991; in Gothic-Lombard style, in the crypt it houses the tombs of Don Orione and Don Sterpi. On the tower stands the bronze statue of the Madonna of about 14 meters, work of Narciso Cassino.

  • Where Via Don Domenico Sparpaglione 4, Tortona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.centromaterdeitortona.it

Sources: centromaterdeitortona.it · comune.tortona.al.it · alexala.it · italia.it

Colle Savo e Torre del Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Roman citadel, the medieval castle besieged by Barbarossa in 1155 and the fortress rebuilt in 1773 by Bernardino Pinto, razed to the ground by Napoleon in 1801; remains fragments of walls and the tower. The Torrione, 34 metres high and visible from the motorway, combines Art Nouveau and Medieval style.

  • Where Colle Savo, Tortona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.tortona.al.it · alexala.it · italia.it

Centro storico, via Emilia e piazze

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Via Emilia crosses the centre with porticoes, shops and locals; there you will find the Aristide Arzano square with Palazzo Guidobono and Malaspina square. In the center there are also the fifteenth-century Casa del Pozzo, with the Virgin painted by Mario Patri, and Palazzo Garofoli, in the present form of Carlo Riccardi (about 1770), who hosted Napoleon and Pius VII.

  • Where Via Emilia, Tortona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · alexala.it · comune.tortona.al.it

Museo delle macchine agricole Orsi

Museums · Themed

In the former Orsi tractor factory, example of industrial archaeology: 1800 m2 with the permanent exhibition in the former tournament of 22 machines of the Orsi and Berri collections (tractors, locomobiles, presses, threshing machines and a harvester) documenting production from the early twentieth century to the late 1950s.

no regular time: visits by reservation or during events of the Municipality

Chiesa di Santa Maria Canale

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Documented from 1151, with apse and side chapels of the second sixteenth century and façade of the restoration of 1853; the interior was decorated by Rodolfo Gambini in 1918 and preserves works by Fiamminghino, Brandimarte della Torre, Vermiglio and a Nativity of Leonardo style.

  • Where Tortona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.tortona.al.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Matteo e Mausoleo di Maiorano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church rebuilt by the Dominicans at the end of the seventeenth century, with a fourteenth-century table of the Madonna and Child of Barnabas of Modena. The Mausoleum of Maiorano (1st century BC), a square structure of about 9 meters on the side linked by tradition to the Maggiorian Emperor, killed in Tortona in 461, is incorporated into the canonical church.

  • Where Tortona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.tortona.al.it · italia.it

Oratori di San Rocco e San Carlo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Oratory of San Rocco (1626-1631), seat of the Brotherhood of Mercy, has the portico of 1725 and paintings attributed to Gherardini and Badaracco; the Oratory of San Carlo (1623-1625) preserves a table of Scipione Crespi and a wooden statue of the Assumption of Montecucco (1852).

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Teatro Civico

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Designed by engineer Pietro Pernigotti and inaugurated on May 2, 1838, it has three orders of stages, the curtain of Luigi Vacca with Orpheus and Eurydice and the medallion of the Muse on the ceiling; closed in 1952, was reopened after the restoration on May 2, 1990.

  • Where Tortona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.tortona.al.it · italia.it

Sepolcri romani e Ponte romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Mausolei to podium in sandstone of the first Augustan age, excavated in the seventies and restored in 1990 by the Superintendence; the Roman bridge (late 1st century BC - mid I AD), discovered in 1982, has shoulders in conglomerate covered with limestone blocks. The Roman archaeological areas are located in via Rinarolo and via alle Fonti.

  • Where Tortona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.tortona.al.it · alexala.it · italia.it

Abbazia di Rivalta Scrivia

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The 12th century Abbey where the Cistercians settled in 1180; the restorations of 1941-42 brought to light frescoes of the second fifteenth century, including a San Cristoforo signed by Franceschino Boxilio in 1490. Next to it stands a noble residence with loggias from the middle of the seventeenth century.

  • Where Rivalta Scrivia, Tortona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.tortona.al.it · italia.it
