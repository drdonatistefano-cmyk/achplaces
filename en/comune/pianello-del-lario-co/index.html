What to see in Pianello del Lario

Comune dell'alto Lago di Como, on the western shore, which rises from the lake between woods and pastures to the peaks, with long beaches loved by windsurfers and kiters; here Don Guanella started his work with the first house of mercy for the elderly. The Museum of the Barca Lariana, in a nineteenth-century spinning mill, is worth a visit.

Museo della Barca Lariana

Museums · Themed

Born from the association "La Raccolta della Barca Lariana," founded in 1978 to save old boats destined for demolition, the museum opened in 1982 in a nineteenth-century mill. In fourteen halls exhibits about 160 of the more than 350 boats owned (fishing boats, motorboats for racing and tourism, sailing boats, smuggling, military and work) with nets, engines, sails, tools and documents on the Larian nautical, even with increased reality. The most important piece is a gondola built in 1860 on Lake Como, with asymmetrical shapes and the felze of the Venetian tradition. Admission fee, reduced for TCI members and a companion.

Spiaggia di Calozzo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Beach of stones in locality Calozzo, quiet and isolated; the area is windy and lends itself to windsurfing and kitesurfing with departure directly from the shore.

  • Where Località Calozzo, Pianello del Lario
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
