What to see in Torgiano

Wine village between Perugia and Assisi, at the confluence of Tiber and Chiascio: the Wine Museum in the Palazzo Graziani Baglioni and the Olive and Oil Museum in an ancient oil mill.

3 places to visit in Torgiano, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

MUVIT – Museo del Vino

Museums · Themed

In the foundations of Palazzo Graziani Baglioni since 1974, designed by Giorgio and Maria Grazia Lungarotti, he tells five thousand years of culture of vine and wine.

  • Where Corso Vittorio Emanuele II 31, Torgiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismo.comune.perugia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · regione.umbria.it · turismo.comune.perugia.it

MOO – Museo dell'Olivo e dell'Olio

Museums · Themed

Going up via Garibaldi, in an ancient building already mill, the museum dedicated to the olive tree and its oil.

  • Where Via Garibaldi, Torgiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.comune.perugia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.perugia.it
