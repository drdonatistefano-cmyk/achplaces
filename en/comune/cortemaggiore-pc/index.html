What to see in Cortemaggiore

Village of the Piacenza plain on the left bank of the Arda, rebuilt from about 1480 at the will of Gianlodovico Pallavicino as an ideal city of the Renaissance on orthogonal roads; it houses the Collegiate Church with the mausoleum of Pallavicino, the Church of the Annunciation with the frescoes of Pordenone and the Eleonora Duse Theatre.

Basilica Collegiata di Santa Maria delle Grazie e mausoleo dei Pallavicino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Also known as Duomo, it was built from 1481 on a project by Gilberto Manzi, with sixteenth-century bell tower and facade rebuilt in 1881 by Gaetano Guglielmetti; the interior is late-Gothic Lombard with Renaissance elements. In the left aisle the mausoleum of Pallavicino, with the ark of Gianlodovico and his wife Anastasia Torelli dated 1499, here transferred from 1812 after the Napoleonic suppression of the Franciscan monastery; it also houses the polyptych of 1499 by Filippo Mazzola, father of Parmigianino, and in the crypt a Christ that comes out of the tomb of 1522 attributed to Antonietto de' Renzi.

Chiesa dell'Annunziata e convento francescano

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Complex started in 1487 by Rolando Pallavicino on a project by Gilberto Manzi and consecrated in 1499: three-nave church with hexapartite vaults between Romanesque, Gothic and Renaissance. The octagonal chapel of the Immaculate Conception, frescoed by Pordenone around 1530, is the true jewel; Pordenone also has the lunettes of the Pallavicino chapel (with vaults attributed to Bernardo Zenale) and a Deposition on canvas, born as a processional standard. The monastery develops around a Renaissance cloister of about 55 x 65 meters, the largest in the province.

Teatro Eleonora Duse

History and architecture · Historic theatres

The theater was born in 1827 from the transformation of the oratory annexed to the Franciscan Tertiary monastery, after in 1822 the City Council had asked Maria Luigia of Austria for the hall; at the beginning of the twentieth century it was named after Eleonora Duse, who had recited it at the age of ten. The neoclassical façade has three doors, three windows between pilasters and a tympanum with the city coat of arms; you visit during the theater season and events.

Oratorio di San Giuseppe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1576 for the Compagnia dei Disciplinati di San Giuseppe, completed in 1593 and consecrated in 1594; the interior was decorated at the end of the seventeenth century with stuccoes by Giovanni Martello, Bernardino Barca and Domenico Dossa. The great ancona of the Dossa (1697-1699) contains a polychrome wooden statue of St. Joseph of 1630; on the canvas walls of Giovanni Battista Tagliasacchi painted between 1722 and 1727.

Centro storico rinascimentale e Palazzo Pallavicino

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Borgo built in the Renaissance according to the model of the ideal city, on a project attributed to Maffeo Vegio da Como, with perpendicular roads set on the cardoon and decuman of an ancient Roman camp; it was small capital of the Pallavicino until the entrance into the Farnesian domains in 1587. Palazzo Pallavicino is private and can only be seen from the outside. Every first Sunday of the month there is the antiques market and the things of the past.

Chiavenna Landi, torrione e chiesa parrocchiale

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

In the hamlet there is a 13th-century tower, massive and with Ghibelline merlons, today surrounded by more recent buildings, and the 16th-century parish church that houses a Renaissance image of the Madonna.
