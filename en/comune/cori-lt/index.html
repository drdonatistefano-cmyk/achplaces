What to see in Cori

Borgo dei Monti Lepini divided between Cori Monte and Cori Valle, surrounded by about two kilometers of polygonal walls; on the acropolis the Temple of Hercules of the first century BC, in the valley the columns of the Temple of Dioscuri, and then the complex of Sant'Oliva with the City Museum and the Oratory of the SS. Announced. In the hamlet of Giulianello the Palazzo Salviati and the lake, a natural monument.

Tempio di Ercole

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the acropolis of Cori, built between '89 and '80 B.C. by the magistrates Marco Maglio and Lucio Turpilio, whose names are engraved on the architrave of the door; it is a Doric temple reinterpreted in an italic key, with the pronao of eight columns in travertine about 7 meters high, trigliph frieze and metope. National monument since 1898, dominates the Pontina Plain.

  • Where Via Tempio d'Ercole 8, Cori
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Tempio dei Dioscuri

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

In the forum of the ancient Cora, in Cori Valle, there are two of the six Corinthian columns of the pronao (1st century BC) with traces of stucco, and a third one incorporated into a house; on the architrave the inscription with the name of the temple and of the magistrates financed. In front, fragments of a black and white mosaic floor; the cell housed the statues of the Dioscuri in marble 1.90 m tall.

  • Where Via delle Colonne, Cori
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Mura poligonali

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built between the 6th and 4th century BC, they run for over two kilometers with towers and bastions, interrupted by the Roman gates, Ninfina and Signina; one of the most suggestive stretches emerges at the foot of the statue of Victory.

  • Where Cori
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Complesso di Sant'Oliva

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

It rises on the remains of a temple of the III-I century BC: the church and the bell tower are of the first half of the XII century, the chapel of the Holy Crucifix (second half of the XV century) was commissioned by Cardinal Guillaume d'Estouteville and by Ambrogio Massari, with frescoes of biblical scenes of the late fifteenth century. The cloister of the former Augustinian convent, by Antonio da Como, has 27 marble capitals all different. Convent and cloister are visited by appointment.

  • Where Cori Valle, di fronte al Municipio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Museo della Città e del Territorio

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 2000 in the former Augustinian convent of Sant'Oliva, it exhibits archaeological finds and testimonies of the history of Cori, including the architectural and sculptural fragments of the Temple of the Dioscuri, a 1:75 scale model and a copy of the statue of Minerva donated by the city of Rome in 1999.

  • Where Complesso di Sant'Oliva, Cori
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Oratorio della SS. Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Chapel of the Scrovegni of the lower Lazio, was built in the fifteenth century at the behest of Cardinal Pedro Fernández de Frías and preserves one of the most important late-Gothic cycles of Lazio, with scenes from the Old and New Testaments; sacristy, bell tower and hermitage were added between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. Managed by the Regional Direction Musei Lazio, you can visit it on request through the Pro Loco.

  • Where Cori
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Fontana di Piazza Monte Pio

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

An ancient fountain of Cori Monte where, according to tradition, the Barbarossa granted himself a bath after plundering the city.

Giulianello e il suo lago

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The hamlet, 8 km from Cori, preserves the remains of the ancient gate, the baronial palace Castello Salviati and the sixteenth-century church of San Giovanni Battista; near the Lake Giulianello, natural monument since 2007.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
