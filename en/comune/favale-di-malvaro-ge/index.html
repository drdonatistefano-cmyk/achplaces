What to see in Favale di Malvaro

Municipality of the Malvaro valley, side of the Val Fontanabuona, on the slopes of Mount Pagliaro; here is the paternal house of Amedeo Pietro Giannini, son of Favalese emigrants and founder of the Bank of America and Italy, today the Museum of the Emigrant.

Museo dell'Emigrante - Casa Giannini

Museums · House museums

Father's house of Amedeo Pietro Giannini, son of Favale's emigrants who, according to legend, left after the earthquake in San Francisco in 1906 with a safe on a cart and founded the Bank of America and Italy. On two floors it preserves original furnishings of the daily life of the nineteenth century, documents, photographs and panels on its life; you can even walk up a mule track. On the last Sunday of June, the country celebrates the "day of the migrant" with the Ligurian communities in the world. Tel. 0185 975195 (Common).

Monte Caucaso e la sua cappella

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the summit you can enjoy a panorama from the sea to the Alps; a hiking ring of the Aveto Park starts from the Scoglina Pass (in the municipality of Favale), crosses a beech forest to the Aquapendente and the top and returns to Barbagelata along a stretch of the Alta Via (about 4 hours, medium difficulty). On the top, near the refuge, there is a small chapel where every year is celebrated the midnight Mass of New Year, reached with a torchlight from Barbagelata; free entrance, not accessible to the disabled, without parking.
