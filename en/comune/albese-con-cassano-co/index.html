What to see in Albese con Cassano

Born in 1928 from the fusion of Albese and Cassano, along the ancient route of the Roman Via Regina, the municipality preserves the Romanesque church of San Pietro with the leaning bell tower, the Lavandée water museum born in the wash-house of 1822, historic villas and the wooded paths of the Albese Valley towards the Cave of the Cepp.

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Example of Romanesque Lombard stone, built on the remains of a previous Lombard chapel; the characteristic leaning bell tower dates back to the 11th century. In the apse frescoes of the 15th century by the brothers Giovan Giacomo and Giovanni Andrea de Magistris. Reported in the FAI census •The Places of the Heart • • .

Museo etnografico e dell'acqua Lavandée

Museums · Themed

Born in 2004 in the building of 1822 that housed a wash-house porch on the ground floor and the elementary school on the first floor, it tells for themes the use of water in rural communities: tools for washing clothes, drawing, transport and domestic and agricultural use, with customs and popular beliefs; among the pieces a fire machine of the second half of the nineteenth century perfectly working. In 2018 the collections passed to the civic center Fabio Casartelli; the wash-house remains open as a permanent exhibition and hosts the municipal hostel. It offers routes to discover fountains, wells, glaciers and ancient aqueducts of the territory.

visits by reservation according to the MiC card (tel. 031 426122)

Sentieri della Valle Albese e Grotta del Cepp

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

From the hamlet of Grotta del Cepp, in the Albese Valley, start the paths along the Cosia stream: from Via Gatti you pass through the Rundanina fountain and the Baita degli Alpini, where there was the tank of the Crespi aqueduct, in a wood of beech and chestnut trees with the stations of the Via Crucis marked by colorful tiles. The cave houses a statue of the Madonna of Lourdes; in the area they live roe deer.

  • Where Via Gatti, Albese con Cassano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Ville storiche di Albese

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Villa Odescalchi Greppi (today Villa Santa Chiara) dates back to the 16th century; the Galaterale, near Piazza Motta, carries the coat of arms with the swan of Parravicini; Villa Ida Parravicini of Persia preserves Baroque frescoes; Villa San Benedetto Menni, built around Villa Bassi Roncaldier, is surrounded by a large Italian garden. The complex of villas was opened for the FAI Days of Spring 2021.

Villa Santa Chiara houses a nursing home and Villa San Benedetto Menni a nursing home (Lombardy)

  • Where Via Roma, Albese con Cassano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · fondoambiente.it

Monumento a Fabio Casartelli

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Behind the parish church of Santa Margherita Vergine, in white Garfagnana marble, the work of the sculptor Bruno Luzzani from 2000; every third Sunday of July there is the Mediofondo Casartelli, a much followed cycling race.

  • Where Dietro la chiesa di Santa Margherita, Albese con Cassano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
