What to see in Angera

Ancient village on the Lombard shore of Lake Maggiore, dominated by the Fortress Borromeo that houses the Museum of the Doll and the Toy; in the center the Archaeological Museum in the Palazzo del Pretorio, to the south the Bruschera Swamp, the largest reed of the western shore of the lake.

Rocca Borromeo di Angera

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built in the 12th century by the archbishops of Milan on a limestone spur above the southern shore of Lake Maggiore, it was contested between Torriani and Visconti, enlarged by the Visconti and in 1449 entrusted to the Borromeos, who still own it. In the Hall of Justice an exceptional cycle of frescoes; you can also visit the Historical Hall, the Hall of the Maiolicas (300 rare pieces), the Castellana Tower with views of the lake and the Medieval Garden; the Ala Scaligera houses exhibitions. Season 2026 from March 12 to November 8, every day.

  • Where Via Rocca Castello 2, Angera
  • Entry €16.50, boys 6-15 years €11
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: vareseturismo.it · in-lombardia.it

Museo della Bambola e del Giocattolo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Inside the Fortress, born from the collection of Princess Bona Borromeo Arese; it is indicated as the most important in Europe of its kind. It has porcelain, wood and plastic dolls, handmade and standard, from Germany, Spain, Japan and other countries, with a core of French school, as well as wooden and tin toys and books and newspapers for boys from the eighteenth century to today.

  • Where Via Rocca Castello 2, Angera
  • Entry Included in the Rocca ticket (€16.50, boys 6-15 years €11)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · vareseturismo.it

Civico Museo Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

Since 1982 on the first floor of the fifteenth-century Palazzo del Pretorio, with ogival portal and stone portico of Angera. The Roman vicus with coins and commerce, the Roman necropolis supplies, the organic remains of the Angera Panini and a 5th century Greek funeral stele of a Syrian merchant; stoneware under the porch and children's section (MABA). Closed from 20 December to 31 January.

  • Where Palazzo del Pretorio, Via Marconi 2, Angera
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Palude e Oasi della Bruschera

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

About 164 hectares south of the centre: it is the largest wetland on the western shore of Lake Maggiore, Special Conservation Zone of the Natura 2000 network; the north part is oasis of protection since 1988. Canneti, yellow water lilies, black alder forests and rare ferns; over 300 species of birds recorded and a colony of cormorants on the Partegora Island. About 4 km of trails and a birdwatching shed, dogs on a leash.

  • Where Ingresso principale tra Via Arena e Via Pertini, Angera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.angera.va.it · in-lombardia.it

Centro storico, lungolago e Museo Diffuso

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historic centre, with the Palazzo del Pretorio and the church of Santa Maria Assunta, overlooks a lakefront with tree-lined avenues, meadows and small ports suitable for walks and picnics. Since 2016 the Museo Diffuso has connected dozens of historical, artistic and landscape monuments of the village, the Capronno and Barzola villages and the surroundings with open-air rooms.

  • Where Lungolago, Angera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Colle di San Quirico

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hill reachable on paths loved by mountain bikers, rather technical, which from the top offers a spectacular view; near the village of Capronno.

  • Entry Free

Spiagge della Noce e della Nocciola

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Beaches on Lake Maggiore for sunbathing, swimming and water sports. Dogs allowed on a leash in the village, with restrictions on the lake and on the beach.
