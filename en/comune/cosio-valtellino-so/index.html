What to see in Cosio Valtellino

One of the most important towns of the Lower Valtellina, of medieval origins, at the entrance of the Val Gerola: in Piagno remain the bell tower and the apse of the priory of Cluniac of San Pietro in Vallate, founded in 1078, and in the village of Sacco a picta room of 1464 preserves the famous figure of the Homo selvadego.

Museo dell'Homo Salvadego

Museums · Themed

In an ancient building near the parish church of Sacco, a room on the first floor is entirely frescoed: decorations, floral motifs and paperwork in Gothic characters with proverbs and moral mottos in Latin and vulgar. Among the figures stands out a man with a hairy body and a thick beard, armed with a knotty stick, who presents himself with the phrase "Ego sonto un homo salvadego per natura," who me offende ge fo paura" (the frescoes are masters Batestinus and Simon and dated 18 May 1464).

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Priorato di San Pietro in Vallate

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Among the oldest and most important Romanesque buildings in Valtellina, dedicated to Saints Peter, Paul and Maiolo: it was built in 1078, when Otto and Bonizza of the Comacina Island donated the land to the monks of Cluny, then depended on the priory of Piona and was suppressed in the Napoleonic era. The low bell tower, the semicircular apse in four sectors and part of the southern wall with a round arched portal remain; the restoration is by engineer Antonio Gussa, between 1914 and 1916, and recent excavations have brought to light the walls of the monks' building. It can be reached in 5-10 minutes by mule track from Piagno, between chestnuts and robinias.

  • Where Via Vallate 32, Piagno, Cosio Valtellino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · valtellina.it

Sacco

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village of Val Gerola where, in addition to the fifteenth-century picta room of Homo selvadego, a house near the church has a refined votive fresco with the Pietà di Andrea De Passeris (1508) and the parish house preserves the Quadreria di San Lorenzo. From here pass the ancient road of the valley, in the stretch between Campione and Rasura, with panoramic views, chestnut woods and higher conifers.

  • Where Sacco, Cosio Valtellino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it
