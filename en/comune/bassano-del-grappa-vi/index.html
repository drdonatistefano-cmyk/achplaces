What to see in Bassano del Grappa

City on the Brenta at the foot of Mount Grappa: the Ponte degli Alpini covered in wood, symbol of the city and national monument, the Civic Museum with the largest collection of Jacopo Bassano and the Remondini prints of Palazzo Sturm.

7 places to visit in Bassano del Grappa, in the province of Vicenza (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Bassano del Grappa in the guide with map and filters

Ponte Vecchio (Ponte degli Alpini)

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Bridge covered in wood on the Brenta at the point where the river narrows, symbol of the city and national monument since 2019; in the Great War the troops headed for the plateau of the Seven Municipalities crossed it.

  • Where Bassano del Grappa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it

Museo Civico

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Founded in 1828 with the legacy of naturalist Giambattista Brocchi, from 1840 in the former convent of San Francesco: the largest collection of Jacopo Bassano and his workshop, a wing dedicated to Canova with plasters and monochromes, and the crucifix of the Guariento.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi 34, Bassano del Grappa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Palazzo Sturm – Museo Remondini e Museo della Ceramica

Museums · Themed · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The eighteenth-century palace of Daniello Bernardi with more than seventy rooms frescoed by Giorgio Anselmi and a view of the Brenta; on the ground floor the Remondini prints, with engravings by Dürer and Mantegna, on the noble floor the ceramics of the city from the sixteenth century.

  • Where Via Schiavonetti 6, Bassano del Grappa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: venetoaroundme.regione.veneto.it · italia.it

Museo degli Alpini

Museums · City history
