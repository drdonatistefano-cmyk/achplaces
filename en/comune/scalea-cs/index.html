What to see in Scalea

One of the pearls of the Riviera dei Cedri, on a large promontory a few meters from the sea, with about 8 km of beaches; the historic center of stairs, alleys and arches rises up to the ruins of the Norman castle, between the Antiquarium of Torre Cimalonga, Palazzo dei Principi and the Tower Talao overlooking the Tyrrhenian Sea.

Centro storico di Scalea

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old town is a combination of stairs, alleys and arches, from which the city takes its name; characteristic the 'Suppourti', covered stretches of the streets born for defensive needs. Scalea was an important maritime port of call from the Norman era and in the Angevin-Aragonese period it exceeded the 5000 inhabitants; bombed in the Second World War, from the 60s the center was progressively abandoned in favor of the coast.

  • Where Via Nazario Sauro, Scalea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Torre Talao

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

16th century coastal tower, today panoramic terrace on the Tyrrhenian Sea and on the cliff of the Ajnella; in the caves of Torre Talao were found stone and bone artifacts of the Paleolithic, with traces of the Musterian phase.

  • Where Torre Talao, Scalea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it

Antiquarium di Torre Cimalonga

Museums · Archaeological · Viewpoint

Museum in a 16th-century defensive tower in one of the most panoramic squares in the historic centre, founded in 1987 and re-arranged in 2004: the exhibits, in chronological order and in windows obtained in the ancient fire vents, range from the stone instruments of the Middle Paleolithic of Torre Talao to the community of the hill of Petrosa (VII-VI century BC) to Ponticello in the Roman age; there are also exhibits of the archaeological Park of Laos. Open Monday-Friday 8.30-15, closed Saturday and Sunday.

  • Where Largo Cimalonga 7, Scalea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Castello normanno di Scalea

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Maniero built by the Normans in the 11th century on Lombard military structures, on the highest peak of the country: Riccardo Cuor di Leone (1190) and Giacomo d'Aragona (1289) passed there, and in 1250 Ruggiero di Lauria was born there, admiral of the Aragonese fleet remembered by Boccaccio in the Decameron. Abandoned at the end of the eighteenth century, the walls remain, the base of a circular tower, a fifteenth-century bulwark and walled blackbirds.

Palazzo dei Principi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Building with several factory bodies, originally part of the walls: the oldest walls (XIII century about) preserve multi-lobed rosettes and an ogival bifora in Amalfi style. Residence of the Spinelli princes, in the sixteenth century it was the meeting point of intellectuals such as Gregorio Calorprese and Pietro Metastasio; today it is of the Municipality, it houses the Municipal Library with eighteenth-century paintings of biblical subject and events in the garden and in the halls.

  • Where Via Dottor G. Oliva 16, Scalea
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Torre della Scalicella o «di Giuda»

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Guard station on the north side of Scalea, connected to the castle, from which the bay was dominated. According to tradition, at the beginning of the seventeenth century the guardian did not alarm the arrival of the Saracens and, captured, was hanged to a tree: hence the name; the historians also propose a bond with the nearby Jewish ghetto.

  • Where Via Faro 50, Scalea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Costa di Scalea: Ajnella, Baia del Carpino e Baia dei Draghi

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The beaches of Scalea, about 8 km long, are of dark pebbles; the cliff of the Ajnella is one of the free beaches most loved on the Riviera dei Cedri. Along the coast, the Pecora Cave opens between the Bay of Carpino and the Beach, the "Piscina" is a stretch with rocky edges similar to a large cistern and the Bay of Dragons owes its name to the rocks that recall mythological creatures.

  • Where Lungomare, Scalea
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
