What to see in Mantua

Città dei Gonzaga surrounded by the lakes of Mincio, a UNESCO World Heritage Site with Sabbioneta: the Doge's Palace with the Chamber of the Spouses of Mantegna, Palazzo Te di Giulio Romano, the Basilica of Sant'Andrea di Leon Battista Alberti, the Round of San Lorenzo and the Theatre Bibiena where young Mozart played.

33 places to visit in Mantua, in the province of Mantua (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo Ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

Official residence of Gonzaga until the 17th century: Corte Vecchia with the Studio of Isabella d'Este, Corte Nuova with the Apartment of Troy of Giulio Romano, the Rooms of Pisanello, the Flemish tapestries on cardboards of Raffaello, the hanging garden; here the Orfeo of Monteverdi was represented in 1607.

  • Where Piazza Sordello 40, Mantova
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website mantovaducale.beniculturali.it

Sources: mantovaducale.beniculturali.it · turismo.mantova.it · in-lombardia.it

Basilica di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Basilicas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

Designed by Leon Battista Alberti in 1472 and finished in 1765 with the dome of Filippo Juvarra; there is the funerary chapel of Mantegna and in the crypt the Sacred Vases, destination of the procession of Good Friday.
