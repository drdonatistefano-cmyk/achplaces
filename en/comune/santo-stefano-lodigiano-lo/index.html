What to see in Santo Stefano Lodigiano

Typical village of Lower Lodigiana crossed by bike paths in the open countryside, it houses the Museum of Toy and Child, which traces the evolution of the toy in the last three centuries.

Museo del Giocattolo e del Bambino

Museums · Themed

It offers a historical perspective on the buzzards of the past and the social value of the game in the last three centuries, with two paths: one historical, from handcrafted to romantic toy to golden age, the period between the two wars to toys of the 1950s; one thematic, on toys for girls, games of war, circus and theatre and educational toys of fun science. Open Sundays and holidays 14.30-19; groups and schools by reservation in other days.

Anello ciclabile della Bassa Lodigiana

Nature and landscape · Landscapes

From Santo Stefano Lodigiano, a village crossed by bike paths in the open countryside, starts a cycle route that touches Fombio, San Fiorano, San Rocco al Porto and Guardamiglio.
