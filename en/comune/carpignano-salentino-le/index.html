What to see in Carpignano Salentino

Borgo della Grecìa Salentina with the oldest Byzantine frescoes of Puglia in the crypt of Santa Cristina, the sixteenth-century sanctuary of the Madonna della Grotta with the nearby underground mill, the Matrice Church in Lecce stone, the large doveyard of the fifteenth century and numerous menhirs.

Cripta di Santa Cristina

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Byzantine crypt of the Madonna delle Grazie, known as Santa Cristina (IX-XI century), dug into the rock in a system of rock houses: it is the oldest church in the country and testimony of the Greek rite. It preserves the most ancient frescoes of Puglia with known date and painter, starting from the Pantocrator of 959 attributed to Teofilatto; outside a small Baroque façade.

Santuario della Madonna della Grotta

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Built in the second half of the sixteenth century on the remains of a Byzantine crypt dedicated to St. John the Baptist, linked to the legend of the apparition of the Virgin to a blind man on July 2, 1568. In the transept 16th century frescoes with the stories of the saints Catherine of Alexandria, Apollonia, Irene, Justina and Orsola; among paintings, Saints Peter and Paul and the Virgin of Purgatory of Ippolito Borghese (1601).

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Frantoio ipogeo

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Crushed mill in the rock, where oil was once produced, part of the cave system of the carpignan basin; Annibale de Capua built the abbey of Santa Maria della Grotta on the crypt of San Giovanni and a nearby underground mill.

Chiesa Matrice dell'Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt between the end of the 17th and early 18th centuries (the bell tower bears date 1709), it has facade in Lecce stone and Baroque interior: mosaic floor by Giuseppe Peluso (1885-86), high altar with relics of San Quinziano, three altars attributed to Placido Buffelli, the altar of the Madonna of Constantinople of 1594 and an unusual baptistery of 1594.

  • Where Carpignano Salentino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.carpignanosalentino.le.it

Centro storico e Palazzo Ducale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center, enclosed by remains of medieval walls, preserves the chapel of the Immaculate, the Clock Tower and portals with dates and Latin mottos; the Doge's Palace was built at the beginning of the eighteenth century by the Ghezzi dukes on the remains of the baronial palace, with the coat of arms and motto NON SIBI SED ALIIS, while Palazzo Chironi rises at the end of the nineteenth century on the site of the castle.

  • Entry Free

Torre Colombaia (Palumbaru)

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

In the district of Cacorzo, near the snowfields, dovecote of the late fifteenth century defined the largest and most severe of the Salento peninsula; on the lintel of the gate the coats of arms of Balzo and Balzo-Acquaviva.

Menhir e Trilite di Santo Stefano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Carpignano is one of the Salento countries with the greatest concentration of megalites: Menhir Grassi remains on the provincial for Melendugno, the fragment of Menhir Staurotomea ("great cross" in griko) on via di Pasulo and Chianca Santo Stefano, a trilite of three plates arranged in door, similar to northern European megalites.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.carpignanosalentino.le.it · italia.it

Serrano e cappella di Santa Marina a Stigliano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the hamlet of Serrano the parish church of San Giorgio Martire, probably fourteenth century and rebuilt in the second nineteenth century, the monument to the fallen with San Giorgio on horseback by Francesco Oronzo De Vitis and Palazzo Lubelli, residence of the barons; in Stigliano the chapel with cross of Santa Marina, of 1762, with below frescoed rock crypt.
