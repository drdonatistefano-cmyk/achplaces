What to see in Montecchio Maggiore

A city dominated by the two Scaliger castles known as Romeo and Juliet, linked to Luigi Da Porto's novella; it houses Villa Cordellina Lombardi with frescoes by Giambattista Tiepolo, the ancient quarries of the Priars and the Museum of Archaeology and Natural Sciences Zannato.

Castello della Villa (Castello di Romeo)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Erected by Cangrande II della Scala in 1354 and dismantled by the Venetians in 1514, during the war of the League of Cambrai; the walls, the mast and a tower remain. In summer it hosts theatre shows and events, and every May 1st, with the other castle, the re-enactment of Montecchio Medievale. Visits indicated on Saturday 15-18.30 and Sunday 10-12.30 and 15-18.30, in the summer.

Castello della Bellaguardia (Castello di Giulietta)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

A few hundred meters above the castle of Romeo; feud of the Pileo in the thirteenth century, passed to the Scaligeri in 1311 and was bought by the Municipality in 1742. In the restorations of the 1930s there was built a restaurant with a panoramic terrace; the statue of Romeo, located in 2016, welcomes visitors.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Villa Cordellina Lombardi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built between 1735 and 1742 on a project by Giorgio Massari for the jurist Carlo Cordellina Molin, with an ionic pronao with four columns in homage to Palladio. In 1743 Giambattista Tiepolo frescoed the living room: on the ceiling the Triumph of Intelligence on the Ignorance, on the walls the Family of Dario in front of Alessandro and the Continence of Scipione. Restored by Vittorio Lombardi between 1953 and 1956, he has been from the Province of Vicenza since 1970.

  • Where Via Lovara 36, Montecchio Maggiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.vicenzae.org

Sources: vicenzae.org

Museo di archeologia e scienze naturali G. Zannato

Museums · Archaeological

In the nineteenth-century Villino Lorenzoni, it is also the center of the Agno-Chiampo Museum System; twelve rooms, five of archaeology, from prehistory to the early Middle Ages with mainly ceramics of the Bronze and Roman Ages, and seven of natural sciences, mainly paleontology and mineralogy. Times indicated: Saturday 15-18.30, Sunday 9.30-12.30 and 15-18.30.

  • Where Piazza Guglielmo Marconi 17, Alte Ceccato, Montecchio Maggiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.vicenzae.org

Sources: comune.montecchio-maggiore.vi.it · vicenzae.org

Le Priare

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

The underground complex of ancient stone quarries, which can be visited with the guides of the Pro Loco Alte Montecchio; in summer it opens on Sunday afternoon, at Christmas it becomes the real home of Santa Claus and on Halloween it hosts themed events.
