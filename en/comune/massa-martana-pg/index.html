What to see in Massa Martana

Altomedieval castle at the foot of the Martani Mountains, along the ancient Via Flaminia: in the village the churches of San Felice and San Sebastiano; in the territory of Santa Maria in Pantano on the Vicus Martis Tudertium, the Catacomba of Villa San Faustino, the only one in Umbria, the Fonnaia Bridge and the Benedictine abbeys.

Centro storico e Piazza Umberto I

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The early medieval castrum rose on a cliff at the foot of the Martani: the walls and the large door to Piazza Umberto I, decorated with carved stones and the coat of arms of the village, with buildings of the sixteenth and eighteenth centuries. On the square the palace that housed Giuseppe and Anita Garibaldi in 1849; the Town Hall, sixteenth century and restored at the end of the nineteenth century, has an elegant bell tower.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Massa Martana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa di San Felice

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On the main square, dedicated to the patron, bishop and martyr of ancient Civitas Martana. The current building is from the Quattro-Cinquecento, restored after the bombings of the Second World War; bell tower of 1637. Single nave with barrel vault, Baroque altar with canvas by Giacinto Boccanera da Leonessa of 1723, 16th century frescoes (Crucifixion; Madonna enthroned between the Saints Rocco and Ambrogio) and fifteenth century Folignate school.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Santa Maria in Pantano e Vicus Martis Tudertium

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Among the oldest churches in Umbria, built in the VII-VIII century on a Roman building along the Via Flaminia; three-four-century façade with rose window, interior with three naves with cinerary urns, inscriptions, Roman fragments, a large Corinthian capital, an altar stone and frescoes. Around the Vicus Martis Tudertium, a village built in the state of Martis della Flaminia: about seven hectares, with well-preserved perimeter-walled buildings brought to light by excavations.

  • Entry To be checked

Catacomba di Villa San Faustino

History and architecture · Archaeology

The only Christian catacomb of Umbria, discovered in 1900 (in the 17th century it was known as "Grotte di Traiano" (Traiano Grottoes) and used between IV and V century: a straight corridor of about 22 meters enters the hill up to 7 meters deep, with four side galleries and numerous locules closed by tiles, without decorations or inscriptions. Digged in the 1940s, it was a shelter against air in war and made visitable with the works of 1996-1997.

visits only by appointment through the Tourist Office of the Municipality

Monti Martani e Martani Trekking

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Massif in the center of Umbria, about 45 km long, with grassy ridges, holm oaks, forre, dolines and caves; there live the butterfly Charaxes jasius and the Rosalia alpina, raptors, peaks and roe deer. The Martani Trekking, created in the mid-eighties by the Region, is a double ring path between ridges, woods, medieval villages and historical roads, for hikers, knights and mountain bikes.

  • Where Monti Martani, Massa Martana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it
