What to see in Sumirago

Municipality of Varesotto developed around the eighteenth-century Villa Molino, now the town hall, on the top of the hill; in the territory the rural village of Caidate and the small church of Sant'Eurosia, both between the Places of the Heart of the FAI.

Villa Molino - Palazzo Municipale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The eighteenth-century villa on the top of the hill of Sumirago, around which the village developed; among the owners the nuns of via Brera in Milan, the Sola Cabiani, the leader Bartolomeo Colleoni and, between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, the Marquises Stanga. With a rectangular plan with a rear central body and two wings, it has to the south a large arcade with arches on the neo-Gothic rustic courtyards and a large park; inside the cantinone with brick vault, the ice-cake, the hall of the coats of arms and the library in the former barn. Today it is the seat of the Town Hall and has been opened for the FAI Fall 2020 Days.

  • Where Via San Lorenzo 21, Sumirago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sumirago.va.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · comune.sumirago.va.it

Chiesetta di Sant'Eurosia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Small church with a single nave with a barrel vault and square apse, a hut façade with an ancient fresco of the saint above the portal and a pyramid cusp bell tower. Inside a wooden altar carved and gilded of the seventeenth century with the relics of the saint, the vault of the presbytery painted in starry sky with the Agnus Dei, frescoes of St. Anthony the Abbot and the Adoration of the Magi and various canvases, some in bad condition. It is among the Places of the Heart of the FAI.

  • Where Via I Maggio 21, Sumirago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sumirago.va.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · comune.sumirago.va.it

Borgo di Caidate

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Rural village surrounded by farmhouses and agricultural land, largely owned by a single family that has preserved its original architectural aspect; it has been reported several times in the census of the Places of the Heart of the FAI (2014-2022).

  • Where Caidate, Sumirago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.sumirago.va.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · comune.sumirago.va.it
