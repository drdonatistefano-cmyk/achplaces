What to see in Legnano

City of Alto Milanese with the Civic Museum Guido Sutermeister, which collects archaeology and local history in a 1928 building inspired by a fifteenth-century residence, and the Torre Colombera, a hunting casino of the Lampugnani of the mid-15th century with Renaissance frescoes.

Museo civico Guido Sutermeister

Museums · Archaeological

Recognized by the Lombardy Region in 2004, it collects the local historical and artistic material gathered by the founder Guido Sutermeister between 1925 and 1964. The 1928 building takes up the plan of a fifteenth-century summer residence, of which it reuses coffered ceilings and columns. In the porch ancient stone materials and fronts of a chimney of the 14th-XVI century; on the ground floor the section on the excavations of Parabiago; in the study the Sala Collection with Greek, Magno-Greek, Etruscan and Roman objects; in the loggia the local archaeology in chronological order; in the tower the numismatic collection. Closed on Mondays and from 1st to 31st August.

Torre Colombera

History and architecture · Towers

The Colombera is located in a Lombard courtyard between Corso Garibaldi and Via Del Gigante, near the church of San Domenico. Built in the mid-15th century as a hunting lodge of the Lampugnani, on two floors, it had frescoes of Roman history and rural life and coats of arms of the Lampugnoni, Visconti and Annoni families; the surviving fragments, noted by Guido Sutermeister in 1934, were detached and relocated together with Renaissance frescoes of wooden palaces, and today the tower also exhibits Renaissance paintings of demolished buildings in the twentieth century.
