What to see in Polino

Small mountain village of the Valnerina ternana at about 830 meters, already dominion of the Castelli Signori di Terni, collected around the fortress of the twelfth century that houses the Museo-Laboratory of the Umbro Apennines; from the village start the paths towards Monte Petano and Monte La Pelosa.

Museo-laboratorio dell'Appennino Umbro «Sotto-Sopra»

Museums · Science and university

Set up inside the fortress, in the center of Polino, it tells with interactive multimedia models and systems the evolutionary history of Umbria, from geology and paleontology to natural sciences: carbonate rocks, fossils of ammonites and formation of the Umbrian mountains. It has an educational workshop on the ammonites and the information point • The window on the Black • • with fossil extraction activities in a small nearby quarry.

Rocca di Polino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress of the twelfth century on a rocky spur, with a polygonal plan with two cylindrical towers on opposite sides; it was protected by a double circle of walls, of which visible traces remain.

Fontana monumentale

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Built in 1615 by the Marquis Giambattista Castelli, it is a work of late Mannerism decorated with heads of lion and mythological figures; fed by mountain springs, it is the meeting point of the inhabitants.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Recently restored, it preserves elements of the seventeenth century: baptistery, holy water and altar of St. Joseph with the altarpiece of the Death of St. Joseph. A plaque recalls the donation of the body of Valentine by Cardinal Orsini to the Castelli family.

  • Entry Free

Monte Petano

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the summit the panorama ranges from Valnerina to Greccio, to Lake Piediluco, to Rieti and Leonessa; numerous hiking trails and, in winter, a small ski area with a cross-country trail and some descents for beginners.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it

Trekking da Polino al Monte La Pelosa

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Itinerary that touches Colle Forgiara, with marine fossils and spring blooms of gentian, tulips and peonies, the village of Colle Bertone with fossils of lilies and sea urchins, and Mount La Pelosa, the highest peak in the province of Terni, from which you can see Valnerina, Sibillini, Monti della Laga and Gran Sasso; in the area roe deer, foxes, squirrels and many species of birds.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
