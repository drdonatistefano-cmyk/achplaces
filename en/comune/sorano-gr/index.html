What to see in Sorano

Fortified village on a tuff cliff dominated by the Fortress Orsini, with Etruscan and medieval Sovana in its territory.

4 places to visit in Sorano, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 3 of the 6 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Fortezza Orsini e Museo del Medioevo e del Rinascimento

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The fortress built by the Aldobrandeschi and then by the Orsini, home to the Museum of the Middle Ages and the Renaissance.

  • Where Sorano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Masso Leopoldino e Collegiata di San Nicola

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The ancient defensive outpost on the opposite side of the village; in the Collegiate Church of St. Nicholas a travertine source of 1563.

  • Where Sorano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Parco Archeologico Città del Tufo e necropoli di Sovana

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The park with the monumental Tomb Ildebranda, the Tomb of the winged demon, the rocky settlement of San Rocco and the hollow streets; in Sovana also the Cathedral of San Pietro.

  • Where Località La Fratta 2, Sorano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Terme di Sorano e Bagno dei Frati

Local life and traditions · Spas · Paid entry

The spa with historical swimming pools like the fifteenth century Bagno dei Frati.

  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
