What to see in Asolo

Village with a medieval profile on the hills of Treviso, dominated by the Rocca on Mount Rich, linked to the queen of Cyprus Caterina Cornaro, Eleonora Duse and Freya Stark; in the center porch the Duomo with works of Lorenzo Lotto and the Civic Museum in the Loggia della Ragione.

Museo civico di Asolo

Museums · City history · Paid entry

It is located in the Palazzo del Vescovado and in the Loggia della Ragione, frescoed around the middle of the sixteenth century; born from the legacy of Andrea Manera and Pacifico Scomazzetto, it has an archaeological section, a gallery from the fifteenth century to today with works by Luca Giordano, Bernardo Strozzi and Bernardo Bellotto, the treasure of the Duomo and the sections dedicated to Caterina Cornaro, Eleonora Duse and Freya Stark.

Rocca di Asolo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress built between the end of the XII and the beginning of the XIII century on the top of Mount Rich, 310 meters, as military garrison of the city; you reach from Porta Colmarion with 276 steps and from the stands the look goes from the Dolomites to the Venetian plain. It often hosts cultural events and costume reenactments.

  • Where Monte Ricco, Asolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Duomo di Asolo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Rebuilt several times, it preserves the Romanesque structure of the façade; inside the Assunta by Lorenzo Lotto (1506) and an Assunta by Jacopo da Bassano.

  • Where Asolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Piazza Garibaldi e centro storico porticato

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The central square has an ancient fountain with the lion of San Marco; around streets porticoed with cafés and taverns, the Loggia della Ragione with the fresco of Contarini, Palazzo Polo with the triphores, the Zen fountain of 1571 and the fifteenth-century frescoed palaces of Via Regina Cornaro.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Asolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Castello della Regina e Teatro Duse

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Remains of the castle of Caterina Cornaro with the Civic Tower and the smallest Reata tower; in the spaces of the castle since 1798 there is the theatre, rebuilt in 1857, today the Duse Theatre with 341 seats.

the theatre is open only for events (theatrical seasons, concerts)

  • Where Via Regina Cornaro, Asolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · comune.asolo.tv.it · italia.it

Giardino di Villa Freya e teatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology

House of writer and traveler Freya Stark (1893-1993), who transformed the garden into a botanical garden with plants collected during the trips; in the park the remains of the Roman theatre of ancient Acelum, discovered in 1879 and dug in the nineties by the University of Padua; restoration completed in 2012.

visit only with guided tours

  • Where Via Marconi 137, Asolo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it
