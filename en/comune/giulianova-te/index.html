What to see in Giulianova

The fifteenth-century town of Acquaviva on the hill, with the octagonal Cathedral of San Flaviano, and the nineteenth-century Lido with the fishing port and the ceramic promenade of Castelli.

8 places to visit in Giulianova, in the province of Teramo (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Giulianova in the guide with map and filters

Duomo di San Flaviano

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

Wanted in the second half of the fifteenth century by Duke Giulio Antonio Acquaviva d'Aragona: octagonal plant and dome on drum, originally covered with blue majolica visible from the coast; it holds the silver urn of the patron and the reliquary arm of San Biagio di Bartolomeo di Paolo da Teramo, of the fourteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Bruno Buozzi, Giulianova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Santa Maria a Mare

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The oldest church of Giulianova, dating back to the 12th century, with terracotta façade and a portal of the 14th century with eighteen engraved tiles.

  • Where Giulianova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
