What to see in Caravaggio

Borgo della piania bergamasca, 26 km from Bergamo, known for the Sanctuary of Santa Maria del Fonte, was built where in 1432 Our Lady appeared to a woman of the people and half of more than two million pilgrims a year; in the village the Gothic church of Saints Fermo and Rustic, the monastery of San Bernardino with the Passion cycle of Fermo Stella and Palazzo Gallavresi with the Pinacoteca civica.

5 places to visit in Caravaggio, in the province of Bergamo (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario di Santa Maria del Fonte

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

It rises where in 1432 Our Lady appeared to a young woman and where a clear spring of water sprang up, which is located under the high altar and feeds two large pools in the entrance square, surrounded by porticoes for the shelter of pilgrims. The church, with a single nave and Latin cross in classic style, was started in the sixteenth century; the reconstruction is attributed to Pellegrino Tibaldi. He preserves works by Juvara, Cavenaghi, Moriggia and Cavedoni; the most heartfelt festival is the 26th of May, the anniversary of the apparition.

  • Where Viale Papa Giovanni XXIII 10, Caravaggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: visitbergamo.net · in-lombardia.it

Chiesa dei Santi Fermo e Rustico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the 13th century, probably on a pre-existing building, it is considered the oldest cult building in the village; the façade follows the canons of the Lombard Gothic.

  • Where Piazza Santi Fermo e Rustico 6, Caravaggio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: in-lombardia.it · visitbergamo.net

Monastero di San Bernardino

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Paid entry

Dedicated to Saint Bernardino da Siena, it includes the church of San Bernardino and the convent of Santa Maria degli Angeli. The church has a unique nave with three small polygonal chapels on the left: in the first, dedicated to the Madonna, frescoes attributed to the Treviso Bernardo Zenale and Bernardino Butinone; on the tramezzo the cycle of the Passion of Fermo Stella, recently restored. Open Monday to Friday 9-17; guided tours by reservation.

  • Where Viale Giovanni XXIII, Caravaggio
  • Entry €5, free under 18 and people with disabilities
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: visitbergamo.net

Palazzo Gallavresi e Pinacoteca civica

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

A noble residence from the 16th century, named after the last owner, today a town hall: Giampaolo Sforza, the first Marquis and son of Moro, lived there, witness of the wedding of Michelangelo Merisi's parents. In the façade tombstones to Vittorio Emanuele II, Garibaldi, Cavour and Mazzini next to ancient openings to Gothic arch; on the noble floor the Pinacoteca civica with works by painters of the Five and seventeenth centuries such as Moietta and Procaccini and paintings of the romantic Moriggia. Visits by reservation.

visits only by reservation (Visit Bergamo)

  • Where Piazza Giuseppe Garibaldi 1, Caravaggio
  • Entry €5, reduced €3 (under 18 and over 65), free up to 6 years
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: visitbergamo.net

Riserva naturale Fontanile Brancaleone

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Reserve of about 10 hectares on the border between Caravaggio and Pagazzano, among woods, cultivated, water mirrors and marshy vegetation, around one of the oldest springs of the area. There live frogs, European mole and foxes and especially many birds, from heron ashes to kingfisher and barbarian. You visit only along the marked routes.

access only with permission of the managing body; groups (max 30) accompanied by staff of the institution (Visit Bergamo)

  • Where Caravaggio (BG)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitbergamo.net

Sources: visitbergamo.net · in-lombardia.it
