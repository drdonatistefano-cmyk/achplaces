What to see in Este

Centre of culture of the ancient Venetians at the foot of the Euganean Hills, Este is surrounded by over a kilometer of walls and towers of the Carrara castle, with public gardens inside; in the city the National Museum Atestino in Palazzo Mocenigo, the Cathedral of Santa Tecla with the altarpiece of Tiepolo, the Basilica of Santa Maria delle Grazie, the Romanesque San Martino and Piazza Maggiore. Byron and Shelley stayed there.

Museo Nazionale Atestino

Museums · Archaeological

In the sixteenth century Palazzo Mocenigo, which includes part of the fourteenth-century walls of the Carrara castle, exhibits protohistoric finds of the Iron Age and Romans from the excavations begun in the second half of the nineteenth century in Este, Lozzo, Arquà and in Lower Padua, in a chronological and thematic path (daily life, cults, funeral rites) that closes with a small medieval and modern section. Among the main pieces the Situla Welcome, symbol of ancient Venetians, the funeral set of the noblewoman Nerka Trostiaia, a gold medallion coined under Augustus in 2 BC and the Madonna with Child of Cima da Conegliano (1504). Hours: Monday 8.30-13.30, Tuesday to Saturday 8.30-19.30, Sunday 14.30-19.30.

Castello Carrarese e Giardini del Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The first traces of the castrum date back to about 1115, with the Estensi, which enlarged it with curtains and residences; destroyed by Ezzelino III by Romano in 1249 and the Paduans in 1294, was rebuilt by Ubertino da Carrara from 1339 and remained at the Carraresi until the beginning of the fifteenth century. Today there are large towers and a wall of more than a kilometer, with the Rocca del Soccorso in the north-east, ancient entrance of the fortress, and in the mast the remains of a small church; around 1570 the Mocenigo built on the south side their palace, today home of the museum. Property of the Municipality since 1887, contains public gardens open in 1915, with trees and centuries-old plants; open every day 9-17.30.

Chiesa di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
