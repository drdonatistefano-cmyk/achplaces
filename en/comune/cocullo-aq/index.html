What to see in Cocullo

Village of the upper Sagittarius Valley, between Valle Peligna and Marsica, fortified in the 12th century and known as the town of snakes for the Serpari Rite: every May 1st the statue of San Domenico Abate parades for the village covered with live snakes. To see the historic center with the district of San Nicola and its medieval tower, the Sanctuary of San Domenico, the church of Santa Maria delle Grazie and the museum-center studies dedicated to popular traditions.

Centro storico e rione San Nicola

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The highest part of the village is one of the oldest and best preserved, with cobbled streets, churches and ancient houses; the village was fortified in the twelfth century and the oldest nucleus preserves the defensive system in the path of roads and buildings. At the highest point stands the bell tower of St. Nicholas, originally a medieval tower of the 12th century of sighting and defence in large blocks of stone; the church of St. Nicholas, cited in documents of the 14th century and damaged by the earthquake of 1915, has on the façade the coat of arms of the legendary Duke Sarchia. The procession of the Saint goes up to the top of the village on May 1.

Santuario di San Domenico

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Recognizable for the large drum dome and the pyramidal bell tower, the sanctuary was almost entirely rebuilt in the twentieth century on the site of a more ancient church dedicated to the Saint from the seventeenth century, when he became patron saint of Cocullo. Here the most heard rituals of the feast take place: the faithful take stone from behind the altar to spread around houses and fields and pull with their teeth the rope of the bell against the toothache; after the mass the statue is decorated with live golds and snakes and carried in procession.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: icpi.cultura.gov.it · italia.it · abruzzoturismo.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the XIII century in Madonna delle Grazie square; after the earthquake of 2009 it houses the statue of San Domenico di Sora and the celebrations of the patronal feast of May 1.

  • Where Piazza Madonna delle Grazie, Cocullo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.cocullo.aq.it

Museo civico etnografico delle tradizioni popolari «Alfonso Maria di Nola»

Museums · Themed

The museum and documentation centre, housed in the premises adjacent to the Town Hall, contains testimonies and images of the feast of San Domenico and the Serpari. The Study Centre has a large library dedicated to the most popular events in Abruzzo and organizes educational activities for schools and visitors.
