What to see in Aquilonia

Centre of Alta Irpinia 750 meters, called Carbonara until 1861: less than 2 km from the village the Archaeological Park preserves the ancient center abandoned after the earthquake of 1930, while the ethnographic Museum "Beniamino Tartaglia" exhibits about 14,000 objects of the Altirpina life.

Museo etnografico «Beniamino Tartaglia» (MEdA)

Museums · Themed

Designed by Prof. Beniamino Tartaglia with the community of Aquilonia, reconstructs houses, stables and workplaces of the Altarpine world: about 14,000 original objects on 1,500 m2, in over 130 environments divided into 12 sections (masters such as barbershop, blacksmith, shoemaker, sawmill and basketmaker, feeding, traditions, archaeology, protoindustry), plus an outdoor area with vintage agricultural machinery. Guided tours of about 90 minutes, only by reservation (tel. 0827 83826).

  • Where Via Carbonara 3, Aquilonia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.museomeda.it

Sources: museomeda.it · cultura.gov.it · touringclub.it

Parco archeologico del centro antico di Carbonara

History and architecture · Abandoned villages

Less than 2 km from the village, it preserves the medieval urban fabric of ancient Carbonara, destroyed by the earthquake of 1930 and restored in a decade on a project by the architect Donato Tartaglia: Piazza Municipio with the pavement, the churches of San Giovanni and of the Immaculate, town hall, pretura, Monte Frumentario and prisons, remains of palaces and a castle. Nearby there is a baroque fountain-washer of the eighteenth century, unique because it develops on two levels.

  • Where Località Carbonara, Aquilonia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: museomeda.it · touringclub.it · cultura.gov.it

Badia di San Vito

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

In the open countryside about 2 km from the centre, it was born from the Benedictine monastery of San Leonardo de Carbonaria of the 12th century, mentioned with certainty from 1269 and stop of the Via Angeina towards Puglia; it became a Badia Regia in 1790, was restored in the mid-nineteenth century and received the three-ordered bell tower with pyramid spire in 1871. Outside there is a centuries-old oak inscribed among the monumental trees of Campania; the festival is June 15th.

  • Where Località San Vito, Aquilonia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: museomeda.it · cultura.gov.it
