What to see in Prarostino

The hillside town of the Waldensian valleys, with the capital of San Bartolomeo: here rise the Lighthouse of Freedom of Gabetti and Isola, monument to the partisans of the Pinerolese valleys, the 19th century Waldensian temple and the Museum of Viticulture; isolated in hamlet Rostagni, the temple of Roccapiatta linked to the Waldensian resistance of 1686.

5 places to visit in Prarostino, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Faro della Libertà

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Inaugurated on June 18, 1967 in honor of the 600 partisans of the 51 municipalities of the Pinerolese valleys that fell into the Liberation, it was designed by Roberto Gabetti and Aimaro Isola: a 15-metre-high stone hollow tower, with a ladder that rises to a 360° panoramic terrace on the Western Alps and the plain. Here in 1943 the first armed group of the Pinerolese was formed; on 17 October 1943 about 500 German soldiers surrounded St. Bartholomew. Free access to the external structure.

  • Where Piazza della Libertà, accanto al Municipio, Prarostino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.prarostino.to.it

Sources: comune.prarostino.to.it

Tempio valdese di San Bartolomeo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A place of Waldensian worship has been documented since 1692, a hut with straw roof replaced by loose in 1724; after long disputes the current temple was built in 1828-29. Inside wooden floor, gallery raised on three sides, pulpit and pipe organ in front of the entrance and semicircle benches.

  • Where Via Ernesto Monnet 4, Prarostino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.prarostino.to.it

Sources: comune.prarostino.to.it

Tempio valdese di Roccapiatta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Probably built in 1592-94 during the occupation of Huguenot commander Lesdiguières, on April 12, 1686 the delegates of the valleys led by Henry Arnaud, who decided to resist the armed decree of Vittorio Amedeo II, destroyed the ducal troops. Rebuilt in 1700 and reopened in 1756, it has a stone floor with inscriptions of foreign Protestant officers at the service of the Savoy.

  • Where Borgata Rostagni, Prarostino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.prarostino.to.it

Sources: comune.prarostino.to.it

Museo della Viticoltura

Museums · Themed

A small museum that with objects, tools, photographs and drawings tells the life of the winemakers of the foothills: the path follows the cycle of the vine, from undoing to grafting, pruning and harvesting; a second section is dedicated to the vinification and preservation of wine.

  • Where Piazza della Libertà, San Bartolomeo, Prarostino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Giro del Faro

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

16.4 km ring with 560 m of altitude difference (difficulty MC+BC) starting from the Lighthouse of Liberty and touching the woods of beech and chestnut trees, the temple of Roccapiatta, the villages overlooking the Chisone valley, Godini (1000 m, highest point), the Didactic Wood, the panoramic Roc Coubalesine and the Cippo dei Martiri del Bric, to close the Museum of Viticulture.

  • Where Partenza da Piazza della Libertà, Prarostino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page comune.prarostino.to.it

Sources: comune.prarostino.to.it
