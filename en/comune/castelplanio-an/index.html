What to see in Castelplanio

The village of Vallesina refounded after the destruction of the fifteenth century with two walls: in the Town Hall, formerly residence of the Counts Fossa Mancini, the civic museum is located; the church of San Sebastiano retains a bust attributed to Mattia della Robbia, and the Path of the Black Crab goes down to the hamlet Macine.

Museo civico di Palazzo Fossa Mancini

Museums · City history

The civic museum is located in the Town Hall, the palace which was the private residence of Count Fossa Mancini.

Chiesa di San Sebastiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church designed by the architect Mattia Capponi: it preserves a bust of San Sebastiano in polychrome terracotta of the sixteenth century attributed to Mattia della Robbia, a Crucifixion on canvas by Claudio Ridolfi and a golden wooden statue of St. Joseph. It once hosted a polyptych by Lorenzo Lotto, whose surviving panels are now in the State Museums of Berlin.

  • Entry To be checked

Chiesa della Madonna del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the hamlet Piagge, where every year on Sunday after July 16 is celebrated the feast of Our Lady, called "day of the consumed" because it fell at the end of a hard work in the fields.

  • Where Frazione Piagge, Castelplanio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sentiero del Granchio Nero

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Walking or cycling trail of about 5 km, with 200 meters of altitude difference, from the village Macine to Castelplanio; it takes its name from the crab that lives in a ditch along the path.

  • Where Frazione Macine - Castelplanio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it
