What to see in Sabaudia

A city founded in 1933, symbol of Rationalism, overlooking the lake of Paola in the heart of the Circeo National Park: more than 20 km of white beach and dunes, the sixteenth-century tower of Paola and the ruins of the great villa of the Emperor Domitian.

10 places to visit in Sabaudia, in the province of Latina (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parco archeologico della Villa di Domiziano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the eastern shore of the lake, about 46 hectares of the villa of the Emperor Domitian (1st century AD): spa area with quadriportico in polychrome marble, gym and latrine with marble seats, the large cisterns (of the Eco and the Navi, with graffiti of boats) and the porticoed fish pond. From here come the Apollo today in Kassel and the Faun of the Vatican Museums. Visit on request on weekdays and in extraordinary openings with booking.

  • Where Strada Palazzo, Sabaudia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: direzioneregionalemuseilazio.cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Lago di Paola

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Coastal Lake of the Circeo National Park, of biological, naturalistic and archaeological interest, inhabited by herons and garzettes: calm waters for kayak and stand up paddle, boat trips, nature trails and birdwatching.

  • Where Sabaudia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Dune e spiaggia di Sabaudia

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

More than 20 km of white beach under the dunes covered with vegetation and Mediterranean maquis, with long stretches of free beach reached by wooden walkways; Sabaudia has obtained the Blue Flag several times. The dunes appear in Alberto Sordi's film "Amore mio assistimi" (1969) with Monica Vitti.

  • Where Lungomare, Sabaudia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Torre Paola

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

16th century coastal tower built against Saracen raids, at the entrance of the lake of Paola, surrounded by water between sea and dunes, with nature trails that connect the lake, dunes and sea.

  • Where Strada Lungomare Pontino 103, Sabaudia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Piazza del Comune e Torre civica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The heart of the city of foundation of 1933, the city of Rationalism is: the piazza porticata of the Town Hall with the civic tower, reference for the whole territory, and around the House of the Fighter, the after-work and the covered market.

  • Where Piazza del Comune, Sabaudia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · parcocirceo.it · italia.it

Palazzo delle Poste

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The work of Angiolo Mazzoni, the most interesting architectural building in Sabaudia: almost entirely covered with blue mosaic tiles, with large windows framed with red marble of Siena.

  • Where Sabaudia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Chiesa della Santissima Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Inaugurated in 1935, with the porch façade decorated with the mosaic of the Annunciation and the bell tower of 45 meters; inside the royal chapel donated by Savoia house, originally to Palazzo Margherita in Rome.

  • Where Sabaudia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcocirceo.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Santuario di Santa Maria della Sorresca

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the shores of the lake, on the remains of a Roman villa of the first century AD: built by the Benedictines in the sixth century, it preserves the wooden statue of the Madonna with Child of the thirteenth century, celebrated on Monday after Pentecost; the bell tower also served as a watchtower.

  • Where Lago di Paola, Sabaudia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcocirceo.it · italia.it

Museo del Mare e della Costa «Marcello Zei»

Museums · Themed

Fossil of the ancient Tyrrhenian Sea, Neolithic instruments and Roman finds, with audiovisual and photographic material on the history of the territory; open Monday to Saturday in the afternoon.

  • Where Sabaudia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parcocirceo.it · italia.it

Museo Emilio Greco

Museums · Modern art

On the ground floor of the Palazzo Comunale, more than one hundred works by the sculptor from 1947 to 1984 between sculptures, prints, engravings, coins and medals, including the Central Gate of the Cathedral of Orvieto and the Grande Bagnante n. 1, with the artist's private archive.

  • Where Via Umberto I, Sabaudia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.sabaudia.lt.it · italia.it
