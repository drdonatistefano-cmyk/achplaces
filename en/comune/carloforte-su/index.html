What to see in Carloforte

The only center of the island of San Pietro, founded in 1738 by Ligurian families arrived from Tabarka and included among the most beautiful villages in Italy: caruggi and eighteenth-century forts, the Civic Museum Casa del Duca in the oldest fort, the Madonna dello Schiavo and, outside the village, Capo Sandalo, Cala Fico, Le Colonne and La Conca.

Centro storico e caruggi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center is divided into the districts Castello, Calcinate and Cassebba, with houses mostly whitewashed joined by alleys (caruggi) and stairs. The arch of via Solferino, the most visited place of the village, was the entrance of Casa Porcile, one of the most important families of the colony; in the village also Palazzo Rombi, eighteenth-century residence of the French consul, and the promenade where fishermen still shelter the nets.

  • Where Centro storico, Carloforte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Museo civico Casa del Duca

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In the fort Carlo Emanuele III, erected in the spring of 1738: the first building in masonry of the village, first guard and then prison. Six rooms and a garden: the trap room with tools and plastic, that of the gallantrymen (the boatmen carrying the ore), documents of the first colonization, a malacological collection, the peasant tools and the paintings of Mario Emanuelli.

  • Where Via Cisterna del Re, Carloforte
  • Entry €2, reduced €1,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Porta del Leone e forti

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Lion Gate, which takes its name from a feline head that became the symbol of the maestral, is part of the walls built in the early nineteenth century against the barbarisc pirates. There are still sighting towers and stretches of belt; from Fort Santa Cristina you can see the roofs of the village and the nearby ponds, while Fort Santa Teresa is today a private residence.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Torre San Vittorio

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

Strong in blocks of trachyte built in 1767 to protect the entrance of the port, in the area of Spalmadureddu; in 1898 it was converted into an astronomical observatory and since 2016 it houses a multimedia museum on the history of Carlofort.

Monumento a Carlo Emanuele III

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Marble group of the Genoese Bernardo Mantero on the promenade, erected as a sign of gratitude to the king who granted the island to the tabarchins: the central statue was inaugurated on 16 July 1786, the two lateral additions in 1788 for the fifty years of the foundation.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa della Madonna dello Schiavo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Neoclassical church started on October 15, 1807 and completed in 1815 to accommodate a small wooden statue, perhaps the polena of a ship, found in 1800 near Tunis by a carlofortino reduced to slavery after the raid corsair of 1798 and brought back home to the ransom of 1803. The statue is venerated as a protector of tabarchins.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa dei Novelli Innocenti

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

At the top of the Fontane hill, in a pine forest with rare Aleppo pines: tradition ties her to the shipwreck of ships of children headed to the Holy Land. Rebuilt in trachite from 1742, enlarged at the end of the eighteenth century by the Porcile family and restored in 1928; a single nave with barrel vault, tombstones of the Porcile, traces of fresco and late Piedmontese Baroque façade.

opens during the feast of St. Peter at the end of June and on special occasions

Capo Sandalo

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Overhanging promontory on the westernmost tip of the island, dominated by a lighthouse of 1864 whose summit is 138 meters above the sea, with a lantern visible up to 28 miles. On the cliffs he nidifies the queen's hawk; in front is the island of the Horn. You go down to the sea with steep paths: the source recommends the boat trip.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Cala Fico

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Narrow and deep limestone creek ending in a small pebble beach between high cliffs, with clear water suitable for snorkeling. It is a nature reserve managed by LIPU, nesting site of the queen's hawk, with birdwatching routes.

Le Colonne

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural monument formed by two pillars of dark volcanic rock in the south of the island, symbol of Carloforte: 16 meters high each, after the tides of November 2013 of one remains only the base. Around crags, caves and mouth of an inactive geyser.

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Isola Piana

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Isolotto of the municipality in front of San Pietro, historical seat of the trap: granted to Don Francesco Pes at the end of the seventeenth century and renewed in 1898, the fishing ceased in 1964 and the village of the tunarotti is now a residence. No cars, motorcycles and bicycles: it is a reserve where they nest the queen's hawk, royal seagull and friar.
