What to see in Fossato di Vico

Municipality in the Regional Park of Monte Cucco, with one part on the floor along the Flaminia and the medieval village of Fossato Alto: the old Town Hall with the clock tower, the covered street of the Rughe of the thirteenth century, churches with frescoes of Ottaviano Nelli and the Eugubina school; in the surroundings two Roman bridges on the Flaminia.

Fossato Alto

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval village in the hills, distinguished from the lower part along the Flaminia state road, preserves the Town Hall, the clock tower, a crenellated tower above the entrance door, the wrinkles and ancient places of worship.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Le Rughe

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

A road covered by stone vaults on the inner side of the western walls, where the portam castri opens: a rare example of castle architecture of the thirteenth century, with a defensive function.

  • Where Fossato Alto, Fossato di Vico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Vecchio Palazzo comunale e torre

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

In the Middle Ages the palace was the castle gate and the tower was the house annexed with the accommodation of the guards; the tower appears in the documents in the middle of the thirteenth century, the brick battlements are after the fourteenth century and the last door is dated 1536 (it remains the Gothic arch with its hinges and the key is preserved). A coat of arms on the tower recalls the domination of Perugia; there is also the clock tower of the brothers Gricci.

  • Where Fossato Alto, Fossato di Vico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Benedetto

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Benedictine Abbey of the thirteenth century just outside the walls, today a national monument; a stone inscription shows the date 1337. Two ogival portals, trilobed monofora, central nave with round arches; remain part of the frescoes of the 14th-XV century of the Eugubina school, including perhaps the oldest portrait of Pope Urban V, attributed to Matteo da Gualdo.

  • Where Via San Benedetto, Fossato di Vico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it
