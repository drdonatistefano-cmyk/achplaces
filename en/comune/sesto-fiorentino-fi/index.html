Tomba etrusca della Montagnola

History and architecture · Archaeology

Tomb to tholos of the VII century BC in blocks of alberese, with dromos, cells and false dome chamber of 5 meters: one of the most important orientalizing monuments of the northern Etruria.

  • Where Via Fratelli Rosselli, Quinto, Sesto Fiorentino
  • Entry To be checked: openings with Pro Loco on reservation
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · destinationflorence.com

Museo Ginori della Manifattura di Doccia

Museums · Themed

The porcelain museum founded by Carlo Ginori in 1737.

In restoration, waiting to reopen; the archive consults by appointment

  • Where Sesto Fiorentino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com
