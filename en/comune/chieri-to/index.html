What to see in Chieri

The city of the hundred towers in the hills east of Turin, a textile city from the fifteenth century: the great Gothic Cathedral, the triumphal arch of the late sixteenth century, the Jewish ghetto and, in Pession, the Martini museum of enology.

7 places to visit in Chieri, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Chieri in the guide with map and filters

Duomo di Santa Maria della Scala

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built at the behest of the Bishop of Turin Landolfo around 1037, it is considered one of the major buildings of the Piedmont Gothic; inside the Gallieri Chapel.

  • Where Piazzetta Santa Lucia 1, Chieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org · turismochieri.it

Arco di trionfo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Monument of the end of the sixteenth century visible from Piazza Umberto I, between medieval palaces and Baroque churches of the center.

  • Where Piazza Umberto I, Chieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismochieri.it · turismotorino.org

Ghetto ebraico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient Jewish quarter, with frescoed ceilings and lunettes decorated above the windows.

  • Where Centro storico, Chieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismochieri.it · turismotorino.org

Museo del Tessile
