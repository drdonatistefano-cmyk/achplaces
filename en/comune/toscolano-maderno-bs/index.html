What to see in Toscolano-Maderno

Municipality of the Brescian shore of Garda born in 1928 from the fusion of Toscolano and Maderno, between the Valley of the Cartiere, the Romanesque Basilica of Sant'Andrea, the Roman villa of the Nonii Arrii and the botanical garden Ghirardi, inside the Regional Park of Alto Garda Bresciano.

7 places to visit in Toscolano-Maderno, in the province of Brescia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Villa romana dei Nonii Arrii

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Built in the first century AD and remained in use until the beginning of the V, it was one of the most important Roman residential buildings on Garda, belonged to the Nonii of Brescia (probably to Marco Nonio Macrino, console in 154). The visitable sector preserves environments with pavement mosaics and remains of parietal paintings; further north there was a basin-fountain about 47 meters long. Open on Saturday and Sunday from May to September, 10-12 and 15-18.

  • Where Piazzale Santa Maria del Benaco, Toscolano-Maderno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitbrescia.it · italia.it

Museo della Carta

Museums · Themed

In an ancient restored paper mill (the Lower Maina) in the Valley of the Cartiere, with sixteenth-century nucleus: it tells the production of paper from the Middle Ages to the twentieth century, with the first printed books in Toscolano in the sixteenth century and demonstrations of paper handmade by former papermakers. It is reached on foot in about ten minutes from the car park; in 2025 open every day 10-18 from 29 March to 12 October.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.valledellecartiere.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: valledellecartiere.it · italia.it · visitbrescia.it

Valle delle Cartiere

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valley of the Toscolano stream where it is made paper from the Middle Ages: in the Quattro-Cinquecento it was the main paper centre of the Republic of Venice and the last paper mill closed in 1962. The paths pass through the ruins of the paper mills and descend to the Greto; the most spectacular point is the walkway on the gorge of the Covoli.

  • Where Via Valle delle Cartiere, Toscolano-Maderno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it

Basilica di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Documented since 1040, the present building dates back to the 12th century: an example of Lombard Romanesque with Verona influences, with a polychrome marble facade and a portal carved with plant, animal and mermaid motifs. In the crypt, a sarcophagus guards the remains of Saint Herculaneum; inside a Madonna by Paolo Veneziano.

  • Where Piazza San Marco, Maderno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitbrescia.it · italia.it

Orto botanico G.E. Ghirardi

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Free entry

Orto dell'Università Statale di Milano was born for the study of medicinal plants: today it hosts more than 600 species, with thematic routes to follow independently. Open from mid-April to mid-October with seasonal hours (in winter only by appointment); guided tours and paid workshops.

  • Where Via Religione 25, Toscolano-Maderno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: ortibotanici.unimi.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Monte Pizzocolo e Santuario di Supina

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Regional Park of Alto Garda Bresciano surrounds the village and is a trekking destination: between the itineraries Mount Pizzocolo, Mount Castello di Gaino and the panoramic Sanctuary of Supina; the Way of St. Herculaneum touches all the churches and chapels of the territory.

  • Where Toscolano-Maderno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
