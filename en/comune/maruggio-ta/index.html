What to see in Maruggio

Borgo della costa Ionica, feud of the Knights of Malta, with the historic center of Sciangài, the Mother Church in carparo and the Palazzo del Commendatore; on the coast the dunes of Campomarino and the Tower of the Ovo.

8 places to visit in Maruggio, in the province of Taranto (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico «Sciangài»

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Once called Terra Murata because it is enclosed in the medieval walls, it is a maze of narrow and winding alleys with ancient whitewashed houses and Renaissance balconies.

  • Where Centro storico, Maruggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa Madre della Natività di Maria Vergine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built by the Knights of Malta in carparo between the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, in Renaissance forms; inside paintings by the Bianchi brothers, painters of Manduria, a wooden crucifix of the seventeenth century and the Chapel of the Blessed Sacrament.

  • Where Centro storico, Maruggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Palazzo del Commendatore

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

It overlooks the main square, where the Porta Grande stood; wanted by the Knights of Malta, several times renovated, it preserves small mills on the ground floor.

  • Where Piazza principale, Maruggio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Ex Convento dei Frati Minori Osservanti (Municipio)

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Today, the town hall has a cloister with a portico of twenty arches on octagonal pillars and 28 frescoed lunettes in the seventeenth century.

  • Where Maruggio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni fuori le mura

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built by the Knights of Malta in honor of their patron, with a small hospital annexed to it today disappeared; according to legend it was commissioned together with the church of the Madonna del Verde.

  • Where Maruggio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Dune di Campomarino

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Site of Community Interest: dunes formed between 7,500 and 3,500 years ago, among the best preserved in Italy, up to 12 meters tall, with Mediterranean juniper maquis, sparto, lentisco, rosemary and thyme.

  • Where Campomarino, Maruggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Spiagge di Campomarino e porto turistico

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 9 km of mainly sandy beaches in the stretch called "mare" of the seven colors. Campomarino, 2 km from the center, is a twentieth-century seaside resort with the largest marina on the Ionian-Salentine coast. Transparent and low water for several meters from the shore.

  • Where Campomarino, Maruggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Torre dell'Ovo

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the three coastal towers erected in 1473 against the Saracen raiders: 14 meters high, rises on a cliff; from the shore emerge the remains of a pier used between the 5th century BC and the III AD.

  • Where Torre Ovo, Maruggio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it
