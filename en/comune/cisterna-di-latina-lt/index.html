What to see in Cisterna di Latina

On the edge of the Agro Pontino along the Via Appia, the ancient Tres Tabernae where St. Paul stood was the center of the Caetani fief and was almost destroyed in 1944; today it offers Palazzo Caetani with its caves, the Biondi Fountain in Piazza XIX Marzo and, in its territory, the Garden of Ninfa, a natural monument among the ruins of a medieval city.

Giardino di Ninfa

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

Eight hectares with about 1,300 plant species, a pond and streams among the ruins of the medieval town of Ninfa: castle, walls, churches and a restored town hall. Ninfa, bought by the Caetans in 1298, was destroyed under Barbarossa and again in 1382 and then abandoned; since 1921 Gelasio, Roffredo Caetani and Marguerite Chapin transformed it into a romantic garden, to which Lelia Caetani gave the present form. Regional natural monument since 2000, is managed by the Caetani Foundation; you can visit with guided tours from March to November according to annual calendar, with mandatory booking on gardendininfa.eu (save 10% for FAI subscribers).

Palazzo Caetani

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built by Duke Bonifacio Caetani in 1560 incorporating the medieval fortress of the Frangipane, on a project by Francesco da Volterra, he lost in the Second World War the oldest part with the rooms painted by Girolamo Siciolante, Stefano Dupérac and perhaps the Zuccari brothers. The Zuccari Room remains with the large fresco of the Caetani's pontoons in the eighteenth century, the trompe-l'oeil of the Loggia Room, the court porticoed with a Roman well and access to the Torre Frangipane, and under the building a network of caves about fifteen meters deep where at Christmas the crib is set up. It hosts exhibitions, concerts, the Cisterna Film Festival and the municipal library.

Piazza XIX Marzo e Fontana Biondi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The square, dedicated to 19 March 1944, opens in front of Palazzo Caetani and the Collegiata and is the object of an Italian garden project that recovers the pre-war structure while preserving the monumental trees. At the center is the fountain of Ernesto Biondi, built between 1885 and 1890, depicting the goddess Feronia who defeats a demonic figure, symbol of the victory of the reclamation on the swamp and malaria.

Parco Pantanello

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Outside the walls of the Garden of Ninfa, it reconstructs the original environment of the Paludi Pontine and hosts events next to the literary paths of Ninfa; it is open from October to May.
