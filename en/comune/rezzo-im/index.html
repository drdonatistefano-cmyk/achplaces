What to see in Rezzo

Borgo dell'alta valle della Giara in the Regional Natural Park of the Ligurian Alps, with the hamlets of Cènova and Lavina: the stones carved by the stonemasons of Cènova (XIV-XV century) are recounted by the museum spread out "Strade di pietra" (stone streets), while in the chestnut wood stands the fifteenth-century sanctuary of the Madonna Bambina frescoed by Pietro Guido da Ranzo; guarding the village the castle of the Clavesana.

Museo «Strade di pietra»

Museums · Themed

Museo-Laboratorio sulle scalpellini (lapicidi) of the valley of Giara, whose school of Cènova produced decorative elements for the architecture of the whole western Liguria. Set up by the Municipality with the Montana Community, it has two sections: the stone museum in Cènova, about 140 m2 on the stone cycle from extraction to laying in operation, with videos, maps and a teaching room where to touch tips, chisels and compass; and three paths in the historical centers of Rezzo, Cènova and Lavina, where each piece is marked on the map with letter and number and described by a plaque. Tel. Common 0183 34015.

Santuario di Nostra Signora del Santo Sepolcro e di Maria Bambina

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

15th century shrine in the chestnut wood accessible from the village. The façade has a rose window made from a single block of stone; inside a wall frescoed by Pietro Guido da Ranzo with Stories of the Passion and a Madonna with Child in Carrara marble by sculptor Filippo Parodi, pupil of Bernini.

Castello dei Clavesana

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

A guard of the village, fortified house with kitchen, barn and cellar; in the basement a prison and a secret passage. Rezzo, documented by before Mille, was a fief of the Republic of Genoa, then disputed between the Marquises of Clavesana, Ceva and Del Carretto. The DMO has organized guided tours.

Parrocchiale di San Martino Vescovo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A 17th century church that preserves a black stone baptismal basin and the remains of an anonymous polyptych from the 16th century. In the village also the frescoed chapels of San Bernardo and Santa Croce dell'Ospedale, with works attributed to Pietro Guido da Ranzo.

Cènova, borgo degli scalpellini

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village of Rezzo is home to the stone-stones active between the 14th and 15th centuries, whose works are found throughout the valley of Giara, in Mendatica and Montegrosso Pian Latte; they worked in the major yards of the Ponente until the Collegiata di Tenda. In the historic center a signposted route of the museum .Strade di pietra .

  • Where Cènova, Rezzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rezzo.im.it

Lavina

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Fraction with the sanctuary of the Madonna della Neve, the late Baroque parish, a sixteenth-century oratory and numerous chapels surrounded by greenery; in the historic center passes one of the paths of the museum "Strade di pietra" .
