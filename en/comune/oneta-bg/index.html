What to see in Oneta

Country of the Val del Riso marked for centuries by mining activity, with mines opened by the Romans over two thousand years ago; to see the parish church of Santa Maria Assunta with a painting of the Moroni, the ethnographic scientific-minerary Museum, the sixteenth-century sanctuary of the Madonna del Frassino and the mine of Foghera.

4 places to visit in Oneta, in the province of Bergamo (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario della Madonna del Frassino

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

It dates back to 1502; according to tradition in 1512 Our Lady appeared to the young Pierina Carobbio, called Petruccia, in a place dominated by a large ash, from which the name. It preserves the Polyptych of the Visitation of Santacroce, a family of painters from the Val Brembana, also active in Venice. It is an important devotional place in a panoramic position on the Val del Riso, reachable with a climb of about 5 minutes.

Museo etnografico scientifico-minerario

Museums · Themed · Free entry

About 450 square meters dedicated to three areas: ethnography, with hundreds of objects of the peasant world, of the weaving and the work of the foresters; mining activities, with vintage tools, a section on minerals and, outside, a stretch of mine visitable; scientific laboratories, with two rooms of experiments on electromagnetism, sound, gas and radio transmissions designed especially for schools. It also exhibits reproductions of machines inspired by Leonardo's Codex Atlanticus (catapults, mowering wagons, flying machines, mobile bridges) made by Gianfranco Zucchi and granted in comodato by his children.

Visitable only by reservation (Comune di Oneta 035 707117 or Fattoria Ariete di Gorno 347 3240391)

  • Where Via Villa 10, Oneta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded shortly after the year 1000 and remodeled in the following centuries, it houses a small canvas of the Assumption of the Virgin by Giovan Battista Moroni and a fresco of the fifteenth century Pietà.

  • Where Centro, Oneta (poco prima del museo etnografico)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.valseriana.eu

Sources: valseriana.eu

Miniera di Foghera

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

The Val del Riso mine is safely and open to the public recently and can be reached by a walk of about 10 minutes from Plassa. From the mountains of the valley were extracted blend, galena and calamine to obtain zinc, lead and sulfuric acid; the mines were opened by the Romans over two thousand years ago and exploited also by the condemned to forced labor.
