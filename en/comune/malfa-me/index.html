What to see in Malfa

The largest of the three municipalities of Salina, on the north coast of the island between Monte dei Porri and Monte Fossa delle Felci, founded according to the tradition by Amalfi refugees in the 12th century: the eighteenth-century church of San Lorenzo, the terrace of Piazza Rosa dei Venti, the cove of Scario, the Aeolian Museum of Emigration and, in the hamlet of Pollara, the caldera where it was filmed "Il Postino" (The Postino).

7 places to visit in Malfa, in the province of Messina (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Pollara e la caldera

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small fishing village on the western side of Monte dei Porri, set in a partially submerged volcanic caldera that forms a green amphitheatre overlooking the sea. Here was shot most of the film Â"Il Postino" (1994), the last role of Massimo Troisi: via Massimo Troisi descends to the sea passing through the statue of postman Mario Ruoppolo; to the north of the village the Casa Rosa, the house of Neruda in the film, is private.

  • Where Pollara, Malfa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Onofrio e murale del Postino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built in the nineteenth century by farmers of Calabrian origin, it preserves the original floor in majolica; the village of Pollara grew around it. In the square in front of the mural with Massimo Troisi and Pablo Neruda (Philippe Noiret) sitting on a bench, from which you can see the Scoglio Faraglione and, in the distance, Filicudi.

  • Where Via Chiesa 13, Pollara, Malfa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.malfa.me.it

Balate e baia di Pollara (Casa di Mario)

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

From Via Massimo Troisi a paved path to steps descends to the bathing area; on the rocks the "balate" are the ancient deposits in stone of fishermen who in the film were the house of Mario Ruoppolo. To the north is the natural arch of Punta Perciato; to the south is the rocky ridge of the ancient beach of the dialogues between Mario and Neruda, now eroded.

the beach of the movie has been eroded: there remain small edges reachable only by swimming or by boat, ongoing recovery project

  • Where Via Massimo Troisi, Pollara, Malfa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo eoliano dell'emigrazione

Museums · Themed

Founded by the International Centre for Research on Aeolian History and Culture (C.I.R.C.E.), it tells the stories of those who emigrated from the Aeolian Islands to the United States, Australia, Argentina and other destinations since the end of the 19th century. Open every day 9-13, on request also at other times.

  • Where Via Fontana 4, Malfa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
