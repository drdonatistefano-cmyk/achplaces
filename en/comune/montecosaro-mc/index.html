What to see in Montecosaro

The village of Macerata is one of the most beautiful villages in Italy, closed by fourteenth-century walls in which you enter from Porta San Lorenzo; in the valley, in Montecosaro Scalo, the Romanesque basilica of Santa Maria a piede di Chienti, one of the most beautiful Romanesque architectures in the territory.

Basilica di Santa Maria a piè di Chienti (Santissima Annunziata)

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Church and monastery of Farfense origin documented since 936, consecrated in 1125 according to two inscriptions; on the left bank of Chienti, it has three aisles, two floors and double-level apse with walkway and radial chapels, scheme of pilgrimage churches. In the upper apse frescoes with scenes from the childhood of Christ, a Madonna with Child attributed to the Master of Offida and two fifteenth-century statues in terracotta; the façade was rebuilt between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, and next to it was rebuilt a caroling garden.

  • Where Via Santissima Annunziata 1, Montecosaro Scalo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Mura e Porta San Lorenzo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The walls of the 14th century still contain the old town, where you enter only from Porta San Lorenzo, the only surviving of the three medieval gates, of the 13th century, facing east and incorporated in Palazzo Marinozzi. At the top, the Cassero Garden is the green area of the historic centre.

  • Where Montecosaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · letsmarche.it

Piazza Trieste, Teatro delle Logge e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

On Piazza Trieste the Collegiate Church of San Lorenzo, rebuilt in 1723 on the ruins of the old parish church, with a single nave with a fresco of the fourteenth-century Virgin, and the nineteenth-century Theatre of the Logge, built from a medieval building. In the village Palazzo Cesarini (former Palazzo dei Priori of the 13th century, of the Marquises Cesarini from 1552) and Palazzo Laureati (1660-1700) with rich brick façade.

  • Where Piazza Trieste, Montecosaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Parco dell'Annunziata

Nature and landscape · City parks

Public garden for families inaugurated in 2010 next to the abbey of Santa Maria at the foot of Chienti: 10,000 m2 of lawn with games, benches and tables, connected by a bike path to the smallest park in Europe.
