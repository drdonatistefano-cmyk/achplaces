What to see in Zumpano

A country at the gates of Cosenza, whose first stable settlement was formed according to tradition by refugees from Cosentino fleeing around the year 1000; around Piazza San Giorgio there are the church of San Giorgio Martire, built around 1480 by Blessed Francesco Marino, and the Museum of sacred art with a triptych of Bartolomeo Vivarini.

Museo d'arte sacra «B. Vivarini»

Museums · Old masters

Opened in June 2008 in the canonical house of the church of San Giorgio Martire, it preserves the so-called Treasure of San Giorgio: sacred silvereries, wooden furnishings and liturgical vestments made largely by local craftsmen. Among the works there is a triptych by Bartolomeo Vivarini with the Madonna and Child between Saints George and Benedict and a wooden pulpit of the mid-sixteenth century. The environments were also a place for community assemblies, as 17th - century manuscripts attest. Without architectural barriers; information at 0984 411380.

  • Where Via Bartolomeo Vivarini 13, Zumpano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa di San Giorgio Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The main church of the village, built around 1480 by Blessed Francesco Marino, born in Zumpano, on the model of the Mother Church of Cosenza.

  • Where Piazza San Giorgio Martire 2, Zumpano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.zumpano.cs.it · italia.it

Piazza San Giorgio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The main square of the village, overlooking historical buildings and the Matrice Church; in the center stands a sculpture of St. George killing the dragon. Without architectural barriers.

  • Where Piazza San Giorgio, Zumpano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa della Madonna del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the hamlet Rovella erected in 1884 on the initiative of the well-off local Pasquale Bonanno, with facade of neoclassical hut and portal in stone arched to the sixth.
