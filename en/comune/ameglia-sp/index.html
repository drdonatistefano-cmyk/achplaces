What to see in Ameglia

Municipality at the mouth of the Magra with the village of Montemarcello, among the most beautiful villages in Italy, from the Roman camping path, overlooking the Gulf of Poets in the park of Montemarcello-Magra-Vara.

6 places to visit in Ameglia, in the province of La Spezia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Montemarcello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village on the hill with fifteenth-century door, ancient tower and streets from stone arches set like a Roman camp; the name recalls the console Claudio Marcello (155 BC). View of the Gulf of Poets with Tino and Tinetto.

  • Where Montemarcello, Ameglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The fifteenth-century church was modified in the seventeenth century, with a marble triptych from 1529 and a wooden triptych from the fourteenth century, on the small square of the village.

  • Where Montemarcello, Ameglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it

Punta Corvo

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Castello di Ameglia

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Castle of the thirteenth century that houses the Town Hall, with the renovated circular tower, among the alleys of the village.

  • Entry Free

Parco di Montemarcello-Magra-Vara

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural area of great biodiversity between Ligurian hills and Tuscan plains at the Magra Mouths; cormorants, seagulls and the kingfisher, symbol of the park.

  • Where Ameglia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it
