What to see in Manta

Borgo at the gates of Saluzzo dominated by the castle of Saluzzo della Manta, well FAI with one of the most extraordinary profane pictorial cycles in Europe.

3 places to visit in Manta, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Manta in the guide with map and filters

Castello della Manta

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

House of the Counts Saluzzo della Manta, well FAI: in the Baronial Hall the Nine Prodi and the Nine Heroines in fifteenth-century dresses and the legendary Fountain of Youth, inspired by the novel "Le chevalier errant" by Thomas III.

  • Where Via De Rege Thesauro 5, Manta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page fondoambiente.it
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets

Sources: fondoambiente.it · cultura.gov.it

Chiesa castellana di Santa Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

At the foot of the castle, the small church dedicated to the Virgin with frescoes from the XV and XVI century, including a cycle on the Passion.

  • Where Manta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fondoambiente.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it

Sala baronale

Museums · Old masters

The salon is frescoed by an anonymous master, one of the most important profane cycles of late-Gothic painting in Europe, brought to light by the restorations of the FAI.

  • Where Castello della Manta, Manta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website fondoambiente.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: fondoambiente.it · cultura.gov.it · ticket.fondoambiente.it
