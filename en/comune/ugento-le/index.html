What to see in Ugento

Ancient Messapian city of Salento with sections of the walls, the Castle with frescoes in 1692, the New Archaeological Museum in the former convent of the Friars Minor, the Crypt of the Crucifix and the Regional Natural Park Litorale of Ugento with its white sandy beaches.

Castello di Ugento

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Probably on a Roman camp and a Byzantine fortress, it became a fortress with the Normans; damaged by the Turks in 1537 and rebuilt since 1564, in 1643 it passed to the Marquises of Love who transformed it into a dwelling. In 1692 Francesco and Nicola d'Amore frescoed the first floor halls in late Baroque style with Greek and Roman myths, Old Testament scenes and word games on the surname with Venus and Cupid. Open from Tuesday to Saturday 9.30-12.30 and 18-21, Sunday 18-21.

  • Where Via Castello 13, Ugento
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Nuovo Museo Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

Born in 1966 for the finds of Ugento and the territory and inaugurated in 1968 as Civic Museum of Archaeology and Paleontology, it was re-equipped in 2009 as New Archaeological Museum, with materials also from other sites of Salento, in the halls of the former convent of Friars Minor Observing next to the church of Sant'Antonio. Open from Tuesday to Saturday 9.30-12.30 and 18-21, Sunday 18-21.

  • Where Via della Zecca, Ugento
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.ugento.le.it · italia.it

Cripta del Crocefisso e Madonna di Costantinopoli

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

A suggestive monumental complex just outside the centre, on the provincial road for Casarano, at the path of the ancient Salentina street. The crypt, a trapezoidal plan with sixteenth-century portal, has two phases frescoes (XIII-XIV and XVII century): the Annunciation, St. Nicholas in Byzantine episcopal dresses, the Christ Pantocrator, the Virgin of the Tenerance of the thirteenth century and the seventeenth-century Crucifixion that gives the name to the place; the thirteenth-century vault is painted with crossed shields, angioin lilies and fantastic animals.

access only by reservation (0833 555819, museum@comune.ugento.le.it) according to the Municipality

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Cattedrale e Museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters

The museum, commissioned by Bishop Vito De Gristis and inaugurated on July 6, 2005, is set up in the rooms below the Cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta, once a burial place, and exhibits works of the Cathedral and the churches of the diocese, considered among the oldest in southern Italy.

  • Where Via dei Cesari, Ugento
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Collezione Colosso

Museums · Archaeological

One of the few historical collections of Puglia, formed in the nineteenth century by Baron Adolfo Colosso with finds of Ugento and of the territory: 794 pieces from the sixth century BC to the high Middle Ages, more armour, weapons and bullets of cannon of modern age, preserved in Palazzo Colosso.

  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Centro storico, mura messapiche e Torre dell'orologio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

In the center you can still see traces of the ancient walls that surrounded the village, the clock Tower and the remains of an archaic necropolis. The bronze statue of Zeus (about 530 BC), found in 1961 at the intersection of via Mazzini and via Fabio Pittore, is exhibited at the National Archaeological Museum of Taranto.

  • Where Centro storico, Ugento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · museotaranto.cultura.gov.it

Parco naturale regionale Litorale di Ugento

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established in 2007, it covers about 1,600 hectares: from the sea to the inland sandy and rocky coasts, humid areas retrodunal and marshland, woods, Mediterranean scrub, age-old olive groves and ravines, parallel karst canals dug by water. Characteristic the basins behind the beach, built since the thirties to reclaim the marshes. Services: guided tours, bike rental, free parking.

  • Where Piazza Adolfo Colosso (sede), Ugento
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it
