What to see in Certaldo

The village of Boccaccio, Orange Flag of Touring: Certaldo Alto in red bricks, reachable by funicular, with the poet's house and the Pretorio Palace.

3 places to visit in Certaldo, in the province of Florence (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Certaldo Alto e Palazzo Pretorio

History and architecture · Villages · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The walled terracotta hamlet and the Palazzo del Vicario (1117-1164) with the façade covered with coats of arms, two of which from the workshop of the Della Robbia, and the Tabernacle of the Executed of Benozzo Gozzoli.

  • Where Via Rivellino, Certaldo
  • Entry €5 whole with Casa Boccaccio, €7 with the Museum of Sacred Art (City of Certaldo); reduced €4 and €5
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.destinationflorence.com
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: comune.certaldo.fi.it · destinationflorence.com · touringclub.it

Casa di Giovanni Boccaccio

Museums · House museums · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The house where tradition wanted lived Boccaccio, rebuilt after the war, with the fresco of Pietro Benvenuti, the library and the panoramic tower.

  • Where Via Giovanni Boccaccio 18, Certaldo
  • Entry In the single ticket with Palazzo Pretorio (€5 full)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.destinationflorence.com
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: comune.certaldo.fi.it · destinationflorence.com

Chiesa dei Santi Jacopo e Filippo e chiostro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Brick church of the first thirteenth century in Certaldo Alto with the cenotaph of Boccaccio, the bust of the poet of Giovan Francesco Rustici (1503), two Robbiani tabernacles and a Romanesque cloister on two floors; in the canonical there is the Museum of Sacred Art.

  • Where Piazza Santi Jacopo e Filippo, Certaldo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: sito2020.comune.certaldo.fi.it · destinationflorence.com
