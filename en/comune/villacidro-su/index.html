What to see in Villacidro

Country of the Middle Campidano at the foot of Mount Linas, the literary home of Giuseppe Dessì, known for the waterfall of Sa Spendula sung by D'Annunzio, the Liberty Washer of 1893, the parish church of Santa Barbara with its museum and the archaeological museum of Villa Leni; behind the forest of Montimannu with the waterfall of Piscina Irgas.

Cascata di Sa Spendula

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Less than a kilometer from the village, the Coxinas river, which is born from the peaks of Santu Miali in the area of Monti Mannu, makes three consecutive jumps for 60 meters total, the last of about 30 meters on granite rocks, under the spire called Campanas de Sisinni Conti, forming three natural pools between oleanders and holm oaks. Gabriele D'Annunzio, in Sardinia in 1882, composed with Ugo Ranieri the verses then became the sonnet . "La Spendula" , published in Captain Fracassa. From the parking five minutes walk on paved path; picnic area and games; in summer the waterfall is illuminated in the evening.

  • Where Strada per Gonnosfanadiga (segnalata Sa Spendula), Villacidro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Lavatoio pubblico liberty

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Inaugurated by the municipal administration in 1893 in the old district of Frontera de sa Mitza, it is a liberty structure similar to a temple in black trachyte of Serrenti, wrought iron and cast iron: cast iron columns hold the sheet metal cover over 36 masonry tanks covered with trachyte, with a fountain still active decorated with lion, lioness, siren and angel faces. The only survivor of a complex that included a slaughterhouse, loggia and watering trough, abandoned since the 1960s, was restored in the 1980s; it is located in the center, near the mill Cadoni and the church of Santa Barbara.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Barbara

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The bell tower and the Mayor capilla with star vault survived the renovation of the late seventeenth century that gave it a neoclassical appearance; in the eighteenth century it was enriched with furnishings, including the main altar. On the churchyard forms a triangle with the oratory of Our Lady with loggia and the oratory of the Souls.

  • Where Piazza Santa Barbara, Villacidro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · sardegnacultura.it

Museo di Santa Barbara

Museums · Old masters · Free entry

Museum of sacred art set up in the oratory of Our Lady (1600) and in the parish premises: liturgical silversmiths of Sardinian silversmiths (Cabras, Usay, Mamely) from the sixteenth century, a Gothic tower-timepiece, the Madonna of the Rosary of the sculptor Lonis, a painful Crucifix of the sixteenth century, a polychrome wooden Annunciation and numerous statues of the seventeenth century, as well as documents from the country church of San Sisinnio, the oratory of Sant'Efisio and the convent of the Mercedari. Open Saturday and Sunday 9-12 and 16-20; guided tours by reservation.

  • Where Piazza Santa Barbara 2, Villacidro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Museo archeologico Villa Leni

Museums · Archaeological

Civic museum housed since 2003 in the former Granatic mountain of 1761, with collections from the Neolithic to the early Middle Ages: finds of the Nuragic village of Cottega (XII century BC), a bronze model of quadrilobed nuraghe symbol of the museum, punic ceramics, the Roman-Imperial kit of the necropolis of Ruinas with a glass, late antique jewelry and Byzantine; possible guided tours.

  • Where Villacidro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Farmamuseo «Sa Potecaria»

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In the renovated Luxus house, it collects furnishings, books and pharmaceutical instruments between the seventeenth and twentieth centuries gathered by Dr. Fanni; the nucleus comes from the pharmacy Mancosu, of which remain counter and balance, and the reconstruction of the ancient pharmacy includes medicines distributed by the American army in the second world war; small library of ancient pharmacology. Free entrance.

Parco e chiesa di San Sisinnio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Municipal park a few kilometers from the center, on the right of the river Leni, on a hill with the church of the martyr San Sisinnio (XVII century) among the most important community of ancient olive trees in the area, with specimens over 13 meters and trunks of about 5 meters in diameter, linked to the legends of the saint who protected children from witches. On the path of Santa Barbara, with barbecue and tables; total motor accessibility.

  • Where Località San Sisinnio, Villacidro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: idese.cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Foresta di Montimannu

Nature and landscape · Mountains

One of the largest state forests in Sardinia, about 4,600 hectares in the heart of the park of Monte Linas, Oridda and Marganai, populated by Sardinian deer and mouflon; it is reached along the lake of Leni and the pine forest equipped with Campus de Monti. The CAI trails start from the former Canale Serci mine (cassiterite, 1866-1946) and the Cantina Ferraris refuge: 113 leads to Piscina Irgas, 109 to the Muru Mannu waterfall, over 70 metres.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Cascata di Piscina Irgas

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers

On the Linas river, near the border with Domusnovas, the waterfall reaches a jump of about 45 meters; it is reached with the path CAI 113 from the forest of Montimannu and is also a destination of canyoning and climbing.
