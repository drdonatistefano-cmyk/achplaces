What to see in Ameno

Holiday village in a panoramic position just a few steps from the eastern shore of Lake Orta: Palazzo Tornielli with the contemporary art museum and the Neogothic Park, the house-museum of Antonio Calderara in Vacciago, the sanctuary of the Madonna della Bocciola, the fourteenth-century parish church and the paths of the Quadrifoglio towards Monte Mesma.

Fondazione Antonio e Carmela Calderara (Casa museo di Vacciago)

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

In the house-studio of the painter Antonio Calderara (1903-1978), building of the late sixteenth century with three loggias on granite columns, there are 327 works: 56 by Calderara and 271 by 133 European, American, Japanese and Chinese artists related to him. The collection documents the international avant-gardes of the 1950s and 1960s (geometric abstraction, programmed and kinetic art, op art, visual poetry); a plan is dedicated to the path of Calderara from figurative to abstract. Open seasonally from mid-May to mid-October, from Tuesday to Sunday 10-12 and 15-18; not accessible to people with motor disabilities for stairs.

  • Where Via Bardelli 9, frazione Vacciago, Ameno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.fondazionecalderara.it

Sources: fondazionecalderara.it · comune.ameno.novara.it · distrettolaghi.it · italia.it

Palazzo Tornielli e Museo Tornielli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The palace of the noble family Tornielli, which held receptions, concerts and dances, combines parts of different eras with two internal courtyards, a staircase of honour and mythological frescoes. Today it houses the Town Hall and the Museum Tornielli, opened in 2012, with a permanent collection of site-specific contemporary art, temporary exhibitions, conferences and cultural events.

  • Where Piazza G. Marconi 1, Ameno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Parco Neogotico

Nature and landscape · City parks

The park next to Palazzo Tornielli, once private and accessible only from the palace, summer residence of the family, is open to the public since 1920 and has been restored after a long intervention: neo-Gothic architecture, a historic stone glacier, centuries-old trees and autochthonous aromatic plants.

  • Where Piazza G. Marconi, Ameno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Santuario della Madonna della Bocciola

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

On the panoramic road between Vacciago and Ameno, there is a 16th-century church that reminded us of an apparition of the Virgin. The current neoclassical building was begun in 1754 and completed in 1834-43: quadrilobed plant, high altar in white marble, an ancient image of the Madonna and nineteenth-century frescoes by Agostino Comerio with scenes from the Old Testament and the life of the Virgin.

  • Where Via Bardelli 11, Ameno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ameno.novara.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Of medieval origin, with three naves, rises in the green south of the village on the site of a more ancient church documented in 1217 and attributed to San Giulio; the current form dates back to about the second half of the fourteenth century, the late-Romanesque bell tower is dated 1505. Sunday Mass in the summer.

  • Where Via Topini 8 (accesso da Viale Matteotti), Ameno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ameno.novara.it · italia.it

Oratorio del Crocifisso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Oratorio eighteenth-century on the old road between Miasino and Ameno, with a small porch reached by stairs; it is part of a circular devotional itinerary with three chapels. At the bottom of the aisle, wooden altar and a painting attributed to the Morazzone.

Riserva naturale del Monte Mesma

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the top you can enjoy a wonderful view of Lake Orta; there is a monumental complex with the convent built in 1600 on the remains of a castle of the fourteenth century and various chapels along a processional path. The area also has archaeological interest (celtic, Gallic and Roman-Imperial finds) and wooded slopes with chestnut and oak. Managed by the Ticino and Lake Maggiore Protected Areas Management Authority.

  • Where Località Monte Mesma, Ameno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ameno.novara.it · italia.it

Quadrifoglio di Ameno

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Four ring paths starting from the centre of the village, each marked by a color and a theme, which can be walked, by mountain bike or on horseback with different levels of difficulty; cross the woods and the protected area of Mount Mesma. The Indaco Ring hosts an educational WWF tour of local flora and fauna.

  • Where Partenza dal centro di Ameno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Villa Monte Oro

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Large Liberty style villa designed by Carlo Nigra in 1926 at the entrance of the village for those arriving from Miasino: more two- or three-storey bodies with a panoramic tower, in a vast park with rare conifers, azaleas, rhododendrons, a forest of araucaria and secular beech trees. Private property, visible only from the outside.

  • Where Regione Monte Oro, via Molino 7, Ameno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.ameno.novara.it · italia.it
