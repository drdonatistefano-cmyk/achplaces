What to see in Berceto

Medieval village of the Parma Apennines at 852 meters, the last stop of the Via Francigena in Emilia before the Cisa pass: the Romanesque Cathedral of San Moderanno with its treasure, the ruins of the fortress, the stone village of Corchia with the museum of the painter Martino Jasoni.

6 places to visit in Berceto, in the province of Parma (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Berceto in the guide with map and filters

Duomo di San Moderanno

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Dedicated to St. Moderano, abbot here since 719, rebuilt between the 12th and 13th centuries and renovated in the 16th and 19th centuries, when the façade was rebuilt in sandstone after a collapse of the ground; it preserves the portal trumpeted of the 12th century with bezel and architrave in relief and the interior with three apsed aisles. It preserves the relics of St. Moderano and St. Abbondio. Open every day 7-19.

  • Where Via Romea, Berceto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · italia.it

Museo del tesoro del Duomo

Museums · Themed

In the chapel of Sant'Apollonia del Duomo: reliquaries, crosses, glasses and vestments, including the green pivial of San Moderano (perhaps byzantine from the 8th to the 9th century or Lucca from the 12th), a glass of 1517 and a rare glass glass, probably of the 8th century, found in 1971 in a tomb under the presbytery.

  • Where Via Romea, Berceto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · parmawelcome.it

Rocca di Berceto

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built in 1221 by the Municipality of Parma next to the Duomo; for centuries used as a stone quarry for the houses of the village, remains the ruins, restored since 2004.

  • Where Berceto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Borgo di Corchia

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small hamlet of stone houses, paved alleys, arches and barns known as "tagge" (tagges), under the Farnese lived of the iron and copper mines of Mount Groppo Maggio, closed in 1943.

  • Where Corchia, Berceto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Museo Martino Jasoni

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

In Casa Corchia, the paintings by Martino Jasoni, a painter born in Corchia, emigrated to the United States in the early 20th century, returned in 1924 and exhibited at the Venice Biennale in 1936: visionary works on emigration and alienation, sacred, dreamlike and rural subjects. Sunday 15-18 (July-August also Saturday and Sunday morning), other days by reservation.

  • Where Casa Corchia, Corchia, Berceto
  • Entry 2 € (over 65 0.50 €, free under 18 years)
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parmawelcome.it · italia.it

Passo della Cisa e santuario di Nostra Signora della Guardia

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valico at 1039 meters on the state road of Cisa, once bordered by the Duchy of Parma and the Grand Duchy of Tuscany, with the ancient post station; on top of a staircase the neo-Romanesque and neo-Gothic sanctuary of Our Lady of the Guard, of 1921.

  • Where Passo della Cisa, Berceto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it
