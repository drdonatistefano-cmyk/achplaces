What to see in San Lorenzo Dorsino

Municipality at the foot of the Brenta Dolomites, at the entrance of the Val d'Ambiez in the Adamello Brenta Nature Park: the village of San Lorenzo in Banale, born from the union of seven ancient villas, the church of the Santi Sebastiano and Rocco with the frescoes of Baschenis, San Giorgio in Dorsino, the House of the Park and the Nembia lake, WWF reserve.

6 places to visit in San Lorenzo Dorsino, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Borgo delle Sette Ville di San Lorenzo in Banale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village is born from the union of seven ancient feuds, Prato, Pergnano, Senaso, Dolaso, Prusa, Berghi and Glolo, each with chapel, saint and own customs; Dolaso has more well-preserved buildings. To see Casa Moscati in Berghi, Casa Martinoni on the Colle dei Frati, Casa Mazoleti in Prusa with a blooming arched and sundial loggia, stone fountains where washing was done and the Cavada, ancient road paved by Prato with the grooves of sleds. The walk "Seven steps to Seven Villas" takes about an hour.

  • Where San Lorenzo in Banale, San Lorenzo Dorsino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Chiesa dei Santi Sebastiano e Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Casa del Parco «C'era una volta»

Museums · Themed

18th century building (House Osèi) renovated as a typical country house, where you can discover the traditions of the past; curated by the experts of the Adamello Brenta Nature Park, it also has a virtual route for children. Open in July and August.

Laghetto di Nembia

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

WWF nature reserve along the road to Molveno, just a few minutes drive from the village: a flat ring of about 3 km (about an hour) with panels on flora and fauna; in the lake trout and salmerini, boats and fishing.

  • Where Nembia, San Lorenzo Dorsino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Val d'Ambiez

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the entrance of the valley stands San Lorenzo in Banale, considered the gateway to the Brenta Dolomites: alpine pastures and mountain paths in the Adamello Brenta Nature Park, starting point for trekking and hiking.

  • Where Val d'Ambiez, San Lorenzo Dorsino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: italia.it · visittrentino.info
