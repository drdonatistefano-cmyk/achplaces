What to see in Gorizia

Border town between Latin, Slavic and Germanic cultures, European Capital of Culture 2025 with Nova Gorica: the medieval castle with the village and provincial museums, noble palaces such as Attems Petzenstein and Coronini Cronberg, Transalpina Square on the border with Slovenia and parks along the Isonzo.

15 places to visit in Gorizia, in the province of Gorizia (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.
