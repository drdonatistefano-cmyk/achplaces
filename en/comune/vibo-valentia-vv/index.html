What to see in Vibo Valentia

The ancient Hipponion, then Valentia and Monteleone: the Norman-Svevo Castle with the National Archaeological Museum ...Vito Capialbi , the sections of Greek walls discovered by Paolo Orsi, the Cathedral of Santa Maria Maggiore and San Leoluca and, on the sea, the port of Vibo Marina with the fish auction.

Castello normanno-svevo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the hill that dominates the city, wanted by Frederick II of Swabia in the thirteenth century to defend Vibo, expanded by Angioini and Aragonese and from 1501 fortified residence of the Pignatelli: an irregular complex around a central court, with circular and hexagonal towers and the portal with the coat of arms Pignatelli; the view arrives at the Tyrrhenian, the Serre, the Aspromonte, Sicily and the Aeolian.

  • Where Via Antica Monteleone, Vibo Valentia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.vibovalentia.vv.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.vibovalentia.vv.it

Museo archeologico nazionale «Vito Capialbi»

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Established in 1969 and since 1995 in the castle, entitled to the scholar Vito Capialbi: the Capialbi collection with ceramics and monetiere, the votive offerings of Greek shrines such as that of Scrimbia, the Greek necropolis with the set of the tomb 19 and the golden laminette with orifical text, and the Roman city with the portrait of Marco Vipsanio Agrippa and a female bust in basaltite.

  • Where Via Antica Monteleone, Castello normanno-svevo, Vibo Valentia
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.vibovalentia.vv.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.vibovalentia.vv.it

Mura greche di Hipponion

History and architecture · Archaeology

Traits of the belt of the Greek city, brought to light by Paolo Orsi between 1916 and 1921 in the locality Trappeto Vecchio: large square blocks of sandstone and limestone with semicircular towers, one of which preserved in the raised and the others in the foundations.

Area open in some periods with visits by reservation to the Museum

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Area archeologica del Cofino

History and architecture · Archaeology

On the hills of Cofino the remains of a Greek temple between the end of the sixth and the beginning of the fourth century B.C., of about 27.50 for 18.10 meters, with colonnade, cell and opistodomum; it is part of the urban archaeological park.

Area open in some periods with visits by reservation to the Museum

Parco delle Rimembranze (Belvedere-Telegrafo)

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

The panoramic point with the remains of a doric temple of the VI-V century B.C. discovered by Paolo Orsi in 1916, partly re-used to build a telegraph; the look goes from Bagnara Calabra to Capo Palinuro and Stromboli.

Parco archeologico di Sant'Aloe

History and architecture · Archaeology

The remains of the Roman city of Valentia, from the 2nd century BC to the VII AD: public baths with calidarium and frigidarium, domus with courtyards and rainwater collection and a stretch of road; from here come the mosaics of the Four Seasons, the Nereide and the starry sky.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Sources: comune.vibovalentia.vv.it

Duomo di Santa Maria Maggiore e San Leoluca

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built from 1680 to 1723 on the site of a Byzantine basilica of the IX century: a Latin cross with a single nave with four chapels, a baroque façade with two bell towers; it houses the Madonna of the Snow of the sixteenth century, a marble triptych by Antonello Gagini and three bronze portals by Giuseppe Niglia.

  • Where Piazza San Leoluca, Vibo Valentia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.vibovalentia.vv.it

Sources: comune.vibovalentia.vv.it · sigecweb.beniculturali.it

Valentianum e Museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters

The convent complex next to the Duomo, seat of the Diocesan Museum: bronze statues of Cosimo Fanzago, the surviving statues of the triptych Gagini, paintings of Monteleoneese painters and works attributed to Paolo De Matteis and Francesco Curia, with the mitria of San Leoluca di Mattia Condursi (1854).

Chiesa di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry
