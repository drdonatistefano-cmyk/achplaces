What to see in Racines

Municipality of the valleys of Racines and Ridanna near Vipiteno: in Mareta stands Castel Wolfsthurn, baroque residence from 365 windows with the Provincial Museum of Hunting and Fishing, in Masseria the great enrichment plant of the Provincial Museum of Mines, and in Stanghe the white marble gorge of Gilfenklamm, natural monument, with the ruins of Castel Reifenegg.

5 places to visit in Racines, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castel Wolfsthurn e Museo provinciale della caccia e della pesca

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

Large 18th-century Baroque mountain residence above Mareta, without fortifications and still of the Sternbach family, built on the game of numbers: 365 windows, 52 doors, 12 fireplaces and 4 portals. Since 1996 it houses the hunting and fishing museum (part of the ethnographic museum of Teodone) with dioramas of fauna and fish, popular art and a collection of fly fishing; on the second floor the rooms preserved in their original appearance, with tapestries, show the noble life of the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries. A thematic path of about 1 km, accessible by strollers, climbs from the village to the castle.

  • Where Kirchdorf 25, Mareta, Racines
  • Entry €5, reduced €3,70, family €10
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.wolfsthurn.it

Sources: wolfsthurn.it · italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo provinciale miniere - sede di Ridanna

Museums · Science and university

In Masseria there is the mineral enrichment plant of about 150 years ago, the unique monument of the industrial revolution.Its main features include large crushing machines, flotation tanks, inclined braking planes and horse-drawn sections of the huge rail transport 27 km from Monteneve to the station of Vipiteno, born after the opening of the Brennero railway (1867). The mine remained active until May 1985; the Poschhaus tunnel runs for 6 km in the mountain towards the Val Passiria.

  • Where Masseria 48, Ridanna, Racines
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.museominiere.it

Sources: museominiere.it · suedtirol.info · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Gola di Stanghe (Gilfenklamm)

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

Gorge excavated in pure white marble from the Racines river, which falls into several waterfalls (according to italia.it 15 meters high), from Stanghe to Ponte Giovo; protected natural monument, you walk on walkways, wooden bridges and stairs made in 1896, fallen between the two wars and restored in the early Sixties. About an hour a journey, 1.5 hours round trip; starting from Ponte Giovo you save 175 m of ascent, and you should avoid 10-12:30.

  • Where Via Giovo 1, Stanghe, Racines
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.ratschings.info

Sources: ratschings.info · suedtirol.info · italia.it

Ruderi di Castel Reifenegg

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

It ruins in the coniferous forest of which only the massive mast remains standing, with walls about two meters thick; it is reached with the ring of 6.5 km from Stanghe that crosses the Gilfenklamm to Ponte Giovo and returns to the path 11A passing from the ruins.

  • Where Sentiero 11A, Stanghe, Racines
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · suedtirol.info

Cascate Burkhard

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

A natural corner hidden at the head of the Ridanna Valley: a scenic path, about one metre wide, starts from the Museum of the mines of Masseria and goes up through meadows, fir woods and moss along the Fernerbach, glacial stream, to the waterfalls. Bus 312 from Vipiteno, stop at Museo delle Mine.

  • Where Partenza da Masseria, Ridanna, Racines
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it
