What to see in Santo Stefano di Camastra

The city of ceramics is overlooking the sea of the province of Messina at the foot of the Nebrodi: rebuilt further south after the earthquake of 1682, it is the historic center of Sicilian ceramics and Matrangela, with streets full of shops and the Civic Museum of ceramics in Palazzo Trabia.

Museo civico delle ceramiche (Palazzo Trabia)

Museums · Themed

It is located in Palazzo Trabia, one of the most important noble palaces in the area, and exhibits an interesting collection of ceramics: italia.it defines it as a true temple of the traditions of Santo Stefano di Camastra, where it traces the entire ceramic history.

Vie e botteghe della ceramica

Local life and traditions · Crafts · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village extends on a high hill above the sea; its streets, like the Viale delle Palme, are crowded with colorful shops selling plates, vases, heads of Moors, pine cones, majolica, jars and bùmmuli, with decorated lava stone inlays, decorated fountains and walls covered with majolica tiles. In artisan workshops you can still see the Matrangela, a figure born from the traditional Stefano ceramic and handmade with techniques handed down for centuries.

  • Where Viale delle Palme, Santo Stefano di Camastra
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info
