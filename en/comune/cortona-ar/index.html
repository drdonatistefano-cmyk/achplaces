What to see in Cortona

Etruscan city on the hill above the Valdichiana, with medieval squares and one of the first public museums in Italy.

3 places to visit in Cortona, in the province of Arezzo (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

MAEC - Museo dell'Accademia Etrusca e della Città di Cortona

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In Palazzo Casali, the museum of the Etruscan Academy founded in 1727, indicated as the first public museum in Italy.

  • Where Piazza Signorelli, Cortona
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Piazza della Repubblica e Palazzo Comunale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The 12th century Town Hall, on the remains of the Roman forum, with staircase, bell tower and clock; next to the Palazzo Passerini of the Captain of the People.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica, Cortona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Tumulo II del Sodo

History and architecture · Archaeology

The archaic Etruscan mound in the plain, called "melone," with a step altar decorated with orientalizing sculptural groups.

  • Where Il Sodo, Cortona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
