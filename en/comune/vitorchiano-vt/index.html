What to see in Vitorchiano

The suspended village of peperino overhanging the ditches, with proffers, medieval houses and the only Moai outside Easter Island.

3 places to visit in Vitorchiano, in the province of Viterbo (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Moai di Vitorchiano

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The only Moai that exists outside Easter Island, 6 meters high, carved in the local pepper by eleven Maori indigenous.

  • Where Vitorchiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Borgo e profferli

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval village in dark pepper, with the external stairs called proffers, balconies and arches; Monicelli shot scenes of the Brancaleone Army.

  • Where Vitorchiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Palazzo Comunale e case del Podestà e del Rabbino

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The Town Hall with important archive documents and stone houses of the Podestà, the Rabbi and Santa Rosa, exiles here in 1250.

  • Where Vitorchiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com
