Castello Lambertini e Torre dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

The Lambertini castle, the family of Pope Benedict XIV, rebuilt in 1475 and in the seventeenth century, now the town hall; the 25-metre-high Clock Tower was prison.
