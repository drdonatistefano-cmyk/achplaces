What to see in Santu Lussurgiu

Santu Lussurgiu is a medieval village 500 meters on the south-eastern side of Montiferru, inside an ancient volcanic cone, known for carpets, knives and saddles. It preserves the churches of Santa Croce and Santa Maria degli Angeli and the Museum of Agricultural Technology; in the nearby woods there are the village of San Leonardo di Siete Fuentes and the waterfall of s'Istrampu de sos Molinos.

Museo della Tecnologia contadina "Maestro Francesco Salis"

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1976 in an 18th-century manor house, home to the UNLA People's Culture Centre, it collects over 2,000 work tools and everyday objects donated by the families of the country, in 11 of the 23 rooms of the building. The halls are arranged for crafts: farmers, shepherds and charcoal makers, artisans' machines, viticulture, cheese-making and mill, spinning and weaving, work and party clothes, transportation and hydraulic machines; panels also illustrate songs and children's games.

Chiesa di Santa Maria degli Angeli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Late church built in the late fifteenth century by the Friars Minor observing together with a convent. Inside there is a wooden group of the Madonna with the Child (Madonna of the Angels) of the sixteenth century, probably arrived from southern Italy.

Chiesa di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the oldest nucleus of the country, consecrated in 1185 and originally dedicated to St. Lussorio, the holy soldier who according to tradition preached here before martyrdom and from which the country takes its name.

Centro storico e terrazza di sa Rocca

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village on the south-eastern side of Montiferru, known for the weaving of carpets and the processing of knives, boots and saddles. The trail of the sources touches Silbanis, the old wash-house on Sauccu and the park of Funtana longa; the panoramic terrace of Sa Rocca has a statue of the Christ of Edgardo Mugnoz. At Carnival Via Roma hosts the horse race of Sa Carrela 'and Nanti, at the end of August the Ardia of San Lussorio around the parish of San Pietro.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

San Leonardo di Siete Fuentes

History and architecture · Villages

Frazione about 6 km from the village and almost 700 meters above sea level, surrounded by woods of holm oaks, corks and secular elm; the village, of medieval origin and from the thirteenth century in possession of the Hospitallers of San Giovanni, gathers around the ancient Romanesque church of San Leonardo. The name comes from the sources (Ad Septem fontes), no longer seven, which feed small ponds with light waters. Since 1906, in the first days of June, it hosts the Regional Horse Fair.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Cascata di s'Istrampu de sos Molinos

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers

Waterfall 30 meters high in all in five jumps, the last of about 15, on the border between Santu Lussurgiu and Bonarcado, in a valley protected as a special conservation area. The name recalls the mills and the galleries for the processing of the cloths operated by the stream; one is preserved next to the major jump. You walk down a mule track with sections of pavement, between holm oaks, oaks, alders and laurel, starting from a stretch on the SP 15.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
