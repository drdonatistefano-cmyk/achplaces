What to see in Castellaneta

Surrounded for two thirds by a protected oasis of over 400 hectares, Castellaneta overlooks the Gravina Grande, one of the deepest in Puglia, in whose caves the peasants fled from the pirate raids; the historic center has the Cathedral, the Bishop's Palace and the medieval districts Sacco and Muricello, and the village is famous as home of Rodolfo Valentino, to which is dedicated a museum. On the coast, the fine sandy beaches and the pine forest of Castellaneta Marina.

Gravina Grande

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The large gravina that encircles two thirds of the country is the largest in the province and one of the deepest in Puglia, with a jump of almost 150 meters. In its caves, probably in the 10th century, the peasants of the nearby farmhouses took refuge to defend themselves against pirate raids: the houses and the rock churches date back to the origins of the country.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Centro storico, quartieri Sacco e Muricello

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient center rises along the steepest edges of a vast limestone crater; the medieval districts Sacco and Muricello have narrow and winding streets that recall the escape routes from the incursions of the past.

Museo Rodolfo Valentino (MUV)

Museums · Themed

Documentary museum opened on 18 June 2010 in the former monastery of Santa Chiara, supported by the Fondazione Rodolfo Valentino and dedicated to the actor born in Castellaneta in 1895, divo of silent cinema. The permanent exhibition "Rodolfo Valentino," the seduction of the myth, includes the Hall of the Great Amateur with photographic panels, projections, 35 posters of his films and the reconstruction of the set (the tent) de "The son of the Sheik" (Sheik), among the rare pieces a 1927 chromolithography advertising cigarettes "Valentino" and a biography published in Hollywood in 1926, year of death. Hours: Friday and Saturday 10-12 and 17-20, Sunday 10-12, Monday to Thursday closed.

Chiesa e convento di San Francesco e Monte Santa Trinità

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint
