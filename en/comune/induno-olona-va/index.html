What to see in Induno Olona

Common at the gates of Varese, between the Romanesque church of San Pietro in Silvis, already parish church of the village, the eighteenth-century church of the Santi Re Magi along Via Olona and Monte Monarco, a short panoramic ascent on the lakes of Varesotto.

Chiesa di San Pietro in Silvis

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque building probably of the 11th century, restored and enlarged in Renaissance age, when the bell tower was erected; the sacristy is of the late 16th century. It was the parish church of Induno until the construction of St. John the Baptist. The hut façade preserves in part the original plaster frescoed with the Virgin, Child and saints; the portal with bezel is made of Viggiu stone and inside remain precious frescoes, marked by time. Open for Fall 2020 and Spring 2022 FAI Days.

  • Where Via Monviso 2, Induno Olona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.vareseturismo.it

Chiesa dei Santi Re Magi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

18th century church built on a plot donated by the Bernasconi family, with a single nave with a small bell tower. The baroque façade alternates concavity and convexity; on the portal a fresco of the Adoration of the Magi and a half bust in stone of Christ blessing. It preserves relics of the Magi, partly from the Basilica of Sant'Eustorgio in Milan, and inside trompe-l'œil decorations and eighteenth-century paintings.

  • Where Via Olona 69, Induno Olona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.vareseturismo.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · vareseturismo.it

Monte Monarco

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Easy hike of about 2 km and 270 meters of difference in altitude (about one hour) from the locality Montallegro, with parking near a farm. The hairpin bends and an intermediate space with benches and grids open the view on the lakes of Varese, Maggiore, Monate and Comabbio; on the summit a chapel houses a statue of the Redeemer.

  • Where Località Montallegro, Induno Olona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.vareseturismo.it

Sources: vareseturismo.it
