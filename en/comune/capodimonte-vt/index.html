What to see in Capodimonte

Borgo on a promontory on the south-western shore of Lake Bolsena, dominated by the octagonal Farnese Rocca restored by Antonio da Sangallo the Younger; from the marina start the trips to the Bisentina and Martana islands, and on the hill towards Valentano remain the traces of the ancient Visentium.

7 places to visit in Capodimonte, in the province of Viterbo (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca Farnese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Octagonal fortress on a volcanic cliff, restored in the sixteenth century by Antonio da Sangallo the Younger, overlooking the historic centre; it is surrounded by an Italian garden and it is accessed by an inclined bridge designed by Sangallo himself. It is privately owned and can be visited on request.

  • Where Piazza della Rocca, Capodimonte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Isola Bisentina

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

Isola del lago di Bolsena quoted by Dante in the Divine Comedy; there are the church of Saints James and Christopher, the work of the Vignola, and an octagonal edict by Antonio da Sangallo the Younger. You can reach it by boat from the marina of Capodimonte, which also touch the island Martana.

  • Where Porticciolo di Capodimonte (partenza battelli)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Lungolago e porticciolo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Walk along the lake of Bolsena with the small port from which the trips to the islands Martana and Bientina depart; the lake is suitable for sailing, fishing, diving, hiking, horseback riding and mountain biking.

  • Where Lungolago, Capodimonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Museo della Navigazione nelle Acque Interne (MNAI)

Museums · Themed

Born in 2010 after the discovery, in 1989, of a protohistoric piroga in the lake near the island of Bisentina, it tells the navigation in the inland waters from the antiquity to the twentieth century, with finds from the lake of Bolsena and boats from Bracciano, Fondi and Posta Fibreno; the route illustrates the naval architecture of fresh water and the activities of fishing, communication and trade. Timetable indicated by the Municipality: Monday-Friday 8.30-13.30 (tel. 0761 872437).

Monte Bisenzo e resti di Visentium

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the hillside along the road to Valentano remain the traces of the ancient Visentium, set in flames by Urbano IV in 1264, rebuilt and finally abandoned at the beginning of the XIX century; the archaeological park of Monte Bienzo also includes an Etruscan necropolis.

  • Where Monte Bisenzo, strada per Valentano, Capodimonte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Presepe subacqueo

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Made in 1992 by the Viterbo sculptor Catia Cutigni: three life-size statues lying about 7 meters deep in the lake, under the gardens of Via della Rocca, laid by divers of the Fire Department. The village also has a needle-embroidered nativity scene and thread with scenes of local life.

  • Where Giardini di via della Rocca, Capodimonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it
