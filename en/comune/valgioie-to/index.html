What to see in Valgioie

Small mountain municipality of the Val Sangone, autonomous since 1272: the paths from the villages go up to the Colle Braida with a view of the lower Valley of Susa, the Lakes of Avigliana and the Sacred of San Michele, touching the 'pietrefitte' of the Colle di Castiglione.

4 places to visit in Valgioie, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Valgioie in the guide with map and filters

Sentiero Modoprato–Colle Braida

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Itinerary of 4.3 km (about 2 hours, difficulty E), on foot or by mountain bike, from the information point of the village Modoprato: mule track with the pillar of the Holy Family (1835), equipped area with views of the lower Valley of Susa and the Lakes of Avigliana, a stretch with views of the Sacred of San Michele and arrival at the Colle Braida.

  • Where Borgata Modoprato, Valgioie
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valgioie.to.it

Sources: comune.valgioie.to.it

Pietrefitte del Colle di Castiglione

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

At the Prese dei Rossi, in a grove at the Colle di Castiglione, remains indicated by the municipality as of Celtic times, called "pietrefitte" or "menhir"; the history page of the municipality binds them to druid settlements.

  • Where Colle di Castiglione, Valgioie
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valgioie.to.it

Sources: comune.valgioie.to.it

Cappella di Colle Braida

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Chapel built in 1900 at the Colle Braida, where a votive pylon stood before, recalling an apparition of the Virgin to a farmer in 1800 (the municipality calls her the Chapel of the Sacred Heart in the itinerary and Chapel of the Immaculate Heart of Mary in the list of places).

  • Where Borgata Colle Braida, Valgioie
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valgioie.to.it

Sources: comune.valgioie.to.it

Sentiero dei Franchi

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Long and with significant difference in altitude, on a ridge at almost 2000 meters, along the ancient path of merchants, pilgrims and warriors; reported as "Sentiero dei Franchi" and, up to Col Bione, also with the signs of the GTA. It can be divided into two with an overnight stay at Col Bione.

  • Where Valgioie
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valgioie.to.it

Sources: comune.valgioie.to.it
