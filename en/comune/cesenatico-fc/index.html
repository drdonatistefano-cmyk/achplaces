What to see in Cesenatico

The canal port designed by Leonardo in 1502, the historical boats of the Marine Museum, Casa Moretti and the beaches of the Riviera.

4 places to visit in Cesenatico, in the province of Forlì-Cesena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 5 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Porto canale leonardesco

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The canal that crosses the center, designed by Leonardo da Vinci in 1502 for Cesare Borgia, with the houses of colorful fishermen.

  • Where Porto canale, Cesenatico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Museo della Marineria

Museums · Themed

Museum of the traditional navigation of the Adriatic, with a floating section of ten historical boats moored in the canal port and a section on the ground.

  • Where Via Armellini 18, Cesenatico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Casa Moretti

Museums · House museums

The house of the writer Marino Moretti overlooking the canal harbour, with original furnishings, books and papers.

  • Where Via Marino Moretti 1, Cesenatico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Spiagge di Cesenatico

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

Seven kilometers of coast between Valverde, Villamarina, Zadina and Ponente.

  • Where Lungomare, Cesenatico
  • Entry Free beaches; establishments with fee
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
