What to see in Guarene

Borgo Bandiera Arancione, one of the most elegant and intact historic centers of the Roero, overlooking Barbaresco, Alba and the Alps, with the contemporary art of the Fondazione Sandretto Re Rebaudengo.

6 places to visit in Guarene, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzo Re Rebaudengo – Fondazione Sandretto Re Rebaudengo

Museums · Modern art
