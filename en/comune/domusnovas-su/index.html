What to see in Domusnovas

Country of the Iglesiente at the foot of the Marganai, famous for the Cave of St. John, crossed by an illuminated road walkable; in the surroundings the nuraghe complex of Sa Domu 'e s'Orcu, the park of Monte Linas - Oridda - Marganai and traces of about fifty mines active until the mid-Twentieth century.

Grotta di San Giovanni

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Limestone cave that crosses Mount Aqua, crossed by an internal road of about 850-860 meters started in 1866 by Count Pietro Beltrami and completed by Gaetano Semenza to connect Domusnovas to the mining valley of Oridda; open to traffic until 1999, today you visit on foot with artificial lighting. With the secondary branches (reserved for speleologists, including Su Stampu de Pireddu) the development reaches 3920 meters; the two entrances remain traces of prehistoric cyclopic walls, and Neolithic and Byzantine finds attest to its use as a refuge. Visit to the hotel with online booking.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesetta di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Memorial church built between secular olive trees next to the southern entrance of the cave, after the demolition of the chapel of Byzantine origin that stood inside, eliminated to make room for the road. In Ferragosto there is celebrated the feast of St. John with folk bands and launeddas players.

  • Where Ingresso sud della Grotta di San Giovanni, Domusnovas
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Nuraghe Sa Domu 'e s'Orcu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Complex nuraghe at the foot of Mount Mannu, between the Marganai and the plain of the Cixerri, built in several phases presumably between the seventeenth and the 11th century BC with Marganai limestone: kept mast for about 4,5 meters, bastion three-lobed 17 meters long and antemural with five other towers, nine in all; in the access corridor a helical staircase rises on the upper floor.

  • Where Ai piedi del monte Mannu, Domusnovas
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Parco di Monte Linas - Oridda - Marganai

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Protected area of over 22,000 hectares, partly in the territory of Domusnovas, where the granite of Mount Linas and the limestone of Marganai are separated from the plateau of Oridda (about 600 m); peak Perda de sa Mesa (1236 m). Woods of holm oak and cork, waterfalls and gorges, caves and underground rivers, deer, mouflons, royal eagles and griffon, and on the plateau the ruins of a nineteenth-century mining plant; the walls of Oridda are suitable for sport climbing. Free entrance.

  • Where Altopiano di Oridda, Domusnovas
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Museo civico «Sigerro»

Museums · Themed

Museum distributed in three locations: Casa Pirinu with the naturalistic and fauna section, the former Montegranatico with minerals and fossils (managed by the mineralogical association Domusnovae) and the exhibition room of the municipal library with archaeological finds. Casa Pirinu and the library are visited on request to the Municipality; the former Montegranatico opens in winter from 17 to 19 and in summer from 18 to 20.

  • Where Corso Repubblica 31-33, Domusnovas (ex Montegranatico in Piazza Matteotti)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Museo della civiltà contadina della piana del Cixerri

Museums · Themed

More than 1300 pieces on the Domusnovas agricultural tradition and on the Cixerri plain: kitchen utensils, containers, tools for making cheese and wine, iron and wood ploughs, wagons and tools for sowing, harvesting and transport. Opens only by telephone appointment with the owner; admission fee.

  • Where Via G. Garibaldi 57, Domusnovas
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Centro storico: chiese di Santa Barbara e della Vergine Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the historic centre, the medieval church of Santa Barbara and the parish church of the Assumption Virgin, dating back to the second half of the eighteenth century, with stone façade. During Holy Week on Friday the processions of the Crucifix and de on Scravamentu take place.
