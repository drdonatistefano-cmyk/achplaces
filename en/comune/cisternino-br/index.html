What to see in Cisternino

White village of the Valle d'Itria founded around an abbey of the Basilian monks in the Middle Ages, with four historic districts around Piazza Vittorio Emanuele, the Norman-Sveva Tower, the Mother Church of St. Nicholas and the viewpoint over the valley; it is considered the capital of the "fornelli" .

8 places to visit in Cisternino, in the province of Brindisi (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico e quartieri antichi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Example of spontaneous architecture of white houses, paved alleys to chianche, arches, hidden courtyards, balconies, stone masks and external ladders, in the four districts Bère Vecchie, Scheledd, u Pantène and L'Isule. The village was destroyed by the Goths and rebuilt around the abbey of the Basilian monks.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele, Cisternino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Piazza Vittorio Emanuele e Torre dell'orologio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Airy square in the center of the four historic districts, with the clock tower; the Touring indicates it as an ideal place for an aperitif.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele, Cisternino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Torre normanno-sveva (Torre Grande) e Porta Grande

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

A 17-metre-high quadrangular tower built by the Normans in the 11th century, rebuilt at the end of the 14th century and renovated several times; it was a sighting point on the Itria Valley and at the top has a statue of St. Nicholas Benedict. Next to it, the Porta Grande is the main access to the old town.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Cisternino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa Madre di San Nicola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in the 12th century on a Basilian church of the 8th century, with neoclassical façade of 1848 and a 16th century baptismal font. It preserves the Madonna del Cardellino, carved in 1517 by Stefano da Putignano, which the Touring defines the most valuable art treasure of the village.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Cisternino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The viewpoint behind the Villa municipale offers, according to Touring, the best view of the Itria Valley and the towns that dot it.

Palazzi storici del centro

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

The Bishop's Palace (1560) has a late Renaissance façade with the coat of arms of the Baron bishop; the Governor's Palace (16th century) has three balconies and Renaissance decorations; in the village also the houses of the Pepe and Cenci families and Lagravinese palace.

  • Where Centro storico, Cisternino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiese del borgo: Purgatorio, Santa Lucia, San Cataldo, San Quirico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Church of Purgatory (XVII century) rises on a primitive church of about 1000 recently discovered; Saint Lucia is of the seventeenth century; San Cataldo was completed in 1783 in Baroque style with dynamic façade; San Quirico was built between the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries.

  • Where Centro storico, Cisternino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Fornelli del centro storico

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Butcheries where you choose meat (bumbette, sausages, gnummeredde, braciole) and they are roasted at the moment; Cisternino is considered the undisputed capital of the cookers of the Valle d'Itria.

  • Where Centro storico, Cisternino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it
