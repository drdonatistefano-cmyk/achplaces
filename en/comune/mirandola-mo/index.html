What to see in Mirandola

The capital for four centuries of the Pico, the city of Giovanni Pico della Mirandola was the epicenter of the earthquake of May 2012: the Duomo reopened in 2019, the Piazza della Constituente with the remains of the castle, the ruins of the church of San Francesco pantheon dei Pico, the museum of the biomedical district and, in the countryside, the marsh valleys with the boats and the bike for Finale Emilia.

9 places to visit in Mirandola, in the province of Modena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Made to be built in 1447 by Giovanni and Francesco Pico in late-Gothic forms, with original polygonal flanks and apses, 17th century bell tower and neo-Gothic façade; severely damaged by the 2012 earthquake, it was restored and reopened in 2019. Among the works mentioned by the Touring two paintings by Sante Peranda, a Crucifix frescoed by Pietro Annigoni (1983), a seventeenth-century ancone in golden wood of school mirandolese and, in the sacristy, a canvas by Alessandro Tiarini (1612).

  • Where Mirandola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it

Piazza della Costituente e Castello dei Pico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient Piazza Grande, wide and tree-lined: on the side of the castle of the Pico, whose fortifications were lost with the explosion of the powder keg of 1714, remain a seventeenth-century porch and the facade of the Galleria Nuova next to a neo-Gothic tower of the twentieth century and the Teatro Nuovo (1905). On the south side, the 1468 Palazzo Comunale with portico on marble columns, flanked by the Palazzo della Ragione, with Gothic elements of the late fourteenth century, and Palazzo Bergomi, fifteenth century with ornate terracotta decorations; to the north is the oratory of the Madonna della Porta.

Castello dei Pico, Palazzo Comunale and Oratorio della Madonna della Porta are closed for restorations after the 2012 earthquake: they can only be seen from the outside

  • Where Piazza della Costituente, Mirandola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Already existing in the thirteenth century and rebuilt in the fifteenth century in Gothic forms such as Pico's pantheon, it kept the marble arches of the lords of Mirandola, including that of Prendiparte by Pier Paolo and Jacobello dagli Masegne (1394), and the nineteenth-century cenotaph of Giovanni Pico. The 2012 earthquake almost completely destroyed it, including the bell tower: only the facade was saved.

Almost destroyed by the 2012 earthquake and closed for restoration: the facade remains visible

Chiesa del Santissimo Nome di Gesù

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Started in 1621, with unfinished façade: inside a single room full of stuccoes and statues of the Six and eighteenth century and altars with wooden frames carved from the school mirandolese; next to the former Jesuit college, established in 1690.

The collapse of the roof with the 2012 earthquake damaged the interior: check if the church is open

  • Entry To be checked

Museo del Biomedicale (Mobimed)

Museums · Themed

Permanent exhibition on the products and history of the biomedical district of Mirandole, considered the most important in Europe: opened in May 2010 in the Castle of the Pico, after the earthquake was reopened on 27 May 2013 in Via Focherini.

  • Where Via O. Focherini 17, Mirandola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Pieve di Santa Maria della Neve a Quarantoli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the locality of origin of the Pico, a parish church built in the IX century, documented in 1044 and rebuilt in the XII century: seventeenth-century facade, fifteenth-century bell tower, an ambo attributed to the school of Wiligelmo supported by two telamones and a loggia of the XII century with baptismal chapel, palotto in Carpigiana scagliola (1686) and a Madonna of Venetian-Cretan school.

Seriously damaged by the 2012 earthquake: check if it is open

  • Where Quarantoli, Mirandola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Valli mirandolesi e Barchessone Vecchio

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Special protection zone for birds of about 800 hectares between wetlands, ponds, meadows and woods, birdwatcher destination. At the gates of the valleys, south of San Martino Spino, the Barchessone Vecchio, a singular sixteen-sided building born as a stopover station of horses (the boats were the host of the army horses until 1861), of municipal property: open Saturday and Sunday 15.30-19.30 with free bike rental on weekends. The nearby Barchessone Portovecchio, with a collection of equestrian and rural traditions, is visited on the second Sunday of the month.

  • Where San Martino Spino, Mirandola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · visitmodena.it · comune.mirandola.mo.it

Stazione Ornitologica Modenese «Il Pettazzurro»

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Observatory run by volunteers who follow migrations: 312 species of birds have been recorded in the area, with days dedicated to birdwatchers and natural photographers.

  • Where Via Montirone, Mortizzuolo, Mirandola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Ciclabile Mirandola-Finale Emilia

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 30 km on the route of a 19th century railway, on the ground floor and almost all away from traffic, suitable for families: it starts from the historic center of Mirandola.

  • Where Centro storico, Mirandola
  • Entry Free
