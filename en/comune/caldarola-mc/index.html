What to see in Caldarola

Cittadina dell'alto Chienti shaped by Cardinal Evangelista Pallotta, with the Pallotta castle (restored after the earthquake 2016), the town hall with the Paradise Room by Simone De Magistris, the Collegiate Church of San Martino, the castles of Vestignano and Cross and the lake of Pievefavera.

Castello Pallotta

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Castle on a hill with origins in the early Middle Ages, gods from Varano from 1240 to 1434, then Maurizi and finally Pallotta, still owners; at the end of the sixteenth century Cardinal Evangelista Pallotta transformed it into summer residence and hosted Clement VIII in 1598 (and then Cristina of Sweden in 1666). You enter from Porta Camerte and a drawbridge; the museum has weapons from the 13th to the 17th century, carriages from the 19th to the 19th century, salt with antique furnishings, ceramics from the Marche and a frieze attributed to Simone De Magistris, and in the park a pine planted in 1598.

Temporarily closed to the public for restoration works after the earthquake of 2016

Palazzo Pallotta e Stanza del Paradiso

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Former cardinal residence, today the town hall: on two sides surrounds the square Vittorio Emanuele II wanted in the sixteenth century by Cardinal Pallotta and forms a single complex with the Collegiate. Inside the Hall of Paradise, frescoed by Simone De Magistris.

Collegiata di San Martino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Teatro comunale

Castello e chiesa di San Martino a Vestignano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Castle donated by Ludovico II to the monastery of Casauria and then passed to the Varano; the annexed church, of thirteenth-century origin, has frescoes of the first fifteenth-century of the Master of Rasiglia and paintings of Nobile da Lucca, Giovanni and Simone De Magistris of the first half of the sixteenth century.

Lago di Pievefavera e castello

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Artificial basin created in 1954 for the electricity of the neighbouring countries, with a Roman archaeological area on the south bank; above the lake the castle of Pievefavera, of the thirteenth century.
