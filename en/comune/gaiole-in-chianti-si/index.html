Castello di Brolio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Castello di origine Longobarda dei Ricasoli since 1141, considered the oldest winery in Italy: you can visit the garden, the museum and the ramparts, with tastings and guided tours; the residence of the baron remains private.

  • Where SP484, Gaiole in Chianti
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Badia a Coltibuono

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Vallombrosan monastery documented since 1037, sold at auction in the Napoleonic era and today winery with hospitality.

  • Where Badia a Coltibuono, Gaiole in Chianti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Castello di Vertine e Pieve di Spaltenna

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The small medieval walled village of Vertine and the parish church of Spaltenna with a fifteenth-century crucifix, along the tour of Gaiole castles.

  • Where Vertine, Gaiole in Chianti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
