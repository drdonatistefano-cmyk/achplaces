What to see in Castiglione della Pescaia

Maremma's seaside village enclosed by ancient walls on the Poggio Petriccio hill, with panoramic castle and canal port on Bruna; in the territory the Etruscan city of Vetulonia with necropolis and museum, and the wet area of the Diaccia Botrona with the Casa Rossa Ximenes.

13 places to visit in Castiglione della Pescaia, in the province of Grosseto (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Area archeologica di Vetulonia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Remains of the Etruscan Vatluna, at the most splendor in the 7th century BC and rediscovered at the end of the 19th century by Isidoro Falchi: the walls of the Arce on the acropolis, the districts of Poggiarello Renzetti (with the Via Decumana and the Casa Medea) and Costa Murata, the Domus dei Dolia and, along the Via dei Sepolcri, the tombs of the Belvedere, the Pietrera (the largest funeral monument of Vetulonia) and the Diavolino II.

  • Where Vetulonia, Castiglione della Pescaia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · cultura.gov.it · italia.it · museoisidorofalchi.it

Museo civico archeologico Isidoro Falchi

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Born as Antiquarium of Vetulonia and reopened expanded in June 2000, it exhibits in seven rooms on two floors the artifacts of the city: urns Villanovian Poggio alla Guardia, gold and silver sets of the circles of the Silver Leoncini and the Golden Fibula, the stele of the warrior Auvele Feluske and an alphabet engraved of Hellenistic age.

  • Where Piazza Vetluna, Vetulonia, Castiglione della Pescaia
  • Entry €5, reduced €2.50 (museum and exhibition €7, reduced €5)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visittuscany.com · italia.it · museoisidorofalchi.it

Casa Rossa Ximenes, museo multimediale

Museums · Themed

Building on three arches designed by Jesuit Leonardo Ximenes under Pietro Leopoldo of Lorraine for the reclamation of the Maremma, regulated the outflow of the swamp waters. It houses the Museum of the Diaccia Botrona Reserve, with documentaries, local models and a virtual hut to learn how to recognize birds in flight; from here start birdwatching and boat visits to the swamp.

  • Where Via Casa Rossa Ximenes, Castiglione della Pescaia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Riserva naturale Diaccia Botrona

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

Considered the most important wetland in Italy, it is a web of canals, water mirrors, reeds and swamps populated by many species of aquatic birds, residue of the ancient lake Prile; you can walk or horse.

  • Where Via Casa Rossa Ximenes, Castiglione della Pescaia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visittuscany.com

Isola Clodia

History and architecture · Archaeology

Small height at the northern end of the Diaccia Botrona, once surrounded by the waters of Lake Prile and landing of the Etruscan boats: there arose in the first century BC a Roman villa attributed to Clodio, then incorporated in a Benedictine abbey of the early Middle Ages of which remains the Romanesque church to a nave with semicircular apse.

  • Where Diaccia Botrona, Castiglione della Pescaia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · italia.it

Palazzo Centurioni

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Renaissance building just inside the walls of the village, with rectangular plan with plastered facade and rustic around windows and gates; it was the seat of the Florentine Podestà and today hosts exhibitions and cultural events.

Eremo di San Guglielmo di Malavalle

Medieval complex nestled in a dense forest, of Benedictine architecture, with a Romanesque church of the thirteenth century; it is a destination of pilgrims for the tomb of San Guglielmo di Malavalle.
