What to see in Pontremoli

The northernmost city of Tuscany, on the Via Francigena at the foot of Cisa, with the mysterious stele statues of Lunigiana.

3 places to visit in Pontremoli, in the province of Massa-Carrara (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 2 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Museo delle Statue Stele nel Castello del Piagnaro

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In the 11th century castle the human figures carved in sandstone between the 4th and 1st millennium BC, among the most important testimonies of European megalithicism.

  • Where Via Piagnaro, Pontremoli
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.statuestele.org

Sources: statuestele.org · visittuscany.com

Cattedrale e Campanone

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The cathedral with polychrome marbles and the Bell Tower, today symbol of the city, in the historic center.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Pontremoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Santissima Annunziata

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Just outside the city, the church with a small temple in polychrome marble of Sansovino.

  • Where Pontremoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
