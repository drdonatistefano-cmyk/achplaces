What to see in Verbicaro

Wine village Doc above the Riviera dei Cedri, with the medieval nucleus of Bonifanti and the Torre dell'Orologio, the chapel of the Madonna della Snow with frescoes of the sixteenth century, the mother church of the Assunta and the Ecomuseum of wine in the former prison; famous the rite of the Battenti of Holy Thursday.

Ecomuseo del vino e della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Set up in the rooms of the former restored prison, it exhibits objects of vinification and peasant life of a time in a path in four sections: environment, grape, cleft and religious rituals; woman and machines; in the cell of isolation the cholera revolt is linked to the epidemics of the nineteenth and twentieth centuries; the house. A video shows the ancient traditions. Booking not required; MiC (2020) does not report up-to-date times.

Chiesa della Madonna della Neve

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

On top of a rock reachable by narrow alleys, with views of the valley between Verbicaro and Grisolia, it is considered the oldest monument of the village. The frescoes, covered with lime, re-emerged in the eighties and were restored by the Superintendence: St. Mark and St. Leonard, dated 1539 and commissioned by Donna Domenica De Donato, a Pietà, a Madonna with Child and a cycle of the XIV-XV centuries with saints and Madonnas; inside a small wooden statue of the Madonna enthroned.

  • Where Centro storico, Verbicaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Borgo di Bonifanti e Torre dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval nucleus of the 11th century was surrounded by walls with three doors (via Bonifanti, via Pampanara, via Orologio) closed at night against pirate and Saracen raids: alleys and stone stairs, houses almost overhanging the rock and portals carved by local chisels. The Clock Tower, built by local workers, housed the civic clock; in the rest of the historic center, the portal of Via Giardino, the external stairs and the ancient fountain bas-relief of Vico Vignale.

  • Where Via Bonifanti, Verbicaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.verbicaro.cs.it

Sources: comune.verbicaro.cs.it

Chiesa madre dell'Assunta e Fontana Vecchia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Begin in the early sixteenth century, it was demolished between 1880 and 1888 for the landslides and rebuilt on the remains, with restorations until 2006 that brought to light pillars, floor and 13 burials of the previous church. Classical stone façade, a single nave with four chapels on each side, a table of the Madonna with saints attributed to Genesio Galtieri, two wooden organs and in sacristy furnishings of the XVI-XVII century. Not far away is the Old Fountain, fountain and washbasin of 1816 with a surviving marble mask, next to the old Town Hall.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele, Verbicaro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.verbicaro.cs.it

Chiesa di San Giuseppe

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Begin in 1897 and open to worship in 1922, it has a single nave with stuccoes and paintings on the life of St. Joseph, wooden and paper sculptures and a wooden organ; it preserves the sixteenth-century stone portal of the demolished Dominican church. His confraternity, founded in 1751, organizes the procession of the Mysteries of Holy Week, with the rite of the Battenti of Holy Thursday and the procession of Friday that leaves at 3 in the morning.

  • Entry Free

Chiesa della Madonna del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built between 1895 and 1896 and consecrated in 1897 in Pampanara, in place of the medieval castle with Petrier and Saettiere demolished at the end of the nineteenth century; inside paintings and a mache statue of the Madonna del Carmine, celebrated on 16 July.

Chiesa di Santa Maria di Loreto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of medieval origin in the open countryside, about two kilometers from the village on the left side of the valley of the Abatemarco, where around the tenth century flourished a Basilian female romerium; inside a Madonna with Child in mache and frescoes in the apse, above the portal a stone statue of the Madonna.

Santuario di San Francesco di Paola

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill above the hill, about three kilometers from the village, it was built at the end of the nineteenth century with the offerings collected by the hermit between Giuseppe Cetraro and the owners of the vineyards, because the saint is protector of the vineyards; diocesan sanctuary since 1997, with a single nave with three arched porch and bell tower. The party is held on the second Sunday of September.

  • Entry Free

Catuvi e vino di Verbicaro

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Verbicaro was considered the capital of Calabrian winemaking and its wine has been Doc since 1995; it survives the crushing in the palm and in the casts, the cellars where the barrels are preserved and you find yourself eating and singing. On December 8th, the feast of the "perciavutt" opens the castuvi with tastings of cold cuts and wine.
