What to see in Castelnuovo di Garfagnana

The capital of the Garfagnana dominated by the Ariostesca Rocca, where Ludovico Ariosto was governor extended from 1522 to 1525 and which today houses the Palazzo di Atlante dedicated to the Orlando Furioso; with the Cathedral of Saints Peter and Paul and the Black Christ, and on the hill the Fortress of Mont'Alfonso, reachable with the Path of Ariosto.

Rocca Ariostesca e Palazzo di Atlante (Museo Furioso)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Of trapezoidal plan, it dominates Piazza Umberto I: on a small fortress of the tenth century, the structure of the twelfth century was enlarged in the thirteenth century, the central tower with the civic clock is owed to Paolo Guinigi and in 1675 the terrace on the square and the monumental arch were added. Ludovico Ariosto resided there from 1522 to 1525 as governor of the extended province; municipal seat until the war, it was severely bombed in 1944 and then restored. It houses the Palazzo di Atlante, a museum dedicated to Ariosto that combines contemporary art and traditions of Garfagnana, with a multimedia installation in the bell tower on the first two episodes of the Orlando Furioso, Follia d'Orlando and Â"Astolfo sulla Luna" .

Duomo dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Rebuilt between the late fifteenth and early sixteenth centuries on the remains of a Romanesque church, with interior renovated in the Baroque age and apse restored in the nineteenth century. It preserves a Saint Joseph in glazed terracotta of the workshop of Andrea della Robbia, an Assumption attributed to Saints of Tito, a Madonna with Child and saints of author discussed (Giuseppe Porta or Giovanni Antonio Sogliani) and, in the chapel of St. Joseph, the fifteenth-century wooden crucifix known as "Cristo Nero" with marble frame attributed to the workshop of Matteo Civitali.

Fortezza di Mont'Alfonso

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built between 1579 and 1586 for Alfonso II d'Este on a medieval village, the last bulwark of the Duchy of Ferrara on the border with Lucca; in the Napoleonic age passed to the Principality of Lucca and Piombino and in the twentieth century was summer residence of the Italian-Scotland family Bechelli, who transformed a building into a liberty villa. Since 1980 it has been of the Province of Lucca and houses provincial offices and the Park of the Apuan Alps: about 1,150 meters of wall with 7 bastions enclose the house of the captain and the quarters of the troops of the sixteenth century, on about 60,000 m2. Exhibitions and events, including the festival "Mont'Alfonso" under the stars "Stars," and 360 degree views of the Tuscan-Emilian Apennines, Pania and Apuane.

Sentiero d'Ariosto

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Hiking route of about 6 km that connects the Ariostesca Rocca to the Fortezza di Mont'Alfonso: from the Rocca follow via Farini and cross the bridge over the Turrite Secca, where the white-red trails begin, then a dirt road leads to the village of Torrite, from which it climbs to the Fortezza. In Castelnuovo there are also the Via Matildica del Volto Santo and the Via del Volto Santo, which ends at the Duomo of Lucca.

  • Where Partenza dalla Rocca Ariostesca, Castelnuovo di Garfagnana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
