What to see in Celico

Birthplace of Abbot Gioacchino da Fiore, at the gates of Sila: tradition places his birthplace under the church of the Assumption and his baptism in the parish of San Michele Arcangelo; the territory reaches to Lake Cecita, where grows a chestnut of about five centuries tied to San Francesco di Paola.

Chiesa dell'Assunta e casa natale di Gioacchino da Fiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built well before 1421 and named after the Confraternity of the Assumption, which had its seat in it, according to tradition, it stands on the birthplace of Abbot Joachim: the corridor of the sacristy would occupy the room in which it was born. An ogival portal introduces to the hall with a single nave; the main altar in stucco is dedicated to the Assumption, with a canvas of St. Peter Martyr, a statue of St. Orsola and nineteenth-century paintings on the ceiling with stories of the Virgin. It is the stop of the Gioachimita Route of Sila Park.

Chiesa di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church in which, according to the municipality, Gioacchino da Fiore was baptized: three naves with basilica plant and presbytery with dome after 1753; damaged by the earthquake of 1905 and restored in 1906. It houses the ceiling painted in 1787 by Cristoforo Santanna, the altarpiece of Our Lady of Heaven with Saints James and John the Baptist of Theodorus of Henry the Flemish, a wooden crucifix of the fifteenth century and a glass blown in the middle of the sixteenth century. Open every day 7-21.

  • Where Via G. Greco 23, Celico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page parcosila.it

Sources: comune.celico.cs.it · parcosila.it

Museo delle Tradizioni Rurali dell'Altopiano Silano

Museums · Themed

Set up in 2003 by the Vagabondaggi group .On the traces of Norman Douglas in the Resource Centre of the Istituto Comprehensivo .Abate Gioacchino . documents work and domestic life on the plateau: section with materials of the Arssa Historical Archives, the cutting of wood in the woods, transhumance, the cultivation of wheat and potato and the seventeenth-century Casino Lupinacci, the first inhabited nucleus of Lagarò.

Castagno di San Francesco

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Castagno called dark bottle, estimated between 4 and 5 centuries, with hollow trunk and blackened about 12,85 meters of circumference and crown of about 15,5 meters; for the tradition it was born from a chestnut thrown to the ground by San Francesco di Paola. The municipality has asked for its recognition as a monumental plant. It rises 1,160 metres in a clearing about 150 metres from Lake Cecita, which can be reached by car, by bike, on foot or by the Parks Cyclovia.

the tree is located on a private plot
