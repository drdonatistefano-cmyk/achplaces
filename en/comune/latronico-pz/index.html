What to see in Latronico

Country on the slopes of Monte Alpi on the Lucanian side of the Pollino National Park, known for the thermal baths and caves of Calda inhabited by the prehistory, the MULA+ museum pole and the works of Anish Kapoor and Michelangelo Pistoletto in the thermal park.

7 places to visit in Latronico, in the province of Potenza (Basilicata). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

In the hamlet Calda, part of the MULA+ pole, in two rooms (Prehistory and Historical Age): finds of the caves of Calda from 8000 to 1300 BC and accessories of the necropolis probably enotria of Colle dei Greci (VII-V century BC), with Greek vases, weapons, bronze kisses, statuettes, ambres and the skeleton of a burial of about 2500 years ago. Open Friday-Sunday and holidays 10-13 and 16-19.

  • Where Via Provinciale SP 101, Calda, Latronico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · basilicataturistica.it

Museo delle arti, dei mestieri e della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

In the MULA+ pole, over 780 pieces of agricultural tools and artisan crafts tools, with panels on the cycles of wheat and grapes; in the same complex the Museum of Thermalism, set up in the old spa of 1928.

  • Where Calda, Latronico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: basilicataturistica.it · italia.it

Grotte di Calda

History and architecture · Archaeology

At the spa, five caves with stalactitic formations and springs have returned stone and bone tools and ceramics from the Neolithic to the Bronze Age (8000-1300 BC); sacred deposits and votive enclosures suggest an ancient cult of healthy waters.

  • Where Calda, Latronico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Parco termale e Terme Lucane

Local life and traditions · Spas · Free entry

Free entrance spa park with tour guide service, where you will find the Earth Cinema of Anish Kapoor, a large excavation in the ground that resounds of the sounds of the earth, and the Terzo Paradiso by Michelangelo Pistoletto; from May to October the Lucane Thermal Baths offer saunas, Turkish bath and emotional showers.

  • Where Calda, Latronico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Lastra fossile di Contrada Iannazzo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Large stone plate, 235 cm long and 95 cm high, with the fossil of a fish of about 30 million years ago, with a 30 cm rostrum, testimony of the marine environment of the Miocene.

  • Where Contrada Iannazzo, Latronico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
