What to see in Fidenza

Historical stop of the Via Francigena, the ancient Borgo San Donnino is dominated by the Romanesque Cathedral with the facade of the workshop of Benedetto Antelami, between medieval gate, Gothic town hall and the Magnani theatre.

11 places to visit in Fidenza, in the province of Parma (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Fidenza in the guide with map and filters

Duomo di San Donnino

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

Masterpiece of the Romanesque padan in three phases (XI-XIII century): the three-door façade with statues of Davide and Ezekiel is from the workshop of Benedetto Antelami, the bell tower of 1569; inside the Christ judge in the apse basin, the final judgment of the thirteenth century and works of Vincenzo Campi, Chiaveghino and Ferdinando Bibiena; in the crypt a Roman sarcophagus. Pilgrim's stop on the Via Francigena.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Fidenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Museo del Duomo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Two sections, in the Bishop's Palace and in the northern matron of the cathedral: the chalice of St. Donnino (Renish goldsmith's church of the XII-XIII century), the Eucharistic dove in bronze, the Madonna in throne with the Child attributed to Benedetto Antelami and the Treasury Hall with works from the XI to the XVI century, gifts of pilgrims. Open Saturday and Sunday, in the weekdays only groups by reservation.

  • Where Via Don Minzoni 10/a, Fidenza
  • Entry 7 €
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Porta San Donnino e Antiquarium

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Ancient medieval entrance with ogival arch next to the Duomo; in the door and in the Cremonini house an Antiquarium with sculptures, mosaics and an epigraph in bronze, and under the door the remains of a Roman bridge of Augustian age on the Stirone. Along Via Frate Gherardo run the Visconti walls.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Fidenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Teatro Girolamo Magnani

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Started in 1812 on a project by Nicola Bettoli on the area of the convent of San Francesco and completed in 1861, with stuccoes and gold by the set designer Girolamo Magnani; the frescoes of the dome were restored in 2004.

  • Where Fidenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Palazzo comunale e piazza Garibaldi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Palace attested since 1191, with crenellated façade and portico with Gothic arches of the early fourteenth century, wings and sixteenth-century tower; to the east of the square there are remains of Roman age.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Fidenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Museo del Risorgimento «Luigi Musini»

Museums · City history · Free entry

Heirlooms and documents from the Napoleonic age to the Resistance, donated largely by the son of the Garibaldi Luigi Musini, including the album of the Thousands with photographs of the protagonists of the 1860 expedition. Free admission, open on weekdays.

  • Where Palazzo delle Orsoline, via dei Mille, Fidenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.fidenza.pr.it · italia.it

Museo dei fossili dello Stirone

Museums · Science and university

Founded in 1980 by the Paleontophilo Fidentino Group: in three fossil rooms of the Stirone stream, shells of molluscs, sea stars, remains of whales and plants, which tell the geological history of Parmense. Times to check with the museum.

  • Where Via Costa 2, Palazzo delle Orsoline, Fidenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it · italia.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It dates back to the 13th century, rebuilt in the first half of the 16th century, with facade of 1893 by Giovanni Musini and interior with Greek cross with 16th century frescoes.

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Chiesa di San Tommaso Becket a Cabriolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Founded between the 12th and 13th centuries and linked to a pceptory of the Knights of Malta, it preserves the arched apse and frescoes of the 14th century with angels, the archangel Michael and the Crucifixion.
