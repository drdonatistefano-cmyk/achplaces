What to see in Carbognano

Borgo della Tuscia linked to Giulia Farnese, sister of Pope Paul III, who since 1506, was a feudal man for 25 years and renewed it: the Farnese Castle, the sixteenth-century church of Santa Maria and the small Saint Lucia, to which the church of San Filippo Neri is added (1636).

Castello Farnese

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Residency of Giulia Farnese "La Bella" (Bella Bella), sister of Pope Paul III, who ruled Carbognano from 1506 for 25 years (his name is engraved on the portal): under her the village, reduced to 25 inhabitants by pestilences, came to about 2.000 and hosted personalities like the Zuccari brothers. In August 2021 part of the vaults of the halls of the noble floor and the ground floor collapsed; the Superintendence is treating restoration and consolidation.

Ex Chiesa di Santa Maria dell'Immacolata Concezione

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

Wanted by Giulia Farnese to venerate the Immaculate Conception and built in 1522 (inscription on the portal) instead of a smaller chapel, of which Gothic mullioned windows remain. Façade with a bezel portal, rose window and a small asymmetrical bell tower; inside the single nave with a trussed roof, three Renaissance windows on the side and an apse with paintings dated 1581, attributed to students of the brothers Zuccari; the restoration started in 2006 brought to light frescoes waiting for restoration. Today it is the municipality and hosts exhibitions and conferences; the tradition wants the remains of Giulia Farnese to be hidden.

Chiesa di San Filippo Neri

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Started in 1626 (first Mass on 26 March) by the Oratorian Father Orazio Giustiniani and finished in 1636, as the inscription on the portal says; Visit Lazio indicates it as the first church in the world dedicated to the saint. The façade is sober and internal baroque with a single nave, with on the main altar a copy of the "Estasi di San Filippo Neri" by Guido Reni; the figure of the saint in the facade niche is Fabio Carosi (2008).

  • Where Piazza Roma, Carbognano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: comune.carbognano.vt.it · visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Santa Lucia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Small church with the characteristics of a castle chapel inside the medieval village, probably of the XIII-XIV centuries, with the curious entrance wall curved to the apse: the legend wants Giulia Farnese to do so to reach it directly from the castle. It was the seat of the Confraternity of the Disciplined; a unique hall with wooden trusses and an eighteenth-century canvas of the Martyrdom of Saint Lucia. Restored in 2006.

  • Where Piazza Santa Lucia 131, Carbognano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com
