What to see in Fortunago

Small village on the hills of Oltrepò Pavese, among oak and chestnut woods, with stone houses, wooden fixtures and paved streets in porphyry; to see the seventeenth-century church of San Giorgio with the triptych of Pesina and, just below, the remains of the castle with the fifteenth-century tower.

Centro storico di Fortunago

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Village surrounded by oak and chestnut woods, with small houses with stone facades, doors and windows in natural wood, paved streets in porphyry that go up the hill and the bell tower. The Town Hall occupies an ancient strong house. The spring, with the flowering of the meadows, is indicated as the best time to visit.

  • Where Fortunago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Chiesa di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church of the seventeenth century with an Annunciation frescoed in the bezel above the portal; inside a wooden triptych in tempera with the central compartment signed by the Pesina.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Resti del castello e torre quattrocentesca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Just below the church of San Giorgio are traces of the walls of the ancient castle and a quadrangular tower of the fifteenth century, the last remnant of the ancient fortress.

  • Where Presso la chiesa di San Giorgio, Fortunago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Parco locale e boschi di Fortunago

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

A recently established local park that includes the capital and the hamlets, with high hill forests of maples, poplars, birch, chestnuts and cherry trees among cultivated fields and views of the hills.

  • Entry Free
