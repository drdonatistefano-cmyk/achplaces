What to see in Parcines

Birthplace of Peter Mitterhofer, inventor of the typewriter, at the gates of Merano: the museum dedicated to him, the waterfall of almost 100 meters in the Natural Park Tessa Group, the Royal and Imperial Museum of Bad Egart in Tel and the World train of Rablà.

7 places to visit in Parcines, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della macchina da scrivere Peter Mitterhofer

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Set up by the Municipality in 1998 with the collection donated by Kurt Ryba, in honor of Peter Mitterhofer, who invented the typewriter in Parcines: over 2,000 objects (about 400 in the museum and 1,600 in a showroom) tell the story from the first wooden model of 1864 to the 1980s, with the cipher machine \"Enigma" of the Second World War.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa 10, Parcines
  • Entry €7, reduced €6,30, up to 16 years and students €2, Family Card €15
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · suedtirol.info · merano-suedtirol.it

Museo Bagni Egart - Museo reale e imperiale

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Bad Egart is the oldest spa house in Tyrol, with a source documented since 1430. The route includes the thermal caves, portraits and objects of the Habsburg monarchy, a Biedermeier kitchen, objects of Tyrolean folk history and a garden with works of art; the special exhibition on Sisi and Francesco Giuseppe exhibits over 4,000 objects.

  • Where Via Stazione 17, Tel (Parcines)
  • Entry €13.50, reduced €12.50, pupils €6.50 (prices 2024)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it

Sources: merano-suedtirol.it

Mondo treno (Eisenbahnwelt)

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Museum of railway modeling on over 1,000 m2 and three floors, presented as the largest digital installation of miniature railways in Italy: over 20,000 pieces in scale 1:87, 40 trains in motion, 7,000 figurines and 400 m of tracks; on the top floor a plastic of 130 m2 reproduces South Tyrol in miniature and children can drive a train.

  • Where Piazza Gerold 3, Rablà (Parcines)
  • Entry €12, children 3-14 years €8.50, reduced €9 (price 2025)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · cultura.gov.it · merano-suedtirol.it

Cascata di Parcines

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Waterfall about 97 meters high in the Gruppo di Tessa Nature Park, considered one of the most spectacular in the Alps: the water of the Zielbach river falls from a rock wall, fed by a steep gorge about 8 km long. The maximum flow rate is from May to July, with the snow melting; a marked path leads to a panoramic platform.

  • Where Parcines (parcheggio Birkenwald; bus linea 265)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it

Sources: merano-suedtirol.it

Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Documented from 1264 but of more ancient origin, built on the foundations of a early Christian chapel; renovated in late Dogotic style around 1502 and enlarged in the sixteenth-sixth century, with chapel of the Madonna of about 1350. In the presbytery, the group of the "Death of the Virgin" by Jörg Lederer of Kaufbeuren, the only remnant of the late-Gothic high altar.

  • Where Piazza della Chiesa, Parcines
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · merano-suedtirol.it

Cappella di San Giacomo a Rablà

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Late-Gothic chapel built in 1513, with two sixteenth-century sundials on the outer wall.

  • Where Rablà (Parcines)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it

Sources: merano-suedtirol.it

Laghetto di Rablà e giro di Bad Egart

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Biotope near the station of Rablà, along the bike path of the Via Claudia Augusta, fed by the Zielbach river: there live royal germans, herons, herons, white dancers, amphibians and dragonflies. From here starts a ring walk, suitable for families and strollers, towards Bad Egart.

  • Where Presso la stazione ferroviaria di Rablà, Parcines
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it

Sources: merano-suedtirol.it
