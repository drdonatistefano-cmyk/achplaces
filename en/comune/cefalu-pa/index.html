What to see in Cefalù

A seaside village with the Norman cathedral, part of the Arab-Norman UNESCO itinerary; one of the most beautiful villages in Italy.

6 places to visit in Cefalù, in the province of Palermo (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 2 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Cattedrale di Cefalù

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

One of the two cathedrals symbolizing Norman power, stage of the Arab-Norman UNESCO itinerary.

  • Where Cefalù
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it

Rocca di Cefalù e Tempio di Diana

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The fortress about 270 meters high that dominates the village: in half an hour of ascent you reach the blackbirds, the remains of the Temple of Diana linked to the cult of water and a castle of the twelfth century.

  • Where Rocca di Cefalù, Cefalù
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo Mandralisca

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

The collections of Baron Enrico Pirajno of Mandralisca: art gallery, archaeology, coins, one of the richest collections of shells in Europe and the Portrait of Man by Antonello da Messina (1460-1470).

  • Where Via Mandralisca 13, Cefalù
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Lavatoio medievale

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The washbasin excavated in the rock and fed by the river Cefalino, active until not long ago, reachable by a staircase in lava stone.

  • Where Cefalù
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Porticciolo e Porta Pescara

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The old fishing port with its small beach, the 70-metre pier and Porta Pescara, the last of the four medieval gates, with colorful boats on the sand.

  • Where Cefalù
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Lido di Cefalù

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Wide crescent of fine sand with stretches of free beach and sweet seabed, suitable for children, with the rock and the cathedral in the background.

  • Where Cefalù
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
