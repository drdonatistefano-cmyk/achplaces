What to see in Frabosa Sottana

Monregalese Valleys with the stations of Prato Nevoso and Artesina in the Mondolè area: at the bottom of the valley the Caudano Caves, discovered in 1898, and the Water Path, in Miroglio the Museum of the Mountain and its People.

5 places to visit in Frabosa Sottana, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Grotte del Caudano

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

System of caves and corridors discovered in 1898 and visited since the beginning of the twentieth century, with stalactites and millenarian stalagmites: the tourist route is spread over 3 km of galleries distributed over 4 overlapping floors, still inhabited by numerous bats. Constant temperature of about 9 °C; visits by reservation, inside the Mondolè Park with adventure park.

  • Where Via Mondolè, Frabosa Sottana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Museo della Montagna e della sua Gente

Museums · Themed

Ethnographic museum in the former restored school of the village Miroglio: reconstructs domestic interiors and old crafts of the first half of the twentieth century with about 600 tools and objects of peasants and mountaineers. Open only by reservation (tel. +39 0174 244481).

  • Where Via Ponte 15, frazione Miroglio, Frabosa Sottana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Sentiero dell'Acqua

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

4,8 km hiking ring (about 2 hours, 250 m of difference in altitude, difficulty E) curated by CAI di Mondovì, starting from the Caudano Caves ticket office: along the water pipeline to the Davì Rock, wall used for climbing, then to the village Crevirola (879 m), with views of the Monregalese to the dome of the Sanctuary of Vicoforte, and return between mule tracks and chestnut trees. Recommended from March to October.

  • Where Partenza Grotte del Caudano, Frabosa Sottana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Prato Nevoso

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

1,500 m station of the Mondolè Ski area (over 100 km of slopes and 30 lifts with Artesina and Frabosa Soprana): more than 36 km of slopes with views up to Monviso and Cervino, the panoramic cable car "La Rossa" can also be used by pedestrians, snowparks, snowtubing and night skiing.

  • Where Via Corona Boreale 1, Prato Nevoso, Frabosa Sottana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Artesina e Panchina Gigante

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

The ski resort of Mondolè offers summer tubing, bike park and a bob descent on a monorail of 1.3 km; in the area the giant red bench of Chris Bangle, reachable all year round, from which on clear days you can see the sea from 1,500 meters. The network of trails Mondolè Ring you can walk, bike or horseback.

  • Where Artesina, Frabosa Sottana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
