What to see in Pertica Bassa

Municipality of Pertiche in Valle Sabbia, with churches rich in wood art of Boscaì and Bonomi and the Museum of Resistance and Folklore Valsabbino in Forno d'Ono, at the foot of Corna Blacca; in the hamlets the sanctuary of the Madonna del Pianto in Ono Degno and the sixteenth-century village of Spessio.

7 places to visit in Pertica Bassa, in the province of Brescia (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Resistenza e del folklore valsabbino

Museums · Themed · Free entry

At the foot of the Corna Blacca, unique in the province, it was born from a gesture of gratitude of Professor Dimitrije Paramendic, teacher, painter and sculptor of Slavic origin. It preserves objects and documents related mainly to the partisan Brigade Perlasca, active in the area at the end of the Second World War, and a section on agricultural and mountain traditions and on the daily life of the valley. Open on the first and third Sundays of the month from 15 to 19 or by reservation (tel. 0365 821131).

  • Where Forno d'Ono, Pertica Bassa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.perticabassa.bs.it

Sources: visitbrescia.it · comune.perticabassa.bs.it

Santuario della Madonna del Pianto

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

It rises in the hamlet Blessed Virgin of Ono Degno, in memory of a miracle of 1601, when the little Catherine Dusi saw tears come down from the eyes of a painted image of the Virgin.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di San Martino a Levrange

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A few hundred meters from the old town, on a plateau sheltered from the winds; of ancient origin, it was rebuilt after 1530 and dedicated to San Martino di Tours.

Spessio e chiesa dei Santi Antonio da Padova e Gaetano

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Not far from Avenone, Spessio is a small group of houses in most of the sixteenth century, with loggias and carved balconies, closed to traffic of cars; there stands the church of Santi Antonio da Padova and Gaetano.
