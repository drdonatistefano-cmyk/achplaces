What to see in Soresina

Center of the Cremona plain with a heritage of churches between the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries, rich in canvases of Genovesino and Malosso, starting from the prepositural of San Siro; in the village there is also the museum dedicated to the Sorese statesman Francesco Genala.

Chiesa prepositurale di San Siro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The first stone was laid in 1582 by the bishop of Cremona Nicolò Sfondati; the current façade is in 1941, the work of the Soresian architect Giulio Carotti. He preserves works by Luigi Miradori called the Genovesino, Giacomo Miradori, Giovanni Battista Trotti called the Malosso, Giovanni Giacomo Pasini and Ermenegildo Lodi, and an organ Serassi-Balbiani (1810-1887); the nineteenth-century bell tower, designed by L. Voghera and completed by C. Visioli, hosts a concert of 12 bells.

  • Where Via Francesco Genala 9, Soresina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.soresina.cr.it

Chiesa di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built around the middle of the sixteenth century and enlarged after 1577 with a nave and a large choir; it preserves paintings of Genovesino and F. Mouth. The bell tower was erected by the Confraternita di San Rocco Pellegrino.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di Santa Croce

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Born as chapel of the ancient Hospital of Santa Croce, founded in 1582 by G. Antonio Valcarenghi, was enlarged and equipped with a tower in the seventeenth century; with a single nave, preserves a Baptism of Christ of the Malosso and works attributed to A. Miradori said the Key and F. Mouthy.

Chiesa del Tempietto (Santa Maria del Boschetto)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The construction was promoted in 1606 by the Confraternity of the Holy Trinity; it has a single nave, six side chapels and a choir-oratory, with wooden sculptures of the sixteenth century. It was reopened to worship in 2001 after extensive restoration.

Chiesa di San Francesco al Dosso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in the first decade of the seventeenth century, it owes its name to the position on a rise of the ground; it has late-Renaissance façade, a wooden crucifix by G. Bertesi on the high altar and an eighteenth-century Way of the Cross painted on crystal.

Santuario di Ariadello

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Sanctuary in the countryside, about 2.5 km north of the center, built by the feudal lord G.B. Barbò with the contribution of the population to remember a miracle: the deaf-domed daughter regained the word while offering flowers of field to a painted Madonna, image still very venerated by the Soresinesi.

Museo Francesco Genala

Museums · City history

He collected documents, manuscripts and objects from the statesman Francesco Genala (Soresina 1843 - Rome 1893), Minister of Public Works in the government Depretis from 1883 to 1887, when he followed the reconstruction after the earthquakes of Casamicciola (1883) and Liguria (1887). Open on weekdays 14.15-18.30, Saturday 14.15-17.30; barrier-free (information: municipal library).
