What to see in Pizzighettone

Walled city divided in two by the Adda, Orange Flag of Touring: the sixteenth-century walls of about 2 km contain the Casematte, vaulted rooms all connected to each other, next to the parish church of San Bassiano, the Tower of Guado and three museums.

9 places to visit in Pizzighettone, in the province of Cremona (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Mura e Casematte

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

A 16th-century bastion that spans the historic centre on both banks of the Adda: about 2 km long, 12 m tall and on average 3,60 m thick. Inside the walls the Casematte, 93 large vaulted rooms once military housing, warehouses and shooting stations, all connected in a path of almost one kilometer; restored by the Volunteers Group Walls, host events and exhibitions.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo civico di Pizzighettone

Museums · City history

Founded in 1907 with the legacy of the Cremona antiques Vincenzo Favenza, damaged after the war and reopened in 1969 in the Palazzo del Quartier Fino, next to the library. Sections of paleontology, prehistory, Roman finds (vases, pirogues, inscriptions), historical weapons, art objects, Renaissance ceramics and contemporary art.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Garibaldi 18, Pizzighettone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismocremona.it · italia.it

Museo delle Prigioni

Museums · Themed

First Lombard museum dedicated to prison history, in the former life sentence: objects, instruments and weapons of the use of prisons between 1785 and 1954, with the graffiti of young prisoners still visible on the walls; it also tells the military prison of 1920-1945, with cells without services nor water and punishment cells.

  • Where Piazza d'Armi 1, Pizzighettone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismocremona.it · italia.it

Museo delle Arti e Mestieri

Museums · Themed

Set up in some casemattes, it recalls the peasant world, life on the river and craft shops, as well as domestic life with furniture, clothes and toys; also exhibited photographs and maps.

Torre del Guado

History and architecture · Towers

Square and crenellated tower, the only intact remains of the ancient castle started by the Cremonesi in 1133; in 1525 the king of France Francesco I of Valois was imprisoned.

  • Where Pizzighettone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · in-lombardia.it · comune.pizzighettone.cr.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Bassiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest church in the village, in Romanesque-Lombard style: it houses paintings by Bernardino Campi and his school, three marble tiles from the fourteenth century and a palace of the main altar donated by Francesco I and woven in Paris.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sources: touringclub.it · comune.pizzighettone.cr.it · italia.it

Santuario della Madonna del Sacro Cuore (chiesa di San Pietro), Gera

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

An eighteenth-century Marian shrine in the hamlet Gera, on the right bank of the Adda, covered inside and outside with golden and polychrome marbles and mosaics dedicated to the Virgin, the work of Fr.Pietro Mizzi, pastor until 1999; it houses an interactive exhibition on its history.

tours only with guide by reservation (in-Lombardy)

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Centro storico, porte e Palazzo comunale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The historical centre, divided by the Adda, preserves the Rivellino of 1404 wanted by Cabrino Fondulo, the doors Cream, Cremona Nuova and Rescue (the latter on the river) and the Town Hall of the second half of the fifteenth century; on the right bank, Gera keeps almost entire its belt with casematte and an ancient powder keg.

  • Where Pizzighettone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.pizzighettone.cr.it · italia.it

Navigazione sull'Adda

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Paid entry · Viewpoint

A circular cruise without stops in the part of the Adda included in the Adda South Park, known for its flora and fauna, starting from the Gera boat; it can be combined with the guided tour of the walls.
