What to see in Altavilla Monferrato

Country of the hills of Monferrato Casale linked to distillation: the Mazzetti d'Altavilla distillery opens to visitors production, barricade and Grappa Gallery; in the village is also home to the Museum of steam and electric tramways, dedicated to the Monferrato trams.

Distilleria Mazzetti d'Altavilla e Galleria della grappa

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Distillery that offers guided tours to the production and the barricade of aging, the Galleria della grappa and the Enoteca Monferrina, an underground cantinone where you taste grappa, distillates, liqueurs, brandy and fruit under spirit.

Museo dei tramways a vapore ed elettrici

Museums · Themed

Documents, historical images and objects of the Monferrato trams: two Lanzo's Henschel & Sohn locomotives, a late nineteenth-century freight wagon, an electric accumulator locomotive and an American Davenport locomotive, as well as telegraphy and telephone equipment, signalling lamps, timetables and track and sleeper samples with the original brands.

the MiC card reports the museum closed every day; check on tel. 0131 225810
