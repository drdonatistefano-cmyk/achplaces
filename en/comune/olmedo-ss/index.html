What to see in Olmedo

Centre of Nurra about ten kilometers from Alghero, with the Romanesque church of Our Lady of Talia of the twelfth century, the megalithic fortress of Monte Baranta of the Copper Age and over twenty nuraghi in the territory.

3 places to visit in Olmedo, in the province of Sassari (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Nostra Signora di Talia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque church of the first half of the twelfth century, probably of Tuscan-Lombard workers, in limestone, reddish tuff and basaltic trachyte; also called Santa Maria di Ulumetu, takes its name from a statue of the Madonna found in the eighteenth century among the ruins of the disappeared village of Talia. Facade with architrave portal, frescoed bezel with Madonna and Child and cross window; interior with three naves on columns with decorated capitals, the central with wooden roof. From Christmas to Epiphany, it houses a crib made entirely of bread.

SardiniaCulture: temporarily closed and unmanaged, external freely accessible

  • Where Piazza Giovanni XXIII, Olmedo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Fortezza di Monte Baranta (su Casteddu)

History and architecture · Archaeology

Fortification of the Copper Age (2500-2000 BC) on the slopes of Monte Baranta, about 3 km from the village, reoccupied in the ancient Bronze and to a lesser extent in Nuragic and Roman age. A wall of large rocks, almost 100 metres long, contains a horseshoe tower and rectangular huts; outside the walls a sacred area with about 80 thick stones arranged in a circle of about 10 meters and a village of seven rectangular huts.

  • Where Monte Baranta, circa 3 km da Olmedo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Nuraghe Talia e terme romane

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the Nuraghe Talia a fig tree grown inside has helped to preserve seven rows of stones; near a small thermal plant of imperial age, part of a villa, with four rooms, marble coverings and traces of mosaics.

  • Where Olmedo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it
