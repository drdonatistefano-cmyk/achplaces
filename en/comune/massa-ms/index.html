What to see in Massa

City of the Cybo-Malaspina dukes between the Apuan Alps and the sea, with Piazza Aranci, the castle on the hill and the Marina.

25 places to visit in Massa, in the province of Massa-Carrara (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Massa in the guide with map and filters

Piazza Aranci

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The monumental heart of the city, with rows of orange trees and the marble obelisk with four fountain lions erected in 1853, enlarged under Elisa Baciocchi.

  • Where Piazza degli Aranci, Massa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Palazzo Ducale

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The Red Palace of the Cybo-Malaspina, with columns, stairs and white marble loggias, now home to Province and Prefecture; the Hall of Mirrors and the Swiss Salon host exhibitions.

  • Where Piazza degli Aranci 35, Massa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · viafrancigena.visittuscany.com

Castello Malaspina

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Medieval fortress transformed into a noble residence and then in prison until 1946, with internal and external frescoes and a view from the plain to the Apuans and the sea.

  • Where Via della Rocca, Massa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Cattedrale dei Santi Pietro e Francesco

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Born as a Franciscan church at the behest of Jacopo Malaspina in the middle of the fifteenth century, with façade of 1936: a Madonna del Pinturicchio, a crib by Benedetto Buglioni and a crucifix of the fourteenth century.

  • Where Via Zoppi 2, Massa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
