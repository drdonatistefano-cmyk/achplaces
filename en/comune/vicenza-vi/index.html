What to see in Vicenza

The treasure chest of Andrea Palladio, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1994 with the Palladian villas (since 1996): the Olympic Theatre, the Palladian Basilica, the Chiericati Palace, the Round and the sanctuary of Monte Berico.

25 places to visit in Vicenza, in the province of Vicenza (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers 7 of the 14 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Teatro Olimpico

History and architecture · Historic theatres · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Palladio's last work, commissioned in 1580 by the Olympic Academy and completed after his death: the oldest theater covered in masonry in the world, inspired by the Roman theatres of Vitruvio, with the fixed scene of Scamozzi and 95 statues; hosts the Cycle of Classic Shows.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Vicenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: italia.it · comune.vicenza.it · museicivicivicenza.it

Basilica Palladiana

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The symbol of the city: Palladio redesigned the Gothic Palazzo della Ragione enveloping it in the marble lodges that for over four centuries watch over Piazza dei Signori; venue of exhibitions, with panoramic terrace.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
