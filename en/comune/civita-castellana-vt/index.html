Forte Sangallo – Museo archeologico dell'Agro Falisco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Masterpiece by Antonio da Sangallo il Vecchio, linked to the Borgia and the brigand Gasbarrone, prison until 1961 and today the museum of the finds of the Agro Falisco.
