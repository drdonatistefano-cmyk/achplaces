What to see in Schio

The city of wool at the foot of the Small Dolomites: the High Factory and the Lanificio Conte recount the 19th century textile industry, between the historic buildings of the center and the Cathedral of San Pietro.

7 places to visit in Schio, in the province of Vicenza (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Schio in the guide with map and filters

Fabbrica Alta

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The large wool mill designed in 1861 by the Belgian architect Auguste Vivroux: 80 meters long, five floors more basement and attic, 330 windows and 125 cast iron columns; abandoned in 1966-67 and public since 2013, national symbol of industrial archaeology.

  • Where Via Pasini, Schio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitschio.it

Sources: venetoaroundme.regione.veneto.it · visitschio.it

Lanificio Conte

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Among the oldest factories in the city, documented since 1757: the four-storey cast iron building of 1866-1884, among the first in Italy with electricity in 1883; since 2000 it has been a public and cultural function and preserves the historical weaving machinery.

  • Where Via XX Settembre, Schio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitschio.it

Sources: venetoaroundme.regione.veneto.it · visitschio.it

Palazzo Fogazzaro

Museums · City history

Built in 1810 on a project by Carlo Barrera, it is the House of the Arts of the city and houses the civic museum.

  • Where Via Pasini, Schio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitschio.it

Sources: visitschio.it

Palazzo Toaldi Capra

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Venue of the town hall from the eighteenth century to 1913; late-Gothic frescoes emerged in the restorations of the eighties.
