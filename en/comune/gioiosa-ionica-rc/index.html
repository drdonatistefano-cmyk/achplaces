What to see in Gioiosa Ionica

Borgo della Locride on the promontory above the Galizzi torrent: the Pellicano castle where the village was born, the churches rebuilt after the earthquake of 1783 and, towards the coast, the archaeological park of the Roman villa of Naniglio with the large pillar cistern.

6 places to visit in Gioiosa Ionica, in the province of Reggio Calabria (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Pellicano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the rocky promontory above the stream Galizzi, where the village was born: ruins of a triangular fortress with two towers, perhaps of Swabian age or of the first Angevin period, Caracciolo and Carafa and from the end of the nineteenth century of the Pellican marquises; you enter from a stone bridge with steps.

  • Where Via Caracciolo 33, Gioiosa Ionica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.gioiosaionica.rc.it

Sources: comune.gioiosaionica.rc.it

Area archeologica del Naniglio

History and architecture · Archaeology

In the archaeological park of the Roman villa of Naniglio, on the slopes of the Serre near the torrent Torbido: a large semi-pogeal cistern with three naves (about 17,5 by 10 meters) with eight pillars and vaults cruising, central well and spiral staircase in a tower, and two rooms later, one with a votive edicule in stucco.

Chiesa Matrice di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It was built around 1500 and enlarged since 1743, was destroyed by the earthquake of February 5, 1783 and rebuilt; in 1803 it received the insignia of the chapter of San Giovanni Maggiore of Naples.

  • Where Largo Dante Alighieri 5, Gioiosa Ionica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.gioiosaionica.rc.it

Sources: comune.gioiosaionica.rc.it

Chiesa dell'Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1594 under the patronage of the Marquises of Joyosa on the remains of the little church of Santa Maria di Maratà, then of the Pellicano; it preserves a large canvas of the Annunciation commissioned at the beginning of the nineteenth century by the Marquis Anna Maria Piccolomini Caracciolo.

  • Where Viale delle Rimembranze, Gioiosa Ionica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.gioiosaionica.rc.it

Sources: comune.gioiosaionica.rc.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Born in the seventeenth century as a small oratory for an image of Our Lady considered miraculous, enlarged with the arrival of pilgrims: a single nave, frescoes and on the main altar the Madonna delle Grazie.

  • Where Viale delle Rimembranze 5, Gioiosa Ionica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.gioiosaionica.rc.it

Sources: comune.gioiosaionica.rc.it

Chiesa dell'Addolorata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1881 by the confraternity of Maria Santissima Addolorata in place of the church of Pietà, rendered unusable by the earthquake of 1783.

  • Where Gioiosa Ionica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.gioiosaionica.rc.it
