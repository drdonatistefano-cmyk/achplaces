What to see in Prelà

Municipality of Val Prino with the hamlets Tavole, Valloria and Villatalla, born around the castle built in the 12th century by the Marquises of Clavesana on the spur of "Petra Lata"; Valloria is the village of the painted doors, Villatalla and the parish church of San Giovanni del Groppo preserve the polyptychs of Agostino Casanova.

Valloria, il paese delle porte dipinte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The village of Prelà 14 km from Imperia, with 42 inhabitants and 144 painted doors, number growing year after year: during the summer, during the festival in Valloria you are partying, three painters paint new doors live.

  • Where Valloria, Prelà
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page provincia.imperia.it

Sources: lamialiguria.it · provincia.imperia.it

Mostra museo delle cose dimenticate

Museums · Themed

In the former oratory of Santa Croce, on the square of Valloria, exhibits objects of peasant life, craftsman and maid recovered with the community: agricultural tools, tools and instruments built by Giacomo Pisani (1886-1959), including a "percussive" halfway between piano and organ, a violin and a self-scatting camera. The MiC card indicates opening every day 8-18 (updated to 2020); the Province says open on request. Tel. 0183 282396.

  • Where Piazza delle Tre Fontane, Valloria, Prelà
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page provincia.imperia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · lamialiguria.it · provincia.imperia.it

Chiesa di San Michele a Villatalla

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish built in the 15th century on a previous Romanesque church and renovated in the Baroque period. On the wall opposite the side door, above an architrave dated 1470 with the Agnus Dei, opens a Gothic window with sharp arch; inside the polyptych of Agostino Casanova painted around 1540.

  • Where Villatalla, Prelà
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page provincia.imperia.it

Sources: comune.prela.im.it · provincia.imperia.it

Parrocchiale di San Giovanni del Groppo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

15th century church that houses a polyptych by Agostino Casanova from 1547. St. John the Baptist is the patron saint of the municipality, celebrated on 24 June.

Ruderi del castello di Prelà

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built in the 12th century by the Marquis of Clavesana on the spur of "Petra Lata" it was an autonomous fief in the territory of Oneglia under the protection of Genoa; in 1233 the inhabitants occupied it, passed to the counts of Ventimiglia, then to the Doria and in 1575 to the Savoy. The Province has a castle and two towers in a bad state of conservation.

the Province indicates castle and towers in bad state and records the Clavesana castle card under Vasia: check access and localization
