What to see in Macerata

University city on a hill between the valleys of Potenza and Chienti, famous for the Sferisterio and its summer opera festival, with Piazza della Libertà, the Civic Tower and the museums of Palazzo Buonaaccorsi and Palazzo Ricci.

16 places to visit in Macerata, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Arena Sferisterio

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Neoclassical arena designed by Ireneo Aleandri and built in the twenties of the nineteenth century with a private subscription; every summer it hosts the opera festival outdoors.

Musei Civici di Palazzo Buonaccorsi

Museums · Old masters

Palace built between 1700 and 1720 on a project by Giovanni Battista Contini, with the Hall of the Eneide on the noble floor; it houses the Museum of the Carriages, ancient art rooms inaugurated in 2014 and works of the Eight and twentieth century.

  • Where Macerata
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · regione.marche.it

Museo di Palazzo Ricci – Arte italiana del Novecento

Museums · Modern art

In Piazza Vittorio Veneto, one of the most important museums of the twentieth century in Italy, with futurists such as Boccioni, Balla, Depero and Pannaggi (Treno in corsa, 1922) and works by Donghi, Scipione, Mafai and Fausto Pirandello.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Veneto, Macerata
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · regione.marche.it
