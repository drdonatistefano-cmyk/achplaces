What to see in Taormina

The city on Mount Tauro overlooking the Ionian Sea and Mount Etna: the ancient Theatre, the largest in Sicily after that of Syracuse, Odeon, Naumachia, Corso Umberto with Palazzo Corvaja and the Cathedral of San Nicolò, the gardens of Lady Florence Trevelyan and, below, the islet of Isola Bella.

Teatro antico di Taormina

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

On the top of the hill, it was born in the III-II century B.C. (they remain a wall in isodome blocks and seats with inscriptions), but what is seen is largely the Roman renovation of the first half of the second century AD, attributed to Trajan or Hadrian; between the second and third century it was transformed into a arena. With 109 metres in diameter it is the largest in Sicily after that of Syracuse; the scene retains the two lateral openings and the columns of the frons are a nineteenth-century reconstruction. In the Casina degli Inglesi an epigraphic collection with Greek inscriptions of the II-I century BC; Goethe wrote that never before did the audience of a theater have such a spectacle.

Odeon di Taormina

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

In the southwestern corner of Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, remains of a peripteral temple of the middle of the third century BC, partly incorporated into the foundations of the church of Santa Caterina; on its south side between the end I and the beginning II century A.D. was built a covered theater, with a brick cavea of eleven rows of seats divided into four sectors, excavated in 1892-1893. The only covered theatre documented in Sicily together with that of Catania, hosted concerts, music competitions and public readings.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Taormina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · italia.it

Isola Bella e Museo naturalistico di Isolabella

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Isolotto in the middle of the cove between Capo Sant'Andrea and Capo Taormina, protected since 1984 and regional naturalistic museum since 2011, combined with the mainland by a thin tongue of pebbles that with the high tide can end under water. Lady Florence Trevelyan bought it in 1890 and built a house there: the red villa, called Villa Caronia by the last owner, is surrounded by about 1.6 hectares of terraces and Mediterranean maquis, marked in 1970 by De Agostini as a botanical garden.

  • Where Via Nazionale, Taormina (sentiero dal belvedere di Isola Bella)
  • Entry €5, reduced €2,50
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Corso Umberto

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The main street closed to traffic that crosses the whole city, with boutiques, jewelers, artisan and antique shops, between side alleys; near Porta Catania a sign indicates the narrowest alley in Italy, 43 cm wide.

  • Where Corso Umberto, Taormina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Palazzo Corvaja

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Medieval fortress with Arab influences that in the fifteenth century housed the Sicilian Parliament; in the inner courtyard reliefs and arched windows. On the first floor a museum and gallery (the Museum of Arts and Traditions of Taormina), on the ground floor the tourist information office.

  • Where Corso Umberto, Taormina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Piazza IX Aprile, Sant'Agostino e San Giuseppe

Palazzo dei Duchi di Santo Stefano

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

At Porta Catania, in the ancient Jewish district, 14th century Gothic palace that blends Arab and Norman elements, residence of the De Spuches, family of Spanish origin; has a garden, hosts cultural events and the Foundation Mazzullo with works by sculptor Giuseppe Mazzullo.

Naumachia

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

A few steps from the Villa Comunale and near Palazzo Corvaja, one of the major Roman monuments of Sicily: a long brick wall with 18 large apse niches alternated with as many small niches; the name recalls the simulated naval battles.

  • Where Taormina centro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info

Villa Comunale (Giardini Duca di Cesarò)

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Free entry · Viewpoint

Born in the mid-nineteenth century as a private residence of the noblewoman Lady Florence Trevelyan, it is an English garden on several levels with botanical garden and the ...Victorian Follies , bizarre constructions between romantic and Gothic taste with strong oriental influences; views of the bay of Naxos and Mount Etna.
