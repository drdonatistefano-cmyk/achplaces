What to see in Pontboset

Villages of the middle Champorcher valley at the confluence of Ayasse, Brenne and Manda, the village of bridges between wild gorges.

5 places to visit in Pontboset, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Passeggiata dei sei ponti

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Easy ring from the center of the village that crosses six bridges on the streams that give the name to the resort.
