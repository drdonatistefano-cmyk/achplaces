What to see in Lecco

The city of Promessi Sposi on the branch of Lake Como, between the Adda and the mountains: the bell tower of San Nicolò 96 meters high, Villa Manzoni, the village of Pescarenico, the Viscontea Tower and the Azzone Visconti Bridge.

9 places to visit in Lecco, in the province of Lecco (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Basilica e Campanile di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Basilicas · Viewpoint

Church developed from a nucleus of the 11th century, with frescoes of the 14th century; the bell tower, among the highest in Europe with 380 steps and 96 meters, offers views of the lake and city.

  • Where Via San Nicolò 1, Lecco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · leccotourism.it

Villa Manzoni

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The family villa of Alessandro Manzoni, today museum with paintings, manuscripts and first editions, and art gallery.

  • Where Via Don Guanella 1, Lecco
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · comune.lecco.it

Palazzo Belgiojoso – Musei civici

Museums · City history

In the eighteenth century Palazzo Belgiojoso four museums: Natural history, set up as it was in the early twentieth century with a fossil of Lariosaur, Archaeological, Planetary and Historical Museum.

  • Where Corso Matteotti 32, Lecco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
