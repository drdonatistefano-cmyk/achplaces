What to see in Ascoli Piceno

The city of travertine and the hundred towers: Piazza del Popolo with Caffè Meletti, Piazza Arringo with the Cathedral of Sant'Emidio and the polyptychs of Carlo Crivelli, the Roman bridge of Solestà and the Quintana of August.

27 places to visit in Ascoli Piceno, in the province of Ascoli Piceno (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza del Popolo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The heart of the city, overlooking the medieval Palazzo dei Capitani del Popolo, the Church of San Francesco, the Loggia dei Mercanti of the sixteenth century and the Caffè Meletti; there you run the Quintana on the first Sunday of August.
