What to see in Celle di Macra

Small town of the Maira Valley linked to the itinerant trade of the anchovies, told by the Seles Museum in the former seventeenth-century church of San Rocco; among the villages stands Serre with a rare log house (Blockbau), and the territory is crossed by four thematic paths, the GTA and the Occitani Routes.

Museo Seles, museo multimediale dei mestieri itineranti

Museums · Themed

It is located in the former church of San Rocco, a renovated seventeenth-century building, and tells the trade of the anchovies of the Maira Valley with testimonies from Dronero, Celle di Macra, Macra and Paglieres. Three sections: the anchovies (clothes, instruments, posters), fishing and the transport of fish (a lampara, chests and the typical cart), trade of anchovies (documents and municipal registers of the nineteenth century); closes a video with interviews with anchovies and ex anchovies. It is part of the Ecomuseum Alta Valle Maira; according to the Municipality is open on Sundays from 23 June to 1 September (10-12, 14-18), otherwise on request (tel. +39 0171 999190).

Borgata Serre

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Borgata with one of the few buildings in the valley with walls in overlapping wooden trunks (Blockbau). A steep covered passage dug into the rock, with lateral stairs leading to the doors of the houses, leads to the path that goes up the ridge towards Castellaro: it is indicated as one of the most interesting covered passages of the villages of the valley.

Sentieri tematici di Celle di Macra

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Four thematic itineraries: the Trail of the Acciugai, on the routes of the typical trade of Celle; the Trail of the Alpine Construction, between the villages with the "casseforti" , sailing buildings rich in carved stones; the Trail of the Faith, dotted with votive pylons; the Trail of the Giants, in a wood of secular larch in the hamlet Ramei. The territory is also crossed by the Great Crossing of the Alps and the Occitani Routes, with a path beyond the pastures of Mount Tibert towards Castelmagno.
