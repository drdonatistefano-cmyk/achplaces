Monteleone e i borghi fortificati di Roncofreddo

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval village of Monteleone, Orange Flag, leaning against the fortress, and the small fortified village of Sorrivoli around the Malatesta fortress.

Rocca di Monteleone

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The quadrilateral fortress built by the Malatesta di Roncofreddo, feudal since 1556, restored in 2003, of which the imposing walls remain.
