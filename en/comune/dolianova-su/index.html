What to see in Dolianova

The centre of Parteolla, born in the early twentieth century from the union of San Pantaleo and Sicci San Biagio: the symbol is the former Romanesque cathedral of San Pantaleo, consecrated in 1289, with its fifteenth-century retablo; in the village also the Museum of the olearia tradition is the "Sa mola de su notariu" and the Villa de Villa.

Ex cattedrale di San Pantaleo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built in sandstone in three phases between the beginning of the twelfth century and 1289, the year of consecration, it was the cathedral of the diocese of Dolia until 1503. Romanesque with Gothic touches: facade and bell tower with arches and mythological figures, a Roman marble with snakes above the entrance, a sarcophagus on columns on the side, capitals with scenes of the New Testament. Inside, a baptismal bathtub of the 5th-6th century, the painting of the Tree of Life and the Retablo of St. Pantaleo of the late fifteenth century in six paintings.

  • Where Via Vescovado, Dolianova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Museo della tradizione olearia «Sa mola de su notariu»

Museums · Themed

Museum dedicated to the tradition of oil, in the seventeenth-century Villa Boyl according to Sardinia Tourism and in a mill of the Locci family according to Italia.it: exhibit tools, presses and documents on the cultivation of the olive tree.
