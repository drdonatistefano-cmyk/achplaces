What to see in Pellezzano

Municipality of the Irno valley behind Salerno, formed by five hamlets of medieval origin, with the former Augustinian monastery of the Holy Spirit, today a panoramic museum complex, and the fourteenth-century church of the Madonna delle Grazie in Capriglia.

Complesso museale dello Spirito Santo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

On a height that was perhaps a Roman fortification under control of the Irno valley, in the sixteenth century the Hermits of Santa Maria di Collereto founded a monastery there; in 1604, entered the Augustinian order, dedicated the church to the Holy Spirit. Repressed in 1653 at the behest of Innocent X, it became a hermitage and then a charity; restored since 2003 as a museum, it has rooms, corridors, cells, ancient cisterns and terraces with views from the Irno valley to the Sele valley, the Gulf of Salerno and the Amalfi Coast. It hosts cultural events.

  • Where Piazza Giovanni Paolo II 3, località Spirito Santo, Pellezzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · fondoambiente.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie a Capriglia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Documented since 1309, with first pastoral visit in 1511; in 1692 it had 11 altars. He kept the icon on the table of Our Lady of Graces, 1428, attributed to the Salerno school of Andrea Sabatino, crowned after the request of 1795 to the Vatican Chapter; at the entrance two ancient paintings with the Last Supper and the Madonna of Carmine. The bell tower, about 47 meters high, is attributed to the Vanvitellian school; on the first Sunday of July the statue of the Madonna goes in procession.

  • Where Centro di Capriglia, Pellezzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
