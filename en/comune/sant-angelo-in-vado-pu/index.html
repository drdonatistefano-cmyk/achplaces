What to see in Sant'Angelo in Vado

The town of the Metauro valley, capital of the white truffle and home of the painters Taddeo and Federico Zuccari: the Domus del Mito with the mosaics of the Roman Tifernum Mataurense, the Santa Maria extra muros complex, the Palazzo della Ragione with the tower "el Campanon" and the museum of old crafts.

10 places to visit in Sant'Angelo in Vado, in the province of Pesaro e Urbino (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Domus del Mito

History and architecture · Archaeology

Archaeological area of about 1,000 sqm in Campo della Pieve, heir of Tifernum Mataurense, Roman town hall destroyed during the Gothic war: a noble domus of the late 1st century A.D. with mosaic floors figured bichrome and polychrome (Medusa, Dionysus, the triumphal chariot of Neptune and Anfitrite) and, in the triclinium, a hunting and a fishing scene within geometric motifs in black and white.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Complesso di Santa Maria extra muros e Museo Antiquarium «Tifernum Mataurense»

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Built in 1331 and then modified, it includes the former monastery of the Servants of Mary and houses the civic museum with the finds of Tifernum Mataurense. The church, just before the centre, has manierist interior with Baroque altars and works attributed to Lorenzo Ghiberti, Raffaellino del Colle, Francesco Mancini and the Zuccari brothers, and four ceramic fragments of the Robbiana school next to the eighteenth-century altar.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page eventi.turismo.marche.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Museo demoantropologico «I vecchi mestieri»

Museums · Themed

In the basement of Palazzo Mercuri reconstructs the workshops of the ancient crafts: ropemaker, blacksmith, print on fabric, with attention to ebony, wrought iron and goldsmithery.
