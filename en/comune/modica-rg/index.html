What to see in Modica

Late Baroque town rebuilt after the earthquake of 1693, UNESCO heritage with the towns of Val di Noto: the Duomo of San Giorgio on top of 250 steps, San Pietro, the rock church of San Nicolò Inferiore and, out of town, the valley of Cava d'Ispica.

5 places to visit in Modica, in the province of Ragusa (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Modica in the guide with map and filters

Duomo di San Giorgio

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Masterpiece of the late Sicilian Baroque on top of a staircase of 250 steps: facade with a 60-metre tower attributed to Rosario Gagliardi, inside with five naves with the polyptych of Bernardino Niger, the Madonna della Neve by Giuliano Mancino and Bartolomeo Berrettaro, the Assunta by Filippo Paladino and a sundial in the floor of the transept.

  • Where Corso San Giorgio, Modica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info

Duomo di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The second largest cathedral in the Baroque city, part of the heritage rebuilt after the earthquake.

  • Where Modica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info · whc.unesco.org

Chiesa rupestre di San Nicolò Inferiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Paid entry

Considered the oldest church of Modica, probably the early Middle Ages, parish of a Greek-speaking district: the apse is covered with Byzantine-style icons with the Christ Pantocrator in almond; identified in 1987 in a cave used as a deposit.

  • Where Modica
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitsicily.info

Castello dei Conti di Modica e Torretta dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Remains of the castle of the counts, with the clock tower become emblem of the city.

  • Where Modica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info

Parco archeologico di Cava d'Ispica

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The valley is about 14 km long between Modica, Ispica and Rosolini, inhabited without interruption from the Bronze Age to the Early Middle Ages: Sicule necropolis with oven tombs, Byzantine hypogeans, Christian catacombs and caves; the visit starts from the Horse Mill.

  • Where Crocevia Cava Ispica, Modica
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitsicily.info
