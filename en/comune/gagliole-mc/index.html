What to see in Gagliole

Borgo dell'alta valle del Potenza dominated by the pentagonal fortress built by Gentile da Varano after 1274; opposite, in a 14th century palace, the Museum of natural history with fossils and minerals, and in the territory the sanctuary of the Madonna delle Macchie with frescoes from the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries.

Rocca dei Da Varano e borgo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On a rocky spur, the fortress was built immediately after 1274 by Gentile da Varano to protect its duchy: it is the only example in the defensive system varanesco with pentagonal mastio surrounded by walls, limestone and sandstone local. From the gate of St. Joseph, to the north, four concentric alleys follow the hill up to the square of the church of St. Michael the Archangel and the palazzetto of the ancient municipality, with the coat of arms of the rooster.

Museo di storia naturale

Museums · Science and university

Set up by the Oppelide Foundation in the 14th-century palace opposite the Fortress, it exhibits fossils and minerals that illustrate the evolution of life through geological ages, coming from the Elce valley and from various countries even outside Europe; many specimens are rare or unique.

Santuario della Madonna delle Macchie

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

An ancient monastic complex with four porticos and two churches: the oldest, Gothic, preserves frescoes from the fourteenth century attributed to Diotallevi di Angeluccio and paintings from the fifteenth century of the so-called Maestro delle Macchie (some detached and in part to the Diocesan Museum of Camerino); in the second, on the apse wall, a large Assumption between fifteenth and sixteenth century inspired by Signorelli. You can reach it on foot or by car on a white road from the S.S. 362.
