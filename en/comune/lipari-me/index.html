What to see in Lipari

The municipality includes the entire Aeolian archipelago except Salina, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2000: in Lipari the Castle with the acropolis, the Cathedral of St. Bartholomew and the Archaeological Museum . Luigi Bernabò Brea , Marina Corta and the viewpoint of Quattrocchi; and then the Vulcano Fossa, the Sciara del Fuoco of Stromboli, Panarea, Filicudi and Alicudi.

Museo Archeologico Regionale Eoliano «Luigi Bernabò Brea»

Museums · Archaeological · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

In Lipari Castle, in restored historical buildings used in part as a confining field in fascism, six pavilions from Prehistory to Modern Age: prehistory of the smaller islands, Greek and Roman times, epigraphy, volcanology, underwater archaeology with the cargoes of the sunken ships and the hall of the theatrical masks with red figure craters. In the former prison, the exhibition of contemporary art "Mare Motus" (since 2014) with works by Mitoraj and Plessi. Open every day 9-19.30, holidays 9-13.30.

  • Where Via Castello 2, Lipari
  • Entry €8, reduced €4
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · italia.it

Castello e acropoli di Lipari

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

Promontory inhabited since the Neolithic, with the ancient acropolis and the remains of the prehistoric inhabitants; it is part of the archaeological park of the Aeolian Islands. Open May-October 9-19, November-April 9-16, holidays 9-13.30.

  • Where Via Castello, Lipari
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · italia.it

Cattedrale di San Bartolomeo e Chiostro normanno

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage

The cathedral, inside the castle, houses the sacred inch of the saint in a silver reliquary, shown during the holidays; next to the cloister, part of the first Benedictine monastery built in Sicily at the behest of Ruggero II.

  • Where Castello, Lipari
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Marina Corta e via Vittorio Emanuele

History and architecture · Squares and old town · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The square of Sant'Onofrio in Marina Corta and via Crispi in Marina Lunga collect bars and restaurants, and from May to October the evening life of the island; via Vittorio Emanuele is the main street of the capital.

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the viewpoint of Quattrocchi the view of the faraglioni Long Stone and Stone Menalda; not far away the traffic light, geophysical observatory in a former traffic light of the Navy, looks on the faraglioni and on Vulcano.
