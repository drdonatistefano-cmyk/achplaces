What to see in Malé

The capital of the Val di Sole, rebuilt after the fire of 1892: around the square with the decanal church of Santa Maria Assunta are the Museo della Civiltà Solandra in the former palazzo pretorile, the Venetian Segheria of the eighteenth century and, in Pondasio, the Fucina Marinelli, one of the few hydraulic forges still operating in the Alps.

Museo della Civiltà Solandra

Museums · Themed

Ethnographic Museum on the ground floor of the former Palazzo Pretorile, once home of the Austrian barracks: it tells the life in Val di Sole in the last century with original tools such as the loom and the kangaroo, the reconstructions of the stua and the cuisine and the traditional solandra house, the carpenter, blacksmith, breeder, weaver and calderaio and miniature models of the Venetian sawmills and farms. A section is dedicated to the mycologist Don Giacomo Bresadola.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi 6, Malé
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · parks.it · italia.it

Chiesa decanale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church dominates the centre of the village, in the square where the narrow streets of the village converge: the origins are medieval, but the current building largely derives from the reconstruction between the end of the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries; inside works of sacred art of the seventeenth century.

  • Where Piazza principale, Malé
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · italia.it

Segheria Veneziana

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Historical structure of the eighteenth century fed by the water of the stream, recovered and made visitable: during the openings and the didactic activities you can see the gears in operation.

  • Where Malé
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · italia.it

Fucina Marinelli

Local life and traditions · Crafts

Rare pre-industrial plant, probably from the 17th century and still in operation, bought by the Marinelli family in 1880 and moved by the water of the Rabbies stream: inside a large hammer of 1834, a double stone forge with a hood over 7 meters high and a collection of anvils, hammers and tanks. Visit by reservation, with guided demonstrations of iron processing when provided; suitable for families.

  • Where Via Trento 80, Pondasio, Malé
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · italia.it

La Lec di Malé lungo il Rabbies

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Relaxing walk in the woods accompanied by the Rabbies stream, which descends from the Rabbi Valley and flows into the Walnut: from Malé you go up to the locality Brewery, on the road to the Rabbi Valley, and you go back through Pondasio and the Marinelli Fucina.

  • Where Partenza da Malé
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · italia.it
