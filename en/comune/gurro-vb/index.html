What to see in Gurro

Small mountain town of the Valle Cannobina, 812 meters above sea level: in Piazza della Repubblica the parish church of the Nativity of Mary and the ethnographic museum of Gurro and Valle Cannobina, with costumes, furnishings and tools of the life of the past.

Museo etnografico di Gurro e della Valle Cannobina

Museums · Themed

In an old house with a loggia on three floors reconstructs the traditional house: kitchen with cupboard and pantry, wooden crockery, beds and cots, a room with tools for agriculture and breeding, tools for spinning and tiles with a large frame, lace, embroidered linen and typical costumes, different from country to country, with a photographic collection of vintage clothes. It is part of the High Verbano Museum Network. The Municipality indicates opening every day 8-20 (from 15 November to 31 March 9-18); steps at the entrance, not accessible to the disabled.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website museogurro.wixsite.com

Parrocchiale della Natività di Maria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Façade in a hut facing west, marked by three-part pilgrims, with portal under a canopy and bell tower in square-based brick crowned by a dome on a drum. The interior with three naves on pillars preserves the main altar of 1823, a sixteenth-century marble tabernacle of Carrara and a fresco of San Carlo Borromeo. The patron saint's feast is celebrated on September 8.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica, Gurro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
