What to see in Tortoreto

Medieval village on a hill just over 200 meters, with the districts of Terranova and Terravecchia, walls, Clock Tower and the terrace of Fortellezza, and a hamlet to the sea with more than three kilometers of sandy beach several times Blue Flag; in Tortoreto Lido the Roman villa of the Muracche.

7 places to visit in Tortoreto, in the province of Teramo (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico di Tortoreto Alto e Torre dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval village on a gentle hill just over 200 meters, divided into the districts of Terranova and Terravecchia, with pre-Romanic and Renaissance remains, towers, arches, alleys, stretches of the medieval walls, palaces and the Clock Tower.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Villa romana Le Muracche

History and architecture · Archaeology

Large rustic villa discovered in 1987 and excavated between 1988 and 2000 on about 1,675 m2 of an estimated complex over 3,700 m2: medium-republic production nucleus for wine and then oil (torks, tanks, large dolia) and residential part of Augustia facing the sea, with mosaics and fragments of painting, in use until the 6th century AD. A 3D rendering of five environments was presented in 2023.

MiC sources indicate openings only with guided tours on event dates, site closed on other days

  • Where Via Enzo Ferrari, Tortoreto Lido
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sabapaqte.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Spiaggia e lungomare di Tortoreto Lido

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

More than three kilometers of fine sandy beach, free and equipped, with many times awarded waters with the Blue Flag; the promenade is surrounded by green spaces.

  • Where Lungomare, Tortoreto Lido
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Pista ciclopedonale della costa (Bike to Coast)

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

A long cycle track that crosses the entire municipal territory along the sea and connects Tortoreto to Alba Adriatica to the north and Giulianova to the south; the first stretch of the Bike to Coast goes from Martinsicuro to Tortoreto.

  • Where Lungomare, Tortoreto Lido
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
