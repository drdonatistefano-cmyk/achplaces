What to see in Bagnolo Piemonte

Country of the stone of Luserna, born around the year 1000 around the castle of the lords of Bagnolo: the feudal complex of Malingri with castle-forte, palace and park, the neo-Gothic parish church of San Pietro in Vincoli and, at altitude, Montoso with the partisan shrine and the quarries.

4 places to visit in Bagnolo Piemonte, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Complesso feudale Malingri: Castello e Parco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The Castle-Forte dates back to the 11th century and preserves medieval characteristics, the Castle-Piano (Palace) dates back to the 14th century with frescoes from the 15th century. The park of about two hectares, in the Grandi Giardini Italiani circuit, has 40-50 meters high centuries-old trees, an elliptical stone pool, rhododendrons and azaleas in spring and summer hydrangeas; the avenue leads to the Chapel of Ognissanti. Group visits by reservation (tel. +39 335 5244080), at least 2-3 hours.

  • Where Via Palazzo 23, Bagnolo Piemonte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website castellodibagnolo.it

Sources: castellodibagnolo.it · visitcuneese.it

Parrocchiale di San Pietro in Vincoli

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt in neo-Gothic style, with brick façade and stone columns of Quinzano; the bell tower, adapted in Gothic style in the 14th century, is the only remaining element of the ancient church.

  • Where Centro, Bagnolo Piemonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Montoso e Sacrario partigiano

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

About 10 km from the center, the village was a centre of the partisan struggle: the shrine is in Martyrs Square of Liberty. In summer it is a destination for hiking and free flight, in winter skiing with the Rucas facilities up to 1,800 m to Costa Ciapè.

  • Where Piazza Martiri della Libertà, Montoso, Bagnolo Piemonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it

Sentiero delle cave da Bagnolo a Rucas

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring of about 39 km by mountain bike or e-bike (1,200 m in altitude, 365 to 1,560 m) that starts from the parish and rises between castle, mill, orchards, vineyards and chestnut groves, passes a dolmen, the quarry of Balma Oro and the large stone quarry of Luserna di Montoso up to Rucas, and then descend to the chapel of Madonna della Neve. Difficulty BC/BC+, for trained cyclists.

  • Where Partenza dalla parrocchiale di San Pietro in Vincoli, Bagnolo Piemonte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
