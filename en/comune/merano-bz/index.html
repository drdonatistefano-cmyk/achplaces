What to see in Merano

Hapsburg City of Care on the Passirio: the Medieval Porticos, the Cathedral of St. Nicholas, the Prince's Castle, the Kurhaus liberty and the winter walks, in summer and Tappeiner; out of town the gardens of Trauttmansdorff Castle, where the Empress Sissi stayed.

14 places to visit in Merano, in the province of Bolzano (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Giardini di Castel Trauttmansdorff e Touriseum

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Paid entry · Viewpoint

About 12 hectares with 80 botanical environments in four thematic areas; in the castle, where Empress Elizabeth stayed between 1870 and 1889, the Touriseum, the first museum of the Alpine arch dedicated to the history of tourism.

  • Where Merano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · merano-suedtirol.it

Duomo di San Nicolò

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built between 1310 and 1455 and dedicated to the patron saint of the city, with altars and pulpit of relief; next to the Chapel of Santa Barbara (1423-1450).

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Merano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.merano-suedtirol.it

Sources: merano-suedtirol.it

Castello Principesco

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Built in the 15th century as the residence of Archduke Sigismondo, it is one of the most complete and best preserved castles in South Tyrol.

  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Kurhaus

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Liberty building on the Long Passirium designed by Friedrich Ohmann and inaugurated in 1914; the Kursaal room has frescoes by the Viennese painters Jetmar and Rothaug. Visitable during the events.
