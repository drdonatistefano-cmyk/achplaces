What to see in Orvinio

The highest town in the Parco dei Monti Lucretili, about 840 metres, called Canemorto until 1863; is dominated by the castle Malvezzi Campsites and preserves churches with works by Vincenzo Manenti, a 17th century painter born in the village.

6 places to visit in Orvinio, in the province of Rieti (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Malvezzi Campeggi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Domina Orvinio from the year 1000: held by the lords of Canemorto until the fifteenth century, enlarged by the Orsini in the sixteenth century (a palace called "Granarone") fortified by the Muti and from 1632 of the Borghese, which in two centuries gave him the present aspect. Cracked walls circling almost all the center, cylindrical mast and rustic portal; inside the halls with wooden ceilings with drawers, frescoes of the late sixteenth century on the exploits of the Barons Muti and traps then transformed into cellars.

Private residence of the Marquis Malvezzi Campsites, the rooms are rented for ceremonies

  • Where Via del Castello, Orvinio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Chiesa di Santa Maria dei Raccomandati

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

A single nave church from the second half of the 16th century in the upper part of the village, connected to the centre by the Scalinata del Borgo; it preserves many frescoes by Vincenzo Manenti and Father Ascanio, both buried here. In the canvas of St. Francis with the stigmata Manenti he portrayed himself as the pope.

  • Where Parte alta del borgo, Orvinio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlazio.com

Chiesa di San Giacomo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Erected in 1612 for Baron Giacomo Muti on a project attributed to Gian Lorenzo Bernini, an octagonal plan, with two side altars painted by Vincenzo Manenti.

  • Where Orvinio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlazio.com

Centro storico e arco di Piazza Garibaldi

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

You enter the medieval village from the ancient arch of Piazza Garibaldi; traces of the walls with defensive towers and Renaissance residences remain. Fifty artistic tiles of the "Narranti Villages" (with QR code) let the stories of the elderly hear in the places where the facts took place.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Orvinio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Parco dei Monti Lucretili

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Orvinio is the highest village in the park; around the village a garden of spontaneous orchids and medicinal herbs, walking, cycling and horseback riding routes, and the passage of the Way of St. Benedict.

  • Where Dintorni di Orvinio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · borghipiubelliditalia.it
