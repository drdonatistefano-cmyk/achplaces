What to see in Ponti sul Mincio

The municipality of Mantua nearest Lake Garda, Orange Flag of Touring, dominated by the Scaliger Castle of 1260-1276; in the surroundings the Ardietti Fort of the Austrian Quadrilateral and the Mincio Park with the Mantova-Peschiera bike.

6 places to visit in Ponti sul Mincio, in the province of Mantua (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Scaligero

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built between 1260 and 1276 in the defensive system of the Scaligeri to control the valley towards Garda, it is a castle fenced with an elongated and polygonal plan with five towers, two of which real buildings closed; lost military function in the eighteenth century. Access from a single staircase, not suitable for wheelchairs and strollers; audioguide in Italian, English and German.

closed to the public for restoration works, guided tours only by reservation (tourist site of the Municipality)

  • Where Via Castello, Ponti sul Mincio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.pontisulmincio.mn.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Forte Ardietti

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Fort of the Austrian Quadrilateral built after the First War of Independence in two phases (1853-1859 and 1859-1861), designed for 612 men and 25 cannons; passed to the Kingdom of Italy in 1866, it was fortress until 1918 and ammunition storage until 1998. Well preserved and almost entirely original, exhibits artillery pieces from the sixteenth century to the Korean war; audio guide in three languages.

hours of the source referring to the season 2024 (Friday-Sunday and holidays), out of season visits only by reservation

  • Where Località Mano di Ferro, Ponti sul Mincio
  • Entry €5, 14-18 years old €2, free under 14
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Museo sacrario del Reggimento «Giovani Fascisti» (La Piccola Caprera)

Museums · Themed

Museum founded in 1960 with relics of the regiment and the countryside in North Africa 1940-1943, in three rooms dedicated to the fallen and the commander, the countryside and the Afrika Korps and the opposing forces; a historical office keeps library, photos and documents. Outside, beyond the tomb of the commander, the alert of remembrance with cippi for forgotten departments. Recognized by Regione Lombardia as a museum regiment.

visits by telephone (tourist site of the Municipality); the MiC card indicates every day closed

  • Where Via Pozzolengo 1, Ponti sul Mincio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismo.comune.pontisulmincio.mn.it · italia.it

Monumento ai Caduti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Memorial in Piazza Parolini designed by engineer Giancarlo Maroni, author of the Vittoriale di Gardone Riviera, conceived as an ascendant shrine: a large staircase rises to the monolith .Pilo of the Heroes with the names of fallen.

  • Where Piazza Parolini, Ponti sul Mincio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · in-lombardia.it · italia.it

Antico lavatoio pubblico

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Public washbasin fed by the resurgences of the area, recently restored and still used by the women of the country to wash the clothes by hand.

  • Where Via Roma, Ponti sul Mincio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it · turismo.comune.pontisulmincio.mn.it

Parco del Mincio, La Palude e ciclabile Mantova-Peschiera

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Regional Park established in 1984, one of the first in Lombardy, along the 73 km of the Mincio; without timetables or gates. In Ponti it extends from the village to the river, with the area of the Swamp retrained with hedges and a walkway; there passes the cycle path Mantua-Peschiera, also walkable, with branch for Ponti.

  • Where Ponti sul Mincio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.pontisulmincio.mn.it · italia.it · touringclub.it
