What to see in Lizzano

Country of land and sea in the Taranto, linked to rural culture and DOC wine that bears its name, with the Civic Museum of Palaeontology and Man in Palazzo Majorano, the rock church of the Annunziata and the Grotto of Sant'Angelo. The Marina di Lizzano stretches for 7 km on the Ionian coast between clear sand, dunes and Mediterranean maquis.

Museo civico della paleontologia e dell'uomo

Museums · Themed

In the nineteenth-century rooms of Palazzo Majorano, in the heart of the historic center: fossils from all over the world, the oldest of about 600 million years, some found on the spot; archaeological finds that tell the town from Neolithic to Middle Ages; an ethnographic section with tools, objects of daily use, an ancient hand frame still working and "lu traìnu" , the chariot pulled by horses or mules. A section on the cultures of Africa and Oceania and some works of contemporary art complete the itinerary. Guided tours and educational activities, tel. 099 9550014.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesetta rupestre e Cripta dell'Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

About a kilometer from the old town, a small rock church with an underground crypt dating back to the IX-X century, in the Lombard age; on the crypt the church was built. For centuries, Byzantine monks lived in the area, leaving artistic and spiritual traces, and it is an important place of worship and Marian pilgrimage.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Grotta di Sant'Angelo

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Viewpoint

It opens on the top of the hill of Sant'Angelo, in the district Serra degli Angeli, about 3 km north-west of the village along the SP 110. Digged into the white-blue limestone, it penetrates for about 50 meters; at the bottom have been found bone remains in advanced state of fossilization and fragments of ceramics of various eras, and the traces of numerous early Christian frescoes testify to its use as an ancient place of worship.

Centro storico e chiese

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient heart of the village gathers the Mother Church of St. Nicholas, already existing at the beginning of the 14th century, rebuilt in the 16th and rebuilt in the fifties of the twentieth century after the collapse of the roof, with some altars remained intact; the Church of the SS. Rosario, built in 1562 in Renaissance style, with rosette and crowning tooth; the chapel of the Crucifix next to the Norman castle, the Palazzo Marchesale, the Church of St. Joseph and the Civic Museum.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.lizzano.ta.it · italia.it

Marina di Lizzano

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry

About 7 km of Ionian coast along the Salento coast, between Marina di Pulsano and Torre Ovo: beyond the town, which reaches Torre Sgarrata, large beaches of clear sand and fine give water, in the days of tramontana, Caribbean nuances. Sandy backdrop in slow slopes, long stretches of free beach alternated with equipped beaches, and behind large dunes with protected Mediterranean maquis (ginepro cuddle), small marshes and pine forests and holm oaks; in the evening movida.

  • Where Marina di Lizzano, Lizzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: interregaismart.regione.puglia.it · italia.it
