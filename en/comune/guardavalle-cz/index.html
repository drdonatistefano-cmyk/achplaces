What to see in Guardavalle

Municipality of Costa degli Aranci, on the Ionian Catanzare, with hilly historic center and 4 km of white beach in Guardavalle Marina, closed by the Ionian calanchi protected as Site of Community Importance; in the village, the eighteenth-century Palazzo Trua and the church of Santa Maria delle Grazie.

4 places to visit in Guardavalle, in the province of Catanzaro (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Spiaggia di Guardavalle Marina

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The last beach in the province of Catanzaro, 4 km long, with white silica sand bottom, free stretches, equipped beaches and tourist villages. Behind the Ionian calanchi are hinged with the historic hilly centre and form an ecosystem declared as a Site of Community Importance.

  • Where Guardavalle Marina, Guardavalle
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Palazzo Trua

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

A trapezoidal building in the old town, built almost certainly in the early eighteenth century as the second residence of the notaries Trua, near the church of Sant'Antonio.

recorded by the MiC among abandoned cultural heritage; status and accessibility not indicated

  • Where Incrocio tra via San Carlo, via Roma Antica e via Mazzini, Guardavalle
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: beniabbandonati.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

It rises on the panoramic area known as "Cimitero Vecchio" overlooking the Lanzo river, and dominates the village and the countryside; the date of construction is uncertain, but the church already existed in 1762, as shown by a pastoral visit.

recorded by the MiC as an abandoned cultural heritage; status and accessibility not indicated

  • Where Località Cimitero Vecchio, Guardavalle
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: beniabbandonati.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo Pietrarotta. Arti mestieri civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

In the farmhouse Fassi of the hamlet Pietrarotta, exhibits a collection of objects of peasant civilization and a section dedicated to the history of art and crafts of the Calabrian territory.

MiC card (updated in 2020) indicates "Closed" every day of the week; check before the visit

  • Where Via Nazionale, Guardavalle
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
