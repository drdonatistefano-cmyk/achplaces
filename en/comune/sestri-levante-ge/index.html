What to see in Sestri Levante

The city of the two seas, a Ligurian village colored on a promontory between the Bay of Silence and the Bay of Fairy tales, with a historical center of painted facades and portals in slate, the MuSel in Palazzo Fascie Rossi, the Rizzi Gallery, the ring of Punta Manara and the seaside village of Riva Trigoso.

11 places to visit in Sestri Levante, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The historic centre, which has been developed since the 12th century, preserves facades painted in trompe l'oeil and portals in slate from the 15th and 16th centuries.

MuSel - Museo Archeologico e della Città

Museums · Archaeological

On the third and fourth floor of Palazzo Fascie Rossi, it tells the history of the territory from the Paleolithic to the Bronze Age, at the Roman era and the Middle Ages, until the development of the city in the twentieth century, with the bond with the sea (fishing, yards, tourism). Organizes conferences, presentations, activities for families and exhibitions; building with elevator. Info: IAT 0185 478530, info@musel.it.

Galleria Rizzi

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Wanted by lawyer Marcello Rizzi, he exhibited in the palace of Via Cappuccini (1926) the collection of paintings and art objects collected with his father Vittorio: works by Lelio Orsi, Vincenzo Campi, Denis Calvaert, Andrea Ansaldo, Giovanni Andrea De Ferrari, Carlo Dolci, Alessandro Magnasco, Sebastiano Ricci, Corrado Giaquinto and Felice Boselli, with seventeenth-century furniture and two drawers by Giuseppe Maggiolini. Tel. 0185 41300.

Riva Trigoso

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Former fishing village on the beach on the two sides of the mouth of Petronio, where since the Middle Ages were built the leudians, Latin sailing boats with which were transported wine, oil and cheese; the nucleus of Trigoso rises around the church of Santa Sabina and the remains of a settlement of the Fieschi. Today it houses the shipyards founded in 1897 (Fincantieri), the walk Descalzo and is home to the bagnun of anchovies.

  • Where Riva Trigoso, Sestri Levante
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page lamialiguria.it

Sources: comune.sestri-levante.ge.it · lamialiguria.it

Miniera di Libiola

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

One of the major Italian deposits of copper and iron sulphides, active from the middle of the nineteenth century to 1962 and already known in the Bronze Age (a handle of pickaxes of 1876 is dated to the age of Copper). The rocks are ancient oceanic crust (serpentinite, gabricus, basalt pillow); the acidic mineral waters color the landscape of reddish deposits. You can reach it on foot or by car on the road that climbs from Santa Vittoria di Libiola to Tassani; management Mediterranean Services (0185 478603).

  • Entry To be checked
