What to see in Aggius

Granite village of the Upper Gallura, Orange Flag for its ancient harmonic and intact core, famous for its carpets and choir: the ethnographic MEOC museum, the Banditism Museum in the old Pretura, the AAAperto open-air museum and, in the surroundings, the Valley of the Moon with the Nuraghe Izzana.

9 places to visit in Aggius, in the province of Sassari (Sardinia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Centro storico di granito

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The granite overlooks walls, paved walls, alleys and squares; historical toponyms such as the Aldia, an ancient guard and toll booth, and the Piazza di li Baddhi, the square of the dances are found. The stone houses are considered one of the most beautiful in Gallura.

  • Where Centro storico, Aggius
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

MEOC - Museo etnografico Oliva Carta Cannas

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

About fifteen rooms on two floors reconstruct Gallura's life: shops of the shoemaker, the blacksmith and the carpenter, cellar, kitchen, loom room where women of the village show live the weaving of the carpet, and a room dedicated to the Chorus of Aggius, founded in 1928 with a letter from D'Annunzio. The MiC indicates it as a permanent exhibition of the carpet from 1600 to today.

  • Where Via Monti di Lizu 6, Aggius
  • Entry €4, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Museo AAAperto

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

Museum of contemporary art in the open air spread in the town, with works by Maria Lai, Tellas and Giovanni Campus.

  • Where Centro storico, Aggius
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Museo dell'Amore Perduto

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

A museum-non-museum opened on August 5, 2019 in an old warehouse then textile workshop: 23 stories of love lost in photographic panels and a frame in the center of the hall supporting the photos left by visitors. A few steps from the Museum of Banditism and the MEOC.

  • Where Centro storico, Aggius
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Vittoria

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church, erected in 1536 according to the documents of the Curia; the façade was rebuilt in 1856 and the bell tower, 33 meters high, in the twentieth century. In the village also the churches of the confraternities of the Rosary and of the Holy Cross and the eighteenth-century of Our Lady of Itria.

  • Where Centro storico, Aggius
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Valle della Luna (Piana dei Grandi Sassi)

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

In Li Parisi, on the border with Trinità d'Agultu and Vignola: a basin of rounded granite boulders, some with human or animal shapes, with tafoni used as shelters, cork oaks and arbutes; hiking trails, mountain biking and horse riding and a viewpoint on the provincial road 74. Set of the movie "The Free Prince" (2018).

  • Where Località Li Parisi, Aggius
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Nuraghe Izzana

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

In the heart of the Valley of the Moon, one of the best preserved nuraghi of Gallura and probably the largest structure of the Bronze Age of the area: mixed type, corridor and tholos, with two entrances and a labyrinth of tunnels and corridors fruit of several construction phases.

  • Where Valle della Luna, Aggius
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Laghetto Santa Degna e Monti di Aggius

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Aggius Mountains form an amphitheater of rocky and serrated peaks, a destination for hiking or mountain biking on ancient pastoral paths; the Laghetto Santa Degna is a wild place between cork groves and granite mountains, with a seasonal kiosk.

  • Where Laghetto Santa Degna, Aggius
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it
