What to see in Arzano

Common north of Naples from the centuries-old tradition of hemp, which appears in the coat of arms, with the tower of the Bourbon clock in the square, the church of the patron Sant'Agrippino and the museum of peasant culture born from the collection of Antonio De Rosa.

Museo della cultura contadina e degli antichi mestieri «A. De Rosa»

Museums · Themed

Recognized regional museum in 2010, it was born from the tools donated by Antonio De Rosa's private collection and is ordered in six sections: plowing, harvesting (bocker's tools, presses), agricultural tools, hemp and flax (tiles, combs, bunches), everyday objects and shoemakers', carpenters and blacksmith's trades.

The MiC card (updated in May 2020) indicates the museum closed every day: check the opening (tel. 081 5737125, museumarzano@hotmail.it).

  • Where Via Isonzo 3, Arzano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.arzano.na.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · comune.arzano.na.it

Torre dell'orologio

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Wanted by Ferdinand II of Bourbon in 1847 among the civic towers of the Neapolitan countryside, it was raised because the sound did not reach beyond the square; in 1889 the Neapolitan watchmaker Alfonso Curci built a new clock. The Bourbon bells, merged for the war, were rebuilt in 1944 with a collection wanted by Mayor Michele Capasso. It can only be seen from the outside: the tower is not accessible to the public.

  • Where Piazzetta Crocifisso 6, Arzano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.arzano.na.it

Sources: comune.arzano.na.it

Chiesa di Sant'Agrippino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Dedicated to the patron saint of Arzano, it was erected in 1560, enlarged in 1598 with a larger portal on the façade and in 1753, after various works, assumed an appearance similar to the present.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.arzano.na.it
