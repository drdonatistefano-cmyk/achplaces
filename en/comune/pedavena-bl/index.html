What to see in Pedavena

Common at the foot of Mount Avena, known for its brewery founded in 1897; between seventeenth-century villas such as Villa Pasole-Berton and churches born on the ruins of ancient castles, rises to the Passo Croce d'Aune and the sanctuary of Saints Tiburzio and Susanna.

Birreria Pedavena

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The brewery, founded in 1897, has rooms with painted walls, staircases, columns and copper mescita counters, and a large park with tennis courts and bowls; it houses exhibitions, tournaments, concerts and outdoor shows. A cycle path along the Colmeda stream takes about 10 minutes to the center.

Villa Pasole-Berton

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Built by the Pasole in the seventeenth century on the ruins of an ancient castle and enlarged in the eighteenth century with curved staircase and pediment, it was the seat of the Accademia degli Erranti, which discussed art, philosophy and literature; the Berton bought it in the early nineteenth century. The garden has a canal-fish, fountain and statues; the chapel of St. Anne, consecrated in 1671, preserves a altarpiece by Domenico Falce in 1661. It can be seen from Via Trento, near the octagonal church of St. Anne.

  • Entry Free

Santuario dei Santi Tiburzio e Susanna

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

At 929 meters on Mount Avena, above Facen, it is reached with a steep path; built by a hermit between the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, probably on the ruins of a watchtower, had the pronao in 1863. For centuries, a destination for pilgrims, for the legend of the rainwater of the circular tank that healed from the cough; near is the Sass del Diaol.

  • Where Monte Avena, sopra Facen, Pedavena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page comune.pedavena.bl.it

Sources: comune.pedavena.bl.it

Monte Avena e Passo Croce d'Aune

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Access is from Passo Croce d'Aune, at 1.015 meters, reachable from Pedavena with the state road 473 and starting point of hiking itineraries, on the route of Alta Via n. 2. In summer the mountain offers pastures and pastures for walks, in winter downhill slopes and a cross-country trail; it is frequented by the free flight and preserves archaeological traces from the Neanderthal era onwards.

  • Entry Free
