What to see in Viggiù

Country of marble artists and sculptors in the north-east of the province of Varese, famous for the quarries of "pietra di Viggiù" worked by the Picasass and for the artists (Butti, Giudici, Longhi, Piatti, Argenti, Galli) active in Rome between 1500 and 1650; in the park of the villa of the sculptor Enrico Butti the Civic Museums Viggiutesi gather the gipsoteca with 87 its plasters and the Museum of the 20th century with the Viggiute sculpture of the 20th century.

Museo Enrico Butti

Museums · Modern art

Gipsoteca di arte contemporanea in sette sale nel parco della villa di Enrico Butti (Viggiù 1847-1932), student and then professor of sculpture in Brera, author of the Minatore (Grand Prix at the Paris Universal Exposition of 1889) and the monument to Verdi in Milan (1913). It preserves 87 original models in plaster and some paintings, donated by the sculptor to the Municipality in 1926; in 2002-2004 the restoration added three rooms for the plasters of the monument to Verdi. Hours: Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 14-18.30, Wednesday and Saturday 10-12 and 14-18.30; Sunday closed in winter.

Museo del '900

Museums · Modern art

Pavilion built in the 1960s in the Butti Park at the behest of the widow of the sculptor Giacomo Buzzi Reschini and reopened in autumn 2006 after the restoration, it presents an anthology of the Viggiuto sculpture of the twentieth century. The central halls are dedicated to Buzzi Reschini, with the monumental group "The good that lands the evil" and sketches of monuments; one room is dedicated to Nando Conti and another to Bottinelli, Cattò, Cedraschi and Freschetti, with the plaster model of the head of the Baptist whose marble is in the parish church. Admission ticket, accessible to people with motor disabilities; opening hours: Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 14-18.30, Wednesday and Saturday 10-12 and 14-18.30, Sunday 16-19, Monday closed.
