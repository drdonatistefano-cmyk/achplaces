What to see in Prali

A mountain country about 1455 meters in the Germanasca Valley, with the talc mines of the Ecomuseo (DiscoverMiniere and DiscoverAlpi), the Waldensian Museum in the ancient temple of 1556 in Ghigo and the glacial basin of the Thirteen Lakes.

6 places to visit in Prali, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Ecomuseo delle Miniere e della Valle Germanasca (ScopriMiniera e ScopriAlpi)

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

The mines Paola and Gianna have more than 4 km of tunnels equipped with two routes: DiscoverMiniere (miniera Paola), where you enter the tunnel on the train of the miners between the shining of talc, and DiscoverAlpi (miniera Gianna). The Ecomuseum also tells about the agricultural and mining heritage of the valley, the Waldensian culture and the Occitan language. Free admission with Museums and Torino+Piemonte Card.

  • Where Località Paola, Prali
  • Entry €16, reduced €13.50; both mines €26
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website ecomuseominiere.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: ecomuseominiere.it · turismotorino.org · comune.prali.to.it

Museo valdese di Prali e Tempio valdese

Museums · City history

The museum is housed in the ancient Waldensian temple of 1556, the only place of Waldensian worship in the valleys that survived the persecutions of the seventeenth century, and tells the history of the valley from prehistory to today, with sections on talc mines, minerals, flora and fauna, agriculture and milk processing; re-established in 2001. Next to it stands the modern temple inaugurated in 1962, at 1455 meters the highest of the Waldensian valleys, with eighteenth-century benches and a large stone mosaic. In summer open on weekdays 15-19 and holidays also 9-12; in winter on request.

  • Where Borgata Ghigo, Prali
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.prali.to.it · turismotorino.org

Museo di Rodoretto

Museums · Themed

Small museum born in 1973 from a local initiative and expanded in 1981, in the solitary valley of Rodoretto: a hundred objects on the job and daily life in the mountains, with environments such as kitchen, room, cellar and school. Visits to be arranged by telephone.

  • Where Frazione Villa di Rodoretto, Prali
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.prali.to.it · turismotorino.org

Conca dei Tredici Laghi

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Conca with about ten glacial lakes, once thirteen, including Lake Ramella, Lake Nero and Lake Primo, where you can meet the rare black salamander. Site of regional importance, preserves military testimonies such as the Perrucchetti shelters and, at Lake Ramella, two pieces of cannon of the Second World War; six itineraries range from a walk of about 2 hours to a 6-hour tour.

  • Where Partenza da Ghigo di Prali
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.prali.to.it · visitpiemonte.com · turismotorino.org

Lago Verde e rifugio

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small lake at 2583 meters next to the refuge Green Lake, reachable in about 2 hours and 20 minutes from Bout du Col or 3 hours and 20 minutes from Ghigo.

  • Where Prali
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.prali.to.it · turismotorino.org

Il sentiero dei bambini

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Family route from Ghigo to Villa with five stages narrated, panels, wooden sculptures, a forest of trees "talking" and audio through QR code; in winter it becomes a cross-country trail.

  • Where Da Ghigo a Villa, Prali
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.prali.to.it · turismotorino.org
