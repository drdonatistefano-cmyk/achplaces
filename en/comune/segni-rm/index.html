What to see in Segni

The ancient Signia, Volsca and then Latina, on the Lepini Mountains about 60 km from Rome, twinned with Mycena for its cyclopean walls: the Saracen Gate, the church of San Pietro on the podium of the temple of Juno, the Nymphaeum of Quinto Muzio and the Archaeological Museum compose the urban archaeological Park \Segni Città-Museo Â .

Museo archeologico comunale

Museums · Archaeological

In the thirteenth century Palazzo della Comunità on the main square, tells the ancient Signia from prehistory to the Middle Ages on two floors: necropolis sets, ex-vote in terracotta (tones, statuettes, a head of newborn in bands), figures of Minerva, Hercules and Venus, sacred and funerary Latin inscriptions, ceramics, architectural fragments and reconstructive plastics of the city. It runs the urban archaeological park; the specialist library is visited by appointment.

  • Where Via Lauri 1, Segni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Mura ciclopiche e Porta Saracena

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The polygonal walls, for which Segni is twinned with Mycenae, go up from the fortifications to the acropolis. The Saracen Gate has a monolithic architrave of over three meters and is the best preserved example of its kind; it was staged on the Grand Tour and designed by Irish painter Edward Dodwell.

  • Where Via Porta Saracena, Segni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Acropoli, chiesa di San Pietro e cisterna romana

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

On the acropolis the church of St. Peter stands on the podium of the Roman temple of Juno Moneta, of the third century BC, and inside it remain visible the original walls of the cell in square blocks. Below is a large Roman cistern, now used for cultural events.

  • Where Acropoli, Segni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Ninfeo di Quinto Muzio

History and architecture · Archaeology

Monumental fountain of the II-I century B.C. of which the architect is known: his signature is written in Greek letters with blue Egyptian glass tiles. Acquired and opened to the public in July 2020, it completed the urban archaeological park.

  • Where Segni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Rebuilt in 1657 by the architect Giovanni Battista Roderi, it is a baroque building with Greek cross with stuccoes, gold and marbles; the chapels preserve works by artists of the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries and the organ Morettini of 1850 is still working. It rises in the area of the ancient hole.

  • Where Segni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Porta Maggiore e Ponte Scarabeo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Porta Maggiore, part of the Palazzo Conti complex, is the monumental entrance to the historic centre, already access to the ancient Signia. The Scrabble Bridge is an arch of the mid-twentieth century in blocks of tuff, resting on two pillars, one anchored to the polygonal walls and the other incorporated into a house; the legend wants a young Carthaginian to give a scarab to a girl of Segni as a pledge of love.

  • Where Centro storico, Segni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Monte Lupone

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

With its 1,378 meters it is the northernmost peak of the Lepini Mountains, covered with beech, maples and oaks; you climb with an easy excursion from the sports field of Segni and the top offers a spectacular view.

  • Where Campo sportivo, Segni
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Marrone di Segni, fregnaquanti e appallocco

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The brown of Segni is celebrated at the end of October with the Festival of Brown and the Copeta procession; on the table the fregnaquanti, very thin egg pasta with meat sauce, the appal of cooked fava beans crushed with garlic, oil and chilli, and the black truffle of Lepini.

  • Where Segni
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it
