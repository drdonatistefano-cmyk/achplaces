What to see in Pettorano sul Gizio

Medieval village of the valley of Gizio, very damaged by the earthquake of 1706 and restored especially in the eighteenth century, closed by walls with five doors still visible and dominated by the Castello Cantelmo with the pentagonal tower; around, the Regional Nature Reserve Monte Genzana Alto Gizio.

Castello Cantelmo e Museo del Territorio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Medieval castle, once among the most important garrisons of the area: the imposing remains are restored and the pentagonal tower is the core of the defensive system. It houses art exhibitions and the route of the Museo del Territorio della Riserva, in five rooms with plastic and reconstructions, from the history of the Peligni to the Middle Ages to the popular traditions.

  • Where Piazza Zannelli 12, Pettorano sul Gizio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.riservagenzana.it

Mura e porte del borgo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Cinta of the late Middle Ages with six doors, five still open, in most of the times of Fabrizio Cantelmo (1611-1658): above Porta San Nicola a 17th century fresco shows Santa Margherita that holds the village; then Porta Cencia, Porta San Marco and Porta Santa Margherita. From the church of the Madonna della Libera start the "rue" , narrow and steep streets that descend towards the walls.

  • Where Centro storico, Pettorano sul Gizio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Palazzo Ducale e Piazza Zannelli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Residence of the Cantelmo in three wings around a square court open on the fourth side towards the valley; the courtyard is today Piazza Zannelli, with a beautiful fountain. In the village also Palazzo De Stephanis, with Rococo façade, the eighteenth-century Palazzo Giuliani and Palazzo Croce, which preserves the only fragment found in the West of the Edit of Diocletian (301 AD).

  • Where Piazza Zannelli, Pettorano sul Gizio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
