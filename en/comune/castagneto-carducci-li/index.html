Viale dei Cipressi di Bolgheri

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The avenue of about 5 km of secular cypresses from San Guido to Bolgheri, made famous by Carducci and considered a national monument.

  • Where Strada Provinciale 16 Bolgherese, Castagneto Carducci
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Borgo di Bolgheri

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The village with the castle and the terracotta statue of Grandma Lucia, among the Supertuscan cellars.

  • Where Bolgheri, Castagneto Carducci
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Castello della Gherardesca e Casa Carducci

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The castle built around the year 1000 with the church of San Lorenzo and the terrace belvedere; in the village the house where Carducci lived, today museum.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
