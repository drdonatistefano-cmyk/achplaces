What to see in Vernazza

Between Monterosso and Riomaggiore, the village of the Cinque Terre with the only marina, the church of Santa Margherita d'Antiochia on the sea and the tower Doria, the most beautiful village in Italy; in the municipality also Corniglia, the only one of the Cinque Terre not facing the shore.

7 places to visit in Vernazza, in the province of La Spezia (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Vernazza in the guide with map and filters

Via Roma e piazza del porticciolo

History and architecture · Villages · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The central street with shops and a permanent photographic exhibition on the flood of 2011, which ends in the colored square in front of the only marina of the Cinque Terre.

  • Where Via Roma, Vernazza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · whc.unesco.org

Chiesa di Santa Margherita d'Antiochia

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Overlooking the square, in Gothic Ligurian with an octagonal tower of 40 meters and a Romanesque plan with three naves; it houses a wooden crucifix of Maragliano.

  • Where Piazza Marconi, Vernazza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Torre Doria

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The remains of the castle that once controlled the bay.

  • Where Vernazza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Santuario della Madonna di Reggio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the 11th century, partly rebuilt, about an hour walk from the village along a path of chapels and vineyards; stop of the Via dei Santuari.

  • Where Reggio, Vernazza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Corniglia

History and architecture · Villages · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the five villages of the Cinque Terre, the only one not built on the shore; near the diving site of the Franata di Corniglia.

  • Where Corniglia, Vernazza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: whc.unesco.org · italia.it

Santuario di San Bernardino

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

The sanctuary above Corniglia linked to the restoration considered miraculous of a painting of the Virgin, stage of the Via dei Santuari.

  • Where San Bernardino, Vernazza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Sentiero Verde Azzurro da Vernazza

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Viewpoint

The stretch that starts from Via Roma and goes up to a viewpoint on the marina.

  • Where Via Roma, Vernazza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
