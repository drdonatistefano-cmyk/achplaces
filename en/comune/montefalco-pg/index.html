What to see in Montefalco

The "Ringhiera dell'Umbria" is a panoramic village of Sagrantino, with frescoes by Benozzo Gozzoli and Perugino in the former church of San Francesco.

3 places to visit in Montefalco, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Complesso Museale di San Francesco

Museums · Old masters

In the former 14th-century church and convent, the cycle of the Stories of St. Francis painted by Benozzo Gozzoli in 1452, the chapel of St. Jerome, the art gallery, a Roman statue of Hercules and the ancient cellars.

  • Where Via Ringhiera Umbra 6, Montefalco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it
