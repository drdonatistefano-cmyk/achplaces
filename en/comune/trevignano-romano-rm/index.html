What to see in Trevignano Romano

Village on Lake Bracciano, on a cliff of leukic lava at the bottom of a small gulf, with the medieval center dominated by the ruins of the Rocca degli Orsini, the tree-lined lake promenade and the Etruscan-Roman civic museum with the furnishings of the tombs of the Olivetello; around the Regional Natural Park of Bracciano-Martignano.

5 places to visit in Trevignano Romano, in the province of Rome (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca degli Orsini

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ruins of the fortress wanted by Pope Innocent III at the beginning of the thirteenth century on a rocky promontory, then passed to the Orsini; it was severely damaged at the end of the fifteenth century in the clash with Pope Alexander VI Borgia and the last tower collapsed during the Second World War. Free access 24 hours a day, with a path lit at night, climbing the staircase next to the church of the Assumption.

  • Where Scalinata accanto alla chiesa dell'Assunta, Trevignano Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Borgo medievale

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The medieval old town, largely preserved intact, stands on a cliff of leukic lava at the bottom of a small gulf, secondary crater of an ancient volcano.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele III, Trevignano Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Lungolago

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lungolago tree-lined of about three kilometers with restaurants, on Lake Bracciano, volcanic lake where motor boats are not allowed and you can go canoeing, sailing and windsurfing.

  • Where Lungolago, Trevignano Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Museo civico etrusco-romano «Gregorio Bianchini»

Museums · Archaeological

On the ground floor of the Town Hall, it exhibits about 350 objects from the Etruscan tombs: buccheri, bronze vases, fibulas, gold and amber ornaments of the VII-VI century BC; there are a large bronze flabello and two amphorae painted by the Annesi Piacentini and the Flabelli tombs, found intact in the 1960s in the necropolis of the Olivetello, a tomb of warrior of the VIII century BC and the remains of two chariots.

Le Pantane e Lagusiello

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

A wet area among the volcanic lakes of the Park of Bracciano-Martignano, shelter of water birds loved by birdwatcher (cormorans, royal germans, folagues), near the small crater of the Lagusiello. The park, established in 1999, has trails for all levels.

  • Where Trevignano Romano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it
