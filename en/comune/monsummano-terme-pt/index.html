What to see in Monsummano Terme

The thermal city of Valdinievole wanted by the Medici around the sanctuary of Fontenuova: the warm caves Giusti and Parlanti, the birthplace of the poet Giuseppe Giusti, the museums of the Osteria dei Pellegrini and Villa Renatico Martini, and above the village Monsummano Alto.

9 places to visit in Monsummano Terme, in the province of Pistoia (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario di Maria Santissima della Fontenuova

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Built in 1602-1605 on a project by Gherardo Mechini and wanted by Grand Duke Ferdinando I, a Latin cross with portico on three sides; in the lunettes of the loggia the seventeenth-century frescoes by Giovanni da San Giovanni with the Miracles of the Madonna di Fontenuova, inside an Adoration of the Magi by Matteo Rosselli.

  • Where Piazza Giusti, Monsummano Terme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: touringclub.it · visittuscany.com

Museo della Città e del Territorio – Osteria dei Pellegrini

Museums · City history

In the Osteria dei Pellegrini built in 1607-1616 by Ferdinando de' Medici, on a project by Mechini, to welcome the pilgrims of the sanctuary: it tells the Valdinievole and the reclamation of the Padule di Fucecchio.

  • Where Piazza Giusti, Monsummano Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: touringclub.it · visittuscany.com

Museo di Arte contemporanea e del Novecento – Villa Renatico Martini

Museums · Modern art

In the eclectic villa of 1887 surrounded by a large park: graphics, painting and Italian sculpture of the twentieth century and contemporary.

  • Where Via Gragnano 349, Monsummano Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: touringclub.it · visittuscany.com

Museo nazionale di Casa Giusti

Museums · House museums

Birthplace of the poet Giuseppe Giusti (1809-1850): on the first floor antique furnishings set by Piedmont romance, on the second a large documentary section; in the library intact the best known portrait of the poet, by Giuseppe Bezzuoli; multimedia installations with readings of his verses.

  • Where Via Giacomo Matteotti 13, Monsummano Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · cultura.gov.it

Grotta Giusti

Local life and traditions · Spas

Karst cave known for the beauty and therapeutic effects of its vapors, a sort of natural sauna in a secular park; symbol of the thermal tradition of Monsummano, which dates back to the Roman era.

  • Where Monsummano Terme
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · touringclub.it

Monsummano Alto

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill that dominates the village stood the ancient medieval walled village; on the highlands Grand Duke Ferdinando I de' Medici had built a villa that still dominates them today.
