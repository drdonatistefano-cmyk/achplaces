What to see in Caprino Veronese

Borgo collected at the foot of Monte Baldo, built on ancient Etruscan and Roman settlements, with squares, churches and historical buildings: Villa Carlotti with the Civic Museum and the 14th-century Companti, the monumental Platano dei Cento Bersaglieri and the panoramic paths of Baldo.

Villa Carlotti e Museo Civico

Museums · City history

It was built on a 15th-century rural house in the Vimercati area and passed to the Carlotti family in 1601, who completed it in 1632 with a large garden with statues and enlarged it at the beginning of the 18th century; bought by the Municipality in 1937, since 1952 it is municipal seat. On the ground floor the Civic Museum, born from the finds collected by the master Giovanni Solinas and expanded by the researchers led by Mario Marangoni, exhibits archaeological and paleontological finds, objects of the Great War and the late 14th century by the church of the Holy Sepulchre, attributed to the Master of Santa Anastasia; the most suggestive room is the Hall of Dreams, of the early seventeenth century, decorated with grotesques. Closed on Monday.

Platano dei Cento Bersaglieri

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Monumental tree of 1370, considered the largest plane tree in Italy: over 25 meters high, about 15 meters in circumference and a crown of 300 m2. In 1937, during the Army's Great Summer Maneuvers, a company of one hundred Bersaglieri hid among its branches; in 1944 the German troops tried to thin it out for fear of partisan ambushes.

Monte Baldo

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

The mountain is known for the richness of its flora and for the wide views of Garda; around Caprino trails, pastures, districts and nature trails, with the possibility of climbing, paragliding and horseback riding.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village, built on ancient Etruscan and Roman settlements, has squares, churches, historic buildings and testimonies of rural life of the past, between characteristic villages and vineyards; in Piazza Roma, in Palazzo Carlotti, is the seat of IAT Baldo Garda.
