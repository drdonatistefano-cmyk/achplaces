What to see in Udine

The "Venice of Friuli": Piazza della Libertà with the Loggia del Lionello under the Castle Hill and its civic museums, the Tiepolo of the Archbishop's Palace, the Duomo and the Oratory of Purity, Casa Cavazzini for the twentieth century and a city of gardens.

21 places to visit in Udine, in the province of Udine (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza della Libertà

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The oldest monumental fulcrum of the city, dominated by the Castle Hill: it is reminiscent of a small Venetian square, with the road instead of the canal.

  • Where Piazza della Libertà, Udine
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Loggia del Lionello

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Castello di Udine e Civici Musei

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

16th-century castle seat of the city museums: Archaeological Museum with materials from Aquileia, Galleria d'Arte Antica from the fourteenth to the nineteenth century with the Consilium in arena of Giambattista and Giandomenico Tiepolo, Museo del Risorgimento from 1797 to 1866 and Museo della Fotografia with the first photo taken in Udine (1856). From the back staircase the panorama of the Carnic and Julian Alps.

  • Where Piazzale della Patria del Friuli 1, Udine
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Palazzo Arcivescovile - Museo Diocesano e Gallerie del Tiepolo

Museums · Old masters

Palace of 16th century origin, set up in the 18th century with the work of Giambattista Tiepolo: the Hunting of the rebel angels on the staircase (1726, his first work in Udine), the Guest Gallery, the Delfino Library and the Red Hall with the Judgment of Solomon; the museum contains wooden sculptures, paintings and sacred art of Friuli.

  • Where Piazza Patriarcato 1, Udine
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Torre di Porta Aquileia

History and architecture · Towers

Part of the fifth and last circle of walls, erected between 1330 and 1440 for 7,117 meters; with Porta Villalta is the only survivor of the thirteen gates of the city, with the path of a round lace.
