What to see in Asciano

Borgo delle Crete Senesi with a 14th century civic museum and, not far away, the abbey of Monte Oliveto Maggiore.

3 places to visit in Asciano, in the province of Siena (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Abbazia di Monte Oliveto Maggiore

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

The Benedictine red brick monastery nestled in the Crete Senesi near Asciano.

  • Where Monte Oliveto Maggiore, Asciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo di Palazzo Corboli

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

Civic, archaeological and sacred art museum in a 14th-century palace, with wooden sculptures by Francesco di Valdambrino and a large mosaic of the 4th century A.D.

  • Where Asciano
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Basilica di Sant'Agata

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

The Romanesque basilica of the village, marked by Visit Tuscany among the monuments to see in Asciano together with Palazzo Corboli.

  • Where Asciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
