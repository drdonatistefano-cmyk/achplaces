What to see in Borgo Valsugana

The capital of Valsugana is the only village in Trentino, developed on both sides of the Brenta, with a historical centre of Venetian origin along Corso Ausugum, the Venetian Bridge, Castel Telvana that dominates the village, the permanent exhibition of the Great War and, in Val di Sella, the open-air museum of Arte Sella.

Arte Sella

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry · Viewpoint

International exhibition of art in nature born in 1986 in Val di Sella, with works especially in stones, leaves, branches and trunks allowed to evolve in the woods: the ArteNatura route, since 1996, on the south side of Mount Armentera, the garden of Villa Strobele and Malga Costa with exhibitions and workshops. More than 300 artists have worked there: among the works the Vegetal Cathedral of Giuliano Mauri, the Third Paradise of Michelangelo Pistoletto, Symbiosi by Edward Tresoldi (2019) and The Broken Oak by Stuart Ian Frost with oaks cut down by the storm Vaia.

  • Where Malga Costa, Val di Sella, Borgo Valsugana
  • Entry €11, 11-16 years €5
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitvalsugana.it · visittrentino.info · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Mostra permanente della Grande Guerra in Valsugana e sul Lagorai

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the former Mulino Spagolla sul Brenta, inaugurated in 2002 and expanded in 2005: windows, relics, photographs and dioramas tell the war on the front and the life of the communities, with the uniform of the Austro-Hungarian aviator Josef Kiss and the memorabilia of General Gabriele Nasci; the second section reconstructs an Austro-Hungarian trenches and an Italian gallery and displays medals of both armies.

  • Where Vicolo Sottochiesa, Borgo Valsugana
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitvalsugana.it · italia.it

Corso Ausugum e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The main street, with portals, late Renaissance and Baroque palaces, porticos, shops and churches; on some facades sixteenth century frescoes of the Virgin with the Child and Saints, linked by tradition to the plague of 1508. The center, of Venetian character, develops on the two banks of the Brenta around Piazza Alcide De Gasperi.

  • Where Corso Ausugum, Borgo Valsugana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · italia.it

Ponte Veneziano

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

A fifteenth-century bridge on the Brenta with two newsstands decorated with graffiti inspired by works of the seventeenth-century by Jacopo Fiorentini; it joins the "Borgo Vecio" to the other half of the country.

  • Where Borgo Valsugana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visittrentino.info · italia.it

Castel Telvana e Scala a Telvana

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Bastión complex that dominates the village, reachable on foot in about half an hour: from the porch after the Venetian Bridge starts the Scala in Telvana, which passes from the monastery of the Clarisses and the Franciscans and continues along the mule track of the castles.

privately owned castle, not accessible interior

La Rocchetta

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Promontory just over 700 meters south of the village which, with Mount Ciolino, barred the valley: in the Great War it was not a battlefield but an observation point for Italians and Austrians. Open all year round with free entry, with marked trails and a panoramic terrace over Borgo and the valley.

  • Where Strada per la Val di Sella, Borgo Valsugana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitvalsugana.it · visittrentino.info · italia.it
