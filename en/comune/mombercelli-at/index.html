What to see in Mombercelli

Country of vineyards and hazelnuts on the hill of the Valtiglione, south of Asti, with the motto "Hospes veniat, fugiat hostis"; probably born as a fortified village of Longobard, preserves the remains of the castle of 1160, the parish church of San Biagio, the neoclassical town hall and two civic museums, the MUSarMO of modern art and the Museum of Vine and Wine.

MUSarMO – Museo Civico d'Arte Moderna e Contemporanea

Museums · Modern art

Civic museum with works by artists of the twentieth century, set up in the building that once housed the prisons of the village, where temporary exhibitions are also held. Limited opening: in January and February only on Sundays for temporary exhibitions or by reservation; from March on the third Sunday of the month, Sundays with events and by reservation (tel. 0141 959610).

  • Where Via Brofferio 21, Mombercelli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.mombercelli.at.it · italia.it

Museo Storico della Vite e del Vino

Museums · Themed

Established by the Municipality in 2001 to preserve the heritage of local viticulture. For timetables and visits you can contact the Municipality (tel. 0141 959610).

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Parrocchiale di San Biagio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Three-nave parish church of the seventeenth century, frescoed in 1852 by the Ivaldi brothers called "Mut" .

  • Where Via Umberto I 20, Mombercelli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Resti del Castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The castle was built in 1160 by the lords of Asti; the fief passed around 1305 to Martino Alfieri, treasurer of Amedeo V of Savoy, and in 1342 to Galeazzo II Visconti. Today on the hill above the center remain imposing supporting walls.

  • Where Via Umberto I 15, Mombercelli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Municipio neoclassico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Neoclassical municipal palace, formerly the prison palace, built in the second half of the nineteenth century with the contribution of the Turin architect Angelo Santoné; today it houses the Art Gallery of the Municipality.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Alfieri 2, Mombercelli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
