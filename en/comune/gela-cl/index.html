What to see in Gela

Ancient Greek city on the Gulf of the same name: the walls of Capo Soprano, the oldest thermal complex discovered in Italy, the acropolis of Molino a Vento and the new Museo dei Relitti Greci in Bosco Littorio; in the center, rich in Liberty residences, the church of Sant'Agostino of 1439, and to the east the reserve of the Biviere for birdwatching.

8 places to visit in Gela, in the province of Caltanissetta (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo dei Relitti Greci

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Inaugurated in February 2026 in the Littorio Forest, where at the mouth of the river Gela stood an archaic emporium, probably also port of the Greek city, exhibits the remains of three Greek ships with their cargo: at the center the wreck The 4000 m2 room, designed by Ettore Di Mauro, has a reversed wooden roof and a room with 3D viewers.

  • Where Area archeologica Bosco Littorio, Gela
  • Entry €6, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Museo archeologico regionale di Gela

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

It tells the history of Gela through ceramics, bronzes and coins; the oldest core are the Navarra and Nocera collections. Among the findings presented by the Park are an archaic mask, a silent antefix, a horse head, a bronze corinzio helmet and a silver drama. It is the seat of the Archaeological Park of Gela; the ticket also includes the Walls Timoleontee and the acropolis of Molino a Vento.

Temporarily closed for demolition, expansion, handling and safety of the finds (Archaeological Park of Gela); from open every day 9-19, tel. 0933 912626

  • Where Corso Vittorio Emanuele 2, Gela
  • Entry €6, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · italia.it

Mura Timoleontee di Capo Soprano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

The walls of the Greek city in Capo Soprano, a remarkable example of mixed construction technique, with the stretch discovered in excellent condition: Italia.it indicates them as the best preserved example of Greek military architecture.

Open only on days indicated by the Park (in October 9-13) and with obligatory booking by email to park.archeo.gela@region.sicilia.it

  • Where Capo Soprano, Gela
  • Entry €6, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · italia.it

Bagni Greci, terme ellenistiche

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Baths of Hellenistic age found in 1957 during the excavations in Via Europa, behind the hospital Vittorio Emanuele, and destroyed in 282 BC, when the city was razed to the ground: according to Italia.it is the oldest thermal complex so far discovered in Italy. Open every day 9-19.

  • Where Via Europa (dietro l'ospedale Vittorio Emanuele), Gela
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · italia.it

Acropoli di Molino a Vento

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

The acropolis of the ancient city, with traces of prehistoric frequentation and ancient protocorinthian ceramics from the end of the 8th century BC. Open from Monday to Friday 9-13; Saturday and Sunday only by booking by email.

  • Where Molino a Vento, Gela
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · italia.it

Area archeologica Bosco Littorio

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

The emporium of the ancient Gela on the south-eastern slopes of the hill, systematically explored in recent times: there are two medieval furnaces, the site of a military camp and the remains of a large residential quarter of the fourth century BC. The Museum of Greek Relics is located there.

Area indicated by the Park as temporarily closed for restoration or maintenance (the Museum of Greek Shipwrecks inside is open)

  • Where Bosco Littorio, Gela
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Agostino

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded in 1439, with a single nave with an annex convent, it is the oldest building in the old town, where the most common style in the noble residences is the Liberty.

  • Where Centro storico, Gela
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Riserva naturale orientata Biviere di Gela

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Reserve established in 1997 and entrusted to the LIPU, with marsh vegetation and riparian: it is important for the wintering, the nidification and the stop of migratory birds, with mothsmen, spatulae, a colony of pendolini and the only Sicilian site of nesting of the partridge of sea; there live also the discoglosso painting and the endemic Leopoldia gussonei. Hiking is free, without leaving the marked trails in zone A.

  • Where Biviere, Gela
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: orbs.regione.sicilia.it · italia.it
