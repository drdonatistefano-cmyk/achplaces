What to see in Mesola

3 places to visit in Mesola, in the province of Ferrara (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello Estense di Mesola

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage

Hunting delight of Alfonso II d'Este built between 1578 and 1583 on a project by Giovan Battista Aleotti, with four crenellated towers; it houses the Ecomuseum of the Bosco and the Deer.

  • Where Mesola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Riserva naturale Gran Bosco della Mesola

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

1,058 hectares of the extensive hunting estate, the last large planitial forest of the coast, where the deer of the dunes lives protected; three routes from one to three hours.

  • Where Bosco Mesola, Mesola
  • Entry Entrance to the free trails; visits by bus for a fee
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Torre dell'Abate

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Key to "vincian doors" of the second half of the sixteenth century, hydraulic work of the extensive reclamation near Santa Giustina.

  • Where Santa Giustina, Mesola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
