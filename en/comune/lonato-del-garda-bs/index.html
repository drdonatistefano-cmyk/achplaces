What to see in Lonato del Garda

Borgo on the morainic hills of the lower Garda, dominated by the Visconti-Venetian Rocca of the Ugo Da Como Foundation with the House of the Podestà and its library of over 50,000 volumes; in the village the Civic Tower of 1555 and the Baroque Basilica, in the surroundings the Abbey of Maguzzano and the Roman Fornaci.

Rocca visconteo-veneta

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress on the morainic hill used by the Visconti and the Venetians, divided into Rocchetta and Main Quarter reached by a drawbridge; in front of its walls Napoleon defeated the Austrians in 1796. National monument since 1912, belongs to the Ugo Da Como Foundation; the ticket includes the immersive room with 360° projections and the Ornithological Museum. Free visit every day 10-18 according to the Municipality.

  • Where Via Rocca 2, Lonato del Garda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.fondazioneugodacomo.it

Sources: fondazioneugodacomo.it · in-lombardia.it · visitbrescia.it · comune.lonato.bs.it

Casa del Podestà – Fondazione Ugo Da Como

Museums · House museums

fifteenth-century house of the Venetian podestà, purchased at auction in 1906 by Senator Ugo Da Como from Bresciano and restored by architect Antonio Tagliaferri. It preserves antique furniture, pharmacy vases, ceramics, paintings, private studio and the Red Room, and a library of over 50,000 volumes, among the largest private collections in Northern Italy, with the smallest book in the world (pages 15 x 9 mm). Admission fee.

  • Where Via Rocca 2, Lonato del Garda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.fondazioneugodacomo.it

Sources: fondazioneugodacomo.it · in-lombardia.it · visitbrescia.it · comune.lonato.bs.it

Museo civico ornitologico G.A. Carlotto

Museums · Themed

In the House of the Captain (Sale del Capitano) of the Rocchetta, exhibits the collection of the ornithologist Gustavo Adolfo Carlotto with over 700 birds. The entrance is included in the Rocca ticket.

  • Where Via Rocca 2, Lonato del Garda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Fornaci romane

History and architecture · Archaeology

Situated with six furnaces from the I-II century A.D. for bricks, tiles and tiles; two furnaces west of the complex remained in use until the fourteenth century. You can visit only the furnace "A" with a circular plan and in excellent condition, with a cooking top of about 6 meters in diameter above the underground combustion chamber; guided tours and educational workshops.

  • Where Via Mantova 54, Lonato del Garda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: visitbrescia.it · in-lombardia.it

Torre civica (Torre Maestra)

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

55 meters high, started in 1555 under Venice in the center of the Citadel; in the Venetian era it hosted the men's prison. In 1880 the metal dome was replaced by the medieval crenellated crown of Antonio Tagliaferri; it has three bells dedicated to the patrons and the mechanism of the clock is visible inside.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Lonato del Garda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Basilica di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Basilicas

An example of Baroque design by the Lonate Paolo Soratini, third reconstruction on the same site, with large dome and facade adorned with marble; inside frescoes, stuccos and shovels by Venetian artists such as Balestra, Cignaroli and Licino. Basilica minor since 1980 by concession of John Paul II.

  • Where Via Tarello, Lonato del Garda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

La Cittadella e la Sala del Consiglio

History and architecture · Squares and old town

The Cittadella is one of the oldest neighborhoods, born fortified inside the walls with two gates, including the Milanese gate to the north, and narrow and long blocks on the slope with paved pebbles. On the first floor of the Town Hall the Council Hall preserves the canvas of Andrea Celesti (1693, 262 x 735 cm) commissioned as a thank you for the end of the plague of 1630, with the Doge, the theological virtues and St. John the Baptist.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Lonato del Garda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Abbazia di Maguzzano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Benedictine Abbey of the late 9th century, burned by the Hungarians and devastated by the Visconti in 1339, united to Polirone in 1490 and rebuilt in 1491-1496 with Renaissance church and monastery; in 1533 Cardinal Reginald Pole stayed there. Since 1938 he has been from the congregation of Don Giovanni Calabria and is a spiritual and ecumenical centre with a school of iconography. The museum in the cloister is free entrance with guided tour only, on Sundays at 16 or by reservation.

  • Where Via Maguzzano 4, Lonato del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · comune.lonato.bs.it

Spiaggia Lido di Lonato

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Public pebble beach about 300 meters long in the gulf of the low lake, between Desenzano and Padenghe, framed by Monte Corno and the hills of Valtenesi; not equipped, with free access at every hour, with parking at a charge of about 100 meters and dogs allowed on the leash.

  • Where Via Catullo 5, Lonato del Garda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: comune.lonato.bs.it · in-lombardia.it
