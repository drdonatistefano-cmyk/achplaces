What to see in Albiano

Country of the Valley of Cembra 18 km from Trento, on a morainic terrace above the Rio dell'Ischion, known in the world for the red porphyry extracted in its open-air quarries: the Museo Casa Porfido and the square of San Biagio, paved with porphyry between the old church of the twelfth century and the new parish.

5 places to visit in Albiano, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Casa Porfido

Museums · Themed

A small modern museum dedicated to the extraction of porphyry, the resistant stone used in urban furnishings: with multimedia tools and an interactive itinerary, it tells the geology of the territory, the ancient history of porphyry and the socio-economic context of quarries. Visits only by reservation (tel. 0461 689799); for singles and small groups every first Friday of the month, I meet at 13:45.

  • Where Via Don Luigi Albasini 8, Albiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.casaporfido.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: casaporfido.it · visittrentino.info

Chiesa vecchia di San Biagio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Cited in a document of 1149, it is a late-Gothic building with trilobed windows and portal with acute sixth, Renaissance portico and Romanesque bell tower; inside remains of frescoes and a wooden Calvary of the eighteenth century. Next to it stands the new parish church, also dedicated to San Biagio and consecrated in 1935.

  • Where Piazza San Biagio, Albiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitpinecembra.it · visittrentino.info

Piazza di San Biagio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Between the two churches of the village, it was paved in 1975 with the local porphyry and is a permanent exhibition of the machining of the porphyry of the Val di Cembra.

  • Where Piazza San Biagio, Albiano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Chiesetta di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Lonely chapel of the seventeenth century at the entrance of the village, with paintings and frescoes dedicated to the saint, made as thanksgiving for the received.

  • Where Ingresso del paese, Albiano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitpinecembra.it · visittrentino.info

Trekking delle cave di porfido

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Easy walk, especially on paths and forest roads in the woods above the village, which shows the numerous open-air porphyry quarries and the sign of man on the landscape; a family variant starts from the Museo Casa Porfido and crosses the upper part of the village between porphyry, chestnuts and deer of the wildlife park.

  • Where Albiano (parte alta del paese)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · visitpinecembra.it
