What to see in Maissana

Municipality of the high Val di Vara known for the Lagora Valley, ancient prehistoric open-air jasper quarry, reachable from the hamlet of Santa Maria, where between the IV and the II millennium BC the stone was extracted for the tools splintered; in the hamlet of Tavarone stands a parish attested already in 1143.

Cava preistorica di diaspro della Valle Lagorara

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry

Open-air jasper mine discovered in 1987 and excavated by the Superintendence between 1988 and 1995, exploited between the 4th and 2nd millennium B.C. (Radiocarbon indicates about 2920-1890 B.C.): the quarryers were made semi-finished for tools in splintered stone, especially arrowheads. The archaeological and naturalistic path shows the extraction surfaces as they were left, two quarry areas and two shelters with splinters, percussors and fragments of ceramic. Tel. 0187 845617.

visit only by appointment with reservation required according to the MiC

  • Where Loc. Valle Lagorara, Santa Maria di Maissana
  • Entry €6, reduced €5, schools €3
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.comune.maissana.sp.it

Sources: comune.maissana.sp.it · cultura.gov.it · lamialiguria.it

Chiesa di San Bartolomeo a Tavarone

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish, then dedicated to St. Sirus and belonging to the diocese of Brugnato, is already attested in 1143 and appears in the Eximum of the churches of the bishopric of Brugnato in 1452. Joined in San Pietro in Frascati in 1498, it passed to the diocese of Genoa in 1519 and was reconstituted parish in 1715 by Cardinal Lorenzo Fieschi; since 1892 it was of the diocese of Chiavari and since 1959 of Spezia. N. S. of the Belt on the second Sunday of July; festive Mass at 11.

  • Where Via Mario Padovani 13, Tavarone, Maissana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lamialiguria.it

Sources: diocesilaspezia.it · lamialiguria.it

Chiesa di San Bartolomeo Apostolo a Maissana

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish already existing before 1225, which became a prevostura in 1823, vicariate in 1838 and archpretura in 1923; on October 11, 1959 it passed from the diocese of Chiavari to that of Spezia. St. Bartholomew is celebrated on 24 August and Our Lady of Carmine is celebrated on 16 July.

  • Where Via dell'Oratorio 24A, Maissana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page lamialiguria.it

Sources: diocesilaspezia.it · lamialiguria.it
