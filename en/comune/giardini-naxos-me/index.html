What to see in Giardini-Naxos

On the bay under Taormina, near Capo Schisò, the Greek colony of Naxos arose in the 8th century BC: today you can visit the archaeological area and the museum, next to the medieval castle of Schisò, the Nike of Mendola and the beaches of Recanati, Schisò and Saia.

Area archeologica di Naxos

History and architecture · Archaeology · Paid entry · Viewpoint

At Capo Schisò was founded in the 8th century BC the Greek colony of Naxos, whose park preserves the remains of the Olympic Temple and the Archaic City. From the garden of the museum, crossed by a stretch of the ancient walls, starts the route in the ancient urban area along a rural road and then the plateau B. The Archaeological Park, established in 2007, also manages the ancient Theatre of Taormina and since 2019 the Castle of Schisò. The ticket (museum and archaeological area) is also purchased online; open every day 9-19.45, last entry 19.

  • Where Via Lungomare Schisò, Giardini-Naxos
  • Entry €6, reduced €3
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Museo archeologico di Naxos

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

It rises on Capo Schisò and tells about the Greek colony and the prehistory of the site with materials coming mainly from the excavations begun in 1953. On the ground floor there is the neolithic cup of Stentinello, the Corinthian and Euboic ceramics of the first colony and the masquerade antefixes of silenum, typical production of the city (late VI-V century BC); on the upper floor are the sacred areas, the archaic and classical city, the necropolis and a numismatic toilet. To see the arula with sphinxes faced (530 BC) called "Heidelberg-Naxos" rebuilt in 1997; in the sixteenth-century tower adjacent to the stone and lead anchors recovered in the bay. Open every day 9-19.45.

Nike di Kalkis

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Bronze statue created in 1965 by the sculptor Carmelo Mendola, inspired by ancient models, in the district of Schisò: it celebrates the bond with the Greek city of Calcide in Eubea.
