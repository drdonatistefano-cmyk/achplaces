What to see in Tuoro sul Trasimeno

Borgo on the north shore of Trasimeno, the theatre of the battle of 217 BC between Hannibal and Rome: the museum and the historical path of the battle, the columns of serene stone of the Campo del Sole at Punta Navaccia and, ten minutes by boat, the Isola Maggiore with the fishing village and the Museum of Lace.

10 places to visit in Tuoro sul Trasimeno, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Annibale al Trasimeno – Centro di documentazione

Museums · Themed

Didactic Museum on the Battle of Trasimeno of 217 BC, in which the Roman army was defeated by the Carthaginian troops of Hannibal: interactive stations, multimedia and a 4D cinema room with an eight-minute video reconstruction in 3D graphics and multisensory effects.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi 7, Tuoro sul Trasimeno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Campo del Sole

Museums · Modern art · Viewpoint

Outdoor museum in Punta Navaccia, designed by Pietro Cascella: a spiral of 27 columns-sculpture in local stone, about 4.5 meters high, leading to a central table with a solar symbol; the works were realized between 1985 and 1989 by Italian and foreign artists, including Mauro Staccioli, Joe Tilson and Leo Lionni.

  • Where Punta Navaccia, Tuoro sul Trasimeno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Isola Maggiore

History and architecture · Villages · Viewpoint

At about ten minutes by boat from Punta Navaccia: an old fishing village inhabited today by about ten residents, with a single street paved between buildings of the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries, the church of San Salvatore with a polyptych of Sano di Pietro and the neo-Gothic Castle Guglielmi, rebuilt at the end of the nineteenth century on the remains of a Franciscan convent; according to tradition St. Francis spent a Lent there.

  • Where Isola Maggiore, Tuoro sul Trasimeno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Museo del Merletto

Museums · Themed

In the Palazzo delle Opere Pie dell'Isola Maggiore he exhibits embroidery from the early twentieth century of the school founded by Elena Guglielmi, daughter of Senator Giacinto Guglielmi, where the fishermen's daughters learned the "Irish Point" in crochet; the school had international recognitions.

  • Where Via Guglielmi 25, Isola Maggiore, Tuoro sul Trasimeno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

  • Where Isola Maggiore, Tuoro sul Trasimeno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Maddalena

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Patronal church of the village, built in the fourteenth century and enlarged between 1885 and 1898, with a dome of 23 meters and in the apse a fresco by Gerardo Doctors of 1949.

  • Where Centro storico, Tuoro sul Trasimeno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Percorso storico della battaglia di Annibale

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Itinerary of about 17 km, of low difficulty, starting from Piazza Garibaldi: follows the main phases of the battle with panels and reconstructions and touches the ustrinae, the pits where the fallen were believed to be burned.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Tuoro sul Trasimeno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Palazzo del Capra

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

A fifteenth-century building just outside the center, built on Roman remains that legend binds to the tomb of the console Flaminio; it preserves a painting from the ancient Hospital of Santa Maria della Misericordia, attributed to the Pinturicchio or Perugino.

  • Where Fuori dal centro, Tuoro sul Trasimeno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Torre pendente di Vernazzano

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Remains of a medieval castle, sloped about 13 degrees and supported by pulls, reachable by a path in the woods about 20 minutes from the hamlet of Vernazzano, about 5 km from the center; view of the lake.

  • Where Vernazzano, Tuoro sul Trasimeno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · italia.it

Lido di Tuoro a Punta Navaccia

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Beach on the lake with tourist services, about half an hour walk from the center, boarding point for the Isola Maggiore.

  • Where Punta Navaccia, Tuoro sul Trasimeno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
