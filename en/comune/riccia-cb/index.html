What to see in Riccia

Borgo del Molise on the valley of Fortore, gathered around the Plan of the Court with the castle of Princes De Capua, the cylindrical tower symbol of the village, the church of Santa Maria delle Grazie and the Magazzeno with the Museum of Arts and Popular Traditions; outside the village the Bosco Mazzocca, oak of about 400 hectares.

Chiesa di Santa Maria delle Grazie (del Beato Stefano)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The church was restored in the fourteenth century by Roberto de Capua and transformed in the sixteenth century by Bartolomeo III de Capua (on the façade date 1500), who enlarged the nave, frescoed the walls and gave it the current title; among the designers mentioned Giovanni Donadio called Mormando and Francesco di Giorgio Martini. Facade in stone with doric parasts, oculus and coat of arms of Bartolomeo and Aurelia Orsini; next to the main altar four sarcophagi of the ancestors De Capua, and in the tomb of Andrea rest also the remains of Queen Constance of Chiaromonte.

  • Where Piano della Corte, Riccia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Castello dei De Capua e Torre

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Castle of probable Longobard origin, modified by the De Capua from the thirteenth century and transformed between 1510 and 1515 by Bartolomeo III in fortress with palace: the portal bears the date 1515 and the Latin motto "If guest, login. Flee, if enemy The cylindrical tower is almost 20 meters high, with crowning of stone bushes, three inner rooms, iron staircase and cistern dug into the rock, on a cliff near the stream Succida that dominates the valley of Fortore.

  • Where Piano della Corte, Riccia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: visitmolise.eu

Magazzeno e Museo delle Arti e delle Tradizioni popolari

Museums · Themed

Building at the highest point of the Court floor, in front of Santa Maria delle Grazie: lower floor in cutting stone with two lower arches, upper floor in bricks with four openings in all sixth and external staircase; perhaps storage of grain, oven, stable and seat of the Parliament. Today it houses the Museum of Arts and Popular Traditions with over 3000 objects of local material culture.

  • Where Piano della Corte, Riccia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: visitmolise.eu

Centro storico e Piazza Sedati

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Historic centre with Renaissance buildings around Piazza Sedati, a 17th century fountain linked to the De Capua and a watchtower at the entrance of the centre, now home to the Town Hall; the church of Santa Maria Assunta has a portal from the 14th century. The Sunday market is held in Piazza Umberto I.

  • Where Piazza Sedati, Riccia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: visitmolise.eu

Bosco Mazzocca e Bosco delle Fiabe

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Oak of caducifoglio of about 400 hectares, Site of Community Importance, frequented for excursions and picnics; inside the Wood of the Fairy tales is a didactic itinerary that tells legends and games of the local folk tradition.

  • Where Bosco Mazzocca, Riccia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: visitmolise.eu · italia.it
