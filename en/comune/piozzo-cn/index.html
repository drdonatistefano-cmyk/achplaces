What to see in Piozzo

Agricultural village between the plain and the Langhe, on the Tanaro, known as the "city of pumpkin" for the fair of the first Sunday of October and home since 1996 of the Baladin brewery; to see the chapel of San Bernardo with frescoes of 1451, the sanctuaries of the Alba Rosa and Madonna della Mirra and the square that recalls the retaliation of 5 July 1944.

6 places to visit in Piozzo, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cappella di San Bernardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

14th century brick chapel with round apse, built on the base of an 11th century watchtower. The cycle of interior frescoes is dated 1451 and signed .Frater Enricus Pinxit: in the Gothic apse the Christ Judge in almond between angels and symbols of the Evangelists, on the walls scenes of the legend of the Pilgrim of Compostela and St. James Major, in memory of the pilgrimage route called Roa Marenca. You can visit independently by booking date and time with the app "Churches with open doors" and by framing the QR code at the entrance.

Piazza 5 luglio 1944

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The main square was created by the filling of the ancient fortress, which began in the first half of the nineteenth century and continued for over a century; it faces the "cappellone" of the Confraternita della Misericordia, started in 1837 on a project by Nicolao Pellegrini, with the external fresco of the Madonna del Carmelo. The name recalls the Nazi-fascist retaliation of July 5, 1944, when Attilio Biamonti was shot on the square and the fire destroyed the upper part of the village, a wing of the castle and the town hall with its archive.

  • Where Piazza 5 luglio 1944, Piozzo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: comune.piozzo.cn.it · visitcuneese.it

Chiese dei Battuti Bianchi e dei Battuti Neri

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the center there are sacred places of the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, including the churches of the confraternities of the Battuti Bianchi (San Bernardino) and the Battuti Neri (San Giovanni Decollato). On July 5, 1944 the bell tower of the Black Battuti was targeted by German machine guns; on the church of San Giovanni the recent restorations brought to light a fascist writing painted on the wall.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · comune.piozzo.cn.it

Birrificio Baladin

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

Baladin beer has been produced in Piozzo since 1996, founded by Teo Musso: the pub overlooks the main square and the production premises are visited with guided tours.
