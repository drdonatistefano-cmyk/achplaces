What to see in Brusson

Sunny location of the Val d'Ayas between woods and pastures, with the gold mines active until the 1980s and the castle of Grains.

3 places to visit in Brusson, in the province of Aosta Valley (Aosta Valley). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Brusson in the guide with map and filters

Miniere d'oro di Chamousira

Museums · Themed

Discovered in 1899 and productive until the end of the eighties; a panoramic structure on the Val d'Ayas houses the museum and allows you to discover the underground world of the mine.

  • Where Chamousira, Brusson
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · balteus.lovevda.it · italia.it

Castello di Graines

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Built in the 11th century on a spur overlooking the town to control the valley, it became a symbol of the domination of the Challants; it can be visited freely within the walls.

  • Where Graines, Brusson
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it · italia.it

Col di Joux

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Panoramic pass reached by the road that from Brusson connects the Val d'Ayas to Saint-Vincent.

  • Where Brusson
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.lovevda.it

Sources: lovevda.it
