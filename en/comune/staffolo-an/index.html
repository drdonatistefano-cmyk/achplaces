What to see in Staffolo

A walled village about 400 meters in the valley of the Esino, called "Balcony of the Vallesina" and "Colle del Verdicchio": the Museum of the art of wine with the municipal wine shop, the walls with Porta Venezia and the churches of Sant'Egidio and San Francesco.

5 places to visit in Staffolo, in the province of Ancona (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo dell'arte del vino ed Enoteca comunale

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In the basements of the former Hospital of the Infirms, built in the 15th century, it follows the cycle of wine from the harvest to bottling with presses, vats, barrels and crushers; the most important piece is the oak press called "frisculu" in 1695. In the rooms next to the wine shop with historic bottles and wines of the territory: tasting for a fee, by reservation.

  • Where Via G. Marconi, Staffolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Mura, Porta Venezia e torrione dell'Albornoz

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

The walls preserve the original shape; you enter from Porta Venezia, whose name recalls the twinning of 1963 with Venice linked to Maria Manuzio, descendant of the family of printers, and along the belt there is a semicircular tower called "del'Albornoz."

  • Where Porta Venezia, Staffolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Centro storico e belvedere sulla Vallesina

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The hilly village, about 400 meters away, is called "Balcony of Vallesina" for its panoramic position on the Verdicchio vineyards.

  • Entry Free
