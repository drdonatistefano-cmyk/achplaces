What to see in Moresco

Borgo stop among the most beautiful villages in Italy, with a triangular plan on a hill above Valdaso, entirely surrounded by walls and marked by the Epthagonal tower of the twelfth century and the fourteenth-century Clock Tower; in the Town Hall the gallery with the great altarpiece of Vincenzo Pagani.

Torre eptagonale

History and architecture · Towers · Viewpoint

The 12th-century 25-metre-high tower, at the top of the triangular village, once a lookout and defense time; the Arabic-style cusp collapsed in 1918 and was replaced by a Ghibelline merlature. In summer it hosts art and photography exhibitions.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Torre dell'Orologio

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Tower of the 14th century at the other end of the village compared to the eptagonal tower, above the ancient gate of the castle, flanked by a loggia of the 16th century; its bell rings every day alternating with the large sixteenth century.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Pinacoteca «Patrizio Gennari»

Museums · Old masters

In the Town Hall, renovated in the early twentieth century, the small civic gallery collects works from churches and private collections; in the council room the large table of Vincenzo Pagani of 1529 with the Madonna in glory with the Child and the saints Lorenzo, Rocco, Sofia and Nicola of Bari, as well as a canvas with Santa Sofia.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Moresco
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Piazza Castello e portico di Santa Maria in Castro

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Of the demolished church of Santa Maria in Castro remains the left aisle, now the porch of the square, with a fresco of the Madonna with the Child of Vincenzo Pagani; from the village, entirely surrounded by walls, the look comes to the Conero and the Gran Sasso.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Moresco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria dell'Olmo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Outside the walls, enlarged in 1521 incorporating an ancient Gothic newsstand frescoed by Vincenzo Pagani with the Crucifixion, which separates the church individually into two; it has two altars. On the last Sunday of April there is a day out with lunch on the lawn.
