What to see in Cavalese

The capital of the Val di Fiemme and seat of the Magnificent Community, which for about a thousand years has managed the woods of the valley: its palace with a gallery and 19th century prisons, the parish church of the Assunta in the Parco della Pieve with the Banco de la Resòn and the Centre of Contemporary Art in a historic palace in the centre.

Palazzo della Magnifica Comunità di Fiemme e Pinacoteca

Museums · Old masters

Cited from the end of the thirteenth century as the seat of the vicar of the prince bishop of Trento, who went up to the valley twice a year to administer justice; renovated in the sixteenth century by artists of the Buonconsiglio yard, prison since the beginning of the nineteenth century, purchased by the Magnifica Comunità in 1850 and reopened as a museum-pinacoteca on July 5, 2012. He painted paintings of the School of Fiemme (XVII-XIX century) in chronological order; in the nineteenth-century prisons the incisions of the prisoners remain. Guided tours every day, even theatricalized.

  • Where Piazza C. Battisti, Cavalese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website mcfiemme.eu

Sources: mcfiemme.eu · cultura.gov.it · visitfiemme.it · visittrentino.info

Centro d'arte contemporanea

Museums · Modern art · Paid entry

Establishment of the Municipality founded in 2001 for a permanent collection of contemporary art with an important section dedicated to Bruno Munari; since 2008 in the halls of a noble palace of the historic center (Firmian Palace according to the MiC), with temporary exhibitions in the Alpine territory between tradition and innovation and workshops. In 2026 open from Tuesday to Sunday in the afternoon.

  • Where Piazzetta Rizzoli 1, Cavalese
  • Entry €5, reduced €2,50; free under 18 years and with Fiemme Cembra Guest Card
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.artecavalese.it

Sources: artecavalese.it · visitfiemme.it · cultura.gov.it · visittrentino.info

Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Archpriest's church already existing before 1112, rebuilt in the fifteenth century in Romanesque-Gothic style and brought from three to four naves in 1610, divided by cylindrical and polygonal pillars; seventeenth-century bell tower by Giuseppe Alberti and atrium with coffered ceiling and frescoes by Antonio Longo. After the 2003 fire, the restoration brought back hidden paintings. Guided tours throughout the year with the Magnificent Community.

  • Where Parco della Pieve, Cavalese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · visitfiemme.it

Banco de la Resòn

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the highest point of the Parco della Pieve, two concentric rows of stone seats around a drilled circular bench: here the representatives of the Community, usually on May 1st to elect the Scario and August 15th, voting with marbles inserted in the forum. In the sixteenth century there were held trials for witchcraft: 28 condemned, 18 at the stake.

  • Where Parco della Pieve, Cavalese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · visitfiemme.it

Parco della Pieve

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest park in the Val di Fiemme, on a small hill just a few steps from the centre, with secular lindens around the parish church and views over the valley, Avisio and mountains; the ring tour is also available in electric wheelchairs (Montagna Senza Limiti project).

  • Where Parco della Pieve, Cavalese
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info · visitfiemme.it

Alpe Cermis

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

The cable car brings skiers and hikers up above Cavalese, with slopes for beginners and experts in winter and high altitude views in summer.

  • Where Alpe Cermis, Cavalese
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visitfiemme.it · visittrentino.info
