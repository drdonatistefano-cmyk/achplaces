What to see in Soave

Medieval village of the East Verona among the vineyards of the Soave, closed by the walls of the Scaligere with 24 crenellated towers and dominated by the Castle on Mount Tenda; along Via Roma line up the Palazzo di Giustizia of 1375, the Gothic-Venetian Palazzo Cavalli and the Palazzo del Capitano, seat of the Municipality, while outside the walls stands the sanctuary of the Bassnella, starting point of the walk in the Classic Soave.

Castello di Soave

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Fortress on Mount Tenda with origins from the early 10th century, at the time of the invasions of the anguar, restored and expanded by Cansignorio della Scala and passed to Venice in the 15th century. You enter from the drawbridge under the tower with the bas-relief of San Giorgio; three courtyards: the first, Venetian, with the remains of a small church with three apses perhaps of the tenth century, that of the Madonna with a fresco dated 1st June 1321 and the tallest with the Mastio, where in the restorations there were human bones. The Palazzo del Signore preserves the "Caminata" with its large fireplace, a frescoed Crucifixion of the thirteenth century and portraits of Mastino I, Cangrande, Dante, Taddea da Carrara and Cansignorio. Hours: 9-12 and 15-18 from April to 25 October, 9-12 and 14-16 in winter; closed on Mondays and with bad weather.

Mura scaligere

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The walls surround the entire ancient village, climbing up to the castle, and is marked by 24 crenellated towers "alla ghibellina" (Ghibelline).

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Palazzo di Giustizia

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Built in about four months, from April to July 1375, under Cansignorio della Scala; twenty-two countries of the Capitanate of Soave contributed to the expense and their names are in the great epigraph, the largest inscription of the Scala. Loggia with four portals with acute sixth, balcony with the Madonna and Child between the Saints Lorenzo and Giovanni Battista and inscription in Latin verses in Gothic characters.

  • Where Piazza dell'Antenna, Soave
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitverona.it · italia.it

Palazzo Cavalli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Gothic-Venice Palace built in 1411 by Nicolò Cavalli, captain of Soave, with trilobed windows with sharp arch, loggia and a Renaissance trifora with lizard and scorpion carved on the columns; the façade was frescoed by Giovanni Maria Falconetto with mythological scenes, today no longer visible. You only see it from the outside.

  • Where Piazza dell'Antenna, Soave
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitverona.it · italia.it

Via Roma, piazze e Palazzo del Capitano

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The axis of the historic center, via Roma and via Camuzzoni, brings together the Duomo, the Palazzo di Giustizia, Palazzo Cavalli and Palazzo Scaligero or the Captain, now the seat of the Town Hall; the squares of Foro Boario, the Horses and the Grani Market recall in the names the medieval commerces. Here in May is held the medieval festival of white wine and in September the Grape Festival.

  • Where Via Roma, Soave
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · www-old.veneto.eu

Duomo di San Lorenzo Martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Pieve documented since 1029, rebuilt in 1303 inside the walls, then demolished and rebuilt between 1744 and 1758 and enlarged in 1884 on a project by Angelo Gottardi. Neoclassical façade with ionic columns and steps in red of Verona; altarpiece of Francesco Morone, paintings of Domenico Morone, Farinati and Cignaroli, statue of the Redeemer of 1553, baptismal font of 1430 and organ of George Trice of 1889.

  • Where Via Chiesa 5, Soave
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitverona.it · italia.it

Santuario della Madonna della Bassanella

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In Borgo Bassano, the oldest area of Soave, outside the walls: church documented from 1098, linked to the apparition of the Virgin to a shepherd in the Ponsara Valley. After the cholera of 1836 (135 died in two months) it was demolished and rebuilt in Doric style on a project by engineer Zanella; on the ceiling the Assunta by Adolfo Mattielli. The façade looks at the lower Val Tramigna.

  • Where Borgo Bassano, Soave
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitverona.it · www-old.veneto.eu · italia.it

Chiesa di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The fifteenth-century chapel, probably erected during a pestilence on an ancient Roman necropolis outside the walls, enlarged in 1521; the altarpiece of San Rocco painted by Francesco Morone in 1529 was moved to the parish. In 1890 the abbot Gottardi overturned its orientation, preserving the bell tower with a dome in terracotta pine. Simple interior with tombs and altar of St. Anne.

  • Where Via Bassano 5, Soave
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitverona.it · italia.it

Passeggiata nel Soave Classico

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ring among the vineyards starting from the sanctuary of Bassnella: climb the road of the castle, walk around Mount Foscarino with views of the lower Valdalpone at the way and the Val Tramigna and the Verona plain on the return; the last stretch leads to the village of Fittà, with panoramic points. Italia.it offers it on foot or by bike, all year round, recommending September.

  • Where Borgo Bassano, Soave
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitverona.it · italia.it
