What to see in Linguaglossa

Country in lava stone on the north-eastern side of Mount Etna, starting point of the Mareneve road towards Piano Provenzana: the Mother Church of 1613 with the wooden choir of 1728, Sant'Egidio with the Gothic portal, San Francesco di Paola with a statue of Domenico Gagini, the ethnographic museum and the museum dedicated to Salvatore Incorpora.

8 places to visit in Linguaglossa, in the province of Catania (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa Madre di Santa Maria delle Grazie

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Started in 1613 in the largest square in the village, it has facade in lava and sandstone attributed to Filippo Vasta and interior with three naves, neoclassical in the naves and baroque in the transept. The wooden choir of 1728 was carved by Giuseppe Turriti, Gioacchino Girelli and Michele and Pietro Orlando; on the main altar a table by Salvatore Mignemi (1756), organ Locatelli of 1903 and works by Salvatore Incorpora, including the twelve apostles in terracotta.

  • Where Piazza Matrice, Linguaglossa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.linguaglossa.ct.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Sant'Egidio Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Perhaps the oldest church in the town, of medieval origin, was a mother church for about four centuries; here the feudal lords swore, from the Crisafi in 1392 to Orazio Bonanno in 1504. At the Latin cross, it has a Gothic portal on the transept and over 450 metres of frescoes rediscovered under the plaster; on the altar the sixteenth-century wooden statue of the patron, carried in procession every 1st September.

  • Where Via Sant'Egidio, Linguaglossa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.linguaglossa.ct.it · italia.it

Museo Francesco Messina - Salvatore Incorpora

Museums · Modern art

Museum dedicated to the Sicilian and Italian twentieth century, in the former Casa del Fascio: it collects artistic nativity scenes, paintings and sculptures of the Calabrian Salvatore Incorpora, whose work is linked to Guttuso, Migneco and Rijeka; it is also named after the sculptor Francesco Messina, baptized in the Mother Church in 1901.

  • Where Ex Casa del Fascio, Linguaglossa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitsicily.info · comune.linguaglossa.ct.it · italia.it

Piano Provenzana ed Etna Nord

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

At about 1,800 meters, you can reach from Linguaglossa with the Mareneve road: in winter ski resort (steps between 1,800 and 2,317 meters), in summer off-road buses to the base of summit craters (about 3,100 m) and trails to the Observatory of Pizzi Deneri (2,900 m), on the edge of the Valle del Leone, and to Monte Nero delle Concazze and the abyss of the eruption of 1923.

  • Where Piano Provenzana, Linguaglossa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info
