What to see in Pieve Torina

A village at 470 metres in the Sibillini Mountains National Park, feud of Camerino in the Middle Ages; in the capital the Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta with the art gallery and the former convent of Sant'Agostino with the Museo della Nostra Terra, while the Water Trail leads to the waterfalls of Sant'Angelo and the mill of Rijeka.

14 places to visit in Pieve Torina, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della Nostra Terra

Museums · Themed

Ethnographic-agricultural museum established in 1976 on the ground floor of the former convent of Sant'Agostino: 14 sections on 750 square meters with rebuilt environments (granary, cellar, workshop of the workshop and shoemaker, kitchen, room, school, tavern, weaving room), carts, large agricultural tools and tools for pastoral and hunting. The route has been renovated and includes immersive experiences.

  • Where Complesso Sant'Agostino, SP 209, Pieve Torina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · turismo.comune.pievetorina.mc.it

Mulino di Fiume

Museums · Themed

Medieval water mill in the hamlet of Rijeka, the detached seat of the Museum of Our Earth, still working with pond, closed and original waterfalls fed by the stream Sant'Angelo. For centuries it was one of the five mills of the territory, with a capacity of over 700 quintals of wheat per year; the route reconstructs the life and work of the miller.

  • Where Località Fiume, Pieve Torina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: turismo.comune.pievetorina.mc.it · letsmarche.it

Sentiero delle Acque

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Cycle path of about 4.3 km in the forest, gravel and earth, suitable for all ages and also accessible with wheelchairs and strollers. At the beginning there is the Kneipp route, opened in 2023 near the stream Sant'Angelo; along the waterfalls and ponds route, and at the end the watermill of Rijeka. Always open; access from the SP 96 with ample parking.

  • Where SP 96, appena fuori dal centro, Pieve Torina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: turismo.comune.pievetorina.mc.it · letsmarche.it

Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta e Pinacoteca

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Pieve dell'XI secolo, attested by a document of the bishop of Spoleto of 1067, with bell tower and polygonal apse on the main square. It houses the art gallery with works already in the church of San Giovanni, palaces from the destroyed churches of Pomarolo and San Teodora, a Madonna with Child, angels and saints of Giovanni Andrea De Magistris and a cycle of frescoes from the second half of the fourteenth century detached from the parish church, important for the origins of the chamber school.

  • Where Piazza Santa Maria Assunta, Pieve Torina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page letsmarche.it

Sources: turismo.comune.pievetorina.mc.it · letsmarche.it

Ponte Romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Roman bridge hidden in the woods above the hamlet of Rijeka, along an ancient branch of the Via Flaminia towards Camerino and the Adriatic; below is the Val Sant'Angelo, with hermitages, cults of the Lombard age and traces of the Bronze Age.
