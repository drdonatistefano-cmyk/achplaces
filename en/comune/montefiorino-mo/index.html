What to see in Montefiorino

The village of the Modena Apennines at 797 m, gathered around the Rocca dei Montecuccoli, which in summer 1944 was the seat of the Partisan Republic of Montefiorino and today houses the museum of the Resistance; in the territory the Romanesque parish church of Rubbiano, the parish church of Vitriola with tower houses and stretches of the Via Bibulca.

6 places to visit in Montefiorino, in the province of Modena (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Rocca di Montefiorino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Born around 1170 as a quadrangular tower guarding the lands of the Badia di Frassinoro, then a castle mast erected by the Montecuccoli in the thirteenth century; after 1429 it was the seat of an extensive podestria until 1797. The restored tower can be visited and at the top has a 360-degree panoramic balcony on the valleys of the Dolo and the Dragon and on the peaks of the Cimone and the Cusna.

  • Where Via Roma, Montefiorino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it

Museo della Repubblica partigiana e della Resistenza italiana

Museums · City history

Inside the Fortress, the Republic of Montefiorino tells (18 June - 2 August 1944), so the country had the gold medal for military valor; re-established in 2015, it develops in nine rooms with weapons, uniforms, documents, daily objects, testimonies and multimedia projections. Open on weekends and holidays, 10-13 and 15-18.

  • Where Rocca di Montefiorino, Via Roma, Montefiorino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Pieve di Santa Maria Assunta di Rubbiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

One of the oldest churches in Modenese, probably built in the middle of the 7th century on the Via Bibulca, with a hospice for pilgrims; the Romanesque building of the 11th century, largely restored, has three naves and three semicircular apses and a holy waterworks with sirens of the Master of the Metops.

  • Where Rubbiano, Montefiorino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Vitriola: chiesa di Sant'Andrea e case-torri

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Borgo at 591 m on the left side of the Dragon, donated in 1071 by Beatrice di Canossa to the abbey of Frassinoro: the parish church of Sant'Andrea Apostolo, considered one of the hundred Matildi churches, has Romanesque façade and sculpted bezel of the twelfth century; in the locality Pignone case-forti thirteenth century, with stone towers houses even over 15 meters high.

  • Where Vitriola, Montefiorino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Centrale idroelettrica di Farneta e museo dell'energia

Museums · Themed

Central built in 1928 and still in operation, about 3 km from Ponte Dolo, fed by the waters of the basins of the Dolo and the Dragon: you can visit the original plants, one of the four turbine-alternator groups and the control panels.

  • Where Farneta, Montefiorino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Via Bibulca

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient Roman route that from the confluence between the Dolo and Dragon climbed to the Passo delle Radici; in the Middle Ages it connected Emilia and Tuscany and was wide enough for the wagons. They are still traversed in the municipalities of Montefiorino and Frassinoro.

  • Where Montefiorino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmodena.it

Sources: visitmodena.it
