What to see in Polcenigo

Village at the foot of the Carnic Alps where the Livenza and the Gorgazzo are born, with the eighteenth-century castle on the hill, the church of San Giacomo of 1262 and, on the border with Caneva, the UNESCO Palù di Livenza building site.

Sito palafitticolo del Palù di Livenza

History and architecture · Archaeology · UNESCO World Heritage

A large spring basin between Polcenigo and Caneva, at the foot of Cansiglio, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2011 among the prehistoric palafittic sites of the Alpine arch. Research since the 1980s has revealed a Neolithic village with huts on the banks and on small islets, dated with dendrochronology to about 4500-3500 BC; the finds are at the Archaeological Museum of Western Friuli in Torre di Pordenone. On site the tracks are poorly legible: italia.it recommends a guided tour; the area also applies to the natural environment.

Sorgenti del Gorgazzo e del Livenza

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

In Polcenigo the Livenza, which flows into the Adriatic, and its tributary Gorgazzo, with blue waters with turquoise reflections easily reachable from the center. The Gorgazzo is a perennial karst spring of Valchiusano type fed by the Cansiglio-Cavallo plateau: the submerged cave, explored by divers since 1964, reached in 2019 the -222 meters and is the deepest cave source in Italy. Dive is prohibited by a municipal order after several fatal accidents.

  • Where Località Gorgazzo, Polcenigo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · catastogrotte.regione.fvg.it

Castello di Polcenigo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

On the hill above the village, after a fire was rebuilt between 1738 and 1770 and transformed into a Venetian villa by the architect Matteo Lucchesi; it connects to the village a stone staircase of 365 steps. From troubled history, today it appears more like an eighteenth-century palace, not yet completely restored, and it is a panoramic destination for walking.

  • Where Collina del castello, Polcenigo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Chiesa di San Giacomo Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Halfway to the castle, it dates back to 1262 and was the church of a Franciscan monastery, among the first of Friuli. It contains a portal from the sixteenth century, frescoes fourteenth-century, the Nativity of the Virgin of Egidio Dall'Oglio (XVIII century), an eighteenth-century organ by Giacinto Pescetti, marble altars and eighteenth-century stables.

  • Where Salita al castello, Polcenigo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Centro storico, palazzi e chiese

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Narrow streets crossed by water and old mills, with Palazzo Fullini Zaia (XVI-XVII century) in Piazza Plebiscito, the Scolari-Salice and Zaro palaces, the sixteenth-century theatre born as the Theatre of the Counts of Polcenigo and the church of Santa Maria della Salute. Just outside the oratory of San Rocco (XIV century, rebuilt in the seventeenth century) and the churches of Ognissanti (1371), San Lorenzo (XIII century, with the altarpiece of the Trinity attributed to Tizianello) and San Giovanni (with paintings by Egidio Dall'Oglio). Each year in early September, for more than three centuries, the center hosts the fair of Thèst (cest).

  • Where Piazza Plebiscito, Polcenigo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Colle e chiesa di San Floriano

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Viewpoint

On the hill there is a park with a path to walk barefoot and a restaurant; on top the parish church of San Floriano, with square pronao and semicircular apse, contains frescoes from the end of the fourteenth-early fifteenth century, partly fragmentary, close to the school of Vitale da Bologna or Tommaso da Modena.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Museo dell'Arte Cucinaria dell'Alto Livenza

Museums · Themed

Permanent exhibition of kitchen utensils, diplomas, letters, photographs, recipes and menus of large banquets in hotels and embassies, belonged to cooks of yesterday and today trained in the Upper Livenza (Polcenigo, Budoia, Caneva, Fontanafredda) and immigrants to work in Italy and abroad. It is part of the regional Ecomuseum of the Friulian Dolomites. Visits by reservation (tel. 0434 749622, municipal library) and open during the main events.

  • Where Vicolo del Teatro 1, Polcenigo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it
