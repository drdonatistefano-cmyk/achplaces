What to see in Blera

Borgo della Maremma viterbese on a plateau of tuff along the Via Clodia, surrounded by Etruscan rock necropolis such as Pian del Vescovo and, towards Barbarano, the archaeological area of San Giovenale excavated by the Swedes since 1956; in the village the collegiate with the tomb of San Vivenzio and the Museum

Area archeologica e necropoli di San Giovenale

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Area brought to light after 1956 by the Swedish Institute of Classical Studies with King Gustavo of Sweden, next to the gorge of the river Vesca. A canopy protects the remains of Villanovian houses; then houses with tubs and fireplaces, hypogeal tombs similar to those of Tarquinia, to mound on the model of Cerveteri and rocks with nut and front of the house (VII-VI century BC). The most important tomb is Casale Vignale; the medieval chapel of San Giovenale and part of the castle of Di Vico remain.

  • Where Tra Blera e Barbarano Romano (S.S. Braccianense Claudia), Blera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · cultura.gov.it

Museo civico archeologico «Gustavo VI Adolfo di Svezia»

Museums · Archaeological

It exhibits Etruscan finds from excavations of the 1950s-1970s and about 600 objects from the rural world. The section "Il Cavallo e l'Uomo" opened in 2002 with audiovisual, dioramas and environmental sounds, tells the relationship between man and horse in the Lazio Maremma: prehistory and protohistory on the first floor, modern age and folklore of the Maremma and the Roman countryside on the lower floor and outside.

Necropoli di Pian del Vescovo

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

A village of tombs with dice dug in a hill of tuff, some reused in Roman times; among the most important burials the Tomb of the Sphinx.

  • Where Pian del Vescovo, Blera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Ponte della Rocca

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Roman bridge built with stones of the nearby necropolis; you can reach it with a path along the river Biedano and a long passage flanked by graves.

  • Where Valle del Biedano, Blera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Collegiata di Santa Maria Assunta in Cielo e San Vivenzio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque church on Via Roma with caves, a bas-relief with the Hunt of Adone under the main altar, paintings of the eighteenth century and a crypt with the tomb of the patron Saint Vivenzio, celebrated on 11 December.

  • Where Via Roma, Blera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Centro storico e Porta Marina

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Along Via Roma, Palazzo Lattanzi and the sixteenth-century Palazzo Pretoria, seat of the civic magistrates until the early twentieth century (visible only from the outside); of the ancient belt remain Porta Romana and the well-preserved Porta Marina; in the center also Palazzo Tornaforte, Palazzo Anguillara called Palazzaccio and the church of the Madonna delle Lacrime.

  • Where Via Roma, Blera
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
