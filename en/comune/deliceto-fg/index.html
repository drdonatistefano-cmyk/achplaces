What to see in Deliceto

Borgo dei Monti Dauni perched on a hill, a few kilometers from Bovino and Sant'Agata di Puglia, dominated by the Norman-Svevo Castle, a national monument since 1902. Between alleys and courtyards of the center, the convent of Sant'Antonio and the chapel of the Greek Rite; around oak woods such as the Bosco Macchione.

Castello di Deliceto

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Symbol of the village, built on the rock and inhabited by Norman lords, Swabians, Angevins and Aragonese, had mainly military function and was declared national monument in 1902. The most imposing part is the Torrione; the towers of the Pier and Parasinno, well preserved, are joined by a walk still accessible. Once surrounded by a moat with a drawbridge, it has a central courtyard with an octagonal cistern for rainwater; legend has it that in the dungeons there was hidden a shaving mill for thieves. Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the main course the visit continues between alleys and courts of the village perched on the hill, with Palazzo d'Ambrosio, Palazzo De Maio, the ancient Church of Purgatory and the Abbey of San Nicola; the Rione Pesco is a district of caves dug in the rock of the spur Elceto. In August the Day of the Black Pig, in February the Feast of St. Matthias with bonfires and singing all night long.

Convento e Chiesa di Sant'Antonio

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Former Franciscan monastery on a rocky step about 600 meters, wanted by the Marquis Piccolomini as a kind church. The Baroque church has three aisles and six chapels, a bronze door with the miracles of the saint, a wooden statue of St. Anthony in Spanish style with inlaid flowers on gold background and an organ of the late eighteenth century by Domenico Antonio Rossi, organist of the Royal Chapel of Naples. Left by the Franciscans with the suppression of Murat in 1811, it passed to the Redemptorists of the Consolation until the suppression of 1886.

Cappella della Madonna di Loreto (del Rito Greco)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Chapel of the sixteenth century just outside the walls, built at the request of the Piccolomini so that the Albanians could celebrate the Orthodox rite. Inside sober with a small altar and an icon of the Madonna with the Child; next stood a civic hospital for faithful and pilgrims, expanded in the seventeenth century by the canon Bartolomeo Ciardi and active until the second half of the nineteenth century, and under the road an ancient ossuary with the victims of an epidemic of the sixteenth century.

Bosco Macchione

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Wood of centuries-old oaks, oaks and oaks between 600 and 900 meters, a few kilometers from the village, inhabited by squirrels, badgers, fairies, wild boars and birds like hawks, peaks and buzzards. Old mule tracks lead to an ancient farmhouse, shelter of shepherds and then of the forestkeepers, today a multifunctional classroom; picnic area, excursions and guided tours.
