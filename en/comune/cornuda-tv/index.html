What to see in Cornuda

At the foot of the Asolani hills, Cornuda houses the Italian mortgage, museum and typographical culture center; on the hill of the Fortress the Sanctuary of the Madonna della Rocca and the ossuary of the fallen of 1848, not far from the Romanesque church of the Saints Vittore and Corona.

5 places to visit in Cornuda, in the province of Treviso (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Tipoteca Italiana

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Museum and cultural center of printing and printing managed by the Italian mortgage Foundation, with museum, gallery, archive, library, print shop in mobile characters and auditoriums; the catalog includes characters, machines, books and printed books and stories of printers, and organize workshops of printing and letterpress printing. Open from Monday to Saturday 9-13 and 14-18, closed on Sundays and holidays; free under 8 years and for disabled.

  • Where Via Canapificio 3, Cornuda
  • Entry €7, reduced €5
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.tipoteca.it

Sources: tipoteca.it · comune.cornuda.tv.it

Santuario della Madonna della Rocca

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

At 350 metres above sea level, next to the oak-tree site that the legend linked to the Holy Land (which collapsed on 18 June 2010); the nucleus is medieval, with additions of the late seventeenth century, and is entrusted to the Missionary Sisters of the Immaculate. The parishes of Cornuda, Coste, Covolo, Maser, Nogarè and Onigo go on pilgrimage every year.

  • Where Colle della Rocca, Cornuda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cornuda.tv.it

Sources: comune.cornuda.tv.it

Chiesa dei Santi Vittore e Corona

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient Romanesque church near the Fortress, along the Ru Nero, where in the twelfth century the bodies of the two saints were transferred.

  • Where Lungo il Ru Nero, Cornuda
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cornuda.tv.it

Sources: comune.cornuda.tv.it

Monumento-Ossario ai Caduti del 1848

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the hill of the Fortress, work of A. Carlini inaugurated in 1898, recalls the clash of 8 and 9 May 1848 between Austrian troops and the Pontifical Dragons.

  • Where Colle della Rocca, Cornuda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cornuda.tv.it

Sources: comune.cornuda.tv.it

Monumento ai Caduti e Municipio

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Building inaugurated in 1930 as a memorial of the fallen of the First World War and used as a municipal asylum, then decorated with panels dedicated to the fallen of all wars; since 1968 it is home to the Town Hall.

  • Where Cornuda
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cornuda.tv.it

Sources: comune.cornuda.tv.it
