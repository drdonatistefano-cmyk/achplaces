What to see in Roccalumera

Country of origin of the family of Salvatore Quasimodo, autonomous municipality since 1816: the Literary Park dedicated to him occupies the ancient railway station with vintage cars, and the fifteenth-century Saracen Tower, sung by the poet, hosts a permanent exhibition; in the church of the district Botteghelle the poet was baptized.

Parco Letterario Salvatore Quasimodo

Museums · House museums

It is located in the former FS station and in vintage freight wagons, with the Quasimodiano Museum, the Train-Museum and the Garden-Museum-A. Quasimodo: he holds documents, relics, writings, audiovisual materials and the Milanese study of the poet, Nobel Prize in literature in 1959.

visits only by reservation required (tel. 347 4086661)

Torre Saracena

History and architecture · Towers

The fifteenth-century tower, perhaps on a defensive structure of Arabic age, linked to the Rocca family that named the village; in the eighteenth century it was a stop of the Grand Tour, after 1830 it had two high-pitched windows and battlements. Restored in 2000 and managed by the volunteers of the Literary Park, since 2023 it hosts a permanent exhibition on Quasimodo, which dedicated the poem "Near a Saracen tower, for his dead brother" (1956), and during the year contemporary art exhibitions.

visits only by reservation required through the Literary Park

Chiesa di Santa Maria del Carmelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1938 in the Botteghelle district, behind a small church documented in 1725 and now disappeared, where Salvatore Quasimodo was baptized by Francesco Maria di Francia; he preserves an ancient statue of the Madonna del Carmelo, a Saint Joseph and a canvas of the Madonna with the souls of Purgatory. On July 16th the procession, and the following Sunday the statue goes by boat on the sea of Roccalumera and Nice.

Chiesa di Santa Maria della Catena

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Completed in 1893 by the inhabitants of the districts Baglio and Ficarra and enlarged in 1937, it has Latin cross plan, coffered ceiling with rosettes and artistic windows; on the main altar dominates the nineteenth century effigy of the Madonna della Catena, carried in procession on the first Sunday of September.

Chiesa dei Santi Cosma e Damiano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Baroque church with a single nave, probably from the seventeenth century (attested in 1674), in a dominant position in the center of the ancient village of Sciglio on the homonymous stream; originally dedicated to the Most Holy Savior, from the eighteenth century venerates the medical saints Cosma and Damiano. Portale in pietra rosacea del 1758, bell tower of the twentieth century, monumental altar of the sixties.

access on request (tel. 0942 744950)
