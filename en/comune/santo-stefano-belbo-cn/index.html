What to see in Santo Stefano Belbo

Cesare Pavese's birthplace (1908), on the Belbo torrent at the border between the provinces of Cuneo and Asti, in the heart of the Moscato area and setting of the moon and the bonfires; to see the Museum of the Fondazione Cesare Pavese, the birthplace, the shop-house of Nuto, the medieval tower and the literary benches.

Museo della Fondazione Cesare Pavese

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

The museum of the Cesare Pavese Foundation preserves the writer's legacy; next door, the former church of Saints James and Christopher, where Pavese was baptized, hosts temporary exhibitions. Five large paintings by Ernesto Treccani dedicated to "The moon and the bonfires." Open every day except on holidays 10-12.30 and 14-18; guided tours on Saturdays at 10, 14 and 16 (about 1 hour 45, with Nuto's house and stop at the home).

from 13 December 2026 to 21 March 2027 closed on Sunday, guided tours only on Saturday

  • Where Piazza Luigi Ciriotti 1, Santo Stefano Belbo
  • Entry €10; guided tour €17; reduced groups and schools €8 (guided €15), university €7 (guided €12); free under 12
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website fondazionecesarepavese.it

Casa natale di Cesare Pavese

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The house where Pavese was born on 9 September 1908 was the summer residence of the parents; sold in 1916 after the death of his father, it does not preserve original furnishings and is private property. Behind it is the hill of Gaminella, opposite that of Salto, and from the house you can see Moncucco, the hill of poetry "I mari del Sud" . It is only seen from the outside, alone or with the guided tour of the Foundation.

private property visible only from outside according to the Foundation; the MiC card (agg. 2021) indicates closed every day

  • Where Via Cesare Pavese 20, Santo Stefano Belbo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondazionecesarepavese.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Casa-bottega di Nuto

Museums · House museums

On the road to Canelli, at the foot of the hill of Salto, is the house-bottega of Pinolo Scaglione, carpenter and musician friend of Pavese, who guided him through the paths and vineyards of the Belbo valley telling him the local stories: in the novel "The moon and the bonfires" became the character of Nuto. After his death the house became a museum.

Visitable with guided tours of the Foundation (Saturday; groups and schools by reservation in the weekdays); the MiC card (Add. 2021) indicates closed every day

  • Where Località Piana, Santo Stefano Belbo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondazionecesarepavese.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Torre medievale

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Above the historic centre remain part of the tower of the ancient medieval castle and traces of the oldest defensive structures; it is reached on foot and is among the Pavesian places signalled by the Foundation.

  • Where Centro storico, Santo Stefano Belbo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondazionecesarepavese.it

Panchine letterarie

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

Book-shaped benches scattered throughout the country and surroundings feature pieces from Pavese's best-known works.

  • Where Santo Stefano Belbo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Collina del Salto

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Along the road to Canelli, beyond the Belbo and in front of Gaminella, is the harshest and most arid hill of the two that act as the scenery to the moon and the bonfires; Pavese describes it with its ridges and large meadows disappearing on the peaks. At his feet the house-boat of Nuto.

  • Where Strada per Canelli, Santo Stefano Belbo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondazionecesarepavese.it · italia.it
