What to see in Como

Water town on the first basin of the Lario: the Duomo with the dome of Juvarra and the Broletto, the Romanesque of San Fedele and Sant'Abbondio, the Voltian Temple, the House of the Fascio of Terragni and the walk of the villas to Villa Olmo.

17 places to visit in Como, in the province of Como (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The Late Dome with the dome of Filippo Juvarra, the heart of the city next to the imposing Broletto.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
