What to see in San Cesario di Lecce

Small center of the Valle della Cupa about five kilometers from Lecce, with the square where the church of Santa Maria delle Grazie faces the Baroque Doge's Palace of 1626, seat of the Town Hall and the Civic Museum; in an alley near the church of San Giovanni Evangelista of 1329 and, in Via Cerundolo, the house-museum of the artist Ezekiel Leandro with the Sanctuary of Patience.

Palazzo Ducale e Museo civico

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

In the centre of the village, the palace belonged to the Marulli dukes has a spectacular Baroque façade of 1626 decorated with statues and busts, about 25 meters high, with baroque balconies; in the inner courtyard a Renaissance well. It is the seat of the Town Hall and houses in the attic the Civic Museum of Contemporary Art. On the same square stands the church of Santa Maria delle Grazie, begun in 1623.

Chiesa di San Giovanni Evangelista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest monument in the village, dated 1329, is located in an alley on the right of the nineteenth-century parish; it preserves a Christological cycle and a universal Judgment painted by Byzantine masters.

Casa-Museo Ezechiele Leandro e Santuario della Pazienza

Museums · House museums

He collects paintings and sculptures by the Salentine artist Ezekiel Leandro (1905-1981), set up by himself in the garden of his home: in addition to the paintings, the Sanctuary of Patience, inspired by the sacred iconography, with statuary groups in stone, concrete and recovery materials.
