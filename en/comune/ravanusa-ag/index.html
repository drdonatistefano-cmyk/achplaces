What to see in Ravanusa

Country of Agrigentino at the foot of Monte Saraceno, an ancient town inhabited by prehistoric times at the third century BC, now an archaeological area of the Valley of the Temples; the excavations are exhibited in the archaeological museum Salvatore Lauricella, in the historical center.

Museo archeologico Salvatore Lauricella

Museums · Archaeological

Inaugurated in 2007 by the Municipality with the Superintendence of Agrigento and set up with the University of Messina, it is named after Salvatore Lauricella, mayor of Ravanusa for fourteen mandates between 1946 and 1990. The two halls exhibit the excavations carried out on Mount Saraceno from the twenties of the twentieth century to the beginning of the thirteenth century, from prehistory to the third century BC, ordered for places of worship, inhabited, fortifications and necropolis: polychrome architectural terracottas with a gorgoneion, painted arules, amphorae, loom weights, a turtle-shaped askos and a piss of 520-460 BC from the set of a child sarcophagus of the necropolis of Via Olimpica, with a satire clinging to a donkey.

  • Where Corso della Repubblica 27, Ravanusa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it

Area archeologica e belvedere di Monte Saraceno

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Ancient Greek-siculous settlement on the hill overlooking the village, excavated from the twenties of the twentieth century to the beginning of the thirteenth century, with places of worship, housing districts, fortifications and necropolis from prehistory to the third century BC; among the remains the village, the entrance of a cave and an ancient road. The municipality also points it out as a viewpoint with a naturalistic path and panoramic views of the village and the countryside. Managed by the Archaeological Park of the Valley of the Temples, with free entrance.

  • Where Monte Saraceno, Ravanusa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it

Sources: parchiarcheologici.regione.sicilia.it · comune.ravanusa.ag.it
