What to see in Cessapalombo

The town of Maceratese on the edge of the National Park of the Sibillini Mountains, of medieval origin, largely destroyed by the earthquake of 1799 and rebuilt in several hamlets: the abbey of San Salvatore in Monastero, the castle of Montalto with its three walls, the Madonna dell'Impollata in Sant'Andrea, the Carbonaia Museum and the Butterfly Garden.

Abbazia di San Salvatore di Rio Sacro (Santa Maria in Insula)

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Abbey in isolated position downstream of the hamlet Monastero, probably founded in 1009 according to a writing by Saint Pier Damiani, on the remains of a Roman villa. In the crypt there is a fresco of the Madonna with the Child in the thirteenth century; in the porch of the upper church there is a St. George who kills the dragon; in two niches frescoes attributed to Andrea De Magistris (the Virgin and the Magdalene, St. Lucia and St. Catherine). Open for the festive liturgy at 10:00.

Castello di Montalto e borgo di Tribbio

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Castle of the ninth century with three walls, to protect the lord, soldiers and the population; today remains imposing ruins of towers and walls in a very panoramic position. At his feet the village of Tribbio with Palazzo Simonelli and the former church of Santa Lucia, partly frescoed; not far from the church of San Benedetto. Montalto was the scene of the massacre of 32 young partisans by the Nazis.

  • Where Frazione Montalto, Cessapalombo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sibillini.net · turismo.comune.cessapalombo.mc.it

Chiesa di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church in Piazza Vittorio Emanuele, rebuilt after the earthquake of 1799, which preserves the fifteenth-century polychrome wooden statue of the Virgin with the Child called "Madonna dell'Impollata."

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele, Cessapalombo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.cessapalombo.mc.it · sibillini.net · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Ausiliatrice

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the locality of Villa di Montalto, it preserves the fresco of the "Mater Misericordiae" with knights of 1468, attributed to Giovanni Angelo d'Antonio da Bolognola.

  • Where Località Villa, Montalto, Cessapalombo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.cessapalombo.mc.it · italia.it

Museo della carbonaia (Centro visita Vallenatura)

Museums · Themed

In the visit centre Vallenatura, which is also House of the Park, the museum is dedicated to the art of the production of charcoal, a traditional craft with ancient secrets. In July and August open every day, holidays included, 9.30-12.30 and 15.30-18.30.

  • Where Contrada Fonte Girata 3, Cessapalombo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it

Giardino delle farfalle

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens

In Montalto, three exhibition and educational halls and a large garden that reproduces the natural habitat of butterflies, with wet areas; along the path panels describe the floral essences. Recommended for families.

  • Where Frazione Montalto, Cessapalombo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · italia.it
