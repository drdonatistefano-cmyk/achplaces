What to see in Romagnano Sesia

Borgo della Bassa Valsesia among vineyards and hazelnuts: on Monte Cucco the neoclassical Villa Caccia di Alessandro Antonelli, seat of the Ethnographic Historical Museum of the Lower Valsesia, and in the historic center the Cantina dei Santi, last authentic remnant of the millenary Benedictine abbey of San Silvano, with fifteenth century frescoes.

Museo storico etnografico della Bassa Valsesia

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Promoted by Maria Adriana Prolo, founder of the Museo del Cinema in Turin, and built in 1973 in the halls of Villa Caccia. It gathers, largely for the gift of citizens, peasants and artisans, the testimonies of the local agricultural and wine civilization: tools and agricultural machinery with a press about 13 meters long (with 3D reconstruction), the phases of the wine production and the cellar of the villa, the crafts with historical photos, and reconstructions of kitchen, room, school and tavern. Open from 15 March to 30 November, Saturday and Sunday 15-18.30; groups and schools by reservation (tel. 342 1631245).

  • Where Villa Caccia, Viale Antonelli 1, Romagnano Sesia
  • Entry €7.50, reduced €5.50, family €15
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismonovara.it

Sources: comune.romagnano-sesia.no.it · turismonovara.it

Villa Caccia e il suo parco

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Viewpoint

Monumental neoclassical villa designed by Alessandro Antonelli and built between 1842 and 1848 on the hill of Monte Cucco, surrounded by a remarkable park with many tree species. Owned by the municipality, it houses the Ethnographic Historical Museum of Lower Valsesia and is a cultural centre; it is reached from Viale Antonelli, with ample parking.

  • Where Corso Roma 62 (accesso da Viale Antonelli), Romagnano Sesia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismonovara.it

Sources: comune.romagnano-sesia.no.it · turismonovara.it

Abbazia di San Silvano - Cantina dei Santi

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

The only authentic remains of the ancient Benedictine abbey of San Silvano, in the center of the village near the parish church; cited as "Cantina dei Santi" in a document of 1777, has older origins and an eighteenth-century portico. One of the two rooms decorated contains frescoes from the second half of the fifteenth century: originally 28 squares, almost all still legible, with biblical stories, especially of the life of King David, attributed with uncertainty to Bartulonus da Novara.

  • Where Vicolo dell'Abbadia, Romagnano Sesia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismonovara.it

Sources: turismonovara.it
