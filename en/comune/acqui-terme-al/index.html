What to see in Acqui Terme

The thermal city of Alto Monferrato, already known to the Romans for its waters: the Bolente that flows about 75 degrees in the middle of the square, the Cathedral, the Palaeologists' Castle with the archaeological museum and the arches of the Roman aqueduct on the Bormida.

4 places to visit in Acqui Terme, in the province of Alessandria (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

La Bollente

Local life and traditions · Spas · Free entry

Thermal spring at the geographical centre of the city, which flows at 74.5 degrees; the water is protected by an octagonal aedicule inaugurated in 1879.

  • Where Piazza della Bollente, Acqui Terme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Mother church of the diocese, a few steps from the Bollente, with the main portal carved at the end of the fifteenth century by Giovanni Antonio Pilacorte; inside the Triptych of the Madonna of Montserrat by Bartolomé Bermejo.

  • Where Acqui Terme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Castello dei Paleologi – Museo Civico Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the medieval castle near the Cathedral, which also preserves the former Napoleonic prisons: sections of prehistory, Roman age (with the reconstruction of the ancient fountain of the Bollente, sculptures and stele) and late antique and medieval age.

  • Where Via Morelli 2, Acqui Terme
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: abbonamentomusei.it · fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Archi dell'acquedotto romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

Arcade in red bricks at the Charles Albert Bridge on Bormida, remains of an aqueduct about 12 km long that with 50 meters of difference in altitude brought the waters of Erro from Cartosio to the city.

  • Where Ponte Carlo Alberto, Acqui Terme
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it
