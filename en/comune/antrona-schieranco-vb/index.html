What to see in Antrona Schieranco

The municipality of the Upper Antrona Valley, in the Nature Park of the same name: Lake Antrona, born from the landslide of 1642, the Baroque parish of San Lorenzo rebuilt after the catastrophe, the basins of Campliccio and Cavalli under the Pizzo Andolla, the mountain pasture of Cheggio and the gold mine of the Taglione open to guided tours.

6 places to visit in Antrona Schieranco, in the province of Verbano-Cusio-Ossola (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Lago di Antrona e la cascata del Sajont

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Natural lake formed by the colossal landslide of July 27, 1642 detached from Mount Pozzuoli, which buried about forty houses and a hundred inhabitants and cleared the torrent Troncone; since 1926 it acts as a reservoir for the central of Rovesca. The Slow Trek from Antronapiana (about 6.5 km, 250 m of difference in altitude, 2 hours and a half walk, from May to October) passes through the stone village of Cimallegra, between the rockfalls of the landslide and on a walkway suspended inside the Sajont waterfall (not recommended with heavy rains or ice).

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The ancient parish church of 1200 was buried by the landslide of 1642; the new, expanding the oratory of San Rocco, was entrusted to the foreman Bartolomeo Tami and completed in 1685, with bell tower of 1656-1660. It preserves golden wooden altars of the Carvers Gualio (Guaglio), among which the ciborium of the high altar of 1686, considered among the most beautiful works of the Piedmontese Baroque, the baptismal font recovered from the rubble, the pulpit of sculptors of the Vigezzo (1720-21) and decorations of 1887 of painters of the Vigezzo Valley, including Bernardino Peretti.

Parco naturale dell'Alta Valle Antrona

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established in 2009, it extends for about 7,400 hectares between Antrona Schieranco and Borgomezzavalle, from 500 meters up to 3,656 of Pizzo Andolla, with the Val Troncone, the Val Banella and the high Val Loranco. It includes the rare natural barrier lake of Antrona and four artificial basins (Cingino, Campliccio, Camposecco and Alpe dei Cavalli) and borders the Swiss reserve of Laggintal.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Laghi di Campliccioli e dei Cavalli (Cheggio)

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Artificial basins of the high valley reachable by road: Camplicioli da Alpe Russi with a hairpin bend up to the dam, with a wide view of the valley; Lake Cavalli da Cheggio, about 6 km of challenging ascent from Antronapiana on the SP 141, framed by Pizzo Andolla and Weissmies (last dirt track). The descent road from Campliccioli is very narrow and with blind curves.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page comune.antronaschieranco.vb.it

Cheggio e l'oratorio di San Bernardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Large sunny alpine pasture north of Antronapiana, at the entrance of Val Loranco. At the beginning of the seventeenth century there was already a chapel dedicated to St. Bernard of Menton, protector of the Alpine people; the oratory was built around 1650 after the landslide, had the bell in 1661 and carved by Giulio Gaglio in 1702-1703. It was a destination for pilgrimages in droughts and a stop towards San Lorenzo di Bognanco.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.antronaschieranco.vb.it

Sources: comune.antronaschieranco.vb.it

Miniera d'oro del Taglione

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Ancient gold mine recovered and equipped for visits with the Interreg Italia-Switzerland Mineralp project, opened to the public since 2023 with guided tours managed by Geoexplora, to enter the mountain to discover ancient places and crafts. Dates and times on the mine site.
