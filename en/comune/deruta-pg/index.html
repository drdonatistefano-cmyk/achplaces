What to see in Deruta

The city of the majolica: the oldest ceramic museum in Italy in the former convent of San Francesco and the sanctuary of the Madonna dei Bagni covered with a former ceramic vote.

3 places to visit in Deruta, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers 4 of the 6 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Museo Regionale della Ceramica

Museums · Themed

In the former convent of San Francesco, the oldest Italian museum dedicated to ceramics, founded in 1898: majolica from origins to contemporary, a rebuilt spices, majolica floors and votive plates.

  • Where Via Francesco Briganti, Deruta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismo.comune.perugia.it

Sources: italia.it · turismo.comune.perugia.it

Santuario della Madonna dei Bagni

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

In Casalina, the church covered with a former ceramic vote from the second half of the seventeenth century to today, a unique documentation of the majolica derutese.

  • Where Casalina, Deruta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.comune.perugia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.perugia.it

Pinacoteca comunale di Deruta

Museums · Old masters

The civic collection of paintings of the village, part of the city's museum system next to the ceramic museum.

  • Where Deruta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.comune.perugia.it

Sources: turismo.comune.perugia.it
