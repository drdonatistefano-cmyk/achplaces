What to see in Canazei

The village of the upper Val di Fassa with the hamlets of Alba and Penia, at the foot of Marmolada, Sella and Gran Vernèl: from here you can climb to the Sass Pordoi, the "Terrazza delle Dolomiti" and to the Passo Fedaia; to Penia the Venetian sawmill La Sia, documented from the sixteenth century.

7 places to visit in Canazei, in the province of Trento (Trentino-South Tyrol). Every entry is checked against official sources.

La Sia - La segheria veneziana

Museums · Themed

Water sawmill documented since the 16th century, with a piston technique introduced in the valley by the Republic of Venice for the trade of timber; the current building is from 1922, work of carpenter Dellagiacoma \Giochelon of Predazzo, restored in 1985-1988 by the Ladino Cultural Institute. A segantino reached 30 tables a day of fir, larch and pine; it is still used by the inhabitants of the village and is owned by the ASBUC of Penia, heir of the ancient Rule.

  • Where Strèda de Ciamp Trujan 30, Penìa, Canazei
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.istladin.net

Sources: istladin.net · visittrentino.info · fassa.com

Museo della Grande Guerra al Passo Fedaia

Museums · City history

Created by Andrea De Bernardin at the foot of the Marmolada and then expanded, he tells the White War on the front of the Marmolada with well-preserved Italian and Austro-Hungarian relics, largely returned by the glacier in retreat; he has a bookstore on the war in the mountains and a room for vintage films.

  • Where Passo Fedaia, Canazei
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visittrentino.info

Museo 1915-1918 Dolomiten

Museums · City history · Paid entry

Opened in 2018 at the departure station of the cable car of Sass Pordoi and curated by the Historical Cultural Association Col di Lana: over 3,000 exhibits on 450 m2 on the war in the Dolomites between Col di Lana and Marmolada, with rebuilt barracks, trenches with machine guns, vintage photographs and objects made by soldiers with splinters, rifles and barbed wire.

  • Where Strèda de Pordoi, Passo Pordoi, Canazei
  • Entry €7, family €14, free up to 10 years
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: fassa.com · visittrentino.info

Sass Pordoi, la Terrazza delle Dolomiti

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

From Passo Pordoi (2,239 m), about 12 km and 28 hairpin bends from Canazei, the cable car climbs in a few minutes to 2,950 m; from the terrace you can see Marmolada, Sella with the Piz Boè, Sassolungo, Catinaccio, Tofane and Pelmo, and on clear days the Austrian and Swiss Alps. On top of a refuge, base for Forcella Pordoi and Piz Boè; the cable car opens from mid-May to early November and reopens before Christmas.

  • Where Passo Pordoi, Canazei
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.sasspordoi.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: sasspordoi.it · visittrentino.info · fassa.com

Passo Fedaia e lago

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Valico about 2,057 m at the foot of the Marmolada, with a plateau of about 2.5 km; the lake is an artificial basin of the mid-Twentieth century that collects the ice-clad waters for hydropower (not bathing), and at the foot of the dam in the fifties arose a laboratory for the study of cosmic rays. From Canazei about 20 minutes by car.

  • Where Passo Fedaia, Canazei
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Monumento a Fausto Coppi

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Along the ascent to Passo Pordoi on the side of Canazei, remembers the champion who several times passed the Pordoi at the top of the Giro d'Italia.

  • Where Strada del Passo Pordoi, Canazei
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Passeggiata lungo l'Avisio tra Canazei, Alba e Penìa

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Easy ring on the two banks of the Avisio that connects Canazei to the hamlets of Alba and Penia, which can be crossed by a trekking stroller or backpack with children; along the path a small beach on the river and, near a playground, a climbing wall with cave.

  • Where Canazei
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: fassa.com · visittrentino.info
