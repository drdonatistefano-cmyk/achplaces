What to see in Castelnuovo Don Bosco

Monferrato Astigiano country where St. John Bosco was born: at the Colle Don Bosco the little house of childhood, the great Salesian sanctuary and two museums, between the hills of Freisa and Malvasia marked by three Romanesque churches and the old farmsteads of Morialdo, Nevissano and Ranello.

Colle Don Bosco – Santuario e casetta natale

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Next to the small rural house where Giovanni Bosco spent his childhood stands the complex of the Salesian Institute: the sanctuary with two overlapping churches, built in the fifties largely in reinforced concrete, with the large pilgrim's square, the church of Mary Help of Christians and the bronze statue of Don Bosco donated by Italian teachers in 1929. Final destination of the Don Bosco Way from Turin.

  • Where Frazione Morialdo 30, Castelnuovo Don Bosco
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.castelnuovodonbosco.at.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.castelnuovodonbosco.at.it
