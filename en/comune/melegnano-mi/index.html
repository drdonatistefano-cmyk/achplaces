What to see in Melegnano

Melegnano, mentioned already in 333 A.D. as ninth miles from Milan on the Lambro, is dominated by the Medici Castle, born as a "receptum" in 1243, fortified by the Visconti and transformed since 1532 by Gian Giacomo Medici, with frescoed rooms in the mid-500s and the civic collection Don Cesare Amelli; in the center the minor basilica of San Giovanni Battista preserves part of a altarpiece of Bergognone, and the city is known for the Fair of Forgiveness of Holy Thursday, linked to the bull of Pius IV of 1563.

Castello Mediceo e Civica raccolta museale Don Cesare Amelli

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built as a "receptum" in 1243, it was enlarged by Matteo I Visconti and Bernabò, who gave it the quadrilateral structure with high angular towers; passed in 1512 to the Marquis Brivio, since 1532 it was Gian Giacomo Medici, who made Melegnano a Marquisate. The halls (Emperor, Battles, Seasons, Hercules) and the staircase are frescoed in the middle of the XVI century with the enterprises of the marquis; on the revel with the bridge remain the coats of arms of Marzia Orsini and of the Sforza. Sold by the Medici to the Province of Milan in 1981 and restored since 1998, it reopened in 2001 with the civic collection dedicated to Don Cesare Amelli (1924-2002); guided tours by Pro Loco by reservation (prolocomelegnano@gmail.com).

Basilica minore di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Basilicas

Basilica minor documented for the first time in 1398; inside it preserves part of a altarpiece commissioned to Bergognone in 1506 and some of the ten paintings on the life of St. John the Baptist ordered by the Municipality in the seventeenth century (the others are in St. Peter). Around the church the old town was formed in medieval times; in Napoleonic age the prevost Candia saved the bells from the French requisition by welcoming Napoleon with a procession. On the fourth Sunday of August there is celebrated the feast of the vow of 1630, burning in the church a globe of bambagia to the motto "Sic transit gloria mundi" .

Chiesa di San Pietro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The new church was begun in 1666 (posa della prima pietra) and consecrated in 1672; next to it stood the Hospital of the Pilgrims, which in 1630 welcomed the sick with plague. It preserves part of the ten paintings on the life of Saint John the Baptist commissioned by the Commune, the other part of which is in the basilica.
