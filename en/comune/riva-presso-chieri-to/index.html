What to see in Riva presso Chieri

Country of the plain of Chierese, a land of Roman centuriation, where the castle donated in 1619 by Carlo Emanuele I became Palazzo Grosso, rebuilt by Bernardo Vittone and today City Hall and seat of the Museum of the Soundscape; Vittone also worked at the parish church of the Assumption, where in 1842 he was baptized San Domenico Savio, born in the nearby hamlet of San Giovanni.

Palazzo Grosso

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

In 1619 the Duke Carlo Emanuele I donated the castle of Riva to the favorite Margherita de Roussillon, named marquise; from 1734 he passed to the counts Grosso di Brozolo, who in 1738 had him rebuilt by Bernardo Antonio Vittone. The decorations form a unique cycle: in the atrium the trompe-l'oeil transform the corridor into a gallery of archaeological finds hung in grappa on a fake stone wall. Today it is the seat of the Municipality; recommended booking (tel. 011 521 1788).

  • Where Piazza Parrocchia 4, Riva presso Chieri
  • Entry €3, reduced €2 (4-14 years); with the Museum of the Soundscape €6, reduced €4
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.rivapressochieri.to.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · comune.rivapressochieri.to.it

Museo civico del paesaggio sonoro

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Permanent exhibition at Palazzo Grosso born from Domenico Torta's research on Piedmontese folk music, with the contribution of scholars of the University of Turin as Febo Guizzi: musical instruments, objects, documents and testimonies of Piedmont's sound culture, waiting for a wider museum project.

temporarily closed for maintenance (the site of the Municipality)

  • Where Piazza della Parrocchia 4, Riva presso Chieri
  • Entry €6 with Palazzo Grosso, reduced €4
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.rivapressochieri.to.it

Sources: comune.rivapressochieri.to.it · turismotorino.org

Chiesa parrocchiale di Maria Vergine Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Rebuilt between 1725 and 1792 on a Romanesque building, with the contribution of Sottis, Plantery, Vittone (which is attributed to the elliptical dome) and Dellala; the medieval bell tower was long also civic tower. It contains nineteenth-century frescoes by Gabriele Ferrero, a Madonna del Rosario from the early seventeenth century attributed to Moncalvo or daughter Orsola, a wooden crucifix considered by Carlo Giuseppe Plura, a baptismal font with two sixteenth-century tanks and the organ of 1868. Here he was baptized San Domenico Savio on April 2, 1842.

  • Where Piazza della Parrocchia 2, Riva presso Chieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.rivapressochieri.to.it

Sources: comune.rivapressochieri.to.it

Santuario della Madonna della Fontana

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Erected by the community at a fountain considered miraculous after the escaped danger of the plague of 1630: a first church of 1633-34 was rebuilt in 1660-64 with stuccoes and paintings; in 1738 Vittone refused the restoration and the current church was built in 1777 by his pupil Luigi Michele Barberis, with the house for chaplains and spiritual exercises completed in 1791.

  • Where Santuario Madonna della Fontana, Riva presso Chieri
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.rivapressochieri.to.it

Sources: comune.rivapressochieri.to.it

Casetta natia di San Domenico Savio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

In the hamlet of San Giovanni, three kilometers north of the village, the house where in 1842 San Domenico Savio, young pupil of Don Bosco was born; in the farm next door, since 1987 he has been working a reception center for groups. Free access and/or upon reservation.

  • Where Via Don Bosco 19, frazione San Giovanni, Riva presso Chieri
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.rivapressochieri.to.it

Sources: comune.rivapressochieri.to.it
