What to see in Sedico

At the foot of the Schiara and the Monti del Sole, at the gates of the Bellunesi Dolomites National Park, Sedico combines the protohistoric castle of Noal, the 16th century oratory of San Nicolò in Bribano, the neoclassical villa of Giuseppe Jappelli ai Patt with the historical Museum of the 7th Alpine Regiment and the cycle path of the ancient roges along the Cordevole.

8 places to visit in Sedico, in the province of Belluno (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo storico del 7° Reggimento Alpini

Museums · Themed

In the Villa Patt renovated by the Province, the route follows for almost a century and a half the history of the 7th Alps, since its foundation in 1887, with photographs, documents, weapons and finds from the former Museum-Sacrario of the barracks Tommaso Salsa di Belluno: 160 weapons, 148 relics, 271 documents and 33 private donations.

open only by reservation (tel. (0437 959273)

Villa De Manzoni ai Patt

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built in 1835 by Giovanni Antonio de Manzoni, whose family had been enriched with the mines of Agordino, designed by Giuseppe Jappelli, the architect of Caffè Pedrocchi in Padua. Neoclassical and sober, with four giant parasts on the façade, it contains frescoes by Giovanni De Min (The fight of the Spartans) and Pietro Paoletti (Esop) and, in the tower, Egyptian views and a ceiling with the constellations of the zodiac; in 1870 Giuseppe Segusini added the neo-Gothic rustic wing. From the Province since 1977, it has been managed since 2014 by Pro Loco di Sedico for concerts, projections and exhibitions.

Chiesa di San Nicolò di Bribano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Built in 1502 by the brothers Buzzatti and today a national monument, it houses a wooden altar and a triptych attributed to Augustine by Lodi.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.infodolomiti.it

Chiesa arcipretale di Santa Maria Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built on a project by Pietro Del Fabbro between 1933 and 1939, in Romanesque style with three aisles, he replaced the ancient archpriest's bell tower in 1955, of which remains the bell tower in Piazza della Vittoria. It preserves a Madonna with Child on a table dated 1505 by Francesco Vecellio (maybe four-handed with Tiziano), paintings by Pietro Marescalchi and Antonio Lazzarini and a wooden case by Andrea Brustolon.

  • Where Viale Venezia 18, Sedico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.infodolomiti.it

Sources: dolomitiprealpi.it · infodolomiti.it

Castelliere di Noal

History and architecture · Archaeology

Fortified settlement with visible remains, in a territory inhabited since the Bronze Age; it is reached with a short cycle path equipped with explanatory panels.

Percorso ciclopedonale Antiche Rogge

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

Itinerary on foot or by bike of 14 km (50 m of difference in altitude), almost all on dirt road, along the left bank of the Cordevole da Bribano al Sasso di San Giorgio, with extension to the bridge of Tappole. It takes its name from the ancient roges, the network of channels that fed sawmills, mills, forges and a paper mill; along the path explanatory panels. Medium difficulty, suitable for families; not recommended during and immediately after bad weather.

  • Where Via Dante Alighieri 19, Bribano di Sedico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.infodolomiti.it

Sources: dolomitiprealpi.it · infodolomiti.it

Prati di Salet e Monte Coro

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the Dolomiti Bellunesi National Park, between Sospirolo and Sedico, wide meadows still mowed surrounded by woods and along the Cordevole: a place suitable for observing deer and chamois, as well as deer, green woodpecker, sliced salamander and amphibians. Easy access from San Gottardo, with parking near the church; recommended spring period.

  • Where San Gottardo, Sospirolo/Sedico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.infodolomiti.it

Sources: dolomitipark.it · infodolomiti.it

Schiara, Monti del Sole e Rifugio Bianchet

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

To the north of Sedico rise the Monti del Sole and the walls of Schiara, Talvena and Gusela del Vescovà; the territory is crossed by the Alta Via delle Dolomiti n. 1 and Rifugio Bianchet is a starting point for excursions to the Bellunesi Dolomites National Park. Paths also on Mount Peron (1486 m) and Pala Alta (1933 m).

  • Where Rifugio Bianchet, Sedico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.infodolomiti.it

Sources: infodolomiti.it
