What to see in Scansano

Medieval village on the inner hills of the Maremma, the land of Morellino di Scansano DOCG; in the surroundings the Etruscan settlement of Ice Forte, in the village the Pretorio Palace with the Archaeological Museum and the Museum of Life and Wine, the Collegiate Church of San Giovanni Battista and, on the hill, the Convent of Petreto.

Insediamento etrusco di Ghiaccio Forte

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Etruscan village founded in the fourth century BC near the border of Vulci, on the right of the Albegna, perhaps refuge from the Roman assaults or center of wine producers; it was conquered and devastated in 280 BC. The best preserved part is the 1 km long walls and about 4 meters thick; inside the remains of the small Temple of a farm deity, whose ex-vote are at the Archaeological Museum.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Museo archeologico di Scansano

Museums · Archaeological

In the Palazzo Pretorio of the fifteenth century, restored in 1998, it tells of the human presence in the Valle dell'Albegna from prehistory to the Etruscans and the Romans. It exhibits the Hellenistic finds of Ice Strong (ceramics, bronze statuettes, objects related to fertility rites) and reconstructions of the Roman villa at the farm Aia Nova, with parts of the floors in cottage.

  • Where Piazza del Pretorio 4, Scansano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.museidimaremma.it

Sources: museidimaremma.it · visittuscany.com

Museo della Vite e del Vino

Museums · Themed

In the same Palazzo Pretorio, in five sections, it tells the link between climate, soil and wine, with objects and recipes in dialect, and presents the six DOCs of the territory, including Bianco di Pitigliano and Ansonica. Morellino di Scansano has been DOC since 1978 and DOCG since the 2006 harvest.

  • Where Piazza del Pretorio 4, Scansano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.museidimaremma.it

Sources: museidimaremma.it · visittuscany.com

Centro storico di Scansano

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Borgo probably arose in the thirteenth century, with a castle of the Aldobrandeschi then became a monastery; from the sixteenth century it passed to the counts of Santa Fiora, which drew its current layout. Under the Lorraine, the public offices of Grosseto moved there in the summer (the extant). It preserves numerous medieval buildings and views of the countryside.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Cantine del Morellino di Scansano

Local life and traditions · Food and wine

The Morellino di Scansano DOCG, made from Sangiovese grapes, is one of the most famous red wines in Tuscany; wineries in the village and surroundings offer tastings, and Scansano is a stop of one of the wine roads of the Maremma. At the end of September the Grape Festival closes a month of tastings and open wineries.
