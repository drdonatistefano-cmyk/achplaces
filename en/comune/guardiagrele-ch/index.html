What to see in Guardiagrele

The town of stone of Annunzio, about 600 meters on the Maiella with views from the coast to the valleys: the collegiate church of Santa Maria Maggiore, the medieval and baroque churches and the goldsmith tradition of Nicola da Guardiagrele.

7 places to visit in Guardiagrele, in the province of Chieti (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Duomo di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Collegiate of the 13th century, born as a cemetery church outside the walls, with the massive bell tower on the façade and the portal to sharp sixth of the fourteenth century; under the porch the fresco of San Cristoforo painted by Andrea Delitio in 1473. The interior was transformed in 1706 into baroque forms.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Guardiagrele
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Nicola di Bari

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the upper part of the village, between the end of the 12th and the beginning of the 13th century: medieval remains the bell tower, the rest was rebuilt in the 18th century, with two Renaissance portals.

  • Entry Free

Chiesa di Santa Chiara

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Born in the second half of the thirteenth century with the convent of the Clarisses, it has stuccoes of the eighteenth century, the great painting of the vault of Donato Teodoro and a wooden crucifix.
