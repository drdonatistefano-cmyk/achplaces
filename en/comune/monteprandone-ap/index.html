What to see in Monteprandone

Piceno village, home of St. James of Marca (1393-1476): in the historic centre Piazza dell'Aquila, the Benedictine church of St. Leonardo and the oratory on the birthplace of the saint; just outside the sanctuary of Santa Maria delle Grazie, which preserves its body, and the museums with the 61 codes of its library.

Santuario di Santa Maria delle Grazie e San Giacomo della Marca

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

On August 22, 1449 Pope Niccolò V authorized the Franciscans, at the request of St. James of the Marca, to build a convent and a church. In the chapel is venerated a 15th century terracotta Madonna donated to the saint by Cardinal Francesco della Rovere; the 16th century cloister is frescoed with stories of his life and the sanctuary preserves the incorrupt body of the saint, invoked as protector of children.

  • Where Via Madonna delle Grazie 13, Monteprandone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.monteprandone.gov.it

Sources: monteprandone.gov.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · turismo.marche.it

Museo di San Giacomo della Marca (Musei Sistini del Piceno)

Museums · Old masters

In the cloister of the sanctuary, it is part of the circuit of the Sistine Museums of Piceno: personal objects of the saint and works from the sixteenth to the nineteenth century, including his liturgical set (dress and crucifix), a seal with the name of Jesus and a seventeenth-century wooden bust of Neapolitan school; a section is dedicated to Bishop Eugenio Massi, native of Monteprandone and missionary in China.

  • Where Via Madonna delle Grazie 13, Monteprandone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.monteprandone.gov.it

Sources: monteprandone.gov.it

Museo civico dei Codici di San Giacomo della Marca

Museums · City history

It preserves the 61 manuscripts, almost all of the XIV-XV century, of the library that the saint instituted in the convent of Santa Maria delle Grazie, one of the most important collections of Franciscan books from the fifteenth century: classical texts, Fathers of the Church, right and grammar, four autographs with sermons and homilies and a letter to St. John of Capestrano; also exhibited two ancient maps of Monteprandone and a coin of 1652 by Carlo II Gonzaga-Nevers.

Museo di Arte Sacra (ex Collegiata)

Museums · Old masters

Founded in 2004 on the initiative of the parish priest Fr Francesco Ciabattoni, it is set up in the nave of the former Collegiate with frescoes from the 14th to the 16th century; it collects paintings, relics and works of the parish of San Nicolò di Bari, including a wooden crucifix of the 13th century, the 16th century statue of the Madonna of Loreto and a section on St. James of the Marca.

  • Where Centro storico, Monteprandone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.monteprandone.gov.it

Sources: monteprandone.gov.it

Centro storico, Piazza dell'Aquila e Porta da Monte

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The main square is closed by Palazzo Campanelli, Palazzo Comunale and Palazzo Montani; along the walls runs Via Limbo, with arches and medieval vaults. Porta da Monte, west entrance, still bears the coat of arms of Ascoli, from which the village depended until 1292; from the Belvedere Don Giuseppe Caselli the panorama spans the Marche and Abruzzo Apennines.

  • Where Piazza dell'Aquila, Monteprandone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.monteprandone.gov.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · monteprandone.gov.it

Chiesa di San Leonardo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Among the oldest buildings in the village, the former Benedictine church of the abbey of Farfa built between the XI and XII century, until the Napoleonic edict was also a cemetery inside the walls. Inside, restored, cross vaults and altar in a high arched gallery on two slender octagonal columns; outside two travertine grifos hold the architrave of the portal.

  • Where Centro storico, Monteprandone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.monteprandone.gov.it

Sources: monteprandone.gov.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Collegiata di San Nicolò di Bari

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in place of the old Collegiate Danger: works started in 1808, open to worship in 1840 on a project by the architect Pietro Maggi, with neoclassical interior with a single nave and six lateral altars. It preserves the relics of St. Cyrinus, the fourth century martyr who arrived in 1653, the country's companion.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.monteprandone.gov.it

Sources: monteprandone.gov.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Oratorio di San Giacomo della Marca (casa natale)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It rises from 1581 near the place where according to tradition the house of the Gangale family was located, where the future saint Domenico Gangale was born; the interior is frescoed with scenes of his life.

Fonte Vecchia e antico lavatoio

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

The Old Fountain, called "Conserva" (Conserva) was the most used spring before the 17th century: in 1831 the Municipality provided it with a watering trough and a stone washbasin. The old communal washbasin was built in 1908 for laundry and home water.
