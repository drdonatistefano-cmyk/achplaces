What to see in Paola

The birthplace of San Francesco di Paola, patron saint of Calabria, on the Tyrrhenian coast: the Sanctuary in the gorge of the Isca stream, the point of arrival of the Way of San Francesco, the historic center with Piazza del Popolo and the Fountain of Pisciarieddi, the castle of probable Norman origins, the Byzantine underground church of Sotterra and the long beach of Marina di Paola.

8 places to visit in Paola, in the province of Cosenza (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Santuario di San Francesco di Paola

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry · Viewpoint

It rises in a gorge of the Isca stream, where St. Francis (born in Paola in 1416) founded a chapel dedicated to St. Francis of Assisi; it is home to the Order of Minims and Regional Sanctuary. The ancient church, with a single nave and still of Gothic system, houses in a chapel the relics of the saint; the double arch cloister leads to the monastery, with library and Renaissance and Baroque paintings, partly rested on an arch above the stream. Around, the area of the wonders with the furnace, the spring of the Cucchiarella, the Devil's Bridge and the cave where the saint prayed; next, on the large panoramic square, the new large basilica for pilgrims. Open every day 6:30-20:00.

  • Where Largo S. Francesco di Paola, Paola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it

Chiesa ipogea di Sotterra

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In Gaudimare district, about 3 km from the village: church of Byzantine origin, remodelled in Norman age, finished under the ground level for landslides or floods and discovered in 1874 during the works of the above church of Madonna del Carmine. The frescoes, dating back to the seventh century, include the Annunciation on the sides of the apse, a group of saints with perhaps Mary Magdalene in the center, the Virgin Galaktotrefouse who feeds the Child with a pomegranate, St. Anthony Abbot and fragments of the Christ Pantocrator.

FAI reports a highly endangered state of conservation; not indicated times

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Centro storico e Piazza del Popolo

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Piazza del Popolo has a 17th-century stone fountain and the clock tower, moved by a mechanism of the 17th century; in the center the churches of the Madonna of Montevergine and the Santissimo Rosario, from the Baroque interior, and the Fountain of the Seven Channels from which you go up to the Church Mother of the SS. Annunciated.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Paola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Fontana dei Pisciarieddi

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Monumental fountain attributed to the sixteenth century, an example of the mastery of the Paolani chiseling: two asymmetrical tanks, one inside the other, fed by four protomes each, and an obelisk in local sandstone. In the main tank a cross, which recalls the Spinelli coat of arms, and a peacock, symbol of Paola.

  • Where Piazza del Popolo, Paola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Castello di Paola

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

A cylindrical tower on a quadrangular bastion, on a rock on a slope overlooking the Tyrrhenian coast; it was the upstream garrison that protected the village and housed the residence of the feudal lord, while the coastal towers controlled the sea. The origins are attributed to the Norman era and then Swabian.

  • Where Paola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it

Torre di Paola

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

Two-storey quadrangular tower used for the sighting and defense of the coast, part of the sixteenth-century fortifications of the Calabrian coast; visible from the outside.

  • Where Località Torre, Paola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Via dell'Eremita, Cammino di San Francesco

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A walk of 62.7 km in three stages between Paola and Paterno Calabro, crossed several times by the saint between the two convents; the last stop from San Fili climbs to the beech forest of Bosco Luta, crosses the coastal ridge and descends on Paola. Upon arrival the pilgrims receive the Witnessium.

  • Where Santuario di San Francesco, Paola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
