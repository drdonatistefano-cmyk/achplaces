What to see in Stenico

Country of the Judicaries at the foot of the Dos della Forra, in the Adamello Brenta Natural Park, dominated by the Castle of Stenico, summer residence of the Princes Bishops of Trento and now home of the Museum Castello del Buonconsiglio; not far from the village the waterfall of the Rio Bianco, the House of the Flora Park and the BoscoArteStenico art trail.

Castel Stenico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Since the twelfth century of the Prince Bishops of Trento, it was the seat of the bishop's captain of the Judicarians and their summer residence: a set of buildings of different eras, from the New Palace of the beginning of the thirteenth century to the Medieval Old Palace, transformed in part from the fifteenth century into a Renaissance residence with loggias, double-fores and triple-fores, enlarged in the sixteenth century by Bernardo Clesio. You can visit the chapel of San Martino with frescoes from the thirteenth century, the Judgment Room, the Bozone Tower called "Torre della Fame" (Torre della Fame), with the dark prison accessible from a trapdoor, and the provincial collections of inlaid furniture, weapons, paintings and applied arts, as well as an archaeological section. The castle also appears in the Cycle of the Months of Torre Aquila in Trento.

  • Where Castello di Stenico, 38070 Stenico
  • Entry €7, reduced €5, families from €14
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.buonconsiglio.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: buonconsiglio.it · visittrentino.info

Cascata del Rio Bianco e giardino botanico

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

In the Adamello Brenta Nature Park, just outside Stenico, a 4.2 km ring walk (about 2 hours, 149 m of difference in altitude, suitable for families) climbs along a dirt road towards the Rio Bianco waterfall, crosses a stretch dug into the rock and reaches the botanical garden with educational positions on the medicinal plants and flora of the park, then continue in the BoscoArteStenico. From the parking lot of the Park information point.

  • Where Località Rio Bianco, Stenico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Casa del Parco Flora

Museums · Themed

Building inaugurated in 1871 as a shooting range, today visitor center of the Rio Bianco Nature Area of the Adamello Brenta Nature Park, with an exhibition on the flora of the park; next there is the botanical garden with an illustrative path on the vegetation. Accessible to persons with reduced mobility.

  • Where Località Rio Bianco 1, 38070 Stenico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

BoscoArteStenico

Museums · Modern art

Museum of art in nature a few kilometers from the Terme di Comano: a ring path signposted in the woods, with works on the theme of the refuge made of natural materials, from traditional wood carvings to avant-garde installations. The path is easy, accessible to all (OPEN mark for accessible tourism), with benches and fountains, and has a children's path, the Path of the little Walkers.

  • Where Via del Dos Marin 24, Stenico
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info

Centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village, at the foot of the Dos della Forra and overlooking the Sarca gorge, preserves traditional houses, noble palaces and fountains in the squares; in the territory are reported prehistoric finds in Stenico and Roman finds in Calferi.

  • Where Stenico
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittrentino.info

Sources: visittrentino.info
