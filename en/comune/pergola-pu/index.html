What to see in Pergola

City of the upper valley of the Cesano, on a large terrace at the confluence with the Cinisco, known for the Golden Bronzes by Cartoceto, a Roman equestrian group exhibited in the former convent of San Giacomo. The centre preserves the Duomo, Baroque churches, the Ducal Palace, the Town Hall and the Teatro Angelo Dal Foco; in autumn the National Fair of Fine White Truffles is held.

Museo dei Bronzi Dorati e della Città di Pergola

Museums · Archaeological

Opened in 1999 in the former convent of St. James (XIII-XIV century), around the cloister. The central piece is the Golden Bronzes by Cartoceto di Pergola, a Roman group discovered in 1946 with two knights, two horses and two female figures, among the very few large golden bronze equestrian groups from ancient times, presented in an immersive room with lights and multimedia contents of the physicist Paco Lanciano. The mosaics and funerary furnishings complete the route, more than 200 coins, paintings and sculptures from the 14th to the 18th century (among others Claudio Ridolfi and Giovanni Santi) and the engravings by Walter Valentini. Opening hours: from Tuesday to Sunday 10-12:30 and 15:30-18:30, Monday closed.

Centro storico e palazzi

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The center brings together the Town Hall, designed after 1750 by the Rimini Gianfrancesco Buonamici, the Doge's Palace (already Malatestano, then Ginevri-Latoni) and numerous noble palaces, including Mattei-Baldini, Badalucchi, Guazzugli-Gabrielli, Caverni, Giannini (now Buschi), Ruffini and Cini. In August squares and streets host the reenactment of Medieval Pergola, with the palio among the districts.

  • Where Centro storico, Pergola
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Duomo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Former Augustinian church with late Baroque interior and nineteenth-century façade. It is the destination of the historical procession of Medieval Pergola, which recalls the arrival in the city of the remains of the saints Secondo, Agabito and Giustina.

  • Where Centro storico, Pergola
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: letsmarche.it · italia.it

Chiese del centro storico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In addition to the Duomo, the center preserves the Gothic church of St. James (13th century), restored with the former convent of the Augustinians; St. Francis, with oval portal of the fourteenth century; Santa Maria Assunta, the church of the Kings Magi and Saint Andrew, with Baroque interiors; and the eighteenth-century church of the Tintes, with Greek cross with octagonal dome.

  • Entry To be checked

  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
