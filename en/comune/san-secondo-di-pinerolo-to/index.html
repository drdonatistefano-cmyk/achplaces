What to see in San Secondo di Pinerolo

The hill country of Pinerolese, developed in the eighteenth century with fruit growing, viticulture and terracotta; in the village of Miradolo the neo-Gothic Castle with its historical park of 6 hectares is now a cultural pole of the Cosso Foundation, while in the village stands out the Baroque parish of Giuseppe Girolamo Buniva.

Castello e Parco di Miradolo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry

Castle mentioned in the XII-XIII centuries and renovated in neo-Gothic style in 1866; it is linked to Maria Elisabetta Ferrero della Marmora, who had the neo-Gothic Serra and the Round Tower built and transformed the Italian garden into a 6-hectare park. Restored since 2007, it hosts temporary exhibitions, concerts, shows and educational activities. The park preserves over 1,740 trees of about 70 species, crossed by irrigation channels; free entry with Museums and Torino+Piemonte Card subscription, recommended booking.

  • Where Via Cardonata 2, San Secondo di Pinerolo
  • Entry €15, reduced €12 / €10 / €5
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.fondazionecosso.com

Sources: fondazionecosso.com · turismotorino.org · italia.it

Centro storico e chiesa parrocchiale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village, mentioned already in the lower Middle Ages, grew in the eighteenth century with fruit growing, viticulture and terracotta processing (the "tupin") and in the nineteenth century it reached 14 manufactures. In the center there are elegant residential buildings and the old well, symbol of the village, in addition to the parish, notable example of Piedmontese Baroque designed by Giuseppe Girolamo Buniva and completed in 1773, and the eighteenth-century church of the Confraternity of the Holy Trinity.

  • Where Centro, San Secondo di Pinerolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: turismotorino.org

Frazione Miradolo e chiesa di Santa Maria

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Frazione at the foot of the hill of San Secondo, attested by the 11th century and cited in 1064 among the lands of the Benedictine abbey of Santa Maria di Pinerolo; it was the most important center of the territory, the capital of the homonymous castle, whose castle, outposted on the Val Chisone, was destroyed at the end of the 16th century. In addition to the neo-Gothic castle, it preserves the parish baroque of Santa Maria, from the mid-eighteenth century, and the villa of the poetess Colombini, today kindergarten.

  • Where Frazione Miradolo, San Secondo di Pinerolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismotorino.org

Sources: comune.sansecondodipinerolo.to.it · turismotorino.org
