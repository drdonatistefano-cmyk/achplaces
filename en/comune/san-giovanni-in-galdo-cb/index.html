What to see in San Giovanni in Galdo

Borgo del Molise centrale with the medieval quarter of Morrutto, the walls with three doors, the abbey palace with the coat of arms of Cardinal Orsini (later Pope Benedict XIII) and churches with paintings by Giuseppe Castellano; about 2 km, on Colle Rimontato, the italic temple of the Sannitic age.

Tempio italico (Santuario sannitico) di Colle Rimontato

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Sacred area of about 740 m2 fenced by large blocks of stone, in panoramic position on the valley of the Fiumarello and close to the tratturo Castel di Sangro-Lucera: a small temple flanked by two colonnaded arcades, of which remains the high podium with molded frames and the cell with floor in cottage and white tiles. The coins under the cell (the most recent one denarius of 104 B.C.) date from the construction; the lamps attest to the use at least until the second century AD. Discovered in the 1990s, it is still the subject of excavations by the University of Leiden and Wisdom with the Superintendence of Molise.

Borgo del Morrutto, mura e Palazzo abbaziale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The Morrutto (broken wall) is the oldest part of the country, a maze of homes of the year Mille built with poor materials and strong defensive asymmetries, some above underground caves; the walls with three doors and the abbey palace with the coat of arms of Cardinal Vincenzo Maria Orsini, then Pope Benedict XIII.

  • Where Centro storico, San Giovanni in Galdo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: visitmolise.eu

Chiesa di San Germano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Medieval church erected in 1480 on an ancient Roman temple and renovated several times: it preserves a pulpit of the fourteenth century with trilobed arch and figures of saints, two saints on Romanesque lions coming from the destroyed church of St. John the Baptist, a canvas of Neapolitan school with the Baptist and, in the apse, the Creation of the world frescoed in 1949 by Amedeo Trivisonno.

  • Where Via Borgo San Germano, San Giovanni in Galdo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: visitmolise.eu

Chiesa di Santa Maria del Carmine

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ancient romoratory (foundation indicated in 1243) along the mule track for Campolieto, already the Grancia of the Scalzi Carmelites, then the Doctrinal Fathers until the suppression of 1809 and the Conventuals since 1840; it preserves paintings by the Neapolitan Giuseppe Castellano (1707) and epigraphs recalling Cardinal Orsini.

  • Where Via mulattiera per Campolieto, San Giovanni in Galdo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: visitmolise.eu

Cappella dell'Annunziata

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Chapel north of the village, with origins indicated in 1118: it preserves the canvas of the Annunciation (1717) commissioned by Cardinal Orsini to Giuseppe Castellano; restored from 1863 to 1903, in 2008 it had ceiling and six lunettes frescoed by Annunziato De Rubertis with the life of Mary.

  • Where A nord del paese, San Giovanni in Galdo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitmolise.eu

Sources: visitmolise.eu
