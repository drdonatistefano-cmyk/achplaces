What to see in San Nicola dell'Alto

Arbëreshë village (Shën Kolli) founded in the first half of the fifteenth century on the watershed between the valleys of Neto and Lipuda, with a medieval stone centre, apotropaic masks on portals and windows, the churches of St. Nicholas and St. Dominic and the Museum of peasant civilization.

Centro storico

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval stone village, founded in the first half of the 15th century, on the Ionian side between Monte San Michele and Monte Pizzuto, on the watershed between the valleys of Neto and Lipuda. Small houses gathered around small squares, the churches of San Nicola and San Domenico and, on the mountain above the village, the sanctuary of San Michele Arcangelo; the apotropaic stone masks on portals and windows recall the Albanian origins, linked to fifteenth-century migrations from southern Albania and Morea.

  • Where Via Papa Giovanni XXIII, San Nicola dell'Alto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Museo della civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Collection started in the early 1990s by the Coplas cooperative, in the former restored slaughterhouse, managed by the Oreste Ventrice Association: tools and objects of agricultural traditions, artisans and pastorals of the arbëreshe community, a bedroom rebuilt with traditional fabrics and a working spinning and weaving corner. Booking not required.

MiC card (updated 2020) indicates closed every day; times to be checked with the Municipality (0962 85042) or the Wind Oreste Association (0962 85100)

Chiesa di San Nicola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

One of the main churches in the historic center; the current building replaced in 1954 the ancient church of St. Nicholas, demolished, which appears in a canvas six-seventh-century Madonna with Child and Saints, where St. Nicholas entrusts church and village to the protection of the Virgin.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: sigecweb.beniculturali.it · cultura.gov.it

Monumento ai caduti

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Monument to the fallen of the two world wars, 5 meters high: the first, erected in 1919-1920, was destroyed in the Second World War; the current (1972) has a high base with the plaque of names and a bezel with helmet between branches of laurel and oak, surmounted by the marble statue of a soldier in combat by the sculptor Alberto Sparapani.

  • Where Via Skanderberg, San Nicola dell'Alto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
