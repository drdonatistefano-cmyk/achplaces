What to see in Renon

The sunny plateau above Bolzano, a holiday destination for about 450 years and accessible without car by cable car and train: the earth pyramids, one of the highest in Europe, the Corno del Renon (2,260 m) with a 360° view of the Dolomites, the Commenda of the Teutonic Order in Longomoso and the museum of beekeeping at the Plattner farm.

Piramidi di terra del Renon

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry · Viewpoint

Phenomenon of erosion: the morainic clay left by the glaciers is very hard to dry but melts with the rain, and the big boulders protect it, leaving standing columns that grow at every storm and collapse when the boulder falls. You can see in three gorges: the Rio Fosco valley between Longomoso and Santa Maria, Rio Rivellone under Soprabolzano and Rio Gasterer in Auna di Sotto; from Collalbo 30 minutes walk to the panoramic points.

  • Where Longomoso / Soprabolzano / Auna di Sotto, Renon
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info · ritten.com

Trenino del Renon

Local life and traditions · Motors · Viewpoint

The last train of its kind in South Tyrol has been in service since 1907, connecting Soprabolzano to Collalbo with a stop at Maria Assunta in about 18 minutes, every half hour; in Soprabolzano comes the cable car from Bolzano (12 minutes, every 4). Free with RittenCard.

  • Where Stazione di Soprabolzano, Renon
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.ritten.com

Sources: ritten.com · suedtirol.info

Corno del Renon

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

The summit is 2,260 m with a 360° free view: Sass de Putia, Odle, Sciliar, Catinaccio, Latemar, Corno Nero and Corno Bianco, with the UNESCO Dolomites arch from southeast to south. Cableway (Via Tann 21, Collalbo, bus 166) open in summer and autumn (2026: from 14 May to 8 November) and in winter for the ski area; with the RittenCard a free climb a day.

  • Where Via Tann 21, Collalbo, Renon
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.suedtirol.info
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: ritten.com · suedtirol.info · rittnerhorn.skiperformance.com

Commenda di Longomoso

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Rural seat of the Teutonic Order: a hospice was built there in 1200, when the crossing road passed on the Renon, to accommodate the travelers, change the horses and cure the sick. The current building, a large walled cube with a pavilion roof, dates back to about 1650; in the majolica stove rooms and stucco ceilings and upholstery with biblical scenes and the history of the Order. Today Renon's cultural centre with concerts and events; visits in season, included in the RittenCard.

  • Where Via Paese, Longomoso, Renon
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info · ritten.com

Museo dell'apicoltura - Maso Plattner

Museums · Themed

In the Plattner farm of Costalovara, a monument of rural architecture of over 600 years restored by the Gramm family with the protection of cultural heritage: the largest private collection of South Tyrolean beekeeping explains the production of honey, and a naturalistic path on the hill tells the world of bees. In the season from April to October, admission from 10 to 17; free with RittenCard.

  • Where Costalovara 15, Soprabolzano, Renon
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info
