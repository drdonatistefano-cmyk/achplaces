What to see in La Maddalena

The capital of the National Park of the Archipelago of La Maddalena, the first of Sardinia (1994), with more than 60 islands: in Caprera, united by a bridge-diga, the Compendio garibaldino where Garibaldi lived 26 years; in the city the Diocesan Museum with Nelson silvers and the naval museum of the wreck of Spargi.

Compendio garibaldino e Casa Bianca

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

Garibaldi settled in Caprera in 1856 and since 1866 built the White House in granite and plaster, with eight communicating rooms, including the bedroom with the deathbed: the clock is stopped at 18.21 on June 2nd 1882. In the courtyard the wooden house, the iron house, the crusher, oven, stable, mill and the Clelia's pino planted in 1867; a path leads to the granite tomb of the general in the family grave. Open Monday to Saturday 8.30-19.30 (last entry 18.30).

  • Where Isola di Caprera, La Maddalena
  • Entry €8; €2 EU citizens 18-25 years old; free under 18; cumulative with Memorial €12 (only at the premises)
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: museigaribaldini.cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Memoriale Giuseppe Garibaldi

Museums · City history · Paid entry

In Fort Arbuticci, about 4 km from the White House, used until the Second World War and then by the Navy: since 2012 it is the first Italian museum entirely dedicated to a historical figure, with a multimedia path on the life and business of Garibaldi. Open Monday to Saturday 8.30-13.45 (last entry 13).

  • Where Strada Cala Garibaldi - Arbuticci, Caprera, La Maddalena
  • Entry €6; €2 EU citizens 18-25 years; free under 18; cumulative with Compendium €12
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: museigaribaldini.cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Museo diocesano e parrocchiale di Santa Maria Maddalena

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the parish, he exhibited the Treasure of Santa Maria Maddalena, a former vote of the faithful with Gallura jewels from the 16th to the 18th century, statues, paintings, tapestries and vestments, and the altar furnishing donated by Admiral Nelson: three neoclassical silvers made in 1804 in Barcelona, with autograph letters. Open all year round from Monday to Friday 10-13.30; afternoons and weekends by reservation.

  • Where Via Baron Manno, La Maddalena
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnacultura.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Museo archeologico navale Nino Lamboglia

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Dedicated to the Roman ship sunk near Spargi around 120 BC with hundreds of wine amphorae and canteen pottery: the central piece is the life-size reconstruction of a section of the hull with 202 amphorae stoked; it also exhibits on-board objects and anchors in lead.

  • Where Strada Panoramica, La Maddalena
  • Entry €4
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Museo del Mare

Museums · Themed

Inaugurated in 2006 by the park with Italia Nostra in Caprera, it follows in chronological order, from prehistory to today, the history of the sea and the routes of the archipelago with photos, documents, models, remnants of boats, navigation and fishing instruments, a copy of the Peutingerian Tabula and a Granite Mother Goddess.

  • Where Caprera, La Maddalena
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Stagnali e museo geomineralogico

Museums · Science and university

Former military village of the early twentieth century on the west side of Caprera, today the only inhabited center of the island and seat of the environmental education center of the park; the mineralogical-naturalistic museum exposes the rocks of the archipelago, the sands of the beaches of Caprera, fossils and fragments of meteorites.

Parco nazionale dell'Arcipelago di La Maddalena

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Established in 1994, Sardinia's first national park: more than 60 granite and shale islands, more than 20,000 hectares of sea and land and 180 km of coast, with almost a thousand plant species (about fifty endemic), turtles and turtles Caretta caretta. Sports, fishing and diving should be allowed. From Palau the ferry arrives in about 20 minutes.

  • Where Arcipelago di La Maddalena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Isola di Caprera

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The second island of the archipelago, entirely protected, united to La Maddalena by the bridge-diga of the Passo della Moneta (about 600 m, 1958): eastern side of pink granite and maquis, reserve since 1982, and western side with plains and pine forest. Between the beaches Cala Coticcio, called "Tahiti sarda" , Cala dei Due Mari and the Beach of the wreck with the remains of a ship on the shore.

  • Where Caprera, La Maddalena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Cala Garibaldi

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the north-western coast of Caprera, golden sand and fine, the seabed gradually descends with natural pools among the rocks, suitable for families; Garibaldi moored the boats there and the pier is still visible.

Punta Tegge

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small beach at the south-western end of the island, sheltered by a granite cliff and the promontory of Fort Santa Teresa, with large golden sand, flat rocks that once served as tiles and seabed that descends quickly, loved by divers. Accessible in a wheelchair, with a bar.

Fortificazioni dell'arcipelago

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

From the second half of the eighteenth century the archipelago was fortified: on the island the Square Tower and the Fort San Vittorio (Old Guard), at the beginning of the nineteenth century the Fort Carlo Felice guarding the Pass of the Coin and the Fort San Giorgio in Santo Stefano; between the late nineteenth century and world wars Nido d'Aquila, Punta Tegge and Punta Rossa. You can see them along the coastal road; the anti-air stations are visited with the guides of the park.

  • Where Strada panoramica, La Maddalena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Centro storico e Piazza Comando

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village overlooks the south coast of the island, with pavements, balconies and benches carved into the local granite; Piazza Comando was built at the end of the 19th century for maritime military command. On July 22, the feast of Santa Maddalena has a procession at sea.

  • Where Piazza Comando, La Maddalena
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
