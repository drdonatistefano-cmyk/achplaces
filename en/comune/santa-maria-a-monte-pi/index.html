What to see in Santa Maria a Monte

Medieval village founded in Lucca, with a single road that spirals up the hill to the archaeological area of the Fortress; among the main places the Collegiate, the Clock Tower, the tunnels of the underground city, the Civic Museum of Blessed Diana Giuntini and the Casa Carducci, where the poet lived when he was young.

Museo Casa Carducci

Museums · House museums

The house where Giosuè Carducci spent his youth with his family and his father Michele, a doctor; he exhibits works by the artist Antonio Possenti illustrating the poet's most famous poems. The gravestones of his father and brother Dante are still visible in the village cemetery.

  • Where Via Carducci 29, Santa Maria a Monte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Area archeologica della Rocca

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

The highest and oldest part of the village, born as a strategic cornerstone: Santa Maria a Monte was the first military outpost of Valdarno and in 1327 yielded to the Florentine siege, which led to the construction of the current Fortress. From the area you can enjoy the view of the surrounding landscape.

  • Where La Rocca, Santa Maria a Monte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Città sotterranea e cisterna medievale

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Network of tunnels dug into the tuff, with tanks and cellars intact, which corresponds to the medieval walls and connects different parts of the country; it probably served military purposes, as a passage for defenders during the sieges, but another hypothesis is the water reserve.

  • Where Centro storico, Santa Maria a Monte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Collegiata di San Giovanni Evangelista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

A single nave church with portal of 1677 in serene stone; a Latin plaque recalls the ancient church of Santa Maria della Rocca of 1189. It preserves a marble pulpit of the XII-XIII century supported by two lions, a wooden crucifix of the XIV century on the high altar, a Madonna enthroned with a polychrome wooden Child of the Giottesque school, the reliquary of the Blessed Giuntini and an octagonal baptismal font of 1468 with bas-reliefs of Rosselli.

  • Where Via Roma, Santa Maria a Monte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Torre dell'Orologio e Palazzo Pretorio

History and architecture · Towers

The tower was part of the defensive system of the second circle of walls; it became a civic tower and received the clock during the municipal period. Today it hosts a permanent exhibition on the seasons and rural life, with paintings by Giovanni Aiello and tools of work in the fields.

  • Where Centro storico, Santa Maria a Monte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: visittuscany.com · terredipisa.it

Museo civico Beata Diana Giuntini

Museums · City history · Free entry

Next to the archaeological area of the Fortress, it is dedicated to the patroness, who lived in the fourteenth century, who chose poverty while coming from a rich family. On the ground floor a fourteenth-century document on a hospital dedicated to her and the only painting that portrays her, from 1734, of the Florentine Baroque painter Bamberini; on the first floor an archaeological path with finds of the lower Valdarno from the Roman era to the Middle Ages. Open by reservation Tuesday and Thursday 9-13, Saturday 10-13.

  • Where Presso la Rocca, Santa Maria a Monte
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: terredipisa.it · visittuscany.com

Montecalvoli e chiesa dei Santi Giorgio e Jacopo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Fraction at the southern end of the Cerbaie, mentioned as early as 728; almost destroyed in the Second World War, preserves medieval remains and a pre-Romanic stone with a pattern intertwined. The church, rebuilt in 1830, has unfinished terracotta façade, serene stone columns and capitals of the demolished church, a wooden crucifix of the sixteenth century and an ancient baptismal font.

  • Where Montecalvoli, Santa Maria a Monte
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.terredipisa.it

Sources: terredipisa.it

Museums · Science and university · Viewpoint
