Palazzo Pepoli e Torre dell'orologio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

Palazzo di fine Quattrocento, today the town hall, with the tombstone of the first Festa degli Alberi in Italy (1899) and the tower of 1724.

  • Where Castiglione dei Pepoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Lago Brasimone e Foresta del Poranceto

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lake 1911 and forest with the park visitor center, 5 km from the village.

  • Where Brasimone, Castiglione dei Pepoli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it
