What to see in Monte Rinaldo

Medieval walled village of Fermano, with Porta Ascolana, the Civic Tower and Palazzo Giustiniani on the square and a terrace on the Sibillini; about 2 km the archaeological area La Cuma, Roman sanctuary started in the middle of the II century BC and discovered in 1950, whose findings are in the Civic Museum, now closed.

Area archeologica La Cuma

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Remains of a Roman sanctuary about 2 km from the village, built from the middle of the II century BC and abandoned in the first century A.D.; the first stone blocks emerged in 1950 during agricultural works. The complex includes a temple dedicated to Jupiter, a worship building behind and a large porch that encloses the sacred area.

Museo civico archeologico «La Cuma»

Museums · Archaeological

In the former church of the SS. Crucifix collects the finds of the sanctuary of the Cuma: ceramics of various eras, architectural decorations and bronze objects dedicated to the gods.

Temporaryly closed for maintenance according to the Municipality (page updated in November 2023) and closed according to Let's Marche

Borgo murato, piazza e terrazza panoramica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

You enter the medieval village surrounded by walls from Porta Ascolana up to the square with the Civic Tower and Palazzo Giustiniani; on the edge of the center a panoramic terrace on the hills and the Sibillini Mountains. In the village the churches of the SS. Sacramento e Rosario (mid 18th century), Saints Leonardo and Flaviano, Saint Mary of the Snow and Saint Anne.

  • Where Porta Ascolana, Monte Rinaldo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Fonte San Flaviano

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners · Free entry

The only surviving public fountain of the municipality, before the fifteenth century, made of exposed bricks: a large vaulted room and a rectangular three-sector bathtub used as a water trough and washbasin. Restored at the beginning of the eighteenth century with a round arch and Doric pilasters; the municipality signals its degradation and a restoration project.

  • Where Via Giuseppe Garibaldi 5, Monte Rinaldo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.monterinaldo.fm.it · italia.it
