What to see in Monsampolo del Tronto

Panoramic village on the Tronto valley, at the end of the Via Salaria Marchegiana: under the church of Maria Santissima Assunta the Museum of the Crypt exhibits about twenty mummy of the XVII-XIX century, under the noble palaces run underground routes and the seventeenth-century convent of San Francesco houses the Civic Museum.

Chiesa di Maria Santissima Assunta e Museo della Cripta, le mummie di Monsampolo

Museums · Archaeological

In the crypt of the church a group of archaeologists and restorers found in 2003 over twenty mummified bodies, dated between the seventeenth and nineteenth centuries; bodies and kits, well preserved, are exposed with the objects found during the excavation.

  • Where Monsampolo del Tronto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · letsmarche.it

Monsampolo sotterranea, percorsi ipogei

Local life and traditions · Hidden corners

Under the hill of the Old Earth, about 120 meters of tunnels under the noble Guiderocchi and Malaspina buildings connect the cellars through hatches and ancient tunnels, used as dispensaries, shelters and escape routes.

  • Where Monsampolo del Tronto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Borgo fortificato e Piazza Castello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

One of the most panoramic points of the area, on the hill reached from the last stretch of the Via Salaria; in the fortified village Palazzo Malaspina and, in piazza Castello, Palazzo Guiderocchi, under which is located the so-called temple form.
