What to see in Torviscosa

The town of Bassa Friulana, Torviscosa was founded in 1937-1938 at the ancient Zuino Tower around the SAICI plant of the Snia group, which obtained cellulose from the gentle cane on land reclamation, and was inaugurated as a village in 1940; in the square dominated by the tower is the CID, which houses archives and collections.

CID Centro Informazione Documentazione

Museums · City history

In the brick building with its large oculus overlooking the square, it preserves the historical, documentary and artistic heritage of the city-factory: historical and photographic archives, plastic works SNIA Viscosa, the technical library and the collections of ceramics and paintings of the Marinotti family, as well as temporary exhibitions; it tells the transformation of a marshland into an agricultural-industrial reality. Torviscosa, città di fondazione

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Country built ex novo around the SAICI plant, whose works began in 1937 and ended in less than a year; inaugurated in 1940 under the name of Torviscosa and celebrated by Filippo Tommaso Marinetti with a long poem. The square is dominated by a high tower and the long brick building of the CID.
