What to see in Valenza

Città sul Po, known as the international jewelry centre, started around 1840 by Vincenzo Morosetti: in the centre the Cathedral of Santa Maria Maggiore, rebuilt in the seventeenth century, Palazzo Valentino and Palazzo Pellizzari; along the river the remains of the walls and the Garzaia, the historical nucleus of the river park.

Duomo di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Rebuilt on a project by Paolo Falcone of Lugano, decided at the beginning of the seventeenth century, and reopened to worship in 1622; the façade was restored in 1890. Inside the Madonna del Rosario del Moncalvo (1620), the main altar of Giacomo Pellagatta (about 1760), the organ Serassi of 1852 in a baroque case of 1779 and the baptistery of 1964 by Luigi Visconti.

  • Where Piazza XXXI Martiri, Valenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valenza.al.it

Sources: comune.valenza.al.it

Museo dell'Opera del Duomo

Museums · Old masters

In the sacristy of the Duomo, wanted by Mgr. Luigi Frascarolo: silverware, wooden furnishings, liturgical fabrics and paintings by the lay companies of the city, including seventeenth-century crosses, monstrers from the seventeenth to the nineteenth century, the reliquary bust of San Massimo in silver lamina and the Book of Baptisms with recordings of 1562-1584. Open on request.

  • Where Piazza XXXI Martiri, Valenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valenza.al.it

Sources: comune.valenza.al.it

Palazzo Valentino

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace in front of the Duomo, seat of the Civic Library and the City Centre of Culture.

  • Where Piazza XXXI Martiri, Valenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valenza.al.it

Sources: alexala.it · comune.valenza.al.it

Palazzo Pellizzari

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palazzo di fine 18th century attributed to Carlo Andrea Pellizzari, today seat of the Municipality. On the stairs the tombstone to the fallen for the freedom of Giacomo Manzù (1957) with verses of Salvatore Quasimodo; in the council hall the frescoes of Luigi Vacca of 1810 with the Apotheosis of Enea. Napoleon stayed there several times.

  • Where Via Pellizzari 2, Valenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valenza.al.it

Sources: comune.valenza.al.it

Mura fortificate

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Surviving section of the seventeenth-century fortifications in the lower part of the city, near the Po, made of brick and superimposed stones; the rest was demolished at the will of Napoleon to recover the material.

  • Where Strada al Po 1, Valenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valenza.al.it

Sources: comune.valenza.al.it · alexala.it

Garzaia di Valenza

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

The historical nucleus of the Po river park, established as a reserve in 1979: it protects a Po's lanca, which for many years was the only nesting site of the red heron in Piedmont, and hosts nitticora, garza tuft and garzetta.

  • Where Lungo il Po, Valenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valenza.al.it

Sources: comune.valenza.al.it · alexala.it

Monte Valenza

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Small hamlet on the high hill, part of the territory of Valencia since the 14th century, developed along a single road: the church of Sant'Eusebio, the church of the Convent and the bell tower. From the village you can see the Po plain with the centers along the Po and, on clear days, Mount Rosa.

  • Where Via Lino Perrone 23, Monte, Valenza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valenza.al.it

Sources: comune.valenza.al.it

Terme di Monte Valenza

Local life and traditions · Spas

Sulphure waters with approximately 1 mg/l of hydrogen sulphide, indicated by the Common for liver, bile, stomach and bowel.

  • Where Via Fontana 13, Valenza
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.valenza.al.it
