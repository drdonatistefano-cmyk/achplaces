What to see in Greve in Chianti

3 places to visit in Greve in Chianti, in the province of Florence (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 2 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Piazza Matteotti di Greve

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The asymmetrical piazza with porticoes of the capital of Chianti, where the Expo of Chianti Classico is held in September.

  • Where Piazza Matteotti, Greve in Chianti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com

Montefioralle

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The best preserved castle of the Greve valley, still closed in the double circle of walls with four doors, among the most beautiful villages in Italy.

  • Where Montefioralle, Greve in Chianti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visittuscany.com · destinationflorence.com

Museo di Arte sacra di San Francesco

Museums · Old masters

In the former Franciscan oratory, works of the churches of the territory, including the Terracotta Complaint of the workshop of Baccio da Montelupo and an archaeological section.

  • Where Via San Francesco 4, Greve in Chianti
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.destinationflorence.com

Sources: destinationflorence.com
