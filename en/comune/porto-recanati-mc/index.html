What to see in Porto Recanati

A sea town born around the Swabian Castle, wanted in 1229 by Federico II as port of Recanati and defended by pirates, which today houses the Moroni Art Gallery, the archaeological exhibition . Divi & Dei , and the Arena Beniamino Gigli; to the south the remains of the Roman city of Potentia and the abbey of Santa Maria in Potenza.

Castello Svevo e Arena Beniamino Gigli

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

In 1229 Frederick II granted to Recanati the lands between Musone and Potenza, with a port and a castle for the defense of the coast: thus was born the first nucleus of the city. The first building was a square tower on the east side, to which in the fourteenth century a second tower was added to the south-west; the walls keep almost intact the walkway, today not accessible. The courtyard, inhabited by a community linked to the port at least until the sixteenth century, in summer hosts concerts and performances of the Arena Beniamino Gigli.

Pinacoteca comunale «Attilio Moroni»

Museums · Modern art

On the first floor of the castle, in rooms born for the activities of the port: works of the nineteenth and twentieth centuries donated by the rector and art enthusiast Attilio Moroni; the entrance hall is dedicated to the painter Biagio Biagetti.

Area archeologica di Potentia

History and architecture · Archaeology

Roman colony founded in 184 B.C. south of the present town: the excavations have highlighted the porticoes of the hole with the tabernacles, a temple on high podium and a domus patricia with mosaic floors and frescoed walls.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
