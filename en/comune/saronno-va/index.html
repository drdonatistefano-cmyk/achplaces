What to see in Saronno

Città dell'alto Milanese known for the Sanctuary of the Blessed Virgin of Miracles, with frescoes by Bernardino Luini and the dome by Gaudenzio Ferrari, and for the collection of ancient ceramics Gianetti.

3 places to visit in Saronno, in the province of Varese (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Saronno in the guide with map and filters

Santuario della Beata Vergine dei Miracoli

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Probably started in 1498 on a central plan of the Amadeus: the bell tower of Paolo della Porta (1511-1516), the frescoes of Bernardino Luini in the main chapel (since 1525) and the dome painted by Gaudenzio Ferrari (1534); the façade was built in 1596 by Lelio Buzzi under the direction of Pellegrino Tibaldi.

  • Where Saronno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Museo della Ceramica Giuseppe Gianetti

Museums · House museums

How to enter a house of the 1930s still inhabited: over 600 ceramics collected by the collector Giuseppe Gianetti since 1932, including 201 porcelains of Meissen of the eighteenth century and Italian and European majolica, with a contemporary ceramic section.

  • Where Via Carcano 9, Saronno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · italia.it

MILS – Museo delle Industrie e del Lavoro del Saronnese

Museums · Themed

The industrial history of Saronnese from the second half of the nineteenth century to the sixties: historical rolling stock of the North Railways, Lazzaroni machinery, a Fiat 501 of 1924 and radio and televisions Phonola.

Closed for renovation, reopening not communicated

  • Where Via Don Griffanti 6, Saronno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: abbonamentomusei.it · italia.it
