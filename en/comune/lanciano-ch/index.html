What to see in Lanciano

City of the four districts around Piazza del Plebiscito, seat of the Eucharistic Miracle: Santa Maria Maggiore, the Cathedral on the Diocletian Bridge and the Montanare Towers.

9 places to visit in Lanciano, in the province of Chieti (Abruzzo). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Lanciano in the guide with map and filters

Santuario del Miracolo Eucaristico – San Francesco

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Church of 1258 on the place of the church of San Legonziano, where tradition places the miracle of the eighth century; a single nave with a Baroque style frescoed vault and eighteenth-century organ, keeps the relics on the main altar.

  • Where Quartiere Borgo, Lanciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it

Basilica di Santa Maria Maggiore

Places of worship · Basilicas · Free entry

Church of the thirteenth century in Burgundian-Cistercian style, with three facades of different eras; it preserves the Cross of Nicola da Guardiagrele of 1432.

  • Where Quartiere Civitanova, Lanciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria del Ponte

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

It rises on the Bridge of Diocletian where was found the statue of the Madonna, rebuilt in the eighteenth century according to a design by Carlo Fantoni, with frescoes by Giacinto Diano and the bell tower 37 meters from 1610-1614.

  • Where Piazza del Plebiscito, Lanciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it

Ponte di Diocleziano

History and architecture · Archaeology

Late-Imperial bridge collapsed in the earthquake of 1088 and rebuilt in the thirteenth century, on which stands the cathedral; in the basement a museum path tells the bridge and the first sanctuary of the Miraculum.

Indicated as closed by the MiC: check at the tour desk

  • Where Lanciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismo.abruzzo.it

Torri Montanare

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Two Norman watchtowers on the southern walls, restored in the Aragonese era, with views of Maiella and Gran Sasso.

  • Where Lanciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it

Botteghe medievali

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

A fourteenth-century square-plan complex with shops on the ground floor and houses above, opened by three arches with the inscription of owner Nicola De Rubeis.

  • Where Corso dei Frentani, Lanciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it

Porta San Biagio e mura

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

The only original door left of the walls of the 12th century, in Lanciano Vecchia; traces of the walls remain in Civitanova and Lanciano Vecchia.

  • Where Lanciano Vecchia, Lanciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it

Piazza del Plebiscito

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The heart of the historic center on which the districts of Lanciano Vecchia, Civitanova, Sacca and Borgo open.

  • Where Piazza del Plebiscito, Lanciano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: turismo.abruzzo.it · italia.it

Museo archeologico – Polo Museale Santo Spirito

Museums · Archaeological

The history of ancient Anxanum from the Neolithic to pre-Roman furnishings and the ex-vote of an Italic shrine of the III-II century BC.

  • Where Via Santo Spirito, Lanciano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page turismo.abruzzo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismo.abruzzo.it
