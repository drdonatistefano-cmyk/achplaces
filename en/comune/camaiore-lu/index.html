What to see in Camaiore

City at the foot of the Apuan Alps born as a Roman outpost along the road between Lucca and Luni ('Campus Major'), with medieval plant, the Collegiate and the Badia di San Pietro on the Via Francigena, two civic museums, the hamlet of graffiti of Casoli and the beach of Lido di Camaiore in Versilia.

8 places to visit in Camaiore, in the province of Lucca (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Collegiata di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Latin cross church of 1278, originally Romanesque, remodelled in Baroque style in the eighteenth century; in the twentieth century the exterior was restored to Romanesque forms.

  • Where Centro storico, Camaiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Badia di San Pietro

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Longobard Abbey founded in the VII century, preserves the original Romanesque plant with apse and facade; it is located along the Via Francigena, whose stage 26 arrives from Massa to Camaiore and the 27 continues to Lucca.

  • Where Badia di San Pietro, Camaiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo d'Arte Sacra

Museums · Old masters · Free entry

Established in the 1930s (opened on June 9, 1934 with Ugo Procacci) in a seventeenth-century building, already home to the Confraternity of San Michele and San Vincenzo. Six rooms with liturgical vestments, sacred goldsmithery, paintings and wooden sculptures from the 14th to the 16th century: among the works the Annunciated Virgin of Matteo Civitali, a polyptych by Francesco di Andrea d'Anguilla, a altarpiece attributed to Vincenzo Frediani and a Flemish tapestry of the early 16th century. Open Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday afternoon with seasonal hours, on Sunday by reservation; free entry with free offer, guided tour €2 upon reservation.

  • Where Via IV Novembre, Camaiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.camaiore.lu.it · visittuscany.com

Civico Museo Archeologico (CMAC)

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In Palazzo Tori Massoni, exhibits artifacts from prehistory to the Middle Ages, with interactive and tactile places including prehistoric instruments and the cast of a Neanderthal femur; the museum project connects it to the Grotto to the Wave, to the Roman villa of Via Acquarella and to the medieval settlement of Montecastrense. Open from Tuesday to Friday in the morning, Saturday and Sunday morning and afternoon; closed on Monday. Also reduced with Carnet Versilia Museums or Edumusei Card.

  • Where Via Vittorio Emanuele, Camaiore
  • Entry €4, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: comune.camaiore.lu.it · visittuscany.com · cultura.gov.it

Grotta all'Onda

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Karst cave in the Apuan Alps Park, near Casoli, inhabited by Neanderthal hunters and then used for agriculture and breeding in the Neolithic and Copper Age; waterfalls feed a stream at its base.

  • Where Casoli, Camaiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com · cultura.gov.it

Casoli, il paese dei graffiti

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

A village known as the "land of graffiti": since 1950 artists have painted murals inspired by Rosario Murabito, with scenes of daily life, legends and local characters.

  • Where Casoli, Camaiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Castello di Montemagno

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

X century castle in Val Freddana, strategic observation point on the Via Francigena, together residence and defensive structure.

  • Where Montemagno, Camaiore
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Lido di Camaiore, passeggiata e pontile

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Beach resort of Versilia frequented from the beginning of the twentieth century, among others by Gabriele D'Annunzio, Eleonora Duse and Vittorio Emanuele III; the beach has the Blue Flag. The walk to the sea is about 3 km long and the modern pier ends in a roundabout of 700 m2, to be seen at sunset in summer.

  • Where Lido di Camaiore, Camaiore
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
