What to see in Leggiuno

Country of the Lombard shore of Lake Maggiore, whose name derives from the Celtic "leze dunum" at the foot of the hill, on a cliff above the lake stands the Hermitage of Santa Caterina del Sasso, while in the village remain the ancient churches of the Saints Primo and Feliciano and Santo Stefano, home of parishes at least from 846.

Eremo di Santa Caterina del Sasso

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Viewpoint

According to tradition, the merchant Alberto Besozzi, escaped a storm on the lake, in 1195 had a small church built in thanksgiving to the saint; the complex then became a monastery, inhabited over the centuries by various religious communities until its closure in 1770. It has been the property of the Province of Varese, it has been restored since 1970 and in 1992 frescoes from the fourteenth century have been re-emerged. It comprises three buildings: the southern convent with a long arched corridor on granite columns and the frescoed capitular hall, the convent with a portico with acute arches, refectory and cells, and the church with fourteenth-century bell tower and mural paintings around the altar. You go down from the square with a panoramic staircase of 268 steps or with the elevator; from the lake, arriving by boat, the steps are about 80.

  • Suggested time 2 hours or more

Chiesa dei Santi Primo e Feliciano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church in the center of the village, with Romanesque bell tower, founded before the ninth century by the family of the noble Eremberto, linked to the Emperor Lotario.

  • Where Via San Primo, Leggiuno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: leggiunoturismo.it · in-lombardia.it

Chiesa di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

In the upper part of the village, it is indicated among the oldest sacred buildings of Varesotto. Leggiuno is the first place to know a document with the term "plebs": at least since 846 it was the seat of the Pieve di Santo Stefano, a role maintained until 1965.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: leggiunoturismo.it · in-lombardia.it

Santuario di Santa Maria Stella Maris

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Viewpoint

Modern shrine, built in the second half of the twentieth century on the initiative of the local community in the hamlet of Reno, directly overlooking Lake Maggiore. It has a simple façade with a sailing bell tower; inside a statue of the Madonna and Child facing the lake, to protect fishermen and sailors. It is a destination of pilgrimages especially in May.

  • Where Frazione Reno, Leggiuno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: leggiunoturismo.it · in-lombardia.it

Spiagge di Reno e di Arolo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

The beach of Reno, sand and gravel, looks at the Borromeo Gulf and the Borromee Islands and has showers and picnic areas. Arolo's is a meadow with trees that give natural shade, clear water and panoramic view, with bars, showers and toilets and parking spaces along the lake and in the village; you swim there and dive from the nearby cliffs.

  • Where Frazioni Reno e Arolo, Leggiuno
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: leggiunoturismo.it · in-lombardia.it

Sentieri verso l'Eremo di Santa Caterina

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

The walk Cellina-Eremo has two easy and marked routes, of 1.1 and 2 km, suitable for families, with panoramic stops. For more experienced hikers there is the ring of Santa Caterina del Sasso, about 20 km and 400 m of difference in altitude in about 5 hours between woods, ancient mule tracks and small villages, and the AVLM 01 trail to the Refuge De Grande Adamoli on the Pizzoni di Laveno, about 14 km and over 1000 m of difference in altitude.

  • Where Partenza da Cellina o dall'Eremo, Leggiuno
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: leggiunoturismo.it · in-lombardia.it
