What to see in Volpedo

The most beautiful village in Italy, the birthplace of Giuseppe Pellizza, where the fourth state was born: its workshop and reproductions of the works next to the landscapes that inspired them.

5 places to visit in Volpedo, in the province of Alessandria (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Studio del pittore Pellizza da Volpedo

Museums · House museums

The atelier opened in 1888, when the painter decided to live and work in his native country, today's museum.

  • Entry To be checked

Percorso delle opere di Pellizza

Museums · Modern art · Free entry · Viewpoint

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Piazzetta del «Quarto Stato»

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

At the bottom of the Church, in the heart of the ancient castrum: here Pellizza painted between 1892 and 1901 some great social works with the peasants as models.

  • Where Via della Chiesa, Volpedo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page borghipiubelliditalia.it
