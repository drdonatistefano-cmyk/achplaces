What to see in Savigliano

City between the rivers Maira and Mellea, between Five and Six hundred candidate in the capital of the duchy of Sabaudo: the medieval Piazza Santa Rosa with the Civic Tower and the triumphal arch, the Palaces Taffini d'Acceglio and Muratori Cravetta, the Civic Museum with the Gipsoteca Calandra and the Piemontese Railway Museum.

9 places to visit in Savigliano, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza Santa Rosa

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The ancient Piazza Vecchia, considered one of the best preserved examples of medieval square in Italy: houses-towers of the Three and fifteenth century, porticos, the Civic Tower, the sixteenth-century triumphal arch, the ancient Civic Palace and the monument to the patriot Santorre of Santa Rosa. In a wrought iron newsstand there is the Tourist Office.

  • Where Piazza Santa Rosa, Savigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · comune.savigliano.cn.it · italia.it

Palazzo Taffini d'Acceglio e Múses - Accademia Europea delle Essenze

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry

Baroque palace built around 1630 for the Marquis Taffini d'Acceglio: the Aula Regia is frescoed with the military campaigns of Vittorio Amedeo I of Savoia by artists of the circle of Giovanni Antonio Molineri, including Jean Claret, and the Rococo salons were wanted by Isabella of Savoia-Carignano. Host Múses, a techno-sensory museum on Piedmontese aromatic herbs and the history of perfumery, with multisensory installations and works by contemporary artists. Open Thursday-Saturday 14-18, Sunday 11-13 and 14-18.

  • Where Via Sant'Andrea 53, Savigliano
  • Entry €12, reduced €8
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · comune.savigliano.cn.it · italia.it

Palazzo Muratori Cravetta

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Manierista palace, owned by the municipality, with courtyard with classical facades designed by the ducal architect Ercole Negri of Sanfront and Italian garden; in 1620 Prince Vittorio Amedeo of Savoia chose him as residence. It hosts temporary exhibitions and is connected to Palazzo Taffini through the courtyard.

  • Where Via Jerusalem, Savigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · italia.it

Museo civico Antonino Olmo e Gipsoteca Davide Calandra

Museums · Old masters

In the former seventeenth-century convent of San Francesco: the Civic Museum has sections of archaeology, ethnography, art and art gallery; the Gipsoteca is dedicated to the Turin sculptor Davide Calandra, with over one hundred works in plaster and terracotta, including the great cast of the frieze for the classroom of Montecitorio. Open Saturday 15-18.30, Sunday 10-13 and 15-18.30.

  • Where Via San Francesco 17, Savigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · comune.savigliano.cn.it · italia.it

Museo Ferroviario Piemontese

Museums · Themed

Founded in 1978, it has saved from demolition more than 60 rotabili between locomotives, automotors and carriages, on an external area of approximately 31,500 m2 around a rotating platform of 21 meters, with two sections covered of railway material, models and dioramas; exchanges and tracks are working. It recalls the connection with Turin of 1855 and the industrial railway tradition of the city. Open on weekends with seasonal hours.

  • Where Via Coloira 7, Savigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website museoferroviariopiemontese.it

Sources: museoferroviariopiemontese.it · visitcuneese.it · italia.it

Collegiata di Sant'Andrea

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

It preserves in the chapel of St. Nicholas pictorial cycles of the fourteenth century, among the oldest in the city, an altar of Juvarra and paintings of G.A. Sweet, Molineri and Claret.

  • Where Via Sant'Andrea, Savigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it · comune.savigliano.cn.it · italia.it

Ex abbazia di San Pietro dei Cassinesi

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

Centre of the religious buildings of the city, linked to the Benedictine origins of Savigliano: it houses a Roman plaque at the main altar, frescoes by G.A. Molineri in the choir and stalli and confessional carved.

  • Where Savigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · comune.savigliano.cn.it

Teatro Civico Milanollo

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Neoclassical theatre built in 1832 and titled at the end of the nineteenth century to the violinists Teresa and Maria Milanollo.

  • Where Savigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Sentiero sul Maira

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

A cycle and pedestrian route, the first stretch of which was opened in 1994 by volunteers and municipalities; a green network connects the center to the banks of Maira and to the countryside of villas and castles, with trekking and cycling routes towards Monviso.

  • Where Savigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitcuneese.it
