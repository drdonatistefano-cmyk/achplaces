What to see in Rezzato

Common at the gates of Brescia with the PINAC, collection of about 8,000 drawings of children from 79 countries born from the collection of Aldo Cibaldi, the sanctuary of the Madonna di Valverde (1602-1615) next to the Round of Santa Maria Vallis Viridis mentioned since 1024, and the Park of 18th century Bacchus on the Colle di San Pietro.

PInAC - Pinacoteca internazionale dell'età evolutiva Aldo Cibaldi

Museums · Themed

Born from the collection started by Aldo Cibaldi in the fifties and became civic at the end of the sixties, it conserves about 8,000 drawings of children and children from 3 to 14 years coming from 79 countries, exposed in rotation, with multimedia places to consult the works catalogued; it also welcomes digital works. It is located in the fifteenth-century church of the Disciplines, which preserves a fresco of the sixteenth century. Open Friday and Saturday 14-17, the other days by appointment; barrier-free.

  • Where Via Disciplina 60, Rezzato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website pinac.it

Sources: pinac.it · comune.rezzato.bs.it · touringclub.it

Santuario della Madonna di Valverde e Rotonda

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

It rises at the place of a Marian apparition of 1399; Bishop Marino Zorzi declared miraculous the image in 1601 and the sanctuary was built between 1602 and 1615, with facade and bell tower added in 1642 and altar of Giuseppe Cantoni (1718) with angels of Santo Callegari; it was jubilee church in 2016. Next to it, the round of Santa Maria Vallis Viridis, quoted from 1024 and similar to the round of the Old Cathedral of Brescia, with 16th century frescoes attributed to Pietro da Marone or his workshop. A tree-lined avenue among the gardens leads to the pond with the chapel of the Blessed Virgin to the Gorgo (1400), place of the apparitions according to tradition, surmounted by a large statue of the Madonna.

  • Where Via Santuario 75, Rezzato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rezzato.bs.it

Sources: visitbrescia.it · comune.rezzato.bs.it

Parco di Bacco

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry · Viewpoint

On the Colle di San Pietro, designed by Giovanni Battista Marchetti, it was built in 1775 at the behest of Count Fenaroli and has been public since the seventies; the temple was destroyed by thieves in 2001. The historic chapel of St. Peter is also located on the hill.

  • Where Colle di San Pietro, Rezzato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rezzato.bs.it

Sources: comune.rezzato.bs.it

Parrocchiale di San Giovanni Battista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The Romanesque bell tower dates back to the fourteenth century; the new church was started in 1631, finished in 1682 and consecrated on 5 November 1724. The main altar is by Vincenzo Baroncini (1735), the painting decorations by Luigi Morgari (1931).

  • Where Rezzato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rezzato.bs.it

Sources: comune.rezzato.bs.it

Chiesa e convento di San Pietro Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Founded in 1008 with a donation from Bishop Landolfo II, it became parish in 1299; rebuilt between 1734 and 1739 and consecrated in 1743, it has frescoes by Angelo Trainini in 1919 and a cloister restored in 2001.

  • Where Rezzato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rezzato.bs.it

Sources: comune.rezzato.bs.it

Cimitero di Rezzato

Places of worship · Monumental cemeteries · Free entry · Viewpoint

Designed by Rodolfo Vantini and realized by the company Gaffuri on the slope of a hill near the sanctuary of Valverde. Vantini also designed and built at his own expense the building of the Scuola Vantini (1857), today Palazzo Comunale.

  • Where Presso il santuario di Valverde, Rezzato
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rezzato.bs.it

Sources: comune.rezzato.bs.it

Villa Avogadro Fenaroli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Villa built by Scipione Avogadro at the end of the sixteenth century, renovated by Giovanni Battista Marchetti in 1731 and in the nineteenth century by Emilio Alemagna; purchased in 1947 by the Scalabrini Fathers, after the restoration completed in 2006 it became a hotel with conference center.

  • Where Rezzato
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.rezzato.bs.it

Sources: comune.rezzato.bs.it
