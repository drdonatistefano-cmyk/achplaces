What to see in Serra de' Conti

Medieval village of Val Misa with urban layout of the thirteenth century, remodelled between the late Middle Ages and the modern era and surrounded by walls. The monastery of Santa Maria Maddalena houses the Museum of the monastic arts, and in the center is the Camaldolese church of San Michele.

Museo delle arti monastiche «Le Stanze del Tempo Sospeso»

Museums · Themed

Set up in the monastery of Santa Maria Maddalena delle Clarisse, built in 1586 on the ruins of a previous monastery and enlarged in the eighteenth century; the annexed church, of 1603, has elliptical plan and decorations of Borrominian inspiration. Chiesa di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church built in stone in 1290 by the Camaldolese monks of the abbey of Santa Maria di Sitria and extensively renovated between 1442 and 1480: the lower part is Romanesque in stone, the upper Gothic in brick. He preserves works of the Marche school including a Madonna and Child in heaven between St Andrew and St Michael the Archangel (1581), a Madonna of Mercy between St Nicholas and a holy bishop (16th century) and a Sedes Sapientiae (1442). Open by day, free entrance.

  • Where Via Leone Amici, Serra de' Conti
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
