What to see in San Benedetto in Perillis

Small village of Aquila mentioned as "Perello" in a document dated 787, which grew around the church of San Benedetto Abate, a three-nave basilica of Longobard plant; the municipality also houses a museum of material culture.

Chiesa di San Benedetto Abate

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Church with a basilica plan with three naves, about 27-28 meters long, with six columns per side, which the Superintendence (Moretti) dates to the IX century and the Municipality classifies as a Longobard construction between VIII and IX century; it was the seat of a Benedictine preposition. The earthquakes of the Tre-Quattrocento brought down the three apses, around 1450 the orientation was reversed and in the eighteenth century came the Baroque covering and the bell tower. The façade has a walled loggia with a pre-Romanesque print and a portal with a carved Greek cross, with Roman materials of re-use; on the medieval apses of a cat with two heads and a lion, and on the walls fragments of fifteenth century frescoes.
