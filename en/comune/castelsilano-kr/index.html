What to see in Castelsilano

Small village of about 700 inhabitants in the heart of Sila became a village of art and colors.The old town is an open-air museum, with the murals of Francesco Candido on rural life and the works of ArCalabria on doors, windows and walls.

2 places to visit in Castelsilano, in the province of Crotone (Calabria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo della cultura contadina (murales)

Museums · Themed · Free entry

Open-air museum through the streets of the historic centre, born from the project "Old Calabria" inspired by the book of Norman Douglas and promoted by the Fondazione Napoli 99: ten murals tell about the work, customs, traditions and economy of the people of Castelsilano, painted by the artist Francesco Candido, born in the village and lived for a long time in Paris.

  • Where Centro storico, Castelsilano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fondoambiente.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · fondoambiente.it

Museo itinerante ArCalabria

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Museum circuit in the historic centre: works by local, national and international artists decorate doors, windows and walls of the village, with braille signs for blind visitors.

  • Where Strada Provinciale 27, 62, Castelsilano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fondoambiente.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it
