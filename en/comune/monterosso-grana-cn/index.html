What to see in Monterosso Grana

Country of the Valley Grana guardian of Provencal culture: in Santa Lucia (Sancto Lucio de Coumboscuro) the ethnographic museum of the Coumboscuro and the Roumiage de Setembre, in San Pietro the headquarters of the Ecomuseum Terra del Castelmagno with the \'babaciu' of straw, and in the woods the ring of the Sarvanot Path.

3 places to visit in Monterosso Grana, in the province of Cuneo (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo etnografico Coumboscuro della civiltà provenzale

Museums · Themed

In the hamlet of Santa Lucia (Sancto Lucio in Provencale), where since the 1950s the cultural association Coumboscuro has contributed to the rediscovery of Provencal culture in Italy; the museum presents local traditions and is considered one of the most interesting collections of the Alpine valleys. Every first week of September the village hosts the Roumiage de Setembre, preceded by the crossing of the Alps of the French neighbors.

Open only by reservation

  • Where Frazione Sancto Lucio de Coumboscuro, Monterosso Grana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.coumboscuro.org

Sources: coumboscuro.org · visitcuneese.it

Ecomuseo Terra del Castelmagno

Museums · Themed

Ecomuseum spread over the history and traditions of the Grana Valley linked to the cheese Castelmagno, based in San Pietro: the museum has an interactive set up on the processing and history of cheese, and in the country figures of straw in ancient clothes, the "babaciu" , tell the mountain life between the nineteenth and twentieth centuries. The route also includes mills, sawmills and hydroelectric power plants and the Extramuseum, a family ring of 4 km with ten totems (magic folder €10). Open Saturday and Sunday or by reservation.

  • Where Frazione San Pietro 89, Monterosso Grana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.terradelcastelmagno.it

Sources: terradelcastelmagno.it · visitcuneese.it

Sentiero del Sarvanot

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A teaching ring of about 5 km that crosses Monterosso, named after the Sarvanot, a mythological little creature that traditionally lives in the woods of the village. Along the route about fifty signs with local stories and news about the trees, and wooden benches in the panoramic points.

  • Where Monterosso Grana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitcuneese.it

Sources: visitcuneese.it
