What to see in Trivigliano

The village of Ciociano is on a hill at 850 m, in front of Torre Cajetani, whose name comes from the Latin "tres watchantes" (the three towers of which one remains); the historic center almost all pedestrian looks over Lake Canterno, regional nature reserve.

6 places to visit in Trivigliano, in the province of Frosinone (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Lago di Canterno

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Lake of karst origin in the heart of the Ernici Mountains, born from the progressive closure of two of the three swallows; about 1.7 km long and 700 m wide, is regional natural reserve of 1,824 hectares between Ferentino, Fiuggi, Fumone and Trivigliano. On the banks of herons, smooth and small, wide ducks and flares; towards Fumone there are chestnut woods. You can walk or bike on a cycle track, free and always open access.

  • Where Lago di Canterno, Trivigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: parchilazio.it · visitlazio.com

Grotta di Corniano

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Cave near the Lake Canterno, in front of the Sanctuary of the Madonna della Stella, with stalactites and stalagmites on the vault and more rooms connected by tunnels and narrow corridors; you reach it from the bike path of the lake.

the interior is accessible only to authorized speleologists, the public sees it from outside

  • Where Pista ciclopedonale del Lago di Canterno, Trivigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: comune.trivigliano.fr.it · visitlazio.com

Centro storico e Porta di Vicolo dello Spreco

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Historic center remained practically intact and almost all pedestrian, with alleys, arcades, portals, steps and panoramic squares. The Porta di Vicolo dello Spreco, between via Monte Santa Croce and via della Libertà, is the only remaining of the three original doors of Castrum; a stone staircase rises to the upper part of the village.

  • Where Via Monte Santa Croce, Trivigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · comune.trivigliano.fr.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta e Belvedere

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry · Viewpoint

The church is located on a square overlooking the valley and Lake Canterno, known as the "terrazza della Ciociaria" (terrace of the Ciociaria), opposite, in Piazza Roma, the panoramic viewpoint.

  • Where Piazza Roma, Trivigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · comune.trivigliano.fr.it

Chiesa di Santa Oliva

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Under the presbytery of the upper church there is the crypt dedicated to the patron saint Sant'Oliva, with in a niche above the altar an ancient statue of the saint that, by tradition, cannot be moved heavy hailstorms on the crops. In June, the feast brings in the night procession the Braccio di Santa Oliva, an arm-shaped relic with an olive branch.

  • Where Trivigliano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Museo della Civiltà contadina

Museums · Themed

Permanent exhibition on folk crafts and traditions with tools of the past and old methods of agricultural work and food preparation, in a building at the entrance to the historic center overlooking Belvedere square.

the MiC card (updated to December 2020) is closed every day; information to the Municipality, tel. 0775 520213

  • Where Piazza del Belvedere, Trivigliano
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: cultura.gov.it · visitlazio.com
