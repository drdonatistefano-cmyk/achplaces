What to see in Bevagna

The ancient Roman Mevania became a medieval village: Piazza Silvestri with the two Romanesque churches and the Palazzo dei Consoli that houses the Torti theatre.

4 places to visit in Bevagna, in the province of Perugia (Umbria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Piazza Silvestri

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Space without symmetry and frontal alignments, one of the most interesting medieval urbanistic achievements of the region.

  • Where Piazza Filippo Silvestri, Bevagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it · umbriatourism.it

Chiesa di San Silvestro

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Overlooking Piazza Silvestri, it is considered a jewel of Umbrian Romanesque architecture.

  • Where Piazza Filippo Silvestri, Bevagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: touringclub.it · umbriatourism.it

Collegiata di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Romanesque church of the XII-XIII centuries with magnificent portal and bell tower cuspidate; restored between 1951 and 1957, has recovered the large circular rose window.

  • Where Piazza Filippo Silvestri, Bevagna
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it · umbriatourism.it

Palazzo dei Consoli e Teatro Torti

History and architecture · Historic theatres

The palace of 1270 on Piazza Silvestri, today seat of the theatre Francesco Torti.

  • Where Piazza Filippo Silvestri, Bevagna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.umbriatourism.it

Sources: umbriatourism.it · touringclub.it
