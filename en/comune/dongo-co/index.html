What to see in Dongo

Almost to the headboard of Lake Como, at the mouth of the Albano stream, Dongo is linked to April 1945, when Benito Mussolini was captured there: the Museum of the End of War in the Napoleonic Palazzo Manzi, seat of the Town Hall. Also worth seeing is the sixteenth-century shrine of Our Lady of Tears with the library of the Franciscan monastery, and behind the Albano Valley, an ancient road to the Passo di San Jorio.

Museo della Fine della Guerra

Museums · City history

On the ground floor of Palazzo Manzi, seat of the Town Hall, a multimedia and immersive tale, with audio guides and vintage films, on the last phases of the Second World War, on the Resistance and the end of fascism, with the capture of Mussolini in Dongo and the shooting of hierarches on the lakefront in April 1945. In summer Pro Loco organizes guided tours to the museum and the village (2 hours, €15, reduced €7.50, free up to 13 years, with aperitif, upon reservation).

  • Where Piazza Paracchini 6, Dongo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Palazzo Manzi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Palace of the Napoleonic era, now seat of the Town Hall, indicated as one of the most beautiful of the Upper Lake: it preserves the Golden Hall, the Chapel of the Immaculate and an ancient library with shelves in walnut.

  • Where Piazza Paracchini 6, Dongo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Santuario della Madonna delle Lacrime e convento francescano

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

A sixteenth-century complex of shrine, convent and bell tower, with frescoes, paintings and wooden statues and a life of travagliata in the last centuries. The library of the convent contains about 30,000 volumes, including medieval illuminated codes, five hundred and six hundred, escaped from Napoleonic and post-unitary dispoliations; the convent also has an internal garden.

  • Where Via delle Rimembranze 8, Dongo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · fondoambiente.it

Valle Albano e Via dei Monti Lariani

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Behind Dongo is the Albano Valley, already in ancient way towards northern Europe through the Passo di San Jorio (2014 m), towards the Val Mesolcina. It crosses the Via dei Monti Lariani, which runs for 46 km from the Albano Valley to Sorico for the Valle di San Jorio and the Valle del Dosso, touching ancient nuclei once used as alpine pastures.

  • Where Valle Albano, Dongo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it
