Museo archeologico e paleobotanico

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the former premises of the livestock market, it collects the finds of the territory from the Paleolithic to the Middle Ages: the vegetable fossils of the petrified forests of the Anglona, the oldest stone instruments found so far in Sardinia, the statue of the mother Reduced for groups of at least 20 people, schools and over 60.

  • Where Via Nazario Sauro, Perfugas
  • Entry €3, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it

Santa Maria degli Angeli e Museo diocesano d'arte sacra

Museums · Old masters

The parish church, with a basilica plan, was built in the early seventeenth century and has been renovated several times; tradition links its origins to the lordship of the Dorias (XIV century). In one of its chapels, the diocesan museum houses the retablo of San Giorgio, the largest in Sardinia, with 54 plates in 14 separable sections painted at the end of the sixteenth century by the anonymous Master of Perfugas, together with a fourteenth-century Madonna, a 16th-century crucifix, a reliquary cross and wooden carvings, silver, remains of a Sardinian female costume of the seventeenth-seventh-century and, from the archive, the parchment of the consecration of Santa Vittoria of 1120 and the fourteenth-century one which appoints Bishop Nicolaus Galuleiu, the oldest known of the diocese of Ampurias.

  • Where Via Garibaldi 29, Perfugas
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Pozzo sacro di Predio Canopoli

History and architecture · Archaeology

Nuragic well temple in the center, discovered in 1924 by the owner of the land, dated according to the scholars between the middle and the final Bronze: limestone cones laid dry, vestibule with counters-seats, eight steps staircase and circular chamber with trunk of cone, without the roof to tholos; the style recalls the well of Santa Cristina in Paulilatino. Around, traces of a trachyte fence on a temple in antis and a village; the bull bronze found here is at the Sanna Museum in Sassari.

  • Where Centro storico, Perfugas
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · idese.cultura.gov.it

Chiesa di San Giorgio martire

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Church in red volcanite in Gothic-Catalan style of the early sixteenth century, in panoramic position in front of a nuraghe; until the nineteenth century it was called San Giorgio de Ledda and was the original seat of the great retablo. The feast of the patron saint is celebrated in late April.

  • Where Perfugas
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria de Foras

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of 1160, on the outskirts of the village, which was the parish church of Perfugas until the seventeenth century, when Santa Maria degli Angeli replaced it.

  • Where Periferia, Perfugas
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it

Foresta pietrificata di Carrucana

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites · Free entry

Pietrified logs with a trunk-conic shape of the Miocene, preserved by silicization: according to a hypothesis an eruption of about 20 million years ago caused the forests to sink into the lakes that covered most of the Anglona. Public municipal good bound since 1979, in good state of conservation.

  • Where Località Carrucana, Perfugas
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.sardegnaturismo.it

Sources: beniabbandonati.cultura.gov.it · sardegnaturismo.it
