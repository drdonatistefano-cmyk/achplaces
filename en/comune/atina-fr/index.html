What to see in Atina

Ancient town in the Comino Valley surrounded by polygonal walls of the 4th century BC, with the Ducal Palace of the Cantelmus and the frescoed chapel of Sant'Onofrio, the Cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta, the Archaeological Museum G. Visocchi and the hill of Santo Stefano.

Palazzo Ducale dei Cantelmo e cappella di Sant'Onofrio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built by the Cantelmo after the earthquake of 1349, in Gothic style, in the highest point of the village, with windows double-fore and Gothic portal; it preserves a mosaic of the II century A.D. and the chapel of Sant'Onofrio with frescoes of the XIV century. From 1655 the hall became theater, active until 1939; today it is the seat of the City Hall, Historical House of Lazio, and hosts exhibitions and events.

  • Where Piazza Saturno, Atina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Museo archeologico di Atina e della Valle di Comino «G. Visocchi»

Museums · Archaeological · Free entry

Civic museum in a building in classical style of the historic center, with finds from the 8th century BC to the late Middle Ages from Atina and the valley: in five rooms the Roman funeral lion, furnishings of the pre-Roman necropolis of Via dei Sanniti (2002) and Monte Santa Croce in San Biagio Saracinisco (VII-III century BC), models of Roman and Sanniti armaments, ex-vote of the Italian shrine of Casale Pescarolo, coins and lapidarium.

  • Where Via Vittorio Emanuele, Atina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.atina.fr.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Mura poligonali e Porta dell'Arco

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

The polygonal walls of the 4th century B.C. develop for about 6 km and are among the largest in the italic world; the monumental Gate of the Arch was one of the three main gates of the ancient city and introduces to the historical center, where you can also see Roman tombs and inscriptions re-used in the buildings.

  • Where Centro storico, Atina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it · visitlazio.com

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals

Built between 1725 and 1746 above the early early Christian cathedral and perhaps on the remains of the temple of Saturn; the Baroque interior has paintings of Neapolitan school, a wooden baroque baptistery and an eighteenth-century organ still working. Next is the Bishop's Palace.

  • Where Centro storico, Atina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlazio.com

Piazza Garibaldi, Convento di San Francesco e cisterna romana

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The square houses the Fontanone, the Roman cistern of the III-I century BC, which within the walls guaranteed water even during a siege, and the Convent of St. Francis of 1630 with cloister, church and portico with four arches, historical residence restored in 1984.

  • Where Piazza Garibaldi, Atina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Palazzo Visocchi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

18th century neoclassical building with a T-shaped plan, with a private chapel dedicated to Our Lady of Loreto and a dining room with French wallpaper from the early 19th century of the Lefevre paper mill of Isola del Liri.
