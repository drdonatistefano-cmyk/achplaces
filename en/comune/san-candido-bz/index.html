What to see in San Candido

Borgo dell'Alta Pusteria was born from the Benedictine monastery founded in the 8th century by the dukes of Bavaria, with the Romanesque Collegiate, considered the most important Romanesque sacred building in the Eastern Alps, the Museum of the Collegiate (MIK), the Holy Sepulchre seventeenth century and the DoloMythos museum in Villa Wachtler; above the village the Rocca dei Baranci with chairlift and summer sled.

Collegiata dei Santi Candido e Corbiniano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Begin around 1143, when the Benedictine monastery founded in the eighth century by the Bavarian duke Tassilo III became a college of canons; it reached almost the present form around 1280, the bell tower was erected between 1320 and 1326 and in 1969 the church was restored to the form of 1250. Three naves with transept and dome at the crossroads, it preserves the fresco of the dome with Creation, the Crucifixion group of about 1250, the crypt and, on the southern portal, a fresco by Michael Pacher of about 1450.

  • Where Piazza del Magistrato 1, San Candido
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

MIK - Museo della Collegiata

Museums · Old masters · Paid entry

In the ancient capitular house tells the history of the church and the village: sacred art, treasure of the Collegiate with the cross of crystal, sculptures, paintings and frescoes, precious fabrics, a library with ancient manuscripts and Celtic and Roman finds. Open from June to September, Tuesday-Saturday 14-19; guided tours on Saturday.

  • Where Via Atto 2, San Candido
  • Entry €5, children up to 14 years free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: mik.bz.it · italia.it

Santo Sepolcro (Außerkirchl)

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Wanted by the host Georg Paprion after the pilgrimages to the Holy Land: in 1633 the round chapel on the model of the Chapel of the Graces of Altötting, then the aisle of the Passion and in 1653 the chapel of the Holy Sepulchre, a copy of that of Jerusalem, a polygonal roundabout with dome where the light falls from above. The statues come from the workshop of the local sculptor Matthias Schranzhofer and are among the most important works of local folk art. It is part of the MIK route.

  • Where San Candido
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: mik.bz.it · italia.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Michele

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in the 12th century as a Romanesque church for the population, in 1735 it was transformed into a bright late Baroque and Rococo ambience; the Romanesque round bell tower has an onion dome, a spiral façade and statues. The frescoes of the vault, by the Tyrolean baroque painter Christoph Anton Mayr, show theological allegories and scenes of the life of St. Michael.

  • Where San Candido
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: mik.bz.it · dreizinnen.com · italia.it

Chiesa e chiostro dei Francescani

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Church deliberately stripped, consecrated in 1697 on the project of the friar Vitus Rastbichler; in the cloister the cycle of St. Francis, 31 large tables with 72 scenes painted by Lukas Platzer between 1706 and 1709. The main altarpiece of Christoph Unterberger, statues of Johann Pater and Andreas Fasching, was restored in 1992 and received the Europa Nostra Prize in 1994.

  • Where San Candido
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: mik.bz.it · italia.it

DoloMythos a Villa Wachtler

Museums · Science and university

Interactive museum in the historic Villa Wachtler on the birth and evolution of the Dolomites: myths and legends, ancient fauna such as the Megachirella wachtleri, ancestor of snakes and lizards, evolution of plants, cave bears and a section with original dioramas and skeletons of dinosaurs. All year round you can try to look for gold, crystals and fossils; activities for children and boys.

  • Where Via P.-P.-Rainer 11, San Candido
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it

Bagni di San Candido e cappella di San Salvatore

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

Sources probably used already in the Middle Ages, with traces of a spa since the 16th century; in 1856 Dr Johann Scheiber built a sanatorium there, which became the Grand Hotel Wildbad at the beginning of the 20th century, at auction in 1939 and then abandoned for a long time. The chapel of San Salvatore, consecrated in 1594, is only reached on foot in about 45 minutes from the village, starting from the parking lot of the chairlift of the Rocca dei Baranci.

  • Where Bagni di San Candido, San Candido
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: dreizinnen.com · italia.it

Rocca dei Baranci (Haunold)

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

The mountain of the legends of the giant Baranci: a chairlift climbs to the summit, where there are panoramic paths and for families, the ring path In winter easy slopes and one of the longest toboggan runs in South Tyrol.

  • Where Rocca dei Baranci, San Candido
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · dreizinnen.com

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Nicolò a Prato alla Drava

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Viewpoint

Cited in 1507, it probably dates back to a pre-Romanesque room of the twelfth century; gothized and enlarged around 1500, it has altars, pulpit and confessionals of the early nineteenth century in rural Baroque style. Of the original decoration remain the Coronation of Mary and the legend of St. Nicholas; on the ascent, a Way of the Cross with four newsstands and wooden figures of about 1700. It's on a panoramic hill outside the country.

  • Where Prato alla Drava, San Candido
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: mik.bz.it · italia.it
