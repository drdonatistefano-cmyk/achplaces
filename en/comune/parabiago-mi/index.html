What to see in Parabiago

City of Alto Milanese linked to the battle of 1339, remembered by the church of Sant'Ambrogio della Vittoria, and the cabinetmaker Giuseppe Maggiolini; in the center the square with the parish church from the façade of Piermarini and Villa Maggi Corvini, on the borders the sanctuary of the Madonna of God 'l Sa.

Chiesa di Sant'Ambrogio della Vittoria

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Azzone Visconti had it built in memory of the victory in the battle of Parabiago on 21 February 1339; entrusted in 1481 to the Order of St. Ambrose ad nemus, had a Cistercian monastery next to him since 1699-1705, then became a school. The current church, rebuilt and consecrated on 19 April 1713, has a bell tower of about 40 meters of 1723 and the organ of 1716 B. Reina with C-carvings. Bossi; preserves canvases by G.B. Sassi and Pietro da Pietra, a altarpiece of the Lampugnani di Legnano, frescoes by T. Formentini and a Gothic inscription in the choir.

Santuario della Madonna di Dio 'l Sa

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

It rises on the road to Nerviano on the remains of an ancient oratory of the country, of which there are still frescoes; tradition attributes it, without confirmation, to Bramante or his pupil. He lived the greatest splendor in the seventeenth century and since 1914 he has been a National Monument. At the altar there is a polyptych from the school of Luinesca with the Virgin with the Child and saints, an eight-faced gallery and seventeenth-century statues of saints inside and outside.

  • Where Strada per Nerviano, Parabiago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page ecomuseo.comune.parabiago.mi.it

Sources: ecomuseo.comune.parabiago.mi.it

Chiesa dei Santi Gervaso e Protaso

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The large square dominates: built from 1610 by order of San Carlo Borromeo on a project originally designed by Pellegrino Pellegrini, it has the façade designed in 1780 by Giuseppe Piermarini, who also designed the square and its trees, covered in travertine with statues in 1951. Inside stuccoes, paintings and frescoes in the vault and sides of Leoni, the Rusca and Levati, called in 1780 thanks to Giuseppe Maggiolini.

  • Where Piazza Maggiolini, Parabiago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page ecomuseo.comune.parabiago.mi.it

Sources: ecomuseo.comune.parabiago.mi.it

Villa Maggi Corvini

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Properties of the Maggi since 1609, which in 1727 obtained from Pope Benedict XIII the private oratory in the hall then called "the columns" (the columns), in 1899 the landscape painter Giovanni Corvini bought it, who rebuilt a wing and decorated it with classical themes. Since 1975 it is part of the Municipality, restored in 1997-1999 and bound since 1973; the Salone delle colonne is intended for art gallery, with the park of the villa.

  • Where Via Santa Maria, Parabiago
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page ecomuseo.comune.parabiago.mi.it

Sources: ecomuseo.comune.parabiago.mi.it

Museo storico-culturale «Carla Musazzi»

Museums · City history · Free entry

Opened on 16 September 1988 and reopened on 31 March 2012 with mainly didactic purposes, it is divided into three rooms: archaeological excavations, battle of Parabiago and great wars; life and activities of the inlayer Giuseppe Maggiolini with some relics; room of curiosities with small collections and fossils. The source indicated the opening on Sunday 15-18.

  • Where Via Randaccio 11, Parabiago
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page ecomuseo.comune.parabiago.mi.it

Sources: ecomuseo.comune.parabiago.mi.it
