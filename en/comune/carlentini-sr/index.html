What to see in Carlentini

Municipality of the Iblei near Lake Lentini, stop of the European Path E1 and land of red oranges; on the hills shared with Lentini stood Leontinoi, a calcidese colony of 729 BC, of which in the territory of Carlentini remain the South Gate and the fortifications; in the municipality there is also the frescoed Byzantine chapel of Santa Margherita.

Area archeologica di Leontinoi

History and architecture · Archaeology

On the hills between Lentini and Carlentini, inhabited by the Neolithic at the Iron Age, the Calcidesi founded in 729 BC. Leontinoi, one of the oldest Greek colonies in Sicily: allied with Athens in 433 BC, fortified by Dionysius in 405 BC, occupied by the Romans from 214 BC and then in decline. In the territory of Carlentini remains the monumental remains of the South Gate with the adjacent fortifications; the North Gate is being excavated with the University of Catania. Open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 9-14, Wednesday 9-17.30, Saturday and first Sunday of the month 9-13.

Chiesa di Santa Margherita

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Chapel of Orthodox rite of medieval times (the FAI card the date to the XI-XII century), with the interior entirely frescoed by a succession of panels depicting icons of saints. Style and iconography are compared to the great Norman music cycles of Monreale, Palermo and Cefalù, and the frescoes are probably the work of Byzantine masters: together with the other rock churches in the area are an important example of Byzantine painting in Western Europe.
