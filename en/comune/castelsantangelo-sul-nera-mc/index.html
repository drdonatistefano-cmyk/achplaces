What to see in Castelsantangelo sul Nera

Borgo dei Monti Sibillini founded around 1300 as Castrum Sancti Angeli, a triangular plan with tower, walls and doors, near the springs of the Black and the foot of Mount Prata; it preserves fourteenth-century churches like San Martino and Santo Stefano and houses the center of the Park dedicated to the deer. The village was hit by the 2016 earthquake.

Borgo medievale, mura e porte

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Castrum Sancti Angels was born around 1300, when the people of the valley floor of the Black gathered there to defend themselves from the raids, under the protection of the archangel Michael: triangular plant, square tower (turris capitis), walls with stretches of battlements and the gates Castello, Sant'Angelo and Nocrina, the latter with double arch.

Chiesa di San Martino dei Gualdesi

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church of the fourteenth century with portal with pillars and bell tower with double windows; the interior with a single nave is rich in Umbrian frescoes, some attributed to Paolo Bontulli from Percanestro, and the main altar is under an ogival canopy.

Monastero di San Liberatore

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Complex built on the ruins of a thirteenth-century castle, made of vaults and narrow corridors in living stone; the chapel preserves paintings of the Umbrian school of the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries and numerous frescoes, and the name would come from the Christ frescoed in the apse.

Centro visita ed Ecomuseo del Cervo, area faunistica

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves

Centre of the Sibillini Mountains National Park with an exhibition area in diorami and a fauna area fenced of about 29 hectares on the south side of Mount Cornaccione, between Castelsantangelo and Vallinfante, where deer and roe deer live in semi-freedom and are observed by turrets; the center supports the re-introduction of the deer in the Park. Guided excursions upon reservation (339 6039429).
