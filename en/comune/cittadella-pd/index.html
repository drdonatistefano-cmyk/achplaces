What to see in Cittadella

Founded by Padua in 1220 and passed to Venice in 1405, Cittadella is closed by an elliptical medieval wall with a round walk entirely accessible; inside the walls the Tower of Malta remembered by Dante, the Duomo with its museum, the Pretorio Palace, the Social Theatre and the Oratory of the Savior.

10 places to visit in Cittadella, in the province of Padua (Veneto). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Mura di Cittadella e Camminamento di Ronda

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

A medieval wall of elliptical shape with original moat and towers of the gates up to 30 meters high; the walkway of the round, 15 meters high and almost two kilometers long, has been secured by a recent restoration and is entirely accessible. Along the tour you will find the Captain's House and the Tower of Malta, with views of Euganean Hills, Berici Mountains, Pedemontana and Monte Grappa. Hours: April-September 9-18, March and October 9-17, November-February 9-16; closed on 25 December.

  • Where Via Porte Bassanesi 2 (Porta Bassano, Casa del Capitano), Cittadella
  • Entry €5, reduced €3, family €14
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website muradicittadella.it

Sources: muradicittadella.it · italia.it

Torre di Malta e Museo Civico Archeologico

Museums · Archaeological · Viewpoint

She was put on inside Porta Padova and built by Ezzelino III by Romano (the municipal card indicates 1251) as a prison for the enemies, and Dante remembers her in the IX canto del Paradiso. The large rooms house the Archaeological Civic Museum, with finds from the Bronze Age to the Renaissance found in the territory, and the Museum of the Assedio; at the top a view almost 30 meters high. Opens at the same times as the round walk.

  • Where Via del Cristo 41, Cittadella
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.cittadella.pd.it · italia.it
