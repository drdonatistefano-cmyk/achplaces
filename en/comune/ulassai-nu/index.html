What to see in Ulassai

Borgo-museo dell'Ogliastra about 800 meters, at the foot of the limestone Tacchi, the birthplace of Maria Lai: her works at the Stazione dell'Arte and at the Washer, the cave of Su Marmuri, the waterfalls of Lequarci and Lecorci and the lakes of Santa Barbara.

Stazione dell'Arte, Museo Maria Lai

Museums · Modern art · Free entry

Born in 2006 in the former railway station of the line Gairo-Jerzu (in use until 1956) and in three restored houses, it houses about 150 works donated to the village by Maria Lai, born in Ulassai and died in 2013: ceramics, sewn books, frames, canvases and terracotta. Lavatoio di Ulassai

Museums · Modern art

Wash-basin built between 1903 and 1905, where women washed wool: a rectangular block with three arched openings framed in granite and two rows of tanks. On the proposal of Maria Lai it has become a small museum: the frame on the ceiling of the same Lai (1982), the fountain that sounds "by Costantino Nivola (1987), the fountain of the wheat" by Guido Strazza (1989) with the mosaic of the churchyard and "The fountain of the spring" by Luigi Veronesi. From the wash room starts the ring of Sa Tappara, just over 2 km.

  • Where Centro abitato, Ulassai
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Grotta di Su Marmuri

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Cave formed about 150 million years ago, with entrance to 880 meters and about 850 meters visitable, at a constant temperature of about 10 °C; walls high in some stretches about 70 meters. Cross the Great Hall (72 for 30 meters), the Organ Room, the Cactus Room with two stalagmites of about 20 meters, the Bat Room and the bowl gallery, with two underground ponds. The visit lasts about an hour on a fairly easy path, but to get to the bottom you need hundreds of steps; entrance fee.

  • Where A circa 1 km dal centro, strada asfaltata tra le gole, Ulassai
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website grottasumarmuri.it

Sources: grottasumarmuri.it · sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Cascate di Lequarci e Lecorci

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Viewpoint

Lequarci, in the locality of Santa Barbara, is described as the largest waterfall of the island: a jump of almost 100 meters, wide up to 70, then another 75 meters of descent towards numerous ponds; it is formed especially in the periods of flood. The waterfalls of Lecorci, about half a kilometer from the village under the cave of Su Marmuri, are born from the underground waters that emerge from the caves and join in Lequarci after about 3 km. You can go up the river Lequarci to the top; at the entrance some services are paid.

  • Where Località Santa Barbara, SP 11 (circa 5,6 km), Ulassai
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Santa Barbara, laghetti e chiesetta

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The area of ponds fed by the waterfalls, in the woods, with a monumental forest, a Byzantine-Romanesque church and the "is cumbessias," accommodation for pilgrims. At the end of May the feast of the saint with procession, launeddas, folk groups, festival and poetic competition; leads there the "strada del rito" with works of Maria Lai.

  • Where Località Santa Barbara, Ulassai
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it

Nuraghe s'Ulimu

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

Nuraghe of 1500-900 BC, about 700 meters away, with two massive towers joined by a thick wall curtain. Nearby eight domus de Janas, a dolmen, three tombs of Giants and three Nuragheddu villages (Nurageddu, the fortress of Seroni and the megalithic village of Seddorrulu, of the third millennium BC).

  • Where Territorio di Ulassai
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sardegnaturismo.it · italia.it
