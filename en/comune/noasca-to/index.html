What to see in Noasca

Country of the Orco Valley in the Gran Paradiso National Park, at the foot of the Noaschetta waterfall, about 32 meters high. In the town hall there is the visitor center of the Park dedicated to the shapes of the landscape; above the village the ring of the high hamlets leads to the waterfall of the Roc, and in about 3 hours on foot you go up to the royal hunting house of the Gran Piano.

Centro visitatori del Parco «Le forme del paesaggio»

Museums · Science and university · Free entry

Permanent exhibition on the geomorphology of the Park: the natural forces that shape the environment, the composition and degradation of the rocks, the evolution of the Alps and the glacial landscape of the valley. Space also to the imprint of man (mines, stone in mountain architecture, water control) and to the bond with ibex, chamois, marmot and eagle; a plastic shows the rocks of the Park, a documentary project hall shot in the Park and a comic book path for children is guided by the Stamby ibex. Tel. 0124 901070.

  • Where Municipio, Via Umberto I, Noasca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.pngp.it

Sources: pngp.it

Cascata di Noaschetta

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

Jump of about 32 meters formed by the stream Noaschetta, right above the center of the village. A signposted and easy path starts from the parish church and in about 15 minutes passes behind the waterfall, under a cave, from where you can observe the jump from a privileged position. Accessible all year round.

  • Where Frazione La Monin, Noasca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.pngp.it

Sources: comune.noasca.to.it · pngp.it

Via delle Frazioni alte e cascata del Roc

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hiking ring (difficulty E, about 500 m of difference in altitude, from 2 to 3 and a half hours, recommended from April to October) that from Balmarossa climbs to Crestet, Varda and Maison to the church of San Luigi (1582 m), then to Mola, Cappelle and Potes, from which you can admire the waterfall of the Roc stream; beyond the bridge, Borgo Vecchio with the church of Sant'Antonio (1534 m), then the descent to the ruined villages of Ertà and Fregai up to Pianchette. Since 2000 the Park Authority has started the rehabilitation of the hamlets, rebuilding scenes of daily life like the school of Maison; around chestnuts, cherry trees, birch trees and a beech wood.

  • Where Partenza dal parcheggio di Balmarossa sulla SP 460, Noasca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.pngp.it

Sources: comune.noasca.to.it · turismotorino.org · pngp.it

Casa reale di caccia del Gran Piano

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

From Noasca a route of about 3 hours leads to the royal hunting house of the Gran Piano, restored by the Park. The Gran Piano is a quiet area where you can see chamois and ibex grazing.

  • Where Gran Piano, a piedi da Noasca
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.pngp.it

Sources: pngp.it

Parrocchiale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The parish church rises just above the village, almost close to the waterfall of Noaschetta; you arrive there with a staircase and a pedestrian ramp, and it is the starting point of the path to the waterfall.

  • Where Corso Umberto I, Noasca
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.pngp.it

Sources: comune.noasca.to.it · pngp.it
