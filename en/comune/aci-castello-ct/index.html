What to see in Aci Castello

Municipality of the Riviera dei Ciclopi north of Catania, dominated by the 11th century lava stone castle that houses the civic museum; the seaside village of Aci Trezza, made famous by the Malavoglia di Verga, preserves the House of Nespolo and looks at the Faraglioni and the Lachea Island, nature reserve and marine protected area of the Cyclops Islands.

Castello normanno e Museo civico

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Built in the 11th century in lava stone on a rocky spur overlooking the sea, with panoramic terrace on the coast and on the Faraglioni of Aci Trezza. It houses the Civic Museum, inaugurated in 1985, with an archaeological collection from prehistory to the Middle Ages, a mineralogical one with minerals of the Cyclops and Aci Castello islands (analcimes, phillipsite, aracicular aragonite) and volcanic origin, and a paleontological one with fossils from Mesozoic to Pleistocene, among which ammonites.

Museo «Casa del Nespolo»

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

In a typical Sicilian house of the mid-nineteenth century in the historic center of Aci Trezza, next to the church of San Giovanni, with courtyard, small vegetable garden and entrance to lava stone arch. The hall .The earth trembles ... collects photos and posters of the film by Luchino Visconti shot in Aci Trezza with actors from the village; the Room of the Malavoglia documents the world of fishermen of the mid-nineteenth century with tools, everyday objects, photos taken by Giovanni Verga and letters to his brother Pietro.

  • Where Via Arciprete De Maria, Aci Trezza, Aci Castello
  • Entry €1,50
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Official website www.visitsicily.info

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it · visitsicily.info

Riserva naturale Isola Lachea e Faraglioni dei Ciclopi

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Viewpoint

An integral natural reserve established in 1998 and managed by the University of Catania: volcanic islets of about 500,000 years ago, linked to pre-ethnic volcanism, among white marl, clay and black basalts, with the columnar basalts of the Faraglione Grande and the arc of marna .Occhio del Ciclope on the Lachea Island, about 400 meters from the coast. There lives the endemic Lizard Podarcis siculus ciclopica; on the top of Lachea the museum of the Stazione Studi sul Mare. According to the myth the Faraglioni are the boulders hurled by Polyphemus against Ulysses. Access to the island only with authorized guided routes.

Area marina protetta Isole Ciclopi

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Viewpoint

It includes the Lachea Island, the Faraglione Grande, the Faraglione Piccolo and four other cliffs arranged in arch, with hundreds of species of algae, prairies of posidonia, saraghi, octopus, starfish and sponges; it is explored with snorkeling and diving or by boat, even with transparent background or with small gozzos of fishermen of Aci Trezza, appreciated at sunset.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: italia.it · visitsicily.info

Borgo marinaro di Aci Trezza

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The fishing village made famous by the Malavoglia di Giovanni Verga preserves its marina identity in the marina with colored boats and nets, with the seafront between clubs and terraces on the sea, the Baroque church of San Giovanni Battista and the fish market; for the patron is held the feast "U pisci a mari" (Pisci a Mari), which recalls the fishing of the swordfish.

  • Where Aci Trezza, Aci Castello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it

Scogliere laviche e lungomare di Aci Castello

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Black basalt cliff shaped by the eruptions of Etna and the waves of the Ionian Sea, suitable for baths and snorkeling, along a coast of 12 kilometers between Aci Castello and Aci Trezza; the promenade is recommended for walks at sunset.

  • Where Lungomare, Aci Castello
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitsicily.info

Sources: visitsicily.info · italia.it
