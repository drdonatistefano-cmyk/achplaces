What to see in Dalmine

Born in 1927 from the union of Sforzatica, Mariano and Sabbio, Dalmine is an industrial city grown around the seamless tube plant of 1906: the company town designed mostly by Giovanni Greppi from the 1920s, the anti-aircraft bunker of 1943 reopened to the public and a museum with over 900 nativity scenes.

Museo del Presepio

Museums · Themed

Born in the seventies on the initiative of Fr Giacomo Piazzoli, parish priest of Brembo, from a first collection of about 250 nativity scenes, today it preserves more than 900 on two floors, from the eighteenth century to contemporary artists, in a former cinema. The tour includes the great work of the Neapolitan Antonio Greco (more than 20 m2 and more than 130 statues), the reconstruction of a Bergamo workshop of late nineteenth century of plaster statues, handicraft nativity scenes of Asia, Africa, Latin America and Australia, paper nativity scenes, antique prints and finds of the Holy Land; a small room houses an electronic scenery with lights and music. Visits by reservation (tel. 035 563383).

Company town di Dalmine

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Around the Mannesmann plant in 1906 the company built since 1924, largely on the project of Giovanni Greppi, neighborhoods for workers and employees, sports facilities and a new center in front of the factories. Here are the church of St. Joseph, inaugurated on March 19, 1931 and donated to the parish, and the civic nucleus of 1938 designed by Greppi with the seat of the City, the former Casa del Fascio, the after-work company and the flag-bearer auction, a single seamless pipe produced in Dalmine and symbol of the city. The Dalmine Foundation organizes guided tours of the industrial city.

Bunker antiaereo del Quartiere Garbagni

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

It is one of the two anti-aircraft shelters built in Dalmine in 1943: excavated at 20 meters deep, it was reopened to the public recently.
