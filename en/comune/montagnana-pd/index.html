What to see in Montagnana

Walled city of the Paduan plain, south of the Euganean Hills, with one of the best preserved medieval surroundings in the world: almost two kilometers of fourteenth-century walls with 24 towers, the Castel San Zeno with the civic museum, the Rocca degli Alberi and the Duomo with the Transfiguration of Paolo Veronese.

Mura carraresi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

It is surrounded by a meadow moat and was strengthened by the Carraresi, who erected the current circle in 1360-62. It is about 1950 metres long in Euganean trachite and terracotta with 24 hexagonal towers up to 19 meters high and four doors. The best view is from the northeast, where Giorgione designed the city.

  • Where Montagnana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Museo civico Antonio Giacomelli e Mastio di Ezzelino

Museums · City history · Paid entry · Viewpoint

In Castel San Zeno, the oldest part of the fortifications (1242), the museum has archaeological sections from prehistoric to Roman times (with the stele of the gens Vassidia), a medieval and modern section with works by Paduanino and Antonio Zanchi and a map of 1566-75, and a musical section on tenors Giovanni Martinelli and Aureliano Pertile. The mast, about 38 meters high, offers a view of the plain and the Euganean and Berici Hills.

  • Where Piazza Trieste 15, Montagnana
  • Entry museum €3,50, reduced €2; mastio €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: comune.montagnana.pd.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Rocca degli Alberi e Porta Legnago

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

Western gate of the city with fortified bridge, built in 1360-62 for Francesco il Vecchio da Carrara on a project by Francesco da Schicci, once surrounded by water; the blackbirds are a nineteenth-century addition.

  • Where Porta Legnago, Montagnana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Duomo di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Built between 1431 and 1502 in Gothic-Renaissance forms, with a white marble portal attributed to Sansovino or his school; it preserves works by Giovanni Buonconsiglio, two counter-façade frescoes attributed to Giorgione and on the main altar the great Transfiguration of Paolo Veronese (1555-56).

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Montagnana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II e centro storico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Ancient market square paved with trachytes, with eighteenth-nineteenth-century portico buildings and the Palazzo del Monte di Pietà, the first bank of the city since 1497; in the center also the Town Hall of 1538 with ceiling carved by Marcantonio Vanin (1605) and the Venetian Gothic palace Magnavin-Foratti with pentaphora.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II, Montagnana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · touringclub.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Villa Pisani di Palladio

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Just outside Porta Padova, designed by Andrea Palladio in 1553-55 for the Venetian nobleman Francesco Pisani; in the atrium the statues of the Seasons (1565-77) by Alessandro Vittoria.

  • Where Porta Padova, Montagnana
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the fourteenth century near Porta XX Settembre, with Romanesque elements in facade and bell tower in terracotta begun in 1429; inside frescoes of the fourteenth century, an eighteenth-century organ and a Madonna with saints of Palma il Giovane.

  • Where Porta XX Settembre, Montagnana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it
