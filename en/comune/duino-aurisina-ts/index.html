What to see in Duino Aurisina

karst coast overlooking the Adriatic between Trieste and Monfalcone: the castle of the princes of the Tower and Tasso where Rilke wrote the Dunean Elegies, the white cliffs, the bay of Sistiana and the Risorgive of Timavo with the mitreum and the Roman villa.

8 places to visit in Duino Aurisina, in the province of Trieste (Friuli-Venezia Giulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Castello di Duino

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Historical residence of the princes of the Tower and Tasso, opened in part since 2003, on high cliffs overlooking the sea: lounges with stuccoes, furnishings and art collections, the Palladian school helicoidal staircase, the park on several levels with an air-raid bunker dug into the rock and, not far away, the ruins of Castel Vecchio (XI century) and the rock of the Lady White. Open Saturday and Sunday.

  • Where Frazione Duino 32, Duino Aurisina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: italia.it · turismofvg.it

Riserva naturale delle Falesie di Duino e Sentiero Rilke

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

White limestone eyelashes overlooking the sea covered with Mediterranean maquis, shelter of reptiles and nesting birds; the Rilke Path, about 2 km panoramic on the Adriatic, connects Duino to Sistiana.

  • Where Tra Duino e Sistiana
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Baia di Sistiana

Nature and landscape · Sea and beaches · Free entry · Viewpoint

Quiet and sheltered bay, already seaside resorts in the Austro-Hungarian era, with marina, establishments and clean beaches between maquis and karst rocks.

  • Where Sistiana, Duino Aurisina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Bocche del Timavo

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry

The Timavo, born in Slovenia, descends into the Karst and re-emerges after about 38 km a few hundred meters from the sea; sacred place cited by Virgilio and Tito Livio, in Roman age with an area of worship of Hercules and Spes Augusta.

  • Where San Giovanni di Duino, Duino Aurisina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: touringclub.it · turismofvg.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni in Tuba

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Late-Gothic church wanted by the lords of Duino, the Walsees, in the first half of the fifteenth century and rebuilt after the wars: a single nave, polygonal apse and the pavement mosaics of the early Christian basilica below, next to the Risorgives of Timavo.

  • Where Località San Giovanni al Timavo, Duino Aurisina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: turismofvg.it

Grotta del Mitreo

History and architecture · Archaeology

karst cave on the slopes of the Hermada, frequented by the Neolithic and adapted between II and V century AD to the sanctuary of Mitra: casts of the two reliefs with the sacrifice of the bull and the inscriptions of the tenderers. Open on Saturday morning, visits with the Flondar Speleological Group.

  • Where Duino Aurisina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismofvg.it

Villa romana del Randaccio

History and architecture · Archaeology

Large maritime villa on three levels, excavated in 1988-1991: about 1,300 square meters and 40 rooms between residence with mosaics and heating and productive spaces, in use from the 1st century BC to the 4th A.D., perhaps also station between Aquileia and Tergeste (Fons Timavi) Visits by reservation in the aqueduct park.

  • Where Località San Giovanni al Timavo, Duino Aurisina
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.turismofvg.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · turismofvg.it

Aurisina e le cave di pietra

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Typical karst village with its ancient nucleus behind a small tree-lined square, between alleys and wells, famous for its precious limestone quarries; it hits the large station, exchange port until 1918.

  • Where Aurisina, Duino Aurisina
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
