What to see in Nichelino

In Stupinigi, in the countryside south of Turin, the hunting lodge of Filippo Juvarra, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, in its natural park.

3 places to visit in Nichelino, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Palazzina di Caccia di Stupinigi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Hunting and party residence designed by Filippo Juvarra since 1729 for Vittorio Amedeo II, crowned by the deer on the dome; since 1919 it houses the Museum of Art and Furniture, today of the Foundation Ordine Mauriziano. UNESCO World Heritage.

  • Where Piazza Principe Amedeo 7, Stupinigi, Nichelino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.italia.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Sources: italia.it · residenzerealisabaude.com · ordinemauriziano.it

Parco Naturale di Stupinigi

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

The ancient Savoia hunting reserve around the building, today a natural park of fields and woods with visitor center, picnic area and recommended itineraries.

  • Where Viale Torino 4, Nichelino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · italia.it

Castelvecchio di Stupinigi

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry

A short distance from the building, the ancient medieval manor house in good state of preservation, historic center of Stupinigi.

  • Where Stupinigi, Nichelino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: turismotorino.org · italia.it
