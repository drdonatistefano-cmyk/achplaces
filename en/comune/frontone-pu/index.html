What to see in Frontone

Borgo at the foot of the massif of Catria, on the border with the provinces of Ancona and Perugia, dominated by the Castle of the Port attributed to Francesco di Giorgio Martini, with views up to Monte Nerone, San Marino and the Adriatic; from the summit of Monte Catria, reachable by the plants, start trails and slopes.

Castello della Porta (Rocca di Frontone) e Raccolta comunale d'arte

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Built around the year 1000 to guard a road used by the Romans to reach the Adriatic, it was fortress of the Duchy of Montefeltro between the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, with a particular arched plant attributed to Francesco di Giorgio Martini; then residence of the family of the Porta, from which the name. Today it is open to visitors, it hosts events and exhibitions and the municipal collection of art with works from the churches of the territory and the castle, partly sorted by chronology and partly by iconography. Reduced for children and groups over 15 people.

Borgo di Castello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The historic centre of Castello, in a panoramic position, with bars and restaurants; among the things to see the red civic tower and the churches of the Madonna del Soccorso and Santa Maria Assunta. At the end of November-December there are the Christmas markets of Santa Claus Castle.

  • Where Castello, Frontone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Monte Catria

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

It rises 1,701 meters with a large metal cross on top; the lifts climb up to the summit and serve ski slopes, a bike park, an adventure park for children, shelters and trails. Hiking, horse riding and biking are practiced: Frontone is a stage of the High Marche Apennines Cycle.

  • Where Monte Catria, Frontone
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
