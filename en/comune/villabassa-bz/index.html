What to see in Villabassa

Country of Alta Pusteria in the Dolomites Region Three Peaks, formerly seat of the Court of Auditors of Gorizia: in Piazza Von Kurz the late Baroque parish church of Santo Stefano and Casa Wassermann with the Museum of Tourism of Alta Pusteria, just outside the village the church of Santa Maddalena in Moos (1490).

Museo del turismo dell'Alta Pusteria, Casa Wassermann

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

In the historic Casa Wassermann, it tells of the development of tourism in Alta Pusteria: on the first floor the origins of tourism, the atmosphere of a traditional inn, the local thermal springs, a detailed reconstruction of the Pusteria railway and a hall on mountaineering; on the second floor the history of the house and noble families von Kurz and Wassermann, a room dedicated to the hotel owner and pioneer of tourism Emma Hellensteiner and one on the daily life of servitude. Guided tours for groups by reservation.

  • Where Piazza Von Kurz 3, Villabassa
  • Entry €5, free children up to 6 years
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.niederdorf.eu

Sources: niederdorf.eu · suedtirol.info

Parrocchiale di Santo Stefano

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Late Baroque church built between 1792 and 1796, with frescoes by Franz Altmutter, sculptures by Nissl and an altarpiece by Martin Knoller; on the left of the staircase a double gothic chapel of the fifteenth century, with the chapel of Sant'Anna on the upper floor and, below, the chapel of the dead frescoed by Simone da Tesido (open every day 8-17).

  • Where Piazza Von Kurz 3, Villabassa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Chiesa di Santa Maddalena in Moos

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in 1490 thanks to a donation by Countess Paola Gonzaga, wife of Count Leonardo di Gorizia; the first-baroque high altar (about 1600) is adorned with a late-Gothic relief with the Adoration of the Magi by Michael Parth (about 1520). Open from early April to early October, 8-19.

  • Where Via Stazione 3, Villabassa (verso Monguelfo)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Chiesa dell'ospedale della Santissima Trinità

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The hospital was founded in 1456 and between 1456 and 1473 a first chapel was joined to it, enlarged in the middle of the seventeenth century and consecrated as a church on January 30, 1655 by the auxiliary bishop of Brixen Jesse Perkhofer; it forms a single complex with the former hospital, connected by a vestibule with a cross vault, and preserves baroque furnishings.

  • Where Via Hans Wassermann 2, Villabassa
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Antico tribunale (Casa Gorizia)

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry

One of the most important historical buildings in the country, it was the seat of the court of the Counts of Gorizia in Pusteria; the north façade is painted with the coats of arms of Gorizia, Tyrol and several noble families.

  • Where Via Stazione 1, Villabassa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info

Kurpark di Villabassa

Nature and landscape · City parks · Free entry

Park of 5 hectares in the center of the village, with pond and stage on the water for cultural events, lighted tennis courts, pavilion for the elderly, walking and running trails, rest areas and a botanical didactic path.

  • Where Via Stazione 1, Villabassa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.suedtirol.info

Sources: suedtirol.info
