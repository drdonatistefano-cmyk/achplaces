What to see in San Colombano Certenoli

With about 43 km2 it is the largest municipality in the Val Fontanabuona, from Passo dell'Anchetta to the Ramaceto massif; in Calvari the Museum of Legacy Cuneo tells about the life and crafts of the past, and from the valley bottom start trails towards the Alta Via dei Monti Liguri and Monte Ramaceto.

3 places to visit in San Colombano Certenoli, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Lascito Cuneo

Museums · Themed · Free entry

In part of the building donated to the Municipality by the Cuneo family in 1905: the hall of memories with furniture, objects and books of the family, including a rare Venetian edition of 1571 of the life of Christopher Columbus written by his son Fernando, late nineteenth century piers and fragments of the "patàffio" linked to the conspiracy of the Tower of 1672; then a shoemaker workshop rebuilt with original tools, an ancient village workshop and, outdoors, agricultural machinery and wine making. According to the Metropolitan City opens Tuesday and Thursday from 15 to 17 or by appointment. Tel. 0185 358791.

discordant times: the MiC card (July 2020) reports "closed" every day, the Metro City Tuesday and Thursday 15-17 or by appointment

Chiesa di Santo Stefano a Cichero

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish already existed in the sixteenth century; during the Second World War the church was severely damaged and the parish registers were destroyed. It preserves a painting of the Madonna dell'Orto, very venerated, escaped from the fire of 1944.

  • Where Piazza Santo Stefano 1, Cichero, San Colombano Certenoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it

Sources: diocesichiavari.it · fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it

Chiesa di Santa Maria a Camposasco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Very ancient parish, of which we have already heard since 1143; in its territory, near the hamlet of Villa Oneto, there are the remains of the ancient Abbey of the Nativity of Our Lord.

  • Where Via San Gaetano 16, Camposasco, San Colombano Certenoli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page fuorigenova.cittametropolitana.genova.it
