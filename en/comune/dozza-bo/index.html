What to see in Dozza

The hamlet of the painted walls, with the Rocca and the Enoteca regionale in its cellars.

3 places to visit in Dozza, in the province of Bologna (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for hour 1 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Borgo dei muri dipinti

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

Since 1960 the Biennale del Muro Dipinto has left on the facades of the village works by Italian and foreign artists.

  • Where Centro storico, Dozza
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: bolognawelcome.com · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Rocca di Dozza

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The fortress of Malvezzi-Campeggi, with furnished noble apartments and views of the hills.

  • Where Piazza Rocca 6/a, Dozza
  • Entry To be checked on the official website (secondary sources indicate about €7.50)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: italia.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Enoteca regionale Emilia-Romagna

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Paid entry

Since 1970 in the cellars of the Rocca: a showcase of wines from all over the region.

  • Where Piazza Rocca, Dozza
  • Entry Free admission; tastings for a fee
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
