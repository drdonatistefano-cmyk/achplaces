What to see in Ronco Briantino

The village is crossed by the cyclendale of the parks of Rio Vallone and Molgora; in the village stands the late Baroque Villa Alfieri Perego, wanted by the canon Giovanni Perego, and the parish church of Sant'Ambrogio, built between 1924 and 1934.

Villa Alfieri Perego

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Late Baroque residence commissioned by the canon Giovanni Perego, humanist and art lover, as a place of holiday and family representation. It is located along the cycle path that crosses the square of Ronco Briantino and continues in the Park of Rio Molgora towards Carnate.

Chiesa parrocchiale di Sant'Ambrogio

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Parish of the Diocese of Milan (decanated in Vimercate), built between 1924 and 1934, the year in which the frescoes and the interior flooring were executed; the presbytery had a structural intervention in 1974. The CEI card records in 1837 the church of Sant'Ambrogio in Nemus, today deconsecrated, and censisce inside the main altar and lateral altars, side chapels, frescoes, stained glass windows, statues, organ, source, tombstones and inscriptions; outside door, bell tower and parish church.
