What to see in Savona

Città dei papi Della Rovere sulla Riviera di Ponente: the Priamàr Fortress on the port with the civic museums, the Torretta di Leon Pancaldo, the Cathedral of the Assumption with the Sistine Chapel commissioned by Sisto IV and the Art Museum of Palazzo Gavotti, with the Sanctuary of Our Lady of Mercy in the valley of Letimbro.

8 places to visit in Savona, in the province of Savona (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Fortezza del Priamàr e Civico Museo Archeologico e della Città

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Great sixteenth-century fortress on the sea built by the Genoese to assert the dominion over the city; in the fifteenth-century Loggia of the Castle Nuovo the Civic Archaeological Museum and the City, inaugurated on April 7, 1990 at the reopening of part of the complex.

  • Where Corso Mazzini 1, Savona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo Sandro Pertini e Renata Cuneo

Museums · Modern art

In the Priamàr Fortress, the museum dedicated to the collection donated by Sandro Pertini to his city and to the works of the sculptor Renata Cuneo.

  • Where Corso Mazzini 1, Savona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo d'Arte di Palazzo Gavotti – Pinacoteca Civica e Museo della Ceramica

Museums · Old masters

In the seventeenth century Palazzo Gavotti the Pinacoteca Civica and the collection of the Milena Milani Foundation in memory of Carlo Cardazzo, with works by Arp, de Chirico, Fontana, Magritte, Mirò and Picasso, connected to the Ceramic Museum.

  • Where Piazza Chabrol, Savona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · luoghidelcontemporaneo.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Cattedrale di Nostra Signora Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Started in 1589 on a project by Battista Sormano, with the dome of Giovanni Cortese and the facade of Guglielmo Calderini; it preserves a heritage of ecclesiastical goods of particular importance.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Savona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: catalogo.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Cappella Sistina

Places of worship · Monumental complexes

Next to the cathedral, the chapel wanted by Pope Sixtus IV, not to be confused with the Roman homonym; there is preserved the funeral monument of the pope's parents, with the Madonna enthroned between Sixtus IV and Saints Francis and Antonio da Padua.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo, Savona
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · sigecweb.beniculturali.it

Museo del Tesoro della Cattedrale

Museums · Old masters

He collects works of painting, textile art and goldsmithery related to the cathedral.

Torre Leon Pancaldo

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry

The tower of the Quarda, simply called "Torretta" at the entrance of the port.

Santuario di Nostra Signora di Misericordia e Museo del Santuario

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Monumental complex arose after the apparition of the Virgin of 1536, with an artistic heritage documented in the archives; next to the museum of the sanctuary.

  • Where Piazza Santuario, Savona
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
