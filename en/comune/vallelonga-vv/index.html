What to see in Vallelonga

Small center of the Serre Calabria, seat of the Sanctuary Basilica of Santa Maria di Monserrato, rebuilt in the 1930s after the earthquake of 1783, and the Surgical Museum Pasquale Castiglione Morelli dedicated to the history of his hospital of the early twentieth century.

Museo chirurgico Pasquale Castiglione Morelli

Museums · Science and university

The museum is a first attempt to reconstruct a part of the health history of South Italy at the beginning of the twentieth century through the story of a hospital in a small town of the Calabrian Serre. Booking not required; MiC (2020) does not report up-to-date times.

Santuario Basilica di Santa Maria di Monserrato

Places of worship · Sanctuaries

Destroyed with the country by the earthquake of 1783, it was rebuilt between 1930 and 1935 on a project by the Florentine engineer William Lentini; it is a minor basilica since 1971 and a diocesan Marian shrine since 2002. The interior has three naves and contains the granite portal of the eighteenth-century Serrese school, a polychrome marble altar from the seventeenth century, paintings from the late nineteenth-century by Andrea Cefaly on the ceiling, twelve stained glass windows with the earthquake and the Marian appearance and the largest reed organ in Calabria. The patron saint's feast falls on the second Sunday of July, with pilgrimages on foot and flowered along the path of the procession.
