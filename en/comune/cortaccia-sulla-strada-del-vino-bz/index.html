What to see in Cortaccia sulla strada del vino

Wine country of the Lower Atesina on the Wine Road, with the parish church of San Vigilio, a pilgrimage destination for the image "lacrimante" of the Addolorata, the Museo Uomo nel Tempo in the Am Orth residence and the churches of the hamlets Corona, Penone and Niclara; among vineyards and apple orchards run the didactic path of the wine and the Murundum routes.

Museo Uomo nel Tempo (Zeitreise Mensch)

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

It tells human life in different eras up to the consumer society, with exhibits of prehistory and protohistory and a collection of historical work tools; the guided tour uses multimedia animations and shows in operation numerous devices. Guided tours every Friday at 10 am from April to October; in the other months on request at +39 338 2032429.

  • Where Ansitz am Orth, Via Botengasse 2, Cortaccia
  • Entry from €4 (guided tour)
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cortaccia.bz.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · comune.cortaccia.bz.it

Chiesa parrocchiale di San Vigilio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Church mentioned already in the thirteenth century, turned star and net at the beginning of the sixteenth century, parish since 1515; the Romanesque bell tower probably dates back to about 1300 and the baptismal font dates back to 1033. The main altar of 1850 frames a altarpiece by Orazio Giovanelli from 1630 with the martyrdom of San Vigilio. It is a pilgrimage destination for the painting of the Sorrowful which, according to tradition, \"piense" from 1733 to 1738.

  • Where Piazza Hauptmann Schweiggl, Cortaccia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cortaccia.bz.it

Sources: comune.cortaccia.bz.it · suedtiroler-unterland.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Church of the hamlet of Corona (Graun) in a late-Gothic style; on the bell tower a restored fresco of San Cristoforo. Patronage party on April 23rd.

Sentiero didattico del vino Cortaccia-Magrè-Cortina

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry · Viewpoint

Themed route of about 8 km (about 2 hours) among the vineyards of the Lower Atesina dedicated to the life cycle of wine, from the vine to the glass: starts from the center of Cortaccia, where the entrance is marked by a large cluster of pebbles polished by the glacier, and touches Niclara, Magrè and Cortina, with information stations on viticulture, landscape and history, some to recognize the aromas of wine. The traits can also be covered individually.

  • Where Centro di Cortaccia (partenza)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cortaccia.bz.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · comune.cortaccia.bz.it

Percorso Murundum n. 2 «Katzenleiter»

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

A short 2.5 km ring (about 45 minutes) from the main square along the historic Millastiege stairway and the steep Katzenleiter sloping steps and sandstone walls: it passes next to the parish church of San Vigilio, to the Milla river with the wooden covered bridge, between vineyards and apple orchards overlooking the valley, and returns from the cemetery and the Man Museum in Time.

  • Where Piazza Hauptmann Schweiggl, Cortaccia (partenza)
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.comune.cortaccia.bz.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · comune.cortaccia.bz.it
