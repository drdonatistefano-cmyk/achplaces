What to see in Larino

Cittadina of the hills of Basso Molise with a medieval historical center dominated by the cathedral Basilica of San Pardo, whose Gothic façade is 1319, and a low town born around the archaeological area of the Roman Larinum, with the amphitheatre of flavia age and the mosaics of the thermal baths; in the Ducal Palace the Civic Museum preserves the mosaic of the wolf with Romolo and Remo.

7 places to visit in Larino, in the province of Campobasso (Molise). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Basilica concattedrale di San Pardo

Places of worship · Cathedrals

The Cathedral dedicated to the Assumption and to St. Pardo, bishop of Greek origin, consecrated in 1319: the Gothic façade is the work of Francesco Petrini of Lanciano, with sculptural compositions of biblical inspiration and thirteen columns instead of twelve, the thirteenth to represent Judas; the back of the building is instead crooked and irregular. Inside, frescoes, paintings, tabernacles and altars of different eras. At the end of May (25-27) the feast of San Pardo brings to the city over one hundred carts drawn by oxen and decorated with paper flowers.

  • Where Via Leone, Larino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Area archeologica e Anfiteatro romano

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry

Pre-Roman center of the Frentani and then Roman town hall, Larinum was abandoned in the 9th century for Saracen raids. The amphitheatre of flavia age (69-96 A.D.), oval of almost 100 for 80 meters and capable according to italia.it of about 5,000 spectators, preserves arena, podium, the lower cavea and corridors of the first level; next, thermal buildings with geometric mosaics, marine and fantastic animals, a paved road with shops and houses (the iconic of the kantharos) and a mosaic with groupers and octopus from a domus of Republican age. Open from Tuesday to Saturday 9-13, other visits by reservation (0874 822787).

  • Where Via Dante Alighieri 5 (ingresso accanto a Villa Zappone), Larino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: sabapmolise.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo civico di Palazzo Ducale

Museums · Archaeological

In the medieval Palazzo Ducale, also seat of the Town Hall, hosts the permanent archaeological exhibition "Ars et Ingenium" with objects of the territory from the 10th century BC to the 4th A.D.: vases, amphorae, ointments, spears, helmets, lamps and statues related to the cult of Mars, protector of the ancient Larinum, a group with Hercules and Priapo and polychrome mosaics of the patrician villas of the municipality. The most famous piece is the mosaic of the third century A.D. with the wolf that feeds Romolo and Remo. The MiC card does not contain usable times: check before the visit.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Larino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo diocesano «Mons. Giovanni Andrea Tria»

Museums · Old masters

In the Bishop's Palace, part of a path that includes Duomo, churches of St. Francis and St. Stephen and Ducal Palace; the first section is in the sacristy of the Cathedral, with altar, bishop's throne, choir stalls, sacristy furnishings, liturgical silvers from the seventeenth to the nineteenth century and two wooden panels from the fifteenth century. On the first floor the paintings of the Molisano Paolo Gamba, a pupil of Francesco Solimena; among the notable pieces the homeral veil of Bishop Belisario Baldevino (1555-1591), who in 1564 founded the first seminary of Christendom. Hours indicated by italia.it: every day 9-12.30 and 17-19.30.

  • Where Piazza Duomo 25, Larino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

14th century church from the inside white with Baroque decorations; behind the altar two coeval frescoes, discovered by chance during the removal of the rubble of the earthquake of 2008. It is part of the path of the Diocesan Museum.

  • Where Centro storico, Larino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it

Centro storico medievale

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The high city, more than 300 meters away, is closed in a medieval defensive structure dominated by the cathedral, between alleys, views and taverns; in a side street next to the Duomo a plaque reminds that there arose the first seminary of the world. Larino is also home to the Gentile di Larino olive oil.

  • Where Piazza Duomo, Larino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitmolise.eu

Tratturi

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

Around Larino there are hundreds of kilometers of ancient tracts, the streets of transhumance used by the shepherds to move the flocks between Abruzzo and Puglia: there are still and you can walk, among wheat fields and olive groves of Gentile di Larino.

  • Where Campagna di Larino
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
