What to see in Collio

The village of the upper Trompia Valley is about 1000 meters away, crossed by the Bavorgo stream, with the seventeenth-century town hall and the 18th-century parish church of the Saints Nazarius and Celsus; it is known for the iron mine of Sant'Aloisio, today's museum and adventure park, the Romanesque church of San Rocco and the Maniva Pass area.

Miniera Sant'Aloisio - Tassara

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Iron mine mentioned already in 1819, gathered in 1870 in the Società S. Aloisio, passed in 1886 to Terni and then to the Tassara Group, which since the 1930s transformed it into a modern mining site; closed in 1984, it is reopened to the public in 2003. You enter with guide, helmet and torch for about 2.5 km of tunnels (8-10 °C, very high humidity: warm clothes and hiking shoes) between roasting ovens and miners' tools; there is also an adventure park suspended in the gallery, 85% indoor. Open from the end of April to October (Sundays; in summer Saturdays and Sundays; every day from 8 to 23 August), 10-12.30 and 14-17.30, mandatory booking; not accessible to disabled people.

  • Where Via Castiglione, Collio
  • Entry Mine €12, reduced 5-13 years €10; adventure park €15, reduced €12; mine + park €24, reduced €20
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.minierasantaloisio.it

Sources: minierasantaloisio.it · civitas.valletrompia.it · in-lombardia.it · visitbrescia.it

Chiesa di San Rocco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Along the road between Collio and Memmo, started in the middle of the fifteenth century as a simple chapel and then enlarged: Romanesque church with sober architecture, with linear facade, rose window and portal in grey stone. It was declared a national monument in 1912, during World War I it was requisitioned and used as a stable for the horses of the artillery. Visits on request.

  • Where Via San Rocco 42, Collio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: visitbrescia.it · in-lombardia.it

Centro di Collio

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The village, about 1000 meters away, is crossed by the Bavorgo, a tributary of Mella; on the central square overlooks the Municipal Palace of the seventeenth century, while the parish church of the Saints Nazario and Celso dates back to the eighteenth century.

  • Where Piazza Zanardelli, Collio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it

Passo del Maniva e Maniva Ski

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Viewpoint

Valico delle Prealpi Brescia between Val Trompia and Val Sabbia, about 50 km from Brescia, reachable from Collio with a winding road well signposted. In winter, the Maniva Ski area offers 40 km of slopes between 1,450 and 2,100 meters (1 black, 6 red, 8 blue) and 9 lifts, with snowshoeing also in the moonlight, ski touring, cross-country skiing, sleddog and ski schools also for disabled; in summer the Bike Park Pezzeda offers 8 slopes for 26 km in the Iron Valley network. The roads from Val Sabbia and Crocedomini are usually closed in winter.

  • Where Via Maniva 256, Collio
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.in-lombardia.it

Sources: in-lombardia.it · visitbrescia.it
