What to see in Agliè

Borgo canavesano among woods and countryside, Orange Flag, told by Guido Gozzano and dominated by the Ducal Castle of the Savoy.

5 places to visit in Agliè, in the province of Turin (Piedmont). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Open Agliè in the guide with map and filters

Castello Ducale di Agliè

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · UNESCO World Heritage

Royal residence born in 1141 and rebuilt several times, today with about 300 furnished rooms, including the large ballroom with stuccoes and frescoes of the "Fasti di re Arduino" by Giovanni Paolo Recchi. UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  • Where Piazza Castello 1, Agliè
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Parco e giardini del castello

Nature and landscape · City parks · Viewpoint

The Italian garden, the park with large trees, bosso hedges, statues, fountains and greenhouses, with the entrance to the large eighteenth-century fountain with horseshoe; open from May to October.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Agliè
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Il Meleto – Villa Gozzano

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The nineteenth-century Art Nouveau villa at the foot of Macugnano Hill, the summer residence of the poet Guido Gozzano.

  • Where Agliè
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di Santa Marta

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry
