What to see in Barcellona Pozzo di Gotto

The city of the Mexican was born between the rivers Idria and Longano, with the ancient nucleus of Pozzo di Gotto, the Cave of Santa Venere of Byzantine age, the Cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta, the Basilica of San Sebastiano, the church of San Giovanni Battista with bulb bell towers and two large museums of artisan workshops, the Park Museo Jalari and the Museo Nello Cassata.

9 places to visit in Barcellona Pozzo di Gotto, in the province of Messina (Sicily). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Parco Museo Jalari

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Park that recreates the life of the workshops of the past: 15,000 artefacts distributed in 42 artisan workshops, preserved in their original context without the "decontextualization" typical of the collections.

  • Where Barcellona Pozzo di Gotto
  • Entry €7
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo etno-antropologico «Nello Cassata»

Museums · Themed

Private non-profit museum dedicated to poet and historian In the Cassata: 45 ancient artisan workshops rebuilt with about 20,000 vintage artefacts, catalogued and bound by the Superintendence of Messina, a garden of rare Sicilian and Mediterranean plants and a restoration workshop.

  • Where Contrada Manno, Barcellona Pozzo di Gotto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Duomo di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Basilica built in the first half of the seventeenth century and rebuilt in the nineteenth century; it houses a marble statue of St. Vitus of the sixteenth century, a seventeenth-century painting of the Blessed Savior within a wreath of flowers and a marble saint of the late seventeenth century.

  • Where Barcellona Pozzo di Gotto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.barcellona-pozzo-di-gotto.me.it · italia.it

Pozzo di Gotto, il nucleo antico

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The oldest part of the city, where a well was dug to irrigate the lands between the rivers Idria and Longano; a document from 1463 links these lands to the Messinian Nicolò Goto.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Free entry
