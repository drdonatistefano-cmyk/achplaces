What to see in Tortora

Borgo della Riviera dei Cedri on the border with Basilicata, in the territory of the ancient Blanda conquered by the Romans in 214 BC: the medieval historical center perched around the castle, with the belvedere on the squares, and the Antiquarium of Palazzo Casapesenna with the finds of the territory.

Antiquarium di Palazzo Casapesenna

Museums · Archaeological

Housed in the rooms of Palazzo Casapesenna, it collects archaeological finds recovered in the territory of Tortora, which has returned traces of Enotri, Greci, Lucani, Romani and Longobardi and where many scholars place the ancient Blanda, taken from the consul Quinto Fabio Massimo in 214 BC and then became Blanda Julia. Booking not required; MiC (2020) does not report up-to-date times.

Centro storico, castello e belvedere

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The first nucleus, called Julitta in memory of the Roman city, was founded on a rocky spur by the inhabitants of Blanda fleeing from Saracen incursions after the 11th century and grew downstream of the castle; the name Turtura appears for the first time in the bull of 1079 of the Archbishop of Salerno Alfano. The ridge village is along the historic axis of via Blanda, with the viewpoints of Piazza Municipio and Piazza Monastero.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Tortora
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page cultura.gov.it

Sources: catalogo.beniculturali.it · cultura.gov.it
