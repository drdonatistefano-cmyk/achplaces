What to see in Aquino

Borgo della Ciociaria between the lower valley of Liri and the slopes of Mount Cairo, Volsco settlement and then Roman colony from 39 BC, home of Giovenale and St. Thomas; remain the Porta San Lorenzo and the Arc of Marcantonio on the Via Latina, the Romanesque church of Santa Maria della Libera, the tower and the House of San Tommaso and the Park of Vallone.

Casa di San Tommaso d'Aquino e torre del castello

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses

Built in the 10th century under Atenolfo II on a travertine bench in front of the Roman and Longobard center, the residence lived with the town until the destruction of the city by Corrado IV in 1252; from the end of the thirteenth century around the palace a fortified center developed. You can see the pseudosquare tower, transformed into a bell tower in the eighteenth century, the remains of the Comital Palace called Casa di San Tommaso, with two double windows in the façade of the fourteenth-XV century, the defensive walls and the remains of a large cylindrical tower guarding the bridge over the lakes.

  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Chiesa di Santa Maria della Libera

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Romanesque church, national monument and among the historic residences of Lazio, built on the ruins of the Temple of Hercules Liberatore; for the municipality is the most remarkable of the buildings of the post-Roman age and example of medieval art. In its garden there is the Arc de Marcantonio.

  • Where Area archeologica di Aquinum, Aquino
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com · comune.aquino.fr.it

Arco di Marcantonio

History and architecture · Archaeology

Roman arch of the 1st century A.D. along the Via Latina, in the garden of Santa Maria della Libera, indicated by VisitLazio as one of the oldest arches in Italy.
