What to see in Curon Venosta

The municipality of Alta Val Venosta on Lake Resia, the artificial basin created in 1949-1950 by combining the natural lakes of Resia and Curon: the submerged water Curon Vecchia and part of Resia, and from the lake emerges only the Romanesque bell tower, today one of the best known images of South Tyrol. The Alta Val Venosta Museum tells the story; near Resia there are the Adige and you can visit the Bunker 20 and the barrier of the Vallo Alpino di Plamort.

Campanile sommerso di Curon

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Romanesque bell tower separated from the church of Santa Caterina d'Alessandria, built around the end of the fourteenth century: it is the only building of Curon Vecchia spared when in 1950 the village was demolished and submerged by the basin, with the inhabitants expropriated and forced to leave the houses in about a week. Legend has it that in the windy days the bells are still heard, removed before flooding; in winter, with the frozen lake, you can reach them on foot.

  • Where Lago di Resia, Curon Venosta
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it

Lago di Resia

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The largest lake in South Tyrol, created by the Montecatini group by combining the natural Resia Lake and Lake Curon (or Mezzo) with a dam for hydropower; the level was raised 22 meters above the ancient village. Exposed to the wind, it is suitable for sailing, kitesurfing, canoeing and kayaking, with boat tours; from the Malga di Resia you can enjoy the view of the lake.

  • Where Curon Venosta e Resia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it

Museo Alta Val Venosta

Museums · City history

Dedicated to the submersion of 1950 and to the fate of Curon Vecchia and Resia: it tells the trauma of the loss of homes and land and reconstruction with historical photographs, models, sacred objects and statues of the ancient churches and testimonies of peasant life; some rooms reproduce a traditional stube. In summer guided tours by reservation.

  • Where Via Principale, Curon Venosta (vicino alla parrocchiale)
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it

Sorgente dell'Adige

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers

At 1550 meters, about 10 minutes from the village of Resia, there is the Adige, the main river of South Tyrol and the second of Italy for length (415 km); here passes the watershed between the Adriatic and the Inn basin and the Danube, towards which flows the nearby Stillebach.

  • Where Via Principale, Resia, Curon Venosta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it

Bunker 20 di Resia

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Bunker near the spring of the Adige at about 1550 meters, partly dug in the rock and partly in concrete, about 20 meters long with about 450 m2 visitable; managed by the association Oculus.

entrance only with guided tour (suedtirol.info)

  • Where Via Principale, Resia, Curon Venosta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it

Sbarramento del Vallo Alpino a Plamort

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Viewpoint

Fortification of the Alpine Valley extended for about a square kilometer to about 2050 meters, with an anti-tank barrier about 250 meters long to dragon teeth; the name comes from ...plan mort , plain of the dead, today natural monument. It is reached by a trip of about 3 hours from the parking lot at the sports field of Resia, also by mountain bike.

  • Where Plamort, sopra Resia, Curon Venosta
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: suedtirol.info · italia.it
