What to see in Santarcangelo di Romagna

The village of Tonino Guerra on Mount Jupiter, crossed by more than one hundred caves dug into the tuff, with the museums MUSAS and MET and the murals of Eron.

4 places to visit in Santarcangelo di Romagna, in the province of Rimini (Emilia-Romagna). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 2 of the 3 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Grotte tufacee

Nature and landscape · Caves and geosites

Over one hundred artificial cavities dug into the tuff under the hill: tunnels with niches that sometimes end up in a circular hall. The origin is still being discussed.

  • Where Centro storico, Santarcangelo di Romagna
  • Entry To be checked: guided tours by the tourist office
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Il mondo di Tonino Guerra

Museums · House museums

More than 60 works by the poet and screenwriter born here: paintings, sculptures, tapestries, ceramics and printed canvases.

  • Where Via della Costa, Santarcangelo di Romagna
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: emiliaromagnaturismo.it
