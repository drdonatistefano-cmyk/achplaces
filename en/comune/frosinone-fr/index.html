What to see in Frosinone

The capital of the Ciociaria dominates from above the plain of Sacco: the viewpoint of Piazza Vittorio Veneto, the Cathedral with its bell tower, the church of San Benedetto, the Roman amphitheatre and the archaeological museum; at Carnival the historic Festa della Radeca.

10 places to visit in Frosinone, in the province of Frosinone (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Belvedere di Piazza Vittorio Veneto

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

The highest point of the city, with a view of the Sacco plain and the nearby towns, suggestive at sunset and moonlight; in July it hosts concerts of the Festival of Conservators.

  • Where Piazza Vittorio Veneto, Frosinone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Chiesa di San Benedetto

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Ancient Church, one of the few spared from the bombings of the Second World War; it preserves the Madonna of Good Counsel, linked to the wonder of July 10, 1796.

  • Where Piazza della Libertà, Frosinone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry · Viewpoint

The cathedral with its 40-metre-square-base bell tower, with double-fores and a clock on each side, which dominates the whole city.

  • Where Centro storico, Frosinone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Santuario della Madonna della Neve

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry
