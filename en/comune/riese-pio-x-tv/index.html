What to see in Riese Pio X

Village between Castelfranco and Asolo where in 1835 Giuseppe Sarto, Pope Pius X was born from 1903 to 1914: you can visit the birthplace with the museum, the parish church of San Matteo with the Annunciation of Palma the Younger and the sanctuary of the Cendrole, the oldest church in the area; in the hamlets, works of Jacopo da Ponte a Poggiana and Francesco Maria Preti in Vallà.

Casa natale e Museo di San Pio X

Museums · House museums

The house where Giuseppe Sarto was born remained basically as it was, with original furnishings and furnishings of the family that recreate the life of a small rural village; it was donated to the municipality in 1926 by sister Maria Sarto. The museum on the south side, built in 1935 for the centenary of the birth, preserves relics and personal objects of the pope, whose first nucleus was donated in 1932 by Giuseppe Parolin, and an exhibition on his life; the complex, restored in 1985 for the visit of John Paul II, is managed by the Giuseppe Sarto Foundation. Group visits by reservation.

Chiesa di San Matteo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built in the eighteenth century by the Padua architect Andrea Zorzi, it conserves paintings from the sixteenth to the eighteenth century, including an Annunciation by Jacopo Palma il Giovane, and the Ark-Cenotaph of St. Pius X.

Santuario della Beata Vergine delle Cendrole

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

In the countryside west of Riese, it is the oldest church in the area, in the eighteenth-century form desired by the architect Ottavio Scotti; it preserves paintings from the sixteenth to the twentieth century, including a Sant'Eurosia by Noè Bordignon. The legend ties him to an apparition of the Virgin to a shepherd threatened by wolves; between church and bell tower remain fragments of the plaque of Lucio Vilonio, Quattuorviro of the Asolo municipality.
