What to see in Cittanova

Cittadina della Piana di Gioia Tauro rebuilt after the earthquake of 1783, with the Matrix Church of San Girolamo, the Baroque sanctuary of the Rosario, eighteenth-nineteenth-century palaces and the Town Villa . Carlo Ruggiero , national monument with botanical garden.

Villa Comunale «Carlo Ruggiero» e Orto botanico

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Free entry

Declared by the Ministry national monument of historical-naturalistic interest, it was realized at the expense of Carlo Ruggiero and donated to the Municipality in 1880. On the 26,515 m2, four gardens alternate with English around a monumental fountain, built with the marbles of the Alcantarini monastery destroyed by the earthquake of 1783, and the Italian villa among the holm oaks; the botanical garden preserves rare species with California redwoods, Lebanon cedars and tulip trees. Since 2008 it houses a Giardino dei Giusti.

  • Where Via Carlo Ruggiero, Cittanova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page comune.cittanova.rc.it

Sources: comune.cittanova.rc.it

Chiesa Matrice di San Girolamo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Built a few years after the earthquake of 1783 on the initiative of Maria Antonia Grimaldi Serra; in the Chapel of the Immaculate Mary Teresa Grimaldi's remains have been resting since 1793 and died in the earthquake.

  • Where Centro storico, Cittanova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page comune.cittanova.rc.it

Sources: comune.cittanova.rc.it

Santuario di Maria Santissima del Rosario

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Baroque-style shrine in the historic centre, with interiors rich in frescoes, stuccoes, angels and plaster statues.
