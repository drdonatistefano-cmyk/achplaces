What to see in Boville Ernica

Borgo Ciociano from the medieval center closed by walls with eighteen towers and three doors; in the Palazzo Filonardi, redesigned by the Vignola, the abbey of San Pietro Ispano houses the Angel of Giotto, a mosaic fragment of the ancient basilica of San Pietro, and a early Christian sarcophagus with one of the oldest Nativity carved.

Centro storico e mura medievali

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

The centre retains its medieval appearance, with winding alleys, squares and arches, enclosed by walls with eighteen towers and three gates (St. Nicholas, St. Mary, St. Francis). The walk on the walls leads to the old wash-house square, to Porta San Francesco and the Posterula with its staircase, with views of the Ciociaria hills. At the entrance of the village, in Piazzale Granatieri of Sardinia, a monument recalls the battle of 1861 between the Kingdom of Italy and Bourbons.

  • Where Porta San Nicola, Boville Ernica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Belvedere dei settantadue paesi

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry · Viewpoint

At the balustrade of the belvedere a stone indicates the seventy-two visible countries, distributed in six provinces and four regions (Latium, Campania, Abruzzo and Molise).

Palazzo Filonardi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

The largest architectural complex of the village, built on an ancient castle: Cardinal Ennio Filonardi had it transformed, around 1530 and on a project attributed to Jacopo Barozzi called the Vignola (his Renaissance portal), into a summer residence of the papal court, dedicated to Paul III in 1532. It preserves mullioned windows, monumental staircases and the floor of a salon with heraldic engravings of the Farnese and Della Rovere.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Abbazia di San Pietro Ispano

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Embedded in the Filonardi complex, it is probably where the Spanish hermit Pietro Ispano lived (X-XI centuries): the crypt of the tenth century is on the cave of the saint. In the Chapel Simoncelli there is the Angel of Giotto, a mosaic fragment of the ancient basilica of St. Peter and the only surviving mosaic of the artist, a marble bas-relief of the Madonna attributed to the Sansovino, a silver bust attributed to Benvenuto Cellini with the relics of the saint, the porphyry cross of the first Jubilee (1300) and a paleochristian sarcophagus (330-350 A.D.), found in 1941, with a Nativity bas-relief considered the oldest carved.

  • Where Palazzo Filonardi, Boville Ernica
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Collegiata di San Michele Arcangelo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

A place of worship already mentioned in a document of 1125, rebuilt in the eighteenth century by Giuseppe Subleyras; it houses the tomb of Cardinal Ennio Filonardi, a San Sebastiano del Cavalier d'Arpino on the altar of the same name and an Addolorata by Antonio Cavallucci (XVIII century).

  • Where Piazza Sant'Angelo, Boville Ernica
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Palazzo Simoncelli e chiesa di San Giovanni Battista

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

17th century palace on the Corso, now home to the Town Hall and a Benedictine cloistered monastery, whose nuns carry on the tradition of embroidery. It includes the small church of St. John the Baptist (open to worship in 1633, today deconsecrated), with a fresco of the Baptism of Christ of the Domenichino.

  • Where Corso Umberto I 3, Boville Ernica
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · visitlazio.com · italia.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Today's exhibition room, it contains frescoes from the 13th to the 15th century, some of Byzantine style, and a fourteenth-century cycle attributed to a student of Giotto; next to the monastery of San Francesco and the Torre Bulgarini.
