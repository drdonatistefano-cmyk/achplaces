What to see in Eboli

Ancient Roman post station on the Popilia street, made famous by the novel of Carlo Levi .Cristo stopped in Eboli , the historic center develops between the Longobard castle and the church of San Francesco, in whose former convent is located the National Archaeological Museum of the Middle Sele Valley.

Museo archeologico nazionale di Eboli e della media valle del Sele

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Opened in March 2000 in the former convent of San Francesco (XIII century, renovated in the XVI century), it follows a chronological order: artifacts of the Upper Neolithic and Eneolithic tombs of Gaudo facies, settlements of the Bronze Age with Mycenian ceramics of the XI century BC, furnishings from the Iron Age to the Roman era, when Eburum was municipium; also materials from Campagna and Oliveto Citra. Closed on Monday.

  • Where Piazza San Francesco 1, Eboli
  • Entry €5, reduced €2
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Francesco

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Area archeologica di Eboli

History and architecture · Archaeology

Remains of part of a Roman villa with first plant of the first century BC, used after various renovations until the Constantine era: testimony of the settlement in the valley of the Sele and the Roman Eburum, town hall as attested by an honorary inscription of the second century A.D.

Visitable only on request with reservation required (tel. 0828 332684)

  • Where Località Paterno, Eboli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Fiume Sele

Nature and landscape · Lakes and rivers · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Sele, which was born from the Picentini Mountains and receives Tanagro and Calore, crosses about 15 km of the territory of Eboli; in the natural area of San Miele they nest blackberries, upups and cards and live barbers, chubs and eels. The river is part of the Foce Sele-Tanagro Nature Reserve.

  • Where Territorio di Eboli
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: comune.eboli.sa.it · italia.it
