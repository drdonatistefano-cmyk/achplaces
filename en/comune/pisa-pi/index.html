What to see in Pisa

Ancient Maritime Republic and university city, with Piazza dei Miracoli UNESCO heritage since 1987, the Lungarni and the city of Galileo.

25 places to visit in Pisa, in the province of Pisa (Tuscany). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in extension: the guide covers for now 13 of the 20 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Torre pendente

History and architecture · Towers · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The bell tower of the Duomo, famous for its inclination: 294 steps to the view of Piazza dei Miracoli and the whole city.

  • Where Piazza dei Miracoli, Pisa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
  • Tickets Tickets on Tiqets
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Cattedrale di Santa Maria Assunta

Places of worship · Cathedrals · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Begin in 1063 by Buscheto, masterpiece of the Romanesque Pisan with classical, Byzantine and Islamic influences: elliptical dome, white and black marbles, the pulpit of Giovanni Pisano and the apse mosaic with San Giovanni di Cimabue (1302).

  • Where Piazza Arcivescovado 7, Pisa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Battistero di San Giovanni

Places of worship · Monumental complexes · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The largest baptistery in the world, begun in 1153 by Diotisalvi, with the fountain and pulpit of Nicola Pisano of 1260; the interior has an exceptional acoustic.

  • Where Piazza del Duomo 23, Pisa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Camposanto Monumentale

Places of worship · Monumental cemeteries · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

The 130-metre-long monumental cemetery with 43 blind arches, Roman sarcophagi and the frescoes by Buonamico Buffalmacco, including the Triumph of Death.

  • Where Piazza dei Miracoli, Pisa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: visittuscany.com

Piazza dei Cavalieri

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

Ancient political center, perhaps the seat of the Roman forum, transformed by Cosimo I in the center of the Knights of Santo Stefano: the Palazzo della Caravana of Vasari, seat of the Normal School, the Palazzo dell'Orologio with the Tower of the Muda of Count Ugolino and the church of Santo Stefano.

  • Where Piazza dei Cavalieri, Pisa
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Museo delle Navi Antiche

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

In the Medici Arsenals seven Roman ships from the third century BC to the VII AD, found since 1998 near San Rossore, with about 800 exhibits, visits and workshops for families.

  • Where Arsenali Medicei, Lungarno Ranieri Simonelli, Pisa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com

Orto e Museo Botanico

Nature and landscape · Botanical gardens · Paid entry · Viewpoint

Founded in 1543-44 by Luca Ghini, it is the first botanical garden in the world: three hectares with plants from all over the world, secular trees and a herbarium of over 300,000 specimens.

  • Where Pisa
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visittuscany.com

Sources: visittuscany.com
