What to see in Morimondo

Borgo was born around the Cistercian abbey founded in 1134-1136 by the monks of Morimond, the first Cistercian abbey of Lombardy, with the exposed brick church, the monastery that can be visited on four levels and the museum of the painter Angelo Comolli, in the countryside of the Ticino Park.

5 places to visit in Morimondo, in the province of Milan (Lombardy). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Chiesa abbaziale di Santa Maria di Morimondo

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

First Cistercian Abbey of Lombardy, founded by monks who came from Morimond in 1134 and built between 1182 and 1296 in Cistercian style already evolved towards the Gothic: exposed bricks, no unnecessary decoration, facade with portal, rose window and double window and some deliberately asymmetric elements. Inside a fresco by Bernardino Luini, a 15th century crucifix, a stack of 14th century holy water and the wooden choir by Francesco Giramo (1522).

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Morimondo
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · in-lombardia.it

Monastero e Museo dell'Abbazia

Museums · City history · Paid entry

The museum occupies the original rooms of the monastery, medieval (XII-XIII century) with renovations between the fifteenth and seventeenth centuries and restorations concluded in 2009: cloisters, capitular hall, working rooms of the monks, hall of the founders, loggia, refectory and dormitory on four levels. You can visit only with a guide by reservation (about 90 minutes); the cloister also with a separate ticket.

  • Where Piazza Municipio 6, Morimondo
  • Entry €10, reduced €8; cloister €3,50
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.abbaziamorimondo.it

Sources: abbaziamorimondo.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Museo civico Angelo Comolli

Museums · House museums · Paid entry

Dedicated to the painter and fresco artist Liberty Angelo Comolli, active in Milan between the eight and twentieth centuries: he exhibits the great preparatory cartoons to charcoal of sacred subject, found in the abbey after 1982, with sketches, paintings, furniture and photographs. Visit accompanied by about half an hour, by reservation or in the free openings scheduled.

  • Where Piazza Municipio 1 (primo piano del Palazzo Comunale), Morimondo
  • Entry €3,50
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: abbaziamorimondo.it · cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Borgo, cascine e grange

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry

The houses of the village are located next to the abbey, with streets and paved square with the traditional "Rizzato" and the farmsteads and granges that were the agricultural centers of the monastery.

  • Where Piazza Municipio, Morimondo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Campagna di Morimondo nel Parco del Ticino

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Around the abbey the monks cleared, reclaimed and watered the lands creating rotten areas; today the territory is part of the Ticino Park, between rows of poplars, irrigation channels and wetlands.

  • Where Morimondo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it
