Palazzo Ducale di Sassuolo

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · Paid entry · Viewpoint

The "delicacy" baroque Este, summer residence of the dukes, with frescoed rooms by Jean Boulanger, the fish pond and the park.

  • Entry €8 whole; €2 reduced; €10 with the Estense Gallery
