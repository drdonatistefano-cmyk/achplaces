What to see in Morano Calabro

Late medieval village of houses leaning on the hill at the foot of the Pollino, called "Presepe del Pollino" after the visit of Escher in 1930; among the majolica domes of the Magdalena, the collegiate of St. Nicholas and Saints Peter and Paul with the marbles of Pietro Bernini, the Norman-Svevo castle and the ruins of the monastery of Colloreto.

Centro storico e palazzi nobiliari

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Late medieval village of houses leaning one to the other along the hill, with alleys and stairs partly carved into the rock and the Pollino in the background; in 1930 the Dutch engraver M. C. Escher pictured the view. Among the noble palaces the seventeenth-century Palazzo Rocco, Palazzo Cozza with the angular portico, the Palazzo dei Cavalieri Marzano with red facades in the district of Judea, Palazzo Lauria with the Baroque portal and Palazzo Coscia (1793). From Porta Ferrante the view extends over the Pollino and the Maddalena.

  • Where Centro storico, Morano Calabro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Castello normanno-svevo

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

The first fortress on the hill arose between the 10th and 11th centuries and was enlarged in the 13th; between 1515 and 1546 Neapolitan architects transformed it into a residence for Prince Pietrantonio Sanseverino. Bombarded by the French in 1806, it remains a tower and the walls, recently restored in part.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Collegiata di Santa Maria Maddalena

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

On the main square, baroqued by Donato Sarnicola in 1732, with neoclassical façade of 1844 and dome (1794) and bell tower (1817) covered with polychrome majolica in 1862. It preserves the polyptych of Bartolomeo Vivarini painted in Murano in 1477 (25 figures of saints, since 1995 in the chapel of San Silvestro), the Madonna of the Angels by Antonello Gagini (1505), the pulpit of 1792 by Agostino Fusco and two angels attributed to Pietro Bernini or one of his students.

  • Where Piazza principale, Morano Calabro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa dei Santi Pietro e Paolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The oldest church of Morano, dating back to about 1000, with interior rebuilt in late Baroque style. It contains marble statues by Pietro Bernini, father of Gian Lorenzo (1592 and 1601), the choir and the lectern carved by the Fusco workshop (1792-1805) and a processional cross in silver from 1445.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Chiesa e convento di San Bernardino da Siena

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries

Late-Gothic monastic church founded in 1452 by Antonio Sanseverino and consecrated in 1485, with ogival portal in yellow stone and wooden ceiling in ship hull, unique in Calabria. Inside there is a fifteenth-century wooden crucifix, the 1611 pulpit, the choir carved in 1538 and the 15th-century cloister.

  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Collegiata di San Nicola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

Ruderi del monastero di Colloreto

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

About 7 km from the village, in a large wood of holm oaks and beech trees at the foot of the Pollino, the remains of the monastery founded in 1546 and suppressed with the Murattian laws of 1809.

Centro studi naturalistici del Pollino «Il Nibbio»

Museums · Science and university

Naturalistic Museum of the Pollino National Park which presents the characteristics of the UNESCO Geopark, with paleontological finds of the ancient local lake basin (Elephas antiquus, rhinoceros, hippopotamus, hyena); it was born from the Il Nibbio association, founded in 1998 to recover the abandoned houses of the historic center.
