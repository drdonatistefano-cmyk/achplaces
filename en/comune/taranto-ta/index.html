What to see in Taranto

The city of the two seas founded by the Spartans in 706 BC: the Old Town on the island with the cathedral of San Cataldo and the Doric Temple, the Aragonese Castle and the revolving bridge, the Umbertino Village with the Marta and the Ori of Taranto, the promenade on the Great Sea.

15 places to visit in Taranto, in the province of Taranto (Apulia). Every entry is checked against official sources.

MArTA – Museo Archeologico Nazionale

Museums · Archaeological · Paid entry

Established in 1887 in the former convent of the Alcantarini, it tells the South of Italy from the Paleolithic to the Middle Ages: figurative ceramics, the tomb of the athlete and the Sala degli Ori of Taranto, jewelry to a cantilever and filigree of the IV-III century BC.

  • Where Via Cavour 10, Taranto
  • Entry paid
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it
  • Tickets Guided tours on GetYourGuide

Sources: italia.it · cultura.gov.it · museotaranto.cultura.gov.it

Castello Aragonese (Castel Sant'Angelo)

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Viewpoint

Started in 1492 by Ferdinando d'Aragona on Greek and Byzantine fortifications, partly demolished in 1887 for the revolving bridge; it is the seat of the Navy and offers even evening visits.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Taranto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museotaranto.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Tempio dorico

History and architecture · Archaeology · Free entry · Viewpoint

In Piazza Castello, dates back to the first half of the 6th century BC and is among the oldest in Magna Grecia; destroyed in 209 BC, it is called popularly Poseidon Temple.

  • Where Piazza Castello, Taranto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museotaranto.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Città Vecchia

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval quarter was built in the 10th century on the Greek acropolis, which became an island in 1480 with the cutting of the isthmus: alleys, stairs, palaces on the back and Byzantine walls along Via di Mezzo.

  • Where Via Duomo, Taranto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museotaranto.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

Cattedrale di San Cataldo

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Norman Romanesque Basilica documented since 1071, with a mosaic of 1160 and the cap of San Cataldo, begun in 1630 and frescoed in the dome.

  • Where Via Duomo, Taranto
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: museotaranto.cultura.gov.it · italia.it

MUDI – Museo diocesano

Museums · Old masters

Opened in 2011 in the former Archbishop's seminary, Palazzo Muscetola: liturgical furnishings, the largest engraved topaz in the world and embroideries in fine linen.

  • Where Città Vecchia, Taranto
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

History and architecture · Archaeology

The Taranto underground: the chamber tomb of the athletes, of the first 5th century BC, and Hellenistic tombs with painted plasters; in the Old City caves, Norman mills and remains of the acropolis.
