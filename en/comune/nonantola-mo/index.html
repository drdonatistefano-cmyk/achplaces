Abbazia di Nonantola

Places of worship · Abbeys and monasteries · Free entry

The Benedictine abbey founded in the 8th century, with its carved Romanesque portal and a hundred-column crypt; next to the Benedictine and Diocesan museum.

  • Where Piazza Abbazia, Nonantola
  • Entry Benedictine and Diocesan Museum for a fee
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page emiliaromagnaturismo.it

Sources: visitmodena.it · emiliaromagnaturismo.it
