What to see in Soveria Mannelli

Country of the Reventino at the foot of Sila Piccola, known for its historical feats: the Charter Museum of enterprise of the Rubbbettino publishing house and the Museum of the Leo Lanificio; in Piazza dei Mille the monument to Garibaldi recalls the disarmament of the Bourbon army of 30 August 1860.

Carta Museo d'impresa e Parco d'arte Rubbettino

Museums · Themed

Museum of the publishing house Rubbettino, born in 1972 from an idea of Rosario Rubbettino, who left the post of secretary of middle school: the first book, of philosophy, was printed entirely with lead, and in 1976 the first plant was born. The museum tells the story of the book in Calabria, from the processing of paper pulp to printing and bookbinding, and hosts conferences, seminars and festivals.

  • Where Viale Rosario Rubbettino 10, Soveria Mannelli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it

Museo d'impresa del Lanificio Leo

Museums · Themed

Museum of the family-run historical wool mill that produces wool fabrics and quality yarns for fashion and does research and experimentation; open to the public, offers a path immersive in the processing of wool and in the garments that are born.

  • Where Via Cava 43, Soveria Mannelli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · fondoambiente.it

Piazza dei Mille e monumento a Garibaldi

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

In the square the white marble monument in Garibaldi recalls the disarmament of the Bourbon army at Soveria Mannelli on 30 August 1860.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Camillo Sirianni

Local life and traditions · Crafts

A company founded in 1909 by Angelo Sirianni in a small warehouse in the historic center transformed into a joinery; today an industry that combines artisan care and processing of wood and steel, with over a thousand items exported in more than 35 countries. Open on the occasion of the 2018 FAI Fall Day.

  • Where Località Scaglioni 30, Soveria Mannelli
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: fondoambiente.it · italia.it
