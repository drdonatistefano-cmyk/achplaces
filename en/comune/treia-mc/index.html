What to see in Treia

Borgo Maceratese whose name derives from Trea-Jana, goddess venerated in antiquity: the square of the Republic with horseshoe with the Georgica Academy and the Town Hall, the Cathedral of Andrea Vici, the Porta Vallesacco and, outside the center, the Sanctuary of the SS. Crucified on the site of the Roman Trea.

15 places to visit in Treia, in the province of Macerata (Marche). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Piazza della Repubblica

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry · Viewpoint

Horse-iron square with aerial balustrade and marble monument to Pope Pius VI, overlooking the Georgica Academy and the Town Hall; from here the view extends over hills and fields and, on clear days, up to Mount Conero and the sea. The village preserves walls of the thirteenth century and towers.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica, Treia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · letsmarche.it

Palazzo comunale e Pinacoteca comunale

Museums · Old masters

The Palazzo Comunale, dating back to the XVI-XVII centuries, houses the Pinacoteca Comunale on the noble floor, in the Sala del Consiglio, in the Sala degli Stemmi and in other rooms.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica, Treia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Accademia Georgica

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Neoclassical building designed by Valadier, home of the Georgic Academy: it preserves more than 14,000 volumes and a historical archive with a parchment almost 12 meters long.

  • Where Piazza della Repubblica, Treia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · eventi.turismo.marche.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Museo civico archeologico

Museums · Archaeological

In the former convent of St. Francis, it collects fragments of sculptures and inscriptions from the Picena to the Roman age, with strong ties with Egypt: two Egyptian sculptures (a female deity and a head of Serapide) found at the Sanctuary of the SS. Crucifix, fragments from the Serapean and Roman statuary from the age of Augustus to that of Hadrian and Antoninus.

  • Where Via Nazario Sauro, Treia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · letsmarche.it · italia.it

Cattedrale della SS. Annunziata

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

The eighteenth century, one of the largest in the region, designed by Andrea Vici, a student of Vanvitelli; it preserves a Pagani bezel, a table by Giacomo da Recanati and a marble bust by Pope Sixtus V, of which a copy is at Victoria and Albert Museum in London.

  • Where Treia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it · eventi.turismo.marche.it

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Porta Vallesacco

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

Porta del Duecento, the most famous of the seven ancient gates of Treia, declared a national monument: in 1263 the battle between the Montecchiesi and Corrado d'Antiochia, depicted on the curtain of the theatre, was fought.

  • Where Treia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Teatro comunale (Condominiale)

History and architecture · Historic theatres

Built in 1801 on a project by the architect Lucatelli and financed by a company of families from Treais, with wooden stages; the curtain painted by Silverio Copparoni in 1865 represents the battle of Vallesacco. Visits are booked through Pro Loco.

  • Where Treia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

History and architecture · Towers · Free entry · Viewpoint

12th century bastion at the southern end of the ancient fortifications, overlooking the Sibillini Mountains, Mount Conero and the Adriatic; at Christmas there is the living Nativity scene.

Santuario del SS. Crocifisso

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

About 1.5 km from the village, in the locality of San Lorenzo, rises on the site of the Roman city of Trea and an ancient parish church built on a temple of Isis: it preserves Roman remains, including a mosaic with ibis, and a 15th century crucifix that tradition wants carved by an angel and some attribute to Donatello.

  • Where Via SS. Crocifisso 1, Treia
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: eventi.turismo.marche.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · touringclub.it · italia.it

Casa di terra di Fontevannazza

Museums · House museums

House-museum close to the center that tells the peasant life between the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries: in the houses of earth and straw, called "earthed" , original tools, a frame, the fireplace and the "prete" , the bed warmer.

  • Where Contrada Fontevannazza, Treia
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes

Roverella monumentale

Nature and landscape · Landscapes · Free entry

Oak of about 450 years, 30 meters high and with a trunk of 6.5 meters of circumference, national monument since 2000; near the Municipality has realized a small natural amphitheatre.
