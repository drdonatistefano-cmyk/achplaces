What to see in Zagarolo

Medieval village with its regular layout on a 2 km long tuff hill, between woods and countryside about 41 km from Rome, dominated by the sixteenth-century Palazzo Rospigliosi, where in 1606 Caravaggio took refuge and which today houses the Museo del Giocattolo; land of the crazy thrush and the white wine DOC Zagarolo.

Palazzo Rospigliosi

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

From 1571, after Lepanto, Pompeo Colonna transformed the medieval fortress into a ducal palace; here in 1606 he found Caravaggio refuge, which for Marzio Colonna painted the "Dinner in Emmaus" and the "Maddalena penitente" (Maddalena penitente). Passed to the Ludovisi in 1622 and to the Rospigliosi in 1670, it preserves grotesque vaults attributed to Orazio Zecca and Domenico Fetti, the Hall of the Battle of Lepanto (frescoes attributed to Giovanni Bianchi called the Bertone), the private chapel of 1630 and the eighteenth-century Alcova with frescoes by Hannibal Rotati. In 1979 Princess Elvina Pallavicini sold it to the Municipality, which has restored it since 2003; she is part of the Latium Historical Houses Network. On the first Sunday of the month there is a guided tour at €7.50, with entrance to the Toy Museum at €1; for other visits you book with the secretariat (info@instituzionepalazzorospigliosi.it, 06 95769401).

Museo del Giocattolo

Museums · Themed · Paid entry

Opened in 2005 in the east wing of Palazzo Rospigliosi, on about 1,400 m2 it exhibits more than 800 pieces, about one third of the collection, which tell the story of the toy in Italy in the last century, with European and American productions (brands like Lehmann, Märklin and Bing), ordered for themes that bind the game to objects and social life; three rooms host temporary exhibitions. Free under 6 years, reduced from 6 to 12 years and over 65 years; closed on Mondays and from 10 to 24 August, with seasonal times (in winter Tuesdays and Fridays 9-13 and 15-18, Wednesdays and Thursdays 9-13, Saturdays and Sundays 10-13 and 15-18).

San Pietro Apostolo e Collegiata di San Lorenzo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church of San Pietro Apostolo was built in Baroque forms by the Rospigliosi princes in the early eighteenth century, perhaps on an older church: it has a Greek cross plan and an elliptical dome 46 meters high. The Collegiate Church of San Lorenzo, dedicated to the patron saint, is in Vignolesco style, with the façade attributed to Carlo Maderno.

  • Entry Free

Centro storico, Lu Giustu e fontana di Piazza Santa Maria

History and architecture · Squares and old town · Free entry

The medieval village has a regular system on the tuff hill; according to legend the exiles of Gabi, destroyed by Tarquinio il Superbo, took refuge there. Along the way you will find the Palazzo del Gonfaloniere, the Convent of Santa Maria delle Grazie, the fountain with Roman bath in Piazza Santa Maria and, in via Fabrini near the Municipality, the ancient public balance with the historical measures of the village guarded by the bust called "Lu Giustu" which depicts Pope Clement IX Rospigliosi.

  • Where Via Fabrini e Piazza Santa Maria, Zagarolo
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Tordo matto e vino DOC Zagarolo

Local life and traditions · Food and wine · Free entry

Zagarolo's crazy thrush, produced by De.Co. and PAT, is a roll of horse meat flavored with the pitartima (coriandolo), celebrated every year in mid-June by the Festival del Tordo Matto in the streets of the village. The white DOC Zagarolo is linked to the tradition of the "fraschette" where a Frasca indicated the sale of new wine; in October there is the Festival of grapes and typical local wines.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com
