What to see in Guidonia Montecelio

The town is 22 km from Rome, which combines the medieval village of Montecelio, with the Rocca dei Crescenzi and the Civic Museum Rodolfo Lanciani, which houses the Capitolina Triad, to the town of Fondazione Rationalista of Guidonia gathered around Piazza Matteotti; to the south opens the Regional Natural Park of the Enviolata.

Museo civico archeologico «Rodolfo Lanciani»

Museums · Archaeological · Viewpoint

On the ground floor of the former convent of San Michele Arcangelo, on one of the two hills of Montecelio, the museum dedicated to the archaeologist Rodolfo Lanciani, family of the place, exhibits around the cloister finds of the entire municipal territory from prehistory to the seventeenth century. The most famous piece is the Capitolina Triad, with Jupiter, Juno and Minerva sitting with eagle, owl and peacock: the most complete and best preserved example of the group. Renewed, reopened on March 15, 2025 together with the Municipal Historical Archives; open Saturday and Sunday 10-18, reservations on tel. 0774 616107.

  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours

Centro storico di Montecelio e Rocca

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

The medieval village is made of winding alleys with several sacred newsstands; in Via Sant'Antonino there is a small stone house with bifora and Palazzo Cesi dates back to about 1500. From the stairway of Via degli Scacchi, beyond the arch of San Lorenzo, you go up to the Rocca that dominates the village: built by the Crescenzi reusing materials of a Roman temple, passed to the Orsini in 1400 and already in 1500 was abandoned.

  • Where Via degli Scacchi, Montecelio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com

Parco naturale regionale dell'Inviolata

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry

Protected area of 535 hectares on the eastern edge of Rome, managed since 2016 by the Parco dei Monti Lucretili: Mediterranean maquis, pastures, tufa ditches, ponds and forests of oaks and farnies, with about 240 species of plants (orchids, narcisses) and over 30 species of birds including herons and fishermen martins. There are the site of the ancient Latin city of Ficulea, the remains of a Roman villa at the Casale dell'Inviolata and Roman tombs such as the Sepulchre of the Torraccet (III century AD) and the Torraccia dell'Inviolata, transformed into a tower in the Middle Ages.

  • Where Inviolata, Guidonia Montecelio
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.visitlazio.com

Sources: visitlazio.com
