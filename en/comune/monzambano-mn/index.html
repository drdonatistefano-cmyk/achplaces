What to see in Monzambano

A village, two castles: on the morainic hills south of Garda, above the Mincio valley, the medieval village of Monzambano rises to its castle, while the hamlet of Castellaro Lagusello, surrounded by crenellated walls, is mirrored in a lake shaped like a heart; the prehistoric site of the territory is part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Castello di Monzambano

History and architecture · Castles and fortresses · Free entry · Viewpoint

Medieval fortification built by the Veronas, symbol of the village: there are still crenellated walls and towers, at the top of a village of streets and alleys that go up to the castle; inside the walls stands the church of San Biagio, linked to the religious origins of the village. From the top there is a wide panorama of the Mincio valley. Free admission (pleasant offers), with opening of the clock tower on Sundays and holidays afternoons from Pasquetta at the end of August and during the Grape Festival in September.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Castellaro Lagusello

History and architecture · Villages · Free entry · Viewpoint

Fortified village born in the 11th century as a castle-house, a simple defensive belt, and still closed by walls Guelfe merlate (XII-XIV century) interrupted by towers; you enter from the large portal next to the bell tower, between paved alleys of river pebbles and rustic houses. The tower houses a photographic collection on the nature reserve surrounding the village and contemporary art exhibitions.

  • Where Castellaro Lagusello, Monzambano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it · monzambanotourist.it

Villa Arrighi Tacoli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas

Built on the ancient Venetian fortress of the village, it has defensive walls, Guelph battlements and cross-windows overlooking the lake; Napoleon I, II and III stayed there and General Mac-Mahon after the battle of Solferino on June 24, 1859. You can visit the Napoleonic halls and the secular garden, from which you go up to the walls; inside the church of San Giuseppe, completed in 1737, with paintings of the seventeenth century.

private house, open only on request

Chiesa di San Nicola

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

Parish of Castellaro dedicated to the patron saint of San Nicola di Bari, renovated in Baroque forms in the seventeenth century on a Romanesque church; it preserves a painting of the saint and a wooden statue of the Virgin of the fifteenth century.

  • Where Castellaro Lagusello, Monzambano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: monzambanotourist.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it · italia.it

Lago a cuore e riserva naturale di Castellaro

Nature and landscape · Parks and nature reserves · Free entry · Viewpoint

Outside the walls the small lake of morainic origin, famous for its heart shape, reflects the village; all around the natural reserve extends. From the square of the church of San Giuseppe a short staircase descends to the lake.

  • Where Castellaro Lagusello, Monzambano
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · monzambanotourist.it · borghipiubelliditalia.it

Colline moreniche e fiume Mincio

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry · Viewpoint

Hills covered with vineyards and olive groves, woods with lake fauna and the banks of the Mincio, which just from Monzambano begins the descent towards the Mantua plain: an area suitable for walking and cycling.

  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
