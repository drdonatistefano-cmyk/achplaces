Museo civico di Taverna

Museums · Old masters

It is located in the former Dominican convent founded in 1465, annexed to the monumental church of San Domenico. Sixteen rooms exhibit works from the seventeenth to the twenty-first century, including works by Mattia and Gregorio Preti, and an important collection of Italian multimedia art from the mid-Twentieth century; from 2023 it is part of the National Museum System. Closed on Mondays; from Tuesday to Sunday 9.30-12.30 and 16-18 (October-March) or 16-19 (April-September).

Chiesa monumentale di San Domenico

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches

The bell tower dates back to 1794 and was built in the Baroque style between 1670 and 1680 after the 1662 earthquake. He kept eleven paintings by Mattia Preti, including the Miracle of St. Francis of Paola (1678), the Lightning Christ (1680) and the Martyrdom of St. Sebastian (1687), a Pietà by Giovan Battista Ortega (1603), a 17th century scagliola palace, an organ by Francesco Ferrazzano (1754) and a golden wooden pulpit. Eight paintings of Priests, stolen in 1970, were found; the church reopened in 1988.

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry

Tourist village in a plateau in the woods of Sila, on the edge of the National Park, in the municipalities of Taverna, Sorbo San Basile and Zagarise; italia.it indicates it among the most picturesque mountain villages of the region.
