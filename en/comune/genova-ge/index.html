What to see in Genoa

The Superba, capital of the ancient maritime Republic: one of the largest historical centers in Europe, the New Roads and the Palaces of the Rolli UNESCO heritage, the Port Antico redesigned by Renzo Piano, the Lanterna and a vertical city of lifts, funiculars and strong up to the parks of Nervi and Pegli.

27 places to visit in Genoa, in the province of Genoa (Liguria). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Notice: Listing in enlargement: the guide covers 29 of the 65 places of culture recorded by the Ministry of Culture in this municipality, as well as churches, squares and nature.

Musei di Strada Nuova – Palazzo Rosso, Palazzo Bianco e Palazzo Tursi

Museums · Old masters · UNESCO World Heritage · Paid entry

Along Strada Nuova (via Garibaldi) a single path combines Palazzo Rosso, Palazzo Bianco and Palazzo Doria Tursi, three sixteenth and seventeenth century residences among the most prestigious Rolli, home to civic art collections and Town Hall.

  • Where Via Garibaldi, Genova
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Official website www.visitgenoa.it
  • Tickets Tickets on the official website

Strada Nuova e i Palazzi dei Rolli

History and architecture · Palaces and villas · UNESCO World Heritage · Free entry

The noble residences that the aristocracy put at the disposal of the Republic to host ambassadors and dignitaries; registered by UNESCO in 2006 as "Genoa": the new roads and the system of the Palaces of the Rolls, today there are 42.

  • Where Via Garibaldi, Genova
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.visitgenoa.it

Sources: visitgenoa.it · italia.it

Cattedrale di San Lorenzo e Museo del Tesoro

Places of worship · Cathedrals · Free entry

Facade with horizontal white and black bands of the early thirteenth century, dome completed at the end of the sixteenth century; in the ancient crypt the Treasury Museum set up in 1956 by Franco Albini, next to the Diocesan Museum.

Ancient residence of the Doges and seat of political power, today the cultural center of the city with exhibitions and events; the Grimaldina Tower was jailed of political prisoners, including Jacopo Ruffini and Niccolò Paganini.

Porto Antico e il Bigo

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

Cultural and commercial complex with free access between the historic center and skyscrapers, redesigned by Renzo Piano and completed in 1992 for the Expo and the 500 years of the trip of Colombo; the Bigo is the panoramic crane elevator.

Casa di Colombo, Porta Soprana e Chiostro di Sant'Andrea

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials

Porta Soprana, from the 12th century, was the entrance to the city for those who came from the east; just beyond the house where Colombo lived between 1455 and 1470 and the cloister of Sant'Andrea, reassembled here after the demolition of the monastery in 1904.

  • Where Via di Porta Soprana, Genova
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.visitgenoa.it

Sources: visitgenoa.it
