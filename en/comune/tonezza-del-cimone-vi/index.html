What to see in Tonezza del Cimone

Alto Vicentino plateau between 900 and 1,700 meters, dominated by Monte Spitz and Monte Cimone, the theatre in 1916 of one of the most famous mine wars on the front: today you can visit the shrine on the crater, the trenches recovered from the Ecomuseum of the Great War, the Austro-Hungarian cemetery and the Visitor Center in the village.

Monte Cimone e Sacrario militare

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry · Viewpoint

The Italians conquered the summit on 23 July 1916; on 23 September 1916 the Austro-Hungarians made a mine shine that killed the occupants, and the imperial troops remained on the Cimone until the end of the conflict. Above the crater rises the ossuary with a high cusp that guards the remains of many fallen; among the beech trees remain walkways, trenches and galleries, partly recovered from the Ecomuseum of the Great War.

  • Where Monte Cimone, Tonezza del Cimone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.vicenzae.org

Sources: vicenzae.org

Museums · Themed

Cimitero austro-ungarico dei Crosati

History and architecture · Monuments and memorials · Free entry

It housed the corpses of 1036 imperial soldiers, exhumed in 1965 and transferred to the ossuary of Citadella. He was abandoned for years, was recovered in 2006 and renamed "Monument to Concord and Peace."

  • Where Località Crosati, Contrà Campana, Tonezza del Cimone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.vicenzae.org

Sources: vicenzae.org

Monte Spitz

Nature and landscape · Mountains · Free entry · Viewpoint

Highest point of the Tonezza plateau, you can reach Passo della Vena for an ancient mule track restored by the Alps; on top a stone base carries a plaque with the names of the 27 visible peaks.

  • Where Passo della Vena, Tonezza del Cimone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 2 hours or more
  • Tourist portal page www.vicenzae.org

Sources: vicenzae.org · www-old.veneto.eu

Sentiero naturalistico Excalibur e Monte Caviojo

Nature and landscape · Hills and trails · Free entry

The Excalibur educational trail is an easy path between beech and larch woods; more challenging is the Monte Caviojo trail, which passes through the military tunnel of Cima Neutra.

  • Where Tonezza del Cimone
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.vicenzae.org

Sources: www-old.veneto.eu · vicenzae.org
