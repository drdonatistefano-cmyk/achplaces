What to see in Grotte di Castro

Country of the Volsini Mountains near Lake Bolsena, built on a volcanic cone of tuff above the Etruscan city of Civita: the Civita Museum in the Vignola Palace, the necropolis of Pianezze and Centocamere, the Basilica of Maria Santissima del Suffragio with its museum and the church of San Pietro on top of the village.

8 places to visit in Grotte di Castro, in the province of Viterbo (Lazio). Every entry is checked against official sources.

Museo Civita

Museums · Archaeological

In a palace designed by Vignola in the second half of the sixteenth century, on two floors the funerary furnishings of the Etruscan necropolis of Vigna la Piazza, Pianezze e Centocamere: on the first floor the daily life and the richness of the elites (room "I Segni del Potere" with the Tomb 31 and the "tomba della bambina") on the ground floor the funeral rite with the life-size reconstruction of a bedroom tomb and an original sarcophagus in monolithic tuff. It is part of the Museum System of Lake Bolsena; the ticket also applies to necropolis.

  • Where Piazza Giacomo Matteotti, Grotte di Castro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Official website www.italia.it

Necropoli di Pianezze

History and architecture · Archaeology · Viewpoint

About 3 km from the village, Etruscan necropolis used between VII and V century B.C. with chamber graves dug in the rock of tuff: so far about 24 monumental burials have been identified, including the so-called Red Tomb. You can visit with the ticket of the Museo Civita.

  • Where Località Pianezze, Grotte di Castro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Necropoli di Centocamere

History and architecture · Archaeology

In front of the plateau of Civita, the oldest necropolis (tombs of the VII century BC), with rooms dug into the tuff connected by a labyrinth of corridors, some opened by the tombbarols; the largest tomb, the CC4, is about 16 meters long with five rooms in axis and three lateral and it welcomed 19 burials. You can visit with the ticket of the Museo Civita.

  • Where Grotte di Castro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 1-1.5 hours
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: visitlazio.com · italia.it

Basilica Santuario di Maria Santissima del Suffragio

Places of worship · Sanctuaries · Free entry

Born around a miraculous image of the Madonna painted on an outer wall of the village: in 1625 the aedicule became a chapel, then it was expanded several times to become a basilica; Pius IX raised her to the Marian shrine in 1854. Neoclassical façade with two bell towers, three naves and, on the main altar, the 16th century fresco of the Madonna del Suffragio carried on canvas; in the presbytery a pipe organ of 1780 still working. The main party is the first Sunday of September.

  • Where Grotte di Castro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it · visitlazio.com

Museo della Basilica Santuario

Museums · Themed

Small museum in the basement of the Basilica of Maria Santissima del Suffragio with sacred furnishings from the 15th to the 18th century and prehistoric, protohistoric and Etruscan archaic finds of the territory; among the main pieces a fifteenth century reliquary and sacred vestments of the early seventeenth and eighteenth centuries.

  • Where Piazza San Giovanni, Grotte di Castro
  • Entry To be checked
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: touringclub.it · italia.it

Chiesa di San Pietro Apostolo

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

The parish church, at the highest point of the village, existed before 1118, when the bishop of Orvieto Guglielmo consecrated it; from a basilica to three naves it was transformed into a single nave, with the inverted entrance. The façade dates back to 1739; the coffered ceiling of the late nineteenth century is by Luigi Fontana, with four paintings and one fifth, the Mission of the Apostles of Father Nazarene Confaloni.

  • Where Via Unione 1A, Grotte di Castro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it

Chiesa di San Giovanni Evangelista

Places of worship · Churches and parish churches · Free entry

In the new part of the village, it rises where an older church was destroyed in 1144 during the war between Acquapendente and Orvieto; the new, octagonal plan with colored windows, was begun in 1987 and inaugurated in 1993, with terracotta by the sculptor Mario Vinci of Acquapendente.

  • Where SR74 76, Grotte di Castro
  • Entry Free
  • Suggested time 30-45 minutes
  • Tourist portal page www.italia.it

Sources: italia.it
